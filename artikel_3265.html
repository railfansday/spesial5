Michelle Monaghan & Justin Theroux Are Having A ‘Sleepover’ With Blumhouse Atomic & Universal

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

That Jacob Chase directed Blumhouse Atomic and Universal movie we told you about in August, Sleepover, well, Michelle Monaghan and Justin Theroux are leading it. The pic remains dated for Oct. 8, 2027.

The Insidious: Out of the Further filmmaker is helming off a screenplay he co-penned with Night Swim‘s Bryce McGuire, which is based on the latter’s original short story. The project is billed as the “quintessential sleepover nightmare”. Jason Blum is producing. Chase, McGuire and Bea Sequeira will serve as executive producers. Sequeira serve as EP for Blumhouse and Lilly Tyson overseeing the project for the company. Jason Blum is producing.
