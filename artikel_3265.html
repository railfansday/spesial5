Ina Garten Reveals Why She Will Never Do ‘Dancing With The Stars’

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Celebrity Chef Ina Garten revealed this week on her Happy Hour podcast that she once turned down Dancing with the Stars.

Discussing fear, overcoming it and whether she had ever turned something down because it was “too scary,” Garten said, “I think I absolutely have…Somebody asked me to do Dancing with the Stars. I thought nobody needs to see that. That’s not happening.”

The chef-turned-podcaster explained, “I thought the closer I got to the day when I had to do it, the more I’d have nightmares. I feel like a lot of people would feel like that. I don’t know. That’s so intimidating.”

Guest Rose Byrne agreed, ‘I would be terrified of that.” Watch on Deadline

Numerous other celebrity chefs have competed on the ABC show, including Rocco DiSpirito, Paula Deen and Giada De Laurentiis, the latter of whom broke her toe while practicing for this season.

Despite her resistance to doing the cha-cha on TV, Garten said that generally she’s “made peace with fear,” given that she often finds herself out over her skis.

“If you push yourself enough, then you have to figure out how to deal with fear. I mean, I feel like um it’s always with me. I never get rid of it. It’s like in the side car next to me while I’m going along.”

The Dancing with the Stars finale will air live coast-to-coast for the first time on Tuesday.
