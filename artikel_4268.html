Ball x Pit's Risen Ballbylon expansion is out now, offering you brand-new battlefields to discover

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Aside from the obvious wealth of puns it allows, Ball x Pit has proven to be a big hit with fans of fast-paced roguelike action games. And now, the biggest expansion yet to Ball x Pit is set to land today, with the arrival of Risen Ballbylon and brand-new challenges for you to take on!

As you might expect from the name, Risen Ballbylon, rather than seeing you descend into the Pit and the ruins of the once-great city, now has you rising above the clouds and into the floating metropolis above. Four new battlefields and forty new varieties of enemies pose a challenge even to the most devoted of ball masters.

And it wouldn't be Ball x Pit if there weren't even more balls to add to your arsenal too! The Beast Ball can latch onto and damage enemies, while the Kaiju Ball causes catastrophic earthquakes, and the Misfortune Ball lays a curse on your foes. And these are just a small selection of the 24 new balls being added!

Yes, the name aside, Ball x Pit is a roguelike that's really grabbed people's attention and been a real success for developer Kenny Sun and Friends, as well as publisher Devolver Digital. So here's hoping now that it's out, that Risen Ballbylon lives up to the hype!
