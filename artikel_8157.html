The Top 10 Most Family-Friendly Resorts in the West (2027)

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Family ski day at Deer Valley Resort, Park City, UT (Photo: Courtesy of Deer Valley)

Family ski trips have a way of becoming the vacations everyone remembers. Maybe it’s the kids finding their ski legs or the whole crew reuniting over hot chocolate at the end of the day. And while theme parks have their place, we’d argue there’s no better way to spend a family vacation than on the mountain.

Some resorts go the extra mile to make those trips special, with standout ski schools, kid-friendly activities, and thoughtful touches that keep everyone smiling. These 10 Western resorts earned top marks from SKI readers for making family ski vacations especially memorable. Here’s where to bring your crew.
