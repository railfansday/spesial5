Prediction: Stockport County vs Tranmere Rovers

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Two camps who will turn their attention back to EFL Trophy action on Tuesday night, Stockport will welcome Tranmere to Edgeley Park.

While Europe’s elite domestic leagues might be in the midst of a fortnight interval due to November’s international break, there are still plenty of attractive betting promotions available.

Ahead of Tuesday’s EFL Trophy showdown, you can find all of the key Stockport County vs Tranmere Rovers H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, while Stockport might have been hit with play-off heartbreak last season, Jimmy McNulty’s men haven’t shown any signs of a hangover this time around. Once again receiving a string of public praise, the Hatters find the mood in their camp at a real high and they are once again scrapping it out at the top of the League One standings. Eventually extending on their FA Cup adventure over the weekend as they clinched a thrilling 3-2 victory away at seventh-tier outfit Cray Wanderers, Stockport will be looking to make a statement when they turn their attention back to EFL Trophy action on Tuesday night. Suffering just a single defeat from any of their previous 10 consecutive appearances across all competitions, McNulty’s side have also gained a reputation for their extended dominance on home soil. In fact, the Hatters have won each of their last four straight showdowns at Edgeley Park by an aggregate score of 7-2.

Yet to make a maiden appearance this season and still sidelined with a long-term knee injury, Stockport will once again be without former Norwich City youngster Brad Hills on Tuesday night. However, one-time Sheffield United academy graduate Kyron Gordon could return to McNulty’s squad here.

Handed a full FA Cup rest away at Cray Wanderers over the weekend, ex-Nottingham Forest midfielder Ben Osborn could be handed a full recall in the middle of the park. Former Fulham youngster Luca Ashby-Hammond should keep his spot between the sticks.

As for the visitors, only just dragging themselves over the finishing line last season as they clung to their League Two status, Tranmere were simply hoping for smoother sailing this time around. However, despite currently scrapping it out in the top-half of England’s fourth-tier, Darrell Clarke’s men are searching for some much-welcomed stability. Watching their respective FA Cup adventure come to a screeching end over the weekend as they slumped to a 3-0 drumming away at Barnet, Tuesday’s guests have failed to register consecutive wins across all competitions so far this calendar year. Facing what will certainly be a stern examination when they travel to Edgeley Park in midweek, Tranmere will be concerned by their most recent showdown against Stockport. Falling to a routine 3-1 defeat when they welcomed the League One promotion chasers to Prenton Park for an FA Cup tie back in November 2025, Clarke’s inconsistent squad find themselves pencilled in as a major underdog contender here.

Still recovering from a long-term knee injury, Tranmere will once again be without the services of veteran striker Connor Jennings on Tuesday night. Likewise, former Bournemouth youngster Zane Okoro remains on the sidelines with a muscle issue and won’t play any part here.

Making an FA Cup cameo appearance off the bench on Saturday, former Salford midfielder Ryan Watson could be handed a full recall. Completing a permanent switch to Prenton Park over the summer, veteran attacker Tom Ince will play some part in the final third.

With their sights set on securing an iconic Championship promotion this season, Stockport certainly find the mood in their camp at a red-hot high. Winning each of their last four consecutive showdowns at Edgeley Park by an aggregate score of 7-2, the Hatters are rightfully installed as a standout contender on Tuesday night. Landing a 3-1 win when they last met Tranmere for an FA Cup showdown 12 months ago, we are expecting a similar outcome here.
