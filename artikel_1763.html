Playmaker dreams of joining Liverpool, Julian Ward has begun the move

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

An exciting playmaker has confirmed he wants to join a club like Liverpool.

The Reds have struggled creatively this season. Andoni Iraola has restored the intensity and has brought largely the best out of most of his players in a short space of time.

Liverpool are also scoring goals. However, there's no denying that creatively they are struggling.

While goals may be coming, the chances are fewer in comparison to the rest of the top performers in the league. Iraola's side have not created enough opportunities from open play in order to be seriously considered a title challenge.

There are plenty of things the Spaniard can look to improve internally, but perhaps the best solution might come from an external source.

How Liverpool can become a creative force againWhen the Reds signed Florian Wirtz it was hoped that their creative issues would be a thing of the past. It was in the same summer that Liverpool lost their biggest creative outlet in Trent Alexander-Arnold. He moves onto pastures new and Liverpool were left searching for their new Philippe Coutinho. They believed they find him in Wirtz. But so far, that hasn't really paid off. Wirtz has been disappointing and even though he is getting better Liverpool are still not creating enough big chances. In order for Iraola's side to be a creative force again, he needs more ammunition and more playmakers. It looks like that is exactly what Julian Ward is trying to do in order to help the Reds become a force again.
