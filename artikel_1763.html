Arsecast Extra Episode 721 – 16.11.2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Welcome to another Arsecast Extra, the Arsenal podcast, with myself and James from @gunnerblog.

In this Interlull episode we start with the recent Arseblog health update, and the outpouring of love and support from the community. After which we chat about the Arsenal involvement in England’s 3-2 win over Spain, in particular a big contribution from Ben White who scored an important goal after some recent difficulties in the international set-up. We ask if it’s time that perception of him changed based on what he’s delivering, and not what rabble-rousers in the media accuse him of. There’s a round-up of other international commitments, discussion of Declan Rice’s new contract, and listener questions about a contract for Martin Odegaard, Bukayo Saka’s role, a transfer window for managers, Santi Cazorla, and lots more.
