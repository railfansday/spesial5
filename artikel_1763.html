How to watch Belgium vs Italy: TV channel and live stream for Nations League today

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Italy will hope to build on their shock win over France when they host Belgium in another heavyweight showdown in the Nations League this evening.

Roberto Mancini’s Azzurri side produced a brilliant performance to defeat Les Bleus at home for the first time in 64 years at the San Siro on Thursday night, battling back from an early deficit in a 3-1 triumph that followed a hard-fought 1-1 draw in Paris last month.

Michael Olise scored against Italy again in Milan to put France - without captain and talisman Kylian Mbappe due to injury - in the driving seat following an error by Gianluigi Donnarumma, only for Pio Esposito to notch a brace either side of an effort from Davide Frattesi as Zinedine Zidane suffered his first loss as Didier Deschamps’ successor.

Sebastiano Esposito missed a penalty for the home team, but it did not matter in the end as his younger brother had more success.

It left Group A1 very finely poised heading into the final matchday, with Italy and France sitting level on 10 points apiece but the former occupying top spot due to their superior goal difference.

Zidane’s men host bottom side Turkey - still without a single win or even a point to their name after five games - at the same time as Italy go to Brussels, with Belgium still very much in the battle for a top-two finish and quarter-final berth on nine points.
