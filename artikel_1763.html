Mauricio Pochettino explains surprise Manchester United squad decision for Sporting clash: ‘Great opportunity’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Mauricio Pochettino has explained his decision to hand Joshua Zirkzee a first start of the season against Sporting in the Champions League.

The Argentine named just his second United team in Lisbon on Wednesday evening as he looked to bounce back from a damning 4-0 defeat by Liverpool on his managerial bow.

In Portugal, he made four changes from the side he named at Anfield, with Harry Maguire, Noussair Mazraoui and Amad Diallo all coming into the side.

Perhaps his most notable call, though, saw striker Zirkzee chosen to lead the line, making his first start of the campaign.

The Dutchman has been bumped up the pecking order somewhat for this match, with fellow striker Benjamin Sesko out injured and deputy Matheus Cunha suspended.

Pochettino, though, said he hopes Zirkzee can kick on as a more dependable option, given United’s thin ranks.

Asked to explain the selection, he told TNT Sports: “Cunha is suspended, of course, Sesko is injured. I think it is a great opportunity for Josh (Zirkzee) to show his quality.

“I think it is an important game to start to think that he can be a very important player for the team, and today is an opportunity for him.”

Zirkzee was entirely peripheral under Michael Carrick and had looked to leave United in the summer transfer window. He was the subject of a loan bid from Everton on Deadline Day, but no move materialised.

Pochettino, though, seems optimistic he can bring Zirkzee in from the cold as United look to salvage their campaign, both domestically and abroad.
