GTA 6’s Donnie Actor Underwent Brain Surgery While He Was Awake And Playing Guitar

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Grand Theft Auto 6 features a big cast of characters with fascinating, memorable storylines, and one such character is the drug-smuggler Donnie. He's played by actor Brad Carter, and he has a captivating life story of his own

Some 13 years ago, Carter underwent brain surgery to address tremors in his hands. For his brain surgery, he needed to be awake for the best chance at success, and as a musician, he played guitar to help the surgeon place the electrodes in the right positions.

His surgery was documented live on Twitter and Vine back in 2013, and you can see some dramatic photos of the surgery on UCLA's website. The surgery, or actually the multiple surgeries he had, did not completely eliminate his tremors, but he was able to play guitar again. The story made international headlines.

Carter's role as Donnie may be the part he ends up being known best for, considering how enormous and culturally dominating GTA 6 is. But he had a long acting and music career before GTA 6, having appeared on episodes of It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia, Dexter, True Blood, True Detective, Justified, Ozark, Yellowstone, and Sons of Anarchy.

GTA 6 is Carter's second big gaming role, following his portrayal of Jonah in Rockstar's Red Dead Redemption (2010).

GTA 6 launched on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it reached 32.5 million copies sold via pre-purchases alone. On day one, GTA 6 generated $3.79 billion in revenue. Over the first four days, GTA 6 has climbed to 44.9 million copies sold and more than $4 billion in revenue, according to a report.
