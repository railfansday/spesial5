Prediction market traders are now betting on whether more revelations will emerge about former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. A new Polymarket contract asking when a fourth Huang affair will be reported has drawn about $158,107 in trading volume since launching on November 13. Traders currently give a 57% chance that a fourth relationship will be reported by December 31, based on more than $100,000 in bets. Odds for a report by November 20 stand at 17%, though that contract has seen almost no trading. A report by November 13 is priced at just 4%. The market follows reports of three relationships linked to Huang, with Alice Huang, Nvidia healthcare vice president Kimberly Powell, and a second unnamed Nvidia employee, which led to his resignation as CEO on Thursday.
