Bayern Munich have a shortlist of 7 names to replace Harry Kane

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Bayern Munich are facing a huge blow with Harry Kane’s injury. The English striker seriously injured his left ankle during the match against Cologne (1-1) on Friday evening in the Bundesliga. Hit after contact with Joël Schmied, the 33-year-old striker was forced to leave his partners in the 20th minute. A premature exit which immediately aroused concern among the Bavarian staff. The medical examinations carried out subsequently unfortunately confirmed the fears of the Rekordmeister, who will have to do without his main offensive asset, recent winner of the 2026 Ballon d’Or, during a particularly important period of the season.

The diagnosis is particularly difficult since Harry Kane suffers from a rupture of the syndesmotic ligament in his left ankle. Bayern Munich made the news official in a press release published Friday evening. “Harry Kane tore the syndesmotic ligament in his left ankle during Bayern’s home Bundesliga match against Cologne on Friday evening. This was revealed by an examination carried out by the FC Bayern medical department. The 33-year-old will therefore be absent from the field for the next few months.announced the German club. A significant unavailability which deprives Vincent Kompany of his starting center forward for several major deadlines. With a busy schedule and high ambitions on the European scene, Bayern must now consider solutions to compensate for this absence during the winter transfer window.

The Bavarian management is starting to seriously look at the transfer market. According to information from Bildsupplemented by La Gazzetta dello Sportseveral profiles are being studied with a view to the next winter transfer window. The Nicolò Tresoldi track particularly attracts attention. The 22-year-old center forward plays for Club Bruges and has established himself as one of the most coveted young attackers on the continent. A German international hopeful, he is at the heart of a sporting battle with Italy, who are also seeking to attract him to their ranks. His performances in Belgium have already attracted several European teams, including AC Milan. Bayern could therefore enter into particularly strong competition to secure his services. Club Brugge would have set its demands at 60 million euros, a significant amount for an operation envisaged from January. The list of possibilities does not end at Tresoldi.

According to the transalpine newspaper, Bayern is also following Ricardo Pepi, former Augsburg striker now at PSV Eindhoven, as well as Igor Thiago, who plays at Brentford. For his part, Bild has put forward the name of Younes Ebnoutalib, the 23-year-old Eintracht Frankfurt striker. Revelation of the season, he scored eight goals in twelve matches and enjoys growing popularity in Germany. The daily newspaper mentions a player appreciated by Jürgen Klopp and compared internally to the young Robert Lewandowski. Paris Brunner is among the profiles mentioned. The former great hope of Borussia Dortmund is now playing in Monaco, where he continues his progress. His younger profile and his potential could represent a different alternative for Munich’s leaders. On the Rock, the Italian press mentions the Folarin Balogun file which is still monitored by Bavarian leaders, while Sky Sport Germany talks about a possible return of Eric Maxim Choupo-Moting, free agent in a few months. So many avenues which testify to the reflection undertaken in Bavaria, even if no official decision has been announced concerning winter recruitment.
