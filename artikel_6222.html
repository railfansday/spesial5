11 Restaurants in NYC That Are Open on Christmas Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Maybe you’re traveling to enjoy the singular experience of Rockefeller Center and the Rockettes at Christmas time, or you’re a local who’s spending the holidays at home. Regardless, this season is one of the most special times to spend in New York City, which makes it an exceptionally busy time of year for restaurants and bars alike.

Even with the foresight to make a reservation or two, it can be a challenge to find a place to enjoy lunch or dinner with a group on Christmas Day; many spots close for the day, while others simply don’t feel quite special enough for a true, New York City holiday. To help you avoid a last-minute scramble for a table or a less-than-festive dinner date, we’ve curated a guide of some of the very best restaurants open in NYC on Christmas day. Many of these spots will be serving their usual, outstanding menus, while some are offering holiday special cocktails, desserts, and entrées.

Whether you’re looking for a prix fixe meal with reserve wine and spirits selections, or you want to find a spot that’ll fill the younger members of your party with a little holiday cheer, we’ve got you covered.

Known for its Art Deco-inspired design and show-stopping caviar service, the Russian Tea Room is offering a special three-course prix fixe menu for $145 (and $95 for children 12 and under), featuring speciality dishes and restaurant classics. Christmas Day highlights include pan-seared Scallops with roasted butternut squash risotto; apricot-glazed duck with apple, cranberry, Brussels sprouts salad; and a creamy Hojicha-based crème brûlée. Their signature Afternoon Tea service will also be available from 11:30 am to 4:00 pm on Christmas.

What’s more festive than tucking into a fancy steak dinner on Christmas? Holiday specials at iconic steakhouse Quality Eats include the Après Ski Sangria, featuring red wine, mulling spices and winter citrus; Home Alone, with Angel’s Envy Bourbon, cinnamon and house spruce bitters; and Quality Nog, with Bacardi Ocho, Cognac and homemade spiced nog. Pair any of them with a dry-aged tomahawk ribsteak and 28-Day dry-aged porterhouse served with tableside truffle service.

If you’re looking for Christmas dinner to enjoy with a group, Limusina’s menu of coastal Mexican-inspired fare is especially shareable; think Serrano Guacamole, Flamed Queso Fundido and a rotating selection of seasonal plates prepared over live fire. The holiday cocktail menu includes the Après Ski Sangria, featuring red wine, mulling spices and winter citrus; Home Alone, with bourbon, cinnamon and house spruce bitters; and Quality Nog, blending Bacardi Ocho, Cognac and a delightful homemade spiced nog.

For Christmas Day, Marcus Samuelsson’s Red Rooster will be serving classic holiday favorites reimagined with a Red Rooster Harlem twist, all with a soulful and spirited celebration filled with music, comfort food, and community. Rooted in the vibrant and inclusive spirit of Harlem, Red Rooster Harlem is inspired by the neighborhood’s African American, Southern, and Caribbean culinary influences with exciting dishes like its signature Yardbird fried chicken, cornbread and gravy, shrimp and grits, oxtail meatloaf, and more.

At Le Crocodile, the cozy French bistro inside Williamsburg’s Wythe Hotel, celebrate Christmas Day with a special prix fixe menu. Dinner starts with hors d’oeuvres like Mushroom Pâté Tarts and Gruyère Cheese Puffs, followed by first-course options such as Beef Consommé with Black Truffle. Roast Beef & Jus and Baked Atlantic Halibut are served family-style with sides including maple-glazed carrots and Brussels sprouts with pancetta. Dessert options include Mont Blanc with Biscoff & Cranberry. Afterwards, head upstairs for a cocktail at Bar Blondeau, and enjoy sweeping views of a (quieter) city.

Recently reopened Koreatown barbecue destination will be open on Christmas Day. It's a luxe alternative to a traditional Christmas setting and a great option for a large group. Menu highlights like the Mother of Pearl Set—with abalone, U10 diver scallops and American wagyu brisket tips grilled at the table—along with the Platter of Nine Delicacies topped with a tin of caviar, will ensure the evening feels appropriately celebratory. Drinks at the downstairs cocktail bar Yangban will begin (or close out) the night on a festive note.

Grillhouse and oyster cellar from Sunday Hospitality (the team behind Rule of Thirds, Café Chelsea, Sunday in Brooklyn and more) is open for dinner service on Christmas Day from 2 PM to 8 PM. Located in the heart of Midtown Manhattan, menu highlights include Roast Bone Marrow, a riff on the classic served with housemade barbecue seasoning and a sweet, sticky glaze, Pork Tomahawk with spiced pork jus inspired by jerk flavors, and the famed Dynamo Burger, that can be ordered “drenched” with prime rib au jus served with beef fat fries.

On Christmas Day, enjoy Southern India with specialties like medhu vada (crispy lentil donuts with sambar) and Kerala beef chukka (boneless beef, coconut, shallots, garlic, and spices), along with Sri Lankan favorites like chicken lamprais served in a banana leaf package and kothu roti, a roadside specialty prepared from shredded paratha stir-fried with vegetables, onions, and egg. The beverage program features playful takes on South Indian classics, including the dosa martini, made with Jamun gin, St. Germain, and Lillet Blanc, and the palm paradise (coconut kallu, curry leaves, lychee cordial, and lemon cordial).

Take a trip through Punjab, Goa, Hyderabad, Madras, Calcutta, and Lucknow at Tamarind. For dinner, the menu features nizami keema (ground lamb with cinnamon, cloves, nutmeg, and lemon zest), paneer pasanda (fresh cheese in a rich cashew-onion sauce), and Achari Hiran Ki Chhampen (venison chops marinated in hung yogurt, pickling spices, and roasted chickpea flour).

One of the few restaurants in the city open for Christmas lunch as well as dinner, Indian fine dining destination Musaafer is serving up its crowd-favorite paneer tikka, eponymous daal Musaafer, coriander shrimp, and more. The highlight of the menu is a festive plum cake layered with black pepper and Old Monk rum crémeux, cranberry compote, and sudachi lime gelée, topped with delicate makhan malai (milk cream). It’s finished with almond-sugar snowflakes, gold and silver pearls, and a dusting of snow sugar.

Why not ring in the holiday with creations from one of the city’s best pastry chefs? At Lysée, chef Eunji Lee offers a seven-course ($130 per person) dessert tasting menu of savory and sweet pastries; menu highlights include Lysée’s signature Black truffle ice cream cone, made with black truffle ice cream and a slice of chocolate waffle cone and chocolate caramel, a Mont Blanc with cognac ice cream, a mini mushroom quiche, an Ossetra caviar tart, and more. Choose from four two-hour seatings available at 11:30 AM, 1:30 PM, 3:30 PM, and 5:30 PM, with an optional wine pairing.
