Gladbach rule out winter sale of 17-year-old Bayern, Leverkusen, Brighton and Liverpool target

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Wael Mohya will remain at Borussia Mönchengladbach for the rest of the season at least, sporting director Rouven Schröder has confirmed.

The 17-year-old has attracted interest from several top clubs, with Bayern Munich, Bayer Leverkusen, Brighton & Hove Albion and Liverpool reportedly among those monitoring his progress.

However, Gladbach have no intention of selling their prized asset midway through the season.

“A sale this winter is completely out of the question,” Schröder told Bild.

The sporting director also insisted that even a €50 million offer from Bayern Munich in January wouldn't change the club's stance.

“That won't happen. He is staying at Borussia; we are absolutely clear about that.”

Gladbach are reportedly working on extending Mohya's contract, which currently runs until 2028, in a bid to fend off interest from potential suitors and strengthen their negotiating position ahead of a potential future sale.

Mohya recently received his first senior Germany call-up, but was left out of the matchday squads for both the 3-1 win over Serbia and the 5-0 victory over the Netherlands.

His omission was purely down to squad restrictions, with Klopp permitted to select only 23 of the 24 called-up players for each game.
