Here’s Every Track Lisa Performed on Night 1 of Her Viva La Lisa Residency

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Lisa has finally touched down for the first date of her Viva La Lisa residency in Las Vegas. On November 12, the Blackpink rapper made history as the first K-pop act to perform a Las Vegas residency date at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace, and to celebrate the occasion, she hit the stage with several hits from her catalog.

Lisa opened up the show with her recently released single, “Tarantino,” and continued the rest of her 90-minute set with tracks from both of her eras, including her new six-track EP, Press Play. “Cobra,” “Sweat,” “V12,” “Cobra,” and “Cut U Off” were highlighted throughout the setlist, with “SaWaDiKa” closing the evening.

The rapper’s solo album Alter Ego also received its fair share of time throughout the setlist’s five chapters. “Badgrrrl” and “Fxck Up the World” were the first from the album to appear during the show, but deeper into the concert, fan-favorite tracks like “Rockstar,” “New Woman,” “Moonlit Floor,” and “Dream” also appeared in the program.

Longtime fans of Lisa were also treated to hits from the earliest days of her solo career. “Money,” her second independent single released in 2021, had its time to shine during the second chapter of her concert, though fans missed out on hearing her first solo track, “Lalisa,” on the setlist.

Lisa’s Viva La Lisa residency was announced as a four-show engagement but was upgraded to include five more dates due to overwhelming fan demand after the first set of dates sold out in 10 minutes. The remaining dates of the residency will take place on November 13, 14, and 27-29.
