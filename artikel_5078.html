Cardano Stablecoin DJED Faces Year End Shutdown, Here’s Date to Note

Cardano-based stablecoin DJED has announced its sunset, as DJED and SHEN services will wind down on December 31, 2026, at 11:59 pm UTC.

DJED represents a crypto-backed, overcollateralized stablecoin protocol on Cardano whose US-dollar peg is maintained algorithmically against an ADA reserve (with SHEN as the reserve coin), rather than by fiat reserves.

"DJED and SHEN services will wind down at the end of December 31, 2026, at 11:59 pm UTC," a DJED-focused X account wrote.

ATTN: DJED & SHEN Holders DJED and SHEN services will wind down at the end of December 31, 2026 at 11:59pm UTC. Please redeem and convert any DJED and SHEN you hold before then. All redemptions are subject to the reserve ratio.

Users are urged to redeem and convert any DJED and SHEN they hold before this deadline. Users are to redeem on the protocol and convert their DJED and SHEN back to ADA on the official Djed page. Users who hold DJED or SHEN in liquidity pools, lending markets, or other DeFi platforms are urged to withdraw those positions first, then redeem or swap.

The Djed team said all systems are fully operational and redemptions are now open, subject to the reserve ratio. It further added that the Djed protocol will be maintained and remain available until December 31, 2026. After this date, all remaining funds will be allocated to the outstanding DJED/SHEN holders once the wind-down is complete.

Djed’s shutdown comes almost three years after its launch, which came in 2023. The stablecoin was officially launched on the Cardano mainnet on January 31, 2023, and was developed by COTI in collaboration with the Cardano ecosystem to be used on DeFi protocols on Cardano as a stable alternative to volatile cryptocurrencies.

In positive news for the Cardano ecosystem, Monument Bank Limited has launched tokenized deposits on the Midnight network, marking a milestone for the network and the decentralized finance ecosystem.

At present, Monument is the first UK bank to tokenize retail deposits on a public blockchain, offering clients a 5.2% AER fixed-term return on savings directly through its existing banking app, delivering institutional onchain finance through a familiar and user-friendly banking experience.

Up to £250 million of client deposits will be represented on Midnight in this opening phase, with deposits recorded one-to-one in sterling.
