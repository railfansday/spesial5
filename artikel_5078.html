Bithumb halts CYBER deposits and withdrawals while trading stays open

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The South Korean exchange cited a suspected security issue and said it would credit deposits received during the halt in order once transfers resume.

Bithumb suspended deposits and withdrawals of the CYBER token after flagging signs of a possible security issue. The exchange said customers could still trade CYBER already held in their accounts. Kraken separately warned of possible delays for CYBER deposits sent over the Optimism network.

Bithumb set the cutoff for 2:18 a.m. Korea time on Nov. 22, or 17:18 UTC on Nov. 21. Seven minutes after the scheduled cutoff, the exchange confirmed that the pause was in effect. It also urged customers to take care when trading CYBER, saying the security concern could lead to sharper price swings.

The Nov. 21–22 exchange notices affected three different ways customers could use CYBER.

Bithumb said CYBER deposits received during the pause would be credited in sequence after service resumed.

For users who had already sent CYBER to Bithumb, the cutoff mattered because sending a deposit and seeing it credited to an account can happen at different times. Bithumb said network congestion or slow confirmations could hold up credit even for transfers sent before the cutoff. It said it would credit deposits received during the suspension in sequence once service resumed.

Customers who had requested a withdrawal before the cutoff and were still waiting for it to complete faced a separate process. Bithumb said it would run system checks and process those requests in sequence, which could delay the account update. The exchange’s processing note applied to requests already in its system when the suspension took effect. New CYBER withdrawals remained subject to the pause.

Bithumb said it would reopen transfers once the network was stable and would post the reopening in the same notice. It gave no reopening time.

Kraken flagged possible CYBER deposit delays on Optimism at 19:59 UTC on Nov. 21 and said its other funding methods were operating normally.

The incident page still listed the issue as identified at 18:12 UTC on Nov. 22, with no resolution posted. Kraken’s update left the cause and timing of a fix open.
