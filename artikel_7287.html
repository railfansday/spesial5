Tilt: Manager, Strategic Sourcing

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

At Tilt, we see a side of people that traditional lenders miss. Our mobile-first products and machine learning-powered credit models look beyond credit scores, using over 250 real-time financial signals to recognize real potential. With millions of customers worldwide, we're not just changing how people access financial products — we're creating a new credit system that backs the working, whatever they're working toward.

Tilt is hiring its first Strategic Sourcing lead, with the opportunity to establish strategic sourcing as a function and practice at the company. The core of the role is commercial judgment and hands-on negotiation: you'll quickly understand what each team needs, assess vendor economics, and secure the best pricing and terms across the US and our international markets in Latin America and Asia. You'll focus your time on negotiations and measurable savings, keeping requirements gathering and vendor selection proportionate to the decision. Our spend spans banking and payments, credit and identity data, cloud and software, marketing, servicing and collections, and contractors and professional services. In this senior, hands-on role, you'll partner closely with Compliance, Legal, and Finance, help prepare Tilt for an initial public offering (IPO), and typically run five or more sourcing projects at a time.

Lead supplier negotiations: Own negotiations from new contracts to renewals and usage-based pricing, using should-cost models, market benchmarks, and competing bids to win better pricing and terms.

Apply practical commercial judgment: Work with business owners to identify essential needs quickly, challenge unnecessary scope, and evaluate pricing, usage commitments, and total cost. Use targeted market checks or competitive bids where useful, and formal RFPs only when the complexity or value of the purchase warrants them.

Apply zero-based vendor budgeting: Partner with Finance and business owners to justify vendor spend from current needs, expected usage, and business value rather than carrying forward last year's budget. Challenge renewals and minimum commitments, and identify services to eliminate, consolidate, or right-size.

Set global category strategy: Manage spend by category across the US and our international markets, consolidate suppliers and negotiate global agreements where it lowers cost or risk, and set simple rules for small, one-off purchases.

Build a simple sourcing and vendor management process: Partner with Legal, Compliance, IT, and Finance to design an easy-to-use workflow from vendor intake and onboarding through ongoing reviews, renewals, and offboarding. Establish clear owners, approval thresholds, risk-based reviews, and turnaround expectations so teams can navigate legal, compliance, and IT requirements without unnecessary handoffs. Build a practical negotiation playbook and coach teams on how to buy well.

Make negotiated savings real: Partner with Finance and Accounts Payable on contract-to-invoice reviews, checking billed rates, usage, discounts, credits, and price increases against agreed terms. Resolve discrepancies with vendors, recover overcharges, and track realized savings against an agreed baseline.

Make procurement IPO-ready: Put in place public-company controls, including purchase approvals, segregation of duties, contract records, and audit trails that support Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) compliance.

Improve the tools and data: Contribute to Tilt's procure-to-pay (P2P) and contract lifecycle management (CLM) processes alongside Finance and Legal, keep contract and renewal records current, and use AI tools to speed up contract review and spend analysis.

6+ years in strategic sourcing or procurement, including at a high-growth technology, fintech, or financial services company; sourcing or management consulting experience is a plus

A track record of leading high-value negotiations directly with suppliers' senior leaders, with documented savings; you prepare thoroughly and know when to walk away

Strong business sense: you get to a team's real requirements quickly and can separate a must-have from a nice-to-have

Experience establishing a sourcing function from scratch or scaling an early one, and comfort working as a team of one

Global sourcing experience across countries, legal entities, and currencies, ideally in Latin America and Asia; Spanish or Portuguese is a plus

Experience partnering with Compliance, Legal, and Finance in a regulated industry such as lending, payments, or banking, and with procurement controls at a public or pre-IPO company

Deep knowledge of at least two of: software and cloud, data and identity, payments and banking, marketing, or contractors and professional services

Strong analytical skills (should-cost and total cost of ownership models), hands-on experience with P2P and CLM systems (e.g., Tropic, Zip, Ramp, Coupa, or Ironclad), and experience deploying AI tools in procurement

Ability to influence without authority and present clearly to executives; bachelor's degree required, with an MBA, CPSM, or CIPS a plus

Don’t meet every qualification? We care about potential over your past. If you're bringing ambition and drive to what we're building, we want to hear from you.

Impact is recognized: Growth opportunities follow your contributions, not rigid promotion timelines.
