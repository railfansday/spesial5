Manchester United XI vs Aston Villa: Confirmed team news, predicted lineup, injury latest for Premier League

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Manchester United will be hoping to welcome back a couple of stars when they welcome Aston Villa to Old Trafford on Sunday.

The Red Devils put a stop to their winless run on Tuesday with a dramatic 3-2 victory over Roma in the Champions League.

Matheus Cunha netted a late brace to turn the game on its head, with the hosts looking a beaten side at half time.

They turned things around in the second half when Bruno Fernandes tucked away a penalty at the second attempt, before Cunha was on hand to lash home with five minutes to play and secure a valuable three points for under-fire manager Michael Carrick.

Carrick had been under serious scrutiny following United’s worst ever start to a Premier League campaign, with his side languishing in 17th in the top-flight table.

Now, however, they will be looking to build some momentum after a much-needed win.

There could be returns for Marcus Rashford and Patrick Dorgu from injury, with the former on the comeback trail of a hip problem, while the latter was a surprise returner to training from a hamstring issue picked up on international duty.

As such, it could hand Carrick a more natural option at left-back, with Diogo Dalot filling in in that position in recent games.

There will also be no place in the squad for Lisandro Martinez, who serves his one-match suspension after his red card in the defeat at Chelsea.

It means that Senne Lammens will be shielded by a defence likely made up of Leny Yoro as a makeshift right-back once more, Harry Maguire and Ayden Heaven in the middle, and Dalot at left-back again with question marks over Dorgu’s sharpness.

Matthijs de Ligt and Noussair Mazraoui are also options in the middle and at full-back respectively.
