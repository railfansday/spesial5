Touching Baresi tribute and Kjaer’s return make Milan’s win over Frosinone more special

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

There were a couple of moments that made AC Milan’s night special on Sunday aside from the five goals against Frosinone.

Firstly, Fondazione Milan took centre stage at San Siro as Match Charity Partner of Milan-Frosinone, putting the stories of the people behind its projects at the heart of the pre-match.

Athletes with disabilities, students from its education programme and Paralympic footballers shared the pitch with Milan legends and first-team players, in a moment that showed how football can make a difference far beyond the pitch.

As part of the pre-match, a special moment before kick-off was dedicated to the late Franco Baresi, celebrating his bond with Fondazione Milan and the support he has given to its projects, with the participation of club employees and legend Daniele Massaro, who couldn’t hold back his tears.

Meanwhile, there was an old face present too as Simon Kjaer was in the stands. The former Rossoneri defender was a key figure in the team that won the Scudetto under coach Stefano Pioli in 2021-22, and he returned to support the team.

The Dane was spotted chatting with owner Gerry Cardinale on the sidelines before kick-off, while Matteo Gabbia – currently injured and expected to return to the squad in early 2027 – also arrived and gave him a hug.
