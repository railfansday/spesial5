Chelsea XI vs Leeds: Starting lineup, confirmed team news, injury latest for Premier League today

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Emi Martinez has reclaimed his place between the sticks as Chelsea host Leeds today.

The Blues return to action at Stamford Bridge on Saturday afternoon and Xabi Alonso was pondering whether to use Mike Penders or the fit-again Martinez.

Penders is seen as the long-term Chelsea No1 and deputised reasonably well while Martinez was out recovering following jaw surgery, but the latter is now back fit and over the international break broke the record as the goalkeeper with the most clean sheets in Argentina history. He has got the nod here.

Alonso still cannot call on any of Joao Pedro, Maxence Lacroix, Jorrel Hato or Reece James after ruling all four out of the Leeds game at his pre-match press conference on Friday.

Chelsea do have Josh Acheampong available - he starts - while Jamie Gittens is now back in training but not yet fit enough for the matchday squad.

Moises Caicedo is fit enough to start, while Estevao is also handed a chance to impress in the front four along with Morgan Rogers, Cole Palmer and Emmanuel Emegha, who makes his full Chelsea debut.

The 18-year-old defender Olutayo Subuloye has joined the senior squad and makes the bench here, alongside the likes of Marco Palestra, Jordan Henderson, Pedro Neto and Geovany Quenda.

Confirmed Chelsea XI vs Leeds (3-4-2-1): Martinez; Acheampong, Fofana, Colwill, Chavarria, Lavia, Caicedo, Estevao, Palmer, Rodgers, Emegha

Subs: Penders, Suboloye, Gusto, Palestra, Barco, Henderson, Neto, Quenda, Welbeck

Injured: Joao Pedro, James, Lacroix, Hato

Time and date: 3pm GMT on Saturday November 21, 2026
