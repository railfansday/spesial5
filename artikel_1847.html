‘It’s a privilege’: Mauricio Pochettino’s first words as Manchester United manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Mauricio Pochettino has spoken for the first time after officially starting work as Manchester United manager.

Pochettino was announced as the new Man United head coach last week, replacing Michael Carrick, but his arrival at Carrington had been delayed due to finishing up with the United States Men’s National Team [USMNT].

The Argentine bowed out of international management by overseeing a 2-0 win for USA against Haiti in the early hours on Wednesday.

After fulfilling his post-match media obligations, Pochettino flew directly from Cincinnati to the UK, touching down at Manchester airport before midday and then travelling to Carrington.

November 18 was always intended to be the 54-year-old’s start date and in his first interview in Man Utd colours, Pochettino said: “Very excited to be here, it's an honour, it's a privilege to be in this amazing club.

"Mauricio Pochettino has officially started work as Manchester United head coach, marking the start of a dream role for the Argentinian," a club statement reads.

"Pochettino’s appointment was announced last Friday with an agreement he’ll begin on 18 November, once he had fulfilled his duties with the US Men’s National Team.

"As Mauricio explained during a press conference last week, it was important for him to maintain complete focus on two remaining fixtures with USMNT.

"Pochettino oversaw two excellent wins over Haiti in the CONCACAF quarter finals. He then boarded a direct flight from Cincinnati to Manchester on Wednesday morning.

"Eager to get started, the 54-year-old travelled straight to Carrington to tour our training complex, meet a selection of players and staff, and begin work as our new head coach."
