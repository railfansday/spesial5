Chelsea snatch late winner vs PSG to move top of WCL table

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Chelsea Women vs Paris Saint-Germain Women. Women's Champions League.

Match report as Chelsea snatched a late 1-0 win against Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League; Veerle Buurman bundled her effort home from close range in the 88th minute; Chelsea now move to the top of the Champions League table with one league phase game to play

Veerle Buurman's late winner snatched a late 1-0 win for Chelsea against Paris Saint-Germain to move them to the top of the Champions League table.

Sonia Bompastor's side looked as if they were heading to back-to-back goalless draws, following the 0-0 draw with BK Hacken last time out in Europe, until Buurman was able to bundle her effort home at the near post in the 88th minute.

The hosts had been the dominant side throughout but were thwarted by Katarzyna Kiedrzynek on more than one occasion.

Alyssa Thompson had come close to breaking the deadlock just before the break after seeing her initial shot and follow-up effort smothered by Kiedrzynek.

The corner for Buurman's timely winner arrived after the PSG goalkeeper got down well to her right to keep Sjoeke Nusken's header out.

Lucy Bronze's delivery and a slight touch on the way through sparked panic among the PSG defence and it was Buurman who reacted first to sweep home the winner, sending her side to the top of the table with one league phase game to go.

The Blues remain unbeaten in Europe, winning three of their five games so far, and are well placed to qualify for the last 16 through the top four automatic qualification spots. They face OH Leuven in their final game, where they will go into it as strong favourites.

Chelsea boss Sonia Bompastor speaking to UEFA:

"I like the resilience from my team. Believing until the end of the game we could score that important goal and it was really important for us to win tonight.

"What I like in my team and squad is the fact they are really flexible; they are really open-minded to changing systems sometimes, adapting to what is happening on the pitch.

"They understand really quickly. I think it was good for us to be able to change, to go to a back four. We wanted to have the width to destabilise them more than we did in the first half."

On her side potentially finishing in the top four: "We knew we had two games left and getting two wins will help us to probably secure top four.

"So we still have things under control and that was why it was really important for us to win."

Chelsea defender Veerle Buurman speaking to Disney:

"It was really important we scored the goal. Winning and three points was really important, especially after the last Champions League game where we drew against Hacken.

"A good team performance. We tried really hard and that I can score a goal is really important for the team."

Chelsea midfielder Keira Walsh speaking to UEFA:

"We spoke about it before the game about wanting to finish the evening on top. We are in a good spot going into the last game.

"It was a massive win. It was tough for us tonight. They defended really well and they're aggressive.

"It was a difficult game physically but to get the win was really important. Hopefully it will push us into the top four."
