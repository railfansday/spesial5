New drinkable croissant turns breakfast pastry into a bottled latte

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

A Japanese beverage brand is releasing a new drink that pairs buttery pastry flavors of croissants with coffee, and it’s just as unique as it sounds.

Croissants and coffee are a staple pairing, especially for those who like a tasty breakfast or light lunch. However, have you ever wished you could capture the flavour profiles of both and enjoy them as a drink? No, me neither, but that hasn’t stopped Japanese drink maker Suntory from giving it a good old go.

Enter the Craft Boss Croissant Latte, a coffee-based drink that’s been designed to recreate the taste of enjoying a croissant and cafe latte all in one drink.

Developed in coordination with a Japanese bakery (Bread, Espresso &), the latte uses lightly roasted coffee beans with fruity notes alongside espresso ingredients. Milk and sugar provide the sweetness, while added salt helps recreate the rich taste of a croissant.

As for the pastry element, Suntory developed a flavoring designed to capture a croissant’s buttery aroma and toasted notes. Each 450ml bottle has a retail price of ¥270, which is around $1.70.

Those who prefer an actual croissant with their coffee can also try a matching menu item at participating Bread, Espresso & locations across Japan from November 10 to December 10. Priced at ¥900 (around $5.70), the cafe version features a croissant caramelized with honey butter and comes served with a foamy cafe latte blended with cream for dipping.

Interestingly, the drinkable croissant coffee isn’t the only eye-catching food item to come out of Japan this year. Back in May, Pizza Hut Japan revealed a “drinkable pizza” that combines melted cheese and rich curry sauce. This was then followed by a spicy noodle pizza that came topped with pork and cheese.
