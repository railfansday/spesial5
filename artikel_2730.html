European MMA show plunged into chaos as main event cancelled due to backstage injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A European MMA show ended in catastrophe when its main event was cancelled at the 11th hour due to a backstage injury to the defending champion.

Mateusz Legierski was due to defend his lightweight title against veteran fighter Ivan Buchinger at Oktagon 98 but tore his hamstring in the warm-up, forcing the headline bout to be scrapped.

"Unfortunately, the Oktagon 98 main event will not take place," a statement read.

"Mateusz Legierski suffered an injury during his warm-up and was rushed to the hospital. He tore his hamstring and was forced to pull out of the bout."

Legierski was filmed being tended to by medics in his dressing room, where he was put onto a stretcher before being taken out of the Werk Arena in Trinec, Czech Republic.

Buchinger, who was preparing for his 57th professional fight, was then called out to the cage in his fight attire to give his reaction.

"This never happened to me," Buchinger said. "Very disappointed, I was very well prepared, but unfortunately we have to come to terms with this situation.

"I wish him a speedy recovery and hope this match will take place after that."

Asked whether he would accept another bout quickly or wait for another title fight, Buchinger said: "I am ready. I have been training hard for this match to get the belt.

"I think I was very well prepared even though the [weight] classes were what they were. I came to get that belt."

The cancellation meant 24-year-old prospect Jakub Batfalsky's split-decision win over Ayton De Paepe ended up closing the curtain on the event.

Oktagon holds the record for the most-attended MMA event outside of Japan in history and attracted 58,000 fans to its marquee show of the year at Eintracht Frankfurt's Deutsche Bank Park in September.
