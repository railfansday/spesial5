Joe Milton: Eagles don't quit, so happy to be on this team

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Six weeks ago, Joe Milton was on the Cowboys’ practice squad. On Thanksgiving, Milton led the Eagles to a win over the Cowboys in one of the most memorable Thanksgiving performances in NFL history.

Milton was outstanding in leading the Eagles from a 21-point deficit to a 39-38 win, completing 21 of 31 passes for 331 yards, with three passing touchdowns and a 15-yard rushing touchdown.

“A lot of emotions, but just mainly so happy for this team,” Milton said. “This team never let up. These guys never dropped their heads. Everybody just kept saying, ‘We’re good. Keep doing what we’re doing.’ As a quarterback, that’s what you want to hear, especially when you’re the new guy on the team and you’ve got a whole bunch of guys that you’re not so familiar with. These guys don’t quit. That’s one thing I love about this team.”

Milton said he still loves his former teammates on the Cowboys. When a reporter told Milton that it looked like at one point he was gloating toward the Cowboys’ sideline, Milton said he didn’t remember that but has nothing but respect for the people in Dallas, even as he was celebrating a victory over them.

“If I did, then I’m sorry, but I was just in the moment. There’s no other explanation for that,” Milton said. “It was just great coming back here and playing in this stadium, in Jerry World, and seeing the guys I worked hard with in camp and OTAs. But it’s about the Eagles.”
