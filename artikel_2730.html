Bengals rule out Dalton Risner, list Dax Hill and Dylan Fairchild as questionable

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Bengals may be without both of their starting guards on Monday night.

They have ruled right guard Dalton Risner out for their game against the Commanders due to injuries to both of his knees. Risner did not practice at all this week.

Left guard Dylan Fairchild (oblique) was limited for two days before moving up to full participation Saturday. He’s listed as questionable for Monday.

Cornerback Dax Hill (illness) didn’t practice all week, but a questionable tag leaves the door open for him to play. Safety Kyle Dugger (quad) is the only other player on the active roster listed as questionable.

Linebacker Swayze Bozeman (forearm) and guard Brian Parker (knee) are listed as questionable to come off injured reserve. The Bengals opened a roster spot for one of them by placing linebacker Oren Burks on injured reserve.

Wide receiver Andrei Iosivas (thumb) and cornerback Jalen Davis (hamstring) are also on injured reserve, but are listed as doubtful to return this week.
