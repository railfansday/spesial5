Know Your Foe: Oklahoma Sooners vs. Texas A&M Aggies

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Oklahoma Sooners will be at home again in Week 12, with a record of 6-4 overall and 4-3 in the SEC. Head coach Brent Venables and his team won consecutive games for the first time all season last week, and they're looking to make it three straight victories this week.

OU's season began with a win over UTEP, which was followed by a loss to Michigan. The Sooners beat New Mexico, but then lost to Georgia. After the bye week, Oklahoma beat Texas, but then lost to Kentucky. A win over Mississippi State was followed up by a loss to South Carolina. Then the Sooners defeated Florida and Ole Miss in succession, setting up this weekend.

Saturday will see the Texas A&M Aggies come to Norman. They went 11-2 last season, winning 11 straight games to open the regular season, before losing to Texas in the finale and Miami at home in the first round of the College Football Playoff.

Head coach Mike Elko is in his third season in College Station, after the Aggies hired him away from his post as Duke's head coach following the 2023 season to replace Jimbo Fisher. Before his two years with the Blue Devils, Elko had been on Fisher's staff at Texas A&M as the defensive coordinator for four years. He's held the same role at both Wake Forest and Notre Dame in the last decade.

Elko's Aggies are struggling in 2026, entering this game with a 4-6 overall record and a 1-6 mark in the SEC. The year started well, with wins over Missouri State (50-0) and Arizona State (48-20), but losses to Kentucky (31-21) and LSU (35-6) followed. The Aggies beat Arkansas (34-7), lost to Missouri (27-6), and defeated The Citadel (41-0), but have now lost three straight games. Texas A&M fell to Alabama (31-20), South Carolina (17-9), and Tennessee (22-7) over the last few weeks.

Both schools are enduring disappointing seasons after playoff trips last year, but where the Sooners have found something over the last two weeks, the Aggies have seen a rough year get rougher over the last three games.

On offense, the struggling Aggies are led by offensive coordinator Holmon Wiggins. He'll be matched up against Venables, who calls the plays for OU's defense. However, Venables said after last week's win that it's a "collaboration" with his defensive staff when it comes to play-calling, after some believed he wasn't calling the plays late in the game against the Rebels. Venables' top assistant on defense is Todd Bates, who coaches OU's defensive tackles.

Wiggins was Texas A&M's wide receivers coach for the last two years, after serving in the same capacity at Alabama for five seasons before coming to Aggieland. Wiggins was promoted after former OC Collin Klein left to become Kansas State's head coach.

The Aggies have been poor offensively with Wiggins at the controls this season, falling off dramatically from last year. Marcel Reed has been the starting quarterback all year, but Texas A&M appears to be making a change to backup Brady Hart as the starter this week. He's less of a dual-threat than Reed is, but the Aggies are hoping he can give the unit a spark.

Running back Reuben Owens II is the team's leading rusher, but Texas A&M has a number of backs who have earned carries. K.J. Edwards, Carsyn Baker, and Jamarion Morrow have all scored multiple touchdowns this season. Wide receivers Isaiah Horton, Mario Craver, and Aaron Gregory are the players to watch in the passing game this week.

On the other side of the ball, OU interim offensive coordinator Jason Witten will be matched up against a very strong unit that is the strength of Texas A&M's team. Witten has been solid since taking over the offense after Venables made the decision to fire former offensive coordinator Ben Arbuckle. On the other sideline, Elko has a deep defensive background, and he began the season calling the plays. However, he handed those duties off to defensive coordinator Lyle Hemphill midseason. Elijah Robinson is the co-defensive coordinator for the Aggies.

Hemphill was promoted to DC ahead of this season after former DC Jay Bateman left for the same role and control of the defense at Kentucky. Hemphill has a long history with Elko, but he was James Madison's defensive coordinator in 2024. Robinson was in College Station for the Fisher era, but spent the last two seasons at Syracuse as the defensive coordinator. He's back working under Elko for his second stint in 2026.

In just his third collegiate game as a play-caller, Witten will be trying to make quarterback John Mateer comfortable against everything Elko, Hemphill, and Robinson are going to throw at him. Defensive end Marco Jones leads the Aggies in sacks, while linebacker Ray Coney and safety Dalton Brooks are the squad's leading tacklers. OU's offense has found an identity over the last two weeks, and it has consisted of a strong commitment to running the ball, dominating time of possession, and setting Mateer up to do what he does best. It's unlikely that the plan is going to change that much, especially in a game featuring two good defenses that figures to be low-scoring.

OU special teams coordinator Doug Deakin will face off against Texas A&M special teams coordinator Patrick Dougherty this week. Dougherty was with Elko at Duke, and he followed him to Texas A&M ahead of the 2024 season. Texas A&M has had strong special teams under Dougherty, while Deakin's unit did not have a good day last week. Kicker Tate Sandell has been inconsistent this year, and he missed two field goals against the Rebels. OU also had a punt partially blocked, and muffed a late onside kick. Some of that was due to the poor weather in the re-scheduled Sunday afternoon game against Ole Miss, but with rain in the forecast in Norman again this week, Oklahoma's special teams unit has to play a lot better than they did last week to win this game.

Of course, Oklahoma and Texas A&M were former Big 12 foes, and they're facing off for the first time as SEC foes this season. OU owns a 19-12 series lead, and the Sooners have an 11-3 record against the Aggies since 1999. The last meeting between the two teams came in the Cotton Bowl for the 2012 season, with Texas A&M dominating in a 41-13 win. The most recent meeting as conference opponents and the most recent meeting in Norman was in 2011, a game OU won 41-25.

Oklahoma has had some iconic moments against Texas A&M, such as linebacker Torrance Marshall's game-winning pick-six in 2000 in College Station, saving the eventual-champion Sooners in a 35-31 win. In 2003 in Norman, OU won 77-0, completely overwhelming the Aggies to the point that they had to stop scoring in order to not run it up on Texas A&M.

Neither team has had the season they expected to have this year, and neither team has put a very functional offense on the field for the majority of this season. However, the Sooners have become bowl-eligible with impressive wins over the last two weeks, and they've found something that has been working for them on offense. Both teams hang their hat on their defense, with two defensive-minded head coaches at the helm. This game has the potential to be a rock fight, and it's hard to see a lot of points being scored on Saturday.

Texas A&M needs to win its final two games to get to bowl eligibility, and with three straight losses and just one SEC win, the Aggies are desperate, and their backs are against the wall. Oklahoma is looking to find a strong finish to a rocky season, and with bowl eligibility already accomplished, the focus turns to making a better bowl game with wins down the stretch.

The Aggies defense makes this game dangerous, as it's the best unit the Witten-led OU offense has seen so far. However, that offense should get its fair share of chances, as Oklahoma's defense certainly has the ability to cause problems for Texas A&M's offense.

Anytime you're playing a wounded and hungry team, the potential for an unexpected result is always there, especially against a roster as talented as Texas A&M's. On the other hand, if Oklahoma can continue its better play, the third-most wins in the Venables era could be accomplished this week. Winning out and getting to eight victories would look much better than finishing with six or seven victories, but the Sooners can't win eight games until they win a seventh game. It isn't the season anyone at OU wanted, but eight wins (and four straight) would signal definite improvement after a 4-4 record through the first two months.

Kickoff for Oklahoma-Texas A&M is scheduled for 2:45 p.m. CT in Norman on Saturday on ESPN2.
