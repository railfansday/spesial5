Packers vs. Rams Week 12 Game Discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

One of the most meaningful games of the last two months of the Green Bay Packers' season is about to kick off. Micah Parsons is back. It's a nationally televised game (albeit on a streaming service) in a standalone time slot.

Tonight, the Packers face the Los Angeles Rams in a Week 12 game that will have a significant impact on the NFC postseason picture. The winner will take a big step forward towards a playoff spot, leapfrogging the other and claiming a critical head-to-head tiebreaker. The two teams are currently tied at 6-4, joined at that record by the Seattle Seahawks, with the Packers holding the tiebreaker for the moment, but that will all change by the end of this evening.

The Packers have had the Rams' number in recent years, however, carrying a 5-game winning streak in the head-to-head series. Will Matt LaFleur be able to carry forward that success against his friend and old boss, Sean McVay, or will the Rams be able to overcome the absence of top receivers Puka Nacua and Davante Adams to earn a big win on the eve of Thanksgiving Day?

Tune in to find out and join us in the comments to discuss the game. Go Pack Go!
