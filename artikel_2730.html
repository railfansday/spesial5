Mosley baseball pitcher Ryan Harless signs with Louisville

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Mosley baseball standout Ryan Harless will continue his career at the University of Louisville after signing with the Cardinals Thursday afternoon.

Harless, who has been committed to Louisville for over a year, said he was excited to make the opportunity official.

Mosley head baseball coach and athletic director Doug Lee praised Harless' impact on the Dolphins, pointing to his leadership, vand work ethic throughout his high school career.

"Every team that I've been a part of that Ryan's been a part of, he's been the ace of that staff," Lee said. "He's always led the team on the mound, had a presence, and honestly was always an offensive threat."

Harless put together a standout junior season, hitting .398 with a .500 on-base percentage, 18 RBIs and one home run. On the mound, he went 10-1 with a 0.41 ERA and 121 strikeouts in 67 2/3 innings. Harless was voted the Bay County Baseball Player of the Year last season.

He also went his first 45 innings of the season without allowing an earned run and recorded 42 consecutive scoreless innings.

"His first 45 innings on the mound last year, he gave up zero earned runs," Lee said. "I don't know if I've ever seen a high school pitcher do that."

Harless' performance earned him the Florida Dairy Farmers 2026 Class 5A Baseball Player of the Year award.

Lee said Harless' success is a reflection of the work he puts in beyond scheduled practices.

"I think the biggest thing that I see in Ryan that I think has led him to this moment is just work ethic," Lee said. "He is constantly always doing stuff."

Harless credited his time at Mosley and the relationships he has developed with the coaching staff for helping elevate his game.

"That's kind of elevated my game because I've been playing for guys that I've loved for a long time," Harless said. "Watching them compete and playing for them has really elevated my game."

Before heading to Louisville, Harless has one more season with the Dolphins. His focus is on making the most of his senior year.

"I want to try to win as many games as I can and win a state championship before I go to college," Harless said.
