Mike Macdonald praises Tory Horton's "tremendous play" on late kickoff return

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

After the Raiders cut the Seahawks’ lead to 21-13 by kicking a field goal with 2:04 left in Sunday’s game, they wanted to force a touchback that would allow them to use the two-minute warning as a third timeout in hopes of getting the ball back.

Seahawks wide receiver Tory Horton made sure that didn’t happen. Horton perfectly timed a leap that allowed him to bat a kickoff that was sailing long into the end zone. Horton stepped out of bounds upon coming down, so he motioned for Rashid Shaheed to return it into the field of play and Shaheed was tackled with 1:59 left to accomplish the Seahawks’ goal of burning the Raiders’ extra chance to stop the clock.

Horton was flagged for an illegal bat of the ball, but the Raiders declined the penalty as it would have forced a re-kick that took more time off the clock and head coach Mike Macdonald praised Horton’s quick thinking in his postgame press conference.

“It was a tremendous play,” Macdonald said, via the team’s website. “Tory’s actually trying to catch that ball, albeit with one hand. Just an incredible play. With George Holani going down, we had to take [Jadarian Price] off of kickoff returns, so those are guys that haven’t necessarily practiced a bunch in a while, especially with this game plan. So to be able to step in and be able to execute some of those critical situations is some great stuff by them, great job by our coaches, and it really helped us close the game out. . . . We told them that was the situation going. Now, there’s a difference between catching it 8 yards deep in the end zone and making a play like that, which is just some really, really great stuff.”

The Seahawks were likely to win the game regardless of how the kickoff played out, but Horton made closing things out easier and his effort will be on the minds of many people around the league if they find themselves in a similar situation.
