Aaron Rodgers throws for 227 yards, TD vs. Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers completed 30 of 44 passes for 227 yards, one touchdown and no interceptions in Week 11 against the Philadelphia Eagles, while actually leading the team with 29 yards on five attempts.

Why it Matters: Going to go out on a limb, and say that this might be the last time Rodgers leads his team in rushing. He was ultra safe, with mostly dinks and dunks in the passing game. Rodgers remains more of a QB2 in most fantasy leagues heading into Week 12 against the Denver Broncos on Friday.
