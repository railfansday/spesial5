Michigan State football seniors talk loss to Oregon

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Michigan State football lost their final home game of the season on Friday night, losing to the No. 13 ranked Oregon Ducks, 45-7. The outcome was less than ideal from the Spartans, who now fall to 3-8 on the 2026 season.

After the game, Michigan State senior leaders Trent Fraley and Kenny Soares Jr. spoke to the media, giving their comments on the outcome on senior day.

See what the MSU seniors said about the loss to Oregon:

Soares Jr.: "Dion (Crawford), he is a dawg. He's the type of dude that always wants to be on the field, always make a hit, always make an impact. If you can't be on the field, I got your back. He's not by himself, things go wrong, you get hurt, I've been there."

Fraley: "You know you have to bring your all every play, you have to do everything you can to win your one-on-one with him,"

Fraley: "It's super special to see how far we both have came along. It's cool to be out there one last time and take it in with your family at Spartan Stadium."

Soares Jr.: "(Pat Fitzgerald will do) tremendous things (to the program). I'm blessed, I'll do anything for him. He's done it, he's been there, he's that type of coach. When I come back to Spartan Stadium, I know it is going to be great."
