Germie Bernard finishes with team-leading 58 yards

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Pittsburgh Steelers WR Germie Bernard caught one pass for 58 yards against the Denver Broncos in Week 12. He was targeted two times during the game.

Why It Matters: Bernard was able to take a short pass 58 yards early in the game to help the team score their first touchdown. He will probably see more targets over the next few weeks and can be considered a No. 5 receiver in fantasy leagues next week against the Houston Texans.
