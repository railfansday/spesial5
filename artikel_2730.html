Busters for Raiders Week 11 vs Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

We move on from the Ballers to the Busters for this week. Those who were most responsible for the team falling behind 17-0 early and ultimately losing 38-24.

A first down pass got the Raiders moving on their second possession. And a holding penalty on Glaze two plays later gave most of it right back and led to a punt. It wasn't until midway through the second quarter that the Raiders would have another first down play. That was followed up by the big Jeanty 46-yard run that put the Raiders in first and goal at the four-yard-line. And on the next play, Glaze would give up a run stuff for no gain. The Raiders would end up settling for a field goal to make it a 17-3 game.

With 1:17 left in the second quarter, the Raiders got the ball with a shot to tie it up. But on the first play, Glaze was driven into the backfield to collapse the pocket and Mendoza was sacked, killing the drive before it even started. He would give up one more sack on the final drive, just for good measure.

Believe it or not, the Raiders were only down 3-0 at the end of the first quarter. But the Broncos were in first and goal, looking to grow their lead. They would end up in third and goal from the four and may have been stopped there, but Stokes was flagged for pass interference in the end zone, giving them first and goal at the one. They scored on the next play.

The very first play of the next drive, Stokes gave up a 24-yard catch. And they would keep it going to score another touchdown. Late in the third quarter, with the score tied at 17-17, the Broncos lined up in third-and-seven from the Vegas 22-yard-line. And even with Maxx Crosby getting through to hit Bo Nix, he still completed the pass with Stokes in coverage for 18 yards. They scored the go-ahead TD on the next play.

With Tonka Hemingway missing the game and several others getting lost to injury throughout, it was up to Booker to step up to the plate. Instead, he didn't record a single stat in 22 snaps. He had consecutive runs go through his position that gave the Broncos first and goal on their first TD drive and was handled on the block on the four-yard TD run that put the Broncos up for good late in the third quarter.

One catch on five targets for Nailor. He and Mendoza just cannot seem to get on the same page. Not all of the issues were on him. But it can't be a coincidence that so many passes his direction either go to a place where Mendoza thought Nailor would be or should be or over his head, perhaps because he couldn't get open. And these issues happened with Cousins too, so it's can't just be a matter of a rookie QB.

The first score of the game was because Ward got beat to give up a punt block. The Broncos got the ball already in scoring range and got on the board with a field goal. Can't afford to be gifting points like that if you're the Raiders.
