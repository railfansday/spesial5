Outmarket AI: Head of Product Marketing

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Outmarket AI · Remote (US) · Full-time · Reports to the SVP of Marketing

Insurance still runs on manual work. Most policies are sold through agents and brokers, and the people who know the work best are buried in the documents: checking policies line by line, comparing quotes, building proposals, on nights and weekends. We think that's backwards. The best people in insurance should spend their time advising clients.

Outmarket, the AI platform built for insurance, makes that possible. Account teams drop in their documents and get back finished work to review, with every finding linked to the page it came from. Outmarket connects to the systems they already use. The judgment stays with the person; the groundwork doesn't.

It's working. More than 10,000 people at over 300 agencies use Outmarket, including 30 of the top 100 U.S. insurance brokers. Most of those agencies found us through other customers. We raised a $34.5M Series B led by SignalFire four months after announcing our Series A because customers were pulling us forward faster than we planned.

Customers who already tell the story. People who use Outmarket get emotional about it, and many have said so on the record.

A product with a real edge. Most agencies still choose between outsourcing the work and grinding through it. We built a third option: the AI does the work, and the judgment stays with the person who has it.

Ground floor. You'll be the first product marketer, joining alongside a new Head of Growth and Head of Content. You'll decide how an industry hears about this for the first time.

A direct line. You'll work with the SVP of Marketing, the CRO, customers and founders who built product and go-to-market at Uber, Meta, Ethos and IBM Watson. You'll see your work show up in the business within weeks.

You own how Outmarket is described to brokers, to the sales team, to the market, and to the analysts and press who will shape the category. You'll pick up positioning work that's already in motion, test it with brokers and sales, and own how the story changes as the product and the market move. You're the first product marketer, so you do the work yourself before you build a team. We expect to add one or two product marketers in 2027.

Own one core narrative and adapt it for each buyer, segment and channel: website, sales deck, talk tracks and in-product messaging.

Build and maintain the sales toolkit: pitch deck, competitive battlecards, objection handling, demo narrative and one-pagers by segment.

Own competitive intelligence and turn it into GTM insights and sales enablement materials.

Turn verified results into value messaging and business cases buyers can take to their partners.

Write the content buyers use to evaluate AI for their brokerage

Run product launches with the product team: naming, packaging, launch messaging, enablement and customer communication.

Partner with sales leadership on enablement, both at kickoff and on a regular cadence, and measure whether the story lands in real deals.

Talk with customers, prospects and lost deals all the time, and be the voice of the broker inside the company.

Inform pricing and packaging with buyer research and competitive data.

5+ years of experience in product marketing. You've owned positioning for a product end to end and can show the before and after.

You're an excellent writer. Your headlines are short, your decks are clear, and sales reps actually use what you make.

You're comfortable with executives who have strong opinions, and you change their minds with customer evidence.

You have startup experience at Series A to C and know what it means to be the first person in a function.

You use AI in your own work and can say where it helps, where it fails and how you check it.

High agency: you find the work instead of waiting for it.

Ownership: you own the outcome, not a task list.

Startup pace: we ship weekly instead of planning quarterly.

You don't need to know insurance. You'll spend your first month in agencies and on calls with producers. Curiosity is the requirement, and becoming fluent is part of the job.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
