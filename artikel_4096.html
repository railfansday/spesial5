GTA 6 Smashes Multiple Records And Earns Xbox Series S Praise In First 24 Hours

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The most affordable way to play GTA 6 impresses

Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 has reportedly smashed all sorts of records within 24 hours of launch, and it's off to a great start on Xbox Series consoles.

As shared by GTA 6 Countdown, GTA 6 has launched to a near-perfect 99 score on Metacritic (151 reviews), apparently sold 24.5m copies in under 24 hours, and seen 2 million Xbox players take a trip to New Zealand to play the game early.

Sensor Tower's sales tracker also estimates $2.36bn (£1.8bn) in revenue from GTA 6 sales, with the console split mostly in favour of PS5, at a 75% to 25% share over Xbox. Still, the data suggests Xbox still took a whopping $588m from 6.15m units sold.

GTA 6 dominated Twitch viewership too, with 50m hours watched and 3.3m viewers during its busiest hour, beating the livestreaming site's previous record (held by Hogwarts Legacy at 1.2m) by more than double.

Beyond all the big numbers we're chucking your way, we've also spotted a lot of praise for the Xbox Series S version of the game. Whilst Digital Foundry was hit-and-miss on the report, the general response online is majorly positive.

It's good news, then, to finally put to bed the lingering question of whether GTA 6 would run well enough on Xbox Series S consoles. Judging by IGN's comparison, it's not all that different of an experience to Sony's flagship PS5 Pro.
