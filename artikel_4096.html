Free-to-Play Pirate Game Sea of Remnants Sails Closer with PS5 Closed Beta Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Remember Sea of Remnants? Announced for PS5 last year during a summer State of Play, we've yet to see very much of the actual game so far, but it seems it won't be long before we can actually play it for ourselves.

That's because a closed beta test is scheduled for 3rd December 2026, and console platforms are being included for the first time. A select number of players will be able to check it out when it opens at 4pm PT on that date.

Yes, you can pre-register via the official website and be in with a chance of taking part in the test.

The news comes alongside a new cinematic trailer, which certainly has the visual flair present in the game's other showings so far.

Included in the closed beta will be a total of 21 playable characters.

"Players can experiment with different team compositions and turn-based combat strategies, from pursuit-focused combinations to area-of-effect (AoE) damage builds," reads a press release. "Character development and build customization offer further opportunities to refine tactics and tackle increasingly challenging encounters."

Participants will also have access to two regions, the White Sea and the Bridge Sea, upon which you can sail a pair of ship types.

I'll be honest, I've been very curious to play this since its announcement, as I still don't fully understand what the game even is. Fingers crossed I get into the beta, then.
