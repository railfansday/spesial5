'The Biggest Launch In WoW History' - Xbox's Latest First-Party Game Is Already Smashing Records

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Bodes well for that eventual console release, eh?

We've talked quite a bit recently about the launch of World of Warcraft: Forever on PC and how it may end up being the biggest success of the year from an Xbox first-party perspective, and it seems that could actually be the case.

Its PC launch happened back on November 4th, and according to the folks at The Game Business, it's already broken multiple records for the series, including the biggest launch in WoW history based on peak concurrent players.

Here's a look at those records that have been broken:

Many people might overlook the success of WoW: Forever for Team Xbox simply because it's not shipping on consoles (at least not yet), but we reckon Xbox CEO Asha Sharma will extremely pleased by the game's success thus far!

It does seem to bode well for an Xbox Series X|S and/or Xbox Project Helix version in the future too - there's seemingly little point leaving money on the table when the new game is already optimised for controllers as it is.

Obviously it's still early days for WoW: Forever and the initial success will inevitibly drop off, but if they can retain enough interest in the months and years to come, Xbox will have an excellent new money spinner on their hands.
