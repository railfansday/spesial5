A map to the Lost Woods in Zelda Ocarina of Time Remake is something that can really help players navigate this maze-like location, as well as an explanation of what this realm is and how you're supposed to explore it. The Lost Woods in OoT is formed out of a series of crossroads, but taking the wrong path will spit you out back at the Kokiri Forest village and you'll have to begin again. However, if you can understand it, the Lost Woods is full of connections to the Zora's Domain, the Goron City, and more besides, acting as a network that can take you between distant regions fairly quickly.Below I'll explain how to explore the Lost Woods in Ocarina of Time, including a full map and guide to what you can find there. Keep in mind that some of these routes won't be usable straight away, as they require items that you'll find later on in the game.

The Lost Woods are full of dark tunnels that lead you back to Kokiri Forest if you pick them. To avoid these, you need to listen for the music and go through the tunnel that it's most clearly coming from.

If you choose right, there'll be an aura of green musical notes as you get close, as shown above. These mark the path that will lead you to the Sacred Forest Meadow and Grove, and thus the essential pathway through the Lost Woods.
