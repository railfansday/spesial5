10 Best Early Game Weapons in GTA VI

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Shane is a Contributor for DualShockers writing guides and other materials. He has a degree in Game Design and Criticism.

In Vice City, you can never come too prepared for a fight. Between the nightlife and our protagonist's penchant for causing chaos, it’s a good idea to stay strapped in case of an emergency.

The only issue is that in those opening hours, you’re pretty limited in what you can get because money is fairly tight. If you’re looking to buy guns, you don’t want to waste your money on something that feels suboptimal.

ATM machines hold a lot of cash in GTA 6. Learn how to break them open and steal everything inside with this guide.

This list includes a bunch of good weapons you can get in the first couple of chapters of the game. It feels a little crazy that the early game can stretch so far in hours, but GTA 6 really is a humongous game.

The Duval Signature ES9 is basically a more powerful version of Jason’s starting weapon, and it’s a great weapon to keep on you at all times. While it is a sidearm, it can still be helpful in all kinds of situations.

The key part of this one is that it has incredibly high handling, meaning you’ll pretty much always hit what you aim for. In the early hours of the game, that can be the difference between life and death.

At a certain point in the game, you’ll be able to grab Donnie’s sidearm and keep it for yourself. This small but mighty weapon is great for people looking to take people out quickly at a shorter range.

You won’t be able to take on a whole army with it, but it fits nicely into an extra slot for when things start to get a little dicey. Donnie is a crazy guy, so it makes sense his gun is just a little crazy too.

Most people will probably feel like a sniper is relatively useless, especially when the cops start to roll up on you. For the most part, that’s true, but with this weapon you can easily take out some people without getting spotted.

With high range, accuracy, and decent damage, you can put down just about anything you come across with relative ease. Just be sure you’re far enough away to not have to worry about when people start looking.

This weapon is an alternative assault rifle you can buy called a battle rifle, and it comes in handy when going up against big crowds of enemies. Not only does it pack a good punch, but you can stay in relative safety while using it.

The only real downside to this one is that using it a little too much might see you moving in a negative way with your Criminal Profile. That’s probably something you’re going to want to avoid if you’re hoping to stick to a happy ending.

This lovely little piece does a great job at putting just about anything down, but you’ll still need to be choosy about how you fire. It does have great handling, but you could still find yourself in real danger if you get surrounded quickly.

Aside from that, it’s a great weapon for car fights since it can pack such a good punch. If you’ve got the aim to use it, this bad boy can take on most any challenge, except maybe some truly unhinged NPCs.

If you are having trouble finding yourself a plane in GTA VI, this guide is for you.

A fancy found weapon, the Scrapper VF5C, is a great weapon to get, especially before you start getting into the really crazy parts of the game. Silenced and ready for action, this gun will be a great tool for taking on even the toughest jobs.

It does lack some stopping power, but with such high handling, it’s relatively easy to use, and that’s a very good thing. As long as you’re smart with it, there’s virtually nothing this weapon can’t handle.

One of the best early-game weapons that you can grab basically right at the start is the Wortham W-14. It’s a little tough to use, but once you have the hang of it, you can put down anyone who crosses your path.

It’s a fantastic weapon, especially if you’re looking to keep your Criminal Profile on the lighter side. One shot is really all you need with this one, and with such high accuracy, it hardly ever misses the mark.

Probably the best early-game longarm weapon you can get, this found weapon will make swiss cheese out of anyone you want. It’s got high damage and good handling, but you’ll be on your own for aiming and distance.

This gun is phenomenal for almost any scenario, but especially in situations where you’re running into a lot of people. Whether it’s the foot or the face, you’ll only ever need to shoot once at someone to take them out of the fight.

Of course, it wouldn’t be a Rockstar game without including some choice references, and what a reference it is. With this lovely piece as a nod to Red Dead Redemption, you’ll find yourself operating like a real cowboy on the streets of Vice City.

The gun is only available as part of the Ultimate Edition, which is a little problematic, but despite that, it isn’t the only powerful early-game sidearm. It does pack quite the punch and lets you pick off enemies from a distance.

The best early-game weapon of all is a found weapon you can get from pretty much the opening hours of the game. This small silenced pistol is great for committing all kinds of nefarious acts without ever having to worry about getting caught.

While its damage is low, it’ll never give away your position and can quickly put holes in people with ease. It works best if you’re looking to avoid complete shootouts, but really you could pretty much use it whenever in this insanely good simulation game.
