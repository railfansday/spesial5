Roger Craig Smith Describes Sonic Movie Snub As His “Biggest Hurt”

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Sam Woods is TheGamer’s Senior Reporter. He’s been in games journalism since 2020 and has undertaken a variety of roles in that time. These have included roles such as SEO Editor, Managing Editor at DualShockers, and Global Head of Gaming, and he's also been seen on the BBC. He was promoted to the Senior Reporter position in 2025 following a series of high-quality reports and exclusives for TheGamer.

Since 2020, Sam has written over 3,000 articles and specializes in telling the stories of the people behind the games. He’s interviewed the biggest game directors, some of the industry’s top talents, told the stories of how indie games came to be, and spoken to players about what really makes them tick. He’s also interviewed some of the world’s biggest musical artists to find out how the medium has inspired their work, and TV and Film actors about their roles in video game adaptations. He even hosted a PAX panel in 2021 and made a documentary in 2024 surrounding the creation of a Disney game.

Alongside this, Sam focuses on breaking news. He got the scoop on Perfect Dark’s cancellation, provided context on some AI drama, and revealed that Lady Dimitrescu’s actress would love to step back into the character’s giant shoes. These articles have been picked up by the biggest gaming sites, including IGN, GameSpot, and PC Gamer. He also loves to dive into the numbers behind the games.

Sam’s journey in games journalism has taken him all over the world. He’s covered Gamescom in Germany, Summer Game Fest in Los Angeles, and Tokyo Game Show in Japan, each on three occasions.

He’s been learning Japanese since 2024 with the intention of identifying and translating Japanese-language news and bringing it to English-language audiences first, something he’s done countless times.

Sam’s first review was 2020’s Battletoads reboot, and he’s since reviewed games in The Legend of Zelda, Super Mario, and Pokemon franchises.

For many, Roger Craig Smith is Sonic the Hedgehog. He debuted as the character all the way back in 2010 in Sonic Colors and Sonic Free Riders, and for 16 years, he's given the role his all. In 2018, however, change was afoot when Ben Schwartz was announced as the voice of Sonic in the then-upcoming movie series.

Craig Smith has often expressed his admiration for Schwartz's depiction of the character, but he's never opened up about whether he actually wanted the role.

We had the pleasure of sitting down with the actor following the release (and subsequent success) of Phantom Blade Zero, in which he played its protagonist, Soul. During that time, I asked Craig Smith if he wanted the role of Sonic on the silver screen, to which he told me, "What do you think?"

Roger Craig Smith is a very cheerful guy, and when I asked him if he would have liked the role of Sonic in the Sonic movies, he couldn't help but laugh.

“What do you think? I mean, what do you think? I mean, like, no, I don't feel like being a part of some of the biggest movies on Earth. It’s like asking me if I’d come back as Ezio in Assassin’s Creed. What do you think?” he tells me with a wry grin. “I mean, as actors we're inherently envious and jealous and, you know, we want to do it all. We want so bad for you to like us, you know. So, of course, I would have."

The actor wasn't even approached for the role, with Schwartz accidentally stumbling into it after recording a temporary placeholder voice. This chance moment meant that Craig Smith didn't even get a chance to audition, or even speak to anybody at Paramount about the role.

That's always been my biggest hurt, that I never even got a chance...

"That's always been my biggest hurt, that I never even got a chance, that it was not even an option to get in and have a meeting or anything. It just never happened. The industry's weird, but I get it. It is what it is. But yes, of course, I would have killed for a chance to get in there. I get I might not be right for you, but having the opportunity is the first and foremost thing for me,” he says.

While clearly upset at not becoming the movie version of the character, Craig Smith couldn't help but share his love for Schwartz once again.

"You couldn't ask for a more wonderful human being and a more talented, creative genius in Ben Schwartz to be in the role, doing such a great job with it and instantly understanding the fan base."
