Leipzig: Brajan Gruda, the man who has already made Yan Diomandé forget

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

RB Leipzig is off to an excellent start to the 2026-2027 season. Sitting in fourth place in the Bundesliga standings, Martín Demichelis’ men display a particularly attractive face, driven by very effective offensive animation. Despite the resounding departure of Yan Diomandé, who left to join Real Madrid for 140 million euros, the Saxon club has not lost its offensive drive and continues to showcase its firepower. With 32 goals scored after ten days, Leipzig confirms that they will have to be counted on in the race for the top places. An offensive success which owes a lot to the emergence of Brajan Gruda.

At 22, the German has established himself as one of the revelations of the start of the season. Impactful, creative and endowed with excellent technical quality, the left-hander makes a difference thanks to his dribbling, his changes of pace and his ability to eliminate in small spaces. Indisputable in the offensive sector of RasenBallSport, Gruda already has 3 goals and 6 caviars in 14 appearances. Revealed in Mainz, passed through Brighton before returning to Germany last February, he quickly convinced Leipzig to extend his loan for the current season and has established himself as one of the most formidable attackers in the Bundesliga in recent months.

Aligned on the right side of the attack, he brings his ability to unbalance opposing defenses and necessarily recalls Yan Diomandé, his predecessor in this position. With 27 successful dribbles, he is the best player in the Bundesliga in this exercise according to statistics published before the 10th day. His importance was further demonstrated against Union Berlin this weekend, as he provided an assist before finding the net himself in the 5-1 victory. His trainer Martín Demichelis does not hide his affection for him: “I love this boy, I have a good relationship with him, both as a player and as a person.”

But it was also in the Mannschaft jersey that the young attacker made his mark during the last rally. Called up by Jürgen Klopp, Gruda perfectly negotiated his first two selections in November, with two goals and two assists against Serbia (3-1) and the Netherlands (5-0). Against the Serbs, he scored a spectacular goal after a solo run from his own half, before doing it again against the Dutch. Four decisive contributions in two matches, a remarkable performance which leads us to believe in a bright future for him with the Mannschaft. Klopp was full of praise for him, calling him a “real street footballer” and hailing him as an exceptional talent. A recognition which confirms the change in dimension of Gruda, now as important in club as in selection. RC Lens, who faces Leipzig this Tuesday, has been warned.
