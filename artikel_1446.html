PSG: Ferran Torres, a shortage which is seriously starting to worry

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

There are evenings which only accentuate doubts. The one experienced by Ferran Torres against Troyes on Saturday evening is undoubtedly one of them. In a meeting where Paris Saint-Germain ended up finding the fault to win (1-0), the Spanish striker, aligned in a right winger role, once again went through the debates without managing to be sufficiently decisive. However, there was no shortage of opportunities and opportunities to find the net presented themselves to him. But as too often in recent weeks, the last gesture was lacking. A new frustrating performance which extends a worrying period of scarcity for a player whose offensive efficiency is nevertheless one of the main missions.

The Trojan evening did not allow Ferran Torres to smile again. From the 38th minute of play, the Spaniard came up against Hillel Konaté in a tight angle, before missing two new opportunities just before the break, in the 45th and 45th+2. In the second half, the scenario repeated itself: served by Fabián Ruiz in the 67th minute, he again saw the Trojan goalkeeper reject his attempt. Four clear chances, four failures and an exit in the 70th minute, replaced by Quentin Ndjantou. The contrast is striking with his Parisian debut: after scoring seven goals in his first six appearances, Torres is now going through a series of eight games without scoring.

As a reminder, his last goal dates back to September 20, during the victory against Olympique de Marseille in Ligue 1. In the Champions League, he has not found the net since his hat-trick against Slovan Bratislava. Faced with growing questions, Luis Enrique however chose to defend his attacker. Asked at a press conference about his difficulties, the Parisian coach, in fact, wanted to put this less good period into perspective: “everyone was concerned about Mika Godts, now it’s Ferran. These are attackers who miss and score goals.. A protective speech which reflects the technician’s desire not to publicly overwhelm his player.

In the mixed zone, Lucas Beraldo also recalled the importance of solidarity within the Parisian locker room, without wanting to dwell on the individual case of his teammate. “Torres? As I said, every match is different, tonight we played against a very good team, we managed to score and win and that’s the most important thing. At PSG, we are a family, in good times and in bad times.. Valuable support for the Spaniard, who will now have to quickly regain his effectiveness. Because if Luis Enrique continues to place his trust in him, the missed opportunities begin to accumulate and the shortage becomes difficult to ignore.
