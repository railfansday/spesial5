Chromecast with Google TV gets first update of 2026 [U: Wide rollout]

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Following the last release 11 months ago, Google is rolling out the first update of 2026 to the Chromecast with Google TV (4K).

Update 11/23: The UTTA.260813.002 Chromecast with Google TV update is now seeing wide availability. This was a strange rollout.

This new firmware first emerged when Google updated its release notes on October 12. Historically, Google posts after an update sees full availability. There were some reports of owners seeing the OTA last month, but it was not a wide release.

On October 29, the Google Home release notes had a Device Firmware section that mentioned the update for both the 4K and HD models. Compared to the original changelog, Google added “Connectivity, media streaming, and display performance improvements.”

Original 10/12: According to Google’s release notes, UTTA.260813.002 is rolling out just for the 4K model. There is no new release for the HD variant today. Both streaming devices are usually updated at the same time.

This update bumps the Android Security Patch Level to August 2026 (from October 2025). Google also notes the presence of “Other bug fixes and performance improvements.”

On your Chromecast with Google TV, go to Settings > System > About > System update to download and install.
