LIMITED-TIME BEST BUY DROP: The PlayStation 5 DualSense Grand Theft Auto VI Controller, a limited-edition release, is available now at Best Buy through its Drop program — while supplies last.

Grand Theft Auto VI launched last week, and it's the biggest game of 2026. If you're playing on PlayStation 5 and you want to show off your love for it, you should go ahead and grab the Limited Edition GTA VI DualSense controller, available now at Best Buy for a limited time. While it's not on sale, quantities are in short supply, so if you've been eyeing one of these, now's the best time to get one.

We've been waiting for this drop all week. If you're not familiar, this product is part of the Best Buy Drops release program. So, to buy this controller, you'll need to download the Best Buy app and log in.

The DualSense controller's best features include its haptic feedback, which lets you get physically immersed in your games. Whether you're playing The Last of Us Part II and feel the recoil of a shotgun, or Horizon Forbidden West and the tension as you pull back the bowstring, you'll be amazed at how the triggers feel. It simulates all of these feelings to heighten your experience.
