The best Black Friday AirPods deals save you $100 on the AirPods Pro 3, $40 on the AirPods 4

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Black Friday sales are live across the web, and headphones deals — including deep discounts on AirPods — are starting to show out.

So far, the main attraction is an unprecedented discount on the AirPods Pro 3, Apple's premium wireless earbuds. Now $149 at Amazon and Walmart, or a full $100 off, they've never been cheaper before this week.

The AirPods 4 and AirPods Max 2 are also on sale for at least 24% off right now, but bear in mind that neither pair has returned to its lowest price on record just yet. We expect both models to get an extra $20 cheaper on Black Friday proper, aka Nov. 27.

Below, we've gathered the best Black Friday AirPods deals that are live so far. We've also made a prediction about the AirPods 5's Black Friday deal, as they're the only model that isn't on sale at all yet. (Spoiler: It probably won't be a huge markdown.)

The AirPods Pro 3 stand among the best noise-cancelling earbuds we've ever tested. Our reviewer called them "a remarkable upgrade in functionality over their predecessors, with better battery life and ANC, as well as a new heart rate sensor." They also last twice as long as the standard AirPods 5 on one charge.

With the AirPods 5 stuck at full price, these on-sale AirPods 4 are currently the cheapest Apple earbuds available. (Just know that we saw them fall to a mere $69 last Black Friday — a bigger discount could be coming.) Our reviewer praised their "honeyed" audio quality and appreciated their snug fit. While they're not as feature-rich as their predecessors, they do last a little longer.

Read Mashable's full review of the AirPods 4.

Apple's over-ear, noise-cancelling AirPods Max 2 are some of the most stylish headphones on the market. Truthfully, they're a bit overpriced compared to flagship offerings from brands like Sony and Bose, so we really only recommend buying them on sale. They're currently marked down to $419, or 24% off, which beats their October Prime Day price by $10. (They were only $399 during Amazon's Prime Day sale this summer, though.)

Read Mashable's full review of the AirPods Max 2.

Apple's latest earbuds, the AirPods 5, have yet to properly go on sale. The cheaper model dropped a few bucks in September, while the $149 wireless charging model has never seen even a small price dip. As of this writing, both variants were sitting at full price.

Alas, Black Friday probably won't bring us a massive discount on the AirPods 5. The AirPods 4 were only $10 to $14 off for their first Black Friday in 2024, so we expect their successors to go on sale for $119 sans ANC and $134 with ANC. We're happy to be proven wrong, though. (In the meantime, you can read our AirPods 5 review.)

The best time to buy AirPods is when they're cheaper than usual. The best AirPods deals of the year usually pop up in late November, on Black Friday and Cyber Monday, and in mid-summer during Amazon's Prime Day sale.

When the AirPods 4 and 5 are both selling for their usual retail price of $129, the newer models are a no-brainer. They have added ANC, Live Translation, an improved Transparency mode, and better audio quality overall, making them a much better value. (They're also slightly more dust- and water-resistant.)

However, if you're trying to save money, you should look for the AirPods 4 on sale instead. Since they're older earbuds, they typically see much bigger discounts. Exhibit A: They're only $89 right now, while the AirPods 5 are still $129.
