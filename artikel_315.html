The Yu-Gi-Oh! Magnificent Monsters Box has dropped to an all-time low price on Amazon — save almost $5

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

TL;DR: The Yu-Gi-Oh! The Magnificent Monsters Box Set is on sale for $35.33 on Amazon, down from its standard $39.95 list price.

There's a fresh Amazon bargain for Yu-Gi-Oh! fans who prefer their trading cards centered on monsters from the hit anime series. Konami's Magnificent Monsters expansion, which only launched in September, has now hit its lowest-ever price on the retailer — a more than welcome early Black Friday deal.

As of Nov. 11, the Yu-Gi-Oh! Magnificent Monsters Box Set has dropped to just $35.33 at Amazon, trimming $4.62 off its usual $39.95 price with a 12% discount. This offer may come from third-party seller serious_gaming, but orders are still fulfilled by Amazon with free delivery available for Prime members and non-members alike.

Only $5.16 above its estimated $30.17 market price on TCGplayer, this is indeed the lowest price this box has been on Amazon since it first dropped at $48.99 (confirmed with the price tracker camelcamelcamel).

Inside each box are three five-card booster packs containing 15 Ultra Rares altogether, accompanied by three additional Secret Rares that have a chance of being upgraded to Starlight Rares. The expansion's biggest attractions include 18 reimagined cards inspired by the first six Yu-Gi-Oh! anime series, six new boss monsters, and extremely scarce, serial-numbered Grand Master Rares that collectors can potentially pull.

For early fans of the first three classic anime series, the go-to monsters include Yugi Moto’s Dark Magician (now Dark Magician, the Pharaoh’s Servant), Jaden/Judai Yuki’s Elemental Hero Flame Wingman (Favorite Hero Flame Wingman), and Yusei Fudo’s Stardust Dragon (Stardust Dragon — Victim Sanctuary).

If you're after any particular cards, you can also buy Yu-Gi-Oh! Magnificent Monsters singles right now on TCGplayer.
