The Home Depot’s Black Friday deals include free tools and batteries with Ryobi, DeWalt, Milwaukee, Makita, and Ridgid purchases, lower prices on Packout storage, mechanics tool sets, and so much more. BobVila.com’s team walked the aisles to find the standouts. The DeWalt 20V Max Oscillating Tool Kit for $99 (was $199) is a good place to start, at half off with a 4.0Ah battery and charger included. Milwaukee fans can pick up the Milwaukee M18 Compact Drill/Impact Combo Kit for $179 (was $249). Ryobi’s free-tool offer may be the easiest win of the event. The Ryobi One+ 18V 3-Tool Combo Kit for $179 (was $268) includes a drill/driver, a circular saw, and a free reciprocating saw, or shoppers can swap the saw for one of 13 other free picks, including a 1/2-inch impact wrench, a compact router, and a jigsaw.

This cordless combo kit pairs a 1/2-inch drill/driver and a 5-1/2-inch circular saw with 1.5Ah batteries and a charger, and the reciprocating saw comes free. Shoppers can swap the saw for one of 13 other free tools instead, including a 1/2-inch impact wrench ($139 value) or a compact router ($99 value).

This brushless oscillating multi-tool kit handles flush cuts, scraping, and sanding with three speed settings, and a 4.0Ah battery and charger are in the box. That battery also fits every other DeWalt 20V Max tool.

This brushless drill and impact driver combo kit includes two batteries, a charger, and a bag, and both tools are small enough for cabinet and closet work. It’s a solid starter kit for anyone moving into Milwaukee’s M18 lineup.

Free-tool offers are the best value of the season when the qualifying kit is something already on the list. Start with the kit, then pick the free tool with the highest price tag that fits the next project, since most offers cap the free item’s value. Batteries are the priciest part of any cordless platform, which makes Milwaukee’s free 8.0Ah Forge battery ($229 value) worth a close look for anyone already on M18.
