By smart building, commercial type is estimated to account for the largest market share in the Smart Cities Market during the forecast period.

Commercial smart buildings lead the smart building segment in the Smart Cities Market due to their high adoption rates in office buildings, retail establishments, and corporate office parks, prompted by the demand for operational efficiency and cost reduction. These buildings incorporate solutions such as automated building management systems (BMS), which minimize HVAC, lighting, and energy consumption. Smart visitor and access systems fortify security and simplify the process of entry, while AI-driven space optimization and data analysis drive maximum usage of space based on people patterns. Moreover, IoT sensors monitor real-time environmental parameters, enhancing occupant comfort and facility maintenance. These solutions cumulatively drive operational efficiency through minimal wastage of power, increased security, and lower operational expenditure. High demand for solutions in the commercial space, accompanied by the fact that they are scalable with measurable ROI, confirms their leadership position in the market, with businesses investing in sustainable, data-driven solutions to address the increasing needs of the modern city.

By smart transportation solutions, passenger information management is expected to register the fastest growth rate during the forecast period.

Passenger information management (PIM) is projected to register the fastest growth rate in the smart transportation segment of the Smart Cities Market due to its critical role in enhancing commuter experiences and operational efficiency. Passenger information management systems use real-time information, IoT, and AI to give precise, timely information about transit schedules, delays, and routes through mobile applications, digital signage, and in-vehicle systems. It increases commuter satisfaction, encourages the use of public transport, and minimizes traffic congestion. Rapid urban growth and the unabated rise in demand for seamless, technology-driven transportation solutions drive the growth in the adoption of PIM. Integration with smart ticketing platforms and mobility-as-a-service platforms further adds to the ease of accessibility. As the focus shifts towards sustainable transport solutions with data-driven city planning, PIM scalability, along with the potential to streamline transit networks, fuels the growth momentum of PIM in the intelligent transport ecosystem.
