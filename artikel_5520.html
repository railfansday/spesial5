Fed officially set October 2027 as its first no-vote strategy session

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The revised tentative 2027 calendar sets Oct. 26-27 for broad strategy talks, with no policy decision to be announced

The Federal Reserve designated its Oct. 26-27, 2027, meeting as a session on broad monetary policy strategy, and the Federal Open Market Committee will not announce a policy decision at the end of it.

The Fed announced the revised tentative 2027 schedule on Thursday. It lists meetings on Jan. 26-27, March 16-17, April 27-28, June 8-9, July 27-28, Sept. 14-15, Oct. 26-27 and Nov. 16-17. The committee will announce its policy decision on the second day of each meeting except the October session, the Fed said.

The calendar has seven meetings with rate decisions in 2027 and none in December, according to CNBC. The October session will give policymakers additional room to debate policy, with no rate vote and no statement issued afterward.

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh announced the move at his Oct. 28 news conference, though he left unspecified which meetings would lose their rate votes. The Fed will keep its eight annual meetings.

"In 2027, one meeting will focus exclusively on broad monetary policy strategy, with neither a vote on the federal funds rate nor a policy statement anticipated," Warsh said. He said he expects two strategy meetings in 2028 and six for setting policy.

Warsh said what the Fed might release after a strategy meeting is "not obvious yet" and that details will not come until fall 2027. He said it is "equally important we reserve time and space, as an intellectually diverse group, for strategic deliberations."
