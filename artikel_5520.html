Cheche Group Announces Extraordinary General Meeting

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

BEIJING, Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Cheche Group Inc. (NASDAQ: CCG) ("Cheche" or the "Company"), China's leading auto insurance technology platform, today announced that an extraordinary general meeting of the Company (the "Meeting") will be held at 10 A.M. on November 25, 2026, Beijing time (9 P.M. on November 24, 2026, U.S. Eastern time) at 8/F, Desheng Hopson Fortune Plaza, 13-1 Deshengmenwai Avenue, Xicheng District, Beijing 100088, China. The Company has established the close of business on November 5, 2026, Eastern time (the "Record Date"), as the record date for determining shareholders entitled to notice of, and to vote at, the Meeting and any adjournments or postponements thereof. The purpose of the Meeting includes, among others:

(1) to approve by ordinary resolution the creation of a new class of ordinary shares of the Company designated as class C ordinary shares, each with a par value of US$0.00035 (the "Class C Ordinary Shares"), with the rights and restrictions set forth in the proposed third amended and restated memorandum and articles of association of the Company (the "New M&A"), including 300 votes per Class C Ordinary Share;

(2) to approve by ordinary resolution that the authorized share capital of the Company be amended:

(i) FROM: US$50,000 divided into 142,857,143 shares comprising (i) 114,285,714 class A ordinary shares of a par value of US$0.00035 each, and (ii) 28,571,429 class B ordinary shares of a par value of US$0.00035 each,

(ii) TO: US$50,000 divided into 142,857,143 shares comprising (i) 114,285,714 class A ordinary shares of a par value of US$0.00035 each; (ii) 18,571,429 class B ordinary shares of a par value of US$0.00035 each; and (iii) 10,000,000 class C ordinary shares of a par value of US$0.00035 each;

(3) to approve by ordinary resolution that 10,000,000 authorized but unissued class B ordinary shares of the Company of a par value of US$0.00035 each be re-classified as 10,000,000 class C ordinary shares of the Company of a par value of US$0.00035 each with the rights attached to it in the New M&A; and

(4) to approve by special resolution the amendment and restatement of the Company's second amended and restated memorandum and articles of association currently in effect (the "Current M&A") be replaced in their entirety with the New M&A to provide for, among other things, the creation and rights of the Class C Ordinary Shares.

Copies of the notice of the Meeting and the form of proxy are available on the Company's corporate investor relations website at https://ir.chechegroup.com/.
