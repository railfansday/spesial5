University of Phoenix to host Bridging Perspectives webinar on maintaining career relevance in the age of AI

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Webinar explores how professionals can build on experience, embrace continuous learning and adapt as artificial intelligence reshapes work

PHOENIX, Nov. 10, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- University of Phoenix will host the next installment of its Bridging Perspectives webinar series, "When AI Changes the Work, How Do You Stay Relevant Without Starting Over?" on Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026. Presented by the Office of Collaborative Learning and Educational Engagement, the webinar is designed for higher education leaders, employers, nonprofit organizations, University of Phoenix faculty, staff, students, alumni and community members. The discussion will explore how professionals can remain adaptable as artificial intelligence transforms workplace expectations and skill requirements.

"AI is changing how work gets done, but human judgment, adaptability and continuous learning remain essential," said Saray Lopez, MBA, director of strategic initiatives at University of Phoenix. "Professionals who build on their experience while developing new skills can position themselves to navigate change and create new opportunities throughout their careers."

Why experience still matters in an AI-driven workplace

As artificial intelligence continues to reshape workplace tasks and workflows, employers and employees are navigating changing expectations around skills, productivity and career development. While technical capabilities are evolving rapidly, many organizations continue to place value on experience, critical thinking and professional judgment.
