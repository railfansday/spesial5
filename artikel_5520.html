Trump says he believes Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang had no idea his affair was a spy

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The president said the FBI is looking into the matter while Nvidia's board investigates a foreign intelligence effort aimed at its CEO

President Donald Trump said Monday that Nvidia $NVDA CEO Jensen Huang told him he did not know the woman he had an extramarital affair with was an alleged spy for China. Trump said he believes Huang.

Trump told reporters at the White House that he called Huang earlier in the day “to see if he’s feeling all right.” Trump said he asked Huang whether he had any idea the woman was a spy. “He said, ‘I had no idea that she was a spy,’” Trump said, according to The Wall Street Journal. “I really believe him.”

Trump said he learned of the allegations "not too long ago." He called Huang an "honorable man" and added that the FBI is reviewing the issue.

“We’ll look into it,” Trump said, according to NBC News. “I know the FBI is looking into it in terms of something else, and I think that he’ll be in good shape.”

The Justice Department is conducting a criminal probe of Megaspeed International, a Singapore-based firm suspected of illegally funneling Nvidia AI chips to China, according to NBC News. NBC News also reported that the Justice Department’s National Security Division is heading the inquiry, working alongside the FBI and the Commerce Department’s Bureau of Industry and Security. It is also scrutinizing individuals connected with Megaspeed, including the question of whether Beijing enlisted one of them as an operative.

That person is Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive who, according to NBC News, American intelligence officials now consider to be an operative working for China. She is not related to Jensen Huang. China's Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun called the allegation unsubstantiated.

Nvidia's board said Sunday it had begun an independent inquiry following reports that Huang had been singled out by a foreign intelligence operation. Huang said he had been "unfaithful to my wife," according to NBC News.

Trump said the intelligence had not come to his attention when Huang accompanied him on a trip to China last May, according to The Wall Street Journal. Pressed on whether he would broach the subject with Chinese President Xi Jinping at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in Shenzhen later this month, Trump said, “I don’t think so.” He added, “I’m sure President Xi will ask me about it.”

Nvidia stock fell as much as 8% in early trading Monday after the board began its probe, which drew attention to the company's lack of a publicly named successor to Huang. Nvidia stock closed down 4.2% on Monday.

A Los Angeles law firm also brought a shareholder class action in San Jose, California, federal court, naming Nvidia and Huang as defendants, according to CNBC. The Rosen Law Firm suit contends that the defendants issued inaccurate or deceptive statements concerning the affair and the relationship between Nvidia and Megaspeed.

House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries pressed Monday for a thorough briefing on the controversy around Nvidia, according to NBC News.
