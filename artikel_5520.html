Outback Steakhouse owner Bloomin' Brands beat earnings and raised its full-year outlook

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The Outback Steakhouse owner lifted its full-year adjusted earnings forecast to 97 cents to $1.02 a share from 90 cents to $1

Bloomin' Brands, the owner of Outback Steakhouse, reported a narrower third-quarter net loss on Friday and boosted its guidance for annual adjusted earnings to a range of 97 cents to $1.02 a share.

The company's prior forecast was 90 cents to $1 a share. It also lowered the upper limit of its annual guidance for same-store sales growth, which is now 1% to 1.5% versus the earlier 1% to 2%.

Net loss attributable to the company was $2.2 million, compared with a loss of $45.9 million a year earlier. Diluted loss per share from continuing operations was 2 cents, compared with 54 cents a year earlier. The adjusted loss was 17 cents a share, compared with an adjusted loss of 3 cents a year earlier. Analysts were projecting a per-share adjusted loss of 24 cents, according to The Associated Press.

Revenue rose 1.7% to $944.3 million from $928.8 million. The company attributed the increase to higher comparable restaurant sales and a change in accounting estimate for unredeemed gift card revenue, partly offset by restaurant closures and openings. Analysts had expected revenue of $924.4 million, according to The Associated Press.

Combined comparable restaurant sales rose 1.9%, with Carrabba's up 0.6% and Fleming's flat. Combined customer traffic fell 1.8%. Bonefish Grill led the gains, according to The Wall Street Journal.

"We are encouraged by our third quarter results and the continued progress on the Outback Turnaround, including five consecutive quarters of year-over-year improvement in Outback guest scores," CEO Mike Spanos said in a statement.

For the fourth quarter, Bloomin' expects adjusted earnings of 7 cents to 12 cents a share and same-store sales growth of 0% to 1%.

Bloomin' Brands stock rose 3.8% to $10.87 in premarket trading on Friday. The stock has gained 70% since the start of the year. The company also filed a Form 8-K with the Securities and Exchange Commission on Friday.

The results follow Bloomin's agreement to buy the assets of an Outback franchisee that filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection this week. Cerca Trova Restaurant Group Holdings, which does business as Out West Restaurant Group, operates 61 Outback restaurants in the western U.S. Bloomin' agreed on Nov. 1 to buy substantially all of Out West's assets for $26.3 million, less amounts owed under a debtor-in-possession loan.

Bloomin' is expected to be designated the "stalking horse" bidder once the court approves, which leaves its offer open to higher bids and a possible auction. The agreement's outside closing date is Jan. 31, 2027, unless extended. The company's earnings release lists the Out West Chapter 11 proceedings and the outcome of its offer among its risk factors.
