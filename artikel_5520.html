Several external factors could improve the chances of recovery. Globally, a durable resolution or meaningful de-escalation in major geopolitical conflicts, such as the Russia-Ukraine war or a lasting ceasefire involving Iran that normalizes shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, would reduce risk premiums and support broader risk assets.

Economically, clearer signals of monetary easing or a sustained period of elevated liquidity from major central banks would benefit high-beta assets like cryptocurrencies.

Within the crypto ecosystem itself, continued institutional adoption (including ETF flows, corporate treasury allocations, and advances in tokenization), regulatory clarity in major jurisdictions, and a renewed capital rotation from Bitcoin into higher-beta altcoins would all contribute to a more sustained bull market phase.
