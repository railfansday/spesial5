Everyone knows Black Friday is a prime time to save on everyday purchases and holiday shopping. But did you know it’s also a great opportunity to book your next trip?

Travel operators, including many hotel brands and vacation rental platforms, tend to offer deep discounts on Black Friday (Nov. 27 this year), Cyber Monday (Nov. 30) and/or Travel Tuesday (Dec. 1). So, now is the time to start thinking about where you want to go this holiday season — or even into 2027, as many of these deals have generous booking windows.

Here, we’ve rounded up the best hotel and lodging deals of 2026 so you can get a head start on savings.

Related: TPG's guide to Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals

Hilton's latest sale is not limited to Cyber Week. From now through Dec. 22, 2026, Hilton Honors members can book participating hotels in the U.S. and Canada for 20% off. You have until ‌April‌ 2, 2027, to complete travel.

Plus, if you register for the More Points to Play offer, you can score up to 3,000 bonus points on every stay through the end of the year.

With this Black Friday and Cyber Monday deal, World of Hyatt members can save up to 25% on qualifying stays at participating hotels and resorts worldwide and enjoy complimentary breakfast at participating hotels in Europe, Africa and the Middle East.

Nonmembers can save up to 20%, but since membership is free, you may as well join the program to maximize your savings.

Book by Dec. 8, 2026, for stays through April 30, 2027.

This is another sale where it pays to be a loyalty member. With IHG's Black Friday promo, One Rewards members can save up to 30% at participating hotels worldwide while non-members can save up to 20%. Book by Dec. 3, 2026, for stays between Nov. 21, 2026 and April 30, 2027.

Plus, IHG is offering up to 60% off at select Iberostar Beachfront Resorts — and for those who like to plan ahead, the travel window is generous. Book by Dec. 1, 2026, for stays through Oct. 31, 2028 (stay dates vary by location).

This Cyber Week, Marriott Bonvoy is unwrapping deep discounts at thousands of participating hotels and resorts worldwide.

To get the maximum savings of 25%, all you have to do is be a Bonvoy member and book through the program's mobile app. Meanwhile, members and nonmembers who book on Marriott's website will save 15%.

Book from Nov. 24 to Dec. 1 for stays between Nov. 29, 2026, and May 2, 2027.

In addition, Marriott Bonvoy is offering two seasonal bonuses. To celebrate Giving Tuesday, the program will match five times the points donated to various charitable causes through its LoveTravels platform between Dec. 1-8. And you can save 30% site-wide at Marriott Bonvoy Boutiques from Nov. 17 through Dec. 2, with free shipping from Nov.30 onward.

Accor is offering savings of up to 35% on hotel stays across the Americas, and ALL Accor members can save an additional 10%. Plus, you can earn up to 4 reward points per dollar spent in the ALL Accor app.

Book between Nov. 23 and Dec. 2, 2026, for travel from Dec.18, 2026, to Dec. 19, 2027.

Preferred Hotels & Resorts, the world’s largest independent hotel brand, is offering 35% off stays of two nights or more at participating hotels worldwide. This applies to the sustainability-minded Beyond Green collection as well.

Book between Nov. 23 and Dec. 3, 2026, for travel through June 30, 2027.

Ready for a beach getaway? With Wyndham Hotels & Resorts, you can score 30% to 65% off at more than 300 hotels across Latin America and the Caribbean. Plus, Wyndham Rewards members can save an extra 12% on promotional rates.

Book between Nov. 19 and Dec. 4 for stays between Nov. 19, 2026, and Oct. 31, 2027.

Rove Miles has multiple hotel deals this Black Friday, including 10 Rove Miles per dollar spent on new Loyalty Eligible hotel bookings — on top of the hotel points and elite night credits you'll earn through the property's loyalty program.

Rove is also offering a transfer bonus to ALL Accor from Thanksgiving Day to Cyber Monday. The bonus miles you can earn depend on what Rove elite status you hold:

That's not all. Rove's other travel deals include airline transfer bonuses, bonus miles on gift cards and more.

If you're not yet a member, you can score 1,500 bonus miles when you join Rove through TPG's link and an additional 5,000 bonus miles on your first hotel booking over $500 with code "BRIANKELLY500."

Priceline's vast menu of Black Friday travel deals — on flights, car rentals, vacation packages, cruises and more — includes hotels as well. Travelers can score up to 40% at select hotels, with the most savings on stays of two nights or more. Plus, you can save $100 on select Disney destination hotels with code "DISNEYBF100."

Members of Priceline's VIP loyalty program (it's free to join) get access to the above deals starting Nov. 16, and the sale opens to nonmembers on Nov. 23. It will run through Dec. 2 at 9 a.m. Eastern.

As with Priceline, the One Key loyalty family — Expedia, Hotels.com and Vrbo — has a slew of travel deals coming up for Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Travel Tuesday. Among them, you’ll find rates slashed up to 50% at thousands of hotels and vacation rentals.

Even better, these deals are valid for travel through Dec. 30, 2027, giving you plenty of time to plan.

Hotels.com has also teased a special coupon for an extra 15% off, set to drop on Cyber Monday.

If you'd like to give the gift of travel, consider buying a Hotels.com gift card this holiday season. You can save 10% on gift cards of $100-plus and 15% on $300-plus from Nov. 23 through Dec. 31.

Is a vacation rental more your style? At Vrbo, travelers can save hundreds of dollars when booking a vacation rental 60 days in advance. Use the Early Booking and Weekly Stay discount filters to search for deals.

You can also expect special flash deals of up to 75% off select stays. These will go live at 9 a.m. ET on Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Travel Tuesday — but act fast, because quantities are limited.

If Vegas is on your wish list this year, you won’t want to miss this sale.

MGM is offering up to 30% off room rates at various Las Vegas properties, including Bellagio Las Vegas, Park MGM Las Vegas, and Aria Resort & Casino. Book by Dec. 3 at noon Eastern time with offer code "TLTMSTN."

Cyber Monday and Travel Tuesday are some of the best days of the year to plan vacations. Major hotel brands offer significant savings on stays you actually want to book, making the deals even sweeter. But don’t sleep on these deals: While the booking window may seem generous, it goes by fast.
