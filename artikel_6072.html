American Airlines Passengers Restrain Man Who Went For The Door As Flight Attendant Blocks Him With A Drink Cart

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Sean Bennett was asleep a few rows back on American Airlines’ San Diego–Chicago redeye when he heard a flight attendant yelling for a man to get away from the door. Passengers rushed up front to help. A second witness says a flight attendant pushed a drink cart up the aisle to keep the struggle from spilling back into the cabin. Bennett posted several videos from his seat. People crowded into the forward galley and got zip ties around the man’s wrists as he kept resisting almost until they landed. American Airlines flight 1494 took off from San Diego at 10:53 p.m. Pacific on Tuesday, November 24. About 40 minutes later, up at around 34,000 feet, it turned right for Phoenix. Police say the man had left his seat, walked to the front and repeatedly ignored instructions to sit back down. Passengers thought the man was trying to “take over the plane.” One report says he went for the cockpit door, but pilots told American’s operations center the man “tried to open the cabin door.” This was reported as a Level 3 threat which means life-threatening behavior. An attempt to breach the cockpit is Level 4. The man wound up on the ground in his underwear in the galley. At altitude, cabin pressure holds an exterior door shut. The Asiana Airlines passenger who actually opened an Airbus A321 exit was able to do it because the aircraft had descended enough on final approach. On another American flight to Chicago in 2024, Zach Etkind (‘the Wonton Don’) and five other passengers wrestled a man into the aisle after he went for an exit. They used duct tape and flex cuffs and the pilots went back to Albuquerque. This time the Airbus A321 landed in Phoenix around 1 a.m. Wednesday morning. Police met it at the gate. The man went to the hospital with a minor injury. The passenger who shared video of the inflight incident reports that he was upgraded on his next connection.
