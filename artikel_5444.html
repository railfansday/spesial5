RADIN Health Unveils RADINA™, the Autonomous AI Ecosystem for Imaging, at RSNA 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

One autonomous AI platform guiding every patient from referral to follow-up, automating repetitive work, connecting workflows, and helping imaging organizations scale without adding administrative complexity.

SHERIDAN, Wyo., Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- RADIN Health today announced RADINA, a next-generation AI platform designed to automate and orchestrate imaging workflows across the entire patient journey. Scheduled to be officially unveiled at RSNA 2026, RADINA serves as the AI layer behind RADINâ¢, connecting operational, clinical, and patient-facing workflows into a single intelligent ecosystem.

Imaging organizations face continued pressure from workforce shortages, rising labor costs, increasing patient volumes, reimbursement cuts, and growing compliance requirements. While healthcare software has digitized many imaging processes, organizations still rely heavily on staff to coordinate referrals, insurance verification, prior authorizations, scheduling, patient communications, reporting workflows, and follow-up management.

"Healthcare doesn't need more dashboards, more queues, or more disconnected applications," said Dr. Alejandro Bugnone, Chief Executive Officer of RADIN Health. "It needs systems that understand what should happen next and can make it happen. RADINA was built to automate repetitive work, eliminate unnecessary handoffs, and help imaging organizations scale without increasing administrative complexity."

Unlike traditional workflow tools that automate individual tasks, RADINA continuously evaluates the state of each patient's journey and determines the next action required to move care forward. The platform follows patients from referral intake through scheduling, authorization, imaging, reporting, and follow-up management while escalating exceptions to staff when human judgment is required.

RADINA moves beyond traditional workflow automation by coordinating the next best action across the imaging journey and executing routine operational tasks from referral intake through follow-up. The platform was built around a simple principle: "If the system knows what has to happen next, nobody should have to remember it."

RADINA Autopilotâ¢ - The Autonomous Imaging Workflow Engine

At the center of the platform is RADINA Autopilotâ¢, an autonomous workflow engine that connects imaging operations into a single continuous workflow. Autopilot manages each patient's progression through the imaging process and automatically initiates the next appropriate step. It runs the entire referral-to-follow-up cycle as one workflow while engaging staff only when decisions require human oversight.

According to RADIN Health, imaging organizations spend significant administrative time moving patients from referral to completed exam. RADINA is designed to reduce manual effort by automating routine tasks and allowing staff to focus on exceptions, patient support, and clinical coordination.

RADINA includes AI-powered products and capabilities spanning imaging operations, radiologist productivity, quality assurance, compliance, and revenue optimization. The platform operates as a unified intelligence layer across the imaging enterprise, following the patient from referral through follow-up.

"RADINA represents a new generation of technology that doesn't just document work, it helps perform it," said Dipali Lohar, Chief Product Officer of RADIN Health.

The platform maintains a single workflow and timeline for every patient, enabling organizations to reduce administrative friction, minimize missed steps, improve patient engagement, and ensure follow-up recommendations are completed.

RADIN is a comprehensive cloud-native platform and intelligent workflow ecosystem for radiology and diagnostic imaging organizations. Its technology unifies enterprise imaging, RIS, PACS, SELECT workflow orchestration, dictation, intelligent worklists, clinical communications, patient engagement, analytics, compliance, and AI-powered automation within a single connected environment. Designed for hospitals, imaging centers, radiology groups, and teleradiology practices, RADIN helps healthcare organizations improve operational efficiency, clinical quality, patient access, financial performance, scalability, and care coordination across the entire imaging journey, from referral to follow-up.
