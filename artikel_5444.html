Small Business Owners Reluctant to Take Next Step in Home-Buying Journey: Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Buying a home is a daunting task for most people, even without considering the latest mortgage rates averaging above 7%.

But how consumers earn an income can greatly affect their transition from homebuyer to homeowner.

A recent survey conducted by mortgage lender Newrez found that small business owners are reluctant to take the next step in their home-buying journeys, while loan officers say that small business owners can qualify for a home much sooner than they realize.

The report found that 77% of mortgage lenders identified tax write-offs/deductions reducing their client’s qualifying income as one of the biggest challenges their small business owner clients face when trying to obtain a mortgage.

“If you’re self-employed, trying to determine if you’ll be eligible for a mortgage isn’t always as simple as asking, ‘How much does my business make?’” the report states. “How your income is documented, the loan program you’re considering and other qualification requirements can all affect your eligibility.”

Inflation—exacerbated by rising energy costs—has left little breathing room for the average consumer. The latest Consumer Price Index (CPI) from the Bureau of Labor Statistics found that the all-items index grew 0.4%, a slight decrease from the previous report, but still a “hotter than expected” estimate.

The CPI report attributed the jump to the increases in shelter inflation (up 0.3%), which contributed to over 30% of the increase. Core inflation also grew 0.3% in October, and has been growing at a constant rate for the past few months.

Other challenges that mortgage lenders highlighted include documentation or requirement-related factors. But having an inconsistent/variable income on a monthly basis can make it difficult to lock in a reasonable monthly mortgage payment.

Businesses of any size can feel the squeeze of high prices and operating costs. As of late, homebuilders point to rising labor costs and ongoing inflation pressures as significant challenges, with National Association of Home Builders Chief Economist Robert Dietz highlighting the effect of tariffs on building material costs.

Another aspect the survey dives into is the information available to small business owners. According to the report, 84% of mortgage originators stated that small business owners could qualify for a mortgage sooner than they realize.

“Originators who work with small business owners said awareness is as big a hurdle as financial qualifications,” the report continues. “Sixty-five percent said the two were equally significant, and 75% said lack of awareness was at least as significant, if not more so.”

In the same vein, 71% of mortgage lenders agreed that small business owners tended to be more anxious about the mortgage process than their W-2 counterparts. A significant majority (73%) of mortgage lenders also agree that small business owners assume they won’t qualify for a mortgage, even before speaking with a broker or lender.

“Small business owners have income that isn’t always fully captured by traditional documentation,” Bob Johnson, Newrez head of origination, stated. “Giving our broker partners and loan officers a variety of ways to meet borrowers’ needs helps us make home happen for more small business owners across the country.”

Rising operating costs are not the only issue small business owners face. The New York Times reported that Etsy—an online marketplace geared toward independent creators who sell handmade items and custom products—is facing a significant amount of backlash over a new policy change.

The policy in question reserves Etsy—and its third-party vendors—the right to use sellers’ content to develop and train AI models. Following the company’s new terms, many independent sellers withdrew from the platform, citing anger and dissatisfaction with the company’s turn toward AI, with one creator saying he won’t let his content “be stolen.”

Rather, the platform states that it is using AI actions such as “translating your listing descriptions so buyers around the world can discover your items, or featuring your listing photos in Etsy marketing campaigns to showcase your creative work.”

It’s unclear whether small business owners will flock back to Etsy’s platform, but the situation does shed light on hurdles small businesses may face.

“Mortgage lenders and originators can play an important role in helping these borrowers understand the available options and feel confident navigating the homebuying process,” Johnson added. “Building a business of your own should help you buy a home, not stand in your way.”
