Eastbourne Town invite manager bids after Macdonald’s controversial sacking

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Saffrons outfit have placed club stalwart Dave Winterton in interim charge, assisted by senior player Arron Hopkinson, while they conduct their search for a replacement for Macdonald.

Town revealed Macdonald’s departure on Saturday night with the team second from bottom of the Isthmian SE division – but the decision has provoked an angry and disbeliving response from many players, while a number of players and volunteers have since left the club.

Macdonald told us he had been told eight days before being sacked his job was ‘bomb-proof’ and he had still not been given a reason for his removal.

Although Town are in the relegation zone they have had a horrendous run of injuries and have shown signs of better results with draws in their past two games, including a creditable 0-0 draw at home to Whitstable last Saturday, Macdonald and Ben Davis’s last game at the helm.

But Town have now publicised the vacant role. They said: “Eastbourne Town FC is seeking a dedicated, ambitious and committed First Team Manager to lead the club into its next chapter.

"We are looking for an individual who shares our ambition, understands the importance of building a strong club culture and is passionate about developing players, creating opportunities and delivering success both on and off the pitch.

“The successful candidate will be someone who: Has a clear football philosophy, strong leadership qualities and the ability to motivate and develop players. Is committed to building a competitive, hard-working and united first-team squad. Recognises the importance of developing young talent and creating a clear pathway from youth football into senior football. Is enthusiastic about developing our U18 and U23 football programmes, ensuring talented young players are given genuine opportunities to progress into the First Team. Is prepared to work collaboratively with the club’s youth coaches and wider football management team. Can build positive relationships with players, volunteers, supporters and the wider club community. Demonstrates professionalism, integrity, commitment and a strong desire to represent Eastbourne Town FC with pride.

"Building for the Future: At Eastbourne Town, we believe the First Team should be an important part of a wider football development pathway.

“We want to create an environment where young players can see a realistic route from youth football through U18 and U23 football and ultimately into the First Team.

"We are looking for a manager who embraces this philosophy, is willing to invest time in developing young players and understands that giving opportunities to homegrown talent can be just as important as recruiting established players.

“This does not mean compromising ambition or competitiveness. We want to build a team capable of competing, progressing and achieving success while developing the next generation of Eastbourne Town players.

“Our Vision: We want a manager who wants to be part of something bigger than just managing a Saturday afternoon team. Someone who wants to build, develop and contribute to the long-term future of Eastbourne Town FC.

“If you have the passion, leadership, experience and ambition to take our First Team forward while helping shape the future of football at our club, we would love to hear from you.

"Please send applications to the Director of Football – [email protected] – the closing date for applications is 1st December 2026. Eastbourne Town FC – Building for Today, Developing for Tomorrow.”
