'So disappointing': Doncaster Rovers boss rues cheap goals - and identifies where Huddersfield Town are League One's best

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Despite playing with an extra man for an hour following the 34th-minute dismissal of Bali Mumba, which enabled Isaac Hutchinson to reduce the arrears for a Rovers side trailing 2-0 after 21 minutes through goals from Cameron Humphreys and Joe Taylor, the hosts still flattered to deceive.

Ilias Bronkhorst grabbed a killer third for Town, who moved joint top, just before the hour mark and despite captain Owen Bailey pulling one back for Rovers late on, it was to no avail.

Doncaster remain in the bottom four of League One, having won just once in their last seven outings. During the run, they have shipped 18 goals.

McCann said: "I felt we started the game really badly and the two goals we conceded in the first half were so disappointing from our point of view.

"We had enough bodies around the ball for both goals, we switch off on the run, the midfield player makes a run into the box and we don't see him.

"With the second one, we had enough bodies around the ball and we didn't get in a position to block the shot from Joe Taylor. Then we got a bit of a lifeline, having started the game so poorly, it was a really good move to get the penalty and they went down to 10 men and Hutch is calm from the spot.

"The message at half time is to go and win the game, a real opportunity to win the game at home, with Huddersfield down to 10 men, but we just huffed and puffed, we didn't show enough quality.

"We didn't create enough overloads in the wide areas which was a plan at half time, we kept coming inside and when you give a team like a transition team like Huddersfield the opportunity they give on a break and we concede a set-play which is really disappointing with four players around three and then we go on and we get done with it.

"That gives us a bigger challenge, just the lack of quality from an attacking point of view and the poor defensive goals that we conceded has done us.

"They've got top quality players, the front three are probably the best three in the division and so quick on the transition, they've got a bit of everything—they've got pace, they've got physicality and athleticism.

"But it's really disappointing, I don't think they've had to do a lot to score those goals and that's the most frustrating thing about it. We've played against Huddersfield and Bradford in the last two games and scored four goals and you're thinking that's enough to get you more than one point, it's not.

"We've conceded five sloppy goals in the last couple of games and that's the reason why we've only got one point from it."
