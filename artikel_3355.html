Late-Night Hosts Take Aim at Trump’s Reaction to the FAA Shutdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) grounded 12,462 flights on Friday, leading to massive delays and a lot of finger-pointing. Donald Trump blamed Joe Biden’s administration, while transportation secretary Sean Duffy and Homeland Security secretary Markwayne Mullin blamed old technology. Either way, late-night hosts had a field day with the news.

On Jimmy Kimmel Live, Jimmy Kimmel quipped, “Sometimes it feels like maybe Elon and his Doge bros shouldn’t have fired more than a thousand employees at the FAA.”

He added, “We learned this weekend a very valuable lesson. We learned what happens when the only experience your secretary of transportation has when it comes to transportation is being on Road Rules Allstars on MTV.”

Kimmel recounted how Trump didn’t post about the incident until Sunday when he called for Congress to replace the current “50-year-old system.”

“Well, I will say this,” Kimmel responded. “If anyone’s capable of getting rid of a 50 year old and replacing it with a younger, more attractive model model, this is the guy.”

On The Tonight Show, Jimmy Fallon also reflected on Trump’s decision to blame old FAA technology. “Today President Trump finally addressed the situation,” Fallon said before playing a clip of Trump telling reporters that the shutdown was caused by “very, very old equipment that had a heart attack.”

“In the times of crisis he really knows how to keep us calm,” Fallon noted. “Trump said the old equipment had a heart attack. And everyone watching that while flying also had a heart attack. Luckily there’s always a defibrillator nearby because that’s how they start the planes at Southwest.”
