Trump Lashes Out at ‘Loser’ Reporter for Questioning D.C. Renovation Obsession

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A defensive Donald Trump lashed out at a reporter who asked him to justify why he was spending so much time renovating Washington, D.C., as opposed to more pressing issues like the aging air traffic control system, which glitched on Friday, abruptly halting air travel across the country for the longest period since the terrorist attacks on September 11, 2001.

“What do you say to critics who say you were too focused on D.C. construction projects while our airline systems were crumbling?” Trump was asked outside the White House on Monday.

“If you take a look at the White House, it’s flawless,” Trump replied.

But the question clearly touched a nerve because the president continued: “Listen to me, I know you don’t see dirt. But if you take a look at the White House — you’d never notice broken windows obviously from where you come.” Trump was speaking to NBC White House Correspondent Yamiche Alcindor, who is of Haitian descent.

Trump went on to declare, “The helipad has gotten great reviews,” while ignoring Alcindor’s follow-up questions about the weekend disruptions at airports across the U.S.

“Oh, you’re so pathetic,” Trump said as Alcindor pressed him about whether he and the Republican Party should have taken responsibility for not securing adequate funding to pay for upgrades to the air traffic control system. “I have a responsibility to make sure the symbol of our country is in decent shape — and it takes me 10 minutes a day. Very little time.”

“This doesn’t cost money. This is peanuts. This is called talent. Talent. You don’t have talent,” Trump said, continuing to insult the reporter. “The White House was in terrible condition, now it’s in beautiful condition. Cost me very little money to do it very little time to do and losers like you come out and say ‘Why are you focused on the White House?’”

Trump’s quest to reshape D.C. in his ideal image has resulted in an endless string of headlines and legal cases. Most recently, Trump’s plans to build a triumphal arch came to a temporary halt when an irate federal judge claimed that the government’s lawyers had basically made up claims that the project would have some kind of benefit to the president’s physical security. Trump had previously mentioned he wanted to station snipers on the arch. His plan to revitalize the East Potomac Links golf course, as well, was halted by a court injunction when Trump’s legal team tried to pull a rush job with little notice, cutting down hundreds of trees in a single day. Trump has ranted online about the golf course several times in recent days, including posting several images depicting it in apparent despair. On Monday, he described the course as “Trash Island” while attacking the judge who ruled against his plans to renovate it. Trump has always had a contentious relationship with the press, but has been making himself both more available and less agreeable to reporters in recent weeks. In September, he took the extraordinary step of outright banning several news outlets from the White House, yet continued to appear for interviews with the remaining press corps. In early October, likely feeling the stress of negative polls before the midterms, he blew up at reporters in the Oval Office for not asking him positive questions. He had a similar outburst later in the month.

The president is particularly sensitive to questions about his redecoration of the White House. During the waning days of the 2026 midterm campaign, former President Barack Obama joked that Trump’s renovations made the presidential home look like “Saddam Hussein opened a Cheesecake Factory.” Trump lashed out in response, alleging that Obama couldn’t understand the imperative to beautify the White House because, like Alcindor, he doesn’t “notice dirt.”
