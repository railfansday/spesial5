UPS Cargo Jet Collides With Commercial Airplane

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

A commercial airplane clipped the nose of a UPS cargo jet in Miami International Airport ... leaving two people injured.

The incident went down Saturday night ... with a Copa Airlines pilot telling Air Traffic Control that "somebody hit us" just after its left wing clipped the UPS flight bound for Bogotá, Colombia.

In the audio ... the UPS cargo flight's pilot admitted to going "too far" on the runway before the Copa Airlines flight "clipped us with his wing."

Miami International Airport spokesperson has since shared that flights were redirected to other runways and that two patients received care from Miami-Dade Fire Rescue ... the California Post reported.

This isn't the only traffic woe for jet-setters this weekend.

As you know ... the FAA issued a ground stop for all flights over several hours on Friday due to "extraordinary circumstances" -- which Sean Duffy ultimately blamed on "old legacy equipment."
