It comes with five games out-of-the-box, including Fruit Ninja, Go Keeper, Party Fowl and others, but a subscription to Nex’s Play Pass unlocks all of the games (more than 60 interactive games) for the system, including Starri | BTS, a new rhythm-based game that features 10 BTS songs in game worlds that’s inspired by the South Korean supergroup’s albums. The game has songs, like “DNA,” “FAKE LOVE,” “Dynamite,” “Butter,” “FYA,” “SWIM,” “Like Animals,” “Please” and others.

It’s designed for the whole family. Unlike traditional gaming consoles, the Nex Playground doesn’t require a controller to play. Instead you use your whole body to interact with games.

Interactive games also feature kid-friendly characters from franchises, such as Bluey, Barbie, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, Peppa Pig, Elmo, Gabby’s Dollhouse, How to Train Your Dragon, Sesame Street, Care Bears, Kung Fu Panda and more.

And since the Nex Playground is from Amazon, you’ll get it shipped to you for free if you’re a Prime member. Otherwise, your cart has to be more than $35 to get free shipping.
