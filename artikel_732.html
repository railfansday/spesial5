KATSEYE Postpones Detroit Concert Due to Illness

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

"Unfortunately, Megan is unwell and unable to perform tonight, and we want to make sure she has time she needs to rest and recover," the group confirms.

KATSEYE’s Michigan debut will have to wait, after the pop group postponed their show set for Tuesday night (Nov. 3) at Little Caesars Arena due to illness within its ranks. Explore KATSEYE See latest videos, charts and news

“Unfortunately, Megan is unwell and unable to perform tonight, and we want to make sure she has time she needs to rest and recover,” explains a message, posted just hours before showtime. “We know so many of you have travelled, made plans and been looking forward to tonight. We are incredibly disappointed that we won’t get to see you, and we’re truly sorry for the inconvenience,” reads the post on KATSEYE’S Instagram Stories.

In another, the venue urged EYEKONS to hang tight. “We’re sorry to share that the KATSEYE show scheduled for tonight at Little Caesars Arena has been postponed,” reads a statement on the venue’s socials.

A new date will be announced, for which Tuesday night’s tickets will be valid. An email with further information will be distributed shortly.

“We sincerely apologize for the disappointment and inconvenience, especially to those who traveled to be with us tonight,” the venue statement adds. “Thank you for your patience, understanding, and continued support.”

According to sources at the show, the group did a VIP soundcheck with Megan missing and reportedly resting-up for the show. Doors had opened and fans were seated when a message confirming the postponement was splashed on the big screens, roughly 40 minutes before the scheduled start.

The group is presenting its WILDWORLD TOUR, which has sold outruns across Europe, the United Kingdom and, currently, North America.

Assuming Megan Skiendiel makes a prompt recovery, the group is next booked for the United Center in Chicago, IL on Thursday (Nov. 5). KATSEYE’s 2026 North America shows are slated to conclude Nov. 28 with the second of two nights at Palacio de los Deportes in Mexico City, MX.

A two-month break will follow, after which the group will embark on a tour of Asia, Australia and New Zealand, kicking off Jan. 27, 2027 at Yokohama Arena, Japan.

Promoted by Live Nation, the sprawling arena trek is in support of WILD (HYBE x Geffen Records), which blasted to No. 1 on the Billboard 200 in August for the group’s first leader. WILD was the followup to 2025’s Beautiful Chaos, which peaked at No. 4 on the all-genres albums survey.
