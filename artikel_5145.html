Bitcoin traded at $65,164.55 on Monday, recovering about 2% over 24 hours after dipping close to $62,000 over the weekend, though it remains down 13.9% for the week. The bounce came from a key support zone between $61,000 and $62,100, where a large pool of liquidity had built up. The daily RSI has also fallen into deeply oversold territory, a level last seen in early June, which often comes before at least a short-term recovery. That points to a possible local low that could hold for days or weeks. The bigger picture is weaker. Bitcoin’s weekly close below about $66,500 is set to flip the Supertrend indicator back to bearish, cancelling the bullish signal seen earlier this cycle. It would be the first false signal from this indicator on the weekly chart since the 2022 bear market. The close also starts to weaken a bullish divergence on the weekly RSI. Fresh liquidity is now forming below current prices, leaving room for another test lower later.
