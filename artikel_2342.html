Conflicting reports on Bayern battle with PSG for Benfica winger Prestianni

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

According to Argentine source TyCSports, Bayern Munich are one of the clubs closely monitoring Benfica talent Gianluca Prestianni.

Paris Saint-Germain and Bayern are both listed as the frontrunners, but a host of unnamed Premier League sides are also keen.

Prestianni's performances on the wing have attracted a lot of interest and a move to a top club is becoming a real possibility.

Despite his contract running until 2029 and a renewal in the works, Prestianni could well imagine a move towards a top league.

Interestingly, however, Sky Germany have been quick to dismiss the rumours linking the Argentine with the German record champions.

The pay-TV channel said reports from Portugal and Argentina, that Prestianni is currently “not a hot topic of discussion” at FC Bayern.
