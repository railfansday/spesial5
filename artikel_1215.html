Former Leeds coach Bielsa continues Italy tour at Venezia vs Bologna

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Marcelo Bielsa was in the stands at the Stadio Pier Luigi Penzo for Venezia vs. Bologna on Friday night, as the former Leeds United and Argentina coach continues his tour of Italian football.

As noted by Calciomercato.com, Sky Sport Italia’s cameras spotted the Argentine watching Serie A’s Matchday 13 opener.

It was his second game in Italy in three days as on Wednesday, he was at Cremona for Cremonese’s Serie B clash with Avellino.

That visit appeared to be personal rather than professional. According to DAZN, via Sportal, Bielsa is a friend of Cremonese coach Fabio Pecchia, and was already in Italy on personal business when he took the chance to watch the Grigiorossi.

El Loco, who famously u-turned on a job at Lazio in 2016, has been out of work since leaving Uruguay after a miserable World Cup. La Celeste took just two points, drawing against Saudi Arabia and Cape Verde and losing to Spain, and finished 37th among the 48 competing nations, the worst World Cup result in the country’s history.

The 71-year-old has stayed busy since then. Just a week before his visit to Cremona, he was reported to be in Panama, leading coaching sessions.
