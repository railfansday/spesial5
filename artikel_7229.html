Working Hours: Following New Zealand business hours

Our client is an established New Zealand-based business group with diverse operations across the property, construction, and related service sectors. The group manages multiple business entities and supports a range of commercial and operational activities, requiring efficient financial administration and accurate record-keeping. They are currently looking for a detail-oriented and organised Accounts Officer to support their finance and administrative functions. You will also provide general administrative support while helping maintain efficient financial processes and accurate business records. This is a great opportunity for someone with solid accounting administration experience who enjoys working independently, managing multiple priorities, and being part of a collaborative team.
