Roberto De Zerbi makes shock future admission as ‘dream’ Tottenham target set

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Roberto De Zerbi has cast doubt on his future, four months into his first full season at Tottenham Hotspur.

After arriving in March, the 47-year-old has presided over a summer window which has seen the likes of Sandro Tonali, Andrew Robertson and Savio touch down in north London.

But De Zerbi, whose contract with Spurs runs until 2031, has admitted doubts over his long-term future in the game.

The Italian told Canal+: “I don’t think I’ll stay in football much longer. Your life revolves around football, but life isn’t just that.

“That time hasn’t come yet. For me, being a coach at this level is already a dream.”

After enduring a tough start to the 2026/27 Premier League season, Spurs have won four of their last five fixtures, most recently beating Ipswich 2-0.

Before arriving at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, De Zerbi spent time in Ligue 1 with nine-time champions Marseille after leaving Brighton and Hove Albion, but a 5-0 hammering by rivals PSG saw the Italian depart in February of this year.

The Italian joined Spurs shortly thereafter with the club one point away from a first relegation since 1977, and clinched Premier League survival with final-day delight against David Moyes’ Everton.

Currently sat in 10th place, De Zerbi’s Spurs are targeting a weekend victory against relegation-threatened Sunderland to continue a run of good form, but the manager has his eyes set on more.

He said: “I don’t measure dreams by the titles won, but more by the human impact you have.

“I always want to stay the same, always look people in the eye. Even when I make mistakes, because I make a lot of them.”
