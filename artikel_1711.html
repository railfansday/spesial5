Le Bris: Unforced errors costing Sunderland

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Regis Le Bris conceded unforced errors are hurting Sunderland after they were held to a 1-1 draw by Jagiellonia Bialystok in the Europa League.

Tymur Tutierov had given Sunderland the lead in the 47th minute, but they were pegged back five minutes later when Kamil Jozwiak latched onto a loose Reinildo Mandava backpass to round Robin Roefs and equalise.

Jozwiak almost repeated the trick shortly after, only to be denied by Roefs on that occasion, with the Sunderland goalkeeper impressing in an end-to-end draw that saw plenty of chances for both sides.

No Premier League side has made more errors leading to opposition goals across all competitions this season than Sunderland (eight – level with Aston Villa).

Sunderland have gone three successive major European matches without a win for the first time (D2 L1), having won five of their first six (L1), and Le Bris is determined to cut out the errors to help improve that run.

"The data is clear: we are making many unforced errors this season, leading to goals," Le Bris, who remains under pressure, said.

"The last two games it didn't happen, but now it's like a reminder. You can't relax, or your opponent at this level can enjoy these mistakes.

"[Sunderland were] disjointed. I think the first half was okay; we created a few chances.

"It was important to score because in the second half we knew we had to make five changes to manage the squad and get ready for the end of the week, and the next week."

Sunderland are 13th in the league-phase table on eight points after five games, and are only two points off the automatic qualification spots.

They have scored just eight goals in their opening 10 home games across all competitions this season, their fewest at this stage of a campaign since 2005-06 (also eight).

It is a worry for Le Bris, who was also dealt a potential blow in defence after Omar Alderete was forced off in the first half.

He was replaced by Luke O'Nien in the 39th minute, but Le Bris says it was down to illness, not injury.

"Omar felt unwell," he added when asked about Alderete.

"I don't know exactly what happened. We'll assess him, and we'll see what happens."
