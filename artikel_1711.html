Don't forget, African sensation is joining Liverpool, deal is already agreed

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A deal has already been agreed for an African sensation who people should not forget about.

Football comes in waves. It ebbs and flows. From one generation to another. Giants are born and giants are toppled. A club like Liverpool has ebbed and flowed a lot more than others over the years.

There was a golden era in the 1970s and 1980s, followed by bleak and dark times, succeeded by even darker times on the pitch barring one miracle night in Istanbul.

Then along game the beacon of hope. Jurgen Klopp restored the faith at the club and took Liverpool back to the apex of the footballing world.

Now it's Andoni Iraola's job to sort out the mess he inherited from Arne Slot and follow in the footsteps of Klopp.

What Julian Ward is planning to make Liverpool the best team in the worldOf course, it's never just the head-coach who is responsible, especially these days in modern football. One of the club's recent recruitment strategies has been to recruit some of the best young players on the planet. This has seen Liverpool break the record for an U23 goalkeeper with the signing of Lucca Brughmanns. They have also secured the future of Djylian N'Guessan and Ifeanyi Ndukwe. But most notably, Liverpool have ventured to different continents in order to complete signings. Samuel Martinez was recruited from Colombia. Meanwhile, Mor Talla Ndiaye has been signed from Senegal.
