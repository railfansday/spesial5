AC Milan Tier List: Who Should Stay and Who Should Go?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

AC Milan are entering a big year, and there are questions about who should stay and go within the squad if it’s to be more competitive.

It’s set to be a big 2027 for Milan. The hiring of Markus Krösche kickstarts a brand new era and he’ll have a big task ahead of him – quite literally straight ahead of him – if the Rossoneri are to become consistent Scudetto contenders in years to come.
