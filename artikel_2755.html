Seth McGowan runs for 77 yards, TD in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Indianapolis Colts running back Seth McGowan ran 12 times for 77 yards and a touchdown in Week 10 against the Miami Dolphins, while he did not have any receptions or targets in the passing game.

Why it Matters: With the Colts trading away their two top receivers, Josh Downs and Keenan Allen, at the trade deadline, Indy used a run-heavy offense. McGowan averaged 6.4 yards per carry on 12 touches, while Jonathan Taylor had 5.2 yards per tote, 16 carries and two receptions. McGowan will have a big role down the stretch, but pump the brakes a bit. The Colts were facing the inept Miami Dolphins. If the same thing happens in Week 11 against Houston a short week Thursday, then it would be more impressive. For now, McGowan is a low-end RB3 or flex fantasy play.
