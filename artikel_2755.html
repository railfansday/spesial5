Parker Washington Pops Up on Jaguars-Titans Thursday Injury Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Thursday's Thanksgiving injury reports are in for Sunday's 1 p.m. EverBank Stadium matchup between the 7-3 Jacksonville Jaguars and the 1-9 Tennessee Titans. Jacksonville is coming off a 23-18 loss to the New York Giants, while Tennessee is coming off a 51-30 road loss to the Dallas Cowboys. While final game designations will not arrive until Friday's final report, Thursday already shows where each team may be thin for Sunday.

RB Bhayshul Tuten (ankle/foot): Wednesday and Thursday. Tuten was the only Jaguar who did not practice on Wednesday and Thursday

OL Patrick Mekari (back): Injured Reserve, designated for return

WR Parker Washington (shoulder): Not listed Wednesday; limited Thursday. Washington had a career-best game in the first meeting with Tennessee, catching seven of nine targets for 152 yards and a touchdown.

DE Danny Striggow (concussion): Limited Wednesday, full Thursday

DE B.J. Green II (forearm): Injured Reserve, designated for return

Parker Washington's Thursday addition to the report with a shoulder issue is clearly a larger one, presumptively occurring in Thursday's practice. If he practices Friday will go a long ways toward determining his final game status.

Similarly, running back Bhayshul Tuten's continued lack of practice while dealing with his ongoing ankle and foot pain continues to be of concern for Jaguars fans. If Tuten does not play, Rodriguez, who practiced both days fully, is the natural beneficiary.

Additionally, the offensive line is the clear second headline. Five linemen appearing on Thursday's report, with every one of them limited, is surely notable. Green's full participation is also encouraging for the pass rush depth, with an expectation that he should be activated from IR this week or early next.

S Terrell Burgess (knee): Wednesday and Thursday

CB Cor'Dale Flott (neck): Wednesday and Thursday

RB Tony Pollard (not injury related, rest): Thursday

T Dan Moore Jr. (knee): Did not practice Wednesday, limited Thursday

LB Dorian Mausi (knee): Not listed Wednesday, limited Thursday

G Fernando Carmona Jr. (ankle): who has returned to practice and is within his 21-day window

DL John Franklin-Myers (back): Limited Wednesday, full Thursday

For Tennessee, the Titans' secondary is the area to watch, with two defensive backs not practicing: Burgess and Flott. Considering Parker Washington's Week 10 success against this secondary, any additional setbacks or injuries may turn a clear Jacksonville edge into a five-alarm Titans' problem area. Moore's knee is another note for an offensive line that Jacksonville's pass rush should test.

Friday's final injury reports will include official game designations for both teams.
