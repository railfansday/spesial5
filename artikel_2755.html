The Cowboys handled the Christian Parker situation poorly

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Its been a frustrating season for the Dallas Cowboys as they sit at 4-6. More specifically, after years of bad defenses, the hope was that things would turn around in 2026, but they haven't. Under defensive coordinator Christian Parker in his first season in the role, the Cowboys are just as bad, if not worse, than they were defensively under Matt Eberflus in 2025, which was the worst defense in team history.

There's no doubt that something had to change in order for the Cowboys' defense to improve, and team owner Jerry Jones and head coach Brian Schottenheimer mentioned on Monday that conversions were being had in that regard. Dallas made a decision on Wednesday that sent shock waves through their fan base and around the NFL. They took away play-calling duties from Parker, and now senior defensive assistant Mike Pettine will call the plays.

Pettine brings a ton of experience as his career in the league started back in 2002 with the Baltimore Ravens. He's been a defensive coordinator for the New York Jets, Buffalo Bills, and the Green Bay Packers. He had success with all three in that role, especially with the Jets when he had four consecutive top 10 defenses. This makes the possibility of this move working out favorable, but there are things about it that raise eyebrows.

Parker was given his first job in the NFL by Pettine in 2019 with the Packers as a defensive quality control coach. It made sense that Parker reached out to Pettine in the spring about a consultant role (which he declined), and once again in the fall when it was clear that the Cowboys' defense wasn't performing the way that everyone had hoped. The Cowboys then brought him in to assist Parker, and while that's understandable, it also kind of pulled the rug from under him as a first time play-caller.

The Cowboys have now played 10 games, and already Parker, who again is calling plays for the first time, has now essentially been demoted. What makes it worse is that Dallas is still letting him keep the defensive coordinator title, but Pettine is now the man that will make all the calls on game day. Keeping that title on him but taking away a huge part of his responsibility is humiliating.

Parker has support from his players like rookie safety Caleb Downs and veteran defensive tackle Kenny Clark. While that's good, the fact that Dallas is making Parker face these men everyday while knowing how he's been embarrassed is just bad business. Also, who do the players report to now? Parker or Pettine? The organization would have been better off just firing Parker and going their separate ways.

Nobody is saying that this is the case, but stripping Parker of play-calling duties and giving them to Pettine after a month makes it seem like the move to bring him in wasn't to help, but rather take his job. The Cowboys praised the hiring of Parker all offseason long, yet they only gave him two months before publicly shaming him in a sense. This is his first time doing this, and it was always going to be a process, but the Cowboys didn't give him the necessary time to grow into the role.

Going back to Eberflus, his unit was atrocious last year. Again, it was the worst in Cowboys' history up until this season. However, he was never stripped of his duties to call plays, so why do that to Parker? Especially when it's his first time as a defensive coordinator and play caller. This is just a bad look for the organization starting with the aforementioned Jones, but also Schottenheimer.

As bad as the defense has been, Dallas has struggled to consistently be good offensively as well despite being ranked highly in certain categories. The case can be made that the Cowboys would have won games against the Baltimore Ravens and Arizona Cardinals had the play calling on offense been better situationally, which falls on Schottenheimer since he calls the plays. So, why hasn't he relinquished his duties as a play caller? Or better yet, why hasn't there been conversations about it like with Parker?

Schottenheimer is the one that brought Parker in. If he doesn't feel like Parker can do his job, which is pretty much what he's saying by taking away play-calling duties, then just fire him. This is just bad business on his part, and it also looks that way on Jones and the organization for allowing it. The bottom line is, the while situation is a terrible look for the Cowboys, and it speaks volumes as to why they've been a laughing stock around the league for quite some time.
