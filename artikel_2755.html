2027 NFL Draft order: Titans still hold first pick

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Your Tennessee Titans still hold the top pick in the 2027 NFL Draft. They are one of three teams in the NFL with one win. The Los Angeles Chargers and the Miami Dolphins are the other two. I've been saying for weeks that the Chargers are going to win 3-4 games. Well, they lost to the New York Jets in Week 11, so I'm not so sure they will anymore.

The best thing the Titans have going for them, well, besides the fact that they are terrible, is that their strength of schedule (.483) is significantly weaker than the Chargers (.531) and the Dolphins (.592). That, as you know, is the tiebreaker in the first round.

The Jets currently hold the 4th pick via the Indianapolis Colts. The Colts, who have 3 wins, have a .477 strength of schedule. The Titans would have to win at least 2 more games for that to matter. It's hard to see that happening.
