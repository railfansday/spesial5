Titans fall to 1-8 with 40-23 loss to Jaguars

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

That game went pretty much as expected. We knew coming in that the Jacksonville Jaguars were going to score a lot of points, and they did just that with 40. We knew that Trevor Lawrence was going to throw for a lot of yards, and he did just that with 352.

The only thing that is important for the rest of the season is how Cam Ward plays. He wasn't as good in this one as he was in the loss to the Cincinnati Bengals, but he still played well today – throwing for 221 yards with 2 touchdowns and 1 interception. He also added 39 yards rushing.

Elic Ayomanor led the receivers with 5 catches for 79 yards. He's another guy that we really need to see continue to develop as this season goes along. He has stepped up in a big way over the last two games with Carnell Tate out with that shoulder injury.

The Titans still hold the second pick in the 2027 NFL Draft. If the Los Angeles Chargers can beat the Baltimore Ravens tomorrow night, it will move the Titans up to the first pick.
