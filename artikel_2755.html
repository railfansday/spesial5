Lakers reveal Austin Reaves’ status for NBA Cup matchup vs. Spurs

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

SAN ANTONIO — Austin Reaves is making his return to the court for the Lakers' NBA Cup Group Play matchup against the Spurs at Frost Bank Center.

The team upgraded Reaves from questionable to available 7 ½ hours before tipoff on Friday afternoon after the star guard was sidelined for the previous five games because of a bruised left heel.

Austin Reaves #15 of the Los Angeles Lakers reacts after a 3-point basket. Getty Images

He suffered the injury during the third quarter of their Nov. 11 road win over the Bucks in Milwaukee.

The 6-foot-5 star guard was a full participant in the team's practice on Wednesday before being upgraded to questionable on the Lakers' Thursday evening status report.

Austin Reaves has been sidelined for the last five games. NBAE via Getty Images

"It felt good to play basketball again," Reaves said on Wednesday in his first public comments since the injury. "Wind was a little bad, but kind of to be expected being out two weeks. But felt good."

He wasn't sure what happened on the play against the Bucks to cause the injury.

"I had no idea," he said. "Tried to play through it, and then I drove baseline, planted on it, and was like, I can't do this. So walked to the back and was in some pretty bad pain. I didn't think I broke my foot, but it sure felt like I broke my foot."

The Lakers went 4-1 after Reaves suffered the injury, but the team clearly missed his offensive production.

Lakers star guards Luka Doncic (77) and Austin Reaves (15) will have a tough task against the Spurs' defense. NBAE via Getty Images

They had a 112.9 offensive rating (tied for No. 18) in the five games Reaves missed compared to the 121.6 (No. 1) offensive rating in their first 13 games.

"Who would have thought that not having an elite offensive player, an All-Star level player would hurt your offense," coach JJ Redick quipped. "That's crazy."

Reaves is averaging 24.7 points (51.3% shooting from the field, 40.9% on 3-pointers), 4.7 assists and 3.3 rebounds on the season.

"One of those things that just, it sucks," Reaves said. "I was rolling, playing well. We were rolling as a team, wanted to continue to be out there and play games. Hate missing games. But it's one of those things, like I said, there wasn't much that we could really do. But we took care of business."

Austin Reaves (15) of the Los Angeles Lakers shoots a free throw against the Milwaukee Bucks on Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, at Fiserv Forum in Milwaukee. NBAE via Getty Images

Reaves' return will give the Lakers a fully healthy roster for the first time since he suffered the injury.

Bronny James was the lone player not with the team, with the Lakers assigning the third-year guard to their G League affiliate, the Coachella Valley Lakers.
