Thursday Night Football: Giants LB Malik Harrison is inactive

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Commanders and Giants had no surprises among their inactives.

The only pregame question is: Will the game start on time?

The field and stands were cleared at MetLife Stadium due to the weather: “Your attention, please. For your safety, we are going to have everyone leave the Seating Bowl area and take shelter inside of the stadium because of severe weather in the arena. Event Staff are available to guide and assist you. Those on the field, please walk calmly to the nearest tunnel, to the West Hall, or to the service corridor.”

Giants linebacker Malik Harrison was the only player on either team with a designation other than out. He was listed as questionable with a groin injury and is among the team’s inactives.

The Giants’ other inactives are cornerback Paulson Adebo, cornerback Nikko Reed, offensive lineman Josh Sills, offensive lineman J.C. Davis, tight end Thomas Fidone II and defensive lineman C.J. Okoye.

The Commanders’ inactives are quarterback Athan Kaliakmanis, cornerback Isaac Yiadom, defensive tackle Ricky Barber, offensive guard Tanoa Togiai, center Lucas Patrick (concussion) and tight end Colson Yankoff (knee). Kaliakmanis is the emergency third quarterback.
