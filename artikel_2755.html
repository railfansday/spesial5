49ers waive WR Brandin Cooks

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The 49ers announced a couple of roster moves ahead of Sunday’s game against the Vikings in Mexico City.

Veteran wide receiver Brandin Cooks has been waived to make room on the active roster for defensive lineman C.J. West. West was activated from injured reserve on Saturday.

Cooks had five catches for 58 yards and a touchdown in five appearances this season. The 49ers hope to have Mike Evans back after missing the last three games with an Achilles injury. He’s listed as questionable for Sunday night.

West had five tackles while starting the first two games of the season.

The 49ers also announced that they have downgraded cornerback Nate Hobbs to out for Sunday. He was listed as questionable with an adductor injury.
