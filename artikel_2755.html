Karl-Anthony Towns draws praise from another former coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Karl-Anthony Towns keeps drawing praise from his former coaches.

Yet another one pointed out how Towns — by playing a central role in the Knicks' championship — disproved much of the criticism that surrounded him throughout his career.

"When we first got him in Minnesota, the playoffs, they were saying how he's a very good player that couldn't win, couldn't get the team to the playoffs," Trail Blazers coach Micah Nori, who served as an assistant with the Timberwolves when Towns was there, said before the Knicks' 97-96 buzzer-beating win Saturday night at Madison Square Garden. "Then, when we got to the playoffs, any time we struggled, it was him that took the fall, unfairly. And I think it was great to see not only the impact that he had on this championship team, but to see the way that he's playing now. … I love KAT; I was happy for him to get to the mountaintop; I was happy for him to play a major role.

"Then, when we made a deep run, they said you can't win a title with him, and he got the blame."

Earlier this week, Timberwolves coach Chris Finch shared while the Knicks were in Minnesota a similar sentiment about Towns, who missed Saturday's game due to an illness, shedding a narrative that he isn't a winner.

Karl-Anthony Towns (illness) did not play in the Knicks' 97-96 buzzer-beating win over the Trail Blazers on Nov. 21, 2026, at Madison Square Garden. NBAE via Getty Images

"Particularly when he started his playoff journey, how much flak he had gotten," Finch said.

"It was fun to see him at the heart of it too, especially as they were kind of tweaking their offense going along and all of his skills came to the surface through that."

Jordan Clarkson, who missed Saturday's game due to right plantar fasciitis, was wearing a boot on his right foot while sitting on the Knicks bench.

OG Anunoby was out Saturday due to left calf soreness.

Towns' absence meant a significant role for James Wiseman, who logged 24 minutes off the bench and recorded five points, six rebounds and three blocks.

"I thought James was great," head coach Mike Brown said. "He ran the floor in both directions, he talked defensively, he impacted the ball at the rim in a phenomenal way. I thought he was really good in his minutes."

He went scoreless Saturday and missed all seven shots he took, including four 3s.
