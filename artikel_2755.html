Broncos had toughest schedule Weeks 1-8; now face one of the easiest

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The analytics nerds are back at this week with some live tracking of strength of schedule. As for the Denver Broncos, no team has faced a tougher schedule through the first eight weeks than they have. By far the toughest of all 32 teams.

There isn't a winning record within ten spots of them on this strength of schedule list and they sit at 6-2 with the second seed in the AFC presently.

They have the seventh easiest schedule moving forward. There are only three teams that are legit playoff threats: Pittsburgh Steelers, Buffalo Bills, and New England Patriots. If they can win two of those, they should be staring at 13-14 wins by the time the playoffs arrive.

If they take care of business against teams they "should" beat. That is always a question.
