Sam Darnold fully participates in Seahawks practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Seahawks quarterback Sam Darnold remains on track to return to the lineup for Sunday's game against the 49ers.

Darnold was a full participant in practice after missing the last two games with quad and rib injuries. Head coach Mike Macdonald said before practice that Darnold's participation would be part of the final determination about his status for Sunday.

"That's the plan, assuming practice goes well," Macdonald said, via the team's website. "Let's get through the practice, make sure everything feels good. He hasn't gone full speed yet since his injury, so this will be his first opportunity to go full speed."

Linebacker DeMarcus Lawrence (shoulder), right tackle Abraham Lucas (ankle), quarterback Jalen Milroe (illness), left guard Quenton Nelson (knee), and defensive tackle Jarran Reed (toe) did not participate in practice. Guard Anthony Bradford (knee, illness), left tackle Charles Cross (ankle), linebacker Ernest Jones (knee), tight end Nick Kallerup (ankle), quarterback Drew Lock (right elbow), wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba (ankle), and defensive tackle Leonard Williams (stinger) were limited participants.
