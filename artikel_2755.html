Sydney Colson, Kamilla Cardoso & Others React After Elizabeth Williams Succeeds Nneka Ogwumike As The New WNBPA President

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Elizabeth Williams inherits a union with a signed collective bargaining agreement that runs through 2032. For a decade, Nneka Ogwumike led the WNBA's players' union through two tense contract negotiations and a giant leap in pay. Now, she has stepped aside, and her fellow players have picked a successor, with the decision already drawing support from teammates, rivals and the outgoing leader.

Along with Elizabeth Williams succeeding Ogwumike as president of the WNBA Players Association, the full executive committee is also set.

Napheesa Collier is first vice president, Veronica Burton is secretary, and Chelsea Gray is treasurer. Alysha Clark, Ezi Magbegor and Maya Caldwell are the three vice presidents.

Some of the warmest cheers came from Williams' own locker room, since Cardoso plays with her in Chicago. Another heart came from the woman she replaces.

Williams won Tuesday's vote over Breanna Stewart of the Liberty and Gabby Williams of the Valkyries. Stewart joined the executive committee as a vice president in 2022, and Gabby Williams is the reigning Finals MVP.

Williams is no newcomer. The 33-year-old Sky center was the No. 4 pick in 2015 out of Duke, won Most Improved Player in 2016, made the 2017 All-Star Game with Atlanta and is a two-time All-Defense selection.

Off the court, she has been the union's secretary since 2018 and sat at the bargaining table for the 2020 and 2026 CBAs. She also served as the Dream's player representative. She becomes the fifth president in union history.

"I have served in the role of secretary for nearly a decade of my WNBA career, and I feel well equipped to lead players as the landscape of our league continues to grow," Williams said in a statement. Her priority is the league's newer faces.

"One of my main goals as president is to increase the engagement of the younger members of this union," she said. "We may not always agree, but I want to make sure every player knows that her voice is being heard."

The committee blends old and new. Collier has been a vice president since 2022, and Clark won a second straight term after six years as a team rep. Burton, Gray, Magbegor and Caldwell join the executive committee for the first time, though Gray, Magbegor and Caldwell came up as team reps, Gray for eight years.

Executive director Terri Carmichael Jackson said the focus now turns to "implementing our transformational CBA."

Before Nneka Ogwumike took over in 2016, the WNBPA had three presidents: Coquese Washington, Sonja Henning and Tamika Catchings, marking three leadership transitions since the union's founding in 1998. Williams was elected as the union's fifth president.

How did Ogwumike do? The numbers make the case. When she was profiled as president in 2019, the maximum base salary was $117,500. Her 2020 deal lifted the average salary to $130,000 and guaranteed full pay for players on maternity leave.

The 2026 deal raised maximum salaries to more than $1 million and created the league's first million-dollar contracts.

Reports called those talks tense, and in 2019, players such as Tina Charles said they might sit out, though Ogwumike said, "We want to play."

However, she never initially planned on taking the job. She said she "wasn't really going for" the job until Tamika Catchings pulled her aside in 2016.

That makes her September words a contrast with her own start. "I'm hoping that now this role is a desirable role. It's a leadership role that people want to step into," she said, adding that it is "kind of light work for the next few years."

A ballot with Stewart and Gabby Williams in it looks like the interest she wanted.

The peace may be real. The Athletic's Sabreena Merchant wrote that Williams will likely preside over "relative labor peace," but the other side of the table is changing, too.

Commissioner Cathy Engelbert retires at the end of the year, and the search for her replacement is ongoing. For now, the union's new era starts with a deep ballot, a signed deal, and a long list of congratulations.
