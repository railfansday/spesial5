Patrick Mahomes' Wife Brittany Mahomes Pregnant With Baby No. 4

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Patrick Mahomes and his wife Brittany Mahomes announced they are expecting their fourth baby together, meaning their kids Sterling, 5, Bronze, 3, and Golden, 21 months, will soon have a new teammate.

Patrick Mahomes and Brittany Mahomes are still filling out their roster.

The Kansas City Chiefs quarterback and his wife revealed they are expecting their fourth baby together with a special announcement alongside their kids Sterling, 5, Bronze, 3, and Golden, 21 months.

“Another blessing from God,” the couple wrote in a joint Instagram post Nov. 10, sharing a photo of their kids with their hands on Brittany’s baby bump. “& then there were 6.”

The news comes nearly two years after Patrick and Brittany—who tied the knot in March 2022—welcomed baby Golden, weeks before the NFL star led the Chiefs to an appearance in the 2025 Super Bowl.

And since becoming a mom, Brittany often calls the plays that make the most sense for her family.

"When you become a mom, you learn to say no to things that aren't as important and focus on what's best for your life and your family," the 31-year-old told Yahoo Entertainment in July. "Keeping my eyes and my mind and my focus on my family and the things that matter the most."

Brittany continued, "Everything I do now is connected to creating more opportunities and creating a future that my children can be proud of.”

In fact, motherhood has “reshaped and redefined” most aspects of the Kansas City Current co-owner’s life.

"It's made me more intentional, more purposeful and honestly, more motivated to keep going and keep showing my kids that there are no boundaries in front of you," she added. "You can achieve anything. You can be a part of anything that you want to. You need to dream big and understand that you have to work hard and do the hard things to do the fun things."
