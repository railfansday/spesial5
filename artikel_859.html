Chrissy Teigen Shares Scary Bedroom Habit When John Legend Is Away

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Chrissy Teigen likes to put on graphic movies like Saw when her husband John Legend is out of the bedroom, telling E! News at the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala, “I'm a horror and gore freak.”

Chrissy Teigen is ready to slice and dice up a solo movie night… with a side of gore.

In fact, the cookbook author detailed how she leans into her scary movie guilty pleasure when husband John Legend is out of the bedroom.

“I'm a horror and gore freak,” Teigen told E! News’ Erin Lim Rhodes at the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala Presented by Paul Mitchell in Los Angeles Nov. 14. “When I finally get to go into bed alone if John's gone or something, I put on the Saw trilogy.”

As the 40-year-old put it, “I love it. It just makes me happy—horror.”

The Sports Illustrated swimsuit model was especially a fan of Baby2Baby’s Giving Tree Award recipient Demi Moore, whose performance in The Substance landed her an Oscar nomination for Best Actress in 2025.

“Seeing that psychological thriller with her?” Teigen added. “Oh, it was so good. So good!”

While she may be energized by watching a horror movie or two, Teigen—mom to kids Luna, 10, Miles, 8, Esti, 3, and Wren, 3, with Legend—finds other spooky traditions to be a little scarier.

“I’m still recovering from Halloween,” she continued. “Four kids with their four class parties and then their own thing? It's been a week, I'll tell you.”

Still, Teigen wouldn’t trade her exhaustion for the world because she’s “so grateful” for her kids.

“They're incredible,” she said. “They're just four little beings.”

And her motherhood journey has not only inspired her work with the children’s nonprofit Baby2Baby for the past decade, but it’s also given her an appreciation for the chaos that her little ones bring to her life.

“It’s taught me that you really can love—your heart has room for so many different beings,” she told Motherly in 2025. “And one of the best things it’s taught me is to really embrace imperfection. When I first became a mother, little spills on the carpet, that kind of stuff drove me insane. But I really have grown into loving living in a lived-in house and a house that feels not so perfect. You get to let your kids be kids.”
