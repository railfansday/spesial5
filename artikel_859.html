All Blacks star Barrett in talks to join Bristol

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Beauden Barrett has had interest from multiple northern hemisphere clubs, including some in France's Top 14; the 35-year-old became only the second All Blacks player to reach 150 caps during his side's Nations Championship defeat by England on Saturday

Beauden Barrett is in talks to join Bristol Bears on a two-year deal following the 2027 Rugby World Cup.

The 35-year-old, who became only the second All Black to reach 150 international caps during England's 37-12 victory over New Zealand on Saturday, is also reportedly attracting interest from clubs in France's Top 14.

Should he put pen to paper on a deal with the Bears, he would likely become the club's marquee signing, meaning his wages would sit outside of Bristol's salary cap.

It would be one of the most significant acquisitions in Prem history, with Barrett, who can play at both fly-half and full-back, revered as one of the All Blacks' greatest players.

The 2015 Rugby World Cup winner is the fourth most-capped player in international rugby history and, for the All Blacks, ranks second for most tries and third for most points scored.

Barrett began his Super Rugby career playing for the Hurricanes in 2011, and after winning the Super Rugby championship in 2015, he moved to the Blues. He has played for the Auckland-based club since.

In recent years, Barrett also enjoyed spells with Japanese sides Tokyo Sungoliath in 2021 and Toyota Verblitz in 2023.

A move to the northern hemisphere would present a great opportunity for Barrett, who will be 36 after the 2027 Rugby World Cup and is unlikely to represent his country after that tournament.

As part of the All Blacks selection requirements, players must generally be contracted to a team in New Zealand to represent the All Blacks, and a move to Europe could likely end his international career.

He would not be the first of the Barrett brothers to play for a European club. His younger brother, Jordie, signed a short-term deal with Leinster in December 2024.

Barrett's potential arrival at Bristol would bolster an already dangerous backline which includes the likes of Benhard Janse van Rensburg, Gabriel Ibitoye and Louis Rees-Zammit.
