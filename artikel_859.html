Newcastle United star Sven Botman has been banned from driving after he was caught using his mobile phone at the wheel.

The Ministry of Justice (MoJ) confirmed that Botman, 26, pleaded guilty at Newcastle Magistrates Court on Wednesday.

The Dutch international defender was given six penalty points and disqualified from driving for six months, as these were added to points he had already accrued.

The MoJ said Botman was fined £900 and ordered to pay £400 costs and a £360 surcharge.

Court documents show that Botman was in a Land Rover on the A189 Haddricks Mill Road, in Newcastle, on 17 September when he was spotted using his phone.

According to The Sun, the court heard he was filmed by a cyclist while he was stationary in traffic.

The Dutch centre-half was signed by Newcastle in a £32 million deal from French club Lille during the summer of 2022.

Since signing for the Magpies, Botman has made 124 appearances and was part of the squad that won the League Cup in 2025, their first major trophy in decades.
