ChargePoint and PistenBully have a charging plan for ski resorts

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The next EV to plug in at a ski resort might be the one that groomed your trail. ChargePoint has struck a deal with Kässbohrer, the German company behind PistenBully snow groomers, to bring charging to ski resorts and indoor ski facilities across Europe and North America.

Under the agreement, Kässbohrer will connect its customers with ChargePoint for chargers, software, and support with planning, installation, and day-to-day operations. Resorts can use the same provider for the snow groomers and other vehicles they keep at the maintenance depot, as well as for the EV chargers skiers use while they’re out on the slopes.

Kässbohrer’s first fully electric groomer, the PistenBully 100 E, uses electric motors to move the machine and spin the tiller that smooths the snow. It has a 196 kWh battery and can handle up to three hours of normal grooming on a charge, according to the manufacturer. It can take up to 22 kW on AC or 150 kW on a DC fast charger. It fast charges from zero to 100% in about an hour.

ChargePoint says Kässbohrer accounts for more than half of the snow groomers sold worldwide each year. Resorts can start working with the companies now. Which resorts will install ChargePoint’s chargers first, and how many will go in, are TBC.
