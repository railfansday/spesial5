Tanggal: 2026-11-18

See the exterior of the Toyota Scion Kei-01 from every angle.

Toyota looked at the compact-pickup segment and said, “Hold my baby.” That baby, of course, is the Scion by Toyota Kei-01, which will be assembled in Indiana starting in 2027. While the Kei-01 is restricted to just 25 mph, Toyota hopes it will go far, sprouting a new era and segment for pickups in America.

The Scion Kei-01 will initially be sold in one configuration, an extended single cab with a five-foot-six-inch cargo bed.

Beyond its name, very few changes were made to prepare the Kei-01 for the U.S. market.

The cargo bed extends into this small cargo space under the cab, which is perfect for a toolbox and provides extra access to the powertrain via a removable panel.

The trifold bed sides increase the already great ergonomics of the Kei-01’s low-load pickup bed.

At 11 feet in length, the Kei-01 measures a full two feet shorter than the Mazda MX-5 Miata.

The Kei-01 is powered by a tiny three-cylinder engine with a continuously variable automatic transmission.

The last time a new automobile in the U.S. had wheel specs this size was the 1994 Geo Metro or Ford Fiesta.
