Germany player ratings vs. Serbia: Gruda steals the show on dream debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Germany won their crucial encounter in the Nations League against Serbia with 3-1.

Below are our player ratings for Jürgen Klopp's side on a scale from 1 to 10, with 10 being the highest possible score.

What a way to announce yourself on the international stage! Brajan Gruda showed exactly why he deserved a place in the starting XI ahead of Bayern youngster Lennart Karl. A dream start to his international career and a Man of the Match-worthy display against Serbia.
