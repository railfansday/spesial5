Vegan Pumpkin Pie

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

PSA: This vegan pumpkin pie is so good, it might rival the classic. It’s just as easy as any other pumpkin pie recipe, if not easier. Bonus points: the nutty, pecan-based crust is naturally gluten-free! Using coconut oil in the crust and coconut milk in the filling, I was able to replicate the richness of pumpkin pie without sacrificing any flavor. This vegan treat will satisfy those pumpkin pie cravings, but with a unique nutty crust that amplifies all the fall flavors.

The best part about this pie is that you don’t need to chill the pie dough at all. Preheat your oven to 375° and grease your pie plate.

Then, use a food processor to pulse the pecans, oats, sugar, and salt until they’re fine crumbs. Stream in the coconut oil and then pulse until the dough forms. You’re done! The easiest pie dough you’ll ever make.

Press the dough into your greased pie plate in an even layer– make sure you press the dough up the sides of the pan as well. Place parchment over the dough and fill with dried beans, rice, or pie weights. Bake for 10 minutes and then remove the parchment and bake for another 10 minutes.

To make the filling, whisk all of the filling ingredients together in a large bowl. Pour it into the par-baked crust, smoothing the top.

Bake the pie in the oven for 50 to 55 minutes. If the crust starts to get too dark, cover the whole pie with foil. You’ll know the pie is done when it looks set and dry on top but is still slightly jiggly in the center. Let cool in the refrigerator before serving with some whipped topping alongside.

Keep your pie in the fridge for up to 4 days, loosely covered with foil. Keep the whipped cream separate and dollop onto each pie slice as you serve. If the whipped cream loses structure, keep it cold and re-whip with a handheld mixer as needed.
