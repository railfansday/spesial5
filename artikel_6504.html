Giant Sheet-Pan Chocolate Almond Croissant

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Recipe by Margaret Knoebel, Milwaukee, Wisconsin

Our giant sheet-pan chocolate almond croissant has a filling of frangipane and melted chocolate chips between two light, buttery sheets of puff pastry.

This giant croissant is practically the size of a sheet pan. The bigger, the better, as they say, but we’re more into its shareable nature and simple-to-make design. Shatteringly crisp puff pastry (store-bought, of course) replaces hard-to-make croissant dough, with extra square footage for both this treat’s iconic croissant fillings: pain au chocolat and almond croissant. Win-win-win. Save this giant sheet-pan chocolate almond croissant for special occasions or holiday mornings.

Preheat the oven to 425°F. On a lightly floured surface, roll out each sheet of puff pastry to a 12×10-inch rectangle. Place one on a parchment-lined 15x10x1-inch baking pan. Top the puff pastry with a second piece of parchment paper and a second 15x10x1-inch baking pan. This keeps the puff pastry from rising too much so it stays flat.

Bake the puff pastry for 15 minutes. Remove the top pan and parchment. Continue baking the puff pastry until it’s golden brown, five to seven minutes longer. Cool the puff pastry on a wire rack. Repeat the same process with the second slice of puff pastry.

Meanwhile, in a small bowl, beat together the softened butter, almond flour and confectioners’ sugar until they’re light and fluffy, two to three minutes. Add the egg and beat well until it’s incorporated.

Place one baked sheet of puff pastry on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Spread two-thirds of the almond filling over it.

Sprinkle the chocolate chips evenly all over. Carefully place the second sheet of baked puff pastry over top, and pipe or spread the remaining third of the almond filling on top.

Reduce the oven setting to 350°. Bake the croissant just until the filling is set, 18 to 20 minutes longer. Cool the croissant completely on a wire rack before serving.

Just as you’re ready to serve, dust the top with confectioners’ sugar and, if desired, drizzle it with warm hot fudge.

Store leftover giant sheet-pan chocolate almond croissant in an airtight container in the fridge. It can be kept for up to three days. Just know that it is best enjoyed the day of, as the frangipane will make the puff pastry soggy.

Yes, you can make certain aspects of a giant sheet-pan chocolate almond croissant ahead of time, but wait to assemble and bake it until you’re ready to serve. You can bake the puff pastry sheets one day ahead, let them cool to room temperature, wrap them tightly in storage wrap and store them at room temperature. Make the frangipane up to two days beforehand and keep it covered in the fridge.

Yes, you can make a gluten-free version of this giant sheet-pan chocolate almond croissant. Simply replace the puff pastry with a gluten-free variety. DuFour, Sweet Loren’s and Schar make fantastic gluten-free puff pastry. All other ingredients are naturally gluten free (but do double-check the hot fudge, if you’re using it); just make sure they weren’t processed in a facility that handles wheat.

Serve your giant sheet-pan chocolate almond croissant with strong coffee or tea. Pair it with other brunch, like egg casseroles, plates of bacon and sausage and a fruit salad. I wouldn’t set out any other sweets—this croissant is plenty!
