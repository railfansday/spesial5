The Fringe Pieces Fashion Insiders Are Wearing This Fall

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Runways say this is the adornment of the season.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Fringe never really goes out of style. No matter the season, you can always expect to see the embellishment swaying from the arm of a suede jacket or dangling off a leather bag. So when it comes to trends around the design detail, it’s a mere matter of what form the fringe takes. Lately, fashion’s found itself in a fringe fixation, prompting the trim to move beyond the Western-inspired looks it’s best known for.

Fringe re-emerged on the scene last fall when spring/summer 2026 catwalks from Bottega Veneta, Givenchy, and Chanel showcased the adornment as playful ornamentation for skirts, bags, and even shoes. By the fall/winter 2026 season, others were beginning to chime in with their own takes. There were the boho-inspired bags from Etro, the romantic wispy dresses from Ulla Johnson, and restrained silhouettes from Altuzarra.

Whether you’re looking for a bold statement or a little wink of style, fringe remains a simple way to add flair to any outfit. And right now, there’s a fringed version of just about any wardrobe piece you could think of. Between the classic leather staples and newer avant-garde styles, you’ll find something for every style sensibility.

Do an online search for fringe jackets, and you’ll find no shortage of options to shop. Leather and suede remain tried-and-true materials for this style of outerwear—but now there’s also a plethora of other textiles to shop as well. Opt for Toteme’s viral scarf coat if you’re in the market for a new winter coat, or this tweed number from Etro if you’re looking for a polished piece you can wear to the office.

If recent fall/winter 2026 runways from Givenchy and Proenza Schouler show us anything, it’s that fringe dresses are reaching new heights. The trim is an especially pretty touch to formalwear and event dresses, providing playful movement with every step to even the simplest silhouettes.

Just like dresses, fringe skirts are experiencing a similarly sophisticated evolution. Lately, the style has favored sleek satin fringe to create a distinct piece that will immediately elevate anything it’s paired with.

The sky’s the limit when it comes to fringe bags these days. There’s long fringe and short fringe. There’s standard leather and suede trim that adds a timeless flair, or more dramatic confections made of sequins, beads, or feathers. With beaded tassel bags on the rise, it makes sense that fringe would take similar cues for an eclectic flourish.

After Givenchy debuted a pair of bold fringed mules as part of its spring/summer 2026 collection, fringe exploded onto shoes—heels in particular—in a big way. These Boudoir mules, as they came to be known, introduced a playful take on fringe footwear. Now it’s an ultra-luxe, fashion-forward style that’s perfect for those who never shy away from making a statement.
