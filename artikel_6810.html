“Dance-Offs” Are Coming to ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Tonight: Here’s How the Scoring Works

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

It’s Grammy Night on Dancing With the Stars, and there’s a twist! On top of performing to award-winning songs, the remaining couples (minus Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson) are competing in “dance-offs,” where they’ll be performing the same dance style to the same song—at the same time. A little confused as to how the whole thing goes down? Here’s what to expect tonight.

As the show puts it in their official press release: “Two at a time, couples face off against one another on the ballroom floor as they dance head-to-head in the same style at the same time. Winners of each of the three Dance-Offs will be selected by the judges, and the winning couple from each dance-off will receive bonus points added to their judges’ totals for the night.”
