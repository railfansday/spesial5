David Fincher Says DC Is ‘Dysfunctional’ and Turned Down ‘Lord of the Rings’ TV Show Because ‘It’s an Extension of a Corporate Interest’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

David Fincher doesn’t want to get tied up in big franchises anytime soon.

The “Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth” director was interviewed on the Nov. 23 episode of Josh Horowitz’s “Happy Sad Confused” podcast and spoke about his hesitancy of working on overly corporate projects.

“I got offered … what is the ‘Lord of the Rings’ thing at Amazon?” Fincher asked during the interview. “I got offered that … to showrun,” Fincher continued. “This is before it went to Amazon, right? And I was like, ‘No way.’ That is an extension of a corporate interest.”

He pivoted to discussing how he didn’t envy James Gunn and the position he accepted as co-CEO of DC Studios, which he recently announced he was leaving.

“I mean, I look at James Gunn and I think, ‘Those are bigger balls than I have. I would not … take on DC and all of its dysfunction. Its innate DNA is dysfunctional.’”

“I think there’s a horrible constricting of the business right now,” Fincher added later in the conversation. “You know, with Paramount, Warner Bros. … It’s becoming more and more difficult to protect the individual voice in cinema.”

Fincher also said that he once met with Kathleen Kennedy and had a long conversation with her about taking Lucusfilm public before it sold to Disney.

“I was very interested in the idea of ‘Star Wars’ and what I said to her was, ‘Why don’t we go to Wall Street?’” Fincher said.

“Cliff Booth” is opening on Imax screens on Nov. 25.
