When will GTA 6 be available on Xbox Game Pass and Cloud Gaming?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

With just days to go before Grand Theft Auto VI comes out, Xbox players might be wondering if they'll have to buy the game at all.

We still don't know if the Xbox version will be downgraded in any way from the PS5 version, but for those who only have an Xbox, that doesn't matter. What does matter is whether the game will be available on the Game Pass subscription service, and when it will be available for streaming via Xbox Cloud Gaming.

One of these questions has an easy answer, and the other one doesn't. Let's get into it.

One very important note here: You have to own the game to be able to stream it.

That takes us to our next question. Xbox Game Pass is a monthly subscription that comes with a rotating catalog of old and new games you can play at no additional cost. At the time of publication, there has been no indication that GTA 6 is coming to Game Pass at or near launch, or on any announced date.

Of course, Rockstar Games could eventually agree to put GTA 6 on Game Pass down the road. But right now, there's no confirmed timeline, so players hoping to play at launch should plan on buying it.
