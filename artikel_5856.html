Measles Elimination Status Revoked: What's Next?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

— Paul Offit and Noel Brewer discuss the measles resurgence and what it will take to reverse course

What does it mean for the U.S. to lose its measles elimination status after more than 25 years -- and what will it take to regain it?

In this 45-minute webinar, MedPage Today Editor-in-Chief Jeremy Faust, MD, speaks with pediatric infectious disease expert Paul Offit, MD, of Children's Hospital of Philadelphia, and public health researcher Noel Brewer, PhD, of the University of North Carolina in Chapel Hill, about the nation's loss of measles elimination status. The discussion examines the resurgence of measles outbreaks, declining vaccination rates, timing of the measles, mumps, and rubella (MMR) vaccine, the influence of vaccine misinformation, and the challenges facing public health officials as they work to restore confidence in vaccination and prevent further transmission.
