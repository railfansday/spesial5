Billy Ray Cyrus Shares Rare Photos of Son Christopher on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Billy Ray Cyrus celebrated Thanksgiving surrounded by family, including his rarely seen son Christopher Cody Cyrus, his wife Nicole and their baby Hudson.

Billy Ray Cyrus is celebrating in the heart of family.

The 65-year-old proved his heart is anything but achy breaky as he and partner Elizabethy Hurley enjoyed a blended Thanksgiving with their loved ones, including a rare appearance from Billy Ray’s son Christopher Cody Cyrus, whom the singer welcomed with ex-girlfriend Kristin Luckey.

“Happy Thanksgiving!” Billy Ray captioned his Nov. 26 Instagram carousel. “I am so thankful for this moment - grateful for this time with my incredible son Cody, his amazing wife Nicole and my beautiful grandson Hudson. Feeling truly blessed.”

In fact, Billy Ray was all smiles as he posed alongside Cody and the 34-year-old’s wife and baby in a restaurant. Also featured in the snaps were Elizabeth and her son Damian Charles Hurley, 24, the latter of whom cuddled baby Hudson close in one photo.

Damian, born in England to Elizabeth and American filmmaker Steve Bing, also leaned into the Turkey Day festivities, sharing Billy Ray’s post to his own Story and writing, “Happy Thanksgiving!”

As for Cody, while he leads a far more private life then many of his Cyrus siblings—Billy Ray also adopted Tish Cyrus’ kids Brandi Cyrus, 39, and Trace Cyrus, 37, during their marriage, in addition to the pair welcoming Miley Cyrus, 34, Braison Cyrus, 32, and Noah Cyrus, 26—he did attend Miley’s nuptials to Maxx Morando on Nov. 21.

In fact, Cody was among the Cyrus family members featured in Billy Ray’s photo carousel from the wedding, with the country singer raving over the family affair.

“My little girl married the love of her life! I can’t describe how beautiful it felt to have all my family together in one room, united by love,” he captioned his Nov. 24 post. “@miley words can’t begin to express how proud you make me every day… I’m the proudest dad alive!!”

The Hannah Montana alum also reserved some special words for his now son-in-law.

“@mmaxxm I’m so honored to welcome you to the family,” Billy Ray continued. “The two of you kids are a match made in heaven… Happy, happy birthday Miley - I can’t wait to keep celebrating you both today and forever!”

For a closer look at how Cody fits into the larger Cyrus family tree, read on.

Billy Ray Cyrus married Tish Cyrus (née Leticia Finley) on Dec. 28, 2003.

At the time they already shared 12-month-old daughter Destiny Hope Cyrus—you may know her as Miley Cyrus—and Tish was pregnant with their son Braison Cyrus, who was born May 9, 1994.

Seven months before Miley was born on Nov. 23, 1992, Billy Ray's ex-girlfriend Kristin Luckey gave birth to their son Christopher Cody Cyrus on April 8.

When the "Achy Breaky Heart" singer married Tish, he adopted her daughter Brandi Cyrus (born May 26, 1987) and son Trace Cyrus (Feb. 24, 1989), whose biological father is their mom's ex-husband Baxter Helson.

Tish and Billy Ray's youngest, daughter Noah Cyrus, was born Jan. 8, 2000.

From 2006 to 2010, Billy Ray played the father of Miley's normal-kid-who-moonlights-as-a-pop-star character in the Disney Channel sitcom Hannah Montana and a 2009 film adaptation.

While the Cyrus family was still based in Nashville, Billy Ray and Miley made the move to L.A. to jumpstart her own entertainment career.

Billy Ray and Tish announced in October 2010 that they were getting divorced, but by the following year were back together.

They filed for divorce again in June 2013 but reconsidered barely a month later, telling E! News at the time that they'd been to couple's counseling and it had done wonders for them.

But before they reconciled, when the news broke that her dad had made the first move in a Nashville court to end his marriage after 19 years, Miley tweeted, "@billyraycyrus since your text and email obviously aren't working would you like to talk like this?"

After starring in 2010's The Last Song together and dating off and on for a decade, Miley and Liam Hemsworth married in December 2018, not long after their Malibu home burned down.

The decision came "from a place of trauma and just trying to rebuild as quickly as we could," Miley recalled on her "Used to Be Young" TikTok series.

But Liam filed for divorce in August 2019 and they finalized the paperwork in January 2020.

"It's honestly very simple," a source told E! at the time. "They fell in love when they were young and they are just different people now."

Braison and his wife Stella Cyrus welcomed son Bear Chance Cyrus on June 8, 2021.

Tish filed for divorce fo a third time in April 2022, and this time it stuck.

Moreover, her petition citing "irreconcilable differences" noted that she and her husband of almost 30 years hadn't lived together since February 2020.

"It is after 30 years, five amazing children and a lifetime of memories, we have decided to go our separate ways—not with sadness, but with love in our hearts," Tish and Billy Ray told People in a statement. "We have grown up together, raised a family we can be so proud of, and it is now time to create our own paths."

The exes noted that they hadn't come to the decision to split up for good "lightly or quickly," adding, "We will always be family and look forward to a continued and loving shared experience as friends and parents."

Billy Ray quietly got engaged to Australian singer Firerose, who's 27 years his junior, in August 2022 but waited to confirm the news until that November.

"Growing up in Australia, my love of music was predominant over everything," Firerose told People. "I've known his music since forever."

What started as a friendship turned into more during the darkest days of the COVID-19 pandemic.

"All of the sudden, the life that I've always known as a touring artist didn't exist anymore," Billy Ray said. "A moment of so much change. And at the same time, Firerose, who had been such a light of positivity, such a best friend. And then when we began sharing the music, it just evolved more into, as musical soulmates, to soulmates, happy, pure love that to me, I didn't know could exist."

Around the time her ex-husband confirmed his engagment in November 2022, Tish went Instagram-official with Prison Break actor Dominic Purcell.

They got engaged in April 2023 and tied the knot that August in Malibu at sunset with Miley, Trace and Brandi all standing by their mom's side.

"Life is unpredictable & always full of changes," Trace captioned a photo of himself and two of his sibling with the bride. "I think the most important thing to do is cherish the past, look forward to the future, & always be happy in the present moment because that’s really all we have."

"To see my mom this happy and in love is very emotional for me," Miley told Vogue ahead of her mom's wedding. "Dom and my mom share the sweetest, most genuine love. It's almost like it's for the first time, which is so fitting for my mom who is such a young soul."

As for her dad's next chapter, however, a source close to Miley told E! News in October 2022 that she wasn't in love with it and didn't have a relationship with her future stepmom.

"The divorce between her parents put a strain on her relationship with her dad and it's been hard to connect with him in the last year," the source said. "She hopes they can eventually work on their relationship."

Billy Ray told People that November, however, that his family had "no hard feelings" about his engagement.

"Everybody's turning the page," he said. "It's been a lot, but everyone knew that it was time for a change."

He and Firerose got married on Oct. 10, 2023, calling it the "beautiful, joyous day that our two souls united as one in holy matrimony."

Only Some of Miley's Family Get Their Flowers

Whatever rift opened up between Miley and her dad apparently hadn't been repaired by the night of the 2024 Grammys on Feb. 4.

Tish and Brandi were there, as was Miley's boyfriend Maxx Morando, and they all got shout-outs when the "Flowers" singer won Record of the Year.

As Miley concluded her speech, "I don't think I forgot anyone."

None of the three officially confirmed or denied eyebrow-raising reports that Noah was involved with Dominic before her mother was, but the Grammy-nominated singer had some choice words for an Instagram commenter in April 2024 after the person crudely suggested that Noah and Tish both had "sexy time" with the actor.

"I'm so sorry but will you pleeeeeass just choke on the fattest c--k," Noah wrote in response. "Just for a lil bit. Great thanks."

Barely eight months after they tied the knot, Billy Ray filed to annul the union, alleging in court documents the marriage was "obtained by fraud."

He listed May 22, 2024, as their date of separation.

However, Billy Ray amended his plan and filed for divorce, which was settled that August.

"This has been absolutely the most crazy insane scam I have ever heard of," Billy Ray said in a statement to E! News. "Let alone the fact I'm right in the center and basically the target of the scheme. It's unfortunate it played out this way."

“Since my earliest memories all I can remember is being obsessed with you and thinking you were the coolest person ever," Trace began a Jan. 22, 2025, Instagram post directed at his father. "I wanted to be just like you. The day you adopted me was the happiest day of my life. Sadly the man that I wanted so desperately to be just like I barely recognize now."

Referring to his sisters, he continued, "Me and the girls have been genuinely worried about you for years but you’ve pushed all of us away."

Noah was especially hurt, according to Trace.

"Noah desperately has wanted you to be a part of her life and you haven't even been there for her," he wrote. "That’s your baby girl. She deserves better. Somehow just like me she still idolizes you though. We are all hanging on to memories of the man we once knew & hoping for the day he returns."

After his son Trace said he and his sisters—including Miley, Brandi and Noah—were worried about Billy Ray, the country music star wrote in the caption of a YouTube Shorts post on Jan. 26, 2025. “Praying for my family. For my children … sons and daughters…and their mother."

In March 2025, Billy Ray seemed to signal that his bond with his kids was improving as he shared a touching tribute to Miley and Noah in honor of their latest music projects.

“Flesh and blood … completely taking their art to a whole new level. Congratulations girls,” he wrote on Instagram at the time. “I’m so damn proud of both of you. I’m actually crying as I write this. Thank God you can’t see me.”

In May 2025, Billy Ray shared a photo of himself with Miley, her boyfriend Maxx Morando and his son Braison Cyrus at what appears to be the "Achy Breaky Heart" singer's farm in Tennessee. He captioned his Instagram post, “Happy Birthday Braison!!!!!"

One day later, Miley broke her silence about the family rift rumors, noting on Instamgram that she and her dad "have had our challenges over the years."

Miley continued, "Now, in my thirties, family is my priority above all else. I'm at peace knowing bridges have been built and time has done a lot of healing. Grateful for the good health and love that flows through my family."
