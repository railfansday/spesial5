Where to buy GTA 6 Limited-Edition Controller: Live restock updates at Amazon, Walmart, and Target

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

GTA 6 is finally arriving next week, and the heavily in-demand limited-edition PS5 DualSense controllers have been making a surprising comeback with select restocks before Rockstar’s long-awaited sequel launches.

Sony’s collaboration with Rockstar Games delivered two distinctive blue-and-purple, neon-infused designs inspired by the streets of Vice City, each available in black and white for $84.99. However, as preorders opened on Sept. 10, stock allocations sold out everywhere — especially the black version, which is sold exclusively via PlayStation Direct.

With both GTA 6 DualSense controllers scheduled for release on Nov. 19, several major stores still have preorder listings for the white edition, with intermittent restocks expected over the next week.

Be sure to keep this page bookmarked as we’ve checked across go-to retailers, confirmed where you can buy DualSense right now, and provided the full list of links you’ll need to check for the best chance of grabbing GTA 6’s limited edition controllers going forward.

Before GTA 6's launch on Nov. 19, these are the main retailers in the U.S. to bookmark and check for preorders:

PlayStation Direct (Black): Your only official U.S. retailer for the black controller. Sony has imposed a one-per-order limit, so don’t try to grab more than one if you’re lucky to get through.

PlayStation Direct (White): Also listed through Sony's storefront, the white version isn't exclusive. With that, there are several alternatives if PlayStation Direct runs out.

Amazon: A live listing for the white DualSense is available to buy now.

Walmart: The GTA 6 limited-edition PS5 DualSense controller is in stock at the time of writing. Shipping’s estimated to arrive on the game’s Nov. 19 release day.

Target: The white GTA 6 controller is in stock at the time of writing, though at the standard $84.99 price. The shipping date’s a little later than Walmart.

GameStop: Currently available to preorder online at $84.99. However, the delivery date is estimated for Nov. 20-25.

Best Buy: The white edition controller at $84.99 is out of stock online, but is currently marked as “Coming Soon."

GTA 6’s massive hype has undoubtedly led shoppers directly to the game’s dedicated PS5 DualSense controllers. It's the natural way of things.

As reported by IGN, 100 million PS5s have now been sold, including 2.1 million in the last quarter. It feels like a lot of recent sales have been driven by GTA 6. PC players need to wait, and the Xbox Series X|S’s install base pales in comparison to PlayStation, making the PS5 the default way to play GTA 6.

Not every one of the 100 million PS5 owners in the world will be playing GTA 6 or would even want the controller, but it does mean that the pool of potential buyers who’d want a dedicated GTA-themed DualSense controller is significant. Both controllers draw inspiration from GTA 6's fictional Vice City setting, though each represents a different side of the game's Florida-inspired location.

The white DualSense adopts a brighter appearance influenced by Vice City's beaches, pastel colors, and a sunny daytime atmosphere. The black alternative draws on the city's neon-lit nightlife, with darker tones surrounding its colorful accents — representing the long nights in Vice City.

Alongside the prominent VI branding on the touchpad, each has textured palm tree detailing across the grips and a shimmering, color-shifting finish that changes appearance as the light catches it. Underneath those cosmetic changes, they're effectively standard PS5 DualSense wireless controllers. That means you're still getting haptic feedback, adaptive triggers, a built-in microphone, motion controls, and the familiar touchpad while you revel in Jason and Lucia's Leonida adventures.

Both controllers can also be used with compatible PCs, Macs, and mobile devices.

If the GTA 6 DualSense is unavailable, there are a few ways to make the search less frustrating:

Get your retailer accounts ready: Sign in to Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy, and PlayStation Direct ahead of time, with your payment and delivery information saved. That way, you won't have to scramble through checkout if new stock appears.

Remember PlayStation Direct’s exclusivity for the black controller: Since it's the only official U.S. retailer carrying this version, there's little benefit in searching elsewhere unless you're considering resale listings.

Use stock alerts where available: Retailer notifications and independent trackers can help flag when inventory is returning without requiring you to manually check every product page throughout the day.

Check local stores around launch: Best Buy, Target, and GameStop could be worth contacting starting Nov. 19. In-store inventory isn't guaranteed, but it offers another option if online orders become unavailable entirely.

Avoid paying collector prices too early: Both controllers have an official $84.99 recommended retail price. Listings substantially above that are not worthwhile while there's still a chance of additional stock reaching major stores.

It's possible, although Sony hasn't confirmed a dedicated Black Friday restock.

With GTA 6 launching on Nov. 19 and Black Friday 2026 taking place on Nov. 27, there's a relatively short gap between the release date and the biggest shopping event of the year. With that, retailers could receive more inventory around launch or hold additional units for the holiday shopping period. However, neither possibility is guaranteed, especially for the black controller, which remains exclusive to PlayStation Direct in the U.S.

Even if there were more stock in time for Black Friday, it doesn't necessarily mean these special editions will be discounted. Given their limited availability and high demand, retailers have no reason to knock anything off the price just yet. At this stage, selling out at MSRP is already almost a guarantee.

For now, the safest approach is to keep checking the retailer pages above and secure your preferred design if it becomes available at a reasonable price. Waiting until Black Friday might pay off, but there's a slim chance that either controller will still be available to buy after Nov. 19.
