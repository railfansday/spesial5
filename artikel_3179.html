‘Sunrise On The Reaping’ Firing Up Thanksgiving With $85M+ 5-Day, 2nd Best Holiday Haul For Franchise – Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

WEDNESDAY AFTERNOON: If extreme weather doesn’t shutter theaters in the Northeast, then Lionsgate is looking forward to a lucrative Thanksgiving stretch with The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping potentially banking $85M+ over five-days.

Should the sixth title in the Suzanne Collins series file in that range, it will rep the second best Thanksgiving haul for a Hunger Games movie after Catching Fire‘s $109.9M Wednesday-Sunday turkey take 13 years ago. Sunrise on the Reaping‘s Wednesday is looking like $15.5M-$16.5M with a second 3-day weekend around $58M-$65M at 4,018 theaters, -46% to -51%. Five day forecasts are always a challenge for the industry to predict: Business dips on Thanksgiving then spikes on Black Friday, which is one of the most active moviegoing days of the year. By Sunday, we’re looking at a running cume for the Francis Lawrence directed movie of $233M domestic, which would be 40% higher than the lifetime cume on 2023’s Hunger Games: Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

Second will either be Disney’s Hexed at 3,725 theaters or Universal/Paramount’s Focker-in-Law at 3,382 sites. Hexed is seeing around $7.8M today (including $2.1M previews), for a 3-day around $20M and 5-day between $35M-$40M. Let’s see if this picks up before passing complete judgement on this $200M original animation production, but it’s clear Hexed isn’t Coco or Frozen. Watch on Deadline

Focker-in-Law is seeing a $5.2M Wednesday, 3-day of $24.8M and 5-day of $36.1M. No Rotten Tomatoes audience scores yet on either title.

Fourth goes to Paramount’s third frame of Johnny Depp Christmas movie, Ebenezer with $1.5M today at 3,180 sites and a 3-day between $6.8M-$7.3M, -6% to -13%, for a 5-day of $10M-$10.6M and running cume on the high end of $45.9M.

Fifth is Amazon MGM Studio’s fourth frame of I Play Rocky at 2,711 with $1.4M today, 3-day of $6.6M, and 5-day of $9.5M for a running cume of $22.5M.

Outside the top 5 is Netflix’s Once Upon a Time in Hollywood sequel, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth at 251 locations with $1M today, $3M 3-day and 5-day of $5M.

WEDNESDAY AM: As we told you last night, Lionsgate scored its best Tuesday ever at the domestic box office with The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping and the best ever for the Suzanne Collins franchise as the day raked in $15.98M.

2013’s The Hunger Games: Catching Fire owned the previous best Tuesday for the franchise with $15.96M. Running cume for the fifth Francis Lawrence movie is $148.1M and by Thanksgiving, Sunrise on the Reaping will surpass its previous 2023 chapter’s lifetime domestic B.O. of $166.3M.

As we told you, Sunrise on the Reaping will dominate the Thanksgiving Wednesday through Sunday stretch as the franchise has historically done before since 2013, this time with a floor of $70M+. Previously, the pics over Thanksgiving cooked up the following: Catching Fire ($109.9M), Mockingjay Part 1 ($82.6M), Mockingjay Part 2 ($75.9M) with Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes way behind ($42.2M).

Lionsgate has had back-to-back global hit openings this year with Sunrise on the Reaping last weekend at $226.1M WW and Michael with $218.8M. This has all happened under the administration and watch of Motion Picture Chairman, Adam Fogelson. Still no word out there whether or not Lionsgate rejected or accepted Legendary’s bid.

Walt Disney Animation’s Hexed came in with $2.1M, $100K above the $2M we were spotting yesterday. That preview figure is higher than Encanto ($1.5M previews, 5-day of $40.5M) and Wish ($1.8M previews including $500K Saturday sneaks; 5-day of $31.6M). No RT audience score yet, but critics were meh at 67% fresh. There is a chance that the traditional Disney pic comes in lower than its 5-day forecast of $40M-$45M as original animated titles are very challenging to launch in the streaming era, but you have to start some place.

Universal and Paramount’s Focker-in-Law, which didn’t have previews last night, rather began showtimes at midnight, has more potential to hit its 5-day forecast of $45M given the Ariana Grande factor and the fact that audiences love the franchise. The first Meet the Parents 26 years ago was the fave among critics at 85% fresh on Rotten Tomatoes. Focker-in-Law isn’t the worst at 26%, as that belongs to 2010’s Little Fockers. 2004’s Meet the Fockers stands at 39%.

Hexed, Focker-in-Law and Sunrise on the Reaping are sharing PLFs with the latter having Imax screens (except for AMC where they are being designated to Netflix’s The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth).

Thank God for Lionsgate Hunger Games at the box office: Sans a Wicked, Moana and a Zootopia, the Thanksgiving box office will certainly be down from last year’s five day robust $290.3M, which was the fifth best ever. Moana 2 and Wicked led the holiday to its best haul ever with $424.9M.
