TikTok is currently down for some users

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

TikTok loading slowly or not at all? The service is apparently experiencing an outage for some users.

Downdetector confirms that TikTok is down. This comes after more than 20,000 reports of the video-sharing service experiencing an outage.

TikTok’s downtime reportedly started over an hour ago, according to social media posts online. TikTok users are seeing “something went wrong” errors when loading videos.

The outage comes after a number of service issues have affected other networks including Apple, Microsoft Outlook, and more.
