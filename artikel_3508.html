Alanis Morissette Comes Out as Pansexual in Podcast Interview

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Alanis Morissette has come out as pansexual in a surprising moment during a candid podcast interview ... leaving the co-hosts shocked.

In a Nov. 3 episode of the "Handsome" podcast ... Alanis was asked if being hurt in the past by men made her want to become a lesbian, the singer replied ... "I'm pansexual, so, yeah."

Co-host Tig Notaro then jumped in and asked ... "Wait, you're not really pansexual, are you?"

If you don't already know ... being pansexual means you're attracted to folks of all sexes, gender identities, and gender expressions.

This isn't the only candid admission the singer has made in recent weeks.

Back in September ... Alanis appeared on the "Call Her Daddy" podcast about her struggles with postpartum depression.

She told host Alex Cooper that her symptoms got worse after each pregnancy -- she has 3 kids -- and that she suffered "harrowing" invasive thoughts.
