Netflix’s ‘Heated Rivalry’ Replacement Is Breaking the Hockey Romance Formula

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Hockey romance shows are rapidly becoming their own niche subgenre. First, Heated Rivalry took off, delivering a steamy look at the relationship between professional rivals. Then Off Campus took it to the collegiate level, exploring the unlikely dynamic between a hockey captain and a music student. These massively popular shows each highlight the sport in the midst of a love story, and the trend isn't stopping there. Icebreaker is poised to add to this list. Based on Hannah Grace's 2022 novel, the series is still a ways off, but it will offer a new twist on the concept while continuing the legacy of these popular series.

Netflix is bringing Grace's story to life, and the adaptation is already in production. Like Off Campus, Icebreaker will feature a college romance with hockey in the background, but one major element will set the story apart. In this case, the romance is between a hockey player and a competitive ice skater, giving the series even more reason to put them on the ice.

There is a lot to look forward to as Icebreaker joins the ranks of this genre. The book follows Anastasia "Stassie" Allen, a dedicated figure skater, and Nate Hawkins, the hockey team's captain, as their paths collide. Each has their own lofty ambitions, as Stassie hopes to go to the Olympics and Nate chases a spot in the NHL, and both are willing to put in the work. However, both of their goals are interrupted when a facilities mishap forces them to share space. With a heavy focus on body image issues and found family, Icebreaker holds a lot of promise as this adaptation moves forward.

Four love stories, four completely different ideas of what it means to be somebody's person. This is the one that matches how you actually fall, and what it would ask of you.

You do not fall for strangers. You fall for the person you have already been talking to for years, which is the slowest and by some distance the most durable way anybody has found to do this.

You want a partner, not an audience. Somebody who wants their own life as badly as you want yours, and who would never ask you to be smaller to make the relationship easier.

The intensity was never the problem. The secrecy is. You are drawn to a love that lives entirely in private, and to the particular ache of wanting somebody you are not supposed to want.

You do not do this cautiously. When it happens for you it is immediate and total and it rearranges everything, and you would not accept a smaller version of it.

The series can expand the genre's focus by featuring two different ice sports. But for the characters, that isn't as compatible as it may seem. Though their mutual love for the ice seems like something they should bond over, they are positioned almost as rivals as they are forced to share the rink. However, that only increases the tension. In some ways, it's reminiscent of Heated Rivalry, which places the main characters on opposite teams. However, Icebreaker opens the doors to more variety as fans can also get insight into the world of competitive skating, which can only lead to more exciting scenes on the ice. Icebreaker has a lot of potential, and as the Netflix series nears, that will only become more obvious.

While there's plenty of anticipation around Icebreaker, the series is still in its early days. Production has started, and, even more notably, Netflix announced the cast. Daisy Jelley and Connor Simos will play the lead roles, playing Stassie and Nate, respectively. Joining them are Gabriel Tierney, Auliʻi Cravalho, Peter Berry, Chaneil Kular, Devin Craig, Aidan Shaw, Paul Wesley, and Sara Canning. Behind the scenes, Jade Halley Bartlett will serve as the series' creator, and Amanda Lasher is set to be showrunner. Additionally, book fans should be excited that author Hannah Grace is expected to be involved in the production. Though Icebreaker has no release date announced, Netflix is actively working on the series, which means fans can expect more information soon.

Ice skating romance is all the rage right now.

Undoubtedly, now is the perfect time to adapt this book, especially as both Heated Rivalry and Off Campus are in production on their second seasons. Other streaming shows have proven there is widespread interest in the concept, but including figure skating adds variety to the growing genre. Grace's book, which kicks off her Maple Hill series, has spent years accumulating fans and offers ways to expand the series beyond this season. Already, Icebreaker has its own audience after the book has sold over 5 million copies, and that is even before impatient fans of Heated Rivalry and Off Campus flock to Netflix. It may still be a while before Icebreaker comes to screens, but it is a production to look out for going into 2027.

Heated Rivalry is streaming on HBO Max and Off Campus is streaming on Prime Video in the U.S.

Heated Rivalry centers on two hockey stars whose fierce competition transforms into a complex relationship as they navigate conflicting feelings and the sport’s demands. As they attempt to balance personal emotions with professional ambitions, their unexpected bond challenges traditional expectations in the world of competitive hockey.

Off Campus follows the unexpected romance between a witty music major who dislikes hockey and the charismatic playboy center at Briar University.
