HBO Max-Paramount Plus Bundle Officially Confirmed for 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Two of streaming’s biggest players are about to join forces

HBO Max and Paramount Plus are officially joining forces, with a new streaming bundle set to launch in early 2027. Skydance Corporation, which completed its acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery in October, confirmed the plans in its third-quarter shareholder letter on Monday.

The company pointed to several recent successes on both platforms in the letter.

On Paramount Plus, Lioness saw its viewership increase by 62% during its third season, while MobLand became the platform's biggest original series ever in the U.K. and Ireland with its second installment. HBO Max, meanwhile, has found success with its DC and fantasy programming. Skydance highlighted Lanterns and House of the Dragon season 3 as two of the platform's strongest launches and returning seasons ever, averaging 22 million and 42 million global viewers, respectively.

The company is also working toward integrating the technology behind its streaming services. Skydance said it expects to align the backend systems for Paramount Plus and HBO Max within a year of closing the Warner Bros. Discovery acquisition, potentially paving the way for a more unified streaming experience.
