Antoni Porowski Talks Holiday Plans, Partner’s Unwavering Support and ‘RHONY’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Fresh off the finish line of the 2026 NYC Marathon, Antoni Porowski has a major milestone to celebrate this holiday season — but he admits he couldn’t have done it without the support of a special someone cheering him on every step of the way.

The Queer Eye star, 42, exclusively opened up to Us about how his partner, Zacharias Niedzwiecki, a personal trainer and Peloton yoga and strength instructor, helped him prepare for the grueling 26.2-mile race.

“I have to give him credit where credit’s due,” Porowski told Us. “The only reason I was able to get through that is he understands my body. He made sure that I was doing plenty of yoga and stretches and preparing, because I don’t know how any of this stuff works, and he literally does that for a living, so I was super lucky to have someone literally by my side the entire time.”

After spending just three months preparing for the urban trek across New York City’s five boroughs, Porowski described the experience as one of the hardest of his life.

“It was the best of times and it was the worst of times when you’re running through the streets and you have complete strangers cheering because, like, your name is on your banner,” he recalled. “There’s something really beautiful and sort of like very community-driven about the whole city, New York.”

One of the most difficult moments, he says, came during the notorious silence that sweeps the Queensboro Bridge, where the cheers of spectators faded, leaving only the sound of runners’ sneakers hitting the pavement and the drive to push through. But by the final eight miles, he says it didn’t take long for him to understand why so many runners return for another marathon.

“That’s how I felt the first five minutes when my partner asked me how I felt,” he joked when asked whether he ever wanted to run again. “But later that night, I was on social media and I was scrolling and I was listening to the cowbells and there was something very soothing in that.”

With 2027 just around the corner, Porowski shared he is already entertaining the idea of taking his next marathon overseas, potentially combining his love for running with his passion for travel. “Maybe it’s Paris or Berlin or London or some kind of a marathon next year,” he told Us. “I’m not ready to think about it yet, but I’m also excited, and I’m thinking like, what else can I do?”

But before committing to another finish line, Porowski is shifting his focus to the holidays — partnering with Marshalls for its Unwrap the Internet campaign, which launched November 10 alongside Lindsay Lohan, Danny Pellegrino and Robin Arzón.

As part of the initiative, the food and travel enthusiast curated the “Frequent Flyers” holiday gift guide, featuring everything from silk sleep masks and toiletry kits to travel-sized skin essentials. The partnership also brings back fond memories for Porowski, who grew up visiting Marshalls with his family and remembers the excitement he felt exploring everything the retailer had to offer.

“Well, to quote my therapist, if it’s hysterical, it’s historical, and one of my favorite experiences shopping with my family was always going to Marshalls,” Porowski told Us. “I know that I might be the travel expert, but going through the entire food section, like all of the kitchen appliances and then clothing, it truly always felt like the one place that we could go to where everyone was happy because there truly is something for everybody.”

When it comes to holiday gifting, Porowski said he refuses to “gatekeep secrets”, though he admits Niedzwiecki, 33, has reminded him that the perfect present isn’t always something he’d pick out for himself.

“If I experience something amazing, I need you to try it because I want you to experience the same joy of either the novelty popcorn or the dopp kit or whatever it is,” he told Us. “But sometimes I get into trouble because my partner also reminds me to try to put myself in the other person’s shoes, and try to imagine what they actually need.”

Porowski says that when it comes to gift-giving, he has also learned to weigh how practical a present is for the recipient’s lifestyle, joking that candles might not be the best choice for households with curious cats—something he knows firsthand now that he is a father to two felines at home.

With NYC Marathon training behind him and the holiday shopping sprint underway, Porowski already has his sights set on another kind of marathon—binge-watching The Real Housewives of New York City.

Although he admits he hasn’t tuned in to season 16 yet, the longtime Bravo fan says he is especially eager to watch his friend Daisy Toye stir things up on the show.

“I actually know Daisy really well from our lives outside of TV, and I heard that she is really bringing in a lot of entertainment this season,” he told Us. “I can’t wait because I know how her brain operates, and she says exactly what she thinks.”

Carole Radziwill also makes the list of Housewives he can’t wait to watch, making no secret of his excitement surrounding her return to the franchise. “The voice of reason, who is all of us, Carole Radziwill is back, which I think is so beautiful, and I loved her book. I love everything about her. I just think she’s an absolute icon, and so I’m excited to revisit.”

But before he can settle in for a much-anticipated RHONY binge, he has one more challenge ahead of him — getting Niedzwiecki to join in on the Bravo drama.

“I just have to convince my partner because he’s typically into, like, David Attenborough natural history docs, and this is a different type of social study, Housewives,” Porowski told Us. “So as long as I convince him to watch it with me.”
