Taylor Sheridan’s Original Western Masterpiece Officially Named Paramount+'s Biggest 2026 Hit

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

2026 has been another busy year for Taylor Sheridan, but that isn’t that surprising to those who have been following his work for the last 10 years. As much impact as Sheridan has in the movie and TV landscape, you’d think he’d been around much longer than 10 years, but he made his feature writing debut only 11 years ago on one of the best neo-Western crime thrillers ever made, Sicario. Sheridan also penned the script for the Sicario sequel that was released in 2018, but he isn’t returning for the third Sicario movie that was just announced a few weeks ago. Steven Knight, the Peaky Blinders scribe who is also writing the next James Bond movie, is penning the script for Sicario 3, so even with Sheridan’s departure, it’s safe to say the movie is in good hands.

Fans could argue that movies like Sicario or even Wind River are what Sheridan is best known for, but the TV show that put him on the map was Yellowstone. Starring Kevin Costner, Sheridan released five seasons of Yellowstone between its premiere in 2018 and its conclusion in 2024, but the show lives on as one of the most-watched projects in the world on streaming. In America, Yellowstone is Sheridan’s only show that streams on Peacock, but it’s still one of the most-watched titles in the world on Paramount Plus globally, where it’s been in the top 10 for over 300 days this year. This is an elite streaming milestone known only to a select few movies and shows, but it’s not surprising that Sheridan’s most popular show ever is still outlasting newer competitors on watchlists.

The original Yellowstone show may be over, but the story of the Duttons is far from over. Earlier this year, Paramount released two new Yellowstone spin-offs, Marshals and Dutton Ranch. The former follows Luke Grimes’ Kayce Dutton now that he’s left ranch life behind for a life in law enforcement, and the latter centers around Cole Hauser’s Rip Wheeler and Kelly Reilly’s Beth Dutton. Marshals is already back for Season 2, with new episodes releasing weekly on CBS, and Dutton Ranch is expected to return for Season 2 next year.
