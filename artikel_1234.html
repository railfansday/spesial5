He was Team-Mates With Virgil van Dijk - Police Found him with £3.1k-worth of Cocaine

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The climb to the very top of the Premier League is full of landmarks and sightseeing opportunities. Once that mountain peak arrives, however, the only way is down and the sightseeing opportunities quickly turn into dangerous cliff edges. The glory is so often torn down quicker than it had originally been built up.

The likes of Raheem Sterling and Dele Alli have fallen by the wayside in recent years. Sterling went from glory at Manchester City to struggles at Arsenal and Chelsea, before pleading guilty to dangerous driving and nitrous oxide offences in 2026 and receiving a 12-month suspended sentence.

Alli was tipped to be the next best thing by many. In 2026, he is a free agent and has turned to Twitch streaming.

Both are devastating cases with their own story, which have followed a similar path to Anthony Stokes' dramatic fall from grace.

Stokes had the world at his feet at just 14 years old when he completed a move to Arsenal from Shelbourne in 2003. He very much looked destined to become the next big Irish talent, especially after a loan spell with Scottish side Falkirk ended with 14 goals in 16 appearances three years later.

What followed should have been his rise to the top. Instead, the striker's career began to suffer a dramatic fall off - starting with a difficult move to Sunderland.

After arriving for £2m, Stokes failed to live up to expectations and scored just three goals in 36 appearances. Loan moves to Crystal Palace and Sheffield United then failed to revive his career in English football and a return to Scotland followed.

It was there that Stokes found his feet again, scoring 22 goals in 44 games for Hibernian and then racking up 135 appearances for Celtic and scoring a further 58 goals. Alas, that would be as good as it would get for the forward, who went onto score a total of just 25 goals for his next nine clubs.

Stokes' failed potential will hurt him more than anyone else, especially after Celtic where he was teammates with Virgil van Dijk and finally at his best.

What followed a disappointing career was even more devastating than the career itself. In 2024, Stokes pleaded guilty to unlawful possession of cocaine at the value of £3.1k, which had been hidden in a sock, and having the drug for sale or supply.

The former Arsenal prospect also admitted driving while over the alcohol limit and four counts of dangerous driving, as well as not producing a driving licence and not having motor insurance.

Stokes originally received a 15-month sentence with a five-year road ban, before an appeal spared him prison time altogether. Judge Jonathan Dunphy instead ordered a suspended sentence and 240 hours of community service, claiming that the former Premier League forward had "a lot to offer once he sorts out his difficulties.”

The court heard that Stokes' drinking problem started during his playing days after "too much too soon" as a young prospect, who uprooted his life to join Arsenal at just 14.

After the incident, Stokes signed for a pub team for Conor McGregor’s Black Forge Inn boozer, returning to football outside a professional level for the first time since 2020.
