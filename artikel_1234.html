Gasperini: ‘Roma have our characteristics and won’t change for PSG’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Gian Piero Gasperini insists Roma ‘stick to our characteristics’ against Paris Saint-Germain, but benching Donyell Malen has raised a lot of eyebrows.

It kicks off at the Parc des Princes at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game and Inter vs. Stuttgart on the Champions League liveblog.

The Giallorossi were absent from the Champions League for seven years, and that lack of experience was all too obvious when throwing away two-goal leads for defeats to both Real Madrid and Manchester United.

They are now up against the side that has dominated the tournament, winning the last two editions with coach Luis Enrique, whose first senior management job was at Roma in 2011/12.

“We must be courageous and intelligent tonight, because this really is the strongest team in Europe we are up against,” Gasperini told Amazon Prime Video Italia.

There are some big surprises in the starting XI, notably the absence of Donyell Malen, Paulo Dybala and Bryan Cristante, so is this an attempt to have something to come off the bench when they traditionally start to falter past the hour mark?

Niccolò Pisilli is starting to put pressure on Vitinha, who starts those Paris Saint-Germain moves from midfield.

“We have our characteristics, this is how we got to play these games, and we are going to stick to them, it would seem absurd for me to change them now.

“Usually, a coach changes when things are going badly. I’ve been playing man for man for over 20 years now…”

The injury crisis is beginning to calm down, as only Devyne Rensch and Lorenzo Pellegrini are on the treatment table.

Sky Sport Italia assure these are all tactical decisions, so Malen is not benched over his fitness, but rather as a choice to hit PSG later in the game.
