Cardinals – Chiefs Week 11 injury reports

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Arizona Cardinals continued their week of preparation ahead of their Week 11 game against the Kansas City Chiefs, one of the best teams in the AFC. The Cardinals have a relatively long list of players not practicing. Given the horrible game the week before, Arizona will need all the healthy bodies it can muster.

Some injury designations are precautionary, but it will be something to watch when the team puts out game statuses on Friday. But the Cardinals are getting back almost every player this weekend.
