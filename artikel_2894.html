Aaron Pico isn't done with Lerone Murphy: 'I know I can beat him'

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

AaronPico is confident he can defeat LeroneMurphy in a rematch.Pico made his Ultimate Fighting Championship debut against Murphy at UFC 319 last year. Murphy knocked Pico out with a spinning backelbow in the opening round and the latter now wants to run itback.

Pico believes he was beating Murphy before he got caught with thespinning elbow. The former Bellatorfighter is certain he beats Murphy in a rematch and believes a winover the Englishman brings him closer to a title shot."I want that fight," Pico told MMA Junkie. "And people are gonnaask me, 'Why do you want that fight?' I'm a competitor; I want tofight and get that one back. I mean, obviously, you watch thefight, I was beating him up. I would have just been a little bitsmarter and would have gotten that win. But there's a lot oflessons that came because of the loss. And that's the reason that Iwould love to run it back. And why wouldn't I? He's Top 5 in theworld, so it just puts me closer to fighting for a title and I knowI can beat him."Pico (15-5) has since rebounded with wins over PatricioFreire and LoseneKeita. Meanwhile, after beating Pico, Murphy (17-1-1) sufferedhis first professional loss in a featherweight title eliminatoragainst MovsarEvloev.
