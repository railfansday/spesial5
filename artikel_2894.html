Fantasy Football Rankings: Hayden Winks' QB/TE/DEF/K Blueprint for Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Blueprint is my weekly column and basically everything that I use to build out the most accurate rankings I can. There will be an RB column, a WR column and this combination column on the onesie positions. You will find film notes and endless charts. Maybe enough charts to stress test the Yahoo website. I kid.

It's impossible to get every ranking thoroughly early in the week, so be sure to keep checking in on these columns through Sunday morning. As always, my full rankings at every position are available.

These aren't my rankings. These are market-based projections for reference. Not all players are listed.

1. Joe Burrow (at ATL) - 21.4 proj. points

Burrow has been dealing as the season-long QB2 (26.2 PPG), generating +0.23 EPA per dropback (QB5). He is lighting up box scores through the air, leading the league in passing yards per game (302) and passing touchdowns per game (2.9). Cincinnati is leaning all the way into his arm, operating with a league-high 66% neutral pass rate (first).

That aggressive approach meets an Atlanta defense allowing 16.3 half PPR points per game to quarterbacks (23rd), where recent signal-callers like Tyler Huntley (24.0 points) and Caleb Williams (16.5 points) have put up functional days. The Bengals head in as 3-point road favorites with an implied team total of 27.25 points (sixth).

2. Brock Purdy (vs. LV) - 21.9 proj. points

Update: Mike Evans (achilles) and Deebo Samuel Jr. (hamstring) are out, but they at least have Demarcus Robinson back in the lineup for what it's worth. He's an upgrade versus what he had in the game before the bye. It's still a really rough WR room right now, but CMC and George Kittle are a sweet backstop.

Purdy has been surgical as fantasy's QB4 (21.7 PPG), leading all qualified quarterbacks with +0.31 EPA per dropback (QB1). Over his last four starts against quality opponents, his efficiency has cooled to +0.03 EPA per dropback (16th) while averaging 17.3 fantasy points per game. His Week 7 outing without his starting receivers was his worst game of the year (16.0).

He draws a dream matchup against a Raiders defense that's allowing the fourth-most half PPR points per game to QBs (21.8). Las Vegas sits at the top right of the intersection between success rate and explosive rate allowed on dropbacks alongside the worst defenses in the NFL. It's allowed four straight QBs to finish as top-12 QBs. The 49ers enter as 9.5-point home favorites with a slate-leading implied team total of 30.5 points (first).

3. Josh Allen (at MIN) - 20.6 proj. points

Allen's 28.2 fantasy points per game is currently ahead of the all-time fantasy record at QB (Lamar Jackson, 2024). He's done so without DJ Moore for half of the season. Moore was used properly last week with unlimited run-away routes, and then Dalton Kincaid found space downfield in multi-TE sets. It's a great formula.

The Vikings have been by far the best fantasy QB defense, however, allowing just 10.4 fantasy points per game. That's insane. Truly. They haven't faced an elite dual-threat QB this season, though.

Allen will be blitzed at a career-high rate, most likely. He's the QB5 in EPA per dropback against the blitz (+0.07), but that is far lower than on non-blitzes (+0.21, QB1). When blitzed, Allen has a 3.4% scramble rate compared to a 9% scramble rate when not blitzed. It's possible the Bills run a bit more often because of the pressure, as opposing offenses to Minnesota only pass 45% of the time in neutral situations (31st).

Buffalo enters as 3.5-point road favorites carrying an implied team total of 25.0 points (11th). It would make sense for Allen's fantasy output to take a hit here.

4. Patrick Mahomes (vs. NYJ) - 19.9 proj. points

Mahomes was quite good last week considering the circumstances, finishing as the QB5 against a healthy Broncos defense. Across the year, Mahomes is the QB14 on QB12 EPA per dropback. He was slowed down by the Seahawks without Rashee Rice in Week 7, but the Chiefs have been one of the best offenses in the NFL when not facing a top-three defense this year. Their weaknesses at LT and WR are hidden best against mediocre opponents.

Insert the Jets, who may not have their top-two defensive linemen here. The Jets just allowed top-five fantasy outings from Kirk Cousins, Malik Willis and Drake Maye in their last three outings. It's obviously a great spot here. Kansas City enters as 9.5-point home favorites with a 28.75-point implied team total (third).

5. Caleb Williams (vs. TB) - 20.6 proj. points

Williams is simply way more accurate this year. He's the QB2 in completion percentage over expected when adjusting for his throw depth. He's middle of the pack in sack rate (7.6%). And he's the QB4 in interception rate. Williams is seeing it extremely well, despite already facing the Vikings, Seahawks, Patriots and the feisty Falcons defenses. He's the QB3 in EPA per dropback, and he'll start spiking fantasy outings once he's pushed by the opponent.

The Bucs defense will be another tough defense to beat. They're fifth-best against fantasy QBs, and from Week 3 on, they've been the best defense in EPA per play, allowing just 15.8 points per game. They slowed down Dak Prescott in Week 5, then completely shut down Bryce Young and Michael Penix Jr. in the past two weeks. They have better pass-rushing depth and have enjoyed full breakouts from CBs Zyon McCollum and especially Jacob Parrish. Tampa Bay also wants to grind out the clock with a run game that is breaking my brain. Chicago returns home as 5.5-point home favorites sporting a 26.5-point implied team total (seventh).

6. Drake Maye (vs. GB) - 19.6 proj. points

Update: Hunter Henry (foot) was limited on Friday and they didn't activate another TE, so he's trending towards playing. They're also getting Mack Hollins back. A.J. Brown and TreVeyon Henderson are out.

After a very slow start, Maye is the QB2 from Week 4 on, sandwiched between Joe Burrow and Josh Allen. This past month, his +0.39 EPA per dropback leads the position. He's been the QB14 in EPA per dropback throughout the season, but timely scrambles and plenty of volume have made up for the efficiency decline. We'll see if A.J. Brown and Hunter Henry can suit up later, but Maye made it work with backups against the Dolphins last week (QB7).

He now faces a Packers defense that has kept QBs in check with a chaotic defensive style, surrendering just 14.9 half PPR points per game to the position (27th). Green Bay recently held Bryce Young to 12.4 points, Jared Goff to 18.0 and Dak Prescott to 15.0. The Patriots head into the matchup as 3-point home favorites with an implied team total of 25.25 points (10th).

7. Dak Prescott (at IND) - 20.1 proj. points

It hasn't been the best Prescott season, and the Cowboys' run game has been so bad that they've lost some inexcusable games along the way. The good news is Prescott has healthy WR and OL units right now, and his defense looks as pathetic as usual. He's been the QB13 in fantasy points per game this year on just QB8 EPA per dropback. It's been much higher than that for long stretches, and I can see that coming back after a recent stretch of games against BAL, HOU, TB and GB. Prescott was the QB6 and QB11 in the last two games against softer opponents.

The Colts defense is one of the worst in the NFL, possibly the worst without DT DeForest Buckner. They were smoked by the Jaguars last week after firing their GM in late October. Dallas heads on the road as 6-point road favorites with a healthy implied team total of 28.5 points (fourth). There are truly no excuses here.

8. Jared Goff (at MIA) - 20.0 proj. points

The Goff outlier dud happened last week, finishing as the QB21 against the Vikings' chaotic defense. Despite that, Goff is the QB4 in EPA per dropback and pacing for 4,770 yards with 36 passing touchdowns. He's the QB6 in fantasy despite not having anything to show with his legs.

The Week 9 environment is pristine against a Miami defense allowing 20.8 half PPR points per game to QBs (sixth). Opposing quarterbacks have moved the ball against the Dolphins regularly, including recent outings from Joe Burrow (24.6 points), Geno Smith (23.5 points) and Drake Maye (22.4 points). Miami is struggling on play action and throws over the middle. That's the entire Lions offense. Detroit is listed as 7-point road favorites with a 28.75-point implied team total (third).

9. Jayden Daniels (vs. LAR) - 17.8 proj. points

Jayden Daniels has been the QB15, QB3, QB7 and QB16 in his healthiest games. Since returning from an elbow dislocation, the Commanders have yet to call a designed run for him. He'll still scramble like a madman, but more of those are resulting in slides and running out of bounds early, too. This will hurt him on the margins, though he's still the QB1 in rushing yards per game (47.2).

The Rams present a fun matchup dynamic, ranking 21st in half PPR points allowed to opposing QBs (17.0 per game) but fourth in points allowed specifically to QB rushing. Opposing offenses facing Los Angeles also feature a 47% neutral pass rate, the third-lowest rate allowed in the NFL. Washington hosts this contest as 5.5-point home underdogs carrying a 21.0-point implied team total (23rd).

10. Trevor Lawrence (at BAL) - 18.8 proj. points

Lawrence is up to QB2 overall in EPA per dropback. He hasn't been pushed many times this year, so he's left fourth quarters without adding to his totals. He's only the QB16 in yards per game, leaving him with QB8 fantasy points per game. That's closer to his floor while everyone is healthy on offense. His ceiling is far higher when games are tighter.

That's the issue with the Thursday Night Game against third-string QB Joe Fagnano. The Ravens are 10-point dogs with a team total below 15 points. The Ravens defense is far more talented and far better coached than their current No. 15 ranking in fantasy points allowed to QBs, too. Nice field position and the occasional 1-on-1 loss downfield with Marlon Humphrey in coverage are enough to combat these minor risks for T-Law as a low-end QB1. The Jaguars are ninth in team total (24.75).

11. Tyler Shough (vs. CLE) - 16.9 proj. points

Shough is off the bye and it's possible WR Jordyn Tyson makes his debut and LT Kelvin Banks (ankle) returns after a prolonged absence. Those would be major upgrades. Shough's been the QB7 in fantasy points per game but was exposed versus the Vikings and Steelers recently while dealing with a broken non-throwing hand. They haven't been tested by above-average QBs this entire month, however, with Aaron Rodgers, Cam Ward, Tyler Huntley and Geno Smith in their recent history. The Browns play man coverage more than anyone else, but the Saints have the WRs to hang in there. New Orleans is listed as a 2.5-point home favorite with a 23-point team total (14th).

12. Jordan Love (at NE) - 17.5 proj. points

Love is playing the sport like nobody else. His absurd aggressiveness and arm talent are amazing to watch every week.

Love has turned into the QB9 on a per-game basis, with the second-most yards per game (281). His top three pass catchers are healthy, and they're starting to figure out the best starting OL and ways to hide them on the margins. He's scored multiple touchdowns in 7 of 8 starts. Even with Josh Jacobs in the fold, the Packers have maintained their pass-first identity. That's how it should be behind this OL.

His Week 9 draw is tougher on paper against a New England defense that ranks 22nd in half PPR points allowed to quarterbacks (16.6). The Packers head on the road as 3-point road underdogs with an implied team total of 22.25 points (17th). That's lower than normal.

13. Malik Willis (vs. DET) - 17.0 proj. points

The Dolphins are definitely better in November than in September. This is the youngest team in the NFL, and it's a new scheme with a new QB. The team is gelling, while also getting healthier at OL and WR. Willis has been fantasy's QB7 since Week 5 on QB14 EPA per dropback. Some of that is because of a Caleb Douglas sideline YAC TD, but him being in the lineup is helpful! Miami is still 30th in neutral pass rate (44%), but they'll be forced to pass here.

The Lions have allowed the most fantasy points to QBs, and opposing offenses have the third-highest neutral pass rate allowed. Their run defense is simply better than their secondary. Detroit will miss star LB Jack Campbell over the middle (and I really hope things progress for him, beyond football), but they did get star S Brian Branch back recently. The Lions have given up the seventh-most rushing QB points this year, partially because of garbage time scrambles. Those count for us! Willis will try to continue this top-12 QB streak, after Kyler Murray (QB10), Jordan Love (QB12), Jacoby Brissett (QB7) and Bryce Young (QB7) have done just that this month. Miami carries a 21.75-point implied team total (18th) as 7-point home underdogs.

14. Matthew Stafford (at WAS) - 18.1 proj. points

Stafford doesn't look fully healthy right now. Some of his sideline and downfield attempts lack his usual zip, and his throwing motion is feeling a little more Philip Rivers-like. I wasn't surprised to see Monday's report that his elbow is sore. That's what his throwing motion looks like right now, and the offense being extremely quick-hitting is further evidence of just that. Over the past month, Stafford's average depth of target is at just 5.4 yards downfield. The good news is the Rams have remained top-five in neutral pass rate in the two games without Davante Adams. Stafford is a lower-ceiling QB2 right now. He is fantasy's QB16 this year on QB16 EPA per dropback.

He now faces a Washington defense allowing the third-most half PPR points per game to quarterbacks (22.0). Opposing passers have found plenty of success against the Commanders, who rank sixth in QB passing points allowed per game. Jameis Winston tagged them for 326 yards and three passing scores in Week 5, while Brock Purdy posted 17.4 fantasy points the following week with backups at WR. The Rams enter as 5.5-point road favorites with a 26.5-point implied team total (eighth).
