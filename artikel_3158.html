Jon Abrahams to Direct Survival Drama ‘Merry Christmas, Jay’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

“Scary Movie” and “Meet the Parents” star Jon Abrahams is set to direct “Merry Christmas, Jay,” a survival drama inspired by real-life mechanic Jay Withey, who saved 24 people during a deadly Christmas blizzard in 2022.

Ryan March penned the film’s screenplay and will produce through Altruistic Films.

Per the film’s description, “Merry Christmas, Jay” follows Withey, “who was stranded in his Chevy Silverado pickup truck with two strangers on Christmas Eve when he broke a window at a school in Cheektowaga, New York, to get them out of the cold. After bringing the group inside, Withey returned to the storm to search for other stranded motorists. The film’s title comes from a note Withey left apologizing for breaking the windows, signed “Merry Christmas, Jay.”

“Having previously directed two feature films in Buffalo, what speaks to me about this story is the humanity of the city of Buffalo, as well as the amazing true story of Jay Withey,” Abrahams said. “It’s a 100 percent true take on a Christmas holiday miracle movie.”

“Jay could have stayed inside that school and waited out the storm. Instead, he went back out, again and again, for people he’d never met,” March said. “When Jay entrusted me with the rights to his story, I knew the film had to honor both the danger of that night and the humanity behind his choice. Jon understands what makes this story extraordinary, and I’m excited to bring it to the screen with him.”

Previously, March produced and edited “The Crawling,” Christian Everhard’s horror feature, and executive produced “In Flight.”

The film is in development through Altruistic Films and Merry Christmas Jay Productions, LLC. Sig De Miguel and Steven Vincent are attached as casting directors. Casting and production dates have not yet been announced yet.
