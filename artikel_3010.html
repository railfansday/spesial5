Brightstar: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Brightstar's direct role in New Jersey and Indiana lottery operations is a different kind of bet than hardware sales, and the balance between them matters for long-term holders.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 27, 2026 at 8:36AM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 10, 2026, Brightstar (BRSL +4.06%) announced that operational revenue growth accelerated to 4% in the quarter. The business is leaning into product sales and digital lottery expansion to drive revenue across international markets. Three key takeaways from management highlight how this strategic pivot shapes the company's long-term outlook.

Product hardware revenue more than doubled as terminal shipments picked up in Portugal and North Carolina. Non-wager-based software services expanded alongside contract wins, creating a predictable income stream that doesn't depend on player jackpot cycles.

Brightstar expands its operational influence through lottery management agreements, which are contracts where the company directly manages day-to-day lottery operations. Localized retail strategies in state markets such as New Jersey and Indiana helped instant ticket sales grow by 4% during the quarter.

Management prioritized shareholder payouts while actively managing its debt structure. The company returned over $200 million through early November using dividend payouts and share buybacks, drawing on $1.9 billion in total available liquidity to maintain financial flexibility.

Operational growth is accelerating across international markets as hardware deliveries scale. Multiyear contract portfolios and digital channels provide operational stability against volatile jackpot cycles. Management is watching pricing discipline and execution under its OPtiMa cost-efficiency program.

Expanding digital capabilities and international product sales support the long-term target of a 5% revenue compound annual growth rate.
