Why Navitas Semiconductor Stock Sank by 12% on Monday

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The latest scandal in the AI space was dinging advanced semi stocks.

Next-generation chipmaker Navitas Semiconductor's (NVTS -11.96%) stock looked more like yesterday's news on Monday. This wasn't because of anything the company reported of its own; rather, it was due to general sentiment about artificial intelligence (AI) stocks and looming developments involving companies associated with the technology. Navitas' stock closed the trading session down by almost 12%.

The rapid pace of the scandal enveloping Nvidia's (NVDA -4.12%) now-former CEO, Jensen Huang, continues to reverberate throughout the tech sector. Investors took this as a stark reminder that no corporate leader, regardless of how successful their company may be, is immune to scandal or apparent misconduct. This is not doing wonders for the industry generally, and for AI companies specifically (Nvidia is a leader in the AI chip segment). It also probably doesn't help that Navitas is a business partner of the beleaguered company.

Still, Nvidia is a powerful and successful enterprise, and this will surely be apparent when it reports its fiscal 2027 third-quarter results after market close tomorrow (Tuesday). Particularly with the slide in Nvidia stock, it's likely that many institutional and individual investors are selling out of other tech titles in order to load up for a potential Nvidia rally. Navitas would be a good candidate for such a shift in allocation.

Another target of reallocation is top AI developer Anthropic, whose stock is slated to begin trading on Thursday following its IPO. Some consider it to be of the better developers; at the very least, it's prominent enough to support initial demand for the stock.

So Navitas' double-digit decline on Monday feels like a combination of overreaction to Nvidia's news, and an opportunistic shifting of funds to more promising AI stock opportunities.

Neither the Nvidia crisis nor the investor swarm that's sure to envelop Anthropic on Thursday will keep other AI stocks down for long, I believe. Navitas is a company in the midst of the AI revolution and, although its performance hasn't been as impressive as some peers, I predict its stock will recover substantially on the still-insatiable demand for increased AI compute.
