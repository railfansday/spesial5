ZTO Express (ZTO) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Retail parcel volume surged 57% as the company prioritized quality over scale.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 18, 2026 at 5:14PM EST

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

ZTO Express (ZTO -0.39%) reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026. Management reported that the company focused on its "two-high, one-low" strategy, prioritizing high-quality market share and high-quality service while maintaining low end-to-end costs. The company reported parcel volume growth of 7% and an expansion of market share to 20.1% in a decelerating industry environment. Management stated that competition is transitioning to a quality-first development track supported by regulatory guidance. The company noted that its retail parcel volume grew 57%, contributing to an improved average selling price and earnings quality.

Nevertheless, thanks to the further implementation of digitization and smart tools, as well as improved lean process management, combined per-parcel transportation and sorting costs still declined by CNY 0.02 compared to last year. Specifically, transportation cost in the third quarter, the cost per parcel was CNY 0.32, down CNY 0.02 from last year. On the reduction side, first, our proprietary intelligent dispatch system adjusts vehicle schedules, route, and capacity matching in real time as parcel flows changes.

This helps us to improve load rates, reduce unused capacity, and avoid unnecessary mileages. Second, we have continued to strengthen fleet management. We have established our standard cost models for vehicle operations and maintenance, introduced tiered incentives based on load rates, and rewarded drivers for cost savings. These measures have helped us bringing in fleet operation and maintenance costs, bringing up the operational efficiency and maintain costs down further. On the fuel prices, diesel price have remained volatile since the second quarter amid international environmental development.

However, diesel price added approximately CNY 0.01 to transportation cost per parcel in the third quarter, and we expect a similar, or if not a little bit more, level of impact in the fourth quarter. We are taking two steps to manage this pressure. First, we continue to expand our fleet of natural gas trucks and have begun testing electrical vehicles on selected routes. Second, we will be utilizing our in-house fuel stations to secure better diesel supply. On sorting, in the third quarter, sorting cost per parcel was CNY 0.25, flat year-over-year. Higher depreciation and amortization expenses associated with newly commissioned facilities put pressure on unit costs.

However, these increases were offset by improvements in facility automation and labor productivity. Specifically, on the equipment front, we continue to invest in smart equipment at our sorting centers and upgrade and retrofit some of the aging equipment to improve efficiency. On the labor front, we also leverage digital tools to optimize shift scheduling, refine position-based performance assessment, and enhance our training and incentive program to keep improving labor productivity.
