US stock futures climbed on Wednesday as strong results from CoreWeave sparked a global tech recovery. Nasdaq-100 futures rose 1.9%, S&P 500 futures gained 0.9% and Dow futures added 76 points. CoreWeave jumped 13% before the open after beating third-quarter revenue forecasts and raising its 2026 revenue and operating income outlook. Nvidia rose 5%, bouncing back after losing nearly 10% earlier in the week over CEO Jensen Huang’s affair with an alleged Chinese spy. The rally spread worldwide. South Korea’s Kospi closed 2.6% higher, led by SK Hynix and Samsung, while Infineon gained 6.3% and ASML rose about 3% in Europe. Rate fears remain. Tuesday’s October CPI rose 0.4% month on month, above expectations, lifting odds of a December Fed rate hike to about 90%. US bond markets are closed for Veterans Day.
