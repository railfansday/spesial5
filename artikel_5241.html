Coinbase XRP looked cheap on Nov. 2 despite identical market value

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

At the Nov. 2 close, an independent comparison narrows XRP’s Coinbase discount from 0.168% to 0.006% after converting USDT into dollars.

XRP’s apparent Coinbase discount at the Nov. 2 close shrinks from about 0.168% to 0.006% when Binance’s Tether (USDT) quote is converted into dollars. The independent exchange-price comparison separates the quote-currency effect from the remaining difference in XRP prices.

The user Arab Chain on CryptoQuant reported an approximately -0.16% XRP Coinbase Premium Index reading on Nov. 2, describing it as an eight-month low and interpreting it as relatively weaker Coinbase demand.

The post also cautioned that the index alone was not definitive evidence of continued downside pressure.

The completed Nov. 2 candles put Coinbase XRP/USD at $1.2481, Binance XRP/USDT at 1.2502 USDT and Coinbase USDT/USD at $0.99838.

The adjustment multiplies Binance’s XRP price by USDT’s dollar price, then compares Coinbase against that converted figure. Expressed as a percentage, the formula is:

100 × [Coinbase XRP/USD ÷ (Binance XRP/USDT × USDT/USD) − 1].

This uses Coinbase’s USDT/USD market as the conversion reference. It is a venue price, rather than a universal redemption rate or evidence about why USDT traded below one dollar.

At the November 2 close, the gap shrinks after converting USDT into dollars. October 31 shows why the adjustment does not always leave near parity.

November 2, 2026 UTC close: raw -0.168%; dollar-adjusted -0.006%. The remaining dollar-price gap is close to zero.

October 31, 2026 UTC close: raw -0.200%; dollar-adjusted -0.078%. A residual discount remains after conversion.

Negative values mean Coinbase was cheaper, while positive values mean it was more expensive. Figures are rounded to three decimal places from the three exchange datasets.

Oct. 30 and 31 adjusted discounts remained about 0.066% and 0.078%, respectively, and the Nov. 2 finding supports checking the currency conversion.

A separate check used 60 matched one-minute buckets from Nov. 2, 12:00-12:59 UTC, combining Coinbase XRP/USD, Binance XRP/USDT and Coinbase USDT/USD. Its median raw gap was about -0.128%, and the median dollar-adjusted gap was approximately +0.006%.

XRP was near $1.28 on Nov. 3. Around 13:15 UTC, Coinbase’s ticker reported a $1.276 last trade, above its $1.2481 Nov. 2 close, while Binance’s rolling ticker showed 1.2780 USDT.

A relative-demand warning would be more persuasive if a discount persisted after currency conversion alongside comparatively heavier seller-initiated XRP trading over equivalent windows.

The measurement stops short of an executable arbitrage test. Coinbase defines candle closes as the last trade within each bucket, while Binance’s candles use matching time intervals. Rounding, bid-ask spreads, fees, and differences between venues are important when a residual gap is this small.

An August 2025 XRP liquidity study exposed a similar disconnect: XRP prices remained closely aligned across Binance and Coinbase even as trading activity was concentrated in different quote currencies.

Price consistency does not guarantee equally accessible liquidity, and traders may see nearly identical quotes but face substantially different execution costs and slippage, particularly during periods of market stress.

On Nov. 2’s closing observations, the unadjusted gap by itself substantially overstates the remaining dollar-price difference.
