Cloudflare: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Cloudflare's lightweight isolates let it run AI workloads at lower cost than legacy container clouds, an edge long-term holders should weigh.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 5, 2026, Cloudflare (NET -8.28%) announced its fastest quarterly revenue growth in nearly four years. Quarterly revenue rose 39% year over year to $782.5 million. Enterprise customer growth and expanding software usage are widening profit margins. Three things management said stand out.

Management replaced its sales chief to improve execution as corporate deals grow larger. The company wants sales teams to spend more face-to-face time with prospective buyers to shorten contract-signing times.

Because massive web traffic flows through its network, Cloudflare spots software bugs before researchers announce them publicly. This early warning system allows the company to protect clients pre-emptively, driving broader corporate adoption.

Traditional clouds rely on software containers that consume computing resources during idle periods. Cloudflare uses lightweight isolates (a technology that runs code instantly and costs customers less) to power artificial intelligence applications.

Customer demand is accelerating across enterprise cohorts, and management is openly leaning into its operational momentum. Expanding profit margins show that high-volume developer platforms scale efficiently alongside core security products. Management is strategically watching pricing discipline and contract renewals as clients consolidate vendors.

The company is accelerating its timeline for sustainable profitability while maintaining capital discipline across its global infrastructure.
