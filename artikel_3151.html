Grupo Aval Acciones y Valores S.A. (AVAL -6.18%) integrated the Itau retail banking business in Colombia during the third quarter, contributing to loan growth and market share gains while impacting cost of risk and efficiency metrics. Management reported a resolution of legal matters involving Corficolombiana, with a court order dismissing the case related to its deferred prosecution agreement with the U.S. Department of Justice. The company observed a divergence in margin performance as loan yields improved through repricing while investment margins were compressed by rising interest rates in the local market. Despite a slowdown in the Colombian economy and persistent inflationary pressures, the company maintained its market leadership in trust services and consolidated its asset management operations.
