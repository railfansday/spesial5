Salesforce is among 33 groups the US picked to export ‘Super Intelligence’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Salesforce’s package joins the first 33 in the American SI Exports Program, which puts US AI stacks on a menu for foreign buyers. Staff now face monthly token caps.

Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff presents the Dreamforce 2026 main keynote

Salesforce’s consortium is one of 33 that the US Commerce Department has picked to sell American “Super Intelligence” technology abroad. Commerce named the first group on Monday under its American SI Exports Program, and Salesforce confirmed its place on Thursday.

The programme carries out President Trump’s Executive Order 14320, on promoting the export of the American SI technology stack. Each consortium had to offer a package covering every layer: hardware, data pipelines and labelling, models, security, and applications.

US commercial diplomats will offer the packages to foreign governments and customers from a “menu”. Chosen packages get priority government-to-government engagement, faster export control reviews, and priority referrals for federal financing.

“The United States is the global leader in trusted SI research, development, and innovation,” Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick said in a statement.

A second, on-demand phase opens for applications on 30 November. It lets companies apply for single deals without offering the full stack. The scheme comes as Nvidia, OpenAI and Anthropic are reportedly set to be named AI national champions.

Salesforce’s package runs on Hyperforce, its cloud architecture on US infrastructure, with Informatica handling data governance. AI agents run on its own xGen models and third-party models, through the Agentforce platform.

Kendall Collins leads the bid. He runs Missionforce, Salesforce’s government cloud business. The company says its technology already supports government work in more than 30 countries and all 15 US Cabinet departments.

The designation caps a crowded few days for the company. On Monday, Salesforce told staff that every role now has a monthly AI token budget. Some employees had spent thousands of dollars a month on tokens, Business Insider reported from an internal memo.

“Starting this month, every role has a monthly token budget, so we can focus our AI spend on the work that matters most for our customers and business,” chief operating officer Miguel Milano wrote.

The caps run from $100 a month for sellers to $1,000 for technical roles in sales. A Salesforce spokesperson told Business Insider the company has no companywide individual budgets and leaves AI usage to team leaders. Apple cut its engineers’ AI budgets this month.

On Tuesday, Microsoft and Salesforce announced Copilotforce at Ignite, the same keynote where Microsoft said Copilot beats Claude on price. It brings Salesforce customer data and 38 prebuilt sales skills into Microsoft Copilot, in public preview.

Salesforce has also started cutting more jobs, Business Insider reported from internal messages. The scale of the cuts is unclear. Salesforce shares fell 9% on Tuesday, according to the same report.
