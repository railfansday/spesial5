Dillon Brooks gets ejected with 23 seconds left vs. Rockets after getting into it with Kevin Durant

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Phoenix Suns guard Dillon Brooks and Houston Rockets forward Kevin Durant exchanged words at the end of Houston's 116-104 win on Wednesday night.

Durant was chippy all night, making sure to deliver extra trash talk against his former team.

With less than 30 seconds left in the game, the Rockets led by 10. In a wild moment, Durant and Brooks weren't really playing; they were just talking to each other near halfcourt while the action continued.

Houston's Fred VanVleet hit a jumper with 23.8 seconds left to make it a 114-102 game in the Rockets' favor. Brooks and Durant were chirping at each other and got in each other's faces.

The referees assessed each player a technical foul. Because Brooks had received a technical foul earlier in the quarter with 4:32 left, he was ejected. VanVleet drained a three and motioned to Brooks; they chirped and were both assessed technicals.

Durant finished the game with 20 points and 10 rebounds. With 46.3 seconds left in the second quarter, the veteran forward drained a 3-pointer. That basket helped him become the fifth player in NBA history to score at least 33,000 points. He joins LeBron James (43,672 and counting), Kareem Abdul-Jabbar (38,387), Karl Malone (36,298) and Kobe Bryant (33,643).

The win moved Houston to 14-5. The Rockets return to action Saturday, hosting the Philadelphia 76ers.

The loss dropped Phoenix to 9-8. The Suns had won four straight before Wednesday's loss, and now turn their attention to taking on the Dallas Mavericks on Friday night.
