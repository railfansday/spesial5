Kirk Cousins called Fernando Mendoza to "debrief" after rookie's first start

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Quarterbacks Kirk Cousins and Fernando Mendoza are no longer teammates after the Raiders traded Cousins to the Vikings last week.

But the veteran is still serving as a mentor to the rookie.

Cousins told reporters this week that he called Mendoza after the No. 1 overall pick made his first start against the Seahawks last Sunday.

“Just wanted to debrief with him, get his thoughts,” Cousins said Thursday, via Kevin Seifert of ESPN. “I’m a big fan of his, and me and my boys will be following him for a long time. When I’m long done playing, we’ll be cheering for Fernando and for his team. So just wanted to get his thoughts.

“I remember my first career start, and that’s one that kind of always [will] stick with you, but I think he did some great things and he’s got a bright future ahead.”

In his own press conference on Wednesday, Mendoza noted that it was great to speak with Cousins again.

“He’s awesome,” Mendoza said, via transcript from the team. “He’s been a great mentor of mine, and [even though] he’s in a different building … I still lean on him, and still lean on all the great mentors that I have in this building with the coaching staff and other players and leaders on our team.”

The Raiders signed Cousins in April with the idea that he could help ease Mendoza into the league as a veteran who understands the nuances of the position. With the two men still in contact after Cousins’ departure, it appears the organization’s plan worked quite well.
