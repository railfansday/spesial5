Jaguars RBs Bhayshul Tuten, Chris Rodriguez were limited in practice Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Jaguars listed their top two running backs as limited participants in practice on Wednesday.

Bhayshul Tuten is dealing with ankle and foot injuries while Rodriguez has a hand injury. They've both been on the injury report for several weeks, but neither has missed a game yet this season.

Center Robert Hainsey (knee) was out of practice and head coach Liam Coen confirmed he will miss Sunday's game against the Giants at his press conference earlier in the day. Defensive tackle Arik Armstead (personal) was the only other player out of practice.

Jonah Monheim is expected to replace Hainsey in the lineup. He was limited with an ankle injury.

Running back LeQuint Allen (hamstring), defensive tackle Matt Dickerson (neck), safety Antonio Johnson (ankle), wide receiver Jakobi Meyers (ankle), linebacker Jalen McLeod (groin), defensive end Danny Striggow (concussion), and defensive end Travon Walker (rib) were also limited participants. Defensive end B.J. Green (forearm) was a full participant.
