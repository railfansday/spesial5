TSSAA football playoffs scores from 2026 Tennessee high school second round

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Welcome to the second round of the TSSAA football playoffs.

It's important to note two things about the outcomes across the state tonight.

First, All Division II winners advance to their classification's state semifinals. All DII teams have smaller brackets and will be off Friday, Nov. 27. All Division I winners reach the state quarterfinals. It's a win or go home for all teams. But DII teams inch a significant step closer to reaching a championship game with a win tonight.

Follow for our score updates. Final scores will be added as they are submitted to the TSSAA.

All eyes will likely be scoreboard watching the Oakland at Maryville game. This is typically a state semifinal game. But with Oakland third in Region 3-6A and a new TSSAA playoff format has led to this game being played two weeks earlier than normal.
