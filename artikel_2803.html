Detroit Lions place Cade Mays on IR, re-sign CB to active roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Detroit Lions have placed prized free-agent center Cade Mays on injured reserve with an ankle injury, his second stint on IR this season. In a corresponding move, the Lions re-signed corner Nick Whiteside to the active roster and have also elevated linebacker Owen Pappoe for their Week 11 game against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

Mays signed a three-year, $25 million contract with the Lions this offseason, but he's had a difficult, injury-plagued first season. After breaking his wrist in training camp, Mays spent the first six weeks on injured reserve before finally returning to action in Week 7. In Week 8, Mays injured his ankle in the first half of the Lions game with the Vikings and could not return.

While initially deemed a "low ankle sprain," Mays was only projected to miss 1-6 weeks. With that prognosis, it wasn't surprising that he was kept on the active roster and ruled OUT for Weeks 9 and 10, as a return seemed possible in the coming weeks. However, now that he has been placed on injured reserve, he must miss the next four games (Weeks 11-14) and is not eligible to return to the active roster until Week 15 (ironically against the Vikings) at the earliest. If his ankle injury is the "high" variety, his season could already be over, but we'll need to wait for an update from coach Dan Campbell before we know more.

With Mays now out for the foreseeable future, the Lions will continue to lean on Juice Scruggs, who has been terrific as an injury replacement. Scruggs is having a career year in Detroit and looks like a perfect fit for the system and culture. Behind Scruggs on the depth chart is Seth McLaughlin, who has only seen time this season in garbage-time/clock-killing situations and on special teams.

Whiteside was released from the active roster earlier this week when the Lions claimed EDGE Tyrus Wheat off waivers, then re-signed him to the practice squad on Wednesday. Now, three days later, he is back on the active roster, providing depth at a secondary that is getting healthier every day, as well as on special teams.

Pappoe has now been elevated for the second week in a row, after contributing on special teams in Week 10 against the Patriots. Expectations are that he will fill the same role against the Bucs.
