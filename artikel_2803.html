Steelers-Bengals on Sunday Night Football draws 21.519 million viewers

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The game was never competitive. That didn't stop a huge crowd from tuning in.

Via Nielsen, Sunday night's blowout win by the Bengals over the Steelers, 38-18, nevertheless averaged 21.519 million viewers.

The Bengals took charge from the get-go, and never relented. By halftime, the Bengals led 24-3, and they had outgained the Steelers, 337 yards to 40.

By the end of the third quarter, the Bengals had stretched the lead to 31-3.

This weekend, the 7-2 Vikings face the 8-1 49ers on Sunday night in Mexico City. The game has massive postseason implications for both teams, and ramifications up and down the NFC playoff tree.
