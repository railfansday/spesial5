Game Preview: Suns chasing their longest winning streak of the season

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Who: Phoenix Suns (7-7) @ Utah Jazz (3-13)

Where: Delta Center — Salt Lake City, Utah

Game three of this five-game road trip comes as the Suns head up to Utah. This is their first meeting with this squad this season. Unfortunately for the Jazz, injuries have derailed their season, and they're coming off a worse record than many anticipated. For the Suns, this is no sleepwalk game, but a chance to make a key statement. A win here would give the Suns their longest win streak of the season with three, and could really be the momentum this team needs to reach its true potential.

The Suns, coming off two different wins against the Denver Nuggets and Sacramento Kings, look to keep their road streak clean with zero losses. With a starting five still trying to find its footing, Jordan Ott seems to have solved the issues this lineup faced earlier in the season. The bench, though, continues to look outstanding and deserves a spot in the conversation among the best in the association. With a big game on the line, especially with it being an NBA Cup game as well, the Suns should aim to make this a statement win.

Mark Williams — OUT (Left Shoulder Surgery)

Harrison Ingram — OUT (G League – Two-Way)

Jaren Jackson, Jr. — OUT (Right Knee; MCL Tear)

Lauri Markkanen — OUT (Right Hand Fracture)

Jusuf Nurkic — OUT (R Foot; 2nd Plantar Plate Tear)

The Suns, in their last two games, have run the same starting lineup but have staggered certain players on the bench to make it easier. That has brought some success to this team and is something to watch for in this game. Dillon Brooks has taken advantage of this switch, as it allows him to be aggressive on offense as the second unit's lead scorer. Not only that, but it has helped on defense as well, with him more locked in. We saw this trend in the Kings game with Brooks, and with Devin Booker as well. He had a stint in the first quarter where he was left with just the bench, which let him hunt his shots and let the team take a lead to end the first.

Having key guys like Jordan Goodwin, Ryan Dunn, and Collin Gillespie, who will do anything to find success and win on the court, makes it easier for guys in Brooks and Booker to be the lead offensive guys. Jordan Ott can maintain success by mixing both units, which would help the Suns find another win.

Two things the Suns need to win against the Utah Jazz are the three-point and rebounding game. With no Jaren Jackson Jr., Jusuf Nurkic, or Lauri Markkanen, one of those should be easier than the other. The Suns cannot lose the rebounding game to Mo Bamba, who is trying to revitalize his career, or Jaxon Hayes, who has bounced from team to team. This is a weak frontcourt, and for the Suns, this is the true test for centers Oso Ighodaro and Khaman Malauch. If they are the real deal, they should excel in these matchups and prove they can handle some of the weaker frontcourts.

On the three-point end, they have Keyonte George, who shoots almost 35% from three, with rookie Darryn Peterson also shooting 36%. Mix that with rotation guys like Svi Mykhailiuk and Brice Sensabough, and this Jazz team can erupt at any time from behind the arc. Key shooters Gillespie and Kennard will have to be at their best to match some of the threes this smaller Jazz team will fire off. Brooks and Booker will have to be at their best as well, since they are the two main three-point shooters in the starting five.

The Suns are on the road, so handle this one like the previous one, too. Even if the Jazz court looks funky due to the NBA Cup, their team is going to look even funkier trying to score on a defense that will be bought in. Phoenix extends their longest win streak of the season while also getting their first win in Group A, taking down the Utah Jazz.
