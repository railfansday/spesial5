‘Old’ Galaxy phones are a better deal; here’s what you should buy

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Old is a label, and saying last year’s Galaxy flagships from Samsung are dated or worthless is disingenuous. You can get a killer deal on a phone a generation or two old.

Beyond that, it still has a flagship SoC in the Snapdragon 8 Elite – the same 3nm foundation as the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5. It also takes on much of the same design, so if you like the rounded corners of the Galaxy S26 Ultra, you’ll be missing out on almost nothing by choosing the Galaxy S25 Ultra for a better deal.

Right now, a 1TB model Galaxy S25 Ultra can be had for $780 on Amazon. It’s a “renewed” model, but that’s an excellent choice over a used device, since Samsung isn’t making them anymore. That’s down from the original $1,659 price tag when it launched. It’s also a good bit cheaper than the best deal you can get on an S26 Ultra – $1,049 for 256GB model from Samsung.

You can get a slightly better deal on something like the Galaxy S24 Ultra, though it might not have the same upsides as the S25 Ultra. There are a couple of reasons for that.

First, it’s already in its second year of a seven-year update policy. In effect, you’re only getting five years of updates. Second, it doesn’t have the newest design language, if that’s something important to you.

On the flip side, you might prefer the boxier look Samsung used to run by on its devices.

The Galaxy S24 Ultra deal on Amazon that sits it at $591 right now also applies to the 512GB model, unlike the deal for a 1TB variant above. Still, it’s a solid deal for a phone that really isn’t that old, and it’s close to what the mid-range Galaxy A57 sells for, new.

It’ll still handle everyday tasks with next to no issues, and that makes it a great gift – possibly overkill for a first phone, to be frank.

The Galaxy S25 Plus is closer to a “first phone” than an Ultra model would be. It has excellent specs, but it only misses out on some of the raw power Samsung’s flagships have and an S Pen.

During Black Friday deal week on Amazon, it looks like an unlocked Galaxy S25 Plus goes for $683. It’s higher than the previous year’s Ultra, but it isn’t as expensive as the renewed Galaxy S25 Ultra. It has the same design language as Samsung’s new phones, and it still has six of the seven years left in its update lifetime.

It certainly beats paying over $900 for the new one, even with a deal.

This is also a good opportunity to get a foldable, if you’ve been eyeing the Galaxy Z Fold lineup. The Galaxy Z Fold 8 series is fantastic, and it blows other devices out of the water. But it’s pricey.

Instead of paying $1,499 for a Galaxy Z Fold 8 right now, even though that’s a great deal, you can get a 512GB Galaxy Z Fold 7 for $867 on Amazon. It’ll be sold under Amazon’s “Renewed” program, so it’ll be covered under some type of warranty.

Doing that is also a great way to get set up for a trade-in deal when the next set of Galaxy Z Fold 9 devices roll out.

Fun fact: Samsung is selling a refurbished Galaxy Z Fold 7 for the exact same price as the Galaxy Z Fold 8 while the new one is on sale. Obviously, the Galaxy Z Fold 8 is the better choice on Samsung’s website.

If you’re absolutely set on getting a Galaxy phone that came out in the last year, Samsung’s deals are better than anywhere else. When Samsung runs deals like it has right now, that’s the best time to buy a device. In any other case, it can be a hard bargain.

The Galaxy S26 Ultra is always a good choice, but it’s going to be $999 without a trade-in. That said, trade-in values are boosted right now. You can get $600 for something like your Galaxy S24 Ultra, but you can’t stack discounts. Trading a Galaxy S24 Ultra in brings the Galaxy S26 Ultra down to $799. Still a good deal.

Samsung’s devices offer the latest Android updates and features, even if they’re a couple of years old. Some of the Galaxy phones we knew as the “best” last year are affordable options right now.
