Witch Hat Atelier Officially Announces Its Own Tabletop RPG

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

While a popular anime getting its own gaming adaptation isn't exactly much of a surprise at this point, with countless low-quality spinoffs or games that fail to take full advantage of their original premise, the latest announcement coming out of this year's TPRG Summit in Tokyo offers one upcoming release that is definitely worth looking forward to.

Aiming to adapt none other than the explosively popular Witch Hat Atelier, which saw its own anime series drop in 2026, the series will be getting its own fully playable tabletop game based on its deep fantasy setting and colorful cast of characters.

Although scarce few details are currently available about the project outside of its initial teaser, there is currently an official website for the game by Hobby Japan that lists nothing besides some key art shared by WitchHatUpdates on X and a reminder of its currently expected release sometime in 2027.

Having initially been teased with an illustration at the start of November, the key art itself shows off a colored version of Qifrey's house, which was also released alongside a poster featuring Coco and her Brushbuddy surrounded by portraits of her classmates and Qifrey, all in front of a map of its fantasy world.

Although there are painfully few details currently available about the project so far, compared to the typical mobile, gacha, or fighting games that most trending anime tend to get these days, a full-blown tabletop RPG feels like the perfect fit for a grounded fantasy franchise like Witch Hat Atelier.

Despite the excitement from fans, however, we'll likely have to wait quite some time before more details are revealed about the upcoming TTRPG, let alone find out if it will be available internationally or exclusively for Japanese fans closer to the actual release date of the anticipated gaming adaptation.
