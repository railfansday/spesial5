10 months on, Stranger Things fans are still holding out hope for an alternate finale

Update: Since writing, Netflix has announced a new Stranger Things prequel series titled 'Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure,' due to hit the streamer in 2028.

Stranger Things Day 2026 has awoken Conformity Gate as fans are convinced that something big is coming over 10 months after the sci-fi series came to an end.

Stranger Things Day is celebrated every year on November 6, marking the day that Will Byers went missing in Hawkins in the very first episode. However, with this being the first Stranger Things Day since the series ended for good, fans are convinced that the extra episode they hoped for back in January is finally on the way, and are spotting clues in the unlikeliest places.

One fan on Twitter found that unrelated outlets such as The Weather Channel and a US game show The Wheel of Fortune have been sharing the message "Stranger Things Have Happened." Now, this could be in celebration of Stranger Things Day, but being the sleuths they are, fans think these are clues. In addition, Netflix Latin America has also posted a picture of a billboard which reads, "2026: Final Chapters," with the caption, "Remember, the screenwriter gets creative in the final episodes."

The plot thickens, as co-creator Ross Duffer posted a picture on Instagram riffing off Nathan Fielder's new movie You Can See Everything, which reads, "I know what's coming on Stranger Things Day," but under a picture of Fielder, it says, "No, you don't."

This is all fueling the theory that first came about after the Stranger Things season 5 finale. Some viewers refused to accept the ending, believing that it was all a ruse set up by Vecna and that a secret ninth episode was on the way. But what is even, pardon the pun, stranger is that the franchise is still acting as a running series, with Sony announcing new Stranger Things-themed headphones, and Netflix releasing a Stranger Things-themed Tamagotchi that isn't due to release until late 2027.

There is a lot of evidence that suggests Netflix could be dropping something Stranger Things-related today. While we don't think an alternate finale is on the way, it wouldn't be too far-fetched to think that the streamer has bonus footage in the works. HBO very recently released an IT: Welcome to Derry special months after the horror show's first season ended. So far, Netflix has just posted a season 5 blooper reel, but that won't be enough to keep this fandom at bay.
