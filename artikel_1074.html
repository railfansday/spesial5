Emma Hayes should manage both US men’s and women’s teams, says former international

Former international Kyle Martino has outlandishly called for Emma Hayes to be the new head coach of the USA men’s team and manage both the USMNT and the women’s side.

USA men are searching for a new coach after it was confirmed on Friday that current boss Mauricio Pochettino will take the Manchester United job later this week after a little over two years in the job.

Ex-Manchester City manager Pep Guardiola is reportedly a “top target” for US Soccer, while the likes of former Germany boss Julian Nagelsmann, Nashville SC coach B.J. Callaghan and current US under-23s boss Steve Cherundolo are also towards the top of the betting.

But Martino – who won eight caps for the USMNT between 2002 and 2006 while playing his club football for the likes of Columbus Crew and LA Galaxy – has thrown an unlikely name into the mix in the form of Hayes, who is the popular manager of the US women’s team (USWNT), led them to Olympic gold just two months into the job back in 2024 and is now preparing for the 2027 World Cup, where the US will look to reclaim the title they lost to Spain in 2023.

Martino was speaking on TNT Sports before the USA’s 6-0 win over Haiti in the first leg of the Concacaf Nations League quarter-final over the weekend and his suggestion drew puzzled looks from fellow pundits Brad Guzan and DaMarcus Beasley.

“I’ve got a name for you guys – and this isn't clickbait,” said the 45-year-old, before making a proposal that many would call the definition of clickbait. “This is a conversation starter, although I’m sure the trolls are going to come out from underneath their bridges and get their thumbs busy.

“Emma Hayes. Hear me out here! Emma Hayes is one of the best coaches in the world. She’s got an incredible résumé. She’s done it abroad and done it here. She’s got a great relationship with US Soccer and she’s got a great relationship with the fans.

“We have equal pay for our [men’s and women’s] players. We were a country that led the charge there, with other nations, let’s lead the charge on equal opportunities. Very rarely does it overlap. The women’s World Cup overlaps with the [men’s] Gold Cup [next summer] but I’ve got an answer there. Let [Steve] Cherundolo [men’s under-23 coach] take the Olympic team to the Gold Cup.

“Emma Hayes. Right now. It’s a commercial business at the end of the day. Let Emma do it. You’ve got a national training centre – both teams there at the same time. Let Emma do both!

“Manage both teams. Manage the men and women at the same time. No one’s ever done it, let’s do it. Let's lead the world of sports."

Hayes made her name during a trophy-laden 12 years at Chelsea, winning seven Women’s Super League titles and five FA Cups, before taking over as USWNT boss in 2024 and immediately securing gold at the Paris Olympics.

She had previously worked in the US early in her coaching career at the Long Island Lady Riders, college side Iona Gaels and Chicago Red Stars before heading back to the UK to be an assistant coach at Arsenal and then taking the top job at London rivals Chelsea.

And Martino – who ran unsuccessfully for the US Soccer presidency in 2018 – was adamant that the 50-year-old has the personality to manage the US men’s side and the opportunity is there for the federation to be trend-setters.

“She's one of the best coaches in the world,” he added. “I mean equal pay, we did that for players, equal opportunity, let's do that for coaches. Also she's just not only an incredible manager. She is one of the most entertaining people that I've have ever seen as well.

“It's a commercial business. She wins. And listen, if it’s complicated, then I'll say she only coaches one team. But I think her name should be in there.

“Unfortunately she's on the committee [to search for new USMNT coach]. Take her off the committee and think about her for the US men. Pochettino and her have a great relationship. So it’ll be a nice little transition, they have similar principles.”
