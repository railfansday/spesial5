Multi-tools are versatile sidekicks for outdoor enthusiasts, professional tradesmen, and DIYers—and perfectly giftable. Ahead, we’ve shopped the major multi-tool manufacturers to assemble our favorites. The list features top Leatherman multi-tools (like the highly rated Leatherman ARC Talos) along with the Gerber Armbar and classic Victorinox Swiss Army knife. These multi-tools range from everyday carry models for the average homeowner to specialized multi-tools designed for outdoorsmen and even heavy-duty models for the professional tradesman and serious DIYer. There’s something for everyone!

The Leatherman ARC is the pinnacle of EDC multi-tools. The highlight of this 20 tool set is the MagnaCut Steel blade, which is renowned for its edge retention, durability and rust resistance. The Talos version of the ARC has a bronze Cerakote finish for added style. And all the tools can be extended within hand. It also comes with a bit driver with a complete set of interchangeable bits. It’s the EDC tool for the serious DIYer in the family.

A Gift of the Year winner, Leatherman’s Wave+ has all the essentials a DIYer or professional needs out of a multi-tool, including a pliers, knife, scissors and wood saw. With its stainless steel construction, it’s exceptionally well made. It’s an ideal everyday carry tool at a mid-tier price.

With its carabiner style clip, it hooks conveniently to the utility loops on a backpack for easy access.
