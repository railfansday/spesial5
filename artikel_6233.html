Light trails

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Star trails wheel around Polaris, the famed North Star that sits less than 1 degree from the north celestial pole. The imager captured this scene from Campo Cecina, near Carrara, Italy, where fireflies left yellow-green streaks across the hillside.
