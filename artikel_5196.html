Bybit Expands RWA Earn Into Japanese Equities With New JPE Product

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Home » Crypto Market News » Bybit Expands RWA Earn Into Japanese Equities With New JPE Product

Bybit’s first equity product in RWA Earn gives access to an SBI Asset Management strategy.

Bybit is expanding its RWA Earn platform into Japanese equities with the launch of JPE, its first equity-based investment product.

Announced on November 9, the product marks the platform’s first equity offering and gives eligible users indirect exposure to a Japanese high-dividend equity strategy managed by SBI Asset Management (SBI AM).

The launch brings a new asset class to Bybit RWA Earn, which previously focused on fixed-income and credit strategies.

According to an official announcement, the JPE investment product will give eligible Bybit users indirect exposure to a Japanese high-dividend equity strategy managed by SBI Asset Management (SBI AM).

JPE is backed by JX, a digital token issued by DigiFT, a platform specializing in tokenized traditional investments.

Plume, a blockchain-based financial infrastructure provider, structures the product for distribution through Bybit’s RWA Earn platform. The underlying Japanese equity strategy remains managed by SBI Asset Management.

Bybit said eligible users can invest in JPE through its RWA Earn platform using USDC, with a minimum subscription of 5,000 USDC.

The exchange will cover all blockchain transaction fees, while subscriptions and redemptions will be free of charge. Redemptions will follow a T+5 settlement cycle, excluding non-trading days in Japan, Singapore and the United States.

Emily Bao, Bybit’s Head of Spot & Assets, said the launch reflects the growing role of partnerships in bringing traditional financial assets onto blockchain networks. She added that the collaboration with Plume and DigiFT allows Bybit to offer users access to institutional-grade investment products backed by established asset managers.

Full details, restricted regions and eligibility criteria are available in Bybit’s official announcement.

The launch marks an expansion of Bybit’s RWA offering into traditional equity markets.

Since its launch in June 2026, Bybit RWA Earn has focused on tokenized fixed-income and credit products, giving eligible users access to investment strategies beyond crypto-native assets.

With the addition of JPE, the platform is moving into equities for the first time, offering indirect exposure to a Japanese high-dividend equity strategy managed by SBI Asset Management.

The global real-world asset (RWA) market reached $39.61 billion in distributed asset value as of November 8, according to RWA.xyz, down 0.94% over the previous 30 days. The value of represented assets stood at $391.90 billion, up 8.87%. The market had around 5.69 million asset holders, an increase of 7.13% over the same period.

U.S. Treasury debt remained the largest RWA category, with $14.11 billion in distributed value, followed by commodities at $5.33 billion and active strategies at $4.72 billion. Tokenized stocks ranked among the larger categories, reaching approximately $3.73 billion.

Tokenized equities are moving from fixed income into stock-based strategies on Bybit exchange. It also gives eligible investors access to a Japanese high-dividend equity strategy through a blockchain-based product.
