Bitcoin buyers face a trading issue as stablecoins just suffer their biggest supply drop since 2022

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Market makers can advance stablecoins for institutional Bitcoin purchases and settle later against fund holdings, even as marketwide stablecoin supply falls $14.2 billion.

An institutional investor can ask a market maker to buy Bitcoin before the investor has turned tokenized fund shares into cash.

Greg Guttas, head of over-the-counter trading at Flowdesk, told CryptoSlate that the desk could pay with its own stablecoins and settle with the client later against the fund holding.

Securitize and Ethena opened a new route for fund holders on Nov. 12 with a facility for BlackRock’s USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Fund (BUIDL) that exchanges fund shares for stablecoins outside banking hours. That gives eligible investors a way to raise trading cash themselves.

In Guttas’s example, a dealer would supply the cash for the Bitcoin purchase upfront.

The question of what dealers can spend is sharper after a drop in stablecoin supply. Glassnode reported that aggregate stablecoin supply fell $14.2 billion in the 30 days through Nov. 9, the fastest contraction since June 2022.

The marketwide total leaves open how much an individual desk has on hand and what it would commit to a client’s trade.

Guttas described how an over-the-counter desk could handle a purchase for a client holding tokenized money-market fund shares:

“The market maker in that scenario could use their own stablecoin balances to buy the BTC immediately and then redeem the tokenized MM funds after the fact.”

That sequence would give the client time to settle using the fund position, while the dealer would spend the stablecoins first and wait for settlement to recover them. Until then, those funds would be unavailable for another purchase.

The longer it takes to turn fund shares into settlement cash, the longer the desk has to carry the exposure. Guttas said larger conversions can hit size limits, and weekends can complicate access.

In January 2025, QCP disclosed that it facilitated a six-month Bitcoin basis trade for Securitize Credit using BUIDL as collateral.

A basis trade aims to capture the difference between spot and futures prices, but QCP’s account leaves the funding currency for the Bitcoin leg and the settlement timing undisclosed.

The Securitize and Ethena facility works in both directions for BUIDL. Eligible investors can exchange their fund shares for supported stablecoins, including USDC, or use those stablecoins to acquire BUIDL.

That route can give a fund holder cash to trade on a weekend.

The facility opened with $50 million of inventory, split between $25 million in USDC and $25 million in BUIDL. Securitize said up to another $50 million may become available within about an hour as liquidity is used, subject to terms and availability, bringing the maximum facility capacity to $100 million.

The relevant starting figure for a holder seeking stablecoins at launch was the $25 million in USDC, while the BUIDL side of the inventory serves investors converting stablecoins into fund shares.

Since February, prequalified BUIDL investors have been able to request quotes through Securitize’s UniswapX integration from approved participants, including Flowdesk, Tokka Labs and Wintermute.

The quoted size depends on the liquidity approved counterparties offer when an investor asks. Once a quoted swap executes, both sides settle together on-chain.

Circle’s USYC, a separate fund token restricted to eligible non-US investors, redeems into USDC in one block below its instant-redemption threshold, with per-block time varying by network.

Above that threshold, Circle lists same-day or next-day settlement, with separate arrangements available for additional immediate liquidity.

Katana CEO Matthew Fisher told CryptoSlate he looks at exchange balances, stablecoins posted as collateral in lending and perpetual futures markets, and how much traders borrow against them.

Those measures help show where stablecoins are being used for trading and leverage, but a balance on an exchange can sit there without becoming a Bitcoin order. Fisher advised watching the deployed number.

How large a trade an institution could finance depends on the dealer’s available inventory, the terms it offers, and how quickly the fund position can be converted into settlement cash. The arrangement would put existing stablecoins to work earlier in the trade.
