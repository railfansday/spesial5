Rasmus Hojlund marks goal against Manchester City with provocative celebration

Rasmus Hojlund celebrated his opening goal in Napoli’s 2-2 draw with Manchester City with a provocative gesture towards the home fans, telling them, “There’s only one club in Manchester”.

The Dane drilled a fine low finish past City keeper Gianluigi Donnarumma in the 55th minute of Tuesday night’s Champions League group stage encounter.

But City twice came from behind to rescue a point, with Ayyoub Bouaddi and Rayan Cherki scoring for the home side either side of Noa Lang’s crisp volley.

After breaking the deadlock Hojlund celebrated in front of the home fans, holding up one finger and saying, “There’s only one club in Manchester”, having spent two seasons at Manchester United before joining Napoli.

He explained the celebration in an interview on TNT Sports afterwards: “Some good banter, there’s the rivalry between Manchester City and Man United and I obviously have a past in Manchester United, so that’s my club - nothing against Man City, but United is my club.”

Asked whether it meant more to score at the Etihad, he said: “Yeah, good to score here, a good point, so happy for that.”

Hojlund swapped Atalanta for Old Trafford in a £72m move in 2023, but was loaned to Napoli after two seasons, having underwhelmed in a particularly difficult second year at the club in which he endured a 21-game goalless run. He scored 26 goals in 95 appearances.

The 23-year-old then completed a £38m move to the Serie A side this summer, with Napoli obliged to buy him permanently after qualifying for the Champions League.

Napoli appeared much the happier side after their draw with City despite twice surrendering the lead, with the hosts having won only once in their past seven games in all competitions.

Hojlund added: “I wouldn’t say it was a free game but it’s one of those were you go, OK, let’s see if we can get something. And actually I’m a bit gutted because we could probably have got all three points today.”
