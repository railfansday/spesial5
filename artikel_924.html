Sheehan starts at No 8 for Ireland vs Springboks as Prendergast plays

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ireland hooker and captain Dan Sheehan has been shifted out of position to start at No 8 for the visit of South Africa to Dublin in Saturday's Nations Championship (4.40pm) in unusual and risky Andy Farrell move; Fly-half Sam Prendergast starts after citing against him dismissed

Andy Farrell has named Dan Sheehan out of position at No 8 for Ireland's crunch Nations Championship Test against South Africa in Dublin on Saturday.

The unusual move, with Sheehan's ordinary position at hooker, comes with Ireland missing trio Caelan Doris, Jack Conan and Sean Jansen due to injury.

Sheehan has only played at No 8 once in his career to date, starting there for Leinster against Munster in last month's URC contest at the Aviva Stadium - a clash in which Leinster badly lost the breakdown battle before coming through to win.

Other changes see full-back Hugo Keenan, centre Robbie Henshaw and lock Joe McCarthy return to the squad to start.

Fly-half Sam Prendergast starts the contest after a citing against him for a hip-drop tackle was dismissed on Wednesday.

Wings Rob Baloucoune and Mack Hansen remain to form the back-three with Keenan, while Stuart McCloskey - who played most of last week's victory over Fiji off the bench - partners Henshaw in midfield.

Scrum-half Jamison Gibson-Park retains his starting place, with Craig Casey staying on the bench.

Into the forwards, Ronan Kelleher starts at hooker with Sheehan at No 8, while props Andrew Porter and Tadhg Furlong start either side of Kelleher in the front-row.

Tadhg Beirne starts again in the second row, alongside McCarthy, while Ryan Baird starts at blindside flanker and Josh van der Flier at openside ahead of Nick Timoney.

On the bench, replacement tighthead Sam Illo has been passed fit to be involved, while hooker Tom Stewart, loosehead Tom O'Toole, lock James Ryan, fly-half Ciaran Frawley and centre Bundee Aki complete the squad alongside Timoney and Casey.

Ireland: 15 Hugo Keenan, 14 Robert Baloucoune, 13 Robbie Henshaw, 12 Stuart McCloskey, 11 Mack Hansen, 10 Sam Prendergast, 9 Jamison Gibson-Park; 1 Andrew Porter, 2 Ronan Kelleher, 3 Tadhg Furlong, 4 Joe McCarthy, 5 Tadhg Beirne, 6 Ryan Baird, 7 Josh van der Flier, 8 Dan Sheehan (c).

Replacements: 16 Tom Stewart, 17 Tom O'Toole, 18 Sam Illo, 19 James Ryan, 20 Nick Timoney, 21 Craig Casey, 22 Ciaran Frawley, 23 Bundee Aki.
