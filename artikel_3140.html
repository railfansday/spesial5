Sequel hits $26M in funding as it adds AI agents to on-site webinars

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The startup says it has tripled revenue two years running and counts ElevenLabs and Datadog as customers.

Oana Manolache, co-founder and CEO of Sequel.

Sequel has raised $15M led by Sorenson Capital, taking its total to $26M. Its software runs webinars on a company’s own site and links what viewers do there, and it now sells AI agents that plan video programmes and turn talks into other content.

Sequel, a startup that runs webinars on a company’s own website rather than on a separate platform, has raised $15M, co-founder and CEO Oana Manolache wrote on Wednesday. Sorenson Capital led the round, with High Alpha, Album VC and Webflow Ventures joining. It takes Sequel’s total funding to $26M.

Manolache, who previously worked in marketing at HP, says standalone webinar tools send interested viewers away from a company’s site. Sequel keeps them there, and links what they watched and asked to what they did before and after.

It now sells AI agents built on that data. They can plan a video programme, turn one talk into other content and help turn viewers into sales leads. Customers can also plug in their own AI tools through an MCP server.

“Anyone can build an agent now. I can build one in five minutes in my spare time. But an agent without context is useless,” Manolache wrote.

Sequel says it has tripled revenue two years in a row and serves more than 400 companies, including ElevenLabs and Datadog. It did not share revenue figures or a valuation. It last raised an $8M Series A in 2022.

Its pitch leans on trust, as AI makes fakes easier to produce. Zoom has started letting users prove they are human on calls. Marketing tools are filling up with agents too: Adobe this month put its marketing agent inside ChatGPT and Claude, and Gradial raised $65M in June.
