Apple is finally adding video podcasts to Apple TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Apple is finally adding video podcasts to Apple TV in a move that trails after major rivals like YouTube and Spotify.

First reported by The New York Times on Monday, the company is testing video podcasts on its streaming platform. This means if your favourite show has a filmed element (which most major podcasts currently do), you'll be able to watch it in the same spot you mainline Apple Originals like Silo and Ted Lasso.

They're not quite available on Apple TV yet, as according to the news outlet, early access is only for testing with developers. However, the NYT reports that video podcasts will be available soon and free for all, even if you don't have an Apple TV subscription.

Despite leading the podcast charge in 2005 and dominating listener stats, Apple has left eyeballs on the table when it comes to video podcasts — and not just because they're celebrity magnets and dynamite for clipped viral social media moments. Notably, the company did add video to its Podcast app in February, with the NYT reporting a 20 percent audience growth for top shows.

With video podcasts, Apple is contending with the behemoth that is YouTube, which has more than 1 billion monthly active podcast viewers. In April 2026, YouTube reported that Premium users watched over 800 million hours of podcasts.

Elsewhere, as of 2026, over 500 million users have streamed a video podcast on Spotify, an increase of 50 percent year over year, according to the company. Like YouTube, Spotify is aware of the impact videos can have on growing podcast audiences. During its May 2026 Investor Day, Spotify also said, "These users engage more, use more devices, and spend more time across podcasts, music, and audiobooks."

Adding a podcast section to Apple TV will inevitably increase viewership for these shows, which is great news for creators as much as Apple. However, not every podcast has a luxurious budget, celebrity hosts, and a polished studio, both of which are becoming increasingly crucial in the incessant battle for views.
