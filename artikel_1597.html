Opinion: Liverpool will make surprise move for Premier League star

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Over the last couple of years, Liverpool have had a lot of work that they've needed to do on their squad.

Well over £600 million has been spent on new players by them so far and, right now, it's still unclear whether they're actually any better off than they were when Jurgen Klopp was in charge.

Obviously, some arrivals such as Ronald Araujo have been a major upgrade on Jeremie Frimpong but, for others such as Bradley Barcola, some questions still remain.

However, with a lot of Liverpool's focus during the last summer window going on their attack and defence, it feels like the club should now be spending most of their time trying to improve their midfield.

Ryan Gravenberch and Dominik Szoboszlai may have been handed new long-term deals last season but, thanks to the way Andoni Iraola uses his midfielders, neither of them have really met expectations.

So, it will hardly come as a surprise to see new midfielders already being linked with Liverpool.

Liverpool could make a surprise move for Leeds United midfielder Ao Tanaka

Right now, one of Liverpool's biggest problem areas is their midfield.

As mentioned, a couple of Iraola's current options just aren't cut out for the double pivot role that he wants his midfielders to play.

However, since they didn't sign any new central midfielders in the summer, it feels like that could be their next port of call in January.

Many big-name players have been linked with an Anfield switch but, a surprise option has just emerged.

Ao Tanaka is currently plying his trade for Leeds United.

And, even though he was signed by them back when they were in the Championship, he's quickly managed to adjust to life as a Premier League player.

This season alone, the number six has featured in 12 games across all competitions for the West Yorkshire outfit and, according to TEAMtalk, thanks to the fact that his contract expires in 2028, Liverpool have thrown their hat into the ring.

The Japanese international is 28 years old with this deal feeling eerily similar to the one that Klopp struck with VfB Stuttgart for Wataru Endo a few years ago.

Tanaka has plenty of Premier League experience under his belt and, for the right price, he could be a solid signing for Liverpool.

Ao Tanaka would be a very out there signing for Liverpool

A new number six should be high on Liverpool's priority list.

Without a new one being brought in, the Reds are going to fall even further behind their title rivals than they currently are.

Indeed, other areas of their squad also need upgrading but, without a new defensive midfielder being brought in, they're always going to be on the backfoot.

Because, even though Alexis Mac Allister and Trey Nyoni are both playing well in their defensive midfield roles, if one of them ends up injured, the Reds are done.

Iraola knows this and by now, you'd hope that Julian Ward does too.

Of course, January is a notoriously hard time to strike deals for players with their prices often being inflated but, in the grand scheme of things, signing someone such as Tanaka, even just as a back-up makes an awful lot of sense.

He'd probably be available for a cut-rate deal and, since he's now 28 years old, this could be his last chance to make a big move.
