That was a wild ride! Milan turned things around completely from 2-0 behind, scoring a whopping four goals in the second half. Hutchinson, Cisse, Pulisic and Moreira were the goalscorers.

With three games left in the league phase, Milan are now on six points. That gives them a much better position as far as securing a ticket in the knockout play-offs goes.

Done deal! Hutchinson leads the counter again, picking out Pulisic, and the American finds Moreira at the back post. He takes his time and scores the fourth for the Rossoneri.

Huge counter-attacking opportunity, led by Hutchinson, but Pulisic couldn’t make the most of Moreira’s pass. He absolutely needed to score that.

Hutchinson nearly created another goal there. Nice low cross that was cleared just before Rabiot could get a touch on it.

OH MY DAYS! Hutchinson twists and turns on the right flank to get free from his opponent and finds Pulisic in the box, who completes the turnaround with an absolutely wonderful volley strike. WOW!

Hutchinson uses the overlap of Gila to get some space and pick out Cisse at the back post. Lovely cross and a good header from the youngster. 2-2!

Moreira complained too much there after a foul given against Diawara, so the referee booked him.

Hutchinson takes everything on his shoulders, winning the ball back and driving forward all the way from Milan’s half, before firing a lovely shot past the goalkeeper. Fantastic goal!

Terrible pass by De Winter, which gifts Olympiacos the ball in a dangerous position. One pass and then a finish was all it needed to double the lead. What a shame.

Moreira is now playing on the left flank, with Musah in the centre and Hutchinson out wide on the right. Cisse appears to be the centre-forward too.
