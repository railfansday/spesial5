Opinion: Liverpool's new transfer plans will mark the end for Jurgen Klopp favourite

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Liverpool have a lot of work to still do on their squad.

Andoni Iraola took charge of the club just after they delivered an extremely shoddy title defence.

They ended up finishing fifth in the Premier League which resulted in them spending another £150 million on new players.

And, while some of those transfers have worked out, it still feels like one or two, such as Bradley Barcola, still have a lot to live up to.

Obviously, as each season goes on, clubs try to add new players to their ranks and, since Liverpool's Premier League title rivals will be doing this, the Reds need to figure out ways to improve their ranks and get back to their best.

Unfortunately, for some of Liverpool's players, this means that their time at Anfield will need to be brought to an end.

On Monday evening, Anfield Watch revealed that Liverpool are eyeing a move for Abdellah Ouazane.

The 17-year-old is currently plying his trade for Ajax in the Eredivisie and, despite being so young, he's already featured in 20 games for the Dutch outfit this term.

During those games, he's contributed to eight goals across all competitions and he has also been played in an array of positions.

The Moroccan was properly promoted to their first-team squad this term after Mika Godts accepted a move to PSG.

Indeed, Ouazane did feature for Ajax once in their domestic cup last season but, this term is the first where he's been a regular part of Miguel Ángel Sánchez Muñoz's team.

To make the deal even sweeter and even more likely, Ouazane is actually a Liverpool fan so, if the Reds went in for him, there's very little chance that he'd say no.

Abdellah Ouazane joining Liverpool would confirm Harvey Elliott's exit

Right now, Liverpool don't really have a proper back-up for Florian Wirtz.

The young German has been the Reds' number 10 for most of the season with James McConnell taking over the role in some of Liverpool's cup games.

And, while McConnell has done a decent job, it's clear that the Reds need to start thinking about having a genuine back-up for Wirtz among their ranks.

Harvey Elliott was probably hoping that would be his role this term but, instead, he's been sent out on loan again.

His time with Valencia started off fairly poorly but, now, he's finally starting to find his feet with his main goal for the campaign likely to be get back on the good side of Iraola.

But, unfortunately for the Englishman, who was often one of the first names on Jurgen Klopp's team sheet, these recent links with Ouazane more or less confirm that his future lies away from Anfield.

That is, unless he can become Valencia's best player and start contributing to goals again.
