Oklahoma State, Eric Morris reportedly agree to 6-year contract extension worth over $30M

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Eric Morris is staying with Oklahoma State as the Cowboys head coach and the school have reportedly agreed on a new six-year deal, according to ESPN's Pete Thamel.

The 41-year-old Morris, who was hired exactly one year ago Wednesday, will see his current contract extended by two years through the 2032 season. The pact is worth more than $30 million, per On3.com's Brett McMurphy.

In his first year in charge, Morris has led the Cowboys to a 7-4 record, which includes a 39-31 win over the No. 6-ranked Oregon in September. Oklahoma State sits tied for fourth in the Big 12 with a 5-3 conference record and will finish up the regular season this weekend against Kansas.

The Cowboys, who were 1-11 last season under Mike Gundy and 0-18 against conference opponent between 2024 and 2025, are bowl eligible for the first time since 2023. They have not won a conference title since 2011.

Oklahoma State has been led this season by quarterback sophomore Drew Mestemaker, who has thrown for 3,533 yards and 30 touchdowns; senior running back Caleb Hawkins, who has amassed 1,270 yards on the ground with nine touchdowns; and sophomore wide receiver Justin Bowick, who has 10 touchdowns and 963 receiving yards on 56 receptions.

Prior to coming to Stillwater, Morris coached three seasons at North Texas. He helped the Mean Green reach two bowl games and an 11-2 record in his final season in 2025.
