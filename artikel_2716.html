ESPN chief: Media coverage of Joe Buck, Troy Aikman dustup is "disappointing"

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Joe Buck vs. Troy Aikman Real Broadcasters of the NFL drama has added another layer of unexpected reality to the ultimate reality show that is pro football. Their mutual boss claims that ESPN doesn’t like the coverage of the situation.

“Joe and Troy have done a great job for us on Monday Night Football.” ESPN president Jimmy Pitaro said Tuesday at the SBJ Media Innovators conference, via Brandon Contes of Awful Announcing. “When I say ‘us,’ they’ve done a great job for ESPN, they’ve done a great job for the league, they’ve done a great job for the game, and most importantly, they’ve done a great job for fans.

“Are we happy about the media coverage recently? Of course not. It’s been disappointing. But these guys are professionals, and they’re delivering every week. If you watched it last night, another super high-quality production and telecast, and I’d fully expect that to continue that through the season and through the Super Bowl.”

At some level, they shouldn’t be disappointed. Any attention is, at some level, good attention. While it’s unlikely that anyone is tuning in for a Monday night game solely to witness the interactions of Buck and Aikman, it adds a little extra something to the overall buzz arising from the broadcast.

The deeper question is who Pitaro and the other big shots at ESPN are “disappointed” with. The situation didn’t come to light because everyone involved decided to keep the dirty laundry in house. People started talking privately. Aikman has said plenty publicly that confirms the situation and otherwise stirs the pot.

ESPN has no cause to be disappointed with those who did their jobs by pulling the threads of a genuinely interesting, uncontived, and in many ways unprecedented story. If the folks who write the checks should be miffed at anyone, it should be at the recipients of those checks who decided not to hold their own tongues in check regarding an obviously sensitive situation.
