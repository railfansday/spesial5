Jameson Williams’ flip into end zone is audacious after Aidan Hutchinson’s injury disaster

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Detroit Lions took care of business once again in Week 11. Detroit defeated Tampa Bay 31-21 on Sunday to extend their winning streak to six games. The game featured plenty of excitement, including some celebrations that should be the talk of the NFL on Monday morning.

Lions fans probably weren't surprised that Jameson Williams was the author of one of those celebrations.

Williams hauled in a 57-yard touchdown reception in the third quarter to help the Lions pull ahead by two scores. The play is receiving a ton of attention purely for the celebration at the end.

Jamo slowed down just before the goal line and front flipped into the end zone. Lions fans everywhere held their breath as Williams somewhat awkwardly landed on his feet before hopping up and continuing to celebrate.

Williams' celebration came just one week after Aidan Hutchinson suffered a season-ending ACL injury while celebrating a sack. That disastrous injury should be a problem for Detroit throughout the rest of the season. That being the case, it was surprising to see Williams attempt such a risky celebration with Hutchinson's injury still so fresh.

Lions head coach Dan Campbell still wants his team to celebrate despite Hutchinson's freak injury. Still, the celebration felt like a poor judgment call from Jamo considering the circumstances.

It will be interesting to see what Campbell says about that celebration when he is inevitably asked about it.

Regardless, Detroit does not have much time to safely celebrate after this one. Next up for the Lions is a Thanksgiving showdown with the Bears at Ford Field.
