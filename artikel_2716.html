Indianapolis Colts Injury Report: Week 12 Who’s In and Who’s Out

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Indianapolis Colts today released their Friday injury report ahead of week 12 of the NFL season against the New York Giants.

Cornerback Charvarius Ward (knee) has been officially ruled OUT for this Sundays game against the Giants. With Ward out again this week expect Kenneth Harris to get another start. The return of Sauce Gardner helps manage the loss of Ward.

Right tackle Jalen Travis (knee) has been officially ruled OUT for this Sundays game against the Giants. With Travis out expect Luke Tenuta to get the start at right tackle or they could decide to kick Matt Goncalves out to right tackle and have Dalton Tucker in at right guard.

Tight end Will Mallory (hamstring) has been officially ruled OUT for this Sundays game against the Giants. Mallory is set to miss yet another game this season after being in injured reserve earlier in the year with a thumb injury. The Colts could be very thin at tight end this week.

Running back Seth McGowan (hamstring) has officially been ruled OUT for this Sundays game against the Giants. McGowan is expected to miss some time due to hamstring injury. Expect a bigger load for Jonathan Taylor and some more snaps for DJ Giddens.

Cornerback Cam TaylorBritt (groin) has officially been ruled as QUESTIONABLE for this Sundays game against the Giants. Taylor-Britt had his 21 practice window opened earlier in the week and managed a full weeks worth of practice. If he can be activated from IR it would be a huge positive for a thinned secondary.

Tight end Drew Ogletree (knee) has been officially ruled as QUESTIONABLE for this Sundays game against the Giants. Ogletree managed to practice fully on Thursday but missed the other two. Ogletree is a big depth piece for the team at tight end abc would be missed.
