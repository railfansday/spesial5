Cowboys vs. Titans Stock Report: Dak Prescott provides masterclass performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A win is a win. That gets old fast, especially when you don't have one that feels like a big one, but it is true for the Dallas Cowboys of this moment nevertheless. Dallas took care of business and then some against the Tennessee Titans, and with Thanksgiving Day ahead, they will now have a chance to build upon it as the stretch run of the season approaches.

Before we get too far ahead of ourselves we need to properly put away what we just witnessed. The Tennessee Titans are not a good team, but they are still an NFL team. The Cowboys did what you are supposed to do against teams of this variety and vanquished them with ease.

Our stock report as a result of it all is fully positive. Happy holidays and what not!

Sunday was Mike Pettine's first time calling defensive plays in literally a handful of years. He was obviously around the Cowboys for a few weeks before it, but like everybody involved he also had to manage the awkwardness that was last week and the Christian Parker situation.

We hardly witnessed the most unbelievable defensive performance ever, but outside of one silly moment (it was very silly) where Dallas let up, they were mostly a professional football team on defense.

Ultimatley the point here is that it was improvement. The bar was low. All that jazz.

The Cowboys offense scored on literally every single possession. There are a lot of people who deserve credit for that and the offensive play-caller is certainly one of them.

What's more is that this past week was difficult for the Cowboys as mentioned. Brian Schottenheimer made a change, a messy one to be clear, but he was proven right in the smallest sample size possible.

Everyone needed a win like the Cowboys just got, Brian Schottenheimer most of all.

We can talk about the state of the NFC East and all that stuff, but so long as Dak Prescott is playing the way that he is then the Dallas Cowboys have a chance if they get in. The quarterback position matters that much.

Obviously dak's stat line was incredible, but his disposition is what really stood out to me. Dak charged up the middle and into the teeth of the Tennessee defense early on for a fourth-down conversion. He lowered his shoulder to reach the endzone on his rushing touchdown. He dove for a first down after that.

Prescott appears to recognize more than anybody how precious time is, how it is fleeting, and that capitalizing on his right now is of the utmost importance. He played like that on Sunday.

The Cowboys offense has been so prolific as of late and a lot of it has been action through the air. As a result of the fanfare we tend to lose sight of how the run game is working out, and while it is not the most stout in the NFL it is in very capable hands with Javonte Williams.

He was on the other sideline on Sunday, but the Cowboys never reached a real point of stability with Tony Pollard. That was obviously partly the team's fault.

I say that to say Williams is arguably the most stable backfield presence that Dallas has had since the peak of the Ezekiel Elliott days. It feels like it has been forever since they have been this reliable in this way.

CeeDee Lamb. George Pickens. Javonte Williams (again). Brevyn Spann-Ford. Sunday was an absolute party if you were a pass-catcher on the Cowboys roster.

It is great to see how many options exist. We pay so much attention to Lamb and Picken exclusively, but the group at large is top-notch.

At the risk of being redundant, Spann-Ford deserves his own shout out. It remains to be seen what the full future is going to hold for the tight end position on the Cowboys with regards to him and Jake Ferguson, but his role continues to grow and develop. He is TE1 right now.

The Cowboys got to Cam Ward a handful of times on Sunday and a pair of them belonged to James Houston. We have certainly seen Houston flash at different points in the past, and many have long argued that he could be most successful when part of a bevy of pass rushers. Sunday was actual proof of that.

Another pair of sacks belonged to Quinnen Williams, not to mention the forced fumble that he caused to put even more ice on top of the game at the end.

Williams has been the most dominant defensive player on the Cowboys in a while. Obviously the peak of a certain player traded away was quite special, but Williams is coming close to that territory. If you have been around long enough to pine for presence along the interior, it is an incredible joy that Dallas actually has it.

It is easy to be aggressive when things are not going your way, but it is harder to keep the disposition when they do not. Early in the game the Cowboys had a chance to go for two after a penalty on Tennessee after a touchdown, and they did not get it. Recent history has shown that Schottenheimer takes his foot off of the gas in those instances.

That was not the case here. The Cowboys showed up on Sunday to vanquish the Titans at all possible costs. That is what we want to see be the disposition of the team at every waking moment.

We have obviously hyped up a lot of different Cowboys linebackers over time, and that is far from my intention here. It is worth saying though that Shemar James has been playing quite well.

James is also handling more work and responsibility while thriving which is impressive to see. The Cowboys need their Day 3 picks to produce into players like that long-term and James being someone of that variety would go such a long way.
