📊 Corinthians held late by Atlético-MG, but leave drop zone; see table

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Matchday 36 of the 2026 Brasileirão officially concluded on Wednesday (25).

At Arena MRV, Atlético-MG and Corinthians drew 1-1.

Corinthians scored in the second half through Kaio César, just four minutes after striker Tiago Borbas was shown a red card, leaving Atlético-MG down to ten men.

Atlético-MG, however, managed to equalize in stoppage time through Cuello.

The result lifted Corinthians out of the relegation zone.

Atlético-MG, meanwhile, remain in crisis: they are now winless in nine consecutive games, with three draws and six defeats.

During that run, Atlético-MG were knocked out of the Copa do Brasil semifinals by Grêmio (3-1 on aggregate) and lost the CONMEBOL Sudamericana final 2-0 to Boca Juniors.
