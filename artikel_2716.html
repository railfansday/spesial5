Lakers Injury Report: Austin Reaves Set To Miss Fifth Consecutive Game Amid Team’s Offensive Struggles

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Los Angeles Lakers will face the Utah Jazz tomorrow night, November 23, in their second meeting with the same team in the past week. Austin Reaves was expected to return in either this game or their next one, which is against the Spurs on Friday, November 27, after a four-day break.

However, the Lakers have now confirmed that Austin Reaves (left heel bruise) will not be playing tomorrow night's game. So we should expect him back for Friday's game against the Spurs.

The Lakers' head coach, JJ Redick, spoke to the media tonight at the post-practice scrum at their facility, where he addressed how the team's offense has suffered without Austin Reaves.

A reporter noted that over the last seven games, Austin Reaves and Luka Doncic have played only a few quarters together, yet the Lakers went 6-1 over that stretch, and asked Redick what stood out the most from the team during that time.

"Our defense is getting better. I think our ability to deal with adversity, particularly this week, both during games and after a loss, has improved," JJ Redick said.

"Nobody felt good about that loss. Nobody's going to feel good about losing, but you can at least feel proud if you play well, stay together, and remain connected. You're going to lose some games that way. We just didn't play well, and I think we found it on Friday in the second half."

"We did some really good things. Our half-court offensive execution in the fourth quarter was awesome. Our defense was spectacular. Our offensive group was meeting with Dom in real time as I was making some statements about our half-court offense over the last seven games."

"The reality is that we're shooting significantly less efficiently at the rim. We're also less efficient on non-rim paint twos, and we're shooting less efficiently from 30 feet. So, we're missing shots, right? Luka had three high-turnover games this week," Redick further noted.

"But the stat that stands out the most is that, over our first 12 games, we were the number one half-court offense. Austin was on the court for 70% of those possessions."

"In the last seven games, we haven't been a good half-court offense. Austin has been on the court for 9% of those possessions. Who would have thought that not having an elite offensive player, an All-Star-level player, would hurt your offense? That's crazy to me. It's crazy. So, we're going to be fine. We're going to be fine," Redick concluded.

Austin Reaves has been a reliable second option for Luka Doncic for most of the season, averaging 24.7 points, 4.7 assists, and 3.3 rebounds while shooting 51.3% from the field and 40.9% from behind the three-point line.

The Lakers had a 122.4 team net offensive rating when Austin Reaves played (first 12 games of the season) and 113.2 in his absence (last four games). His injury is clearly hurting the team's offense.

Unfortunately, the Lakers will now have to rely on Luka Doncic's supporting cast to help the Slovenian superstar lead the offense tomorrow night as well.

While the team awaits Austin Reaves' return, it will certainly be interesting to see what adjustments they make on both ends of the floor to compensate for his absence.
