Carlos Alcaraz confirms how he is feeling physically after beating Flavio Cobolli following injury concerns

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Carlos Alcaraz has continued his winning run at the ATP Finals, despite the concerns surrounding his left hamstring injury.

Alcaraz underwent an MRI scan following his win over Daniil Medvedev, but was cleared to take to the court for his final round-robin match against Flavio Cobolli, where he would sport a sleeve on his left leg.

The fates of both players had already been decided in this group, with Alcaraz already confirmed to have topped the group, while Cobolli was already eliminated.

Alcaraz showcased the gulf in quality by beating Cobolli 6-4, 6-1, to obtain his 12th consecutive win and after the match he provided an update on how he was feeling physically.

Alcaraz was asked straight away about the topic on everyone's lips, and he admitted that the biggest challenge he is facing right now is off the court to be physically ready to compete on court, with just one day until his semi-final against Ben Shelton.

"The challenge is not on the court, it's off the court," said Alcaraz. "To do whatever it takes to be in good shape and feel physically ready for a battle tomorrow.

"It is going to be really important, the day I have ahead of me with my team, with the doctor, with the physio, but I am doing these little things. I felt great on court today, I tried not to move that much, to be aggressive and dominate the match as much as I can and I think I could do it.

"I am really happy I am getting more confident with the way I am hitting the ball and seeing what is going on in the match and after that trying to find solutions. I am really happy and proud of the level that I am playing today."

Alcaraz is not the only semi-finalist at the ATP Finals who is currently facing physical issues, with world number one Alexander Zverev feeling a recurring injury issue in his latest match. Despite Alcaraz and Zverev's injury concerns, neither player has had their serve broken in their first three matches in Turin.

Watching Alcaraz's match courtside was former world number four Tim Henman, who had previously expressed real concern about the nature of the Spaniard's injury he felt in his previous match.

Despite this, Henman had nothing but positive things to say about Alcaraz after watching his latest win, which he called 'an incredible shot making extravaganza'.

"It was just an incredible shot making extravaganza from Alcaraz," Henman said live on Sky Sports. "He has that in his locker. We knew he was through to the semi finals but if he was to write the script for a match that he came into with a bit of an injury that was perfect. He didn't have many extended rallies, I think there were one or two over nine shots and his timing was exquisite."

Alcaraz will now have a day to prepare for his semi-final match at the ATP Finals against Shelton, who will have Juan Carlos Ferrero in his corner for this match.

This will be a sixth meeting between Alcaraz and Shelton, with the former leading their head-to-head 4-1. Alcaraz won their most recent meeting in the final of the Paris Masters, but Shelton picked up his first win in their rivalry at the US Open this year.
