Elias Lindholm Delivers Historic Performance As Bruins Outlast Capitals

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Elias Lindholm Delivers Historic Performance As Bruins Outlast Capitals originally appeared on NESN. Add NESN as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Boston Bruins needed Elias Lindholm to be good Saturday night. They probably weren't expecting him to deliver one of the most remarkable individual performances in franchise history.

Lindholm scored four goals and added an assist as Boston defeated the Washington Capitals 7-5 at TD Garden. The veteran forward recorded his third career hat trick and first with the Bruins, finishing with a career-high five points.

His four goals matched Boston's franchise record for goals in a single game.

David Pastrnak added a goal and two assists, while Morgan Geekie and Charlie McAvoy each contributed three assists. Jeremy Swayman made 30 saves as the Bruins improved to 15-7-1 and earned their second consecutive victory.

The performance was particularly meaningful for Lindholm, who has quietly become one of Boston's most important offensive contributors.

The Bruins trailed 1-0 when Lindholm scored his first goal on the power play midway through the opening period. He added another goal in the second period before completing his hat trick with a deflection that gave Boston a 5-4 advantage late in the frame.

What happened next was almost as memorable as the goal itself.

Only one hat initially landed on the ice after Lindholm's third goal. Even Pastrnak later acknowledged that he hadn't immediately realized his teammate had completed a hat trick.

Lindholm scored again early in the third period, redirecting another Pastrnak shot to extend Boston's lead to 6-4. This time, the crowd responded with considerably more enthusiasm. The fourth goal capped a remarkable night for a player whose contributions often extend beyond the scoresheet.

Boston's offensive explosion was impressive, but the Bruins didn't exactly make things easy on themselves. Washington scored five times, including a short-handed goal and multiple power-play goals. The Capitals repeatedly responded when Boston appeared ready to take control.

Jakob Chychrun's third-period power-play goal brought Washington within 6-5 before Fraser Minten sealed the victory with an empty-netter.

That defensive inconsistency will give head coach Marco Sturm something to address before Boston's next game.

His performance helped Boston recover from a difficult stretch and demonstrated how dangerous the Bruins can become when their top offensive players are producing together.

Boston returns to action Wednesday against the Winnipeg Jets.

The Bruins will want a cleaner defensive performance, but they'll gladly take another night of offensive production resembling what Lindholm delivered Saturday. Four goals, five points and a place alongside some of the franchise's most memorable individual performances.
