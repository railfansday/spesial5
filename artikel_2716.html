Rams extend lead to 6-0 after Tucker Kraft fumble

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Rams doubled their lead after forcing the first turnover of Wednesday night’s game.

Linebacker Nate Landman punched the ball out of tight end Tucker Kraft’s hands and safety Quentin Lake recovered the ball on the Rams’ 43-yard-line. The play came one snap after a Kam Curl interception was wiped out by an illegal contact penalty.

The Rams picked up a first down when Packers defensive tackle Devonte Wyatt was flagged for roughing Matthew Stafford and added another on a 15-yard pass to Konata Mumpfield. Packers defensive end Micah Parsons pressured Stafford into an incompletion on the next play and a botched backward pass cost the Rams six yards before they settled for Brandon McManus’s second field goal.

It’s now 6-0 with 3:12 to play in the first quarter.
