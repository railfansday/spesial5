Matthew Stafford gets candid about Puka Nacua’s conduct amid NFL suspension

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Los Angeles Rams star wide receiver Puka Nacua is expected to miss the rest of the season after needing knee surgery.

Even if healthy, Nacua would have had to miss the Rams' next three games after getting suspended by the NFL last week.

Los Angeles Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua (12) warms up before an NFL football game against the Los Angeles Chargers in Inglewood, Calif., Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026. AP Photo/Caroline Brehman

The star receiver's suspension stemmed from his several off-field transgressions this past offseason, including a civil lawsuit filed against him in March that accused Nacua of biting a woman on New Year's Eve.

But Nacua seems to have turned a corner after this tumultuous offseason. At least, that is what his quarterback, Matthew Stafford, said during a recent appearance on "The Lets Go Podcast" with Jim Gray.

Matthew Stafford of the Los Angeles Rams looks on after a 20-13 victory over the Washington Commanders in Landover, Maryland, Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026. Getty Images

"As far as the suspension goes, you don't ever want to see guys go through that kind of thing. But, you know, from what I've seen, he has done a heck of a job this season, putting his best foot forward and trying to learn from some of the things in the past. And I thought he has done that," Stafford said.

Nacua checked into rehab during the offseason, when several off-field incidents allegedly involved alcohol. In a September interview with Netflix, Nacua said he was "kind of drowning myself" in alcohol to escape stress and avoid dealing with personal issues.

Matthew Stafford of the Los Angeles Rams celebrates with Puka Nacua after a touchdown against the Las Vegas Raiders in Las Vegas, Nevada, Oct. 25, 2026. Getty Images

It's one thing for Nacua to be staying out of trouble during Los Angeles' season.

The real test is whether he can sustain this good behavior in the upcoming offseason.
