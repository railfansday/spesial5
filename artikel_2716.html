Ravens' Kyle Hamilton exits Jaguars game with hip injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Baltimore Ravens were getting blown out by the Jacksonville Jaguars, whose defense was slowly suffocating an offense led by a third-string, undrafted rookie quarterback. And then things got worse.

Ravens All-Pro safety Kyle Hamilton was ruled out for the rest of the game, a 33-10 Jaguars win, with a hip injury in the third quarter. He was later seen standing on the sideline, which could be a hopeful sign that the Ravens didn't lose the best defensive player only a few days after losing Lamar Jackson.

It's unclear when exactly Hamilton sustained the injury, but Jonas Shaffer of the Baltimore Banner speculated it could have been on a play where he dove for an errant pass by Trevor Lawrence.

Ravens head coach Jesse Minter didn't have much of an update after the game, telling reporters the team should learn more later:

"Anytime someone leaves the game, you're concerned. Don't know much about it yet, [we'll] get more information tonight and tomorrow and see where that's at."

Hamilton in his fifth season and has earned All-Pro honors in each of the past three years, first-team in 2023 and 2025 and second-team in 2024. His athleticism, versatility and football IQ make him arguably the most valuable part of the Ravens defense.

A significant injury would also be a first for Hamilton in his NFL career, as he played at least 15 games in each of his first four seasons.
