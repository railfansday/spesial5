NFL Week 9 TV schedule: full list of games, start times, TV channels

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

It's time for another week of NFL action as the 2026-27 season rolls on, with Week 9 kicking off on Thursday, November 5, when Baltimore Ravens host the Jacksonville Jaguars in a game between two teams on completely different trajectories. Baltimore is fading fast, while the Jaguars are looking like real contenders, so we'll have to see if Jacksonville can avoid a letdown on a short week.

Other standalone games in Week 9 are an early morning Sunday game between the Cincinnati Bengals and Atlanta Falcons in Madrid, Spain, while the Chicago Bears host the Tampa Bay Buccaneers on Sunday Night Football. To wrap up the week, fans will be treated to a Monday Night Football clash between the Minnesota Vikings and Buffalo Bills.

This week, the Pittsburgh Steelers and Tennessee Titans are on a bye week.

When and where will your favorite team be taking the field? A full look at the TV schedule for Week 9 of the 2026-27 regular season can be seen below (all times Eastern).
