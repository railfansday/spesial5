Jaguars players react to team's surprising decision on Bates, Davis

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Jacksonville Jaguars made a huge splash ahead of the trade deadline by acquiring safety Jessie Bates and defensive tackle Jordan Davis to help bolster the defense. The Jaguars are not only locks for the playoffs, but have clear aspirations for the Super Bowl, and while the defense is already one of the best units in the league, Jacksonville clearly is looking for Bates and Davis to make it even stronger.

It was perhaps surprising, then, that both Bates and Davis did not play against the Tennessee Titans in Week 10. Neither player had a bye with their previous teams, and the Jaguars' bye week had already passed, meaning they would not have any time off had they played. Jacksonville coaches and staff, then, intentionally sat them both so they could have a week to rest and prepare for the rest of the season.

According to NFL insider Jordan Schultz, the move was incredibly impressive to the rest of the team.

Schultz first pointed out that the additions of Bates and Davis, as well as DaVon Godchaux, has given a boost to a team that was already doing well.

"The Jags are on fire right now. You can make the argument that they're certainly one of the very best teams in football," he began. "Last week they traded for Jessie Bates, DaVon Godchaux, and Jordan Davis, transforming a defense they believe can be top three in football. I spoke to several players after those moves on the team, and there's an energy and excitement in the locker room they haven't felt in a long time. Even compared to last year's playoff run and long winning streak, this feels different."

The defense is among the top 10 in the NFL, but there are some areas, like missed tackles and stopping explosive plays, that can be improved. It's expected that all three will do just that, and if they do, Jacksonville could possibly end up with the best defense in the league.

But Jaguars players were not only impressed by their potential Super Bowl run. Schultz said multiple players reached out for an entirely different reason about the trade, and that's the decision to give Bates and Davis an unofficial bye week.

Neither player had received a bye with their previous team. So Jacksonville already had its bye. So the coaches and staff decided collectively to give both players the week off to basically recover, learn the playbook, and prepare.And if you think about that, Jacksonville just gave up a first round pick, two twos, a fifth rounder for these two players, Bates and Davis. And they're sitting them in their first game. And several players brought this up to me unprompted later in the week.They were genuinely impressed that an organization making such aggressive moves to win a Super Bowl would also prioritize the players' well-being. And I think that tells you a lot about what's happening in Jacksonville right now. Because every team talks about being player first, but Jacksonville just backed it up with a very real decision. And then when you get to December and January, when guys are banged up and the stakes are at their highest, that kind of trust... that matters. Players remember how an organization, how a team treated them. And I think that's the type of thing that will bring the locker room closer.

Both players are expected to make their Jacksonville debuts against the New York Giants on Sunday, giving the Jaguars an opportunity to evaluate their revamped defense as they continue their push toward the postseason. And regardless of what happens on the field, Schultz's reporting suggests the organization has already accomplished something important.

Jacksonville has demonstrated to its players that their well-being remains a priority, even as the franchise pursues its first-ever Super Bowl appearance. And in a league where winning often takes precedence over everything else, that decision appears to be resonating throughout the locker room.
