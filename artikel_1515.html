Roma prepare two-year contract extension for Hermoso

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Mario Hermoso is one of the most Gasperini-like players in the Roma squad: he attacks and defends with the same aggressiveness and desire to make an impact.

Since being coached by the Grugliasco-born coach, the Spanish center-back has scored seven goals, three of which came in the first half of the season, the last of which opened the scoring at home against Sassuolo.

Among his peers, only Van Dijk and Querfeld have scored better during this period, with eight goals.

The 1995-born player’s contract expires on June 30th, but for the Roma coach, it’s already time to quickly clarify the future of one of his most loyal soldiers.

Roma and Mario Hermoso are already discussing a contract renewal. As reported by Corriere dello Sport, the parties are close to reaching a deal: the former Atlético Madrid player is expected to sign a new deal until 2029 with the option of a further year (2030).

An agreement with a significant salary cut, as other key players in the dressing room, such as Paulo Dybala and Lorenzo Pellegrini, have already done. The story between the Giallorossi and Mario Hermoso is set to continue.
