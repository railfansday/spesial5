Romano: Roma finalize Hermoso’s new contract until 2029

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

From obscure figure to undisputed leader. Mario Hermoso is enjoying a particularly successful period in his career with Roma, and his performances have earned him a contract renewal.

According to Fabrizio Romano, the Giallorossi’s Spanish number 22 is one step away from signing a contract that will keep him at the Trigoria club until June 2029.

This season, the former Bayer Leverkusen and Atlético Madrid player has already made 11 appearances and scored two goals against Atalanta and Sassuolo; his tally rises to three if you include the Champions League, a competition in which the Spanish centre-back also scored against Slovan Bratislava.

Along with Mancini, Ndicka, and Balerdi, Hermoso is Gasperini’s most used defender and has become a fixture in the Giallorossi defense. A two-year extension is imminent, with Roma ready to lock him down until 2029.
