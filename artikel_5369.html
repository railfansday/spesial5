XRP Price Was Worth Less Than a Penny When GTA 5 Launched: Could GTA 6 See It Hit $100?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The CryptoNews editorial team is composed of seasoned writers specializing in cryptocurrency and blockchain technology. Their expertise ensures comprehensive, accurate, and insightful content for...

XRP price was trading below one cent when Grand Theft Auto V arrived in September 2013. Thirteen years later, Rockstar is preparing to release GTA 6, while XRP has grown into one of the largest cryptos. XRP is a financial giant today.

The comparison is striking. On September 17, 2013, the day GTA V launched, XRP was trading at $0.009613. Today, XRP trades near $0.95, representing a price gain of almost 100 times from that launch-day price.

But can XRP deliver another extraordinary rally as GTA 6 arrives? A $100 price target would turn a relatively small crypto investment into a fortune, but it would also require XRP to reach a market valuation never seen before in the crypto industry.

When GTA V introduced players to Los Santos in 2013, XRP was still largely flying under the radar. Its price sat below one cent, and its market cap was ranging around $75 million. Then the following years changed everything.

XRP broke above $0.10 in May 2017 before accelerating during the historic crypto bull market. By December 2017, the token had crossed $1 and continued climbing toward its January 2018 all-time high of $3.84.

That is an extraordinary transformation from its GTA V launch-day price. A $1,000 investment at $0.009613 would have purchased more than 100,000 XRP. At $3.84, that position would have been worth nearly $400,000. Something that only crypto can do.

However, XRP’s journey was far from smooth. The token endured successive bear markets, regulatory uncertainty, and the SEC’s lawsuit against Ripple in December 2020. Its recovery eventually pushed XRP back toward record territory in 2025, when it reached $3.65.

Now, XRP is trading around $0.95, substantially below its previous peaks. Will another major recovery begin before GTA 6 is released?

Rockstar’s GTA 6 arrives more than 13 years after GTA V, creating an interesting milestone for XRP investors. XRP has experienced multiple market cycles during the same period, transforming from a sub-penny asset into a major digital currency.

XRP currently has 63.5 billion tokens in circulation. At $100 per token, its market capitalization would reach $6.35 trillion. That would put XRP among the most valuable financial assets in the world, exceeding the market capitalization of most publicly traded companies.

The bullish argument rests on XRP’s potential to capture a much larger share of institutional payments, cross-border settlements, and digital asset liquidity. Ripple has spent years building financial infrastructure around the XRP Ledger, while institutional interest in crypto investment products continues to develop.

A stronger crypto market could also provide the liquidity needed for another substantial XRP rally. But XRP would need considerably more capital, adoption, and sustained demand to justify a multi-trillion-dollar valuation.

The historical comparison remains fascinating. XRP went from less than a penny when GTA V launched to several dollars during subsequent crypto cycles. Few people back then would have believed XRP could get to where it is now.
