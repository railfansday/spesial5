The Red Cathedral brings its competitive cathedral-building board game to iOS and Android

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It’s been a little while coming, but Devir’s digital edition of The Red Cathedral is finally out on iOS and Android (and on PC through Steam, for that matter). I wrote about it back when it was first announced in May, and Iwan had another look in September once it got a release date, so we’ve been keeping tabs on this one for most of the year.

You’re after the Tsar’s favour - Ivan the Terrible, no less, and so is everyone else at the table. Up to four of you are chipping in on the same cathedral, though each of you has claimed your own bits of it, and you need to go and find the materials to actually build those bits and then decorate them. The clergy and the city’s guilds come into it too. Whoever’s racked up the most prestige by the time the whole thing is finished wins.

The board game itself is by Shei & Isra, and it’s done well for itself, with more than 100,000 physical copies sold. Devir has reused the art from the original for the digital version (adapted and expanded a bit, so it isn’t just a scan of the box), and there’s a step-by-step tutorial as well, which I’d imagine will come in handy for a lot of people picking this up on their phone without having seen the tabletop version first.

If you can’t get anyone together, there’s an AI to play against, with three difficulty levels. Or there’s multiplayer for up to four, locally or online, and you can invite friends or just go up against random people. It’s cross-platform too, so you might well end up across the table from someone on Steam, which should mean less time waiting around for a game.

The Red Cathedral costs $6.99 on iOS and Android, a few dollars less than the $9.99 Steam version, and there’s 20% off for the first two weeks. The discount should be around until roughly November 25th.
