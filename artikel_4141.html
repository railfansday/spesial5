Warhammer Survivors: How To Unlock Every Character

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

There are over 40 characters to unlock in Warhammer Survivors across its 40K and Age of Sigmar universes: here's how.

Warhammer Survivors has over 40 characters to unlock across two universes, from Boltgun original Malum Caedo to the iconic Gotrek Gurnisson, brandishing his signature Fyrestorm Greataxe, and Marneus Calgar, the legendary Space Marine.

You'll need to unlock certain characters if you want to unlock different weapons and evolutions, like the incredibly powerful Poisoned Throwing Knives or the Vampire Survivors staple—garlic. Well, here it's called Shield of Faith and Nebulous Sea-Fog. So, in this guide, we'll walk you through every character in the game, what weapon they start with, and how to unlock them.

Daedalosus is one of the trickier characters to unlock, as he's tied to a quest that spans across several stages. First, you need to find him in Desert Wasteland, east of the spawn point.

Warhammer Survivors' 40K universe has 21 evolutions to find, and unlocking them all will grant you a special in-game reward.

Purchase the Teleportarium for 10,000 coins—this will unlock the Astropathic Relay on every Warhammer 40K stage. Activate it on the Desert Wasteland and Daedalosus will start popping up on other stages.

Once you've activated them all, Daedalosus will be unlocked as a playable character.
