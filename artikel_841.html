Jenny McCarthy’s Son Evan Asher Tearfully Vows to Buy Back Family Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jenny McCarthy’s son Evan Asher broke down in tears while sharing the “saddest moment” of his life, moving out of his mom and stepdad Donnie Wahlberg’s home, noting he hopes to "one day" buy it back.

Jenny McCarthy’s son Evan Asher isn’t masking his feelings about leaving his childhood home.

Over a month after the Masked Singer judge confirmed she and her husband Donnie Wahlberg were moving from St. Charles, Ill. to Las Vegas, her 24-year-old son shared an emotional video, in which he tearfully expressed his sadness over saying goodbye to his childhood home.

“The saddest moment of my entire life has hit me harder than ever,” Asher, who was diagnosed with autism around age 3, wrote in a Nov. 15 Instagram post, which featured a video of him crying in the car. “I have officially moved away from my home and life just won’t be the same anymore. And I’m so sorry I was very emotional.”

“But when you live somewhere most of your life, have so many friends, and make more nostalgic memories than everything in any other house that you’ve lived in it’s a lot to take in,” the content creator continued. “So I knew I had to share my emotions with you all.”

While Asher—whom McCarthy shares with ex John Asher—navigates the difficult goodbye, he voiced his hope that the new owners will have an impactful experience as he did. That said, the YouTuber did reveal he plans to return to the Chicago-area home someday.

“One day. ONE DAY. I will buy this place again and start a family of my own,” he promised, adding, “Thank you house for everything overall. I love you and god bless you.”

And Asher didn’t hold back in sharing his emotions with his followers, breaking down in tears as he filmed himself riding in a car.

As he admitted, “It just really hurts. It hurts really bad. I’m just not doing great.”

In late September, McCarthy and Wahlberg listed their Tudor-style home, with the Two and a Half Men alum revealing in the comments of a People Sept. 24 Instagram post that the family was “going to Vegas. Not Cali.”

While the couple has yet to open up about the reason behind the move, McCarthy shared earlier this year that she was having “constant infections” due to the mold in her home, sharing that she was “living in a tent” at one point.

It’s no surprise the couple are looking to make Vegas their permanent home. Wahlberg—dad to sons Xavier, 33, and Elijah, 25, with ex-wife Kim Fey—has been hitting the stage with New Kids on the Block for the past year and a half for their Vegas residency. Plus, his younger brother Mark Wahlberg and his family also live in the city.

Despite his emotional reaction to the move, Asher emphasized that his mom and stepdad “made the right decision.”

And McCarthy proved that she supports his decision to buy the home when he gets older.

“Promise someday you’re gonna knock on that door and buy it for your family,” she commented on his Instagram video. “It’s not goodbye. It’s see you later. I love you. Hugging and kissing your heart.”
