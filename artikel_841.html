Pregnant Jen Affleck Enjoys Maui Babymoon Amid Zac Affleck Divorce

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Jen Affleck revealed she packed her bags for Maui for a babymoon as she prepares to welcome her and her estranged husband’s fourth baby.

Jen Affleck's new secret weapon is putting herself first.

As the Secret Lives of Mormon Wives star navigates her divorce from Zac Affleck while pregnant with their fourth baby, she decided to treat herself to some R&R under the Hawaiian sun.

“Finally made it to Maui,” Jen captioned a Nov. 16 Instagram Story. “Taking this time to slow down and soak in every little moment."

Next to her in the snap was her and Zac’s eldest child, 5-year-old Nora, who wore an apt floral headband as she snuggled next to her mom. The mother-daughter pair also wore matching flower clips in their hair.

The time away comes shortly after Jen—also mom to kids Luca, 3 and Penelope, 16 months, with Zac—admitted navigating her divorce has taken its toll.

“I'm not doing the best right now,” she said to E! News in an exclusive interview earlier in November. “I'm just taking one day at a time, and really just focusing on my kids. And obviously, the timing sucks, especially with me being pregnant."

Nevertheless, the Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County star noted, “Ultimately, it was the right decision for us.”

In fact, she feels more herself than ever—and her time filming the spinoff series was just the start of that.

"I feel like for the past seven, eight years of my life, I've tried to mold myself into who I think people want to see, whether it be for my marriage or for TV or for the world,” the 27-year-old said. "And this year was the first time that I really felt like I was reclaiming my identity and who I actually am.”

As for Zac, he previously shared insight into his and Jen’s split in the wake of her Nov. 3 divorce filing.

After initially telling People Nov. 5 that he would "always be Jen’s supporter," the Brigham Young University graduate opted to "speak from the heart" through TikTok.

Noting that he learned about the filing from TMZ, Zac confessed the split was "something we've been talking about for a long time."

"Jen came to me about a year ago after Dancing with the Stars and told me she was unhappy and wanted a divorce," the 30-year-old explained. "I basically begged her to stay."

He also admitted, "I still love Jen," identifying her "light" and "good heart" as the reasons he fell in love in the first place.

As such, the decision to go their separate ways romantically has weighed on Zac, especially as he feels disappointed for their four children.

"The thing that's eating me alive the most is I feel like I've just I feel like I failed my kids," he continued. "I'm worried that I'm going to cause them damage, and I just hope that we can still give them a normal childhood."

He ultimately attributed their fallout to the result of when "life happened" but also said that in his eyes, the Hulu series had become "a show that's really hurt a lot of people's lives."

Read on for where the rest of the SLOMW couples stand.

Taylor Frankie Paul & Dakota Mortensen: Broken Up

While Taylor Frankie Paul's romance with Dakota Mortensen—they welcomed son Ever in 2024—bloomed on The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives, they called it quits due to Dakota's infidelity.

"Right after Ever was born, Dakota and I were living together and in bliss with our new baby, and I was even considering engagement," Taylor—who began dating the real estate agent in July 2022—revealed during the season two premiere of the Hulu series. "And then I learned that he was lying this entire time."

Taylor was told by a woman named Jenna that she and Dakota had been intimate and he had "spent the night more than once." (He has denied the claims, writing in a September 2024 TikTok that he "never cheated.")

On the series, Taylor said that Dakota accused Jenna of instigating their affair.

"No, it was him," she emphasized in the confessional. "He said he had just kissed her on the couch. No, he went on the couch, they hooked up on the couch, and then they went to the room and they hooked up."

Since ending her romance with the Basin Tiling founder, the mom of three (she shares two kids with ex-husband Tate Paul) was set to find love once again on season 22 of The Bachelorette, but things got a bit thorny when news broke in March 2026 of an alleged confrontation between the exes weeks earlier.

A spokesperson for the Draper City Police Department confirmed to People that there was an open "domestic assault investigation," telling the outlet "allegations have been made in both directions.”

Days later, a video of a previous 2023 physical altercation between Taylor and Dakota surfaced online, prompting ABC to cancel the MomToker's season of the dating franchise.

As for Dakota, he has kicked off a romance with show guest star Shinia Powell, though he previously apologized to Taylor for hooking up with her during a season four episode.

"I think it's because when we were going through our personal stuff—and I know you haven't seen it—but he struggled through a porn addiction," she explained. "There's a scene where one of my friends says something along the lines of how they could see how he was confused sexually."

Through the ups and downs, the reality star emphasized that she and Connor—who were 23 and 22, respectively, when they got married—are stronger than ever. In fact, they even renewed their vows in February 2026 for their 10th wedding anniversary.
