Bradford City boss Michael Duff on 'big turning point' versus Doncaster Rovers plus praise for Nick Powell and two substitutes

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A low-key derby came to life in the final half-hour after Nick Powell – making his first league start of the campaign – headed the hosts’ in front on 55 minutes.

Simkin repelled the attempts of City to make it 2-0 and Rovers levelled on 74 minutes, courtesy of an overhead kick from Jordan Thomas as City conceded a spectacular goal from the visitors’ first effort on target for the second successive league game, following Joe Low’s opener for Huddersfield Town at BD8 on October 31.

Soon after, substitute Humphrys, impressive when he entered the fray, fired the hosts back in front following perceptive work from fellow replacement Will Swan.

That looked to be that, only for City to switch off in the fifth minute of stoppage time from Robbie Gotts’ corner, allowing Sean Grehan to secure a precious point for lowly Rovers.

Duff, whose side moved up to sixth, said: “I thought the performance was good. We scored twice, the only thing we didn’t do was get a two-goal lead.

"At 1-0, the big turning point is probably the goalkeeper save. If it gets to 2-0, I think we win the game.

"But when there’s only one goal in it, you’re always on the edge of your seat. We just switched off from a corner in the 95th minute which is unlike us.

"They’ve had two shots on target and scored two goals. Jon (McCracken) has not made a save and one of them is an overhead kick again.

"You take that out of it and there were a lot of good things. We looked a real goal threat against another team that’s come and completely changed the way they normally play.

"The positive is that we looked like we had a few more ideas and looked a real goal threat.

"I thought we were pretty much on top for most of the game. Even from the moment we got the second goal until they scored, it wasn’t like we sat in and it was wave after wave.

"If you play every week and they’re only having two shots – and they’re not scoring from an overhead kick that’s very similar to the Huddersfield. That won’t happen all season.

"No one likes conceding in the 95th minute but we should have won the game.

"There’s disappointment in the dressing room but I don’t mind it. It’s a good thing to walk out with.

"Then Monday is your evidence about what was good and what we need to tidy up.

"It’s hard to prep when you don’t know what the opposition are going to do at the minute. The fact they changed it, we saw it and reacted really well.

"Grant (McCann) is normally a possession-based manager and didn't try and play out at all today, but that's part of the challenge.”

Powell found the net for the ninth time this season and continued his excellent start to the campaign.

On the veteran striker, Duff, whose side welcome former club Barnsley – now bottom – on Tuesday, continued: “I was really pleased for him. he had to work hard to get to that.

"I thought Stephen Humphrys was good when he came on as well, so again we've scored two goals, we've got two nines on the pitch that both scored.

"Obviously Jake Beesley missed out through injury but he'll be back at some point, so there's good competition for places in that part of the pitch.

“Swanny’s (Will Swan) came on and has been involved and impacted the game well.”

The game saw Josh Neufville make his first-team return after two months out with hamstring trouble.

Duff added: "We probably could have got a little bit more out of Josh but you've got to think of the bigger picture. Powelly and Josh will be better for the minutes in them as well.”
