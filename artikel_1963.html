Manchester United’s tormentor in the Champions League open to move as Bruno Fernandes successor

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Bruno Fernandes and Manchester United are locked in contract negotiations but they may have already identified his successor at Old Trafford.

Manchester United could have an opportunity to sign Nico Paz in 2027, with the Como midfielder reportedly open to a move to Old Trafford. He is viewed as a a long term replacement for Bruno Fernandes.

The 22-year-old has attracted plenty of attention following an impressive start to the season in Italy. Paz has become one of Como’s most important players, recording six goals and eight assists across all competitions.

His Champions League performances have been particularly impressive, with three goals and five assists in five appearances. One of his standout displays came against Manchester United, when he scored twice in Como’s 4-1 victory over the Premier League side.

According to The Athletic, United are among the clubs interested in the Argentine international, who would be willing to consider a move to Manchester. However, Champions League qualification is expected to play an important role in his decision.

Arsenal and Liverpool have also been linked with the attacking midfielder.

United’s interest comes amid uncertainty surrounding Fernandes’ future. The Portuguese captain recently confirmed that contract discussions had been put on hold, despite the club offering him a new deal.

Fernandes is under contract until the end of the 2026-27 season, although United have an option to extend his stay by another 12 months. At 32, the club must also consider their longer-term plans for the attacking midfield position.

Nico Paz fits the profile perfectly to take that responsibility. His performances for Como have been brilliant as he continues to score and provide.

However, United face competition from Real Madrid, who have a buy-back option worth €80 million. Paz is reportedly reluctant to return to the Bernabeu without assurances over regular first-team football, particularly with Jude Bellingham and Arda Guler already ahead of him in that position..

Real Madrid and Como have officially confirmed that the midfielder will remain in Italy for the 2026-27 campaign, with Madrid retaining the option to bring him back for the following season.

There could also be a small advantage for United in their pursuit, with Paz and head coach Mauricio Pochettino sharing the same representatives.

Much will depend on United’s progress during the remainder of the season. Champions League qualification could be decisive in convincing Paz that Old Trafford is the right place for the next stage of his career. Without Champions League football, that might be a tough ask to have him at Old Trafford next season.
