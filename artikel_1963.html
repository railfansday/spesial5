Alan Shearer makes Arsenal claim after win over Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Alan Shearer hailed Arsenal’s defence as the ‘best in world football’ after a hard-fought win over Liverpool.

The reigning champions moved to the top of the Premier League with a 1-0 victory at Anfield on Sunday afternoon.

Kai Havertz gave Arsenal the lead on 16 minutes and that proved to be enough to bag all three points despite some intense Liverpool pressure.

While Liverpool mounted attack after attack, they mustered just one shot on target against an Arsenal side who have conceded just 10 goals in 15 games this season.

‘That is how you work as a team,’ Shearer told Premier League Productions after Arsenal’s first win at Anfield in 14 years. ‘That is how you defend as a team.

‘Every single one of them to a man were outstanding. When they were under the cosh in the second half, you could see how much they wanted to defend and what it means to them to keep a clean sheet.

‘I don’t think there is a better team in world football that defend quite like Arsenal defend. Every player does their bit, it isn’t just the back four.

‘It was brilliant sitting behind the Arsenal bench today and seeing what it means to them. The team ethic and team work and belief Arteta has in them.

‘He didn’t get flustered at all even when Arsenal had to suffer. He trusts his players.’

Former Arsenal winger Theo Walcott also heaped praise on the Gunners’ defence, insisting that solidity will make Mikel Arteta’s side ‘difficult to stop this year’.

‘Arsenal frustrated them,’ Walcott said. ‘To limit Liverpool to no shots on target shows the level of this team. They just love defending, don’t they?

‘I felt like they don’t mind that pressure. These are champions who can do a bit of everything. They are going to be difficult to stop this year.

‘I look at Arsenal’s forward players and at no point do they get frustrated working for the team. That is different to many years ago.

‘I remember the Arsenal teams I played in and we always wanted to attack. Now they are happy to work for the team.

‘To win championships, you have to keep clean sheets, and that starts from the front. There are no players switching off, or giving up.’

While Arsenal underlined their status as Premier League title favourites at Anfield, Liverpool suffered a humbling defeat after a promising run under Andoni Iraola.

Liverpool hero Steve McManaman said: ‘They were the better team today, but they’ve walked off losing with just one shot on target.

‘In the second half they had lots of possession, their effort and work rate and enthusiasm was all there. They were just missing that little bit of magic.’

Arsenal and Liverpool are both in Champions League action this week, with the Gunners facing Slavia Prague and the Reds visiting Fenerbahce.
