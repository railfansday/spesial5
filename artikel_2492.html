Portland’s terrible defense kills them when it counts

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

High-flying Spirit run rampant, score four on Thorns’ quarterfinal defense

In September, the Portland Thorns announced that the club was bringing on Becky Sauerbrunn as a technical consultant, in part because of the four-time NWSL Defender of the Year’s “meaningful defensive expertise.” Portland’s 4-1 quarterfinal loss to the Washington Spirit on Sunday demonstrates why.

For all the attacking might on the Portland roster — and with Sophia Wilson, Pietra Tordin, Caiya Hanks, and Morgan Weaver, there is plenty — the team has had an awful defense all season long. That liability proved fatal against a high-flying Spirit side.

Washington got the party started in the sixth minute, when Trinity Rodman stripped Carolyn Calzada of the ball on the sideline without much fuss. She drove toward the endline, attracting the attention of every Portland defender and leaving a patient Mélissa Bethi entirely open to slot Rodman’s cross home. It was somehow even worse defending than when the teams played each other in Portland two months ago. In that match, Rodman and Bethi combined on a similar goal that saw Portland’s defense, which was standing between Bethi and the goal, static.

In the regular season, Portland never won a match after conceding first, so it was always going to be a tough hole to dig themselves out of. Washington made sure of this by running rampant all over the pitch. Portland could barely get the ball to their attacking threats, seeing as their defense and midfield were so thoroughly smothered and uncreative.
