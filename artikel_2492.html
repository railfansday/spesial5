Fans react to Blackhawks’ Connor Bedard injury update after surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Fans react to Blackhawks' Connor Bedard injury update after surgery appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Chicago Blackhawks shared a statement Monday night providing an update on the status of star forward Connor Bedard following his surgery.

The update also revealed the expected timeline for a full recovery for Bedard, who is in his fourth season in the NHL.

"This afternoon, captain Connor Bedard underwent successful surgery to repair his right shoulder. We expect him to make a full recovery in an approximate timeline of four months," read the statement the Blackhawks posted via their official account on X, formerly Twitter.

Meanwhile, Blackhawks supporters and NHL fans in general have shared all sorts of reactions to the update on the former No. 1 overall NHL draft pick.

"FOUR MONTHS? Oh my god bro Bedard is cooked that's 3 shoulder surgeries already," a fan said.

"Best wishes for a speedy and full recovery! The boys need to set the table for your return!" a social media user on X shared.

"Bring Austin Matthews to Chicago. Trade away 1 st round pick in 2027 and 2029 and give them a few of our bums," another one commented.

"Well. That like March so the season is over for him just have rehab and get him ready for next season no need to risk further damage," a different fan said.

"That's ok. It'll mean this season is a flush, so you'll be able to aquire another top tier draft pick you can flip for another middling defenceman," another comment read.

Bedard's lengthy absence will surely have a huge impact on the Blackhawks, who were already struggling on the ice even before Bedard got injured though they have now won three of their last four outings.

In 15 games played in the 2026-27 season, Bedard has scored seven goals and assisted 11 times for 18 points.
