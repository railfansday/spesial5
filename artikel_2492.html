“I think I helped him win the Ballon d'Or" – Luka Vušković makes cheeky Harry Kane admission

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After England's 2-1 win over Croatia last week, Harry Kane revealed that he had been keeping an eye on Luka Vušković in the Premier League and praised the young centre-back.

Vušković, who faced Kane and Bayern Munich while on loan at Hamburger SV last season, was asked about the England captain's comments by Croatian outlet HRT Sport – and came up with a humorous response.

“It means a lot to me. I think I helped him win the Ballon d'Or too, because he scored about 10 goals against me,” he said with a laugh.

“I'm very glad he won that award because he had an incredible season and deserved it. I'm delighted he said that about me.”

In reality, Kane scored three goals against HSV last season – netting twice in Bayern's 5-0 victory in September 2025 before scoring once more in a 2-2 draw in January 2026.
