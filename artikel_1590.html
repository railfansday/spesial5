Hull City vs. Brighton &amp; Hove Albion injury, suspension list, predicted XIs

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

By Seye Omidiora | 21 Nov 2026 14:00

Second-placed Brighton & Hove Albion travel to face newly promoted Hull City on Sunday in the first-ever Premier League meeting between the sides, with Fabian Hurzeler's men bidding for a third consecutive top-flight victory.

Meanwhile, Sergej Jakirovic's Tigers sit 13th and three points clear of the relegation zone, aiming to preserve their unbeaten home record while ending an eight-match winless run in the league.

Here, Sports Mole rounds up all of the latest team news for both clubs.

Doubtful: Mohamed Belloumi (thigh), Nobel Mendy (knee), Charlie Hughes (groin)
