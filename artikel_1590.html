League of Nations: Italy-France, at what time and on which channel to watch the match?

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

France are the only undefeated team in the group, with three wins and one draw in four matches. It also displays the best defense of the four selections, conceding only two goals. The Blues won 1-0 in Turkey, then 1-0 in Belgium, before sharing the points 1-1 with Italy on October 2 at home, and winning 4-1 against the Belgians on the 4th day.

Italy follows with seven points. The Squadra Azzurra lost their first match 2-0 against Belgium, before recovering by winning 4-1 in Turkey, then bringing back this draw in France and beating the Turks 3-1. With eight goals, she has the best attack in the group.

In the event of a victory in Milan, France would secure first place before the last day, with Italy no longer able to exceed ten points. Belgium, third with six points, remains in the race and travels to Türkiye to open the day. Turkey, beaten four times and still without a point, brings up the rear with eleven goals conceded.

The Italy – France match, day 5 of the Nations League, is broadcast unencrypted on TF1 this Thursday, November 12, with an on-air broadcast at 8:35 p.m. for a kick-off at 8:45 p.m. which will be given at the Stadio Giuseppe Meazza, in Milan. The channel extends its evening with the program L’après-match at 10:50 p.m. The other matches of the day are offered by the L’Équipe channel and its L’Équipe Live Foot platform. The Blues will return to Turkey on Sunday November 15 for the last day of the group, still on TF1.
