Slovakia: Thousands protest against pro-Kremlin leader Robert Fico

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Prime Minister Robert Fico has been under fire over his ties to Russia. The rallies were held on the anniversary of the 1989 Velvet Revolution that toppled communism in Czechoslovakia.

Thousands of people protested across Slovakia on Tuesday to demonstrate against Prime Minister Robert Fico and his pro-Russian government.

The demonstration was organized by opposition parties and non-governmental organizations (NGOs).

The largest protest was on SNP Square, which was the site of the main anti-Communist protests during the 1989 Velvet Revolution, in the capital, Bratislava.

Organizers said 25,000 people had gathered there.

Other rallies were also held across the country, which is an EU and NATO member​​​​​​, including in the cities of Kosice and Banska Bystrica.

The rallies were held on the anniversary of the 1989 Velvet Revolution that ended decades of communist rule in what was then called Czechoslovakia.

Protesters commemorated the fall of the communist dictatorship, which ruled Czechoslovakia for 41 years. They also called for more support for Ukraine.

The demonstrators mostly focused on Slovakia's prime minister, however, chanting slogans such as "We've had enough of Fico!"

One banner told the prime minister to "Go to hell."

Since Hungary's Viktor Orban was removed from power in April, Fico stands as Europe's most pro-Russian leader.

He opposed military aid for Ukraine and has maintained close ties with Russian President Vladimir Putin, despite European Union sanctions against Russia.

Fico, who has served as prime minister four times in over two decades, has faced numerous protests since returning to power in 2023.

Fico has also repeatedly clashed with the EU. Last year, Slovakia's constitution was amended to state that national law takes precedence over EU law.

He has also been criticized for trying to exert greater control over the media and culture, and curbing LGBTQ+ rights.

"We're the last ones in the European Union to be negotiating with the Russians... It's a disgrace," Igor Vico, a 74-year-old doctor, told AFP.

"We're at the bottom of the world, not just in Europe, and it makes me sad," he said.
