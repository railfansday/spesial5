GTA 6 Will Be Playable on XBOX One, According to Rockstar

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Daniel is a lifelong gamer with a love for various genres and gameplay styles who enjoys exploring games and playing from various angles.

A big part of what’s making the steadily-encroaching release of Grand Theft Auto 6 interesting in the current gaming climate, besides, y’know, being Grand Theft Auto 6, is that it’s only going to be available on consoles, and specifically XBOX and PlayStation family consoles. PC and Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 are being left out in the cold, at least for the time being, although rumors abound that the PC launch may come sooner than expected in order to beat out AI slop knockoffs.

I don’t claim to know all of the precise reasons for this particular launch strategy, beyond perhaps a pursuit for immediate at-launch optimization for the most readily-available consoles. That said, it would seem that, even if you own a particular console from the previous generation, you may still be able to play the game, albeit in a slightly diminished state.

Rockstar announced on its official news blog today that pre-loading for Grand Theft Auto 6 has officially begun, with the many who pre-ordered the game digitally now able to start downloading, and those who purchased codes-in-boxes able to start redeeming them and do the same. Buried within this announcement was a rather unexpected detail: it’ll be possible to play Grand Theft Auto 6 on the previous generation’s XBOX One console.

GTA 6, Rockstar's upcoming open-world adventure, is taking up 210 GB on PlayStation 5.

Here’s the interesting bit: since XBOX Cloud Gaming is also available on the XBOX One console, that means you can, in fact, play Grand Theft Auto 6 on an XBOX One by streaming it. At least as far as I’ve determined, the XBOX One will be the only previous-gen console that will be able to play this brand-new game, and an exceptionally high-end game at that.

Since XBOX Cloud Gaming is also available on the XBOX One console, that means you can, in fact, play Grand Theft Auto 6 on an XBOX One.

Of course, there are some catches here. First, no matter what XBOX console you’re playing on, any instance of Grand Theft Auto 6 played via Cloud Gaming will use XBOX Series S-tier graphical settings, i.e. not the best possible ones. Additionally, as is always the case with cloud streaming, how good the game looks and runs is largely dependent on the quality of your internet connection, so if you’ve got lousy speeds, this isn’t going to work well for you.

As GTA 6's launch approaches, everyone wants part of the PR pie, including internet ISPs.

On a semi-related note, Rockstar also confirmed that Grand Theft Auto 6 will work with Remote Play on both PlayStation and XBOX consoles, for those who want to stream the game from their console to elsewhere in their home.

I probably don’t need to remind anybody of this, but here it is anyway: Grand Theft Auto 6 is officially launching on Thursday, November 19, with the unlock time being whenever the clock strikes midnight in your particular time zone.

Whether through an installed digital copy or, apparently, on an XBOX One via Cloud Gaming, anyone who is interested in this game will, with certainty, be playing it as soon as it launches. I shudder to think what those poor digital storefronts are going to be going through at midnight on the 19th. Maybe wait until after school or work that day before you try to start playing, at least assuming you’re not one of the metric whoa-ton of people who are projected to be calling in sick that day.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is encroaching upon our reality, and upon its advent, there is a non-zero chance life will never be the same. If you’re not too busy playing the game for an indeterminate blur of time, maybe you can check out Rockstar’s official launch party in Miami the following weekend.
