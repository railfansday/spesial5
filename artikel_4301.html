2028 Ford Mustang GTD Updates Include an Interesting Special Edition

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Ford drops the Spirit of America version of the GTD in favor of a new 1901 edition inspired by Henry Ford’s ‘Sweepstakes’ car.

Despite incredibly low production numbers, even range-toppers such as the Ford Mustang GTD get year-over-year changes. For the 2028 model year, the GTD’s changes are pretty limited, but they’re headlined by a new 1901 special edition. As hinted at in the title, the 1901-spec GTD honors the Ford Sweepstakes, the vehicle Henry Ford took racing 125 years ago.

As with the Spirit of America GTD it’s replacing, production of the 1901 edition will be artificially limited. Ford says the 1901 edition will be kept to around 20 percent of the 2028 allocation, which we’re told could be anywhere from 60 to 140 cars. The livery is primarily finished in Royal Red paint, with black diagonal accents along the roof. The interior is similarly roseate, with Mahogany Red leather and Race Red stitching.

Along with the new special edition, Ford is also finally bringing magnesium wheels to the GTD. The optional wheel setup saves 50.3 pounds compared with the traditional setup. Ford originally planned to launch the GTD with the lighter wheels, but supplier constraints forced a delay. While Ford already offered 3-D-printed titanium paddle shifters and a titanium shift ring, the arrival of the 2028 GTD includes a fresh design for the parts. Changes are rounded out with the addition of full-grain leather that now covers the door panels, center console, and instrument panel, while the exterior adds new livery options and two new paint colors.

Make sure to get your letter to Santa mailed quickly, because order books for the 2028 GTD are slated to open in December. Should you secure a build slot, you won’t find your new Mustang parked under the Christmas tree, as deliveries aren’t expected to start until the second quarter of 2027. Exact pricing hasn’t been released, but the 2028 car will surely follow in the footsteps of its predecessors with a starting price of at least $330,000.
