Best civilizations in Rise of Kingdoms - Every nation ranked

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Updated on November 7th, 2026 - Version: 1.1.13.72

If you want to learn what some of the best civilizations in Rise of Kingdoms are, then I'm here to help you pick the best one for you to quickly advance and build a powerful empire.

But wait - why does it matter which civilization I pick in Rise of Kingdoms?

Well, my dear friend, that's all up to you. Are you a casual player and want to pick based on looks, history, and the famous characters associated with it? Or are you a hardcore player, dedicated to picking the absolute best civilization for the early game, then switching to another one that excels at a specific task, and finally changing once more for the late game?

If you're easily overwhelmed by everything I just mentioned, don't worry. It's a lot easier than you might think. No matter what nation you pick, you're going to do well if you just keep progressing at a steady pace and constantly upgrade your kingdom.

Is there a best civilization in Rise of Kingdoms? To tell you the truth, some will argue that there is no such thing as the best civilization in Rise of Kingdoms, although you will only start seeing a major difference in the endgame.

Of course, there are so many factors that determine what makes a good nation that it's not even funny. Take your role in your Alliance for example - what do you plan to do, and what should your main focus be? Do you want to choose one based on that?

Or maybe you want to choose a nation based on what your friends need (let's say you play RoK with a couple of friends). No matter what, some nations excel at specific tasks and others are generally good. I didn't even start getting into the F2P or premium-play style - if you plan to use real money in the game, that can also affect the civilization you should play!

So yes - it's quite a complicated process, to say the least. But taking all of that into consideration, let's take a look at the best civilizations in Rise of Kingdoms.

Britain: is an amazing choice of nation for beginners as it is good for both PvP and PvE. It's archer-focused, and Boudica is great for farming barbarians. Egypt Why?

Egypt: is a great nation for completely new players and those who want to switch their civilization, since the increased building speed and research are among the best for PvE. Germany Why?

Germany is a cavalry-focused civilization. It is great for troop training speed and especially good in the late game for the action point recovery. China Why?

China is a very good nation to pick overall. It gives Sun Tzu and it's great given the increased action point recovery speed and increased building speed.

Vikings is a civilization for infantry and melee fighters, amazing at PvP and not too shabby in PvE. It's a good, well-rounded choice (and a really cool one too!)

Korea is an archer-focused civilization. It's great for development, especially research, and has really cool-looking buildings. Arabia Why?

Arabia is another cavalry-focused nation. It's good for hunting and farming barbarians. Japan Why?

Japan is a good infantry-focused nation. It's amazing for actively farming resources, and of course, it looks really really good! France Why?

France is good for development. It is especially good if you plan to be a premium player. It gives Joan of Arc and the increased hospital healing speed is really nice.

Spain is a cavalry-focused nation. It's a good choice thanks to the passively producing resources and increased EXP Spain gains from neutral objectives. That makes this nation solid during the early game. Ottoman Empire Why?

Ottoman Empire is a solid archer-focused civilization, really good if you want a nation which benefits from being a premium player. It is focused on active skill damage during battles.

Byzantium is an okay civilization for PvP and PvE alike. It has good cavalry units and cavalry focus and it's good for farming stone. Rome Why?

Now that we've covered all the best civilizations a thought might cross your mind, and that is changing nation. If you want to switch to another nation, there are two main ways to do it: Use 10,000 Crystals (which is quite expensive if you ask me) Spend 2,000,000 Alliance Currency in the Alliance Shop for Civilization Swap Tokens (this is the recommended way, which is why you should join a good and active Alliance).
