To understand the CurrentBody Series 3 LED Mask, you have to quickly understand what LED masks do in general. LED light—whether it’s red, blue, green, or yellow—delivers low-level wavelengths of light into your skin, which get absorbed by your skin cells and boosts cellular energy. How it affects your skin depends on the wavelength (aka color) of light. Red, for example, can help trigger collagen and elastin production in your skin to reduce fine lines and firm skin. Blue, though, targets acne-causing bacteria to prevent and treat breakouts, while yellow light can potentially soothe redness, and green light targets the melanocytes in your skin to fade hyperpigmentation.

FWIW, red is the most studied, but there still isn’t enough information out there yet on the potential further benefits it can have for skin and body (like decreasing inflammation and promoting faster wound healing). There’s also near-infrared and deep infrared, both of which penetrate the deeper layers of your skin to encourage tissue repair.

Now that you understand LED, let me get into this mask’s specs. It’s a full-face flexible silicone mask with a chin strap that covers your jawline and the area directly under your chin (I’ll wax poetic about this more later). In total, there are 750 LED bulbs, all of which can take all six wavelengths of light, meaning you don’t have to have multiple LED masks for your different needs.

The most defining characteristic of the CurrentBody Series 3 LED Mask is the introduction of MyDose. When you connect the mask to Bluetooth and use the CurrentBody smartphone app, you can customize your own treatments based on where you want the wavelengths to go. When you use the brand’s smartphone app, you can create your own treatments by painting on the exact spot on your face that you want certain wavelengths to go. And because MyDose automatically calculates the optimal (and safest) dose for your skin, you can actually use multiple wavelengths at once in one area. If you wanted, you could honestly do all six at one time, and the device will manually adjust the wavelength settings so it’s safe. If you don’t wanna create your own custom setup, you don’t have to—there are four preset treatment options (advanced anti-aging, advanced clearing, complexion corrector, and balance & brighten).

CurrentBody did the research to figure out just how long you would need to get the maximum benefit from the wavelengths in this mask, and I’m pleased that it’s only 10 minutes. I usually do my treatments in the morning, after swiping my face with some micellar water. It’s become a ritual I look forward to. I’ll sip my coffee and write out my to-do list for the day (or, occasionally, scroll on my phone—duh). Before I know it, the mask can come off. The device itself is also extremely easy to figure out, even if you’re not tech-savvy.

The device itself is cordless (meaning you don’t have to plug it into a wall), but there is a rechargeable box with a touch screen on a short cord for choosing your treatment. It’s not a deal-breaker, obviously, but something to keep in mind if you like to wear your mask up and about. If I have to be seated for 10 minutes of my day, so be it.

I’m normally app-averse when it comes to beauty devices (and any device, tbh). I have enough damn apps on my phone! What is the difference between the CurrentBody Series 2 and Series 3 LED masks?

If you already have the CurrentBody Series 2 LED Mask, you don’t need the series 3 by any means. But if you’re deciding between the two of them, I absolutely recommend Series 3. First of all, there are more than 500 more LED bulbs in the Series 3 than the Series 2, meaning you’re getting a lot stronger of a treatment for only a little over $100 more. The biggest difference, though, is that the Series 2 only offers red, near infrared, and deep infrared wavelengths, not the blue, green, or yellow light functions. Plus, the app-controlled MyDose function is only available on the Series 3.

Right now, the CurrentBody Series 3 mask is actually only $455 with the brand’s 20% off Black Friday deal, making it less than the Series 2. We probably won’t see a deal like that again until next year, so if you were considering upgrading or just jumping in to the latest model, now’s your time.

How soon can I expect results with the CurrentBody LED mask?

LED therapy is a marathon, not a sprint. The brand says you can see results in as soon as eight weeks. But it really depends on what you’re aiming for. I noticed within just a few weeks that my breakouts were curbed and my skin was a bit less inflamed. But collagen synthesis, which is what softens wrinkles and firms skin, can take time and consistency. I wouldn’t expect to see major results on that front for about three months.

The topic of “is it worth it” is difficult without knowing someone else’s financial situation. But on the LED mask front, I believe this is the best one out there right now. The combination of the 750 LEDs with the MyDose technology is really unique to many of the masks I’ve tested. And it’s also one of the most comfortable to wear too.
