Start a game night tradition with these Black Friday board game deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Looking to start up a board game night tradition? Well, Black Friday week is the perfect time to host the inaugural event, with price drops across all kinds of games — from family games to hardcore strategy brain-teasers — in Amazon's Black Friday sale.

A board game isn't just the perfect thing to add to your arsenal of entertainment items at home. It's also a good gift idea for a hard-to-shop-for relative, older kids, or family who just doesn't want or need anything else — not to mention the potential for lots of fun with family and friends over Thanksgiving and Christmas.

Board games — or "table top gaming", to give it a proper name — is having a moment, which means there are lots of new and innovative titles available. There are twists on classics as well as ingenious new concepts. Don't worry if you're a bit lost in the gaming world, when it comes to board games we know our stuff, and we've combed the Black Friday listings to find you the best discounted games.

Here are the best Black Friday board game deals we've found so far. Keep checking back, however, as new deals will be added as Black Friday week rolls on.

There's a current trend for deception games, partly driven by the popularity of TV's The Traitors. Chameleon sees a group of players take turn to describe a word or concept without actually saying it. But one player is actually the Chameleon and has to guess the word from the other players' clues — all while avoiding detection. It's a ridiculous amount of fun, and perfect for big groups and players of all ages.

Catan is a veritable board game classic, and it's a great pick to snag while it's discounted. Three to four players settle a randomly laid-out island, collecting wood, brick, wool, grain, and ore to build roads, settlements, and cities. Games wrap up in about an hour to 90 minutes, and it's fairly easy to learn. And if you're tired of playing the same things over and over, Catan's modular board means no two games set up the same way. It's a significant upgrade from Monopoly that's suitable for the whole family.

The classics are classic for a reason. Mouse Trap has delighted kids and families for years, and nothing has changed after all this time. Indeed, it's as fiddly to set up as it ever was, but there's no denying the fun of setting the trap in motion. Kids will love the mechanics, parents will love the nostalgia. You can also find Black Friday discounts on other kids classics, including Connect 4, Battleship, and Candy Land.

If you've ever wanted to get started with Dungeons & Dragons but didn't know how, this board game is the perfect introduction. It has a quick-start guide, player boards for the Cleric, Rogue, Wizard, and Fighter, and cards for species, backgrounds, spells, and gear so nobody has to build a character from scratch. Three to five players run through three adventure booklets split between combat, exploration, and roleplaying, and the poster maps for the Keep and the Caves of Chaos pull from one of the oldest modules in the game’s history. It's got everything beginners need and then some, so it's a much less daunting introductory experience.

If you haven't played the Horrified series, it's worth checking out for team-based beast-slaying. Players join forces to take on various classic monster — in this version it's American monsters that include Bigfoot, the Jersey Devil, and Mothman — with different ways of killing each monster. Board game fans will be familiar with the mechanics of the gameplay but the great thing about it is that you can mix and match the monsters, so each time you play is different. It can also get pretty tense as you ratchet up the difficulty level.
