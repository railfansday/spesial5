HBO Maxs Black Friday deal is top dog once again: Get half off a year of streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

HBO Max has dropped one of the best Black Friday streaming deals for the second year in a row. While the 2026 deal isn't quite as impressive as the 2025 deal, it's still the only offer we've seen so far this season that covers 12 full months of streaming. For what it's worth, HBO Max is also one of the only streamers that didn't raise its prices this year.

Yes, you have to deal with ads throughout your streaming sessions, but for the price, it's easy to look past that. With the Basic subscription, you also won't get access to live sports.
