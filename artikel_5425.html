APEC 2026 Host City Promotional Video Shenzhen: Where the Future Takes Shape Released Worldwide

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

SHENZHEN, China, Nov. 14, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Shenzhen: Where the Future Takes Shape, the official promotional video for the host city of the 2026 Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders' Meeting, was released worldwide on November 15, as APEC Economic Leaders' Week got underway in Shenzhen.

Presented by the Publicity Department of the CPC Shenzhen Municipal Committee and produced by Shenzhen Media Group, the video takes "Where the Future Takes Shape" as its central theme.

It brings together Shenzhen's pioneering reforms, its achievements in building a modern, people-centered city, and the theme of 2026 APEC "China Year," "Building an Asia-Pacific Community to Prosper Together." Through the story of Shenzhen, the video offers international audiences a vivid look at Chinese modernization in practice, while extending a warm welcome to guests from across the Asia-Pacific and beyond. It also reflects the host city's commitment to openness and its vision of working with regional partners to create a better future.
