EA Sports FC 27 Adds AI Voice Lines of Late Commentator

EA Sports has worked with John Motson’s family to bring his iconic commentary to the hit sports game, EA Sports FC 27, as a digital tribute. If you don’t know the name, you will definitely recognize the voice, even if you aren’t a die-hard football fan.

Motson passed away in February 2023 at the age of 77 and was the main commentator for BBC Sport for 50 years. He commented on over 2,500 games and 20 international tournaments on television and radio during his tenure. Motson starred in the same role for the FIFA video game series as its first commentator from FIFA 1996 to FIFA 2006, and also made an appearance in FIFA 19.

FC 27 is the best thing a Career Mode player could ever wish for, but don't expect an easy transition.

The voice of football will return in EA Sports FC 27 in the next game update for all players to use if they wish to do so.

With permission from Motson’s widow and his son, his voice will return as the first late commentator to appear in the series. According to the BBC, EA paid Motson's family for the rights to use his likeness and recreate his voice, but the company would not disclose the financial terms of the deal.

Fred Motson, the son of John Motson, told the BBC it "means an awful lot" to have his dad's voice in EA Sports FC once again. "I grew up with FIFA," he said. "I always used to tell my parents that I needed the latest console for Christmas because otherwise dad couldn't see how the game worked."

Mat Prior, Senior Game Design Director at EA Sports FC, told the BBC, "There was no way we would have moved forward with this in any way, shape or form if they weren't fully on board."

Motson’s voice will return using a combination of old recording sessions and generative AI. Prior explained that Motson's commentary in EA Sports FC 27 was constructed using old lines he recorded over the years and AI. He said it’s "the only way" to make it happen, since recordings don’t exist of Motson speaking about and mentioning the names of modern players.

However, EA assures that the use of AI to recreate Motson’s voice won’t become commonplace. "It's not there to replace commentators in any way, shape or form... there's certainly no future plans to replace anyone with AI," said Prior.

"From a gaming standpoint, it's something we've used for many years - like machine learning to help drive animation systems and so on and so forth," outlined Prior.

The style of football commentary is unique to each individual, and every fan has their favorites. The little quips, tone, and moments of laughter make the voices feel natural, responsive, and immersive, something AI is unable to replicate.

John Motson’s tribute arrives in Title Update v1.3.0 on 26 November 2026 as an optional commentary, alongside the existing selection of broadcast teams.

Also on its way with Title Update v1.3.0 is further gameplay tuning based on player feedback, including dribbling balancing adjustments, foul call improvements, and interception recovery tweaks. Moreover, you can expect updates to matchmaking and Amps functionality in The Grounds, along with quality-of-life improvements in Manager and Player Career.
