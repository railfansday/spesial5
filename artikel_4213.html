Where To Find Every Magic Bean Location In The Ocarina Of Time Remake

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake has been released, which means it's time to do a bunch of side quests for Hyrule's strangest citizens. One of the easiest side activities to overlook is collecting and planting the ten Magic Beans in the soft soil spots throughout the map.

Since you will need these Magic Beans if you want to collect all the Gold Skulltulas and Pieces of Heart, here is a breakdown of where to find all ten so you can ensure you don't miss anything when exploring Hyrule.

To get the Magic Beans, you need to be Young Link, as the seller doesn't exist when you're an adult. Once you're in the past, head to Zora's River and look for a large, bald man sitting near the broken gate. Speaking with the Bean Seller, you will learn that he is, well, selling ten Magic Beans; however, the price for these will really drain you of Rupees.

The price for one Magic Bean will start out costing 10 Rupees, with each subsequent one increasing in price by an additional 10 Rupees. This means if you want to buy all of the Magic Beans, you need a whopping 550 Rupees. Of course, you do not need to buy them all at once, so it can be beneficial to keep picking up Magic Beans as you progress through the game.

Once you have a Magic Bean, look for a Bug Hole location and approach it. Equip the Magic Bean and then use this item while standing next to the soil to plant a flower. Remember, each of these Bug Holes also produces a Gold Skulltula if you drop an insect from a bottle on top of it. Make sure to do this so you can collect the Gold Skulltula Token, then plant the Magic Bean into the soft soil.

Remember, all this can only be done as Young Link, as those planted Magic Beans will sprout into colorful flowers you can ride to previously inaccessible locations.

Remember, there are a total of ten Magic Bean spots. You can only plant the Magic Beans as a child; however, some areas you will need to open up first as an adult before returning as a child. That being said, eight of the ten can be accessed before you even pull the Master Sword out of the Temple of Time.

The first Magic Bean location is right next to the Bean Seller. Directly to the right of the vendor, you will find a Magic Bean spot that you can use to reach the central ruins as an adult, where you can both reach a Gold Skulltula that spawns along the cliff walls and the Piece of Heart if you didn't snag it as Young Link.

Your next Magic Bean spot can be found at Lake Hylia, just to the left of the Lakeside Laboratory's main door. Using this Magic Bean Flower will take you around the lake as an adult, allowing you to access the fishing area without the Scarecrow Song. It will also take you to the Laboratory Roof, where you can use the ladder to reach a Piece of Heart at the top.

Magic Bean spot number three is located way back in Kokiri Forest. When you arrive, go to the store where you purchased the Deku Shield. Behind the store, to the right of the door, you will find a soft soil spot in the brush and grass. Using this flower will take you up to a spot with a single Purple Rupee and several Green Rupees.

One of two Magic Bean spots in the Lost Woods. From the Kokiri Forest entrance, go Left and then Left again. When you enter, look directly to your left, and you will see the bug hole where you can plant the bean. This flower will take you to a small chest which contains a Purple Rupee.

The next Magic Bean location is also in the Lost Woods. From the Kokiri Forest entrance, go Right, Left, Right, Left, and Left to enter a small grove with two Business Scrubs. Defeat them both, and you'll find a soil spot to the right where you can plant a Magic Bean. Riding this flower as an adult will bring you to a Gold Skulltula spawn location above you.

Upon entering Kakariko Graveyard, take the first two sets of stairs directly in front of you and then go to the left by the tree. You'll find the Magic Bean spot along the stone wall. This will give you a flower as an adult that you can ride to reach a Piece of Heart in the ruins above you.

For this Magic Bean spot, make your way up the Death Mountain Trail to the entrance of Dodongo's Cavern. Just inside the entrance, to the right, you will find a soft soil spot. Taking this flower will bring you to the top of Dodongo's Cavern's entrance, where you will find a Piece of Heart.

This one is a little trickier to obtain, as you'll need to progress through the story until you reach the entrance to the Fire Temple as Adult Link. When you do, Sheik will teach him the Bolero of Fire song, allowing you to warp to this spot in Death Mountain Crater. Now, use your ocarina to warp back to the Temple of Time and switch to Young Link. Play the Bolero of Fire as Young Link to go back to this spot and go to the soft soil spot directly behind you next to the crest of the Fire Temple on the ground. Remember, you won't have the Goron Tunic as a child, so work fast to avoid burning to death. Then return to this spot as an adult to find a flower that will take you to a Piece of Heart.

The most unnecessary Magic Bean Flower. Go to Gerudo Valley as a child and jump down into the river below near the waterfall. Swim to the stairs that lead to the area where the Gerudo warrior is tending to her cow. At the end of this path, near the rocky wall, you will find the ninth soft soil spot. Using this Magic Bean Flower will take you to a hidden Piece of Heart behind the nearby waterfall, but you can just go here as a child, making this a bit redundant.

The final Magic Bean spot is located in the Evil Desert Colossus right outside the Spirit Temple entrance. Just like the Death Mountain Crater one, you need to reach here as an adult and learn the Requiem of Spirit song from Sheik. Once they've taught you the song, go back to the Temple of Time, become a child, and play this new tune to teleport to the Spirit Temple entrance. To the left of the door into the temple will be the soft soil spot. Using this flower as an adult allows you to reach both a Gold Skulltula spawn point on one of the large rocks in the desert and a Piece of Heart!
