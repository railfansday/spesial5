Full practice for Alvin Kamara on Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

New Orleans Saints running back Alvin Kamara (back) was able to practice in full on Friday, Nov. 6, and he was removed from the injury report ahead of the Week 9 game against the Cleveland Browns.

Why it Matters: Kamara is worth a look as a RB3 or flex fantasy option heading into Week 9, but as long as Kendre Miller is healthy, he is expected to lead the backfield. Expect 10-12 total touches, at most, for Kamara against the Browns.
