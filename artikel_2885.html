Why Did Firefly Aerospace Stock Drop Today?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

One Wall Street analyst expects Firefly stock to double in 12 months. Is it right?

By Rich Smith – Nov 25, 2026 at 11:37AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Firefly Aerospace ›

Firefly Aerospace (FLY -7.54%) stock tumbled 8.4% through 11:15 a.m. ET Wednesday -- which is kind of curious, because the only Firefly news on the wires today is actually good news.

As TheFly.com (no relation to "FLY," btw) reports, investment bank Needham & Co. just upgraded Firefly stock to "buy" with a $35 price target.

A $35 price target on a stock that costs less than $17 today? That sounds pretty good. If Needham is right, it means Firefly stock could literally double in price over the next 12 months -- and then keep going up!

But why does Needham think that will happen?

While still a start-up space stock, Needham thinks Firefly has already "demonstrated success" in its early efforts at building small rockets, medium rockets, and lunar landers. "With global demand accelerating," says the analyst, Firefly is well positioned to capitalize on supply gaps left open by larger rocket makers. And at a share price only one-third of what it cost as recently as May, the banker believes Firefly stock looks attractively valued.

Needham may be right -- but I admit I have reservations. As regards small rockets, out of seven Alpha rocket launches, Firefly has scored only three completely successful launches, versus four complete or partial failures.

Meanwhile, on medium rockets... well, Firefly hasn't launched any of those yet (though it is working on a couple).

That said, Firefly is currently the only space stock that can boast it landed a spacecraft on the moon with 100% success. That's a record worth praising -- although I'm not sure it makes Firefly stock worth 9x trailing sales, which is what it currently costs!

It's still my single best argument in favor of buying Firefly, however. If you want to bet on a moonshot, you might as well bet on the stock with the very best record on the moon.
