AFC North Week 10 Recap: Browns pull off another comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Three teams in the AFC North appear poised for a playoff push down the final stretch of the season.

The Bengals took the division lead with a 38-18 dismantling of the Steelers on Sunday Night Football.

No quarterback has played better than Joe Burrow over the past month. Against Pittsburgh, he completed 35 of 44 pass attempts for 406 yards and four touchdowns. Burrow is now the betting favorite to win MVP with a league-high 27 passing touchdowns through 10 weeks. His top target, Ja'Marr Chase, has been red-hot as well in recent weeks, culminating in his best game of the season against the Steelers with 231 receiving yards on 11 catches. Chase Brown led the team in rushing with 44 yards on 10 carries while adding 39 yards and a pair of touchdowns on eight receptions. Dohnte Meyers and Drew Sample accounted for Burrow's other two touchdowns.

Cincinnati's free agent additions continue to show up on defense as both Boye Mafe and Jonathan Allen had sacks against Pittsburgh, bringing the pair's combined sack total to 10.5 on the season.

The Steelers fell to 6-3 and second place in the division with their 38-18 loss to the Bengals.

Pittsburgh found themselves in a 31-3 hole to start the fourth quarter. It was only then that Aaron Rodgers and the offense found some rhythm with consecutive touchdown drives. Rodgers finished the game with 15 completions on 29 attempts for 143 yards and a pair of touchdown passes — one to DK Metcalf, who led the team with 54 receiving yards on two catches, and the other to Pat Freiermuth. Jaylen Warren struggled to get going on the ground, rushing seven times for just 15 yards. Instead, it was fourth-year running back Lew Nichols who led the team with 43 rushing yards on six carries.

The Steelers' defense had no answers for Burrow and Cincinnati's passing attack. Patrick Queen had the defense's lone sack of the night while Payton Wilson racked up 19 total tackles.

After starting the game down 17-0, the Browns rallied to defeat the Houston Texans, 27-24, to improve to 6-4.

Deshaun Watson returned to the field after missing Week 9, completing 25 of 40 pass attempts for 312 yards and an interception while adding a team-high 49 yards and one touchdown on eight carries. This was Watson's sixth fourth-quarter comeback this season. Harold Fannin Jr. continues to look like a rising star at the tight end position as he led the team with 91 receiving yards on seven catches.

As usual, Carson Schwesinger led Cleveland's defense with 13 total tackles. Isaiah McGuire had the only sack on C.J. Stroud while Tyson Campbell came away with an interception in the second quarter.

The Ravens suffered their fifth straight loss in Week 10, falling 23-20 to the previously winless Los Angeles Chargers.

Veteran journeyman Jacoby Brissett made his debut at quarterback for Baltimore after just 10 days with the team. Brissett threw the ball 49 times, completing 33 passes for 340 yards and an interception. Mark Andrews seemed to have an immediate connection with his fourth quarterback of the season, leading the team with 82 receiving yards on eight catches — both season highs. While the passing attack found life for the first time in weeks, the run game was the complete opposite. Derrick Henry was repeatedly hit behind the line of scrimmage, finishing the game with just 10 yards on 10 carries, though he did find the end zone twice.

The Ravens' defense continues to be a disappointment under Jesse Minter. Omarion Hampton had his best game of the season, crossing 100 rushing yards for the first time in a game this year. This was also the first game that Justin Herbert did not throw an interception all season. Mike Green was a bright spot for the unit at least, with two sacks on Herbert and several strong plays against the run.
