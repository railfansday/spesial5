Raiders’ Wild Card hopes are still in play after Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Las Vegas Raiders lost their seventh game in eight tries, falling 38-24 to the Denver Broncos in Week 11. However, the Raiders are still alive in the race for the third AFC Wild Card spot or seventh seed in the division, especially with the Pittsburgh Steelers losing and entering the conversation. With that in mind, let's take a look at the AFC Playoff Picture.

[Editor's note: The standings below are before the Cincinnati Bengals-Washington Commanders game on Monday Night Football. Silver and Black Pride will update when that game goes final.]

2. Denver Broncos (8-2, 4-0 vs KC common opponents)

5. Kansas City Chiefs (8-2, 3-1 vs DEN common opponents)

10. Las Vegas Raiders (4-7, 3-4 vs. AFC for tiebreaker over BAL)

In the AFC West, the tiebreaker is the record versus common opponents now that Kansas City and Denver have the same division record. The Broncos are 4-0 against the Raiders, Chargers, Seahawks, and Cardinals. The Chiefs are 3-1 against the same opponents.

The Browns were on a bye and managed to move up one spot with the Steelers' loss, owning the tiebreaker over Pittsburgh. That sets up a massive battle in Cleveland on Sunday Night Football, as a loss would pretty much eliminate Las Vegas. Meanwhile, the Steelers have lost back-to-back games and will host the Broncos next week on Black Friday.

New England was also off this weekend, giving the Patriots some extra time to figure out their quarterback situation now that Drake Maye is expected to miss six weeks with a broken fibula. Still, the Pats have a winnable game in Los Angeles against the Chargers next week. Then there's the Texans, who have won four out of their last six games and host the sliding Ravens on Sunday.
