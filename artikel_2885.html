In first start, Keisean Henderson rallies No. 24 UH to walk-off win over West Virginia

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

MORGANTOWN, W.Va. - Jonathan Dimas made a 23-yard field goal as time expired as No. 20 Houston rallied from a 10-point deficit in the second half to beat West Virginia 34-31 in quarterback Keisean Henderson's first career start.

Henderson, a true freshman who was the nation's No. 1 recruit in 2026, was forced to start in place of Conner Weigman. Weigman was ruled out about an hour before kickoff with a shoulder injury.

Team officials originally thought Weigman would be able to play but he "did not feel right" during pregame warmups, according to the TNT broadcast.

On the third play of the game, Henderson found Amare Thomas wide-open near midfield for a 68-yard catch-and-run touchdown.

Makhi Hughes rushed for 158 yards and touchdown runs of 21, 2 and 3 yards

Henderson was 18-of-28 for 258 yards and a touchdown.

Before Saturday, Henderson had been limited to 17 snaps this season. His most extensive work was in a 77-6 win against Southern in Week 2.

Henderson marched the Cougars down the field for a 13-play, 86-yard drive that was capped by Hughes' third touchdown, a 3-yard run to tie the game at 31-31.
