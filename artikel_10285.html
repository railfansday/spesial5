Android Auto 18.2 update starts rolling out with more light mode teases

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Google has started rolling out the Android Auto 18.2 update, but don’t expect much to change on your car’s display.

Heading first to beta users, Android Auto 18.2 has started rolling out. This comes a little over a week after Android 18.1 hit the stable release track.

As Android Auto users should be very used to at this point, the update itself doesn’t bring any new user-facing changes that we can see. That’s not to say nothing is changing. There’s an endless list of bugs Google can fix. Just a few recent examples include Gemini bugs, foldable headaches, strange UI issues, connection problems, double-skipping songs, and more. Bug fixes are the primary point of these smaller Android Auto app updates.

Notably, this update also doesn’t fully cut off users on older Android versions yet. Google has confirmed Android 11 will be the new minimum requirement, but Android Auto 18.2 still supports as far back as Android 9.
