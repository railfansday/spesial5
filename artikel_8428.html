We tend not to like individual shares here, for precisely this reason. There are specific factors with each company and stock, and even the professional investors get it wrong - let alone us who have other day jobs.

Preferred shares are a highly specialised entity. In some ways like stocks, and in others like bonds. Upside is somewhat capped, and the prices tend to move in the opposite direction of interest rates. And if the company gets into financial trouble, it will pass on even preferred dividends.
