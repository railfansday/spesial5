Apple TV unveils major new sci-fi series that’s coming soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Apple TV (only $6.99/month for Black Friday) is about to debut its next major sci-fi series: Neuromancer. A recent behind-the-scenes piece reveals new details on what could be the streamer’s next Severance or Pluribus-sized hit.

Apple TV is home to many beloved sci-fi series, most notably Severance and Pluribus. While we await new seasons of those hits, Apple is about to debut its next big sci-fi show: Neuromancer.

Neuromancer debuts Friday, January 22 with two episodes, followed by weekly releases through March 19.

The series was first teased this July, when Apple debuted the trailer at San Diego Comic-Con. It’s rare for the streamer to hype new shows so far in advance, but Neuromancer was an exception.

After months of minimal news on Neuromancer, a recent WIRED story unveiled what to expect via interviews with series creators Graham Roland and JD Dillard and lead actors Callum Turner and Briana Middleton.

Callum Turner teases: “The world will be taken on a wild ride. It’s an epic. It’s Blade Runner; it’s 2001: A Space Odyssey; it’s The Fifth Element.”
