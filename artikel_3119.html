Star Equity Holdings, Inc. (STRR +11.98%) reported third quarter results that reflected the impact of its 2025 merger and subsequent transition into a diversified holding company. Management reported that headline revenue and gross profit grew due to the inclusion of new operating divisions, though underlying market conditions in construction and professional talent recruitment remained soft. The company is actively executing a capital allocation strategy that includes share repurchases, bolt-on acquisitions in the Energy Services and Business Services segments, and the monetization of non-operating real estate assets to generate gross proceeds exceeding $9 million. Strategic focus remains on realizing merger synergies, which have already exceeded initial expectations, and leveraging a significant $215 million tax asset to enhance future after-tax returns.
