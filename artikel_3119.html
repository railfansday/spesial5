Hut 8 (HUT) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

AI campus pivot gains 949 MW of contracted capacity, backed by new credit line.

Hut 8 Corp. (HUT -23.23%) reported a transition toward becoming a power-first energy infrastructure platform focused on the delivery of gigawatt-scale AI data center campuses. Management stated that the company has secured 949 MW of IT capacity under long-term leases, representing approximately $27 billion in contracted revenue. The financial results included a $413.8 million gain on digital assets and the planned runoff of non-core legacy business lines. The company expanded its capital management strategy by increasing its share repurchase authorization to $1 billion and closing a $1.07 billion revolving credit facility to support corporate liquidity without diluting existing shareholders.

AI is delivered as software, but it is produced by physical infrastructure, power, land, transmission, buildings, cooling, equipment, and the people who operate them. The next generation of compute will be constrained not only by chips, but also by that infrastructure. Of everything that infrastructure requires, power is a scarcest input. That is why we are a power-first company. The input that is hardest to secure is the one we secure first.

We developed the discipline of building within that constraint in Bitcoin mining, which required us to operate power-dense compute to manage cooling and uptime, and to procure and build quickly. AI is the largest opportunity we have yet encountered to apply that same discipline. Securing power, however, only earns the right to build.
