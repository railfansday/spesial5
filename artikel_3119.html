Was that a turning point? Man Utd's thrilling comeback victory over Roma in the Champions League on Tuesday certainly gave the club a much-needed lift, but Michael Carrick himself admitted afterwards that one game counts for little. The manager remains under pressure, and a defeat by Aston Villa on Sunday could spell the end of his tenure.

On the other hand, a Man Utd victory would potentially earn Carrick a stay of execution. He remains popular with matchgoing fans, and the team's problems are far from his doing alone. On Sunday he will be up against a master of the trade in Unai Emery, who is set to sign a new deal with Aston Villa after attracting interest from Man Utd.

Villa lost 3-0 to Barcelona in midweek, but they are on a five-game unbeaten run in the Premier League. However, their recent record at Old Trafford is poor: Man Utd have won five home matches in a row against their upcoming opponents.
