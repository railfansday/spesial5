Xometry: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Its Siemens Designcenter integration puts Xometry's pricing and manufacturability tools inside design workflows, with initial testing set for early 2027.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 3, 2026, Xometry (XMTR +16.55%) announced its first quarter of profitability under generally accepted accounting principles, or GAAP, alongside reaching a $1 billion annual revenue run rate. Sustained adoption of the company's artificial intelligence marketplace demonstrates how digital platforms are capturing market share from traditional offline custom manufacturing. Three things management said stand out.

Marketplace revenue expanded 43% year over year to $238 million while gross margin reached 36%. Operational efficiency drove adjusted EBITDA to $21.4 million.

Accounts spending at least $50,000 annually expanded 26% year over year to 2,172 customers. Automated matching between purchase orders and invoices embeds the marketplace deeper into enterprise workflows.

Embedding core artificial intelligence tools directly into Siemens Designcenter software connects design workflows with production capacity. Management expects initial testing in the first quarter of 2027 to generate new customer adoption.

Industrial buyers are shifting procurement online, and Xometry is capturing expanding volume across enterprise accounts. Network effects from over 5,000 active suppliers and deep e-procurement integrations establish high switching costs while management tracks pricing discipline and account expansion.

Less than 1% of the $275 billion custom manufacturing total addressable market currently runs through digital channels. The long-term thesis relies on converting legacy offline transactions into automated online orders.
