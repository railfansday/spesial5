Dev Material Archive

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A new resource resurrecting lost web-dev articles and posts:

This is an archive of written articles about web development, web design, and web accessibility that once existed on the World Wide Web and which would otherwise be lost to time. Publications close, SEO strategies change, rights lapse, standards alter, content management systems are migrated between, acquisitions happen, websites get redesigned.

Whatever the reason may be, so much high-quality web writing has been lost through the years.

I love the effort, which is ongoing. Link rot is a real thing. It’s links that point to either removed content or a page that’s changed URLs. Either way, it’s an awkward topic in our field. No one is obligated to archive and retain content, but doing so clearly benefits us all.

A big thanks to this site for helping preserve the things we care about. The same can said about Jay Hoffman and his long-running work on The History of the Web.

Andy Clarke shows you how to create designs that people recognize and remember in his brand-new e-book, Standout Designs for the Web. Order it today!

Great picks! FYI, dxsrc (formerly Frontend Dogma) makes browsing most and eventually all of these sites a bit easier: https://dxsrc.org/topics/ (I personally have spent days going through archives like these to make sure the material lives on).

They aren’t directly navigable but you will stumble upon them in the https://dxsrc.org/topics/2000s/ and https://dxsrc.org/topics/2010s/.

I'd love the original publication date beside the archive date on each piece. Coming back to an old browser workaround is much more interesting when you can see which constraints the author was working around.
