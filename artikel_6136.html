Between shopping for gifts, planning holiday getaways and squeezing in activities with family, the last few months of the year can get expensive.

Fortunately, the holidays are also a great time to put the benefits and statement credits on my to work.

The Amex Platinum comes with a long list of benefits, but its $895 annual fee (see rates and fees) means I try and get as much value from the card as possible. And while I use certain benefits throughout the year, the holiday season gives me even more opportunities to take advantage of credits I might not otherwise use.

From knocking out some Christmas shopping to enjoying a nice dinner, here's how I make the most of my Amex Platinum credits before the year ends.

Apply for the : Find out your offer and see if you are eligible for as high as 175,000 bonus points after spending $12,000 on purchases in the first six months of card membership. Welcome offers vary, and you may not be eligible for an offer.

The up to $300 annual Lululemon statement credit comes in especially handy during the holiday shopping season. The credit is divided into four quarterly credits of up to $75 each. (Enrollment is required, only applies to purchases at U.S. stores and online, excluding outlets).

Lululemon offers some of its biggest discounts during Black Friday and Cyber Monday, making the holidays a great time to stretch that $75 credit even further (almost as far as my favorite Lululemon Align leggings). You can even get a head start on holiday gifts — assuming you don't accidentally end up shopping for yourself instead.

I also have a little shopping trick to share. If I find something I want but I'm worried it'll sell out before the sales start, I can buy it ahead of time and keep an eye on the price. If it drops, I'll reach out to Lululemon's customer service to request a price adjustment.

Lululemon has a great price drop guarantee policy and will issue a gift card for the difference. Just make sure to pay attention to the details since price adjustments aren't guaranteed and Black Friday deals are often excluded. Still, it never hurts to ask.

One of my favorite things about the holidays is having more opportunities to spend time with friends and family — and you better believe those get-togethers almost always involve food. My Resy credit comes in handy for these occasions.

Resy is a restaurant reservation platform featuring participating restaurants across the U.S., with select Tock restaurants also eligible for the credit. Cardmembers receive up to $400 in annual Resy statement credits, divided into quarterly credits of up to $100 each. Enrollment is required. With the final quarterly credit available through Dec. 31, the timing works out rather conveniently for holiday get-togethers.

You don't need to make a reservation through Resy to use the credit, either. As long as you pay with your enrolled Platinum Card at an eligible restaurant, the purchase qualifies for the statement credit.

The offers up to $600 in annual lodging statement credits, divided into two semiannual credits of up to $300 each. The credit applies to eligible prepaid Fine Hotels + Resorts®, The Hotel Collection and Airbnb Luxe bookings* through American Express Travel® or the Amex Travel App™.

*The Hotel Collection and Airbnb Luxe stays require a two-night minimum. Beginning Jan. 1, 2027, Fine Hotels + Resorts will require stays of two nights or more to be eligible for the lodging statement credit.

Fine Hotels + Resorts bookings also come with additional perks, including daily breakfast for two, a $100 property credit toward eligible charges and guaranteed 4 p.m. checkout. Room upgrades and early check-in are also available (subject to availability).

Each year, I use this credit a little differently. In years when we decide to travel for the holidays, I put it toward our accommodations. But for the times we stay home, I use the credit to book something for the following year.

That's one of my favorite things about this beneift: You don't have to complete your stay before Dec. 31 to use the credit. As long as I make an eligible prepaid booking before the end of the year, I can put my remaining 2026 credit toward a trip in 2027.

Of course, I still compare the rate through Amex Travel with other booking options to make sure the credit represents worthwhile savings.

Cardmembers receive up to $200 in Uber Cash annually, with up to $15 available each month and an additional up to $20 bonus in December. That's up to $35 to put toward eligible Uber rides or Uber Eats orders in the U.S. To receive the benefit, you must add your to your Uber account and pay with an American Express card.

If you know anything about Utah's soda culture, it probably won't surprise you that, as a local, some of (or, if I'm being honest, most of) my Uber Cash goes toward my dirty soda habit. And since I'm happy to pick up my own drinks, I can skip the delivery fees and put more of my credit toward the actual order.

These days, Uber Eats is for much more than takeout. You can even order groceries from participating stores and have them delivered just a few hours later. Some stores also let you link your loyalty account to take advantage of member discounts and coupons.

That can be particularly useful if I realize I'm missing an ingredient for a holiday meal or need something at the last minute.

The monthly Uber Cash expires at the end of each month, so make sure to use the extra December allotment before it's gone.

The offers up to $200 in annual airline fee statement credits. The credit covers eligible incidental charges, such as checked baggage fees and certain onboard purchases, with one selected qualifying U.S. airline each year. Enrollment is required, and the credit doesn't cover airfare itself.

Before heading out for the holidays, I always check how much of my credit I have left. According to TPG's guide to airline baggage fees, most major U.S. airlines now charge around $45 to $50 each way for a first checked bag. So if I'm bringing extra luggage for gifts, that could easily cost $100 or more on a round trip.

Since the credit resets each calendar year, I try to use any remaining balance on eligible expenses before Dec. 31.

Before tackling my holiday shopping list, I always check my available Amex Offers to see if I can save a little extra.

Amex Offers provide opportunities to earn bonus Membership Rewards points or receive statement credits on eligible purchases at select retailers. Offers vary by cardmember, and they need to be added to your  before making a qualifying purchase.

During the holidays, I pay particular attention to offers from retailers where I'm already planning to shop. Sometimes I can even stack an Amex Offer with a holiday sale or promotional discount for additional savings.

I also use Cashback Monitor to compare shopping portal rates and see where I can earn the most cash back or bonus points. When everything lines up, I can stack a holiday sale, an Amex Offer and shopping portal rewards on the same purchase.

Make sure you check your offers before making a purchase, not after. There's nothing more frustrating than discovering you could have saved money on something you've already bought.
