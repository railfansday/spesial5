Kim Kardashian aims to retake bar exam

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Reality TV star Kim Kardashian speaks onstage during the 2020 Winter TCA Tour on Jan. 18, 2020, in Pasadena, California. (Photo by David Livingston/Getty Images)

Reality TV star Kim Kardashian still holds ambitions to become a lawyer and plans to retake the bar exam—someday.

Kardashian, who failed the California bar exam in 2025, said she is “always thinking about the bar” and is “still all in,” she told People on Monday. “I’m going to take my time and wait until the moment is right, when I have some free time to focus on it.”

Kardashian, who plays a lawyer on Hulu’s legal drama All’s Fair, was among the 7,362 candidates who completed the July 2025 administration of the California bar, according to the State Bar of California.

The state’s test is considered one of the most difficult in the country, and only 54.8% passed last year and 48.3% in July, according to the state bar.

Kardashian did not attend law school and does not have an undergraduate degree. She has followed the state’s alternative pathway that allows candidates to take the bar exam by learning the law through apprenticeship and study.

California is one of the few states that allow lawyers to become lawyers by “reading the law.”
