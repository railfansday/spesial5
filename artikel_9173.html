Hasselblad X2D II Photographers Can Now Shoot Tethered Into Capture One

Capture One and Hasselblad have jointly announced that real-time, wired tethered shooting for the X2D II 100C is now supported in Capture One with the latest versions of the camera’s firmware and Capture One software.

Tethered shooting support was announced as in the works back in July when Hasselblad first revealed its partnership with Capture One.

“There is a well-documented history between Hasselblad and Phase One, and we’re not here to dwell on it. Capture One has operated as an independent company since 2020, and what matters to us is serving photographers,” Capture One’s CEO Rafael Orta told PetaPixel last summer. “And they have asked us for this for years — on forums, in feature requests, in conversations. That’s why we’ve been working for a while with the Hasselblad team to deliver their shooters a native Capture One workflow.”

Now, not only can Hasselblad photographers edit their X2D II RAW files inside of Capture One, the full studio workflow is supported through tethering. Each image will appear on screen at full resolution at nearly the same moment it is captured when connected with a USB tethering cable. Hasselblad says that photographers can check focus and exposure while the set is still live and nobody has moved.

“[That] means no waiting around or breaking the flow. The full Capture One toolset is available from the first shot, so the grades and masks photographers apply to the first frame carry over to all subsequent shots,” Hasselblad says.

Studio photographers don’t need any convincing as to the value of tethering, but those who are less familiar might not know that the capability is hugely important for commercial sets where clients are on hand. It’s far easier to set clients at a digital tech workstation watching a monitor as results come in rather than hovering over a photographer’s shoulder trying to get a look at the rear LCD.

“This means creative direction and collaboration happen in real time, turning every session into a truly collaborative shoot,” the two companies add.

“We are always listening to our photographers, and tethering support for Hasselblad cameras was one of the top requests when our collaboration with Capture One was announced earlier this year,” Bronius Rudnickas, Global Marketing Manager at Hasselblad, says. “We are delighted that this workflow option is now available, giving Hasselblad photographers and creatives worldwide greater flexibility in their creative journeys.”

Wired tethering for the Hasselblad X2D II 100C is available starting today in Capture One 16.9.1 for desktop and Mobile 4.3.0, requiring X2D II 100C firmware version 1.4.15.7.
