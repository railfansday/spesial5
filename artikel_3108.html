Where Will VOO Be in 30 Years? Here's What History Says.

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It's impressive what the long-term power of compounding can produce.

By David Dierking – Nov 20, 2026 at 8:30AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Vanguard S&P 500 ETF ›

The Vanguard S&P 500 ETF (VOO -0.49%) has been one of the simplest and cheapest ways for investors to build long-term wealth. By investing in the S&P 500, you're investing in practically the entire U.S. economic growth story.

Historically, the S&P 500 has delivered an average annual return of around 10%, assuming that dividends are reinvested. That doesn't guarantee similar results in the future, but it does give us a useful benchmark for estimating where the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF could be in 30 years.

Let's assume you invest $10,000 in the Vanguard S&P 500 ETF today, reinvest all the dividends you get along the way, and leave it untouched for 30 years.

If it can match its historical 10% annualized return, that initial investment would grow to approximately $174,500, more than 17 times your starting amount without contributing another dollar over the subsequent three decades.

Even at a more conservative 8% annual return assumption, that same investment would grow to roughly $100,600. It's a testament to the power of long-term compounding growth.

Of course, the market won't deliver a consistent 10% return every year. There will be recessions, bear markets, and significant drawdowns along the way that'll test your discipline. But if you're able to ride these periods out and even keep contributing to your investments over time, the rewards will very likely be substantial.
