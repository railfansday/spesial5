Stock Market Today, Nov. 6: Iren Soars 20% on Strong AI Cloud Revenue Growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Today, Nov. 6, 2026, investors are weighing whether customer prepayments and contract wins can sustain the AI cloud momentum.

Iren (IREN +20.02%), a vertically integrated AI cloud infrastructure provider, closed at $21.64, up 20.02%. The stock rose after fiscal first-quarter results beat expectations and AI cloud revenue nearly tripled. Investors are watching whether customer prepayments and contract growth support the higher AI cloud target.Trading volume reached 110.5 million shares, coming in about 128% above its three-month average of 48.5 million shares. Iren IPO'd in 2021 and has fallen 11% since going public.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC +0.31%) rose 0.31% to 7,377, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +1.09%) gained 1.09% to 24,579.
