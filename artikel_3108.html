Apple said the update fixes issues that could expose sensitive data.

Apple released iOS 27.2.1 on Monday, the same day the company released the first public beta for the upcoming iOS 27.3. The latest update fixes nine issues on your iPhone, including a couple that could expose your data to malicious actors.

You can download iOS 27.2.1 now by going to Settings > General > Software Update > Update Now and following the prompts on your screen.

Apple says the update provides bug fixes and security updates for issues on your iPhone, two of which may lead to memory corruption on your device.

Memory corruption happens when a program on your device incorrectly reads, writes or frees up memory. This can cause the program to crash, or it could expose sensitive data to others.

You should update your iPhone as soon as possible. Apple didn’t say that these issues were being actively exploited. However, it’s important to keep the software on your device up to date so you’re better protected against potential security threats.
