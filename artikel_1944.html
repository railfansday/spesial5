Noni Madueke is a player transformed: one of his stats for Arsenal vs Hull is startling

Noni Madueke enjoyed a fantastic performance as Arsenal narrowly beat Hull City 1-0 on Saturday.

The 24-year-old would ultimately prove key to the all-important breakthrough, which came when his shot was saved into the path of Martin Odegaard, who made no mistake.

The Gunners have begun to forge a gap on the top of the Premier League table, winning nine of their opening 10 league fixtures.

Madueke is likely enjoying the best form of his Arsenal career so far, having regularly impressed over the last month.

The winger, who was a £52million signing from Chelsea in 2025, has often failed to show why Mikel Arteta was so keen to bring him into the squad.

Throughout the match on Saturday, however, the England international's assets were clear for all to see.

Noni Madueke with a reminder to the rest of the Premier League 🔴🏆 pic.twitter.com/w7QYkP5qGk

From the first whistle, Madueke looked to create danger in and around the Hull box.

The winger gave his defender no respite, running at him time and time again, often beating him in the process.

He came close to scoring on several occasions, and really should have added to the score line when he went through on goal in the closing stages.

Regardless, Madueke remained Arsenal's driving force throughout. He had 13 touches in the opposition box - five more than anybody else.

He created four chances, took four shots - the joint-most - and delivered two accurate crosses.

Perhaps most impressive was the forward's dribbling. He made five successful dribbles throughout the game, at least three more than anyone else.

He was fouled twice, making four recoveries and winning seven of the 10 duels he contested.

Madueke never stopped getting up and down the pitch. His astonishing 21 sprints was at least seven more than any other player, and it would have made Arteta very happy indeed.

Arsenal's attacking signings under Arteta have been called into question on several occasions, and Madueke is often used as a stick to beat him with.

Yet the 24-year-old has looked like an entirely different player in recent weeks, and is proving that he has the ability.

"He's a great example for the rest of the group because before the first international break he wasn't playing," Arteta said after Saturday's game.

"And what he did is, instead of going on holidays, he went to Portugal and got somebody to train with him and got in better shape and condition and immediately he got back into the rhythm.

"You can see now he is full of confidence and trying things and getting past players and he's impacting games with goals and assists and we are very happy with that."
