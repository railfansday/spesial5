Daniel Stendel's latest admission after Barnsley FC loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Barnsley were the architects of their own downfall yet again as Jensen Weir fired a winning goal on 75 minutes for a Latics side who moved out of the relegation zone with victory.

The Oakwell outfit, meanwhile, reside in 23rd place and are only kept off bottom spot on goal difference ahead of Friday's home derby with Sheffield Wednesday.

Stendel said: "At the moment, the situation is really, really difficult.

"I'm really ready to work hard every day with this team. I do everything I can do every day in the league, but I can understand if you ask why, what (regarding own position). It's the one and only solution for the situation.

"At the moment, we have to concentrate on the games we have until January to get so many points that we can get. At the moment, the situation is really, really difficult. "Everybody that we signed says they are ready to play this level and they have to learn.

"It's different to maybe other teams, we have to learn so much quicker, because there's no-one else who can take responsibility. And this is a big task for the young guys and you can see (at Wigan) it was a big task.

"The one and only thing we can do is stand up. The derby is a big game, not only in our situation, but also for the club and for the supporters. It's a big opportunity for us to give a little bit back from what we got from our supporters."

Top-scorer and captain Cameron McGeehan picked up his fifth league booking of the campaign at the Brick Community Stadium.
