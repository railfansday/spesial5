Huddersfield Town's Marcus Harness on his mixed autumn - and players reconnecting with fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The midfielder paid the price for a low-key performance in the Terriers' 2-1 reverse at Bromley on September 19 - the League One leaders' only reverse at league level to date.

He found himself dropped to the bench for the game at former club Burton Albion at the start of October and then made a fateful impact when coming on late on in the derby with Sheffield Wednesday, giving away a costly stoppage-time penalty.

After biding his time, the Midlander has impressed on his return. As he did in the opening month.

Harness said: "I've been around long enough to know it's up and down and you've just got to keep trying to apply yourself in the best way that you can. It will work out in the end. It always comes around."

After starting last season in eye-catching fashion, Town fell away by mid-autumn. This time around, they are showing more stamina and aptitude.

They head into Saturday's home encounter with fellow form side Leicester City on the back of a six-match unbeaten sequence with fans reconnecting with the club again.

Harness, previously promoted from the third tier with Ipswich and Burton, added: "You're only as good as your last game really.

"So we'd like to keep it going as long as we can and hopefully we can keep the good feeling going.

"We're going to get a set-back at some point and just hope that everyone can stay with us and see what we're working towards and what we're trying to do.

"We're only going to be better for having the fans with us. Long may it continue, we're enjoying it.

"We're not naive enough to think we're just going to go unbeaten forever. It's just the way we go about it."
