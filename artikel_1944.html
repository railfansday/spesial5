Is Wrexham v Middlesbrough FC on TV? Channel and streaming details for Championship clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Middlesbrough are back on the road this evening (November 4) with a trip to Wrexham in the calendar.

Kim Hellberg’s men remained on track at the weekend, breezing past Charlton Athletic with a 3-0 victory. Sebastian Berhalter fired Boro ahead at the Riverside before the free-scoring Will Lankshear extended the advantage with a brace.

Middlesbrough’s boss said: “We’re happy with the performance overall. I think we played good, but maybe not at our best.

“There were things we could have maybe done better, but at the end of the day I think 3-0 is a fair result and says a lot about this group and the quality they have.

“A lot of it is about being better every day and that’s what you can control. I’m not thinking about what will happen after 46 games in May as that will not help anyone. I want to finish first or second of course, but for us it’s all about being better.”

They will be hoping to extend their lead at the top of the Championship in Wales, where they will tackle a Wrexham side who lost to Sheffield United in their last outing.

Here are the key TV and streaming details ahead of Middlesbrough’s trip to Wrexham.

Kick-off at the Racecourse Ground is scheduled for 7:45pm tonight (November 4).

Yes - those without a ticket can enjoy the action at home. The fixture has not been given top billing on any of the main Sky Sports channels but will be available to view as an alternative broadcast via the Sky Sports+ service. Subscriptions can be purchased online.

Supporters can stream via the official Sky Sports app or on NOW TV with a valid subscription. There will also be live audio commentary on BBC Radio Tees.
