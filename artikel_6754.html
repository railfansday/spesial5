Where Are Sam Bankman-Fried and Caroline Ellison Now? Here’s What Happened Since the Events of ‘The Altruists’

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Is there potential for a You Can See Everything-ish update to The Altruists? Where are Sam Bankman-Fried and Caroline Ellison now? The Altruists ends with Bankman-Fried (Anthony Boyle) getting arrested just before getting to tell Ellison (Julia Garner) that he loves her over the phone. What happened next, in real life, is hopefully not all that sequel-worthy.

Following the cut to black, there are some updates, as based-on-a-true-story shows and movies often include just before the credits. If you turned Netflix off and didn’t read them, here’s what they said:

But still, that was years ago! Did The Altruists leave anything out? Here’s an update as to what else Bankman-Fried and Ellison have been doing.

Bankman-Fried began serving his penalty on December 12, 2022. He is imprisoned at Federal Correctional Institution, Lompoc I in California. Fun (?) fact: Changpeng Zhao aka CZ, Bankman-Fried’s former investor who also appears on The Altruists, served four months at the same prison in 2024.

Ellison was sentenced to 24 months in federal prison and released after 14 months at the beginning of 2026. She was held at Federal Correctional Institution, Danbury in Connecticut. That particular prison has a whole history of fun facts. It’s where Piper Kerman, the author of Orange Is the New Black, served time. That’s right, this prison is the “real” Litchfield. It has also appeared in popular culture multiple times and other infamous inmates IRL include Steve Bannon, Lauryn Hill, and Teresa Giudice.

Over the summer, Ellison was discreetly hired at Manifund, a nonprofit for matching donors with charity organizations. The company’s CEO, Austin Chen, pledged to give her a second chance, and praised Ellison’s “relatable” personal writing on his own Substack. He noted that FTX gave him seed money, and that he was one of the victims of Bankman-Fried and Ellison’s crimes. (And just flagging for work-life balance reasons that he also said she gets along great with his young daughter.)

“I understand that some of our users, donors, and partners may now be skittish about working with Manifund, given this announcement,” Chen concludes. “I appreciate any trust you are willing to extend, and ask that you judge us by our historical and future outputs. You (or your LLM) can take a look at Manifund’s source code, underlying data, finances and research; if you wish, you can see Caroline’s specific code commits and comments on Manifund.”

So that’s what she’s up to; potentially turning over a new leaf at a new company. Or potentially repeating the same problematic patterns with new colleagues. Surely someone so math-savvy would notice that before it happened. Right?
