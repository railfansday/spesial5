Opinion - They'll regret this: Chelsea give green light for Liverpool for signing

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Chelsea have given Liverpool the green light to go and complete a signing. The Blues will regret this.

Liverpool do desperately need to sign someone in midfield. Or at least, it felt that way not very long ago but Trey Nyoni’s form in the middle has been fantastic.

Fantastic enough to change the plans? We really, really expected Liverpool to enter the winter market for a midfielder just a few months ago, only for a 19-year-old with minimal experience to show the kind of ability you pay £60m for.

He’s even doing it away in the UEFA Champions League now. Nyoni is something special but we do wonder if Liverpool will feel safer with a more senior option in the squad alongside him.

Nyoni is still only 19, after all, and hasn’t yet had to deliver with anything particularly at stake. No one should be giving up on him but we’d also understand if Liverpool don’t want to put all of their eggs in one basket.

Which again brings about the question of potentially doing business in January. And there’s been a development there.

Liverpool's big problem with signing a midfielder has been Chelsea. The Blues also need a high-profile midfielder, having sold Enzo Fernandez at the transfer deadline without a replacement.

They did try to sign one, of course. Lamine Camara was a serious option and they did bid. AS Monaco ended the deal right at the death, however, as they felt a bigger one could be had in 2027 - Liverpool were supposedly the reason why, essentially.

Alex Scott is another heavily linked with Liverpool, especially as his former coach, Iraola, is in charge at Anfield. But Chelsea were supposed to be ahead in that race, going all-out for the Bournemouth star.

But David Ornstein reports that Chelsea aren't actually going for a midfielder right now, in what's a massive surprise to everyone. He told USA Sports that he's not aware of any January ideas from the club. Instead, it appears they're going to wait for the summer.

That leaves the door wide open for Liverpool, who could conceivably make a move for an elite midfielder before Chelsea even get to the table. Their biggest rival for competition isn't putting up a fight - and that's a huge advantage in any talks.

It stops the price rising too high, it stops players' heads getting turned, and it also takes away an immediate time pressure to strike a deal. Though, there is still the time pressure of getting it done in January.

But there's no question about it: Chelsea's stance is an advantage for Liverpool if the Reds are still happy to sign a midfield player.
