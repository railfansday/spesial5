A night of telling performances: Fan player ratings from AC Milan 0-2 Ferencvaros

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

After another disappointing midweek performance, AC Milan fans have left their ratings to the team’s performance against Ferencvaros.

The derby win was a chance for Milan to build some momentum before the international break and they failed to take that chance against Ferencvaros. From the first minute, they simply weren’t good enough and the 2-0 defeat was hardly a shock.

Of course, this meant our own ratings were pretty low – as you’d expect – with Lorenzo Torriani given the worst rating. However, he wasn’t exactly an outlier. It was a night of poor performances, unacceptable from the Rossoneri.

Taking a look at the ratings that you, our readers, provided via our player ratings tool, the consensus was very similar. The highest rating was a 5.5 from the bench, with no starter scoring above 4.7. Like we say, telling.

Filippo Terracciano was the standout performer of the night, scoring a horrible 3.0 rating, whilst Goncalo Ramos and Ardon Jashari’s performances weren’t viewed well either, with both scoring a 3.8.

It’s a performance that leaves more questions than answers, especially with key players failing to make a difference, too. Koni De Winter scored a 4.0, Yunus Musah scored a 4.1 and Adrien Rabiot got a 4.6, so even the ‘starters’ weren’t on it last night.

In total 138 votes were submitted, a drop from the weekend’s win – but this just shows the effect of another lacklustre performance…
