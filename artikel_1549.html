D’Amico confirms: “We’ll add a dynamic winger in January.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Roma sporting director confirmed that the Giallorossi will seek reinforcements in the January transfer window.

The Italian executive was asked about potential improvements to the current squad that is presently in third place in Serie A behind Lazio and Inter.

In an interview with DAZN, D’Amico hinted at a specific transfer request made by Gasperini.

“This is the problem we realized at the end of the transfer window. Namely that we need another forward.”

“We’re missing a dynamic winger who can counterattack. We’ll try to find him in January by evaluating the opportunities. We’ll try to give these characteristics to the manager.”
