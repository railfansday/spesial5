RB Leipzig and US youth international reportedly open to parting ways

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Two and a half years after joining RB Leipzig from Croatian outfit NK Kustošija for a reported €3 million fee, 19-year-old Dino Klapija looks set to move on.

According to Sky Germany, both parties are open to parting ways, with a move back to the United States potentially on the cards for the young striker, who has yet to make an appearance for RB Leipzig's first team.

Klapija has represented the United States at U18, U19 and U20 level. He has scored four goals in 10 appearances for the U20 side.

According to Transfermarkt, he has found the net 16 times in 69 appearances for RB Leipzig's U19 team.
