Republican congressman launches impeachment bid against Pentagon chief Hegseth

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Kentucky congressman Thomas Massie moved on Monday to force a vote in the House of Representatives to impeach Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth, putting pressure on his fellow Republicans to decide whether to remove one of the Trump administration’s most prominent figures following the party's crushing defeat in midterm elections.
