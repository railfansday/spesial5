Angelina Jolie and Brad Pitt settle legal battle over French winery

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have settled a long-running legal battle over her sale of a stake in their French vineyard, which unfolded amid a bitter divorce.

"We have settled out of court in the best interests of our family and will not be commenting further," said the Hollywood stars' representatives in a joint statement.

In the lawsuit, Pitt, 62, argued that Jolie, 51, acted without his permission in selling her share in the winery to a Russian oligarch. She counter-sued, accusing Pitt of drunkenly attacking her and their children on a flight, prompting her to file for divorce in 2016.

The settlement announced on Wednesday resolves both of their respective legal actions.

"Any comments in media stories attributed to 'sources' are in fact not coming from either of us, or anyone close to us," said the statement from their representatives.

Pitt's lawsuit had accused Jolie of seeking "to inflict harm" on him when she sold her stake in their co-owned vineyard, which he claimed had forced him to partner with "a stranger with poisonous associations and intentions".

The Fight Club star said he and Jolie had agreed not to sell their stakes in the venue without the permission of the other.

It was alleged that Jolie had sold her share to a Luxembourg-based spirits manufacturer controlled by Russian oligarch Yuri Shefler, without Pitt's knowledge.

Jolie hit back with her own lawsuit, accusing Pitt of grabbing her by the head and attacking two of their children during a 2016 private jet flight trip.

Pitt also verbally abused and poured alcohol on his family during the flight from France to Los Angeles, her lawsuit had alleged.

Her lawyers alleged Pitt began shouting, accusing her of being "too deferential" to their six children - aged eight to 15 at the time.

Pitt was not charged following a police investigation into the incident and he denied the allegations.

They reached a divorce settlement late last year after an eight-year legal battle.

It is understood that Pitt's litigation with Stoli Group, a beverage company owned by the Russian oligarch, is ongoing.

At the time of their 2014 marriage, Pitt and Jolie were among the most lucrative names in Hollywood.

They met on the set of the 2005 film Mr and Mrs Smith and were soon dubbed Brangelina by the press.

The marriage was Pitt's second after a relationship with Friends star Jennifer Aniston.

It was Jolie's third, after actors Billy Bob Thornton and Jonny Lee Miller.

Jolie adopted her first child, Maddox, from Cambodia in 2002 a few months after he was born. Pitt later adopted him after he and Jolie had begun their relationship.

In 2005, the pair adopted daughter Zahara from Ethiopia when she was a few months old.

The couple's first biological child, Shiloh, was born in Namibia in 2006.

Their son Pax was adopted at the age of three from Vietnam in 2007, and twins Knox and Vivienne were born in 2008.

Since the couple split, four of the six children have legally changed their last name to Jolie rather than Jolie-Pitt, according to court filings citied by Hollywood press.
