Should I get a more absorbent or a more breathable diaper to prevent rash?

Both. Absorbency keeps wetness off skin, and breathability lets heat and humidity escape. Look for a diaper that does both.

Is a fragrance-free or an extra-absorbent diaper better for preventing diaper rash?

You want both in one diaper. Fragrance-free cuts down on irritants, while absorbency handles the moisture behind a lot of rash.

Is it worth paying more for a rash-preventing diaper, and which one is best?

Not necessarily. Frequent changes and barrier cream often matter more than price. Pampers Swaddlers is our top pick, backed by a Pampers-sponsored study showing 50% fewer instances of severe redness, at a mainstream price.

What’s the best rash-preventing diaper for a preemie with delicate skin?

Preemie skin needs extra-gentle materials, frequent changes, and clinician guidance before you switch products. Pampers says its Swaddlers Preemie diapers were developed with NICU nurses, are fragrance-free and hypoallergenic, and come in sizes P3 to P1 for babies under about 6 pounds.

Is a wetness-indicator diaper or an extra-absorbent diaper better for preventing rash?

Absorbency is what actually helps prevent rash. A wetness indicator is just a helpful reminder that it’s time to change.
