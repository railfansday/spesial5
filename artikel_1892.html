It has now been revealed what each of the 20 Premier League clubs received from the 2025-26 TV cash payouts.

The information made public via the publication of the EPL’s official 2025-26 annual report.

This report including a breakdown of how much money each of last season’s 20 Premier League clubs banked.

In total, the 20 Premier League clubs received £3,142m for the 2025-26 season.

This is a 10.9% increase on the 2024-25 season.

Banking the most were Arsenal with £198.1m, whilst Wolves received the least with £117.2m.

The Premier League is actually one of the very fairest major European leagues in terms of the gap between which clubs get the very most and very least. Arsenal getting 1.69 x what Wolves received.

This is the breakdown of the total distributions of £3,142m between the 20 Premier League clubs (in order of placings in the Premier League table for the 2025-26 season):

£198.1m Arsenal £192.7m Man City £191.3m Man U £182.1m Aston Villa £181.6m Liverpool £170.2m Bournemouth £167.7m Sunderland £161.7m Brighton £160.8m Brentford £163.4m Chelsea £154.5m Fulham £153.7m Newcastle United £147.1m Everton £144.2m Leeds £136.4m Palace £135.9 Forest £136.6m Spurs £127.9m West Ham £118.6m Burnley £117.2m Wolves

As you can see, some clubs got more than one or more of those that finished above them in the table. This is due to how many times the relative clubs were chosen for live UK coverage, this is the ‘Facility Fees’ portion of the cash.

The Newcastle United total of £153.7m is broken down as:

UNITED KINGDOM (VIA THE TV DEALS ETC.) Equal Share £31.9m Facility Fees £20.3m Merit £15.4m

OVERSEAS (VIA THE TV DEALS ETC.) Equal Share £56.5m Merit £18.4m

Newcastle United received £6.5m less than 2024-25 as they fell eight places in the Premier League table. However, it would have been a far bigger drop without the overall 10.9% increase in the entire share out and the fact that NUFC were selected 31 times for UK TV coverage across 2025-26.
