Gallery: ‘Cardinale calls Endrick’, ‘Amorim fires up the team’ – Today’s headlines in Italy

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

AC Milan return to Serie A action this evening as they welcome Frosinone to San Siro, hoping to build off the Genoa win before the break.

Wins for Como, Lazio and Roma on Saturday have added pressure for Milan to keep pace with the other contenders around and above them, and it will not be an easy game against a newly-promoted Frosinone side who have started the season very well.

Below is how the newspapers in Italy look this morning, with a couple of the front pages referencing the game tonight and also Gerry Cardinale’s words yesterday.

La Gazzetta dello Sport front page: “Cardinale calls Endrick: ‘The Brazilian is top-class, and Rabiot will renew’. 18:00 today: Milan vs. Frosinone. Como overtake: they’re up to 4th.”

La Gazzetta dello Sport inside: “Cardinale already looking to the transfer market: ‘Milan will spend. Endrick is top-class, and Rabiot will renew’. The owner spoke about the future at Milanello yesterday: ‘We’re ready to seize opportunities in January’.”

“Amorim fires up the team. He wants goals from Ramos against Frosinone. The current league position and the team’s recent performances aren’t enough for the coach: he demands more from his players.”

“Cagliari interested in Camarda. An option for Mendy.”

QS: “Milan, Cardinale fires up the team. The mission for Europe resumes against Frosinone.”
