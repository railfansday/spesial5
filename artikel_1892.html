EFL Trophy: Draw details for Bradford City, Huddersfield Town, Doncaster Rovers and Sheffield Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Bantams and Town won northern groups C and H respectively, while the Owls finished in second place after a 4-0 home loss to Accrington Stanley in their final group A game recently.

Rovers finished in runners-up spot to the Terriers in group H.

The group stage phase of the competition concludes next Tuesday, with four fixtures scheduled.

Sky Sports presenter Teddy Draper will host the draw alongside former Crystal Palace striker Clinton Morrison and ex-Huddersfield Town defender Tommy Smith.

Ball numbers for the draw will be finalised next Wednesday morning and published on EFL channels. Round of 32 ties are to take place in the week commencing December 7.

Jersey Mike’s Trophy fixtures remain regionalised until the semi-final stage.
