Louis Saha says Man Utd should have signed £65m former Chelsea star

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Louis Saha believes Manchester United should have signed Nicolas Jackson before his £65million move from Chelsea to Aston Villa.

Jackson enjoyed a fast start to life at Chelsea, scoring 17 goals in all competitions during his first season at Stamford Bridge.

But the Senegalese striker failed to hit those heights during the 2024-25 campaign and Chelsea sent him on loan to Bayern Munich last season.

The performances of Joao Pedro complicated Jackson’s future upon his return from his loan in Germany, and a sale to Villa was eventually sanctioned, reuniting the 25-year-old with his former Villarreal boss Unai Emery.

Back under Emery’s tutelage, Jackson has returned to his best, with nine goals in 14 appearances so far this season.

And Saha believes United missed a trick by not trying to sign Jackson over the summer, particularly given Benjamin Sesko’s injury woes, which have seen him miss large swathes of this campaign.

‘Nicolas Jackson is a player that I think Man Utd really missed out on because he’s an experienced striker who plays well under pressure,’ Saha told CasinoHawks.

‘Of course, he misses chances sometimes, but his work rate and ability to keep opposing centre-backs busy are vital for team balance.

‘Aston Villa have done really well with those two players, I would’ve loved them at Old Trafford.’

Saha feels similarly about midfielder Leon Goretzka, who joined Villa on a free transfer from Bayern Munich this summer, with the Frenchman adding: ‘Leon Goretzka was a bargain for Aston Villa.

‘There’s a bit of jealousy seeing someone like Goretzka go to Aston Villa while Man Utd lose Casemiro. You definitely need experienced players in that central area.

‘Adapting to the Premier League isn’t always easy, but Villa went for proven quality. It’s frustrating, as those are the types of opportunities Man United should be capitalizing on.’
