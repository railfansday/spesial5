Pochettino says ‘give me time’ as he prepares for Man Utd venture

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Mauricio Pochettino defiantly stated, "You will see, give me time", before jetting off to begin his role as Manchester United head coach.

The Argentine rounded out his duties with the United States by beating Haiti 2-0 in the second leg of the CONCACAF Nations League quarter-finals to secure an 8-0 aggregate victory.

Following that fixture in Cincinnati, Pochettino flew straight to Manchester, then headed to the Red Devils' Carrington training complex.

United find themselves in the Premier League relegation zone after a tumultuous 2025-26 campaign thus far.

Club legend Michael Carrick was sacked after winning just one of 10 top-flight fixtures this season.

Former Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea coach Pochettino was hired in his place, but wanted to see out the Haiti double-header.

Following the game, he was told by former USA and Manchester City midfielder DaMarcus Beasley that, "Manchester is blue, it always be blue".

Pochettino replied to the quip by saying: "You will see. Give me time.

"The new challenge is a big challenge, but at the same time, it's adrenaline you want to feel. We need to add our common sense.

"We know the situation is not a situation that deserves to be a club like Manchester United, the fan base is amazing.

"The mentality is to learn from everyone. You need to have an open mind because I think the players can teach us every day, we are a much better coaching staff now.

"Football is about attitude, and it's about the approach. It's about connection, feelings and communication."

United later posted a video on social media of Pochettino arriving, in which he said: "I'm very excited to be here. It's an honour, it's a privilege, to be in this amazing club. I'm looking forward to starting work."

Pochettino's first assignment could scarcely be a more difficult one, with United travelling to Anfield to face fierce north-west rivals Liverpool on Sunday.

He will be the first United boss to see his maiden league game in charge come against Liverpool.

This is the latest into a league season (MD11) that Liverpool and United have faced with one of the sides in the relegation zone since November 1972, when bottom side Man Utd beat league-leaders Liverpool 2-0 at Old Trafford.
