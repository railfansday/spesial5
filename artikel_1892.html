Jair Cunha 'exposed' away from Nottingham Forest after incident vs Japan

Nottingham Forest defender Jair Cunha was given a harsh reality check as Brazil lost 3-2 to Japan on Saturday.

The centre-back had been riding high after scoring in last week's 3-1 win over Manchester City, but was hooked midway through the second half in Singapore.

Goals from Takefusa Kubo, Keita Nakamura and Tatsuhiro Sakamoto were enough to cancel out Vinicius Junior's brace, with a fiery game ending with Raphinha being sent off.

Cunha has enjoyed a rapid rise at Nottingham Forest over the last year, making his debut for the senior Brazil team in September.

The 21-year-old, who was signed from Botafogo last year, was playing for the Under-21s in January, before breaking into the team in the final weeks of the season.

But he has been constantly involved under Oliver Glasner this season, finding a home for himself on the right of the back-three.

Saturday's match will go down as one to forget for Cunha, as it will for much of the Brazil team.

Carlo Ancelotti has still not managed to find the right system for his side, who cannot seem to produce any consistent form against strong opposition.

Cunha was taken off after 58 minutes, after he had made several mistakes. He misplaced nine passes, completed just one long ball and played a role in Japan's opening goal.

He was not the only culprit - Gabriel had been caught up the pitch, allowing the ball to be played through to Kubo - but the Nottingham Forest man was slow to react.

He opted to run alongside the Japan forward, and was caught far too flat-footed when he did attempt to close him down, allowing the ball to be rolled by him and tucked into the bottom corner.

"He had good moments with crosses into the box and interceptions, but ended up exposed in the play that led to both Japanese goals in the first half. Kubo had time to dribble, run, and shoot for the first goal," wrote Ge Globo, awarding Cunha with a five out of 10.

Bol shared a similar sentiment, rating him a 4.5. They wrote: "He was exposed on Japan's first goal when he came out to cover after a mistake by his teammate Gabriel Magalhaes and couldn't prevent Kubo's goal."

It would be easy for Cunha to let his head go down after what was a disappointing performance on Saturday.

But the youngster's reaction will be important to watch at both international level and for Nottingham Forest.

He may be offered the chance to bounce back against Singapore on Tuesday, or will need to get himself mentally prepared to face Bournemouth next week.

Cunha clearly has all the talent, but this will be a question of his mental fortitude.
