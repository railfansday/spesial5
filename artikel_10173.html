This is the final issue of What’s !important on CSS-Tricks.com. The good news is that it’s coming back in an expanded form as the CSS-Tricks newsletter. I had ya there for a second, didn’t I? Sorry, I couldn’t decide whether to trick or treat, so I went for both. Happy Halloween!

So, let’s go out with a big bang for our “final” issue. It’s packed with some stuff that I’ve been holding onto since before the site came back to life earlier this week, but also some new stuff, and I can’t wait to finally get it out to you.

Scooch over Apple, Rockstar has something they’d like to say about fancy scrolling effects (to be fair, they’ve had 13 years to create them). First, check out the GTA 6 website (which oddly doesn’t work in Firefox), then watch Kevin Powell’s GTA 6 scroll effects walkthrough, which is frankly the most fun that you can have while waiting (and waiting) for GTA 6.

Mojtaba Seyedi demonstrated how to build a scrollspy with modern CSS. What’s a scrollspy, you ask? Well, if you have a navigation that links to different sections of the page, a scrollspy highlights the section that you’re currently on in the navigation.

Brecht De Ruyte showcased and explained some absolutely stunning random() demos.

Lea Verou explained, in detail, the different ways of implementing icons on the web. Many exciting CSS features of the future are mentioned, including Linked Parameters, ident(), @mixin and @apply, the @property shorthand, and more, so it’s a really deep dive with a lot of intrigue.
