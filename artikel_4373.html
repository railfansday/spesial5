Trusted online information may help networking skills translate into startup ambitions, survey suggests

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

A study of almost 200 undergraduate and postgraduate students in South Korea suggests that the skills of aspiring entrepreneurs are more likely to translate into plans to start a business when supported by trustworthy digital networks and useful online resources. Details of the findings are reported in the World Review of Entrepreneurship, Management and Sustainable Development.
