Texas's incoming attorney general vows to scrutinize a SpaceX-Tesla merger

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nathan Johnson, who takes office in January, said he has no qualms about examining Elon Musk's ties to the state

Texas Attorney General-elect Nathan Johnson vowed to scrutinize any merger between Elon Musk's SpaceX and Tesla $TSLA, the state's two highest-valued companies, he told Bloomberg on Thursday.

"Any merger of companies of that consequence warrants tremendous scrutiny," Johnson said, according to Bloomberg. He would not get into details about a possible SpaceX-Tesla combination.

SpaceX and Tesla are progressing in merger negotiations and could announce a deal as soon as December, with an announcement in early 2027 also under discussion, according to Bloomberg. The deal would be the biggest ever, and the combined company would span carmaking, battery storage for energy, and rocket and satellite businesses. SpaceX is valued at over $1.9 trillion, and Tesla's valuation is roughly $1.5 trillion. SpaceX and Tesla had not responded to requests for comment from Bloomberg.

Johnson said Musk has benefited from his ties to the Republican Party. "I think he's received a great deal of deference due to his alignment with the Republican Party and the tons of money he spent for the Republican Party," Johnson said, according to Bloomberg. "I have no qualms about examining his relationships to the state."

"I don't want anybody to have such control of the public sector that they can bend public power to their private gain," Johnson said of Musk. "I think his empire is at great risk of crossing that line."

Johnson said neither he nor his transition team has contacted Musk. "If Elon wants to talk, we can talk," he said.

Johnson, Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa and Sen.-elect James Talarico have pledged to rein in Musk’s influence, according to Bloomberg. Democrats, who will control Congress starting in January, have promised to investigate Musk’s dealings in both business and politics.

Johnson will take office in January. He has promised to focus on the "nuts and bolts" of the office, including consumer protection and antitrust litigation, according to Bloomberg.

Tesla and SpaceX stock extended a selloff last week after Democrats won majorities in both houses of Congress and key offices in Texas. Hinojosa defeated Gov. Greg Abbott $ABT in the governor's race. Musk's election spending totaled at least $474 million, including $224 million in Texas, where Republican Senate nominee Ken Paxton and Abbott lost.
