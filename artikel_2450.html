Gladbach keen to extend Mohya's contract as Bayern monitor situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Borussia Mönchengladbach, as BILD reports, are determined to fend off interest from Bayern Munich for Wael Mohya.

Other top clubs from around Europe are also keeping tabs alongside Bayern, but Gladbach are eager to extend Mohya's contract.

The 17-year old's current deal, which runs until 2028, does not contain a release clause, even in the event of the Foals suffering relegation.

Mohya's agents are likely to insist on a release clause if he were to extend, which would be on the lower end in case of demotion.

However, if he agrees a new deal and Gladbach crucially avoid relegation, then the fixed price for his release clause would be higher.

Gladbach's goal is to get a club record fee for Mohya once they decide to sell – Transfermarkt already values the teenager at €17million.

The Rhineland outfit's current record departure is held by Granit Xhaka who joined Arsenal back in July 2016 for €45million.
