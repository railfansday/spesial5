If you’re looking for a new laptop or a gift for the student in your life, you can save $50 on the MacBook Neo at several retailers, in both storage sizes and all four colors. The 256GB model is currently discounted to $649 (Amazon, Best Buy, Target), while the 512GB model with Touch ID is down to $749 (Amazon, Best Buy, Target). None of the retailers currently list an end date for the discount, which is the biggest price drop we’ve seen since Apple raised the laptop’s MSRP by $100 in July in response to the ongoing RAM crisis.

Apple’s budget-friendly MacBook leverages the A18 Pro processor, a chip originally found in the iPhone 16 Pro, to keep costs down. Both the 256GB and 512GB models feature 8GB of memory, and my colleague Antonio G. Di Benedetto was impressed by the vivid 13-inch screen and speakers, the latter of which are better than those in many cheap (and even midrange) Windows laptops. The Neo isn’t super performant, especially in terms of storage speed and total memory, but it’s great for everyday tasks, casual use, and students.
