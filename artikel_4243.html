I remember the first of my many Baldur's Gate 3 playthroughs well. Having never played a game like it, I dipped quickly. The controls were finicky, and combat was way too difficult. I got beaten by the Intellect Devourers by the crashed Nautilus ship, and comfortably again by the goblins at Emerald Grove. Eventually it clicked, and subsequent runs were a bit more of a doddle—never smooth sailing, but challenging enough.

The addition of Honour Mode made things significantly harder, but for some players, that clearly wasn't enough, as Larian has just vetted, approved, and uploaded a new "Brutal" difficulty mode for the console versions of Baldur's Gate 3.

Another week has passed, and another huge set of content has dropped for console players of Baldur's Gate 3. Larian is doing its utmost to keep things fresh for players by pulling the best mods from the PC version of the game and implementing them into the console version.

We've had some absolutely spectacular additions this year, including a version of Shadow of Mordor's Nemesis System, the ability to create a party of villains or recruit any NPC, and an incredibly in-depth cheat menu. Brutal Difficulty is on a par with those.
