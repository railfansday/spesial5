How To Make Money Fast In Loftia

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Loftia might look like a cozy paradise where you can spend your days decorating, farming, and making friends, but all those adorable outfits and cute decorations aren't going to pay for themselves! Dew is the main currency you'll need to get your island looking fabulous, and you'll quickly find yourself wanting a much bigger wallet.

Making money doesn't have to mean spending hours grinding away or selling every little thing you own. With a bit of planning and some smart resource management, you can turn your humble little farm into a surprisingly profitable operation. Time to put on your cutest overalls and get that Dew!

Farming, cooking, and selling crops/food is one of the best ways to make money in Loftia, and the following table breaks down the most profitable options.

Purchase three eggs and one butter from Coon's shop, then cook them at your kitchen.

Costs 20 Dew in ingredients and sells for 25 Dew, earning 5 Dew profit per dish.

Grow them at your Garden. Buy the seeds in Loftia City.

Grow potatoes and purchase milk from Coon's shop. Cook the ingredients together to make potato soup.

Costs 30 Dew in ingredients and sells for 65 Dew, earning 35 Dew profit per dish.

Grow potatoes and purchase cooking oil from Coon's shop. Use one cooking oil for every two potatoes.

Grow tomatoes and use them to prepare tomato soup.

Costs 30 Dew in seeds and sells for 65 Dew, earning 35 Dew profit per dish.

Grow carrots and use two carrots per recipe. The recipe is available for free.

A great low-cost option since you don't need to purchase a recipe.

Forage wild berries in the Hills area and purchase sugar from Coon's shop. Use two berries and one sugar per smoothie.

A great way to turn free foraged ingredients into sellable food.

Sell unwanted clothing and furniture through your island's vending machine.

Earn Dew from items that cannot be sold through the regular delivery box.
