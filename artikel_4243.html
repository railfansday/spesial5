Shopify Rebellion Black beat Xora 2-0 in opening VCT Americas Kickoff qualifier match

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Shopify Rebellion Black opened the North America Qualifier Playoffs with a 2-0 win over Xora Esports. The non-partner organisation is now one win from the playoffs that decide a guaranteed São Paulo Kickoff place.

The best-of-three Group Stage meeting with Xora began at 00:10, opening Shopify Rebellion Black’s VCT Americas Kickoff: North America 2027 campaign.

The result counts for more because Shopify Rebellion is now outside Riot’s partnership system. The organisation was one of the Americas sides that said it had not been chosen for the 2027-2028 partnership programme, Sheep Esports reported. Cloud9, ENVY, M80, FlyQuest and LYON were also on that list. Riot now has eight partner teams in each of its four regions.

The Swiss Stage runs from 20 to 22 November 2026. Every match is best of three. Two wins send a team through to the playoffs, and two losses knock it out. The eight teams left after the Swiss phase move into a double-elimination bracket played over six competition days, with the playoffs set for 1 to 6 December. The event is listed as an online, A-Tier Riot Games qualifier for 16 teams.

Shopify Rebellion Black’s next opponents are Oafs, who also won their opening match 2-0, against QoR. Both teams are therefore one win from the playoffs. Oafs already have a record against Xora. They knocked them out of the Qualifier 1 bracket on 8 November with a 2-1 win. Xora had still reached the Qualifier Playoffs by finishing joint third in the 512-team North America Qualifier 1.

Across 83 rated matches, Shopify Rebellion Black hold a 59-24 record. Before this event their most recent series was a 2-3 defeat to 2GAME Esports at the VCL Americas Last Chance Qualifier 2026. Earlier in that event they had beaten 2GAME Esports 2-1 and Contra 2-0.

The organisation’s second line-up had a harder start. Shopify Rebellion Gold lost 0-2 to Maryville University. Several of the other organisations left out of the partnership programme won their first matches. Cloud9 beat counterfeit 2-0, Team Envy beat Yellow Dragon Gaming 2-0 and FlyQuest edged Masterclass 2-1. Elsewhere, Winthrop University beat SaD Esports 2-0 and YFP Gaming beat Wildcard 2-1.

The prize for coming through the bracket is a place in São Paulo. Riot will stage the 2027 VCT Americas Kickoff in Brazil. Its eight Partner Teams get direct entry. Non-partner teams can win four places: one guaranteed slot each for North America, Brazil and Latin America, plus one for the Americas Last Chance Qualifier. For a non-partner side, a later run to Masters would be worth a fixed US$200,000 and a place at Champions US$400,000, Dot Esports reported. The regional Kickoff events are due to start on 14 January 2027 and run until 7 February 2027.
