Alvaro Arbeloa remains at high risk of being sacked by Fulham despite their recent turnaround, sources have told Football Insider.

After what was a slow start to the Premier League season, the Whites have lost just one of their last seven league games, including impressive draws against Liverpool and Aston Villa.

However, the lack of wins at Craven Cottage remains a concern given their proximity to the relegation zone, and a downward turn would likely spark renewed pressure on the boss.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Alvarto is among the likely candidates to follow Michael Carrick and Pierre Sage out of the door.

The former Real Madrid boss was the odd-on favourite to be the first manager sacked earlier in the season, and despite avoiding the axe, he remains under pressure.

Should Fulham's results not improve following the international break when they face a tricky run of fixtures, Arbeloa will find himself in the firing line once again.

Former Everton CEO Keith Wyness warned Football Insider last month that the Fulham board are "looking over their shoulder" with the relegation zone not out of sight.

Despite a recent upturn in form, there are still doubts over the manager's position, with a downturn in form likely to lead to new doubts about his future in the dugout.

Arbeloa remains one of the favourites to lose his job this season, and while Fulham are optimistic he can continue their recent run, they are making plans just in case.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Now that there's been two managers lost their jobs in the Premier League, it normally means that it does open the doors for more to follow.

"I wouldn't say there’s a clear front runner right now. Obviously there's been a lot of talk about Alvaro Arbaloa at Fulham.

"Especially earlier in season when they were struggling for results, he was the bookie's favourite to be the first man to lose his job.

"Obviously he's turned things around there at Fulham and they've picked up results, bu if things were to go wrong again, he could quickly find himself under pressure."

Arbeloa has already outlasted two Premier League managers so far this season, and could avoid being the next to be sacked, with other bosses facing increasing pressure.

Clubs below Fulham in the table include Nottingham Forest, who have asked questions of Oliver Glasner, and Sunderland, with Regis Le Bris also facing new doubts.

Former Sunderland chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider on 18 November that Le Bris might be "in trouble" given the ever-growing list of injuries at the Stadium of Light.

Granit Xhaka and Brian Brobbey have both picked up blows in recent weeks, and their absence will come as nightmare news for the Sunderland boss.

Meanwhile, Le Bris has come under pressure in recent weeks following what has been a nightmare start to the season, with Sunderland now second bottom of the league table.
