Derek McInnes on Findlay Curtis Rangers future as he reveals player stance

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Derek McInnes has made it clear Rangers want Findlay Curtis to commit his long-term future to the club, with the youngster also determined to remain at Ibrox and win trophies.

The 20-year-old is out of contract in the summer and faces stiff competition for regular starts in McInnes' side.

However, the Rangers boss has been impressed with Curtis' attitude and contribution and believes the academy graduate is firmly on the path towards establishing himself as a first-team regular.

"He knows how much we want him to be here longer term," said McInnes. "The club have made that clear to him as well.

"And Findlay's telling me that he wants to be here. He wants to win trophies here. This is his club.

"He knows what we think about him. I'm not here to say to him, 'be patient', because I want him to be in a hurry to get more starts.

"But I believe that he is well on his way to being a good Rangers player going forward."

McInnes insists there is no shame in Curtis having to battle for minutes given the competition for places at Ibrox, but has challenged the youngster to make the most of every opportunity.

"There's no shame in getting into this Rangers team week in, week out," he said. "He's got good competition there.

"For any player, whether it's homegrown or not, they've got to accept that at Rangers there should be competition for places.

"You don't waste your minutes. If you're in the team, do your best to stay in the team.

"Findlay has contributed really well for us, really strongly. He is a good trainer. He's a brilliant kid."

McInnes also revealed he has enjoyed working with Curtis since taking charge and highlighted the importance of the minutes he has already accumulated, whether from the start or off the bench.

"Getting those minutes is vitally important," he added. "Hopefully this season he can get the minutes that he's happy with, but we're certainly happy with a lot of his contributions."
