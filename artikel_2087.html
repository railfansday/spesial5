Jack McGlynn & Viktor Johansson join Stoke City fans' George Hirst trend

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Stoke City have made it five league wins on the bounce for the first time since 2008 following their 2-1 home win over Millwall, as the likes of Jack McGlynn and Viktor Johansson have joined in with Potters supporters' trend involving George Hirst.

Stoke continue their incredible run of form in the Championship, making it 11 wins, two draws, and one defeat in their 14 league games, after having lost all three of their opening games.

The Potters have emerged as surprise automatic promotion contenders this season, and with West Ham United away on Saturday, face arguably their biggest task yet since this mighty run of form began.

Back-to-back home victories over Birmingham City and Millwall mean they will head to the capital full of confidence, as the goalscorer from those games has drawn attention on social media.

Football League World takes a closer look at the best value-for-money signings in the Championship this season

George Hirst joined Stoke in the summer window from Ipswich Town for an initial £6 million, rising to £10 million, and with just one goal in his first 14 Championship games for the club, drew criticism from some supporters.

However, after netting the only goal in Stoke's 1-0 victory over Birmingham and both of the Potters' goals in their 2-1 win over Millwall, the Scottish international has silenced a few doubters, with his goal celebration against Blues not going unnoticed.

Celebrating his goal against Birmingham with Luke Graham, Hirst mimed spreading butter on bread, a nod to his interview whilst with Ipswich, where he scored a late penalty, incidentally against Blues, saying in his post-match interview: "[Scoring the penalty] is what I'm paid to do. It is my bread and butter."

Now, some Stoke players have seemingly picked up on this, and taken to Instagram to react to his recent brace against Millwall.

Setting up Hirst's first goal against the Lions was American international Jack McGlynn, who has adapted tremendously to English football following his late summer switch from Houston Dynamo.

The midfielder has been a revelation for the Potters, and among their most important players this season, and alongside star shot-stopper Viktor Johansson, has taken to social media to react to Hirst's brace.

With just two simple bread and butter emojis, McGlynn and Johansson know exactly what they're making a nod to, and if Hirst continues to score at the rate he has over the past couple of games, it certainly won't be the last time those pair use those emojis this season.

With Hirst undergoing a slow start to life upon his move to Stoke, he has certainly silenced some of his doubters this week.

Lamine Cisse's injury has afforded the Scot a run of games in the side, though with both Sam Gallagher and Robert Bozenik waiting in the wings, Hirst must ensure he keeps firing at this rate if he wants to keep his place in the side.

There is growing optimism around the Potteries right now that this year could be Stoke's year, and Mark Robins will be hoping Hirst can keep firing them in at this rate to ensure that dream becomes a reality.
