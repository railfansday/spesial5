PSG are among several clubs reportedly tracking the progress of Chelsea starlet Ibrahim Rabbaj.

The 17-year-old signed his first professional contract in January, putting pen to paper on a deal until 2028.

The Blues will now hope that they can convince him to commit to a longer contract, with the winger turning heads across Europe.

Rabbaj is currently with Morocco's Under-17s for the U17 World Cup in Qatar, and starred in their opening 6-0 win over Fiji on Friday.

The teenager scored and assisted, undoubtedly stirring up the growing interest in him once more.

Chelsea signed Rabbaj in 2021 from Crystal Palace and will hope he stays put to eventually make his first team debut at Stamford Bridge.

From just a glance at Rabbaj, it is easy to see why he is so highly rated and so in demand.

The winger is bursting with talent and confidence, showing it in countless instances against Fiji on Friday.

In one, the 17-year-old controlled the ball and kept it up several times before passing, while another saw him take the ball past two defenders, sitting the goalkeeper on his backside before rolling into the net.

That goal was ultimately disallowed for offside, but Rabbaj had already found the net earlier in the game when he controlled an excellent volley into the ground and past the goalkeeper.

The Chelsea starlet also picked up an assist in the game, won a penalty and created a staggering five chances - the joint-most of anybody.

He took three shots, had eight touches in the opposition penalty area, attempted four dribbles, put eight crosses into the box and was fouled twice.

Rabbaj got his defensive work done, too, making a tackle, an interception, two recoveries and winning four duels.

Chelsea will be worried about the growing interest in Rabbaj, and for good reason.

Players are being targeted at younger and younger ages nowadays, and will be presented with financial commitments unheard of in prior years.

Most importantly for most young players remains the pathway, however, and Chelsea may not offer the best one for Rabbaj.

The likes of Georgia Quenda, Pedro Neto and Estevao are problems for the 17-year-old on the right of Xabi Alonso's attack, and they do not look to be going anywhere, anytime soon.

Rabbaj will have to be convinced, and it will up to Chelsea to make that argument.
