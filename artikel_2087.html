Data estimates how much Huddersfield Town loan star Cameron Humphreys is worth ahead of potential permanent swoop

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Huddersfield Town are the team to watch in League One this season. HTAFC sit 1st in the table and, despite a first-round exit of the FA Cup, will be hopeful of a return to the Championship.

The Terries have seen several outstanding performers with the likes of Derensili Sanches Fernandes and Joe Taylor. However, it’s Cameron Humphreys who has come to life in the 2026/27 campaign under Martin Drury.

Huddersfield Town signed the 23-year-old on loan for the second consecutive term in the summer, and he has established himself already with five assists in 13 games. If he continues at the same level, the Terriers may have to consider turning his spell into a permanent deal.

Humphreys is under contract at Ipswich Town until 2028, so if they cash in on the midfielder, they could demand a relatively substantial fee. However, given the fact he rose through the academy at Portman Road and would generate absolute profit, he could go for a cheaper fee.

That is what the data suggests, and Huddersfield Town would certainly entertain a move for the midfielder if his value is accurate. FootballTransfers value the youngster at €700,000, which is just under £600,000.

Given his displays in League One this season and the current market rate for most 23-year-olds in the ranks of a Premier League side, it wouldn’t be a surprise to see ITFC actually demand a seven-figure sum to acquire Humphreys.

The midfielder has won 57% of his duels this season and has won 32 fouls for HTAFC. With eight big chances created and 18 successful crosses, Ipswich Town will undoubtedly view his price tag as a lot higher should bids come in for him in 2027.

Huddersfield Town’s main chance of signing Humphreys comes down to whether or not Ipswich Town remain in the Premier League by the end of the campaign. The 23-year-old is excelling in League One this season, and if they were in the Championship again, the Tractor Boys would likely include him in their plans.

That being said, HTAFC currently sit top of the third tier, and promotion to the second tier would give them a great pull to sign the midfielder permanently. Drury has the Terriers playing a good brand of football, and if Humphreys were a part of his plans, there’s no reason why he wouldn’t want to join.

The 23-year-old has made just 44 appearances for Ipswich Town since his debut in 2021. However, since the 2023/24 campaign, he has appeared only 14 times for the Tractor Boys and his patience could be running out for a future at Portman Road.
