Walmart Is Selling These 5-Cent Chocolate Bars For One Day &amp; They Make Great Stocking Stuffers

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Hershey’s is bringing back a price tag straight out of 1900.

Delish editors handpick every product we feature. We may earn commission from the links on this page.

The day before Thanksgiving is getting even more epic than usual. On November 25, Hershey’s is traveling back in time to when the company first released its iconic Milk Chocolate Bars. Back in 1900, the bars sold for just five cents a piece, and for one day only, the company is bringing back that original price.

Timed to coincide with the November 25 release of Hershey, a film about founder Milton Hershey starring Finn Wittrock and Alexandra Daddario, the super-discounted chocolate bars make for the perfect stocking stuffer or post-Thanksgiving treat.

There are a few ways to score the deal, although the rules vary depending on where you're shopping. Starting at 9 a.m. EST, customers can purchase the five-cent chocolate bars through Walmart.com for online pickup with a Walmart account. The offer is also available in stores through the Ibotta app, where shoppers must first purchase a bar at regular price and submit their receipt to receive cash back. Both options have a five-bar limit per person.

The bars will also be available through Amazon Grocery for same-day delivery where available. Hershey's will also share a promo code on its social channels for free shipping to qualifying addresses. Amazon purchases are limited to two bars per person, and additional fees for non-Prime members and taxes may apply.

Lucky visitors to Hershey's Chocolate World in Hershey, Pennsylvania, and Times Square in New York City can also enjoy the deal, starting at 9 a.m. and 10 a.m., respectively. Purchases at these locations are limited to 10 bars per person while supplies last, and taxes may apply.
