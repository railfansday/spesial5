Newspapers deliver mixed verdicts after Italy’s Nations League collapse against Belgium

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Newspapers in Italy have delivered mixed verdicts following the national team’s 4-1 defeat against Belgium on Sunday evening, which saw the Azzurri knocked out of the Nations League despite starting the evening at the top of the group.

Italy had started Sunday in first place in their Nations League group following an impressive 3-1 win at home against France on Thursday night, but ended up in third place after losing 4-1 to Belgium and seeing France claim a 2-1 victory over Turkey.

The in-form Pio Esposito opened the scoring with his 10th goal for the Nazionale, but Italy would go on to concede three goals within 11 minutes from Maxim De Cuyper, Nathan Ngoy and Jeremy Doku in the first half. Milan’s Diego Moreira added a fourth towards the end and Esposito missed a penalty to make the scores 4-2.

The reactions in the Italian press on Monday morning have brought mixed reviews.

La Gazzetta dello Sport criticised Mancini’s decisions on the night, particularly his personnel choices with an untested front three of Esposito, Luca Koleosho and out-of-position central midfielder Niccolo Pisilli starting across the front three together for the first time.

The Pink Paper felt that Italy had a strong opening 10 minutes, resulting in Esposito’s 10th Azzurri goal, but that they simply stopped playing from that point onwards. They suggest that on another night, Italy could have conceded twice as many goals as they did.

However, La Gazzetta dello Sport admits that it is better for this sort of result and performance to have happened now rather than later down the line.

The Corriere dello Sport agreed that Italy could have conceded several more had it not been for a solid performance between the sticks from Gianluigi Donnarumma and that Italy’s first-half collapse was a major disappointment after all the hard work they had put in in their previous Nations League outings.

Pisilli and Nicolo Fagioli were singled out for individual criticism after being hooked at half-time, but there was praise for Esposito, who is now up to 10 goals from his first 15 national team caps.

The Corriere dello Sport concludes that ‘you can’t ask youngsters to learn and grow and then demolish them for their mistakes, we must support them’.

Tuttosport agrees that there have still been plenty of positives from Italy’s Nations League campaign and insists that the Azzurri have ‘come a long way’ since their first game against Belgium back in September.

Tuttosport pointed out that Italy did well to pick up 10 points from a group that contained France, Belgium and Turkey, who all went to the 2026 World Cup that the Azzurri infamously missed out on.

They praised Mancini for bringing Italy back to modern European standards by deploying a 4-3-3 and praised his confidence in young players and his ability to get the best out of Esposito at centre-forward.

The major issue that Tuttosport pointed to was with Italy’s defence, given that they failed to keep a clean sheet in any of their six Nations League group stage fixtures, even conceding twice against Turkey, who finished the group stage with six straight defeats.

Still, Tuttosport urges Italy to look to the future and to focus on the upcoming Euro 2028 qualifiers, which are more important than the Nations League campaign anyway.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
