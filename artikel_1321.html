De Bruyne given extra time off before Napoli training

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Napoli coach Max Allegri has tried to patch things over with Kevin De Bruyne by giving him extra time off after playing for Belgium in the Nations League.

The Partenopei will be back on the pitch on Saturday evening against Torino in Serie A after the two-week break for international duty.

De Bruyne started both matches for his country, securing qualification for the Nations League quarter-finals by beating Turkiye 2-1 and Italy 4-1.

According to Sky Sport Italia, the original plan was for the midfielder to return to Naples on Monday in order to resume training today.

However, Allegri and the player agreed to postpone his training return to Wednesday, giving him an extra day off.

It also remains to be seen whether De Bruyne will start in Serie A this weekend, considering he has a Champions League trip to his old club Manchester City coming up next Wednesday.

De Bruyne has played 11 Serie A games and four in the Champions League for Napoli so far this season, contributing four goals and two assists.

He has not disguised his frustration at Allegri putting him in a deeper midfield playmaker role at times, with the coach consistently trying to convince him that this will extend his career.
