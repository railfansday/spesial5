Pochettino Suffers Fresh Man Utd Blow as Rashford Ruled Out of Liverpool Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

New Manchester United manager Mauricio Pochettino has suffered a blow ahead of his first match in charge against Liverpool on Sunday, as GIVEMESPORT understands that Marcus Rashford remains sidelined and won't be fit for the trip to Merseyside.

Pochettino failed to give an update on the fitness of his players in his first press conference on Friday, perhaps influenced by Drone-gate in the lead up to the big game at Anfield.

The Argentine will be determined to get off to a positive start as Man United manager, with the club lying in 19th position in the Premier League table after winning just one of their opening 10 top flight fixtures this term.

The Red Devils dropped a place in the table on Saturday after Sunderland picked up a point against Aston Villa at Villa Park to leapfrog them in the table, and they were fortunate that Crystal Palace were beaten by Coventry City in Thomas Frank's first game at the helm, otherwise they'd have been rock-bottom.

It won't be an easy task against Andoni Iraola's side, and Pochettino would have been hoping to welcome back some of his injured stars to help him on Merseyside.

There was anticipation that Harry Maguire, Luke Shaw and Rashford might all return to face Liverpool after various injury issues, and while there is no update on the former two yet, GMS understands that Rashford won't be available.

The fact that the 29-year-old remains sidelined with his ongoing hip issue could also raise doubts over his availability for the trip to Sporting Lisbon on Wednesday in the Champions League.

United will also be without Benjamin Sesko and Carlos Baleba against Liverpool, with the duo also both on the sidelines because of injury, meaning Pochettino's options are limited in certain positions.

With reports suggesting that the Argentine has 'always loved' Rashford, being without him already will be a blow for the former Spurs boss.

With Sesko and Rashford both confirmed absentees for the trip to Anfield, United's frontline almost picks itself unless Pochettino is ready to make an unexpected pick.

Should the Red Devils line up in a 4-2-3-1 formation, it would be expected that Bruno Fernandes stays in the No.10 position, given we know the Argentine manager is a big fan of the Portugal international.

Fernandes has struggled to live up to the level he showed last season, but he continues to be their most influential player in the final third.

Youth and experience collide under Pochettino as unexpected starters step up to face Liverpool in a high stakes debut.

Pochettino could bring Joshua Zirkzee in from the fringes to play as a centre-forward, but starting Bryan Mbeumo there seems more likely given the Dutchman's lack of minutes this term.

If Mbeumo does start through the centre, then Amad Diallo and Matheus Cunha could get the nod on the flanks, although Shea Lacey is pushing for a starting spot after impressive cameos off the substitutes' bench against AS Roma and Aston Villa in recent weeks.

Elsewhere, Patrick Dorgu should start at left-back if Shaw isn't deemed fit enough, while Lisandro Martinez, Kobbie Mainoo and Youri Tielemans also appear likely to make the starting XI given the injury issues in the squad.
