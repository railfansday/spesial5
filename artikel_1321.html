Pep Guardiola Claimed One British Ground is ‘Like No Other Stadium in the World’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Pep Guardiola, during his respective spells in charge of Manchester City, Bayern Munich and Barcelona, experienced some of Europe's most famous football stadiums – yet there is one venue he believes stands above the rest, describing it as like 'no other stadium in the world'.

The 55-year-old is widely regarded as one of the finest managers in football history, with an extraordinary collection of silverware that includes three Champions League trophies, six Premier League titles and three La Liga titles, highlighting his remarkable success.

Given his longstanding presence among Europe's elite clubs, Guardiola has visited countless iconic grounds throughout his managerial career. His travels have taken him everywhere from Manchester United's Old Trafford to Slavia Prague's home, the Fortuna Arena.

However, neither the famous home of his current rivals nor any of the other impressive stadiums he has experienced can match Liverpool's Anfield, in his opinion. The famous ground has proved to be a particularly difficult place for him to win over the years.

Although Guardiola was a direct competitor of Liverpool for much of his time in English football, the Catalan holds enormous respect for Anfield, a stadium widely regarded as one of the most intimidating and atmospheric venues in world football.

In an interview with Catalan newspaper Ara, as reported by Sky Sports, Guardiola discussed Barcelona's dramatic Champions League semi-final exit in 2019, when the Spanish giants suffered a 4-0 defeat on Merseyside. The Santpedor-born manager offered a memorable description of the atmosphere at Anfield.

He explained that the incredible noise and unique feeling surrounding Liverpool's historic home make it a "special" place, capable of leaving visiting teams feeling "small". Guardiola said: "I thought they'd [Barcelona] score a goal at Anfield.

“And I am sure the players knew that Anfield is Anfield. The motto "This is Anfield" is no marketing spin. There's something about it that you will find in no other stadium in the world.

“They score a goal and over the next five minutes you feel that you'll receive another four. You feel small and the rival players seem to be all over. We've all gone through what happened to Barca."

Reflecting on just how challenging it can be to play at Anfield, Guardiola also remembered City's 3-0 defeat against Jurgen Klopp's Liverpool in the opening leg of their 2018 Champions League quarter-final tie.

“They were laughing at me when we were losing 3-0 after the first 15–20 minutes of the quarter-final,” he recalled. "It's a bugger of a ground, and it's the sort of competition where one hand, one offside, Anfield…”
