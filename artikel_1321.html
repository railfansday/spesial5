Barcelona fans were full of praise for Anthony Gordon during his first few weeks at the Spotify Camp Nou.

Chants of “Gordon, Gordon, Gordon!” rang out from the stands, with the Catalan faithful immediately won over by his work ethic and selflessness. The club’s supporters even gave him a couple of nicknames, including “Sir Anthony Gordon”.

But fast-forward to November and the Gordon’s honeymoon period with his new club’s fans, it seems, may well be over.

Gordon made his 15th appearance for Barca during Sunday’s 2-1 away win over Atletico Madrid, playing 86 minutes before being subbed off for Karim Adeyemi.

On paper, the England international’s stats so far are reasonably impressive: three goals and five assists in 15 appearances. That’s more than one goal contribution every other game.

However, Gordon’s stats look far less eye-catching when compared to Barcelona’s other attackers.

Lamine Yamal has 13 goals and 12 assists (25 goal contributions) in 16 appearances, while Raphinha has 19 goals and three assists (22 goal contributions) in just 14 matches. Meanwhile, Adeyemi is roughly on par with Gordon this season with four goals and three assists in 15 appearances.

And the underrated Fermin Lopez, who scored both of Barcelona’s goals against Atletico Madrid, now has eight goals and seven assists (15 goal contributions) in 15 games.

Fermin was handed a 9.5/10 rating from Barca Universal, while Gordon’s 6.5 rating was followed up with some rather concerning analysis.

“Anthony Gordon does not do a lot wrong in terms of holding on to the ball and passing it around, but for the nth time, his attacking output was nothing extraordinary,” they said of the former Newcastle United winger.

“The player struggled to take on his man and create any attacking openings of note against Atletico Madrid. Most of his touches were either horizontal or passes played backwards. He did not add much value as a creative option, a pace runner, or even a good ball-striker.”

Many Barcelona fans on social media are in complete agreement. They’re now questioning whether Gordon, who cost £69.3 million (€80 million), is good enough to play for one of the world’s biggest football clubs - particularly when up against the best sides.

One fan wrote on X (Twitter): “Who advised us to sign Gordon? This guy can’t shoot, pass or take on players… I’m genuinely curious to know what does he actually offer?”

Another commented: “Gordon is just another [Marcus] Rashford in disguise.” While a third added:

“I loved Gordon, but I'm done with him now. Guy has ZERO flair and impact, just passes back again and again. You CANNOT be doing that at FC Barcelona, especially with Adeyemi on the bench.”

Another said: “Are we opening a file on Anthony Gordon? No dribbling, no 1v1s, no shots. Too unremarkable for a player in that position at Barca.”

A fifth tweeted: “Anthony Gordon was loving it against the relegation-threatened teams, we gave you a little time to settle in but now there’s no excuse. No personality, no intensity, no desire. Honestly, I see no difference between him and Rashford.”

Another said: “I’m sorry guys, but we have wasted €80 million on Anthony Gordon.”
