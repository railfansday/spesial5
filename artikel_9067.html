18 Stunning Finalists From Close-Up Photographer of the Year

The finalists for this year’s Close-up Photographer of the Year (CUPOTY) have been announced — a competition showcasing the very best of close-up, macro and micro photography.

A record 15,388 images were entered into this year’s competition. Everything from acrobatic chameleons and nose-picking birds to opportunistic ants and spawning salmon was put before a panel of expert judges.

Spanning 11 galleries, the shortlist celebrates the hidden wonders of the world, with categories covering everything from Animals, Arachnids and Insects to Intimate Landscape, Studio Art and Underwater.

“Close-up photography has a wonderful way of revealing details and perspectives that often go unnoticed,” says competition co-founder Tracy Calder. “This year’s shortlist is a fabulous showcase of imagination, technical expertise and natural curiosity.”

The overall winner of CUPOTY 8 will be announced in January 2027.
