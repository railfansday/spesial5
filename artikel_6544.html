Chanel’s Watches & Fine Jewelry Dinner Christened Daniel Humm’s New Hotspot

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

“Yes, it’s my first time here, too,” was the refrain repeatedly heard around the room at Humm on Hudson Street on Tuesday evening, as guests stole glances at their Première Galon watches to count down the minutes to dinner. After a candlelit cocktail hour, right on cue, at 8 p.m., the moment had come to experience Daniel Humm’s new restaurant, some two years in the making.

Chanel has been keeping New York’s best-dressed guests busy as of late: The last few weeks alone have seen outings for the Museum of Modern Art’s Film Benefit, the Dia Art Foundation, the Women’s Prize Trust, and Tribeca Through Her Lens. This time around, the annual Watches and Fine Jewelry holiday dinner was as good an excuse as any for the group to get dolled up and enjoy a pause before the busiest season of all.

Sarah Pidgeon, who became an official ambassador for the house’s watches and fine jewelry last year, was among the earliest to arrive. For the occasion, the Love Story star donned a Coco Crush necklace and bracelets. The night was also a chance for the actress to reunite with fellow Ryan Murphy-universe newcomer Ella Beatty, fresh off her acclaimed turn as the notorious Lizzie Borden.

Though the restaurant has been open for just a month and change—Chanel’s get-together being the first brand event hosted there—word has traveled fast about its menu. Luckily, the evening’s sampling incorporated the soon-to-be-renowned caviar-topped miniature bagels, which, along with the disco-tiled bathroom, seem destined for Instagram Stories fame.

A crowd, including Ella Rubin, Jack Harlow, and Quen Blackwell, settled in, drinking up the oxblood and velvet interiors, co-designed by Pierre Yovanovitch and Humm’s longtime friend, Italian artist Francesco Clemente. Clemente—yes, the namesake of the bar at Humm’s Eleven Madison Park—covered the ceilings in hand-painted stars, bricks, tiles, and trains. Works by Louise Bourgeois, Rashid Johnson, and Roni Horn round out the feast for the eyes.

As chocolate mousses and endless trays of espresso martinis did the rounds and the clock struck 10 p.m., Pidgeon stood to introduce a surprise performance by breakthrough Canadian singer girlsweetvoiced to end the night. “I was listening to her earlier today while walking around New York, and she captures something that’s very tender and honest in her stories,” Pidgeon said. “Her music is both singular and expansive and entirely her own.”

After girlsweetvoiced serenaded the room with three songs—“Cranberry,” “Spearmint,” and “Tonight,” her Joni Mitchell–approved single that samples “California”—the night drew to a close. Guests slowly made their way toward the exit, taking engraved gold matchboxes home with them as a keepsake of a reliably good time with Chanel, no matter the theme.
