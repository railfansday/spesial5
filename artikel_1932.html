Uckfield hit Westfield for six

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Just 12 minutes into the game, George Coleman broke into the box and was brought down, earning Uckfield a penalty. Rhys Taylor stepped up and confidently tucked it away to make it 1-0.

Just four minutes later, Coleman played a delightful ball down the right to Joey Garroch, who found himself one-on-one with the goalkeeper. He showed great composure, taking it around the keeper before slotting home for 2-0.

Garroch wasn't finished there! Finding himself around the goalkeeper again, this time from a wider angle, he managed to dig out a cross to the oncoming Oscar Morris, who powered his header home to make it three.

In the second half, the boys continued to control the game. Just shy of the 75th minute, Alex Saunders took a short corner to Taylor, who dropped his shoulder to beat his defender before whipping the ball into the bottom corner for 4-0.

Five minutes later, Taylor was involved again, playing a lovely ball down the left to Morris, who took on two defenders, worked his way into the box and fired into the near-post corner to make it 5-0.

In the closing moments, Tyler Grant picked up a loose ball just beyond the halfway line before driving towards the box. He cut the ball back to Fraser Argyle, who finished brilliantly with the outside of his foot to complete the scoring at 6-0.

It was a fantastic team performance, featuring six goals and another clean sheet.

Uckfield are fourth and visit fifth-placed Reigate Priory twos on Saturday.
