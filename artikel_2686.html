Patrick Reed had the perfect two-word reaction to receiving his PGA Tour card

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Patrick Reed is coming home, and he couldn't seem happier.

The 36-year-old left the PGA Tour to play on LIV Golf in 2022, but left that league ahead of this year with the intent playing his way back. He did just that by winning two times early in the year and easily earning one of 10 PGA Tour cards through the DP World Tour.

After it was official on Sunday, Reed was handed his new PGA Tour card. And he had a perfect two-word reaction.

It's not quite a Michael Jordan returning to the Bulls in 1995 "I'm back," but that's pretty good. Reed was then asked how it felt and he added, "It feels good. It feels good."

And you can tell how much it means to him by the big grin on his face.

Reed is a nine-time PGA Tour winner with his last PGA Tour title coming at the 2021 Farmers Insurance Open.
