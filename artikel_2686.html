Aaron Rodgers' numbers this year are similar to Mason Rudolph's career with the Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Steelers have benched Aaron Rodgers for Mason Rudolph, which means they’re replacing a quarterback who has given them mediocre production this year with a quarterback who has given them mediocre production throughout his career.

Rodgers’ numbers this year are remarkably similar to Rudolph’s numbers during his time with the Steelers.

Rodgers has a career-low passer rating this season of 86.6. Rudolph’s passer rating as a Steeler is 86.2.

The Steelers are 6-4 this season with Rodgers as their starting quarterback. Rudolph has started 14 games with the Steelers, spread across five seasons. The Steelers are 8-5-1 in those games.

Rodgers is averaging 6.2 yards per attempt this season. Rudolph has averaged 6.6 yards per attempt during his time with the Steelers.

There’s no real reason to think the Steelers will get better with Rudolph at quarterback, but there’s no reason to think they’ll get worse, either. Which says plenty about how far Rodgers has fallen.
