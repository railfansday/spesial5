Mensah And Toney Secure Spot In College Football’s Top Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Darian Mensah and Malachi Toney landed on the final lists for two of college football's biggest honors on Tuesday. The Miami Hurricanes quarterback and their star receiver earned this nod after a season filled with record-breaking plays and tough wins. This recognition comes just days before the team faces a huge test in the ACC Championship Game.

Mensah joins a short list of top quarterbacks for the Davey O'Brien Award. He also made the cut for the Maxwell Award, which goes to the best player in the nation regardless of position. According to Sports Illustrated, he sits alongside Utah's Devon Dampier and Wake Forest's Gio Lopez for the quarterback prize. The Maxwell list includes those two plus Ohio State wideout Jeremiah Smith. These choices show just how dominant the Miami offense has been under his command.

Malachi Toney faced a major setback when a high ankle sprain stopped his season in its tracks. He suffered the injury in practice on November 4 and had surgery the very next day. Despite missing the last three games, his numbers before the hit were impossible to ignore. Per Sports Illustrated, Toney posted 73 catches for 1,027 yards and 13 scores in just eight games. That average of more than 128 yards per game puts him in elite company.

He set a new Miami record with 16 catches for 194 yards and three touchdowns against Pittsburgh. He also exploded for 234 yards and three scores against Stanford. Mario Cristobal, the head coach, mentioned late last week that Toney has reached running speeds between 19 and 20 mph. The coach noted that the recovery has been great and that Toney could return in about a week and a half. This timeline lines up perfectly for the ACC Championship Game on December 5 against Wake Forest.

Darian Mensah has turned the Miami offense into one of the best in the country. Through 11 games, he completed 267 of 354 passes for 3,317 yards and 29 touchdowns. The Miami Hurricanes reported that he leads the nation with a 75.4 percent completion rate. He also ranks third in passing touchdowns and fourth in total yards. His efficiency has helped guide the team to a 10-1 record and a spot in the title game.

On December 11, the College Football Awards show on ESPN will distribute the awards. The Maxwell Award winner gets their trophy in Atlanta in March 2027. Mensah's path to this point included stops at Tulane and Duke before he arrived in Coral Gables. Leading the Blue Devils to a conference title, he earlier received All-ACC honors. Now he stands as a top contender for the nation's highest individual prizes.

Both players will be honored as the season reaches its climax. December 11 on ESPN's College Football Awards will reveal the Davey O'Brien and Maxwell winners. Atlanta is set to host the formal Maxwell Award presentation in March 2027.
