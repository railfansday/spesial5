Elliot Anderson roars back from England nightmare in style 💫

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Elliot Anderson returned from the international break as a man on a mission.

Anderson had joined Manchester City to great fanfare during the summer with a nine-figure fee paid to Nottingham Forest heightening expectations of the midfielder.

But the England international was further under the microscope in Saturday's Premier League encounter with Fulham on the back of his recent Nations League dismissal.

Anderson was hit with a straight red card as the Three Lions went on to edge out reigning world champions Spain during last weekend's five-goal thriller in Madrid.

Back on home soil, however, the 24-year-old stepped up as Enzo Maresca's side started down the barrel of a potential four-match losing streak in the top flight.

Trailing at home to Rodrigo Muniz's early opener, Anderson produced a sublime volley from the edge of the penalty area to restore parity for the hosts.
