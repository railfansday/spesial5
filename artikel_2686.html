Bo Nix throws directly to Jalen Ramsey for pick-six as Steelers dominate the first half

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Broncos quarterback Bo Nix has been awful today in Pittsburgh. And he just made his worst play yet.

Nix threw a perfect pass directly to a wide-open Jalen Ramsey for a touchdown, which would be great if Ramsey played for the Broncos, but was not so great because Ramsey plays for the Steelers.

It’s impossible to tell what Nix thought he was doing on the play, but he threw the ball right into Ramsey’s hands, and Ramsey returned the interception 36 yards to the house for a pick-six that gave the Steelers a 24-5 lead.

It was the second Steelers defensive touchdown off a Nix turnover; Nix also dropped the ball in the first quarter and T.J. Watt recovered for a touchdown.

Steelers quarterback Mason Rudolph hasn’t been great, but he’s been a lot better than Nix, and the Steelers are rolling over the Broncos in the first half.
