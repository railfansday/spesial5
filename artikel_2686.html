Jaylen Brown experiencing hamstring tightness

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

In many ways, Jaylen Brown had an outstanding Monday night in Brooklyn. He scored 31 points, grabbed a season-high 13 rebounds, and dished four assists in Philadelphia's 135-123 win over the Nets. However, it was also a tough night physically for the veteran wing. Early in the fourth quarter, Brown collided with Julius Randle, resulting in a cut on his forehead that briefly caused him to exit the game.

Fortunately, Brown was able to return with a bandage over the cut secured by a headband, which led to a fun moment.

While the head injury no doubt hurt, what's more concerning for the Sixers moving forward is Jaylen exiting the game with 1:44 remaining with what Nick Nurse termed 'tightness'.

Brown elaborated after the game on what is a left hamstring injury, indicating an MRI is a possibility.

The Sixers next play in Orlando Wednesday night, followed by a home meeting with the Bucks Saturday afternoon. I'd think it's extremely doubtful Brown will suit up against the Magic two nights after needing to leave the game with tightness. We'll see how further evaluations go before speculating on Jaylen's possibility of playing on Saturday and beyond.

Brown has been extremely durable across his career. Excluding the two NBA seasons shortened by COVID, 63 games played is the fewest total for Jaylen since he entered the league. He has had previous hamstring injuries that caused him to miss a bit of time and said he wasn't overly concerned about this one. Brown's Sixers tenure has gotten off to a solid start, averaging 22.6 points on .494/.386/.705 shooting splits, 6.6 rebounds, 3.6 assists, and 1.3 steals. Hopefully, this latest hamstring injury is minor and he can return to the court soon.
