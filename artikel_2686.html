Browns schedule: Bye week is followed by some interesting matchups

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Week 10 of the 2026 NFL season concludes on Monday Night Football, but Cleveland Browns fans get two weeks to relax and enjoy their team's 6-4 start to the season and holding a current spot in the AFC Playoff picture. The bye week comes at a perfect time, according to HC Todd Monken, as the coaching staff will get a better chance to self-scout for the closing schedule.

Perhaps that will help Cleveland start faster after the same concern has popped up week after week, including in their Week 10 victory over the Houston Texans.

Either way, the time off will be good for the players' bodies, while the coaches rework the plan. As of now, the Browns do not play again until November 29th at 1 PM against the Las Vegas Raiders. "As of now" is important, as the NFL can flex that game to Sunday Night Football instead of the early slot. That decision will have to be made by Tuesday.

After facing the Raiders, with rookie QB Fernando Mendoza, Cleveland then welcomes the Cincinnati Bengals, the current AFC North leaders, and the Atlanta Falcons, led by their former head coach Kevin Stefanski, to finish off their four-game homestand.

That leaves a finishing stretch with three out of four on the road: at the New York Giants, at the Baltimore Ravens, at home with the Indianapolis Colts, and finishing on the road against the Bengals again.

Seven games, four at home and three on the road, starting with six wins on the record books coming out of the bye week. Some juicy storylines are included in that schedule as well. If the Browns can win two of the next three, Cleveland fans will start the 12 Days of Christmas with a clear sight of the NFL playoffs, and that is all we can ask for.

Week 12 – 11/29 – 1 PM – Las Vegas Raiders at home

Week 13 – 12/6 – 1 PM – Cincinnati Bengals at home

Week 14 – 12/13 – 1 PM – Atlanta Falcons at home

Week 15 – 12/20 – 1 PM – New York Giants on the road

Week 16 – 12/27 – 1 PM – Baltimore Ravens on the road

Week 17 – 1/3 – 1 PM – Indianapolis Colts at home

Week 18 – TBD – Cincinnati Bengals on the road
