Andy Reid disagrees with overturned Rashee Rice catch after Chiefs' loss to Bills: 'That was not clear and obvious'

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Kansas City Chiefs wide receiver Rashee Rice appeared to have a much-needed first down against the Buffalo Bills on Thanksgiving. The NFL's replay center disagreed.

With the game tied 10-10 and two minutes left in the fourth quarter, the Chiefs got the ball deep in their own territory. A pair of incompletions left them facing third-and-10, at which point Patrick Mahomes appeared to connect with wide receiver Rashee Rice for 11 yards and the first down.

However, the play was surprisingly overturned on replay, with the official only saying, "After review, the ball hit the ground, it's an incomplete pass."

The call was popular at Buffalo's Highmark Stadium, but not so much on social media.

The video replay indeed showed the ball hit the ground while pinned between Rice's right hand and shoulder, but that alone doesn't make the play an incomplete pass. The ball must also come loose while hitting the ground before Rice establishes possession, and that was very much up for debate.

Additionally, the NFL maintains a "clear and obvious" standard for overturning plays. So the replay officials in New York City not only believed Rice hadn't completed the catch, they also decided it was clear to anyone watching.

Chiefs head coach Andy Reid very much didn't seem to think that was the case after his team's 13-10 defeat, via Kansas City's KSHB 41:

"Not [a call] that I agreed with, but yeah. This came from New York, so it's not from these guys. Our guys called it a completion, the guys who were working the game. I'm not quite sure what they saw. I saw he had control of the ball and his elbow hit and then the ball hit, but he had control.

"Normally, in those kind of situations, it's gotta be clear and obvious. To me, that was not clear and obvious."

Rashee Rice appeared to have a first down for the Chiefs. The NFL's replay center disagreed.

Mahomes' wife Brittany, meanwhile, used the occasion to dispute the idea the Chiefs have benefited from calls during their recent run, writing the following on her Instagram story:

"Guess we can't even play football without getting it called against us… not sure what an actual catch is after that I guess? I don't wanna hear s*** anymore from anyone"

She also posted a collection of social media posts criticizing the call to her story.

NFL vice president of instant replay Mark Butterworth spoke to a pool reporter, pointing to Rice's hand moving up the ball as evidence that he lost control.

To be clear, this play did not singlehandedly cost the Chiefs the game, though it's undeniably frustrating. Had the Rice catch stood, they still would have needed to travel half the field to reach field-goal range, on a day in which struggling kicker Harrison Butker made one field goal from 26 yards and missed another from 46.

Facing fourth-and-10, Kansas City was instead forced to punt out of its own end zone. The teams immediately exchanged three-and-outs and appeared headed to overtime, only for field position to bite the Chiefs again when a disastrous punt sequence put the Bills in field-goal position for the final play.
