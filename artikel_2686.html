What channel is the international NFL game on today? Time, schedule to watch Patriots-Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Week 10 of the NFL season brings an international showdown as the New England Patriots take on the Detroit Lions at Allianz Arena in Munich, Germany. This matchup is one of the final two games on the NFL's international schedule for the season.

This marks the Patriots' second appearance in Germany, and New England will be desperate to bounce back after a 45-27 defeat at the hands of the Green Bay Packers in Week 9. Quarterback Drake Maye endured a difficult outing, throwing three interceptions and completing just 21 of 38 passes for 299 yards and three touchdowns.

The Lions, meanwhile, head to Munich on the heels of a four-game win streak after a 19-9 victory over the Miami Dolphins in Week 9. Quarterback Jared Goff faced a challenging day, finishing with 170 yards on 17-of-25 passing, while being sacked three times and picked off by rookie cornerback Chris Johnson early in the game. However,  kicker Jake Bates nailed four field goals to secure the win.
