Sandro Mamukelashvili was in the gym with Flava Flav trying to break out of his slump

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Since going a combined 10-of-16 from deep against the Atlanta Hawks and Milwaukee Bucks, which raised his season-long 3-point percentage to 41.2, Sandro Mamukelashvili has not made a triple try. Over the Los Angeles Lakers' last five games, the Georgian big man is 0-of-16 beyond the arc.

Prior to Monday's win over the Utah Jazz, head coach JJ Redick detailed the approach he's taking to try and help Mamukelashvili snap out of his drought from deep, which includes his own experiences as an NBA player and wisdom shared by one of the league's sharpest coaches.

"I think, right now, with Sandro is us getting to just know each other and understand each other. Part of my job is to understand the psyche of each and every one of my players and why is that important? So that in moments like this I can help them," Redick told reporters. "[Erik Spoelstra] told me one time it's more art than science, Really, just trying to help him. I shared with him some stories and we actually went on Basketball-Reference the other night and went through some game logs for my career. It's funny because I was like, 'My 5th year I started 3-of-25 and shot 39.7%. My second year with the Clippers. I think I had a 4-of-28 stretch to start the season. I shot 44%' and he's like, 'Oh, I know..I went and looked. I went and looked.' So, I think him and I are on the same page."

Following Wednesday's loss to the Jazz, when Mamukelashvili missed all four of his long-range attempts and was 1-of-10 overall from the floor, he got shots up at the practice facility until 2:30 a.m. Joining Mamukelashvili in the dark of night was Flava Flav, who subdued his typically boisterous personality as his friend aimed to snap out of a shooting funk.

"He's always so loud, but he saw how intense I was," Mamukelashvili told reporters on Monday morning. "So he was like **whispers** 'yeahh boyy'"

Mamukelashivili will hope his early morning shooting session alongside Flava Flav and conversations with Redick finally turn the tide when Los Angeles returns to the floor Friday night against the San Antonio Spurs.
