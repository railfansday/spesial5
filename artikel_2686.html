Sean McVay casts doubt on Puka Nacua’s Rams future after season-ending surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Puka Nacua's season is over. Whether his future with the Los Angeles Rams is headed toward the same fate remains an uncomfortable question nobody inside the organization seems ready to answer yet.

With Nacua entering the final months of his rookie contract, Rams head coach Sean McVay was pressed Monday about the star wide receiver's future following season-ending MCL surgery that will sideline him through the playoffs.

Los Angeles Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua (12) warms up before an NFL football game against the Los Angeles Chargers in Inglewood, Calif., Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026. AP Photo/Caroline Brehman

McVay confirmed the surgery went well and expects Nacua to be ready for the 2027 season. But when asked whether he could guarantee Nacua would still be wearing a Rams uniform next year, the answer wasn't exactly reassuring.

"I can't say for certain anything," McVay said.

Nacua's future has become one of the Rams' biggest offseason questions after a turbulent season filled with injuries, a three-game NFL suspension and off-field controversy.

McVay acknowledged the challenges his young receiver has faced but remained steadfast in his support.

"You love the human," McVay said. "I think he's growing and I trust his heart. I think he's making a lot of steps that are in the right direction."

That affection, however, doesn't automatically translate into a contract extension.

The Rams have a laundry list of impending free agents, including several offensive linemen, defensive contributors and players from Nacua's draft class.

Los Angeles Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua reacts after winning an NFL football game against the Washington Commanders in Landover, Md., Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026. AP Photo/Stephanie Scarbrough

"There are mechanics that we'd like to be able to try to figure out how to keep these really good players and people that we love," McVay said. "Certainly Puka is at the forefront of our mind."

For now, McVay insists his attention remains on Wednesday night's critical NFC showdown against the Green Bay Packers.

But once the season ends, the Rams face a potentially franchise-altering decision.

"He's obviously a guy that is a very important piece for us for now and for the future, but there are a lot of guys that fall under that category," said McVay.

Nacua has already lost the remainder of 2026. Whether Los Angeles is willing and able to keep him beyond that remains to be seen.
