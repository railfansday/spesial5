Mikal Bridges buzzer-beater, Victor Wembanyama monster stats highlight NBA action

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

There were six NBA games on Saturday, Nov. 21, which is a lighter schedule for the 30-team league.

But fewer games did not mean a shortage of action. Stars across the country had impressive nights, from career-highs to a thrilling buzzer-beater.

Victor Wembanyama had a monster night by scoring 50 points as the anchor of an incredibly rare statline to keep the San Antonio Spurs' momentum strong.

Tyler Herro also scored 50 points, which was the Milwaukee Bucks star's career-high.

Elsewhere, Mikal Bridges wowed with a buzzer-beater to give the New York Knicks their fourth straight win.

Here's a breakdown of the NBA highlights from the day:

Wembanyama proved why his nickname is the Alien in the Spurs' 114-91 win over the New Orleans Pelicans. The San Antonio big man posted 50 points, 19 rebounds, four steals and five blocks in the effort.

According to the NBA, Wembanyama is the first player since the 1973-74 season to notch 50 points, at least 15 boards, four steals and five blocks in one game. ESPN adds that he is the first player in league history to have 50 points, five blocks and five made 3-point shots in a single matchup.

He is also the third San Antonio player to record multiple 50-point games in the same season. He had 52 points and 24 rebounds earlier this month in a win over the Dallas Mavericks.

The Spurs have won 14 of their last 15 games and had a 12-game winning streak that was broken earlier in the week.

The national hype might be around Herro's former team, the Miami Heat, with the release of "Grand Theft Auto 6." But the Bucks guard made basketball fans pay attention to him when he scored a career-high 50 points. He added 11 rebounds and almost scored the game-tying 3-point bucket, but his shot was milliseconds too late and the Bucks lost to LeBron James and the Philadelphia 76ers 132-129.

According to the Bucks, Herro, who joined the Heat in the Giannis Antetokounmpo trade, is the first player in NBA history to have at least 50 points, 11 boards, seven 3-point shots, and shoot 65% from the field in a single game.

Joel Embiid led the 76ers with 26 points, 17 rebounds and nine assists.

Mikal Bridges wowed by scoring the game-winning shot for the Knicks at the buzzer.

With 3.2 seconds left, he took the inbound from Josh Hart, dribbled and took a side step in front of Portland Trail Blazers forward Toumani Camara and then launched a shot from behind the arc that drained the net smoothly.

The Knicks beat the Trail Blazers 97-96 after Portland came back from a 26-point deficit. Bridges finished with 12 points and Finals MVP Jalen Brunson led New York with 30 points and seven rebounds.
