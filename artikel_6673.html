Amanda Seyfried Makes the Case for Fall Florals in a Sheer Carolina Herrera Dress

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The actor joined Jimmy Fallon in an embellished runway piece

Florals... for fall? While not quite groundbreaking, per say, the pattern is certainly not as common during the chilly months as it might be in the springtime. But leave it to Amanda Seyfried to show us how it’s done.

For an appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon, the actor wore a black Carolina Herrera midi dress that, despite being from Wes Gordon’s Spring/Summer 2027 collection, felt like an appropriate choice for November.

Covered in 3D flowers, the sleeveless number played with texture and depth. It was crafted from a sheer mesh base and stitched with black flower-shaped crochet appliqués, each adorned with a cluster of small snow-colored beads that brought in a bit of winter white.

Seyfried paired the embroidered dress with shimmering Christian Louboutin Miss Z pumps, crafted with a similar sheer base and embellished with crystals and black beads. The signature red sole gave the look an extra holiday-ready touch, and the star finished things off with a pair of diamond studs from Tiffany & Co.

The Mamma Mia! actor joined Fallon for a conversation about how she got involved in Nathan Fielder’s Elizabeth Holmes documentary, You Can See Everything. “It was never my intention to recreate Elizabeth Holmes ever again,” Seyfried, who previously portrayed the Theranos founder in biopic series The Dropout, told the host. “But Nathan Fielder, the director, reached out and said, ‘Hey, I’d love to pick your brain.’”

Continuing, she revealed that the two talked on FaceTime for an hour, before Fielder asked if she would be comfortable being filmed watching the footage. “A couple weeks later, I was watching the footage with four cameras in my apartment,” she revealed. “I was like, ‘How did I get here?’”
