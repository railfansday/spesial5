Anne Hathaway Gives Birth to Her Third Child

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Congratulations are in order for Anne Hathaway! The actor has welcomed her third child with husband Adam Shulman, making them a family of five.

On Tuesday, November 17, Hathaway was photographed out and about in New York City with a newborn nestled against her chest in a baby carrier. People has since independently confirmed the happy news. The baby’s name, sex, and exact birth date have not yet been shared.

Hathaway and Shulman, who married in 2012, are already parents to two boys: Jonathan, 10, and Jack, who was born in November 2019. The couple have largely kept their children out of the public eye.

The Oscar winner first announced her third pregnancy in June, with a sweet Instagram video soundtracked by Barbara Lewis’s “Baby I’m Yours.” Wearing a flowing white linen two-piece by Nothing Fits But and gold-embellished Cult Gaia sandals, Hathaway walked into the frame, revealed her growing bump, beamed at the camera, and ran playfully out of sight. “x Baby, I’m yours x,” she captioned the post. During an appearance on Late Night With Seth Meyers the following month, she affectionately referred to the surprise addition as the couple’s “buzzer-beater” baby.

It’s been a pregnancy style journey for Annie. Working with stylist Erin Walsh, Hathaway delivered some of the year’s most memorable maternity looks: goddess-like custom Dior and Prada gowns on The Odyssey press tour, as well as more playful, bump-baring separates, and an ethereal lilac Alexander McQueen dress during New York Fashion Week. She rounded out her run of red carpets at the Verity premiere in September, wearing a dramatic black sequined tulle Prabal Gurung gown.

Indeed, if anyone has earned a little downtime, it’s Anne Hathaway. The actor has had an extraordinarily busy 2026, with five films released this year alone: Mother Mary, The Devil Wears Prada 2, The Odyssey, The End of Oak Street, and Verity. Between reprising her beloved role as Andy Sachs, playing Penelope in Christopher Nolan’s Greek epic, and diving deep into Colleen Hoover’s much hyped psychological thriller, Hathaway has scarcely stopped the promo tour.

But now, she’s looking forward to some time away from the spotlight. Speaking to Vogue at the Verity premiere, Hathaway shared that her favorite moment of the entire year had been discovering she was pregnant, and the forthcoming opportunity to take a pause. “I’m really excited to meet my baby, and to be in that little bubble in time,” she said.

Five films plus a fabulous maternity wardrobe, and a new baby. Congratulations once again to Anne Hathaway and Adam Schulman!

Vogue has reached out to Anne Hathaway’s reps for comment.
