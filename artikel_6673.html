Robert Pattinson Is the Next James Bond

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After a whirlwind month of speculation, unsubstantiated rumors, and endless Kalshi prognostication, white smoke has finally emerged from the Amazon MGM chimney. There’s a new James Bond—and his name is Robert Pattinson.

The announcement ends the long-running—and quite frankly, exhausting—mystery over Daniel Craig’s successor and starts a new creative era for the 007 franchise, which Amazon acquired from longtime Bond custodians Michael G. Wilson and Barbara Broccoli in February 2025, four years after No Time to Die was released. Now, Pattinson will tux up as the seventh cinematic Bond under the stewardship of super-producers David Heyman and Amy Pascal and the vision of GQ’s Director of the Year Denis Villeneuve, marking arguably the biggest role of the 40-year-old London-born actor’s career.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime,” Pattinson said in a statement to The Hollywood Reporter. “I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya, and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”

Rumors of Pattinson’s selection caught fire in October, when delays on The Batman Part II gave him a window to audition for Bond at Berkshire’s Bray Studios, alongside peers Callum Turner, Josh O’Connor, Jack Lowden, and an unnamed fourth actor. The chatter heightened a couple weeks later when various British tabloids suggested that Turner, O’Connor, and Lowden were each the preferred choice to satisfy the demands and responsibilities of the beloved British action hero—heightening suspicion that a final decision was looming. According to THR, the finalists were judged “based on stunts, camera presence.” But Pattinson’s last-minute arrival clearly tipped the scale.

“Before anything else, I want to express my deep gratitude to the remarkable actors who joined us on this journey. We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007,” said Villeneuve in a statement to THR. “I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin.”

It’s both a particularly inspired and obvious choice. Pattinson’s physical traits and characteristics hue close to Ian Fleming’s original Bond—tall and slim, with dark hair. He’s already a bona fide movie star, the current face of DC Studios’ Batman franchise; meanwhile, the selection catches him at the tail end of another booked-and-busy year. Unlike his auditioning peers, the acclaimed actor will enter the franchise well-experienced working in IP and with big-name directors. After co-leading this spring’s The Drama, he chewed up scenes as Antinous in Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey (the second time working with Nolan after Tenet), made a creepy turn as Chris Hansen in Primetime, and will close 2026 as Scytale in Dune: Part 3—now the first of future Villeneuve collaborations, a relationship that surely helped him acquire his license to kill.

“James Bond is one of the most beloved, inspiring, iconic, and yet enigmatic characters in literature and movies,” Pascal and Heyman stated jointly to THR. “The legacy of this character is in great hands with Robert Pattinson. His fearlessness as an actor, his self-deprecating wit, and his ability to continually reinvent himself make him the perfect Bond for our times.”

The decision to hire a movie star isn’t a risky choice, but there’s not much precedent within the franchise—especially recently. When he succeeded Pierce Brosnan as Bond in 2005, beating out Clive Owen, Michael Fassbender, and Henry Cavill, Craig boasted an under-the-radar resume. He’d just come off Matthew Vaughn’s crime thriller Layer Cake, but otherwise had played supporting roles in Road to Perdition, The Mother, and Enduring Love. His only big-budget studio experience came several years earlier as Angelina Jolie’s love interest in Tomb Raider.

Naturally, the British press drew its fangs upon his selection, critiquing everything from his general obscurity to his physical features, which stood in stark contrast to previous Bond actors. “The Mane’s Blond, James Blond,” chirped The Irish Times, while The London Daily Mirror played with the same pun: “The Name’s Bland … James Bland.” At his official introduction to the press along the Thames, Craig arrived on a speedboat wearing a life vest, attracting instant mockery—the first of several digs about his less-than-sleek aesthetic and physical abilities. (One tabloid insulted an on-set sunburn and his inability to drive a stick shift car.)

Pattinson, however, has mostly conjured genuine excitement—not in spite of, but because of, his extreme familiarity with moviegoers, dating back to his Young Adult days in the Harry Potter and Twilight franchises. Since then, over the last decade-plus, his eccentric characters and commanding screen presence immediately qualifies him as someone “stirring and exciting and different,” descriptors that Pascal said she prioritized in their methodical search.

Thanks to director Martin Campbell, Casino Royale returned the Bond franchise to earth and established Craig as a grounded, gritty action star in the mold of Jason Bourne. The movie ended up raking in $616 million globally, the highest earning Bond film to date, and launched Craig into four more sequels. Similarly, Pattinson will have the benefit of a highly-respected, Oscar-nominated director in his corner with experience shepherding IP into new places and eliciting compelling performances about tortured male protagonists.

With Villeneuve at the helm of this 26th installment, don’t expect another hard creative pivot; the man behind Prisoners, Blade Runner 2049, and Dune wasn’t hired to bring back the colorful, absurdist, gadget-heavy era that Austin Powers parodied throughout the 90’s. Still, new leadership means a new direction—a clean slate for Pattinson to burnish an already diverse, robust career and prove his chops again at the highest level of scrutiny.
