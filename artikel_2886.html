Jeffries wins Democratic House speaker race, on track to make history in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries won the nod from his caucus to become the next speaker of the House of Representatives.

Jeffries, D-N.Y., still needs to be officially elected when the new Congress convenes in January. But he appears to be on a glide path to winning the gavel after Democrats voted 251-11 to nominate him, according to a person familiar with the vote who asked not to be named because the meeting was private.

The win for Jeffries comes after months of debate over whether Democrats would stand by their existing leadership or oust them amid a progressive tide. Democrats trounced Republicans in the midterm elections and are poised to hold at least 257 House seats.

Jeffries would make history as the first Black speaker of the House if he is elected in January.
