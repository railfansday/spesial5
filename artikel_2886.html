Cavs react to Sam Merrill’s tough missed free throws: ‘We all trust him’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

CLEVELAND — There's no tougher way to lose a basketball game than at the free-throw line. It's even worse when the culprit is arguably the best shooter on your team.

For Sam Merrill and the Cleveland Cavaliers, tonight was one of those losses.

Merrill came up empty on a pair of free throws with the Cavs trailing by one point to the Memphis Grizzlies and under 5 seconds to play. That isn't what you expect from a career 89% free-throw shooter.

Even so, the Cavs aren't upset with their sharpshooter.

"I mean, there's a reason I made the pass [to Merrill]," said Donovan Mitchell. "We all trust him, you know, and I would do it again tomorrow; I'd do it again the next night; you're not defined by two free throws at the end of the game."

Sometimes you make the right play and things don't work out. Mitchell was doubled by Memphis in the final seconds; that's why Merrill had the ball and was crashing towards the rim with a trailing defender. It was the correct approach from everyone — the final shots just didn't go in.

"I love the process; I love that he drove, he's so smart to drive the ball, that's what I look at," said Kenny Atkinson. "This is the sport, right? It happens to the best, but I loved how he got to the line."

None of that makes the loss feel any better. But, for what it's worth, the Cavs feel comfortable with the opportunity they had in front of them. It seems like they'll win that scenario more often than not.

It's also true that Cleveland had other avenues towards winning tonight's game. They couldn't get key stops in the fourth quarter, and Merrill wasn't the only player who missed a shot.

"I told him we're not in that situation if I knock down the corner three or if I hit the floater that I missed," said Mitchell. "So we can all point to something."

The beauty of basketball is another opportunity is almost always right around the corner. The Cavs have a fast turnaround with another home game against the Indiana Pacers on Friday.

"For [Merrill], go get 30 in a half against Indiana, and we'll be fine," Mitchell joked.
