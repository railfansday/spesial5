Are the Packers playing today? NFL Week 11 schedule, next game,

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Green Bay Packers could not have picked a better week to rest.

Green Bay is off in Week 11, and after what the Minnesota Vikings did to Jordan Love last week, nobody in that locker room is complaining. In 56 starts, Love had never been sacked more than five times. Minnesota got him eight times on Nov. 15.

The 30-10 loss at Lambeau Field left the Packers 6-4 and alone at the bottom of the NFC North. Love finished 17-of-27 passing for 220 yards with a touchdown and an interception. Green Bay turned it over four times, ran for 56 yards and never led. Kirk Cousins, five days into his second stint with Minnesota after a deadline trade with the Las Vegas Raiders, threw two touchdown passes.

The bye buys Love a week to heal and LaFleur a week to fix his line. The Packers are back on the field Nov. 25, the night before Thanksgiving, against the Los Angeles Rams.
