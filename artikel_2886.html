Steelers activate WR Roman Wilson ahead of Eagles matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Steelers are getting one of their top wide receivers back ahead of Week 11's matchup against the Philadelphia Eagles.

Pittsburgh announced Saturday that wide receiver Roman Wilson has been activated to the 53-man roster ahead of Sunday's game. In addition, the team announced the signing of edge rusher K.J. Henry from the practice squad to the roster. The two signings fill the open spots on the Steelers' roster.

The Henry signing follows the Steelers announcing outside linebacker Nick Herbig as out for Sunday's game. Now, Pittsburgh has an open slot on its practice squad.

Besides adding Wilson and Henry to the roster, the Steelers also announced that wide receiver Max Hurleman and offensive lineman Doug Nester have been elevated from the practice squad to the active/inactive roster ahead of the Eagles game. Both will revert back to the practice squad following the game.

Both Wilson and Hurleman were recently on injured reserve for the Steelers. Wide receiver Michael Pittman Jr. and offensive lineman Gennings Dunker remain on IR but in their 21-day return windows.
