Ty Snell, Allen continue to roll after dominant bi-district win over Hebron

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

ALLEN – In the eyes of Allen quarterback Ty Snell there were small things that could have been better on Thursday for his team. The casual observer would have needed a microscope to see the flaws the Eagles made.

Allen just about had a perfect first half, with Snell leading the way to a 56-0 halftime lead and eventually a 73-0 rout of Hebron in a Class 6A Division I bi-district playoff game at Eagle Stadium. Snell piloted an Allen offense that was razor sharp in the first half, scoring touchdowns on all six of its drives. It was Allen's 10th consecutive game to score at least 50 points.

Snell was 18 for 23 passing for 280 yards for a season-high five touchdowns in the first half and added a one-yard pass in a handful of plays in the third quarter. The sophomore also had 41 yards rushing on four carries.

"We played sharp because of our preparation and our coaches are amazing," Snell said "We know where we want to go at the end of the day, so we prepare like that.

"I wouldn't say the first half was as good as we could play. We left a lot out on the table. We had small mistakes and mental errors."

Allen (11-0), ranked No. 2 in The Dallas Morning News' Class 6A area poll and the Associated Press' 6A state poll, advances to play the winner of Friday's bi-district playoff between Euless Trinity and Plano East. Allen improved to 18-1 in bi-district playoff games since 2008. Hebron (5-6) has lost eight consecutive games to Allen, the last three in the postseason.

The Allen High School football team takes the field before kickoff as Allen High School hosted Hebron High School in a 6A, Division I, bi-district playoff game at Eagle Stadium in Allen on Thursday, November 12, 2026. (Stewart F. House/The Dallas Morning News)

Six different receivers caught passes from Snell in the first half, with the most successful being Zach Williams. Snell threw a precise, 45-yard scoring bomb to Williams to give Allen a 28-0 lead with 8:50 left in the second quarter. Williams had seven receptions in the first half for 123 yards and three touchdowns, his totals for the game.

Allen's defense was also dominant in the first half, adding in two interception returns for touchdowns. Tyler Shaw got a 46-yard pick six to give Allen a 42-0 led with 4:35 left in the first half, a week after he had a fumble return for a touchdown in the second quarter of Allen's 63-7 win over Prosper Walnut Grove. Defensive tackle Beckham Parker had two tackles for a loss in the first half, including a sack as Allen held Hebron to 42 total yards in the first half and 180 for the game.

Reserve quarterback Devorian Hambric led Allen with 70 yards rushing on three carries, including a 30-yard run to set the game's final score: a three-yard touchdown run by Julian Jackson with 2:47 remaining.
