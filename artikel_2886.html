Ty Gibbs Is Heading to Australia to Race a V8 Supercar That Won 7 Races

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Ty Gibbs will race a V8 Supercar in the Australian Touring Car Classics at Sandown, near Melbourne, Dec. 4-6, in a one-off start before his 2027 NASCAR season.

The Joe Gibbs Racing driver will be in an older Triple Eight-built Holden VF Commodore fielded by Eggleston Motorsport, with sponsorship from Monster Energy and his No. 54 on the doors, Supercars announced Nov. 19. Sandown International Motor Raceway is in Springvale, Victoria.

The car, chassis 888A-040, made its debut with Jamie Whincup and Paul Dumbrell in the 2015 Sandown 500 and won seven Supercars Championship races, V8 Sleuth reported.

Gibbs accepted an invitation from Australian driver Jack Perkins, who made two O'Reilly Auto Parts Series starts for JGR in 2025 and worked with him on road and street courses this season. Perkins and the Eggleston team set up the Australian Touring Car Classics to bring previous-generation V8 Supercars back to the track, and Sandown is its first event.

"This is such a cool opportunity. I've been really keen to race Aussie V8 Supercars, having been racing and driving them on iRacing for years now. Jack tells me the Sandown circuit is one of the best in Australia, his family has a lot of history there, and I'm really looking forward to the weekend and spending some time down under," Gibbs said.

"Having Ty Gibbs, current NASCAR driver and race winner, in an ex-Triple Eight-built Aussie Commodore on the grid at Sandown is just an awesome opportunity for everyone. We've become great mates, and for him to experience a Supercar at such an iconic track like Sandown will be special. It's a great fit for the Australian Touring Car Classics," Perkins said.

The trip caps the best Cup season of Gibbs' career. The 24-year-old earned his first Cup win at Bristol in April and added a second at Iowa in August, holding off Christopher Bell.

He made the Chase and finished ninth in the final 2026 standings. Over the summer, he scored more points than anyone across a five-race stretch from Sonoma to Indianapolis.

Gibbs, the grandson of team owner Joe Gibbs, already has two championships. He won the 2022 NASCAR Xfinity Series title, in what is now the O'Reilly Series, and the 2021 ARCA Menards Series title, when he won 10 of 20 races.

The trip reverses the path Shane van Gisbergen took to NASCAR. He won three Supercars titles and three Bathurst 1000s before moving to NASCAR full time. He won in his Cup debut at the 2023 Chicago Street Race.

Van Gisbergen has eight Cup wins, all on road courses and street circuits. Gibbs will now try the type of car van Gisbergen raced in Australia, on a circuit where braking and shifting matter far more than on an oval.

The Sandown event comes a little over two months before the 2027 NASCAR season starts in February.
