Joe Lacob talks Juste Jocyte’s future after Natalie Nakase drama

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Golden State Valkyries owner Joe Lacob isn't shutting the door on Juste Jocyte.

Juste Jocyte didn't bite her tongue after spending basically the entire WNBA Finals glued to the pine, seeing just six total minutes on the floor.

The 20-year-old made her feelings known in an interview with the Lithuanian media, making it clear she was miserable with how Natalie Nakase used her.

Now, heading into the offseason, Jocyte is putting the Valkyries front office on notice, telling them she has zero interest in riding the bench next year, and she won't stick around without real minutes.

Joe Lacob is trying to play damage control before the situation spirals. Instead of taking the bait and firing back at Jocyte's comments, the Valkyries owner is playing the long game, making it clear he doesn't want to let the 20-year-old walk away.

He said: "I kind of understand she's competitive and would like to win and didn't play. But she's also only 20. We had a pretty great roster, very deep. I think she can be a very good player and we'd really love to have her back.

"So I think she will certainly earn a lot more playing time in the future. She didn't get to play as much as she wanted to, and that's understandable.

"I think she can be a very good player and we would really love to have her back."

Even with Lacob trying to play nice, it's hard to picture Jocyte sticking around in the Bay. That bridge with Nakase is burned, and Golden State isn't about to let locker room bad blood poison the culture they're building. Once trust is shot, a clean break is the only realistic way out.
