Sixers host Milwaukee Bucks for afternoon game

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Philadelphia 76ers are coming off several strong performances, stringing together wins and most recently earning a comeback victory over the Orlando Magic despite missing several key players. Now, they'll return to Philly to host the Milwaukee Bucks for an early afternoon affair.

The Bucks, as things stand, sit in the basement of the Eastern Conference with a 1-15 record. Fresh off trading Giannis Antetokounmpo to Miami, they've kicked off a rebuild focused on players such as Tyler Herro, Jaime Jaquez Jr., Kel'el Ware and Ryan Rollins. They also added Brayden Burries and Nate Ament as two lottery picks in a stacked draft, and now they're looking to right the ship following the departure of their franchise star.

As their record suggests, things have not gone smoothly. Myles Turner, who remains with the team, has also endured a rough start to the season amid the turmoil. While all of the young players mentioned above have shown flashes of their potential, the Bucks are still far from being a consistent, winning basketball team.

The Sixers, on the other hand, are finding their groove and sit atop the Eastern Conference. They're coming off a strong win, with several of their key players filling up the box score. LeBron James has begun to find his rhythm, while Joel Embiid has played a crucial role in several of their recent victories. VJ Edgecombe, playing without Tyrese Maxey in their last game, was instrumental in their win over Orlando, hitting what ultimately proved to be the game-winning shot while making his presence felt on both ends of the floor.

There are a few player statuses to monitor heading into this one, but the sophomore has proven time and time again that he can step up and hit big shots when called upon. The same can be said for the Sixers' backcourt rotation, which also features Anfernee Simons and rookie Labaron Philon. Philon saw meaningful minutes in their last game and posted solid numbers in limited playing time.

Even Tacko Fall logged meaningful minutes, providing a crucial presence in the frontcourt. The Bucks have no shortage of frontcourt players to throw at the Sixers, and this could be another contest where Fall has an opportunity to solidify his role amid the team's myriad injuries.

Speaking of injuries, there are a few to monitor heading into this game. Jaylen Brown will miss the matchup as he recovers from a hamstring strain, while Maxey is listed as questionable with a foot strain. Dominick Barlow is probable and could make his season debut, while Adem Bona and Dean Wade remain out. For Milwaukee, Ousmane Dieng is also out.

All of this presents a great opportunity for the Sixers to extend their winning streak against a less talented opponent. Games like this won't come often on their schedule, and they'd be wise to take advantage. Early afternoon games can always get weird quickly, but the Sixers have a golden opportunity to add another win and maintain their early-season momentum. Let's see if they can get it done.

When: Saturday, November 20, 1 p.m. ETWhere: Xfinity Mobile Arena, Philadelphia, PA Watch: NBC Sports PhiladelphiaRadio: 97.5 The FanaticFollow: @LibertyBallers
