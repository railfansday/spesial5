Packers work out quarterbacks and tight ends

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

On Monday, the Green Bay Packers worked out six players, all either quarterbacks or tight ends. Currently, the team does not have an open roster spot on the active roster, but they do on the practice squad. In theory, they have space to add, at least until running back Chris Brooks and edge defender Micah Parsons return to the 53-man roster, which could displace someone to the practice squad.

Carter turned down college scholarships to power programs like Indiana, Mississippi, Pittsburgh, Rutgers, West Virginia and Wisconsin to start his college football career at Toledo, where he posted a 5-5 record before transferring to South Alabama for his final two seasons of eligibility. In the Sun Belt, he was named an all-conference player, earning him an opportunity with the Las Vegas Raiders as an undrafted free agent in 2024.

He's also had previous experience with the San Francisco 49ers and Jacksonville Jaguars, whose head coaches come from the same coaching tree as head coach Matt LaFleur. So far, Carter — the son of long-time defensive coordinator Gus Bradley — has yet to throw a regular-season pass.

This is Carter's second workout since he was waived with an injury settlement at the cutdown deadline. His other workout came with the Arizona Cardinals, coached by LaFleur's brother.

Morton was previously a Green Bay Packer, as he signed with the team as an undrafted free agent back in 2020. He was cut during the preseason, but he eventually signed a reserve-futures deal with the Indianapolis Colts for the 2021 season, and he was waived again during the preseason.

Since his stints in the NFL, he's gone to play with the Hamilton Tiger-Cats (CFL), Birmingham Stallions (USFL/UFL), Southwest Kansas Storm (CIF), Houston Roughnecks (UFL) and Columbus Aviators (UFL).

Notably, he's a running threat, which could be one reason why the Packers want to add him before facing the likes of Kyler Murray and/or Carson Wentz. This is Morton's first workout with an NFL team since the Aviators' season concluded in the spring.

Last season, Morton was 31 of 72 for 433 yards, four touchdowns and three interceptions for the Aviators. He also ran the ball 25 times for 140 yards and two touchdowns.

Davidson is a former college punter who received all-conference honors at the Division II level before also contributing as a tight end later in his Central Missouri career. He was drafted in the fifth round of the 2021 draft by the Minnesota Vikings, but he didn't make it through final cuts and spent his rookie season on their practice squad.

Since then, he's also been a member of the Buffalo Bills, New York Giants and Louisville Kings (UFL). In NFL regular seasons, he's played three games, playing 49 offensive snaps and 26 special teams snaps.

In the 2026 UFL season, Davidson caught 19 balls for 250 yards and two touchdowns for the league-winning Kings. After the UFL season, he's worked out with the New York Giants, Pittsburgh Steelers and Miami Dolphins.

Fletcher is a rookie undrafted free agent who moved from defensive end to the offensive side of the ball in 2024 while at Rutgers. He was not a highly touted UDFA in the 2026 class, signing for just $3,000 guaranteed with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers — the lowest rate of any tight end who received any guarantees in this class.

He was waived by the Buccaneers at the cutdown deadline, but he later spent about a month with the Indianapolis Colts' practice squad. Since being let go by the Colts, he also worked out with the Seattle Seahawks.

Kuntz is a former draft pick who had a lot of hype coming into the league as a raw project due to his athleticism. He originally was a major recruit for Penn State before transferring to Old Dominion. He's since kicked around on the New York Jets, Miami Dolphins, Las Vegas Raiders and Dallas Cowboys, but has only played two regular-season games — most recently in 2024 — with 10 of his 13 snaps on special teams.

Since being let go by the Cowboys at the cutdown deadline, he's also had a workout with the Indianapolis Colts.

Rogers was a former super recruit who was a one-year starter for the TCU Horned Frogs in 2025. In undrafted free agency, he signed with the Dallas Cowboys for $200,000 guaranteed, the fifth-highest mark among 28 UDFA tight ends. For perspective, the Packers signed their UDFA tight end, R.J. Maryland, for $15,000 guaranteed.

In the preseason, Rogers sprained his MCL, leading to him being waived with an injured designation at the cutdown deadline. Because he wasn't claimed, he reverted back to the Cowboys' injured reserve, where he was waived with an injury settlement in late October.

This appears to be Rodgers' first workout since being let go by Dallas.
