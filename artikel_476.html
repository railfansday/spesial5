National Fried Chicken Sandwich Day 2026 deals: Score free food from Burger King, KFC, McDonalds, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

National Sandwich Day took place on Nov. 3, but sandwich lovers have another chance to secure free food this month.

National Fried Chicken Sandwich Day (Nov. 9) is here. We know you've had this date circled in your calendars all year. Forget Thanksgiving and Christmas. This is obviously the date that matters.

A number of popular chains are celebrating this special occasion by launching BOGO offers, limited-edition products, and genuinely free deals. We've scanned the market to bring you a selection of standout promotions.

B-Dubs Rewards members can pick up a free classic or spicy chicken sandwich when they pair it with any $10 food or drink purchase.

Royal Perks members can get a BOGO offer on the elongated Original Chicken Sandwich, or claim a free Royal Crispy Chicken Sandwich with a qualifying digital purchase of $3 to $5.

My Rewards members can get a BOGO $1 Hand-Breaded Chicken Sandwich deal valid across Original, Nashville Hot, or bacon-packed BLT builds.

Jollibee Rewards members unlock an instant BOGO deal across all signature chicken sandwiches.

Place an order of $10 or more on the KFC website or official mobile app to get a free Extra Crispy Chicken Sandwich at checkout.

Make a $5+ minimum order, and you’ll automatically unlock a free Original or Spicy Chik sandwich.

Claim a $2 classic or spicy McCrispy sandwich through the McDonald's app.

Rewards members spending $10 or more online or via the app can snag a free full-size chicken sandwich.

Spend $10 or more on digital orders and enter coupon code SCARYGOOD at checkout to unlock a free chicken sandwich. The offer is active through Nov. 10.

Loyalty members can get a BOGO chicken sandwich reward, available to claim digitally or scan in-store at participating storefronts.

Get a free housemade drink when you order a fried chicken sandwich, plate, or bowl with Katsu.

Urban Bird is running BOGO chicken sandos all day on Nov. 9, for both dine-in guests and digital carryout orders.

Rewards members ordering digitally through the app on Nov. 9 can score a $2 Signature Whatachick’n or Spicy Chicken Sandwich.

Rewards members can grab a free chicken sandwich in any of the chain’s 12 signature dry rubs or sauces with any qualifying menu purchase through Nov. 9.

Wonder is running a BOGO special on Streetbird fried chicken sandwiches across all active regional hubs, accessible directly through the Wonder app.
