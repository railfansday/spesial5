APOD: 2026 November 27 – The Moon Watchers

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Discover the cosmos! Each day a different image or photograph of our fascinating universe is featured, along with a brief explanation written by a professional astronomer.

Explanation: On November 24, all denizens of our fair planet could watch the Moon rise opposite the setting Sun. Easy to spot in twilight skies above fields, fences, backyards, and even stone walls, the Moon was big and bright, and at its full phase. Known to some as a Beaver Moon, November's Full Moon was also near perigee, the closest point in its elliptical orbit around planet Earth. That made it one of the biggest and brightest Full Moons, also known as a supermoon. In fact, November's Full Moon was the first in a series of four consecutive Full Moons near perigee.
