“Quite simply disgusting”: Gary Neville tears into Manchester United over Carrick sacking

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Graeme Hanna is the site lead of Read Rangers and also writes about West Ham for Read West Ham. A Rangers season-ticket holder and freelance writer, he has previously written for Vital Rangers, Rangers Review, Football Ground Guide and Give Me Sport, and has a long association with the Follow Follow fanzine.

Gary Neville has launched a furious attack on Manchester United’s leadership over the way the club handled Michael Carrick’s dismissal.

United confirmed on Thursday that Carrick had been sacked, four days after the 4-4 draw with Aston Villa left them 18th in the Premier League with eight points from 10 games.

The club said it had reached the “extremely difficult conclusion that a change is necessary”.

Neville had already branded the process “classless” on The Overlap’s Stick to Football podcast before the announcement, pointing out that it was widely known Carrick was about to go while United were holding talks with potential replacements.

Speaking to Sky Sports after the decision was confirmed, the former United captain went even further.

“It’s just quite simply disgusting,” he said.

“That falls well below the standard of what you would expect in any other business, in any other industry. It would be constructive dismissal and you’d be getting sued.”

Neville took particular aim at the decision to hold the crucial meeting in Monaco, with chief executive Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox flying out to meet Sir Jim Ratcliffe while Carrick was still taking training at Carrington.

“The optics of being in Monaco whilst the team, the staff, the people who are running the club on a day-to-day basis are in Manchester, it’s just not good and it reflects badly on the leadership,” he said.

It seems the only ones not to realise that are the trio above, but they aren’t stupid. How they explain it, or attempt to rationalise, it will be painful to listen to.

Neville also argued the club should have acted sooner. Looking back to the 3-0 home defeat to Bournemouth in October, he said United “effectively watched Michael Carrick resign on the pitch” as he waved goodbye to supporters at full time, only for the board to hand him three more games.

He summed up the episode as “indecision, lack of authority, just basically really poor leadership”.

It’s more than an episode, it’s an omnishambles that reflects the dysfunctional state of MUFC in its current guise.

United are now expected to move quickly for Mauricio Pochettino. The United States head coach is reported to have reached an agreement in principle with the club, which is prepared to pay his release clause.

First, they will need to wait until the USMNT completes its CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final double header against Haiti on Saturday and Wednesday.

Yes. Man United to wait until an international break to appoint an international manager…

For Neville, though, the bigger problem lies above the dugout and no manager can be the scapegoat for that massive issue.
