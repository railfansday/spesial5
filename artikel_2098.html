Arsenal face January dilemma as Liverpool and Chelsea join Alex Scott transfer race

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Arsenal have made contact with Bournemouth over a potential move for Alex Scott. Mikel Arteta may have to rethink his transfer plans if competition for the midfielder intensifies.

According to The i Paper, Arsenal, Liverpool and Chelsea have all made exploratory enquiries about the 23-year-old. The clubs want to establish what it would take to persuade Bournemouth to sell. However, none appears close to agreeing to a deal.

Arsenal are believed to be keen on a summer move. Liverpool and Chelsea are more interested in signing Scott in January. However, if they were to make a serious offer, it could be hard to hold out.

Scott has become one of the most exciting midfielders in the Premier League. He is an attractive option for clubs looking to improve their squad going forward due to his age, experience and being a homegrown player.

However, there is some uncertainty around Scott’s future. The England international has turned down two contract offers and is unwilling to sign a new deal. There are 18 months left on Scott’s contract, meaning it is an increasingly important decision for Bournemouth.

Arsenal are interested in Scott with doubts surrounding their own midfield options. There have been reports linking Martin Zubimendi and Mikel Merino to exits.

Scott would give Arteta another option, but Arsenal will have to consider if they want to make a move and give Liverpool and Chelsea an advantage.

Bournemouth are reported to want more than £100m to let him go during the January transfer window.

It would be a big asking price, especially given that Scott has 18 months left on his contract. There are a few reasons why Bournemouth could be holding out for a big fee.

The Cherries had to pay £25m to bring Scott in from Bristol City, who will receive 20 per cent of any future transfer fee. They will also have to find a suitable replacement at the halfway stage.

Reports said Chelsea received a number of offers worth around £70m in the summer, but Bournemouth have rejected claims that a bid of £80m will get them talking.

Scott is believed to be happy to stay at the club until the end of the campaign. Bournemouth are also having a great season under Marco Rose, currently sitting in seventh place in the Premier League with eight straight wins in all competitions.

Arsenal have little to hurry into any deal and could use the summer to negotiate a better deal. This is especially true with Scott’s contract situation becoming more of an issue.

The problem is that Liverpool and Chelsea are keen competitors and could lead to a bidding war in January, leaving Arsenal with little room to negotiate.

Whilst Arsenal have made their interest known, this doesn’t necessarily mean he will be moving anytime soon. If Scott is a genuine target, then they must be prepared to pounce if the situation arises.
