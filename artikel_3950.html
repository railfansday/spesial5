Aniplex and CYBIRD announce dark fantasy visual novel LunaReverse for Switch, PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Publisher Aniplex and developer CYBIRD have announced LunaReverse, a dark fantasy visual novel coming to Switch and PC via Steam. A release date was not announced. It will support English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese language options.

Here are the first details, via the game’s official website:

A dark fantasy visual novel by Aniplex and CYBIRD.

“This is the story of my revenge—and the story of my fall…

“What awaits me below? Paradise… or darkness?”
