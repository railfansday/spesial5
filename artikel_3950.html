A 17-year-old girl who had been missing for more than a year was rescued and returned home by the FBI after a friend she made while playing Among Us, and his mother, were able to track down her location and pass it along to law enforcement.

Landon Tresp told the BBC that he and the anonymous girl "kind of just clicked" when they started chatting online while playing Among Us. She eventually told Tresp she was a runaway who'd been held against her will for more than a year. After learning more, Tresp, 16, told his mother, Khristina, about the exchange; she was initially suspicious, but after speaking with the girl directly on Discord and reading reports of her disappearance, she took action, asking for a piece of mail or document with a full name on it, which the girl provided.

With that, Khristina began digging into tax and property records to find relevant addresses, which she then used to find photos on real estate sites. She showed the photos to the missing girl on Discord, who picked out the one where she was being held; with that information in hand, Khristina contacted the girl's mother on social media and spoke to her briefly on the telephone, and also received a call from the FBI asking if she could confirm that the girl was still alive.

"I said: 'I have; I video chatted with her, I've seen her'," Khristina told the BBC. "And she goes 'Oh my God, I cannot believe any of this'."

The kidnapped girl contacted Khristina the following morning, after her alleged captor, 39-year-old Adam Hunter Harmel, had left the house. In a conversation monitored by the FBI, she said she'd met Harmel on social media, where he'd pretended to be disabled and offered to pay her to help him get from Utah to Oregon. When he picked her up, however, he threw her phone into a river, then drove her to Oregon.

The girl told Khristina that she was kept in his basement and forced to sleep against the wall in his bed, while he kept a knife under his pillow to prevent escape. He gave her a mobile phone that wasn't able to make calls but could connect to wifi, which is how she was able to play online and eventually reach out for help.

While Harmel was out, law enforcement began monitoring his street and house; when the moment came, police had Khristina tell the victim that it was time to make her escape. She was able to leave the house safely and into the custody of state troopers, while Harmel was subsequently arrested and charged with transportation with intent to engage in criminal sexual activity.

A social media post by the Salt Lake City FBI confirmed the outcome, although the crucial role played by Landon and Khristina Tresp was omitted: It says only that "the FBI and the Rich County Sheriff’s Office have been actively investigating this case for more than a year and pursuing every lead received from across the US and in other countries."

A separate Department of Justice press release, however, acknowledges that "on Nov. 1, the FBI received a tip about the possible location of the minor victim and that the victim met Harmel on a social media application in May 2025."

Khristina and Landon have remained in daily contact with the girl they helped rescue, and they're trying to arrange an in-person meetup as well. "Sometimes we just sit on video chat, just so she knows she has someone that cares," Khristina said. "She was on video last night when I cooked dinner …it's nice to know that when she has a bad day or a bad moment, she can call me right away or call Landon right away. She's doing great for what she's gone through."
