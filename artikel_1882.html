Mauricio Pochettino has already broken a Premier League rule at Manchester United

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Mauricio Pochettino has already been bending Premier League rules just days into his tenure as Manchester United manager.

It has been a whirlwind start to life at Man Utd for Pochettino, who flew into the club’s training ground in Manchester on Wednesday, took charge of his first training session on Thursday - which was inadvertently overseen by a drone - and will conduct his first press conference on Friday.

Luckily, though, the Premier League have lent a a helping hand.

Under league rules, Friday press conferences must take place by 1.30pm at the latest.

However, according to Manchester Evening News, Pochettino has been given permission to start his at 2pm because of the unusual circumstances surrounding his arrival.

The Argentine will speak to the assembled media later than any other manager to discuss his first game in charge, which is a daunting trip to face Liverpool at Anfield.

It will be Pochettino’s second game inside five games, having overseen the United States national team beating Haiti on Tuesday.

But the former Tottenham and Chelsea manager is not running on fumes just yet.

“I have plenty of energy,” he said this week. “It's a privilege to be head coach of Manchester United. And we are not wasting time. We need to be ready to compete on Sunday.
