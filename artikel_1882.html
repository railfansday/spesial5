Sources: Conor Coady in contention to be Blackpool manager

Conor Coady is emerging as a leading contender to take the manager's job at League One side Blackpool, sources have told Football Insider.

The former Everton and Wolves centre-back recently retired to focus on his coaching career, and could now be in line to take the reins at Bloomfield Road.

The 33-year-old joined the England U21 setup as an assistant manager back in September, and is understood to have impressed FA officials with his credentials.

Sources say that his work with the Young Lions caught Blackpool's attention, and he is now a genuine contender for the job.

The Tangerines are languishing just outside the relegation zone in the third tier, having sacked previous boss Ian Evatt on his birthday last week.

The North West outfit were tipped for a strong campaign before the season got underway, but have now won just one of their last 12 games.

Coady was vastly experienced in leadership roles during his playing career, which saw him rack up 198 appearances in the Premier League.

He spent a prolonged period as club captain during his time at Wolves, and skippered the Black Country outfit to promotion from the Championship back in 2017-18.

However, his experience on the coaching front is limited, and Blackpool could potentially become his first-ever managerial role.

The Tangerines are in a difficult situation in the lower reaches of the League One table, and have had their squad decimated by a sickness bug in recent weeks.

They scored late to snatch a 1-1 draw with Leicester City on Tuesday night, but conceded 30 shots throughout the game on home turf.

Blackpool's hierarchy are keen to appoint Evatt's successor in the dugout sooner rather than later, and Coady is quickly emerging as a top candidate.

Blackpool recently became the latest League One struggler to opt for a change of boss, and they will hope that their decision has a similar impact to the one at Bromley.

The third tier newcomers recently appointed Karl Robinson to lead the team, and have seen the former Salford boss take four points from his first two league games in charge.

The Ravens have seen an instant boost from their change in the dugout, and that is something that Blackpool are now hoping to replicate.

They have difficult clashes against Stevenage, Luton and Oxford in their next three games, but will hope that a new boss could revitalise them.
