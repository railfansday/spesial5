Marching on together, Ao? Leeds look to tie down Tanaka to a new deal after shining this season - with Dominic Calvert-Lewin also in extension talks amid Man United interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

By SIMON JONES and TOM COLLOMOSSE, FOOTBALL REPORTER

Published: 14:08 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 03:18 EST, 25 November 2026

Leeds United are in advanced talks with Japan international Ao Tanaka over a new contract.

The 28-year-old midfielder is close to agreeing terms on a deal that will keep him at Elland Road until 2029 with his current contract due to expire in the summer of 2028.

Leeds have lost only 10 games out of the 66 Tanaka has started since joining the club from Fortuna Dusseldorf in 2024. He has had an impressive start to the current campaign, becoming a key figure in Daniel Farke's side, starting in nine of Leeds' 11 Premier League matches. They currently sit seventh in the table.

Leeds have already tied down Ethan Ampadu to a four-year deal while Joe Rodon, Jayden Bogle and Brenden Aaronson all signed new contracts during the summer transfer window.

Talks are also expected to progress over a new deal for Dominic Calvert-Lewin, who has been linked with a move to bitter rivals Manchester United as they look to bolster their striking department.

Discussing Tanaka's form ahead of their emphatic win over Chelsea at the weekend, Farke said: 'It was a big ask for him after spending many years on second-tier level in Germany or here in the Championship.

Ao Tanaka (left) is in advanced talks with Leeds United over signing a new contract until 2029

Leeds are also in talks with striker Dominic Calvert-Lewin over a new deal to stay at Elland Road

'Last season was his first year in the top league. I think he’d shown glimpses of his potential, but he needed to find a bit of consistency. I would agree that he is finding this during the season.

'It was not always a 10 out of 10 performance but I think it's important for a player, once you're mature, that on the weaker days you're still a six or a seven out of 10, and this is what he's doing.

'So perhaps even on the more challenging days, he's at least solid and plays first of all with good confidence, like he always does, but also with a seriousness to be consistent and to work for the team, also against the ball, strong in the duels.

'Still sometimes in decision-making and in front of goal he can be a bit calmer. But overall, I think he's on a really good path to mature more and more. And if you do this, then you become more and more important for us. And we're all happy that he is in such a shape at the moment.'
