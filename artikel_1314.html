Serie A | Atalanta 4-4 Inter: Incredible comeback, Pio hat-trick not enough

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Atalanta completed an incredible comeback from 3-1 down to hold Inter 4-4 despite Pio Esposito’s first Serie A hat-trick, as Charles De Ketelaere and Franck Kessie snatched a point.

The Nerazzurri had Curtis Jones, Lautaro Martinez and John Stones only fit for the bench after several weeks out, with Ange-Yoan Bonny and Aleksandar Stankovic injured. La Dea saw their disastrous run of two points from four rounds ended by a 1-0 win away to Monza going into the break for international duty.

The treatment room was finally emptying out, leaving only Isak Hien and Odilon Kossounou, as Gianluca Gaetano and Thomas Kristensen were back in the squad. However, Marco Carnesecchi pulled out in the warm-up with a knee injury, replaced by Marco Sportiello.

Atalanta have a famously poor record against Inter, scraping six draws and 11 defeats since the 4-1 victory here in November 2018.

Hakan Calhanoglu’s early effort from the D was deflected wide, but the deadlock was broken when two Nicolò Barella crosses in a row were deflected, a poor Giorgio Scalvini clearance allowing him to put the second into the path of Pio Esposito to sweep in first-time from nine yards.

It should’ve been 2-0 when Marcus Thuram mistimed his jump on a Carlos Augusto cross to waste the free header. Moments later, Josep Martinez had to parry the Nikola Krstovic strike at the near post, then stuck out a boot to clear the loose ball from Charles De Ketelaere.

Sportiello made a tricky double save on Thuram’s angled drive and the Pio Esposito follow-up, but on the resulting corner, the Italy international was allowed a free header to complete the brace.

It came at a price, because Calhanoglu limped off with a suspected flexor strain, replaced by Petar Sucic.

Just as Inter were getting back into shape, Atalanta got one back. Nicola Zalewski pulled back from the left for Eljif Elmas, whose first attempt was charged down, but he rifled the second into the roof of the net from 12 yards.

Benjamin Pavard’s header was deflected inches wide, but Pio Esposito completed his first ever Serie A hat-trick after the restart when sent through by an inspired Thuram slide-rule pass to beat Sportiello from 10 yards.

Elmas threatened to reduce the gap again on a De Ketelaere knockdown, but Thuram went on the counter and was brought down by Scalvini for a yellow card.

Jones made his first appearance since September 19, but Atalanta got back into it when Josep Martinez fingertipped a Krstovic header over the bar, then was beaten by a very similar attempt by De Ketelaere on the resulting corner.

They had barely finished celebrating when Thuram tried to surprise Sportiello from the tightest of angles, Davide Zappacosta got back to clear off the line, but then jutted out an elbow as he fell on it, pushing the ball away. A penalty was awarded and a yellow card rather than red, as Petar Sucic swept the spot-kick into the near bottom corner.

Lautaro Martinez also made his comeback after five weeks out, but Josep Martinez used his legs to deny De Ketelaere a tap-in from the Giacomo Raspadori pull-back.

It wasn’t over, as De Ketelaere then controlled the Jonathan Rowe cross and smashed his shot into the roof of the net from 12 yards with the aid of a slight deflection for 3-4.

Josep Martinez had to rush outside his area to clear from Krstovic on the counter, then made a sensational one-handed save to keep out the Mario Pasalic scorcher at the 87th minute.

Alessandro Bastoni jumped with his arms raised and the De Ketelaere header struck it for a penalty in the final minute. Franck Kessie converted sending Martinez the wrong way for 4-4, the second time Atalanta have had this result in Bergamo this season after another chaotic thriller with Frosinone on October 25.
