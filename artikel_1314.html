Crystal Palace Reach Agreement With Thomas Frank To Become New Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Thomas Frank has an agreement in place to become the new Crystal Palace manager, GIVEMESPORT understands.

Frank has been the frontrunner to take over at Selhurst Park ever since Pierre Sage was sacked on November 12, with the Eagles bottom of the Premier League table.

However, while no quick appointment was made, it appears that the former Brentford and Tottenham boss is now set to take over in south London, with the international break still in full flow.

Talks over the Dane's backroom staff are also at the final stages, with an appointment expected to be made this week.

Frank will arrive at Selhurst Park and get to work with his new side ahead of the return of the Premier League this weekend. The Eagles return to action with a game against Coventry City away.

The 53-year-old has been out of work since leaving Tottenham in February. He was sacked after a 2-1 defeat to Newcastle United that left Spurs in 16th position and looking over their shoulders at the relegation zone.

Following a dismal interim spell of Igor Tudor, Tottenham eventually turned to Roberto De Zerbi to get them out of trouble, with safety only secured on the final day of the season.

Frank had a shocking 26.9% win percentage in the Premier League with Spurs, with that improved to 34.2% in all competitions, although that gave him the worst record of any permanent Spurs manager.

Prior to his spell in north London, Frank had made a name for himself with Brentford, guiding the club to promotion from the Championship in 2020/21, having just missed out on promotion the year before, and turned them into an established Premier League club.

Palace chairman Steve Parish will now be hoping the Dane can work his magic and guide the Eagles away from the relegation zone.

Speaking about his exit from Spurs in the summer, Frank said: "Leaving Tottenham has given me the chance to step back and reflect on my journey so far. Football management is a profession that demands complete commitment every single day, and periods like this are a rare opportunity to assess, learn and gain a fresh perspective.

"From the outside, it may have looked like a time of many challenges at Tottenham when results were not what we wanted. From within, however, it becomes clear why the club is so special - full of talented people who work tirelessly every day. I have no doubt Tottenham has a bright future.

"Football remains a huge part of who I am, and I have chosen to use this time productively. Over the coming months, besides spending time with family and friends, I will continue studying, observing and learning from other leaders both within sport and beyond it.

"There have been conversations and opportunities since leaving Spurs, but I have decided not to rush into the next role. For me, this summer is not the right time to go back into management.

"When the time is right, I will look forward to my return as a manager, ready to embrace the job with great energy and dedication."

It seems that the time is now right for Frank and Palace is the right club. Time will tell if that is the right call.
