Serie A official line-ups: Venezia vs. Udinese

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Oumar Solet and Keinan Davis both start for Udinese on Paolo Zanetti’s debut, whilst John Yeboah and Kike Pérez come into the Venezia side.

It kicks off at 20:45 CET [19:45 GMT] at the Stadio Pier Luigi Penzo, with Juan Luca Sacchi refereeing.

This is a crucial game at the bottom. Venezia are last in Serie A on three points, and Udinese are 19th on five. Davide Nicola’s side won his first game in charge against Napoli, but have lost four straight since, most recently 1-0 at Como last Sunday.

Paolo Zanetti takes charge of Udinese for the first time, and his debut comes against the club he took into Serie A in 2021. He replaced Kosta Runjaic, who was sacked on Monday after a 5-2 home defeat to Roma left Udinese eight games without a win. “With me, those who run and deserve it play,” Zanetti said at his unveiling on Wednesday.

Thierry Correia is back in the Venezia squad after a hamstring problem. Nicola has said he is there mainly to get used to matchday again and is unlikely to play a major role. Andrea Adorante (back), Bartol Franjić (shoulder) and Marin Šverko (hip) are still out.

Oumar Solet is back for Udinese after a calf injury, but Zanetti has said his minutes will be carefully managed. Keinan Davis is a late doubt with a muscle problem, and Vakoun Bayo has only just returned from a hamstring injury. David Alaba (calf) and Alessandro Zanoli (muscle) won’t be back until after the international break, and Gueye is out after ankle surgery.

Venezia (3-5-2): Stanković; Halhal, Moreno, Juan Jesus; Hainaut, Kike Pérez, Busio, Sohm, Mazzocchi; Yeboah, Adams Coach: Nicola

Udinese (3-4-2-1): Okoye; Abankwah, Palma, Solet; Vojvoda, Piotrowski, Karlström, Kamara; Zaniolo, Ekkelenkamp; Davis Coach: Zanetti
