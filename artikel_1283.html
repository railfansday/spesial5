Allegri: ‘Losing to Man City won’t be decisive for Napoli, club making assessments’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Massimiliano Allegri maintained his stance that Tuesday’s Champions League match against Manchester City will not be the be-all and end-all for Napoli, even if they have only picked up three points from four games in the competition so far and are at risk of missing out on qualification for the knock-out rounds.

Allegri sat down for his pre-match press conference on Monday evening, ahead of Tuesday night’s test against Man City in the Champions League league phase, which kicks off at the Etihad Stadium at 20.00 GMT.

Earlier on Monday, City head coach Enzo Maresca had joked that ‘We’re always good at complaining in Italy’ after being told what Allegri had said about Tuesday’s fixture over the weekend.

Allegri had made it clear that Man City were the obvious favourites and that Napoli’s qualification for the Champions League knock-out rounds depends on their final three league phase fixture, and not this one against Man City.

At his press conference on Monday evening, Allegri clarified: “I just said that it won’t be decisive, because mathematically, even if we win tomorrow, six points won’t be enough to qualify. So, I will only believe that we’ve qualified once we have achieved it on the final matchday. It’s a matter of numbers. We definitely need at least three wins, mathematically.”

Looking ahead to Tuesday’s game, Allegri added: “We know how difficult it will be, because we’re playing against one of the strongest teams in Europe, even after 10 years of winning in the Premier League and a Champions League victory and with so many changes of players. It’s a new group, but they have extraordinary qualities.

“It’s a Champions League match against one of the favourites to go on and win it. As always, we’ll need great technique when we have the ball and when we don’t. We’ll have to do our best to create the conditions to counter Man City.”

Allegri went on to provide an injury update on his squad. Napoli are without 11 first team players for their trip to Manchester, the latest being Italy international Antonio Vergara, who is out for the rest of the calendar year and beyond with a thigh injury he suffered over the weekend.

“We have some players who are recovering,” Allegri confirmed. “Di Lorenzo and Badiashile will definitely be available for Lecce, Favasuli and Marianucci are not on the Champions League squad list, then we’ve also lost Meret and Vergara, but that’s part of the game.

“I’ve seen the injury numbers across Europe. Lots of teams have injured players. We have to make do with what we’ve got and know that it will take even more desire to win it as a team.

“From Wednesday onwards, we will be preparing for our next game in the league, where we are a bit behind because we could have picked up a few more points.”

Asked how he motivates his team ahead of such a big game like tomorrow’s against Manchester City, Allegri said: “These games prepare themselves in terms of motivation. If we aren’t motivated, we clearly don’t understand where we are playing.

“That said, there will be objective difficulties against a team with such great technique and physicality and we will have to play well. We have to defend as a team when we don’t have the ball and not make the mistakes we made against Porto, where we got a bit scrambled trying to recover the result.”

Allegri has been in management for over 20 years, but has never coached outside of his native Italy. Asked if he would like to take on a job abroad at some stage, Allegri said: “It’s always intrigued me, but the choices I’ve made have been different and I have no regrets.

“In fact, I’m very happy because I’ve been very lucky. I’ve poached some massive teams in Italy: Juventus, Milan and Napoli. Let’s not forget Cagliari, where I started, either.

“Coming to play in these sorts of stadiums and facing these sorts of teams is always great. We do this job to play in these types of matches and to prove that we are one of the best. Tomorrow will be a great game. The clear goal is to progress, regardless of what happens tomorrow.

“Of course, getting a result would be wonderful, especially after the win against Torino, but at the same time, if we do lose, we haven’t compromised anything.”

Kevin De Bruyne was sat next to Allegri for the pre-match press conference. The Belgian was asked questions on his future with the club and Allegri was then asked for his thoughts.

“Kevin’s already said it, I haven’t spoken to him because we’re in the middle of the season,” said Allegri. “We will have the Coppa Italia on December 16 and then when we have the Christmas break from December 21, the club will make its assessments.

“We want to fix the squad. It also depends on the will of the players, whether they want to stay or leave. But we need to focus on the goal, getting to December 20.”

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
