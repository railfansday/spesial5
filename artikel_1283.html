Tottenham Want to Sign Midfielder Yaya Fofana, Set to Watch Him Live

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Tottenham will watch Stade de Reims star Yaya Fofana in action this weekend ahead of a possible transfer move, according to French outlet Foot Mercato.

Fofana has enjoyed a remarkable season for the Ligue 2 side and he will undoubtedly feature heavily in the headlines over the coming weeks following his goalscoring exploits as the January transfer window edges ever closer.

A versatile midfielder capable of playing in a number of positions, Fofana, who was recently called up to the Ivory Coast squad for the first time, has been utilised as an attacking midfielder by Reims boss Nicolas Usai this term and that has resulted in a real purple patch in front of goal.

Fofana has ten goals and three assists to his name from his 12 appearances this season, including nine goals in his last eight league games for his club side.

His form in the final third has come to the attention of Tottenham, with Foot Mercato reporting that the north London club will have people in the stands to watch the 22-year-old in action against St-Etienne on Saturday evening.

The French outlet report that Reims do not want to see their player leave during the January transfer window.

However, a permanent move with a six-month loan move back to Les rouges et blancs for the remainder of the season could well work for all parties concerned.

It is believed that a transfer away for Fofana would have to be in excess of the €25m (£21m) they recently received for Patrick Zabi following his move to Paris FC in the summer.

So what exactly would Fofana bring to Tottenham?

Well, Roberto De Zerbi would certainly be inheriting a very talented player and one that can play in a number of positions.

Fofana's ability to play further forward and flourish in the final third has been evident this season after so far amassing ten goals in 12 league appearances for Reims.

"Yaya is an instinctive player, so you have to give him a lot of freedom," said Usai, as reported by Morning Foot.

"He is able to play in a double pivot in the fairly low middle, he is able to play playmaker, he is able to play attacker, he is able to play on one side."

He added: "It's a talented player, but I insist that it's really an instinctive profile, so he must be left free.

"Of course he has defensive instructions and that he must tune defensively in the service of the collective, but when he has the ball or when he is able to dezone, the partners simply need to be able to adjust their movement."

On the prospect of losing his player in January, Usai said: "No, no, no fear. Really, it's early November, so I don't ask myself that kind of question."
