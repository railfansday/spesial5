Pio Esposito has the best goals-per-minute ratio in Italy history

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Francesco Pio Esposito hasn’t only scored nine goals in 14 games for the Italian national team, but also holds the best goals-per-minute ratio in the Azzurri’s history.

Inter star Pio Esposito currently has the best minutes-per-goal ratio in Italy’s history.

With one goal every 88 minutes at the international level, the 21-year-old has a better record than Enrico Chiesa, who averaged one goal every 91 minutes but scored only seven goals with the Azzurri in his career.

The third-ranked player is Julio Libonatti, a former striker with dual Argentine-Italian citizenship who played for the Azzurri from 1926 to 1932 after representing Argentina. Libonatti scored 15 goals in 17 games with Italy, one every 102 minutes.

Pio Esposito even has a better scoring rate than two of Italy’s best strikers ever: Luigi Riva and Silvio Piola.

Riva, the all-time leading scorer in the Nazionale’s history, netted once every 106 minutes and scored 35 times with the Azzurri, more than any other Italian player.

Piola sit third in the best-scorers ranking with 30 goals, one every 103 minutes.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
