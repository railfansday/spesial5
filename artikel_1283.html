Vinicius Jr Named 3 Toughest Opponents He's Faced - Picked Two Englishmen

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

By combining exceptional technique, blistering pace and an eye for goal, there are few defenders that Real Madrid and Brazil star Vinicius Jr would be intimidated by – yet, throughout his career, the winger has singled out three particularly difficult opponents.

That's right, even a player as naturally gifted as the 2024 Ballon d'Or runner-up – who is regularly considered among the finest wingers in world football today – can occasionally find himself up against a challenging opponent, even if such occasions are relatively rare.

GIVEMESPORT Key Statistic: In June 2024, Vinicius became the youngest player to score in two Champions League finals.

Vinicius has now enjoyed many seasons wearing the famous white shirt of Los Blancos, meaning he has come up against some of football's most formidable defenders. Interestingly enough, he named two Englishmen amongst his very toughest.

According to SPORT in 2023, the Brazilian international identified Ronald Araujo as the 'best defender' he had ever faced. The Barcelona star is now on loan at Liverpool, but clearly always gave Vini Jr a tough time when they met.

Speaking about the Uruguayan centre-back's impressive physical strength, Vinicius explained that Araujo's quality is precisely why he represents one of football's biggest clubs in Barcelona. As reported by The Sun, the winger stated:

The best defender I have ever faced is Araujo. He's very strong and very good. That's why he plays for Barca.

The two South Americans have shared numerous memorable battles, particularly during their regular El Clasico meetings – although their head-to-head record remains perfectly balanced, with six victories each.

Araujo joined the Catalan outfit in 2018 before making the step up to the first team in 2020. He then established himself as an integral member of the squad, recording more than 150 appearances for the club, before heading to Liverpool on loan in 2026/27.

Before naming Araujo, however, Vinicius had previously spoken highly of two current England internationals – Chelsea skipper Reece James and Manchester City icon Kyle Walker – during an interview with SPORTbible in 2022.

I've played against a lot of them and the most difficult opponents were Chelsea's [Reece] James and [Kyle] Walker, of Manchester City.

Looking at James first, the injury-hit Chelsea defender has faced the challenge of containing Vinicius on four separate occasions. Walker, meanwhile, has come out on top three times, although he has yet to find the net in any of their previous encounters.

Walker possesses the kind of explosive speed that can trouble even the most dangerous attackers in the game – something Vinicius has experienced on six occasions. The Brazilian has won half of his battles against the former Tottenham Hotspur defender, who is widely regarded as one of the finest right-backs in football history.
