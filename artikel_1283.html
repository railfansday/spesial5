McKennie and Pochettino admit ‘inevitable’ Manchester United rumours for USMNT stars

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Juventus midfielder Weston McKennie admits it is ‘inevitable’ there will be rumours he’ll follow USMNT coach Mauricio Pochettino to Manchester United, while the new Old Trafford boss is also not shying away from the possibility.

The moment Pochettino was lined up to replace sacked Michael Carrick in the Premier League job, the reports began to swirl that one of his first requests was a reunion with McKennie.

The versatile midfielder, who has played every role at Juventus from full-back to centre-forward, is a Joker in the pack who does a job wherever placed in the side.

“It’s a potential, we have a lot of guys on our team who are due to make moves within the next year,” McKennie told reporters at the USMNT camp when asked about the Manchester United rumours.

“So it’s inevitable, but you never know what’s going on in coaches’ minds. But you can see guys from here being talked about going to Manchester United.”

That question was also put to Pochettino himself, who did not rule out the possibility of targeting some of the players he has worked with on international duty for a club connection in Manchester.

“Now is not the time to talk about this,” said the coach.

“All the good players always have dreams to reach the highest level. Manchester United is one of the teams that is in the highest level and it’s the dream for many of the players.”

Another who could be linked is Christian Pulisic, but that would be hampered by the fact he very recently signed a new contract at Milan.

Having said that, McKennie only signed a new deal with Juventus in March 2026, which runs to June 2030.
