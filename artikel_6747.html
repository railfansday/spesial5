ELLE Editors Share the Best Color Cosmetics and Nail Products of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

We’re calling it: 2026 was the year of makeup. After many years of clean girl beauty and an emphasis on minimalism, it’s time to explore colors and self-expression again. This year’s best products go far beyond the typical mascara or run-of-the-mill concealer. Our testers raved about a brow pencil that relies on friction to amp up volume, blushes that looked natural and stayed put for hours, and lip stains that mimicked popsicle-like shades. For all of this and more, keep reading below for our 2026 Future of Beauty winners. We found 26 (the most ever!) color cosmetics worth your attention, spanning lips, eyes, face, and even nails.

The creamy formula brightens, blurs, and smooths with a soft-matte finish, proving that full coverage doesn’t have to feel heavy. Infused with hyaluronic acid for hydration, the lightweight texture layers seamlessly without settling. Our tester proclaimed it better than another well-known concealer. “Very easy to use, a lot of product, great coverage, and long wear, too.”

This mosaic of pressed powders boasts an eight-hour wear time, blurring power, and color-correcting and setting capabilities too. Our tester liked this compact’s elegance and said, “It is definitely blurring and mattifying, giving skin a soft, smooth, and natural finish.”

Another 10 rating came in for this product, which our tester said she would continue to purchase on her own. “It gave me even, dewy skin, but still looked natural, and I was able to use a brush (which doesn’t usually work on my dry skin) without any caking or visible dry texture.
