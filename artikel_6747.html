Meghan Markle Confirms a Burgundy Coat Is a Stylish Alternative to Navy

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Head-to-toe burgundy is the look of the day.

Meghan Markle and Prince Harry recently took part in their first official engagement since returning to British soil, and to mark the occasion, the Duchess of Sussex wore an all-burgundy ensemble.

The pair arrived in Nottingham last month to visit a youth and community charity, when they were pictured greeting fans outside. Meghan opted for a high-waisted slimline midi skirt, paired with a brown-toned roll neck and a single-breasted oversize coat in a matching hue. She finished the outfit off with simple court shoes.

Sitting somewhere between cherry and oxblood, burgundy takes its name from the red wines of France’s Burgundy region (what’s not to love?). It’s luxurious without being loud, and, crucially, is very easy to style. Even those of us who rarely stray from head-to-toe black can dabble in the shade without feeling out of our depth.

The color has infiltrated every corner of fall wardrobes in recent seasons, from standout leather and liquid satin to plush knitwear. Across the fall/winter 2026 runways, designers made the strongest case for burgundy with slews of the shade seen across Elie Saab, Max Mara, and Julien Dossena. Elsewhere, Balenciaga proved that the wine-tinged hue can perform just as well as any neutral, opening the show with all-black looks before quickly introducing the moody hue.

Meghan is not the only notable person to be favoring burgundy right now. Just this week, Victoria Beckham stepped out in a burgundy midi skirt, while the Princess of Wales also leaned into the trend with her handbag of choice.
