Kim Kardashian’s Latest Red Carpet Corset Gown Took 500 Hours to Make

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Kim Kardashian is no stranger to a statement-making corset, but her latest red carpet look required more than 500 hours of craftsmanship.

Ashi Studio designed the gray gown and revealed the lengths its team went to construct the piece on Instagram. “The creation brings together the expertise of multiple artisans, from the intricate construction of the corset and the precise application of lace to the delicate arrangement of each individual cord and the meticulous craftsmanship involved in working with crocodile leather,” it wrote.

Kardashian wore the dress to the New York City premiere of All’s Fair season 2 last night. The strapless design featured a structured bodice with molded cups, a fitted waist, and sheer panels.

Thin cords hung from the sides of the bodice, wrapping loosely around Kardashian’s arms before falling along the length of the skirt. The gown extended into a train, while a matching leather collar covered much of her neck.

Kardashian kept her accessories relatively minimal, opting for diamond stud earrings and a diamond ring. She wore her dark hair in a sleek ponytail with two loose strands framing her face and finished the look with smoky eye makeup and a pale-pink lip.

The fashion house revealed that it first showed Kardashian the gown during a fitting in Los Angeles. The team worked closely with her stylist Law Roach as it completed the design at its Paris atelier.

Roach has been dressing Kardashian throughout the All’s Fair press tour. Just last week, she attended the show’s Rome premiere in a black-and-white polka-dot gown from Galia Lahav’s fall 2027 bridal couture collection.

Kardashian attended Monday’s premiere with her co-stars Sarah Paulson, Naomi Watts, Niecy Nash-Betts, and Glenn Close. The second season of the Ryan Murphy legal drama premieres November 4 on Hulu.
