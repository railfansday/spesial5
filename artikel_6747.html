‘Love Is Blind’ Star Rob Feeley Releases Lengthy Statement, Claims Fans Saw “a Small Part of a Much Bigger Experience”

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Love Is Blind Season 11 star Rob Feeley is breaking his silence after a truly messy season. Quick recap: Rob got engaged to Michaella Giorgio in the Pods, but things went south during a cast-wide bar hang, where Rob told everyone he didn’t care about their relationship. Obviously, Michaella ended things, and Rob has some things to say now that he’s watched the season.

“Now that the reunion has aired and this chapter comes to a close, I’ve been reflecting on everything this experience has taught me,” Rob wrote on Instagram. “I’m truly grateful for the people I’ve met, the memories made, and the personal growth that came with both the incredible moments and the challenges. Looking back, there are moments I wish I had handled differently. There were times when I felt overwhelmed and let my emotions get the best of me. While those moments don’t reflect the person I strive to be, I recognize that they happened, and I take responsibility for how I reacted and where I could have communicated better.”

Rob went on to “acknowledge the hurt I caused Michaella,” saying “she deserved to feel heard, respected, and understood. I’m genuinely sorry for the times I fell short and for how I reacted in certain situations. My intentions don’t change the fact that my actions affected her, and I take responsibility for that.”

Rob also confirmed that he and Michaella have been in touch since the show filmed two (two!) years ago, and that they have moved on with “mutual respect.” He then reminded fans that they only saw “a small part of a much bigger experience, and not every conversation or situation can be fully understood from what makes it onto the screen.”

The burgeoning reality star ended his musings by teasing some “projects and opportunities” he’s been working on, and noted that “There’s a lot to look forward to in the coming weeks, and I’m excited to share what’s next.”

So...guess we’ll see what happens (next season of Perfect Match, perhaps?).
