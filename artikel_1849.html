Bruno Fernandes reveals his stance on new contract and Manchester United future

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Bruno Fernandes has left his Manchester United future up in the air, confirming that he has been offered a new contract but there are no negotiations ongoing.

The 32-year-old is into the final few months of his current deal, which runs out at the end of the season, although the club has the option to extend it for another 12 months.

The Portugal international has been an integral figure since joining from Sporting in January 2020 and Manchester United want to keep him, having made offers of a new deal.

Fernandes is not ready to commit just yet, leaving his options open for now after Mauricio Pochettino arrived this week to replace Michael Carrick as manager.

The Red Devils captain has dismissed the idea that he is holding out for more money or a longer contract, but does not feel the time is right to discuss terms.

‘No, there’s no negotiations ongoing,’ he told Sky Sports. ‘But I’ve seen people saying that he’s asked for more money, he’s asked for more years. If I wanted more money, I wouldn’t be here. I had somewhere to go to get much more money than being at Manchester United.

‘I could have been in a place that is much warmer than Manchester.

‘Two summers ago, three summers ago, last summer, I always had the chance to leave and I always chose to be here. The thing is, I’ve always showed how much I care about the club and how much I want to win at this club. And I always said, in the moment the club thinks it’s time for me to move on, I will move on.

‘I’m not going to be the player that I’ve seen many in this time at Manchester United. They’re going to stay here just by staying, just finish my contract and then we will see. In the moment the club says, “Bruno, it’s time to part ways”, I’m going to be the first one walking out the door.

‘Yeah, the club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay.’

Fernandes’ future will, at least in part, be down to the opinion of Pochettino, who is tasked with rebuilding the Manchester United team into a successful one.

The Argentine takes charge of the Red Devils for the first time on Sunday as they travel to face Liverpool and Fernandes has liked what he has seen already from his new boss.

‘He’s someone that likes to bring a very good energy to the team, someone very positive, very strong, strict, said it straight away that he won’t allow us to not give him what he demands from the team, from each player,’ said the Portuguese.

‘He wants the club to succeed so much, he wants to win games, and that’s pretty clear and obvious by the way he and his staff speak and want us to improve.’
