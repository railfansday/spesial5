Kulusevski criticism ‘completely unfair’, says Sweden boss Potter

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Graham Potter has hit out at critics of Dejan Kulusevski following the playmaker's cameos for Sweden over the international break.

Kulusevski made his long-awaited return from injury last month, and has since made four substitute appearances for Tottenham.

The 26-year-old subsequently earned a recall to the Sweden team for their Nations League games against Bosnia-Herzegovina and Poland.

Sweden ensured their place at the top of their group by beating Bosnia last week and then drawing 1-1 with Poland on Tuesday. They are now unbeaten in their last 13 Nations League games.

Adrian Benedyczak put Poland ahead in the 16th minute, but Hugo Larsson swiftly equalised for Sweden.

Following on from a cameo against Bosnia-Herzegovina, Kulusevski came on in the 65th minute versus Poland. He had one shot, created one chance and completed 4/4 passes, though he did not attempt a dribble and lost possession four times.

However, Potter feels criticism of Kulusevski's displays is out of order.

"I think the criticism is completely unfair, to be honest," Potter told Viaplay.

"He has trained very, very well. You have to judge someone based on a match like that, like it was against Bosnia. It wasn't easy for the substitutes to come into that match.

"Now he just needs to get playing time, and that's completely normal. It's completely normal for anyone who's been out with an injury and is now getting back into match form."

Potter also said Sweden were being extremely careful with how much workload they placed on Kulusevski.

He added: "We are of course in contact with Tottenham and it is logical that we work closely with them. They have worked with Dejan for so long to get him back to the level he is at now. It's reasonable that we have a close dialogue.

"We have a pretty good idea of ​​how we can use him. But the most important thing for us is that he is here."
