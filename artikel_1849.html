Premier League attendance table: Leeds United, Hull City, Aston Villa, Sunderland, Crystal Palace & more ranked

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Premier League attendance table with Leeds United, Hull City, Sunderland, Aston Villa and more ranked

The top tier of English football is home to some of the world’s most passionately supported clubs.

There are two Yorkshire representatives in the Premier League - Leeds United and Hull City - and each have tested the patience of their supporters over the years.

Leeds endured an infamous fall from grace, enduring a decline that dropped them into the third tier. Hull also have memories of League One, but both the Whites and the Tigers now compete in the so-called promised land.

Away from Yorkshire, there are the likes of Manchester United and Manchester City across the Pennines, the famous north-east giants Newcastle United and London institutions such as Arsenal and Tottenham Hotspur.

But how do these Premier League clubs compare when it comes to average league attendances this season?

Here are the average attendances of each Premier League club, ranked from highest to lowest, with data courtesy of Transfermarkt.
