Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The company recently filed for bankruptcy and is now offering all of its assets for sale, including the contents of user chat sessions. Some 42 state attorneys general are trying to block this, but it’s unclear whether they will succeed …

A group of 42 attorneys general are trying to block the chatbot company from auctioning off users’ chat histories and other sensitive information […] “Nothing in the agreement restricts what the buyer may do with the data after closing, including selling it,” the AGs say in a court filing […]

“Character․AI’s plan to auction off its platform, including the IP addresses and private chats histories of its users — many of them children — with zero protection for those users is appalling,” California Attorney General Rob Bonta said in a statement. “If this sale goes through, users’ sensitive data would go into the hands of a buyer even though Character․AI promised consumers that their data would not be sold.”
