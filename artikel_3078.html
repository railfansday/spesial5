US Probes If Alice Huang Helped Smuggle Super Micro Products

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Justice Department is probing Alice Huang, the woman US officials suspect as a possible spy targeting the ousted head of Nvidia Corp., over whether entities linked to her were involved in efforts to smuggle products made by Super Micro Computer Inc. to China, according to a person familiar with the matter.
