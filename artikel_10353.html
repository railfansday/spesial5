Motorola accidentally releases ‘Moto Tag 3’ tracker early with new colors

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Despite the fact that Moto Tag 2 has only been on the market for a few months at this point, Motorola is apparently getting Moto Tag 3 out in the not-too-distant future, and it’s already on sale (probably by mistake).

An Amazon UK listing went up this week for “Moto Tag 3,” a new version of Motorola’s Android Find Hub tracker. The listing shows the tracker in “Pantone Lucent White,” while a second image shared by Evan Blass shows the new model in a vibrant purple color.

There’s no indication of major changes in “Moto Tag 3,” with Motorola still claiming similar battery life, using a CR2032 battery, and supporting UWB in Android Find Hub.

“Moto Tag 3” is on sale from Amazon UK, though it comes with a “1-4 month” shipping estimate. This is almost certainly a slip-up, especially given the timing.

This comes much earlier than expected. The original Moto Tag launched in 2024, with its sequel being announced in early 2026 before going on sale in the US less than six months ago in June 2026. Such an early move to “Moto Tag 3” is interesting, to say the least, so we’ll have to wait and see if there are any changes in tow beyond just the new colors. That said, these are really nice colors. It’s also possible this is just the same model with a slip-up in branding – the model number on the Amazon listing is unchanged.

We’ll have to wait and see what happens. Moto Tag 2 did debut at CES in January, and with Razr Flex also in the works, Motorola almost certainly would launch an updated tracker at the trade show if it truly does plan to do so soon.

This comes right as Moto Tag 2 has hit its lowest price ever at just $18.99 amid Black Friday sales.
