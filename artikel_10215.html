Tanggal: 2026-11-28

A video doorbell for under $40? Kinda wild, but that’s how it goes with Ring Black Friday deals this year.

If you woke up this morning and were hoping to get a new video doorbell for dirt cheap, Ring has a Black Friday deal you’re going to love. The company’s latest Ring Battery Doorbell 2K model is down to $38.99 for Black Friday, which is the lowest price ever since its release in April this year.

Meanwhile, the cheapest you can find the Nest Battery Doorbell today is $154.99, and that model is a whopping five years old. That camera operates at 960 x 1280 resolution, while the $39 Ring Battery Doorbell 2K operates at a perfectly square 1920 x 1920 resolution. Square sensors have taken root as the better option for doorbell cameras because of the aspect ratio, and the significantly higher resolution of Ring’s camera offers 6x digital zoom as well.

Like Google’s Nest cameras, Ring cameras don’t offer local storage options built into the camera, but Ring plans are just $9.99 per month and cover all cameras on your account with up to 180 days of playback history, so it’s not exactly pricey to keep them all running.
