Tiger confirms date for fresh PGA Tour comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Tiger Woods has been added a tournament invite for the Hero World Challenge from December 3-6, hosted by his foundation; Woods has not featured in a competitive event since 2024; watch Woods' latest comeback live on Sky Sports Golf

Tiger Woods has confirmed he will return to competitive action at the Hero World Challenge next month, live on Sky Sports.

The invite-only tournament - hosted by Woods' foundation - has previously been used as a comeback event for Woods, who has played a limited schedule since suffering career-threatening injuries in a car crash in February 2021.

Woods has not appeared on the PGA Tour since missing the cut at The Open in July 2024, with the 15-time major champion missing the entire 2025 season after rupturing his Achilles tendon.

He underwent fresh back surgery last October and missed the 2026 campaign after "stepping away" from PGA Tour golf, following a car crash in March that saw him charged with reckless driving, but is now scheduled to return in Albany, Bahamas.

The 50-year-old is one of three sponsor's exemptions added to the field for the 20-man event, held from December 3-6, with 2019 US Open champion Gary Woodland and Rickie Fowler the others to be included.

Woods was a notable absentee from the initial line-up announcement last month, with Hideki Matsuyama returning as defending champion and Scottie Scheffler looking to win for a third time in four years, while Players champion Cameron Young and former US Open winner Matt Fitzpatrick also feature.

The former world No 1 is currently listed as 'unranked' in the OWGR standings, having only completed four 72-hole tournaments over the last six seasons and not had a worldwide top 10 since January 2020.

He last featured at the Hero World Challenge in 2023, his first appearance since withdrawing mid-tournament from The Masters earlier that year, although won the event in 2001, 2004, 2006, 2007 and 2011.

Woods was listed in Jupiter Links GC's expanded five-player roster for the 2026/27 TGL season, beginning December 27, with Kevin Kisner - replaced by Keith Mitchell in the line-up - expecting the five-time Masters champion to feature more frequently.

"I'm not on the team anymore," Kisner told the Fore Play Podcast last month. "I had a two-year contract with Jupiter Links. Tiger wants to play and they wanted to sign Keith Mitchell, which is totally fine with me.

"Rob [McNamara], ​Tiger's guy, he texted me a long text last week. Just telling me what they did on their decisions, how they were moving around and what players were going to do and that Tiger wanted to play a full schedule."

Woods previously described the PNC Championship as his "fifth major", with the 36-hole team competition seeing a former winner of a major or The Players tee it up alongside a family member.

He featured in every edition since 2020 with his son, Charlie Woods, who fired a hole-in-one during the 2024 contest before they lost in a play-off to Bernhard Langer's team, although missed last year's contest due to injury.

Team Woods have yet to confirm their entry into this year's edition, held from December 19-20 and also live on Sky Sports, although there are spaces for them still to be included in the line-up.

"He plays there [at the Hero World Challenge] and PNC [Championship]," Kisner predicted. "He's going to play both.

"Come back, get your feet wet at the Hero, play with Charlie [Woods] in the Father/Son, play some TGL, get ready for Augusta."
