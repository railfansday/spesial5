Arsenal survive dominant Barca to earn precious WCL point

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Match report as Arsenal draw 1-1 with Barcelona at the Spotify Camp Nou; Gunners survived an onslaught from the European champions to earn a point thanks to Frida Maanum's goal against the run of play; Caroline Graham Hansen notched deserved equaliser for hosts

Arsenal managed to withstand Barcelona's dominance to claim an impressive 1-1 draw against the European champions.

The Gunners were hoping for a repeat upset of the 2025 Champions League final, and while Frida Maanum's opener gave the underdogs something to cling onto, they were unable to keep out a relentless Barca for 90 minutes.

After a start that saw Misa Rodriguez called into action on several occasions early, the Gunners stole the lead as Maanum tucked home a rebound from Caitlin Foord's thwarted effort.

It was a rare moment of escaping the Barca barrage led by Caroline Graham Hansen, who hit the post with the hosts 18th and final attempt of the first half alone.

The Gunners needed a miracle to withstand another 45 minutes without conceding, and such luck didn't come their way. Hansen finally punished the visitors for leaving her in too much space as she bent a beautiful curling effort beyond the reach of Rodriguez.

Although the equaliser looked set to open the floodgates, Barcelona soon ran out of steam, with Alessia Russo spurning the best chance to snatch a winner at Spotify Camp Nou.

The result means that Arsenal climb back up into the top four places needed to progress straight into the Champions League, while also remaining just two points behind leaders Lyon, who they face off against next week.

Arsenal goalscorer Frida Maanum speaking with Disney+:

"We were leading 1-0, but I think it's a good performance from us defensively. I think we ran a lot and disrupted their attacking game. We get away with a point that is quite good against Barcelona away.

"We had 30 per cent possession but we created chances. Caitlin [Foord] had a good one in the first half and then Alessia [Russo] in the second half. Overall it's a good performance creating that many chances against Barcelona away.

"It's London City Lionesses on Sunday but obviously we are going to build from this."

Tonight's draw will be an interesting one for Renee Slegers to look back on once she touches down in north London.

On one hand, to suggest that Arsenal were anything other than lucky based on their first half display would be incredibly difficult to defend. Barcelona not scoring before the break had nothing to do with any resolute defending from the visitors. They were carved open time and again. They just had Misa Rodriguez and poor finishing to thank.

Yet, having weathered that storm, or more accurately tsunami, the hosts didn't offer the same level of threat after the break. The fact that Hansen was left so free for the equaliser given that by then, Slegers' side had settled into a strong defensive shape, has to leave a sense of frustration.

What's more, it's not like Barcelona went gung-ho for a winner. If anything, they backed off and Russo's missed chance in the 66th minute should've put the WSL outfit back ahead and given them another lead to hold onto.

From that perspective, Slegers may feel hard done by. Her side had survived by the skin of their teeth in the first half, but a far more compact and composed display after the break ultimately proved their undoing.
