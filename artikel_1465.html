Roma continue monitoring Gittens as January reinforcement

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Roma continues to monitor Jamie Bynoe-Gittens.

Fabrizio Romano confirmed this on his YouTube channel, where he stated that the Englishman remains one of the Giallorossi’s most sought-after prospects ahead of the January transfer window.

Already linked to Roma during the summer transfer window, Gittens’s arrival was stalled by Chelsea’s desire not to loan him out, thus blocking his departure to Trigoria.

A few months later, however, the situation could change, with the London club possibly having changed their minds about the player’s possible loan departure.

During a pre-match press conference for Roma vs. Sassuolo, Roma sporting director Tony D’Amico reviewed the Giallorossi’s situation, reiterating the need to acquire an attacking winger in January to provide Gasperini with a quick player capable of attacking spaces and creating superiority.

Gittens’ name could therefore be a hot topic again ahead of January, although the Giallorossi are concerned about the 2004-born player’s physical condition, as he’s been dealing with a knee problem that has prevented him from playing so far this season.
