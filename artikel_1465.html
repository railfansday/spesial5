“I will have to go on the pitch and hit someone”, José Mourinho put on a show at a press conference

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

José Mourinho had a lot to say after Real Madrid’s stunning victory over Celta Vigo. Jostled by the Galicians, the Merengues had to fight to snatch a precious success thanks to Bellingham and Carlos Espi. A more complicated meeting than expected for the Portuguese technician, who notably pointed out the fatigue of his internationals and the lack of concentration of his team at the start of the match. But at the press conference, the Special One especially put on a show when discussing his offensive choices. Asked about Carlos Espí, who continues to be decisive despite limited playing time, Mourinho explained at length the qualities of his striker. “He’s a surface player. When you dominate an opponent who is content to defend, his game is devastating. He has quality in the area, he plays with his back to goal, he is good with his head, he attacks both posts… He has all the qualities of an area player. History is full of players like him, decisive even with limited playing time”he said.

And Mourinho was keen to underline the impeccable state of mind of his player, who does not seem disturbed by his status in the Madrid offensive hierarchy. “We are happy with him. He’s the kind of player who doesn’t get distracted by discussions about his playing time: Should Kylian be a substitute or play more? He knows that he is progressing, that he has the respect of the coach, his teammates and the supporters. He is happy. And I hope to have the opportunity to give him tenure, 90 minutes or more”continued the Portuguese. A way of also justifying the use and playing time of Carlos Espi with a Madrid team rather focused on offensive transitions and depth.

But the José Mourinho show really began when a journalist asked him if he was considering combining Kylian Mbappé and Carlos Espí at the forefront of his attack. A possibility that Mourinho does not completely rule out, but which would pose serious balance problems for his team. And the Portuguese technician found a rather original way of explaining it. “I can do it, but when we lose the ball, I, being on the sideline, will have to enter the field in transition and punch someone to stop the counter-attack! Because playing with two pure strikers means the wingers have to defend inside. And it will certainly not be Diomandé, Vinicius, Arda… A little Brahim, perhaps. But with the characteristics of our wingers, we are not made to play in a classic 4-4-2”he said. Behind the joke, Mourinho insisted on the tactical constraints of such an association. “It’s more complicated than you might think. It’s easier for the supporters, as well as for you. But the reality is a little more difficult. »

Finally, José Mourinho also took advantage of this press conference to end the suspense concerning Vinicius Junior. While questions surrounded the presence of the Brazilian in the starting lineup during the next meeting in Madrid with his goals for Brazil during the break, the Special One preferred to cut short speculation with his usual humor. “I said it yesterday, it doesn’t matter whether he’s a substitute or a starter. To avoid you asking me the question on Monday and having too much to say, I’m going to be a bad boy and end the story: he will start Tuesday in the Champions League.he announced. An answer which has the merit of being clear, while Mourinho wants to juggle and alternate between offensive positions. And after a difficult victory against Celta Vigo, the Madrid coach recalled that at Real Madrid, aligning all the offensive talents was not necessarily enough to build a balanced team.
