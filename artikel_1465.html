In great shape, Malen’s Roma wants to defeat PSG!

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Five goals scored in Parma, a quadruple from Donyell Malen and a fourth consecutive victory in Serie A: AS Roma could not have better prepared for their European meeting against Paris Saint-Germain. Saturday evening, the Giallorossi swept Parma (5-1) at the Ennio-Tardini stadium, confirming their excellent momentum upon returning from the international break. Second in the Italian championship, just two points behind Lazio, Gian Piero Gasperini’s team is establishing itself as one of the most attractive teams at the start of the season. With 36 goals scored in just twelve days, the Romans even displayed a historic offensive performance. You have to go back 76 years to find such a prolific team at this stage of Serie A. Enough to approach the trip to Paris with great confidence, where the reigning double European champions await them.

And this demonstration obviously bears the signature of a man: Donyell Malen. The Dutch international tormented the Parmesan defense with a quadruple, confirming his status as Roma’s main offensive danger. His statistics are dizzying: already 26 goals scored in the calendar year 2026 in the Roman jersey, a performance that no player at the club has achieved since Bobby Manfredini in 1960. An efficiency which also rewards the work of Gasperini, who seems to have found the ideal position to exploit the qualities of the former Borussia Dortmund striker. “Perhaps I played him where he expresses himself best. There are many great strikers in Italy, we can think of Lautaro, but Malen is extraordinary at the moment, especially in the way his characteristics combine with those of our team”greeted the Italian technician after the meeting.

But reducing this Roma to the sole success of Malen would be a mistake. Since the start of the season, Gasperini has built a team capable of stifling its opponents, multiplying dangerous situations and, above all, reacting when put in difficulty. Facing Parma, the Romans had to deal with tougher opposition than the final score suggests. “The result doesn’t really reflect the match. Parma were in good shape, aggressive, and their long balls gave us problems. We conceded a bad goal which could have changed the psychological aspect of the match”recognized the former Atalanta coach. But it was precisely the reaction of his players that impressed him: “We now have the confidence and ability to score and win. This is our greatest quality. We play all our matches with the desire to win them. And we can win them all”. Mental strength that accompanies obvious collective progression, both tactically and physically.

This rise in power comes at the best time for La Louve, who is preparing to meet PSG in the Champions League. And this meeting comes at the best time for the Italian club, in great form, facing a PSG still ambitious, but forced to deal with the absences of Khvicha Kvaratskhelia and Désiré Doué, two major elements of its offensive sector. The Parisians obviously have the weapons necessary to bring down the Italians, but they will have to be wary of this Roma’s ability to exploit spaces and punish the slightest error. Gasperini can also count on the depth of his squad, an asset that he himself highlighted after the victory in Parma. “That’s the advantage of having a Champions League squad, with five or six quality changes that can turn a match around”. PSG has been warned, this Roma will not come to Paris to make up the numbers.
