Top-scoring attacking trios in 2026-27: Barcelona way out in front already

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Bayern Munich had the best front three in Europe last season, but there’s already a different runaway leader this time around.

We’ve taken a closer look at the highest-scoring trios across the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1 this season. It doesn’t matter whether the three players were on the pitch at the same time or not, just how many goals each team’s three best scorers have got.

It’s very much the early days of this ranking, so expect plenty of change as the season wears on.

But for now, here are the top 10 attacking trios in Europe’s top five leagues this season.

Barcelona have banished any fears they’d have a void up front after the Pole’s exit this summer, with Raphinha reborn as a centre-forward. And a pretty good one at that, too.

Lamine Yamal is in his typically outrageous form as well, and so Barca are way out in front with their best three-way combination for goals this season.

We imagine five-goal Fermin Lopez might not be part of the trio for much longer, since he isn’t really playing as an attacker anyway. Anthony Gordon, your time will come.

Jose Mourinho’s second Real Madrid reign hasn’t been the most convincing so far, but it’s helped that some of his star attackers are still doing the business.

That’s especially the case with Kylian Mbappe, who’s scored 12 goals in La Liga so far – including a brace in El Clasico.

Jude Bellingham has chipped in with a few as well and summer signing Yan Diomande has netted three times.

Highlights have included a 3-0 win at Fiorentina, beating Cesc Fabregas’ Como and knocking four past Sassuolo and five past Lecce.

Antonio Raimondo leads the way with eight, with midfielder Giacomo Calo adding five and winger Giorgi Kvernadze contributing three. Consider them firmly on our radar.

Raimondo will be out for a few months though with an injury, just as he was starting to flourish. Football can be cruel.

Esteban Lepaul is the star here, with seven goals already after netting 20 last season. No wonder he recently made his international debut for France.

Boulaye Dia and Sebastien Szymanski have chipped in with four each too.

Never mind the off-field distractions, it’s been business as usual on the pitch for City.

Erling Haaland has gone nine from nine so far in the Premier League and Rayan Cherki has already matched his total of four from last season.

No one quite knows what these goals are going towards, but until a recent slip-up or two, City are by and large just beating what’s in front of them for now.

Last year’s most prolific trio are really starting to kick up a gear, scoring five goals between them in Bayern’s 6-1 win over RB Leipzig.

Michael Olise currently leads the way with six, but Luis Diaz was the hat-trick scorer against Leipzig and Kane will hit double figures before your next bedtime.

Roma have been enjoying their highest-scoring 10-game run at the start of a season in Serie A with 28 goals.

Half of those have come from Donyell Malen, Rodrigo Mora and Matias Soule, which has become their best option for a front three while Paulo Dybala has been injured.

It’s Malen doing most of the heavy lifting with eight goals in the league so far.

Hang on a minute. Yep, that’s Niclas Fullkrug, the worst Premier League signing of all time.

He’s a man reborn at Werder Bremen this season with nine goals. Shoutout to Marco Grull as well, who’s assisted four of them (and three for other teammates) as well as scoring twice himself.

JP Morgan? CPR? We’ve seen some frankly awful shouts for what Chelsea’s new front three should go by, but one thing’s for sure – they seem to have clicked straight away.

Morgan Rogers was the missing ingredient, joining Joao Pedro and Cole Palmer in a big-money (OK, huge money) move from Aston Villa in the summer.

He’s hit the ground running with five goals in the league so far, the same amount as striker Pedro.

An attacking trio featuring a midfielder, a 20-year-old and a wing-back. Fair enough.

Milan will be hoping big summer signing Goncalo Ramos can start repaying their faith, but for now, their main scorers have been Adrien Rabiot, Alphadjo Cisse and Diego Moreira.
