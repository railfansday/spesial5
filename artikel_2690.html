Kyren Williams off injury report, set to play Wednesday vs. Packers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Rams running back Kyren Williams was listed on the injury report this week with a toe injury, but he is still set to play in Wednesday’s game against the Packers.

Williams has no injury designation after he was listed as limited on Sunday. Los Angeles listed him as full on Monday and Tuesday, though the Rams did not practice so Tuesday’s report is an estimate.

Coming off their bye, the Rams treated Monday as a normal Friday and Tuesday as a normal Saturday.

Williams leads the Rams with 616 rushing yards and six touchdowns. He’s also caught 38 passes for 267 yards with a TD.

As expected, L.A. ruled out offensive lineman Steve Avila (hamstring), offensive lineman Coleman Shelton (hamstring), and tight end Terrance Ferguson (ankle/hamstring).

The Rams have no other game statuses. Tight end Davis Allen (oblique), cornerback Trent McDuffie (back), tight end Colby Parkinson (ribs), linebacker Nate Landman (toe), offensive lineman Justin Dedich (hand), defensive end Kobie Turner (groin), long snapper Joe Cardona (ankle), edge rusher Myles Garrett (knee/rest), and quarterback Matthew Stafford (right elbow) are all set to play.
