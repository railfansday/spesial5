Steelers WRs Michael Pittman, Roman Wilson return to practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Steelers are opening the practice window for several players on Wednesday.

Head coach Mike McCarthy told reporters that receiver Michael Pittman, receiver Roman Wilson, guard Gennings Dunker, and practice squad receiver Max Hurleman have all been designated to return.

Pittman has been sidelined by a foot injury. He’s appeared in three games this season — his first with Pittsburgh — catching seven passes for 89 yards.

Wilson has caught 14 passes for 188 yards with two touchdowns in five games this year. He’s been sidelined by a dislocated finger.

While he hasn’t appeared in a game, Dunker has been on IR since Sept. 26. He was a third-round pick in this yer’s draft.

Additionally, the Steelers have placed guard Mason McCormick on injured reserve and released Isaiah Hodgins from the practice squad.
