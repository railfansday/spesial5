Broncos lead Raiders 17-10 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Broncos got out to a 17-0 lead, but the Raiders have settled in after a rough start to narrow that advantage to 17-10 at halftime.

Denver opened the scoring with a 38-yard field goal by Wil Lutz off of a blocked punt in Las Vegas territory. The club then scored on a 1-yard pass from quarterback Bo Nix to tight end Adam Trautman and had another 1-yard touchdown from Jonah Coleman on a run.

But after that, the Raiders came alive. First, a 46-yard run by Ashton Jeanty set up a 28-yard field goal by Matt Gay to get Las Vegas on the board. Then Jeanty came through for rookie Fernando Mendoza, catching a pass deep down the right sideline and taking it all the way for a 68-yard touchdown to narrow Denver’s lead to seven points.

Mendoza finished the first half 6-of-13 passing for 127 yards with a TD. Jeanty has 75 yards rushing on nine carries and three receptions for 87 yards.

Only two other players have caught passes for the Raiders, who are without star tight end Brock Bowers.

On the other side, Nix is 12-of-23 for 138 yards with a touchdown and an interception. The pick came on the last play of the half as Nix threw an unsuccessful Hail Mary.

On the injury front for the Raiders, defensive tackle Tim Keenan (knee), receiver Dareke Young (stinger), and defensive tackle Adam Butler (head) are all questionable to return.

The Raiders will have a shot to tie it up to start the third quarter, as they’ll receive the second-half kickoff.
