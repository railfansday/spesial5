Move to bench Aaron Rodgers reportedly was "coach's decision"

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Tuesday night’s surprising move by the Steelers to announce the benching of quarterback Aaron Rodgers was reportedly the product of the mind of the man who was his first NFL head coach.

Via Brooke Pryor of ESPN, the move was a “coach’s decision.”

While that’s usually the case, a big decision like this could carry fingerprints of the G.M. and/or ownership. Presumably, coach Mike McCarthy let Omar Khan and Art Rooney II know what was coming — and gave them a chance to chime in if not veto the move — before it was implemented.

The timeline remains confusing. On Monday, McCarthy responded to a question regarding the possibility of one of the other quarterbacks being used when the offense is sputtering by saying, “Aaron Rodgers is our quarterback.”

When did that change? Why did that change?

Or did McCarthy take a page from the Nick Sirianni playbook, deliberately concealing the plan to pivot from Rodgers to Mason Rudolph?

With a short week between Sunday’s game at Philadelphia and Friday’s game against the Broncos, everything was compressed and frenetic. Still, at some point the lightbulb activated for McCarthy between Sunday and Tuesday night. When that precisely happened isn’t known.
