Brooklyn's Nolan Traore out 8-10 weeks with hamstring strain

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

French point guard Nolan Traore has been one of the bright spots in Brooklyn this season, averaging 12.9 points per game and shooting 40.4% from the 3-point line.

Unfortunately, the Nets will be without him for 8-10 weeks due to a hamstring strain, the team announced.

The 20-year-old was the No. 19 pick just two years ago but had a strong Summer League and looked like a player ready to take a step forward in his second season. He has done just that, and last week against Philadelphia tied a career high with 23 points. He might end up being the best of the five first-rounders the Nets used in that draft.

Look for Egor Dëmin and Danny Wolf to get more run with Traore out, and one of those two will move into the starting lineup.
