KC Concepcion 6-yard touchdown run ties Browns-Texans at 17-17

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

After going down 17-0 early, the Browns have tied it up against the Texans.

Rookie receiver KC Concepcion took an inside handoff 6 yards for the first rushing touchdown of his career, making the score 17-17 midway through the third quarter.

Cleveland had good field position to start the possession with Denzel Boston’s punt return to to Houston’s 47-yard line. The team got into scoring position with Deshaun Watson’s 18-yard pass to Malachi Corley, then got close with Harold Fannin’s catch and run with a facemask penalty tacked on.

The next play was Concepcion’s 6-yard run.

Houston has gone three-and-out on two drives so far in the third period.
