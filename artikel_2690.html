Ben Skowronek, Derrick Harmon return to Steelers practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Pittsburgh Steelers will face their division rival Cincinnati Bengals in Week 10 with the lead in the AFC North on the line, and it looks as if they will have help on the way.

Wide receiver and special teams ace Ben Skowronek was a full participant in practice after missing the last three games with a calf injury. Derrick Harmon also returned to practice, but remains on IR with the foot injury he sustained in Week 4.

Cornerbacks Doneiko Slaugher and Brandin Echols were full participants. Both are dealing with hamstring injuries. Safety DeShon Elliott was a limited participant with an ankle injury.

Darnell Washington, T.J. Watt, and Cam Heyward were given veteran days off. No other names appeared on the injury report.
