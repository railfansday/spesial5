'Good one': Patriots star abruptly ends interview after host tells him to lay off the pumpkin pie

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Listed at 6'1″ and 234 pounds, New England Patriots linebacker Robert Spillane falls well within the typical range of an NFL linebacker. But at least one Boston-area radio host wants to make sure that weight doesn't start creeping up on account of Thanksgiving dinner.

The Patriots are slated to play the lowly Los Angeles Chargers on Sunday as they try to course-correct their season after losing starting quarterback Drake Maye, and the team's defense is expected to be a large part of that equation. Spillane ranks 45th out of 81 eligible linebackers per PFF, and there are certainly voices in New England that would like to see a bit more production out of him. But overall, linebacker is far from the most pressing issue on the roster.

Perhaps that's why Spillane took exception to a joke thrown out by Boston sports radio host Greg Hill on Wednesday, who hosts The Greg Hill Show on WEEI. In an attempt at some light razzing, Hill told Spillane, "maybe just one piece of pumpkin pie [this Thanksgiving], because we need you to have your feet under you on Sunday."

"Good one. Have a good one," Spillane replied, abruptly ending the interview.

Hill appeared shocked. "I was kidding," he said in disbelief. "I think he was very upset by that. Now I feel bad."

Maybe Spillane wasn't in the best of moods to begin with, and Hill's joke just hit at a poor time. Or maybe there was some genuine resentment from a world-class athlete receiving diet advice from a sports radio host.

Either way, it's safe to say Spillane might not be reappearing on Hill's show anytime soon.
