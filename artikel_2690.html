Preview: The Wizards play the Pistons on Monday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Washington Wizards face off against the Detroit Pistons tomorrow. Let's get to it.

How to listen: The Team 980 AM, 106.7-2 FM

Wizards: Reece Beekman, Tristan Vukcevic, Justin Champagnie and Jamir Watkins are out. Anthony Davis is day-to-day.

Pistons: Jalen Duren is out. Isaiah Joe is day-to-day.

The Wizards will play the Pistons for the second time in the 2026-27 season tomorrow. Okay, I'll clarify. The Wizards will play the Pistons for their first regular season game tomorrow on the road, but it isn't their first match if you include preseason games.

Washington defeated Detroit 114-108 on Oct. 10 at home though … preseason is preseason. Though the Wizards are starting out better than the last couple seasons, they are now on a three game losing streak and this is not the time for them to fall further behind.

The Pistons are 6-2 as of the time of this writing, and will have a winning record regardless of what happens with their game against the Los Angeles Lakers. At least Washington will have the benefit of not being on a back-to-back. Detroit will. We'll see what happens.
