Patriots vs. Chargers Wednesday injury report: Four non-participants heading out of bye week

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The New England Patriots practiced without four during Wednesday's work on the Los Angeles Chargers.

Quarterback Drake Maye, wide receiver A.J. Brown, offensive lineman Walter Rouse and cornerback Carlton Davis III remained non-participants for the 53-man roster. But safety Craig Woodson returned following back-to-back absences coming out of the bye week. And so did wide receiver Romeo Doubs, whose 21-day activation window has opened along with guard Alijah Vera-Tucker's.

Here's the first injury report ahead of Sunday's flexed-out 4:05 p.m. ET kickoff at SoFi Stadium.

DT Teair Tart (not injury related — rest)

LB Khalil Mack (not injury related — rest)

While there's uncertainty under center for the 1-9 Chargers, too, the 5-5 Patriots haven't closed the door on the rest of Maye's 2026. But the fractured fibula that the captain suffered prior to the bye week will keep him sidelined for the foreseeable future. And for now, with seven of the team's eight return designations used, he remains on the active roster. A wait-and-see situation also goes for the No. 1 wide receiver on the depth chart amid a campaign that's brought a high ankle sprain, knee concerns and now a hamstring injury that saw him downgraded during the 35-10 loss to the Detroit Lions in Munich.

"I wouldn't anticipate him playing this week," head coach Mike Vrabel recently said of A.J. Brown, who stands with 11 receptions for 128 yards on 26 targets.

New England remained down one starting corner on the eve of Thanksgiving, too. Davis has been limited to five games this fall while battling through a neck injury as well as a knee injury that saw him ruled out ahead the Germany trip.

G Alijah Vera-Tucker (hamstring — IR return)

A shoulder injury sustained on a 42-yard touchdown has left the Patriots without Doubs since October. But the veteran wideout has since ramped up from injured reserve as a limited participant. Ditto for Vera-Tucker, whose activation window opened to begin the week after missing four games along the interior line with a hamstring injury.

They were joined by a fellow March signing in New England's partial group. The fullbacking Gilliam made his way back from injured reserve prior to the meeting at Allianz Arena.

A contributor at left guard, right guard and center has graduated to full participation after missing time with a knee injury sustained in early November versus the Green Bay Packers.

Ben Brown's third season in Foxborough includes seven appearances and 282 offensive snaps as part of a line that's started six different combinations. He has made four starts through the attrition.
