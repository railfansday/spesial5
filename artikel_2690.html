Steelers activate DT Derrick Harmon

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Defensive tackle Derrick Harmon is set to return to the Steelers lineup against the Bengals on Sunday night.

The Steelers announced that they have activated Harmon from injured reserve on Saturday. Harmon returned to practice this week after missing the last four games with a foot injury.

Harmon had 12 tackles, 1.5 sacks, and a forced fumble in the first four games of the season. The 2025 first-round pick had 27 tackles, three sacks and a fumble recovery during his rookie season.

The Steelers released defensive back Terrell Smith in a corresponding move. He was signed off the Commanders’ practice squad in October and made one tackle in his lone appearance for Pittsburgh.
