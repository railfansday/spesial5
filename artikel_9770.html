Why do closed businesses still show as open on Google Maps?

Closures are easy to miss, because the signals around a closed business can keep looking alive. In our test, one closed chain's phone line still answered with its usual menu weeks after the company announced on its own website that every location had closed.

How do you check if a business is permanently closed?

Look for independent evidence: a closure notice on the business's own website, a registry or obituary record, a relocation notice, or a disconnected phone line. When the evidence conflicts, call the business. To do this at scale, the VOYGR Enrich API returns an open_closed status (open, closed, unknown or not_exists) for each place you send.
