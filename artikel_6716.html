Stanley Tucci Reveals His Family's Holiday Tradition That Always Ends In Chaos

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The fun starts once everyone stops taking it seriously.

Stanley Tucci has spent years taking audiences on culinary adventures through Italy, so it’s no surprise S.Pellegrino would tap the icon for a holiday collaboration built around the dinner table. Now, he’s teaming up with the brand again, this time on limited-edition S.Pellegrino bottles with labels designed to keep the conversation going even after the meal turns cold.

Tucci has partnered with the brand since 2022, but he’s been a fan for far longer. In fact, he even has a trick for using it in a martini. “If you make a martini on the rocks—vodka martini, gin martini, whatever you want—a little splash of San Pellegrino in it is great,” he shares with me. “It gives it a little lift.”

Cocktails aside, one thing he thinks his fans would be surprised to learn is that the devout coffee drinker sometimes enjoys a cup of rooibos tea. “I find it very comforting, particularly when you’re on set, making a movie. That, with a little bit of honey,” he says of how he enjoys the drink.

His actual holiday traditions can get a little more animated. “Around the holidays, sometimes we’ll play charades with the kids in the evening, and it’s just fun because I don’t like to keep score,” he shares. “The fun part of it is watching people get up and trying to get a point across; it’s hysterical to me.”

It’s the generational divide, though, that really makes him laugh.

“What I love about charades too is when you play with little kids,” he says. “They’ll get upset because they don’t fully understand it—they’ll get upset if you get it quickly, which is sort of the point, because they want to stay up there and keep doing it, as opposed to the adult who just wants it to be over with.”

So while the new S.Pellegrino bottles may get people talking, it’s what unfolds afterward that seems to stick with Tucci most.

His favorite holiday moments are the ones “when people just suddenly start bursting into song, or say, ‘Hey, let’s play charades,’ and then the charades go on forever, all the rules go out the window, and nobody really keeps track of who’s winning or losing,” he says. “That’s the most fun.”
