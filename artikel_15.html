Singer-songwriter Mélissa Laveaux: 'Women need more positions of power in the music industry'

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

One of France's top musical artists has spoken to FRANCE 24 about the need to empower more women in music. Mélissa Laveaux is currently taking part in the "Les Femmes s'en mêlent" or "Women get involved" festival, which is holding a series of events across the country in an initiative that started nearly 30 years ago and is still going today. The aim is to address the underrepresentation of female artists at music festivals. She spoke to us in Perspective.
