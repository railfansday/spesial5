New US tariffs: 'Trump lashing out really indiscriminately', expert says

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The United States on Wednesday unveiled proposed tariffs on 14 economies including the European Union and China, after a months-long probe into alleged excess production. Speaking with FRANCE 24's Sharon Gaffney, Dean Baker, Senior Economist at the Centre for Economic and Policy Research, says that "there has to be some mechanism for better coordinating China's involvement in the world trade system and ideally the US, the EU, Canada would have a common front, some clear ground rules that we could agree to, that China could agree to, to be mutually beneficial. It's not a step in that direction, we have President Trump lashing out really indiscriminately. It's very hard to see his approach being very productive".
