'Citizens angry with the establishment' as Spain heads to the polls

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Three party leaders are vying in Spain's general election on Sunday, which leftist Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez called following a parliamentary setback over housing legislation that underscored the fragility of his minority coalition government. Speaking with FRANCE 24's Sharon Gaffney, Javier Carbonell, Policy Analyst at the European Policy Centre, says that despite solid economic growth in Spain the population "have suffered a housing crisis and inflation, citizens are still angry with the government and the economic system regardless of macroeconomic data", adding that "in the end citizens are angry with the establishment, in this case it will be Sanchez, but to be honest I think they will be angry at PP and Vox very soon".
