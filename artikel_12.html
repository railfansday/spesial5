EU-Indonesia agreement sees the light after ten years of negotiations

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The European Union and Indonesia have signed a major trade deal, ten years after talks began.The agreement removes almost all tariffs between the EU and Southeast Asia's biggest economy: 98.5 per cent of them. For Europe, it means access to a market of 280 million people and a steadier supply of nickel for batteries. For Indonesia, easier exports of palm oil, textiles and footwear, and it arrives just before its current EU trade preferences end on the 1st of January. To discuss the deal Airlangga Hartarto, Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs in Indonesia joined us on the programme.
