Xi welcomes Trump to China ahead of high-stakes Putin meeting

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

President Xi Jinping personally greeted US counterpart Donald Trump on the tarmac at a Chinese airport on Wednesday, with the two leaders briefly hugging ahead of their highly anticipated meeting with Russia's Vladimir Putin. Beijing has indicated the war in Ukraine will not be discussed during the trilateral meeting, but Trump had said the conflict would find its way onto the agenda, something that experts say is likely to please Putin.
