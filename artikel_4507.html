The Acura Integra Type S Goes Retro in Blazing Phoenix Yellow for 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Acura’s flagship sports car picks up light upgrades, and a special edition that’s a nod to the original Integra Type R.

The Acura Integra Type S made waves when it first launched in 2023—Road & Track even named it a winner of our 2024 Performance Car Of The Year test—but it hasn’t changed much since then. That said, the Japanese luxury automaker is about to give the front-drive sports sedan its first major update.

To celebrate the 30th anniversary of the U.S. debut of the DC2-generation Integra Type R, which was sold here from 1997-2001, Acura is launching the Integra Type S Heritage Edition for 2027. Model-exclusive Blazing Phoenix Yellow paint serves as the highlight of the special edition, but a titanium shift knob and 19-inch, 10-spoke, Copper-finish alloy wheels are also included. Only 700 units will be produced for the U.S. market. The 2027 Integra Type S Heritage Edition will be formally revealed on Saturday, November 14, at the Super Street After Dark event in Santa Ana, California.

“The original Integra Type R became an icon because it delivered an exhilarating yetattainable, and unmistakably Acura performance experience, and the Acura Integra Type S Heritage Edition is an incredible way to honor that legacy,” said Mamadou Diallo, executive vice president of the Auto Business Center at American Honda Motor Co., Inc. “For 2027, we’re taking the Integra Type S even further with refreshed styling and more standard features, enhancing the experience of driving the most powerful, best-performing Integra ever.”

But it isn’t just the Integra Type S Heritage Edition that’ll see changes for the 2027 model year. Gloss black wheels and mirror caps will be standard on regular Integra Type S models; meanwhile, yellow Brembo brake calipers, dark gray trim, and three redesigned upholstery options are available with contrasting yellow, blue, or red perforations and accents. Other key additions include a driver memory seat, mirror reverse tilt-down, and memory brake hold. The flagship sports car will remain mechanically unchanged—not that it needed much in the way of changing in that regard.

Standard 2027 Integra Type S models will be priced at $55,995, including a $1395 destination and delivery fee. Heritage Edition Type S models will start at $58,295, and are set to arrive at dealers this December. The Acura Integra Type S will continue to be built at Honda’s Marysville, Ohio plant.
