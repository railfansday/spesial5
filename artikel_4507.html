2028 Hyundai Elantra N Is a Sporty, Affordable Sedan Worth Waiting For

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The second iteration of the Elantra N promises to bring an even more expressive design and more power thanks to a new 2.5-liter engine.

The performance-focused version of Hyundai’s compact sedan returns for a second generation. Although the Elantra wasn’t the first car to wear the N badge in North America—that would be the unorthodox Veloster N—the sporty four-door has been the performance division’s bread and butter. The first iteration, which arrived in 2022, has provided plenty of smiles in the ensuing years, with a punchy turbocharged engine and an available six-speed manual. The next Elantra N promises to preserve those qualities while bringing a more aggressive look thanks to a widebody design and a more potent engine.

Seemingly out of nowhere, Hyundai has earned a reputation as a maker of fun, enthusiast cars, both in the gas realm and the world of EVs, with the Ioniq 5 N. The Elantra N has carved out a niche with its affordable price and manual transmission, giving Hyundai more credibility among car lovers, and this new model has the chance to cement that spot.

The N model will ride on the same platform as the regular Elantra, which also underpins models such as the Hyundai Kona and Kia K4. The Elantra N will almost undoubtedly get upgrades to make it stiffer and more agile, such as reinforced engine mounts, as well as revised steering and suspension components. The Elantra N is also getting an even wider track than before to improve handling, which will be boosted by meatier tires, with spy photos revealing 275/30ZR-19 rubber (up from the current car’s 245-section-width tires).

The Elantra N is set to debut a new engine that Hyundai tested earlier this year at the 24-hour Nürburgring race. While the automaker has yet to divulge any details on this new engine, it is expected to be a turbocharged 2.5-liter four-cylinder.

Hyundai says the new engine will be more powerful and responsive than the 2.0-liter turbo four found in the current Elantra N, which makes 276 horsepower and 289 pound-feet of torque. We think the new model could reach the 300-hp mark, but there are reports it could crank out as much as 330 horses. The N will also likely remain front-wheel drive, although some rumors suggest the Elantra N could be offered as an all-wheel-drive hybrid, adding an electric motor on the rear axle.

The Elantra N is expected to return with the option of either an eight-speed dual-clutch automatic or a six-speed manual, although the column-mounted gear selector in the regular Elantra has cast some doubt on the latter.

The Elantra N will continue to battle the Volkswagen Jetta GLI and Subaru WRX and serve as a more affordable alternative to the Honda Civic Type R.

Given how bold the regular 2027 Elantra already looks, the more extreme N model could prove to be too flashy for some customers. Hyundai will also need to keep the Elantra N affordable, as its reasonable base price has been one of its strongest selling points in a competitive segment. Ditching the manual transmission could also push away enthusiasts who are looking for the maximum level of engagement.

The Elantra N will likely be revealed in early 2027 and go on sale later next year as a 2028 model. The current Elantra N starts at $36,595, but with a new engine and a significant dose of extra horsepower, we think the starting price for this next-gen N could creep closer to $40,000.
