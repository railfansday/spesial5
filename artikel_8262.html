Personal Consumer Issues • Re: How often do you buy a new car?

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

I love cars and as a result have spent alot of money on them over the years. We have more cars than people in our household. We generally keep our family car for
at least 10 years. The intent is to keep the current family car for a least 10 years. My spouse and I also have sports cars. My plan between now and when I’m no longer able to drive is to trade out my sports car every couple of years. Not the most economically sensible decision, but when I think of the opportunity cost of that money, I’m not sure what I would do with it otherwise that would bring me happiness so cars it is.
