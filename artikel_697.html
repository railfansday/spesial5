Thai Randolph’s NILE & Co. Buys Yahoo’s MAKERS Women’s Leadership Platform

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

NILE & Co., the content and ecommerce startup aimed at women founded and led by media veteran Thai Randolph, has acquired Yahoo‘s MAKERS, the women’s storytelling and leadership platform.

Yahoo has signed on as presenting sponsor of the in-person MAKERS Summit under a two-year commitment. Financial terms of the deal weren’t disclosed.

The MAKERS brand, its library of first-person leadership interviews, the MAKERS Summit, the MAKERS community and its digital channels are now part of NILE & Co.’s portfolio. As part of the transaction, the MAKERS team has joined NILE. Randolph is the founder and CEO of NILE & Co., which stands for “Narrative, Influence, Legacy, Enterprise.” She launched the venture earlier this year after acquiring BuzzFeed’s As/Is, repositioned as a “whole-life platform for high-performing women,” and Goodful, a “wellness-to-wealth platform for women who invest in themselves.” Randolph is the former CEO and co-founder of Kevin Hart’s Hartbeat, which she exited in 2023.

Founded in 2012, Yahoo’s MAKERS over the years has profiled numerous influential entrepreneurs, executives, athletes and “change agents,” including Oprah Winfrey, Shonda Rhimes, Spanx founder Sara Blakely and Billie Jean King. Speakers at its live events have included Goop founder Gwyneth Paltrow, Honest Company founder Jessica Alba, Oscar-winning actress Octavia Spencer, actor-author-producer Constance Wu, and creator and actor Liza Koshy. In addition, Thai Randolph spoke at the 2025 MAKERS Summit.

NILE & Co. said it will expand MAKERS’ community of creators, founders, executives, artists and athletes, partnering with them to develop original content, year-round experiences and new ventures. The goal is to make MAKERS “a place where women tell their stories, find their collaborators, architect their careers and build their enterprises,” according to the company.

“Women drive the consumer economy. As entrepreneurs, they’re turning ideas into businesses and influence into ownership. In sports, they’re reshaping the game and the business around it,” Randolph said in a statement. “They’re making money, making moves, making change and making history. MAKERS brings together the founders, executives, artists, athletes and creators behind that momentum. We want it to be where those worlds meet, where women share what they know and build what comes next.”

Randolph added: “MAKERS has spent more than a decade documenting women making history. Our investment is in the history they make next. History is in the makers. And these women are making it every day.”

The transaction was led by Randolph and Manveer “Monti” Sehmi, NILE’s founding partner and CFO. NILE & Co. was advised by Granderson Des Rochers LLP.

Yahoo is majority-owned by private equity firm Apollo Global Management; former owner Verizon retains a 10% minority stake.
