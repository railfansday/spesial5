‘Club Kids’ Jordan Firstman and Reggie Absolom Recreate ’90s J. Crew Catalogues in New Holiday Campaign

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Variety may receive an affiliate commission.

Jordan Firstman and his 10-year-old “Club Kid” co-star Reggie Absolom are headed to the snow (no, not that kind…) in J. Crew’s newest holiday campaign.

The new spot, titled “Present Company” launched Nov. 17 and takes inspiration from the retailer’s 1990s catalogs. Photographed by Alasdair McLellan and styled by Miyako Bellizzi, the campaign features Firstman and Absolom in J.Crew staples including Fair Isle sweaters, tailored jackets, flannel pajamas and striped layers. The campaign is made up a series of intimate portraits, the two modeling matching sweaters and seasonal layers.

“Jordan and Reggie have an incredible chemistry that feels both genuine and unexpected,” said Julia Collier, J.Crew’s chief marketing officer. “This comes through in the film, of course, and we loved the idea of celebrating that by bringing them together in a classically J.Crew way with a nod to the holidays.”

The campaign arrives on the heels of Firstman’s feature directorial debut, “Club Kid,” which premiered at the Cannes Film Festival earlier this year before A24 acquired distribution rights. The film, which opened in theaters Nov. 6, follows a hard-partying New York City club promoter (Firstman) whose hedonistic lifestyle is upended when he discovers he has a 10-year-old British son (Absolom) he never knew existed. The comedy-drama, which also stars Cara Delevingne and Diego Calva, has earned Firstman Gotham Award nominations for best feature and breakthrough director.

The partnership is the latest in J.Crew’s growing lineup of entertainment-driven campaigns. In October, the retailer tapped comedy trio “Please Don’t Destroy” — Ben Marshall, John Higgins and Martin Herlihy — for a comedic short promoting its suiting collection. Earlier this fall, Emma Chamberlain fronted a campaign for the brand’s updated Tiny Rollneck sweater, while Spike Lee, Bowen Yang, Dave Franco and Jenny Slate were among the 160 personalities featured in a sprawling campaign celebrating the 40th anniversary of J.Crew’s Barn Jacket.

“Present Company” launches Nov. 17 across J.Crew’s digital and social channels. The holiday collection is available in stores and online at J.Crew.com.
