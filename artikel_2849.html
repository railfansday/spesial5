Commanders injury report: Treylon Burks, Chig Okonkwo make strides

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Treylon Burks had a big Tuesday ahead. He needed to work and show the Commanders that he can play again. This was a big practice, and, honestly, the only one where the veteran receiver could prove worthy of playing in Thursday's game against the New York Giants.

Are we sure he's going to go? We are not.

But there's some hope after he was considered limited on the Commanders' official injury report.

Tight end Colson Yankoff was a DNP for the second straight day with a knee injury, putting his availability in a tough spot.

Head coach Dan Quinn also made it clear in his Tuesday press conference that center Lucas Patrick is in the concussion protocol and won't play against the Giants. He clearly stated that Nick Allegretti will start in his place.

In a positive development, tight end Chig Okonkwo was a full participant on Tuesday. That's huge considering Washington's issues at receiver.

C Lucas Patrick – Is in the concussion protocol

TE Colson Yankoff – A knee injury suffered in Week 8

WR Treylon Burks – Suffered foot injury vs 49ers, missed last week's game

TE John Bates – Hurt his shoulder vs. Eagles

QB Jayden Daniels – Still listed with Week 2 elbow dislocation

CB Amik Robertson – Incurred an ankle injury in Week 9

TE Chig Okonkwo – Dealing with a hip injury from Rams game

DL Shy Tuttle – Has a shoulder issue from Week 8
