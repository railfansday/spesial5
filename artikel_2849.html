Raiders TE Brock Bowers day-to-day with ankle injury that was 'not as bad' as feared, HC Klint Kubiak says

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Las Vegas Raiders appear to have averted a crisis: Star tight end Brock Bowers is day-to-day with an ankle injury, Raiders head coach Klint Kubiak told reporters on Monday. That's a much more favorable outcome for an injury that was initially feared to be serious.

Bowers exited Sunday's loss to the Seattle Seahawks early after appearing to suffer the injury during the first quarter. In Monday's press conference, Kubiak said that the team was "still evaluating" Bowers' injury, but that things were better than originally expected.

"It's not as bad as we thought, but still is a significant injury, so we'll keep monitoring him," Kubiak said.

Bowers seemingly hurt his ankle after a first-quarter block, with the tight end going down awkwardly after getting his legs tangled up in a pile. Bowers limped to the sideline after staying down for a moment, and though he later returned to the game, he exited for good in the second quarter. He was ruled out just before the start of the second half.

The Raiders went on to lose the matchup 21-13 after the Seahawks scored two third-quarter touchdowns, behind an impressive performance from backup quarterback Drew Lock. Las Vegas quarterback Fernando Mendoza had some solid moments in his first NFL start, but couldn't pick up the win.

Bowers missed the first two games of the season after undergoing a meniscus trim. But since returning, he has been a crucial piece of the Raiders' offense: The tight end leads the team in receiving with 676 yards and six touchdowns.

If Bowers does end up out this weekend, it'll be a tough blow. The 4-6 Raiders are set to take on the Denver Broncos on Sunday in a big AFC West matchup.
