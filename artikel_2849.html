Rangers Sports Network names Michelle Montaine as new field reporter

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Texas Rangers have found their new field reporter.

The Major League Baseball club announced Friday that Michelle Montaine is joining Rangers Sports Network as a full-time field reporter for the 2027 season (whenever that might happen).

Montaine worked around 20 games in a supplemental role for the RSN last season. She replaces Laura Stickells, who had spent the previous two seasons with Rangers Sports Network. She left to pursue other opportunities.

A University of Texas graduate, Montaine previously worked as a sports anchor for a Tulsa TV station before joining Dallas ABC affiliate WFAA in 2024. She has also worked as a sideline reporter for San Antonio Spurs games. She has also contributed to several national and regional platforms, including CBS Sports, Fox Sports, and Sports Illustrated.

"After contributing to our broadcasts last season, it is clear that Michelle will be a great addition to our team," said Angie Swint, Rangers Senior Vice President, Broadcasting & Executive Producer, Rangers Sports Network, in a statement. "She brings extensive experience covering sports in this market, along with an energy and work ethic that elevates everyone around her."

The Rangers have seen quite a bit of turnover in recent years with their field reporters. Longtime reporter Emily Jones, who joined the team in 2005 as a pre- and post-game host before later transitioning into the reporter role, moved into an ambassador role in 2025.

Hopefully, for Montaine, the elevated role means we won't see a repeat of the very awkward mistaken-identity case from 2024.
