Buffalo Bills: Land Ball Hawk With 5 Interceptions to Boost Secondary

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Buffalo Bills' pass defense remains an issue, especially against high-level quarterbacks rather than backups. The Bills allowed just 195 passing yards over the past two games because Lamar Jackson left on the first play of Week 8, and Tyler Huntley left in the second quarter, forcing Joe Fagnano to make his rookie debut. In Week 9, Carson Wentz missed several open reads before Max Brosmer replaced Wentz in the fourth quarter.

In Weeks 1-6, the Bills allowed 1,453 passing yards and 15 touchdowns against starting quarterbacks. Their average of 242 passing yards allowed per game during that span would rank third-most in the league.

Tennessee Titans safety Amani Hooker (37) catches the ball during warm ups before facing the Minnesota Vikings at Nissan Stadium in Nashville, Tenn., Sunday, Nov. 17, 2024.

According to Tom Pelissero of The Ringer and Netflix Sports, the Bills acquired safety Amani Hooker and a 2027 fifth-round pick from the Titans in exchange for a 2027 second-round pick. Buffalo inherits a $4.47 million salary in 2026, and he will earn an additional $500,000 for recording six interceptions. Additionally, the team will assume Hooker's $9.57 million base salary, with $4.14 million of his salary guaranteed on the fifth day of the new league year and $10.28 million in cash in 2027, and a $11.79 million base salary, $1 million roster bonus due on the fifth day of the new league year, and $13.5 million in cash in 2028, per Spotrac.

Amani Hooker has had a strong start to the season and is currently leading the league in interceptions. Hooker has 52 tackles, one tackle for loss, five interceptions for 114 yards, and six pass deflections across seven games. In his last game, he intercepted Joe Burrow three times and recorded three pass deflections.
