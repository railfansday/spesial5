Week 11 fantasy rankings: Patrick Mahomes projects as fourth-best QB

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The experts at ESPN have ranked Kansas City Chiefs signal-caller Patrick Mahomes as the fourth-best fantasy football quarterback in Week 11 of the 2026 NFL season.

After an impressive 348-yard, five-touchdown performance against the Atlanta Falcons last week, Mahomes has reclaimed his spot as one of the league's best shot-callers.

For those considering starting Mahomes this week, here are his predicted stats against the Arizona Cardinals at Arrowhead Stadium:

Patrick Mahomes should have a fairly easy matchup against the Cardinals defense, which ranks 23rd in the league on total yards allowed.

The addition of wide receiver Josh Downs has already had a significant impact on the Chiefs' offense, and he has clearly earned Mahomes' trust in just one game. I expect the two-time MVP to look Downs' way often this week.

Mahomes will need to be quick on his feet this week with two starting offensive linemen out with injuries. Arizona's pass rush isn't particularly great, but Kansas City's offensive line is extremely battered.

Expect Mahomes to throw for 300+ yards and three touchdowns in Week 11.

Quarterback Patrick Mahomes' best game against the Falcons took place in Week 11 of the 2022 regular season. The two-time MVP threw for an impressive 360 yards on 30 completions and five touchdowns. Mahomes posted a near-perfect passer rating of 144.8 in the season opener.
