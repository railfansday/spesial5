Another win over Tebas: Supreme Court backs Real Madrid in CVC case

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Real Madrid have secured a significant legal victory in their institutional battle with LaLiga over its agreement with investment fund CVC. In an official statement, the club announced that the Supreme Court had dismissed the appeal on points of law filed by the Spanish football league.

This decision makes the earlier ruling by the Madrid Provincial Court final. That ruling had already upheld the annulment of the agreement approved at the General Assembly on August 12, 2021, following a lawsuit jointly brought by Real Madrid and Athletic Club.

The court based the annulment on the fact that the financial agreement was approved without clubs having the information they needed to assess its true impact. The Supreme Court also criticized LaLiga for attempting to impose its own self-serving judgment over established legal safeguards.

As a direct consequence of the ruling, the league, headed by Javier Tebas, must pay the legal costs arising from the appeal.

Real Madrid welcomed the decision as a resounding endorsement of their defense of legality, transparency and good governance within Spanish football.
