Scott Parker’s first West Ham starting XI predicted as Cardiff City clash awaits – Tomas Soucek in

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

West Ham have acted quickly to replace the sacked Nuno Espirito Santo, naming Scott Parker as their new head coach on Wednesday morning. The former WHUFC captain jumps straight into the action too, as he will lead his new club in the clash with Cardiff City later tonight.

Parker will be backed by a first-team coaching staff including Steve Potts, Gerard Prenderville and Mark Noble, who have overseen preparations for the clash with the Bluebirds.

The new boss will need time to work out his strongest starting XI as he gets to grips with the squad at his disposal. However, his first lineup against Cardiff City could see some welcome reintroductions.

Hermansen; Veltman, Mavropanos, Kilman, Morato; Engels, Soucek; Bowen, Kante, Austbo; Piroe.

Parker has primarily played with a 4-2-3-1 formation over his managerial career to date. It is the system West Ham played in under Nuno too, so for the sake of continuity given the lack of time on the training ground under the new boss, it seems inevitable that they start in this setup against Cardiff City.

The new boss prefers his goalkeepers to be proficient in possession, as shown by James Trafford’s success in his Burnley team. As such, Mads Hermansen should keep his place ahead of Alphonse Areola despite recent questions over the Dane’s form.

Kyle Walker-Peters limped off in the defeat to West Brom, so Joel Veltman should start. Morato continues on the left flank, but in a big boost, Konstantinos Mavropanos may return in place of Airidas Golambeckis as he trained on Monday ahead of the game. If he is not fit though, the youngster will keep his spot.

In midfield, Tomas Soucek replacing Divine Mukasa seems a smart move to give Parker and WHUFC a bit more experience and physicality in midfield. He will partner Arne Engels, and Mohamadou Kante should retain his place in a more advanced roles.

Captain Jarrod Bowen is a shoo-in for a start on the right, and on the left, it’s a toss-up between Edvin Austbo and Manor Solomon. The latter is struggling for form though, so could be benched again.

Up top, Joel Piroe could be the proven Championship goalscorer Parker needs. He has Taty Castellanos and Pablo to pick from too.

It is a rarity to see a head coach appointed and lead their first game on the same day. However, Parker is wasting no time in getting to work with his new West Ham group.

He will lean on his coaching staff for the first game, before likely taking on a more leading role in team selection when WHUFC face Blackburn Rovers on Saturday. Parker will have had more time to assess his players and should have a better idea of who he wants in his preferred lineup.

Cardiff City will pose a tough test, as they look to dominate possession and control their games. The Bluebirds are unbeaten at home, winning their last three on their own patch against Birmingham City, Watford and Charlton Athletic.

Tonight’s game kicks off at 19:45 and will be broadcast live on Sky Sports+.
