‘Obvious candidate’: Tony Cottee names who he wants as new West Ham manager over Scott Parker

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

West Ham are in the hunt for a new head coach following their decision to part ways with Nuno Espirito Santo. WHUFC are positioned in 8th place in the Championship table after 13 fixtures and they are in the play-offs along with Wolves, West Brom, Southampton, Birmingham City and Stoke City.

Howe, 48, cut ties with Newcastle United at the end of July after just under six years in the North East. He is now weighing up his next move in the game.

Cottee, who had two spells with the Hammers during his playing days, wants the Buckinghamshire-born man appointed at the London Stadium and has said on talkSPORT: “The obvious candidate, the stand out, the best manager available at the moment is Eddie Howe, whose done a great job at Bournemouth and Newcastle. Could he come to West Ham? I think he could. Does Eddie want it? I don’t know. Is he looking at bigger clubs? I don’t know the situation. Secondly, you could go for a West Ham connection. I’m thinking along the lines of Scotty Parker, Craig Bellamy.”

West Ham are expected to turn to Scott Parker as their replacement for Nuno. He played for WHUFC during his playing days and has managed the likes of AFC Bournemouth, Fulham and Burnley in the past.

As Cottee, Howe would be an eye-catching coup for the Hammers if they manage to get him. However, it appears they have turned their attention to Parker instead.

Howe would also probably have reservations about dropping into the second tier. He has worked at Premier League level for a large chunk of his managerial career.

He guided AFC Bournemouth up the leagues before Newcastle United came calling in November 2021 after their takeover. He then got the Toon Army into Europe during his time there.

Assuming a deal is completed for Parker, he will be under immediate pressure to get West Ham promoted. WHUFC’s proposed new boss has done it before with Fulham, AFC Bournemouth and Burnley so knows what it takes.

A move for Howe is unlikely, despite Cottee’s desire to see him join the Hammers. He is likely to get a position in the top flight at some point down the line after the work he did at Newcastle United.

West Ham lost 3-1 at home to West Brom last time out in a result that proved to be the final straw for Nuno. They have slipped down to 8th and are clinging on to the final play-off spot amid pressure from the likes of QPR and Sheffield United below them.

They are back in action on Wednesday night as they aim to return to winning ways. They face a trip to South Wales to take on Cardiff City.
