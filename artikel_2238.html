HEARTS 1-2 HIBS - PLAYER RATINGS: Which midfielder ran the show at Hampden? Who missed chances to force extra time?

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Published: 12:59 EDT, 1 November 2026 | Updated: 13:09 EDT, 1 November 2026

Hibs ripped up the form book to  claim a thrilling victory over city rivals Hearts in  the Premier Sports Cup semi-final at Hampden.

Despite falling behind to an early header by Claudio Braga, the Premiership's bottom-placed side came roaring back with goals from Martin Boyle and Jamie McGrath.

Hearts piled on the second-half pressure, but Marink Reedijk's side held firm to secure a place in the final against Rangers next month.

After a dramatic derby that will be remembered for years to come, Paul Forsyth assesses the individual performances.
