Celtic vs Kilmarnock: TV channel, live stream and kick-off time

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Celtic return to Premiership action against Kilmarnock at Celtic Park on Sunday.

The sides meet in Glasgow on November 8 with a 4pm kick-off, as Celtic look to strengthen their position at the top of the table. They currently have 28 points, while Kilmarnock sit 11th with nine points.

There will be no live television coverage of the game in the UK, but the match will be available to stream.

The game takes place at Celtic Park on Sunday, November 8.

No. The match has not been selected for live TV coverage in the UK.

Yes. Celtic are making the game available to watch live through CelticPlayer.
