Bitcoin’s $700M liquidation wave deepens – Is crypto’s $2T market cap at risk?

Anthropic’s post-IPO performance appears to have created a volatile market setup.

On the technical side, ANT opened at $111.50, around 34% above its $83 IPO price, before losing much of its early gains and closing at $97.17. This shows that initial demand was strong, but profit-taking quickly kicked in. The impact also seems to have spilled over into crypto, with the total market cap forming its third consecutive lower low at $2.08 trillion, a level last seen in early Q3.

Naturally, this raises a question: Is crypto’s weakness simply a result of ANT-driven volatility? Interestingly, recent Glassnode data points to a possible link. As the chart below shows, Anthropic futures on crypto platforms recorded $9.5 billion in trading volume ahead of its Nasdaq debut. In comparison, SpaceX, the largest IPO on record, attracted $3.9 billion in pre-listing futures volume before its June debut.

In essence, ANT’s pre-IPO futures generated more than 2.4x SpaceX’s.

This highlights how much larger speculative interest in ANT was compared to SpaceX, setting a new record for pre-IPO trading activity on crypto platforms. However, the initial hype quickly turned into profit-taking, with ANT dropping 13% from its opening price. This shift in sentiment may have added to Bitcoin’s [BTC] selling pressure, especially as liquidation activity picked up alongside the broader market breakdown.

So, is Bitcoin’s latest breakdown the start of an overall deleveraging process in crypto markets? In other words, could IPO-driven volatility have accelerated long liquidations, forced overleveraged traders out of positions, and triggered a general market reset?

It looks like ANT’s futures activity on crypto platforms may have added to the selling pressure.

According to CoinGlass, over $700 million worth of positions have been liquidated in the last 24 hours. Of that amount, nearly $600 million was long positions, indicating that leveraged bulls have been badly hit by the correction. However, can this flush-out in Futures markets lead to a rebalance?

Worth mentioning here is the ongoing liquidity drain. In the last 30 days, $11.5 billion has left the stablecoin supply. That outflow is greater than it was in the aftermath of the Terra collapse in 2022. While many in the crypto market are looking at Bitcoin’s price movements, the real problem is the decreasing capital in crypto.

Why does this matter? Lower stablecoin supply means less available liquidity to support buying activity, which could keep pressure on Bitcoin and the broader market.

So, while ANT-related volatility may have contributed to market deleveraging, liquidity is still very poor for a sustained recovery. In this context, ANT’s IPO may have added to an already fragile market, while concerns around its valuation may also “continue” to create a risk-off sentiment in U.S. markets.

Against this backdrop, a drop in the total crypto market cap below $2 trillion cannot be ruled out.
