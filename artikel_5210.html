Following Huang’s resignation as Nvidia CEO, who is the Apple exec being tipped for the permanent role, and how has the NVDA price reacted?

Today (November 12, 2026), Nvidia announced that co-founder and longtime CEO Jensen Huang has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and Board member, effective immediately.

The decision follows intense scrutiny over personal and potential national-security-related controversies. The resignation stems from revelations of two extramarital affairs. The first involved Alice Huang, a businesswoman linked to the Singapore-based AI company Megaspeed, a significant Nvidia customer. US authorities have identified her as a suspected asset of China’s Ministry of State Security.

Reports indicated Huang had been photographed with her on multiple occasions, raising concerns about potential intelligence targeting of the Nvidia CEO.

Huang publicly apologized to employees for being unfaithful to his wife but denied compromising national security or company secrets. The situation escalated further when additional reporting alleged a second relationship with Kimberly Powell, Nvidia’s vice president of healthcare and a direct report to Huang.

These dual revelations prompted Nvidia’s board to open an internal investigation and ultimately accept Huang’s resignation after a late-night meeting.

The combination of personal misconduct involving a subordinate and the national-security implications surrounding the alleged Chinese intelligence connection proved decisive.

These firms include Cisco (where she was CFO of the Business Technology and Operations organization) and Microsoft (where she was CFO of the Server and Tools division), as well as earlier positions at Texas Instruments.

She holds a bachelor’s degree in finance from the University of Arizona and an MBA from Southern Methodist University. During her tenure as CFO, Nvidia’s revenue grew dramatically from roughly $4Bn to over $200Bn as the company became the dominant force in AI accelerators.
