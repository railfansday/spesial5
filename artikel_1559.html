€23m deal is there for Liverpool to complete, Fabrizio Romano confirms it

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Fabrizio Romano has confirmed it. Liverpool have a €23m deal if they want it - they just need to complete things.

Liverpool could use a new left-back. Kostas Tsimikas, currently their no.2 option in the position, will almost certainly leave the club at the end of the season with the expiry of his contract.

The jury remains out on Milos Kerkez, too, meaning it's unclear what kind of full-back Liverpool are after. Do they just want a backup option to replace Tsimikas?

Or maybe a development project who can potentially overtake Kerkez but start as an understudy? Or, maybe, Liverpool just want a new starting left-back - someone to replace the Hungarian from the get-go.

There are certainly options to for any choice there and Liverpool will have some big decisions to make in 2027 as they lock-in their immediate future for the position. And one option there looks particularly tempting.

Archie Brown has a release clauseFabrizio Romano has confirmed that Fenerbahçe left-back, Archie Brown, does indeed have a release clause in his contract. Romano is also sure to point out that 'several clubs' have expressed interest in Brown. It's thought that Liverpool are one of the clubs that Romano is talking about. Turkish outlet Takvim claims the Reds want to sign Brown and could trigger the release clause.

Fabrizio Romano has confirmed that Fenerbahçe left-back, Archie Brown, does indeed have a release clause in his contract. Romano is also sure to point out that 'several clubs' have expressed interest in Brown.

It's thought that Liverpool are one of the clubs that Romano is talking about. Turkish outlet Takvim claims the Reds want to sign Brown and could trigger the release clause.

The player is a particularly interesting one to Premier League clubs. Not only does he boast UEFA Champions League experience and not only is he playing very well, he's also a homegrown talent for English clubs.

It makes Brown incredibly tempting. The 24-year-old looks to be on the cusp of something special and is available for a really good price. The fact he also ticks that homegrown box just makes him near-impossible to pass up.

And without a clear signing at left-back, we're not sure Liverpool will find a better choice. Brown is a very good player and ticks the right boxes. He may well be the Reds' next left-back.
