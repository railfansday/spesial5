Report: Liverpool risk losing academy graduate to Manchester United

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Liverpool's academy is one of the best in the country, and it has produced some world class talents over the years.

The likes of legendary captain Steven Gerrard, Ballon d'Or winner Michael Owen and Champions League hero Jamie Carragher all came through the Reds' academy, but there have been some more recent success stories too.

Over the past decade the likes of Trent Alexander-Arnold, Harvey Elliott, Conor Bradley, Curtis Jones and Caoimhin Kelleher have graduated from the Kirkby Academy to the first-team at Anfield.

One of the most successful products of Liverpool's academy recently has been Jarell Quansah, who now finds himself a regular for England, but the former Red could soon be joining a bitter rival.

Manchester United want Quansah in JanuaryQuansah has enjoyed great success in the Bundesliga with Bayer Leverkusen, and the Englishman has attracted interest from several top clubs. Liverpool reportedly hold a buy-back clause for the summer, but Manchester United are reportedly trying to sign the Englishman before Liverpool get the chance to activate it. That's according to Indykaila, who claims that united have made enquiries to the German club with a sight to sign the 23-year-old in the winter window.

Sunday's result at Anfield showed just how badly United defence needs reinforcing, and it is hard to argue that Quansah wouldn't be a fantastic addition to Mauricio Pochettino's squad.

The 11-capped England international has been almost ever-present for Leverkusen this season and has stepped up his game since leaving Anfield in 2025.

United's interest could prompt Liverpool to accelerate their own interest in Quansah and attempt to sign him in January, but there is no clear place in Andoni Iraola's starting XI for him.

Liverpool may be powerless to stop their biggest rivals from signing one of their brightest academy graduates this winter, something that will sting for the Anfield faithful.
