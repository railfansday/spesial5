McDonald’s Just Brought Some Of Its Most Popular International Dishes To The U.S.

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Why let other countries have all the fun?

You can find a McDonald's in over 100 countries, each offering unique international menu items. But you may not know that you can try some of them in the States—no passport required.

The restaurant at McDonald's Chicago headquarters has featured a special rotating menu of global favorites since opening in 2018. Its newest lineup has officially dropped, and it includes some dishes you don't want to miss.

The menu currently features two sandwiches. The first is Costa Rica's Mushroom Deluxe McCrispy, which comes with a crispy chicken fillet, cooked mushrooms, Swiss cheese, mayo, and crispy onions and garlic. You can also order a Chili Cheese McDouble from Germany that upgrades the classic burger with pickled jalapeños and a hot chili cheese sauce.

The Global Favorites menu wouldn't be complete without McNuggets, either. McDonald's is currently offering a version from Japan with spicy garlic black pepper seasoning.

Instead of the chain's classic fries, you can round out your global meal with Malaysian Twister Fries—otherwise known as curly fries. And to top it all off, there's a Strawberry Cheesecake McFlurry from Canada. (If there's any menu item that deserves a nationwide American debut, it's this one.)

Fast food fans reacted to the new menu with a combination of excitement and serious FOMO. "I'm moving to Chicago just to eat at the HQ full time," one Instagram user wrote. On X, another fan added, "Garlic black pepper nudges? You have my attention."

If you want to try any of these menu items for yourself, you'll have to visit the McDonald's headquarters restaurant at 1035 W. Randolph Street in Chicago. The Global Favorites menu rotates regularly, so don't put off your trip for too long.
