Russell Brand’s Message From Prison Claiming He’s ‘Not Guilty’ Under Investigation for Potentially Breaking Rules

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Russell Brand‘s message from prison on Sunday — in which he asserted his innocence after being convicted of rape and sexual assault — is being investigated by the U.K. Ministry of Justice.

The Guardian reported on Monday that the message Brand’s wife, Laura Gallacher, read out on their “Sunday Service” podcast could have broken prison rules. “Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, God bless you for your support,” Brand wrote in a statement delivered by Gallacher. “As you know, I am not guilty for the offenses I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening.” Related Stories 'Cher Adores Michael Douglas,' Icon's Rep Says After Douglas Disses Her Oscar Win for 'Moonstruck' AFM Dealmakers, Talent Agents and Hollywood Stars Left Stranded by Nationwide Flight Stoppage

A spokesperson for U.K. Prime Minister Andy Burnham told The Guardian: “This is clearly appalling and is being urgently investigated. We expect action to be taken if he’s found to have broken the rules. Prisoners are strictly banned from making statements that cause distress to victims and speaking to the media is only permitted in exceptional circumstances. Prisoners cannot get around these rules by having someone else publish their comments.”

Victims’ Commissioner for London Andrea Simon told BBC News that Brand’s message will “certainly retraumatize his victims and other survivors of rape who have to hear it.”

In a statement to Sky News, U.K. foreign secretary Ed Miliband called Brand’s crimes “heinous and awful,” adding that “it’s revolting that he shows no remorse today in his comments.”

Brand has just started a prison sentence of 10 years and six months after being convicted of three counts of sexual assault and one count of rape in a highly publicized criminal trial in London. The former actor and comedian was found not guilty of two other rape charges and a count of attempted indecent assault.

After Brand’s entertainment career plummeted following sexual assault accusations in 2023, he became a Christian influencer and podcaster. Gallacher added in the podcast that she’s been speaking to Brand and “he’s okay and praying, unsurprisingly.”

She added, “We trust God, and we trust His plan, and we trust everything He’s doing right now.”
