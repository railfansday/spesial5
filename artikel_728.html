John Summit’s Tour Bus Involved in Deadly Crash in Florida

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The popular DJ's production crew was headed to Miami, where he's scheduled to perform this week for his CTRL Escape Arena Tour.

John Summit‘s tour bus was reportedly involved in a deadly crash in Florida Monday morning.

A source told TMZ that members of the popular DJ’s tour production crew were on board at the time of the crash that happened in Marion County.

Summit, who took to X earlier in the morning to slam the Grammys after he didn’t earn any nominations this year, was reportedly not on the bus when the wreck occurred.

A spokesperson for Florida Highway Patrol told The Hollywood Reporter that troopers responded to the fatal crash at approximately 8:58 a.m. The bus was stopped facing north and a sedan was headed east in an unknown lane of travel. “For reasons currently under investigation, the Toyota sedan traveled off the roadway in a southeast direction and collided with the front of the stopped tour bus,” authorities said. “The driver of the tour bus, a 64-year-old Louisiana man, was wearing a seatbelt and was not injured. Nine adult passengers aboard the bus were also uninjured.”

The spokesperson said the driver of the sedan, a 40-year-old man from Ocala, was not wearing a seatbelt. “He was transported to a local hospital, where he was later pronounced deceased. The crash remains under investigation,” authorities stated.

Summit is scheduled to perform two shows at Kaseya Center in Miami on Friday and Saturday for his CTRL Escape Arena Tour. TMZ also reported that another bus picked up the DJ’s production crew as they continued to Miami.

THR has reached out to Summit’s rep for comment.

About 30 minutes before the crash, Summit took to X to call the Grammy Awards “a fucking joke” after getting snubbed from the nominations list this year. “I’m not just talking about myself either there’s zero respect for real electronic dance music & dj’s,” he wrote. “The whole dance category is just music industry fueled pop stars.”
