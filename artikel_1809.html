‘I play him because I want to win games’ – Klopp backs Havertz ahead of Netherlands clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Jurgen Klopp has backed Kai Havertz to deliver for Germany despite his recent struggles in front of goal, describing the Arsenal striker as a "top player".

Ahead of Germany's Nations League clash with the Netherlands, the former Liverpool boss defended Havertz, who has been a regular starter under Klopp when fit.

Havertz started Germany's 3-1 victory over Serbia last Friday but has found the net just once in his last five international appearances.

Germany must beat the Netherlands on Monday to progress to the quarter-finals, and Klopp is trusting Havertz to lead the line.

"I've played against Kai many times, and I can tell you that preparing for a game against him is no walk in the park," Klopp said in his press conference.

"You get a real sense of just how complete a player he is. You always have to be prepared for him to produce an outstanding performance because he has all the qualities to do so.

"He's a top player. Mikel Arteta is someone who, as a person and as a coach, is absolutely determined to win. And unless several negative scenarios all come together, Kai Havertz starts games for Arsenal.

"The fact that he plays for Germany is not simply because I don't want to leave him out, but because I want to win games.

"Take the game against Serbia, for example. Kai put in an outstanding performance. He could have scored two goals and, under certain circumstances, perhaps even should have.

"What interests me is his potential and what we can achieve together with him going forward, rather than dwelling on what has happened so far.

"I've watched Kai Havertz – I wouldn't claim to have seen every single international match, but whenever Kai Havertz has been on my radar, I've considered him an absolute top player.

"So I'm really looking forward to working with him. And if there's anything more we can get out of him, I'm pretty confident that we'll manage to do that together."

Against Serbia, Havertz registered three shots (one on target), six touches in the opposition box and two chances created, while accumulating an individual expected goals (xG) value of 0.81 – ranking second only to Florian Wirtz across all four metrics.
