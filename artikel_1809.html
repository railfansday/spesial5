TMW: A ‘try-out’ at San Siro – Milan to watch Frosinone star up close amid interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Gabriele Calvani is expected to start for Frosinone against AC Milan at San Siro, and it could be an audition of sorts.

TMW report that the game will be a chance for Calvani to show off his qualities, especially in front of those who are following him closely in view of the winter. Milan’s management will watch the defender up close, who is currently owned by Genoa and on loan to the Ciociaria.

He has been added to the Rossoneri’s list of potential reinforcements. In recent weeks, Milan representatives have been watching Frosinone, also to closely evaluate the Italy U21 international. After leaving the Azzurrini training camp with a hamstring problem, the defender will start tonight.

Calvani will face Goncalo Ramos and Christian Pulisic at San Siro, so the task won’t be an easy one. However, the 22-year-old has already demonstrated his ability to combine physical strength, speed and reading of the game.

These are characteristics that make him an attractive fit for Ruben Amorim’s style, which requires his defenders to be proactive and sometimes survive in one-on-one situations. Milan are looking for reinforcements for January too, so things could line up.

The top choice remains Gonçalo Inacio, previously coached by Amorim at Sporting CP, but the Portuguese club’s financial demands are pushing them to consider other options. Among these is Calvani, for the reasons mentioned, but also because he knows the league already.

Not only that, but the report mentions that Genoa being the club where Calvani is permanently registered is not as big an issue as it might seem, given that Milan have a great relationship with the Rossoblu.
