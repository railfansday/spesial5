Leny Yoro: Why Manchester United star was sent off vs Liverpool as statement issued

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Leny Yoro was sent off to mark a nightmare start for Mauricio Pochettino as Manchester United manager at Liverpool.

The Argentine head coach could only watch on in horror as his new side were thumped 4-0 at Anfield, thanks to goals from Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz.

The Reds took full advantage of their visitors’ red card on eight minutes, when Yoro was penalised for hauling down Alexander Isak as the Swede sped through on goal.

It stemmed from a Matthijs de Ligt mistake, after the Dutchman, making his first Premier League appearance in over a year after a persistent back injury, misjudged the flight of a clipped ball over the top from Jeremy Jacquet.

Isak seized upon the error, Yoro failed to cover his central defensive partner in time, and the 20-year-old was forced to fell the advancing striker before he could get a shot away at Senne Lammens’ goal.

The Premier League Match Centre explained the decision to send the young French defender for an early bath with a post on its social media channels.

“The referee’s call of red card to Yoro for denial of an obvious goal-scoring opportunity (DOGSO) was checked and confirmed by VAR,” the statement read.

Referee Sam Barratt explained to his video assistant referee: “It's a red card for DOGSO. On-field decision is outside [the box], red card, DOGSO.”

The VAR replied: “Defender bundles into him into the back, gets too tight, I think it's a foul.”

Yoro’s last-ditch challenge ultimately went punished regardless, as Szoboszlai stepped up to slam home the resulting free kick, arrowing into Lammens’ far corner to open the scoring.

The result leaves United mired in the Premier League relegation zone, in 19th place, after just one win in 12 games in all competitions.
