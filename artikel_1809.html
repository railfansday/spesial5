Report: Zlatan Ibrahimovic eager to sign Liverpool star, talks look certain

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Zlatan Ibrahimovic is reportedly eager to sign a Liverpool star. Talks look absolutely certain at this point as AC Milan look to move.

AC Milan are looking to rebuild a bit after slumping post-title win in 2022. They've only won Serie A twice since 2004, in fact, and Zlatan Ibrahimovic played a role in both.

He was the star back in 2011. He'd signed there from Barcelona, getting out of the way of an emergent Lionel Messi and winning the Scudetto with a third Italian side. Ibrahimovic would then rejoin the club as the last stop in his career, scoring eight times as they won the title in 2022.

And now the Swede is involved again, though not as a player. He's helping to lead the club from a director's role, essentially as a proxy for the club's ownership.

The position has him lining up a bold move for one Liverpool star, too. Or maybe even more than that.

Gazzetta Dello Sport claim that Milan have Virgil van Dijk high on their shortlist. The Italian giants hope the Liverpool captain fancies a move there at the end of the season when his contract expires.

It’s an interesting one. Milan know they stand no chance of winning a financial race for Van Dijk. Clubs elsewhere can offer far more money but they feel the sentimental battle might be in their favour.

Van Dijk has made no secret about his admiration for Paolo Maldini and Alessandro Nesta in the past. The historic centre-backs of previous Milan sides might just appeal to him - certainly, that’s the hope.

The report mentions that Ibrahimovic himself is also pushing hard for the move. Talks look near-certain, too, given it’s only a month-and-a-half until Van Dijk is completely free to talk with non-English clubs.

So this is one to watch. It’ll all depend on what Van Dijk wants from what it almost certainly his final major contract in football and a final chance to lock down time chasing for elite honours.

No one seems to have a proper idea of what he’ll actually do. We’re still hoping that Liverpool persuade him to stay, of course.
