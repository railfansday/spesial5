Lego Yoshi set for adult collectors confirmed by Nintendo

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Nintendo has confirmed a new Lego Super Mario set featuring Yoshi, with the character set to receive another brick-built model aimed at adult collectors.

The announcement appeared in the Nintendo Today app in November, showing a close-up of Yoshi’s face alongside the message “New Lego Super Mario Set Hatching Soon.”

While the full model hasn’t been revealed, the image shows Yoshi’s eyes, nose, and cheeks recreated using Lego bricks, suggesting a much larger build than the smaller figures found in the Super Mario range.

The set is part of Lego’s “Adults Welcome” collection, which already includes larger Nintendo-themed models such as those for Bowser, Piranha Plant, and Mario and Luigi in their respective Mario Kart vehicles.

However, it’s unclear whether Yoshi will be released as a standalone figure or accompanied by something else, such as a kart of his own.

This isn’t the character’s first appearance in a Lego set aimed at adults. In 2024, Lego released Super Mario World: Mario & Yoshi, a 1,215-piece model recreating the characters’ appearance in the classic SNES game.

Nintendo hasn’t confirmed a price, piece count, or release date for the new set.

The announcement does feature a 2027 copyright notice, suggesting it could arrive next year. Previous leaks suggest Lego plans to release several Mario-themed sets over the next 12 months.
