Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando Are Married

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando have officially tied the knot. Entertainment Tonight reported that they got married in Palm Springs on November 21.

Few details about their wedding have emerged, which isn’t surprising given that the couple kept most of the planning private. In March 2026, a source told People that they weren’t interested in having a big wedding—instead, they were envisioning a “smaller and meaningful” celebration.

Cyrus had “ideas” about what she’d like, “but she’s not planning some huge, traditional event,” the source added. “Maxx is extremely private, and that’s just not who he is. When they do get married, it will be something smaller and meaningful to them. Not a big Hollywood production.”

The Bass Persuades singer announced her engagement in December 2025, unveiling the bombé cushion-cut diamond ring Morando gave her at the Avatar: Fire and Ash premiere as she posed with him.

In July, Morando spoke to Wonderland about how he first met Cyrus in 2021. “Sam in my band [Liily], his sister’s friend knew Miley,” he said. “They just put us on a blind date. Which, from Miley’s perspective, I don’t think is really possible, because she’s so in the public eye. So it felt like if there was a way to have a blind date for her, this might be the most blind date that she could do.”

They connected during the date and “just never stopped hanging out,” he said.

It took Morando some time to adjust to the attention Cyrus receives. “When I first met her, that part of it was strange,” he said. “I’ve never experienced people waiting outside places for you and things like that. But as time’s gone on, now it’s kind of the inverse. Whenever we go to some event, that’s when I am reminded that she is who she is, you know. But I love going with her to certain things to support whatever she’s doing.”
