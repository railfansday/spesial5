Tom Holland and Zendaya Wore a Couple of Fantastic Gold Watches to the Governors Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The timepieces themselves? Drastically different. The vibes? Entirely in sync.

Tom Holland and Zendaya were among the stars attending this past weekend’s Governors Awards in Los Angeles, where both made interesting choices when it came to their watches.

Holland, dressed in a black double-breasted Louis Vuitton dinner suit, white shirt, and black bow tie—with tan leather Christian Louboutin boots—was photographed pulling back his cuff to reveal a rose-gold Tank Louis Cartier on a leather strap.

Introduced in 1922, it's one of watchmaking's great enduring designs, with a list of famous admirers that includes Andy Warhol, Yves Saint Laurent, and Princess Diana.

Zendaya, meanwhile, went vintage. Her choice was a small, chic, solid-gold Rolex dating from the 1960s, believed to be the very watch she bought at a vintage market in her hometown of Oakland just over a week ago.

According to the San Francisco Chronicle, the actress spotted the watch at a stall run by independent dealer Wrist Glow Co., tried it on, went off to browse and returned an hour later to buy it. The price? A little over $6,000.

Watch-spotters have identified the Rolex as a ladies' cocktail watch, reference 8196, although that identification has yet to be independently confirmed.

So, what have we learned? Perhaps that for two stars who do old-school Hollywood glamour so well, there are few better choices for an awards-night watch than a classic in gold. Which one you go for, though, remains a matter of personal taste.
