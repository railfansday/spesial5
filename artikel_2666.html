Brock Bowers &quot;should be good to go next week&quot;

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Raiders tight end Brock Bowers was limited to 11 snaps on Sunday, due to an ankle injury that knocked him out of the game against the Seahawks. Current indications are that Bowers will be back in Week 11.

Per a source with knowledge of the situation, the initial assessment was that there seemed to be nothing structural and nothing serious, and that he "should be good to go next week" at the Broncos.

The Raiders, who are hanging around the outer fringes of playoff contention at 4-6, could pull themselves into the chase for a wild-card berth.

At some point, however, the current 1-6 slide needs to end. Sunday's annual return to Denver provides a perfect opportunity to do that. But it won't be easy; the Broncos are 7-2 and they'll be well rested for the game, given that they were off in Week 10.
