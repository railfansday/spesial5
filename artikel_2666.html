The real reason John Harbaugh opted to receive after Giants won coin toss

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

John Harbaugh was prepared for a curveball just minutes before kickoff.

The Giants — like most NFL teams — typically defer their choice to the second half if they win the opening coin toss. But they broke from the norm last game by choosing to receive.

Because of some strategic advantage specific to the Commanders?

No, because cornerback Tae Banks wouldn't have been ready if the Giants started the game on defense, backup Colton Hood was already banged up (dislocated finger) and backup Paulson Adebo was a healthy scratch.

It shows the level of thought and quick-thinking adjustments needed to be an NFL head coach.

"We had a little issue come up right before, in warmups, where … actually Tae got dinged on his shoulder, and he was getting looked at," Harbaugh said.

"So we weren't going to have him for the first series. So that being the case, we figured hopefully he'll be out here for the second series."

Gregory Desrosiers returned the opening kickoff 24 yards, and the offense picked up two first downs before punting.

"Offense was gracious enough to at least put some plays out there and buy some time," Harbaugh said, "and [Banks] was able to play. So that determined that decision."

Giants head coach John Harbaugh speaks at a press conference in East Rutherford, N.J., on Nov. 18, 2026. Robert Sabo for NY Post

CB Nic Jones (knee) did not participate in Wednesday's practice.

OLB Malik Harrison (groin) and S Jason Pinnock (hip) were limited participants.

Returner Charlie Jones and CB Nikko Reed, who were cut Monday, returned to the practice squad. OL Geno VanDeMark and RB Eric Gray were released.

Struggling kicker Dominic Zvada, who has missed six field goals this season, was in on the Giants' off day Monday to work on his technique issue from the right hash mark.

"That's probably the thing you can work on the most for that position. Probably more than any other position," Harbaugh said.

"He had a great day [Wednesday]. He was dead on. He hit the middle pole — the camera pole — numerous times on it on kicking field goals. And from that amazing, scary right hash. Just nailed it."
