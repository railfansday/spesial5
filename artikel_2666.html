Bo Nix apologizes for letting Broncos down vs. Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Bo Nix apologizes for letting Broncos down vs. Steelers appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Bo Nix suffered a disasterclass performance in the Denver Broncos' 27-5 loss to the Pittsburgh Steelers on Friday, calling himself out after the game.

Nix has been playing in the NFL since 2024, going through his third season with the Broncos. He stands out as one of the rising quarterbacks in the league, elevating Denver to serious playoff contention.

Denver is off to a great start this season, having one of the best records in the league. They had solid momentum going into the Pittsburgh contest, but they severely struggled against the Steelers' defense. Nix was the biggest negative in the game, throwing interceptions and losing a fumble. The quarterback reflected on his struggles after the loss, holding himself accountable.

"I let the team down today," Nix said, per reporter Zac Stevens.

Bo Nix and the Broncos have some work to do after falling to the Steelers in brutal fashion. Only scoring a safety and a field goal won't cut it anywhere, especially as Denver seeks to be in contention for Super Bowl 61 this season.

Pittsburgh controlled the game from start to finish, giving up all five points in the second quarter. The Steelers did most of their damage in the first half with 24 points, doing everything on defense to shut down the Broncos' attack.

Nix had a terrible night to forget as Pittsburgh kept him in check. He only completed 22 out of 40 passes for 169 yards and two interceptions.

Jonah Coleman was a bright spot in the offense, racking up 15 carries for 26 yards while catching eight passes for 46 yards. Meanwhile, Jaylen Waddle led receivers with 53 yards on five catches.

The Broncos will look to bounce back in their next matchup, being at home. They host the Miami Dolphins in Week 13 as kickoff commences on Dec. 6 at 4:05 p.m. ET.
