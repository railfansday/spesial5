Josh Hokit’s teammate makes bizarre hospital revelation after UFC 334

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

JoshHokit's teammate, JaredMcLoughlin, believes the Ultimate Fighting Championship heavyweight deserved betterafter his failed title bid at UFC334.Hokit challenged Ciryl Ganefor the heavyweight title in the main event of UFC 334 on Saturdayat Madison Square Garden in New York. While Hokit started strong,Gane rallied in the championship rounds to secure a closelycontested unanimous decision win. McLoughlin recently discussed theaftermath of the bout, slamming the hospital Hokit was sent to.

McLoughlin, Hokit's teammate at Jackson-Wink MMA, was in his corner at UFC 334. He revealedthat both Hokit and Gane were sent to the hospital forprecautionary scans after their fight.McLoughlin claims that the room Hokit was sent to had a bottle ofurine in the corner. He also says the hospital was shoddy overalland believes "The Incredible Hok" deserved better treatment afterputting on one of the most exciting fights in heavyweighthistory."Why did they send us to the most skid row hospital in NYC?"McLoughlin said on his YouTube channel. "They put us in this roomin the corner, we're sitting here for an hour, look over, andthere's a jug of piss sitting in the corner. Our nurse picked it upwhen we pointed it out… But why is it there? The guy just, maybe,won the heavyweight title… He at least put on the greatestheavyweight title fight in the history of the sport. And they sentus to a room with a gallon of piss in the corner… Is it like, 'Itwas 48-47 him, you're going to have to hang out in the room with abottle of piss.' Is that how it works?"McLoughlin further claimed they waited at the hospital for scanslate into the night, while Gane was discharged in four hours.
