Blocker and Popoola give DePaul 65-56 win over Purdue in Fort Myers Tip-Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

FORT MYERS, Fla. (AP) — Layden Blocker scored 16 points, Ade Popoola had 15, and DePaul downed Purdue 65-56 in the Fort Myers Tip-Off on Tuesday night.

Blocker was 6-of-10 shooting and Popoola grabbed five rebounds for the Blue Demons (6-0), who have won their first six games for the second time in the Chris Holtmann era.

Koree Cotton had 13 points (4-for-9 shooting), and Fabian Flores paced DePaul with six rebounds.

DePaul lost the rebound battle 36-27, but held Purdue to just 35% shooting (14% from deep) after entering the game ranked seventh in the nation and pacing the Big East in shooting defense.

The Boilermakers had an early 10-0 run that put them ahead, but DePaul chipped away at the lead and trailed just 33-32 at the break.

The Blue Demons pulled away down the stretch thanks to 7-0, 5-0, and 6-0 runs to secure a nine-point victory.

Luke Ertel scored 12 points on 4-of-9 shooting for the Boilermakers (4-3), and Gicarri Harris added 11 points and six rebounds. Purdue had 16 total offensive rebounds, led by Caden Pierce, Jack Benter, and Ertel with two each.

DePaul: plays Pitt in the Beach Division championship game on Thursday.

Purdue: plays Oklahoma in a consolation game on Thursday.
