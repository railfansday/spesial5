Not trading for A.J. Brown Rams best move of the offseason?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Rumors and or reports came out during the 2026 offseason that the Los Angeles Rams had reached out to Philadelphia Eagles to inquire about adding wide receiver A.J. Brown, but that trade between L.A. and Philly never came to be. It may have been lucky, and perhaps the best move of the Rams' offseason.

Brown has done little to nothing for his new team in New England to justify their trade, and while L.A. is having receiver injuries and issues of their own, would Brown have added depth or another piece for Sean McVay and Matthew Stafford to utilize?

Knowing what know now, no. Instead of helping, Brown would've been an even bigger question mark on a wounded Rams team that has enough question marks of its own without the injured Brown.

The Rams made a couple big trades over the offseason include trading for cornerback Trent McDuffie. You can make the argument that McDuffie was the best move by Les Snead this past year. Myles Garrett was another newsworthy move by the Rams leading up to the 2026 regular season, but Garrett's been hurt. Aside from one dominant game against the Washington Commanders, Garrett hasn't done too much on the field for L.A.

As good as the McDuffie trade looks so far, can you imagine how much trouble the Rams would be in had they traded for Brown? Maybe Brown would've come to L.A. and stayed healthy, but if we accept that trade while keeping everything else the same, Brown would have been a disaster for the Rams.

Through the 2026 season, Brown has 11 receptions for 128 yards with only four games played. He's been hurt, he's been ineffective, he's looked like a bad trade for the Patriots, and an addition by subtraction for the Philadelphia Eagles who moved on from him.

It is unclear when Brown will suit back up for the Patriots this year as he continues to deal with injuries.

It was never quite clear or revealed what the Rams planned to trade for Brown or what the Eagles wanted, but one possibility was trading Davante Adams for Brown to the Eagles, or sending Adams elsewhere so the Rams could make room for Brown on their own roster. It was unlikely that the Rams would have been able to keep all of Puka Nacua, Adams and Brown due to cap space.

Regardless of the exact trade details, Adams in comparison to Brown, has had a fine 2026 season despite his own injuries. Through 2026 Adams has 30 receptions, 480 yards and four touchdowns in six games played. This includes a massive 195-yard performance against the New York Giants.

As it stands, had the Rams sent some combination of Adams and or draft capital for Brown, then the Rams would likely be sitting with a worse record than 6-4, less draft capital, and an even murkier future with Brown being stuck on L.A.'s roster.

In 2026, Brown has been out-played and out-produced by basically every other receiver on the New England's roster including DaMario Douglas, Kyle Williams and Romeo Doubs. It's unclear if Brown will be able to return to the form that made him desirable via trade in the first place.

McVay and Snead aren't perfect, but they seemed to get this move right. Brown in 2026 would have been a liability to the Rams and not a boost.

Sure, there's a world where Brown looked great in 2026 and the Rams regretted not adding him, but this isn't that world. Instead, we are in the world where the Rams clearly made two (maybe three) great offseason moves heading into 2026, and not adding Brown may be the best move the Rams made.

Life isn't all about saying "yes", it's knowing when to say "no" too, and we'll continue to see how these moves and decisions age, but so far with regard to the Rams passing on Brown, so good.
