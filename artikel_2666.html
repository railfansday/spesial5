Rangers lines, notes, how to watch Black Friday road game against Blackhawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A pair of banged-up Original 6 teams clash on Black Friday, when the New York Rangers visit the Chicago Blackhawks at United Center.

The Rangers (13-10-1) try to get back on track after losing two straight, including 5-1 at the Buffalo Sabres on Thanksgiving Eve. Gabe Perreault became the latest Rangers player to land on IR following that game; the 21-year-old forward is out 4-6 weeks with a lower-body injury.

Perreault joins a long list of Rangers on injured reserve, including forwards Pavel Dorofeyev, Oliver Bjorkstrand, Joe Veleno, and defenseman Alberts Smits. Star goaltender Igor Shesterkin is on season-ending injured reserve.

To date, the Rangers have 98 man-games lost to injury already this season, a number that will only increase in the coming weeks with so many players out longer-term. There is a sliver of hope that Dorofeyev, who's missed three games with a lower-body injury, may return Friday after practicing Thursday, but that feels more unlikely than likely.

The Rangers recalled forward Juuso Parssinen from Hartford of the American Hockey League on Thanksgiving Day. It'll be interesting to see where he fits, and what the Rangers line combinations look like against the Blackhawks with so many regular forwards out of the lineup.

No matter who plays or not, the Rangers must find a way to dial up some offense against the Blackhawks (9-14-2), who are the third-worst defensive team in the NHL statistically, allowing an average of 3.84 goals per game. During their two-game skid, the Rangers managed three goals, and only one by Vladislav Gavrikov at even strength. They are now tied for 21st in the League, averaging 2.92 goals per game.

Chicago's shown some life recently, however, despite losing Connor Bedard for roughly four months following shoulder surgery. The Blackhawks are on a season-high three-game winning streak — all on the road — and outscored the Calgary Flames, Vancouver Canucks, and Seattle Kraken 13-7 in that span. That's saying something, considering Chicago has a minus-30 goal differential overall, tied for second worst in the League.

The Blackhawks are also without key defenseman Bowen Byram. But they do have red-hot future Hall of Famer Patrick Kane. The former Rangers forward has nine points (one goal, three assists) during a six-game point streak. Forward Roman Kantserov is also hot, with six goals and eight points during a three-game goal/point streak. He leads NHL rookies with 15 goals this season.

Dylan Garand is expected to start in goal fort he Rangers, after Joonas Korpisalo made 25 saves Thursday against the Sabres. In his most recent start, Garand stopped a career-high 36 saves but allowed four goals (there was also an empty-netter) in a 5-2 loss to the Carolina Hurricanes.
