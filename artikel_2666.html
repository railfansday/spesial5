Knicks coach Mike Brown compliments Raptors coach despite him mocking Jalen Brunson during clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Toronto Raptors head coach Darko Rajakovic made it clear that he was not a fan of New York Knicks guard Jalen Brunson flopping during their game.

The Knicks picked up a 111-102 win over the Kawhi Leonard-less Toronto Raptors on November 17 to improve their record to 8-6 for the season.

Brunson is overcoming his slow start to the season and put up 31 points, five rebounds and five assists in the win. However, his on-court antics seem to run Raptors coach Rajakovic the wrong way.

Rajakovic was caught on camera imitating Brunson for flopping after a shot, with cameras catching how openly Rajakovic mocked Brunson after that play.

Nonetheless, Brown was positive about Rajakovic after the game.

"Darko does a heckuva job…those guys play hard, really good defensively, give us fits. I thought our execution, especially down stretch at a pretty high level both ends of the floor, in particular, offensively… Our pace, spacing, screening, take care of basketball down stretch.

Brunson had the last laugh, leading the Knicks to a much-needed win over the Raptors and establishing themselves as an East contender after a slow start to the season.

Brunson is averaging 27.0 points, 5.9 assists and 2.9 rebounds this season, and shooting 7.2 free throws per game. He ranks 11th in free-throw attempts this season, so his flopping hasn't helped him get to the line much.

Brunson's scoring is the key offensive weapon the Knicks are built around, evidenced by his performances in their run to the 2026 NBA Championship.

While Rajakovic did not do anything excessive in terms of complaining about Brunson's actions, he did display his feelings in a hilarious fashion.

This was Toronto's fifth straight loss of this season, falling to 5-9 on the season. Rajakovic has the support of the organisation and likely felt comfortable calling out Brunson with his gesture instead of biting his tongue.

However, complaining about calls will not change the Raptors' fortunes this season, as evidenced by the loss to the Knicks.
