What’s wrong with the Boston Celtics? (Topic Tuesday)

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Life comes at you fast, huh? A week ago, the Boston Celtics were 11-2 and looking like one of the best teams in the league. There was a lot of "better without you-know-who" talk and Tatum was in the MVP discussion. After four straight losses, including (at least) 2 really bad ones, the umbrellas are out and the forecast is calling for heavy skyfall.

So what happened? Or more specifically, what's going wrong? This is literally the same team that was 11-2, but now the results are different. They played a lot of bad teams early in the schedule, but at least 2 of the teams in this last stretch are bad and/or shorthanded. We can't even blame injuries for this stretch (aside from load management and illness).

Did the league crack the code on the Celtics offense? Is it just a prolonged shooting slump? Are they falling into old bad habits? Are the rotations bad? Are they playing with the rotations too much? Did Joe Mazzulla forget how to coach? Did they stop listening to him? Is it all the refs fault? (Miss you Tommy)

Yes, I know that we're not at 20 games yet. I'm just starting a discussion that may not have a definitive conclusion. And before we get too carried away, it is worth noting that last year's team was 9-8 at this point. All the games count, but a 4 game losing streak is a lot better in November than it is in the Spring. There's still a lot of season left to burn through.

The next logical question is "what does this team need?" Followed shortly by "what moves can they make?" I'm not prepared (mentally, emotionally, whatever) to address that topic yet, but you are welcome to start it here.
