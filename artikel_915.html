Enzo Maresca insists Man City’s slump is ‘normal’ after Pep Guardiola exit

Enzo Maresca remains unconcerned by Manchester City’s recent slump in form, maintaining that a period of adjustment was to be expected during the club's "reset" following Pep Guardiola's departure.

After opening his debut campaign with seven consecutive Premier League wins, the manager has seen his side go four league games without a victory while trying to establish balance in midfield and defence.

The Italian coach last week rejected suggestions that City's current difficulties were connected to the club being found guilty of extensive financial breaches in a long-running legal battle with the Premier League.

Instead, he views the current issues as temporary growing pains after taking over from Guardiola.

"For me, it’s part of the process because this is like a reset," he said Monday. "It’s like a restart after 10 years with the same manager, same face, same words, same method.

"It’s not easy to suddenly, all of a sudden, change. For me personally, it’s all normal and hopefully, as soon as possible, we can find the right balance in all the lines, defense and midfield, and we can be consistent. That, for me, is the difference in this moment between us and other clubs. "

City break from domestic competition on Tuesday when they host Napoli in the Champions League.

The match brings Kevin De Bruyne back to the Etihad Stadium, with the Belgian — considered by many to be City’s greatest player — having left in the summer of 2025 after 10 years at the club.

Maresca, who coached De Bruyne while serving as assistant during City’s treble-winning campaign in 2022-23, called him "no doubt" one of the finest players he has worked with.

"Not only personally, I think also for the history of this club," Maresca said. "In the last 10 years, all the trophies the club achieved, Kevin was here and an important player, a fantastic player, a top guy, polite, fantastic. For me it was a pleasure to work to work with him."

De Bruyne previously returned to City last season in the Champions League league stage, though he was substituted after 26 minutes during Napoli's 2-0 defeat after teammate Giovanni Di Lorenzo was sent off.

Ahead of the game, Maresca confirmed that right-back Matheus Nunes is back in contention following an injury.

City sit seventh in the 36-team Champions League standings, having won three of their four league phase fixtures so far.
