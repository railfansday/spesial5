Grand Slam of Darts 2026: Littler alongside Greaves in mouth-watering Group A

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Luke Littler has been drawn in Group A alongside Beau Greaves and Niels Zonneveld at the Grand Slam of Darts 2026; you can watch the Grand Slam of Darts in Wolverhampton live on Sky Sports from November 14-22

The draw for the Grand Slam of Darts 2026 has taken place with Luke Littler grouped alongside Beau Greaves and Niels Zonneveld to make a mouth-watering Group A.

Littler and Greaves have faced each other twice before, with each young star picking up a victory and they will once again face-off in the evening session on Saturday November 14.

The world No 1 and two-time defending champion in Wolverhampton beat Greaves 6-5 in a deciding-leg at Players Championship 24 back in August.

Greaves beat Littler in a 6-5 thriller to clinch a spot in the World Youth Championship final in 2025.

Zonneveld and Littler played each other earlier this year, with the former securing a 6-5 win in the quarter-finals of the European Tour in March. Meanwhile, Greaves beat Zonneveld 6-3 in their meeting in Players Championship 31.

Luke Humphries, the 2023 winner, will take on Joe Cullen and Paolo Nebrida, while Group K catches the eye as James Wade faces Ricky Evans and Cam Crabtree.

Lisa Ashton is in Group B alongside Jermaine Wattimena and Alan Soutar, with Josh Rock in Group C with Kevin Doets and Tommy Morris, who surprisingly won Players Championship 34 recently.

Stephen Bunting, Cameron Menzies, Gerwyn Price, Jonny Clayton, Michael van Gerwen, Chris Dobey, Rob Cross, Gian van Veen, Ross Smith and Nathan Aspinall are also involved in the tournament.
