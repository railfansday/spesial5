Hailey Bieber Recalls Past Suicidal Ideation Amid Social Media Criticism

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Hailey Bieber is opening up about experiencing suicidal ideation after facing backlash on social media.

“I had people telling me to kill myself in the comments every single day,” Bieber, 29, recalled during The New York Times’ DealBook Summit on Wednesday, November 18. “I was going through a time where people were, like, yelling at me in the street. People were putting tip jars up in Starbucks of me versus this person.”

Hailey faced an onslaught of online criticism when she started dating husband Justin Bieber in 2018 following his breakup from on-off girlfriend Selena Gomez. (Gomez, 34, Justin, 32, and Hailey have since all shut down any bad blood or relationship overlap.)

“I had never experienced anything like that before,” the Rhode founder noted on Wednesday. “The way that people were making me feel was that maybe it would be better if I wasn’t here anymore because that’s how intense I felt other people’s feelings were towards me at the time.”

She continued, “I think you don’t get all of the good [of social media] without the bad and vice versa. I don’t really think there is a balance. I think there’s a resilience that you build with so much time of enduring and going through such intense scrutiny and being so perceived all the time.”

According to Hailey, she is expressly aware of any backlash.

“I’m just somebody who pays a lot of attention, like, I’m not that girl that can be like, ‘Yeah, I’m just going to ignore it,’” she acknowledged. “I don’t work that way, like, I’m too sensitive and I care too much to be that person that tries to pretend like it doesn’t bother me.”

Even so, Hailey has worked to try not to allow the online noise to affect her daily life.

“I try to just do, I guess, internal regulation and just be like, ‘OK, that’s enough for today. We’re going to put it away, we’re going to put it down. We’re going to be present and spend time,’” the model explained. “I mean, it is tough, right, to try to limit yourself. It’s just so available and it’s so there.”

Gomez, for her part, married Benny Blanco in 2025 and has made it clear that she holds no grudge against Justin and Hailey.

“Hailey Bieber reached out to me and let me know that she has been receiving death threats and such hateful negativity,” the Only Murders in the Building actress wrote via an Instagram statement in March 2023. “This isn’t what I stand for. No one should have to experience hate or bullying. I’ve always advocated for kindness and really want this all to stop ❤️.”
