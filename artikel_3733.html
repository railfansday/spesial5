Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce Leave Chiefs Game in Cream Vintage Cadillac

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Travis Kelce celebrated the Kansas City Chiefs defeating the New York Jets by whisking wife Taylor Swift away in a set of vintage wheels.

In photos published by Page Six just after the Chiefs won 35-12 at Arrowhead Stadium on Sunday, November 8, the tight end, 37, and “Patient Zero” hitmaker, 36, were seen in a cream-colored vintage Cadillac with contrast-hued brown rear doors and chrome details.

Leaving the stadium together, Kelce was seen grinning behind the wheel before the couple both waved for the cameras.

Hours prior, Swift was snapped watching the game alongside longtime BFF Karlie Kloss. The pair’s public outing comes as a rarity, with Kloss, 34, maintaining mostly a low-key relationship with Swift since their intense public-fronting friendship wound down in 2016. (Kloss did, however, attend Swift and Kelce’s July Madison Square Garden wedding.)

Per various social media photos from Sunday’s game, the friends were seen seated beside one another in Kelce’s Arrowhead Stadium suite, where Swift has become a fixture this season, attending all three Kansas City home games so far.

In the pics, the duo were seen happily chatting together while watching the action on the field, with Swift donning a red shirt to support her team’s colors. The famous pals were also joined in the suite by Travis’ mom, Donna Kelce, as well as Swift’s parents.

Sunday’s Chiefs win comes after Travis recently opened up about approaching the holiday season as Swift’s husband. “I’m excited for my first holiday as a married man,” he said during an interview with Women’s Wear Daily on Thursday, November 5. “I love all the old traditions and can’t wait to start some new ones. I start celebrating as that tree goes up.”

He also detailed his “perfect holiday” during the interview, revealing that he loves Donna, 74, baking blueberry muffins. Travis also hinted at his favorite holiday attire, wondering out loud if the team had any “Tommy Christmas onesies somewhere?” (The athlete is a global brand ambassador for Tommy Hilfiger.)

Travis and Swift were married on July 3 in a star-studded reception at the famed sporting and entertainment venue. While Adam Sandler, who invited Travis to appear in 2025’s Happy Gilmore 2, served as the wedding’s officiant, other guests included Tom Cruise, Beyoncé, Brad Pitt, Tom Hanks and Hugh Grant amid all of Swift’s renowned besties spanning Selena Gomez, Gigi Hadid, Ice Spice, Cara Delevingne, Ed Sheeran and more.

A source exclusively told Us at the time that there were “about 1,000” guests in total. “The whole night felt really intimate and personal despite being in an arena and how star-studded the guest list was,” the insider explained.
