Atlético: Diego Simeone flies to the aid of Julián Alvarez

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

While Julián Alvarez had a particularly difficult evening against FC Barcelona, ​​Diego Simeone wanted to publicly defend his attacker at a press conference. Despite two clear missed opportunities and the whistles coming down from the stands of the Metropolitano, the Argentinian received the support of his coach after the defeat of Atlético (1-2). The Madrid technician believes that his team delivered a convincing performance, but paid for its lack of efficiency against a much more clinical Barça.

Far from wanting to overwhelm his number 19, Simeone even insisted on his importance in the rojiblanco project. “He played a crucial match, involved in three actions and providing an assist to Giuliano. In the second half, he had this opportunity to lob the goalkeeper, then this cross for Baena. What more could you ask for? »he defended. Before lending his support: “he’s an extraordinary player, we love him, we need him, we need the best Julián, and we’re going to work with him. » A clear message from the technician, determined to maintain his confidence in the Argentine striker despite this missed evening.
