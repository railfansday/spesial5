The 15 highest-paid players in world football: Ronaldo, Messi, Kane…

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Harry Kane has signed a new deal at Bayern Munich – but where does he rank in the best-paid players in world football?

Three players entered our top 15 in the final weeks of the last summer window; Rodri, Ollie Watkins and Enzo Fernandez.

Barcelona are paying Rodri £444,782 per week, while Watkins’ move from Aston Villa to Al-Hilal comes with a £410,618 weekly wage. Fernandez’ move to Manchester City earned him pay parity with Virgil van Dijk at Liverpool.

Lionel Messi appears at 13th on £360,876 at Inter Miami. In the Premier League, Manchester City’s Erling Haaland makes more than anybody else, as he earns a base wage of £525,000 per week.

Kane is now up to seventh, as he’s earning around £460,000 per week with Bayern Munich.

When it comes to Real Madrid, only Mbappe (£512,922) and Vinicius Junior earn enough to gain entry here.

In Ligue 1, Ousmane Dembele is the highest earner on £298,000 per week and in Serie A, it’s Lautaro Martinez, who’s taking home a weekly wage of £273,000 at Inter Milan.

Other than in Europe, the remaining highest-paid superstars are all playing in Saudi Arabia.

Cristiano Ronaldo still ranks as the highest-paid player in the Saudi Pro League and world football, earning a whopping £3.4million per week with Al-Nassr.

Other players earning more than £400,000 per week in Saudi Arabia include Sadio Mane, Kalidou Koulibaly, Sergej Milinkovic-Savic, Ivan Toney, Ruben Nuves and Watkins.
