AC Milan, Portugal: Gonçalo Ramos is at rock bottom!

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Nothing is going well for Gonçalo Ramos. Recruited for 74 million euros by AC Milan last summer, the Portuguese striker was expected to be one of the Rossoneri’s great offensive weapons. At 25, the man who has a total of 33 caps with the Seleção (14 goals) arrived with solid experience at the very highest level, after his stints at Benfica and Paris Saint-Germain. However, a few months after his arrival in Lombardy, the observation is particularly worrying. The former Parisian is going through a very complicated period and is struggling to meet the expectations placed on him. All competitions combined, he has now gone 661 minutes without scoring a single goal. His last achievement dates back to October 18, against Atalanta, from the penalty spot. A shortage which is seriously starting to weigh on an attacker recruited at such a price.

And its difficulties are no longer limited to its low yield in Lombardy. The international break should have allowed him to regain confidence with Portugal, but the Seleção’s last two meetings did not help his situation. Already particularly discreet during the heavy defeat against Denmark (1-4), where he did not touch a single ball in the opposing area, Ramos was not more convincing against Wales (3-2). Replaced after just 59 minutes, he only touched 11 balls, completed four passes out of seven attempts and attempted only one shot, with a header. A particularly meager record for the man who is called upon to take on more responsibilities at the forefront of the Portuguese attack. Especially since his three goals in three starts during previous international meetings had given hope for a real awakening.

But this bad patch also begins to cause tensions with his teammates. In recent days, La Gazzetta dello Sport affirmed, in this respect, that Bruno Fernandes would have lost patience with his compatriot during the victory against Wales. On a counter-attack, the Manchester United midfielder overflowed down the left side before sending a low cross into the area. Except that Ramos, who should have accompanied the action to try to regain the ball, had interrupted his run too early. An attitude which would have greatly annoyed the Portuguese captain, who would not have hesitated to let him know. The two men would also have exchanged after the meeting, Bruno Fernandes reminding his teammate of the responsibilities which accompany the succession of Cristiano Ronaldo at the forefront of the Lusitanian attack. An episode revealing the pressure which is beginning to surround the former Parisian.

Beyond this clash, it is above all the offensive performance of Ramos which calls out. His lack of presence in the area, his difficulties in obtaining clear chances and his current inability to materialize his situations are starting to become problematic. His investment in pressing and his work without the ball remain appreciable, but they are no longer enough to mask his shortcomings in the last gesture. In Milan, Ruben Amorim expects more from his center forward, even if the team’s collective difficulties and its lack of ability to settle down sustainably in the last thirty meters do not make his task easier. For a player who represents an investment of 74 million euros, the margin of patience will not be eternal. He will have to quickly regain his goalscoring instinct to prevent the questions from turning into a real crisis of confidence.

Precisely, Gonçalo Ramos – who can boast of three goals and two assists since his arrival in Milan – will have a new opportunity to get back on track this Sunday. For the 12th day of Serie A, AC Milan, 4th in the standings, will welcome Frosinone to San Siro and their number 9 will inevitably want to take advantage of this meeting to end their long drought. Faced with an opponent against whom the Rossoneri will hope to impose their game, the Portuguese striker will have to find presence in the area and above all that efficiency which he has sorely lacked for several weeks. The pressure will be significant, especially since the latest images from the Portuguese selection have not helped his situation. In Milan as in Portugal, Gonçalo Ramos no longer really has the right to hide and a revival is logically expected.
