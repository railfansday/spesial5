Turning biomass into renewable fuels and chemicals using microorganisms

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Texas A&M University researchers have developed a process that converts biomass into renewable fuels and chemicals. Dr. Mark Holtzapple, professor in the Artie McFerrin Department of Chemical Engineering, aims to make producing these products more efficient and affordable by using microorganisms to break down plant material.
