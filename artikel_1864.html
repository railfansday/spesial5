Liverpool XI vs Manchester United: Predicted lineup, confirmed team news, injury latest today

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Liverpool captain Virgil can Dijk has been passed fit for the visit of rivals Manchester United in the Premier League on Sunday.

Reds boss Andoni Iraola had plenty of good news to deliver at his pre-match press conference on Friday, as he confirmed that Dutch trio Van Dijk, Ryan Gravenberch and Jeremie Frimpong - all previously considered doubts - would be fit to feature at Anfield.

All three missed out on international duty with the Netherlands, with Van Dijk pulling out of the squad after concerns over a ”little niggle”.

Dominik Szoboszlai has also struggled with fitness in recent weeks, but came through two games for Hungary unscathed. Alexander Isak was rested for Sweden’s second game of the international window, leaving Liverpool’s top scorer in top condition for the weekend.

Alisson will start in goal, while Van Dijk should continue his strong centre-back partnership with Jeremy Jacquet. Milos Kerkez is first-choice at left-back, but injuries at right-back have caused Iraola headaches all season.

Ronald Araujo has deputised on the right for the majority of the season, and he will likely start there again on Sunday.

A fully fit Szoboszlai is Liverpool’s best midfielder, so the Hungarian is slated to start alongside Alexis Mac Allister - now just one yellow card away from a suspension - in the engine room against United.

Florian Wirtz saw his best run of form since signing for the club last summer interrupted slightly by injury, but the German should soon get back into a rhythm with another start at No10.

Bradley Barcola and Cody Gakpo should start out wide, with Isak leading the line. Conor Bradley and Giovanni Leoni are both now back in training but won’t return here, while fellow long-term absentee Hugo Ekitike is expected to play again in January.

Predicted Liverpool XI vs Man Utd (4-2-3-1): Alisson; Araujo, Van Dijk, Jacquet, Kerkez; Szoboszlai, Mac Allister; Barcola, Wirtz, Gakpo; Isak
