England player ratings vs Croatia as Harry Kane scores on record-breaking appearance

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Harry Kane celebrated his record-breaking 126th England cap with the winning goal as the Three Lions won 2-1 against Croatia in the Nations League.

After a slow start from the hosts, Thomas Tuchel’s side opened the scoring when Morgan Rogers bundled Jude Bellingham’s cross home from close range.

A mistake from Jarell Quansah allowed Petar Sucic to equalise from a corner after the break.

But the Bayer Leverkusen defender made amends just moments later, winning a penalty from a corner, which Kane stepped up to convert and secure England their place in the Nations League quarter-final.

Metro was at Wembley to rate England’s performances on a historic night for their captain.
