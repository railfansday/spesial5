GdS: ‘We have Pavlovic and Diawara’ – how Milan’s €3m man could’ve changed January plans

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Today, a report has suggested that Sankhoun Diawara has taken the keys ahead of the January mercato, changing AC Milan’s plans.

When he joined in the summer, most would’ve questioned how much Diawara could provide to Milan. However, he’s become one of the most important players in the squad in recent games.

Filling in wherever needed in the backline (and even further forward), Diawara has proven himself ready and it could’ve shaken up the January plans.

As Gazzetta dello Sport writes, the Diawara signing may now actually change the club’s recruitment strategy in some ways and Ruben Amorim’s comments suggest the same.

When asked about Goncalo Inacio, the Portuguese named Sankhoun Diawara as a ready option – alongside Strahinja Pavlovic – and the suggestions about a new defensive priority are telling.

The Frenchman has proved that ‘second-string players can even contemplate’ becoming starters. It’s not been without issues for other players, but he’s focused on his job and delivered when called upon.

In short, the Rossoneri have another ready defender and Diawara has proven his ability to play virtually anywhere that Amorim needs him to. Maybe Inacio may not be needed in January after all; maybe that money will be freed up for the attack.
