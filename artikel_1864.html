First meeting against a team 20 unbeaten: All the key stats ahead of Milan vs. Ferencvaros

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The first ever competitive meeting between AC Milan and Ferencvaros will take place at San Siro this evening in the Europa League.

Milan have played four times in history against Hungarian opponents. The first time was in the 1967-68 Cup Winners’ Cup, when the Rossoneri draw 2-2 away and 1-1 at home against ETO, which saw them progress on away goals.

The other two occasions came 15 years later in the 1982 Mitropa Cup group stage against Szombathelyi Haladas. The Diavolo won both encounters: 2-0 at San Siro, and 1-0 away from home.

So, tonight’s contest is an unprecedented one, and it pits two teams together that have made very different starts to their Europa League campaigns.

➤ Alphadjo Cisse has caught the eye for Milan in Europa League play. The striker is the side’s top scorer with three goals, which is joint most in the competition, one of those having been the critical first of the match.

➤ Kristoffer Zachariassen has been the most prolific player for Ferencváros, scoring on three occasions in the current Europa League campaign, including one match-opening goal. He is tied as the competition’s top scorer in 2026-27.

➤ The Rossoneri secured an impressive 3-1 victory over city rivals Inter at the weekend, strengthening their position in the Serie A top-four race. Milan have lost just once in their opening ten league matches, recording six victories and three draws, although their continental performances have been considerably less convincing.

➤ The Diavolo suffered a comprehensive 3-0 defeat away to Bournemouth on matchday three, leaving them with two losses from their opening three Europa League fixtures and only one victory in their last six European matches overall.

➤ Milan’s struggles have extended to home soil, where they lost 2-0 against Benfica in their only previous Europa League fixture at the Giuseppe Meazza this season. Another defeat would see them lose consecutive European home matches within a single campaign for the first time since 2010/11.

➤ Nevertheless, their recent attacking form offers some encouragement: five of their last six competitive matches have produced at least three goals, and Amorim’s side has scored before the 25th minute in each of their previous four home fixtures.

➤ Ferencváros, by contrast, have enjoyed an outstanding start to their European campaign, winning all three league-phase matches and scoring freely along the way. Their most recent outing produced a convincing 4-1 victory over Torreense, with Bamidele Yusuf, Mariano Gómez and Krisztián Lisztes among the scorers. Balázs Borbély’s side have now won five of their last six competitive matches, drawing the other, and have scored 18 goals while conceding just four during that sequence.

➤ The Hungarian champions are unbeaten in 20 competitive matches since late July, recording 13 victories and seven draws, while each of their last four wins has come by at least two goals. They also boast an impressive away record, having avoided defeat in their last ten competitive matches on the road and conceded no more than once in any of those fixtures.

➤ Victory in Milan would see Ferencváros win four consecutive matches in major European competition for the first time since the 1967-68 Inter-Cities Fairs Cup, when they recorded six successive victories on their way to the final.

➤ This will be the first competitive meeting between Milan and Ferencváros, although both clubs have limited historical experience against opposition from the other’s country. Milan’s only previous European encounters with Hungarian opponents came against Győri ETO in the 1967/68 Cup Winners’ Cup, with both matches ending level.

➤ Ferencváros, meanwhile, have failed to win any of their last four European away fixtures against Italian teams, drawing twice and losing twice, since defeating Roma in the 1965 Inter-Cities Fairs Cup. With Milan seeking to revive their European campaign and the visitors determined to preserve their perfect record, both sides have considerable motivation heading into this encounter.
