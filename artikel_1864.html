Pundit rages as Man City predicted to only end up with '30 charges'

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Manchester City's players and members of the club are being heavily affected by the ongoing legal battle against the Premier League.

That is according to experienced former Tottenham, Everton and Aston Villa scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

Enzo Maresca's side have only won one of their last seven matches across all competitions, most recently drawing 2-2 with Napoli on Tuesday, 24 November.

The Citizens are now six points behind Premier League leaders Arsenal, while also dropping down to sixth in the Champions League table.

Owners, fans, players, and members of the club alike are all waiting patiently to learn Man City's fate after being found guilty of 114 finance-related breaches.

And there is a suggestion that the nervous wait is beginning to show on the pitch, as frustrations grow already over Maresca's future.

Speaking to Football Insider, Mick Brown has already rejected the idea that players know the result of the Citizens' legal battle.

However, concerns continue to emerge at the Etihad Stadium, and King is desperate for the situation to conclude as soon as possible.

"I think [it’s affecting] not only the Man City playing staff, but also the entire football club," King told Football Insider when discussing City's charges.

"This non-decision has been hanging around now for what, four months? When were they found guilty? A month into the season, wasn't it? And there's not going to be a decision on this until, they say, February next year.

"So if they're banged to rights, why isn't it out now? Which leads me to believe that 114 charges will probably end up being about 30 charges, don't you?

"Manchester City have 80 lawyers on this. Either there's a rule, or there isn’t. It’s been two years, hasn't it? Well, can you imagine the legal costs?

"What's going to happen here? It's no good saying they're guilty of 100. Either they are guilty, or they are not guilty. And if they are guilty, make the decisions, make the penalty, make the charge.

"And then Manchester City have either got to say, is it a point deduction or is it a large payment, or is it relegation? Now, those are the options here."

Man City's results have taken a sharp downward trajectory, so it is only normal that the manager's tactics are being heavily questioned.

Wyness told Football Insider that Maresca's remains safe for now, but that could change ahead of a very tricky run of fixtures.

Arsenal are up next, followed by Leeds United, Brentford, Barcelona and Chelsea, and these games could seal his fate at the Etihad.

Filling Pep Guardiola's shoes was always going to be an impossible task, but things are taking a turn for the worse this season.
