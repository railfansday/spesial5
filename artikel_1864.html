Littlehampton Town hope Kingstonian draw is small step in right direction

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Golds were disappointed not to take the win after goals by Dan Simmonds and Marcel Powell gave them a 2-0 interval lead.

But every point is valuable at the moment and bosses Mitch Hand and George Gaskin will aim to build on the point in three games in eight days.

Town went into the Kingstonian game having lost their last six in all competitions – and shipped six goals in each of their past two.

Golds’ defence has become alarmingly leaky this month, with 20 conceded in five games before the Ks’ visit.

In attack Golds had netted just twice in five before Tuesday.

After narrowly losing 2-1 at rivals Bognor a couple of weeks ago, the Golds have failed to build on the promise of that display.

They are now bottom of the Isthmian South Central division on eight points, seven points off the safety line.

Town visit Hayes & Yeading United on Saturday, AFC Portchester on Tuesday then host Bedfont on December 5.
