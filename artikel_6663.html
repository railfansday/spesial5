22 Best Airbnbs in Upstate New York for a Weekend Away from the City 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

If you live in New York, pretty much anything qualifies as an excuse for a weekend Upstate. A clanging radiator blasting dry heat into your apartment when it’s still warm outside? Time to flee to greener pastures. An overwhelming work week spent mostly in the company of your inbox? You deserve to unplug. The very first hint of a reddening leaf in the park? A seasonal sign from above—get yourself to an apple orchard.

With most small towns within a few hours’ drive of the city (and several available on a train line, for those without a car), you don’t really need a reason to spend a few days in Upstate New York. And while there are plenty of boutique hotels dotting the Hudson Valley and the Catskills, for true relaxation, why not book an Airbnb?

This time of year is all about the outdoors. Spend your days hiking along mountain trails and creeks, exploring local markets to sample all the fresh produce (and the baked goods they inspire), or hitting a brewery in the evening for a glass of cider under twinkling lights. As the weather cools, the charm offensive continues: fire pits come out, farms open for pumpkin and apple harvesting, and following the foliage becomes a perfectly acceptable way to spend a lazy fall day. Some of our favorite Upstate New York Airbnbs are located in or within walking distance of quaint towns like Hudson and Woodstock; though if you prefer a truly off-the-grid experience, we found plenty of cabins and cottages surrounded only by meadows.

Ahead, the 22 best Airbnbs in Upstate New York.

Enjoy a slower pace at this newly renovated 1890s-era farmhouse in the Western Catskills. The home is set on 25 acres of rolling meadows and forest, which lead to two streams and a waterfall. While the home has been updated to feature all new appliances (including a Vitamix), details like reclaimed wood and a bread-baking wood-stove cement the snug vibes. The main area is one big room: an eight-person dining room table connects to the kitchen, where you can spend afternoons making the most of local ingredients you picked up from a nearby farmstand. And when you head upstairs, don’t miss the claw-foot tub—it’s reason enough to book this gorgeous stay.

Rooms: 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms; sleeps 5

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, window AC unit, heating, indoor fireplace, backyard, outdoor dining area.

This spot is billed as a “boutique nature retreat”—but let’s call it what it really is: an adult treehouse. It sleeps two, with a king-sized bed and hot outdoor showers. With no cell service or electricity, this is your chance to go fully off the grid; since the whole house is surrounded by forest, you’ll feel completely cozy and secluded. When you want to re-join society, the town of Phoenicia (and, more importantly, the local favorite Phoenicia Diner) is just a 10-minute drive away.

Amenities: Outdoor shower, fire pit, free parking

Best For: Fulfilling childhood treehouse dreams.

“Upstate” in perhaps only the most abstract sense of the word (it borders New Jersey, just about one hour out of the city), this lakeside spot is filled with sunlight, a far cry from the heavy wooden cabins that permeate the area. Outside, there’s a private garden with a waterfall and lily pond—and, of course, those unbeatable views of Greenwood Lake. There are plenty of wineries and antique stores nearby, as well as hikes for the adventurous weekender (the home is a five-minute walk from a path that connects to the Appalachian Trail) and a massive claw-foot tub to soak in when it’s time to unwind.

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 1.5 bathrooms; sleeps 4

Amenities: HDTV, bathtub, record player, crib, board games, wood-burning fireplace, patio, outdoor furniture

Best For: Those who crave nature, but are afraid of the whole cabin-in-the-woods idea.

Renovated for modernity but restored with heaps of vintage touches befitting the older home’s roots, this light-filled apartment right in the heart of Hudson is great if you prefer to stay in a town rather than remote wilderness. From the zellige tiles to the antique stove, the kitchen is a particular star—anyone looking for style inspiration for an upcoming remodel will find several nooks to bookmark here. The Nespresso and tea station helps you start each morning off on a caffeinated foot, while in the afternoons, you’ll want to hang out on the porch or in the bubblegum pink claw-foot tub with a book.

Amenities: TV, sound system, crib, patio.

This new listing is the epitome of stripped-back country living: There’s no television and few amenities, so if you’re looking for a spot to indulge your off-the-grid dreams, this is it. (Though, lest you change your mind, the hamlet of Livingston Manor, with its concept stores and coffee shops, is walking distance away.) The house is a freshly renovated farmhouse, done in the Scandinavian style—from the antique furnishings to the original wood floors, every corner is steeped in character. There’s a bunk room for the youngest members of your travel party, plus a spacious kitchen for whipping up hearty breakfasts at home before heading out for the day.

Amenities: Air conditioning, pet-friendly.

Best For: Anyone looking to truly disconnect for a weekend.

Picture the prototypical lake cottage. Wood, everywhere, is a given; as is an A-frame silhouette, a fire pit and deck for grilling, and perhaps a wood-burning fireplace or two. Welcome to the Airbnb embodiment of that vision, complete with all of the above plus a few modern amenities (the Nespresso machine is a welcome addition). Sleepy Hollow Lake is a quick 10-minute walk down a trail away, for summertime guests. In the colder months, Hunter mountain and its ski slopes are a 45-minute drive away; and in between, you’ll have a third of an acre worth of land to explore on the property itself.

Amenities: Bathtub, record player, high chair, travel crib, board games, fireplace, beach/lake access, fire pit, pet friendly.

Distance from NYC: 2 hours by train, 2.5 hours by car.

No car, no problem: This charming one-bedroom is right in the town of Cold Spring, easily reachable from the city via the Metro North. You’ll be able to walk everywhere (Cold Spring Coffeehouse, a must-visit for your morning cappuccino and cardamom bun, is just 15 minutes on foot) and spend your weekend like a local. The home itself is clean and minimally decorated, with just enough original touches—including a gorgeous fireplace—to keep it from feeling too sparse. Remote workers will appreciate the spacious dining table, too, which doubles as a workspace you can spread out in in a picturesque alcove.

Distance from NYC: 1.5 hours by car, 1.5 hours by train.

Inspired by a Swiss Alpine home, this modern space is compact and minimalist, with oversized windows that really let the mountainside views seep in. Take it in up close and personal on the (covered) patio, which, in warmer months, is a great spot for a breakfast or dinner. Indoors, a colorful kitchen, shower skylight, and pellet stove all make for memorable decorative touches. It’s the right size for a solo traveler or a couple looking for a short-term stay; you’ll have all the necessities you need, but you’re encouraged to make the most of your surroundings.

Amenities: Outdoor shower, pellet stove, patio, fire pit, EV charger, pet-friendly.

Distance from NYC: Around 3 hours by train.

Best For: The no-frills traveler who still appreciates a little aesthetic effort.

Built in the ’80s by a master stonemason, this is one of the more unique options available to Catskills travelers. The home is actually a tower, with vaulted ceilings and wraparound windows offering sweeping views of the valley below. (The bathtub on the second floor is an especially great spot to take them in.) Just a quick seven-minute drive to the town of Woodstock, you’ll be left alone but not fully remote. And if it’s still warm enough, be sure to factor in enough free time for some evenings spent outdoors—the views are incredible, and between the hot tub and the deck, there are plenty of spots to take them in.

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms; sleeps 4

Amenities: Washer/dryer, bathtub, piano, record player, board games, air conditioning, fireplace, fire pit, hot tub.

Best For: Someone who read one too many fairytale books growing up.

This charming A-frame in Tannersville is full of lush plants, good energy, and nostalgic charm. The home dates back to 1971 and is right in the heart of the Catskills, just two hours north of New York City. While the interiors are supremely serene, the outdoor deck with its BBQ and twinkly lights is where you’ll probably spend most of your time—especially if you’re visiting in fall or spring, when the weather is its mildest. Prefer to stay in? The home comes stocked with a record player and board games, for cozy nights with the whole group that are probably why you booked an Airbnb Upstate in the first place.

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, AC, heating, backyard, BBQ.

Best For: Plant parents who need a getaway.

If the idea of staying in a cabin in the woods with a cedar wood-fired hot tub sounds up your alley, book a stay at this bright home just 10 minutes from Woodstock. It’s not quite your old-school cottage: Aside from the light palette and modern decor, the bedrooms feature Casper mattresses, and the Breville espresso machine will make morning coffees on the deck all the more enjoyable. Outside, a fire pit and cedar wood hot tub and sauna make the case for ditching the dinner reservation in favor of staying in; plus, the house comes with a movie projector, for DIY movie nights.

Amenities: Washer/dryer, AC, central heating, backyard, fire pit, hammock, BBQ, hot tub.

Best For: A soak in the hot tub, beneath the trees.

From the wood-paneled A-frame ceilings to the forest green paint theme to the coffee-colored zellige tiling in the bathroom, there are plenty of decor ideas to steal from this Airbnb. Design aside, it’s well-located (hop in the car for a quick drive to the Ashokan Reservoir and little towns like Kingston and Woodstock) and surprisingly spacious. Come summer, we predict you’ll want to spend all your time on the sprawling front deck overlooking the trees—ditto the fall, as long as you pack the appropriate amount of wool sweaters to stay warm.

Amenities: Washer/dryer, sound system, indoor fireplace, fire pit, outdoor furniture, patio, pet-friendly.

We can already feel our blood pressure lowering simply admiring this tranquil Airbnb located on the outskirts of Kingston. The property offers numerous nooks for experiencing the natural surroundings—a fire pit encircled by Adirondack chairs, a tranquil pond best savored from the home’s swinging hammock, and even a screened porch just off the kitchen for early morning coffee. Inside is equally as dreamy, with plenty of natural light and cozy touches like exposed brick and a small fireplace. One perk: You can bring your pups for a small fee, so no member of the family is left behind for the next weekend Upstate.

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms; sleeps 4

Amenities: Washer/dryer, central AC, indoor fireplace, backyard, fire pit, hammock, BBQ.

Hide yourself away in this atmospheric cabin perched atop Bramley Mountain near Bovina Center, a great spot for cozy restaurants and country stores. The home has a spacious atmosphere thanks to the cathedral ceilings and open floor plan, but you never lose that innately cozy feel: a wood-burning stove, wood paneling, and an atmospheric porch are all there to remind you that you’re very much in the woods. Not that there’s a bad time to book a stay here, but if you have your pick, aim for the fall. The house is surrounded by trees that turn surreal shades of scarlet and persimmon come October.

Amenities: Bathtub, outdoor shower, washer/dryer, indoor fireplace, backyard.

Best For: Turning your phone on airplane mode.

There are two types of upstate getaways: the first, characterized by spending the entire weekend in one place, relaxing and recharging. The second is spent diving headfirst into the region’s many charms, exploring shops, restaurants, and markets in several small towns. This Airbnb is ideal for the latter sort of traveler, who wants friction-less access to destinations such as Woodstock, Saugerties, and Kingston.

It also happens to be impeccably designed, with highlights like an indoor fireplace, mid-century modern decor, and a backyard BBQ and fire pit you’ll have a hard time parting ways with—it even comes stocked with firewood.

Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, record player, indoor fireplace, fire pit, hammock, BBQ.

Best For: Exploring several nearby towns (and an impromptu game of chess to end the night).

Leaning into Wabi-Sabi design, this ranch-style home in the Warwick Valley is close to several lakes and hiking trails (as well as trendy breweries and restaurants). Natural materials and soft lighting set a relaxed tone for the space, and a trip to The Shed (the home’s meditation room) for a yoga session will begin each day on the right foot, as will the complimentary freshly-roasted bag of ground coffee from the owner’s favorite purveyor, Voyager Espresso. When you’re living in the foreground of the dense green forest, visible from most windows in the home, it’s impossible to not feel zen.

Amenities: Washer/dryer, yoga studio, central AC and heating, lake access, fire pit, backyard, BBQ, EV charger.

Best For: Coming back from your vacation actually recharged.

This secluded sanctuary in the Catskills forest sits on a hill overlooking an abundance of beauty, from woodlands and stream-fed ponds to a sprawling wildflower meadow. The cabin is petite (just 400 square feet) but a Wabi Sabi-inspired design lends a calming effect. A few standout details—and there are several—include the open floor plan with a winding spiral staircase in the center, natural earthy textures throughout, and a peaceful outdoor area complete with a fire pit, picnic tables, and twinkly lights. Even the bedroom is impeccably designed, with its antique chair nightstand and matching bedding; twin beds have never looked chicer.

Amenities: Toiletries, washer/dryer, record player, books and board games, window AC unit, indoor pellet stove, kitchen, fire pit, charcoal BBQ.

Best For: Reading your book beneath a canopy of trees.

Think of the typical log house, then flip that idea on its head. That’s what you get with Fox Ridge Chalet, which is anything but ordinary: A lot of it is white-washed, making it feel even bigger and brighter than it is, and the assortment of furniture is so eclectic, it has the air of a cool vintage shop. Situated on seven private acres of land, the picturesque property beckons to be explored. For the days when you decide you want to stay in, the movie screening projector, pool table, hot tub, exercise bike, and game collection should more than suffice.

Amenities: Hot tub, pool table, exercise equipment, fire pit, outdoor furniture, grill

Best For: Multigenerational summer getaways with the family.

Part of the National Register of Historic Places, this residence dubbed “The Gatehouse” was last restored in 2016—updated with more modern decor and comfy furnishings. Immerse yourself in scenic views of the Catskills Mountains and Hudson River from this historic French home while relaxing in the backyard, where a separate carriage house offers covered outdoor dining for the colder months. (Or the front yard—that storybook-perfect porch demands a few mornings or afternoons spend lounging on it.) Stay occupied with limitless activities such as hiking, biking, boating, and antique shopping, all of which can be found in the historic town of Hyde Park.

Amenities: Private balcony/patio, indoor fireplace, outdoor dining area, fire pit, BBQ grill

Best For: Staying in a home on the National Register of Historic Places.

The Pond House is a property with some serious art history cachet: It’s the former studio of social realist painter Reginald Marsh. An architect lovingly restored it into a single-family home that exudes country elegance: there are exposed beam ceilings, chandeliers, and floor-to-ceiling windows that look out onto the estate’s pond. Its most unique selling point is the main bedroom: With floor-to-ceiling glass windows slotted into wood paneling, you get a front row seat to the great outdoors from the comfort of your bed. It’ll be like sleeping in the world’s most relaxing fishbowl. (The adjacent bathroom, with its spa-like tub and equally oversized windows, is a nice companion.)

Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 1.5 bathrooms; sleeps 5

Amenities: private balcony, bathtub, indoor fireplace, fully equipped kitchen

If you’re traveling with a large group (or looking for a spot to stay around a wedding), this home can fit over 16 guests. It’s the perfect luxurious weekend getaway—perched upon a sprawling 16 acres, this converted-barn home has a design-forward interior complete with 30-foot ceilings as well as five-and-a-half bedrooms. The service is also top-notch, as the property comes with a 24-hour digital concierge as well as a private chef upon request. You’ll only be a few minutes from Woodstock village, if you want to venture out for breakfast before heading out to traverse the area’s many hiking trails.

Rooms: 5 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms; sleeps 16+

Amenities: private hot tub, indoor fireplace, hammock, private balcony, patio and backyard

Best For: A grand fireplace that will take your breath away.

The sister property to the earlier Hudson home in our edit (all designed by New York design firm Zio & Sons), this three-bedroom offers a little more space. It’s a charming townhouse in the Hudson Valley right by the main stretch of shops and restaurants, chock full of antiques and old-world details like salvaged library doors in the bedroom and an 1840s-era mantel. This is a spot to save if you love design—from the cast iron tub to the copper pan-lined tile kitchen to the original wallpaper in the living room, the home is brimming with inspiration at every turn.

Amenities: private balcony/patio, bathtub, outdoor dining area,

Distance From NYC: 2 hours by car and train.
