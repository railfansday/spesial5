So Many Dyson Hair Tools Are on Sale Right Now—These Are <i><em>Cosmo</em></i> Editors’ 6 Favorites

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

4 beauty editors. 6 Dyson tools. Here’s our official ranking.

We may earn commission from links on this page, but we only recommend products we love. Promise.

As we haircare lovers all know, Dyson has truly solidified itself as the crème de la crème of buzzy hair tools. From the viral Airwrap i.d. to the OG Supersonic Hair Dryer, Dyson = high-tech hairstyling. Despite the higher price points, these tools are everywhere these days, from your favorite creator doing their hair on your TikTok feed to your hairstylist doing your hair in the salon chair. It seems like everyone loves a Dyson product (and that includes pretty much every Cosmo editor too). They’ve won tonssss of Cosmo beauty awards, including a 2025 Holy Grail Beauty Award and 2026 Reader’s Choice Beauty Award for the Airwrap, back-to-back wins in the Reader’s Choice Beauty Awards in 2024 and 2025 for the Supersonic, and many, many more.

However, at $250-$500 a pop, it’s not like most people can afford to own every single Dyson tool out there. So which ones are the most worth the hundreds of dollars they cost? Luckily, we’ve here at Cosmo have tested all of ‘em—these are the ones we truly recommend spending money on.

Why we love it: Despite so many Airwrap dupes out there, us Cosmo editors keep going back to the original Dyson hair styling tool that changed the game forever. Talk about true innovation—the Airwrap created a whole new category of styling products that dry, curl, volumize, straighten, and blow out hair all in one handy tool that, oh by the way, uses hot air instead of high heat that could potentially damage your hair more.

It’s also on rare sale right now—23 percent off—for Black Friday and Cyber Monday, so if you’ve been thinking of scooping one up, this is your time to shine. Run, don’t walk!

One Cosmo editor says: “If the Airwrap has zero fans, I’m dead. For years, I didn’t know how to style my hair. The round-brush-and-dryer combo was a lot for me, I didn’t know how to curl my hair, and quite honestly, all I ever did was straighten it with a flat iron (which was perfect for the mid-2010s style at the time). But when I got my hands on my first Airwrap, that all changed. Suddenly, I could do it all, and I was imparting wayyy less heat damage onto my strands than if I had been using traditional hot tools. The i.d. is great because the it has all the attachments I need, it dries and styles my thick, wavy hair super quickly, it eliminates frizz, and keeps my style in place all day.” —CATHARINE MALZAHN, CONTRIBUTING BEAUTY WRITER

Why we love it: The one that started it all and turned hair dryers from a humble styling tool into a must-have item... Dyson’s Supersonic dryer launched 10 years ago in 2016, yet it still remains one of the best hair dryers out there. And honestly, it’s not hard to see why. Beyond the basics you’d be looking for (long cord, negative ions, multiple heat and speed settings, relatively lightweight, that kind of thing), its powerful airflow dries hair noticeably quickly while the dryer monitors the temperature 40 times a second to prevent extreme heat on your strands.

It’s pretty straightforward and easy to use, making a salon-worthy blowout at home feel a whole lot less intimidating. A decade later, the Supersonic is proof that some beauty investments really do stand the test of time.

One Cosmo editor says: “This is the first Dyson hair tool I ever used, and I still think it's probably the most worth-it tool price-wise if you wanted to splurge on one thing. I used to hate blow dryers until I tried this one. It's quick and powerful without sacrificing the health of my hair. This fully dries my hair in under two minutes (yes, two), but it doesn't leave my hair crunchy or fried either.” —BETH GILLETTE, BEAUTY EDITOR

Why we love it: Straightening without hot plates? And you can use it on wet hair? Sign me up. The Dyson Airstrait uses airflow to dry and straighten hair at the same time, meaning you can skip the blow-dryer-and-flat-iron combo entirely. Perfect if you’re trying to cut down on styling time in the morning!

Unlike traditional flat irons, the Airstrait doesn’t use heated plates. Instead, it uses airflow to smooth and dry hair, delivering a soft, naturally straight finish with movement rather than that super-flat, pin-straight look. Plus, it has a dry mode for touching up your style between washes. When our editors with varying curl patterns (from wavy and fine to 4b curls) tested it, it straightened with ease and speed.

One Cosmo editor says: “I have an interesting hair type because while it’s curly and fine, I have a lot of it. It can take a very long time for me to dry my hair, but the Airstrait cut my styling time dramatically. This tool takes my hair from damp to fully dry in under 10 minutes. However, it did take a slight learning curve at first.” —MARY HONKUS, CONTRIBUTING BEAUTY WRITER

Why we love it: Meet the Dyson hair dryer that had three Cosmo beauty editors with three different hair types completely obsessed. The Dyson Supersonic r takes everything we love about the original Supersonic and makes it lighter, smaller, and easier to maneuver, thanks to its curved, ergonomic design. In fact, it’s so lightweight that it weighs less than a pound, making it perfect for professional stylists as well as anyone who doesn’t want an arm workout just to dry hair.

Speaking of which, your arms probably won’t get super tired because of how quickly it works. Beauty director Lauren Balsamo said it dried her soaking-wet hair in just six minutes(!!!). Add in the intelligent attachments that automatically adjust heat and airflow and remember those settings for the next time you use it, the multiple attachments, and the compact size, and it’s easy to be willing to overlook the hefty price tag.

One Cosmo editor says: “The first thing I noticed when I picked up the Supersonic R was the weight, or rather, the lack thereof. It seriously feels like you're holding a bottle of hairspray—that’s how airy and easy it is to handle. Then came the assembly. The magnetic attachments snapped on effortlessly. And as a wavy-haired girl who can either lean curly or reach for a round brush for a bouncy blowout, I really appreciated the range of attachments for everything from creating curls to smoothing flyaways.” —JASMINE HYMAN, ASSISTANT BEAUTY EDITOR

Why we love it: Dyson finally made a one-step blow dryer brush, and at $250, it’s the brand’s lowest-priced hair tool (though it’s still an investment!). Like the Airwrap, the Dyson Airsmooth is another wet-to-dry styler, using powerful airflow to dry and smooth strands without extreme heat. Its teardrop shape helps you get closer to the root for more lift and volume, while the bristles are designed to minimize snagging, pulling, or breakage. It can smooth thick, wavy hair in under 15 minutes, leaving it sleek, shiny, and noticeably less frizzy.

If you’re shopping for a new blow dryer brush, this is a great pick. It’s perfect for anyone who loves a smooth, bouncy blowout but doesn’t want all the bells and whistles of an Airwrap.

One Cosmo editor says: “I was thrilled to find out Dyson had created a one-stop tool for drying and styling, complete with upgraded bristle technology. I want to preserve as many of my precious strands as possible, and what I love most about this tool (besides the low-for-Dyson price) is that its bristles aren’t your basic plastic teeth that tug at your hair. Instead, they bend with your strands as you pull the brush through, releasing them rather than fighting against the tension. I usually finish styling with a solid clump of hair left behind on my tool, but with the Airsmooth, there were truly fewer than five strands caught in the bristles.” —JH

Why we love it: An update from the iconic and original Supersonic, this even-more-high-tech dryer features a Scalp Protect mode that uses sensors to detect how close the tool is to your head and automatically adjusts the heat to help maintain a scalp temperature of approximately 131°F. Translation: You can dry your roots without blasting your scalp with so much heat that you flinch when your dryer gets too close.

It also features intelligent attachments that remember your preferred settings, plus a Pause Detect function that reduces airflow when you put the dryer down. And, of course, you still get the powerful, fast-drying performance Dyson is known for.

One Cosmo editor says: “I have the Supersonic at my parents' house (I gifted my old one to my mom a few years ago), but the Nural is at mine. This is the more high-tech version of the Supersonic, which automatically shifts air flow and heat based on how close the tool is to your scalp. If blow dryers often feel like they're burning off your scalp, this one will turn that around.” —BG

Dyson hair tools are definitely not one-size-fits-all, and what works wonders on someone’s fine, straight hair might not do the same for thick curls or coils.

Before dropping hundreds of dollars on a Dyson, ask yourself: What do I actually want this thing to do? If you’re constantly switching up your hairstyle, the Airwrap’s versatility might justify the splurge. But if your goal is simply to dry your hair quickly, you probably don't need all those attachments—a Supersonic will do the trick. Love a sleek, straight finish? Try the Airstrait. Simply consider which tools you’d actually see yourself using every day before taking the plunge.

Short answer: Heck yeah! There’s a reason Dyson hair tools have earned cult-favorite status among us beauty editors, hairstylists, and pretty much everyone on TikTok. We’ve tested practically every Dyson out there, and we’re consistently impressed. For thick, long, or doesn’t-want-to-stay-styled hair, these can be especially worth the money.

Of course, there’s no denying that they’re a serious investment. So if you’re looking for a budget alternative, there are plenty of options out there too. But in our experience, the Dyson’s quick-drying capabilities sans excessive heat damage, customizable settings, and long-lasting results make them worth the splurge—especially if you’re someone who styles their hair regularly. And while you certainly don't need to own every Dyson tool (your bank account can thank us later), finding the right one for your hair type and styling goals can be a total game changer.
