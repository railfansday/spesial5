Cherry Peach Pie with Salted Pecan Streusel

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

This pie celebrates a road trip we took to explore our family's history. I paired southern peaches and pecans with tart Door County cherries to reflect who we are: Wisconsin Yankees with strong roots in Kentucky and Tennessee. I can't think of a better way to put my heritage on a plate. Peaches and cherries are a match made in heaven, and the gingersnap crumble topping adds a lovely crunch to contrast the filling. —Molly Stephens, Oconomowoc, Wisconsin
