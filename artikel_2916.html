49ers’ Colton McKivitz carted off after gruesome leg injury on 'Sunday Night Football'

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The San Francisco 49ers lost their starting right tackle Colton McKivitz to a gruesome injury during their Week 11 "Sunday Night Football" game against the Minnesota Vikings.

McKivitz was injured after blocking on a Christian McCaffrey run in the latter half of the second quarter. The 30-year-old tackle was rolled up in a pile as McCaffrey was tackled and as McKivitz came out of the pile, his left foot was facing the wrong direction.

McKivitz's 49ers teammates were immediately upset looking at the tackle's injury. Christian McCaffrey fell to a knee looking at him while Trent Williams was shown on the broadcast looking dismayed, and he forcibly spiked his helmet into the ground.

McKivitz was quickly tended to by medical staff on the field and was carted into the locker room as a result of his injury. Swing tackle Vederian Lowe checked into the game to replace him.

49ers coach Kyle Shanahan provided an update about McKivitz's status after the game. He told reporters McKivitz suffered a fractured and dislocated left ankle and will miss the remainder of the season because of it, per ESPN's Nick Wagoner.

McKivitz entered play Sunday having played all 563 snaps for the 49ers offense, one of two San Francisco linemen to do that along with left guard Connor Colby.

McKivitz was injured while blocking on a Christian McCaffrey run. McCaffrey was tackled on the play and the veteran running back landed directly on the back of McKivitz's left leg.

McKivitz remained down on the field after the play and eventually rolled out of the pile, revealing that his left foot was facing the wrong way.

Vederian Lowe is McKivitz's backup. The five-year veteran was originally selected by the Vikings in the fifth round of the 2022 NFL Draft and has appeared in 55 career games with 25 starts, all of which came with the New England Patriots.

Lowe spent the previous three seasons with the Patriots and was a part of the team that made it to Super Bowl 60. Now, he will be tasked with starting in place of McKivitz with the 30-year-old right tackle set to miss the rest of the season.

The 49ers have four offensive tackles on their 53-man roster. Below is a look at the pecking order within the unit.

Cruz, a fifth-round rookie out of Kansas, is inactive for Sunday's game but will likely be active in future games as the team's swing tackle with McKivitz sidelined.

The 49ers also have Foster Sarell, who has appeared in 41 games (four starts) with the Los Angeles Chargers, and Isaac Alarcon of the International Player Pathway available on their practice squad to help man the position.

Additionally, San Francisco has Austen Pleasants, who appeared in 15 games for them last season with one start, on IR with a return designation. It isn't clear when he will be ready to return, but he could also be an option to replenish the team's tackle depth.
