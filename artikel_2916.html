NFL Has 1 Word for Patrick Mahomes, Brittany Mahomes After Personal Announcement

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Kansas City Chiefs star Patrick Mahomes and his wife, Brittany, have more reason to celebrate. The couple made a personal announcement via Instagram on Tuesday, and the NFL had a one-word reaction to it.

Kansas City is fresh off a 35-12 win over the New York Jets last Sunday. It snapped the team's two-game skid and got back on track. Mahomes powered the offense with 23-of-31 completions for 242 yards, two touchdowns and zero interceptions. He has been playing like his old self after returning to action in Week 1. The three-time Super Bowl champion suffered an ACL and LCL injury last December, and the Chiefs missed the playoffs for the first time since 2014.

Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes (15) greets wife, Brittany, prior to the game against the Las Vegas Raiders at Arrowhead Stadium.

Brittany and Patrick Mahomes made a joint Instagram post to announce that they will welcome their fourth child. Their family posed for photos at the beach and added a short caption.

The NFL dropped its reaction in the comments section.

Several players around the league, along with other football personalities also sent their well wishes. The couple already has three children, Sterling, Bronze and Golden Raye, whom Brittany gave birth to in January 2025.

The joyful news could be what the two-time NFL MVP needs to further push himself and his team to another Super Bowl win. They entered this season looking to reclaim lost glory and prove that they are still title contenders. Kansas City added key pieces in the offseason like reigning Super Bowl MVP Kenneth Walker III to boost their championship hopes. The team also traded for wide receiver Josh Downs ahead of Tuesday's trade deadline.

Mahomes and Co. will play their next game on Sunday against the Atlanta Falcons. CBS will broadcast the Week 10 showdown live at 1 p.m. ET from Mercedes-Benz Stadium.
