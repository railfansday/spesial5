GTA 6 players love its in-game memes

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) offers a satirical rendition of the U.S., filled with cars, crime, and shenanigans worthy of the game's Florida-inspired setting. It also incorporates many digital elements of modern life, including social media. Of course, where there's social media, there are inevitably memes.

In-game social media app Snapmatic is GTA 6's version of Instagram, offering a scrollable feed of posts from fictional characters showing off their very lootable possessions. However, Snapmatic isn't just filled with posts sharing potential theft coordinates. It also contains a plethora of memes specifically created to populate GTA 6's social media.

Players quickly took note of GTA 6's memes, praising them for accurately capturing the irreverent vibe and frequently off-colour humour that typically characterises such media.

"GTA 6 is so realistic that I thought this was a real meme," posted X user @thisdopenose, sharing an image of an in-game meme. In the meme, the text "bisexuals when they realise you can have sex more than twice" is edited onto a monochrome image of a man crying. The popular post has 94,000 likes at time of writing.

"They need to give a pay rise to whoever came up with the shitposts on Snapmatic bro these things are so funny," wrote @vandalfeet.

Some of GTA 6's in-game memes even referred to memes about GTA 6 itself. It's been 13 years since GTA 5 launched, with many fans having eagerly awaited the franchises' latest instalment for over a decade. In the interim, it became a common meme for people to jokingly express disbelief that various other events had occurred "before GTA 6" arrived.

Developer Rockstar Games appears to have been aware of such memes, creating its own versions referring to GTA 6's fictional video game Race and Chase 7. ("Race'n'Chase" was the original working title for the first Grand Theft Auto game released in 1997).

Rockstar Games had teams dedicated to creating in-game media, aiming to flesh out GTA 6's world and make it feel more immersive. Judging by fans' reactions, it appears to have paid off. Parent company Take-Two Interactive estimates that GTA 6 achieved $3.79 billion in sales on day one alone, making it one of the biggest entertainment media launches ever.
