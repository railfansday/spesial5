<em>Emily in Paris</em> Season 6: Everything We Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Emily Cooper enjoyed Rome in Emily in Paris season 5—but after that romantic cliffhanger in the season finale, fans are wondering if she’ll be giving love a try once again, with a gorgeous new European location as the backdrop for season 6.

Netflix confirmed the show’s renewal in January. In the months since, the cast and showrunner Darren Star have teased what to expect in the series’s next installment. Here’s everything we know so far about season 6 of Emily in Paris. Warning: Spoilers ahead.

On January 5, Netflix announced Emily in Paris has been renewed for a sixth season. Lily Collins appeared in the video where she remotely blew out a six candle on a croissant. “Home sweet home 🇫🇷 EMILY IN PARIS is returning for Season 6!” the streaming company wrote.

The announcement timeline is similar to when season 5 was confirmed: a month after season 4 was released. Netflix’s Ted Sarandos seems to be a huge fan of the show as well, telling Variety that his wife, Nicole, loves it too.

In an interview with Deadline in December 2025, showrunner Darren Star said there’s more of Emily’s story he is curious about. Season 5 ends with Miss Cooper receiving an invite to Greece from her on-again-off-again French boyfriend, Gabriel.

“I hope Emily says yes to that invitation,” Star mused. “[Emily and Gabriel] reconnected in a nice way. [As for] where that takes them, I think it definitely hints at the idea that they’ll stay in each other’s lives.”

The official logline for season 6 reads: “Stepping into a new era of ambition and romance, Emily faces defining choices that test her ultimate loyalties. But as unexpected career conflicts and shifting relationships force her to reevaluate where her true future lies, she must make one final choice between her career, her heart, and the life she’s built in Paris.”

Season 5 ends with Emily and her Italian boyfriend Marcello splitting up as he decides to stay in Italy to lead his family’s company. Emily returns to Paris, where she and Gabriel reconnect after Sylvie sends a sly text telling the French chef his gal is back in town—and single. He then invites her to Greece, setting the stage for yet another reunion between the pair. The new season will pick up from there—and may even reveal whether Gabriel and Emily finally make things work.

There is also the tension between Mindy and Alfie, despite her engagement to Nico. Plus, for fans who prefer business intrigue over love triangles, Agence Grateau is now being run by Sylvie and Princess Jane, after the latter made a hefty investment in the company.

Collins revealed a little about season 6 in April while speaking to Us Weekly at PaleyFest. She teased, “I know we end up in Greece, but I haven’t read anything.”

She added, “I’m happy when Emily’s happy.”

Star also shared more about the upcoming season’s locations, saying, “We’re going to be in Mykonos. And we will get to Monaco for a bit of a surprising reason. We’ll be in Paris a lot this season.”

Hinting at Emily and Gabriel’s future, he added, “They deserve each other. I hope so [that they will be together]. But I can’t promise.…She’s got a lot of options out there—and some possible new options too.”

Variety reported in April 2026 that the show filmed in locations set in Greece, Monaco, and Paris.

“I wanted the final season to feel like the biggest Emily in Paris experience yet,” Star said in an August 2026 press release. “We went to multiple locations this season, and Greece was somewhere we really wanted to showcase in a spectacular way. That’s part of the fantasy of Europe: you’re always within reach of another extraordinary place, another culture, another adventure. I wanted the audience to have that vicarious experience of traveling right along with Emily.”

Lily Collins will return as the titular character, and series regulars Ashley Park (Mindy), Philippine Leroy-Beaulieu (Sylvie), Lucas Bravo (Gabriel), Samuel Arnold (Julian), Bruno Gouery (Luc), Lucien Laviscount (Alfie), and Paul Forman (Nicolas) are expected to reprise their roles, as well as Minnie Driver (Princess Jane). It’s unclear if Eugenio Franceschini as Marcello will have a regular place in the show now, but the series does love to keep Emily’s past boyfriends around.

On September 18, Collins hinted that they were about to wrap. She posted an Instagram in which she reflected on her last day of shooting the series’s final season.

“My last day on set as Emily…” she wrote. “I’m speechless. It’s impossible to find the words for what this journey has meant to me. For so many years, Emily has been a part of my life, and through every season, she’s continued to surprise me, inspire me, and challenge me. “Thank you, Darren Star, for dreaming up this endlessly optimistic American girl in Paris who finds herself constantly outside of her comfort zone, learning to trust herself while leaning on others as she navigates her new life,” she continued. “I’m so grateful you trusted me to bring her to life. Thank you to our unparalleled crew, our writers, our directors, and of course our incredible cast who is now family and who I cannot imagine this without. You have made Emily, Emily. You made this world feel like home. And merci to all of you who have been supporting us over these past 7 years and 6 seasons, keeping us inspired to dream bigger along our epic adventure. “I will carry this chapter with me forever. Saying goodbye feels completely surreal and bittersweet, but mostly, I feel so incredibly proud and grateful that I got to be her. And be part of something so joyful. This has forever changed me. Au revoir, Emily Cooper…”

Emily in Paris season 6 will premiere on Netflix on December 24, just in time for Christmas.

Yes. On November 11, Netflix released the first teaser trailer for Emily in Paris season 6, which takes viewers through past seasons from her time in France.

“When I came here, Paris was this fun little detour. I thought it would be a good story, but it’s complicated, and chaotic,” she tells Mindy, cut between scenes of her time working at Agence Grateau and flashbacks to her love triangle with Gabriel and Camille. “This isn’t just a fun year abroad anymore. This is your life,” Mindy responds.

“While you’re here, fall in love, make mistakes. If you’re gonna do Paris, for God's sake, do it right,” Sylvie tells Emily, as scenes of her dalliances with Alfie and Gabriel play. Emily makes her fair share of bad decisions, too, as seen in a moment where Mindy catches her cutting her hair into bangs in the bathroom sink.

Paris, however, takes a backseat by the end of the final season teaser. “Let’s party in Greece, baby!” Mindy declares, setting the backdrop for the next installment. Yachts, beach clubs, and day parties await the cast of characters, and for Emily, an unexpected reunion with Gabriel. “Are you ready?” he asks her, smiling ear to ear.
