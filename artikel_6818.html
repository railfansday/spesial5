There are elements to the landslide that are worthy of their own posts, and we’ll get to them as the day goes on. But here are some other highlights of the Night of the Great Reckoning.

Say what you will about Hakeem Jeffries, but he rose to this challenge. He sent out word to states with Democratic-majority legislatures to get to work on their own plans.
