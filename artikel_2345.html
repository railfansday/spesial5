Tottenham reportedly reserve matching right clause to re-sign Kane from Bayern

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

According to a report by Matt Law in the Telegraph, Tottenham Hotspur still retain an option to re-sign Harry Kane.

Even after his recent contract extension with Bayern Munich, the London club apparently have a route to sign the Ballon d'Or winner.

The clause is a “matching right”, which means that Spurs can match any bid made on Kane should FC Bayern ever decide to sell him.

So, in theory if a club offer a €80million for Kane in 2028, Spurs could match it and Bayern would be legally bound to sell to the latter.

This clause goes back to the original deal and transfer fee negotiated between Bayern Munich and Daniel Levy back in 2023.

However, it's a clause that is likely to end up irrelevant, as every sign indicates that Harry Kane will finish his top level career at Bayern.
