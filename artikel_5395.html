Unishippers Improves 18 Spots on Franchise Times' Top 400 Ranking Driven by Over 32% Increase in Revenue

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Leading 3PL provider named sales growth leader in the annual ranking's shipping category

DALLAS, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Unishippers, a leading third-party logistics (3PL) provider serving small and mid-sized businesses, was named the sales growth leader in the Franchise Times Top 400 list's shipping category. The annual ranking evaluates the largest U.S.-based franchise systems by global systemwide sales, based on the previous year's performance.

Unishippers reported strong growth in 2025, up 32.4% in sales from the prior year. The company's performance pushed Unishippers up 18 spots to No. 81 overall. Driving the growth, Unishippers expanded its franchise units to 236 in 2025 compared to 206 in 2024.

"We love this part of the business because we support entrepreneurs, and we make sure they have the resources they need to succeed," said Dustin Wesley, Executive Vice President of Partner Channel at Unishippers. "The franchise owners who joined us in 2023 and 2024 are now driving growth, and the hands-on, peer-to-peer training we focused on in 2025 is paying dividends for them. When our franchise owners succeed, Unishippers succeeds."

"Unishippers is a phenomenal company and I'm very thankful to be a franchisee," said Rich Pyle, Unishippers franchise business owner. "The company has been committed to our success from the start, providing the support we needed to hit the ground running and helping us to successfully implement the playbook. Having the ability to work alongside my family is a wonderful privilege and I wouldn't trade it for the world."
