Can MRVL's Custom Silicon Business Drive Its Next Growth Phase?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Marvell Technology MRVL is expanding its presence in the artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure market through its custom silicon business. The company develops custom compute and connectivity solutions for hyperscalers seeking to build and optimize AI infrastructure. Its portfolio includes custom XPUs and XPU-attach products, which help address growing requirements for AI computing, memory and data movement.

MRVL’s custom silicon opportunity is gaining traction as hyperscalers expand their investments in AI infrastructure. The company expects demand for its XPU and XPU-attach solutions to accelerate in the second half of fiscal 2027. Management remains confident that custom silicon revenues will more than double year over year in fiscal 2028, with further acceleration expected in fiscal 2029. This provides greater visibility into the business' contribution to MRVL’s multi-year growth outlook.

An expanded commercial agreement with a Tier 1 hyperscaler further strengthens this opportunity. The agreement covers existing programs, new design wins and potential future projects. Its scope extends beyond AI inference accelerators to include storage controllers, network interface controllers (NICs), memory interface controllers and near-memory compute solutions associated with the customer's tensor processing unit ecosystem. The broader engagement creates opportunities for MRVL to supply multiple components across AI computing and connectivity architectures.

The company is also benefiting from its expanding relationships across the AI ecosystem. Its strategic partnership with NVIDIA and investments in networking technologies complement its custom silicon portfolio. By combining custom compute solutions with high-speed connectivity, switching and optical interconnect technologies, MRVL is positioning itself to address multiple requirements of increasingly complex AI infrastructure. This broader portfolio could strengthen its competitive position as hyperscalers develop next-generation AI systems.

However, the expansion carries execution and profitability risks. Custom silicon programs typically depend on customer design decisions, deployment schedules and production ramps. Moreover, management expects the accelerating custom product mix to weigh on near-term gross margins. MRVL’s increasing dependence on large hyperscalers also raises customer concentration risks. Nevertheless, sustained design wins and the expected revenue acceleration position custom silicon as an important driver of its growth through fiscal 2028 and beyond.

MRVL faces stiff competition from Broadcom $AVGO AVGO and Advanced Micro Devices AMD $AMD. Broadcom is a leader in the domain of custom silicon solutions for data centers. Broadcom’s advanced 3.5D XDSiP packaging platform is critical to ensure the performance and efficiency of custom AI XPUs.

Broadcom’s relationships with leading hyperscale and frontier AI customers give it direct insight into workload requirements and help shape differentiated accelerators. The company continues to work with six core XPU customers across multiyear programs. Advanced Micro Devices is another established player in the custom silicon solutions and AI accelerator market. Advanced Micro Devices offers semi-custom SoCs and Instinct Accelerators to power data centers. AMD is broadening its data center AI opportunity from Instinct accelerators to the Helios rack-scale platform.

Shares of Marvell Technology have gained 71.3% year to date compared with the Zacks Electronics - Semiconductors industry’s growth of 3.5%.

From a valuation standpoint, Marvell Technology trades at a forward price-to-sales ratio of 7.10X, higher than the industry’s average of 2.88X.

The Zacks Consensus Estimate for MRVL’s fiscal 2027 and 2028 earnings implies year-over-year growth of 48% and 76%, respectively. The estimate for fiscal 2027 has remained unchanged for the past 30 days, while the estimates for fiscal 2028 have been revised upward in the past 30 days.
