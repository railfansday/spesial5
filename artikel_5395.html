Garmin Signal VHF named Overall Winner in 2026 DAME Design Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Innovative all-in-one marine communication solution earns top honors at Metstrade

OLATHE, Kan., Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Garmin (NYSE: GRMN), the world's largest1 and most innovative marine electronics manufacturer, today announced the Garmin Signalâ¢ VHF series was selected as an Overall Winner of the prestigious 2026 DAME Design Awards. The system also earned top honors in the Navigation & Communication category. Winners were announced at Metstrade, the world's largest exhibition of marine equipment, materials and systems, held Nov. 17-19 in Amsterdam.

This year's competition drew 153 entries, with 74 products nominated for the awards â the highest number of products shortlisted in over two decades. For the first time in the program's history, the jury of industry and design experts and media representatives selected three Overall Winners that represented excellence in every facet of product design across eight categories.

"We designed the Garmin Signal VHF series to rethink what boaters should expect from one of the most essential pieces of equipment at the helm. From its modern touchscreen design to integrated AIS and advanced communication capabilities, every detail makes the experience simpler and more intuitive. We're honored that the DAME jury recognized this new system with Garmin's first Overall DAME Design Award win." âJarrod Seymour, Garmin Vice President and Marine Segment Leader

In one compact solution, Garmin Signal VHF delivers communication, situational awareness and intuitive operation. The radios feature an industry-first 3.5-inch color touchscreen with edge-to-edge glass, and the Signal VHF 400 adds an integrated Class B AIS transponder that helps boaters stay aware of surrounding vessel traffic. Boaters can transmit and receive VHF, DSC and AIS communications through a single VHF antenna, and can pair the system with the Signal RM 100 remote station to control the radio from a second helm or flybridge.

The jury noted: "The design of the new Garmin Signal range of VHF marine radios expands on all expected functions of a VHF and adds much more, including fully integrated AIS transmit and receive (using just one antenna), advanced channel monitoring capabilities and the ability to recall the last three minutes of a transmission. The radio and visually identical remote station appear to take their styling cues from Garmin's Fusion audio range with an aesthetic that will sit nicely within any modern helm station."

The coveted DAME Design Award caps off another prolific year for Garmin Marine and follows other industry recognitions from the National Marine Electronics Association (NMEA), IBEX, Soundings Trade Only, and the Sea Tow Foundation.
