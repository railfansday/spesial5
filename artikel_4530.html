Uterine pressure guides head-to-tail axis formation in mouse embryos

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

During animal development, the establishment of the three primary body axes—head-to-tail (anterior-posterior), dorsal-ventral and left-right—is essential for building a proper body structure. A fertilized egg, initially a single cell, undergoes repeated cell divisions to form a multicellular structure called an embryo, and body axes are formed within the embryo.
