Going the distance: these electric semi trucks cover over 600 miles EVERY DAY

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

“Sure, diesel is high, but electric trucks don’t work.” It’s a common refrain these days – especially from people selling diesel trucks. Perception lags reality, however, and the reality is that these electric semi trucks are covering more than 600 miles per day, seven days a week.

Over the course of the last year, Hendrik Müller, managing partner of DEFRU Logistik GmbH has transformed his company’s shipping fleet, switching thirty of their HD prime movers (semi trucks) to electric power and immediately putting the Mercedes-Benz eActros 600s to work, covering 1,000 km (~620 miles) of German highways each and every day.

Benjamin Budick, a Senior Manager, Own Retail Mercedes-Benz Trucks Dortmund & Haan, took to LinkedIn to announce the successful deployment, and posted some great pictures of the handover showing the big Mercedes trucks in full DEFRU livery.

Mercedes’ eActros 600 packs a whopping, 621 kWh battery that helps the trucks cover 500 km per charge, allowing fleets to move goods between ports and regional fulfillment centers, charging along the way, or while they’re getting loaded and unloaded at their destination, all the while saving the fleet serious money – even before the latest Iran War sent diesel prices skyrocketing.

That big battery sends electrons to Mercedes-Benz’s proprietary electric drive axles, which deliver 1.61 kWh / mile efficiency while rolling out at an average of 36 metric tons (~79,400 lbs.) combined vehicle weight – specs that rivals the theoretical efficiency claims of the Tesla Semi. Except in the real world. And in winter.

The eActros is a serious beast. As mentioned there are many configurations and they seem to work well in Europe. I've seen one that was configured with 620kWh battery, on one side a MCS port plus a CCS2 port (so thats either 1.2MW or 600kW charging options) and on the other side a CCS2 port.

In Europe with CCS, 350kW chargers are easy to find, 400kW are becoming more common and 600kW models are starting to arrive, therefore with the limitations on driving hours and speeds, the eActros really is an all day every day truck.

Of course if you are in the far North of Europe and are allowed to haul 60 tonnes, Scania have made a complete monster of a truck with over 800kWh of battery!

The company recently expanded its eActros lineup to include over 40 possible configurations, giving customers a choice of two cab designs, two or three battery packs, multiple wheelbases and new axle configurations, covering everything from short-haul drayage to serious, off highway vocational work.
