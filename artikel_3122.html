Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Management lifted its low-end guidance as a large data center pipeline takes shape.

Entergy New Orleans, LLC (ENO +0.36%) reported increased quarterly earnings driven by favorable summer weather and expanding retail sales volumes. Management raised the lower end of its annual guidance range and expanded the five-year capital plan to include battery storage projects and grid infrastructure improvements. The company highlighted successful regulatory settlements and the approval of significant transmission projects to support a large pipeline of data center and industrial demand. Strategic focus remains on operational efficiency through standardized plant designs and non-traditional financing structures to manage capital requirements and maintain credit health.
