Here's What $10,000 Invested in Bloom Energy (BE) Stock Could Be Worth in 3 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

This stock has soared in recent years -- but it's volatile.

If you'd invested in Bloom Energy (BE +3.42%), a company supplying fuel cells used in AI data centers, three years ago, you'd have done well. More than well, actually. A $10,000 investment would be worth more than $165,000! On an annual basis, that averages to a 155% gain.

The S&P 500 performed well above average over the past three years, but it only averaged annual gains of 21%, turning $10,000 into $17,857.

You might now wonder, what would $10,000 invested in Bloom Energy be worth three years from now? Well, with any stock, I would caution that no one can know exactly how it will perform over any particular period. See? A bunch of down years and several years with whopping gains.

The stock's valuation can offer some insight. For example, the recent price-to-sales ratio of 17.2 is well above the five-year average of 3.35, suggesting that the shares are quite overvalued. The recent price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio of 130, meanwhile, is down from 1,086 in 2025, but is still quite high. This overvaluation would lead me to rein in my growth expectations.
