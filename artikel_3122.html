Hagerty: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Hagerty's claims team grew 40%, and its member service staff now handles 2,800 policies per employee, a productivity gain that bears on long-term loss control.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 3, 2026, Hagerty (HGTY +7.17%) raised its full-year earnings guidance after generating $257 million in adjusted EBITDA, or earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, through the first nine months of the year. The specialty insurer added 402,000 new members while scaling its high-margin distribution partnerships and auction platform. Three things management said stand out.

Gen X, Millennials, and Gen Z account for the majority of new-quote volume as younger buyers collect post-1980s vehicles. This demographic shift expands the addressable market beyond older collectors of classic automobiles.

Growing the internal claims team by 40% helped reduce physical damage costs and liability claims below expected levels. Specialty underwriting and proactive storm warnings keep loss ratios more stable than standard auto insurers.

Partnerships with large carriers and 54,000 independent agents deliver a steady stream of new business. The rollout with State Farm alone includes converting 525,000 existing classic-car policies to Hagerty-backed coverage.

Workflow automation and scaled distribution are strengthening the company's competitive advantage while driving operational efficiency. Management is maintaining pricing discipline and leveraging its partner network to capture market share.

Long-term value creation rests on converting auction attendees and car enthusiasts into multi-year policyholders across an underpenetrated vehicle market.
