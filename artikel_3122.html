Getting some help with holiday gift shopping for my dad

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

My feelings were low-key hurt when one of my friends called me “The King of Gift Cards” last year after I passed them a wrapped-up box containing…you guessed it, a gift card!

I laughed at it, then went to the bathroom to look at myself in the mirror and say to myself, “I have to get better at my gift-giving next year.” Now that we’re near the closing stretch of 2026, I’m doing some super early Christmas gift planning. And this time, I’m veering away from my penchant for picking up a bunch of gift cards and throwing them at my friends and family like it’s a massive giveaway.

In particular, I’m going the extra mile to make sure I give my father something he truly cherishes. He doesn’t really ask for much around the holidays, but I still want to give him something extra special. Shopping for my mom is so much easier since she tells me exactly what she wants, but it’s a bit harder to do that for my dad.

With ChatGPT and Gemini in my corner, I used both chatbots to see which one could bring up the best gift recommendations. And one of them came out ahead a bit more than its competition.

Since I know my dad’s two main interests, I included them in my prompt alongside a few of his other faves to make sure both chatbots generated the most appropriate gifts possible. This is the prompt I ultimately went with:

“My father is a drummer and a vinyl collector. His favorite artists are Jimi Hendrix, James Brown, The Beatles, The Monkees, A Tribe Called Quest, and Prince. Show me the 7 best gift recommendations I should consider getting him for Christmas this year.”

ChatGPT certainly understood the assignment, as it combed the internet and came back with a wide selection of books and records worth throwing onto my dad’s gift wishlist. As for items geared toward his profession and hobby as a drummer, the chatbot brought up one product that should be right up his alley. Alongside each of its picks, it attached them to different categories and listed their prices so I could have as much information about all its listed items as possible.

At the end of my Christmas gift recommendation experiment, I had a hard time deciding which chatbot supplied me with the better selection of products.

Both of them featured a strong selection of vinyl that I’m confident my dad would go crazy over after I revealed any of them as his gift. As for the gifts that were geared towards his music-based profession, both ChatGPT brought up one item while Gemini mentioned two recommendations. It was a close race, but I ended up siding with Gemini more during this latest AI tool faceoff. ChatGPT’s list is strong, no doubt. But Gemini’s clever inclusion of a custom rubber turntable slipmat with his favorite artist’s logo and custom-engraved hickory drumsticks stood out to me over ChatGPT’s inclusion of a record-washer kit.

My dad is always in the market for new drumsticks and I’m sure he’d do a backflip at the sight ot a turntable slipmat adorned with the iconic Prince symbol. Major kudos to Gemini for inching out ahead of ChatGPT’s gift recommendations and making my shopping a whole lot easier for him this holiday season (no gift cards this year!).

I implore everyone reading this to prompt both chatbots to give them equally memorable gift recommendations for the people they love based on their standout hobbies and interests.
