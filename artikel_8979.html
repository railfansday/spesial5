Bellano Cotton Mill Museum

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

On the eastern shore of Lake Como in Bellano, Italy, the former Cantoni Cotton Mill tells a story of water, machinery and working life. Its substantial buildings and rhythmic façades offer a different perspective on a landscape more often associated with villas and waterfront gardens. The Bellano Cotton Mill Museum competition invites participants to give this industrial landmark a new cultural role.

On the eastern shore of Lake Como in Bellano, Italy, the former Cantoni Cotton Mill tells a story of water, machinery and working life. Its substantial buildings and rhythmic façades offer a different perspective on a landscape more often associated with villas and waterfront gardens. The Bellano Cotton Mill Museum competition invites participants to give this industrial landmark a new cultural role.

Organised by Buildner in partnership with Selpi Srl, the competition asks participants to transform the smaller building within the complex into a museum and cultural destination. The task is to develop an architectural response that balances preservation with transformation, exploring how the existing structure can accommodate exhibitions, welcome visitors and reveal its character through contemporary use.
