The Division 2 Y8S3 Red Horizon Week 11 Manhunt HVT: Scout 11 solutions

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Division 2 Y8S3 Red Horizon Week 11 is now live, and we can immediately access this season’s Climax mission. There are three tasks to complete as usual, and things aren’t looking too difficult in Week 11. However, there’s a HVT mission to complete before we can get the Exotic.

Once again, you’ll have to use the clues for the first task to complete it. This will reveal what needs to be done for the next one (and then the last one). In this guide, I have decoded all three clues, so you know what has to be done to access the Climax mission.

Living World Activities represent all the random stuff happening in the background. For this task, you’ll need to finish any three of them, at any difficulty of your liking. However, they must belong to the Black Tusk faction to qualify. My preferences are shorter events like Hostage Rescue and Public Executions since they take less time.

Invaded mission refers to the Black Tusk version of ordinary missions. You’ll get to make this switch before starting a fresh mission from the menu. To finish this task, you’ll also need to finish Tidal Basin first so that you’re in Tier 5.

The easiest way to locate these missions is by seeing the red-colored hexagon pattern that replaces the traditional grey one.

Finally, the bounty will be available once you clear the first two tasks. The location can be found on the map itself, and you’ll get 15 minutes to activate it. Then, you can begin the assault to complete the bounty. The mission is straightforward if you have done it as part of the main campaign.

Once you eliminate all the enemies, Higgins will appear in his special gear. Take him down, and you’ll complete the final task. You have to finish all three HVT hunts to take part in the climax mission.
