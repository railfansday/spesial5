ZoomInfo is seeking a Principal Product Manager, Identity to own the intelligence layer that connects digital signals to real-world business outcomes. In this high-impact role, you'll be responsible for the identity resolution engine that de-anonymizes web traffic, links visitors to companies and contacts, and powers products like WebSights and FormComplete.

As a Principal PM, you'll work at the intersection of platform engineering, data science, and customer value creation. You'll drive systematic improvements to visitor-to-person and IP-to-company match rates, integrate new data sources, and ensure identity data flows seamlessly across ZoomInfo's GTM Intelligence Platform. Your work directly impacts how thousands of customers identify, engage, and convert their ideal buyers.
