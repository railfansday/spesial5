NFL forced Saquon Barkley to sell Polymarket stake, treating prediction markets as sportsbooks

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Eagles running back Saquon Barkley sold his equity stake in Polymarket after NFL rules barred players from holding prediction-market investments. The case sets the league apart from the NBA and MLB, which have signed deals with the exchanges, as the NFL argues at the Supreme Court that sports event contracts are gambling.
