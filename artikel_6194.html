Hard Rock Hotels Plug Into Accor Without Selling a Stake

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Hard Rock keeps its equity and hands Accor the task of developing its pipeline of non-casino hotels. Accor collects ongoing fees and gets a well-known brand to essentially rent out to its loyalty members.

Earlier this year, Hard Rock International listed its hotel in New York City on Accor’s booking site and app and told almost no one.

The first month brought 84 room nights. By July, the channel was delivering more than 1,500 a month, more than Hard Rock’s own site produced for the hotel, said Jeff Hook, chief operating officer of Hard Rock. More than 90% of those guests were new to Hard Rock.

The New York hotel test was a prelude. On Monday, Accor and Hard Rock announced a global partnership. Hard Rock’s hotels will join ALL, Accor’s loyalty program. Ennismore, Accor's lifestyle joint venture, will help develop Hard Rock hotels worldwide, and Accor's global sales team will help Hard Rock win group and meetings business.

Hard Rock will have about 25 standalone hotels once the integration is fully underway, Hook said. The company also has about 10 casino hotels and three of them might eventually be listed on Accor, too.

Accor and Hard Rock International announced a global partnership on Monday that puts Hard Rock's hotels into Accor's ALL loyalty program, gives Hard Rock access to Accor's global sales team for group and meetings business, and enlists Ennismore, Accor's lifestyle joint venture, to help develop Hard Rock hotels worldwide. No equity changes hands. Accor isn't investing capital, and Chief Commercial, Digital, and Tech Officer Alix Boulnois described the structure as closer to a franchise model in which Accor is paid for the business it brings. The deal follows a quiet test: Hard Rock listed its New York hotel on Accor's site and app earlier this year, and the channel grew from 84 room nights in the first month to more than 1,500 a month by July — more than Hard Rock's own site produced for that hotel, with more than 90% of those guests new to Hard Rock, said Hard Rock COO Jeff Hook. Hook said bookings from Accor's channel are "substantially less expensive" than those from Expedia, Booking.com, or other online travel agencies. Hard Rock will have about 25 standalone hotels in the arrangement, talked to four large hotel companies before choosing Accor, and turned down offers to buy all or part of its hotel division. Ennismore's operational role will vary deal by deal, from finding development deals to full management, with casinos excluded.
