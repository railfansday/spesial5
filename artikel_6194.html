Alaska Atmos Rewards Summit Card Bonus: 100K Points & 25K-Point Global Companion Award

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The $395 annual fee Atmos™ Rewards Summit Visa Infinite® Credit Card is currently offering its best-ever publicly available welcome bonus, which can earn you 100,000 Atmos Rewards points plus a 25K-point Global Companion Award (which I consider to be worth face value).

We only saw this bonus return a couple of weeks ago, and I’d like to provide an update, as I’ve received word that this offer is ending soon (though I don’t have an exact date to share). Obviously this was a very short term limited time offer, so you’ll want to apply ASAP, if interested.

This is one of the most lucrative airline credit cards on the market — it’s a card that I have, and it’s also one that I spend a lot of money on. If you don’t yet have the card, you really can’t go wrong with this, and this is the time to apply.

Link: Learn about the Atmos Rewards Summit Card bonus offer

The Atmos Rewards Summit Card is currently offering a limited time welcome bonus that consists of 100,000 bonus points and a 25,000-point Global Companion Award. To qualify, you need to spend $6,000 or more on purchases within the first 90 days of opening your account.

Based on my latest miles & points valuations, Atmos Rewards points are worth 1.5 cents each. I consider the offer to be worth 125,000 points, putting the value of this at a staggering $1,875. Even if you’re not convinced on the long term value proposition of the card, I absolutely think it’s worth giving a try, given all the uses of Atmos Rewards points.

If you’re interested in applying for the Atmos Rewards Summit Card, the good news is that the application restrictions are pretty limited. Specifically, you are eligible for this card if you have any of the other Atmos Rewards cards, whether personal or business. So you’re eligible for this card even if you have the Atmos™ Rewards Ascent Visa Signature® credit card (learn more) or Atmos™ Rewards Visa Signature® Business Card (learn more).

Personally, I find Bank of America to be one of the most straightforward and easier card issuers to get approvals from, though that’s anecdotal, and I’ve had good luck with these cards.
