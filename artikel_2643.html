T.J. Hockenson, Andrew Van Ginkel listed as limited participants on Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Vikings tight end T.J. Hockenson did not play in Week 10, but he appears to be on the right track for a return to the lineup in the near future.

Hockenson was able to practice on a limited basis Wednesday. He is listed with an ankle injury, but was also listed with a chest injury when he missed practice all last week.

Linebacker Andrew Van Ginkel (quad) was also listed as a limited participant. He was injured late in last Sunday’s win over the Packers.

Linebacker Blake Cashman missed that game with a concussion, but was up to full practice participation on Wednesday. Wide receiver Jauan Jennings (calf) was also a full practice participant after missing the last two games. Defensive lineman Tyrion Ingram-Dawkins (ankle) was also in that group.

Wide receiver Jordan Addison (thumb) and quarterback Kyler Murray (hamstring) did not practice. Wide receiver Jeshaun Jones (shoulder), linebacker Kyle Van Noy (calf), and safety Jay Ward (knee) were listed as limited participants.
