The Falcoholic Reacts Survey: Any chance for a stunning win?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year we ask questions of the most plugged-in Falcons fans and fans across the country.

After facing two of the AFC's top teams, the road doesn't get any easier for the Falcons. They will square off against two more top contenders coming from the league's best division. The Vikings and Lions pose serious challenges for any team, let alone one lacking an identity. While the Falcons will have ample time to prepare for these matchups coming off the bye week, it remains a tall order given how disjointed the offense is and how limited the defense is.

The Vikings will be enraged following a one-sided loss to the 49ers. Brian Flores has a long history of overwhelming quarterbacks who struggle to operate under pressure. A reunion with Kirk Cousins will give the defense opportunities to make plays, but the veteran quarterback is a clear upgrade over Kyler Murray and Carson Wentz.

The Lions will have extra time to rest after playing on Thanksgiving in a highly anticipated matchup against the Bears. How the Falcons respond to adversity in these matchups against far more talented teams will be a solid indicator of what this team and coaching staff are made of.

While a win is highly unlikely in either matchup, who could you see the Falcons beating over the next two games, if you had to make a pick? Let us know below.
