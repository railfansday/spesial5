Rumors of early loan recall for Mikey Moore appear unfounded

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Cologne's reliable GEISSBLOG reports that 1. FC Cologne have no intention of terminating Mikey Moore's loan agreement with Tottenham Hotspur in January, despite speculation emerging in England that the 19-year-old could return to North London prematurely.

English outlet TEAMtalk recently suggested that Tottenham were considering recalling Moore during the winter transfer window, with Cologne reportedly open to ending the agreement ahead of schedule. GEISSBLOG nevertheless directly disputes the suggestion that the Bundesliga outfit would entertain such a move.

According to the Cologne-based publication, the club's sporting management remains convinced that Moore can develop into an important attacking contributor over the remainder of the season. There are currently no plans to abandon the arrangement, notwithstanding the Englishman's difficulties adapting to the Bundesliga.

Moore joined Cologne on a season-long loan after a prolonged transfer pursuit last summer. The highly regarded Tottenham academy product arrived amid considerable expectations, but his performances have yet to produce the attacking returns anticipated by either club.

As Get German Football News reported ahead of Cologne's recent Bundesliga derby against Bayer Leverkusen, Moore had already experienced a difficult period in which inconsistent performances, tactical considerations, and workload management contributed to several spells on the substitutes' bench.

Head coach René Wagner nevertheless handed the youngster another starting opportunity against Leverkusen. Moore failed to make the desired breakthrough in the subsequent 2-1 defeat, earning rather unflattering reviews for another performance characterised by considerable effort but limited attacking effectiveness.

After eight Bundesliga appearances, the English youth international remains without a goal or assist. His continued struggles in the final third have prompted questions about whether he can consistently justify a starting position in Wagner's side.

Nevertheless, Cologne's sporting director Thomas Kessler continues to express confidence in the player's development.

"Mikey is important for us in progressing the ball," Kessler explained after the Leverkusen defeat. "He has plenty of situations in which he beats his man and helps us reach the final third. He's still a bit unfortunate there, but we're nevertheless very happy to have him with us."

Wagner has similarly urged Moore to remain confident despite his difficulties.

"He needs to keep being brave and getting into those situations," the Cologne coach explained. "Then he'll get his reward."

GEISSBLOG also highlights a significant regulatory obstacle to another potential loan move. Moore has already featured competitively for both Tottenham and Cologne this season, meaning that FIFA's restrictions on appearances for multiple clubs would prevent him from representing a third team during the same campaign.

A return to Rangers, where Moore spent last season on loan, would therefore not be a straightforward option. The teenager would instead be restricted to playing for Tottenham or Cologne for the remainder of the current season, subject to the applicable regulations.

His previous spell in Scotland nevertheless offers some encouragement for those hoping that his fortunes in Germany might improve.

Moore initially struggled to establish himself at Rangers before developing into a more influential attacking presence during the second half of the campaign. He eventually finished his Scottish loan with seven goals and three assists.

Cologne hope that a similar improvement will follow in the Bundesliga. For the time being, however, the club's position remains clear: Moore's lack of goals, assists, and consistent performances has not persuaded the club to reconsider the loan agreement.
