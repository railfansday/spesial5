When Brian Flores defenses face Kyle Shanahan offenses, Flores is 5-0

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Vikings defensive coordinator Brian Flores will face off with 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan on Sunday night in Mexico City, and if history is any guide, Flores has the advantage.

Teams with Flores as a head coach or defensive assistant have played against teams with Shanahan as a head coach or offensive assistant five times, and Flores is 5-0.

Since becoming defensive coordinator of the Vikings, Flores has coached against Shanahan’s 49ers twice, with Flores’ team coming out on top both times. In 2023, the Vikings beat the 49ers 22-17. In 2024, the Vikings beat the 49ers 23-17.

Flores and Shanahan coached against each other as head coaches once, in 2020, when Flores’ Dolphins beat Shanahan’s 49ers 43-17.

The most famous game with Flores and Shanahan on opposite sides came in Super Bowl LI, when Flores was linebackers coach for the Patriots and Shanahan was offensive coordinator for the Falcons. Shanahan’s Falcons jumped out to a 28-3 lead, only to have Flores’ Patriots come back and win 34-28 in overtime.

And in 2011, New England beat Washington 34-27 when Flores was a defensive assistant in New England and Shanahan was offensive coordinator in Washington.

Shanahan was once on a coaching staff that got a win against a team with Flores on the staff, in the final game of the 2009 season, when the Patriots had already clinched their playoff spot, rested starters and lost to a Texans team with Shanahan as offensive coordinator. But Flores was a special teams assistant, not a defensive assistant, on that Patriots team, so that game wasn’t a Flores defense vs. Shanahan offense competition.

When it’s Flores coaching defense against Shanahan coaching offense, the result has been the same across different teams and in different coaching roles: Flores has five wins and Shanahan has none.
