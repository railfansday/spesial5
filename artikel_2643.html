Knicks’ Pacome Dadiet has big night after Mike Brown calls him out

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Mike Brown sent a warning to Pacôme Dadiet:

Dadiet responded with his best game of the season Wednesday, dropping seven points in 14 minutes of the Knicks' 121-102 win over the Cavaliers — including a couple of big buckets in the fourth quarter.

Pacome Dadiet, who scored seven points off the bench, goes up for a layup during the Knicks' 121-102 win over the Cavaliers on Nov. 25, 2026, at Madison Square Garden. Vincent Carchietta-Imagn Images

"I didn't think he played hard in the game before," Brown said. "And I actually called him into my office and showed him some film. I told him, 'As a young guy, if you don't play hard, I can't play you.'

"And I said, 'Believe me, I got a lot of guys I can go to. I'm not afraid to go to somebody else. So the least you could do is play hard.' And I showed him how he wasn't. And I thought [Wednesday], he played hard."

OG Anunoby, who is recovering from a calf strain, progressed to individual on-court work Wednesday morning, according to Brown.

Anunoby missed his third straight game with the injury, sitting out Wednesday's game.

The Knicks are classifying Anunoby's recovery as "day to day." Calf strains have become dangerous injuries in the NBA because they've developed into Achilles tears. But Brown said he's blissfully unaware of that risk for Anunoby.

"There are a lot of medical terms that are above my head," Brown said. "They tell me and I don't really pick up on the injury. If he's out, I change my minutes sheet. And then I'll just wait for them to tell me when he's back. [Wednesday], OG's individual work happened before shootaround and I didn't even know he had individual work until his player development guy told me later on. You never want a guy to hurt himself more. But I don't think about it."

Jalen Brunson (illness), Karl-Anthony Towns (knee soreness) and Josh Hart (sprained shoulder) all played despite being on the injury report.

Miles McBride (left adductor tightness) sat out his second straight game.

"Tightness and just being smart," the Knicks said.

McBride had surgery around that area last season.

Andre Drummond suffered a head injury in the first half Wednesday and played just eight minutes.

Lead assistant Chris Jent returned to the sideline Wednesday after missing nearly a month for personal reasons.

Jent is the Knicks' offensive coordinator and was recently lauded by LeBron James for developing the future Hall of Famer's jumper.

"I used to watch [Jent] shoot in practice," said James, who was with the Cavaliers when Jent was an assistant under Brown. "And I just started to work with him a lot.

"I would be like, 'Yo, CJ, I need you to come with me. We need to work.' And he was kind enough to join me. So I give a lot of credit to where my shot is today from where it was to Chris Jent."

Assistant TJ Saint served as Jent's replacement over the past month.
