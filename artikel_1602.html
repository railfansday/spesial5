Fabrizio Romano confirms it: Liverpool could sign striker for €35m

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Fabrizio Romano has confirmed Liverpool's opportunity to sign a striker for €35m. But will they go for it?

The January transfer window is very nearly here and Liverpool may just have work to do. There's a sense that while the Reds are playing well under Andoni Iraola, they're doing so with something of a makeshift squad.

Or at least, a squad that isn't very balanced. There's room for improvement, with Liverpool lacking a proper defensive midfielder, seemingly unsettled on any right-back, and lacking any wingers who naturally play from the right.

If they get one or two players? It could well transform them from a very good, competitive team to one actually trying to win the major honours. And Iraola is certainly suggesting with his efforts that the potential is there.

Liverpool have been linked with Gustavo Varela, who is currently lighting up Serie A with Monza. He's got 11 goals and an assist in his 11 league matches this season, including a brace against Fiorentina at the weekend.

It's a strange one, though. Liverpool don't necessarily need a striker, given Alexander Isak is the clear first-choice and scoring goals, while Bradley Barcola and Cody Gakpo can also play there.

Of course, there's also Hugo Ekitike - we do expect the Frenchman will be back this season, even if he's been out long-term. Exactly how much of an option he is this campaign isn't clear but it feels highly unlikely that Liverpool would sign someone to essentially replace him.

Unless they feel a new striker is necessary to compete for trophies this season and the next. And they might. Ekitike is a gamble at this stage.

It certainly appears that the chance to sign Varela will be there. Fabrizio Romano has confirmed that he's available for around €35m, which is potentially a steal if his goal scoring numbers hold up.

Though, Serie A's history is littered with strikers who started a season remarkably well only to fade away gradually, never replicating the form again. So while Varela is playing brilliantly, he'd be an enormous risk.
