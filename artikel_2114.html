What Dave Challinor said after Sheffield Wednesday win has sparked a reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The new Sheffield Wednesday manager had qualified praise for his team's performance following his team's South Yorkshire derby win at Barnsley.

Following a slightly rocky start, Dave Challinor is starting to settle in as the new manager of Sheffield Wednesday. Two Jamal Lowe goals in the opening stages of their Friday night trip to Barnsley gave them the perfect start at Oakwell, but Wednesday were pegged back by a goal from Vimal Yoganathan and ended up hanging on a little for the win.

The three points lifted them to third place in the table, though as this was a Friday night fixture, they might well not still be there by the end of the weekend, and after the match Challinor had some praise for his team's performance, though he was very clear that they still have a lot of work to do to get the team to the level of the manager's satisfaction.

Speaking to the Sheffield Wednesday media team after his team's win at Barnsley as posted to the social media platfiorm X, Challinor was qualified in praise of his team's performance: "I'm really pleased with the win. I won't say I'm disappointed with our performance, but I think there's loads more to come from us. As I've just said to the players, my job's not to really over-celebrate wins, it's to look at things analytically and make sure we're better, and there's stacks more to come from us."

And it's clear that Challinor wants to see his team playing on the front foot more: "Scoring two goals is the best thing that could happen and probably the worst thing too because, from a player's perspective, we became really passive, too negative, and almost thought the game was won. We have and will continue to take ourselves out of our comfort zone and expose ourselves a little bit. We need to defend miles higher up."

Asked about the performance of Jamal Lowe, Challinor revealed that the forward was disappointed not to receive the Man of the Match Award, given his two goals: "It's nice to score from a set-piece again. Jamal's done another brilliant job for the team in a position that's not his position, but he's got two goals from there, which is great for him, and on another day he gets himself a hat-trick, potentially. Strangely, he hasn't got the Match of the Match from Sky, which he's disappointed about, but we can deal with that because we've got three points."

And he also singled out Liam Cooper for praise: "Coops is a leader for us. With him, his experience and his age, he is going to be important, but as I've told him, I've got to get him to get the rest of them up the pitch and not be defending too deep. I've been there. The last thing you want to be is a centre-half who's not rapid against strikers who want to run in behind, looking behind and having loads of space between your backside and your own goal. But it's going to paramount to what we do and how we go about it. And for him to get through two 90 minutes in the last week is brilliant for us. We've got to recover and make sure we're good to go on Tuesday."

Sheffield Wednesday's 2-1 win at Barnsley continued their uplift under Dave Challinor. Defeat away to League Two Oldham Athletic in the FA Cup was not the result that anyone at Hillsborough was expecting from his first match in charge, but his team has recovered well in the two games since then, with a convincing 3-0 win against Plymouth Argyle in his first home match as manager followed by a welcome derby win away from home at Barnsley.

The win pushed Wednesday up to third place in the League One table, though it's unlikely they'll still be there by the end of the weekend. Fourth-placed Oxford United are at home to Wycombe Wanderers, while fifth-placed Luton Town are away to Burton Albion. Wins for both of them would drop Sheffield Wednesday back to fifth place in the League One table again.

But Dave Challinor is an experienced enough manager to know that league positions don't matter too much at this point of the season. What matters more is the direction of travel that the team are in under their new manager, and two straight wins strongly suggests that Sheffield Wednesday are heading in the right direction under him.

And the reaction of the Sheffield Wednesday fans makes clear that the manager's desire to uphold the highest standards is exactly what they need at the moment too. Wednesday fans had an awful time of things in the Championship last season, and when backs are against the wall in that way, upholding standards on the pitch could come to feel like the last thing on anybody's mind. As such this sort of reaction to a win from the manager might be considered a reflection upon normality returning to Hillsborough, as the team continues to push for a quick return to the second tier.
