Stockport County avoid transfer emergency before Reading FC game after international u-turn

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Stockport County have reached an agreement with England for goalkeeper Ollie Whatmuff to remain at the club, which means he can play against Reading on Saturday.

It has been a positive season for the League One outfit so far, as they currently sit second in the third tier, whilst they came from two behind to beat Cray Wanderers in extra time to book their place in the second round of the FA Cup.

However, that victory came at a cost, as Stockport saw goalkeeper Luca Ashby-Hammond sent off in the first half against the non-league side.

Whilst Ashby-Hammond has been backup to Whatmuff for the majority of the campaign, the on-loan Man City youngster had been called up by England’s under-20's side over the international break.

So, a suspension to Ashby-Hammond left Jimmy McNulty in a very difficult position, as the Stockport chief would be without his two goalkeepers for the game against Reading.

As a result, it was revealed the club would hold talks with Darren Moore about the possibility of Whatmuff not joining up with the Three Lions’ under-20 side, which would have potentially meant they'd have the game postponed due to international call-ups.

Of course, Stockport couldn’t enforce that, but in a fresh development shared by journalist Sam Byrne, it has been confirmed that the 19-year-old has withdrawn from international duty.

If Whatmuff had remained with England, Stockport would’ve been forced to look into the emergency loan market to bring in a player for seven days to play against the Royals.

Obviously, it’s an honour for Whatmuff to be picked to play for England at youth level, and he will no doubt enjoy linking up with the national team.

But, the reality is that playing against Reading in a competitive League One game is going to be better for his development than featuring in games against the U20 sides of Czechia and Portugal.

So, Moore and England deserve credit for putting the needs of Stockport and the player ahead of themselves.

As an ex-EFL manager with a host of clubs, Moore will understand the position that McNulty and Stockport found themselves in, so it’s a call that makes sense.

This is good news for Stockport, and, as outlined above, it’s something that should benefit the player as well.

Now, though, Whatmuff will be focused on the Reading game, and all connected to County will see this as a great opportunity to take advantage in the promotion race.

That’s because a host of Stockport’s rivals at the top aren’t playing, and McNulty’s side know that they can go joint-top with Huddersfield if they pick up three points against Reading.
