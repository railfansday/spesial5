Docker: Director, Developer Success Engineering

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Docker is the platform at the center of how intelligent software gets built. For more than a decade, Docker has helped millions of developers and thousands of organizations build, ship, and run software consistently across any environment. Today, Docker extends that same trust to the AI era. Docker Hardened Images form the foundation agentic code is built on, local and cloud Docker Sandboxes isolate where agents run, and Docker AI Governance sets enterprise-wide policy. Together, they give builders and organizations the confidence to adopt any model, tool, or agent without compromising security, governance, or control.

_______________________________________________________________________

The Director, Developer Success Engineering leads the technical half of Docker's Developer Success team: the senior engineers who build the demos, Labspaces, workshops, tech talks and technical content that show developers what Docker can do. This role gives that team a leader who can go deep on the technology, raise the bar on technical quality, and grow senior engineers in their careers. It owns three outcomes:

Own Docker's technical story for developers. Demos, Labspaces, reference applications and content that make AI Governance, Sandboxes, Docker Hardened Images and the core Docker experience easy to understand and adopt.

Turn technical content into adoption and revenue. Equip customer-facing teams, Captains and partners with enablement that lands and expands accounts, and measure its impact.

Be the developer's voice inside Docker. Bring hands-on feedback from developers, Captains and customers back to Product and Engineering so it shapes what Docker builds.

You will be part of the DevRel team's mission for Docker to become the destination where current and future developers improve their craft, get support and find their community home. You report to the VP of Developer Success (DevRel & Docs) and lead a team of four staff and principal engineers. You work side by side with the Director, Developer Success Programmes, whose team runs the community, Captain, influencer and partner programmes your team's technical work powers.

You'll be successful if you are an engineer at heart who loves building and explaining technology, and who wants to multiply that impact through a team. The FY27 centre of gravity is product adoption and revenue enablement, not awareness metrics alone.

_______________________________________________________________________

Own Docker's technical story for developers

Set the technical content strategy for Developer Success, aligned with Docker's product priorities: agentic development with Sandboxes, AI Governance, Docker Hardened Images and the core Docker experience.

Own the developer-facing product narrative for Captain briefings and launches, so the team delivers most briefings and demos itself rather than relying on ad hoc presenters.

Lead the Labspaces catalogue as Docker's reusable demo and learning format: every strategic demo, briefing, talk and workshop is packaged once and reused across Captains, revenue, partners, events and Docs.

Set technical quality standards for demos, code samples, reference apps and tutorials, including how they are tested, kept current with fast-moving products, and retired.

Partner with Docs on hands-on learning content on docs.docker.com, including the Learn experience.

Turn technical content into adoption and revenue

Scale the technical enablement content for customer-facing teams: tech talks, Docker Days, SDLC demos and workshops, prioritised with Revenue leadership on a regular cadence.

Equip Solutions Engineers, Professional Services, Captains and partners to deliver Docker's story themselves, with kits that cut their demo preparation time.

Track the impact of technical enablement on product adoption, pipeline and expansion ARR, and report it alongside the programmes team.

Bring the team's technical depth to tier-1 events, partner work and the community programmes run by the Developer Success Programmes team.

Build structured feedback loops from developers, Captains and customers into Product and Engineering, including pre-launch reviews of new features.

Test new products early, report friction in the developer experience, and follow it through to fixes.

Represent Docker credibly in technical forums: conference talks, open source communities, technical blogs and developer communities.

Lead and grow a team of senior developer advocates and engineers: set clear goals, give technical and career coaching, and build growth paths for senior individual contributors.

Balance the team's capacity between strategic content, revenue enablement and inbound requests through a clear intake and prioritisation process.

Hire to fill capability gaps as Docker's product portfolio grows.

A software engineering background, with deep hands-on knowledge of containers, Docker and modern developer workflows. You can build and debug a demo yourself.

Working knowledge of AI and agentic development (LLMs, agents, MCP) and of software supply chain security. Experience in either is a strong plus.

Experience leading a team of senior engineers, developer advocates or solutions engineers, and a track record of growing senior individual contributors.

A strong technical public presence: conference talks, workshops, technical writing or open source contributions.

Experience building technical enablement for sales, solutions or customer success teams, and tying it to adoption or revenue outcomes.

Ability to build trusted relationships with Product and Engineering leaders, and to influence roadmaps with evidence from developers.

Analytical and data-driven in how you prioritise and measure technical content and programmes.

Able to coordinate across many teams and perform in a fast-moving environment; self-directed, self-driven and able to prioritise strongly.

Strong verbal and written English communication skills.

Get to know each team member, their technical focus areas, current work and career goals.

Get hands-on with Docker's products, especially Sandboxes, AI Governance and Docker Hardened Images, and run the team's top Labspaces and demos yourself.

Meet key stakeholders in Product, Engineering, Revenue, Professional Services, Docs and the Developer Success Programmes team, and learn what they need from technical Developer Success.

Live the Docker culture: humility, developer obsession, open collaboration and a bias for considered action.

Set the team's technical content and enablement roadmap for the next two quarters, tied to product priorities and launch cadence.

Put in place an intake and prioritisation process with Revenue and Product so the team's capacity goes to the highest-impact work.

Agree the measures that link the team's work to adoption and revenue, such as Labspace usage by account and ARR influenced by tech talks.

Become a trusted technical partner to Product, Engineering and marketing leadership.

Deliver a consistent, current catalogue of demos, Labspaces and talks that tells Docker's story across AI, security and developer productivity.

Show a measurable link from technical enablement to product adoption and expansion revenue.

Establish a regular feedback channel from developers and Captains that visibly influences Docker's product roadmap.

Grow a team of senior engineers and advocates with clear career paths, recognised internally and externally for technical excellence.

Help Docker succeed in its vision to increase the time developers spend on innovation and decrease the time they spend on everything else.
