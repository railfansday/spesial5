Nicole Kidman’s Long ‘Almond Cream’ Nails Are the Sophisticated Go-With-Everything Trend to Try Now: Get the Look

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

This manicure proves long almond nails never go out of style.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

There are certain manicures that never go out of style, regardless of the season. Case in point: Nicole Kidman’s long ‘almond cream’ nails. Spotted at the LACMA Art+Film Gala wearing softly tapered almond nails in a glossy milky shade, the actor’s manicure served as a reminder that winter manicures needn’t be dark or glittery to make an impact.

“Kidman’s nails are a great example of how to wear a longer length without them looking too dramatic,” says Susan Carroll, founder of Young LDN. “The almond shape is one of my favorites because it’s incredibly flattering on the hands. The sides taper in slightly before finishing in a soft, rounded point, which helps elongate the fingers and gives a much more delicate appearance than a square or coffin shape.”

Serving as an ideal entry point for anyone looking to experiment with longer nails without committing to a dramatic stiletto shape, the almond silhouette offers a more wearable alternative. Carroll also recommends adapting the length to suit your lifestyle and comfort level. The appeal lies in its versatility, as it can be easily achieved without the need for extensions. “The shape does a lot of the work, and keeping the color subtle makes the overall look feel much more elegant,” she adds.

Re-creating the look is very straightforward, as the key lies in the shape. Ask your nail technician for a soft almond silhouette with rounded tips, rather than anything too sharply pointed. “The key is making sure the nails are evenly shaped and the cuticles are tidy, as there’s nowhere to hide imperfections with such a simple manicure,” says Carroll. To finish, simply apply two coats of a sheer milky beige polish before sealing with a glossy top coat for a light-reflecting finish.

The result is an effortlessly elegant manicure that feels equal parts timeless and wearable.
