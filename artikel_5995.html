The Great Chinese Heist

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The scale of China’s theft of U.S. intellectual property over the decades is almost unfathomable, and it’s ongoing.

There have been surprises in the Nvidia saga — not least the shocking return of Jensen Huang — but what’s unremarkable is that China was using underhanded means to acquire U.S. technology.

This a core competency of the Chinese state.
