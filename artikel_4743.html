Our Favorite On Shoes Are Up to 37% Off for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Score stylish sneakers for less before they sell out.

If you’ve ever found yourself doing a double take at someone’s sneakers on the street, there’s a good chance they were from On. The Swiss sneaker brand has become something of a fashion-world fixture, and for good reason: Its signature CloudTec cushioning makes its shoes comfortable for logging miles and stylish enough to wear every day. As a Women’s Health style writer who’s tested my fair share of comfortable sneakers over the years, I can attest that On has earned its spot in my regular rotation. The only downside? These popular kicks don't exactly come cheap.

Most On sneakers typically cost between $150 and $200, which makes finding them on sale all the more exciting. Enter Black Friday. While the brand isn't slashing prices across its entire website, its Last Season section is offering some pretty impressive markdowns on select styles. We're talking nearly 40% off a few popular pairs, including the Cloudsurfer Next, which clocks in at under $100 after its discount. Plus, markdowns on everyday favorites like the Cloud 6 (31% off) and Cloud X4 (31% off)—a top pick that recently made our list of the best On shoes for walking. That's a pretty compelling reason to finally pull the trigger on the pair you've been eyeing (or replace the ones you've practically worn into the ground).

Ahead, I've rounded up the best On sneaker deals worth shopping right now. Whether you're looking for a comfortable everyday sneaker, a versatile workout shoe, or something that can handle a little rain, there's a good chance you'll find a pair worth adding to your cart. Just keep in mind that Last Season inventory can be limited, so I'd recommend shopping sooner rather than later if your size is available.
