Should you defuse or provoke Mandy in GTA 6?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

What the best dialogue option to pick in the "Pipework" quest

What the best dialogue option to pick in the "Pipework" quest

When you meet Mandy in Grand Theft Auto 6 for the first time, you can pick between the "defuse" and "provoke" dialogue options to determine how Jason is going to approach her during the conversation. This encounter takes place during the "Pipework" quest in Chapter 1. Jason is doing some jobs for Brian, who asked him to collect the rent from a couple of tenants. Mandy is one of them, but her condition for paying the rent is that her sink be fixed, and she specifically asked for Jason to come and help her.

If you're curious about what is the best dialogue option to pick in this scene, we've got you covered. Here's a rundown on whether you should defuse or provoke Mandy in GTA 6.

When talking to Mandy, the game gives you the option to either provoke or defuse. Both options are valid, as they lead to the same outcome: Mandy locks herself in a room while telling Jason to leave. Although Mandy implies that she has feelings towards Jason, this encounter is not connected to the game's romance system. Jason will not have sex with Mandy, no matter which option you pick. However, depending on the dialogue option you pick, the scene will play out differently, letting you see distinct reactions from Mandy.

This whole situation happens after Jason fixes Mandy's sink and removes whatever was obstructing the pipe. She offers him some cigars as a reward for helping her. However, the conversation escalates as she asks Jason if he doesn't like her. She goes out of her house due to some confusion involving her neighbor and throws the cigars on the ground.

You can leave after that, as you have already completed the quest by this point, but you will miss the chance to talk to Mandy. Instead of leaving, return to Mandy's house to find her sitting in the living room (but don't forget to pick up the cigars from the ground). Press L2/LT and the "provoke" and "defuse" options will appear.

If you choose to calm her down, Mandy will ask Jason to stop his mind games, while telling him that they make him more attractive. On the other hand, if you decide to push her buttons, then she will basically threaten Jason. Regardless of which option you choose, eventually Mandy moves to her room and lock herself inside.

Maybe that's for the best. Afterall, if you investigate outside of Mandy's house and the inside of her trunk, you may find some interesting items that point to Mandy being... well, unpredictable.
