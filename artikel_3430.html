Wizard Of Oz Gets A Mad Max Twist In Dark New Adaptation Releasing In 3 Months

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

David Pepose’s comic book The O.Z. has found a home with Boom! Studios, and he explains what the series is about and how the idea came to be.

David Pepose is one of the most versatile writers in comics. Readers can see him bringing Layla El-Faouly from the MCU's Moon Knight to Marvel Comics and writing the amazing Cable: Love and Chrome. Then, he’ll turn around and give horror fans an amazing comic book like Mad Cave Studios' The Devil That Wears My Face. Among the amazing comics David Pepose has written is a bold reimagining of the classic book and movie, The Wizard of Oz, The O.Z.

The O.Z. began its push on Kickstarter, but was recently picked up by Boom! Studios. In a recent interview ScreenRant was fortunate enough to have with Pepose, he talks about what the story is about, the way it came to be, and more.

The O.Z. is one of the most creative changes to the classic book and movie. When asked about what the story is about, Pepose goes into great detail about it and why it's different from other Wizard of Oz tales.

David Pepose: THE O.Z. is like Mad Max meets The Wizard of Oz — it’s the story of Dorothy Gale’s granddaughter and namesake, who is an Iraq war veteran grappling with PTSD and disillusionment. But when our new Dorothy is caught up in a tornado, she finds herself stranded in the land of Oz — where she learns her grandmother’s adventures plunged the country into a brutal civil war. Now Dorothy must team up with the Tin Soldier, the Prince of Lions, and Jack Pumpkinhead in order to find the Silver Slippers and bring peace to the Occupied Zone… or as the locals call it, The O.Z.

Many people have brought different versions of The Wizard of Oz to various genres, but Pepose's has been the most adventurous. Considering the source material, that says a lot. When asked about that, he said, "I’ve always been a fan of mashing up different genres, so doing a fantasy war story was something I’d wanted to do early in my career." However, how he landed on The Wizard of Oz is a funny story.

Pepose: Landing on The Wizard of Oz came across as kind of a happy accident — I was typing different fantasy staples to see if anything could serve as inspiration, and my cursor froze in the middle of the word “Oz.” That had me thinking about the word Oz as an acronym, which turned into “Occupied Zone” — and that moment instantly crystallized the entire narrative for me.

When asked why he went this particular route with Dorothy, Pepose gives an interesting reason.

Pepose: Back in my days as a newspaper reporter, I’d interviewed a ton of returning veterans about their experiences coming back home, and those stories had always stuck with me — and that became the basis for Dorothy’s story in THE O.Z.

Now that The O.Z. has been picked up by Boom! Studios, the next question is whether or not the series will continue. Pepose seems hopeful that this won’t be the last comic book fans will see of the characters and this alternate reality Wizard of Oz story. However, there are some factors to consider.

Pepose: Never say never! Whether we do a sequel storyline will ultimately be determined by demand and me and Ruben Rojas’ respective schedules, but I do have a few ideas in my back pocket, which I’d actually pitched to BOOM!’s Editor-in-Chief Andy Schmidt and our O.Z. editor Gregory Hopkins. So there’s definitely room to return to the Occupied Zone if enough people demand it.

The O.Z. #1 is scheduled to be released in February 2027 by Boom! Studios.
