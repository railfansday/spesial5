‘Spider-Man: Brand New Day’ Officially Returns to Theaters With New Footage

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After years of waiting, a creative overhaul, and massive casting reveals, only one month remains until Doomsday falls upon the MCU. The latest big Avengers crossover flick has Disney's full attention right now, with new teasers rolling out, merch collections, and even an appearance by Robert Downey Jr.'s Victor Von Doom during a special World Series sneak peek. Even the end of VisionQuest, the capper of the WandaVision trilogy, set the stage for the supervillain's takeover as he appears to bring the Scarlet Witch (Elizabeth Olsen) under his control. Before that, though, a certain wall-crawler is about to steal the spotlight once more.

Spider-Man is officially swinging back into theaters with a brand new Brand New Day. After it was reported back in September that Sony and Disney were working on a special re-release of Tom Holland's latest web-slinging outing, the date is now officially set. Titled Spider-Man: [Brand New] Brand New Day, this new edition of the superhero megahit will launch on December 4 exclusively on the big screen, complete with a few surprises that weren't in the original cut. Most notably, it features the return of Rosario Dawson as Claire Temple, aka the Night Nurse, after her scene was initially dropped from the film.

For now, any other new additions to Destin Daniel Cretton's record-breaker are being kept as surprises for those who go to see the revamped cut. More cameos are expected as part of [Brand New] Brand New Day's extra footage, perhaps in a similar vein to those seen in Spidey's early crime-fighting spree, including Tarantula, Boomerang, and the live-action MCU debut of Marvin Jones III's Tombstone. Dawson's inclusion was the only publicly known cut, after the actress spoke out about shooting for the blockbuster. This will mark her big-screen debut as Temple, having previously appeared as the go-to nurse of Marvel's street-level vigilantes in Daredevil, Iron Fist, Luke Cage, and The Defenders.

Although it's a quick turnaround for a rerelease, Brand New Day is a different beast. The return of Holland five years after the emotional ending of Spider-Man: No Way Home blew past expectations at the box office, becoming the highest-grossing film domestically in U.S. history with $959 million and the third-highest-grossing film at the worldwide box office with a grand total of $2.52 billion. Its new cut will now get to capitalize on a relatively soft post-Thanksgiving weekend and further cement its status as one of the biggest and most beloved depictions of Peter Parker to date.

Holland was joined by fellow returning stars Zendaya and Jacob Batalon in the blockbuster, with Mark Ruffalo reprising his role as Bruce Banner, Florence Pugh making a brief appearance as Yelena Belova, and Jon Bernthal making his feature debut as The Punisher. Alongside them were a wealth of newcomers, including Trammell Tillman, Liza Colón-Zayas, and, in a big sign for the MCU's future, Sadie Sink as the mutant Jean Gray. She's slated to return in 2028 with Jake Schreier's much-anticipated X-Men movie, which has since filled out its cast with some massive names. Before that, though, viewers will see both her and Holland in Doomsday's follow-up, Avengers: Secret Wars, hitting theaters next December.

Tickets will go on sale for Spider-Man: [Brand New] Brand New Day on November 19 before it hits theaters on December 4. Stay tuned here at Collider for more information on the re-release until then.
