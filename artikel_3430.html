Pokémon Gives Glaceon An Official New Look Ahead Of Winds & Waves Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Pokémon Winds and Waves is releasing sometime in 2027, although a specific release date hasn't been officially confirmed by Nintendo or Game Freak yet. Since the first trailer for the Gen 10 games debuted earlier this year, fans have understandably been pretty hyped about what could be awaiting them in Winds and Waves' new region. Official details from Pokémon Day 2026 certainly haven't stopped additional rumors and alleged leaks from surfacing online either, with many fans hoping to see a new Eevee evolution for Gen 10.

While most of us eagerly await more information regarding Pokémon Winds and Waves, and particularly the release date of these games, 2026 has still been a great year for Pokémon fans in general. Thanks to the franchise's 30th anniversary, we've seen a number of re-releases (like FireRed and LeafGreen on Switch and Switch 2) as well as brand-new merchandise like Adidas sneakers, a Stern pinball machine, Santa Cruz skateboard decks, and McDonald's Happy Meal toys. And because Eeveelutions are so iconic, many of them have been a part of these festivities too.

This includes Glaceon, the Ice-type Pokémon that debuted in the Gen 4 games. Like the other Eevee evolutions, or Eeveelutions, Glaceon has gotten its fair share of appearances in games and the anime. And it's also been featured on everything from Build-A-Bear stuffed animals to sweaters and action figures. And now, Pokémon has officially given Glaceon a bold new look that's just in time for the holidays thanks to the Glaceon Pokémon Undersea Holiday Plush from Pokémon Center.

The Glaceon Pokémon Undersea Holiday Plush is priced at $19.99, and Pokémon Center offers free shipping on orders of $20 or more. The plush sits at 8 1/4 inches tall and is 8 inches long. The new design shows Glaceon in a snowflake-covered cape with a red-and-green bow tie featuring a Poké Ball-themed emblem. Some holiday ornaments, which are sewn together with the plush, are stacked at Glaceon's feet.

The Glaceon plush is a part of the Pokémon Undersea Holiday collection, which is unique to the official Pokémon Center online storefront. You can see an image of the Glaceon Pokémon Undersea Holiday Plush below.

With the holidays quickly approaching, this Glaceon could make a great gift for yourself or any Pokémon fan in your life. And with Pokémon Winds and Waves arriving next year, the timing is also pretty perfect. Glaceon will undoubtedly be included in the Gen 10 games, and while Glaceon may not wear a holiday outfit like this one, fans of the games and Eeveelutions may still want to grab this plush while it's available.

Right now, Pokémon Day 2027, which is February 27, is expected to be the next time we see a trailer for Pokémon Winds and Waves. The games have a rumored release window for November 2027, which makes sense, considering most mainline Pokémon games are released during that month. In the meantime, it's great to see official merch like this Glaceon Undersea Holiday Plush, which can be a nice way to show off your love for the franchise during the holiday season.
