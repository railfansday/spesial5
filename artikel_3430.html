Diablo: Legacy Of Terror Officially Announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Diablo franchise has been around for a long time and continues to grow and evolve to give its players a fresh experience in the classic dungeon-crawling genre. Despite Diablo 4 being out and the announcement of Diablo 5 in development, the 2000 game Diablo 2 received the remaster treatment with Diablo 2: Resurrected​​​​​​ in 2021, and its players are now getting even more as part of Diablo's 30th anniversary celebration.

Starting on November 13, 2026, and running for only six days, Diablo 2: Resurrected will host the Legacy of Terror event to celebrate the 30th anniversary of the series. This comes only months after the popular Warlock class was added to the game, as well as several quality-of-life updates that prove that this game is alive and well. In fact, it has a player base that could easily rival that of the more recent Diablo 4 title, particularly as Diablo 2 has often been heralded as the best entry in the series.

Diablo 2: Resurrected - Legacy of Terror event launches Season 16 of the competitive, seasonal Ladder system experience. However, this special anniversary event is available for both Ladder and Non-Ladder players.

The event brings numerous perks for players and unique gameplay experiences. A quick breakdown of the information you need to know about the Legacy of Terror event is as follows:

On the Hell difficulty, players will have the chance to face off against the Diablo Clone once every six hours. This Uber Diablo is a more challenging variant of Diablo from Diablo 2.

After players defeat Baal in Normal, Nightmare, or Hell difficulty, that difficulty will then feature simultaneous Terrorized Zones in every zone. Terrorized Zones are an endgame feature where random areas will become high-level hunting grounds, offering better loot and a chaotic experience.

The Terrorized Zones can be disabled in the Game Creation menu if you choose, and turning it off will not affect any other features of the Legacy of Terror ​​​​​​event.

The Cow Level has always been one of the most ridiculous and popular features of Diablo 2, and Blizzard is leaning into the experience for this anniversary celebration. During the Legacy of Terror week, players can enter the Cow Portal in the Rogue Encampment any time they want after defeating Baal once on a difficulty level.

The Cow Portal will then remain active at all times during the event. Additionally, after slaying monsters, you have a rare chance to reanimate them as Hell Bovine Minions.

As if all that wasn't enough, during the Diablo 2: Resurrected - Legacy of Terror event, players can also enjoy 100% Magic Find and a 15% discount on all vendors, including Gamblers, on every difficulty level.

Although it would be nice to get even more classes since the addition of the Warlock earlier this year, for now, having this special event to enjoy is exciting and sure to bring many players back into the game.

On a side note, someone at Blizzard is clearly a Dungeon Crawler Carl fan, as the official statement regarding the Diablo 2: Resurrected Legacy of Terror event ends with the line, "Now get out there and slay, slay, slay!" which I, for one, absolutely love.
