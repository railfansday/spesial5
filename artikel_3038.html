This 8%-Yielding Dividend Stock Pays You 16 Times a Year. Here’s What a $25,000 Investment Deposits Each Month.

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Main Street Capital is an income-generating machine.

Most dividend stocks pay you four times a year. Main Street Capital (MAIN +0.03%) pays you four times as often. The business development company (BDC) pays a regular monthly dividend around the 15th of each month and a supplemental quarterly dividend near the last day of each quarter, for a total of 16 payments each year.

Here's a look at how much dividend income you'd collect each month if you invested $25,000 in the high-yielding dividend stock.

Main Street Capital pays a regular monthly dividend of $0.27 per share ($3.24 annually). At its current share price of around $56, the BDC has a 5.8% yield based solely on its regular dividend. The company has never cut or suspended its regular monthly dividend since its 2007 IPO. It has raised its monthly payment by 3.8% over the past year and by 145% since coming public, including 13 increases since the fourth quarter of 2021.

Additionally, the BDC pays a supplemental dividend of $0.30 per share each quarter. The supplemental dividend brings its annualized dividend to $4.44 per share, pushing its yield to nearly 8%. The company has declared a supplemental dividend for 21 consecutive quarters, paying the current rate since early 2024. However, it has reduced and suspended this payment in the past, so it's not as bankable as the monthly dividend.

Based on its recent share price, a $25,000 investment would purchase about 446 shares. This investment would generate $120.53 per month in dividend income (roughly $1,446 per year). Additionally, investors would receive another $133.93 in quarterly supplemental dividends (about $535 per year). That works out to around $165 in dividend income each month.

That base income stream should steadily rise. Meanwhile, the BDC should continue to pay meaningful supplemental dividends, as long as market conditions remain healthy. Those dual income streams make Main Street Capital an attractive passive income investment.
