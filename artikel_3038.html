Crypto Market Today, Nov. 19: Bitcoin Approaches $60,000 as ETF Outflows Surpass $2.5 Billion in 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Investors are continuing to reduce their exposure to digital assets as the cryptocurrency bear market drags on.

By RJ Fulton – Nov 19, 2026 at 5:25PM EST

Cryptocurrency markets extended their losses on Thursday, November 19, as a broad-based sell-off on Wall Street, mounting concerns over further Federal Reserve interest rate hikes, and weakening ETF demand continued to weigh on digital assets.

As of early evening, Bitcoin (BTC -1.18%) was down 2.15% to $61,635.18, moving dangerously close to the psychologically important $60,000 level. Meanwhile, Ethereum (ETH -0.59%) fell 1.64% to $1,745.57, and Solana (SOL -0.46%) declined 1.56% to $72.47.

Pressure on cryptocurrency markets continued on Thursday as yesterday's Federal Reserve meeting minutes reinforced expectations of further interest rate hikes. With inflation remaining stubbornly elevated, the prospect of tighter monetary policy is weighing heavily on speculative assets and diminishing expectations for improved liquidity conditions heading into 2027.

These concerns were compounded by a sharp sell-off on Wall Street, with all three major stock market indexes declining by at least 2%. Gold also fell more than 2%, highlighting the extent of selling pressure across asset classes.

Spot Bitcoin ETFs remained under pressure on Thursday, with the iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF (IBIT -0.79%) falling 0.79% and the Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC -0.83%) declining 0.83%. Meanwhile, the iShares Ethereum Trust ETF (ETHA +0.40%) bucked the trend, gaining 0.40%.

According to CoinDesk, U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded more than $300 million in net outflows on Wednesday, bringing cumulative net outflows for 2026 to more than $2.5 billion. Total assets held by these funds have also fallen to a three-month low.

Perhaps the biggest challenge facing cryptocurrency markets is the absence of sustained buying pressure. Both Bitcoin and Ethereum continue to experience substantial ETF outflows, while Solana's recent return to positive ETF flows has yet to establish a meaningful trend. The persistence of these outflows highlights deteriorating investor demand and the challenges facing the cryptocurrency market as it attempts to establish a bottom.

With Bitcoin approaching $60,000, investors should be particularly mindful of how the market responds at this level. A decisive break below $60,000 could put additional pressure on the broader cryptocurrency market, particularly as Ethereum and Solana are already struggling to regain lost ground.

Looking ahead, the prospect of further interest rate hikes and mounting weakness in equity markets make it increasingly difficult to identify a convincing catalyst for a cryptocurrency recovery before year-end. Rather than attempting to anticipate a bottom, investors should look for evidence that capital is returning to the market, selling pressure is subsiding, and major cryptocurrencies are beginning to reclaim key price levels.
