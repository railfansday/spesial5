Report: Apple accounted for 57% of US smartphone shipments in Q3

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Apple’s share of US smartphone shipments jumped from 50% to 57% year over year last quarter, with the company being the only major smartphone maker to buck the broader trend of declining shipments. Here are the details.

Yesterday, we covered a Counterpoint Research report on Q3 Latin American smartphone shipments, which showed Apple recording the smallest year-over-year decline among the region’s top five smartphone makers. As a result, Apple increased its share of regional shipments from 7% to 9%.

The report echoed the findings of several others published throughout this year, which showed how Apple has managed to navigate component shortages and rising costs better than many of its competitors, some of which have had to raise smartphone prices multiple times.

Apple, by contrast, kept iPhone pricing largely stable for much of the year, holding off on broader price increases until the launch of the iPhone 18 Pro lineup in September.

Today, Counterpoint Research released its report on US smartphone shipments for Q3, which showed that Apple increased its share from 50% to 57% year over year, amid a 9% decline in overall shipments.

According to a separate Counterpoint report published last Thursday, Apple was the only major smartphone maker to avoid a year-over-year decline in shipment volume during the quarter.

In today’s report, Counterpoint notes that rising memory costs hit the low-end Android segment particularly hard during the quarter. Samsung was the only major Android smartphone maker to increase its shipment share, seeing a one-percentage-point bump to 25%, “helped by a portfolio spanning flagships and the low-end Galaxy A-series.”

Carriers also felt the impact of the broader downturn, with smartphone sales across the three major US carriers collectively falling 4% year over year, per the report.

Looking ahead, Counterpoint expects memory costs to remain elevated and US carriers to be more selective with their holiday promotions, with smartphone sales expected to remain weak in Q4 despite it typically being the strongest quarter of the year.
