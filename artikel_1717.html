Amorim rules out Inacio reunion at Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

AC Milan boss Ruben Amorim has ruled out the potential signing of Sporting CP defender Goncalo Inacio in January.

Amorim coached Inacio during his tenure at Sporting from March 2020 to November 2024, with the defender progressing through the club's youth ranks.

Inacio played 123 Primeira Liga matches under Amorim's tutelage, winning the league title in 2020-21 and in 2023-24, though the Portuguese coach left midway through the latter victory to join Manchester United.

Among Sporting defenders during Amorim's reign, Inacio ranked second for tackles won (94), interceptions (140), duels won (460) and aerial duels won (219) in the league, coming in behind Sebastian Coates in all four categories.

Sporting are third in the Primeira Liga this season, but have won three of their four Champions League games (L1).

Inacio has been linked with a move to Milan in January to reunite with Amorim, but the latter has denied the rumours.

"No, that is not true. Goncalo will not arrive," Amorim told Sport TV, as quoted by O Jogo.

"He is a great player, I had the pleasure of working with him. We have [Strahinja] Pavlovic and [Sankhoun] Diawara, who is a young man who came from the French second division but is performing well.

"We have to move forward and think about other players for other positions."

Milan are fourth in Serie A, five points behind leaders Lazio, following their 5-1 thumping of Frosinone on Sunday.

In the Europa League, however, Milan have won just one of their four league phase games (L3) ahead of facing Olympiacos on Thursday.
