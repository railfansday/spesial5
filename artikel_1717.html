Opinion: Real Madrid announce deal and now the door is open for incredible Liverpool move

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The door is undoubtedly open for Liverpool to strike an incredible move. Real Madrid opened that door with a deal announcement.

Liverpool need to boost their midfield. That's apparent to all, even after striking two big-money contract deals with Ryan Gravenberch and Dominik Szoboszlai this year.

But do either actually get into Liverpool's first-XI right now? Virtually everyone would pick Trey Nyoni and Alexis Mac Allister at the pairing and that absolutely was not the plan for the campaign.

Nyoni is an interesting one, though. He's proven that Liverpool look better with a proper defensive, controlling midfielder in there - something most people figured would be the case before the summer.

Liverpool, however, did not believe that. They didn't sign any midfielders and instead sold Curtis Jones without replacing him, leaving the squad short and over-reliant on attack-first options in the middle.

Nyoni has shown that Liverpool were wrong. Of course, it looks like they'd already figured that out by the end of August, with moves for midfielders quickly heading to the top of their agenda for 2027 after just a few games this season.

And we don't expect Liverpool to rely so heavily on Nyoni for the entirety of the campaign. He does need some degree of help and the midfield could use a reshuffle.

Put it all together and a move for Eduardo Camavinga looks like it should be high-priority for Liverpool. The Frenchman is one of the best defensive midfielders on the planet, even if Real Madrid don't quite rely on him all that much.

Our Value Adder model suggests Camavinga is very, very underrated and the reason for that is largely Aurelien Tchouameni. Tchouameni might be the world's best defensive midfielder, honestly, and casts an enormous shadow over his compatriot.

That's why Camavinga hasn't entirely established himself at Real Madrid and why certain clubs were pushing to sign him in the summer. He decided to stay put, however, and we imagine that was to try and force his way in under Jose Mourinho.

That hasn't happened. Camavinga has only started two La Liga fixtures and reports suggest they hold interest in signing Martin Zubimendi. That's a signing that would usurp Camavinga.

More than that, Real Madrid have doubled-down on Tchouameni, committing to a big new contract this week. There's essentially no way for Camavinga to get into that starting XI regularly - and the new contract confirms that.

Which is why we feel the door is now open pretty wide for Liverpool. Camavinga is a fantastic, high-energy player who could be exactly what the Reds need to improve their midfield. The opportunity looks to be there and they should try to take it.
