Mikel Arteta provides fresh David Raya injury update ahead of Arsenal vs Borussia Dortmund

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Mikel Arteta has ruled David Raya out of Tuesday’s Champions League game against Borussia Dortmund but delivered an upbeat bulletin on the goalkeeper’s health.

The Arsenal star sustained an injury during the second half of Saturday’s 2-2 draw against Newcastle.

While it was clear that the Spain international was in significant discomfort, Arteta was unable to clarify the extent Raya’s injury in the immediate aftermath of the game.

The former Brentford shot stopper was reportedly able to drive home once he and his teammates had arrived back from the north east on Saturday evening, but he was absent from Monday’s opening training session.

Asked for an update on the health of his No.1 who underwent MRI scans yesterday, Arteta said: ‘He’s not fit for tomorrow unfortunately but it doesn’t look too serious and we need more tests tomorrow but it’s quite encouraging with the pain that he was showing immediately after the action.

‘We always assess every situation to make the right call,’ said Arteta when asked who will be selected to start in Raya’s absence.

‘It is quite clear what we are going to do.’

He added: ‘We know and we have seen what David brings to the team. That’s why we are well protected with Kepa and Illan.
