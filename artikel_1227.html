UECL | Borac 2-3 Atalanta: Another amazing Dea comeback against 9 men

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Atalanta wasted so many chances in Bosnia, but after Borac went down to nine men, completed another incredible comeback to win 3-2 with Nikola Krstovic, Sead Kolasinac and Davide Zappacosta.

La Dea needed to get back on their feet, as after beating Pafos in the opener, they lost to Ajax and were held 1-1 by Kairat Almaty. Marco Carnesecchi joined Isak Hien, Odilon Kossounou and Thomas Pompei on the absentee list, so Marco Sportiello took the gloves. FK Borac had one win and two defeats in the Conference League, but Srdan Grahovac and Stefan Savic were injured.

Giacomo Raspadori’s low drive forced a tricky save in the first three minutes, while Lorenzo Bernasconi did just enough to prevent Luka Juricic getting on the end of a cross from the left.

Raspadori turned his angled drive across the face of goal after Jonathan Rowe won the ball back in midfield, but Juricic failed to make the most of a Mario Pasalic failed defensive header.

Atalanta continued to waste chances, Pasalic this time skimming the upright from a tight angle, then Raspadori too slow to get on the end of an Ederson roll across.

They were made to pay for it, as Borac took the lead when a low cross from the left was flicked on by Omar Sijaric for Juricic, who was able to control with his back to goal, turn Sead Kolasinac and sweep in from eight yards.

Just as La Dea were pushing forward, they were caught out on the counter again down the same flank, this time the Juricic cross was flicked in at the near post by Matej Deket.

Raspadori’s effort inside the area was charged down by Branimir Cipetic, while the goalkeeper got a glove to the Nicola Zalewski cross-shot and Pasalic was unable to turn in the rebound after the restart.

Atalanta’s finishing remained a huge problem, as Pasalic was sent through by Rowe only to blast over from seven yards. Incredibly, when Mladen Jurkas was off his line after misplacing the pass out from a corner, Rowe still managed to fire wide of the open goal.

David Vukovic threatened a third for Borac, his volley going wide from the edge of the D on a cleared corner, and the Sandi Ogrinec header clipped the top of the crossbar on 67 minutes.

Charles De Ketelaere was almost as bad with his finishing, somehow turning over from close range on the rebound from the save on Pasalic.

Borac went down to 10 men after Abel Pascual hauled down Rowe just outside the area for an inevitable red card. On the resulting free kick, Rowe found himself clear on goal at the near post, but fired straight at the goalkeeper’s face from point-blank range.

Davide Zappacosta saw a scorcher deflected over for a corner, and Atalanta finally got one back when Raoul Bellanova floated a cross to the back post for the Krstovic header.

Moments later, Borac went down to nine men, as Sebastijan Herrera received a straight red card for his wild tackle from behind on Zalewski.

On the resulting Zalewski free kick, it was deflected onto the upright by a Borac defender, but Sead Kolasinac was ready to bundle the rebound over the line and draw 2-2.

Just days after coming back from 4-2 down to hold Inter 4-2 in Serie A, Atalanta did it again by completing the turnaround from 2-0 down to win 3-2 in Bosnia. Davide Zappacosta cut inside from the left and blasted right-footed into the far bottom corner in added time.

Juricic 22 (B), Deket 30 (B), Krstovic 86 (A), Kolasinac 90 (A), Zappacosta 93 (A)
