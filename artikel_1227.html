Gary Neville tears into Man United's Matthijs de Ligt for 'unforgivable' defending that led to Leny Yoro's red card - and claims duo played like 'they have never had basic defensive training'

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Published: 12:23 EST, 22 November 2026 | Updated: 05:04 EST, 23 November 2026

Gary Neville led the criticism of Manchester United's defending in the opening minutes against Liverpool as Mauricio Pochettino's reign started in disastrous fashion at Anfield.

After just eight minutes, his plans were thrown into tatters when errors from Matthijs de Ligt and Leny Yoro saw the latter sent off before Liverpool took a swift lead.

A long ball from Ronald Araujo should have been dealt with easily by De Ligt, but the Dutchman found himself in no man's land, left the ball, and Alexander Isak raced clear.

That left Yoro scrambling to try to recover the situation for his side, but Isak stepped across him and he ended up fouling the Reds forward.

Referee Sam Barrott wasted no time in pulling out the red card, and United found themselves reduced to 10 men just minutes into a vital clash.

And former United defender Neville didn't hold back when it came to his analysis as he tore into the two players at fault.

Leny Yoro is SENT OFF after eight minutes! 😱 pic.twitter.com/VKYvckvBBP

Gary Neville torn into Manchester United duo Leny Yoro (right) and Matthijs de Ligt after the former was sent off

Neville (pictured) questioned why the duo were playing as if they had 'never had basic training of how to play in defence'

'When I saw De Ligt's name on the team sheet I have to admit I was absolutely stunned,' he said, speaking on Sky Sports. 'And the reason I was stunned was because of this. If you'd watched him in Como four weeks ago he was very much well off it and that is just a basic ball over the top.

'It's his problem to start with, Yoro can be further across, but De Ligt is miles away from it, then Yoro has to get further round, he reacts late, his anticipation is poor. It's so clumsy from Yoro, he makes a lot of these types of mistakes.

'You could argue Isak delays to make sure Yoro runs into him, but it's naivety.'

Pochettino was left scrambling to think of ideas to tighten up the defence but, before he could bring Harry Maguire on, it was 2-0 through Florian Wirtz.

Maguire eventually came on but it felt like too little, too late.

'We saw him in Como and he has been out for a year,' Neville continued on De Ligt. 'It is really tough to come back. To have him out there in a game like this, he has been exposed. Yoro's positioning is poor, he has to be across, you have to cover your mate. It is bad all around from United.

'Michael Carrick will be watching and say that's what I was having to deal with.'

And his criticism didn't stop there. After having the chance to re-watch the incident, Neville's criticism grew even stronger.

De Ligt (left) failed to deal with a long ball as Alexander Isak (right) raced away before the foul

Former United captain Roy Keane (pictured) added that he was 'amazed' by the errors made

'It's as bad defending from two centre backs defending a long ball as you will see,' the former defender, who likened a return from a year out to playing after six pints of alcohol, added. 'De Ligt is well out of it - he's been out so I have some sympathy. But between them, to let the ball beat you, it's unforgivable.

'It is shocking. Whilst Man United have those types of defenders in their team, they have no chance of keeping a clean sheet.

'It's basic understanding of how to play a position. It's the fundamentals. It's like these guys have never had basic training of how to play in defence.'

Former United captain Roy Keane chimed in too, adding at half-time: 'It doesn't matter if it's a back four or back five, you do the basics right.

'I'm amazed at this level, I know he's been out a while, but it's really poor defending. He's never getting that ball.

'If you're a defender you always expect the worst. Isak's done well and I actually don't think he's going to get the ball.

'There's no way he's (Yoro) getting the ball, it's clumsy by United. Straight ball over the top and he's in big trouble. But there's no way he's getting that ball. I know he's quick and sharp but it's really poor defending. Players not smelling danger.'
