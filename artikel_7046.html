The Gilded Age Is Back—Here’s Everything to Know About Season 4

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Season four has arrived with familiar faces, new players, and plenty at stake.

With dazzling Newport mansions, an ever-changing social hierarchy, and even a John Singer Sargent appearance, HBO’s The Gilded Age has lasting popularity. It’s a series that knows its strengths—eye candy and society drama—and continues to lean in. The third season, which ended on August 10, 2025, delved further into the conflicts between old and new money, the politics of marriage, and even the era’s technological advances.

But fans want more—and they’re in luck. The Gilded Age returned for its fourth season on November 1, bringing viewers back into the lavish homes and complicated lives of New York’s social elite. With the premiere now streaming and seven more episodes to come, there’s plenty of society drama still ahead. Here’s what to know about the Julian Fellowes show’s fourth season, from the release schedule to the new faces joining the cast.

Season 4 premiered Sunday, November 1 on HBO, with episodes also available to stream on HBO Max. The season consists of eight episodes, released weekly on Sundays through the December 20 finale.

The official season 4 logline reads: “Bertha Russell (Carrie Coon) changed Society at a cost. Now, her family must reckon with the consequences as Agnes van Rhijn (Christine Baranski) seizes an opportunity to regain her position. Meanwhile, Marian (Louisa Jacobson) forges a new path for herself, and Peggy (Denée Benton) works to be accepted by her future in-laws. In this new era, you must be careful what you wish for.”

Kelley Curran, who has played Enid “Turner” Winterton since Season 1, has been promoted to series regular for Season 4. She joins a star-studded returning ensemble that includes Coon, Spector, Jacobson, Benton, Cynthia Nixon, Baranski, Richardson, Blake Ritson, Taissa Farmiga, and Audra McDonald.
