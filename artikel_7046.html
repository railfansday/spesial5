CapsLock: Creative Marketing Project Coordinator

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

CapsLock is a global IT marketing company that pioneers unique, scalable customer acquisition solutions for B2C clients. We solve complex sales challenges across a wide range of industries, primarily for large North American partners. By integrating our expertise in Digital Marketing, IT, Design, AI-driven analytics, proprietary MarTech, and sales consulting, we deliver powerful, data-informed customer acquisition solutions.

At CapsLock, people, technology, and forward-thinking innovation lie at the heart of everything we do. Our diverse, global team, fluent in over 10 languages, thrives on open collaboration and the exchange of new ideas, pushing boundaries to ensure our clients' success.

Our creative team produces hundreds of video ads, images and ad copy variations every month, and we test them across our campaigns to find out what works best. Behind all of that is a fast-moving pipeline, and we’re looking for a Creative Marketing Project Coordinator to keep it running smoothly.

You’ll be working with a team of 15–25 creative strategists, video editors, designers and media buyers. You’ll turn ideas and requests into clear tasks, keep everyone aligned on deadlines, review the final work before it goes live, and make sure every asset gets launched and tested. Many of our workflows are already automated, and we’d love someone who enjoys finding smarter ways to work as we grow.

If you love bringing order to a busy environment, have a sharp eye for detail and are curious about the creative side of marketing, this role is a great place to grow. You’ll also be invited to brainstorming sessions and can contribute your own ideas to the creative process.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
