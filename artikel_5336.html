ADA Advancing Regulated Asset Tokenization as Brazil’s Petrobras Brings Sustainable Aviation Fuel Certificates to Cardano

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Petrobras’ decision to launch its sustainable aviation fuel on the Cardano blockchain highlights ADA’s expanding role in powering next-gen tokenization.

ADA continues to gain popularity in tokenized real-world assets and institutional blockchain adoption. Petrobras’ move to bring sustainable aviation fuel certificates to Cardano demonstrates this.

Brazil’s state-controlled energy company Petrobras has taken a pioneering leap towards the digital revolution by integrating Cardano blockchain technology to track environmental claims associated with sustainable aviation fuel. This is according to the interview shared earlier today by the Cardano Foundation.

As per the broadcast, Brazilian energy giant Petrobras has tapped Cardano to use ADA-based blockchain applications to track environmental claims linked to sustainable aviation fuel. As stated in the podcast, Petrobras, one of Brazil’s largest companies, has officially started using the Carbon blockchain to verify environmental data connected to low-carbon fuels. Announcements made in late September showed that Petrobras was testing the blockchain.

Latest updates indicate that the Brazilian state-controlled oil company is now using Cardano to track sustainability data for its sustainable aviation fuel (SAF). This blockchain application tokenizes environmental attributes (linked to SAF) into digital tokens designed to prevent reuse or duplication, according to a Wednesday report from the Cardano Foundation.

The SAF system tokenizes sustainable aviation fuel’s environmental attributes on Cardano, turning its sustainability claims into verifiable proof. As a result, the SAF tokens, which represent the fuel’s environmental attributes, prevent the same claim from being used more than once. This explains how the application solves the double-counting problem in carbon markets, enhancing efficiency, trust, transparency, and scalability.

By tracking fuel across production, shipment, and utility, the SAF system improves supply-chain trackability and emissions reporting. Through this innovative approach, the Petrobras aviation project helps prevent multiple firms from claiming ownership of emission-reduction rights associated with using sustainable fuel.

The launch represents another of Cardano’s largest public-sector deployments and expands its utility beyond crypto and DeFi into government tokenization and greenwashing in the aviation industry.
