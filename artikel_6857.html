These Embroidered Coats Are Going Straight Onto Our Winter Mood Boards

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Statement outerwear is the easiest way to make cold-weather outfits less boring.

Just because it’s getting cold doesn’t mean your fashion has to become less exciting. I’ve been scrolling online nonstop and the wardrobe piece I’m eyeing this winter is most definitely embroidered coats. Statement coats are the trend of the season, and there’s been a lot of talk about military and band-style jackets, which led me to the embroidery of it all, too.

While I feel like all of my favorite embroidered pieces tend to be better suited to the spring and summer months—largely blouses and light-wash jeans—there’s something incredibly chic about an embroidered coat that can add some flair to your cold-weather outfits.

If you’re looking to venture into the popular trend right now, I’ve gathered some stylish examples below for ample inspiration. Take a look at all the outerwear they’re embroidering and tell me you don’t want it! I dare you!

I know we already own too many black leather jackets as is, but an embroidered one can make any simple outfit all the more interesting. Pair it with a pair of denim jeans, and a chic little pump, and you’ve got magic.

For the Boho girlies still figuring out their fall and winter outfits, leaning into a light-colored embroidered jacket (with a slight crop like this one) is the surefire way to stay warm, but still true to your roots.

The band jacket honestly is so popular right now, I’ve been seeing it everywhere. It already has a fun silhouette and design but adding in the embroidery elements makes it a statement jacket everyone will be complimenting. It also pairs well and can be layered with other coats if you’re living in the frigid Northeast like me.

Now, a harder find I’ve realized, but still a worthy pick if you can get your hands on one, is an embroidered long coat. It truly will serve as the centerpiece for your outfit, so keeping it simple underneath and letting the coat shine is the way to go with this style.
