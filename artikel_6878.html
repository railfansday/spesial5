You Should Be Wearing Embellished Sweaters at Your Company Holiday Party

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

And naturally, we’ve found the best ones.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Once again, holiday party season is approaching, and you’ve got nothing to wear. You could rewear that sequin dress that you’ve worn three years in a row, but you’re better than that. It’s time to get yourself something new for the festive season, and an embellished sweater is the perfect option.

The best part about the style is that you can take it at your own pace. You don’t need to be a complete maximalist to partake in the style, but you can be. Run full speed ahead with Cos’s sequined-out knit—a bold, glittery choice. Or, dip your toe into the glamour with a subtly beaded cardigan from Rag & Bone. And once you pick out your sweater, the fun part (and perhaps the most critical part) is styling it.

Embellished sweaters are indeed versatile, but you’ll want to balance the statement sparkle with complementary pieces. Crystal studs or pearl embellishments often pair well with a relaxed denim. Give a sequin-trimmed sweater an edgy look with a black leather legging. Embrace the luxe look and add a velvet trouser or satin midi skirt on the bottom. No matter the way you style it, let the embellishments take center stage.
