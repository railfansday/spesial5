The 19 Best Men’s Skincare Sets to Gift This Holiday

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Featuring discovery collections and full-size staples he’ll be thrilled to unwrap

Even if you’re shopping for a guy who has everything, it’s hard to go wrong with the gift of good skin. The best skincare gift sets for men include nearly everything he’ll need for a fresh, balanced complexion, making it easy for newbies to build a streamlined routine or for skincare buffs who know their peptides from their retinols to restock their favorites.

Many of the top-rated sets we’ve gathered below are formulated specifically for men—whose skin, experts say, tends to be thicker than women’s—and are designed to tackle common concerns like oiliness, blackheads, ingrown hairs, and post-shave irritation. From targeted solutions to luxe everyday upgrades from coveted brands like Kiehl’s, Aesop, and Augustinus Bader, there’s truly something here for every skin type and preference.

Ahead, find the best men’s skincare sets to impress your dad, partner, uncle, sibling, or close friend this holiday season.

If he loves a good face mask (even if he’ll never admit it), consider gifting him this beloved set from Jaxon Lane. Along with four of the brand’s hydrating “Bro” sheet masks and two pairs of eye gels, this sleek bundle includes three additional bestsellers—the Shake and Wake Powder Enzyme Cleanser, Relax and Repair Face Moisturizer, and Rain or Shine Daily Sunscreen SPF 50—to help streamline his daily routine.

If you want to give him full sizes rather than trials, go for this set from Kiehl’s. It comes with the Facial Fuel Invigorating Face Wash and Facial Fuel Invigorating Moisture Treatment for nearly $20 less than the going rate of both on their own.

Jack Black was one of the first skincare brands to cater specifically to men, and more than 20 years later, its classic, affordable formulas remain a go-to for guys everywhere. This value-packed set features the brand’s essentials—a daily cleanser, scrub, moisturizer, and SPF 25 lip balm—in TSA-approved sizes he can take just about anywhere.

This blemish-friendly stocking stuffer set includes two sheets of Good Light’s pimple patches along with its New Moon Balm, which helps soothe just-popped pimples. If you know a teen who can’t keep his hands off his face, this streamlined set might help.

This back-to-basics kit from Atwater (founded by former Kiehl’s president Chris Salgardo) features a gentle cleansing gel, eye treatment, and moisturizer, all suitable for sensitive skin. While this version is designed for normal or combination complexions, the brand carries a bundle for dry skin as well.

The stars of this kit are the eye products, which include Dark Circle Defense Balm and Instant Dark Circle Corrector, which help brighten under-eyes instantly and over time. But the rest of your face won’t be left out, thanks to the Charcoal Face Wash, Daily Detox, and Daily Moisturizer.

Because he deserves a little TLC, too, elevate his shower ritual with this coveted body care trio from Aesop. It pairs a refreshing body wash and smoothing scrub with a rich body balm to help revive dry winter skin, all in the brand’s lush geranium scent.

Men critically underuse sunscreen, so this set is perfect for getting them in the habit. Containing a daily SPF 30 gel moisturizer that’s great for both sun- and cold-damaged skin, plus the brand’s best-selling vitamin C+E ferulic serum for added antioxidant protection, it's the ultimate three-step system to protect and hydrate skin.

Acne-prone and oily skin types will love this set, which features ingredients that help smooth, brighten, and keep it balanced. Start with the hydrating panthenol- and aloe-infused face cleanser, then swipe on a gentle exfoliating toner pad, and finish with the nourishing—not heavy—face gel.

For a decadent, multisensory shave, this sandalwood-scented quartet from the Art of Shaving is a crowd-pleasing pick. It includes full-size bottles of the brand’s popular pre-shave oil, shave cream, and after-shave balm, plus its premium shaving brush for working up a rich lather. Bonus: the formulas are designed with sensitive skin in mind.

There’s a little of everything in this luxe men’s skincare set: a shave and cleansing gel, an exfoliating face lotion to smooth skin, a lightweight peptide-packed moisturizer, and a hydrating SPF 33 for daily sun protection.

This set from beloved skincare brand Clinique includes a gentle charcoal face wash, face scrub, and a full-size hydrating gel to give dry skin an extra moisture boost. As an added perk, all three products are TSA-friendly, so he has no excuse not to bring them home after the holidays.

For those looking to tackle hyperpigmentation and discoloration, this luxe set of Augustinus Bader minis pairs an SPF 50 with three best-selling vitamin C treatments: The Vitamin C Serum, The Cream, and The Face Cream Mask. Together, they work to visibly even out tone and reduce dark spots while smoothing texture and boosting hydration. Each formula is also powered by the brand’s patented TFC8 complex, a powerhouse blend of vitamins, lipids, and peptides designed to support collagen production and skin barrier recovery.

Inspired by in-office treatments, this new mask duo from celebrity esthetician Sofie Pavitt works basically like a mini facial. Start with the Enzyme Treatment Mask, which warms on the skin as it activates to exfoliate and plump, then follow with the Cool Down Hydrating Exosome Mask to hydrate and soothe (or calm skin post-shave). The set also includes an applicator spatula with a textured side for deeper exfoliation, which Pavitt notes makes it ideal for those with facial hair, too.

When wrinkles, sagging skin, and other signs of aging become the main focus in your routine, this three-piece bundle from men’s skincare brand Caldera + Lab has you covered. It includes a purifying cleanser to prep the skin, a firming and brightening serum, and a lightweight moisturizer that boasts 48 hours of hydration while helping to minimize the appearance of fine lines.

Yes, $510 is a big ask for a skincare set. But hear us out: This comes with a cleanser, the beloved hyaluronic acid serum, and a face cream to provide hydration and smooth out uneven tone and texture. While it’s definitely a splurge, it might be the right investment if you’re serious about taking care of your skin—or just love the finer things in life.

No matter his age, if he’s new to a skincare routine, The Ordinary will ease him in. Even skincare newbies can handle the three simple, nourishing steps: a squalane cleanser, a hyaluronic acid serum, and a barrier-boosting moisturizer—all suitable for every skin type.

A skincare travel set makes the perfect gift for the person who is always racking up air miles. This set from Oars + Alps contains five essentials like deodorant, moisturizer, and eye cream, and a solid face wash to have your skin feeling—and smelling—great while on the go.

Inspired by Pharrell’s own skincare ritual, this three-piece set from his line offers an efficient routine that is super easy for anyone to follow. Humanrace’s Skincare Routine Pack includes the Rice Powder Cleanser, Lotus Enzyme Exfoliator, and Humidifying Cream, which work together to leave skin soft, radiant, and plump.
