A Deep Dive Into Brittany Snow and Hunter Moreno’s Increasingly Public Relationship

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Yes, Brittany finally confirmed that they’re engaged.

In case you missed it: The Hunting Wives star Brittany Snow is engaged to photographer and creative director Hunter Moreno, and if the news took you by complete surprise due to having literally no idea she was dating anyone, you’re not alone. Their relationship is extremely low-key—like, so low-key that they’ve only been photographed together on one or two occasions, and the only reason we, the people, first learned about their engagement is because Claire Danes was caught asking about it on a red carpet.

That said, there are some cute details to go over, so keep reading for a full timeline of Brittany and Hunter’s relationship!

Brittany and her ex-husband, Selling the OC star Tyler Stanaland, announce their split after two-and-a-half years of marriage in a joint statement:

“This decision was made with love and mutual respect for one another,” they wrote on Instagram (via People). “We have realized we need to take some time and make sure we are each living our most fulfilling and authentic lives. We started this journey as best friends and our relationship will continue to be a priority not only for us but for our dog Charlie.”

Four months after announcing their separation, Brittany reportedly files for divorce, citing “irreconcilable differences.”

Brittany and Tyler’s divorce is reportedly finalized five months after she filed.

Brittany makes an appearance on Call Her Daddy, where she tells host Alex Cooper, “It’s so weird for me to be single. I’m saying weirdly because it’s very easy for me to get in a relationship, and I’ve been actively trying to be alone and be with myself and just feel the feelings and not have to band-aid it with sparkly, shiny, emotional things.”

She adds that she’s been “in and out” of some flings, but has been pretty much keeping the vibes casual.

Brittany and Hunter start dating—or, at least, that’s when they’re first snapped kissing by paps while walking a dog.

Brittany tags Hunter in these pics, seemingly giving him photo cred:

Brittany shows up at the The Beast in Me premiere, and red carpet footage seemingly catches her telling Claire Danes that she’s engaged. At least based on lip readers thinking Claire says, “You’re engaged. When did that happen?!”

Not long after, TMZ confirms that, yep, Brittany and Hunter are engaged after a year of dating!

A source tells Us Weekly that “Hunter is an extraordinary guy. He’s a mental health advocate and just a salt-of-the-earth kind of guy. They are so happy together.”

Another insider says, “Brittany and Hunter are so private and low-key, they just started telling friends recently within the last few weeks. It happened about a month ago. It was low-key and private.” Apparently Brittany’s friends “love Hunter for her” and say, “He treats her well. They are both very low-key people.”

We’ve got ourselves a hard-launch! Brittany celebrates Hunter’s birthday with a gushing Instagram tribute, writing, “Happy birthday to my favorite person on the planet.”

She continues, “I must have done something right to deserve someone like you. Everyone who knows you, knows you are sunshine in human form. This was the first picture we ever took together (we didn’t know it was being taken) and I’ve never stopped hugging you & laughing with you since. It’s my favorite place to be. I love u @huntermoreno.”

At long last, Brittany fiiiinally confirms that she and Hunter are engaged. During an appearance on The Drew Barrymore Show ahead of The Hunting Wives season 2 premiere, Drew asks Brittany about the engagement rumors, to which she responds, “You know what? For the first time, I’m gonna tell you, I’m gonna tell you. Yes, we’re engaged, yeah.”

She adds, “I haven’t said it before. It’s okay. It’s not that big of a secret. I mean, like, who cares?”
