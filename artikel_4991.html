😱 Crypto Hits Extreme Fear as Bitcoin Plunges

The Crypto Fear and Greed Index printed 10 on Sunday. A month ago it read 63, which is Greed. A week ago it was 28. It has been sliding every day since, and 10 is about as low as that gauge goes.

This weekend’s price action certainly was extremely fearful. Bitcoin touched roughly $62,000 before stabilizing in the mid-60s, down about a quarter from its early-October high and down something like 9% in three days. ETH fell under $1,850. Solana, XRP and dogecoin are all down more than 25% on the month. Bitcoin is now trading below its 200-week moving average, a level that has historically acted as the floor beneath every cycle, and it lost its 50-week average a week earlier.

It wasn’t just majors. Zcash fell to $800. Hype fell to $62. VVV went sub-$17. Nothing was spared.

The billion-dollar question of course—why is Bitcoin (and crypto) selling off so hard?

CZ addressed the question directly on Sunday, saying “No one knows for sure. Correlation does not prove causation.” But he did give potential reasons.

No one knows for sure. Correlation does not prove causation. Here are some potential reasons.

The reasons he listed: money rotating into the Anthropic IPO, ETF outflows, a run of security incidents including the Binance hack itself plus drained iPhone-linked wallets, a Chrome exploit and a Microsoft outage, hot US inflation raising the odds of another Fed hike and strengthening the dollar, and political uncertainty after midterm results that point toward stricter crypto policy.

Those are all pretty valid. Some of those overhangs remove soon (Anthropic IPO), some will fade over time (political uncertainty), but the cyber attack threat will remain until Bitcoin and broader crypto can present a real solution. And if we are faced with a hiking cycle in 2027 due to inflation, that will certainly be a continued headwind.

So where does this leave us? Nvidia reports Tuesday with an interim CEO while its former one openly campaigns to return. The Fed meets December 9 with inflation running hot enough to put another hike in play. And Bitcoin is sitting under a technical level that has held for three cycles.

Extreme fear has marked bottoms before. It has also marked the middle of declines. Now we wait for something other than sentiment to turn.
