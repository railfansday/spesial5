Layoffs Hit XCOM Dev as It Struggles with Production of Third Entry

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A third of Firaxis staff to lose their job

XCOM and Midnight Suns studio Firaxis Games has lost roughly one-third of its workforce in a round of layoffs today.

Around 60 people lost their jobs, and studio head Heather Hazen is also leaving the developer.

In a statement, a spokesperson said the team is "restructuring and recentering" around "designing world class strategy games". As a result, Firaxis is being downsized to focus on "design, technology and the player experience, which means saying goodbye to talented colleagues who helped build Firaxis. These decisions are never easy, but they’re essential to build the next chapter of Firaxis."

Quite how cutting one-third of your overall staff only now means you can refocus on making the best strategy games in the business makes little sense to me.

Apparently, the developer does actually have a third XCOM game in the works, but it's been hit with development issues. The Bloomberg report from Jason Schreier said Firaxis Games missed a "significant milestone over the summer".

Development of the game continues, but it may be affected by today's layoffs.
