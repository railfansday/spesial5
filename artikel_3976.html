Team Cherry Uses The Pull Of Silksong To Highlight A Trio Of Wonderful-Looking Indies

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Just when we thought that we couldn't have any more respect for Team Cherry, the dev goes and uses its platform to highlight even more adorable-looking indies coming out of Australia.

The Cherry Time! showcase promised "a bunch of exciting announcements," and these were not Hollow Knight-related (until the last one, of course), but instead focused on a handful of titles coming out under the studio's publishing arm.

We've got the trailer and info for each of the three games included below, but honestly, what a wholesome way for Team Cherry to use its indie power! We came for Silksong DLC news (and we got it), but we left with another three games to add to our wishlists.

All of the following are listed as "Currently in development" at the time of writing, and none have been confirmed for Switch yet. That said, given the success Team Cherry has seen on the platform with Hollow Knight and Silksong, we'd be amazed if they don't extend the publishing our way at some point.

So, let's meet the three games highlighted in the show...

On this page: Team Cherry Uses The Pull Of Silksong To Highlight A Trio Of Wonderful-Looking Indies

Solve a grand mystery atop a gigantic walking city in Blokkan: Secret of the Walking City! Gather clues, meet eccentric characters, and solve playful puzzles in an unforgettable adventure

Moody lofi beats meet playful platform adventuring in a ruined, rainy world! Search rain-spattered streets, take on an army of mischievous creatures, and work together with your spirit companion to find a way home

Solve a planet-spanning plant mystery as the interstellar botanist Hatti and her trusty translator, Oliver! Investigate a lush, natural world and befriend a cast of crazy creatures in this breezy, beautiful adventure

After all that, the dev wrapped up with a closer look at the Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow DLC, confirming that it'll launch on Switch and Switch 2 on 11th December. You'll find all the details on that trailer in our coverage below.
