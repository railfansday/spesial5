Newcastle kit fiasco sums up haphazard pre-season

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Newcastle’s pre-season friendly against Bayer Leverkusen at St James’ Park took a bizarre turn when a kit clash prompted the hosts to change shirts midway through the first half.

The German team’s white away kit with blue trim proved hard to distinguish from Newcastle’s home strip, which has a white back and blue stripes across the shoulders.

During the first-half hydration break, Newcastle decided to change into their lilac third kit, which improved the viewing experience for fans who took to social media to question the original choice of kits.

Newcastle would go on to lose the game 2-1 in new manager Matthias Jaissle’s first game at St James’, a result which followed a 3-1 reverse against Everton on Wednesday evening.

Leverkusen, the 2023-24 Bundesliga champions now managed by Spaniard Carles Martínez Novell, scored after just 44 seconds through a powerful Ibrahim Maza strike.

Jaissle’s team found their feet as the first 45 minutes progressed, with Yoane Wissa their biggest attacking threat. The striker endured a difficult first season at the club, but his performances at the World Cup with DR Congo appear to have restored confidence.

Newcastle were level just before half-time, when Malick Thiaw rose highest to plant home a back-post header from a corner. Former Brentford goalkeeper Mark Flekken appealed in vain for a foul, but there was no infringement.

After changing all 10 outfield players at half-time against Everton, Jaissle was more circumspect with second-half substitutes; Rory Finneran for Lewis Miley was the only change before the 80th minute.

Newcastle faded though, and the game was won by Victor Boniface scoring just a few minutes after coming on as a substitute. The Nigeria striker wrong-footed Sean Steur with an impudent piece of skill before rolling the ball into the bottom corner.

The North-East club are coming to terms with the departures of Anthony Gordon, Sandro Tonali and Bruno Guimarães this summer, as well as manager Eddie Howe. They have one more pre-season friendly to play, against Strasbourg on Sunday, before they begin their Premier League campaign at home to Liverpool.

Newcastle attacking with more urgency since conceding, but there are moans and groans in the ground at a misplaced Elanga pass. Sean Neave has just replaced Woltemade, and the 19-year-old wins a free-kick in a crossing position with his first action of the game.

Kofane, who impressed up front last season in Germany, throws himself at a cross.
