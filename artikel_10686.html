Car smashes into clothing store on livestream after driver falls asleep at wheel

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

A clothing store’s livestream took a terrifying turn when a car smashed through its front window, sending employees scrambling as viewers watched the crash unfold in real time.

The incident occurred on November 5 at the Use Guta Store in Manaus, Brazil, where owner Dailane Augusta da Silva Baba and her employees were showcasing clothing and promoting Black Friday discounts.

Footage from the livestream shows two women presenting outfits to viewers, completely unaware of the vehicle approaching the storefront.

Suddenly, a loud crash interrupts the broadcast as a Jeep Renegade smashes through the glass entrance. A partition collapses behind the women before the screen goes dark.

Separate CCTV footage shows the vehicle approaching what appears to be a parking space outside the store before suddenly accelerating into the building.

💥🫢 A employee for a fashion retailer was injured after a livestream captured a car crashing through her workplace in Manaus, Brazil. 🎥: Guta Store via Storyful pic.twitter.com/juqBFQTC8B

According to Brazilian outlet A Crítica, the accident happened at approximately 4:30 PM local time.

The driver reportedly told police she had fallen asleep behind the wheel, causing her to lose control of the Jeep and crash into the storefront.

One employee suffered abrasions to her shoulder and received emergency medical attention. The store’s owner also reported experiencing body pain following the collision.

Fortunately, neither woman suffered life-threatening injuries.

In a statement, Dailane reassured followers that everyone was safe, although they remained shaken by the ordeal.

“My employees are fine. We had a lucky escape. God is good all the time,” she said.

The crash left the store’s glass facade destroyed, with authorities launching an investigation to determine exactly what happened.

It’s not the first time a vehicle has crashed into a business while someone was filming content.

Another TikToker captured the moment a car crashed into her workplace while recording a video on her final day at the job in 2022.

In the latest incident, investigators have yet to confirm the driver’s account of falling asleep or announce any potential charges.
