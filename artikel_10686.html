McDonald’s is letting customers use their rewards points to get cheaper Uber rides

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

McDonald’s is expanding its rewards program through a new partnership with Uber, allowing customers to redeem their points for discounts on rides.

The fast food giant announced the changes on November 9 as part of a multi-year agreement that will introduce new delivery and loyalty features across 14 markets, including the US, UK, Canada, and Australia.

McDonald’s and Uber first partnered in 2017, with the restaurant chain now available on Uber Eats in 30 countries. The expanded agreement will introduce new ways for customers to earn and spend rewards points and change how deliveries are handled through the McDonald’s app.

Beginning in November 2026, MyMcDonald’s Rewards members in select markets will be able to redeem their accumulated points for discounts on Uber rides through a seasonal promotion in the McDonald’s app.

The companies have not specified which markets will receive the promotion first, how many points will be required for a discount, or the exact date the feature will become available.

Additional rewards features are also planned, including the ability to earn MyMcDonald’s Rewards points on eligible McDonald’s orders placed directly through Uber Eats.

Those points will then be redeemable for rewards and benefits within the McDonald’s app, including future Uber ride offers. However, Uber has not announced a launch date for these additional features.

The agreement also makes Uber McDonald’s exclusive delivery and rideshare rewards partner in participating markets.

Customers will be able to place delivery orders and track their food directly through the McDonald’s app without switching to Uber Eats. Most of these orders will be fulfilled through Uber Direct in partnership with Deliverect.

Ordering McDonald’s directly through Uber Eats will remain an option.

“By building on our longstanding relationship with Uber, we’re making the customer experience even easier, more reliable and more rewarding, while continuing to give our fans choice in how they order McDonald’s,” said Mason Smoot, McDonald’s Senior Vice President of Global Franchising and Delivery.

The announcement comes just days after Uber Eats announced a new partnership with Amazon, giving Prime members access to free delivery on eligible restaurant orders of $25 or more.

The deal also allows Prime members to order food directly through Amazon’s website or shopping app, with an introductory promotion offering up to $40 off their first two qualifying orders.
