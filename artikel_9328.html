The Best Gifts Under $25 (That Don’t Look or Feel Cheap)

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Finding a great gift for less than $25 can feel like a challenge, especially when you’re looking for something more memorable than another candle or pair of cozy socks. But a small budget doesn’t have to mean settling for a mundane present. Often, the most interesting holiday treats are the little things: a cleverly designed everyday object, an unexpected pop of color, or an accessory that brings a bit of personality to an otherwise ordinary routine.

For this year’s guide, we’re focusing on affordable finds that deliver style beyond their price tag. From playful home accessories to useful objects with a creative twist, these gifts under $25 prove that thoughtful design can come in surprisingly small packages.

Photography courtesy of MoMA Design Store

A single stem gets a colorful upgrade with this sculptural bud vase. A slender glass tube rises from a chunky, multifaceted crystal base that catches the light and casts colorful reflections across nearby surfaces. Available in orange and green, red and blue, and light blue and peach, the playful design makes an impression even without flowers, especially when placed somewhere sunny.

The Faceted Crystal Bud Vase is available at ⁠store.moma.org for $25.

A personalized gift doesn’t have to come with a personalized price tag. Anthropologie’s Striped Stoneware Monogram Mug pairs playful stripes with an oversized initial, giving an everyday coffee cup a bit of extra personality. Available in all 26 letters, the glazed stoneware mug holds a generous 15.2 ounces and is both microwave- and dishwasher-safe. At just $16, it’s an easy gift for coworkers, friends, or anyone who takes their morning coffee seriously.

The Striped Stoneware Monogram Mug is available at anthropologie.com for $16.

Forget reading tea leaves. The Pasta Tarot serves up fortunes with a generous helping of Italian-American culture and a healthy dose of humor. Illustrated by Lindsay Mound, the 78-card deck reimagines traditional tarot suits as different pasta shapes, from stuffed ravioli to long strands of spaghetti. Pop culture references and colorful illustrations bring a playful twist to the centuries-old practice, making it a fun gift for pasta lovers, design enthusiasts, and anyone curious about what the future holds.

The Pasta Tarot is available at pieceworkpuzzles.com for $22.

A handwritten note feels even more special when the stationery is this striking. Featuring patterns from Liberty’s textile archive, this notecard set brings the London design house’s distinctive approach to color and pattern to everyday correspondence. It’s a gift well-suited for anyone who appreciates the art of putting pen to paper.

The Liberty Premium Notecard Set is available at galison.com for $19.99.

A small piece of marble goes a long way, especially when it comes with a playful edge. Mud Pie’s set of four coasters trades the usual round silhouette for a softly scalloped shape that feels both classic and contemporary. Crafted from white marble, each four-inch coaster features natural veining that makes every piece slightly different. They’re a wonderful holiday gift for a host or new homeowner that looks more expensive than it is.

The Scallop Marble Coaster Set is available at mudpie.com for $21.

John Derian’s fascination with antique prints finds a new home on the covers of these three notebooks. Each features a different design on the front and back, drawn from the designer’s extensive archive of vintage imagery. With stitched spines and 64 blank pages apiece, they’re equally suited for sketches, lists, and whatever else deserves to be put on paper.

The Color Studies Notebook Set is available at johnderian.com for $20.

Sardines are having a moment, and this playful trinket tray from Ban.do brings the trend to your nightstand or dresser. Shaped like an open tin of sardines, the ceramic dish features colorful fish illustrations and a metallic-looking rim that mimics the real thing. It’s just the right size for holding rings, earrings, and other tiny treasures, adding a touch of humor to an otherwise practical accessory.

The Sardines Trinket Tray is available at bando.com for $19.95.

Who says kitchen towels have to be boring? London-based artist Lakwena brings her signature bold typography and vibrant color to this everyday kitchen essential, adapting artwork originally painted on cardboard packaging collected from East London’s Ridley Road Market. The graphic design draws on the visual language of market signage, transforming something ordinary into a celebration of color, culture, and creativity. Made from 100% organic cotton, it’s equally at home drying dishes or hanging on display.

The Heaven Sent Real Food Tea Towel is available at 4artpartners.com for $22.

A good pen can make jotting down even the most mundane to-do list feel a little more special. Papier’s Wave Fountain Pen brings a playful detail to a classic writing instrument with its sculptural, wavy clip and smooth, weighty barrel. Available in gold or dusty sage, the refillable pen uses water-based blue ink and has a satisfying heft that belies its $18 price tag. It’s a perfect stocking stuffer for anyone who still appreciates putting pen to paper.

The Wave Fountain Pen is available at papier.com for $18.

Danica Studio brings a playful mix of color, pattern, and texture to the kitchen with its jacquard-woven cotton towels. The Pome design features a cheerful fruit-inspired pattern that adds some much-needed fun to everyday chores. Measuring 18 by 28 inches, the machine-washable towel is as practical as it is visually appealing. And with plenty of other colorful patterns, from bold geometrics to whimsical florals and wildlife, there’s a design to suit just about any kitchen.

The Pome Jacquard Dish Towel is available at danicastudio.com for $12.

These petite stoneware bowls prove that even the smallest tableware can have personality, with their contrasting matte exteriors and glossy interiors. Handmade in Thailand and available in six colors, they’re equally suited to serving olives, sauces, and dips or containing tiny odds and ends around the house.

The Dip Bowls (Set of 2) are available for $25 at ⁠us.gethommey.com.

The Henriette Hen Kitchen Timer is available at williams-sonoma.com for $19.95.

Give old records a second spin with these clever coasters made from reclaimed vinyl. Designed by Jeff Davis and handmade in Philadelphia, each set of six preserves the original record labels, creating a colorful assortment of miniature music memorabilia. No two sets are exactly alike, and the coasters are backed with felt to protect your furniture. They’re a fun way to bring music history to the coffee table, even when the record player isn’t spinning.

The Upcycled Record Coasters are available at uncommongoods.com for $24.

Fredericks and Mae gives the classic game of dominoes a colorful geometric makeover. Instead of traditional dots, each Baltic birch tile features silkscreened shapes, with the number of sides representing values from zero to six. It’s a clever visual twist that makes a familiar game feel fresh without changing how it’s played. Equal parts graphic design and old-fashioned entertainment, this set is a fun gift for anyone who appreciates creativity on game night.

Shape Dominoes are available at fredericksandmae.com for $20.

Here’s a small upgrade for the friend who takes their cheese boards seriously. Made from stainless steel, these three knives feature exaggerated oval handles that give otherwise utilitarian tools a sculptural quality. Each serves a different purpose, with blades designed for hard, soft, and creamy cheeses. They’re also dishwasher-safe, making them as practical as they are distinctive.

The Cheese Knife 3-Piece Set is available at crateandbarrel.com for $20.

Who says you need a whole bouquet to make a statement? Kinto’s Sacco Vase is designed to give even a single stem its moment in the spotlight. Made from colored glass with a subtle gradient, its softly rounded, asymmetrical silhouette reads as a small sculpture, even when empty. The angled opening adds an unexpected twist, while the weighted base keeps taller stems stable. Available in green, brown, and gray, it’s a thoughtful gift for anyone who appreciates good design.

The Sacco Vase is available at kinto-usa.com for $24.40.

Who knew a bathroom sink could make such a charming catchall? Puebco’s miniature ceramic washbasins borrow the familiar forms of traditional bathroom fixtures, complete with tiny faucets and plumbing details. Available in two styles, including a rectangular basin and an oval pedestal sink, they’re sized for holding rings, earrings, or other small treasures. Inspired by architectural design, it’s a playful addition to the nightstand or dresser.

The Ceramic Washbasin Tray is available at pieceworkpuzzles.com for $24.

There’s something satisfying about sealing a letter the old-fashioned way. Cate Paper Co. gives the centuries-old tradition a contemporary update with five design options, such as a ribbon bow, citrus fruit, and delicate florals. Each set includes a reusable brass stamp and a wax stick, ready to add a personal touch to handwritten notes, invitations, or wrapped gifts. The one-inch stamp can also be used with ink pads, making it a versatile addition to any stationery collection.

The Wax Seal Stamp & Wax Stick Set is available at catepaperco.com for $24.

Even making a cup of tea could use a little whimsy. Designed by Wang Lei, these miniature fishermen perch on the edge of your mug, dangling tea bags from tiny stainless steel hooks while they steep. Inspired by the Chinese story of Jiang Taigong, who believed fish would come to him when they were ready, the design is a playful reminder that good things take time. Made from silicone and stainless steel, the set of four turns an everyday ritual into a charming scene.

The Fishermen Tea Bag Holders are available at kikkerland.com for $20.

Hay’s popular Colour Crates get the miniature treatment with these stackable storage bins, perfect for corralling everything from paper clips and pens to makeup. Made entirely from recycled plastic, the ventilated crates come in an assortment of colors that make organizing feel less like a chore. Like their larger counterparts, they even fold flat when not in use—although their playful, industrial-inspired design makes a good case for keeping them on display.

The Mini Recycled Colour Crates are available in sets of two at dwr.com for $14.
