Apple TV’s 10/10 Thriller Series Is Officially a 200-Day Streaming Sensation

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

It's been another successful year for Apple TV, with some of the best shows of the past twelve months exclusively on the platform. The return of the beloved Ted Lasso after three years away didn't just take the show past the 1000-day mark on the Apple TV top ten; it also broke a record for the streamer, as the premiere of Season 4 drew 296.6 million views during its first two days, including over 200 million minutes watched during its opening 24 hours.

Even with such huge success for Ted Lasso, many would still consider the show's best title of 2026 the genius horror-comedy Widow's Bay. In ten episodes, between April and June, viewers were welcomed into the titular fictional New England island town, as supernatural evils fueled by a centuries-old curse lurk in the shadows. Mayor Tom Loftis (Matthew Rhys) dreams of rebranding the town into a tourist destination, although his timing couldn't be much worse.

"Widow's Bay becomes a haunting, deeply rewarding, and oddly charming series if you stick with it," wrote Collider's Emily Bernard in her review of the first season, which was one of plenty of glowing reviews, helping the series reach a near-perfect 98% rating on review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes. The opening two episodes reportedly reached 2.5 million U.S. viewers, with the season finale week drawing 415 million viewing minutes. The show's success hasn't stopped since then. At the time of writing, Widow's Bay has officially surpassed the 200-day mark on the Apple TV top ten in the U.S., with the show spending almost every day since its debut in the charts.

Not just a big hit with audiences, Widow's Bay was named this year's biggest small-screen awards darling at the 78th Primetime Emmys. The series won a total of 14 prizes from a total of 19 nominations, including prizes for outstanding comedy series, Lead Comedy Actor (Rhys), Supporting Comedy Actor (Stephen Root), and Supporting Comedy Actress (Kate O’Flynn). Other Apple TV shows to prove popular at the Emmys include the Elle Fanning-led adaptation Margo’s Got Money Troubles, which won three prizes on its first try, and Pluribus, which won six trophies, including a win for Rhea Seehorn in Outstanding Actress in a Drama Series.

Widow's Bay has officially surpassed the 200-day mark on the Apple TV top ten in the U.S. Stay tuned to Collider for more.
