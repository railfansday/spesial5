Rockstar Games Has A GTA 6 Hit On Its Hands, But Its Union Vote Is A Loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Roughly 75% of Rockstar Games staff in the UK have voted against a formal recognition agreement with the Independent Workers' Union of Great Britain (IWGB). The news came in the wake of GTA 6's record-breaking launch, and had the IWGB been recognized, Rockstar Games management would have been legally obligated to negotiate collective bargaining terms with the union on topics such as pay, hours, and conditions.

Voting took place across two weeks, and turnout was massive, with over 93% of UK staff casting their ballot (roughly 1,544 employees). The IWGB said it was undeterred by the result and would continue working to build support among Rockstar employees. "While the result means Rockstar will not be formally required to hold regular meetings with union representatives under a recognition agreement, it does not prevent workers from continuing to organize and push for workplace improvements through the IWGB, joining the union or exercising their individual trade union rights and protections," the union said in a statement.

The vote also comes hot on the heels of the IWGB taking Rockstar to an employment tribunal, alleging unlawful dismissal and union busting after it fired over 30 employees last year for gross misconduct over alleged leaks of confidential information. Closing submissions were wrapped up shortly before the vote results came out, and a ruling is still pending. While game dev unionizing has gained significant momentum over the years, a decisive loss at one of the industry's biggest studios could be a notable speed bump for union organizers.

With Rockstar also riding the wave of GTA 6's monumental success, employees may be focused on bonuses and post-launch momentum, which could make pushing through a formal union structure even harder. "For anyone at Rockstar who believes in a fairer workplace, a fairer sector and a fairer society--this isn't a sign to retreat; it's a sign to double down on what we stand for. Hundreds of our coworkers voted in favor of our union at Rockstar, yet sadly we recognize we have more work to do to build understanding and win the trust of more of our colleagues,' the IWGB added.
