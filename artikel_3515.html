Joe Jonas and Girlfriend Tatiana Gabriela Split After 1 Year of Dating

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

It’s over. Joe Jonas and model Tatiana Gabriela have broken up after dating for just over one year.

“They had a really fun summer traveling together, but then things shifted in the relationship,” a source with knowledge can exclusively confirm to Us. “Things started to shift as Joe got busier with work commitments and they were spending more time apart.”

The insider continued, “They had a lot of fun together and were a really good match, so friends are still hopeful they might be able to work things out. But right now, they just have different lifestyles.”

Joe, 37, and his brothers, Nick Jonas and Kevin Jonas, are booked through the end of the year with their Burning Up Tour All Over Again, which is scheduled to wrap up with two pre-Christmas shows in Newark on December 21 and December 22. He also coparents daughters Willa, 6, and Delphine, 4, with his ex-wife Sophie Turner, who lives in London.

“Joe has a lot on his plate between work and being a dad, and that can be a lot for someone to take on,” the source shared with Us.

Us has reached out to representatives for both stars for comment.

Fans initially speculated that Joe and Gabriela may have gone their separate ways after she reposted a TikTok video that said, “Thought I saw love in your eyes but it was just a reflection of mine.”

Nearly one year earlier, Joe first hinted at his connection to Gabriela in January by “liking” one of her Instagram posts and commenting with a “drooling” emoji.

A source exclusively confirmed to Us that same month that Joe had recently introduced Gabriela as his girlfriend to his friends and family, his two children and ex Turner. (He and the Game of Thrones actress, 30, finalized their divorce in September 2024.)

“[Joe and Gabriela] started seeing each other at the end of the summer [in 2025],” the insider revealed at the time.

The couple finally hard-launched their relationship in April with an Instagram carousel, including a black-and-white photo of Gabriela resting her arms around the Jonas Brothers star’s neck. The musician also shared a vlog from a recent trip to Puerto Rico, showing Gabriela trying to teach Joe how to speak Spanish and enjoying lots of the local cuisine.

“She’s helping with my Spanish,” Joe declared in the YouTube video, adding, “Then we went to a waterfall, we jumped in, it was so nice.”

Prior to his year-long relationship with Gabriela, Joe had a brief romance with model Stormi Bree in 2024.

“I was seeing somebody at the time, and I was kind of having this idea of dating again. It was really scary and intimidating,” Joe confirmed during a TalkShopLive livestream in May 2025. “Love takes different shapes and forms, and I was rediscovering what that was.”

A source told Us in May 2024 that Joe and Bree, 35, had hit pause on seeing each other because of the demands of their busy schedules.

“Joe has a very busy schedule between his kids and his career, so his romantic life has to take a back seat for now,” the insider explained.

Joe was previously married to Turner for four years before they announced their separation in September 2023.

The exes ultimately finalized their divorce settlement in September 2024 after a full year of negotiations.

“Afer a productive and successful mediation, we have agreed that the children will spend time equally in loving homes in both the U.S. and the U.K.,” they told Us in a statement at the time. “We look forward to being great coparents.”
