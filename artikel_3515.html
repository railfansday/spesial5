Diablo 4’s Most Nostalgic Season Is Becoming (Mostly) Permanent

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Diablo 4 is gearing up for the arrival of Season 16, but it won't be leaving its popular previous season behind entirely.

As detailed by Blizzard in a breakdown of the upcoming Season of Tyrael's Gambit, much of the previous season, Hell's Legacy, will become a permanent part of Diablo 4 going forward. Season 15 served as celebration of the franchise's history, and added a smorgasbord of items and callbacks to previous Diablo titles, including a storyline revolving around Deckard Cain and the return of enemies and boss encounters from Diablo 2. 

Most of that is carrying forward, Blizzard said. Due to "overwhelming requests" from the game's community, Blizzard outlined what all from Hell's Legacy will survive.

Hell's Legacy's capstone dungeons will be added, as will the ability to summon a World Boss version of Diablo and take on the cow god Moopisto. The Prime Evil Council is also becoming permanent, and will work the same way the game's other Lair bosses do, with players needing a specific item to open their reward chest after successfully defeating the encounter.

Waking Nightmare dungeons will still be accessible, but only by finding Chronicle of Nightmares dungeon keys. Monsters from previous Diablo games will also have a chance to spawn in The Pit. Those who progressed their secret cow level journey can also rejoice, as the secret cow island and its corresponding waypoint will be staying.

Many of Hell's Legacy's best items were new versions of classics from older Diablo games, and those Legacy Uniques will be added permanently as well, including the Annihilus Seal and Hellfire Torch Charm.

That's all for now, but Blizzard said more of Hell's Legacy could make a return in the future.

"Other popularly requested features are being evaluated for rework and may appear in the future," Blizzard said.

Diablo 4 Season 16 launches December 1. In addition to bringing forward much of Hell's Legacy, Tyrael's Gambit will additionally overhaul the game's War Plans system and make its Helltide activity easier.

Diablo 4 won't receive new expansions going forward, but it is receiving a new class, the Amazon, next year. Blizzard is also hard at work on Diablo 5. Though it's not slated to arrive until 2029, Blizzard has already revealed three of Diablo 5's playable classes and promised it will launch with more class options than any previous Diablo game.
