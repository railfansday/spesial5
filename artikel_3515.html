All The Game Awards 2026 Nominees Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It's almost time for The Game Awards to kick off a night of trailers and appreciation for the 2026 gaming year, but right now, it's time to see who's in the running to earn some well-deserved kudos. The 2026 Game Awards will be livestreamed from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles on December 10, and nominees for the best games across several categories have been announced.

Like previous years, the nominees have been chosen by a global jury of media publications and creator outlets--including GameSpot--and were announced through a dedicated livestream.

Leading the nominations is Grand Theft Auto 6 with eight nominations, Control Resonant and Resident Evil Requiem with six nominations each, and Phantom Blade Zero has earned an impressive five nominations. Big Walk, 007 First Light, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake have also earned plenty of nods, with four nominations each.

Here's a closer look at which games are in the running for several of the biggest prizes at The Game Awards this year.

Game of the Year

The best of the best for 2026, the game that excelled across multiple creative and technical fields.

  • 007 First Light
  • Control Resonant
  • Grand Theft Auto 6
  • Phantom Blade Zero
  • Resident Evil Requiem
  • The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time

Best Game Direction

For the game that was built with an outstanding creative vision and executed it through polished direction and design.

  • Control Resonant
  • Grand Theft Auto 6
  • Phantom Blade Zero
  • Pragmata
  • Resident Evil Requiem

Best Narrative

Awarded to the game that not only offers the best story of the year, but also delivers its with exceptional storytelling skills.

  • 007 First Light
  • Control Resonant
  • Grand Theft Auto 6
  • Resident Evil Requiem
  • The Blood of Dawnwalker

Best Art Direction

Video game graphics have come a long way over the years, and this award recognizes the 2026 game that featured incredible artistic design and animation.

  • Control Resonant
  • Grand Theft Auto 6
  • Mouse P.I. For Hire
  • Phantom Blade Zero
  • The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time

Best Score and Music

Great game visuals also need equally good audio, and this award is for the best music in a game, pulled from its score, original songs and/or a licensed soundtrack.

  • Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve
  • Grand Theft Auto 6
  • Mixtape
  • Phantom Blade Zero
  • The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time

Best Audio Design

Sticking with sound, this award goes to the game with the best in-game audio and sound design.

  • Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve
  • Control Resonant
  • Grand Theft Auto 6
  • Resident Evil Requiem
  • Star Wars: Galactic Racer

Best Performance

We've also seen the rise of video game thespians in modern gaming, and this award honors the individual for their voice-over acting and performance capture.

  • Angela Sant'Albano --  Resident Evil Requiem
  • Liam McIntyre -- Marvel's Wolverine
  • Manni L. Perez -- Grand Theft Auto 6
  • Rahul Kohli -- Saros

Video games are capable of delivering profound messages, and the Games for Impact award is handed out to the one title that gave players a thought-provoking experience.
