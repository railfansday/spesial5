Current get revenge over Gotham with Haley Hopkins’ late-game winner

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

After defeating Gotham 2-1, the Current will make their first NWSL Championship appearance since 2022.

Haley Hopkins’ teammates were atop her in seconds, a pile of teal on the Icahn Stadium pitch.

Hopkins had just netted the game-winning goal for the Kansas City Current in a 2-1 win over Gotham FC in Manhattan on Sunday, sending her team to the National Women’s Soccer League Championship.

In that celebratory huddle, there was jubilation at punching a ticket to the championship for the first time since 2022. There was relief, for having escaped extra time. And underneath it all, there was vindication.

Just last year, the Current, fresh off winning the NWSL Shield, lost to Gotham in a quarterfinal upset at home. Gotham went on to lift the championship trophy over the Washington Spirit.

This year, Kansas City found itself on the other side of the equation as they faced the 2026 Shield winners and No. 1 playoff seed.
