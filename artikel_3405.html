James Cameron’s $1.5 Billion Sci-Fi Thriller Officially Returns With New Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

If there's one filmmaker who doesn't believe in doing things by halves, it's James Cameron. The director has spent decades pushing the boundaries of blockbuster filmmaking, whether that's sinking the Titanic, sending audiences into the future with the Terminator, or creating an entire alien ecosystem that looks suspiciously more appealing than most places on Earth. Apparently, though, even a movie running for more than three hours isn't enough to squeeze in everything Cameron wants audiences to see.

Disney's 20th Century Studios has officially confirmed a new Collector's Edition of Avatar: Fire and Ash, which is going to arrive December 8, 2026, armed with 12 deleted and extended scenes and more than six hours of previously unreleased bonus content. The release will include more than nine hours of supplementary material, or three Avatars worth. Now that sounds like James Cameron.

The movie stars Sam Worthington (Clash of the Titans) as Jake Sully, Zoe Saldaña (Guardians of the Galaxy) as Neytiri, Sigourney Weaver (Alien) as Kiri, Stephen Lang (Don't Breathe) as Colonel Miles Quaritch, Kate Winslet (Titanic) as Ronal, Jack Champion (Scream VI) as Spider, Britain Dalton (Goliath) as Lo'ak, and Oona Chaplin (Game of Thrones) as Varang.

The first film, which became the highest-grossing film of all time (twice) follows ex-Marine who finds himself thrust into hostilities on an alien planet filled with exotic life forms. As an Avatar, a human mind in an alien body, he finds himself torn between two worlds for himself and for the planet’s indigenous people. 13 years later, The Way of Water follows Jake Sully and Neytiri, and their children, as they're driven from their jungle homes to the coasts of Pandora while battling to stay alive and discovering new sea creatures and Na'vi cultures.

Fire and Ash brings us back to the Sully family, still dealing with the death of Neteyam, while a new threat emerges from the Mangkwan Clan, also known as the Ash People, led by Varang. Their arrival creates further conflict as Jake and Neytiri struggle to protect their family from the threat of Quaritch.

Avatar: Fire and Ash Collector's Edition arrives December 8, 2026, with 12 deleted and extended scenes and more than nine hours of supplementary material.
