GTA 6: How To Get Any Safe Code

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

GTA 6 is full of surprises. While there are tons of ways to make money, some of them are faster than others. But those methods are generally riskier, creating an interesting push-and-pull in how fast you earn cash in the game.

While other GTA games have had these elements front and center, GTA 6 expands the robbery mechanic quite a bit, allowing players to use disguises and hit a ton of places in rapid succession, quicker than ever before. There are a lot of factors to consider, but here's how to get any safe code, whether it's a small pawn shop or a big bank.

Before you do any of that, turn off the security feed and make sure you're isolated from what's going on outside. Taking Jason or Lucia with you can help you control the crowd, and avoid the police showing up earlier as you're searching for (or demanding) the safe code.

Generally, smaller places (like pawn shops) will have the code written down, and one employee present. These codes might be on a giant board close to the register, in a back room, or in an office.

The easiest way to try and force the code out of an employee. You can hold them up directly by aiming a weapon at them, holding L2 (LT on Xbox), and repeatedly pressing the Square (X on Xbox) button. You'll interrogate them for a code, and even if they don't give it to you, you can still try the same method on someone else. This method may also force NPCs to take cash out of the register and place it on the counter, making your robbery go a little faster.

Often, in the hectic event of robbing something (especially if it's a massive bank), it's easy to miss the notification that you acquired a safe code verbally. Because of this, make sure you look for the safe before leaving a bank for any reason, in case you picked up the safe code by accident. It'll take less than 30 seconds to load up your duffle bag with cash from the safe.

For the most part, any business will have an on-site safe, but that's not always the case. Look for locations to rob with a specific icon that shows how much cash the store has on hand and whether there are any illicit items in the backroom (like illegal substances).
