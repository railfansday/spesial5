All the Times Robert Pattinson Basically Manifested Becoming the Next James Bond

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Robert Pattinson has officially received his license to kill. As James Bond, that is. Hollywood’s favorite leading man was announced as the next 007 on Monday, November 16, after Puck News reported that he screen-tested for the role in the eleventh hour.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime,” Robert wrote in a statement, per ABC News. “To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”

As it turns out, Robert has unknowingly manifested the role of a lifetime throughout his banger-after-banger career. No, seriously—R-Patz locked into Ariana Grande as Galinda-levels of manifesting here, whether he believes it or not.

Even after all these years as a Twihard, I’m genuinely shocked at how much this man was asked about potentially playing the famous spy. So, for the sake of your scrolling time, I’ve rounded up all the times Robert Pattinson manifested his role as James Bond, right here.

All roads lead back to Twilight. Robert got his big break playing swoonworthy sparkly vampire Edward Cullen in the film franchise, and the first movie lowkey predicted his current role as 007. Don’t believe me? Check out this scene.

When Bella’s friend group discusses the school’s upcoming Monte Carlo prom theme, Eric says, “Gambling, tuxedos, and Bond, James Bond,” before the camera cuts to Edward and Bella stepping out of his car.

Robert starred alongside another Bond, Pierce Brosnan, in 2010’s Remember Me. While doing press for the movie, he was constantly asked whether he’d want to take on the iconic role at some point.

During an interview with Fandango, he admitted that he would “quite like to play a villain in a Bond film.”

“That would be fun,” he continued. “Or maybe a male, like, Bond girl. A Bond boy.”

The BBC separately asked if he’d be into the role, to which he sheepishly responded, “Oh, I don’t know. That’s quite big shoes to fill. If I did it right now, it’d probably be pretty bad. Pretty embarrassing.”

He remained humble at the time, but fast forward 16 years, and he’s officially been cast as 007 himself!

The Bond questions kept coming after Remember Me, though Daniel Craig had led the franchise since 2005.

During an interview with Shortlist, Rob said he was open to taking on the role in the future.

“Yeah definitely, but more like 20 years,” he said. “I think there’d be nothing worse than, like, ‘Let’s get a fresh-faced Bond!’ That would be the worst idea in the world. After Daniel Craig, you have to have some baggage. It would be ridiculous to reinvent [him] as some young posh kid. Or, if [he] was, I’d have to get so tortured in the first few scenes. I’d have to do the first film with one arm or something.”

In a separate conversation with the BBC, Rob was asked about the role again, responding, “Why do you keep asking me this? There already is a James Bond. I don’t know. I mean, I love James Bond. So, I don’t know.”

Okay, so this technically wasn’t spoken prophecy from R-Patz, but someone previously tied to another Bond project expressed interest in seeing the actor take on the famous role.

Danny Boyle, who was originally tapped to direct the last Bond movie, No Time to Die, told The Guardian that while he watched Robert’s flick High Life, “I was sitting there thinking: ‘Oh my God, they should get him to be the next Bond.’”
