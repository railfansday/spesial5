UCLA football rumors: Ty Dieffenbach didn’t suffer season-ending injury vs. Michigan

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

UCLA football rumors: Ty Dieffenbach didn't suffer season-ending injury vs. Michigan appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

During Saturday's triple-overtime defeat against the Michigan Wolverines in Ann Arbor, No. 4 UCLA experienced a concerning moment on the very first play of overtime when quarterback Ty Dieffenbach was tackled awkwardly and had to be carted off the field. This unsettling scene was noted by ESPN's Adam Rittenberg and Pete Thamel, prompting head coach Bob Chesney to turn to Madden Iamaleava, the younger brother of the already sidelined starter Nico Iamaleava.

While there were initial fears that the Bruins had lost their second quarterback for the remainder of the 2026 season, subsequent medical evaluations brought significant relief to the Westwood community. ESPN's Pete Thamel reported that Dieffenbach's injury is less severe than first thought. He stated on X, "Sources: UCLA quarterback Ty Dieffenbach isn't expected to be out long-term. While his status is uncertain for this week against USC, Dieffenbach is dealing with a minor hip injury. He is expected to be available for the College Football Playoff if UCLA secures a bid."

Adding to this optimistic outlook, CBS Sports insider Matt Zenitz confirmed the reports on X, providing an update on Dieffenbach following his lower body injury during the loss to Michigan. Zenitz mentioned that Dieffenbach underwent additional testing, and sources indicated that there is optimism it isn't a long-term or season-ending injury.

Although Chesney must still prepare Madden Iamaleava as a backup option for the crucial rivalry game against USC on November 28, avoiding a season-ending injury allows Dieffenbach to remain a viable option for UCLA's Big Ten championship and College Football Playoff aspirations.
