This New Jaeger-LeCoultre Watch Looks Perfect for Robert Pattinson’s James Bond

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The actor’s favorite watchmaker has unveiled a pink-gold Reverso with impeccable timing.

Just a week after Robert Pattinson was announced as the next James Bond, Jaeger-LeCoultre, the Swiss watchmaker for which he's an ambassador, has unveiled a new Reverso that looks rather well suited to 007.

It's certainly a good match for Pattinson's personal tastes. “I definitely like really thin watches,” he said in a recent interview. “That's the main thing. In general, I would probably prefer a leather strap.”

The new Reverso Classic Monoface Small Seconds checks both boxes. Measuring just 7.56mm thick, it has an 18-carat pink-gold case, a silver-colored dial with a texture inspired by stacked coins, and a black alligator strap. Its smaller proportions–40.1mm by 24.4mm—recall the original Reverso of 1931, whose ingenious swiveling case was designed to protect the watch during polo matches. Inside is a manually wound movement with a 42-hour power reserve.

At $18,400, it's a somewhat more elegant alternative to the more beefy dive watches traditionally associated with Bond. And with its art deco styling and slim profile, it would slip neatly beneath a dinner-jacket cuff with an ease the next Madeleine Swann would surely appreciate.

Of course, Omega has been Bond's official watch partner since 1995, and there's no suggestion that's changing. But as a watch for the man playing Bond, this new Reverso feels intriguingly on point.
