Jennifer Lopez’s ‘Frosted Pearl’ Nails Are This Season’s Dreamiest Manicure Trend: How to Try Them Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Between fashion week appearances and making a return to music, Jennifer Lopez has become a major beauty muse for fall 2026. A few weeks ago, her cozy, “chai latte” nails—courtesy of her longtime manicurist Tom Bachik—served as our top inspiration for new-season manicures. Now? The duo has added “frosted pearl” to the list.

In an Instagram post yesterday, Lopez sported a sheer base with a soft, luminous sheen; the result felt like a fresh take on classic chrome. According to pro nail artist Jessica White, the new trend features a reflective “glaze”—which Hailey Bieber famously popularized—but in a subtler way. “Rather than an intense, mirror-like chrome, it catches the light gently, giving the nails a pearlescent finish.”

Lopez’s natural blush-toned base color is essential to the frosted pearl effect, says White. “A sheer milky white or soft nude creates a delicate pearl effect, whereas a pinker base gives a warmer finish.”

With the colder seasons in full swing, understated, minimalist nail trends are to be expected; those who typically refrain from bold colors and elaborate nail art will often turn to traditional nudes, creamy neutrals, and your-nails-but-better manicures.

But White considers frosted pearl to be the best modern alternative. “There’s something about the subtle shimmer that feels a little more elevated and luxurious for the coming months,” she notes.

“For minimalists who want to introduce something a little different, it still feels clean, understated, and incredibly wearable, just with an added touch of luminosity.”

For the pearl finish, White explains that “chrome powder is the professional technique, but you can find chrome-like nail polishes, which are perfect for at-home.” Opt for one with a pink base. Sealing everything with a quality top coat will extend the life of your manicure and enhance the glossy shine.
