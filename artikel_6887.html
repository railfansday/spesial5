‘Dancing With the Stars’ Grammy Night Schedule: Songs and Dance Styles Revealed as Couples Perform “Dance-Offs”

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

We’re less than a week away from the last theme night of Dancing With the Stars season 35 before we head into semifinals! DWTS is capping off an iconic run with “Grammy Night,” an all-new theme that’ll see celebs and their pro partners perform to Grammy-winning songs from the likes of Beyoncé and Lady Gaga.

On top of that, the seven remaining couples will compete in dance-offs set to live performances from Brandy, Monica, Maren Morris, and Sheryl Crow. Though, please be advised that Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson have immunity from the dance-off portion of the evening thanks to getting the highest scores last week.

So, what Grammy-winning songs are everyone dancing to? Here’s the full lineup!

Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov: dancing a paso doble to “Bring Me to Life” by Evanescence

Connor Wood and Rylee Arnold: dancing an Argentine tango to “SexyBack” by Justin Timberlake

Ezra Frech & Daniella Karagach: dancing an Argentine tango to “Crazy in Love” (Remix) by Beyoncé

Harry Shum Jr. & Jenna Johnson: dancing a Salsa to “Die With a Smile” by Lady Gaga and Bruno Mars

Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy: dancing Jazz to “All for You” by Janet Jackson

Maura Higgins & Mark Ballas: dancing an Argentine tango to “Thong Song” by Sisqó

Tatyana Ali & Jan Ravnik: dancing a Tango to “Boom Boom Pow” by Black Eyed Peas

Reminder, Dancing With the Stars is back to its regular Tuesday night schedule next week, so tune in on November 10 at 8 p.m. ET to see Grammy Night in all its glory.
