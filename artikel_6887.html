Meet the All-Civilian Cast of ‘The Traitors: New Blood’—and Keep Track of Who’s Still in the Castle

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

An astrophysicist, a barrel racer, and a funeral director walk into a castle.…

Another season of The Traitors is here…but this time around, you won’t be seeing any of your fave reality stars roaming the halls of Ardross Castle. Instead, NBC brought 22 normies to the Scottish Highlands to compete for that $250,000 cash prize, including an astrophysicist, a barrel racer, an ironworker, and a funeral director. Count me in!

The Traitors: New Blood premiered September 17 on NBC (with episodes available to stream the next day on Peacock), and there is at least one familiar face this season, because host and executive producer Alan Cumming is once again leading the charge. But as for everyone else? Keep scrolling to meet the first all-civilian cast of Traitors and Faithfuls, who were selected from more than 80,000 applicants—and find out who’s already gotten the boot.
