Stellantis is exiting factory motorsport as CEO pushes cost-cutting plan

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The automaker will end World Endurance Championship and Formula E entries, keeping customer racing and high-performance vehicle work

Stellantis $STLA said it will end its official participation in FIA World Championship racing and refocus its motorsport resources on customer racing and high-performance vehicle development.

The company will conclude its World Endurance Championship (WEC) participation by the end of the 2026 season and leave Formula E after Season 13, which ends in summer 2027, Stellantis said. Peugeot is the brand departing the WEC, and the Formula E exits fall to Opel and Citroën, according to Bloomberg.

Stellantis said the decision fits its FaSTLAne 2030 strategic plan, which directs the company's efforts toward new products, quality and broader market coverage. The five-year plan includes 50 product introductions and updates in Europe, 25 of them new products.

Stellantis Motorsport will use its engineering and competition expertise to strengthen customer racing, the company said. Stellantis said it holds a prominent position in customer rally car sales through its multi-brand offering. The unit will keep contributing to exclusive high-performance models for Alfa Romeo and Maserati, along with Veloce, GTi, HF and GSE derivatives for Alfa Romeo, Peugeot, Lancia and Opel.

Stellantis said it intends to retain and redeploy employees affected by the end of its FIA World Championship activities. About 200 employees are assigned to motorsport, and their jobs will either shift elsewhere in the company or carry on within the customer racing and rally car operations, according to Bloomberg. Stellantis could take charges related to its racing platforms.

CEO Antonio Filosa must demonstrate that the company's new models and quality improvements are delivering results, according to Bloomberg. Filosa is trying to trim spending even as rising prices for raw materials and chips weigh on the company. Stellantis stock has lost two-thirds of its value so far this year, the steepest decline among Stoxx 600 index members.

Stellantis unveiled its FaSTLAne 2030 plan in May, committing €60 billion over five years, with Jeep, Ram, Peugeot and Fiat receiving 70% of total brand and product investment.

When Stellantis reported third-quarter results on Oct. 28, Filosa said the company was on course to have at least 40% of its targeted €6 billion in run-rate efficiencies implemented before year-end.
