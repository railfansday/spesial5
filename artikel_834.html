Chris Brown Gives Elderly Woman a Night to Remember With Sultry ‘Take You Down’ Performance in Los Angeles

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Lupe Fiasco playfully called the move "diabolical."

Chris Brown has made a habit of bringing different women on stage for the “Take You Down” portion of his performance on the R&B Tour, but now he’s entered another age bracket.

C. Breezy had an older woman — who goes by Ms. Joyce — escorted to the stage at Sunday night’s (Nov. 15) show inside Los Angeles’ SoFi Stadium, and she was dressed to impress for her moment, which found Ms. Joyce rocking a zebra-print mink coat, a furry hat and shades while walking with a red cane.

Clips quickly made the rounds on social media as CB serenaded Ms. Joyce on a stripper pole and then laid her down and danced on her on a red couch for the top 5 Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart hit, which had Ms. Joyce in a trance and in another dimension mentally.

Brown posted photos from the viral interaction. “Love her,” Breezy captioned the pictures, which Ms. Joyce should have framed and hung up somewhere in her home.

A bunch of the R&B star’s celebrity friends had a laugh at the heartwarming exchange and possible bucket list item for Ms. Joyce. “God bless your heart babe. You gave her a night she will NEVER forget,” actress Taraji P. Henson wrote.

Rapper Ray Vaughn joked, “Life alert bout to go off!” And Lupe Fiasco called the move from Breezy “diabolical.”

With the Nov. 16 date at SoFi Stadium of the R&B Tour canceled, Ms. Joyce will have to travel to get another opportunity to get eye-to-eye with Breezy again.

Next up, the lucrative trek is heading to New Orleans for a pair of shows this weekend before returning to Houston to finish off November. The R&B Tour is currently slated to wrap up in Tampa Bay, Fla., on Dec. 12.

The R&B Tour became the highest-grossing and best-selling run for the month of September. According to Billboard Boxscore, the co-headlining tour with Usher grossed $77.4 million and sold 423,000 tickets across 10 shows in September.

On the awards front, Chris Brown also collected another pair of Grammy Award nominations, as his Brown (The Chocolate Edition) LP is up for best R&B album, while he’s nominated in best R&B collaboration or duo/group performance for his team-up with Leon Thomas on “Fallin’.”
