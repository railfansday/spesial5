Southampton held at Sheffield United

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Sheffield United and Southampton at Bramall Lane on Tuesday night; Sydie Peck scores again as the Blades hit back to earn a point and cancel out Lewis Dobbins opener.

Sydie Peck equalised in the 90th minute as Sheffield United came from behind to secure a 1-1 Championship draw with Southampton at Bramall Lane.

Lewis Dobbin had given the visitors an early lead but the Blades stepped up a gear after the break and finally found a way past Saints goalkeeper Daniel Peretz through Peck.

The equaliser saw the Blades move within a point of the top eight and remain two behind their sixth-placed opponents.

United have lost just once in their last seven games while Southampton have only suffered one defeat in their last nine outings.

Southampton struck in the 10th minute when Dobbin cut inside from the left wing and fired into the far corner of Michael Cooper's net.

Shortly after, Dobbin tested Cooper with a shot from 25 yards as the Saints pushed for a second.

United's solitary first-half effort on goal came from Romelle Donovan, whose angled shot was comfortably dealt with by Daniel Peretz.

United were lacklustre in the opening period as they missed loanee Ben Brereton-Diaz, who scored twice at the weekend but was unable to face his parent club.

In a bid to strengthen his side's firepower, manager Chris Wilder introduced Patrick Bamford at the start of the second half with Tom Cannon making way.

It did not take long for Bamford to have his first chance, putting a close-range header over after Peck's corner had been headed towards goal by Mark McGuinness.

Peretz had to keep out a near-post header from Jordan Ayew as the hosts looked to capitalise on their improved second-half performance.

The Southampton keeper was also called into action to thwart Harrison Burrows, Tahith Chong and Peck. The pick of his saves was when he flung himself to his right to keep out Chong's shot while Peck was disappointed to head straight at Peretz.

Southampton's resistance was finally broken in the final minute of the game when Ayew wriggled past his marker on the right byline and pulled the ball back to Peck, who slotted beyond Peretz for his fifth goal in four games.

Substitute Samuel Edozie spurned a great chance to score a winner for Southampton in added time. He was put through on goal by Finn Azaz and looked certain to score but Cooper stuck out a leg to divert the ball wide.

A Southampton winner would have been very harsh on the Blades who deserved at least a point from the game after dominating the second period.

"It was after 25 minutes of the first half. You know, they've come with a different shape, they've got really technical players.

"We didn't get to grips with their shape, they had control of the game, really technical, possession-based players. Seven touches in our box, first half.

"We had to get to grips with their press, our press, which we did after half an hour and started to get a foothold in the game and wrestle back a little bit of momentum because, yeah, they were on top.

"So we said to the players, 'need to be brave, need to wrestle this back. Game's not done, we're not three or four down, we're not out of sight, and we need to just be a little bit better in possession, get that press right', which we did.

"And the ball came back to us, and then off we went, started creating opportunities, being really positive, driving.

"I think it would have been the cruellest of cruel blows if they'd have nicked a winner. And I think we definitely deserve something from the game with the attitude and the way we played, and how brave we were."

"As I can remember, it's been one of the best first halves that we've had away from home in a long, long time.

"I think it's a very good start into the season, I think, in a long, long time. So I think very good start into the game. Went up on a great finish from Lewis. And I think throughout the first half, he had complete control.

"And then it is what it is. Like these games, they need a second goal at one point. I think we had the chance to score one in the first half. Then we went into half-time and we knew that the game is going to be completely different.

"For me, in the second half, it's the same thing. If you don't score a second one, then the game is just open until the very end.

"Then you need to bring it over the line, and we didn't manage to do that today.

"I think the game today needed a second goal from us, and we had the chance when we were up, we had the chance when we were drawing."
