CorSport: Cardinale goes ‘all-in’ – Milan’s strategy risky with squad costs already up €40m

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Owner Gerry Cardinale is ready to put his money where his mouth is when it comes to building an AC Milan team that can win trophies.

Cardinale has already spoken publicly about his desire to strengthen the squad available to Ruben Amorim with some further reinforcements in January. While money will not be spent for the sake of it, the American seems infatuated by Endrick, and he certainly will not come cheap.

Corriere dello Sport (via PianetaMilan) confirm this morning that Milan are preparing for ‘significant investment’ in the January window, but they also take a look at the Rossoneri’s financial situation, trying to determine whether these costs are sustainable.

Looking at the summer transfer window, €160m was invested on incomings with the club-record signing of Goncalo Ramos as well as the additions of Mario Gila and Diego Moreira. Another €70m could be spent in the winter on Endrick, making it €230m in 2026-27.

Then, two renewals were closed, while work is still underway for Adrien Rabiot. Christian Pulisic went from €3.5m to €5m net per season, while Strahinja Pavlovic went from €1.7m to €3m net per season.

As the newspaper reports, the cost of the squad has already increased by €40m this season. With these investments and without any major sales, the 2026-27 budget could close with a deficit of €140-150m.

The club have room to keep the squad ratio under control, but sporting results will be needed to support it. Otherwise, there’s a risk of ending up under another Settlement Agreement with UEFA, which brings further constraints.

A full-back and centre-back have been tipped to arrive too, which would make things even more precarious. Corriere dello Sport conclude that Cardinale’s move is risky, and it will require almost guaranteed Champions League qualification for the year.
