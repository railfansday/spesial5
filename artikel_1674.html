LAFC relishing home advantage against St. Louis in Game 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Los Angeles FC defender Sergi Palencia believes his side can benefit from home advantage in Game 2 against St. Louis City SC in the MLS Cup playoffs.

St. Louis won the opening matchup in the best-of-three Round One series 3-2 at Energizer Park, already putting LAFC in a must-win situation.

Palencia was one of the goalscorers for LAFC, while Denis Bouanga also netted from the penalty spot in the defeat.

The Spaniard has urged his team-mates to put the defeat behind them and instead embrace the home encounter on Saturday.

"It's one game, but it's at home," said Palencia. "We have to be positive.

"There's some positives [from Game 1], and there's some things that we have to learn, but we're sure, with our people, we can win the next game and then let's see. Our full focus has to be on that.

"Saturday is a fresh start. They'll likely make some adjustments, and we will too. We'll also look to improve by analysing what didn't go quite right, with the goal of securing a win."

St. Louis' Game 1 win was their first-ever victory in MLS playoff history, and it was sealed by a brilliant goal from Simon Becher.

City boss Yoann Damet heaped praise on Becher, as well as his side's humility in key moments.

"He's had a great season in terms of how much he's grown and also his output," Damet said about Becher.

"What's special in our locker room is there are a lot of [selfless guys], and you can see that Simon is coming in [and] having an impact."

Becher added: "I think the group is really comfortable pushing each other.

"The guys that are in the 11, the guys that are on the bench, and the guys that aren't on the bench… knowing their roles and coming on to help however they can."

Playing in his 16th career MLS Cup playoff match in Game 1, Bouanga scored his 11th career postseason goal.

The only players in MLS history with more playoff goals are Landon Donovan (25), Carlos Ruiz (16), Roy Lassiter (13) and Jaime Moreno (12).

In Game 1, Jeong Sang-bin became the second South Korean player to score a goal in the MLS Cup playoffs, joining LAFC's Son Heung-min (three goals in last year's playoffs).

The Opta supercomputer has backed LAFC to get the job done on home soil, but St. Louis are in much better form.

St. Louis' victory in Game 1 was their second straight win over LAFC after the club did not win any of their first seven matches in the fixture. St. Louis have scored five goals over the two victories compared to two total goals in their seven previous meetings.

LAFC are in the midst of a three-game losing streak and a five-match winless run. Their longest ever losing streak in MLS play (regular season and playoffs) is four games, something the team have only done once (August 2021).

St. Louis, meanwhile, have now won 15 of their last 25 MLS matches (regular season and playoffs, D6 L4). In the club's previous 25 MLS matches immediately prior to their current run, City had six victories (D5 L14).
