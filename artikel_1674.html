Andoni Iraola: Liverpool cannot ‘sustain the level’ I want

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Andoni Iraola has conceded that Liverpool cannot currently “sustain the level” he expects after his side squandered a two-goal lead for the second match in a row.

In the Spaniard’s first game at Anfield since becoming manager, Monaco fought back to win 3-2 by virtue of Paris Brunner’s late glancing header seven days on from Leeds United’s 4-2 victory in Chicago, emphasising Liverpool’s alarming lack of depth, particularly in defence.

“We played a good first half, especially a good 30 minutes. Right now we probably don’t have much more, in terms of the levels required to play like this. They deserved to turn around the game.

“We won the first two pre-season games, but the last two we cannot sustain the level we want for all of the game. As soon as we made some changes, players start to get tired and we cannot sustain that level. So, we have work to do.”

Alisson, Virgil van Dijk and Cody Gakpo all made their first appearances and contributed to a lively first-half in which Iraola’s commitment to man-to-man marking and an intensive pressing structure were evident out of possession. With the ball in the first half they demonstrated the kind of slick passing that Bournemouth employed to carve opponents open on the counter-attack.

A nimble one-two between Ryan Gravenberch and Dominik Szoboszlai allowed the Dutch midfielder to set up Alexander Isak to score with a shot from 16 yards and the most expensive player in English football history laid claim to the second, too, when he seemed to hook in a rebound from Lukas Hradecky’s save while lying in the six-yard box.

Later he would graciously acknowledge that he and Florian Wirtz had connected with the ball simultaneously and he was happy to yield the credit to his team-mate.

At that point it seemed that victory would be a cakewalk but just before half-time Aleksandr Golovin caught Van Dijk out with a feint and late swerve to the right, tripping over the Liverpool captain’s clumsy hack. Despite a heavy limp, Golovin hammered the penalty past Alisson’s dive to make it 2-1 at the break.

Iraola rationed his early changes at the start of the second half to two but Liverpool could not regain their rhythm and Mika Biereth capitalised on a Gravenberch error to score the equaliser after battling to be first to the rebound when Giorgi Mamardashvili saved his first effort, stroking his effort through Wataru Endo’s legs.

Cue five substitutions as soon as that goal had gone in and a raft more followed, killing the remaining half-hour of all momentum and cohesion. By the time Brunner exploited a dozy mix-up between Milos Kerkez and Federico Chiesa about whose responsibility he was at a corner to flash his header past Mamardashvili, only two of the 22 starters were still on the pitch.

Good afternoon and welcome to live coverage of the first of two Liverpool home friendlies after their preseason tour of the United States. Andoni Iraola’s side saw off Wrexham and Sunderland in their first two matches but gave away a two-goal lead at half-time in the final fixture and ended up being beaten 4-2 by Leeds United in Chicago in the last game after multiple changes. What did we learn? Not a huge amount in the absence of Virgil van Dijk, Alisson, Víctor Muñoz, Cody Gakpo and Alexis Mac Allister, the first four of whom are available today other than an ongoing vulnerability from set-pieces and Curtis Jones looking like he is in desperate need of a fresh start.

This afternoon at Anfield they take on AS Monaco who finished seventh in Ligue 1 this season but are under new management with the recruitment of the former Chelsea and Atlético left-back Filipe Luís as head coach. It’s quite the coup for Le Rocher given he led Flamengo to their first Brazilian title in five years, the Copa Libertadores and the Copa do Brasil in his 18 months in charge.

In pre-season so far he has overseen a 5-2 win over fourth-tier AS Saint-Priest, a 2-0 defeat by Sporting Clube, a 2-2 draw with Cercle Bruges and a 1-0 victory over Getafe. They have sold their best player, Maghnes Akliouche, to PSG for £43m and Aladji Bamba to Newcastle for £34m but have so far retained Mamadou Coulibaly and Folarin Balogun, signed and made Ansu Fati’s loan move into a long-term deal.

Iraola may well persevere with Trey Nyoni in midfield who, after his performances on tour, ought to be on the threshold of a breakthrough season and it would be interesting to see him paired with Ryan Gravenberch just behind Dominik Szoboszlai.

Ifeanyi Ndukwe, who played well in America after coming on for the injured Joe Gomez, does not have a UK work permit and will have to go abroad on loan for the season but Jérémy Jacquet, who has yet to feature, was due to have a fitness test this morning and would be a welcome to boost to a squad whose defence still requires significant reinforcements.
