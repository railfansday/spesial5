Man City’s blip in form ‘normal’ under new manager, says Maresca

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Enzo Maresca remains unfazed by Manchester City's current form, saying it is "not easy to suddenly change" after 10 years under Pep Guardiola.

City have failed to win any of their last four Premier League games (D1 L3), losing ground on leaders Arsenal, after being sat top following a seven-game winning streak to start the season.

They are now looking to put that form behind them when they host Napoli in the Champions League on Tuesday.

City sit seventh in the table with three wins from four so far (L1) and are looking to win three consecutive group/league-phase games in the Champions League for the first time since the 2023-24 season, when they won all six under Guardiola.

And Maresca is not worried by their recent struggles, saying it is all part of the process as they continue to get used to his management.

"It's not complicated, it's just a matter of getting to know each other," Maresca said.

"I need to understand who is the ideal one for these kinds of games, different kinds of games. It's not just midfield; we are trying with defensive players.

"We are trying to find the right solution. For me, it's part of the process; this is like a reset, a restart after 10 years with the same manager, same face, same words, same method. It's not easy to suddenly change.

"For me, it's normal; hopefully, as soon as possible, we can find the right balance between defence and midfield, and we can be consistent. That's the difference in this moment between us and other clubs."

All five previous meetings between City and Napoli have been in the group/league phase of the Champions League.

After failing to win home and away against Napoli in 2011-12 (D1 L1), City have won each of the last three encounters.

Maresca lost his only previous European game against an Italian side (Atalanta 2-1 Chelsea in December 2025 in the Champions League). In his managerial career overall, he has only won four of his 15 matches against Italian opposition (D5 L6).

"We are always looking to win games no matter the competition," he added.

"For us, the four games in a row in the Premier League that we didn't win have been a shame, but we have the chance to win and continue our path in the Champions League, which has been very good.

"It's important to qualify; it's very important. Also, if you are not able to finish top eight, you have two more games – with the amount of games we have, it's important to try to reduce games by finishing in the top eight."

Kevin De Bruyne could make his return to the Etihad Stadium for the first time since leaving at the end of the 2024-25 season.

He enjoyed a trophy-laden 10 years with City and was part of their treble-winning squad in 2022-23, helping the club clinch their first Champions League title.

"Fantastic, unbelievable season [winning the treble with De Bruyne in 2023]," Maresca said when asked about De Bruyne's return.

"He is a top player, has been one of the most important players in this club. We are very happy to see him back."
