Arsenal player ratings vs Slavia Prague: 9/10 statement performance in nervy Champions League win

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal survived a late goal to beat Slavia Prague 2-1 and continue their strong start in the Champions League.

Noni Madueke opened the scoring after charging through on goal in behind and Viktor Gyokeres, who assisted that opener, then got on the scoresheet himself with a wonderful curled finish.

At that stage the Gunners were cruising, but they dropped off significantly in the second half and allowed Slavia to grow in confidence and get back into the game.

Wiktor Nowak scored in the 90th minute as Slavia threatened a grandstand finish but Arsenal did enough to hold on and move up to third in the league-phase standings.

Here is how Matt Verri rated the Arsenal players...
