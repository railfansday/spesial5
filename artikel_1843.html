'I'll get him a ticket' - Kevin Blackwell hopes to see Neil Warnock at Crawley Town game soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Blackwell, 67, was appointed manager until the end of the season last week.

And the 67-year-old marked his appointment with a crucial victory over Fleetwood Town at the Broadfield Stadium on Saturday (November 21) – the Reds’ first win at home since March.

Blackwell and Warnock go way back, having coached alongside each other at a number of EFL clubs.

With the Reds travelling to Exeter City on Saturday (November 28), Blackwell was asked if he thought Warnock might be in the crowd.

“I speak to Neil regularly,” the Reds boss said. “We've been together for nearly 35 years, player and coach. So, we have a good chat.

“Myself and Neil have ended up, [for] the last seven or eight years, firefighting at Cardiff [City], at Rotherham [United], at Middlesbrough, where we've gone into this situation that we're in now, so we have a good idea.

“And when he rings, we have a chat about, ‘do you remember that time at Rotherham when that happened and we did that? Do you remember that [time] at Middlesbrough and we did that?’ So, we have a little chat. I've known him for 45 years, so I'm sure he'll be there at the Exeter game. I'll get him a ticket anyway.”

Before that trip to Exeter on Saturday, the Reds welcome high-flying Barnet to the Broadfield Stadium on Tuesday night (November 24).

Blackwell’s side remain bottom of League Two and three points adrift of safety but have at least two games in hand on many of their closest rivals.
