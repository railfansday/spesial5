Bayern Munich confirm Harry Kane surgery as England captain issues statement from hospital bed

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

England captain Harry Kane has undergone successful surgery on an ankle ligament injury which is expected to keep him sidelined for three months.

The recently crowned Ballon d’Or winner started for Bayern Munich as they drew with Cologne on Friday evening, but was forced off after just 20 minutes.

He was caught in a late tackle by Joel Schmied as he looked to latch onto a Michael Olise cross and immediately went down clutching his lower leg. He attempted to play on, but after spending a few minutes as a virtual bystander, he signalled to the bench that he could not continue.

It was confirmed at full-time he had suffered a tear to the syndesmotic ligament in his ankle, with Bayern head coach Vincent Kompany predicting he would miss around three months.

And on Saturday morning the club confirmed he had undergone successful surgery on the injury, with the ball now rolling on a lengthy period of rehabilitation.

Kane later shared a photo of himself on the edge of a hospital bed, bearing a broad grin and brandishing two thumbs-ups despite wearing a protective boot on his left foot.

He wrote: “The highs and lows of life! Obviously very disappointed to get this injury but as always it’s an opportunity to show resilience and improvement going into 2027. I’ll be back fresh and ready to go for the business end of the season!

“‘Tough times don’t last, tough people do’ - my guy [NFL legend Julian Edelman] told me this one!

“Thank you for all the messages! Alles gut!”

Ankle injuries plagued Kane throughout his 14-year stint at Tottenham, but he has largely managed to stay fit since moving to Munich in 2023.

It remains to be seen whether Kane will be fit in time to lead England out in the Nations League quarter-finals in March 2027. The two legs of that tie will be played on March 27 and 30.

The projected three-month rehabilitation period suggests he will be back in contention in late February, and Thomas Tuchel will hope he can get back fit and firing in time to don the captain’s armband in Oslo.

Kane has been fielding plenty of well wishes. England’s official Instagram account replied to his post confirming his succesful surgery, writing in a comment: “Wishing you a speedy recovery, Harry!”

Chelsea and England forward Morgan Rogers has also joined Joshua Kimmich, Manuel Neuer and Dayot Upamecano in sending their support to the 33-year-old.
