Prediction: Real Madrid vs Celta Vigo

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Marking their return from November’s international break on Saturday night, Real Madrid will welcome Celta Vigo to the Bernabeu.

With the rest of Europe’s elite domestic leagues making their own respective returns this weekend, there are plenty of eye-catching betting promotions available.

Ahead of Saturday’s showdown from the Bernabeu, you can find all of the key Real Madrid vs Celta Vigo H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, while there might have bene plenty of raised eyebrows across the continent following Jose Mourinho’s blockbuster return over the summer, Real Madrid are clearly relishing life under the former Manchester United boss. Last seen heading into November’s international break with a routine 3-0 victory away at Valencia, Los Blancos will simply be looking to carry on from where they left off prior to the fortnight interval. Signing off October with a sensational 2-1 win against arch-rivals and reigning La Liga champions Barcelona, Real Madrid have won each of their previous eight consecutive appearances across all competitions. Likewise, flying out of the traps since opening their European adventure, Mourinho’s superstar squad have also continued to shine with their performances in the final third. In fact, the Spanish icons have notched 23 goals on their eight-match winning run.

While Brazilian international Rodrygo might be working his way back to full fitness after sustaining a long-term knee injury earlier in the year, the 25-year-old is not expected to feature until January. However, now finishing his controversial four-match La Liga suspension, Vinicius Junior will return to Mourinho’s squad here.

Finding the net against Valencia prior to November’s international break and registering 13 goals in his 12 La Liga appearances this season, French skipper Kylian Mbappe will lead the line in Madrid. Making an immediate impact since completing a £100million move from RB Leipzig, Yan Diomande will join Mbappe in the final third.

As for the visitors, while Celta Vigo might have entered November’s fortnight interval by extending on their Copa del Rey adventure thanks to a routine 5-0 victory away at fourth-tier outfit Salamanca, Claudio Giraldez’s squad have drastically failed to find their feet among Spain’s elite. Although the Sky Blues might mark their return this weekend sat just one-point adrift of breaking into the top-half of the La Liga standings, some in Celta’s squad could be looking over their shoulder at a potential relegation tussle. Signing off October with a thumping 3-0 defeat against Rayo Vallecano, Giraldez’s men have mustered just a sole win from any of their previous five straight La Liga appearances. However, it should be highlighted, the Sky Blues did land an iconic 2-0 victory when they last travelled to the 15-time European champions back in December 2025.

Undergoing knee surgery back in September, Moroccan youngster Jones El Abdellaoui remains on the sidelines. Likewise, while Swedish international Carl Starfelt might be inching towards a maiden La Liga appearance of the season, the former Celtic defender remains a major doubt on Saturday night.

Now in his third campaign at Balaidos, one-time Real Madrid starlet Marcos Alonso will start at the back. Despite notching just a pair of La Liga strikes so far this season, academy graduate Pablo Duran should keep his spot leading Giraldez’s attack this weekend.

Heading into November’s International break in the midst of a sensational eight-match winning run, Real Madrid will simply be looking to carry on from where they left off prior to the fortnight interval. Marking their return on Saturday night sat three points adrift of their arch-rivals, Mourinho’s men are riding a serious wave of momentum. Clearly relishing life under the former Manchester United boss and gaining a reputation for their free-scoring performances in the final third, Madrid will be confident of laying down another title marker.
