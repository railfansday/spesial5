Manchester United identify three Premier League signings for Mauricio Pochettino in January transfer window

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Manchester United have identified three potential defensive signings for Mauricio Pochettino in the January transfer window, according to reports.

Pochettino arrived at United’s training ground on Wednesday afternoon to begin his stint as head coach following the decision to sack Michael Carrick last week.

United have faced criticism over their transfer activity in the summer as they spent £155 million on signing three midfielders in Youri Tielemans, Andrey Santos and Carlos Baleba.

Pochettino now arrives at Old Trafford short of options with striker Benjamin Sesko sidelined until next year with a shin injury, while Luke Shaw has missed United’s last seven fixtures.

According to Sky Sports, United are willing to bolster Pochettino’s squad in the January window and the club will consult the Argentine over any potential signings.

The report claims that Everton centre-back Jarrad Branthwaite and Fulham’s Calvin Bassey are two players who United are considering for January.

United had two bids rejected for Branthwaite in 2024, including an offer of £50 million, and held talks with the defender’s representatives earlier this year.

The report adds that Fulham left-back Antonee Robinson is also one of United’s options and the 29-year-old has already played under Pochettino with the United States national team.

Elsewhere, reports in Spain claim that Barcelona left-back Alejandro Balde is open to a move to United in the upcoming transfer window.

United had considered a deal for Balde in the summer but the 23-year-old wanted to fight for his place at Barca.

However, Balde has not started a game in La Liga or the Champions League this season and Barcelona remain open to selling the defender.
