No progress for Pilgrims as Lewes Women win well at the Pan

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

An early goal from former Pilgrim Ella Stephens set Lewes on their way. and a set-piece goal either side of half-time allowed them to see out the victory.

Lewes started positively with Stephens, a summer transfer from Plymouth, pushed higher up into attack for this game.

The decision paid dividends inside 10 minutes as she followed in a Paula Howells’ effort to tap home the opener for her third league goal of the season.

Bethany Kaluya opened her account for Lewes just before half-time, rising highest to head in a Howells’ corner.

Lenny Priest sealed the win in the 77th minute, again from a corner, nudging the ball over the line after Giles had misjudged the flight.

The result and performance was in stark contrast to last season where Plymouth won all three encounters with Lewes without as much as conceding a goal.

After missing out on automatic promotion to the WSL2 and then going on to lose the play-off final 1-0 to Wolves, Plymouth have since reevaluated their ambitions within the league.

After letting almost all of their players go in the summer, including Dalton, Stephens and Lue to Lewes, they now sit at the bottom of the FAWNL South with one win to their name.

The starting 11 that faced Lewes this time round had an average age just short of 20 years old and despite their plucky spirit, they look set to be relegated come the end of the season.

That context is key to the match this past weekend, with Lewes not getting out of first gear for the entirety of the 90 minutes.

Rooks forward Emma Jones could have scored a hat trick by half-time, spurning several chances to make the result a lot more reflective of the one sided nature of the game.

Poulter in goal for Lewes, did not have a single save to make. Although the 545 fans in attendance would have liked to have seen more goals, they will be more than happy with the three points that take Lewes up to 5th at the halfway point in the season.

The coaching staff will also be happy with the control that the squad demonstrated throughout and will be looking to take the momentum into three important December fixtures.

They welcome Exeter to the Dripping Pan on December 6 before travelling to AFC Wimbledon in the FA Cup 3rd Round on Sunday 13.

The 4th round will see the introduction of WSL1 sides such as Arsenal, Chelsea and Manchester City. The prospect of hosting one of these top clubs will hopefully provide enough motivation for Lewes to better last month’s 0-0 draw at Wimbledon.

Lewes host Dartford in the League Plate on December 20.
