Man City ‘close’ to major injury boost as Enzo Maresca reveals latest team news before Arsenal

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Antoine Semenyo is close to returning for Manchester City, Enzo Maresca has confirmed.

The Ghana international has not featured for Man City since sustaining a knee injury in the 3-2 victory over Ipswich on October 17, missing the last eight games across all competitions as a result.

During this period, City have not won a Premier League game and head to the Emirates Stadium on Sunday to face leaders and reigning champions Arsenal.

Semenyo is not expected to be available for the trip to north London, though he could return against either Leeds or Brentford within the next week following a positive update from Maresca.

“We have good news, Antoine is quite close,” Maresca said in Friday’s pre-match press conference. “It’s good news for us. He is already on the pitch - he is very close, hopefully it can be next week, we hope so.

“I was laughing with him yesterday or two days ago. I told him in the Premier League the last game we won was with him on the pitch, so I said we need him back soon to win games.

“Antoine is an important player but we have more players in his position that are doing well like Iliman in this moment. But he needs time to recover.”

Semenyo had been enjoying a decent start to his first full season as a Man City player before being struck down by injury, scoring twice and weighing in with three assists in seven appearances.

The winger also started City’s big away wins over Manchester United and Liverpool, two results that has boosted Maresca’s belief that his side can end their recent slump with victory at Arsenal.

He added: “I think we already showed this season we are able to win important games, to win away. So I don’t see any reason why we are not able to win there.

“We have shown this season we can win important games away. Old Trafford and Anfield are two important away games and Sunday we have another one.

“We showed already and we can win again.”
