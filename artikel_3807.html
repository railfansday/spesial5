It’s probably not news to a lot of players that there is a way to play WoW with a controller, and we have ConsolePort and other add-ons to thank for that. But Forever is the official instance in which Blizzard is adding native controller support to WoW, so now it’s much easier to just sit back up, grab a joystick, and explore Azeroth without hunching in front of the PC.

Switching to your controller is rather straightforward. Just go to the game menu, find Options, then Gameplay. You should see Gamepad in there with a checkbox. Tick that setting to switch to controller input, and untick it to switch back to keyboard-and-mouse.
