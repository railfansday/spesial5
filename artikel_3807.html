Coral Island for Switch 2 launches December 7 alongside version 1.4 update

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Switch 2 version of simulation game Coral Island will launch on December 7 alongside the game’s version 1.4 update, publisher Balor Games and developer Stairway Games announced.

Coral Island first launched in Early Access for PC via Steam and Microsoft Store on October 11, 2022, followed by a full release across PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC via Steam and Microsoft Store on November 14, 2023.

Here are the latest details, via Balor Games:

Coral Island on Nintendo Switch 2 invites players to leave city life behind, build their dream farm and get to know a vibrant island community with plenty to discover both on land and beneath the waves. Players can settle into island life solo or with friends in multiplayer for up to four players, with cross-play across supported platforms.

Launching alongside the Nintendo Switch 2 release, the free 1.4 “Kids Grow Up” Update brings new family milestones, cutscenes, and interactions as children grow from babies to toddlers, school-age kids, and teenagers, developing unique personalities and interests of their own. The update also adds new cosmetics, user interface improvements, fishing bobbers unlocked through Fishing Mastery, lighting enhancements, and other quality-of-life improvements.
