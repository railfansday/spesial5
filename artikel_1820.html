What happened last time Mauricio Pochettino interviewed for Man Utd job?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Mauricio Pochettino has long been of interest to Manchester United, having twice previously interviewed for the manager job.

Pochettino continues to enjoy a stellar reputation in the Premier League thanks to his work at Tottenham over five-and-a-half years, during which time he twice came close to winning the Premier League title and took Spurs to a Champions League final.

The Argentine's time with Southampton, previous to Spurs, was also eye-catching, while his one-season spell with Chelsea, which ended two years ago, showed signs of promise before his mutually agreed exit.

So it was Pochettino's success at Spurs which twice previously attracted interest internally at Manchester United.

And as Pochettino emerges as the favourite to become the new Man Utd manager, we look back on the previous times he has been sounded out for the job.

Man Utd reportedly came calling in late 2018, following the sacking of Jose Mourinho.

According to The Mirror, Pochettino - who at the time had Spurs in third place in the Premier League standings and was six months away from guiding them to the Champions League final - was a standout candidate and had the approval of Sir Alex Ferguson.
