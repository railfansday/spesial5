Ref Watch: Three Szymon Marciniak blunders in Sporting vs Man United

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Correspondent AUTHORITY Football journalist with experience at Reach PLC and PA Media; University of Central Lancashire graduate. FOCUS Fan-engagement content and match analysis across the Premier League and Championship. THE INSIGHT Oliver utilises a network of club and industry contacts to deliver verified reporting and fan-first content. He provides the human stories behind the results to ensure fans get the full picture as it happens.

Szymon Marciniak was the centre of attention as Man United were held to a 2-2 draw by Sporting CP in the Champions League.

Mauricio Pochettino’s side conceded an early penalty as Lisandro Martinez clipped Geny Catamo, but Senne Lammens saved Luis Suarez’s weak spot-kick.

Lammens then brought down Maxi Araujo in the box just over a minute later, and Suarez made no mistake with his second penalty.

Bruno Fernandes equalised after the break from the spot and then put the visitors ahead soon after, but Suarez bagged his brace late on.

Youri Tielemans was sent off in the closing stages as Marciniak refereed in a completely bizarre manner throughout.

Martinez was left dumbfounded when Marciniak pointed to the spot in the 29th minute following his challenge on Catamo.

It certainly looked contentious and he went down easily, and replays showed that there was minimal contact and the Sporting winger was searching for a foul to then go down.

The Polish official bought his theatrics and gave Sporting a penalty, so it was just as well that Lammens saved it.

One of the worst decisions seen in a long time came five minutes before the break as Tielemans was adjudged to have fouled Goncalo Inacio before Amad levelled the scoreline.

The Belgian midfielder slid in but did not even touch his opponent as he played Amad in before he eventually forced the ball home on the rebound.

Marciniak incredibly blew his whistle for a foul just moments before the ball crossed the goal line, meaning VAR could not get involved.

It was never a foul and should have stood, with United rightly feeling robbed and Tielemans booked for a foul that never was.

United's bad luck continued as they were robbed of a late winner by Marciniak and his officials.

Fernandes' corner was met by Sporting goalkeeper Pereira as he diverted the ball into his own net but quickly complained of being fouled.

None of the Red Devils players did anything to impede him, yet the referee reviewed the goal and incredibly ruled it out.
