Arsenal boss Slegers wary of Lyon’s athletic qualities ahead of showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Renee Slegers warned her Arsenal players that they will come up against an "athletic" Lyon side when they square off in their Champions League encounter on Thursday.

The Mangata Developments Stadium Meadow Park is the venue for the clash between two sides that have yet to taste defeat in this season's competition.

Arsenal are eighth in the 18-team standings with eight points from their opening four fixtures, two fewer than Lyon, who have the opportunity to top the table with a victory.

Slegers' side come into the match following a 1-1 draw with London City Lionesses in the Women's Super League, while Lyon have nine wins from nine in the Premiere Ligue, keeping them five points clear of Paris Saint-Germain.

This will be the 12th meeting between Arsenal and Lyon in the Champions League, with the French side winning seven of the previous 11 encounters (D1 L3).

Indeed, their number of victories are the most against the Gunners in the competition, and Slegers acknowledged the difficulties that await her team.

"They are three different teams, so Barcelona work with very close relationships, close connections on the pitch," Slegers said.

"London City was a little different in that sense, but there were similarities. What they do similarly is very high and aggressive pressure, jump pressure.

"Lyon are a very athletic team as well, they have a lot of fast players, so we'll have to manage that really well and defend that really well, and make sure that we're in good areas on the pitch and have numbers around the ball.

"It will be a different game, it's also a different pitch. So what we'll do to turn that into a win is do our very best to put a good plan together that the players are going to execute.

"This game is different in that sense. I think we'll have to manage their speed in this game and look at our chances as well."

Arsenal will have confidence, though, given they have won their last seven home games in the Champions League, including two wins without conceding this season.

However, each of Arsenal's last two home defeats in the competition came against Lyon, who boast an impressive defensive record in Europe so far in 2026-27.

Lyon are one of two sides yet to concede a goal in the Champions League this season (also Chelsea), but they have also been sensational in attack.

They have scored 20 goals, the joint-most along with Barcelona. Lyon's +20 goal difference is the best four games into a UWCL campaign since Barca in 2023-24 (also +20).

And key to that scoring form has been Marie-Antoinette Katoto. She has scored seven goals in the Champions League this season, the most by a player four rounds into a UWCL season since both Vivianne Miedema (10) and Ada Hegerberg (nine) in 2019-20.
