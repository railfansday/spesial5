Guardiola ‘felt embarrassed’ after turning down Italy

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Pep Guardiola revealed he "felt embarrassed" after turning down the chance to become Italy head coach in the summer.

Guardiola left Manchester City at the end of the 2025-26 season after 10 years at the helm, with his trophy-laden stint including six Premier League titles and the club's first Champions League.

He took charge of 593 games at City, his most at any club, winning 416 of those matches (D87 L90) and seeing his side score 1,423 goals.

Italy, meanwhile, were on the hunt for a new head coach after their failure to qualify for the 2026 World Cup saw Gennaro Gattuso step down from the role.

The Azzurri have failed to qualify for the last three World Cups, leading to Guardiola and Brazil boss Carlo Ancelotti being approached for the job.

Paolo Maldini and Leonardo led the search for the Italian federation, though they failed to acquire their targets before eventually resigning, which led to the reappointment of Roberto Mancini.

Guardiola admitted he was "embarrassed" to turn down the offer, but said it came too soon for him after his City departure.

"This is the first time I have spoken about this, but yes, I did talk to them," Guardiola told former Barcelona team-mate Romario on Romario TV.

"I felt embarrassed to say 'no' to Italy, a country like Italy, it would have been one of the greatest honours of my career to coach the Italian national team. But I realised I needed to stop.

"If they had called two years earlier, I probably would have accepted. But when Paolo Maldini and Leonardo, whom I love dearly, reached out, it was at a point where I simply had to stop.

"I needed some time for myself. After 18 years as a coach, with matches every three days, week in and week out, there comes a moment when you just have to say 'phew.'

"For now, I'm doing fine. If I ever return, it will be because my gut tells me to, and because someone wants me, then I will go back. But it's been many years, so many matches."

Guardiola has recently been mooted as an ambitious target for the United States men's national team, after Mauricio Pochettino's departure to Manchester United.
