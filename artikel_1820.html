Belgium aim to keep their hopes of advancing in the Nations League alive when they face Turkey this evening.

Group A1 is lead by World Cup semi-finalists France with 10 points from four games, closely followed by Italy in second.

As such, Belgium need a positive result at the Gursel Aksel Stadium to keep within touching distance of the Italians heading into the final matchday, when the two teams do battle in Brussels.

Mark van Bommel’s side trail Italy by one point, though can also reel in France if Les Bleus were to lose both matches during this round of international fixtures.

Turkey, meanwhile, require a win to keep their faint hopes of avoiding relegation alive, but the odds are mightily stacked against them after losing all four Nations League matches during the autumn break.

This is because the UEFA tiebreaker first looks at points won head-to-head when sides are locked on the same amount of points, and then total goal difference in the two games between equal opponents.
