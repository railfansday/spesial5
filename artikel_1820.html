'Fair play' - Former QPR striker makes Peterborough United comparison after Crawley Town owners' letter to fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Raphael Khalili and co penned the letter following the appointment of Kevin Blackwell as manager until the end of the season.

In the letter they said ‘take full responsibility for the direction of this football club’ and recognised ‘significant shift in direction was necessary’ and that ‘difficult decisions have now been made’.

They also gave updates on strengthening the club’s leadership and the ownership group.

This was the first time the fans had heard from the owners this season following their poor start under Colin Kazim-Richards’ management. Following the embarrassing FA Cup exit to seventh tier Wimborne Town, Reds fans called for the owners to come and say something – and a week and a half later they did.

And Washington, as part of his weekly EFL column for BestBettingBonuses.co.uk, said owners need to be honest and transparent, especially when things aren’t going well.

"Fair play to the owners for taking responsibility,” he said. “I think ultimately what most football fans want from their owners is honesty and transparency, particularly when things aren't going well. Darragh MacAnthony at Peterborough is a really good example of that.

"Whether you agree with everything he says or not, he's incredibly transparent about what's happening at the football club, and I think the majority of Peterborough fans appreciate that. It's easy to communicate with supporters when things are going well, but arguably it's more important to do so when they're not.

"Silence only creates speculation and discontent amongst the fanbase. The important thing now is that the owners follow through on what they've said and maintain that level of communication going forward.”

And Washington said he things Blackwell is a good appointment and believes he can turn things round. “I can understand the decision to go with someone like Kevin Blackwell until the end of the season. When things aren't going well, I think players can sometimes have more trust in someone with Kevin's experience than a younger, aspiring coach, regardless of how good a coach they might be. There's a reassurance in knowing your manager has been in these situations before.

"The Fleetwood result was massive, particularly given how long they'd gone without a home win. Kevin has now had a period of time to assess the squad, understand where the deficiencies are and identify what needs addressing in January. There's still a lot of football to be played, and if he can simplify things, restore some confidence and build on that result, there's every chance he can turn it around.”
