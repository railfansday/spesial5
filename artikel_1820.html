GdS: Cisse becomes a pillar as Amorim as experiments with four-man attack

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Alphadjo Cisse has definitely been the breakout player of the season so far and Ruben Amorim has no intention of dialling down his presence in attack.

Milan had a very clear plan when they headed into the current season: Goncalo Ramos would be the main man in attack. Instead, however, it’s the underdog Cisse who has been running the show, while the Portuguese striker has been at the bottom in terms of output.

As highlighted by Gazzetta dello Sport this morning (see image below), that’s why there cannot be – and likely won’t be – a Milan without Cisse in attack. Ruben Amorim now wants to build the attack around him, as opposed to introducing him every now and then.

The most recent clash, the 2-1 win against Genoa, was a good example of this. Amorim went for a bold formation with Cisse, Ramos, Christian Pulisic, Diego Moreira, Luka Modric and Adrien Rabiot together. A team with a lot of quality and an attacking-minded style.

It was a shape that reminded us of 2009-10, when Milan were guided by another foreign coach: Leonardo. He chose a very attacking-minded side with four attackers and two midfielders, which is something that Amorim almost replicated.

Modric and Rabiot in front of the defence (Pirlo and Ambrosini then) and the fantasy project with Pulisic, Moreira, Cisse, and Ramos, comparable to the Ronaldinho, Pato, Seedorf, and Borriello of the old days.

Despite this, the goals were scored after two mistakes by Genoa (even if Milan’s pressing helped), so the goal is to repeat the experiment against Frosinone and to increase its effectiveness, the experiment should be repeated tomorrow.

In that sense, Cisse will be important as he’s the top scorer: three goals in Europa League and five in Serie A. He has scored with both feet and his head, demonstrating a combination of talent and an eye for goal: characteristics that are very important for a team like Milan.

The average also speaks for itself: eight goals spread across 658 minutes means he scores a goal every 82 minutes.  Best of all, his versatility means that he can play as an attacking midfielder, a left-winger, and even as a centre-forward.

At just 20 years and 17 days old, thanks to the fifth goal in his ninth Serie A appearance, Cisse became the second-youngest player to reach the milestone in the first ten games with Milan, at least since 1994-95. Only Pato did it younger, at 18 years and 181 days.

Furthermore, he’s one of only three players born after 2006 (top five leagues) to have scored eight or more goals in all competitions, along with Lamine Yamal (13) and Paris Brunner, who also scored eight.

As the pink newspaper concludes, Ramos has also produced eye-catching numbers, but in this case in a negative sense: 1,191 minutes on the pitch, three goals scored, meaning one goal scored every 398 minutes.
