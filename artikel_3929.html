Xenoblade Chronicles 3 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition ‘Overview’ trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Publisher Nintendo and developer Monolith Soft have released a three-minute overview trailer for Xenoblade Chronicles 3 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition, which is due out on December 3.

Here is an overview of the game, via Nintendo:

In Xenoblade Chronicles 3 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition, you will join Noah and Mio, members of the opposing nations of Keves and Agnus, as they embark on a stirring journey through a war-torn world that harbors a dark secret. Master real-time RPG combat and enjoy newly voiced event scenes, enhanced 4K resolution in TV mode (with compatible television), full HD in handheld mode, and smoother, sharper gameplay at 60 frames per second.

On this adventure, you’ll command six characters, as well as a new mythical Hero called Shimmer—a speedy attacker who unleashes devastating rapid-fire combos and wields the power of Foresight to see the future. Lead your band against hordes of enemies, auto-attacking with each character’s distinct Arts and weapons. Pairs of characters can also Interlink to form a mighty Ouroboros capable of taking down the most threatening enemies with powerful Arts.

You can also test your strength against waves of enemies in the new Heroes’ Vault mode. Here, with each enemy you defeat, you’ll power up a gauge you can use to enhance your abilities and gain additional allies. But choose wisely—you’ll need to prepare well to defeat the almighty foe awaiting you at the end of each mission!
