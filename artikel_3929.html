Professor Layton and the New World of Steam digital pre-orders now available for PS5 and PC, international voice cast announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Digital pre-orders for Professor Layton and the New World of Steam are now available for PlayStation 5 and PC via Steam for $64.99 / £53.99 / €64.99 / ¥7,920 ahead of its launch on December 10, LEVEL-5 announced. Switch 2 and Switch pre-orders will open at a later date.

Pre-orders include the “Layton Series Soundtrack Selection,” a digital soundtrack featuring 11 pieces of music from the Professor Layton series. The tracks are accessible via the “Secret Content” menu in-game.

A new story in Professor Layton, the puzzle-filled fantasy adventure series that has sold over 20 million copies worldwide.

Set in America after the end of the Wild West era, this new adventure takes place in Steam Bison, a remarkable town of invention transformed by rapid advances in steam technology. But as something mysterious is taking place there, Professor Layton and Luke find themselves drawn into a new adventure.
