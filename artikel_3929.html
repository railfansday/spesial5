PlayStation Has No GOTY Nominee at The Game Awards for First Time in 12 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

For the first time in 12 years — since all the way back in 2014 — there is no Sony-published PlayStation title amongst The Game Awards' Game of the Year nominees.

In that time, Sony has won three Game of the Year awards at Geoff's annual show, making it the most successful publisher in the event's history.

But its over-a-decade streak of nominations will finally come to an end here in 2026, as none of its releases from this year have made the cut.

That includes God of War: Sons of Sparta, Marathon, MLB The Show 26, Saros, Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls, and Marvel's Wolverine.

In particular, Saros and Marvel Tokon got really strong reviews, but given just how stacked this year's competition is, it's no real surprise to see these relatively niche titles be left in the cold.

At the start of the year, many would have probably predicted that Wolverine would be in the mix — but a lukewarm critical reception has ultimately sealed its fate.
