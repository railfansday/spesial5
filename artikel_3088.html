OpenAI bugging suspect Alfozan pleads not guilty

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Saudi national charged with planting a listening device in the headquarters of OpenAI while he worked there pleaded not guilty on Friday during an appearance in San Francisco federal court.

Abdulrahman Alfozan, 35, was sent back to jail after the brief hearing. Alfozan has been held without bail since his arrest on a criminal complaint in early October.

He faces a maximum possible sentence of five years in prison if convicted in the case.

A grand jury indicted Alfozan on Oct. 27 on a single count of interception of oral communications, court records show.

Prosecutors say that he put a recording device in a television in an executive conference room at OpenAI's offices in San Francisco on Sept. 28.

Alfozan's lawyer declined to comment after the hearing before Magistrate Judge Peter Kang.

The judge ordered Alfozan to next appear in court on Nov. 17.

Alfozan is a venture partner in a Saudi Arabian venture capital firm, Raed Ventures.

"He currently serves as Engineering Lead at OpenAI, with a focus on AI Agents and the data platform," Alfozan's bio page on Raed's site says.

Prince Khalid Bin Bader Al Saud, a member of Saudi Arabia's royal family, is a venture partner in the same firm.

Alfozan's case comes as U.S. companies such as OpenAI are making rapid advancements in developing artificial intelligence agents, and as concerns grow about the potential theft of AI tech from those companies.In June, President Donald Trump, in an executive order on AI innovation, said it was U.S. policy "to protect American ingenuity and intellectual property from exploitation and theft by adversaries; and to cultivate America's advanced AI-enabled capabilities."Trump has had a close relationship with Saudi Arabia. But he has also taken steps during his second term in the White House to limit the number of visas for highly skilled foreign workers.OpenAI earlier this year held discussions with Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, the country's sovereign wealth fund, about investing in the company.

— Jordan Novet reported from San Francisco
