PLANTIQUE gives houseplants a little robot companion to express their needs

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

designed by karan aneja, the device turns soil moisture readings into expressive facial feedback, giving houseplants a playful way to communicate their needs.

PLANTIQUE by Karan Aneja is an interactive plant-monitoring device that translates environmental readings into expressive visual feedback. Combining a capacitive soil moisture sensor with a small OLED display, the orange, robot-like object gives houseplants a recognizable interface, replacing numerical readings with animated facial expressions.

The project explores how emotional visualization and familiar forms of communication might encourage people to pay closer attention to the living things around them. Plants constantly respond to changes in their environment, but these processes are largely invisible to the people who care for them. Soil moisture, light exposure, and other environmental conditions can influence their growth, yet understanding when a plant needs attention often requires observation and experience. PLANTIQUE approaches this communication, turning sensor information into a more approachable everyday interaction.

The device takes the form of a compact character with a rounded rectangular body, a flexible-looking neck connection, and three antenna-like protrusions extending from its head. Its black OLED screen displays turquoise facial expressions, giving the object a recognizable personality. A slender capacitive soil moisture sensor extends from its base into the soil, allowing the device to monitor moisture levels while remaining integrated into the pot of the plant.

PLANTIQUE’s changing expressions offer an immediate indication of the conditions detected by the sensor, making technical information easier to interpret without requiring users to understand the underlying measurements. The device introduces different character identities, including Zest, Sunny, and Sprout, each presented through variations in color and facial expression.
