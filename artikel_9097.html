Nan Goldin, working mothers in the art world, new London Museum’s ancient axe head—podcast

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

From breaking news and insider insights to exhibitions and events around the world, the team at The Art Newspaper picks apart the art world’s big stories with the help of special guests. An award-winning podcast hosted by Ben Luke.

An exhibition at the Hayward Gallery in London is showing new and recent work by Nan Goldin. It is curated by Rachel Thomas and Ben Luke talks to her about the show.

Nan Goldin's Still from You never did anything wrong, Part I (2024)

© Nan Goldin, courtesy the artist and Gagosian

A study on motherhood and discrimination in London’s commercial art world, published by the European Journal of Cultural Studies and The Art Working Parents Alliance last week, exposes a workplace culture in which mothering children often comes at considerable professional cost. The Art Newspaper’s contributing editor Anny Shaw speaks to the authors of the report, Kate McMillan and Cleo Roberts-Komireddi.

Kate McMillan (left) and Cleo Roberts Komireddi (right)

And this episode’s Work of the Week is a Neolithic jadeite polished axehead (4000-3500 BCE). The piece is one of thousands of exhibits in the new London Museum, formally the Museum of London, the first phase of which opens this weekend in the historic General Market in Smithfield, at the heart of the City of London. Our digital editor, Alexander Morrison, discusses the axe head with Kate Sumnall, Curator of Archaeology at the museum.

We discuss Matthew Barney, Igshaan Adams, Eileen Agar and Louise Bourgeois

What’s behind the troubles facing auction houses and galleries? Plus, Sasha Skochilenko recounts her experience of being arrested—and incarcerated—in Russia, and the story behind a 1937 Surrealist painting by Ernst

Plus, a protest-themed tenth edition of the Performa Biennial and a restored portrait by Hans Holbein the Younger
