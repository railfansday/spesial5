Where To Find Wild Ginseng In Crimson Desert

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Here are three of the best places to farm Wild Ginseng for your Spirit meter upgrade in Crimson Desert.

There are a lot of crafting materials in Crimson Desert. From the plethora of minerals used to refine your weapons to make them stronger to the various ingredients you'll hunt down to cook meals, you can easily miss some of the more obscure, but important materials in the game.

One of these is Wild Ginseng, which is used to further increase your Spirit meter beyond Level 8. However, Wild Ginseng isn't a plant you can find everywhere, as there are a few key locations you'll want to visit where it's guaranteed to spawn.

Updated on November 12, 2026, by Kaitlyn Peterson: This article has been updated and reformatted to make sure you can find all the information you need as quickly as possible.

If you're looking for Wild Ginseng, head north to Silver Wolf Mountain. Specifically, you'll want to go just southeast of the Abyss Gate right by the Longleaf Shade Outpost. When you warp here, begin climbing up the rocks until you reach the top and look for a small outcropping with several tall trees and some lavender plants.

Along the rocks, you'll want to look for some small red buds, as these are the Ginseng plants you are looking for. There are typically only two to three of them, but harvesting these plants can net you one to four individual pieces of Ginseng, which is helpful. Given the close proximity to the Abyss Gate and relative ease of access at all levels, this is one of the best spots to farm this material.

Sticking near the Silver Wolf Mountain, this time you'll want to head southwest of the mountain itself to a place called Longroots. Specifically, you will want to find the Abyss Gate that is overlooking this area, which is on top of a mountain (shown above). From here, you'll want to look west and jump down to the bottom of the mountain.

Now, turn and face the mountain you just hopped off and look for a small cave entrance that has a large tree just out front of it. This can be a little tricky to find since the area is covered in an incredibly dense fog. Once you find the cave entrance, make your way inside the cave and follow the path until you cross some water.

The final location I recommend going to if you need Ginseng is actually a street vendor in Pailune. However, to even unlock this person, you will need to progress through the main story until you complete Chapter 7. This is because the main city in Pailune is under the control of a hostile faction until this series of quests is beaten.

Once you've progressed through Chapter 7, go to the capital city of the Pailune region and then head to the northeast side. Specifically, you will want to go to the large temple where you fought Myurdin. Outside this temple, there will be a street vendor who has set up a small stall on the right side of the main doors.

In this vendor's inventory, there will be five Ginseng you can purchase for 1.50 Silver each. Since vendors refresh every 24 hours, this is a great place to consistently buy Ginseng if you have a lot of money to spend and don't feel like doing the legwork to harvest it.

Remember, you'll need a total of 10 Wild Ginseng to progress your Spirit meter, so if you hit all these locations, you should have enough without needing to go back and farm more.
