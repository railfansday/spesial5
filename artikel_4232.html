Three Xbox Games Are Shutting Down Their Servers In November & December 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

A few notable delistings on the way as well

November has officially arrived, and therefore we thought we'd bring you our latest roundup of games getting delisted over the next few weeks, with this roundup specifically focusing on November and December 2026.

The good news is that there's not much to talk about! Call of Duty: Warzone is undoubtedly the most notable of the bunch here, which has been set to go offline on Xbox One and PS4 for some time. They originally announced that it would go offline when Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 began, and that's now officially set for November 6th, 2026.

Beyond that, NBA 2K25 is losing its servers at the end of the year, while Synduality: Echo of Ada is turning off its in-game purchases in December ahead of a planned full server closure in May of 2027.

Beyond this, we also wanted to bring your attention to Amazon's New World: Aeternum, which is officially shutting down its servers at the end of January 2027. We'll remind you again nearer the time, but it's worth being aware of it now too!

As for upcoming delistings, both Street Outlaws and Street Outlaws 2 will disappear off the Xbox Store at the end of December 2026, so you'll need to buy them before then if you want the ability to re-download them in the future.

We've seen on many occasions that announcements like these can spring up at the last minute, so we'll keep an eye out and let you know of any big reveals over the next couple of months. For now though, the damage doesn't look too bad!
