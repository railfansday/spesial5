The Eliminated: San Diego Wave, Angel City, Seattle Reign, Portland Thorns

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The first round of the NWSL playoffs sent the expected teams home, barring one shocking upset.

The National Women’s Soccer League playoffs are in full swing, and four more teams have nothing more to look forward to in 2026. Few games in the quarterfinals were much to write home about, and most of them went the way we anticipated. One remarkable upset meant that the no. 2 seed San Diego Wave were sent on vacation two weeks early, and no more playoff matches will be played west of the Mississippi River.

In this week’s edition of The Eliminated, we flip the script. I’ll first break down what made these four Championship hopefuls special, then try to understand why the first round was where their journeys ended. We’ll compare preseason expectations to final results and underlying performances, using an average of two different projection models: a player-performance-based model from American Soccer Analysis and an historical team performance model from Trevor Larner. Expected points are an average of models from ASA and Opta.
