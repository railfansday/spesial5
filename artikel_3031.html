Nvidia in Crisis: What Next? | Bloomberg Tech: Europe

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Nvidia faces is in crisis after the sudden departure of Jensen Huang, the rock star CEO who turned it into the company at the heart of the AI boom. As markets digest the fallout, the shockwaves are spreading across Europe’s technology ecosystem. In this special edition of Bloomberg Tech Europe, Tom Mackenzie explores what it means for the tech industry, investors and AI sovereignty in Europe. "Bloomberg Tech: Europe" spotlights the biggest names and trends shaping the region's technology ecosystem as the global competition heats up. This monthly, 30-minute magazine-style show features in-depth interviews with top technology leaders, as well as major investors and policymakers - giving you a compelling A to Z of the most consequential innovations, opportunities and challenges. Today's guests includes Richard Windsor, Radio Free Mobile Founder and Owner, Roxanne Varza, Station F Director, Dan Akarca, Callosum Co-Founder. (Source: Bloomberg)
