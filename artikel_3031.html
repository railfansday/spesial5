Stock Market Today, Nov. 24: Bitmine Shares Sink 8.6% as Confidence Wanes

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Today, Nov. 24, 2026, Bitmine's drop came alongside a wider crypto treasury sell-off, with volume running well above its usual pace.

By Emma Newbery – Nov 24, 2026 at 5:36PM EST

Bitmine Immersion Technologies (BMNR -8.60%), an Ethereum (ETH -3.02%) treasury accumulation and Bitcoin (BTC -3.49%) ecosystem services provider, closed at $13.18, down 8.60%. Shares fell as the company added to its ETH position and a broader crypto pullback hit treasury stocks.Trading volume reached 51.9 million shares, coming in about 44% above its three-month average of 36.1 million shares. Bitmine Immersion Technologies IPO'd in 2025 and has grown 82% since going public.

S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.50%) closed at 7,201, down 0.50%, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -1.48%) finished at 23,734, down 1.48%. Among Ethereum digital asset treasury peers, Sharplink (SBET -5.61%) closed at $4.54, down 5.61%, and Bit Digital (BTBT -14.22%) closed at $0.49, down 14.22%.

Ethereum has fallen 28% in the past month as rate fears, the tech sell-off, and investor jitters weigh on crypto prices. Bitmine Immersion Technologies, which is primarily an Ethereum treasury company, has plummeted further: the stock has dropped 41% over the past month.

Holding stock in crypto treasury companies is not the same as owning the digital assets directly or buying a crypto ETF. In the case of Bitmine, it set out to buy 5% of all the Ethereum in circulation and generate returns by staking its holdings. The firm has bought steadily through market turbulence, adding to its Ethereum holdings every week since June 2025 and now holds 4.98% of circulating ETH.

The difficulty for investors is that Ethereum’s price has fallen around 30% since the firm started its weekly purchases, and Bitmine is underwater on its holdings. I am optimistic about Ethereum in the long term -- as the biggest smart-contract crypto, it is well-positioned to benefit from any growth in the stablecoin and real-world asset tokenization markets. However, I am less optimistic about Bitmine because the treasury model carries too many risks, including share dilution, liquidation, and management changes. Ethereum ETFs allow investors to access Ethereum through their brokerage accounts and custody solutions, without the risks associated with treasury companies.
