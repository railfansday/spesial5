Warriors standout erupts at Grizzlies fan in heated postgame confrontation

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Golden State Warriors' frustration appeared to boil over after their 126-98 road loss to the Memphis Grizzlies on Thursday night.

A clip of Warriors forward Georges Niang (who finished the game with five points in 15 minutes played off the bench) confronting a fan postgame is getting attention on social media.

Golden State Warriors forward Georges Niang talking to a fan after playing the Memphis Grizzlies. (X/@ClutchPoints).

Niang looked to be walking toward the fan in question moments after the game ended. He then pointed at the fan and said, "Yo, who the f—k are you talking to?"

Niang kept pointing at the fan for several seconds before turning away. Several other Warriors players were in the vicinity, seemingly expressing frustration about the fan while they were being managed by security, referees, and what looked to be members of Golden State's coaching staff.

Golden State Warriors players congregating near a fan after their loss to the Memphis Grizzlies. (X/@ClutchPoints).

It's currently unclear what the fan might have done to provoke Niang and his teammates.

What's for sure is that Golden State has struggled mightily amid Steph Curry's absence, having lost its past five games since the star guard injured his oblique earlier this month.

Golden State Warriors center Charles Bassey (28), forward Georges Niang (32), and guard De'Anthony Melton (8) talk in Memphis, Tennessee, Nov. 19, 2024. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

The Warriors' season has been a series of streaks. They lost the first three games of the season, then won eight straight, and now have lost the past five. But what is truly worrisome is less the fact that they have lost five straight, and more how they lost.

Golden State has been outscored by 116 points during the losing streak, an average of just over 23 points per game.

They will look to buck that trend against the 11-4 Boston Celtics on Sunday.
