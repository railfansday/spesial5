Colts rule out CB Justin Walley, LB Akeem Davis-Gaither

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Colts ruled out cornerback Justin Walley (knee) and linebacker Akeem Davis-Gaither (knee) ahead of Sunday's game against the Cowboys.

Walley injured his knee during the Week 7 game against the Vikings and has not practiced or played since. Davis-Gaither did not practice this week.

Rookie linebacker CJ Allen will wear the green dot, coach Shane Steichen said, with a rotation of other linebackers slotted next to Allen.

The Colts added safety AJ Haulcy (ankle) to the injury report Friday, and he is listed as questionable. He was limited in practice Friday.

Offensive tackle Jalen Travis (thumb/hamstring) is also questionable. Travis was a full participant in practice all week.

The Cowboys ruled out linebacker Malachi Lawrence (ankle) and wide receiver Kavontae Turpin (ribs). Defensive end Jonathan Bullard (calf), tight end Jake Ferguson (ankle), linebacker Marist Liufau (forearm), linebacker DeMarvion Overshown (hamstring, back), wide receiver George Pickens (hamstring), cornerback Joey Porter (ankle) and tight end Luke Schoonmaker (groin) are questionable.
