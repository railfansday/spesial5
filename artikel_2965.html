Prediction: Take-Two Interactive Stock Will Make You a Lot Richer by 2030

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The launch of "Grand Theft Auto VI" is poised to take shares of Take-Two to the next level.

By Keith Noonan – Nov 18, 2026 at 9:15AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Take-Two Interactive Software ›

After years of anticipation and repeated release delays, Take-Two Interactive's (TTWO +6.01%) Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA VI) is finally hitting the market on Nov. 19. The game's debut is poised to be a watershed moment for the company and the video game industry at large, and it will likely wind up being one of the most successful entertainment products ever.

GTA VI is the most important game that Take-Two has released since it published Grand Theft Auto V all the way back in 2013, and it's poised to supercharge the company's sales and earnings performance. As such, I think Take-Two stock has what it takes to deliver big wins for shareholders through 2030.

Grand Theft Auto VI has been in development for over a decade and is a follow-up to the most successful game of all time -- GTA V. As of the most recent update from Take-Two, GTA V had shipped more than 235 million copies worldwide. The game also generated billions of dollars in sales through its online multiplayer mode.

Thanks to the combination of unit sales and revenue from in-game purchases, GTA V isn't just the most successful video game ever -- it's the single most profitable entertainment product ever released. Thanks to the high quality of the single-player game and the evolving, highly engaging nature of its online multiplayer mode, Grand Theft Auto V had incredible longevity. The game generated $1 billion in sales across its first three days on the market, and subsequent rereleases on new platforms and new content added to the game's online mode helped keep the title a key performance driver for Take-Two for 13 years. Now, Grand Theft Auto VI is poised to take the series and the company's net income performance to the next level.

The period shortly following GTA VI's release will almost certainly be followed by announcements of huge sales for the game, but investors should understand that these might not cause Take-Two stock to rise in the near term. With such a highly anticipated release, it's not unreasonable to expect that there could be a certain amount of "buy the rumor, sell the news" when it comes to the stock's performance shortly following GTA VI's release. Bearish pressures that have been impacting the broader market as of late could also put a damper on the company's stock performance.

On the other hand, I think the long-term outlook for Take-Two stock appears very promising. With this crucial and long-awaited title finally releasing, Take-Two looks like a worthwhile buy-and-hold investment through 2030.
