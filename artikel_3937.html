Cook, Serve, Delicious: Re-Mustard! launches January 18, 2027 for PC, later in 2027 for consoles

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Cook, Serve, Delicious: Re-Mustard!, a reimagined version of the original Cook, Serve, Delicious!, will launch for PC via Steam on January 18, 2027, followed by PlayStation, Xbox, and Switch consoles later in 2027, developer Vertigo Gaming announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via Vertigo Gaming:

From the creators of the Cook, Serve, Delicious franchise comes Cook, Serve, Delicious: Re-Mustard!—a brand new take on the original Cook, Serve, Delicious! game.

Whether you’re a long time fan, or you’ve never played a Cook, Serve, Delicious! game before, this is the game for you. Here’s what we’re serving up!

Your journey to culinary greatness begins today. Take your restaurant from a one star abomination to a five star extravaganza. You’ll design your menu, manage your restaurant, and serve food to the hungry masses.

Cook, Serve, Delicious: Re-Mustard! includes all thirty foods from the original game, plus a host of new recipes, new ingredients, and new upgrades! We’ve also added hundreds of new in-game emails and new surprises to keep things fresh!

Everything is better with friends, and cooking is no exception! With two multiplayer modes, you can play through the entire campaign in either the classic Chef / Expediter Mode, or spice things up with the all-new Split-Screen Multiplayer Mode!

Cook, Serve, Delicious: Re-Mustard! features gorgeous new art that runs at a crisp 60 frames per second! But that’s not all, it also features an expanded soundtrack from series composer Jonathan Geer, which includes dozens of tracks from all four games in the franchise. That’s a lot of music!

We’ve also carefully balanced the difficulty, to give you that “just one more day…” feeling!

We’ve worked hard to ensure Cook, Serve, Delicious: Re-Mustard! plays great on Steam Deck! Now you can cook on the go!

So what are you waiting for? Let’s get cooking!
