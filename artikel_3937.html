Rockstar firings tribunal hears closing statements, with judge promising to deliver a verdict "as quickly as I possibly can" - though it may take a while

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The employment tribunal over Rockstar's firing of 34 workers last year has come to an end for now, pending a final verdict from the judge. Said judge hasn't offered a concrete estimate of when her judgement will arrive, but has reportedly asserted that she'll provide it "as quickly as [she] possibly can".

That's according to People Make Games' Chris Bratt, who's reported on the tribunal throughout the proceedings."I cannot give a time frame for producing the judgement in this case," he cited judge Lucy Wiseman as having said following closing arguments from Rockstar and the union representing the fired staff over the past couple of days. "What I will say is that I will use my best [endeavours] to get that judgement out as quickly as I possibly can."

Bratt reported that could mean anything from weeks to a few months of waiting, leaving the fired staff looking at more time in the limbo they've occupied throughout the tribunal.

To recap the arguments Wiseman will have heard, Rockstar accused the fired staff of leaking "confidential information on a public forum", while the Independent Workers’ union of Great Britain (IWGB) union have accused Rockstar of being motivated by union busting in letting the workers go. According to a previous report from People Make Games, discussion of a new company Slack policy in a union-run Discord server preceded the firings.

The fired devs haven't called for supporters to boycott GTA 6 - the game they were working on prior to being let go - in solidarity, but instead to consider buying specially-designed union t-shirts, the proceeds of which will go towards the workers' "Fighting Fund".

Devs still at Rockstar and the IWGB have formed the Rockstar Games Workers Union since their fired colleagues' tribunal proceedings began and are in the midst of a vote on union recognition as of writing.

Writing in The Guardian's Comment Is Free section this week, Rockstar animation programmer Nestor Uriarte expressed that it's "hard not to worry about what the future holds" for developers at the studio, with the prospect of layoffs potentially happening now the big game is out the door concerning staff. Uriarte made clear that, as of the writing of his piece, Rockstar staff "have had no indication that layoffs are coming".
