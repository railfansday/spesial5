The Golden Scale in Zelda OoT is a reward and prize from the Fishing Minigame in Lake Hylia

The Golden Scale in Zelda Ocarina of Time is a reward for winning the Fishing minigame off the edge of Lake Hylia, but that's easier said than done. There's several factors involved in the Remake OoT's fishing game, as well as certain elements that have been tweaked since the original. It's worth it though, as the Golden Scale upgrades your ability to dive so you can reach even further depths, including getting the Heart Container Piece from the scientist's lab just outside. The only way to get the Golden Scale in Zelda Ocarina of Time Remake is to go to the Fishing Pond in East Lake Hylia as an adult, and catch a fish of 60cm or larger.

The fishing minigame costs 30 Rupees to play as an adult, but you can keep making fishing attempts until you have gotten a fish big enough, so effectively you need only ever pay once for this reward.

Once you have the fish, make sure you choose to keep it, then bring it over to the Fisherman who runs the pond and he'll give you the Golden Scale as a reward.The Golden Scale allows you to dive deeper than before. Most players will have the Silver Scale at this point, which allows a medium depth dive, acquired while trying to find Ruto in Zelda Ocarina of Time.
