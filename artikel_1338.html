Serie A: Parma vs. Roma – Probable line-ups and where to watch on TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Roma will have three key players back and available for selection when they take on Parma away from home in their first Serie A match after the international break, kicking off at 17.00 GMT.

The Giallorossi are one of two teams still unbeaten after 11 Serie A matches of the 2026-27 season, along with city rivals Lazio, and went into the international break in third place with eight wins and three draws on their record. Gian Piero Gasperrini’s side are currently on a three-game winning streak in the league, looking for their fourth against Parma.

Alberto Gilardino’s side, meanwhile, are hoping to get back to winning ways after a 2-1 defeat at home against Bologna last weekend, a game that saw them play with 10 men from the ninth minute onwards.

Youngster Simone Lontani is back from his recent experience with the Italy national team squad and is expected to partner El-Bilal Toure in attack this evening. Adrian Bernabe is also back and available for selection, but is most likely to start from the bench on this occasion.

Roma, meanwhile, will have goalkeeper Mile Svilar, centre-back Gianluca Mancini and star attacker Paulo Dybala back and available for selection after their recent injury lay-offs. All three are in contention to start against Parma.

Matias Soule is among the players who could be given a rest after a long journey back from Argentina on Thursday, along with compatriots Santiago Castro and Leonardo Balerdi. Lorenzo Pellegrini and Devyne Rensch remain out injured.

Parma (3-5-2): Daffara; Delprato, Troilo, Carboni; Britschgi, Fabbian, Sierro, Keita, Valeri; Lontani, Toure.

Roma (3-4-1-2): Svilar; Mancini, Ndicka, Hermoso; Molina, Cristante, Kone, Wesley; Mora; Dybala, Malen.

Today’s game will be available to watch live in the UK exclusively on DAZN from 17.15 onwards. Supporters in the USA are able to tune in via Paramount+.
