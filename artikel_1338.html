He Played For Arsenal at 17, Then He Was Sent to Prison For 15 Months

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Virgil van Dijk and David Raya are now two of the best players in the Premier League, accumulating 528 English top-flight appearances between them. Naturally, this means that the pair have played alongside some of the very best in the game.

Van Dijk made a name for himself in British football at Celtic and Southampton, before completing his big move to Liverpool. At Anfield, he's established himself as one of the greatest defenders in Premier League history, whilst winning two league titles and a Champions League in the process.

Meanwhile, Raya doesn't quite have the longevity at the top level as his Dutch counterpart, but he has developed into one of the world's best goalkeepers since signing for Arsenal in 2023. Last year's Premier League title triumph strengthened his case for being the very best shot-stopper on the planet.

While the pair have had the pleasure of sharing dressing rooms with an array of world-class players, they've also played alongside several players who didn't quite reach the outstanding level that they did. Former Celtic number nine Anthony Stokes falls into that category.

Eight years after leaving Celtic, Stokes was sentenced to 15 months in prison.

Born in Dublin, Stokes joined Arsenal's academy at the age of 15. After developing through the Gunners' youth system, Stokes left the North London club after making just a single appearance for the first team.

He'd go on to have stints at Sunderland and Hibernian, before making his way to Celtic, where he enjoyed the most fruitful spell of his career. Netting 76 goals in 191 appearances for the Scottish giants, he then left Glasgow and spent the last six years of his career jumping from club to club, spending time in Greece, Azerbaijan, Turkey and Iran.

Three years after hanging up his boots, Stokes was arrested and found guilty of drug and driving offences. However, he successfully won an appeal against the verdict, and the sentence was eventually overturned.

A prolific striker in his youth career at Arsenal, Arsene Wenger started to take note of Stokes heading into the 2005/06 season. In October 2005, the Frenchman called the then 17-year-old up to the Gunners' matchday squad for a League Cup tie against Sunderland.

Wenger handed Stokes his senior debut that day, introducing the Irishman in the 88th minute in place of Arturo Lupoli, with the North Londoners running out 3-0 winners. While this proved to be his one and only appearance for the Premier League giants' first team, it wasn't yet the end of him at Arsenal.

To aid his development by playing regular minutes, Wenger sent Stokes out to Falkirk on loan in 2006. The forward enjoyed a prolific stint at the Scottish club, scoring 16 goals in 18 appearances, prompting praise from the legendary Gunners coach upon his return to North London.

"Anthony has made a big step up to the SPL and has done very well - I'm very happy about it. Ideally, I'd like all of our young kids on loan to come back to Arsenal." - Wenger on Stokes in 2006 (via BBC Sport).

Despite Wenger's words, there was no room for Stokes at Arsenal. He joined Sunderland in January 2007 for £2 million, and after struggling to make an impact at the Stadium of Light, he eventually made his way back to Scotland in 2009, signing for Hibernian.

Lighting up the Scottish Premiership again, scoring 23 goals in his solitary season at Hibs, Celtic came in and acquired the striker in August 2010. Signing a four-year contract in Glasgow, Stokes quickly established himself as a fan favourite at Celtic Park.

He enjoyed two seasons where he surpassed the 20-goal mark, and ultimately left the Bhoys with four Premiership winners' medals in his back pocket. This spell with the Hoops is where he met Van Dijk, whom he shared a dressing room with between 2013 and 2015, before the centre-back signed for Southampton.

After a loan spell back in Edinburgh with Hibs, Stokes briefly returned to English football, leaving Celtic for Blackburn Rovers in 2016. His stint in Lancashire wasn't very successful, making just 14 appearances for the Blues and Whites, although he did cross paths with future Yashin Trophy nominee Raya at Ewood Park.

Stokes then spiralled into a classic 'journeyman' path as he entered the latter stages of his career. He returned to Hibs for a third time, before playing for Apollon Smyrnis, Tractor, Adana Demirspor, Persepolis and Livingston, all in the space of four years.

Many thought his spell at Livingston would be his final, but Stokes returned to the game in August 2025, five years after last playing, signing for Conor McGregor's pub team, Black Forge.

Stokes had a history of criminal convictions before he was finally sentenced to prison in 2024. In 2013, the striker was convicted of assault after headbutting park attendant Anthony Bradley in a Dublin nightclub.

In 2019, he pleaded guilty to stalking his ex-girlfriend and her mother in Scotland. He spent short periods in prison for each of these offences before he was handed a 15-month sentence in 2024.

The former professional footballer was caught following a high-speed car chase in Dublin and was reportedly found guilty of possessing £3,300 worth of cocaine in the process. Stokes was deemed to be over the alcohol limit and driving at 99mph when he nearly collided with another motorist in early January 2023.

A former Arsenal player, who played alongside Jack Wilshere and Cesc Fabregas, was sentenced to four years in prison back in 2025.

He also entered an additional guilty plea to possessing 86 illegal street tablets in Dublin, ten days after the traffic pursuit.

Alongside his 15-month sentence, Stokes was banned from driving for five years. However, he was released within hours in order to appeal the severity of the sentence. In November 2024, his sentence was overturned on appeal, meaning he successfully evaded jail time.
