Oyster Estate Brings a New Design Perspective to Greenport

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

For years, travelers visiting Long Island’s East End have often found themselves choosing between aging roadside motels or high-priced luxury resorts. Oyster Estate, a new 34-room boutique hotel in the heart of historic Greenport Village, is looking to offer something in between, combining considered design with relaxed, unpretentious comfort.

The latest project from Boston-based investment group Blue Flag Capital, Oyster Estate is part of a broader effort to bring new life to the region’s historic hospitality properties. Having already worked on properties including Hotel Corduroy in Montauk and Faraway Sag Harbor, the company has now crossed the Peconic Bay to the North Fork, transforming the longtime Greenporter Hotel.

Brooklyn-based Islyn Studio—the practice behind projects including New York’s Brass & Tusk Bar and Mercantile Cafe in Albuquerque—collaborated with Blue Flag Capital on the renovation of the former Greenporter Hotel. Rather than erase the building’s mid-century architectural character, the design team drew inspiration from Greenport’s maritime, agricultural, and winemaking heritage. Instead of relying on overt nautical references, the interiors incorporate these influences through materials, finishes, and carefully considered details.

“We approached the Greenporter with a lot of respect for its history and the role it has played in the community, while recognizing the opportunity to evolve it into something entirely new,” says Brad Guidi, Blue Flag Capital co-founder and chief development officer. “The bones of the property remain, but nearly every aspect of the guest experience was reimagined, drawing inspiration from Greenport’s maritime heritage and creative spirit to create something that feels distinctly rooted in the North Fork.”

Custom architectural millwork, wooden built-ins, and layered textiles give the interiors a tactile quality, complemented by locally sourced objects and subtle maritime references. In the lobby, flowers collected in Greenport were dried and pressed into tiles, bringing a tangible piece of the surrounding landscape indoors.

Handcrafted details lend the spaces a sense of age and individuality. Both guestrooms and public spaces balance clean architectural lines with warm, natural finishes, creating an environment that feels considered but comfortable.

At the physical and social center of the property sits a newly constructed courtyard pool and patio. Designed as a gathering place, the outdoor area gives guests room to lounge and socialize while maintaining the property’s intimate scale. The setting also reflects the North Fork’s quieter coastal character, distinct from the Atlantic-facing beaches of the South Fork.

The property’s hospitality offerings are expected to expand with Fortune Favors—an on-site, Asian-inspired bar and dining venue with a hi-fi listening room. Planned features include atmospheric lighting and a custom sound system, extending the hotel’s communal spaces into the evening.

With year-round accommodations, Oyster Estate offers visitors a way to experience Greenport beyond the busy summer season. Its combination of preserved architectural character, contemporary interiors, and shared gathering spaces suggests a hospitality model rooted as much in the surrounding community as in the guest experience.
