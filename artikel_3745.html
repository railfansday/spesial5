Brittany Snow and Fiance Hunter Moreno's Low-Key Relationship Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Brittany Snow and creative director Hunter Moreno have kept their romance under wraps since first sparking dating speculation in late 2024.

The couple quietly got engaged in November 2025, with Snow calling her fiancé “the best” during a Drew Barrymore Show interview the next year.

Snow was previously married to Selling the OC star Tyler Stanaland from 2020 to 2023 before moving on with Moreno. Scroll below for their complete relationship timeline:

Snow and Moreno were first romantically linked while the actress was filming the first season of The Hunting Wives. Her costars even played a hands-on role in vetting her then-boyfriend.

“Well, we all met him,” Malin Åkerman said on The Drew Barrymore Show in November 2026. “We got to be the first ones to ‘judge’ him, and the second I met him, I was like, ‘Aw, Brittany, he’s The One.’ His smile, right away, you know when you meet a person and you can just feel their energy? You’re like, ‘This is the right energy for you,’ and he really is.”

Åkerman added that her husband, Jack Donnelly, also bonded with Moreno, joking, “We call them all the Hunting Husbands, all of the husbands or boyfriends, whatever. [They] all get along.”

After months of keeping things offline, Snow offered fans a rare glimpse into her romance in an Instagram carousel. One shot showed her cuddling up to Moreno in the backseat of a car.

“A sucker for a good time,” Snow captioned the post.

Bachelor alum Colton Underwood chimed in with a comment, writing, “You look soooo happy ❤️🙌.”

Moreno proposed to Snow in a private moment that the couple initially kept to themselves, a source told Us.

“Brittany and Hunter are so private and low-key, they just started telling friends recently within the last few weeks,” the insider said. “It happened about a month ago. It was low-key and private. He treats her well. They are both very low-key people.”

A second source praised Moreno’s character, calling him “an extraordinary guy.”

“He’s a mental health advocate and just a salt-of-the-earth kind of guy,” the insider added. “They are so happy together.”

Snow’s The Beast in Me costar Claire Danes spilled the beans about the couple’s private engagement when she appeared caught off guard by Snow’s ring.

“You’re engaged? When did that happen?” Danes asked Snow in footage from the red carpet premiere.

Snow addressed her engagement publicly for the first time on an episode of The Drew Barrymore Show.

“For the first time, I’m going to talk about it,” she told Barrymore. “I’m going to tell you, yes, we’re engaged. I haven’t said it before. It’s not that big of a secret. I mean, like, who cares? But yeah, he’s the best.”
