How to get free AHA Advocate ID Card and Badge in Phasmophobia

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

There are lots of ways you can customize your experience in Phasmophobia, including an array of ID Cards and Badges you can equip to highlight various achievements and events. Sometimes, these special items are given away to all players, as is the case with the AHA Advocate ID Card and Badge.

ID Cards and Badges are some of the rarest and toughest items to unlock, as many of them require you to sink lots of time into checking off lengthy, complex goals. Because of this, free, easy-to-grab ones are always rather exciting additions you won’t want to miss out on, so here’s how to get the AHA Advocate ID Card and Badge in Phasmophobia.

You can unlock the AHA Advocate ID Card and Badge by logging onto Phasmophobia anytime during the redemption period. This is a free reward granted to all members of the community in celebration of over $200,000 being raised during Phasmophobia’s fundraiser for the American Heart Association Liive.

There are no additional steps you need to take to obtain this item, which is unusual for earning ID Cards and Badges, so it can take a bit of getting used to. Simply boot up the game, visit the board in the lobby, and select the “Accept” option on the AHA Advocate pop-up anytime before Nov. 15 to claim this reward and have it added to your collection forever.

Once you’ve got the AHA ID Card and Badge set unlocked, you can find them under the Customization tab in the lobby. If you’re having trouble finding them, here’s a full breakdown of where to look.

You have to navigate through quite a few different pages, tabs, and sections to successfully find the AHA Advocate ID Card and Badge, so they can be a bit tough to locate at first. Look for the bright red color of both items to make finding them a bit easier once you’re in the right section.

You can only grab the AHA Advocate ID Card and Badge until Nov. 15. After this date, it’ll no longer be obtainable and likely will never return, as has been the case with all other special event items. Because of this, you’ll want to be sure to log on and grab it while you can if you’re interested in permanently adding it to your collection.

If you’re looking for additional items you can unlock, there are lots of tasks you can work on to claim more freebies. Consider working on challenges like Artist’s Retreat, Entangled Terror, and Farmhouse Fieldwork next.
