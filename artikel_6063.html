The FAA Still Uses Floppy Disks In Air Traffic Control—And Hopes To Replace Them In 2028 [Roundup]

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

News and notes from around the interweb: FAA expects to finally retire floppy disks for air traffic control by 2028 The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) announced…that it has begun phasing out floppy disks used in air traffic control operations. …The first five facilities receiving the upgrades include Atlantic City International Airport, Nashville International Airport and Greater Rochester International Airport, as well as data processing hubs in Atlanta and Salt Lake City. The FAA says it plans to install E-IDSs at 435 sites by the end of 2028.
