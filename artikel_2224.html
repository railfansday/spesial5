Harry Redknapp says 46-year-old will be a ‘great fit’ for West Ham as managerial appointment nears

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

West Ham are expected to turn to their former player as their new head coach following their decision to part ways with Nuno Espirito Santo. WHUFC are positioned in 8th place in the Championship table after their first 13 fixtures of the season as they eye promotion back to the Premier League.

Parker, 46, cut ties with Burnley in late April and is available for work. He has been weighing up his options over the last six months.

Redknapp, who both played for and managed the Hammers, has delivered his verdict on talkSPORT: “Scott’s got a great record in the Championship and has taken teams up. He knows the club. He was a very, very good player. I took him from West Ham to Tottenham. Good lad. He’s a very astute manager. I think he’s a great fit for West Ham.”

He added: “He hasn’t got an easy job on his hands. Take (Jarrod) Bowen apart, there’s very few players there who would have got into too many West Ham teams over the years. They’ve got great support, it’s a big club that unfortunately needs a big lift now.”

Parker played 129 games for West Ham during his playing days. As well as WHUFC, he also had spells at Charlton Athletic, Chelsea, Newcastle United, Tottenham Hotspur and Fulham.

The ex-England international, who made 18 caps for his country, hung up his boots in 2017. He has since delved into the managerial world.

He started coaching at Spurs before returning to Fulham as their manager in 2019. He guided the Whites to promotion to the Premier League in 2020 before doing the same with AFC Bournemouth two years later.

The London-born man also got Burnley up in 2025 so he knows what it takes to get out of the EFL. He has also managed abroad following a stint at Club Brugge in Belgium before his switch to Turf Moor.

As Redknapp has alluded to, Parker has promotion experience and knows West Ham well already. He should be able to get the WHUFC fans onside.

The Hammers need to find some form as they look to claw themselves back into the top flight at the first time of asking. They are in the top eight along with Wolves, West Brom, Southampton, Birmingham City and Stoke City as they chase down the top two Middlesbrough and Swansea City.

They lost 3-1 at home against West Brom last time out and were poor against the Baggies. They are back in action on Wednesday night with an away trip to South Wales to take on Cardiff City as they aim to get themselves back on track and return to winning ways.

West Ham managed to keep hold of Jarrod Bowen in the last transfer window. He has been prolific so far this term but needs some of his teammates to step up now.

The London outfit will get the chance to bolster their ranks this winter. They will also have the opportunity to let some players head out the exit door as well in January.
