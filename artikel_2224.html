Michael Chopra drops Cardiff City exit verdict on ‘unbelievable’ star

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Former Cardiff City cult hero Michael Chopra has issued a claim on the form of Jack Moylan, whose fine start to life at the Cardiff City Stadium continued with a first-half hat-trick in the 3-3 draw with West Ham United.

Much of the pre-match discourse in South Wales was on Scott Parker's first game in charge of the Hammers, but it seemed as though the Bluebirds were going to make it three successive losses for them through Moylan's individual brilliance.

The Republic of Ireland international opened the scoring with a stunning strike from distance after 11 minutes, before netting twice more before the interval.

After drawing a foul from Morato, Moylan beat Mads Hermansen from the penalty spot before glancing home his hat-trick and second in as many minutes just six minutes before half-time.

Ultimately, West Ham staged a thrilling fightback and were level on 82 minutes through goals from Divine Mukasa, Arne Engels and Max Kilman, but that took nothing away from the stunning form Moylan continues to demonstrate in South Wales.

Moylan arrived in the Welsh capital in August from fellow Championship newcomers, Lincoln City, despite only signing fresh terms at the ProAmpac Stadium six months ago.

After scoring 11 times in 31 League One appearances for last season's third tier winners, the attacking midfielder is already the joint-second-highest goal-getter in the Championship, with his treble against the Irons seeing him match Andri Gudjohnsen's tally of nine for Blackburn Rovers.

The six-time international has posted three further assists as Cardiff sit 18th on 15 points, and Chopra, who scored 63 times in 159 appearances, had this to say on the 25-year-old.

"Said it a couple months ago @JackMoylan8 is some player," the former striker posted on X. "A first half hat trick unbelievable.

"@CardiffCityFC going to have a fight to keep hold of him in January," he added.

Many of Barry-Murphy's squad have earned praise in recent times for the way in which they have performed, with some still in the early stages of their highly promising careers.

At 25, there is also more room for Moylan to enhance his potential, which has only grown through his recent performances, and unsurprisingly, many Cardiff fans share Chopra's sentiment.

"I'm worried like you Michael he has come in and really lifted the club up players and fans we have really got to try and hold on to him our season will have a real bad hit if he goes," said one Bluebirds supporter.

Another responded: "No it isn’t mate cmon ! Only been here a couple months! End of season maybe but not January!"

However, not all were of this opinion, with another social media comment reading: "January is going to be a chance to see how the board see us when the bids for Moylan come in."

"Difference between Cardiff pushing for top half and being dragged into a relegation battle. Nobody else in that team has that level of quality in front of goal," was another verdict of Moylan's immediate impact.

Given Chopra's cult hero status at Cardiff, it was high praise that one Cardiff fan wrote: "Nearly, but not quite, as good as you!," in response to the former striker's opinion.

Moylan's performances have also been noted by supporters of other clubs, with a Birmingham City fan adding: "No chance he doesn’t get a prem move in January, bloke is class."

Few would be surprised if Moylan's current form perks the interest of clubs ahead of the winter window, although Cardiff fans will be hoping they avoid a quick sale of the playmaker.

However, the Bluebirds would still hold significant power in negotiations after agreeing a four-year deal with the Dubliner, whilst those associated with Lincoln are hoping that a sell-on clause was inserted in his sale.

At present, Moylan is proving to be the difference-maker for Barry-Murphy's side, and many will be expecting him to feature again in Heimir Hallgrímsson's Ireland plans against Austria and Kosovo next week.
