Adnoc Gas doubled profit and raised its 2026 forecast as LNG exports recover

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Net income rose to $1.34 billion from $665 million in the second quarter, and guidance is now at least $4 billion

Adnoc Gas, a unit of Abu Dhabi National Oil Co., raised its full-year net income guidance on Tuesday, setting a floor of $4 billion, after its profit in the third quarter came in at twice the second-quarter level, the company said.

Net income reached $1.34 billion, up 101% from $665 million in the second quarter. EBITDA rose 70% from the prior quarter to $2.03 billion. Adnoc Gas had previously forecast full-year net income of $3.5 billion to $4 billion.

Sales within the UAE rose 20% from the second quarter, and domestic gas EBITDA climbed 28%. EBITDA from international product sales rose 106%, and sulfur EBITDA rose 249%.

Adnoc Gas shipped more liquefied natural gas across the Strait of Hormuz during the third quarter compared with the second, according to The Wall Street Journal. "It is a month-by-month improvement in the number of cargoes that are crossing the Strait of Hormuz," Chief Financial Officer Peter van Driel told The Wall Street Journal. Shipments have yet to climb back to what they were before the war, even with U.S. Navy ships escorting vessels, van Driel told The Wall Street Journal. Adnoc Gas's September exports were about 88% of 2025 levels.

The company said market conditions improved in the third quarter but that disruption to maritime movements through the strait continued to strain operations. It said its outlook depends on regional maritime conditions.

"Our operational strength and commercial agility enabled us to safely and reliably meet domestic and international demand and drive strong financial results despite continued challenging external conditions," Chief Executive Fatema Al Nuaimi said in a statement.

Al Nuaimi told The Wall Street Journal that Adnoc Gas has made no decision on projects to bypass the strait, including an LNG export facility at the Port of Fujairah on the Gulf of Oman. "At this stage, there are no decisions made on any of these projects," she said.

The board approved a quarterly dividend of $940 million, up 5% from a year earlier and payable in December. Adnoc Gas said restoration of the damaged Habshan complex is on track to reach full capacity by the second quarter of 2027.

The U.S. struck Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz last week. Tehran has said the strait could reopen within two days if Washington meets seven demands, while the U.S. rejects Iran's claimed authority over the waterway. A U.S.-Iran memorandum of understanding signed June 17 collapsed.

The strain on the waterway has pushed Abu Dhabi to expand its own gas production. In July, Adnoc approved a $6.2 billion investment in the Umm Shaif Gas Cap, which is expected to begin production by 2030.
