Meta warns that building new data centers is about to get harder

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Meta President Dina Powell McCormick expects more review of new U.S. projects as the company weighs building data centers abroad

Andrej Sokolow/picture alliance via Getty Images

Meta $META Platforms President Dina Powell McCormick said launching new U.S. data center projects will grow more difficult once the midterms are over, according to The Wall Street Journal. She spoke on Wednesday at the WSJ Tech conference.

Powell McCormick said "many different factors" contributed to the Democrats' sweeping wins, noting that hostility toward data centers loomed large in political advertising and exit polling, according to The Wall Street Journal. She said she expects "a lot more review" of new projects. "To start new data center build-outs today in the United States will be very challenging," she said.

She also described a conversation with a newly elected governor who had made data centers a large part of a campaign platform. "As soon as we get past the inauguration, let's meet because we don't want to lose this investment in our town. We don't want these jobs to go elsewhere," the governor told Meta, Powell McCormick said.

Expanding existing data center projects should be easier, she said, because communities have had years to see the benefits rather than the costs. She added that every large cloud provider is weighing whether to build outside the U.S., and that hedging such large investments is wise. Meta is looking at other countries, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Meta has projects under construction in Louisiana, where Hyperion is rising, and in Ohio, home to Prometheus, plus a Texas site it is developing jointly with BlackRock $BLK, according to The Wall Street Journal. Both Ohio's and Texas's incoming governors have urged conditional halts on building new data centers, The Wall Street Journal said.

Meta has said it plans to spend as much as $145 billion on capex this year while it builds the computing capacity behind personal AI agents. Powell McCormick said securing computing power takes up 80% of her working hours.

Meta has announced plans to introduce a fresh category of shares without voting rights for upcoming equity raises, along with a plan to raise as much as $95 billion by selling new stock, according to The Wall Street Journal. The raises follow the first time since 2012, its IPO year, that Meta’s free cash flow dipped below zero.

Opposition to data centers was visible on the ballot as well. Voters in more than 30 towns and cities across nine states backed bans, moratoriums and other curbs on data centers on Tuesday. Democrats ran ads attacking the tech industry or data centers in more than 130 federal and gubernatorial contests this cycle.

Developers had called off at least $260 billion in planned U.S. data center projects as of mid-October, and most of those projects died at the local level over permits or action by town and county governments.
