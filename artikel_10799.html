Thief returns stolen $300k watch with apology letter – but they’ve still not been caught

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

A man in Japan returned a $300,000 rare watch with an apology letter after stealing it from a store.

For most thieves, the aim of the game is to steal something and get away with it. They never typically aim to get caught or even have to hand the items back.

However, over in Japan, one man decided to issue an apology after he snatched a rare Audemars Piguet watch worth around $292,000. As per The Japan Times, the robbery happened on October 29 in Tokyo’s Nakano Ward.

The man, who hasn’t been named, asked to try on the rare watch before fleeing the store and escaping the two employees who were working at the time.

On November 1, he returned the watch via a courier and left a note saying sorry for the robbery, with the returned watch as well.

The store confirmed that it was the watch that had been stolen, as they checked the serial number.

While the apology note did contain a name and address on it, employees believe the details are fake. An investigation is still ongoing, with CCTV footage of the man seemingly spotting him travelling via train and taxi during his getaway.

Back in August, a bookstore in Califronia went viral after revealing that a book stolen over 40 years ago had finally been returned to them.

Booklegger said the rare, first-edition copy of The Unstrung Harp finally been handed back to them with an apology letter and $100. The thief noted they were in “desperate need of money” at the time and apologized for their “utter lack of principles.”
