Round led by HorizonArc Ventures; proceeds to fund AI technology, product expansion, compliance and global growth

Global digital asset and structured finance platform OpenVault today announced the completion of a $2 million seed round led by HorizonArc Ventures, with a post-money valuation of $20 million.

The proceeds will be primarily used for AI technology development, product portfolio expansion, compliance infrastructure, and global market growth.

OpenVault is an innovative financial platform focused on digital asset yield management, RWA (real-world assets), and structured financial products. Through its standardized Vault architecture, the platform integrates stablecoin yield, quantitative investment strategies, and diversified asset allocation, providing global users with a transparent, efficient, one-stop digital asset investment gateway.

Powered by its proprietary AI engine, OpenVault significantly lowers the barrier to participation:

With “standardized Vault + AI-driven” as its core, OpenVault aims to turn complex digital asset and structured finance products into user-friendly tools accessible to ordinary investors. The platform emphasizes asset transparency, strategy traceability, and quantifiable risk control, striving to deliver a more robust and intelligent wealth management experience in the digital asset space.
