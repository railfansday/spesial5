Powerful NY Democrat Andrea Stewart-Cousins dead at 76, reports say

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Andrea Stewart-Cousins, the New York State Senate majority leader and the first Black woman to lead the chamber, has died at age 76.

Andrea Stewart-Cousins, the trailblazing majority leader of the New York State Senate, has died at age 76, according to multiple reports.

Stewart-Cousins, the first Black woman to lead the state's upper chamber, was found dead in her Yonkers home on Friday morning, police sources told the New York Post.

Her daughter reportedly tried to get in touch with her on Thursday night before calling for a welfare check.

Stewart-Cousins, a Democrat, was first elected to the state senate in 2007. She then became majority leader in January 2019 after Democrats won a majority in the chamber in the 2018 midterm elections.

"She was a groundbreaking leader who shattered one of New York’s highest glass ceilings," Mike Murphy, a spokesman for the Democratic majority, said in a statement.

Stewart-Cousins entered a state government marked by a tumultuous Democratic conference amid feuds and power struggles, Politico reported.

In 2018, Democrats eventually won 39 of the state Senate’s 63 seats, giving the party its first stable majority since World War II.

The next year, state lawmakers spent their time addressing progressive pieces of legislation, such as immigration, abortion protections and tenants' rights, according to the report.

The Business Council of New York State issued this statement: "We are deeply saddened to learn of the sudden passing of Senate Majority Leader Andrea Stewart-Cousins. As the first woman to lead a conference in the New York State Legislature, she made history and will forever be remembered for her strong leadership and dedication to New Yorkers.

"A partner to The Business Council, Andrea was always willing to listen to employers and work toward common ground on behalf of the business community. We extend our deepest condolences to her family, loved ones, and colleagues during this difficult time."

U. S. Congressman Ritchie Torres hosts a news conference in support of the re-election of Gov. Kathy Hochul. (Lev Radin/Pacific Press/LightRocket via Getty Images)

Rep. Ritchie Torres, D-N.Y., said he was "saddened" to hear of Stewart-Cousins' death.

"She was a trailblazer whose leadership helped shape New York and opened doors for generations to come," he said. "Her commitment to public service and the people she represented will not be forgotten. My thoughts are with her family and loved ones."
