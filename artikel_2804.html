Celtics will be without key player against Hawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

BOSTON — The Celtics will be without Mitchell Robinson when they host the Atlanta Hawks on Friday night in their final NBA Cup Group Play game. Robinson, initially listed as questionable, has been downgraded to out due to an illness.

Robinson has been sidelined due to illness for four straight games: he missed the Celtics' games the Sacramento Kings, Golden State Warriors, Washington Wizards, and now the Atlanta Hawks. Unrelatedly, he also sat out the Celtics' November 1st game in Orlando for rest.

Robinson has appeared in 14 games, including five starts, and is averaging 5.4 rebounds per game, the fewest of his career. He also has the team's worst on-off rating.

But Brad Stevens said last week that he expects Robinson to figure it out.

"You can tell it's obviously a transition for him, and that's not all that atypical for guys, and so again, it's so early that we'll see how this all plays out," Stevens said. "But I trust that what he does when he's at his best will translate because it always has, right?"

With Robinson out, Neemias Queta will continue to anchor the Celtics' frontcourt, while Luka Garza and rookie Chris Cenac Jr. could see increased minutes behind him. Queta averages 10.4 points, 6.9 rebounds, 2.0 assists, and 1.2 blocks in 23.4 minutes per game; on Tuesday, he scored 21 points on 9-for-11 shooting.

"Obviously, as the years go by, I think I'm also [entering] my prime, so I'm becoming more confident, more comfortable out there," Queta said at Celtics shootaround on Friday morning.

The Celtics snapped a four-game losing streak with a 131-116 win over the Washington Wizards on Tuesday night, behind 41 points from Jayson Tatum and 28 from Paul George.

Friday's game is the Celtics' final game of NBA Cup group play, and a win would put them atop East Group C and advance them to the quarterfinals.

Atlanta comes into Boston riding a four-game winning streak, and has won eight of its last 10 games.

The Hawks have been led by Nickeil Alexander-Walker (21.3 points) and Jalen Johnson (19.1 points, 9.1 rebounds, and 6.9 assists),

The Celtics are 12-6 on the season; the Hawks are 11-6.

Celtics-Hawks tips off on Friday night at 8pm ET.
