Drew Lock has an oblique injury, "up in the air" for Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Seahawks have another injury issue at quarterback.

Head coach Mike Macdonald said at his press conference after Sunday’s 17-3 win over the Cardinals that Drew Lock has an oblique injury. Macdonald said that Lock’s status for Week 10’s game against the Raiders is “up in the air” as a result.

Lock was 19-of-28 for 175 yards and a touchdown in the win.

Lock started against Arizona because Sam Darnold was ruled out with a quad injury. His status for next Sunday is also uncertain.

2025 third-round pick Jalen Milroe is the next man up on the quarterback depth chart in Seattle. The Seahawks also added Adrian Martinez to the practice squad in the wake of Darnold’s injury.
