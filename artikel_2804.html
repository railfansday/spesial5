Arizona Cardinals place Andrew Wingard and Jordan Burch on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Well, the Arizona Cardinals got their asses whooped in their loss to the Los Angeles Rams, both figuratively and now we are finding even more literally.

We knew about Jordan Burch, but now Andrew Wingard, who Mike LaFleur said he didn't see being placed on IR, is being placed on IR.

The Arizona Cardinals Football Club today announced the following roster moves:

– Signed defensive lineman Jared Harrison-Hunte and safety Wydett Williams Jr. (why-DETT) to the active roster from the practice squad

– Signed wide receiver Ronnie Bell to the practice squad

– Re-signed safety Jerrick Reed II to the practice squad

– Placed linebacker Jordan Burch and safety Andrew Wingard on injured reserve

– Released offensive lineman Albert Reese IV from the active roster

– Released wide receiver James Proche II from the practice squad

The signings took place today at the Dignity Health Arizona Cardinals Training Center in Tempe, AZ.

Bell (6-0, 192) has played 28 games in his career with San Francisco (2023-24) and New Orleans (2025) and has 10 receptions for 113 yards, 10 punt returns for 70 yards and two kick returns for 23 yards. He entered the league with the 49ers as a seventh-round selection (253rd overall) in the 2023 NFL Draft out of Michigan. The 26-year old Bell has also been on the practice squads of the Raiders, Saints, Lions and 49ers in his career. He spent the preseason with New Orleans and was on the Las Vegas practice squad earlier this season.

Harrison-Hunte will wear #98, Williams will wear #31, Bell will wear #37 and Reed will wear #33.

Let's hope the Cardinals can stay healthier the rest of the season.
