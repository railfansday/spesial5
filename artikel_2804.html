Xavier beats Bay Port to win girls flag football title hosted by Packers

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

APPLETON – The championship trophy isn't gaudy or flashy, but the words inscribed on it do have deep meaning: "Forever The First."

That's what the Xavier girls flag football team will have in its trophy case as a reminder of the Hawks' historic achievement being Wisconsin girls flag football champions – the state's first recognizable championship in the growing sport.

The Green Bay Packers hosted the tournament at Emplify Health Field on Nov. 7 with Xavier knocking off Bay Port 19-6 in the title game. Sophomore quarterback Addy Hafner had three touchdowns passing with senior Hailey Hafner adding 10 carries for 90 yards along with six catches for 56 yards and a touchdown. Senior receivers Abigail Anderson and Macy Hietpas also had touchdown receptions in the win.

Xavier's players received championship rings from the Packers as part of the celebration and the thought is this inaugural tournament is a precursor to the sport becoming an officially sanctioned WIAA sport.

"I think it's just so cool that we are able to set an example for other girls to be like, 'We can play and we can make a sport when we show interest in it,'" Hietpas said. "I know there's so many ways that people got brought into football. A lot of people were recruited from other people. That's how I started. And it was just like, 'It's so fun and a good time.' You get to play with all your friends and then just enjoy it."

The tournament featured the top six teams from around the state, many of which won regional league titles. Xavier won the Central Wisconsin Girls Flag Football League championship, a league comprised of teams from Appleton East, Appleton West, Appleton North, Neenah, Green Bay East, Little Chute, Freedom, Weyauwega-Fremont and Amherst.

The Packers have been supporting girls flag football since 2024. That was when Xavier head coach Glenn Higgins contacted the Packers about recent advertisements about girls flag football and the team quickly offered support both monetarily and with equipment.

Higgins said the Packers contacted the team in June about putting together the tournament. That meant the team, comprised of multi-sport athletes, had to find time to practice for the tournament in November, which was after their spring flag football season.

Graduated players that were part of the championship season this past spring were allowed to come back and compete as well.

"We had one practice a week," Higgins said. "We came out on Sundays for three weeks. We invited other girls to come out if anybody wanted to see what flag football was about. We ran patterns and did a little bit of that type of work.

"Oct. 7 is when we started practicing in earnest."

Xavier rallied to defeat Divine Savior Holy Angels 26-12 in the semifinals after being down 12-0. The Hafners were key again for the Hawks with Addy passing for two touchdowns and Hailey running for 108 yards and two more scores. Lucy Des Jardins had a 55-yard touchdown catch with Anderson contributing a 20-yard touchdown catch.

That led to the title game where the victorious Hawks beat Bay Port, earning the trophy and championship rings.

"Mine was kind of nice," Anderson said. "I've seen my brother have the football rings after buying them, but when I got mine it was kind of like, it's mine. I have multiple awards from other sports, but the ring was special because it was different than other sports."

"I feel like the ring is so special to the sport, because that's how the NFL gets awarded and stuff," she said. "So I think it's so cool to get that over a medal that's awarded with every other sport."

Higgins said the tournament games showcased the skill and competitiveness of the athletes.

"People didn't get the opportunity to see how competitive the games were and how well the football is played," he said. "Our two games – and we watched the other games too – these are good athletes playing really pretty good football.

"We've gone beyond the part where this is powder puff. We're at the point now where they've practiced, they know the patterns, they know route combinations, they know spacing. They know when to rush and when not to rush. They know how to stay back and when to come up and guard their player, and that's what is the exciting part for people to see happening."

The Xavier flag football roster included Hailey Hafner, Lydia Foth, Liv Des Jardins, Gail Anderson, Paxtyn Trinkner, Raya Zurfluh, Brianna Podojil, Olivia Kraft, Lucy Des Jardins, Macy Hietpas, Gabby Congdon, Quinn Hervey, Ella Mares, Tina Mameril, Kayla Fournillier, Addy Hafner and graduates Abby Ourada, Joy Krull, Brynn Krull and Cecilia Higgins.
