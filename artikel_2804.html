Seahawks shut down Raiders in second half of 21-13 win

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Raiders quarterback Fernando Mendoza’s first NFL start started with a bang, but the Seahawks slammed the door on him in the second half on Sunday.

Mendoza ran for a touchdown to open the game helped the Raiders to a 10-7 lead over the defending champions through 30 minutes of play in Las Vegas. The second half was a very different story, however.

The Raiders were shut out until a Matt Gay field goal with 2:04 left to play in the game and the Seahawks scored a pair of touchdowns to pick up a 21-13 win. The victory pushes their record to 6-4 as they head into their bye week and it came after one of the smartest special teams plays we’re likely to see this season.

After Gay’s field goal, Seahawks returner Tory Horton jumped to bat the ensuing kickoff back in bounds rather than let it sail out for a touchback. He also had the presence of mind to tell Rashid Shaheed to return the ball since he stepped out of bounds and couldn’t do it himself. The play wound up taking the clock through the two-minute warning, which meant the Raiders could only stop the clock twice rather than three times.

The Seahawks only ran 17 offensive plays in the first half of the game, but they found their footing behind Zach Charbonnet and Jaxon Smith-Njigba in the second half. Charbonnet ran 12 times for 55 yards and Smith-Njigba continued his remarkable season with seven catches for 160 yards after the break. One of those was a 71-yard touchdown from Drew Lock.

All in all, Seattle picked up 274 yards in the final two quarters while holding the Raiders to 27 yards on their final six possessions. They sacked Mendoza four times in the second half and the loss of tight end Brock Bowers to an ankle injury left the rookie without real options in the passing game.

Mendoza, who finished 17-of-29 for 143 yards, will now try for his first win in Denver next weekend.
