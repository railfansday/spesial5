Shane Steichen on Daniel Jones remaining the starter: "We'll evaluate everything"

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

As the Colts continue to struggle offensively, they continue to cling to quarterback Daniel Jones.

Asked by reporters after the 26-3 loss to the Texans whether Jones will remain the starter, coach Shane Steichen said, “We’ll evaluate everything.”

The No. 2 quarterback is 2023 top-five pick Anthony Richardson. On Thursday night, he got a garbage-time drive in the fourth quarter. The three-and-out, which started at the Indianapolis 39, consisted of Richardson running for two yards and throwing two incomplete passes.

It was Richardson’s second appearance of the season; in Week 8, he got a couple of mop-up drives when the Colts trailed the Jaguars, 42-7.

At some point, Richardson deserves a real chance to play. How much worse can the Colts’ offense be?

The Colts were outgained by the Texans, 354 yards to 167. They converted one of 11 third-down tries. Jones completed 12 of 27 passes, for 144 yards, no touchdowns, and an interception.

There’s a business reason that points to the Colts eventually getting Jones off the field. His contract has a $10.51 million injury guarantee for 2027. It vests in March. They’ll inevitably bench Jones to keep him from getting an injury that would keep the Colts from releasing him before the injury guarantee converts to a full guarantee.

That’s what the Giants did to Jones two years ago. He then requested, and received, his release.

The same thing could be happening, sooner rather than later, for Jones in Indianapolis. Which means that he could be available to another team.

That possibility highlights the Colts’ decision to not trade him for whatever they could get. And maybe they tried.

Maybe, in the end, his availability in trade is what helped the Vikings keep the Raiders’ asking price for Kirk Cousins to a third-round pick.

And maybe, in the end, the Vikings will have a chance to add Jones to the depth chart while showing Carson Wentz the door.
