Mixed day for Smyth as Saints lose in Chicago

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Charlie Smyth kicked two field goals from over 50 yards but they weren't enough for the New Orleans Saints who fell to a 21-13 defeat by the Chicago Bears at Soldier Field.

It wasn't a perfect day with the boot for the 25-year-old from Mayobridge in County Down as he missed from 47 yards and sent two kickoffs out of bounds which allowed the Bears to start drives on their own 40.

Smyth signed a new contract with the NFC South franchise after twice being elevated from the practice squad in previous weeks, following Dan Carlson's release at the start of November due to mixed form.

On Sunday, the former Gaelic footballer was successful with an extra-point attempt after after quarterback Tyler Shough connected with Alvin Kamara for a touchdown.

An opportunity for a field goal in the second quarter drifted wide to the right from 47 yards, but he closed out the first half with a successful 56-yarder - one yard short of his career record - that was helped over by the crossbar.

Trailing by 11 late in the fourth quarter, the Saints drove into field goal territory and Smyth converted from 51 yards. It left them needing to recover an onside kick to get the ball back with 1.10 remaining, but his effort was easily gathered by the Bears.

The result means the Saints' record drops to 3-7, two games behind the 5-5 Carolina Panthers who lead the division.
