Mike Vrabel: Amy Adams Strunk didn't want to pay A.J. Brown

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

One of the most compelling pieces of draft-room video came in 2022, when the Titans unexpectedly traded receiver A.J. Brown to the Eagles and coach Mike Vrabel's discomfort with the move was manifested by the repeated jerking of his head.

Now that Brown and Vrabel have been reunited in New England, after an offseason process that had the Rams tap out due to concerns about Brown's knee, chatter has surfaced that the Titans made the trade because they'd been told by doctors that Brown's knee — as of 2022 — had only two years left.

On Monday morning, Patriots coach Mike Vrabel addressed that narrative during his weekly appearance on WEEI.

Was Brown traded because the Titans believed his knee had a short shelf life?

"No, the owner didn't want to pay A.J. Brown 25 million dollars," Vrabel said, via Mike Reiss of ESPN. "That's what it was. He went to Philly, and I mean, A.J. has participated in quite a handful of games and has missed a few since he went to Philly."

Brown played four years with the Eagles. He missed six regular-season games. He had four straight 1,000-yard seasons. He qualified for two Pro Bowls. He was an Associated Press second-team All-Pro three times.

The real question isn't whether Brown had two years left in his knee in 2022. It's whether the knee had enough to justify trading for him and paying him in 2026. The Rams decided it wasn't. The Patriots decided it was.

The fact that Brown has missed six games due to injury has brought the knee problem back into focus, opening the door for revisionist history as to why the Titans traded him.

"But by all indications, unfortunately, after the training camp that he had, I wouldn't have anticipated anything," Vrabel said. "And then injuries are part of the game. He gets rolled up on [in the season opener]. And then gets dragged down the other day, and that was unfortunate. So we'll just keep trying to get him healthy and whenever he can get back out there to help us, I know that he will."

Currently, Brown has a hamstring injury. Vrabel made it clear on Monday that Brown will miss at least the Week 12 game at the Chargers.

Nothing about Brown's current condition — as it relates to his knee or any other body part — changes the fact that the Titans made a huge mistake when they decided not to give Brown a second contract, to trade him to a team that would, and to replace him with a first-round pick that was used on receiver Treylon Burks.

In three years with the Titans, Burks had 53 catches for 699 yards and one touchdown. During those same three years, Brown had 261 catches for 4,031 yards and 25 touchdowns with the Eagles.

And two Super Bowl appearances and a Super Bowl win.

Regardless of whether the Patriots made a good decision to trade for Brown in 2026, there can be no credible dispute that the Titans made a horrible decision to trade him in 2022.
