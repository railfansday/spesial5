Eagles vs. Steelers: 19 winners, 8 losers, 4 IDKs – BGN

BYRON YOUNG. Jordan Davis who? I'm really not even trying to be disrespectful to the former Eagles defensive tackle. But it's impossible to not point out that Davis had 1.5 sacks and two TFLs in nine games this season … and Young had two sacks and two TFLs in just 22 defensive snaps played on Sunday. I know some Eagles film grinders aren't very big fans of Young. But this was a pretty nice game for the 26-year-old defensive tackle. I don't know that he's a future starter for this team but he could be proving useful as a rotational/situational defender.

QB is a trickier discussion. Hurts should come back from the blood clots and be healthy for 2027. Hurts is a good QB. We can see how different the offense has looked with and without him. The question is how he fits into the long term plans. Hurts and his agent may want a new deal. Would the Eagles want to commit to him long term? He will turn 29 next summer so he's potentially got several good years left. At the same time, athleticism is a key part of his game and as that starts to fade it could affect his overall level of play. The Eagles are likely to have a Top 10 pick and could take a QB if there is a player they like. They don't draft that high very often so there is something to be said for getting a good QB prospect when you have that chance. The Eagles could draft a QB and try to keep Hurts for the next few years, giving the young guy time to learn and get ready. The Packers had great success with Aaron Rodgers and Jordan Love both sitting for multiple years before starting. It will be really interesting to see how the Eagles handle this situation. The good news is that they will have Hurts next year so they have the freedom to add a player if they like someone or could just stick with Jalen. They have options.

Eagles-Steelers what we learned: What to make of Sean Mannion, Nick Sirianni's offensive changes – PHLY Eagles

Going back to a past iteration of this Eagles offense is neither a good or a bad thing in a vacuum, but the results spell out why the ceiling may be limited for this group regardless. For as much encouragement as many had for the performance, Sunday still ranked ninth of the Eagles' 10 games in offensive success rate and seventh out of 10 in EPA/drive. The second-half stagnation also persisted in this game. The Eagles gained 141 net yards in the first half, but managed just 73 yards in the second half and had just one first down in the third quarter on four drives. And a minor nitpick … The Eagles used 11 personnel almost exclusively against the Steelers, deploying three receiver on 82% of their offensive snaps despite having a whopping five tight ends active on game day. That limited tight-end usage for such a crowded position group came at the expense of Eli Stowers, who wasn't on the field for a single offensive snap against the Steelers. For a team that should be thinking for the future, playing Zach Ertz and Grant Calcaterra over the second-round rookie, however slowly his career may have started with the Eagles, is hard to understand.

Please welcome Braden Mann to the wonderful world of Roob's Eagles Stats – NBCSP

THE WORLD'S GREATEST BRADEN MANN STATS: Braden Mann averaged 59.3 yards on four punts vs. the Steelers, the highest single-game average ever by an Eagles punter on four or more attempts. The previous high was Mat McBriar's 56.6 on five punts in a game against the Lions at the Linc in 2012. Mann now holds four of the top-six punting games in Eagles history, with Briar and Cameron Johnston – who kicked for the Steelers Sunday – responsible for the other two. Mann has had 29 games since joining the Eagles in 2023 at 50 yards per punt or more, more than twice as many as any other Eagle. Johnston is second with 14. Mann is tied for most 50-yard games by any NFL kicker since 2023 with A.J. Cole of the Raiders, who also has 29. Mann's 49.2 average since joining the Eagles is highest in franchise history, smashing the previous record of 47.0 set by Johnston from 2018 through 2020. McBriar (46.5) and Donnie Jones (45.4) are also over 45.0. Mann also added two more punts inside the 20 Sunday with no touchbacks. So far this year he has 24 inside the 20 and two touchbacks. Mann had a 70-yard punt Sunday, a game after booting an 86-yarder against the Giants. According to a Stathead custom search, he's the first Eagles punter as far back as records go – which is 1977 – with a punt of at least 70 yards in consecutive games and the 11th NFL punter to do that since 1977. And since the Eagles didn't have a punt of 70 yards between 1963 and 1976, he's the first with a 70-yarder in back-to-back games in at least 64 years and most likely ever.

The Steelers need to cut DK Metcalf – Behind The Steel Curtain

Metcalf was ejected from the game after two skirmishes that both resulted in unsportsmanlike conduct penalties, the latter of which he reached into the facemask of Eagles defensive back Cooper DeJean. It's the second time in two seasons Metcalf has gotten into a physical altercation since joining the Steelers. Last season, he slapped a fan that was taunting him in Detroit during the team's win over the Lions in Week 16. He was subsequently suspended by the NFL for the final two games of the regular season. Given his history, the league will likely view him as a repeat offender and give him another suspension of some kind. But what should the Steelers do? Should they get ahead of the league and suspend him internally for conduct detrimental to the team? I'll do you one better – they should cut him.

Greg Newsome injury: NY Giants CB out multiple weeks with ankle injury – Big Blue View

New York Giants starting cornerback Greg Newsome will be out multiple weeks with a high ankle sprain, head coach John Harbaugh said on Monday. Newsome left Sunday's 23-18 victory over the Jacksonville Jaguars after playing only four snaps. He was replaced by Paulson Adebo, who ended up with an interception and four passes defensed. "It's a serious high ankle," Harbaugh said. "It's going to be one of those lengthy high ankles. Gonna be multiple weeks." Harbaugh did not say whether Newsome would undergo surgery or be placed on injured reserve.

Bengals-Commanders on 'Monday Night Football': What We Learned from Washington's 22-20 win – NFL.com

Washington's NFC East hopes remain alive. And just like that, the Commanders have life in the divisional race. Following a Sunday in which the Giants, Cowboys and Eagles all won, Washington desperately needed to upset the Bengals to keep pace. Somehow, someway, the Commanders did, and they're now two games back of the 6-3-1 Giants with the all-important head-to-head tiebreaker. The Cowboys also sit ahead of them in the standings at 5-6. With little chance of getting involved in the NFC wild-card race, the division is the Commanders' path to a playoff spot. Tonight's victory showed they can stick with and beat anyone, and they actually have a couple very winnable games coming up against the Cardinals (3-8) and Titans (1-9). It gets tougher thereafter, especially Washinton's final three games against the Vikings (7-3), Jaguars (7-3) and Cowboys, but at least there remains a glimmer of hope heading into Thanksgiving. The Commanders gave themselves that by starting their first winning streak since the 2024 postseason in improbable fashion, in the process completing the first 4-0 week for the NFC East since Week 2, 2023.

The Vikings need to be done with Kevin O'Connell – SB Nation

There's absolutely no shame in losing to the San Francisco 49ers. The Niners have been the NFL's best team this season, battling through injuries and a brutal NFC West to an 8-1 record that somehow still underscores how good they've been. If you're the Vikings though, there should be profound shame in losing 35-10 in a game which only looked close for one quarter. Vikings fans are understandably waking up Monday morning trying to figure out how their team was so flat. Did the 49ers draw up a game plan that caught them unaware? Were the Vikings suffering from the altitude in Mexico City? Was this just a bad game compounded by mistakes? Whatever path you choose to explain the beatdown, they all come back to one common thread: Head coach Kevin O'Connell.
