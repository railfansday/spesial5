NFL picks: Who the experts predict to win in the Broncos-Raiders game

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Denver Broncos (7-2) are set to face the Las Vegas Raiders (4-6) in the first meeting between the teams this season. These are two clubs going in opposite directions. The Broncos are riding a five-game winning streak. Meanwhile, the Raiders have lost six of seven following a surprising 3-0 start to the year.

Denver is coming off its bye week and looking forward to facing a much more favorable schedule than it saw in the first half of the season. Fernando Mendoza, the rookie quarterback for the Raiders who was the No. 1 overall pick in the draft, will be making his second career start.

So let's ask the experts what they think about this one. Does anyone see the Raiders pulling the upset?

"Fernando Mendoza will be making his first road start, which can be tough for a rookie," wrote Prisco for CBS. "But it's also against a really good defense coming off a bye in a tough place to play. That's a real challenge. Look for the Broncos to get after him in this one. The Raiders' defense isn't playing well, so maybe it's just what Bo Nix needs. Broncos big."

"Rookie Fernando Mendoza will make his first road start against the Broncos and tight end Brock Bowers (ankle) will need to be monitored throughout the week," Bender wrote for The Sporting News. "Denver has 26 sacks, and the Broncos are coming off a bye week. The Broncos are 4-0 S/U and 3-1 ATS after a bye week under coach Sean Payton."

"The Broncos and their defense will be rested for this one, coming off a bye. That's not good for Fernando Mendoza in just his second NFL start," wrote Cluff for Arizona Republic.

"Fernando Mendoza drew the short stick in having to face a pair of top-10 scoring defenses in his first two NFL starts," Cersosimo wrote for NFL.com. "However, the Broncos aren't quite Mike Macdonald's unit in Seattle, as they rank 27th in yards allowed -- and pose an opportunity for the rookie QB to build on his debut. To do so, he'll need his supporting cast to be healthy (Brock Bowers) and show up (Ashton Jeanty). Even so, not sure that'll be enough against a Denver team fresh off the bye and riding a five-game win streak. The Broncos have made their hay late in games this season with Bo Nix leading the league with five fourth-quarter comebacks and six game-winning drives (tied). Facing a rookie QB and one of the NFL's worst defenses (31st in scoring, 30th in yards) in the confines of Empower Field at Mile High, where the Broncos have lost just one regular-season game since the start of 2025, is the perfect scenario for Nix and Co. to build an early lead and keep it."

The rest of the panel over at NFL.com (Jeremy Bergman, Gennaro Filice, Bobby Kownack and Dan Parr) all picked the Broncos to win as well. It's a game the Broncos can't afford a letdown at home, within the division. Good teams put teams like this Raiders squad away easily, and the Broncos need to do just that on Sunday.
