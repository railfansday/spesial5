FHSAA brackets, schedule for Pensacola-area third round playoff games

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Two Pensacola area teams will be practicing Thanksgiving week.

Pensacola Catholic will be playing in the Region 1-2A final and Jay will playing in the Independent League Pool B North Regional Final, with both teams hitting the road for long bus rides into the eastern time zone.

Both the Crusaders and Royals will play on Black Friday on Nov. 27.

Below are the game times for both games next week. Times are listed in central time, were taken from MaxPreps and are subject to change.

(2) Pensacola Catholic at (1) Bolles, 6 p.m.

Independent League Pool B North Regional Final

(2) Jay at (1) Mount Dora Christian, 6:30 p.m.
