Titans DE Jaylen Harrell is hospitalized after "serious car accident"

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Titans defensive end Jaylen Harrell has been involved in a “serious car accident.” The team disclosed the situation on Saturday.

Per the Titans, Harrell was taken to a hospital in his hometown of Tampa.

Harrell, 24, was a seventh-round draft pick in 2024. He played college football at Michigan.

Currently on injured reserve, Harrell appeared in all 17 games during the 2024 and 2025 seasons.
