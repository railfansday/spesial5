Millwall open disciplinary proceedings against Baker

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Baker featured in five Premier League games for Brighton in the 2023-24 season

Millwall are undertaking disciplinary proceedings against forward Benicio Baker following social media posts critical of senior figures at the club, BBC Sport understands.

The 22-year-old winger has played only for the Lions' under-21 side since joining from Brighton in February 2025 on a long-term deal.

BBC Sport understands the proceedings relate to a number of off-field matters, including social media activity.

Posts attributed to Baker's account, which has since been deactivated on Instagram but has been widely republished, appeared critical of senior figures at Millwall and suggested he "can't even train with the (under) 21s".

It is understood Baker was initially unavailable because of injury, but he is now the subject of disciplinary proceedings which, in the most serious circumstances, could lead to the cancellation of his contract.

Baker is also understood to be exploring options away from Millwall.

At the height of his career, Baker made six first-team appearances for Brighton under former manager Roberto de Zerbi, including in the Premier League.

The England Under-20 international also spent time in the academy systems at West Ham and Porto before making 14 senior appearances on loan as Port Vale finished second in League Two in 2024.

While at West Ham, before joining Porto, he was linked with moves to overseas clubs including Barcelona, Paris St-Germain and Benfica.

Millwall have declined to comment, while representatives for Baker have been contacted.
