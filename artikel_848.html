McKinney earns England Test call up for South Africa series

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Uncapped Durham opener Ben McKinney replaces Jacob Bethell in England Test squad for South Africa after he was ruled out with a knee injury; England's white-ball side sweating on the fitness of Will Jacks after suffering a finger injury in their series-opening T20I defeat to Australia

England have added Ben McKinney to their Test squad touring South Africa next month, with the uncapped Durham opener serving as a replacement for the injured Jacob Bethell.

McKinney, a 6ft 7in left-handed batter, has become a regular in the England Lions set-up and underlined his growing reputation on this month's tour of St Kitts, hitting 145 against West Indies A.

He will provide competition and cover for England's top order that includes county colleague Emilio Gay.

Bethell underwent a second bout of knee surgery on Thursday after being ruled out of the series due to an ongoing issue that also required an operation in the summer.

The 23-year-old had linked up with England's white-ball squad in Australia earlier this month, only for his anticipated comeback to be cut short by a flare-up of his knee injury.

England's the three-Test series in South Africa gets under way in Johannesburg on December 17.

Meanwhile, England's white-ball side could be scrambling to find a captain by the end of their Australia tour after Will Jacks suffered a bloody finger injury in the closing moments of their series-opening T20I loss in Melbourne on Saturday.

Jacks hade to leave the field in the penultimate over of the three-wicket defeat with blood streaming from his right hand following a painful attempted caught-and-bowled off Ben Dwarshuis.

Jacks had been set to captain England for the first time in the fifth and final T20 in Canberra, with regular white-ball skipper Harry Brook having brought forward his departure ahead of next month's Test tour of South Africa.

"I don't know [how bad it is],-he obviously came off with a lot of blood but I couldn't quite see what happened," Brook said of Jacks' funger after Saturday's loss at the MCG.

"Hopefully he is alright because he has been such a valuable player for us in the last two years. It would be sad to see him go home early.

"I've not even thought about that one [the captaincy] yet, that's a decision we'll have to discuss but I don't have an answer yet."
