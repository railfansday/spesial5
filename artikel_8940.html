ICA Miami Appoints Gensler to Design Second Museum Building in Miami Design District

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Institute of Contemporary Art, Miami (ICA Miami) has appointed Gensler to lead the renovation and expansion of its second building in Miami's Design District, with the project scheduled to open in late 2027. Acquired by the museum in 2024, the building is located on the same block as its existing facility and will add 30,000 square feet of space, doubling ICA Miami's facilities. The expansion will introduce new galleries, educational spaces, and venues for public programming, establishing a multi-site campus intended to extend the museum's exhibition and community activities.

The Institute of Contemporary Art, Miami (ICA Miami) has appointed Gensler to lead the renovation and expansion of its second building in Miami's Design District, with the project scheduled to open in late 2027. Acquired by the museum in 2024, the building is located on the same block as its existing facility and will add 30,000 square feet of space, doubling ICA Miami's facilities. The expansion will introduce new galleries, educational spaces, and venues for public programming, establishing a multi-site campus intended to extend the museum's exhibition and community activities.

The renovation will introduce eight galleries across the building's first and second floors, including dedicated spaces for ICA Miami's permanent collection and additional galleries for temporary exhibitions. The project will also establish the Knight Foundation Gallery for New Media, a space intended to support artists experimenting with emerging technologies and new forms of artistic expression. The third floor will accommodate a flexible venue for talks, lectures, symposia, performances, and other public programs, expanding the museum's capacity to host activities beyond its exhibition spaces.

The design reimagines the building's entrance and facade through a more transparent arrival sequence, aiming to establish a closer relationship between the museum and the surrounding public realm. Renovated outdoor spaces will incorporate shaded courtyards and sculptural landscapes, providing areas for gathering, reflection, and community activities. According to the museum, the project will extend its existing facilities through a combination of exhibition spaces, public areas, and educational resources, further connecting its cultural programming with the Miami Design District. The project also forms part of ICA Miami's broader sustainability commitments, which include sustainable shipping methods for exhibitions and carbon offsets for selected major exhibitions.
