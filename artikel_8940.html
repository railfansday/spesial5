Art Basel Hong Kong Lines Up 232 Exhibitors and a New Floor Plan for Its 2027 Edition

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Art Basel Hong Kong has announced the 232 exhibitors that will participate in its upcoming 2027 edition, scheduled to run March 25–27, with VIP preview days on March 23–24, at the

Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre.

The upcoming fair has contracted slightly, with 2027 having eight fewer exhibitors than the 240 the fair had in 2026. (Both figures do not include the Zero 10 participants.) Similarly, the main Galleries section will have 171 participants, down from 182 in 2027, while Discoveries (for emerging artists and galleries) has stayed constant at 25. Insights (curated presentations for Asia-Pacific artists) and Echoes (for work made in the past five years) will both have 18 exhibitors; in 2026, Insights had 20 and Echoes 13. Like the Miami Beach fair next week, the 2027 Hong Kong iteration will also feature a new floor plan. The four special sections—Discoveries, Insights, Echoes, and Zero 10—will move to a different floor from the Galleries section, giving the smaller sections “greater space and visibility, while enhancing opportunities for discovery through a thoughtfully curated visitor journey,” per a release.

The fair will also have 31 first-time exhibitors, with 14 of them making their debuts in the Discoveries section. Newcomers to the Galleries section include Galerie Nordenhake, Yares Art, Richard Saltoun Gallery, Matthew Brown, and VeneKlasen, while Kayokoyuki, Good Weather, Make Room, and Temnikova & Kasela will be first timers in Discoveries. Five galleries will return after a hiatus, including Marian Goodman Gallery, Goodman Gallery, and Vadehra Art Gallery.

More than half of the exhibitors have spaces in the Asia-Pacific region, with 27 having locations in Hong Kong, like 10 Chancery Lane Gallery, Blindspot Gallery, Empty Gallery, Kiang Malingue, Ora-Ora, and Rossi & Rossi. Other geographical areas with strong showing are Mainland China (32 exhibitors), Japan (20), South Korea (16), and Taiwan (8). International blue-chip galleries include Gagosian, Hauser & Wirth, David Zwirner, Pace Gallery, White Cube, Gladstone, Lisson, Thaddaeus Ropac, Esther Schipper, and Sprüth Magers.

The Encounters section for large-scale works will be organized by Mami Kataoka, with Isabella Tam, Alia Swastika, and Hirokazu Tokuyama, while Zero 10 will be done for the first time by Sunny Cheung, an independent curator and former curator of design and architecture at M+. Artist Ellen Pau will once again organize the Film section. Details for these sections will be announced at a later date.

“The most important and innovative dialogues emerge at the intersection of different worlds, where artistic, cultural, and business communities come together to generate new ideas, perspectives, and opportunities for exchange. As Asia continues to shape the global cultural landscape, Art Basel Hong Kong provides a platform for these connections to flourish,” ABHK director Angelle Siyang-Le said in a statement.
