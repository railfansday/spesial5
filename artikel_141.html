Harman Kardon’s alien-looking SoundSticks became synonymous with the Mac in the early 2000s, and they sounded pretty great. But did you know the company still makes them? After selling steadily for $300 for several years and then spiking to $330 in recent months, the last-gen version of the iconic transparent speaker system is available for an all-time low of $149.99 from Harman Kardon, or $199.95 on Amazon. The discount puts them within range of most other desktop speakers you might consider sticking, or displaying, in your lounge area.

In addition to 3.5mm aux connectivity, the SoundSticks 4 support Bluetooth audio. The 2.1-channel system features two wired satellite speakers, each containing four drivers, and a powerful subwoofer encapsulated in a see-through dome. You can adjust the system’s volume by tapping the touch-sensitive plus or minus sections flanking the logo on the subwoofer. That’s not ideal if your plan was to hide the sub from sight, but I’d argue that most people interested in this model likely plan to showcase it on a shelf instead of tuck it away.

The newer SoundSticks 5 are available on sale for $279.95 (originally $349.95), and beyond a couple of minor design updates, the system features LED lighting and an HDMI ARC port for those who want to use it as their home entertainment system.
