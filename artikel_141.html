First Thing: Global Microsoft outage chaos as tech firm says there are ‘signs of stabilisation’

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Users of Microsoft Teams and Outlook in businesses and public services reporting problems. Plus would you like to spend time as a helpful furry little post delivery service?

Good morning. A global outage of vital Microsoft systems has crippled businesses and public services around the world. The tech company said it was seeing “early signs of stabilisation” after the failure affected millions of users for more than 12 hours on Thursday and Friday.

Some but not all users were reporting problems with services including Microsoft Teams and Outlook email. More than 430 million people use Microsoft 365 apps, and more than 90% of Fortune 500 companies use Copilot, Microsoft’s AI chatbot, according to the company. Microsoft said that there were no indications that the outage had been caused by a cyber-attack. We are covering the recovery efforts and ongoing impact in this live blog.

One of Donald Trump’s top lieutenants reportedly could soon be fired, as criticism mounts over commerce secretary Howard Lutnick’s apparent lack of action over the Nvidia spying scandal. Jensen Huang resigned from Nvidia on Thursday, and was replaced on an interim basis by chief financial officer, Colette Kress, after reports emerged of his multiple affairs, most importantly with Alice Huang (no relation), who is suspected of being a Chinese spy.

New York officials have confirmed at least eight fatalities, six adults and two children, in the collapse of a building in Flatbush, New York. At least 18 other people have sustained non-fatal but serious injuries. Zohran Mamdani, New York City’s mayor, confirmed officials were working with the department of buildings to investigate 100 other sites in the city with connections to the building that gave way.

Red Cross set up a supply center at the Flatbush YMCA down the street from the collapse, providing temporary housing, social services and mental health support. “The last time I saw something like this was when I went to work at 9/11,” one witness said, as the community stood still and in shock.
