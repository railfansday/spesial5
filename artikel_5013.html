XRP sellers could lose an extra $3,146 on a large trade if five accounts pull their buy orders

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Removing five accounts’ buy orders in a simulation cut sampled XRP market depth by 86% and large-sale proceeds by 3.3%.

Five XRP Ledger accounts supplied 72% of one day’s trading volume in XRP against Ripple USD (RLUSD), according to XRPL.to.

For a test of the next day’s order book, CryptoSlate selected the five addresses that led the Nov. 25 session in fills of at least 1,000 XRP, then simulated removing their buy orders.

Funded RLUSD in the first 100 buy offers fell from about 63,925 to 8,805. In the model, the matching pool, a standing reserve of both assets, took most of a 100,000 XRP sale after the removal. A seller would receive about 3.3% less RLUSD than with the full sample.

The last XRP/USD trade on Kraken was $0.94935 when the ticker was checked at 13:07:24 UTC on Nov. 26. On the ledger exchange itself, XRP/RLUSD accounted for 84% of all volume measured in XRP over the seven days covered by XRPL.to’s reconstruction.

In the 24 hours ending at 01:30 UTC on Nov. 25, XRPL.to’s reconstruction counted 25,801 fills moving about 6.3 million XRP. Traders took resting offers from 142 accounts or traded against the automated pool.

The biggest account, whose ledger address starts with rpiFwLYi, supplied 41% of executed volume. Together, the five largest supplied 72%, and the pool accounted for 3%.

A replay through the public trade history endpoint yielded 6.32 million XRP and counted 25,712 fills for the same window, 89 fewer than the published tally.

The index can group multiple executions into one transaction row. In one row covering 525.58 XRP, the validated transaction showed about 514 tokens went to one account’s buy orders and about 12 tokens to another’s.

To test whether concentration persisted, CryptoSlate used XRPL.to records and split combined fills using validated ledger changes.

The comparison covered same-pair fills of at least 1,000 XRP in three 24-hour windows ending at 01:30 UTC on Nov. 24, 25 and 26. The same five addresses, selected by Nov. 25 qualifying volume, accounted for about 71%, 88%, and 79% of qualifying volume, respectively.

The address starting with rpiFwLYi led the middle session, with about half the qualifying volume. An address starting with rURtT5MM led the sessions on either side.

That left the same group with a large share in all three windows even as the leading address changed, and the turnover includes trades in which the addresses gave or received XRP.

The two sides of the XRP Ledger’s order book draw on different inventories. A resting order to buy XRP is funded in RLUSD, which the seller receives. A resting order to sell XRP draws on the seller’s inventory.

The relevant depth for a seller is the RLUSD that buyers can deliver at the displayed prices.

At validated ledger 108584374, which closed at 13:09:20 UTC on Nov. 26, the first 100 buy offers could take about 67,549 XRP at the displayed prices.

Excluding buy offers from the five large-fill leaders left room for about 9,302 XRP in that same sample. The funded RLUSD in the sample fell about 86%.

The matching XRP/RLUSD pool held about 1.99 million XRP and 1.89 million RLUSD. The model applied its standard 0.315% fee and allocated each portion of the sale to the better available price.

The test held the remaining offers fixed and used only the sampled book and the direct pool, with deeper orders, replacement orders, and additional fees outside the simulation.

In the model, a 1,000 sale returned 947.96 RLUSD with the full order sample and 947.87 without the five addresses’ buy orders. At 10,000, proceeds slipped from 9,474.85 to 9,470.90 RLUSD. At 100,000, proceeds fell from 94,197.18 to 91,050.74 RLUSD.

The pool took about 32,451 XRP in the first case and 90,698 after the orders were removed.
