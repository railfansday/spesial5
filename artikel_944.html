Former India captain MS Dhoni has retired from playing with Chennai Super Kings in the Indian Premier League and will take up a new role as team mentor from the 2027 season.

Dhoni was bought by the Super Kings in the inaugural IPL auction in 2008 and went on to guide the franchise to five IPL championships.

The 45-year-old spent 17 out of 19 seasons with the Super Kings, also playing for Rising Pune Supergiant when the Chennai club were suspended. Dhoni finishes his IPL career with 5,439 runs and an average of 38.30 across 278 matches.

"There is no CSK without MS Dhoni," Super Kings owner N Srinivasan said on the club's website. "For nearly two decades, we have walked and grown alongside each other, through triumphs, setbacks and everything in between.

"Dhoni will always be a part of our identity. This will always be his home."

Dhoni, who hails from Ranchi, enjoys huge popularity in the southern Indian city of Chennai where ‌crowds throng stadia just to get a glimpse of their "Thala", the Tamil word for leader.

Runs stopped flowing from his blade in recent years ‌but legions of his fans still filled the stands to watch Dhoni batting lower down the order.

Even that was not possible this year, however, and Dhoni sat out the entire season with a calf strain, which effectively ⁠signalled the end of his career.

Dhoni has been the ​face of the franchise since joining them in ​2008 and played two seasons with Rising Pune Super Giant ​only when Chennai were handed a two-year suspension following a corruption ⁠scandal.

"While we will miss seeing him in yellow on the field, we are glad that he will keep guiding our players," Chennai managing director KS ⁠Viswanathan said.

"The bond will continue to grow ​and shape this team for years to come."

Nicknamed "Captain Cool" for ​his astute leadership and unflappable temperament, Dhoni led India to World Cup titles in the 20 and 50-over ‌one-day formats.
