Xbox CEO Issues Statement On GTA 6 And New '10-Year Partnership' With Its Publisher

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The game is also now the most pre-ordered Microsoft Store title ever

We're getting a whole ton of GTA 6 details coming out today, and Xbox CEO Asha Sharma has taken to social media to issue a statement about the game's pre-orders, as well as a new 10-year partnership with its publisher Take-Two.

Sharma didn't share any specific details about the partnership in her post, but we did cover the deal at the beginning of October, which allows Take-Two to develop and sell Xbox-compatible products across all Xbox devices. The Xbox boss did also highlight that GTA 6 has now set a record for the most pre-orders of any Microsoft Store / Xbox Store game in history.

It's no surprise that the most anticipated game of all time is picking up its fair share of pre-orders ahead of launch. The numbers were looking good as early as August, when Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick described GTA 6's pre-orders as "unprecedented and astonishing".

Of course, Zelnick wasn't talking solely about Xbox's pre-order total, but it's great to see Sharma commenting on Microsoft's numbers specifically.

Beyond handling Rockstar's GTA and Red Dead Redemption IPs, Take-Two Interactive also manages 2K and Zynga titles. If Sharma intends to entertain more than a billion players every day, striking up a cloud streaming deal with Take-Two is a great place to start.
