When you find and earn one of the many MW4 DMZ keys, here's where you need to go to use it

Whether you've earned one of the various MW4 DMZ keys by completing a Story Mission, finding it somewhere on the map, or, dare I say, by killing another player (shame on you), then you might be wondering where to use it.In MW4's DMZ mode, there are loads of keys that unlock valuable lockers and vaults, but their description only gets you to a general area - not the precise location of the wares. To help you out, here's a full list of every DMZ key and where to use it, so if you're looking something in particular, find it with CTRL+F or your browser's search function.

These are all the keys that can be used in the Political Prison area of the map:

Some of the keys can be used at different locations, including the likes of Blackmarket Weapons Lockers and Silo Control Rooms, both of which you can find in this northwest part of the map. This is a common spawn area, as well, so if you have one of these multi-use keys, this is a good place to use it.

Keys 5 to 11 in this list are a little harder to use as you'll need to breach the actual prison, and you'll of course encounter plenty of hostility as you do so.

These are all the DMZ keys that can be used in the Cheongun Village and Mirae Hospital areas of the map:

The village is a fairly simple area to loot as it's lots of small buildings rather than one well-locked down area like the hospital or the casino. The casino is a particularly tough location to get into, so make sure you're well kitted out with your best MW4 guns and a medkit or two.

Speaking of the Casino, the Counting Room Vault key is one of the better keys you'll get early on if you're completing Story Missions, and we've got a handy guide to getting into the DMZ Counting Room Vault if you'd like some help.

These are the keys that can be used in the Hanuel Nuclear Reactor area of the map:

The Nuclear Reactor area is the hardest location to enter as you'll be faced with a radiation field that will quickly deal you damage if you're not wearing a gas mask. You're still not immune even with a gas mask, though, as they deteriorate in time, so plan your entry into this area carefully, and keep any keys safely in your stash until you're fully ready for the challenge.

These are the keys that can be used in the Songdo Wharf and The Grid areas of the map:

This area is a good place to use any DMZ bank vault keys, as explained in more detail in our standalone guide, as there are three different banks here you can attempt to access (including the one slightly further south, as you'll see below). One is the main bank tower located inside The Grid, though, which itself is a high-threat area, so enter at your own risk.

The following keys are used in the Wolves Stadium and Hajin River Heights areas of the map:

As I've mentioned above, you've got a bank vault on the boundary between this area and The Grid above it, plus another one to the south of the stadium, both of which are pretty easy to access with the right materials. Meanwhile, another of the DMZ police stations is located here, so if you've got both keys and a big enough backpack, it's a great place to hit.

These are the keys that you can use in the Imjin Farmland area of the map:

There isn't much need to come to this area unless you have any of the specific keys above as it's quite an isolated area with not much else going on. Other than the Fire Station Storage key, though, these are all unique to this area, so if you find any, you might want to collect them and hit the area in one go. You may even find some others while you're looking around, so dedicate your entire match to this zone and have a good search.

These are all the keys that are used in the Air Base and Deadtown areas of the map:

This western area of the map is another common spawn area, so if you've got a Blackmarket Locker key or Silo Control Room key, this is a good place to consider. There are also plenty of good weapons to grab around here, so take your time and explore carefully.
