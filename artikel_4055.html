A former volunteer is threatening legal action against fan-made game project Skyblivion

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

A former volunteer is threatening legal action against Skyblivion, the long-running fan remake of Oblivion built in Skyrim's engine, over code they contributed to the project.

The unofficial project, which remakes 2006's The Elder Scrolls 4: Oblivion, is being made by volunteers from the TES Renewal Project, the modding group that also works on Morrowind remake Skywind.

Bethesda has allowed the project to continue on condition that it replaces all of the original game's assets, and the team continued working on it after Bethesda released its own Oblivion Remastered in April 2025.

In a statement posted to Twitter/X on 30th October, the Skyblivion team said that after the volunteer left, the two sides disagreed over the "continued use and maintenance" of their work.

The main issue was the team's decompilation of parts of that code, which it said was needed "to maintain functionality and fix issues" after its access to the original source code repository was revoked.

According to the team, the volunteer then "escalated to sending legal threats", including demands for the real names and home addresses of its volunteer team leads. The team said this was "something we couldn't accept and think is disproportionate".

The team said it has consulted legal counsel and is "completely confident in our right to maintain the code they submitted". Even so, it said it removed the code "to protect our volunteers from undue stress and ensure a clean break", rebuilding that system from scratch.

Despite this, it says the threats have continued. Its post says the former volunteer is "attempting to sue", but it's not clear whether a lawsuit has actually been filed. The volunteer has not been named.

"Please don't make this a witch-hunt," the team told fans, adding that it wishes the former volunteer "the very best" and hopes to "get this resolved as soon as possible and get on with our planned release of Skyblivion".

Last December, the team delayed Skyblivion from 2025 into 2026.
