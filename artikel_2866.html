Newcastle vs. Arsenal — Team News & Match Preview

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Newcastle United hosts Arsenal at St James' Park on Saturday for its Premier League Matchday 11 fixture, with Matthias Jaissle's side returning from the international break following a 1-1 draw at Fulham, where Jacob Murphy opened the scoring before Rodrigo Muniz equalized. Newcastle is winless in its last three matches across all competitions.

The Magpies remain in the bottom half of the Premier League table and have struggled at home recently, suffering consecutive 1-0 defeats to Aston Villa and Everton. Newcastle has kept just three clean sheets in all competitions this season.

Jacob Ramsey returns from suspension, while Nico Gonzalez and Joe Willock are expected to be available after illness. Joelinton remains doubtful with a thigh injury, and goalkeeper Ewen Jaouen is sidelined. Matias Fernandez-Pardo has also recovered from illness and could return to the starting lineup.

Arsenal enters the fixture leading the Premier League with 27 points from ten matches, holding a five-point advantage over Manchester City. Mikel Arteta's side has won five consecutive league games, most recently defeating Hull City 1-0.

The defending champions have kept four consecutive Premier League clean sheets and won four of their five away league matches without conceding. Arsenal won its previous visit to St James' Park 2-1 last season.

Martin Zubimendi and Bukayo Saka are unavailable through injury, while Max Dowman is suspended.

Here are some of Jaissle's wisdom nuggets.

"I don't want to be too precise with the medical update. We will get feedback from the specialists and we will find out how long Joe will be out.

"We will get an update, and hope he is back soon."

"He was out for a long time and not only once. We're really happy to have him back.

"We manage his load management quite closely and in a smart way, because we want to have him available for a long time now.

"He's a great footballer, a great human being. I am pleased he is able to be in the squad right now and is making good progress."

On players returning to fitness in his squad…

"We had nine injuries – now most of them are back, slowly but surely we will get them back to match fitness.

"We need to be patient with them. It gives us flexibility."

On Lewis Hall, who turned in a fine display in England's win over World Cup winners Spain earlier in the week…

"He is a marvellous player. I said that at the start when he committed his long-term future to the club.

"We are really pleased to have him here. He is a young lad with huge potential. He is doing a great job, not only for us, but also for his national team."

"If you face the champions, that is motivation enough. We are playing at home in front of our fans – they can be the twelfth man.

"The fans can give us so much power and support. This is a great opportunity, a big challenge."

Location: St. James' Park, Newcastle (England)
