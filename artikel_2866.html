Does 49ers trade for Keenan Allen solidify their Super Bowl hopes?

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The San Francisco 49ers acquired veteran wide receiver Keenan Allen in a trade with the Indianapolis Colts for a 2027 sixth-round pick just hours before the NFL trade deadline on Nov. 10.

It's no secret around the NFL that the Niners have a shortage of available wideouts, making the addition of Allen all the more vital to San Francisco's passing game.

The 49ers have been dealt injuries throughout the season Mike Evans, Deebo Samuel Sr., De'Zhaun Stribling, Ricky Pearsall, Demarcus Robinson and Christian Kirk. Each week, there has been someone in the Niners' WR room who steps up to the mantle. Now, Allen gets his turn as San Francisco found their plug-in at receiver.

He returns to the Bay Area having played his collegiate years at Cal from 2010 to 2012. From Cal, he was selected by San Diego Chargers with the 76th overall pick in the third round of the 2013 NFL Draft. Allen was runner-up for Offensive Rookie of the Year but he developed into the Chargers' No. 1-receiver. He would become a six-time Pro Bowl selection in 10 seasons, even as the team moved to Los Angeles.

He spent a season with the Chicago Bears in 2024, then returned to LA for another season with the Chargers. He signed with Indianapolis during the offseason.

With Allen back on the West Coast, the hope is to earn the league's most prized piece of jewelry.

Does Allen have enough left in the tank to help bring a Super Bowl to the Bay Area?

Allen comes to San Francisco as a 34-year-old veteran receiver in his 14th NFL season, which bears the question of if he has enough in the tank to be a contributing piece to the 49ers offense. Allen is added to a star-studded wide receiver room, but still, looming injuries loom are a cause of concern, at least until guys start trending upward.

He'll likely come in as a starter. Allen is known for his elite route-running, high IQ and superb ball-tracking and catching ability. Nothing thrown Allen's way gets dropped.

Allen caught 40 balls for 351 yards and 1 touchdown in eight games with the Colts during the 2026 NFL season. He's shared 22% of targets and has a nearly 69% catch rating. He averages 8.8 yards per catch.

His addition provides Brock Purdy with another weapon to find trust in, especially while the San Francisco receiver room slowly gets healthy. Adding Allen means the team doesn't necessarily have to rush guys back to the field.

Plus, Jordan Watkins is coming off a week where he caught four passes for 86 yards and a touchdown. He continues to improve. Having a threat like Allen alongside Watkins will likely open things up for himself.

The 49ers are still the favorite to win the Super Bowl with +550 odds, according to BetMGM, currently tied with the Buffalo Bills.

For San Francisco, the team's hopes will ride on health and availability. Allen isn't a young buck anymore at 34. Example A, Evans is 33 years old and look how much time he's missed.

No question at what the 49ers will get from the vets on the field, it's a matter of getting them on the field at 100%. Allen has been relatively healthy in 2026 and that's good news for San Francisco.

Allen appeared in every game, except one, for the Colts having missed Week 4 with a groin injury. Since then, he's been dependable, especially production wise. Maybe not the fantasy pickup he once was, but Allen does the things that contributes to winning football games.
