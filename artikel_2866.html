49ers dominating Raiders, lead 21-10 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The 49ers offense has been tough to stop all year and that has remained the case in Sunday’s game against the Raiders.

San Francisco turned long drives into touchdowns on each of the club’s first three possessions to take a 21-10 lead into halftime.

The 49ers got on the board with a 1-yard touchdown run by Christian McCaffrey. It’s his seventh consecutive game with a touchdown run, which is now the longest streak in a season in team history.

Quarterback Brock Purdy had a 1-yard touchdown run to put the 49ers up 14-0, finishing off a 13-play, 89-yard drive.

McCaffrey then had a 12-yard receiving touchdown, giving San Francisco three scoring drives of at least 12 plays to begin the game.

The only time the 49ers didn’t get going was in a potential two-minute drive, with the offense going backward on a sack and an illegal shift.

Purdy was 8-of-12 for 123 yards with a touchdown plus six carries for 40 yards with a TD in the first half.

McCaffrey has 13 carries for 58 yards with a touchdown.

On the other side, Kirk Cousins had the Raiders’ touchdown early in the second quarter with a 2-yard QB sneak. Ironically, that made him the team’s rushing touchdown leader this season with two.

Cousins is 6-of-8 for 48 yards. Ashton Jeanty has 24 yards on five carries.

The 49ers doubled up time of possession in the first half, leading 20:35 to 9:25.

Las Vegas will receive the second-half kickoff.
