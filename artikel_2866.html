Arizona Cardinals make roster moves after Dallas Cowboys win

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

We knew the Arizona Cardinals would be down a couple of important pieces, but they made it official today, placing both Jeremiyah Love and Kendrick Bourne on injured reserve.

The Arizona Cardinals Football Club today announced the following roster moves:

– Signed wide receiver Harrison Wallace III to the active roster from the practice squad

– Re-signed tight end Rivaldo Fairweather to the practice squad

– Placed wide receiver Kendrick Bourne and running back Jeremiyah Love on injured reserve

– Released defensive lineman Cam Horsley from the practice squad

– Placed tight end Kenny Yeboah on practice squad injured reserve

The signings took place today at the Dignity Health Arizona Cardinals Training Center in Tempe, AZ.

Wallace will wear jersey #83 and Fairweather will wear #46.

They also added Wallace to the active roster while bringing back Fairweather to the practice squad, after their practice squad took a hit with Yeboah going on injured reserve.

Let's hope things get better quickly for the Cardinals health wise.
