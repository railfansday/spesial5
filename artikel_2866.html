Eagles make 3 injury roster moves before Steelers clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Eagles make 3 injury roster moves before Steelers clash appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

As the Philadelphia Eagles prepare for their Week 11 home matchup against the Pittsburgh Steelers, they face a challenging 2-7 record amid a turbulent season. On Friday, the team announced in their injury report that wide receiver DeVonta Smith would return after missing three games due to a hamstring injury. NFL Network's Ian Rapoport confirmed, "WR DeVonta Smith is officially back." However, linebacker Zack Baun and wide receiver Dontayvion Wicks were ruled out due to back and ankle/foot injuries, respectively.

Before the game, the Eagles' front office made several significant roster changes that affected the timeline for two injured players and welcomed back an important offensive asset. On Saturday, NFL Network insider Mike Garafolo reported the team had placed both Baun and Wicks on injured reserve, stating, "The #Eagles placed LB Zack Baun and WR Dontayvion Wicks on IR. RB Will Shipley was activated from IR." This decision to place Baun and Wicks on IR means they will be sidelined for at least four games, indicating their injuries require extended recovery periods. This will put additional pressure on Philadelphia's coaching staff to rely on their remaining depth in those positions.

On a positive note, activating Shipley, who had been out since Week 5 with a foot injury and was listed as questionable entering the weekend, provides immediate support to a backfield eager to enhance its running game alongside Saquon Barkley.
