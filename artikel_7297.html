Week of Nov 9 Evening News Ratings: WNT and CBS Evening News Grow

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A week after the midterm elections and the week the FAA issued a ground stop for all planes flying over the U.S. airspace, ABC World News Tonight with David Muir and CBS Evening News with Tony Dokoupil finished with gains in total viewers and the Adults 25-54 demo for the week of Nov. 9.

With more than 9 million total viewers for the second week in a row, WNT had its largest viewership since the week of Feb. 15, 2021. It continued to be the most-watched program on TV, including broadcast and cable (excluding sports).

Note: The weekly averages of ABC News and NBC News are based on four days (Tuesday to Friday, and Monday to Thursday, respectively). Monday’s broadcast (11/09/26) of ABC News was retitled to WNT, and Friday’s (11/13/26) edition of NBC Nightly News was retitled to NBC Nitely News. These broadcasts were excluded from the weekly and season averages.

According to Nielsen national live plus same-day Big Data + Panel and program ratings, ABC World News Tonight averaged 9.251 million total viewers and 1.366 million Adults 25-54 demo viewers for the week of Nov. 9. The evening newscast was up 1% in total viewers and 3% in the A25-54 demo compared to the previous week. Compared to the same week in 2025 (the week beginning Nov. 10), WNT was up 22%* in total viewers and 42% in the demo.

NBC Nightly News with Tom Llamas finished as the No.2 newscast in total viewers and the demo, averaging 7.117 million and 1.161 million viewers, respectively. Compared to the previous week, Nightly News was down by 2% in total viewers and flat in the demo–the only broadcast not to see any growth in any of the measured categories. Compared to the same week a year ago, Nightly News was up by 7% in total viewers and 30% in the demo.

Finally, CBS Evening News finished the week of Nov. 9 with 4.633 million total viewers and 689,000 demo viewers. CBS News’ newscast saw an audience lift of 7% in total viewers and 4% in the demo compared to the previous week. Same-week, year-ago numbers showed CBS Evening News grew by 11% in total viewers and 31% in the demo.

*Note: ABC’s 2025 national availability was limited due to a carriage dispute between Disney and YouTube TV.
