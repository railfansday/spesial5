GdS: Endrick and Tresoldi could both join Milan – how a double deal could work

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

There is a chance that AC Milan could sign both Nicolo Tresoldi and Endrick in 2027, but there would be a caveat to the moves.

Earlier today, it was reported that Milan had readied €70 million for the January mercato. With that money, the plan is to bring a new young attacker and there are two choices: Nicolo Tresoldi and Endrick. However, the possibility of both joining cannot be ruled out.

According to this morning’s edition of Gazzetta dello Sport (seen below), it would be possible to see both Endrick and Tresoldi representing the Rossoneri. However, a double swoop in January is hard to see, if not impossible.

The likelihood would be that one joined in January, with the other arriving in the summer after Champions League qualification if it were to happen at all. Both are strong and young, with high ceilings. So, a double coup would make sense.

Importantly, Gerry Cardinale is aware of this and he isn’t planning to give up on either. Of course, the context of the team matters and after January, only one of them may be necessary.

However, the American wants to raise the level of the squad and send a message. A six-month double swoop for two of the top talents in Europe would do that.
