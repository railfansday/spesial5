England throw it away with defending unworthy of a top nation

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

There had been so much talk – first raised by Thomas Tuchel after the shocking World Cup exit to Argentina – about England’s DNA. About whether they could keep the ball, pass the ball, control the ball, the way Spain do.

The world and European champions were England’s first opponents – after the World Cup – in this Nations League campaign and came away from Wembley with a deserved win. They are now 39 games unbeaten with substitute Mikel Oyarzabal, who scored the winning goal against England in the final of the last European Championship, doing so again.

Mikel Oyarzabal SCORES against England AGAIN! 🎯Spain retake the lead after a slick finish from the Spaniard 👏#ITVFootball pic.twitter.com/pPT3KvRHWl

That may have been no surprise, even if the inevitable “olés” after Spain’s third goal will have hurt. But the nature of it? For the England manager this was another shocker. Not in the way his team went about the task. They were positive, fun, attacking. But in the way they conceded – goals and chances – and lost.

On this performance, DNA, for England, stands for Defensively Not Able, as they threw away what could have been a famous and restorative victory. Instead, with Croatia beating the Czech Republic in the other group game, England are under immediate pressure to win in Prague on Tuesday.

In fact, England have conceded 13 goals in their past six matches – in fairness including four in that third-place World Cup 6-4 win over France – and that is not good enough for a top-tier nation. Tuchel needs to sort it out defensively.

It could have been very different if Harry Kane had not missed from the penalty spot, slipping as he struck the ball, with England improbably 2-1 ahead. “It would have been the decisive punch,” Tuchel stated. Maybe so, even if there was always the feeling that Spain could go through the gears.

Afterwards there were claims, led by the impressive Anthony Gordon, that this was a missed opportunity for England. A bit like the World Cup. They need to shake this theme.

The irony was this was one of their best creative displays for quite some time. They took the game to Spain and could have scored more. And conceded more.

In truth it could have been Spain 4 England 0 inside half an hour and before Tuchel’s team laid a glove on their opponents.

Another let off for England 😧Ferran Torres misses another big chance one-on-one with James Trafford #ITVFootball pic.twitter.com/KFkJoJUW6c

The manager had also spoken about how England should “embrace the chaos” as their best route to success. That may appear a questionable tactic – does chaos ever win a tournament? – but England certainly did that. This was as chaotic and, admittedly, entertaining as it gets.

England attacked with pace and power with the pick of their players the two based in Spain: Gordon, who scored, and Jude Bellingham. And the defenders? This was a bad night for Marc Guéhi, his Manchester City team-mate Nico O’Reilly (cue speculation about whether they were distracted by the verdict on the Premier League charges against their club) and for Jarell Quansah at right-back who is so evidently a centre-half.

A penny, once more, for the thoughts of the unused Trent Alexander-Arnold. The fourth defender, Ezri Konsa, vented his frustration at what was happening around him.

It was not just the errors but the unforced nature of them. Poor in possession with misplaced passes and defensively wide open. There is a gap between Spain and England but not as big as the gaps that were exposed in England’s back four. They endured a full house of calamities. They seemed strangely unaware – or incapable of dealing with – the danger posed by the outstanding Lamine Yamal.

Maybe because of the opposition – or the criticism – England felt the need to play out. But what an horrific start. What an inexplicable moment for Guéhi as he received the ball from O’Reilly and turned into trouble, gifting possession to Yamal who ran on and scored. Inside two minutes.

Spain walked out with the second star on their shirt – to mark their second World Cup triumph. That was the star Tuchel had promised for England.

Now, straight away, they were up against it. It could have turned very ugly. Ferran Torres, who had scored the winner in the World Cup Final, had one disallowed for offside, then was thwarted by James Trafford after – inexplicably again – being played onside by Quansah.

It could – it should – have been game over. Then England unexpectedly turned it around. They had kept attacking despite what was going on at the back and struck through a truly thrilling counter-attack, with Gordon and Bellingham combining before the Barcelona winger calmly beat goalkeeper Unai Simon.

England dominated for a while and Bellingham won a penalty, after a VAR intervention, only for Kane to strike the crossbar.

How Spain made them pay and worryingly so. O’Reilly passed the ball straight to Olmo who swept it inside. Substitute Álex Baena checked and beat Trafford from the edge of the area. Even worse was how Spain cut through the heart of England defence with Baena finding Oyarzabal. Why was no defender closer to him? What was Guéhi doing? And O’Reilly? Oyarzabal exquisitely dinked the ball over Trafford.

Thomas Tuchel does not think Manchester City’s guilty verdict over almost all of their Premier League charges distracted his England players in the Nations League defeat to Spain.

City players Marc Guéhi and Nico O’Reilly were at fault for two of Spain’s goals and both Tuchel, the head coach, and England captain Harry Kane confirmed news of the verdict had been discussed among the squad.

Tuchel does not plan to address the issue with his players ahead of the remaining Nations League games against Czech Republic and Croatia.

Asked if the City verdict had been a cause of discussion ahead of the Spain game, Tuchel replied: “Not for me, but I can imagine maybe for the Man City players. I won’t comment on it as it’s an ongoing case.

“I didn’t make it a topic. It was not a topic for me. I’m just thinking about Spain and the team and meetings. The players have more time than me. So I can imagine they talk about it. It’s good they do. I don’t think it was a distraction for them.”

Kane added: “There was a discussion, but when we’re with England, we kind of put club football to one side and it was no different this week.”

Tuchel also refused to point the finger of blame at Guéhi, O’Reilly or Kane, who missed a penalty, for England’s defeat despite admitting his team got off to the worst possible start by conceding a goal after two minutes.

“We will focus on the positives, but also highlight the stuff we can do better,” Tuchel said. “Not even speaking about individual mistakes because this is normal. It just happens. It should not happen, and for sure not in this amount. But there is no sense in showing the individual mistakes or telling them ‘please don’t make mistakes’. That is normal, but we can do things better. We can defend the third goal better. But we will focus on the positives. There is a lot to take away from the game.

“We wanted to be the team desperately to beat them [Spain] and we think we had them. We still think we had them at half-time. That was the way the team showed up after half-time. So we will focus on that, but the reality is we lost and the reality is we need to prove it again.”

“It was a terrible start, the worst possible start, which makes it so difficult against a team like this. The reaction was very good. After 10/12 minutes, we were the better team but we gave away too many mistakes. We were the better team. If we had converted the penalty, it could have been the decisive moment. We have to keep going and learn from the mistakes. We could have created more if we had been more clinical in the final 20 metres. We showed great bravery and courage with our high-ball wins. There will always be moments you suffer against teams like this. The effort and quality were there tonight but the result does not prove it today.”

“I think there is a gap [between Spain and England]. They [Spain] are the world champions and European champions. Mikel Oyarzabal was never going to miss. He was always in control of the situation. Honestly, people talk about sexy football, that is sexy football. I would watch that all night.”

Harry Kane wants England to address how they lost their identity and went “against our beliefs” at the World Cup.

In a brutally honest assessment of the tournament, the England captain admitted Thomas Tuchel’s team did not play to their full potential in several games – even before the painful semi-final exit against Argentina.

Kane, who played golf instead of watching the World Cup final, revealed the 2-1 Atlanta defeat hurt more than missing a penalty four years earlier in Qatar, when England were knocked out by France in the quarter-finals.

“This one hurt more than any other one. I would probably say even more than when I missed a penalty in Qatar because of the manner that it happened,” Kane said. “It was not us as a team.

“We trust our players and the player know very well that if you miss a camp then someone plays well in your position that the competition is always on. You have to make sure to be available and to produce top-level performances in camp and if you do so, that counts for something. Right now, it is not on my list of worries. I am not wired like this. From the moment I arrived at SGP my full focus is 100 per cent with the players who are here.

“There is no threat, distrust or threat of consequences. It will come naturally. I truly believe that we have the opposite. You will talk to our captain, Harry Kane, who has the most minutes for club and country and is happy to be here while teammates of his are enjoying a 10-day break. He is the role model and that is the vibe we have here.”

James Trafford is poised to start for England ahead of Jordan Pickford against Spain tonight.

Pickford has been England’s No1 for eight years but Thomas Tuchel is set to make a change to face the world champions in the Nations League opener at Wembley.

Trafford went to the World Cup in the summer and has started the Premier League in good form following his move to Leeds.

In the first match since the World Cup, changing his goalkeeper is a major selection decision from Tuchel.

Elsewhere, Jarell Quansah is set to play at right-back ahead of Trent Alexander-Arnold while Myles Lewis-Skelly is also in line to return to the starting XI after being recalled into the squad after missing the World Cup.

England begin their Nations League campaign with a home game against the world champions Spain. This is England’s first game since the World Cup, with their dreams of glory ended by Argentina in the semi-finals, but they did finish third after beating France in a high-scoring third/fourth play-off. The last meeting between these sides saw Spain beat England to win Euro 2024. They also faced off in the Nations League back in 2018, with Spain winning at Wembley and England taking victory over in Spain.

England’s plans for these games has been disrupted by a number of withdrawals. Cole Palmer had earned a recall having been left out of the World Cup squad but the Chelsea forward has withdrawn due to a minor injury, despite playing the full game for Chelsea against Brentford in the final game before the international beak.

“He [Palmer] plays with discomfort and pain and cannot play on the highest level with discomfort and pain,” Tuchel said. “Chelsea informed us. That is my understanding. We are not managing load. This is not an ‘international break’. It is a competitive environment, where the players love to train and be together. That is what we created. We will never compromise on that. We will never let this feeling creep in that the players do not want to come [to England camps]. We focus on the guys who absolutely want to play for England.

“For sure it is a missed opportunity. It is another missed opportunity. He misses too many opportunities. I do not blame him for it, it is just a fact. He misses way too many camps with injuries. I only want to rely on what I personally witness and see within my team. For that you have to be in camp. If you are not in camp there is a next level to impress that you cannot achieve because you can only do it with me on the pitch and with us on the pitch. It is a missed opportunity.”

Declan Rice has also withdrawn from the squad despite playing the full game in Arsenal’s defeat to Brighton as have Kobbie Mainoo, Marcus Rashford and Tino Livramento. Morgan Gibbs-White and James Garner have been called up as a result.

Trent Alexander-Arnold is back in the fold for the first time since June 2025. 18-year-old Liverpool winger Rio Ngumoha made his England debut against New Zealand during the World Cup warm-ups and takes his place in this squad, although he does make the matchday squad for tonight’s game. England will also take on Czechia, Croatia and then Cezchia again during this international break.

Having been crowned world champions back in July, Spain are now champions of both Europe and the world. Back in June 2025, Spain made the Nations League final but lost on penalties to Portugal. Ahead of this international window, The Royal Spanish Federation has extended Luis de la Fuente’s contract until 2032. After tonight, Spain will host Croatia and Czechia before travelling to Croatia to cap off this international window.
