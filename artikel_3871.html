WPCA: World Phasebound Control Authority ‘World’ trailer, closed alpha test set for December 16 to 22

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Publisher KRAFTON and developer 5minlab have released a new trailer for cooperative supernatural anomaly containment hunting action game WPCA: World Phasebound Control Authority introducing the game’s world, as well as announced that a closed alpha test will run for both PlayStation 5 and PC from December 16 to 22. Registration is available now via the official website.

“[WPCA: World Phasebound Control Authority] is an anomaly containment hunting action game,” reads the trailer description. “As an Operator of the Korea Branch, track down supernatural anomalies called Phasebounds, contain them, then craft weapons and refine your build to take on stronger ones.”

WPCA: World Phasebound Control Authority is due out for PlayStation 5 and PC via Steam in 2028.
