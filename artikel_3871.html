A fan-made recap of the entire Multiverse Saga has made its way online, and it's a pretty dang good one in case you don't have time to catch up before Avengers: Doomsday.

The video, which can be viewed below, takes all 15 of Disney Plus's suggested titles to watch in preparation for the upcoming film and turns them into a pretty snappy and concise 6-minute clip.

The titles include: X-Men, X2, Captain America: The First Avenger, The Avengers, Avengers: Infinity War, Avengers: Endgame, Loki (seasons 1 and 2), Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings, Spider-Man: No Way Home, Black Panther: Wakanda Forever, Captain America: Brave New World, Deadpool & Wolverine, Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, Thunderbolts, and The Fantastic Four: First Steps.

Marvel's been pretty good lately about making sure that you don't need to watch earlier projects to understand brand-new ones. For example, Echo was easily the most standalone Marvel project, though it did lead into Daredevil: Born Again and Wilson Fisk's decision to successfully run for Mayor of New York City. In VisionQuest episode 1, Vision gives the audience a quick rundown of what happened to him in Avengers: Infinity War and WandaVision.

My personal recommendation? The only thing you really need to watch before Doomsday is Avengers: Endgame... and maybe Loki, so that you have a solid understanding of the TVA.

Avengers: Doomsday hits UK cinemas on December 16 and worldwide on December 18. For more, check out how to watch the Marvel movies in order.
