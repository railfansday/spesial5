For Its Home Console Debut, New PS5 Professor Layton Game Ups the Price to $65

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Professor Layton and apprentice Luke aren't playing about with small handheld Nintendo DS and 3DS releases anymore; they're in the big leagues now with a proper PS5 and Switch 2 release. Professor Layton and the New World of Steam is the first time the series has appeared on a home console, and the price of the new entry matches that: £53.99 / $64.99.

Compared to previous titles in the franchise, this is a significant price increase — the DS and 3DS games were generally around $30 to $40 at launch, increasing as the series went on. Now, as the New World of Steam plans for a December 2026 release, it'll cost roughly double the price of Professor Layton and the Curious Village, which was the first game in the series.

It'll be interesting to see how much the PS5 remake of that costs when it comes out next year.

Nevertheless, this is no longer a PS5 game in impulse-purchase territory; Professor Layton and the New World of Steam is only $5 cheaper than Marvel's Wolverine!

As it makes the jump onto home consoles, Level-5 is promising the most puzzles in the series, a brand new exploration system, and a new puzzle added to the game every day for over a year after its launch in December.

Pre-orders are live now on the PS Store ahead of the 12th December 2026 release. Do you think this is a fair price for a Professor Layton game or not? Share your thoughts in the comments below.
