Hollow Knight: Silksong DLC News and 'Exciting' Reveals from Dev Team Cherry Coming This Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Team Cherry will provide an update on the Sea of Sorrow expansion it has in the works for Hollow Knight: Silksong this week, alongside a selection of other "exciting announcements".

A timed YouTube presentation is already uploaded to the developer's channel, with the description: "Join us for Cherry Time! A special presentation with a bunch of exciting announcements, and an update on Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow!"

Having released last year, Team Cherry has shared Hollow Knight: Silksong will be treated to multiple content updates, and Sea of Sorrow is just the first. It's been slated to launch in 2026 for a while now, so it seems likely we will get a firm release date on Friday, 27th November, when the video airs.

As for the other reveals, I wouldn't get your hopes up for anything substantial like another game or a look at the next big DLC. I would expect these updates to be more like merchandise lines and physical collectibles for fans.

Still, it's nice to know we'll learn about Sea of Sorrow at the end of this week.
