Congratulations to These 2026 Continental Divide Trail Finishers (Part 2)

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Continental Divide Trail is often regarded as one of North America’s most difficult and iconic thru-hikes, stretching over 3,000 miles between the US/Mexico border in New Mexico and the US/Canadian border in Montana. Every year, hundreds of hikers set out on this unforgettable journey, pushing their limits both physically and mentally.

Finishing the CDT is a massive accomplishment that requires physical determination, mental toughness, and a little bit of luck. Hikers from all walks of life are impacted by their time spent on the trail, and we are thrilled to spotlight the hikers who completed the CDT in 2026!

Have you just finished your CDT thru-hike? If so, please celebrate your finish with us by submitting a photo through the form below so we can feature you in a subsequent post. We can’t wait to share your incredible achievement!

“This thru-hike was epic. I am seriously contemplating doing it again in a couple of years’ time.” -Hitchmaster

“The photo on the US/CA border obelisk with the bear spray is from September 30. I completed the trail on October 20 at Rabbit Ears Pass near Steamboat Springs, CO, patching in a section I skipped over to catch up with friends after getting off trail for a month to work a job.” –Side Quest

“Talk to strangers, touch grass.” -Cap’n Crunch

“What a great adventure that concluded with both north terminus locations!” -Chit Chat

“It’s amazing what a walk across the country can do for the soul.” -Journey

Congratulations to all the 2026 CDT finishers — your achievements are remarkable and worth celebrating!
