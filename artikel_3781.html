Super Meat Boy Again+ announced for PC

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Team Meat has announced Super Meat Boy Again+, a game described as “everything you remember from Super Meat Boy presented through the wonderful lens of nostalgia with stuff added to it,” including new art, music, cutscenes, levels, secrets, and unlockables. It will be available for PC via Steam. A release date was not announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:

Super Meat Boy is a tough as nails platformer originally released to much fanfare and great acclaim in 2010. It is held as a gold standard in difficult but fair platforming. You play as Meat Boy, a boy with no skin who leaves blood everywhere, who is saving his girlfriend, Bandage Girl, from Dr. Fetus, a fetus in a jar wearing a top hat and a monocle.

Super Meat Boy Again+ everything you remember from Super Meat Boy presented through the wonderful lens of nostalgia with stuff added to it. Everything is new but familiar. We have new art, new music, new cutscenes, new secrets, and new stuff.

Since 2010, millions of people played Super Meat Boy, all the secrets are known, all the levels are mastered, all the characters unlocked, so we have to give fans familiar with the game something to pull their hair out over. That’s the “+” in Again+, and no, we won’t spoil it. You’ll go into it blind like you did the first time you played Super Meat Boy.

For our old fans, we want to present the game as you remember it through the wonderful lens of nostalgia.

For our new fans, we want to give you a great looking, easy to pick up but difficult to master platformer that you may have missed because it came out like four years after YouTube was invented.
