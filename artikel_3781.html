All Pirate Chest loot and rewards in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Treasure Hunters mini-season has spiced up Fortnite, since the regular chests will now include valuable items in them. While you can still get the usual stuff, some special cosmetics have also been included in the pool of rewards.

This guide will list every special reward you can find in the chests. Do note that certain rewards will rotate on a weekly basis.

The Pirate Shop Access tokens are perhaps the most valuable loot you can find through the course of the mini-season, since they can give you free Item Shop cosmetics. The odds for the items are different, and unfortunately, we have no way of knowing what the exact numbers are.

Based on what I have seen so far, the Pirate Shop access tokens are really rare, but you need to get lucky just once. There will be Power Hours as well to take advantage of if you have yet to find your access token. As of writing, we are waiting more information about the chest rewards that will be available during the second, third, and the fourth week.
