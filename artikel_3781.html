How to get the Piercing Gaze Hunter Mask in The Division 2: Echoes of Central Park

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Piercing Gaze Hunter mask is a secret item you can unearth in The Division 2, but you’ll need to access the Echoes of Central Park DLC. Then, you’ll have to solve a set of clues and recover a few letters to get your hands on this mask.

Let’s find out how to get this secret gear piece added to your in-game collection.

First, you have to head to the location shown on the image above. Go to the Lincoln Square district, and the spot will have a wall covered in leaves. It’s not difficult to find. You’ll notice a bunch of text saying “Break my Walls”.

Shoot the wall, and you’ll find other letters on it. Keep shooting until all the letters are visible. This is the first location where you can find them, but there are more in a second location.

Head to the marked spot on the image above, and you’ll find a second wall with the text “Break my Walls” written on it. Again, start shooting until you get three different letters.

Once the parts mentioned above have been solved, go to the southern part of the Lincoln Square district. You’ll find a roof with plant pots and statue. Destroy all of them.

Some of these pots seem indestructible, but you need to keep shooting at them. This will eventually summon the Hunter.

The only way to get your mask is to defeat the Hunter. As long as you stay clear from the Seeker Mines, you shouldn’t have any problem defeating the Hunter and obtaining the secret mask.
