Musk Joins 160,000 Signers Calling for Jensen Huang’s Nvidia Comeback: How Is NVDA Stock Reacting?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Jensen Huang petition goes viral and Elon Musk joins 160,000 signers. Return odds doubled Sunday and Nvidia jumped 3%, then faded. Levels to watch.

Elon Musk said on Friday, November 13, that he is signing the Jensen Huang petition, an open letter asking Nvidia’s board to bring back its former chief executive. Huang resigned a day earlier, after reports of extramarital affairs.

By Monday, the “Stand with Jensen” letter had more than 160,000 signatures. Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan and Uber co-founder Travis Kalanick have also backed it. The letter asks the board to reinstate Huang while an independent investigation establishes the facts.

We matched Polymarket’s odds of Huang’s return with a 24/7 Nvidia stock contract on Hyperliquid. Musk’s post lifted the odds but barely moved the stock. A sharper jump in the odds on Sunday evening did: the stock rose 3% within two hours, then gave it all back by Monday.

So how is Nvidia’s stock reacting, one day before earnings?

Musk posted on X that “a petition is underway to reinstate Jensen and I’m signing it too.” He had said earlier that hiring Huang would be “an honor.”

The petition is hosted at standwithjensen.com. Signers report their own job titles, and the site does not verify them. More than 7,500 say they work at the company, about 18% of its staff.

The company says it is choosing a search firm for the CEO role and has not formally approached any candidates. Chief financial officer Colette Kress runs the company in the meantime.

Nasdaq is shut on weekends, but the stock still trades around the clock as a perpetual contract, a futures-style contract with no expiry date, on Hyperliquid. To see whether the Jensen Huang petition moved the stock, we lined up its hourly price with Polymarket’s odds that Huang rejoins by December 31, 2026.

Polymarket’s longer bets show how far the Jensen Huang petition still has to go. Traders give Huang a 37.5% chance of rejoining by the end of 2027, but only 5.6% by November 30.

On the daily chart, Hyperliquid’s NVDA contract trades at $156.93. It sits below its 50-day exponential moving average (EMA), an average that gives recent prices more weight, at $199.26, and its 200-day EMA at $206.42. On Nasdaq, the shares closed Friday at $162.85, down 18.6% since November 4.

The Jensen Huang petition has moved the odds more than the stock. Musk’s backing barely shifted the price, and Sunday’s jump faded within a day. Tuesday’s earnings, the first under Kress, are likely to matter more to Nvidia shares than any petition.
