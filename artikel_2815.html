Josh Giddey sees progress after win over Nets

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Josh Giddey sees progress after win over Nets appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Chicago Bulls gave the Brooklyn Nets a rude awakening with a 137-107 road blowout. From the opening bell, the Nets put up points. The Bulls set a franchise record with 15 3-pointers in the first half. And the result showcased the best basketball they have played thus far.

That alone signaled greater things ahead, according to Josh Giddey, per K.C. Johnson of The Chicago Sports Network. After a poor start, which included an eight-game losing streak and injuries, Giddey was, pardon the pun, Giddy.

"I like where the team's going, and we're getting better every day," he said.

Altogether, Giddey finished with a double-double of 18 points and 14 rebounds along with nine assists. A real bounce back for him, given that he started the season struggling. Giddey sustained a wrist injury in the preseason opener against the Phoenix Suns. From there, he experienced poor shooting and turnovers.

He also scored his 5,000th career point in the game.

Giddey is averaging 10.5 points, 8.5 assists, and 5.9 rebounds per game. He is also shooting 43.1% from the field and 31.%% from the 3-point line.

Matas Buzelis set the tone with 25 points and went 5-for-9 from 3. Norman Powell scored 17 points and was also 5-for-9 from beyond the arc. Nic Claxton and Jalen Smith each finished with 14 points. Issac Okoro came away with 12 points, Buddy Hield had 11 points, and Tre Jones had 10 points.

The Bulls are now 4-10 and will take on the Washington Wizards at home on Friday night. Then, they will play against the Denver Nuggets on Sunday in Chicago.
