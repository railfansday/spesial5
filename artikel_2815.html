Dolphins sign K Daniel Carlson to practice squad, make several more roster moves

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Dolphins have made several roster moves on Tuesday.

Aside from placing defensive lineman Jordan Phillips on injured reserve due to a biceps injury, the club has also placed defensive lineman Zeek Biggers on the same list.

With more open spots on the 53-man roster, the Dolphins have signed quarterback Brady Cook, defensive lineman Jordan Jefferson, and edge rusher Eric Watts off of the team's practice squad.

Additionally, the Dolphins have signed receiver Brady Boyd, defensive lineman Matthew Butler, kicker Daniel Carlson, and defensive lineman Chris Smith to the practice squad.

Of the moves, Carlson is particularly notable because he becomes the kicker with the club. Zane Gonzalez was let go after missing two field goals and an extra point in Sunday's loss to the Colts.

Carlson hit 8-of-12 field goals and 16-of-18 extra points in seven games for New Orleans earlier this season.
