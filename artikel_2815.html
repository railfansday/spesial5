Saints vs. Panthers odds: Opening lines for Week 10 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

It's Week 10, and the New Orleans Saints (3-5) are back home again this week as they prepare to host the Carolina Panthers (3-5) in the Caesars Superdome on Sunday, November 15.New Orleans enters the matchup following a hard-fought 23-22 victory in overtime over the Cleveland Browns, while Carolina looks to rebound from a 26-21 loss to the Denver Broncos.

For the Saints, quarterback Tyler Shough will be a key factor as New Orleans looks to establish offensive consistency. Wide receiver Chris Olave remains an important weapon in the passing game, while the Saints' defense will need to create pressure and limit explosive plays.

For the Panthers, quarterback Bryce Young will look to lead Carolina's offense, with running back Chuba Hubbard providing a significant threat on the ground. Establishing the running game and controlling the clock could prove crucial for Carolina.

The key to victory in Sunday's divisional showdown could come down to turnovers, third-down efficiency and execution in the red zone. Both teams are tied for first in the division, so a win would provide momentum moving forward and put them on top the NFC South.

According to FanDuel, the Saints are currently 1.5-point favorites against the Panthers, with an over/under of 47.5 points. However, those odds should move as kickoff approaches.
