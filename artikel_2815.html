Rapid Recap: 76ers 132, Bucks 129

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The best team in the East, the Philadelphia 76ers, beat the reeling Milwaukee Bucks, 132-129. The win means the 76ers increase their win streak to five, while the loss means the Bucks increase their losing streak to 13. Despite the loss, it was a valiant effort by the Bucks, with Tyler Herro scoring a career-high 50 points and Brayden Burries posting a career-high 26, but Joel Embiid's 26-point, 17-rebound double-double proved too much.

Following LeBron starting the game with a vintage hammer, Tyler Herro (going back to the headband) quickly gave the Bucks their first points with a three. This back-and-forth from the stars continued for a bit, until Herro took the upper hand going into the first stoppage in the first quarter, at 6:24, with another three—the Bucks holding a 15-8 advantage. This seven-point advantage got trimmed to one, with the 76ers getting what they desired in the paint, taking a 21-20 lead. Although Jabari Walker started to get in on the paint punishment for the Sixers, Kel'el Ware jams and Herro's deep threat weathered the storm. The Bucks took a 30-29 lead into the second quarter, on the shoulders of Headband Herro's 15 points.

The second quarter began with more rim-wrecking and traded buckets, with Ware continuing to be a presence in the restricted area and LeBron going showtime to Dominick Barlow and VJ Edgecombe. At the second's first stoppage, at 6:41, the game remained close but in Milwaukee's hands, 43-40. After being a game-time decision and held quiet early, Tyrese Maxey showed up in the second quarter, scoring six points in a little over three minutes; however, Herro remained hot to neutralise him—20 points with 2:34 remaining in the half! An and-one late in the quarter for Joel Embiid would give the Sixers a 60-59 lead going into the break.

Again, Herro gave the Bucks their first bucket of the half, and Ryan Rollins followed that up with a fast-break lay. But LeBron continued to exploit the Bucks' struggling interior defense with a one-handed slam (even if he showed his age—this was no signature tomahawk). While the Bucks took a 68-63 lead, they lost it before the third's first break at 7:58, going down 70-71. Herro tried to counter Embiid's quick double-double, but between Kyle Kuzma shooting below 30% from the field and Myles Turner hucking threes at a 0/5 clip, the game began to slip away. 83-73 Sixers. Or was it? Herro's 35 after three had the Bucks within striking distance, down just six, 92-98, going into the fourth.

With five unanswered points less than two minutes into the quarter, Barlow forced the Bucks into a quick timeout. 103-92 Sixers. However, Headband Herro wouldn't quit! He'd have a 40-ball with eight minutes left. This, in turn, forced the Sixers into a timeout, as their lead was cut to three, 106-103. The end of the game went blow for blow, Herro and Brayden Burries vs. Embiid and LeBron, but age, a missed out-of-bounds call, and a waived-off buzzer-beater helped the Sixers prevail. What a heartbreaker, but what a game!

Tyler Herro scored 50 points on 15/23 shooting! While it didn't give the Bucks the win, it kept them within fighting distance of the top team in the East. It also could have been more if his game-tying buzzer-beater wasn't called off.

Split Myles Turner's minutes amongst Kel'el Ware and Jericho Sims. At least they grab boards and have awareness of their three-point capabilities.
