Joe Buck reveals why Northwest Stadium lights went out during Commanders’ win over Bengals, and it’s really silly

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Washington Commanders beat the Cincinnati Bengals 22-20 on Monday Night Football at Northwest Stadium, with Drew Stevens making a game-winning field goal. However, the night also included an unscheduled interruption, as the stadium briefly went dark in the third quarter, and ESPN broadcaster Joe Buck addressed the issue.

While the blackout didn't last long, Buck shared why it happened.

"If you want to know the reason why the lights went out earlier, the official word is the lighting vendor never disabled the regularly scheduled maintenance for 10 p.m.," Buck said.

"It's a weekday thing, and they forgot it was a Monday night game. Somebody turned out the lights. We're being told that it's power-related, and not the stadium."

Washington was down 10-7 at the time of the outage, with Stefon Diggs set to run a pass play. Cincinnati took a 17-13 lead and a 20-19 lead before Stevens' winning field goal. He ended the game with five successful field goals.

"Let there be light," Buck said once power returned to the stadium. The same venue hosted a Bears-Commanders Monday night game last October, and the lights stayed on at 10 p.m. ET that time.

In the game, rookie receiver Antonio Williams had nine receptions for 122 yards, with one being a 42-yard pass reception during the minute-left drive that led to the Commanders' game-winning kick. The win ended Cincinnati's five-game winning streak. Joe Burrow was 28-of-40 with 300 yards passing and two touchdown passes in the loss.

The win helped Washington go up to 4-5-1 with back-to-back wins, while Cincinnati dropped to 7-3. But the mistake did not decide the game, as Washington still had to move the ball and make the kick after play resumed.

The Commanders will play the Arizona Cardinals on Nov. 29, and the Bengals face the New Orleans Saints next week.
