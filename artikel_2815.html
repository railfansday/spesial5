Pistons' massive fourth-quarter collapse fuels loss to Hawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

ATLANTA — The Detroit Pistons entered the fourth quarter up by 11 points against the Atlanta Hawks. At the time, the Pistons had all the momentum and looked on the verge of their 11th win of the season. However, a seven-minute stretch inside State Farm Arena led to a 107-97 loss on Sunday afternoon, Nov. 22.

During that stretch, the Pistons put together one of their worst performances on both ends, arguably since the start of their resurgence began two years ago. On offense, the Pistons opened the final period shooting 0-for-7 from the field. Their defensive struggles matched, as they allowed the Hawks to embark on an 18-0 run. The pinnacle of the Pistons' struggles came at the 4:42 mark when Aaron Wiggins nailed a 3-point field goal to give Atlanta a 92-85 lead. The Hawks would go on to win the final period 33-12.

Isaiah Joe led the Pistons with 19 points, while Ausar Thompson added 18 points and 10 assists. Nickeil Alexander-Walker led the Hawks with 25 points.
