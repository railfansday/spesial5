This is confusing because Chai tea is black tea with milk in it.
Actually "chai" IS tea in much of the world.
This makes a nice spiced (masala) chai, popular in India and much of SE Asia.

Spice Mix (for making a mix to have on hand)
▪24 green cardamoms
▪18 black peppers
▪1 teaspoon anis seeds
▪1/2 teaspoon ginger powder
▪1/4 teaspoon cinnamon powder

grind all to powder in mortar and pestle

For making about 2 cups of chai
▪1-1/2 cup water
▪1/2 cup milk
▪2 teaspoons tea leaves
▪2 teaspoons sugar adjust to taste
▪1/2 teaspoon chai spice mix from above

Start with the spice and bring almost to a boil with water and milk, then add the tea leaves (loose black Assam or Jasmine work fine), cover, and turn off the heat, Let sit 2 minutes (longer if you want it stronger). Strain and enjoy a nice cuppa homemade chai.
