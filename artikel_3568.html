Man Kidnapped Teen Girl, Dog Before Abandoning Her 200 Miles Away From Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

An Ohio man found himself behind bars after he allegedly picked up a teenage girl and her labradoodle before abandoning her in Virginia nearly 200 miles away from her South Carolina home while keeping the dog for himself.

Darlington County Sheriff Michael August confirmed to Us that Matthew Morris Hawkins was charged with one count each of kidnapping, grand larceny and contributing to the delinquency of a minor following the incident that took place in August.

Authorities said that deputies responded to the victim’s home in Florence, South Carolina, on August 6 after they received a report regarding a missing juvenile, according to a Monday, November 16, press release.

A preliminary investigation was launched into the matter, and authorities concluded that Hawkins, 41, had gone to the 17-year-old girl’s home to pick up the teen and her dog.

Hawkins then allegedly took the teen to Martinsville, Virginia, where he subsequently “abandoned her.”

The defendant allegedly picked the girl up from her home at night without her parents’ knowledge or permission, according to an arrest warrant obtained by WBTW. One day after she went missing, Henry County Sheriff’s Office deputies found the girl in Virginia on August 7.

Troopers with the Kentucky State Police located Hawkins the following day on August 8 in Winchester, Virginia, and he was arrested without incident. Authorities reported that Hawkins allegedly had the girl’s labradoodle still in his possession at the time he was arrested.

Hawkins was taken into custody at the Clark County Detention Center in Kentucky before he was extradited to Henry County, Virginia. He was eventually extradited last week and processed at the W. Glenn Campbell Detention Center on Friday, November 13.

The defendant went on to make his first appearance in Darlington County District Court, where a magistrate judge ordered him to be held without bond.

It’s not currently clear whether Hawkins has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following his arrest. The Darlington County Sheriff’s Office had no additional updates to share when contacted by Us about the case.

The investigation into Hawkins and the alleged kidnapping remains active as police work to determine what events led up to his arrest.

Authorities have not yet revealed how Hawkins and the victim first connected or how long they were communicating prior to the alleged kidnapping.
