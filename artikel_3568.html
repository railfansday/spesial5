Perez Hilton Released From Treatment Facility 3 Months After Self-Harm Stream

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Perez Hilton has returned home from a treatment facility three months after a livestreamed self-harm incident.

“We wanted to share some more positive news about Perez and his continued recovery,” Hilton’s family began in an update on his website on Wednesday, November 18. “As we mentioned in our last update, Perez transitioned into a partial hospitalization program (PHP) in October while staying in supportive housing. We’re happy to share that he has since successfully completed that phase of treatment and is now back with his family while continuing his recovery through an intensive outpatient program (IOP).”

His family added that the program allows Hilton, 48, to “receive ongoing therapy and mental health support as he gradually returns to everyday life.”

The post continued, “Over the past few weeks, Perez has enjoyed several successful weekend visits with his mom and children. Being reunited with his loved ones has meant so much to everyone, especially after these past few difficult months.”

Hilton’s family added that they are “looking forward to spending some much-needed quality time together as a family and enjoying a little peace and quiet during the holiday.”

The update concluded, “Perez continues to make encouraging progress, and we’re taking things one day at a time. If and when he’s ready, we look forward to Perez being able to share an update with you personally. Thank you all for your continued support.”

Hilton was hospitalized in August after a disturbing livestream in which he engaged in acts of self-harm. He underwent surgery due to significant blood loss and remained in the hospital for several weeks before being transferred to an inpatient facility to focus on his mental health and recovery.

In October, Hilton began his PHP treatment, during which time he resided in supportive housing and continued receiving mental health care.

Earlier this month, Hilton’s family shared that he was able to spend time with his three kids and celebrate his mother’s 73rd birthday. (The blogger’s mom, Teresita Lavandeira, gained temporary custody of his children during his treatment, Us confirmed.)

“This entire experience has been a learning process for our family,” the November 3 update read. “Like many people who have never been through something like this, we didn’t fully understand just how much time, care, and support recovery from a serious mental health crisis can require.”

The post continued, “Through conversations with others who have been through similar situations, we’ve come to understand that getting through the initial crisis is only the beginning. There’s no one-size-fits-all timeline, and we’re learning the importance of taking things one step at a time.”
