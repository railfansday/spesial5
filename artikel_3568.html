Hayden Panettiere's Mom Reveals Why She Didn't Attend Daughter's Funeral

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Hayden Panettiere‘s mother, Lesley R. Vogel, is breaking her silence about why she did not attend her late daughter’s celebration of life in September.

“I just didn’t want to rock the boat; it was probably for the best,” Vogel, 70, revealed during the Monday, November 16, Inside Edition episode.

It was previously reported that Panettiere’s Nashville costars Connie Britton and Charles Esten, Evan Ross and Ruby Rose were among the 80 mourners to attend a September 19 private service in Malibu. Both of Hayden’s parents, Vogel and Skip Panettiere, were reportedly invited to the memorial though Vogel chose not to attend.

“I knew that some of the people there wanted to believe I was the horrible mother because Hayden was angry and made statements,” she explained to Inside Edition on Monday.

Vogel claimed that she’d last spoken to Hayden in “September [or] October of 2025,” adding of their notoriously complicated relationship, “It was a roller coaster ride, for sure.”

“She was very happy and we did spend a tremendous amount of time together up until the age of about 16, and then once she became the face of this major hit TV series Heroes, that’s when things started to change,” she alleged.

The Nashville star exclusively opened up to Us about where she stood with Vogel in 2025, revealing that they did not “have a relationship” at the time.

“That doesn’t mean that I don’t leave the door open for the opportunity to present itself one day,” she added. “It’s hard for me to say, but I’ve chosen to be brutally honest. So that’s the honest truth of it.”

Hayden acknowledged that some of the strain came from Vogel managing her early career as a child star before the two cut professional ties when she was 19.

“It’s so ingrained in me to be a people pleaser. I went on set, and it was all about being professional, nailing it and always hitting my mark. I had to be perfect. It was nice to hear positive feedback from people like the directors or producers, but without [my mom’s], nothing else mattered,” Panettiere told Us in May. “I felt like I had an identity crisis at 12 years old. I didn’t know who I was.”

Hayden was found unresponsive at an apartment in Greenville, South Carolina, in August, with investigators later determining that her death was accidental and caused by “toxic effects of fentanyl, 4-ANPP, Alprazolam, Methocarbamol, and Quetiapine.”

Following her daughter’s death, Vogel told the Daily Mail in August that she was “not abusive by any stretch of the imagination” while managing Hayden’s career.

“I think that Hayden put a pressure on herself,” Vogel argued. “I know that she has mentioned, you know, [that she] ‘always had to please Mom,’ or make sure it was, you know, I succeeded or whatever. I didn’t put that on her. I think she put that on herself.”

Hayden shot her first commercial just three months after Vogel signed her up with the famed Wilhelmina modeling agency at 8 months old. She landed her first recurring TV role as Sarah Roberts on One Life to Live at age 5, ultimately appearing on the soap from 1994 to 1997.

Before Hayden’s death, Vogel’s son, Jansen Panettiere, died at age 28 in February 2023 from “cardiomegaly (enlarged heart), coupled with aortic valve complications,” per a family statement.
