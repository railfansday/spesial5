The Trade Desk and Dstillery Link AI Agents to Reshape Audiences Mid-Campaign

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Trade Desk is connecting Ask Koa, its conversational agentic platform, with adtech firm Dstillery’s agentic platform, DS-1, to help ad buyers create custom audiences in minutes rather than days.

The integration lets the two companies’ AI agents communicate during live campaigns, using performance insights and an advertiser’s targeting goals to identify and build new audiences that can be activated through The Trade Desk.

Advertisers could already buy Dstillery’s custom audiences through The Trade Desk, but building them required manual coordination and typically took four days, said Taejin “TJ” In, Dstillery’s chief product officer.
