The Colts cut QB Daniel Jones. Should the Cardinals make a claim?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Arizona Cardinals are one of the league's worst teams this year. Another bad team, the Indianapolis Colts just released their starting quarterback, Daniel Jones.

Should the Cardinals make a waiver wire claim? Before Jones was released, he had trade value. The waiver wire is situated where the worst team has the first dibs, then the second worst, and so on.

This means that the Cardinals are seventh in line. The Titans drafted Cam Ward last year, the Chargers have Justin Herbert, Miami spent big bucks on Malik Willis in last year's free agency period, and Geno Smith for the Jets is back into the fold.

The big question might be for Arizona is if they grabbed Jones off waivers, would he become the default starter or just another stallion in the QB stable? Would he play, or sit until Carson Beck becomes injured, frustrated, or incapable of assuming the starting role?

While with the Colts, Jones was under a two-year $88 million deal.

Titans: Ward is doing okay considering the offensive line is weak. The team has a capable backup in Mitch Trubisky.

Chargers: LA got their first win with Herbert still under center. His backup, Trey Lance, is on IR which means QB3 D.J. Uiagalelei is now Herbert's backup.

Dolphins: Willis is on IR with Kyle McCord starting and Brady Cook his backup.

Jets: Starter Geno Smith has just returned from IR. Rookie Cade Klubnik is his backup.

Eagles: We are all aware that starter Jalen Hurts is on IR and gone for the year. In his place is Joe Milton who isn't exactly tearing up the league. Tanner McKee and Cole Payton are backups.

With the Colts picking at #6, they are out. The Titans look good along with the Jets. But the remainder could use Jones. So could Arizona.

Jones was taken with the sixth overall selection in the 2019 NFL Draft by the New York Football Giants after a standout career at Duke. He was supposed to be the future after Eli Manning retired. In 2022, Jones made NFL history by becoming the first player ever with 300+ passing yards, 2+ touchdown passes, and 70+ rushing yards in a playoff game.

The next year, Jones inked a four-year $160 million extension, but had a knee injury in Week 9. Late in 2024, he was benched for undrafted Tommy DeVito who set the New York area on fire as an Italian-American quarterback who was winning games and his downhome attitude. In late November, the Giants shocked everyone by releasing Jones.

He spent one season with the Minnesota Vikings before hooking on with the Colts in 2025 who signed him to a one-year $14 million deal and competed with Anthony Richardson for the starting role. Jones tore his Achilles tendon late in the season and landed on IR.

This season, he was named the starter for Week 1. Jones started all 11 games for a listless offensive unit which has been selling off their key players. Their offensive line is absolutely porous. This year, Jones hasn't had any time to throw and has paltry production such as 144 (twice) yards, 108 (twice), 143, and 166 in games. He has thrown seven touchdowns against 13 interceptions.

For his eight-year career, Jones' stats: 94 games with 93 starts, 1,903 completions on 2,976 attempts for 19,657 yards, converted 979 first downs, 96 TDs, 68 picks, a 63.9% completion ratio, sacked 257 times, an 84.1 QB rating, and a 35-57-1 starting win-loss record. He is a very good runner with 478 carries for 2,441 yards with another 21 scores.

Thursday night, the Colts lost to the then-3-6-0 Houston Texans 26-3 in a totally lopsided contest. Indy had 38 rushing yards while Jones went 12 for 27 for 144 yards, sacked twice and had a single pick with zero TDs.

The Colts' offense is ranked #30 in the league. They have allowed the eighth most sacks (28) and thrown the most interceptions with 13. Despite losing one of the best offensive guards in the league by trading away LG Quenton Nelson to the Seattle Seahawks before the trade deadline, the Indianapolis offensive line is ranked #17.

By claiming Jones, it could mean the Cardinals would have a capable quarterback for their remaining games this season who would compete with Gardner Minshew and Carson Beck in training camp next year.

Then again, what would be the cost to bring him in? None of the four quarterbacks on the roster are costing the franchise hardly anything.
