Knicks’ fading NBA Cup hopes on line against Cavaliers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Knicks' slim NBA Cup hopes hang in the balance.

Wednesday's game against the Cavs at Madison Square Garden is the Knicks' third of four group-stage games, having dropped their first two group-stage games to the 76ers and Pacers, respectively.

In the three-year history of the Cup, no team has ever advanced to the knockout round with anything worse than a 3-1 record. The winner of each of the six groups along with two wild cards (the top team from each conference that finished second in its group) advances to the next round.

Jalen Brunson (left) and Mikal Bridges high five during the Knicks' win over the Pacers on Nov. 22, 2026 at Madison Square Garden. NBAE via Getty Images

So while those first two losses didn't officially eliminate the Knicks, they put them in a deep hole to defend their NBA Cup title. The Knicks enter Wednesday's game last in their group and as the only one without a win.

Another loss Wednesday would officially eliminate the Knicks.

Wednesday's matchup is already the third meeting between the Knicks and Cavs this year, having split the first two. The Knicks lost the most recent matchup 115-107 on Nov. 11 at MSG.

"They were real physical in Game 2," coach Mike Brown said after practice Tuesday. "You give them credit. They talked about keeping us off the glass and some other things and they did it. They're a great coached team with Kenny Atkinson and a great team in general.

"There's gonna have to be a lot of things that we do better in this game, starting with the level of physicality, while getting out in transition, if we expect to get a win."

The Knicks will still be short-handed despite a two-day gap in between games.

OG Anunoby (left calf strain) and Jordan Clarkson (right plantar fasciitis) are both out for Wednesday. They both missed the Knicks' back-to-back wins Saturday and Sunday and neither practiced Tuesday.

Anunoby's injury changed from calf soreness to a calf strain, but the Knicks are still optimistic that he and Clarkson should be short-term absences and consider the duo day to day.

Josh Hart (right shoulder sprain), Karl-Anthony Towns (right knee inflammation) and Miles McBride (left adductor tightness) are all questionable. All three practiced Tuesday, though.

McBride was a late scratch for Sunday's win over the Pacers due to the injury.

The Cavs will be without Evan Mobley, who has a toe injury.
