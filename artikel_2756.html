Where Jaguars land in Week 12 power rankings after Giants loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Jacksonville Jaguars lost in a stunning 23-18 upset to the New York Giants. The Jaguars had won three games in a row and were receiving tons of buzz as possible Super Bowl contenders, and while they remain on top of the AFC South, losing to what many likely see as an inferior team will stop that momentum in its tracks.

Jacksonville would have to go on a massive losing streak through the rest of the season to miss the playoffs, but the question is still inevitably going to be raised about how legitimate this team truly is.

The Jaguars are now 7-3 and maintain a three-game lead over the Houston Texans in the division, but they will need to be able to defeat difficult opponents, like the Texans and the Chicago Bears, to reclaim their status as Super Bowl-bound contenders.

Here is where Jacksonville landed in the Week 12 power rankings.

How strong do the AFC's division leaders look as the final third of the regular season approaches? Not strong enough to beat apparently middling NFC East outfits.

That was a terrible showing against the Giants. Was it a one-game aberration or a hint of who they might be?

A "disastrous" loss for the Jaguars at this point constitutes a five-point defeat in which one of the most clear and obvious touchdowns of the season was called incomplete. Somehow, I think Liam Coen and the boys will be O.K.

The Jaguars lost to the scrappy New York Giants, but the bigger concern was how awful they looked in losing that game. QB Trevor Lawrence was under constant pressure and was rattled into some remarkably awful throws. The Jaguars committed an uncharacteristic 13 penalties. Head coach Liam Coen even made some confusing and inexplicable analytics-based decisions that cost his team. They steamrolled their first three opponents coming out of the bye week, but it looks like they just ran out of gas in New Jersey. It happens to everyone at some point, but it's still not a great sign for a team that loaded up at the trade deadline. The Jags' deals were supposed to make them stronger. Instead, they looked weak.

Stat To Know: Jacksonville snapped a four-game streak of scoring at least 30 points in the loss to the Giants.

Something to be thankful for: The AFC South

The Jags still have a three-game lead in the division despite Sunday's stumble, and it doesn't look like they're going to get any competition for the division title with the way the Houston Texans, Indianapolis Colts and Tennessee Titans are playing. This team still has some things to prove, though. Jacksonville hasn't beaten a team with a winning record since Week 4.

Extension candidate: WR Parker Washington

Washington is the Jags' No. 1 receiver, and more importantly, he's the one quarterback Trevor Lawrence goes to in critical situations. Washington leads the team in targets (81), catches (44) and receiving yards (748); his average of 17 yards per catch is tied for third in the NFL. He is also just 100 yards shy of surpassing his career high for receiving yards (847 last season). The 2023 sixth-round pick and the team have been in extension discussions since the offseason, but nothing has materialized yet. The Jaguars might have to use the franchise tag to buy extra time for a deal.

Even the best of teams stub their toe every now and again. Maybe Sunday's sloppy loss in New York was just a one-off—a bump in the road on the way to a deep playoff run.

But this was the second loss in three games this season on the road against a team with a winning record at the time. Jacksonville quarterback Trevor Lawrence threw a pair of picks in one of his more uneven performances of the year. The Jags rushed for just 77 yards as a team. They also committed 13 penalties for over 100 yards.

Barring an epic collapse, the Jaguars are going to run away with the AFC South, and just two of their last seven opponents sport winning records.

But a Week 13 trip to Chicago now looms larger on the schedule.

Because the Jags need to show they can beat a good team on the road.

They need to figure out how to win when it's not coming easily.
