Game Preview #14: Wolves vs. Trail Blazers Game Thread

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Timberwolves had done a great job in currying good favor so far this season with their play in Ant's absence, but that was put to the test in a Saturday night brutal loss to the Chicago Bulls. The Bulls were on an eight-game losing streak and took it right at the Timberwolves from the opening jump. Combine that with the bad shooting variance for Minnesota, and it was a tough pill to swallow after the highs of the five-game winning streak.

As with anything in life, it's not about about getting knocked down but more how you get back up. Minnesota has a great opportunity to do just that this week with multiple marquee matchups as they continue plugging along on their long home stand. Tonight's matchup features the first of a back-to-back against the same team in the Portland Trail Blazers

The Timberwolves have made this a staple of their season over the past few years, usually heading out to Portland to take on this squad in a mini series. This time, they get it at home with a great chance to right the ship against a Portland team that has come out of the gates red hot.

Who: Minnesota Timberwolves (9-4) vs. Portland Trail Blazers (9-3)

Where: Target Center – Minneapolis, Minnesota

For years, it was a question of when – not if – Chris Finch's trusted assistant coach and halftime interview icon Micah Nori would get the opportunity to run his own team. That chance came for him this summer as the Portland Trail Blazers moved on from Tiago Splitter and gave the charismatic Minnesota assistant a chance to head up the young Trail Blazers roster. While much was made about the terms of the deal – owner Tom Dundon opted for an atypical one-year contract – Chris Finch aptly silenced that discourse with one true statement: "I just know this. There's 30 of these jobs. They're hard to get."

Anyone who watch the Timberwolves since Micah Nori got here knew that he would someday get his chance to be the head coach of a team. His combination of basketball smarts with his charismatic personality combine into a great leader for any organization to get behind. Timberwolves players, coaches, front office and fans have an affinity for Coach Nori, and it's a certainty that he will get a nice homecoming when they face off for the first time tonight.

Saturday's loss was one that everyone wants to forget about. While it is frustrating and there are any number of things that can be dissected and agonized over, the beauty and curse of the NBA is that there is always another game on the horizon. The Timberwolves and specifically LaMelo Ball had gone on a tear over the best week plus to navigate their way to five wins in a row, and the Bulls game felt like a punch in the face after all that success. Ball shot four of 19 from three as the team shot 24% as a unit from downtown. That's a jarring drop-off from their usual numbers this season.

It will be important to see how Ball and the team respond from that matchup and look to keep the general arrow going up. Portland has been playing extremely good basketball as of late and they are very committed to a harassing style at the point of attack. LaMelo Ball has progressively gotten better at attacking those defenses early on in this season and he'll need to continue that trend as the Wolves look ahead to this mini two-game series.

The Rockets game is the blueprint, and something to watch is his ability to be flexible and bounce back after the loss. The mini series against the same opponent brings with it a chance to have a more in depth gameplan, so watching what LaMelo's focus is in these two games will show exactly how Finch wants to deploy him after a game like Saturday.

Jaylen Clark made one of his typical hustle plays before saving the ball and coming up wincing during the Bulls game. After walking back to the locker room immediately, he was diagnosed with a hamstring strain that will hold him out for a couple of weeks. Soft tissue injuries are nothing to mess around with and we are all wishing him a speedy recovery as he had been a crucial part of the non-Ant Wolves over this stretch of time. Looking ahead, it will be something to watch as to how Chris Finch backfills that time.

Finch had mostly been going with a 10-man rotation to start the season without Edwards (and when Shannon was healthy), but with Clark now unavailable it will be interesting to see how he navigates that going forward. If he still wants to keep 10 guys in the rotation, Cody Williams or Isaiah Evans would be the next logical players to get a crack at playing time. Cody Williams had a small chance earlier on in the season, but wasn't able to gain much traction when he was out there. He will most likely will shrink the rotation to nine, as was the case when the Wolves were without Terrence Shannon Jr., but it will be interesting to see if one of those end of the bench guys gets a shot at playing minutes in this time without Jaylen Clark.
