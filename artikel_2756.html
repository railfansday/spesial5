Skip Bayless questions Vikings’ Kirk Cousins trade as he makes bold Kyler Murray claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Skip Bayless has questioned the Minnesota Vikings' decision to trade for Kirk Cousins, insisting they could have had a better chance of winning the NFC North if they had stuck with Kyler Murray.

The Vikings acquired Cousins from the Las Vegas Raiders for a 2027 third-round pick after struggling to find consistency at quarterback. The 38-year-old is expected to take over as the starter following the team's decision to move on from Murray.

Bayless, however, believes Minnesota may have given up on Murray too quickly. Despite backing the Vikings to win the division before the season, the analyst remains convinced the former Arizona Cardinals quarterback offered them a higher ceiling.

Speaking about the trade on social media, Bayless admitted he was surprised by Minnesota's decision.

"My mind is on Minneapolis and the move the Vikings just made," Bayless said. "I did pick the Vikings to win the North. I'm hanging in with it as they fall to 6-2. The two reasons I picked the Vikings were obviously: No. 1, the defense, and No. 2, Kyler Murray. I remain a big Kyler fan… And I'm amused and shell-shocked over the new narrative he's too little…"

Bayless also argued that Murray's recent form made the decision even harder to understand.

"I think the Vikings' ceiling would have been higher with Kyler Murray, because he's been playing pretty well the last three games," Bayless continued. "I don't hate the Kirk Cousins move, only because he's been there, done that… He's obviously better than Carson Wentz… But in the end, I don't know why they gave up so quickly on Kyler for Kirk Cousins."

However, the statistics favour Cousins at this stage of the season. Murray has recorded 1,022 passing yards, five touchdowns and three interceptions through six games, completing 64.9 per cent of his attempts.

Cousins, meanwhile, has thrown for 2,340 yards and 20 touchdowns with eight interceptions, completing 64.1 per cent of his passes.

Former NFL linebacker Emmanuel Acho has also urged caution over Minnesota's Super Bowl hopes, saying: "Let's not now act like Kirk Cousins is Peyton Manning. Kirk Cousins is still who Kirk Cousins is; he's competent enough to get you to the playoffs."

Cousins now faces the challenge of helping Minnesota turn its quarterback change into results, with a divisional meeting against the Green Bay Packers awaiting him.
