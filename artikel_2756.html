Raptors star Scottie Barnes gives Allen Graves ‘my MVP’ praise after win over Pistons

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Toronto Raptors finally got back in the win column on Friday night, holding off a late push from the Detroit Pistons for a 105-102 NBA Cup victory at Little Caesars Arena. While Scottie Barnes led the way offensively, the Raptors star made sure to shine the spotlight on rookie Allen Graves after the youngster's impressive first career start.

Graves delivered a double-double in the win, finishing with 20 points and 10 rebounds on 6-of-9 shooting while going a perfect 6-of-6 from the free-throw line. The 20-year-old also racked up three steals and a block, making his presence felt on both ends of the floor.

After the game, Barnes couldn't stop raving about the rookie's performance.

"We fought, we stayed resilient, we stayed together. These young guys played so well. We had guys banged up that was playing tonight, just being resilient, we fought," Barnes said, via NBA on Prime. "AG played a hell of a game, oh my gosh. Rebounding, steals, his on-ball defense was amazing, rebounding. AG, my MVP."

Barnes was spectacular in his own right, pouring in a game-high 29 points on 14-of-21 shooting to go along with five rebounds and five assists. Immanuel Quickley also recorded a double-double with 18 points and 10 assists.

Toronto led by as many as 14 points, but Detroit made things interesting late. Cade Cunningham finished with 23 points and nine assists for the Pistons, while Kevin Huerter added 20 points and four 3-pointers.

The victory snapped a six-game losing streak for the short-handed Raptors, who improved to 6-10 on the season. Barnes made it clear that the team isn't satisfied with where it stands.

"It's a daily progress, daily progression. Take one game at a time, keep getting better," Barnes said. "We know what we're about. We know we're not losers. That's not what we're about. We're trying to win, we're trying to build success here, we're going to do whatever it takes."

For Graves, the No. 19 overall pick in the 2026 NBA Draft, the performance was a significant step forward. After earning a spot in the rotation as a reserve early in the season, the rookie showed on Friday that he can handle a much bigger role.
