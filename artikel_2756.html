Chiefs News: Chiefs bring back rookie return man to practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

On Wednesday, the Kansas City Chiefs reunited with a rookie undrafted free agent who spent time with the team this summer and at training camp. Jacob De Jesus, listed at 5 feet 6 inches, is a backup returner who will monitor the injury report for the Chiefs' starting returner this week.

Kansas City Chiefs bring back familiar face at wide receiver on the practice squad after hosting 9 players for tryouts on Wednesday | A to Z Sports

The timing of the move stands out. Chiefs wide receiver Nikko Remigio was limited on Wednesday's injury report with a hip injury. DeJesus served as the team's second punt returner during training camp and the preseason, working behind Remigio. The decision to sign De Jesus could have something to do with Remigio's status and availability.

At the very least, it's good depth. It also creates some redundancy in the return game, where the Chiefs have been OK but not necessarily explosive. De Jesus is the type of player who could add that explosive element. He could be elevated from the practice squad if Remigio has to go on injured reserve or miss time for an extended period.
