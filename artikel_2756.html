NY Giants bring return man they just cut back to practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Charlie Jones, just dropped from the 53-man roster this week when Xavier Gipson was signed off the Philadelphi Eagles' practice squad, has been re-signed to the team's practice squad.

Jones had a 10.0-yard average on 16 punt returns and a 23.8-yard average on 14 kickoff returns. He also has two receptions for 66 yards in limited time at wide receiver.

Offensive lineman Geno VanDeMark was dropped from the practice squad to make room for Jones.
