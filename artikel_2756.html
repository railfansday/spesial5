Jerry Jones makes surprising statement about 49ers takeover

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

One of the biggest shockers from the 49ers' 46-40 road victory against the Cowboys on Sunday was the takeover of AT&T Stadium by San Francisco fans.

Cowboys owner Jerry Jones appeared on "105.3 The Fan" on Tuesday morning and was asked his reaction to 49ers fans filling up his stadium and said, "That was not a shocker."

Jerry Jones downplays 49ers fans taking over AT&T Stadium on Sunday. AP Photo/Jeff Roberson

"Well, I don't know if you saw the price of those tickets that they were paying … but it was unbelievable," Jones said. "I would say the highest price of any ticket."

Jones said the 49ers have "always had a way above-average attendance to our away games here in Dallas" and that longtime Cowboys season ticket holders blamed "this secondary market."

Jerry Jones meeting with members of the media following Sunday's game against the 49ers. AP Photo/Jeffrey McWhorter

"Many of our fans have seats licensed. So they technically own those seats," Jones said. "For the most part, fans that have been out there for years, and years, and years. A lot of times, our ticket holders will maybe take two or three key games of the year and sell those tickets."

While Jones insisted that Cowboys fans price-gouged 49ers fans for their tickets, Dallas has certainly enjoyed a large turnout by its fans this season.

According to ESPN, Dallas ranks first in the NFL for attendance, averaging 91,766 fans per home game. San Francisco averages 71,131 fans per home game.

After the game, Jones was asked about the 49ers fans' invasion and gave a defensive response: "We have a lot of [Cowboys fans] on the road ourselves."

San Francisco 49ers fans seen filling up AT&T Stadium during Sunday's game. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

That is accurate, too. ESPN reported that Dallas has averaged 73,450 fans per road game.

Cowboys quarterback Dak Prescott was also aware of the sea of red in the seats and told reporters he "[t]ricked myself, told myself early the red was for my high school."

Prescott applauded the 49ers Faithful. "Their fans were more present than ours. But it created a great atmosphere, so take the good out of it one way or another. Leave it there."

San Francisco's fans impacted Prescott's offense, forcing Dallas to utilize a silent count.
