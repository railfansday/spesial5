Monday Night Football: Chargers lead Ravens 17-10 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Chargers lead the Ravens 17-10 at halftime on Monday. That is more points than the winless Chargers have scored in five games and the same as they scored in a 23-17 loss to the Broncos.

They scored 23 against Seattle and 22 last week against Houston.

Los Angeles scored on 3-of-5 first-half possessions, getting a 22-yard touchdown run from Justin Herbert, a 38-yard Cameron Dicker field goal and a 1-yard Omarion Hampton touchdown run.

Hampton’s touchdown came on fourth-and-goal with 1:56 remaining in the half. Roquan Smith initially had Hampton stopped before the running back got a push from offensive lineman Cole Strange and a pull from fullback Scott Matlock. Officials should have penalized Matlock for the pull, as pulling is illegal.

The Chargers outgained the Ravens 175 to 161, with Baltimore having no rushing yards on six carries.

Herbert, who injured his left hand in last week’s loss to the Texans but wasn’t on the injury report this week, went 6-for-9 for 91 yards in the first half. He also ran for 45 yards on six carries.

Hampton has 36 yards on 10 carries, and Tre’ Harris has five catches for 86 yards.

Jacoby Brissett, making his first start for the Ravens after the trade from Arizona, has completed 14 of 23 passes for 163 yards and an interception. Del’Shawn Phillips had the pick on a pass that was behind Ja’Kobi Lane and deflected to the Chargers linebacker.

Derrick Henry has a 2-yard touchdown run, but he has minus-1 yard on five carries. Mark Andrews has four catches for 41 yards.
