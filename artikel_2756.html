Betting odds released for Michigan Football at Oregon

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Michigan Wolverines are on a downward spiral and now have a road trip to a hostile environment. Michigan will be heading to the Pacific Northwest and taking on the Oregon Ducks this Saturday.

Per FanDuel Sportsbook, Michigan is currently a 11.5-point underdog against Oregon, with the over/under set at 37.5 points.

Michigan is 3-3 all-time versus Oregon but hasn't beaten the Ducks this century — going 0-3 since 2003. Michigan most recently lost to Oregon 38-17 at Michigan Stadium in 2024.

Oregon is currently 6-3 this season and is coming off an impressive 41-28 road win over Ohio State. Oregon's losses have come against Oklahoma State, UCLA, and Illinois. Quarterback Dylan Raiola has started to play well after being thrust into QB1 duties following Dante Moore being out for the season — Raiola has thrown for 11 touchdowns and two interceptions with a 65.4% completion rate.

Michigan is trending in the wrong direction after losing 15-6 to Michigan State. Michigan offensive coordinator Jason Beck was fired on Sunday, and offensive coordinator Jim Harding will be Michigan's interim OC. This doesn't feel like something that will bode well for Michigan winning the game or covering the spread.
