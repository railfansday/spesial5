Warriors without Steph Curry leads to embarrassing NBA downgrade

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Steph Curry is expected to miss significant time with an oblique injury.

Friday's blowout loss against the Spurs was the Warriors' first matchup without their superstar. The following day, the NBA announced that Wednesday's game between the Mavericks and Warriors will no longer be televised by ESPN.

Instead, the network will broadcast Celtics-Nuggets at 6:30 p.m. PT. The Warriors' game was changed to 5 p.m. PT.

Stephen Curry #30 and Yaxel Lendeborg #1 of the Golden State Warriors high five during the game against the New Orleans Pelicans on November 9, 2026 at Chase Center in San Francisco, California. NBAE via Getty Images

Without Curry in the mix, it's clear the Warriors lack a certain amount of star power on the screen.

Rookie Yaxel Lendeborg has had a phenomenal start to the season, but his performance against San Antonio showed there is still room for improvement as the rookie attempts to navigate a leadership role with Golden State.

Golden State Warriors head coach Steve Kerr speaks to the media before a game against the Miami Heat on Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, in San Francisco. Yalonda M. James/San Francisco Chronicle via AP

The Warriors will reevaluate Curry in two weeks, but until then, Lendeborg will be a pivotal part in trying to keep the team afloat while its superstar is sidelined.

Last season, the Warriors went 13-26 in games Curry didn't play. The hope is that Golden State can retain its winning record while the 38-year-old recovers.
