Mid-Hudson men's basketball: Army, Marist open 1-1, Orange 2-0

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The 2026-27 Mid-Hudson college men's basketball season got underway on Monday, November 2, and will conclude in mid-March.

Our local coaches are Kevin Kuwik (Army, fourth season), John Dunne (Marist, ninth season), Keith Kenney (SUNY New Paltz, tenth season), Mike Duffy (Mount Saint Mary, first season), Tom Rickard (SUNY Orange, 14th season), Tim Johnson (SUNY Sullivan, first season) and Jonah Kaminer (SUNY Ulster, third season).
