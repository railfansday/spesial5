DK Metcalf ejected after two personal fouls and two receiving yards

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Steelers wide receiver DK Metcalf had a miserable game today in Philadelphia, and now his day is over.

Metcalf was ejected after picking up two personal foul penalties today against the Eagles, both for taking completely unnecessary shots at Eagles players after the play was over.

Other than the ejection, Metcalf did nothing notable today. Aaron Rodgers threw him four passes, and Metcalf only caught one of them, for two yards on third-and-3. (The Steelers got stuffed on fourth-and-1 on the next play.)

It’s been a terrible season for Metcalf, who has done nothing to justify the big price the Steelers paid for him when they traded for him and signed him to a new contract last year. Metcalf’s cap hit for the Steelers will be more than $35 million next year whether he’s on the team or not. Trading for him has proven to be a big mistake.

The Steelers have had a rough day today for other reasons, including the loss of Alex Highsmith with an ankle injury. Highsmith had two sacks and a forced fumble before leaving the game, and his injury represents a big loss for the Steelers.

The score is 16-9 Eagles in the third quarter.
