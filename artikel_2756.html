There’s something to like about the way the Suns lost in Houston

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Phoenix Suns went to Space City to battle the team whose logo resembles one of those silly little patches that go on astronaut suits, ultimately losing by 12. What that final score doesn't tell you is that with 4:45 remaining, the Suns were down three points. What killed them down the stretch is what killed them throughout the game, and it shouldn't come as a surprise to any of us. Rebounding was the culprit. Or, more specifically, their inability to rebound.

Phoenix was outrebounded 51-38 overall and 20-13 on the offensive glass, but it was the 27 second-chance points they surrendered that ultimately sunk them. When Alperen Sengun pulls down 11 rebounds, and seven of them are of the offensive variety, you know you've done something wrong.

But my takeaway from this game? The impressively physical brand of basketball the Suns played. They stood up to a Rockets team that clearly had revenge on its mind as they ran up and down that bright red clowny court. After all, Phoenix had already beaten Houston earlier this season. When the Rockets came out with a chip on their shoulder, the Suns rose to the challenge and battled them throughout.

The final result comes down to two things. Houston possesses a more talented roster, and they're bigger, badder, and better on the boards. The attitude with which the Suns played, however, was something I thoroughly enjoyed watching. That's who this team was a season ago, and perhaps they needed a team like Houston to push them around a little, to jostle them enough to draw a line in the sand and say, "We're done with this."

The hope is that they carry that attitude forward. Because if they can bring that level of intensity and fight night in and night out, nobody is going to want to play that version of the Phoenix Suns. We know that because we saw it last year.

The rebounding issues aren't going away, and there will be nights when Phoenix simply doesn't possess the personnel to overcome them. But physicality and effort are things this team can control regardless of the opponent. What we witnessed in Houston was a willingness to compete on somebody else's terms without being intimidated by them. The Suns lost the basketball game, but somewhere on that obnoxiously red court, they rediscovered a little bit of their swagger.
