Mowbray steps down from Blackburn due to health issues

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Tony Mowbray has stepped down from his role as Blackburn Rovers head coach due to health issues.

The 62-year-old returned to football at West Brom last year following his recovery from bowel cancer, before taking charge at Blackburn in June.

In a statement, Mowbray said: "Unfortunately, due to health issues, I have to step back from my role as head coach of Blackburn Rovers.

"I'm immensely proud of this group of players. Their dedication and professionalism have given us some memorable performances and wins, and I've loved working with them.

"Thank you to my coaching staff for all your hard work and for the support you've given me. It's been a pleasure working alongside you.

"To the fans, thank you for the way you've supported me and the team this season. I've always tried to give you a team that you can be proud of. Your support has meant a great deal to me.

"I'm grateful to the owners and Suhail [Shaikh, Blackburn's chief operating officer] for giving me the opportunity to lead this great club once again.

"Thank you for your understanding. Tony."

Mowbray had enjoyed an impressive start to his second spell at Blackburn. Tipped for relegation by many, Rovers currently sit just three points off the play-offs in 13th. In their last game before the international break they beat West Ham 2-1.

Damien Johnson and Mike Dodds will take charge of first-team affairs heading into Sunday's game with Burnley.
