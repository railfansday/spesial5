Sky Sports to show women's TGL as Hull, Woad star in new league

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Charley Hull and Lottie Woad are among the LPGA Tour stars involved in the inaugural women's TGL season; every match of the WTGL is live on Sky Sports, featuring five teams, with the season launching on November 8

Sky Sports has secured exclusive rights in the UK and Ireland to the WTGL, the new women’s team golf league in partnership with the LPGA Tour.

Beginning on November 8, the inaugural WTGL season will bring the innovative team golf format - played in a custom-built indoor arena in Florida - to the women's game.

Both the 2026 and 2027 competitions will be broadcast exclusively on Sky Sports as part of an expansion of its golf rights, which also includes a further two years of TGL presented by SoFi.

English stars Charley Hull and Lottie Woad, plus former world No 1 Jeeno Thitikul, will be among the first wave of players to compete in the inaugural season of the women's TGL.

Five teams - each consisting of a five-player roster - boast 48 combined Solheim Cup team appearances between them, including eight of the 2026 line-ups and 15 major championships.

The competition will feature the same key components implemented in the men's, including mic'd up players, risk-reward team decisions and a variety of match formats.

The addition of the WTGL further strengthens Sky Sports' commitment to championing women's sport, bringing fans closer to the biggest moments, with every match - including the post-season play-offs from December 20-23 - live on Sky Sports.

New York Golf Club: Linn Grant, Danielle Kang, Lydia Ko, Yana Wilson and Rose Zhang

The men's competition will expand to seven teams in 2027, with Rory McIlroy, Shane Lowry and Brooks Koepka among the players involved for season three.

McIlroy's Boston Common Golf - who signed Shane Lowry in the off-season - kick off their campaign against new team Motor City Golf Club, featuring Brooks Koepka and rising star Jackson Koivun.

Tiger Woods' Jupiter Links Golf Club will feature in the same double-header against defending champions Los Angeles Golf Club, a rematch of last year's SoFi Cup Finals, with the 15-time major champion hoping to play more frequently than last season.

Tommy Fleetwood and Justin Rose feature for Los Angeles Golf Club, with season three launching December 27 at the SoFi Center and at least 12 new PGA Tour players joining TGL ahead of the new campaign.
