Derby ease pressure on boss Eustace with comeback win over Bolton

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Derby County vs Bolton Wanderers. Sky Bet Championship.

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Derby County and Bolton Wanderers at Pride Park on Friday night; Rams hit back in second half with goals from Ryan Nyambe, Jack Robinson and Rhian Brewster.

Derby eased the building pressure on boss John Eustace as they hit back to beat Bolton 2-1 in the Championship.

It was just a second win all season, a first home win at Pride Park, and a first victory in 11 games on Friday night.

They still find themselves four points adrift of safety, but will be feeling far better heading into the international break.

It looked like it was going to be another bad night for Derby as Bolton dominated the first half and took the lead just before the break through Ruben Rodrigues.

But the change was rapid after the break. Ryan Nyambe poked in his first ever career goal - in his 309th appearance - within 60 seconds of the restart. Jack Robinson then quickly netted to complete the turnaround.

Bolton pushed to find a way back, but Rhian Brewster thumped in a late third to seal a badly-needed win.
