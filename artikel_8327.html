Top 10 Resorts in the East for Grooming (2027)

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Powder days are rarer out East compared to the West, so grooming crews have learned to make the most of what they have. Readers noticed: this year’s survey brought more praise than ever for the overnight teams who leave the slopes in perfect corduroy by morning.

Grooming can be a thankless job, because most skiers will never encounter a snowcat during the day. Night crews work tirelessly to ensure that skiers have the best possible time on the slopes—and our seasons would be far shorter without them.

The resorts below are where that work shines, with reliable, well-kept grooming and a mix of natural and manmade snow made for carving. Whether you’re still learning to link turns or live for fresh corduroy, read on.
