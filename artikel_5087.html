Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse has blamed giant tech IPOs for draining money out of crypto, as XRP trades near $0.90 despite strong growth at the company. Speaking to Bloomberg in Singapore last week, Garlinghouse was asked why XRP’s price is not keeping up with Ripple’s expansion.

Garlinghouse said crypto is a risk asset, so investors sell it first when they need cash. When SpaceX went public earlier this year, many traders moved money out of crypto to buy into the listing, and the market fell sharply. He said the same thing happened around Anthropic’s IPO, which took place on the day of the interview.

He admitted it has been a tough year for the whole crypto market.

Utility Will Separate Winners From Losers

Looking ahead, Garlinghouse expects crypto assets to stop moving together. Tokens with real use and real demand should start to outperform, while coins driven only by hype will struggle. He warned that being “just a meme coin trading on speculation” is a hard place to be over the long term.

The gap between the company and the token keeps getting wider. Ripple is targeting a $900 million annual revenue run rate, and its RLUSD stablecoin has grown about 80% over the past year. Large XRP holders have also pulled more than $127 million from Binance since November 10.

At the time of writing, XRP price is trading near $0.91 and is down 1% in the last 24 hours.
