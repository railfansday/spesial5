Toobit, the award-winning global cryptocurrency exchange, has been named Best Digital Assets Trading Platform at The Digital Banker’s Digital Assets Awards 2026.

In its assessment, the judging panel highlighted Toobit’s global reach, range of trading services, and security measures. Serving more than 4 million traders, the exchange offers spot and derivatives trading alongside automated strategies and copy trading. The panel also noted Toobit’s 1:1+ Proof of Reserves, $40 million Shield Fund, and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification.

Toobit has continued to develop its trading capabilities, offering more than 2,500 trading pairs across crypto, TradFi, and on-chain markets. Its futures trading features include up to 500x leverage and guaranteed price for added slippage protection on eligible orders. The exchange has also expanded its AI-first trading suite, including SmartK indicators and the AI Trading Assistant to support market analysis and trade planning.

This marks Toobit’s sixth industry award of 2026. Earlier honors include Digital Asset Derivatives Platform of the Year from Hedgeweek, Global Exchange of the Year from FinanceFeeds, and Best Exchange for Trading Tokenized Equities from CoinGape.

The Digital Assets Awards recognize organizations advancing digital asset services through innovation, measurable results, and operational excellence. This year’s programme evaluated more than 100 initiatives submitted by 75 institutions across 20 markets, with entries assessed on innovation and differentiation, client and market impact, depth of implementation, and security, controls, and scale.

Toobit is where the future of crypto trading unfolds. The award-winning cryptocurrency derivatives exchange provides zero-fee spot trading, AI trading tools, and high leverage for both crypto and TradFi markets. Built for those who thrive on exploring new frontiers, Toobit maintains a fair, secure, and transparent environment for traders to navigate digital asset markets.
