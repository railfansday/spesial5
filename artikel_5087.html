OpenAI’s Changes to ChatGPT Could Drastically Cut Usage Limits for Paying Subscribers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

“We’re beginning to roll out the combined Chat and Work experience to a small group of Pro users to learn from feedback before we roll out more broadly,” OpenAI's Thibault Sottiaux announced on X Tuesday.

Under the new system, spending an afternoon discussing philosophy with ChatGPT could then leave users with less capacity to build an app later that evening. Conversely, developers who exhaust their allowance writing software could find themselves restricted to Instant chat—and that model is quite dumb.

OpenAI will measure consumption in tokens, the small units of information AI models process and generate. Instant chat will not count toward the shared allowance, so the change does not mean all conversations will suddenly become capped.

“We expect most users won't need to wait for their usage to reset more often than they do now,” OpenAI wrote in its guidance. What the company hasn't disclosed is the new token budget for each plan, whether it will preserve the equivalent of both existing allowances combined, or how much individual users' effective limits could shrink.

The responses to Sottiaux's announcement were largely negative, with most users arguing that keeping conversational and agentic usage separate was precisely what made ChatGPT worth paying for.

“My weekly allowance in Codex was always used in under 8hrs. Now I’ll have barely any usage to do anything,” wrote X user Rooke Poole in response.

Imagine be a paid user, Pro subs at that, and OpenAI do this to you without you even wanting to be in the small group. Acting like this is a good thing is terrible PR, honestly honesty would be their best policy on this one. The only reason for the merging of Chat and Work is for…

Another user, Nicolas Z, reported that ChatGPT and Codex displayed identical usage allowances after the merger reached his account. Veronica Craft reported losing the ability to edit messages when using certain models.

It also fits into OpenAI's broader push toward agents, which has shifted the company's attention from answering questions to performing entire jobs.
