Insta360’s X6 Winter Update Syncs 360° Video Capture with the Slopes App

Insta360 has announced a winter update for the X6 360-degree camera that ties footage to activity data in the popular ski and snowboard app Slopes, linking the footage to GPS data without needing a dedicated GPS accessory.

Slopes is a popular ski and snowboard app that provides digital trail maps for thousands of resorts worldwide. Because it is tied to a smartphone, the app tracks GPS data and provides riders with detailed information about their runs beyond just standard fitness data, such as top speed, average speed, lift versus run times, and more.

The Insta360 X6 now pairs with the Slopes app through the Insta360 app, providing riders with the ability to tie their footage to the data Slopes records. For example, “footage and data help riders review their progress and revisit moments from their season, from a favorite descent to a family ski day,” Insta360 explains.

“Riders can also connect their phone to X6 using the camera’s new Phone GPS Link feature. Once connected, X6 records GPS data with each shot, with no separate import step or dedicated GPS accessory required.”

Because the X6 captures 360-degree video, riders can combine that footage with the additional data provided by Slopes for specific cuts, like terrain or friends, as well as the ability to highlight specific runs.

“AI Director can identify highlights and assemble an edit,” Insta360 explains. “Dedicated skiing and snowboarding templates give riders another quick way to turn the day’s footage into a video they can share. When filming a friend, InstaFrame 2.0 keeps the selected skier or snowboarder in frame and produces a ready-to-share 4K flat video. With supported settings, 360 Video Backup also saves the full 360 degree view, so riders can go back and choose a different angle from the same run.”

The update to the Insta360 app, which allows the X6 to pair with the Slopes app, is free and will be available starting November 24. The integration will require a firmware update to the X6 as well, but that is performed through the app per usual. Slopes also requires a separate subscription.
