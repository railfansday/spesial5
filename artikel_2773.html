ESPN previews Saturday's massive USC-Indiana matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

On Saturday, the USC football team will travel on the road to Bloomington to face Indiana. With both the Trojans and Hoosiers still alive in the Big Ten and College Football Playoff races, it is a massive game for both teams.

On Friday, Bill Connelly of ESPN previewed the matchup.

"Indiana fell five spots in the CFP rankings without playing. Curt Cignetti generally doesn't seem to need help motivating his players, but he has an extra card to play in that regard this week.

"Motivation is one thing; the Hoosiers still need better quarterbacking. Josh Hoover's performance fell off a cliff after suffering an only vaguely specified arm injury against Ohio State, and Cignetti needed backup Grantto save the Minnesota game two weeks ago. It appears Hoover will start Saturday, but running back Turbo Richard is out, and the Hoosiers won't defend their national title without better quarterback play.

"They do still have a dynamite defense. IU ranks first in yards allowed per play (4.1), and they're equally good against run and pass. But USC will test the Hoosiers secondary's depth. IU cornerback A.J. Harris is working back from an injury, and though Jamari Sharpe remains an excellent CB No. 1, USC quarterback Jayden Maiava has distributed the ball to a number of targets -- five players have between 21 and 32 catches. USC was shut down by Wisconsin but scored at least 25 points against four other SP+ top-30 defenses. If the Trojans put at least three touchdowns on the board, can IU's offense follow suit?"

Kickoff between USC and Indiana is set for 12:30 p.m. Pacific time/3:30 p.m. Eastern time Saturday on CBS.
