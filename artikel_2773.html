Wan'Dale Robinson out for season with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Titans will not have one of their key receivers for the rest of 2026.

Receiver Wan’Dale Robinson is out for the season after being placed on injured reserve this week with an ankle injury, head coach Robert Saleh told reporters in his Wednesday press conference.

Robinson signed with the Titans as a free agent in the offseason, reuniting him with former Giants head coach Brian Daboll, who serves as Tennessee’s offensive coordinator.

In nine games with three starts this season, Robinson caught 38 passes for 272 yards with three touchdowns.
