In-form Cumbria rivals set for derby

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Cumbria rivals Barrow and Carlisle meet in the National League on Saturday with both teams going into the game in exceptional recent form.

The eighth-placed Bluebirds have won seven and drawn one of their past eight league games, while the Cumbrians, who are sixth, have won six and drawn one of their past seven.

Barrow are looking for a first league win over their county rivals since April 1960 and midfielder Keaton Ward has said the team are thriving.

"Every day we come into training, standards and intensity are through the roof and it is showing on a Saturday," he told BBC Cumbria.

"That comes with results and backing from the gaffer and the fans. We know they are behind us and if we try something and it doesn't come off they'll be right behind us again.

"I know when three o'clock comes on a Saturday everyone is willing to go to war for one another."

Carlisle boss Rob Elliot wants his side to stick with the principles that have helped them break into the play-off places after a slow start to the campaign.

Elliot's men were 19th after winning just two of their opening 10 league matches.

The former Newcastle United goalkeeper said his charges need to avoid matching the "physical" approach of their opponents at Holker Street on Saturday.

"You have to be very strong in terms of the way you play and double down on your concepts and the way you go about things," he told BBC Cumbria.

"If you don't, you fall into the trap of not being yourselves and trying to match the opposition at what they do and the fact is Barrow are the best at what they do, that's why they've had the results they've had.

"If we try to match them or play into their hands then ultimately that is what they want.

"It's obviously a bit of a chess match but I think, for us, it's that we create an identity and we stick to it, that's what the best teams do."
