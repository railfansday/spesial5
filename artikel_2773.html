Live Coverage: Patriots celebrate Rob Gronkowski as they take on Packers

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The New England Patriots will cap off a weekend that included Rob Gronkowski's induction into the team's Hall of Fame and a celebration of the 2016 championship team when they play the Green Bay Packers during Week 9.

New England is once again without A.J. Brown and will rely on Drake Maye to overcome their depleted wide receiver corps. Maye will have Mack Hollins back.

12:25 — Maye is sacked by Isaiah McDuffie and the Patriots go 3-and-out quickly.

13:19 — 13 yard touchdown run by Jordan Love. Miserable opening for the Patriots who start the game with an illegal shift and an interception that turns into a score for Green Bay. Trey Smack gets the extra point.

14:14 — On the second play of the game, and his first pass of the game, Maye is easily intercepted by Xavier McKinney. The pass was intended for Mack Hollins. The interception puts the Packers at the 14 yard line.
