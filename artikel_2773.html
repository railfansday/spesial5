What is fan confidence after the Puka Nacua injury?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Puka Nacua is expected to be out for the next two months, essentially ending his 2026 season, and the news should be a major blow in confidence among the team and their fanbase. The Rams went into the year as favorites to win the Super Bowl by plenty of betting sites, and talking heads. Heading into their Week 11 bye and Week 12 matchup against the Green Bay Packers before Thanksgiving, the Rams don't look as promising.

A survey was posted this week, and by its conclusion it had over 60% of Rams fans expressing confidence that L.A. was headed in the right direction. Knowing what we know about Nacua now, is confidence still high? If almost 36% of fans were concerned before L.A. essentially lost their best offensive player for the year, how many more fans are concerned now?

The only thing at this point that will give the fans any faith, is a win over the Packers on Wednesday. A loss, and the season isn't over, but it would be on life support. A win, and perhaps even with the Nacua news, fans might still believe that L.A. is headed in the right direction, and on track towards the playoffs or more.
