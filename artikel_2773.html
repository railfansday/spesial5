Colts elevate Natrone Brooks, Reid Holskey for Thursday's game vs. Texans

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Colts have made a pair of elevations for Thursday’s game against the Texans.

Indianapolis announced the club will have cornerback Natrone Brooks and guard Reid Holskey on the active roster from the practice squad for the start of Week 11.

Brooks is set to make his Colts debut. He’s appeared in 26 career games with one start, all for Atlanta from 2024-2025.

Holskey is set to appear in his first regular-season game.

Kickoff between Indianapolis and Houston is set for 8:15 p.m. ET.
