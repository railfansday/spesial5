Report: Cowboys to sign RB Kendre Miller to the practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Cowboys are expected to sign running back Kendre Miller to the practice squad, Joseph Hoyt of the Dallas Morning News reports.

He visited the team facility today after the Saints cut him last week.

The Cowboys need depth at the position, with Tyler Goodson expected to miss multiple weeks with a knee injury. Fullback Hunter Luepke is the only player behind starting running back Javonte Williams on the active roster. Israel Abanikanda and Jashaun Corbin are on the practice squad, and it is unclear whether the Cowboys will cut one of them to sign Goodson.

Miller had 57 carries for 159 yards and two touchdowns in six games for New Orleans this season. The 2023 third-round pick had 184 carries for 656 yards and five touchdowns during his injury-plagued run with the Saints. He also caught 29 passes for 217 yards.
