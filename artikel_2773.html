Bojan confident Barcelona starlet Hamza Abdelkarim ‘will be one of the best’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Bojan has given his full backing to Barcelona youngster Hamza Abdelkarim and think he has a big future ahead of him.

The striker is having to be patient at Barcelona this season but the club have demonstrated their faith in the Egypt international by handing him a contract until 2030.

Bojan has been asked about the best attackers in the world and chose to speak about the Barcelona youngster and give him his full backing.

"We have the best forwards in the world," he told Tot Costa.

"What's more, we have a young player who will be one of the best. Hamza Abdelkarim. He demonstrated his goalscoring in pre-season, it's something hard to find and we have it."

Abdelkarim has returned to Barcelona after featuring as a substitute in Egypt's two AFCON qualifiers against Malawi.

There's been speculation over the international break about a possible January move away from Barcelona, but he is expected to stay and will be hoping for first-team chances with Hansi Flick's side.
