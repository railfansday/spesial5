Console Archives Nobunaga Oda den launches November 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Console Archives Nobunaga Oda den will launch for PlayStation 5 and Switch 2 on November 12 for $11.99 / 1,200 yen, developer Hamster announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via Hamster:

Nobunaga Oda den was released by Koei in 1998 for a 32-bit home console.

This simulation RPG vividly depicts the glorious life of Nobunaga, who ushered in Japan’s early modern era.

Experience historically accurate battle scenes, including the Battle of Okehazama, the Siege of Inabayama, and the Battle of Nagashino.

A branching storyline where your choice of tactics and battle outcomes can lead to alternate historical paths.

A cast of 22 key figures, including members of the Oda clan, warlords from various provinces, and cultural figures.

Command allied forces by analyzing intelligence and executing precise strategies…

…and enjoy all the rich elements that bring “Nobunaga Oda den” to life!

Can Nobunaga escape the blazing flames of Honno-ji and finally bring an end to the Sengoku period?

(The game includes only the Japanese version. Please note that all in-game text is in Japanese only. The options menu and manual are available in Japanese, English, French, German, Italian, and Spanish.)

The Console Archives series is designed to bring classic games originally released on home consoles to modern gaming platforms.

In Console Archives series, features such as customizable button layouts, rapid-fire settings, multiple save points, and for some titles, a rewind function have been added, to support players in completing these classics.

Additional options include screen layout adjustments and filters that recreate the the nostalgic atmosphere of CRT TVs back in the day, allowing players to customize the visual experience to their preference.

Whether it’s a title you played long ago or one you’re experiencing for the first time, we hope you’ll find a favorite and enjoy the game your way.
