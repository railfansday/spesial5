The “Choppy Rachel Bob” Is the Most Flattering Winter Hair Trend—How to Recreate

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Jennifer Aniston, the inspiration that you are.

I may be dressing like a walking blanket thanks to the drop in temperature (comfort > style, am I right?), but at least my hair—and more specifically, my recently cut bob —looks great. There's something about winter that calls for a total beauty refresh (and in my case, a drop in fashion presentation, but we shall overlook that), and seemingly, Meghann Fahy would agree, having just debuted a chin-length style inspired by the ’90s. So long, Goldilocks!

The The White Lotus actor debuted the haircut in question on The TODAY Show earlier this week, where she spoke all about her role in new thriller The Good Daughter. And while we're, of course, excited to hear all about the series, it was the bob chat that piqued our interest.

When asked about her new ’do, Meghann shared that the reference photo she showed her hairstylist was of “the Rachel,” Jennifer Aniston's iconic haircut from Friends. And yes, that is thee haircut that sent approximately every woman with a television to her local hair salon in the mid-’90s.

"It's like looking in the mirror," Meghann joked when shown a picture of Rachel Green.

Now, while we're not talking an exact replica of the original, Meghann's take feels like a much more wearable update (because we can’t all have that ’90s volume at all times). So, if you, too, want to recreate the style for winter 2026, because why wouldn't you, ask for a blunt yet choppy silhouette to create the illusion of fuller strands, and a little textured wave for movement and body. Oh, and, of course, show a reference picture of Rachel. Duh!

And honestly, winter is the perfect time to try the shorter style. The neck-skimming length looks oh-so-chic when peeking out from oversized scarves, while the shape gives hair a little more structure when the weather is doing its absolute worst.

Rachel Green (and Meghann Fahy), you've done it again.
