Xbox Game Pass Has A Busy November Ahead With Several Day-One Launches

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The start of the month is a good time to be an Xbox Game Pass subscriber, as that's when Microsoft reveals the next batch of games coming to the service. November is frontloaded with new game releases before November 19--we can't imagine why--and several of them are day-one releases.

Kicking things off is the online horror co-op game Pit of Goblin. This has been in early access since 2024, and November 3 marks its 1.0 release.

Next up is Escape Academy 2: Back to School on November 5. This is a day-one release, and it's all about exploring a campus, escaping life-threatening puzzle rooms, and uncovering a deadly conspiracy. November 10 will be a big day for Warhammer fans, as Warhammer Survivors launches. It's Vampire Survivors with a grimdark spin, so expect dozens of characters and weapons to master in this bullet-heaven game.

Hope in the City launches on November 11, and this stylish game follows a young girl as she searches for her parents in Shenyang's seedy underbelly. Later in the month, you can blast zombies in John Carpenter's Toxic Commando, join the PAW Patrol for a dinosaur adventure, and wrap things up with the third-person sci-fi adventure.

As a reminder, more games will leave Game Pass this month, with 14 titles heading out in the first wave of departures.
