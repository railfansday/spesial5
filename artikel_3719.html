How Meredith Marks Really Feels About Monica Garcia's Possible ‘RHOSLC’ Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

When it comes to Monica Garcia possibly returning to The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City, Meredith Marks isn’t completely disengaging with the idea.

“Well, as you know, that’s not up to me. But I don’t know,” Meredith, 54, exclusively shared with Us when promoting season 7 of the Bravo reality show. “I mean, I have to see how things unfold a little bit. I don’t know where it would make sense for Monica to come back in or not.”

Last month, Andy Cohen raised eyebrows when he was asked which one-season Housewife may deserve a second chance.

“I’m gonna say, I mean look, if any of them were friends with her, Monica Garcia,” he said during the “Ask Andy” portion of Bravo Fan Fest 2026.

Monica, 43, herself saw the clip and decided to reshare the moment via Instagram, writing, “We know this made some people BIG MAD😜🥰😘 Happy Friday🫶🏽🤍 #rhoslc.”

News broke in January 2024 that Monica would not be returning to RHOSLC after making a splash in season 4.

During a cast trip to Bermuda, Monica owned up to posting on the RealityVonTease account. The social media page often posted cruel rumors about the cast. According to Monica, the page was created to expose former friend and RHOSLC alum Jen Shah.

“I have a lot of questions about things that did happen in Bermuda that I feel have been a bit unanswered as to, like, why things really did unfold that way,” Meredith explained to Us. “I wouldn’t say I’m completely opposed. I don’t know if it makes sense, but I am definitely not completely opposed.”

The DJ and creator of Meredith Marks Caviar went as far as to compliment Monica for her appearances on Carlos King’s “Reality With the King” podcast, where she often recaps new episodes of RHOSLC.

“I mean, full disclosure, I will say I have found Monica and Carlos King to be quite entertaining together,” she told Us. “I will say that. So, you know, I don’t know if it’s the right move or not. I can’t tell you that, but I’m not totally closed off from it now.”

Instead of worrying about future casting decisions, Meredith is focused on navigating the changing dynamics in the current cast of ladies. There also may be a new diss track ready to be released in the coming months.

“I mentioned working on a diss track, but that wasn’t really ‘Hypocrisy and Bulls***,’” Meredith teased, referencing a song she controversially sang at costar Bronwyn Newport’s party in a recent episode. “I had written, after last year with the plane and all the nasty behavior toward me, I had written this diss track. And then I tabled it for a while. … I decided to take it off the table, and it is in production right now. It’s coming.”

The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City airs on Bravo Wednesdays at 8 p.m. ET. Stream new episodes the next day on Peacock.
