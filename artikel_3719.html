Nicole Richie and Joel Madden's Daughter Kate Signs With Modeling Agency

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Nicole Richie and Joel Madden’s 18-year-old daughter, Kate, is ready to make her mark on the fashion industry.

“I’ve always dreamed of modeling, so getting to start this next chapter with The Lions is incredibly exciting,” Kate told Women’s Wear Daily in a Friday, November 20, statement, revealing she signed with the modeling and talent agency. “Fashion has always been a way for me to express myself, and I can’t wait to explore that in a whole new way.”

She continued, “I’m looking forward to learning, growing, meeting new people and seeing where this journey takes me.”

The Lions founder Ali Kavoussi is equally excited to work with Kate.

“We’re thrilled to welcome Kate Madden to The Lions,” Kavoussi told WWD in a separate statement. “She brings a distinct point of view, a clear creative vision and the ambition to build a career beyond traditional boundaries.”

The fashion exec added, “We see tremendous potential in Kate and look forward to championing her individuality and building for the long-term, connecting her with the right partners and opportunities to expand her influence and pursue her creative and entrepreneurial ambitions.”

Kate, who has gone by her middle name since childhood, made her modeling debut earlier this month in Balenciaga’s 25th anniversary campaign for its Le City handbag. (The purse was initially closely associated with Richie, 45, in the early aughts.)

“I’ve wanted to model since I was young, but I felt like, now that I’m 18, and I have a very specific look, it’s the perfect time for me to start,” Kate told W magazine in a Thursday, November 19, profile of the campaign costarring Richie. “It was so fun to do it with my mom. It was good for me to do my first campaign with her because I had someone who has more experience helping me through it.”

As Kate steps into the limelight, she has her parents’ full support. (Madden, 47, and Richie also share son Sparrow, 17.)

“She’s such a nice girl, she’s really smart, she’s a lot like her mom,” Madden gushed on a March episode of the “Dumb Blonde” podcast. “She’s incredibly talented with what she loves, which is fashion and design and things like that. She has a great eye, and I’m excited to see what she does, what she chooses to do.”

Elsewhere on the podcast, Madden revealed it “wasn’t a big deal” when Kate opted against being known by her birth name, Harlow.

“We went home [from a parent-teacher conference], we’re like, ‘You go by Kate at school?’ She goes, ‘Yeah, I like it.’ And we were like, ‘OK, great,’” the Good Charlotte musician recalled. “Everyone who knows her calls her Kate. It’s kind of smart, actually. She knows if someone called her Harlow, they don’t really know her.”
