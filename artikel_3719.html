Memorable Golden Bachelor and Bachelorette Contestants: Where Are They Now?

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Guy revealed in April 2025 that he had “found my person” in Johanna Boston.Several contestants from The Golden Bachelor and The Golden Bachelorette have captured the hearts of Bachelor Nation and become another generation of memorable faces from the franchise.

After not finding The One in OG Golden Bachelor Gerry Turner, Joan Vassos was crowned the next lead of the series and ultimately got her happily ever after with Chock Chapple. As Joan entered the new chapter of her life, she also played matchmaker with the casts of The Golden Bachelor and her season of The Golden Bachelorette.

“I’ve already set three of them up — we have love connections that are brewing right now. And I have two more that I’m looking at and working on,” Joan exclusively shared in November 2024 as part of Us Weekly’s cover story. “We have to see how that is going to unfold. There are already five guys from this season [now] involved with somebody. They came off of this and they were popular guys, and I’m not surprised that some of them have girlfriends already!”

Vassos announced her split from Chapple in November 2026, saying their journey had not “turned out the way [they] had hoped.”
