Rangers explore return for Cerny - gossip

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Two familiar wingers linked with Rangers returns in January as SFA redraft proposals to address youth development crisis...

Rangers are working on a deal to re-sign Vaclav Cerny with Besiktas open to letting the Czech winger, who spent the 2024-25 season on loan at Ibrox, move on in January. (Football Insider, external)

Oscar Cortes looks set to return to Rangers in January with reports in South America claiming no club is willing to pay the Ibrox side's asking price for the winger currently on loan at Huracan in Argentina. (Daily Record - subscription required, external)

The Scottish FA will put forward revised plans to address the lack of game time given to young players before a meeting tomorrow after Celtic, Dundee United, Hibernian, Motherwell and Rangers expressed concerns over the impact of the original proposals. (The Herald - subscription required, external)

New Aberdeen head coach Mario Frick has agreed a deal which could potentially run until the summer of 2030, with the club's official statement on the appointment not including the length of contract. (Press & Journal - subscription required, external)

Dundee United manager Jim Goodwin is pleased with the strength of his squad after 14 summer arrivals but says the club will "try to strengthen if the opportunity is there" in January. (The Courier - subscription required, external)

Falkirk and Scotland Under-17 goalkeeper Joe Hastings is training with Manchester City with a view to a January move. (Football Insider, external)
