Grand Theft Crypto? GTA 6’s Fictional Coin Becomes an $18M Meme as DacityCoin Rockets 150x

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

GTA 6 meme coins are here: DacityCoin hit $18M as the game launched. PumpSwap volume doubled for a day, but meme coins and PUMP stay weak.

DacityCoin (DCTY) hit an $18 million market cap, the day Rockstar Games released Grand Theft Auto VI. But what is DacityCoin? Traders were racing to find GTA 6 meme coins before the game had even finished downloading.

The Solana meme is named after a fake cryptocurrency inside the game. Life imitating GTA, you could say. DCTY traded near $0.019 at 11:00 UTC, up more than 150x in 24 hours.

What the fuck? pic.twitter.com/cWJV4vGKM2

DacityCoin’s fomo doubled PumpSwap’s daily volume on Wednesday, yet the exchange was busier than it was on 12 days since October 1. Dogecoin, Pepe and Bonk barely blinked, and Pump.fun’s own token, PUMP, is sitting near a one-month low.

So is this the spark for a new run in meme coins, or just a fun one-day side quest?

DacityCoin started as a background joke. In Rockstar’s launch trailer, the name is painted on a stunt plane. Then this week, gameplay footage from Brazilian streamer Combo Infinito showed an in-game phone app listing “DacityCoin (DCTY)”, complete with buy and sell buttons.

Crypto traders did what crypto traders do: within hours, the Solana token with the same ticker went vertical.

Before the footage, DCTY had never been worth more than about $54,000. Here’s what its main pool on DEX Screener shows now:

One important detail: Rockstar hasn’t linked the in-game coin to any real token, and DCTY has no official tie to GTA 6 or Take-Two. The name game is getting messy, too.

A separate Pump.fun token called “Dacity Coin” hit a $10.8 million market value on Wednesday, then slid to about $800,000. Exchange Bitrue joined in as well, listing DacityCoin futures with up to 10x leverage on Wednesday.

If a fresh meme coin season were kicking off, Pump.fun is where you’d see it first, since that’s where most new Solana tokens of this kind are born. So we pulled PumpSwap’s daily trading from DefiLlama, which tracks exchange data across blockchains.

Outside the GTA hype, the bigger meme coins are having a rough year. All eight large meme coins we tracked on Binance are down for 2026, by a median of 54%, while Bitcoin is down 28%. The past week stung too, with drops between 11% and 23%:

Source: Binance daily closes, Coinspeaker calculation, November 19.

Zoom out, and DCTY is still tiny. The whole meme coin sector is worth $22.5 billion on CoinGecko, with Solana’s share at only $2 billion. One token can steal the spotlight for a day, but a real run for meme coins needs the big names and overall trading to rise together. So far, that hasn’t happened.

Pump.fun did get a payday out of the DacityCoin rush. The platform earned $1.62 million in revenue on Wednesday, up from a $1.28 million average over the five days before. Since April, Pump.fun says it spends half its revenue buying PUMP on the open market, so a day like Wednesday means roughly $800,000 of buying.

The worst day was November 14, when PUMP dropped 15%. Token trackers list a scheduled release of locked PUMP tokens in mid-November, though they don’t agree on the exact date. So far, buybacks of under $1 million a day haven’t been enough to soak up that selling.

GTA 6 meme coins are hot now, but the thing to watch is breadth, meaning how many coins move together. If PumpSwap volume stays above $300 million a day after launch week and Dogecoin or Pepe join in, DacityCoin could be the start of something bigger.

If DCTY fades and volume slips back to $150 million, it’ll go down as a fun launch-day story and not much more.
