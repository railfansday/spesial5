Kraken Suffers Temporary Glitch Allowing Users to Buy Bitcoin for $16,900

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Bitcoin reportedly became available at a massive discount on Kraken after a sharp dislocation in Kraken's dollar trading markets.

Users reported being able to purchase BTC for as little as 24,250 CAD, equivalent to $16,900 at that time.

Apparently, the incident has only affected the BTC/CAD trading pair on the platform, although some users claim the disruption appeared across several CAD markets.

The cause the glitch is still unclear, while the reported transactions are yet to be verified.

The incident might have involved thin liquidity problem in Kraken's CAD order books.

Large sell orders reportedly overwhelmed available buy orders, leaving insufficient demand to absorb the selling pressure.

According to some accounts, at one point about 20 BTC was being sold every 15 minutes, causing the imbalance to push BTC price against CAD further down.

One such transaction resulted in BTC price at around C$24,250, or $16,900. Compared with Bitcoin trading between $57,000-$58,000, the discount amounted to a staggering 71%. Other users reported BTC briefly trading around $27,000.

Kraken's status page continued to show its systems as operational during the incident. No official explanation from the exchange was available at the time of writing.

The reported dislocation highlights the risks associated with trading in markets with limited liquidity. When an order book lacks sufficient buy-side depth, even relatively large sell orders can cause prices to move sharply away from the broader market.

On Friday, Bitcoin price dropped below $60,000. At some point, it fell 5.10% to $55,830, the lowest level since September 2024.

Liquidations on crypto exchanges reached $1.57 billion over 24 hours on Nov. 20.

According to CoinGlass, 265,525 traders were liquidated over 24 hours, and long positions accounted for $1.43 billion of the total.

Bitcoin saw $554.28 million in liquidations, and 91.3% of that amount came from long positions. Ethereum (ETH) fell below $1,700 on Nov. 20, and ETH traders saw $331.08 million in liquidations.

Binance accounted for 45.8% of all liquidations, and the largest single order was also on Binance: an $11.98 million ETH/USDT long position that was liquidated at $1,638.78.
