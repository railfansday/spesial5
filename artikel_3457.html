GTA 6: Should You Kill Or Spare The Skunk Ape In "Advanced Skunk Apenomics"

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

One of the various Strangers & Freaks side quests in Grand Theft Auto 6 deals with the legendary Skunk Ape, an elusive sort of Bigfoot-esque creature that's stalking around the swamps in Pelican Valley. Early on, you'll be able to trigger a quest by viewing the signs posted around the starting area and contacting Kirk, eventually tracking down a series of effigies in the swamp.

!!! WARNING: THIS GUIDE HAS SPOILERS FOR THE SKUNK APE MISSION IN GTA 6 !!!After you've found each of these effigies and waited a few days, Kirk will get in contact with you for a meetup in the swamps again, this time with a partner and a helicopter. Jason will have to shoot the Swamp Ape, aka Mr. Mangrove, with a tranquilizer gun from the helicopter, which will cause the creature to run into a shed and collapse. Once Jason tries to pick it up, some very strange things happen.

First, players will turn into a frog and be required to hop around the docks and eat several flies, and then will be transferred into a fly and be asked to find Kirk, listening in on the Swamp Ape hunter's nefarious plans to use the QR code tattooed on Mr. Mangrove for a large sum of money. When Jason wakes back up, you'll have to choose whether to save Kirk from the Swamp Ape.

Killing Mr. Mangrove the Skunk Ape is the most straightforward option of the bunch, and has the clearest and most immediate reward. First, if you're planning to kill the Swamp Ape, be sure to question Kirk about who Harry Sherrick is by both threatening and questioning him. You'll have found out by now that Kirk was planning on leaving you for dead anyway, so putting a little fear into him isn't all that big of a deal.

Afterward, you can kill Mr. Mangrove and choose to save Kirk. This will result in a monetary reward of anywhere between $10,000 and $12,500 depending on if you questioned/threatened Kirk, and he'll then promise to give you a cut of the QR code payout, but that doesn't end up happening, seeing as the helicopter will explode and Kirk and Noah will die.

There are actually two different ways to spare the Skunk Ape in this mission, the first being to shoot Kirk with the rifle, which will knock him unconscious. The Skunk Ape will then pick him up and carry him off into the swamp. The second option is to just let the Skunk Ape go ham on Kirk and squeeze his head until it pops. Mr. Mangrove will then carry Kirk's corpse off into the swamp as well.

Seeing as Mr. Mangrove will still be alive, it's very likely he can be found later in the game or even after you've completed the main story and return to Leonida for the Post-Game experience. As of this writing, it isn't clear specifically what this entails, but based on my experience with another Strangers & Freaks mission involving the mutant form of Kyle, there are likely benefits to leaving the Swamp Ape alive.

If sparing Mr. Mangrove is anything like the Hulk-Kyle "Buddy?" quest, my guess is that the Swamp Ape will clear out the Kingfisher drug dens in the swamp area, though this has yet to be confirmed.

Despite not knowing the exact benefits of sparing Mr. Mangrove, it's likely that all the Strangers & Freaks missions have benefits that are immediately clear. While the $12,000 or so seems like a good chunk of change, assuming Mr. Mangrove helps clear out the Kingfisher operations, you'll be able to go into these encampments with zero resistance and arguably benefit more than you would from the cash.

That said, this is only really worth it if you're completing the mission in Chapter 2, as going back to these areas when you're in the later chapters isn't all that much of a challenge anymore. Further, since I haven't been able to confirm the specific results of the Skunk Ape surviving and taking Kirk into the swamp, it'd be understandable if you want to take the cash reward instead.

It's also entirely possible that you can find the Skunk Ape in the endgame and claim the QR code prize that Kirk and Noah were after, though this is purely speculation as of this writing. This GTA 6 guide will be updated as soon as more details become available.
