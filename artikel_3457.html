‘Tracker’ Officially Returns to CBS This Sunday After Surprise Break

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Once known almost exclusively for playing Dean Winchester in Supernatural, Jensen Ackles has settled into his role as one of TV’s biggest and brightest stars like he was born to it. Back in 2022, Ackles made his debut as Soldier Boy in the third season of The Boys on Prime Video, and while he did not return in Season 4, the franchise wasn’t done with him. Ackles returned to a recurring role in the fifth and final season of the show, and he has also been enlisted to lead the franchise’s second spin-off show, Vought Rising. Last year, Ackles also starred in the Prime Video crime thriller show, Countdown, which was canceled after only one season despite solid reviews from fans. The show co-starred the late Eric Dane, who sadly passed away earlier this year.

Before The Boys debuts its new spin-off series next year, Jensen Ackles has another big TV role to fall back on. We’re talking, of course, about Russell Shaw, who he has played across all four seasons of Tracker on CBS. Ackles showed up as Russell a few weeks ago on the new episode of Tracker, and after stealing the show once again, fans are already asking more questions about a potential Tracker spin-off following Russell. Fans were devastated last week when CBS announced that Tracker was taking the week off, but after some time away, the show is finally ready to return with another chapter in Colter’s story this Sunday, November 15. In the new episode, Reenie enlists Colter to help search for her mentor’s estranged daughter, but they find themselves in the middle of a dangerous conspiracy.

CBS has not officially picked up Tracker for a fourth season, but there are signs that indicate the show will be given the chance to continue beyond Season 4. After the first three seasons of Tracker were filmed in Vancouver, CBS decided to move the show’s production hub to LA, which isn’t a move that would have been made if the network wasn’t interested in investing in the show for at least a few more years. CBS also confirmed a few weeks ago that Josh Holloway is soon to debut in a future episode of Tracker. Holloway is best known for roles in Lost and Yellowstone.

Check out new episodes of Tracker every week on CBS and stream them the following day on Paramount Plus.

Tracker follows lone-wolf survivalist Colter Shaw as he navigates the country as a reward seeker. Utilizing his expert tracking skills, he aids private citizens and law enforcement in solving various mysteries while grappling with issues from his fractured family.
