The prolific Steven Knight can be rather hit or miss, given the sheer amount of work he takes on. For instance, he's currently writing the hotly anticipated James Bond reboot for director Denis Villeneuve, but he also has two shows running concurrently — A Thousand Blows and House of Guinness. Additionally, he also wrote the script for this year's Peaky Blinders movie, and served as the creative force behind the Oasis concert film. On top of his work as a writer, Knight has also dabbled in directing. And now, the best-reviewed movie of his filmmaking career is available to stream for free.

The movie in question was released in 2014, and it was headlined by Tom Hardy. Knight and Hardy have collaborated on several projects together, including Peaky Blinders and Taboo. The movie received tremendous reviews and is now sitting at a "Certified Fresh" 90% score on the aggregator website Rotten Tomatoes.

We're talking, of course, about Locke. It features Hardy as a contractor who must decide whether to be with his mistress as she gives birth to their child, or to return home to his wife and son one evening in London. The entire movie unfolds during a car ride, with Hardy being the only actor to appear on-screen. He does, however, have phone conversations with characters voiced by Olivia Colman, Ruth Wilson, Andrew Scott, and a young Tom Holland.

Knight's James Bond reboot is currently in the casting phase, with a handful of young stars including Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, and Jacob Elordi reportedly shortlisted for the role. The casting process is reportedly in its final stages, and an announcement is imminent. The film is expected to enter production next year, once Villeneuve fulfills his commitments to the upcoming sci-fi epic Dune: Part Three. Hardy, who was once considered an ideal pick for Agent 007, is arguably too old for the role now. However, you can watch him in Locke on Pluto TV this month, and in MobLand on Paramount+.
