Papa John’s wants you to take a picture of your pizza. The reason might surprise you

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Papa John’s is looking to improve the quality of pizza that arrives at your door, and it’s doing so by having you snap a photo of your pie before chowing down.

The company launched a “Snap a Pizza, Get a Free Pizza” program this week in which customers are asked to photograph their orders and leave feedback in exchange for a free pizza. The images and the feedback will be analyzed using an AI-based quality-control system.

As part of Papa John’s broader “Unbox Better” strategy, this promotion aims to improve customer experience by addressing quality control in its pizza.

The program is designed to help train its in-house AI system. According to Reuters, the technology will work like in-store quality-control cameras that detect whether a pizza is burnt or unevenly cut.

Customer-submitted images will be used by the company to train a similar system that, while less accurate than in-store cameras, can be implemented more widely. Currently, only hundreds of its 6,000 stores are equipped with these cameras.

The promotion follows a 6.5% decline in quarterly same-store sales, Papa John’s reported in its third-quarter earnings. Per Reuters, the new quality-control system will aim to give every store a direct view into what needs fixing.

“We see the pizza pics, the unboxings, and the hot takes online every day, and we know our customers aren’t shy about sharing what they think,” Chris Lyn-Sue, global chief marketing officer at Papa John’s, said in a press statement.

The offer is valid from November 9 through November 20, and customers wanting to participate will need to sign in or create a Papa Rewards account. Once logged in, users must opt in to SMS messages to be eligible for the offer.

After ordering a pizza ($15 minimum), customers will receive a text message prompting them to take and upload an unedited photo of the pizza before eating it, as well as to leave an honest review of the bake, the crust, and their overall eating experience.

Once the photo and feedback are confirmed, customers will get a Papa Rewards offer, unlocking a free large one-topping pizza on their next order ($5 minimum).

Lyn-Sue added: “Every photo and every review helps us better understand what customers love and where we can improve as we continue our journey to deliver a better experience every time they choose Papa John’s.”
