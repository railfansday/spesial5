Best Early Black Friday Deals Vogue Editors Are Shopping in 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

I spend the majority of my day browsing retailers, keeping tabs on what’s new and what’s marked down, adding things to my ever-growing wish list. And from what I’ve found, there’s absolutely no need to wait until Black Friday—let alone Cyber Monday—officially kicks off to score a good deal. Early sales have already started rolling in, which means it’s time to get strategic about what earns a spot in my cart before Thanksgiving Day. Naturally, I like to start with recommendations from my fellow Vogue editors.

This year, I’m using the first promotions as an opportunity to invest in clothing pieces I know I’ll wear on repeat, from Khaite’s deep indigo jeans to Arma’s sandy suede blazer and A.Emery’s little black sock boots that go with just about everything—all handpicked by yours truly.

I’m also making notes of what Vogue shopping editors are eyeing, whose wish lists are filled with luxe outerwear, cozy cold-weather accessories, covetable footwear, and loads of chic home finds. From the holiday shopping deals worth snagging now to the pieces we’re hoping will be committing to over the next week, consider this your head start to shopping the early Black Friday deals worth scouting now before the main event.

“Table lamps, floor lamps, portable lamps… I love lamp and always have room for one more, even if space suggests otherwise. The Lumens sale is where I’ll be spending most of my time, mulling over Gubis and Poulsens and whether or not I need to replace my current set of stools with these from Artek… I think the answer is yes.” —Talia Abbas, shopping director

“Black Friday is always a time to restock on some kitchen and home essentials. I plan to stock up on pots and pans and get some bed sheets. We recently reorganized our apartment, so we want to get a new rug. We love this one from Nordic Knots, and we also love these portable lamps for our bookshelf.” —Andrea Zendejas, shopping market editor

“It’s officially that time of year for the essentials restock. First stop? Leset’s early Black Friday sale for all of the classics–think crisp white tees, a new car coat, and cozy lounge pants. Also on the list for the holiday shopping season: a great pair of jeans and a few cozy knits to carry me through the colder months.” —Minty Mellon, shopping market editor

“I have had my eye on a few pieces from both Deiji Studio and Belle Anna, and think I will finally pull the trigger during their Black Friday sales. My boyfriend also wants to restock our kitchen cookware, so I think we will be hitting Made In Cookware’s selection of stainless steel pots and pans.” —Dalya Benor, senior shopping writer

“The early deals are the best place to look before the madness of Black Friday kicks in. I was thrilled to find this suede Arma blazer within Net-a-Porter’s markdowns in my size, along with Khaite’s indigo denim and A.Emery’s little black boot—essentially the components of my everyday fall uniform. I can never have enough black cashmere turtlenecks in the winter, and I’m investing now in Lisa Yang’s perfectly slouchy silhouette at Shopbop—especially since it’s still full price elsewhere.” —Kristina Rutkowski, contributor

“This Black Friday, I’m prioritizing shopping for my baby boy, due in early 2027, but I can’t help sneaking in a little something for myself, too. This Shrimps striped cardigan is simply too cute to pass up, while the Doona stroller is a newborn must-have I’m ready to splurge on. Nour Hammour makes some of the most stunning, timeless outerwear, and I imagine this roomy shearling coat will be especially bump-friendly for my winter travels. Lastly, I’ve been loving the zebra-print trend taking over fashion right now, with my eye especially on these striped flats from Toteme. Fingers crossed all of these make it into the Black Friday sales!” —Laura Jackson, contributor

“My brain is currently on some wicked, shopping hamster wheel: tall boots, leather biker jacket, apartment decor, body-care. I basically refuse to buy anything else otherwise, so looking towards Black Friday, I’m laser focused on finding the best product for the best price.” —Kiana Murden, beauty shopping editor

“I’m alternating between two extremes: The panic that winter is coming, and my cold weather accessories are woefully unprepared for the job, and the understanding that Black Friday is the best time to find discounted summer pieces on final sale. Lightweight cashmere layers have found their way onto my wishlist—alongside cozy gloves.” —Elly Leavitt, lifestyle shopping editor

“From all the wear and tear throughout the year, I use the end of year sales to restock on the best footwear to carry me through the months ahead. I usually stick to neutral colors to make sure I have options with every outfit. On my way to check out I’m usually tempted by a few spontaneous accessories, too…” —Kasey Busiel, marketing & production manager, shopping

“I feel like I’m in a good place in terms of basics right now, so this Black Friday, I’m on the hunt for some fun accents and pieces to play up my everyday outfits.” —Fred Sahai, associate commerce producer
