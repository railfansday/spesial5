So...Beekeeper Matt Reportedly Got Engaged on ‘Love Is Blind,’ but the Show Left It Out

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Here’s what we know about his fiancée—and where they currently stand.

I don’t know about you, but I’m personally exhausted by Love Is Blind leaving out a bunch of engagements from the show’s final edit. You’d think I’d have accepted it by now since it happens every season, but that just isn’t the case!

That being said, there’s another unseen engagement to come from the show. It turns out Matt Waldrip—aka Beekeeper Matt—fully proposed to someone in the pods, and it wasn’t Michaella Giorgio.

According to some new intel from Reality Receipts podcaster Rikkii Wise, Matt allegedly formed a meaningful connection with hairstylist and fellow single parent Jenn Mouser.

“We didn't see much of her on this season, but the two of them did go up to engagement day,” Rikki shared in her video.

Per Jenn’s official bio on Netflix’s Tudum, she joined the experiment because she felt “defeated by the dating apps.” She apparently had a goal of finding “a guy who’s also in touch with his softer side,” sharing, “Someone who’s not able to emotionally regulate gives me the absolute ick.” (Same, girl. Same.)

“I don’t know anything about the reveal or what happened after, but I know that these two didn’t date for long,” Rikki added.

Michaella also shared insight into Matt’s relationship status when she hopped on The Viall Files mic on November 11 and admitted she went on some dates with Matt “a few weeks after filming.”

As for Jenn, she seems to be recovering from a recent accident. According to a post shared on September 27, she said, “This past month has been the hardest of my life.”

“When I’m ready to post about the accident, I will. Please know I’m healing and feeling stronger everyday,” she wrote, in part. “Your love, support, prayers and donations have filled my heart with so much gratitude. I’m truly speechless.”

She separately shared a GoFundMe link to help raise donations for her recovery.

It’s unclear where Beekeeper Matt currently stands with Jenn, since there’s no evidence of their engagement on her social media profiles.
