How Noah Cyrus’s Deep Connections to ‘The Hunger Games’ Led to Her Chilling “Ash and Snow” Music Video for ‘Sunrise on the Reaping’ (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The singer-songwriter says she “absolutely manifested” being part of The Hunger Games universe.

Noah Cyrus knows ball when it comes to The Hunger Games. So it’s no surprise that the country crooner lent her richly smooth vocals and deeply confessional songwriting chops to the soundtrack of the latest film in the franchise, Sunrise on the Reaping.

When her song, “Ash and Snow,” was officially announced as part of the soundtrack—which, like those of Hunger Games past, includes undeniable bangers—her fans celebrated how their manifestations came true. It turns out Noah had long dreamed of contributing to Suzanne Collins’s universe, too.

“I absolutely manifested this!” Noah exclusively tells Cosmopolitan. “I have watched the movies millions of times. These are films I go back to over and over again. I watch them multiple times a year. I feel like I’ve been holding in the words, just ready for the chance to contribute in my own way.”

“Ash and Snow” is a chilling track that calls back to President Snow’s cruelty in the Second Quarter Quell we see pan out in the latest installment—but the video mostly contains visuals inspired by the OG trilogy’s heroine, Katniss Everdeen.

“Throughout the process, I was thinking of Katniss and President Snow and her courage in leading a rebellion against the Capitol and Snow,” Noah says, adding that she had written the track in under an hour. She explains that “Ash and Snow” isn’t only connected to one film but the whole Hunger Games franchise.

“I spoke with the studio about the plot and all the imagery, and the words just came flooding in. It was something I felt in my body. I knew what I wanted to say and write,” she continues. “There has been a lot in my life where I feel like I’ve had to sacrifice parts of myself, and I’ve had times where I wanted to seek vengeance when I’ve felt wronged or hurt. This song embodies strength, but it also comes from a place of pain and seeking justice.”

Luckily, Noah gave Cosmo the first look at her new music video for “Ash and Snow.” Come for the achingly beautiful visuals, and stay for the vengeance, justice, and sacrifice that shaped the song.
