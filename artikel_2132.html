Derby County release exciting Eiran Cashin update

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Derby County have confirmed that Eiran Cashin is back in training, with the defender in line to feature for John Eustace’s side after the international break.

The Rams have endured a miserable campaign so far, and even though they beat Bolton last time out, they are 23rd in the Championship, having won just twice all season, and they are five points from safety.

So, it’s a concerning time to be a Derby fan, and Eustace has understandably come in for fierce criticism at times.

However, the one major mitigating factor in Eustace’s favour has been the number of injuries that Derby have suffered.

The Rams chief has had to do without a number of important players throughout the season so far, particularly in defence, with Cashin, Sondre Langås, Matt Clarke, Charlie Taylor and Derry Murkin among the absentees.

That forced Derby into the free agent market, with Jack Robinson and Shane Duffy joining to bolster the ranks.

And, whilst the duo played their part in the win over Bolton, with Robinson getting on the scoresheet, Eustace will be desperate to get his first-team players back out on the pitch as soon as possible.

Pleasingly for the Rams, there has been some positive news on the injury front, as the club shared a social media post of Cashin back with the group.

The 25-year-old, who returned to Pride Park on loan from Brighton in the summer, has not played since the 2-1 defeat at home to Birmingham City in September due to a calf injury.

That has kept the Irish international out of action for two months, but he is now back training with his teammates, which is a massive boost for Eustace.

Therefore, provided he doesn’t suffer any setbacks, it seems highly likely that Cashin will be in the starting XI when Derby return to action at Southampton on November 21.

This season has been a real struggle for Derby fans, and with away games at Southampton, Wolves and Middlesbrough in the next four games, there may be some more difficult days ahead for the Rams.

But, it’s important to recognise how injuries have impacted the club, as Eustace has been robbed of so many key players, especially at the back.

Of course, that doesn’t absolve him of all blame, but it’s needed to add context around Derby’s situation.

So, all connected to the East Midlands outfit will be delighted to see Cashin back involved, and they will expect the centre-back to be an influential figure very quickly.
