Everything you need to know about Amazons Black Friday 2026 sale: Best deals, Cyber Monday price comparison, daily drops

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Amazon's 12-day holiday shopping marathon has reached the most anticipated day of discounts, Black Friday. The sale began last week, and it's due to run through Cyber Monday on Nov. 30

In a company blog post, Amazon boasted that its holiday savings events would feature "millions of deals across popular categories and new daily themed deal drops," including "double drops" on Black Friday and Cyber Monday proper. Now Black Friday is here; this is what you need to know before you start spending.

Amazon's Black Friday Week sale went live at 3:01 a.m. ET (12:01 a.m. PT) on Nov. 19 — kicking off a full week before Thanksgiving Day. This was the fourth year in a row that Amazon started its Black Friday sale at least a week early.

Amazon is hardly the only major retailer to draw out its Black Friday sale. Best Buy, Target, and Walmart have also offered Black Friday deals throughout November — a win for early birds who worry about popular gifts selling out as the holiday shopping season revs up.

Amazon hasn't officially said when its 2026 Cyber Monday sale will start, but in the past, it's kicked off the day right after Black Friday. With that in mind, Mashable's Shopping team expects Amazon to make the shift into Cyber Monday mode on Saturday, Nov. 28 this year.

It stands to reason that shoppers want to know which of these days has better deals: Black Friday or Cyber Monday.

It's hard to know whether to take the plunge on the deals today or hang on until Monday. There's no straight answer, as we won't know the deals until they land. But it's worth remembering that Cyber Monday has traditionally been a day for big discounts on electronics and tech. Our advice? If we highlight a deal as a record-low price on Amazon, it's probably worth grabbing it — that price is unlikely to drop much further. On each day of its Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday sales, Amazon is running themed daily deal drops called "Today’s Big Deals." These involve limited-time discounts of up to 60% on popular products from brands like Balsam Hill, HydroJug, and Theragun, and they go live once a day except on Black Friday and Cyber Monday proper. Shoppers get two batches of Today's Big Deals on those days: once at 3 a.m. ET, then again at 11 a.m. ET.

These are some of Amazon's most competitive deals of the year. However, know that its discounts aren't as impressive as they've been on past Black Fridays. That's because myriad products have seen price increases in 2026 due to factors like higher fuel costs, tariffs, and the ongoing memory shortage, aka "RAMageddon."

The latter is the reason why Amazon raised the prices of its Kindle e-readers, Fire TV Sticks, and most Echo smart speakers in August.

Amazon's Black Friday sale isn't exclusive to Prime members. This is one of the main things that separates it from other Amazon events like Prime Day and Prime Big Deal Days.

No, Amazon does not price-match competitors' deals — not on Black Friday, nor any other day of the year. If you find a better price on something you've recently purchased there, you'll have to return it to Amazon and re-buy it where it's cheaper.

Most items purchased on Amazon from Nov. 1 to Dec. 31 can be returned through Jan. 31, 2026, except for Apple products, which have a return deadline of Jan. 15, 2027.
