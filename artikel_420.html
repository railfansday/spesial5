Clickeez are selling out everywhere: Where you can buy the viral Series 3 and Hello Kitty packs

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Every holiday shopping season brings one runaway toy craze that vanishes from retail shelves overnight, and this year that title belongs entirely to Clickeez. What started as an online obsession with the clicky sound and feel of mechanical keyboards has quickly turned into a massive playground trend. Demand has heavily outpaced supply since launch, turning the hunt for stock into a wild goose chase.

The appeal is simple: satisfying, screen-free fidgeting. Each piece is an individual keycap that clicks and clacks like a real computer keyboard. Kids clip them onto keychains, hang them off backpacks, or trade different characters with friends.

The Sanrio crossover serves as an easy stocking stuffer and an approachable introduction to the craze. The Series 1 Hello Kitty & Friends 3-Pack bundles three character keys featuring favorites like Kuromi, Cinnamoroll, and Hello Kitty herself, alongside a bonus charm, a connector keychain, and a collector guide. There are over 30 Sanrio characters across the series to track down, plus an ultra-rare Golden Hello Kitty keycap with 1-in-10,000 blind-box pull odds.

Die-hard Sanrio fans who want a broader character roster right away will get much more out of the Series 1 Hello Kitty & Friends 7-pack. Packing seven character keys, a bonus charm, and a connector ring, it is the most practical way to assemble a kawaii collection without gambling on individual blind boxes.

Anyone after the updated rounded keycaps can pick up the Series 3 Bubble Pops lineup, which swaps out the classic sharp clack for a softer popping sound across 70 characters from the Pink Life, Baked Goodies, Magical Glow, and Forest Pals themes. If you just want a starter set, the 5-pack gives you five keys, a bag clip, and a shot at the rare Bubbly Cow chase key.

For kids who want to assemble multiple keychains right away, jumping to the 12-pack is the smarter move — it packs a dozen characters and includes two separate keychains so they can build multiple clips or swap a loaded chain right away.

Tweens who prefer a dedicated backpack accessory over loose keys can check out the Clickeez Series 3 Deluxe Keychain Bubble Pops kit. The set swaps out standard loose chains for a multi-sided fidget cube holder, a lanyard, two clip tags, three logo caps, and four Bubble Pops keys that click, pop, and fidget on the go.

The biggest build in the lineup is the Clickeez Keyboard Mega Pack. Instead of just clipping onto a bag, the set comes with a full set of clicky keys and frames you can snap together into a mini keyboard for a desk or shelf.

If you want to browse other packs or see what else is in stock, head over to Amazon's Clickeez storefront before the viral clicky keycaps sell out.
