Christopher Nolan says you can watch The Odyssey on a phone, but new IMAX tickets have just dropped

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey sails back into IMAX and IMAX 70mm next month, IMAX announced this morning.

Beginning Dec. 11, The Odyssey will once again be available in the premium formats it was specifically designed for.

Nolan's critically heralded hit is the first feature shot entirely with IMAX film cameras, so the only way to see all of the frame is by catching it in IMAX 70mm, with its square-like 1.43:1 aspect ratio. Standard IMAX is the next biggest option, with a 1.90:1 aspect ratio. The distinction between viewing formats turned IMAX 70mm tickets into a hot commodity this summer, with people spending hundreds of dollars and even undertaking international trips for the chance to see The Odyssey.

The IMAX re-release news comes one day after The Odyssey became available on digital. While Nolan's reverence for large-scale formats and theatrical experiences has sparked memes like "watching the way Nolan intended," the Academy Award-winning director expressed enthusiasm for people to catch the film at home, and yes, that includes on their phones.

"People watch movies the way they want to watch movies," Nolan told USA Today. "In the course of my lifetime, the way we're able to engage with movies at home has gone forward leaps and bounds – from VHS to DVD to streaming at 4K. I'm actually excited for it to reach a new audience."

That The Odyssey will be back in theaters even after a home release speaks to industry confidence in Nolan and large formats to draw viewers back to movie theaters. Plus, it'll be a great way to remind Academy voters of the box office power of this epic as Oscar season kicks off.

With $1.8 billion under its belt, The Odyssey is the second highest-grossing film of 2026, behind Spider-Man: Brand New Day. The Odyssey is also the highest-grossing R-rated film of all time, beating out Deadpool and Wolverine, as well as both Universal Pictures' and Nolan's highest-grossing film ever.

With the IMAX re-rerelease, The Odyssey looks to extend its box office glory, perhaps even setting the $2 billion mark in its sights.
