Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s Situation-Chip is Meme Gold

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The internet is having a field day after the tech mogul confessed to having an affair with a suspected Chinese intelligence spy.

After two Astronomer executives were caught canoodling on the jumbotron during a Coldplay concert last year, another tech CEO's extramarital affair has become the meme of the season. Since confessing to his affair with suspected Chinese spy Alice Huang on Sunday, November 8, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has set off a firestorm across the tech and geopolitical spheres and, to our amusement, social media.

In a message shared with his employees on November 8, the 63-year-old CEO acknowledged the affair and extended an apology to his wife, Lori Huang, and his two children, both of whom work for the chip-producing company valued at over $4 trillion. Jensen Huang is viewed as somewhat of a deity in the AI world, having founded Nvidia in a Denny’s restaurant booth in 1993 and turned it into a key player in the AI rivalry between China and the United States.
