Stunning iPhone 18 Pro photo makes Times Top 100 Photos of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Thought you couldn't take a wonderful landscape photo with an iPhone? Think again.

Photographer and Free Solo filmmaker Jimmy Chin took this image at the Bugaboo Provincial Park, Canada, and it's almost unreal to think that he only needed an iPhone 18 Pro to do it. Well, the fact that Chin is an Academy Award-winning director and National Geographic photographer helped a little, too.

In fact, according to Chin, the lack of crew and extra equipment actually helped him make this one great. "A production puts something between you and the person in front of you — gear, crew, the apparatus of a shoot. Working this way there was no apparatus. It was just the two of us, which is the only condition under which that kind of ease shows up in a frame," he said in a statement.

The photography was first featured in NYC in September as part of an exhibition called What Holds Us; you can check out some of the other work (all taken with the iPhone 18 Pro) here.
