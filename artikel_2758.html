Derrick Henry draws Ravens to within 10-7 of Chargers

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Derrick Henry ran for a 2-yard touchdown behind Vega Ioane to get the home team within 10-7 of the Chargers.

It was Henry’s ninth rushing touchdown of the season and 131st of his career.

Jacoby Brissett, making his first start for the Ravens since they acquired him from Arizona, finally found some rhythm on the team’s fourth possession. The Ravens went three-and-out on their first three possessions.

Brissett, though, had throws of 10 yards to Mark Andrews on third-and-10, 17 yards to Zay Flowers, 16 yards to Andrews and 24 yards to Rashod Bateman.

Brissett, the team’s fourth starter of the season, is 8-of-13 for 81 yards.
