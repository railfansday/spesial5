At 7-2, Have the Bears Met or Exceeded Your Preseason Expectations?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Chicago Bears have completed what Ben Johnson would refer to as "2 quarters" of the 2026 NFL season. Despite early adversity, they've managed to come away with only two losses so far – a mark that would be more impressive if they weren't still sitting in 3rd place in their division. Still, the halfway point of the season presents us with a great opportunity to look back at the record predictions of a few of our WCG writers and evaluate just how close we each were to predicting this level of performance from the Bears. It also gives us a chance to look at our predictions for the remaining games and how confident we may now be feeling in those predictions.

First, here's the Bears' remaining schedule:

Week 11 – Nov. 22 vs. New Orleans Saints, noon on FOXWeek 12 – Thanksgiving Day at Detroit Lions, noon on CBSWeek 13 – Dec. 6 vs. Jacksonville Jaguars, noon on FOXWeek 14 – Dec. 13 at Miami Dolphins, noon on CBSWeek 15 – Dec. 19, Saturday at Buffalo Bills, 7:20 p.m. on CBSWeek 16 – Christmas Day, Friday, Dec. 25 – vs. Green Bay Packers, noon on NetflixWeek 17 – Jan. 3 vs. Detroit Lions, 3:25 p.m. on FOXWeek 18 – Jan. 10 at Minnesota Vikings, TBD

Donald Gooch: I had the Bears at 12-5 on the season, so if you project out their record over the next eight games based on the first nine, the expected record is between 11-6 and 12-5, so the Bears are on pace for what I expected before the season. I expect the Bears to meet, and perhaps exceed, my preseason prediction.

Jacob Infante: My expectation was that the Bears would finish the year with a similar 11-6 record to what they had in 2025. Now, my expectations are higher, especially seeing their offensive improvement and after the addition of Jeffery Simmons to the defense. Their divisional schedule will be difficult to deal with, but my expectation is now that they'll end the season with a 12-5 record.

TJ Starman: My original prediction was for the Bears to end up 11-6. I was pretty accurate with my expectations for the first half of the season, though I did ultimately trade a predicted win in week 2 vs the Vikings for actual wins against both the Eagles and Seahawks. That said, I still expect 3 more losses in the next 8 games, which would put my adjusted prediction at 12-5.

Josh Sunderbruch: I had the Bears at 10-6-1 and just barely making the playoffs in May, and I had guessed 6-3 at the bye, so they've beaten my expectations by one win so far. I can see them going 4-4 down the stretch, for 11-6, but I'd love for it to be 5-3 (for 12-5). Either way, I see them making the playoffs but losing out on hosting a home wildcard game due to a poor record against divisional foes.
