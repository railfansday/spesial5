2026 NFL Trade Deadline live updates: Tracker, deals, news, rumors, analysis

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The NFL trade deadline is at 4 p.m. ET on Tuesday, November 10, and PFT has you covered with news and analysis of every trade, potential trade, rumor of a trade and trade that won’t be happening.

Here’s what’s happening on the NFL trade front:

Kirk Cousins to Vikings: Cousins is returning to Minnesota, while the Vikings are sending a 2027 third-round pick to Las Vegas.

Falcons and Ravens swap offensive linemen: Baltimore acquired offensive lineman Ryan Neuzil in exchange for offensive lineman Andrew Vorhees and a 2028 sixth-round pick.

Josh Downs to Chiefs: The Colts have traded Downs to the Chiefs for 2027 fourth- and fifth-round picks, with the Colts also throwing in a 2027 seventh-round pick.

Daiyan Henley to Broncos: The Chargers have traded Henley to the Broncos for a 2027 fourth-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick.

Keenan Allen to San Francisco: The Colts traded Allen to the 49ers for a sixth-round pick.

Amani Hooker to Buffalo: The Bills will second a second-round pick to the Titans for Hooker and a fifth.

Jordan Davis to Jacksonville: The Eagles traded Davis to the Jaguars for a 2027 first-round pick and a 2029 second-round pick.

The Bengals won’t trade for Eagles pass rusher Arnold Ebiketie: The deal was agreed upon, but it was called off after its impact on the compensatory draft pick formula came to light.

Will the Vikings trade for a quarterback? Coach Kevin O’Connell says he’ll defer to GM Nolan Teasley.

Giants are looking to add: John Harbaugh is prioritizing now over the future.

Will the Cowboys make any deadline deals? Team owner Jerry Jones doesn’t see anything “significant” happening in Dallas.

Falcons agree to trade S Jessie Bates to the Jaguars: The Falcons will pick up most of Bates’s remaining salary in exchange for a 2027 second-round pick and a 2028 fifth-rounder.

Saints agree to trade DT Davon Godchaux to the Jaguars: The Jaguars doubled down on defensive additions.

Jets send Andre Cisco to Lions: The Lions are sending the Jets a 2028 sixth-round pick.

Bengals won’t make a trade: Zac Taylor says they’re good where they are.

Jonathan Taylor trade appears likely: The Colts are expected to take the best offer they get for Taylor.

Jets LB Will McDonald requests a trade: The 2023 first-round pick would like to be on a new team.

Colts may trade more players: Indianapolis has told teams it wants more than a fourth-round pick for Josh Downs. Jonathan Taylor and Daniel Jones could both be traded, perhaps both to the Vikings.

Jacoby Brissett is headed to Baltimore: The Ravens and Cardinals announced a deal sending the veteran quarterback and a 2027 fifth-round pick to the Ravens for a 2027 fourth-round pick and a 2028 seventh-round pick.

Derrick Henry says he’s not thinking about changing teams: The Ravens star expressed a desire to help the team improve after a fourth-straight loss.

Jonathan Taylor wants to know where he’ll finish the season: Taylor said no one likes uncertainty when asked about talk that the Colts will trade him.

Chargers say they’re not trading Khalil Mack: Chargers coach Jim Harbaugh said, “I want Khalil Mack to be here and Khalil Mack wants to be here.”

Jeffery Simmons to Bears: Chicago receives Simmons and a 2028 fifth-round pick. Tennessee will receive 2027 and 2028 first-round picks along with a fourth-round pick in 2027.

Quenton Nelson to Seahawks: The Seahawks will send a 2027 third-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick back to Indianapolis.

DeForest Buckner to 49ers: Indianapolis will receive a 2027 fourth-round pick from San Francisco.

Jonathan Taylor trade talks have picked up: The Colts may not be done making deals.

The Jets have reportedly rebuffed Garrett Wilson trade interest: The Jets are not expected to deal their top wideout.

The Colts are open for business: Indianapolis is building for the future after firing General Manager Chris Ballard. Jonathan Taylor hasn’t requested a trade. Josh Downs says he’s “open to whatever.”

Budda Baker’s name surfaces in trade rumors: Baker says he hasn’t asked to be traded from Arizona and also hasn’t asked not to be traded.

Brian Thomas is a name to watch: The Jaguars trading Thomas frequently comes up in trade rumors.

Garrett Wilson wants to remain a Jet: The talented receiver says he is hoping not to get traded.
