Alperen Sengun heading towards third straight NBA All-Star game

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The new NBA season is still pretty young, but some things are slowly coming into focus, and one of them is the potential NBA All-Star participants. The Houston Rockets' Alperen Sengun is in the midst of perhaps his finest season to date, and people are really taking notice. In fact, ESPN just recently named their top potential All-Star participants, and Sengun was listed in the top four. Here's what they had to say about Alpie:

"Trivia time: Who is the leading scorer for the NBA's most efficient offense?

Somehow, the answer is Sengun, who is posting a career-best 22.1 points per game for a balanced Rockets attack that has all five starters averaging in double digits.

While Fred VanVleet's return from injury has shifted Kevin Durant into a slightly lower-usage role, the 24-year-old Sengun has remained a central part of Houston's offense. The Turkish big man ranks in the top five leaguewide by PER and win shares, and he has dished more assists than any center besides, well …"

That center is obviously Sengun's idol Nikola Jokic. But Sengun is having a very Jokic-like season, averaging 21.9 points now, 9.2 rebounds, 7 assists and 1 steal per game. Sengun is shooting 55.1 percent from the field and is shooting 50 percent on triples while putting up 2 attempts per game. It's not a full breakout due to Houston's egalitarian offense, but Kevin Durant's willingness to take a back seat has been good for Sengun and the Rockets.

Speaking of KD, ESPN thinks he's on track for his 17th NBA All-Star appareance, as he's having himself another fine year. He had a season-high 32 points in last night's win over the Clippers, and ESPN had this to say about everyone's favorite Twitter burner holder:

The field for the West's final two All-Star spots has been thinned out by injuries to Edwards, Lakers guard Austin Reaves, Spurs guard Dylan Harper and the Thunder's Jalen Williams and Holmgren. From that group, Reaves is best positioned to make a speedy return and claim a spot.

For now, Durant narrowly edged out Minnesota's Gobert, Portland's Lillard and Phoenix's Devin Booker. The 38-year-old forward has ceded some authority in Houston's offense but has maintained 51/40/88 shooting splits and a healthy plus-8.3 net rating.

The quality of Durant's contributions remains high, even if his 21.0 points per game are the fewest since his rookie campaign. Houston's league-leading offense, which ground down in last year's playoffs, needs Durant's gravity to thrive, and he has led the Rockets in minutes despite being by far the team's oldest player."

KD is now averaging 21 points per game to go along with 5.4 rebounds and 3.8 assists, while shooting 51 percent from the floor and 42 percent from deep. Again, KD's willingness to take a slight back seat in Houston's pecking order has been good for the development of the offense, and KD has shown he can still step up when needed, which was the entire point of getting KD to begin with. It wasn't for him to be a heliocentric one-man show like he was often last season.

Houston stands at 13-5 and in fourth place in the Western Conference. They are currently on a 59-win pace. It sure would be some NBA season if 59 wins gets you only to the fourth, but my guess is this current standings bunch-up starts to thin out here pretty soon. But with two All-Stars like Sengun and KD in the mix, the Rockets will be in the thick of things either way.
