Titans place RB Tyjae Spears on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Titans have placed running back Tyjae Spears on injured reserve, the team announced.

Spears, who injured his knee in the loss to the Jaguars on Sunday, has played seven games this season. He has carried the ball 22 times for 94 yards.

The Titans elevated running back Michael Carter for Sunday’s game, giving the team additional depth at the position. Carter has four touches for 14 yards in three games this season.

The Titans will have safety Tony Adams, who has cleared concussion protocol and does not have an injury designation.
