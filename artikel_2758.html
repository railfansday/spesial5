Panthers Get Bad Tommy Tremble, Darren Waller Injury News Before Ravens Game

The Carolina Panthers spent Wednesday's practice shorthanded, particularly at the tight end position.

Both Tommy Tremble and Darren Waller were held out of action due to injury.

It's the start to the preparations for Week 11 for the Panthers, who will play the Lamar Jackson-less Baltimore Ravens on Sunday — so there's still plenty of time for these TEs to get on the practice field.

But one day into the practice week, Carolina is at least raising the possibility that its tight end usage could be different than usual on Sunday.

GettyNEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA – NOVEMBER 15: Tommy Tremble #82 of the Carolina Panthers runs after a catch against the New Orleans Saints in the second quarter of a game at Caesars Superdome on November 15, 2026 in New Orleans, Louisiana. (Photo by Wes Hale/Getty Images)

Tremble was held out of Wednesday's practice due to what was listed as a knee injury.

He had played during Sunday's win over the New Orleans Saints. Tremble finished that game with four catches for 48 yards.

GettyCHARLOTTE, NORTH CAROLINA – NOVEMBER 08: Darren Waller #88 of the Carolina Panthers celebrates after scoring a touchdown during the third quarter against the Denver Broncos at Bank of America Stadium on November 08, 2026 in Charlotte, North Carolina. (Photo by Grant Halverson/Getty Images)

Waller was also held out of Wednesday's practice with what's being listed as a knee injury.

Like Tremble, Waller played on Sunday. He had two catches for 24 yards.

A veteran player, Waller could be getting some rest more than anything else, but it wasn't listed as a rest day for him, so it's worth monitoring.

GettyNEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA – NOVEMBER 15: Chuba Hubbard #30 of the Carolina Panthers celebrates after his team's 20-17 win against the New Orleans Saints at Caesars Superdome on November 15, 2026 in New Orleans, Louisiana. (Photo by Morgan Simmons/Getty Images)

The biggest name on the DNP list was running back Chuba Hubbard.

Hubbard was listed as out for "rest" — which is something the Panthers have done in recent weeks. He seems totally fine health-wise, and it's just a precaution.

Bobby Okereke, the standout linebacker, remained out of practice with the foot injury that has forced him to miss Carolina's last two games.

They were also without safety Isiah Simmons at practice. He's listed with a knee injury.

For what it's worth, if Waller and Tremble had to miss Sunday's game, the other two TEs Carolina would turn to would be Mitchell Evans and Feleipe Franks.
