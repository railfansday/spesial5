NFL news, live updates: Lions LB Jack Campbell says he has 'full movement' in lower body after neck injury; Cardinals reportedly trading Jacoby Brissett to Ravens

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

We're gearing up for Week 9 of the NFL season (and the looming NFL trade deadline), where teams will have to make a decision on the intended course of the remainder of their season — are they going all in? Or is it time to tear it all down and look toward the future?

One of those teams will be the Baltimore Ravens (4-4), whose quarterback, Lamar Jackson, suffered a thumb injury that will require surgery and has landed him on injured reserve. Backup Tyler Huntley then suffered what is reportedly a season-ending turf toe injury in relief of Jackson, putting undrafted free agent Joe Fagnano into action as the emergency third quarterback.

Now, Fagnano will get the start as the Jacksonville Jaguars travel to face the Ravens on "Thursday Night Football" in Week 9. The 25-year-old, who played his college ball at Maine and then UConn, will be the second undrafted rookie to start under center for Baltimore in the Ravens' 32-year history, per ESPN's Jamison Hensley.

NFL fans also await news on the fate of star running back Jonathan Taylor, who has been touted as a potential trade-deadline mover as the Indianapolis Colts kicked off their franchise reset with a fire sale. They've already traded away two foundational players, Quenton Nelson and DeForest Buckner, for several draft picks as they look to the future, leaving us to wonder whether their star running back could be next.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for the latest NFL news, injury updates and more ahead of Week 9.

For the second straight day, the Patriots have designated wide receiver A.J. Brown and running back TreVeyon Henderson as DNP, leaving their offensive production in doubt ahead of Sunday's game against the Packers.

Brown, who returned from a high ankle sprain in Week 6, said last week that he's been "pushing through" the injury. He is listed as dealing with ankle and knee injuries.

TreVeyon Henderson is again listed as being out with an illness, though the team has not elaborated on his condition. Last month, Henderson was expected to be ruled out multiple weeks with a high ankle sprain, but continued playing for two more weeks before missing last week's game against the Dolphins.

Los Angeles Rams cornerback Trent McDuffie appears to be sitting out his second consecutive day of practice with a back injury suffered in Week 8.

In six games this season, his first with the Rams, McDuffie has 25 tackles, 2 interceptions and 8 passes defended.

Perhaps looking ahead to McDuffie being unavailable for Week 9, the Rams signed veteran David Long to their practice squad, The Athletic's Nate Atkins reports.

Long, 28, has played seven NFL seasons, most recently with the Carolina Panthers last year. During his career, he has averaged 20 tackles and 2 passes defended per season. Long was a 2019 third-round pick by the Rams.

A trade was reportedly in place to send Minnesota Vikings receiver Jauan Jennings back to the San Francisco 49ers, his previous professional home for six seasons, until the player himself shot the deal down.

"It was contingent upon [Jennings] hopping on a FaceTime with Kyle Shanahan and John Lynch and saying, 'OK, yeah, I want to come back,' but he didn't. He said, 'Actually, no I want to stay here in Minnesota.'"

Shanahan addressed the matter Wednesday, confirming the report and telling reporters he was "not really" surprised that Jennings turned down the Niners. He did, however, emphasize the call was friendly in nature.

The Philadelphia Eagles will start quarterback Joe Milton this weekend against the New York Giants, per ESPN's Adam Schefter.
