Celtics vs. Wizards predictions: Odds, recent stats, trends and best bets for November 24

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Boston Celtics (11-6) visit the Washington Wizards (7-11) on Tuesday night at Capital One Arena, with the teams arriving in sharply contrasting form…but not the form their records would suggest. Boston has lost four straight after beginning the season 11-2. Washington carries a three-game winning streak into the matchup after previously dropping eight in a row. Despite their recent struggles, the most consistent aspect of the Celtics' game is their defense. Boston owns the NBA's stingiest defense, allowing only 102.1 points per game, while the Wizards surrender 115.4 per contest.

Boston's skid continued Sunday with a painful 88-87 loss to the Golden State Warriors, its second consecutive one-point defeat. The Celtics scored 33 points in the third quarter to take control but managed only 12 in the fourth, missing their final five field-goal attempts as an eight-point lead slipped away. Jayson Tatum finished with 27 points, 10 rebounds and six assists, but missed a potential game- tying free throw with 56 seconds remaining as well as a contested 16-footer at the buzzer. Derrick White added 14 points and five assists, though he made only two of his 12 three-point attempts as Boston finished 14-for-47 from deep.

Washington enters with considerably more momentum after routing the Orlando Magic 116-96 on Sunday. The Wizards scored the game's first 11 points and scored 38 first-quarter points. They never trailed, connecting on 22 of 39 three-point attempts while producing 31 assists and placing seven players in double figures. Orlando trimmed a 20-point deficit to seven early in the third quarter, but Washington answered with a decisive 12-0 run and carried a 97-75 advantage into the fourth. Kyshawn George scored 24 points, Anthony Davis added 22 and Trae Young recorded 11 points and 11 assists, while rookie AJ Dybantsa supplied 16 points, eight rebounds and six assists.

This is the first meeting of the season between these teams. Their most recent meeting came on March 14, 2026, when Boston defeated Washington 111-100 at TD Garden. With that win, Boston swept the three-game season series against the Wizards. Boston, in fact, has captured the teams' last seven meetings overall.
