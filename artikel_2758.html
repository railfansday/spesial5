Gardner Minshew having tests on right arm after injury on Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Cardinals quarterback Gardner Minshew remained in Sunday’s loss to the Seahawks after hurting his right arm late in the first half, but his status for the team’s Week 10 game against the Rams is not certain.

Head coach Mike LaFleur said at a Monday press conference that Minshew is undergoing tests on his arm and he said it is a possibility that he will not be able to play this week.

The Cardinals traded Jacoby Brissett to the Ravens last week, which puts third-round pick Carson Beck in position to start if Minshew is unable to play. Beck has not appeared in any games yet this season.

Minshew’s injury is one of several negative injury updates LaFleur delivered on Monday. He also announced that wide receiver Michael Wilson is out for the year with a neck injury and that center Hjalte Froholdt is in the concussion protocol.
