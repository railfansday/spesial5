The Wizards have a chance to advance in the NBA Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Washington Wizards won against the Chicago Bulls last Friday. This wasn't just another regular season win. It's also their second win in NBA Cup group play, a franchise record (I'll go over why in a bit). Washington also improves to 2-1 in East Group C play.

Washington has one more game for the NBA Cup which is against the Boston Celtics on Tuesday. If they win, Washington improves to 3-1 but are mathematically eliminated from winning East Group C. It is possible that the Wizards could make the knockout stage as a wild card team if the Celtics win on Friday against the Atlanta Hawks. This would give the Celtics, Hawks and Wizards 3-1 records with the Celtics winning the group.

Now how do the Wizards claim a wildcard spot as a second place team? That's through cumulative point differential in regulation. Currently, the Wizards are third in the wildcard standings behind the Cleveland Cavaliers (+21) and the Hawks (+13) who have the same point differential as the Wizards but the Hawk hold the tiebreaker.

It will be a sign of progress if Washington can make the knockout stage of the NBA Cup. This is because Washington has finished in last place every year since 2023-24 when this tournament was introduced. In the first two years of this tournament, the Wizards finished with 0-4 group play records, while they went 1-3 last season. The Wizards also set a record two wins in group play this season, which is encouraging.

Hopefully, luck will be on their side this week when they play the Celtics on Tuesday.

(And yes, I know they host the Orlando Magic tonight).
