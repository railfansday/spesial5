10 Takeaways from another Celtics NBA Cup win over Wizards

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It felt like Jayson Tatum was due to have a big game coming into this matchup and he delivered, finishing with a season-high 41 points, 8 assists,6 rebounds, and 2 blocks on 13-25 shooting from the field and 6-15 shooting from three. Tatum put on an absolute masterclass in the first half, with 28 points and all 6 of his three pointers.

The Wizards defense seemed to have the game plan of just putting Alex Sarr on an island and he got cooked. Tatum spent the entire first half just raining down threes and the Wizards had no way of stopping it. Just look at the way his eyes lit up when he saw Sarr was trying to defend him.

In the second half, we saw Tatum struggle with shooting the ball again (4-12 FG, 0-6 3PM), but I would say he still did a great job on offense. He was able to get to the basket with relative ease and did a good job getting to the free throw line. The only concern had to be the free throw shooting, finishing 9-14. I don't even remember the last time we saw him miss 5 free throws in a game, but even he said in his postgame interview that he wasn't happy with his second half performance and left some points on the board.

Either way, this game might not go down as his best performance, but it will still go down as his 2nd 40-point game of the season and the 31st time he has done it in his NBA Career. That is second in Boston Celtics history, only behind Larry Bird who did it 47 times.

I don't know what it is about playing games on NBC that turns Paul George into one of the best players in the league. George finished with 28 points and 9 assists on 11-20 shooting from the field and 6-9 shooting from three.

Similar to Tatum, George was absolutely incredible when it came to his three-point shooting in this game. Washington's defense just could not stop Boston's offense in the first half. In the second quarter alone, George dropped 13 points. He hit some absurd shots in this game and it just looked effortless, like in the beginning of the second quarter when he hit back-to-back threes, one on a silky step-back and the other on a pull-up that caused Washington to call timeout.

The best highlight of the night for George came in his 12-point third quarter performance. When Kyshawn George tried to play 1-on-1 defense midway through the quarter, Paul George hit him with a mean crossover that sent him falling down. Paul then paused and splashed the mid-range jumper for one of the nastiest ankle-breakers we've seen all season.

Sadly, the Celtics don't have another game scheduled on NBC until December 28th against the Cavaliers, so we will have to wait a little bit until we get another NBC PG game.

Coming into this game, I was interested to see how Neemias Queta would match up against the Wizards front court with Anthony Davis and Alex Sarr, but Queta absolutely feasted like it was Thanksgiving. He finished with a season-high 21 points on 9-11 shooting along with 5 rebounds and 2 blocks.

Queta's first quarter was some of the best basketball we have ever seen out of him, finishing with 13 points on 6-7 shooting — he was just getting anything he wanted. He scored 8 of the Celtics first 13 points coming on attacking the basket with force. Queta was able to get behind the Wizards defense on almost every roll, with Tatum and George finding him alley-oops and easy finishes.

With a new season high of 17 points set in the first half, Queta did get into foul trouble in the third quarter and didn't play a lot of minutes in the second half. You could tell the Celtics were thrown all out of whack without Queta on the floor, furthering the importance of his impact when he is out there. It was really cool to see them feed Queta in this game and I just love watching him on the court.

After scoring 80 points in the first half, it felt like the Celtics were finally getting back to normal. They already have one of the best NBA Cup point differentials ever and there was a chance it could have gone up exponentially in the second half but it didn't work out that way. Washington outscored Boston 40-24, shooting 13-20 (65%) from the field and 5-10 (50%) from three. The Celtics on the other hand shot 9-22 (40.9%) from the field and 3-9 (33.3) from three.

All credit to the Wizards for showing fight, but it just was not a good look for the Celtics, especially since on their four-game losing streak, the second half offense falling off was a huge problem. Luckily for Boston, the lead was big enough to where the Wizards couldn't make a full comeback, but it was still a nightmare third quarter. The defense looked lackadaisical and the open shots that were falling early in the game just stopped.

The fourth quarter was even worse shooting wise (8-27 FG, 4-16 3PM) but at least they were able to generate some stops on defense in the end. This second half performance shifted the entire vibe about this game. I hate to say it, but there were times where it genuinely felt like Boston was going to blow this lead. It is not a great feeling that you are almost waiting for it to happen, but this last stretch of games have not instilled much confidence.

With under three minutes left in the game, the Wizards cut the Celtics deficit to 7 points and they had all the momentum. Boston was looking for someone to come through and stop the bleeding and they got an unlikely hero in Baylor Scheierman. Going into the fourth quarter, Scheierman had yet to score and wasn't looking to be aggressive for most of this game.

With 2:31 left, Jayson Tatum was doubled on the block and found Scheierman for a deep three that pushed the Celtics lead back up to 10. This was a huge basket for Boston, but he wasn't done. After an Anthony Davis miss, Derrick White missed a three and Scheierman drew a foul on Trae Young during the offensive rebound to get to free throws where he made both. Young would make a layup on the next possession, but Scheierman would follow it with his second three in a row off a nice behind the back pass from Tatum, forcing a Washington timeout.

That 8-2 run by Scheierman completely changed the trajectory of this game. If he didn't hit those shots, it felt like Washington was going to find a way to tie or even take the lead. He benefitted greatly from the gravity Tatum created for him and did not hesitate when it came to shooting the big shots. It shows how much confidence Scheierman has now and the trust Joe Mazzulla has in him to put him in this situation.

For the 11th time this season, Joe Mazzulla has put out a brand-new starting lineup. This time the Celtics went with Derrick White, Sam Hauser, Paul George, Jayson Tatum, and Neemias Queta. This is the third game in a row Hauser has started, replacing Jordan Walsh in the opening lineup. Coming into this game, this lineup had only played 9 minutes together but had a net rating of +46.8 with an offensive rating of 136.8 and defensive rating of 90.0.

This lineup looked to be, according to the numbers, the best option for the Celtics to go with and felt fitting they tried it here. This starting lineup would proceed to open this game on a 17-9 run, giving Boston a spark that the offensive desperately needed. With more of a sample size of 26 minutes, Boston has a net rating of +52.2 with an offensive rating of 146.3 and a defensive rating of 94.1.

For as much as I like the idea of a guy like Walsh or Hugo Gonzalez in the starting lineup to defend the best player, I do think Hauser is the best choice when it comes to the offensive upside of this team. I hope we see this lineup get more minutes moving forward because it feels like the Celtics have found something that really works here.

Maybe the biggest story of the Celtics losing streak was how bad their shooting was three-point range. Over those 4 games, Boston shot 68-for-205 (33.2%) from the perimeter, but this game was a refreshing change of pace as Boston finished shooting 22-51 (43.1%) against the Wizards. However, even this number can be a little bit misleading.

In the first half, the Celtics could not miss from beyond the arc, shooting 15-26 (57.7%). This was aided due to the fact that it felt like the Wizards were leaving them wide open on almost every attempt and it looked like all the frustration from the last four games were coming out all at once. Then the second half hit and Boston's shooting started to mirror what they've been doing, finishing 7-25 (28%). Washington didn't necessarily change their defensive game plan, giving Boston the same open threes but the Celtics just weren't knocking them down.

It is encouraging that Boston did hit 22 threes in this game, showcasing the elite form they were playing with earlier in the season. I am still concerned with the second half shooting continuing to drop off. This trend is due to the lack of rim pressure the Celtics have on the roster. Outside of Tatum, who consistently gets a lot of their points when it comes to attacking the basket? This results in the Celtics taking all of these threes and if they aren't falling, it will lead to losses. This is not a new problem by any means, it just seems to be a more glaring issue during this rough patch in the season.

The Celtics free throw shooting is not normally an issue that we have had to worry about in years past but to start this season we have seen Boston shoot very poorly at the charity stripe. Boston is ranked 24th in the NBA at 75.8%. There were a lot of misses in this game as the Celtics shot 17-25 from the line. Tatum missed 5 free throws in this game alone and although it didn't hurt them, we have seen it cost Boston games a few times already.

On the Celtics four game losing streak, we saw them lose almost every game at the free throw line. Against the Magic, they missed 8 free throws and lost by 10, against the Nuggets they missed 2 free throws and lost by 4, against the Kings they missed 10 free throws and lost by 1, and finally against the Warriors they missed 3 free throws and lost by 1.

With the 1st pick in the 2026 NBA Draft, the Washington Wizards selected Brockton, MA native AJ Dybantsa form BYU. Dybantsa for years has been in the zeitgeist of the NBA world and even the Celtics to an extent. Dybantsa has been a well known Celtics fan for a long time now and last season he played against UConn in TD Garden for the first time. In this game it was the first time he played against his hometown team and he showed out.

Dybantsa finished with 20 points, 11 rebounds, and 7 assists on 7-16 shooting from the field and 2-6 from three. He was incredibly explosive when it came to attacking the basket and even hit some big time shots running the offense for Washington in the fourth quarter. It was cool to see a kid from Massachusetts make it big to the NBA and he gave the Celtics a pretty good fight. In his postgame press conference he was asked how it felt to face off against his childhood team and he said, "I've been a Celtics fan since I came out the womb."

I hate to play conspiracy theorist, but it sounds like we could be seeing AJ in a Celtics jersey at some point in his NBA career. Just wait until he teams up well fellow New England star Cooper Flagg in 2035. It will be special hoops. Jokes aside, I think Dybantsa will have a great NBA career and will be thorn in the Celtics side in Eastern Conference battles to come. He will surely get a big standing ovation when the Wizards come to TD Garden for a mini two-game series on December 14th and 16th.

When it comes to the NBA Cup, no one has been better than the Boston Celtics this season. They are currently 3-0 in the standings with an incredible +69 point differential. This was aided from two blowouts against the Chicago Bulls and Charlotte Hornets but even the 15 point win against Washington makes them look even more impressive. The second best team according to point differential is the San Antonio Spurs at +53.

With the Celtics win in this game, they have a 99.9% chance of making it to the next round of the NBA Cup ahead of their last matchup against the Atlanta Hawks on Friday. If they win, they will be the number one seed in the Cup Playoffs and get home court advantage. If they lose by less than 40 points, they would end up as the four seed.

We have seen multiple players on this team talk about how they really want to win the NBA Cup this year. Sam Hauser talked about the thrilling matchup between the Knicks and Spurs last season and both of those teams made the NBA Finals. Neemias Queta also said he likes the idea of the NBA Cup and talked about how the Celtics had never made it very far and they have used that as motivation this season. If the numbers didn't already show it, Boston looks to be taking the NBA Cup very seriously and could potentially find themselves taking a trip to Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indiana for the Championship Game.
