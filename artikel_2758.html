T.J. Hockenson expected to play in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Minnesota Vikings TE T.J. Hockenson (wrist) fully participated in practice Friday, Nov. 20, and is not on the injury report for Week 11.

Why It Matters: Hockenson is on track to return to the lineup after missing the last game because of injury. He will likely see a handful of targets this week if active and can be considered a No. 2 tight end in fantasy leagues.
