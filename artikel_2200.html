Blackburn Rovers man wanted for longer by loan club

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Blackburn Rovers let the youngster join the non-league side on a temporary basis at the end of October. He is due to return to his parent club at the end of the month.

Houghton, 19, has since made a positive impression with FC Halifax Town in the National League and has caught the eye. He has made three appearances for the Shaymen in all competitions to get some valuable experience under his belt in the fifth tier.

His head coach McGrath, who played in the EFL for the likes of Doncaster Rovers and Burton Albion as a midfielder in his playing days, has shared this update on the player’s situation, as per a report by the Halifax Courier: “He’s done really well, we’d definitely look at extending it. Credit to Blackburn for trusting us with his development, and I thought he was good again on Saturday.”

McGrath’s men are sat in 10th place in the table after 16 fixtures and they are only four points off the play-offs behind Altrincham. They lost 2-0 away at League Two outfit Cheltenham Town over the weekend in the first round of the FA Cup.

Blackburn Rovers need to weigh up whether to let Houghton stay at FC Halifax Town. He is getting regular minutes at The Shay which will boost his development.

The prospect is a versatile operator who can play multiple positions. He is predominantly a right-back but can also play on the left as well if needed, as well as in defensive midfield.

Houghton has made 38 outings for Rovers’ U21s and has chipped in with two goals and six assists from the back. He has also played twice for their first-team.

He has risen up through the academy at Ewood Park and has been on the books of the Lancashire outfit for his whole career to date. He has been a regular for them at various different youth levels.

Blackburn Rovers will be monitoring Houghton’s progress at FC Halifax Town. They should be pleased with how things are going for him.

There is no reason why they shouldn’t let him stick around in Yorkshire for a bit longer. They are well stocked up with defensive options and don’t need him at the moment.

Rovers have a decision to make regarding his long-term future. He is out of contract when this term ends and is due to become available on a free transfer.

Tony Mowbray‘s side beat West Ham 2-1 over the weekend. They are back in Championship action after the international break against rivals Burnley away.
