Will Lankshear earns eye-catching Man Utd claim after Middlesbrough rise

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

EFL expert Adam Virgo has claimed that Middlesbrough star Will Lankshear would get into the current Manchester United starting lineup.

The former Tottenham Hotspur striker is enjoying a strong start to the campaign at the Riverside after making the summer switch to Kim Hellberg’s side.

The 21-year-old has so far scored 13 goals from 13 appearances in the Championship, with the Teesside outfit in the mix for a top two place in these early months of the season.

This has led to calls for him to potentially be called up to the England national team, with many comparing his goal scoring feats to previous second division stars like Aleksander Mitrovic and Ivan Toney.

The Serbian broke the EFL goal scoring record when he helped Fulham earn promotion in 2022, finding the back of the net a remarkable 43 times.

There is still a long way to go in the current campaign, but it’s a sign of Lankshear’s performances so far that he is being mentioned in the same breath as the current record holder.

Aaron McClean and Adam Virgo both debated what Premier League clubs Lankshear would be capable of starting in this season, with the former responding positively when asked about potentially getting into the Man United team.

The former suggested that Spurs could use him in their team as well, having only agreed to sell the striker to Boro last summer.

“He’s left Tottenham, and I watched Tottenham on the weekend against [Crystal Palace], I think it just proves again why Tottenham have just struggled in front of goal, in terms of the amount of chances that they’ve created,” said Virgo, via the Inside the 72 podcast.

“Would he get in the Spurs side now, yes or no?”

“And then we’re looking at teams that maybe looking at strikers, could he get into these clubs: Arsenal?" asked Virgo.

“So you don’t think Everton need a striker?” continued Virgo.

“Well they’ve got one striker at the moment, I don’t think he walks straight in,” replied McClean.

“Could he walk into the Man United team at the moment?

“That’s interesting. I mean, Sunderland, yes. Coventry, yes. Hull, yes. Forest, Palace, Brighton,” said Virgo.

“I don’t think he gets in Brighton, bearing in mind they’re the top scorers in the league at the moment. I don’t think he walks straight in,” answered McClean.

Lankshear has a non-penalty expected goals (xG) of 0.88 per 90 from 13 appearances for Boro so far this season (all stats from Fotmob).

While he has over-performed expectations, he is still putting up quite impressive figures, taking a total of 4.12 shots each game, 2.15 of which are on target.

Middlesbrough agreed a deal with Spurs worth a total of £20 million for Lankshear, with the club hoping that he could be a difference-maker in their push for promotion.

And the forward has made a big impact so far, as Hellberg’s side go into the final weekend before the November international break sitting top of the Championship table.

The Teesside outfit have earned 28 points from their first 14 fixtures, putting them one point clear of second place Wolves.

Next up for Boro is a trip to face Watford on Saturday in a 3pm kick-off at Vicarage Road.

Lankshear’s previous loan spell at Oxford United last season showed a lot of promise, as he scored 11 goals from 44 appearances, but he has already surpassed that tally for Boro.

The forward has taken his game to another level in the Championship, and potentially is ready to compete in the Premier League.

The 21-year-old has shown for Hellberg’s side what Spurs are missing, with Roberto de Zerbi’s side particularly struggling to find a convincing number nine to lead the line.

Whether or not he could walk into the current Man United team perhaps depends on the fitness of Benjamin Sesko, who is currently out until January, as they otherwise lack a real striker in their squad.
