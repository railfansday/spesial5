Anthropic (ANT) Debuts on Coinbase Amid Volatile IPO

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Leading cryptocurrency exchange Coinbase has announced the official listing of artificial intelligence (AI) giant Anthropic (ANT) shares on its trading platform in the U.S. and U.K.

The listing comes as Anthropic, the company behind the popular AI chatbot Claude, officially made its much-anticipated stock market debut on Thursday, Nov. 19, with its shares seeing massive volatility.

As per the latest X post from Coinbase, the exchange announced the availability of Anthropic (ANT) shares for eligible users in the U.S. and U.K.

It's important to mention that ANT isn't a cryptocurrency but a traditional stock. Coinbase's support for ANT shares shows the company's efforts to expand its offerings beyond crypto into traditional financial markets.

Anthropic has set a new historic precedent, having successfully raised about $81 billion in its initial public offering (IPO). This is the biggest IPO ever.

The artificial intelligence company sold 980 million shares at $83 per share, significantly higher than its previously announced price range of $70 to $78.

This brought Anthropic's initial valuation to about $1.5 trillion, making the company one of the most valuable publicly listed firms in the world right from the get-go.

But as Anthropic shares started trading on the Nasdaq exchange, the ANT ticker saw massive volatility.

As per Reuters, Anthropic shares began trading at $111.50. This represented an enormous 34% premium over the IPO price.

At the initial peak, Anthropic's market cap exceeded $2 trillion, surpassing SpaceX's valuation when it debuted on the market in June.

Anthropic shares slipped to around $95 as the stock gave back a significant chunk of its gains. This was partly due to investors looking to book their profits.

Nonetheless, Anthropic shares were still trading comfortably above their $83 IPO price.

The volatility surrounding Anthropic's stock market debut signals increasing doubts about the AI investment craze.

The listing of Anthropic shares is just one of Coinbase's moves to enter traditional financial markets.

Instead of just providing cryptocurrency trading, the exchange is looking to become a platform for multiple asset classes.

Interestingly, Coinbase had already listed Anthropic pre-IPO perpetual futures for eligible non-U.S. users on Nov. 18.

The perpetual futures were derivative contracts that allowed traders to speculate on Anthropic's valuation before its shares officially started trading on Nasdaq.

With the latest listing, eligible Coinbase users in the U.S. and U.K. can now trade the publicly listed Anthropic shares.

Anthropic's blockbuster debut comes as financial markets have been rocked by recent volatility, with tech stocks and crypto facing selling pressure.
