The Rings of Power is coming back for a third season, and the stakes are even higher this time around following a five-year time jump—Sauron’s army is blazing a path through Middle-earth to continue his war with the Elves, and there will no doubt be casualties on his quest to craft the One Ring that grants him total rule.

“The five-year jump allowed the story to have scope, for the world to change,” the show’s co‑showrunner, Patrick McKay, said in an interview with SFX Magazine. “The war has been raging across Middle-earth, and now we’re at a tipping point. Sauron has conquered everything except for two pockets of Elven resistance that are defended by their rings.”

He added, “This third season is where every single storyline becomes part of the large, epic war between Sauron and the Elves, and hopefully the show is deeper in its emotionality and the psychology of the characters.”
