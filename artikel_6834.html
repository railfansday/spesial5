Thanksgiving is coming up fast. We hope, for the sake of your wallet, that you’ve already booked your flights. Airfare prices are enough to make us consider driving. The prices are so insane that we’re already thinking about Black Friday—skip the food and family and whatever we’re thankful for. Our wallets are begging for some deals.

It’s no surprise that Amazon is one of the main places we’re looking for deals, and there are already some nice markdowns on Apple tech, watches, and Lego sets. We’re waiting to see if Bezos and his team slash the prices on Kindles, vacuums, and big-ticket furniture.
