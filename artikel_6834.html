The 7 Best Nail Trends to Try Right Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

From “Winter Wine” to “Tiger’s Eye,” these looks are perfect for colder months ahead

It’s a little too early for holiday nail art manicures, and maybe even too late for a fall palette of maples, golds, and pumpkin polish shades. So, what to do in the weeks of November ahead when it comes to your nail look?

“November calls for rich, warming shades such as chocolate brown, mocha, espresso, and caramel, alongside deep cherry, oxblood, and olive green,” says Townhouse founder Juanita Huber-Millet. “Our Flawless collection captures the season beautifully, with coffee-inspired shades like Chai Latte and Mocha, and timeless reds such as Red Apple and Cherry for an effortlessly chic fall manicure.”

Huber-Millet also recommends burgundy, classic red, and deep plum for a timeless winter shade. “This season, expect modern twists with glossy chrome and cat-eye finishes, colored French tips and delicate gems that add a festive touch without compromising on sophistication,” the professional adds.

Below, discover our edit of the best nail trends and colors to wear this November and beyond, including gemstone-inspired cat-eye shades and modern, high-shine neutrals to suit any occasion.

A rich, high-shine black-brown shade is the new classic color for the season. The key is to opt out of milkier shades and lean into depth, with crisp, clean cuticles and a generously glossy top coat.

Dress up purple and burgundy shades with subtle polka dots, embracing the year’s most playful nail art trend with a sophisticated, grown-up edge. The key is to keep the dots spacious and minimal.

A clean, black manicure is as much a timeless winter staple as a gray layering piece or a pair of leather boots. The key is to keep your nails short and smart with a soft-square shape, and pay extra attention to hydrating and tidying your cuticles.

Trade cow print and leopard spots for a more elevated, minimal zebra print this November. The best method is to put a playful twist on the classic French tip manicure, using fine lines to create subtle definition.

November’s take on the burgundy manicure is the darkest, glossiest yet. Pair deep, shiny pinotage tones with a classic shape, like almond or soft-square, and embrace purple-leaning hues over classic reds.

“Chrome and cat-eye finishes are key trends this winter,” says Huber-Millet. “At Townhouse, we’re seeing brown chrome become a firm favorite, combining rich, chocolatey tones with a luminous finish for a manicure that feels luxurious, contemporary, and perfectly suited to the season.”

The result? A multidimensional finish evocative of the classic tiger’s-eye gemstone.

Skip natural pink chrome and cat-eye this season and embrace rich, dark bases, from black to brown to burgundy. For a wintry twist, opt for a reflective blue or silver sheer chrome that allows the depth to shine through while highlighting its unique shift.
