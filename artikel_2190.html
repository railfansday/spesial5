Team news: 8 players set to miss Wolves vs Charlton Athletic

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Championship returns this weekend after the November international break, and at Molineux, Wolves look to extend their unbeaten league run to seven against Charlton Athletic.

The Addicks picked up a much-needed 2-1 win over Sheffield United at the Valley in the final game before the break, snapping an 11-game winless streak and alleviating some of the pressure that may have been building on Nathan Jones.

Now the next task is to replicate that on the road, where they've failed to win since their first away game of the season against West Ham United, but it won't be easy against a Wolves side who are within touching distance of top spot.

There are still parts of the Old Gold fanbase who are sceptical of head coach Cesar Peixoto, but one thing they can agree on is that, Bolton Wanderers aside, they've been well in control of their home games this season.

Both sides will be keen to take all three points, but these eight players won't be available to help do so.

The key injury in the Wolves camp remains with Raul Jimenez, with the veteran striker set to miss the rest of the calendar year after going in for knee surgery in mid-October.

The 35-year-old rejoined the club this past summer after three years at Fulham, where he still showcased his Premier League goalscoring qualities, netting 28 league goals in those three seasons.

He scored three in his first six back at Molineux, but isn't close to being able to add to that.

Left-back Ghislain Konan was brought to Wolves in the summer from Gil Vicente, reuniting with Cesar Peixoto in the first place. The 30-year-old provided some much-needed experience, with 59 Ivorian caps throughout his career.

However, Konan hasn't gotten going in the Old Gold just yet, and after just three appearances, he was sidelined until late December after picking up an MCL injury.

Charlton boss Nathan Jones was as coy as ever when discussing injuries ahead of the trip to Wolves, stating that those who missed Sheffield United are "closer than they were two weeks ago," and instead putting the focus on those who came in to replace those injured players.

Club captain Lloyd Jones was absent from a league squad for the first time this season against the Blades. He'd played every minute up until then. The hip injury he picked up could see him back this weekend, but he remains a doubt.

Karlarn Grant has also been a key part of Nathan Jones' side since making his return to the side he came through the academy with this past summer.

Nathaniel Chalobah was the third absentee from that win over Sheffield United after picking up a thigh injury.

It's the latest setback in what has been a troubling few years for the 31-year-old. He's already missed two months of the current season with a similar issue, and only played just over 1,000 minutes of league action across two years at Sheffield Wednesday prior to his summer move to the Valley, due to injury, too.

Reece Burke is another in the Charlton squad who has battled injury problems throughout his career. He reunited with ex-Luton Town boss Jones ahead of last season, having played fewer than 27 league games per year in his four campaigns with the Hatters.

He only managed 17 appearances last season and has yet to see the pitch this year after suffering an innocuous injury ahead of an EFL Cup match with Tottenham Hotspur back in August.

Charlton are more transparent with their long-term setbacks. Rob Apter is one of those, with a knee injury picked up a month ago, sidelining him for "an extended spell."
