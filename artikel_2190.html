Nathan Jones confirms worry over ‘tough’ injury setback for Charlton Athletic summer signing

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Nathaniel Chalobah made his first start for Charlton Athletic since the end of August on Tuesday night. However, he only lasted until half time following a crunching challenge from Akin Famewo.

And, unfortunately for CAFC, it appears that the tackle aggravated the thigh injury that kept Chalobah out for two months. Speaking to South London Sport, Nathan Jones said: “He got injured. I think it is the same thing as what kept him out at the start of the season – his thigh.

“We will have the diagnosis, but it wasn’t a tactical decision, we won every game with him at the start of the season and then he got injured. We tried to bring him back – he has trained with us for nearly two weeks so it is not too early. We were always going to give him 55-60 minutes anyway, but I think he has pulled his thigh. It is a tough one again.“

Chalobah’s last start came against Preston North End, a game Charlton Athletic won 1-0. The Addicks won their first three games of the season with the former Chelsea and Sheffield Wednesday man in midfield, but they have not won since.

Chalobah has gained a wealth of experience in the Championship, but he has often struggled with injuries. Previous club Sheffield Wednesday spent large spells without the 31-year-old, and the same fate looks to have hit the Addicks. He played only 31 times in a two-year stay at Hillsborough.

Chalobah’s most notable injury came during his time at Watford, when he had major knee surgery in 2018 and spent a total of 340 days sidelined.

Fears are that his injury struggles are set to persist at CAFC. His influence is clear to see as a strong defensive presence in the middle of the park, but the likes of Conor Coventry, Oliver Skipp and potentially the rarely-seen Timothy Ouma will have to cover in his absence.

In the summer, Charlton Athletic recruited some experienced Championship including midfielders Chalobah and Skipp. However, the former is dealing with injuries, and the latter is yet to win a game in CAFC colours.

They bolstered their forward line with Millenic Alli and Karlan Grant. Grant has scored two goals this season, however, Alli is yet to score and both are being outshone by academy graduate Tyreece Campbell – who has not scored in the last five league matches himself.

The Addicks need more from their summer signings, with disappointment over Chalobah’s lack of fitness and the limited impacts of bigger names like Grant, Skipp and more.

Charlton Athletic have some tough matches on the horizon, with Sheffield United, Wolves, Lincoln City and Blackburn Rovers to play before the end of the month. Time will tell just how long Chalobah faces out, but Jones has to find a winning formula with his available players, otherwise his future at The Valley will be in doubt as their winless run stretches to 11.
