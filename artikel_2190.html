Middlesbrough and Will Lankshear’s stance on January transfer as further interest emerges

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Middlesbrough striker Will Lankshear has put himself in the spotlight with stunning form since moving to Teesside in a £20m package deal. The Boro man has 15 goals in 16 games across all competitions, and has already been the subject of transfer speculation with Newcastle United said to be keen.

Now, a report from TEAMtalk details further interest. They claim that all of Manchester United, Brighton & Hove Albion, Everton, Sunderland and Borussia Dortmund have all scouted Lankshear, while North London rivals and former clubs Spurs and Arsenal are both monitoring the situation as well.

However, Boro have no desire to sell Lankshear, and the 21-year-old is not looking for a swift step up either. The Championship’s top scorer is ‘delighted’ with his progress and focused on maintaining his form as Kim Hellberg‘s side push for promotion to the Premier League.

There’s a lot of hype around Lankshear’s future and excitement over what the coming years could have in store. It’s understandable too, as it isn’t every day that a 21-year-old English striker averages a goal every 78 minutes for a Championship club.

But, for now, it is of paramount importance that Lankshear is allowed to focus on his football as he continues to progress his game. Harry Kane – who the Middlesbrough star has already been compared to – has urged patience regarding his emergence and development.

Moving up to a Premier League club would bring increased pressure and scrutiny for Lankshear while he is still progressing. Stepping to a higher level too soon could actually stunt his growth.

While he is in flying form and playing regularly, Lankshear is in the ideal place for his progression. Boro are on the up themselves as they challenge for Championship promotion as well, so it could be that he makes the rise on Teesside.

Although Middlesbrough have no intention of cashing in and Lankshear won’t be pushing for a move, that doesn’t mean that interest will ease up. Clubs are sure to keep monitoring his situation and tracking his performances given his high ceiling and impressive form.

Their stances may well change the plans of any admirers who were planning a winter approach. However, it seems that those who do make moves will either be told that Lankshear is not available at any price, or they will be given a sky-high valuation to fend off further interest.

Lankshear seems bound for the top, but there should be no rush to get there. He can make the rise in good time, but only if the developmental years of his career are managed correctly.
