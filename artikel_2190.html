Four chances created: West Brom starlet impresses in long-awaited league debut for loan club after challenging spell

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

West Brom signed two new right-backs in Nolan Galves and Brayann Pereira in the summer transfer window. It meant first-team opportunities were set to be limited for WBA academy prospect Alex Williams, so a loan move to Scottish Premiership side Hibernian was sanctioned.

Frustratingly, Williams’ stint at Easter Road has been challenging. The manager who signed him, David Gray, didn’t give the Baggies prospect many opportunities and he was then sacked.

Williams had played just 11 minutes before Saturday’s trip to Pittodrie to face Aberdeen. However, the 21-year-old was called into action when Felix Passlack was forced off early on, and he took his chance to impress in a rare opportunity.

Passlack has been the favoured starter on the right for Hibernian, including under new boss Marinek Reedijk. Kanayo Megwa was his backup early in the campaign before injury hit but West Brom’s Williams was still finding opportunities hard to come by.

A chance came his way on Saturday though, and he took it. Williams played 81 minutes as Hibs ran out 4-0 winners against the Dons. He provided a complete performance at right-back, offering attacking threat and defensive solidity.

The WBA talent created four chances and completed 30 of his 33 passes for a completion rate of 91%. He was frequently trusted in possession with 56 touches and he saw to his defensive duties impressively too, winning four of his seven ground duels while also completing two tackles, three clearances and two interceptions (stats via FotMob).

Williams’ performance was a testament to his attitude. Despite incredibly limited chances to date, he was sharp and ready to step up for Hibs when they needed him, and that can not go unnoticed.

West Brom and Williams will be hoping this serves as a turning point in his Hibernian loan. The extent of Passlack’s injury remains to be seen, but it could give the Baggies loanee a chance to make the starting spot his own.

With more performances like the one against Aberdeen, Williams can ease the blow of losing Passlack. It will also strengthen his chances of keeping a place in the plans of new boss Meerdijk.

WBA are sure to be monitoring his situation closely. If minutes remain hard to come by, the Championship side will not hesitate to bring Williams back and send him out on loan to a new club – which they can do, as his only appearance before joining Hibs came in the Carabao Cup.

Williams can find the minutes he needs and deserves in Passlack’s absence, so it remains to be seen just how long he faces out.
