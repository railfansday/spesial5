Semtech (SMTC -17.72%) reported record financial results for the third quarter, which management attributed to significant expansion in data center and wireless connectivity end markets. The company stated that its high-speed analog portfolio is benefiting from a broader industry shift toward AI networking architectures that require lower power consumption and reduced latency. Management noted that it has secured multiple design wins for its signal integrity solutions across several hyperscale customers, including new platforms for inference and training. The company indicated that it is actively increasing manufacturing capacity for its photonics components to support the ramp of next-generation optical interconnects.
