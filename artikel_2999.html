Lubbock High football snaps 18-year drought in Battle of the Silver Spurs

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

It's not just another high school football game when Lubbock High and Monterey face off.

Ask any Lubbock native, and they'll say the same thing when you mention the town's two oldest schools: "Oh, the Battle for the Spurs?"

The matchup, which started back in 1956, is part of the fabric of high school football in the Hub City.

In that same vein, the silver spurs haven't moved from its perch inside the Monterey High School display case since 2007. The spurs come along with the football from the first game, which saw Monterey claim a 27-7 victory on Thanksgiving back in 1956.

That spot will have a vacancy following Lubbock High's 45-26 victory over the Plainsmen in a District 2-5A Division I contest Friday, Nov. 6, at PlainsCapital Park at Lowrey Field.

The Westerners (4-5, 3-4) had to fend off a second-half offense spurt from Monterey (3-7, 2-5) to claim the famed trophy and add it to their display case.

Lubbock High had some margin for error in the final two quarters, though, building a 24-0 advantage at the halfway point.

After leaving the locker room down by more than three scores, Monterey showed some spunk by scoring on its first drive of the second half — getting a 75-yard touchdown run from Legin Williams — to cut the deficit to 24-6 following a missed extra point with 9:55 left in the third quarter.

The Westerners continued to keep the pressure on as Johnson scored his second touchdown of the game to keep Lubbock High ahead 31-6.

But, Monterey found its spark on offense as Williams bursted for a 75-yard touchdown that he went down the sideline to provide his team with some energy and slice the deficit to 31-13 with 8:34.

Last season, the Plainsmen downed the Westerners 56-34 in 2025.

Lubbock ISD executive athletics director Mike Meeks said the decision came down to participation, per previous reporting from the Lubbock Avalanche-Journal. As each program welcomed a new head coach, Meeks said LHS didn't have near the roster size of Monterey. It would have been a non-district contest after the Westerners dropped to Class 5A Division II for the 2022-24 alignment cycle.
