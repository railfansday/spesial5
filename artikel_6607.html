The Black Friday Beauty Deals You Should Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The number of texts, DMs, WhatsApp messages, and Slack chats I receive asking for beauty recommendations only skyrockets around Black Friday and Cyber Monday. For better or worse, I’ve become the resident beauty expert among my friends and family, and it seems everywhere I turn, someone has an inquiry about what to buy (especially when the best Black Friday beauty deals are on the line).

Gone are the days of lining up outside mega retailers right after your turkey dinner to rush in store and snag holiday gifts. As an introvert, I’d never endure a line or consumerism chaos in pursuit of any deal, ever. But for those who are sale-insatiable, online shopping requires no change out of your post-Thanksgiving sweats (or leaving the couch). Somehow, Thanksgiving has come and gone—and yes, the deals are here. Of course, holiday shopping season is also good time to stock up the best deals for skincare products, coveted gift sets, and more from your favorite beauty brands. Below is a tight curation of the best Black Friday deals, so far.

The Westman Atelier Black Friday sale is one of those rare moments when beauty lovers can score the brand’s coveted clean cosmetics at a true steal. Remember: These products rarely go on sale. Known for its thoughtful, skin-enhancing formulas and weighty, sculptural packaging, the brand is rolling out 20% off sitewide, with up to 50% off select items. What to buy? Baby Cheeks blush stick imparts a natural flush, while Eye Want You mascara leaves lashes feathery and soft to the touch. Meanwhile, the brand’s tinted liquid highlight leaves skin with a lit-from-within glow.

Believe it or not, there’s another shopping platform to peruse—say hello to Shop, the consumer-facing sister of Shopify. Everything from those buzzy direct-to-consumer brands to K-beauty favorites is in one place, with no tab-hopping. What to buy? Take Beauty of Joseon’s brightening face mask, or Merit’s non-sticky lip oil—you can add both to cart right now.

The “everything store” seems to have deals on everything—and sometimes that means sorting through bizarre, random products to find what you’re actually looking for. Amazon is the place to go for almost any annual, semi-annual, or ad hoc sale, from Prime Day to Cyber Monday and more. For a tinted SPF, Colorescience’s formula color-adjusts to become your perfect shade. Plus, Charlotte Tilbury’s Exagger-eyes mascara is a best-selling volumizing formula.

For Soft Services’s deals, the more you spend, the more you save—so make your shopping cart heavy. Up to $49 and you’ll get 10% off; spend between $50-$99 and the discounts bump up to 15%; Cross into the triple-digit threshold, and you’ll get 20% off when you spend up to $149. Finally, spend $150 and beyond, and you’ll unlock 25% off. From body retinols (and retinol hand creams), to buffing bars and beyond—your smoothest skin yet has never been more affordable.

Something about Nordstrom brings up nostalgic memories of wandering into a department store with my mom (and inevitably, being a child, wanting to go home). But there’s no need to peruse between departments IRL. Nordstrom’s virtual sale is notoriously good each year—especially for beauty. There’s prestige skin-care brand Allies of Skin, with their offerings of peptide and grow factor serums on sale. Plus, 111Skin’s acclaimed skin-firming face mask enriched with black diamonds.

Credo—with its meticulously vetted products that marry safety, sustainability, and performance—is the go-to destination for the clean beauty buff. Take Sakara’s probiotic and collagen supplements, designed to improve your skin health from the inside out. Then there’s Michelle Pfeifer’s Henry Rose, which offers fine fragrances with clean formulations; plus Saie’s makeup brush kit to elevate your glam.

While you’re filling your cart with trending clothing and accessories at Revolve, it’s well worth taking a peek at the beauty deals, too. The selection might surprise you. Those looking to target signs of aging, hyperpigmentation, or even rosacea will find Lyma’s professional grade at-home laser is on sale. Plus, there’s are plenty of bodycare and makeup, like Hanni’s hydrading mist or Summer Friday’s lip staining marker.

For the minimalist beauty lover, Merit’s annual cyber sale is a can’t-miss moment—it’s quite literally the brand’s only sale of the year. The deals have already started (and will last through Cyber Monday), offering up to 44% off sitewide. Those who dread taking off their makeup after a long day will appreciate Merit’s Great Skin Double Cleanse—the foaming formula works double duty to remove makeup and cleanse skin all in one go. Meanwhile, the brand’s no-liner lip liner offers a soft, contour-like definition that enhances lips without harsh lines.

Right now, you can score up to 25% off everything—makeup, skincare, fragrance, even merch at Glossier. As for scents, the brand’s beloved You eau de parfum remains the ultimate skin fragrance: warm, intimate, and entirely addictive. Then there’s Plush Blush, the cushioned, powdery evolution of the beloved original liquid Cloud Paint; it blends like a dream, wears like a whisper, and looks almost good enough to eat.

Speaking of clean beauty powerhouses, Sasha Plavsic launched Ilia 14 years ago with a mission to create skin-first formulas that perform. The brand has since become a staple for beauty insiders, offering everything from milky toners (a favorite of Vogue’s beauty editor Kiana Murden) to serum-like skin tints with SPF and natural-looking lip liners. In short, Ilia has something for everyone. Right now, the brand is offering 20% off sitewide, with surely more deals ahead in the coming days.

Clean beauty fans raise your hands. You’re in luck! This Black Friday, Kosas is offering 25% off sitewide—meaning you can snag its cult-favorite skincare-makeup hybrids at a fraction of the price. We’d recommend starting with the Cloud Set loose powder, a favorite of Vogue beauty editor Kiana Murden. Meanwhile, Hailey Bieber swears by Revealer Extra Bright in her quick makeup routine.

Founded in 2012 by Korean American aesthetician Charlotte Cho, Soko Glam has been bringing K-beauty to the States long before TikTok made the category mainstream. Known for its innovative formulas, targeted solutions, and indulgent textures, K-beauty merges modern science with long-standing tradition. “At a young age, Korean children are taught the importance of moisturizing, exfoliating, cleansing the skin thoroughly, and protecting their skin with SPF,” Cho previously told Vogue.

For those keen to cross some K-beauyt off their wishlists this Black Friday, here are a few of our favorite deals. Round Labs hydrating and whitecast-free sunscreen is a necessity yearround. Plus, VT Cosmetics’ viral spicule serum delivers a microneedling effect without a trip to the dermatologist, And Axis-Y’s eye cream offers a vegan collagen alternative designed to soften under-eyes and smooth crow’s feet.

Posh Spice has a well-honed eye for chic things—whether she’s designing glamorous clothes or formulating makeup staples. Her beauty brand champions 100% clean formulas housed in sleek, weighty packaging that feels as luxurious as it looks. Now through Cyber Monday, you can take up to 20% off sitewide—no code needed, and a rare opportunity considering the label seldom marks down its best sellers. Among the standout recent launches is Colour Wash, a silky, liquid blush tint, while the brand’s latest launch, Blush Stylus, offers a vibrant creamy format.

Vogue’s Kiana Murden uses Victoria Beckham Beauty’s Blush Stylus.

Vogue’s Kiana Murden uses Victoria Beckham Beauty’s Colour Wash liquid blush.

One of the main beauty mega-retailers stateside is Ulta Beauty—a beauty wonderland with everything from affordable to prestige prices in nearly every subcategory. For a hair dryer that delivers salon-worthy blowouts at home, try Dyson’s Airwrap multistyler, complete with three interchangeable attachments. For those more keen on smoothing and straightening, Bio-Ionic’s iron is a Vogue favorite. Meanwhile, the Oura ring is perfect for the wellness-conscious.

Salt & Stone is known for its clean, active-ingredient-infused body-care formulas—from body washes to mists, creams, natural deodorants, and more. For those looking to find their favorite scent, the brand offers a body mist discovery set—and once you’ve settled on it, you can get a candle with the same notes. There’s also a bundle of their bestsellers, or a decadently rich body cream.

Eadem aims to make skincare that’s not only luxurious but also safe for all skin tones. First, there’s a gift set with the brand’s best-selling exfoliating lip balm (a personal favorite of mine!), plus a four-step skin-care set for congested skin (gua sha included!).

This Cyber Week at Nécessaire, you can save in a few ways: the brand is offering 20% off sitewide, and once you hit $125, the discount jumps to 25% and includes a free lip balm. For anyone on a hair-growth journey, the Rosemary Hair Essentials—a shampoo, conditioner, and scalp serum powered by (you guessed it) rosemary oil—is a smart place to start. And don’t overlook the Hand Duo (and hand cream and retinol) in its chic metal tin as a gift for the beauty lover in your life.

When it comes to French cool girls, Violette Serrat leads the list. The Paris-born, New York–based makeup artist infused her signature effortless aesthetic into her eponymous beauty line, creating products that channel that impossibly chic, just-bitten French allure. The brand is especially loved for its lip offerings: from Lip Nectar (a silky, staining gloss) to Bisou Balm (a soft matte and blurred, sheer lipstick), to Balm Amour (an ultra-hydrating lip balm—which now comes in tinted versions)—all of which are on sale individually, or come in several variations of covetable sets. Plus, the brand’s new cream-to-powder eyeshadows and blushes are on sale, offering fail-proof, diffused washes of color. Whatever you buy, enjoy 25% off sitewide.

Is anyone binge-watching The Body on Netflix? If so, you’ll appreciate Dieux’s heavenly product name references—such as the Auracle peptide eye serum and soothing Skin Mercy face cream. Its collection also features limited-edition holiday sets too, offering combos of best-selling products perfect for stocking stuffers or gifts to yourself. This Black Friday through Cyber Monday, Dieux is offering 20% off sitewide, but once you hit $125, those savings jump to 25%. Plus, there’s 30% off all sets, and anyone who spends $85 or more gets a complimentary hand cream.

“The Jones Road philosophy has always been about learning to love yourself, flaws and all, and using makeup to enhance your natural features,” Jones Road founder Bobbi Brown previously told Vogue. The clean beauty label has quickly carved out its own space in the industry, championing straightforward formulas and an inclusive approach that ensures people of all ages, tones, and skin types can find something that works. Best of all? Their 20% off Black Friday sale applies to every product, and 30% off mini collections. Among the standouts: a non-comedogenic tinted moisturizer that feels weightless on the skin, alongside both complexion and color cosmetics sets. A balm that blends seamlessly, and an eyebrow pencil offered in six thoughtful shades—yes, including options for redheads and silver strands.
