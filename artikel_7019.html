Winter’s Most Wanted: Tommy Hilfiger Holiday Picks for Every Occasion

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

From sharp knits to travel-ready extras, these are the pieces worth putting on your list.

The sweater that looked fine in October suddenly feels too thin, the jacket you reach for every day isn’t warm enough, and a weekend away calls for more than whatever you can squeeze into an overnight bag. Then there’s the rest of the season: office parties, cold walks across town, slow mornings at home, and the occasional trip out of town. Getting dressed for all of it takes a few reliable essentials.

Tommy Hilfiger has been building its take on American style since 1985, when the brand launched with a fresh spin on classic menswear. Its signature mix of preppy staples, sporty details, and relaxed ease has made it a year-round proposition, with pieces that move from everyday dressing to more seasonal needs. For winter, that means familiar wardrobe staples with a little extra warmth, practical layers for unpredictable weather, and accessories suited to life on the go. These six picks cover the season from the first cold snap to the last weekend getaway. Here’s what we’re putting on our fashion gift list.

A cable-knit sweater is a cold-weather staple, and Tommy Hilfiger’s take keeps the look classic. Made from combed cotton with a traditional cable pattern and ribbed trim, it’s an easy layer under a wool coat or a natural choice for a casual holiday gathering. Pair it with dark jeans or chinos for a straightforward winter look.

For trips, a jacket that barely takes up any room in your bag is always useful. Tommy Hilfiger’s lightweight puffer folds into its own interior zip pocket, making it easy to stash for a weekend away or keep on hand when the forecast is unpredictable. It has thermal insulation, a regular fit, and side pockets, so it’s a practical layer for travel and everyday errands alike.

A good winter evening often calls for staying in, and this sleep set makes a comfortable night at home feel a little more put together. This one pairs a simple T-shirt with plaid flannel pants, bringing a classic cold-weather pattern into the mix. It’s an easy gift for someone who appreciates a slow weekend morning as much as a cozy night in.

A warm beanie is one of winter’s most useful accessories, and this one gets an upgrade with a cotton-cashmere blend. The classic knit is an easy finishing touch for everything from a morning commute to a weekend walk. It’s also a practical gift that will see plenty of use once the temperature drops.

A card holder and keychain are the kind of everyday essentials that make an especially easy gift. Tommy Hilfiger’s leather set brings the two together in one package, with a polished finish that feels a step up from the usual stocking stuffer. It’s a useful pick for someone who likes to keep their daily carry simple.

A weekend away calls for a bag that can handle a change of clothes and a few extra essentials without looking like you’re heading to the gym. Tommy’s leather duffel brings a more polished feel to the overnight bag, making it a smart companion for a winter getaway or a quick trip home for the holidays.
