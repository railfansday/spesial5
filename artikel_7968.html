John Yoo Joins the Exodus from California; Departs for Texas

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Mostly law professors | Sometimes contrarian | Often libertarian | Always independent

The University of California at Berkeley's law school loses its last right-leaning professor to teach in public law.

The Daily Californian reports that Professor John Yoo is leaving the University of California at Berkeley's law school. Beginning next year, he will be full-time at the University of Texas at Austin's School of Civic Leadership.

"Let the celebrations at my fan clubs in the City of Berkeley begin," Yoo said in an email. "Great as our university is, it would not allow a school to focus on restoring the central importance of western civilization to the education of all undergraduates." . . .

"I am sorry, however, that my departure will reduce the number of conservative professors in public law at Berkeley by 100 percent," Yoo said in the email. "I hope that the university will take this as an opportunity to consider that the notion of diversity should, most importantly, include ideology, not just the usual race, gender, and economic categories that my colleagues usually mean by diversity."

There had been one other right-leaning law professor at Berkeley, our co-blogger Orin Kerr, but he left Berkeley for Stanford in 2025.

According to Dean of Berkeley Law Erwin Chemerinsky, Yoo's decision to leave the faculty was entirely his own.

"John (Yoo) has been an important part of the faculty for many years," Chemerinsky said in an email. "This was entirely John's decision to retire. … He has indicated to me for a few years his thoughts about retiring. John is a very successful teacher and an eminent scholar." . . .

Yoo's move is also less drastic than it might appear, as he is moving from one blue state to another.  All of the other California transplants that have moved to Austin helped make sure of that (with a major assist from the Republican Party).
