Bilt Lava Acquisition: How Loyalty Follows the Fan

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Loyalty programs like the one behind the high-profile Bilt Lava acquisition are evolving to meet people where they already spend time, not just inside branded apps. The shift is especially clear as Bilt brings personalized rewards into stadiums by acquiring sports loyalty platform Lava, showing loyalty's next big move.

By acquiring Lava, Bilt instantly accesses a live network of sports fans that would have taken years to build alone. According to PYMNTS, Lava brings 56 professional sports franchises spanning the NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, and MLS—including the Detroit Lions, Denver Nuggets, Carolina Hurricanes, and LAFC—directly into Bilt’s ecosystem. Lava also supplies over 300 technology adapters, according to Banking Dive, linking systems for POS, ticketing, payments, and loyalty, so venues can deliver real-time recognition and benefits to fans.

Bilt, which started in June 2021 with rewards for rent and later expanded to condo fees, mortgages, and hospitality, has a network of about 7 million members and 50,000 merchants. The Lava deal now connects this base to stadium venues and supports Bilt’s mission to reach customers where they already gather.

The push into sports is about tapping the power of community. Bilt views loyalty as a neighborhood commerce ecosystem, recognizing that communities are not just formed by addresses but also by shared fandom. Bilt CEO Ankur Jain, cited by PYMNTS, believes sports communities influence how people spend, where they go, and who they interact with.

Lava’s co-founder Vivek Ranadive, owner of the NBA’s Sacramento Kings, envisions turning sports loyalty into a "neighborhood economy" benefiting fans, teams, and local merchants, according to Banking Dive. For Bilt, this acquisition is also a chance to leave behind a public fallout with Wells Fargo—its former major card partner—finding new growth in the lives of fans who may not be reached through cards alone.

Loyalty programs are shifting from static, app-based experiences to dynamic, real-time recognition wherever members are. Traditionally, programs asked users to log points in a dedicated app, missing the context and intent behind real-world activities. The new approach lets teams and businesses recognize fans at stadium gates, concession stands, and even in local restaurants, seizing not just the game day but every related moment. Lava CEO Wen Miao highlighted that loyalty today needs to extend well beyond the event itself, as Banking Dive reported.

Bilt plans to blend its AI agents with Lava’s instant engagement tech, using data and partnerships to offer personalized benefits that arrive in the moment. Instead of static coupons, members can receive real-time offers based on their actions right then and there. The loyalty program becomes an identity layer: always present, recognizing members, and responding in real time—not just tallying points.

On November 5, 2026, GlobeNewswire reported firmly's $15 million Series A for agentic shopping. firmly Connect already touches over 11 million merchants and $900 billion in gross merchandise value. firmly’s new Ask Agent product lets consumers shop conversationally—describing their needs in natural language and checking out within the chat, with loyalty rewards integrated into the process.

The figures are persuasive: early results show up to 18% higher conversion rates and a 23% lift in order value for merchants, as reported by GlobeNewswire. AI-driven shopping visits to United States retail websites are expected to grow 130% year over year, making it clear that embedding loyalty in the buying moment is a fast-growing trend.

Even small businesses and programs can adopt a loyalty model that meets customers wherever they are. By using loyalty that plugs into existing tools, a program can function on a customer’s phone and seamlessly connect with existing systems. Digital wallet passes let even small merchants recognize and reward customers at the shop, stadium, or during checkout, no app required.

Bilt paid $300 million for Lava, announcing the deal on November 2, 2026. This gives Bilt access to 56 sports franchises and more than 300 technology adapters, integrating with its 7 million members and 50,000 merchant base.

Bilt believes that communities formed around sports are as meaningful as where people live. By reaching fans at stadiums and through their favorite teams, Bilt extends its loyalty program beyond residential life into areas heavily influenced by shared passions.

firmly’s Ask Agent, launched with its $15 million funding round on November 5, 2026, delivers a conversational shopping experience where shoppers can earn and redeem loyalty rewards as part of their purchase, directly in the chat.

Bilt’s acquisition of Lava and firmly’s funding for agentic commerce both demonstrate that loyalty is no longer confined to apps. Programs that follow members into the stadium, onto the street, and inside the checkout—responding in real time—are setting the new standard for customer loyalty.
