Peppermint Bark Cookie Bars

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Line a 13×9-inch pan with parchment paper, letting the ends extend up the sides. In a large bowl, use a hand mixer or stand mixer to cream the butter, sugar and vanilla extract until it’s light and fluffy, five to seven minutes. Beat in the egg yolks until they’re combined.

In another bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour and salt. Gradually beat the flour mixture into the creamed mixture just until no streaks of flour remain.

In the top of a double boiler or a metal bowl over hot water, melt the dark chocolate, stirring until it’s completely smooth. Remove the bowl from the heat and spread the dark chocolate over the cooled bars.

Editor’s Tip: Never let the water touch the bottom of the bowl, or it will burn the chocolate. Also, try not to get any water into the chocolate, or it will seize.

Repeat the melting and spreading process with the white chocolate, then immediately sprinkle the top with crushed peppermint candies while the chocolate is still wet.
