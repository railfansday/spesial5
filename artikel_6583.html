Check-ing In! A Very Burberry Bash at The Mark Hotel

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The pre-holiday party season is in full swing—and if you needed any further proof, one just had to look around the jam-packed dining room at The Mark on Monday evening. Despite the body heat radiating from the shoulder-to-shoulder crowd, guests including Teyana Taylor, Gabrielle Union Wade, and Apple Martin gathered in Daniel Lee’s latest outerwear to raise a festive tipple to Burberry’s takeover of the Upper East Side hotel.

In the hotel’s first fashion partnership in several years, the collaboration sees a Burberry stamp everywhere you turn at the Madison Avenue property. Just in time for the cold snap, the hotel is wrapped up in the brand’s checks and quintessentially British shade of pillar-box red—from welcome flags and the Knight-emblazoned carpet to a colossal bow adorning the entrance. Even the doormen are keeping cozy in woolly scarves.

Not since the first Monday in May has the popular hotel been so thronged with famous faces at the beginning of the work week. To celebrate the unveiling of the collaboration, friends of the house donned trenches, car coats, and leather jackets, posing in front of the Burberry-ified ribbon-laced tree in the glossy black-and-white lobby.

Taylor, in oodles of fur and supersize shades, oozed star power that overshadowed the well-lit tree as she posed for photographer Tyrell Hampton in a corner (as well as a half dozen iPhones). Paige and Tommy Paul, still radiating newlywed bliss, paused to peek at a customized gingerbread house, while content creators posed in front of the outdoor chalets and hot chocolate cart. Meanwhile, upstairs, VICs slipped discreetly into The Mark’s famed penthouse—the $400,000-a- night suite—to book their private shopping appointments.

Inside, a model named Ocean Van Exel was the center of attention, as she debuted a honey-blond wig painted in Burberry checks. “I actually already had this from a shoot I did a few weeks ago,” she told Vogue, as passersby gushed that they were “gagged” over the look. “I worked with an incredible wig artist named Emmett on it.”

The night also saw a surprise performance by People I’ve Met, a new-ish rock band fronted by Moses Martin (son of Coldplay’s Chris). As guests tucked into mini burgers and fries, the trio launched into “For Hire,” a song that soundtracked Burberry’s Escape to the Countryside campaign, in which Moses also made his modeling debut.
