Amazon unveils $250 security drone that patrols your house and flies between rooms

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Amazon has unveiled a $250 AI-powered security camera that can fly around your house on its own to investigate suspicious activity.

Announced on November 10, the Ring Drone Cam can travel between rooms, check doors and windows, and automatically respond when a connected security camera, sensor, or alarm is triggered.

Unlike traditional security cameras, which remain fixed in one location, the Ring Drone Cam uses AI to navigate around furniture and other obstacles.

Owners can send it to specific locations, set custom flight routes, or schedule regular patrols around their homes.

If a connected security device detects activity, the drone can automatically fly to a predetermined location to investigate, allowing owners to check what’s happening remotely.

Amazon says the camera records in 2K resolution and features built-in lights for capturing footage in darker rooms. When not in use, it returns to its charging dock, where its camera remains switched off.

The company has also announced the Ring Rover Cam, a $999.99 wheeled security robot designed for businesses.

Rather than flying, the Rover patrols stores and offices, using an adjustable camera to see over counters and shelving. It can also investigate triggered alarms and activate a built-in siren and light tower.

The Rover is scheduled to begin shipping in the US on December 16, 2026, while the $249.99 Drone Cam is targeting January 27, 2027.

However, the flying camera still requires authorization from the Federal Communications Commission before it can be sold. Certain features, including scheduled patrols, also require a compatible Ring Protect subscription.
