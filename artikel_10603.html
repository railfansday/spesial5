Cat takes 37-mile train trip to London before staff escort him home

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A cat in England has been reunited with his family after he travelled on a 37-mile solo train trip before finally being sent home by station staff.

It’s been a big year for animals travelling vast distances, as we’ve seen everything from stolen dogs making cross-country escapes to their original homes to a French cat that travelled 155 miles home from Spain.

Now, an orange cat (it’s always orange) named Zeus has entered the competition after he boarded a train from Essex all the way to London.

Reported by the BBC, the four-legged fare dodger caught the 8:08pm train from Braintree, a town in Essex, England, on November 10 and travelled 37 miles to London Liverpool Street.

Why four-year-old Zeus decided to take a trip to London remains to be seen, but he didn’t manage to do much sightseeing as staff later found him at the station. Zeus was then taken into an office before being escorted on another train all the way back to his hometown.

Owner Lily Burroughs said she was shocked, but not exactly surprised by Zeus’ recent escapade as it turns out the crafty cat isn’t exactly a stranger to train travel.

“I just thought, ‘oh my God, he’s done it again,’” Burroughs told the BBC. Before moving to Braintree, Zeus had made trips from his former home in Stansted Mountfitchet to London Stansted Airport.

Burroughs said Zeus wears two trackers, but keeping him indoors has proved difficult. “I’ve tried to keep him in before, and he just does himself damage, he’s constantly trying to break out. He’s just not happy being indoors all the time,” she said.

“He’s quite a social cat, he likes to see everyone, so yeah, without everyone else sort of looking out for him, it would be a world of pain.”

Zeus’ owner revealed that London is the furthest the travel-loving cat has gone, but did say “he would probably go further if he had the chance.”

Burroughs thanked the staff who looked after Zeus, while the train company that runs the service Zeus used said he “enjoyed all the attention from staff at the station and customers on the train home.”
