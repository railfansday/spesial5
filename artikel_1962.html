Mika Baur Celtic injury latest as midfielder pulls out of Germany under-21s squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Mika Baur has been ruled out of Germany's under-21s squad for coming fixtures following his injury suffered against Kilmarnock.

The midfielder has had a strong campaign for the Hoops so far and become one of the standouts in the engine room with his performances.

He went down at Parkhead with what looked like a sore one after being caught by Aaron Tshibola of Killie in the second-half of his side's 1-0 victory in Glasgow.

Manager Martin O'Neill confirmed Baur, 22, would undergo scans on his ankle to determine the extent of the issue. And having been called into the Germany under-21s set-up by head coach Antonio Di Salvo, the football federation confirmed this afternoon that he had to withdraw.

Ahead of clashes with Belgium and Croatia, a short injury updated provided read: "Lukas Ullrich, Mika Baur, and Arijon Ibrahimovic are unfortunately unavailable. We have called up Tom Rothe, Kofi Amoako, and Maurice Krattenmacher as replacements.

"Furthermore, we currently don't know if Karim Coulibaly will be available, as he is showing symptoms of illness. We will have to wait until tomorrow to see if he is available."

It remains to be seen how long, if at all, Baur will be sidelined at Celtic but leaving the international camp provides him with the opportunity to potentially rehab the possible strain quickly.
