Lewes down Town to reach Sussex Senior Cup last eight

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

On a damp evening on the Sussex coast, Lewes defeated Step 4 Town 2-1 to make the last eight.

Two goals of varying difficulty from Bassett laid the foundations for the win, with the Rooks then withstanding Town’s pressure after a Sonny Walsh strike to make progress.

While it was not the most fluent outing for Lewes, several performances on an evening of experimentation will give reason for optimism.

The Rooks arrived at The Saffrons with six changes to their starting XI, including Josh Spinks’ return as captain and a debut for new attacking signing Kwesi Ntim.

Ntim, who has scored 20 goals in 33 games for Lingfield in 2026, was a bright spark early on, cutting in from the left flank and posing problems for the home defence.

Genuine chances took a while to arrive on the south coast, with the opening stages dominated by safe possession football.

After 25 minutes, however, Lewes created their first threat as Bassett worked space on the right and forced goalkeeper Chris Winterton to punch a cross into the path of Brian Garzon, whose right-footed effort was cleared off the line.

The Rooks slowly built the pressure from here, with Tyler Christian-Law and Mackenzie Richardson driving at goal before the opener soon arrived.

In the 37th minute, Ntim added a flash of brilliance with a pacey drive in from the left, setting up Richardson for a first-time effort at goal. With ex-Rooks ‘keeper Winterton spilling the ball, Bassett was on hand to tap home.

Richardson had another bite at a goal of his own before the break, but Winterton stood up well to a rising effort from the left of the box to keep the score at 0-1.

Lewes did not hesitate in doubling down on their advantage at the start of the second half, and Bassett was the man to deliver again.

Barely 30 seconds into the half, the forward stormed down the right and cut into the box before producing an audacious chip on Winterton that dropped into the top left corner.

It was a moment of magic, and whether Bassett had meant it or not, nobody could begrudge the striker his fourth goal of the season thus far.

The Rooks got into a rhythm after this, regularly wriggling free of Town's attentions and finding space on the counter without truly putting the game to bed.

Bassett and Ntim were similarly denied in the area, while Garzon had at least two notable chances while cutting inside that flew inches wide of the post.

All the while, Town were never out of the tie, and they got their spark in the 61st minute.

Sonny Walsh was set free on the right of the box and blasted an effort past Louis Rogers to halve the deficit.

Town had two brilliant chances to level the scores in the final 30 minutes, but as has been their story in the last few weeks, luck was against them.

A ball in from the right was stabbed over the crossbar from close range, and in the dying stages, Walsh was again set free on the right but blasted over the target.

Equally, Lewes could have added to their advantage as substitute Omar Al-Seadi prodded wide of the left post when excellently teed up by a combination of Bassett and Kurtis Cumberbatch.

Altogether, Lewes were relieved to hear the final whistle as they made it to the final eight, but were good value for the result across the 90 minutes.

At the time of writing, the timing of the draw for the Quarter-Finals, and the dates of this round, are yet to be announced.

We anticipate news of both in due course, and urge supporters to keep tabs on our social media pages for the latest updates.

This Saturday, the Rooks are back in Isthmian Premier Division action away at Ramsgate FC.

With Lewes in 5th and Ramsgate - with a game in hand - just three points behind in 6th, it has all the makings of an excellent game at Southwood Stadium. Kick-off is at 3pm.

Lewes FC: Rogers; Mundele, Spinks (C), Hamstead, Cox (Allen, 69’); Christian-Law, Garzon, Richardson (Unwin, 76’); Fisher (Cumberbatch, 85’), Bassett (Mahmoud, 90’), Ntim (Al-Seadi, 69’)
