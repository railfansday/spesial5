Little Common boss takes heart despite defeat at Eastbourne United

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Following their narrow defeat to high-flying Roffey the previous weekend, Common once again demonstrated their resilience and fighting spirit, giving as good as they got against a strong United side.

The visitors defended heroically for long periods of the opening half whilst also posing a threat at the other end, creating several opportunities of their own.

Goalkeeper Matt Cruttwell continued his impressive recent form, producing two smart early saves to deny the hosts and keep the scores level. At the other end, Ollie Hull came close to breaking the deadlock following a superb mazy run, weaving his way through the home defence before firing narrowly wide of the target.

Sam Ellis then went even closer to giving Common the lead when he cut inside from the left and unleashed a goal-bound effort, only to be denied by an excellent save from the United goalkeeper.

Despite Common's encouraging display, it was the hosts who struck first in the 36th minute. Jorge Nones found himself in space on the edge of the penalty area and slid the ball home to give United a slender advantage heading into the interval.

The home side began the second half brightly, firing an early opportunity over the crossbar as they looked to extend their lead. There was then a moment of controversy when Freddie Warren appeared to be brought down whilst racing through on goal, but despite Common's appeals, the referee waved play on.

With the visitors continuing to battle for an equaliser, Cruttwell was once again called into action, producing another excellent save to deny James Stone and keep Common firmly in the contest.

As the game entered its closing stages, Common continued to push forward in search of an equaliser, but their hopes of taking something from the afternoon were finally extinguished two minutes from time.

Substitute James Hull raced through on goal and calmly slotted the ball past Cruttwell to double United's advantage and secure all three points for the hosts.

Common boss Russ Eldridge said: “I thought the lads were excellent again on Saturday. We've now played two of the best sides in the division in successive weeks and competed really well against both.

“We knew Eastbourne would have plenty of the ball, but we defended brilliantly at times and created some very good opportunities ourselves. On another day, we could easily have gone in front, and I think that would have changed the game.

“I was disappointed with the decision when Freddie went through in the second half. From where I was standing, it looked a clear foul, and those are the decisions you need to go your way in tight games like this.

“Matt has been outstanding for us recently, but I thought everyone put in a tremendous shift. The second goal came when we were pushing to get something from the game, which was frustrating.

“The performances are certainly improving, and the lads deserve enormous credit for their attitude and commitment. However, we can't keep talking about playing well and coming away with nothing. We need to start turning these performances into points, and hopefully that will happen sooner rather than later.”
