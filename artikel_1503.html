‘A team we all respect’ – Jota Silva and Pirola preview Milan test for Olympiacos

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Olympiacos duo Jota Silva and Lorenzo Pirola are looking forward to welcoming AC Milan to the Karaiskakis Stadium this evening.

Milan travel to the Greek capital hoping to get their Europa League campaign up and running. They have won just one of their opening four league phase fixtures, which was a 4-0 drubbing of RB Salzburg.

Defeats to Benfica, Bournemouth and Ferencvaros – all without scoring a single goal – have seen the Rossoneri slide into the bottom 12 positions in the table. With only 12 points up for grabs, they must start to put things right. By contrast, Olympiacos have won three of four, and sit sixth.

Jota Silva spoke to Gianluca Di Marzio about tonight’s game in Athens, admitting that the Rossoneri are a team that they respect despite their slow start in Europe. His words are via MilanNews.

“It will be a great match, between two teams that usually play in the Champions League. Milan are a well-equipped opponent with an extraordinary history: they are a team we all respect. We are two historic and important teams: they, like us, are having a good season.”

They only have three points in the Europa League…

“They will certainly want to fight to advance to the next round of the European competition. They have players of excellent quality, including many internationals and many highly experienced players. Amorim is a fantastic coach and needs no introduction.”

Meanwhile, defender Lorenzo Pirola spoke alongside his head coach Imanol Alguacil at a press conference on the eve of the game.

What’s your message for the fans, who will once again fill the stadium?

“As with every match, we promise to give it our all this time. We face a tough opponent. It’s another important match, and we have a very significant evening ahead of us. We want to finish the game with a win and get the three points.”

Having come through Inter’s youth academy, do you have any extra motivation for facing Milan?

“In any case, I spent four years at Inter, and it was a very wonderful and important experience. It’s a great experience to be able to play against Milan, but for me the most important thing is the three points from the win. That’s my goal.”

Does the fact that this is a crucial match for Milan in terms of the league position provide additional motivation for Olympiacos to remain focused?

“It’s an important match for both Milan and Olympiacos, even though we have ten points. We’ll do everything we can to get the points we need to win and reach thirteen. We’ll give our all to achieve that.”

Could this match also be a calling card for you for the Italian national team? Also, how much have Milan changed since you faced them with Inter?

“Considering that I’ve been here for almost three years and that my career is going well, I don’t see the match against Milan as a positive or negative opportunity for the national team.

“Milan are always a dangerous team and has a good squad. Anything can happen in a match, and as I’ve said before, we’ll do everything we can to win.”

What do you expect from Milan in tomorrow’s match, and how do you see them competing for the title?

“It will be a tough and intense match, with both teams pressing hard. I think it will be a very close game, and we’ll try to give our best. As for the league, everything is still open. We’re only at the start of the season, so I can’t give a precise assessment right now.”
