CELTIC RATINGS: Star turns emerge from the bench to save the day but how many of Martin O'Neill's starting XI earned pass marks? Very few...

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Published: 18:29 EDT, 5 November 2026 | Updated: 18:30 EDT, 5 November 2026

Substitutes Callum Osmand and Camilo Duran sparked Celtic into life as the Scottish champions got off the mark in the Europa League in a 3-2 victory over Besiktas.

Martin O’Neill’s side were heading for a seventh defeat in 11 matches and bottom place when Junior Olaitan netted on the rebound after Sam Johnstone had saved his 54th-minute penalty.

But Osmand drilled home the equaliser with his first touch in the 62nd minute, seconds after coming off the bench in a triple change, and the 20-year-old set up Duran to put the hosts in front.

Besiktas sub Orkun Kokcu equalised from long range but Celtic went back in front within 60 seconds when Duran stabbed home an 87th-minute winner from six yards.

The win hands Celtic a fighting chance of progressing, with Portuguese second-tier side Torreense as their next opponents. But who caught the eye last night and who flattered to deceive on what turned into a long-overdue glory night at Celtic Park?

Sebastian Tounekti competes with Emirhan Topcu but in truth he had another shocker overall
