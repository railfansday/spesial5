Man United: Yoro’s terrible mistake against Bournemouth

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Starting in the Manchester United defense this Sunday at Old Trafford, Leny Yoro completely missed his intervention by sliding on the left side of his area, leaving Bournemouth to take advantage of the offering. Truffert immediately recovered the ball before serving Tavernier, completely alone against Lammens.

The Bournemouth winger did not tremble to adjust the Belgian goalkeeper on his left and allow the Cherries to make the break. A big individual error for Yoro, who weighs down the Red Devils a little more in an already complicated period. Manchester United is sinking into crisis and its young defender risks thinking about this slide for a long time.
