Foxconn posted profit up 34%, beating expectations on AI server demand

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Foxconn expects AI rack shipments to grow by a high double-digit rate in 2027, while the chairman flagged tight chip and component supply

Foxconn, the world’s largest contract electronics maker, reported a 34% rise in third-quarter net profit to NT$77.52 billion ($2.36 billion) on Thursday as demand for AI servers stayed strong.

The result beat an LSEG consensus estimate of NT$69.6 billion, according to Reuters. Revenue for the quarter reached NT$3.016 trillion, and cloud and networking products made up more than half of it.

Foxconn kept its forecast of strong revenue growth for this year. The company projected that shipments of its AI server racks would climb by a high double-digit percentage in 2027, helped by a growing roster of server clients and a wider global production network.

"We remain very confident about our operations next year and expect continued growth," Chairman Young Liu said at an earnings conference, according to Reuters. Liu said he expects capital spending on AI by cloud service providers around the world to keep climbing next year. He said cloud and networking products should account for more than 60% of revenue within three to five years.

Liu also pointed to risks. Semiconductors, memory and certain critical parts are in short supply throughout AI and consumer-electronics sectors, he said. He said new production capacity should progressively relieve the crunch, though he expects demand to outpace available supply next year. Liu said he takes a guarded view of prospects for the PC segment.

Liu described the geopolitical and economic backdrop worldwide as highly uncertain, adding that movements in inflation and currency rates could weigh on operating costs. He said the U.S. midterm elections are unlikely to move the industry much in the near term, though their longer-run consequences deserve watching.

Foxconn stock slipped 0.8% at Thursday's close, before the results were published, while Taiwan's benchmark stock index dropped 2.6%. According to The Wall Street Journal, the shares are down roughly 20% from their June high.

Foxconn builds AI servers for Nvidia $NVDA and is erecting plants in Mexico and Texas to produce them, according to Reuters.
