Nintendo missed on earnings as Switch 2 sales dropped sharply amid price hikes

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nintendo kept its forecast of 16.5 million Switch 2 units for the year ending March 2027 after selling 6.49 million in six months

Nintendo missed analyst estimates for fiscal second-quarter revenue and net profit as Switch 2 unit sales fell 41% from the year-ago period, according to CNBC.

Nintendo sold 2.67 million Switch 2 units in the three months ended Sept. 30, down from 3.82 million in the prior quarter. Revenue for the quarter was 448.2 billion yen, and net profit was 73.5 billion yen. Analysts had expected 481.37 billion yen in revenue and 82.16 billion yen in net profit, according to CNBC.

Operating profit for the quarter fell 16% to 73.9 billion yen as sales declined 15%. Analysts had projected no change in operating profit compared with a year earlier, according to Bloomberg.

Nintendo raised Switch 2 prices in the U.S., Canada and Japan during the quarter, according to CNBC. The U.S. price rose $50 to $499.99 on Sept. 1, according to Bloomberg.

For the six months ended Sept. 30, Switch 2 hardware sales fell 37.4% to 6.49 million units, while Switch 2 software sales rose 18.8% to 24.49 million units. Net sales fell 12.1% to 966.0 billion yen. Operating profit rose 49.1% to 216.5 billion yen, and net profit rose 11.0% to 220.9 billion yen.

The half-year profit figures include about $300 million that Nintendo recorded as a reduction of cost of sales in the first quarter, tied to refunds of tariffs imposed under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act. The company said it had borne most of those tariff costs rather than passing them on to consumers through product prices.

Nintendo left its full-year forecast unchanged from the one announced May 8. It expects net sales of 2.05 trillion yen, operating profit of 370.0 billion yen, net profit of 310.0 billion yen and Switch 2 hardware sales of 16.50 million units. The company said the forecast includes an impact of about 100.0 billion yen from rising component prices, including memory, and tariff measures. It set an interim dividend of 76 yen, up from 42 yen a year earlier.

Nintendo topped revenue and profit estimates in the first quarter, even as Switch 2 hardware sales fell 34.4% to 3.82 million units. Operating profit in that quarter climbed 150.5% to 142.5 billion yen, helped by the tariff refunds.

Nintendo released “The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time” on Thursday. Its launch schedule also lists two Pokémon games, “Pokémon Winds” and “Pokémon Waves,” for 2027.
