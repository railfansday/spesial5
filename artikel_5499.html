X-Sense XR0A-iR Makes Smart Radon Monitoring Accessible Through Lowe's U.S. Stores

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

NEW YORK, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- X-Sense's XR0A-iR Wi-Fi Smart Radon Detector is available at Lowe's stores across the United States. With an MSRP of $99.99, it offers a reusable solution for continuous home radon monitoring.

Its retail presence includes locations in Pennsylvania, Colorado and Iowa, extending access in states with significant radon concerns. Pennsylvania reports elevated radon in approximately 40% of tested homes, while Iowa is classified entirely within EPA's highest radon-potential zone.(Pennsylvania DEP, Iowa HHS)

Radon is an invisible, odorless radioactive gas that can enter homes from the ground. EPA estimates that it causes approximately 21,000 U.S. lung cancer deaths annually and is the leading cause of lung cancer among nonsmokers. (EPA)

For routine monitoring, the XR0A-iR reduces reliance on repeated paid professional visits and eliminates mailing samples for each check, saving scheduling time and recurring testing expenses. Unlike single-use mail-in kits, it provides ongoing readings and trends without laboratory processing.
