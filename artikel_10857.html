Razer x Durex collab gives gamers “Long-Lasting Battle Armor” condoms and a mouse pad

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Razer and Durex have teamed up for a China-exclusive gaming gift set containing two limited-edition condoms and a matching Razer mouse pad, prompting a wave of jokes and questions from players online.

Razer is best known for its gaming mice, keyboards, headsets, and other PC accessories, while Durex is one of the world’s biggest condom brands. The unlikely partnership brings the two together around the phrase “Work hard, game harder,” with the campaign encouraging customers to enjoy a “long-lasting round.”

The collaboration was announced by Razer on X on November 24. The post, which calls the release a “China exclusive,” attracted more than 78,000 views and hundreds of likes within hours. Gamers quickly began making jokes about the unexpected pairing and asking what the gift set included.

Work hard, game harder – Razer x Durex (China exclusive) pic.twitter.com/3iPBFRQGyF

The promotional images show a black-and-green Razer Heavy Armored Beetle mouse pad alongside two Durex Long-Lasting Battle Armor condoms. The packaging uses the brands’ familiar colors and logos, with the campaign slogan presented as “For gaming, bring Razer; for romance, bring Durex.”

The announcement immediately drew attention because of how far apart the two product categories are. Reactions centered on the collaboration’s double meaning, with gamers joking about the idea of preparing for a gaming round and a romantic one with the same bundle. Others focused on the mouse pad, which appears to be a custom version of Razer’s Heavy Armored Beetle design.

The promotion is also being treated as a limited gift rather than a normal product line. Razer and Durex have not announced a US or global release, and the announcement does not list a standalone price for the set.
