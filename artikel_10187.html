No, the Pixel Tag is not available today

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Our first impressions on Google’s new Pixel Tag are live, but orders are not. There appears to be some confusion running around the web, but the Pixel Tag is not available for purchase today.

Announced in August, the Pixel Tag is the first Find Hub tracker directly from Google, and it’s been worth the wait. In our initial review earlier today, we found it to be a solid option with polish not found on third-party alternatives. It doesn’t magically make Find Hub a perfect tracking network, but it’s as good as it gets, at least based on our brief experience so far.

But the Pixel Tag isn’t available to buy just yet.

Rather, the official Pixel Tag release date is November 11. As it stands today, the Google Store is not accepting orders, nor are any third-party retailers that you may have seen linked in posts around Pixel Tag availability today. Pricing is still set for $29.99 for a 1-pack and $99.99 for a 4-pack.

Google explicitly said in a blog post just this morning:

Pixel Tag offers peace of mind so you always know where your most important things are — and it’s available November 11.

We’re eager to get our hands on more of these trackers too, but we’ve got just a bit longer to wait.
