Geminus Secures Investment from Bapco Energies Through BeVentures, Signs MoU

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

MoU signed to advance digital innovation across the Kingdom's energy sector

CAMBRIDGE, Mass., Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Geminus, the physics-native AI decision layer for mission-critical systems, announced a strategic investment from Bapco Energies, through its corporate venture arm BeVentures. As part of the partnership, the companies signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to explore the development of a digital platform spanning Bapco Energies' operations.

The MoU further expands the existing relationship between Bapco Energies and Geminus, which focuses on the application of advanced AI technologies within the Kingdom of Bahrain's upstream operations.

The transaction establishes BeVentures as the first strategic investor in Geminus from the oil and gas sector in the region, reinforcing Bapco Energies' commitment to enabling a more intelligent, efficient, and integrated energy ecosystem through technology-led innovation.

Building on this foundation, the three-year initiative will explore the implementation of a unified digital twin that brings together key components of the energy value chain into a single platform. By integrating operational data, asset intelligence, and advanced AI capabilities into a single platform, the initiative aims to enhance scenario planning, improve system-wide visibility, strengthen coordination, and enable more informed decision-making across the Group.

Mark Thomas, Group Chief Executive Officer of Bapco Energies, said: "The Kingdom of Bahrain's energy sector has always evolved in response to changing industry needs and global developments. As we look to the future, maintaining that progress will require continued investment in technology, talent, and new capabilities. Through such collaborations, we are not simply adopting new tools; we are creating the foundations for a smarter, more integrated system that supports the Kingdom's long-term energy priorities and sustainable economic growth for generations to come."

Areije Al Shakar, Chief Executive Officer of BeVentures, said: "At BeVentures, we are focused on connecting innovation with real-world opportunities. This collaboration with Geminus provides a platform to explore how emerging technologies can be applied in an industrial setting, accelerating digital transformation and supporting the adoption of solutions that can deliver meaningful impact across the energy industry."

Greg Fallon, Chief Executive Officer of Geminus, said: "National energy systems are usually run as a collection of separate assets, and the real value sits in the connections between them. Bapco Energies is building one live, physics-grounded view of its entire gas and power system, owned in Bahrain and run by Bahraini engineers. That is what national anchored AI can look like when it is applied to physical infrastructure, and we are excited to have Bapco Energies set the model for national energy companies everywhere."

The collaboration will also create a framework for testing and scaling state-of-the-art solutions across Bapco Energies' operations, enabling the Group to unlock additional value from its assets and systems.

Geminus is the AI orchestration layer for intelligent twins of mission-critical systems. Grounded in physics and trusted by many of the world's largest energy companies, the platform helps operators simulate, predict, and optimize complex physical assets in real time, delivering double-digit performance gains. For more information, visit geminus.ai.
