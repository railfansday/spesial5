Samsung’s Galaxy Watch 9 includes a free $50 Amazon gift card ahead of Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Smartwatches can practically feel like a must-have these days, especially for tracking your health and fitness while on the move. Just as with most gadgets, though, prices just keep rising thanks to the ongoing RAM crunch. This Black Friday, you can pick up Samsung’s Galaxy Watch 9 in either size while getting a $50 gift card back to put towards the rest of the holiday season.

Discounts on Samsung’s latest mainline wearable series have been all over the place — up to and including prices that may or may not appear for you on the brand’s official storefront. Luckily, this Amazon deal is pretty straightforward. Pick your watch sized, color, and whether you want a cellular model, and before you add to cart, you’ll have the option to switch to a bundled variant with a free $50 Amazon gift card that, at least for me, arrives physically in the box with your watch. Remember, with that free gift card, you can effectively take an additional $50 off your final purchase price, bringing the total cost as low as just $329 before tax.

In my eyes, the best deal here is on Samsung’s Bluetooth 44mm wearable. That’s the size we don’t see discounts on as frequently, and should keep you covered with long-lasting battery life, a big, bright display, and reliable activity tracking for years to come. While it’s possible Samsung’s website adds more sales as we get closer to the end of the week, if you’re a frequent Amazon shopper — and these days, who isn’t? — this is a great deal on a really excellent Wear OS-powered watch.

But hey, if you prefer Google’s Pixel Watch experience, don’t forget its latest wearable is down to just $300 for the 41mm model and $330 for the 45mm. If you’re already living in the Pixel ecosystem, it might be the better buy this holiday season, even without a bundled gift card.
