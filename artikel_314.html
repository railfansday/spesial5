David Walliams, UK Celebrity, Is Accused of Sexual Misconduct

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Guardian and ITV News published claims from multiple women against the comedian and writer, who fought a weekslong court battle to keep the claims private. He denies the accusations.

David Walliams, a British TV personality and best-selling children’s author, was publicly accused on Tuesday of exploiting his celebrity status to prey on women and teenagers as young as 15 after a weekslong court battle to keep the accusations private.

The allegations emerged after a judge decided on Nov. 3 that The Guardian newspaper and ITV News, a British broadcaster, could publish the claims against Mr. Walliams.

Mr. Walliams denies the allegations. In a letter sent to news outlets, he said he accepted he had “behaved thoughtlessly towards other people I had casual sexual relationships with,” but he added: “I am not a sexual predator. I have not broken the law.”

The news organizations had been held back from publishing their joint investigation because of a temporary injunction, an English legal measure that a judge can impose to bar the identification of an individual. The secrecy had driven a swirl of online speculation in recent weeks about the identity of the unnamed celebrity.

Mr. Walliams is a household name in the United Kingdom who rose to fame in the 2000s as a star of “Little Britain,” a popular BBC sketch show that poked fun at British archetypes, and he later became best known for his children’s novels. In recent years, he has been dogged by accusations over his professional conduct, including reports that he had made derogatory and sexually explicit remarks about contestants during the recording of an episode of the television show “Britain’s Got Talent,” for which he was a judge.

The investigation that The Guardian and ITV News published on Tuesday, which the two organizations had worked on since April 2023, was based on accounts from 30 people who accused Mr. Walliams of abusing his power to sexually prey on women and targeting teenagers, some of whom met him when they were as young as 15.
