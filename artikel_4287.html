Scalpers Already Want Insane Sums For The Ocarina Remake With Exclusive Bits

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

There's nothing new with secondhand sellers trying their luck to make a big profit from day one. It doesn't help when special editions like the Fire Emblem: Dagdan Collection sell out almost instantly, either. The FOMO is real and scalpers gonna scalp.

However, it seems that sellers on eBay UK are taking things to fresh extremes with the Switch 2 Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake, with some Buy it Now listings for the foil cover version plus a couple of exclusive extras listed for over £700.

As you'll know if you read Jim's excellent feature on the London midnight launch, 500 copies of the game were available to buy on Wednesday night, and Nintendo threw in a cardboard replica of the original PAL N64 box and a little Ocarina keyring for anyone willing to queue on Tottenham Court Road on a chilly autumn night.

And as you'd expect, some copies from this midnight event soon found their way onto eBay. But even knowing how these things go, the asking prices are shocking.

Feature "I Will Have Been Here For 30 Hours" - London's Midnight Zelda Launch Was Wild

£646.77. £700.17. £513.91. £717.25. £696.62... and it goes on. If you're happy to sacrifice the Ocarina keyring, one seller in France has the game and the replica box for a thoroughly reasonable £321.62 + £54.22 postage. Another has just the box and the keychain — not even the game — listed for £479.89 or Best Offer. Hmm, might a tenner get a bite?

For context, the sold-out Zelda-themed Switch 2 console (which is £434.99 retail price) is listed for less than some of these scandalous Buy-it-Now punts.

Obviously, these sellers are hoping to snare a Jabu-Jabu who simply cannot live without a slightly disappointing facsimile of an old N64 box. Likewise, the foil cover version of the remake is notionally "limited", but we've yet to see the standard variant amongst NL staff's copies, and some of us just wandered into a supermarket on launch day.

Similar box-based bonuses were available at retailers in other territories, so this familiar pattern of speculative auction listings will crop up everywhere.

As always, if you're desperate to own any of these cute bonuses, you do you. Unsightly corrugated pattern aside, the box is a neat little nod to the original game, and who doesn't like a cute keyring? But having procured a couple of these "RARE" boxes, we can confirm they are almost certainly not worth 700 of your quids.

Consider this: You could get a PAL copy of the original game in decent condition, plus the remake and any number of alternative Ocarina keyrings, for far, far less.
