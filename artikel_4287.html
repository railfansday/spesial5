Nintendo Celebrates Zelda's 40th Anniversary With An Exclusive Pin Set (US)

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Timed in with the launch of Ocarina of Time on the Switch 2 this week, Nintendo has released a Zelda 40th Anniversary Pin Set.

It's currently available to Nintendo store users in the US and will set you back 800 Platinum Points. It comes with the series' 40th anniversary logo, and the other pin is based on the new Ocarina of Time remake.

Here's the official description, along with a look:

Mark the 40th anniversary of The Legend of Zelda™ series with this two-pin set.

One pin features the 40th-anniversary logo, while the other features the logo for the Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time game. Add them to your collection or pin them on your backpack before you head off on your next adventure!

Pin sizes: 2" W × 1.5" H and 1.25" W × 2" H Product size (packaging): 6" W × 4" H

This item is limited to one order per account, and as usual, shipping fees apply. So grab it now, if you're interested!
