2027 Toyota 4Runner Starts over $45,000 for the First Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Every 4Runner now costs more than last year, but the base-level SR5’s $1220 price hike is the steepest.

The Toyota 4Runner’s starting price will clear $45,000 for the first time ever. That comes after the base-level SR5 trim receives a $1220 price increase, bringing the least expensive 4Runner up to $45,085. While every trim has increased in price, the SR5’s rise is the most substantial.

To help offset the upcharge, the base model now features standard heated front seats. The driver and front passenger seats also get standard eight-way power adjustments. Previously, heated seats were standard on the next-step TRD Sport trim, which started at $50,345 and featured six-way manual buckets. However, if you’re someone who muscles through long, cold winters and enjoys a heated backside, the $1220 price hike might feel like a discount when stacked against last year’s options.

For the 2027 model year, the TRD Pro adds standard red-painted front tow hooks and an exclusive Hot Honey color, which is available for an extra $695. The Platinum trim now features standard puddle lamps that project the 4Runner logo on the ground when the front doors are opened. Also, the Black exterior color has been renamed “Inked” on all trim levels. Fancy stuff.

Beyond those tweaks, several trims roll into 2027 unchanged. However, prices are up across the board. Most trims have increased by about $200, but the hybrid version of the Platinum sees a steeper climb, costing $490 more than last year. The TRD Sport, TRD Off-Road, and hybrid TRD Off-Road have all increased by $870.

Four-wheel drive still comes standard on most trims, but it’s available for an extra $2000 on the SR5, TRD Sport, TRD Sport Premium, and Limited trims, just like last year. All 4Runners use a 278-hp turbocharged 2.4-liter four-cylinder with an eight-speed automatic transmission, and hybrid models add an electric motor to make 326 horsepower.

Every 4Runner is equipped with Toyota Safety Sense 3.0, which includes a host of driver assists such as lane-departure warning, adaptive cruise control, pedestrian detection, and road-sign assist. The base 2027 4Runner SR5 starts at $45,085, while hybrid models start at $55,945. New 4Runners are available to order now, with vehicles slated to reach dealerships toward the end of this year.
