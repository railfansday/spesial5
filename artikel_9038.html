There are several big advantages of a handheld PC over a beefy gaming PC, as well as in comparison with more family-friendly consoles like the Nintendo Switch 2.

Despite what my headline says, it isn't necessarily about which of these consoles is the "best", as they're catered to 2 entirely different demographics.

I had never considered a handheld PC prior to getting the Atlas, given that I'm more of a cosy gamer. Animal Crossing, Pokopia, and Disney Dreamlight Valley have always been my comfort games, but knowing that I can play juicier titles like Jurassic World Evolution 3, Marvel Rivals, and even a bit of Death Stranding from the comfort of my sofa if I fancy is a luxury you can't put a price on.

I've had my PlayStation 5 for what feels like a decade now, but it's become something of a paperweight recently since my desk has been overtaken by work priorities and filming gear. While I did consider the PlayStation Portal, the reviews weren't really selling it for me.

Having a powerful handheld PC that I can pick up and move with me on the go means clutter doesn't limit my gaming time anymore. I can pick it up and put it down whenever without relying on a large monitor or being tied to a chair.

For portability, comfort, power, performance, and ease of use (no building your own PC from scratch), this console is five stars and worth every penny in my opinion. Sure, you can get rivals like the ASUS ROG Ally or Lenovo Legion Go for a lot less, but the Intel Arc G3 processor alone is revolutionary and currently the strongest PC gaming handheld silicon available.
