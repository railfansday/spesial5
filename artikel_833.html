Hunger Games’ Mckenna Grace, Joseph Zada React to Fans Shipping Them

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Sunrise on the Reaping stars Mckenna Grace and Joseph Zada shared their candid reaction to fans shipping them as a couple in real life: “It’s pretty weird.”

And if fans burn, they want the cast of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping to burn with them.

Stars Joseph Zada and Mckenna Grace, however, are willing to take it all in stride. And by “it,” we mean fans taking their onscreen bond as Haymitch Abernathy and Maysilee Donner and hoping it becomes romantic IRL.

In reality, the concept made the pair laugh aloud when they were asked about being shipped in an interview.

“Yeah, it’s pretty weird,” Zada said with a smile to Refinery29 Australia, pointing out, “We’ve both got partners.”

He added that Grace feels like his “younger, younger sister,” which made the 20-year-old fire back at her costar, who is only a year older, “Younger? Definitely not.”

But while Zada and Grace may not have volunteered as ship tributes, Grace—who has long had her finger on the pulse of Gen Z internet trends—knows its par for the course in such a big franchise.

“Joe’s like family,” the Gifted star—who is dating her Regretting You costar Mason Thames—said. “I think that’s like the fun of the internet, is that they can ship whatever characters or people or anything that they want as long it’s not intruding on people’s own lives.”

Plus, Zada and Grace were willing to be more than just a piece in fans’ games, offering up alternative ships featuring their costars Ben Wang (who plays District 12 tribute Wyatt Callow), Laura Marcus (District 1 tribute Silka Sharp) and Whitney Peak (Haymitch’s love Lenore Dove).

As Zada noted, “I’ve been shipped with Ben, and like I can see that,” to which Grace added, “Honestly, my favorite ship is Ben and Joe.”

And when Zada suggested Marcus and Grace could also be a good ship, she offered up a challenge to her costar.

“I like Maydove,” she said, referring to her and Peak’s characters Maysilee and Lenore. “I’m coming for your girl.”

But not only does Grace have decades in the spotlight preparing her for this moment, but she also received advice from the boy with the bread, Josh Hutcherson, who played Peeta Mellark in the original movies and whom Grace starred alongside in Five Nights at Freddy’s 2.

As she revealed, Hutcherson was a wonderful sounding board as she prepared herself to step into the Hunger Games fandom.

“I just talk to Josh a lot because we were on that press tour together and spent a lot of time together,” she told E! News at the Coach Fall 2026 show on Feb. 11. “I spent a lot of time asking him, ‘Was your arena experience as intense as ours was?’ And he was like, ‘Oh yeah, yeah it was.’”

But while Zada and Grace have not dated, nor are they ever likely to, they’re not the only costars who have found themselves the subject of dating speculation. Read on for more costars whose relationships were strictly platonic—despite fans’ hopes to the contrary.

The Anyone But You costars may have found love onscreen, but that's where it stayed. Despite the onslaught of dating rumors amid their 2023 press tour, the pair emphasized that their relationship was strictly platonic.

"The two things that you have to sell a rom-com are fun and chemistry," Glen told the New York Times in April 2024. "Sydney and I have a ton of fun together, and we have a ton of effortless chemistry. That's people wanting what's on the screen off the screen, and sometimes you just have to lean into it a bit—and it worked wonderfully. Sydney is very smart."

Meanwhile, Sydney—who was engaged to businessman Jonathan Davino at the time, though they split in March 2025 after seven years together—asserted that she and Glen didn't play up their chemistry ahead of the rom-com's release.

"Honestly, the press did it themselves. There was no leaning in, per se," she told Variety in October 2025. "Truly, the tabloids and journalists just created it and kept going."

"Glen and I genuinely care for each other and have a love and respect for one another," she emphasized. "I love being with him. And I think people just truly saw a really beautiful pairing."
