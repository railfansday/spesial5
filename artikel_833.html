Janet Jackson Makes Rare Surprise Appearance on DWTS for Jenna Dewan

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

During Dancing With the Stars' Grammys Night episode on Nov. 10, Janet Jackson made an ultra rare appearance in a video message for her former backup dancer Jenna Dewan.

Janet Jackson is all for Jenna Dewan on Dancing With the Stars.

The "Rhythm Nation" singer surprised the Step Up star with a sweet video message during the dancing competition’s Grammys Night episode on Nov. 10, telling Dewan—who was Jackson's backup dancer in her "Doesn't Really Matter" and "All for You" music videos—that she was "so excited" about the routine.

"I cannot wait to watch you perform tonight," Jackson began. "You're performing to 'All for You.' I feel so honored all for you."

The 60-year-old, who won a Best Dance Recording Grammy for the 2001 smash hit, went on to reminisce about their time working together.

"Weren't those such fun times we had back then during those days for the All for You Tour?" she continued. "I miss you. I love you."

Assuring Dewan, 45, that she and pro dance partner Val Chmerkovskiy will do an "incredible job" with their jazz routine, Jackson added, "Let's get together when I'm back in L.A."

Dewan was visibly moved by the Diff'rent Strokes alum's kind words, wiping tears from her eyes. In fact, the Rookie actress considers Jackson as "the start of my dance career."

"Putting Grammys and Janet Jackson together, this is a full circle moment," she shared in a video package shown before her routine, which ultimately received a total score of 28 out of 30 from judges Carrie Ann Inaba, Derek Hough and Bruno Tonioli. "It makes me really, really happy and emotional that I get this opportunity, so this dance cannot be anything but perfect."

And while Jackson has stepped away from the spotlight in recent years to focus on raising her 9-year-old son Eissa Al Mana, who she shares with ex Wissam Al Mana, the pop star is always happy to connect with fans.

“You never get tired of it,” Jackson told Today in 2022. “It’s part of why you do what you do—hoping that you could touch someone in a positive way. Making a difference in someone’s—even if it’s just one person’s life—you’ve accomplished. You’ve succeeded."
