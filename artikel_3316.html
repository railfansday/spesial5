However, if you're looking for great Black Friday mattress deals on more premium mattresses, we've rounded up our top king sizes from the Casper, Tempur-Pedic, and Purple mattress sales — here's how these four king beds compare by value, body type, and sleep need.

We've had the privilege to test all four mattresses, and here's what we think of each one in terms of value for money, sleep needs, and support for different body types...

If saving money is your top priority when looking for a king mattress, then the Nectar Classic is our top recommendation. At $899 for a king mattress, the mid-range Nectar Classic is the cheapest option of the four and one of the best mattresses under $1,000. Plus, it comes with unbeatable benefits, including a year-long sleep trial and a lifetime warranty.

Another good mid-range choice is the Casper One, which is under $1,150. However, the benefits (including a 10-year warranty and a 100-night sleep trial) are decent but average. The Purple Plus and the Tempur-Adapt, meanwhile, are in the same price tier as the best luxury mattresses.

While Nectar doesn't provide a specific weight limit, they claim that its beds are engineered to accommodate various body types. Likewise, Tempur-Pedic says its mattresses do not have a weight limit either. According to the brand, Casper mattresses "are designed to support all shapes and sizes."

As for Purple mattresses' weight capacity, its king size beds can support at least 500 pounds and are tested to support 700 pounds of total weight, making them suitable for a wide range of body weights.

While none of the mattresses above are designed specifically for heavier bodies, there are plenty of mattresses designed for plus-sized sleepers. For instance, the WinkBed Plus is built to support sleepers and couples who weigh over 250lbs.

As all our top picks here are made entirely of foam layers, each one displayed excellent motion isolation in our tests, which means you won't feel your partner's wriggling travelling over to your side of the bed. As for temperature regulation, our testers found that the Purple Plus's ventilated design sleeps incredibly cool if you or your partner tend to overheat.

If you find yourself sleeping close to the edge of your bed because of a partner who likes to stretch out and hog most of the mattress, then the Nectar Classic is a great option as it showcased strong edge support in our at-home and lab-based tests. Its edge are sturdy and won't make you feel as if you're going to roll out of bed when sleeping near the sides.

In terms of comfort, the Nectar Classic and the Casper One offer firm support to back and stomach sleepers, while the Tempur-Adapt is best for back sleeping. As for side sleepers who need plusher pressure relief, the Purple Plus offers both support for back and front sleepers and body-contouring comfort for side sleepers.
