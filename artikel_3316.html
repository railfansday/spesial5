Watch Goo Goo Dolls’ Complete NFL Halftime Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It’s a Black Friday miracle. After NBC angered fans by not showing the entire halftime performance by the Goo Goo Dolls during the Kansas City Chiefs and Buffalo Bills game on Thursday night, the Bills dropped the band’s complete set online.

For their 10-plus-minute performance at Highmark Stadium in Buffalo, the Goo Goo Dolls played “Iris,” “Broadway,” and “Slide.” Instead of airing the entire thing, NBC opted to let Mike Tomlin and Mike Tirico chew the fat about Aaron Rodgers, much to the annoyance of many viewers.

Fans in attendance at the game praised the rainy performance, with some going so far as to call it “one of the greatest shows ever.” NBC ended up running only about 30 seconds of the band’s set on their broadcast. One fan wrote, “Aaron Rodgers ruins one last thing as NBC airs Tomlin interview instead of Goo Goo Dolls concert.”

Steven Van Zandt wrote on X, “They are a great band. That was badly handled and disrespectful.”

Goo Goo Dolls were one of several artists performing across NFL halftime shows on Thanksgiving. Earlier in the evening, Wu-Tang Clan took the stage in Detroit at Ford Field during the Chicago Bears match-up against the Detroit Lions. The New York hip-hop group, joined by Red Hot Chili Peppers’ Chad Smith on drums, kicked it off with “Triumph” from their 1997 album Wu-Tang Forever. The rappers also showcased a medley that included “C.R.E.A.M,” “Da Mystery of Chessboxin,” and “Bring Da Ruckus.” Notably, Ol’ Dirty Bastard’s son Young Dirty Bastard performed his father’s lines off 1995 single “Shimmy Shimmy Ya.”

Later, Miranda Lambert showcased a medley of her hits during the Dallas Cowboys and the Philadelphia Eagles game at AT&T Stadium in Dallas. The Grammy-winning singer took the stage to perform “Snakeskin Boots” (sorry, no Ella Langley cameo), “Kerosene,” “Gunpowder & Lead,” “Mama’s Broken Heart,” and “Little Red Wagon.”
