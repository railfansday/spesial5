Anthropic shares close 17% above their IPO price on first day of trading

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Claude maker’s debut came as US indexes hit seven-month lows and Asian markets fell on Friday.

Anthropic sold about 980 million shares in the offering, raising roughly $81bn. That makes it the largest IPO on record.

The wider market was less upbeat. Major US stock indexes fell to seven-month lows on Thursday on worries about the outlook for AI, according to The Guardian. Asian markets dropped by up to 4% in early trading on Friday.

Bob O’Donnell, chief analyst at TECHnalysis Research, said the listing was only a first step towards calming investors.

“The blockbuster Anthropic launch is a critical step in reassuring the market about the potential for AI, but it’s really just the beginning,” O’Donnell said.

Anthropic’s prospectus shows fast growth and heavy losses. The company made $16.2bn in revenue in the first half of 2026, up from $4.6bn in all of 2025. However, it lost $104bn in the first nine months of the year, much of it from a one-off charge.

The company also faces political pressure. Democrats will control both chambers of Congress next year. Last month, Senator Elizabeth Warren wrote to chief executive Dario Amodei, warning that the IPO could bring volatility and risk into the financial system, The Guardian reported.

Protesters also gathered outside Anthropic’s San Francisco headquarters on Thursday.

Rival OpenAI plans to go public in 2027. Its chief executive, Sam Altman, told Semafor this week that demand for AI shares far outstrips supply.
