How to gain more Followers in Warhammer Survivors

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Gaining Followers in Warhammer Survivors has many benefits when it comes to the survival aspect of the game. Not only do they help you survive and last longer in the battle, but you’ll need to gain a minimum number if you want to complete the objectives.

While this method talks about gaining 5 followers, it can be any arbitrary number. I say 5 because that’s a requirement of an in-game objective that unlocks a crucial item. There is more than one way to gain followers, but this will be the easiest one for you.

First, unlock Trooper Bartosz Kozlowski and Sister Superior Amalia Novena, followed by their respective weapons. Now, begin a fresh run with Malum Caedo.

Once you’re deeper into your run, you’ll be offered the Lasgun and Shield of Faith as available weapons. Grab both of them, and then upgrade the Lasgun to Level 6. This will make Bartosz Kozlowski bring the required number of followers.

If you’re one short, that’s where Sister Amalia comes in. Once you acquire the Shield of Faith as a weapon, you’ll manage to have her as a companion. This will also unlock the Figure Case passive skill that allows you to return after dying.

You can repeat the same process with other characters in the game. Several characters allow you to add to the count of Followers when you unlock their signature weapon and level it up. However, the characters I have mentioned above are easy to unlock, and they will allow you to get to five Followers fast.
