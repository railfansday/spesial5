Rivals of Aether II adds Burnette on December 1

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Platform fighting game Rivals of Aether II will add Burnette, “The Scorched Shield,” as its next free additional character update on December 1, developer Aether Studios announced.

Voiced by Hololive Virtual YouTuber Elizabeth Rose Bloodflame, Burnette is the eighth free character update since the game’s launch. The patch will also introduce a new in-game event, a new ranked season, cosmetics, and more.

Rivals of Aether II is available now for PC via Steam.
