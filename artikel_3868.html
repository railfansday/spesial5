All Witch’s Grimoire Weapon and Item codes in Queen’s Domain

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Witch’s Grimoire is the most powerful item you can pick extremely early in Queen’s Domain, and it will entirely change your gameplay. You can inscribe different codes in it to unlock the game’s best weapons and tools.

In this guide, I will go through all the different codes that you can inscribe (as long as you have sufficient charges).

The following table has every code I have come across so far on social media. I have also mentioned the exact item you get from inscribing the codes. At first, you can inscribe up to three codes before you’ll need to refill the charges.

The Witch’s Grimoire can be found from the large tree near where you wake up. Move towards the back of the tree (where it meets the wall) and continuously press E.

To refill it, you can use the top of the record of sin behind the hidden wall. It automatically gains one charge whenever you defeat a boss.
