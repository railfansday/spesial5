Drop Zero Digital: Operations Associate (Hiring or Client Delivery)

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Before you apply, spend ten minutes on our website and LinkedIn. In your Loom we will ask what we do, where you found out about us, and why you want to work at Drop Zero Digital. Answers that could be about any company will not pass.

We are Drop Zero Digital, a lifecycle marketing agency. We are hiring one person who is good at deciding. Our AI image service is our fastest-growing service, and whichever area you own, you will be helping it grow.

You will own one named area. Which one is decided during the trial rather than now, based on what you turn out to be strong at. It will be one of these two:

Both are the same job underneath: you notice what has slipped, you decide what to do, and you close it without being asked.

This is not an assistant job and it is not a coordinator job. You will make real calls every day, and you will be judged on the quality of those calls rather than on how much you got through.

We are building a team of people who decide well without being managed. If you want to be told what to do each morning, this job is not for you.

We do not ask for a number of years. We check your work history against what you tell us, and a false answer ends the application.

Whatever employment you do list, we verify: employer, dates, title and paid status, evidenced by contract and payslips, in one exchange. You need to have held a full-time job at some point in the last 12 months. Beyond that there is no minimum, and a short history costs you nothing here. Misrepresenting the history you do have is disqualifying. Tell us the true version and you will not be penalised for it.

The trial is where the decision actually gets made, including which of the two areas you end up owning. From the first week you will be making calls on live work, and we will tell you straight how each one landed. It is paid from day one: half rate for the first two weeks while you find your feet, full rate from week three onward, and a completion payment when you finish the trial, whether or not we hire you. If we stop early, you are paid for the days you worked.

2100 USD per month from day one, set by responsibility and demonstrated capability, not by how long you have worked.

Responsibility and pay move quickly here for people who earn them. Authority over decisions that reach a client moves slowly and deliberately, and it is earned on a track record rather than on a good first month. We would rather tell you that now than have you discover it in month two.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
