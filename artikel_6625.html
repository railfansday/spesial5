I Chewed 7 Popular Gum Brands Until They Lost Their Flavor—Here's Which Lasted The Longest

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Chewing gum can be incredibly frustrating. You unwrap a fresh piece expecting a burst of minty flavor that'll last a while, only to find yourself chewing flavorless rubber five minutes later. And most of the time, it doesn't even make it that long.

So I decided to put seven popular gum brands to the ultimate test. I would chew each piece until the flavor completely disappeared, timing exactly how long it took before I was left with nothing but a rubbery wad of disappointment.

I judged each gum on the intensity of its spearmint flavor, how pleasant it was to chew, and, most importantly, how long that minty taste actually lasted. And after spending a frankly ridiculous amount of time chewing gum, I have some thoughts (and jaw pain). Here's how all seven brands ranked, from shortest-lasting to longest-lasting.

I quickly knew Pür wasn't going to be a strong contender. Of all seven brands, this had the least flavor right from the beginning. While the other gums delivered at least some noticeable burst of spearmint, Pür barely gave me enough to enjoy before the taste started disappearing.

But the bigger issue was the texture. Almost immediately, the gum became tough and waxy, making it unpleasant to chew even before the flavor was completely gone. Instead of that soft, satisfying consistency I want from gum, it felt like I was working way too hard for very little reward.

By the time I hit one minute and 36 seconds, there was absolutely no reason to keep chewing.

Considering the packaging literally advertises long-lasting flavor, I had pretty high expectations going into this one. Unfortunately, the results were anything but impressive.

The spearmint flavor disappeared after just two minutes and one second, making Extra the second shortest-lasting gum in the entire test. When a brand makes longevity such a prominent selling point, I expect to get more than two minutes of enjoyment out of a piece.

I'm not saying I need my gum to taste fresh all afternoon, but two minutes feels like a pretty low bar. I don't like liars, Extra. And you’re not extra, you’re underwhelming.

Trident managed to outlast Extra and Pür, but not by a particularly impressive margin. The biggest thing I noticed was the texture. This gum leaned more toward the waxy side, which isn't exactly my favorite quality in something I'm supposed to be chewing for an extended period.

The spearmint flavor lasted two minutes and 30 seconds before fading away, putting it firmly in the bottom half of the ranking. While it held on slightly longer than Extra, the difference wasn't enough to make Trident feel particularly long-lasting.

And considering how much better the top four performed, this one simply couldn't compete.

Orbit immediately impressed me with a very strong burst of mint flavor. Compared with the previous three brands, the initial taste was much more noticeable, making the experience more enjoyable from the start.

Even after the flavor began to fade, Orbit remained soft and pleasant to chew. It never developed that tough, waxy consistency that made some of the lower-ranked gums so unpleasant.

The mint flavor lasted four minutes and seven seconds, which was a significant improvement over Trident.

If we're judging these gums purely on how intense the flavor is when you first start chewing, 5 Gum wins. This had the biggest, boldest burst of spearmint flavor in the entire lineup. The initial mint sensation was noticeably more powerful than anything else I tried, making it an excellent choice for someone who likes their gum to pack a punch.

And thankfully, the flavor wasn't just strong for the first few seconds. It lasted an impressive seven minutes and six seconds before finally fading away, putting it among the longest-lasting gums in the test. The only thing keeping 5 Gum from the top spot was that two other brands held on just a little longer.

Ice Breakers delivered one of the most interesting experiences of the entire taste test because its intense cooling sensation and overall mint flavor seemed to have two different lifespans.

For the first two minutes and seven seconds, the gum delivered a powerful cooling effect that made my mouth feel especially refreshed. But even after that initial cooling sensation faded, the spearmint flavor kept going. In fact, I was able to chew it for another five minutes and two seconds before the flavor finally disappeared, bringing the total to seven minutes and nine seconds.

That's just three seconds longer than 5 Gum, making this an incredibly close competition.

I have to admit, Eclipse surprised me more than any other gum in this ranking. Going into the test, I wasn't expecting it to outlast every other brand, especially after experiencing the intense initial flavor of 5 Gum and the cooling sensation of Ice Breakers.

The spearmint flavor lasted an impressive seven minutes and 33 seconds before finally disappearing, making it the longest-lasting gum of the seven I tested. While 5 Gum delivered the strongest initial flavor and Ice Breakers impressed with its cooling effect, Eclipse ultimately won the competition that mattered most: longevity.

It outlasted Ice Breakers by 24 seconds and 5 Gum by 27 seconds. Those aren't enormous differences, but when you've spent this much time counting every second of mint flavor, they certainly count. Eclipse might not have been my most anticipated winner, but the numbers don't lie.
