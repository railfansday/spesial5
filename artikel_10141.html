‘Enough Phone’ is stuffing a bigger battery, more RAM & storage into its small Android phone at no extra cost

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The “Enough Phone” is trying to fulfill the small Android phone dream and, now, it’s getting some spec upgrades to the battery, RAM, and storage, all with no added cost.

Announced earlier this month, the “Enough Phone” is an Android phone set to launch in 2027 at a size about the same as an iPhone 13 Mini.

Now mostly funded on Kickstarter, “Enough Phone” originally promised to back up its 5.3-inch display with 8GB of RAM, 128GB of storage, and a battery roughly on par with what you’d expect from a tiny phone. We’re now getting some upgrades on those original specs.
