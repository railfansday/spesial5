Crystal Dynamics Cuts Even More Developers As It Prepares To Ship Two Massive Tomb Raider Games: ‘My First Layoff In 14 Years’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Crystal DymamicsEmbracerLayoffsTomb Raider CatalystTomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis

Crystal Dynamics is letting more people go. The studio says it’s only eight staff, but it includes designers and engineers. Making the cuts all the more confusing is that the studio is gearing up to release two major games, a remake of the original Tomb Raider and the first new sequel since 2018.

“After several rounds of layoffs at Crystal Dynamics, today it was my turn,” design manager Joshua Mitchell post on LinkedIn (via Vertical Slice). “My first layoff in 14 years making games. Catalyst, meanwhile, has been pushed back until 2028, a decade since Shadow of the Tomb Raider. Crystal Dynamics shipped Marvel’s Avengers in 2020 and worked on Xbox’s now-canceled Perfect Dark reboot in-between.

“Crystal Dynamics made a difficult, but necessary business decision to reduce our current studio staff by 8 people,” the studio told Gamesradar in a statement. “Whenever we phase roles off a project, we make every effort to find new spots for our great colleagues first. Everyone affected receives a full severance package as well as job placement assistance to aid them through this transition. We wish them the best and hope to work together in the future.”

It’s the fifth layoff since March 2025. Around 20 people were laid off in March of 2026, and dozens more in 2025. The cancelation of Perfect Dark certainly played a role, but Amazon, Tomb Raider‘s publisher, has also been backing away from AAA game development. Embracer purchased Crystal Dynamics in 2022. Current CEO Phil Rogers said over the summer that Amazon’s own recent cuts and restructuring around its gaming division have impacted the Tomb Raider franchise.
