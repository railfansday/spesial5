How to collect the second Map Fragment in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Fortnite Treasure Hunters has six different map fragments to discover, and the second one is already available for us to find. The core process is the same as getting the first map fragment.

We have a new location this time around, however, so let’s figure out how to obtain the second fragment.

To find the map fragment, you must head to the northern part of the new island map. You have to visit Lil Uzi’s Stronghold, to be precise, and this is a hot zone. Not only can you find the map fragment at this location, but you will also find the Lil Uzi boss to take down.

To find the map fragment, however, you need to go southeast of the base. You’ll find a small shack/house on the outskirts that’s pretty easy to locate. There’s just one door, and this shack will have the map fragment on the floor. You can also find an ammo chest inside to refill your weapons.

Once you find and add the map fragment, you’re done. Just like the first time, Epic Games has released just one more fragment. With four more fragments set to arrive in the future, we will have to wait for few more weeks (unless Epic introduces changes to its schedule).

The quest related to the second fragment will automatically update as soon as you collect it. You can find the remaining map fragments in any order you want.
