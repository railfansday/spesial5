I thought I would have a look just in case, see if by chance any Newcastle United v Arsenal tickets were available on Saturday afternoon.

Just in case you aren’t aware, the club allow Newcastle United season ticket holders to sell their tickets for individual Premier League games.

This was a very positive move by the club as it means that seats don’t need to be left empty if a season ticket holder is ill for example.

Using the online club system, season ticket holders can make their ticket available for a league match, with Newcastle United members then able to go onto the NUFC official ticketing site (you have to be then logged in as a member to see the tickets that are available) to buy.

The club also make money from this. As I understand it, the fan (season ticket holder) selling the ticket is credited with the pro rata match price of their season ticket and if the ticket is then bought by a member, they are charged the far higher single ticket price, with the club banking the difference.

As a member, I had no luck in the ballot for Newcastle United v Arsenal tickets.

The demand was huge, I had a ridiculously high number for the queue sale and stood no chance, along with countless thousands of other Newcastle United members.

Anyway, I have just went onto the club’s ticketing site (just before 2pm on Saturday) and I couldn’t believe how many Newcastle United v Arsenal tickets were on sale!

To be honest, I wasn’t expecting there to be any, I was just looking in the vain hope I might hit very lucky.

What I found was I had a choice of all around St James’ Park! All four stands with plenty tickets up for sale from season ticket holders.

I snapped up a couple for me and my mate for the East Stand and after sending this article into The Mag I will be heading off to the Toon to meet up with the lads (mostly season ticket holders) for some pre-match refreshments.

After buying our tickets I counted up all the Newcastle United v Arsenal tickets that season ticket holders are trying to sell and there were 147 available (it had been 149 I guess before I snapped up ours).

Apologies for my wonky photography skills but the red areas are where there were Newcastle United v Arsenal tickets available just over three and a half hours before the match…

I am over the moon getting these tickets but at the same time absolutely stunned that so many season ticket holders are trying to flog their tickets.

Is it because they think Arsenal will win?

Is it because they will get too upset seeing Bruno Guimaraes in an Arsenal shirt?

Anyway, if you are a member who didn’t get a ticket for today’s match, log onto the official ticketing site and take your pick!!
