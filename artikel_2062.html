FC Thun vs Hearts almost immediately halted as pyro smoke fills stadium

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Conference League match between FC Thun and Heart of Midlothian had to be halted almost immediately.

The match referee stopped the match with less than a minute played due to smoke filling the stadium in Switzerland.

Pyrotechnic devices had been set off in the home end of the stadium, leading to very poor visibility on the pitch.

The match had to be stopped until the smoke cleared. The delay lasted around eight minutes before play was resumed for the Euro clash.

A Hearts statement on social media read: "Well, we did kick-off, but the game was almost immediately stopped, due to smoke from pyrotechnic devices let off in the home end.

"Should be back underway in the next minute or so."
