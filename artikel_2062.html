Welcome news for Parker : Key man’s return to form with international double | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The value of international friendlies has always seemed questionable mid season: During a challenging domestic schedule the disruption of thousands of miles of travel, different coaching regimes and playing styles can easily prove disruptive: Let alone the risk of injuries to jaded players.

West Ham United have their fair share of absentees on international duty right now, which Scott Parker must be rueing as he tries to rebuild the playing style of the squad he has inherited from his predecessor.

However there’s an upside to  those international fixtures if it allows squad members to improve their game and their confidence – and several of the Hammers’ squad will come back to their club in far better spirits than they left.

As we covered yesterday Mads Hermansen’s headline winning performance to keep out Portugal can only have done him good.

International debut for Hammers youngster Mohamadou Kanté

Mohamadou Kanté must be made up at making his international debut for Mali – on the winning side in his first time wearing the national shirt means the midfield man will come back with a little more of a swagger than he left with, whilst news of Joel Piroe’s brace on international duty for Suriname will have been similarly well received at Rush Green.

With Castellanos and Pablo misfiring so badly, much is expected of the on-loan Leeds man and for Piroe to grab two goals in his national side’s 4-0 victory over Eswatini (formerly Swaziland, for anyone else who wondered) can only be a confidence boost for West Ham’s number nine ahead of the resumption of the Championship at the weekend.
