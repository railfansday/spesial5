Roberto De Zerbi is being pushed to hand Luca Williams-Barnett more regular minutes at Tottenham, sources have told Football Insider.

The 18-year-old made his first Premier League appearance for Spurs last time out during the 2-0 win against Crystal Palace, in what was the third senior appearance of his career.

His display after coming off the bench set tongues wagging, and there are now growing calls for the winger to play an increased role in the first-team squad from now on.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, De Zerbi is keen to keep Williams-Barnett around his squad for the rest of the season.

There had been suggestions that the teenager could be allowed to leave Spurs on loan in search of more regular minutes to continue his development in senior football.

However, Tottenham now have plans to keep a close eye on Williams-Barnett's progress by keeping him in and around De Zerbi's squad after his impressive showing.

Football Insider revealed last month that Tottenham are weighing up whether to send Williams-Barnett on loan, with multiple clubs keen to agree a January deal.

However, after his impressive performance against Crystal Palace, Spurs are more likely to keep hold of the winger with hopes he can continue playing a role for them.

There is a feeling that he will play a valuable role in De Zerbi's plans over the coming months, and could be better served by continuing on his current pathway.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I think that cameo probably showed everything Spurs fans have been talking about for a while now in regards to Luca Williams-Barnett.

"He's an exciting player, he gets fans off their seats, and he has given Roberto De Zerbi something to think about with that performance off the bench against Crystal Palace.

"So I think he's probably needed immediately right now rather than considering a possible loan away or anything like that.

"He's proved that he is good enough to get minutes in the Premier League off the back of that showing against Crystal Palace as well.

"I'm sure he'll be hoping that he can get more minutes on De Zerbi rather than thinking of having to go out and loan to get minutes."

Williams-Barnett has long been highly rated by Tottenham, with his Premier League debut having come following months of calls for him to be involved in the first team.

De Zerbi recently described him as a "special talent" and a "special personality" and has joined others in North London who have piled high praise on the teenager.

In fact, former Spurs scout Bryan King told Football Insider last month that Tottenham believe Williams-Barnett can be the "next Cole Palmer" if he continues his trajectory.

The forward featured heavily in pre-season and came off the bench when the Lilywhites beat Charlton in the Carabao Cup in August, and has now been involved in the Premier League.
