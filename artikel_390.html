This year, Black Friday falls on Nov. 27. While retailers often start featuring their best markdowns on Black Friday itself, there are plenty of early deals worth shopping in the lead-up. Most retailers' official sales do start before the actual day, so shopping strategically ahead of time can save you from missing out on a deal entirely (or dealing with the chaos the day after Thanksgiving).

Cyber Monday takes place on the Monday following Thanksgiving. This year, the shopping event takes place on Monday, Nov. 30. While Cyber Monday originally began as an online counterpart to in-person shopping on Black Friday, the two events have merged into an extended online shopping bonanza.

The mega-retailer just wrapped up its October Prime Day sale, but naturally, it's also getting in on the Black Friday action. While Amazon hasn't released official sale dates yet (it's still in the final days of the Holiday Beauty Event), last year's Black Friday sale started over a week before the official day. For 2026, Amazon's official Black Friday sales kick off on Nov. 19.

Amazon: Amazon recently announced that its Black Friday sale will begin on Nov. 19, with new deals dropping through Cyber Monday, on Nov. 30.

Best Buy: The big box store is hosting its Early Black Friday Deals + Doorbusters sale from Nov. 6 through Nov. 18, and the official Best Buy Black Friday sale kicks off on Nov. 19 and runs through Nov. 28. The Best Buy Cyber Monday Sale Event runs for 48 hours on Nov. 29 and Nov. 30.

Walmart: Walmart has three major sale events throughout November: the early Black Friday sale from Nov. 12 to Nov. 15, the Black Friday sale from Nov. 23 to Nov. 29 (with deals online only from Nov. 23 to Nov. 26), and the online-only Cyber Monday sale from Nov. 30 to Dec. 2.

Target: Target hasn't released its full Black Friday lineup yet, but we do know its first Early Black Friday sale kicks off Nov. 5 to Nov. 7. From Nov. 1 to Dec. 24, Target's Deal of the Day offer kicks off, featuring new deals every day. Every weekend after Nov. 7, the retailer will also drop new limited-time holiday offers.
