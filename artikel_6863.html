Kendall Jenner Shared a Peek Inside Her Chic Horse Girl-Themed Birthday Celebration

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Kendall Jenner rang in her 31st birthday with a horse girl-themed celebration.

On Friday, the model, who was born on November 3, offered a peek inside the festivities on Instagram. Naturally, there were plenty of flowers, along with a cake and a fashion moment worth a closer look.

Jenner’s cake featured a white horse figurine surrounded by tall candles. It was a particularly fitting choice for Jenner, who has a lifelong connection to horses. In a 2023 interview with Stella McCartney as the face of its winter campaign, Jenner shared that she once dreamed of becoming a professional equestrian. Later, in Vogue, she shared that she first sat on a horse at age 2 and took her first lesson at age 10.

“I think as an adult now, doing it almost gives me a sense of feeling like I’m a kid again,” she said.

Beyond the ode to her horse girl history, Jenner celebrated her birthday in style. For what appeared to be dinner, the birthday girl opted for a butter-yellow robe from Conner Ives’s fall 2026 collection, which features intricate floral and bird embroidery inspired by antique tapestries. She paired it with the matching silk bodice for a look that brought both romance and drama.

Jenner rounded out her birthday photo dump with a shot of herself riding along the beach wearing a plaid poncho.

This wasn’t the model’s only birthday celebration. She made headlines last week for the Halloween party she threw to mark the milestone early.
