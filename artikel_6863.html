Meet the ‘Guarding Stars’ Cast and Characters

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The ‘Gossip Girl’ and ‘Gilmore Girls’ crossover we deserve.

Netflix is stepping up its Christmas romance game with a book adaptation that’s action-packed and super sweet. It also, not to brag, has a pretty iconic millennial pairing and more in the cast. Let this be your guide to the stars of Guarding Stars.

The film is based on the 2022 novel The Bodyguard by Katherine Center, not to be confused with the 1992 romantic thriller The Bodyguard starring Kevin Costner and Whitney Houston or the Netflix drama series Bodyguard starring Richard Madden. This is strictly a home-for-the-holidays romantic comedy about a pretend relationship that a superstar, played by Jared Padalecki, and his bodyguard, played by Leighton Meester, fake so that her family doesn’t know she’s in danger.

Most of the characters are from the book, but there are a few newcomers and changes to keep readers on their toes. There’s also a character called “Brody” played by Jake Foy (Designated Survivor) we know very little about. And a lot of the supporting cast is played by folks who made their names in the sports and/or music industry. Get to know them and who they play in Guarding Stars below.

Hanna is an EPA, executive protection agent, assigned to Jack.

Leighton Meester is best known for playing Blair Waldorf on Gossip Girl, and recently recurred on I Love LA and The Buccaneers. Other notable TV roles include How I Met Your Father, Single Parents, and Making History. Meester’s filmography also includes The Roommates, Life Partners, Country Strong, Monte Carlo, and Going the Distance. Real ones also know she released an album in 2014.

Jack is an A-List actor with an escalating stalker problem.

There are two types of people in this world: those know how Jared Padalecki as Dean from Gilmore Girls and those who know him as Sam from Supernatural. Maybe there’s even a third kind of person who only knows him from Walker. Still, those are three pretty major credits. Movie-wise, you’ve also seen him in House of Wax, Flight of the Phoenix, Friday the 13h (2009), and Christmas Cottage.

Andie MacDowell won an Independent Spirit Award for Sex, Lies, and Videotape and is a rom-com queen best known for Groundhog Day and Four Weddings and a Funeral. She also appeared in Monte Carlo, and more recently in films like Magic Mike XXL, The Other Zoey, and Ready or Not as well as the series Cedar Cove and the miniseries Maid.

Noah LaLonde is a former hockey player and actor who plays Cole Walter on My Life with the Walter Boys. His film credits include Asbury Park and Deer Camp ‘86.

Siobhan Williams’ first job was playing Jamie on Heartland. Her other television credits include UnREAL, Black Box, Deadly Class, Sacred Lies, The Hunting Party, and the upcoming season of Mayfair Witches. She also had a supporting role in the 2018 film Welcome to Marwen.

Walker Hayes is a country singer who has released five studio albums. The song “Fancy Like” won a Billboard Music Award in 2022. He has also been nominated at the Grammy Awards, CMT Awards, and AMAs.

Taylor is one of Hanna’s former EPA colleagues and former best friend.

Rachael Ancheril is a Canadian actress you know from Rookie Blue, Star Trek: Discovery, Nurses, Wyonna Earp, and Killjoys.

Robbie is also one of Hanna’s former colleagues, as well as her ex.

Toby Sandeman is a double gold medalist, NBD, for the 200-meter dash. He recently started acting and has had nothing but lead or major supporting roles in the shows The Royals, Power Book III: Raising Kanan, The Game, and Running Point as well as the Netflix movie Voicemails for Isabelle.

Doghouse is yet another one of Hanna’s former colleagues.

Also known as Phillip Brooks, CM Punk is a WWE wrestler. He’s not totally new to professional acting either. Some of his prior credits include Heels, Mayans MC, and Revival as well as movies like Night Patrol and Zootopia 2.

We don’t know what her supporting role is yet, but Jack does have a “PR relationship” with another actress in the book...

Ava Max is a singer-songwriter best known for her acclaimed, very popular song “Sweet But Psycho.” This is her acting debut!
