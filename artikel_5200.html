Shiba Inu's exchange activity keeps on bringing more to the table. This time, 372.39 billion tokens flowed into exchanges. Inflows recorded an increase of 0.36% over 24 hours, which once again raises the chances of a surge in market pressure, data from CryptoQuant suggests.

While the inflow number to exchanges is quite high, the total netflow doesn't hit the value of 100 billion, suggesting that the amount of inflows after withdrawals doesn't really disrupt the overall picture for Shiba Inu. Outflows accounted for 280.82 billion SHIB, leaving a net inflow of 91.57 billion. About three quarters of the deposits were offset by withdrawals.

Exchange reserve value is still above the important psychological threshold of $300 million. CryptoQuant puts it at $313.98 million, up 3.8% on the day. In token terms, exchange reserves increased by 0.11% to 86.02 trillion SHIB.

Following Bitcoin's drop below $60,000, SHIB and other altcoins witnessed an increase in selling pressure. At the time of the snapshot, Shiba Inu was trading at $0.00000365, successfully bouncing from the local low of $0.00000337.

That bounce looks speculative after the sell-off. Some of the buying volume may have come from orders placed prior to the drop. SHIB was up about 8.3% from the low and was still below $0.000004.

Momentum stays as weak as before. An RSI around 27 suggests the market is still oversold, which means there are no guarantees for a quick reversal. Luckily, though, there's some bullish traction as Shiba Inu is printing the second green candle in a row, making a retrace somewhat of a possibility sooner than we might've expected.

Speaking generally, Shiba Inu is in a tough spot: apart from the cryptocurrency market, the economy in general is struggling and we are not seeing recovery prospects just yet. Unfortunately, there's a good chance crypto and altcoins specifically will continue to struggle from here.
