Tether Gold Under Pressure: Bitcoin and Gold Losses Erase 70% of USDT’s Cushion

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Falling gold and Bitcoin prices erase an estimated $5.7 billion from Tether’s holdings, putting its USDT reserve cushion under pressure.

Gold just slid toward $3,500 an ounce and silver fell below $40, extending a sell-off that has put Tether gold back in focus. Why? Tether keeps billions of dollars of gold bars in the reserves behind USDT, so the crash hits its balance sheet directly.

Based on Tether’s latest reserves report and the blockchain, the company holds about 168 tons of gold: 142.8 tons it owns in its USDT reserves, plus 25.7 tons backing its Tether Gold (XAUT) token.

Gold just crashed below $3,500 and silver below $40, hitting their lowest levels in almost 15 months.

At today’s prices, the gold Tether owns has lost about $3.2 billion of value since September 30. Add Bitcoin’s slide, and about 70% of Tether’s $7.95 billion reserve cushion is gone on paper.

USDT, meanwhile, is shrinking. Its supply has fallen $16 billion from its May peak.

The XAUT gold belongs to token holders, so its price swings hit them. The 142.8 tons in the reserves are Tether’s own, so their losses come out of Tether’s cushion.

Tether’s assets topped its liabilities by $7.95 billion on September 30. That gap is the cushion that protects USDT holders if Tether’s investments lose value.

We repriced Tether’s two riskiest holdings at Wednesday’s prices.

That is not the full picture. Tether earns interest on $122.8 billion of US Treasury bills, and it reported profits of $1.04 billion and $1.5 billion in the first two quarters of 2026. Fourth-quarter income will rebuild part of the gap. Its $4.18 billion of stocks, which the report says include gold and bitcoin exposure, may have fallen too.

Tether’s main business is also cooling. USDT supply peaked at $190.4 billion on May 20. It stood at $174.4 billion on Wednesday.

Redemptions shrink the pile of Treasury bills that earns Tether’s interest income. Falling rates would hurt too, but for now the market expects the Fed to raise them.

XAUT trades at $3,473. Our chart uses PAX Gold (PAXG), a rival token that also tracks one ounce of gold, because its Binance history goes back much further than XAUT’s March listing.

PAXG trades at $3,478, below its 50-day exponential moving average (EMA), an average that gives recent prices more weight, at $3,924, and its 200-day EMA at $4,187.

Tether gold is still a huge pile of metal, and Tether earned more than $1 billion in each of the first two quarters. But its reserve cushion now depends on gold and bitcoin stopping their fall.
