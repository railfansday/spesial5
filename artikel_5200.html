The crypto market feels cautious, with traders selling rallies instead of chasing them. Bitcoin sits at the center of that mood after a new forecast argued it may stay below $150,000 through 2030.

The call lands as Bitcoin trades far below its record high and just above a key support level. Any Bitcoin price prediction for the coming weeks starts with whether buyers can defend that floor.

Traders who weigh long-term forecasts against short-term swings also watch early-stage projects. One of them is MEMASA ($MEMASA), an upcoming AI trading app that lets users research and trade crypto through a chat.

A recent Motley Fool analysis on Yahoo Finance argues Bitcoin will likely stay below $150,000 through the end of the decade.

Author Dominic Basulto says the $1 million targets popular among Bitcoin bulls should be shelved for now.

His math leans on JPMorgan Asset Management, which expects Bitcoin to beat stocks, bonds and gold over the next decade.

The firm’s bull case assumes yearly growth near 17%, which would land Bitcoin close to that ceiling on the same timeline.

Still, JPMorgan warns the ride will come with extreme volatility, and Bitcoin has lost about 40% over the past year. That slump explains why even Strategy’s Michael Saylor has hinted that a return to six figures may prove difficult.

Bitcoin now trades near $74,800, down about 10% over the past month. The nearest strong support sits near $73,500, while resistance stands much higher at about $84,400. Technical indicators lean bearish, so a clean break below support could open a path toward $70,000.

For any near-term Bitcoin price prediction, reclaiming $84,400 would be the first sign that buyers are back in control. Long-term forecasts like JPMorgan’s leave traders with plenty of questions, and some new tools aim to answer them faster.

MEMASA is an upcoming AI crypto trading app that lets users analyze markets, check token risks, and trade on-chain by chatting with an AI assistant.

The app tackles problems familiar to crypto traders: too many charts, scattered market data, scam tokens, and complicated interfaces. MEMASA plans to combine market intelligence, risk checks, automated strategies, and trading in one self-custodial app.

If the crypto market enters another bullish phase, growing interest in altcoins could bring more people into trading.

MEMASA aims to serve that audience by making market research and trading as simple as a conversation. Its potential appeal extends from beginners struggling with trading interfaces to experienced traders who want faster access to market insights.

Can $MEMASA benefit from the next crypto rally?

$MEMASA is the utility token powering the MEMASA ecosystem. Its planned uses include premium AI intelligence, trading fee discounts, marketplace payments, and staking for analysts and AI agents.

The token’s potential derives from stronger sentiment across the crypto market and growing demand within the MEMASA ecosystem. If the app attracts a wide audience, it might drive the demand for $MEMASA.

MEMASA is currently offering early access to $MEMASA through an on-chain early sale, with prices below the project’s anticipated public listing price. Earlier stages offer lower prices, while later stages provide faster token unlocks.

A Bitcoin rally could create favorable conditions for MEMASA’s debut. The bigger test will be whether the app delivers on its promise to simplify crypto trading and attracts enough users to build lasting demand for its token.

Bitcoin’s next test is the support near $73,500. Holding it keeps a recovery toward $84,400 in play, while a break could send the price toward $70,000.

The long view looks calmer, as JPMorgan’s bull case still points to about $150,000 by the end of the decade, though with sharp swings along the way.

MEMASA faces a different test, as it needs to turn an AI trading concept into an app people use regularly. If it succeeds, demand for its utility token could grow with the ecosystem, and today’s $0.0155 sale price would become its reference point.

Both Bitcoin’s path and MEMASA’s outcome remain uncertain, and higher potential comes with higher risk.
