CMAs Photo Gallery: See Taylor Swift, Ella Langley, Kacey Musgraves, Lainey Wilson, Stella Lefty and Other Stars on the Carpet and On-Stage

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Senior Music Writer and Chief Music Critic

It very much felt like ladies’ night at the 60th annual CMA Awards, with Ella Langley taking a record six awards home and a big spotlight on stars like Taylor Swift, Miranda Lambert and host Lainey Wilson. The men of country also got their turn, with performances from or awards for Chris Stapleton, Brooks & Dunn, Zach Top, Vince Gill, Brad Paisley, Tim McGraw and many others.

Here’s a gallery from Variety photographer Michael Buckner, who captured the night’s leading lights on the red carpet as well as on-stage. He also trained his camera on other top country stars like Stella Lefty and Megan Moroney, and some of the guests who came into the 2026 CMAs from beyond Nashville, from actors Glen Powell and Jessica Chastain to performers like Mumford & Sons and Lzzy Hale.
