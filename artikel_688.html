The Best Gifts to Shop at Best Buy: From Home Theater Upgrades to Espresso Machines

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Variety may receive an affiliate commission.

Finding the perfect holiday gift is easier when you take a cue from Hollywood. From the rise of AI-powered eyewear among celebrities to the growing appeal of elaborate home theater setups, some of this year’s most coveted gifts take inspiration from the screen.

At Best Buy, shoppers can find everything from immersive sound systems for the cinephile to sleek coffee machines for the friend who has perfected their at-home latte routine. JBL’s latest soundbars and portable speakers make it easier to recreate the movie theater or concert experience at home, while Nespresso’s espresso machines are an appealing upgrade for anyone looking to upgrade their morning routine in the new year. Meanwhile, smart glasses are becoming an increasingly visible part of the fashion and entertainment conversation. Meta’s collaborations with Ray-Ban and Oakley have brought AI-powered wearables into the mainstream, combining recognizable eyewear silhouettes with hands-free cameras, audio and voice assistants. For a more practical gift, Brother’s wireless printers and label makers are particularly useful for creatives, aspiring screenwriters and anyone building a home office.

Whether you’re shopping for a devoted film buff, a music obsessive, a fashion enthusiast or someone who simply loves a good gadget, these Best Buy finds make the perfect gift this holiday season:

For the movie buff who wants the full theatrical experience without leaving the couch, JBL’s Bar 1000MK2 is a serious home entertainment upgrade. The 7.1.4-channel soundbar features Dolby Atmos and DTS:X, a wireless subwoofer and detachable surround speakers that can be placed behind the sofa for more immersive sound. It’s particularly appealing for action movies, concerts and prestige TV marathons.

JBL’s Charge 6 is a versatile gift for the music lover who brings a playlist everywhere. The portable speaker delivers punchy bass, features AI Sound Boost to optimize audio and offers up to 28 hours of playtime with Playtime Boost enabled. Its waterproof, dustproof design makes it equally suited to beach days, backyard parties and weekend getaways.

Compact enough to toss into a backpack but powerful enough to soundtrack a gathering, the JBL Flip 7 is an easy win for almost anyone on your list. The portable Bluetooth speaker features upgraded audio with AI Sound Boost, a waterproof and drop-resistant design and up to 16 hours of playback with Playtime Boost. It also comes in a range of colors.

For the coffee obsessive, Nespresso’s Vertuo Creatista is a countertop upgrade that looks as good as the drinks it makes. The stainless-steel machine combines Nespresso’s single-serve brewing system with an integrated steam wand, allowing users to prepare everything from cappuccinos to flat whites with textured milk. It’s a particularly thoughtful gift for someone who spends a small fortune at their neighborhood café.

For the fashion-conscious tech enthusiast, Ray-Ban’s second-generation Meta Wayfarers combine one of the eyewear brand’s most recognizable silhouettes with hands-free technology. The smart glasses let wearers capture photos and videos, listen to music, make calls and interact with Meta AI using voice commands. They’re a particularly fun gift for travelers, content creators and anyone who wants to spend less time looking at a phone.

Oakley’s Meta Vanguard glasses are designed for the athlete who loves tracking every adventure. The performance-focused smart glasses combine a built-in camera, open-ear audio and Meta AI with sporty wraparound frames and Oakley’s Prizm lenses. Designed with outdoor activities in mind, they’re an especially compelling gift for runners, cyclists and fitness enthusiasts looking to capture their workouts hands-free.

A practical gift for students, freelancers and anyone setting up a home office, Brother’s INKvestment Tank printer handles printing, scanning, copying and faxing in one device. The wireless model supports mobile printing and comes with up to a year’s worth of ink in the box, helping reduce the need for frequent cartridge replacements. It’s an upgrade they’ll appreciate long after the holidays.
