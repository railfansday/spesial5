Ray Tintori, Music Video Director and Filmmaker, Dies at 43

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The helmer was the boyfriend of 'SNL' alum Heidi Gardner

Ray Tintori, who directed music videos for bands including Arcade Fire and MGMT and worked on the film “Beasts of the Southern Wild,” has died in New York. The New York City Office of Chief Medical Examiner said the death is under investigation and the cause is unknown.

Tintori was one of the founders of Court 13, the filmmaking collective that made “Beasts of the Southern Wild.” He served as special effects unit director on the film which was nominated for four Oscars. The director and screenwriter, who was the boyfriend of “Saturday Night Live” alum Heidi Gardner, also directed music videos for Chairlift, Bonnie Prince Billy, The Cool Kids, The Killers and Solange.

Most recently, he co-directed a performance of Ethan Coen’s play “Waiting” in New York with Jordan Fish, with whom he hosted the podcast “To the White Sea” about the unproduced Coen brothers screenplay.

In Josh Safdie’s “Marty Supreme,” Tintori had a cameo as a newsreel photographer.

Prior to the release of “Marty Supreme,” Tintori posted on his Instagram, “Sincere thanks to Josh Safdie, Eli Bush, and Jeremy Marks for a true dream filmmaking/acting role in Marty Supreme, shooting the black and white 16mm footage for the newsreel sequence while appearing in the film as a cameraman in the Wembley finals.

“This was a super fun callback to the 16mm unit work that Josh, myself, Jordan Fish, Jeff Delauter, Dave MacNutt and Bob Weisz did on Ang Lee’s ‘Taking Woodstock,’ where we embedded with the cast, in costume, shooting handheld footage that was cut into the film. This time my role was Newsreel Unit Producer. I worked again with master cinematographer Ryan Marie Helfant. For certain setups we used Josh’s old crank Bolex camera that the early Red Bucket Films shorts were shot on. Ryan used an Arriflex 16SR and I operated an Arriflex 16S. Our unit also filmed the scene of Endo working in the clock factory,” he wrote.

Tintori’s thesis film “Death to the Tinman” premiered at Sundance, winning a short filmmaking award. He worked in a number of different areas on various films and television shows, including acting, special effects and in the art department on projects such as “Eon Flux.”

The son of film editor and NYU Kanbar Institute chair John Tintori and the late script supervisor Mary Cybulski, he graduated from Wesleyan University.
