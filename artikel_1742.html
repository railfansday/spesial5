Bradford City boss Michael Duff on why he felt the nerves in victory over Barnsley FC

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Leading thanks to Stephen Humphrys’ early goal, City failed to add to their tally by the interval against a rock-bottom Reds side who were very unconvincing at the back, especially in the first half.

A spirited start to the second period from a Reds side without a win in their previous 10 league games created a sense of unease at Valley Parade.

But a second goal midway through the second half from ex-Barnsley forward Humphrys settled the contest. Substitute Jake Beesley added a stoppage-time penalty.

Duff said: “I was disappointed at half-time because I thought we should have been put the game to bed.

"I didn't think we started the game particularly well, but Humps showed a little bit of quality that he’s got.

"We lost too many duels in the first half. We just felt that once we won a duel and made a couple of passes, we got in.

"If I was playing centre-half in the team, I’d have picked one of the forwards up and throttled him at half-time.

"You live on a knife-edge constantly, and the longer it goes on, you feel the nerves in the stadium.

"We weren't particularly good today, but I don't think we're in a position where we can start turning our nose up with 3-0 wins at home.

"We were better on Saturday and drew. The positive is it’s the first time we’ve scored three goals in 51 league games and it’s one defeat in 17 now.

"We need to be more clinical. You're not always going to create that many chances in the game.

"The forwards know that. Like I said, it's not a nice feeling living on a knife-edge, living on a bounce of a ball or an overhead kick, like we were on Saturday.

"We have to put the game to bed and I think once the second goal went in, it calmed a little bit.

"Barnsley are random in the way that they play. They put people in strange positions and it's really difficult to play against.

"But when you do turn it over, they're wide open. So, I just felt that's where those duels were important.

"I know people will say they're bottom of the league but it's not easy winning a game of football. We've seen that.

"I watched them play Sheffield Wednesday last Friday, and after the first 10 minutes, they dominated the game.

"We have to be respectful of what we're up against and be humble enough to go, ‘we can improve’.
