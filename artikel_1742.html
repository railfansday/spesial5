Arsenal stars ‘not comfortable’ with teammate who could start against Man City

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Arsenal could be without David Raya against Manchester City this weekend with former striker Paul Dickov suggesting the Gunners’ back line will not be ‘comfortable’ with Kepa Arrizabalaga stepping in as his understudy.

Raya was forced off in the second-half of last Saturday’s 2-2 draw with Newcastle United, leaving the pitch in agony after suffering a shoulder injury.

The sight of the Spainard making his way off the pitch in clear distress sparked huge concerns over a player who has been described as ‘irreplaceable’ in Mikel Arteta’s starting XI.

The Arsenal boss revealed this week Raya will not require surgery which would have sidelined him for months. While the club are still to disclose the exact nature of the injury or a timeline for his return, Raya may be considered a major doubt for the visit of Enzo Maresca’s side.

City meanwhile have fallen off the pace, without a win in their last four in the Premier League having lost three on the bounce in that run. But they could very well take encouragement from Raya’s absence.

Kepa was selected ahead of the Arsenal no1 in the Carabao Cup final last March having started every previous round of the competition.

The former Chelsea shot stopper gifted City their opening goal that afternoon, dropping a cross into the path of Nico O’Reilly who headed home.

Former striker Dickov, who began his career at Arsenal before achieving legend status at City, believes Raya will be a massive loss for the champions this weekend. While convinced Arteta has full trust in Kepa, he suggests Arsenal’s backline will privately have concerns.

‘The one thing City will definitely be looking at is the loss of David Raya who is the best goalkeeper in the world for me,’ Dickov told Metro via Casino.com Ireland ahead of Sunday’s showdown in the capital.
