Tactics Column: Midfield carousel unlocks evolution

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Anyone else quickly want to think about the Premier League again after Tuesday’s League Cup defeat? Good! We have just the thing for you.

Much has been said about Arsenal’s football over the past few weeks, but Saturday’s win over Everton was a clear step forward in terms of playing against a defensive setup and consistently getting into dangerous areas.

With no Bukayo Saka, who has been the team’s biggest goal threat this season, Mikel Merino came into midfield to play behind Viktor Gyokeres, allowing Martin Odegaard to roam deeper. It was Odegaard’s constant roaming in midfield, with Rice advancing ahead of the ball, and link-up play with Myles Lewis-Skelly, tucking into a midfield role from left-back, that helped stretch Everton and create plenty of opportunities.

Those combinations didn’t directly lead to a goal, but they helped Arsenal dominate and threaten throughout the 2-0 win. A PSG-like fluidity in midfield and plenty of vertical passing looks like a real step forward for this Arsenal side and should make the team a lot more exciting to watch.
