Theo Walcott sends message to ‘frustrated’ Arsenal star after Borussia Dortmund draw

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Walcott has urged Arsenal star Noni Madueke to ‘big himself up more’ after Mikel Arteta revealed last night’s man of the match was frustrated in the wake of a 1-1 draw against Borussia Dortmund.

For the second match in succession, the Premier League champions threw away a lead and squandered numerous chances to record a convincing win.

Viktor Gyokeres opened the scoring with a blistering strike in the 10th minute, but the Sweden international, like many of his teammates, was profligate thereafter.

Ultimately, the Gunners were forced to settle for a point following Maximilian Beier’s second half equaliser and Mikel Arteta’s side are now likely to require two wins from their remaining three group stage games to secure a top eight finish and avoid a two-legged play-off in the New Year.

Madueke, meanwhile, walked away with the man of the match accolade after another lively display. The England international has been to the fore in recent weeks and, in the absence of Bukayo Saka, appears to have won the faith of the manager who raised eyebrows with his decision to sanctionthe decision to sign him from Chelsea in a £52million deal 18 months ago.

The 24-year-old was, however, far from pleased with a display that failed to help yield a win that Arsenal’s dominance deserved, according to Arteta.

‘Again he was really good, but if you were in the dressing room (you would see) he is not satisfied because he knows he had so many situations again that we can put the ball in the net or finish the action much better,’ said the Arsenal boss.

‘But again he was a real threat, he was super connected to the game with and without the ball. He was a real threat.’

Walcott, meanwhile, agreed with his former Arsenal teammate and believes Madueke is showing signs that he is capable of maintaining a regular starting spot.
