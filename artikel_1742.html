Omar Marmoush injury update as new Tottenham footage emerges

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Fears that Omar Marmoush has picked up an injury have been allayed after the Tottenham forward was pictured in training ahead of Sunday’s trip to Sunderland.

Concerns had grown online this week that Marmoush was set for a period on the sidelines, with speculation he was set to visit a knee specialist.

The 27-year-old came off the bench during Spurs’ 2-0 victory at home to Ipswich last weekend and did not appear to pick up an injury, as Roberto De Zerbi’s side returned to winning ways at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

Inevitably, though, concerns had gained traction and Marmoush’s involvement against Sunderland had been considered doubtful.

Spurs have dismissed the claims as online speculation, and footage of Marmoush on the Hotspur Way training pitches was posted on the club’s social media on Thursday.

In a major boost for De Zerbi, who is currently sweating over the fitness of several attacking players, the Egyptian international now appears fit and available for selection.

Spurs are expected to be without wingers Mykhailo Mudryk, Mathys Tel and Savio against Sunderland, so Marmoush being able to deputise on the flanks as well as through the middle will give De Zerbi more options heading into the weekend.

Mudryk is still struggling with an ankle ligament injury, with De Zerbi suggesting last week the Chelsea loanee had suffered a setback in his recovery.

Tel, meanwhile, is expected to be out for up to three weeks after sustaining an ankle injury against Leeds before the international week.

With Mudryk and Tel absent against Ipswich, De Zerbi selected Savio in a matchday squad for the first time since October 10 after he suffered a muscular injury in training.

The Brazilian was introduced from the bench in the 64th minute, but had to come off again after suffering a recurrence of the muscular injury that had kept him out for the last six weeks.
