GTA 6 breaks the all-time livestream viewer record hours before it's available to play for most of the world

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A week dedicated to all things Grand Theft Auto, as the long-awaited GTA 6 finally arrives.

Hours before it's even playable in most of the world, Grand Theft Auto 6 has already broken the record for the most viewers across livestreaming platforms like Twitch.

According to streaming data and analytics website Streams Charts, GTA 6 had amassed 6.38m live viewers across Twitch, YouTube, and Kick at 12:30 UTC today. At that time, the game had been officially available for a few hours in New Zealand, and elsewhere by using a region-switch trick.

The previous record for most live viewers was set by Valorant, Riot Game's popular tactical shooter, during its closed beta in xx. That game brought in 1.74m live viewers, but it's worth noting there was a promotion offering players a chance to access the beta if they watched a stream. Despite having no such incentive, GTA 6 has more than tripled Valorant's record.

Twitch is the largest platform for GTA 6 streams according to Streams Charts, and already the game is the 6th biggest category in Twitch history. It's also expected the figure will increase as GTA 6 launches officially in more regions of the world.
