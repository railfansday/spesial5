GTA 6 Players Captivated By Mandy Apparently Include A Former Nintendo President

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A character named Mandy has the attention of many Grand Theft Auto 6 players--and that appears to include Reggie Fils-Aimé, the man who used to run Nintendo of America.

Per GamesRadar, former Nintendo marketing and public relations manager Krysta Yang joked on social media that GTA 6, which is front-loaded with a lot of simulation-like busywork in the first chapter, is "adult Animal Crossing," posting images that highlight parallels between GTA 6 and Animal Crossing: New Horizons. Fils-Aimé quoted the post, saying: "If so, where's Mandy?"

It's slightly eyebrow raising that the former executive for a family-friendly company like Nintendo seems to have a strong opinion for a rather promiscuous video game character. No judgment to Fils-Aimé, of course, but talk around Mandy carries a certain... tone. Minor spoilers for the early parts of GTA 6 follow.

One of the earliest missions in GTA 6 has Jason stopping by Mandy's home to collect rent. Long story short, she flirts pretty aggressively with Jason, who clearly isn't reciprocating, and Mandy doesn't take it well. And no, even if players wanted to, you can't romance Mandy.

It doesn't help that it's implied that Mandy carries a dark secret, with Jason discovering a severed finger while fixing her sink's pipes. I'm not sure if anyone in Animal Crossing is that shady.

So what does this post say about Reggie Fils-Aimé as a person? It's unclear what he thinks of the character--all we know is that he'd really like to know who the Animal Crossing equivalent of Mandy would be. It certainly wouldn't be Isabelle, so take your pick of any weird villagers you've encountered. Either way, it seems that Fils-Aimé is playing GTA 6 like the rest of us or, at the very least, is in tune with the massive cultural zeitgeist the game has produced.
