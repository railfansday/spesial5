World of Warcraft: Forever has more weekly players than WoW has seen in 15 years, says Blizzard

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

World of Warcraft: Forever now has more people playing each week than World of Warcraft has seen in 15 years, according to Blizzard.

"Another fun statistic for you! #WoWForever has more people playing weekly than we've seen in World of Warcraft in 15 years!" executive producer and World of Warcraft vice president Holly Longdale posted. "Thank you all for joining us in Azeroth. We're doing this together, for Forever!"

Longdale was quoting a post from the official World of Warcraft account, which shared a batch of "By the Numbers" stats for the new edition.

Since it launched on 4th November, players have created 15,160,147 characters and 140,764 guilds, completed 1,480,343,357 quests, and defeated 9,786,925,806 enemies.

They've also formed 71,309,889 parties, used 28,696,386 camping items, and there have been 28,938,569 PvP kills.

Collectively, we've died a lot, too - 196,851,761 times in total, with Archmage Arugal, the final boss of Shadowfang Keep, seemingly the deadliest boss.

"You're breaking records by the day!" the post read.

Earlier this month, Blizzard said WoW: Forever was the biggest launch in World of Warcraft history by peak concurrent players, and that it had brought in the highest number of returning players the game has ever seen.

Longdale added at the time that it had pulled in the most new players for a WoW launch in 15 years. It has since suffered its first controversy, though, with a now-fixed gold exploit.

Last week, players pieced together a hidden quest chain that leads to two special swords. At the time of writing, only one has been found, though progress continues in pursuit of the second.

The game has been out for just over two weeks now, and continues to prove popular among new and returning players.
