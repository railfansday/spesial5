Chad Lowe Honors Late Daughter Fiona on What Would've Been Her 14th Birthday

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Chad Lowe has marked what would have been the 14th birthday of his late daughter, Fiona.

“With broken hearts we honor and celebrate our beloved Fiona on what would’ve been her 14th birthday. We miss her more than words could ever express. Our love is eternal,” Chad, 58, wrote via Instagram on Sunday, November 15. “Thank you all for your love, support, and prayers.”

The actor also shared a photo of Fiona performing a beautiful jeté leap in the air. Dressed in an ethereal yellow dress and ballet slippers, the late teenager’s hair flowed wildly.

Lowe and his wife, Kim Painter, announced on September 29 that Fiona had died by suicide at 13.

“We are deeply heartbroken by the loss of our beloved Fiona,” Lowe and Painter, 52, wrote in a joint statement, calling Fiona “a loving young girl who was smart, creative and loved to dance.” (Lowe and Painter also share daughters Mabel, 17, and Nixie, 10.)

Lowe’s father subsequently mourned the loss of his granddaughter in a separate statement.

“Rest in peace sweet Fiona,” Chad’s dad, Chuck Lowe, succinctly captioned an Instagram photo of the late teenager in a pool.

One year earlier, Chad celebrated Fiona’s 13th birthday with a social media tribute.

“Celebrating Fiona officially becoming a teen today!!” Lowe wrote via Instagram in November 2025. “Keep dancing through life FiBear! Love you soooo much!! ❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️❤️.”

The actor had previously been candid about his life as a “girl dad.”

“I’m a girl dad. It’s so interesting because [I’m] always wanting to have my own experiences and not live in the shadow or follow in the footsteps of somebody,” the Pretty Little Liars alum said on the “Still Here” podcast in 2024. “I feel like having daughters is such an incredible privilege.”

Chad, the younger brother of Rob Lowe, further noted how raising daughters shifted his mindset.

“I look at the world from really their perspective, and I see the world and a lot of the injustices in the world from their perspective. It’s not all doom and gloom,” Chad said at the time. “Society is changing. I see how we’re really becoming conscious of the role of women. We may, who knows, have the first female president.”

He added, “[We’re] really empowering women and seeing how much women have to offer. I personally think that if women were in charge of nations, we’d be a lot more peaceful.”
