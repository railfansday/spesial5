Rangers handed quadruple injury boost as comeback dates revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Daisuke Yokota is closing in on a return as Rangers manage several injury concerns.

The Japanese winger is expected to be back by the end of November as he continues his rehabilitation.

Ross McCrorie is further ahead in his recovery and could return to training next week, around the beginning of the international break.

Providing an update on the pair, Rangers boss Derek McInnes said: “Well, it’s certainly a good carrot for them to aim towards. But Yokota should be back with us by the end of the month.

“Ross McCrorie will be back with us at the beginning of next week, maybe at the start of the international break, doing some training with us. So there's two, there's a wee bit of optimism.”

Cammy Devlin and Lawrence Shankland are also making progress, with both targeting a return to the pitch during the first week of December.

That leaves the pair facing a race against time to be available for the League Cup final.

McInnes said: “I think Cammy and Lawrence will probably both look to be on pitch the first week of December.

“But there's still a bit to do, still a few hurdles to overcome. But I think if everything goes well for them, then there is an opportunity maybe to be available for that game.

“But I think we just need to let them be for now and see where it is. But the fact it's a cup final, it doesn't do them any harm in terms of pushing them on.”

Badredine Bouanani, meanwhile, has reported some illness as a bug continues to work its way through the Rangers squad.

However, the winger is expected to be fine for the weekend. He is due back in on Thursday and should return to training on Friday ahead of Saturday night’s Premiership clash against St Mirren.

Emmanuel Fernandez is also expected to be available despite picking up an ankle problem in Rangers’ win over Aberdeen at Hampden.

The defender’s ankle was described as “a bit of a mess” in the aftermath of Saturday’s match, but he is due to train on Thursday and is expected to be fit for the trip to Paisley.

Yokota’s progress will provide further encouragement for McInnes, with the winger potentially only a matter of weeks away from his return.
