United Airlines Opens First New Concourse In 20 Years At Washington Dulles Airport

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

It's no secret that United Airlines is by far the dominant operator at Washington Dulles International Airport (IAD). According to data from Cirium, an aviation analytics company, the US 'big three' legacy carrier and Star Alliance founding member accounts for 7,830 of the 10,336 flights that are scheduled to depart from Dulles this month (76%). This includes those operated by its regional partners.

The airline's commitment to the airport was underlined on Monday as it previewed Concourse E, a brand-new part of Dulles that marks its first new concourse in close to 20 years. United Airlines unveiled the 435,000-square-foot (40,413-square-meter) concourse with the Metropolitan Washington Airports Authority, with this development marking part of a wider $20 billion investment in the carrier's DC hub.

Following Monday's preview, Concourse E will officially open to the public on Tuesday. In a corresponding statement that it released earlier today, United Airlines said that, among other things, this new area will offer "14 new gates, direct AeroTrain access, and new dining and retail options" to its passengers flying from and through the capital. As a result, it will be able to operate 300+ daily flights next year.

The upshot of this is the fact that United Airlines will subsequently be able to "grow its connectivity to other markets by nearly 30% during peak travel times, giving customers more flights and new destinations from Dulles." Concourse E will continue to grow, with United targeting 36 more gates and an international customs processing area in future phases. Scott Kirby, the CEO of United Airlines, said that:

"It's only the beginning as we work with the Airports Authority and the US Department of Transportation to transform Dulles into one of the greatest airports in the world."

Taking up almost 10% of the new real estate in Concourse E, arguably the most impressive aspect of this latest expansion is the new United Club lounge. Located across from gates E14 and E16 and open every day from 5:30 AM to 10:00 PM, this sprawling facility measures close to 40,000 square feet (3,617 square meters), making it the second-largest Club lounge in the United network.

Such is its size that United's overall lounge footprint at Dulles is set to grow by some 70% upon its public opening on Tuesday. This, the airline says, will "further elevate the experience for United customers traveling through the hub." Drawing upon the city's character, it features a collection of specially curated artwork from local artists, which will "bring a distinct sense of place to the travel experience."

In terms of what visitors can expect, United promises impressive connectivity, with "complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi and convenient power access at nearly every seat." Eight private phone rooms are also available for more discreet conversations. As always, food and drink will play an important role in bringing this lounge experience to life, with a bar and two buffet stations ensuring that flyers stay refreshed.

Dulles just tripled its modernization budget to more than $20 billion, with Concourse C and D set for replacement.

As previously noted, United accounts for 76% of Dulles' departures this month, with a grand total of some 7,830 flights scheduled. Of these, 1,058 are international, with Toronto (YYZ) being the most frequently served foreign destination (three times a day). United also flies twice a day from Dulles to Cancun (CUN), Frankfurt (FRA), London (LHR), Montreal (YUL), Ottawa (YOW), and Punta Cana (PUJ).

Domestically speaking, San Francisco (SFO) is United's top destination from Dulles, with up to nine flights a day. Elsewhere, Denver (DEN) peaks at eight daily rotations, compared to seven for Chicago O'Hare (ORD) and Los Angeles (LAX). While the scale of United's ops at Dulles is certainly impressive, the DC airport is actually only its sixth-busiest hub by flights. Chicago O'Hare sits first, with 18,920.
