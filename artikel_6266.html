Transfer Citi ThankYou Points To Turkish Miles&Smiles With 40% Bonus: Worth It?

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

At the moment, it’s possible to earn up to a 40% bonus when you transfer Citi ThankYou points to Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles. However, the bonus isn’t being offered on Citi’s end, but instead, is being offered on Turkish Airlines’ end.

Between November 1 and November 30, 2026, it’s possible to receive a 40% bonus when you move points from the Citi ThankYou program in the United States to Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles. However, there are some important terms to be aware of:

Ordinarily points transfer at a 1:1 ratio (in 1,000 point increments), while through this promotion you’d get 1,400 Miles&Smiles miles for every 1,000 Citi ThankYou points transferred, though only after the fact.

There are several great cards earning Citi ThankYou points, including the Citi Strata Premier® Card (learn more) and Citi Strata Elite℠ Card (learn more), and it’s easy to combine points between the cards.

Back in the day, the Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles program offered incredible redemption values across the board, but that’s no longer really the case. At this point, I’d say the sweet spot with the Miles&Smiles program is if you’re looking to travel on Turkish Airlines specifically to or from Istanbul.

That’s because the Miles&Smiles program now charges per segment for awards on Turkish Airlines metal. You can find the cost of one-way saver awards below, just to give you a sense of pricing.

So as an example, a one-way business class award between the United States and Istanbul will cost you 65,000 miles. In other words, with a 40% transfer bonus, you’d be paying around 47,000 Citi ThankYou points for a business class award, which is a pretty great deal.

If you know what you’re doing and have specific redemption plans in mind, this could be a great deal. Just be sure you understand the complexities of the program, and read my guide to the Turkish Miles&Smiles program. Also keep in mind the Miles&Smiles expiration policy, which is that miles expire at the end of the third year after which they’re earned, regardless of activity.

The other big restriction is that the bonus miles will only post after the campaign is done, so you couldn’t transfer points and then immediately redeem all of them (including the bonus). The cap on 50,000 bonus miles is also limiting. But hey, if you value Turkish miles, this could definitely offer some value.

Between November 1 and November 30, 2026, it’s possible to receive a 40% bonus when you move points from Citi ThankYou to Turkish Airlines Miles&Smiles. The catch is that the bonus miles only post after the end of the campaign, and you can only earn 50,000 bonus miles with this offer.

If you’re looking to travel on Turkish Airlines to or from Istanbul, there are some good deals to be had by taking advantage of this.
