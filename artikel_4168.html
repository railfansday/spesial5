8 Reasons Jason and Lucia’s Relationship is Better Than Yours

Do you think you have a good relationship? Do you and your partner get along, and are you both happy? Do you have trust, and are you both still making each other laugh? If you are iffy on any of those questions, Lucia and Jason may just have a better relationship than you.

Grand Theft Auto 6 puts a big emphasis on the relationship between Jason and Lucia, with it being a key part of the story. From the very start of the game, you need to put them first if you want them to have a strong connection.

Well, their relationship is good. They can trust each other, and they will put their lives on the line to keep one another safe. Can you say the same?

A key part of learning about your partner is asking them questions. You need to have a sense of interest in your significant other so that they know you truly love them. Without any type of interest, you end up becoming stale.

That is not a problem for Lucia and Jason, as they ask each other questions all the time. If you simply drive around the city, you will hear conversations between the two of them where Lucia will ask Jason how he ended up in the military.

You will also hear Jason ask Lucia about her past, about prison, and about their son. This interest shows how much they care about each other and want to know more about one another.

Trust is a key part of any relationship. If you don’t trust the person you are with, odds are you won’t make it very long. It is one of the most core tenets of any relationship, and Jason and Lucia show that they can trust each other constantly.

They put their lives on the line every time they step out of their apartment. They are constantly dealing with drug lords, gang members, and more, so they need someone they can trust, and they can trust each other.

Both of them know that the other one has their back, and when one of them makes a strong move, the other is there to back them up.

The internet made a huge stink about not being able to cheat on Lucia as Jason. Many people kept saying they wanted to see what a relationship with Mandy would be like. It would probably be terrible, but they were interested.

However, the game not having a cheat mechanic in it just shows the strength of Jason and Lucia’s relationship. You can’t betray Lucia. You can’t go behind her back. You are required to stay loyal, but that is also the kind of guy Jason is.

Jason wouldn’t go behind someone’s back, no, he is too good for that. He is the type of guy who would rather break it off if things weren’t going right than go behind someone’s back.

GTA 6 removes the ability solicit, a GTA feature since 2001. Rockstar says the decision supports Jason and Lucia’s story.

If you want to get to know your partner, and you want to have a solid foundation, you need to spend quality time together doing things that you both enjoy. This can be a hobby like bowling, mini-golfing, or maybe even robbing banks.

For Jason and Lucia, you have the option to have them spend time together constantly. They are able to go for walks around Vice City, take a drive, or participate in any of the various side activities found throughout the game.

This type of constant quality time is great for a relationship, and it is okay to spend time apart. After all, you can split up from each other in the game, but the quality time is there, and it builds their relationship.

Communication is everything when it comes to being in a relationship. You don’t want to keep secrets, and you always want to check in to make sure that your partner is okay. This style of communication is key to making things work.

Jason and Lucia are a model example of this. When playing as either of them, you have the option to check in with the other to see how they are doing. Your partner will also sometimes send you a text or give you a call to check in.

This type of relationship works because they keep a constant line of communication. They share group chats, and they also talk about what happened when they were split up.

Let's face it, in GTA 6, Lucia is not the greatest role model. In fact, she has some major red flags.

While it isn’t necessary for you and your partner to have the same exact goals in life, it certainly does help to make things operate more smoothly. For instance, if you don’t want kids but your partner does, that can cause a rift.

Jason and Lucia don’t need to worry about this rift forming as they know exactly why they are doing what they are doing, and they have the same goals. They both want to provide for their child, and they don’t want to be criminals forever.

They are criminals now to get enough money to live a good life later on. After that, they don’t need to keep going, as they will be able to live happily as a family.

A nice thing about being in a relationship is that you have somebody you can connect with - somebody that can make you smile, laugh, and feel a range of emotions. To keep things going well, you need to keep up the humor, you need to be flirty, and you need to be playful.

The relationship between Lucia and Jason in GTA 6 isn’t stale. They are consistently making each other laugh, flirting with one another, and more. They know how to make each other smile.

You can complete a job, and the two of them will joke about something, or you can make it back to the apartment, and they will be intimate. You can also relax in bed staring at your phone. There is a connection, and it is strong.

This doesn’t apply to everyone. After all, not many couples out there are actively committing crime together and bailing each other out. Jason and Lucia have a bond that is forged in fire and a hailstorm of bullets. I wish I was joking.

Their relationship may seem okay at first, but once you are out of chapter one, you see how Lucia goes the extra mile for Jason. She puts her life on the line for him, and he does the same in return.

The two are willing to die for each other if it means that their partner stays safe. Those aren’t just words to them. Each time they go out, they are risking their lives for each other, and it shows how madly in love they are.

Grand Theft Auto 6 puts a large emphasis on keeping your relationship sound, so you need to know what ways are the best to raise the romance between Jason and Lucia.
