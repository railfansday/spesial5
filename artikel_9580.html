Not Regular Butter: Popcorn Is Crispier When You Reach For This Ingredient

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Few snacks welcome modifications more readily than popcorn. You can go with a sweet cinnamon sugar recipe or a batch of spicy, chile-powder-covered kernels. Regardless of which direction you tweak the dish, fat is always welcome — it helps keep your seasoning on, but it's also simply delicious. Butter is the classic choice, but don't sleep on ghee, either. This foodstuff makes for an even crispier batch of the iconic movie theater snack.

Also a dairy-based fat, ghee is clarified, which means the ingredient contains less water and more fat solids than butter. Ghee is further differentiated from clarified butter by way of the toasting process, which gives ghee a pleasant nutty flavor. Together, such qualities enhance popcorn in both taste and texture. Amplified crunch is the main draw. Once melted and applied to kernels, this translates to a much less soggy consistency. The popcorn still takes on a signature buttery flavor, but with less moisture clinging to each bite, the increased fat composition turns the snack extra crispy.

Thankfully, working ghee into a batch of popcorn is straightforward. You can readily buy a jar of ghee on platforms like Amazon or Walmart, add around a tablespoon per a third cup of kernels, then pop away into a reimagined crunchy bite. It's a simple twist with a satisfying payoff.

Ghee popcorn doesn't need much to shine; a simple sprinkle of salt rounds out a mouthwatering rendition of the snack. However, if you're a fan of a more complex popcorn rendition, don't shy away from bolder seasonings, too. Ghee is often used in South Asian cuisine, which means Indian spices are a natural start. The nutty notes make a terrific canvas for warm flavors like cinnamon, coriander, and cumin. Or for an even more dessert-like spin, mix in saffron, cardamom, and fennel — spices traditionally used to flavor nuts. Whipping up such blends is like making a spice-infused oil: You'll want to slowly bloom the components in the ghee.

That said, don't rule out drawing from other cuisines with ghee, too. The milk-based fat can be the foundation of a smoky-sweet seasoning flavored with chile peppers and vanilla. Or you can incorporate a zesty complexity by using citrus rind instead. In addition to crisping up popcorn, ghee works as a remarkable flavor canvas, readily enveloping the snack in aromatics.
