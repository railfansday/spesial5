Exclusive Dewsbury-Hall Interview Headlines Merseyside Derby Programme

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

An exclusive interview with Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall headlines this weekend’s matchday programme for the Merseyside derby.

The in-depth feature includes the Blues midfielder discussing his strong form for David Moyes’ side, what the famous fixture means to him and his reaction praise from the red side of the city.

Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall played an integral role in the historic first derby match hosted at Hill Dickinson Stadium, producing a slide-rule cross for Beto to become the first Everton goalscorer at our new home in a derby match.

The occasion lived up to expectations.

“It was electric,” Dewsbury-Hall tells the Official Matchday Programme. “It was amazing to be part of the first one at the new stadium.

“The atmosphere was electric, it was mental. It was feisty in the first half, with a lot of crunching tackles and a lot of physicality. The last minute apart, it was a good occasion to be a part of.

“The Merseyside derby is an iconic fixture and being someone who has lived and breathed football my whole life I’ve watched many, many over the years.

“It’s always a feisty game! It’s always a game which is exciting, so it was a nice feeling to know I’d be involved in them when I signed for Everton.

“The two I played in last year were pretty exciting. Obviously, they didn’t end the best for us, especially with the last-minute goal here. But for me it was a taste of what the fixture was like. So I’m very excited to experience it again.”

The fixture in April was the 214th league clash between the two old rivals, but the first not to have been staged at either Anfield or Goodison Park.

All Premier League matches are famously competitive, but Dewsbury-Hall admits there is definitely something special when Everton and Liverpool collide.

“It is a little bit different,” he admits. “Of course it’s a high-profile fixture, but there’s the added incentive of it being a derby. Naturally it gets spoken about before the game, how important it is.

“I mean, there’s a lot of staff here who support the Club. They let you know exactly what it means to Everton.

"I knew that anyway, but they’re all like, ‘Come on! It’s a Merseyside derby! As long as we beat Liverpool!’ So, there’s always that added incentive.

“As players we know the importance of the fixture – if you can win this game it’s significant, because notoriously, over the past couple of years, Liverpool have been higher than us in the table. But we’re trying to close that gap now.”
