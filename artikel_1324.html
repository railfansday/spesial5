Iraola confirms ‘very good news’ after Leoni return to Liverpool training

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Liverpool head coach Andoni Iraola says that it is ‘very good news’ to have Italy talent Giovanni Leoni back in training after a nightmare 14-month injury lay-off, but has confirmed that the 19-year-old will not be available to face Manchester United this weekend.

Leoni has recently returned to training with the Liverpool first team after suffering an injury on his first appearance for the club well over a year ago.

Though Leoni was originally expected to miss around nine months of action with what was originally reported as a cruciate ligament (ACL) injury, Iraola later explained that it was a complex issue with the youngster’s knee and that he hoped to see the former Parma defender return to training during the November international break.

It has been 14 months since Leoni’s first and only appearance for Liverpool, in an EFL Cup second round victory over Southampton last September.

Liverpool have recently shared footage of Leoni back in training with the Liverpool first team squad at the AXA Training Center in Kirkby.

Iraola was asked for an update on Leoni’s progress during his pre-match press conference on Friday, ahead of Liverpool’s upcoming Premier League clash against Manchester United.

“Very good news from both sides, the break was the best moment for us to introduce them slowly,” said Iraola, referring to Leoni and teammate Conor Bradley.

“They have started doing things with the team, not available for Sunday and we treat them carefully.

“We are in the last part of this process and I hope they can play in some weeks.”

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
