Manchester United and England legend Wayne Rooney went viral on social media after completing a remarkable body transformation earlier in the Premier League season, and has now lifted the lid on his secrets ahead of his highly-anticipated appearance on this year's I'm a Celeb.

Rooney is widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers of his era and deservedly so.

Man Utd’s all-time top scorer (253 goals), it’s fair to say the Liverpool-born forward justified the hype, and his £30 million price tag, after Sir Alex Ferguson brought him to Old Trafford from Everton in 2004.

This was the same year Rooney announced himself to the world with a series of stunning performances for England as a raw 18-year-old at Euro 2004.

Eleven years later, Rooney surpassed the legendary Sir Bobby Charlton to become England’s all-time leading goalscorer.

Although the 41-year-old has since been leapfrogged by Harry Kane, his status as a true great of English football has been cemented forever.

Despite his legendary status, Rooney admitted to his former teammate Rio Ferdinand in 2025 how he’s always been “insecure” about his weight, struggling with his body image even during his playing days.

“I’m actually very insecure about a number of things. I have been my whole career over my weight, over how I look,” Rooney confessed.

“Even when I was in the best shape I was in as a football player, I wouldn’t take my top off."

He added: "I was strong, I was powerful. I never had abs but I was powerful - the best body you need to be a footballer.”

However, after it was announced that Rooney would follow his wife Coleen by taking part in ITV’s ‘I’m a Celebrity… Get Me Out of Here!’ later this year, the retired striker has wowed fans by showing off his impressive new physique.

Rooney - who recently underwent surgery on his nose, affecting his voice for a little while on Match of the Day - appeared on the red carpet for the premiere of his new Disney+ reality show The Real Rooneys in tip-top physical shape.

He turnd 41 in October, but Rooney appears to be in the best shape since his playing days. In fact, it looks like he could probably still do a job for struggling Manchester United upfront.

Lifting the lid on the things he did to make sure he was as fit as possible for the jungle, Rooney told The Sun that his marriage with Coleen is in a “better place” after cutting out booze for the past six months and losing more than three stone to prepare for the Ant and Dec staple show.

Speaking ahead of entering the camp, the dad-of-four said: “I have stopped drinking for six months now. To be honest, I am probably eating the healthiest I have ever eaten, training and getting back into a routine so it all ties in and all helps. I think I am in a really good place, a really happy place.”

He also said his fresher complexion is down to ditching the booze. “I think losing weight helps with your skin and not drinking helps from a health point of view.”

Coleen won the hearts of the nation when she finished second on the show behind McFly guitarist Danny Jones in 2024. Of her advice, her husband said: “To just be myself. I know quite a few people who have been in there as well as Coleen and I think you have to experience it for yourself and that is what Coleen said.”

Asked about her final words before he got on the plane, he joked: “She just told me to put my seat-belt on when I am on the plane.” Meanwhile, on the topic of outdoing Coleen by winning the title, he said: “There is no competition. I was very proud of Coleen and her time in the jungle. I thought she did brilliantly, but there is a different set of people in there, so our one could be completely different.

“If I was to win it, then great, and, if not, I have always wanted to do the show. As I said, there is no competition — but I would like to do a little bit better than her.”
