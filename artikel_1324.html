Milan could bring forward Tresoldi move as Club Brugge demand €40m

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Milan could bring forward a move for Club Brugge striker Nicolò Tresoldi to January as they look for cover for Gonçalo Ramos.

According to Corriere dello Sport, Tresoldi is one of Gerry Cardinale’s favourite targets, and incoming head of football Markus Krösche also rates him highly. Brugge won’t sell him in January for less than €40m.

Ramos is seen as Milan’s No 9 now and in the future. Francesco Camarda is expected to go out on loan, with several Serie B clubs already interested, so the Rossoneri will need a new back-up striker. If they can’t find a cheap loan option, they could move early for Tresoldi.

It wouldn’t be the first time Milan have looked at him. In 2021, when he was in Hannover’s youth system, Paolo Maldini and Ricky Massara discussed contract terms with him, but they chose Pietro Pellegri instead.

Five years on, the Cagliari-born forward is one of Europe’s most sought-after young strikers. He scored a Champions League hat-trick against Inter at San Siro in October, and both Italy and Germany want him.

Tresoldi is also a lifelong Milan fan. “My dream is this: for me, the ultimate is Milan,” he said in January.
