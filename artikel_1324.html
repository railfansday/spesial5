Why Serie A players are wearing red paint on their faces in Week 12 fixtures

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Serie A players and coaches will be donning a splash red paint across their cheeks during the 12th round of league fixtures coming up this weekend to raise awareness for the ‘Un Rosso alla Violenza (‘a red card to violence’) campaign.

This will be the 10th time that Serie A has participated in the Rosso alla Violenza campaign, which is aimed to raise awareness around domestic violence towards women.

A number of gestures to raise awareness for the campaign are planned for the 12th round of Serie A fixtures: Players, coaches and officials will don the red face paint, and for the first time, supporters in the stands have been encouraged to participate as well.

Serie A captains will also wear a symbolic armband during Week 12. The hashtag #unrossoallaviolenza and several adverts and graphics will be shown in the stadiums and will be broadcast on TV ahead of kick-off.

“Serie A has been at the forefront of the fight against violence towards women for 10 years, and coincide with the international day for the elimination of violence against women during the 12th round of league fixtures, we will renew our determination and commitment to combating all forms of abuse and especially towards women,” said Lega Serie A President Ezio Simonelli.

Marco Chiesara, President of WeWorld, who organise the campaign, added: “Violence against women is not a private matter and does not only concern those who suffer from it. It is a collective responsibility. With this campaign, we want to reach a wide audience, particularly men, and invite them to question their own role in combating it.”

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
