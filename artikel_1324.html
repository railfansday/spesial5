Chiellini on Premier League economic power, Italy problems and England pressure

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Giorgio Chiellini warns that the Premier League financial gap with Serie A means ‘Bournemouth, with all due respect, can spend more than Juventus,’ but Italian football also ‘does not have real defenders’ anymore.

The retired defender and current director at Juventus sat down with the Business of Sport podcast to discuss the current landscape, and the difficulties that Italian teams are having in Europe.

He noted that practically all leagues are now feeding the Premier League talent, and that only a handful of individual clubs like Bayern Munich, Paris Saint-Germain, Real Madrid and Barcelona can realistically compete on the open transfer market with English sides.

“If you think that Bournemouth, with all due respect, can spend much more than Juventus today, and if you’d told us that 10 years ago, we’d all be laughing at that idea,” said Chiellini.

“I’m curious what the situation is going to be five years from now.”

However, Italy beat England in their last major head-to-head, with Chiellini in the side that won the EURO 2020 Final at Wembley Stadium in July 2021.

“We learned how to manage a game, how to manage a situation, because we conceded a goal after two minutes,” said the former defender.

“We maintained a good balance and knew we had the possibility to come back, as the biggest risk was to concede another straight away, and then the game would’ve been done.

“We had players like Bonucci and I who played that game, we knew the importance of saying calm, calm, calm, and the coach knew for sure. That’s what we said at the end of the first half.”

Bonucci had also said that England felt too confident playing the Final at home, despite not winning any silverware apart from that 1966 World Cup.

“We knew the pressure on England was very high, and it continues to be very high,” added Chiellini.

“The English team always has big pressure from media, fans, especially at home. That was an advantage we knew that we had and we took it.”

That EURO 2020 triumph was an outlier in a spell where Italy failed to qualify for three consecutive editions of the World Cup, with Chiellini involved in two of those.

What is behind the lack of available talent for the Azzurri now?

“Not investing in developing talent, developing the Italian quality that has been for 100 years, which is we are a land of defenders and big talents, but now we do not have real defenders or big talents,” said Chiellini.

“Of course, football has changed, but if you look at other countries, they still maintain their main quality. We should start from youth, and I hope now people are working on a programme for four to 15-year-olds, it’s how we should start to create talent again.

“We have very good coaches, but we also need a good development from the grassroots. We had lots of Italian coaches who won the Premier League too, Carlo Ancelotti, Antonio Conte and Roberto Mancini.”
