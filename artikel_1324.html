Milan ready to open talks for Tresoldi January transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

La Gazzetta dello Sport reports that Milan are ready to enter talks with Club Brugge for Nicolò Tresoldi, knowing that the Belgian side will ask for €60m.

Milan have made U21 Germany international Tresoldi their top attacking target for the January transfer window, according to Gazzetta.

The pink paper already reported last week that Club Brugge’s asking price for Tresoldi in January will be €60m, and today’s update says the Rossoneri are ready to negotiate with the Belgians.

Whether they’ll eventually match that fee remains to be seen, but Tresoldi is surely the target the Serie A giants have identified to strengthen their attack.

Milan already signed Gonçalo Ramos from PSG last summer, making the Portuguese international their most expensive signing ever, for circa €75m.

According to Gazzetta, Tresoldi and his family are eager to move to Milan, but this doesn’t really come as a surprise, as it is no secret that the Rossoneri are the striker’s favourite team.

Italy-born Tresoldi has represented Germany at youth levels and has scored 18 goals across all club and international competitions this season.
