Palladino punishment for Heggem, fresh Bologna injury updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bologna coach Raffaele Palladino reveals he will punish Torbjorn Heggem for ‘over the top’ comments made on international duty by dropping him against Udinese, while there are injury updates.

Serie A returns this weekend following the break for international duty, and the Rossoblu aim to build on their run of four consecutive victories by taking on troubled Udinese.

However, Heggem will not be part of the squad after his criticism of Palladino and the lack of playing time at Bologna, complaining to the media in Norway that he “cannot keep sitting on the bench” for the rest of this season.

“When an important player like him doesn’t get as much playing time, he wanted to express his unease,” said Palladino in a press conference today.

“Perhaps some of his quotes were over-emphasised in the media, but he too went over the top. This is why he is not being called up for this game.

“He is part of our squad, and although he will not be called up this weekend, he will be with us for the following matches.”

The break for international duty was damaging for other reasons to Bologna, because some of Palladino’s players picked up fresh injury problems.

They included Italy international Nicolò Cambiaghi, who was so impressive in the 3-1 victory over France, then withdrew from the side for their trip to Belgium.

“Cambiaghi had this ankle issue. Juan Miranda got hurt against Parma and will be out for another few games, but we’ll see if we can get him ready to face Venezia.

“I tried Alhassane and a few other alternatives in his position, but I don’t want to give the players any guarantees on who will start.”

Among those contenders for the wing-back role is Federico Bernardeschi, who already played there for Fiorentina, albeit some years ago.

Nikola Moro in midfield and Ukraine international striker Artem Dovbyk have also struggled for regular playing time under Palladino.

“I don’t see first choice players, but I pick those who show me more in training. Dovbyk returned from international duty with a different spirit, he has made progress and I am pleased about that.”
