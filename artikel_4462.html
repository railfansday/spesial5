You pried yourself away from your marathon GTA VI session long enough to look at the internet and we’re going to reward you by saving you tons of money. Amazon just officially launched its Black Friday deals and we’ll be tracking the best of the best until the dust has fully settled on Cyber Monday. Amazon says the full Black Friday sale runs through November 30, and it covers far more than fits in one post, so we stuck to brands we know in categories you actually care about. We’ll update throughout and throw the best deals in, so check back regularly.

A 5.5-quart Ninja air fryer for under $90

If you still reheat pizza in the microwave, consider this your intervention. The Ninja Air Fryer XL is $89.99, half its $179.99 list price. Amazon’s listing puts the basket at 5.5 quarts, which Ninja says fits 3 pounds of wings, and the heat tops out at 400 degrees. It also roasts, reheats and dehydrates, and the listing calls it dishwasher-safe for the cleanup you’d otherwise put off.

Bose’s tiny waterproof speaker for under $80

Small speakers make easy gifts because nobody has to guess a size. The Bose SoundLink Micro (2nd Gen) speaker is $79.00, which is $50 under its $129.00 list price. Bose rates the palm-sized speaker for up to 12 hours of battery life and calls it waterproof and dustproof, so it can ride along on a bike, a backpack or the edge of a hot tub. This price applies to the Sandstone color.

The family motion-gaming system for $90 off

The Nex Playground drops to $209.00 from a $299.00 list price. It’s a small cube that plugs into a TV and uses a built-in camera to track players’ movements, so nobody has to hold a controller. Amazon’s listing pitches it for game nights, parties and playdates. That makes it a strong candidate for the gift that gets everyone off the couch after a holiday dinner.

Breville’s grind-and-brew espresso machine for $205 off

A coffee shop habit gets expensive, and this machine is one way out. The Breville Barista Express espresso machine is $494.95, down from a $699.95 list price. This is the BES870XL in brushed stainless steel, and it has a grinder built in, so whole beans go in the top and espresso comes out the bottom. Breville’s listing calls it a manual machine, so expect a short learning curve before the lattes start looking like the ones you pay for.
