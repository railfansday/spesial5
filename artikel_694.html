Michelle Monaghan and Justin Theroux to Star in ‘Sleepover’ From Blumhouse Atomic and Universal

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Michelle Monaghan and Justin Theroux are set to star in “Sleepover” from Blumhouse Atomic and Universal Pictures. The horror movie is set for theatrical release on Oct. 8, 2027.

“Insidious: Out of the Further” filmmaker Jacob Chase is directing the project from a script he co-wrote with “Night Swim” screenwriter Bryce McGuire. “Sleepover” is based on McGuire’s original short story. Plot details and further casting remain under wraps. Variety exclusively reported in October that Parker Posey was set to star in “Sleepover,” but she has since dropped out of the project. Monaghan was cast in her place. Posey revealed the exit during a recent “Today” interview. When asked about the project, she told Craig Melvin, “I’m not doing it anymore … Something came up.”

Monaghan, known for roles in films like “Kiss Kiss Bang Bang,” “Source Code” and “Gone Baby Gone,” most recently starred alongside Robert De Niro and Adam Scott in the Netflix crime thriller “The Whisper Man.” Also for Netflix, she appeared in the buddy comedy “Little Brother” with John Cena and Eric Andre. On the horizon for Monaghan is Netflix’s hockey drama series “The Sticks,” which also stars Emilie Bierre and Shai Chase, and the third season of Apple TV’s “Your Friends and Neighbors.”

Theroux is no stranger to the horror genre. One of his best-known roles was playing the scorned movie exec Adam in David Lynch’s twisty masterpiece “Mulholland Drive.” More recently, he starred in “Beetlejuice Beetlejuice” as the manipulative TV producer boyfriend of Winona Ryder’s Lydia Deetz. His most recent big-screen credit was for “The Devil Wears Prada 2.” On the TV side, Theroux last starred in Netflix’s sports comedy “Running Point” and played the iconic post-apocalyptic capitalist Robert House in Prime Video’s “Fallout.” Upcoming projects for Theroux include “The Billion Dollar Spy” with Russell Crowe and Apple TV’s Siegfried and Roy miniseries “Wild Things.”

“Sleepover” reunites Chase and Blumhouse Atomic after “Out of the Further.” The sixth installment in the “Insidious” franchise opened in August to $60 million worldwide and went on to gross $169 million against an $18 million budget.

Jason Blum serves as producer. Chase and McGuire serve as executive producers, with Bea Sequeira serving as executive producer for Blumhouse and Lilly Tyson overseeing the project for the company. “Sleepover” is a Cinescape Entertainment production, presented by Universal Pictures and Blumhouse Atomic.

Monaghan is represented by Gersh, Anonymous Content, Entertainment 360, Narrative and Sloane, Offer, Weber & Dern. Theroux is represented by CAA, Lighthouse Management & Media, Ingenuity Group and Toczek Suddleson Law Group.
