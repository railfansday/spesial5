How Jennifer Lopez and More Stars Are Celebrating Thanksgiving 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Thanksgiving 2026 is finally upon us, and Hollywood’s biggest stars are eager to kick back and relax this Turkey Day. From Hoda Kotb to Jennifer Lopez, see what they got up to.

It’s time to get basted—because Thanksgiving 2026 is officially here.

And with the most gratitude-filled day of the year comes a slew of stars ready to stop, drop and pass the dinner rolls with their loved ones. As for how they’ll be celebrating Turkey Day this time around?

Well, Lena Dunham offered a sweet glimpse into her first Thanksgiving as a mother, sharing a photo of her and husband Luis Felber's baby girl as they went on a family stroll.

As she captioned the Nov. 26 Instagram post, "Thankful."

Meanwhile, Jennifer Lopez revealed that while her and Marc Anthony’s 18-year-old twins Max and Emme are enjoying their first semester of college, they’ll be making their way back to Jenny’s block for the holiday season.

“I told them, I said, 'I don't care what you do,’” the singer told Drew Barrymore on the Nov. 23 episode of her eponymous talk show. “‘I know you're going to have friends. I know you're going to fall in love. I know you're going to do all the things. Thanksgiving and Christmas, I don't care if you bring 20 people, just come home. Please, just come home.’”

But it’s safe to say that their sweet reunion won’t make for your average Thanksgiving gathering. After all, the whole fam will be getting loud for the 57-year-old’s The JLO Show concert film, which premieres on CBS and Paramount+ on Nov. 26 at 8 p.m. ET.

“For me, as an entertainer, my job is to make sure everybody enjoys themselves and has a good time,” the “Jenny from the Block” the singer told E! News in an interview published Nov. 20. “Everything about the show is put together to tell a story.”

Barrymore, on the other hand, is sticking to her newfound blend of Thanksgiving traditions.

“I didn't have them growing up, and I bet there are a lot of people out there who probably had that same kind of experience,” she told CBS Nov. 23. “And the good news is, you can find them and build them because now in my life, rich traditions.”

As for a few of the 50 first traditions she’s put in place?

“I really love decorating our door with like turkey hand puppets, and we have a whole box of Thanksgiving decorations, and we go out and get some gourds,” she explained. “We go to this pumpkin patch every year.”

“Growing up in West Hollywood, Los Angeles, there's no pumpkin patches around, and so the fact that I get to do that with my kids, I never thought that would be my life, and it is,” she added. “So even if your life didn't start that way, it can totally get there. And now I look forward to that pumpkin patch every year.”

Then there are some holiday traditions with deep roots. After all, this year’s Thanksgiving marks Macy’s 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade, which is hosted by none other than Savannah Guthrie, Hoda Kotb and Al Roker.

And while this year's event has been postponed to Nov. 28 due to severe weather, the Joy 101 founder made it clear there will be no rain on her parade.

“It’s OK,” Kotb said on the November 25 episode of Late Night With Seth Meyers. “We waited 100 years for this parade, we’re gonna wait two more days. We’re gonna have it on Saturday. It’s gonna be a beautiful, sunsplash day.”

In the meantime, she'll be spending the holiday watching last year's parade, which NBC announced would run as a place holder on Thanksgiving Day. As she put it, “We’re gonna watch an old parade and you won’t know the difference, OK? It’s gonna be spectacular.”
