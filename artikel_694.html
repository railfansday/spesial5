Inside the Kardashian-Jenner Family's Thanksgiving 2026 Celebration

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Kardashian-Jenner family celebrated Thanksgiving Day 2026 with a poolside getaway, with Kylie Jenner and Kourtney Kardashian sharing a peek at the celebrations on social media Nov. 26.

The Kardashian-Jenners are rising and shining with gratitude.

The reality TV family shared an inside look at their Thanksgiving celebrations, proving that no one knows how to prepare a holiday party like the Kar-Jenner crew.

Case in point? Sisters Kylie Jenner and Kourtney Kardashian revealed their loved ones took the holiday poolside this year.

The Kylie Cosmetics founder shared a video on her Instagram Stories Nov. 26 of a gorgeous sunny morning featuring a sparkling pool in a backyard surrounded by palm trees with the mountains in the background.

Alongside the serene footage, Kylie wrote, "happy thanksgiving."

Kourtney, meanwhile, shared a snap of what appeared to be the same greenery-filled backyard on her Instagram Stories, but her focal point was an elaborate coffee machine on the patio.

And the Lemme founder appeared to be grateful to gobble up the caffeine this Thanksgiving as she wrote alongside the shot, "thankful."

But Kylie—mom to daughter Stormi Webster, 8, and son Aire Webster, 4, with ex Travis Scott—showed that the Kardashian-Jenner family always keeps up in the kitchen each year as the 29-year-old shared a photo of a green bean casserole being whipped up.

And for this year, Khloe Kardashian proved the pie's the limit when it comes to how much love surrounds the family during the holidays.

After all, the 42-year-old—who shares kids True Thompson, 8, and Tatum Thompson, 4, with ex Tristan Thompson—marked Turkey Day by sharing a sweet look back at special Thanksgiving memories from over the years on Instagram Nov. 26.

Khloe's video compilation featured footage of heartwarming family moments from Thanksgiving past, including throwback home videos showing Khloe and her sisters Kim Kardashian, Kourtney Kardashian, Kendall Jenner and Kylie Jenner, along with brother Rob Kardashian, celebrating the holiday in the 90s.

"Happy Thanksgiving my loves. Today and every day, I am reminded of just how much there is to be grateful for," Khloe wrote alongside the video montage. "The greatest blessings in life are not things, but the people we get to share this beautiful life with. The laughter, the memories, the little moments that end up being the big moments."
