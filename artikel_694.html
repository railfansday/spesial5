Justin Chadwick, Roy Lee Among Sony Future Filmmaker Awards Jury Members for 2027 Edition

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Sony Future Filmmaker Awards revealed on Wednesday the jury panel for its 2027 edition on Wednesday.

The jury members include Sony Pictures Animation director Alan Hawkins (“The Haunting of Hotel Transylvania,” head of character animation on “Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse,” “The Mitchells vs. the Machines”), film and television producer and founder of Vertigo Entertainment Roy Lee (“The Ring,” “The Departed,” “Weapons”), cinematographer Christopher Ross (“Shōgun,” “Yesterday,” “The Day of the Jackal”), filmmaker Dana Adam Shapiro (“Murderball,” “Monogamy,” “Better Angels”) and director Justin Chadwick (“Mandela: Long Walk to Freedom,” “The Other Boleyn Girl,” “Brothers Under Fire”), who returns as jury chair for the fifth consecutive year. “Every year, the shortlist reminds us of the remarkable filmmaking talent out there waiting to be uncovered,” Chadwick said in a statement. “Last year’s filmmakers arrived in Los Angeles from every corner of the world and left with relationships and insights that will continue to shape and further their careers for years to come.”

This year’s awards are introducing a new Immersive category, presented by Meta, open to short films in 3D, 180° and 360° video, in any genre, fiction or non-fiction, live-action or animation.The awards will also partner with Snapdragon for the Snapdragon Shorts Award, which will award $10,000 each to five shortlisted filmmakers, along with a Snapdragon-powered filmmaking kit (smartphone, PC and smart glasses) to make an original short.

The 2027 Sony Future Filmmaker Awards will include a four-day program at the Sony Pictures Studios in Culver City from June 7-10 giving recognizing filmmaking talent, giving their work a global stage and connecting them directly with the industry. Submissions for the 2027 edition close on Dec. 15. Filmmakers can enter short films across six categories: fiction, non-fiction, immersive, animation, student and future format. The Awards shortlist will be announced in the spring.
