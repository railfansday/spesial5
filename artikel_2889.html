A.J. Brown to miss second straight game for Patriots

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

It will be another Sunday without wide receiver A.J. Brown for the Patriots.

Head coach Mike Vrabel ruled Brown out for this weekend’s home game against the Packers during his Friday press conference. Brown also missed last Sunday’s game due to a knee injury and did not practice at all this week. Brown missed four games while on injured reserve with an ankle injury and the ankle remains part of his listing on the injury report as well.

Running back TreVeyon Henderson will also miss the game for New England. Henderson released a statement on Friday saying that he was recently hospitalized for neurological reasons and that he is working with medical professionals to return to health.

Vrabel also said that tight end Hunter Henry (foot) and cornerback Marcus Jones (foot) will be listed as questionable to play this weekend.
