Here’s How Many Coca-Cola Shares It Takes to Earn $1,300 Every Quarter in Dividends

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

You would need to own more than 2,450 shares of Coca-Cola to generate $1,300 in dividend income.

By Matthew Benjamin – Nov 25, 2026 at 6:28PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Coca-Cola ›

Coca-Cola (KO -0.03%) has been a great stock to own in 2026.

Shares of the giant beverage maker are up more than 40% year to date (compared with the S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.25%), which has risen just about 4.5% this year).

In addition to that impressive gain, Coca-Cola is also a Dividend King, a company that has raised its dividend for at least 50 consecutive years. In fact, Coke has raised its dividend for 64 consecutive years.

That annual dividend currently stands at $2.12 per share, or $0.53 each quarter.

So, to earn a healthy $1,300 per quarter in dividends, how many shares would you need to own? Well, it's as simple as dividing $1,300 by 0.53.

You would need to own 2,453 shares to generate that much quarterly dividend income. That's a hefty investment: That many shares would cost $240,394 at the current share price of $98.

All that said, I believe Coca-Cola is a very good investment right now for two main reasons. First, the stock has historically held up well in recessions and bear markets. And second, while the rise of GLP-1 drugs is hurting some snack food and beverage companies, about 68% of Coca-Cola's portfolio consists of low- or no-calorie drink options.

So, you might not be able to afford enough KO shares to generate over $1,000 in income per quarter, but an investment in this stock is, in my opinion, a wise one.
