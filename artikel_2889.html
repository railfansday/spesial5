Eagles vs. Steelers predictions for Week 11 of the 2026 NFL season

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Eagles vs. Steelers predictions for Week 11 of the 2026 NFL season originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Eagles (2-7) are back from their bye week and will host the Pittsburgh Steelers (6-3) on Sunday afternoon at the Linc.

Someday this year the Eagles will win another game. I don't think it will be this one. The Steelers do a lot of things well. They're third in the NFL in run defense and seventh in scoring defense, allowing only 20 points per game, and T.J. Watt is still terrorizing quarterbacks at 32 years old. Undrafted Jaylen Warren leads the NFL at 5.7 yards per carry and is a dangerous receiver out of the backfield, future 1st-ballot Hall of Famer Aaron Rodgers can still make plays when he's protected – he's got 15 TD passes and six INTs this year – and D.J. Metcalf isn't what he used to be with Seattle but still capable of coming up big a few times a game. The Steelres are 6-3 and 4-1 in their last five and they don't have a ton of quality wins, but they aren't playing a quality team Sunday afternoon at the Linc. The Steelers aren't a Super Bowl threat, but they do a lot of things well. The Eagles don't do anything well. Except lose. They're good at that. Getting DeVonta Smith back and a healthier Saquon Barkley should help Joe Milton, but I just don't see the Eagles competing for 60 minutes with a solid team like the Steelers.

I don't think this Steelers team is as good as their record but I do think they're better than the Eagles. Even though the bye week should definitely help the Eagles find some better answers on offense as Joe Milton learns the scheme, that Steelers defense is good up front. There are some serious concerns about pass pro holding up in this game and the Steelers are hard to run against. The one advantage the Eagles have is at receiver as DeVonta Smith and Dontayvion Wicks return. But Milton will need time to throw. The Eagles' defense should be able to slow down this version of Aaron Rodgers but I just don't have a lot of faith that the Eagles will be able to snap their losing streak.

Last week's bye week was a much-needed break for this Eagles team. Players needed to heal up physically and mentally. As a player, the bye week allowed me to decompress and refocus on the season. Even when the season is going well, getting away helps the mind and body reset for the remainder of the season.

Coaches don't usually get that break. They try to figure out how to get the players performing better, fix what's going wrong and reinforce what they should continue doing right.

On offense, getting DeVonta Smith back will surely boost the passing attack for Joe Milton. Helping the O-line with protection should be the offense's No. 1 focus. Markel Bell will need help against one of the best pass rushers in the league in T.J. Watt. They must send a chipping RB, TE presence and slide the line toward him. Sean Mannion cannot let the right side of the line wreck the entire offense.

Defensively, Vic Fangio will have to call an aggressive style of play. The DBs will have to play tighter coverage or Aaron Rodgers will pick the defense apart. Stopping the run without Jordan Davis will also be a priority. A collective effort will be required to compensate for losing Davis on 1st and 2nd down. Can the Eagles keep the win streak going against the Steelers who have not won in Philadelphia since 1965? Maybe. Will they? Probably not.

Fresh off the bye week with some reinforcements on the way at receiver, you'd have to hope the Eagles offense shows some level of improvement. Against the Giants, they managed just one score and 30 of the 60 yards gained on that drive came via penalty. It has been an abysmal effort of late. Having DeVonta Smith and more time on task for new starting quarterback Joe Milton should — in theory — lead to better results.

Aaron Rodgers turns 43 next month and just lost his top interior protector on the offensive line in Mason McCormick. That should spell a big game for Jalen Carter. He tends to feast on inferior opponents. However, the Eagles are down both of their defensive captains. Zack Baun is dealing with a back injury and Jordan Davis was just dealt to the Jaguars. I suspect they'll struggle to stop the Steelers' rushing attack, led by Jaylen Warren.

The Steelers haven't won a game in Philadelphia since 1965. That changes on Sunday.

The Steelers are much better on paper than the way they are actually playing. Pittsburgh has a great defensive line… that doesn't get a lot of pressure. Talented receivers… to go along with one of the worst passing offenses in the NFL. The stats don't match up to the talent you see on the Steelers team. However, they are good at two things — running the football and winning close games. Two things the Eagles cannot seem to figure out so far this season. I am encouraged to see if the Eagles offense can make any strides with Joe Milton's second game at quarterback and the return of DeVonta Smith and Wicks. The Eagles' defensive line has a winnable matchup against a banged up Steelers offensive line but Aaron Rodgers is known for getting the ball out quick. I don't trust anything the Eagles are doing right now to be able to win this game.

It's good to have football back in the city of Philadelphia. I know it's been a frustrating campaign to say the least, but when football is in town, the city has a different type of energy to it. Against the Steelers, you know exactly the type of ballgame it's going to be. They want to play a slow, monotonous brand of football where they run the ball, churn clock, smother you defensively, and leave town with a win without doing anything spectacular. They're a veteran-laden unit headlined by future Hall of Famers in Aaron Rodgers and T.J. Watt, but it's their blend of youth-infused talent that showcases a well structured football team.

For the Eagles, I'm excited to see Joe Milton once again with what looks to be a WR core at full strength at time of writing. With extra time to prepare via the bye, I expect Milton to make some throws this week allowing his guys to make a few plays downfield. Pittsburgh is underwhelming in the secondary, and if Milton is granted just a few ticks of time to work through his progressions, the Eagles could create some explosive plays this week. Everything, however, starts with the offensive line, and I hope this coaching staff gives Markel Bell help against Watt or the longtime Steeler could have a handful of sacks by himself this weekend. That simply cannot happen. Double teams, chips, sliding protection, do something to help out your rookie RT whose confidence looks to have wavered mightily since the Jacksonville and Carolina games.

Defensively, limiting RB Jaylen Warren is the key to this week. He's the most underrated player in football in my book and the explosive engine that gets this whole thing going on offense for Pittsburgh. Limit him, and you have more than just a shot at winning this ballgame. My eyes are also on the edge rushers to create pressure on the soon-to-be 43 year old Rodgers, and the LB core which could be without Zack Baun. Shedding blocks with consistency and tackling in open space has lacked within this defense — that must improve on Sunday. Overall, I haven't seen this team adjust in months and this Steelers team is a rugged ballclub that just got embarrassed on Sunday Night football against the Bengals. Do I hope HC Nick Sirianni and OC Sean Mannion made adjustments during the bye week? Absolutely, but, my confidence in executing said changes is a whole other conversation.
