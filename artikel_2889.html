Preview: Back to it

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Eintracht Frankfurt and TSG Hoffenheim both find themselves in mid-table ahead of their meeting on Matchday 10. Eintracht boss Adi Hütter has a perfect record against Saturday's opponents.

The Eintracht squad returned to the training pitch on Tuesday afternoon to begin preparations for the upcoming home match against Hoffenheim. "I think we're two or three points short," said head coach Adi Hütter ahead of the meeting on Saturday 21 November (15:30 CET) at Deutsche Bank Park. "We're not in a bad position, but it's still not good enough."The Eagles are tenth in the standings with 12 points going into Bundesliga Matchday 10, level with Hoffenheim in ninth. Eintracht collected a point last time out in a 2-2 draw at SC Paderborn, while the visitors were 2-1 winners at FC Schalke 04.

Hoffenheim released a total of 14 players for this month's international break, while Frankfurt had 13 players representing their country.

Hoffenheim haven't managed to ruffle the Eagles' feathers too often in the past – especially not when Hütter has been in charge. The Austrian has come up against the Sinsheim outfit eight times as a coach, registering seven wins and one draw. As Eintracht boss, his record is a perfect six out of six – the only impeccable record in the 56-year-old's coaching career, which spans nearly two decades.

Hütter will cross paths with compatriot Christian Ilzer for the first time on Saturday. It's no secret that the Hoffenheim coach demands a high degree of intensity from his side, as a few stats from the 2026/27 Bundesliga season prove…

•⁠ Fastest player: Adam Daghim, 36.75 kilometres per hour

•⁠ ⁠Second-most sprints (club): TSG Hoffenheim, 1,564

•⁠ ⁠Fourth-most sprints: Eintracht Frankfurt, 1,518

•⁠ ⁠Second-most distance covered: TSG Hoffenheim, 1,105.5 kilometres

•⁠ ⁠Third-most intensive runs: TSG Hoffenheim, 6,426

•⁠ ⁠Fourth-most fouls: TSG Hoffenheim, 110

•⁠ ⁠Second-most fouls (player): Ozan Kabak, 18

•⁠ ⁠Second-most duels won: TSG Hoffenheim, 953

It seems like a match made for someone like Elias Baum, who has contested the most tackles in the league with 21. However, the defender will be forced to watch this one from the stands after his dismissal against Paderborn. Either way, the excitement is building again after the international break and everybody in Frankfurt is looking forward to Bundesliga football at Deutsche Bank Park on Saturday – and hopefully a continuation of Hütter's run against Hoffenheim.
