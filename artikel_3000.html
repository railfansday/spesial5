(FAC -4.70%) transitioned to a commercial-stage company in the third quarter of 2026, recording its first revenue from solid-state battery cell deliveries to the aerospace market. Management reported reaching an energy density of 450 watt-hours per kilogram for its aerospace cells, meeting its year-end performance target ahead of schedule while achieving a pilot line yield of 85%. The company is executing a fabless business model, forming strategic partnerships with manufacturers like SK On to leverage existing global production capacity for the automotive sector. Factorial revised its full-year spending and capital expenditure guidance downward, citing cost reclassifications and disciplined operational management as it prepares for meaningful revenue growth in 2027.
