Walker Kessler is confident the Lakers will bounce back strong from loss to Utah

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Despite the Utah Jazz snapping the Los Angeles Lakers' nine-game winning streak and dropping them to 13-3, Walker Kessler doesn't want to dwell on the defeat for too long. With another game Friday against the Sacramento Kings, Kessler intends to quickly absorb whatever lessons he can and prepare for Sacramento, all while sounding pretty content with the process offensively Wednesday night.

"It is frustrating. Tonight wasn't our night, but I think our team is going to respond well to this loss. With 82 games, when you have a bad game like this, you don't want to spend too much time lingering on the losses," Kessler told reporters postgame. "You want to learn from it and move on because you have to. You have another one Friday. So our offense, as far as it not going tonight, we were getting good looks, just wasn't falling necessarily."

Quentin Grimes, who scored 20 points on 8-of-20 shooting, feels similarly to Kessler. He believes it was a "winnable game," one the Lakers squandered by not meeting the precedent they've set this season during their 13-2 start (13-3 now).

"Just not to overthink it. Tonight wasn't our best outing for sure. It was a back to back, but we still feel like we could have won the game," Grimes told reporters Wednesday. "I feel like if we would have made a couple more shots and just took care of the ball in two possessions and get a stop, we would have easily won the game. So it's just a positive takeaway from it is knowing that it was a winnable game that we kind of let slip away."

Grimes, Kessler and the rest of the Lakers will look to begin a new winning streak — featuring much better shotmaking — when they welcome the Kings to Crypto.com Arena Friday at 7 p.m. They'll also need to win this game to keep their NBA Cup Quarterfinals hopes alive since they already dropped a group stage game Oct. 30 against the Golden State Warriors. A 1-13 Sacramento squad could help them achieve that and potentially beef up their point differential — a critical tiebreaker in determining wildcard seeds.
