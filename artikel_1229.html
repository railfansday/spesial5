Hojlund: ‘My message to Manchester City fans, Napoli are coming back’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Rasmus Hojlund reveals what he said to the Manchester City fans after scoring for Napoli in the Champions League, harking back to his United past. ‘We’re coming back.’

The Denmark international was on target at the Etihad to give Napoli the lead in this evening’s Champions League clash, which eventually ended 2-2.

Although the Partenopei were twice in front, as Noa Lang also volleyed in, the Citizens clawed their way back with Ayyoub Bouaddi and Rayan Cherki.

When he scored, he seemed to mouth something to the Manchester City fans, so what was his message?

“I was really happy for my goal, the fans were jeering me, so I was saying there’s only one club in Manchester,” Hojlund grinned on Sky Sport Italia.

“We were really happy with the game and the result. It was a difficult season for us so far, but there’s a long way to go, so we can turn things around. We’ve got to stay positive, listen to the coach, and keep working hard.

“We had a lot of injuries, but Napoli are coming back and we hope to get back on track in Serie A and the Champions League.”

Hojlund has struggled for consistency and to maintain the kind of form he has shown on international duty, but it is also fair to say he is playing non-stop for Napoli, as there are no real understudies to cover him.

“I think I’ve been quite unlucky, a couple of chances where I could’ve done better, but also some with good goalkeeping from the opponents. For example, the Lazio goalkeeper made some incredible saves, I also remember against Venezia it was cleared off the line,” noted Hojlund.

“I want to keep positive, I scored lots of goals on international duty, I scored at San Siro, against Como, against City, so I need to keep improving, listen to my teammates and coach, and hope to score more goals.”

This result means Napoli have four points after five games in the Champions League, having only beaten Bodo/Glimt 4-3, along with defeats to Arsenal, Villarreal and FC Porto.
