Mario Kart World Has Been Updated To Version 1.9.0, Here Are The Full Patch Notes

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Knockout Tour updates, adjustments, fixes and more

Nintendo has rolled out a new update for its Switch 2 exclusive Mario Kart World this week. It bumps the title up to Version 1.9.0.

According to the official patch notes, the Feather and Lucky Cat Rally have been added to Knockout Tour, and multiple courses have now been added to VS Race, Time Trials and races in online and wireless play. The replay feature has also been enhanced, and multiple other adjustments & fixes have been made to improve the overall experience.

Nintendo: "A free update is available now in the Mario Kart World game, only on Nintendo Switch 2. Eight familiar courses are racing in, each with brand-new twists and turns! Plus, enjoy two new routes in Knockout Tour, and watch replays of past races and battles with the newly enhanced Replay feature."
