GTA 6's Exclusive Selfridges Merch Is Daylight Robbery

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

You may need to turn to crime in order to afford UK retailer Selfridges’ exclusive roster of GTA 6 merch.

Available online and in-store now, items include a £2,500 (~$3,352) varsity jacket with the Rockstar and GTA 6 logos emblazoned on them. You can also get a leather Burger Shot takeaway bag for £125 (~$167).

The variety of items is admittedly eclectic: there’s a Vice City Manatees American football for £80 (~$107), a ton of different t-shirts, and even a bunch of baseball caps.

To be fair, items like the Grotti keychain for £12 (~$16) look like a nice novelty.

The retailer has set up a huge GTA 6 display in its flagship store on Oxford Street in London, so it’s another example of Rockstar going all out on the marketing.

I’d probably buy the American football if it was a quarter of the price, but £80 is daylight robbery for that. Perhaps that’s Selfridges trying to keep with the crime theme here.
