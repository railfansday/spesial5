ICYMI: Call Of Duty: Warzone Is Now Available For Switch 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Much of the focus has been on Zelda: Ocarina of Time this week, but in case you missed it, Call of Duty: Warzone is now available to download for the Switch 2.

This is a free-to-play battle royale experience, where you can squad up and drop in. It's a separate mode from the Modern Warfare 4 single-player, multiplayer and DMZ mode.

It can be downloaded via the eShop and requires an Activision account to play. On our end, our total file size is now 122GB.

"The start of Season 1 marks the launch of Call of Duty: Warzone on Nintendo Switch™ 2! Drop into Hajin, Zodiac, and all the other maps and playlists as the free-to-play game arrives on the handheld system for the first time."

Alongside this is the release of Season 1 for Modern Warfare 4 and Warzone. In the MW4 multiplayer, this seasonal update comes loaded with additional multiplayer maps (Backstab, Canyon, Shoot House and more), modes, events, and rewards across both the game's multiplayer and DMZ.
