Act Quick! Nintendo's Discounted Its Retro NSO Controllers For Black Friday (Europe)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Those who have long wanted to get their hands on Nintendo's classic range of NSO controllers are in luck, as the full collection has landed a special Black Friday discount on the European Nintendo Store.

And yes, we mean all of 'em. This means you can pick up the double NES controller pack for £34.99, the SNES pad for £18.89 (currently out of stock), the SEGA Mega Drive one for £27.99, and the N64 model for £27.99. Heck, even the Switch 2 GameCube controller has got a discount and now sits at a sweet £41.29.

As we mentioned, the SNES option is already out of stock, and based on how quickly these restocks usually tend to fly off the virtual shelves, we imagine that some others might not stick around for too long either.

All of these are only available from the Nintendo Store, and are exclusive to Nintendo Switch Online paid members — so remember to sign in before purchasing.

The classic Joy-Con 2 are also seeing a discount as part of the Black Friday savings and can now be found for £52.49.

There are no discounts on the Virtual Boy headset, unfortunately, so you'll still need to cough up full price if you need to add that one to your collection.
