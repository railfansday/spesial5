Cisco to link all future products to its SaaS-y Cloud Control management tool

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Global rollout of unified portal finally happening in 2027

Cisco will no longer create products that don’t integrate with Cloud Control, its SaaS-y management platform that offers netadmins a single tool to manage all of the networking giant’s devices and much of its software portfolio.

It appears Cisco is now satisfied it has sorted out those issues. Jeff Schultz, Cisco’s senior vice president for portfolio strategy and marketing, today told the company’s Live conference in Australia that Cloud Control will come online in the European Union, UK, and Down Under in February 2027. Japan, India, and Canada will get their turn in May.

Schultz said Cisco is “working tirelessly” to deploy Cloud Control across the world as soon as possible.

He also revealed that, starting next year, Cisco will no longer ship products that don’t integrate with Cloud Control.

That doesn’t mean those products will be manageable only if they can connect to Cloud Control. Instead, the decision reflects Cisco’s belief that the main thing stopping its customers buying more of its products is the complexity of operating them in concert. Schultz also feels that Cloud Control represents “platform advantage” for Cisco, because when users integrate networking, security, and observability the result is better manageability and resilience.

Schultz also told the conference that Cisco will bundle its Thousand Eyes observability and security tool with the “Essentials” and “Advantage” licenses. “We admit it was hard to license and deploy,” he said during his keynote speech, adding that the tool will be deployed automatically with Wi‑Fi 6E and Wi‑Fi 7 hardware.

Another product Cisco plans to bring to more customers is Live Protect, a tool that implements compensating controls for flaws that organizations can’t immediately patch. The product has become particularly useful since Anthropic's Mythos model, and similar AI offerings, made it possible to quickly find more software bugs and flaws and overwhelmed tech teams whose patching apparatus was not set up to handle hundreds of montly CVEs.

Live Protect currently works on some of Cisco's datacenter switches, plus SD-WAN and WLAN controllers. From December, Cisco will extend it to 8000 series routers, secure firewalls, campus gateway, and to Cloud Control itself. From May 2027, Live Protect will work with Cisco’s ASR 9000 series service aggregation routers and Network Convergence System.

"Expect this to be a major investment area," Schultz added. ®
