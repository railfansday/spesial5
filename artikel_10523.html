Store hires overnight guards after thieves steal $50,000 worth of Pokemon cards

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

A card shop in Tacoma, Washington, has hired overnight security guards after thieves used a pickup truck as a battering ram to steal around $50,000 in Pokemon and Magic: The Gathering cards.

The break-in at Northwest Sportscards occurred shortly after 5 am on November 2, and it’s the second time in four months that thieves have rammed a pickup through the shop’s rear metal doors, according to Tacoma police.

Surveillance footage shows a black Dodge pickup smashing open the rear entrance before two masked thieves run inside with totes, clearing shelves and the storeroom.

“It’s scary. They came in with a lot of force, in and out in just a couple of minutes,” owner Don Knutsen told KOMO News. “They came in looking for Pokemon. They ended up getting a lot of Magic as well.”

Many of the stolen cards were in sealed boxes with no serial numbers, which makes them harder to trace than graded sports cards.

Tacoma police have asked the public to help identify the suspects, sharing a description of the truck used in both burglaries.

Police are looking for a dark 2006 or 2007 Dodge Ram 2500 or 3500 with a Mega Cab or extended cab, a broken driver’s side taillight and damage to the rear bumper.

Crime Stoppers of Tacoma/Pierce County is offering up to $1,000 for information leading to an arrest and charges, on top of a $5,000 reward from the shop.

Knutsen, who has run the shop for nearly 40 years, has now brought in overnight security guards seven days a week. He has also installed bollards around the building to stop vehicles getting through.
