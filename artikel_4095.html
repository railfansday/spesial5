Talking Point: What Are You Playing This Weekend? (November 7-8)

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Anything you need to complete before...y'know?

Just two more weekends to get stuck into the games you're likely to abandon come November 19th.

It's been a fairly chill week for new releases with ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut the only notable launch (at least in my opinion). Hopefully something from our Deep Discounts sales roundup, or Xbox's Free Play Days titles are worth a shot.

Alrighty then, let's get into it. Here's what we're playing on Xbox this weekend:

I think I'm in need of a nice, feel good weekend of gaming. I'd like to play stuff that leaves me feeling all warm and fuzzy. It's appropriate timing, really, considering just how quickly it's getting dark now that the clocks have changed here in the UK.

Top of the agenda is starting The Wolf Among Us Remastered. I claimed the original version with Games with Gold, yet I've still not managed to boot it. Somehow, I've managed to avoid all spoilers in the thirteen year stretch. All I know is there's a pig called Colin or something. Anyway, I'm very excited to finally play it, as I've only ever heard good things about it.

Another game I'd like to experience for the first time is It Takes Two. My lady love and I are looking for our next co-op adventure after completing Split Fiction, so what better choice than Hazelight's other phenomenal game?

Oh, and if I get time, I'd also like to check out Routine. It isn't exactly giving warm and cosy vibes, but I'm worried it could leave Xbox Game Pass soon, and with GTA 6 on the horizon I don't have spare funds floating around.

I've got quite a lot going on this weekend, so I don't expect to be playing too much. Hopefully I might be able to fit in some more Star Wars Galactic Racer and Tape to Tape though - both of those games are so incredible!

Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade on Xbox Game Pass is also something I'm planning to spend a lot of time with, possibly when I go on holiday in a few weeks' time. I played the demo and loved it, so now it's time to try the full thing!
