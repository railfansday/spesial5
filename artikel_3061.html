Grove reports first quarter of positive GAAP net income and adjusted EBITDA

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Grove Collaborative Holdings, Inc. (GROV -5.46%) reported positive GAAP net income and adjusted EBITDA for the same quarter for the first time as a public company. Management attributed the results to a stabilized top line and continued cost discipline, including a 22.1% reduction in operating expenses. The company is shifting its strategic focus toward human health through the implementation of the Grove Standard and an expanded assortment via dropship capabilities. Management indicated that 2027 is the target year for returning to year-over-year revenue growth.
