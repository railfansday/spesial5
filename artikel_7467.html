Meta brings its updated Creator Dashboard to the web

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The new dashboard will replace other management tools as the primary interface for managing a creator’s in-app presence.

Creators and social media managers, get ready for Meta to redirect you toward yet another content management tool, which will be slightly different from the one that you just got used to.

Meta announced on Wednesday that its new Creator Dashboard is now active on the web, and that it will replace other management tools as the primary UI for managing a creator’s in-app presence.

Meta has been rolling out its updated Creator Dashboard on mobile devices over the past year, including its Creator Studio app. These new systems offer more artificial intelligence tools and more video creation options, aligning with broader trends.

The updated functionalities are now available on desktop as well, which should provide expanded capacity for overall management and oversight.

But it will also mean learning the specifics of another new management tool.

This sentence acts as a pretty good summary of the confusion that Meta regularly causes with changes to its content management apps: “Creator Dashboard’s revamp began last year, back when it was still called Professional Dashboard. Since then it has become Creator Dashboard, with Business Dashboard as the counterpart for businesses, so the tools in front of you as a creator are the ones that matter most.”

As per this overview, Meta has several management tools available, which also includes Meta Business Suite and the controls users can access in-stream. Meta keeps updating these systems to ensure that its management tools incorporate the latest functionality, in line with its evolving systems.

But understanding which platform creators and social media managers are supposed to be using can get confusing.

Meta said the updated web composer in Creator Dashboard has been redesigned for feed posts and Reels, with full-screen overviews and live previews, so creators can see what their post will look like before publishing.

There’s also bulk video upload options, an improved content library and more insights into content performance.

Content protection is also available in the new app. Meta's content protection process enables creators to register their original uploads, which Meta’s system can then use as a reference point to detect when others re-post the same thing. Meta expanded content protection to Instagram earlier this week.

Meta said that creators will also now be able to invite people who’ve checked out their content to become followers from the Creator Dashboard, and access Messenger on the web. There’s also a monetization section, where creators will be able to access relevant information about potential monetization opportunities.

So, more Facebook and Instagram management and creation tools, integrated within a single app. For now, at least, till Meta comes out with the next iteration of its management platform and forces everyone to learn an all-new system once again.
