See the interior of the 2027 Rivian R2 Performance from every angle.

The Rivian R2 Performance continues the classy design language seen on previous models from the startup company, despite a significantly lower starting price.

The dashboard features high-quality materials, with a synthetic leather upper, wood trim in the middle, and a fabric lower section.

The R2 is spacious and comfortable, with the tall, boxy shape providing plenty of headroom and the glass roof creating an airy vibe.

The main touchscreen is paired with an 11.5-inch digital gauge cluster, and the unique thumbwheels mounted on the steering wheel allow you to control a wide variety of functions without lifting your hand from the wheel.

Nearly every function is handled through the 15.6-inch touchscreen, which has a clear, well-organized interface.

The rear seat is roomy and has heated outboard seats and USB-C ports hidden in the back of the front seats.

The cargo area is much larger than that of its closest competitor, the Tesla Model Y, thanks to the R2’s rectangular shape.
