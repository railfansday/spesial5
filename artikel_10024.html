Scifi Magazine 'Starlog' Relaunches, and Back Issues Return to Internet Archive

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Fine Print: The following comments are owned by whoever posted them. We are not responsible for them in any way.

Everyone and anyone interested in their episodes, or data preservation in general, should go download the full collection immediately!

The company who purchased the IP rights during their bankruptcy, Fangoria Holdings, still believes they own part of the IP rights.This is the company that tried (and largely succeeded) to have all available copies destroyed, and are the reason they were removed from the internet archive.

A quick sampling of PDF, PDF w/ text, EPUB:

- PDF is what you'd expect, fat but full scans, some slightly crooked.

- PDF text is a great space savings and the formatting is nice and the OCR has done a good job putting a PDF back together. The image quality though suffers a great deal, it's been heavily re-compressed. Download both versions of #001 and go to page 29, look at McCoy's face in the background of the car accident scene. That's the level of degredation in images in the PDF text format.
