Travis Scott, Slayyyter, Offset, Anitta & More Power ‘Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album’ Deluxe Edition: Stream It Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Between the original and deluxe, the GTA album now boasts 57 tracks in total.

Three days after releasing the 34-track Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, there is now a deluxe edition. Released via Atlantic Records on Monday (Nov. 23), the deluxe arrives with 23 new original songs featuring Travis Scott, Slayyyter and more.

Disc 2 also boasts a versatile lineup filled out by Offset, 21 Savage, Pooh Shiesty, The Dare, Anitta, Young Nudy, Quavo, Sofaygo, Sexyy Red with GTA duo Real Dimez, Latto, Kodak Black, Denzel Curry, Health, Overmono, Chris Stassy and Neil Frances.

Fans who played the video game throughout the weekend might be familiar with some of the songs, as a portion can be heard on GTA’s in-game radio stations. Others like Travis Scott’s opener “Burn This Shit Down” soundtrack the explosive final mission of the video game’s cinematic chapter one.

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album has become a blockbuster affair, with the original boasting a star-studded crew that included Dua Lipa, Playboi Carti, Cardi B, JENNIE, Tove Lo, Lana Del Rey, Young Thug and Kali Uchis.

Between the original and now the deluxe, 57 songs make up the complete project, which will looks to become the first video game soundtrack to top the Billboard 200.

Grand Theft Auto VI boasts more than a staggering 800 songs across 23 radio stations as part of the video game’s expansive experience. There are even artist-hosted radio stations, which find Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey, Morgan Wallen, Trina and Trick Daddy, PinkPantheress, Burna Boy and Robyn patrolling Leonida’s airwaves.

“Music is part of the DNA of these games,” said Rockstar vice president of music Ivan Pavlovich in Billboard‘s Grand Theft Auto VI digital cover story published on Monday. “We’re probably licensing three times the amount of songs we end up using. If we’re getting 800-plus songs in the game, you can imagine the amount of time, love and effort that all these people put into clearing music.”
