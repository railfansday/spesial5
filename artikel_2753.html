Four downs from DeWitt football's state semifinal loss to Zeeland West

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

CALEDONIA — Here is reaction from the DeWitt football team falling in a Division 3 state semifinal to Zeeland West 42-26 in a matchup of the last two state champions in D3.

Niko Mongkhoun, Zeeland West: The senior the tone for the Dux with an 80-yard touchdown run on the opening play of the game. Zeeland West never looked back from there and never trailed while advancing to the state final. Moungkhoun ran for 185 yards on 11 carries and recovered a fumble.

DeWitt trailed 22-13 at half and was set to receive the kick to open the second half. Zeeland West recovered a squib kick to open the third quarter. Less than two minutes later the Dux had capped the drive with a touchdown and added a 2-point conversion to take a 30-13 lead.

DeWitt closes its season with an 10-3 record and reached the state semifinals for a third straight year. This is the second time in three seasons the Panthers have had their season end with a loss to Zeeland West in the state semifinal. DeWitt dropped to 0-3 against the Dux in the postseason. Zeeland West reaches the Division 3 state final for the second time in three years with the win and will face Harper Woods at Ford Field on Saturday, Nov. 28.

"Momentum swings are big in any game but especially games of this magnitude. That (squib kick) wasn't a great start for us by any means (in the second half) but we made a lot of mistakes today and part of it is because of them and part of it is us not being as disciplined as we needed to be against a team like that on both sides of the ball and with special teams. If you're going to beat a team that doesn't make many mistakes you can't make them yourselves and we made some today for sure." DeWitt coach Rob Zimmerman
