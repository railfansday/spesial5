Sunday Night Football: 49ers lead Vikings 28-10 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The 49ers entered Week 11 with the NFC’s best record.

Through two quarters, they sure look like the conference’s best team.

San Francisco leads Minnesota 28-10 at halftime in Mexico City.

The 49ers scored touchdowns on three of their first four possessions, getting two TD catches from George Kittle before Mike Evans found the end zone for a 4-yard score in his first game since Oct. 19.

But the 49ers got another score within 10 seconds of game time, as after Evans’ score, safety Ji’Ayir Brown picked off Kirk Cousins and returned it 18 yards to the end zone for a pick-six.

It was Cousins’ fourth interception thrown to the 49ers in two games this season, though the first matchup came when the quarterback was with the Raiders.

Cousins finished the first half 12-of-19 passing for 121 yards with the pick. T.J. Hockenson leads with 40 yards on four catches while Justin Jefferson has 36 yards on two receptions.

The Vikings had two more chances to put up points, as Will Reichard missed a pair of field goals. The first was more realistic than the second, as he sent a 55-yard attempt wide left. But Reichard also had a 71-yard attempt to end the half with a fair catch/free kick situation. Reichard’s last attempt fell just short.

49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan has gotten the better of Vikings defensive coordinator Brian Flores to this point in the game. The numbers are solid across the board for San Francisco, but emblematic of the team’s success is that the offense faced just three third downs in the first half. The 49ers were 1-of-3 in the category.

Vikings left tackle Christian Darrisaw (shoulder) is questionable to return.
