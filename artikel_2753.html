Bay Area Thanksgiving turkey trots bring fun, food drives and a pre-feast workout

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Stretch your legs before stretching your waistband, the Bay Area's turkey trots are back this Thanksgiving.

With races and walks that support local food banks and charities, turkey trots are a healthy and fun way to kick off the daylong feast tradition. Check out the Chronicle's guide to active ways to work off that extra slice of pumpkin pie.

The Coyote Point Park Gobble Wobble offers a post race festival and pancake brekfast. (Coyote Point Park Gobble Wobble)

All events take place on Thursday, Nov. 26, unless otherwise noted.

The event includes a youth 1-mile race starting at 8 a.m. There are also 10K and 5K races that take off at 8:25 and 8:30 a.m. Race packet pick-up is available from 5-7 p.m. Tuesday, Nov. 24 and noon-5 p.m. Wednesday, Nov. 25 at Sports Basement, 100 Vintage Way in Novato.

7 a.m. $29.63-$73.43, registration required. Races start and finish at 1800 Ignacio Blvd., Novato. www.marinturkeytrot.com

Scheduled to include Thanksgiving-themed costume contests with Little Turkey, Big Turkey, Flock of Turkeys and dog costume categories, this untimed, annual event will benefit the Tim & Jeannie Hamann Foundation, which helps fund cancer research and provides assistance to patients in need during treatment.

8:30 a.m. $33, children under age 11 free; registration required. Benicia Community Park, 540 Rose Drive, Benicia. 707-315-1443. www.beniciaturkeytrot.com

Return to nature after all the holiday feasting and head for the hills of China Camp with other nature lovers to discover the verdant results of early-winter rains.

10:30-11:30 a.m. Friday, Nov. 27. Free, reservations required. Trailhead, end of Knight Drive, San Rafael. www.friendsofchinacamp.org

The Concord Turkey Trot runs start at Newhall Park in Concord at 8 a.m. (Concord Turkey Trot)

The Diablo Valley Federal Credit Union presents a family-friendly event that includes a 5K run/walk, and a kids dash for children 8 and younger. Proceeds will benefit Loaves and Fishes of Contra Costa.

8-11 a.m. $5-$135, registration required. Newhall Park, 1351 Newhall Parkway, Concord. www.concordturkeytrot.com

A 3-mile course begins at Town Park and is welcome to runners, walkers and bikers. Post-race celebration and refreshments will take place outside of the community center.

8:30 a.m. Free. Town Park, 498 Tamalpais Drive, Corte Madera. www.cortemadera.gov

A 10K (8:05 a.m.), 5K (8:20 a.m.) and kids fun run (9:15 a.m.) with post-race festival and pancake breakfast at 8:40 a.m. Awards ceremony scheduled for 9:30 a.m. Proceeds benefit NAMI Contra Costa.

8 a.m. $5-$55, registration required. Coyote Point Park, 1701 Coyote Point Drive, San Mateo. www.arunnersmind.com/gobblewobble

Though the registration window has closed, it's a race worth spectating. The mountain challenges participants to complete a famously formidable trail course, with nearly 9,000 feet of vertical terrain, not once, but four times back-to-back. The trail includes sections through Muir Woods and offers panoramic views of the Pacific Ocean.

7:30 a.m. Saturday, Nov. 28. Registration required. Old Mill Park, Throckmorton Avenue and Cascade Way, Mill Valley. http://www.quad-dipsea.com/

Event includes 10K (8 a.m.), 5K run/walk (8:20 a.m.) and a kids 1-mile fun run (9:30 a.m.) as well as an expo area with food vendors, live entertainment, a raffle and a turkey costume contest (9:20 a.m.). Proceeds will benefit Meals on Wheels and the Kroc Center.

7:50 a.m. $15-$65, registration required. Solano Community College, 4000 Suisun Valley Road, Fairfield. www.solanoturkeytrot.org

Includes a kids 100-meter dash (7:50 a.m.), 10K (8 a.m.) and 5K (8:20 a.m.) races. Thanksgiving-themed costumes encouraged. Walkers welcome. Awards presented after races.

7:30 a.m. $54, registration required. Kids dash free, no registration required. Napa Valley College, 2277 Napa Vallejo Hwy., Napa. 707-258-6272. www.athleticfeat.com/events/napa-valley-turkey-chase-pg1255.htm

Features 5K and 10K races (9 a.m.) and Little Turkey 100-yard dash featuring the costumed Nitro Turkey in the mix (8:45 a.m.). Proceeds will support the Food Bank of Contra Costa and Solano and attendees are encouraged to bring nonperishable items to donate on race morning.

8:30 a.m. $48-$63, registration required. Point Pinole Regional Shoreline, 5551 Giant Hwy., Richmond. www.brazenracing.com/nitroturkeyk

Join the 10th annual Oakland Turkey Trot, with runs set to kick off at 8:15 a.m. near Oakland's Lake Merritt Pergola at Lakeshore Ave.. (Oakland Turkey Trot)

Includes a 5K (8:30 a.m.), a kids 200-yard dash (9:40 a.m.) and a finish-line festival with live music and a fun zone. Proceeds benefit the Alameda County Community Food Bank, the Oakland Public Education Fund and Running for a Better Oakland.

8 a.m. $24.98-$136.64, registration required. Lake Merritt Pergola, 550 El Embarcadero, Oakland. www.oaklandturkeytrot.org

This event has no fees, minimal registration and zero waste, so bring your own water bottle. Runners, trotters and walkers of all ages are welcome. Warm up at 8:15 a.m. The run (4 or 6 miles) starts at 8:30 a.m. Costumes encouraged. There will be a food drive, and all proceeds will benefit the Redwood Empire Food Bank.

8 a.m. Free, $5-$25 suggested donation; registration required. Kaiser Parking Lot, 3958 Cypress Drive, Petaluma. 707-765-9222. www.madarc.com/turkey-trot-petaluma

A 5K walk/run through downtown Piedmont, with proceeds benefiting the Piedmont High School cross-country and track and field teams.

8:30 a.m. $35-$55, registration required. Exedra Plaza, Highland and Magnolia avenues, Piedmont. 510-333-4344. www.piedmontturkeytrot.org

Celeste Bradley, center, 1, holds her parents hands Ben Bradley, left, and Charlotte Gjedsted, right, as she crosses the start line during the 2025 San Francisco Turkey Trot in San Francisco. (Lea Suzuki/S.F. Chronicle)

The turkey is stuffed and sign-ups are full, but you can still observe the spectacle with the big turkey at the annual event, set to include a children's Gobbler Chase at 8 a.m., as well as the Turkey Trail Trot and Pilgrim Promenade scheduled to start at 8:40 a.m. Starting point is 36th Avenue and John F. Kennedy Drive. Thanksgiving-themed costumes encouraged.

8 a.m. Free-$53, registration required. Polo Fields, Golden Gate Park, JFK Drive, S.F. https://sfturkeytrot.wixsite.com/sftt

This year's event includes a kids fun run (8:20 a.m), half-marathon (8:30 a.m.), 10K (8:45 a.m.) and 5K (8:55 a.m.) options on a course along Coyote Creek Trail.

8 a.m. Free-$75.20, registration required. Hellyer County Park, 996 Hellyer Ave., San Jose. www.runsignup.com/Race/CA/SanJose/ZoomTurkeyTrot

Event includes a 5K, 10K, kids runs and fun zone, plus a costume contest. A children's area will open at 9 a.m. and the fun run starts at 10:15 a.m. at the intersection of South Autumn and West St. John streets. Proceeds will benefit Second Harvest in Silicon Valley and Santa Cruz, the Healthier Kids Foundation and Homefirst nonprofit organizations.

7:30 a.m. $33-$265, registration required. Race starts at 1 West Santa Clara St., San Jose. www.svturkeytrot.com

This year's Walnut Creek trot includes 10K (8 a.m.), 5K (8:10 a.m.) and a kids fun run (8:15 a.m.). Proceeds will benefit White Pony Express, Pledge to Humanity and the Forma Kids Foundation.

8 a.m. $20-$60, registration required. Civic Park, 1372 Civic Drive, Walnut Creek. www.walnutcreekturkeytrot.com
