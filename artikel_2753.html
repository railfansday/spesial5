Giants make move to replace injured CB Greg Newsome on 53-man roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

As expected, the New York Giants placed cornerback Greg Newsome on injured reserve Tuesday. It has previously been reported that Newsome will undergo tightrope surgery to repair his high ankle sprain, with the hope being that he could return to action before the season is over.

To replace Newsome on the 53-man roster, the Giants have signed edge defender Dawuane Smoot off their practice squad.

The 31-year-old Smoot is in his 10th NFL season. He was signed to the Giants' practice squad in mid-October, and has been elevated to the active roster for each of the past three games. He has a sack, a quarterback hit, and a tackle for loss in 102 snaps over that time. Smoot has played mostly in pass rush situations.

The Giants will turn to veteran Paulson Adebo to replace Newsome in the starting lineup. Adebo had an interception and four passes defensed Sunday after Newsome was injured just four snaps into the game against the Jacksonville Jaguars.

To fill Smoot's spot on the practice, the Giants re-signed offensive lineman Geno VanDeMark. VanDeMark had been released from the practice squad last week.
