Lakers' Luka Dončić 'feeling good' after injury scare, per report

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Los Angeles Lakers star Luka Dončić had an injury scare against the Milwaukee Bucks on Wednesday, Nov. 11.

Dončić suffered a right ankle injury with 4:25 left in the game after landing on Kyle Kuzma while attempting a shot. The point guard was awarded a foul and attempted his free throws before leaving for the locker room.

Coach JJ Redick stated after the game that Dončić had an ankle "tweak."

"I'm good," said Dončić, who finished with 32 points. "Obviously painful a little bit, but I'll be fine."

ESPN's Shams Charania reported Thursday that Dončić "actually woke up with no pain.

"He's feeling good. He will go through his normal treatment process," Charania said.

Dončić is expected to play against the Phoenix Suns in the Lakers' next game on Nov. 15.
