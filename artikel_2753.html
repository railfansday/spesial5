Survey – Who is the biggest threat in the NFC East?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It's safe to say that Week 10 didn't go as planned for the New York Giants.

The Giants lost 27-24 to the Washington Commanders in a crucial divisional game. It was a game the Giants could (and probably should) have won, but instead they squandered opportunities at the end of the game. So instead of being 6-2-1 with a comfortable lead in the division heading into a tough stretch of games, the Giants are 5-3-1 and Washington (3-5-1) has the head-to-head tiebreaker over the Giants.

The good news for the Giants is that the Dallas Cowboys (4-6) couldn't quite come out on top in a shoot-out with the San Francisco 49ers. That loss maintained the Giants' lead over their closest division rival as we enter the second half of the season.

So with that in mind, we want to know which team Giants' fans think is the biggest threat to the Giants in the division.

The Cowboys have a better record than the Commanders, and there's still the Week 17 game between the Giants and Cowboys. That game could be for the division.

On the other hand, the Commanders have the tiebreaker and a much easier schedule down the stretch. It's possible they could go on a run and catch the Giants.
