Texas A&M football rumors: Aggies ‘expected’ to start Brady Hart vs. Oklahoma

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Texas A&M football rumors: Aggies 'expected' to start Brady Hart vs. Oklahoma appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Texas A&M Aggies have had a frustrating season, as the quarterback situation has been less than consistent. Head coach Mike Elko finally made the decision to bench Marcel Reed during the 22-7 Week 11 loss to the No. 9 Tennessee Volunteers. On Friday, going into Week 12, rumors suggest that backup Brady Hart is going to maintain the starting position.

Reports indicate that Hart is expected to start for Texas A&M against the No. 22 Oklahoma Sooners, according to Matt Zenitz and Chris Hummer of CBS Sports. If that is the case, then the sophomore signal-caller will make his first start of the 2026-27 campaign.

"Texas A&M is currently expected to make a quarterback change and start sophomore Brady Hart against No. 22 Oklahoma tomorrow, sources tell me and Chris Hummer," Zenitz reported. "Hart, a Class of 2025 four-star recruit, led the way with first-team offense at A&M's practice [Friday]."

Hart subbed in for Reed in the fourth quarter against Tennessee in the Aggies' previous game. He completed two of his three attempts for 22 yards, while his lone incompletion was an interception. The Florida native has appeared in five games this season entering Week 12, recording 165 yards, two touchdowns, and two picks while completing 58.3% of his pass attempts.

Although his numbers aren't all that impressive in his small sample size, a change at quarterback was seemingly needed for the Aggies. Reed has been downright awful this year, as the redshirt junior has only thrown for 1,712 yards, eight touchdowns, and nine interceptions. His 55.7% completion percentage is the lowest of his collegiate career. With two games remaining on the schedule, Brady Hart has a chance to close out the campaign for Texas A&M.
