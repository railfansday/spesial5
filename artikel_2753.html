Report: Jalen Hurts makes an appearance at team facility

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Eagles quarterback Jalen Hurts made an appearance at the team’s practice facility on Wednesday, Tim McManus of ESPN reports.

It is the first time some players had seen Hurts since he went on injured reserve with blood clots in his lungs.

Hurts sat in on the team meeting and additional meetings, but he did not address the team.

Coach Nick Sirianni did not address a question Wednesday about whether the quarterback had been at the facility.

“I’ve talked to him and am in constant communication with him and know he’s resting and recovering,” Sirianni said. “I miss him like crazy. But nothing new on that front. I know he’s resting and recovering, and we’re all still praying for him and hoping for a speedy recovery.”

Hurts, 28, went on injured reserve on Oct. 17 and is expected to miss the rest of the season.

Tanner McKee went 0-3 in Hurts’ absence before being benched for Joe Milton this week.
