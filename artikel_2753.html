Chiefs-Falcons Week 10 Thursday injury report: Jaylon Moore misses 2nd day

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Each practice day this season, the Kansas City Chiefs release an official injury report ahead of the next game. In Week 10, the Chiefs play the Atlanta Falcons on Sunday afternoon.

Here is the Chiefs' second official injury report of the week, courtesy of the team's official website:

Rookie defensive tackle Peter Woods (neck) did not participate in practice for the second straight day and hasn't since exiting the Week 8 game against the Denver Broncos with a neck injury.

Starting right tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) did not participate in practice for the second straight day after leaving the Week 9 game in the third quarter with an ankle injury.

Second-year defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) was a limited participant in practice again. He has been limited in practice for going on three weeks and will need to be activated to the active roster by next Tuesday, November 17, in order to play this season.

Rookie running back Jaydn Ott (hamstring) was added to Thursday's injury report as a limited participant; he was signed to the 53-man roster after running back Brashard Smith was put on the Reserve/Injured list a few weeks ago.

The following players were full participants in practice both days this week:

Quarterback Patrick Mahomes (knee / right elbow)

Defensive tackle Gervon Dexter Sr. (illness) was upgraded to a limited participant after missing Wednesday's practice. He has played 57% of the defensive snaps for Atlanta this season and has four tackles for loss, five quarterback hits. 1.5 sacks and a pass batted down.

Linebacker Divine Deable (forearm) did not practice on Thursday after being limited on Wednesday. The team's starting linebacker has already missed two games this season due to an arm injury. He has 30 tackles, one for loss, two quarterback hits and a pass defended this year.

Rookie linebacker Kendal Daniels (achilles) was upgraded to a limited participant after missing Wednesday's practice. He leads Atlanta's linebacker position in snaps this year and has 61 tackles (five for loss), one sack, two passes defended and an interception.

Edge rusher James Pearce Jr. (back) was upgraded to a limited participant after missing Wednesday's practice. The former first-round pick made his season debut last week and played 15 snaps.

Rookie wide receiver Zachariah Branch (hamstring) did not participate in practice for the second consecutive day. Branch leads Atlanta in punt returns (8) and kick returns (14) with a long of 61 yards on a kickoff return. As a role player on offense, he has 10 catches for 64 yards this season.

Starting defensive end (and former Chiefs player for a brief time) Cameron Thomas (groin) was added to the injury report on Thursday as a limited participant. He has played 63% of Atlanta's defensive snaps this season and has 25 tackles, one for loss, 12 quarterback hits, two sacks, and a batted-down pass.

Former Chiefs right tackle Jawaan Taylor (NIR) rested Thursday and did not participate.
