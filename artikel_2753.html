Fantasy Basketball Top 200 Rankings: Franz Wagner has been on a tear lately

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Orlando Magic forward Franz Wagner's start to the season was anything but bad, as he recorded averages of 19.6 points, 6.4 rebounds, 8.5 assists, 1.3 steals and 0.9 three-pointers per game in his first eight appearances. However, the efficiency was lacking, as he shot 43.1 percent from the field and averaged 4.4 turnovers per game during that stretch. There have been no such issues recently, as Wagner has been one of the hottest players in the NBA.

After scoring 30 points or more in four straight games, Wagner is averaging 34.0 points, 7.5 rebounds, 7.8 assists, 1.5 steals, and 2.5 three-pointers per game over his last four. While he's also averaged 4.0 turnovers, his shooting percentages have improved. Wagner's shooting splits over his last four games are 53.3 percent from the field, 35.7 percent from three and 96.8 percent from the foul line.

In his most recent outing, a November 13 win over the Bucks, Wagner recorded a line of 35 points, nine rebounds, 12 assists, one steal, one block and three three-pointers, shooting 13-of-22 from the field. The Magic have been without Paolo Banchero since the second game of the season, and Jalen Suggs just returned from a knee injury. Those injuries have opened things up a bit for Wagner, and he deserves credit for taking advantage of the opportunity.

The question fantasy managers will have to ask themselves is: can Wagner maintain this production level (or something close to it) after Banchero returns, or should they sell high while he's rolling?

Week 5 Look-Ahead: Stephen Curry and Caleb Wilson go down, and toss Jeremiah Fears the keys

In another eventful week in the NBA, there were key injuries, a trade request and a historic performance from Cade Cunningham.

C Alperen Şengün, Houston Rockets: In Şengün's last four games, he has averaged 25.3 points, 11.0 rebounds, 7.5 assists, 1.3 steals, 0.8 blocks and 1.0 three-pointers per game while shooting 61.9 percent from the field. Fantasy managers rostering the Rockets center also benefited from him keeping his turnovers down, totaling seven in those four games.

G Davion Mitchell, Miami Heat: Having entered the season with an ADP outside the top-100, Mitchell has been a pleasant surprise in fantasy basketball. In his three games last week, the Heat guard recorded 34 assists (17 vs. Cleveland on November 13) and six turnovers. Mitchell also averaged 18.7 points, 4.7 rebounds, 1.3 steals, 1.0 blocks and 3.0 three-pointers.

G Cade Cunningham, Detroit Pistons: Of course, Cunningham became the first player since the NBA began recording turnovers to put up at least 50 points, 10 assists and zero turnovers in the Pistons' November 12 win over the Wizards. But he had an excellent week overall, averaging 34.0 points, 4.3 rebounds, 8.7 assists, 1.0 steals, 0.7 blocks and 2.0 three-pointers per game.

C Domantas Sabonis, Sacramento Kings: Sabonis ended his Week 4 with a double-double against the Thunder, but his value has taken a hit recently. For the season, the Kings center is averaging 8.8 shot attempts per game, which would be the lowest since his rookie season if that holds. With Darius Acuff Jr. being the focal point in Sacramento, the Kings' veterans have seen their values take a hit.

C Rudy Gobert, Minnesota Timberwolves: Speaking of a center whose scoring opportunities have decreased, we have Gobert. He's averaging 5.8 attempts per game this season and went 0-of-7 in the Timberwolves' November 14 loss to the Bulls. Despite Anthony Edwards being out, Gobert's opportunities have decreased, and he's shooting a career-low 46.4 percent from the field.

F AJ Dybantsa, Washington Wizards: Efficiency was an issue for Dybantsa last week, as he shot 36.1 percent from the field and 50.0 percent from the foul line while also averaging 3.6 turnovers per game. Rookies struggling with inefficient play isn't a surprise, so Dybantsa managers have no reason to panic.
