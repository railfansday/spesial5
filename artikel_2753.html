Penalties and forward progress ruling help Cowboys score before halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Eagles are not happy with the officials today in Dallas.

The Cowboys’ final drive of the first half was aided by three straight plays: A 10-yard pass interference penalty, a 30-yard pass interference penalty, and a questionable call on which the officials ruled Javonte Williams’ forward progress had stopped before he dropped a ball that Eagles cornerback Riq Woolen picked up and raced to the end zone for a would-be touchdown.

There was no Eagles touchdown because of the forward progress ruling, and the Cowboys scored a touchdown of their own after that to take a 28-10 lead at halftime.

In the first half Williams has three touchdowns, CeeDee Lamb has 57 receiving yards and George Pickens has 49 yards.

Eagles quarterback Joe Milton is playing well, with 145 passing yards and a touchdown, but the Eagles have lost tight end Dallas Goedert and running back Saquon Barkley, and they are going to need something spectacular to mount a second-half comeback against a Cowboys offense that can’t be stopped today.
