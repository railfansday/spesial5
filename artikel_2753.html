Pistons vs. Bucks preview: Detroit looks to seal Cup division with win

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

An undermanned (due to injury) Detroit Pistons team will be taking on an undermanned (due to rebuilding) Milwaukee Bucks team today at Little Caesars Arena. A win will essentially seal Detroit's win in East Group A of the NBA Cup. The best any team can finish, including Detroit is 3-1 overall, and Detroit has the tiebreaker against the Orlando Magic. Detroit's 29-point differential also easily eclipses the next closest team, Toronto, which stands at just plus-9. The winner of each group advances to the knockout round.

When: 7:30 p.m. ETWhere: Little Caesars Arena, Detroit, MichiganWatch: TV: TV20 Detroit | Streaming: DAZN-DetOdds: Pistons -10.5

In the way are the Bucks, and look, we all remember what the Pistons were like once they cleared the books of Reggie Jackson, Andre Drummond, and Blake Griffin. It was a bit of a hodgepodge mess. Milwaukee is currently in its hodgepodge era. They have the worst record in the NBA, winning just two of their 19 games. They have a bottom-five offense, a bottom-five defense, and the second-worst net rating in the NBA behind the Kings. They are one of the worst-shooting teams in the NBA becaue they don't really have pieces that fit together. They have some talent — I like Ryan Rollins and Jaime Jaquez, and Tyler Herro is an exceptionally dangerous offensive force. But they are a team currently better than the sum of their parts. And that probably isn't changing at any point this season.

But Detroit has issues of its own entering tonight. Namely, they will be down three starters, including superstar Cade Cunningham. Cade has been nursing a sore knee, and the Pistons are understandably taking zero chances. They will also be down Jalen Duren, who is close to a return, and Duncan Robinson, likewise.

The Pistons were also without all three starters in their most recent game, and still easily dispatched the Orlando Magic 104-86 because Detroit locked in defensively and got plenty of quality minutes from Ausar Thompson and Daniss Jenkins. Isaiah Joe also had the best game of his brief tenure with the Pistons, leading Detroit with 21 points and looking comfortable knocking down 3-pointers.

Detroit can muddle through the season without Cade; they showed it last season, too. They truly need to do whatever is needed to ensure this knee issue doesn't linger and he is helpful for what we hope is a deep playoff run.
