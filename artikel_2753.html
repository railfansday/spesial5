A glimpse of the team we thought the Suns could be

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

There's no denying that the Phoenix Suns have struggled this season, and through the first 12 games, you could point the finger squarely at the starting unit. But in game number 13, we may have witnessed the beginnings of a collective breakthrough.

We already know that the second unit possesses many of the characteristics that made the Phoenix Suns special a season ago. They're scrappy, they're disruptive, and they're connected. Perhaps some of that stems from having clearly defined roles and understanding how their individual skill sets complement one another. Whatever the reason, what has become abundantly clear through the early stages of this season is that if the starters could begin to string together productive stretches of basketball, the Suns could be a really good team.

In Denver on Monday night, particularly in the second half, we witnessed a starting unit that looked much more connected and began playing a brand of basketball reminiscent of the Phoenix Suns of a year ago. They were jumping passing lanes, attacking the glass, and playing with a collective sense of urgency. You could feel the energy typically associated with the second unit beginning to manifest itself within the starting five.

Was it perfect? Far from it. Both the Suns collectively and their starting unit specifically still have a considerable amount of work to do. But what I witnessed on Monday night gave me hope that the characteristics we expected from this basketball team can become more prominent moving forward, especially as the schedule begins to ease up.

I'm not ready to declare that the Phoenix Suns turned a corner with their victory in Denver. But they certainly started walking in that direction.
