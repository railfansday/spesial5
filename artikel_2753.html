MLB announces Pete Crow-Armstrong, Cody Bellinger as Platinum Glove winners in Friday night news dump

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Pete Crow-Armstrong and Cody Bellinger have won MLB's Platinum Glove awards, meant to honor the top overall defender in each league. It's unclear if the league actually wants people to know that.

For reasons that remain clear only to MLB, the 2026 Platinum Gloves were announced at 11 p.m. ET on a Friday night, with the Chicago Cubs and New York Yankees taking home an award that is decided by an easily swayed fan vote. It is the first time either player has won the award.

The pair seems happy with their new hardware.

The Platinum Gloves are selected from among the 10 Gold Glovers in each league. This year, those were:

Pitcher: Tanner Bibee, Cleveland Guardians

First base: Christian Walker, Houston Astros

Second base: Cole Young, Seattle Mariners

Left field: Cody Bellinger, New York Yankees

Center field: Ceddane Rafaela, Boston Red Sox

Right field: Wilyer Abreu, Boston Red Sox

Catcher: Gabriel Moreno, Arizona Diamondbacks

Second base: Brice Turang, Milwaukee Brewers

Shortstop: Geraldo Perdomo, Arizona Diamondbacks

Left field: Jackson Chourio, Milwaukee Brewers

Center field: Pete Crow-Armstrong, Chicago Cubs

Right field: Corbin Carroll, Arizona Diamondbacks

The timing of the announcement was nothing new, nor was the methodology. The winner is decided by combining a fan vote held by Rawlings with "votes" from a stat called the SABR Defensive Index (SDI).

Bellinger led the AL in SDI, while Crow-Armstrong ranked second behind only Milwaukee Brewers second baseman Brice Turang, another Gold Glover. As for other advanced defensivee stats, Crow-Armstrong ranked first in his league in Statcast's Fielding Run Value and third in Defensive Runs Saved. Bellinger was 21st and second in those stats, respectively.

It is, of course, extremely difficult to compare defenders across different positions, as these stats are trying to figure out how, say, an outfielder with a penchant for difficult catches compares to a catcher who can throw out runners and frame pitches. It's an inherent flaw of the Platinum Gloves, which would be more of a problem if anyone took them seriously.

At the very least, the Platinum Glove is another feather in the cap this season for Crow-Armstrong, who is widely expected to win NL MVP next week. The Cubs star posted MLB's seventh 40-40 season this year, making him an elite player on both offense and defense this season.

Bellinger, meanwhile, received his first All-Star nod since winning NL MVP in 2019 and was among the Yankees' top players this season after returning to the team last winter on a five-year, $162.5 million contract.

Rawling also announced the Toronto Blue Jays and Chicago Cubs as the winners of the Team Gold Gloves. The Cubs ranked third in MLB this season in defensive efficiency (i.e. the rate at which they turned balls in play into outs), while the Blue Jays were 13th. Toronto, which had no Gold Glovers, also led the AL in errors.
