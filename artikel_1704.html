Opinion: Liverpool handed surprise chance to sign perfect Milos Kerkez successor

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Milos Kerkez has been one of Liverpool's biggest disappointments over the last couple of years.

The Hungarian was brought in to replace Andy Robertson last season.

However, by the end of the campaign, the Scotsman managed to make himself Liverpool's first-choice left-back once again.

But, by the time the Reds realised that they needed him, he's already agreed a new deal with Tottenham.

This thrusted Kerkez into becoming the Reds' undeniable first-choice left-back with Kostas Tsimikas serving as his back-up.

Most people thought this added responsibility would help Kerkez but, if anything, he's managed to get worse.

This lack of form has been noticed by fans and pundits alike with the perfect option to replace him now emerging for Liverpool.

Rayan Ait-Nouri now wants to leave Manchester City

Ever since it was made clear that Kerkez's form wasn't going to improve, rumours have been circling about the next left-back that Liverpool could target.

Several different options have popped up recently with Brentford left-back El Hadji Malick Diouf seemingly on top of Iraola's shortlist.

However, since he's only just joined the Bees, a deal for the Senegal international feels highly unlikely to get over the line.

And, he's also had quite a slow start to life with Keith Andrews' men which is likely to have caused a worrisome thought or two.

But, since the footballing world is forever changing, new targets can emerge and, luckily for Liverpool, that seems to be exactly what has happened for them.

"Rayan Aït-Nouri could be a key player in the upcoming winter transfer window. The Algerian left-back is seriously considering leaving Manchester City and even wants to force a move as early as January," Sky Sports CH wrote.

"Two Italian clubs have already expressed interest: Juventus and Napoli. Contacts are underway between the player's representatives and the management of both clubs, who have now added Aït-Nouri to their shortlist to strengthen their left flank.

"The challenge now is to find the right formula to convince Manchester City. While the Citizens would ideally prefer a permanent transfer, this option appears financially difficult to realize this winter. Both Juventus and Napoli may lack the funds to meet the demands of a straight transfer.

"Under these conditions, the most likely scenario would be a paid loan with a purchase option, which could become mandatory under certain conditions."

Rayan Ait-Nouri would fit perfectly into Andoni Iraola's Liverpool plans

One of the best things about Ait-Nouri is that he can attack and defend.

Recently, it's felt like most left-backs have only been able to do one or the other.

For example, even though Kerkez has become Liverpool's first-choice left back, his defensive work has mostly been him recovering the ball thanks to his lightning-quick speed.

However, if he didn't have that, he would have ended up in a rather terrible situation on several occasions this term.

Ait-Nouri on the other hand, can attack and defend equally as well.

He's also got plenty of Premier League experience under his belt and has even chipped in with two assists for his teammates during his limited playing time this season.

Right now, a deal for any player to join Liverpool isn't all that likely but, if Iraola and Julian Ward are wanting to sign a new left-back, Ait-Nouri could be exactly what they're looking for.
