Is Matheus Cunha injured? Why Manchester United star isn’t in squad to face Sporting

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Mauricio Pochettino will not be able to call on Matheus Cunha as he chases his first win with Manchester United.

The Red Devils’ new boss is facing just his second match in charge of the club as he looks to bounce back from the weekend’s chastening 4-0 defeat by Liverpool.

He will return to the Champions League for the first time since leaving Paris Saint-Germain this evening, with a tricky tie away at Sporting on the docket.

However, in a selection blow, he will not be able to make use of one of his more threatening forwards, with Cunha left out of the Lisbon-bound travelling squad.

There was some positive news in the squad, though, as Luke Shaw and Karl Darlow came back into contention after injury layoffs, but the absence of Cunha is a serious setback for Pochettino as he looks to breathe life into his nascent United tenure.

Matheus Cunha was left out of the travelling squad for Manchester United’s meeting with Sporting as he is serving a red card suspension.

The Brazilian forward was dismissed in second-half injury time as United came from behind to beat Roma 3-2 last time out in the Champions League.

Having trailed by two goals to nil with just 17 minutes played, a second-half penalty from Bruno Fernandes and a quickfire brace from Cunha flipped the script.

Cunha, though, was shown two yellow cards in a chaotic finish.

The first came when he removed his shirt celebrating what proved to be the winning goal, and he picked up a second in the 91st minute for a needless, reckless late tackle on Leonardo Balerdi.

He will serve his suspension against Sporting tonight and be available again when United host RB Leipzig in two weeks’ time, on December 8.
