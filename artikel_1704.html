Can Ethan Nwaneri play against Arsenal for loan club Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League tonight?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal’s Champions League clash with Borussia Dortmund tonight carries an interesting subplot - with Gunners starlet Ethan Nwaneri currently on loan at the Bundesliga outfit.

Nwaneri joined Dortmund on a temporary basis towards the end of the summer transfer window, the attacker drafted in to replace summer signing Giannis Konstantelias, who suffered an ACL injury on debut.

The clash between Nwaneri’s parent club and current team has led many Arsenal fans to wonder if the Hale End academy product could line-up against the Gunners.

In the FA Cup, loan players are prohibited from playing against their parent clubs, whilst in the Premier League, the rules state a “player shall not play against the transferor [parent] club unless the transferor club and the transferee [loan] club agree in writing that the player can play against the transferor club.”

UEFA rules state that clubs cannot exert "any influence whatsoever over the players that another club may field in a match". This means that Arsenal could not add a clause to Nwaneri’s loan deal preventing the teenager from facing his parent club.

Ultimately it may be a moot point regarding Nwaneri’s eligibility to face his permanent employers. The 19-year-old has struggled for game time since joining Dortmund on a temporary basis, with Nwaneri last starting for Dortmund in mid-October.

In the last two Champions League games, Nwaneri has come off the bench in the 89th minute - and all season the winger is yet to play more than 65 minutes in a game.

Arsenal hold the option to recall Nwaneri from his loan early in January, but boss Mikel Arteta said that he remains “convinced” the spell at Dortmund remains beneficial for the England under-21 international’s development.
