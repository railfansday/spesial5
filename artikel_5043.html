Bitcoin is Vanishing into “Deep Storage” Faster than Miners Make It

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Miners produce about 450 new Bitcoin a day, but over the past year about 630 a day moved into wallets dormant for 10 years.

Miners produce about 450 new Bitcoin a day, but over the past year about 630 a day moved into wallets dormant for 10 years. Sean Hagan says that means about 1.4 coins went into deep storage for every new coin mined, after the trend stalled in 2025. He explains why Bitcoin may be disappearing into deep storage faster than new supply is being issued.
