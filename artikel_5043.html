Coinbase Launches ChatGPT Plugin for Trading Bitcoin, Stocks, and Derivatives

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Coinbase launched an official plugin for ChatGPT on Tuesday that lets users connect a Coinbase account and trade bitcoin, other cryptocurrencies, stocks and derivatives without leaving the conversation, provided they approve every order.

The company said the tool puts trading “in the same chat you use for everything else.” Users can connect an account, review a portfolio and place orders from ChatGPT on the web, on mobile or on desktop. Chief executive Brian Armstrong wrote that “starting today anyone with ChatGPT can connect their Coinbase account” and can “trade right inside your Coinbase account.”

Coinbase posted a link to the official plugin in ChatGPT’s directory. Its developer documentation describes a short setup: sign in, review the access being requested and choose which portfolios the plugin can see. Interactive confirmation cards in the chat cover new orders, edits, cancellations and conversions between dollars and USDC. Read-only requests, such as checking a balance, do not use them.

The announcement did not single out bitcoin, but the plugin sits on the same rails as Coinbase for Agents, the company’s toolkit for connecting AI apps to a trading account. That documentation lists spot trading in more than 900 pairs, including bitcoin, along with eligible U.S. stocks and dated futures from Coinbase Financial Markets. The examples include a market buy of bitcoin and a dated bitcoin futures contract. Orders go through Advanced Trade, so those fees and eligibility rules apply. Fees vary by product, fee tier and whether an order adds or takes liquidity, and they are shown on the order preview.

The official plugin is narrower than the full agent toolkit. A separate custom connection is required for the company’s Global Derivatives products and for x402 payments, which agents can use to buy market data. Coinbase already offers U.S. customers stock and ETF trading on its own platform, but the agent documentation says equity orders trade during regular market hours by default. The plugin should not be read as a new around-the-clock stock venue. What a user can buy still depends on the products already attached to that account.

Armstrong’s “anyone with ChatGPT” line is broader than the documentation. The official plugin is currently available to U.S. Coinbase users, and availability also depends on a user’s ChatGPT account and workspace. If the plugin does not appear, the company tells users to check their workspace’s plugin policy. In a disclaimer attached to Tuesday’s announcement, it said product availability, eligibility and Coinbase entities “vary by jurisdiction,” that trading can result in a total loss, and that “AI agents may fail.”

The same post said users direct all AI activity and assume full responsibility. The developer documentation adds that agents can misinterpret instructions or produce inaccurate output, and that the user is responsible for reviewing and authorizing trades. Access can be revoked later in an account’s security settings, under connections.

OpenAI’s help pages describe a distinct Finances feature in the United States that can connect bank and credit-report data but cannot place trades, and note that third-party plugins that support transactions are a separate product.

Tuesday’s plugin is the most visible version of work the company has been shipping for months. In June, it introduced Coinbase for Agents, a login and a command-line tool that let an AI app trade inside limits set by the user, including a gradual rebalancing toward a bitcoin-heavy portfolio. On Nov. 11, it added Agent Controls, server-side caps on trade size and 24-hour volume, plus an auto-stop if a portfolio falls by a set percentage. Those controls live on the company’s servers, so an agent cannot switch them off.

The official plugin is meant for people who want to stay in the loop. The company also documents a custom ChatGPT connection for automated workflows that does not add a confirmation card before each trade, though sign-in, consent and authorization for financial actions still apply. For the official plugin, every bitcoin, stock or derivatives order still needs a human yes.
