Yeti is cutting its full-year forecast as cautious consumers weigh on sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The cooler maker sees adjusted sales growth of about 6% in 2026, down from 7% to 8%; Yeti stock fell 14% in premarket trading

Yeti Holdings cut its full-year sales and earnings outlook on Thursday, saying it expects a cautious consumer environment to pressure demand in the fourth quarter. Yeti stock dropped 14%, to $37.01, ahead of the opening bell.

The Austin, Texas-based maker of coolers and drinkware now projects adjusted sales will grow roughly 6% for the year, a step down from the 7% to 8% range it had previously forecast. It lowered its adjusted earnings per share range to $2.90 to $2.95 from $2.94 to $3.00. Adjusted operating income is expected to grow 7% to 9%, compared with 10% to 12% in the prior outlook.

The company kept its adjusted operating margin target of about 14.9% and its free cash flow forecast of $200 million to $225 million.

“Looking ahead, we anticipate the consumer environment will remain cautious and put pressure on demand in Q4,” Matt Reintjes, Yeti’s chair, president and chief executive, said in a statement, according to The Wall Street Journal. He said sales momentum was inconsistent in the U.S. and Canadian markets at points in the third quarter, and that shoppers appear to be choosier about discretionary purchases.

Yeti reported third-quarter net sales of $509.4 million, up 4% from a year earlier. Adjusted sales rose 6% to $517.9 million. Net income rose to $44.4 million, or 60 cents a share, from $39.4 million, or 48 cents a share. Adjusted earnings were 91 cents a share, up from 61 cents.

Drinkware sales rose 8% to $284.0 million, and the coolers and equipment category was unchanged at $215.6 million. International sales grew 19% to $119.1 million, and U.S. sales rose 1% to $390.3 million.

Wholesale sales rose 19% to $237.3 million. Direct-to-consumer sales fell 6% to $272.2 million, with the company citing declines in U.S. owned e-commerce and the U.S. Amazon $AMZN marketplace.

Third-quarter sales came in below the $523.1 million analysts polled by FactSet expected, according to The Wall Street Journal. Adjusted earnings per share topped the 84 cents those analysts expected. For the full year, they had forecast adjusted earnings of $2.98 a share.

Yeti scheduled a conference call on the results for 8 a.m. ET on Thursday.
