17 Best Airbnb Cabins for a Picture-Perfect Outdoor Retreat (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Picture this: A wood-burning stove, a claw-foot tub, no cell service, and no news. Are you in heaven? Or are you in one of the best Airbnb cabins we’ve rounded up across the US? Whether it’s an organic design marvel in Washington that would make Frank Lloyd Wright weep or a sauna-equipped “bird house” cabin in Wisconsin that piques your interest, there are so many places to enjoy the great outdoors in style. So, if immaculate cabin rentals make up your fall and winter travel mood board, look to these cozy cabins, tiny homes, chalets, and comfortable cottages for a weekend getaway. Think: elevated A-frames and Lincoln log-style beauties, fleshed out with curated amenities and thoughtful design details. Here, you can get a little lost in the beauty of the wilderness (without giving up too much in the way of modern accommodations).

Perks: Indoor fireplace, private patio, barbecue grill, sound system, security system, free parking

A steeply pitched roof and black metal siding give this tiny cabin near Oregon’s Mount Hood a striking silhouette against the towering pines. Designed by a professional architect, the 190-square-foot retreat takes its cues from shinrin-yoku, the Japanese practice of forest bathing. The exterior pairs dark metal with warm pine cladding, while oversized windows look out to picturesque woodland views. Inside, there's a sound system, queen bed, wood-burning stove, and compact kitchenette, but the real luxury is falling asleep to natural white noise from the surrounding forest. Even the shower comes with a forest view, making this a particularly appealing place to spend a weekend off the grid. From $381 per night.

Perks: Mountain views, forest views, private balcony, fireplace, fire pit, nearby hiking trails, nearby skiing, private security

At nearly 11,000 feet above sea level, this Breckenridge cabin puts you practically eye to eye with Colorado’s towering Quandary Peak. Getting there involves climbing about 60 steps through the evergreens, but the payoff is a private balcony with sweeping mountain views. Inside, the renovated retreat has a Scandinavian-inspired aesthetic, with warm wood finishes, cozy textiles, and a gas fireplace for snowy evenings. A spiral staircase leads to the sleeping quarters, while outside, a firepit offers another spot to take in the alpine scenery. Downtown Breckenridge is just 15 minutes away, though between the mountain views and the deck, there’s plenty of incentive to stay put. From $365 per night.

Perks: Mountain view, wood-fired hot tub, indoor fireplace, fire pit, private backyard, hiking, free parking

The Blue Ridge Mountains are the main attraction at Doah House, a cabin perched high above Virginia’s Shenandoah Valley. A 16-foot wall of sliding glass doors opens onto a wraparound deck, making the one-of-a-kind forest views visible from anywhere in the living room. Inside the blonde wood interiors, a wood-burning stove, heated floors, and a lofted bedroom equipped with a queen-sized bed add to the appeal. The bathroom features Moroccan tile, a rainfall shower, and a floor-to-ceiling window overlooking the trees. Outside, a private wood-fired hot tub and firepit make a strong case for spending the evening at the cabin, though Shenandoah National Park, Appalachian Trail hikes, and local wineries are all within reach. From $204 per night.

Fresh eggs from the chicken coop, herbs picked straight from the garden, and a creekside sauna are just a few of the unexpected perks at Firelight at Shope Creek, a collection of eight cabins spread across 22 wooded acres outside Asheville, North Carolina. Cozy flourishes fill this 650-square-foot retreat with modern design, like a wood-burning stove, heated bathroom floors, and a spacious deck overlooking the surrounding mountains and meadows. There's also a private hot tub just steps from the king-size bedroom, ideally positioned for stargazing after dark. Guests can wander the property's private hiking trails, gather around the communal firepits, or settle into the covered outdoor pavilion, which has its own fireplace. And despite the secluded setting, downtown Asheville and the Blue Ridge Parkway are only about 20 minutes away. From $320 per night.
