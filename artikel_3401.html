Bungie Officially Shows Off Marathon’s Most Exciting Update Feature

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Marathon's big Symbiosis update has been teased for weeks, but now it's almost here. Developer Bungie is set to release the major overhaul of its extraction shooter on Dec. 8, bringing plenty of new features such as dedicated PvE. One of the biggest additions, however, is an entirely new shell (think of these as Marathon's character classes) called Bishop. Now Bungie has shared our best look at Bishop yet.

The studio posted a cinematic trailer for the shell on its official X account. The caption for the trailer simply reads: "Bishop Shell // Hunter Killer. Strike once, Strike fast." The video itself, although it isn't gameplay footage, offers a look at the shell's visual design and hints at how it will play. As the description suggests, Bishop seems to be a fast and lethal option for players. We see a Bishop shell taking on another group of Runners (other players) and utilizing some of the game's typical weaponry alongside what appear to be unique skills.

Bungie previously described Bishop as "a new Runner shell built around the fantasy of a fast, surgical blade-wielding robot." That appears to be true. Bishop has an extendable blade in one arm and boosters on its legs, which together should let players move quickly, close large gaps, and get within striking distance of enemies for a lethal strike. More details will be shared closer to Symbiosis' release.

Symbiosis will be the culmination of Bungie's months-long work to revitalize Marathon after what was perceived as a poor launch​​​​​​​. While the update includes the addition of new weapons and the Bishop shell, it also focuses on tweaking the core experience of the extraction shooter to make it more generally appealing. That will mostly be accomplished by giving players a way to engage with the game outside PvP.

Marathon will get​​​​​​​ a handful of features that echo the social features and PvE systems in Bungie's Destiny games​​​​​​​. A social space called Limbo will let Runners connect and challenge each other in low-stakes duels. Expedition will be a roguelite PvE-only mode. Deathmatch, which will pit two teams of three against each other in bite-sized matches​​​​​​​, will be introduced in beta. You'll also be able to play Marathon for free from Dec. 8 to 15​​​​​​​ to celebrate the release of Symbiosis.
