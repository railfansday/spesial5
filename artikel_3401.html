Amazon is offering early Black Friday discounts on like-new versions of its 2024 Kindles, with prices starting at $161.99 for the Paperwhite Signature Edition. These models are a generation behind Amazon's current lineup: The company introduced redesigned Paperwhite and Colorsoft models (plus accessories) in October, with the new Paperwhite now starting at $199.99, and replaced the 2024 Scribe with a third-generation model in December 2025. As for its “like-new” label, it means these are pre-owned devices that Amazon certified to look and work like new.

The Like-New Kindle Paperwhite Signature Edition (32GB) is down to $161.99 from $224.99, almost 30% off. For that price, you're doubling the storage of a standard Paperwhite and getting wireless charging, an ad-free lock screen, and a front light that automatically adjusts to your surroundings—on top of the same sharp seven-inch display, IPX8 water resistance, and up to 12 weeks of battery life as the standard Paperwhite, as noted by this PCMag review.

The Like-New Kindle Colorsoft Signature Edition (32GB) is down to $179.99 from $287.99, almost 40% off. And for that price, you're getting twice the storage for a standard Colorsoft, which is particularly useful for comics, manga, and graphic novels that take up more space than regular ebooks, along with wireless charging, an automatically adjusting front light, and an ad-free lock screen. However, as PCMag notes in its review, the color display produces more subdued tones than a conventional tablet, so the Paperwhite remains the more economical choice if you primarily read novels.

The Like-New Kindle Scribe (16GB) is down to $251.99 from $359.99, the biggest dollar discount in this roundup. Unlike the other Kindles here, it's built for reading and writing, with a 10.2-inch, 300ppi glare-free display and a Premium Pen with an eraser and shortcut button—making it the most versatile option for readers who want an e-reader that doubles as a digital notebook.

In short, yes, Black Friday still offers discounts that can be rare throughout the rest of the year. If there’s something you want to buy, or you’re shopping for gifts, it’s a good time to look for discounts on what you need, especially tech sales, home improvement supplies, and fitness tech. Of course, if you need to save money, the best way to save is not to buy anything.

Black Friday sales officially begin Friday, November 27, 2026, and run throughout “Cyber Week,” the five-day period that runs from Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday,  November 30, 2026. But Black Friday and Cyber Monday dates have expanded as retailers compete for customers. You can get the same Black Friday sales early, and we expect sales to wind down by December 2, 2026.
