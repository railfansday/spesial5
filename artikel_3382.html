We all love a classic movie quote. Maybe it's my age or perhaps it's the cinematic legacy of the decade, but I've found myself in many conversations where a line from an iconic '80s movie is casually referenced to approving nods or little chuckles.

And I've recently learned that Tom's Guide readers are pretty good at identifying movie quotes. As I type, 25 people have now named all 10 of the '80s movies from a single iconic line, with Ian A holding the top score. Meanwhile 31 have scored 10/10 at matching the opening lines to the correct '80s movie, and Steve L is the person to beat there. I clearly need to ramp up the challenge. This week's homework is naming the '80s movie from the last (English) line spoken. Go!

How did you do? While doomscrolling mercifully wasn't an option when these movies were released, perhaps you had nodded off by the time some of these last lines were spoken, or you simply haven't seen all of these classics? If it's the latter, hopefully I've inspired your next retro movie night.
