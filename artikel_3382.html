Dominic West Joins Irish-Set Drama ‘A Weekend In The Country’ — AFM

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

EXCLUSIVE: Dominic West (The Wire) has joined the cast of the Irish-set feature A Weekend in the Country.

Dominic Geraghty will direct West in his feature directorial debut. The official synopsis reads: Set over a weekend at Oughterard Castle in Ireland, the film follows Henry, the 29th Earl of Oughterard, who gathers his family and oldest friends to celebrate his 55th birthday. West plays Daniel, Henry’s closest friend from university and now a hugely successful actor. An adrenaline junkie, spendthrift and incorrigible mischief-maker, Daniel arrives with his younger wife Ayesha and becomes the weekend’s most disruptive presence. Among the other guests is Julian, who was Henry’s secret boyfriend at Trinity College Dublin and with whom he hasn’t spoken in thirty years; Julian’s younger lover, Marcus; Charlotte, Henry’s saintly younger sister; and Hamish, Charlotte’s viciously right-wing husband. Over the chaotic weekend, aristocracy clashes with socialism, religion with debauchery, and tradition with modernity. Lunch, walks, dinners, and dancing. Watch on Deadline

Production is scheduled to take place in early 2027 on location in Ireland. The film is produced by Lucinda Rhodes Thakrar (Boudica) for Picture Perfect and Giles Alderson (The Dare) for Figi Productions, with Nicky Kentish Barnes (Back to Black, About Time, Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again) serving as executive producer. The producers will be at the upcoming AFM with the title.

“Daniel is the life and soul of the weekend and the man most likely to set fire to it, and Dominic brings exactly that mix of charm and danger,” Alderson said in a statement.

“Having him play a celebrated actor among old university friends gives the film a wonderful extra layer. Director Dominic Geraghty has written something funny, honest and deeply moving, and Dominic West’s arrival is a big moment for the project.”
