Tinashe Crashes a Plane and Belts Out ‘Melatonin’ During Hilarious ‘GTA VI’ Stream

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The whole world has been counting down to Grand Theft Auto VI finally being released, and now that it’s here, people are calling out of work and school to play. One person who wasted no time? Tinashe, who dove straight into the video game for her fans through a hilarious livestream. The singer hopped on X right after midnight, tweeting: “Streaming GTA on twitch lets gooo.”

Once she was locked in, she got to work. The stream captures her cracking fans up by trying to make an epic getaway by flying an airplane and hysterically crashing it into the ground. She also screams her head off as she gets in a shootout but quickly runs out of ammo and has to rely on hand-to-hand combat. “I have no bullets!” she screams. “Holy shit, I’m going to have to beat the fuck out of them myself.”

At one point, during another shootout, she gets so stressed out that she starts singing the bridge of her own hit “Melatonin,” yelping, “Fucking with me, hope you know I’m what you asked for, asked for! Tinashe is a big gamer; in the past, she’s played a lot of Call of Duty: Warzone under the name SniperNashe and streamed it on Twitch and YouTube for gamer fans.
