Amazon MGM Studios India Comedy ‘VIBE’ Debuting On Prime Video

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

EXCLUSIVE: VIBE, Kunal Kemmu’s Amazon MGM Studios India‘s movie, starts streaming on Prime Video today.

The pic, which released theatrically in September, is high-stakes action-comedy focused on Hardik (Kemmu) and Bugs (Sparsh Shrivastava), two inseparable friends whose seemingly ordinary lives spiral into an unpredictable adventure when they are unexpectedly tasked with saving the nation from a looming threat.

Starting today, VIBE will stream on Prime Video in Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu.

Kemmu is the writer, director and star, and he also produces alongside Chirag Nihalani under the Drongo Films banner. Also in the cast are Yashpal Sharma and Dinesh Lal Yadav.

“VIBE has been incredibly special to me because it brought together so many things I love about cinema,” said Kemmu. “It has action, comedy, romance, and music, but at its core is a friendship that is tested in the most unexpected circumstances. I’ve always believed that some of the funniest moments emerge when ordinary people find themselves in extraordinary situations, and that’s exactly what we set out to do with this film.

“Being able to bring this story to life, both in front of and behind the camera, has been an immensely rewarding experience. It was equally wonderful to have a cast that brought such distinct personalities and energy to their characters, making this journey even more enjoyable. I’m grateful to Amazon MGM Studios for believing in our vision, and I’m delighted that audiences around the world can now experience and enjoy VIBE on Prime Video.”
