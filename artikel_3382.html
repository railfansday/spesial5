One of the biggest sleeper hits on the PVOD market this year is a nondescript action-thriller that will turn 20 years old in a few months. The movie wasn't exactly a major hit in its theatrical run, but it has had a robust life on home video. It inspired a television spin-off, and now seems to be attracting the same audience that tunes into shows such as Tracker and Reacher. The film in question is currently streaming on Paramount+ in the United States, where, according to FlixPatrol, it has spent nearly 90 days in the top 10 this year. The movie is also available on Tubi, but only for a little while longer.

The last decade has seen a revival of procedural content, both on linear television and streaming. Shows such as Yellowstone, Bosch, and The Lincoln Lawyer have found a sweet spot between old-fashioned episodic television on TV and the cinematic scope of streaming content. Unsurprisingly, this has led to a renaissance for films like the 2007 action-thriller, likely because those who tune into these shows are promptly pointed toward similar programming on the same platforms.

This is one of the reasons for the success of movies such as The Contractor, starring Chris Pine, and the continued interest in John Krasinski's Jack Ryan franchise. The 2007 action-thriller falls squarely in the same category, and is headlined by arguably the most high-profile star to make streaming his primary home. We're talking, of course, about Mark Wahlberg, who has starred in more than half a dozen streaming originals in the last decade or so, after experiencing enormous success as a big-screen movie star.

The movie is Shooter, directed by Antoine Fuqua. Shooter is based on a novel that follows a military sniper who is framed for a crime he didn't commit; the sniper is taken in by a kind woman while he's on the run from the government. Shooter grossed $95 million at the worldwide box office, against a reported budget of $65 million. While this is a much smaller haul than Fuqua's other action hits — The Equalizer, Olympus Has Fallen, and Training Day — the film ultimately found its audience at home.

This is reflected in the film's 80% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes, which it continues to hold despite a 50% critics' score. The aggregator website's consensus reads, "With an implausible story and numerous plot holes, Shooter fails to distinguish itself from other mindless action-thrillers." Shooter inspired a television reboot starring Ryan Phillippe, which ran for three seasons on the USA Network. You can watch the movie for free on Tubi until December 1.

Shooter follows a highly skilled marksman who is coaxed out of retirement for a mission to thwart an assassination plot against the President. However, he finds himself framed for the attempt, prompting a perilous journey to clear his name while uncovering a deeper conspiracy.
