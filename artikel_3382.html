John Carpenter’s Zombie Apocalypse Is Officially Available for Free

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Hannah covers horror, sci-fi, and games at Collider, where she writes news and features, reviews new releases, and interviews the people who make them. She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

A truck can get four people out of a zombie-infested town, provided somebody knows how to drive it. In one of John Carpenter’s latest forays into horror, taking a shortcut through the mud can leave the whole crew stuck while the infected close in. Somebody has to work the winch, while everybody else has to buy them enough time to use it. If there’s a better way to find out which of your friends you’d trust in a zombie apocalypse, it probably takes longer.

There’s as much Big Trouble in Little China in that setup as there is The Thing. Saber Interactive wanted the feel of a late ’80s or early ’90s action movie, and by the time the studio approached Carpenter, it already had the basics of its zombie outbreak. Saber’s Tim Willits told the Epic Games Store that Carpenter suggested additions to the story and composed music for the game. He also voiced a zombie in its animated trailer, which gives him a chance to enjoy the outbreak from the other side.

John Carpenter’s Toxic Commando joined Xbox Game Pass on November 12. It’s available on Xbox Series X|S, PC, and Xbox Cloud Gaming, with access through Game Pass Ultimate, Game Pass Premium, and PC Game Pass, depending on the platform. The first-person shooter originally launched in March and supports up to four players in co-op.

Your nightmare does not wait outside the door. It waits for you to close your eyes, which you are going to have to do eventually, because nobody has ever won that particular argument.

Your nightmare is not really the doll. It is the face of the person you tell, and the way they decide, very kindly, that you have imagined all of it.

Your nightmare has your number, knows your name, and has almost certainly been inside your house before. The worst part is that you already know them.

Your nightmare is not really about you. It is about the people asleep down the hall, and the feeling that whatever is here is not interested in leaving.

Saber brought ideas from both World War Z and SnowRunner into the game. The studio’s Swarm engine can put enough infected on screen to make a fortified position feel temporary, while the driving gives the squad decisions to make before the shooting starts. There’s time to look for supplies and prepare for a siege, and there’s always a chance that getting to the next objective will turn into a rescue mission for the person who insisted the truck could make it through.

The campaign runs nine missions, which gives a group something to work through together over a few nights. Solo players can fill out the squad with bots, although it's definitely a challenge to handle objectives on your own, leaving a lot for one person to do once things go bad. With friends, even an ugly escape can be the part of the night everybody remembers, especially if the person who got the truck stuck is the one who has to get it moving again.
