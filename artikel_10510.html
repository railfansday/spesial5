Goob’s viral arrest video exposed as staged charity promo after Joey Swoll callout

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Fitness influencer Goob’s apparent arrest sent social media into a frenzy after Joey Swoll posted footage of him being taken into custody, but Suffolk County police now say the entire scene was staged to promote a charity food giveaway.

Goob, whose real name is John Dorsey, is known for creating fitness content and elaborate holiday charity promotions. His latest Instagram reel told viewers to “Please watch until the end” while tagging the Suffolk County Police Department, HummusFit and GymKatie.

Joey Swoll, another prominent fitness influencer, initially presented the footage as a serious incident. His original Instagram post showed Dorsey apparently being escorted by police and was captioned, “Welcome to the show John.”

“I’m not gonna say what it is that he did, because I don’t have all the facts yet,” Swoll said. He then told his friend to “do better.”

Despite the growing speculation, Dorsey insisted, “It’s not a skit.”

The story changed after a Suffolk County Police Department spokesperson told TMZ that Dorsey was never actually arrested.

“It was part of filming social media posts advertising a charity food giveaway that is taking place later this month,” the spokesperson said.

Swoll later posted a follow-up reel captioned “UPDATE! 😳😳😳 @goob_u2,” while Goob’s own follow-up video asked viewers to watch until the end.
