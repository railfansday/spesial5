Xbox Publisher Announces Four Games Being Delisted By End Of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

All set to disappear in a few weeks' time

It's never good news when games get delisted on the Xbox Store, but we haven't actually got many for the end of 2026 as things stand, and at least we're getting some notice on the four titles that are set to be removed.

All of these games are coming from one publisher - GameMill Entertainment. Their games can be very hit-and-miss, but most of them involve some pretty big IP, and I've enjoyed my time with a few of them in the past.

The four games they will be delisting on Xbox very soon are as follows:

The delist announcement for all four of these games was shared on Steam, and it only mentions Steam. However, GameMill has done this kind of thing before, and it's usually resulted in delistings for multiple platforms including Xbox.

As for whether they're worth it? Well, Avatar: The Last Airbender - Quest for Balance doesn't exactly have the best reputation, while childish platformer Hotel Transylvania 3 only fares slightly better. Street Outlaws 2 is considered the better of the two and certainly looks intriguing, but still has mixed impressions based on what we've seen.

Honestly, you shouldn't be buying any of these games at full price in our view, but it's worth keeping an eye out to see if they get heavy discounts before the end of 2026. Street Outlaws 1 and 2 have already been reduced by 90% on Steam ahead of their delistings, and we reckon the same thing might happen on Xbox in the near future.
