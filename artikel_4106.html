Video: Long Journey Coming Up? Here Are Our Favourite Switch 2 Travel Accessories

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

With the holiday season rapidly approaching, we'd wager that you likely have a long journey or two to make in the coming weeks — we know we sure do! Switch 2 remains our console of choice for lengthy travels like these, natch, but over the last year and a half, we have begun accumulating a handful of accessories that make the experience that bit less painful.

Above, the ever-lovely Alex Olney showcases some of his favourite Switch 2 travel accessories that he has found so far. This includes everything from cases to headphones, portable docks to AR glasses (yes, really).

A bunch of great products get a shoutout from Mr. Olney, including the Anker Zolo power bank, Austrian Audio headphones, and Waterfield's particularly lovely Tech Folio Backpack to keep it all in. You'll find links to all of the featured products in the above video description, but we have also included a handful of them below.
