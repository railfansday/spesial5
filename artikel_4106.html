Feature: 25 Years Later, How Many Of These Original Xbox Launch Titles Have You Played?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

From the original North American launch on November 15, 2001

Welcome to the month of Xbox's 25th anniversary! Xbox will be celebrating that milestone officially on November 15th, but before we get there, we thought we'd use the chance to highlight the very first games that ever launched on Xbox.

To do that, we're going back to the initial list of North American Xbox launch titles, of which there were 20 in all. Some of them are obvious standouts, such as Halo: Combat Evolved, Fuzion Frenzy, Dead or Alive 3 and Project Gotham Racing, while others are more obscure - Arctic Thunder, Dark Summit, Mad Dash Racing, etc.

Here's the full list of all 20 launch titles:

Now, of course, some of these games are still playable to this day on Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S, while others have always remained locked to the original Xbox. The likes of Project Gotham Racing, Shrek and Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 2x are highly-requested additions to the Xbox backwards compatibility library, but it seems unlikely they'll ever make the cut at this point - almost certainly due to licensing reasons.

However, you may not know that games like Cel Damage and Oddworld: Munch's Oddysee are still playable to this day on modern consoles, and they're still well worth checking out despite their age.

So, how many of these original Xbox launch titles have you played? Let us know down in the comments section below, and please do give us your recommendations with some of the obscure ones - NHL Hitz is still one of my faves!
