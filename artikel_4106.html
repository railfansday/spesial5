007 First Light's 'Starter Pack' Is Available To Claim For Free On Xbox

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

IO Interactive's phenomenal James Bond adventure, 007 First Light, has just released a 'Starter Pack', letting players experience the game's first mission for free!

The Starter Pack whisks players off to Iceland's stormy shores, putting them in the boots of a young Bond long before he earns his license to kill. Fraser and I both love what IO has done with First Light, and think it's one of the best games of the year. The first mission is a particularly juicy opener too!

Above are some screenshots from the prologue 'Against All Odds', which tasks Bond with investigating a compromised MI6 drop, tracking down a missing science team and, well, just being Bond.

007 First Light currently holds a brilliant Metacritic score of 88 on Xbox, and we can't recommend the game enough. If you'd like to have a go for yourself, just search for '007 First Light' and download the 'Free Starter Pack' edition.

The game is also currently on sale via the Xbox Store. So if you enjoy this free little taster, you can bag the full adventure for its lowest price ever: £35.99 / $41.99.
