Mega Man: Dual Override Reveals Robot Master Design Contest Winner

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Capcom has announced the winner of the Mega Man: Dual Override Robot Master design contest, with the winning character set to officially appear as an in-game boss.

In a video hosted by director Shinya Ikuta and producer Hiroyuki Minamitani, the pair showcased the six finalists before revealing that the winner is... Cleanser Man!

Submitted by artist 'iregyuradabii', Cleanser Man was chosen as the winner "after many discussions and careful consideration", with Ikuta-san stating that the selection process was "incredibly difficult for the entire team". The remaining finalists will have their names appear in the in-game credits.

All of the 6 finalists had some really great and unique ideas, and the entire dev team has been very impressed and amazed at the amount of creativity on display.

It wasn't easy picking a winner, but we ended up deciding on Cleanser Man due to his whimsical motif and unique color pattern.

The lid part on the detergent has a very Mega Man-esque design to it, and we like that the detergent itself is very colorful as well.

You have to be careful not to introduce too many elements to prevent the design from becoming too cluttered, so we like that the artist kept things relatively simple.

But there's still enough detail to it to give it that genuine Mega Man feel.

Once again, thank you to everyone who joined in the contest.

We really appreciate the time and effort everyone put into this.

And here's a look at Cleanser Man. Be sure to check out the video above to see it in action:

Mega Man: Dual Override is scheduled to release on Switch and Switch 2 in Spring 2027. So far, five Robot Masters have been announced, including Twinkle Girl, Jet Man, Tank Man, Cook Man, and the prize-winning Cleanser Man. There are still three left to reveal, so we'll keep a close eye on future announcements.
