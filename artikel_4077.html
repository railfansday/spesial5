Unknown 9 Dev Being Shut Down Two Years After Disaster PS5 Launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Do you remember Unknown 9: Awakening? Published by Bandai Namco, the story-driven stealth-action title had a fairly hard marketing push ahead of its release back in October 2024 — but it was labelled a "failure" just months after its launch.

It was a commercial disaster, selling way below expectations, and developer Reflector Entertainment was subsequently hit with layoffs, before revealing that it would be put to work on an existing Bandai Namco property.

Bandai Namco itself had acquired the Canadian studio in 2020. We don't know how much the deal was worth, but it's now been confirmed that the publisher ate an "extraordinary" 3.5 billion yen loss on Unknown 9. That's approximately $22 million / £16 million / €21 million.

That's according to Bandai Namco's own press release, which also confirms Reflector Entertainment's closure.

"After comprehensively considering future business plans, necessary additional investment, and the possibility of profitability and business continuation, the Company has decided to discontinue Reflector’s business, dissolve and liquidate it," the company writes.

It's a brutal end for a team that has seemingly been in limbo since launching Unknown 9. Whatever existing property the developer was working on, the project clearly didn't make the cut.

We wish those impacted by the closure all the best for the future.
