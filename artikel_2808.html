Titans put Peter Skoronski, Tommy Townsend on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Titans ruled left guard Peter Skoronski out on Friday due to a hamstring injury and his absence is going to last beyond this Sunday's game against the Jaguars.

Skoronski was placed on injured reserve on Saturday. Punter Tommy Townsend (knee, Achilles) also landed on the list and both players are going to miss at least four games as a result of the moves.

The Titans also announced that they have waived running back Michael Carter. They signed guard Chandler Zavala, defensive back Isaiah Oliver, and punter Thomas Morestead to the active roster to fill the open spots.

Wide receiver Xavier Restrepo and linebacker Dominique Hampton were elevated from the practice squad to round out the day's moves.
