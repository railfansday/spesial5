Dolphins sign K Zane Gonzalez to 53-man roster; elevate QB Brady Cook, WR Jalen Reagor

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Dolphins announced a handful of roster moves ahead of Sunday's game against the Colts.

They have signed kicker Zane Gonzalez to the active roster from the practice squad. They also elevated quarterback Brady Cook and wide receiver Jalen Reagor from the practice squad on a temporary basis.

Gonzalez was elevated the last three weeks, so signing him was the only way to bring him up this week. He is 5-of-7 on field goals and 5-of-5 on extra points for the Dolphins.

Cook will back up Kyle McCord with Malik Willis now on injured reserve. Cook started four games for the Jets last season and the Jets went 0-4 in those contests.

Reagor was a 2020 first-round pick of the Eagles, but has not appeared in a regular season game since he was with the Chargers in 2024.
