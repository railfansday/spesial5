5 key plays help Rams feast on Packers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Los Angeles Rams feasted on the Green Bay Packers on Thanksgiving Eve, earning a convincing 26-13 victory behind a dominant defensive effort and a punishing running game. While the final score tells part of the story, it was a handful of key plays that ultimately allowed the Rams to take control and keep Green Bay from mounting a comeback. Defensive takeaways helped swing momentum in Los Angeles' favor, while the ground game consistently put the Rams in favorable situations and allowed them to dictate the pace. From game-changing turnovers to explosive runs, these five plays helped define the Rams' Thanksgiving Eve victory and showed just how dangerous this team can be when both sides of the football are clicking.

Nate Landman leads the NFL over the last two seasons with 8 forced fumbles. The Les Snead signing before 2025 was a fantastic find. It has led to a number of splash plays from the defensive leader.

8 minutes 31 seconds. An absolute clinic of a drive to start the 2nd half for Los Angeles. It featured 9 carries by Blake Corum. It featured the Rams going for 4-for-4 on 3rd down.

With the Rams holding the lead, they still needed to put the game out of reach. Blake Corum, the Rams spark plug took matters into his own hands with two big plays to set up a Brandon McManus field goal. The first was a swing pass to Blake Corum in the flat for 14 yards. The second was a 3rd & 1 run that popped for a 35 yard gain on the ground; bringing the Rams into Packer territory.
