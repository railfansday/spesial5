Jalen Brunson shows off his acting chops with appearance on 'Law & Order: SVU' with pal Mariska Hargitay

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Jalen Brunson is known as the "King of New York" after leading the Knicks to last season's NBA title. On Thursday night, he added another name to his résumé: Detective Jay Madison.

The Knicks guard guest-starred on the latest episode of NBC's "Law & Order: SVU" with his good friend Mariska Hargitay, who has played the character of Olivia Benson since the show's pilot aired in 1999.

During Thursday's episode, Brunson's scene begins with his father and Knicks assistant coach Rick walking into a restaurant in front of Hargitay. The 2026 NBA Finals MVP is sitting at the bar as Hargitay congratulates him on his recent promotion.

"Focused on my goals. Gave it 110%. Things worked out," Brunson responds.

Brunson then talks about a recent case he was involved that could help Hargitay's current investigation. Hargitay thanks Brunson for the information and then calls assistant district attorney Sonny Carisi asking for a warrant as she leaves the restaurant.

"It's a weird feeling, but grateful for the opportunity," Brunson said this week of his cameo role.. "Grateful for Mariska [Hargitay]. It's pretty cool."

Brunson has said in the past that he started watching the show when he was younger after his dad got him into it. He's remained a fan to this day.

"It's a pregame show for me. I've always loved that show," Brunson said on his "Roommates" podcast in May 2025. "I've never been like super super like starstruck — super starstruck. I saw [Hargitay], I was like 'Oh shit.' The first time we met, it was awesome. We talked and everything."

Hargitay, a big Knicks fan, was a regular at Madison Square Garden in the spring as the team went on its title run. Once she learned that Brunson was a fan of the show, a friendship was born.

The two have continued supporting each other's work with Hargitay showing at Knicks games — and the team's championship parade — and Brunson supporting her Broadway play "Every Brilliant Thing."
