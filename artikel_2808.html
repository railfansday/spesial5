NFL playoff picture: What does Packers-Rams mean for NFC standings?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The NFL's annual calendar expansion has a new destination this year.

For the first time, the league has scheduled a game the night before Thanksgiving, with the Los Angeles Rams hosting the Green Bay Packers at SoFi Stadium on Wednesday night. To help add to the excitement, the NFL has called upon U2 to perform during the halftime break.

But this game is important for other reasons. At the moment, the two teams are tied at 6-4, along with the Seattle Seahawks, and if the NFL playoffs began today, the Packers would be in due to a tiebreaker advantage over the Rams and the Seahawks. Which means this game could very well shape the eventual postseason picture.

Here is what that game means for the NFC standings.

Here are the current standings in the NFC:

1. San Francisco 49ers (9-1)2. Detroit Lions (8-2)3. New York Giants (6-3-1)4. Carolina Panthers (5-5)5. Chicago Bears (8-2)6. Minnesota Vikings (7-3)7. Green Bay Packers (6-4)

8. Seattle Seahawks (6-4)9. Los Angeles Rams (6-4)10. Dallas Cowboys (5-6)11. Washington Commanders (3-5-1)12. Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-7)13. Atlanta Falcons (3-7)14. New Orleans Saints (3-7)15. Philadelphia Eagles (3-7)16. Arizona Cardinals (3-8)

For the moment, the Packers are in while the Rams and Seahawks are out.

It comes down to tiebreakers. With all three teams at 6-4, under the NFL tiebreaker system we first "eliminate" one of the NFC West teams, either the Rams or the Seahawks. Seattle's 2-1 record in NFC West games, compared with the Rams' 1-2 record in division games, knocks out the Rams.

Then between the Packers and the Seahawks, Green Bay's 4-4 record in conference play is enough to give the Packers the edge, as the Seahawks are just 2-3 in conference games. So entering Week 12 the Packers are on the No. 7 line, while Seattle slots in at No. 8 and the Rams sit at No. 9.

With a victory, the Packers would hold on to the No. 7 seed, and own a head-to-head advantage over the Rams. However, they could not catch the Minnesota Vikings for the No. 6 spot, even if Minnesota loses to the Atlanta Falcons, as Green Bay lost to Minnesota earlier this season.

With a loss, the Packers would drop out of the playoff picture, and the Rams would take their spot at No. 7, at least for the moment.

As outlined above, the Rams currently sit at No. 9 in the NFC standings, due to the tiebreakers with the Packers and the Seahawks.

But with a win, Los Angeles would replace Green Bay as the No. 7 seed in the NFC standings, and could even move up depending on results from Sunday. For example, if the Rams win on Wednesday night, and the Atlanta Falcons beat the Minnesota Vikings, the Rams would move up to No. 6 in the conference, while the Vikings would drop to No. 7. Los Angeles would be 7-4 and the Vikings would also be 7-4, and the Rams would have a 5-2 record in NFC games, compared to 5-3 for Minnesota.

If the Rams lose on Wednesday night, however, their chances of making the playoffs take a big hit. They would remain outside the top seven, and the Packers would have a head-to-head tiebreaker advantage should the teams finish tied at the end of the season.

The winner of this game will have a head-to-head tiebreaker advantage, which is the first means of breaking a tie for a wild-card spot between teams.
