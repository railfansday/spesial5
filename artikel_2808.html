49ers hope Mexico City bus doesn’t get lost with 12 starters, AGAIN

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Playing an NFL game internationally requires a team to prepare for an opponent, a different environment and, occasionally, a bus driver who cannot find the stadium.

Kyle Shanahan can explain that last part.

The 49ers coach has stressful memories of his team's previous trip to Mexico City, where complications getting into the country, getting to the game and getting back home tested an operation built around schedules.

The 49ers won anyway, beating the Cardinals 38-10 in 2022.

Getting everyone where they needed to be proved more complicated.

San Francisco 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan answers questions after an NFL football game against the Dallas Cowboys in Arlington, Texas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/Julio Cortez

The trouble started Saturday night, when a lengthy immigration process delayed their arrival at the hotel and forced Shanahan to adjust the team's plans.

"That was a con, just in terms of how long it took to get through immigration," Shanahan said. "So we had to change all of our night meetings and stuff."

Teams typically arrive at the stadium three to three and a half hours before kickoff, leaving time for treatment, meetings, warmups and the routines players rely on to get ready.

A bus carrying about a dozen 49ers starters cut considerably into that window after getting lost on the way to the stadium.

Christian McCaffrey of the San Francisco 49ers runs for a touchdown against the Dallas Cowboys in Arlington, Texas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

Shanahan was spared the anxiety of the situation because, as he explained it, people figured keeping him informed would create another problem.

"I know they didn't tell me because they thought I would have freaked out, which I probably would have," Shanahan said. "But usually our last bus of players gets there like three hours before the game. And they got lost somehow on the way to the stadium. They didn't get there until an hour and 15 minutes before the game and it had like 12 starters on it, so that was somewhat stressful.

Winning made for an ending on the field. There was still the matter of leaving.

"After the game, it took us about 10 hours to get out of there, just because we had to wait through customs for the Cardinals to go first. So we got back the next Tuesday morning at like 9 a.m. because we played on a Monday night. But besides those, it's nothing."

San Francisco 49ers tight end George Kittle (85) reacts after a game against the Dallas Cowboys at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

The recollections provide some perspective as the 49ers prepare to face the Vikings in Mexico City on Sunday.

Coaches can script practices, organize meetings and account for every minute of a road trip. International travel adds variables that can undo those plans quickly.

For Shanahan, having everyone aboard is only part of the assignment.
