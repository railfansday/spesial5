Colts sign WR Nick Westbrook-Ikhine from the practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Colts signed wide receiver Nick Westbrook-Ikhine to the 53-player roster from the practice squad, the team announced.

They also announced they signed long snapper Jacob Bobenmoyer to the practice squad.

Westbrook-Ikhine originally signed with the team as a free agent on March 18. He was cut out of the preseason and signed back to the team’s practice squad on Aug. 31.

He has played three games this season, so he was out of elevations.

Westbrook-Ikhine has played 96 career games with 42 starts in his time with the Colts (2026), Dolphins (2025) and Titans (2020-24). He has totaled 137 receptions for 1,862 yards and 19 touchdowns.

Bobenmoyer has appeared in 98 career games in his time with the Jaguars (2026), Raiders (2023-25) and Broncos (2020-22). He has compiled 22 special teams tackles and one fumble recovery.
