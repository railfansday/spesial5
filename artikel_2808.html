Poll: Do you find the Brown-Tatum drama narrative interesting?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Tonight marks the first regular season game between the Philadelphia 76ers and the Boston Celtics since the offseason trade sending Jaylen Brown to Philadelphia took place. Understandably, we're receiving a fresh wave of reports and rehashing of events surrounding the purported fallout between Brown and Celtics star Jayson Tatum. I feel compelled to ask, do you find this narrative interesting?

I'll start here: I totally understand why this is a thing. The NBA has become a 24-hour, 365-day entertainment vehicle that many of us consume obsessively. That reality is driven as much by drama between personalities, both real and manufactured, as by what actually takes place on the court. Brown and Tatum played together for nine seasons, their entire careers overlapping for all but one year, for a club perennially in contention which won one NBA title. If there was a fallout between them, and there seems like there's definitely at least something there, that's a story. If Brown had a disagreement with how he was treated by Celtics management, that's a topic worth exploring. We've hit on those points on this very website (thanks for reading, tell your friends!). If you have lamb chops baking in the oven today and popcorn ready for your viewing experience tonight, cool. I get it.

Still, I can't find myself really caring about it. Whatever happened, it all happened behind the scenes. There was no public infighting or drawn-out trade demand. The Jaylen Brown trade came completely out of left field like a flash of lightning. What Brown said the other day almost exactly aligns with my thinking on the matter:

In today's NBA, guys change teams all the time. Plenty of breakups have been a lot messier than this one, including in Philadelphia. I'll chuckle when someone makes a clever joke about everything, but beyond that, I can't find myself getting invested in the whole thing. It's Philadelphia vs. Boston. That's more than enough for me.

How about you? Are you still finding this drama interesting or is it entirely in the rear view mirror for you as well? Vote and let us know how you feel in the comments.
