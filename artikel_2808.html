Confirmed lineups: Galatasaray vs. VfB Stuttgart

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

VfB Stuttgart are looking to continue their unbeaten start to the Champions League campaign as they face Turkish giants Galatasaray.

Sebastian Hoeneß's side have collected seven points from their opening three European fixtures and head into the encounter following an impressive 4-2 Bundesliga victory over Bayer Leverkusen on Saturday.

Meanwhile, Galatasaray have won their last four matches in all competitions, including a 4-2 victory away to Lille in their previous Champions League outing, and will be eager to build on that result.

With kick-off scheduled for 18:45 CET, both sides have announced their starting lineups, which you can find below.

Galatasaray: Cakir - Sallai, Sanchez, Bardakci, Jakobs - Torreira, Lemina - Akgün, Batrakov, Yilmaz - Osimhen

Stuttgart: Bredlow - Hendriks, Chabot, Mittelstädt - Leweling, Karazor, Prömel, Führich - El Khannouss, Undav - Demirovic
