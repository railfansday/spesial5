The Apple MacBook Neo finally got a real discount: Save $50 in Amazons Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

SAVE $50: As of Nov. 19, the Apple MacBook Neo is finally $50 off at Amazon and Best Buy — its biggest discount ever for non-students. Prices start at $649 (normally $699) for a model with 256GB of storage and no Touch ID.

It's an early holiday miracle: The MacBook Neo just got its first real discount. We've been waiting for the MacBook Neo to get a real discount since it launched in March, and especially since Apple increased the price in June.

Both variants of Apple's stellar budget laptop are currently $50 off at Amazon and Best Buy in Black Friday sales. The base model with 256GB of storage is now just $649, while the upgraded Neo with a 512GB capacity and Touch ID has fallen to $749. Target is charging 99 cents more for them, FYI.
