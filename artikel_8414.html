Investing - Theory, News &amp; General • Re: Chasing Yield. Is there a point where it is worth the risk?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

What I know for a mortal certainty is I would never have the inclination to put enough into a fund like this for it to really matter.

Basically, I have enjoyed gradually building up my global business empire, an empire that now includes ownership shares in something like 10000 for-profit companies around the world. And gradually building up that business empire has served me well so far.

So the idea of taking investments out of my global business empire in order to make temporary bet on junk bonds strikes me as an unnecessary distraction. And so I would never divert funds from my global business empire to speculative junk bond funds at any sort of meaningful scale. In which case why do it at all?

So, basically, to reverse the common phrase: out for a pound, then out for a penny.
