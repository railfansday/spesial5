The best Black Friday AirPods deals save you $70 on the AirPods Pro and bring the AirPods 4 down to $89

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Black Friday is just under two weeks away, and headphones deals — including on AirPods — are starting to show out ahead of the year's biggest shopping holiday.

Black Friday sales start much earlier than the day itself, but it's in the immediate lead up (the period we're in now) that the deals start to really get strong. Case in point: the AirPods Pro 3 dropped to $179, slashing $79 off their list price.

Most major retailers Black Friday sales, including Amazon, have either started already in some iteration or kick off this week. This may mean further price dips on AirPods (the Maxes and AirPods 4 are also on sale), or first time major deals (as in the case of the AirPods 5). However, in years past we've seen retailers will wait until Thanksgiving or Black Friday to bring out any new record lows, and then AirPods can sell out quickly. All this to say, these early price dips are relatively substantial and not likely to get too much cheaper in the run up to Black Friday proper. So what's the best way to approach your AirPods purchasing journey this holiday season? In addition to gathering the best deals live now, we also used our deals expertise and years of experience covering shopping holidays and Black Friday deals to give context on which deals we're still holding out on.

The AirPods Pro 3 stand among the best noise-cancelling earbuds we've ever tested. At $179, they're both 28% off and just $10 away from their lowest price ever. Based on trends of Black Fridays' past, I expect that any further price drops will go down to $169 — possibly around $165 if retailers want to bring out a new record low. I keep my fingers crossed for $159, but I'm not holding my breath — and I wouldn't expect that price until Black Friday weekend, when it's likely to sell out quickly. In other words, if the maybe savings of $20 (and fighting other deal hunters) isn't too enticing, I recommend grabbing these noise-cancelling buds now.
