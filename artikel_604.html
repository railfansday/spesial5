‘Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping’ Devouring $85M Over Thanksgiving Weekend as ‘Focker-in-Law,’ ‘Hexed’ Launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Lionsgate's sixth film in its dystopian franchise looks to win its sophomore frame after notching series records with its debut Monday and Tuesday.

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is claiming the head of the table at the Thanksgiving weekend box office as Focker-in-Law and Hexed enter the arena.

Lionsgate’s sixth film in the dystopian Hunger Games franchise screens in 4,018 North American theaters for its sophomore frame, as it grossed around $15.1 million on Wednesday. Director Francis Lawrence’s adaptation of author Suzanne Collins’ 2025 prequel novel is looking at a five-day sum around $85 million to take its domestic cume to roughly $233 million through Sunday. After opening to the fifth-biggest launch in Lionsgate history last weekend with $119 million domestically and $225 million worldwide, The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping has continued its momentum. The $150 million-budgeted film, which earned an A- CinemaScore, grossed $15.98 million domestically on Tuesday to notch the franchise’s biggest first Tuesday, narrowly topping the $15.96 million for 2013’s Catching Fire. Sunrise’s Monday sum of $12.8 million also bested the series’ previous top opening Monday of $12.3 million for Catching Fire.

The East Coast has been impacted by a major storm stretching from the Midwest to the Eastern seaboard, leading the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York to be moved to Saturday for the first postponement in its 100-year history. The inclement weather could slow down moviegoing throughout the weekend, including on Black Friday, which tends to be the biggest day for the box office over the Thanksgiving frame.

Disney released Hexed in 3,725 North American theaters on Wednesday, with the animated fantasy film collecting an estimated $7.6 million on opening day, including $2.1 million in Tuesday previews. Directors Jason Hand and Fawn Veerasunthorn’s movie, which carries a budget north of $200 million, could be heading for a five-day launch around $35 million. This looks to be down a bit from the $45 million opening that the studio had initially been projecting.

Hailee Steinfeld, Rashida Jones and Jodie Foster lead the voice cast for Hexed, which centers on a mother and her teenage daughter grappling with the girl’s magical powers. The studio has been hopeful that it will continue to run throughout the holiday corridor and follow the lead of this year’s original animated features with pleasing performances, including Goat and Hoppers, although other recent originals like Forgotten Island have struggled.

Earning an A- CinemaScore, Hexed holds a 66 percent critics score on Rotten Tomatoes. The Hollywood Reporter critic Angie Han wrote in her review that it offers “more than enough to make for a pleasurably transportive trip to the theater.”

Universal Pictures’ Meet the Parents sequel Focker-in-Law launched in 3,382 North American theaters on Wednesday, grossing $5.3 million on its opening day. Director John Hamburg’s film appears to be in line for a $36.3 million opening over the five-day frame, also a bit below the initial projection in the $45 million range. Ariana Grande joins franchise returnees Ben Stiller and Robert De Niro, as she plays a woman set to marry the son of Stiller’s Greg Focker.

Meet the Parents opened to $28.6 million domestically in 2000 (en route to its $166.2 million domestic cume). Sequel Meet the Fockers launched even bigger with $70.5 million over the five-day Christmas frame in 2004, while 2010’s Little Fockers dropped to $45 million for its five-day opening frame.

Focker-in-Law earned a B+ CinemaScore from audiences and holds a rough 29 percent critical score on Rotten Tomatoes. In his review for THR, critic Frank Scheck wrote that the sequel “feels like the nadir in a series that recycles the same tired jokes in increasingly desperate manner.”

Disney animated features are known for debuting over Thanksgiving, as Moana 2 opened to a massive $225 million over the five-day weekend in 2024 to help that year’s holiday frame set the all-time record with $423 million across all titles. Last year, Zootopia 2 launched on the Wednesday before Thanksgiving and collected $158 million over the five days to help lead the frame to a combined sum of $290 million. The total for this year’s holiday frame is expected to rank below both of those.

In limited release, Netflix launched director David Fincher’s The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth on 252 Imax screens in North America. The Brad Pitt-led follow-up to 2019’s Once Upon a Time in Hollywood appears headed for around $5 million domestically over the five days before it begins streaming Dec. 23.
