8 Great Airbnbs in Marfa to Book for a Design-Forward Vacation

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

If you’re a design and art lover, Marfa is one of those places you need to see at least once. With about 1,800 residents, it’s a blink-and-you’ll-miss-it town in the high desert of West Texas, put on the map by the minimalist artist Donald Judd. Judd left New York for Marfa in the 1970s, bought buildings downtown, and later took over a decommissioned army post outside of town. He filled it with permanent installations, turning the town into a creative hub that continues to attract a steady stream of art-devoted acolytes.

But even those who aren’t chasing the museums or the Prada Marfa store will get something out of the town to love; the nature-driven traveler, for example, can find some of the best stargazing in the country. So whether your itinerary is packed with gallery visits or you plan on doing little more than watching the sun set from a porch, kick things off by booking an Airbnb in Marfa.

These eight spots offer just as much inspirational design as the town itself; here’s where to stay in Marfa for a memorable desert getaway.

Quite naturally, this home takes its cues from Donald Judd, with clean lines and furniture that leaves plenty of space around it. It’s laid out as three separate studios, each with its own entrance, bath, and walled patio, so a group of friends can come together but still have a quiet place to retreat. The main studio is anchored by a fireplace with a low sofa and two lounge chairs—ideal for cozy nights in—while the library studio has a vintage wood-burning stove and worktable. Judd’s work at the Chinati Foundation is minutes away and the Marfa Lights viewing area is a short drive.

Amenities: Private soaking pool, fireplace, wood-burning stove, full kitchen plus two kitchenettes, Starlink WiFi, pet friendly

In this beautiful two-bedroom rental, a large tumbleweed hangs from the ceiling of the sunroom and the floor-to-ceiling glass doors open onto Southwestern-style gardens. The desert theme is not subtle, but it is done in an elevated, tasteful way—right down to the limewashed walls that echo the tones and textures of the West Texas landscape.

Head outside to the private courtyard to discover an oversized chiminea; stay inside, and you’ll find a fireplace and a hi-fi record player. It sleeps a small group or a couple of friends, and the detached casita next door can be booked for bigger groups.

Amenities: Fireplace, outdoor chiminea,WiFi, flat top grill and smoker, AC, dog friendly

Built in 1937 and carefully restored, this Spanish Revival home keeps its original wood beams and plaster walls, but has been warmed up with textiles and Western artwork. From the pink kitchen to the blue tile that outlines the television, this is definitely a house where style leads.

In fact, each of the four bedrooms in the main house has its own flavor: the Library Suite is lined with books, the Treetop Suite looks out over the desert. A detached casita with its own king bed and kitchen sits across the property with an expansive outdoor area. The whole place sleeps 10 adults, which makes it great for a group trip. The living room, with its fireplace and roomy sofa, is where everyone is likely to end up after a day at the galleries, which are a short walk away.

Amenities: Fireplace, detached casita with kitchen, WiFi, dedicated workspace, AC, outdoor backyard

This solar-powered house sits alone across 1,000 acres of ranch land, with the Rio Grande at the edge of the property and Mexico on the horizon. There is a modern wood-burning stove inside which heats the living room, and both bedrooms have their own tiled baths and windows framing the view. It’s a true escape for two couples; or perhaps a writer who needs a distraction-free setting from which to work (especially since there’s no cell service, only a landline and internet). Chinati Hot Springs is 20 minutes away for a soak, and Big Bend Ranch State Park is about an hour off for mountain biking.

Amenities: Solar powered, wood-burning stove, chef’s kitchen with gas stove, outdoor grill, washer, internet and landline, outdoor TV

Few houses in Marfa get photographed as often as this 320-square-foot box on the edge of town. It looks like one of Donald Judd’s sculptures turned into a place to live—to see the real things, Judd’s concrete and aluminum works at the Chinati Foundation are a short walk away.

Downstairs is an open kitchen with a dining area and a walk-in shower; two doors open from the kitchen straight onto a shaded patio with outdoor dining. A steep staircase leads up to the bedroom, which juts out over the first floor and faces the desert. You can watch the sunrise from the queen bed, and a heated mattress pad helps on cold fall nights.

Amenities: Fire pit, shaded patio with outdoor dining and loungers, heated bed pad, full kitchen, walk-in shower

Attention, green thumbs: The garden here is so stunning, it’s been featured in four books. The hosts co-wrote a field guide to Chihuahuan Desert plants and have filled all 1.25 acres of the property with native plantings and walking trails. The outdoors is very much the focus here: This casita is a 500-square-foot room, with a kitchenette and large covered porches that look out to the garden and mountains. Its al fresco appeal extends after hours, too. McDonald Observatory has certified the property as night-sky friendly, so after dark you can see even the Milky Way from the porch.

Amenities: Covered porch, native garden with walking trails, kitchenette (induction cooktop, convection toaster oven, microwave)

Most of the action at this Marfa adobe happens out back, under a big pergola with climbing vines, two long outdoor sofas, and many areas to sit and gather. Indoors, rooms are filled with bold paintings and modern furnishings like a low curved sofa in chocolate brown, worn leather armchairs, an iron chandelier that hangs over a white brick fireplace, and a cute checkerboard floor kitchen. Downtown is within walking distance, so you can stroll to galleries and dinner and come back to a fire.

Amenities: Fireplace, covered pergola patio with outdoor lounge and dining, dedicated workspace, WiFi, AC

If you’re coming to Marfa for a milestone birthday or special event, book this place for the barn alone. It’s a historic 1,250-square-foot space with room for a large dinner or party. The home itself was newly renovated to include a cook’s kitchen, a gym, and two king bedrooms—all set under 10-foot ceilings. Best of all, you’ll be just a short walk from downtown, and since dogs are welcome, you can extend the guest list to the whole family.

Amenities: AC, dedicated workspace, barn for events, gym, WiFi, pet-friendly
