Unlike the days of yore, Black Friday isn’t really just on the Friday after Thanksgiving anymore. Honestly, it’s more than Cyber Week too. We basically have a whole month of deals to look forward to once November rolls around. And if you were looking to stock up on your favorite skincare products, try out a new beauty device, grab a gift set for your bestie or mom, or just avoid paying full price for your shampoo and conditioner (me), this month has some fantastic deals for you.

I’ve covered Black Friday as Cosmopolitan’s beauty editor for the past five years (!), so I know what beauty deals are actually worth shopping during this huge sales event versus better deals you can find throughout the year. As a shopping expert and beauty editor at Cosmopolitan, I’ve been tracking all the Black Friday beauty deals for a few weeks already, and there are quite a few things you can already get on a good discount right now.

Huge deals this year include everything from LED masks (I’m really into the 20% off sale at CurrentBody right now), microcurrent devices, blow dryers, Korean skincare, luxury perfume, new makeup, and so much more.

Keep in mind that there will be sales all month. I’ve only added the truly good deals below, not including anything that I know (or think) will be on a steeper discount come Cyber Week and true Black Friday. If it’s below, it’s on a really good sale already, so there’s no need to wait. I’ve kept a log of legit everything going on sale this month, though (knowing what’s about to go on sale is a Cosmo editor perk), so keep checking back here for the full report all month.
