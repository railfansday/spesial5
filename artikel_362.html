Amazon has dropped the M5 MacBook Air to a new record-low price for Black Friday — act fast to save $300

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

SAVE $300: As of Nov. 23, the 13-inch M5 MacBook Air (16GB RAM, 1TB SSD) has hit a record-low price of $1,299 in Amazon's Black Friday sale. That's $300 off its list price of $1,599.

Apple deals are always a treat to find in Black Friday sales, but that's especially true in a year where price hikes hit the brand. If a MacBook has been on your shopping wishlist during this seasonal sale event, Amazon's here to help you out, dropping a deal on the 13-inch M5 MacBook Air.

As of Nov. 23, this MacBook model is listed at $1,299, $300 below its new list price, marking a record low since Apple raised prices in June. Just last week, we reported this model on sale for $1,349, so it is a nice surprise to see the extra $50 knocked off it in the lead-up to Black Friday proper. The 13-inch M5 MacBook Air, which launched this year, is a mighty powerful upgrade. Mashable's Timothy Beck Werth wrote that it "might be too powerful for its own good" in his review, noting that it "takes everything good about the MacBook Air and adds the faster M5 chip" for improved performance.

Plus, it features a stunning Liquid Retina display, up to 18 hours of battery life, and Apple Intelligence, alongside a 1TB SSD and 16GB of RAM. This size is one we recommend for college students, as it's "just as fast and pretty as the 15-inch model, but easier to carry across campus."

Who knows how long this discount will stick around. Act fast to save $300 on the M5 MacBook Air at Amazon.
