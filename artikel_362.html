The Star Wars: Unlimited Ashes of the Empire Prerelease Box hits record-low price at Amazon — save $4.50 ahead of Black Friday

SAVE $4.50: As of Nov. 24, the Star Wars: Unlimited Ashes of the Empire Prerelease Box is on sale for $25.49 at Amazon. That's a 15% discount on the list price.

Amazon's Black Friday sale is really ramping up, and it's hard to believe the official big day is still a few days away. The retailer kicked off its discounts last week, and we've seen a good range of items drop in price. For trading card fans, there's plenty to get excited about.

As of Nov. 24, this Star Wars: Unlimited Ashes of the Empire Prerelease Box has dropped to a new low price of $25.49.

The set gives you everything you need to get started with the new expansion, which focuses on the heroes and villains from the Battle of Endor.
