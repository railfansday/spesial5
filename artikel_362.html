With Black Friday officially less than two weeks away, Pokémon fans still have plenty of trading card deals worth jumping on before the big event kicks off. Whether it's the incredibly popular 30th Celebration expansion or the newly released Delta Reign you're trying to buy, Mashable has picked out the best available price cuts right now.
