Anthropic Prices at $83 in the Biggest IPO in History. Here’s What to Watch When Trading Starts.

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

By Adria Cimino – Nov 19, 2026 at 6:05AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Anthropic ›

Today is likely to be a historic one for the stock market. Anthropic (ANT +0.00%), one of the most well-known names in artificial intelligence (AI), is making its market debut. The AI lab priced its initial public offering at $83 yesterday, increased its offering due to high demand, and raised more than $81 billion in the biggest IPO on record. This surpasses Space Exploration Technologies, which held the record until this point.

AI stocks have been driving market direction over the past few years amid investors' enthusiasm about this new opportunity in tech. This "next big thing" could make companies more efficient and innovative, and therefore power earnings higher. Chip designers and manufacturers, as well as cloud services companies, have been big winners in this movement so far.

Now, Anthropic is the first AI lab to go public, offering investors a fresh AI investing opportunity. (Rival OpenAI says it aims to launch an IPO at some point but hasn't offered a time frame.)

Here's what to watch when Anthropic starts trading.

So first, a quick catch-up on Anthropic and its IPO. The company, founded by Dario Amodei and a small group of researchers -- all formerly of OpenAI -- back in 2021, is the name behind AI model Claude. The various versions of this model have helped Anthropic's revenue soar over the past three years as individuals and enterprise customers rush to use it. For example, in the first nine months of this year, revenue soared more than 1,200% to about $33 billion.

Anthropic increased the number of shares sold to 980 million and priced them at $83 apiece, raising $81.3 billion. SpaceX, by comparison, raised $75 billion during its IPO, then through the exercise of an overallotment option, that figure increased to more than $85 billion.

Now, let's consider what to watch today as Anthropic trading begins. It's important to note that IPO stocks generally start trading well after the 9:30 a.m. Eastern Time market opening. This is because market makers and underwriters must hold an auction to determine an opening price. Buy and sell orders will come in and be collected, and the official opening will take place when a price is established and a certain equilibrium among orders is in place -- so that normal trading can unfold for the rest of the trading day. It's possible that Anthropic shares will begin trading late in the morning or around noon.

The company will debut with a fully diluted -- this accounts for all shares that exist -- market value of about $1.5 trillion, putting it on par with today's market giants such as Nvidia and Apple. It took years of trading for these older tech powerhouses to reach a trillion-dollar market cap, so Anthropic's entry at this level illustrates the magnitude of the AI investing theme.

This year, other AI-related IPO stocks rose on their first day of trading, with Cerebras Systems climbing 68% and SpaceX advancing almost 20%. So, if history is a guide, today could be a positive one for Anthropic and its shareholders. And this means that companies that have invested in Anthropic, such as Amazon and Alphabet, also may see positive momentum today.

Amid all of the excitement, however, it's important to remember that one or even a few days of trading won't determine whether the company or the investor is successful over the long term. If you're thinking of investing in Anthropic, it's critical to consider the full picture, including the potential risks and rewards. And if you decide it's right for you, aim to buy at a reasonable price -- this might mean waiting for a dip -- and hold for the long term.
