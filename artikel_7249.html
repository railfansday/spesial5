Senior iOS Engineer, Foundations at Raya

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

5+ years of professional iOS development experience, with strong proficiency in Swift, UIKit, and MVVM.

Strong experience with RxSwift or similar reactive programming frameworks, and with Redux-style state management patterns.

A track record of shipping consumer mobile apps to large audiences, with strong judgment on architecture, performance, and user experience.

Experience owning client platform or infrastructure work that other engineers depend on (modularizing a large iOS codebase, build and CI systems, shared libraries, design systems, or release tooling) and driving its adoption.
