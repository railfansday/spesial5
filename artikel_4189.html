Professor Layton And The New World Of Steam English Voice Cast Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

It has been a tense wait to see whether Level-5 would bring in real, human voice actors for the regional dubs of Professor Layton and The New World of Steam, or whether it would slip into the ol' AI that its CEO seems to bring up at every given opportunity. Fortunately, it's positive news.

The dev has today revealed the game's English voice cast, and it's headed up by Christopher Robin Miller and Lani Minella — the returning North American Layton and Luke voice actors from the main series. You can even hear a snippet of them in action on the game's website, and yes, the nostalgia is a welcome listen.

The returning actors will be joined by the likes of Amber Lee Connors (Persona 4 Revival, Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave, Unicorn Overlord), Nic Olsen (Genshin Impact), Arthur Romeo (Street Fighter 6, AI: The Somnium Files - nirvanA Initiative) and more.

Interestingly, Cat Protano (Xenoblade Chronicles X: Definitive Edition, Trails in the Sky) seems to be taking on the role of Luke in the UK dub, and not the previous main series actor, Maria Darling.

The website also showcases the cast list for the game's French, Italian, German and Spanish dubs, all of which use real voice actors — what a concept!

Pre-orders for the New World of Steam's PC and PS5 releases have gone live today, with Switch 1 and 2 pre-orders expected at a later date.

It's worth pointing out that the newly published Steam page doesn't feature the site's AI disclosure, which is required for all games that use visual or other in-game AI assets. The lack of a disclosure doesn't mean AI hasn't been used under the hood (CEO Akihiro Hino said it hasn't been used anywhere in the game), but the last few years following Level-5 have made us cautious at every turn.

Years after its initial reveal, Professor Layton and the New World of Steam finally got a firm release date back in the September Direct: 10th December. We're cautious about getting our expectations too high around it, but the returning voice cast goes a long way toward alleviating some initial fears.
