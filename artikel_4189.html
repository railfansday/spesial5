Shard Squad combines Vampire Survivors and Pokémon in a fast-paced roguelike, now in pre-registration

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Well, it could only be so long today before we had yet another Survivors-like hitting the headlines. Fortunately, this is another one that follows the trend of actually interesting spins on the genre, as Shard Squad, a fusion of Pokémon and Vampire Survivors, opens pre-registration on iOS and Android.

Yes, if that concept doesn't grab you immediately, then you're likely not familiar with either franchise. In Shard Squad you unlock a variety of quirky creatures, each with their own element-based abilities, as you work to save the kingdom of Mellunia by fighting your way through hordes of enemies.

I've been pretty pleased with the glut of new Survivors-likes hitting mobile. Doubtless there are some stinkers still floating around, but the vast majority that have crossed my desk have had interesting ideas even if you break it down to just the elevator pitch.

Certainly, I'll bet some of you are chomping at the bit when you here Vampire Survivors meets Pokémon, and by all accounts it looks as though Shard Squad is set to deliver on such an exciting promise. So if you're interested be sure to pre-register for Shard Squad ahead of its expected release on December 10th!
