Katoto double sends Lyon to 6-0 Champions League win at Juventus

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

To help deliver timely coverage of women's football, we may use AI tools to support content creation and editing. While we strive for accuracy and editorial quality, readers should independently verify important information.

Marie-Antoinette Katoto struck twice in the 31st and 35th minutes as Lyon took Juventus apart at the Stadio Pozzi Lamarmora on Wednesday 11 November. The match was played on matchday 4 of the Champions League league phase, and Jonatan Giráldez’s side were 0-3 up by half-time.

Melchie Dumornay, Lily Yohannes, Korbin Rose Albert and Wendie Renard added the rest. Renard’s goal came at the second attempt in the first minute of stoppage time, after she had missed a penalty moments earlier.

The breakthrough took until the 31st minute, when Katoto opened the scoring. Four minutes later she doubled the lead from a Jule Brand assist. Before the break Dumornay made it three, finishing a move set up by Tabitha Chawinga in the 40th minute.

Katoto thought she had completed her hat-trick in the 52nd minute. VAR ruled the goal out.

Lyon kept pushing. Yohannes scored in the 80th minute and Korbin Rose Albert added a fifth in the 85th. Juventus defender Martina Lenzini was booked for a foul in the 83rd minute, and Yohannes later picked up a yellow card for Lyon. Renard then failed from the spot before putting the ball in to complete the scoring.

The numbers matched the scoreline. Lyon had 24 shots to Juventus’s 3, with 10 on target against 2. They also built 5.02 expected goals compared with 0.21 for the hosts. Lyon had 58% of the ball and completed 584 passes from 652, while Juventus managed 422 from 491.

Juventus did not win a single corner, against 4 for the visitors. Lyon also had 6 of their efforts blocked, and only the 4 saves made at the Juventus end kept the margin from growing further.

The gap was stark given recent history. The previous meeting between the clubs, on 19 November 2025, finished 3-3, and Juventus had beaten Lyon 2-1 on 23 March 2022.

Lyon have now won their last 6 matches in all competitions, a sequence that includes a 7-1 win over Lens, a 6-0 win over Inter Milano and a 4-0 victory against Paris Saint Germain. Juventus had come into the game on the back of three straight wins, over Sassuolo, Fiorentina and Austria Wien.

Both sides return to domestic action at the weekend. Lyon host Saint-Malo in the French top flight on Saturday 14 November, and Juventus travel to Inter Milano in Serie A on Sunday 15 November, against the same opponents Lyon beat 6-0 in their recent run.
