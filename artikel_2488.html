Los Angeles has lost five straight regular-season matchups against Baltimore, including a 30-23 setback at home in 2024. But the Ravens are in disarray themselves, having lost four games in a row after getting off to a 4-1 start. Their top two quarterbacks also were knocked out with injuries -- potentially for the rest of the season -- in their Week 8 setback against the Buffalo Bills.

The injury required surgery that is expected to keep him sidelined for 6-10 weeks. If the Ravens are out of the playoff picture, it's unlikely they would put Jackson back on the field this year.

Backup Tyler Huntley sustained a season-ending toe injury in that same contest, and undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano started Baltimore's Week 9 loss to the Jacksonville Jaguars. The club acquired Jacoby Brissett from the Arizona Cardinals just before that game, and the veteran journeyman will make his Ravens debut on Monday Night Football.

The 33-year-old Brissett threw for 1,719 yards with 10 touchdowns and seven interceptions in eight games with Arizona before being benched in Week 8 and subsequently losing his starting job to Gardner Minshew. Brissett did guide the Cardinals to a season-opening victory over the Chargers, however, throwing for 277 yards and a TD in a 26-14 triumph.
