Learnosity is a B2B company providing assessment infrastructure to the publishers, platforms, and product teams serving the global learning industry.

The numbers: 47 million learners, 1.02 billion assessments and 24 billion questions delivered a year.

Build is designed for every team that works on assessment. Now, let’s check out some of those teams.

AI-assisted authoring helps content teams create more without sacrificing quality, across more than 50 question types. Item Bank Health Check uses AI to find and fix issues across an entire item bank, from missing alt text to cultural sensitivity and language gaps, keeping content accurate, aligned, and accessible.

Engineers built on one API-first engine for the whole lifecycle: integrate once, deliver everywhere. Build is agent-ready, so teams can work with it directly from AI coding tools like Cursor, Claude, and Copilot. That leaves them free to spend their time on the features that make their product distinctive.

Operations teams get assessment that runs at the standard their institutions require: burst-ready autoscaling for peak periods, SLA-backed uptime, round-the-clock monitoring, ISO 27001 certification, and compliance with FERPA, COPPA, GDPR and WCAG 2.2 AA.

Learnosity’s AI work also covers grading and feedback. Feedback Aide provides accurate AI scoring and written feedback, from short answers to extended essays, at any volume. Learnosity Math is an AI-enriched math engine that can generate question variants and grade open-ended responses. Teams can choose manual, AI, or adaptive grading, aligned to their rubrics and always under human control.

Whatever the team, the model is the same: Learnosity handles the assessment infrastructure, so its customers can focus on the learning experiences that set them apart.
