USELESS Jumps 15% as Buyers Enter Sideways Memecoin Market

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Home » Altcoins » USELESS Jumps 15% as Buyers Enter Sideways Memecoin Market

A satirical meme coin stands out from the heavily-red market with double-digit percentage gains as some buying sentiment returns.

USELESS coin rose 15% after announcing its LONG launch, pulling buyers back into the token while conditions across the broader memecoin market stayed uneven. The move reversed near-term sentiment sharply, yet current signals still do not point to a clear or lasting recovery.

The LONG announcement gave USELESS a clear short-term catalyst and helped lift the price as traders stepped back in. A 15% gain stands out for a smaller, sentiment-driven token, where relatively modest shifts in demand can produce outsized swings, as seen after the midterms.

This price advance looks driven more by renewed attention around the launch itself than by any confirmed improvement in the wider trading environment. Event-driven moves can attract quick inflows, but they also tend to fade once the initial reaction cools or broader appetite softens.

The recovery faces at least two immediate risks: sellers using the bounce to exit positions, and market conditions that fail to support continued speculative demand. Without steady buying after the initial launch reaction, USELESS could struggle to hold its gains.

Memecoin price action depends heavily on momentum, attention, and liquidity. When any of those cools, a token that has risen quickly can also retrace sharply, especially if many buyers entered mainly for the nearest-term USELESS meme coin catalyst.

The 15% rise shows buyers remain willing to respond to new developments. Mixed market conditions, however, leave the meme rebound vulnerable until demand proves more durable.

For now, the LONG launch has brought fresh interest, but the token still needs consistent participation beyond the initial reaction if the move is to develop into something more lasting.
