"Rich Dad Poor Dad" author Robert Kiyosaki wrote on X on Nov. 20 that he is "most excited about Bitcoin's crash" and is "waiting to buy more." On the same day, Bitcoin was trading at $61,666, more than 50% below its October 2025 peak of about $126,000.

Gold fell 1.24% to $3,579 an ounce, and silver lost 0.95% to $42.42, according to TradingView data. The S&P 500 dropped 2.02% on the same day.

Kiyosaki started his post with a question. "CRASH: Good or Bad? Gold, silver, Bitcoin, crashing. Weak hands selling. Strong hands waiting to buy?" he wrote.

According to Kiyosaki, the reasons include "incompetence in FED, Banks, and Bond markets." He also wrote that America's debt is "only to grow bigger" as "Banksters steal our wealth via their system legally."

The author pointed to Bitcoin's fixed supply. There will be only 21 million BTC in total, and about 20 million coins have already been mined, Kiyosaki wrote. "As far as I know, there has never been a 'money' such as Bitcoin ever before," he added.

"With price of Bitcoin crashing, I can't wait to buy more coins… just because only 21 million are ever to be mined. It could be a 'nothing burger'…. or the best money I've ever spent," Kiyosaki wrote.

In late August, Kiyosaki said on podcasts that he owed creditors $1.2 billion. His ex-wife and business partner, Kim Kiyosaki, later said that the figure covered the loans of a partnership that owns about 1,500 rental units. "We have a lot of apartment houses with our partners," she said.

According to Kim Kiyosaki, Robert Kiyosaki's own share of the debt is $30 million to $60 million. Each of their real estate investments is held in a separate LLC. Robert Kiyosaki also commented on the debt at the time. "If it all comes to hell, you can talk to my attorney," he said.

In 2012, Kiyosaki's company Rich Global LLC filed for bankruptcy after a court ordered it to pay about $24 million.
