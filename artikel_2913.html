Kane out for 'few months' with ankle ligament tear

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

England captain Harry Kane will be out "for the next few months" after tearing ligaments in his left ankle during Bayern Munich's 1-1 Bundesliga draw with Cologne on Friday.

The striker, who last month won the Ballon d'Or for the world's best player, was forced off after 20 minutes following a challenge from defender Joel Schmied.

The 33-year-old initially tried to continue before being limping off the pitch to be replaced by Tom Bischof.

A Bayern statement said Kane had "suffered a torn syndesmotic ligament in his left ankle" and would "therefore be sidelined for the next few months".

Bayern trailed to a goal from England Under-21 winger Mikey Moore - who is on loan at Cologne from Tottenham - at the time, before they equalised through a Nathaniel Brown goal in Kompany's first game back after a five-game absence.

Kompany had been absent after being diagnosed with meningeal irritation - an inflammation of the protective membrane surrounding the brain and spinal cord.

Kane's injury comes on the same day England were drawn against Norway in the Nations League quarter-finals, with the two legs on 27 and 30 March.

He had scored for his country in wins against Croatia and Spain this month to help England finish top of their Nations League group, with this being Bayern's first game back after the international break.

Kane has scored four goals and registered eight assists in 10 league appearances this season, while managing four goals and three assists in four Champions League games.

Kane has had a good fitness record since his move to Germany from Tottenham in August 2023, but he does have a history of ankle issues.

He injured his left ankle playing for Bayern in March 2024, prior to an international break, and missed friendlies against Brazil and Belgium.

While at Spurs, the striker was ruled out for two significant spells in the second half of the 2018-19 season, missing seven games when he damaged ligaments in his left ankle and then nine more matches with a lateral ligament injury.

In 2017-18 he missed a month with a similar injury to the right ankle, and endured separate absences of six and four weeks during the 2016-17 campaign.
