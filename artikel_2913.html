Fantasy Football quarterback rankings for Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

We are into Week 9, and fantasy football managers need to make the right decisions at quarterback to keep their teams competitive. With favorable matchups, tough defensive tests, and plenty of potential surprises across the league, here are the Week 9 QB rankings to help guide your lineup decisions.

MORE: Fantasy Football studs & duds Week 9 of 2026 NFL season

Oct 18, 2026; Paradise, Nevada, USA; Buffalo Bills quarterback Josh Allen (17) throws in the fourth quarter against the Las Vegas Raiders at Allegiant Stadium. Mandatory Credit: David Brytus-Imagn Images

Allen at no.12…what's up with that? While he is at or near the top of fantasy points, he is having a very pedestrian season by his standards. He is only 14th in passing yards and 15th in TD passes. It is his legs more than his right arm that have saved him and the Bills this season. And this week against the Vikings, who have the top defense in the league, will make it extremely difficult for him…passing and running.

If Aaron Rodgers could tear up the Browns defense last week, Shough will also have his way with them at home. His rushing ability gives him a chance to add extra points to his total.

The Bengals defense hasn't been great this season…what's new? The Falcons play them this week in Spain, and these international games can often be entertaining, putting on a show for the international community. You know Joe Burrows and the Bengals are going to put up pts… they are averaging nearly 30pts per game. Penix will use all his tricks, and aside from leaning on Chris Olave, he'll get the ball to Bijan Robinson out of the backfield, early and often, to try to keep pace.

The Dolphins are a disaster, but Willis has been a decent QB for them. The benefit of a QB on a bad team is that they are always playing catch-up, forced to throw the ball. They have the perfect matchup this week against the Lions and their 32nd-ranked pass defense.

This is a big spot for McCarthy, cracking the top 12. John Harbaugh is proving to be a genius. The Giants have a road divisional game this week, heading to Philadelphia to face a wounded Eagles team. Sometimes wounded animals are the most dangerous, but sometimes they just die. The Giants will put them out of their misery this week. They cannot afford a letdown game, and neither Harbaugh nor McCarthy will let it happen.

Oct 4, 2026; Orchard Park, New York, USA; New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye (10) warms up before the game against the Buffalo Bills at New Highmark Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Gregory Fisher-Imagn Images

Maye is back at home after a couple of road games, which saw them go 1-1. Last week's win against the Dolphins didn't prove much, and neither did Maye's solid performance. They face a Packers team that puts up points, and they will have to match them. Maye is doing a fantastic job getting his second-tier WRs into the action, as well as his backup TEs and RBs. Expect another solid performance from Maye, whether they win or lose this one.

Lawrence has cracked the top five in MVP voting, sitting at +950. He has been putting up unbelievable numbers this year…He is third with 17 TD passes, with only four interceptions. He's become the leader Jaguar fans have been waiting for. And they have a stranglehold on the division. This week's matchup against the Ravens in Baltimore would normally be tough, but without Lamar Jackson at the helm, they are a shell of themselves. Expect Lawrence to put up numbers similar to what Josh Allen and Joe Burrow did to them over the last two weeks.

Darnold has to turn it around for the Seahawks. They are now 4-4, and their only path to the playoffs will be from the Wildcard…which they are currently on the outside looking in. They go from the vaunted Bears defense to the cupcake Cardinals defense, which ranks 31st against the pass. This is going to be a monster bounce-back game for Darnold…expect 150+receiving yds to Jaxon Smith-Njigba, and a couple of TD passes.

Burrow has been a monster this year. He leads the league with 20 TD passes and is fourth in passing yds. He has 13 TD passes in his last three games! He may not get to four or five again this week, but he should add at least three more to his league-leading total.

Oct 18, 2026; Kansas City, Missouri, USA; Kansas City Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes (15) throws a pass during the first quarter against the Los Angeles Chargers at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Denny Medley-Imagn Images

Mahomes was embarrassed two weeks ago in Seattle and followed it up with a heart-breaking loss in Denver. He is going to take it out this week, at home against the Jets. Last week Brock Bowers dominated the Jets, and Mahomes and Kelce are going to duplicate that this week. Bowers had seven catches for 92 yds and two TDs.

The Lions actually won last week with their defense. Facing the Vikings, Goff didn't have an easy go of it but did enough to win the game. This week, in Miami, he won't have the same issues. The Dolphins rank 27th against the pass and give up nearly 26pts per game. Expect Goff to put up over 30pts this week.

The 49ers face the Raiders at home this week. While the Raiders are 4-4 and put up a ton of points, they also give up more, with their defense allowing nearly 29 points per game. The Raiders even gave up 33pts to the Jets. Purdy will make mincemeat out of them. He may be a game manager, but he's the best in the league at it. He is tied for fifth with 15 passing TDs, and when he needs to score, he does. In this game, he'll need 24pts. It remains to be seen what Christian McCaffrey will have, even after a bye. Expect Purdy to throw for three TDs and a season-high in passing yards.
