Cowboys injuries: Jalen Thompson closer to return with new status

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Both the Dallas Cowboys and Tennessee Titans teams enter Week 11 dealing with injuries to several key contributors. For the Cowboys, it raises questions about their depth. Injuries have taken their toll on both sides of the football.

It's shaping up for another week that the Cowboys are without several primary starters. The Titans aren't entering this matchup at full strength, either. Tennessee's injury report features concerns at linebacker, in the secondary, but their offensive playmakers may be the most compromised group.

Here's the latest we know after today's practice.

Safety Jalen Thompson (Hamstring) was a full participant after being limited yesterday It's a great sign for Thompson as he hopes to return from injured reserve this week.

To no surprise many other players were listed as DNP today. DaRon Bland (Ankle), Tyler Booker (Ankle), Jake Ferguson (Knee/Ankle), P.J. Locke (Concussion), and Jonathan Mingo (Ankle) all missed practice Thursday. Booker and Bland are expected to miss some time.

Donovan Ezeiruaku (Ankle) was upgraded to limited today. Malik Hooker (Groin), Otito Ogbonnia (Finger), Joey Porter Jr. (Ankle/Foot), and Juanyeh Thomas (Hamstring) were cited as full participants.

For the Titans, Tony Adams (Concussion) was upgraded to limited Thursday as well as Tyjae Spears (Knee) and Carnell Tate (Shoulder). Cody Barton (Concussion) and John Franklin-Myers (Back) were full participants. Meanwhile, Kevin Winston Jr. (Shoulder) was downgraded to limited.
