Colts loss on Thursday Night Football is good news for the Jets in 2027 NFL Draft order

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Indianapolis Colts were blown out on Thursday Night Football by the Houston Texans. The defeat dropped Indianapolis to 3-8 on the season. As we move into the weekend, the Colts' first round pick is currently sixth overall. Of course that is relevant as the Jets own that pick from last year's Sauce Gardner trade.

There is currently a big logjam of teams with three to four wins. An Indianapolis victory would have dropped their pick outside the top ten even if only temporarily.

In the first round, the Jets have their own selection, Indianapolis', and whichever pick is higher between Dallas' and Green Bay's selections.

According to Tankathon, the Jets currently own three of the top twelve picks in the 2027 NFL Draft.

Picks 19 through 32 are determined by the results of NFL Playoff games.
