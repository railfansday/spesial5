Eyebrow raising West Ham admission : “I see a desperate team”

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

It is not, according to Scott Parker, only the fans who are going through the wringer at the current predicament of West Ham. Relegated, out of even the play-off places, divided, most of the best players sold in the summer and riven by angst and upset, the club feels anything but ‘United’ just now.

The players are going through it too, according to their newly appointed head coach.  As the BBC.co.uk article today suggests, Parker sees it as his role to mend the fractured club:

…Claims their article, detailing the manager’s eyebrow-raising admission that his players are just as mortified as supporters over the club’s predicament:

100%, I see a bit of a desperate team……Desperate as in the sense there is a burden here at this moment.

“Like with anything when there is an expectation, whether it’s an expectation of my 12-year-old son doing his maths work, that can easily become a burden.

“What I need to do is support this group and give them the belief and the confidence to show their real quality and for sure I will do that…..I can see the pain in these players. I see a committed group. It’s my job and my responsibility to try to get us pushing in the right direction.”

Just in case Parker didn’t have enough on his plate. Sometimes more than an arm round their shoulders, under performers need a break from the very thing that is causing them so much upset. There are on for two – Hermansen in particular – who looks as if he just needs a spell away from the limelight to go and rediscover how to do the basics of his job.

The weight of expectation is something that we’ve been highlighting for weeks and something Parker has to find a way to help his players handle better, for it is surely that which is causing the team to struggle so badly: Evidently the relegation hangover is far from cured just yet.
