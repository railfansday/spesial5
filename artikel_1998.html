West Ham 1 – 1 Preston North End : The morning after

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

That eight point gap between West Ham United in tenth and the Championship front runners is starting to look mighty huge with just sixteen games completed.

Of course the reality is that there are still thirty games remaining but West Ham will have to find a way of converting all the huffing and puffing into points or else we will find ourselves scratching around even trying to make the enlarged play off places come May next year.

“Wasteful West Ham”: Not my words but the description on the official club web site which sums up perfectly an afternoon of toil and frustration against the bottom side in the league.

Scott Parker pronounced himself happy with the effort, if ‘bitterly disappointed’ with the result. As were we all.

The team announcement produced a wave of optimism, and I’m not sure yet that we should ditch that: Tenth place is not where we expected to be at the end of November but there is still plenty of time to turn the West Ham supertanker back on course and away from the rocky peril of mid table, second division mediocrity.

But three years’ decline won’t be fixed in three games.

Introducing Fin Herrick and Edson Alvarez were bold decisions which could have backfired totally, especially after the maddening concession in the first half. Now Scott Parker needs to keep his nerve and persevere with that set up, go again on Tuesday night away at Fratton Park and trust for better luck and better finishing.

Ninety points still up for grabs this season, Hammers.

At least the ownership saw fit to ditch Nuno in time to turn the ship around. In the bad old days of David Sullivan, we’d still be on about game 5 of 20 ‘must win’ games before Nuno had to walk the plank. (Not sure where all the nautical terms came from this morning).

In any case, there’s not another manager who’d do any better in Scott Parker’s place right now. Don’t panic. Yet. COYI!
