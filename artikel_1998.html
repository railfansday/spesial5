CorSport: Two loans, two permanents – Milan hoping to be busy with exits in January too

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

AC Milan will be a key player in the winter window, not only with incoming reinforcements but also with a lot of potential outgoing activity.

As highlighted this morning by Corriere dello Sport (via MilanNews) under the headline ‘January Outgoings. Here are the moves’, the Milan management are looking to bolster the squad but also streamline it too, offloading players who are not an immediate part of the plans.

The path is mapped out for Christian Comotto and Francesco Camarda, two players who Ruben Amorim opted to keep despite enquiries arriving in the summer, but who are both playing very infrequently thus far.

The midfielder is a target for Daniele De Rossi’s Genoa, while the striker has attracted serious interest from numerous Serie A teams and several foreign clubs, ready to guarantee the young striker the playing time he needs to continue his development at the highest level.

Alongside those moves, Milan are working on some permanent exits too. The first player expected to leave Milanello once and for all is Pervis Estupiñán. The Ecuadorian full-back’s departure will come six months after Benfica and Aston Villa made a move to sign him, before Amorim vetoed his exit.

Ruben Loftus-Cheek’s contract expires at the end of the season and he is expected to complete a permanent departure. Besiktas in particular are pressing hard for the English midfielder and want to convince him to head to Turkey.
