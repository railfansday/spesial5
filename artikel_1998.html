What channel is Switzerland v Scotland? TV and live stream for Nations League clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A Scotland side still finding their feet under new head coach Sebastien Pocognoli conclude their Nations League B1 campaign on Monday night when they face group winners Switzerland in St Gallen.

Pocognoli earned the first home win of his tenure at the third attempt on Friday with a 2-1 win over North Macedonia but the unconvicing performance suggested the Belgian still has work to do to get the best out of his squad.

Switzerland have already secured automatic promotion to Nations League A after collecting 13 points from their five matches thus far, including a 3-0 win over 10-man Scotland at Hampden on September 29.

But while the hosts can relax, the game is not a dead rubber for Scotland, who can still claim a runners-up finish in the section with what would be a famous away victory over the side ranked 12th in the Fifa world rankings - 31 places above them.

The game is also hugely significant for captain Andy Robertson who will become Scotland’s most capped player, eclipsing Kenny Dalglish by reaching 103 appearances, if he plays on the night as expected.

Scotland will only finish second in Group B1 if they beat the Swiss and Slovenia fail to collect three points against North Macedonia in Skopje. Slovenia, on eight points, are one point ahead of Scotland in the standings but have a superior head-to-head record after their 2-1 win at Hampden last month that will see them claim the higher spot in the table if the sides finish on the same number of points.

Second place for Scotland would secure a Nations League promotion play-off in March against one of the third-placed teams from a Group A section. It would also guarantee pot two seeding for the Euro 2028 qualifying draw, which takes place next month. If Scotland finish third in Nations League B1, they will drop into pot three and face high-ranking teams in pots one and two.

The Nations League B1 fixture between Switzerland and Scotland takes place at Berit Sitterstadion in St. Gallen on Monday, November 16, 2026. Kick-off is 7.45pm.

The match will be broadcast live on BBC 2 and the BBC Scotland channel with coverage starting at 7.30pm.

The game can be live streamed for free on the BBC Sport website and the BBC iPlayer.

Scotland picked up no fresh injury concerns against North Macedonia on Friday but head coach Sebastien Pocognoli is expected to make changes. “I will need to consider the best approach for the game regarding the quality and also the fitness of some players,” he said. “We have some players in the squad who we have to manage. They cannot play two games in a row.”

Switzerland, meanwhile, are expected to rotate their squad. Head coach Murat Yakin will be without Internazionale defender Manuel Akanji, captain Granit Xhaka of Sunderland, Aston Villa's Johan Manzambi and Nottingham Forest winger Dan Ndoye. Yakin revealed that 20-year-old Sion striker Winsley Boteli will earn his fourth senior cap, while 23-year-old Young Boys midfielder Alvyn Sanches will be handed his seventh, both from the start.

The entire officiating team is from the Netherlands with referee Danny Makkelie taking charge on the field. He is joined by assistant referees Hessel Steegstra and Jan de Vries, with Alex Bos acting as the fourth official. Erwin Blank serves as the Video Assistant Referee (VAR), who will be joined in the VAR booth by Jeroen Manschot.
