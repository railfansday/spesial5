Portsmouth 1 – 0 West Ham United | The morning after

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

West Ham’s manager Scott Parker, from his demeanour last night, is understandably in a pretty dark place just now after losing his second game in charge, four games now without a win: West Ham now twelfth in the second division with two points from their last eighteen.

“If you are going through hell, keep going” is the oft-quoted popular saying, accredited to Winston Churchill but applicable to just about any circumstance when deep in the smelly stuff.

It’s only football. We understand. But it is supposed to be the very salvation from all of the bad things going on in the world right now.

Which is why it feels pretty lousy being a West Ham fan right now, especially those intrepid away fans who braved the worst of the British transport system and weather to turn up and support their team at Fratton Park.

“The day is darkest just before the dawn” – It was pretty damn’ dark at Fratton Park last night

How long must that journey home have felt, arriving home in the wee small hours replaying a kind of mental lowlights reel – and there were a few lowlights, let’s face it.

Most worrying of all is that the Championship’s top scorers seemingly now cannot put the ball in the back of the net.

However, a sneaky peek at the ‘bookies latest odds suggest they know something we don’t, with West Ham still odds-on (3/10 this morning) to finish in the top six. I’m not a betting man but strip away the emotion (which this ‘morning after’ column is supposed to do ) and there’s still a cold, calculating expectation that someone – presumably Scott Parker – can turn this ship around. There’s still time.

West Ham are the width of a goal post away from fourth place. The season is long and it is not yet December. Get behind whichever eleven are sent out to do battle with Stoke on Saturday. And wait for the dodgy decision in our favour or the outrageous deflected goal that we don’t deserve – which will signal a change of fortunes. COYI!
