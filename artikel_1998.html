‘Why can’t they?’ - Curtis Davies makes Leeds United claim as Crystal Palace example cited

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Former Premier League defender Curtis Davies does not see why Leeds United cannot aim for European qualification this season.

Leeds have made a strong start to the 2026/27 campaign and sit ninth in the table after 10 games.

Whites boss Daniel Farke has made an effort to quieten talk of a push for Europe, but there are pundits who see qualification as a genuine possibility.

Among them is former Hull City star Davies, who has outlined FA Cup glory as a potential route for Leeds.

He told BOYLE Sports, who offer football odds: “I think you have to dream. It's Leeds United, I'm old enough to know that Leeds are a big club and have obviously got a massive fanbase and I used to watch them play in the Champions League and get to the Champions League semi-finals.

“We're talking about a big football club there. However, recent history hasn't been that. But when you look at the likes of Sunderland after being promoted, getting themselves into Europe, and then Bournemouth, little old Bournemouth, I used to play against Bournemouth in League One.

“It shows that with Leeds United and the stature of that club, why can't they aim for Europe? And especially with the cup competitions, you know, going into the cup competitions trying to do your best in those, you never know.

“I just think when you look at the Crystal Palace model, when you look at the way they got into Europe and the trophy (FA Cup) that they won, it's a good way to do it.”

Leeds defeated Tottenham Hotspur before the international break and have another London giant to slay this weekend, with a trip to Chelsea booked in for tomorrow (November 21).

Xabi Alonso’s Blues will be heavily fancied, but Davies has described them as defensively “fragile”.

He said: “They are one of the most potent attacks probably in English and European football, but defensively they're very fragile.

“I think you're kind of only as good as the whole squad. It's not just about Alonso going in and plugging gaps and being able to teach these defenders how to defend.

“Ultimately it's Chelsea, and the expectancy will always be there to be there or thereabouts at the top. But I think they're kind of whereabouts you maybe expect them to be, I expect them to be in the top four.”
