Beats' Newest Over-Ear Headphones Are Down to a Record-Low $249 for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

While you can find plenty of deals on older headphone models during Black Friday sales, it’s rare to see a record-low price and a nearly 30% discount on newer, highly rated models from top brands. That’s exactly why this deal on the critically praised Beats 360 headphones should be on your radar. They’re now down to $249 (originally $349.99) in Amazon’s early Black Friday sale.

With a design that looks like the love child of the AirPods Max and the Beats Studio Pro, these have an adjustable telescoping frame and a unique modular design that lets you mix and match ear pad colors. The color-swapping bundle isn’t currently on sale, but it’s reassuring to know you can replace the ear pads as they wear out.

They’re controlled by two buttons on each earcup and powered by a dedicated Beats chip, with support for both Android devices and iPhones. You can switch noise modes and, on an iPhone, activate features like Personalized Spatial Audio in Settings. They last up to 32 hours with ANC on, which is longer than both the more expensive Sony WH-1000XM6 and AirPods Max 2.

According to Beats, the 360 offer up to 1.75 times the noise cancellation of the Beats Studio Pro. PCMag’s review, which gave the headphones an “Excellent” rating, was impressed by how well they blocked out everything from plane engine noise to café chatter. The noise cancellation falls short of pricier models, and PCMag found the Sony WH-1000XM4C slightly better in that respect.

The PCMag review says the headphones deliver “nice, deep bass, reasonably crisp highs, and a more neutral tuning than you might expect for a pair of Beats headphones.” If you’re after long battery life, rich audio, and plush earcups you can swap for a pop of color, $249 is a great price for the Beats 360 headphones.
