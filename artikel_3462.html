Yellowjackets is back for its fourth and final season on Friday, Nov. 20.

The series will finally reveal the survivors’ rescue after being stranded in the wilderness for almost two years. But re-entry into the real world won’t be easy for the teenagers after their dark experiences in the woods. In the present day, an old flame returns with her own agenda that could cause trouble.

Season 4 will be the last chapter of Yellowjackets, which first premiered in 2021. The series, created by Ashley Lyle and Bart Nickerson, became a breakout hit, earning 10 Emmy nominations.

Ahead is everything you need to know about Yellowjackets Season 4, including how to stream the final season online.

Yellowjackets Season 4 premieres on Friday, Nov. 20 at midnight PT/3 a.m. ET. New episodes will release weekly on Fridays until the series finale on Jan. 22, 2027.

New episodes of Yellowjackets Season 4 will be available to stream on Paramount+, but you’ll need the upgraded Paramount+ Premium tier subscription to watch the new episodes.

Once you’re signed up, you’ll be able to stream all new episodes of Yellowjackets right when they drop. Paramount+ is also where you’ll find past seasons of Yellowjackets if you want a refresher or a full rewatch before diving into Season 4.
