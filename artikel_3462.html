J.K. Rowling's "Secret" New Series Officially Confirms Release Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

There are very few authors who have enjoyed quite as much success as J.K. Rowling, and while the world will forever associate her with Harry Potter, the writer has spent more than a decade quietly building another enormously successful franchise. This time, however, there are no wands, spells, or magical castles to be found, just a grizzled private detective, and his partner.

The BBC has officially confirmed that Strike will return for its seventh series, titled Strike: The Running Grave, in December 2026. The new five-part mystery will once again star Tom Burke as private detective Cormoran Strike alongside Holliday Grainger as Robin Ellacott, adapting Rowling's seventh novel in the bestselling crime series published under her pseudonym.

When Sir Colin and Sally Edensor approach Cormoran Strike and Robin Ellacott, they are desperate to reconnect with their estranged son, Will. The Edensors enlist the detectives to gather evidence to discredit the Universal Humanitarian Church, a religious cult who have indoctrinated Will and siphoned off his trust fund.

Rowling originally published the Cormoran Strike novels under the pen name Robert Galbraith for the release of The Cuckoo's Calling, hoping to establish a new writing career without the impossibly high expectations set. However, media investigations quickly uncovered Rowling's true identity just months after the novel's publication. However, that's done nothing but help the series, which has since become a huge success with eight novels published to date, and a ninth currently in development. Rowling, of course, is also a large part of the creative team behind HBO's upcoming adaptation of Harry Potter.

The series stars Burke (Furiosa: A Mad Max Saga) as Cormoran Strike, Grainger (The Capture) as Robin Ellacott, James Fleet (Four Weddings and a Funeral) as Sir Colin Edensor, Nichola McAuliffe (Surgical Spirit) as Shelley Heaton, Keeley Forsyth (Happy Valley) as Mazu Wace, Fabian McCallum (The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power) as Will Edensor, John Lynch (The Fall) as Jonathan Wace, and Ruth Sheen (Another Year) as Pat.

Returning cast members also include Jack Greenlees (The Last Kingdom) as Sam Barclay, Tupele Dorgu (Coronation Street) as Midge, Natasha O'Keeffe (Peaky Blinders) as Charlotte, Sarah Sweeney (The Bastard Son & The Devil Himself) as Lucy, Ben Crompton (Game of Thrones) as Shanker, Stephen Hagan (The Larkins) as DCI Richard Murphy, and Caitlin Innes Edwards (The Witcher) as Ilsa.

The new adaptation has been written by Tom Edge, who previously adapted several installments of the series, including The Ink Black Heart, Troubled Blood, and Lethal White, with Sue Tully directing. Strike: The Running Grave is scheduled to premiere on BBC One and BBC iPlayer in December 2026, although an exact date has not yet been announced. Previous seasons are available to stream on BBC iPlayer.
