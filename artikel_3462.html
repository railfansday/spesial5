GTA 6: Should You Choose Cut Or Pure In "Dealer's Choice"

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Chapter 3 of Grand Theft Auto 6 will finally take Jason and Lucia to Vice City proper, and they'll be in for a whole new laundry list of shenanigans throughout their time in the big city. One of the very first missions you can embark on in Chapter 3 is "Dealer's Choice," Jason's first foray with the legendary Boobie.

As you listen to Boobie's explanation for the job, he'll make it clear that there's a way for Jason to make a little extra money if he can cut the product, and eventually players will need to make a choice about whether to use the cut product for some extra cash or to stick with the pure product.

Choosing the cut product in "Dealer's Choice" is a much more violent and chaotic option. Once you arrive at the meetup with the buyers and hand over the cut cocaine, one of them will take it into the car to check it out, noticing right away that something is off. They'll call Jason out and you'll have to make a choice of whether to hand over the product for half the price or tell them to take it or leave it.

If you give it to them half price, you obviously only get half the money, and they won't take the product because it's impure. If you choose to go the violent route, there will be a quick-time event that you'll need to complete to avoid getting capped in the head, and then it will be a full-on shootout with the buyers where you and Dre'Quan will need to escape.

When Jason and Dre'Quan go to Boobie's supplier, Tyrone, he'll further iterate that there's an opportunity to make some extra money if you go with the cocaine that's cut with baby powder. Dre'Quan will leave the decision up to Jason, and you'll be given a choice between the cut product with a bunch of dollar signs or the pure product by itself.

Choosing the pure product will result in the deal going off without a hitch and Jason acquiring the funds as expected, and you'll be able to complete the mission relatively easily, despite not making any "extra" money from cutting the product.

There really aren't any benefits to choosing the cut product, despite it appearing that you'll make more money if you do so. There are only downsides in fact, with the buyers either giving you only half of what's owed or trying to kill you outright. The alternative is much better, and the only potential altercation is with a vagrant on your way out as opposed to having to shoot your way out through dozens of enemies in the street and then making a getaway.

Ultimately, choosing the Pure product is a much safer and easier decision in Grand Theft Auto 6's "Dealer's Choice" mission in Chapter 3.
