Nvidia delivers earnings without Huang, consumer favorites get set to report, and more…

Team Rule Breakers’ updated 5-year price projection is $364.85

Team Hidden Gems’ updated 5-year price projection is $290.79

For 33 years, Jensen Huang was Nvidia (NVDA -0.86%). Today after the market closes, the company reports its fiscal 2027 third-quarter results, followed by an earnings call led by Colette Kress, interim CEO and CFO.

The backward-looking figures still matter, but will only serve as a closing tally of the Huang era. The call will be Nvidia’s real post-founder stability test. Here’s what we’ll be watching out for:

Nvidia’s business is excellent, but the stock now carries three open questions: who leads, how revenue is reported, and what the investigation finds. Wait for answers before making your next move.

The Nasdaq fell 2.49% yesterday. The S&P 500 wasn’t far behind, slipping 1.92%.

Weeks like this tempt investors to do one of two things. Some sell a long-held winner (e.g. Nvidia) because the headlines look scary. Others rush into the hottest new name (like Anthropic). Either move can matter as much as which stocks you own.

That’s why the Rule Breakers approach begins with behavior. This week, two of the six habits deserve extra attention.

Picture two investors who bought Garmin (GRMN +0.24%) on March 17, 2022. That’s the day Team Rule Breakers recommended it in Stock Advisor, at $111.93. Within months, sales started slipping. The stock fell to the low $80s. It didn’t climb back above $111.93 until late 2023. The first investor gave up during the slump and sold. The second gave the business time. Today, the shares trade around $283. The second investor’s patience paid off.

Sitting through the slump was only half the job. By late 2024, Garmin had roughly doubled from its recommendation price. That’s when many investors sell half to “play with the house’s money.” Anyone who cashed out at the double, around $224, have missed about another 25% since. Big winners rarely rise in a straight line. Letting one run means sitting through the swings.

You can’t control how Nvidia’s report lands or how Anthropic’s shares trade on day one. But you can choose how you respond.

Nvidia itself shows why that matters. It has been a standout pick for both Team Rule Breakers and Team Hidden Gems over the years. Hidden Gems’ May 2024 recommendation is still about 25 percentage points ahead of the S&P 500, despite the recent slide. Investors who let it run have been well rewarded.

Every Garmin investor in this story owned a good pick, too. Their results came down to what they did next.

The holiday shopping season starts with a checkup, with three consumer favorites reporting before the market opens on Wednesday.

Team Rule Breakers sees CrowdStrike (CRWD -2.86%) reaching $365 within five years, an implied annualized return of around 10%. It has been recommended three times in Rule Breakers, and all three are beating the S&P 500 by triple digits. As hackers become more sophisticated and digital break-ins more costly, businesses need cyber protection more than ever.

Our thesis for CrowdStrike held that “cybersecurity is essential for any business on the cloud, and that has become even more mission-critical these days.” That statement holds up remarkably well. The company’s AI-powered Falcon threat intelligence platform works to detect and prevent breaches before they start.

Given the prevalence of AI-powered threats, the company’s strong industry position, and continued impressive growth, we believe there’s a long runway of growth ahead for CrowdStrike.
