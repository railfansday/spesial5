Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Jumia narrowed its loss, but supply shortages and cocoa price drops pressure guidance.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 19, 2026 at 6:07AM EST

Jumia (JMIA -13.50%) reported a narrowing of its quarterly loss and double-digit revenue growth while simultaneously lowering its full-year guidance due to deteriorating conditions in key markets. Management reported that while business fundamentals remain stable, external factors including smartphone supply shortages and a collapse in cocoa farmgate prices have constrained consumer purchasing power. The company completed a $50 million capital raise during the quarter, anchored by the International Finance Corporation, to strengthen its balance sheet and provide flexibility for growth initiatives in 2027. Despite current headwinds, management confirmed its expectation to achieve positive cash flow in the fourth quarter of 2026 by selling through inventory and collecting corporate receivables.
