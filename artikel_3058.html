Over 150 child safety groups urge Amazon and eBay to drop AI toys

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A Fairplay-led coalition wants AI chatbot toys off sale until independent testing proves them safe, with an answer before Christmas.

A global coalition of more than 150 child safety groups and experts has asked Amazon and eBay to stop selling toys with built-in AI chatbots. The request came in an open letter sent on Monday to Amazon CEO Andy Jassy and eBay CEO Jamie Iannone.

The campaign is led by the nonprofit Fairplay, joined by groups such as Zero to Three and Jonathan Haidt’s Anxious Generation Movement, with signatories from every continent except Antarctica. The letter notes that over 50 AI toys were recently found on sale on Amazon in the US.

The groups want the toys pulled “this holiday season and until they are proven safe.”

The letter argues the two marketplaces already remove unsafe products, so suspending the listings is well within their power. Beyond that, it asks the companies to warn customers of the dangers. Moreover, the campaigners want a public answer before Christmas, so they can take their case to governments and parents.

Amazon has already moved a little, and the company said it removed some AI toys that NBC News and a consumer group had recently tested.

Pressure is coming from several directions at once, as a group of parliamentarians wrote to the two companies earlier this month with the same demand. Some retailers and toy makers have also started pulling AI toys of their own accord.

The campaign also marks an escalation, as last year, Fairplay and its allies simply told parents not to buy the toys; this year, they are going after the shelves themselves.
