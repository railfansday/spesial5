BrainsWay: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Strategic partnerships with enterprise networks could sustain BrainsWay's growth as its installed base expands beyond 2,000 systems.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 10, 2026, BrainsWay (BWAY -8.33%) announced that its revenue grew 34% year over year to $18.1 million. The company is converting high customer demand into recurring revenue while expanding reimbursement coverage for its non-invasive brain stimulation systems. Three things management said stand out.

Expanding the footprint of Deep Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (Deep TMS), a therapy that uses magnetic fields to treat mental health disorders, generates recurring revenue through remaining performance obligations (RPO), which measure contracted revenue under multiyear customer contracts.

Securing insurance coverage for SWIFT, an accelerated Deep TMS protocol that shortens treatment duration from weeks to days, increases clinic throughput and provider demand.

Higher production volume lowers the bill of materials (BOM), or component costs, allowing BrainsWay to convert top-line growth directly into higher profit margins.

Commercial momentum is pushing full-year financial results toward the upper end of management guidance. BrainsWay relies on expanding enterprise partnerships and lower unit production costs to drive long-term value while monitoring pricing discipline.

Strategic investments and broader reimbursement support expansion across global interventional psychiatry markets.
