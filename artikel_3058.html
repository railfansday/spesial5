Blackstone Digital (BXDC) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Its first investment is a $5 billion Amazon data center sale-leaseback deal.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust Inc. (BXDC +0.30%) reported its third-quarter results and announced its inaugural investment, a sale-leaseback agreement with Amazon. Management reported that the company remained substantially invested in cash during the quarter, as real estate operations are not expected to begin until the closing of its first transaction in late 2026. The company stated that it signed a definitive agreement to acquire a 75% majority interest in 13 data centers across two campuses for a total portfolio value of $5 billion. Management indicated that hyperscalers are increasingly utilizing sale-leasebacks to fund their capital expenditure requirements, which are projected to reach $800 billion globally in 2026.

Nicholas Pell: Thanks, Tim. Today marks an important milestone for BXDC. We are pleased to announce our inaugural investment, a majority interest in 2 newly constructed powered shell data center campuses executed through a sale-leaseback with Amazon. This is a landmark transaction negotiated directly with an investment-grade hyperscaler and underscores Blackstone's deep relationships with the largest global companies serving as the backbone of the AI and digitalization megatrends and who are the most relevant customers to BXDC's investment strategy. Since our IPO, our top priority has been finding the right assets to form a strong foundation for predictable cash flows, future growth and long-term shareholder value.

We diligently evaluated numerous transactions against the high standards of our defined investment strategy, continually assessing opportunities as market conditions evolved. We believe this disciplined approach has paid off as we successfully navigated capital market volatility and have now secured a unique high-quality investment for our shareholders that firmly underscores Blackstone's position as a leading capital solutions provider within the rapidly expanding stabilized data center market. I'll first discuss the transaction in detail before touching on the compelling opportunity set going forward. We have a signed definitive agreement to acquire a 75% majority interest in a $5 billion portfolio, comprising 13 powered shell data center assets across 2 campuses with approximately [indiscernible] megawatts of total power capacity.

These are newly built assets fully leased to Amazon on a triple net basis with an initial 12-year lease term and 32 years on a fully extended basis. Notably, Amazon will not only be our tenant, but they will also retain a 25% minority interest as our joint venture partner, which, among other benefits, demonstrates strong long-term alignment. We are buying these assets at a 7% going-in cap rate, the high end of our target range and a highly attractive entry point considering the underlying creditworthiness of our investment-grade rated tenant.

We are also able to structure the lease to align with Amazon's operating model and our focus on predictable cash flows with limited ongoing landlord responsibilities, another favorable outcome relative to our expectations for turnkey data center assets. The lease further benefits from 2.75% annual rent escalators, which supports an 8.1% GAAP yield and strong organic AFFO per share growth while preserving Amazon's responsibilities for the operations and maintenance of the assets. Taken together, we believe these terms meaningfully exceed the expectations set forth at our IPO more than offsetting higher debt financing costs and driving a going-in AFFO yield in the low to mid-5% range versus the mid- to high 4% illustrative yield we discussed during our roadshow.

Additionally, our investments have other notable attractive characteristics, including a basis that is more than 50% below estimated turnkey replacement costs, rents more than 70% below those of comparable turnkey assets in these markets, the substantial investment in the servers, racks and mechanical and cooling equipment that were all made by and maintained by the tenant and a parent guarantee from Amazon of payment obligations under the lease. The portfolio is diversified across 2 markets with more than 2/3 concentrated in Northern Indiana, the fastest-growing submarket of the broader Chicago data center market, which is experiencing robust demand given strong connectivity, advantaged power pricing, scale and development headwinds in Illinois.

It is a location that can support essentially every major workload, just 60 miles from downtown Chicago and within 1 to 2 milliseconds of latency to Chicago's major interconnection hubs, our Northern Indiana assets can serve AI training, inference and core cloud. These highly desirable attributes in Indiana are driving the 4 largest hyperscalers to significantly grow their footprint here over the next 2 years. Indiana is already the same size as the Chicago market today, and we expect Indiana to more than triple in size and become 50% larger than metro Chicago by year-end 2028. That would make it the sixth largest data center market in the country on its own and the fifth largest together with Chicago.

The rest of the portfolio is in Mississippi, an emerging hyperscale market with significant access to power given it is located in the energy-rich Gulf. With strong connectivity to Atlanta and Dallas, the market can also support a diverse set of use cases from AI training to inference and cloud. Amazon's own commitment reflects this dynamic with publicly announced plans to significantly invest in data center infrastructure across the state. Together, these markets offer the power, connectivity and operating environment that hyperscalers need, which is why both are critical hubs for Amazon and why they continue to invest in these locations.

Our conviction to deploy in these markets highlights BXDC's unique ability to see where markets are moving and capture compelling risk-adjusted yields, a distinct advantage of our access to the deep data center market insights we have within Blackstone. Putting this all together, we are pleased to deliver a deal that we believe checks every box that we outlined during our IPO. These are newly delivered mission-critical assets primarily located in a Tier 1 market. They are fully leased on long-term triple net contracts backed by an investment-grade rated hyperscaler tenant with alignment for long-term partnership.

The going-in cap rate and rent escalators are both at the high end of our target ranges, which we expect will drive strong returns for investors. And the attractive basis of these powered shell assets offers both downside protection and increases renewal likelihood, setting up for long-term value creation. We believe our ability to work directly in partnership with Amazon to provide a bespoke solution for their goals was instrumental in producing a compelling transaction for both of us, and we look forward to moving together toward closing by year-end. We also believe this transaction reflects an important shift in how hyperscalers are approaching capital allocation, bolstering the future opportunity set for BXDC.

With CapEx expected to eclipse $800 billion in 2026 alone and to climb further in 2027, hyperscalers need access to a broad set of capital sources. We have seen them meaningfully diversify how they fund growth, turning to both debt and equity markets. With those markets facing dislocation, we believe sale-leasebacks are another important source of capital for hyperscalers, particularly at a time when certainty of execution is paramount.

In that context, we believe BXDC is well positioned as a long-term capital solutions provider, and we see the hyperscaler sale-leaseback market as an additional avenue of growth for us, one that doubles our total addressable market to more than $2 trillion by 2030, and we view this transaction as a solid blueprint for future deals. Recent volatility in the capital markets has been a clear focus from investors. But importantly, we believe this will continue to create compelling opportunities for BXDC, especially as our investable universe expands meaningfully in the near term. The pipeline of potential investments we outlined during our IPO continues to grow with the delivery of new data centers into the market each quarter.

In just 2027 alone, we are tracking over $300 billion of newly constructed developer and hyperscaler-owned data centers expected to deliver in the United States, and we see more following in 2028 and beyond. Worth noting, more than half of expected 2027 deliveries are concentrated outside of historical Tier 1 markets and within emerging hubs like Richmond, San Antonio and Reno, illustrating how supply constraints are pushing demand across state lines and into more fertile operating environments. Many of these will be the Tier 1 markets of tomorrow, just as Phoenix, Atlanta and Columbus have developed into established Tier 1 locations over the past several years.

While our primary focus remains on Tier 1 markets, we expect we may also see more opportunities that align with our strategy in high-growth markets as they continue to attract hyperscaler customer demand and evolve into more mature data center markets over time. We believe BXDC's inaugural transaction provides a strong foundation to capitalize on the tremendous market opportunity in front of us. And it is a clear illustration of our ability to provide creative capital solutions and generate attractive risk-adjusted returns for our shareholders in a dynamic market. The scale and differentiated sourcing pipeline of Blackstone's global platform provides access to a wide scope of on- and off-market deals.

And as the only publicly traded company dedicated to acquiring stabilized data centers in the market today, BXDC is in a unique position to capture this highly compelling investment opportunity.
