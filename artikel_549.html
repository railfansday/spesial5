The best early Black Friday trading card deals: Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, and Yu-Gi-Oh! on sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

With Black Friday officially kicking off on Nov. 27, there are already plenty of early trading card bargains worth checking out on Amazon. From the Pokémon TCG's highly sought-after 30th Celebration Booster Bundle getting closer to market price, to record lows on Magic: The Gathering's Star Trek expansion, there are some fantastic chances for fans to beef up their collections for a lower price before the main event.

What's more, lovers of Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece, and Disney Lorcana can get in on the action, too, with discounts on everything from booster boxes to beginner-friendly sets. As of Nov. 18, these are the best early Black Friday trading card deals we've found so far. Be sure to bookmark this guide, as we'll be keeping an eye out for even better offers as the sale gets closer.

After boasting an estimated market price of over $120+ on TCGplayer back in September, you can now grab the Pokemon TCG’s six-pack 30th Celebration Booster Bundle for just under $80 at Amazon.

Even beating our $93.95 Prime Day deal coverage by over $10, that’s around just $13.24 per pack. Each bundle includes six 30th Celebration packs, with six holographic cards in each and a guaranteed Pikachu rare from 30 different variations. Other sought-after cards include Mewtwo ex, Mew ex, Umbreon ex, Salamence ex, and Greninja ex.

Magic's Star Trek crossover has arrived in time for Black Friday, and its 30-pack Play Booster Box is selling for $109 on Amazon, down from the typical $124.99 — about $3.63 per pack. That’s not only over $20 cheaper than the previous $129.95 deal we covered over a week ago, but is also one of the lowest Amazon prices we've ever seen (confirmed with Mashable's own pricing research).

Each booster contains 14 cards, including a traditional foil and one to four rare-or-higher cards, with alternate-art variants possible. The featured seller is third-party BetterShopping4You, so check the delivery estimate before ordering.

Konami's Magnificent Monsters has hit a 90-day low on Amazon, now $32.88 instead of $34.99. That’s actually the lowest price this newly released box has ever been. If you’re a fan of the original anime series, this is a must-buy for grabbing revamped versions of classic cards, like Dark Magician and Elemental Hero Flamewingman.

You get three five-card packs of Ultra Rares and three additional Secret Rares, each with a chance to upgrade to Starlight Rare. There are 18 reimagined anime cards to collect altogether.

If you've been curious about getting into the One Piece Card Game, this early Black Friday bargain is the best way to jump in, with Bandai's Learn Together Deck Set now down to just $57.99, down from $81.69 — a brilliant 29% discount.

Built around Luffy, Zoro, and Nami, it includes three ready-to-play decks of 51 cards and 10 DON!! cards apiece. You'll also receive guides, storage cases, three five-card extra packs, and a two-player playsheet.

It's an excellent time to get into the KPop Demon Hunters Collectible Trading Cards series, with Kayou's Energy Edition reduced from $45.99 to $41.25 on Amazon in a limited-time 10% off deal. This works out to roughly $3.45 per pack, with eight cards in each.

Unlike the Classic Edition, Energy Edition features 11 rarity levels, with 130 different card types to collect. These include the more sought-after SE cards, inspired by the song Golden, featuring laser-etched finishes and individual numbering from 1 to 366. There are also exclusive BP cards numbered up to 999, featuring HUNTR/X in their black stage outfits.

For the early Black Friday season, Disney Lorcana's Illumineer's Quest: The Great Hunny Rescue has slipped to a mega-low price of $52.75. Instead of opening packs, you'll be battling a self-playing Vine deck to rescue Piglet. The box has two prebuilt 60-card decks, a 50-card scenario deck, encounter cards, tokens, and a board. Play solo or with up to three friends, provided two bring their own decks. It's sold by Kings Games, shipped by Amazon, and marked as a final sale.
