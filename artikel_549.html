The Smosh cast breaks down their biggest Werewolf yet

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Earlier this November, YouTube channel Smosh Games took its Werewolf gameplay to the next level.

Smosh cast members usually play Werewolf or similar social deduction games in a deliberately casual setting, squished together on a couch surrounded by shelves on shelves of video games. However, in two supersized Werewolf episodes, Smosh Games throws its cast members into a mansion for a Traitors-style showdown. Heated roundtables, elaborate production design, and even werewolf masks make their way into the fray, resulting in the channel's most ambitious project since 2025's Smosh Summer Games.

Mashable's Belen Edwards spoke with Smosh Games channel director Spencer Agnew and cast members Amanda Lehan-Canto and Tommy Bowe to learn more about the work that went into planning and shooting Werewolf.

"It just immediately raised the stakes for me," Lehan-Canto said of the massive scale of the production. "I was like, 'It's Werewolf. Someone is gonna die tonight.'"

Agnew, Bowe, and Lehan-Canto detailed their biggest Traitors inspirations, how to play the perfect Werewolf game, and what they hope they can do next with this massive format. Plus, the trio broke down one of Smosh Games' most ridiculous rounds of Werewolf: a prank round where everyone was made a villager.

"I'm still really proud of that prank, because it was such a goofy innocent [round]," Agnew said. "I did feel bad though, because I had to keep lying and keep lying."
