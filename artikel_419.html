The Hunger Games cast roasts Jimmy Fallon in an elevator

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Ahead of Sunrise on the Reaping hitting cinemas, The Tonight Show opened with a musical throwback on Monday, with Jimmy Fallon, Questlove, and Tariq being joined in the elevator by three of the OG Hunger Games cast: Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson, and Woody Harrelson.

The Tonight Show trio quickly break into a Hunger Games-themed song in the clip, which Hutcherson, Lawrence and Harrelson eventually join in on — but when Fallon leaves, they manage to get in a quick roast.
