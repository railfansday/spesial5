Proof of Reserves (abbreviated as PoR) is a transparency mechanism born in the crypto industry to address trust crises. Its logic is very straightforward: platforms publicly disclose on-chain wallet addresses to prove that the assets they actually hold are at least equal to the total amount of user deposits. As long as the reserve ratio is ≥ 100%, the platform will have sufficient funds to meet withdrawal demands even if all users request withdrawals at the same time.
