The Shortest Games in a Game Backlog Are the Ones People Finish Least

I expected "play the short ones first" to work. Data from 129 real game backlogs says short games get finished at a third of the rate of mid-length ones.

Every guide to clearing a game backlog says the same thing: start with the short games, get quick wins, build momentum. I built a game backlog tracker, so I could check whether people actually do that.

I joined each game with its HowLongToBeat main-story time and looked at what share ends up in "Done". The data is 129 accounts and 25,276 games, taken on 2026-11-10. Only games with a known length are counted.

The shortest games are the largest group by far (more than half of games with a known length), and they are the ones least likely to be finished. Mid-length games, 5 to 30 hours, are finished about three times as often.

I can't separate these from the data. What the data does show is that "short" is not enough of a reason to pick a game.

Games with a clear end (platformers, shooters, horror) get finished more often than open-ended ones (strategy, simulation). That fits: if a game never really ends, "Done" is a decision, not an event.

Caveats: Small, self-selected sample. Many people imported whole Steam libraries, so "not done" includes games they never meant to play. Genres overlap (one game counts in several rows). The finish rate is a measure of what people recorded, not what they played.

If you track your game backlog somewhere, I'd be curious whether your short games get finished.
