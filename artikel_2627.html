Joe Burrow, Bengals beat down Steelers, 38-18

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

This article originally appeared on SteelersNOW.com.

Joe Burrow threw for over 400 yards for the Cincinnati Bengals against the Pittsburgh Steelers on Sunday, in one of the most dominant performances by a quarterback ever when facing the Steelers defense.

Burrow completed 35 of 44 passes for 408 yards, the most allowed by the Steelers defense in four seasons, as the Bengals beat-down the Steelers, 38-18 taking a strong lead in the AFC North.
