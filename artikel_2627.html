Sixers upgrade Brown, Wade and Bona to probable vs. Blazers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Sixers upgrade Brown, Wade and Bona to probable vs. Blazers originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The 14-3 Sixers are on a five-game winning streak and first in the Eastern Conference. They also appear to be trending up in terms of injury news.

The team upgraded Jaylen Brown (left hamstring strain), Dean Wade (left hamstring injury management) and Adem Bona (cervical spine sprain) to probable on their initial injury report for Monday night's game vs. the Trail Blazers. The one Sixer listed as out was Ariel Hukporti, who's sidelined indefinitely after he suffered a right Achilles tendon tear during the preseason.

Brown and Wade have each missed the Sixers' last two games, victories over the Magic and Bucks. Bona has been out since the team's Nov. 4 win against the Pistons.

Dominick Barlow returned from a back injury in the Sixers' win Saturday over Milwaukee and he played 11 sharp minutes, posting 12 points on 5-for-6 shooting in his season debut. Adding Wade and Bona should further bolster the Sixers' frontcourt depth.

Brown will presumably retake his spot in the team's starting lineup. He had a high-scoring back-to-back prior to his injury, tallying 31 points against both the Raptors and Nets.

The 10-6 Blazers will be down Ja Morant (left hamstring strain), Shaedon Sharpe (right meniscus surgery), Vít Krejčí (left ankle fracture) and Chris Youngblood (right heel contusion/bone bruise) for Monday's matchup at Xfinity Mobile Arena.
