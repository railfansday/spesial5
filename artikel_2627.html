AFC North roundup and standings entering Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

All three AFC North teams that played this week lost by a field goal or less, while Cleveland had its bye.

Pittsburgh fell 19-16 to Philadelphia, and Baltimore dropped its sixth straight in a 35-33 loss to Carolina.

Division-leading Cincinnati saw its five-game winning streak come to an end Monday night when Washington kicked a field goal as time expired to beat the Bengals 22-20.

Let's take a look at the week that was in the North.

Washington took over at its own 28-yard line with 39 seconds left and no timeouts. Jayden Daniels needed only two completions to rookie Antonio Williams to get the Commanders to the Cincinnati 8. Daniels spiked the ball with five seconds remaining, and Drew Stevens hit the game-winning 26-yard field goal as time expired.

The Bengals had taken a 20-19 lead on Evan McPherson's 56-yard field goal. Joe Burrow's 32-yard completion to Mike Gesicki put Cincinnati in range after Stevens had kicked a 41-yarder to put Washington ahead with 2:26 left. The Commanders sacked Burrow to push the Bengals back, leaving McPherson to make the long kick.

Cincinnati had led 10-0 after Burrow found Tee Higgins for a 31-yard touchdown, but Jacory Croskey-Merritt scored from a yard out with 12 seconds left in the first half. A brief stadium lighting outage interrupted Washington's opening drive of the third quarter, and Stevens tied the game at 10 when play resumed. Burrow answered with a 4-yard touchdown pass to Colbie Young, but the Bengals managed only McPherson's field goal the rest of the way.

Burrow finished with 300 passing yards and two touchdowns, but Washington limited Higgins and Ja'Marr Chase to seven catches for 74 yards combined. The Bengals had scored at least 31 points in each of their five straight wins before the Commanders held them to 20.

Cincinnati's defense was on fire in the first half, with Boye Mafe going off for a career-high three sacks and Dexter Lawrence II adding a tackle for loss.

But the second half told a different story as the Commanders scored on every single possession to drop the Bengals to 7-3.

Now, the Bengals will look to rebound in Week 12 when they host the 3-7 Saints.

The Pittsburgh Steelers lost their second straight game Sunday, falling to a Philadelphia Eagles team that had dropped seven in a row. Saquon Barkley broke loose for a 38-yard touchdown run as the Eagles built a 13-0 first-quarter lead. Jake Elliott kicked four field goals, including second-half tries from 53 and 55 yards, to put Philadelphia ahead 19-9.

Pittsburgh had a chance to close the gap when Carson Bruener recovered a fumble on the opening kickoff of the second half and returned it to the Philadelphia 24-yard line, but the Steelers settled for one of Chris Boswell's three field goals. Aaron Rodgers completed 17 of 20 passes in the first half for just 95 yards and finished with 227. Philadelphia sacked him five times and held Pittsburgh to 73 rushing yards.

Rodgers found Max Hurleman for a 20-yard touchdown with 1:21 left to cut the deficit to three. But Joe Milton connected with DeVonta Smith for seven yards on third-and-6 with 1:09 remaining, giving the Eagles the first down they needed to run out the clock. Pittsburgh fell to 6-4 and has now lost 12 straight road games against the Eagles, a drought that dates to its last win in Philadelphia in 1965.

Now, the Steelers are moving on from Rodgers and going to Mason Rudolph for the remainder of the season. They'll look to start a winning streak when they host the 8-2 Broncos in the NFL's Black Friday game.

Ryan Fitzgerald kicked a 37-yard field goal with 1:44 remaining, and the Carolina Panthers held on to hand the Ravens their sixth straight loss. Fitzgerald's kick produced the seventh lead change of the game, but Carolina didn't seal the victory until Will Lee III deflected Jacoby Brissett's deep pass into the hands of Tyrel Dodson with 33 seconds left. Lee also forced two fumbles, including one that went out the back of the end zone for a touchback as Justice Hill neared the goal line.

Baltimore piled up 338 yards in the first half and got an 86-yard kickoff return for a touchdown from Dontez Walker. Brissett finished with 276 passing yards, including a 52-yard touchdown to Rashod Bateman on a trick play. Derrick Henry ran for 94 yards on 13 carries and put the Ravens ahead 33-32 on a 15-yard touchdown run with 6:32 left. He later left to be evaluated for a concussion and did not return.

Bryce Young threw for 254 yards and two touchdowns, while Chuba Hubbard carried 27 times for 120 yards and two scores. Hubbard joined Christian McCaffrey as the only Panthers to reach 1,000 yards from scrimmage and 10 touchdowns through the first 10 games of a season. Carolina improved to 5-5 and opened a two-game lead in the NFC South, while Baltimore fell to 4-7.

The Ravens will look to keep their very slim playoff hopes alive this week when they face the 4-6 Texans in Houston.
