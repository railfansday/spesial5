Jahmyr Gibbs is on pace to break the NFL record for yards from scrimmage in a season

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Detroit’s Jahmyr Gibbs hasn’t just been the most productive running back in the NFL this season. He may produce more yardage than any player in NFL history this season.

Through nine games this season, Gibbs has an NFL-high 1,000 rushing yards. Gibbs also has 384 receiving yards. That total of 1,384 yards from scrimmage leads the league, and with an average of 153.8 yards per game, Gibbs is on pace total 2,614 yards from scrimmage over a 17-game season.

That would break the NFL record, held by Chris Johnson of the Titans, who totaled 2,509 yards from scrimmage (2,006 rushing yards and 503 receiving yards) in 2009.

Gibbs has touched the ball an NFL-high 240 times this season, combining 199 carries and 41 catches. With that kind of workload, the biggest concern for the Lions will be whether he can stay healthy for 17 regular-season games and, the Lions hope, some playoff games after that.

But given how well Gibbs is playing, it would be hard for Dan Campbell and the Lions’ coaching staff to reduce his workload. He’s having a special season. He’s halfway to one of the greatest seasons any running back has ever had.
