Dan Campbell won't wallow in "what ifs" arising from injuries

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Lions have, in recent weeks, lost both linebacker Jack Campbell (neck) and defensive end Aidan Hutchinson (knee) to significant injuries. Coach Dan Campbell won’t wallow in worrying about what may have been if the injuries hadn’t occurred.

“I think there’s times where, if you’re not careful, you lay in bed at night and you start thinking about all the ‘what ifs,’” Campbell told reporters on Tuesday. “And at some point, you just become a miserable piece of shit. And I’m not gonna do it, man. We’re not doing it.

“I refuse to look at it that way. Does it sting? Yeah, it sure did in the moment. It’s time to move on. We have to.

“And what has happened with Jack Campbell and with Hutch now will not be in vain. We can’t allow it to. They got us to this point. And so what are we gonna do about it, man? Are we just gonna let it go to waste, or are we gonna do something and pick up the slack?”

It’s the right attitude. Injuries happen. Sometimes, they happen to great players. (The more great players a team has, the greater the chances that one of them will be injured.)

The only approach is to tap the next man up on the shoulder, expect him to do his job to the best of his ability, not allow it to become a “woe is us” situation, and keep going.

That becomes easier, of course, if a team has quality depth. Which is why some teams that are top heavy with highly-paid stars find themselves in a pinch when those stars aren’t available to play.
