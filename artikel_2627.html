DeVonta Smith blasts narrative that the Eagles should shut him down during lost season

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

For the first time in what feels like forever – it was actually Week 6 against the Carolina Panthers – the Philadelphia Eagles are expected to have DeVonta Smith back in their starting lineup when they take on the Pittsburgh Steelers in Week 11.

Now, to some, the idea of playing the Pro Bowl wide receiver in the middle of a lost season is borderline malpractice, as why risk reinjury in the middle of a lost campaign? But understandably, Smith doesn't feel that way. Why? Well, because if he's going to keep doing the work, he wants to play now that he's fully healthy.

"I was 100 percent… I know someone asked Nick about shutting me down during the season. I didn't bust my ass this whole summer — I didn't continue to get up at 4:30 in the morning every day — to come out here and not play," Smith said via The Athletic's Zach Berman.

"My focus has been getting to where I was supposed to be. I felt 100 percent that game. I feel 100 percent now. Something happens out of my control because I know I do everything I can do for my body to make sure I'm in tip-top shape."

To Smith's credit, when he's been healthy, the pride of Amite City, Louisiana, has looked like a certified WR1 in place of AJ Brown, amassing 26 catches on 41 targets for 310 yards and two touchdowns over just four games of action. While Smith will be catching balls from Joe Milton instead of Jalen Hurts in Week 11, another 1,000-yard season technically isn't outside the realm of possibility, as he'd need to just average 86.25 yards per game down the stretch.

For fans who haven't had much to root for in recent weeks, that could be a fun storyline to follow, assuming it doesn't ruin Howie Roseman's pursuit of the No. 1 overall pick.
