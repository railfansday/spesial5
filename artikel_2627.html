Browns roster seen as the best fit for one of the best 5 free agents left in the NFL

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

As every Cleveland Browns fan knows, the roster is never finished being adjusted under GM Andrew Berry. That doesn't mean every move works out or is even a big one, but Berry's time with the Browns has seen consistent movement with the 53-man roster as well as the practice squad, including multiple moves during the team's bye week.

Cleveland didn't make any moves at the NFL trade deadline, leaving concerns about the team's edge rush and tight end groups to continue to linger into the second part of the 2026 NFL season, while the Browns compete for an AFC playoff spot.

With the trade deadline having passed, NFL free agency looks a little different now. All players are waived, giving teams a chance to claim them no matter what their experience in the league is. Prior to the deadline, players with four or more years of service become street free agents with a chance to sign with whatever team they please.

The Athletic put together a list of the top five free agents left on the market, with two players who could help Cleveland's roster, including DE/OLB Leonard Floyd. Floyd was noted as a perfect fit for the Detroit Lions after they lost star pass rusher Aidan Hutchinson for the season, leaving TE Will Dissly to be connected to the Browns:

At 6-4, the Browns are perhaps the biggest surprise of this season. One of their top needs heading into the trade deadline was blocking at tight end. They did not make a deal. Harold Fannin Jr. is an emerging star as a receiver, but blocking is not necessarily a strength of his game. Dissly has been a proven run blocker over time with the Seattle Seahawks and Los Angeles Chargers. He battled injuries in 2025 with the Chargers, leading to his release with a failed physical in the spring. If healthy, Dissly would fill a need for the Browns, who rank 25th in EPA per designed rush this season, according to TruMedia.

Dissly was a fourth-round pick of the Seattle Seahawks in 2018, playing six seasons in the Pacific Northwest before joining the Los Angeles Chargers. A strong blocker, Dissly's best receiving year came in 2024 when he caught 50 passes for 481 yards.

Finding snaps for Dissly could be difficult for HC Todd Monken, with WRs KC Concepcion, Denzel Boston, and Jerry Jeudy alongside TE Harold Fannin Jr. and RB Quinshon Judkins getting a majority of the specialist snaps.
