Bills vs. Chiefs weather updates: Rain in forecast for Thanksgiving game

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Buffalo Bills. Kansas City Chiefs. Mother Nature.

Three teams are taking the field at Highmark Stadium to cap off the NFL's Thanksgiving tripleheader, with Mother Nature positioned to be the biggest factor in the contest. Rain is threatening to soak the region, part of a big storm set to wreak havoc on travel plans, outdoor activities and even this football game.

Well, according to the National Weather Service (NWS), rain amounts between a "quarter and half of an inch" are possible. And that comes on the heels of potential for snow in the earlier part of the day, a sign that this won't just be a storm that passes through.

All indications are that the Bills and Chiefs will be battling the elements all night long. Does that mean both teams will have to trade their aerial attacks for a more run-focused plan?

Here's the latest weather update for the Thanksgiving matchup between the Bills and Chiefs.
