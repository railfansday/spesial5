What is weather forecast for Alabama vs. Auburn in Iron Bowl?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

One of college football's biggest games during rivalry week takes place in Tuscaloosa as Alabama welcomes Auburn to Saban Field at Bryant-Denny Stadium for the latest edition of the Iron Bowl. Will the weather have an impact?

According to James Spann of the Alabama Weather Network, the weather Saturday night in Tuscaloosa will be cold under clear skies with no threat of rain. Winds are not likely to be strong, making it ideal late November weather for one of college football's biggest Week 13 games.

Temperatures at kickoff Saturday night in Tuscaloosa are also forecasted in the mid 50's at kickoff, per The Weather Channel, as well as to fall into the 40's during the second half.

Alabama enters the latest Iron Bowl at 9-2 (6-2 SEC) needing a win to secure a College Football Playoff berth. The Tigers come to Tuscaloosa at 7-4 (4-4), and will be looking to snap a seven-game skid inside Bryant-Denny Stadium that dates back over a decade in Alex Golesh's first taste of the rivalry.

Kickoff is set for 6:30 p.m. CT on Saturday night in Tuscaloosa. The game will be available to watch live on ABC.
