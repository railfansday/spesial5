Daniel Jones clears waivers, becomes free agent

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

As expected, former Colts quarterback Daniel Jones has cleared waivers. He’s now a free agent.

The same thing happened two years ago, after the Giants benched Jones for the same financial reasons (i.e., to avoid the vesting of injury guarantees) and then released him at his request.

Two years ago, Jones eventually joined the Vikings’ practice squad. And joining a practice squad seems like a viable option again, unless he prefers to remain truly free and clear until the planets perfectly align for him to join a contender and start playing.

He’ll continue to be paid by the Colts because his full 2026 salary is guaranteed. Given the rollercoaster ride he has experienced this year in Indianapolis, at this point he may just want to take a break.

Still, as we’ve seen time and again, there aren’t enough good quarterbacks to go around. Even if it’s debatable, based on his performance in 2026, whether he remains a “good quarterback.”
