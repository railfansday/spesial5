Player Ratings: Real Madrid 5–1 PSV

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Fede Valverde brought Real Madrid level, but it was Jude Bellingham who changed the game after half-time. His winner was one of the goals of the season, and he still had time to set one up. Kylian Mbappé finished with two goals and an assist.

Thibaut Courtois — 6. This might be the lowest rating we've ever given Courtois, and he barely did anything wrong. PSV beat him from the penalty spot and didn't force him into a save all game. His distribution was poor when PSV pressed.

Fede Valverde — 9. Scored the equalizer and was unstoppable when he carried the ball. His runs off it were dangerous, too. Valverde won five of his six ground duels, including a superb sliding tackle as the last man back.

Éder Militão — 4. Looked a step slow defending the high line. He never quite got up to speed before José Mourinho took him off at half-time.

Antonio Rüdiger — 5.5. Fared slightly better than Militão, though he struggled to defend the space behind him, too. Mourinho replaced both centre-backs at the break.

Marc Cucurella — 7.5. Nearly gifted PSV a goal with one of his first passes, then got better as the game wore on. Made a terrific clearance just before half-time, and his run down the left was vital to the build-up to Bellingham's winner.

Bernardo Silva — 6.5. Good on the ball throughout, but shaky defensively. On one occasion, he let a runner go and PSV had a free header in the box. They let him off the hook.

Aurélien Tchouaméni — 5.5. Like Bernardo, he was fine on the ball. A rash challenge gave PSV a penalty, though, and with Tchouaméni on a yellow card, Mourinho took him off at half-time.

Arda Güler — 8.5. Finished with two assists and a game-high five key passes. His distribution helped Madrid play through PSV's press, and he was just as sharp leading the press himself.

Yan Diomande — 8. Gave PSV trouble whenever he ran at them down the right. He made four key passes and still put in a shift defensively.

Vinícius Júnior — 8.5. Looked quicker and more decisive than usual. He hit the post twice, scored a well-taken goal on the break and helped create several other chances.

Kylian Mbappé — 9. Somehow, two goals feels like a modest return from nine shots worth 2.58 xG. That's the bar Mbappé has set for himself. His link-up play was excellent, too. He made four key passes and picked up an assist.

Ibrahima Konaté — 7. Had little to do after coming on at half-time, but defended well whenever PSV called him into action.

Dean Huijsen — 7. Had as quiet a half defensively as Konaté. What stood out was his distribution. He barely misplaced a pass.

Jude Bellingham — 9.5. About as close to a perfect half as a footballer can play. He scored one of the goals of the season to put Madrid ahead, added an assist and pressed like a maniac. Even at that pace, he completed 95% of his passes and played three key passes. He completely changed the game.

Eduardo Camavinga — 6.5. Moved the ball well as Madrid saw out the game. Picked up a yellow for a nasty tackle that could have earned him a red.

Brahim Díaz — 7.5. Only had a few minutes to make an impression. He used them to set up Mbappé with a dazzling assist.
