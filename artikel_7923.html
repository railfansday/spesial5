Garden-inspired tree ornaments are such a fun way to bring the outdoors indoors for Christmas, and put your favorite hobby at the center of festivities. From pruners to potting benches, watering cans to woodpeckers, seed packets to sunflowers, there's a garden tree decoration to suit. So many, in fact, that you can match your tree to your gardening style.

Love to grow veggies? Then decorate your Christmas tree with radishes, corn cobs and carrots. More of a cottage-gardener? A birdbath, bees and plenty of daisies should adorn your tree. And there's even a bauble you can use as a hummingbird feeder once the holidays are over!

The run-up to the big day is such a busy time in between winterizing your garden and searching for memorable presents for loved ones, so treat yourself to holiday ornaments that make you smile. Here's the Gardening Know How edit of the best Christmas tree decorations for gardeners.
