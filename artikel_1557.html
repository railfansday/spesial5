Reports: Milan reach agreement with Eintracht Frankfurt for Krösche – January start date penned

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

AC Milan have made their move for Marcus Krösche and it seems like it’ll be a matter of time until it’s official, according to reports in Germany.

Krösche is maybe the most anticipated signing of the season, with reports linking him with a move to Milan since before the summer. Even before then, the Eintracht Frankfurt Head of Football has been linked with a move to Italy, but it’s only really heated up this year.

Gerry Cardinale has hinted at a move being ready to be completed soon, and he’s not the only one who’s seemed confident of a deal being agreed. Now, it appears that it has been.

This afternoon, Sky Sport Germany reporter Florian Plettenberg reported that a deal between the Rossoneri and Krösche was done, with a €5 million deal agreed to release him from his contract with Eintracht Frankfurt.

He’ll officially start on January 1, but it seems that he’s already been getting to work, according to reports.

German outlet BILD also reported something similar earlier today, via MilanNews, stating that an agreement was reached with Frankfurt for a €4m fee to be paid. Again, the start date of January 1 was suggested, but it was also written that the date could be brought forward.
