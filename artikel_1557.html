Liverpool: Virgil van Dijk opens the door to a departure

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Virgil van Dijk does not seem particularly worried about his future at Liverpool. While his contract with the Reds expires next June, the 35-year-old Dutch defender has still not made a decision on the rest of his career. Discussions with club management are expected in the coming weeks and months, but the captain prefers to remain focused on the current season. “I want to keep playing and doing what I do, so we’ll see what the season brings.”he explained.

Before adding: “If this continues, so much the better. Otherwise, I’ll go elsewhere. There’s nothing to report at the moment, but that doesn’t mean I’m panicking. Mentally, I’m fine; physically, I’ve had a little problem over the last two weeks, but I’m doing everything possible to be present on the pitch”. Arriving at the end of his previous contract in June 2025, the Dutchman finally extended it for two seasons in April of the same year. This time, no decision has yet been made regarding his future, while Liverpool may have to consider the succession of their captain.
