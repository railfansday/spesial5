Amanda Seyfried Finally Responded to All That Oscar Buzz for ‘You Can See Everything’

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Never been more invested in awards season.

Did you come away from You Can See Everything ready to invest all your time and resources into Amanda Seyfried’s Oscar campaign? Very much same.

Despite the movie being a documentary, fans are fully on board with Amanda getting a Best Supporting Actress nomination for playing Elizabeth Holmes. Yeah, she was technically being herself playing someone else—but she was still acting and her performance was brilliant.

So, how does Amanda feel about the buzz? She finally weighed in…via Instagram comments.

Nobody Wants This star Timothy Simons recently said what the rest of us are thinking on The Kyle Buchanan Show, musing “I would understand if somebody was like, ‘Well, this is a documentary. It’s not eligible.’ I don’t know how the rules of this work. But one thing that I can say is that both times that I saw it, when she finally walks into the recreation of Nathan visiting her in jail, she does that sort of tippy toe hug and there’s a shot over his shoulder onto her face. Both times that I saw it, my mind had completely melded the two people together in that when I was looking at her, I was looking at Elizabeth Holmes in a way that I can’t describe.” He added, “I don’t know how the rules break out but just what I saw, the performance was incredible.”

Cut to Amanda, who hopped onto Instagram after seeing the conversation and wrote “TIMOTHY” alongside a couple heart-eyes emojis.

As for whether Amanda can technically be nominated for Best Supporting Actress in a documentary, Variety reports that (1) she certainly can, and (2) A24 is actively launching a campaign for her. Love to see it.
