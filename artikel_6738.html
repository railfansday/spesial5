Fans Are Convinced Taylor Swift Teased ‘Debut TV’ in the Opening Moments of Her CMAs Performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Hello there, everyone who has been diligently clowning Debut TV for the past few months: we have a very compelling update. Last night, Taylor performed at the CMAs for the first time in 13 years and paid honor to her country roots with a medley of “I Knew It I Knew You” from Toy Story 5 and “Our Song” from her debut self-titled album.

And while some fans (me) were convinced Taylor would use the CMAs as an opportunity to announce Debut TV, we got the next best thing: a bunch of Easter eggs in the opening moments of her performance.

Taylor kicked things off backstage in a green room, where she was surrounded by recognizable dresses from her debut era.

I mean…this seems very significant! Especially since it was recently the 20th anniversary of Taylor Swift’s first album. Meanwhile, Tay went ahead and confirmed she recorded Debut TV in her letter to fans about buying back her masters.

“I’ve already completely re-recorded my entire debut album, and I really love how it sounds now,” she said. “Those 2 albums [Taylor Swift and Reputation] can still have their moments to re-emerge when the time is right, if that would be something you guys would be excited about. But if it happens, it won’t be from a place of sadness and longing for what I wish I could have. It will just be a celebration now.”
