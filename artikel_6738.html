Sam Hartman’s Exes Have Entered the Chat After He Was Announced as the Next ‘Bachelor’ Lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

One likened his personality to a vegetable…

Surprise! Sam Hartman has officially gone from being the Washington Commanders’ quarterback to becoming the lead for The Bachelor season 30. Some fans are all too familiar with the NFL athlete, but in case you don’t go here in the sports department…there’s apparently already some drama behind the scenes.

After ABC officially announced Sam as the next Bachelor on Friday, November 13, some of his ex-girlfriends reacted to the news. Let’s just say they didn’t seem too pleased with his quarterback-to-reality-star pipeline.

An interior designer from Naples named Becca Hunt shared an old photo of herself with Sam—who she claims to have dated in the past—on TikTok.

“The most toxic relationship of my life is now the bachelor LOL,” her post reads.

In a separate comment, she added, “look yall i am not going to say anything more abt this i just thought it was ironic because it’s a dating show.”

Naturally, her comments section went wild over the revelation.

“This tracks. A man can’t have a face card this lethal and be pure of heart,” one fan alleged, while another chimed in, “I said ‘wow he’s hot’ then they asked what he was looking for in a wife and he said ‘competitiveness’ and lost interest immediately.”

As it turns out, she isn’t the only ex of Sam’s who has spoken out amid his announcement.

Lauren Blake Boultier, who reportedly dated the football star for seven months (and who, earlier this year, was accused of photoshopping her face onto Black content creator Tatiana Elizabeth’s body) jokingly told TMZ that he has the “personality of asparagus.”

As for what that really means? According to Lauren, “He’s good for you, but he’s not exactly the most exciting thing on the menu.”

She emphasized that she’s “genuinely rooting for him, even if their own romance didn’t work out,” and even named some of his redeeming qualities: “family oriented, down to earth and absolutely husband material for the right woman.”

While the model and DAZN boxing host is more adventurous and spontaneous, she claimed that Sam loves his routine, which includes football, family, and fishing in his free time.

What’s more: She took to TikTok to post a photo of herself and Sam together with the caption, “When you get casted to go on the Bachelor but the Bachelor turns out to be your ex”…only to delete said post.

As far as exes go, Sam is also rumored to have dated former Wake Forest soccer star Tyla Ochoa (having confirmed he was dating a Wake Forest soccer player during a 2024 interview), but she has yet to weigh in on the casting news.

So…what is the truth? Is this man boring, toxic, both, or neither? Guess we’ll have to find out when The Bachelor season 30 hits ABC next year.
