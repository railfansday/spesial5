Kate Middleton Is Releasing <i><em>Heal</em></i>, a Documentary About Her Life After Cancer: Here’s What We Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Kate Middleton is sharing a deeply personal look at life after cancer. On Saturday, November 21, the Princess of Wales released the first trailer for Heal, a new documentary that will explore the emotional realities of a cancer diagnosis, recovery, and finding hope in the face of uncertainty.

“Cancer does change you,” Kate said. “It changes your body. It changes you physically. But tHere’s a much, much deeper part to what a cancer diagnosis shifts in you.”

Kate first publicly shared her cancer diagnosis in March 2024, revealing that doctors had discovered cancer following major abdominal surgery two months earlier. At the time, she said she was undergoing preventative chemotherapy and that she and Prince William had taken time to explain the news to their three children before making it public. In September 2024, she announced that she had completed chemotherapy, while emphasizing that her recovery was ongoing. “My path to healing and full recovery is long and I must continue to take each day as it comes,” she wrote. In January 2025, she confirmed that her cancer was in remission, but noted that adjusting to a “new normal” takes time. Later that July, during a hospital visit, she spoke candidly about the challenges of life after treatment, explaining that the period following chemotherapy can be “really, really difficult” as patients adjust to life outside the clinical care system.

The documentary follows the princess as she takes on the National Three Peaks Challenge, climbing the highest mountains in Scotland, England, and Wales within 24 hours. Along the way, she reflects on her own experience and connects with others navigating serious illness. The trailer also offers glimpses of Kate embracing her children and husband, Prince William, as well as an emotional exchange with her younger brother, James Middleton.

“Isn’t it when you leave the Royal Marsden, you ring the bell?” James asked Kate, referring to the tradition of ringing a bell to mark the end of cancer treatment.

“Yeah, but I didn’t ring the bell,” she responded.

“So maybe this is your ringing of the bell,” he affirmed.

Through Heal, Kate hopes to help others navigate diagnosis, recovery, and life beyond cancer. According to the documentary’s official announcement, the princess also hopes to help transform access to holistic cancer care that supports well-being, resilience, and quality of life during and after a diagnosis. All funds received through the documentary’s distribution will go to The Royal Marsden Cancer Charity, which supports people living with and beyond cancer.

“Healing is a journey,” she says in the trailer. “How to reconnect with the things you once knew and how to find a sense of hope and joy alongside uncertainty.”

Here’s everything to know about Heal, including its release date and where to watch.

Heal explores the physical and emotional impact of serious illness, focusing on the ways people find comfort, strength, and hope during and after a cancer diagnosis. The film also highlights the importance of connection and holistic care, with Kate sharing her own reflections alongside conversations with others navigating illness.

The documentary was produced by Will Warr’s Detail Films and directed by Benji Jackson. Its original score was composed by Erland Cooper.

All funds received through the documentary's distribution will go to The Royal Marsden Cancer Charity. Kate and Prince William became joint patrons of the hospital in January 2025, following her treatment there.

Heal will premiere in the United States on Monday, November 30, at 8 P.M. ET on NBC. It will also air on Bravo at 11:30 P.M. ET, with streaming available on Peacock beginning Tuesday, December 1.

In the United Kingdom, the documentary will be available on BBC iPlayer beginning at noon on Sunday, November 29, before airing on BBC One that evening. It will premiere in Australia on Channel 9 and 9Now on Tuesday, December 1, and in Canada on CTV and Noovo on Monday, December 7, with streaming on Crave beginning December 8.
