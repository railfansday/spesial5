Board diversity and digital readiness linked to corporate adaptability during pandemic

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Think "pandemic" and the word "pivot" is not far behind. What happened to organizational communications during COVID-19, though, was an unprecedented sea change. For businesses trying to stay connected to investors like pension and mutual funds, in-person roadshows, brokerage-hosted conferences and "analyst day" meetings were adapted into virtual versions filtered through the safety of personal digital devices as everyone hunkered down at home.
