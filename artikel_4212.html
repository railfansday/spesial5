The Best Abyss Gears In Crimson Desert

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

These are the strongest Abyss Gears to use in your weapons and armor.

Abyss Gears are one of the most essential parts of upgrading your gear in Crimson Desert, as these upgrade items imbue your weapons with certain special effects and abilities. The strongest Abyss Gears range from offering superb abilities that deal damage to allowing you to fire as many arrows as you'd like for your powerful bow and arrow build.

Deciding which Abyss Gears to use can be challenging, so we sifted through dozens of options to select the Abyss Gears that offer the most additional damage, alongside others that enable entirely new play styles when used in tandem with the right gear.

Updated on November 12, 2026, by Kaitlyn Peterson: This article has been updated and reformatted to help you discover the best Abyss Gears in the game, and why they were chosen!

The best Abyss Gears in Crimson Desert are primarily composed of easy-to-use, damage-dealing modifiers. While certain Abyss Gears put powerful modifiers to use, the strongest options offer damage in the form of additional skills or attacks that trigger in tandem with your swings. These are the best Abyss Gears to add to your collection in Crimson Desert.

You can extract any Abyss Gear from its original item and embed it into another piece of gear.

Find a Raptor and bring some meat along to offer your new friend.

Summons orbs that track enemies after a short delay, dealing damage.

Ator's Orb is a fantastic Abyss Gear that you can find embedded in the Darkbringer sword, which you can find either in the open world, or as a reward for defeating the Archon of Antumbra.

Ator's Orb tracking projectiles and additional damage are massively helpful against both individual boss enemies and large crowds.

These orbs track enemies regardless of where you slash your sword, so they'll track and impact on enemies even when you're not facing them.

Summons a murder of crows to viciously attack nearby enemies.

Ator's Orb is even better when combined with Crow's Pursuit, as this Abyss Gear triggers when you use a heavy attack.

Similar to Ator's Orb, the murder of crows from Crow's Pursuit will track your enemies, though they do a better job when you aim your slash directly at an enemy.

While the damage itself isn't massive, the extra range and consistent hits from Crow's Pursuit make it a must-have option for your favorite two-handed sword.

Summons a wave of frozen spikes in a frontal cone to deal damage.

Frost Spike is an exceptionally difficult Abyss Gear to get, since you'll need to defeat Beloth, the Darksworn and claim the Armor of Shadows en route to earning this powerful boon.

Frost Spike is best used against crowds of enemies, since the wave of frozen spikes offers enough of an AoE to hit multiple people.

Shatters the ground in a cone in front of you to deal damage.

Groundsurge is a fantastic alternative to Frost Spike, and offers a similar effect without requiring you to beat Beloth. This powerful Abyss Gear is excellent against groups, and its relatively low additional Spirit cost makes it easy to spam in tough situations.

While it doesn't offer extra elemental damage, Groundsurge is a great Abyss Gear that you should add to any two-handed weapon.

Has a chance not to consume Arrows. 60 percent for Infinite Arrows 3, 40 percent for Infinite Arrows 2. These effects stack to 100 percent.

Automatically applies when the Abyss Gear is embedded.

Infinite Arrows is easily one of the best Abyss Gears in the game, especially when combined with itself. Using Infinite Arrows 2 and 3 in tandem will guarantee that every arrow you fire is returned, functionally giving you an infinite supply of arrows.

Combining this Abyss Gear with Explosive Arrows is an exceptionally strong strategy that can trivialize every boss fight in the game.

Unleashes three swipes forward, dealing additional damage.

Shadow Claw is another excellent Abyss Gear for dealing damage, which you can acquire after tracking down and defeating Black Fang.

This Abyss Gear mimics Black Fang's attack, allowing you to unleash a claw-like swipe of damage at any enemies in your path.

Unleashes three blades of dark energy that push enemies back and deal damage.

Wound of Darkness is a similar Abyss Gear to Shadow Claw, but luckily, it can be combined without issue.

The best part of Abyss Gears that use the same mapping is that pressing the button combination triggers them all at once.

Combining Wound of Darkness and Shadow Claw will help you deal damage to several enemies in front of you, making it one of the best AoE Abyss Gears in Crimson Desert.
