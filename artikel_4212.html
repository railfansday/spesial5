Nintendo Reconfirms Release Windows For Upcoming Switch 2 Games

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Nintendo has released its latest financial data, and as per tradition, it's also reconfirmed the release windows for its upcoming software.

There are zero Switch 1 titles here – nada – so we'll be focusing purely on the Switch 2. There are some serious big hitters though, with Samus making her grand return at the start of 2027, and those magicians over at Monolith Soft working on their next big RPG for the new year.

So without further ado, let's see what's in store...

Of course, let's not forget that in addition to the above, the one and only The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is officially out for the Switch 2, so what are you waiting for..? Go get it!

Naturally, this won't be the whole roster for the upcoming 12 months or so, but goodness, what a line-up! A brand new 2D Metroid game is enough cause for celebration, but a Kirby follow-up to Forgotten Land? Hell yeah. Pokémon games that actually look reasonably pretty? Heeeeeell yeah.

So yes, lots to look forward to, and no doubt a few surprises along the way too.
