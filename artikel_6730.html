The 10 Hottest Designer Bags of the Season, According to ELLE Editors

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Anyone who follows the trend cycle knows that there’s a new launch seemingly every other week. Luxury houses are doing it all: releasing newly minted silhouettes for all to see and reimagining older styles, drawing inspiration from their own heritages. Ahead, we’ve researched the latest bag trends to narrow down the best designer bags worth adding to your rotation.

Below, you’ll find a tight edit of the only bags you need to know right now. From sultry, slouchy options from Balenciaga and Gucci to more structured, classic totes, you’ll find a range of styles from our favorite fashion houses. Bookmark these now and put them on those vision boards and wish lists.

Slouchy is in—and what better way to take on the trend than with the Balenciaga Rodeo? “For my first major splurge on a work bag, I wanted form and function—something stylish that would also be a true workhorse, able to endure the rough and tumble of a daily New York City commute without sacrificing attitude,” says Daniel Taroy, ELLE’s social media director. “[This] bag does it all, thanks to its wide shape, roomy double compartments, and an intentionally relaxed aesthetic that lends itself to regular use. And as a fringe benefit, its slouchy shape and lack of ostentatious branding achieve that rarest and most prized of It bag qualities: effortlessness.”
