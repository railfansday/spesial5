Let me explain laser hair removal first. In a laser hair removal session, your provider will use a handheld laser device that uses intense pulsed light (IPL) all across your skin where you want less hair to grow. The laser uses concentrated beams of light to target the melanin in your hair follicle and destroy it using heat. This slows down hair growth and can even prevent future growth for a limited time. IMO, laser hair removal is the key to super-smooth, clear-looking skin from head to toe.

Although the lasers are stronger and don’t require as much upkeep as an at-home laser hair removal device, they can be pretty expensive. But with this solid 60% off deal, it really cuts down on the cost. To get the deal, sign up with your name, phone number, and email address and make an appointment at your nearest location (reminder: there are more than 400 around the country, so there’s likely one wherever you are, not just in big cities!). At your appointment, you’ll have a consultation about the right package and treatment for you based on your skin type and goals.

What’s super cool is that Milan Laser offers something I’ve never seen before: an Unlimited Package, which gives you a lifetime guarantee and unlimited sessions per body area and free touch-ups for life. You won’t have to worry about not having enough to get the desired effect you want, because remember, laser hair removal isn’t truly permanent and will require touch-ups. You also need quite a few appointments depending on your hair type. Some with thick, coarse hair could need up to 16 appointments, while those with fine, thin hair might only require eight. It’s really personal, which is where an unlimited package can take away some of the stress.

I usually shy away from recommending deals with providers you’ve never tried before (like grabbing a cheap Groupon), but because you get a free consultation first, it takes some of the stress out of figuring out if this is the right place for you. And Milan Laser is more than just a random med spa. They also use only licensed medical professionals (and have a physician overseeing everything) to ensure safety.
