Infinity Nikki Players Are Planning To ‘Girl-cott’ The Game Over Maye Musk Ad

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Infold Games, developers of Love and Deepspace and Infinity Nikki, are 0 for 2 in pissing off their international fanbase for both games. Earlier this year, the studio announced, then cancelled, Love and Deepspace‘s sixth love interest, the werewolf Valko, inspiring some players to go on a sustained boycott that seems to have had a noticeable impact on the gacha game’s revenue in the months since. Now some Infinity Nikki players are hoping to achieve a similar effect, planning a “girl-cott” after Chinese ads for the dress-up gacha game featured Maye Musk, mom of noted racist and transphobe Elon Musk.

Beyond the practical discussions of how exactly to inform Infold Games of player dissatisfaction, girl-cott conversations on the Infinity Nikki subreddit have devolved into thorny back-and-forths on where to draw the line about the thing you enjoy. Some players can’t play the game in good conscience, knowing that Infold Games would choose to partner with the mother of guy who said he fed the USAID organization, responsible for providing food and medical assistance to countries in need, “into the woodchipper,” an act projected to result in widespread death, infection, and suffering for millions, including children. Others don’t see the issue because Maye Musk is not her son, however odious he may be, and the antipathy for him seems to be a uniquely American sentiment with little bearing in the wider, international (and Chinese) Infinity Nikki community.

As a player of both Infinity Nikki and Love and Deepspace, it’s interesting to see both communities arrive at the same fracture point in their relationships with their respective games over two very different events. It’s too early to tell how much of an impact the girl-cott will have, but its timing will no doubt hamper the momentum of the game’s upcoming 3.0 release later this month. If this girl-cott is anything like what happened with Love and Deepspace, Infold Games is once again looking at potential losses in the millions.
