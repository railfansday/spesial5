All weapon evolutions in Warhammer Survivors

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Warhammer Survivors has several weapon evolutions to unlock, and you can use them to power up your equipment. These require you to combine the weapons with specific items to unlock their passives.

The following table has a list of 21 different weapon evolutions.

While the Age of Sigmar evolutions share the Nuln Oil and D6 Dice with the 40K universe, the rest of them are unique.

Evolutions are directly linked to unlocking characters, since that will grant you access to more weapons. For the items, you’ll need to complete specific objectives to get access.

Evolutions are also required to unlock the unique bosses you’ll encounter in the game. There are six of them, and they spawn across the different stages in both universes. To do so, you’ll first need to unlock specific characters for their signature weapons.
