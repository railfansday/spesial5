10 Best Bedside Lamps & Table Lamps (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Before you spin into a redecorating frenzy (step away from the chandeliers…), consider the transformative power of the table lamp. The best bedside lamps are a necessary lighting addition to any bedroom—nighttime reader or not. They also have staying power away from the bedside, should they find their home in another part of the house as your taste shifts: add them to a shelf on a bookcase, show them off in an entryway, let one adorn your kitchen countertop.

Accent lighting has always been a thing, but lately, with the rise of anti–Big Light content on TikTok and Reels, the demand for cute, trendy ambient lighting options is going way up. And we get it—relying on built-in overhead light fixtures feels cold and corporate, while smaller lamps create a warmer ambience. That’s why we had the bright idea to test a curated selection of top picks from some of our favorite brands (in addition to other great options that score high design marks in our books). In each of our reviews you’ll find an AD editor’s testimony as to what makes each light stand out, how they use it in their space, and what’s something buyers should keep in mind. The hardest part now will be deciding on just one (or two).

While you can use a higher watt light bulb than your light fixture suggests, we wouldn’t recommend it. Wattage relates to how much power a particular device uses, and if more than the design allows is being used, you face the risk of ‘overlamping,’ meaning your fixture will start to run particularly hot. This is bad for a few reasons: Overheating can melt bulb sockets and wiring and, in the worst cases, lead to an electrical fire. It may also cost you more in the long run and be less energy-efficient—if your bulbs are drawing too much energy, it’ll likely mean having to replace them more frequently. Did the instruction manual for assembly look like it could have been ripped out of an astrophysics textbook? We wanted to let you know exactly what to expect from your purchases and called out anything surprising. We also double-checked if a brand provided you with a complimentary bulb or if it’s something you should add to your next Amazon order.
