Andreeva storms to WTA Finals title without dropping a set

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Mirra Andreeva defeated Elina Svitolina 7-5 6-3 to win the WTA Finals; They were playing each other for the second time in the tournament after Andreeva's dominant win over Svitolina in the group stage; Svitolina was far more competitive in the final but could not derail the teenager

Mirra Andreeva concluded a stunning week with a second victory over Elina Svitolina to claim the WTA Finals title.

The Russian teenager, who laughed off being called a diva by rival Jessica Pegula in a 'hot mic' moment ahead of the tournament, did not drop a set on her singles debut at the season-ending showpiece, finishing with a 7-5 6-3 success.

At 19, the French Open champion is the first woman to claim the title before her 20th birthday since compatriot Maria Sharapova 22 years ago.

Andreeva had conceded only 18 games on route to the final, the fewest since the reintroduction of the round-robin format in 2003, with four of those coming in a dominant group win over Ukrainian Svitolina.

Their rematch in the final was much more competitive, with the pair, who are two of the best athletes in the sport, engaging in lengthy, lung-busting exchanges in intense rallies.

But Svitolina just could not get her nose in front and Andreeva finally served out the opening set at the second time of asking.

Svitolina, who won the title back in 2018, was grimly determined to hang on in the second set but Andreeva was strong, quick and unrelenting. The teenager jumped in the air with delight when Svitolina blasted an overarm smash into the net to concede the second match point.

Andreeva became the 11th player to win the Finals without losing a set, joining among others Chris Evert, Martina Navratilova and Serena Williams.

Andreeva, who will climb to second in the world rankings on Monday, told Sky Sports: "Coming into this match, it was the second time in my life that I thought I'm just going to die on the court but she's not getting the win.

"I'm going to have blisters, I'm going to have cramps, I'm going to have everything that you possibly can have, but I'm not going to lose this match.

"This spirit, this fighting mindset, just helped me to get through, helped me to get more brave and more aggressive in the important moments. It's crazy, something unbelievable. I'm so, so happy."
