England weren't at their best against Croatia on Thursday, but they didn't need to be. A 2-1 win at Wembley, sealed by a penalty from Ballon d'Or winner Harry Kane, ensured they remain in control of their own destiny.

Thomas Tuchel's Three Lions couldn't have asked for a tougher test in their final group fixture. They face European champions and World Cup winners Spain in Madrid, less than two months after losing to them at Wembley.

Lamine Yamal, Alex Baena and Mikel Oyarzabal scored Spain's goals in a 3-2 victory that night, with Baena on the scoresheet again as Luis de la Fuente's side beat Czech Republic 2-0 on Thursday.

A draw would be enough for Spain to finish top, but the job is effectively done for both teams already. England, three points behind heading into the final fixture, have won all four of their games against Czech Republic and Croatia – not surprising, but satisfactory.

Their last win against Spain came in the Nations League in Seville eight years ago, when Raheem Sterling, Marcus Rashford and Kane all got on the scoresheet. There's been plenty of water under the bridge since then.
