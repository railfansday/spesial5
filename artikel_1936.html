GdS: The ‘hero of two worlds’ – why Pulisic remains a decisive force for club and country

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

If anyone from AC Milan was awake to watch America’s second game against Haiti, they’d have been delighted with what they saw.

It’s been a season of confirmation for Christian Pulisic so far. He’s recently signed his renewal, confirming his long-term future with the club. More importantly, though, he’s confirmed himself as the star of Milan after a difficult first half of the year.

With his current form, there’s seemingly no stopping his rise.

This morning, Gazzetta dello Sport has called him ‘the hero of two worlds’. He shone against Haiti and hopes to do the same when he returns for the Rossoneri, especially on their return to San Siro.

They haven’t yet had a chance to rectify that defeat against Ferencvaros, and this weekend’s game will give them an opportunity to do so, with Pulisic desperate to keep his fine form going.

This season alone, he’s notched up seven goals, with the latest being the bicycle kick against Haiti – which defines his form and confidence in one action.

He won’t be back in training until Friday, allowing him a day of rest. Nevertheless, he could well be given an opportunity to start by Ruben Amorim if he’s feeling ready to hit the ground running back at San Siro.

He’s back being central on all fronts again, exactly what Gerry Cardinale had hoped in the summer. Captain America is back and his new contract means he’s going nowhere.
