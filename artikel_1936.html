Leeds United back on track, Guiseley's FA Cup heroics and who next for Yorkshire's clubs - The YP FootballTalk Podcast

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

On this week’s FootballTalk Podcast, The YP’s Stuart Rayner and Leon Wobschall join host Mark Singleton to discuss the latest results, performances and talking points from the Yorkshire football scene.

They start in the Premier League, playing its last round of games before the latest international break, where Leeds United ended a five-game winless run with a stirring 1-0 win over Tottenham Hotspur, leaving them ninth in the table ahead of their trip to Chelsea on November 21.

Doncaster Rovers, Sheffield Wednesday and Huddersfield Town may have fallen by the wayside but there remains plenty of interest in the second round draw of the FA Cup, not least with the inclusion of Northern Premier League outfit Guiseley, who host League One Notts County after seeing off Sutton United with a dramatic injury-time winner from the penalty spot at Nethermoor.

The panel assess The Lions’ fantastic performance, their upcoming encounter against the Magpies while also assessing the chances of Barnsley, Bradford City, Rotherham United and York City of making the third round.

Bradford’s 4-0 win over York City in the EFL Trophy in midweek is also analysed before Stuart picks out his Yorkshire Player of the Week before Leon chooses his Team of the Week.
