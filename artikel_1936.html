Ace bins Motherwell mannequin role after injury setback left him with ‘meaningless’ feeling

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Paul McGinn feels ready to make a Motherwell impact after being left like a mannequin through injury.

The 36-year-old centre-back enjoyed a stunning season last term in the Jens Berthel Askou side that finished fourth in the Premiership. He suffered a knee injury away to Rangers towards the end of campaign 25/26 that has limited his involvement with Alfred Johansson’s team this term, making his comeback on Halloween during a 1-0 win versus St Mirren.

He was happy not to botch the clean sheet after appearing as a late sub, but in any case, McGinn is just pleased to be back involved. The club captain loathed his watching brief from the stands that left him feeling useless once the ball got rolling.

McGinn said: "I was content after it finished. I thought, 'imagine if you come on for two minutes and you make a mess of it! I just felt a relief to be back on there and it also helped that we we seen it out. I think the home games you normally as a player have to come, but as captain, you're expected everywhere.

“I was at them all and I don't enjoy it one bit. I feel as if you're just in the way and you're not contributing. I know that everyone knows I can't do anything about it but you just feel like you're a mannequin in the way. I just feel much better even on the bench to know that you can contribute if you're asked to.

"Even if you can be vocal and try and help with tips etc, as soon as the game starts, you're meaningless. So you're just sitting in the stand, people going, when are you back? When are you back? And you are trying not to lie to them, but also telling them nothing. It's just not a nice place to be but it's part of the game."

McGinn will hope to play a role against Rangers at Fir Park on Saturday night as the hosts aim for a huge Premiership win under the lights. The last time Motherwell’s captain faced Ibrox opposition was in the 3-2 win away from home in April, and after a mixed start to Johansson’s tenure domestically, McGinn says they are striving for confidence like they found under predecessor Askou.

He added: “You need a bit of luck in these games as well, of course you do. We won at Ibrox, we should have been out of sight, and then they should have probably been in the lead at one point, then we sneaked one at the end. I've always thought that we need that bit of luck, especially in these games. We had a spell last year where the confidence was so high. It takes you so far in football and it's something we'll be striving to do again.

“If you go on a run then everything becomes easier, how you press, and it's just the nature of being human. We're striving to be in that position again and that's three unbeaten for us. We're striving to be in that position again. I always like the night game. I don't know why, apart from after when you can't sleep until four in the morning! I think it'll be lively out there definitely."
