Arsenal: Jurgen Klopp reveals why he shares Mikel Arteta’s trust in ‘complete’ Kai Havertz

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Jurgen Klopp has hailed Kai Havertz as a “complete” player, as he explained why he shares Mikel Arteta’s trust in the Arsenal striker.

Havertz led the line for Germany in their 3-1 win over Serbia on Saturday, returning straight to his country’s starting lineup after injury.

He started Klopp’s first match in charge of the national team against the Netherlands in September but then missed the next three games during that break due to a hamstring issue.

Despite having limited opportunities to work with Havertz in the early stages of his Germany reign, Klopp has made it clear just how highly he rates the 27-year-old.

“I’ve played against Kai many times, and I can tell you that preparing for a game against him is no walk in the park,” Klopp said.

“You get a real sense of just how complete a player he is. You always have to be prepared for him to produce an outstanding performance because he has all the qualities to do so. He’s a top player.

“Mikel Arteta is someone who, as a person and as a coach, is absolutely determined to win. And unless several negative scenarios all come together, Kai Havertz starts games for Arsenal.

“The fact that he plays here is not simply because I don’t want to leave him out, but because I want to win games.”

Havertz started the season in superb form for Arsenal, scoring in wins over Coventry and Chelsea in the opening weeks of the campaign.

His performances have dipped in recent weeks since the hamstring issue, though Arteta has insisted that injury is not still impacting Havertz.

The criticism of Havertz in the last chunk of matches for Arsenal has been his finishing. Particularly in the Carabao Cup defeat to Fleetwood, Havertz was extremely wasteful.

“Let’s take the game against Serbia, for example,” Klopp said. “Kai put in an outstanding performance. He could have scored two goals and, under certain circumstances, perhaps even should have.

“What interests me is his potential and what we can achieve together with him going forward, rather than dwelling on what has happened so far.

“I’ve watched Kai Havertz – I wouldn’t claim to have seen every single international match, but whenever Kai Havertz has been on my radar, I’ve considered him an absolute top player. So I’m really looking forward to working with him.

“And if there’s anything more we can get out of him, I’m pretty confident that we’ll manage to do that together.”
