Gamaya Farms / Biome Environmental Solutions

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Text description provided by the architects. Gamaya Farms redefines architectural iconism not through bold geometry, but through absolute camouflage and tectonic erasure. Situated near Vikarabad, along a forested plateau and rolling hills, the project serves as a shared amenities zone and eco-resort for a farm-plot community. Rather than treating the program as a monolithic footprint, the master plan deconstructs the traditional resort layout into distinct, site-specific architectural typologies.
