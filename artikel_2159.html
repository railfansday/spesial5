Leeds United left-back exit looms: Bolton Wanderers & Cardiff City must be on red alert

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The January window is on the horizon, so clubs in the Championship will be looking at ways to strengthen their squads.

It’s a notoriously difficult month to get deals done, as clubs don’t want to lose key men for obvious reasons, and there can be a premium attached to any permanent sales.

Therefore, the loan market is a route that many clubs will explore, and one player who is set to be available for a temporary switch, according to Daily Mail reporter Tom Collomosse, is Leeds United’s Jayden Lienou.

The 18-year-old is very highly-rated at Elland Road, but with Gabriel Gudmundsson and Melvin Bard ahead of him in the pecking order, the teenager knows he isn’t near the first XI right now.

Yet, the fact he has been training with Daniel Farke’s squad shows that the German views him as a long-term solution, whilst Lienou was also called up to the Wales senior squad earlier this year.

So, he is a player with a lot of talent, and the sensible next step is a move to the Championship, which Leeds are ready to sanction.

With that in mind, we look at TWO clubs who should have Lienou on their radar going into the New Year…

It has been a positive return to the Championship for Bolton, and they will be pleased that they’re seven points above the relegation zone right now.

However, Steven Schumacher won’t allow complacency to creep in, and Bolton’s defensive performances are a concern, so it will be the area that they look to prioritise in the winter window.

Max Conway has been doing a decent job at left-back this season, but he does struggle at times defensively, and he may be better suited higher up the pitch.

So, a natural left-back would be welcome, and whilst Lienou is young, he could slot in straight away for the Trotters. As well as that, his strengths, which include his ability on the ball, look ideally suited to the way that Schumacher wants to play the game.

Conway would no doubt back himself to keep his place in the XI, but extra competition could help him raise his game, and it would also benefit Lienou to be in and around the first-team on a regular basis, meaning this is something that could help all parties.

Despite Lienou’s undoubted talent, it’s unrealistic to think he would walk into any Championship XI, but Cardiff would surely welcome competition at left-back.

Like Bolton, there are similarities between the two clubs in how they’ve handled their return to the second tier. Yet, Cardiff’s performances have been even more impressive, but their defensive issues are a real concern.

Whilst injuries haven’t helped Brian Barry-Murphy, they’re still lacking depth. Joel Bagan, who would be first-choice left-back, has spent a lot of the season in central defence, and, to be fair, he has coped well.
