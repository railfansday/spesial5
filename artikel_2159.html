Stoke City defender ‘can be pleased’ with international performances despite 4-0 thrashing, say local media

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Stoke City signed Luke Graham from Dundee in the summer, and the 22-year-old has quickly become a hit at the Be635 Stadium. He has started in 13 of SCFC’s 15 Championship matches, playing the full 90 minutes in each of the last 12.

Graham’s form has seen him emerge as a starter on the international stage for Scotland too. He earned plaudits for his display in the win over North Macedonia, but Monday marked a tougher night for the Tartan Army as they were thrashed 4-0 by Switzerland.

Despite this, Graham still earned praise from The Scotsman. In their player ratings after the heavy defeat, journalist Lewis Anderson wrote: “A second consecutive start for the Stoke City man and once again barely put a foot wrong, providing a steady presence at the heart of the defence. He deflected Amdouni’s strike into his own net, but can be pleased with his contribution during this international break.”

There’s no hiding that the Switzerland loss was a tough one for Scotland, Graham included. He may have given a decent account of himself despite the heavy defeat, but Sebastien Pocognoli’s side were thumped on a night that was supposed to be fondly remembered for Andy Robertson breaking the caps record.

The Stoke City man did do a decent job individually though, so the praise is justified. He managed a 93% pass completion rate with 53 of 57 completed, and he made the joint-most defensive contributions at nine. Graham also won 75% of his ground duels.

Graham did not need to be as active defensively against a weaker North Macedonia side, registering only five defensive contributions. However, he was still strong in duels with six won (two ground, four aerial), and he showed his ability in possession with his 56 passes the most by any player in the win (all stats via FotMob).

Graham has been in good form for Stoke City, and he has carried that momentum into his international duties. It means he should be confident of maintaining his high levels as SCFC return to Championship football this weekend.

The Scottish youngster will surely retain his place in Mark Robins‘ backline as they look to continue their impressive league form. The Potters sit 3rd in the table after a five-game unbeaten streak, including four wins.

A challenging game awaits next though, as table-toppers Birmingham City visit the Bet365 Stadium. It would be a real statement of intent by the Potters if they were to win, as they could move to the top of the Championship table.
