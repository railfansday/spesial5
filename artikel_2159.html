Wolves man sent off and slammed for ‘schoolboy’ performance in UEFA Nations League defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Wolves centre-back Ladislav Krejci serves as Czechia’s captain and was called up once again for their latest round of UEFA Nations League fixtures. The 27-year-old was sent off against Spain in his last international outing, and unfortunately, he met the same fate in another fixture against the world champions on Thursday.

Krejci was sent off for dragging down Roberto Fernandez as he broke through on goal, and his side went on to lose 2-0. Somewhat unsurprisingly, the Wanderers man faced strong criticism from the Czech media post-game.

As quoted by Sport.cz, former Czech international and pundit Marek Jankulowski said: “The red card was well-deserved; he misjudged it. The disallowed goal was also a schoolboy mistake, and offside saved us there. Krejčí weakened the team and the chances of equalising decreased.”

The same outlet issued a scathing review in their player ratings, highlighting inaccurate passes and failure to track runs, with Yeremy Pino’s disallowed goal as an example. His red card brought an end to a ‘defensively lacklustre performance’, Sport.cz wrote.

Krejci holds a key role with Czechia as their captain, and he played in the FIFA World Cup over the summer. When fit and available, he nearly always starts for his country and usually plays the full 90 minutes.

However, red cards have cut his last two games short – and against the same opposition. The Wolves man must hate to see Spain fixtures approaching, with both matches ending in defeats as well.

Krejci acknowledged a need to be “careful” about being sent off ahead of the match, but it seems he didn’t heed his own warning. A tough game for the defender was capped off by another red card, and he will be eager to move on and leave it in the past.

This means he will be suspended for Sunday’s game against Croatia. That is the final international fixture of 2026 for Czechia.

It has been another tough international break for Krejci, but he has found better fortunes with Wolves. Following an uncertain summer, he has reclaimed his place in the starting XI and has had a positive impact on form.

The defender has started the last five Championship games, none of which have ended in defeat. Krejci scored in the win over Cardiff City and provided an assist in the late comeback against Bristol City too.

Despite his Thursday night struggles, Krejci will hope to maintain his strong club form when league football returns next week. Wanderers host Charlton Athletic in the Championship on Saturday 21st November, and the Czechia defender will be backed to retain his spot in Cesar Peixoto‘s side.
