Barry Bannan reacts as Ali Al-Hamadi drops Sheffield Wednesday message

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Sheffield Wednesday striker Ali Al-Hamadi has taken to social media to reflect on his start to life at Hillsborough, and team-mate Barry Bannan has reacted to his post.

Al-Hamadi was one of 18 new signings to join Wednesday this summer as part of the club's rebuild under new owner David Storch, but it is fair to say his loan move from Ipswich Town has not gone as well as he would have hoped so far.

It has been an inconsistent start to life back in League One for Wednesday, with the South Yorkshire outfit currently sitting ninth in the table, two points from the play-off places, after winning just two of their last nine league games.

After parting company with Henrik Pedersen last month, the Owls appointed former Stockport County boss Dave Challinor as their new manager this week, and the 51-year-old is set to take charge for the first time in Sunday's FA Cup tie at Oldham Athletic.

However, while Challinor will be keen to run the rule over his new squad over the coming weeks, he may be without Al-Hamadi for the opening games of his tenure after he suffered a fresh injury blow.

Al-Hamadi is yet to open his account for Wednesday since his summer arrival, and injuries have limited him to just eight appearances so far, with his only start coming in the 2-0 defeat to Doncaster last month which proved to be Pedersen's final game.

After missing a few games earlier in the season with a foot problem, Al-Hamadi was not in the squad for Sunday's 1-1 draw with Blackpool due to a new injury, which manager Matt Hamshaw revealed could keep him out for "a number of weeks".

Unfortunately for Al-Hamadi, he has had his fair share of injury woes in recent years, particularly during his loan spell at Luton Town last season, where he scored just one goal and provided one assist in 14 appearances.

Al-Hamadi would have been to get a consistent run of games under his belt at Hillsborough and establish himself in the Wednesday team, but despite finding himself in the treatment room once again, it seems the Iraq international is determined to bounce back from his latest setback.

"Frustratingly, it has been stop-start the last few weeks for me personally," Al-Hamadi wrote on Instagram on Tuesday.

"Working as hard as ever to stay on that pitch, help the team and reach our goals for this season."

Al-Hamadi was inundated with support after his candid reflection on his time at Wednesday, with the post attracting over 16,600 likes at the time of writing, and among those was his Owls team-mate Barry Bannan.

Al-Hamadi has proven himself to be a prolific scorer in the EFL previously during his spell in League Two with AFC Wimbledon, but he has struggled to produce his best form since departing Plough Lane for Ipswich for a fee of just over £1 million almost three years ago.

Despite his injury problems at Luton last season, it is easy to see why Wednesday took a gamble on Al-Hamadi this summer, but as he continues to be plagued by fitness issues, his loan stint at Hillsborough is in danger of turning into another flop.
