Scotland star eyes dream Nou Camp return after childhood visit

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Aston Villa captain John McGinn does not want the Champions League clash with Barcelona to be another tourist trip to the Nou Camp.

The Scotland international went on a stadium tour as an eight-year-old when he was on holiday in nearby Salou and remembers it as the day Ricardo Quaresma signed for the LaLiga club in 2003.

But on Tuesday he will be leading Villa out at the famous stadium as the club’s remarkable journey under Unai Emery continues, which has seen them beat Bayern Munich and Paris St Germain in the Champions League and win the Europa League.

McGinn admits he is living out his childhood dream, but wants matches like this to be a regular occurence.

“I used to go on holiday to Salou, and I was here for the stadium tour the day that Ricardo Quaresma signed for Barcelona, so I think I was eight years old,” McGinn said.

“I’ve never been since, but tomorrow night I’ll be excited to represent Aston Villa on the big stage.

“I am 32 years old. The trajectory of this club is allowing me to dream the same way I dreamed since I was 9, 10 and 11, to play in front of the biggest crowds in the biggest arenas and obviously this is a very historic stadium.

“But what the club are trying to do now is make this normal, make this a yearly experience and make Aston Villa compete with the best in England and the best in Europe.

“So it’s a pleasure to be on this journey with the supporters. I’m sure they’ll enjoy tonight and tomorrow.

“But as professionals, we only enjoy the experience if we have three points and that’s what we’ll be trying to do.”

Villa have won their opening three matches of the league phase, but face their stiffest test yet.

Being back at the Nou Camp probably will not bring back as many happy memories for Emery as it will McGinn as the scars of the 2017 ‘Remontada’ still loom large.

Emery was in charge of PSG when they squandered a 4-0 first-leg lead in the last 16 after a harrowing 6-1 defeat in Barcelona, where the hosts scored two goals in injury time.

The Spaniard looked like a man who is yet to exoricse his demons when asked about it ahead of the match.

Asked what he learned from that experience, he said: “(It was) one match more. Everything that happened in that match I learned in my career.

“I started coaching in 2004, 22 years in a row. Played in Europe 16 years in a row, different competitions, Champions League, Europa League and Conference League, and I’ve learned in all of the competitions.

“And in that match I learned so, so much. One sport side and another not a sport side. The necessity of VAR for football in that moment. The necessity of VAR.”

Emery has lost 14 of 15 visits to the Nou Camp as a manager and says Barca are the favourites for the match.

“To play against Barcelona is to play against the elite,” he said. “Barcelona is among the favourite to win the Champions League, I could pick five teams vying for the title, and they are one of them.

“Barcelona have six points, we have nine, they lost to PSG. To play against PSG is demanding, it was a clash between two favourites.

“We need to adapt, but we need to play our own game.”
