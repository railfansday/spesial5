“(Marcel) Reed Just Can’t Be The Starting QB”: Texas A&M Fans Fume at Aggies Star After Disappointing Display in Loss to Gamecocks

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The South Carolina Gamecocks pulled off their fourth straight win in the SEC to ease pressure on head coach Shane Beamer, but the defeat put even more pressure on Texas A&M.

The Aggies have already lost five games this season under Mike Elko, and the latest loss brought even more unwanted attention to quarterback Marcel Reed.

Fans expressed their displeasure with Reed's performance, with one saying, "Reed just can't be the starting QB," after he missed key chances and finished with no touchdowns, no interceptions, and 251 passing yards.

As Marcel Reed nurses his proverbial wounds, LaNorris Sellers was nursing real ones after he was forced off the field in the first half, and Shane Beamer expressed concern about the injury at halftime in an interview.

"We're concerned," Shane Beamer told ESPN at halftime. "He's got an ankle, and he's getting an X-ray on it. I don't have anything else."

Thankfully for South Carolina, Sellers returned in the third quarter and seemingly shook off any ill effects of his injury, leading his team to victory.

South Carolina plays Arkansas next on Saturday.
