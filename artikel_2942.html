Game Day: Louisville vs. Pitt

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Announcers: Jay Alter (play-by-play), Tom Luginbill (analyst), and Lauren Sisler (sidelines)

Last Meeting: Louisville won 34-27 on Sept. 27, 2025 in Pittsburgh

Pat Narduzzi and his offense have been dealt a rough hand this year after starting the season 5-0. Their star quarterback Mason Heintschel tore his ACL in the middle of an upset win over Virginia Tech, and the Panthers have only won one game since that game. Pitt has had to battle through injuries this year while trying to figure out how to settle their offense after Heintschel's injury. It hasn't gone well for them, but this offense has plenty of talent to put points on the board.

Pitt is one of the few teams in the country that has started three different quarterbacks this season, and it appears that they will have a different starter this weekend than they did this past Friday. Holden Geriner missed the game last week with an injury, but he was expected to play in that game. He should be back to full strength this weekend to lead this offense. Geriner led Pitt to a big comeback win against VT when Heintschel went down with his injury, but he has been sporadic at best since then. His play has fallen off in each game since the VT game, and I don't know that he has shown anything so far to make one believe that he will improve.

If Pitt goes with a different quarterback, it will be Corey Dailey, who is a true freshman with a huge arm. Dailey was solid against FSU last weekend, but he also looked like a true freshman in his first start. He is a big kid with an arm that has to be seen to be believed, and he is decently mobile for such a tall player. While they didn't take a lot of shots down the field, Dailey's arm strength showed up on comeback routes and stick routes to the outside, where he was able to put the ball on a line to his receivers. His accuracy will improve as time goes on, as well as his ability to sense pressure in the pocket. But for the situation he was placed into, he played pretty well.

The Panthers have a likely freshman All-American in Damon Ferguson at running back. The true freshman has taken over the starting role due to injuries, but he has been a pretty consistent player for them all season. Ferguson has a nice blend of speed and solid power in a smaller frame, but his quickness is what stood out to me. He can make people miss in small spaces, and he tends to get positive yardage on most carries.

The depth at running back is solid with Boosie Turner returning to action. He was the starter at the beginning of the season, and he should be back to full strength against the Cards. Turner reminds me of Jawhar Jordan in the way that he runs. He is a complete back who can break off a big run if he gets in the open field. Former North Hardin star La'Vell Wright is the short-yardage option for this offense, and they have been consistnet with bringing him in for any situation where they need a power back.

Former Card, Blue Hicks is one of the best receivers in the ACC, and he will be extra motivated to show out against his former squad. Hicks is averaging 19.3yards per catch this year, even with the questionable quarterback play, as Hicks has become a complete receiver who can do just about everything in this scheme. He is the obvious candidate to beat the defense for an explosive play.

Pitt hasn't found a consistent second option out of the wide receiver group but Carson Kent is one of the better tight ends in the ACC. He has only scored one touchdown, but his 32 catches show how reliable he is for the offense. Censere Lee is another good option in the passing game, though he can be hot and cold depending on the game. He and Malik Knight give the offense a couple of options outside of Hicks to work with.

The offensive line for the Panthers is one of the best Louisivill will see this season. They are exceptional when it comes to pass blocking with their quarterbacks having plenty of time to throw the football. Sacks that have been given up have typically come on coverage sacks or quaterbacks holding onto the ball too long. They do a solid job in run blocking but they have given up some negative plays in the run game at times. This will be an interesting matchup to watch with Louisville's pass rush coming into form late in the season.

Pat Narduzzi knows defense, and he is fielding another great group this year for the Panthers. After losing Randy Bates to retirement, Narduzzi elevated long-time assistant Cory Saunders to the defensive coordinator spot. The Panthers haven't skipped a beat with the second-ranked run defense in the country, and defenders at all three levels making big plays each week.

Nick James sets the tone up front for Pitt from his defensive tackle spot. The veteran big man leads the team with 32 pressures to go along with his 8 tackles for loss. Pitt has always relied on at least one defensive tackle who can cause havoc up front and chew up blocks to let their linebackers make plays behind them. James is a mix of all of the very good players they've had over the years. He can be a menace for blocking schemes, but he can also go and make plays himself. He will be a handful for the Louisville offensive line this weekend.

The Panthers were without Sean Fitzsimmons this past weekend, but the defense didn't skip a beat. The mainstay in the middle of this defense is nearly impossible to move at the point of attack, and like James, he is steeped in this defensive scheme. Look for Jeremiah Warren to pick up the slack if Fitzsimmons can't go.

The edges are manned by Isaiah Neal and Jimmy Scott, who are also returners after being key cogs in this defense last year. Both have the size and motor that are valued by Pitt, and they play their role well to stretch out outside runs so that the full defense can attack the ball. They are also good at pushing the pocket as pass rushers. While these guys are the starters, Jaeden Moore is the guy to watch on third downs. He leads the team in sacks and tackles for loss.

Linebacker is the position that has fallen off on this defense, which is to be expected when Kyle Louis declares for the draft, and Rasheem Biles grabs a massive bag from Texas in the portal. Braylan Lovelace is a returning starter, but he has not been able to account for the lost production. He and Jeremiah Marcelin have not been making the havoc plays that we saw from Louis and Biles. That's not to say that they are playing poorly, but the defense needs those big plays, and they typically come from the linebacker position.

Pitt is solid in the secondary as usual, but they have an emerging star in Shawn Lee. The true sophomore broke out as a freshman, and he has only improved his game this season. Lee has not given up a touchdown this season while being an absolute lockdown coverage corner in zone and in man assignments. He and Raion Strader make up the best cornerback duo in the ACC, in my opinion. Strader has allowed a couple of touchdowns, but he is holding opposing quarterbacks to just a 59.2 passer rating, per PFF. These guys are the real deal.

Cruce Brookins is the top player on a very good defense for Pitt. He leads the team in tackles from his safety spot, and he does just about everything else you want from your top safety. He is very good in run support, but I've been impressed with how well he has played at the point of attack in pass defense. He has 6 pass breakups, with some of those plays looking like a cornerback in coverage. The Panthers have struggled to a degree against the pass this year, but it's not due to a lack of talent in the back end.
