Watch Victor Wembanyama's dunk... or 'shrunk' — a jump shot down into the rim

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Victor Wembanyama has 72 dunks this season. To put that in perspective, that not only leads the league (Giannis Antetokounmpo is second with 49), but Wemby has more dunks than 20 NBA TEAMS.

But on Thursday night, Wembanyama did something with a "dunk" we've never seen before.

What is that? A jump shot from zero feet? On the broadcast, Sean Elliott ultimately called it a "shrunk" because it's a shot and a dunk. Officially, it registered as a dunk, but how do you define a dunk? If it's some variation of "the ball is thrown in a downward motion into the basket," then I guess this is a dunk (much like Blake Griffin and others threw the ball down into the rim, although he did it with much more force).

Wembanyama explained after the game he's doing that because he's got a blister on his finger and all the dunks hurt.
