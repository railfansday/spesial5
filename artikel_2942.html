Washington Commanders vs Arizona Cardinals Week 12: Five Questions with Revenge of the Birds

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It's week 12 of the NFL season and the 4-5-1 Washington Commanders will be facing a 3-8 Arizona Cardinals team on the road at State Farm Stadium in Glendale, AZ this coming Sunday at 4:25 p.m. ET. The Cardinals are a team in transition; they only had a placeholder QB to start the season in Jacoby Brissett. They traded him away to the Ravens in week 9, backup QB Gardner Minshew sustained a season-ending injury after his first start, and now the Cardinals are deciding between rookie 3rd-round pick Carson Beck (who is injured) and recently-signed 40-year old journeyman Josh Johnson to lead the offense. The Cardinals are in their 4th year of GM Monti Ossenfort (formerly of the Titans) and 1st year of HC Mike LaFleur (formerly of the Rams, and younger brother of Packers' HC Matt LaFleur).

On offense, the Cardinals are coached by HC Mike LaFleur and OC Nathaniel Hackett, with LaFleur calling the plays. As a McVay disciple, LaFleur runs a McVay/Shanahan style offense that is built on formation shifts, pre-snap motion, and play-action passing to confuse opposing defenses. As part of this tree, the offense is built around an outside-zone run offense with pin-and-pull concepts designed to move defenders with angles and set up play-action and bootlegs. Arizona's primary weapon in the passing game has been TE Trey McBride, who has 689 receiving yards on 87 receptions so far this year. Given how many receptions Washington seems to give up to TEs, this should be a matchup to watch. Overall, Arizona's offense ranks 23rd in passing yards/game and 27th in rushing yards/game. Note that 3rd-overall pick in the 2026 draft Jeremiyah Love is still on injured reserve, which no doubt impacts the running game.

On defense, the Cardinals had been led by DC Nick Rallis, a holdover from Jonathan Gannon's coaching staff. However, Rallis was fired after giving up 34 points to the Chiefs. Teryl Austin, former DC of the Steelers, will be taking over as defensive playcaller and interim DC. It's anyone's guess what Austin's defense will look like, but he is taking over a defense ranked 19th against the pass and 27th against the run. Despite DE Josh Sweat ranking 13th in the NFL in pass rush win rate, the Cardinals overall have been ranked 13th in pass rush win rate and ranked 22nd in run stop win rate.

I asked Seth Cox of Revenge of the Birds five questions about the state of the Cardinals and what to look for in this game.

1) The Cardinals have just fired DC Nick Rallis. What did you think of Rallis, how much of the problems on defense were due to him vs personnel, and what do you think will change with Teryl Austin replacing him?

I think Rallis was terrible at his job and should have been fired with all of the Jonathan Gannon staff, but no one wanted this job.

He had great ideas, but they are unable to execute them because of their talent, and that makes you bad at your job.

If you are continually doing the square peg, round hole thing and continually terrible, that is on you to not make changes and instead put new pieces out there and fail the same way.

I think Rallis' schemes are excellent, but he needs the right personnel to get it to work.

2) What are the early returns on HC Mike LaFleur and what do you think of the Cardinals' coaching staff in general?

Terrible roster construction from the general manager.

So, let's just say it's unfair to judge him, but his offensive scheme is awful thus far and that is super disappointing. But is it because the roster is awful? Likely. Could his scheme also be awful? Absolutely.

3) What do you think of GM Monti Ossenfort and how he is building the team?

He legitimately may be the worst GM in NFL history if they lose out this season.

Seriously, think about all the bad GM's and we are approaching the point where if Ossenfort loses out, his win percentage as GM would be worse than Matt Millen's.

Things have not been going well in Arizona and Ossenfort is a massive reason why.

4) It's currently unclear if Carson Beck or Josh Johnson will start at QB this week. What have you seen out of each of them and what will the offense look like with them at the helm?

Bad? I mean Josh Johnson had been here 12 days so I guess you can make an exception, but Beck was mostly bad, then has been dealing with a recurring back injury. It makes the entire situation for the Cardinals and their fans even harder to understand and stomach because you can't even get to enjoy watching Beck play because his back is so bad.

So instead, we get 40-year old Josh Johnson and I guess we will see if newly brought back Kedon Slovis will be the young player we get to watch and hope turns into something.

5) How should Washington gameplan for this matchup on both sides of the ball?

Seriously, Teryl Austin will be calling the defense for the first time, but the talent is what it is. So, when you look at what the Cardinals defense has done well, it wasn't terrible in pass coverage, but mainly that's because it is so awful at stopping the run that other teams don't throw the ball much.

When you actually want to throw the ball, you'll see no pass rush, and that "good enough" coverage is really tough to maintain when you are covering for more than 3.5 seconds.

On offense, nothing. They don't run the ball well, they don't throw the ball well. It's genuinely miserable to watch.

Outside of a miraculous quarter and some time against Dallas, the Arizona Cardinals have five touchdowns scored since the Detroit Lions loss. Five touchdowns in six games and three quarters. Three touchdowns in one quarter against the Cowboys (I know you guys will enjoy that nugget). This offense stinks.

Anyways, Commanders win and cover unless Austin is somehow a wizard.

Good luck and good health in this one to you all.

There will be no companion article to this one, as Seth is enjoying Thanksgiving and probably scouting new GM candidates, so it was generous of him to answer our questions here.

Thanks again to Seth Cox for taking time out of his day to answer our questions about the Cardinals.
