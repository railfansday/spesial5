For many enthusiasts, our love affair with cars started with our dads. Whether we were helping him wash the family car, watching him change its oil and filters, or sneaking into the garage to play with his tools, our lifelong fascination with the automobile was developed at an early age, and it’s been part of our lives ever since.

Whether it’s his birthday, Father’s Day, or the holidays, finding the perfect gift for your car-loving dad can be tough. After all, what can you give the man who already has everything? Luckily for you, a lot of the tools, gadgets, and auto accessories your car-loving dad has in his garage are obsolete. Think about it: Is dad still plugging in his power tools? Consider upgrading him to a wireless system.

With plenty of car enthusiasts on our team at Car and Driver, we know exactly what makes dads smile. From innovative gadgets to timeless accessories, there’s something here for every car-loving dad or grandpa on your list.

Start with how he enjoys cars. A dad who likes working in the garage might appreciate a tool set or a creeper seat; a tech fan might prefer a dash cam or a radar detector; and a car enthusiast who enjoys the finer things may like personalized muscle-car glasses or McLaren headphones.

A car-themed T-shirt or air freshener is an easy, low-cost option. A travel tumbler is another practical pick. Prices can change, so check current listings before purchasing.

A dash cam can record the road, while a car-cleaning kit is a good fit for someone who takes pride in keeping his vehicle spotless. A compact flashlight or multi-tool can also come in handy in the garage or on the go.

Consider a mechanic’s tool set for repairs, a creeper that converts into a shop seat, or a bright compact flashlight for inspecting tight spaces. Choose based on what he already owns and the kind of work he does.

Driving gloves or driving shoes make thoughtful gifts for someone who enjoys time behind the wheel. For a sim-racing fan, a gaming wheel and pedal set paired with a game like Forza Horizon 6 or iRacing should do the trick.

Check compatibility first. The G920 sim-racing wheel listed above is for Xbox and PC, while Apple AirTags are designed for Apple devices. For wearable gifts, confirm his size and preferred style; for car accessories, make sure they suit his vehicle.
