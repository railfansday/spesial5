Exciting young talent JJ Gabriel makes debut as Man Utd beat Atletico Madrid

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

JJ Gabriel showed glimpses of his potential as he made his Manchester United debut in their 2-1 win over Atletico Madrid in Stockholm.

The 15-year-old may have been introduced with only eight minutes remaining, but, given how highly rated he is, it was a significant move by Michael Carrick to give him game time in the senior team.

There were hints of why Gabriel is so well regarded during those eight minutes, as he found space in the centre of the pitch and linked well with Leny Yoro. But he was also on the receiving end of some physical challenges – something he will have to grow accustomed to the more he plays at senior level.

Yoro, who himself made his senior debut as a 16-year-old for Lille, was impressed by Gabriel and said: “He’s a really good player. He is still really young and still has a lot to learn and a lot to improve on but he’s really humble and still working, so I’m happy for him to get his first minutes. I hope, for him, it’s going to continue like that.”

United fought back after conceding an early goal against Atletico to secure their second win of pre-season.

They fell behind in the sixth minute when Arnau Ortiz’s cross into the box evaded everyone, including United keeper Tom Heaton before hitting the back of the net.

United got the equaliser their performance deserved when the impressive Shea Lacey showed his skill in the box and was fouled. From the resulting penalty, Bryan Mbeumo sent the goalkeeper the wrong way from the spot.

In the 72nd minute, Mbeumo got his second of the afternoon after he bundled the ball home after the United press forced Atletico into a mistake.

The Spanish side hit the post twice in the closing stages but United held on to win.

“We had a clear plan before the game, everyone stuck to it and made the game enjoyable,” Mbeumo said. “We work really hard during training for these games, the main goal is to be ready for the season starting. I have done great things on the right but also as a striker.

“I think I am more experienced on the wing but I enjoy playing as a striker. For an offensive player, it is always good to score for the team.”

Iker Luque shoots from distance. It looks good but flies over the bar. And that should be the final effort of the match...

Mendoza and Hjulmand both hit the post in quick succession. Heaton was beaten on both occasions. United were a bit fortunate there.

Now that they are behind, Atletico are starting to have a bit more possession and play on the front foot.

A slow second half comes alive as United take the lead. A slack pass in defence by Atletico is pounced upon by Fletcher. He runs to the byline, cuts the ball back to the unmarked Mbeumo, who taps in.

Early goal for Atletico. They take a short corner, Arnau Ortiz whips in a dangerous ball that beats everyone and drops in at the far post. Heaton never moved.

United dominating possession in the opening stages with Atletico happy to sit off and let the Premier League side have it. Even in pre-season, Diego Simeone has his side set up well to defend.

Gianni Infantino is facing an ultimatum from Uefa: resign or face an emergency no-confidence vote.

It is understood all 55 of Uefa’s member nations would opt to oust Infantino in a vote of no-confidence if he refuses to step down. Uefa can trigger such a vote with just 43 member nations.

European football’s governing body announced a loss of confidence on Saturday morning and has demanded change after Infantino was forced to scrap his £15bn plan to sell a stake in the World Cup.

Uefa’s statement followed widespread opposition and a revolt within Fifa’s ranks that staff are calling “kill the monster”.

The Football Association backed Uefa’s stance, saying: “We fully support Uefa’s position. It is time for a full and robust review of Fifa’s leadership and governance to ensure that the global game is run transparently, for the benefit of all 211 member nations and with the long-term stewardship of football at its heart.”

Good afternoon and welcome to pre-season coverage of Manchester United against Atletico Madrid.

Michael Carrick’s squad continue their preparations for the new season with a trip to the Swedish capital, Stockholm, where they face Diego Simeone’s La Liga side.

Last week, United cruised to a 5-0 victory over Rosenborg but today will be their first real test. They are slowly starting to get players back but the likes of captain Bruno Fernandes and Youri Tielemans will not be involved.

“I think we’re in a good place. I think there’s four weeks to go to the start and we’re gradually getting some boys back from the World Cup as we progress,” Carrick said.

“But I think a little bit of patience and time and trying to keep ahead and as I said, keeping everyone fit but moving forward is a really good direction to go in. And as it stands right now, I think we’re in a pretty good place. So, the boys will get better as we go.”

One player who will be looking to impress is Mason Mount. He has had an injury-hit spell at Old Trafford but is fit and ready to go for the season. He is also growing into a leadership role at the club.

He told BBC Radio Manchester: “You have different types of leaders, you have ones that are very vocal. I always want to see myself as one that leads by example and never stops working on the pitch.

“I’ve been captain previously years ago. I love the role especially with a lot of younger players on the pitch being able to speak to them and make them feel comfortable in the game and off the pitch as well. It’s a role that I really like and loved having it [the captain’s armband] in the second half.”
