Middlesbrough FC legend Tony Mowbray steps back from Blackburn Rovers job due to health issues

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Middlesbrough legend Tony Mowbray has stepped back from his role as head coach of Blackburn Rovers due to health issues.

The 62-year-old, who represented Middlesbrough with disinction as a player and later as their manager, took the reins at Ewood Park in the summer.

He has steered Rovers into 13th place in the Championship, defying low pre-season expectations, but has now stepped aside.

Mowbray said: “Unfortunately, due to health issues, I have to step back from my role as head coach of Blackburn Rovers. I’m immensely proud of this group of players.

“Their dedication and professionalism have given us some memorable performances and wins, and I’ve loved working with them.

“Thank you to my coaching staff for all your hard work and for the support you’ve given me. It’s been a pleasure working alongside you. To the fans, thank you for the way you’ve supported me and the team this season. I’ve always tried to give you a team that you can be proud of. Your support has meant a great deal to me.

Mowbray previously stepped away from his role as Birmingham City boss in 2024 and later confirmed he had been diagnosed with bowel cancer.

Blackburn have confirmed Damien Johnson and Mike Dodds will oversee first-team management duties “for the time being”.

Blackburn’s owner B. Balaji Rao said: “Our thoughts are with Tony and his family as he steps back from his duties to focus on his health.

“Through our many meetings over the years, we’ve come to know Tony well and grown very fond of him. We’ve always admired his professionalism, his dedication and the passion he brings to his work at Blackburn Rovers.

“Tony, please know that you have our unconditional and unwavering support. We send you and your family our love and strength, and we will do all we can to support you through this time.”
