Bayern Munich: the president makes an announcement on the future of Michael Olise

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

After formalizing Harry Kane’s contract extension, Bayern Munich could quickly look into the future of another of its offensive executives. Present at a press conference on the occasion of the formalization of the English striker’s new lease, Jan-Christian Dreesen, chairman of the board of the Bavarian club, was asked about the possibility of seeing Michael Olise be the next player to be extended. Having become one of the essential elements of the Munich squad, the French international is necessarily an important file for German leaders, who wish to retain their best elements in the long term.

But for the moment, Bayern Munich does not seem particularly in a hurry. “This is something that Max and I are jointly responsible for. We have always said that we not only have a fantastic player in Harry, but also other great players in our squad, including Michael Olise. His contract runs until 2029, and we will sit down to discuss things at the appropriate time.”explained Jan-Christian Dreesen, referring to Max Eberl, sporting director of Bayern. A statement which confirms that the Olise file remains in the Bavarian club’s thoughts.
