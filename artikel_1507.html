Man Utd predicted XI vs. Liverpool for Pochettino’s first game in charge

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

We’re finally ready to get our first glimpse of a Mauricio Pochettino Manchester United team as the new head coach prepares for his debut in the dugout against Liverpool on Sunday.

It’s a big one to kick off the Pochettino era, with United travelling to Anfield to face their long-time rivals Liverpool. The prospect of a Pochettino-led Man Utd side has been a hypothetical for years and now it’s finally going to become a reality. But what will its first version look like?

There’s only so much you can guess from drones being flown over training. Pochettino was seen speaking closely with a group including Harry Amass and Joshua Zirkzee in the leaked footage and we’d be stunned if either got their first start of the season in the league in such a big game.

It’s more likely that Pochettino will be sticking with the tried and trusted for now. Well, maybe not so trusted anymore given United’s recent form under Michael Carrick, but it’s Pochettino’s job to change that outlook.

Like his predecessor, Pochettino usually favours a 4-2-3-1 formation. It’ll probably be the starting point for his first United lineup, although who fits into it remains to be seen.

There are some safer bets. Senne Lammens in goal, Bruno Fernandes in attacking midfield, Bryan Mbeumo – who’ll be making his 50th appearance for the club – somewhere in attack. These things rarely change, regardless of who’s in charge.

What Pochettino will have to weigh up more carefully is whether to throw his compatriot Lisandro Martinez back into defence as he returns from suspension. The suspicion is that he will.

Moreover, he might have Luke Shaw – his former Southampton prodigy – back from injury to select at left-back. If not, Patrick Dorgu took part in Pochettino’s first training session, which is promising.

The new boss will also hope to be able to call on Harry Maguire, who’s been back in training after a back issue.

Midfielder Carlos Baleba and striker Benjamin Sesko remain out for the foreseeable future with their injuries. Marcus Rashford is still a doubt as well, but isn’t too far off a comeback.
