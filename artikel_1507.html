PSG – Troyes: the official compositions

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Unstoppable, with its current series of 10 consecutive successes in all competitions, PSG is playing its last match before the international break against the barrage Troyes (kick-off at 9:05 p.m.), who hopes not to suffer the same treatment as Le Havre (1-5) and Villarreal (0-6), scattered by the reigning double European champion in recent days.

Still deprived of many elements of his 11 type (Marquinhos, Nuno Mendes, Dembélé, Kvaratskhelia), Luis Enrique is also without Hakimi, left to rest, and Akliouche, injured during the match in Spain on Tuesday. The rotation of the Iberian technician is therefore limited and it is a team with only 3 changes which will appear on the lawn of the Park. Torres will start at the forefront, supported by Doué and Godts, the latter taking over from Akliouche. In the middle, Mayulu goes down one place after having played at the forefront in the Champions League and Zaire-Emery replaces Hakimi in the right lane of defense. Note that it is Vitinha who will wear the armband this evening.

Stéphane Dumont also showed a certain stability, following on from his team’s success against Le Mans last Saturday (2-0). The Trojan coach’s 2 changes: Boura takes Gozzi’s place on the left side of the defense, while Diawara will play on the left wing, sending Nkada to the bench.

PSG (4-3-3): Safonov – Zaire-Emery, Zabarnyi, Pacho, Hernandez – Mayulu, Vitinha (cap.), Ruiz – Doué, Torres, Godts.

Troyes (4-3-3): Konaté – Maronnier, Monfray (cap.), Diaz, Boura – Mille, Karim, Diop – Ifnaoui, Ripart, Diawara.
