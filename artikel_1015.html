Portugal head coach Jorge Jesus has defended himself in the wake of remarks from Cristiano Ronaldo’s mother as the dispute over the forward’s international retirement continued in Wales.

Ronaldo called time on his Portugal career on 5 November, less than a month after the Portuguese Football Federation handed him a 30-day suspension and a fine of 255 euros (approximately £210) for walking out on the national team.

The 41-year-old had left the squad last month shortly before a Nations League fixture in Denmark following a row with Jesus after he was an unused substitute in Portugal’s win over Norway.

The former Manchester United and Real Madrid forward’s mother, Dolores Aveiro, said she was pleased by the result as Denmark crushed Portugal 4-1 in the Braga return on Saturday, because her son had been deceived by Jesus.

“Now I have to respond to Cristiano Ronaldo’s mother?” Jesus said at his pre-match press conference in Cardiff.

“She is a lady I greatly respect, she suffered a lot to raise her children.”

Asked if debate about Ronaldo’s retirement from international football would stop, Jesus said: “I don’t know when it’s going to stop. If you guys (reporters) ask the questions, I have to answer. If you ask about Ronaldo next year, I will answer.”

Portugal will be without the suspended pair of Joao Palhina and Ruben Dias in Cardiff, with Manchester United playmaker Bruno Fernandes set to take over the captaincy in the absence of the injured Bernardo Silva.

Barcelona defender Joao Cancelo returns after missing the Denmark game through suspension.

Jesus said: “I am going to change some players, but it’s not only physical. We have four players on yellow cards. After the game against Denmark everyone is asking me about different individuals, not about the team or winning here.

“If we win we will have 15 points, which is only matched by England and Spain. We played one bad game and it’s harder to move on when we have a result like that, but we are going to try and win the game.”

Heading into Tuesday’s game, Portugal are three points clear of Denmark as they aim to secure top spot in their Nations League group.
