England's road to the 2027 Nations League final revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Nations League 2026/27: England's route to the final explained, with Norway awaiting in the quarter-finals and Germany or Belgium possible semi-final opponents after the draw; world champions Spain will face France, while Netherlands take on holders Portugal in the last eight

England are three wins away from being crowned Nations League champions after the draw mapped out their route to the 2027 final.

Thomas Tuchel's side will face Norway in the quarter-finals, with a semi-final against either Belgium or Germany awaiting them should they progress.

The Three Lions secured top spot in their group with an impressive 3-2 victory over Spain in Madrid, earning themselves a seeded position for the knockout stages and the advantage of playing the second leg of their quarter-final at home.

England will travel to face Erling Haaland and Martin Odegaard's side in the first leg between March 25 and 27, before welcoming them to Wembley for the return fixture between March 28 and 30.

Norway secured their place in the last eight in dramatic fashion, coming from two goals behind to beat Denmark 3-2 and qualify at their opponents' expense.

Should England overcome that challenge, their route to the final would see them face the winner of Belgium vs Germany in the semi-finals.

Belgium finished runners-up in Group A1, while Germany topped Group A2 after inflicting a 5-0 defeat on Netherlands.

On the other side of the draw, world champions Spain will face France, while Netherlands take on holders Portugal. The winners of those two quarter-finals will meet in the other semi-final.

The quarter-finals will be played over two legs during the March international break.

England will play away in Norway before hosting the return leg at Wembley.

The Nations League Finals will take place between June 9 and 13, 2027.

The four quarter-final winners will progress to the final tournament, which consists of two semi-finals, a third-place play-off and the final.

England will need to win their quarter-final and semi-final to reach the showpiece, where they will have the opportunity to be crowned champions of Europe in this competition.

The four semi-finalists will be invited to submit bids to host the final tournament.
