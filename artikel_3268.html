‘Percy Jackson’s Courtney B. Vance On “Doing Right By” Late Lance Reddick After Taking Over Zeus Role

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

As the gods and demigods of Percy Jackson and the Olympians return for Season 3, Courtney B. Vance had a mighty toga to fill.

The Golden Globe nominee, who returns after taking over the role of Zeus from the late Lance Reddick, remembered the actor as “a great, great man” before Reddick’s death at age 60 in March 2023.

“His stamp on this role is very strong, so I’m taking my time and making sure that I’m doing right by him,” he told People. “And by doing right by him, I do right by myself.” Watch on Deadline

Vance added, “You have to do things right. Start the way you want to finish. I’m stepping into a very well-established family, so I want to make sure that I’m doing the right thing by them and myself. It was a little challenging at first, but they were very welcoming to me, so I’m grateful.”

Shortly after wrapping on Season 1 of Percy Jackson, Reddick died at age 60 on March 17, 2023. Although a coroner ruled his cause of death to be heart disease, Reddick’s family disputed that report as inconsistent with his habits and fitness.

Although the show was initially hesitant to re-cast the character, Vance filled the role in late 2024, debuting in January’s Season 2 finale episode “The Fleece Works Its Magic Too Well.”

With new episodes available to stream Fridays on Disney+, Season 3 of Percy Jackson and the Olympians marks the first time The Titan’s Curse, the third book from Rick Riordan’s bestselling series, has been adapted for the screen.

If Percy Jackson (Walker Scobell) learned anything after completing two epic quests with Annabeth Chase (Leah Sava Jeffries), it’s that he would be lost without her. So when Annabeth is captured by the forces of Kronos, Percy must embark on a perilous journey to rescue her. He must free a goddess in chains, confront the Titan’s curse, and avoid triggering the fall of Olympus. With Grover (Aryan Simhadri) by his side, Percy must join some unlikely questmates to confront a prophecy with dire consequences.
