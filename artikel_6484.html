Cranberry Cream Cheese Spread

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Of all the incredible dips we’ve created over the years, our cranberry whipped feta dip is an internet and personal favorite—so much so that I just had to come back with an equally luxurious variation: this cranberry-topped whipped cream cheese spread. With the holiday party season just around the corner, this spread is begging to be included in your party spread. Plus, it's as easy to make as it is delicious—a tangy, sweet cranberry sauce gets finished with brandy, then is spooned atop a lemony whipped cream cheese for a guaranteed winning combo.

Fresh Or Frozen Cranberries? I used both while testing, and they both work interchangeably. I found the fresh cranberries broke down a little more than frozen, so it depends on whether or not you’re looking for more or less texture in your sauce.

What temperature should the cream cheese be? Make sure the cream cheese is chilled and softened but not super cold. It should be somewhat pliable when whipping to avoid lumps; this will ensure the mixture is light and airy. A little heavy cream will loosen the mixture if it's too thick.

The lemon zest: I found that the cream cheese layer needed a little more oomph. If you get a bite of cream cheese on a cracker, it shouldn’t be one-dimensional. Adding a touch of lemon zest makes it more palatable.

Storage:Store the cream cheese spread, with or without cranberry sauce, in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
