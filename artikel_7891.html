The 2027 Colors of the Year Are Here — Here’s What Your Favorite Paint Brands Picked

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Paint brands have revealed their 2027 Colors of the Year, from soft greens to deep browns. See which shades could work in your home.

Top paint brands around the world have announced their featured paints for 2027, giving homeowners new options to consider for their next project. This year’s selections cover quite a range, including soft greens and blues, darker earth tones, and even a wood stain.

Each brand has its own approach to what homeowners might want in the coming year.

Sherwin-Williams and HGTV Home by Sherwin-Williams chose Celery (SW 6421), a light green with yellow undertones.

“With subtle yellow undertones, this soft, muted green strikes a perfect balance between an airy pastel and a grounded, herbal hue,” according to Sherwin-Williams.

The color pairs with cream trim and natural wood and works well on walls, cabinets, and ceilings.

Behr selected Grounded (T27-01), a dark olive green with brown undertones inspired by nature.

“The shade’s organic warmth creates a sense of calm and renewal, making every room feel inviting and effortlessly livable,” said Kayla Kratz, Head of Color at Behr Paint Company, in a company release.

Behr recommends Grounded for interior and exterior projects, including living rooms and kitchens.

Valspar chose Cottage Door (8004-38E), a muted blue-gray with warm undertones.

“A cultural shift toward intentional living and self-reflection calls for authentic and heartfelt hues,” according to Valspar.

The color pairs with light neutrals and natural wood, making it suitable for bedrooms and living rooms. It also works on exterior siding and front doors.

Glidden selected Artifact (PPG10-16), a smoky blue-gray with hints of aqua.

“A calm, fresh aqua that feels like a reset for your whole space,” according to Glidden.

The shade works in bedrooms, bathrooms, and living rooms. Glidden also pairs Artifact with lighter blues and earthy greens.

Dunn-Edwards chose French Press Coffee (DEBN07), a dark brown with subtle purple undertones that resemble dark roasted coffee.

“The overall effect is one of layered, lived-in luxury,” according to Dunn-Edwards, describing its 2027 color trends.

The brand recommends French Press Coffee for powder rooms, home offices, and entryways, where its darker color can stand out against lighter finishes.

Dutch Boy selected Deep Rooted (311-7DB), a warm orange that resembles terracotta.

“As paint trends show people seeking more grounding, authenticity, and connection, Deep Rooted brings stability and a sense of calm to the home,” according to Dutch Boy.

The brand suggests pairing the color with soft whites, blues, or greens on walls and furniture.

Clark+Kensington, available at Ace Hardware, selected Vantage Point (38B-4), a rich blue with muted undertones.

“Vantage Point brings a sense of warmth and character to interiors,” said Kim Lefko, Chief Marketing Officer at Ace Hardware, in a company statement.

The brand pairs it with warm neutrals and lighter colors through its Drift and Mellow palettes.

Graham & Brown chose Rose Earth, a soft pink with earthy undertones.

“Rose Earth takes its cue from the natural mineral pigments traditionally sourced from clay deposits in Italy,” according to Graham & Brown.

The brand designed it to complement its Roselle Grove floral wallpaper, though the color also works with greens and warm neutrals.

C2 Paint selected Pueblo (C2-510), a warm shade between dusty pink and terracotta.

“For 2027, we’re looking outward again, toward the things that make us feel alive,” according to C2 Paint.

Pueblo changes with the light, appearing pinker during the day and darker in the evening. C2 Paint suggests pairing it with deep browns, muted greens, and natural materials.

Minwax chose Pickled Oak, a light wood stain with warm undertones, as its 2027 Color of the Year.

“As homeowners gravitate toward more organic interiors, Pickled Oak answers the call for clarity and connection,” according to Minwax.

Unlike paint, the stain lets the wood grain show through. Homeowners can use it to lighten the appearance of old furniture, shelving, and other wood projects.
