Home Depot (NYSE: HD) shares fell around 2% in pre-market trading despite beating Wall Street expectations for its third-quarter revenue and earnings. The retailer reported revenue of $43.4 billion, up 4.9% year-over-year and above the $42.78 billion estimate. Adjusted earnings per share came in at $3.86, beating the $3.84 consensus estimate. Comparable sales increased 2.2%, including a 2.2% rise in US comparable sales. However, investors reacted to the company’s tighter full-year profit guidance. Incoming CEO Richard McPhail said quarterly results exceeded expectations and the company gained market share.
