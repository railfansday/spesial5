Anthropic’s market value climbed above $2 trillion on its first day of trading, making it the 10th US-listed tech company currently worth $1 trillion or more. The milestone puts the Claude developer ahead of SpaceX, Broadcom, Meta and Tesla in the rankings, though still well behind Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Alphabet and Amazon. Shares opened at $111.50 against an $83 IPO price before easing later in the session as investors took profits. Trading was heavy, with more than 640 million shares changing hands by midday, already above the 522 million traded on SpaceX’s full first day in June. Anthropic was the most actively traded stock on the Nasdaq, and retail demand for the offering was the largest on record. The debut also drew political pushback. Senator Mark Warner unveiled a bill requiring public companies to disclose AI-related financing, while Representative Pramila Jayapal and Senator Chris Van Hollen called for urgent AI regulation.
