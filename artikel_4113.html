Here's What The Expanse: Osiris Reborn's Recast Main Character Looks and Sounds Like

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Developer and publisher Owlcat Games has revealed the new face and voice of the male protagonist in The Expanse: Osiris Reborn.

Following negative feedback from fans, the company announced it would be recasting the role and making updates to the character's appearance.

In the below video, the revamped captain makes his debut:

The new voice is provided by actor Blythe Melin, who will perform as both the male protagonist and his brother, J.

The video also shows a quite dramatic visual change for the character's default look. Players will be able to fully customise their protagonist, but Owlcat says in a press release it's "important to have a default look players can use as a basis for their own captain."

"The developers have redesigned the male captain’s model to give him a less standard military personnel design with more detail and polish for players to work with. His handsome appearance is also matched by his new voice, brought to life by voice actor Blythe Melin, who previously worked on titles like Genshin Impact, Honkai Star Rail, Diablo IV, Ready or Not, and more. Blythe doesn’t just take over as the captain, he also voices his more carefree and relaxed identical twin J."

Owlcat adds that more information about additional members of the cast will be shared "at a later date."

As mentioned, the original version of the male captain was widely panned, a criticism we made in our hands-on preview earlier this year.

Early responses to the changes appear to be pretty positive, though, which is good news.
