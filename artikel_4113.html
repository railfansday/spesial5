GTA 6 Removes Replayable Missions And Medal Objectives

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Despite some concern surrounding its overly positive early review scores, Grand Theft Auto 6 impressions are already going viral now that the game has been released for players worldwide. While the release of GTA 6 seems to have been well worth the wait, players have also started discovering the new gameplay mechanics coming or being changed in the new entry for better or for worse.

Although most of the random encounters and gameplay surprises have been met with plenty of praise from eager fans, the removal of Rockstar's classic replayability feature has others more than a little concerned about its long-term gaming potential.

Unlike more recent Rockstar games that let you simply select completed missions from a menu to experience them again whenever you want, reports from early sources like Pirat_Nation on X have confirmed that the feature is completely absent from GTA 6.

While players are still discovering Easter eggs in GTA 6, this callback one was far too iconic to miss.

While some players were praising the change since it also means the often tedious medal challenges for every mission are no longer included and required for 100% completion, others are lamenting the change entirely. As noted by IGN, who originally spotted the change, it liekly comes as a result of the branching storyline that causes missions to be altered dramatically based on previous story decisions.

Even if it's a little disappointing that you can't replay your favorite mission whenever you want, Rockstar does have a few other gameplay changes that will let you get a somewhat similar gameplay experience, the first of which comes from the 15 manual save slots you'll be able to choose from.

Rather than simply playing on a single file, you'll now be able to make a new save whenever you want before major milestones or story moments that you can go back and revisit later on.

Letting you skip the lengthy introduction segments that Rockstar games are known for, GTA 6 seems to offer more incentives this time around than usual thanks to its diverging story for replaying the game again, and jumping right into the best parts each time.
