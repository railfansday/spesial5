Why I’m Wary of a Publicis-Owned LiveRamp—and Your CMO Should Be Too

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Publicis understands that the next decade of marketing will be driven by data and AI agents. That’s why for them, buying LiveRamp is a brilliant move.

But as a client on the other side of the table, I see the fundamental issues with what Publicis is asking us to do: Compromise on our intelligence.

When I shared that sentiment on LinkedIn, I found many of my fellow CMOs have the same concern.

To be clear, I’m not saying that Publicis has done anything wrong in acquiring LiveRamp. But for clients, the incentives have changed.

As part of the infrastructure that connects brands with publishers, platforms, and retailers, one of LiveRamp’s main advantages was its independence.

Benoit Vatere is the chief media and digital commerce officer of canned water brand Liquid Death.
