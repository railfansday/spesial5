Creamy Stovetop Mac and Cheese

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Thick and creamy mac and cheese – made on the stovetop! Everyone loves this one!

Published by Lindsay Ostrom on November 11, 2026

Thick, creamy, and I might even say Panera-style in its creamy, cheesy, velvety glory.

This summer, my daughter and my husband biked 50+ miles to raise money for frogs (I know, can’t make this up, but stick with me). We had a little welcome home party at the end of the bike ride, and I made this mac and cheese for 25 people at that party. Multiple friends came up and asked me for the recipe.

Since then, I’ve made this several more times, always when we have friends for dinner or I’m feeding a big group of people.

So in my mind, this is a party mac and cheese. You can serve it to a lot of people, and mostly everyone will love it.

(Pictured here with the BBQ meatballs that I made for our friends last night! So yum.)

When I do this for a group of people, I’ll do a couple things ahead of time:

Then when it’s time to assemble, I reheat the bechamel, add the cheese, and stir the noodles in. A bit of warm water at the end helps it stay loose and creamy!

My favorite cheese lately has been the cheddar and Gruyere blend blocks that you can buy at ALDI or Trader Joe’s, but I also love some straight cheddar or even a few slices of American for a milder, creamier finish.

It’s just uncluttered, creamy, comforting homemade mac and cheese. Hope you love it!

Pasta, cheese, butter, milk – all the essentials!

Cook your noodles and set aside (I love to use classic elbow macaroni!).

Melt butter, add flour, and whisk until nice and bubbly!

Go slowly and whisk after each little bit. Stir stir stir until it’s nice and smooth and thickened.

Chef’s choice, but I like Gruyere and mild cheddar!

Add your noodles and bring it all together!

That’s it! Cheesy, comforting, and so good.

Thick and creamy mac and cheese! People always ask me for this recipe. We call it party mac and cheese because it’s perfect for a crowd!

This will thicken up as it rests, so it is best to serve almost immediately.

If you want to prep it ahead (which I do quite a bit, being that it’s party mac and cheese) – I cook the noodles ahead, grate the cheese ahead, and even make the bechamel sauce with the flour, butter, and milk. Then, right before serving, I re-warm the sauce (adding water and whisking to bring make it smooth again), gently melt the cheese in, and toss the noodles in the sauce.
