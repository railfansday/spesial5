YouTuber JaackMaate announces his own Christmas event after city drops him over “edgy” videos

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

YouTuber JaackMaate has announced plans to hold his own Christmas celebration in Norwich after the city council dropped him from its official lights switch-on over videos he made more than a decade ago.

The 33-year-old creator, whose real name is Jack Dean, was removed from Norwich’s November 19 Christmas event following a single complaint about his “edgy” content from 2014.

Dean had originally been invited to switch on the lights alongside his Happy Hour podcast co-hosts Stevie White and Robbie Knox, describing the opportunity as a childhood dream.

However, rather than abandoning the festivities entirely, the trio have now announced their own plans for November 19, including a Christmas lights switch-on, a party and a charity comedy show.

“We’re disappointed, we’re upset, but the main reason we’re sad is because we know a lot of you guys, the Happy Hour listeners, were coming down to join us,” Dean explained in a new video.

The trio revealed that some fans had already booked hotels and train tickets to attend the original celebration, prompting them to organize an alternative.

“Just because Norwich Council won’t let us switch on the main Christmas lights, it doesn’t mean we can’t switch on other lights,” they announced.

The group plans to spend November 19 visiting locations around Norwich before finishing at the Murderers pub, where they’ll switch on its Christmas lights and host a “massive party” with fans.

They also announced a one-off live performance at The Lanes Comedy Club to raise money for Mind the Gap, the Lord Mayor’s Civic Charity, with more details to follow.

Dean and his co-hosts are encouraging local independent businesses, community projects and charities to contact them if they want the trio to switch on their Christmas lights.

“We want to show you why we believe this is the best city around,” the group added, encouraging fans to still make the trip to Norwich.

The announcement comes after Norwich City Council apologized for failing to identify concerns about Dean’s previous content before inviting him to participate.

The council’s official Christmas lights switch-on is still scheduled for November 19, although Dean will no longer be taking part.

Despite the controversy, the YouTuber ended his announcement by thanking fans for their support and promising to see them on November 19.

The Happy Hour team’s plot was a smash hit with fans.

“I’d say this is better than the original plan,” one said.

“What a way to turn a negative into a positive,” another chimed in. “Shows just how much you’ve grown as a person and shouldn’t be judged for your past.”
