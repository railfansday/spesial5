McDonald&#8217;s Splits Top U.S. Marketing Job As U.S. CMO Alyssa Buetikofer Exits

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Alyssa Buetikofer is leaving McDonald’s after 15 years, and less than two years into her role as U.S. chief marketing and customer experience officer.

Buetikofer, who took over from Tariq Hassan after his 2025 departure, has chosen to step down to spend more time with her family, according to an internal memo ADWEEK obtained.

While McDonald’s searches for a permanent U.S. CMO, its global CMO and head of new business ventures, Morgan Flatley, will take on the additional remit of interim U.S. chief creative officer (CCO). She’s no stranger to the market, having served as U.S. chief marketing and digital customer experience officer from 2017 to 2021.
