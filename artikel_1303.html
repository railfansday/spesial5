Mbuemo Disaster: Man Utd Players Rated and Slated vs Roma

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Manchester United produced a scintillating second half comeback to come from 2-0 down to beat Roma 3-2 in a Champions League classic on Tuesday.

The Italian side started on the front foot and smelt nerves around Old Trafford, pressing from the front and causing United to make errors.

And that is what happened as Roma pounced on a mistake from Kobbie Mainoo with Matias Soule producing the finishing touch to give the visitors an 11th-minute lead.

Soon after, United made another error at the back which led to Roma being given a huge opportunity to double their lead from the penalty spot. Donyell Malen took it with aplomb, slotting the ball into the bottom corner to put Gianpiero Gasperini's side 2-0 ahead and stun Old Trafford.

The immediate response from the Reds was not what Carrick would have wanted, though he did get one after half-time with United coming out with a point to prove.

Bruno Fernandes halved the deficit from the penalty spot at the second attempt, and that gave United some momentum to get back into the game.

After Bryan Mbeumo and Harry Maguire missed guilt-edge chances to equalise for United, Matheus Cunha stepped up to the plate and levelled, slotting home after a mistake from Roma.

Then Cunha became the new hero at United as he scored his second and the Reds' third to put them 3-2 in front, completing the comeback with a powerful strike which flew in off the post.

Although Cunha's evening ended on a sour note with a red card for two quick-fire bookable offences, United held on for a much-needed win for Carrick.
