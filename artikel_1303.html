Mourinho makes Endrick stance clear following interest from Milan and Roma

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Jose Mourinho has made it clear that he does not want to lose hold of Brazilian talent Endrick, who has been linked with January moves to Milan and Roma, but admits that the situation will be beyond his control if the ‘top management’ decides to ‘do something different’.

According to recent reports, Milan are willing to invest €60m on the signing of Endrick in January, and Roma are also said to be interested in the event that the Brazilian becomes available for loan.

Mourinho, meanwhile, has made it clear that he ‘needs’ Endrick at his disposal, but has admitted that the final say is not his to make.

“Look, for me, the situation is very clear. For me, Real Madrid is much more important than Endrick. I love Endrick very much, I care about him a lot, he’s a fantastic guy. Real Madrid needs him, the Real Madrid coach needs him,” said the Special One (via La Gazzetta dello Sport).

He continued: “There’s your response. You have it, those who write about it have it, the agents have it, the clubs that send stories to the newspapers have it. You have my answer. But, if the top management at Real Madrid, meaning my superiors, decide to do something different, in today’s football, you have to respect it.”

Endrick is not the only player being linked with a move to Milan or Roma, though. Club Brugge’s Nicolo Tresoldi is also of interest to the Rossoneri and Friday’s reports claim that VfB Stuttgart’s Ermedin Demirovic is also an option.

Roma, meanwhile, have been linked with moves for a few other traditional wingers, including Chelsea’s Jamie Bynoe-Gittens and Nottingham Forest’s Callum Hudson-Odoi.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
