O'Neill hails recalled midfield duo Oxlade-Chamberlain and McCowan after both net to help sink St Mirren

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Published: 15:17 EST, 21 November 2026 | Updated: 15:31 EST, 21 November 2026

Celtic manager Martin O’Neill hailed midfield duo Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain and Luke McCowan after the recalled pair both scored in the 4-1 home win over St Mirren.

With Mika Baur facing 10 weeks out with an ankle injury and Benjamin Nygren nursing a heel problem, O’Neill handed McCowan his second start of the season and Oxlade-Chamberlain his fifth, as the two joined forces for the first time.

Oxlade-Chamberlain and McCowan netted either side of Camilo Duran in a nine-minute flurry of goals late in the first half with Callum McGregor making it four for Celtic in the second period before Killian Phillips reduced the deficit for Saints from the penalty spot.

O’Neill said: ‘Alex has been waiting around for a while to get into the side. He’s a brilliant passer of the ball. He took his goal very well with a calm finish in the bottom corner.

‘He was feeling a little bit sore, so taking him off was just precautionary.

‘I was also delighted Luke got his goal. That should give him a boost in confidence.’

Oxlade-Chamberlain congratulates McCowan after he scored to make it three for Celtic

McCowan faced criticism after Celtic’s Champions League collapse against LASK and his only previous start this season saw him taken off at half-time in a home defeat by Rangers.

O’Neill said: ‘I thought he did really well, also playing the full game given it has been some considerable time since he has done that.

‘He got the ball early on and played it almost blindly and it was intercepted and I thought, “oh, gosh, here we go”. But he roared back, took his goal well and I am really pleased for him. He is a real genuine lad.

‘It has been tough for him. In the game against Rangers, he was thrown into a position, and it didn’t start well for him in the match. I think he felt it was deja vu for him in terms of the game at the back end of last season too. That will be good for him.’

The Celtic manager also introduced 19-year-old former PSV Eindhoven midfielder Joel van den Berg for a debut in the second half.

‘I thought he did very well,’ said O’Neill. ‘He’s coming on after an injury which kept him out for some time.

‘I am pleased there is a bit of competition for places now.’

The veteran midfielder celebrates his strike that opened the scoring against St Mirren

St Mirren manager Craig McLeish felt Celtic were clinical in his team’s heavy defeat in Glasgow.

‘It’s a really sore one with the way the scoreline ended up,’ said McLeish.

‘I thought we started the game quite well and then that flurry of goals can happen here when you’re playing against such a strong team. The difference was the quality in our box compared to theirs.

‘They are just so clinical, and they get into those good areas and they punish you.

‘I felt we got into some similar areas throughout the game. We didn’t give up, we didn’t move away from what we were trying to do, but we were just really lacking that final bit.’
