In Greece, our campaign is primarily centred on reducing the suffering, abuse, and neglect of animals while increasing public awareness of their needs and rights. For years, our programs and ideas have been influencing how animal charities operate and making permanent changes for a better future for animal welfare in Greece.

We created the following programs: Volunteer, SMART Family Video Series, Veterinary Internship, SMART Yards, and Adoption Vacation, all of which are operational every day and are detailed on the Programs page on our website, if you would like to take a closer look. We could not function without the help of our amazing Volunteers.

We successfully found wonderful homes for 73 cats in 2025. I'm not 100% sure, but I would say we are one of the leading cat adopters in Greece!

We receive multiple enquiries from tourists who have found a cat or kitten, and they want to take it home with them to their country. Many times they have cared for them during their stay, provided medicine, bottle-fed babies, etc. This has become increasingly common, with visitors seeing cats all over Greece. Therefore, I decided to offer the Adoption-Vacation as a package. We care for the kitten or cat until it is ready to travel, taking care of the necessary vaccines, passport, microchip, etc. Once the cat is ready to travel, we make travel arrangements with the adopter.

You can see our cats, kittens, and dogs for adoption on our website and have a video chat with us about your requirements and meet a cat if you have already fallen in love (we created a Kitty Picker to help you choose the character of your cat), and then arrange a visit with us when you arrive in Greece. If the home check is secured, we arrange the papers for your new pet to travel home with you. Or we can foster a cat you rescue while you are in Greece and deal with the veterinary needs, papers etc. The cat can travel after you leave with a flight buddy or one of our volunteers.

What better souvenir to have from Greece than a beautiful cat that needed special love and care?

Everything we do is linked. The more sterilizations we achieve, the safer and healthier the animals are in the community; they are interacting better with each other, and people know they are sterilized.

We would like the municipalities and communities all over Greece to enjoy the benefits of the SMART Yards and help take care of the animals. Working together is the future.  We are also collaborating with some officials regarding some policies involving the strays and to help create some standards for housing the strays. Animals and people need to coexist in a humane way. Not every cat needs to be inside a home but can remain cared for and part of our ecosystem.

A rather big goal is to find someone to help us purchase the property we rent, make refurbishments and sustainable features, and add a vet clinic for shared use with other charities and our vet interns. This is a tall order, but we are looking for someone who would like to leave a legacy.

We also want to extend our ability to provide food and shelter after sterilization so that every animal is cared for.

Our animals live in a home-style environment, so every day is the same as if they were living in a family home. Cats are either in bedrooms; we have a retirement room, a special dietary needs room, and a kitten nursery, each with cats that are selected to share a room together, or they are in the main house and free to mingle and hang out on our netted balconies in cute wooden houses and take in the fresh air and nature, since we are in a rural setting, just 15 minutes from Athens International Airport.

Some of the funniest moments are with kittens and playful cats. Plus, bonded pairs â we have a husband-and-wife team called Jasper and Margot who need to be adopted together, and they are very entertaining with their games and spats.

Cats and dogs choose who they like and want to go home with. We find people fall in love with cats that come to them, and a chemistry is formed. Sometimes a visitor leaves, and they cannot stop thinking about the cat they fell in love with and make an application to adopt. We are so happy when this happens. We have repeat adoptions from families and visitors all over the world. We also have Sponsors and Patreon for people to support our work.

Volunteering is open to everyone around the world who would like to come to stay and help us. If you love animals, it's an opportunity to help save lives. It's pure joy to see a cat or dog that was sick transform into good health and finally live in a wonderful family home. That's what the team at Let's be S.M.A.R.T. wants for them all: to be safe, healthy, and happy.

People can also help by following, subscribing, and sharing us on socials. Donations are greatly appreciated, as are supplies, and we created the Amazon Wishlist so gifts can be sent to us directly from Amazon Germany, tax-free.
