Alex Scott Chelsea Transfer Blocked as Bournemouth Eye January Stay

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Bournemouth will block any Alex Scott Chelsea transfer in January. That is according to reports from the Daily Mail and BBC Sport. Both outlets say the Cherries want to keep their young midfielder as they fight to protect their Premier League status this season.

Chelsea have been linked with serious interest in the 22-year-old. Reports suggest the Blues have already tested Bournemouth’s resolve with a bid worth close to £64 million. Bournemouth have given no indication they are willing to negotiate. Scott, the reports say, simply is not for sale this winter.

Reports in the Daily Mail and on BBC Sport state that Bournemouth have no intention of sanctioning a sale this winter. The club’s hierarchy reportedly view Scott as central to their survival plan, wary of leaning on new signings mid-season rather than trusting the squad they already have.

A fee of around £64 million, first reported by Football London, is said to have been offered by Chelsea during recent discussions.

Scott joined Bournemouth from Bristol City in 2023. He has quickly become one of the most sought-after young midfielders in English football. Interest from a club the size of Chelsea shows how far his reputation has grown, though Bournemouth’s stance makes a January move increasingly unlikely.

Scott’s rise has been underlined by his first appearance for England. He came off the bench for a 20-minute cameo as Thomas Tuchel’s side beat Czechia 2-0 in the Nations League. The manager praised him afterwards.

“Alex Scott has maybe one of the best performances in midfield in the Premier League since the start with Bournemouth.”

“Individually he’s one of the outstanding midfielders so he clearly deserves to be with us.”

“He took another step into the right direction. Very impressive and happy that he is on that route. Now he plays in Europe for Bournemouth and deserves to be with us.”

That praise from their former manager will only increase Chelsea’s interest. Scott’s energy and composure suit the identity Xabi Alonso wants to build at Stamford Bridge. The Spaniard has already shown he is willing to rotate his squad, as seen in Xabi Alonso’s squad management decisions this season.

Chelsea travel to face Bournemouth in the Premier League soon. It is a fixture that will give Alonso a close look at a player repeatedly linked with his club. Build-up to that game is already underway, with team news arriving ahead of the Bournemouth clash.

The prospects of an Alex Scott Chelsea transfer happening in January now look slim. Supporters hoping for fresh competition in central midfield may need patience, with Chelsea more likely to revisit their interest when the summer window opens.
