Nottingham Forest told to 'cough up' minimum '£15m' for Birmingham City transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Birmingham City have been urged to demand a strong fee after transfer speculation involving German centre-back, Phil Neumann has come to light once again during the November international break.

The 29-year-old centre-back has been a key reason as to why Chris Davies' side are topping the Championship table after 15 games played.

Blues' record of just 14 concessions during said timeframe is the best in the division as they sit ahead of Middlesbrough and Stoke City on goal difference.

However, transfer speculation at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park has never been too far away, such is the ambition showcased in this part of the world.

That said, it would be a huge blow for the club if they were to lose the imperious defender's services, and exactly why Football League World's Birmingham City fan pundit, Jason Moore, believes at least £15m should be demanded as a result.

Neumann is now in the thick of his second Championship campaign, having officially signed for Blues last July after striking a pre-contract agreement during his final months with Hannover 96.

Despite an indifferent first season in English football, the 6"3 figure was the subject of reported interest from Serie A side, Sassuolo, in January, although a swift exit would not materialise.

A clear trend has developed involving Tom Wagner and Jay Stansfield at Birmingham City.

Neumann then reaffirmed in August that his main aim was to help Birmingham's promotion prospects after further links with Hull City and Borussia Mönchengladbach, whilst Sky Deutschland now claim that Nottingham Forest and Fiorentina are admirers of the former youth international.

It has also been suggested that a move could happen in January, with the defender's valuation being between €5m and €10m.

Neumann has been an ever-present for Birmingham this season, with his 15 appearances in the league helping him surpass 50 in total for the club.

As well as aiding Davies' side's stellar defensive record, the defender's brace at Wrexham in August was critical in a timely success following a 6-1 thrashing in the EFL Cup against Brentford three days prior, which increased the heat on the Blues boss at the time.

Such is his importance at the spine of Blues' side, the aforementioned Moore made his feelings clear over this transfer saga when asked by FLW.

"It's got to be £15m at the bare minimum," he said.

"The guy is an absolute form of his life, and there's an argument there that he's been the best centre-half in the division. We've probably got the best centre-half pairing in the entire division as well.

"£15m, and I'm only saying £15m because of his age, so cough it up. But, for me, he doesn't go anywhere," Moore claimed.

"He's too vital to what we're doing, and currently we look like we're in a good position to potentially try and go up. I just don't see a world where we'd even entertain it unless someone's going to cough up some serious money for him.

"For me, it has to be a minimum of £15m. If someone's not going to cough that up, then no."

Neumann is under contract until June 2028, meaning Birmingham will hold the majority of the cards when it comes to deciding the defender's short-term future.

Having never played in any top-flight thus far, it would be no surprise if such a move perked the defender's interest, but there is certainly a chance that such feats could transpire in B9, where he is likely to continue receiving more gametime.
