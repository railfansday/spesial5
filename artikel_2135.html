Former EFL player highlights ‘worry’ about QPR’s promotion chances

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

QPR are sat in 8th place in the Championship table after 15 fixtures ahead of West Ham and Sheffield United. They are in the play-offs along with Stoke City, Wolves, West Brom, Swansea City and Southampton.

The Hoops won 2-1 at home against Lincoln City last time out before the international break after goals by Richard Kone and Glen Kamara. They are back in action next weekend against Millwall at The Den.

Parkin, who played for the likes of Ipswich Town, Luton Town and Swindon Town, has delivered his verdict on the London outfit on the Benjamin Bloom YouTube channel: “If you offered the top eight to any QPR fan right now they’d snap your hand off. It’s been a terrifically positive start to the season. I think I’ve been 100% positive about Julien Stephan throughout.”

He added: “There’s more to come from some of the forward players. I still do worry if Kone was to get injured again and (Rumarn) Burrell misses games this season, is there enough depth in the forward positions to get the goals and get QPR to where they want to be.”

QPR swooped to sign highly-rated goalkeeper Pierce Charles on loan from Manchester City in the summer. He is excellent with the ball at his feet and the former Sheffield Wednesday man has been a useful asset for the Hoops in this campaign to date.

In defence, the R’s have some solid operators like Jimmy Dunne and Ronnie Edwards. However, only Middlesbrough have conceded more than them in the top eight this term so they could do with tightening things up at the back a little bit.

Kamara has turned into a shrewd addition following his arrival in the last transfer window. He battles it out with the likes of Jonathan Varane and Nicolas Madsen for a place in Julien Stephan‘s starting XI in the middle of the park.

Further up the pitch, QPR have a host of dangerous wingers like Harvey Vale, Koki Saito, Kwame Poku and Paul Smyth. Kone has been in decent form over recent times, with Rumarn Burrell still out injured.

As Parkin has alluded to, QPR could struggle for goals if Kone joined Burrell in the treatment room. They don’t have too much competition and depth in their position at this moment in time.

Therefore, the Hoops should look to sign a new striker in the January transfer window. They will get the chance to bolster their attacking arsenal this winter by landing another option.

The R’s will also have the opportunity to let some players head out the exit door as well if needed. That would help free up space and funds in their squad to pave the way for reinforcements.

QPR will be in confident mood after their win over Lincoln City. They will be hoping for more of the same against Millwall next up.
