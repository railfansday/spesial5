West Brom told to ‘find the money’ and sign Blackburn Rovers star

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

EFL pundit Tom Garratt has urged West Brom to ‘find the money’ to sign Blackburn Rovers ace Andri Gudjohnsen during the January transfer window.

Speaking on the latest edition of the Pyramid Podcast, Garratt, hosting alongside Callum Fowler, believes Gudjohnsen could be the missing piece in the Albion jigsaw to help the Black Country outfit seal promotion back to the Premier League.

James Morrison’s men continue to be in the promotion conversation, heading into the latest international break unbeaten in their last six games and securing four victories in that period.

Their latest 3-0 romping of Millwall last weekend leaves Albion fifth in the second-tier standings, sitting just one point off the summit in such a compact top eight.

Much was made at the beginning of the season about West Brom’s lack of a clinical frontman, with it one of the main concerns as to why some connected with Albion feel a promotion push may fade away in time.

Controlling shareholder Shilen Patel may be able to fix the issue in the January transfer window, should funds become available, and it could well be the difference maker in whether they return to the big time or not.

So, with this in mind, well-known EFL pundit and West Brom supporter Tom Garratt has demanded the club make a deal to sign one of the best-performing strikers in the Championship, at this moment in time.

Despite having such an excellent summer transfer window, one criticism from some West Brom supporters at the club was not going all out to recruit a striker who could score regularly at Championship level.

Albion were reportedly interested in Southampton’s Cameron Archer, but missed out on a deal due to his high wages on the South Coast, with the 24-year-old eventually going on to move to French outfit Auxerre instead, where he has scored six goals in eight Ligue 1 games.

The Baggies were busy during the summer window revamping some of the forward line, replacing the outgoings of Josh Maja and Daryl Dike with Barney Stewart and Jimmy-Jay Morgan.

Morgan in particular has been a roaring success so far, grabbing eight goal contributions in 15 league matches so far for the club, but is not a conventional centre-forward, favouring drifting over to the left-hand side while a proper number nine stays central.

Stewart, since moving from Scottish side Falkirk, has found opportunities hard to come by and has netted just once for his new club so far, while Aune Heggebo in his second season at the club has also found himself in and out of the side, with most of his success coming off the bench and making an impact in recent times.

Thankfully, much of Albion’s early success has been down to players in other positions chipping in with goals, with the likes of Isaac Price notching six times and providing three assists, while fellow summer arrival Felix Horn Myhre now has three goals to his name in the blue and white stripes.

West Bromwich Albion signed Felix Horn Myhre for just £3.1 million in the summer ahead of Spanish outfit Elche

Despite that, Albion fans would be put at ease to see a clinical frontman lead the line from January onwards, with a regular source of goals badly needed for the Baggies to continue to fight towards the top end of the division.

Speaking on the latest episode of the Pyramid Podcast with Callum Fowler, EFL pundit Tom Garratt has demanded West Brom ‘find the money’ to sign Blackburn Rovers star Andri Gudjohnsen in January.

Gudjohnsen is already into double figures for goals from his opening 14 league games this season, notably scoring a brace at The Hawthorns against West Brom last month, while also netting in Rovers' 2-1 victory over West Ham last weekend.

And Garratt was gushing about the 24-year-old's strike against the Hammers, believing he is the second-best striker in the division and can help the Baggies turn their promotion hopes into a reality.

Garratt said: “This is a plea to West Bromwich Albion again.

“Find the money, find the money somewhere, it’s there somewhere. (Tom) Fellows went for a lot of money. We have sold players in recent years, go and buy (Andri) Gudjohnsen.

“His movement for the goal is so, so good. Don’t get me wrong, it’s a ‘tap in’, but his movement to get there is unbelievable. He is the second-best striker in this league.”

There’s no doubt that what’s missing most in West Brom’s squad is the addition of a striker that can usually net between 20 and 25 goals in a full Championship season, with Albion continuing to bank on the likes of Isaac Price to perform and find the back of the net consistently.

But over time, that’s at risk of drying up, so Albion must do all they can at the turn of the new year to bring in a forward who has an eye for goal and can make a real difference at the top end of the pitch.

And while it’s likely to be one of the areas the Baggies hierarchy look to address, the chances of Blackburn’s Andri Gudjohnsen signing on the dotted line at The Hawthorns is bound to be extremely low.

After having much of a revamp during the summer transfer window, Albion are likely to not possess many financial funds in January, with clubs across the country much more active in the loan market as bigger sides to themselves decide to temporarily part ways with players who can’t break into their first-team crop.

Meanwhile, Gudjohnsen’s impact at Blackburn cannot be understated. His 10 goals so far have been a massive reason as to why Rovers sit on the periphery of the top eight, and while the Lancashire outfit have let go of some stars on the cheap in recent times, there’s no reason to suggest why Blackburn wouldn’t want a massive fee to part them from their Icelandic star - a price that only Premier League level sides can afford.

You do feel if Gudjohnsen was to move on from Ewood Park in January, then it would be permanently, so factoring in what would be such a high price to secure his services and an unwillingness to let him leave on loan, in particular to a Championship rival, you would think it’s a very low chance that West Brom will be Gudjohnsen’s next club.

However, there’s no reason why Albion shouldn’t be able to land a similar goalscoring forward on loan in January, with the winter window always throwing up such unexpected deals the closer the clock ticks towards the deadline.
