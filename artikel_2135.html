Michael Beale pleads for job as ex-Rangers boss admits 'we are in trouble'

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Michael Beale has pleaded for his job after the ex-Rangers manager suffered six defeats in a row at his club in Belgium.

The English coach was appointed Zulte Waregem at the start of the season, but his move to Belgium has turned into a nightmare after his side's weekend 4-1 defeat at Sint-Truiden meant they had lost their last six league games.

Beale received a vote of confidence last week, but angry fans turned on their team after their latest loss.

And the former Sunderland boss admitted the decision is out of his hands.
