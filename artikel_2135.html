Aidan Morris transfer twist emerges involving Middlesbrough, West Ham and Southampton

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Middlesbrough may be flying high in the Championship at present, but they are reportedly at risk of losing star midfielder, Aidan Morris.

According to German publication, BILD, the American is one of three second tier stars currently being eyed by Bundesliga outfit, VfB Stuttgart, with the January transfer window edging ever closer.

Kim Hellberg has restructured his squad at the Riverside Stadium for the current campaign, 12 months on from his initial appointment in place of controversial former head coach, Rob Edwards.

Boro enjoyed a remarkable recruitment drive by adding the likes of Radek Vitek, Ash Phillips, Max Arfsten, Sebastian Berhalter, Amario Cozier-Duberry and Will Lankshear to the Swede's ranks, despite the high-profile sale of Hayden Hackney to Everton following May's play-off final defeat.

Morris has since upped his performance levels even further in the first 15 games of the Championship season, with the North East side sat second as a three-goal swing is all separating them and current leaders, Birmingham City.

Morris, 25, arrived on Teesside from Major League Soccer outfit, Columbus Crew, two summers ago in a $4m (£3m) deal, and has since become one of Middlesbrough's key men.

The 14-time USMNT international is nearing 100 Championship appearances, having only missed the opening-day victory over Lincoln City so far this season whilst also netting against Burnley and Bolton Wanderers, with the strike at Turf Moore being his first for the club.

Morris' all-round play has been integral to the way in which Hellberg's side create a bundle of chances in the majority of fixtures, and it was claimed last month that clubs on the continent had been alerted by such performances, although Tom Bogert's report also revealed his desire to lead Middlesbrough back to the Premier League.

However, BILD's fresh report claims Morris is being identified as a potential replacement for Angelo Stiller, whose future at the Mercedes-Benz Arena is far from certain, with AC Milan credited with interest in the nine-cap German international.

Stiller's contract expires in 2028, and there are suggestions that he may be sold next summer in a bid for Stuttgart to make a strong sum of cash from his services.

It comes as no real surprise that Morris' collection of strong performances at the Riverside have attracted potential suitors, but such exit talk involving the American also revolves around the futures of two divisional counterparts.

Stuttgart were heavily linked with a move for Southampton's Caspar Jander earlier in the year. It was claimed after the Spygate scandal that the 23-year-old was seeking an exit from St Mary's after the German top-flight club's initial interest surfaced.

Jander remained on the South Coast but has endured an injury-hit start to the season, and BILD report that there are still question marks from the UEFA Champions League-competing outfit over a €25m price-tag.

It has also emerged that Sebastian Hoeneß's side have earmarked Arne Engels as a potential target, three months on from the Belgian's £22m switch from Celtic to West Ham United which smashed the EFL's all-time transfer record.

The Belgian has made 14 Championship appearances for the Hammers so far, scoring once and providing three assists.

Morris' influence under Hellberg is echoed by the fact he has averaged the second-highest number of accurate passes per 90 in the Championship, ironically behind Middlesbrough defender, Adilson Malanda.

With plenty of his career still to go, some will feel it is a question of when, rather than if Morris reaches the top-flight in the near future, and Boro will hope that comes as a result of their own 10-year exile ending at the conclusion of the 46-game season.

Before Morris began to add goals to his game this term, Hellberg said of the American in June: "I think there's a lot of players in our team that can play in the Premier League, lots of good players, and Aidan is one of them. He's improved throughout the season, each game better and better."

With the midfielder's contract expiring in June 2028, Boro chiefs may face a dilemma as to what Morris' immediate future holds, albeit those thoughts are clearly secondary right now, with promotion the first aim for the club and player.
