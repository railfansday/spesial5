Docusign Brings Contract Intelligence and Workflows to ChatGPT for Sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Docusign plugin for ChatGPT helps sellers prepare for renewals, spot opportunities to grow accounts, and move deals forward.

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Docusign today announced new ways for sales teams to use the Docusign plugin through ChatGPT for Sales, so customers can see what was agreed to in a contract and act on that information in the same conversation. Sellers can prepare for a renewal, create a sales contract, or start a request with Legal directly in ChatGPT.

Docusign provides the agreement intelligence layer for these workflows. It brings signed terms, renewal deadlines, and commitments together with account context from a connected CRM like Salesforce. Sellers can spend less time tracking down contract details and more time talking with customers about what comes next.

"Every signed contract tells you what the customer bought, what you promised to deliver, and when it's time to talk about what's next," said Allan Thygesen, CEO of Docusign. "With Docusign in OpenAI's new ChatGPT for Sales, sellers can use that information to prepare for a renewal, draft the next agreement, or get Legal involved. Wherever people and AI work, Docusign provides the agreement intelligence to make informed decisions and the workflows to act on them."
