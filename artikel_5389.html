Moso Networks, SOLiD and CTS Introduce Managed CBRS for DAS, Bringing Private Wireless to Existing In-Building Infrastructure

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

SACRAMENTO, Calif., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Moso Networks, a leader in private 5G infrastructure and management solutions, today announced a strategic collaboration with SOLiD, a leading provider of Distributed Antenna System (DAS) infrastructure, and Communication Technology Services (CTS), a leading national in-building wireless systems integrator, to help enterprises transform traditional in-building cellular systems into converged wireless platforms capable of supporting both carrier services and private wireless networks.

Historically, deploying private wireless alongside DAS could require additional in-building distribution infrastructure, adding cost and complexity for enterprises seeking both carrier coverage and dedicated private cellular connectivity. While each service retains its own network components, the collaboration between Moso Networks, SOLiD, and CTS makes it more practical and economical to share the DAS distribution infrastructureâreducing duplication, lowering deployment costs, and helping enterprises get more value from their wireless investments.

By leveraging the shared-spectrum framework established under FCC Part 96, organizations can deploy a converged, in-building wireless architecture that supports both licensed carrier services and secure enterprise private networks. This approach enables building owners to maximize existing DAS investments while introducing dedicated wireless connectivity for operational technology, IoT, automation, security, and other business-critical applications without deploying an entirely separate network infrastructure.

"Building owners and enterprises need to provide reliable indoor carrier coverage, and now they can get more from that investment," said Stephen Leotis, CEO of Moso Networks. "Extending DAS with CBRS private wireless lowers a major barrier to private 5G adoption, which is the cost of building a completely separate in-building network. Enterprises can now serve people and operations from the same distribution infrastructure."

The solution combines SOLiD's carrier-grade DAS infrastructure with Moso Networks' CBRS-based private wireless platform to create a converged in-building wireless architecture that supports both public cellular services and enterprise private network applications over shared DAS distribution infrastructure, while maintaining the distinct network components required for each service. SOLiD's ALLIANCE DAS platform distributes licensed carrier signals throughout a facility while simultaneously transporting CBRS spectrum across the same antenna infrastructure. Moso's private wireless solution provides the CBRS radio access network (RAN), spectrum management, and core network capabilities required to deliver secure, dedicated wireless connectivity for enterprise devices and operational applications.

Together, the integrated architecture enables organizations to leverage shared DAS distribution infrastructure for carrier coverage, voice services, mobile broadband, and CBRS-based private wireless applications such as IoT devices, automation systems, security platforms, and mission-critical communications. The result is a scalable, future-ready wireless platform that simplifies deployment, reduces infrastructure costs, and enables enterprises to support both occupant connectivity and operational technology requirements from a common in-building wireless infrastructure.

CTS brings the engineering expertise and systems integration capabilities needed to turn this converged architecture into a practical, deployable solution. As a national in-building wireless integrator, CTS designs, deploys, integrates, and commissions DAS and private cellular networks, bringing together SOLiD's DAS infrastructure and Moso Networks' CBRS technology to support both commercial carrier services and enterprise private wireless applications through a shared distribution infrastructure.

Beyond initial deployment, CTS provides ongoing monitoring, maintenance, performance optimization, and lifecycle support, allowing enterprises to rely on a single partner for implementation and long-term network operations.

"For enterprises already investing in DAS, deploying a separate private cellular network can mean duplicating portions of the in-building infrastructure," said Dr. Juanyu Bu, Executive Vice President of Engineering at CTS. "By integrating CBRS into a shared DAS distribution architecture, we have an opportunity to reduce that duplication and improve the economics of private cellular deployment. The key is engineering a solution that meets the performance requirements of both carrier services and enterprise private wireless networks."

The solution is particularly attractive for commercial real estate owners, healthcare systems, manufacturing facilities, higher education campuses, airports, transportation hubs, logistics centers, and large public venues where both carrier coverage and operational connectivity are essential.

"As enterprises continue to expand their wireless requirements, infrastructure flexibility becomes increasingly important," said Shane Hague, Senior VP, Sales and Business Development at SOLiD. "This collaboration demonstrates how DAS investments can evolve beyond traditional coverage applications to support a broader range of enterprise wireless services."

For years, DAS has been the answer for indoor cellular coverage. Now it can be much more. Together, Moso Networks, SOLiD, and CTS are helping organizations evolve existing in-building wireless infrastructure into a platform that delivers carrier coverage, private wireless, and the flexibility to support the next generation of enterprise wireless applications.
