Endrick: “I hope to stay at Real, but Mou decides my minutes.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A goal and an assist for Brazil in the 7-0 win over Japan. His tally for Brazil rises to five goals and three assists for the Selecao, but there seems to be no room for him at Real Madrid.

While Carlo Ancelotti continues to call him up, knowing he will be central to the future of the Brazilian national team, José Mourinho is not giving him any space.

After taking the Blancos’ number 9 and expecting significant playing time at one of the world’s best clubs, the Brazilian has played only seven games (156 minutes) and scored just one goal, at the Olimpico in the Champions League.

The 2006-born player could leave Spain as early as January, and in the capital he could enchant Serie A under Gian Piero Gasperini.

Endrick is Gasp’s number one target for the January transfer window, but competition is fierce, and Tony D’Amico will have to work overtime to convince Real Madrid with a convincing and superior formula. In Italy, AC Milan is also monitoring the Blancos’ number 9.

Meanwhile, back home, the Brazilian made some comments in the mixed zone after the resounding victory against Japan, in which he addressed his lack of playing time under the Special One: “I think it’s Mourinho’s decision, right? Of course, I’m always ready to help Real Madrid, which is the biggest club in the world. I hope to be able to spend my life there to help Real Madrid and always be of help to my teammates.”

Upon his return from the international break, Mourinho could give him more playing time. Otherwise, a January move seems increasingly likely.
