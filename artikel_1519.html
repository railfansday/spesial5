Serie A: Naples hangs on and beats Torino

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Napoli season is not going smoothly at the moment. After eleven days of Serie A, Kevin De Bruyne’s club is hanging around the middle of the rankings. This Saturday at home, the Neapolitans barely beat Torino (1-0, 12th day), enough to temporarily climb to eighth place.

With now five wins, three draws and four defeats on the clock, Naples still seems far from being healed. However, this success against the Turinese is valuable in the race for Europe.

The debates were divided, even if it was Massimiliano Allegri who had the most chances. No less than twenty-two shots, ten of which were on target. Opposite, Torino struck twelve times, but only twice towards the frame.

For a long time, the locals stumbled upon the opposing defense and goalkeeper. And it was finally Scott McTominay who delivered his team at the dawn of the last quarter of an hour of play. The Scottish international took over Noa Lang’s effort to give his team a definitive advantage (1-0, 73rd).

This makes two matches in a row without defeat for Naples in the league after the draw conceded just before the international break against Lazio (1-1). For its part, Torino is twelfth.
