Harvard Law School suspends students’ access to library after ‘study-in’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Harvard Law School suspended at least 18 students’ library access for two weeks after they participated in a “study-in” supporting clinical instructors earlier in November. (Photo from Shutterstock)

Harvard Law School suspended at least 18 students’ library access for two weeks after they participated in a “study-in” supporting clinical instructors earlier in November.

The students received emails Friday notifying them of the short-term suspension, according to a story by the Harvard Crimson. The “sit-in,” described as silent, happened Nov. 6 on the fourth floor of the library and lasted about an hour.

In an email reviewed by the Harvard Crimson, Amanda T. Watson, the assistant dean for ibrary and information services at Harvard Law School, cited students’ involvement in an “organized demonstration” in the library as the reason for the suspension.

The email stated that the students’ physical access to the library would be suspended until Nov. 27, but they retained the ability to borrow library materials and have online access, as well as access to other Harvard libraries. The email also stated that the suspension would not be included in their disciplinary records.
