Opinion: Liverpool's perfect right-back is here, and he's phenomenal

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

An exciting right-back has arrived on the scene who is perfect for Liverpool.

It has been a long dilemma for the Reds just how to replace Trent Alexander-Arnold. Back during the start of 2025, there were still hopes the scouser would stay at Anfield.

However, as the months evolved, Alexander-Arnold's abrupt announcement that he was leaving the club for pastures new forced Liverpool's hands.

Amid a scramble, Jeremie Frimpong was purchased and Conor Bradley promoted.

The latter has subsequently struggled with injury problems. Meanwhile, the former just simply doesn't look like a good fit for Liverpool.

It is telling that in the past two seasons Dominik Szoboszlai has spent a considerable amount of time playing the right-back role and now Ronald Araujo is doing the same - even though neither are natural right-backs.

What do Liverpool need in the right-back position?Araujo has been a fantastic addition in the defensive sense for Liverpool. The Uruguayan brings fervent passion that the fans love. He is a no-nonsense defender who makes excellent challenges and will put his body on the line for the shirt. But he also lacks the attacking edge Liverpool need from a full-back. Andoni Iraola at his previous clubs has always used offensive full-backs who can impact the game in the final third and like to make overlapping runs. Equally, they also need to be good defensively and be comfortable at building out from the back. Those are the innate qualities Liverpool's scouts will be looking for when identifying their next right-back. It's a difficult market but there are some intriguing options.
