GTA 6 Launch Times Confirm How You Can Play Early

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

With the Grand Theft Auto 6 launch just days away, players are already looking for ways to get into Vice City before everyone else. And while Rockstar isn’t offering an early-access period, its confirmed regional release schedule has given gamers a familiar workaround to consider: the New Zealand trick.

Rockstar Games has officially confirmed that GTA 6 will unlock at midnight local time on November 19, across all regions on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. This means players won’t all be getting access at the same moment. Instead, the game will slowly go live as the clock strikes midnight in each time zone, putting New Zealand among the first places to receive it.

For players in the US, GTA 6 will unlock at 12am local time on November 19. However, New Zealand players will get access much earlier, with the game going live at midnight on November 19 in their region. Depending on the US time zone, this could give players a head start of several hours by switching their console location to New Zealand.

The New Zealand trick is a popular workaround among Xbox players who want to access games before their local release time. It involves changing the console’s location to New Zealand, allowing players to potentially access a game when it becomes available in that region.

There’s a catch, though. Neither Rockstar nor Microsoft has explicitly confirmed that this workaround will work for GTA 6. The regional release schedule is official, but that doesn’t automatically guarantee that changing your console location will unlock the game early.

There’s also a potential account risk worth considering before attempting the workaround. Microsoft’s Xbox Community Standards specifically prohibit attempts to circumvent regional content restrictions. Its guidance also recommends changing your country or region when you have actually moved to another country, rather than simply using a different location to access content.

Microsoft could take action against accounts found violating these restrictions. Possible consequences include warnings, suspensions or bans, although that does not mean every player attempting the trick will automatically face enforcement. So, do it at your own discretion.

Unfortunately, PS5 owners don’t have an equally simple option. PlayStation accounts are tied to the country selected when they are created, and Sony does not allow users to change that country later.

Accessing the New Zealand storefront would therefore require a separate account registered in that region, along with purchasing the game through that storefront. Simply changing the console's time or location won’t move an existing purchase to New Zealand.

For now, Xbox owners have a potentially easier route to an earlier Grand Theft Auto 6 session, but it remains unconfirmed whether it’d work as expected. With the November 19 launch approaching, players will have to decide whether a few extra hours in Vice City are worth the uncertainty.
