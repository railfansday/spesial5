10 Most Memorable Jujutsu Kaisen Characters

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Few shonen series have introduced as many unforgettable characters as Jujutsu Kaisen. From exceptionally powerful sorcerers and terrifying curses to students struggling to find their place in a dangerous world, Gege Akutami's manga has no shortage of personalities who leave a lasting impression. Some stand out because of their incredible abilities, while others become fan favorites through their relationships, personal struggles, or the emotional weight of their stories.

Of course, being memorable doesn't necessarily mean being heroic or even likable. Several of Jujutsu Kaisen's most recognizable characters are responsible for its cruelest moments, while others remain beloved despite the shortcomings of their storylines. Whether they changed the course of the narrative, delivered some of its most devastating moments, or simply became impossible for fans to forget, these characters helped make Jujutsu Kaisen the phenomenon it is today.

Jujutsu Kaisen introduces Mahito, an undeniably hateable villain, who is almost universally disliked by viewers. From his early appearances, Mahito is crafted to leave a lasting impression with each twisted action, becoming progressively more despicable. His manipulation of Junpei solidifies him as one of the most loathsome characters, but his defining moment comes during the Shibuya Incident arc.

During the height of this arc, Mahito had killed thousands of humans, including one of the most loved characters from the series, Nanami, and left the fate of the female lead, Nobara, hanging. The satisfaction of seeing Yuji finally end Mahito’s reign of terror makes this a pivotal moment, with Mahito brought to his lowest point, solidifying his place as one of the series’ most memorable villains for all the wrong reasons.

The deuteragonist, Megumi Fushiguro, is easily mistaken as the series protagonist instead, as the series intricately weaves many key narratives around him. Megumi possesses the traits commonly associated with shonen lead character, such as hidden abilities, a powerful lineage, and a father whose actions have deeply influenced his life. Unlike some other characters, Megumi has a clear and compelling motive for becoming a sorcerer, making him an intriguing figure to follow.

Additionally, Megumi has also become the subject of a lighthearted joke among fans, who note how quick he is to summon his trump card, Mahoraga, even in situations that don't seem to warrant it. His significance is further emphasized when he becomes a vessel for Sukuna, solidifying his importance in the series. With all these elements, there’s little doubt that Megumi will remain one of the most memorable characters in Jujutsu Kaisen.

While the Shonen genre often struggles with the portrayal of female characters, Jujutsu Kaisen takes this issue to another level. Nobara Kugisaki, the female lead, initially seemed poised to become one of the series’ strongest characters. However, as the narrative progressed, she was sidelined for almost half of the story. Her fate during the Shibuya arc was left ambiguous, and with no return in sight, fans had even accepted that perhaps the character is indeed dead.

Near the end of the series, it’s revealed that Nobara is alive, but by then, the opportunity to craft a compelling arc for her had long passed. Her portrayal feels oddly incomplete, and while she remains a memorable character, it’s for reasons that highlight the series’ missed potential in developing its female lead. Still, there's no doubt that Nobara ranks among Jujutsu Kaisen's strongest women.

Jujutsu Kaisen features several villains, but few are as compelling or memorable as Suguru Geto. The Hidden Inventory arc, which details Geto’s tragic descent into villainy, weaves a poignant and emotionally charged narrative centered around his deep bond with Gojo Satoru. The shock of seeing Geto, once allied with the strongest sorcerer, fall to the dark side and ultimately meet a heartbreaking end at the hands of his best friend makes his arc all the more impactful.

What makes Geto’s story even more intriguing is the revelation that Kenjaku, a millennium-old sorcerer, has taken over his body. Kenjaku’s centuries-old plans come to life, adding layers of intrigue and mystery to the narrative. With his extensive experience as a cursed user, Kenjaku emerges as one of the series’ most fascinating characters, arguably even more so than Sukuna, therefore it is a shame that the series fails to address this character's origin.

The strength of the characters in Jujutsu Kaisen is measured by the amount of cursed energy the characters possess. Therefore, for a character such as Toji, who possesses zero cursed energy, it instantly puts him at a rank of his own. With no cursed energy, Toji is blessed with a side effect that improves all of his senses with superhuman strength. His portrayal leans toward villainy, largely due to his complex relationship with the Zenin family, which led him down a dark path.

One of Toji’s most defining moments is when he nearly killed Gojo Satoru, the strongest sorcerer of the modern era, who possesses a vast amount of cursed energy and the Six Eyes. While Toji was essentially killed in this battle, he has become memorable for almost killing Gojo while not many could even touch the strongest sorcerer.

Yuta Okkotsu, often seen by fans as a more fitting protagonist than Yuji Itadori, does indeed meet all the criteria of a Shōnen protagonist. His portrayal in Jujutsu Kaisen 0 is especially compelling, largely due to the well-crafted narrative that surrounds him. Blessed with immense cursed energy, Yuta is not only able to hold his own in any battle but often comes out victorious. Jujutsu Kaisen 0's manga was also a prototype for the series as a whole, meaning Yuta was originally the series' protagonist before Yuji.

What sets Yuta apart is his unique cursed technique, which allows him to copy the abilities of others and even wield their weapons against them. This versatility, combined with his raw power, makes him a standout character. Arguably, Yuta is one of the strongest characters in Jujutsu Kaisen, and in a heavy-action battle series such as this, it already gives him an edge to be memorable.

Watching Jujutsu Kaisen for the first time will leave an impression on the viewers as if Yuji Itadori isn't the main character of Jujutsu Kaisen. The portrayal of Yuji isn't what is generally expected of a Shōnen hero, with the character even lacking a main motive to enter the world of the sorcerer. It was only after an incident that Yuji was pushed into this new world but since, Yuji has made a remarkable journey.

Born with an extraordinary body, Yuji was able to harbor Sukuna inside him and since then, has done his best what is expected of him. Over time, the series flirts with the "chosen one" trope, hinting that Yuji may share a deeper connection with Sukuna. However, it’s not just this potential destiny that defines him—it’s his inherent uniqueness. This quality makes Yuji not only a standout character in Jujutsu Kaisen but also a refreshing presence within the Shonen genre.

Essentially, Jujutsu Kaisen is Shōnen Jump's most battle-focused manga, so the popularity of the characters is rooted in how strong the characters are. Ryomen Sukuna, the series's main antagonist, is arguably the strongest character of the series, with a scale of power so high that even Gojo Satoru, deemed as the strongest, didn't stand a chance. It was only through the cumulative damage inflicted by various sorcerers and the fact that Sukuna had not yet regained his full strength that he was eventually brought down.

Jujutsu Kaisen has many sorcerers with overwhelming powers and insane cursed techniques, but only a few can be called the strongest in the franchise.

Every action Sukuna took throughout the series was driven solely by his own ambitions, and in the process, he sacrificed thousands without a second thought. Fans initially believed that Sukuna would play a role similar to Kurama from Naruto, aiding Yuji in some way. However, Sukuna’s purpose was singularly his own. Sukuna's ruthless nature and overwhelming prowess make him one of the series' most memorable and unique characters.

Amidst the characters wielding incredible supernatural powers and villains with centuries-old schemes, Kento Nanami stands out as the most relatable figure in Jujutsu Kaisen. His grounded presence introduces a sense of realism that naturally draws viewers to him. Unlike many characters with lofty dreams and aspirations, Nanami simply desires to live a good, uneventful life.

As Yuji Itadori's mentor, Nanami brings a heartwarming dynamic to the series, forming what is arguably its best duo. His reluctance to fully embrace the life of a sorcerer, due to the profession’s inescapable ties to death, makes his eventual fate all the more heartbreaking. Written with a relatable realism that resonates with Jujutsu Kaisen fans, Nanami is one of the most memorable and well-crafted characters in Jujutsu Kaisen.

Satoru Gojo, the strongest sorcerer of the modern era, also known as the 'Honored One' or simply, 'The Strongest,' goes by many names, and it’s hardly an exaggeration to say that his popularity is more than the whole series itself. From the series' commencement, the series depicted Gojo as the strongest, and since then, the fleets of the strongest sorcerers have been unachievable. Gojo's portrayal in Jujutsu Kaisen has become such an iconic element that his influence has affected fans to act in real life. Jujutsu Kaisen's creator also famously does not like Gojo, and has joked about it several times.

When Gojo was first sealed inside the prison realm, an X account (formerly Twitter) was dedicated solely to counting the real-time duration of his confinement. Whereas, when Gojo met his demise in the battle against Sukuna, fans in Chile even held a real-life memorial in his honor, tied to the series' promotion. These actions from fans demonstrate just how deeply Gojo resonates, arguably making him the most memorable character in Jujutsu Kaisen, perhaps even more iconic than the series itself.

Jujutsu Kaisen is a Japanese anime and manga series created by Gege Akutami. The story is set in a world where Cursed Spirits, born from negative human emotions, prey on humanity. It follows high school student Yuji Itadori as he becomes entangled in the world of Jujutsu Sorcery after swallowing a cursed talisman—Ryomen Sukuna's finger—and becomes the host for one of the most powerful curses. Yuji joins the Tokyo Metropolitan Magic Technical College to learn how to combat curses while searching for the remaining fingers of Sukuna to exorcise him permanently.
