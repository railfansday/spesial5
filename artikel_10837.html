New Warzone mode lets you buy Nuke for $1,000,000 to end the game

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Warzone is making it easier than ever to get your hands on a Nuke, with a new Season 1 mode allowing players to purchase one for $1 million in-game.

Nukes are nothing new to Warzone, having featured in previous versions of the battle royale as a reward for completing the Champion’s Quest.

However, Season 1 introduces a much more straightforward way of obtaining one, allowing players to save up enough Cash and buy it directly from a Buy Station.

The feature is part of Rally Point, a brand-new mode arriving with Modern Warfare 4’s integration, which acts as a reintroduction to Warzone. Unlike traditional Battle Royale, Rally Point takes place on the new Zodiac map, supports up to 20 players, and doesn’t feature a shrinking gas circle.

It also offers unlimited respawns and a drop-in, drop-out system, meaning players can continue fighting and completing objectives without worrying about being permanently eliminated.

But those looking to bring the match to an end can work together to purchase a Nuke for $1 million from a Buy Station. Once bought, a countdown is triggered before a cutscene plays showing you and your squad evacuating as the map is destroyed.

FIRST IN THE WORLD TO DROP A NUKE ON ZODIAC! pic.twitter.com/bmAN5E0GYy

As mentioned in the Season 1 patch notes, the Killstreak is sold on a first-come, first-served basis, meaning only the quickest squad to raise enough money can secure it.

As always, players can earn Cash by completing contracts, eliminating enemies, and taking part in activities around the map.

Rally Point also features designated safe areas where players can change loadouts, receive Cash payouts, or team up with others. The Nuke provides a way to end the match, rather than continuing to fight indefinitely.
