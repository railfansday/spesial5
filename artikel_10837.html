Just Eat reveals limited-edition controller with order food button for instant deliveries

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Just Eat has unveiled a limited-edition gaming controller featuring a dedicated button that allows players to order food without putting down their controller.

The custom-built device, called the Stay In The Game controller, has been designed to keep gamers playing during lengthy sessions by connecting directly to their Just Eat accounts.

The announcement comes on November 19, 2026, as one of the decade’s most anticipated video games launches, with Just Eat also partnering with two UK streamers to give away the controller to fans.

The Stay In The Game controller features a dedicated button called the ‘ChEAT Button,’ which is built directly into the hardware.

Once synced to a player’s Just Eat account, pressing the button triggers a food delivery order, allowing users to keep playing while their meal is delivered to their door.

The company has partnered with UK gaming streamers Angry Ginge and Danny Aarons to give fans a chance to win the limited-edition controller.

Both creators will showcase the device during Twitch livestreams, with giveaways taking place through their social media channels.

Three months of Just Eat credit worth £300 will also be included with the controller, providing winners with £100 per month until the end of January.

Fans interested in winning one are encouraged to follow the streamers’ Instagram and TikTok accounts for further details about the giveaways.

Tina Koehler, UK&I Marketing Director at Just Eat, said: “When you’re deep into a high-stakes gaming session, nothing kills the moment faster than having to pause the action just to sort out food. With the Just Eat ChEAT Button, Just Eat has unlocked the ultimate hack to evade the kitchen, enabling gamers to stay just where they are and locked into play.”

Alongside the controller giveaway, Just Eat is offering free delivery from selected restaurants and retailers in the UK between 6 PM on November 19 and 5 AM on November 20.

Participating chains include KFC, McDonald’s, Pepe’s, and Popeyes, while supermarkets Sainsbury’s and Co-op are also included.

The promotion is subject to availability, geographical restrictions, and minimum spending requirements, with service charges and other fees potentially applying.

Just Eat’s controller arrives just one day after McDonald’s announced a new Discord collaboration that allows gamers to order McDelivery through an activity within the platform.

The McDonald’s integration directs users to Uber Eats to complete their orders, while Just Eat’s controller is designed to trigger food delivery with a single button press. Both promotions coincide with the launch of GTA 6.
