Seth Meyers reacts to Elon Musks Im not MAGA claim

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Elon Musk, a man who described himself at a MAGA rally wearing a MAGA hat as "not just MAGA, I'm dark, gothic MAGA" this week told the New York Times, "I'm not MAGA."

“Well, at least, I'm not, like, I'm not sure what MAGA means,” Musk told NYT columnist Andrew Ross Sorkin on Wednesday at the publisher's DealBook Summit event. "If that means sort of blind adherence to a set of policies by the Republican Party, then I certainly don’t have that."

On Late Night, Seth Meyers reacted to the tech billionaire's sudden "I'm not MAGA" U-turn with a list of facts:

"You spent nearly half a billion on MAGA candidates. You spoke at the inauguration. You campaigned with Trump, and on top of all that, you were at the White House so often you were basically their Kramer. You even sat in the driveway with Trump while he marveled at all the screens in your stupid car," recapped Meyers, all of it accurate.
