Tom Wagner must plot Birmingham City transfer for Brentford’s ‘top talent’ who Jurgen Klopp loved

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Birmingham City's ambitions of reaching the Premier League could very much come true in this Championship season, with Chris Davies' side making an extremely impressive start to the campaign.

For the first time in 18 years, Blues currently top the Championship on goal difference after taking 29 points from their first 15 games whilst also boasting the division's meanest defensive record.

Such exploits should also take nothing away from the fact Davies' men have looked streets ahead this season compared to last in terms of their attacking efficiency, which, in large parts, is down to a tactical rethink which was brought about during a five-game unbeaten streak which closed out 2025/26.

Davies revealed in pre-season that his side needed to score at least 70 times in 46 games to stand a chance of being deemed promotion contenders, and they have already found target 28 times, an average of 1.9 per game.

With the January transfer window looming, it has already been debated what activity will be conducted at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park, but one particular deal could unlock the keys to promotion.

It was recently explained by newly-appointed director of football, John Murtough, that should an opportunity to add quality to the current Blues squad arise, those in B9 would be "ready to react."

Although the aforementioned figures showcase that goals have been flying in from all angles for Davies' side this season, more firepower could be needed to ensure they keep up their momentum, as well as the added bonus of depth.

Birmingham are in the mix to win promotion this season and they should target an ambitious deal for Blackburn's Andri Gudjohnsen in January.

Ambition is also a buzzword often associated with the Midlands side right now, and whilst not every high-profile transfer made under Knighthead's ownership has paid dividends, a swoop for Brentford playmaker, Fabio Carvalho, could benefit all parties in January.

The Portuguese was signed from Liverpool two summers ago in a deal worth £27.5m on a five-year contract, but due to fitness issues, the 24-year-old is yet to fully make his mark at the Gtech Community Stadium despite a tally of eight goals in 35 appearances.

Carvalho's career still has many years to go, and few have lost sight of the technical qualities the former England and Portuguese youth international could bring to any side, particularly at Championship level or even beyond.

In 2021/22, the attacker enjoyed a stellar breakthrough season with Fulham, with his 10 goals and eight assists in just 36 second tier outings enough to earn a nomination for the division's Young Player of the Season award, which was eventually won by Brennan Johnson.

Such form alerted the attention of Liverpool, who acquired his services for a bargain £7.5m, with Jürgen Klopp quick to offer rave reviews.

"What a talent he is, hey? A player who can bring a stadium to its feet," said the current German national team boss.

"He puts his personality into his performances, he is confident and adventurous with the ball, and he wants to make things happen. He is a versatile player for sure and is very much an attacking threat with his creativity and dynamism.

Carvalho later reminded everyone about his qualities at Championship level, scoring nine goals in just 20 appearances for Hull City in 2023/24 before signing for the Bees.

Having been held back in his progress through a serious ACL injury and further issues, regular minutes are a must for the playmaker, and Birmingham's current style would certainly get the best out of the playmaker.

When at his very best, Carvalho has struck fear into opposition defenders with his unpredictability and direct nature - a theme which currently runs through Birmingham's attack, showcased by the recent form of Liam Millar and Patrick Roberts on the flanks, as well as a vast improvement to Jay Stansfield's all-round game.

Whilst adjusting well to the 'number 10' role, Stansfield has since netted three times in his past two games when returning to the focal attacking role due to an injury sustained by August Priske, which will give Davies plenty to think about.

Carvalho is still under contract with Brentford until 2029, but as recently as January, it was claimed that a potential permanent exit was being discussed.

Whether such feelings are being debated by Keith Andrews at present remains to be seen, but his addition to Birmingham's frontline, if he avoids further issues, would be a significant statement of intent, although an alternative option could be a second swoop for Anis Mehmeti of Ipswich Town, who snubbed a Championship return in the summer window.
