Kenny McLean teases Teemu Pukki return at Norwich City

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Norwich City captain Kenny McLean has "no doubt" that Teemu Pukki will be back at Carrow Road soon enough, after the ex-Canaries frontman hung up his boots earlier this month.

Pukki is highly regarded as a modern-day Norwich legend, netting 88 times in 210 appearances for the Canaries over five years at the club, which included 29-goal and 26-goal campaigns in their Championship title-winning terms under Daniel Farke in 2018/19 and 2020/21.

The all-time top scorer for the Finnish national team left Norwich back in 2023, and after a year and a half in the MLS with Minnesota, re-joined HJK Helsinki to see out the rest of his career.

He bowed out with 11 goals and 13 assists in 42 games in all competitions in the Finnish capital, but couldn't end with a title, after a 3-3 draw on the final day of the season against AC Oulu handed the trophy to Inter Turku.

Despite not ending with silverware, Pukki bows out as a legend for numerous different clubs, but none more so than at Norwich.

He created plenty of memories at Carrow Road, and current Canaries captain Kenny McLean, who joined Norwich at the same time as Pukki did back in the summer of 2018 and is now in his ninth season in Norfolk, shared them with him.

Evidently, there's a mutual respect and friendship between the two, and the 34-year-old hailed an incredible career after the Finnish marksman's retirement was confirmed.

"An incredible career, and the status that he has at this football club is undeniable. He's undoubtedly a legend, and I think everybody would back me up in saying that," McLean said, per the Pink Un.

"I spoke to him last week after his final game. I think it's definitely time for him – that's where he seems to be. He's got three young children and his wife, Kiki, so they can go and spend some time together.

"He deserves it because he's had a hell of a career, some ups and some downs, and thankfully some success along the way here."

With Pukki finding his Saturdays free now, though, there's one question on Norwich fans' minds: when are we going to see him back at Carrow Road?

"He'll be over at some point, no doubt, but I think I'll let him have some time with the family first," McLean responded.

Those Daniel Farke-led Norwich squads, especially in the Championship, were a joy to watch, and Canaries fans loved them.

Evidently, the camaraderie amongst the group was high, too, and it needed to be, with the majority of them enduring the highest highs of second-tier title wins, but also the lowest lows during the subsequent relegation campaigns out of the Premier League.

Pukki even said in an interview back in June that he'd created quite a close bond with McLean and his compatriot Grant Hanley during his time at Norwich, with those two part of both title-winning teams.

It's not often that a well-liked squad can spend a few years together at one club, and those in the stands at Carrow Road still reminisce about those years, with those teams being the last ones to oversee success in the Championship.

Therefore, they'll be delighted to see them all remaining close long after they'd gone their separate ways.

But despite the good feeling that surrounds the entire squad from that era at Norwich, there's no one more well-liked amongst the Canaries fanbase than Pukki.

The Finnish frontman immediately endeared himself to the Norwich fanbase with a Golden Boot-winning campaign in his first season in England, and then followed that up with 11 goals in the Premier League, including a hat-trick against Newcastle United and strikes against Liverpool, Chelsea and Manchester City.

Given his excellence in the division below and the ability he showed in the top flight, there'd have been few who would have held it against Pukki for wanting to continue his career in the Premier League or elsewhere after relegation in 2020, but the striker stayed.

What followed was another emphatic scoring season and another 11-goal campaign in the top flight, with his loyalty to the club celebrated alongside all the goals he was scoring.

Norwich fans will be making sure they're inside Carrow Road when the Finnish legend makes his return to Norfolk, and they'll be ready to give him the praise he clearly deserves.
