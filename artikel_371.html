GTA 6: How to save your game

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Autosaving in games has spoiled us all. We've become complacent. We used to actively save games like our lives (or Banjo and Kazooie's lives) depended on it.

But saving in Grand Theft Auto VI is absolutely crucial if you're the type of person who regrets choices immediately or wants to follow different paths with myriad saves. You didn't want to kill that guy or trespass on that lawn? Everyone's mad at you, the police are in hot pursuit, but the last time the game saved was an hour ago? I feel that.

The question is: can you manually save in GTA 6? In short: if you're on a mission, no; if you're not, yes.

As in most games, you can manually save GTA 6 in the open world sections between missions. Just hit the pause button, open up the options menu, hit save, and pick a slot. Here, you can enter multiple saves in case you want to see how a storyline with a major choice pans out multiple ways.

But you must save before starting a mission if you're looking to explore a sliding doors moment. During missions in GTA 6, you can't save the game.

When Radio Times asked GTA 6 game system design director Ben Rollinson why they made it impossible to save during missions, he told the outlet, "It was on the cards for a while, but there was a concern with issues around maintaining and testing loading fresh into every checkpoint of every mission on top of all the other game states that we save. We ultimately decided to optimize how saves were supported to ensure the experience was clean throughout the full game."

If you want to change your actions during a mission, you'll have to do the whole thing again by loading a previous save or by selecting to restart the mission or last checkpoint. So, make sure you're manually saving GTA 6 as you go, or at least right before missions.
