Here's How Much the Average Social Security Benefit Will Increase in 2027 After the 3.6% COLA

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

It may not be as large a boost as you'd expect.

By Maurie Backman – Nov 21, 2026 at 3:08AM EST

Many seniors on Social Security were happy to learn that benefits are getting a 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment, or COLA, in 2027. That 3.6% COLA is the largest to arrive in years and far surpasses this year's 2.8% increase. And it could help many Social Security recipients more easily keep up with their costs.

The average Social Security benefit as of late 2026 is $2,095, according to the Social Security Administration. After applying a 3.6% COLA, the average monthly benefit should rise to $2,170, marking a $75 increase.

But that increase doesn't account for the cost of Medicare Part B. If you're enrolled in both Social Security and Medicare, your COLA may put a bit less money than expected in your pocket come January.

While most Medicare enrollees do not pay a premium for Part A, which covers hospital care, there's a monthly premium for Part B, which covers diagnostics and outpatient services. This year, the standard monthly Part B premium is $202.90, though higher earners who face surcharges known as income-related monthly adjustment amounts can pay more.

In 2027, the standard Medicare Part B premium is rising to $209.40, an increase of $6.50. As such, the average Social Security benefit for someone not enrolled in Medicare will rise by about $75 once the 3.6% COLA is applied. For someone who collects an average benefit and is also covered by Medicare, the typical increase will be $68.50.

The good news is that the cost of Medicare Part B did not rise as sharply for 2027 as it did for 2026. So seniors who collect an average benefit should still get to keep the majority of their COLA.

While next year's Part B premium increase may be modest, do keep in mind that several Medicare costs are going up. Part B's annual deductible, for example, will be $292 in 2027, a $9 increase from 2026.

Hospital care covered by Medicare Part A will also be more expensive in the new year. The inpatient hospital deductible is rising from $1,736 in 2026 to $1,776 in 2027. The daily coinsurance rate is going up, too.

If you're looking forward to your 3.6% Social Security COLA, do keep in mind that various Medicare costs might chip away at that raise. Coming up with a budget now could help you manage your money better and avoid financial stress.
