Galiano Gold (GAU) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Mill throughput climbed and costs eased as the Asanko mine's hedge book nears settlement.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Galiano Gold Inc. (GAU +17.93%) reported increased production and strengthened operating cash flow while advancing its long-term life of mine plan at the Asanko Gold Mine. Management stated that the company is on track to meet annual production and cost targets following improved mill performance and higher ore grades from the Abore deposit. The company indicated that the impending completion of its gold hedge program by the end of 2026 will allow for full participation in prevailing gold prices starting in 2027. Strategic focus remains on the development of the Nkran Cut 3 pit and exploration programs aimed at converting mineral resources to reserves.
