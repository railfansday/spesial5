AI-native CRM Dreamteam raises $53M to challenge incumbents

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Accel, Together Fund and Premji Invest back the AI-native CRM. Dreamteam learns from emails, meetings and customer conversations, and four AI teammates help move deals forward.

For over two decades, CRMs like Salesforce and HubSpot have depended on sales teams remembering to manually enter conversational data. Dreamteam is built around the opposite idea. In the era of AI, CRMs should extract signals from sales conversations automatically and actually do the work to move deals forward.

Dreamteam announced on Monday it has raised $53M from Accel, Together Fund, and Premji Invest. The money will scale its AI-native CRM. The CRM understands the full context behind a customer relationship, reasons over that information, and takes action through specialized AI teammates.

Legacy CRM architecture is built around structured fields and rigid hand-coded workflows. But the information AI needs to reason well is often semantic: whether a prospect sounds hesitant on price, a competitor was referenced on a call, or a board is suddenly scrutinizing costs.

Any trained salesperson can make sense of those signals immediately. A legacy CRM with only a few manually updated fields can’t provide the full picture. Layering more tools fragments the customer story across systems. Meanwhile, the unspoken context behind decisions often goes uncaptured.

Most CRMs on the market add to the workload of sales teams that are already short on time. AI can lift that load. It can handle operational tasks like account research, meeting prep and email drafting. It can also handle strategic work like sales forecasting and win-loss analysis.

“The CRM was supposed to give companies a complete picture of their customers, yet the richest parts of that picture have always lived outside the CRM,” said Anand Venkatraman, Co-founder and CEO of Dreamteam. “AI finally gives us a way to change that foundation. Dreamteam learns from the work your team is already doing, keeps the context alive and gives every salesperson skilled AI teammates that can reason over it and move work forward. Your sellers and leaders can spend their time on relationships, judgement and closing deals, while Dreamteam takes care of everything required around them.”

At the heart of Dreamteam is the Company Brain. It is a living memory of a business built from customer conversations, emails, meetings, and workflows. It connects the dots across every interaction. It captures buyer intent, objections, competitor mentions, stakeholder dynamics, and more.

Four specialized AI coworkers turn intelligence into action:

Each AI co-worker is skilled in a specific job. It is ready to start work without teams having to build and train their own agents. Upstream, sales leaders stay in control. They get immediate answers to questions legacy CRMs are blind to, like which deals are stalling and why.

A select group of fast-growing, AI-forward GTM teams co-created Dreamteam in close partnership and helped shape the product. This collaborative approach ensured Dreamteam is grounded in real-world sales workflows from day one. It also factored in the evolution of sales processes for the AI era.

Today, hundreds of companies across diverse industries use Dreamteam, including AI, online media, healthcare, manufacturing, and consulting.

“I truly believe this is so far ahead of everything any other CRM is doing, that it would revolutionise our sales,” says Patrick Doyle, CEO of DMS Health.

At invideo, Head of Enterprise GTM Dhruv Dewan started off with a small group to pilot. He expanded quickly to the entire sales team. Dreamteam reviews customer interactions each morning and surfaces insights, which were never possible before.

For Plex, Inc., Co-Founder and CPO Scott Olechowski says Dreamteam’s combination of simplicity and powerful AI capabilities “hits the sweet spot,” calling its ability to work with existing AI tools like Claude “a game changer.”

Anand Venkatraman, Ramesh Parthasarathy, and Senthil Kanthaswamy founded Dreamteam. They are former senior executives at Freshworks who helped scale the company from its early startup days through its IPO.

The founders have over 25 years of experience each building and selling B2B software. They saw firsthand how traditional CRMs lose valuable customer context. That leaves teams with stale pipelines and unreliable forecasts.

The founders have built AI applications since the GPT-2 era. They scaled them to tens of thousands of businesses worldwide.

They recognized a shift when AI reasoning made it possible for software to understand context and execute work. They saw an opportunity to reimagine CRM from the ground up, rather than simply layering AI onto existing systems.

“In the AI era, the companies that understand their customers deeply have an enormous advantage. Today, everyone on a team sees a different version of the customer, because no central system holds all the information,” said Shekhar Kirani, Partner at Accel. “Dreamteam is building that system with AI agents. It creates a shared, important, codified record of every conversation, so the whole team can serve customers better. We’re delighted to partner with Anand, Senthil and Ramesh to redefine the $100B+ CRM market.”

Looking forward, the team’s vision extends well beyond sales. The company is building a shared intelligence layer across the entire customer lifecycle, from customer acquisition to upsells.

To complement this, Dreamteam offers Magic Apps, a capability that brings the power of vibe coding directly into the CRM. Using natural language, business users can create purpose-built apps and custom dashboards tailored to their business, without writing code.

“Businesses shouldn’t have to choose between the limitations of off-the-shelf software and the complexity of building their own,” said Anand. “The future is software that understands your business, adapts to how you work and gets smarter every day. We believe that the future will redefine business software, starting with CRM.”

“Twenty-five years ago, the cloud wave created a generation of iconic enterprise software companies,” added Girish Mathrubootham, Founding partner at Together Fund, the first institutional investor in Dreamteam. “AI is an equally massive opportunity, but it demands software rebuilt from the ground up, not AI layered onto legacy systems. CRM is among the biggest software categories ripe for disruption. I’ve worked alongside Dreamteam’s founders since day one as their vision for an AI-native CRM took shape. Their deep CRM expertise and proven record of scaling enterprise software make them uniquely positioned to lead this reinvention.”

“CRM systems must fundamentally evolve from systems of record into systems of intelligence and action, removing legacy pain points to keep pace with the modern enterprise. Dreamteam sets out to intuitively understand user intent, build deep contextual intelligence and execute workflows seamlessly through autonomous AI teammates,” said Saravanan Nattanmai, Partner, Early Stage Investments and Financial Services at Premji Invest. “We believe the future of enterprise software lies in AI-native systems that don’t just augment existing workflows but fundamentally reimagine how work gets done, freeing people to focus on what matters most. We are proud to partner with a founding team whose proven track record spans enterprise software, AI innovation and CRM leadership to disrupt the category.”

Dreamteam is an AI-native CRM built for fast-growing B2B sales teams. It brings together complete customer context, reasons over it like an experienced sales leader, and gets work done through specialized AI teammates that help move deals forward.

Unlike traditional CRMs that rely on manual data entry, Dreamteam self-updates. It continuously learns from the teams’ emails, calendar, meetings and connected tools to keep a shared memory called the ‘Company Brain’.

Four specialized AI teammates put this intelligence to work. Rachel researches accounts, Sally drafts follow-ups and recommends deal updates, Raj identifies pipeline risks, and Alex simplifies CRM setup and administration through natural language. Sales teams stay in control, reviewing and approving actions proposed by their AI teammates.

Dreamteam is built from the ground up for the agentic era. It combines a familiar CRM experience for humans with a first-class API and Model Context Protocol (MCP) server for AI agents.

Its conversational approach to setup and administration is designed to dramatically simplify implementation. It reduces the need for specialists to implement the software and lengthy deployments.

Former Freshworks executives Anand Venkatraman, Senthil Kanthaswamy, and Ramesh Parthasarathy founded Dreamteam in 2025. It is headquartered in Sacramento, California. For more information, visit dreamteam.ai.
