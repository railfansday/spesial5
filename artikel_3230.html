Phathom: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Back-to-back quarters of operating profit show the company is converting revenue growth into cash, a milestone that matters for debt covenants and financial sustainability.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 18, 2026 at 3:45PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Phathom Pharmaceuticals ›

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 29, 2026, Phathom (PHAT -3.64%) announced net revenues of $84.7 million for the quarter. The biopharmaceutical company is turning revenue growth into cash flow while expanding coverage for VOQUEZNA, its treatment for gastroesophageal reflux disease, or GERD.

Management shares three takeaways on how the business is executing.

Phathom shifted its sales focus to educate physicians on prior authorization, which is health plan approval before coverage, and driving prescriptions through its partner pharmacy, BlinkRx. To boost fulfillment rates, it introduced a $0 copay offering through BlinkRx for eligible commercially insured patients.

Operating expenses rose slightly due to clinical trials and sales investments, but quarterly net revenue grew 71% year over year. That top-line growth allowed the company to deliver positive operating profit and generate positive cash flow for two consecutive quarters.

Generic drug manufacturers filed litigation challenging the Food and Drug Administration's decision that extended VOQUEZNA's new chemical entity exclusivity, or statutory market protection against generic competition, through May 3, 2032. Phathom intervened in the lawsuit to protect its intellectual property.

Commercial momentum accelerates across target prescribers as Phathom reiterates full-year 2026 revenue guidance of $310 million to $325 million. The company relies on established gastroenterology relationships and broad commercial coverage while maintaining pricing discipline.

Revenue growth is expected to outpace operating expenses, strengthening cash flow to satisfy debt covenants without requiring additional capital raises.
