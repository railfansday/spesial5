Fabrice Fiorèse, what happens to the ex-footballer who had him kidnapped?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

In the Riolo ClubFabrice Fiorèse returned to the kidnapping of which he was the victim in 2012. A kidnapping whose instigator was none other than one of his former teammates, Ghislain Anselmini.

Indeed, the two men had worn the colors of Guingamp at the same time, between 1998 and the year 2000. IN 2016, the former defender was sentenced to five years in prison for criminal conspiracy by the Savoie Assize Court.

Including his pre-trial detention and the period following his conviction, Ghislain Anselmini spent approximately two years behind bars. Quickly, he was able to find activity in the world of football. First in the Rhône District, by intervening in training and technical supervision.

Coach of the national U17s of AS Saint-Priest between 2017 and 2019, this native of Coteau then became assistant to Laurent Roussey at Lyon-Duchère, before taking charge of CS Neuville-sur-Saône. After leaving the latter two clubs, he then briefly returned.

More recently, Ghislain Anselmini joined the Sporting Club de Toulon, first as assistant coach in 2024-2025, then as head coach from February 2026. In December 2019, in an interview given to Parisianhe assured that prison had deeply affected him and that he would carry this experience with him all his life.
