Gift Guide: The Absolute Best Gifts for Car Lovers

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Our car experts choose every product we feature. We may earn money from the links on this page.

Whether it’s the holidays, a birthday, or any occasion, we’ve got you covered with these editor-picked gift ideas for auto enthusiasts.

This article was updated with new products and information in November 2026.

If you’re here, chances are you or someone on your gift list is a self-proclaimed car lover. Simple, right? No, not so much. Car lovers consist of different personalities and interests, and finding the right gift for your favorite one can be tricky. To complicate things further, each car enthusiast has their own favorite ride or style, making it challenging to find a one-size-fits-all gift.

We scoured the interweb (and our own collections) for the best car-adjacent goodies and discovered some fantastic gifts sure to bring a smile to any auto enthusiast’s face. Whether you’re shopping for a birthday, graduation, or the holiday season, here are the best gifts for car lovers, as selected by the editors of Car and Driver.

The Model T needs no introduction, as it is perhaps the most influential vehicle of all time. Your favorite car lover will enjoy building this Lego-fied Tin Lizzie, complete with white rubber tires, a gold grille, and a fabric folding roof. You can even turn the crank to rotate the fan (no worries about arm-breaking kickback here!). At just over 1000 pieces, it hits that engaging sweet spot between too simple and overly complicated.

Or, if you’re looking for a Lego alternative, Mattel has sprung onto the scene with their Brick Shop builds, featuring an impressive catalogue of vehicular options. We tried out this RS2 kit, the Audi Quattro, and the 1990 Acura NSX, and were blown away by the quality. These aren’t knock-off buildable cars; they’re just as good, and their price points make them especially alluring.

Better yet, these building sets come with their own die-cast version of the car you just built. For a car lover, these are affordable yet impressive gifts for earning some nostalgic brownie points.

Forza Horizon 6 was the most popular racing game of the year thanks to its stunning setting in Japan, where you can explore with a wide array of vehicles. Xbox players have been enjoying it since launch, and the PlayStation version will be released at the end of January 2027. We’ve been thoroughly loving our playthrough—we think your giftee will too.

A glossy, 400-plus-page tour of 34 screen legends on two and four wheels. From Bond’s DB5 and Bullitt’s GT 390 to Thelma & Louise’s ’66 Thunderbird and the Dark Knight’s Tumbler, each chapter pairs knockout photography and illustrations with behind-the-scenes stories. Edited by Chad Oppenheim, Chris Nashawaty, and Matt Stone—with a foreword by Jay Leno and fresh voices, including Stephen King, Michael Bay, and Jeff Gordon—this is the coffee-table pit pass for anyone who judges movies by their chase scenes.

This is the dash cam for drivers who hate the look of dash cams. Roughly the size of a key fob, the Mini 3 hides neatly behind a rearview mirror and runs a “set it and forget it” loop without cluttering your view.

The big upgrade for this generation is a built-in polarizing filter, which significantly cuts down on the dashboard glare that often washes out footage in bright sunlight. Because it lacks a screen, you’ll need to rely on the Garmin Drive app to review clips or adjust settings, but the voice controls are responsive enough to save video of an incident without taking your hands off the wheel.

This is more than just a NASCAR driving experience—it’s your favorite car lover’s chance to drive a real NASCAR race car on the same track where legends like Dale Earnhardt and Jeff Gordon carved their names in history. They’ll feel the roar of 600 horsepower as they blaze down the 3800-foot straightaway, tackling the renowned 31-degree banked turns, and reaching top speeds of up to 160 mph. Whether you give them a quick eight-minute session or go for the full 48-minute marathon, this is a driving experience they’ll never forget.

If your favorite car lover is interested in new ways to avoid speeding tickets, consider gifting this top-of-the-line radar detector from Uniden. It was our top pick in our most recent radar detector test, boasting excellent results across the board for sniffing out Smokey.

For a more affordable option, try the Uniden R4w. It’s not as feature laden as its bigger brother, but the price is sure tough to beat.

We love vintage automotive shirts, and this one from Popfunk celebrates the iconic Civic in all its throwback glory. Featuring a cool blue background with a classic Civic font and the car star of the show, it's a must-have for any JDM fan.

The Marlin is Timex’s midcentury legend, and now it comes in a racing-inspired design. Racing stripes line the dial of this chronograph tachymeter, which features a 24-hour subdial decorated with checkered flags. Driven by an ultraprecise quartz movement, it includes a chronograph for tracking elapsed time and a tachymeter index along its outer minute track for measuring speed.

It retains classic Marlin features with a sturdy 40mm stainless steel case, domed acrylic crystal, retro-style applied indices, and a leather strap. We got our hands on the blue variant and vainly enjoy the countless compliments we receive on this watch.

A jump starter and a battery charger? Count us in. This handy device from industry stalwart Battery Tender is one of the handiest devices we’ve ever used—particularly on vehicles that are typically stored over the winter months, such as classic cars, motorcycles, and UTVs. This charger boasts up to 1200 amps of jump-starting power and 4 amps to help maintain your battery.

Any driveway mechanic will tell you that hand tools have a way of sprouting legs and making a run for it and that they’re usually successful in getting away. Refresh your favorite car tinkerer’s kit with this DeWALT Mechanic’s Tool Set. It comes with 172 pieces in a hard-shell blow-molded case with 72 tooth ratchets.

From the Lego Technic Ultimate Car Concept series: Your favorite car lover will be able to recreate the features of the Ferrari Daytona SP3 in classic red with chrome-painted rims. Features include steering, an eight-speed sequential gearbox with paddle shifter, a V-12 engine with moving pistons, and opening butterfly doors.

Created especially for adults, the completed 1:8 scale model will make a fantastic display piece for any auto enthusiast’s office or workspace.

Ranger Station creates some excellent scents, and we’ve thankfully gotten our hands on a few of their car air fresheners. With collaborations including Noah Kahan and Willie Nelson, these unique aromas are perfect for making your ride smell great.

This monstrous book, published by Taschen, is the ultimate collection of Ferrari lore and exploits, beautifully bound in Ferrari red. Chock full of gorgeous, full-page photos (including many previously unseen), documents, and sketches, this book connects the aura of Ferrari—perfect for the Ferrari fan in your life.

Most of us can say the first car we ever bought was a Hot Wheels, and this limited-print deluxe hardcover goes under the hood of the world’s number one toy brand, tracing its history and revealing the design process from first sketch to prototype to die-cast.

Legendary designer Larry Wood pens the foreword, and designers past and present talk shop about iconic builds such as the life-size Darth Vader Hot Wheels. There are incredible photos, drawings, and—make room in your garage—an exclusive special-edition Custom Camaro die-cast, honoring one of the Original 16 Hot Wheels cars. Display-worthy box included.

Corny? Depends on your POV, but at this price point, you can rationalize these interior LED lights as a gag gift. Thanks to a built-in mic, the colors and light speed adjust automatically to any music type. The app lets you access 16 million colors, a host of dynamic light effects, and an easy-to-use DIY mode. The strip lights boast a two-line design, which is ideal for the cockpit of any car, truck, or SUV.

Yep, it’s silly all right. But funnier than a Crown Royal bag. Plenty of colors are available.

Head-up displays are all the rage these days, and rightfully so. By displaying information closer to where your eyes are while driving, checking your speed becomes less of a distraction. But what if you want to see more information on your vehicle? This heads-up display shows coolant temperature, engine rpm, and more. It simply plugs into your OBD-II port and mounts on your dashboard.

We love BougeRV electric car coolers! These handy devices are real game-changers on road trips, car camping vacations, or even during your daily commute. They come in a variety of sizes for any traveler, and most are available in black or white. You can also check out the new Rocky line of overlanding-specific truck and Jeep coolers.

Want to see how many calories you can burn carving up mountain roads? Yes, that’s a thing—and the Polar Loop health band can tell you. The screenless design is ideal for driving (no distractions), and the live heart rate and sleep scoring provide great data to see how spirited driving benefits your health—something we’re very supportive of.

The best gift depends on how they enjoy cars. A model-building kit or automotive book suits a collector; a dash cam, jump starter, or battery maintainer is useful for a hands-on driver; and a racing game or track experience can make a memorable gift. This guide includes ideas across all of those interests.

A portable jump starter is a useful pick for someone who likes to be prepared for a dead battery. A dash cam can help record the road, while a battery maintainer is handy for vehicles that sit unused for long stretches, including classic cars and motorcycles.

Look for something they can enjoy outside the garage, such as a NASCAR driving experience, a beautifully illustrated book about car design, or a display-worthy model kit. A gift tied to a favorite marque, era, or racing series can make it feel more personal.

A small Lego set, a racing game, or interior LED lights can be fun gifts at a lower price point. Prices vary by retailer and can change, so check current listings before you buy.
