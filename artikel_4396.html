10 PS4 Adventure Games Where You’re Actually the Villain

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Eric Warner is a Journalist and Multimedia Producer based in New England with over 7 years of experience producing stories for multiple publications

Of all the PlayStation consoles, the PlayStation 2 is widely considered to be the most successful as it still holds the title of being the best-selling game console of all time, with the Nintendo Switch coming close behind as the second best-selling game console.

While the PS2 would see a wide variety of great video games debut on the platform, I would argue that the PlayStation 4 featured a greater variety of titles that, more often than not, featured an overall superior quality compared to their PS2 predecessors.

In one respect, this can be seen in the increased freedom PS4 games offered players, with several adventure and open-world titles granting them the ability to embody villainous, often chaotic and destructive, roles. Watch out for major spoilers for the following titles, if you haven't played these PS4 games!

Traveller's Tales’ LEGO video games are famous for adapting some of cinema’s most famous series in comical LEGO form, but LEGO DC Super-Villains broke that mold by featuring a mostly original story loosely inspired by DC Comics’ Forever Evil event.

Here, people got to create their own super-powered character simply known as The Rookie, as they join Lex Luthor and the Legion of Doom to take down the Crime Syndicate, evil alternate versions of the Justice League, before they can find the Anti-Life Equation.

While the Legion of Doom is ultimately successful in defeating the Crime Syndicate and The Rookie is offered an option to join the Justice League, players can reject the League’s offer and permanently join the Legion to take part in their future criminal activities.

The PS4 era was a bountiful time for new PlayStation hits like Horizon Zero Dawn and Ghost of Tsushima to appear, but it was also a somewhat somber time that saw beloved franchises end. This included the superhero-like Infamous series, which concluded with Infamous Second Son.

Infamous Second Son follows the new protagonist Delsin Rowe as he inadvertently acquires Conduit powers, setting out to save his Akomish tribe and the greater Seattle population from the corrupt Department of Unified Protection.

However, Delsin’s time fighting the DUP can wind up corrupting him, as players can commit several evil Karma actions like corrupting and executing people. This can result in Delsin taking all of the Conduits’ powers, taking control of Seattle, and even destroying his own reservation by the end of the game.

Fans of the original Xbox who want play adventure titles that feature villains as protagonists should check out these 10 games

Arkane Studios’ Dishonored was famous for the sheer number of ways people can play through the game and change the city Dunwall through their actions. Arkane made sure to expand upon this level of player freedom in 2016’s Dishonored 2.

Like Dishonored , players in Dishonored 2 can complete the game without being spotted once or even without killing anyone, but they can also sow high chaos by killing practically everyone they see in the Empire of the Isles.

If players go down this high chaos route as Corvo, the former bodyguard will take the Empire’s throne for himself and become the brutal Emperor Corvo the Black, while a high chaos ending as Empress Emily Kaldwin will end with her ushering a new imperialistic rule and becoming known as Empress Emily the Butcher.

For anyone who played Assassin’s Creed Valhalla, they already knew the endgame of Assassin’s Creed Mirage, but they likely didn’t expect just how devastating the past of the late ninth century Hidden One Basim Ibn Ishaq would be.

Set roughly a decade before Valhalla in Baghdad, Assassin’s Creed Mirage follows the young orphan and thief, Basim, tormented by nightmarish visions of a jinni as he sets out to become a Hidden One after his friend Nehal accidentally killed the Caliph al-Mutawakkil.

While Basim is able to become a Hidden One and assassinates many Baghdad members of the Order of the Ancients, it’s eventually revealed that the jinni and Nehal were the consciousness of the ancient Isu known as Loki, who deceived Basim into killing the Caliph and eventually into giving up control of his own body.

Most of the protagonists of Ubisoft’s Far Cry series are chaotic anti-heroes, not true saviors, but one lead from the series could be considered as a villain due to their actions that lead to the deaths of thousands. That would be the Deputy from Far Cry 5.

Taking place in the western U.S. state of Montana, Far Cry 5 sees the unnamed Deputy travel to Hope County as they attempt to apprehend the cult leader Joseph Seed, and stop his militant followers from taking over the county based on a belief that Seed would save humanity.

However, if players choose to fight the cultists and ultimately arrest Seed, it’ll turn out that Seed was actually right all along as nuclear bombs begin to detonate all over the country, making the deputy responsible for numerous deaths whose lives could have continued on into the apocalypse seen in Far Cry New Dawn.

If you like games that don't force you to be the good guy just because, try these ten FPS games where you're the anti-hero.

The Lord of the Rings universe is inching closer and closer to its 90th anniversary, and over those 90 years, there have been countless adaptations and expansions upon J. R. R. Tolkien fantasy world. One of the worst expansions of his work was undoubtedly The Lord of the Rings: Gollum.

A LOTR game starring only Gollum, The Lord of the Rings: Gollum follows the Stoor Hobbit as he attempts to escape Mordor and searches for the Shire during the events of The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring.

While Gollum does make a few friends during his imprisonment, he doesn’t go out of his way to save them from Orcs and other creatures like the giant spider Shelob, and still vows to eventually make his way to Shire so he can kill Bilbo Baggins and take back the One Ring.

The eighth console generation was an era full of remasters and remakes, with one of the PS4’s best exclusive adventure game remakes being Bluepoint Games’ Shadow of the Colossus.

A complete modern remake of Team Ico’s PS2 masterpiece of the same name, 2018’s Shadow of the Colossus follows the same plot as the original game, with players embodying the young Wander as he sets out to slay 16 massive colossi in an effort to revive a fallen damsel.

As Wander slays each colossi, however, his body begins to slowly deteriorate, up until it’s eventually revealed that the colossi were actual peaceful giants who were keeping the evil entity Dormin at bay. The final colossi’s death frees Dormin to possess Wander’s body.

Unlike The Lord of the Rings: Gollum, one of the better LOTR video games to debut on the PS4 was Middle-earth: Shadow of War, the sequel to Monolith Productions’ Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor.

Set primarily in between The Hobbit and Fellowship, Shadow of War follows the ranger Talion and the Elf spirit Celebrimbor once more, as they forge a new Ring of Power to take over the armies of Mordor, defend Gondor, and ultimately defeat Sauron.

However, all of Talion’s efforts in Shadow of War and Mordor turn out to be in vain, as Celebrimbor plans to abandon Talion and simply replace Sauron to become a new, imperialistic Bright Lord. Talion is forced to become a Nazgul and eventual servant to Sauron to stop Celebrimbor.

JRPGs have a reputation for turning a simple story into one that challenges your viewpoints, and the PS4 era had some of the best.

The PS4 era saw the God of War franchise be completely renewed with the phenomenon that was 2018’s Norse mythology-focused God of War, but before that game debuted, PS4 players were able witness Kratos’ most destructive acts in God of War III Remastered.

Taking place immediately after the events of God of War II, God of War III Remastered sees Kratos and the Titans launch an all-out war against the Greek Gods of Olympus, with Kratos personally slaying most of the Olympians and Titans such as Gaia, Cronos, and his father Zeus.

While Kratos is attempting to avenge his fallen family, Spartan worshippers, and Pandora, his quest for vengeance ultimately dooms most of the world’s population as Poseidon’s death floods the world, Hera’s death kills most plant life, and Hermes’ death unleashes a deadly plague.

One of the better PS4 adventure games that features villains as protagonists is Naughty Dog’s The Last of Us Part II as it features not one but two playable villains: Ellie and series newcomer Abby Anderson.

The Last of Us Part II is largely focused around the fallout of Joel Miller’s decision to stop the Fireflies from experimenting on Ellie in The Last of Us, to find a potential cure for the zombie-like Cordyceps infection, with Abby being the daughter of the doctor who was supposed to experiment on Ellie.

What ensues is a bloody adventure of revenge as Abby and her Fireflies friends murder Joel for killing Abby’s dad, causing Ellie to seek her own revenge by killing all of Abby’s friends, leaving both characters as traumatized, murderous shells of their former selves and villains in the eyes of many survivors.
