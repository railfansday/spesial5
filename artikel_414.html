Last-minute Pokémon TCG preorders for Delta Reigns ETB are over $20 off on Amazon

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Delta Reign Elite Trainer Box is on sale for $115.88 at Amazon, down from its $138.99 list price.

We're almost at the finish line for the Pokémon TCG's Delta Reign expansion, and while trading card collectors only have a couple of days left before its Nov. 6 debut, Amazon has a welcome last-minute saving on the set's Elite Trainer Box.

As of Nov. 4, the Pokémon TCG Delta Reign Elite Trainer Box can be preordered for $115.88 at Amazon, taking $23.11 off its regular $138.99 price. That works out to a 17% reduction, leaving you with a little extra money to put towards other Pokémon cards. Considering Amazon’s preorders first opened up at $140 back in early October, that’s a hefty $24.12 saving by comparison. This new $115.88 price is about $12.88 per pack before you consider all of the ETB’s extras.

Keep in mind that these lower prices are currently offered by third-party sellers like reVend and BetterShopping4You, but you can still get free delivery as of Nov. 12 (at the time of writing).

The Delta Reign ETB grants you nine booster packs from the upcoming Mega Rayquaza-centered Mega Evolution expansion, a full-art foil Kyogre promo, 65 card sleeves, 40 Energy cards, seven dice, a plastic coin, six dividers, a collector's box, a player's guide, and a Pokémon TCG Live code card.
