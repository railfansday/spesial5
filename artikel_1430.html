Juric recalls ‘negative’ Roma spell: “It was difficult for me.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Roma-Monza is approaching, a wave of Giallorossi is arriving at the Olimpico, and Ivan Juric is ready to face his past.

The Croatian coach introduced the match against the Giallorossi in a press conference, praising his mentor Gian Piero Gasperini and the team.

On Roma and Gasperini. “Roma are in fantastic form, and we saw it against PSG too. They’re going strong, and Gasperini is managing to implement all his ideas. They’re the main contenders for the Scudetto, along with Inter and Lazio.”

“Every team at the Olimpico is in serious danger; they score 4-5 goals per game. They’re going strong, and in the Champions League, they kept Malen and Dybala on the bench. They have a high-quality squad that can make the difference.”

On his experience at Roma. “It was a negative experience in Rome. It wasn’t easy emotionally, but those are the experiences that help you learn the most. Now I’m at Monza, and my only goal is to grow with this club and with the young players.”
