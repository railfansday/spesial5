How GTA 6 Is Eligible For The Game Awards This Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

We've known for some time that Grand Theft Auto 6 was likely to be eligible for The Game Awards, and now that 2026's TGA nominees are live, it's official: Not only is GTA eligible, but it actually has the most nominations of any game.

That's somewhat of an unusual situation, given that GTA 6 reviews are not yet live. Regardless, this invites some questions of how or why GTA 6 can be eligible before reviews go out and before the game is available. (Nominations even confirmed a key actor in the game.)

For months, the official website for The Game Awards has listed November 20, 2026 at 9 PM PT as the cutoff date for this year's ceremony, which means that any game that launches before that time can be considered for nomination. As GTA VI is scheduled to launch one day earlier, on November 19, GTA 6 is eligible just like any other game already released this year.

Now, before torches are lit and pitchforks are sharpened, evidence suggests that date was not put in place specifically for GTA to be eligible this year: The Friday before Thanksgiving has routinely been the cutoff for TGA nominations.

Last year, the cutoff was November 21, as referenced in this Polygon article regarding Dispatch's eligibility. In 2024, eligibility was cut off on November 22, as evidenced in this Polygon article exploring the games that missed 2024 and were eligible in 2025.

2018 was the last time a Rockstar project was eligible for most categories at The Game Awards, as Red Dead Redemption II landed 10 nominations and four wins. Notably, one of those wins was not Game of the year, which went to Sony Santa Monica for the God of War reboot.

Grand Theft Auto VI's eligibility for TGA 2026 was never in doubt--well, unless it got delayed again. Now that we're clear of that scenario, it's only a matter of time until we see just how many trophies are headed to Vice City.
