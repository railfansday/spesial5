3 Best New HBO Max Series To Binge-Watch This Weekend (November 6–8, 2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

From midlife reinvention to high-stakes courtroom battles and old-money rivalries, these are the 3 best new HBO Max series to binge-watch this weekend. As far as new releases go on HBO Max right now, there is a limited selection of top-rated series that are actually worth your time this weekend, whether you're looking for something that will make you laugh, boost your adrenaline, or transport you back in time to a bygone era in American history. Watching any of these three series will kick off the first weekend of November on a high note.

If you're looking for more streaming options on other major platforms this weekend, check out ScreenRant's updated binge-watching guides for Netflix, Prime Video, and Paramount+, featuring the latest and greatest series available to dive into as soon as this evening. With so many great new releases this Fall 2026, ScreenRant helps you take the hassle out of streaming by recommending the highest quality shows that we'll be watching all weekend long. Cue up your watchlist and get ready to discover HBO's best new shows to binge.

Youth is one of HBO Max's most critically acclaimed new series of 2026, earning a near-perfect Rotten Tomatoes score of 94% in its debut season. Blending real-world drama with lighthearted comedy, Youth follows a woman named Alex, a recently divorced literary agent who navigates the middle-aged dating scene while maintaining her familial duties to her parents and adult son. Sharon Horgan stars as Alex and created the series, using her real-life experiences as a basis for the plot and character development.

Now is the perfect time to start binge-watching Youth as its season 1 finale just landed on HBO Max on Sunday, November 1. Consisting of just eight episodes, each only 28 to 30 minutes long, Youth is extremely easy to watch in just one weekend and can be reasonably consumed in just one sitting, with a total runtime of just under 4 hours. HBO has already renewed Youth for a second season, making it a valuable investment and a testament to its quality. If you enjoy series like Catastrophe or Divorce, Youth is worth a shot this weekend.

War is another top streaming choice on HBO Max this weekend, especially now that its sixth of eight season 1 episodes are now available to enjoy. War is another new critically acclaimed series on HBO Max this Fall, receiving an 85% Rotten Tomatoes score and having already been renewed for a second season. While this season stars Sienna Miller and Dominic West as a wealthy married couple going through a high-profile divorce, War ​​​​​​is designed as an anthology series, meaning that a new cast and story will be introduced in season 2.

War is created by George Kay, best known for Lupin and Hijack, and is currently the third most-watched HBO Max series in the world. Critics praise War for its high entertainment value and great performances, impressively adding fresh elements to a familiar legal drama premise. ScreenRant gave the series 9/10 stars, noting, "HBO's War is a gloriously dramatic story of power struggles and betrayals that can only be described as Shakespearean." If you're a fan of corporate legal dramas like The Good Fight, Succession and Damages, War should be a hit for you on HBO Max.

The Gilded Age is the best new HBO Max series to watch this weekend, following its season 4 premiere on Sunday, November 1. The Emmy-winning period drama is perfect for fans of Bridgerton and Downton Abbey, featuring highly detailed production and costume design that seamlessly recreate a prominent historical era. The Gilded Age takes place during the late 19th century in New York City, centering on social clashes within America's elite and high society. The Gilded Age season 4 earned a 90% Rotten Tomatoes score and is currently HBO Max's most-watched series worldwide.

Prime Video's new 8-part biopic traces a legendary boxer's rise, exploring family, faith, and the convictions that shaped him into a cultural icon.

The Gilded Age sports a fantastic ensemble cast led by Carrie Coon as Bertha Russell, an ambitious socialite and wife of a wealthy railroad businessman who rapidly climbs the big city social ladder, disrupting the old-money status quo. While the series is primarily a serious historical drama with great cultural authenticity, much like Mad Men, it does offer humor in the form of witty and satirical dialogue. The Gilded Age is expected to remain one of the top new series worth watching on HBO Max this Fall until its finale arrives on December 20.
