'GTA 6' Bombshell: Husband Melts Down After Wife Hid Her Role In Game For Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A long-kept GTA 6 secret almost destroyed a marriage

The gaming industry is notoriously secretive, and that's doubly true for anything related to Grand Theft Auto 6. Rockstar Games kept much of the game tightly under wraps, including one of the title's biggest secrets — a remarkable feat when you consider how much of the game leaked prior to launch. All of this might be true, but it didn't take the sting out of one husband's recent discovery about his wife's work on the record-breaking open-world title.

Amber Wallin is a comedian who voices several non-playable characters in GTA 6. Wallin's voice can be heard during an early segment of the game where Lucia is notified she will be allowed to carry out her sentence via a quasi-home arrest program. Wallin appears in a few other roles throughout the game, such as a character who roasts Jason after he harasses her.

Given her profession, it's no surprise that Wallin's characters are a hoot. "Motherfucker wanna clock out soon when shit gets real," she says via the aggravated NPC. "Clock in, ho. Time is money!"

While Wallin's various roles are minor, the game is already the biggest entertainment launch of all time, meaning anyone who can say they were involved in its creation has much to brag about. So, imagine the surprise of Wallin's husband when he learned she was in the game like any other player. According to a video the couple recently posted on social media, Wallin apparently kept her involvement in the game a total secret from even her husband for two years.

"NDAs do not apply to husbands," Wallin's partner screams. The video is not entirely serious — at one point, Wallin suggests Rockstar Games could assassinate her for talking about it even now. The clip was obviously planned beforehand and is staged to look like a dramatic segment in a reality TV show as well. Yet the feelings expressed in it, and Wallin's tight-lipped approach to her work, appear to be 100% genuine.

Perhaps the most ironic part is that Wallin has a video where she playfully describes her relationship with her husband. In it, she describes a time during pregnancy where she took care of a handful of tedious house chores. When she was finished, she wanted to treat herself with a pint of ice cream — but when she opened the fridge, all she found was an empty carton. On top of not throwing it out, the husband evidently did not even bother to put the ice cream back in the freezer.

Given GTA 6's focus on relationships and how much it encourages men to be thoughtful partners, perhaps playing through the game will be instructive and remind Wallin's husband that he has NPCs at home.
