GTA 6 Online Could Be Bigger Than Fortnite, Bank Of America Stock Analyst Says

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Rockstar Games and parent company Take-Two are riding high currently from the success of Grand Theft Auto 6 with a reported 45 million copies sold and $4 billion in revenue already, but this could just the beginning of how much money GTA 6 could bring in. According to Bank of America stock analyst Omar Dessouky, the initial success of GTA 6 reinforces the company's belief that the game has a massive reach, and with that comes the possibility of monetizing the players even more when the next evolution of GTA Online is released.

In fact, SeekingAlpha reported that Dessouky said he is so bullish that he believes GTA Online alone "can become a multibillion-dollar franchise, potentially larger than Fortnite." He said Fortnite generates as much as $5 billion in annual revenue, and GTA Online could be bigger in the fullness of time.

The stock analyst went on to raise his price target on Take-Two's stock to $350. That would represent a significant upside, considering Take-Two currently trades at around $210/share.

Take-Two's stock got hammered in 2026 and never fully recovered. The company's shares are down 16% so far in 2026 and down 12% over the past year.

Investing is a long-term game, though. Zooming out, Take-Two has crushed it since GTA 5 released. If you bought $10,000 worth of Take-Two stock on the day GTA 5 came out in 2013, and never sold, you'd have more than $120,000 today.

As for the next iteration of GTA Online, Rockstar has not said a word about what to expect or if such a mode is even coming. GTA 6, after all, is marketed as a strictly single-player game.

That being said, it seems plainly obvious that a new version of GTA Online is coming. According to multiple reports, the new version of GTA Online for GTA 6 could launch in 2027, along with a PC edition of the game.

GTA Online for GTA 5 has been, and continues to this day, to be a major moneymaker for Rockstar and Take-Two, and it's expected that the developer will find even more ways to make money from players with GTA 6 Online. Some have theorized that GTA 6 Online could have some form of user-generated content, for example, potentially leading to a marketplace of some kind.

GTA 6 launched on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it reached 32.5 million copies sold via pre-purchases alone. On day one, GTA 6 generated $3.79 billion in revenue. Over the first four days, GTA 6 has climbed to 44.9 million copies sold and more than $4 billion in revenue, according to a report.
