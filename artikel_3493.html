Yellowjackets Creator Finally Sheds Light On The Show's Mysterious Missing Episode

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Yellowjackets might be coming to a close, but one of its creators hints at the status of a long-lost episode.

The acclaimed survival thriller is returning for its fourth and final season this month, bringing the story of a stranded high school soccer team to a close. While the upcoming episodes are expected to tie up all loose ends and answer questions about the survivors' experiences, viewers have also been waiting for an explanation about something that was supposed to happen years ago after Yellowjackets season 2.

It was announced several years ago that the supernatural thriller series filmed an episode that never aired. Not much is known about the mysterious installment, even now. However, Yellowjackets' co-creator, Bart Nickerson, has offered some new information about the series bonus episode that got cut.

Nickerson revealed that the reason it never aired was that it didn't fit the storyline of the series. In an interview with Deadline, the co-creator addressed the missing bonus episode, explaining that it doesn't match what ended up being the show's established continuity. While he didn't reveal exactly why it was shelved, his comments imply that it was due to the creative team's decision to take the hit series in a different direction.

I mean, ultimately, the canon of the show is sort of what’s aired, and for us, the fourth season is like the final installment of a complete story. And so, along the way of the making of it, there were lots of paths considered and things like that were explored, but I think ultimately, the final version of the show is what it was supposed to be.

The co-creator also stated that Yellowjackets season 4 wraps up the series the exact way he and Ashley Lyle wanted it to. This likely means that the missing episode didn't affect the way they wanted to tell the story. According to Nickerson, the show's creative team explored several different ways the project could pan out, but the version audiences will watch is the way it was intended to end.

His explanation raises questions about whether the unaired episode contains storylines or character details that are no longer canon to the series. However, Nickerson did not specifically confirm that the episode was removed because of continuity problems, and this is all just speculation among viewers at this point. He also didn't state whether it might eventually become available to view as bonus content or on any collector's editions that might be released in the future.

The existence of the missing Yellowjackets episode was first confirmed in June 2023, shortly after the show's second season ended. At the time, audiences noticed that season 2 only had nine episodes when the first installment had 10. Rumors began circulating online about a possible unaired episode, with some viewers wondering if there would be a surprise release somewhere down the line.

Lyle later addressed those rumors on social media. She confirmed that another episode was planned to air between the second and third seasons. However, despite revealing that an additional episode does exist, she told viewers not to get their hopes up about ever actually watching it.

Yellowjackets season 4 will debut on November 20, 2026, on Showtime and Paramount+.
