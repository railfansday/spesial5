GTA 6 Has Dexter References, And Now Dexter Himself Has Reacted

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Actor Michael C. Hall has reacted to how GTA 6 features references to his Miami-set TV show Dexter, in which he famously played the serial killer Dexter.

Appearing on the Barstool Movies show, Hall said he was "informed" of the Easter eggs in GTA 6 about his character. "I wasn't consulted," he said with a laugh. "It's pretty cool to feel like [Dexter] has folded into the zeitgeist."

After the GTA 6 Extended Look premiered in August, people immediately spotted what they thought was Dexter's apartment building from the TV show. Now that GTA 6 has arrived, people found the apartment building in the game, and you can, too, by following GameSpot's Dexter apartment guide here.

On the TV show, Dexter dismembers his victims, stuffs them into garbage bags, and dumps them into the nearby harbor on his boat, the Slice of Life.

Players can also get into a kill house that bears a resemblance to the one that Dexter uses on the show, complete with plastic sheets for the blood splatter, hammers, and cleavers. Players have also found bones underwater, presumably another reference to Dexter.

GTA 6 is full of Easter eggs, so be sure to check out GameSpot's breakdown of GTA 6's best Easter eggs to seek out while you're traveling around Vice City.

GTA 6 launched on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it sold more than 32 million copies and brought in close to $4 billion in revenue by day one.
