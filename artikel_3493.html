All 3 ‘NCIS’ Shows Officially Return to CBS Tonight

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Although the entire NCIS franchise has been on a roll to start the fall television season, the trio of crime procedurals ran into the one thing that could stop them last week — democracy. Midterm coverage meant Parker, Gibbs, Hanna, and the rest took the night off while the results of House, Senate, and other elections rolled in. It's far from unusual for network programs to be preempted at some point in the fall, especially for those airing on Tuesday nights in an even year, but it nonetheless slowed the series' momentum at a time when the franchise ratings are at a high. Fortunately for fans, their absence on CBS won't last any longer.

All three of NCIS, NCIS: New York, and NCIS: Origins are locked in to return tonight in their usual time slots, starting with the flagship at 8 p.m. ET/PT. Season 24 has been riding a ratings high with the reintroduction of Michael Weatherly's fan-favorite agent Tony DiNozzo as the new director of NCIS in place of the late Leon Vance (Rocky Carroll). As part of a strong CBS premiere week, the series returned with a 20% increase in viewers over the previous season's first episode, drawing in 6.45 million viewers and carrying the momentum in Nielsen's live plus 7 days of multiplatform viewership. Episode 5, "Speechless," will next send the team to take on the case of an eight-year-old girl, played by NCIS star Katrina Law's own daughter, Kinley Andreen, left alone when her Marine mother goes missing. In a race against time to find her parent, they learn the lengths the mom was willing to go to for her family and discover that their shy witness' unexpected skill may be the key to blowing their search open.

The newcomer New York, meanwhile, will continue Sam Hanna's (LL COOL J) homecoming at 9 p.m., with an episode that hits close to home for his new partner, Nick Schaeffer (Scott Caan). The Big Apple's agents are called in to investigate after a naval interrogator is violently murdered in her own home. In the background, however, the corruption investigation into Nick's old partner is still unfolding, and he's unsure how to process it all. Finally, at 10 p.m., Origins will continue where its previous episode left off with "Baila Esta Cumbia," as Gibbs (Mark Harmon) and Bishop (Emily Wickersham) continue their off-the-books mission with an intense interrogation while, in 1993, Franks (Kyle Schmid) leads the team on a murder case involving a con woman that hits Lala (Mariel Molino) especially hard.

While Weatherly's return as Tony and Harmon's return as Gibbs have both drawn extra attention to NCIS and Origins early on, at least one of them will be gone from the screen once more before the year ends. With the addition of the LL-led New York to the lineup, Origins is one of several series CBS cut down to just ten episodes instead of the 18 its past two runs have clocked in at. The shortened Season 3 means the finale is expected to air in December, leaving room for NCIS: Sydney to return for Season 4 sometime next year. That means only five episodes are left after tonight to wrap up one of the most dangerous and personal cases of Gibbs' career that has tied so deeply into his past at Camp Pendleton.
