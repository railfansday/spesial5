GTA 6 Includes Compelling Evidence That It’s Set In The Same Universe As Red Dead Redemption

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Grand Theft Auto 6GTA 6Red Dead Redemption 2

Grand Theft Auto 6 contains a few new interesting pieces of evidence that seem to indicate Rockstar’s popular crime-sim franchise lives in the same universe as its also-popular cowboy series, Red Dead Redemption.

Let’s start with one of the most compelling and obvious connections between GTA 6 and Red Dead: a book you can find in the new game that directly references events from RDR 2. As spotted and shared online by GameRoll, there’s a book players can find early on in Brian’s Marina in GTA 6 that contains many references to both Red Dead Redemption and its prequel, Red Dead Redemption 2. For example, the book mentions a gunslinger players can meet in RDR 2, as well as mentions of locations from those games such as Blackwater and New Austin.

A more subtle connection, but one that has some interesting implications, can be randomly spotted on the license plates of some cars in GTA 6. Every so often, you might spot a car with a New Austin license plate. In the world of Red Dead, New Austin is intended to be the game’s version of Texas. Things get a bit weird if you think about all of this too much, as Texas has been mentioned in both franchises before. But then again, GTA V mentions Florida, which has been retconned into Leonida in GTA 6. So this stuff happens sometimes.

A sillier connection to Red Dead can be spotted at a western-themed mini-golf course in GTA 6. If you explore that place, you’ll quickly discover a small dog with a cowboy doll attached to his back. Look closer, and you’ll be able to identify that cowboy as Arthur Morgan.

And speaking of Arthur Morgan, you can actually find what looks a lot like his famous hat in GTA 6, as well. It’s the hat I wear when I go hunting as Jason. Before all this, people spotted connections between RDR2 and GTA 6 in pre-release trailers and screenshots. Some of these connections were also spotted in GTA V, like a president from RDR being featured in artwork.

So does this all mean that GTA 6 is in the same universe as Red Dead Redemption 2? I think it clearly indicates that Rockstar is playing around with that idea. The problem is that if you dig into the lore of both franchises, you’ll find some discrepancies. But Rockstar has shown in the past that it’s willing to simply retcon these kinds of issues without feeling the need to explain anything.
