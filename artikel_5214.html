Harmony was built with an ambitious vision: to make decentralized applications fast, scalable, and affordable using random state sharding. At its peak, Harmony positioned itself as a high-performance alternative to Ethereum, capable of finalizing transactions in seconds while keeping fees low.

However, Harmony’s journey has not been smooth. Following security incidents and declining developer activity, the network entered a prolonged rebuilding phase.

Since then, Harmony’s native token ONE price has seen a drop of 89% from its peak.

Harmony’s (ONE) project announced plans to shut down its Layer-1 blockchain. The rally is mainly linked to plans to move ONE to Ethereum and give holders new ERC-20 tokens.

Under the plan, Harmony will take a snapshot of ONE balances and airdrop matching ERC-20 tokens on Ethereum. Major exchanges and wallets are expected to support the migration, giving traders a reason to buy ONE before the transition.

Trading volume has also surged more than 1,800%, showing strong activity around the token. The project also plans to redirect future ONE token emissions toward a new AI-focused venture called “The Remix Economy.”

On September 6, 2026, the team proposed permanently closing the network due to the rising cost of protecting it from hackers and AI agents. The decision followed major attacks, including the $100 million Horizon bridge hack in 2022 and a recent August exploit that allowed billions of ONE tokens to be minted.

Validators have also been told to shut down their nodes from September 10, while the project plans to redirect future token emissions toward its new AI-focused venture, “The Remix Economy.”

Harmony is strengthening its long-term outlook through its 2026 Vision roadmap. The recently launched Mainnet v2026.1.0 upgrade improves network security and validator performance, while new Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) upgrades make it easier for Ethereum developers to build on Harmony.

The project is also expanding into AI-powered DeFi with Autoswap v2 and automated hedging tools that aim to reduce impermanent loss while offering yields of up to 32%. Another bullish factor is Harmony’s tokenomics, as every ONE token used for gas fees and smart contract execution is permanently burned, reducing supply over time.

If development continues and adoption grows, these upgrades could support stronger price growth over the coming years.
