Early Zcash (ZEC) Holder Breaks 9-Year Silence With $11.4 Million Move

A Zcash (ZEC) whale that bought 23,412 ZEC nine years ago moved 15,750 ZEC worth $11.39 million after nine years of inactivity, Lookonchain reported on Thursday. According to Lookonchain, the whale paid $5.04 million for the coins at an average price of $215.

Lookonchain also said that after nine years of holding, the whale was finally selling, and that his total profit on the position was more than $12 million.

Arkham data shows that the whale sent the ZEC in two transactions. The first one, 8,000 ZEC worth $5.73 million, went to an address without an Arkham label. About an hour later, the wallet sent 7,750 ZEC worth $5.66 million to a Kraken deposit address.

The whale got the coins nine years ago from Zcash's shielded pool. He withdrew most of them in batches of 250 ZEC, according to Arkham.

On Thursday, Lookonchain also reported that another whale deposited about 47,500 ZEC, or $35.8 million, to Binance.

ZEC dropped as low as $675.67 over the past 24 hours and was trading at $748.87 on Thursday, according to CoinGecko. CoinGecko put the market capitalization of ZEC at $12.77 billion, which made it the 11th-largest cryptocurrency. On Nov. 18, the price of ZEC was around $927.

A day before that, BitMEX co-founder Arthur Hayes set a $1,800 target for ZEC with no deadline. Hayes put Zcash in a group of four tokens that he called the "Four Horsemen," together with Ethereum, NEAR and Venice Token.

"The Four Horsemen shall pump and then usher in the end of the AI bubble," Hayes wrote on X. According to Hayes, the size of his position is "small to medium as conviction is meh."

Bitwise filed an S-1 for a Zcash ETF on Nov. 17 as well. The filing shows that the fund would trade on the Texas Stock Exchange under the ticker ZEC and would not use staking or shielded transactions.

On Thursday, Zakura, the Zcash development team led by Sean Bowe and Dev Ojha, asked users to move their ZEC out of the Sapling pool. Zcash launched Sapling in October 2018, and according to Zakura, about 400,000 ZEC still sits in it.

On Nov. 18, 2020, the Canopy upgrade at block 1,046,400 stopped new deposits into Sprout, Zcash's first shielded pool, according to the Electric Coin Company. Zcash activated its latest upgrade, NU7, at block 3,543,405 on Nov. 6. According to ZIP 218, NU7 cut the target block time from 75 seconds to 25 seconds.

"Practical pairing breaks are highly speculative," Zakura wrote about the cryptography behind Sapling.
