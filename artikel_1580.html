Watch: Milan loanee Fofana enjoying life at Sevilla as fans show their appreciation

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

AC Milan loanee Youssouf Fofana seems to be enjoying life at Sevilla, just as the fans appreciate his efforts on the pitch so far.

Fofana was part of Milan’s Bomb Squad this summer as Ruben Amorim felt he wouldn’t be useful in the team. However, the Rossoneri struggled to find a new club for the midfielder and in the end, he was sent to Sevilla on a dry loan.

It was far from the best outcome for Milan who, according to reports, are paying a whopping 92% of his salary still. In other words, the Spanish side got a very good deal, especially since the French midfielder has been doing really well so far.

Fofana has one goal and three assists in nine games, consistently receiving a high player rating as well from the stat sites. Clearly, the fans are very happy with his performances, as seen when they greeted the team last night ahead of today’s derby against Real Betis.

The midfielder himself seems to be very happy too, but whether he will stay at Sevilla beyond this season is an entirely different matter. Milan will be looking to cash in and several clubs have expressed an interest, so the Spanish side might find themselves outbid.
