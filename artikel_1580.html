RECAP: Nottingham Forest 3-1 Man City - Premier League: Enzo Maresca's men suffer third defeat in a row, as Liam Delap stars to lift his side out of drop zone - at Man United's expense

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

RDaily Mail Sport's live coverage of Nottingham Forest vs Man City.

Oliver Glasner channelled his inner Jose Mourinho when Delap scored Forest's third goal, sprinting down the touchline to celebrate with his players.

In such a precarious job, this victory will mean a lot to him. There's never any guarantee of owner Evangelos Marinakis' patience.

For the first time since 2024, Manchester City have lost three consecutive games in the top fliight.

They're also just the second team in top-flight history to win their first seven games then lose the next three. The previous side to do that were Sheffield Wednesday in 1904-05.

I suspect they'll beat Fulham after the international break, but you never know!

Manchester United end a day in the bottom three of the to -flight after having played at least nine games for the first time since November 1986.

Ouch. Thanks to Opta for the stat. They'll be looking to make amends against Aston Villa tomorrow but I don't back them.

Donnarumma had another poor game today, looking flappy. I'm not sure there was much he could have done about the goals, which were all worthy strikes, but conceding three goals won't help a man who has come under increasing scrutiny in recent weeks.

Here's why Maresca stood by him. Do you think it's time for a change?

Why Maresca is sticking with Donnarumma despite Leipzig horror show

JACK GAUGHAN: These are tough times for Donnarumma, a calamitous night in the Champions League adding to the feeling that the past few weeks have not reflected kindly on him.

Marc Guehi and Josko Gvardiol looked lost today. Enzo Maresca hooked the at half-time after each was at fault for one of the goals. Gvardiol was asleep, giving away the ball for the first, and Guehi just retreated as Delap ran towards goal for the second.

Worrying. Guehi has looked shorn of confidence in recent weeks and this isn't the first time he's been withdrawn at the break.

Today we saw the version of Delap which we know he can be. An absolute bulldozer.

His main flaw is that he typically lacks the finishing. To demonstrate: coming into today, he had scored just two Premier League goals since the start of LAST season. Not a good look.

Well, his finishing was explosive here, as was his running and strength. He's scary when he plays like that.

Manchester United have never been in the relegation zone at this stage of a Premier League season before. Truly abysmal for them.

As for Forest, they can smile after being rewarded for their good performance. They're far from out of the woods, but they did two key things today: score at home, and get Liam Delap's confidence up. Both are critical.

Maresca straight down the tunnel and it didn’t seem as if there was a handshake between him and Oliver Glasner. Dismal night for City but the performance is in keeping with how they are playing. So susceptible on the break and when you’re making this many individual mistakes then the opponents know that they can just wait for them.

Liam Delap has been speaking to Sky Sports.

'It's been coming. We've been performing like that every week but not putting it in the net,' he said.

'We did everything right, got the three points, and are happy going into the international break.

'I just wanted to run and shoot and luckily go there and it went in the net (for first goal).

'Thankfully I put it in the net (for the second).

'We'll have to see it back (Glasner's run down the touchline). Never (seen him run like that).

'We've been deserving this for a long time. Hopefully we can build on it.'

Man City have now lost three times on the spin in the Premier League. Whatever faux title charge they were mounting is collapsing fast at this rate.

The full time whistle blows an Oliver Glasner fist pumps. A HUGE win for Nottingham Forest, one which propels them out of the relegation zone, and plunges Manchester United into it.
