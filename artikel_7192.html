Pastor Talarico, 3 Billion Views, and $56 Million on Broadcast: How James Talarico&#8217;s Team Flipped Texas

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

James Talarico was a state representative from Pflugerville, Texas when his committee-room clips started pulling tens of millions of views on TikTok. On Tuesday, he became the first Democrat to win the U.S. Senate race in Texas since 1988, defeating Ken Paxton.

The masterminds behind his campaign told ADWEEK that its social media videos drew over 3 billion organic views since launch in September 2025. Meanwhile, the campaign put most of its ad dollars into broadcast, at $56.5 million, followed by CTV with $8.3 million and YouTube at $8 million, while radio got just $210,000, according to figures shared by the Talarico campaign.

Trishla is an Adweek staff reporter covering AI and tech.
