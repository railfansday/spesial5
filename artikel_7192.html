Upsun: General Accountant - Accounts Payable

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Upsun is the software factory for human-AI workflows. We give teams one unified platform to create, review, deploy, and run software, with AI agents and humans working in sync. Developers, DevOps engineers, and platform teams use Upsun to build and ship with confidence, without wrestling with infrastructure.

Upsun brings this together through two products: one that shapes the work, and one that runs it.

The name says it all. "Up" means uptime, reliability, and acceleration. "Sun" reflects our follow-the-sun-support, a 24x7, globally distributed support team keeping the lights on while you rest. Our core belief is that software should power brighter solutions and greater innovation.

What's our commonality, our cultural fabric? A curious spirit and a thirst for knowledge; an eagerness for innovative ideas and cultures. We believe we can build anything together in an environment that frees you to do your best work.

As a General Accountant - Accounts Payable, you will keep our vendor payments accurate, controlled and on time across multiple entities and currencies as Upsun grows globally.

You will join our Finance and Accounting team of 9 people, which provides accurate reporting, strong controls and operational rigour across the business.You will work closely with accounting and consolidation on month-end close and reconciliations, support the external audit, partner with FP&A on spend visibility, and manage relationships with suppliers and our banking and treasury partners.

At Upsun, remote work isn’t just a trend. It’s our foundation. Upsunners are a global workforce, and we thrive in a multicultural team. We’re committed to open source and an open, welcoming environment. Our team spans more than 30 countries and a wide range of experience levels, and we’re currently hiring for this role in Canada, France or Spain. We’re unable to provide visa sponsorship at this time, but we welcome applications from all qualified candidates who are legally authorized to work in these countries.
