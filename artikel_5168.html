Bitcoin (BTC) Price Analysis for November 20: Death Cross Is Here

With Bitcoin trading at approximately $63,000, the market stays somewhat pressured today. Thankfully, we see a 13.6% increase in trading volume, hitting $33 billion. The price has recently closed at $63,426.

The price is down more than 20% over the last month, after the drop that started from around $81,000 at the beginning of November.

If we take a look at social sentiment, it stays in the "fear" zone, which suggests a heavily oversold market. The Fear & Greed Index stands at 10, down from 28 a week ago. The possibility of a reversal is unfortunately slim, and sentiment on its own doesn't provide any foundation for a bullish market reversal.

For now, the market is pricing in the CME rate hike. Governor Christopher Waller backed a 25-basis-point hike in December. The October CPI came in at 0.4%, above the 0.3% forecast.

As usual, in the case of a rate hike, risk-on assets like Bitcoin struggle and have to endure outflows. U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs lost around $240 million on November 17, according to APA.

However, considering the liquidation volume during the breakdown and the further downtrend, there's barely any volume left.

BTC has been moving between $62,000 and $65,000 since the drop on November 14, and it's trading below all major moving averages. Three of them have converged around $75,500, which is far above the price.

Citi cut its 12-month target by 12% to $99,532, citing the drop below the 200-day average, among other things.

In the last 24 hours, more than $391 million has been liquidated, with $288 million in longs. It clearly suggests the presence of pressure on the market, which suggests that larger investors are aiming to liquidate their holdings rather than accumulate or add more buying volume.
