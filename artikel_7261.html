Lightbox Animation: Sets&Props Concept Artist Internship

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Headquarters: Córdoba, Andalusia, Spain
URL: https://lbox.es

Your mission

Knock, knock...Any Lightboxer in the room?

If you are passionate about 3D animated films and want to create magic cinematographic, you can´t let this opportunity go!

We are looking for a talented Sets&Props Concept Artist Internship with enthusiasm to join our great team on the exciting new project, Animated Feature TAD 4!!
You will be responsible for creating outstanding backgrounds and props.

First of all, you're probably interested in knowing that...we offer 100% remote work!


WE NEED YOU TO BE ABLE TO DO:

  • Create Design conceptual artwork under directions of the Director and collaborate closely with Artists.
  • Develop the project´s artistic vision, showing understanding of the goals creating designs which fit the style of the animation.
  • Demonstrate your artistic ability, with a strong understanding of form, color and light, with complex and high detail models.
  • Knowledge of environments: understand architecture, have good awareness of city spaces and countryside landscapes.
  • Complete the packs with references for colors and materials.
  • Assemble influential research & reference material for the 3D team.
  • Ensure the production meets the highest standards of quality.
  • Attend reviews, take feedback and the ability of going back to preliminary steps if needed.
  • Ability to work autonomously and independently in a fully remote environment, with that comes the supreme individual responsibility to prioritize and manage multiple tasks and maintain your own initiative.
  • Great team player, create a collaborative and welcoming environment.
  • Proven ability to meet deadlines, prioritize workload and within budgets, adhere to the production schedule and deliver completed assignments on time.
  • Align with the style, sensibility of the project and the Director vision.

WE WILL MAKE THE PERFECT MATCH IF YOU HAVE:
  • Knowledge of Photoshop
  • Knowledge of Maya, Blender is a plus.
  • Excellent drawing skills and understanding of design fundamentals.
  • Strong knowledge of color, lighting, atmosphere & overall composition.
  • Ability to visually interpret Director and Supervisor ideas.
  • Passion for creating new worlds.
  • Ability to take feedback constructively and implement it effectively, to reach the quality desired.
  • Anticipation and quick reaction for problem solving.
  • Demonstrate great autonomy and proactivity in your work.
  • Great attention to detail.
  • Outstanding interpersonal and collaborative skills.
