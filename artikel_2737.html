Buccaneers’ brief stint back on top of the NFC South is over

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers ascended to the top of the NFC South following the Carolina Panthers loss on Sunday. It was short lived, however. Tampa's loss to the Chicago Bears on Sunday night handed the division lead back to the Panthers (3-5).

Elsewhere in the NFC South, the New Orleans Saints won to also get to 3-5 (they lose the tiebreaker to Carolina) and the Atlanta Falcons lost to fall to 3-6. The Bucs are also 3-6, but Tampa has a head-to-head win over Atlanta.

The division is still very much in play and as we've seen in the past, all you have to do is get a seat at the table and get hot at the right time.
