Oklahoma State wrestling vs Stanford live updates, start time, TV channel, streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

After a 28-10 win at Arizona State on Friday night, the No. 2 Oklahoma State Cowboys continue heading west for a wrestling dual at No. 7 Stanford.

The Cowboys spent Friday night in Tempe and worked out on Saturday morning in Arizona. OSU flew to Stanford on Saturday and will get another workout in before another evening dual.

"It'll be another really good crowd," OSU coach David Taylor said. "Stanford has a really good team up and down the lineup so we got to be a little better, but I know our guys will rise to the challenge and I know they're looking forward to it."
