Denzel Boston listed as questionable after missing Friday's practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Browns will be on Sunday Night Football this weekend, but it remains to be seen if rookie wide receiver Denzel Boston will be in their lineup.

Boston missed his third straight practice on Friday with a quad injury and the team listed him as questionable to face the Raiders. The second-round pick has not missed a game this season and has 33 catches for 523 yards and three touchdowns in 10 contests.

Defensive end Derek Barnett (hamstring) also missed practice all week and has been ruled out for this Sunday.

Right guard Austin Barber (knee) and running back Raheim Sanders (hip) are listed as questionable. Sanders will need to be activated from injured reserve in order to play against Las Vegas.
