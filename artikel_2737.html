Steelers' playoff odds soar following AFC North rival's embarrassing Week 10 loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Fans of the Pittsburgh Steelers, rejoice! The Steelers' playoff odds are on the rise following an AFC North rival's embarrassing loss on Monday Night Football in Week 10.

Taking to social media after the Baltimore Ravens' crushing loss to the previously winless Los Angeles Chargers, Steelers analyst Derrick Bell revealed that Pittsburgh's odds to make the playoffs had risen to 76%, with a less than 1% chance to earn the No. 1 seed in the AFC, an 11% chance to host a wild-card playoff game, a 65% chance to earn a wild-card spot, and a 24% chance of being out of playoff contention.

Even without Lamar Jackson, the Ravens were projected as heavy favorites to defeat the then-0-8 Chargers. Ultimately, a complete lack of run game from Baltimore allowed Los Angeles to get the upper hand early. And although the Ravens attempted to battle back late with the score set at 23-20, wide receiver Zay Flowers fumbled the ball with approximately 2:03 remaining in the fourth quarter.

One AFC North team's struggles are another's advantage, as the Steelers find something positive to take away from Week 10 — which seemed extremely difficult to do following their equally as embarrassing blowout loss to the Cincinnati Bengals on Sunday Night Football.
