Fernando Mendoza: It's good to learn from getting punched in the face

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Fernando Mendoza didn’t look overmatched early in his first NFL start, but the road got tougher for the Raiders’ rookie quarterback over the final three quarters of Sunday’s game against the Seahawks.

Mendoza capped the team’s first offensive possession with a touchdown run and led the Raiders to a field goal on their second possession, but the Raiders would pick up just 68 yards and three points over the rest of their 21-13 home loss. Things got particularly bad in the second half as the Raiders failed to pick up a first down while losing 17 yards over their first five post-halftime possessions.

Mendoza was sacked six times and hit 12 times over the course of the game, but found a way to put a positive spin on the experience when he spoke to reporters after the game.

“The Seahawks have a fantastic defense, we’ve seen it all season,” Mendoza said, via the team’s website. “A lot of credit to them. . . . It’s good to get punched in the face because now it’s a great opportunity to grow and learn and to really unveil all the deficiencies that I have in my game and [become] a future hopeful leader of this offense.”

The Broncos are next up for the Raiders, which means Mendoza will face another experienced and accomplished defense that could teach him some painful lessons that he uses to steel himself for future success as an NFL quarterback.
