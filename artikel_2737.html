Caitlin Clark turns heads at NCAA basketball game with boyfriend

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Caitlin Clark has spent her third WNBA offseason staying close to basketball, and Thursday night found her back in a college gym for a reason tied to her personal life. The Indiana Fever star showed up at the State Farm Center in Champaign for a men's matchup between Illinois and Penn.

Illinois ran away with the game, beating Penn 112-70 to improve to 2-0 on the season, shooting 58.9% from the field. But for much of the crowd, the game itself wasn't the only story drawing attention.

Caitlin Clark watched the game from behind the Penn bench alongside her boyfriend, Connor McCaffery, the Daily Illini's Sahil Mittal reported. McCaffery is the son of Penn head coach Fran McCaffery.

Both Clark and McCaffery played basketball at the University of Iowa, where McCaffery finished second in program history with 166 games played.

Clark wore no team gear, while McCaffery was spotted in a grey Penn Quakers hooded sweatshirt supporting his father's team.

Illinois freshman Quentin Coleman, who scored 19 points off the bench, had no idea Clark was in the building until a reporter told him mid-interview.

"Yoooooo! I gotta tell my sister bro," Coleman said, putting his hand to his forehead. "Caitlin Clark still here? Shoot."

Illinois coach Brad Underwood joked postgame that the gym should have held a three-point contest, saying no one who played that night could have beaten Clark in a shootout.
