Packers waive RB Pierre Strong

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Packers made room for Micah Parsons' return on Thursday, waiving running back Pierre Strong Jr.

Strong's departure reduces the Packers' roster to 52 players. Parsons' 21-day return-to-practice window expires on Nov. 25, the day of the Packers' game against the Rams.

Strong originally joined the Packers on Sept. 8, 2025, signing with the practice squad. He has spent most of his time with the team on the practice squad.

The Packers elevated Strong to the active roster before the Week 5 game against the Bears. It seems likely he could return to the practice squad.

Strong has rushed for 59 yards on 25 carries in five games this season.
