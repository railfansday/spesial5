D.J. Hall has 19 points, 18 rebounds, Texas State defeats UTSA 87-67

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

SAN ANTONIO (AP) — D.J. Hall's 19 points and 18 rebounds helped Texas State defeat UTSA 87-67 on Sunday.
