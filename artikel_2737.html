Utah Jazz take on the Lakers in LA: Preview, start time, how to watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

After a near-win against the Dallas Mavericks, fueled by Svi Mykhailiuk, the Utah Jazz head to LA to take on the Lakers.

The Utah Jazz, for some reason, have lost all momentum since their win over the Lakers. What was such a huge lift in spirits feels nearly forgotten after two demoralizing losses in their last two games. Now, the Jazz have a chance to play the Lakers again. Is this another chance to turn their season around? The Lakers are not themselves after losing Austin Reaves to injury. It looks like they'll face the Lakers without Reaves again.

This gives Utah a chance to win this one and potentially start another run, but it'll take a better effort from their young core than they got tonight. It'll also take some better shooting. Darryn Peterson's has suddenly fallen into a three-point slump. He's missed his last 15, and the Jazz could absolutely use a bounceback game from him.

Utah also needs Keyonte George's leadership to show up again. It's a tough spot for the Jazz right now, but it's also a chance for them to show what they're truly made of. Do they flounder when times are tough or are they going to rise above? We'll find out tomorrow. But one thing that they can always count on is their own demeanor and attitude. It has not gone as anyone hoped or expected, but that doesn't mean the Jazz can't turn things around with energy and execution.

When: Monday, November 23, 2026 | 8:00 PM MT

Where: Crypto.com Arena | Los Angeles, CA
