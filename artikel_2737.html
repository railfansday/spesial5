NBA Europe appears headed for 'soft launch' next fall

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Commissioner Adam Silver has said he wants NBA Europe to launch in the fall of 2027 — and it appears he is going to get his wish.

Sort of. Just not in the way he or others may have originally envisioned.

There are only 11 months between now and Silver's proposed launch date, and NBA Europe has yet to have any formal agreements with teams. Negotiations with the EuroLeague — some of the teams the NBA is eyeing are members of it — have slowed. That could lead to a "soft launch" of the league next fall, reports The Athletic's Joe Vardon. A member of one of the ownership groups of a potential NBA Europe club put it this way to Vardon:

"Broadly speaking, it is implausible that a serious league could be launched (by next October)."

The idea of a "soft launch" has been floated before and is not something the NBA has pushed back on — this was always a possibility. Building something sustainable long term was always going to take time and the league understood that.

The NBA is partnering with FIBA (the international basketball organization that puts on the World Cup and helps pick teams for the Olympics) on an NBA Europe plan that calls for 12 permanent teams and four open spots teams could play their way into through domestic leagues and tournaments. Those dozen permanent teams would be made up of some existing European powerhouses — FC Barcelona, Real Madrid, Bayern Munich, and Panathinaikos of Greece have been mentioned, among others — plus teams in major European markets such as Rome, Paris, Lyon and others (some of those teams could be tied to existing soccer powers, but not all).

What would make things smoother is an agreement or merger with the EuroLeague — the continent's existing preeminent basketball competition — but a couple of months ago EuroLeague shareholders rejected the NBA's initial proposal. The challenge is that EuroLeague has 13 permanent members and only some of them would be jumping to NBA Europe, which is a bit messy. That said, the sides are still talking, and it's possible they reach some kind of arrangement.

What would a "soft launch" look like? From The Athletic:

So, a soft launch could, in theory, include a qualifying tournament in the fall of 2027. Another possibility for NBA Europe's 2027 version is if, as expected, the NBA formalizes agreements with at least some existing European teams to join as NBA Europe licensed franchises over the next few months. Those teams could play each other in some precursory competition that ultimately becomes NBA Europe.

NBA owners are fully backing Silver and the NBA Europe plan because it's essentially free money for them. Silver and the NBA have met with potential teams and investors and expect franchise fees in the $500 million to $1 billion range for each of the 12 permanent members.

What the NBA wants is to modernize – and better monetize — the basketball infrastructure in Europe. As part of its plan, the league is looking at a multibillion-dollar infrastructure fund to pay for arena construction, including for existing teams (which may already have arenas), bringing more modern NBA-style arenas to those cities.
