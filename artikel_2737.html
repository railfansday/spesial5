Aidan Hutchinson wasn't fined for sack that injured Drake Maye

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

As expected, Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson wasn’t fined for the tackle that may have ended Patriots quarterback Drake Maye’s season.

The weekly announcement from the league includes no fines against Hutchinson.

A question emerged as to whether Hutchinson’s sack of Maye amounted to a hip-drop tackle. During the broadcast, Fox rules analyst (and former NFL senior V.P. of officiating) Mike Pereira said it wasn’t a hip-drop tackle. On Monday, NFL in-house rules analyst Walt Anderson confirmed that it wasn’t a hip-drop tackle.

But it’s not Anderson who makes the decisions on fines. Jon Runyan makes those decisions. Not surprisingly, Runyan decided that the tackle was not a violation of the rules.

Under the rules, a hip-drop tackle happens when a player “grabs the runner with one or both hands or wraps the runner with both arms; and . . . unweights himself by swiveling and dropping his hips and/or lower body, landing on and/or trapping the runner’s leg(s) at orbelow the knee.” Anderson said Hutchinson did not “unweight” himself or “swivel.”

Hutchinson suffered a torn ACL in his knee while celebrating the sack. Although it’s not clear whether Maye will play again in 2026, Hutchinson is done for the year.
