My wife and I were in a similar situation a few years ago after we both retired. We found out it really really pays to shop — not just in terms of how much in fees you end up paying, but also in respect to finding a firm with the right mix of services. This ongoing forum thread lists specific advisors who offer Bogleheads-friendly investment and financial planning services:
viewtopic.php?t=440158
Besides getting financial advice, we searched for a good tax preparer and estate attorney. Those searches led to some interesting places, including local banks and trust companies. Even though our estate isn't complicated, we wound up signing up with an independent local fiduciary to handle execution of our trust and wills. You can search this site and find a number of in-depth discussions of these sort of issues that might help your research. One example is:
