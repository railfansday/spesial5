For a family trip of three nights or more, compare a hotel suite, extended-stay room, or rental with a kitchen to a standard room. Add up the kitchen’s extra cost for the whole stay, any cleaning fee included, and book the one with the kitchen if cooking breakfast and a few dinners would save your family more than that.

That extra cost shows up in the nightly rate. The savings show up at every meal your family doesn’t buy at a restaurant.

Say a room with a kitchen costs your family of four $30 a night more than one without. Breakfast out costs $50 and dinner $100, against $15 and $30 for the same meals from a grocery store. Cooking breakfast saves $35 a day, which covers the $30 on its own.

Over five nights, cooking every breakfast and two dinners saves $315, or $165 after the kitchen’s extra $150. On a stay of a night or two, the kitchen often doesn’t pay off, because some costs come once per stay, such as the coffee, oil, and spices from your first grocery run. A rental’s cleaning fee is one of them. A $100 fee adds $50 a night to a two-night stay, more than the $35 a day you save by cooking breakfast, but only $20 a night to a five-night one.

Extended-stay chains put a kitchen in every room. Extended Stay America’s suites all have full kitchens, and the chain discounts longer stays. Candlewood Suites’ studios and suites come with full kitchens too. Some extended-stay chains also serve a free breakfast, so there the kitchen saves you money only on the other meals you cook. Check for a stovetop, since some kitchenettes have only a microwave.

On Airbnb or Vrbo, compare a rental’s total price for your dates with a hotel’s. Under the Federal Trade Commission’s fee rule, that total must include the cleaning fee, as a hotel’s must include any resort fee, though taxes can be added before you pay.

Shop on day one for breakfasts and two or three dinners that share ingredients, such as taco meat one night and burrito bowls the next. Plan a dinner out every day or two so nobody spends the trip at the stove.

If cooking breakfast saves your family more than the kitchen’s extra cost per night, with any cleaning fee divided across your nights, the kitchen pays for itself before you cook a single dinner.
