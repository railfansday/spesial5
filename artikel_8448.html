Investing - Theory, News &amp; General • Re: [2026 Boglehead] Conference experiences?

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

I attended the recent Bogleheads conference and thought it was exceptionally well done. Whenever I hear William J. Bernstein speak, I feel the spirit of Jack Bogle and come away reassured. Rick Ferri is a pleasure to listen to, and Dr. Jim Dahle is direct and humorous in the best way. Christine Benz does a wonderful job and is always gracious, and I always learn a great deal from Mike Piper, who is talented, knowledgeable, and incredibly receptive and humble.

The conference is a great experience for seasoned investors and novices alike. I'd suggest that newcomers read *The Bogleheads' Guide to Investing* beforehand, since it would help them get far more out of the sessions. My one small caution is that a few of the people who "help" by gathering audience questions on slips of paper can sometimes be fishing for financial planner business, so it's worth staying alert. Otherwise it's a wonderful event, and the feedback and knowledge you get from these generous speakers is second to none.
