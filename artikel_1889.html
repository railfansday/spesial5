Is Huddersfield Town v Liverpool U21s on TV? Channel and streaming details ahead of EFL Trophy meeting

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Huddersfield Town are set to host Liverpool Under-21s in their final EFL Trophy group stage game.

The game is something of a dead rubber with the Terriers already through to the next stage of the competition - and only a big win for the visitors would deny Huddersfield top spot.

However, Martin Drury’s men will be keen to get back to winning ways after exiting the FA Cup in the first round with a 2-1 loss to fourth-tier Exeter City at the weekend.

Drury’s assistant Jon Stead said: “I think it's just getting back to our identity and looking and feeling and showcasing what we've been about for 14 games. I think it's important that we show that and we show that it (the loss to Exeter) was just a moment in time rather than anything bigger.

“We've got a squad where, with us not having the game on Saturday, we can look towards the Leicester game and use these two games to make sure everybody's in the perfect position come Leicester, which would be a huge task for us. So, yes, of course, it's another game that we want to win. Martin will stress that enough.”

Here are the key TV and streaming details as Huddersfield prepare to do battle with Liverpool’s youngsters.

Kick-off at the Accu Stadium in West Yorkshire is scheduled for 7pm tonight (November 10).

Yes - those not attending will be able to watch the action unfold at home. The fixture has not been given top billing on any of the main Sky Sports channels but can be viewed as an alternative broadcast via the Sky Sports+ service. Subscriptions are available online.

Yes - fans can stream the action via the official Sky Sports app or on NOW TV witha valid subscription. There will also be live audio commentary on BBC Radio Leeds.
