Why Cody Gakpo and Ryan Gravenberch are missing for Liverpool against Manchester United

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola has explained why Cody Gakpo and Ryan Gravenberch were unavailable for Sunday's Premier League meeting with Manchester United at Anfield.

Gakpo picked up a muscle injury in his lower leg while on international duty with the Netherlands, while Gravenberch has been managing pain despite returning to training.

It had been hoped that Gravenberch would be passed fit, with his fellow Dutchman Virgil van Dijk and Jeremie Frimpong shaking off niggles of their own to be included in the matchday squad against Man Utd, but the midfielder sits out the game.

Gakpo is yet to train, so it remains to be seen if he will be fit for the midweek trip to Club Brugge in the Champions League.

Speaking to Sky Sports before the match, Iraola said: “Gakpo got injured with the Netherlands, he left the game with a soleus injury.

“He hasn’t been training with us, nothing since getting injured from the national team.

“Ryan has been training but still is managing the pain.

“With Ryan we are going day to day – some days he can. We have been waiting until the end but he was in pain yesterday.
