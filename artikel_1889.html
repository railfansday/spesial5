Is Rochdale v York City on TV? Channel and streaming details ahead of League Two meeting

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

York City are set to visit Rochdale with their sights set on a much-needed victory.

Scott Lindsey’s men have stuttered of late in League Two, losing to Barnet and Grimsby Town across back-to-back games.

They are back on the road tomorrow (November 14), with a trip to the Crown Oil Arena in the calendar. York will be fancied to pick up a result, as the Minstermen sit seventh in the table and the Dale are languishing in 20th.

After his side’s loss to Grimsby, Lindsey said: “We scored goals, but we can’t concede in the fashion we did today. It was nowhere near good enough. We’ve just got to get back to the training ground and work on it. It’s as simple as that, you work harder.

“You only get out of football what you’re prepared to put into it and I’m really disappointed with this performance.

“We were poor and that’s on me, because it didn’t look like us for long spells. You get one of these games every now and then at this level, but we can’t be happy with that.”

Here are the key TV and streaming details ahead of York’s trip to Rochdale.

Kick-off at the Crown Oil Arena is scheduled for 3pm on Saturday, November 14.

Yes - those without a ticket can watch the action unfold from the comfort of their own homes. The fixture has not been given top billing on any of the main Sky Sports channels but can be viewed as an alternative broadcast via the Sky Sports+ service. Subscriptions can be purchased online.

The 3pm blackout can only be enforced when half of the matches across a country’s top two tiers are played at the same time and there are none scheduled to take place.
