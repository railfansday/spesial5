Anthony Elanga has the perfect tool for Newcastle United to secure a shock win over Brighton.

The Seagulls are flying high in the Premier League, and Fabian Hurzeler's side are the favourites to pick up all three points.

Matthias Jaissle is under pressure at St James' Park, and he could potentially ease that by selecting the 24-year-old to start.

The Swedish international has only started once for the Geordies since 5 September after picking up an injury, and has only started four Premier League games in total.

He has been back in contention since the end of October, but has yet to force his way back into the starting XI.

Now is the perfect time for him to do so ahead of facing the second-placed side in the English top flight.

Elanga could provide Newcastle with the attacking threat they need to come up with the goods against Brighton.

Brighton are one of the most dangerous attacking sides in the Premier League, and they often commit plenty of bodies forward to score.

Hurzeler's side are leading the Premier League in goals scored.

That will leave Newcastle under a lot of pressure defensively, and it could be tough for the Magpies to find their own route to goal.

Hurzeler's side will look to suffocate them, but Elanga can be their way to counter it.

As per FotMob, among Premier League wingers, the Swedish international has clocked the third-highest speed with 35.8 kilometres per hour.

That pace on the counter will allow the Geordies to knock the ball clear and maintain an outlet to give the Seagulls something to be wary of.

Interestingly, teammate Barnes tops the list with 36.2 kilometres per hour, providing a winger duo that could ultimately be devastating on the counter.

Newcastle are second in the Premier League for counter attacks per 90 (1.18), according to Data.MB, and having Elanga raring to go will fuel their approach.

Across Elanga's career, he has not scored or assisted more against a team than he has against Brighton.

In nine appearances, he has registered four goal contributions - one goal and three assists.

That record should give him plenty of confidence if selected to start, and with Jaissle needing to turn Newcastle's results around.

Elanga's speed and his history against the Seagulls make a compelling case for his inclusion from the very first whistle.
