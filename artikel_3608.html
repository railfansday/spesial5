Lords Of The Fallen 2 Delayed Yet Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

CI Games has announced that Lords of the Fallen II is coming in April 2027, meaning it's been delayed again. An exact release date will be announced in December, the company said, and people are theorizing that could mean the date will be announced at The Game Awards on December 10.

Lords of the Fallen II was originally scheduled to launch in 2026, but CI Games delayed it to Q1 2027 (January-March) to avoid GTA 6 and give the team more time to polish the experience. Now it's delayed to Q2 2027.

For the previous delay, CI Games said feedback and tests showed that there are “meaningful opportunities” available to “further refine and strengthen the overall experience” with more time.

Despite its name, Lords of the Fallen 2 is actually the third game in the series, following Lord of the Fallen (2014) and Lords of the Fallen (2023).

Based in Poland, CI Games is also known for the Sniper: Ghost Warrior series, the latest entry being 2021's Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts 2.
