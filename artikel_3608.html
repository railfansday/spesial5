NFL Alums Rob Gronkowski and Julian Edelman Slam Sam Hartman Over 'Bachelor'

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

NFL alums Rob Gronkowski and Julian Edelman may not be tuning in when Washington Commanders practice squad quarterback Sam Hartman makes his debut as the next Bachelor.

“We’re promoting [The Bachelor] in the middle of a season? What are we doing?” Edelman, 40, complained on the Monday, November 16, episode of the “Gronk & Jules Show” podcast. “He better be getting like a mill! He better be getting a lot of money.”

The retired football stars and their producer Jack McCarthy were all in agreement that the timing was wrong for ABC’s Friday, November 13, announcement confirming Hartman, 27, as the next star of the hit reality dating show.

“If you were on the current roster and you accepted this, you deserve a slap in the face,” Gronkowski, 37, argued. “Like, if I was the organization, your priorities aren’t straight, obviously. I would just let you go right on the spot if it was the current roster.”

Gronk ultimately backed off his harsh criticism when McCarthy clarified that Hartman is currently on the Commanders’ practice squad. (Hartman was initially released by the Commanders in August but quickly re-signed to the practice squad the following day.)

“Practice squad? We’re playing with fumes but I’d let it go,” Gronk added. “But current squad? Yeah, you’re out, because you’re not showing that you’re all in. In the NFL, you’ve gotta be all in. This would’ve been a big distraction.”

Gronkowski went so far as to suggest his former New England Patriots head coach, Bill Belichick, “would’ve released” anyone who put reality TV ahead of their football commitments.

Edelman questioned how a paycheck from ABC would go over with other members of the Commanders roster.

“I don’t know how it is now, but with us? Oh my god. This guy wouldn’t live [in my era],” he quipped. “But the kid’s got some aura.”

Earlier this week, Hartman insisted that football is his top priority for the rest of the NFL season as he got back to practicing with the Commanders.

“Really excited to get back to work,” Hartman told his TikTok followers on Tuesday, November 17. “I gotta get back to the day job running around throwing the football. All the focus is there. But once the season wraps, can’t wait to join. Can’t wait to meet all the beautiful women. Can’t wait to go on this journey with you guys. I’m super excited.”

He added, “Bachelor Nation, I just wanted to give a huge shout-out to you guys. I appreciate all the support. Appreciate all the love, and especially the jokes.”

Season 30 of The Bachelor premieres on ABC March 21, 2027, at 8 p.m. ET.
