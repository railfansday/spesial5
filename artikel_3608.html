GTA 6 Doesn’t Have Any Doja Cat Music, And She Didn’t Censor Her Message To Rockstar

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 is out now, and the game features a lot of music from famous real-world artists. But Doja Cat is not among them.

She's not happy about that, either.

Doja Cat said online that she is "so f**kin bitter" about that. In follow-up posts, she told Rockstar to "eat my d**k." She said she loves GTA 6, and being left out of the game breaks her heart. Directing her posts at Rockstar in this way, with this language, is unlikely to win her any fans at the studio, so it would be surprising indeed if we see her music get added to the game at any point.

Doja Cat's music is not on any of GTA 6's in-game radio stations, nor do any of her tunes feature on the 34-song GTA 6: The Album, which is out now. The album includes music from Travis Scott, Dua Lipa, Morgan Wallen, Young Thug, Cardi B, Keith Richards, Lana Del Rey, and more.

The in-game radio stations, meanwhile, feature music from Tom Petty, George Strait, Glen Campbell, Bad Bunny, Drake, Lil Wayne, Slayer, Sepultura, System of a Down, and Tool, just to name few.
