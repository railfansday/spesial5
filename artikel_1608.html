Tottenham Hotspur injury, suspension list and return dates vs. Leeds United: Savio, Lucas Bergvall, Mykhaylo Mudryk latest

Spurs are ninth in the Premier League table, picking up 12 points from their first nine matches, and they have been victorious in their last three league fixtures.

Here, Sports Mole rounds up all of Tottenham's latest injury and suspension news ahead of their meeting with Leeds in Saturday's early start.

Possible return date: November 21 (vs. Ipswich Town)
