Starbucks Red Cup Day 2026: How to get your free holiday cup today

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

TL;DR: Starbucks Red Cup Day 2026 takes place on Nov. 17. To qualify for your free cup, buy any handcrafted holiday or seasonal beverage (hot, iced, or blended) in any size.

Starbucks has been very busy lately. They launched their holiday menu on Nov. 5, quickly followed by a fresh lineup of viral Bearista cups.

That's obviously very exciting, but it's not the main draw for seasoned coffee lovers and cup collectors. We're all thrilled about the Peppermint Mocha, but Red Cup Day reigns supreme. This is arguably the biggest day on the coffee lovers' calendar. Starbucks hands out a free, limited-edition red reusable cup with every qualifying handcrafted holiday beverage order on this special day. And you guessed it: fans and collectors go wild.

Starbucks has officially confirmed the date for Red Cup Day 2026: You can score a free reusable holiday cup on Nov. 17.

The 2026 reusable red cup is made with 95% recycled materials and features an illustration of a coffee tree branch with coffee cherries tied with a red bow. The cup is encircled by a green and white grosgrain ribbon motif that also features on paper holiday cups, Christmas Blend coffees, and holiday decor in coffeehouses this season.

Getting your hands on the free collectible cup is pretty straightforward, but we should note that supplies run out notoriously fast.

To get your free cup, buy any handcrafted holiday or seasonal beverage (hot, iced, or blended) in any size. The promo applies in the café, at the drive-thru, via the Starbucks app, and through Starbucks Delivery with DoorDash, Grubhub, and Uber Eats. Once you have your reusable red cup, bringing it back on future visits saves you $0.10 per drink.

Just be prepared for heavy app delays and long drive-thru lines on launch morning — mobile ordering at least 20 minutes ahead is non-negotiable if you want to dodge the rush.
