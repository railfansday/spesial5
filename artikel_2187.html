Middlesbrough wanted ambitious summer loan deal for Tottenham Hotspur player along with Rangers

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Middlesbrough were keen on an ambitious move for the Premier League prospect before missing out on a deal. Boro are currently eyeing promotion to the Premier League from the Championship and they are joint-top of the table after 15 fixtures along with Birmingham City and Stoke City.

Moore, 19, is due to spend the entire season with FC Koln in the Bundesliga but he has struggled to make an impact so far. He has made nine appearances in all competitions since his switch in the last transfer window, eight of which have come in the Bundesliga, but he has managed only one assist and no goals.

According to i News, Boro were interested in a swoop for him ahead of this campaign but they weren’t able to lure him to the Riverside Stadium in the end. Rangers were also in the frame for his signature after his spell at Ibrox last term but Spurs sent him to the RheinEnergieStadion instead.

Middlesbrough drew 0-0 away at Watford last time out before the international break. They are back in action this weekend against Bristol City at home.

Moore, who can play as an attacking midfielder or winger, would have been an ideal addition for Middlesbrough. Luring him to the second tier would have been a big statement of intent by Boro.

The England youth international is highly-rated by Spurs and is being tipped for a bright future in the game. He spent last season at Rangers and scored seven goals in 47 matches for the Glasgow giants.

Moore, who is from London, would have injected more quality into Kim Hellberg‘s starting XI. He knows Will Lankshear from their time at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium together and they could have formed a formidable partnership.

However, it wasn’t to be and Spurs opted to let him join FC Koln in the German top flight instead. Moore has been on the books of his current club for his whole career to date and rose up through their academy ranks.

Moore’s move to FC Koln hasn’t worked out so far. He has taken a bit of time to adapt to life in Germany.

Middlesbrough will get the chance to reignite their pursuit down the line. If they get promoted, they could be a perfect next destination for Moore.

Boro are winless in their last two matches and have missed Lankshear up top. They need to return to winning ways on Saturday as they gear up to play out of form Bristol City.
