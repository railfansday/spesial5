Sheffield Utd should target Ipswich Town & Plymouth Argyle transfers this January

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

After a slow start to the season, Sheffield United have started to find form under Chris Wilder as they push for a play-off place.

The off-field situation at Bramall Lane contributed to a frustrating summer transfer window, with many Blades fans feeling that Wilder was left short in the market.

As we know, the Yorkshire outfit aren’t in a position to splash huge sums, and that’s not going to change in the New Year.

Nevertheless, Wilder will hope that there is room to do some business, and he has shown numerous times over the years that he has an eye for a player.

Despite Sheffield United’s impressive recent form, it’s clear that fresh additions are needed, and the boss will no doubt feel a few positions should be addressed.

Therefore, reports linking the Blades to ex-Birmingham left-back Emmanuel Longelo, who is starring for Motherwell, make sense, as he would certainly improve the group.

But, he may not be the only new face through the door, and here we look at TWO other players that should be on Sheffield United’s radar…

It seems inevitable that Wilder will be targeting attacking players, as the Blades have sometimes lacked a spark in the final third.

One player who could make a big difference is Ipswich Town’s Anis Mehmeti, who has proven himself in the Championship over the years with the Tractor Boys and Bristol City.

The attacking midfielder is way down the pecking order at Portman Road right now, and whilst he rejected summer moves in a bid to fight for his place, he will recognise that he must leave for regular game time, as he hasn’t even made Gary O’Neil’s squad for a Premier League game.

So, he’s certain to be available, and, from a football perspective, Mehmeti is exactly what the Blades are lacking. He looks to find pockets of space, he can play on the half turn, and, most importantly, he is capable of beating his man and creating chances.

The obvious concern is that a host of Championship clubs will also be tracking Mehmeti, but Sheffield United will be able to offer first-team football and a key role in the side, and a loan move means this could be very realistic.

Wilder does have several strikers to choose from, but they are lacking a number nine with genuine pace who can run in behind and offer that threat.

There will be a belief at the club that Ryan One could become that player, but he has suffered bad luck on the injury front, and he is still unproven at this level.

Either way, they could still do with a young, hungry striker who has good physical attributes, and Plymouth’s Bim Pepple ticks all the boxes on that front.

It certainly won’t be easy to prise him from Home Park in January, especially as Argyle chase promotion, but we know that money talks, and the appeal of moving to Sheffield United for the player is obvious.
