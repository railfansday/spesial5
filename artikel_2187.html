Leicester City must consider January action over £4m starlet’s loan if game time struggles continue: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Leicester City brought in Michael Golding from Chelsea for £4m in the summer of 2024 with the hope that he could grow into a first-team asset at the King Power Stadium. He had a strong youth career and a growing reputation at Chelsea, but has struggled to make the breakthrough at LCFC thus far.

Golding is only 20 though, and he’s out on his first senior loan with League Two side Shrewsbury Town. The Foxes will have hoped he could find regular minutes with Gavin Cowan’s side, but that has not been the case.

There are two months until the January transfer window opens, and it will be an important period for Golding. If his Shrewsbury Town loan continues how it has started, he could be recalled and relocated by Leicester City.

Golding moved to Shrewsbury Town late in the transfer window in August. Given his lack of senior experience to date, it was perhaps anticipated that the Leicester City prospect would need time to grow into a role at his temporary home.

However, with over a quarter of the League Two campaign down, Golding has played only 115 minutes of fourth-tier football for Salop. He has been used off the bench in the last two games after being unused against Bristol Rovers and Crawley Town, although that was off the back of an absence through injury.

The LCFC loanee gave a taste of what he can offer with a first senior goal in a 2-1 EFL Trophy win over Everton’s U21s last month. However, other players have been favoured in midfield with all of Tom Sang, Isaac Fletcher, Will Gray and Jay Turner-Cooke finding more minutes than Golding.

As he rebuilds his fitness after an injury, it will be hoped he can gain momentum and find more opportunities. As of yet though, chances have been few and far between in League Two.

Golding has impressed in the Leicester City academy with four goals and three assists in 40 games for the U21s. He has only one first-team appearance to his name but still has plenty of time to maximise his potential at only 20.

However, he needs game time to develop and show how good he can grow to be. If Shrewsbury Town aren’t going to offer him meaningful opportunities, LCFC will have to consider recalling Golding in January.

This is a key season for the midfielder’s future, as he will have only one year left on his deal when his Salop stay is currently scheduled to end in the summer. At that point, the Foxes will have to decide whether to renew his contract, or potentially sell him to recoup a fee.

Golding’s 2026/27 campaign will prove decisive in this. If he can show progress on the senior stage, he could earn more time to fight for a place in Russell Martin‘s future plans.
