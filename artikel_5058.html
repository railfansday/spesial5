Bitcoin Price Holds Near $65,000 as Nvidia Turmoil Drags Nasdaq Down 2.5%

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Bitcoin price traded near $65,000 on Monday afternoon, according to Bitcoin Magazine Pro data, up more than 1% from where it sat at the time of this morning’s report. The world’s largest cryptocurrency held its ground even as a fresh round of selling in Nvidia dragged the Nasdaq down about 2.5%.

The session was choppy. Bitcoin started the day near $64,000 after a weekend plunge to $62,000, then jumped to about $65,600 shortly after the U.S. stock market opened. The bounce faded within the hour, and the bitcoin price slipped back toward $64,000 as tech stocks extended their losses. Buyers stepped in there, and bitcoin has spent most of the afternoon trading around $65,000.

Nvidia was again the heaviest weight on markets. CNBC reported shortly after noon that the chipmaker’s board is not considering bringing back Jensen Huang as CEO after he resigned last week. The board is instead focused on an investigation into whether Huang’s conduct compromised national security or company secrets, the report said. Earlier Monday, President Donald Trump said he would not mind seeing Huang return.

Nvidia shares fell to a fresh intraday low after the report and closed down about 4%, a day before the company reports quarterly results. U.S. chipmakers dropped more than 5%. The Nasdaq Composite slid about 2.5% and the S&P 500 lost nearly 2%, with investors also bracing for Anthropic’s IPO later this week.

Bitcoin did not follow stocks to their lows this time. As the Nasdaq sank to its worst levels of the day after the CNBC report, bitcoin stopped going down and held near $64,800. That marks a change from the past week, when the bitcoin price traded largely in step with chip stocks.

The macro backdrop offered no relief. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh, speaking at a San Francisco Fed conference, avoided any guidance on the near-term path of rates. He said artificial intelligence could lift productivity but “could bring substantial labor market disruption as some jobs are displaced, rather than complemented, by technology.”

His remarks did little to change expectations for a rate hike next month. Fed funds futures imply a quarter-point increase in December, and the dollar index touched its highest level since April 2025. A stronger dollar tends to weigh on bitcoin and gold, and gold fell to its lowest level since October 2025 earlier in the session. The 10-year Treasury yield eased to about 5.12%.

Bitcoin-linked stocks took a heavier hit than the coin itself. Strategy (MSTR) fell nearly 8% and Coinbase (COIN) dropped about 6%, while Strive (ASST), which bought 251 bitcoin last week, slid about 12%. Strategy also added 485 bitcoin last week, but it faces about $2.8 billion of potential index-fund selling when MSCI removes it at the Nov. 30 close, JPMorgan estimates. Spot bitcoin ETFs shed $185 million on Friday, their seventh straight day of outflows.

The next test arrives Tuesday after the bell, when Nvidia reports its fiscal third-quarter results. Analysts expect earnings growth of 90% on revenue of $109 billion. Minutes from the Fed’s October meeting follow on Wednesday, the same day Anthropic is expected to price its IPO.

For now, bitcoin has bounced twice from the $64,000 area on Monday. A move above this morning’s high near $65,600 would put Sunday’s bounce toward $66,000 back in view, while a break below today’s low near $63,800 would bring the weekend low around $62,000 back into play.

At the time of writing, the bitcoin price is near $65,000.
