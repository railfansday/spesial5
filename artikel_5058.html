SHIB Targets Multiple Billions Via America’s Strike Event

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Home » Shiba Inu (SHIB) » SHIB Targets Multiple Billions Via America’s Strike Event

Despite substantial deleveraging across markets, Shiba Inu earns a comfortable spot at USA’s price-calling giants.

Shiba Inu’s (SHIB) meme currency just got critical acclaim in major United States Perpetuals markets. Nadex, short for North American Derivatives Exchange, filed a motion to the United States Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), pursuing time-capped price events.

Conducting business as Crypto.com Derivatives North America, the exchange self-certified a Shiba Inu Strike Event Contract under Commodity Futures Trading Commission regulations. This now allows traditional Derivatives market players exposure to cash-settled event bids.

These SHIB price-tracking contracts do not use traditional margin leverage. They are fully prepaid or collateralized, so the maximum loss per contract is limited to the price paid to enter (plus fees). There is no margin call or unlimited downside in the style of Perpetual Futures.

By this strategy, Shiba Inu’s been placed within an honorable roster of major-cap altcoins. The exchange also submitted strike event contract notices covering blue-chips like XRP, Solana, Stellar (XLM), Dogecoin (DOGE), Litecoin (LTC), Hedera (HBAR), Ondo & Polkadot (DOT).

The new Shiba Inu (SHIB) price-linked product is a binary-style swap that lets traders take a position on whether SHIB’s benchmark price finishes above a chosen strike at time expiration. Contracts are eligible for listing since November 13, 2026. Will that lift SHIB’s sloppy volumes?

The popular Japanese guard dog-encrusted meme currency saw a tremendous drop in Spot and Futures markets during Q3 of 2026, pushing the meme coin to dwell at new yearly lows. With the Derivatives markets piquing their interest in SHIB, a multi-billion influx could come.

Right now, the trading volumes present a grimy picture of $44.56 million in daily Spot trading volume. This puts Shiba Inu at the sixth position in the meme coin category, ranked by 24-hour trading volume. The canine coin’s down 24% in 30 days, while parent chain ETH slid by 25%.

Further on, the 24-hour turnover rate stood below 2%. On Spot markets, turnover counts SHIB trades already completed, while depth describes orders displayed at the moment of the reading. The same capital can trade repeatedly during a day. Low volumes resemble vague demand.

Next up is the major demand retest: if Shiba Inu’s (SHIB) $0.00000353 support bubble holds, the next checkpoint revolves around $0.00000515, with the mid-range extending to $0.000006. Traders need to stay alarmed due to the Parabolic Stop & Reverse (SAR) flashing a ‘sell’ signal on the one-day price charts: bottom may not be in yet.

Meanwhile, key data on Perpetual markets showcase a gradually rising total Open Interest (OI), as leveraged players increase their positions before a presumed drastic price swing. The soft volume hikes haven’t flipped the switch back to bullish.

Long price plays still account for most of liquidations, soaking up $79.81K out of $87.20K in liquidations on SHIB’s regular smart contract.
