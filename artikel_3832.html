Rethinking Couch Cooperative Play for Shoot ‘Em Ups

Sturmjager features a two-player local couch cooperative mode designed around shared fun rather than punishing failure.

If a player loses all their lives, they aren’t locked out with an early “Game Over.” Instead, as long as the remaining player survives solo for roughly 30 seconds, the downed player automatically respawns back into the action.

Traditional shoot ’em ups often punish skill gaps in cooperative play, leaving less experienced players spectating. This mechanic ensures both players stay engaged.
