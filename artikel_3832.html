In Ashes of Singularity 2, you have to choose between AI Substrate, humanity, or the Post-Human Coalition. This will determine the fate of the solar system as you have to use your strategy to ace the battles.

Command thousands of units in real time as the battle rages on across continents. You can use Strategic Zoom to micromanage the fight and help shape the terrain with your decisions.

In Ashes of Singularity 2, you can play solo or fight against other players. You can enjoy the complete campaign at your own pace and learn more about the main story and its agents.

While Ashes of Singularity 2 is a sequel, you can also enjoy it as a standalone adventure. The second part also includes plenty of QoL features that were absent from the first game that came out in 2016.
