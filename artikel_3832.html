The Great Outdoors quest is one of the first you'll find after leaving your initial area in WoW: Forever, in which you meet an NPC who teaches you about camping.

It's an extremely simple one—you perch your backside in front of a campfire and then wait for a short while as a buff accumulates, teaching you the importance of regularly resting at fires for their passive bonus. Basically, you just:

Simple stuff, but this quest is bugged for some players. When they try to sit, it doesn't register, meaning you can't complete the first stage. I assume Blizzard will be working on a hotfix, but one solution that works for some players is pressing X to sit in front of the fire, then typing /sit into chat. Give it a try and see.

Otherwise, you might have to return to this one later, which is definitely frustrating, as this NPC's follow-up quests introduce you to different aspects of some early-game professions and crafting.
