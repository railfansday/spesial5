Everything to know about Target Black Friday 2026: Store hours, best live deals, ad highlights

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Target's Black Friday 2026 sale is coming in hot, folks. Things kicked off extra early this year, with the first official shopping event of the season on Nov. 5. Now, the official sale is off and running, with plenty more deals on the way.

While most holiday shopping is done online these days, Target still offers that classic Black Friday in-store shopping experience for those who want to soak it all in. The deals will really start to heat up on Black Friday proper, but you can start checking off your lists as soon as today.

With every retailer holding major Black Friday events, it's easy to get your wires crossed. To help you keep all the details straight and get ready to shop at Target on Black Friday 2026, here's everything you need to know, including store hours, what to expect with early Target Circle 360 deals, and what will be on sale.

Black Friday proper falls on Nov. 27 in 2026, but most retailers start dropping deals well before that. Target's first early Black Friday sale kicked off on Nov. 5, with new limited-time deals dropping every weekend throughout the holiday season. The official Black Friday sale is now on and runs Nov. 22-29, with new weekend deals available Nov. 26-29.

Target's Deal of the Day program also returned on Nov. 1. Shoppers can score a new featured deal each day until Dec. 24, with a one-day preview of the offers.

Target is following Walmart's lead again and giving its paid members a head start on some of its earliest discounts this shopping season. Having a Target Circle 360 membership unlocks select deals 24 hours early, with access beginning on Wednesday, Nov. 25 at 3 a.m. ET. That's a full day before Target's Black Friday weekend deals open to everyone on Nov. 26. Target has yet to confirm the deals that will be available on Wednesday, so we'll keep you updated when the discounts drop.

Target has two membership tiers: its paid Target Circle 360 option, and its free Target Circle program. You can pay $99 a year or $10.99 a month to join. That's just $49 a year for Target Circle Card holders. Target also takes more than 50% off for verified teachers, college students, military members, and government assistance recipients.

Joining gets you free shipping and same-day delivery, plus monthly freebies, a 30-day return extension, and early access to select sales, such as Target's Black Friday deals. It's more than worth signing up for if you frequent Target or want to get additional discounts when Black Friday sales kick off.

You can shop Target's Black Friday sale both online and in stores. Most Target stores will open at 6 a.m. local time on Black Friday, Nov. 27. There may be exceptions, so double-check the exact opening hours for your local store. Target stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day.

Target has made waking up early on Black Friday worth it once again, as the first 100 guests in line will receive a Target gift card valued from $25 to $100. One lucky guest will even receive a $1,000 Target gift card. Visit Target.com/BlackFridayGiveaway for official rules and details.

Target has a wide assortment of Black Friday deals, ranging from toys to tech. We expect to see some steep discounts on toys and will definitely be keeping an eye on Lego sets and Pokémon 30th Anniversary products. In years past, we've also seen great deals on small appliances, TVs, headphones, floor care, and gift cards. Standout deals from 2025 included $69 AirPods, $600 off TCL's The Frame TV dupe, 20% off gift cards, and a $49 air fryer. This year, the deals look to run the gamut from some great early Apple offers to several other goodies, like fitness trackers, printers, and more, that you won't want to pass up.
