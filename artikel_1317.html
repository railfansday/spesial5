Zlatan Ibrahimovic Named English Icon He Wished Played in Europe

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Over the years, Zlatan Ibrahimovic earned himself a fearsome reputation. On the pitch, he was never afraid to clash with anyone – including teammates and managers.

In retirement, as a pundit, he's been just as sharp-tongued when appropriate. However, that doesn't mean he's afraid to dish out deserved praise. Indeed, the Swedish icon – who won league titles with Ajax, Inter Milan, Barcelona, AC Milan, and Paris Saint-Germain across his distinguished career – is known for occasionally recognising greatness.

During his playing days, he actually expressed disappointment that one 'fantastic' icon, who just so happened to be one of the best English footballers of all time, never left to play for a club in Europe.

Zlatan felt Ronaldo 'took all the glory' for himself.

The man in question is Steven Gerrard. The Liverpool legend will forever be remembered as one of the greatest midfielders in football history. Most famously, he won the Champions League with the Reds and also earned 114 caps with England.

While many European giants would have loved to have signed Gerrard, he remained committedto Liverpool during his prime years. Ibrahimovic lamented this, once claiming he wanted to see the Englishman play abroad.

In 2012, ahead of the Three Lions' clash with Sweden, with Gerrard set to win his 100th cap, Zlatan spoke about how much respect he had for the midfielder:

"He's a fantastic player, always someone who plays at a high level. He's a good fighter and has good skills also and is strong with the ball.

"He feels more like an international player than a normal English player. Whenever I play against Steven the coach always says, 'Be careful with that player because he can make the difference.'

"He is respected throughout Europe but I would like to see Steven in, of course Liverpool is a big club, but in a big international club. I would like to see that also for England because it is very exciting when someone goes abroad and shows who he is in another competition.

"For me a fantastic player can make a difference in every country, wherever he plays, and I'm pretty sure that Steven would do that also. Of course, he is playing in one of the best leagues in the world now but he's a fantastic player."

Ibrahimovic was also asked to clarify what he considers a 'typical English player' to be, the then-PSG forward replied:

"Someone with a big heart, always fighting, aggressive, who never gives up. For me Steven Gerrard has more international skills than a normal player. England are used to playing 4-4-2 and the big teams in the Premier League are playing 4-3-3 with a lot of foreign players. It is developing like every other competition and every other country. They are a fantastic team with fantastic players."

Sadly for Ibrahimovic, and many football fans, the Swede didn't get his wish. Gerrard would never play for a European club, spending the bulk of his career at Liverpool.

However, the Englishman would leave Anfield in 2015 to spend one season in the United States. Coincidentally, he joined LA Galaxy, the same MLS side Zlatan would also later play for. Their paths did not cross in America, though, with the striker joining Galaxy in 2018, two seasons after Gerrard retired.

Ibrahimovic also came to England for a while, playing for Manchester United from 2016 to 2018. During his time at Old Trafford, he did at least get to share the pitch with another English icon he 'liked more than anyone', Wayne Rooney.
