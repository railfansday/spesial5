Jordan Pickford has been a valiant servant for England. Across his 94 appearances, he's conceded 63 goals and managed 49 clean sheets. Add two World Cup semi-finals and two Euros finals to that list, and there's no denying that he's one of England's best of all time.

Trafford repaid his faith tenfold. It is an exciting time for the Leeds goalkeeper, whose ceiling feels limitless. Pickford will be classy, as he always has been, amid this transition, but it won't be easy for the player who earned his first cap nine years ago under Gareth Southgate.
