Erling Haaland Was Spotted Playing GTA V Before Champions League Final

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Footballers are often big fans of video games. From Grand Theft Auto to FIFA/EA Sports FC, many big names over the years have been known to be avid gamers.

Current Ballon d'Or holder Harry Kane spoke about how playing Fortnite helped him when he was away with England at the 2018 World Cup in Russia, recalling: “As silly as it sounds, playing Fortnite passes the time quite well. There are quite a few of us playing a few games of that.”

Fellow England star Cole Palmer recently got suspended for streaming the new FC27 on Twitch ahead of its agreed embargo time. There are many more footballers who love gaming, and Erling Haaland can be counted among them.

Haaland has hit 36.5 km/h — but how does that compare to Mbappe, Ronaldo, Messi, an NFL sprinter and Usain Bolt himself?

The Manchester City and Norway star is among the best players on the planet right now, and has been for some time. Scoring goal after goal for club and country, there are few more fearsome strikers in the game.

Still only 26, the Man City ace has already won two Premier League titles, two FA Cups, the League Cup and the Champions League with his current team. Undoubtedly, his best season came in 2022/23, when he won the treble under Pep Guardiola.

Across that campaign, the Norwegian scored 52 goals in 53 games for City (finishing second in the Ballon d'Or race). The crowning moment, however, came on 10 June 2023. The English side would beat Inter Milan 1-0 to win the Champions League for the first time in the club's history and seal that aforementioned treble.

It was perhaps the biggest achievement of Haaland's career to date, and yet on the eve of the final, he appeared to be in an incredibly relaxed mood. Indeed, the striker was actually spotted playing GTA V on PlayStation 5.

The final that year was held at the Ataturk Olympic Stadium in Istanbul, Turkey, so Haaland wasn't playing it at home. This may explain why he appeared to be playing the Prologue mission, having clearly just started a new save on the GTA V story mode.

The Man City ace was soon asked to switch games and play FIFA 23 (the franchise's final release under EA Sports), but hilariously shot back:

"I’m in the middle of a heist! I need the cash, so… The police is after me!"

Haaland is evidently a big fan of GTA, having also been once heard whistling the game's soundtrack at the Etihad after beating Napoli in the Champions League

With all that in mind, the Man City star will surely be one of many desperate to play the new Grand Theft Auto VI when it is finally released on Thursday, 19 November. He's actually on the record as saying: "I can’t wait for the new GTA.”

He's not the only star footballer to play video games shortly before a career-defining match. Most notably, Italy's Andrea Pirlo played PlayStation all day before then going on to win that 2006 World Cup final that evening.

Famously, he recounted in his autobiography:

"I don’t feel pressure... I don’t give a toss about it. I spent the afternoon of Sunday, July 9, 2006 in Berlin sleeping and playing the PlayStation. In the evening, I went out and won the World Cup.”

No wonder the iconic midfielder is regarded as one of the coolest footballers to ever grace the sport.
