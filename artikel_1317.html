Nations League Liveblog: Italy vs. France

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from the Nations League, as Italy host Zinedine Zidane’s France for top spot in Group A1.

It kicks off at San Siro at 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET).

Les Bleus lead on 10 points, followed by the Azzurri with seven and Belgium on six going into Matchday 5.

With Italy visiting Brussels on Sunday and France hosting rock bottom Turkiye that same day, it’s all to play for with only the top two reaching the quarter-finals.

For some reason, the big screen at San Siro said Bastoni was booked. It was definitely Frattesi, so no suspension against Belgium.

“Pio was meant to take the penalty. Did they get confused? Well next time they won’t get confused again.”

“It’s a fantastic moment, it was a great performance and wonderful to win here at San Siro,” Scalvini told Sky Sport Italia.

“The coach told us before the game these are nights we’ll remember when we are old, so we knew that we had to make it count.

“France are such a strong side with phenomenal pace, so they came out well, but we stayed united and managed to take control in the second half. I get along great with Bastoni, I always said he was an idol for me, and playing alongside him is fantastic. It doesn’t change much whether we’re in a three-man defence or four.

“We’ve got to enjoy this victory tonight, then from tomorrow we’ll start preparing for the trip to Belgium. We need the right attitude and to be fired up.”

“It makes me very happy, because this is a truly special jersey, the one that we all wear the moment we start kicking the ball, and is the dream of everyone. It is always an honour to wear it and we must prove we deserve to wear it every single time.

“On the penalty, there was an extra responsibility, which was to wipe out the error made by my brother. When I saw it go wide, that was tough for me as well as for him. I had to score and finish the game for him.

“On the sheet, both our names were there. Seba has more penalties converted in his career than anyone else in the squad, it can happen to anyone, then I scored the second.

“When I stepped up, my first thought inevitably was Bosnia, but I couldn’t hide forever. You take penalties, you miss them, it happens, you’ve just got to keep going forward.

“Since the coach arrived, we were still a work in progress against Belgium, but after that we started to really find our feet. We have a mix of youth and experience, we need to keep this enthusiasm going.”

“A great performance, the lads did so well, I am happy for the fans and hope they enjoyed themselves,” Mancini told RAI Sport.

“The goal was an error, unfortunately these things happen. We managed to stay calm, continued playing, and eventually thoroughly deserved the victory.

“There are too many wonderful moments, unfortunately this victory counts to a certain degree, because we need to go and win in Belgium anyway, so football is incredible.

“We have work to do, there is a long path ahead of us, the lads certainly want to make up for everything that has been, and we’re at a good point. Conceding a goal against France after 10 minutes could’ve destroyed us, instead we stayed calm, and after seven or eight minutes, we got back on track.”
