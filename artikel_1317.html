Ben White Names 2 English Stars Who Can Win Ballon d’Or After Kane

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

For the first time since 2001, the current Ballon d'Or winner is an English footballer. That man is Three Lions captain and record goalscorer Harry Kane.

The Bayern Munich star beat the likes of Lamine Yamal and Kylian Mbappe to lift the prize in a London Ceremony at the end of October. Having netted six times for his nation at the 2026 World Cup, Kane finished 2025/26 with a total of 73 goals.

Add to that his wonderful playmaking skills, top-level footballing IQ, and a Bundesliga title, and it's easy to see why the former Tottenham striker lifted the award. Ending a 25-year wait since the last Brit, Michael Owen, won the Ballon d'Or, it will be interesting to see if the next Ballon d'Or winner arrives in a shorter time frame.

On that note, Arsenal and England right-back Ben White was recently asked to name a potential future winner. Speaking to BBC Five Live Sport, he picked two talented stars.

White was quick to suggest Arsenal teammate Bukayo Saka as one of his shouts. He explained:

"Just playing with him, every week, it's something new. What he does is amazing. You don't see anyone with the same mentality as him."

White regularly lines up on the right flank alongside Saka under Mikel Arteta, so he is perhaps best placed to judge the 25-year-old's talent more than anyone else in the world right now. The pair have a great understanding too, as they've shared the pitch 172 times together for club and country.

Sadly, injuries have impacted both players in recent seasons, but they were still vital for the Gunners as they won the Premier League for the first time since 2004 last season.

Saka's best Ballon d'Or finish came in 2024, when he ranked 21st. He also lifted the Socrates Award at the 2026 ceremony for his impressive humanitarian and off-pitch social work.

Tracking the leading contenders to win the prestigious Ballon d'Or award in 2027.

Next up, White named a youngster who is yet to play for England's senior team but looks set to have a very bright international future. Indeed, many expect Max Dowman, already one of the best young players in world football, to be a leading light over the next decade.

Aged 16 years, 10 months and 13 days, Dowman recently eclipsed Jude Bellingham as the youngest player to represent England's U21 team. He's also the youngest to ever win the Premier League and score in the competition.

Unsurprisingly, White has been blown away by the teenager, backing him as a future Ballon d'Or winner:

"Obviously, doing what he's doing at Arsenal is unheard of. So I'd probably put money on him. He's got so much talent it's crazy. Can only imagine what he's going to be like when he's 20 or 21."

With 19 appearances for the Gunners before he's even turned 17, it feels only a matter of time before Dowman wins a call-up from Thomas Tuchel. Once he's a regular in the England set-up, expect Ballon d'Or chat to swiftly follow.
