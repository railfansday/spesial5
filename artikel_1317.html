Former England Star Dele Alli ‘to Earn Huge £7.6 Million Payday’

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Former Tottenham Hotspur and England midfielder Dele Alli is set to earn £7.6 million despite not playing a minute of football over the last 19 months.

Alli, who made 269 appearances for Spurs between 2015 and 2021, last played under Cesc Fabregas for Italian side Como during the 2024-25 season. He made just one appearance for the up-and-coming Serie A side, which came back in March 2025.

The former England international was introduced in the 81st minute of a league game against AC Milan, and his cameo was short but not sweet, as he was sent off after just ten minutes for a reckless foul on ex-Three Lions teammate Ruben Loftus-Cheek.

Como released the player at the end of his first season in northern Italy, and he hasn't been employed by a club since. This lack of football won't, however, prevent the 30-year-old from enjoying a lucrative payday in the near future.

While Alli hasn't officially retired from football and he hasn't publicly declared that he's given up looking for a new club, his attention appears to have shifted towards streaming video games online.

His latest business move certainly suggests that he's preparing for life post-football. According to The Sun, Alli is closing his image rights company - a decision that will see him pocket £7.6 million.

The company has £8.3 million in assets, but also owes a £635,362 corporation tax bill, bringing his take-home sum to around £7.6 million.

Alli put the business into liquidation, with this declaration of solvency allowing the details of the closure to emerge.

These aren't the midfielder's only business ventures. Alli also owns six rental properties worth more than £1.5 million, while he has an investment company as part of his portfolio, called Dele Investments, worth £336,267. These enterprises remain open.

The former starlet has endured a turbulent few years since leaving Tottenham. While his last two years with the Lilywhites marked the beginning of his decline, he was still playing somewhat regularly before his departure from North London.
