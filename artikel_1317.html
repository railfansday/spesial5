PSG fear Doue ACL injury in latest blow ahead of Roma clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Paris Saint-Germain fear Désiré Doue has suffered a serious knee injury, a fresh blow for Luis Enrique days before Roma visit the Parc des Princes in the Champions League.

According to Foot Mercato, the 21-year-old’s right knee gave way while he was shielding the ball, with no contact from an opponent, late in the first half of Saturday’s Ligue 1 game at Nice. He had scored to make it 2-1 only minutes earlier.

Doué left the pitch in tears on a stretcher and was replaced by Ferran Torres. PSG are waiting for scans to confirm how serious the injury is, but Foot01 report that the club fear a ruptured anterior cruciate ligament.

If confirmed, it would be a second major knee injury in barely a month for the European champions. Khvicha Kvaratskhelia ruptured the ACL in his right knee in October’s 4-2 win over Barcelona and is expected to miss most of the season. Ousmane Dembélé and Marquinhos also suffered thigh injuries in that game.

Doué had only just returned from an earlier injury when he joined up with France this month.

PSG are chasing a third consecutive Champions League title, and they host Gian Piero Gasperini’s Roma on Wednesday at 21:00 CET [20:00 GMT]. Whatever the scans show, Doué is very unlikely to face the Giallorossi.
