Why Milan have Endrick advantage over Roma and Arsenal, with €60m offer ready

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Milan are prepared to offer €60m plus add-ons to buy Brazil talent Endrick from Real Madrid in January, claim Italian reports, with one very important advantage over Roma and Arsenal.

It is no secret that these three clubs are interested in picking up Endrick during the January transfer window, as the 20-year-old is frustrated at the lack of regular playing time for the Merengues.

Jose Mourinho is adamant that the youngster should not leave, but considering the loan experience at Olympique Lyonnais last season, the decision might be beyond him.

According to the Corriere dello Sport, there are two elements that put Milan at the very top of the list for the striker.

The first is that while Roma and perhaps Arsenal are only prepared to offer a loan, the Rossoneri can put €60m on the table to purchase Endrick outright, and could add some bonuses on top worth another €10m.

This could even be negotiated to give Real Madrid a buy-back clause set at €80m, giving them the option of controlling his future.

The other big element in this deal is the fact Endrick’s agency is Roc Nation.

Their ties go very deep indeed with Milan, because the new CEO of Milan Sports and Entertainment is Brett Yormark, whose twin brother Michael is President of Roc Nation.
