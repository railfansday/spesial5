Forget Konsa: Aston Villa's Biggest Mistake Was Selling Donyell Malen

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Aston Villa were held to a 0-0 draw against relegation-battling Sunderland on Saturday afternoon, as Unai Emery's side continued their mixed start to the campaign.

The Villans have won just three of their first 11 games in the Premier League this season after the result at Villa Park, leaving them in 11th place in the table, despite having seen the majority of their starting lineup depart during the summer transfer window.

Morgan Rogers joined Chelsea, Youri Tielemans joined Man Utd, Lucas Digne joined Paris Saint-Germain, Ezri Konsa joined Arsenal, Ollie Watkins joined Al-Hilal, and Emi Martinez joined Chelsea, leaving Emery incredibly short of experience and familiarity within his squad.

Emery brought in 10 new players during the summer window to help replace them and strengthen the squad, but the team have struggled to adapt and the results have not been as forthcoming as they would have hoped.

But while Konsa has gone from strength to strength since his move to the Emirates Stadium, establishing himself as one of the best central defenders in the Premier League, the lack of goals in the side has been more of a problem.

Watkins' departure has left a major hole in the side, and while Nicolas Jackson's performances have been largely good, that reliable threat of scoring regularly seems to have gone.

But what is not being spoken about enough is the fact that Aston Villa also allowed Donyell Malen to leave the club permanently in the summer, and he would be the perfect solution to a lot of the issues they are currently having.

The Netherlands international has been in remarkable form since joining AS Roma on an initial loan deal in the January transfer window, scoring 25 goals in Serie A - 12 more than the next-best players in Marcus Thuram and Anastasios Douvikas.

The forward extended his place at the top of that chart with a stunning performance on Saturday night as he scored four goals in their win over Parma, and showed Aston Villa exactly what they are missing in their current side.

“He is strong, inside the area he is devastating.

“He has quickness in his control, in moving the ball, in shooting, and he is composed in front of goal. For us he is an extra weapon. We are keeping him close and we hope he continues like this in these last two matches.”

When Malen signed for Aston Villa, he had a reputation for being a goalscoring winger with great pace, but Emery seemed to never fully trust him, and ultimately his place in the team was never assured.

Now that he has gone over to Italy, he is showing what could have happened if he had been given that trust. He is scoring goals for fun in a league that is best known for its defensive qualities, and is making it look easy.

If Emery had ever given him that opportunity, then it may be that the sale of Watkins wouldn't have been such an issue. They would have already had a reliable source of goals in the squad, and there would be less pressure on Jackson and youngster Brian Madjo to hit the ground running.

The manager allowed several key players to leave in the summer, but it now seems clear that his biggest mistake was letting Malen leave and not replacing him properly.

With key injuries depleting options but no place for Garnacho, Villa contemplate cancelling the loan in January.
