Hojlund criticised for ‘one club in Manchester’ celebration in 2-2 Man City draw

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Rasmus Hojlund joked that there is ‘only one club in Manchester’ after netting against Manchester City in Napoli’s 2-2 Champions League draw at The Etihad on Tuesday evening, but his comments were not well received by pundits on CBS, who criticised the Dane for his ‘poor timing’: ‘He’s playing for Napoli, who by the way are in absolute shambles’.

Hojlund opened the scoring on his return to Manchester in an eventual 2-2 draw away against the Premier League heavyweights on Tuesday evening. Napoli were widely praised by the Italian press for their performance on the night, and despite coming into the tie as underdogs, Napoli were just a few minutes away from holding out for the full three points.

Hojlund joked after full-time that there is ‘only one club in Manchester’, nodding back to his time with Manchester United.

“I was really happy for my goal, the fans were jeering me, so I was saying there’s only one club in Manchester,” said the former United striker in his post-match interview after full-time.

Those comments did not go down well with pundits in the CBS Studio, who criticised the Dane for his lack of judgement.

“He’s playing for Napoli, who by the way are in absolute shambles,” said Mike Grella after watching Hojlund’s post-match interview.

“Second year on the turn they might not qualify for the knock-out stages. They did not win the game tonight. 36 teams, 24 go through, 12 of them get eliminated. There’s that.

“You’re also wearing the Napoli badge, they’re paying your stub at the moment and you’re talking about the time you spent at Manchester United, which, by the way, was another shambolic moment for you and Manchester United.

“Look, I think he’s trying to be harmless and funny with that, but man, really poor timing and really not reading the room.”

Troy Deeney added: “Irrelevant of all of that, they got rid of you. They got rid of you because you’re not good enough. You’re at a massive club. You’re not playing for Mickey Mouse FC down the road, you’re playing for Napoli, a huge club with history.

You’ve not only infuriated the rest of us, you’ve infuriated Napoli fans, who are now gonna go this weekend, after you’ve come out and questioned the manager’s tactics last time out in the Champions League and now you’re saying that’s your club, off you pop mate.”

There has also been criticism of the criticism.

Some on social media labelled the comments from the CBS pundits as ‘stupid’. “Troy Deeney, the greatest sportsman who never played for Man Utd or Napoli. Never got close to them either. Don’t even know who the others are,” wrote one supporter on X.

“Done nothing wrong! Can’t question his commitment to Napoli! They have had a drop off this season but he generally has been a very good servant to Napoli regardless of his personal football allegiances,” another added.

One Manchester United supporter commented: “Chill out lads, he’s a long time United fan scoring against a rival. He didn’t mean “that’s my club right now” and have you got any other languages between you?? How’s your Danish?”
