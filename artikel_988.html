Porto rally around defender that was left in tears by horror own goal

Porto have rallied around Francisco Moura after the player scored a horror own goal in his side’s Portuguese Cup tie on Friday, which reduced him to tears.

The 27-year-old defender passed the ball into his own net in the 57th minute of the fourth-round tie against third-division side Paredes, inexplicably kicking it into the bottom left corner after a save by his goalkeeper.

Moura dropped straight to the ground with his hands over his face, covering his head with his jersey as he started crying. He then signalled to the bench that he wanted to be substituted.

Moura, who received support from both sets of players following the blunder, was not immediately substituted and later came off in the 70th minute.

The episode prompted a call for support for Moura during Porto's general assembly on Saturday, which drew applause from fellow members.

“Members took the opportunity to express their vote of confidence in Francisco Moura,” Porto's general assembly chairman Antonio Tavares said.

"Even though things sometimes go wrong, the members made it clear today that they fully understand the situation and stand in solidarity with him, sending him a warm embrace and wishing him success.”

Club president Andre Villas-Boas also endorsed the call for support.

The cup tie finished 1-1 after extra time, with Porto eventually overcoming Paredes on penalties.

Moura had previously faced abuse on social media. He restricted comments on Instagram after conceding a late handball penalty that led to Sporting Lisbon’s equaliser in a league game in February, then temporarily deactivated his account following a draw at Benfica in March.
