Mardle: The Worlds is now the 'incentive' for Littler

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Sky Sports Darts' Wayne Mardle believes Luke Littler has to use winning the World Darts Championship as his motivation following his Grand Slam of Darts loss; you can watch the Grand Slam of Darts in Wolverhampton live on Sky Sports until November 22

Luke Littler suffered a shock 10-8 defeat to Jermaine Wattimena at the last 16 stage of the Grand Slam of Darts and Sky Sports Darts' Wayne Mardle believes the world No 1 now has to throw all his energy into being ready for the World Darts Championship.

Littler was attempting to become the third player ever to win the Wolverhampton event three times in a row alongside Phil Taylor and Michael van Gerwen.

However, he came up against an incredible performance from Wattimena, in which he was hitting T20s for fun and averaged over 101.

With Littler also missing the Players Championship, which is the last major before Alexandra Palace in December, the next time we will see Littler on the TV stage is when he plays his opening Worlds match on Friday December 11 as the reigning champion, live on Sky Sports.

Littler went into 2026 targeting a clean sweep of majors and while he won the Premier League, Masters, UK Open, and World Matchplay, his loss in the second round at the World Series Finals to Dirk van Duijvenbode has started a run without a major win.

He has since not defended his World Grand Prix title, Grand Slam title, or clinched the European Championship, which has eluded him.

For Mardle, Littler has to put everything else to one side, use his time away to get fresh, practice, and win the major that just has something a bit different to the rest.

"There is no doubt that every player gets more motivated. The Worlds is different," Mardle said.

"The run-up is different and the build-up to it is gigantic.

"He went basically nine months into the year holding this belief and everyone else thinking he's going to do the clean sweep.

"So it was nine months of all this and then it ends, but the Worlds is out on its own. The incentive is that it is the Worlds. That is all the incentive you need. It is the biggest and it is the best event.

"If he loses in that then he will have gone four months without a major.

"The Worlds is the one, and Michael van Gerwen said this for years: what is the point in winning anything else? You only need to win this one.

"I think that is just the same with Luke's mentality."

The talk has already turned to whether Littler's time away from the TV spotlight will be a positive or a negative as he attempts to become the second player ever, alongside Taylor, to have won the World Darts Championship three or more times in a row.

Mardle insists that, while it would be better for Littler to be heading into the Worlds as a potential Players Championship winner after his loss at the Grand Slam, if he brings his A-game, it is his to lose.

"I don't know how he really feels about losing here at the Grand Slam but what I do think going forward, because he's not playing in the Players Championship, so his next outing will be at the Worlds.

"This is the way that I would frame it - would he be better not playing, or would he be better winning the Players Championship? You are better winning the Players Championship.

"But that is the kind of situation that you don't know. You don't know if he goes to the Players Championship, wins it or loses.

"If he loses, it is like maybe he shouldn't have gone to that, in hindsight and everything else.

"I really believe for Luke, if he can turn up fresh, if he can turn up ready, if he can turn up with anywhere near his A-game, he probably wins the Worlds again. That will be three times on the bounce by the age of 20, which is ridiculous."

Wattimena picked up the biggest win of his career against Littler at the Grand Slam but it is not the first time he has come out on top against 'The Nuke' as he beat him in August on the way to winning a Players Championship title.

Wattimena said that win gave him confidence and reinforced that Littler is "not a robot" and Mardle believes that win can be credited for the performance we saw from the Dutchman in the Grand Slam.

"I know this is a thing. I was about when Phil Taylor was hardly ever beaten and until you actually beat him, you don't believe you can," he said.

"You see others beat him, there were loads throughout the years. There was Andy Callaby. Adrian Gray beat him. There were others that beat him that were not as good as me.

"I remember thinking why can't I ever beat Phil? It is not until you do that you know that someone's not a robot.

"So that win was massive for Jermaine and it was one of the reasons why he won yesterday.

"Now going forward after yesterday, he can beat anyone on that stage. Luke Humphries has to watch out if he plays Jermaine. Gerwyn Price, they should all watch out now.

"It is really important to have belief. Now he has got it and he has got the ability."
