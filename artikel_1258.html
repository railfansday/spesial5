Mauricio Pochettino warns Man United cannot be stuck in the past as he sets out his vision for the club - and confirms Bruno Fernandes WILL remain captain amid contract stand-off

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Published: 04:01 EST, 20 November 2026 | Updated: 13:06 EST, 20 November 2026

Mauricio Pochettino has warned that Manchester United cannot be stuck in the past as he begins the job of dragging the biggest club in English football out of the Premier League relegation zone.

Pochettino faces a huge first game in charge against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday, and he is determined to make a fresh start at United uninhibited by the history of the club.

‘We arrived with the idea to create something from scratch, from our own ideas and philosophy, in the way that we love to work. I think our job is to adapt and to go ahead a little bit,’ he said at Carrington.

‘The culture of the club and the identity of the club is very clear. It's an historic club with years being one of the best clubs in the world. It's difficult to find another club the size of Manchester United. It needs to be in a different place than we are now, no?

‘But I don't think too much in the past. If we want to create something different and positive, and the people feel proud about that, it's about building our own way here. Thinking on what happened yesterday is not going to help. You cannot be stuck in the past.’

Pochettino’s appointment at United has been a whirlwind after the Argentine oversaw his final two games in charge of the US national team before flying through the night to start work on Wednesday.

‘I think it's a race to arrive in condition to be competitive against Liverpool, and now our focus is 100 per cent on Liverpool,’ he added.

‘My first target is to sleep, but I cannot sleep – only three or four hours per day – but I think the target is to know the people, the staff, all the people here in the club, and of course the players and try from the beginning to have a positive impact.’

Pochettino admits he has missed being involved in day-to-day coaching at club level, saying: ‘Of course, we missed it a lot. We are animals of the grass. We like to feel the grass, we need to smell the grass and to help the players to improve every day.

‘It's true that it's a different job when you are in the national team, but I think we are much better coaches now.’

The 54-year-old told United that he wants to be involved in the all the key decision-making and transfers despite only being named as the club’s head coach.

Pochettino demanded more control during his five years in charge of Tottenham, and the difference between manager and head coach has also been a bone of contention at United in recent years.

Manchester United travel to rivals Liverpool on Sunday searching for their first league win since August

Ruben Amorim was sacked after taking on the United hierarchy over his job title, while Erik ten Hag refused to give up his power to veto transfers.

Pochettino said: ‘I know that being a head coach, I am the guy that is going to decide the squad for Sunday and I'm going to pick the starting 11.

‘I'm going to make the changes and of course that, in the future, I would like to be involved in all the main decisions that will happen in the squad. That is (why) in the end, Manchester United is a football club, no?

‘During all my (career), when I started at Espanyol, head coach, coach, manager. In the end, it's not changing too much because I think the way that we work is indifferent to the title description.

‘If you are manager it’s not necessarily that you have all the decisions. Maybe you are a coach and have more power in the decision.’

Pochettino revealed that he met most of the players individually at Carrington on Thursday, including captain Bruno Fernandes who cut a frustrated figure towards the end of Michael Carrick’s time in charge.

Pochettino confirmed that out-of-form Bruno Fernandes will remain as United captain

‘Bruno is our captain and I think he's a very important player, but all the players need to feel that they are important from the top to the bottom,’ said Pochettino.

‘Yesterday we spent three hours chatting in an individual way in my office with all the players.’

Concerns have been raised over the players’ fitness and attitude under Carrick this season, with the statistics suggesting that United have not been running enough.

‘That's a good question, no?,’ said Pochettino. ‘It’s a debate that I don't like to go into because I think we cannot talk about things without context, and data is about context. I think it's very dangerous to talk. We are here not to denounce things, we are here to build something.’

Pochettino later shared a message on Instagram thanking supporters for their 'warm welcome' and declared the Red Devils 'cannot afford to waste a moment'.

'An intense first few days. Thank you to everyone for a warm welcome,' the Argentine wrote.

'We cannot afford to waste a moment. All our energy and attention is focused on preparing for Sunday.

'We can say many things, but what matters is the work we do and what we show on the pitch.'
