Video: Varela scores cheeky backheel flick to make it 11 goals in 11 Serie A games

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The in-form Gustavo Varela notched his 11th Serie A goal on his 11th league appearance for Monza on Monday evening with an audacious backheel flick to send his side 2-0 up in an eventual 3-0 victory over Fiorentina.

The 21-year-old, who is already attracting transfer interest from some of Serie A’s biggest clubs, is second behind Roma’s Donyell Malen (12) for league goals in 2026-27 thanks to his brace against Fiorentina on Monday evening.

The recently-called-up Portugal international won and converted a penalty at the end of the first-half to send his side into a 1-0 lead against Fiorentina, before finishing off a swift attacking move with a cheeky backheel flick in one of the late contenders for the Serie A goal of the month.

With cup figures included, Varela is now up to 13 goals from 12 competitive appearances since joining Monza from Benfica over the summer.
