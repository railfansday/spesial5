Champions League Liveblog: Man City vs. Napoli, Como vs. AEK Athens

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from the Champions League, as Napoli and Kevin De Bruyne visit Manchester City, while Como take on AEK Athens.

Both fixtures kick off at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET) in Matchday 5 of 8 in this league phase.

While Como are in fourth place after three victories and one defeat, Napoli risk early elimination following just one win and three losses so far.

This is a special game for De Bruyne against his former club, especially as last season he had to leave the field early following Giovanni Di Lorenzo’s red card on 21 minutes.

Enzo Maresca explained some of his surprising Manchester City tactical choices in the 2-2 draw with Napoli, and confessed ‘the main problem is we are conceding too many goals.’

Cesc Fabregas feels Como really made a Champions League statement in their 1-0 victory over AEK Athens, and jokes that they can ‘sell Luis Milla for €20m in January’ after his goal.

Rasmus Hojlund reveals what he said to the Manchester City fans after scoring for Napoli in the Champions League, harking back to his United past. ‘We’re coming back.’

Max Allegri admits Napoli did something ‘really well for the first time’ in the 2-2 draw with Manchester City to keep Champions League ambition alive, and assures Kevin De Bruyne ‘will remain until June.’
