Phasmophobia's next update lets you ask ghosts to throw things and it's out next week

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Phasmophobia's next update, Resonance, arrives on 24th November, bringing a brand-new piece of ghost-hunting kit, more voice commands, and a move to the Unity 6 engine.

The headline addition is dowsing rods, which will help you track down the bone, making them particularly handy on bigger maps like Brownstone High School. As with the rest of the game's equipment, the higher the Tier, the more accurate the reading, and Tier 3 Dowsing Rods will pinpoint the exact floor the Bone is on.

The Sound Sensor is getting an overhaul too, with a reworked interface to help you work out where sounds are coming from. Icons will show whether a sound is ongoing or a one-off, and you'll be able to use the map to identify ghosts by how fast they move.

You'll also be able to ask ghosts to interact with doors and switches, and even throw nearby objects, with a list of usable phrases in the Journal. If you're tired of the same maps, you'll be able to build your own pool of maps to randomise from when picking a contract, and new buttons let you quickly pick small, medium, or large maps.

"We want to ensure every item in our Ghost Hunters' kits can add as much value as possible in investigations," said Daniel Knight, CEO and game director at Kinetic Games.

"Resonance also brings Phasmophobia's move to Unity 6 - while this transition is a technical upgrade first and foremost, it has allowed us to implement major lighting improvements to make the game even more immersive."

Alongside more realistic lighting and better stability, the switch to Unity 6 also brings expanded graphics and quality options, VR Eye Adaptation, and a suite of colour-blindness settings.

Resonance will also fix several prominent bugs, and introduce a conduct warning system that alerts players before they're banned, except in cases of severe violations.

Phasmophobia is also adding Xbox Play Anywhere support, so if you own the Xbox digital edition, you'll be able to play on Xbox Series X/S, PC, and devices like the ROG Ally.

Resonance is a free update and will be available on all platforms.

Phasmophobia is available in Early Access on Steam, PS5, PS VR2, and Xbox Series X/S. It's also coming to Nintendo Switch 2, although we still don't quite know when.

Earlier this month, the Phasmophobia community raised more than $230,000 (around £173,000) for the American Heart Association, more than doubling last year's total.

Earlier this year, Kinetic Games confirmed Phasmophobia's 1.0 launch had slipped from this year into 2027, as the studio said it was prioritising quality over speed following a rocky reception to its Player Character Update. The studio ended up apologising to players for issues with the long-awaited update, admitting it "missed the mark" and "didn't deliver on our promises to you".
