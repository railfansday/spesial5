Musiala expected to make Bayern squad return against Köln

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Bayern Munich are set to receive a notable boost for their Bundesliga encounter with 1. FC Köln on Friday

According to Sport1, Jamal Musiala is expected to return to the matchday squad following the international break, provided he suffers no setbacks during this week.

The 23-year-old has been sidelined since picking up a thigh injury during the warm-up ahead of Germany's 1-1 draw with the Netherlands on September 24.

After missing the start of the season due to a neurological disorder, Musiala has made just four appearances for Bayern this term, scoring twice.
