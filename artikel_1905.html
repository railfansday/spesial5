Bradford City team news: Loanee pushing for a place with key figure set to return

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The 20-year-old midfielder, on loan from Queens Park Rangers, is yet to start for Bradford City in league football but impressed in their last two matches, at home to Marine and York City in the FA Cup and Football League Trophy.

Those games have brought Morgan and Stephen Humphreys, who found the net against York, into Duff’s thoughts for Saturday, when Josh Neufville is set to make his return from injury.

"He's played 50, 60 games in the Championship at 20 years-old," noted manager Duff of Morgan. "So he can handle the ball, but it's getting him to understand different roles.

"He hadn't played many minutes before we (Duff and his management team) came in (in October).

"There's going to be a lot of games (in the final weeks of 2026) and a lot of turnover. Everyone's going to be needed.

"So these people are all going to be a valuable part of the squad."

Wing-back Neufville is yet to play for Duff due to a hamstring injury, and his manager revealed he could have come back earlier.

"Josh is an explosive athlete," he said. "He could have played in the York game but we thought the extra two weeks’ training, which you don't often get, (would benefit him).

"Whether he starts or not, it's another conversation but he's under consideration.

"Can he play 90 minutes? Very unlikely. But can you get 50, 60 minutes out of him there? These are conversations that go on."

Ibou Touray is due back in training on Friday after representing Ghana this month and will therefore have to be assessed, but having already resumed training, goalkeeper Jon McCracken will almost certainly play.
