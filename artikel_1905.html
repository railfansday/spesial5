Luca Netz eyes Nottingham Forest revival as teammate's injury fears emerge - verdict

Luca Netz will be eyeing a Nottingham Forest revival, with a possible chance emerging following injury fears over Neco Williams.

The Forest defender was withdrawn early against Norway, with the Welshman seen limping heavily.

Williams has been a consistent and key performer for Forest, featuring in all 10 Premier League games, and only failing to play 90 minutes once across those.

Oliver Glasner will be sweating on the defender's fitness ahead of the return of domestic football.

Elsewhere in the squad, Netz has been rarely used by Glasner, and truthfully, since he arrived in the 2026 winter window.

But Netz may have to come into the side, as Williams' impressive display against Norway came to an end early.

Nottingham Forest will be hoping Williams is OK, but his heavy limping was not a great sign against Norway.

Williams, despite being 2-0 down to Norway, was having quite a good game against the Scandinavians.

The Nottingham Forest ace completed 91 per cent of his passing, and defensively had seven contributions as per FotMob.

These were made up of four tackles, two interceptions and one clearance, while he also managed to routinely come out on top in his duels.

He won 71 per cent of his ground duels (five out of seven) across his 72-minute performance.

This season, Williams has been used as a left-sided defender, and that is a position Forest do not have many natural options in.

It is safe to say that Netz hasn't exactly impressed Glasner at the City Ground, with the German left-back only making five appearances totalling 58 minutes.

The 23-year-old is clearly out of the manager's plans, but the latest with Williams could hand him a chance.

Of course, Forest are stacked in the wingback position, with Ola Aina also capable of filling in at left wingback.

But Netz is a natural left-footer, which certainly is an advantage for him.

While he also didn't feature much in 2025-26, there is at least enough data for his stats to emerge.

Netz ended the season in the 73rd percentile for expected assists per 90, and with more game time, perhaps he could have more influence.

Additionally, Netz' 0.11 xA per 90 is not too dissimilar to Williams' 0.12 from this season.

But, that will ultimately be up to Glasner to make a decision on.
