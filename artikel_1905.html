Home James! Stone and Waters are back at Saffrons

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The pair, who played influential roles in Town's Southern Combination League play-off triumph, return to Jude Macdonald's squad following spells away from the club.

Stone's return adds proven firepower to Town's attacking options, with the forward having scored 20 goals in 47 appearances during the 2024/25 campaign. His goalscoring exploits helped Town establish themselves back in the Isthmian League South East Division following a ten-year absence, as Macdonald's side enjoyed an impressive first season back at Step 4.

Stone is also closing in on a personal milestone, having made 91 appearances for the club, leaving him just nine short of a century in a Town shirt.

Meanwhile, Waters' return sees Macdonald reunited with a player who has been central to his tenure at The Saffrons. The midfielder holds the distinction of being Macdonald's first signing following his appointment as manager in the summer of 2022, going on to become a regular fixture in the side over the following seasons.

Waters made 123 appearances and scored 23 goals during his first spell with Town, playing an important role in the club's rise from the Southern Combination Premier Division to the Isthmian League.

He departed for Haywards Heath Town earlier this summer but now returns to The Saffrons, renewing his association with the manager under whom he enjoyed considerable success.

Both players featured in the Town squad that secured promotion through the play-offs in May 2024, ending the club's decade-long absence from the Isthmian League. Stone scored Town's second goal in the final.

Their return brings valuable experience of Macdonald's playing style and the demands of Step 4 football, as Town look to improve their position in the South East Division after a tough start.

For Stone, the move offers a chance to rediscover the goalscoring form that made him one of Town's most effective attacking players and return to his boyhood club, while Waters will look to resume the most promising spell of his career at the club.

Town are at home to Whitstable on Saturday.
