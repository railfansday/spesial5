Sources: Liverpool leapfrog London club in Ismael Ganiou pursuit

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Liverpool have joined the list of clubs keen on signing Ismaelo Ganiou, sources have told Football Insider.

The 21-year-old has attracted significant attention with his performances for Lens in Ligue 1 after breaking through last season.

He was rewarded with a new contract, putting pen to paper in August on a deal that is set to run until 2030.

As revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Crystal Palace had been eager to sign Ganiou in the summer transfer window.

Pierre Sage had already quit Lens for the Eagles, and it was hoped that the young defender would be willing to follow his former manager.

But the France youth international, who has also been capped by Burkina Faso, ultimately turned down Sage, who was sacked by Palace this week.

It has been a large transition at centre-back for Palace over the last year, with both Marc Guehi and Maxence Lacroix leaving the club.

The Eagles would bring in around £72million for the pair, but opted against spending any transfer fee on a centre-back in the summer transfer window.

Instead, Palace signed former Arsenal man Takehiro Tomiyasu on a free transfer alongside loan moves for Axel Disasi and Honest Ahanor.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, Callum O'Connell explained: "I think that’ll [Liverpool's interest] be sort of frustrating news for anybody following Crystal Palace.

"Pierre Sage wanted to bring him in during the summer, wanted to bring him to Palace with him.

"Palace eventually missed out on that. They were waiting to sell Maxence Lacroix, and that didn’t go through maybe as soon as they’d hoped.

"Ganiou eventually signed that new contract at Lens. So one that they’ve missed out on, one that got away really."

It appears that Thomas Frank will be the man chosen to replace Sage at Crystal Palace.

Fixing the defence will be one of the Dane's first priorities, with the Eagles bottom of the Premier League after conceding 24 goals in 10 games - more than anybody else.

With the internal solutions lacking, decision-makers at Selhurst Park could turn to the January transfer window to back Frank.

They will be well aware just how financially damaging relegation will be, more than the cost of any potential signings.
