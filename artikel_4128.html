Xbox's New PC App Update Is Impressive, Even If There's A Few Teething Problems

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Just yesterday, Microsoft began rolling out an update for the Xbox PC app, making it faster and more responsive on Windows desktop and handheld. We've had a little play with the new version, and our first impressions are mostly positive.

Xbox boss Asha Sharma posted a short video showing off the boot speed of the revamped Xbox PC app. It took a mere 5.2 seconds to load into the home page, clocking in a full 10 seconds quicker than the current version.

Having tried the new Xbox PC app ourselves, we can confirm that it's noticeably faster than the current version. It opens wonderfully swift on my Windows 10 desktop, stocked with 64GB of Sam Altman's precious FU-RAM, but it's also pretty nippy on my first-gen ASUS ROG Ally.

It was important to me that this new version worked well on my ROG Ally, because the current one takes an age to boot in the handheld's 15W Windows mode. I've never enjoyed that feeling of being forced to switch the ROG Ally into Performance or Turbo mode just to get the Xbox PC app running. Now, I don't have to. Big W for this update!

Beyond the speed improvements, the new app looks so much cleaner and feels much more in line with the Xbox console experience, which has been something I've always hoped we'd see: a kinda merger between the PC and Xbox console interfaces, with the latter serving as more of a base.

The side bar is much slicker too. Social stuff like who's online and what they're up to now display alongside Home, Library, Game Pass, and Store options. Cloud Gaming has been removed from the left-hand bar though, which made it a little awkward to access.

I found launching a game via the cloud a bit confusing. Almost like it's been hidden on purpose. I expected to be able to head to a cloud-enabled title, open its game page, and find the button to launch it via the cloud. Just like before. However, there's nothing to select. It simply says "I own this". Instead, I had to head back to the library page, hit Select, open the submenu, and choose "Play on Cloud". It's handy, but took a little searching to get working.

Remote Play has also been tucked away in a dropdown menu for some reason. I'd much rather see it on the left, alongside the other main tab options. I reckon with a bit more time the new Xbox PC app will be fantastic, and with a couple of updates it should work out some of the kinks I mentioned above.

So far I think the app update is really slick, very fast, and a much nicer experience than the current version. It's a good step in the right direction, and that seems to be the general consensus among Xbox users online, too:
