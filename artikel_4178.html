Grand Theft Auto VI Shatters Records, Making $3.79 Billion In 24 Hours As The Biggest Entertainment Launch Ever

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Grand Theft Auto VI dropped yesterday to massive success, and while we expected it to do well, hearing the numbers behind the historic launch day is still staggering. According to a statement obtained by Bloomberg's Jason Schreier and IGN, Rockstar has confirmed that the open-world juggernaut made $3.79 billion in 24 hours. This makes it not just the biggest video game launch of all time – it's the biggest entertainment launch of all time, and it's not especially close.

Schreier goes on to confirm that much of that money comes from GTA VI's record-breaking pre-orders. It turns out, there were 32.5 million pre-sales, which is a truly mind-bending number. For context, its predecessor, Grand Theft Auto V, currently the second best-selling game of all time, sold 11.21 million copies in the first 24 hours – GTAVI nearly triples that figure. In pre-orders alone, the game has become the 30th best-selling game of all time, with more copies sold in the first day than Elden Ring and Super Mario Odyssey have sold, period.

In terms of the dollar amount, GTAVI's $3.79 billion in 24 hours is not just more money than any film has made in its opening weekend; it's more money than any movie has ever made in the box office – even Avengers: Endgame, which currently holds the highest worldwide gross, is still shy of $3 billion after several successful theatrical runs.

Grand Theft Auto VI also set the record on Metacritic for the highest-reviewed game of all time (with an average critic score of 99), and it headlined this week's rollout of the Game Awards 2026 nominees, with more nominations than any other game. We also loved it, and our editor-in-chief Matt Miller scored it a perfect 10.
