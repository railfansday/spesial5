MTG Is Getting Its Own Animated Netflix Show With Planeswalker

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Magic: The Gathering has been in talks for years about getting an animated debut in addition to the upcoming live-action movie, but despite rumors that the project had been canceled back in 2024, a recent reveal at this year's MagicCon Atlanta show says otherwise.

Towards the end of the preview panel, it was revealed that an animated series in partnership with Netflix is not only in the works, but is already well into production and will be released sometime in 2027, as noted in the reveal trailer on Netflix's official YouTube channel.

The official reveal of MTG's "epic fantasy saga" Planeswalker wasn't just a quick teaser either, as the Sneak Peek showed behind-the-scenes details of its creators bringing the show to life, alongside several pieces of concept art and early development footage for players to pick apart.

Fans can also rest assured that the Wizards story team behind Magic: The Gathering's lore has also been a part of the production of Netflix's Planeswalker from the very start, which will follow the struggle for power between powerful mages starring the Planeswalker Chandra alongside several other iconic characters from the trading card game.

For longtime fans of the franchise, it's been a long time coming.

Aside from the Wizards and Hasbro Entertainment teams, MTG's Planeswalker will also be headed by VisionQuest and Star Trek: Picard creator Terry Matalas as the showrunner, alongside numerous creatives like Cindy Appel and Patrick Osborne as leads on the project.

While the announcement of an official MTG show with a similar animation style as League of Legends' massively popular Arcane series is more than fans could ask for, that's far from everything revealed at MagicCon this year either, with some new books and card previews for the upcoming Nauctis set to look forward to.

Combined with the upcoming live-action film, which has already found one of its first starring actors with Maya Hawke, the world of Magic: The Gathering is about to get even bigger than ever.
