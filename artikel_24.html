Madagascar's near-total abortion ban puts women's lives at risk

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

November 25 marked the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women. Activists argue that tackling gender-based violence also means addressing the wider inequalities that put women at risk, including restrictions on reproductive rights. In Madagascar, abortion is almost entirely banned, even in cases of rape, incest or when a woman's life is at risk. FRANCE 24’s Aurélie Kouman reports on the consequences of the country's strict laws, which force thousands of women to seek unsafe, clandestine procedures.
