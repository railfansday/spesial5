UK’s Burnham on a mission to build public support EU reset

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

While he insists it would be unthinkable to rejoin the bloc without another referendum, British PM Andy Burnham has nevertheless reopened the debate over Brexit. After striking a deal that brings the UK and the EU closer on issues including food export control standards, youth mobility and carbon emissions, he's set to discuss the future of the relationship with Brussels in the coming weeks and will present the results of the talks in December. It comes despite criticism from a host of former Conservative prime ministers.
