Khloé Kardashian Says Lamar Odom Is ‘Dead’ to Her After His Comments About Marrying for Family’s ‘Lifestyle’—Inside Their Former Marital Mansion

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Khloé Kardashian is drawing a line through her relationship with her ex-husband, Lamar Odom.

On the Nov. 11 episode of “The Kardashians,” Kardashian got candid about what the former NBA star  said regarding their marriage in the Netflix documentary "Untold: The Death and Life of Lamar Odom."

“I did watch the Lamar documentary. I’m not too thrilled about it,” she said.

The mom of two, who was involved in the making of the documentary, previously expressed her “regret” in doing so on the April 15 episode of her podcast, “Khloé in Wonder Land.”

“I’m watching the documentary, and I’m like, 'Wait, I took time out of my life to sit here and do a favor for you,'” she continued on the family’s reality show. “I’m not being paid. I’m doing this ‘cause I’m trying to be nice.”

Kardashian found “many things” Odom said in the documentary to be “really distasteful.”

“You’re saying that you weren’t really in love with me, you married me just so you could be more popular in pop culture, all these things, which is so weird,” she explained. “Even if that is your truth, why don’t you say it without me being on the other side of these interviews? I just thought that was really, really lame.”

In the documentary, Odom said he found the Kardashian family’s “lifestyle” to be enticing. The couple married in 2009 just 30 days after meeting.

According to comments Odom’s friend Anthony “Pumpkin” Booker made in the documentary, on the day of the former couple’s wedding, the basketball player said he was “doing it to try to better his future” because the family had “mad connections.”

The Good American co-founder didn’t hold back when speaking about where she stands with Odom today.

“The bridge is burned, and there’s no more rope left to throw over. So he is dead to me,” she said. “This just solidifies what liars, con artists, cheats, thieves, trolls that men are. I just don’t want to be involved. Play with yourself, don’t play with me.”

Odom and Kardashian filed for divorce in 2013, citing “irreconcilable differences.” It was not finalized until 2016, as Odom had a near-fatal overdose in October 2015, prompting Kardashian to put the legal process on pause.

“He OD’d during the divorce, and I was his next of kin, even though it was still, the divorce was still—it was on the judge’s desk,” she said during a 2019 podcast appearance. “It was like, two years, or like a year or two of us trying to get the divorce going, and then this happened.”

She explained that the pause was “not for any romantic reasons.” It was so she could continue to “help take care of him and make sure that he would be OK again.”

After the split, it was reported that Kardashian kept the couple’s marital mansion, which was located in an upscale guard-gated community called Mulholland Estates in the Los Angeles neighborhood of Tarzana.

The former couple purchased the home for $3.95 million in December 2009, about two months after their wedding day.

The six-bedroom, 8.5-bathroom, Mediterranean mansion was heavily featured on both “Keeping Up With the Kardashians” and their spinoff, “Khloé and Lamar.”

All six of the en suite bathrooms were embellished with designer wallpaper, tumbled stone, mosaic accents, and custom closets. The primary suite also had a spalike bathroom and boasted expansive views of the city.

The 8,400-square-foot estate was equipped with all of the amenities you’d expect from a home of an NBA pro and a Kardashian: a home gym, a library, an office, and a theater room, among other luxuries.

Outside, a resort-like pool included stunning water features and was accompanied by fire pits and a spa.

The home was sold for $5.49 million in January 2014, with “The Big Bang Theory” star Kaley Cuoco snapping it up for nearly $1.5 million over the asking price.

Mulholland Estates wasn’t the only place Odom and Kardashian called home during their marriage. After Odom was traded to the Dallas Mavericks in 2012, they rented a posh condo at W Dallas Victory Hotel and Residences.

The $7,500-a-month dwelling had two bedrooms and two bathrooms and featured slate flooring, a wet bar, and bathrooms tiled with mother of pearl.

The building had amenities that included two fitness centers, a spa, an entertainment room, and a zero-edge infinity pool.

Following her separation from Odom, Kardashian purchased a $7.2 million Calabasas home in June 2014. The 9,214-square-foot estate was the former home of Justin Bieber.

Today, Kardashian lives in a stunning $17 million custom-built abode in Hidden Hills, CA, with the two children she shares with ex-boyfriend Tristan Thompson. The property is located right next door to her mother, Kris Jenner.
