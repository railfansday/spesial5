Assassins’s Creed Codename Mars Leaked, Set In Ancient Rome

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

We Might Be Waiting A Bit For Codename Mars

Almost immediately after we published this article, Insider Gaming shared a report backing up Codename Mars and reporting that Assassin's Creed Hexe has been internally delayed. With it now suggested to launch in 2028, that very likely means that the Ancient Rome game is even further down the line.

A reliable Ubisoft insider has reported that two new Assassin's Creed games are in the works, including Codename Mars that will be set in Ancient Rome.

Considering it wasn't that long ago that Ubisoft came out and revealed two massive mainline Assassin's Creed games in a row, a remake of one of the most popular entries, and a bunch of spinoffs, it's weird how little we currently know about the future of the series. All that's been outright confirmed is the awesome-sounding Assassin's Creed Hexe, the Netflix show, and some mysterious side projects.

Of course, there's no doubt that the studio is already hard at work on whatever big game in the series comes after Hexe and the recently wrapped-up Black Flag remake, but we don't have any idea what it could be. That's just changed thanks to a reliable Ubisoft insider who claims that two more games are in the works, including a mainline entry that sounds pretty massive.

As pointed out by Redditor Gyatto- on the GamingLeaksAndRumours subreddit, reliable Ubisoft and Assassin's Creed insider xj0nathan recently shared a video that details two upcoming games in the series. The first, and undeniably the biggest, is a project that goes by the codename "Mars", which a reliable source tells him is set in Ancient Rome.

Despite the weight of the world on its shoulders, Assassin's Creed Shadows is a great entry in the series with one of its best worlds to date.

The insider sadly doesn't have many more details on Codename Mars outside of the time period and project name, but it is interesting considering the Netflix series is confirmed to be set at the same time. It's not hard to imagine the two tying into each other a bit, and I can already picture a very similar experience to Odyssey.

Although this is the first time we'll be exploring Ancient Rome, Assassin's Creed Brotherhood was set there in the 1500s.

The other game that was revealed by xj0nathan is a mobile game set in Jerusalem called Assassin's Creed Pocket, which will apparently have similar gameplay to Valhalla's Mastery Challenges. While another smaller Assassin's Creed game isn't that exciting personally, the claim that it'll have similar visuals to Prince of Persia: The Lost Crown does make it a bit more interesting.

We finally got a chance to play The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake and it's still one of the best games ever made.
