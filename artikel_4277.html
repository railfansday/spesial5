Bugs and glitches are just a part of modern gaming. Some can be game-breaking, others produce some funny moments, and some can actually be useful. One GTA 6 glitch that is gathering a lot of attention is one that lets players access a first-person mode.

As expected, GTA 6 is played in third-person and although many players appreciate a first-person mode, it is absent at launch, or at least, officially.

There is a glitch that enables a first-person look. It can be a little fiddly at first, but once you get the hang of it, you will have a better view at the sights. We have all the details you need to know about how to enter first-person mode the next time you load up the game.

YouTuber OneBoyler posted a video demonstrating how to do the GTA 6 first-person glitch on PlayStation. To perform the glitch, pause the game and go to the in-game settings, under the Controller tab, scroll down until you see Touch Pad Button. Change the Touch Pad Button to Camera 2. This setting changes your touchpad from opening the map to altering your camera view.

Next, sit in the driver’s seat of a vehicle and take out your mobile phone by pressing up on the D-Pad. Put your phone away by hitting circle on a PlayStation controller and then instantly spam your touchpad button.

Once successful, you will see the world from your character’s perspective, holding the wheel of the car. From this point onward, you can safely exit the car with first-person view intact and even access your loadout. Equipping a gun allows you to hold it realistically, and the animations are compatible with first-person mode.

Also, you can drive in first-person, bringing a new level of immersion to the already gorgeous and detailed world around you. The city lights reflecting off the front of your car hit different when you’re cruising around the streets.

It’s worth noting that to return to third-person mode, you must reload your save. Make sure you save at the right time and don’t make any progress you want to risk losing while experimenting with the first-person view.

When GTA 5 launched on XBOX 360 and PlayStation 3 back in 2013, a first-person mode was absent. Only when GTA 5 was ported to XBOX One, PlayStation 4, and PC did a first-person perspective arrive in the game. Included were an optional first-person cover system, a new targeting system, traditional FPS controls, and fresh animations that allowed players to see the world in a whole new way.

If history were to repeat itself, a first-person mode could be on the cards for GTA 6 in a future update. For now, if you want to explore Leonida in first-person, you’ll have to do so using the glitch.

It’s unknown what the first post-launch GTA 6 patch will entail, but if it doesn’t bring a first-person mode with it, players will be hoping that Rockstar Games decides against patching out this particular bug.
