West Ham backed to ‘come good’ by ex-EFL man Jobi McAnuff after Scott Parker chat

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

West Ham were relegated from the Premier League last season after they finished in the bottom three along with Burnley and Wolves. WHUFC are now eyeing an immediate promotion back from the Championship.

The Hammers are positioned in 9th place in the table after their first 15 fixtures and they are one point off the play-offs behind both Southampton and QPR following their 2-1 loss away at Blackburn Rovers last time out. They are back in action after the international break with a home clash against Preston North End at the London Stadium.

Former Watford, Cardiff City and Reading man McAnuff has delivered his verdict on the London outfit on BBC 72+ Podcast: “I think West Ham will come good still. I think that is a real good appointment, Scotty going in.”

He added: “I had a chat with him recently. Sometimes during these interviews there’s a line or two that just really stuck. He said he needed a bit of oxygen. He needed to get out of Burnley. You know, it was so tough. Tough ending to his tenure there.”

West Ham could do with a new goalkeeper in the upcoming January transfer window. Mads Hermansen has struggled to make an impact in this campaign, whilst Alphonse Areola has slipped down the pecking order at WHUFC.

The Hammers have conceded way too many goals so far this term. They need to tighten things up at the back if they are to compete for the top two.

They are doing fine at the top end of the pitch though and have no problems scoring goals. In fact, they are the top scorers in the division having found the net on a whopping 37 occasions already.

West Ham managed to keep hold of skipper Jarrod Bowen in the summer which was a massive boost. They then boosted their attacking departments with additions such as Manor Solomon and Joel Piroe.

West Ham’s appointment of Parker made perfect sense. He knows WHUFC already and understands what it takes to get out of the second tier having guided Fulham, AFC Bournemouth and Burnley up in the air.

As McAnuff has alluded to, the Hammers should come good over the coming weeks and months. They have a lot of quality at their disposal in attack and if they can stop leaking goals, there is no reason why they can’t challenge the likes of Birmingham City, Middlesbrough and Stoke City at the top.

Parker is an experienced head coach and needs to get some results now to get the fans fully on side. His side are winless in their last three matches, two of which were under his guidance.

West Ham’s next match against Preston North End is an ideal chance to return to winning ways. The Lilywhites are in the drop zone with Burnley and Derby County.
