Patrick Stewart Cause of Death Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Patrick Stewart‘s cause of death has been revealed.

The beloved English actor died at age 86 on Oct. 28 in his Los Angeles home. His death was confirmed by a statement on his social media, which added that the cause was “a brief and poignant journey with Alzheimer’s disease and lewy body dementia.”

His death certificate, obtained by People, lists cardiopulmonary arrest as his cause of death, and neurocognitive disorder with Lewy body as an underlying condition. TMZ was first to report the news.

The Emmy-winning performer, known for both his good natured persona and indelible roles in popular franchises like Star Trek and X-Men, was adored by his collaborators and the public alike. Stewart’s career spanned decades with work across more than five dozen films and over 50 television appearances, from X-Men and Star Trek to Dune and American Dad. His stage roles also ran the gamut from Shakespeare’s Scottish play to Waiting for Godot to his memorable one-man version of A Christmas Carol. In 1966, he joined the Royal Shakespeare Company and he remained with the storied company until 1982. During his tenure at the Royal Shakespeare Company, Stewart featured in numerous productions, including Hamlet, King Lear, and The Taming of the Shrew. He would continue appearing onstage throughout his career, notably joining longtime friend and actor Ian McKellen for Waiting for Godot in 2009 and again in 2014.

In a wave of tributes from Hollywood following his death, William Shatner remembered Stewart, who succeeded him as captain of a Star Trek franchise. “Sir Patrick Stewart was a friend, a colleague, and a magnificent actor,” wrote Shatner. “From the stage to the bridge of the Enterprise, he brought intelligence, grace, and a deep sense of humanity to everything he did.”
