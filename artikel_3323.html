Jamie Lee Curtis is playing cleanup on aisle 4 after she announced in her Instagram birthday post that everyone in her immediate family was dead.

The actress clarified her comments in a second IG post on Monday after getting backlash from others who blasted Jamie for ignoring the surviving members of her family. In her original post, Jamie wrote that both of her famous parents, Janet Leigh and Tony Curtis, were dead, along with her sister, Kelly Lee Curtis.
