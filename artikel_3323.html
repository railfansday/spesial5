Frank Whaley Signs With Artium Talent

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

EXCLUSIVE: Frank Whaley has signed with Artium Talent for theatrical representation.

Since his debut opposite Meryl Streep and Jack Nicholson in the 1987 film Ironweed, Whaley has built a career spanning more than 80 films across comedy, drama, and action.

His film credits include Pulp Fiction, Field of Dreams, Swimming with Sharks, Swing Kids, Career Opportunities, Born on the Fourth of July, The Doors, Broken Arrow, JFK, Red Dragon, School of Rock, World Trade Center, The Freshman, Hoffa, and Vacancy. More recent features include Hustlers, opposite Jennifer Lopez, Constance Wu, and Cardi B, and Paramount’s Monster Trucks.

On TV, Whaley portrayed Agent Van Miller on Showtime’s Ray Donovan, starred opposite Richard Dreyfuss in the ABC miniseries Madoff, and played Detective Rafael Scarfe in Marvel’s Luke Cage. His other small screen appearances include Law & Order: SVU, The Blacklist, Gotham, Sneaky Pete, Curb Your Enthusiasm, Under the Dome, Empire, Burn Notice, and Psych. He also recurred on Netflix’s The Good Cop and Amazon’s Jack Ryan. Whaley also appeared in the Hulu anthology series Into the Dark. Watch on Deadline

Whaley’s signing with Artium Talent marks a new chapter in his theatrical representation as he continues to pursue projects across film and TV.

He continues to be managed by Karen Forman.
