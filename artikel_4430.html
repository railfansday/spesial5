Next-Gen 2028 Honda CR-V Follows the Toyota RAV4 as a Hybrid-Only SUV

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Honda has confirmed that the next-generation CR-V will debut a new platform and a new two-motor hybrid system.

The current Honda CR-V can be had with or without a hybrid powertrain. However, when the compact SUV’s newest generation arrives sometime next year, it’ll only be available as a hybrid. Honda’s announcement comes a year after Toyota applied the same strategy with its new RAV4. And considering over half the CR-Vs Honda sells are hybrids, the move makes a lot of sense.

Apart from introducing a standard hybrid powertrain, the 2028 Honda CR-V will mark the start of the bestselling nameplate’s seventh generation. It will also introduce a next-gen platform, which the automaker claims will prioritize stiffness and lightness; its modular nature will promote parts sharing too. While we’re still eagerly waiting for Honda to release more details about its next CR-V, the automaker has shared some breadcrumbs about its hybrid powertrain.

The new fifth generation of Honda’s two-motor hybrid system will once again pair with an Atkinson-cycle four-cylinder gas engine. We don’t yet know how much power the combo will make, but Honda says it “will greatly increase the standard power and efficiency.” When it comes to efficiency, the next-gen hybrid system is targeting an improvement of over 10 percent compared with the outgoing version. For reference, the current CR-V hybrid makes 205 horsepower, and it’s rated at up to 38 mpg combined.

We expect the 2028 Honda CR-V to be revealed sometime later this year. Honda says it’ll be the first of several hybrid models that it plans to launch by 2030.
