Kompany returns to Bayern training with Köln game earmarked for full comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

BILD has reported that Vincent Kompany returned to the pitch and led today's training session for the first time since his collapse.

The coach was wearing an arm sling as he still recovers from a dislocated shoulder, but it was another step towards normality for Kompany.

Additionally, Bayern Munich's training group included Jamal Musiala, Luis Díaz, Sven Ulreich, Sacha Boey and several academy talents.

As it stands, the chances of Kompany returning to the sidelines for Bayern’s next home game against 1.FC Köln this Friday look good.

The final decision will be made at short notice in consultation with the doctors, who check on Kompany daily, although nothing will be rushed.

Overall, Kompany is doing well, but it will depend on how he feels on matchday and whether the doctors give him the green light.

Sky Germany understands that the final decision has yet to be made by the German record champions in that regard.
