Jason Michael is NC State’s new offensive coordinator

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Philip Rivers tapped into his NFL connections to find his offensive coordinator and brought in Jason Michael, who most recently was the tight ends coach for the Philadelphia Eagles.

"I'm really excited to add Coach Michael to our staff," said Rivers. "I have history with him from my time with the Chargers and with the Colts and we are aligned in our vision for building this program. His expertise and experience in the offensive system that we are going to implement here at NC State is second to none, and I can't wait for him to get started."

Michael has been in coaching for over two decades but has spent very little of that time in the college game, and hasn't worked for a college program since 2008. He also has only two years of experience as an offensive coordinator, and those came with the Titans back in 2014 and 2015, so it's been a minute since he's called plays. All of that is slightly concerning.

Maybe this is an indicator that Rivers wants to call the plays himself—he was asked about that on Monday and didn't answer one way or the other, which was surprising. I don't know why you'd name a guy offensive coordinator if you were gonna call the plays yourself, but the experience between the two is pretty even, unfortunately.
