Duke basketball vs SIU Edwardsville live updates: Follow nonconference matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

DURHAM, N.C. — Duke basketball is back home to host SIU Edwardsville on Friday, Nov. 13, at Cameron Indoor Stadium.

The No. 2 Blue Devils (2-1) are coming off their first loss of the year, falling 79-73 to Michigan State in the State Farm Champions Classic in Chicago. Duke never led against the Spartans.

Tipoff between Duke and SIU Edwardsville (2-2) is set for 8 p.m. ET on the CW. The Cougars are coming off their second straight loss, falling 96-58 at Missouri on Nov. 9. SIU Edwardsville previously fell 78-77 at Lipscomb on Nov. 7 after opening their season with wins over Eureka College (91-38) and Concordia Chicago (85-61).
