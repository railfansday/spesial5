Buffalo Bills at Minnesota Vikings, ‘MNF’ Week 9 live game chat

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Buffalo Bills are holed up in cozy U.S. Bank Stadium and ready for a primetime showdown with the Minnesota Vikings under the spectacle that is Monday Night Football. Tonight, it's a showdown between two 6-1 teams with vastly different roads to success in 2026.

The Bills and their league-best scoring offense are of course led by quarterback Josh Allen while featuring one of the most lethal rushing attacks directed by running back James Cook III. Where they've struggled is on defense, and against opponents' passing attacks.

Is that a recipe for disaster this evening with wide receivers Justin Jefferson and Jordan Addison set to play catch with quarterback Carson Wentz? Well, expect Jefferson and Addison to get plenty of chances to see how they fare against cornerbacks Christian Benford and rookie Davison Igbinosun.

Tonight does present a massive challenge for Buffalo's offense. Allen will have an entirely new pair of offensive linemen protecting him on the left side — and against a ferocious Vikings pass rush. It's not an ideal situation, to be clear, having a starting pair in left tackle Dion Dawkins and left guard Alec Anderson sidelined for this game. In their place will be offensive tackle Tylan Grable, and offensive guard Austin Corbett.

No pressure, right? It's time to find out what the next men up have up their sleeve to take on the NFL's best and stingiest defense, a Vikings unit that blitzes as though their life depended upon it. It's likely we see head coach Joe Brady call a lot of early runs and quick passes to both wear the defense down and allow Allen to settle into rhythm.

Winning in the NFL is tough, and this might be the Bills' biggest challenge yet. Though you may be looking for Allen and company to air it out, and expect Buffalo's defense to continue an upward trajectory based off last week's thrashing of the Baltimore Ravens, a win is a win, signature or otherwise.

Remember, for those of you without cable or streaming service options, the ESPN broadcast is also shown live on your local ABC station. With that, here's your open thread for all of tonight's action during Bills at Vikings for Week 9 of Monday Night Football! As always, be kind to one another — and in all things… Go Bills!
