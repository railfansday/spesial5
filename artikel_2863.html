Commanders rule out WRs Dyami Brown, Treylon Burks

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Commanders will play without two of their receivers against the Rams on Sunday.

They ruled out Dyami Brown (calf) and Treylon Burks (foot). Burks didn’t practice all week, and Brown went from limited Thursday to a non-participant on Friday.

Burks is the team’s third-leading receiver with 233 yards and two touchdowns on 12 catches, and Brown has contributed nine catches for 94 yards.

The Commanders list defensive tackle Johnny Newton (pectoral) as questionable, but he was a full participant all week and seems likely to play.

Right guard Sam Cosmi (knee) closed the week with a full practice Friday, and he has no designation.
