Lakers squeak by lowly Kings to get back on track

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Lakers continue to look like a team in need of the benefits that are supposed to come from this stretch in Southern California.

Because after looking like one of the NBA's best teams for 3 ½ weeks regardless of the competition, they've struggled against some of the league's bottom-feeders during their four-game homestand.

Luka Dončić shoots a 3-point basket against the Sacramento Kings. NBAE via Getty Images

That was once again the case on Friday night, with the Lakers barely beating the lowly one-win Kings, 106-104, at Crypto.com Arena to get back on track after losing to the Jazz on Wednesday.

After Walker Kessler missed a pair of free throws with the Lakers up by two points with five seconds left in the fourth, Zach LaVine grabbed the defensive rebound and dribbled up the court before firing a 29-footer that hit the front rim to secure the victory for the Lakers.

Cameron Carr drives to the basket. NBAE via Getty Images

But until the final buzzer sounded, there was an unease about whether the Lakers were going to lose back-to-back games for the first time.

They trailed 62-54 at halftime, with a general malaise from Tuesday's overtime win over the Hornets and Wednesday's loss to the Jazz carrying over into Friday.

It showed defensively, with the Lakers allowing too many drives to the rim.

It showed with the seven first-quarter turnovers they committed, which directly led to 11 Kings points.

The Lakers continue to look like a team in need of the benefits that are supposed to come from this stretch in Southern California. NBAE via Getty Images

But the Lakers, led by Luka Dončić (25 points, eight assists, four rebounds and two steals), found enough of the groove they had been searching for in the second half to turn what was an 11-point deficit in the third into an eight-point lead in the fourth.

And they held on just long enough to ensure they entered the weekend with a 14-3 record before a two-day break before hosting the Jazz again on Monday.
