Skip Bayless’ revealing Lakers rant about ex-teammates: ‘Not saying he hated LeBron’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Lakers have done well in the post-LeBron James era.

Nobody expected Los Angeles to emerge as one of the top teams in the Western Conference, and yet, they are. Sports media personality Skip Bayless believes the Lakers' recent success can be attributed to the departure of James.

FS1 journalist Skip Bayless attends the 7th Annual IAVA Heroes Gala at Cipriani 42nd Street on Nov. 12, 2013, in New York City. WireImage

Bayless claims Doncic didn't enjoy being teammates with the four-time NBA champion because it prevented the 27-year-old from showcasing his immense talent.

"Luka is in a league of his own. They do not miss LeBron James because Luka just couldn't stand it anymore. I'm not saying he hated LeBron, but he didn't love him," Bayless said on the Double Coverage Podcast.

LeBron James of the Philadelphia 76ers shoots a free throw against the Boston Celtics on Tuesday, Nov. 10, 2026, at Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia. NBAE via Getty Images

"He definitely didn't love sharing the stage because LeBron is LeBron."

James informed the Lakers he would not return for the 2026-27 season in the offseason and entered free agency. The NBA's all-time leading scorer dominated headlines as the league and fans alike waited for his decision.

Los Angeles Lakers' Luka Doncic smiles during an NBA basketball game against the Milwaukee Bucks on Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, in Milwaukee. AP Photo/Aaron Gash

Ultimately, James joined the 76ers on a two-year, $8 million deal.

The 41-year-old never looked back and neither did the Lakers.

General manager Rob Pelinka orchestrated a massive roster overhaul this summer and many believed the new additions weren't good enough to make up for the many free agent departures in LA, including James.

Thus far, the Lakers have been able to make do largely because of Doncic's leadership and talent on the court.
