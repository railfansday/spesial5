Fantasy Football Week 9 wide receiver rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Fantasy football managers face another important week of lineup decisions as the 2026 NFL season enters Week 9. With injuries, tough defensive matchups, and changing target shares impacting the wide receiver position, choosing the right starters could make a significant difference. Here are the Week 9 fantasy football WR rankings to help set your lineups and identify the best options.

Oct 4, 2026; Houston, Texas, USA; Houston Texans wide receiver Nico Collins (12) warms up prior to a game against the Dallas Cowboys at Reliant Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Thomas Shea-Imagn Images

When Collins plays, CJ Stroud plays better, and the Texans generally win. He is a true difference-maker on the field. Other than his first game back from injury on October 4th, Collins has been quiet. Stroud hasn't looked at him much, targeting him only five times in each of his last two games. The Texans are playing the winless Chargers, and they can't afford to lose this game. Look for Collins to get far more looks and finally put together a solid game.

It is so rare to see St. Brown this far down this list. For all of Miami's woes, their secondary hasn't been awful. They rank third in the league against the pass. And realistically, Detroit will have an early lead, which will favor RB Jahmyr Gibbs' stats more than St. Brown.

Olave is playing a Cleveland team that is reeling after a brutal loss to the Steelers. Aaron Rodgers ate up Cleveland's secondary, and now the league has a blueprint on how to attack them. Tyler Shough will exploit what he's learned, and Olave will be the beneficiary.

The Packers face a Patriots team ranked 23rd against the pass. While the Pats do have Christian Gonzalez, he can't guard both of them, meaning one of them will go off. The assumption is Gonzalez will be on Watson, which will leave Golden to run wild.

McConkey's absence with a concussion is one of the reasons Justin Herbert has been so atrocious this season. This is a huge game for Herbert, and McConkey…and OC Mike McDaniel. Another drubbing could mean his job…he should already be fired, actually.

Oct 4, 2026; Cincinnati, Ohio, USA; Cincinnati Bengals wide receiver Tee Higgins (5) walks off the field after the game against the Jacksonville Jaguars at Paycor Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Katie Stratman-Imagn Images Credit: IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect (Reuters)

While Chase is considered the number 1 for the Bengals, Higgins is having an amazing year, and the Bengals' passing game is carrying this offense. Higgins could have a monster week in what should be a very high-scoring game.

Nabers is sixth among WRs in receptions and ninth in yardage. He has only found the end zone twice. Every week he is getting more comfortable with JJ McCarthy. He has four consecutive weeks with over 100 receiving yards.

Chase had a Chase-worthy game last week. He caught 11 of 16 targets for 141 yards and a TD. This week he'll head to Spain to face a Falcons team that generally holds WRs in check

While it is likely that Christian Gonzalez will be on Watson, the Pats have been playing a ton of zone. But regardless, Watson is too good to be held in check by any CB…look for the two Green Bay WRs to each put up crazy numbers in this game. In a game that could end up in the 50s, Jordan Love will have to pass the ball early and often to his two main threats.

Jan 4, 2026; Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA; Minnesota Vikings wide receiver Justin Jefferson (18) warms up prior to the game against the Green Bay Packers at U.S. Bank Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Jeffrey Becker-Imagn Images

This has to be the most frustrating season of Jefferson's career. Every week seems like he'll have a breakout game, yet every week he just puts up pedestrian numbers. He is too good not to have a Justin Jefferson-type game…even with a subpar QB getting him the ball. He doesn't have a 100-yard game yet this season and is only catching 70% of his targets…which are also down. This week is the week… they'll have to put up more pts to avoid a two-game losing streak, facing the Bills, who rank 27th against the pass.

Like the top two RBs in the league, Jahmyr Gibbs and Bijan Robinson, who are often 1-2 on this RB list, Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Puka Nacua are 1-2 here. Nacua is third in receptions but only has three TDs for the season. He is facing a road matchup against a Washington team that showed some life recently. If Matt Stafford can't go or is limited, Nacua may need to drop a few slots on this list.

Here is Smith-Njigba, again 1-2 on this list. Last week, Smith-Njigba had an off week, "only" catching six passes for 118 yards and a TD. In fairness, he was targeted 11 times and was held ineffective in the second half. For the season, he leads WRs in receptions, yardage, and TDs. He has a very favorable matchup this week against the Cardinals, who have given up the most pts in the NFC.
