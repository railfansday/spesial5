Eagles injury update: Offensive reinforcements return after bye week

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Eagles injury update: Offensive reinforcements return after bye week originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Eagles are getting some key offensive reinforcements as they return from their Week 10 bye week.

Both DeVonta Smith (hamstring) and Dontayvion Wicks (ankle) were practicing on Wednesday as the Eagles (2-7) began their to prepare to host the Pittsburgh Steelers (6-3) on Sunday afternoon at the Linc.

Smith and Wicks each missed the last three games before the bye week. The Eagles didn't place them on IR, which signaled a hope that they would be back for the Steelers game. That plan appears to be on track.

"We'll see how that plan unfolds as it goes," head coach Nick Sirianni said. "Like I said, we're hopeful. We'll see how the week goes. I don't want to promise anything, but we'll see how the week goes, how it unfolds and excited to get out there and practice today."

It ought to help quarterback Joe Milton, who will make his second-career start on Sunday, to have the team's top two receivers back on the field with him. During the last three games, rookie Makai Lemon has been the Eagles' leading receiver.

The only player on the 53-man roster who wasn't practicing was Pro Bowl linebacker Zack Baun. He was a spectator on Wednesday with a back injury.

Limited: DeVonta Smith (hamstring), Michael Carter (abdomen), Britain Covey (knee), Jonathan Jones (hamstring), Will Shipley (foot), Nolan Smith (illness), Dontayvion Wicks (ankle)

Full: Saquon Barkley (hamstring), Jihaad Campbell (hamstring/quad), Jonathan Greenard (ankle), Tanner McKee (rib), Smael Mondon (ankle), Drew Mukuba (knee)

A few other players, in addition to Smith and Wicks, also returned to practice after missing the Giants game before the bye: LB Jihaad Campbell (hamstring/quad), DB Michael Carter II (abdomen), WR Britain Covey (knee) and CB Jonathan Jones (hamstring).

Will Shipley also returned to practice on Wednesday. The Eagles opened up his 21-day practice window earlier in the week. Shipley has been on Injured Reserve with a foot injury since Oct. 16. The only running backs on the 53-man roster right now are Saquon Barkley and Jaydon Blue. Shipley was getting reps as a kick returner during the early portion of practice.

The Eagles will release their full injury report later on Wednesday.
