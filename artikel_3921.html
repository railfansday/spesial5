Pokémon Teams Up With Casetify On A New Line Of Phone Cases And Accessories

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Pokémon Company really is going all out with the collaborations for its 30th anniversary. Phone case brand Casetify is next in line for a crossover, as it has announced that its new collection of cases and accessories will be launching next week.

The collection will go live on 1st December at 8am GMT / 9am CET / 3am ET, and will consist of "pixel-art Pokémon cases, charms and tech accessories, including returning icons and new special-edition designs," according to Casetify. There's even a waitlist you can sign up for online, so you know the very minute it drops.

We seem to remember previous Pokémon phone collaborations being quite the hit, so it might be worth checking out this one sooner rather than later, if you're keen to get some new phone protection in time for Christmas.
