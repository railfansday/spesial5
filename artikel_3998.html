To get into the Kingpin Bowling Alley safe in GTA 6, you can either obtain the safe code, which is far harder than it sounds, or you can blow it open, which is the much easier way to get in. If you'd still rather unlock the safe the classic way, I'll explain both methods of getting into the safe below, including how to get the GTA 6 bowling alley safe code. Whichever road you go down, it'll help you save up some cash and get back on your feet - and take down a few of those pesky Kolt gang members while you're at it.

The GTA 6 Bowling Alley safe code is 0300. To find it for yourself, you need to go to Brooks Boatworks, as hinted at in the notepad next to the safe. The safe code is on a map on the wall in one of the buildings there, as marked on the image above.

By the time the bowling alley is mentioned, Brooks Boatworks should be marked on your map as another location held by the Kolt crew, and you may have even raided it already. Whether you've been there or not, the map is found on the wall of an office in the raised buildings to the east of the area, and you can find two other GTA 6 safe codes on this map, too.

You'll have to fend off a lot of Kolt members at both the bowling alley and the boatworks, so make sure you've got some decent GTA 6 guns to hand, and certainly as much Zombix as you can carry. You'll find more at each location, too, so keep an eye out.

You'll also see the option to plant explosives on the GTA 6 bowling alley safe, but if you haven't got the right explosives to hand, the option is grayed out. Fortunately, there are some sticky bombs conveniently stashed in the next room, meaning it's the much safer option that heading out to the boatworks.

We previously believed this option required reaching one of the Chapter 3 GTA 6 missions, at which point you unlock the ability to buy sticky bombs from Ammu-Nation, but with the few explosives available in the bowling alley, you're golden. Keep the spares on you, and you can use them to get into other safes around Leonida, too.
