Persona 4 Revival Wasn't Meant to Be Part of Packed February 2027 Release Schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Persona 4 Revival was initially designed to launch for PS5 in the late summer months of this year, not the very crowded February 2027 release calendar it's now found itself in.

In an interview with 3DMGAME, chief producer Kazuhisa Wada shared the original plan was to launch the remake "as early as possible — we originally planned to release it about six months earlier, which would have been just in time for summer, but we ultimately couldn't make it that way".

The original Persona 4 often sparks memories of summer for players, as a large part of the game takes place during the season and includes trips to the beach and festivals specific to that part of the year. Putting the PS5 remake out during that same season would have made a lot of sense, but the Atlus executive claims the firm "couldn't make it that way".

It's not clear whether Wada is referring to development issues or a different problem that affected the release.

Either way, Persona 4 Revival now launches on 18th February 2027, a month full of other big PS5 releases. The delay to God of War Laufey gives everything else a touch more breathing room, but the schedule still looks like this:

As an RPG, its strongest competition is probably Fable, which releases just five days after Persona 4 Revival. The Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis remake should take up a lot of mindshare in the weeks before it, too.

We shall still play it in February, but getting to experience it again during those quieter summer months would have been bliss.
