PlayStation Portal Midnight Black Japan-only model announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Sony Interactive Entertainment Japan Asia has announced a Japanese-language-only SKU for PlayStation Portal in Midnight Black. It will include a one-month PlayStation Plus Premium subscription and launch on December 10 for 39,980 yen.

The company also announced that the new model will include additional internal storage, which will be utilized for future functionality. Details will be announced at a later date.
