Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks live, where to buy the 40th Anniversary handheld

Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks have been filtering onto the shelves in the last week, following the Ocarina of Time remake's release date on November 5. With a number of stores bringing 40th Anniversary edition consoles to the party during midnight releases, or well-timed online drops, many lucky stock hunters managed to snag their handheld in time to face Ganondorf.

Right now, there's no Legend of Zelda Switch 2 stock live on the shelves, and even finding that Pro Controller or carry case is proving difficult. There's a ray of hope to be had here, though.

In the last week, Target and Nintendo have both dropped new Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks, while Walmart's had the special edition gamepad on the shelves. GameStop is prioritizing in-store orders only, so your best bet is to head down to your local store to check on their inventory.

In the UK, Amazon has been rolling out its restocks in smaller waves, running through last week, over the weekend, and once again on Monday.

Here's hoping we'll see more Legend of Zelda Switch restock action in the coming days - we'll be covering all of it live right here.

I've been tracking hard-to-find stock for over six years now, starting with the incredibly limited releases of the PS5 and Xbox Series X. I've been reporting on how retailers operate with high-demand products, from anniversary releases to brand new consoles, ever since then - including the Nintendo Switch 2 itself.

Amazon has been quieter for restocks since it dropped its first round of handhelds at launch. Still, there's been a good amount of time since release day - enough for the online retailer to potentially re-fill its warehouses?

Walmart's had a bit of a time of it. The retailer cancelled a number of pre-orders just the day before launch, and has been catching up on its backlog ever since. While new stock might be already allocated to those burned by pre-order mishandling, it's worth keeping an eye on any movement here.

Target has been the most active of US retailers in the last week, running scheduled in-store and online restocks around November 5-6 last week. While that inventory might take some time to re-emerge, it's worth keeping an eye for accessories here as well.

Best Buy kept its most recent allocation of Legend of Zelda Switch 2 stock for its midnight Ocarina of Time launch event. Since then, the online side of this retailer has been pretty quiet.

Nintendo itself has held its own online Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks pretty regularly since launch. This is always one to keep an eye on.

GameStop is only running Legend of Zelda Nintendo Switch 2 restocks in stores, so you won't find any online stock here. That goes not just for the console, but for the Pro Controller and carry case as well.

Amazon UK has been dropping Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks in smaller waves over the last week, so it's always worth keeping that tab open and regularly checking back.

EE has offered Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks twice since launch, and has also been offering additional discounts on Ocarina of Time and Pokemon Pokopia bundles as well.

Smyths Toys was one of the first retailers to drop its original wave of Legend of Zelda Switch 2 stock when the handheld was announced, so we're expecting more action here in the near future. It's also one of the only UK retailers I'm seeing with listing pages up for the Pro Controller and carry case.

Here's the thing with Very. It often bundles up difficult-to-find stock with extra accessories or games to keep resellers at bay. That means you'll be paying a little more here (though still only RRP for everything you're getting) but stock often holds out a lot longer.

Argos was previously offering Legend of Zelda Switch 2 stock ahead of launch, but things look to have run dry now. The store organises its inventory by postcode, though, so it's always worth checking your local area's delivery and collection options.

Currys previously had a listing page up for the Legend of Zelda Switch 2, but it seems to have disappeared now, instead sending us to a generic consoles search page.

Nintendo has also stated more stock is on the way, so you'd expect its own store to be one of the first with fresh handhelds up for grabs.

There's not been much movement today at all, not even the whisper of a carry case. While retailers may be recuperating their supplies after a heavy week last week, we're holding out hope for more drops later in the week.

We've seen retailers like Amazon and Nintendo dropping Legend of Zelda Switch 2 stock in dribs and drabs over the last few days, but that could ramp up to a bigger wave next week. That's giving stores a good two weeks to restock after last week's Ocarina of Time launch, and retailers - like Walmart - who are yet to see major online drops plenty of time to catch up.

Nintendo itself has been securing additional Zelda Switch 2 handhelds after it initially underestimated demand. The brand has been hosting its own very short, but far more regular, drops over the last few days so it's worth staying in touch here and checking back regularly.

It's well worth signing up to as many notification systems as possible while your Zelda Switch 2 stock hunt continues, Nintendo's offering its own and Target has also now launched its own email registry as well.

Had you asked me at the start of this stock hunt, I probably would have guessed that the special edition Pro Controller would be easier to find in stock than the handheld itself. That doesn't seem to have been the case, though.

The Legend of Zelda Switch 2 might be difficult to find reliably on the shelves, but it has been flitting in and out of stock at different retailers since launch. I can pinpoint maybe two or three times I've seen the Pro Controller file back onto the shelves - and it was gone just as quickly if not faster than the console.

While still currently out of stock, EE has been offering Nintendo Switch 2 bundles with the special edition handheld and discounted games. Nestled between Pokemon Pokopia and Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream, you'll find the Ocarina of Time remake with £10 off its regular price. Once stock returns to these shelves, we're hoping the bundles will once again become active.

The UK has also seen its own restocks over the past week, primarily from Amazon with a few from EE earlier in the handheld's life. While those Amazon restocks are fleeting, I haven't spotted any dropping so far today. It's well worth keeping in touch there, though, as the retailer seems to be adopting a 'little and often' approach.

From what I can see over the course of our restock tracking so far, Walmart has never publicly offered online restocks of the Legend of Zelda Switch 2 itself. Accessories have been spotted, but with the retailer reportedly fumbling its pre-order phase (and having to catch up as a result), things have been particularly quiet here. That could mean big things once those inventories are reset, though.

Nintendo's listing page states that "more inventory is on the way," undoubtedly a good sign for future Legend of Zelda Switch 2 restocks. However, it's worth remembering you can also add the special edition handheld to your Nintendo Store app wishlist and get notifications on your phone when stock does return.
