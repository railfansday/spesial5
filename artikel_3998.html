GTA 6 Interactive Map - Every Location in Leonida

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Leonida covers six regions, from the beaches and islands of Vice City to the forests of Mount Kalaga, and it is packed with places worth knowing about. Safehouses, banks worth robbing, the nearest Ammu-Nation and the closest Pay 'n' Spray are all out there, if you know where to look.

The interactive map below fixes that. It puts 1,075 places across all of Leonida on one map you can search, filter and tick off as you go, from robbery targets to hidden packages. Here is how to use it and what you will find.

The map works like any online map: drag to move and zoom in on the part of Leonida you care about. Every marker belongs to a category, and the sidebar is where you choose what to see.

The map sorts its 1,075 places into nine categories, listed here in the same order as the sidebar. Food & Drink is by far the busiest, so switch it off first if a district looks crowded.

Banks (25), Illegal Operations (43), Smash 'n' Grabs (36), Payday Loans (7), Fences (13)

Gyms (9), Golf Courses (3), Gang Compounds (2), Random Events (5)

Gas Stations (62), Pay 'n' Sprays (19), Mod Shops (16), Car Washes (7), Vehicle Dealers (2), Public Transport (44)

Clothing (70), Footwear (17), Hair & Beauty (19), Tattoos (11), Electronics (15)

Street Food (151), Bars (138), Cafes & Takeout (104), Convenience Stores (75), Fast Food (33), Liquor Stores (14)

Landmarks (41), Cities & Towns (10), Marinas (11), Nightclubs (6), Police Stations (5), Fire Stations (4), Hospitals (3), Cinemas (1)

Some markers may sit slightly off their actual locations, and new places are added as players find them. For more on Leonida's regions, characters and activities, head to the Fextralife GTA 6 Wiki, which features the same map on its Interactive Map page.
