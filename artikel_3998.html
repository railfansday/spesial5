Following BioWare Closure, a Documentary About the Legendary Studio Has Been Announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

In case you missed the news, publisher Electronic Arts recently pulled the plug on one of its most revered studios, BioWare, as part of a wider round of layoffs.

The developer, known for its many beloved contributions to the RPG genre, is sadly no more, and the game it was working on, a new Mass Effect, was also cancelled.

Interestingly, in the aftermath of this news, it's been announced that a documentary all about the studio is in the works, and will be released next year.

Titled Massive Effect: The BioWare Story, the film comes from Shane Fennessey and Adam Scorgie, producing for Canada‘s Super Channel.

It sounds like the documentary charts the company's history, from its founding in 1995 all the way through to its unfortunate closure just weeks ago.

According to a report from Deadline, filming for the documentary began two years ago, and is endorsed by BioWare founders Ray Muzyka and Greg Zeschuk.

They will appear in the doc, along with many other figures including Geoff Keighley, voice actor Jennifer Hale, former EA CEO John Riccitiello, and more.
