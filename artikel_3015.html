Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Yield over volume paid off, but fuel costs and currency swings still cloud the outlook.

Azul S.A. (AZUL +1.01%) reported a sequential improvement in financial performance for the third quarter of 2026, focusing on capacity discipline and liquidity strengthening following its restructuring. Management stated that the company prioritized yield over volume, resulting in record unit revenues despite a high fuel price environment. The airline secured significant funding through government-backed financing facilities and a strategic equity investment from American Airlines. The company is not providing specific financial guidance at this time due to ongoing volatility in fuel prices and exchange rates.
