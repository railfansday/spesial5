At Its Current Rate, AGNC's Dividend Pays Back Today's Stock Price in About 6 Years. Will It Last That Long?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

This mortgage REIT is currently yielding a jaw-dropping 17.5%. There's no free lunch.

The star attraction of AGNC (AGNC -0.24%) is, understandably, its dividend. The popular mortgage real estate investment trust (REIT) distributes $0.12 per share in cash to its shareholders every month. Over the course of the year, that translates to a current yield of 17.5%.

That's a lot, but you should approach any double-digit yield with a fair amount of skepticism. The allure of making back your initial investment in less than six years -- 5.7 years, to be exact -- is real. The risks are just as gargantuan.

AGNC has consistently shelled out $0.12 per share since April 2020. That's 79 consecutive distributions. It would take just 69 months of that payout to make back your initial investment. It seems like easy money, but it's not.

A month before the first of those 79 consecutive payments, the REIT's per-share dividend was paying $0.16. It was $0.18 per share a year before that. Keep the time machine running, and you will unearth a couple more dividend cuts. The monthly rate has been cut nearly in half over the past dozen years.

Investing in mortgage REITs is risky. The actual Agency-backed mortgage securities may seem safe, but the spread between the interest it pays on its funding and the income it collects from the securities is far from stable. Throw in AGNC's leveraged operations in a climate of rising rates, and things start to get a lot more unpredictable.

Don't be fooled by today's stock price. You see the $8.23 close heading into this week of trading. Work the math, and AGNC has paid out $9.48 per share over the past 79 months at the same distribution rate. The problem is that at the start of this run, the stock was at $12.74 before the first of those reduced monthly disbursements. Its book value is deteriorating. The yield appears fantastic, but be aware of the risks.
