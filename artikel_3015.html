Why TSMC Stock Soared Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

AI stocks jumped on news of Jensen Huang's return.

By Jeremy Bowman – Nov 23, 2026 at 4:00PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing ›

Shares of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Corporation (TSM +14.09%) (TSMC) were soaring today, as it was one of the biggest winners on the news that Jensen Huang was returning as CEO of Nvidia.

TSMC is the world's biggest chip manufacturer and Nvidia's exclusive manufacturing partner. It also produces more than 90% of the world's most advanced chips.

Investors rushed back into AI stocks on the news, with TSMC up 14.5% and the Nasdaq Composite gaining 6.9%.

As the world's biggest chip manufacturer with a majority share of contract chips, TSMC is the linchpin of the semiconductor industry and is highly exposed to both the health of that industry and the success of AI more generally.

Nvidia is now its biggest customer, reportedly accounting for 19% of its revenue in 2025, so the success of Nvidia is also key for TSMC.

Jensen Huang has been the mastermind behind Nvidia's dominance of AI chips and the biggest champion of AI more broadly, and investors were fearful that the industry would lose much of its momentum and stability without him.

His return makes investors more confident in Nvidia's growth, which benefits TSMC, and reassures them about the broader strength of the AI sector.

At TSMC, 68% of its revenue now comes from high-performance computing, much of which is data center AI chips, so AI has become the biggest driver of its business.

Recently, reports emerged that Nvidia had asked TSMC to consider manufacturing 3D memory chips, which would be an entirely new business for TSMC, as it has historically only made logic components.

It's unclear whether anything will come of that proposal, but it shows the potential for growth in the Nvidia-TSMC relationship.

Demand for AI chips continues to outstrip supply, which is good news for TSMC. The stock still looks well-priced at a price-to-earnings ratio of 23.6.
