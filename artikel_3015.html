Montauk (MNTK) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Covenant pressure and Turkey facility delays cloud the company's liquidity outlook.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Montauk Renewables, Inc. (MNTK +22.62%) reported third-quarter revenue growth of 10.9% while concurrently negotiating an amendment to its senior credit facility to avoid a potential covenant default. Management reported that difficulties in meeting operational requirements at the Turkey, North Carolina facility have limited borrowing capacity, leading the company to evaluate liquidity alternatives such as potential asset sales and the deferral of up to $120 million in capital expenditures. Despite these liquidity concerns, the company reported increased revenue from its GreenWave joint venture and maintained its full-year renewable natural gas production guidance.

Our joint venture, GreenWave, continues to address the limited capacity of RNG utilization for transportation by offering third-party RNG volumes access to unique, exclusive, and proprietary transportation pathways. GreenWave matches available dispensing capacity with available third-party RNG volumes and separates and distributes RINs to the partners of GreenWave. As a result, we received approximately 1.1 million in separated RINs distributed from GreenWave in the third quarter of 2026. In July 2026, we began generating power for sale from our Turkey, North Carolina facility and continue to generate power, RECs, biochar, and biocrude at nominal levels throughout the third quarter of 2026.

In August 2026, we received notice from our power purchase utility that the facility had received notice of commercial operation date. Our production of power and notice from the utility permitted us to generate both swine RECs and enhanced RECs in subsequent months. We identified specific programming modifications to our installed electrical switchgear, which were completed during the third quarter of 2026. Throughout our operations over the third quarter, we identified enhancements to address two specific concerns limiting the commercial production volumes of the facility, those being the moisture control of our inlet feedstock and the preservation of syngas temperature from our reactors to our compression and power generation.

These concerns reduced the volume of feedstock that can be conveyed to our reactors and increased the frequency of maintenance intervals to address the consequences of gas separation through the facility due to premature temperature loss. We anticipate these enhancements to take approximately three months to complete from the placement of componentry orders and cost approximately $15 million. We expect to commence these enhancement projects immediately upon the conclusion of our amendments to our new senior credit facility. We continue to progress with our installation of feedstock collection at our targeted 400,000 to 450,000 hog spaces.

As of the end of October, we have entered into long-term agreements with over 80 separate farming locations, providing us access to at least 400,000 hog spaces. We are currently able to collect from more than 300,000 hog spaces. We continue to collect from farms as we are able to dry and store at our facility. While our recent development focus has been prioritized on achieving and increasing production and revenue at our Montauk Ag Renewables project, our continued difficulty in meeting certain engineering review and operational requirements in placing our Turkey, North Carolina facility into service has impacted our ability to draw down additional borrowings under our new senior credit facility.

We have executed a non-binding term sheet with the lender of our new senior credit facility for an amendment that will provide for additional borrowings and temporarily forbear and standstill certain debt covenant requirements. We are also in discussions with our board on potential additional shareholder financing directed towards the planned enhancements of our Turkey, North Carolina facility. Our capital investment expectation for this first phase of the project remains unchanged at $200 million, though exclusive of amounts required to complete our planned enhancements to increase capacity and reduce maintenance. With that, I will turn the call over to Kevin.

Kevin Van Asdalan: Thank you, Sean. I will be discussing our third quarter 2026 financial and operating results. Please refer to our earnings press release, Form 10-Q, and the supplemental slides that have been posted to our website for additional information. Our profitability is highly dependent on the market price of environmental attributes, including the market price for RINs. As we self-market a significant portion of our RINs, a decision not to commit the transfer of available RINs during a period will impact our revenue and operating profit. Total revenues in the third quarter of 2026 were $50.2 million, an increase of $4.9 million, or 10.9%, compared to $45.3 million in the third quarter of 2025.

The increase is primarily related to environmental attribute revenues from the RINs distributed and sold from our GreenWave joint venture of approximately $4.0 million, compared to $1.1 million in environmental attribute revenues related to RINs distributed and sold in the third quarter of 2025. Our third quarter of 2026 RNG volume sold under fixed or floor price contracts decreased approximately 73.9% as compared to the third quarter of 2025 as a result of the expiration of these contracts. Our RNG commodity revenue decreased approximately 52.2%. These decreases were offset by an increase in RINs sold of 12.2%. Our RINs generated and unseparated decreased approximately 84.0% as a result of the transition to Biogas Regulatory Reform Rule in 2025.

Total general and administrative expenses were $7.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $1.1 million, or 16.4%, compared to $6.5 million in the third quarter of 2025, driven primarily by the timing of incentive compensation accrual adjustments. Turning to our segment operating metrics, I will begin by reviewing our renewable natural gas segment. We produced 1.5 million MMBtu during the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 97,000, or 6.7%, compared to 1.4 million MMBtu during the third quarter of 2025. Our McCarty facility produced 60,000 MMBtu more in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025 as a result of landfill host wellfield operational and collection system enhancements.

Our Rumpke facility produced 42,000 MMBtu more in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025 as a result of expansion of the wellfield collection system. Our Apex facility produced 26,000 MMBtu more in the third quarter of 2026 as compared to the third quarter of 2025 as a result of increased feedstock gas from our improvements related to the landfill collection system. Our Pico facility produced 30,000 MMBtu fewer in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025 as a result of digester operations maintenance related to our racetrack digester.

Revenues from the renewable natural gas segment during the third quarter of 2026 were $41.9 million, an increase of $2.0 million, or 5.2%, compared to $39.9 million during the third quarter of 2025. Average commodity pricing for natural gas for the third quarter of 2026 was 3.9% lower than the third quarter of 2025. In the third quarter of 2026, we self-marketed 13.9 million RINs, representing a 1.5 million increase, or 12.2%, compared to 12.4 million RINs self-marketed during the third quarter of 2025. Average pricing realized on RIN sales during the third quarter of 2026 was $2.58, as compared to $2.29 during the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 12.7%.

This compares to the average D3 RIN index price for the third quarter of 2026 of $2.47, being approximately 12.8% higher than the average D3 RIN index price for the third quarter of 2025 of $2.19. At September 30th, 2026, we had approximately 0.5 million MMBtu available for RIN generation, 0.1 million RINs generated but unseparated, and 0.1 million RINs separated and unsold. At September 30th, 2025, we had approximately 0.3 million MMBtu available for RIN generation, 0.7 million RINs generated but unseparated, and 10,000 RINs separated and unsold.

Our operating and maintenance expenses for our RNG facilities during the third quarter of 2026 were $16.1 million, an increase of $2.2 million, or 15.4%, compared to $13.9 million during the third quarter of 2025. Our Apex facility operating and maintenance expenses increased approximately $1.0 million, primarily related to increased media changes, waste disposal, flare rent, and utility costs. Our Rumpke facility operating and maintenance expenses increased approximately $0.5 million, primarily related to increased media changes. Our McCarty facility operating and maintenance expenses increased approximately $0.3 million, related to increased media changes and timing of gas processing equipment preventative maintenance. Our Pico facility operating and maintenance expenses increased approximately $0.2 million, primarily related to timing of digester maintenance.

Our Coastal facility operating and maintenance expenses increased approximately $0.2 million, related to increased media changes, waste disposal, and wellfield operational enhancements. We produced approximately 41,000 MWh in renewable electricity during the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of approximately 3,000 MWh, or 6.8%, compared to 44,000 MWh during the third quarter of 2025. Our Tulsa facility produced approximately 1,000 MWh less in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025 as a result of an engine repair that was completed in July 2026.

Our Pico facility produced approximately 1,000 MWh less in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025 as a result of a repair that was completed in August 2026. Revenues from renewable electric facilities during the third quarter of 2026 were $4.3 million, an increase of $24,000 or 0.6% compared to $4.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. Our renewable electricity generation operating and maintenance expenses during the third quarter of 2026 were $5.9 million, an increase of $3.3 million or 126.3% compared to $2.6 million during the third quarter of 2025.

The increase is driven by an increase in non-capitalizable operating costs of approximately $1.9 million at our Montauk Ag Renewables project in Turkey, North Carolina. Our Bowerman facility operating and maintenance expenses increased approximately $0.9 million, primarily related to increased wellfield operational costs and timing of electrical processing preventative maintenance. We recorded approximately $4.1 million related to the cost of RINs distributed from GreenWave and the cost related to pathway dispensing associated with our dispensing RNG in exclusive, unique, and proprietary pathways in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $3.3 million compared to $0.9 million in the third quarter of 2025.

During the third quarter of 2026, we recorded impairments of $0.4 million, an increase of $0.4 million compared to $48,000 in the third quarter of 2025. The increase relates to specifically identified discrete or non-operable assets and were not associated with our expectations of future cash flows. Operating loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $1.0 million, a decrease of $5.4 million compared to an operating income of $4.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. RNG operating income for the third quarter of 2026 was $9.9 million, a decrease of $1.1 million or 10.6% compared to $11.0 million for the third quarter of 2025.

Renewable electricity generation operating loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $3.0 million, an increase of $2.8 million compared to $0.2 million for the third quarter of 2025. Other income in the third quarter of 2026 was $1.6 million, an increase of $2.7 million compared to other expenses of $1.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. In the third quarter of 2026, we recorded approximately $2.6 million in income related to our joint venture investment in GreenWave. There was no such income reported during the third quarter of 2025. We received approximately 1.1 million RINs distributed from GreenWave in the third quarter of 2026.

We sold approximately 0.8 million RINs and recorded revenues from those RINs sold of approximately $2.1 million. Additional information on GreenWave can be found in the supplemental slides that have been posted to our website. Turning to the balance sheet, at September 30th, 2026, $155 million was outstanding under our new senior credit facility. As of September 30th, 2026, we are in compliance with all applicable financial covenants under the new senior credit facility. As Sean mentioned, we executed a non-binding term sheet with the lender of our new senior credit facility to borrow additional debt and forbear and stand still certain debt covenant requirements.

The non-binding term sheet contains terms to borrow additional debt and forbear and stand still certain debt requirements for a period of 12 months in the future, as without such amendment, we believe it is probable that we will not meet our existing debt covenant requirements through the 12 months following the issuance of these financial statements.

To preserve liquidity, in addition to our evaluation of other liquidity and restructuring alternatives, we are temporarily deferring approximately $100 million-$120 million of development capital expenditures and reducing approximately $10 million-$15 million in discretionary and uncommitted maintenance capital expenditures, primarily beginning in 2027, and expect to receive up to $15 million of additional shareholder financing required for the development of our Montauk Ag Renewables project in Turkey, North Carolina.

In addition to pursuing an amendment under our new senior credit facility and the shareholder financing noted above, other potential plans available to us also include limiting development and discretionary capital expenditures, prioritizing expenditures needed to support existing operations and revenue recognition, negotiating payment arrangements with certain vendors, and evaluating other liquidity and restructuring alternatives, including potential asset sales under the amendment being negotiated. Delays in our planned capital expenditures could impact the timing of additional or new production and revenue streams related to our announced development opportunities. If we continue to experience delays with our Turkey facility generating power and producing revenues, we will continue to delay the accretive impact of this facility.

In addition, our delays in other projects due to liquidity concerns could subject us to the loss of deposits and penalties. Notwithstanding the above, there can be no assurance that the company will successfully obtain an amendment to our new senior credit facility to waive financial covenant requirements and borrow additional debt, raise additional capital through other funding sources, or generate sufficient cash flows from operations to sustain operations and fund necessary expenditures within the year following the date of the issuance of our consolidated financial statements. These condensed consolidated financial statements do not include any adjustments that might result from the outcome of these uncertainties.

We had approximately $12.8 million in capital expenditures included within our accounts payable or accrued liabilities at September 30th, 2026. EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 was $9.8 million, a decrease of $3.0 million or 23.6% compared to EBITDA of $12.8 million for the third quarter of 2025.
