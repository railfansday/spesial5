Buffalo Bills DT Ed Oliver questionable for Week 11 vs. Miami Dolphins

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The injuries continue piling up for the Buffalo Bills ahead of their Week 11 home game against the Miami Dolphins. A surprise report originating from the team on Saturday afternoon revealed that Bills starting defensive tackle Ed Oliver is dealing with an oblique injury.

Oliver is now listed as questionable for Sunday's home game, though the news is not all doom and gloom. Matt Parrino provided some context to Oliver's injury, tweeting that "(p)er a league source, Ed Oliver notified Bills training staff this morning that he felt pain in his oblique. The staff checked it out and there's optimism that he'll play against the Dolphins."

Per NFL rules, Oliver's ailment must be accounted for on the injury report even at this late stage, which is why he's being listed as questionable. In the past, Oliver's situation would have likely had him tabbed as "probable" for game day. The NFL has since done away with that injury designation, which has seen the questionable term pick up the slack in filing injuries throughout the league.

Should Oliver be unable to go (or limited in some fashion) it would be a huge blow to Buffalo's defense, which already has to account for injuries to starters in cornerback Christian Benford and inside linebacker Dorian Williams. Both Bills defenders have been ruled out ahead of Week 11.
