Match Group Keeping AI Out of Dating

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Match Group is embracing artificial intelligence across its business, but CEO Spencer Rascoff says AI agents won't be allowed to date for users on Tinder, Hinge and its other apps. Rascoff says Match wants its platforms to remain a “human-only zone,” even as the company uses AI to verify identities, improve safety and block harmful messages. He joins Bloomberg Businessweek Daily to discuss where companies should draw the line on AI and how the technology is transforming Match Group. He also weighs ibn on Jensen Huang’s departure from Nvidia and why he thinks companies are ultimately bigger than any one CEO. (Source: Bloomberg)
