Stock Market Today, Nov. 27: Sandisk Surges 16% as Memory Stocks Rebound From AI-Driven Selloff

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

On Nov. 27, 2026, Sandisk shares climbed as memory names recovered, with investors now watching Micron's earnings and NAND pricing.

By Will Healy – Nov 27, 2026 at 4:48PM EST

Sandisk (SNDK +15.96%), a NAND flash storage solutions provider, surged to $85.36, up 15.96%. Shares rose in premarket and early Friday trading as memory stocks rebounded from an AI-driven sell-off, and investors are now watching Micron's (MU +7.60%) earnings and NAND pricing.Trading volume reached 96.2M shares, coming in roughly 3.6% below its three-month average of 99.9M shares.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC +1.60%) closed at 7,297, up 1.60%, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +2.31%) finished at 24,130, up 2.31%. In semiconductor memory and storage devices, Western Digital (WDC +7.22%) closed at $292.11, up 7.22%, and Seagate Technology Holdings (STX +7.90%) closed at $509.66, up 7.90%, as sector rivals tracked the stronger tone in chip trading.

Sandisk stock experienced a rebound on one factor that investors have forgotten over the last few weeks -- market demand.

Admittedly, the sell-off in semiconductor stocks over the last few weeks is understandable. The uncertainty surrounding Jensen Huang's departure and sudden return to Nvidia (NVDA +0.80%) has cast doubt on whether the massive demand for semiconductors will hold up in the coming quarters.

Moreover, the state of Nvidia and the AI industry at large is critical to Sandisk because data centers need its memory to sustain the rapid build-out. That need came to light when one analyst forecasted Sandisk's growth could slow as soon as early 2027.

However, the fact that this supposed slowdown is not yet a reality also deserves consideration and probably has investors giving thanks after Thanksgiving.

In the first quarter of fiscal 2027, ended (Oct. 2), revenue increased by 378% yearly, and analysts forecast a 313% annual rise when Sandisk reports its fiscal Q2 results early next year.

Furthermore, Micron, another NAND memory company, will release its next earnings report on Dec. 17 and is likely to confirm that its triple-digit annual revenue growth has continued, which should bode well for all NAND memory companies, including Sandisk.

Finally, industry uncertainty has likely contributed to Sandisk's P/E ratio falling to just 5.5. Even with doubts about AI growth, that low valuation probably meant a stock price recovery was inevitable.
