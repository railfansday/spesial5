X-rays reveal fleeting intermediate step as iron-containing molecule responds to light

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A research team has traced how a molecule rearranges its electrons when excited by light. The findings, published in Angewandte Chemie International Edition, help clarify a long-standing question and open new possibilities for studying chemistry in motion, such as bonds breaking or forming—fundamental steps in reactions involving catalysts and biological molecules. The collaboration included Helmholtz-Zentrum Berlin (HZB), the Paul Scherrer Institute (PSI) in Villigen, Switzerland, and European XFEL.
