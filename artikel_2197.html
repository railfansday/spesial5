Ex-Burnley, West Brom & Southampton star Jay Rodriguez names best-ever teammate

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Jay Rodriguez announced his retirement last week, leaving many reminiscing over his time at Southampton, where he played a big part in their mini-Golden era in the early 2010s.

The 37-year-old scored goals wherever he went, whether that was at hometown club Burnley, West Brom or Southampton, but his best scoring season in the Premier League came at St Mary's, where he was one of the talismanic players under Mauricio Pochettino in the 2013/14 campaign.

Saints finished eighth that year, and while the Argentine left the club and numerous key players followed suit, they laid the foundations for numerous years on the fringes of, or actually getting into, Europe, and a run to the EFL Cup final in 2017.

Rodriguez scored 15 times that season, partnering with Rickie Lambert, who netted 13, with the likes of Morgan Schneiderlin, Jack Cork and a young James Ward-Prowse breaking through behind them.

But it was someone else in that Saints side whom the former striker proudly states is the best player he ever played with.

Rodriguez appeared on the 'I Had Trials Once' podcast last week to talk through his career, and spoke with Gaz Seddon and Gary Stopforth about that Southampton side, of which he made his solitary England appearance while playing in.

The trio ran through some of the players in that squad, including Nathaniel Clyne, Luke Shaw and Jose Fonte, alongside some of the names mentioned above, and Rodriguez was then asked if any of those stood out as "top" players while playing with them.

"Lallana," the ex-Saints man said, pretty easily. "I always say it in everything; he's the best I've ever played with.

"Everything, technical, tactical, as a captain, he'd kill you if you weren't working hard in training, because he'd be working to his maximum. He's really passionate; he loves football."

Lallana was part of the Saints class who departed to 'bigger' things after that eighth-placed finish in the Premier League in 2013/14. He, Lambert and Dejan Lovren all left for Liverpool, while Luke Shaw went to Manchester United and Calum Chambers signed for Arsenal.

There's little surprise in Rodriguez stating that Lallana was the best footballer he'd ever played with, as the now-retired 38-year-old oozed class all the way from his time in League One with Southampton to the Premier League at Liverpool.

The midfielder was one of the driving forces in Saints' double promotion under Nigel Adkins, scoring 23 and assisting 15 across both League One campaigns and then registering 11 goals and 10 assists in their Championship promotion term in 2011/12.

Even the Premier League didn't seem too large a step-up, and when he moved to Anfield in 2014, he was one of the more important players in the early Jürgen Klopp days after the German boss became Liverpool manager in 2015.

Lallana netted eight and assisted seven in the 2016/17 Premier League campaign, but a hamstring injury the following year saw him miss 31 games. Unfortunately, he could never really get a lengthy run without breaking down after that as a Liverpool player.

At Brighton and Hove Albion, he never played more than 1,600 league minutes in each of his four years there, with his minutes being managed even when he was fit to play, and he retired after a year back at Southampton during their most recent Premier League campaign in 2024/25.

Nearly a decade on from that initial hamstring injury, questions are still being asked regarding what his career could have looked like if he had remained healthy while Liverpool were starting to find their feet under Klopp, en route to becoming one of the best clubs in the World.

Rodriguez retires having been one of the more underrated goalscorers in the EFL over the 14 or so years he was in the limelight.

More often than not, he'd go under the radar towards a double-digit scoring campaign regardless of whether he was in the Championship or the Premier League, with his best year coming at West Brom in 2018/19, where both he and strike partner Dwight Gayle netted over 20 each for a Baggies side who finished in the play-offs.

Even back in 2022/23, a then-33-year-old Rodriguez netted ten for the Vincent Kompany-led Burnley side en route to the Championship title. From then, injuries and a place further down the pecking order stopped him from making much of an impact, both at Turf Moor and then at Wrexham.

Now the boots are hung up, it's fun to look back on some of the squads he was part of, but no doubt that early Southampton side in the Premier League with Adam Lallana pulling the strings in the midfield was the most entertaining.
