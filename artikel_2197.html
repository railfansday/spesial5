Portsmouth seriously considering move for 21-year-old left-back with Sheffield United also keen

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Portsmouth and Sheffield United will already have eyes on potential January transfer window targets as they bid to strengthen their squads for the second half of the 2026/27 campaign. And, according to reporter Rudy Galetti, the Championship pair could go toe-to-toe for one target.

Galetti states that Lyngby BK left-back Renzo Tytens has drawn interest from Pompey, the Blades and clubs in France. At present though, it is the Fratton Park club who are leading the race for his signature and they are ‘seriously considering’ a move for his services.

Portsmouth are said to have followed Tytens closely for the past two months as they look to strengthen at left-back. Among the other interested clubs are Toulouse FC, who play in Ligue 1.

Fans of Portsmouth and Sheffield United can be forgiven if this is the first they’re hearing of Tytens. He’s a 21-year-old left-back playing his football in Denmark after spells in the academies of Belgian sides KV Mechelen and Club Brugge, and Dutch giants PSV Eindhoven.

His senior breakthrough has come at Lygnby BK though, who he joined in January. They have struggled in the Danish Superliga with two wins in 14 games to date, but Tytens has given a good account of himself.

In the ongoing campaign, Tytens has chipped in with one goal in his 14 league appearances, starting every match. His underlying numbers are solid, with 58 duels won, a 72.2% aerial duel win rate, 833 touches, 77 defensive contributions and 96 line-breaking passes (all stats via FotMob).

He stands at 180cm tall and at 21, there is plenty of room for him to develop his game. Tytens is under contract until 2030 though, so it would likely take a decent fee for one of Pompey or the Blades to bring him in.

Of the two Championship admirers named, it does feel as though Portsmouth are a more likely destination for Tytens. Amid injury to Connor Ogilvie, loan prospect Jayden Meghoma is the only natural first-team left-back available to John Mousinho at present.

At 21, Tytens could be a future starter for Pompey on the left. He can be the long-term successor to Ogilvie that Jacob Farrell was meant to be before the Australian hit cruel injury luck.

There’s no hiding that Sheffield United could be an attractive destination, but they have Harrison Burrows and Sam McCallum on the books. It means that Tytens would find game time hard to come by.

Toulouse and other overseas admirers could have a say in the battle yet, but Pompey seem well-placed to get a deal done if they do make a move.
