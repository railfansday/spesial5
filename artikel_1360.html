Join Paul Gascoigne For A Special Afternoon At Hill Dickinson Stadium

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Football fans have the chance to enjoy a special afternoon with England legend and former Everton midfielder Paul Gascoigne at Hill Dickinson Stadium on Saturday 5 December (2pm).

'An Afternoon with Paul Gascoigne’ will take place in The Yard from 2pm, with Sky Sports’ Alan Myers hosting an exclusive conversation with one of English football’s most iconic and entertaining personalities.

Widely regarded as one of the most naturally-gifted players of his generation, Gazza earned 57 England caps, scored 10 international goals and became a national hero with his performances at Italia ’90 and his unforgettable goal against Scotland at Euro ’96.

His remarkable club career included spells with Newcastle United, Tottenham Hotspur, Lazio and Rangers before he joined Everton in 2000, where he spent almost two seasons playing under Walter Smith at Goodison Park.

Now, more than 25 years after first pulling on the Royal Blue shirt, Gazza will share memories of his time at Everton, the dressing room characters and former teammates, and recall fascinating stories from his career on some of football’s biggest stages.

Expect plenty of laughs, behind-the-scenes stories and unforgettable football memories as Gazza reflects on the moments and the people that shaped his extraordinary career.
