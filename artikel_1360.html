Wayne Rooney Named His Pick For ‘Best Dribbler’ in Football History

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Wayne Rooney is widely considered one of the finest talents English football has ever produced. Alongside his achievements on the international stage, he established himself as one of the most iconic players to wear the Manchester United shirt.

After spending much of his career competing at the highest level, Rooney had the opportunity to play alongside some outstanding footballers and test himself against some of the game's biggest names. His experience makes his perspective on various footballing debates particularly interesting.

In 2025, Rooney was challenged to name the greatest dribbler in football history, and there was little doubt about who he believed deserved that distinction.

Despite playing alongside Cristiano Ronaldo for several years at Manchester United — where the Portuguese star regularly showcased his incredible dribbling skills during his younger days — Rooney opted for a player who was one of Ronaldo's biggest rivals. When asked who he considered the greatest dribbler ever, his answer was short and decisive: "Messi".

While Rooney didn't expand on his choice during that particular discussion, his admiration for the Barcelona legend has been evident for years. In 2018, the former Manchester United forward hailed Messi as the greatest player in football history, looking back at United's Champions League final losses against Barcelona in 2009 and 2011, both of which saw the Argentine produce decisive performances.

"In both games if we had been against any other team but Barcelona you would have fancied us to win. In my eyes they are the best team ever and Messi is the best player ever. What he gets to do is incredible. Messi has just got a bit of everything.

"Ronaldo has gone from a winger to a striker and a goalscorer. Messi a bit of everything - you see him scoring goals from playing probably a deep midfield role at times, and where I'm probably still walking on the pitch he's scoring. So he is probably the best."

It is easy to understand Rooney's verdict when considering Messi's extraordinary close control and ability to beat opponents. Even at just 20 years old, Messi was completing an average of 8.4 successful dribbles per game — more than any other player featured in the StatsBomb database.

Pep Guardiola, who managed Barcelona during Messi's emergence as a global superstar, regularly admired the Argentine's efficiency and simplicity in possession. The former manager described him as “a player who doesn’t rely on unnecessary tricks,” explaining that Messi rarely needed stepovers or extravagant flicks to get past defenders because of his remarkable effectiveness.

Jose Mourinho also provided a memorable summary of just how difficult Messi was to defend against:

"When Messi is one-on-one, you are dead."

Across football history, only a select few players — including Diego Maradona and Garrincha — can be placed alongside Messi for their extraordinary dribbling ability. However, Rooney had the unique experience of facing the Argentine during his own playing career, most notably in those two Champions League finals. Having witnessed Messi's quality first-hand, it is understandable why he regards him as the greatest dribbler of all time.

He mentioned two names but gave the nod to one Man United legend.
