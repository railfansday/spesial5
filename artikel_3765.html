Not only have several members of the Treehouse abandoned us, citing flimsy excuses like "James, you remember I've got that holiday booked, right?," many that remain have forsaken their duties and are playing something called a Play Station. What, all those Not GTA retrospectives we ran this week not inspiring enough for you? Can't even wait an indeterminate amount of time for a completely unconfirmed PC port? If you're not down for lots of Red Dead Redemption 2-esque world interactivity in lavishly-rendered Miami it may not be your bag, but I like that kind of thing alongside my wheely stealing, so it is mine.

Julian, Edwin, Callum and Dion either aren't here, or have once again failed to invite me to one of the team-building Hide & Seek games.

And you, reader dear, what are you playing this weekend?...
