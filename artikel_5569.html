Bella Hadid Purchases $5.5 Million ‘Enchanting Creekside Compound’ in Colorado

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Supermodel Bella Hadid is Colorado-bound.

The Victoria’s Secret angel snapped up a gorgeous creekside compound in Crested Butte, CO, according to the Wall Street Journal.

Hadid, 30, purchased the three-bedroom, three-bathroom retreat for $5.5 million—a sharp decrease from its $6,995,000 asking price, set in June this year.

Listing agent Channing Boucher of LIV Sotheby’s International Realty told the publication that the daughter of the former “Real Housewives of Beverly Hills” star has already resided elsewhere in the Crested Butte area for “several months.”

In fact, she recently gushed about the state in a Nov. 9 Instagram post, shared after attending a Colorado Avalanche hockey game. Hadid posted that she's a "Colorado girl at heart" and that it "was only a matter of time before" she would find her way to the team.

It seems as if Hadid has found a perfect slice of the Centennial State to call her own.

“Tucked away on a private alley in the most desired west side of town, this enchanting creekside compound is a secret sanctuary, impossible to replicate in character or setting,” the listing boasted.

The home is nestled on a 7,841-square-foot lot with 200 feet of Coal Creek frontage. While the dwelling itself was constructed in 2001, it’s a historical property that incorporates two “original 100-year-old coal sheds,” which have been lovingly restored to serve as livable spaces.

Inside, an open floor plan features “soaring 30-foot ceilings,” and design details salvaged from century-old structures include the oversized ceilings beams sourced from a historic Columbia River warehouse, along with iron bolts and other charming items.

“The line between old and new vanish in the craftsmanship,” reads the listing, which also touts the weathered lumber used to construct the exterior and the 18th-century Virgina barn hardwood floors.

It’s easy to see why Hadid would choose the idyllic property as an escape. The listing explains that the home is in a secluded spot at the end of a dead-end gravel lane with only one other home, “free from traffic, tourists, and noise.”

The primary suite is in its own wing and constructed from a former coal shed. Skylights provide stunning views of the natural landscape, while the luxurious bathroom makes for a comfortable sanctuary.

The outdoor space is a striking complement to the intentionally designed home. It includes a creekside patio for dining and entertaining, and a soothing hot tub is located just at the edge of the water.

Though the home seems as if it exists in a world of its own, it’s not far from the vibrant attractions Crested Butte has to offer, including upscale dining and boutique shopping.

Hadid's family has been known to seek out residences that allow them to connect with nature. Mom Yolanda Hadid has a stunning Pennsylvania farm, which she previously told Realtor.com® is a place where she can “heal and reconnect with Mother Earth.”

"Eight years ago, after my divorce and years of battling chronic neurological Lyme disease, I needed to get away from the noise of city life," she explained. "I longed for a sanctuary where I could heal and reconnect with Mother Earth just the way I grew up in Holland.

"When I found the farm, I instantly fell in love. … There were no gates at that time, so I parked on the main road and walked down the long driveway; the energy was unbelievable. I instantly knew I was home even before I saw the buildings."

Yolanda listed the property for $10.88 million at the end of 2025, but pulled it off the market just three months later. However, it is understood that the delisting is temporary, and the property will most likely return to the market in the future.
