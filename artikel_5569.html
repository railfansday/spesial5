Trump threatened to double Mexico tariffs immediately over migrant caravans

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The president gave Mexico no timeline to act, and the peso slipped to session lows after his social media post

US President Donald Trump speaks in the Oval Office of the White House in Washington, DC on July 14, 2025. (Andrew Caballero-Reynolds/AFP via Getty Images)

President Donald Trump threatened on Thursday to make tariffs on Mexico twice as high unless the country halts migrant caravans that he says are heading to the U.S., according to Bloomberg.

"MEXICO MUST STOP THEM, NOW, and if they do not do so, the Tariffs on Mexico will be IMMEDIATELY DOUBLED," Trump wrote in a social media post. "They will not be accepted!" He wrote that Mexico, the governor of Texas, Border Patrol and ICE had been informed, and added: "There will be no leniency!"

The group is an estimated 1,500 people, most of them from Central America, according to Reuters. They left the southern Mexican city of Tapachula, near the Guatemala border, on Tuesday. Reuters also reported that the migrants are hoping last week's Democratic midterm win in the U.S. could make it possible for them to settle and find jobs there.

Trump did not say how soon Mexico would have to respond, according to Bloomberg. Bloomberg also said it was uncertain whether the president can lawfully raise the levies twofold, given a Supreme Court ruling from earlier this year that limited his tariff powers.

Mexico's peso weakened after the threat, falling to its lowest point of the trading session.
