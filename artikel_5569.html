Roaring Kitty's Mario post sends Nintendo stock higher

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

GameStop stock also rose Thursday as the wider market fell, after the Roaring Kitty account posted an image of Cat Mario on X

Roaring Kitty, the online persona of investor Keith Gill, posted an image of Nintendo's Mario character on X $TWTR on Thursday, and Nintendo's American depositary receipts rose 3.2%.

The image showed Cat Mario, a form of the character that debuted in “Super Mario 3D World,” according to Bloomberg. The post carried no caption.

GameStop $GME stock rose 1.5% on Thursday, according to Bloomberg. The broader market declined, with all 11 S&P 500 sectors lower.

"Nothing else is working," Matt Maley, chief market strategist at Miller Tabak & Co., told Bloomberg. "The allocation to retail investors for Anthropic was small, so they're not benefiting much from that IPO. With all 11 S&P sectors in the red today, they're looking for anything they can sink their teeth into."

Gill first drew widespread attention in 2021's meme-stock episode, when his involvement helped send GameStop shares soaring, according to Bloomberg. He has made few posts on X since 2024, and several have moved stocks.

In May, GameStop and Chewy shares climbed after Gill's account put up and later removed an image of a cat, along with a second image showing Pepe the Frog in Roaring Kitty's red bandana, according to Bloomberg.
