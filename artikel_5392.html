Bloomberg Indices and HKEX Launch Co-branded Offshore RMB Bond Index Family to Support Growing Investor Demand in the Dim Sum Bond Market

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

New index suite helps meet growing demand for index-based investment products in offshore RMB bonds

HONG KONG, Nov. 15, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Bloomberg Index Services Limited ("BISL") today announced the launch of a new family of offshore RMB bond indices in collaboration with HKEX FICC Indices Services Limited, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEX), providing investors and asset managers with transparent measures of the offshore RMB bond market, commonly referred to as the Dim Sum bond market.

HKEX earlier today introduced the HKEX Offshore RMB Bond Index, a benchmark administered and calculated by BISL. Serving as the parent benchmark for the broader index suite, it forms the basis of a family of co-branded Bloomberg HKEX indices designed for use across investment products including passive investment strategies such as exchange-traded funds (ETFs), as well as active portfolio management benchmarking and market analysis.

In addition to the standard index family, BISL may develop customized indices based on the framework to support specific investment objectives and product needs of asset managers and other market participants.

The new indices screen bonds from the flagship Bloomberg Offshore Renminbi (CNH) Index, which includes eligible RMB-denominated Treasury, government-related and corporate debt securities issued in offshore China markets. An additional eligibility criterion requires securities to be listed on The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK) and/or lodged with the Central Moneymarkets Unit (CMU).

Using data as of the end of October, 2026, the Bloomberg HKEX Offshore RMB Bond Index universe would include 305 bonds representing approximately CNH 864 billion in market value. Government-related bonds accounted for 49.59% of the index, followed by corporate bonds (29.19%) and Treasury securities (21.22%). Issuers from China and Hong Kong represented the largest regional exposures in the index at 88.49%, followed by the United States (5.17%) and Singapore (1.98%).

"Bloomberg has a long history of providing transparent, rules-based fixed income indices to global investors, acting as a pioneer in supporting the development of local bond markets, exemplified by the inclusion of China onshore government and policy bank bonds into our flagship Global Aggregate Index," said Ji Zhuang, Head of Asia Pacific for Bloomberg Index Services Limited. "Together with HKEX, we're supporting the continued development of the Dim Sum bond market and our shared vision for RMB internationalization. We're encouraged by the strong interest from ETF issuers exploring products linked to the index family and look forward to supporting the growth of index-based investment solutions in the market."

Bonnie Y Chan, HKEX Chief Executive Officer, said: "Hong Kong is the world's leading offshore RMB financial hub, and the launch of the HKEX Offshore RMB Bond Index is a significant step forward in advancing its fixed-income market from an issuance centre towards an ecosystem with greater depth, liquidity and investability. By establishing a transparent and widely recognised benchmark anchored in Hong Kong's market infrastructure, we are supporting greater visibility and investability of offshore RMB assets, encouraging broader market participation and further advancing the internationalisation of the RMB. We are delighted to collaborate with Bloomberg, whose global index expertise and market reach will help broaden access to and deepen participation in the offshore RMB bond market."

Xinrong Wu, General Manager of E Fund and Chairman of E Fund HK, said: "As the offshore RMB bond market continues to expand rapidly, investor interest in allocating to and trading these bonds keeps growing. This index delivers the first transparent, trackable and product-ready benchmark for the offshore RMB bond market, precisely addressing a critical market need and laying a solid foundation for product innovation. As one of the first authorised managers, E Fund will utilize the Group's fixed-income investment and research capabilities, together with E Fund HK's local product development and distribution experience, to accelerate the launch of innovative index-linked products and help investors allocate to offshore RMB assets more efficiently. We look forward to working with HKEX, Bloomberg and all market participants to drive the offshore RMB bond market towards greater depth and breadth."

Bloomberg clients can access the new indices through {IN }, Bloomberg's index search and discovery function on the Bloomberg Terminal. Research and index methodology documents are available on the Bloomberg Indices Documentation page.
