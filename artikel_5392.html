ZTO Reports Third Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Results

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

10.2 Billion Parcels Expanded Market Share to 20.1%Adjusted Net Income Increased to RMB2.6 Billion

SHANGHAI, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- ZTO Express (Cayman) Inc. (NYSE: ZTO and SEHK: 2057), a leading and fast-growing express delivery company in China ("ZTO" or the "Company"), today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026[1]. The Company grew parcel volume by 7.0% year over year while maintaining high quality of service and customer satisfaction. Adjusted net income increased 2.6%[2] to RMB2.6 billion. Net cash generated from operating activities was RMB3.7 billion.

Mr. Meisong Lai, Founder, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of ZTO, commented, "In the third quarter of 2026, ZTO held to its strategic principle of high-quality service, high-quality market share and low end-to-end costs. Parcel volume reached 10.2 billion, up 7.0% year over year and 4.1 percentage points ahead of the industry average. Operating profit rose 23.8% to RMB2.98 billion, and adjusted net income was RMB2.6 billion. Daily average retail parcel volume grew 57% year over year and accelerated from the second quarter as higher-value services scaled up generating a favorable mix shift that lifted margin."

Mr. Lai added, "China's express-delivery industry continued to benefit from constructive regulation, and anti-involution has become an industry consensus rather than a policy slogan. Competition is more rational, pricing gradually recovered into the peak season, and competition shifted from a price focus to quality and value. ZTO's Quality-First approach, built on industry-leading efficiency and equitable network policies, is driving that shift into results. We are not trading long-term quality for short-term market share, and we are not buying volume at any price. Rooted in Shared Success culture, we help our partners and couriers earn more while keeping reasonable profit for the company. This is how we are able to sail through any industry and economic cycles."

Ms. Huiping Yan, Chief Financial Officer of ZTO, commented, "For the third quarter this year, our core express ASP rose 13.7% year over year, which was supported by an improved revenue mix from higher-value key-accounts, including rapidly expanding reverse-logistics business. Despite cost pressure from high oil prices, combined unit sorting and transportation costs decreased by 2 cents, thanks to end-to-end digitization and lean operations. SG&A, excluding SBC, was approximately 4.2% of revenue, down from 5.3% in the same period last year. Adjusted net income grew 20.7% year over year taking into consideration of RMB376 million tax refund received in the same quarter last year. Operating cash flow was RMB3.7 billion, and capital expenditure totaled RMB1.3 billion."

Ms. Yan added, "Our results reflected our simple but consistent financial discipline: we seek profitable growth through scale leverage without sacrificing fair price, optimize asset utilization and rely on technological innovations to stay cost competitive. With volume growth decelerated, we are directing focus and effort on building last mile cost advantage and strengthening earnings by our franchise partners and couriers. With that, we are maintaining our parcel-volume growth guidance for the year, and are well positioned to further expand our industry volume leadership."

Third Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Results

Total Revenues were RMB14,361.6 million (US$2,142.1 million), increased 21.0% from RMB11,864.7 million in the same period of 2025. Revenue from the core express delivery business increased by 21.6% compared to the same period of 2025 as a result of a 7.0% growth in parcel volume and a 13.7% increase in parcel unit price. Within core express delivery revenue, key account revenue, generated by direct sales organizations, increased by 68.3% mainly driven by increase in e-commerce return parcels. Revenue from freight forwarding services decreased by 8.1% compared to the same period of 2025. Revenue from sales of accessories, largely consisted of sales of thermal paper for digital waybills, increased by 2.8%. Other revenues were mainly derived from financing services.

Total cost of revenues was RMB10,829.3 million (US$1,615.2 million), an increase of 21.6% from RMB8,908.7 million in the same period last year.

Line-haul transportation cost was RMB3,327.7 million (US$496.3 million), increased 0.8% from RMB3,302.0 million in the same period last year. The unit transportation cost decreased 5.9% or 2 cents mainly attributable to better economies of scale and improved load rate through more effective route planning offsetting higher diesel prices.

Sorting hub operating cost was RMB2,595.9 million (US$387.2 million), increased 8.4% from RMB2,394.1 million in the same period last year. The increase primarily consisted of (i) RMB110.8 million (US$16.5 million) increase in labor-associated costs partially offset by automation-driven efficiency improvements, and (ii) RMB66.0 million (US$9.8 million) increase in depreciation and amortization costs associated with automation facilities and equipment upgrades. As of September 30, 2026, there were 805 sets of automated sorting equipment in service, compared to 761 sets as of September 30, 2025.

Cost of accessories sold was RMB139.9 million (US$20.9 million), increased by 3.2% compared with RMB135.6 million in the same period last year.

Other costs were RMB4,570.4 million (US$681.7 million), increased 59.1% from RMB2,872.2 million in the same period last year, which was mainly due to an increase of RMB1,665.7 million (US$248.4 million) for pickup and dispatching costs paid to network partners associated with serving key account customers, primarily for handling e-commerce return parcels.

Gross Profit was RMB3,532.4 million (US$ 526.9 million), increased by 19.5% from RMB2,956.0 million in the same period last year. Gross margin rate was 24.6% compared to 24.9% in the same period last year.

Total Operating Expenses were RMB555.9 million (US$82.9 million), compared to RMB550.9 million in the same period last year.

Selling, general and administrative expenses were RMB599.3 million (US$89.4 million), decreased by 5.3% from RMB632.6 million in the same period last year, mainly due to (i) RMB13.3 million (US$2.0 million) increase in compensation and benefit expenses, offset by (ii) RMB44.6 million (US$6.7 million) decrease in depreciation and amortization costs associated with administrative facilities and equipment.

Other operating income, net was RMB43.4 million (US$6.5 million), compared to RMB81.7 million in the same period last year. Other operating income mainly consisted of (i) RMB106.6 million (US$15.9 million) in losses on disposal and impairment of fixed assets, (ii) RMB94.7 million (US$14.1 million) of government subsidies and tax rebates, and (iii) RMB55.3 million (US$8.2 million) of rental and other income.

Income from operations was RMB2,976.5 million (US$444.0 million), increased 23.8% from RMB2,405.0 million for the same period last year. The operating margin rate increased to 20.7% from 20.3% in the same period last year.

Interest income was RMB103.1 million (US$15.4 million), compared with RMB185.2 million in the same period last year.

Interest expenses was RMB48.0 million (US$7.2 million), compared with RMB54.4 million in the same period last year.

Gain from fair value changes of financial instruments was RMB103.5 million (US$15.4 million), compared with a gain of RMB102.3 million in the same period last year. Such gain or loss from fair value changes of the financial instruments is quoted by commercial banks according to market-based estimation of future redemption prices.

ZTO Express (Cayman) Inc. (NYSE: ZTO and SEHK: 2057) ("ZTO" or the "Company") is a leading and fast-growing express delivery company in China. ZTO provides express delivery service as well as other value-added logistics services through its extensive and reliable nationwide network coverage in China.

ZTO operates a highly scalable network partner model, which the Company believes is best suited to support the significant growth of e-commerce in China. The Company leverages its network partners to provide pickup and last-mile delivery services, while controlling the mission-critical line-haul transportation and sorting network within the express delivery service value chain.
