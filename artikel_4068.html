Xbox Fans Make Their Feelings Known In Response To Yooka-Laylee Upgrade Announcement

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Update: Studio confirms lack of Xbox version

Update [Fri 27th Nov 2026, 3:38pm]: Developer Playtonic has now confirmed we won't be getting an Xbox version (at least at launch). We've shared their statement elsewhere on Pure Xbox:

News Yooka-Laylee Developer Issues Statement On Why Definitive Edition Is Skipping Xbox

"...just isn't viable for us at this time"

Original Story: Here's a strange one for you! The fantastic Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair has been one of the best platformers on Xbox since it launched back in 2019, but the game's new "Definitive Edition" appears to be skipping Series X and S.

A new announcement today revealed that the upgraded Definitive Edition will include enhanced visuals, up to 4K resolution at 120FPS, two orchestrated soundtracks and more, but it will only be available on PC, Switch 2 and PS5.

As you can imagine, that hasn't gone down well with Xbox fans.

The comments section of Playtonic Games' post is full of reactions like these, and at the time of writing, no response or statement has been provided for why the Definitive Edition isn't coming to Xbox in addition to other platforms.

There may well be a very good reason for it, and here's hoping we might get clarification at some point in the near future, but at least for the time being, it seems we're going to have to miss out on this new enhanced version.

As disappointing as this is, the 2019 original is still well worth playing if you're interested!
