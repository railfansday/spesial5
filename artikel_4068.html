GTA 5, Silksong, Baldur’s Gate 3, And More Nominated For Steam Hall Of Fame

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Valve has introduced a brand-new category to The 2026 Steam Awards — Hall of Fame.

Every single GOTY winner from the last ten years, from PUBG: Battlegrounds all the way through to Hollow Knight: Silksong, is going head-to-head for the crown. It's a pretty solid lineup:

FromSoftware and Rockstar are the only devs to have two nominations for Hall of Fame.

There are a couple of games that feel out of place (cough, Village, cough), and for my money, Titanfall 2 deserves the 2016 spot, but like I said, solid lineup. My vote's going to Red Dead Redemption 2, which I hope wins to make up for its Game Awards snub. Failing that, Baldur's Gate 3, Elden Ring, and The Witcher 3 all feel like worthy winners, but it's down to the community who takes home the crown.

You have until November 30 to make your nominations, and then voting begins in earnest on December 17.

Otherwise, there are the standard categories: Game of the Year, VR Game of the Year, Labor of Love, Best Handheld Game, Just Five More Minutes, Defies Description, This Is My Job Now, Better With Friends, Outstanding Visual Style, Best Game You Suck At, Best Soundtrack, Outstanding Story-Rich Game, and Sit Back and Relax.

Geoff Keighley has just revealed all nominees for The Game Awards 2026, and you can find them right here.

And with GTA 6 not on PC yet, the winner here isn't a foregone conclusion. Of course, we're only in the nomination phase, so there's no list of GOTY potentials to share yet. You can enter any game that came out after the last Autumn Sale for each award (excluding Labor of Love), but obviously, niche picks are less likely to make the cut.

If you complete all four tasks — nominate a game in at least one category, nominate a game in each category, play a game you've nominated, and review (or update your review) for a game you've nominated — you'll earn yourself a unique 2026 Steam Awards badge that you can proudly display on your profile. There are four tiers, one for each task completed, but they're easy enough to do, so it's worth taking ten minutes to sit down and fill out the forum.

The nominees for 2026's Game of the Year are in. Time to play them all if you haven't already.
