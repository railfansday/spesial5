KFC reveals Avengers: Doomsday menu with black burgers, green mayo and ‘Doom Dust’

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

KFC has announced a collaboration with Marvel Studios ahead of Avengers: Doomsday, introducing a limited-time menu inspired by Doctor Doom.

The fast-food chain is teaming up with Marvel for the first time, bringing a range of themed burgers, wings, fries, drinks, and collectibles to restaurants from November 9.

Although it will be available in the UK, through January 2027, the United States will miss out.

The new menu features several items inspired by the Marvel villain, including the Doom Burger, which combines KFC’s Original Recipe chicken with lettuce, pickles, cheese, Doom Cola sauce, and Smoky Green Mayo inside a black sesame bun.

The Doom Cola sauce is described as a sweet, sticky cola glaze with smoky BBQ notes, while the green mayo and Doom Dust seasoning add more unusual flavors to the lineup.

Other menu items include Doom Dripped Wings coated in the cola sauce, Doom Loaded Fries topped with both sauces, and a Doom Bucket featuring chicken, dipping sauces, and Doom Dust.

There’s also a Doom Supreme Fizz, featuring sparkling lemonade, green apple or kiwi syrup, and boba pearls, alongside Masked Mozza Melts served with the themed sauces.

However, some items won’t be available in some countries, with KFC saying it will vary between markets.

The collaboration also introduces five collectible Colonel Sanders figurines, each dressed in costumes inspired by Avengers: Doomsday characters. These will be available with qualifying menu purchases in select markets while supplies last.

All themed menu items will also come in special Doctor Doom packaging featuring the villain’s iconic mask.

The campaign also includes an advertisement showing Doctor Doom overlooking a devastated New York City following a battle with the Avengers, with one KFC restaurant mysteriously left standing.

“This is KFC’s most expansive joint venture to date, and we can’t wait to welcome guests into our extension of the MCU,” said KFC Global CEO Scott Mezvinsky.

“We’re not just teaming up with the most anticipated movie of the decade. We’re leaning into its biggest, baddest character, and delivering a menu worthy of the moment.”

The promotion arrives ahead of Marvel Studios’ Avengers: Doomsday, which is scheduled to hit theaters on December 18, 2026.
