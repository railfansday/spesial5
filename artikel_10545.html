Grindr boss defends dating app’s $120 premium sub after it used to cost $499 a month

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Grindr boss George Arison has defended the platform’s $120 premium subscription tier, noting it is only aimed at a “small” portion of their users.

At this point, pretty much every dating app offers a premium subscription for users who are struggling to find love. These bonuses come at a cost, typically giving you unlimited swipes and better matches along the way.

However, Edge quickly came under fire online after users of the world’s biggest LGBTQ dating app noted that it could cost up to $6,000 to use it for the year. Some screenshots showed the price ranging from $80 to $499.99 per month.

Speaking to the BBC, Grindr’s George Arison noted that the premium AI-powered subscription was aimed at a “very small community of users” to offer them a “very different” experience on the app.

“I think we’re a capitalist society, and everything hinges on whether you can afford it or not,” the Grindr CEO added.

The price of the subscription has settled at $120 per month, but Arison noted that it could increase in the future. He told the BBC that Edge users “being willing to pay more for that set of features will result in us being able to subsidise the product for everybody else. It’s a choice that people make on whether they want to be on it.”

As noted, other apps have expensive premium services too. Tinder launched a $499 subscription of its own called Tinder Select back in 2023.
