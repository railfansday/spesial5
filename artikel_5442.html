HomeServices of America Names Maria Latushkin Chief Technology Officer

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

HomeServices of America has announced the appointment of Maria Latushkin as chief technology officer, a newly created role with enterprise-wide responsibility for technology strategy, development, engineering, infrastructure and IT operations. Latushkin brings more than 20 years of technology leadership at some of the nation’s best-known consumer-facing businesses.

According to a release, Latushkin will report to Chris Kelly, president and CEO of HomeServices of America, and will oversee all technology initiatives and IT functions across HomeServices, HSF Affiliates, Prosperity Home Mortgage, HomeServices Insurance and HomeServices Title. The structure brings greater alignment, coordination, and accountability to how the company invests in, builds, deploys and supports technology.

“Technology is central to how real estate professionals win business and serve their clients, and our agents deserve the strongest tools in the industry,” said Kelly. “Maria has a track record of delivering. She has built and scaled technology for major consumer brands, and she knows how to turn investment into results people can see. Her leadership will help us implement a robust technology package that serves both consumers and our agents and keep improving it long after launch.”

The release notes most recently, Latushkin served as group vice president of Technology and Engineering at Albertsons Companies. As part of the senior leadership team, she helped guide technology and product strategy across retail, payments, customer service and supply chain operations. She led a global technology organization of approximately 1,000 people and oversaw more than $400 million in annual operating expenditures. Her work included advancing artificial intelligence and modernizing digital platforms to improve operational performance and better connect the business with its customers.

Earlier in her career, Latushkin served as chief technology officer at Omada Health, Narvar and One Kings Lane, and held technology leadership roles at Peet’s Coffee and Walmart.com. She has built and scaled consumer-facing technology, modernized e-commerce platforms and improved digital customer experiences, with a focus on measurable business results, the company stated.

For HomeServices operating companies, agents and employees, HomeServices says the new structure means a more coordinated approach to technology and faster delivery of new capabilities. Platforms will be enhanced more quickly with a strong focus on adoption and giving agents and consumers a more complete real estate experience.  As the company continues the rollout of Maestro, OnePoint Digital and its broader technology ecosystem, beginning November 30, Latushkin will focus on continuously improving those tools and expanding what they can do.

For more information, visit www.homeservices.com.
