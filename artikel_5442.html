Hewlett Packard Enterprise is adding a former JPMorgan banker to its board

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Jennifer Nason, JPMorgan's former global chair of investment banking, joins as HPE amends its Elliott cooperation agreement

Hewlett Packard Enterprise appointed a former JPMorgan $JPM executive to its board of directors on Wednesday. Jennifer Nason, who was global chair of investment banking at JPMorgan, became a director effective that day, the company said.

Nason retired from JPMorgan in February 2025, the company said. In her role there, she counseled HPE while it weighed buying Juniper Networks $JNPR. The deal was valued at $14 billion, according to The Wall Street Journal.

HPE and activist investor Elliott Investment Management also signed a second amendment to their cooperation agreement on Wednesday. After the 2026 annual meeting closes, the board will not exceed 15 directors until the 2027 annual meeting. If the agreement's expiration date has not occurred by then, the board will not exceed 13 directors from the close of the 2027 meeting until that date, according to the filing. The amendment allows HPE to add Nason, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Nason will receive pro-rata portions of the annual equity and cash retainers that apply to all non-employee directors for the rest of the current board year, the company said. After that, she will take part in the full director compensation program. HPE said she has no family relationships with any executive officer or director and no arrangements or understandings with any other person tied to her appointment.

"She brings tremendous strategic judgment, deep knowledge of the technology industry and HPE, and a global perspective that will make our Board even stronger as HPE executes its strategy and creates long-term value for shareholders," board Chair Pat Russo said in a statement.

"I look forward to contributing my experience and perspective as the company expands on its recent performance," Nason said in a statement.

Nason was global head of technology, media and telecommunications investment banking at JPMorgan from 2004 to 2012, the company said. She was a managing director focused on telecommunications investment banking from 1998 to 2004. She serves as a director at Accenture $ACN and Rio Tinto and is a trustee of Dodge & Cox Funds.

HPE signed its original cooperation agreement with Elliott on July 16, 2025, and the two parties amended it a first time on May 29, according to the filing. Elliott built a position in HPE worth over $1.5 billion in April 2025, according to The Wall Street Journal. HPE added Elliott partner Chris Hsu to its board this spring under the agreement.
