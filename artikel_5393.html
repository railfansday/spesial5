Can Amazon's Ad Revenue Growth Continue to Underpin the Stock's Value?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

AMZN's advertising revenues are poised for high-teens growth in Q4 2026, supported by AI-powered ad tools and live sports despite rising infrastructure costs.

Amazon $AMZN.com's AMZN advertising business remains one of the main supports for its earnings, and fourth-quarter 2026 guidance points to a rebound after a third quarter distorted by event timing. Amazon expects year-over-year advertising revenues to grow in the high teens in the fourth quarter of 2026. That would build on $21.3 billion of ad revenues in the year-ago quarter. The ad outlook sits within total fourth-quarter net sales guidance of $240.0-$247.0 billion, up 12-16%. Operating income is guided at $28.5-$33.5 billion compared with $25 billion a year ago.The third-quarter results show why the outlook matters. Advertising services revenues rose 11% (12% excluding foreign exchange) to $19.6 billion from $17.7 billion a year ago. That was a sharp slowdown from the second quarter, when ad revenues reached $19.8 billion on 26% growth. Amazon attributed the slowdown to timing anomalies, primarily related to Prime Day, plus a couple of other timing factors. Prime Day timing shifted this year, with the event occurring in the second quarter for most large countries. In 2025, Prime Day occurred entirely in the third quarter.Several recent developments support the high-teens target. Amazon Ads Agent now serves as a single AI-first platform for advertisers. Full-Funnel Campaigns launched for all U.S. advertisers, and DVA+ began rolling out in late October. Live sports inventory is also deepening. Thursday Night Football's fifth season averaged 17.7 million viewers over six games, and NBA on Prime launched its second season in more than 200 countries. The fourth quarter also opened with Prime Big Deal Days on Oct. 6-7.Ad revenues fell from $19.8 billion in the second quarter to $19.6 billion in the third, and

Amazon competes for advertising budgets with Meta $META Platforms META and Alphabet $GOOGL GOOGL. Meta Platforms generated third-quarter 2026 advertising revenues of $63,061 million, up from $50,082 million a year ago. Its ad impressions rose 10%, and average price per ad increased 14%. Meta expects fourth-quarter revenues of $73-$76 billion. Alphabet’s Google advertising revenues reached $82,012 million, up from $74,182 million. Within that, Google Search & other grew 12% to $63.35 billion, and YouTube ads rose to $11.21 billion from $10.26 billion. Alphabet has greater scale, and Meta is growing faster, but Amazon’s high-teens fourth-quarter ad outlook keeps it in the competitive mix.

Amazon shares have returned 16% in the year-to-date period, outperforming the Zacks Retail-Wholesale sector’s growth of 3.2%.

From a valuation standpoint, AMZN stock appears overvalued, trading at a forward 12-month price/earnings ratio of 24.32X, higher than the Zacks Internet – Commerce industry’s 19.62X. Amazon has a Value Score of D.

The Zacks Consensus Estimate for AMZN’s 2026 earnings is pegged at $12.90 per share, indicating a 79.92% increase from the figure reported in the year-ago quarter.
