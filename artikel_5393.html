Anthropic's IPO is set to create thousands of millionaires

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Roughly 2,800 present and past employees might see vested stock worth $1 million or more before taxes if the company is valued at $1.35 trillion

Anthropic is set to turn thousands of current and former employees into paper millionaires as its stock begins trading on the Nasdaq $NDAQ on Thursday.

Sacra, a private-markets research firm, calculates that roughly 2,800 present and past employees might see vested stock worth $1 million or more before taxes if the company is valued at $1.35 trillion, according to Reuters. The Sacra figures and those from Hill, a marketplace for private-company stock, come from models built on public information and have not been independently verified.

At $83 a share, the average Anthropic employee could hold more than $40 million in stock, according to CNBC. Combined employee holdings would total about $232 billion, or $44 million per employee, based on share counts in the company's filings.

Early hires would hold far more than later ones. No fewer than 400 of the early staff would hold stakes valued at $100 million or higher at an $83 share price, and several could reach billionaire status, according to CNBC, which cited investors in the company. Hill puts the number of non-founder employees with post-IPO equity of $100 million or more at 598, according to Reuters.

Anthropic disclosed in its IPO filing that option holders held $119 billion in unrealized gains on Sept. 30, based on a weighted-average strike price of $2.91 a share. Roughly $73 billion of that sum had already been earned based on length of service and was available to exercise right away. The company employed about 5,300 people on Sept. 30, up from roughly 940 when 2024 closed.

According to the filing, the average exercise price was 52 cents for options that were granted through 2023 and remained outstanding as 2023 closed. Hill CEO Andrew Benson said early employees in junior roles could come out ahead of senior hires who joined shortly before the IPO, according to Reuters. "Early employees who started in a very junior role should have actually a better financial outcome than somebody who joined in a very senior role right before the IPO," Benson said.

Non-founder employees may sell as much as 10% of what they own on the first day of trading, and as much as an additional 35% once fiscal 2026 earnings are out, according to Reuters.

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share on Wednesday, above the $70 to $78 range it had guided. The offering totals $81 billion and is expected to close on Nov. 20. The stock is expected to trade under the symbol "ANT."

The seven co-founders are not counted in the employee total. Each owns stock worth about $20 billion at $83 a share.
