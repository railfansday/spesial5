IntraFi Cofounder and CEO Mark Jacobsen Named EY Entrepreneur Of The Year in Financial Services

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Recognition honors Jacobsen's invention of reciprocal deposits and nearly 25 years of innovation, helping banks compete and serve their communities

ARLINGTON, Va., Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Mark P. Jacobsen, Cofounder and CEO of IntraFi, was named the EY Entrepreneur Of The YearÂ® 2026 National Award winner in the Financial Services category, recognizing his pioneering contributions to the banking industry and his leadership in building the nation's largest reciprocal deposit network.

The recognition celebrates Jacobsen's contributions to helping community and regional banks attract and retain deposits, allowing them to grant access for their customers to aggregate higher levels of FDIC insurance coverage and enabling them to better serve their communities.

Nearly 25 years ago, Jacobsen recognized a challenge facing community banks and their customers. Businesses and municipalities often needed to maintain deposits exceeding traditional FDIC insurance limits, while smaller banks struggled to compete with larger institutions for those relationships.

Jacobsen "conceived the idea of reciprocal deposits," wrote EY in its recognition. "IntraFi created an entirely new banking product."

Reciprocal deposit services allow community banks to place uninsured deposits through a deposit placement network in increments below FDIC insurance limits and receive a matching amount of insured deposits in return. This allows small businesses, public funds and nonprofits to obtain higher levels of aggregate FDIC insurance. This benefits both the bank and its customers, allowing depositors to have a single relationship with their preferred institution while the bank can use the full amount of the deposit to fuel economic growth.

"Jacobsen brought the idea to life by combining a regulator's understanding of the financial system and an entrepreneur's instinct to challenge conventional thinking, plus the resolve to see the challenge through," said EY, noting Jacobsen's former role as the chief of staff of two federal banking regulators. "He earned a dozen patents and worked with all the federal banking agencies as well as state and local governments, prompting more than 40 state laws to enable the use of reciprocal deposits."

Today, IntraFi operates the nation's largest reciprocal deposit network, connecting more than 3,000 financial institutions, the vast majority of which are community banks. IntraFi's scale, sophisticated infrastructure, and proven reliability distinguish it from other providers, with established systems that have supported banks through multiple financial crises and periods of market stress.

"Any recognition belongs solely to the employees of IntraFi and the thousands of banks that have put their faith in us," said Jacobsen. "We built this network bank by bank with a commitment to do things the right way, not relying on marketing puffery or short-lived promises. And we remain committed to constant innovation. Whether it's helping community banks compete for deposits or exploring how our network could support the next generation of payments, our goal remains the same: giving banks the tools they need to better serve their customers."

IntraFi's unmatched network scale and proven operational reliability reinforces its position as a trusted partner to banks of all sizes.

The EY Entrepreneur Of The Year program recognizes entrepreneurs whose innovation, leadership, and vision have transformed industries and created lasting economic and community impact.

IntraFi has built the largest financial network of its kind, connecting thousands of financial institutions nationwide. The company is the nation's leading provider of reciprocal deposit services and a leading provider of overnight and term funding options. IntraFi helps financial institutions manage liquidity, access funding, and provide customers with solutions for keeping large deposits safe.

IntraFi is not an FDIC-insured bank, and deposit insurance covers the failure of an insured bank. A list identifying IntraFi network banks appears at https://www.intrafi.com/network-banks. Certain conditions must be satisfied for "pass-through" FDIC deposit insurance coverage to apply. Use of an IntraFi service is subject to the terms, conditions, and disclosures in the applicable program agreements.
