Swell Partners Wins Two 2026 Stevie® Awards for Women in Business

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Company of the Year, Business Services and Best Female CEO of the Year awards recognize Swell's people-first culture and leadership

NEW YORK, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Swell Partners, a leading provider of talent solutions and business consulting, is proud to announce that it has won two 2026 StevieÂ® Awards for Women in Business: Company of the Year -Business Services and Best Female CEO of the Year, recognizing founder and CEO Cari Gatto.

The Stevie Awards, which has been hailed as the world's premier business award, honor the achievements of women executives, entrepreneurs, employees, and the companies they lead. These honors celebrate Swell Partners' continued growth and the leadership philosophy behind its success: that exceptional business results and a positive workplace culture go hand in hand.

"These awards are especially meaningful because they recognize both our company and the people-first values that guide us," said Cari Gatto, Founder and CEO of Swell Partners. "At Swell, we believe that when people feel valued, supported, and connected to meaningful work, they find a greater sense of purpose, bring their best selves to work, and make a lasting impact on the teams and businesses they support. I'm incredibly proud of our team and the relationships we've built with our consultants and clients."

Swell's vision is to create a world of happy work people and happy workplaces. Its personalized approach combines talent expertise, technology, and meaningful human connection to help hiring leaders and professionals bring out the best in each other.

The Stevie recognition adds to a milestone year for Swell Partners. In 2026, the company was named to the Inc. 5000 list of America's fastest-growing private companies for the fifth consecutive year, ranking No. 294 among New York companies. Swell was also named as one of Inc. magazine's Best Workplaces in 2026.

Entries from organizations and individuals in 56 nations and territories were submitted for consideration. Stevie Award winners are determined by a formal scoring process from more than 200 judges.

Details about the Stevie Awards for Women in Business and the list of winners are available at Women.StevieAwards.com.
