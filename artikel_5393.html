According to fresh data from RWA Foundation, citing Token Terminal, XRP Ledger (XRPL) remains the number one undisputed leader in the tokenized commodities market. This is based on the distributed asset value in this particular segment of the RWA tokenization market.

Since the beginning of the year the net inflow of money into XRP-focused ledger totalled $2.2 billion. The nearest competitor is Ethereum, which records a positive delta of $1.6 billion year-to-date. Avalanche closes the top 3 with $335.2 million in attracted capital. Other blockchain platforms like BNB Chain, Solana, and Coinbase amassed collectively less than $100 million out of yearly $4.2 billion flow.

The key driver behind the tokenization breakthrough is JMWH fund by Justoken platform worth $2.23 billion. It represents tokenized contracts on electroenergy generation in Latin America, structured within Argentine and UK legal fields. At the moment there are 172 holders of this on-chain fund.

Apart from this, other tokenized commodities on the XRP Ledger look slightly modest. Generally they are presented with a retail collection of tokenized diamonds from Ctrl Alt with the biggest position estimated at $105 million. The Justoken contract is so massive that it is the biggest real-world asset overall in the XRP-based ecosystem, making the Ripple USD (RLUSD) stablecoin and tokenized bonds by Ondo Finance yield to it as well with $896 million and $185 million respectively.

Without Justoken, the XRP Ledger would be just 5th in the segment ranking. Nevertheless, two months are left in 2026, and the blockchain platform continues to headline the tokenized commodities market, making its success one of the most notable industry breakthroughs of the year.
