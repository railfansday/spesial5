Black Friday is almost here, and there's a host of great flight deals currently available, including those from major U.S. carriers.

We’ve rounded up the best airfare deals thus far, so you don’t miss a bargain. We'll update this post with new offers throughout the week, including Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Travel Tuesday, so be sure to bookmark this story.

In case you don’t see what you want right away, here’s some advice on how to find the best travel deals.

If you’re looking for more travel deals, check out our roundup of the best hotel deals currently available.

And make sure you read until the end for a few bonus deals.

Air Canada's Worldwide Seat Sale discounts flights from the U.S. to Canada, Mexico, the Caribbean, Central and South America and Asia-Pacific (excluding India), in cash or Aeroplan points. Book by Nov. 29 for travel between Nov. 30, 2026, and Sept. 30, 2027.

New Chase Air Canada Aeroplan® cardholders can earn 50% bonus points on purchases in categories that normally earn 1x (up to 20,000 bonus points), and Aeroplan members get 10,000 points off hotel redemptions and 15,000 off car rentals when redeeming 50,000 points or more (codes "BFHOTEL26" and "BFAUTO26," through Nov. 30).

The American Airlines flight sale won't be live until early Friday, but the airline did give us an idea of what will be on offer:

You can also expect some special offers for American Airlines AAdvantage members, so be sure to check back on Friday.

Alaska Airlines has announced it will offer some good Black Friday deals, but you'll need to wait until Friday to see the details.

You can also expect some great points deals using Alaska Airlines Atmos Rewards points.

Allegiant is offering a big Black Friday sale. We don't yet have details, but last year, fares dropped as low as $39 one-way.

The promotion applies to both nonstop and connecting flights through Arajet’s hubs in Santo Domingo and Punta Cana.

Avelo Airlines is offering a special early Black Friday deal for members of "Plus" — the carrier's paid loyalty membership program that costs $59 for the first year (and $99 per year after).

You can get 90% off base fares on select flights for travel through April 20, 2027.

No code is needed, but as stated, you'll need to be a Plus member.

The sale ends the night of Nov. 25, 2026.

BermudAir and its affiliate AnguillAir are offering 30% off flights to Bermuda and Anguilla for Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Travel Tuesday when you use promo code "BLACK30" for travel dates between Dec. 1 and April 30, 2027. (Blackout dates apply.)

You can also get 75% off the base fare when you bundle flights and hotels with BermudAir Holidays when visiting Bermuda, Anguilla, Belize, Providenciales, South Caicos and Guatemala.

Breeze Airways has a Black Friday sale that gets travelers as much as $100 off round-trip flights when using the promo code "WISHLIST" from Friday, Nov. 27 through Sunday, Nov. 29. That's up to 60% off its base fares.

British Airways has flight and vacation deals for travel from the U.S. to London and several other top European destinations. Round-trip coach fares start at $623 and span across many dates. Customers can also save on vacation packages.

Delta will be offering a big Cyber Monday sale on Monday, Nov. 30, lasting through Dec. 2. Stay tuned for more details. Meanwhile, Delta already has some holiday offers available now.

Delta SkyMiles members can boost their rewards balances by earning bonus miles — at a rate of up to 5 miles per dollar spent — on Delta gift card purchases of $400 or more.

The United Arab Emirates' national carrier is offering discounts of up to 20% across many of its routes, bookable through Dec. 1.

Fiji Airways is offering Black Friday deals, with flights as low as $899 round-trip on nonstop flights from Dallas, Los Angeles and San Francisco to Nadi, Fiji, and then on to Sydney and Auckland.

JetBlue is offering one of its biggest sales of the year this Black Friday, including flights for as few as 3,200 JetBlue TrueBlue points.

BlueFirst — JetBlue's first-class awards — start at fewer than 20,000 points each way. You can also score as much as $800 off vacation packages.

Qatar Airways is offering up to 25% off fares through Nov 29, 2026, for travel from now through Oct. 31, 2027 (with some blackout dates).

There are a few additional promotions right now, too.

Singapore Airlines will host a fun Black Friday sale, with details to be released soon. The airline usually offers discounts of between 15% and 20% on all cabins from U.S. cities to Asia.

TAP Air Portugal is offering some great deals on fares to Europe from various U.S. airports. Prices start at $226 each way in economy, and there are some deals in business class. The sale ends Dec. 2.

Southwest has a Cyber Week sale, with details to be released after Black Friday. In the meantime, the airline told TPG: "From Nov. 20 through Dec. 3, customers can save on Getaways by Southwest® vacation packages, hotels, rental cars, and cruises, with Cyber Week offers that can help make the next getaway even more rewarding."

United Airlines is expected to offer Cyber Week deals. Stay tuned for more details in the days ahead.

Virgin Atlantic is likely to have some great deals for travel to the U.K. or Europe, but we don't have details yet. We'll update this post when we get those.

Thrifty Traveler is throwing the biggest sale of the year on its Thrifty Traveler Premium flight deal alert service.

This sale won’t last long: It ends at 11:59 p.m. EST on Wednesday, Dec. 2.
