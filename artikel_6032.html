Hegseth expands National Guard operations in DC, authorizes police patrol cars, Humvees and boats

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Former D.C. homicide Det. Ted Williams discusses the Washington, D.C., City Council’s request that governors withdraw National Guard troops on ‘Fox Report.’

War Secretary Pete Hegseth has authorized National Guard troops in Washington, D.C., to use dozens of marked police cars, military vehicles and boats as local officials demand an end to the deployment.

Two Pentagon officials confirmed to Fox News Digital that Hegseth signed an Oct. 29 memo authorizing up to 60 marked patrol cars, Humvees, military tactical vehicles, D.C. National Guard boats and up to 20 light towers and generators.

Roughly 3,000 National Guard troops are deployed in the nation's capital as part of President Donald Trump's crime crackdown.

"The secretary of war has authorized the continued use of National Guard personnel and capabilities in support of the Make D.C. Safe and Beautiful mission," a War Department official told Fox News Digital.

"The October 29, 2026, guidance authorizes the use of up to 60 marked patrol cars, military tactical vehicles, up to 20 light towers and generators and D.C. National Guard maritime boat assets in support of federal and local law enforcement operations."

Politico first reported the authorization, which follows Hegseth's decision last year to allow National Guard troops to carry service-issued weapons when required by their mission.

The move comes as D.C. Councilmember Brooke Pinto, chair of the council's public safety committee, held a hearing Thursday examining the federal law enforcement and National Guard presence.

"I am very hopeful and optimistic that we now have the partners we need at the state and congressional level to push back and demand that this unnecessary, harmful and unlawful military presence be removed from the district," she said during the hearing.

Pinto called on newly elected Democratic governors in Georgia, Iowa, Nevada, Ohio and Texas to withdraw the combined 628 troops she said their states have deployed to Washington.

National Guard vehicles were stationed in Washington, D.C., Tuesday amid the Trump administration's surge to combat crime in the capital. (WTTG)

Pinto also urged congressional Democrats to increase oversight of federal law enforcement operations in January, including requirements for identifying badges and body cameras.
