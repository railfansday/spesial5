Baldur’s Gate 3 Is Giving Players a Good Reason to Return With New Official Mods

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Covering the video games industry since 2017, with experience in news, articles, and reviews. Currently a writer at DualShockers and gaming magazines.

If you've been looking for an excuse to start another playthrough of Baldur's Gate 3, Larian Studios might have just given you one. The acclaimed RPG has received another batch of mods approved for consoles, bringing 14 new additions to PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

And while some are simply great ways to give your characters a fresh look, others introduce new gameplay options that could make your next adventure feel a little different!

The biggest highlight is undoubtedly “The Legend of Drizzt”, a custom class inspired by Drizzt Do'Urden, the legendary drow ranger from the Forgotten Realms universe. If you've spent any time exploring the wider world of Dungeons & Dragons, chances are you've come across the character before. Now, players can bring some of that fantasy into their own Baldur's Gate 3 campaigns, which is pretty neat.

And that's not all! Another mod adds Drizzt's famous swords, Icingdeath and Twinkle, giving players even more ways to recreate the character's fighting style – not a bad combination for anyone planning to return to the Sword Coast.

Beyond the Drizzt-themed additions, this latest collection offers several new ways to experiment with character builds. “Way of the Sword Saint” introduces a Monk subclass focused on mastering weapon techniques, while “Wild Spring Barbarian” adds another subclass for players who prefer a more aggressive approach to combat. Paladins haven't been left out either, thanks to “Zatheris' Paladin Cantrips”, which gives the class access to additional cantrips.

Moving on, the new selection also includes plenty of cosmetic additions. Players can find new armor sets and camp clothing through mods like “NYX” and “ASGORATH”, while “ASE Summer Vibes” adds beach-themed outfits and accessories. There's also “Vigilantia”, a new light armor set, and “Midnight Sparkle”, which introduces another outfit option.

Larian Studios has just approved 20 new mods for BG3's console versions, including the half-elf, half-orc subrace.

All 14 mods are available through the game's built-in Mod Manager, accessible from the main menu. Larian introduced official mod support for consoles with Patch 7 in 2024, allowing PS5 and Xbox players to enjoy community creations without manually installing files. However, it is important to remember that, unlike PC players, console users can only access mods that have passed the studio's approval process.

It's been quite some time since Baldur's Gate 3 received its last major official content update, but the modding community clearly isn't ready to leave the Forgotten Realms behind. And with additions like these arriving regularly, returning for another adventure might be more tempting than ever!
