“This Is An Excellent Entry Point” – UTXO’s Loren Asmus Gives Bitcoin Outlook

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Loren Asmus of UTXO Management says Bitcoin's long-term case is intact after a brutal year for price.

Loren Asmus of UTXO Management says Bitcoin’s long-term case is intact after a brutal year for price. “The thesis hasn’t changed. The protocol hasn’t changed,” he says. “We’re all still here talking about it. It’s not going away.” He says UTXO is focused on building vehicles that make it easy for institutions and allocators to invest in Bitcoin over the next 10 to 20 years.
