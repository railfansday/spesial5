Arsenal: Mikel Arteta reveals David Raya injury hope for crunch Man City clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Mikel Arteta is hoping that David Raya will be fit to play for Arsenal against Manchester City on Sunday.

The Spanish goalkeeper is in a race against time to recover from the shoulder injury he picked up against Newcastle last weekend.

Raya watched from the stands in midweek as Arsenal drew at home to Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League, with deputy and compatriot Kepa Arrizabalaga filling in over Illan Meslier.

Scans showed that Raya's injury was not a serious one, despite initial fears, and there are still hopes that he could hand Arsenal a major boost by being ready to start in the high-profile clash with City.

Asked at Friday’s pre-match press conference about Raya's availability, Arteta was keeping his cards close to his chest.

“I don’t know,” he said. “We still have a few hours before the game. We’re just trying to get everybody back.”

Asked if he hoped that Raya would be ready to face City, Arteta replied: “Yeah.”

Pressed on whether the goalkeeper was training, the Arsenal boss added: “Yeah. They’re all training. Bukayo [Saka] as well is training,” alluding to the individual work the winger is doing away from the main group.

If Raya is not fit, then Kepa will be expected to continue in goal for Arsenal, having deputised without any major issues on Tuesday.

It would be Kepa's first appearance against City since the Carabao Cup final last season, when he started at Wembley and endured a painful afternoon.

The former Chelsea stopper - who remains the most-expensive goalkeeper in football history - was booked after charging out of goal and bringing down Jeremy Doku, before spilling a cross right at Nico O'Reilly's feet to gift City the opener in their 2-0 win.

However, Arteta insisted that match would not be on Kepa’s mind if he was called into action.

“The experience that Kepa has is already incredible,” Arteta said.

“Just look at the teams he’s been involved [in], the squads that he’s been involved [in], the trophies that he’s won. There is none of that.”
