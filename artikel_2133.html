Newcastle United slap massive €80m price tag on defender amid Manchester United interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Shumail Sajid writes about Manchester United for Read Man Utd and Crystal Palace for Read Crystal Palace, with occasional pieces on other Premier League clubs. He has also written for FanSided's Red Devils Dispatch, Yardbarker, Anfield Central and The Sports Side.

Manchester United are currently in the Premier League relegation zone after winning just one of their opening 10 league matches.

Their defensive record has been shambolic, and Pochettino needs to fix it as soon as possible. They have conceded 19 goals in the Premier League this season, the third fewest after Crystal Palace and Ipswich Town.

Their defensive struggles could encourage the Old Trafford board to invest in a new centre-back, with Newcastle’s Malick Thiaw emerging as a potential option.

Amid their defensive struggles, Manchester United are among several clubs keeping tabs on Malick Thiaw as they look to address their problems under new manager, Mauricio Pochettino.

Newcastle United have set a massive € 80 million asking price for Malick Thiaw amid growing interest in the German international.

Several top European clubs have the 25-year-old centre-back on their radar after his impressive performances at St James’ Park. However, Newcastle are determined to not lose him on a cheap.

According to reports by Sky Germany, Matthias Jaissle’s side would want at least €80m for Thiaw, who remains under contract with the Magpies until 2029.

The German defender has also earned recognition from national team manager Jurgen Klopp, while Jaissle considers him an important part of his Newcastle side.

Newcastle signed the former Schalke defender from AC Milan in 2025 for a reported €35million.

Since arriving at St James’ Park, Thiaw has established himself as a key figure in the Magpies’ defence, making 64 appearances and contributing five goals and two assists.

His performances have attracted attention from several clubs, but Newcastle are in a strong position given his long-term contract.

Although a departure next summer has not been entirely ruled out, the Magpies are unlikely to entertain any approach that falls short of their €80million valuation.

Manchester United have a clear gap to fill in their defence line. Harry Maguire and Lisandro Martinez haven’t eased the nerves this season and the Red Devils need a change at the back. Malick Thiaw can be the answer but he isn’t going to be cheap cost United heavy on their pockets.

The big question isn’t the transfer value but whether he is the profile that Mauricio Pochettino needs when he rebuilds Manchester United.
