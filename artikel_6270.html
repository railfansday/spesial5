If you’ve been frustrated by soaring airfare, now may be your best chance to book flights without the sticker shock.

American Airlines just launched one of its biggest AAdvantage award sales of the year. And you'll want to act quickly: It’s only scheduled to be live for 72 hours.

American has put more than 1 million main cabin seats on sale for the winter months.

If holiday fares were out of your budget this year, this could be a chance to redeem miles for a long weekend getaway in the Caribbean in January — or to hit the ski slopes over President’s Day weekend in February.

American’s winter AAdvantage award sale covers flights from Jan. 11 through Feb. 28, 2027.

The deals are slated to run through 12:59 a.m. EST on Nov. 12.

These aren’t the cheapest AAdvantage award redemptions we’ve ever seen (a similar award sale last year offered award prices even lower than this year’s).

And some of these redemptions are in the range of what I typically look for in the quieter winter months.

The good news here is that these cheaper award prices are widely available. And that’s significant in a year where fares (and award prices) have surged.

As an AAdvantage member myself, I'd feel pretty good about booking at any of these price points.

You won’t find these discounted award prices on every flight, date or route. Instead, American simply says it’s discounting more than 1 million main cabin seats across its network.

Fortunately, there aren't blackout dates — which means you don't need to fly at 10 p.m. on a Tuesday to take advantage of this deal. There are options on Fridays, Sundays, peak long weekend travel days and during other popular times.

These deals apply to economy bookings; American is not offering up these discounts on premium economy, first class or business class.

However, these cheaper award redemptions could be a way to get a spot on the plane and then watch for a targeted upgraded offer to a premium seat.

You can find domestic bookings within the 48 contiguous U.S. states for as low as 8,000 miles one-way.

That includes longer transcontinental flights.

For instance, here’s a January flight from Philadelphia International Airport (PHL) to Los Angeles International Airport (LAX).

There are also short-haul international redemptions to warm-weather island destinations at that rate, too — like this February flight from Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA) to Turks and Caicos.

As a Raleigh-based flyer, I could book this Martin Luther King, Jr. weekend flight from North Carolina to Zurich, Switzerland, for 22,500 miles one-way.

There are a ton of AAdvantage redemptions to Europe at that price point, with options for both nonstop and connecting itineraries, depending on your final destination.

I also found pretty open award availability for 25,000 miles one-way.

Here are some examples: LAX to Tokyo’s Haneda Airport (HND), and another to Sydney, Australia.

Passengers in Chicagoland are the biggest winners.

American's award sale includes discounted AAdvantage redemptions starting even lower than the rest of the country, at 7,000 miles one-way.

These AAdvantage deals for the winter months aren’t the cheapest award discounts American has ever offered. But they’re pretty solid — and widely available — for the upcoming winter months.

And for consumers plagued by high prices this year, that could be reason enough to lock in a winter getaway.

Just be sure to book by Wednesday night to take advantage of these deals.
