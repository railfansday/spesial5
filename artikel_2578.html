VJ Edgecombe may have just given 76ers fans a new quote to print on shirts after win

The Philadelphia 76ers, coming off a shockingly bad 2024-25 campaign, swung for the fences during the 2025 NBA Draft and hit a grand slam. Not only did they manage to keep their pick that would have conveyed to the Oklahoma City Thunder had it landed outside the top four, they also selected the best player available in VJ Edgecombe.

Edgecombe showed from the jump that no moment is too big for him. He is built to face the bright lights head-on and not even squint, and he showed that once more on Wednesday in the 76ers' 121-116 win over the Orlando Magic. Once again, they came back from a fourth-quarter deficit, and Philly, with this latest victory, now has six comebacks from down double digits on the season.

And Edgecombe, complete with his steely demeanor, may have just given 76ers fans a new quote to print on shirts with his postgame remark.

"I love the clutch," Edgecombe said with a laugh, via Underdog NBA on X.

Edgecombe has played in the most clutch games thus far this season, at 11. The 76ers have won all of those contests. While it's the team's veterans that give this team an edge in close games due to their combined experience, Edgecombe being able to carry himself in this manner also helps in that regard.

Last season, Edgecombe played in 38 clutch games, as per NBA.com, and had an incredible plus/minus of +2.1 in those contests. The 76ers guard may not be the most experienced in the league, since he's just in his sophomore campaign, but his fearlessness has been contagious for a Philly team that keeps on finding ways to string together wins.

On Wednesday, against the Magic, Edgecombe recorded 24 points, eight rebounds, and six assists in his best all-around effort of the season. He also hit one of the biggest shots of the night for the 76ers. Down by one, 112-111, with 1:36 to go, he drilled a go-ahead triple to give Philly the lead for good.
