Cavs at Bulls: How to watch, odds, and injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Cleveland Cavaliers will look to build some positive momentum as they take on a bad Chicago Bulls team that is coming off a 151-145 overtime loss to the Denver Nuggets on Sunday night.

It's been an uneven start to the season for Cleveland, but they have a chance to build on some of the good things that we've seen over the last week. They're slowly putting things together, and James Harden is starting to look closer to the player we saw last winter, as he dropped 51 points in a recent win over the Indiana Pacers.
