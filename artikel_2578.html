What a roller coaster season this has been for Texas. Despite the hand-wringing over midseason losses to Oklahoma and Florida, the Longhorns have the best resume in the country after their dramatic win over LSU.

The defense is dominating. Arch Manning keeps coming up clutch. In his updated College Football Power Rankings, our own Brandon Marcello bumped the 8-2 Horns to No. 6. It certainly helps that Texas has been red hot against AP Top 25 teams.

Earlier in the season -- when Manning was really struggling -- there was speculation that he could transfer out in the offseason. Now, the Texas quarterback might be eyeing the NFL once again. Either way, the Horns will be one of the teams on what should be a chaotic SEC quarterback carousel in just a few weeks.

Meanwhile, LSU entered last week's showdown against Texas ranked No. 13 in CBS Sports' College Football Power Rankings, but fell out of the top 25 after suffering its fourth loss. Still, the Tigers could impact the SEC Championship race with a win over Tennessee on Saturday.

It's not quite Feast Week just yet, but it sure does feel like. We've got some midseason tournaments underway today — as well as some tremendous non-conference matchups on campus.

That has created a college basketball slate that can get even the most casual fan fired up in mid-November, and our team of experts has picked winners (straight-up and against-the-spread) for every notable matchup.

One of those marquee games features No. 2 Michigan State traveling to Knoxville for a showdown against No. 14 Tennessee. The Spartans have already taken down Duke, but the Volunteers are capable of giving them problems. That's why our own Isaac Trotter is rolling with Tennessee.

After that loss to Michigan State, No. 5 Duke will quickly get a chance to redeem itself against No. 10 Illinois tonight. The winner of that game will surely get a nice bump in our Top 25 and 1 on Wednesday morning.

Current superstars will be all over the floor tonight, but some future stars announced their college plans on Monday. Five-star guard Adan Diggs announced his commitment to Arizona, and Arkansas landed five-star point guard Cayden Daughtry.
