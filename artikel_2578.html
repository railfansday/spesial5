Bengals get on the board in second quarter, lead 3-0

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Bengals offense is off to a slow start, but they have still taken a lead in the second quarter of Monday night’s game in Washington.

Evan McPherson kicked a 38-yard field goal with 6:17 to play in the first half. The kick broke a scoreless tie and put the Bengals up 3-0 over the Commanders.

It was a 15-play drive covering 71 yards for Cincinnati and the workmanlike grind illustrates the lack of explosive plays for one of the league’s top offenses.

The Bengals punted the first two times they had the ball and their third drive ended after Samaje Perine and Joe Burrow were both stuffed for no gain on third and fourth down. The Commanders took over in Bengals territory, but Boye Mafe’s second sack of the first half led to their third punt of the night.
