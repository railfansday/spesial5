Los Angeles Rams vs. Green Bay Packers, Thanksgiving Eve game chat

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Well, the NFL went and found reason to schedule a game on a Wednesday, crowning what's certain to become it's own brand of holiday with what's been dubbed "The Thanksgiving Eve" game. In the early decades of the NFL preceding the 1940s — back when lights on funny looking cars, street lamps, and those indoors were still sometimes powered by oil and kerosene, and a short while later by carbide gas — Wednesday games were not unusual.

Since 1940? Before tonight, six NFL games of notoriety were played on a Wednesday in the last 86 years, and none from 1949 through 2011. Do you know when the most recent game was played before this evening's matchup?

September 22, 1948: Los Angeles Rams vs. Detroit Lions (First Wednesday game after 1940)

September 5, 2012: Dallas Cowboys vs. New York Giants (Moved from Thursday to avoid President Obama's DNC speech)

December 2, 2020: Baltimore Ravens vs. Pittsburgh Steelers (COVID-19-rescheduled game)

December 25, 2024: Kansas City Chiefs vs. Steelers (Christmas Day game)

December 25, 2024: Ravens vs. Houston Texans (Christmas Day game)

September 9, 2026: Seattle Seahawks vs. New England Patriots (2026 NFL season opener ahead of Thursday game in Australia)

Yesterday's NFL is not today's NFL, and with each of today's games put in front of scores of holiday eyes, new interest grows. The move lately has been to put juicy matchups in the best slots of each major holiday.

But tonight? This evening, the Los Angeles Rams, Green Bay Packers, and the NFL get a new "holiday" all to themselves. Okay, maybe they'll have to fight for the attention of people out at informal high school reunions, but you know the game's going to be on even at Applebee's. (The impetus for that behemoth's name is as thrilling as you might guess.)

But here we are as a species, soaking in every single second of NFL football available to us. Truth be told, tonight's game has real potential to be great. Two 6-4 teams are fighting to remain in playoff contention, with Green Bay hanging on to the seventh (and last) seed in the current NFL playoff race. If the regular season ended today, the Rams would be watching the postseason from home as the ninth seed in the NFC.

Rooting interests? It's another NFC-NFC game, so nothing carries too much weight. Additionally, the Bills already played the Rams and will play the Packers Week 14 — that means no real advantage by having one win over other for strength of schedule benefits.

Hoping to watch the game? You'll need a Netflix or NFL+ subscription. Here's your open thread for the first-ever "Thanksgiving Eve" NFL game. As always, be kind to one another — and in all things, Go Bills!
