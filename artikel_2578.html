Injured Warriors guard says ‘s–t been tough’ with offensive struggles

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Brandon Williams is just one of the casualties who has been affected by injuries in the Golden State Warriors' locker room, and it's resulted in the team struggling offensively.

Williams, who is nearing a return, was asked if he sees himself as someone who will bring "some juice" to the team's offense and admitted he hopes so. Williams also revealed that it's been tough to watch lately.

Brandon Williams and Steph Curry on the court laughing during an NBA game against the Mavericks. NBAE via Getty Images

"I ain't gonna lie, that s–t been tough watching that, but I know exactly what I need to do," Williams said. "Just watching from afar, not being in the game, you see a little bit more."

Williams admitted the Warriors' offense has been getting "stagnant at times" and wants to be part of fixing it.

"I know that's what they brought me in here for, to keep it rolling, keep it going, keep everybody organized," Williams said.

Williams has been out with a hamstring injury he suffered on Nov. 9 against the Pelicans.

He said he noticed his hamstring started hurting during the first play of the game but wanted to see if he could continue playing.

Brandon Williams is eager to return after being sidelined due to injury. NBAE via Getty Images

Williams said on the second defensive possession, he wanted to "test it out" by performing a move he usually does when he was guarding Jordan Poole, and that's when he noticed it started to tighten.

"I felt it that second time with Jordan Poole, we called a timeout, and the entire timeout, I was stretching it out a little bit," Williams said. "Then I came back here, and ultimately, it just led to me being out."

Williams is in his first season with the Warriors and has been one of their better shooters not named Steph Curry.

Since his injury, he remains fourth in shooting on the team and is averaging 10.4 points per game, behind Brandin Podziemski, Yaxel Lendeborg and Curry.

The fifth-year veteran signed with the Warriors during the offseason on a one-year, $2.6 million contract to be a role player after averaging 13 points with the Mavericks last season.

Wednesday will mark two weeks since Golden State has been without Curry, who suffered an oblique tear against the Rockets. Last Sunday was the first time they had won a game since.

Golden State Warriors forward Draymond Green celebrating during Sunday's win against the Celtics. Getty Images

Williams admitted it's been challenging and mentally tough to deal with the team's recent performance during its losing skid.

"San Antonio game, Houston game, Pelicans, all of them," Williams said. "Draymond [Green], Steph, Al [Horford], all of them have been keeping us together, in good spirits, so we can't keep our heads down at all."
