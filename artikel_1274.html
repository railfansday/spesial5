Man Utd Injury News: Bruno Fernandes Misses Training for Brentford Clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes is a doubt for the Premier League clash with Brentford this weekend after missing training on Friday, GIVEMESPORT understands.

The Red Devils currently sit 19th in the table after just one win in their opening 11 games, while they were held to a 2-2 draw with Sporting CP in the Champions League in midweek as new manager Mauricio Pochettino chases his first win since taking over.

But the Argentine boss will take charge of his first home game at Old Trafford on Saturday afternoon when the Bees travel up north, but he may now be without his captain for the hugely important clash.

According to a GMS sources, the Portugal international missed Friday's training session at Carrington due to illness as a precaution ahead of the weekend's clash.

The playmaker was present at the training ground but decided to sit out the session after reporting to staff that he felt under the weather. United's medical staff now plan to assess him over the next 24 hours, and will leave a decision on whether to select him for the game until the last possible moment.

Fernandes has had a difficult start to the campaign so far, but has rediscovered his goalscoring touch in recent games with five goals and two assists in his last six games for club and country.

That included two goals in the 2-2 draw with his former club Sporting in midweek, where he was honoured by the Portuguese side pre-game.

It has also emerged that defender Harry Maguire could be sidelined for the game after picking up an injury in Lisbon, despite completing 90 minutes in the Champions League clash. There is hope from within Old Trafford that he will be available, however.

With Fernandes one of the most important players at the club, his absence would be a huge blow for Pochettino as he looks to claim his first win as manager.

But the attacking midfield position is one where the club have got plenty of options to fill in if necessary, and the boss must decide who the ideal replacement would be.

Mason Mount has been used as a substitute in both of Pochettino's games so far, replacing Fernandes in stoppage time in midweek as the number ten, but his record since joining the club has been poor and it's unclear if his output would be enough to plug that gap.

Matheus Cunha is set to be available once again after serving a suspension in midweek, which could see him come into his preferred number ten role, while youngster Shea Lacey is also understood to be pushing for a start.
