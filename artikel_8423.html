If you'd prefer IT you should consider opportunities in federal/state/county/city/school situations.

I've worked in a couple of government positions but mostly in private companies. While it wouldn't require my advanced degrees I always sort of idolized the job my elementary school janitor had. Everybody liked him and no one disrespected him in any way. The job had a routine that many may find relaxing, when you're out the door you just leave the job behind. He had a solitary assignment so there were no co-workers to deal with. When I found out later that he had retired and subsequently passed away years later, I had nothing but admiration for his life well lived.
