COLD, RAIN, MUD, and PUDS. Joyfully attempting my 4th AT thru hike at 55!

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

2019:  Off at Harpers Ferry (foot injury)

2020:  Off at Fontana Dam (You know…..COVID)

2022:  Off at Bland, VA.  (Lost momentum)

Hey guys!   Doc K. here.   I suppose I should give a bit of an introduction, and a little insight into why I’m heading back on trail for a 4th time.  I’m 55, married to an amazing lady, I live in South Carolina, a great job in research, and have a pretty comfortable life in the Lowcountry.  Why, then, am I going for my 4th attempt?  Stubbornness?  Pride?  Sadism?  Nah.

Many years ago, I watched an Appalachian Trail documentary called 5 Million Steps, and I was immediately enthralled.   Watching the rawness of that video, I began to feel a draw to experience the power of this narrow strip of dirt.   Of course , life got in the way during the ensuing years, but the trail never left my mind.  It wasn’t until I divorced in 2017, that I seriously started planning a thru hike.

I literally had no hiking experience, or any faint idea of what was in store for me.   I had never heard of a “trail family”, zero/nero, PUDS, or any of the other trail terms, but I quickly learned, and understood them all.  Let’s face it; the trail is hard!  I started in early March of 2019 with the cheapest gear I could find.  Stepping off with no experience, and a pack weighing 43 pounds, I had a horrible first few weeks.  As I continued, I met some amazing people, but most of all, I regained my faith in the good of humanity.   From trail angels,  shuttle drivers, and random people giving acts of kindness, I realized that I was exactly where I needed to be.

Since that first hike, I’ve learned, researched, and analyzed why I’m doing this hike again……well….it’s simply to finish what I started.

It’s to finally feel that thrill and pride of completing such a mammoth adventure.  It’s also to, once again, experience the amazing culture and aura that surrounds every individual who hikes or helps hikers on this historic trail.   This is my year, and I want to share, experience, and inspire everyone I meet!
