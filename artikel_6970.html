Senior iOS Engineer, Core Discovery at Raya

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

We are seeking a Senior iOS Engineer to join our Core Discovery client team. Core Discovery owns how members discover and connect with people and places, including our Maps, Location, and Directory experiences. The team builds some of the most interactive and visually rich surfaces in the app, helping members explore their communities and make meaningful connections.

This is a product-focused, generalist iOS engineering role with opportunities to work across a broad range of features. The ideal candidate has experience building polished, high-quality consumer applications, strong technical judgment, and an eye for the details that make an experience feel intuitive and delightful. You’ll work closely with product, design, and server engineering to bring new experiences to life, solve complex technical challenges, and continuously improve the quality and performance of the app.
