ATLAUA Expands Beyond E-Commerce by Climbing Into Retail in Colorado, and Takes Cyber Monday to New Heights

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The functional luxury recovery beverage brand launches its full portfolio in Colorado for its first retail market entry and marks the milestone with a special Cyber Monday celebration, rewarding those who arrive first with up to 70% off.

DENVER, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- At the top of a mountain, freedom feels different. With the climb behind you, the horizon opening in every direction and the noise falling away, there is a sense of freedom that can only be earned through effort.

Colorado captures this feeling. Defined by movement, endurance and the freedom found beyond the climb, the state embodies much of what ATLAUA stands for. For a brand built around ancient water mythology and the ritual of recovery, it's a natural place to be present.

ATLAUA enters retail with its full portfolio in Colorado and celebrates with special Cyber Monday offers.

ATLAUA today announces the December launch of its full portfolio in Colorado, marking the brand's first state of retail distribution. The entry is deliberate, beginning with its first retail doors and building a presence within a distribution opportunity of over 10,000 locations. The first doors will be revealed soon.

"Colorado represents something we believe in deeply. Freedom is earned, and it comes after the effort, not before it. And it just feels right to step into our next chapter in the Headwaters State," said Adrian Goransch, Founder of ATLAUA.

The launch arrives as Colorado enters one of its most anticipated seasons, welcoming people from around the world to ski, hike, gather and get away. Whether visiting for a week or calling Colorado home, ATLAUA introduces a ritual that flows with them and creates an intentional moment to unwind.

To celebrate its arrival, ATLAUA is taking Cyber Monday to new heights.

Black Friday week is the effort. Cyber Monday is the recovery.

To mark its momentum, ATLAUA is opening a special Cyber Monday celebration, with rewards reaching up to 70% off for those who choose to be part from early on.

After months of showing up, pushing forward and pursuing more, the holiday season offers a moment to appreciate the effort behind it all, making room for the rituals that carry us into tomorrow. That could mean stocking up on ATLAUA recovery blends for yourself or gifting them to someone whose dedication deserves recognition.

Some rewards are worth keeping. Others are worth giving.

Inspired by Colorado's elevations, the celebration offers four levels of access, where the higher the elevation, the greater the reward.

Registration is open now and closes November 29, exclusively at drinkatlaua.com. Sign up order determines the reward, with the highest elevations belonging to those who arrive first. Once a place is claimed, it is reserved. On Cyber Monday, November 30, registrants will receive a personal access link, opening their place:

As an additional gesture of appreciation, one lucky person from the registered pool will receive six months of Club of A's Bronze membership, including a monthly supply of 12 shots and 10% off every additional order, representing a total gifted value of $354. Offering a ritual to return to, month after month because consistency matters more than intensity.

The Colorado launch marks a new chapter for ATLAUA, and the Cyber Monday celebration rewards those following the journey closely.

The climb starts now. The ambition reaches far beyond.

About ATLAUAATLAUA is a luxury recovery brand for those who refuse to leave tomorrow's performance to chance, built on one principle: Recover to Perform. Founded by former professional soccer player Adrian Goransch, the Los Angeles based brand creates functional blends for after the effort, before tomorrow. Unwind, Recover, Last. For more information, please visit drinkatlaua.com or instagram.com/atlaua.official.
