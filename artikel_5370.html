Amazon picks its Bond: Robert Pattinson to play 007, ending years of speculation over coveted role

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

by Kurt Schlosser on Nov 16, 2026 at 8:31 amNovember 16, 2026 at 8:32 am

Last year, Jeff Bezos asked social media, “Who’d you pick as the next Bond?” Amazon now has its own answer: Robert Pattinson.

The 40-year-old Pattinson — best known for “Twilight,” “The Batman” and this summer’s “The Odyssey” — becomes the seventh actor to play 007 in the official film series. He takes over from Daniel Craig, whose fifth and last Bond film, “No Time to Die,” came out in 2021.

Amazon got a 50% stake in the Bond films when it bought MGM for $8.5 billion in 2022, and it took creative control last year in a joint venture with longtime producers Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson. Fans and bookmakers had been guessing about Craig’s successor since he said in 2019 he was leaving the role.

Over the years, the rumored contenders included Idris Elba, Tom Hardy, Aaron Taylor-Johnson, Tom Holland and Henry Cavill. By the end, there were five finalists. Pattinson beat out Nicholas Hoult, Jack Lowden, Josh O’Connor and Callum Turner after screen tests in London in late October, according to The New York Times. Casting director Nina Gold ran the tests, which covered stunts, dialogue and camera presence.

Bond is one of the most valuable properties Amazon got in the MGM deal, its second-largest acquisition ever behind Whole Foods. The franchise has earned nearly $7 billion at the domestic box office, adjusted for inflation, since “Dr. No” in 1962. Now Amazon is running it without the Broccoli family, whose stewardship over six decades built it into one of Hollywood’s biggest moneymakers.

Pattinson’s casting is the most visible move yet in Amazon’s plans for Bond. As the Times noted, the 2025 deal followed years of behind-the-scenes friction over Amazon’s ambitions to expand the Bond universe beyond the films. The Times also pointed out that Amazon has to win over a generation of moviegoers who grew up during the franchise’s long hiatus and gravitated toward superheroes, horror movies and video game adaptations.

Amazon is already putting the back catalog to work. Alongside Monday’s announcement, it published a guide to streaming the 007 films on Prime Video.

To lead the next film, Amazon brought in producers Amy Pascal, the former Sony Pictures chairwoman, and David Heyman, who produced all eight “Harry Potter” movies. Denis Villeneuve (“Dune”) is directing from an original screenplay by “Peaky Blinders” creator Steven Knight. No release date has been set, and the Times reports that a theatrical release before 2028 is unlikely.

“Today marks a defining moment for our studio and a significant milestone in the history of James Bond,” Mike Hopkins, head of Prime Video and Amazon MGM Studios, said in a statement. Hopkins said Pattinson’s “remarkable range, magnetic screen presence, and quiet intensity make him an exceptional choice” to carry on the 007 legacy.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime,” Pattinson said. “To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement.”

Some longtime fans have worried that under Amazon, Bond will lose his distinctly British character and become more corporate and American, the Times reported. Pascal and Heyman, in a joint statement, called the English actor “the perfect Bond for our times.”

Bezos had yet to weigh back in on social media about Amazon’s pick, but Amazon CEO Andy Jassy did in a LinkedIn post, writing that he was “excited” to have Pattinson in the role, calling him “one of the most versatile actors of his generation, who consistently makes fearless choices, and keeps surprising people with his performances.”

Fans online were split. Some posted images of Elba or Cavill, calling Pattinson as 007 a “huge miss.” Others said the actor was on a “generational run” right now with the roles he’s landing. Others shared GIFs of Sean Connery, saying there will only ever be one Bond.

I checked in with my mom, a longtime Bond lover and the biggest movie fan I know. She quickly responded via text.

“No way … not sexy enough for me,” she wrote of Pattinson. “I really liked Daniel Craig, but Sean Connery has always been a favorite.”
