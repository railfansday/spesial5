Six Flags is permanently closing California's Great America in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The Santa Clara park's Farewell Season runs from March to Labor Day, Sept. 6, 2027, ahead of a land lease expiring in June 2028

Six Flags Entertainment announced plans to close California’s Great America in Santa Clara, California, after a final season in 2027. The company made the announcement on Nov. 6.

The park's Farewell Season kicks off in March and wraps up on Labor Day, Sept. 6, 2027, the company said. The park's land lease expires in June 2028, and Six Flags opted not to extend it.

"For more than five decades, this Santa Clara theme park has brought people together, created family traditions and served as the backdrop for unforgettable memories," Dave Vosejpka, park manager of California's Great America, said in a statement.

Six Flags said guests can get a 2027 Gold or Prestige Pass that covers as many trips through the gates as they like in the park's closing year. Passholders also get access to Six Flags Discovery Kingdom in Vallejo, California.

Cedar Fair, the park's former owner, sold the land under the park to the San Francisco-based logistics company Prologis for $310 million in 2022 and leased it back to run the park, according to the San Francisco Chronicle. Six Flags merged with Cedar Fair in 2024, according to the San Francisco Chronicle.

The park's rides and attractions are scheduled to stay open through its last year, though upkeep needs, bad weather or other day-to-day conditions could interrupt service, according to the San Francisco Chronicle. Its South Bay Shores water park is expected to run on its regular seasonal calendar, while outings for groups, events for companies and programs for schools and young people will carry on, the San Francisco Chronicle said.

Six Flags plans to share further specifics about when the park will be open in 2027, according to the San Francisco Chronicle. The company said it will mark the final season through experiences in the park and stories shared on its digital and social channels.

The park opened in 1976 as part of the country's Bicentennial festivities, and Six Flags said tens of millions of people have visited since.
