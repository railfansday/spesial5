Ligue 1: PSG provides the essentials against Troyes and consolidates its leadership position

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Four days after their performance at Villarreal in the Champions League (0-6), Paris Saint-Germain (1st) returned to the Parc des Princes for the reception of Troyes (16th) in Ligue 1. Deprived of Achraf Hakimi (rest) and Maghnes Akliouche (injured), Luis Enrique launched Warren Zaire-Emery at right-back, Senny Mayulu in the middle with Fabián Ruiz and Vitinha, and Mika Godts alongside Désiré Doué and Ferran Torres. Opposite, ESTAC was coming off an important success for confidence against Le Mans (2-0), but had to hope for a miracle to leave Paris with the three points.

The Parisians quickly took control of the meeting. After a first alert with this shot from Senny Mayulu diverted onto the bar by the Trojan goalkeeper (5th), PSG opened the scoring. After having already called on Hillel Konaté two minutes earlier, Illya Zabarnyi perfectly headed a cross from Mayulu to open the scoring (39th, 1-0). Just before half-time, Ferran Torres had the opportunity to make the break, but the 2026 world champion lost his one-on-one against the Burkinabè goalkeeper (45th+1).

Returning from the locker room, Renaud Ripart faced Matvey Safonov, but his attempt did not find the target (56th). PSG then had several opportunities to make the break, notably by Zaire-Emery, whose header from a cross from Doué narrowly missed (61st), then Ferran Torres, again defeated by an excellent Hillel Konaté (69th). The Parisians finally trembled until the end, when Maronnier thought he equalized after a corner, before finally being flagged offside (84th).

Despite overall domination and several clear chances, Paris Saint-Germain was therefore content with the bare minimum against Troyes. After its festival in C1, the Capital club won its 11th victory in a row in all competitions and its seventh success in the league to increase its lead at the top of Ligue 1 before the international break. For its part, Troyes remains a barrier and could enter the relegation zone at the end of this 10th day.
