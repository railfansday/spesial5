Despite the good momentum and the eight consecutive victories in all competitions of the Madrilenians, A.S. emphasizes one point, namely the number of mistakes suffered by José Mourinho’s men in the league since the start of the season. With 171 fouls suffered, Real is quite simply the team which has suffered the most fouls. And according to the media, the thermometer exploded at Mestalla, where the Madrilenians suffered 26 fouls, or one every 3:30 minutes, the highest total since the start of the season. This feeling of poor referee treatment is not new within the club, but the situation is now starting to directly affect the players and there is significant anger in the locker room. And the media does not stop there and makes the comparison with the other big names in the championship. Atlético comes far behind in the ranking with 113 fouls suffered and Barça even further, in penultimate position with 108 fouls suffered. And A.S. also highlights the players most regularly affected and here too Real is present en masse with 4 of its players, namely Bellingham Mbappé, Vinicius and Güler, who appear in the top 10. Behind the first Blaugrana, namely Lamine Yamal, only arrives in 18th position, specifies the Spanish daily.
