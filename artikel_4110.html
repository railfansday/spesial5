GTA 6: How To Know Which Buildings Can Be Entered

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Before launch, Rockstar teased that GTA 6 would have "hundreds" of interior spaces that could be explored.

If you've been cruising through Leonida, you might've already tried to enter a few, awkwardly ramming into locked doors over and over and wrecking havoc on Lucia's shoulder until you give up. Thankfully, there's an easy way to tell which buildings can be entered.

It's not immediately obvious, but as highlighted by Reddit user u/Saladinismo, every building with an interior is marked on the map in a darker shade of blue, making them distinct from the closed-off buildings. Just don't expect every single door to be unlocked when you pull up.

"I've also noticed that just because an entire building is dark blue, doesn't mean every entrance will work," explained u/TypeS2K_ in the comments. "For example, I saw a dark blue house but all the main doors wouldn't do anything. It was an open entrance to a basement around back. Just fyi, for anyone wondering. Sometimes you'll need to search around a bit."

All we had to do was stop the damn train, CJ!

Dark blue could mean anything from the building itself to the garage, rooftop access, or even just the toilet, but it's still a good way to know where the interior spaces are. And just looking from the map, Rockstar wasn't kidding. There are hundreds of spots to explore, meaning plenty of places to rob. Don't worry about doing your shoulder in trying every possible entrance, though. As noted by u/xx_HotShot_xx, "Activating 'focus' will highlight doors that can be opened."

It's worth keeping an eye on the dark blue buildings in Vice City, especially around the richer areas, as you can actually break into the expensive homes and steal special vehicles from the garages. So, it's not just a gimmick to make Leonida feel bigger — it all ties back to the robberies and heists that line Jason and Lucia's pockets.

However, if you find a blue building without an obvious entrance, it might be for a story mission later on, so don't stress too much if you can't always get in. Usually, after the mission, you can enter them later in free roam.
