Guide: Pokémon Pokopia Apples to Applin Event Guide, From Syrupy Apples To A Kitchen Table

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Cor! Five new Grass- and Dragon-types for a limited-time only

The Apples to Applin event runs from 16th November to 1st December, 2026, adding five Gen 8 Pokémon to Pokémon Pokopia.

If you've played previous limited-time events, you'll know what to expect. A new Pokémon, Applin, will drop outside the Pokémon Center, asking you to find a currency (Syrupy Apples) in exchange for new items, helping you construct several brand new habitats.

This Pokémon Pokopia guide explains how to start the Apples to Applin event, how to get Syrupy Apples, and everything you need to find Applin evolutions Flapple, Appleun, Dipplin and Hydrapple.

On this page: Pokémon Pokopia Apples to Applin Event Guide, From Syrupy Apples To A Kitchen Table

Before you start, you need to have rebuilt a Pokémon Center. Once done, make sure you have the latest update, and are playing between the dates of 16th November to 1st December, 2026.

As with recent live-events in the second half of the year, you couldn't time travel to this event ahead of time. But once the start date has passed and once you've connected the game to the internet to authenticate the date, you are able to time travel back if you missed it.

Either way, when you log in during the above dates, Applin will appear outside of the Withering Wastes Pokémon Center and ask for you Syrupy Apples. Once you have enough, you can then trade them for event-specific items inside a Pokémon Center, and build three unique habitats.

To get Syrupy Apples, you must visit Dream Islands with the help of Drifloon by interacting with a doll.

Once in a Dream Island, look for apples growing on trees; give them a headbutt and collect your spoils.

You'll find around 40-50 per visit, meaning you'll need to visit on another day to get 80, which is enough to buy one of every event item (or if you're simply unlocking the event habitats, then you just need 70).

Alternatively, can grow your own Syrupy Apple Tree by buying Syrupy Apple Seeds during the event - one of which is a requirement for one of the habitats - if you so choose, allowing you to source Syrupy Apples in a location of your choosing.

This means you need to acquire a total of 70 Syrupy Apples to get items for three Habitats.

Unlike past events, this isn't a simple case of buying and combining the items, as the Abundant Apples and Apple Kitchen requires some additional prep and / or other items first.

For Abundant Apples's Syrupy Apple Tree, plant a Syrupy Apple Seed tree in soil prepared with the Rototiller, then water it each day until it grows and sprouts fruit, turning it into a Syrupy Apple Tree.

This will take around three to four days in total, though you can always change the time to do this quicker, and is definitely not what we did to speed up the process.

As for Apple Kitchen's Kitchen Table, if you don't have one already, you can find one of these in the building on the edge of the water west of the Pokémon Center in Bleak Beach (see the mini-map in the below screenshot for the exact location). From there, you can use a 3D Printer to copy a couple more.

Best of luck getting all three habitats before the event wraps!
