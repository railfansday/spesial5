Mysterious Bandai Namco Trademark Has People Talking About a New Fighting Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Hmmm, something tells me this is only going to lead to disappointment...

Earlier this week, Bandai Namco registered a video game trademark for 'FATAL ICHOR'. And as is so often the case with trademarks, a complete lack of context means that it could relate to anything.

But the current speculation is that this ties into a new fighting game that's yet to be announced. That's because Bandai Namco's been hiring for such a project since earlier this year — and the job listings have all referenced some kind of "fighting" or "action fighting" title.

And of course, people are getting ahead of themselves already. There are a few suggestions that this could have something to do with a new SoulCalibur, for example, but I think that seems well wide of the mark.

Even if SoulCalibur was destined to make a return, 'Fatal Ichor' just doesn't sound like it belongs — surely SoulCalibur 7 is the logical choice.

And it's obviously far too early to start talking about Tekken 8's successor, which will inevitably be named Tekken 9 anyway (unless we somehow get Tekken Tag Tournament 3 first).

At least to me, Fatal Ichor has an anime ring to it; like an expansion for Code Vein 2, or something to do with Sword Art Online — or even the upcoming Gundam Rogue Orbit.

Indeed, this trademark doesn't necessarily have to correlate with the aforementioned, unannounced fighting game project — it's just that the timing adds up.

So, in conclusion, I wouldn't get too excited over this one. At least, not yet. We'll have to wait and see whether 'Fatal Ichor' shows up over the coming months; if it doesn't, then maybe there really is a connection here.
