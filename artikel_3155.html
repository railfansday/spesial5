X now lets you share 5x more photos and videos per post

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Since the Twitter days, X has limited media-per-post to four items. As of today, you can share five times more photos and videos per post.

X now lets you post up to 20 photos and videos per post, dramatically increasing the number of attachments.

The change is already live on the web. If you’re not seeing support for the vastly expanded media sharing feature on mobile, update to the latest version of X for iOS and Android.

Today’s update comes after the service added over a dozen new app icons to the mobile app last week.

X has also pushed a new financial service this year with X Money. Meanwhile, XChat, which launched this year as a standalone app, was discontinued in favor of building out chat inside the X app.
