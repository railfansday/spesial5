‘Sunrise On The Reaping’ $16M Tuesday Scores Records For Lionsgate & Franchise; ‘Hexed’ Begins Previews – Box Office

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

As we told you last night, Lionsgate scored its best Tuesday ever at the domestic box office with The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping and the best ever for the Suzanne Collins franchise as the day raked in $15.98M.

2013’s The Hunger Games: Catching Fire owned the previous best Tuesday for the franchise with $15.96M. Running cume for the fifth Francis Lawrence movie is $148.1M and by Thanksgiving, Sunrise on the Reaping will surpass its previous 2023 chapter’s lifetime domestic B.O. of $166.3M.

As we told you, Sunrise on the Reaping will dominate the Thanksgiving Wednesday through Sunday stretch as the franchise has historically done before since 2013, this time with a floor of $70M+. Previously, the pics over Thanksgiving cooked up the following: Catching Fire ($109.9M), Mockingjay Part 1 ($82.6M), Mockingjay Part 2 ($75.9M) with Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes way behind ($42.2M).

Lionsgate has had back-to-back global hit openings this year with Sunrise on the Reaping last weekend at $226.1M WW and Michael with $218.8M. This has all happened under the administration and watch of Motion Picture Chairman, Adam Fogelson. Still no word out there whether or not Lionsgate rejected or accepted Legendary’s bid. Walt Disney Animation’s Hexed came in with $2.1M, $100K above the $2M we were spotting yesterday. That preview figure is higher than Encanto ($1.5M previews, 5-day of $40.5M) and Wish ($1.8M previews including $500K Saturday sneaks; 5-day of $31.6M). No RT audience score yet, but critics were meh at 67% fresh. There is a chance that the traditional Disney pic comes in lower than its 5-day forecast of $40M-$45M as original animated titles are very challenging to launch in the streaming era, but you have to start some place.

Universal and Paramount’s Focker-in-Law, which didn’t have previews last night, rather began showtimes at midnight, has more potential to hit its 5-day forecast of $45M given the Ariana Grande factor and the fact that audiences love the franchise. The first Meet the Parents 26 years ago was the fave among critics at 85% fresh on Rotten Tomatoes. Focker-in-Law isn’t the worst at 26%, as that belongs to 2010’s Little Fockers. 2004’s Meet the Fockers stands at 39%.

Hexed, Focker-in-Law and Sunrise on the Reaping are sharing PLFs with the latter having Imax screens (except for AMC where they are being designated to Netflix’s The Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth).

Thank God for Lionsgate Hunger Games at the box office: Sans a Wicked, Moana and a Zootopia, the Thanksgiving box office will certainly be down from last year’s five day robust $290.3M, which was the fifth best ever. Moana 2 and Wicked led the holiday to its best haul ever with $424.9M.
