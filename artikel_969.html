Drone footage of Pochettino's first Man Utd training session leaked online

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A 12-minute video of Mauricio Pochettino's first training session as Manchester United boss has been leaked online, showing the new head coach doing training drills ahead of Sunday's match against Liverpool at Anfield, which is live on Sky

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

Mauricio Pochettino’s first Manchester United training session today has been leaked online in a major security breach.

The 12-minute drone footage over their Carrington training ground was uploaded onto YouTube.

It shows Pochettino doing various tactical drills with his new team as they prepare for their clash with Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.

United have signs around the Carrington area warning that use of drones are "strictly prohibited" and anyone caught "will be reported for prosecution and subject to civil action".

The uploaders of the drone footage, CC Overview, claimed to have not breached any laws.

On the video they posted: "For the people asking about legality. It is unrestricted airspace, meaning anyone can fly over it, the drone remained in line of sight at all times, it didn't fly over anyone or even near anyone so was a perfectly legal flight going by the CAA rules that regulates drone usage.

"The drone is correctly labelled and I hold all the relevant licences, public liability and insurance required.

"As for leaking team news/ tactics, it was half an hour into the manager's first training session, there are many media there so certainly won't be the only footage of training released."

The training today was a closed session and not open to the media.

Manchester United declined to comment when contacted by Sky Sports News.

"This 12-minute video starts off innocuously. We see large numbers of Man Utd players on a separate pitch doing what is known as rondos, those small groups of six or seven players with one or two in the middle trying to intercept the ball. It's the classic pre-training warm-up. That's pretty innocuous. That won't give Liverpool or anyone else much of an insight into what Pochettino's tactical thinking might be for his first game in charge.

"But then on the next pitch, as the drone moves across, it zooms in, and the zoom is quite incredible. You can make out faces and we see Pochettino hands-on, walking them through certain match scenarios. At one stage Pochettino calls all those players in for a huddle and a briefing.

"I'm not going to give you any information about who those players are. But these 11 players Pochettino calls in - 10 outfield players and one goalkeeper. We shouldn't assume this is his starting XI, necessarily.

"Saturday would be the day we'd expect United to do their specific tactics and shape to prepare for the game at Anfield the next day. If this was drone footage of that session it would be even more damaging, even more explosive.

"But the fact it's 11 players separated from the group... it's a very difficult situation for Pochettino and Manchester United.

"I don't think [Pochettino and the players] were aware of it at the time.

"It's questionable whether the person handling the drone has done anything illegal. All around Carrington training ground, they have a large numbers of signs up saying drones are prohibited. But it's clear from these images the drone was not flying directly above United's property. It was to the side of United's property and zoomed in.

"There's absolutely no suggestion Liverpool are involved in this, they are clearly not. But you have to say, it could be advantageous to Liverpool and Andoni Iraola if he's planning his tactics for Sunday's game to look at this and get an idea of how Manchester United might be lining up and what Pochettino might be thinking.

"While it seems as though there are probably no laws that have been broken here, nevertheless it's an awful lot of very specific footage which Manchester United and Pochettino would not want out in the public.

"If you look at the impact of what we're talking about, Man Utd earlier today released some images of the training session and it showed Harry Maguire and Patrick Dorgu among the main training group. Both of those have had injury concerns of late. So those official images told me, Man Utd supporters and Liverpool's tactical preparation experts that Maguire and Dorgu may be in line for possible selection.

"It's gone a stage further now with this leaked drone footage because that has shed even more light on that. It gives a pretty clear picture of who's fit, who's out on the training field, who's involved and who isn't.

"That has to be a significant tactical advantage to Andoni Iraola and Liverpool as they go into this huge game on Sunday."
