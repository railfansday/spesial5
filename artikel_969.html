Naderi strike snatches late win for Rangers at Motherwell

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Motherwell vs Rangers. Scottish Premiership.

Report as Rangers score a late winner against Motherwell; Emmanuel Fernandez opened the scoring; Jake Girdwood-Reich pulled the hosts level; Ryan Naderi's late deflected strike secured the win for Rangers to cut Celtic's Premiership lead back to four points

Football Journalist @AlisonGConroy

Ryan Naderi was the hero for Rangers as he scrambled a late winner against Motherwell to keep up the pressure in the Scottish Premiership title race.

After Celtic's impressive 4-1 win against St Mirren, Rangers knew a win was vital to remain within touching distance of their Old Firm rivals at the top of the table.

Emmanuel Fernandez gave them the perfect start, nodding in from a corner to put them in front at Fir Park.

But Jake Girdwood-Reich struck his first Motherwell goal against the run of play before the break.

Rangers had 29 shots at goal in total but found Motherwell goalkeeper Alex Paulsen in top form and the New Zealand international pulled off one of the saves of the season to deny Dujon Sterling with five minutes left.

But they were undone late on when Naderi's shot from eight yards went in off Martin Moorman after the Austrian had blocked a shot from Badredine Bouanani.

It handed Rangers their first win at Fir Park for three years to remain four points behind Celtic ahead of their game in hand against Aberdeen on Tuesday, live on Sky Sports.

Motherwell - who had been unbeaten in three - remain 10th.

Former Rangers and Motherwell midfielder Andy Halliday on Sky Sports:

"That's such a massive win for Rangers. You look at the next six games, five of them are at Ibrox so it's so important for them to keep the pressure on Celtic at the top end of the table.

"From a Motherwell point of view, I feel for Martin Moormann because I thought him and Paulsen were the reason it was 1-1 for so long.

"Take nothing away from Rangers, they were terrific and the introduction of Findlay Curtis changed the game.

"He was so direct, so positive, put so many crosses into the box. He's bided his time all season and he was excellent.

"Rangers kept asking questions of Motherwell. Ryan Naderi was pivotal again.

"He always puts himself about and his work rate is incredible.

"He might not be cleanest and he might look untidy at times, but nobody can fault his work rate.

"Derek McInnes said the most important thing was to leave with three points and that's exactly what Rangers have done."

Rangers manager Derek McInnes speaking to Sky Sports:

"A first-class performance. We were really strong throughout. Very rarely can you go away from home in the Scottish Premiership and have so much dominance, but with that dominance there has to be more authority in the game, more control of the game in terms of scoring goals and taking the game away from the opponent.

"We were a bit unfortunate, we conceded the corner with a wee deflection.

"They get the break in, they get the corner, and then we were just so sloppy. One of our players isn't doing his job, picking up when he should have been. It could have cost us, but we made the game.

"Again, it's disappointing that we had to wait to the very end, but when it comes it's a great moment for us. A last-minute winner is always so brilliant to get and to do it in front of our supporters made it special because they were brilliant back and forth the whole night.

"We've all been around football enough to know that those last-minute ones are kind of systematic with a team who are successful, so hopefully that is the case.

"The difference between that one point and three points is obviously clearly important to us."
