GTA 6 Caused a Mass 'Migration' of Over 2 Million People

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Grand Theft Auto 6 was released on Thursday, Nov. 19... officially. Millions of players around the world actually got to play it on Nov. 18 by moving to New Zealand. Well, not actually. Players just changed their account's region to New Zealand. It's a known trick that lets gamers access digital games before release when companies opt for a staggered launch, as with GTA 6. Reports indicate that, in the UK and the US, 2 million Xbox players used the New Zealand trick to gain access to GTA 6 early. And that doesn't even account for the rest of the world or PlayStation players.

The report doesn't mention how many PlayStation 5 players resorted to this method because the process involves jumping through several hoops, so the number is likely much lower. You need a whole new PSN account, then enable game sharing, and purchase GTA 6 through the NZ account using New Zealand dollars. On Xbox, you just have to change your console's region. For many, it's just not worth the time and effort, and they'd rather wait a few hours.

Additionally, given the hype around the release, it wouldn't be surprising for fans in every country to have followed this method — even players in neighboring Australia, who had to wait an extra full hour. All in all, with about 20 million copies pre-ordered, the migration must've included much more than 2 million people.

The biggest time difference was for players in the US, who were able to play GTA 6 21 hours before its scheduled launch in their region. It almost sounds too good to be true, so you might be wondering if this is a bannable offense. The truth is, it's all a bit iffy. Terms of service are a serious matter in the gaming world. Technically, Xbox and Sony don't want players to circumvent access restrictions to software or provide false information when creating an account. However, neither company seems to want to go as far as punishing players for playing a game a few hours early — especially when they've been waiting as long as they have for Grand Theft Auto 6. At least for now.
