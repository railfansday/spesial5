FBI's Scola Promises Nina Their Family Can 'Handle Anything' Amid Move

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

FBI’s Scola and Nina have always been a team, but can they survive a cross-country romance?

In Us’ exclusive sneak peek of the Monday, November 16, episode of the hit CBS series, Special Agent Stuart Scola (John Boyd) tries to convince his wife, Special Agent Nina Chase (Shantel VanSanten), that she’s making the right decision in moving from New York City to California for the job of a lifetime — even though he’s not sure himself.

“San Diego will be your chance to run the show, make your own right calls,” Scola tells Nina amid frustrations over their hunt for a man on the most wanted list.

“Yeah, or the wrong ones,” Nina mumbles under her breath.

Scola asks, “What does that mean?” which sends Nina into a spiral.

“I don’t know. I don’t know, I don’t know,” she admits. “I don’t know if it’s the right move, us being apart.”

Scola, who works on a different team at 26 Fed, explains, “It’s what we do every day. We step into the unknown.”

He confesses, “I don’t want you going on this op without me, but that’s the deal we made for [our son] Dougie. Partners everywhere, except in the field. Now we’re just going to be doing it across the country.”

Nina takes a deep breath, responding, “Yeah, and that worries me too. I mean, it’s a lot of distance.”

Scola, who originally told his wife to pass on the promotion, then gets serious with her as they wait for the elevator to open.

“What, are you gonna not take the job? You will regret that forever,” he says.

Nina takes a beat before asking about how their family will navigate living in different states.

“[Our] family can handle it. We can handle anything,” Scola reassures her.

As Nina gets into the elevator, she teases her husband, saying, “You’re going to be a mess, you know.”

“Me? I feel bad for you,” Scola jokes. “All the phone calls and texts. All your new field agents are going to be wondering, ‘What’s up with your guy?’”

Nina laughs off his response, saying, “OK,” as the doors start to shut.

Nina cheekily replies, “Try and stop me.”

During Monday’s episode, fans will learn how Nina’s promotion will affect Scola, who has been part of the FBI team since day one. As his wife plans to leave the city and move to San Diego with their son, he must decide whether their long-distance plan is really what he wants.

“When we meet the couple at the beginning of the episode, Scola’s just made this decision that he’s not willing to deprive the person he loves of the connection that she has to the work that both of them do,” Boyd, 45, exclusively told Us ahead of the episode. “I think, at the top of the episode, we meet a couple who’s a little bit in dreamland about how this is actually going to work, that it’s going to function.”

He teased, “The episode ends with them coming to terms with the truth of what’s best for their family and how to make it work.”

VanSanten, 41, echoed her costar’s sentiments, telling Us, “They definitely have a roadmap for the road trip ahead, and they have all the stops planned. And it seems like, ‘OK, we see what it is,’ and you know, they’re not really in it yet. And it seems like this is a good solution.”

The actress noted that Nina gets to “live out [her] dream and be a boss and provide a little bit more stability to Dougie, because [she’ll] be a boss, and that’s incredible.”

However, the job does come with “sacrifice,” which is why she will waver a bit on the plan as her moving date looms.

“We sacrifice for the job. We sacrifice for each other, and this is the best solution. It doesn’t mean it’s not bittersweet and complicated,” VanSanten explained.
