Israel’s Election Fight Shifts to Coalition Building

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Bloomberg Middle East reporter Dan Williams explains why Gadi Eisenkot has been given the first opportunity to form Israel’s next government despite Benjamin Netanyahu’s bloc winning more seats. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Williams discusses the role of Arab parties in coalition negotiations, Netanyahu’s political future and what could happen if Eisenkot fails to assemble a majority.
