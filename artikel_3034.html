Anthropic Stock Is Now Trading as ANT. Should You Buy Today or Wait?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Tech stock IPOs have a long history of disappointing early investors.

By Matthew Benjamin – Nov 26, 2026 at 2:30PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Anthropic ›

Anthropic (ANT +1.08%), the company behind the popular AI chatbot Claude, began trading last week under the ticker symbol ANT following the largest initial public offering (IPO) in history.

After a strong first day, when investors bid the new stock up from its IPO price of $83 to about $111 per share, it has been falling since. Three trading days after its debut, the stock has dropped below its IPO price and traded at around $78 per share.

So, does that make this exciting new stock a buy, given that it's cheaper now?

I would say no. Even investors determined to add this stock to their portfolio should wait. That's because the initial pop in the share price, followed by a downward trend, is nothing new. In fact, IPO stocks have a long history of disappointing early investors.

IPO stocks are exciting, and that excitement often gives their shares an initial market boost. The share price of an IPO stock typically sees a first-day return of 18%, according to research by Edward Jones. Technology stocks do even better on day one, with a 35.2% increase.

But IPO tech stocks then fall, on average, 3% after three months and 14% after six months, according to that same research. So waiting six months, until the stock finds its footing beyond the initial IPO excitement, is your best bet.
