49ers final injury report: Mike Evans is ‘going in the right direction’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

San Francisco 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan spoke about the final injury report on Friday. The team did not rule out any of the 53-man roster. There are nine players with questionable designations:

WR Mike Evans (Achilles)LB Dre Greenlaw (neck)CB Upton Stout (shoulder)RB Jordan James (calf)DT CJ West (knee)CB Nate Hobbs (adductor)DE Khalid Kareem (ankle)QB Mac Jones (illness)LB Luke Gifford (chest)

When asked about his first day of practice on Thursday, Shanahan said, "He had a good day of practice yesterday. As always, he's a little sore today, but going in the right direction."

Shanahan said the team will see how Evans does during Saturday's walkthrough before deciding his game status. When asked how much of a difference Evans makes, Shanahan said: "I think the guys have done a good job of holding it down without Mike out there. Mike's our guy. A very unique player. Been doing it at a high level for a very long time. Played really well in the games he played this year. Be great to get him back."

The 49ers practiced at half speed on Friday, according to Shanahan. If that's the case, and knowing that Saturday is a walkthrough, it'd be a surprise if Evans wasn't in uniform this Sunday night.

Earlier in the month, Shanahan said they felt Evans could have played if the circumstances were different. After disposing of the Raiders and Cowboys, the next month's schedule will feature four consecutive teams jockeying for playoff position.

On Dre Greenlaw, Shanahan said, "Hoping he'll be alright. You never know with nerves and neck and stuff. But hoping it continues to go in the right direction."

Starting cornerbacks Renardo Green and Deommodore Lenoir are doing "a lot better" and are off the injury report. "Good. They didn't practice last week and weren't sure if they were going to fully play, and they're not on the injury report this week, so a lot better."

We've seen the other team's biggest pass-catching threat have a field day against the secondary in two straight games. Justin Jefferson is probably licking his chops after what he's seen in the past couple of games.

The 49ers need to decide whether cornerback Nate Hobbs or defensive tackle CJ West can improve their roster. Their 21-day practice window closes on December 2. If the Niners don't activate either player by Wednesday, the season is over for Hobbs and West.

The Hobbs signing was always strange, because he's never been the type of player who played on special teams. He didn't hold up on the perimeter at cornerback, so he became a slot by necessity. He's a veteran, but it's unclear where he'd help the secondary.

On Mac Jones feeling better: "I believe so. I think he's trying to get out there today."
