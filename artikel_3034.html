Cohere's Gomez on $25B Valuation, Growth

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Aidan Gomez, co-founder and CEO of Canadian AI Startup Cohere, discusses the company's growth strategy following its latest funding round, which valued the company at $25 billion. He shares his outlook for the business amid surging interest in AI investments and growing investor scrutiny over spending in the sector. He speaks with Haidi Stroud-Watts on Bloomberg: The Asia Trade. (Source: Bloomberg)
