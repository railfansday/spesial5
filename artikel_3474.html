‘Harry Potter’ Star’s Agatha Christie Mystery Officially Finds a New Streaming Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

BritBox has cemented itself as the place to be for fans of the Queen of Crime, Agatha Christie. One of the freshest and most exciting Christie adaptations is on the way to the platform, as My Lady Jane's Edward Bluemel takes on the role of Hercule Poirot in the upcoming Hercule, written by Benji Walters. The series has been described as an "intimate study" of Poirot himself, with the moustache-twirling crime-solver joined by the likes of Henry Ashton, James D'Arcy, Guy Remmers, and Rivals star Katherine Parkinson.

For Hercule to become BritBox's best Christie series, it will have to take down the brilliant 2026 arrival Tommy & Tuppence, based on the two fictional detectives that recur throughout Christie's work, first appearing in her second novel, 1922's The Secret Adversary. Josh Dylan and Antonia Thomas star in the series, which brings the iconic duo to the modern day, as they become entangled in their own relationship and a string of puzzling mysteries. The series also features Harry Potter's Imelda Staunton.

After impressing in the U.S., the series has finally found a British home in time for Christmas. It's been confirmed that 5 have acquired Tommy & Tuppence, with the six-part series expected to air over the festive season. "Agatha Christie's Tommy and Tuppence brings together everything audiences love about a great mystery: clever plotting, intriguing characters and plenty of twists along the way," wrote 5's Acquisitions and Co-Production Director, Cherry Vantreen, in a statement. Vantreen continued:

"With its charming central duo and blend of suspense, adventure and wit, it's a fantastic addition to 5's drama offering, and we're delighted to be bringing this classic Agatha Christie title to our viewers."

Tommy & Tuppence was billed as the first-ever English-language contemporary Christie television adaptation, and it didn't disappoint critics. Collider's own Meredith Loftus was one such critic impressed by the series, calling it the "perfect Only Murders replacement," and scoring it 9/10 in her review. "Agatha Christie's Tommy & Tuppence isn't trying to reinvent the wheel when it comes to the genre, but it does live up to expectations as a mystery-comedy series blended with an irresistible romance," she added.
