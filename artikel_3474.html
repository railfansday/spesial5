This Eufy Robot Vacuum and Mop Is $400 Off Right Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Robot vacuums have been around a while, but it can still be tough to find one that is also good at mopping. But unlike many robot vacuum and mop combos that use spinning pads, the all-in-one Eufy E25 robot vacuum and mop has a self-cleaning roller mop: The roller is rinsed with clean water as it mops, then cleaned at the dock, meaning you don't need to spend as much time cleaning it yourself. It's a smart design, and it's a great time to pick one up: Right now, the E25 is 40% off in an early Black Friday deal at Amazon, taking it down to $599.98 (originally $999.99).

Another perk is that the E25 is equipped with an anti-tangle brush and multiple side brushes that get into tight corners; this is a valuable feature at this price point, as is the 20,000 Pa suction power, which results in a deeper clean on both hard flooring and carpeting, features that are especially appealing for pet owners.

Users can map an area, set zones, schedule cleans, and define different features via the Eufy Clean app; the customizable options include cleaning intensity, suction power, water level, and deciding whether you want the E25 to make multiple passes in an area.  It uses LiDAR mapping and AI for obstacle detection and is highly automated, with a dock that empties the robot, washes and dries the roller mop, and refills its water supply. If you live in a home with pets and want a low-maintenance robot vacuum and mop that can handle routine floor cleaning with minimal intervention, the Eufy E25 can cut some serious time from your cleaning routine.
