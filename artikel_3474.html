All sprites in the Fortnite Chapter 7 mini season

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Sprites have taken over another season with their inclusion in the Fortnite Chapter 7 mini season, "Treasure Hunters." These sprites work functionally the same way as they did in the last seasons: find them around the island, level them up, and extract them to master them. Mastering sprites will unlock different rewards, like sprite dust and cosmetics.

The sprites from previous seasons are not usable this season, but you can interact with them in the Sprite Garden game mode, and you can use them as decorative back bling styles (if you mastered the sprite).

Below we list the new sprites added as part of the Fortnite Chapter 7 mini season's "Treasure Hunters" update.

Update (Nov. 8): We've updated this guide with the new sprite added on Lil' Uzi Vert's Captain Day.
