Bahia Principe Coupons: 40% Off This November

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

For nearly three decades, Bahia Principe Hotels and Resorts has been a go-to for travelers seeking all-inclusive luxury with unbeatable value. The hotel chain consists of 27 properties in the Caribbean, Mexico, and Spain—some adults-only, others family-friendly, and many all-inclusive. Property services are dependent on their category, with all Bahia properties falling into one of these four: Sunlight, Grand, Luxury, and Fantasia—each tailored to different styles of travel. More recently, the hotel has become part of a strategic partnership with Hyatt that allows travelers to earn and use points on hotels, resorts, and sometimes even flights.

This month, savvy travelers can lock in exclusive savings with up to 40% off early bookings, discounts on adults-only hotels, and last-minute deals across dreamy beachfront destinations.

Looking for a sun-soaked getaway without the luxury price tag? Bahia Principe Hotels & Resorts has several offers worth browsing for your next beach escape. From Tenerife to the Dominican Republic and beyond, score on the site’s seasonal promotions with up to 35% off stays on selected resorts across the Caribbean. As for those planning a trip to Mexico, you can save with Bahia Principe promo code ESPECIAL for an extra 5% off eligible all-inclusive stays at select Riviera Maya resorts. Just book by November 16, 2026, for travel through December 20, 2027, and make sure to check if any restrictions apply, of course.

Seeking a kid-free, relaxation-filled, and, most importantly, discounted escape? With a simple and free sign-up to the brand’s loyalty program, travelers can receive up to 40% off adults-only hotels and resorts (discount dependent on availability), from Bahia Principe Escape Aquamarine in the Dominican Republic to Bahia Principe Escape Coral Playa, nestled within the golden coastline of Mallorca, Spain. Think oceanfront suites, infinity pools, and top-tier dining—all at an unbeatable price, starting at only $120 per night.

There’s no shortage of fabulous Bahia Principe properties in desirable locations—from private baysides to lush jungles. Try one of the four all-inclusive resorts in Riviera Maya, Mexico—or the Fantasia Punta Cana property that is beachside and family-friendly—and included in Traveler’s roundup of the best all-inclusive resorts in the Dominican Republic.

Having trouble getting your Bahia Principe promo code to work? Before giving up on those beach vacation savings, double-check the fine print. Make sure your selected stay qualifies and that your booking falls within the promotion period. If the code still won't apply, visit the official Bahia Principe offers page to review current deals or contact the resort's booking team for assistance.
