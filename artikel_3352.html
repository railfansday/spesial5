Mckenna Grace Sci-Fi Sequel Hits Huge Box Office Milestone 6X Faster Than The Last Movie

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping has a reason to give thanks today.

2026's Sunrise on the Reaping is the sixth installment in the Hunger Games franchise, which adapts Suzanne Collins' dystopian young adult novel series of the same name. It is a prequel following the early years of Haymitch Abernathy (Joseph Zada), marking the franchise's second prequel after 2023's The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, which was set 40 years earlier. The movie, which co-stars Mckenna Grace, Whitney Peak, Ralph Fiennes, and Glenn Glose, earned a 3-day opening weekend of $119.3 million, giving it the second-best domestic debut in history for a prequel, behind only Rogue One ($155.1 million).

Lionsgate has officially confirmed that The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping earned an estimated $15.15 million at the domestic box office on Wednesday, bringing its cumulative domestic total to $163.3 million. This sees it crossing the $150 million domestic milestone after just six days in theaters, making it six times quicker thanThe Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, which passed that milestone on day 36 and legged out to an overall domestic total of $166.3 million.
