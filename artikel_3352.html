Jamie Foxx Halloween Party: 1 Person Stabbed, Suspect Arrested for Assault With a Deadly Weapon

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Jamie Foxx's annual Halloween party held at his sprawling Thousand Oaks mansion took a violent turn Saturday night when a stabbing took place ... TMZ has learned.

Law enforcement sources tell TMZ … deputies and paramedics responded to a party at Jamie's house.

A male -- who was stabbed in the neck -- suffered a superficial wound and was treated by paramedics at the scene.

In a chaotic video taken outside the party -- which is organized by Jamie's daughter Anelise -- multiple individuals can be seen in cuffs as hundreds of costumed attendees are directed off the premises.

In a pic from the scene ... a sign clearly states a "No Real or Fake Weapons" rule.

Sources tell us ... deputies ended up arresting a male suspect for assault with a deadly weapon.

It's unclear if Jamie -- who hosts the party at his sprawling mansion every year -- was home at the time.
