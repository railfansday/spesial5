Shygirl, Moby Drop New Songs for ‘Club Kid’ Soundtrack

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Shygirl, Moby, and Brutalismus 3000 contributed new songs to EP Club Kid (Music From & Inspired by the Film), the soundtrack to Jordan Firstman’s recent film Club Kid. Released by A24 Music, the five-track EP also includes two distinct covers of Arthur Russell’s “The Boy with a Smile” by Christine and the Queens and Nirosta Steel.

Shygirl, who appears as herself in the film, offered ethereal song “Waves,” which comes on the heels of her second studio album Swallow!.

Moby offered “I Want a Rocket,” a dance floor-ready number with a pulsating beat.

Club Kid, now in select theaters, stars Firstman, Reggie Absolom, and Diego Calva. It debuted at Cannes earlier this year. Rolling Stone then wrote that the film was the “closest thing to a seismic hit the king-making event has had this year, and will likely be the defining movie of the 2026 edition.”

Firstman recently spoke to Rolling Stone about writing and directing the movie, his directorial feature debut. Club Kid is the story of a club promoter named Peter who ends up having a particularly wild night out. Ten years later, someone shows up at his door and introduces him to the son (played by Absalom) he never knew he had. The reluctant dad and his kid end up bonding. A longtime fixture in New York City nightlife and a regular at hot spots like Basement, Firstman had been spending a lot of time in Berlin doing what he termed an “excessive” amount of party drugs (“a lot of different letters, a lot of different numbers”) when he realized he needed to slow things down. Then, at a dinner party, he began talking a friend’s kid and “I was saying a bunch of inappropriate stuff around [him]. Everyone was laughing. I thought, ‘Oh, there’s something here.’”

He explained that making the film helped him to personally become more responsible. “I feel like I’ve stepped up in a lot of ways,” he said. “I feel like I’ve stepped up for myself. I feel more responsible. I go home at 4 a.m. I party once every three to four months now. And now I can have fun at the party.”
