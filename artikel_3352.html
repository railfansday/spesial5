4 Years After The Series' End, Disney's Amphibia Returns With Strange Voyage

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Disney’s Amphibia is returning more than four years after its animated finale, although not with a new season. Creator Matt Braly is bringing the story back through Amphibia: Strange Voyage, a serialized comic published by TOKYOPOP and Disney Manga. Its first installment is scheduled to reach comic shops on January 13, 2027.

The announcement gives fans a reason to revisit Wartwood and the Plantars, while also setting up an adventure that moves beyond the television series’ final battle. Amphibia ran for three seasons on Disney Channel and ended on May 14, 2022, with “The Hardest Thing.” Strange Voyage picks up after that conclusion rather than retelling Anne Boonchuy’s original arrival.

The opening premise begins months after Anne saves Amphibia from the Core and returns to Earth. With that crisis behind them, the Plantars embark on a voyage to find a new continent. Their expedition does not go according to plan: a dangerous turn leaves the crew stranded on an unfamiliar island, where unexpected secrets await.

That setup gives the comic room to explore a part of Amphibia that the television series did not have time to map out. The animated show balanced its fantasy setting with comedy, friendship, and a coming-of-age story, but Strange Voyage introduces new territory and a fresh mystery. The island may prove as important as the destination.

Matt Braly is writing the new series, keeping its storytelling in the hands of the person who created Amphibia and guided its three-season run. Beatrice Bovo is providing the artwork, including the cover designs. Their collaboration gives the project a direct creative link to the original series while allowing a different artist to interpret its familiar characters.

Fans should not expect a handful of disconnected comic strips. Braly has described Strange Voyage as one continuous narrative planned across ten issues, with a new installment arriving every three weeks. He has also characterized it as an important canon miniseries, showing that the journey is intended to matter to the larger Amphibia story.

That distinction is worth paying attention to, especially for viewers who want the comic to build on the series rather than simply recreate its episodic humor. A serialized format gives the writers space to develop the island’s mysteries, the Plantars’ expedition and the consequences of the choices made along the way. The first three issues establish the opening stretch.

The release schedule starts with Amphibia: Strange Voyage #1 on January 13, 2027. Issue #2 is scheduled for February 3, followed by Issue #3 on February 24. Each issue is listed at $4.99, and the first three installments are 32 pages apiece, giving readers a regular way to follow the story.

Readers who would rather sample the comic series for free will have another opportunity in the spring. Amphibia: Strange Voyage – Issue #1 is among the titles announced for Comics Giveaway Day 2027, Penguin Random House’s branding for the 2027 Free Comic Book Day event. Participating comic shops are scheduled to hand out the giveaway books on Saturday, May 1.

There is an important distinction between that promotion and the regular release schedule: the listed giveaway is Issue #1, not a free collection of the first three installments. The January and February issues are separate $4.99 releases, so readers who want to keep up with the story before May will need to pick them up individually.

Comics Giveaway Day could also introduce the series to readers who missed Amphibia during its Disney Channel run. The 2027 lineup includes books from several publishers, making the event a broad entry point for comic newcomers. For returning viewers, the bigger attraction is Braly’s continuation of the Plantars’ story in a planned canon adventure.
