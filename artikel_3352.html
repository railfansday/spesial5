Rachel Brosnahan-Led ‘Presumed Innocent’ Sets Season 2 Release Date, Unveils First Photos Of Matthew Rhys, Courtney B. Vance & More

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Apple TV has slotted Friday, February 19 for the Season 2 premiere of its critically acclaimed anthology series Presumed Innocent, from David E. Kelley and J.J. Abrams. Season two will launch globally on Apple TV with the first two episodes on Friday, February 19, 2027, followed by one new episode every Friday through April 2.

Season 2 stars Rachel Brosnahan, who also executive produces, along with Emmy winner Matthew Rhys. The streamer also released several first-look photos, which you can see below, and a teaser above.

The sophomore season is based on Dissection of a Murder, the debut legal thriller novel by Jo Murray, who also serves as a co-executive producer. In it, up-and-coming defense attorney Lelia Reynolds (Brosnahan) faces off against her district attorney husband (Rhys) in a murder case that threatens to destroy both their careers and their marriage Watch on Deadline

In addition to Brosnahan and Rhys, season two stars two-time winner Courtney B. Vance, Emmy nominee and BAFTA Award-winner Fiona Shaw, Jack Reynor, Lesley-Ann Brandt, and features Judith Light as a special guest star.

Kelley serves as showrunner on season two and executive produces through David E. Kelley Productions in association with Warner Bros. Television alongside Matthew Tinker. Abrams executive produces through Bad Robot Productions. Rachel Rusch Rich, Dee Johnson, Dustin Thomason and Brosnahan also serve as executive producers. Jake Gyllenhaal, who starred in the first season, executive produces under his Nine Stories banner. Presumed Innocent author Scott Turow is a co-executive producer.

Presumed Innocent season one earned several Emmy Award nominations, including Outstanding Lead Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie for Jake Gyllenhaal; Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie for Bill Camp; Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie for Peter Sarsgaard; and Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie for Ruth Negga.
