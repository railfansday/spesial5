Sinner aiming for Australian Open return

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Jannik Sinner has confirmed his plans to return from injury at the Australian Open in January; The World No 2 has not played since July and recently lost the top spot in the rankings to Alexander Zverev; watch the WTA Finals this week, live on Sky Sports Tennis

Jannik Sinner has confirmed he plans to compete at the Australian Open in January.

The Italian has not played since defending his Wimbledon title in July due to a knee injury.

Sinner, who has lost his world No 1 spot to Alexander Zverev, won the Melbourne title in 2024 and 2025 but was beaten by Novak Djokovic in this year's semi-final.

"The goal is to return to Australia," the 25-year-old said at press conference in Milan.

"Maybe play a few tournaments earlier. But I have to take everything one step after the other every day towards the goal because if we hurry then maybe it will be worse. But yes, the goal is Australia"

The five-time Grand Slam winner also lauded Brit Jack Draper alongside Matteo Berrettini and Carlos Alcaraz for his support during his period out with injury.

Draper himself has ruled out returning before 2027 after missing every Grand Slam this year, and said he was in a "difficult place" after his first-round defeat by Martin Landaluce in the Cincinnati Open in August, admitting he was struggling to escape the mental fatigue that had built up during 15 months of battling serious injury.

"When I announced that my season was over, Berrettini and Draper got in touch straight away," Sinner added. "Jack is also going through a difficult period, so he understands what I'm going through.

"Alcaraz had sent me a message, just as I message him. I really appreciated it.

"We are trying to find a way out of this situation and working towards experiencing those emotions again.

"For me, tennis is not a job. I continue to play because I enjoy it."
