Brighton have arguably been the story of the Premier League season — and they added a glorious chapter to that passage with last Saturday's 1-0 win at Manchester City.

It means the Seagulls have now beaten the two title favourites without conceding a goal, having crushed champions Arsenal 3-0 in September, while they started the weekend as the league's highest scorers (24 goals) and boasting its' second-meanest defence (just seven conceded).

Fabian Hurzeler's men take on Brentford less than 72 hours after a 1-0 away win over Spanish side Getafe in the Conference League, so the German manager could rotate his side.

He may also have Ferdi Kadioglu back from injury, with Femi Azeez and Pascal Struijk also closing in on their return to action.

No team has tasted defeat fewer times in the Premier League than Brentford this season, with the Bees beaten just once in the top flight this term.

The Bees start the weekend five points behind Brighton and they would be even closer had they turned some of their five draws into wins, with last Saturday's 2-0 victory over Nottingham Forest just their third triumph of the campaign.

That may change now last season's top goalscorer Igor Thiago appears to have rediscovered his form, with the Brazilian finding the net in each of the past two games.

Brentford have no fresh injury concerns, but Antoni Milambo and Josh Dasilva are not expected to be available until after the international break.
