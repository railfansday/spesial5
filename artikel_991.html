Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Liverpool fans did not miss a beat. They never do. “Poch is at the wheel,” they goaded as this calamity unfolded. But at the wheel of what, a clown car? The look of thunder on Mauricio Pochettino’s face said it all as he got off to the worst start of any Manchester United manager – or head coach – in 100 years.

The Pochettino bandwagon rolled into town with the usual fanfare of a new era, new belief, new hope; and then the wheels came off, the doors collapsed and the engine exploded. The opening quarter of an hour really was that farcical.

In that time, away to Liverpool of all places, they were 2-0 down, a player down – and down and out. If Pochettino had any uncertainty over the scale of the task ahead of him, there it was laid bare. Of course United had some mitigation. They played almost the entire game with 10 men, but teams have coped with that before and shown far more resistance and resilience. By the time United did that, it was game over. No one expected a quick fix, but come on.

Any regrets, Mauricio? Whatever the salary, he could be forgiven for having a few as he watched on. This is a far cry from being in charge of the United States and watching them roll over Haiti 8-0 on aggregate as he did last week. If Liverpool had been more ruthless, it could have been 8-0 here.

In the end it was 4-0 – the same scoreline by which Lal Hilditch lost his first game at United, away to Bolton Wanderers in October 1926. Hilditch was a stop-gap player-manager and former central defender Pochettino may well have been tempted to bring himself on, even at the age of 54. He could barely have done worse.

Pochettino, too, can take some responsibility. Eyebrows were certainly raised when he dropped Harry Maguire – who he then had to bring on after 22 minutes – to hand Matthijs de Ligt his first Premier League start in 357 days. De Ligt was then at fault for the first and, arguably, the second goal.

Did the head coach start with a back three or a back four? The players seemed confused. It eventually ended up as a back five.

But this was a collective failing for United. From top – with director of football Jason Wilcox caught on camera with his head buried in his coat – through the club and out on the pitch. Pochettino is big on building a culture. So he has to abolish the comedy and the chaos and restore credibility.

United are deep in a relegation battle. Have no doubt. Eight points after 11 games means they are currently on target for fewer than 28 points, which is a lower points per game total than when they were relegated in 1974 (32 over 42 games).

The work needed at United is monumental. The transfer strategy has been inexplicable, with the decision being to rebuild the attack last year, the midfield this summer and wait until next year to remodel the defence. The folly of that has been exposed by the fact that United have conceded 34 goals already this season, the most in the top five European leagues.

United will argue, with justification, that Leny Yoro’s eighth-minute sending-off for denial of a goalscoring opportunity was harsh given that Alexander Isak appeared to have already lost control of the ball. But what was Yoro thinking as he bundled into the back of the forward? And what was De Ligt doing as he watched on, or lumbered on, after failing to track the run from Ronald Araújo’s ball over the top?

Speaking in his first press conference as Man Utd boss, Pochettino was asked about creating a culture in a seemingly tricky environment and replied: “We arrived with the idea to create something from scratch, from our own ideas and philosophy in the way that we love to work. I think we find very supportive people in the club that make the adaptation easy, but at the same time, we need to improve our players, like always, you need to improve your players, improve all the ways to create… the way to find a way to perform.

“I think what we need to do is to be very open-minded and try to do our best to try to perform and the players need to buy the idea of we need to improve, and that is our job. After a few days working, the job is to try to find a way to, from Sunday, perform and to start getting the results that the club historically needs, and it’s because Manchester United needs to be in a place, in a different place than we are now.”

Meanwhile, Liverpool are sat in fifth but have only managed one win in five league matches at Anfield this season, meaning they have the third-worst home form in the division. However, Andoni Iraola’s men will move into the top four with three points here and will smell blood against a beleaguered United side.
