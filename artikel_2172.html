National League clubs interested as 31-year-old leaves Millwall by mutual consent

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Millwall have had experienced goalkeeper Joel Coleman on the books as cover since the summer of 2025. He is yet to make an appearance for the Lions and was left out of their 25-man EFL squad for the ongoing campaign.

Now, writing on X, reporter Pete O’Rourke states that Coleman and the club have come to an agreement over the mutual termination of his contract. It frees the 31-year-old to depart and find a new team outside of the transfer window, and there are National League sides keen on the shot-stopper.

Coleman has played the majority of football across his career in League One, but he has appeared in League Two and the Championship too. He was in three matchday squads for Alex Neil‘s side this season prior to his omission.

Including Coleman, Millwall have had five goalkeepers on their books this season. Even with Lukas Jensen and Filip Marschall cast to the sidelines through injury, the former Bolton Wanderers and Huddersfield Town man has been third choice behind Max Crocombe and George Evans for the most part.

Injuries can bring opportunities for the backup ‘keepers, but the Lions’ depth in this position means it would take a serious crisis, dismal form from several players or both for Coleman to come into contention for minutes in Neil’s side.

It means the Championship club can afford to let him go mid-season without signing a replacement. Coleman joined on a free transfer as well, so they’re not taking a big loss in terminating his contract either.

It remains to be seen just where he winds up after his contract end, but this move should benefit Coleman.

Coleman has spent much of his career as a backup goalkeeper, but his minutes have dried up in recent times especially. He never played for Millwall and he didn’t appear in his final season at Bolton Wanderers in 2024/25 either.

Having kept training, the Bolton-born shot-stopper will hope he can move to a National League club and quickly become a hit. He’ll have plenty of experience and pedigree to offer so could become an influential asset on and off the pitch.

Coleman’s best season came as he broke through at Oldham Athletic, keeping 12 clean sheets and conceding 35 goals in 32 League One appearances across the 2015/16 season. He will hope to tap back into that form in the bid to become a starting shot-stopper again.
