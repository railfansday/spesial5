Sheffield United told they have ‘more than enough’ to get into the play-offs by former player

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Sheffield United are positioned in 10th place in the table after their first 15 fixtures just behind West Ham. SUFC are two points off the top eight behind both QPR and Southampton following their 2-1 defeat away at Charlton Athletic before the international break.

The Blades are back in action next weekend with a clash against Watford at Bramall Lane as they aim to return to winning ways in front of their own fans following their disappointing defeat at The Valley last time out. Their upcoming opponents are sat in 20th and are five points above the drop zone.

Sharp believes the South Yorkshire outfit can challenge for promotion in this campaign and he has delivered his verdict on Sky Sports‘ Essential EFL Podcast: “They’ve got more than enough, I’ve said it before. They’ve got a group of midfielders who have got so much talent. I think Kalvin Phillips is becoming more like the Kalvin Phillips that we all know. He looks fitter and stronger.”

He added: “I’m still quietly confident that Sheffield United will be one of those teams in the play-offs.”

Sheffield United have a solid goalkeeper in the form of Michael Cooper. When he plays to his best, SUFC’s stopper is one of the best in his position in the EFL.

The Blades could do with tightening things up at the back and have let in 19 goals this term. Only West Ham have conceded more in the top 10 than Chris Wilder‘s side.

As Sharp has alluded to, his former club have some useful players in the heart of midfield. They have the likes of Kalvin Phillips, Oli Arblaster, Sydie Peck and Joe Rothwell to pick from and they all possess quality at second tier level.

Further up the pitch, Patrick Bamford is a proven goal scorer and has been an effective weapon for Sheffield United since his move. They also have other options like Tom Cannon, Jordan Ayew and Tyrese Campbell, whilst Brentford loan winger Romelle Donovan is a bright young talent who can make things happen out of nothing.

Sheffield United need to be more consistent if they are to establish themselves as genuine promotion contenders. SUFC would have been level with QPR and Southampton if they had beaten Charlton Athletic.

The Blades haven’t been able to string together a proper run of wins yet. Wilder will be working hard with his players on the training ground during the break to get them fully up to speed.

Their next game against Watford is a great opportunity to return to winning ways. The Hornets have been poor recently under under pressure boss Alessio Dionisi.
