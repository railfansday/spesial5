Sell vintage, collectibles, one-of-a-kind finds, and used electronics on eBay, where 136 million active buyers shop and auctions let bidders decide the price of rare pieces. Before you list, set your price to cover its fee of 13.6% of the whole sale up to $7,500 in most categories, shipping and sales tax included.

Say a $60 handmade cutting board sells with 8% sales tax and free shipping on a $10 label, and you pay no ad fees or eBay listing fee. By our math, you keep about $41 on eBay or Amazon Handmade and about $44 on Etsy.

Vintage dealers, collectors, and anyone selling one-of-a-kind finds or used electronics get the most from eBay’s buyers and auctions. Makers building a brand do better on Etsy, which leaves about $3 more of that $60 sale, or with an online store of their own.
