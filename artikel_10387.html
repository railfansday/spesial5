Motorola Signature 27 gets Pixel’s Gemini Intelligence features with Rambler, Create My Widget

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Motorola is adopting Google’s Gemini Intelligence features in the first major expansion beyond Pixel, with Gboard’s Rambler and more supported.

While Google’s Gemini Intelligence is technically supporting Samusng’s latest Galaxy devices too, it’s Motorola that’s opening the floodgates beyond the Pixel lineup. The new Motorola Signature 27 is officially getting support for the bulk of Google’s new features, including “Create My Widget” which, so far, has only been available on Googlebook.

Supported Gemini Intelligence features on Motorola Signature 27 include:

Taking those one by one, “Create My Widget” is, again, the most intriguing addition. Google announced this feature early this year but, even for Google Pixel phones, it’s not yet gone live. That’s presumably soon to change, seeing as Motorola’s launch date for this device is November 19 and the company confirmed that this will be available out of the box on that date.

Regardless, the feature itself allows users to describe the functionality they want in a widget to Gemini before the AI spits out a functional widget.

Gboard Rambler and Sign-to-Text are also supported here. The former supports taking long-winded voice-to-text prompts and cleaning up filler words and even add formatting, while Sign-to-Text translates American Sign Language via video to English text.

Finally, Gemini task automation allows users to assign certain tasks to Gemini, with the AI then running the bulk of the task through supported apps such as Uber and DoorDash in the background. This is also available on Pixel and select Samsung Galaxy devices.

Motorola Signature 27 pre-orders are open now, with the flagship priced at $1,599 in the US.
