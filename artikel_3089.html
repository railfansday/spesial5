Ariane 6 launches 36 Amazon Leo satellites nine days after Vulcan failure

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Amazon Leo now has 432 satellites in orbit, Ars Technica reports. Europe is weighing how to build more Ariane 6 rockets, with a decision possible next month.

An Ariane 6 upper stage being lifted onto DLR’s P5.2 test stand, February 2021

Amazon is moving its next SpaceX launch for Amazon Leo forward to December. On Tuesday, Europe’s Ariane 6 put 36 more of its satellites into orbit. An Amazon official told Stephen Clark of Ars Technica that the Falcon 9 flight was originally due next year. Several more Falcon 9 launches will follow in quick succession.

SpaceX also runs Starlink, the network Amazon Leo is built to compete with. Starlink has more than 11,000 satellites, according to Ars.

The Ariane 6 lifted off from French Guiana at about 6:15am New York time, Summer Maxwell reported for Bloomberg. It released all 36 satellites about two hours later. It was the rocket’s 10th flight and Arianespace’s fourth for Amazon. Arianespace has now launched 136 Amazon satellites.

The launch came nine days after a Vulcan rocket failure destroyed 40 Amazon Leo satellites. United Launch Alliance’s Vulcan is Amazon’s main launch provider. It cannot fly until an investigation led by the US Federal Aviation Administration ends. Blue Origin’s New Glenn has not flown since May, and its return to flight has slipped to early next year.

Amazon Leo now has 432 satellites in orbit, Ars reported. Amazon has booked 108 launches to deploy 3,232 satellites, and 17 have flown so far, with one failure. Amazon has not yet started its internet service, Bloomberg reported. In 2025 it had said the service would start that year.

Amazon added six Ariane 6 launches in September, taking its total with Arianespace to 24. Arianespace plans to raise the rocket’s cadence to 12 launches a year by about 2030, according to Bloomberg.

Europe’s factories can support up to nine or ten Ariane 6 flights a year, Ars reported. Arianespace is likely to reach six this year, with one more before the end of December. European ministers are weighing plans to build more Ariane 6 rockets, and a decision could come next month.

Amazon built its 1,000th satellite in October. Hundreds now wait in storage for a ride to orbit, according to Ars.
