Your Weekly Horoscope, November 8-14, 2026: Moving Forward Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The week ahead begins with emotions, depth, and insights. Shortly after the new Moon in Scorpio turns the night dark, both Mercury and Venus draw their retrogrades to an end and initiate forward movement once again. It’s a time of transition as we start to slowly unravel and shift our focus from inward to outward, review to integration. Your weekly horoscope for November 8-14, 2026, helps you understand where to turn your awareness and efforts.

November 8 or 9 | New Moon in Scorpio November 13 | Mercury retrograde and Venus retrograde end

A new Moon in the sign of Scorpio meets us on November 8 or 9, 2026, depending on location. Look into the night on a new Moon and nothing is visible. The Moon is still there, about to begin her cycle once again yet hidden. This is a necessary space of closure before initiation can take place. As the Moon prepares to continue in Scorpio, Mercury continues its retrograde in the same sign. This means we are initiating a new chapter even as our awareness is still looking backward through our existing stories, memories, and inner material. This lunation knows that before we can begin again, we must be ready to tell a new story. And to tell a new story, we must be aware of the story we are telling ourselves, both consciously and unconsciously.

The new Moon in Scorpio draws us toward our innermost layers and asks us to observe and to feel as we dispense with pretense and sit with only what is real. It also reminds us to trace the origins of stories we encounter that belong to voices other than our own. What lies beneath them. Is there fear or shame, an unmet need or discouraged desire wanting to be acknowledged? The Moon beginning again nudges us to move differently, more truthfully, so old stories no longer speak over present and future experiences and patterns that once felt like cages reveal there was a door all along.

Our final Mercury retrograde of the year comes to an end a few days after the new Moon on November 13, 2026. Here our minds emerge from three weeks of excavating the depths of our unconscious.

Slowly, our awareness begins reorienting outward once again toward the world around us. Slowly, our insights begin to relate what was observed and questioned in order to be understood and integrated. And slowly, we reorder ourselves as we begin to move ahead once again after a necessary pause.

After Mercury moves direct, it remains in Scorpio until December 6, 2026, so there remains an element of introspection, depth, and observation. Here we allow the Pluto-ruled sign to bring the mind focus, intuition, questioning, and sight that extends beyond our usual awareness.

After five weeks of retrograde, Venus stations direct in Libra on the same day as Mercury in Scorpio. Our moment of pause, review, and inward focus officially begins spiraling outward into something new.

Whereas Mercury invited the mind to begin rearranging and integrating according to what was observed in its retrograde, Venus asks our bodies and our emotions to do the same. This relates to the way we connect, share, extend love, and allow ourselves to receive love in turn. The way we protect ourselves and our vulnerabilities from ourselves and others. The way we desire and permit ourselves to be honest about those desires. And the way we accept, within our bodies and our life, our innate value.

As Venus stations direct, it’s a time to sit with the awareness, thoughts, realizations, and questions you held during her retrograde. What thoughts surrounding love, relationships, connection, or beauty moved through you? What insights, yearnings, desires, fears, and hopes presented themselves? What inner patterns became apparent through the way you connect, share, and open or close yourself to another? Venus direct brings space for what was observed through conscious awareness to begin organizing and integrating in life.

This week asks for spaciousness and honesty. In exchange, it delivers continued momentum. You begin telling yourself new stories, unravelling old fears, and rearranging the way you think, act, and exist to be more aligned with what truly matters to you.
