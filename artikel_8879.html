Church and Cultural Centre, Veneux-les-Sablons, France / Bertrand Taquet Architectes

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Text description provided by the architects. In Veneux-les-Sablons, south-east of Paris, the church of Saint-Philippe-et-Saint-Jacques reopens ten years after the fire that partly destroyed it in 2016. Built in 1828 by subscription among the villagers, it had been enlarged in 1959 by a second nave set at right angles to the first. The fire destroyed that extension and most of the roof of the original nave. The Town of Moret-Loing-et-Orvanne decided to restore the church and rebuild its extension as a shared building: the historic nave remains a place of worship, the new extension becomes a cultural hall.
