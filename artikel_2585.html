What Are Yankees' Options At Third Base After Alec Bohm's Reported Deal With Royals?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Ryan McMahon has not been a good addition for the New York Yankees at third base, and they entered the offseason with a clear need for an upgrade.

However, the Yankees have yet to find a replacement for him. They were reportedly interested in signing Alec Bohm, but he has reportedly agreed to a two-year, $20 million deal with the Kansas City Royals. What are the Yankees' options now?

The Yankees could pivot in free agency and target someone like Eugenio Suárez. They could also find a way to move McMahon and then look for a platoon option to pair with José Caballero. Si-Hwan Roh, posted by the Hanwha Eagles, could also be an option.

The reality is that there are not really any great third basemen available in free agency. The Yankees could explore the trade market and target someone like Casey Schmitt. The team was reportedly interested in Maikel Garcia before he was traded.

The Yankees reportedly like what they have at second base with Anthony Volpe and Luis Garcia Jr., but one possible fix at third base is to move Volpe there full time. That would let Garcia stay in the lineup every day and give the Yankees an option to replace McMahon.

The third base market is extremely thin now that the top options have all signed. The Yankees need to decide quickly which direction they want to go.
