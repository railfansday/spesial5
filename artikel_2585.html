Rams versus Packers live discussion: We’re all cheeseheads tonight

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The seventh-seeded Green Bay Packers take on the 9th-seeded Los Angeles Rams. That is not something you would have thought would be true about these two teams at this point in the season if somebody had told you that in August. The Packers hold the tiebreaker over the Rams, thanks to a better conference record.

This is a matchup the Packers have dominated. Against the Rams, Green Bay is 20-2 in their last 22 games. They are 10-1 in the last 11. When you own a team to that extent, it's no longer a trend. It's domination. Green Bay has fared pretty well in their last dozen contests against the NFC West in general. It's odd, but it can't be ignored.

The Rams are without Puka Nacua and Davante Adams. That hasn't slowed down the Rams during the past month, as they still rank sixth in the league in EPA per play and success rate. But they've faced the Cardinals twice, the Commanders, Chargers, and Raiders since Week 6. The last time they faced a top offense was in Week 5, and the Bills scored 37 points.

To their credit, the Rams have been stingy defensively. Only one team has scored more than 18 points since the Bills game. They've dictated much of the game, but this will be an offense that can truly challenge the Rams in ways they haven't been all season.

The Packers are one of those "all-or-nothing" kind of offenses. They can go multiple drives without scoring, and then hit on a big play or three to flip the scoreboard. This should be a lower-scoring game. It probably won't be fun to watch.

History says the Packers will win. Micah Parsons is back, and that should make it a living hell for Matthew Stafford, who has been off all season.
