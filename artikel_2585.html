Matchup: Pittsburgh Steelers vs. Cincinnati Bengals

Online Streaming: DirecTV, Fubo, Hulu + Live TV, Sling TV, Peacock

Sunday Night Football takes place on Sunday (Nov. 15) with the Pittsburgh Steelers (6-2) taking on the Cincinnati Bengals (6-2) at Paycor Stadium in Cincinnati, Ohio. Kick off is at 8:20 p.m. ET/5:20 p.m. PT.

In 2026, Sunday Night Football broadcasts on TV on NBC, while the game livestreams on Peacock.

The NFL is known for celebrity sightings, so it's likely there may be a number of famous actors, actresses and recording artists in attendance during the game. Artists who have been known to appear at games in the past, include Cincinnati Bengals fans Olivia Ponton, Nick Lachey, John Legend, Gary Owen and others.

For cord-cutters, there are a few ways to watch Sunday Night Football, if you don't have cable, especially if you want to watch for free. DirecTV has a five-day free trial, while other streaming services, such as Fubo and Hulu + Live TV, also offer free trials, so you can watch the game for free.

A subscription to DirecTV gets you access to live TV, local and cable channels, including NBC, at $64.99 for the first month of service ($94.99 per month afterward). The service even offers a five-day free trial to watch for free, if you sign up now. It comes with access to ESPN Unlimited too for even more ESPN networks.

Sling Blue comes with 40+ broadcast and cable channels.

Sling TV offers the Blue plan, which comes with NBC. With more than 40 channels, the streaming plan goes for $54.99 per month. All plans come with a free cloud DVR and an extra 600 or so free channels too. Please note: Pricing and channel availability vary from market-to-market.

Fubo is an affordable option offering a five-day free trial for new users who sign up.

Fubo starts at $63.99 for the first month ($88.99 per month afterward) with more than 230 channels, including local and cable, like NBC, that are streamable on smart TVs, smartphones, tablets and on web browsers. And with a five-day free trial, you can watch for free, if you act fast and sign up now.

Comes with Hulu, Disney+ and ESPN Select.

Broadcast network NBC is available to watch with Hulu + Live TV. Prices for the cable alternative starts at $99.99 per month for more than 100 live channels, while each plan comes with Hulu, Disney+ and ESPN Select at no additional cost. Hulu + Live TV might be best for those who want all of these streaming services together in one package.
