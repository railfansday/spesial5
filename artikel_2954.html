Yankees Cut Ties With Luis Gil Two Years After Rookie Of Year Award Win

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Luis Gil was the 2024 American League Rookie of the Year, but the Yankees non-tendered him on Friday.

Gil was a key part of the Yankees reaching the World Series in 2024. However, he dealt with a lat strain for most of 2025 and dealt with shoulder inflammation last season. He has thrown 113 1/3 innings since the end of the 2024 season across all levels.

He was projected to get $2.3 million in arbitration, but now he is free to sign with any team. Plenty of teams could be willing to take a chance on him off his 2024 numbers alone.

In 2024, Gil had a 3.50 ERA in 29 starts. He did struggle with walks that season, but the potential alone is enough for some team to sign him. However, he may have to sign a minor league deal.

Even though he won Rookie of the Year just two years ago, he is already 28, and that may be something teams are not too fond of.

As for the Yankees, they could re-sign Gil on a minor league deal if they still think there's a chance he can replicate his success from 2024. However, that scenario seems unlikely. They got through the season without needing him in the rotation, despite countless injuries.

The Yankees have plenty of starting pitching options outside of their big four of Gerrit Cole, Cam Schlittler, Max Fried and Carlos Rodón.
