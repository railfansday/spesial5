Coinbase Global vs. Visa: Which Financial Stock Is a Better Buy for 2027?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Coinbase's crypto exchange business faces wild swings, while Visa's payments network posts margins above 50%, so which one deserves a spot in your portfolio?

By Jake Lerch – Updated Nov 23, 2026 at 10:30AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Coinbase Global ›

Coinbase Global (COIN +7.96%) and Visa (V +2.01%) are both giants in the world of digital money, yet they follow very different paths. You must decide which stock is the better addition to your portfolio for 2027.

Coinbase is the leading exchange for the crypto economy, while Visa operates the most recognized global payments network. This comparison highlights the choice between the high-growth volatility of emerging assets and the established reliability of traditional transaction processing for long-term investors.

Coinbase Global operates a trusted platform that allows users to trade and store Bitcoin (BTC +2.05%) and other digital assets. The company serves a diverse base of consumer and institutional clients and has a strategic partnership with Circle for stablecoin services. As it expands its Base Chain network, the company reinforces its position within the rapidly changing fintech sector.

In the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached nearly $5.6 billion. The company reported a net loss of approximately $701.6 million during this period, which resulted in a net margin of nearly -12.5%. This performance represented a decline in revenue growth of roughly 26.6% compared to the prior twelve-month period, highlighting how sensitive trading revenue is to broader market price fluctuations.

As of its December 2025 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio is nearly 0.5x, indicating total debt relative to shareholders' equity. The current ratio, which measures the ability to cover short-term debts with short-term assets, is roughly 2.3x. In the fiscal year ended Dec. 31, 2025, free cash flow was close to $2.4 billion. Note that stock-based compensation accounted for roughly 34.6% of operating cash flow, inflating reported cash generation, since SBC is a non-cash expense added back to cash flow.

Visa is a global payments technology company that connects consumers and merchants through its vast digital network. It works with over 14,000 financial institutions and maintains high-profile sponsorships with the International Olympic Committee and FIFA. The company continues to integrate with digital wallets and e-commerce platforms to capture more electronic transactions across more than 200 countries and territories.

In the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached nearly $45.9 billion. During the same period, net income was approximately $23.5 billion, resulting in a net margin of close to 51.2%. This performance represented revenue growth of roughly 14.8% compared to the prior twelve-month period, reflecting the steady expansion of electronic payments globally.

As of its September 2026 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was close to 0.6x, a metric that compares total debt to shareholders' equity. The current ratio stands at approximately 1.0x, indicating a balance between current assets and current liabilities. In the fiscal year ended Sept. 30, 2026, free cash flow was nearly $22.1 billion, which is calculated as operating cash flow minus capital expenditures.

Coinbase Global faces risks from the high volatility of digital assets, which directly impacts its transaction volume and overall revenue. The company also navigates an uncertain global regulatory environment that could lead to litigation, investigations, or licensing hurdles. Cybersecurity remains a critical concern, as any potential loss of customer assets or private keys could damage trust and cause significant financial losses.

Visa appears more attractive on an earnings basis, while Coinbase trades at a significantly lower price relative to its sales over the past 12 months.

When comparing Coinbase and Visa, investors should consider a few key factors. Let's have a look at them and see what that tells us about each stock.

Firstly, we should consider revenue growth. Since 2023, Coinbase has delivered higher average year-over-year revenue growth, with a rate of 44%; Visa's revenue growth has averaged 12%. However, in recent quarters, Coinbase's growth has fallen off substantially. Indeed, in its latest quarter, the company reported a 35% revenue decline. Visa, by contrast, reported 12% revenue growth. That gives Visa the edge in this category.

Another factor to consider is each company's profitability. Again, Visa can claim an advantage. Its 66.5% operating margin demonstrates the company's excellent profitability. Coinbase, on the other hand, has seen its operating margin steadily decline in recent quarters, and it currently sits at -32.6%.

Lastly, there's valuation. Here, we must use price-to-sales (P/S) due to Coinbase's recent net losses. Coinbase currently has a P/S ratio of 5.6x, while Visa has a P/S multiple of 17.0x. Therefore, Coinbase stock is more affordable than Visa.

To sum up, both Coinbase and Visa are financial stocks. However, they differ in several key respects. Visa can point to more stable revenue growth and very healthy profit margins. Meanwhile, it could be argued that Coinbase stock is more affordable -- at least on a P/S basis. Yet, across the most important fundamentals, Visa still comes out ahead in this head-to-head matchup.
