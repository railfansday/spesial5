Huskeys Taps Former HackerOne CCO Eyal Kaldes to Run Its Customer Organization

Huskeys has a new executive in charge of making sure its customers succeed.

The Network Edge Security Management (NESM) company announced in a press release that it has hired Eyal Kaldes as Chief Customer Officer, putting him in charge of its customer strategy and the entire post-sale organization. Kaldes arrives with more than 20 years of customer success and post-sales leadership across enterprise security, networking and AI, most recently as Chief Customer Officer at HackerOne.

His brief is simple to state and big in scope: help enterprise customers get measurable value out of the Huskeys platform, from the day they first deploy it to the point where they are running NESM at scale.

At HackerOne, Kaldes ran a post-sale operation with an unusually wide footprint. Customer success, professional services, technical support, triage and vulnerability validation, onboarding and community all sat inside his organization.

That is a serious pedigree. HackerOne is a global cybersecurity leader and a pioneer of crowdsourced security, connecting organizations with ethical hackers through bug bounty programs so they can find vulnerabilities. The company has played a major role in shaping how enterprises approach vulnerability discovery and security testing.

Earlier in his career, Kaldes led client services at PerimeterX and served as GM and Vice President of Client Services at Aerohive Networks, supporting more than 30,000 customers through a global network of more than 500 resellers and distributors and working alongside strategic partners such as Dell.

He also holds a seat on the Customer Success Advisory Board of CloudShare.

At Huskeys, Kaldes becomes the most senior Israeli executive on the team, according to the company. Along with his leadership experience, he brings a strong connection to the Israeli cybersecurity ecosystem.

Itai Gafni, CEO and Co-Founder of Huskeys, tied the hire directly to what the company has learned by working closely with its customers.

"Working side by side with our customers, we saw that the edge had become the most critical and least managed layer of the enterprise, and that organizations need NESM now," Gafni said.

He went on to explain why Kaldes was the pick. "Our job is to make sure every step of our customers' journey delivers real, measurable protection. Eyal has built customer organizations for some of the world's largest enterprises and scaled them through rapid growth. He brings a rare combination of cybersecurity depth, operational leadership and a genuine obsession with customer outcomes, and he is exactly the leader we need to keep every Huskeys customer ahead of what's coming at the edge."

Huskeys is building an NESM platform that helps organizations understand, manage and secure network edge environments, which keep growing more complex in the agentic AI era. The platform does not ask companies to rip out the tools they already rely on. Instead, it connects and orchestrates existing CDNs, WAFs, cloud security and other network edge technologies through a single unified agentic layer.

What customers get is continuous assessment, plus the ability to dynamically adapt policies, reduce exposure and protect business-critical applications at scale.

A platform with that kind of reach puts a premium on the team that helps customers roll it out and grow with it. The appointment reflects Huskeys' focus on building a customer organization that can scale alongside the business and keep pace with evolving market needs.

Kaldes sees a clear shift in who, and what, is driving traffic at the edge.

"AI agents now drive a growing share of edge traffic, and security teams are being asked to protect it faster and with less visibility," he said. "The enterprises I've worked with don't just want a platform. They want a partner who gets them to outcomes quickly. My focus is building a customer organization at Huskeys that delivers that from day one and scales as the company grows."

In practice, his work will center on helping customers reach meaningful outcomes while setting up the processes and relationships that support long-term partnerships. For Kaldes personally, the role is a chance to use his experience across several stages of the customer lifecycle and to help shape what comes next for Huskeys in the world of AI.

The bottom line for Huskeys customers: the person now running their experience has spent more than two decades doing exactly that, at enterprise scale.
