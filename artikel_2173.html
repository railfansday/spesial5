Barnsley vs Sheffield Wednesday predictions lodged as the Tykes ‘saddening’ decline continues

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Barnsley come into Friday’s Yorkshire derby against Sheffield Wednesday in dire straits. The Tykes sit 23rd in the League One table with just nine points to their name after seven defeats in their last nine matches in the third tier.

Daniel Stendel‘s side had found some respite in domestic competition with wins over Aldershot Town and Oldham Athletic in the FA Cup and EFL Trophy respectively. However, their league woes continued with a 2-1 defeat away at fellow strugglers Wigan Athletic last Saturday.

As for Sheffield Wednesday, they claimed a valuable three points in Dave Challinor’s first League One game last weekend. Billy Mitchell, Davis Keillor-Dunn and Di’Shon Bernard scored on the way to an important 3-0 win over Plymouth Argyle, marking the Owls’ first league victory in four matches.

SWFC have to get back on track on the road though, having lost back-to-back games against Notts County and Doncaster Rovers by 2-0 scorelines as Henrik Pedersen‘s tenure came to an end. Wednesday now sit 5th, six points away from the automatic promotion spots.

“Barnsley and Sheffield Wednesday are in drastically contrasting positions. The Tykes are lingering in the League One relegation zone with continued discontent towards the ownership, while SWFC are hoping to challenge for promotion as they rebuild under ambitious leadership.

“Daniel Stendel marked an exciting summer appointment at Oakwell but he has been completely hung out to dry. The decline in quality of that squad is just saddening, and with 24 goals conceded this season, I have little faith that their defence can curtail the visitors’ quality this Friday.

“SWFC are finding their feet under Dave Challinor, but the 3-0 win over Plymouth Argyle marked an exciting result in his first league game. Davis Keillor-Dunn is in fine form and Di’Shon Bernard is a true Championship quality centre-back, and a big asset at this level.

“Perhaps the occasion can motivate Stendel’s young side and drive them to a surprise result. However, you can’t help but feel the visitors win in pretty comfortable fashion at Oakwell.”

“Barnsley are in serious danger of being relegated this season. Their form has been abysmal at home and with no clean sheets at Oakwell this term, they will be up against it on Friday.

“It’s a great opportunity for the Tykes to incite revenge on the Owls who beat them in the play-off final three years ago. However, the difference in squad talent is a lot greater this time around.

“Sheffield Wednesday have been terrific on the road and with an average of 1.67 points a game, promotion is a real possibility. However, two losses away from home in a row should give a glimmer of hope to Stendel’s side.

“I can’t see the hosts winning this one, though, and Challinor will be eager to continue his winning start as boss. I think SWFC will be far too strong on the night.”
