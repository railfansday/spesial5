Moto Buds Ultra 27 and Watch Fit 27 help complete the company’s new premium ecosystem

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The duo of Motorola accessories will see a North American release in the coming months, featuring Motorola Qira integration.

If you’re planning on upgrading to the extremely ultra-level Motorola Signature 27 flagship phone this year, I have good news for you: you’ll have plenty of time to save up for Motorola’s accompanying accessories after you buy the $1,600 phone. Motorola isn’t announcing a specific release date or price for either the Moto Buds Ultra 27 or Moto Watch Fit 27, but here’s what you can look forward to when they eventually come out.

Starting with the Moto Buds Ultra 27, which Motorola calls its “most feature-packed pair of earbuds yet.” These sport Bose-branded ANC and SoundDesign tuning, plus six high-SNR microphones with two dedicated Voice Pickup Units in what’s now become a conventional wireless earbud design with tips. Motorola says it created the microphones not just for great sound quality, but also for content creation, which means they’re clearly confident in the quality of these mics.

The buds deliver around 8 hours of continuous playback on a single charge, and the included case adds another 37 hours of use. They’ve also got built-in Motorola Qira support, plus AI Live Translate, head gestures, dual connection and audio share (so you can pair them with two devices, or pair two sets of buds with one device), plus a wear detection auto-pause feature.

They also sport IP57 protection, support Google’s Find Hub, and feature 11mm dynamic drivers for crisp audio. Moto Buds Ultra 27 will debut sometime in the near future in two colorways: Pantone Reflecting Pond (dark blue), and Pantone Temple Gray.

The Moto Watch Fit 27 is the antithesis of the recently launched Motorola Watch Ultra, focusing more on daily health than anything else. That means nearly two weeks of battery life on a single charge (instead of a day or two as Wear OS watches have), plus a Polar-powered health engine that’s designed to get your health metrics right the first time.

The square design features a rotating crown and a customizable physical button below the crown, IP68 and 5ATM protection, Bluetooth connection, built-in Motorola Qira support, all in an aluminum frame. A 1.85-inch gOLED display sports ultra-thin 2mm bezels and Gorilla Glass 3 on top. The display supports always-on visibility, and the built-in Qira assistant gives you hands-free command of the watch’s abilities.

As for health metrics, this one supports SPO2 monitoring, stress insights, heart rate powered by Polar, menstrual cycle tracking, auto activity detection, step and distance counter, over 100 tracked workouts, and dual-frequency GPS for accurate tracking. The Polar engine also supports holistic tracking, including sleep, calories, inactivity, and active workouts. Moto Watch Fit 27 launches sometime in the future in three colorways: Pantone Stratified Sea, Pantone Pigeon, and Pantone Ballerina.
