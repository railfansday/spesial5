19 Of This Year's Best Xbox Games Now Have Free Trials For All Players

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Star Wars: Galactic Racer being the latest one

It's been an amazing year for new releases on Xbox, and something you might not be aware of is that many of those games also come with free trials, allowing you to see whether you're willing to commit to a full purchase.

We've highlighted some of these before, but there are new ones as well - the incredible Star Wars: Galactic Racer has literally just added a free trial this week. Most of these trials include the full game but are limited to a couple of hours (sometimes more) before they lock you out, but that's plenty of time to discover whether you're a fan or not!

There are some exceptions where trials are provided in the form of demos or a "Starter Pack", so we'll be sure to mention those in the list below. We've gone through the top 50 Xbox games of 2026 based on Metacritic's rankings, so anything you see below is (according to them) one of the best 50 Xbox releases of the year.

For anything that's listed here as a "free trial", you'll want to go to the Microsoft Store app on your Xbox console and locate the game in question, where a "free trial" button should appear next to the ability to buy it.

All the other demos and separate things like a "Starter Pack" are standalone items on the Xbox Store, so if you search for "007 First Light - Free Starter Pack", for example, that's where you'll find the free trial for that game.

Many of these titles are also available in the Xbox Black Friday Sale right now, so if you're not too busy with GTA 6 and fancy trying some of these out, you might just be able to decide whether it's worth buying the full versions at a discount.

We wish Xbox did a better job at making it clear when new trials have arrived on the Xbox Store, so hopefully that's something that might be addressed in 2027. For now, hopefully this list proves useful for the Pure Xbox community!
