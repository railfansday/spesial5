PS5 Dynamic Pricing Impacts New PS Store Black Friday Offers, Roughly $5 Variations in Prices

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Three first-party PS5 games seemingly affected

It seems that the dynamic pricing issues Sony is currently experimenting with on the PS Store is impacting what kind of discounts PS5 owners are receiving in today's new PS Store Black Friday sale.

With evidence from the Cheap Ass Gamer social media account, there are reports first-party PS5 titles Death Stranding 2, Astro Bot, and the Complete Edition of Horizon Forbidden West are all affected.

@dskatter Transparency is a different issue and agree it's important. Otherwise, price differentiation is a good thing for consumers. For example, an anime game might sell for $70 in Japan because demand is particularly strong there, but $50 in the U.S. because consumers are less willing to pay the higher price. The publisher can capture more revenue in Japan while making the game more affordable to American consumers. The benefit is that one universal price might leave money on the table in Japan while pricing out potential customers in America. Differential pricing allows the company to serve both markets rather than treating their different demand conditions as identical. P.S. Do you find it immoral when your grandparent gets a movie discount because of their age even though you are seeing the same movie? Or a veteran discount?
