Tutoring pays by the hour, and the rate depends on what you teach, so price each subject and grade level you offer by what tutors near you charge for it. Find your first students through parents, teachers, and school counselors, and use tutoring sites, which keep a cut, to fill the hours referrals don’t.

Private tutoring takes no teaching license, just a subject you know well, and you set your own hours, so it fits around a day job or classes.

What you can charge depends on what you teach. Wiingy, a tutoring site, tracked nearly 14,000 tutor listings for its 2026 price index and found SAT math averaging about $60 an hour, against about $45 for Spanish. Rates for the same subject also vary widely from city to city. Charge one rate for everything, and you either undercharge for test prep or price yourself out of everyday homework help.

List each subject and level you could teach, such as SAT math, middle school algebra, and early reading. Search a tutoring site like Wyzant for each one the way a parent near you would. Its results show every tutor’s hourly rate, and the middle of those rates is a fair starting point for yours.

Find your first students locally: tell parents you know, ask teachers and school counselors to pass your name along, and post in your town’s parent groups. Wyzant also brings students, but it keeps 25% of your posted rate on the ones it finds you. A retired engineer who charges $60 an hour for SAT math and fills six hours a week through two parents’ referrals is paid the full $360 a week. The same six hours booked through Wyzant would pay him $270, so save tutoring sites for the hours referrals leave open.

Run it as a small business. Put your cancellation and payment terms in writing, such as 24 hours’ notice to cancel, and keep a background check ready for families who ask. Deposit tutoring income in a separate bank account and set part of each payment aside for taxes. Once your self-employment profit reaches $400 in a year, the IRS charges self-employment tax of 15.3% for Social Security and Medicare, on top of income tax.

A full schedule is the signal to raise the rate you quote new families, while families you already work with keep the rate they started with.
