Add Square Savings to set aside a share of every card sale for taxes. If clients mostly pay by bank transfer, pick an account that sets aside part of every deposit.

Sutton Bank provides Square Checking, a business account you open and use in Square’s apps. Without it, a free payout of your sales to an outside bank takes until the next business day, while an instant one costs 1.95%, which works out to $19.50 on a $1,000 day of sales.

Sole proprietors who take most of their payments through Square should open it, since it costs $0 a month and usually makes each card sale spendable within minutes. Square’s automatic set-aside works only on card sales, so freelancers paid mostly by bank transfer do better with Novo Business Checking, which can route a share of every deposit to taxes for no monthly fee.
