When I read a book, there is an implied contract between the author and myself. I believe that the author is writing something with intention: carefully expressing some thought or feeling, trying to broadcast some meaning... or at least trying to make me laugh. When you use AI to "help," it becomes a deception. You're hoping the reader won't notice that you didn't actually write that sentence, that paragraph, that chapter. Maybe a human tweaked it, but maybe a human didn't even read it. The concern is that this exchange now just becomes entertainment, devoid of intent. As long as the output appears meaningful, it may be good enough... but it becomes a sad, hollow affair. I'm deeply afraid for the future of expression.
