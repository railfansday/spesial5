J.Crew’s new Miles Davis capsule honors the jazz icon’s preppy style

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Miles Davis was arguably as famous for his fashion as he was for his music—American Ivy suits, African dashikis, and textured Issey Miyake shirts were all part of his wardrobe.

Now, 100 years after the artist’s birth, J.Crew has released a limited-edition capsule collection inspired by his legendary style and the American heritage look that is at J.Crew’s core. Launched November 20, the collection includes a T-shirt, an Oxford shirt, and a baseball cap.

“From his tailored suits and Ivy League-inspired wardrobe to the way he mixed classic pieces with a more individual, unconventional sensibility, Miles helped shape a look that continues to influence today,” says Golnar Khosrowshahi, founder and CEO of music rights company Reservoir Media. (Reservoir acquired 90% of Davis’s music publishing catalog last year, alongside shared control of his name, image, and likeness).

The lasting style is apparent in the capsule’s three objects. First, there’s a co-branded Oxford shirt, technically a J.Crew 1983, that is an homage to the mint-green button-down Davis wore on the cover of his 1958 album Milestones. To help the shirt hang just like the one on the album, J.Crew opted for a “heritage fit” that features a slightly longer collar and substantial weight from the 100% woven cotton, resulting in a relaxed take on the classic.

Then there’s a vintage-wash cotton shirt emblazoned with an illustration of Davis. The reference image dates back to 1970, photographed by Glen Craig at a performance at the Fillmore East in New York City.

Lastly, a classic black baseball cap is adorned with Davis’s signature, embroidered in vibrant blue thread against the rugged-wash canvas.

The collection follows a series of limited-edition drops from J.Crew, which has partnered with the New York Fire Department and The New Yorker as part of the retailer’s broader post-bankruptcy strategy to be at the “center of culture.”

“Collaborations create a meaningful entry point for people who may know Miles Davis well and are discovering J.Crew through the collection, while giving longtime J.Crew customers a new perspective on familiar pieces,” a J.Crew spokesperson tells Fast Company. “They help us keep our classics in the cultural conversation.”

It’s not surprising that the clothing company would want to feature the most well-known jazz musician of the 20th century in its product line. But for Khosrowshahi, landing on J.Crew as a partner for Davis’s centennial celebration was a way of highlighting Davis’s legacy beyond music, focusing on fashion instead.

“The goal isn’t to put an artist’s name on as many products as possible; it’s to find the right collaborators and create experiences that reflect the artist’s identity, values, and influence,” Khosrowshahi says. “This J.Crew collaboration is one example of how we’re bringing that broader vision to life.”

The collection also brings Davis into the smaller details. The inside labels pair the J.Crew logo with a red silhouette of Davis playing the trumpet in place of the classic J.Crew Oarsman. The items’ tags also stand out for featuring Davis’s signature.

The capsule is available online and at select J.Crew stores, including Bond Street, Rockefeller Center, and Fifth Avenue in New York City; Century City in Los Angeles; Newbury Street in Boston; North Michigan Avenue in Chicago; and Georgetown in Washington, D.C.

“Preserving Miles’ legacy doesn’t mean keeping it frozen in time,” a spokesperson from the Miles Davis Estate says. “Miles was always looking ahead, always experimenting, and always pushing boundaries. Whether through music, fashion, visual art, or other creative collaborations, we want people to recognize just how far his influence extends.”
