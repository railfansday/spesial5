Frosinone halt Kvernadze’s exit in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Roma are interested in Frosinone’s Giorgi Kvernadze.

The Georgian left winger has made a strong impact in Serie A: 3 goals and 5 assists in 11 games. Gian Piero Gasperini is looking for a winger capable of adding pace, physicality, and goals.

The 2003-born player is surprising experts at the start of the season and has attracted the attention of several clubs.

Meanwhile, the Roma management is also looking elsewhere.

For the January transfer window, the most likely candidates are Endrick and Jamie Gittens.

The Real Madrid Brazilian talent seems to be more attractive than the Chelsea winger, who has struggled with various physical issues in recent years.

Kvernadze is a player who has ended up on the Giallorossi management’s shortlist. However, as La Gazzetta dello Sport reports, Frosinone has no intention of letting go of the 2003-born player in January.

Both Gasperini and D’Amico are interested in the Georgian winger, so any discussions will be postponed until the summer transfer window. In January, Roma will consider other players, with Endrick and Gittens in the running.
