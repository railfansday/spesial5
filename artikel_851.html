Mauricio Pochettino calls for Manchester United to show no fear against Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

New head coach’s first game is at Anfield on Sunday

‘We are going there with the idea to win and be brave’

Mauricio Pochettino has called on Manchester United to have “no fear” when they travel to Anfield on Sunday for his first game. The Argentinian does not want his players to take a backward step as he targets a fast start.

United are 18th in the Premier League and Liverpool fourth but the head coach will not accept any self-doubt. Pochettino is known for being attack-minded and is keen to instil those ideals, but will have had only three days with the players since replacing Michael Carrick.

“We are going to Anfield with the idea to win and be brave and to be Manchester United,” Pochettino told the club’s website. “The history talks for us, and what we need to expect. We need to be sure that we are brave and we are protagonists and go there with no fears and challenge a very good team like Liverpool.”

Pochettino arrived in Manchester on Wednesday after an overnight flight, having taken charge of his final USA match against Haiti. He toured the training ground and held meetings with senior staff and players not on international duty.

Members of Pochettino’s staff were in place earlier in the week as they seek to revitalise a team with one league win. Pochettino is waiting for United to ratify the appointment of Sílvia Tuyà, a strength and conditioning coach who worked with him with the USA. She travelled with him by private jet to Manchester but was not named among the staff later in the day.

“We need to go little by little, step by step,” Pochettino said. “We cannot go too fast but also we need to, at the same time, attack the priorities to build something.”

He said it was important he and his staff adapt to the culture of the club. “And then, with this sense of knowing the history of the club, try to create all together, because we are all different, a culture, a philosophy that can respect that identity, the history of the club. But at the same time, performing. If we don’t perform, the responsibility is mine.”

Pochettino has coveted the United job and is a keen admirer of Sir Alex Ferguson, whom he describes as “the best coach in history”. Since the Scot’s retirement in 2013, United have failed to match his success but Pochettino said he was looking at “the potential of the club”. The weight of past achievements has hindered individuals over the past 13 years.

“The players need to express their talent,” Pochettino said. “We need to create the environment and the platform for the players to perform. That is the most important thing and that is our priority now: it’s to work with them and try to take the pressure.

“Pressure needs to bring energy, responsibility needs to bring energy. Because if not, it’s too heavy to put all that pressure and responsibility on your back. When you connect with the game, you connect through your talent when you feel freedom, when you feel happy, when you want to play for your teammates, when you want to play for your family, when you want to play for your club, your fans.”

He sounded an upbeat note, saying: “With the power of this club and the fans supporting us, I think we will get in the place that we deserve to be.”

United’s total borrowings have risen to £784.5m, with long-term debt up by almost £100m over the past year. Figures published for the three months to 30 September show long-term borrowings of £577.4m, compared with £481.2m 12 months earlier. United’s cash reserves fell from £80.5m to £55.5m during the same period despite an increase in revenue.

The club reported a quarterly net loss of £18.1m, compared with £6.6m ​in ​the same period a year earlier. Revenue rose by 4% to £146m.
