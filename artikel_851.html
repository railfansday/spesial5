Victor Munoz is in a race against time to be fit for Sunday’s Merseyside derby as Liverpool could be without three midfielders or wingers.

Cody Gakpo will definitely miss the trip to Everton while Ryan Gravenberch is unlikely to be available.

Summer signing Munoz went off in Wednesday’s 3-0 win over Club Brugge with a knee problem he sustained in a collision and while Liverpool are confident there is no major problem, they are waiting to see if he can play at Hill Dickinson Stadium.

“With Victor, I think the good news is after assessing him, there is no [structural damage] in the knee but he has a big knock,” said head coach Andoni Iraola. “He hasn’t trained [on Friday] but he wants to try tomorrow so we will see. I cannot tell you right now.

Gakpo was injured on international duty with the Netherlands and has missed Liverpool’s last two games and Iraola suggested he could be a doubt for next Saturday’s game against Chelsea.

“Cody is definitely not [in contention for Everton],” Iraola added. “He has a muscle injury so we have to wait a little bit until the process goes on and it heals.

“This kind of injury is normally about two or three weeks. He did it in the last game against Germany so normally so normally it is this timeline. Normally, I don’t like to say timelines with injury because every injury is different and some of them go quicker than others. Normally, this isn’t a big injury but there is something there and you have to give it the time to heal.”

Midfielder Gravenberch has an issue with his heel and Iraola does not expect him to face Everton.

“I think it is going to be difficult, I cannot say no but this kind of injuries but he has the pain and doesn’t go,” he added.
