Kansas City Current stun Washington Spirit to win NWSL Championship – as it happend

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

⚽️ Current win first NWSL Championship in club history ⚽️ Spirit lose third consecutive Championship ⚽️ Sign up for our free newsletter | Instagram

Quite a night, with a very fraught first forty minutes making way for an entertaining back-half of the final. DiBernardo’s brilliance a worthy opening salvo, while Debinha was thusly rewarded for legging out a break with Chawinga for an invaluable insurance goal.

That’s a wrap for me – thanks for following along!

It took five years, 90 minutes and a goal for the ages, but the Kansas City Current have their first-ever NWSL championship.

Riding a wave of momentum created by a 54th-minute Olimpico from midfielder Vanessa DiBernardo, and capping it off with an all-important breakaway winner from Debinha, the Current silenced the partisan, sellout crowd at the heavily favored Washington Spirit’s home ground with a 2-1 victory on Saturday.

The Spirit, heartbroken at the final whistle, have now lost three consecutive NWSL Championships.

Well then! Was hoping that DiBernardo’s trip to the punditry desk would include her thoughts on SCORING AN OLIMPICO IN A TITLE GAME, since the first interview failed to reference her goal. But then, she’s cut off mid-unrelated answer and told that the broadcast is over, and they’ll get somebody else after they change stations.

What a way to cap what’s been a very disjointed and distracted broadcast on CBS.

There we go –confetti of gold and silver with smoke and fireworks to flank. Blast that cover version of We Are The Champions, MC!

“It took literally every single person, everyone in teal. It probably took those years to build that grit,” LaBonta says in her captain’s remarks. “It took us years to get here, I’m so glad we got that troph– where’s the commissioner?”

For all this time on stage, Berman still hasn’t presented the championship trophy.

NWSL commissioner Jessica Berman announces DiBernardo as the championship MVP. LaBonta crowds the mic to redefine the acronym: “Mom-V-P!”

Baffling scenes, as the owners are named and medaled as part of the pre-trophy presentation. I can’t think of another time where they’ve been folded into the players and coaches.

Fun to see DiBernardo and Sharples play such big parts in a title-winning team. The pair were crucial during the last great era for the Chicago Red Stars, pre-rebrand, and their departures coincided with the Stars’ decay into their current and uncompetitive state.

Lots of work for Vlatko Andonovski to do in the Windy City. Wonder if he gets a ring for his work in building this KC side, having only left in September.

The officials are roundly booed by the Audi Field faithful. A thankless job on a good night, but supporters took umbrage with the lack of caution associated with the hard challenge on Rodman to open the game.

Bit harsh from my point of view, especially since Washington were as prone to those committing hard challenges as KC.

Really impressive work by the Current to stymy the Spirit on their own turf. They put their numerical advantage in midfield to work, both with physicality and being able to dictate how this game flowed.

While Andi Sullivan is excellent, she struggled to overcome the collective control of Vanessa DiBernardo and Lo’eau LaBonta.

Final figures from this one, with Washington’s stats listed first as home team:

Shots: 18-15 Shots on target: 3-4 xG: 0.77-2.32 Possession: 60%-40% ’Big’ chances: 0-3 Fouls committed: 18-17

Little evidence that the Spirit did enough to go (stay) home as champions.

All smiles on the other half of the field. DiBernardo especially overjoyed, having scored a rare Olimpico to open the scoring.

“We knew it was going to be a battle,” she tells the broadcast after. “We just had to be patient and finish off the game.”

“It’s really special to be a part of this. I felt like we were so close last year, and we stuck together as a group. I feel like we really deserve this. I’m grateful.”

Rodman is shown on the broadcast with head in hands, sobbing as her team falls in another championship game. Sadly, too often the case for the Spirit, who have won just once in six trips to the NWSL Championship.

Runners-up in 2024. And 2025. And, now, 2026.

That’s it! A madcap stoppage time salvages what had been a grotesque first hour to this final in terms of the soccer. DiBernardo, Debinha, and Lorena lead the way to make the Current a title-winner!

90+12 min: Custom is for a minute to be added to stoppage time for every goal scored, but still we play on with no end in sight. Washington are fighting to force this game to endure.

90+11 min: Lorena punches the ensuing corner and then claims the second effort. And yet, the game continues!

90+10 min: KC can’t believe it’s still an open contest. Now another Spirit corner kick…

Robinson on the ground after getting contact in the bread basket. A necessary easing of the sting for KC to weather this.

90+9 min The Spirit earned a quick free kick, and a Rudd looper found Rodman fading at the far post for a floating header to the far post. Beautiful stuff. Let’s see if it saves the game!

ALREADY! Rodman heads home a free kick, and there’s a minute or two left!

90+7 min: The Spirit tried their best to find a second look after Rudd clattered her attempt off the wall, and committed every body they could to the cause. A turnover sprung a break with Debinha and Chawinga alone on goal, and Chawinga needed to do little more than be a decoy.

Kansas City is a city of champions once again!

90+7 min: The Spirit dawdle with the recirculation. Uh oh…

90+6 min: …which Rudd takes and sends into the wall.

90+5 min: Rodman gets the ball at the top of the box, but has it poked away from her feet before she can shoot. Washington work upfield again, and LaBonta is called for a foul on Bolt at the left-center edge of the box…

90+4 min: The Current look genuinely shocked to have multiple passes in the opponent’s half, and the last of them clatter off of one of their knees for an unforced turnover. Back to panic stations!

90+2 min: Another Spirit free-kick, another easy claim for Lorena. Brazil will be thrilled by her big game mettle on the doorstep of next summer’s World Cup on home soil.

90+1 min: The Spirit have a free kick at the edge of the final third and head the ensuing effort about, but it falls into Lorena’s embrace before any touch is directed towards her goal.

89 min: Four turnovers in the past sixty seconds. Throw your tactical intentions out the window, it’s now or never.

88 min: Another KC sub, this time a Richmond, VA native entering close to home. Cooper exits having avoided a second yellow, and Elizabeth Ball, a longtime servant of the club, will see things out for the Current.

87 min: Rodman is up and alright. So is Lorena. Sideline reporter Hailey Sutton has characterized the state of the sideline as “emotional.”

85 min: Rodman in the middle of another tough challenge. This time, a bit more routine in this sport as she rose for a header but clattered into Lorena, who was trying a punch and grazed Rodman’s temple with her shoulder.

“Ref you suck” is among the many chants from the Spirit faithful, and Washington’s coaches are absolutely incensed. The more replays you see, however, it really does look like a pretty cut and dry 50/50 with goalkeeper and attacker both going for the ball. I’m not sure where a foul could be called on Lorena, in truth.

84 min: Desperation has long set into the Spirit in the final third. Of their 10 shots thus far, only three have come from inside the Current box.

The KC midfield and center-backs have been exceptional throughout.

83 min: A second straight NWSL Championship game where Adrian Gonzalez is bound to face questions about not removing injured players. Rodman, of course. Last year, his indecision about removing Hal Hershfelt as she struggled to continue after a leg injury may have cost his team the game, as Rose Lavelle scored the decisive goal while she tried to hobble through it.

81 min: Finally some changes to KC, including the could-be matchwinner DiBernardo making way. She’s joined by Bethune en route to the bench. Rocky Rodriguez and modern Brazilian great Debinha check in for the final nine minutes or so.

81 min: Another card, this time on Tara Rudd for an incidental handball. Well-spotted, to be fair.

And yet, the crowd at Audi Field are hardly up for such spots. The cameras cut to the Spirit seats outside their suite, and the team could hardly look more tense.

78 min: And so this match has settled into what’s bound to be its final state: Washington setting up in the final third and straining to find avenues into the box, and KC breaking quickly after any turnover to release some pressure.

76 min: That Rudd effort was Washington’s first shot on target, and it had jolted the Rowdy Audi faithful back into full voice.

Still no changes made by Chris Armas, coaching in his first playoff final in any league.

75 min: NEARLY! Tara Rudd gets on the end of a corner kick and heads it to the far post, but Lorena quickly dives to her right to stifle the ball and prevent any second chance.

74 min: Another sub window for the Spirit, with Luvanga coming off and Rose Kouassi taking her place. A frustrating end to the season for Luvanga, who was unstoppable after debuting for Washington in late September.

Kouassi’s some alternative to tap from the bench, a two-time honoree on the Division 1 Féminine Team of the Season.
