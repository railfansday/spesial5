How to follow the Premier League on the BBC this weekend

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

As another busy midweek of European football draws to a close, Premier League clubs now turn their attention to the final week of domestic fixtures before the November international break.

Manchester City have lost their footing at the top of the table to Arsenal, while Tottenham will hope to build on their upturn in fortunes that have propelled them into the top half of the league.

Throughout the 2026-27 season, you can watch highlights from every game on Match of the Day, get live updates in our text commentaries on the BBC Sport website and app and keep across all the goals as they go in on Final Score.

You can get live score updates on your phone lock screen simply by following your favourite teams.

And you can listen to match coverage wherever you are in the UK via BBC Radio 5 Live and the BBC Sounds app.

Digital highlights will be available post-match on the BBC Sport website and app in the UK.
