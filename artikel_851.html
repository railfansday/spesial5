Romario demands Ancelotti sacking as war of words continues

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Few would have predicted that Carlo Ancelotti would be facing the wrath of a Brazil legend just 18 months into his tenure as head coach of the national team.

But Romario, Brazil's fourth-highest scorer behind only Neymar, Pele and Ronaldo, has publicly demanded legendary manager Ancelotti be relieved of his duties twice in the past week.

It all started after Brazil suffered a 3-2 friendly defeat by Japan on Friday thanks to a controversial stoppage-time winner from Tatsuhiro Sakamoto.

Ancelotti received a red card for dissent in the aftermath - the first of his international management career, as Barcelona forward Raphinha also saw red.

And after Brazil's friendly defeat, Romario took to social media to air his frustrations about the former Real Madrid boss.

"Ancelotti, enough is enough! Pack your bags and leave!" he wrote on Instagram.

"Nobody questions your history. You are one of the greatest coaches world football has ever seen, but that was at club level."

Brazil were eliminated by Norway in the last 16 at the 2026 World Cup, meaning they failed to qualify for the quarter-finals for the first time since 1990.

And Romario alluded to this too, stating the country "can't take the risk of throwing another World Cup in the trash - the time to act is now!"

The Brazil boss has been in the game long enough to know how to deal with all sorts of opinions.

Ancelotti has won the Champions League a record five times as a head coach and won the league title in all of Europe's top five divisions, along with multiple domestic cups.

But his record with Brazil is far from perfect. Across 21 matches, they have recorded 12 wins, four draws and five defeats.

However, while Ancelotti acknowledges the need for improvement, he emphasised the fact that it is still "a new project".

Speaking before Brazil's friendly with Singapore on Tuesday the Italian said: "In a democratic country with freedom of speech, everyone is allowed to express their opinion.

"It's not up to me to say whether the things he says are correct, wrong or just stupid. The truth is that Romario talks a lot. A lot. It's his right."

Despite the criticism he has faced, Ancelotti feels Brazil are on the right track - and who is to tell him otherwise when you look at his track record?

"I really like what we're doing," he said.

You would have thought that would have been enough to let it lie for the time being.

But before Brazil had even kicked a ball in their next friendly against Singapore, Romario was speaking out again.

The former striker, who is the ninth-highest scorer in football history with 784 goals, doubled down on his calls for Ancelotti to be sacked.

"I talk a lot because when it comes to the national team, I have size, weight and power of speech," Romario, 60, said.

"I do not speak to gain followers, I do not speak for people to like me. I speak my mind.

"I have worn this shirt for many years and I am pretty sure I was one of the ones that helped the 'little yellow' be respected all over the world."

He said he respected Ancelotti but added he hadn't "added anything new" to the national side.

"I will say it again: resign! We will find another coach who will do a better job than you.

"And I'm saying this to your face, not through a message, including to the president of the [Brazilian Football Confederation], who is long overdue to fire you."

Brazil faced Singapore in their final match of 2026 on Tuesday and won 7-0.

Real Madrid forward Vinicius Junior, who has come under scrutiny of his own so far this season, scored a hat-trick within the opening 21 minutes while Bournemouth winger Rayan and Chelsea's Estevao were also on the scoresheet.

That result that may bring a pause to proceedings in this war of words for now.

However, if this past seven days has proved anything, it is that this saga is likely far from over.
