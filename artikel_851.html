Merson: My worry is that Liverpool thought Alonso wasn't good enough

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Chelsea have suffered back-to-back defeats in the Premier League and are slipping towards the middle of the table; the Blues have won just once in five games in all competitions; watch Nottingham Forest vs Chelsea live on Sky Sports this Friday, kick-off 8pm

Football Expert & Columnist @PaulMerse

In his latest Sky Sports column, Paul Merson has raised doubts about whether Xabi Alonso is good enough as Chelsea manager - after another disappointing defeat in the Premier League.

The Blues were beaten 4-1 by arch-rivals Leeds at Stamford Bridge on Saturday, with the Blues now on a run of just one win in their last five matches in all competitions.

Chelsea have now slipped down to eighth place - and sit five points behind the top four. They do sit two points behind fifth, but Brentford could go ahead of the Blues if they win on Monday Night Football against Everton.

Merson believes Liverpool may have snubbed Alonso for the head coach role - eventually taken by Andoni Iraola - due to concerns about his pedigree.

There was no vacancy at Liverpool by the time Alonso took the Chelsea role on May 17, with Liverpool not sacking Arne Slot until June 1.

Read below for the Magic Man's thoughts...

My biggest worry is that Liverpool could have had Xabi Alonso and they chose not to. Obviously, Liverpool didn't think he was good enough.

Alonso took the Chelsea job and Liverpool sacked Arne Slot two weeks later. You can't tell me that Liverpool couldn't have spoken to Alonso and say: 'Don't take the Chelsea job, we're going to take you'.

He was out of a job as well, Liverpool could have had him and he'd have gone there all day long. So why didn't they take him? Did they know something that we're seeing now?

It just always sits in my mind. That was my worry - and now it's playing out.

If this was Liam Rosenior in charge at Chelsea and getting these results, he'd be in major trouble. One hundred percent, he'd be in trouble. And remember, Rosenior didn't have Morgan Rogers in his Chelsea team, bought for £116m.

But it's because of the unbelievable job he did at Bayer Leverkusen that everybody is thinking: it will be alright.

Tottenham and Leeds at home are Chelsea's two biggest games of the season for the fans - and they've lost them both. But while they're big for the fans, I'm not sure the players think that way. Not at all.

They probably think their biggest games are Arsenal, Manchester United and Manchester City.

The Leeds game changed when Moises Caicedo came off. For all the money they've spent… take Caicedo and Joao Pedro out of that team and Chelsea aren't good enough. They're the two players you cannot afford to miss.

They're not dominating football matches. The other major problem is defending. The goals they give away, the way it happens - it's mind-blowing. It's poor defending. Inexperienced defending.

The first goal against Leeds, Josh Acheampong gets stuck under the ball. I know he's a young kid but he still can't make that mistake. Then the ball gets to Noah Okafor, and Levi Colwill just jockeys to the side of him. He has to do something to stop that shot happening. He just dangles his leg out.

It's not be-all-and-end-all defending. You look at players like Dan Burn and Lewis Dunk for Newcastle and Brighton. They would dive in front of the ball and let the ball be smashed against them.

Sol Campbell, Tony Adams, Rio Ferdinand and Nemanja Vidic all had that mentality. Their job was to make sure you didn't score - and if you did, you'd have to produce something special to beat them. I just don't see that with Chelsea's defenders. It all seems far too easy.

We're talking about two Chelsea centre-halves, who are meant to be top drawer. One of them is an England international, and a future England captain not long ago in Levi Colwill. And then Wesley Fofana cost Chelsea £70m at a time when £70m was a lot of money.

Maxence Lacroix hasn't pulled up trees at Chelsea either. When he went off injured, it wasn't as if everyone had their head in their hands like if Caicedo or Pedro went off like that.

I expected Chelsea to get in the Champions League this season. Get in that competition and it's a great season. They're a million miles off winning the league.

The problem is there are a lot of teams above them. But now I don't see them beating Nottingham Forest on Friday, after what Forest did to Manchester City a few weeks ago.

That's a huge, huge, huge game at the City Ground. Every game you play for Chelsea is big, but if they win it - then I'd expect them to beat Crystal Palace after all and it's all looking rosy again.

They need Joao Pedro back. We go on about their front three. But if you had The Three Musketeers but took one of them out, you'd struggle to sell a film.
