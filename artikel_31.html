French students block dozens of schools, vow more protests

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

French students blocked the entrances to dozens of high schools on Tuesday, November 10, as they kept up protests for better learning conditions. These protests across France have spiraled into larger cost-of-living demonstrations in recent weeks, some marked by violence and police brutality. FRANCE 24's Luke Shrago reports from the ground.
