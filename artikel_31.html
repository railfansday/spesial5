Émilie König, 'the muse of the Islamic State group' goes on trial in France

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

French anti-terrorism prosecutors once dubbed her "the muse of the Islamic State group". On Thursday, Émilie König appears before a special criminal court in Paris, seeking to convince judges that she has been deradicalised. Originally from western France, König spent a decade in Syria and is accused of being one of the Islamic State group's most active French propagandists. Charged with criminal association with a terrorist organisation, she maintains that she has no blood on her hands.
