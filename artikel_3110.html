Keysight: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Keysight's test gear is now gated by how fast it can build capacity, and its software mix is quietly reshaping the margin story.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 25, 2026 at 11:40PM EST

On its fiscal fourth-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 19, 2026, Keysight (KEYS -1.66%) announced that full-year orders surpassed $8 billion. The electronic test equipment maker is capitalizing on hardware build-outs for artificial intelligence, or AI. Expanding manufacturing capacity allows the business to process its record order backlog, or pool of unfulfilled customer orders. Three takeaways from management's comments highlight where the business is headed.

Hardware developers require specialized measurement tools for research and development (R&D) to test advanced silicon chips and optical links before commercial production. Keysight expanded its design solutions, which doubled its customer count across artificial intelligence infrastructure projects.

Surging customer demand created a temporary bottleneck as orders outpaced immediate shipping capacity. Executive leadership initiated strategic operational changes, including adding manufacturing facilities and broadening component supply partnerships, to accelerate product delivery.

Shift toward software platforms provides steady cash flow while deepening ties with enterprise customers. Annual recurring revenue (ARR), or predictable subscription-based income, reached 25% of total revenue as software adoption grew across key end markets.

Demand across aerospace, defense, and commercial communications continues to outpace historical levels. Keysight maintains structural advantages through early research partnerships in 6G wireless standards and silicon photonics. Operations focus on supply chain capacity and pricing discipline to convert record demand into long-term profit.

The company enters the new fiscal year with substantial momentum across its primary markets.
