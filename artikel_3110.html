Stock Market Today, Nov. 24: Tech Sell-Off Drags Nasdaq as Dow Gains on Value Rotation

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Investors rotated into healthcare and value names as AI momentum cooled and retailers' earnings beats lifted sentiment, today, Nov. 24, 2026.

By Howard Smith – Nov 24, 2026 at 4:51PM EST

Down 0.50% to 7,201, the S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.50%) slipped on tech weakness, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -1.48%) fell 1.48% to 23,734 as artificial intelligence momentum cooled. In comparison, the Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI +0.85%) gained 0.85% to 50,019 as investors favored value sectors.

Gold fell 2.67% to $3,554.00 as of U.S. market close, the 10-Year Treasury yield held steady at 4.92%, and healthcare led sector gains. Industrials lagged, and technology dropped 1.54% relative to the broader market.

Kohl's Corp. jumped 19.48%, and Dollar Tree Inc. surged 11.18% after reporting quarterly results that topped expectations. Among specific tech names, Analog Devices Inc. tumbled 17.37%, and Hewlett Packard Enterprise Co. fell 16.31% amid the broad technology sell-off.

Artificial intelligence leaders retreated after yesterday's rally, with Nvidia (NVDA -5.86%) dropping 5.86% as traders take some profits in high-growth semiconductor names amid the broader tech sell-off today.

That came after consumer confidence data coupled with several strong earnings reports gave investors reason to shift into value and consumer-oriented names. The Conference Board's headline confidence index jumped to 103.3, up 11.4 points from October and well above the 92.2 consensus forecast.

Defensive sectors in general helped boost the Dow index. Today's moves are a good reminder for investors to stay diversified, as one sector's slide can result in another's gains.
