Why Nvidia Stock Just Fell Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Nvidia (NVDA -5.47%) stock fell 5.5% on Tuesday, Nov. 10, after a report deepened the scandal surrounding its CEO, Jensen Huang.

The S&P 500 and the Nasdaq Composite were down 0.4% and 0.7%, respectively.

Nvidia was already falling over the investigation into Huang's relationship with an alleged Chinese intelligence asset. But this morning, The Information, a major tech news outlet, reported that the relationship may have helped move at least $7.5 billion worth of chips over several years to Chinese firms, including some banned for sale in China.

To be clear, these are still just allegations and nothing has been proven -- Reuters said that it could not independently verify the report -- but The Information is a highly reputable news outlet and its reporting should be taken seriously.

This isn't the sort of thing that shows up in the next quarterly report, but if there is truth to these allegations, it could have major ramifications for the company. Jensen Huang has until Wednesday to answer three House committees' request for a briefing. The political blowback will only get louder if The Information's reporting is proven true.

As always, remember to keep a level head, but I would consider reducing your exposure to Nvidia given the potential fallout.
