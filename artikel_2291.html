Craig Halkett delivers positive injury update as Hearts star to take big step forward

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Craig Halkett has delivered a major injury boost for Heart of Midlothian after revealing he has received “really good news” from a specialist.

It comes at a time when injury news from Tynecastle Park has been less than ideal with 11 players currently out following Stephen Kingsley's hamstring issue in the 3-1 loss to Thun on Thursday night.

Halkett has been sidelined since rupturing his Achilles in May, an injury which required surgery and began a lengthy rehabilitation process.

Speaking on TNT Sports, the Hearts captain said: “It’s going really well, thanks. I’ve been back running for the past two or three weeks.

“I actually had a scan and an appointment with a specialist this morning, and he gave me really good news.

“In the next few days, I’ll get the boots back on, and it’s time to start ramping it up.”

The latest development is another encouraging milestone for the 31-year-old centre-back, who had previously targeted a return to first-team action around the New Year.

Halkett joined Hearts from Livingston in the summer of 2019, having agreed a three-year pre-contract earlier that year.
