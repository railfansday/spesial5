US police forces worried GTA 6 players will start committing real crimes, warn officers “are not NPCs”

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Police departments across the United States are warning GTA 6 players not to recreate crimes from the game in real life, reminding fans that stealing cars and reckless driving have serious consequences outside Vice City.

Rockstar Games’ long-awaited Grand Theft Auto 6 finally launched on November 19, bringing players back to Vice City after a 13-year wait between mainline GTA releases.

However, with millions of gamers diving into Rockstar’s open world, several American police departments have issued warnings about copying the game’s criminal activities.

In a social media post shared ahead of launch, the Atlanta Police Department warned fans against bringing the chaos into the real world.

“GTA is not a real place, but Atlanta is!” the department wrote. “Don’t confuse virtual entertainment with real-life decisions.”

GTA is not a real place, but Atlanta is!🎮Don't confuse virtual entertainment with real-life decisions.Drunk driving, reckless driving, auto theft and violence have real consequences.Keep it in the game. Don't bring it to our streets. pic.twitter.com/Y7PdJFHLsZ

Police specifically highlighted drunk driving, reckless driving, auto theft, and violence, urging players to remember that committing these offenses can have real consequences.

The South Fulton Police Department issued a similar warning, reminding players that fictional crimes have no place on real streets.

“Video games are fiction. Real crimes have real victims, and dangerous driving can change lives forever,” the department said. “There’s no respawn, restart, or undo button.”

The warnings haven’t been limited to Georgia.

As reported by the San Antonio Express-News, police departments in Texas have also been reminding players to keep their criminal activities confined to the game.

The San Juan Police Department published a GTA-inspired Instagram video featuring an officer closing a jail cell door alongside the franchise’s iconic “Busted” message.

A post shared by San Juan Police Department (@sjtxpd)

“We know you’re exciting and we know you’ve been waiting a long time, but what happens in GTA VI stays in GTA VI,” they said.

Meanwhile, Floresville police warned that their city wasn’t an extension of Los Santos or Vice City and reminded players that officers aren’t NPCs.

They also warned that running red lights could result in a traffic stop, while changing a car’s color in an H-E-B parking lot wouldn’t make a real-world wanted level disappear.

Interestingly, these warnings come after the Greensboro Police Department in North Carolina said it couldn’t wait for GTA 6 to launch, hoping the game would keep people indoors and give officers a break.

Meanwhile, police in the UK have already used GTA 6 to mock a criminal who was arrested following a dangerous car chase, joking that he would now miss the game’s release.
