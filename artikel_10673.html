Man says he used technique from Detective Conan to save his life after car plunged into river

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

A man in Japan claims he survived a terrifying car accident by remembering a technique from the anime Detective Conan, which showed him how to escape a sinking vehicle.

The driver, who posts on X as nozinozi053, revealed on November 8 that his car had ended up in a river following a collision with another motorist.

According to his account, the other driver had been reversing near a cemetery when they suddenly pulled forward as he attempted to pass, resulting in a collision.

With his vehicle submerged, the man initially believed he was going to die. However, a scene from Detective Conan suddenly came to mind.

“For the first 20 seconds or so, I really thought I was going to die,” he wrote. “But then this scene suddenly flashed through my mind, and I managed to escape alive.”

“I can’t thank the creator of Conan enough!”

The scene comes from episode 1078, titled ‘The Black Organization’s Scheme (Landing),’ which originally aired on April 1, 2023.

In the episode, FBI agent Andre Camel drives into the ocean while being pursued by the Black Organization. Trapped underwater, he discovers that his car doors won’t open because of the pressure outside.

Conan instructs Camel to breathe from an air pocket near the roof until the vehicle fills with enough water for the pressure inside and outside to equalize, allowing him to open the door and escape.

The sequence is detailed in the anime’s official episode synopsis.

The real-life driver said he recalled the scene while underwater and desperately searched for the door handle.

“I pulled on it, and the wall to my right moved a little. I realized it was the door, so I pushed it open with all my strength,” he explained. “It was a little heavy, but it opened relatively easily.”

When asked whether the other driver had helped him, he revealed that he hadn’t even seen the person responsible for the collision. Instead, a nearby resident helped him climb onto the riverbank.

Police, emergency services, and local residents subsequently gathered at the scene. Although the man survived, his car was reportedly written off.

In a follow-up post, he also revealed that his jacket had floated away in the river. He had assumed someone would retrieve it, but nobody did, leaving him almost as upset as losing his car.

Despite the successful escape, the technique shown in Detective Conan is not the recommended first option for escaping a submerged vehicle.

According to AAA, drivers should immediately unbuckle their seat belts and attempt to escape through a window. Waiting for a vehicle to fill with water is extremely dangerous because pressure may not equalize immediately, leaving occupants at risk of drowning.
