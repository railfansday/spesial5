LEGO is turning a fan-made Interstellar spaceship into an official set

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

LEGO has officially approved a fan-made Interstellar design, meaning fans will soon be able to build their own version of the iconic spacecraft from Christopher Nolan’s sci-fi movie.

The company announced the winners of its first 2026 LEGO Ideas review on November 9, confirming that an Interstellar project created by user Minibrick41587 will be developed into an official product.

The fan-made design recreates the Ranger spacecraft from the 2014 film and features 561 pieces, along with minifigures of Cooper, played by Matthew McConaughey, and Dr. Amelia Brand, portrayed by Anne Hathaway.

It also includes brick-built versions of the robots TARS and CASE, while the spacecraft itself features an opening rear hatch and interior seating for the two astronauts.

However, this isn’t the first time the creator has attempted to bring Interstellar into the LEGO universe.

Minibrick41587 previously submitted a similar Ranger design that attracted the 10,000 supporters required for LEGO Ideas consideration, but the company ultimately rejected it.

The creator returned with an updated version, which once again reached the required milestone in February 2026. This time, the project was approved.

Interestingly, Minibrick41587 is already responsible for an official LEGO product, having designed the fan concept that became the Polaroid OneStep SX-70 Camera set.

According to IGN, the Interstellar build was one of just three projects approved from 97 submissions in the latest review. The other winners were Tulip Field Windmill and Chinese Cork Carving Art.

Two more designs, based on The Fifth Element and Plants vs. Zombies, remain under consideration.

The announcement follows several other successful fan projects. LEGO previously approved a fan-made Godzilla model, while a Yu-Gi-Oh Exodia design won a LEGO Ideas contest in September 2026.

There’s currently no confirmed release date or price for the Interstellar set. Its 561-piece design could also change before launch, as LEGO’s designers work to turn the fan submission into a finished retail product.
