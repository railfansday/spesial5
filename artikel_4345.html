Fermi's 14-year all-sky map bridges astronomy's elusive 'MeV gap'

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Gamma rays, the most high-energy type of electromagnetic radiation in the universe, have always had a blind spot. We've always had trouble measuring them in the "megaelectronvolt" (MeV) energy range, which astronomers have taken to calling the "MeV gap." But a new paper from an international group of scientists working with the Fermi Large Area Telescope (LAT) Collaboration, recently published in The Astrophysical Journal Supplement Series, takes a stab at closing that gap by identifying 450 cosmic sources in the 20–200 MeV range, providing the clearest view of low-energy gamma rays ever.
