Malik Nabers doesn't practice, says he's good for Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Giants wide receiver Malik Nabers was named the NFC offensive player of the month for October on Thursday, but he didn't take part in practice with the team.

Nabers sat out the workout because of his knee. He was not on the injury report at all on Wednesday and told reporters that he would be good to go for Sunday's game against the Eagles.

The Giants will issue an official designation for Nabers on Friday. They'll do the same for running back Cam Skattebo, but the outlook seems less bright for him after he missed his second straight day with a hamstring injury.

Right tackle Jermaine Eluemunor (calf), wide receiver Charlie Jones (hand), left tackle Andrew Thomas (groin), and defensive lineman Darius Alexander (foot) were all limited participants. Linebacker Abdul Carter (illness), linebacker Malik Harrison (groin), cornerback Colton Hood (illness), tight end Theo Johnson (neck), offensive lineman Marcus Mbow (knee), defensive lineman DJ Reader (knee), tight end Thomas Fidone (thumb), linebacker Trace Ford (knee), and linebacker Micah McFadden (foot) were all full participants.
