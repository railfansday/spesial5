Where did Texas football land in the AP top 25 poll after beating Missouri on the road?

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Texas football moved up a bit in the rankings Sunday.

After beating Missouri 34-20, the Longhorns rose two spots to No. 8 in the latest Associated Press Top 25 poll. The Tigers ended up falling out of the rankings entirely.

Texas Longhorns head coach Steve Sarkisian instructs players during the game against Missouri at Faurot Field at Memorial Stadium in Columbia on Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026. (Mikala Compton/Austin American-Statesman)

But the chaos of college football continued. Previous No. 1 Miami lost its first game to then-No. 11 Notre Dame. Georgia became the No. 1 overall team while the Hurricanes fell to No. 5 and the Fighting Irish rose to No. 3. Kentucky dropped from No. 6 to No. 13 after losing to Tennessee and Ohio State dropped to No. 21 after being ranked No. 12 and losing to unranked Oregon.

UCLA and Utah are both 9-0 and are the only undefeated teams left.

Texas stays on the road next weekend with a trip to Baton Rouge to play No. 24 LSU who fell nine spots after losing to Alabama.
