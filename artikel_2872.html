2 Diagnostics Stocks to Buy in November Before Cancer Screening Goes Mainstream

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

These two companies are helping drive awareness of early-stage cancer testing.

By Lee Samaha – Nov 23, 2026 at 11:45AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Guardant Health ›

Grail (GRAL +6.76%) and Guardant Health (GH +6.03%) represent two companies with significant upside potential in multi-cancer early detection (MCED) testing. However, they differ significantly in their strategies and developmental timelines for MCED tests.

Here's why both are attractive for investors, and why they are a surprisingly complementary fit.

In a nutshell, Guardant already has an early-stage test with pre-market approval (PMA) -- Shield for colorectal cancer -- with commercial coverage and is working toward achieving a PMA for the Shield platform in multi-cancer detection (MCD).

In contrast, Grail doesn't have its Galleri MCED test approved yet, or commercial or Medicare coverage yet, but it offers potential early detection of more than 50 cancers in one test.

As such, investing in Guardant offers investors a more secure pathway to cash flow generation (revenue grew by 36% year over year in the most recent quarter), including a tripling in Shield test volume to 83,000 as commercial coverage grows. Moreover, Guardant offers other oncology tests that act as companion diagnostics for cancer patients receiving therapy. These include tests to determine whether a cancer therapy is working effectively.

These are the near-term drivers of Guardant's revenue and earnings, but over the long term the company is looking to establish Shield as a MCD test. Guardant has the advantage that the Shield platform was developed "as a multi-cancer detection platform from a single blood draw; physicians can screen for colorectal cancer and if the patient opts in, receive results for additional cancers," according to Co-CEO and founder AmirAli Talasaz.

In other words, Shield MCD and the already-approved Shield are the same platform, meaning the lab work is the same, but Guardant still needs to be approved for other cancers.

That said, a patient ordering Shield for colorectal cancer test can opt in to use Shield as an MCD, and Guardant is gathering and using that data to ultimately support a PMA submission. However, as Talasaz also notes, "we are in relatively early innings of this MCD opt-in," and Shield MCD is a profit driver one for the longer term.

In contrast, Grail's Galleri test is not one for the long term. In fact, management expects it will receive PMA from the Food and Drug Administration in early 2027. It's a critical part of the story, but not necessarily the key part. I'll explain.

Grail already offers the Galleri MCED test on a self-pay basis, but it needs FDA approval to open a pathway to Medicare adoption under the Nancy Sewell Act, which allows Medicare coverage for FDA-approved MCED tests, provided the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) determines such coverage is appropriate. Grail has been in discussions with CMS for some time, and management expects it to initiate a national analysis of Galleri after the FDA grants a PMA.

Similarly, commercial insurers are understood to be waiting for Galleri's PMA to begin discussions about providing coverage. It's a somewhat complex process at the best of times, but complicated by Galleri missing its primary endpoint (detecting cancers at an earlier stage and so reduce the incidence of late stage detection) in its landmark three-year trial with England's National Health Service (NHS).

On the other hand, the Galleri test did demonstrate more impressive data in the 12 deadly cancers that cause most cancer-related deaths in the U.S., and that's the basis of potential coverage for Galleri.

All told, Guardant offers a slower but more secure route to widespread cancer testing via Shield and Shield MCD, while Grail offers investors more near-term upside but also risk around Medicare and commercial insurer adoption, as well as uncertainty about test pricing. The difference makes them complementary stocks for an investor looking for exposure to the investment theme.
