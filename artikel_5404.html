BetaNXT Partners with Taurus to Expand Digital Asset Capabilities for U.S. Financial Institutions

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The integration will bring Taurus-PROTECT's digital asset custody and blockchain connectivity to BetaNXT's securities processing and wealth management platforms

NEW YORK, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- BetaNXT, a leading provider of wealth management technology solutions with real-time data capabilities and advanced AI innovation, today announced a partnership with Taurus, a leader in digital asset infrastructure for financial institutions, to integrate its Taurus-PROTECT custody solution into BetaNXT's existing platforms.

The integration will enable BetaNXT's broker-dealer, custodian, asset manager, and wealth management clients to introduce digital asset services alongside traditional securities, using their established technology and operational processes.

Bringing digital assets into existing securities workflows

U.S. financial institutions rely on established systems to process transactions, maintain client records, and manage securities operations. Integrating digital asset capabilities into these environments can help firms expand their offerings while reducing the need for separate infrastructure and manual processes.

The collaboration combines Taurus-PROTECT's institutional-grade custody, wallet management, and blockchain connectivity with BetaNXT's securities processing technology. Through their partnership, the companies aim to simplify transaction processing, reconciliation, and settlement, helping financial institutions manage digital assets within their existing workflows.

"Our clients are looking to introduce new asset classes and services without disrupting the operations they already have in place," said Tim Rutka, President of Trading and Settlement at BetaNXT. "Integrating Taurus-PROTECT will extend our technology to digital assets, giving clients a more connected, frictionless way to manage custody, transactions, and securities processing."

Supporting the adoption of tokenized securities

The integration also creates opportunities for financial institutions exploring tokenized securities, building on both companies' involvement in DTCC's tokenization initiatives.

As a participant in DTCC's 50-plus-firm Industry Working Group, BetaNXT has helped advance industry readiness and the operational and technical workflows needed to bring tokenization assets into established financial infrastructure. Over the last several months, BetaNXT joined more than 30 firms completing production transactions using DTC-tokenized assets across workflows including collateral, securities lending, Treasury repo, equity delivery-versus-payment and margin. In October 2026, DTCC named Taurus an ecosystem collaborator and integrated wallet provider for its Tokenization Service.

The service enables eligible securities held at The Depository Trust Company (DTC) to be represented on blockchain networks. Taurus has integrated with DTCC's APIs and nodes to provide wallet infrastructure and connectivity for participating institutions.

Building on these developments, Taurus and BetaNXT aim to help eligible clients incorporate tokenized securities into their existing custody and securities processing workflows, subject to applicable participation requirements. Further details are available in Taurus's DTCC Tokenization Service announcement.

"Financial institutions want to expand their digital asset offerings while maintaining control over their operations," said Lamine Brahimi, Co-Founder and Managing Partner at Taurus. "Integrating Taurus-PROTECT with BetaNXT will give U.S. financial institutions access to secure custody and blockchain infrastructure through their existing technology, helping them introduce new services without duplicating their operational systems."
