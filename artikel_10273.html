Samsung’s paid Galaxy AI plan leaks for $5/month, but will you pay?

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Samsung has long left the idea of paid Galaxy AI features on the table for Galaxy devices, and now there’s finally some evidence that it’s coming and could cost $5/month.

Since the suite was introduced, Samsung has been offering Galaxy AI features for free, with an asterisk. Originally, they were only free through “the end of 2025,” but Samsung kept saying that it had no plans to start charging and, so far, has not made any announcements.

SammyGuru spotted evidence in One UI 9.5 for a new “Galaxy AI Premium” subscription. Based on strings, Samsung would be charging $4.99/month for its Galaxy AI features, though the good news is that existing features remain free.
