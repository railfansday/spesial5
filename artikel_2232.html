Birmingham City told to strike Nottingham Forest transfer for 95-cap international star

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Birmingham City's impressive start to the season has got many fans at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park dreaming of a return to the Premier League for the first time since 2011.

Chris Davies' side have only lost two of their first 14 matches of the Championship season, winning four of their last five and scoring 14 goals in the process.

Tuesday's controlled 3-0 win over Millwall saw Birmingham reach the automatic promotion places for just over 24 hours, before they were knocked down to third for the time being after Wolves' win at Bristol City.

Such a strong start wasn't exactly forecast by many in this part of the Second City at the beginning of the season, as it was argued that the squad was left short in certain areas.

However, given the well-documented ambitions of American investors, Knighthead Capital Management, many are now debating what activity will be conducted in the winter window if Blues are still among the promotion contenders.

With Birmingham's forward department currently made up of August Priske, Jay Stansfield, Luis Vazquez and Marvin Ducksch, it has been questioned what changes will be made going forward.

Priske has already netted six times this season whilst Stansfield has now firmly aclimatised to playing as a 'number 10', whilst exit talk continues to surround Ducksch after a failed summer departure to Valencia, and Vazquez is yet to convince the majority of supporters.

As such, it was recently debated by Blues YouTuber, Tommy Kelsall, whether a reunion for Chris Wood, who scored 11 times in 29 appearances on loan in the 2011/12 season under Chris Hughton, makes sense, given his similarities with Priske, but also the experience under his belt.

The in-form striker was absent from Tuesday's dominant success over Millwall

Wood has only made two Premier League starts under Oliver Glasner at Nottingham Forest this season, having seen his 2025/26 campaign blighted by a knee injury.

Beforehand, the 95-time New Zealand international scored 20 times as the Reds secured European football in 2024/25, and his record of 170 strikes in the top two divisions is indisputable.

With his contract at the City Ground up next June, Football League World's Birmingham City fan pundit, Jason Moore, believes the 34-year-old would be a great option for Davies to call upon.

"I don't mind the shout," Moore told FLW.

"People might say he's aging, but he's, again, a proven Premier League striker. He's done a decent job in the Premier League.

"He's not pacey. But, I don't think that's his game and he'd probably be, like that tweet says, a like-for-like for August Priske in the way we play, so I'd be all up for it.

"You know he's going to work hard, he'd take that pressure off Priske playing every single game, and he's a miles better replacement than Luis Vazquez.

"If you can look at Chris Wood and if he's not playing at Forest, then I'd be all for it. If we could pull that off, that's a great shout. Him or Che (Adams), I'd be all up for it."

"I'd narrowly favour Che for his pace, but I'd be more than happy with Chris Wood if Che wasn't available. Both are excellent options."

Having a focal point in attack such as Priske has allowed Birmingham to source goals from across Davies' squad, and whilst the Dane has become a feared striker this term, Wood's experience would certainly aid a promotion bid.

Like Priske, he has the ability to bring fellow attackers into the game and make their own deep runs into the box and create chances, whilst also having a poacher's instinct despite not being particularly fast.

If Birmingham were also to end their Premier League exile, then a player of such calibre would also be worth retaining, given Wood's extensive record in both divisions.

The nature of the Championship schedule also means that the forward is likely to source more minutes in the West Midlands than he currently is in the East.
