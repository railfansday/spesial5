Get Tiller if you already budget in Google Sheets or Excel and are tired of entering every purchase by hand. For $99 a year after a 30-day free trial, it fills your spreadsheet with each day’s bank and card transactions. Skip it if you want a phone app that budgets for you.

Tiller links your bank, credit card, loan, and investment accounts through the data services Yodlee and Plaid and writes the latest transactions and balances into your Google Sheets or Excel file once a day. It suits people who like building their own formulas and keeping their own copy of the data, which stays in the file even if you cancel.

At $99 a year, about $8.25 a month, Tiller pays off for people who budget in a spreadsheet and would otherwise download or type in every account’s transactions. Budgeters who want a phone app that sorts spending for them are better served by Monarch Money, which categorizes transactions automatically in its iPhone, Android, and web apps.
