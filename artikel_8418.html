Are you sure it's mold? Unless you have a true ventilation issue, it could be just soap scum and/or mineral deposits accumulating. For the vinyl sweep edge at the bottom of the door, every two weeks or so I simply put a rag or a Scott towel on my index finger and wipe it a few times trying to get in between those fins. Even now after a couple of years that strip still looks new.

The edges where the panels meet the pan, over the years it could be water has gotten behind the silicone caulk. You could try to remove the existing caulk, do a thorough cleaning, let it completely dry, then recaulk.

Keep the room and shower doors open to allow ventilation when not in use.
