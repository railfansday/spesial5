Quentin Grimes feels Lakers win over Hornets was a ‘statement game’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Following the Los Angeles Lakers' come-from-behind victory over the Charlotte Hornets Tuesday night, head coach JJ Redick praised his team's resiliency as the Lakers erased a 14-point fourth-quarter deficit to win, despite the absence of critical starters in Austin Reaves and Walker Kessler.

Among those who elevated their game to mitigate the loss of Reaves and Kessler was Quentin Grimes, who recorded a career-high 47 points (16-of-24 shooting, 10-of-14 beyond the arc), six rebounds, three assists and one steal. His night was headlined by a game-tying triple in regulation and go-ahead free throws during overtime.

Grimes echoed his head coach's message of resilience and thought Los Angeles' gutsy win made clear this team's identity.

"I'm calling this one a statement game. This was a statement game for the team," Grimes told reporters postgame. "We were down 18, so having guys know we're shorthanded and being able to rally around everybody, not just by my scoring but coming together and trying to gut this win out. I feel like this was a big time statement game showing how hard we play and how resilient, how tough this team could be for later on in the season."

While Grimes stepped up considerably without Reaves, it seemed as though the entire rotation collectively worked together and helped soften the loss of a defensive presence like Kessler. This season, the Lakers are allowing 111.0 points per 100 possessions with Kessler on the floor and 118.4 points per 100 whenever he sits, per Cleaning the Glass. On Tuesday, a night Kessler didn't play at all, they held the Hornets to a 110.8 offensive rating.

Yes, Charlotte is the league's 30th-ranked offense, generating a meager 105.3 points per 100 possessions, but Los Angeles still found a way to cobble together stops without its interior anchor — particularly in the second half — something it's not often done through the first month of the year. That's the sort of performance deserving of Grimes' labels: resilient, tough and hard-playing.

The Lakers return to the floor aiming for a 10th consecutive Wednesday night when the Utah Jazz stroll into Crypto.com Arena. Tipoff is scheduled for 7 p.m.
