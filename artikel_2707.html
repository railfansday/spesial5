Ashton Jeanty bottled up in Week 10 loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Las Vegas Raiders RB Ashton Jeanty rushed nine times for 18 yards and caught four passes for 46 yards against the Seattle Seahawks in Week 10.

Why it Matters: Jeanty had another ugly day running the ball, but he did end up leading the team in receiving in Fernando Mendoza's NFL debut. With Jeanty seemingly splitting carries with Mike Washington Jr., his receiving production is driving a lot of his value. At this point, RB2 production seems like the best-case scenario for Jeanty.
