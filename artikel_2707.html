Warriors vs. Mavericks injury report: Steph Curry, Al Horford out

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Golden State Warriors have been in a free fall ever since Steph Curry suffered an abdominal injury, and tonight they'll try to right the ship. It won't be easy, though, as they're on the road facing Cooper Flagg and the Dallas Mavericks.

And, once again, the Dubs are working from a talent deficit due to a whole bunch of injuries. Here's the full injury report.

We're still a week away from Curry getting re-evaluated, and that doesn't guarantee that he'll be back anytime soon. Right now the Dubs are in survival mode, and it's not going well.

Out — Al Horford (bilateral low back injury management)

The Warriors play again tomorrow night, and Horford doesn't play back-to-backs, so it was just a question of whether he'd sit for this game or tomorrow's. Horford and the Warriors decided on today, and the veteran will be available on Thursday against Zach Edey, Cam Boozer, Quinten Post, and the Memphis Grizzlies.

Out — Brandon Williams (left hamstring strain)

The Warriors released a positive update on Williams today, but also said that he'll be re-evaluated on Saturday, meaning he'll miss tonight and tomorrow at the bare minimum.

Out — Jimmy Butler III (right ACL repair surgery)Out — Moses Moody (left patellar tendon repair surgery)Out — Kristaps Porziņģis (not with team)

The standard trio on every injury report these days. No new news, for better or for worse.

Available — Draymond Green (right elbow inflammation soreness)

Green was a question mark going into this game, but thankfully got the all clear and will suit up for the Dubs. His defense and playmaking are desperately needed.

Out — Dereck Lively II (right foot surgery)

Lively had an awesome rookie season, but has struggled to stay on the court since: he's appeared in just 43 games over the last three seasons, and has yet to make his season debut.

Out — Tarik Biberovic (right heel bursitis)

Biberovic was a late second-round pick by the Grizzlies in 2023, and is finally in the states to make his NBA debut. Unfortunately, a right heel injury has delayed that debut, and he currently has no timetable for a return to the court.

Out — Sergio de Larrea (right knee sprain)

The 25th overall pick in June's draft out of Spain, de Larrea has played sparingly, but has really impressed in his limited action. It would have been fun to see him in this game.

Like de Larrea, Lawal has not found the court much in his rookie season. A second-round pick in June, he's appeared in eight games but is averaging just 4.5 minutes, mostly in garbage time.

Available — Santi Aldama (left ankle sprain)Available — Max Christie (left ankle sprain)Available — Caleb Martin (right thumb splint)Available — Walter Clayton Jr. (right ankle sprain)

Some notable names in Dallas' rotation were questionable coming into this one, but are ready to go. Should be a good game!

Enjoy the action, everyone. It tips off at 5:00 p.m. PT on NBC Sports Bay Area.
