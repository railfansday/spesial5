SoftBank drops 14% as Wall Street AI sell-off ripples across Asian tech names

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Asian technology stocks tumbled Wednesday, led by a rout in Japanese semiconductor stocks, as renewed concerns over the artificial intelligence trade rattles investors following an overnight sell-off on Wall Street.

Japanese semiconductor stocks tied to the global AI supply chain were among the hardest hit. Advantest, a supplier of chip testing equipment to Nvidia, plunged 12.7%. Tokyo Electron, which supplies chipmaking equipment to Nvidia's manufacturing partner TSMC, slumped 11.82%.

Technology conglomerate SoftBank Group shed 13.8%, while semiconductor inspection equipment maker Lasertec lost 10.15%.

Other Japanese chip stocks also came under heavy selling pressure, with Disco Corporation falling 9.41% and Renesas Electronics losing 9%.

Tech stocks have seen heightened volatility over the past few months amid concerns over capital expenditure, skepticism over return on investments, circular AI financing, with Jensen Huang's ouster as Nvidia CEO adding fresh worries, and his return sparking a massive rally.

In South Korea, memory chip giants Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix both plunged more than 7%. Both companies are key suppliers of the high-bandwidth memory chips used in AI accelerators.

Other South Korean technology stocks also traded lower, with LG Innotek shedding 3.8%.

In Taiwan, shares TSMC tumbled 6.73%, while Hon Hai Precision Industry plunged 9.05%. Electronic components maker Foxconn Technology shed 6.13%.

Some Chinese technology stocks listed in Hong Kong bucked the broader regional sell-off, with Xiaomi jumping 5.66%, Kuaishou gaining 3.5% and Alibaba adding 1.4%. Tencent rose 0.82%, while Meituan and Baidu edged lower.

U.S. technology stocks fell overnight with the Nasdaq Composite giving back some of the sharp gains from the previous session that were fueled by Huang's return as Nvidia CEO.
