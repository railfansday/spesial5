Mike Vrabel on Drake Maye: I wouldn't imagine him being available out of the bye

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

There has been mixed reporting about the status of Drake Maye’s ankle injury after the Patriots lost to the Lions in Munich on Sunday.

New England will be on its bye in Week 11. In his Monday press conference, head coach Mike Vrabel would only go as far as noting the quarterback’s likely status for Week 12.

“I’m not going to make any comments about his status until he gets back here,” Vrabel said. “He’s en route back here. He stayed with a few of our people and they’re en route back here. So, once he gets back here, our people are able to visit with him again, talk with him again, I think we’ll have more to offer.

“But I wouldn’t imagine him being available when we come out of the bye.”

PFT reported on Sunday night that Maye is expected to be out for a while, with others reporting on Monday that the quarterback may not be back at all in 2026.

But Vrabel said at this point, he doesn’t expect the team to go and sign another quarterback. Tommy DeVito and and Behren Morton are the other QBs on the 53-man roster. The team does not have a quarterback on the practice squad.
