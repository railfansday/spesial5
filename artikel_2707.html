Senators rumors: Ottawa interested in trade for 2 Penguins veterans

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Ottawa Senators are playing good hockey at this time. Ottawa is one of the top teams in a tough Atlantic Division. The Senators are looking like they could hang in the Atlantic. And they are already considering potential trades they could make this season.

Sportsnet NHL insider Elliotte Friedman appeared on an episode of Saturday Headlines. He mentioned that general manager Steve Staios recently attended a game between the Pittsburgh Penguins and the Nashville Predators. The Predators are in a playoff spot, so the attention is on the Penguins roster.

"It is believed the Senators have interest in both Rickard Rakell, who has already vetoed one trade, and Bryan Rust. We know the Penguins are open for business," Friedman said.

Friedman referred to a report he wrote about Rakell nearly being traded to the Chicago Blackhawks. That trade would have seen the Penguins acquire a 2027 first-round pick, veteran Teuvo Teravainen, and an AHL player. As Friedman alluded to, though, Rakell said no to the deal.

Rakell joined the Penguins in 2021-22 and has been a very reliable middle-six forward for them. Last season, he scored 24 goals and 48 points to aid Pittsburgh's run to the Stanley Cup Playoffs. This year, he has eight goals and 13 points in 22 games.

Rust, meanwhile, has spent his entire career with the Penguins. He has won two Stanley Cups with the franchise and is coming off back-to-back 65-point seasons. The veteran is currently on injured reserve.

What the Senators look to do certainly remains to be seen. However, it appears they are preparing to buy on the trade market. It will be worth seeing whether the Penguins end up being an ideal trade partner for them this season.
