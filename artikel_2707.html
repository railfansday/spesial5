Friday Cannon Fodder: drawing a blank

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Happy Friday, TSFers! The muse did not speak to me this morning. Nothing brilliant, insightful, nor funny struck me to share with y'all in CF. I'm sorry, I know you're devastated. I'll try to be and do better going forward.

Speaking of going forward, Arsenal have a couple more decisions to make on extensions after the flurry of new contracts went out over the November international break. The biggest question marks are Martin Ødegaard and Kai Havertz. There's also Mikel Merino, Ben White, and the backup keepers.

I'd bet Merino would be offered a bumper deal. I doubt the club will want to commit significant term or money to him, but if he wants to stick around on, say a three-year contract with a fourth-year option, I could see that happening. It seems likely Ben White will be sold this coming summer or will walk when his contract expires.

Earlier today, Mikel Arteta said Martin Ødegaard is next in line for contract talks and that he wants to keep the club captain in North London. Ødegaard recently expressed that he's happy and would like to stay too but that talks have yet to begin. It seems as though that deal will happen in due course. And rightly so. He's an incredibly important player, a leader, and it seems as if he's rediscovering his elite form now that his fitness is cooperating. He strikes me as the type of player who will be able to adapt his game and play as his physical tools start to wane.

Which leaves Kai Havertz. I would be happy for Arsenal to keep him at the club. He's a versatile player capable of filling several different roles and playing either as a more physical target / outlet or linking up in combination play. His ceiling is absolutely good enough to lead the line at Arsenal for the next couple of years. But form and fitness have been a bit of an issue that have kept from hitting that level arguably as frequently as the club need him to do.

People have suggested that Havertz might become a Gabriel Martinelli-esque roster casualty — a guy you'd like to keep, people at the club would like to keep, but will be sold because of the fee he commands. I'd be fine with that outcome, too. I like him as a player more than most but I'm not overly attached to him. If there are better options out there, go for it. But I think "better than Kai Havertz" is a much higher bar to clear than most believe.
