Tee Higgins did not practice Friday, questionable for Sunday vs. Saints

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Bengals receiver Tee Higgins did not practice on Friday, but the injury should not keep him out of Sunday’s game against the Saints.

Via Ben Baby of ESPN, head coach Zac Taylor told reporters after Friday’s practice that Higgins woke up with hamstring soreness and the team gave him a day to work through it.

Higgins is officially questionable for the Week 12 contest.

In 10 games this season, Higgins has 867 receiving yards with seven touchdowns. He’s well on pace to set a career high in receiving yards in 2026.

Guard Dalton Risner (knees) is out. Returning from injured reserve, cornerback Jalen Davis (hamstring) is doubtful while receiver Andrei Iosivas (thumb) is questionable.

Safety Bryan Cook (ankle) is set to play.
