Dubsky scores 21 in UNC Asheville’s 101-74 victory over Erskine

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

ASHEVILLE, N.C. (AP) — Connor Dubsky had 21 points in UNC Asheville's 101-74 win against Erskine on Wednesday.

Dubsky shot 6 for 11 from beyond the arc and 3 of 3 from the free-throw line for the Bulldogs (1-1). Daniel Thomas scored 17 points and added nine rebounds. Khayri Dunn went 5 of 10 from the field (3 for 7 from 3-point range) to finish with 14 points.

Daniel Enowachou led the way for the Flying Fleet with 18 points. Erskine got 15 points, six assists and two steals from Keyaron Hendricks. Braxton Dowdle had 14 points.

UNC Asheville's next game is Saturday against Western Carolina at home, and Erskine visits Citadel on Friday.
