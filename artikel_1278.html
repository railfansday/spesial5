Spain 2-3 England: Lamine Yamal's Comment About Harry Kane Resurfaces

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

England defeated Spain, in a thrilling 3-2 win on Sunday night at the Metropolitano in Madrid in a UEFA Nations League fixture.

The first hour was sensational for England, as Harry Kane converted from the penalty spot before goals from Ezri Konsa and Ben White put the Three Lions in dreamland.

However, Elliot Anderson was sent off in the 63rd minute after fouling Lamine Yamal inside the penalty area. Mikel Oyarzabal slotted home before Nico Williams made it 3-2.

Thomas Tuchel's side were able to hold on to a narrow victory after playing over 30 minutes with 10 men. The world champions threw everything they had at them but James Trafford and his backline remained resolute.

Following the historic victory, Kane gets another one over Yamal, whom he beat to the 2026 Ballon d'Or award. Now, it's interesting to look back on comments Yamal made about his English peer.

In an old interview with former footballer Jorge Valdano via Movistar Plus (per GOAL), Yamal was asked who he thinks should've won the Ballon d'Or, and he gave an interesting response, although he didn't go as far as naming a player.

He said: "I think the Ballon d'Or should go to the best player in the world, full stop. Not to a player who has scored 80 goals, because if you score 80 goals, you get the top scorer award for that. Those are two completely different things that people don't understand.

"The best player in the world is the one where, as a commentator, you are following a match and enjoying it, you see him and think: that is a player who clearly stands out from all the others."

In Yamal's view, the award goes to the most impactful players on the pitch, regardless of how many goals they score or titles they have won.

With voting set to end on September 28, Yamal made some rather interesting comments about Kane.

He even went one step further, saying: "Mbappe and I, we are the two best in the world, but I think I would have deserved it this year. For me, that is clear, and everyone who watches the match knows it." Very bold words from the teenager.

Kane proved those words wrong as he was crowned the first English Ballon d'Or winner since 2001 at the London Palladium theatre last month.

He beat the likes of Lionel Messi, Yamal, and Mbappe to the honour after an incredible campaign in front of goal for both Bayern Munich and England.
