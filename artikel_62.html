French ex-PM Villepin announces presidential run

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

France's former prime minister and foreign minister Dominique de Villepin, famed abroad for his excoriating 2003 denunciation of US plans for invading Iraq, said on Friday he was running for president. Villepin enters an already crowded field of candidates seeking to succeed President Emmanuel Macron, with polls pointing towards a possible run-off between frontrunner Marine Le Pen of the far-right National Rally (RN) and hard-left France Unbowed (LFI) veteran Jean-Luc Melenchon.
