Game of the Month: Phantom Blade Zero (October 2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Update [Fri 6th Nov 2026, 6pm]: October offered up a bumper Game of the Month vote thanks to a truly stacked release schedule on PS5 — but as always, only one title can win our prestigious award.

And this time around, that title is Phantom Blade Zero. The debut action game from rapidly emerging Chinese studio S-GAME stole the show by some distance.

It's important to note that it was up against more than 20 other PS5 releases — the most any winner's had to overcome in any of our Game of the Month polls to date.

Over 1,100 votes were cast by readers of Push Square, and these were the top five best PS5 games of October 2026, according to you lot:

Phantom Blade Zero had only been out for a handful of days when voting opened, which just goes to show the kind of immediate impact that it's managed to have.

There's no doubt it's a Game of the Year contender, given review scores and overall popularity.

And it's a good action title, too — a damn good one. Impressive in its scope and ambition, it's a seriously meaty wuxia-style romp, packing top class combat and some surprisingly great RPG elements, like being able to bolster your style of play through a huge selection of perk-based weapons and accessories.

Liam dubbed it "one of the generation's greatest action experiences" in our Phantom Blade Zero PS5 review, blessing it with an 'excellent' 9/10 score.

Original Story: Ah, October, otherwise known as the month before GTA 6.

Indeed, it was an incredibly busy 31 days for new PS5 releases, following on from an equally manic September — and that's going to make this latest Game of the Month poll all the more intriguing.

October's most high profile title arguably launched just a few days ago: Phantom Blade Zero, the PS5 console exclusive that had been wowing onlookers for years with its gameplay trailers, finally arrived on the 29th.

The Chinese action game earned extremely impressive review scores, and it seems to have been a big hit with fans of the genre.

Meanwhile, Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 actually managed to deliver what many are calling the series' best campaign in years, and Castlevania: Belmont's Curse revived Konami's iconic franchise in some style.

Other standouts included the eye-watering Star Wars: Galactic Racer — a fantastic racer that's gone down far better than anyone thought it would — and Final Fantasy Resonance, which turned out to be a brilliant callback to the property's traditional JRPG roots.

Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve deserves a shoutout too; another super highly rated release, and quite possibly the most engaging Ace Combat game of the series' modern era.

Then you've got the more divisive stuff that some of you are bound to vote for: both Stupid Never Dies and Hellraiser: Revival struggled to capture the hearts of critics, but they're the kind of games where if you enjoy them, you probably really enjoy them.

But as always, only one of these titles can be crowned Game of the Month. Before we get to the all-important vote, though, here's a quick recap of our 2026 winners so far...
