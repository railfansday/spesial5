Grand Canyon Small Plane Crash Kills 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A missing plane that vanished near the Grand Canyon has been found wrecked ... with all three people aboard confirmed dead.

According to the National Park Service, crews located the missing Piper Comanche aircraft and a debris field around 10:15 AM Monday in the Basin area of Grand Canyon National Park's North Rim.

The plane was first reported missing around 11:30 PM Sunday, prompting an extensive search operation ... it was last known to be in the vicinity of the Basin area with three people said to be on board.

Authorities launched an aerial search early Monday morning, with the Arizona Department of Public Safety using forward-looking infrared technology and night vision to try to locate the aircraft, according to NPS. The search effort also included Coconino County Search and Rescue, Kane County Search and Rescue and the Air Force Rescue Coordination Center.

The National Transportation Safety Board says they will investigate the crash, with assistance from the National Park Service, however it's still unclear what caused the plane to go down at this time.

The identities of the passengers have not yet been released.
