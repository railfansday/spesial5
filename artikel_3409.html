One of the Best Comic Anti-Heroes of All Time Is Officially Getting His Own Netflix Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Just about everyone loves a good antihero. Morally gray protagonists who are more than willing to engage in decidedly unheroic actions for the greater good have become more and more popular on-screen as audiences seek out more challenging and interesting characters. Comics, in particular, have birthed some of the most beloved leads of this kind, from Marvel's Punisher, Deadpool, and Wolverine, to John Constantine from DC, and Todd McFarlane's legendary Image Comics icon, Spawn. Just as it once introduced viewers to Jon Bernthal's rendition of Frank Castle, Netflix is now bringing another storied antihero to life from the pages of an independent classic that's been around for over 40 years.

The streamer has officially announced a new animated adaptation of Matt Wagner's long-running Dark Horse Comics noir title, Grendel. Accompanying the news is a new image giving a taste of what the series' gritty portrayal of New York could look like once it hits the screen, with the vigilante himself standing front and center. Bobak Esfarjani, the Emmy-winning producer of Apple TV's breakout horror comedy hit Widow's Bay, among other things, has been tapped as writer, showrunner, and executive producer. This project will be the latest addition to Netflix's ever-growing catalog of adult animation, which, to this point, has taken on games like Devil May Cry, Castlevania, and League of Legends and even ventured into the cinematic realm of King Kong with Skull Island.

First published in 1982, Grendel follows Hunter Rose, a successful author who becomes a masked vigilante after the love of his life is horrifically murdered. He dedicates his life to seeking vengeance, tearing through "street gangs, the elite, and New York City’s supernatural underworld" to find and put an end to her killer. During his quest, however, the opportunity to pursue a real life arises, forcing him to make a choice that could either save him or inevitably turn him into the very monster he's been hunting the whole time. Wagner issued an official statement praising the new project as a faithful embrace of his original comic from the storytelling to the action, writing:

"It’s incredibly exciting to see my creation finally make this leap to the animated screen! Netflix’s dynamic new series retains the source material’s dark adult themes and also brings a fresh and thrilling approach to the narrative. This is Grendel as I’ve always wanted to see the character portrayed — a hyper-kinetic level of action, bracing explosions of violence, and a poignant, engaging storyline.”

Handling the animation for Netflix's Grendel series is Bobbypills, the studio best known for their work on James Gunn's DC Creature Commandos series. They're familiar partners to Netflix, having previously been involved with Adi Shankar's Ubisoft mash-up Captain Laserhawk: A Blood Dragon Remix. Chris Tongue, Tim Wiesch, and Jay Komas also serve as executive producers for Dark Horse Entertainment, joining Lydia Antonini and Wagner. In a separate statement, Esfarjani expressed his gratitude and excitement to the entire team working with him on the comic adaptation, adding:

“Being able to bring a character as iconic as Grendel to life has been a true thrill. Having the support of Matt Wagner, my great EPs, and the awesome team of Netflix, has made it all the more exciting, and partnering with Bobbypills Studio has made it all a true joy.”
