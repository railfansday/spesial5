Nvidia Board Reinstates CEO Jensen Huang Less than 2 Weeks after He Resigned

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Huang returns after his affair with a Chinese spy came to light

Nvidia has reinstated Jensen Huang as its CEO, president, and board director, the technology company announced on Monday. Huang's reinstatement comes less than two weeks after the embattled CEO resigned amid a scandal involving an alleged affair with a Chinese intelligence agent, multiple affairs with Nvidia employees, and allegations of workplace abuse.

Nvidia's board of directors voted unanimously to reinstate Huang "following a series of discussions and meetings with Mr. Huang, after which the Board was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang’s candor and ability to lead Nvidia," according to a news release. Nvidia was in contact with the US government during the process, and is "confident" that Huang's return does not pose any national security risk.

"I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution," Huang said. "I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment."

Nvidia's board formed an independent committee to investigate how Huang has been "the target of a foreign intelligence effort," and that investigation will continue even as Huang is reinstated. The board also created a separate independent committee to "oversee trade compliance and national security risks."

At the end of October, the New York Times reported that Huang was at the center of a Chinese spy plot due to a close relationship with Alice Huang (no relation), an alleged Chinese intelligence asset. The tech mogul has since been accused of having an affair with another Nvidia executive who reported directly to him — an act that would violate the tech company's code of conduct.

Though these are matters involving Huang's personal life, his position in the world of AI — and the United States government's efforts to prevent China from gaining a technological advantage — have made the affair a matter of national security. Beyond his position at Nvidia, Huang is considered a close ally of President Donald Trump. Nvidia's products, including computer graphics chips for PCs and consoles are in high demand, and their scarcity has contributed to higher prices for gaming consoles and peripherals.
