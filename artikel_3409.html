Hulu’s 6-Part Murder Mystery Series Officially Returns This December

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Hulu is quietly building its reputation as the perfect destination for comedy fans. Many of its comedy shows over the past several years have been positively received by fans and critics. From Deli Boys to The Great and, recently, Phony, the streamer is doing something right. However, one of its comedies towers over the others in critical reception and longevity. With five seasons and an average score of 95% on Rotten Tomatoes, the dramedy Only Murders in the Building is Hulu's undisputed flagship mystery series. Now, the show is set to return for a sixth season that shakes things up by trading the familiar halls of Manhattan's Arconia for a brand-new setting.

Unofficially rebranded as Only Murders in London, the upcoming sixth season sends amateur sleuths Charles (Steve Martin), Oliver (Martin Short), and Mabel (Selena Gomez) to the British capital to untangle their latest homicide. For five seasons, the whodunnit thrived on the hyper-local charm of New York's Upper West Side and its eccentric building residents. But after five murders, the premise has started to strain believability, and the upcoming season aims to shake things up and possibly counter the slight decline in acclaim.

Relocating the trio to London injects fresh cultural friction, new suspects, and a new brand of humor into a formula that has consistently captivated audiences. Yet, regardless of the accents and architecture, the series' true secret weapon remains the effortless, intergenerational comedic chemistry between its three main characters, as captured in the Only Murders in the Building Season 6 trailer. The new season officially arrives with a two-episode premiere on Tuesday, December 8.

Britain makes more detective television than any country has any business making, and somehow almost all of it is good. Here is the one you belong in, what it says about you, and the case for it being the best of the four.

You want the tea, the gossip and the vicar behaving oddly. Midsomer is a county where the scenery is beautiful, the fete is on Saturday and the murder rate is roughly that of a small war, and absolutely nobody involved thinks this is strange.

You feel other people's grief as if it were handed to you personally. Broadchurch is less a whodunnit than a study of what a small town does to itself when it finds out that the answer was always going to be somebody everybody already knew.

You go through things rather than around them. John Luther is the best detective in London and a liability to everybody who works with him, and the show is completely honest that those two facts are the same fact.

You say the thing and you do not dress it up. Vera Stanhope turns up in a hat and a mac, is rude to almost everybody, and is the only person in the room prepared to ask the question that the family has been waiting years for somebody to ask.

Cinda Canning (Tina Fey) is dead, and despite being a competitor, she needs them. In the Season 6 premiere "Changing of the Guard," the trio is "forced to reckon with the fact that Cinda Canning called upon them to solve her murder. Returning to where she so hastily departed – London – the trio look for evidence whilst meeting an eccentric cast of residents in a whole new building… as danger lurks just outside their doorstep." The season welcomes a slew of British talent, including Olivia Colman, David Tennant, Nicola Coughlan, Martin Freeman, Peter Capaldi, Jodie Whittaker, Simone Ashley, Richard E. Grant, and more. The investigation is fully underway in Episode 2, "The Girl with the Curls." The official longline teases:

"After a disturbing discovery and a disastrous prison visit, the trio dig deeper into Cinda Canning's final investigation. As clues pull them from the tabloids to a royal estate, the trio begin to question everything they thought they knew about the case… and realize they may not be the only ones searching for answers."

Catch the two-episode premiere of Only Murders in the Building on Tuesday, December 8, and stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
