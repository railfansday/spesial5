High Potential Season 3 Needs 1 Gimmick To Solve Its Tragic Morgan Cliffhanger

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The return of High Potential in less than two months needs to include one narrative device in order to move past its season 2 cliffhanger ending. Despite High Potential usually premiering in September, it wasn’t part of ABC’s Fall 2026 lineup. Instead, RJ Decker took over its Tuesday evening timeslot for little under two months, offering a similar approach to High Potential’s in the way it belonged to both comedy and drama despite falling squarely in the police procedural genre, given R.J.’s different weekly PI cases.

ABC moving successful TV shows to midseason isn’t unheard of, despite High Potential never following such a strategy. Indeed, the Disney-owned network has previously moved Will Trent, Shifting Gears, and The Rookie to some success. The reason given for High Potential season 3 premiering in January by a top ABC executive was a wish for the crime drama to have fewer interruptions in its scheduling, which would inevitably be present had the TV show premiered in the fall.

High Potential season 3 is also being rewarded with a special broadcast after Super Bowl LXI, making its move to midseason feel particularly auspicious. High Potential returning for season 3 on Tuesday, January 5, 2027, nevertheless means that the ABC crime drama will have to update viewers about Captain Wagner’s likely unfavorable fate after season 2’s cliffhanger ending. It’s unclear how High Potential season 3 will go about it, but a time jump would massively help avoid the disheartenment that Wagner possibly dying in its first few minutes would bring.

High Potential season 1 ended in a cliffhanger just like season 2, and despite the threats issued by the Game Maker to Morgan’s family in the form of the cards he managed to slip in their groceries, proving he could get to them, the stakes weren’t as high. Indeed, although Morgan cut it very close, she still thwarted all of Matthew Clark’s plays in both High Potential season 1’s finale and season 2’s premiere, making him a danger but not one that Morgan couldn’t manage. Captain Wagner possibly dying as a result of helping Morgan is another thing altogether.

High Potential season 2, episodes 17 and 18 were extremely intense with Wagner saving Morgan’s life, their sharing a kiss, and confronting Wagner Sr. and Willa Quinn. High Potential season 2 ending with Wagner in mortal peril raised the stakes even more. High Potential season 3 picking up right where season 2’s ending left off would mean having to continue with the same pacing, which was easier between seasons 1 and 2 with the stakes relatively lower but could be unbearable with a key character’s life on the line.

High Potential season 3 employing a time jump would solve the pacing issue. After all, High Potential season 3 will need to reveal what happened to Wagner, whether he dies or just leaves Major Crimes following the ordeal, as Steve Howey is officially not returning to High Potential as series regular. With a time jump, High Potential season 3’s premiere can still focus on the procedural aspects of the ABC series without having to abandon the storyline, which can be an extremely consequential one for Morgan because of the way Wagner’s fate will affect her.

Despite the doubts Soto and Karadec had about Wagner’s reasons to be part of the Roman investigation, he was the one who gave them the first real chance at finding out what went down through the name he got from his father, especially after Eric Hayworth’s death. The only reason Wagner was at the park was to help Morgan, and possibly dying as a direct consequence of the investigation will inevitably have an impact on Morgan.

ABC finally announced High Potential season 3's premiere date of early 2027, and it's extremely positive news for viewers and the network alike.

A time jump is the best way to fully explore the ramifications of Wagner’s likely demise and its effects on Morgan, as it simultaneously lets High Potential return to tell its usual stories via its weekly cases and leaves all possibilities open as to when guilt will hit Morgan and how. The promise to investigate Roman’s disappearance was what convinced Morgan to join Major Crimes, but answers wouldn’t be worth it if a colleague died, especially considering how deeply Morgan feels her emotions.

Until now, High Potential operated under the assumption that Morgan’s skills would come through and she could save anyone. Wagner possibly dying while investigating something for Morgan has the potential to affect her so profoundly that how Morgan reacts can be completely unexpected, ensuring an extremely interesting, albeit heartbreaking, High Potential season 3 as a result.
