Setting myself up for a weekend full of cheap meals

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

The city I’ve inhabited since birth is known for so many things: the ease of public transportation, the strange smells, the bustling nightlife and the incredible food are chief among them.

On that last note, that sticks out to me as one of my favorite aspects of New York City. Everywhere I look, there’s your average hot dog vendor, a standout food truck, a NYC restaurant staple or a hidden gem that’s sure to blow up once it gets shared across IG. I’m prone to eating with my eyes and not thinking about how full I can get after just a few bites (especially since my metabolism isn’t what it once was).

Even so, I still love to go adventuring in my city with a friend in tow to enjoy all the good eats littered across every borough. Since I’m planning to enjoy that same activity this weekend with one of my closest (and always hungry!) buddies, I came to ChatGPT and Gemini with a simple request: find me the best and most affordable places I can eat at with a $100 budget in mind.

Here’s which chatbot gave me the best cheap eateries and the prompt you can put in place to try it out for yourself.

With this prompt, I requested ChatGPT’s help with locating a specific number of affordable spots to grab some grub at:

“Find me 7 of the best cheap restaurants in all five NYC boroughs for a total budget of $100, prioritizing filling meals. Include specific orders, current prices and a map.” Of course, you can change the number and location in that prompt to make it personalized to your preferences.

ChatGPT took note of my request and laid out a solid selection of dishes worth taking a couple of train transfers for: You'll get Chinese roast pork, Puerto Rican chicken and rice, Nepalese dumplings, Sri Lankan kottu roti, and more. After laying out a map of each location alongside its numbered review scores, the chatbot proceeded to detail its seven picks and an estimated price for each location’s best menu selection:

When it was Gemini’s turn, Google’s chatbot acknowledged my hunger for cheap, carb-heavy, protein-packed portions that my fellow culinary adventurer and I could enjoy.

And while it didn’t showcase a map as descriptive as ChatGPT’s, Gemini’s version still proved to be helpful since it laid out which trains and ferries I could take to get to each location.

Gemini produced its list of recommendations and delivered it in the form of an easy-to-read chart that highlighted the restaurant’s name, location, a specific order I should consider getting and its estimated price. A good example of that format is how Gemini listed an Italian restaurant located in Gravesend, Brooklyn that could fill me and my friend up with 2 Sicilian "Upside Down" Square Slices for a total of $9.

Underneath a section entitled “What Makes Each Spot Unbeatable & Filling,” Gemini went into further detail about the inexpensive eateries it chose for me:

Besides both chatbots generating the best and cheapest NYC food options I should consider, both ChatGPT and Gemini also gave me a budget breakdown of every meal.

ChatGPT also factored in the leftover money I’d have before public transportation fees, the taxes attached to each meal and any tips me & my hungry cohort may leave behind. Gemini’s aforementioned chart produced an estimated total for its recommended restaurants and meals, plus an approximate amount of money I’d have left over for sales tax, cash tips and subway swipes.

Out of the two chatbots, I ended up adding most of the suggestions Gemini brought forth. While ChatGPT and Gemini both mentioned Wah Fung No.1 Fast Food, 188 Bakery Cuchifritos and New Asha Sri Lanka Restaurant, Gemini got the nod with its suggestions since ChatGPT recommended an average halal food vendor that I can find anywhere in the city. Gemini also brought up a halal food truck, but the fact that it has a signature sauce and was given an esteemed culinary achievement made me consider it more than ChatGPT’s option.

Both chatbots gave me plenty of great choices to choose from besides that one place, but Gemini’s options made my stomach growl a bit more. Gemini’s mention of a pizza spot alongside its other cheap food selections ended up being the deciding factor for me to make a delicious playlist out of its endorsements.
