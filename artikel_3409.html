Pokémon Go 'Twilight Wishes' event guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Pokémon Go’s "Twilight Wishes" event is here to wrap up the "Twilight Trails" season. Running from Nov. 24-30, this event brings back the Pokémon featured in the previous events throughout the season, and marks the return of Espeon and Umbreon in their themed scarfs, as well as Pikachu in their sun and moon crowns.

During the event, there'll be an increased chance to find shiny Staryu and shiny Elgyem, so keep your eye out for those. The other event bonuses are tied to the Go Pass, as usual.

Below, we list out all the perks of Pokémon Go's "Twilight Wishes" event, including the Go Pass, Field Research, and more.

This event features a Go Pass with 30 ranks (plus 30 more bonus ranks). You can unlock encounters with a lot of the featured Pokémon from this season, like Bramblin and Electrike, as well as encounters with the special costumed Pikachu and Umbreon. To get Espeon from this pass, you'll have to upgrade to the deluxe version — but don't fret, as you can find Espeon in raids.

As usual, you can rank up by winning raids, catching Pokémon, and hatching eggs. There's also a daily task that yields more points.

Upgrading to the deluxe pass will also add a Star Balloon cosmetic pose to unlock at Rank 16.

Spinning a PokéStop during the event period may yield one of these tasks. Different days will have different Field Research.
