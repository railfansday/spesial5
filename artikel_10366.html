5 books to calm AI-induced existential dread

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Artificial intelligence is transforming how we work, connect, and live. But with its extraordinary potential come some big questions about trust, human relationships, and the future of society. These five books explore the promises and perils of AI, offering fresh perspectives on a technology that’s changing our world faster than ever.

Artificial Intimacy: Who We Become When We Talk to Machines

By Sherry Turkle

Tracing our new habit of talking to machines through the human life cycle―from children’s earliest attachments to how we face death―Turkle offers both a cautionary tale and a road map for reclaiming our humanity in the age of AI. Listen to our Book Bite summary, read by author Sherry Turkle, or view on Amazon.

Trias Algorithmica: What Code Rules

By Hamilton Mann

Whether you oversee digital transformation, shape regulation, or design algorithmic tools, this book provides the conceptual and practical foundations for accountable algorithmic governance. Listen to our Book Bite summary, read by author Hamilton Mann, or view on Amazon.

The Eureka Machine: Why AI Is the Key to Unlocking a New Era of Scientific Discoveries

By Richard Socher

The most meaningful application of AI will be the automation of scientific discovery itself: a Eureka machine that combines all human knowledge, simulates billions of experiments, and generates breakthroughs. Listen to our Book Bite summary, read by author Richard Socher, or view on Amazon.

Manage the Machine: How to Harness Human-AI Collaboration at Work

By Paula Goldman

From Salesforce’s chief ethical and humane use officer, a prescriptive guide for how to integrate AI into the workplace effectively. Listen to our Book Bite summary, read by author Paula Goldman, or view on Amazon.

Human Raised: Nurturing Connection, Curiosity & Lifelong Learning in the Age of AI

By Dana Suskind

The challenge isn’t to reject AI as a parenting tool—it’s to use it in ways that strengthen, rather than substitute for, the messy, imperfect interactions children need to flourish. Listen to our Book Bite summary, read by author Dana Suskind, or view on Amazon.
