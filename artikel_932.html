Just days after making her long-awaited return to the pitch, Manchester City star Vivianne Miedema joins Ellen and Ben on this week’s episode! Fresh off Saturday’s top-of-the-table clash that saw Chelsea reclaim top spot in the WSL table, Viv opens up about the injury setbacks that have sidelined her early this season, and the emotions of being back on the pitch. The trio also unpack Arsenal versus London City Lionesses. With Arsenal’s wait for a home league win dragging on, the team ask whether the Gunners are starting to lose their spark at the Emirates. To wrap up, Ellen, Ben, and Viv preview a massive midweek in the Champions League as the league phase hits its penultimate round and two French heavyweights heading to London!
