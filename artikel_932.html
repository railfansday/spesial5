Khloe Kardashian's Holiday Lights Need to Be Seen to Be Believed

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Khloe Kardashian gave fans a look at the "fairy tale-esque" Christmas lights that the Kardashians star decked out her home with: "Everything is so magical."

Khloe Kardashian is keeping up with the holiday spirit.

After all, the Kardashians star shared a glimpse into the "razzle and dazzle" from the thousands of Christmas lights adorning her Los Angeles home this season.

"I love the holidays for so many reasons, but when there are twinkle lights everywhere, it's amazing," Kardashian began in her Nov. 9 Instagram Story. "I love. And then these hedges are lined with lights. Everything is so magical."

All the trees and bushes along her curved driveway were decorated with bright lights leading to her home, which sat beside a decked-out massive oak tree with a swing. She praised, "It is like razzle and dazzle."

As for what lighting style the reality star—who shares kids True, 8, and Tatum, 4, with ex Tristan Thompson—went for?

"I love when my house is gingerbread style, so basically, you trim the perimeter of things," she explained, noting how bright the area was now. "It's so crazy what this does, how peaceful and fairy tale-esque and gorgeous these lights make this. It is absolutely spectacular, and I could not be any happier."

Though Kardashian has continued to prove that she's always doing the holidays amazing, sweetie, she credits Kris Jenner—mom to kids Khloe, Kourtney Kardashian, Kim Kardashian and Rob Kardashian Jr., with ex Robert Kardashian Sr., as well as Kendall Jenner and Kylie Jenner with ex Caitlyn Jenner—with their traditions.

"When I think of Christmas, you are the holidays, any holiday," she said to the matriarch on her Khloe in Wonder Land podcast in December 2025. “It could be like Presidents' Day. Every morning, that was a significant thing. You were like, 'Happy Presidents' Day!' There would be a napkin and something cute in our lunchbox."

The mom of two had followed suit when she got older, adding, "That's something that I definitely copy and paste from you. And I love it."

From decorations and desserts to presents and parties, Jenner had always prioritized celebrating the holidays.

"I remember all of our Christmas Eve parties, but not necessarily one over the other," Kardashian, 42, recalled. "It's just the tradition and the consistency of them that's so safe and nostalgic and comforting. I think it's so important that you've created those safe spaces for us."

"It can be so silly, but what that does for those core memories at home, how that makes you feel and the safety of it," she added. “I want to emulate that and mirror that in my life."
