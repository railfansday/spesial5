Kick-off times and TV schedule revealed for Premier League’s January fixtures

The Premier League has revealed the kick-off times and TV schedule for its remaining January fixtures, with a number of huge match-ups moved into prime-time slots.

Liverpool host Manchester United in Mauricio Pochettino’s first game as United boss this weekend and the reverse fixture between the two bitter rivals takes place in matchweek 22, with the game being moved into the Sky Sports slot at 4.30pm on Sunday 24 January.

Earlier that day will have seen a London derby between Crystal Palace and Tottenham as one of four 2pm fixtures, while the previous day (Saturday 23 January) is headlined by Brighton vs Manchester City at 8pm on TNT Sports, with reigning champions Arsenal facing Newcastle at 5.30pm on Sky Sports that day.

The following weekend is also a blockbuster, with the Merseyside derby kicking matchweek 23 off at 12.30pm on Saturday 30 January on TNT Sports, while Sky’s Super Sunday match is Man City vs Arsenal at 4.30pm. Brentford and Man United will round that weekend off at 8pm on Monday 1 February on Sky.

Meanwhile matchweek 21 sees the rare occurrence of Arsenal, Man City, Liverpool and Chelsea all playing at 3pm on a Saturday (16 January) with Hull, Nottingham Forest, Crystal Palace and Sunderland the respective opponents.

Aston Villa vs Man United is the 5.30pm clash that day, while Tottenham hosting Leeds headlines Sunday’s action at 4.30pm – with both those clashes on Sky Sports.

The Premier League also confirmed that the 2027/28 season will begin on Friday 13 August 2027 with the final round of fixtures being played on Sunday 21 May 2028, with all 10 fixtures kicking off simultaneously in the normal manner.

The start date gives 75 clear days from the end of the 2026/27 season, while the last day of the 27/28 campaign – which will consist of 33 weekends and five midweek match rounds in total – comes one week before the Champions League final.

The Premier League also indicated that across Christmas and New Year, no two matchweeks will take place within 60 hours of each other to address the congested scheduled in the festive period.
