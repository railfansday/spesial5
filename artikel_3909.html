The Excellent Warhammer Survivors Reveals Roadmap Full of Free Updates, Teases Something Big

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Warhammer Survivors launched just a few days ago, and I think it's an absolutely fantastic take on the criminally addictive Vampire Survivors formula.

Imagine my glee, then, to learn that developer Auroch Digital has big plans for the game going forward.

The Bristol-based studio has revealed a chunky content roadmap that leads into next year — and there's some really good stuff on the way.

It all starts with a major update that's landing in December. It'll bring new characters, weapons, and weapon evolutions to the already packed release.

Another two admittedly lesser but still welcome updates are expected in January; one promises quality of life improvements — which is interesting in itself — and the other will add cross-save functionality, so you can carry your progress between multiple platforms.

After that, a second major update drops in early 2027. Like the one that's expected in December, this patch flushes the game with yet more new characters, weapons, and weapon evolutions. You'd best get grinding before then!

And finally, at some point in the second quarter of next year, there's going to be a "larger piece of content". I'm guessing this'll be some sort of DLC or expansion; as of right now, Auroch Digital's teasing it and nothing more.

"We have been blown away by the support of Warhammer Survivors since launch, and we want to share our appreciation for everyone who has played the game so far," the developer writes.
