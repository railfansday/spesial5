North Korea Releases Outrageous Propaganda Photos of Kim Jong Un and Daughter

North Korean state media has published a series of propaganda photos starring supreme leader Kim Jong Un and his heir apparent daughter.

The rare images published by the Korean Central News Agency (KCNA) have caused an online sensation thanks to Kim Jong Un’s outrageous pose: smoking a cigarette while sitting on a throne-like chair at a ski resort.

Kim watched as his daughter Ju Ae (although her name has never been officially confirmed) hit the slopes, smiling as she carved the snow.

The photos were taken by KCNA over the weekend, capturing Kim Jong Un and his daughter visiting Samjiyon, a tourist town that’s being built close to the Chinese border. Under construction are Samjiyon airport, a tourist railway line, and a ski resort.

According to KCNA, Kim pronounced the resort’s hotels, lodges​, and housing “flawless and worthy of top marks.”

That his daughter, Ju Ae, features prominently in the photos is telling. South Korean intelligence believes she has been selected as his heir. She is thought to be 13 years old and still in training to take over the Kim dynasty that has ruled North Korea with an iron fist for almost 80 years. She would be the fourth Kim and the first female if she were to succeed her father.

North Korean state media does not publish photos accidentally, and the fact that Ju Ae has been photographed standing shoulder to shoulder with her father carries symbolic weight.

The internet lapped up the North Korean propaganda photos, saying that despotic Kim has “aura.”
