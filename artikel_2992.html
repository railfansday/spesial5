Why Bullish Stock Crushed it on Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It topped analyst estimates in its latest reported quarter.

By Eric Volkman – Nov 18, 2026 at 7:43PM EST

The investing world was very bullish on cryptocurrency exchange operator Bullish (BLSH +5.66%) on Hump Day. That's mainly because the company posted an earnings report featuring a massive leap in headline profitability. Mr. Market rewarded this by sending Bullish's shares up by almost 6% in that trading session.

Before market open, Bullish, perhaps best known for owning the popular crypto news and analysis web portal CoinDesk, reported that its non-IFRS (adjusted) revenue in the third quarter was $84.4 million. That was up a sturdy 10% year over year, and was well above the average analyst estimate of $79.3 million.

On the bottom line, the company's net income swelled to over $576 million ($3.57 per share) from $18.5 million in the third quarter of 2025.

It was the vagaries of the crypto market that juiced Bullish's profitability. The value of its proprietary digital asset holdings flipped from an $11.4 million loss in the year-ago quarter to a $598.4 million gain. Stripping that and other one-time and unique items out of the equation, the company's adjusted net profit still recorded a gain, coming in at $18.7 million ($0.12 per share) from $13.8 million. Analysts were modeling $0.08 per share for the metric.

In the earnings release, Bullish announced the launch of a $300 million share repurchase program. It did not specify an end date for the initiative.

In the release, CEO Tom Farley signaled Bullish's strategy to grow its business via its pending acquisition. In May, the company agreed to acquire shareholder services company Equiniti in a deal valued at $4.2 billion; the transaction is expected to close this coming January. Farley aims to combine that company’s sprawling public-company shareholder registry with Bullish’s platform to offer tokenized stock trading.

I like that Bullish is active and assertive in building out its operations, and it's admirable that management is seeking ways to distinguish it in the crypto services sphere. That said, I'm not convinced tokenized securities will be a powerful motor of growth, so I'd temper my enthusiasm about its third-quarter showing.
