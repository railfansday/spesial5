Jensen Huang Resigns as Nvidia CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Nvidia's Jensen Huang resigned as CEO under pressure from the chipmaker's board after reports of two affairs came to light, including one with a woman who is alleged to be a Chinese spy. The board thanked Huang for his contributions and named Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress as interim CEO, as well as appointing Harvey Jones as chair of the board. Bloomberg's Mandeep Singh breaks down the implications for the chip giant and the AI industry at large. (Source: Bloomberg)
