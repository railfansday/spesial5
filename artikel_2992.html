Memphis Grizzlies showing Warriors the new generation

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Golden State Warriors are landing in Memphis tonight caught in a freefall, but context is everything. Steph Curry's Dubs sent Grit and Grind home in 2015 and Ja and Jaren home in 2022. Now Memphis is on its third version, and it's already won at Chase Center. Tonight the Warriors get their second look at these Grizzlies, this time without Steph.

The timing is rough. The Grizzlies are 11-4 and fifth in the West. The Warriors (8-7) limp in on a four-game skid and the second night of a back-to-back, sitting in the last play-in spot. That skid started the night Steph left Houston with an abdominal injury that turned out to be an oblique tear.

Memphis spent the last 18 months taking apart everything it had built. Grit and Grind reached the 2013 conference finals and were up 2-1 on the Warriors in 2015 before Golden State took the next three. The Ja and Jaren teams won 56 games in 2021-22, lost to the Warriors in the second round, and were done after a first-round sweep by OKC in 2025.

Since then, Desmond Bane went to Orlando, Jaren Jackson Jr. went to Utah at the deadline, and in June Ja Morant was shipped to Portland for Jerami Grant and Kris Murray. Coming off a 25-57 season, the Grizzlies added No. 3 pick Cameron Boozer to a young core with Cedric Coward and Zach Edey. Nobody expected much. Then they opened the season with six straight wins, and the rebuild turned into the surprise of the NBA.

The face of the surprise is a familiar one. Ty Jerome, the same guy Steve Kerr called a "godsend" as a two-way player in 2022-23, went from 6.9 points per game in Golden State to leading Memphis at 25.4. In the Nov. 2 overtime loss to New Orleans he dropped 40, including 23 after the third.

Neither team will look like itself tonight. The official report has Curry out with the oblique tear, Jerome out with a right hip impingement, and Draymond Green and Zach Edey out too. That leaves two questions worth watching. Can Brandin Podziemski and Yaxel Lendeborg create enough good shots without Steph and Draymond running the offense? And without Draymond in the paint, can Golden State keep Boozer off the offensive glass?

Steph has seen two versions of the Grizzlies come and go. The third already has a win at Chase. Tonight somebody else has to go into Memphis and get one back.
