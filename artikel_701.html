De Bruyne's feelings for Man City will 'never change'

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Kevin de Bruyne won six Premier League titles, the Champions League, two FA Cups and five League Cups during his time at Manchester City

Kevin de Bruyne says his feelings for Manchester City will "never change" even if the club are stripped of their titles after being found guilty of breaching the Premier League's financial rules.

De Bruyne, 35, enjoyed a trophy-laden decade at Etihad Stadium, lifting 16 trophies including the Treble in 2023 before leaving on a free transfer to join Napoli in 2025.

The Belgian returns to City for the second time on Tuesday when Napoli play them in the Champions League.

De Bruyne says he has "nothing but affection" for the club and that "will never change" even if City were forced to give up their domestic titles.

"Of course, it's a lot of things that's been going on but I cannot change anything about that. I am proud of what I have done as a football player and, honestly, this is my club," he said.

"I love the owners, I have talked about a lot of stuff outside football - I have even spoken to [chairman] Khaldoon [Al Mubarak] about how to buy a club."

City deny any wrongdoing and are appealing the decision.

In September it was revealed that an independent commission found that City had arranged "sham contracts" with a number of commercial partners as part of a scheme to disguise secret funding of more than Â£830m.

City await any sanctions that may be imposed following their guilty verdict, with possible punishments ranging from a hefty fine, points deductions, relegation from the Premier League and being stripped of titles.

Asserting its innocence, City said it had filed "a comprehensive body of irrefutable evidence [that] exists in support of all of its positions" on 2 October.

Sources have told BBC Sport that City were expected to claim that key sponsorship deals were funded by the Abu Dhabi government, rather than the club's owners, as part of an appeal.

Criticism of City has been intense, but De Bruyne said he can't "change anything" about people calling his former club a "disgrace".

"Of course, in life there are rules, but everybody plays on the limit, I would say, and for me, I played in between the white lines," he said.

"I don't think a lot of people will change in their mind how they view me as a player, or how we played, but everybody has their own opinion."

De Bruyne will be afforded a generous ovation when he returns to Etihad Stadium, having played more than 400 games for City, scoring 108 goals.

After his final home game for the club in May 2025, it was announced that a statue will be built in his honour outside the stadium.
