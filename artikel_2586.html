Jaguars-Giants boosts late Sunday window to more than 26 million

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The 4:25 p.m. ET window on Sunday continues to produce the biggest numbers of the NFL week.

Most recently, Jaguars-Giants helped generate 26.019 million viewers in the late-afternoon slot on CBS. Raiders-Broncos also kicked off at 4:25 p.m. ET on CBS.

The original schedule had Steelers-Eagles in that slot. It was shifted to 1:00 p.m. ET, with Jaguars-Giants flexed onto the bigger platform.

This week, Fox has the 4:25 p.m. ET window, with Seahawks-49ers being the top game. Also kicking off at that time is Commanders-Cardinals.

Per CBS, its 4:25 p.m. ET package has averaged 25.431 million in 2026, making it the most-watched program on TV this season.
