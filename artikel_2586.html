Caitlin Clark shows off insane color technology and special holiday edition of her Nike CC1s

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Caitlin Clark shows off insane color technology and special holiday edition of her Nike CC1s originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Caitlin Clark is getting sneaker fans ready for an upcoming release.

Clark made a guest appearance on Jason Kelce and Travis Kelce's New Heights podcast on Wednesday, Nov. 18, when she showed off her Nike CC1s and their color-changing technology.

The brothers thanked the Indiana Fever star for sending them her Nike signature shoes before she began displaying the unreleased product. She explained that the colors change from white to black

She joked that she wanted it to light up when she made a shot on the court. Clark showed that there are several modes in the shoe, like a button on the Nike logo that, when pushed, forces the shoe to change colors. She also showed that if she taps her foot, the shoe can change colors, as well as having a flashing light feature.

Jason and Travis were amazed at the shoe's color-changing technology. The retired NFL player then asked if she would ever consider actually playing basketball in the shoe, to which she responded that she would "love to" in the future.

"The technology is insane though," Travis responded.

"It's like you're on fire," Jason exclaimed.

"It's like you're a little kid," Clark jokingly corrected him, to which Jason then repeated his fire line.

Travis chimed in again, laughing, noticing that Jason was referring to the Nintendo 64 NBA Jam video game they used to play as kids. When a player would make a shot, they would be on fire in celebration as they continued to race down the court.

In addition to the color-changing shoe, she also teased a bright green shoe for Nike's Christmas line. She noted that she was inspired by Kobe Zoom 6s, which debuted on Christmas Day in 2010 when the Los Angeles Lakers played the Miami Heat.

"So Nike is doing a Holiday pack of all the signature athletes and you're either green shoe, the nice list, or red shoe, the naughty list. So obviously I'm on the nice list. You know the Kobe Grinch's? That's kinda what these remind me of. All green. They're really shiny in person too," said Clark.

Nike is showing how much they are investing in the technology of their shoes, and with Clark, as they are expanding her signature line. The WNBA star confirmed that the Cailin 2s are on the way. She commented on Ben Slamin's Instagram post last month.

"And we are already cookin on the 2," she wrote.

At this time, there is no release date for the color-changing shoes, but the green holiday-themed ones will drop near Christmas.
