Highlights: MSU basketball dominates second half, tops Arkansas on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Michigan State basketball was a turkey day winner -- but it wasn't easy by any means.

The Spartans used a dominant second half performance to rally to a 70-67 victory over Arkansas from the Little Caesars Arena in Detroit on Thanksgiving. The Spartans trailed for a good chunk of the game. But a 24-2 run in the second half gave them the edge that was good enough to pull out the win. Luckily for Michigan State, poor free throw shooting in the final minute didn't haunt them, as a last second three-point attempt from Arkansas bounced out to seal the victory.

Michigan State was led by All-American Jeremy Fears Jr., who finished with a double-double at 20 points and 10 assists. Jordan Scott also was a key factor in the win, providing stellar defense and 15 points.

The Spartans improve to 6-1 on the year with the win over Arkansas. Michigan State will next play Minnesota on Wednesday.
