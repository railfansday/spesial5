David Bailey already drawing Hall of Fame comparison 10 games into Jets career

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Demario Davis is in his 15th NFL season, and he has played with some greats.

The Jets linebacker sees traits in rookie edge rusher David Bailey that he has observed in some of those great ones. Davis praised Bailey's "process" on Monday, saying the rookie does not let outside factors affect his preparation routine, similarly to the best players he has been around.

"I don't know what all goes into his process, but I know I see him in the same spots around the building all the time," Davis said. "That type of stuff is what I've seen from guys who are now in the Hall of Fame. When you come in and you go through the week, you see them in the same spots in the building all the time, whether it was an All-Pro wide receiver or an All-Pro running back or an All-Pro quarterback, an All-Pro defensive end in Cam Jordan — a solidified Hall of Famer, one of the best I've ever seen do it — it's all about that process. Those guys have a regimen and a routine that doesn't change based on what's going on around them. That's very rare to see for a rookie."

Jets linebacker David Bailey rehabs during practice in Florham Park, N.J., Thursday, Oct. 29, 2026. Bill Kostroun for NY Post

Bailey has been a star on the field. He added two sacks against the Chargers to bring his season total to 9 ½, just shy of Hugh Douglas' Jets rookie record of 10 set in 1995. He is five away from Jevon Kearse's NFL rookie record of 14 ½, established in 1999.

Davis said Bailey has only scratched the surface of what he can do.

"He's only going to get better," the veteran linebacker said. "It's going to be scary for a lot of quarterbacks for a long time and it's going to be good for the Jets for a long time."

The Jets gave up a touchdown just before halftime Sunday when Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert ran for a 10-yard score as time expired. The Jets showed an interesting defense on the play, with three players rushing and eight players lined up on the goal line. Herbert took off, and the Jets could not bring him down. If they had tackled him, the Chargers might have been unable to even kick a field goal. Both Dane Belton and Davis missed tackles on the play.

"They had a chance to try to score on a throwing play and we had guys in the end zone," head coach Aaron Glenn said, "but then again, if he had a chance to run it, we had guys able to converge on the ball and I think if you look at that play, I think we had two missed tackles, which would've put them in a tough situation with six seconds to try to run the ball in that situation. The thing is we just got to make that tackle. We make that tackle; we put ourselves in a good position."
