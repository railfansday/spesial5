Masked Ethan Brown shines in Illinois win

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

CHAMPAIGN, Ill. — Just four days ago, Illinois freshman guard Ethan Brown broke his nose in multiple different places.

Two days ago, he got surgery to repair that broken nose, causing many to speculate that he may take some time off to rest and recover.

Friday night, Brown returned to the Illini starting lineup without skipping a beat, playing a key role in the team's big win over the Western Kentucky Hilltoppers.

First and foremost, it was a remarkable efficient night for the young guard from a scoring standpoint. He finished with 22 points while shooting 8-12 from the field, and 4-7 from downtown.

He also stuffed the stat sheet, adding eight rebounds and four assists.

Like usual, Brown played beyond his years in terms of making winning plays.

In one sequence in the first half, he cleaned up a missed shot from Zavier Zens with an offensive rebound and putback on one possession, then dished out a clean pass to a cutting Zvonimir Ivisic for a transition dunk shortly after.

Brown has been instrumental through the team's first eight games, as he leads Illinois in both assists and rebounds, while also being a double-digit scorer on average.
