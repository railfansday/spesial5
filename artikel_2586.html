Ricky Rudd Revisits His Richmond Rivalry With Kevin Harvick Two Decades Later

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Ricky Rudd never got out of his car the night Kevin Harvick climbed on top of his own car next to it at Richmond in 2003. More than two decades later, Rudd says it would have been more than a quick fight if he had.

Rudd talked about the feud on The Scene Vault Podcast, in a clip the show posted on X on Nov. 23.

"Well, I don't know. It'd probably have been. I don't think it'd been a one punch fight, I don't think. I mean, it might've gone on a while. You know, I had Pat Tryson, probably the smart thing, he's standing at my car window, wouldn't let me get out," Rudd said.

Tryson was Rudd's crew chief at Wood Brothers Racing.

The host had described the scene in his question, saying Harvick "jumped up after the crew guys kind of cleaned things out. He was a little late to the game."

The rivalry started two years earlier at the same track. In the Sept. 8, 2001, race at Richmond, rookie Harvick bumped Rudd's No. 28 Ford with fewer than 20 laps left.

Rudd answered with a nudge of his own and passed Harvick with six laps to go to win, giving Robert Yates Racing its 50th victory as an organization.

They met again at Richmond on Sept. 6, 2003. Rudd, by then driving the No. 21 Ford for the Wood Brothers, was running third when he hit the rear bumper of Harvick's No. 29 Chevrolet with fewer than 10 laps left.

Harvick hit the Turn 1 wall and fell to 16th. Ryan Newman won the race.

Harvick parked beside Rudd on pit road and climbed on top of his own car. Crew members from Richard Childress Racing and Wood Brothers Racing crowded in before NASCAR officials separated them. Harvick crew members damaged the hood of Rudd's car.

NASCAR fined Harvick $35,000, suspended two of his crew members and fined two others. Rudd was not penalized, and Tryson was fined $5,000 for improper language.

Harvick later said Richard Childress made him pay all of the team's fines himself, which he said came to $155,000.

Rudd retired from full-time NASCAR Cup racing after the 2007 season with 23 wins. Harvick won 60 Cup races and the 2014 championship, and he will be inducted into the NASCAR Hall of Fame in January as part of the Class of 2027. He now co-owns the CARS Tour, a late model racing series.

Harvick was part of another well-known post-race fight in 2014 at Texas, when he shoved Brad Keselowski from behind as Jeff Gordon confronted Keselowski over late contact.

The host had told Rudd he "really, really, really wanted you to be able to get to Kevin and see what might happen."
