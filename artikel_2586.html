Inter Milan star amazed by Real Madrid&#8217;s Jude Bellingham: &#8216;He&#8217;s a complete midfielder&#8217;

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Speaking to TNT Sports Mexico, Inter Milan midfielder Nicolo Barella has heaped praise on Real Madrid superstar Jude Bellingham, hailing him as a 'complete midfielder'.

The 23-year-old England international has been in sensational form since the start of the season and has been earning praise from all quarters – teammates, opponents, former players, and pundits alike.

Now, it is Barella who has waxed lyrical over Bellingham and his brilliant run of performances for Real Madrid and the England national team.

"Jude Bellingham is good at everything. He's a complete midfielder," the Italian international said.

Barella in awe of Bellingham. (Photo by Claudio Villa/Getty Images)

Continuing, Barella added: "When you watch him play, you realize how good he is, but when you face him on the pitch, you really realize what an exceptional player he is."

Going a step further, the Inter Milan mainstay called the Real Madrid ace his favourite footballer, saying: "He's my favourite player right now."

Barella's praise for Bellingham comes in the aftermath of his spectacular displays for Real Madrid and England.

The 23-year-old midfielder has managed to score and assist seven goals in his last seven appearances for club and country, showcasing why he is considered one of the best in the game in his position.

In terrific form. (Photo by Angel Martinez/Getty Images)

Overall, this season, Bellingham has recorded a tally of seven goals and five assists from 18 matches in all competitions for Real Madrid, which includes his spectacular volleyed strike in the win over PSV Eindhoven earlier in the week.

Having endured a difficult last season due to injuries and instability in the team, the England international appears to be back to his best under new manager Jose Mourinho.

Given his form and growing stature as one of the leaders of the team, Real Madrid have already begun working on a new long-term contract for Bellingham.

The Merengues have recently renewed the contracts of Arda Guler, Aurelien Tchouameni, and Thibaut Courtois, and Bellingham is seen as next in line to pen a new deal, with a verbal agreement reportedly in place already.
