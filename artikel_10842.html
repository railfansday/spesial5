Woman “devastated” after paying $63k for ‘endless’ cruise that was cancelled before she boarded

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

An Australian woman has revealed that she spent over $63,000 to go on the “endless” Villa Vie Odyssey cruise, but it was cancelled before she even boarded.

Back on October 27, passengers on board the Villa Vie Odyssey cruise were told to get off while the ship was docked up in Sihanoukville, Cambodia. The “residential” cruise was supposed to last for over three years, but was cut short by almost 12 months.

Villa Vie Residences blamed the rising cost of fuel for cutting the journey short, saying prices have jumped from $1,800 per cabin per month to around $8,000, and they refused to pass that onto passengers.

The abrupt ending has left some passengers looking for answers, especially those who sold their homes and spent $1 million to travel around the globe.

That includes Lisa McKay, a retiree from Queensland, Australia, who was set to board the ship in the coming weeks.

As per ABC, McKay, had paid over $63,000/$45,000 US to take a “trip of a lifetime” aboard the Odyssey. However, the cancellation has meant that her planned boarding date of November 15 has been scrapped.

“I don’t have that money to waste and lose. It’s just been devastating, absolutely devastating,” she told ABC. “My chances of getting my money back [are] very, very slim. Travel insurance doesn’t cover boutique ships.”

According to the report, the former teacher did have concerns about the cruise operator given some negative reviews online, but was told that bookings wouldn’t be offered if it wasn’t going to go ahead. Hence why she paid up.

Other passengers have described the ship’s last few weeks as being “tense,” with a “split” onboard between different groups.

One called it an “emotionally exhausting” period despite not being on board for a few weeks.

It remains to be seen if the cruises will run again, or if they will be stopped for good, seeing as they planned for a second residential cruise ship to enter service in early 2029.
