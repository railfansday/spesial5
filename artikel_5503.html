Neonode Reports Quarter Ended September 30, 2026 Financial Results

STOCKHOLM, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Neonode Inc. (NASDAQ: NEON) ("Neonode" or the "Company") today reported financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

FINANCIAL OVERVIEW FOR THE QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2026

Revenues from continuing operations for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $0.5 million, an increase of 24.4% compared to the same period in 2025. License revenues were $0.4 million, an increase of 9.4% compared to the same period in 2025. The increase was mainly due to new license agreements. Revenues from non-recurring engineering for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $91,000, an increase of 279.2% compared to the same period in 2025. The increase was the result of increased project deliveries.

Operating expenses from continuing operations for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $1.8 million, a decrease of 13.2% compared to the same period in 2025. The decrease was mainly related to lower payroll and related costs and lower professional fees.

Loss from continuing operations for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $1.1 million, or $0.07 per share, compared to an income from continuing operations of $13.9 million, or $0.83 per share, for the same period in 2025, which included the net gain from the patent assignment of $15.5 million.

Cash used by operations was $1.6 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $1.5 million for the same quarter in 2025. The increase was primarily due to a decrease in accounts payable, accrued payroll and employee benefits.

Cash and accounts receivable totaled $20.2 million and working capital for continuing operations was $19.2 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $25.8 million and $24.1 million as of December 31, 2025, respectively. Our financial position and liquidity provide stability and enable us to execute our strategy to secure more licensing opportunities for our innovative technologies.
