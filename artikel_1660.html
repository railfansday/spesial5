Jacobone: Milan and Juventus launch ‘preliminary enquiries’ for Man Utd striker Sesko

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

AC Milan and Juventus are both keeping tabs on Benjamin Sesko’s situation at Manchester United, according to a report.

United paid around €80m to sign Sesko from RB Leipzig, hoping he would be the long-term fix for the No.9 position. Last season he performed reasonably well, scoring 11 goals in 30 Premier League games which was not bad for a 22-year-old in a new league.

This season, however, he is stuck on one goal in four league games, having spent time out injured too. With big changes at Old Trafford – including the arrival of Mauricio Pochettino as the head coach – it is another blank slate for the squad.

For example, club captain Bruno Fernandes has been linked with a move to Milan. Now, according to journalist Alessandro Jacobone, a new idea has emerged for the centre-forward position, perhaps if the pursuit of Nicolo Tresoldi does not go to plan.

“Juve and Milan are keeping an eye on things. But summer is the most realistic scenario. Benjamin Šeško could leave Manchester United, with Juventus and Milan having already made some preliminary enquiries to assess the financial feasibility of a potential move,” he said.

“For now, these are only preliminary discussions, useful for finding out the figures, conditions and possible deal structures. No concrete negotiations have been initiated.

“Sources close to the player’s camp are also playing down the possibility of a transfer as early as January, making a potential move in the next summer window considerably more likely. One to watch.”

Four of those 11 goals in his debut campaign came off the bench too as he developed a knack for being an impact substitute. This could give Milan food for thought, given they are looking for an alter-ego to Goncalo Ramos rather than someone to solely take over the striker role.
