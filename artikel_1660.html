Bassett urges Portland to ‘set the tone’ in must-win clash with Dallas

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Portland Timbers midfielder Cole Bassett believes they must set the tone against FC Dallas in Game 2 of their MLS Cup Playoff clash.

Dallas cruised to a 3-0 victory in Game 1, with Joaquin Valiente scoring a brace before Sam Sarver added a third goal in second-half stoppage time.

That defeat saw Portland's four-game winning run in MLS play come to an end, but they can keep the series alive with a victory at Providence Park on Saturday.

Bassett has urged his team-mates to make a fast start in front of their home fans, insisting an early goal could make the difference.

"We're in professional sports here. This is a big game, a must-win," Bassett told reporters.

"And you have to play, because all it takes is one moment, at the end of the day, to turn it around.

"We love playing in front of them [the fans], and we know Providence Park is going to be rocking this weekend.

"But there are little things that I think can help: the first duel, first tackle, kind of setting the tone for the match.

"One of the keys against this Dallas team is scoring. For us, an early goal or a first-half goal is huge."

Portland head coach Marti Cifuentes added: "I do know that in the playoffs, [with] every kind of adversity, you need to be mentally strong to approach that.

"And I think this group has been showing that. So, of course, we're going to learn from what happened in Dallas.

"We need to make sure that all that anger that you feel after losing a game, that you need to be ready to push.

"What better occasion than to do it at home in front of our fans, who always help us?"

Only four Timbers players have more postseason goals in the club's MLS history: Diego Valeri (six), Sebastian Blanco (five), Dairon Asprilla (four) and Felipe Mora (four).

In the first match of this series with Portland, Valiente became the fourth Dallas player to score a brace in a playoff game.

Valiente joined Carlos Ruiz (2005), Ariel Graziani (1999) and Dante Washington (1997) as the Dallas players to score multiple goals in a playoff match.

While Dallas stormed to victory in Game 1, the Opta supercomputer has predicted a closer matchup on Saturday, in a match which would be decided by penalties if it ends even after 90 minutes.

Dallas' 3–0 win in Game 1 was tied for their largest margin of victory in a playoff match. The club also secured 3–0 victories against the LA Galaxy in the 2010 conference finals and the 1997 conference semi-finals.

Dallas are now unbeaten in their last seven matches (W4 D3) against Portland and earned their first postseason victory in five tries (D2 L2). Dallas have won just once in 18 road games against the Timbers (D8 L9), with that victory coming in April 2016.

Portland have been shut out in back-to-back playoff games for the first time in club history. The Timbers allowed at least three goals in both of those defeats, joining Inter Miami (2020–22) as the only teams in MLS Cup playoff history with consecutive shutout defeats by three or more goals.
