Buildkite: Senior Customer Success Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Buildkite's CI platform is trusted by the world's leading engineering teams, helping ship software used by more than 1 billion people every day. Companies including Canva, Meta, Mistral AI, and NVIDIA rely on Buildkite to run builds, tests, and deployments on their infrastructure, ours, or both - giving them the speed, reliability, and flexibility to ship with confidence.

As a Senior CSM at Buildkite, you'll be a senior individual contributor responsible for driving measurable business outcomes across a portfolio of Buildkite’s Enterprise customers. You are the customer’s strategic partner in value realization - accountable for the adoption of Buildkite, the outcomes the customer achieves with it and the long-term health of the partnership.

You’ll co-own each account with an Account Executive. The AE drives the growth of the partnership and owns the commercial relationship. You own the value the customer gets from what they’ve already purchased. You’ll work alongside a Technical Account Manager, who leads technical advisory on the customer’s current solutions, while the AE partners with a Solutions Architect on what the customer is evaluating next. You and the AE both build relationships at every level of the customer’s organization, each at your own angle.

You’ll join a small, senior team while Buildkite’s Customer Success function is maturing quickly and you’ll help shape the playbooks and operating model that come next. We are looking for someone who wants to improve the role, not just fill it.
