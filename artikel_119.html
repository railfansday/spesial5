Did Life Begin in an RNA World?

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Where Readers Share Great Links. Join Free

Some researchers think life may have started with self-copying RNA-like molecules that could mutate and evolve. But experiments suggest self-sustaining RNA evolution is difficult, and RNA alone may not have been enough to make life truly begin.
