Costco is recalling Kirkland Signature whipped cream in 12 states over cleaning chemical

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Members reported a chemical smell and taste in one batch; no serious adverse effects requiring medical care have been reported

Alamance Foods is recalling a batch of Costco $COST's Kirkland Signature Real Heavy Whipped Cream sold in 12 states over possible contamination with trace amounts of a sanitizing agent, the company said in a Nov. 7 notice to Costco members.

Alamance Foods said it is recalling the product "out of an abundance of caution." Members have noticed an odd chemical odor and flavor, but the company said it has received no reports of serious health problems that needed a doctor's care.

The recall covers 15-ounce (425-gram) cans with Costco item No. 1777000 and item code AC50, in a case pack of 8CT/3PK. Affected cans are marked with an April 2, 2027, best-if-used-by date and a timestamp falling between 00:35 and 02:45.

Costco records show members, or their add-on members, bought the item between Oct. 11 and Oct. 31, the notice said. It was sold in Alabama, Florida, Georgia, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Missouri, Mississippi, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee and Wisconsin.

Members who have the affected product should stop using it and bring it back to a nearby Costco warehouse to get all their money back, the company said. Alamance Foods asked members with questions or concerns to email [email protected].

The notice neither named the chemical nor described how it may have ended up in the whipped cream, according to the San Francisco Chronicle.

The recall arrives as Thanksgiving approaches, a holiday when whipped cream is a common finishing touch for pumpkin pie, according to Fox Business.
