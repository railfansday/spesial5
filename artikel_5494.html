Armada Named a Priority Team Leader in the American SI Exports Program

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

American Sovereign SI Stack, built on the Armada Sovereign SI Grid, gives allied nations super intelligence infrastructure they own, operate, and control, in months, not years

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Armada, the Sovereign SI Infrastructure company, today was named a priority team leader in the American SI Exports Program, the U.S. Department of Commerce's national accelerator for bringing complete American Super Intelligence (SI) technology solutions to global markets. Armada leads the American Sovereign SI Stack, a consortium with Microsoft and Dell.

Nations and enterprises that want to own their intelligence face the same infrastructure gap. Traditional data centers take years to build, concentrate capacity in single points of failure, and leave critical operations dependent on providers, networks, and jurisdictions they don't control. The result is customers who are delayed, exposed, and dependent.

The American Sovereign SI Stack closes that gap. It is built on the Armada Sovereign SI Grid, a distributed network of factory-built modular data centers that customers own, operate, and run as one system. The stack combines Armada's Galleon and Leviathan modular data centers and the Armada Platform with Dell compute and storage and Microsoft Azure Local and Foundry Local.

Armada and WinDC, the Australian SI factory and colocation provider, have signed a letter of intent to expand their partnership, turning curtailed wind and solar power at Australia's renewable energy sites into tokens. The companies plan approximately 11 MW on Armada Leviathans across five Australian states, a further 230 MW targeted for 2027, and 3 GW across approximately 40 sites in Australia and New Zealand by the end of 2030. They estimate more than $30 billion in project investment, including more than $20 billion in Armada's U.S.-manufactured infrastructure.

Speed, scale, and sovereignty for U.S. allies and partners

Speed to compute. Galleons and Leviathans are fabricated, not constructed. Every unit ships with compute, power, cooling, networking, and connectivity tested as one and goes where power is available now, including stranded, renewable, and behind-the-meter sources. Customers go from available power to new compute capacity in months.

Scale with demand. A customer can start with a single site and grow the grid as demand does, from a suitcase-sized Galleon for inference where the work happens to 10 MW Leviathans for fine-tuning and inference at factory scale. The Armada Platform places workloads, balances load, and fails over across every site from a single control plane.

Sovereignty at every layer. Customers own the hardware, the data, the models, and the keys, inside boundaries they define: in-region, private, or fully air-gapped. The stack is built on an open architecture, so customers keep the partners they already trust.

Through the Program, designated consortia receive U.S. Government advocacy for strategic projects, prioritized processing of export license applications at the Bureau of Industry and Security, and priority access to federal financing referrals. U.S. Government representatives are also available to facilitate deals between trusted foreign buyers and designated consortia. Designated packages, including Armada's, are listed on a "menu" that U.S. and Foreign Commercial Service representatives will offer to foreign buyers globally.

Armada manufactures its American-designed modular data centers in the United States at Galleon Forge One in Gilbert, Arizona, creating the jobs and supply chain the SI race depends on. In Europe, they are manufactured at Galleon Forge Two in Dublin, Ireland.

"Every nation now has to decide who controls its intelligence. Until today, the options were to rent it from someone else or wait years to build it," said Dan Wright, Co-founder and CEO of Armada. "Our Galleons and Leviathans are fabricated, not constructed, so a country can go from signature to sovereign SI in months, not years, on infrastructure it owns, operates, and controls. This designation puts U.S. Government advocacy, export licensing, and financing support behind the allies and partners who make that choice."

Armada is the Sovereign SI Infrastructure company. The Armada Sovereign SI Grid is a distributed network of factory-built modular data centers that organizations own, operate, and run as one system, from suitcase-sized Galleons for inference where the work happens to 10 MW Leviathans for fine-tuning and inference at factory scale, delivering speed to compute, scale with demand, and sovereignty at every layer. Founded in 2022 and headquartered in San Francisco, Armada manufactures its modular data centers at Galleon Forge One in Gilbert, Arizona, and Galleon Forge Two in Dublin, Ireland, in partnership with Johnson Controls.

Armada is backed by investors including Founders Fund, Lux Capital, Shield Capital, 8090 Industries, and M12. Learn more at www.armada.ai.
