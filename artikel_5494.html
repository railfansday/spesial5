The ‘Twilight’ Home in Forks, WA, Is Hiring a Social Media Manager—and the Job Comes With Complimentary Stays

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

First, Robert Pattinson was announced as the next James Bond. And now, the original Swan House in Forks, WA, is offering a rare opportunity to work and stay in the storied property.

“We’re looking for someone who genuinely loves Twilight, understands the fandom, and is excited about sharing the story of this very special house and the town of Forks,” the home’s owner, Christine Haning, tells Realtor.com®.

The vacancy was first announced in a Nov. 17 job listing, which calls for a “creative, ‘Twilight’-loving social media manager to help grow Bella Swan House.” The post says the team is looking to hear from anyone who loves “creating Instagram Reels, keeping up with "Twilight" fandom news, dreaming up fun content ideas, and connecting with fellow fans.”

While they’re not necessarily looking for someone with an established platform of their own—or many years of experience—Haning hopes to find someone who shares the same passion for the now-iconic vampire stories. And the paid, part-time, remote opportunity comes along with a major perk: complimentary stays at the house for content creation.

“We’re actually almost completely booked for the next 12 months, and that’s only because we don’t open our calendar any further in advance!” Haning notes. “So, the complimentary stays are a pretty unique perk. Not just because they’re free, but because getting a reservation at the house can be difficult.”

The home is available to book directly on the Swan House website, or through Airbnb. Pricing begins at $400 a night.

Haning explains that certain dates are “intentionally” blocked off throughout the year for “maintenance, photography, and content creation.” These are the times the social media manager will be able to stay at the home and experience its magic firsthand.

“I think being able to actually spend time in the house and explore Forks is such a valuable part of creating authentic content that fans will connect with,” she says.

The historic farmhouse was built in 1905, and while it’s not the St. Helens, OR, home fans may recognize from the films, the Swan House’s connection to the franchise goes back much further.

“One thing I’ve realized since taking ownership of the house and running its social media account is how few people know the history behind the books versus the movies,” Haning adds. “Most fans are familiar with the house in Oregon where the first movie was filmed, but before the movies, there were the books!”

Haning confirmed the abode is the original home that inspired Bella and Charlie Swan’s dwelling in the books—a linkage that’s been recognized by the town of Forks since 2006.

“That literary connection is what makes it so special to me,” says Haning.

Haning says the house has “so much history and character.” She purchased it in June 2025, and has worked to create a “really special blend of the books and films” in the time since.

There’s a yellow kitchen, just like Bella mentions her mom, Renee Dwyer, painted in the books. Haning also decorated one of the three bedrooms with purple bedding and wall art to closely resemble Bella’s room in the films. There's even a cardboard cutout of Edward Cullen to watch you sleep.

Charlie’s dining table makes an appearance, too.

The home sleeps eight, and has a fully-equipped kitchen, as well as a gourmet coffee and tea bar with a waffle breakfast station.

It’s also stocked with all of the “Twilight” DVDs, as well as a record player with the saga’s complete soundtrack collection.

A recent addition includes a display featuring “all the different editions of the ‘Twilight’ books,” which Haning notes includes some “rare, hard-to-find editions and memorabilia.” She says she’s having lots of fun acquiring “little treasures” to add to the home.

“What I love is that it doesn’t feel like a movie set or a themed hotel,” she says. “It’s a real, historic home in the actual town of Forks, filled with little details that fans recognize and appreciate. It brings together the literary history, the visual nostalgia of the films,and the experience of actually being in the place where the story takes place. It’s really the best of both worlds.”

Given Forks’ major role in the series, the home’s central location in the town is a major plus. The Swan House is surrounded by the Olympic Peninsula’s stunning landscape.

“It’s honestly one of the most beautiful places I’ve ever been,” says Haning, adding that the entire town has embraced the world author Stephanie Meyer created.

There are stores with “Twilight” merchandise, the real Forks High School is nearby, and the hospital even has a reserved parking spot for Dr. Carlisle Cullen. And of course, the coastal community and Native American reservation of La Push is “just down the road.”

“It’s such an immersive experience. Sometimes when I’m there, I find myself getting a little bit lost in it all, and the story starts to feel surprisingly real,” Haning gushes. “I’d love to find someone who shares that excitement and can help us bring it to life through creative content.”
