2026 BCDA Grand: Newfield Farm

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Five acres of the Newfield master plan have been designated as the Newfield Farm. The design objective of the farm was to preserve the site’s agricultural heritage while creating a place where residents and visitors can experience farming, nature, and community. Approximately 70% of trees and shrubs on Newfield Farm are native species, and the majority of landscape plantings were selected for drought tolerance.

Productive farmland, community gardens, edible landscapes, and gathering spaces are connected to create a walkable environment. A greenhouse, chicken coops, pollinator habitats, and bee houses produce agriculture that is a part of community life while sustainable farming practices support biodiversity.

Award: GrandCategory: Landscape DesignArchitect: AgmenityLandscape Architect: CultivateLANDBuilder: Mattamy HomesLocation: Palm City, Florida

Through farm tours, workshops, seasonal programming, community-supported agriculture offerings, and interactive agricultural experiences, residents can gain a deeper understanding of sustainable farming and local food systems. The farm utilizes locally produced compost, avoiding synthetic pesticides and chemical fertilizers, and incorporate low-till farming and crop rotation to support long-term soil health.

By combining food production, conservation, recreation, and community gathering, Newfield Farm demonstrates how landscape design can help strengthen community identity and create value for residents.
