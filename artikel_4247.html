"I'll Leave It To Your Imagination" - Aonuma's Keeping Quiet On Bombchu Girl's Relationship Status

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

With the Ocarina of Time launching on Switch 2 today, producers Eiji Aonuma and Hidemaro Fujibayashi have taken part in a series of interviews about the original game, its remakes and their fondest memories of it. All of them have provided a nice read and some fascinating insights into one of our all-time faves, but Game Informer has taken one for the team and asked the question that we all really want to know: what's the situation with the Bombchu Bowling girl?

Most of us will admit to having a little childhood crush on the bowling alley owner when we first played the game, and with the remake bringing everything back to life with its brand-new visual style, we'd wager that it will relight some long-forgotten fires.

Game Informer tried to get some more info about the purple-haired princess from Aonuma-san, asking, "The woman who runs the Bombchu Bowling game. What happened to her after seven years. And is she single?" Straight to the point, we respect that.

The series producer was expectedly coy in his response, mind you, only saying the following:

That's something we're not going to touch, and I'll leave it to your imagination.

Ah, we really thought we were in for some big lore drop there! Seriously though, how funny that Aonuma-san would rather not touch the question than give any ideas to those with a ~30-year-old crush.

In this week's Ask the Developer interviews, the producing pair were joined by the remake development team, talking all things OoT. We've added a selection of the tidbits we learnt from their discussion below.
