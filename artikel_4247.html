8 Best Ways to Build Lucia and Jason’s Relationship in GTA 6

Grand Theft Auto 6 has two main protagonists. They are Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, and the pair are madly in love with one another. At least, you are going to want them to be. They have a chance to be more like friends by the end of the story.

They don’t automatically start off being thicker than thieves. Instead, they have a budding relationship that you must help develop, and there are multiple ways that you can help it grow and flourish throughout the game.

You have to make sure to care about your partner, and not just treat them as any other character in the game. Do you think you can do that?

When you start up Grand Theft Auto 6, you play as Jason for a while. You don’t actually start playing as Lucia until the second chapter of the game. This is mostly because the first chapter is dedicated to her getting out of prison and adjusting to life outside.

When you get the call that she is getting out of prison soon, you need to start prepping the house if you want her to be happy. This means that you will want to fix the roof, outside steps, and the wall in the bedroom.

Each of these will help Lucia feel more at home and make her more happy when she gets out of prison, and it will give a serious boost to the relationship.

Your partner will drop hints in dialogue about things they expect you to do. For instance, Lucia and Jason will go to a bar shortly after she gets out of prison. During this time, she will ask Jason to grab her another drink.

If you don’t grab her a drink, it will be brought up in the next conversation, and it will ultimately decrease your relationship score because you didn’t listen to what she asked of you.

You need to pay attention to what your partner says, and if they ask you to do something, it is important that you go out of your way to do it so they don’t get upset.

GTA 6 removes the ability solicit, a GTA feature since 2001. Rockstar says the decision supports Jason and Lucia’s story.

Early in the game, you need to set yourself up a BuckMe account. It is this game’s version of Venmo or PayPal, which allows you to send and receive money. It also lets you save money, and that is the key.

As part of the story, Lucia and Jason are saving up money for their family. This means that they need to put money aside for the future. This will also help them open a real bank account.

Whenever you are able to, put money aside so that you can put it towards your future, as this will make your partner very happy. It shows you are being responsible.

A big part of any relationship is showing your partner that you care and that you are there for them, and you can do this in multiple ways, but one way is physical affection. This can take many forms, even the simple holding of a hand.

Whenever Jason and Lucia are together, they have the option to hold hands. You will see the option to hold your partner’s hand in the bottom right corner of the screen. When this appears, it is good to pay attention to it.

Holding hands in public shows your partner you are there with them and lets them know they can count on you. It’s a simple but important gesture.

Another means of showing your partner that you care is purchasing them gifts. Everyone loves to get a gift, as it shows that they are being thought about, and the same applies for both Lucia and Jason in GTA 6.

There are various opportunities for you to purchase gifts for your partner. For instance, Jason can purchase a necklace for Lucia at the start of the game before she gets out of prison.

Gifts don’t have to be lavish. Instead, you can simply think about what your partner is going to eat. Go out to the store and grab a couple of food items for them and they will definitely be happy about it.

Jason and Lucia are played by Dylan Rourke and Manni L. Perez, respectively.

It should go without saying that your partner should be the one you think of first when doing activities, but it might need to be spelled out for some people. Throughout the game, you have the opportunity to put the needs of your partner first or do other activities.

If you choose to do something else, they will be upset and you will lose points in your relationship. For instance, there is a time when Lucia will want Jason to talk to Mattia.

If you choose to do other things first, like hang out with Cal, Lucia will be noticeably upset with Jason that he chose to do something else before the thing she asked him to do. Putting your partner first will increase your relationship score quickly.

After a while, you will have full access to Vice City and the glorious sun-baked land of Leonida. That means that you have plenty of activities you can take part in. At this point in the game, you will be playing the game mostly as a duo, which means you can take part in activities together.

There are games that can be played like mini-golf, and then there’s other stuff like kayaking that you can do. These are all considered dates, and they are important to take part in, as they will immensely help your relationship.

As part of the dating mechanic, your partner will mention things they are interested in doing, and taking part in these activities will increase your bond, so make sure you pay attention to what they say while you are out and about.

A big part of GTA 6 is the ability to play as both Jason and Lucia. Rockstar made a big deal about being able to seamlessly switch between these two as you complete missions and explore the world.

This means that you can take on heists by yourself or you can complete the job with your partner, and it is better overall to do the latter.

Having your partner with you means that you have twice the manpower, and it also means that you can get more loot, and someone watching your back for if trouble comes. Plus, it raises your relationship status to have your partner in crime by your side.

Grand Theft Auto 6 delivers an enormous open world, a gripping story, a vibrant Vice City and an astonishing range of activities.
