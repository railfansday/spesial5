Ocarina of Time Remake May Have Just Broken One of Zelda’s Biggest Traditions

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Covering the video games industry since 2017, with experience in news, articles, and reviews. Currently a writer at DualShockers and gaming magazines.

Link has faced countless monsters, found 100 Skulltulas more than once, and saved Hyrule more times than we can count. But after all these years, one of the most recognizable things about the hero has remained largely unchanged: he rarely speaks.

That might be why a tiny detail in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake has caught the attention of fans, as it appears to give Link an actual spoken line.

The discovery was recently shared by The Legend of Zelda Updates on X (formerly Twitter), which posted a clip showing Link briefly speaking Japanese during a scene with Princess Zelda.

Nintendo has expanded one of the original Zelda: Ocarina of Time's most heartbreaking side stories.

And while it's certainly not a lengthy conversation or a dramatic speech, hearing the usually silent protagonist say something out loud is still a pretty big deal for the franchise. Here, take a look by yourself (and don’t forget to turn up the volume, of course):

Watch the clip shared by The Legend of Zelda Updates on X.

This moment takes place during the game's closing sequence, as Link and Zelda attempt to escape the collapsing castle. As the hero takes hold of the princess in his arms, he can be heard saying "さあ" (pronounced "Sā"), a Japanese expression that can be translated as "come on" or "let's go," depending on the context.

There's a catch, though: the line can only be heard when playing with Japanese audio! Players using other language options won't get to hear Link deliver this little piece of dialogue, making it an especially easy detail to miss – I, for instance, played it in English and Portuguese, and I can confirm that he was silent on both.

Some of the original's most charming quirks from Zelda: Ocarina of Time didn't make the journey.

This was quickly shared on gaming forums and social media, since it appears that the Ocarina of Time remake may have broken one of the franchise’s biggest traditions of having a silent protagonist! But it is also worth pointing out that this isn't exactly the first time Link has ever spoken. The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker, for example, features a brief “Come on!” from the hero when players call companions and even a “Meow” – and I don’t know about you, but for me, that’s pretty close to this time’s “let’s go” in the remake.

Still, Link has largely remained a silent protagonist throughout the series, with his thoughts and responses usually left to the player's imagination. So, interestingly, the remake has decided to tackle that idea again, and only in the Japanese dub. And it doesn't suddenly turn Link into a talkative protagonist, nor does it suggest that Nintendo is abandoning one of the series' most recognizable traditions. It's just a small surprise that fans probably weren't expecting to hear!

And honestly, I can understand why this has caught everyone's attention. After nearly four decades of adventures, hearing Link say even a couple of syllables feels strangely significant. Whether this was an intentional creative decision or simply a small addition to the Japanese performance, it's another example of how even the tiniest changes can get Zelda fans talking!
