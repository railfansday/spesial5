The hit Prime Video show has just been renewed for season 4

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power is officially returning for season 4, and showrunners J.D. Payne and Patrick McKay are thinking even farther ahead into the show's future.

"And there's a very clear plan for the rest. We know where we're heading. We even know what the last shot is," McKay told IGN. "There is some problem-solving we have yet to do, like how much of this? Or, how are we going to do that? But the garden of forking paths is narrow ahead. We know what we're doing next [season], and I think we have a pretty good idea what we're going to do the time after that. It does feel like we're over the hump, and it does feel like the destination is in sight.”

Season 3 focused on the forging of the One Ring, with Charlie Vickers' Sauron taking center stage. Jamie Campbell Bower also joined the season at Celeborn, and his tumultuous relationship with both Galadriel and Sauron absolutely sealed the deal for me. Vickers deserves an award for just the line delivery of, "She'll come to you, but she'll come for me" alone.

Season 3 also pulled in a whopping 33 million viewers in just 12 days on Prime Video, prompting Prime to greenlight the show for season 4. Payne and McKay initially outlined the show for five seasons... and we wonder what that final shot will be.

The Rings of Power season 3 is currently airing weekly on Prime Video, though the final two episodes are set to drop on the same day. For more on that, check out The Rings of Power season 3 release schedule.
