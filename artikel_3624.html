Dan Campbell Issues Update on Aidan Hutchinson Following Celebration Injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Detroit Lions head coach Dan Campbell discussed a shocking injury to star defensive end Aidan Hutchinson, which came during a sack celebration on Sunday, November 16.

“We’ll know more when we get back in the States,” Campbell, 50, told reporters after the Lions’ 35-10 victory over the New England Patriots in Munich, Germany, on Sunday. “Anytime you lose a player of his caliber, the type of teammate he is — he’s a captain of ours — that doesn’t make things easy. But I thought guys stepped up in his wake and showed up for us today.”

Hutchinson, 26, injured himself during the first quarter on Sunday after he jumped in the air to celebrate a sack of Patriots quarterback Drake Maye.

Maye, 24, was also hurt on the play and missed the rest of the game. ESPN reported on Monday, November 16, that Maye will miss “an extended period, and possibly the remainder of the season.”

While the Lions have not confirmed Hutchinson’s injury status, ESPN reported on Monday that he tore the ACL in his right knee and will miss the rest of the season.

Hutchinson received an initial MRI in Germany before returning home with the team on Sunday. He is expected to undergo further testing in the coming days.

The injury on Sunday is the latest in a string of unfortunate incidents for Hutchinson. In 2024, he broke the tibia and fibula in his left leg in Week 6 against the Dallas Cowboys, forcing him to miss the rest of the season, including the Lions’ playoff loss to the Washington Commanders.

Hutchinson also underwent surgery for a fracture in his right ankle suffered during his junior season at the University of Michigan.

The Lions (7-2) are currently tied for first place in the NFC North with the Minnesota Vikings and Chicago Bears. Detroit returns to action on Sunday, November 22, when they host the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

Hutchinson’s devastating injury comes just weeks after the Lions lost fellow defensive stalwart Jack Campbell.

Jack, 26, remains hospitalized after suffering a neck injury against the Vikings on November 1. The linebacker broke his silence about the incident in a statement released by the Lions on November 4.

“My family and I want to sincerely thank everyone for the overwhelming amount of love, prayers, and support we’ve received over the past few days,” Jack said. “It means more to us than words can express.”

He added, “I’m incredibly encouraged by the progress I’ve made in such a short period of time,” he announced. “I have full movement in my lower body, and I’m continuing to make progress with movement in my upper body. I know there’s still work ahead, but I remain very optimistic. I’m taking things one day at a time, staying positive, and focusing on getting better.”
