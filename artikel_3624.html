Bruce Willis' Daughter Apologizes for Sharing 'Intimate' Photo From Visit

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Tallulah Willis issued a public apology after sharing a rare new photo of her father, Bruce Willis, amid his frontotemporal dementia battle.

“Hi, I posted a photo of my dad yesterday because we were having a BEAUTIFUL visit,” Tallulah, 32, wrote via Instagram on Sunday, November 22, addressing her upload from the day prior. “I had someone take a super sweet photo of a shared moment between us watching a video of Frankie Valli singing.”

She continued, “I was very excited by the sweetness of the moment that I didn’t think farther ahead that this was possibly too intimate a photo to show. I can acknowledge that.”

Tallulah shared a photo with Bruce, 71, on Saturday, November 21, in which they sat side-by-side on a porch with the caption, “🆒😎.” She has since deleted the post.

“[I] vow to do better,” she told her Instagram followers on Sunday. “I know you all love him. I love him, and he’s still always the absolute hero we know him to be.”

Tallulah captioned her post, “I can do better.”

Bruce shares Tallulah and sisters Rumer and Scout with ex-wife Demi Moore, in addition to younger daughters with wife Emma Heming Willis. The family announced in 2022 that Bruce was diagnosed with aphasia and, later, frontotemporal dementia that forced his four-decade storied Hollywood career to come to an end.

Bruce now lives apart from his family, and with a full-time care team, as he navigates life with his diagnosis.

“It was one of the hardest decisions that I’ve had to make so far,” Heming Willis, 50, said in Emma and Bruce Willis: The Unexpected Journey – A Diane Sawyer Special, which aired in August 2025. “I knew, first and foremost, Bruce would want that for our daughters. You know, he would want them to be in a home that was more tailored to their needs, not his needs.”

Throughout his health battle, his family has remained by his side. Bruce was even present during Tallulah’s wedding weekend this summer.

“A tender moment with my dad and Justin [Acee] at the start of wedding weekend,” she captioned a wedding photo shared with Vogue.

Tallulah and Acee, 35, said “I do” at the family’s Hailey, Idaho, residence in August.

“It’s the place where I learned to run free and be the dreamer that I am today,” Tallulah told Vogue of her ceremony venue. “Justin and I have spent so much time up there that even though it has such emotional pull for me, he feels very much a part of the fabric of the property. We toyed with the idea of making it simple and eloping, but deep down, I really loved the chance to be an eleganza.”
