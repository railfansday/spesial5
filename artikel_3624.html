The holiday season may be the most wonderful time of the year, but if you’re at a loss when it comes to buying gifts for kids, it can also be the most stressful. Without a full list for Santa, coming up with ideas can leave you drawing a blank or browsing Amazon for what feels like a million hours. For instance, what do you get the tween who knows more about beauty than you do? Or the baby who barely even knows what’s happening?

Not to worry — we’ve come up with a list of holiday presents for babies, kids, tweens and teens that’ll have them convinced that guy in the red suit is the real deal. . . or, at the very least, produce a smile or two. Ahead, you’ll find the best 40 gifts for your little loved one, ranging from toys to books and even gift cards they’ll actually use.

Nothing’s better than a gift you can keep on giving, and that’s what you get with Toniebox. This interactive device is made for independent play for kiddos ages one through nine, letting them listen to stories, play games and more. Each figurine offers a unique experience, so once you've purchased the starter pack, you can keep buying new options (including Ms. Rachel or a Disney Princess bundle) for birthdays and other special occasions.

Not only does this set include the essentials like a stethoscope and a thermometer, but you also get a tiny plush dog and cat, so they can get to work right away!

In a world where so much is digital, give them a camera that prints their photos! Thanks to the three volumes of paper, it’s possible to print up to 372 black and white pics, but the included SD card also saves a color version of the shot.

Let them practice dunking from their bedroom, or get the whole fam in on the fun by hanging a hoop in the playroom. Either way, your little sports lover will have a chance to improve their skills (and parents who grew up in the ‘90s will enjoy a bit of nostalgic fun).
