The £3m-rated Scottish winger with 16-goal contributions after SPFL exit for nothing

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Dinamo Bucharest president Andrei Nicolescu has revealed they will allow Scottish winger Danny Armstrong to leave the club for £3 million....just over a year after he left Kilmarnock for free.

The 29-year-old has scored six goals and provided ten assists in just 14 league games this season and has helped Dinamo reach the top of the Romanian league

His club are trying to win the title for the first time in 20 years, but Nicolescu admitted if an offer came in for Armstrong in January that was too good to turn down, they would have to accept it.

He said: "The only way Danny Armstrong would leave in the winter would be if an offer came in that broke our expectations.

"So yes, if a bid came in for £3 million for him, he is definitely leaving because there is no way we can say no to an offer like that.

"That would still apply even if we were still challenging for the title.

"Let's be realistic. £3 million for a transfer of a player in Romanian football is a very large sum."
