Bitcoin Price Tumbles 19% Ahead of Anthropic IPO: Is SpaceX Crash Repeating?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Bitcoin news turns to Anthropic’s upcoming IPO as BTC repeats its pre-SpaceX listing decline, raising fears of another price drop.

Bitcoin news takes a new turn on Monday, November 16, as BTC trades near $64,700 with just three days to go before Anthropic’s highly anticipated Nasdaq debut under the ticker ANT. The slide looks a lot like the one before SpaceX’s record listing in June.

Anthropic plans to price its IPO on Wednesday, November 18, at $70 to $78 a share, and list on Thursday. At the top of the range, it would raise about $64 billion, the second-largest IPO ever after SpaceX’s $75 billion.

Bitcoin has fallen 19.3% since October 26, about when Anthropic’s filing went public, 24 days before the listing. We lined that up with the same 24 days before SpaceX’s debut on June 12. Three days out, Bitcoin was down 19.7% then and is down 19.3% now.

Bitcoin fell 19% into the biggest IPO ever. Now it's down 18% heading into the second biggest.

Some traders think giant IPOs pull money out of crypto as investors sell other assets to buy in. Here is what Bitcoin did before, during and after the SpaceX listing, and what that could mean for the Anthropic debut.

SpaceX made its filing public on May 20 and set a fixed price of $135 a share on June 1. Nasdaq listed its Class A shares on June 12.

The recovery came later. By September 10, 90 days after the listing, Bitcoin was back near where it started, and it reached $87,396 on September 21.

For this Bitcoin news analysis, we indexed Bitcoin’s daily closes to 24 days before each listing: May 19 for SpaceX and October 26 for Anthropic. The two lines start and end in almost the same place.

This time the slide came with its own triggers. Leveraged long traders were forced out on November 13, and Bitcoin hit $62,006 on November 15. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index fell to 10, close to the readings that marked this year’s February and July lows.

If Bitcoin follows the SpaceX script, the listing itself would bring a short relief bounce, not the bottom.

Two IPOs are not a pattern, and the timing in this Bitcoin news may be a coincidence. Both slides also had other causes, such as forced selling of leveraged bets. Still, the June playbook says the listing day is a poor guide to the bottom.

On the daily chart, where each candle covers one day, Bitcoin trades at $64,560. It sits far below its 50-day exponential moving average (EMA), an average that gives recent prices more weight, at $76,680, and its 200-day EMA at $75,977.

For readers following Bitcoin news this week, the SpaceX precedent points to a bounce around Thursday’s listing and a retest of the lows later. Whether Bitcoin follows the script depends on how much money the Anthropic deal pulls out of other markets.
