"A close decision": Klopp reveals why Thiaw plays and Schlotterbeck misses out

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Jürgen Klopp has shed light on his decision to leave Nico Schlotterbeck out of Germany's starting XI against Serbia, with Malick Thiaw getting the nod instead.

The Germany coach expects a physical challenge from their opponents, particularly in the air.

"We are expecting lots of long balls, so Thiaw and Tah have a slight advantage because of their aerial ability," Klopp explained.

The demanding schedule also played a part, with Germany facing another match just 72 hours later. Klopp stressed that the decision was far from straightforward, adding: "It was a close decision."

For Schlotterbeck, the omission is therefore not necessarily a reflection of his standing in the squad, but rather a tactical choice tailored to Serbia's expected approach.
