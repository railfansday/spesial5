Adobe unveiled Photoshop 2027 at Adobe Max, and the headline additions are two new AI-driven adjustments: Relight and Generative Restyle. Both are layer-based, so you can return to them after making other edits. There are also other AI additions, designed to save you time.

This comes alongside a host of Firefly-based AI updates, which effectively turn it into a full creative studio. You can read more about that here.

Adobe is rolling out Creative Agent, an AI agent, across several Creative Cloud apps, Photoshop included. It’s pitched at handling busywork, and can also answer how-to questions, like where a tool lives or how to complete an edit. Photoshop’s AI Assistant also gets a new AI-first workspace built around natural language prompts, with a simpler interface. You can leave it at any time to get back to the full toolset.
