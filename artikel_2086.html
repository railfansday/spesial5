Alan Shearer explains why Ezri Konsa has impressed him

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Alan Shearer believes Ezri Konsa has been the Premier League’s best player this season after an impressive start to his Arsenal career.

But the former Newcastle United striker has been impressed by the Gunners’ backline and feels Konsa should take a large amount of credit for the fact that they have not missed William Saliba so much.

Konsa’s arrival at Emirates has provided Mikel Arteta with a defender, but his performances have caused some issues for the Arsenal boss. Konsa is now a viable option for Arteta to play, as Saliba is fit again.

Shearer feels Konsa has done enough to be regarded as the best player in the division so far.

He stated in the Metro that Konsa has been effective while playing for Arsenal in the defence.

The former Newcastle player believed that Konsa has been the best player in the Premier League during this season. He pointed out the proof of the performance in the defence, arguing that people did not notice the missing Saliba until now.

Konsa has proved just how much he has grown since moving to the Emirates. Arsenal have been very strong at the back with Konsa playing a massive part.

But there is a problem with Konsa’s performances. Even if Saliba is fit he has done more than enough to make his place difficult to take.

Saliba is still one of Arsenal’s most important defenders, so Arteta will be delighted to have him back. However, he can’t ignore what Konsa has done in his absence.

It would be a tough call to leave the Englishman out, as he has been in such great form. But it means an experienced first choice defender would be left on the bench.

Konsa and Saliba could make a good partnership if they are given time to understand each other on the pitch. They will need to play regularly to do this.

Konsa has made it very hard not to pick him. He has been performing so well that he has created a dilemma for the manager.

Shearer highlights how well he has done by saying this. Arteta will have to figure out how to make the most out of his two quality defenders.
