Zverev books place in ATP Finals semi-finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Alexander Zverev beat Ben Shelton in straight sets to book his place in the knockout stages of the ATP Finals; Rafael Jodar will not progress past the groups after he suffered defeat to Arthur Fils; watch the ATP Nitto Finals live on Sky Sports Tennis

Alexander Zverev booked his spot in the knockout stages of the ATP Finals after sealing a ruthless 6-2 7-6 (7-4) victory over Ben Shelton in Turin on Tuesday night, in a rematch of this year's US Open final.

His victory means debutant Rafael Jodar will not advance to the semi-finals after the Spaniard suffered defeat to Zverev in their opening match of the tournament, before being bested 4-6 6-2 6-2 by Arthur Fils earlier in the day.

Zverev, whose service game was impeccable against Shelton, will jump to the top of the John McEnroe Group standings, having not dropped a set so far during the tournament, as he swept away the American to bring up a flawless 7-0 winning record against the 24-year-old.

"I think for me, if I focus on myself and play good tennis, the results will take care of themselves in a way," Zverev said after the match.

"The results are not what I focus on, it's about playing good tennis, and I felt like I did that today, and I did that against Rafa [Rafael Jodar] two days ago.

Shelton dropped to a record of 1-1 and will likely need to win his final group match against Jodar to advance to the semi-finals, with Fils also sitting at 1-1.

Zverev came out of the blocks quickly, breaking Shelton in the first game of the match, before following up with a second to go 4-1 up, and would see out the first set from there.

As the German looked to advance, Shelton responded, forcing Zverev to a tiebreak in the second set, but the world No 1 was too strong, outmuscling his opponent with a clinical forehand back down the American's left tramline to seal victory.

"From Zverev's point of view, it's looking very routine. It's not as if Zverev is hitting 'wonder-shots'.

"The serve is doing such a good job of consistently hitting the spots and getting so many cheap points and holding serve from the back of the court.

"He is more aggressive, he's moving the ball around, and he played smart tennis as well, and he moved forward at the right time.

"He's looking very comfortable and confident as this new world No 1."

Fils claimed his first win at the ATP Finals by rallying past fellow debutant Jodar in three sets.

The Frenchman, like Shelton, now needs a big result against top-ranked Zverev in his final group match to advance to the semi-finals.

Jodar, meanwhile, appeared to be struggling physically midway through the second set and was given medicine to mix into his water bottle during a medical timeout. He also devoured a banana, a gel and an energy bar during the next changeover.

The 20-year-old is the first man to qualify for the year-end finals in the same season in which he made his tour-level debut. He has appeared fatigued recently.

"I felt that I couldn't compete," Jodar said.

"Physically, I wasn't at the level today, especially in the last two sets.
