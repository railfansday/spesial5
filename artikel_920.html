Candace Cameron Bure Praises Daughter's Postpartum "Strength"

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

After Natasha Bure, who recently gave birth to her first baby girl with husband Bradley Steven Perry, shared insight on her health struggles, her mom Candace Cameron Bure championed her “strength.”

Everywhere Natasha Bure looks, she’s got Candace Cameron Bure’s heart and hand to hold on to.

As the Full House alum’s daughter shared insight into the health complications she’s experienced in the month since she and husband Bradley Steven Perry welcomed their daughter Winnie, she had her support system by her side.

“My beauty,” Candace—mom to Natasha, 28, as well as Lev, 26, and Maksim, 24, with husband Valeri Bure—wrote in a comment under her daughter’s Nov. 11 Instagram post. “Papa and I will always drop anything for you. You so loved and always have strength by your side.”

For her part, Natasha admitted that she “could write a novel” about the complicated feelings surrounding her six weeks postpartum.

“In the midst of the sweetest baby moments that feel like heaven on earth, there is also the uncomfortable reality of recovery,” she wrote. “It’s been the opposite of how I ever imagined this chapter. It has given me a level of respect and awe of mothers that I can’t even explain.”

Although Natasha has emphasized her daughter is a “dream” her early days of motherhood have “continuously rocked” her.

“Between the health complications, nights in the hospital without my baby, crippling anxiety, hormonal shifts, the physical toll of it all,” the singer wrote, “and trying to mentally stay afloat for myself and my family—most days still just feel like survival mode.”

But while the last few weeks have been difficult for her, The Voice alum praised the “village” of support she had.

“My sweet husband who has been my rock, my parents who drop everything to come to my rescue, and friends who have prayed over and encouraged me,” Natasha continued. “My gratitude is endless for the nurses and doctors who have literally held my hand and pushed me through each obstacle.”

Natasha also highlighted how important it was for her to share the coexisting struggles and elation that come with welcoming a new baby.

“While I am ridiculously in love with my daughter and so thankful for the gift of being her mom, it’s okay to also acknowledge that this season is unbelievably difficult,” she added, “and maybe even the hardest thing I’ve ever experienced.”

As she put it, “Both things can exist at once.”
