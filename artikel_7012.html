LaunchDarkly: Senior Product Marketing Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Teams are putting AI agents into production everywhere, and almost no one owns controlling them once they’re there. Agents make decisions at runtime, change behavior with every model update, and touch systems that were never designed for them. The teams that figure out how to govern, observe, and control agents in production will set the pace for the AI era. That’s the category we’re building.

LaunchDarkly is hiring a Senior Product Marketing Manager to own the story and go-to-market for how teams govern, observe, and control AI agents in production, a new capability within the LaunchDarkly platform. You’ll set the strategy, build the story, and be judged on pipeline and revenue, not assets shipped. You’ll work with Product and Demand Gen on go-to-market strategy, with Sales on live deals, with RevOps on pricing and packaging, and with partners and analysts on defining the category. It’s early, it’s moving fast, and you’ll have real ownership.

This is a senior individual contributor role for someone who operates with minimal direction. You’ll thrive here if you’re data-oriented and a GTM operator at heart: curious about what’s happening in the market, quick to dig into the numbers, and able to identify and size new opportunities before anyone asks. You’d rather connect what product, sales, and marketing are each seeing into one strategic direction than optimize one part of the business, and you want to be measured on business outcomes, not deliverables.
