Amorim: ‘Hutchinson changed the game more than me’ as Ramos injury concern emerges for Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ruben Amorim believes Milan’s comeback win over Olympiacos could be “the start of something”, but warned that his side still have plenty of work to do.

With quotes via MilanNews.it, the Milan coach was speaking to Sky Sport Italia and at his press conference after the Rossoneri came from 2-0 down to win 4-2 in Piraeus. Omari Hutchinson scored one goal and set up two more.

“It definitely changes our future. We did well in the second half, but we made a lot of mistakes again and we have to improve,” Amorim said. “We have a lot to do to become a very strong team, but the spirit is there. This is the start of something.”

The result gives Milan’s Europa League campaign a lifeline. They had won just one of their first four league-phase games, beating Salzburg, before tonight’s victory moved them on to six points.

“To believe it, you have to see it,” Amorim added. “Today we saw that we came back from two goals down and won 4-2. Our confidence will grow.”

Amorim insisted Hutchinson’s display wasn’t a surprise.

“I always expect this from him. He has the potential to do much more. He has talent, like other players in the squad, but it’s the head that counts,” he said.

He also played down his own role, despite the impact of his substitutes.

“I didn’t change the game. Ramos came off because he was in pain, so it was a normal substitution. Hutchinson changed the game more than I did. What changed it was the first goal,” he said.

That will be a concern for Milan. Gonçalo Ramos was also taken off as a precaution with thigh pain in Sunday’s win over Frosinone, and the Rossoneri travel to Cagliari on Monday.

Amorim was also critical of his side’s first-half build-up play, singling out Koni De Winter, who was replaced eight minutes after the break.

“We lost the ball without being under pressure. Koni sometimes put the ball into areas where there was pressure, when he should have carried it. He’s responsible for pushing the team up and leading us out.”

Fikayo Tomori, who replaced De Winter, earned praise, and Amorim admitted he had changed his mind about the defender since the start of the season.

“He has speed, which was important today, and he has experience. He can improve a lot. I’ve changed my mind compared to the start of the season, and I’m grateful for that.”

Amorim then apologised for Diego Moreira’s celebration after the fourth goal.

“It won’t happen again. Moreira is a young lad who plays, but he has to grow. We know we wouldn’t like it if they did that to our fans. It’s my responsibility, and I apologise, but he didn’t do it with bad intent.”

Really impressed by Hutchinson’s run on the first goal. It was obviously a wonderful solo effort but for me the fact he didn’t go down under the initial challenge and did everything possible to keep going was the most satisfying part. He still had everything to do at that point. You almost never see that in modern football. Players are looking for the slightest touch, ready to go down rather than taking their chances.
