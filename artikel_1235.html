Italian Media Cast Strong Djed Spence Verdict After First Few Months at Inter Milan

Djed Spence's four-year stint at Tottenham Hotspur came to an end this summer when he left the club to join Serie A giants, Inter Milan.

Despite emerging as a regular in Spurs' first team, the north London club decided to cash in on Spence in August, with the England international signing for the reigning Italian champions in a €31.5 million deal. He became one of the highest-paid English footballers abroad after putting pen-to-paper on a five-year deal worth around £90,000-per-week.

Spence was made to wait for his Inter debut due to injury. On October 10, nearly two months after signing for the club, he made his first appearance when he played the final 27 minutes in his side's comfortable victory over Parma.

"I am very happy to have made my debut in this beautiful stadium in front of these fantastic fans; they gave me an amazing welcome," Spence told Inter TV after the match.

"It was a great feeling; it has been a tough few weeks recovering from my injury, but I am happy to be back and I thank the fans."

He has since played seven more times for I Nerazzurri, starting five of those matches and coming off the bench on two occasions.

According to stats website WhoScored, he has recorded an average rating of 6.55 out of 10 from his five Serie A matches, making him the 17th highest-rated player from Inter's squad.

Spence has now been torn apart by high-profile pundits in Italy following his slow start to life at Inter. Antonio Cassano, one of the most outspoken pundits in world football, has slammed Spence following Inter's 4-4 draw with Atalanta, saying he's not good enough to play for the 21-time Italian champions. He said, per Goal:

"Spence? He's rubbish in my view. He was on the bench at Genoa, he was in and out of the team at Tottenham, then he ended up playing at the World Cup. I wasn't that keen on Dumfries, but he was useful all along the flank. I'd pick him every time.

"I'd pick [Andy] Diouf every time too. I don't understand what's going on. He used to play, he was a regular starter, and he was even called up by Zidane. Now, all of a sudden, Spence plays all the time, and he's a player who simply isn't up to it.

"He let Atalanta back into the game by giving away the corner. The ball was on his right foot to clear or, if necessary, shield. You always shield it towards the touchline. If you do the opposite, you have no understanding of football: you could play basketball, baseball, anything else. Someone who plays for Inter and England can't make a mistake like that".

Valon Behrami, who played for seven clubs in Italy including Lazio and Napoli, has also criticised Spence. He said, per Daniele Mari:

“This isn’t the NBA, where entertainment matters more than results. Spence arrived like a Hollywood actor. He’s a role player, but he showed up like a Hollywood actor, as if he were the one everyone had been waiting for.”

He added: “He’s getting back on track now, but I don’t like it when players present themselves like that. It’s hard to reach the level he thinks he’s at.”

Inter are currently third in Serie A, having amassed 29 points from their opening 12 matches. They are three points behind table-toppers Lazio.

Christian Chivu's side are in Champions League action on Wednesday evening when they welcome Bundesliga outfit VfB Stuttgart to San Siro.
