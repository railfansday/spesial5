Relive Daily Mail Sport's coverage of Tuesday's Champions League action as Arsenal clashed with Borussia Dortmund and Manchester City took on Napoli.

It was not a good night for the Premier League sides in the Champions League. Three teams played; none won.

Aston Villa kicked the evening off with a disappointing defeat away at Galatasaray, with the backing track of whistling to make it an even more miserable day for them. I can still hear it in my ears.

And then it was the turn of both Arsenal and Manchester City to struggle...

Arsenal dominated Borussia Dortmund from minute one to 90, even going 1-0 up, but somehow left the Emirates with just an extra point on the board.

Meanwhile, City had to come from behind twice in order to save themselves from a fourth defeat in seven games in all competitions.
