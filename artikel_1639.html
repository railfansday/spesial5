Camarda recalls emotion of disallowed UCL goal and Gabbia’s words: “I burst into tears”

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Francesco Camarda has spoken about his Champions League goal that never was today, and how it was an AC Milan dream come true.

Camarda first became known to Milan as a youngster, but he announced himself further when he became Serie A’s youngest-ever appearance maker. He almost then went to a European level, with a goal in the Champions League. However, it wasn’t to be.

On his debut in the competition, the starlet bagged a goal which would’ve made him the Rossoneri’s youngest-ever scorer in Europe’s main stage. Yet it was ruled out for offside and a few years on, the now 18-year-old is still waiting for a first competitive goal for the Diavolo.

Speaking to Vice, the striker opened up about his dream of playing in the Champions League at San Siro, his emotions after his goal (and it later being disallowed) and how his teammates rallied around him, as MilanVibes relayed.

“As a child, I used to say to my dad: imagine if one day I could walk into San Siro and hear the Champions League anthem, accompanied by the roar of the entire stadium. When it actually happened, I thought back to that little boy and realised I had realised a dream.

“When I stepped onto the pitch, I had an incredible adrenaline rush. Then the goal came, and I felt the whole stadium explode. I also felt the affection of my teammates, who immediately raised me into the air.

“However, when I looked at the scoreboard and saw that the VAR check was in progress, I was already convinced the goal would be disallowed.

“When I returned home that evening, with my parents and cousin, I burst into tears. I was overjoyed by the goal, but at the same time deeply disappointed by what had happened. It wasn’t easy at first, but I also worked on my mental health and managed to put that moment behind me.

“Today, I’m proud of the journey I’ve taken and of having overcome those difficulties.”

“I remember what Gabbia told me: he was sorry for what happened, but he explained that the experience should give me even more motivation. It should push me to improve, to train harder, and to work so I can relive even more wonderful emotions.”

“In football, there are positive and negative opinions; it has always been this way and will continue to be this way. A player’s true strength lies in not being influenced by what is said, because, regardless of whether you do well or badly, someone will always find a reason to judge you.”
