Flexmls Accelerates Real Estate AI With the Launch of Scout

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Glowing home icon integrated into a microchip on a digital circuit board, representing smart home automation, connected devices, IoT technology, and intelligent digital infrastructure.

FBS—the makers of Flexmls—has announced the availability of Flexmls Scout, an AI assistant that enables real estate professionals to use natural-language search to access trusted MLS information, analyze market activity, and gain actionable insights about their business.

Built directly into the Flexmls platform, FBS noted that Scout combines permissioned MLS data with AI to streamline daily workflows. Scout is available to Flexmls MLS customers as an enterprise solution.This launch marks a key milestone in Flexmls’s strategy to embed AI into the core MLS experience.

Unlike general-purpose AI assistants, FBS added that Scout operates within Flexmls’s established security and permissions framework. Users can access only the information they’re authorized to see, while MLS organizations retain control over how their data is accessed and used in accordance with their licensing terms and policies. MLS data is not retained or used to train or retrain AI models.

For more information, visit https://flexmls.com/discover/.
