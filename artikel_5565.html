Obscure CVD Billing Codes; Radiotracer Supply Recovers; Baby Heart Surgery Timing

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

— Recent developments of interest in cardiovascular medicine

Two highlights from a U.S. Government Accountability Office report of Medicare coverage for cardiovascular preventive services: Out-of-pocket costs can affect beneficiaries' use of specific services such as cardiac rehab, and many providers remain unaware of coverage for atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (CVD) risk assessment and atherosclerotic CVD risk management.

The FDA alerted to a recall of certain vascular guidewires from Heraeus Medevio due to a potential issue with their hydrophilic coating. No related serious injuries or deaths have been reported to date.

Individuals with a genetic propensity towards high LDL cholesterol were at greater cardiovascular risk with more saturated fat intake in an observational analysis. (JACC: Advances)

In a cohort of adults with prehypertension, nearly half had masked hypertension, which was in turn linked to left ventricular hypertrophy. (JAMA Network Open)

Among low-risk individuals not on antihypertensive medication, elevated blood pressure was linked to a greater prevalence of silent atherosclerosis and more active atherosclerosis progression over 6 years. (European Journal of Preventive Cardiology)

Sotatercept (Winrevair), approved for pulmonary arterial hypertension, may work beyond pulmonary vascular unloading to exert direct myocardial protection in the right ventricle. (Circulation)

Researchers developed a cardiac electrical signal analysis model that may allow implantable cardioverter-defibrillators to anticipate a severe arrhythmia event months in advance. (Circulation: Arrhythmia and Electrophysiology)

Following a shortage, Sun Radiopharma is back to producing the radiotracer Tc-99m pyrophosphate, according to an alert from the American Society of Nuclear Cardiology.

Maternal anemia and iron deficiency were associated with increased risk of acute heart failure during pregnancy and postpartum, an Indian case-control study found. (JAMA Cardiology)

It was feasible to implement pharmacogenomic testing prior to heart transplant so that patients get a list of actionable gene-drug interactions and dosage recommendations for tacrolimus, voriconazole, etc., a pilot study found. (Journal of Heart and Lung Transplantation)

A meta-analysis indicated that after an embolic stroke of undetermined source, anticoagulation was no better for secondary prevention than aspirin, though people with left atrial enlargement leaned toward benefit in a subgroup analysis. (Lancet Neurology)

Habitual physical activity before and after stroke was associated with better mental health during recovery, a registry study showed. (Stroke)

The number of U.S. adult stroke survivors is expected to double to 20 million by the year 2050, the American Heart Association announced.

Body weight doesn't necessarily dictate optimal timing for most neonatal heart surgeries, registry data showed, the Norwood procedure and arterial switch operation being exceptions. (Annals of Thoracic Surgery)
