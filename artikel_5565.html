Hypotheses Scaled. Verification Didn't. SciGround Debuts as a Mathematical Verification Layer for LLM-Driven Research

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

SciGround keeps the researcher's AI assistant as the reasoning partner and moves verification outside the model, checking design, data, analysis and claim as one chain. SciGround MCP (Public Beta) connects any MCP-capable assistant

TOKYO, Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Licklider, Inc. today launched SciGround, a mathematical verification layer for research that uses large language models (LLMs). Researchers keep their own AI assistant as the reasoning partner and interface. SciGround, outside the model, checks whether design, data, analysis and claim agree. SciGround MCP (Public Beta, v0.1.0) connects it to any assistant that supports the Model Context Protocol.

AI now produces hypotheses, analysis code and manuscript text at scale. The ability to check them has not kept pace. A July 2026 report from the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy notes that "the cost of generation has decreased exponentially, the cost of verification has not." A September editorial in Nature Computational Science observes that LLMs make reproducibility "arguably more challenging."

Each workflow has defined inputs and claims: https://licklider.ai/sciground/analyses/

"An LLM is an excellent partner to think with," said Tasuku Kobayashi, founder and CEO of Licklider. "But the proof, not the model, should speak for a conclusion. SciGround makes AI-assisted research something others can check."
