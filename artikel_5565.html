Germany's Merck KGaA is raising its annual forecast for the third time this year

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The German science company is forecasting 2026 net sales of €21.5 billion to €21.9 billion after 5.1% organic sales growth in the quarter

Merck $MRK KGaA raised its full-year 2026 guidance on Thursday, pointing to stronger core operations and a boost from exchange-rate shifts. The Darmstadt, Germany-based company expects net sales of €21.5 billion to €21.9 billion ($24.1 billion to $24.5 billion) and EBITDA excluding one-time items of €6.2 billion to €6.4 billion.

The increase is the third this year, according to The Wall Street Journal. The previous ranges were €21 billion to €21.8 billion for net sales and €5.9 billion to €6.3 billion for EBITDA excluding one-time items. Merck also expects organic net sales growth of 3% to 4% and earnings per share before one-time items of €8.30 to €8.70.

Third-quarter net sales rose to €5.6 billion from €5.3 billion a year earlier, up 5.1% on an organic basis. EBITDA excluding one-time items was €1.7 billion, up 0.4% organically. The company said the year-earlier quarter benefited from several one-time effects. Profit after income tax fell 49.9% to €450 million.

Net sales topped the consensus estimate of €5.46 billion from analysts polled by Vara Research, according to The Wall Street Journal. Merck KGaA stock climbed by up to 3.1% shortly after the Frankfurt market opened.

Process Solutions, a Life Science unit, posted organic sales growth of 10.5%. Semiconductor Solutions grew 18.7% organically, and Rare Diseases grew 41.5%. The company said incoming orders suggest weaker demand for Process Solutions in the final three months of the year.

The guidance assumes no U.S. sales of multiple sclerosis drug Mavenclad from November, compared with an earlier assumption of August, amid generic competition. Mavenclad sales fell 25.1% organically in the quarter. The guidance also assumes the Bio-Techne $TECH acquisition contributes for December, though the company said the actual closing date may differ.

"Our upgraded full-year guidance reflects our confidence" in solid performance in 2026, Chief Executive Kai Beckmann said in a statement.

Merck KGaA agreed in June to acquire Bio-Techne, a Minneapolis-based life-sciences tools supplier, in a deal valuing it at an enterprise value of about $11.3 billion. Beckmann became CEO in May, and the Bio-Techne agreement was his first significant deal in the role.

In October, the company agreed to acquire U.K. biotech Silence Therapeutics, adding the experimental polycythemia vera drug divesiran to its pipeline. That deal is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027. Merck KGaA has also signed an agreement to sell its contract development and manufacturing business to Lonza, expected to close in mid-2027.

Merck KGaA is separate from U.S.-based Merck & Co.
