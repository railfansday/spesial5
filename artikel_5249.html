Analyst Floats $13 As XRP’s ‘Most-Likely Region’

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Home » Ripple (XRP) » Analyst Floats $13 As XRP’s ‘Most-Likely Region’

Long-time crypto strategist explains the macro expansion influence over XRP’s long-term market value.

Egrag, the popular French crypto chart analyst, is floating around an idea of a double-digit XRP price tag during the next bull-cycle. The theory’s based on a macro expansion thesis visuals highlight specific price zones, including  $0.95, then $1.80 to $2 as early bullish confirmation.

He believes the weekly XRP chart presents a bullish trajectory that could develop over several years. From 2018 onward, the shared graphic projects strong macro bullishness via Elliott Wave-style counts combined with measured moves with targets up to $27 by the early 2030s.

Further, the veteran trader outlined the roughly $12 to $13 as “the most possible region”, basing this XRP prediction on the Elliott Wave theoretical rules. Abiding by those, Egrag established that the upcoming Wave 3 is normally 161.8% of the initial wave within the same structure.

The analyst has also drawn attention to the strategy used to buy & sell XRP coins. He urged his followers to treat the investment no differently than buying cars, furniture or real estate: things that require a consistent strategy & capital protection rather than capture every upswing.

Another Elliott Wave analysis pointed out that confirmation of a sustainable XRP breakout would require a five-wave advance above $1.34 resistance, potentially starting a larger impulse toward much higher targets if the structure holds.

Right now, XRP, the fourth largest digital asset by market capitalization, is trading at $1.25, soaking up a 4.20% deficit in 24 hours, according to CoinGecko. Meanwhile, the liquidation action has been intense on the Futures markets. Judging from CoinGlass’ stats, a whopping $10.83 million out of $11.36 million in liquidations were excessively-leveraged longs.

In contrast, short-sellers stood at $528.64K cumulative liquidation. If bears push XRP’s price way below $1.24, the next major demand zone lies at $1 – $1.10. These liquidity pockets have been tested before. A few months back, that range served as a trampoline to $1.69, with the same pattern occurring on both August & September, 2026.
