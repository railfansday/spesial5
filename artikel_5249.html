A multi-year ascending triangle dating back to 2017 points to potential support near $0.78 and major resistance around $3.50.

A confirmed breakout above $3.50 could set up an ambitious long-term rally toward $18, although the pattern remains unconfirmed.

XRP price could be heading toward $0.78 as a nearly decade-long chart pattern approaches a crucial test. The token has already suffered a steep correction from its October peak of $1.55, bringing its rising weekly support trendline into focus. While the $3.50 resistance remains the key barrier for a major bullish breakout, XRP may need to endure another correction before that recovery becomes possible. Will the $0.78 level become XRP’s next major bottom?

XRP’s latest correction has put a critical support level back on the radar. A weekly chart analysis identifies a large ascending triangle that has been developing since 2017, with successive market lows forming a rising support trendline and resistance holding near $3.50.

The structure suggests that buyers have consistently entered the market at higher levels during previous downturns. However, XRP has yet to secure a sustained breakout above the triangle’s upper boundary.

Now, after falling more than 43% from its October high of $1.55, XRP is moving closer to the pattern’s rising support. The analysis projects that trendline toward approximately $0.78, making it the next major level to watch if selling pressure continues.

A move toward $0.78 would represent another substantial decline from the October peak. More importantly, it would put the long-term bullish structure to its most important test in years.

The $3.50 level is more than another resistance zone. It represents the upper boundary of the multi-year ascending triangle and the price XRP would need to overcome to confirm a major bullish breakout.

A sustained weekly close above this barrier could change the broader technical outlook. However, traders would also want to see XRP revisit $3.50 and hold it as support. Such a retest would provide stronger evidence that buyers have taken control of the long-term trend.

Until then, the bullish breakout remains unconfirmed. XRP’s immediate challenge is to stop the decline and establish a stronger base before attempting another move toward its major resistance.

The $18 projection is the most ambitious part of the long-term chart setup. It represents a potential upside target if XRP completes the ascending triangle breakout and sustains its move above $3.50. The scenario would require several stages: buyers first defend the rising trendline, XRP rebuilds upward momentum, and the token eventually clears the horizontal resistance. A successful retest of $3.50 could then strengthen the case for a much larger advance.

However, the distance between the current correction and that potential target makes $18 a speculative long-term projection rather than a near-term forecast. Broader crypto market conditions and sustained demand would also determine whether such an expansion is achievable.

For now, the $0.78 support and $3.50 resistance matter far more than the distant upside target. XRP’s next major move may depend on whether buyers can defend nearly a decade of rising support before the market gets another chance to challenge $3.50.
