Dogecoin has shaved about 9% off its futures-to-spot volume ratio over 24 hours, suggesting that risk-off tendencies are taking over investors’ desire to expose themselves to volatility.

According to CoinGlass, futures traded at about eight times spot volume on November 20. The previous 24-hour reading was closer to 8.8 times spot.

Futures quite often precede a pickup in volatility. A reduction in futures positions suggests a way calmer market dynamic, as fewer leveraged positions are there to amplify price moves. Open interest fell 2.25% over the same window. Spot turnover grew faster than futures turnover, which explains why the ratio went down while trading picked up in both markets.

CoinGlass still recorded around $4.4 million in net spot selling. Futures saw around $1.4 million in net buying, even as the total value of open positions declined. The market may be taking some risk off, and spot sellers still have the upper hand. CoinGlass volume and flows

From the price standpoint, things aren’t looking that great. Dogecoin is most likely going to invalidate the $0.057 level on a daily close, after already slipping to around $0.0566 intraday. It closed around $0.0574 on the Binance chart after the November 14 sell-off. The low from that session was around $0.0554, which is where the market could look for support again.

The breakdown below $0.057 is unlikely to be critical on its own. There’s barely enough volume on the market right now to create conditions for a substantial drop. Unfortunately, it brings no positivity whatsoever. On Binance, daily spot volume was down to around $21.5 million on November 19, from $78.6 million during that sell-off.

The technical picture is still bearish. Volume is descending and RSI is back in oversold territory, as it was on November 14. The daily reading is closer to 26 now, compared with around 20 then. The 26-day EMA is also moving down below its longer-term counterparts, hinting at a continuation of the currently bearish trend.

The local decline in volume is unlikely to help the price in the medium to long term, and the possibility of the trend aggravating is still there.
