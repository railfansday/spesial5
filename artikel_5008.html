Tokenized Anthropic Holders Doubled in 3 Days

An onchain trade requires both the tokenized asset itself and an onchain payment that clears it. Most of this year's progress has been focused on the asset. Last week bank money started to move onchain too.

Last Tuesday, Fed Governor Christopher Waller said that central bank money needs to stay the foundation of tokenized markets, with stables and tokenized deposits built on top.

Singapore has made progress toward that end. MAS sold S$100M (about US$77M) of 4-week bills issued natively on its own ledger, paid in wholesale central bank digital currency. Demand was significant, with bids coming in at more than two times the amount offered, with MAS planning more issuance and wider use as collateral in 2027.

Commercial banks are seeing the value in tokenized deposits too. Ant International launched a pilot program with DBS and Franklin Templeton for a protocol that settles tokenized deposits directly against tokenized money market funds.

The assets on the other side are already onchain. Tokenized Treasury and money market funds hold $14.1B, up 55% this year, though down from a peak of about $16B in July. The drop since July stems mostly from BlackRock's BUIDL and Circle's USYC, with the rest of the market increasing to a record $10.5B. As bank and central bank money scale onchain, institutions will be able to buy tokenized assets 24/7 with regulated deposits instead of private stablecoins.

Anthropic listed on Nasdaq on Nov 19, and tokenized versions from several issuers were trading onchain the same day. Across the 4 tracked by RWA.xyz, holders doubled from 2,161 on Nov 19 to 4,335 by Nov 22. xStocks' ANTx holds $25M of the $30M in combined market value.

With one stock split across many tokens, we expect liquidity to concentrate around tokens that redeem into real shares. Alpaca provides exactly that service and has processed $10B in minting and redemption.

Tokenized sterling government debt has roughly quadrupled since the end of September, from about $20M to $83M, according to RWA.xyz. That makes sterling the third-largest currency in tokenized government debt outside the US dollar. Most of the increase came from Aviva Investors, which launched a fund on the XRP Ledger in October and seeded it with £50M of its own capital.

The UK is also planning to issue the first digital gilt on HSBC's Orion platform by Q1 2027. That will make the UK the first G7 nation to issue tokenized sovereign debt and will create a model for other governments.
