Bitcoin hedges could expire an hour before ETF trading resumes on Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The November contracts settle at 3 a.m. ET. Traders relying on them for continuous protection need their next hedge in place by then.

Deribit’s November Bitcoin options are due to expire at 3 a.m. ET on Friday, Nov. 27, an hour before early trading begins on NYSE Arca after Thanksgiving. ByKaranteli’s Monday snapshot put open interest in the expiring coin-margined contracts at about 112,000 BTC-equivalent. Traders using those contracts to hedge through Arca’s early open would need replacement coverage in place before expiry.

ByKaranteli’s Deribit chain showed 111,900 BTC-equivalent of open interest in the Nov. 27 expiry in its 07:00 UTC capture on Nov. 23. Deribit reports public open interest with an eight-hour delay, so the 07:00 UTC capture reflects older positions. Traders can also close or roll contracts before Friday, changing the amount left to settle.

A separate Nov. 23 daily reading for the same expiry put calls at 58,200 BTC-equivalent and puts at 53,800 BTC-equivalent. In the hourly capture, the $68,000 call held open interest of roughly 2,400 BTC-equivalent. The $48,000 put showed 1,100 BTC-equivalent, and the $54,000 put showed 883. ByKaranteli featured those strikes in a table of the biggest 24-hour changes in open interest. Each figure is the open interest in the listed call or put.

Calls and puts at selected Deribit Bitcoin options expiries in ByKaranteli’s Nov. 23 daily snapshot. Values are rounded BTC-equivalent open interest and can change before settlement.

Open interest counts outstanding contracts. Measuring net dealer positions or any related trade in spot Bitcoin would require separate data.

Deribit’s November monthly expiry falls at 08:00 UTC. The exchange calculates the delivery price using a time-weighted average of its Bitcoin index from 07:30 to 08:00 UTC, the final half-hour before expiry.

Under Deribit’s inverse options rules, an option with intrinsic value settles into a matching futures position at the option’s strike. That future shares the option’s expiry and cash-settles immediately in BTC. Out-of-the-money options expire worthless. For a put holder, the payout reflects where the index settled relative to the strike. Once that amount is fixed, price moves during Arca trading leave the November payout unchanged.

NYSE Arca’s equities schedule puts Friday’s early open at 4 a.m. ET and the core open at 9:30 a.m. ET. The holiday schedule moves the core close to 1 p.m. ET.

Deribit’s 07:30 to 08:00 UTC delivery-price window converts to 2:30 to 3:00 a.m. Eastern. Subtracting the 3 a.m. expiry from the 4 a.m. Arca early open gives 60 minutes. Both timelines use proportional spacing.

A suitable later-dated option already in place can keep a hedge in force between expiry and Arca’s early open. The choice of options platform affects which expiries are available and whether a replacement order can fill in time. Traders seeking protection only from the open can still put on a new hedge during that hour if their orders fill in time.
