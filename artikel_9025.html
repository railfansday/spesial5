translucent Y2K electronics inspire iridescent skin of cadillac’s F1 race car

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

cadillac wraps its MAC-26 in iridescent circuitry graphics inspired by translucent electronics for the 2026 las vegas grand prix.

The Cadillac Formula 1 Team has unveiled a translucent-look livery for its MAC-26 race car ahead of the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix. Inspired by the illuminated streets of the Las Vegas Strip, the design covers sections of the car’s familiar black-and-white bodywork with a web of iridescent circuitry. Pink and violet lines spread across the nose and sidepods to give the impression that the car’s electronic components are glowing beneath a transparent shell.

The effect is created through layered graphics applied to the car’s bodywork. From certain angles, the colored lines appear suspended beneath the surface, while soft washes of blue and purple give the finish a sense of depth. The Cadillac team will debut the design during its final American race of the season, when the cars take to the Strip Circuit under the city’s lights.

The livery of the Cadillac F1 race car brings to mind the translucent consumer electronics of the late 1990s and early 2000s, when computers and gaming accessories arrived with colorful plastic casings that revealed their internal workings. The team at Cadillac takes a similar visual approach, with fine lines resembling a circuit board running across a glassy, iridescent surface. The familiar mechanical silhouette of the Formula 1 car becomes the canvas for a graphic treatment that suggests something happening beneath its outer skin.

The design becomes especially intricate around the cockpit and engine cover, where branching lines overlap in shades of electric pink and blue. The front wing carries a softer version of the same pattern. Against the surrounding black carbon-fiber elements and white bodywork, the colorful detailing gives the MAC-26 an almost computer-generated appearance.

Beyond its Las Vegas inspiration, the circuitry is a visual reference to the work involved in establishing Cadillac’s Formula 1 operation. The team describes the livery as a tribute to the people and engineering efforts behind its debut season, which has seen an entirely new American team enter the championship. The intersecting lines suggest the complex network of systems required to bring a Formula 1 car to the grid.

This idea comes through most clearly in the close-up renderings, where the colorful pathways appear to run through the body of the car itself. The graphics play with transparency while preserving the streamlined form and aerodynamic components of the MAC-26. The effect is particularly striking across the broad white surfaces, where the circuitry stands out against the darker mechanical elements.

The Las Vegas design completes a series of special liveries introduced throughout Cadillac’s inaugural Formula 1 season. The team first revealed its asymmetrical black-and-white identity during a Super Bowl commercial in February 2026, before introducing an American-inspired design in Miami. A red, white, and blue edition followed at Silverstone for the Fourth of July, while Austin brought another variation on the team’s American identity.

For its fourth and final special livery of the year, Cadillac turns to the lights of Las Vegas and the technology behind its new racing operation. Drivers Sergio Pérez and Valtteri Bottas will bring the design to the Las Vegas Grand Prix on November 21, where the circuitry will be seen in motion against the illuminated city skyline. After the race, the cars will return to their regular black-and-white livery for the remaining rounds in Qatar and Abu Dhabi.
