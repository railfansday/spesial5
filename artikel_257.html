6,000-year-old Stone Age arrows emerge from melting ice in Norway

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Where Readers Share Great Links. Join Free

Archaeologists working in Norway’s Innlandet mountains are finding older and older artifacts as ice patches shrink, including arrowheads and shafts dated to the Stone Age and Bronze Age. After 20 years of work, the team says most ice patches with archaeological remains may disappear within two decades, revealing both rare finds and the scale of rapid melting.
