The best Black Friday trading card deals: Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, and Yu-Gi-Oh! on sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

With Black Friday here at last, Amazon has unleashed some of its biggest trading card discounts of the year, with prices on select Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, and Yu-Gi-Oh! products dropping to new lows — with highlights including the Pokémon TCG's 30th Celebration Tech Sticker Collection falling below $40, alongside a massive 29% discount on MTG's Marvel Super Heroes Play Booster Box.

There are plenty of other bargains worth snapping up, as well, including price cuts on One Piece starter decks, KPop Demon Hunters cards, and Disney Lorcana sets. We've rounded up the best Black Friday trading card deals currently available across Amazon, covering everything from individual booster bundles to larger display boxes. Although, we'll also keep updating this guide as more offers go live throughout the whole BF weekend, so be sure to keep this guide bookmarked for the latest discounts.

With the popular 30th Celebration Booster Bundle now out of stock, the Tech Sticker Collection is the next best way to grab affordable packs from the Pokémon TCG's anniversary expansion on Black Friday — now as low as $39.95 at Amazon with a whole 20% off, or $10.04 off its current $49.99 list price.

For that money, you’re getting three 30th Celebration booster packs, a foil promo card featuring either Lucario or Alolan Exeggutor, and a matching sticker sheet.

You’re not only paying $13.32 per pack before counting the extra collectibles, you’re also only paying $6.24 above the Tech Sticker Collection’s $33.24 market price on TCGplayer.

Surprisingly coming in with a bigger price drop than ever for Black Friday’s trading card deals, the 30-pack box for Magic: The Gathering’s Marvel Super Heroes crossover expansion is now only $104.90 at Amazon.

With that $43.05 price cut off the current typical price of $147.95, you’re only paying $3.50 per pack — and only $5.99 above TCGplayer’s estimated $98.91 market price. The lowest price Amazon has ever seen for this box (confirmed with Mashable’s price tracking methods), we don’t expect this deal to last very long in particular.

Each booster contains 14 cards, including a traditional foil and one to four rare-or-higher cards, with the chance to pull special borderless variants featuring Marvel favourites like Iron Man, Hulk, and Doctor Doom. This box is even shipped and sold directly by Amazon rather than a third-party seller, although the listing is marked as a final sale.

Konami's Magnificent Monsters has hit a 90-day low on Amazon, now $29.99 instead of $34.99. That’s actually the lowest price this newly released box has ever been. If you’re a fan of the original anime series, this is a must-buy for grabbing revamped versions of classic cards, like Dark Magician and Elemental Hero Flamewingman.

You get three five-card packs of Ultra Rares and three additional Secret Rares, each with a chance to upgrade to Starlight Rare. There are 18 reimagined anime cards to collect altogether.

If you've been curious about getting into the One Piece Card Game, this early Black Friday bargain is the best way to jump in, with Bandai's Learn Together Deck Set now down to just $57.99, down from $74.99 — a brilliant 23% discount.

Built around Luffy, Zoro, and Nami, it includes three ready-to-play decks of 51 cards and 10 DON!! cards apiece. You'll also receive guides, storage cases, three five-card extra packs, and a two-player playsheet.

It's an excellent time to get into the KPop Demon Hunters Collectible Trading Cards series, with Kayou's 12-pack Energy Edition reduced from $45.88 to $37.34 on Amazon in a limited-time 10% off deal. This works out to roughly $3.11 per pack, with eight cards in each.

Unlike the Classic Edition, Energy Edition features 11 rarity levels, with 130 different card types to collect. These include the more sought-after SE cards, inspired by the song Golden, featuring laser-etched finishes and individual numbering from 1 to 366. There are also exclusive BP cards numbered up to 999, featuring HUNTR/X in their black stage outfits.

For the early Black Friday season, Disney Lorcana's Illumineer's Quest: The Great Hunny Rescue has slipped to a mega-low price of $52.74. Instead of opening packs, you'll be battling a self-playing Vine deck to rescue Piglet. The box has two prebuilt 60-card decks, a 50-card scenario deck, encounter cards, tokens, and a board. Play solo or with up to three friends, provided two bring their own decks. It's sold by Kings Games, shipped by Amazon, and marked as a final sale.
