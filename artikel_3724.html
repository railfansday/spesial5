Principal Arrested for Not Reporting Teacher Who Photographed Up Girl's Skirt

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A principal in Florida was arrested after she allegedly failed to report a child sexual abuse incident that took place at Ocala Christian Academy Elementary School when a teacher allegedly photographed a student under her skirt.

Teacher Joel Gould was fired from the school and arrested on October 27 after he allegedly admitted to taking the photo up the student’s skirt.

The Ocala Police Department said that principal Sharon Loyd was aware of the incident and failed to report it, according to WESH. After becoming aware of the incident, she allegedly sent an audio recording to Gould, 34, telling him to “keep this under as much as we’re going to be able to,” according to the arrest affidavit viewed by the outlet.

Loyd, 80, was arrested on Wednesday, November 18, and was charged with failing to report suspected child sexual abuse as a mandatory reporter. She has since been released from custody after she posted a $1,000 bond.

Meanwhile, Gould is currently in custody at Marion County Jail after he was charged with digital voyeurism. He made his first appearance in court on Wednesday, where he was not given a bond.

Authorities first learned of the incident when concerned parents reported that Gould acted in a disturbing way during recess. A witness claimed they saw Gould holding his phone underneath a student’s skirt as the young girl stood on a picnic table next to him.

The student later claimed to police that she felt something touch her leg several times, and she said she later realized it was Gould’s phone with the camera pointed upward.

Officers then spoke to Gould, “who admitted to taking photographs underneath the child’s skirt.”

The arrests of Loyd and Gould have left the school’s community in shock, and two parents told News 6 that they plan to pull their children from the school effective immediately.

“They are no longer protecting our children. They are protecting the bad behaviors within that school,” parent Eleana Coscarelli told the outlet.

Both Coscarelli and fellow parent Stephenee Pearson said that they are frustrated with the lack of transparency from school leadership in light of the arrests.

“I think the answer is change and accountability and new leadership all around,” Pearson said.

Coscarelli concluded that she wanted the school to take accountability for the incidents and urged for there to be “more transparency.”

It’s not currently clear if either Loyd or Gould have entered pleas following their arrests. The Ocala Police Department did not immediately respond to Us’ request for comment regarding updates in the case.
