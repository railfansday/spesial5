Best recipes to craft in Casualties: Unknown

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Crafting in Casualties: Unknown can be a huge life-saver if you want to reduce the difficulty of the game. While crafting is optional, it allows you to make use of resources to produce important items.

The complete crafting menu can be quite daunting to look at with all the different recipes. In this guide, I will help you figure out the most essential recipes you’ll need to craft on any run at any in-game difficulty.

I have divided the craftable resources into two categories. First, we will have a look at non-food items that you can’t consume directly. However, these are essential items that will raise your chances of survival.

For starters, you’ll want to dig these materials.

The Foliage Bag, the Canteen, and the Flimsy Knife are the three most important resources you’ll need when you’re starting out. They’re going to make your dig easier to manage.
