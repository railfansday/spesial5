Seattle mayor orders park named for American president renamed

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

XX-XY Athletics owner Jennifer Sey and high school athlete Frances Staudt join 'America Reports' to discuss Washington State's initiative to protect girls' sports as Democrats push to block the effort.

Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson has ordered a city park named after President Andrew Jackson to be renamed to better fit "the values of our city."

The city argues that Jackson's legacy includes policies that forcibly displaced Native American peoples from their ancestral lands. Wilson directed the Seattle Parks and Recreation to begin a community renaming process on Monday. The 160-acre Jackson Park plays host to the Jackson Park Golf Course.

"We’ve heard from community members who want to change the name of Jackson Park, and now is the right time to move this conversation forward. With new light rail connections and opportunities to make the park more accessible and welcoming, we should have a park that reflects the values of our city," Wilson said in a statement.

"I’m directing Seattle Parks and Recreation to begin a community naming process to find a name that better reflects this place, its history, and the people who enjoy it," she added.

The renaming effort comes just days after Wilson also backed an effort to block a girls sports initiative that overwhelmingly passed a statewide vote.

Wilson said last week that she supports the Seattle School Board's unanimous vote to pursue legal action against Initiative 638 — which would enforce separation of boys and girls in K-12 sports in Washington state.

Voters passed the measure by a 13-point margin last week, and it won in 36 of the Evergreen State's 39 counties. But after deliberating behind closed doors Monday, the Seattle School Board voted publicly to authorize potential legal action against the measure without hearing public testimony.
