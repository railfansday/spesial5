The new Google Wallet app brings Gemini agentic shopping to US-based iOS users - sorry, no tap-to-pay.

The Google Wallet app is available for iPhone users in the US for the first time. iOS users who download the new Google Wallet app can manage online payments for faster checkout at places that support Google Pay. However, Apple restricts Google’s ability to offer tap-to-pay service on the iOS app, so users won’t be able to use Google Wallet for contactless checkout on iPhone.

The new Google Wallet app lets you digitize physical cards, including payment, loyalty, and membership cards, as well as boarding passes. Google Wallet app users on iOS will also be able to view receipts and track orders pulled from Gmail in one app.

There used to be a Google Pay app for iOS, but it was discontinued in 2024. The old Google Pay app also didn’t support tap-to-pay on iPhone, but it offered limited functionality, including support for loyalty and gift cards, as well as purchase tracking.

The biggest difference between the old Google Pay app for iOS and the new Google Wallet is the AI component, which supports agentic shopping, likely as Google’s longer-term play.

One of Google’s goals for the Wallet app on iOS is to make it a central place to manage purchases made by its AI agents. The Agents section in Google Wallet for iPhone lets you manage and monitor purchases made through Gemini.

Agentic commerce is on the rise, as evidenced by the growing use of Meta’s Muse. Gemini can make purchases and payments through the Tasks feature, which lets you give Gemini permission to buy the items you want and set your maximum budget.

The Google Wallet app for iOS becomes the central hub for purchase management in agentic commerce through Gemini. In it, you can view active tasks to see which purchases Gemini is working on, as well as completed purchases and receipts.

Google has been working on two technologies to support its agentic commerce strategy: Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) and Agent Payments Protocol (AP2).

UCP is a technical language that enables AI agents to communicate with retailers and make purchases, while AP2 is the payment framework that establishes rules and authorization for agents to make payments.

Google Wallet serves as both the AI-powered wallet and the consumer-facing record of transactions made by Gemini.

Along with Google, major retailers supported the development of UCP, including Shopify, Walmart, Target, and Etsy. When Google announced AP2, more than 60 organizations became involved, including PayPal, Salesforce, Mastercard, and American Express.
