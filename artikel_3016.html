Trump, Duffy Deflect Responsibility for FAA Groundstop

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Henry Harteveldt, president of Atmosphere Research Group, described Friday's total FAA groundstop that interrupted thousands of flights as, 'Humpty Dumpty falling off the wall, smashing into a billion bits and then being run over by a tank.' Harteveldt said that he gives airlines a lot of credit for working to get through the backlog, but said that the FAA has been 'starved' of funding by both Democratic and Republican administrations, including the first Trump Administration. (Source: Bloomberg)
