Eagle Materials (EXP) Q2 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Record cement volumes offset diesel costs as wallboard demand stays soft.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Eagle Materials Inc. (EXP +2.82%) reported record second quarter revenue of $652.7 million but experienced a decline in net earnings due to elevated diesel prices and higher interest rates impacting residential construction demand. Management reported that modernization projects at the Mountain Cement facility in Wyoming and the Duke Wallboard plant in Oklahoma remain on track, with the Laramie facility expected to be substantially complete by the end of the current calendar year. The company continues to prioritize its low-cost operating position and capital allocation through share repurchases and strategic investments despite macroeconomic headwinds and cost spikes in transportation and raw materials.
