Prediction: This Cryptocurrency Will Outperform Bitcoin in 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Solana has a track record of significantly outperforming Bitcoin during bull market cycles.

By Dominic Basulto – Nov 18, 2026 at 8:10AM EST

Given that Bitcoin (BTC -2.27%) typically leads the crypto market higher or lower, it's no surprise that it gets the most attention from crypto investors. Bitcoin is a reliable barometer of how the crypto market is doing at any point in time.

But here's the thing: Altcoins typically have higher highs than Bitcoin, as well as lower lows. For that reason, they tend to outperform Bitcoin significantly during crypto bull market cycles.

If you're expecting the crypto market to rally next year, it will be altcoins dazzling investors with their performance. The one altcoin that I'm focused on right now is Solana (SOL -0.22%), and here's why.

Ever since its launch back in 2020, Solana has been touted as a potential Ethereum (ETH -2.97%) killer, due to its faster speeds, lower costs, and superior scalability. In 2023, Cathie Wood of Ark Invest specifically pointed to Solana as a potential disruptive force in the crypto market. After years of waiting, 2027 could be the year that Solana finally puts it all together.

For one, Solana is rapidly outgrowing its reputation as simply a place to buy, sell, and speculate in meme coins. It's moving into institutional-grade finance, and is picking up some big-name backers along the way. That's raising Solana's profile in the world of decentralized finance (DeFi).

Take tokenized equities, for example. Solana recently partnered with the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation (DTCC) to bring tokenized equities to the Solana blockchain. Heading into 2027, Solana is already one of the top blockchain networks for trading tokenized equities, or crypto that represents the ownership of stocks or other assets.

The same story is being repeated in other key niches of the blockchain and crypto world. That includes stablecoins, where Solana is now rapidly challenging the market-leading role of Ethereum.

When big-name corporations want to launch new stablecoins, they now turn to Solana. Case in point: Solana partnered with Western Union (WU -0.57%) in May on a new stablecoin launch.

Admittedly, the data set on market performance is limited, given that Solana has only been trading since 2020. But the numbers certainly hint at the type of outperformance Solana is capable of delivering over short periods of time (12 months or less).

In 2021, for example, Solana skyrocketed by 11,166% while Bitcoin cruised to a gain of 60%. In 2023, Solana soared by 920%, while Bitcoin rose by 155%.

On the flip side, Solana definitely has lower lows than Bitcoin. In 2022, for example, Solana collapsed by 94%, while Bitcoin lost 64% of its value. That helps to explain why Solana is down 45% this year, while Bitcoin is only down 28%.

If you're counting on a crypto rebound in 2027, then it will be altcoins leading the way, not Bitcoin. And the one altcoin that I'm expecting to be at the front of the pack is Solana.
