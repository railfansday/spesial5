Sam LaPorta upgraded to full participation at Lions practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Lions tight end Sam LaPorta took a step in the right direction on Tuesday’s injury report.

The Lions listed LaPorta as a full participant after listing him as limited with an ankle injury on Monday. Both reports were estimations because the Lions only held walkthroughs the last two days.

Safety Chuck Clark (hamstring) moved to a limited listing after being listed as out to start the week.

Defensive end DJ Wonnum (back) and cornerback Ennis Rakestraw (shoulder) were listed as out of practice for the second straight day. Defensive lineman Skylar Gill-Howard (knee) was also out and head coach Dan Campbell announced earlier in the day that he’ll miss the rest of the season.

Cornerbacks Khalil Dorsey (wrist) and cornerback D.J. Reed (hip) were both full participants.
