Fred Johnson finds new NFL home after being cut by the Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Former Philadelphia Eagles swing tackle and Super Bowl LIX champion Fred Johnson is signing with the Kansas City Chiefs' practice squad, as noted by our friends over at Arrowhead Pride.

The Eagles notably waived Johnson and Hollywood Brown late last week. Neither player was claimed off waivers, making them free agents available to sign with any team.

KC is a nice landing spot for Johnson. The 29-year-old blocker is going from a No. 1 overall pick in the 2027 NFL Draft contender to a Super Bowl contender. And he might actually find some playing time since the Chiefs are dealing with some offensive line woes. Maybe Big Fred can get another championship ring by winning with Big Red instead of beating him this time.

As for the Eagles, they're seemingly moved forward with John Ojukwu as their new swing tackle. The team signed him from their practice squad to their roster on Monday. If he has to fill in for either Jordan Mailata or Markel Bell, maybe he'll show some potential to be the Eagles' next Fred Johnson.
