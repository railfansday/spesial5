Motherwell 1-2 Rangers: What McInnes said

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Rangers manager Derek McInnes: "Three points is what we came here for. We'll take the good from it, we'll take the three points and it was a brilliant moment when you score late on. Those sort of late winners are systematic with teams that you think can be successful.

"Obviously, you would much rather have the game far more comfortable in terms of the result. I thought we defended really well. My two centre-halves were magnificent throughout. We didn't dominate but we were very close to it. The performance was really strong. We should've been far more comfortable.

"Taking the game away from the opposition on chances created, that should've been the case.

"But, when the game is still in the balance and you're putting a bit of pressure on, it's good to know that you've got a bit of evidence there that you can score late winners. I think there's a belief in the team that there's goals, certainly a belief from myself and the staff that we feel there's a goal coming."
