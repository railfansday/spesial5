Nick Bosa: I don't think I've played my best football

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

49ers defensive end Nick Bosa's only stats in Sunday's 46-40 win over the Cowboys were one quarterback hit, four pressures and three penalties in 34 snaps. It was his fourth consecutive game without a sack, a first in his career.

"I think in the past when I was younger, it would eat at me a lot more, just strictly the stat aspects," Bosa said Wednesday, via Nick Wagoner of ESPN. "But now I'm really just focused on the people around me, and if I feel like I'm playing good football, then I really don't care much about the stats. But I don't think I'm playing my best football right now, so that's what gets to me more so than the actual stats."

Bosa exited the injury report in Week 10. But he has worked his way back from the torn ACL in his right knee in Week 3 of the 2025 season and a calf injury in Week 3.

He has played catch up with new coordinator Raheem Morris' scheme.

"I think there's a learning curve and . . . a lot of guys haven't had many reps in the scheme so far," Bosa said. "I'm trying to break it down to the basics and just kind of get back. I missed a year and then missed camp. I kind of took for granted some of the things that I did automatically, and I thought I could just kind of coast and continue to do those things automatically and focus on other things. But I need to, especially with a newer defense, really hone in on the things that made me the player that I am."

Bosa's last double-digit sack season was 2023 when he had 10.5. He totaled nine sacks in 14 games in 2024 and two in three games in 2025, with none this season.

"I'm not going to make excuses because nobody really cares," Bosa said. "But I'm going to get better and I'm pretty confident in that physically and how I'm playing."
