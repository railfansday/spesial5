Rajat Patidar scripts SMAT history, set new state record for most sixes

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Rajat Patidar delivered a stunning batting performance in the Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy on Monday, smashing an unbeaten 135 off just 48 balls to help Madhya Pradesh post 250/6 against Assam in New Chandigarh.His innings included 13 sixes and eight fours, with his century coming in just 41 deliveries.The knock was special for several reasons, as Patidar became the first Madhya Pradesh batter to score a century in the history of the tournament.He also set a new state record for the most sixes in a single Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy innings.

Patidar came to the crease when Madhya Pradesh were struggling at 57/3 in the eighth over. However, the captain wasted little time and launched an aggressive attack on Assam's bowlers.He received excellent support from Shivang Kumar, who joined him in the middle. The duo added 153 runs from just 62 balls, recording the second-highest partnership for Madhya Pradesh in the tournament.Patidar was particularly impressive, facing only five dot balls throughout his innings.The explosive performance comes at an important time for Patidar, especially with the IPL 2027 retention deadline falling on November 17. He is an important player for Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB), and his latest knock will be encouraging news for the franchise.Patidar had recently withdrawn from India's Rising Stars Asia Cup squad in Doha to manage his existing injuries. He was initially named vice-captain of the India A team but informed the BCCI that he did not wish to be considered for 50-over cricket at the time.Ayush Badoni replaced him, while Rinku Singh took over as vice-captain.Despite the fitness concerns, Patidar has started the domestic season strongly. He scored an unbeaten 36 against Keralam in Madhya Pradesh's opening Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy match and also registered back-to-back centuries against Puducherry and Bihar in the Ranji Trophy.
