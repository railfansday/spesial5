The best part of writing the Thursday AM newsletter is knowing that we get to kick off a new week of NFL football while reading Pete Prisco's game-by-game predictions.

The Texans and Colts will get Week 11 started with an AFC South battle tonight, but let me take us to Philadelphia. The Steelers and Eagles meet in the battle of Pennsylvania, and both sides need a win. Pittsburgh just got blown out by the Bengals, and Philly has lost seven straight games.

Prisco likes the Steelers to get it done on the road, despite the Eagles coming off a bye.

Still, not all of our experts think the Steelrs will roll. Tyler Sullivan says Pittsburgh could be on upset alert in The Linc. In his complete Week 11 picks, Sullivan predicts a few blowouts.

The most highly anticipated matchup of the week features the Vikings taking on the 49ers. More specifically, the chess match between Kyle Shanahan and Brian Flores will be worth the price of admission.

When Anthony Edwards went down with injury just four games into the season, it seemed like the Timberwolves might be cooked. Instead, Minnesota is sitting at 11-4, fourth in the West and up to No. 3 in our latest NBA Power Rankings.

Much of that success is owed to LaMelo Ball, who is shining in his first season with the Wolves. He's averaging 17.7 points, 5.5 assists and 1.9 steals. For more evidence of Ball's impact, just look at how the team he left is doing without him. Brad Botkin explains:

Botkin: "The LaMelo effect is being felt in two places: Minnesota, of course, but also Charlotte, where the offense has plummeted from a 123.3 rating with Ball on the court last year to a league-worst 103.1 this season. Being down Kon Knueppel doesn't help, but it's night and day watching this unit try to generate clean looks without Ball's creation and passing."
