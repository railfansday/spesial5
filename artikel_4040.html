How Long Does It Take To Complete Grand Theft Auto 6?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

GTA 6 is finally here, and it's much bigger than any of us could have dreamed. Here's how long to expect it to take over your life.

It's been a very, very long road but Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here, bringing with it the most detailed and expansive gameplay, world, and story that the series has ever seen.

Considering how many years we've been waiting for GTA 6 and all of the things that Rockstar managed to keep secret for so long, you're no doubt wondering how big the game is. Let's get into it.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is split into five main story chapters, with a total of 93 story missions to complete. Story missions range from being massive set-piece moments that can take nearly an hour each to shorter scenarios that won't take you that long. Each chapter in the game is about the same length, although Chapter 2 and Chapter 5 are a little less lengthy than the others.

If you're looking to just mainline it to the end of the game (which would be a massive mistake, honestly), then you're probably going to spend a minimum of 50 to 60 hours to see the credits. Of course, those story missions are just the start, as there are tons of side missions, robberies, exploration points, and other objectives for Jason and Lucia to complete.

A more balanced playthrough of Grand Theft Auto 6 where you spend some time exploring the world, saving some money, and taking the time to complete some objectives is going to take upwards of 70 hours. By the time I hit the credits, I was 80 hours in and had only completed 62 percent of the entire game, and that was with a little bit of deadline speediness.

That should probably clue you in to the fact that 100 percenting Grand Theft Auto 6 is going to take you a very long while. To do so, you'll need to complete all 100 optional tasks, clear all 39 robbery opportunities and 22 bank jobs, fence $100,000 in narcotics and jewellery, collect all the trading cards, find every meditation spot and flamingo, and oh so much more.

It's such an undertaking, in fact, that it was major news when one player managed to get the Platinum a few days after launch. I can't even imagine how that went down.

Simply put, 100 percenting GTA 6 is a huge undertaking that's going to take you a very long time, which is at least made easier with all the trackers in the Progress menu. I'm nowhere near doing that myself after nearly 100 hours, so I'd easily put an estimate of around 180 to 200 hours (if not more) to 100 percent complete Grand Theft Auto 6.
