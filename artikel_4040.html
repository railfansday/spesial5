MR. ELEVATOR coming to PS5 on November 27

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

GIFT TEN INDUSTRY will release a PlayStation 5 version of bizarre puzzle game MR. ELEVATOR on November 27, the developer announced.

MR. ELEVATOR first launched for Switch and PC via Steam on October 15, 2025.

Here is an overview of the game, via GIFT TEN INDUSTRY:

The talking elevator’s doors open to reveal a strange other world.

Five protagonists. Five worlds. One ending where it all comes together.

Each has something to find. Follow clues in sounds and symbols as you delve deeper into the story in this puzzle adventure.

Listen closely as you explore the world of MR. ELEVATOR.
