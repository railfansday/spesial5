Is PlayStation down? How to check the system status for GTA 6 launch.

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Many gamers have already taken advantage of a sneaky little trick that allowed them to play the long-awaited Grand Theft Auto VI one day early on New Zealand time.

However, the vast majority of gamers in the U.S. are awaiting the official launch at midnight on Nov. 19. And in the hours ahead of the game's debut, some users are already reporting problems accessing the PlayStation Network. User error reports for PlayStation spiked on DownDetector around 2 p.m. ET on Nov. 18, and reports remained elevated into the evening.

The good news: The official PlayStation Service Status page shows that all systems are go for launch, as of this writing.

The question is: Will PlayStation and Xbox be able to handle all the millions of gamers trying to download and access the game all at once?

With so many different companies and services suffering from outages recently, gamers may be understandably nervous. And we have you covered. Here's how you can easily tell if PlayStation or Xbox is down — or if it's just you and your internet connection.

If you run into any issues while trying to load up GTA 6 on your PlayStation, head over to the official PlayStation Service Status page. If there is a problem, you can also check the PlayStation Support account on X for updates.

These official resources will let you know at a glance whether Sony PlayStation services are up and running. And if there is an issue, the status page will tell you which PlayStation service is having problems.

Again, as of publication, PlayStation's services are all up and running.

Remember: You'll need to update your PlayStation console before you can play GTA 6.

Microsoft has a similar status page for Xbox users. If you run into trouble trying to enter Vice City on GTA 6 launch day, simply head to the official Xbox status page.

This page will tell you if any Xbox services are having problems.

As of publication, Xbox's services are all up and running as well, despite some elevated activity on DownDetector.
