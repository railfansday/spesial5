Official: Kean undergoing new medical assessment after Como setback

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Como have released a statement confirming Italy striker Moise Kean is ‘undergoing further medical assessment’ after an incident in training, as his return to action is further postponed.

The centre-forward has been struggling with a recurring tibia issue for many months, going back to his time at Fiorentina before the summer transfer to Como.

He played through pain, returned and had relapses, the most recent appearance coming in early October for Italy in the Nations League.

Kean had been given a recovery time of five weeks after specialist treatment, but there were reports of another setback in training.

“Following an issue during training, Moise Kean is undergoing further medical assessment,” confirmed a club statement this evening.

“Our priority is supporting his recovery. We will provide an update when we have further information to share.”

The tone of the statement from Como does suggest this could be another serious setback for the Italy international.

There are fears he could require a surgical intervention to resolve the issue once and for all, as conservative treatment has so far proved unsuccessful.

The 26-year-old has made six competitive appearances this season at club level for Fiorentina and Como, scoring one goal with one assist.
