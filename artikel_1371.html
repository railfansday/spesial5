Exclusive: Ashley Young Drops Verdict on Mauricio Pochettino Man Utd Appointment

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Former Manchester United captain Ashley Young has revealed what Mauricio Pochettino needs to be a success at Old Trafford, while speaking exclusively to GIVEMESPORT.

The Red Devils announced the arrival of the 54-year-old on a two-and-a-half-year contract last week, replacing Michael Carrick in the dugout after a poor start to the season which has left the club sitting 18th in the Premier League with just one win in their opening ten games.

Pochettino has left his role as the USMNT boss to take over, declaring it a dream come true for him to finally step into the dugout at Old Trafford, with his first game in charge to come on Sunday afternoon against arch-rivals Liverpool at Anfield.

Pochettino was a somewhat surprising appointment, having signed a new long-term contract with US Soccer following the World Cup last summer. But he is someone that Man Utd have been linked with on a number of occasions over the years, although they have opted to go in alternative directions previously.

The Argentine is best known for his time in charge at Tottenham, where he helped guide them to a runners-up finish in the Premier League and Champions League, while he has also been manager at Espanyol, Southampton, Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea.

Coming into Man Utd is a big task, especially with their current situation, but there are ways Pochettino can revolutionise Man Utd, and when asked for his thoughts on the appointment, Young told GIVEMESPORT that he feels it could be a good hire if he is given control over certain things.

"For me I think that it was an appointment that they've obviously looked at different managers out there who were out of work, but they've looked at what he did, especially when he was at Tottenham," he said.

"But for me it was one of them where he's had a rebuild at Tottenham, but it took two or three years. But then he's got Tottenham from lower in the league to third in the league, second in the league, Champions League final. So it does show that he's got that pedigree.

"He's even said that he always wanted to come back to the Premier League. He loved the Premier League.

"I think it's an appointment where things can go well, but if he's got the control of players coming in, players going out, and the board really give him the confidence and back him."

Young spent eight-and-a-half years at Man Utd as a player, having joined from Aston Villa as a winger before eventually becoming a regular at full-back.

During his time at Old Trafford, he made 261 appearances, scoring 19 goals, and won the Premier League, FA Cup, League Cup, Community Shield and Community Shield before joining Inter Milan and winning Serie A under Antonio Conte.

He then returned to England for spells with Aston Villa and Everton before dropping into the Championship to help Ipswich Town earn promotion to the Premier League, before retiring this summer.

The former USMNT head coach begins his new role with a trip to Anfield to take on Liverpool on Sunday afternoon.
