30 Best Football Teams in the World [Ranked]

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

What makes a team the best in the world? It's a rather subjective question. Sure, the number of wins and trophies a club has won in recent times can be a good indicator, but in a sport where anyone could beat anyone, it feels like a tough task to pick just one team that trumps the rest. After all, comparing teams from different leagues requires pure guesswork.

As a prime example, Manchester United have never locked horns with Cristiano Ronaldo’s Al-Nassr. And while many may think the former would easily come out on top, it’s practically impossible to know for sure. Admittedly, it’s opinionated and there is technically no right or wrong answer – but everyone could use some help from the folks over at Opta, who have calculated (and recently updated) their Power Rankings.

Is the Premier League really the best in the world?

Using an Elo algorithm which analyses results from over 2.5 million games, a rating is then assigned to that team, which is comparable to other sides across the world. Points are determined by in-league ratings, the overall rating of a division, a country's rating, among others, and the rating of the continent a team plays in. With all that in mind, here are the 30 best teams in world football as things stand, how well they've performed in competitions in seasons gone by and exactly why they are placed where they are.

Kicking off this list, in 30th and 29th respectively, are Fulham and Nottingham Forest. Both teams appear to be in a relegation battle in England's top tier this season.

Flamengo - South America's most followed football club - are the 27th best team in the world and the only side from outside of Europe to feature in the top 30. They are sandwiched in between Portuguese giants Porto and Sporting CP.

Newcastle United have had a turbulent start to the season under new boss Matthias Jaissle. They are now statistically the 25th best team in the world, a place below Leeds United. Daniel Farke's side enjoyed a decent campaign back in the Premier League as they finished 14th. They will be looking to push for European qualification this time round after finding themselves in 12th after their first nine matches.Manchester United are now the 22nd best team in the world, a place above Royale Union Saint-Gilloise and a place below AS Roma. Michael Carrick is now fighting for his job after the Red Devils' nightmare start to the 2026/27 campaign.

Brentford, who continue to pull off upset after upset with Keith Andrews at the helm, are currently the 18th best side in the world, according to data. Imagine saying that when they were languishing in League Two just two decades ago! They have been given a 92.0 rating, along with Atletico Madrid and Everton.

Belgian giants Club Brugge have been rated as a better team than the three aforementioned clubs, while Tottenham Hotspur are now statistically the 16th best team in the world. The north London club have been impressive in recent weeks after overcoming a terrible start to the campaign.

Borussia Dortmund just scrape into the top 15, with Benfica and Como following them in 14th and 13th respectively. The Italian club, who are managed by Cesc Fabregas and are competing in the Champions League for the first time ever in 2026/27, have soared up the rankings in recent weeks after beating Man Utd 4-1 and then thrashing Lens 9-1.

English duo Aston Villa and Bournemouth just miss out on the top 10. Both Premier League clubs are fighting to qualify for Europe this season.

The 20 most valuable squads in the world today have been revealed with the likes of Manchester City, Arsenal, Real Madrid, and Man United included.

The top 10 are made up of the crème de la crème of world football, though every team here – unsurprisingly – resides in Europe. Chelsea, with their happy-go-lucky spending seeing them start the season well under new manager Xabi Alonso, kick things off. The Blues are followed by reigning Italian champions, Inter Milan.

Premier League giants Liverpool are in 8th, with the Reds holding a 94.8 rating despite a poor campaign in which they went trophyless and also conceded a record amount of goals. But their squad is still strong, and they'll be hoping to bounce back under Andoni Iraola this season.

Brighton are, statistically, a better side than Liverpool. Fabian Hurzeler's side have made a blistering start to their Premier League campaign, having picked up 19 points from their opening nine matches, scoring 24 goals along the way.

Real Madrid sit in eighth, with a 96.0 rating, despite going trophyless for a second consecutive season. The returning Jose Mourinho will be hoping to guide Los Blancos to success in 2026/27, and early-season form suggests that the Special One is capable of doing so.

Surprisingly, Paris Saint-Germain are ranked in fifth. Luis Enrique's side were exceptional again last season as they were crowned French champions and also successfully retained their Champions League title with victory over Arsenal in Budapest.

Spanish champions Barcelona follow in fourth. Hansi Flick's side claimed a second consecutive league title after a fine campaign, in which they scored an exceptional 95 goals, and won all 19 of their home games.

The 10 favourites to win the Ballon d'Or have been ranked on their probability of winning football's most prestigious individual honour.

Man City come in at third in the rankings, with a rating of 97.6, after completing the domestic double by winning the Carabao Cup and FA Cup last season. Enzo Maresca will be looking to establish City as the best team in the world once more after replacing Pep Guardiola at the helm. The Cityzens have made a strong start to the season under the Italian.

Bundesliga champions Bayern Munich take second spot, with a 99.4 rating. Vincent Kompany's side had a record-breaking 2025/26 campaign, with their incredible attacking unit of Harry Kane, Luis Diaz, Michael Olise, Serge Gnabry, Lennart Karl, and Jamal Musiala putting teams to the sword throughout.

Mikel Arteta's Arsenal are statistically the best football team in the world, despite an agonising defeat to PSG in the Champions League final in May. The Gunners, who claimed their first Premier League title in 22 years, have one of the best defences in Europe, but also scored well over 100 goals in all competitions this season to reach a perfect score.

All Power Rankings courtesy of Opta – correct as of 05/11/2026.
