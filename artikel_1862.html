Frosinone boss Alvini content with aggressive approach vs. Milan despite ‘naive’ mistakes

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Frosinone head coach Massimiliano Alvini has admitted that his side simply gave AC Milan too many opportunities.

It has been a very positive return to the top flight for Frosinone so far, given that a point last night at San Siro would have seen them move back into the European places. However, they were ultimately given a bit of a humbling by Milan, who won 5-1 thanks to a ruthless counter-attacking display.

Some have questioned if the Canarini were a bit too aggressive in their approach to the game, given they played with such a high line and allowed space for Adrien Rabiot, Diego Moreira, Goncalo Ramos, Ardon Jashari, Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic to exploit.

Alvini was present in the press conference room at San Siro after the full-time whistle and he analysed what was perhaps the most difficult night of the season for his team. MilanNews transcribed his assessments.

Should the game have been played differently?

“Frosinone played their game but made a few mistakes they don’t usually make, but that’s certainly not my concern, losing by such a large margin. What’s worrying are the mistakes we made that we could have avoided.”

“We made a mistake on the first goal, which was a mistake of our own, while the other goals we conceded were goals we absolutely could have avoided. We conceded them and were naive.

“It’s a mistake of our own, and so today, from this perspective, if you come to Milan against Milan and make these mistakes, it’s only natural that we end up losing like this, by such a large scoreline.

“That’s the analysis, even though I think the scoreline is even too large: the first half, in my opinion, was extraordinary on our part.”

Did the absences have an impact, forcing you to experiment a bit?

“There’s nothing experimental about it. We played with the same principles, running forward to get to Rabiot. We preferred to bring on a defender who would push forward rather than a midfielder.

“In my opinion, this definitely didn’t influence the result, but rather the attitude, the idea, or the match strategy. And we certainly made – as I said before and I’ll repeat – mistakes we don’t usually make.

“If you give Milan certain chances, with such strong players and the quality of this team, you risk losing, and that’s what happened. You risk losing with a heavy defeat, and that’s what happened.

“He had a problem with that back heel in the box, and he definitely needs tests. It’s too early now; we’ll have to wait for the tests, which will be done starting Tuesday, but it’s definitely a problem.”

“We had a chance with Monterisi after a minute, we hit the post, and we created a lot. I have nothing to say to the team for their attitude and the way they played.

“We, I repeat, made some naivety, some mistakes. It’s also up to me to understand that there are important players on the other side who can cause problems.”

“It’s a result that definitely needs to be managed, navigated, and channeled, but it doesn’t change my attitude at all. We have 20 points today. The path we need to take is to try to stay in Serie A, and we’ll try to do so with this attitude, with this identity: this is the path we’ll take.

“Four years ago, I drew a game against Milan by playing defensively, and we took home a point. Today we made some mistakes, but this is the path we’ll take. I’m happier with the management, with the team’s identity, to try to achieve that objective, and this is what we’ll continue to do.”
