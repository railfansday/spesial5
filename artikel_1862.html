Bradford City 2 Doncaster Rovers 2: Untidiness at home resurfaces for Michael Duff as good mate Grant McCann has last laugh

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

"The untidiest man I have ever lived with in my life" was his pre-match assertion ahead of Saturday's reunion with his old housemate.

As a manager, Duff could not be more different. The Bradford City chief - a businesslike defender back in the day - prides himself on order and neatness.

His early days in his new workplace have reinforced that. City kept five clean sheets in his first five matches in charge and in all competitions, they recorded seven in his opening eight games.

In the club's last two league games at Valley Parade, derbies against Huddersfield Town and McCann's Doncaster Rovers, there has been some disorder at home again.

After letting in three goals in uncharacteristic fashion against Town, City's famed defence was opened up twice in Saturday's final quarter.

The first, like Town's opener, was a majestic overhead kick which you have to doff your cap to - Jordan Thomas, take a bow. The second, in the fifth minute of stoppage time, was a different matter as Rovers helped themselves to a second equaliser in a finale which was as lively as the opening hour was eminently forgettable.

Unmarked Sean Grehan, who had an eventful second half, benefited from City's inattentiveness following Robbie Gotts' corner. It was an untidy concession, but a precious moment for Rovers, given recent events on the road.

McCann’s side needed a spark. The game definitely got one when George Broadbent clashed with Bantams rival Kieran Morgan in the second half, the prelude to a brief melee among players and kerfuffle between both benches. The crowd got involved and it became a proper derby.

Duff, cautioned alongside McCann, remarked: "I've been doing that with Grant for 30 years. We used to have arguments on PlayStation games, when we were 20 years old and who's going to walk the dog? And we'd kick lumps out of each other in training.

"So that's just two blokes who want to win. I think that's why we're still in a job. We're both nearly 50 years old. You look back, it's pathetic, really."

In the red corner, McCann added: "Michael was just in my technical area and I just said 'Get out of it', that was it.

"He should know better than to be in my area or else he'll get kicked out of it.."

If any of the ex-video game enthusiasts had a grumble at the result, it was Duff. It wasn’t on a Grand Theft Auto scale, but it was a mini heist.

City were in prime position to win the game twice but allowed Rovers to score from two efforts on target. Very un-Bradford-like.

The fact that two strikers scored for the hosts at least provided further evidence to dispel the notion that City don’t have enough goals in them.

Nick Powell - whose minutes must be strictly monitored - was entering the red zone when he struck in the 55th minute.

It was a classic centre forward’s header after getting in between Grehan and Tom Nixon following Callum Connolly’s superb cross.

It was the first defensive blemish on the day for Rovers, as disciplined at Bradford as they were dishevelled at Kidderminster and MK Dons.

A magnificent save from Tommy Simkin kept out a header from Powell’s replacement Stephen Humphrys. A ‘big turning point’ said Duff. It was the cue for Thomas to come to the party.

A low shot flew inches wide before a volley hit the woodwork. It was a case of third time lucky when he splendidly levelled in sublime fashion after debutant James Collins expertly cushioned a header into his path following Grehan’s deep centre.

Making his long-awaited bow, Collins' canny, authoritative performance after coming on midway through the second half whet the appetite.

Play then swung back to the Kop with Will Swan picking out Humphrys after Rovers were caught cold from Jon McCracken’s punt forward. His drilled finish was unerring.

More Simkin heroics then thwarted Humphrys. Rovers dug deep and got their precious reward.

Bradford City: McCracken; Pennington, Baldwin, Pratt (Gillesphey 85); Neufville (Swan 65), Connolly, Morgan (Phillips 77); Touray; Sarcevic, Jackson; Powell (Humphrys 64). Substitutes unused: O’Keeffe, Matthews, Hadi. Doncaster Rovers: Simkin; Nixon (Ayinde 66), Grehan, Pearson (Adelakun 80), Senior; Bailey, Broadbent (Byrne 80); Gotts, Hutchinson (May 66), Thomas; Hanlan (Collins 67). Substitutes unused: Clarke, Robinson. Referee: D Drysdale (Lincs).
