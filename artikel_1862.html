GdS: ‘Ambitious, surprising, Italian’ – Milan set guidelines for 2027 transfer business

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

When building a squad it is important to have a vision of the end goal, and AC Milan have exactly that heading into 2027.

La Gazzetta dello Sport begin a report by stating that there are no transition years when it comes to Milan. Frequently they have chosen to overhaul the squad in the summer window, and sometimes even in the winter.

For example, 2024 brought Paulo Fonseca with Morata, Pavlovic, Fofana, Emerson Royal, Jimenez and Abraham. Then, 2025 brought Gimenez, then Massimiliano Allegri with Modric, Rabiot, Nkunku, Jashari, and De Winter. Last summer brought Ruben Amorim with Ramos, Moreira, Gila, and Cisse.

A not-so-bold prediction: 2027 won’t be slow. Gerry Cardinale even hinted at this, stating: “We’ll continue to be aggressive and opportunistic. It will take a couple more transfer windows to complete everything.” The two transfer windows? Winter and summer, 2027.

The Rossoneri have two key needs right now: a striker and a central defender. They’ll try to secure them as early as the month after next. Nicolo Tresoldi and Endrick are the names that have been talked about for weeks, but the transfer market has surprises.

The difficulties are well known: Club Brugge do not want to sell their jewel in January unless a €60m check materialises, while with Endrick it seems the will of Jose Mourinho must be taken into account. So, alternative names could emerge for the attack.

A central defender is less of a pressing need. Eight months ago, Milan decided to explore the possibility of signing Virgil van Dijk, who turned 35 in July and is regarded as one of the best of his generation.

A year ago, the conditions weren’t there, but now they might be. Van Dijk’s contract expires in June and he’s always been fascinated by Milan and Italy. It’s complex, but it’s worth a try, and Cardinale seems to agree.

Then there is Gabriele Calvani. He’s Italian, 22, plays for Frosinone, and is expected to start San Siro on Sunday. The Diavolo are interested, just as they are keen on Sporting CP’s Gonçalo Inacio, whom Amorim has already coached and attempted to sign in the summer.

Milan will undoubtedly make some changes to their bench over the next 12 months, aiming to mix young players with experienced players. Filippo Terracciano will leave in January, Vladimirov needs to play, Hutchinson is on loan until June, and Jashari will have to reconsider his future.

It’s certain that some Diawara-like players will arrive, i.e. promising prospects chosen through scouting and data. Markus Krösche – if he becomes the new Head of Football, as is now almost certain – will have extensive powers in this regard.

The idea of ​​making the team more Italian is there, but we know how the market works, making it difficult to plan. Not only that, but some Italians will soon leave Milan too. Above all, Christian Comotto and Francesco Camarda are heading out on loan.

The former seems destined for Daniele De Rossi’s Genoa, while it remains to be seen where and under what terms the altter will leave. A loan with an option to buy, leaving Milan free to control the future, probably. To a small Serie A team? Possibly, but a move abroad is possible too.

Belgium and the Netherlands are countries where strikers historically have more opportunities in teams that are younger, while Germany could be another destination. If he takes a flight north, don’t be surprised.
