Mauricio Pochettino drops big tactics hint in first interview as Manchester United manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Mauricio Pochettino says he wants the Manchester United squad to feel "free" when playing in the new manager's first hint at his tactical approach.

Pochettino arrived at the club's Carrington training ground on Wednesday and on Thursday took charge of his first training session. On Sunday comes his first game, away at Anfield against Liverpool.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea manager will be tasked with dragging Man Utd out of the relegation zone and back into the top echelons of English football.

The now-sacked Michael Carrick oversaw the club's worst start to a season in 40 years, and much of the malaise was put down to his tactical setups and a lack of energy from the players on the pitch.

And Pochettino, who is known for his hard drills in training, wants to bring the energy back to his players.

“It's a mistake to see the [current] situation. We need to see the potential of this club,” Pochettino told the club's official website.

“Of course, the circumstances are the circumstances, and we need to be responsible for the circumstances. But at the same time, we need to understand that it's a game and, to connect with the game, we need to feel free.

“The players need to express their talent. We need to create the environment and the platform for the players to perform. That is the most important thing and that is our priority now – it’s to work with them and try to take the pressure.

“Pressure needs to bring energy, responsibility needs to bring energy. Because if not, it's too heavy to put all that pressure and responsibility on your back. When you connect with the game, you connect through your talent and when you feel freedom, when you feel happy, when you want to play for your team-mates, when you want to play for your family, when you want to play for your club, your fans.

We need to love the players that we have, we need to support the players

“That is what we need to create all together and I think we have an amazing staff - we were talking a lot today. Then [we need to] settle all the principles and the ideas and to create our own philosophy and start from zero.

“I think with the power of this club and the fans supporting us, I think we will get in the place that we deserve to be.”

On the significance of culture to success, he adds: “To create the culture is the most important [thing]. Create the habits, the principles, and I think that is what we want to create.

“We need to [be] looking forward and to find a way to create the best habits and the culture that makes us close to winning every single game.”
