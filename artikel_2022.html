West Ham will rue lack of sell – on clause as player’s value skyrockets

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Sixteen players departed West Ham United last summer as the relegation exodus took its toll, along with the huge debt and cash flow issues that forced the Hammers to part with most of their more valuable assets.

£190 million raised from the sale of just four players certainly kept the accountants happy, whilst fans have had to deal with the anguish that comes from watching former favourites turn out for clubs who once were West Ham’s rivals.

One player in particular looks to be about to realise his stated ambition: Whilst he was still a Hammer, El Hadji Malick Diouf made no secret of his desire to play for a ‘top club’ one day – which didn’t exactly endear him to Irons supporters.

£35 million plus add-ons for Diouf seemed too good to turn down: Now there are claims he’s worth £20 million more

His transfer to Brentford bolstered the West Ham accounts by a cool £35 million but it seems west London is just the next rung on the ladder for the ambitious 21 year old.

Despite a dodgy start to his Brentford career, apparently Diouf’s left-footed curling crosses and pacy wing work is getting him noticed in all the right places. ‘Top clubs’, it seems, are indeed casting envious eyes over the left back’s CV, according to caughtoffside who claim the defender is now worth up to £55 million as “Chelsea and Liverpool make contact over €60m rated Diouf”.

I doubt anyone at Hammers HQ had the foresight to put a sell-on clause in his sale contract: West Ham fans will be incredulous, as was I, that the amiable left back with the booming crosses but dodgy defensive positioning has magically morphed into a £55 million rated player in a matter of months.
