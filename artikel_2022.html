A Newcastle United injury update ahead of playing Arsenal at the weekend.

A 5.30pm kick-off at St James’ Park on Saturday.

Matthias Jaissle and his Newcastle United team returning to action after yet another international break.

Thursday afternoon seeing the United boss giving a Newcastle United injury update.

Newcastle up against the league leaders on Saturday and needing as many of their squad available as possible.

Matthias Jaissle stated this today on players returning from injury:

“We had nine injuries, now most of them are back, slowly but surely we will get them back to match fitness. We need to be patient with them. It gives us flexibility.”

Ewen Jaouen is a long-term injury and will still be unavailable.

No Joelinton either in the squad against Arsenal.

“We will get feedback from the specialists, we will find out how long Joe will be out. We will get an update and hope he is back soon.”

In better news, Jacob Ramsey is back from suspension.

Whilst Matthias Jaissle had this to say about Tino Livramento:

“He was out for a long time, we are really happy to have him back. We are managing his load management quite closely as we want to have him available for a long period. He is a brilliant footballer, a great human being. I am pleased he is able to be in the squad right now and is making good progress.”
