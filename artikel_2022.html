Player Ratings: Blackburn 2-1 West Ham as Hermansen Has a Nightmare

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

West Ham’s promising start at Ewood Park ultimately counted for very little as Scott Parker’s side slipped to a 2-1 defeat against Blackburn Rovers. Despite enjoying plenty of possession and creating opportunities, the Hammers were undone by poor defending and some particularly questionable goalkeeping.

Joël Piroe’s late goal gave West Ham a chance of rescuing something, but the longer the game went on, the worse we seemed to play. There were some decent individual performances, particularly early on, but others will want to forget this afternoon in a hurry.

A really poor afternoon for the goalkeeper. I thought he could have been far more commanding for Blackburn’s opening goal, whether that meant catching the cross, punching it or simply getting something on the ball. He did make a straightforward late save from Todd Cantwell, but that doesn’t change my overall assessment.

One of West Ham’s more reliable performers before being substituted. Made some useful defensive interceptions and regularly pushed forward to support Bowen down the right. Tried a couple of long throws into the penalty area, although they didn’t really cause Blackburn much trouble.

Had some positive moments going forward, including a driving run from defence and a header that went narrowly wide early in the second half. However, I thought he could have done better for Blackburn’s first goal, and his performance deteriorated badly towards the end. Was fortunate not to concede a penalty before slipping twice during a dreadful passage of play that handed Blackburn a dangerous counter-attack.

Probably West Ham’s most dependable defender on the day. Made several important interceptions, particularly down the left where Blackburn repeatedly threatened, and was willing to carry the ball forward to help build attacks. Also produced a decent cross for Castellanos, although the resulting header went wide.

A mixed afternoon for the youngster. I was impressed with his willingness to get forward, his passing combinations with Austbø and some of the crosses he delivered. However, Blackburn repeatedly targeted his side, often leaving him facing two opponents, and he was beaten too easily in the build-up to the opening goal. Still very much learning the position.

Didn’t think he had a particularly good game. Had plenty of involvement, took the corners and got himself into shooting positions, but too often the final product was disappointing. Lost possession unnecessarily on occasions, failed to close down Blackburn’s second goalscorer quickly enough and wasted a promising late free kick with poor delivery.

You certainly couldn’t fault his work rate. At times he seemed to be everywhere, covering at left-back, appearing on the right and getting forward to support the attack. However, he wasn’t nearly as influential on the ball as he had been during the second half against Cardiff, and his shooting was disappointing. Replaced by Castellanos after 64 minutes.

One of West Ham’s more inventive players, particularly during the first half. Looked to move the ball quickly, attempted some clever through passes and wasn’t afraid to shoot, forcing one save and seeing another effort deflected behind. Not everything worked, but at least he was trying to make things happen.

I was genuinely surprised when he was substituted because he’d been one of West Ham’s livelier attacking players. Produced a lovely dribble past two defenders in the first half, linked up well with Mayers and delivered an excellent cross that Bowen headed against the post. Could perhaps have offered Mayers more defensive protection before Blackburn’s opener, but I thought he was contributing plenty going forward.

Blackburn 2-1 West Ham: Austbo was one of the Hammers better players

Worked hard to make things happen and was heavily involved in West Ham’s attacking play, particularly from the right. Unlucky to see his header strike the post following Austbø’s cross, while a difficult chance from close range went wide as he was falling and sliding. Also produced a lovely chipped delivery for Souček’s late header, but couldn’t quite provide the decisive contribution West Ham needed.

A frustrating performance rescued to some extent by an excellent goal. Too often he seemed hesitant in promising positions, allowing defenders to recover, while one particularly poor pass in the second half wasted a good attacking opportunity. However, he reacted brilliantly to Castellanos’ cross in the 82nd minute, firing high into the roof of the net. Whatever else you say about his performance, he took that chance extremely well.

Replaced Austbø after 58 minutes and was practically anonymous. Considering how involved Austbø had been, the substitution certainly didn’t improve West Ham’s attacking threat down the left.

Introduced for Kanté after 64 minutes and provided the cross that led to Piroe’s goal. Also had a header go wide from a Kilman delivery and was involved in the scramble that presented Bowen with a difficult chance. At least contributed something tangible to the attack.

Didn’t have a great deal of time to influence proceedings, particularly with West Ham having already lost their shape. Played one very silly ball into Orford inside our own penalty area, creating unnecessary danger. However, he also got into one of those familiar goalscoring positions in stoppage time, only to direct his header straight at the goalkeeper from Bowen’s excellent delivery.

Another substitute who came into a team that had lost its shape and was struggling for confidence. Was put in a difficult position by Souček’s poor pass inside the penalty area and was fortunate not to concede a penalty. Difficult to judge him too harshly given the limited time he had on the pitch and the state of the game when he entered.
