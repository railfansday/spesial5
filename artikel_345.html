This pack includes four AirTags to keep an eye on your favorite devices and items in the Find My app. If the worst-case scenario hits and you lose track of something, the app can give you step-by-step directions to find it. The tracker even boasts a speaker that's 50% louder than its predecessor, with a brand-new chime, so you can hear it while you're looking around.

Another benefit of the AirTag is that only you or an authorized user can see its location. If you decide to share that information with another individual and need extra help tracking down your lost item, you can do so temporarily and securely. Plus, no location data or history is stored on the AirTag itself, and its battery can last more than a year before it needs replacing.
