The Best Burgundy Shoes to Wear With All Your Favorite Fall Outfits

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

From office-ready loafers to weekend sneakers, these rich maroon pairs go with everything.

Few colors are as synonymous with fall as burgundy. Something about this moody tone instantly elevates everything it touches, whether it’s a dress, a wool cardigan, a sleek handbag, or a pair of shoes. And speaking of shoes, we’ve scoured the internet to find the best styles to wear now with your favorite autumn pieces.

Everything from boots to ballet flats, Mary Janes, and even sneakers is found in the perfect shade of maroon. Think of this wine shade as a neutral in disguise. It flatters black, navy, olive, camel, charcoal, and cream with equal ease, and it’s the rare color that moves effortlessly from the office to dinner to a weekend farmers market without a second thought.

Ahead, we’ve rounded up the burgundy styles worth your attention this fall, from loafers and boots to heels and flats that earn a place in your rotation.

We didn’t know we needed polka dot mesh Mary Janes in this rich burgundy shade until we came across these Sézane stunners. They’re the kind of shoe you can trust on any occasion, and compliments tend to follow wherever they go. On cooler days, pair them with your favorite cashmere socks.

A classic penny loafer with a slightly tapered silhouette, this pair is ready to carry you from busy workdays or school days to laid-back weekends alike. It’s a timeless staple that swaps the traditional black or brown for a fresh burgundy, giving everyday fall pieces a new perspective.

Unlike most ballet flats, these patent leather pairs feature a cute drawstring peep-toe, an elasticized back, and flexible soles for easy walking. We love the timeless silhouette and glossy finish, which instantly make them feel more party-ready.

Materials: Upper: 100% Cow Leather, Lining: 100% Goat Leather

There’s something undeniably chic about burgundy sneakers, and this Jimmy Choo pair is exhibit A. The rich, wine-toned finish dresses up denim and dresses down tailored trousers, making them an easy way to add polish to a weekend uniform.

These sleek burgundy pumps instantly elevate any ensemble, denim included. They’re the go-tos you can count on whether you’re headed to dinner, a coffee date, or just running errands around town.

Despite their familiar silhouette, these Le Monde Beryl mules bring a fresh perspective to fall dressing. A rounded toe and slightly curved heel add a cool factor to the overall look, and the bordeaux shade is undeniably chic on its own.

With a chic kitten heel and a sharp, structured silhouette, including an ultra-pointed toe, these black cherry leather boots lean into every mood and occasion.

Luxury speaks volumes in these stunning stilettos, crafted from silky burgundy satin. The open toe and slender heel create an elongated line, making them a showstopping choice for holiday parties and special evenings out.

If your feet can’t take stilettos, consider a tall platform heel instead. This burgundy option features a mega block heel, a rounded toe, and a delicate ankle strap, combining for a sophisticated pair with retro flair.

Designed in collaboration with Alison Roman, this snakeskin mule is her go-to hosting shoe, and we can see why. The polished silhouette says party-ready, while the kitten heel keeps things comfortable and understated. Extra points for the burgundy snakeskin python print!

Materials: Python-embossed Italian calf leather

These burgundy wedges with a V-shaped vamp and a soft matte finish are an easy way to add a little height and a lot of polish to your fall wardrobe.

Inspired by retro running shoes, the Nikoletta sneakers land at the intersection of sporty and cool. The crinkled patent stripes contrast beautifully with the soft leather, while the red logo-embossed tongue adds another bold statement.
