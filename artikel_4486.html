The 2026 Mazda CX-50 Turbo packs some impressive power.

Under the CX-50 Turbo’s hood lies a turbocharged 2.5-liter inline-four producing 256 horsepower and 320 pound-feet of torque on 93-octane gas.

On lower-octane fuels, the CX-50 produces a little less power and torque, but at least you aren’t limited to premium alone.

In our hands, the last CX-50 we tested shot to 60 mph in 6.4 seconds, which is pretty quick for a compact family SUV.

It’s a decently grippy family machine, too, circling our skidpad at 0.84 g.

Lower trims of CX-50 make do with a naturally aspirated 2.5-liter, but all models include all-wheel drive.
