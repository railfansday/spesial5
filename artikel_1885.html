Liverpool 4-0 Manchester United: Red Devils mired in bottom three after nightmare Mauricio Pochettino debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Mauricio Pochettino endured a nightmare debut as Manchester United manager as he watched his new side fall to a 4-0 defeat to Liverpool.

The Red Devils endured a horrendous start to the match, with Leny Yoro sent off inside eight minutes and the hosts two goals up inside the opening quarter of an hour on Merseyside.

Dominik Szoboszlai slammed home the resulting free kick after Yoro had denied Alexander Isak a clear goalscoring opportunity, who latched onto a mistake from Matthijs de Ligt, who misjudged a ball clipped over the top.

It was two not long after as Florian Wirtz hooked home with an improvised left-footed volley after Rio Ngumoha had darted beyond Diogo Dalot as if he weren’t there down the left, dinking it into the middle for the German to slot in via the crossbar.

Isak produced a superb centre-forward’s performance but was wasteful with his finishing, and he sliced wide after another De Ligt error in the second half, and he was denied by Senne Lammens, who parried well after a brilliant break through the middle from Barcola.

It was three after the hour, as Ngumoha slotted home a deserved third after Barcola had left Lisandro Martinez for dead, burning away from the recovering Bruno Fernandes before squaring for his opposite winger to slide in the killer.

Substitute Victor Munoz emphatically slammed in the fourth after more superb work from Wirtz, who found the Spaniard having won the ball deep in United territory.

Pochettino could only watch on in horror as the enormity of the task he has undertaken became crystal clear to him. For now, his men sit 19th in the Premier League table, firmly mired in the relegation zone.
