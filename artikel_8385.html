Personal Consumer Issues • Re: Built in Grill Options

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

I'd suggest taking a moment to evaluate your functional cooking needs (especially because you already have a BGE) and work forwards from there:
1) What do you plan to cook on the gas grill
2) How much volume do you plan to cook on the gas
3) Do you need / want high sear capability
4) Do you need / want low temp cooking capability
5) What is your price sensitivity
6) Is this a marine application - if so, there are grills made with better suited steel (316)

A lot of my cooking is sous vide + sear and I briefly considered a standalone sear station in addition to a gas grill.

Ultimately ended up going with a TEC Grill which has a unique technology that minimizes food drying due to convection and is able to deliver both sear level heat and very low heat. I've been happy so far. Downsides are cost, size and single zone control.

Separately, assuming you're bringing electrical to your outdoor kitchen, strongly recommend the minimal upcharge to bring 220 V electrical and add a NEMA 6-20 plug to your kitchen which opens up a whole world of reasonable cost, but significantly more powerful commercial accessories (e.g., an induction hob) without the cost of a built-in.
