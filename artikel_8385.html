Personal Consumer Issues • Re: Passkeys vs Passwords

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Passkey Implementation varies all over the place. In my experience, Schwab’s implementation is the most straightforward. You click on the link on their website and it uses one of the passkeys you have implemented and nothing else. In my case, either windows hello or a Yubikey.

Other websites introduce their own little kinks. For example, Wells Fargo bank requires you to enter a six digit code from a text message after you’ve logged in using the passkey. Fidelity allows you to login using only a pass key, but does not allow you to name the passkeys you have registered.

Still, the experts here say their adoption by the financial industry is a substantial increase in cyber security.
