Al Michaels shares text from Aaron Rodgers during 'Black Friday' Steelers game

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Veteran broadcaster Al Michaels shed some more light on Aaron Rodgers' departure from the Pittsburgh Steelers during the broadcast of the team's "Black Friday" game against the Denver Broncos.

Michaels shared that Rodgers had texted him that day about his release from the Steelers after his benching and subsequent scorched-earth comments criticizing Pittsburgh coach Mike McCarthy in a Wednesday, Nov. 25, episode of "The Pat McAfee Show."

In the text, Rodgers justified his actions and also revealed the Steelers had barred him from the facility after his benching.

"Earlier today, I heard from Aaron," Michaels shared on the Amazon broadcast after a commercial break in the fourth quarter. "(He) said, 'A lot of talk about the sanctity of the locker room. The code or sanctity exists to protect the integrity of the locker room, not to provide immunity from accountability for its leadership.'"

"He then also said, 'They took this public. Never heard from the head coach. Then they leaked (the benching) at 11 p.m.,'" Michaels continued, reading Rodgers' text off his phone in the booth. "'They also barred me from the facility, that's why I went to McAfee.'"

Despite Rodgers' defense, he has faced plenty of criticism for his comments about McCarthy, as many former players have disagreed with his choice to air his grievances in such a public forum.

Notably, one of Rodgers' former teammates, T.J. Lang, referred to the quarterback's maneuver as "despicable." Meanwhile, former NFL offensive lineman Damien Woody said the 42-year-old quarterback put his teammates in "an incredible bind" by throwing McCarthy under the bus.

And on Amazon Prime Video's pregame show, legendary tight end Tony Gonzalez called Rodgers' decision to air his dirty laundry "selfish" while former NFL quarterback Ryan Fitzpatrick opined Rodgers had "sacrificed" McCarthy to end his time in Pittsburgh.

The feeling among Pittsburgh's fans was a bit more balanced. While few were happy with Rodgers, others were equally upset about the issues he outlined about McCarthy.

Perhaps they will feel differently after seeing the Steelers improve to 7-4 with a 27-5 win over the Broncos on "Black Friday."
