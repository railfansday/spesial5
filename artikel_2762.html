Best performers from 49ers' shootout win vs. Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The San Francisco 49ers traveled south to take on the Dallas Cowboys at AT&T Stadium on Sunday for a battle between non-division NFC rivals, and Kyle Shanahan's team earned a 46-40 win, moving to 8-1 on the year.

San Francisco's offense played very well, while the defense left a lot to be desired, but it was enough to drop the Cowboys to 4-6.

As we continue to comb through this victory, let's take a look at some of San Francisco's top performers from this Week 10 matchup.

Purdy played an incredible game despite being under constant pressure and taking five sacks. He completed 78.6% of his passes for 389 yards and three touchdowns while rushing for another 21 yards on two scrambles. This looked like the performance of a potential MVP.

Sunday was one of the best games of McCaffrey's career. He rushed for 62 yards and a touchdown on 4.8 yards per attempt, but he was even more effective as a pass catcher, hauling in all four of his targets for 161 yards and two touchdowns. Not being forced to be the bell cow this year has really helped him this year.

Spelling McCaffrey a lot this year, Black has played really well in his rookie year. Against the Cowboys, he ran for 53 yards and a touchdown on 4.8 yards per attempt. His 25-yard score was incredible, as he ran over two defenders on his way to the end zone. Black also caught two passes for 13 yards.

Samuel made his return to the field this week after missing time due to an injury. He caught three passes for 31 yards, including an 11-yard touchdown. He also carried the ball three times for 78 yards, including a 70-yard score on a reverse. Samuel brings a different dimension to this offseason when he's healthy.
