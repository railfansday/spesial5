Tiger Woods Set to Return to Competitive Golf for First Time in 2 Years at 2026 Hero World Challenge

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Tiger Woods is returning to competitive golf

The 82-time PGA Tour champ will play in the Hero World Challenge, a tournament he hosts, in the Bahamas next month

Woods last played in The Open Championship in July 2024, about eight months before he ruptured his Achilles tendon

Tiger Woods is returning to competitive golf.

The 82-time PGA Tour champion will play in the Hero World Challenge next month, an unofficial PGA Tour event in the Bahamas on Dec. 3 featuring a 20-player field.

Woods, 50, was among the final three players announced on Tuesday, Nov. 17 that have been added to the tournament, along with Rickie Fowler and 2019 U.S. Open winner Gary Woodland.

The golfer last played competitively in July 2024 at The Open Championship. In the spring of 2025 ahead of the Masters, Woods announced that he had ruptured his Achilles tendon, and he spent the following year rehabbing the injury.

Tiger Woods in February 2026 at the Genesis Invitational Golf Tournament.Credit: Javier Rojas/PI via ZUMA Press Wire/Shutterstock

Woods is also expected to compete in Jupiter Links GC's season-opener on Dec. 27, and could return to the PNC Championship, which he has historically played with his son, Charlie, according to ESPN.

Last week, the 15-time majors champ said he was "training quite a bit" and "feeling better," nearly eight months after he was arrested and charged with a DUI in Florida.

"I'm feeling better, no doubt about that," Woods told Sirius XM PGA Tour Radio in an interview that aired Tuesday, Nov. 10. "I've been training quite a bit. I will be at Hero [World Challenge]. I'm there either way, so I'll see you there."

Woods was arrested after he rolled over his Range Rover in a two-car accident in Jupiter Island, Fla. on March 27. In an affidavit obtained by PEOPLE at the time, deputies with the Martin County Sheriff's Office found two hydrocodone pills in Woods' left pants pocket while at the scene.

Woods apologized after the incident and underwent rehab in Switzerland.

During the court proceedings in September, the father of two changed his plea in the case from not guilty to no contest for lesser charges. Judge Darren Steele suspended Woods' license for five years, according to court records obtained by PEOPLE at the time.

Later that month, excited onlookers observed Woods hitting balls during a clinic at Liberty National in New Jersey ahead of the Nexus Cup, in a video shared on Woods' TGR Live Events Instagram account.
