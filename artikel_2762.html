Miami commit A'mir Sears to be honored as a 2027 Navy All-American

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Navy All-American Bowl Road to the Dome tour will visit Christopher Columbus High School in Miami, Florida, on November 13th to recognize A'mir Sears as a 2027 Navy All-American. Christopher Columbus High School will honor Sears before classmates, teammates, family, and friends during a pep rally.

Sears will play in the annual East vs. West matchup inside the Alamodome in San Antonio, Texas at 1 p.m. ET on Saturday, January 9, 2027, live on NBC and Peacock, which marks the twenty-seventh anniversary of the event.

The nationwide Road to the Dome tour will honor 100 All-Americans with live and virtual jersey presentations. The series will be released weekly through social platforms across NBC Sports.

What: A'mir Sears to be officially honored as a 2027 Navy All-American and presented with his honorary Navy All-American jersey. Where: Christopher Columbus High School 3000 SW 87 Avenue Miami, FL 33165
