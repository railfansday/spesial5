Indianapolis Colts Injury Report: Who’s In and Who’s Out

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Indianapolis Colts today released their Friday injury report for week 10 of the NFL season against the Miami Dolphins.

Tight end Mo Alie-Cox (groin) has been ruled OUT for Sundays game against the Dolphins. The Colts are looking thin at tight end this week. With Alie-Cox out the team will have to lean heavily on Warren and Ogletree. There could be a practice squad elevation needed too.

Linebackers Jaylon Carlies (neck) and Akeem Davis-Gaithers (knee) have both been ruled OUT for Sundays game against the Dolphins. CJ Allen and Bryce Boettcher will start at linebacker this week backups Austin Ajake and Tahj Chamnets will have a bigger role to play and expect a practice squad call up for West Weeks.

Long snapper Luke Rhodes (hamstring) has been ruled OUT for Sundays game against the Dolphins. With Rhodes out the Colts will have have to elevate from the practice squad to fill the void Rhodes leaves. The Colts signed long snapper Jacob Bobenmoyer to the practice squad earlier in the week.

Right tackle Jalen Travis (knee) has been ruled OUT of Sundays game against the Dolphins. With Travis out this week the Colts will have to find a suitable replacement at right tackle, it could mean Luke Tenuta starting in his place or a reshuffle moving Matt Goncalves to right tackle a starting Dalton Tucker at right guard.

Defensive tackle Jerry Tillery (calf) has been ruled OUT of Sundays game against the Dolphins. Expect a bigger role in the defensive tackle rotation for Tim Smith and Kevin Givens with Tillery out. There could also be a practice squad elevation for Cam Ball.

Defensive end Arden Key (hamstring) and Tight End Will Mallory (hamstring) are both QUESTIONABLE for Sundays game against the Dolphins. Key managed to practice all week but hadn't played since the London game. Mallory was a full participants all week but injured his hamstring during Fridays practice.
