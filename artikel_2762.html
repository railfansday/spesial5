Don Willard to serve as the head referee for 2 NFL games on same day after league switches up assignments

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

NFL referee Don Willard is set to serve as the head referee for two NFL games in the same day. It is not readily known whether this has happened before, according to The Athletic.

He's being pressed into double duty because of the league scrambling to reassign officiating crews due to travel issues caused by Friday's unprecedented four-hour nationwide ground stop for domestic flights in the United States.

This impacted travel plans across sports leagues, including a college football game between Ole Miss and Oklahoma, which was moved to Sunday.

It didn't impact start times for any NFL games, but it did force the league to switch up some referee assignments.

Willard and his crew — umpire Terry Killens, field judge Jabir Walker and side judge Justin Larrew — will begin the day calling the Miami Dolphins-Indianapolis Colts game, set to kick off at 1 p.m. ET in Indiana.

After the game, Willard and Co. will then drive 110 miles to Cincinnati, Ohio, to call the Pittsburgh Steelers-Cincinnati Bengals game for Week 10's "Sunday Night Football," which begins at 8:20 p.m. ET.

While only three officiating crew changes were needed, one could be for the history books.

Willard has been an NFL official for nine years, including officiating six playoff games. He typically serves as a side judge for Scott Novak's crew.

The NFL made the reassignments based on driving distance from officials' hometowns. The other two moves were to have umpire Barry Anderson take over for Alex Moore in the game between the Jacksonville Jaguars and the Tennessee Titans, while Duane Heydt steps in for Shawn Smith in the Carolina Panthers-New Orleans Saints NFC South battle.

Originally, Carl Cheffers was supposed to referee the Miami-Indianapolis game, while Clete Blakeman was supposed to lead the Pittsburgh-Cincinnati game.

Willard will now be the head referee for both games.
