Kyle Shanahan has positive injury update on WR Mike Evans

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The San Francisco 49ers offense could be in line for a significant lift against the Minnesota Vikings.

Head coach Kyle Shanahan on Monday in a conference call with reporters said wide receiver Mike Evans is traveling to Colorado Springs where the team is staying through the week before heading to Mexico City. The hope, per Shanahan, is that Evans will be able to practice in the leadup to Sunday night's showdown with the Vikings.

"I'm hoping to, just not sure yet," Shanahan said. "Still too early. He's flying in tonight with the guys who are left behind, who didn't travel with us to Dallas. So, we'll see how these next few days go and see if he can get out there sometime this week."

Evans has been on the sidelines since Week 6 with Achilles tendinitis. He hasn't had a practice since Friday of Week 5 when he was a limited participant heading into the 49ers' game against the Seattle Seahawks. He hasn't been a full participant in practice since Week 2.

Even if Evans isn't able to get on the field for Sunday, the optimism about his return to the field for Week 11 is a good sign for the star receiver moving forward. It wouldn't be a surprise if the team runs him through a handful of limited practices before eventually bringing him back.

San Francisco has been very patient with Evans' injury and it wouldn't make a ton of sense if they went away from that at this juncture.
