Oregon vs. Oregon State men's basketball updates, score, TV, streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

They may now be in different conferences, but Oregon vs. Oregon State in men's basketball still constitutes a rivalry.

The Ducks and Beavers meet at Portland's Moda Center for the 367th game between the two programs with a 6 p.m. Nov. 19 tipoff.

Both teams are undefeated and playing under first-year head coaches after Oregon's Dana Altman retired and Oregon State's Wayne Tinkle was let go.

The Register-Guard will have live updates, photos, video and postgame coverage from Oregon vs. Oregon State. Come back to follow along.
