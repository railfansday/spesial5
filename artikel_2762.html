Week 11 AFC West roundup, Chiefs keep pace with Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

While we already know that the Denver Broncos managed to get a win in Week 11, taking down the Las Vegas Raiders 38-24 on Sunday, the Broncos are still in a tight battle for first place in the AFC West with the Kansas City Chiefs. Also, we have to take a peek at what the Los Angeles Chargers are doing to see if there's any drama at the bottom of the division standings.

QB Patrick Mahomes: 27/39 (69.2%), 324 yards, 3 TDs, 0 INTs

RB Emmett Johnson: 23 carries, 105 rushing yards (4.6 ypc), 1 TD, 4 catches, 38 receiving yards

TE Travis Kelce: 6 catches, 74 yards, 2 TDs

EDGE George Karlaftis: 4 tackles, 2.5 sacks, 2 TFLs, 5 QB hits

CB Nohl Williams: 3 tackles, 2 PDs, 1 QB hit

This game was such a blowout that Mahomes barely even played in the fourth quarter, as Justin Fields got some reps off the bench. The Chiefs' offense has been trucking along without Kenneth Walker III for the second week in a row, as the rookie, Johnson, has done a great job picking up the slack. Also, Kansas City's defense has looked really sharp recently, allowing 13 or fewer points in each of the last three contests, while Karlaftis has really come alive as a pass rusher.

The win allows the Chiefs to keep pace with the Broncos for first place. Denver has the advantage currently with a better record against common opponents, but this will be a battle of which team flinches first down the stretch. They split the season series, so one loss over the next seven weeks could be the difference.

QB Justin Herbert: 15/21 (71.4%), 131 yards, 2 TDs, 0 INTs, 8 rushes, 32 rushing yards, 1 rushing TD

RB Omarion Hampton: 14 carries, 89 yards (6.4 ypc)

WR Ladd McConkey: 5 catches, 37 yards, 2 TDs

After getting their first win last week, the Chargers had a chance to make it two in a row against the lowly Jets. However, Los Angeles continues to prove it is one of the worst teams in the league, getting outgained by 80 yards despite the three-point loss. The Bolts' defense really let them down this time, allowing New York to go nine-for-11 on third downs while Geno Smith was hit just once and no defender had a pass breakup.

Fernando Mendoza not satisfied: "Although I believe [my performance] was an improvement, there's a lot of things I need to improve on for the next week," the rookie quarterback said during his post-game press conference, via a video The Athletic's Sam Warren posted on X/Twitter.

Klint Kubiak on what he's learned about Mendoza: "I've learned that he can run the operation. Now he has to get reps, improve, and get points on the board for the team," via Warren. "At the end of the day, it's a quarterback's job to move the ball down the field and get points. As a group, we have to limit turnovers and score more."

19 things we learned in Denver: "Las Vegas running back Ashton Jeanty had a run of 46 yards and a catch for a 68-yard touchdown," S&BP's Bill Williamson wrote. "It's his best explosion since early in the season. The Raiders had 143 rushing yards, the first team they cracked 100 yards on the ground since Week 3."

Raiders open as underdogs: In what's pretty much an elimination game for Las Vegas' playoff hopes, the Silver and Black are opening as 4.5-point underdogs on Sunday Night Football, according to our friends at FanDuel Sportsbook.
