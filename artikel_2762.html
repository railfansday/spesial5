How Daniel Jones is expected to approach looming free agency with plenty of interest expected

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Daniel Jones will wait until it feels just right.

If the quarterback, who was released by the Colts on Friday, can clear waivers and be a free agent on Monday, he is unlikely to sign immediately, sources told ESPN Sunday. Rather, Jones will wait for the right opportunity with the right team.

Jones, who is out just six months into the $88 million deal he signed before the season, is expected to draw much interest, possibly from the Patriots, Cardinals and Ravens, among others.

However, sources are saying he will wait and could likely help come the playoffs if a team is navigating an injury.

Indianapolis Colts quarterback Daniel Jones (17) looks on from the sideline during a game against the Houston Texans in Houston, Texas, Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

Following a strong 8-2 start to the 2025 season, Jones suffered a torn Achilles and was sidelined for the remainder of the year and watched the Colts miss the playoffs for the fifth consecutive season.

This season has been a disaster for the franchise, which fired its general manager, Chris Ballard, and was a seller at the trade deadline, dealing away Quenton Nelson, DeForest Buckner, Keenan Allen, and Josh Downs.

Last week, the Colts lost to the Texans 26-3 to drop to 3-8 this season.

Jones threw for 1,974 yards, recording seven touchdowns and 13 interceptions.

The Ravens previously made an offer to acquire the 29-year-old and a fifth-round pick around the deadline in exchange for a fourth-round pick before turning to Jacoby Brissett, according to ESPN's Adam Schefter.

Indianapolis Colts quarterback Daniel Jones (17) scrambles against the Houston Texans on Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026, at Reliant Stadium in Houston. Getty Images

The offer had a condition in which the fifth-rounder would return to Indianapolis if Baltimore went to the playoffs.

Jones will hit the waiver wire and it will cost any team $2.3 million this season, with $38 million non-guaranteed for the 2027-28 season.

Anthony Richardson Sr. will be the starter for the organization against the Giants in Week 12.
