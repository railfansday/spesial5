Trish Stratus Marks Huge Personal Milestone 25 Years Later

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Trish Stratus was a superstar from the first day she showed up on WWE, or WWF, television, and her career just continued to blossom in the years that followed.

She took to social media on Wednesday to commemorate her first-ever championship, which she won 25 years ago at Survivor Series.

With the 2026 Survivor Series coming later this month, this post and this memory are right on time. Let's talk wrestling.

Greensboro Coliseum, Greensboro, North Carolina

Six-pack challenge for the vacant WWF Women's Championship

Lita, Jazz, Ivory, Jacqueline, Molly Holly

Stratus won the vacant title in a six-pack challenge at the Greensboro Coliseum on Nov. 18, 2001, beating Lita, Jazz, Ivory, Jacqueline and Molly Holly. The belt had been vacant since Chyna, the previous champion, left the company without dropping it.

Jazz was making her debut in the match. The Greensboro card was headlined by Team WWF vs. The Alliance in a winner-take-all elimination match.

Stratus' first title was important because it validated her as an in-ring performer and not just an on-screen presence.

Initially, she showed up and caught almost everyone's attention because of her striking looks and style.

Stratus debuted as a valet on WWF Sunday Night Heat on March 19, 2000, before moving into full-time competition.

But later, she would show that not only was she a student of professional wrestling, she was determined to become good at the actual art form inside the ring.

She went on to win the Women's Championship seven times, a record at the time. The seventh came in her retirement match at Unforgiven 2006 in her hometown of Toronto.

Stratus' career trajectory is very similar to, and in some instances a microcosm of, the development of women's wrestling as a whole, especially in WWE.

Stratus and Lita main-evented Raw in December 2004 with the Women's Championship on the line. WWE fans voted Stratus the Diva of the Decade in 2003.

Women performers went from being primarily an on-screen visual presence to becoming active participants in the in-ring action.

Stratus' career mirrored that evolution, from where women's wrestling was when she started to where it is now, as she sits as a semi-retired, recognized legend.

Stratus entered the WWE Hall of Fame in 2013 and has returned for occasional matches since. Those include a 2023 feud with Becky Lynch that ended in a steel cage match at Payback and an appearance in the 2025 Royal Rumble.

Survivor Series 2026 takes place Nov. 28 at Daikin Park in Houston, with a women's WarGames match on the card.
