Wembanyama scores 50 as Spurs crush Pelicans to close homestand

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The San Antonio Spurs closed out their three-game homestand with a 114-91 victory over the New Orleans Pelicans at the Frost Bank Center on Saturday night in front of a sold out crowd, which included one very special celebrity guest, singer and actress Selena Gomez.

Victor Wembanyama posted 50 points, 18 rebounds, 5 three-pointers, and 5 blocks in 32 minutes. At one point during the first half, Wembanyama hit one of his five threes on the night, turned towards the crowd and smiled in Gomez's direction.

"I had to show off for Selena (Gomez)," he said, smiling as he answered.

Wembanyama came out aggressive from the opening tip, scoring 17 of the Spurs' 32 points in the opening quarter. He went to rim for dunks, hit free throws, and scored from the mid range. San Antonio had 30 assists on the night and 14 of those were off a made Wemby basket. Nothing New Orleans did worked on this night to stop the Spurs' French star from accomplishing his goal of scoring 50 points, no matter how hard they tried.

Pelicans coach Jamahl Mosley doubled Wembanyama with multiple players, but the Spurs' forward backed down the smaller player right into the paint or when he was triple teamed, he found Julian Champagnie or Devin Vassell for an open three.

"I thought he started the game off aggressive," Spurs Head Coach Mitch Johnson said of Wembanyama after the game. "It's so interesting when people play like that, especially with his talent and then it helps with your shots. I thought he did a really good job of playing with force."

Despite Wembanyama's great night, Johnson still had a criticism for his star player and that is his passing. Late in the half, Wemby secured a defensive rebound and brought the ball up court. Instead of finding the open Keldon Johnson, the French star pulled up for three and missed, ignoring his open teammate.

"Keldon has to have the ball in that moment," Mitch Johnson said. "When you play the right way, things like that happen."

As good as the Spurs looked on Saturday, they still struggled in the non-Wemby minutes. New Orleans outscored the Spurs 22-13 in the first half when Wembanyama sat, but things were even when he sat in the second half. The reason? Johnson put in Luke Kornet, Harrison Barnes, and Carter Bryant. However, he kept Devin Vassell and Stephon Castle on the floor with that second unit.

"I thought we had some moments where we did a really good job defensively in terms of shrinking the floor and showing crowds and gang rebounding," Mitch Johnson said. "That group, probably, the worst minutes of the non Victor, has nothing to do with Luke, in my opinion. It's when we take quick shots, and then we're in transition defense a lot, and we're on our heels."

As a result of those lineup changes, Castle turned it on and scored 19 points on the night to go along with six assists.

"It's been reflective ever since he's been on this team," Wembanayama said of Castle. "He's been impactful in a meaningful way and he's impacted winning ever since he arrived here."

Keldon Johnson had 10 points off the bench, scoring in double figures for the fourth time in the last five games. If he can continue to deliver at that level consistently, the bench unit will be much improved as the season goes on. "When I do the right things on the court, the scoring will come," Keldon said after the game.

De'Aaron Fox slipped on a wet spot on the floor during the first half and was limping a bit before being taken out of the game. He would return later and Mitch Johnson said after the game that Fox was fine. Still, Fox scored just 5 points on 1-for-4 shooting and one has to wonder if his hip injury is bothering him.

The Spurs are now 14-3 on the season and will return to the court on Monday night when they face the Sacramento Kings at 9 p.m.
