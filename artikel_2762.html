Real Madrid still interested in Bayern Munich superstar; 2027 sale possible if renewal not agreed

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Real Madrid's interest in Michael Olise remains a transfer storyline to watch despite the arrival of Yan Diomande, but Bayern Munich are determined to protect their investment in the French winger.

Reports from L'Equipe continue to link the 24-year-old with a move to the Santiago Bernabeu, while Bayern are working towards securing his long-term future through a contract extension.

The outlet mentions that Los Blancos continue to admire the French forward and their interest has not faded despite last summer's disappointment.

Olise's situation is an interesting challenge for Real Madrid. The Spanish giants previously explored the possibility of signing him but were reluctant to pursue an expensive deal while Bayern refused to negotiate.

His existing contract, which runs until 2029, also gives the German champions considerable control over his future.

Real Madrid's pursuit of Olise is not an entirely new development.

Real Madrid's interest in Michael Olise has not faded. (Photo by Adam Pretty/Getty Images)

Notably, the Frenchman was already on the club's radar before the 2026 summer transfer window, but Bayern's refusal to entertain negotiations prevented the Spanish side from making significant progress.

Indeed, Olise wanted to join Real Madrid, although Bayern remained determined to retain him.

This time around, however, the likes of Kylian Mbappe and Ibrahima Konate have discussed the possibility of a move with their international teammate during France gatherings, giving the potential transfer a major boost.

Despite the fact that Real Madrid now have Diomande, that recruitment decision does not necessarily eliminate their longer-term interest in Olise, whose quality and attacking versatility make him an attractive prospect.

As far as Bayern Munich are concerned, their priority is to prevent another summer of transfer uncertainty, which is why the German champions have opened discussions over an improved contract, with the intention of rewarding Olise's importance to Vincent Kompany's attacking project.

Bayern Munich are keen to extend Olise's contract. (Photo by Alex Grimm/Getty Images)

The initial discussions have already taken place, although an agreement has not been reached. In fact, Bayern officials have also indicated that the club wants clarity on the winger's intentions by next summer.

Importantly, Bayern are under no immediate pressure to sell because they are comfortable with Olise's current situation and see no urgency to accelerate contract negotiations.

However, the outlet clearly mentions that the outcome of the contract discussions could determine whether Real Madrid revisit their interest next summer.

If Olise agrees to extend his stay, Bayern would strengthen their position further and make negotiations considerably more difficult.

Conversely, a failure to reach an agreement could create an opportunity for Real Madrid to explore a transfer, although it would not automatically mean Bayern would accept an offer.
