As U.S. Infrastructure Crumbles, ABC’s ‘World News Tonight’ Posts Highest Week-Long Daily Ratings Since COVID

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A week after the midterm elections generated tremendous audiences for the network evening newscasts, last week’s unpredictable string of news — including the Microsoft Outlook blackout, followed by the nationwide grounding of air flights — propelled ABC’s “World News Tonight” to heights the telecast hasn’t seen in years.

The busy news week also included the Nvidia scandal, the Brooklyn building collapse, the Pete Hegseth impeachment vote, fallout from the midterm elections (and late-breaking results like Mississippi’s Sen.-elect Scott Colom) and more. According to ABC News, “World News Tonight” averaged more than 9 million Total Viewers on each of its five telecasts last week — the first time it has done that in nearly six years, during the COVID era (specifically, the week of Feb. 15, 2021).

“Audiences are turning to ‘World News Tonight with David Muir’ in numbers we haven’t seen since the COVID pandemic, a powerful reflection of the trust they place in our journalism during uncertain times,” said ABC News president Almin Karamehmedovic in a statement to Variety. “Amid a relentless week of breaking news, culminating in Friday’s nationwide ground stop, more than 9 million Americans tuned in every single night. We are deeply grateful for their confidence and never take that responsibility for granted.”

Over the entire week (except for Monday, which wasn’t included due to Monday Night Football), “World News Tonight with David Muir” was No. 1 with total viewers (9.251 million) on all of broadcast and cable (excluding sports) for the second week in a row and landed its best number in four years, according to Nielsen’s Live+Same Day Big Data Plus Panel Program Ratings.“World News Tonight with David Muir” was also No. 1 with adults 25-54 (1.366 million) and adults 18-49 (993,000). In comparison, “NBC Nightly News” averaged 7.117 million total viewers, 1.161 million with adults 25-54 and 849,000 with adults 18-49, while “CBS Evening News” averaged 4.633 million viewers, 689,000 with adults 25-54 and 527,000 among adults 18-49.

Season to date, “World News Tonight” is up 14% year-to-year in total viewers (8.679 million vs. 7.586 million), up 34% with adults 25-54 (1.214 million vs. 907,000) and up 40% with adults 18-49 (883,000 vs. 629,000), based on Most Current Big Data+Panel Ratings. ABC notes that “World News Tonight” is posting its best ratings since the 2020-2021 season and that its total viewers lead over “NBC Nightly News” is its largest advantage in 32 years, and its lead over “CBS Evening News” is its largest advantage in at least 35 years.
