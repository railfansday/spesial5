Gisele Bündchen Shares Rare Family Photos in Honor of Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Gisele Bündchen shared a glimpse inside her life as a mom of three in honor of Thanksgiving: “Gratitude is contagious.”

Gisele Bündchen is giving a peek inside her private world.

The supermodel—who shares kids Benjamin, 16, and Vivian, 13, with ex-husband Tom Brady as well as a 21-month-old son with husband Joaquim Valente—shared never-before-seen family photos in honor of Thanksgiving.

“The amount of goodness we experience in life depends on our ability to notice it,” Gisele captioned her Nov. 26 Instagram post alongside a slew of family memories. “Gratitude is contagious, and the more we can appreciate the beauty around us, the more joy we bring into our lives and the lives of others.”

"May we always find something to be grateful for, not just today, but every day," she added. "Happy Thanksgiving! Sending you all so much love."

Offering a closer look into her model life, the 46-year-old shared a photo holding her youngest child up over her head as they enjoyed a day at the beach.

Meanwhile, another snap showed the toddler getting some father-son time as Joaquim held his hand by the ocean at sunset. And later in the photo dump, the trio all posed together as Gisele planted a kiss on the jiu-jitsu instructor’s cheek.

Allowing her fans to strut even further into her life away from the runway, Gisele also shared insight into her blended family’s sweet bond, with one photo capturing her eldest two kids sitting outside while their little brother clung on tight to Vivian.

The teen also cuddled up to her baby bro as they traveled on an airplane. While Vivian—donning a bright pink top—placed a kiss on the baby’s head, he cozied in close for an extra dose of sibling love.

And Benjamin definitely wasn’t left out on the sidelines as another photo captured the shirtless teen holding his brother on his hip as they admired LED lights.

Elsewhere in the post, Gisele also shed light on her wellness routine, which included practicing yoga, eating açaí bowls and bonding with animals.

And while the Lessons author—who tied the knot with Joaquim in December 2025—is notoriously private about her personal life, this Turkey Day wouldn’t be the first time she gave thanks to her husband.

After all, she gave her him some love on his 39th birthday, calling him a “great man” on his special day.

“How lucky I am,” she continued on Instagram Stories Nov. 7 in a message translated from Portuguese. “I love you so much!”

As for what makes him a model life partner? She previously thanked him for “leading by example, and for the values you embody, love, humility, integrity, discipline, kindness and consistency.”

“You are an incredible role model,” she added in the June 21 Instagram post. “We are all so grateful for you, and we love you so much.”
