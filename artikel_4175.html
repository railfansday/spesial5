Japanese Charts: Zelda: Ocarina Of Time Physical Takes The Lion's Share Of Its Japanese Sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

After Nintendo's reveal this week that The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time had shifted 5.2 million units in its first four days, it will come as no surprise to hear that it's a smash hit in Japan. But still, Famitsu's latest numbers are in, and the physical is flying!

According to this week's chart, Link's latest produced ~537,000 physical sales in its chart debut — giving it the lion's share of Japan's 810,000 sales that Nintendo reported, and leaving around 273,000 sales for digital. As we say, it's hardly surprising, but still!

It makes all the other entries this week look piddly by comparison. Nintendo Switch Sports Resort and Rhythm Heaven Groove round out the podium with a comparatively tiny 16,621 and 8,793 sales, respectively, and Stellar Blade: Complete Edition made its debut in fourth.

While Switch 2 sales are actually down following last week's bump, the console still leads this week's hardware chart by a comfortable margin. Nintendo's latest pulled in an additional ~53,000 sales this week, topping the PS5 Digital Edition, which came in with 11,377.

Once again, however, those Digital Edition sales swing things in Sony's favour over the Switch 1 when we look at the total SKU numbers for the week. The three PS5 systems combined this time for 11,699, while the Switch 1 units came in at 5,148.
