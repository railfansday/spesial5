Who Voices The Characters In The Legend Of Zelda: Ocarina Of Time Remake

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Since The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time was originally released nearly 30 years ago, it only makes sense that the remake introduced a mostly new cast filling these iconic roles. Another reason is that, unlike the original, most of the characters in the remake actually have fully voiced lines rather than occasional grunts or quick reaction lines.

There's no need to worry, though, as Nintendo has made a point of casting incredible talent who you'll likely recognize from other notable projects. So without further ado, here are all the significantly voiced characters and the actors who voice-act them that you'll encounter in the English version of the remake.
