NetSol Technologies, Inc. (NTWK -23.30%) reported first quarter results featuring revenue growth and a return to profitability, driven by its North American operations and the migration of long-standing customers to its modern platforms. Management reported that gross margin expanded to 50.5%, reflecting a more disciplined delivery approach and the capitalization of software development costs. The company stated that its strategic focus remains on scaling Transcend Retail in the United States, upgrading its legacy installed base, and embedding artificial intelligence across its product suite. Furthermore, the company is evaluating strategic options for its Virtual Lease Services subsidiary and has proposed a share repurchase program at its Pakistan-listed subsidiary to optimize capital allocation.
