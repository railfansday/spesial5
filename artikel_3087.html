Reservoir: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Reservoir is betting that legal action against unlicensed AI use can protect catalog value as it leans on NIL deals and disciplined buying.

On its second quarter of fiscal year 2027 earnings call, held on Nov. 4, 2026, Reservoir (RSVR +5.18%) raised its full-year revenue guidance after organic sales grew 4% and catalog acquisitions pushed top-line growth to 10%. For long-term investors, the music publisher's execution demonstrates how disciplined capital allocation turns legacy sound recordings into durable cash flows. Three things management said stand out.

Reservoir is securing name, image, and likeness (NIL) rights alongside publishing and master recording catalogs to create broad commercial monetization deals for legacy artists.

Despite public market discounts on music companies, seller price expectations for music catalogs have not dropped, keeping management disciplined on deals.

Management is pursuing litigation against artificial intelligence (AI) developers who use copyrighted music without obtaining proper license agreements.

Streaming price increases and steady catalog usage continue to drive operational cash flows higher across both business segments.

Long-term growth depends on multi-genre acquisitions, active talent partnerships, and strict pricing discipline.
