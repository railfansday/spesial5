KFC reveals massive Doom Triple Down sandwich in another green Avengers: Doomsday menu

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

KFC has unleashed another outrageous Avengers: Doomsday menu, this time featuring a monstrous Triple Down sandwich that replaces bread with three pieces of fried chicken.

With Marvel’s upcoming Avengers movie taking over fast food chains around the world, KFC South Korea has decided that two pieces of chicken simply aren’t enough.

The chain has revealed five new Doctor Doom-inspired menu items, all featuring the villain’s signature black and green colors, including a towering sandwich that looks like something even the Avengers would struggle to defeat.

It follows KFC’s previously announced Avengers: Doomsday menu, which introduced black burgers, green mayo, and a mysterious seasoning called Doom Dust.

The star of KFC Korea’s new lineup is undoubtedly the Doom Triple Down, an absolute unit of a sandwich that ditches traditional buns in favor of even more chicken.

It features two crispy Zinger chicken fillets with a juicy chicken thigh fillet sandwiched between them, alongside a hash brown, cheese, and two signature sauces.

Essentially, it’s KFC’s famous Double Down concept taken to its logical, slightly terrifying conclusion.

However, the Triple Down isn’t the only creation getting the Doctor Doom treatment.

The Doom Zinger features a black brioche bun, crispy chicken, spicy green jalapeño sauce, and black barbecue sauce.

There’s also Doom Fries covered in the same green and black sauces, Doom Nuggets served with the two sauces, and a Doom Egg Tart featuring a black pastry shell with a soft, sweet egg filling.

According to local reports, the limited-time menu launched across participating South Korean KFC restaurants on November 10 and will remain available until January 4, 2027.

Of course, KFC isn’t the only fast food chain embracing Marvel’s upcoming blockbuster.

Burger King has already unveiled its own green Whopper and chicken sandwiches, while Outback Steakhouse went even further with a massive Thor’s hammer steak.

Even Dunkin’ has assembled an Avengers: Doomsday menu featuring Doctor Doom and the X-Men.

At this rate, Doctor Doom might conquer the fast food industry before he gets a chance to take on the Avengers when the movie arrives in theaters on December 18.
