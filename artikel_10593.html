Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns amid spy controversy

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Nvidia CEO and co-founder Jensen Huang has resigned after 33 years leading the company, with an interim replacement taking over immediately.

The announcement on November 12 comes just four days after Nvidia launched an independent investigation into reports that Huang had been targeted by a foreign intelligence operation involving a suspected Chinese spy.

Huang, who co-founded Nvidia in 1993, has stepped down as president, CEO, and a member of the board of directors.

Nvidia confirmed that Colette Kress, its executive vice president and chief financial officer, will serve as interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent replacement.

Kress joined Nvidia in September 2013 and has overseen its financial operations during a period of significant growth. She will retain her existing responsibilities while serving as interim CEO, with the company considering both internal and external candidates for the permanent position.

The leadership change follows reports that Huang had an extramarital relationship with Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based technology company Megaspeed, whom US intelligence officials reportedly suspected of working for Chinese intelligence.

The two are not related, and the allegations prompted Nvidia’s board to announce an independent investigation on November 8, overseen by outside legal counsel.

At the time, Nvidia said it had no reason to believe that national security or company secrets had been compromised. The company also expressed confidence in Huang’s leadership and confirmed that he would cooperate with the investigation.

Huang subsequently acknowledged being unfaithful to his wife in a message to employees, apologizing to his family and colleagues while maintaining his commitment to the company.

The controversy also attracted attention from US lawmakers, with three House committee chairs requesting a closed-door briefing with Huang on November 18 to discuss his interactions with Alice Huang and Nvidia’s security procedures.

China’s Foreign Ministry has rejected the espionage allegations, while Singaporean authorities have said they did not identify Alice Huang as a Chinese intelligence asset.

Nvidia’s November 12 announcement did not address the status of its investigation or whether Huang would continue cooperating following his departure.

Alongside Kress’s appointment, Nvidia announced that Harvey C. Jones would become the company’s first-ever chair of the board.

Jones, who has more than 30 years of experience in the technology industry, said the company expects its operations and product roadmap to continue without interruption.

“Colette has helped lead NVIDIA through a period of extraordinary growth and has a deep understanding of our business, customers, operations and financial position,” Jones said.

“She is supported by an experienced leadership team that has been instrumental in executing NVIDIA’s strategy and advancing its technology roadmap. The Board has full confidence in Colette and the leadership team, and we expect the company’s operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption.”

Kress also addressed her appointment, thanking Huang for his contributions to Nvidia over the past three decades.

“NVIDIA’s strength has always come from its people, and together with our leadership team, I am committed to building on the extraordinary foundation Jensen created over the last 33 years,” she said.

“We will continue advancing the work that has made NVIDIA a leader in accelerated computing and AI, and execute on the opportunities ahead. Our focus remains on serving our customers, partners, developers and shareholders.”

Huang’s departure comes as Nvidia faces increased scrutiny over its leadership and business dealings with China, with its advanced AI chips remaining subject to US export restrictions.

The company is scheduled to release its third-quarter fiscal 2027 financial results on November 17, followed by an earnings call later that day. Nvidia confirmed both events will proceed as planned.
