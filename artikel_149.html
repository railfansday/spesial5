BU study says giant crane ‘species’ was just a larger common crane

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Where Readers Share Great Links. Join Free

Bournemouth University researchers say an extinct giant crane named in the 19th century was probably not a separate species at all, but a larger form of the modern common crane. The conclusion comes from ancient DNA analysis of 18 crane bones found across Europe, challenging a long-held identification based on bone size alone.
