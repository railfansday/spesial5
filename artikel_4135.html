GTA 6: How to Unlock the Apartment Laptop in Chapter 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Adam Beck is a Canadian who has covered the gaming industry for over twenty years. He has a passion for RPGs, action, stealth and horror games.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is one of those games that has seemingly infinite things to do. There are the main quests, side quests, side activities and then there are hidden quests that you can’t track in a standard in-game journal.

When you get to Vice City in chapter 3, you will be housed in a full “furnished” apartment, meaning that the last tenant up and left all their stuff. With it, though, you’ll be able to go through her stuff, including her personal laptop, if you’re able to find the password.

All of the clues are found within the apartment itself, so you’ll just need to do some exploring.

This will give you the clues you need. This girl was obsessed with her dog and used him as her password:

The dog’s name is Muffins, she drew her as a queen on the notepad, and on the back of the notepad is the class of ’15 reunion reminder. With that, you can search the computer. The important tab is in WhatUp with the Unknown user, where they will give Vanessa a password to a place.

The next step in this surprisingly hidden side quest is finding the keypad that’s assigned to this keycode. Thankfully, there are a couple of hints that are given in the original email:

The first part is pretty self-explanatory as there’s a district to the south of the apartment that’s called La Perle. You’ll go there to meet Tyrone. Near Auntie’s is a little more difficult only because you need to find environmental clues.

Head into town and then look for Tyrone’s place. If you haven’t gotten to this part yet, which is the first mission into chapter 3, look for the clothing thrift store on the map. From Tyrone’s place, head south one block and on the right, you’ll find a restaurant called Auntie’s Soul Food. There will be a parking lot behind this building and slightly to the north. Head there and enter the code on the panel.

Surprisingly, there are two different rewards in this quest. For starters, you will get the Growler vehicle for free. If you want to sell it, it will get you $14,750, which isn’t anything to scoff at. With that said, only really sell it if you desperately need space in your garages.

The more important reward, though, is that the garage that you opened with the keypad is now yours to keep. There is an additional one car, on top of being able to go there to sell marked bills, jewelry and narcotics. There’s also a weapons locker in the back, which is a little more important given Tyrone is nearby for stuff to sell.
