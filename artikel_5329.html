Bitcoin’s Current Drawdown Ahead of Anthropic IPO Mirrors SpaceX Listing — Why a $90K Rebound Could Be Coming by February

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bitcoin’s latest price decline may be setting the stage for a familiar market pattern, and history could give bulls reason to be optimistic. The benchmark cryptocurrency has suffered a sharp drop ahead of Anthropic’s upcoming red-hot stock market debut, echoing its 19% plunge in the 50 days leading up to SpaceX’s IPO earlier this year. However, if the historical pattern repeats, Bitcoin could be gearing up for a significant recovery, with a potential rebound toward $90,000 by February now drawing attention.

Strive CEO Matt Cole has observed an interesting historical parallel between Bitcoin’s current price action and its performance ahead of SpaceX’s record-breaking IPO. In the 50 days leading up to SpaceX’s $75 billion public debut on June 12, Bitcoin plunged 19%.

Now, with Anthropic preparing for a potential $64.4 billion IPO next week, BTC has posted a remarkably similar 18% decline over the same period, Cole noted in a post on X.

At press time, Bitcoin was trading at $64,665, according to CoinGecko, extending its recent losses after breaking below the $70,000 mark on November 13. The cryptocurrency continued its downward slide over the weekend, falling beneath $67,000 as bearish pressure intensified.

The Strive chief noted that overlaying the two price movements reveals an almost identical pattern, raising the possibility that Bitcoin could be repeating its pre-SpaceX sell-off ahead of another historic market debut.

Bitcoin extended its decline after SpaceX’s market debut, reaching its lowest point 18 days later before reversing course. The subsequent rally lifted BTC 28% above its IPO-day closing price within 100 days.

“If Anthropic follows the same pattern, most of the drawdown is behind us, and it could reverse quickly over the next couple of months,” Cole opined.

If history repeats itself, Bitcoin could climb toward $90,000 by late February, Cole suggested.

Binance co-founder Changpeng “CZ” Zhao also weighed in on the chart comparison, sharing the analysis on X and describing Cole’s findings as “interesting.”

However, the historical comparison comes with important caveats. Bitcoin’s sell-off ahead of the SpaceX IPO unfolded alongside market concerns over potential BTC sales by Michael Saylor’s Strategy, suggesting that factors beyond the anticipated listing may have contributed to the decline. As a result, attributing the entire downturn to IPO-related sentiment would overlook other pressures weighing on the market at the time.
