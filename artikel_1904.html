Sources: Crystal Palace 'concerns' over Thomas Frank emerge amid new delay

Crystal Palace are still working on a deal to appoint Thomas Frank as their new manager, sources have told Football Insider.

The Eagles had hoped to land a quick appointment following Pierre Sage's dismissal earlier this week, and have been locked in talks with the former Brentford boss as a result.

A full agreement has not yet been reached, despite suggestions that the manager has been keen to take over at Selhurst Park and that talks had been positive.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, the delays surrounding Frank's appointment are due to attempts to land his backroom staff.

Crystal Palace fans will remember the summer of 2021, when Nuno Espirito Santo looked set to take over, before the club pulled the plug due to his overinflated backroom team.

This situation is believed to be different, with optimism on both sides that a deal will be agreed before too long, but concerns are starting to creep in at Selhurst Park.

Football Insider exclusively revealed on 12 November that Crystal Palace have faced delays in Frank's appointment with work ongoing to land the manager's backroom staff.

The Eagles are keen to provide Frank with their backing by bringing in a number of his most trusted allies, as they did with Sage following his arrival from Lens.

However, that has led to delays in the appointment and despite their confidence and having wanted to do a deal quickly, the Eagles remain locked in negotiations.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "What I've been told with regard to Frank is that there are also discussions about the backroom staff who would come in with him.

"There was, I think, three or four staff who came with him to Tottenham from Brentford. They all left with him when he left Tottenham, and he would be looking for them to come in at Palace.

"Palace fans will remember a couple of years ago, before Patrick Vieira came to the club, Palace were close to bringing in Nuno Espirito Santo.

"Eventually, they pulled the plug on that because of difficulties with his backroom staff. They didn't want to pay for what they viewed as sort of an overinflated team.

"I don't think that will be the situation with Frank. I don't think it'll there'll be any sort of major concerns on that. It's just something that maybe Palace have to get over the line.

"Frank will want to bring these people with him and hopefully they'll help him be a success at Palace."

Despite optimism that Frank will still be the next Crystal Palace manager, a number of potential alternatives have already been assessed amid the ongoing delays.

The likes of Vitor Pereira and Rob Edwards have been linked with the position, alongside a number of lesser-known names from across European football.

Meanwhile, former Man United coach Mick Brown told Football Insider that Crystal Palace would be making a mistake if they did not appoint Sean Dyche as their next manager.

Defensive issues have been the main concern for Crystal Palace this season, and the former Everton boss has been backed as the man to ease those worries.

Meanwhile, Dyche would bring a clear and recognisable style of play, while questions have been asked about Frank's tactical setup after his struggles as Tottenham manager.
