Keith Wyness explains controversial Nottingham Forest 'protest'

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Nottingham Forest were trying to prove a point by voting against the idea of covering legal defence fees for Premier League directors amid the ongoing saga surrounding Manchester City.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Reds were simply making their dissatisfaction known.

Man City's leading sponsors Etihad have threatened to take legal action against the Premier League, with 18 clubs voting to cover the cost of defences for each league director should that scenario play out.

Wyness believes that enough clubs were always going to vote in favour of the idea, leaving Forest with the chance to simply prove their point.

14 clubs had to vote in favour of the idea for the notion to be passed, and Forest and City were the only two clubs who voted against.

The East Midlands outfit have had plenty of run-ins with the league in recent years, including a controversial points deduction back in 2024, and Wyness feels they are just trying to get their own back.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy business advising elite clubs – believes that Forest's vote was always likely to be ineffectual, given the stances shown by the other clubs.

As a result, he feels that owner Evangelos Marinakis used the vote as the chance to make a "protest" against the treatment his club have received in recent years.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained the likely thinking behind Forest's decision.

"Well, I haven't seen it officially revealed that it was Forest, but I'm pretty sure that all the reports are accurate, so we'll go on that one," he said.

"Let's say that, Marinakis must think that the Premier League are an inconsistent referee, is what I would say.

"And he also claimed that he didn't have enough information. So that's where we are with it.

"Look, I think they've made their protest, and good for them, they've said it, so that's the situation, and they were never going to lose the vote so Forest made their point."

Football Insider revealed on Tuesday that Roma are keen to sign Forest winger Callum Hudson-Odoi on loan in January.

The wideman has been firmly down the pecking order for Oliver Glasner of late, and has played just 45 minutes of Premier League football so far in 2026-27.

Roma are keeping a close eye on the 26-year-old's situation, but are not prepared to make a permanent move for Hudson-Odoi mid-season.

However, a loan move is firmly on their radar at the moment, even though Chelsea's Jamie Gittens currently stands as their first choice target.
