Apple adjusts iPhone and Apple Watch trade-in values, adds Samsung foldables

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Apple has once again adjusted the trade-in values for some of its devices, while adding several Samsung foldables to the list. Here are the new values.

For at least the seventh time this year, Apple updated its trade-in values table today, having just raised the amounts offered for iPads and Macs a few weeks ago.

With today’s update, Apple raised trade-in values for many iPhone and several Apple Watch models, while leaving Mac and iPad estimates unchanged. Apple also added six Samsung foldable phones to its Android trade-in list, while lowering estimates for several Google Pixel models and some newer iPhones.
