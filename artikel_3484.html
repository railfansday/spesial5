iOS 27.3 brings Apple Intelligence features to China

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

iOS 27.3 beta 1 is here, and it includes the first Apple Intelligence features in China. Siri AI isn’t available yet, but a lot of other capabilities are. Here’s what to expect.

For the first time, Apple Intelligence is now available in China for beta users.

Apple Intelligence launched in the US in October 2024, so users in China have had to wait more than two years for the features to arrive.

But today with iOS 27.3 beta 1, like was announced last week, Apple Intelligence is officially available in China too.

Not all of Apple’s AI capabilities are here yet. Siri AI, notably, is missing. However, Apple outlines a wide array of AI features debuting in China with iOS 27.3.
