ABC's 'Rookie' Replacement Is Still the Best Procedural of All Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

For over eight seasons, ABC's The Rookie has been one of the best procedurals on television today. The drama provides plenty of heart-racing action and intriguing relationships between the characters. Whether you tune in for the exciting police chases or the developing romance between Chenford, The Rookie continues to be a good time. However, there's one more long-running network procedural that is still proving it tops even The Rookie's action-packed episodes.

ABC's 9-1-1 has just entered its 10th season, but there are no signs of the series spinning its wheels. Since its premiere back in 2018, the Ryan Murphy-produced show continues to offer a fascinating portrayal of the lives of first responders working in Los Angeles. With characters who take on positions in Station 118 of the Los Angeles Fire Department, the Los Angeles Police Department, and a 9-1-1 dispatch center, the drama showcases these brave individuals who will stop at nothing to save lives. Each episode features wild emergencies that our characters must fight their way through. The hallmark of the show has become a new citywide emergency that threatens lives every season. By now, our first responders have tackled everything from an earthquake and a tsunami to a Beenado and a volcano erupting in downtown LA. These high-stakes disasters have allowed the series to continue to top itself, forever ensuring that viewers always stay on the edge of their seats.

But it's not just the terrifying and sometimes bizarre emergencies that make 9-1-1 worth watching. The series also thrives by creating characters that feel authentic and lovable. It's easy to root for the brilliant, tough-as-nails Athena Grant (Angela Bassett), as she solves cases for the LAPD or handles drama with her children. We're always cheering on Evan "Buck" Buckley (Oliver Stark) as he continues to find his way both at the fire station and in life. His sister, Maddie (Jennifer Love Hewitt), and her now-husband, Howard "Chimney" Han (Kenneth Choi), have both been put through the wringer every season, but their loving relationship has been a joy to watch unfold. Sometimes the series offers silly or lighthearted conflicts between characters, but the chemistry the whole cast has with each other is one of the main reasons that 9-1-1 is still going strong so many seasons later.

The aftershocks of this episode are still felt in the series.

The creative team behind 9-1-1 took a major risk in Season 8, and a dramatic scripted event has led to the show having a new lease on life. Season 8 saw the death of one of the show's main characters, Captain Bobby Nash (Peter Krause). The episode was a heart-wrenching and thrilling story, where the always heroic Bobby ends up succumbing to a deadly viral outbreak. Many fans were furious at Bobby's demise, as he felt like the heart of the show. But this storyline actually proved to be a valuable reset for the veteran series. With this plot point, the characters all faced a truly life-changing event, and his death altered the dynamic of the fire station and the relationships between many of the characters. Bobby's death created a multitude of storylines for the series, many of them allowing the show to head in a wide variety of intriguing directions with new challenges and conflicts for the characters. Although the death was a major shake-up for the show, in the end, it created a refresh in tone that has given the show a new direction, and helped 9-1-1 to avoid any ennui that plagues some long-running shows. 9-1-1 has also shown wisdom in starting to focus on younger characters, like May (Corinne Massiah) and Harry Grant (Elijah M. Cooper), who can continue to carry the mantle for a new generation of first responders ready to save the day.

Although the series sometimes trends into campy territory, that's what makes it all the more fun to watch. You never know what outlandish circumstances the team will find themselves in, and this is why 9-1-1 is continuing to stand the test of time. Season 10 kicked off in October with a lethal citywide emergency, several life-threatening situations for the characters, and a promise that the procedural shows no sign of slowing down yet.

9-1-1 airs Thursday nights on ABC and is available to stream the next day on Hulu in the U.S.
