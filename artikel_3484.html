ABC Narrowly Escapes A Major Plot Hole With Grey's Anatomy Franchise's Official Meredith Replacement

As the Grey's Anatomy universe prepares to enter a new chapter, the long-running franchise was able to narrowly escape a massive plot hole after announcing Meredith Grey's replacement in their first major casting choice. While the original series won't be going anywhere, the new Grey's Anatomy spinoff is shifting gears as it moves closer to production despite a few recently announced hiccups. While Grey's Anatomy season 23 is continuing as planned, the show's next spinoff is tackling a far different sort of infrastructure than the long-standing Grey Sloan Memorial Hospital, making its mark as the franchise expands in a new way.

While Ellen Pompeo, who's been one of the few original cast members to stick with Grey's throughout its historic run, has minimized her time as Meredith on screen, she's still a massive part of the show's production and overall identity. With her character splitting her time between Boston and Seattle, Meredith isn't as present on screen throughout Grey's, but it's likely that she'll have some hand in the show's new franchise, which is set to tackle the challenges of rural healthcare in a West Texas town. While the spinoff is set to feature ties to Grey's Anatomy, the show is narrowly avoiding some serious issues in its composition.

Karla Souza, best known for her role as Laurel Castillo in Shonda Rhimes' How To Get Away With Murder, has been announced to be taking on the anchoring role in the new Grey's spinoff, and her time in the Shondaland universe seemed like it could cause an unexpected rift.

With the new Grey's Anatomy spinoff recently announcing that their Meredith figure will be a character played by Souza, longtime Shodaland fans have a lot to be excited about. Souza, who spent six seasons playing Laurel Castillo on How To Get Away With Murder, has been a mainstay of the Shondaland shows in the past, and her complicated portrayal of Laurel tied into the larger mystery of the show. While she wasn't the main character of the series, a title that belonged to Viola Davis' Annalise Keating, her role as one of the mysterious lawyer's students was a huge piece of the show's puzzle.

When the Shondaland lineup was at its peak, the creator's shows were running an entire block of programming, taking up ABC's Thursday nights. TGIT, the cheeky lineup that played on the network's past TGIF gimmick, featured Grey's Anatomy, Scandal, and How To Get Away With Murder. Souza's time in the Shondaland machine was during one of the production company's most unprecedented times, with a major spotlight on the series she appeared in. How To Get Away With Murder was a hugely successful series, and Souza's work was praised consistently throughout her time on the show.

Souza's character in How To Get Away With Murder was an intense, complicated woman who grew throughout the winding mystery of the six-season series, but she'll be bringing something new into the Grey's Anatomy spinoff. Her character in the upcoming series, Carolina Hall, doesn't have a connection to Laurel or the larger universe, which will allow her to step into a whole new chapter.

Throughout their time on the air, especially right before and during the TGIT era, Shondaland shows had crossover episodes. While there were always going to be crossovers between shows like Grey's Anatomy and Private Practice or Station 19, which are direct spinoffs of Grey's, it didn't seem as likely for the shows that hadn't directly spawned from Rhimes' biggest show to cross over with one another. While Scandal and How To Get Away With Murder were both in more of a legal and political space, it wasn't clear for quite some time that they took place in the same universe.

As the Shondaland brand gained traction, however, it became clear that viewers would be interested in seeing the characters from each show interact if it made sense within the story. Annalise and Olivia Pope (Kerry Washington), specifically, were a draw for audiences, as both strong and capable women had high-powered positions and commanded every room they were in. While there were crossovers between the shows within the programming block, the crossovers that would matter the most as the Grey's franchise branches out would be anything that involved Souza's character, Laurel, and anyone from the Grey's Anatomy universe.

Thankfully, there wasn't ever any crossover between the How To Get Away With Murder cast and anyone from the Grey's universe, as the shows actually don't appear to share a world directly. While Scandal and How To Get Away With Murder exist on the same plane, their crossovers didn't mix with anything to do with Grey's or its several spinoffs.

While Souza may be best known for her role in How To Get Away With Murder, moving into the new Grey's Anatomy spinoff will give her the chance to step into a brand new character. Carolina "Lina" Hall is described to be a haunted and cynical doctor working to run a rural West Texas medical center that's in desperate need of help and change. While the rest of the show's scope hasn't been established yet, the setting and nature of the hospital for the spinoff are already vastly different than what Grey's viewers are used to from healthcare within the universe.

The rural setting and the lack of affluence make it clear that the sometimes luxe teaching hospital viewers see in Grey Sloan won't be a part of the picture for the upcoming Grey's spinoff. Instead, the show will be exploring the systemic issues that crop up in healthcare from an entirely different angle, which will allow for new stories, new conflicts, and new explorations of those issues to come to light. Framing the series in a completely different setting and interconnecting it to Grey's establishes that there will be a high standard for the show, but it won't be a carbon copy of its parent series.

Although there's been some issues surrounding the spinoff, which has been delayed after the filming location for the series fell through, the Grey's Anatomy spinoff is still set to debut in 2027. With Souza on board and a great story to tell, the upcoming series seems ready to start a whole new chapter for Shondaland.
