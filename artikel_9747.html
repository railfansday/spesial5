45% of my app's first downloads came from ChatGPT, not from my website

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

I build Teleprompter Offline & Private, an iPhone teleprompter with voice-follow scrolling that works offline, with no account and no tracking. I'm a solo developer and it earns about $100+ a month right now. Not life-changing, but real, and it is growing slowly.

What surprised me in App Store Connect: the App Referrer report shows ChatGPT as the source of 45% of the app's first downloads. Not Google. Not my landing page. ChatGPT seems to quote App Store listings, Reddit threads and comparison pages, rather than my own site.

App Store Connect, Analytics, Sources, App Referrer. Look at the share of web referrals that are AI assistants. The numbers are small, so do not over-read them, but the direction was clear for me.

The listing is the page that gets quoted, so it deserves more work than the landing page. A question like "which teleprompter works offline without an account" gets answered from whatever text states that plainly.

Question for you: where do your first users actually come from? Have you checked the referrer data lately?
