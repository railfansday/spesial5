Phrack 73

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Last year marked the 40th anniversary of Phrack so we went big. We publishedour anniversary launch release in print at Defcon 33, HOPE, WHY25 and someadditional variations at several conferences around the world includingAlligatorCon, B-Sides Canberra, Out of the Box, Datenspuren, DistrictCon,OffensiveCon, Recon, and Toorcamp. We included a CTF with exploit challengesand had puzzles and easter eggs included in the zine. We threw parties andhad talks at Defcon and WHY25 to celebrate this milestone.

More importantly, we got new research into people's hands. The point of therelaunch was to get people reading, researching, writing, and contributingagain. We wanted the next person picking up Phrack to find something thatmade them sit down at a computer and start pulling a system apart.

We owe a huge thanks to the authors, editors, artists, publishing partners,donors, and staff who made it happen. Writing the paper, working throughrevisions, putting the print editions together, paying the bills, andgetting boxes of zines to the right places all take time and effort. Most ofthat work is invisible when you are holding the finished issue. Weappreciate everyone who did it.

And to the readers: thank you for giving a shit. Seeing someone get excitedabout an article, connect it to something they are working on, or realizethat they could contribute something themselves makes the effort worthwhile.That exchange is how this thing keeps going. The relaunch worked, and thisissue is the follow-through.

This is the third release curated by the new staff, and we are not slowingdown. This issue is larger than last year's, with 23 main papers and eightadditional articles in Line Noise. The subjects range from exploitation andreverse engineering to machine learning, hardware, and the history andculture that grew around this work.

We have container escapes through the vDSO, use-after-free exploitation, QNXuserland shellcode, Linux kernel research, and a Chromium renderer sandboxescape through Skia. Elsewhere, the issue takes on Windows smart cardarchitecture, static taint analysis in Binary Ninja, and autonomousunderwater vehicles.  There is also work examining backdoors in machinelearning and what it means to trust an AI system. One paper takes us fromPhrackCTF to two WinFSP zero-days, which is a pretty good argument forcontinuing to put challenges in a zine.

We also have four more articles in Phrack World History, including the storyof the infamous SSH1 CRC32 vulnerability featured in The Matrix Reloaded aswell as stories about blue boxing free phone calls, a confession of a hackerwho tried to backdoor Linux kernel in 2003, and an early discovery ofbotnets for hire. Our Prophile features Halvar Flake, whose contributions toreverse engineering and security research are legendary.

In 2026, we find ourselves in a time of rapid technological change. It ishard to say what vulnerability research or red teaming will look like inanother five years. AI has moved well beyond completing a line of code intoagentic systems capable of analyzing source code and reverse engineeringbinaries to find and exploit vulnerabilities.

We can be sure that the future will require us to continue to explore newideas, create new tools, and push the boundaries of what is possible in theface of new challenges and opportunities created by the advancement of AIand automation systems. As the effort required to put ideas into practicefalls, creativity and human intuition forged through experience is valuedeven higher.

We hope that this release of Phrack will inspire new ideas, discussions, andcollaborations that will keep the human side of hacking a critical elementin shaping the future of technology and society. We will continue to do ourpart to support the community and provide a platform for sharing knowledgeand ideas. We look forward to seeing what the future holds and we areexcited to be a part of it.

Read the papers, try the ideas, and challenge the conclusions. When you findsomething worth sharing, write it up and send it in. We have another issueto put together, and some of you should be in it!

To those who have supported us the past three years helping to restorevitality to Phrack, we thank you.
