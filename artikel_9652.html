AI Visibility Isn’t Just for Enterprises: How Operato AI Helps Small Businesses Track 6 AI Assistant

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

When someone asks ChatGPT for the best accountant in Lyon, a reliable roofer in Austin or a project management tool for a 10-person agency, the assistant doesn’t return ten blue links. It returns a short answer, usually with three to five names. If your business isn’t one of them, you aren’t on page two. You simply aren’t there.

Large brands understood this early. A whole category of AI visibility platforms has grown up to track how assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity describe and recommend companies. Most of these platforms are built and priced for enterprises: custom quotes, annual contracts, a sales call before you see a single result.

That leaves out the businesses for whom one AI recommendation can matter the most: local services, independent professionals, small software companies and online shops. They are asked about every day, and they rarely know what the assistants say. Operato AI was built for them.

An AI assistant answering a buyer question draws on two things: what it learned during training and, more and more often, what it finds on the web at the moment of the question. Either way, it leans heavily on what other sites say about you.

In October 2026, Operato AI looked at 2,320 answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity to the questions buyers ask about 38 brands, mostly small and mid-sized businesses in the US, France and Israel. The questions were written the way buyers ask them, without the brand’s name, for example “best accounting software for a 10-person agency”. These are signals from businesses that chose to measure their AI visibility, not an industry benchmark. But the direction is clear: your own website is a small part of what the assistants read about you, and ranking on Google does not guarantee a place in the answer.

It’s tempting to think AI visibility is a big-brand problem. The opposite is closer to the truth.

Big brands are named by default. A household name appears in training data thousands of times. A two-person law firm or a family-run bakery does not. The assistant has to find them, and it finds them through directories, reviews, local press and comparison articles.

Local context changes everything. In one case from the study sample, a small studio tracked in Operato AI was named third out of six when ChatGPT was asked for a recommendation in its city. Asked the same questions without the city, none of the four assistants named it. Small businesses win or lose on details like this, and they can’t see them without checking.

Being readable is not the same as being recommended. A B2B software company in the sample scored 100 out of 100 on technical AI readiness: crawlers allowed, structured data, sitemap, everything in place. Asked the question its buyers ask, ChatGPT recommended five competitors and didn’t mention it once. A technical audit alone would have said everything was fine.

Every assistant is different. For more than a third of the brands, the gap between their best and worst assistant was 20 points or more. A business can be recommended by Perplexity and missing from Gemini in the same week. Checking ChatGPT once, by hand, gives a partial picture.

What Enterprise Tools Get Right, and Who They Leave Out

Enterprise AI visibility platforms do real work: thousands of prompts, many brands and markets, single sign-on, compliance documents, a dedicated success manager. For a global brand with several business units, that’s worth a custom contract.

But the market is drifting upmarket. In September 2026, one of the category’s best-known vendors removed its self-serve plans for brands, leaving a capped free trial or an enterprise quote (pricing page snapshots: September 14, 2026 and October 14, 2026). That is a rational choice for a company growing contract value. It also means fewer options for a marketing team of one, a dentist, or a founder who does marketing on Sunday evenings. Self-serve tools such as Operato AI take the other route: public prices and a first result within minutes.

Buyers don’t all use the same assistant. Some ask ChatGPT, some ask Claude, some use Perplexity for research, and many never leave Google, where AI Overviews and AI Mode now answer first.
