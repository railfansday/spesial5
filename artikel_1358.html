Match Guide: Everton Women v Durham Women

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Everton Women host Durham Women in their latest Subway Players Cup clash at Goodison Park on Wednesday November 11 (7pm KO).

This game marks Everton Women’s annual Remembrance fixture, which will see a tribute take place ahead of kick-off involving former servicemen and women supported by Everton in the Community’s Veterans Programme, the laying of commemorative poppy wreaths in memory of the fallen and a rendition of The Last Post. More details can be found HERE.

Our comprehensive Goodison match guide, below, will help you plan your Goodison matchday and includes more on food and drink, digital tickets, travel and accessibility.

FOR OUR FULL EVERTON V DURHAM WOMEN MATCHDAY GUIDE, CLICK HERE

Grab your ticket online, over the phone (0151 556 1878) or in person at Hill Dickinson Stadium’s South Stand Ticket Office.

On matchday, Goodison’s Park End ticket office will be open from 90 minutes before kick-off. Opening times for all our ticketing outlets can be found here.

Everton are looking to maintain their 100% record in this year’s cup competition, having won all three of their six group matches to date. Victories over Newcastle United, Birmingham City and Sheffield United see Scott Phelan’s side currently sitting fourth place in the league table, which will see the top eight teams qualify for the knock-out phases once the group phase is complete.

Everton’s remaining games, following Wednesday’s visit of Durham Women, are as follows:

Manchester United Women (A): Wednesday 18 November

Sunderland Women (A): Wednesday 16 December

A reminder that Goodison now has a dedicated Buggy Park, to make attending games even easier for fans with young families. Buggies, strollers and prams can be booked into a safe space at the corner of the Park End and Bullens Road stands, accessed via the entrance on Goodison Road.

Free spaces at the Buggy Park, which will open one hour prior to kick off and close 1 hour after the final whistle, can be pre-booked HERE

If you’re celebrating, or simply looking to make more of your matchday, there are great options available at Goodison Park throughout 2026/27.
