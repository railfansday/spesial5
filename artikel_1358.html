Aston Villa Must Axe 'Awful' Boubacar Kamara After Man Utd Shocker

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Aston Villa will be absolutely furious with themselves after blowing a two-goal lead twice at Old Trafford to be held to a 4-4 draw by Manchester United on Sunday evening.

The visitors were 2-0 up inside four minutes after goals from Alysson and Nicolas Jackson, before Bruno Fernandes and ex-Villan Youri Tielemans got the hosts level before half-time. But John McGinn and Leon Goretzka scored inside the opening eight minutes of the half to restore the two-goal cushion.

However, injury-time goals from Joshua Zirkzee and Patrick Dorgu saw United nick a late draw, leaving Villa 10th in the Premier League table heading into the international break, with just three wins from their ten games so far.

But while the manager would usually be the one to receive criticism for such results, the blame is being passed on to the players by most fans, who acknowledge that Unai Emery has been dealt a rough hand this season after the entire spine of his squad stripped away during the summer.

Replacements were brought in, but the majority of Villa's summer signings are struggling to make an immediate impact. However, while speaking after the game on his social media account, club legend Gabriel Agbonlahor called out a senior player who has been around for several seasons as someone who has been 'awful' this season.

"Kamara can't move and has been awful this season!" he wrote.

He then continued and vented his anger at several other players.

"Maatsen can't defend against anyone. Defensively we lack pace. Goalkeeper [has] been found out with crosses. Barkley comes on and plays like he's on the 5-a-side pitch.. Zero game management is why the manager stormed off. Crap."

Since Kamara joined the club on a free transfer, it's fair to say that he has established himself as one of the best and most underrated midfielders in the Premier League under Emery.

But the Frenchman has been way off his usual standards this season, and it's clear that right now his reputation from previous campaigns is saving him from being dropped from the team.

Emery has always been a very loyal coach, and he likes to let people try and play through their bad form. But performances like the one against Man Utd are costing Villa from picking up vital points, and they need to be stamped out. A bit of time on the sideline to get himself back to his usual level could be ideal, and would also give other players a chance to step up.

Lamar Bogarde has found himself out of the matchday squad at times this season, but he performed well last year as a utility man. Alongside him, Goretzka, John McGinn, and Joao Gomes are all capable of stepping into that role to give him a rest.

With a big game against Sunderland next up after the international break, followed by a trip to Istanbul to take on Galatasaray, Villa must get victories, and that may mean leaving Kamara out until he finds his level again.
