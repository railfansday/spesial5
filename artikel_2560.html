College Football TV Ratings: Tracking the Top 10 most-watched teams of the 2026 season

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After seeing a 9% increase in minutes viewed in 2025, college football is looking to see TV ratings continue their upward trajectory in 2026. Nielsen has also unveiled its official list of most-watched teams.

Nielsen ranked the Top 10 teams based on average viewership this year. ABC, CBS, The CW, ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, FOX, FS1, NBC, TNT and USA Network are the networks included in the data set. Big Ten Network is not included, nor are partial and regionalized telecasts.

On3 continues to track college football TV ratings throughout the 2026 season, including the most-watched games of the year. Here are the most-watched teams through Week 12, based on average viewership, according to data provided by Nielsen.

Note: SEC Network, ACC Network and CBS Sports Network do not pay for Nielsen to measure viewership.

Fueled by the viewership for the victory over Ohio State, Texas sits at No. 1 in the most-watched college football teams list of the year through Week 12, based on average Nielsen TV ratings data. The Longhorns have played in three of the Top 10 most-watched games of the season, all of which were on ABC.

Texas' loss to Oklahoma also became one of the five most-watched games as the Red River Rivalry delivered for ABC. The thrilling Week 11 victory over LSU also drew Top-10 viewership heading into Week 13.

Through Week 12, Ohio State played in the most-watched game of the season. The Buckeyes' loss to Texas brought nearly 14 million viewers in Week 2, and they remain one of Nielsen's most-watched teams.

In fact, Ohio State has played in two of the top five games of the season entering Week 12. The Buckeyes' thrilling road win over Indiana was also the top draw of Week 7 with 10.38 million viewers on FOX in the Big Noon Saturday time slot.

Alabama played in one of the most-watched college football games of the year in Week 6, according to Nielsen TV ratings data. The Crimson Tide's loss to Georgia averaged 12.2 million viewers, and they also sit at No. 5 in the Top 10 most-watched teams heading into Week 13.

In Week 7, Alabama again played in one of the most-watched games of the season. The rivalry matchup against Tennessee averaged 8.1 million viewers on ABC to draw Top-10 viewership on the year, according to data compiled by On3.

Entering Week 13, LSU is squarely one of the Top 10 most-watched teams in college football, according to Nielsen. The Tigers' loss to Alabama became one of the top games of the season and helped them return to the rankings.

LSU has played in three of the Top 10 most-watched games of the season heading into Week 13. That is led by the matchup against Ole Miss, which averaged 10.90 million viewers on ABC in Lane Kiffin's return to Oxford.

Indiana's Top-5 showdown against Ohio State became one of the most-watched college football games of the year through Week 12. The Hoosiers came up just short against the Buckeyes, but jumped into the Top 10 most-watched teams list, per data provided by Nielsen.

That Week 7 game averaged 10.38 million viewers on FOX and came one week after IU's win over Nebraska averaged 6.73 million on the network. All told, the Hoosiers have played in six games on FOX this season.

One of the most anticipated games of the year delivered as one of the most-watched games of the season through Week 12. Ole Miss took down LSU in Lane Kiffin's return to Oxford in front of nearly 11 million viewers on ABC.

Ole Miss' loss to Florida also brought huge viewership with 8.29 million viewers on average in Week 4. That helped put the Rebels at No. 7 in Nielsen's Top 10 most-watched college football teams heading into Week 13.

Georgia's victory over Alabama drew 12.2 million viewers on ABC, sitting only behind Ohio State vs. Texas as the most-watched game of the season. UGA also moved up to No. 1 in the AP Top 25 after that win, coupled with the Longhorns' loss to Oklahoma, before falling to Auburn a week later.

Georgia's win over Alabama became the second most-watched game of the season through Week 12. As a result, the Bulldogs are at No. 8 in Nielsen's Top 10 most-watched teams in college football, according to Nielsen TV ratings data.

Led by the victory over Oklahoma in Week 2, Michigan remains in Nielsen's Top 10 most-watched teams of the season. The Wolverines have played in four games on networks that are in the data set, including the Week 4 loss to Iowa, which brought 4.46 million viewers on CBS.

The Week 2 win over Oklahoma was the second-highest viewership of Week 2 and is one of four Michigan games to average at least 4 million viewers through Week 12. That helped put the Wolverines in the Top 10 most-watched teams of the year.

Oklahoma is squarely among the Top 10 most-watched teams in college football and has played in four full games included in the measurement. That includes the Red River Rivalry, which is one of the most-watched games through Week 12.

OU's win over Texas averaged 9.8 million viewers on ABC and helped propel the Sooners back into the Top 10. Their game against Michigan in Week 2 is also one of the most-watched of the season with 8.2 million viewers on FOX.

After the Week 9 loss to Georgia became the most-watched game of the week, Florida is back among the Top 10 most-watched college football teams, according to Nielsen's data. The Gators are averaging 5.323 million viewers across the networks included in the measurement.

UF's game against Ole Miss was its biggest draw of the year with 8.29 million viewers in Week 4. Additionally, the rivalry game against Georgia in Atlanta also topped 7 million viewers as part of the Week 9 slate.

On3 will continue to update the most-watched teams in college football throughout the 2026 season, according to TV ratings data provided weekly by Nielsen. The data will continue throughout the regular season.
