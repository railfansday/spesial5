Seahawks vs. 49ers odds: Opening lines for Week 12 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Despite coming off back-to-back wins against the Arizona Cardinals and Las Vegas Raiders, the Seattle Seahawks open as slight underdogs against the NFC-leading San Francisco 49ers — who got the better end of the deal when the two sides squared off earlier this season.

According to the current lines at FanDuel Sportsbook, the Seahawks are a 3-point underdog (-110), marking the first time this season they haven't been the favorite to win. Their moneyline is set at +132 and San Francisco's at -118, while the line for total points is 46.5, seemingly predicting a steady game offensively but one that'll be decided by whoever outdoes the other defensively.

Although it was San Francisco who won the defensive battle when they took the highly-anticipated season opener 32-29 in Week 5, there's no indication that'll once again be the case.

The first time around, Seattle was forced the lean heavily on third-string running backs Emanuel Wilson (20 carries, 61 yards, rushing touchdown), with George Holani having also received work as Zach Charbonnet and Jadarian Price remained out. Although they were somewhat effective on the stat sheet, both fumbled in Seattle's own territory — each having resulted in 49er touchdowns.

It wasn't just Seattle's ball security issues that helped San Francisco secure the pivotal win but also another uninspiring outing from quarterback Sam Darnold, who was picked off twice by cornerback Renardo Green — one of which came deep in the Seahawks own end.

Despite a flurry of injuries since, the Seahawks are coming off their bye and in line to be near full health. Following their Week 1o win over the Las Vegas Raiders, head coach Mike Macdonald expressed optimism Darnold (quad, ribs) as well as OT Charles Cross (ankle) will be able to return against San Francisco after each missed the past two games.

Given Seattle was on bye, the team didn't practice last week so it currently remains unclear whether offensive guard Quenton Nelson (knee), offensive tackle Abraham Lucas (ankle) and defensive tackle Leonard Williams (stinger) could suit up.

In addition, the Seahawks run game appears to finally be developing into the unit the coaching staff hoped they'd become as Charbonnet and Price have combined for 100+ rushing yards the past two games.

Even though plenty seems to be trending Seattle's way, San Francisco is red-hot, and have proven why they still deserve to be favored.

Since losing to the Atlanta Falcons in Week 7, the 49ers have ripped off three consecutive wins — headlined by a dominant 35-10 victory over the Minnesota Vikings this past Sunday and they currently sit atop the NFC with a 9-1 record.

Prior to their blowout victory over the Vikings, Minnesota led the league by allowing 273 yards and a league-best 14 points per game. Just over a quarter in, quarterback Brock Purdy had already led two touchdown drives and the offense had generated 130 yards.

On the season, San Francisco is averaging 396.4 yards per game, trailing only the Kansas City Chiefs and despite an aging receiver core, head coach Kyle Shanahan has helped create playmakers out of nearly all his offensive weapons. On top of possessing one of the league's best quarterback rushers in Purdy, Christian McCaffrey has been dominant on both the ground and in the air, whereas rookie Kaelon Black has filled in as an effective change-of-pace option.

As for their pass-catchers, veteran receiver Mike Evans still profiles as the main threat downfield and on outside routes, with tight end George Kittle often being the go-to-guy over the middle and in the red zone. Although the Seahawks predominantly shut down the 49ers receivers in Week 5, Evans made the most of his mismatch against cornerback Josh Jobe and logged 113 yards plus a score on seven receptions.

Regardless of their success in recent weeks, Seattle's defense has proven they're up for the task — the question is whether the offense is as well.

Considering Macdonald said Nelson and Lucas would not have practiced last week if the team wasn't on bye, it appears likely the Seahawks will be without at least one of their offensive lineman, a loss that would significantly impact the offense as well as Darnold's time to throw and the line of 46.5 seemingly aligns with that.

Given the 49ers possess a three-game lead over Seattle in the NFC West, Sunday's matchup likely marks the Seahawks final glimmer of hope at winning the division so expect them to come out in an aggressive pursuit of their third consecutive victory.
