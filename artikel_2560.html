Why Tennessee finally jumped Alabama in CFP rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The College Football Playoff committee finally did the right thing and ranked Tennessee above Alabama in the latest 2026 playoff ranking on Tuesday night.

Before Tuesday, Alabama curiously ranked above Tennessee despite the Vols owning the head-to-head over the Crimson Tide from October's win at Neyland. Nobody really understood why.

Well, Alabama's strength of schedule has faded down the stretch, with its best wins against Kentucky and South Carolina now looking less impressive with both of those teams out of the CFP rankings altogether. Alabama officially hasn't beat a team ranked by the CFP as of Tuesday night.

CFP committee chair Hunter Yurachek explained that Tennessee moved up in the rankings above Alabama because the Vols are "now directly comparable" to the Crimson Tide in the committee's eyes.

At 10-2, Alabama should absolutely be ranked high by the committee in a sea of 3-win and 4-win teams. However, the team should not be ranked above Tennessee right now. It's good the committee finally fixed this.
