Amon-Ra St. Brown has his say on Jameson Williams’ backflip celebration after controversy

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Amon-Ra St. Brown has backed Jameson Williams' decision to continue doing flips as part of his celebrations after a touchdown.

Following the injury to the Detroit Lions' star defensive lineman, Aidan Hutchinson, head coach Dan Campbell asked players to stop celebrating by doing a backflip. A week after the injury, Jameson Williams scored a touchdown against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers and hit a front flip.

Campbell went on 97.1 The Ticket Show to give his take on what he felt about Williams' celebration.

"Well, I said no backflips," Campbell said. "Listen, I've got a million things going on. We're trying to get this stuff schemed up and make sure that we're right with everything, but no, all good."

Overall, Williams had a great game against the Bucs, as he recorded 4 receptions for 93 yards. His TD came from a 57-yard pass from quarterback Jarred Goff.

Amon-Ra St. Brown is himself known for his creative celebrations after a touchdown. Even in the game against the Bucs, the wide receiver had his own celebration that went viral online.

The 27-year-old has also done the backflip celebration in the past, which is why he did not have a problem with his teammate doing it.

"I love it. I love Jamo. Keep celebrating, Jamo. Don't let anyone tell you different. I mean, at the end of the day, look, everyone's going to say something if you get hurt doing anything. It's like, 'Oh, you should have done this.' But being an athlete, that's all I've got to say. Just be an athlete. Know what you can and can't do. Know your limits. And Jamo's been flipping for a long time, so I've got nothing to say to Jamo, man. Keep being you," said the Lions WR on the St. Brown podcast.

On social media, Williams' front flip was widely discussed as his landing was not the cleanest.

Fans would definitely not want another player to be ruled out due to an injury while celebrating. With the Lions already having suffered a serious injury, their hope is that Williams moves to a safer celebration, but it remains to be seen if he changes his ways and finds something different.
