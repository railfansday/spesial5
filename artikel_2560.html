Dodge’s NASCAR Return Gets Its First Crew Chief

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Dodge is finally coming back to the NASCAR Cup Series in 2027, and the team has picked its first crew chief. Kaulig Racing named Aaron Kramer to lead Ty Dillon's car for the upcoming manufacturer return.

Kramer has a specific skill set that fits the long-term plan. He is 38 years old and hails from Wellsville, New York. Before this job, he served as Cole Custer's crew chief at the Haas Factory team during the 2025 and 2026 seasons. His background includes years in engineering roles at RFK Racing. There, he worked as Chris Buescher's lead race engineer when Buescher won five Cup Series races between 2022 and 2024. He also worked with Ryan Newman during his time there.

The new hire is a UNC-Charlotte mechanical engineering graduate who started his career in 2010. Kaulig Racing CEO Chris Rice highlighted the need for this specific background in a statement. "Aaron has the exact engineering mindset and practical background we need as we lay the groundwork for 2027," Rice said, according to AOL.com. The team is hiring a driver coach and building a technical foundation from scratch.

Kramer himself sees the job as a major career step. He noted that his resume includes car preparation, vehicle performance, and race engineering. "Stepping into this role with Kaulig Racing at such a big turning point for the team is a great opportunity," Kramer said in a prepared statement. Solid engineering, relentless preparation, and immense focus are required to launch a Cup program from scratch with a returning manufacturer like Dodge, he noted.

The team faces a heavy workload in the months ahead. The No. 10 and No. 16 Dodges are being tested and developed on track by Ty Dillon alongside veteran A.J. Allmendinger. This is the second straight year Kaulig has faced a massive development task. Marking the OEM's comeback to NASCAR competition following a 13-year absence, the team ran five Ram trucks in the Craftsman Truck Series in 2026.
