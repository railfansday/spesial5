iOS 27.3 upgrades Apple Music in two ways, here’s what’s new

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Apple Music is getting two upgrades in iOS 27.3, which just debuted in beta this week. Here’s what’s new.

The most noteworthy upgrade is a new ‘Selfie Sing’ feature for creating karaoke videos. It was discovered by Aaron Perris in iOS 27.3 beta 1, and beta users can try it by searching for Apple’s ‘Selfie Sing’ playlist.

Here’s a walkthrough of what it looks like via Digital Trends on X:

Apple Music has a new selfie karaoke mode now, and it's so much fun. It just landed with iOS 27.3 developer beta update. pic.twitter.com/O6AcDOSppu

Essentially, Apple is leveraging the existing Apple Music Sing features into a standalone selfie video tool. You’ll be able to create your own selfie karaoke videos for sharing on platforms like TikTok, Instagram, and more.

Another iOS 27.3 upgrade for Music is found when using the lyrics view in landscape mode.

Apple needs to find a way to recover lost or grayed out songs. I have hundreds of songs like that. The sync method doesn’t work and I have to search for a duplicate song one at a time. I pay for Apple Music and they need to fix this.

Similar to what’s available on iPhone Duo, the animated artwork for what’s playing now bleeds further into the lyrics view. It’s a great look that’s now coming to all iPhone models.
