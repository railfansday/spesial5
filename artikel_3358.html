Hear Ella Langley’s Reflective ‘Hunger Games’ Single ‘Here Then We’re Gone’

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Ella Langley shared a new song, “Here Then We’re Gone,” which was created for the soundtrack to forthcoming film The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping. The pensive, acoustic country number was written by Langley, Aaron Raitiere, and Ernest, and produced by Dave Cobb.

“Things, they don’t always work out the way that I’d like/ Like how I wish he was still here to hold me tonight,” Langley croons on the track. “But I’m movin’ on, I’m climbin’ that hill/ Can’t slow down time, but could I make time stand still?”

The Sunrise on the Reaping soundtrack, out today, also features Noah Kahan’s previously-released song “Draw You Out.” It clocks in at 29 tracks, and includes songs by Noah Cyrus, Bella Kay, Stevie Nicks, Smashing Pumpkins, Violet Grohl, Gigi Perez, and more. Sunrise on the Reaping is in theaters on Nov. 20. It is a prequel to the original trilogy that follows a young Haymitch Abernathy (Joseph Zada) as he participates in the 50th edition of the annual Hunger Games. The film also stars Mckenna Grace, Whitney Peak, Ralph Fiennes, Jesse Plemons, Elle Fanning, Kieran Culkin, Kelvin Harrison Jr., Maya Hawke, and Lili Taylor. It was directed by Francis Lawrence, who has helmed the past four Hunger Games films.

Langley’s contribution to the soundtrack follows a busy year for the singer. She unveiled her sophomore album, Dandelion, in April and toured in support of the album throughout North America. She will perform as part of the 2026 CMA Awards on Nov. 18. Langley leads the pool in nominations for the awards with nine nods, including Entertainer of the Year.
