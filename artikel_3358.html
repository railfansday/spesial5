'Widow's Bay' and 'The Lowdown' Stars Reunite on New Episode of Ethan Hawke's 9-Part Horror Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

After an already-busy 2025, Ethan Hawke has somehow kept up the pace this year with one project after another. Theatrically, he's shared the screen with Russell Crowe in their new historical crime drama The Weight and lent his voice to Andrew Niccol's ambitious animated fantasy I, Object. On the small screen, meanwhile, he once again sought out the truth as the danger-prone Lee Raybon in Season 2 of The Lowdown, and he reprised his role as the Caped Crusader for DC's Batwheels series. Yet, in the middle of all that, he still found the time to launch his first podcast passion project, co-created by his Sinister and The Black Phone partners Scott Derrickson and C. Robert Cargill, and inspired by the AM talk radio days of Art Bell.

Midnight Radio is now halfway through its nine-episode run, and there are yet more eerie calls to take. The serialized supernatural show unfolds in real time, with Hawke taking the lead as the host of a cult-favorite late-night radio show. While used to hearing conspiracy theories and other bizarre claims, one night sends him into a chilling loop when a man claiming to be part of a long-lost U.S. space mission gives his line a ring. Their call opens the floodgates to stories of an entire web of government cover-ups, psychological warfare, and interdimensional breaches, alongside odd signals breaking through the static that raise questions about who, or what, is talking to the host. With such a wide-open theme, each episode brings its fair share of colorful characters with their own curious tales to tell, voiced by a series of big-name stars.

After previewing the series ahead of its debut, Collider is excited to once again partner with the iHeartPodcasts production and unveil the first listen for Episode 5, "Bone Machine." During this session, the host invites listeners to question whether the dead can communicate with the living through the power of cassette tapes. He has a selection of Electronic Voice Phenomena, or EVPs, to share that supposedly contain the words of the deceased, but there's more than meets the eye. Callers keep ringing in with claims about the existence of a clandestine ring of gambling groups where those recordings are the prize and, supposedly, among them is one so horrifying it not only stands out from the rest, but features the host himself.

Four franchises, four completely different ways of getting underneath your skin. This is the one built out of your particular fear, why it works so well on you, and what it would leave behind.

Your nightmare does not wait outside the door. It waits for you to close your eyes, which you are going to have to do eventually, because nobody has ever won that particular argument.

Your nightmare is not really the doll. It is the face of the person you tell, and the way they decide, very kindly, that you have imagined all of it.

Your nightmare has your number, knows your name, and has almost certainly been inside your house before. The worst part is that you already know them.

Your nightmare is not really about you. It is about the people asleep down the hall, and the feeling that whatever is here is not interested in leaving.

To help parse the strange recordings and discern the voices of the dead within them, Hawke's host will need the help of an expert. Fortunately, he just so happens to have one on hand in the form of Dr. Jill Esteban, played by the Oscar nominee's recent The Lowdown Season 2 co-star, Betty Gilpin. Like Hawke, Gilpin's had a busy 2026, between their neo-noir crime comedy, Aaron Sorkin's The Social Reckoning, and Apple TV's breakout horror comedy darling, Widow's Bay. Fittingly, one of the callers they'll deal with is also played by someone familiar with the island of Katie Dipold's Emmy winner — Hamish Linklater. Known for his time in Mike Flanagan's Midnight Mass, he voices "Speedy," a shady customer somehow entangled in the seedy ghostly tape trade.

In addition to iHeartPodcasts, Midnight Radio was produced by Ethan and Ryan Hawke's Under The Influence Productions banner, Cargill and Derrickson's Crooked Highway, and Mickey Schiff. Gilpin and Linklater are also just the tip of the iceberg as far as stars go. Noah Centineo, Vincent D’Onofrio, Willa Fitzgerald, Levon Hawke, Austin Pendleton, and Hubert Point-Du Jour were also previously announced to be appearing in the series. With four episodes remaining, there may yet be more surprises with spooky stories to share with listeners for those late-night drives.

Midnight Radio Episode 5 is available now through the iHeartRadio app and all major podcasting platforms. New episodes air every Thursday at 12 a.m. PT/3 a.m. ET. Listen to the latest episode through the official player above.
