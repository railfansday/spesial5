Katseye Postpones Chicago & Minneapolis Concerts As Megan Skiendiel Recovers From Concussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Katseye is taking a few more nights off their Wildworld Tour as Megan Skiendiel recovers from a concussion.

On Thursday, the group announced that they are postponing their Nov. 5 and 7 stops in Chicago and Minneapolis, which they plan to “reschedule as soon as possible” after also pushing Tuesday’s show that was scheduled for Detroit.

“The label has made the decision to postpone our shows in Chicago and Minneapolis as Megan is recovering from a concussion,” the group wrote on their Instagram Story.

“We are incredibly sorry to disappoint our fans. We know how much you’ve been looking forward to these shows, and that many of you have traveled long distances to be there. We don’t take that lightly, and we’re truly sorry for the disappointment,” they added. “We’ll announce the rescheduled dates as soon as possible, so please hold on to your tickets. We can’t wait to see you in both cities again soon. Thank you for your continued love, support and understanding.”
