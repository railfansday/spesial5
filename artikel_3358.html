Apple TV’s 2-Part Detective Series Officially Returns in 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Andrew Yancy is many things: a disgraced cop, a dedicated restaurant inspector, an animal lover, and a wiseass. Now, he's back for another season of action and misadventure in Bad Monkey. The dark crime comedy series is returning to Apple TV next week.

The first season of Bad Monkey saw Yancy (Vince Vaughn) busted down from detective to restaurant inspector after an altercation with his girlfriend's ex. Despite being reduced to looking for dead cockroaches in soup tureens, he gets involved with a twisty murder case when he's assigned to escort a severed arm to the morgue. There, he soon finds common cause with no-nonsense coroner Rosa Campesino (Natalie Martinez), who becomes his partner in life and crime-solving, as they and his cop pal Rogelio (John Ortiz) delve into a murder case that involves a Caribbean Obeah queen, a Medicare scam, a real estate scheme, and, as the title would indicate, one really bad monkey.

Now, in the second season, Yancy is still trying to get back on the force. This time, an opportunity has presented itself in the form of an ambitious state attorney (Yvonne Strahovski), who pairs him with a real Boy Scout of a forensic accountant to take on Florida's organized crime problem. That puts him in the crosshairs of Spencer (John Malkovich), a nasty crime boss with a fondness for ten-dollar words. Meanwhile, he's also got to deal with an unorthodox animal relocation expert, his own aging father (Scott Glenn), and his lingering feelings for Rosa after they parted ways in the season 1 finale. It all kicks off on December 2, exclusively on Apple TV.

A great detective is really just a personality with a method attached, which is why we keep remaking the same four of them. Here is yours, what it says about you, and the case for them being the best in the business.

You let people think you are not a threat and then you simply never go away. Columbo is the only major detective show that tells you who did it in the first ten minutes, because the pleasure was never the puzzle, it was watching a rich man slowly realise he is in trouble.

You are the most interesting person in the room and you know exactly how to use it. Rian Johnson built Blanc as a gentleman detective who is also an outsider everywhere he goes, which lets him be charming at people who would never otherwise talk to him.

Everybody tells you everything, and you have never once asked them to. Jessica Fletcher is a crime novelist from a small town in Maine who is so warm and so obviously trustworthy that suspects confess to her essentially as a social courtesy.

You expect to be lied to and you are almost never disappointed. Chandler wrote Marlowe as a man with a rigid private code in a city that has none, which is why he takes the case, solves it, gets nothing out of it and takes the next one.

Season 1 of Bad Monkey was based on the 2013 novel of the same name by Florida crime novelist Carl Hiaasen (R.J. Decker); although Hiaasen penned a sequel novel, Razor Girl, in 2016, it did not serve as the basis for season 2. Bill Lawrence (Shrinking) showruns and executive produces the series via his Doozer Productions; Vaughn, Hiaasen, Jeff Ingold, Matt Tarses, Liza Katzer, Adam Sztykiel, and Chris Downey also executive produce. The second season will also feature recurring roles for Sam Jaeger, Nate Jackson, Brent Morin, Peter Billingsley, June Diane Raphael, and Dax Shepard.

Season 2 of Bad Monkey premieres on December 2 exclusively on Apple TV. Stay tuned to Collider for future updates.
