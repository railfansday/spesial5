For Arteta, there are some interesting selection calls to make. It is anticipated that Jurrien Timber will replace Ben White at right-back, and first-choice left-back Riccardo Calafiori has been rested for the Champions League games so far. Should Arteta continue that policy, Myles Lewis-Skelly will fill the position, which will likely mean a midfield start for Bruno Guimarães or Mikel Merino.

Arsenal’s manager must also decide between Noni Madueke and Eberechi Eze on the left, and Viktor Gyokeres and Kai Havertz up front. Havertz is Arteta’s preference, but he also has to consider a relatively short turnaround before Saturday’s league game against Everton.
