Save On IHG Hotel Stays With Amex Offers (Targeted)

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Amex Offers program often has promotions that can save you money on hotel stays. We frequently see offers with Hilton and Marriott, since these are also Amex co-branded credit card partners. We’ve now seen a new offer rolled out for IHG hotel stays, which has the potential to be lucrative.

There’s a targeted Amex Offers deal for stays at mainstream IHG brand properties over the next few months:

Hotel stays can be expensive, especially at luxury brands, so it always makes sense to use a credit card that maximizes your return on hotel spending. The truth is that there are a limited number of Amex cards with good spending multipliers on hotels.

That’s why I think it’s worth pointing out that it’s typically not an issue to split payment methods at check-out, when staying at a hotel. So you could use an Amex card on which you have the Amex Offers deal for $250, and then put the remaining amount on a card that has a better rewards structure for hotel spending.

With a new targeted Amex Offers deal, you can save $50 when you spend $250+ at IHG properties in select regions, including the United States. This has the potential to be a very good deal, so if you have any upcoming IHG stays, be sure to register if eligible.
