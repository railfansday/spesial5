The Mandalorian and Grogu movie may have put the N1 out to pasture, but it's still a seriously cool ship and with seats for Mando and his little green pal, it has great play potential.

As seen in The Phantom Menace, this STAP-riding battle droid will hopefully survive a little longer than its on-foot counterparts. A particularly cool bonus is that it comes with a small minifigure version, with its own tiny STAP vehicle.

Jango Fett's iconic spaceship, and later Boba Fett's, this vessel might not look like much but it'll strike fear into the hearts of your other Star Wars minifigures. And unlike the bigger Ultimate Collector Series models, it's suitable for swooshing around the house.

Heavily discounted at Walmart, this Lego Star Wars Kylo Ren's Command Shuttle has been retired, so it could be your last chance to get your hands on this menacing vessel. Sadly, there's no Kylo Ren figure but it's a cool set nonetheless.

The UK may not celebrate Thanksgiving but Black Friday is still very much a thing and there are some great Black Friday Lego Star Wars deals, including £140 off the huge Lego Star Wars Venator-Class Starship set.

In our Lego Star Wars Venator-Class Republic Attack Cruiser review, we called this a "Stunning Lego recreation of one of Star Wars' coolest (and most underrated) ships." If you've got the space (it's over a metre long), this is a real bargain. And, since it's soon to be retired, this could be your last chance to buy it.

Another soon-to-be-retired set, this Jabba's Sail Barge set comes with an amazing ten minfigures, as well as a mega-sized Jabba the Hutt model. As seen in Star Wars: Return of the Jedi, there's no Sarlacc, sadly, but it's sure to be a major talking point wherever you set it up.

Straight out of the movie based on Andy Weir's book, this set features the film's ship, complete with the spinning mechanism that keeps Ryan Gosling's Ryland Grace and his alien pal Rocky from floating around.
