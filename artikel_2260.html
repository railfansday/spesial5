‘I don’t want him to leave’ – West Brom star dubbed the ‘best’ in the league

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

EFL pundit Tom Garratt has claimed West Brom’s Ousmane Diakite is the best holding midfielder in the Championship.

Speaking on the latest episode of the Pyramid Podcast that analysed all the midweek games in the second tier, Garratt reacted alongside co-host Callum Fowler to Albion’s 1-1 draw with Southampton on Tuesday evening, which was dubbed a fantastic advert for the Championship by Baggies boss James Morrison.

It was a tale of two halves in the Black Country, with Morrison’s men dominating Southampton throughout the first 45 minutes, as an Isaac Price penalty saw them go into the break with the lead.

But the Saints, who were so poor in the first half, turned up the heat in the second, going on to have the majority of possession and chances, as Samuel Edozie’s close-range effort got them back on level terms a matter of minutes into the second period.

The Baggies will have been disappointed to not have snatched all three points in the latter stages themselves though, as Aune Heggebo’s close-range effort was denied well by Daniel Peretz, while Tammer Bany clipped the outside of the post with a low-driven effort on a late Albion counter-attack.

Overall, a point was a fair result between two top Championship sides, who both should be in promotion contention come the end of the season.

And one player who really stood out in the game was Ousmane Diakite, with EFL pundit Tom Garratt making quite the claim about the man from Mali.

In a topsy-turvy game in B71 on Tuesday evening, while most West Brom supporters will have been content with a point, there will have been disappointment that they didn’t claim maximum points following a dominant first half and the opportunities they carved out late into the second.

But to have that feeling against a side that would have been top of the division without their four-point deduction at the start of the campaign, it’s a world apart from where Albion were just a few months ago under James Morrison, with the Black Country outfit scraping their way to survival.

It just goes to show how far the team and club have come in such a short space of time, and so much credit has to go to Morrison, who is getting unbelievable amounts out of his current squad, which has quite a few players in it who are experiencing the rough and tumble of the Championship for the very first time.

Playing a side like Southampton off the park for the first 45 minutes and forcing them to not even have a shot just goes to show that Albion can mix it with the very best in the division, and it was a real statement performance that they definitely are in the crop of teams that stand every chance of gaining promotion back to the Premier League.

Speaking on the latest episode of the Pyramid Podcast, (from 34:06), West Brom fan and EFL pundit Tom Garratt has claimed Ousmane Diakite is the best holding midfielder in the division following his performance against Southampton.

The Mali international was a titanic force in the middle of the park against the Saints, making 11 recoveries and six tackles, while possessing the pace to drive Albion up the pitch and make six line-breaking passes on the night, as per stats from FotMob.

Garratt is fearful that Albion could lose their star man if he keeps up these types of displays, while being shocked at the progress he’s made under Morrison's guidance.

Garratt said: “Say it quietly, because I don’t want him (Diakite) to leave, we might have the best holding midfielder in the league. He’s a joke mate.

“It’s been his third season now, but when he came in, I thought he was a donkey, like a donkey, and he’s just fast, he’s powerful, you can’t get the ball off him, he’s technically getting better and better each week, like his passing range is really improving.

“Molumby was excellent as well, and they really complement each other well.”

It was an awesome display from Ousmane Diakite against Southampton, who has come back with a vengeance after dropping out of the team in recent times.

Diakite was dropped to the bench for clashes against Wrexham and Blackburn Rovers as Toby Collyer stepped into the fray, but an injury suffered by the former Manchester United man allowed Diakite to get his place back in the starting XI, and he’s grabbed the opportunity firmly with both hands.

His performance against the Saints was a reflection of how far he’s come under James Morrison, with the current Albion boss the only man in the dugout to really hand Diakite a starting role on a regular basis, with much of his time at The Hawthorns seeing him be an impact player off the bench.

But he’s now proving why he’s one of the top performing players in the squad, with Diakite adding so much energy and aggressiveness with his defending, while he then shows bursts of pace to break into more advanced areas and setup teammates for chances with some cutting-edge passing.

The 26-year-old really does have so much to his game at both ends of the pitch, and it’s coming out in abundance now his confidence levels are skyrocketing.

Baggies supporters will be amazed at what they’re seeing from Diakite, who has gone from what looked to be a complete flop into a Championship superstar.

Diakite will be hopeful of another influential performance when Albion host Millwall this weekend before the international break.
