Blackburn Rovers sweat over injuries to two key defenders as West Ham challenge awaits

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Blackburn Rovers face West Ham in their final game before the November international break this Saturday. They come into the tie off the back of a 2-1 defeat against Stoke City, and Tony Mowbray’s side were without centre-back Tom Atcheson for the tie, while full-back Ryan Alebiosu came off in the first half.

Now, speaking to the press, Mowbray has said the Rovers duo will require further assessment to see if they can face the Hammers as they struggle with minor issues. As quoted by the club’s official media, the boss said: “Big Tom has still got a little niggle in his groin, so it’s about whether we take a chance with him or not. Ryan is going to go for a scan, and I hope it isn’t too bad.”

Alebiosu has been a mainstay for Blackburn Rovers this season, starting every Championship match and playing the full 90 minutes in all but two games. Atcheson meanwhile has made 11 appearances in all competitions, including 10 league starts.

Blackburn Rovers aren’t blessed for depth, so it would be a blow to go into Saturday’s game against West Ham without Alebiosu and Atcheson. Mowbray will have to work out a solution if either are ruled out.

Alebiosu has been the mainstay at right-back this season, but his absence would likely offer an opportunity for summer signing Gady Beyuku to impress. The 20-year-old has only made four substitute appearances to date, but he came on for 49 minutes against the Potters on Tuesday.

At centre-back, Hayden Carter partnered Sean McLoughlin with Atcheson out. The former has had injury problems of his own in recent times, so Mowbray may be hesitant to put him in for another full 90 minutes just a few days later.

This is an area where Mowbray has very little depth though. Scott Wharton was the only other central defender in the squad on Tuesday, and he has been on a long road back of his own.

It means Carter may start again if Atcheson is out, with Wharton called upon off the bench late on.

Of course, the hope will be that both Alebiosu and Atcheson are fit and ready to go against West Ham this weekend. However, if they aren’t deemed fit, the Blackburn Rovers defenders will have the international break to recover and rest up before the next club fixture.

After Saturday, the next league fixture sees Rovers head to Turf Moor to face rivals Burnley. Mowbray will be determined to have key men Alebiosu and Atcheson available for the fixture.

One potential worry is Atcheson’s call-up to the Northern Ireland squad for the upcoming international fixtures. If he isn’t deemed fit for Saturday, there will be questions over whether he should link up with Michael O’Neill‘s national side as planned.

Nigeria have not announced their squad for their AFCON qualifiers, but Alebiosu would be in a similar predicament if he is included in their ranks.
