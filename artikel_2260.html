Bailey Cadamarteri needs to escape Wrexham: Portsmouth & Plymouth must act

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Bailey Cadamarteri made the switch to Wrexham from Sheffield Wednesday in the January window earlier this year.

But the forward has struggled for consistent game time since signing for the Red Dragons, making just four appearances in the Championship so far this campaign.

The 21-year-old has yet to make a start in the league for the Welsh outfit, as he approaches a full year since making the switch from Hillsborough.

His lack of minutes under Phil Parkinson has prompted a lot of speculation surrounding his immediate future at the Racecourse Ground, with his Dad even hitting out after defeat to Sheffield United.

With the January window now quickly approaching, it’s becoming increasingly apparent that a temporary, or perhaps even permanent, exit could be on the cards in the new year.

Here we look at two clubs who must act if Cadamarteri becomes available in the January window…

Portsmouth have scored the fewest number of goals in the top half of the Championship table after the first 14 games.

John Mousinho’s side have found the back of the net on only 19 occasions, and Marko Milovanovic’s injury in their 3-1 midweek win against Swansea City only raises further concerns regarding their attacking options.

The Pompey boss has shown that he can work well with younger players, and someone like Cadamarteri could be a smart option to bolster their ranks in attack.

If the forward wants to remain in the second division, then a move to Fratton Park for the second half of the campaign could be his best bet.

The chance to work under Mousinho is promising, and players like Odin Bailey, Adrian Segecic and Tyrese Shade could complement the 21-year-old, to get the best out of him.

Depending on the extent of Milovanovic’s injury, Cadamarteri could find himself earning far more regular game time at Fratton Park, particularly with the likes of Eoin Kenny and Thomas Waddingham for competition.

If Cadamarteri is willing to take the step down to League One, then he could do worse than joining Plymouth Argyle on loan.

There is already speculation surrounding Bim Pepple’s position at the club following his impressive performances in Tom Cleverley’s side.

If the striker departed Home Park in the January window, then that could open the door for someone like Cadamarteri to step in and take on the mantle of leading number nine.

With the success of Pepple, and previously Lorent Tolaj, working under the Plymouth boss could be an exciting opportunity to improve as a player, and receive regular game time.

It is vitally important for the 21-year-old that he prioritises consistent minutes with any potential January exit, and a switch to League One might be the step he needs to take in order to guarantee that.

If Pepple is moved on, then that leaves players like Owen Oseni to compete against for game time, and Cadamarteri should fancy himself in that setup.

The Devon outfit are also aiming to fight for promotion to the Championship, and helping the club earn a return to the second tier would be a real showcase of what he is capable of at this stage of his career.
