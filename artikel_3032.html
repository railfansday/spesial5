SLR Investment (SLRC) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Management flags rising defaults even as record originations keep coming.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

SLR Investment Corp. (SLRC +3.31%) reported third quarter results characterized by record originations and stable net investment income that fully supported its dividend. Management reported a strategic focus on specialty finance, with 95% of the $575.1 million in new investments allocated to collateral-based lending rather than traditional cash flow loans. The company maintained a conservative leverage profile at 1.1x net debt-to-equity and reported significant liquidity with over $850 million in available capital. While net asset value per share declined slightly to $17.83 due to the performance of a single nonaccrual loan, the company noted that its broader portfolio remains healthy with limited exposure to cyclical 2021 and 2022 vintages. Additionally, the firm utilized its stock repurchase program to acquire $10 million in shares post-quarter end, which is expected to be accretive to future net asset value per share.
