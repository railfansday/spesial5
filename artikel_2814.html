Aidan Hutchinson got hurt celebrating a sack. A Lions LB thought he was joking

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Detroit Lions edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson suffered a serious-looking knee injury in his team's 35-10 win over the New England Patriots in Munich.

Hutchinson's injury occurred as he celebrated a sack of Patriots quarterback Drake Maye in the first quarter of the Week 10 game. The 26-year-old jumped in the air and as he landed, his right knee buckled. He collapsed to the turf in pain as a result of that.

While Hutchinson immediately knew something was wrong, his plight didn't immediately register with his teammates, as Lions linebacker Derrick Barnes explained following the game.

"You know how people do the fake injury thing and they get up and start doing the dance?" Barnes told the Detroit Free Press, part of the USA TODAY Network, in the locker room after the game. "Aidan being as goofy as he is, I thought that's what he was doing."

"And then I looked, I was like, 'Aidan,' I was like, 'Don't scare me like that,' cause I thought he was just joking," Barnes continued. "And he was just like, 'I'm not.' I said, 'Are you serious?' He's like, 'Yeah.'"

Lions coach Dan Campbell told reporters during a postgame news conference the team did not have any further updates on Hutchinson's status. The star pass rusher will undergo further evaluation of his injury once the team returns to the United States.

That said, multiple reports indicate that Hutchinson's injury is "feared" to be significant. So, certainly, his injury is no joke, and the Lions may need to prepare to be without the two-time Pro Bowler for the long haul.
