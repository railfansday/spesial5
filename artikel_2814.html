Pistons vs. Pelicans preview: Closing out the road trip in New Orleans

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Pistons have played a lot of road games so far this season. They have also played less games than a lot of other teams because of that. Last night's game against the San Antonio Spurs was probably their toughest test of the season so far, and they passed it with flying colors.

The Spurs were in the midst of a 12 game winning streak and playing at home against a Pistons team down 4 players who were in their opening night rotation. Despite that, they went into San Antonio and imposed their will and came out on top.

Now, they head to New Orleans to close out this 4-game road trip against a Pelicans team that has struggled this season. After a game like last night, these are the exact kind of games that can be a bit of a trap, so hopefully the Pistons come out ready to play and close out this road trip with a 3-1 record.

The Pistons are still set to be short-handed, but as they proved last night, that doesn't matter to this team.

Where: Smoothie King Center in New Orleans, LAWhen: Wednesday, November 18 at 8 pm ESTWatch: Scripps Sports NetworkOdds: Pistons (-7.5)

I mentioned the Pistons injuries above, as they are expected to once again be without Isaiah Joe, Duncan Robinson, Taurean Prince, Ron Holland, and Jalen Duren. However, New Orleans is also without a slew of rotation players like Benedict Mathurin, Trey Murphy III, and Dejounte Murray. The biggest difference is the Pistons have one of the deepest rosters in the league to overcome missing some key players while the Pelicans have a less talented roster.

None of that is to say that this game will be easy, but after a hard-fought battle against the Spurs last night, it will be nice to not have to deal with an imposing force like Victor Wembanyama again.

The Pistons depth was put to the test against the Spurs and the Pistons proved why they are one of the best teams in the Eastern Conference by coming out on top. Of course, it was led by great 2-way performances by Cade Cunningham and Ausar Thompson, but that is to be expected of the Pistons two stars.

The Pelicans don't present that same size-related issues the Pistons have had to deal with in their last few games, which is lucky for them. The Pelicans do have a couple of players capable of taking over a game, like Jordan Poole and Zion Williamson, so it is important that the Pistons stay disciplined on defense, but Zion Williamson at least doesn't tower over players like Ausar Thompson, who has had to take on more big matchups with the Pistons opting to start John Collins at center.

The Collins at center lineup has had a bit of diminishing returns lately as he has struggled to deal with size and rebounding, but it has allowed the Pistons to start Daniss Jenkins, who has been on a heater lately. He had 25 points, 5 rebounds, and 5 assists last night against the Spurs and a couple of clutch shots. He also had 21 and 22 points in back-to-back games against the Wizards after a 31 point game against the Lakers. The Pistons will have a tough decision to make when Jalen Duren returns as they probably should continue to start Jenkins, but Duren doesn't bring the same level of spacing as John Collins does and John Collins was signed to complement Jalen Duren at the four.

But, those are the good problems to have when you have a team as deep as the Pistons.

Another player to watch for the Pelicans is Jeremiah Fears, who had a triple-double in the Pelicans' dismantling of the Golden State Warriors on Monday. He is starting to put things together and develop into their future at point guard right at a time when Dejounte Murray has requested a trade. Say what you want about the process in how the Pelicans have drafted and acquired picks in the last couple seasons, but they at least appear to have hit on Fears.

Another reclamation project they have hit on is former Piston, Saddiq Bey. He earned an extension in the offseason after putting together a pretty solid season last year after recovering from the torn ACL he suffered a couple years ago. As Pistons fans know, Bey is capable of getting hot, so he is another player they will have to look out for.

Overall, this shouldn't be too difficult of a matchup for the Pistons, but after the emotions of a hard-fought battle last night, I would not be shocked to see a slow start out of the Pistons. But, even with the injured players, the Pistons are the more talented team and should be able to win this game.

Detroit Pistons (9-4): Cade Cunningham, Daniss Jenkins, Kevin Heurter, Ausar Thompson, John Collins

New Orleans Pelicans (5-10): Jeremiah Fears, Herb Jones, Saddiq Bey, Zion Williamson, Yves Missi
