Bengals vs. Falcons: How to watch, game time, TV info, online stream, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Cincinnati Bengals crossed the Atlantic Ocean to meet the Atlanta Falcons in Madrid, Spain. The game starts at 9:30 am ET, so most Bengals fans will be watching over breakfast.

This one has all the makings of a good game for the international fans who will be taking it in. Joe Burrow has been playing some of his best football, airing it out to Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins, and their defense can be very splashy with sacks and turnovers.

On the other side, running back Bijan Robinson represents everything that has given this Cincinnati defense issues. The Bengals have had issues containing most running backs this season that are willing to run to the outside. He also is a danger with screens or quick passes. Anything that isolates him on a linebacker or safety Jordan Battle is going to be an issue that will likely result in plenty of explosive runs.

This game has all the makings for some eye-popping highlights passing and rushing the football. Who will be dipping their churros in victory while the other is left to run with the bulls?

Location: Santiago Bernabéu Stadium in Madrid, Spain

Broadcasters: Bob Wischusen will call play-by-play alongside Louis Riddick as analyst and Molly McGrath as sideline reporter.

Radio Broadcast: The Bengals Radio Network broadcast can be heard in Cincinnati on 700 WLW, 1530 WCKY, and 102.7 WEBN. Dan Hoard will call play-by-play alongside analysts Dave Lapham and Tony Pike. You can also listen to the game on SiriusXM Ch. 263 and Westwood One Sports.

Series Leader: The Bengals lead the series 10-5. The Bengals have won three straight since 2014.

Odds: FanDuel Sportsbook has the Bengals as 3-point favorites, with an over/under of 52.5 points.
