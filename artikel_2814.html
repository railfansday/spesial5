Paul George, traded for Brown after two seasons with the 76ers, finished 6-of-11 from 3-point range and keyed two separate runs that helped Boston run away with the game. The nine-time All-Star has taken advantage of more offensive responsibility in Boston, where he's averaging more points and more shot attempts through 10 games than he did last season with the Sixers. "I'm a little bit better with more usage, and that's no knock to how my opportunities here with Philly, you know, quite frankly I wasn't healthy to take on the workload that I feel I'm at my best at," George told the NBC broadcast after the game. "But here in Boston they gave me an opportunity to come out here and play and be myself."ESPN
