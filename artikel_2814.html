Caleb Williams finishes with 345 passing yards

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Chicago Bears QB Caleb Williams went 30-for-44 for 345 yards and two touchdowns against the Detroit Lions in Week 12. He also rushed for 15 yards on three carries during the game.

Why It Matters: Williams posted his third 300-yard game of the season against the Lions and has thrown at least two touchdowns in four of his last five games. He will be facing the Jacksonville Jaguars in Week 13 and can be considered a No. 1 quarterback in fantasy leagues.
