Tom Brady, after selling Birmingham City stake, buys piece of Chelsea

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Tom Brady’s exit from the English soccer business was only temporary.

After selling his stake in Birmingham City, Brady has reportedly purchased a slice of the Chelsea soccer club, albeit indirectly.

Via ESPN, Brady will become a minority investor in Clearlake Capital, the entity that controls Chelsea.

Separate reporting from the Times characterizes the situation as Brady being “in talks” to take a “small minority stake” within the portion of Clearlake Capital that owns Chelsea. Brady, per the Times, also would become a member of the board of advisors.

Whether that means he’ll eventually be criticizing the work ethic of the team’s manager remains to be seen.

The end result is that Brady hasn’t streamlined his overall portfolio in an effort to achieve more “balance” in his life. Instead, he’s swapping out his piece of a second-tier club for interest in a member of the Premier League.
