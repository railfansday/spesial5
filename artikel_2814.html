Vikings rule Jauan Jennings out for Monday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Wide receiver Jauan Jennings passed on a possible trade back to the 49ers in order to remain with the Vikings, but he won’t be in Minnesota’s lineup against the Bills on Monday night.

The Vikings ruled Jennings out due to a calf injury that kept him out of practice the last two days. Jennings has six catches for 54 yards this season, but his limited role in the offense didn’t cause him to reach for the exit door last month.

With Jennings out, the Vikings could elevate Odell Beckham Jr. from the practice squad to make his first appearance for the team. The Vikings have until Monday afternoon to elevate him for the game.

Quarterback Kyler Murray was ruled out with a hamstring injury earlier this week. Carson Wentz will make his second start of the season on Monday night.

Running back Demond Claiborne (knee) will also miss the game. Safeties Theo Jackson (quad) and Jay Ward (knee) are listed as questionable.
