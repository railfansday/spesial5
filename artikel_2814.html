Ravens discussion: loss to Chargers would be most embarrassing since…?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Ravens have some bad losses on their ledger over the last several years.

But even with all the blown leads and second-half collapses in recent years, falling to the Chargers – the winless Chargers – on Monday night might be a new low. The following is true of Jim Harbaugh's squad this year:

-98 point differential through eight games…

…good for a 12.25-point average margin of defeat

18.9% of offensive drives end in a turnover

The Chargers will also be without star wide receiver Ladd McConkey on Monday night, while a few other key players are questionable.

All of these factors should tip the scales in the Ravens' direction, even with Jacoby Brissett set to make his debut. But there is a reason winless seasons are rare. This is an Any Given Sunday league, wherein last week's defeat is forgotten in the fog of a new war. The Chargers are probably going to beat someone eventually. Wouldn't it be so spot-on for the Ravens' 2026 season if it happened in Baltimore on Monday night?

That would almost certanily be the Ravens' most embarrassing loss this season. That's saying something after off three straight defeats of 22 points or more, but those came against three of the best teams in the AFC with a combination of Tyler Huntley and Joe Fagnano under center. Last year's 44-10 loss to the Texans looms large given the margin, but what more were you expecting from Cooper Rush, a bad O-line against a fiercesome Houston front, and a suddenly banged-up defense?

My pick: Week 10 of the 2021 season, a 22-10 loss to the Dolphins. The Ravens had no answers for Brian Flores' defense. Lamar Jackson could not summon his usual magic to dance away from pressure and make plays downfield, and Baltimore's offense walked on the field like lambs to the slaughter every drive. A solid showing from the defense – ironically, against Brissett for much of the game – was not enough to rescue one of the worst showings of Jackson's career.

Dishonorable mention: Come to think of it, Week 2 of the 2022 season – Mike Macdonald's second game as defensive coordinator – was an embarrassing loss, too. The Ravens seem to only throttle the Dolphins or melt down in spectacular fashion.
