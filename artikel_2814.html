One thing Patriots must fix coming out of Week 11 bye week

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The biggest thing that the New England Patriots need to fix after their 35-10 loss to the Detroit Lions last week is their running game, which offered little support for the team's offense. Establishing consistent production on the ground must come first as New England evaluates its shortcomings.

Corey Kiner led the Patriots' backfield with 25 yards on 10 carries against Detroit. Starter Rhamondre Stevenson did play through injury, but still managed only four yards with the six carries he was given, leaving the pair with just 29 yards on 16 rushes. Neither back provided the sustained production needed to keep the offense balanced.

Kiner's performance marked a step backward from Week 9, when he gained 55 yards and scored a touchdown on eight carries against Green Bay. As for Stevenson, while his efforts while fighting through injury have been commendable, he struggled for a second consecutive week after rushing six times for 32 yards against the Packers, extending concerns about the Patriots' offensive backfield with TreVeyon Henderson out.

Admittedly, the Patriots' offensive line has dealt with a wave of injuries this season, with starting guards Mike Onwenu and Alijah Vera-Tucker both still on injured reserve, Ben Brown having missed the game against the Lions and Greg Van Roten having returned from injury last week. However, with Vera-Tucker being eligible to return from IR next week, reinforcements might be on the way, which could bode well for the team moving forward.

The Patriots need Henderson's explosiveness to jolt their offense and turn routine carries into meaningful gains. His big-play ability would provide a missing element to New England's offense once he returns from injury, but improved blocking and execution remain essential as well. New England needs dependable rushing production moving forward to string together more wins for the rest of the season.

The team might be able to fix its issues on the ground by signing a free agent to aid their efforts while Stevenson works his way back to 100 percent health and Henderson recovers. Or, they could give second-year running back Lan Larison more carries to see what he is capable of giving the team.

Given the fact that Patriots quarterback Drake Maye got hurt in the game and was replaced by Tommy DeVito, the run game became the biggest glaring issue that held the team back from having a more productive outing, and it is what needs to be addressed most prior to Week 12.
