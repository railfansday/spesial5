LeBron James gets warm welcome in Cleveland before Cavaliers rout 76ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

LeBron James might have chosen to join the Philadelphia 76ers instead of returning to the Cleveland Cavaliers, but it was all love in "The Land."

James and the 76ers took on the Cavaliers in Cleveland for an NBA Cup game on Friday, Nov. 6. and the atmosphere was warm and welcoming for the 24-year veteran and Ohio native.

In the tunnel to the locker room, there was a video board that said "Welcome Home LeBron!" with an image of him holding up Cleveland's 2016 NBA Finals trophy. During the Prime pregame show, a young fan held up James' St. Vincent–St. Mary High School jersey, and other fans were shown wearing Cavaliers jerseys with James' name on them.

Just before tipoff, James did his signature chalk toss and fans were crowding around with their phones to record the moment. A video from ESPN NBA reporter Dave McMenamin on X showed that as the 76ers were being announced, Jaylen Brown and Joel Embiid received boos but James was greeted with cheers.

All of that happened before the Cavaliers smacked Philadelphia 119-95. They held their hometown hero to eight points, and James committed six turnovers. The loss snapped the 76ers' five-game winning streak. James' eight points also snapped his own 396-game streak of scoring 10+ points in regular-season games in Cleveland, the "NBA on ESPN" social media account reported.

James has now gone three years without beating teh Cavaliers in Cleveland. According to the Prime pregame show, James hasn't beat the Cavaliers in their home arena since November 25, 2023 when the Lakers won 121-115, when he had 22 points, six assists and six rebounds.

This offseason, James informed the Los Angeles Lakers that he was going to leave the team. The Cavaliers were among the teams that the NBA's all-time leading scorer was considering. The 76ers won the battle with James crediting Tyrese Maxey for helping him make his decision.

James' 76ers teammate Dean Wade also joined Philadelphia this offseason after spending the first seven seasons of his career in Cleveland. He spoke to the media ahead of the game about his memories facing James in his hometown.

"In my seven years, anytime that LeBron came back it was just a madhouse. It was crazy," he said in a video posted by McMenamin on X. "The fans were awesome. So I expect that to be what tonight's going to be like. Super loud, a lot of energy. I think it's gonna be a fun night."

James grew up in Akron, Ohio and joined the Cavaliers out of high school as the No.1 overall pick in the 2003 draft. He spent the first seven seasons of his career in Cleveland before leaving to join the Miami Heat in "The Decision" in 2010. After winning two championships in South Beach, he returned for the 2014-15 season. He guided the Cavaliers to their first NBA title in 2016 when he was named Finals MVP. James left again in 2018 to join the Lakers and won his fourth championship in 2020.
