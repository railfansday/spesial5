GodLike Esports sweep Trigger Point Esports to stay unbeaten in South Asia Kickoff qualifier

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

GodLike Esports beat Trigger Point Esports 2-0 in the upper bracket of the VCT Pacific Kickoff South Asia qualifier. Only two South Asian sides will reach the Last Chance Qualifier, where a single place at Kickoff in Bangkok is on offer.

GodLike Esports opened their VCT Pacific Kickoff: South Asia – Qualifier 2027 playoff campaign with a 2-0 win over Trigger Point Esports on Monday 23 November 2026. GodLike took both maps of the best-of-three upper bracket quarter-final. Trigger Point Esports now drop into the lower bracket of the double-elimination format.

The playoffs are being held online by Riot Games and JioBLAST. Series in the bracket are played as best-of-three or best-of-five.

GodLike arrived in form. They reached the playoffs with an unbeaten 4-0 record in the open qualifier, VLR.gg reported. They shared joint first to fourth place in that event, which ran from 17 to 20 November. Trigger Point finished in the group placed fifth to eighth.

GodLike fielded Karam1L, Paradox and RvK from India, alongside sayoo from Indonesia and azyshaha from the Philippines. Sabyasachi ‘Antidote’ Bose is listed as head coach, with Samar as substitute. Trigger Point’s listed players are SangraM, WEELJACK and RaJ.

South Asian teams cannot qualify directly for Kickoff. The top two from the playoffs join the Oceania playoff winner and the Southeast Asia playoff runner-up in a four-team Last Chance Qualifier. Only its winner goes through to Kickoff in Bangkok, according to Dot Esports.

The stakes are high. Twelve teams will play at Kickoff, and the top three will qualify for Masters 1. The route stays narrow later in the season too. South Asia has no direct place at the Cups, with Cup 1 in Manila and Cup 2 in Tokyo. It sends two teams to an eight-team Pacific LCQ, and the winner takes the last Cup place.

The qualifier also has a new organiser. Riot picked JioBLAST to run the South Asia qualifiers instead of NODWIN Gaming, the previous organiser. Riot has not said why it made the change.

The UK interest lies with SIGN Esports. They beat Zexar Esports 2-0 in another upper bracket quarter-final. Russel ‘Russ’ Mendes joined SIGN Esports on 16 November 2026. He is the only player from the UK in the eight-team playoff field.

Russ was born in 1998 and is of Indian descent. He won the VALORANT Challengers 2025 South Asia Grand Final with Velocity Gaming on 13 July 2025, when they beat S8UL 3-1.

GodLike Esports and SIGN Esports both remain in the upper bracket, one defeat away from the lower bracket. Trigger Point Esports and Zexar Esports must win from the lower bracket to stay in contention for a top-two finish. The playoffs run until 29 November 2026.
