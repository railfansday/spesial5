Earlier this month, rumors surfaced about a potential Magic: The Gathering x Dune Secret Lair Superdrop. The rumors quickly spread like wildfire across internet forums like Reddit, but nothing came directly from Wizards of the Coast.

That was until now, when the official Secret Lair Twitter account teased the crossover, revealing it's set to drop in less than two weeks' time.

"The thumper is set and Shai-Hulud is on the move," Wizards of the Coast teased on social media, with a short video of a thumper pounding the sand, followed by the Secret Lair logo. "My Arrakis, My Superdrop goes on sale November 30. #DuneMovie"

This MTG Secret Lair tweet all but confirmed that everything in the recent leak was true. The name, "My Arrakis, My Superdrop," matches the one that was leaked, so we can probably assume everything else was correct.

It said there would be three individual drops based around Sandworms, artifacts, and enchantments, followed by a set of legendary creatures based on eight characters from the Dune movies. The set is reported to include reprints like Sandstorm Convergence and Darksteel Forge, but 11 of the 20 leaked cards are completely new to the Secret Lair drop.

Further details have been confirmed on Reddit, with one of the drops coming in Halo Foil. The commander cards will include some fan-favorite characters, including Paul Atreides, Baron Vladimir Harkonnen, and Chani. The third set, Spice Harvest, is the only one whose price has leaked so far, retailing for $ 59.99.

The Secret Lair Superdrop arrives just in time for the Dune: Part Three movie, set to release in theatres worldwide on December 18, the same day as Avengers: Doomsday. It will be the 13th Secret Lair Superdrop of the year, following the likes of The Hobbit, Lofi Girl, My Little Pony, and Star Trek.

We'll have more coverage of this drop coming soon to TheGamer, including a handy guide, so keep your eyes peeled.
