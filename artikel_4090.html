Tanggal: 2026-11-08

What a bleddy sale we have for you this week. Xbox has massively discounted a tonne of games, so we're back to rummage through them and pick out a banger or two for you.

Xbox is currently running an ID@XBOX Deep Discounts sale, knocking a fair bit of coinage off some awesome indie titles. We've highlighted plenty of those indie bangers, but also thrown in a few other notable deals worth checking out.
