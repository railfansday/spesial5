Pierce Brosnan Welcomes Robert Pattinson Into the James Bond Family: ‘You Are Going to Be Magnificent’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Pierce Brosnan, a former James Bond himself, has taken to social media to enthusiastically endorse the newly anointed Robert Pattinson, who was announced as the new 007 on Monday.

“Dear Robert, Welcome to the world of Bond,” Brosnan, who played the character in four films from 1995 to 2002, wrote on Instagram Wednesday. “You are going to be magnificent. I have fond memories of our time together making ‘Remember Me.’ It has been a joy to watch you create a remarkable body of work and to go from strength to strength. Enjoy the ride. As the “Parent Trap” actor noted in his caption, the two stars first worked together on 2010’s “Remember Me,” a romantic drama also starring Emilie de Ravin and Meghan Markle. With his statement, Brosnan joins stars including Pattinson’s “Tenet” co-star Elizabeth Debicki and former Bond villain Rami Malek in congratulating the “Twilight” actor on securing the role.

Pattinson’s announcement as the next Bond put an end to long-swirling rumors surrounding which actor would step into the role. Other stars in the mix included Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, Nicholas Hoult, Josh O’Connor, Tom Francis, Aaron Taylor-Johnson, Harris Dickinson and even Harry Styles. Denis Villeneuve will direct the forthcoming film from Amazon MGM.

Before the casting news broke, Brosnan told The Times in September that he thought the new James Bond would be “someone that none of us know about.” He had also noted that “there are lots of good hats in the ring” and “lots of great actors” being considered. When it came to names, Brosnan remained neutral: “I wouldn’t pick anyone,” he continued. “My wife told me not to say anything, and I’ve said too much. Because once I say something it goes [everywhere] and I get hanged.”
