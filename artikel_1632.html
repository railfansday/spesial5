Arena aiming to make home advantage count in San Jose’s playoff opener

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Bruce Arena insisted the San Jose Earthquakes must use the PayPal Park atmosphere to their advantage in their MLS Cup Playoff opener against the Houston Dynamo.

San Jose are back in the playoffs for the first time since 2023 after a strong regular season that saw them finish fourth in the Western Conference, one point clear of Houston.

The Earthquakes have won just one of their last seven MLS games (D4 L2) and enter the postseason on the back of a 3-1 loss to Minnesota United.

And their record on home soil has not been the best in 2026. San Jose won just seven matches in front of their own supporters in the league, the fifth-lowest tally in the West.

Arena, however, believes the San Jose supporters have a big role to play when Houston visit for Game 1 of their best-of-three series.

"Making the playoffs was our goal, and we achieved that," said Arena. "Now it's going to be important to get a big crowd for the first game against Houston.

"Home-field advantage means something, so if we can get the support from the stands and play well on the field, hopefully that produces a win."

Houston, meanwhile, signed off for the playoffs with back-to-back defeats, including a Decision Day loss against St. Louis City SC, with Lukas MacNaughton netting a late winner.

But when it comes to the playoffs, the Dynamo have great pedigree. Only three teams have won back-to-back MLS Cup titles, including the 2006-07 Houston side.

The Dynamo also reached the MLS Cup final in 2011 and 2012, but were beaten by the LA Galaxy on both occasions, with the team out for revenge 15 years later.

They have already won the 2023 U.S. Open Cup under head coach Ben Olsen, and now could tie the Columbus Crew (three) for the third-most titles in league history.

Judd was the key to San Jose's offence this season, scoring 17 goals in the MLS regular season.

No Earthquakes player has scored more in a single regular season since Chris Wondolowski scored 27 in 2012.

Houston built its season on a strong defence, which can be highlighted by Halter’s tackle success rate.

Of Dynamo players who played a minimum of 1000 minutes, no player was more successful in their tackles, as he recorded a tackle success rate of 73.1%.

San Jose and Houston have never met in the MLS Cup playoffs but have played 37 regular-season matches, with the Dynamo leading the series with 19 wins to San Jose's 12 (six draws).

The Earthquakes are, however, currently on a four-game undefeated streak against the Dynamo (W2 D2), having not lost since 2024, setting up an intriguing encounter.

It is the first time since 2023 that San Jose have qualified for the playoffs. They have advanced past Round One only once since returning to the MLS in 2008, knocking out New York Red Bulls in 2010.

Houston, meanwhile, are making their first appearance in the playoffs since 2024, coming in with a record of W18 D11 L11 in the postseason.

Their last playoff win came against Sporting Kansas City in 2023, and they have since managed a record of W0 D2 L1.
