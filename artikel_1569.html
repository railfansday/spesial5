Manchester United should finally fire Michael Carrick quickly!

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

It is as relegation players that many Manchester United players will leave their club to join their selection for this international break. Indeed, after 10 days of the championship, the Red Devils are in a sad eighteenth place in the Premier League standings. A catastrophic situation given the legendary status of the club and the investments made in recent years.

Obviously, one man is singled out: Michael Carrick. Although he had obtained satisfactory results last season, taking over from Ruben Amorim and convincing the decision-makers to offer him the position over time, the former Mancunian player is very criticized. And if the English media were still explaining this morning that he was not yet in danger, the trend has changed.

Several English media like The Athletic Or The Guardian thus indicate that he should be removed from office quickly. The Athletic relies on sources within the club, who claim that the dismissal process has already begun and that the club is working on a replacement, confirming that there will be a change before the next Premier League match against Liverpool after the break.

The Guardian is slightly less affirmative, but confirms that the trend is towards dismissal, while sporting director Jason Wilcox spoke on this subject with Omar Barrada, the executive director of the club. According to English media, the club already has several potential candidates to replace Carrick, including Eddie Howe and Mauricio Pochettino. To be continued.
