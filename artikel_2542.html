NFC playoff picture: 49ers cement spot at top of standings, Vikings plummet after SNF beatdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The San Francisco 49ers were confronted by a formidable opponent Sunday night in Mexico City when the Minnesota Vikings brought their dominating defense south of the border. The Vikings were coming off an impressive 30-10 road victory over the Green Bay Packers, and their confidence was high after trading for QB Kirk Cousins.

The Vikings may have had their share of supporters, but the crowd of 85,612 at Estadio Azteca was clearly backing the 49ers. Those in attendance got to see Brock Purdy, Mike Evans and Christian McCaffrey at their best and 49ers rolled to a 35-10 triumph. San Francisco came into the game as the No. 1 seed in the NFC playoff structure, and that position looks even more secure after their Week 11 victory.

There are still seven games to go on their schedule, but head coach Kyle Shanahan, his coaching staff and the Niners are surely going for the top spot. They have a 3.0-game lead in the NFC West, and they have the talent to put up a fight every week with the Seahawks, Giants and Rams on the schedule the next three weeks..

They played with no fear against the Vikings, and that attitude and demeanor will undoubtedly continue.

The NFC North is currently a battle between the Chicago Bears and the Detroit Lions. Those two teams are both streaking and they will meet Thanksgiving Day in a traditional holiday matchup in the Motor City. Both team are 8-2 and the winner will be in a position to chase the Niners while gaining a foothold on the No. 2 seed.

The Lions have won six games in a row as their offense is hitting on all gears with Jared Goff under center along with running back Jahmyr Gibbs and wide out Amon-Ra St. Brown. The Lions have lost defensive stars Aidan Hutchinson (knee) and Jack Campbell (neck), and those are huge blows, but Dan Campbell's defense is playing effectively.

The Bears have won five games in a row since suffering a 31-7 loss to Green Bay in Week 5. The Bears have high hopes that QB Caleb Williams can lift the team down the stretch in the regular season, but he has not been as effective as he was in their playoff run last year. However, the acquisition of Jeffery Simmons has given their defense a major lift.

The experts appear to prefer the Bears over the Lions, because Chicago is a 2.5-point favorite over their ancient rivals even though they will be playing on the road.

The NFC East does not compare with the NFC North in terms of strength. The New York Giants (6-3-1) are the only team above .500 in the division. The Giants have responded very well to John Harbaugh in his first year with the team after his long run with the Baltimore Ravens.

Former Viking J.J. McCarthy has been moved into the QB1 position, and Harbaugh likes his hustle and determination. However, he is far from a finished product. The Giants have a couple of dynamic defensive players in Arvell Reese and Tremaine Edmunds. If the Giants are going to hold on to their division lead and take the NFC's No. 3 seed, the defense will likely play a major role.

The Giants are trying to hold off the Dallas Cowboys (5-6). Dak Prescott and the Cowboys offense is on fire, and they have scored 91 points in the last two games. However, a closing schedule that includes the Seahawks, Rams and Jaguars in consecutive weeks could be problematic.

The NFC South may belong to the Carolina Panthers (5-5) for the second consecutive season. The Panthers are merely an ordinary team, but they have a 2.0-game lead over their three division rivals. The Panthers should have a good chance to hold on to that lead and gain the No. 4 seed in the NFC playoff structure.

The NFC North is the best division in football and it would not be a surprise if all the Wild Card teams came from the division.

In addition to the loser of the Bears-Lions matchup, the Vikings and the Packers can also play formidable football on occasion.

The Vikings (7-3) may have been schooled by the Niners, but they have played sensational defense and their offense should improve as Cousins gets used to the Kevin O'Connell offensive system and he finds a way to get the ball to ace receiver Justin Jefferson.

The Packers (6-4) could end up as the best team in the division. They are getting edge rusher Micah Parsons back in the lineup when they meet the Los Angeles Rams (6-4) on Thanksgiving Eve. Parsons is one of the game's best pass rushers when healthy. If the Packers win that game, they could be in a position to have a great closing burst against a very reasonable schedule.

However, while the Rams got off to an unimpressive start after they were named as Super Bowl favorites by many of the preseason prognosticators, they have won four games in a row and can't be overlooked despite a season-ending injury to WR Puka Nacua.

Neither can the defending Super Bowl champion Seattle Seahawks (6-4). Quarterback Sam Darnold struggled and has been battling a quadriceps injury and a rib issue, and he has been replaced by backup Drew Lock. The Seahawks also have Jaxon Smith-Njigba, and he can make a claim as the best receiver in the NFL. Darnold could return to the lineup shortly.
