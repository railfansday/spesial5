Titans' Jaylen Harrell hospitalized after serious car accident, remains in hospital in Tampa

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

Titans edge defender Jaylen Harrell was involved in a serious car accident Friday night in his hometown of Tampa, Fla., and remained hospitalized, the team announced via a statement.

The Tampa Police Department provided preliminary information on the crash, which happened at around 8:45 a.m. on N. MacDill Ave.

Harrell, a Tampa native, was riding as a passenger in the back seat of a white Kia Sorento rideshare when "he reportedly assaulted the driver while the vehicle was in motion," according to police.

The rideshare driver, a man in his mid-30s, reportedly swerved, striking a second car before crashing into a utility pole. Upon impact, police say Harrell was thrown forward, striking the windshield and losing consciousness.

Harrell, 24, tore his ACL in August and has been on injured reserve ever since, which explains his absence from the team. The Titans visit the Cowboys on Sunday.

A seventh-round pick out of Michigan in 2024, Harrell has spent his entire NFL career with the Titans. He appeared in all 17 games in both 2024 and 2025, contributing on defense and special teams. He had 4.5 sacks last season as well. He is the son of former Lions and Chiefs linebacker James Harrell.
