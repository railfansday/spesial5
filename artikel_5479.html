Homebuilders and their partners team up to salute our veterans

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Builders recognized Veterans Day through nonprofit partnerships, including mortgage-free home giveaways and support for transitioning service members. Regional builders also promoted limited-time military savings, typically $1,000 to $3,000, and some offered up to $2,500 toward closing costs in select communities.  AI Summary

Each Veterans Day, millions of Americans take a moment to honor the sacrifices that our military veterans have made for generations.

On a personal note, today I’m remembering both of my late grandfathers. One served in the Navy, while the other received a Purple Heart and numerous other medals for his bravery and service during the Vietnam War.

Across the country, homebuilders are marking Veterans Day with a range of initiatives, including special promotions and discounts for military families, mortgage-free home donations and workforce development programs designed to support veterans transitioning into civilian careers.

The following are some of the ways homebuilders are recognizing and supporting veterans this year.

PulteGroup, in partnership with Operation Homefront, announced that it gifted multiple newly constructed homes to military service members in cities like Anna, Texas, Wixom, Michigan and Richmond Hills, Georgia.

Meritage Homes, also in partnership with national military nonprofit Operation Homefront, will donate a brand-new, mortgage-free home to a military family in Gulfport, Mississippi.

The lucky recipient of Meritage Homes’ giveaway was Navy Personnel Specialist 2nd Class Maggie Coleman, who served 13 years in the Navy. Coleman will receive a 2,000-square-foot home with four bedrooms and three bathrooms.

“I want to give my children everything I didn’t have. I want this home to be a place where my kids and I can share stories and make memories,” Coleman said in a press release.

Lennar partnered with national nonprofit Operation Finally Home and country music artist and U.S. Army Reserve soldier Craig Morgan to surprise two military families with brand-new, mortgage-free homes in celebration of Veterans Day. The surprises unfolded Monday evening in Austin, Texas, during a reception in which the veterans believed they were simply attending as honored guests of the foundation.

And earlier this week, Invitation Homes announced that it has teamed up with Home Builders Institute to fund hands-on training, industry credentials and job placement for transitioning service members and veterans.

Many smaller, regional homebuilders, which don’t have the resources to give away homes for free, are honoring Veterans Day by offering temporary promotions for Veterans and military service members.

Taylor Homes, which builds in Kentucky, Indiana and Ohio, announced that it doubled its “We Salute You” bonus to $2,000 throughout November, giving military members a price reduction on a new home.

Tennessee-based Pratt Home Builders, meanwhile, is offering veterans, active-duty military and reservists $3,000 in savings toward a new home during November and December. Buyers can apply the savings toward the purchase price, design upgrades or appliances.

Other builders are offering similar incentives in select markets, either temporarily or on an ongoing basis. D.R. Horton, for example, is offering up to $2,500 toward closing costs through its Home of the Brave program in select San Antonio-area communities.

Landmark Homes offers active-duty military members and veterans a $1,000 incentive that can be combined with other promotions in Northern Colorado.

In Idaho, Hubble Homes offers a $2,500 Homes for Heroes discount, as does Drees Homes in markets like Jacksonville and San Antonio.

David Weekley Homes, meanwhile, provides military personnel with $1,000 in Flex Dollars in the Charleston, South Carolina market.

Many of these programs extend beyond veterans and military personnel to include teachers, police officers, firefighters and other first responders, grouping them under broader Hometown Heroes or similar initiatives.
