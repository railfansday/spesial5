Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Among this year's i-Ready Super Stretch School honorees, 55 schools are being recognized for the second consecutive year, demonstrating that strong student growth can be achieved across multiple school years. Eleven of those schools earned repeat recognition in both reading and mathematics, while 44 earned repeat recognition in one subject.

The recognitions are based on the percentage of students who met their i-Ready Stretch GrowthÂ® goals in reading or mathematics. These goals are designed to be ambitious yet attainable, helping students who are below grade level make progress toward proficiency while challenging students already performing on grade level to continue advancing.

Research has shown that meeting Stretch Growth targets for two consecutive school years puts students on a path toward grade-level proficiency, regardless of starting grade-level placement.

"Behind every one of these recognitions are educators who set high expectations for their students and work every day to help them succeed," said Elizabeth Bassford, vice president of content and implementation at Curriculum Associates. "What makes these schools stand out is the intentionality around ambitious goals. Their accomplishments remind us that where a student starts does not curb how they grow."
