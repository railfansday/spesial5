Keyser Represents Dircks Moving & Logistics in 337,897-Square-Foot Phoenix Industrial Expansion

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The transaction brings Dircks' Phoenix footprint to more than 1 million square feet, highlighting continued growth and favorable leasing opportunities in the Southwest Valley.

PHOENIX, Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Keyser, a national tenant-only commercial real estate advisory firm, announced that it has represented Dircks Moving & Logistics in securing a 337,897-square-foot industrial lease at 7210 W. Van Buren Street in Phoenix, Arizona.

The transaction, finalized in October 2026, marks another significant milestone in Dircks' continued expansion, bringing the company's total Phoenix industrial footprint to more than 1 million square feet. The new lease is scheduled to commence in the first quarter of 2027.

Jonathan Keyser, Managing Partner, Ryan Steele, Partner and Industrial Practice Group Leader, and Dimitri Anagnostopoulos, Industrial Commercial Real Estate Advocate, represented Dircks in the transaction.

"Ryan and the team at Keyser were fantastic to work with. As we grow, we seek out industry-leading partners to align ourselves with, and on the commercial real estate side there is no better choice," said Matt Dircks, President and CEO of Dircks Moving & Logistics. "They did an outstanding job helping us throughout the entire process, and we look forward to continuing our partnership in the future."

Southwest Valley Industrial Market Creates Opportunities for Tenants

The transaction comes as Phoenix's Southwest Valley industrial market continues to adjust to an influx of new construction and available inventory. While Arizona's long-term economic fundamentals remain strong, elevated supply has created a more competitive leasing environment, giving tenants increased leverage in lease negotiations.

"Arizona remains exceptionally well-positioned for long-term industrial growth, but the current supply environment is creating meaningful opportunities for tenants," said Steele. "Landlords are competing more aggressively, and companies that approach the market with the right strategy can achieve more favorable lease terms and greater flexibility."

For companies evaluating expansion, relocation, or lease restructuring, Steele believes the current market presents a valuable window to reassess their real estate strategies.

"Dircks' expansion is a great example of the continued confidence businesses have in the Phoenix market," Steele added. "Surpassing 1 million square feet is an impressive milestone and a reflection of their continued success. We're proud to support their growth while helping them capitalize on favorable market conditions, allowing them to better serve their customers."

The transaction reinforces Keyser's commitment to representing the interests of tenants/occupiers exclusively, leveraging market intelligence, strategic planning, and experienced negotiation to help organizations make real estate decisions that support long-term business objectives.

Dircks Moving & Logistics is an Arizona-based moving, storage, and logistics company serving residential, commercial, and corporate clients. The company provides comprehensive relocation and logistics solutions, supporting businesses and individuals through local, national, and international moves.

Keyser is one of the largest independent commercial real estate advisory firms of its kind in the U.S. Through its EXIS Global platform of more than 600 commercial real estate advisors, Keyser provides clients with sophisticated local-market expertise and seamless execution across virtually every major global market. Built around a commitment to excellence, extraordinary selfless service, and ferocious client advocacy, Keyser's model combines the reach and capabilities of a global platform with the responsiveness, accountability, and client-first approach of an independent firm.
