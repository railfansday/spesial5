Ashton Woods Names Nicholas Czar Senior Vice President of Sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ashton Woods has appointed Nicholas Czar senior vice president of sales, putting the home building industry veteran in charge of sales teams across the company’s Ashton Woods and Starlight Homes brands.

Czar brings more than 25 years of new-home sales leadership experience to the role. He will oversee sales strategy, leadership development, and execution across Ashton Woods’ 16 markets, working with local sales leaders throughout the organization.

“Throughout his career with builders of every size, Nick has focused on developing high-performing teams, driving growth, and delivering exceptional customer experience,” Ashton Woods president and CEO Ken Balogh says. “Nick will help us identify new opportunities to succeed in today’s market and position the company for continued growth.”

Czar most recently served as group vice president of builder solutions at New Home Star, where he advised home builders on sales organization design, leadership development, and customer conversion across more than 40 markets.

Earlier in his career, Czar led sales for Maronda Homes’ Pittsburgh, Columbus, and Cincinnati divisions and worked with Garman Builders in the Philadelphia market. His new-home sales experience also includes roles with Pulte Homes, KB Home, and Meritage Homes.

“Through Ashton Woods and Starlight Homes, we have two distinct brands and a tremendous opportunity to serve buyers at different stages throughout the home buying lifecycle,” Czar says. “In this challenging environment, the best prepared sales teams win. My priority is making sure every one of our sales professionals has the training, coaching, and tools to do exactly that.”

Czar will be based in Atlanta. He holds a master’s degree in management from Harvard University and a bachelor’s degree in marketing from Central Michigan University.
