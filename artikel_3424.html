Daniel Radcliffe Officially Admits He May Never Watch HBO's 'Harry Potter' Reboot

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

For an entire generation, Daniel Radcliffe is Harry Potter. The British star spent a decade bringing J.K. Rowling's famous wizard to life across eight movies, growing up alongside Emma Watson and Rupert Grint as the franchise became a global phenomenon. Now, 25 years after the first movie arrived in theaters, HBO is preparing to introduce an entirely new trio of Hogwarts students. But while millions of fans are counting down to the reboot's Christmas premiere, the original Boy Who Lived might not be joining them.

Radcliffe has admitted that he may never watch HBO's upcoming Harry Potter series, despite previously expressing his support for Dominic McLaughlin, who is taking over the title role. Speaking with the Associated Press at the New York premiere of his Netflix project Steps, however, Radcliffe clarified that it was more down to the fact that he didn't have the time to spare, and that, in case you didn't realize, he's already done this. Oh and also, he's missed out on some of the finest television ever made.

I don't know if I will [watch], honestly. I've been in a lot of Harry Potter already… and also I don't watch any TV. I haven't seen Breaking Bad. I haven't seen Mad Men. I haven't seen some of the like the real classics. So, you know, eight hours is a lot of anything to watch.

That's not to say he's not for the new series doing well. Although Radcliffe might not be watching, he has been supportive of McLaughlin, who will lead the new adaptation alongside Arabella Stanton as Hermione Granger and Alastair Stout as Ron Weasley. Radcliffe previously explained that McLaughlin doesn't need advice from him, and he expressed his concern that his own comments could follow the young performer throughout his career. Radcliffe has also warmly praised the youngster and expressed his hopes that their paths will cross soon.

The upcoming series features John Lithgow (The Crown) as Albus Dumbledore, Janet McTeer (Ozark) as Minerva McGonagall, Paapa Essiedu (I May Destroy You) as Severus Snape, and Nick Frost (Shaun of the Dead) as Rubeus Hagrid. The new Harry Potter series premieres December 25, 2026, on HBO and HBO Max.
