3 Best Movies on Prime Video This Week (Nov 9-13)

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The number one movie on Prime Video this week is James Nunn’s 2026 creature feature Hungry. Starring Madison Davenport, the film follows a group of vacationers on a boat trip in Louisiana, where they encounter a deadly hippopotamus. While it’s had somewhat mixed reception from critics, it is a fun movie, but Hungry is hardly the only movie worth checking out on the streaming platform. Here’s a look at three great movies that we think you should watch on Prime Video this week.

A film adaptation of Bret Easton Ellis’s novel directed by Mary Harron and starring Christian Bale, American Psycho is truly a 21st-century classic. The plot follows Bale’s Patrick Bateman, a wealthy New York City investment banker obsessed with his status, who leads a double life as a serial killer, hiding his psychopathic ego from his friends and acquaintances. The film also stars Willem Dafoe, Jared Leto, Justin Theroux, Chloë Sevigny, and Reese Witherspoon in notable roles.

Though initially considered an unfilmable novel, American Psycho has since become an icon of its genre, marked by Bale's disturbingly brilliant performance as the unhinged yuppie. The film can be called a darker mirror to 1987’s Wall Street, which blends psychological horror and black comedy to satirize the 1980s consumerist culture. When it first premiered in 2000, American Psycho received polarized reactions from critics and fans, receiving praise for the writing and acting, but also criticism for its gore and violence, though the latter has become significantly more appealing to audiences in subsequent years.

You do not fall for strangers. You fall for the person you have already been talking to for years, which is the slowest and by some distance the most durable way anybody has found to do this.

You want a partner, not an audience. Somebody who wants their own life as badly as you want yours, and who would never ask you to be smaller to make the relationship easier.

Based on the romance novel of the same name by Melissa Hill, Something from Tiffany’s is a holiday romance comedy directed by Daryl Wein. Starring Zoey Deutch as Rachel, the film sees her life suddenly upended when she mistakenly believes that her boyfriend, Gary, was going to propose to her, unaware that his real gift got switched, setting off a chain of serendipitous events. Kendrick Sampson, Ray Nicholson, and Shay Mitchell star as other key characters.

Something from Tiffany’s is a comforting, charming romcom that warms its way into your heart without needing any big moral or life lesson. The film has generally received a positive reception from critics and fans alike and has been praised for its lighthearted comedy and seasonal story. Something from Tiffany’s might not rank among the best Christmas romance movies of all time, but it surely does make the holiday movie experience cozier with its festive setting and adorable love story.

Actor-comedian Aziz Ansari’s feature directorial debut starring himself, Good Fortune follows Arj, a down-on-his-luck gig worker working for an uber-wealthy man named Jeff, whose life gets swapped with Jeff’s after an angel named Gabriel fails to show Arj the right path. This results in Gabriel losing his wings and living with mortals, while Arj and Jeff’s respective lives begin to unravel, leading to hilarious outcomes for all three of them. Keanu Reeves and Seth Rogen round out the main trio, with Keke Palmer and Sandra Oh as other notable characters.

Good Fortune is a strong directorial debut for Ansari, who brings his brand of humor alongside the phenomenal comedic deliveries of Reeves and Rogen, a combination that makes this film a delightful experience. While sometimes heavy-handed, the timely social satire and the comedic timing of the film are unmissable. The movie premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival and has received generally favorable reviews from critics, earning praise for its quality humor and the comedic timing of its leading cast. The film easily ranks among the best comedies of 2025.
