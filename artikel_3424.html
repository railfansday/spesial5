GTA 6 Just Brought Back the Best E3 Meme of All Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Based on famous battles which actually took place in ancient Florida

After years of waiting, Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here, and it's already astonishing players with its immaculately detailed environments, unexpected celebrity cameos, and absurdly interactive NPCs. Much like Leonida's various radio stations play all-time bangers and deep cuts, GTA 6 is also packed with references to memorable memes and pop-culture moments, and many of them are easy to miss. For instance, the game contains an homage to a hilarious viral moment from E3 2006, and it's not even a full-on side quest but a random discovery you'll stumble upon as you explore GTA 6's intricate open world.

North of the starting area of Key Lento, you'll find what appears to be an abandoned research site. There's a broken fish tank, some small dead crabs, and industrial water tanks scattered around. There's a computer waiting for you to log in, with a conspicuous crab logo above the password field. It contains what appear to be field notes for an ongoing experiment, reminiscent of something you'd find in Resident Evil. Hearing a scuttling noise, Jason turns around as a crab the size of a loveseat flings itself from the rafters, all pincers, hissing, and jangling legs. It pins him to the ground, but Jason manages to break free. YouTuber xGarbett posted a clip of the sequence; the action starts around the 45-second mark.

While the unexpected appearance of a very large crustacean is noteworthy in its own right, "giant enemy crabs" have a special meaning in the world of video games. Back in 2006, during Sony's E3 presentation, one of the developers of Genji 2 explained that the game's stages were based on actual battles that took place in ancient Japan, with hundreds of warriors on either side. Sounds quite gritty and rooted in historical realism, right? Turns out, "based on" is doing some heavy lifting here, because in addition to ordinary men, you'll also be fighting "giant enemy crabs."

There is sadly little in the historical record to suggest that Japan's coastlines were populated by giant crabs at any point in its history, "ancient" or otherwise. Even so, the cringiness of the presentation made it a viral phenomenon on mid-00s YouTube, inspiring several remixes and blog posts. It even inspired a fan-made Flash browser game. In the years since, the "giant enemy crab" has been lovingly referenced in tons of other games, including League of Legends, Vampire Survivors, Borderlands, Viva Pinata, and Astro Bot. Two decades later, the gag's made it all the way to the biggest video game of all time.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is out now for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X.
