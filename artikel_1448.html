GdS: Top-24 quota and direct qualification dream – Milan’s updated Europa League picture

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

AC Milan needed to win against Olympiacos to keep their heads above water in the Europa League, and also to cultivate a dream.

As La Gazzetta dello Sport report, Milan are sadly not in the position that Juventus are in. After four wins from their opening five European games, direct qualification for the round of 16 via a top eight spot is a almost a formality, but the Diavolo had stuttered in their first four.

Milan’s more concrete objective is the minimum threshold of 9-10 points, ideally needed to finish among the top 24 and qualify for the play-offs. Had they not come from behind last night in Greece, even that would have been a distant dream.

After their resounding victory over Omonia, Juve are now within touching distance of the round of 16, while they’ve already exceeded the playoff quota. With 13 points and a near-perfect record, only Bournemouth are above the Bianconeri.

Luciano Spalletti’s team will face Hapoel Beer Sheva, who have only picked up one point so far, and then two higher-level teams: Ferencvaros, another candidate for a direct spot in the top eight, and Real Sociedad, who are struggling a bit more but are an opponent with pedigree.

The victory over Olympiacos in Athens boosts the Rossoneri, who have been far below their potential in the Europa League so far. All is not lost, of course. Milan still have three games to reach the play-offs and reach the highest possible finish, even dreaming of getting into the last-16 directly.

Their next opponents are Sunderland at the San Siro (in the bottom three in the Premier League), an away match against Levski Sofia, and finally Ararat at home (on zero points). If Milan took nine from nine, they can perhaps harbour hopes of sneaking into the top eight.
