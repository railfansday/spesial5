Sporting Lisbon enter race for Endrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Roma isn’t giving up on Endrick for January, despite Mourinho publicly closing in on the player.

The 2006-born player, as he told the Brazilian Federation, wants to play more and will do everything he can to advance his career.

An opening for Gasperini? We’ll see what happens in the coming weeks, while the Giallorossi management is working to bring the Real Madrid striker to Trigoria.

As La Gazzetta dello Sport reports, contacts between Roma and Endrick’s entourage are continuing.

Contacts between the Giallorossi and the Brazilian striker have resumed, and after the Champions League match between the Roman club and Real Madrid, a meeting was held in Trigoria.

D’Amico’s goal is to sign the 2006-born player on a loan with an option to buy and a possible buy-back option from the Spanish club.

However, Roma isn’t the only club interested in Endrick, as they must be wary of the interest of Sporting Lisbon, where Paulo Fonseca recently arrived.

The former Roma coach already coached the Real Madrid striker in Lyon, which is why the player is seriously considering the Portuguese option. The Roman club must accelerate to secure the 2006-born player.
