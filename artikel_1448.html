PSG rumor causes big headache at Benfica for Gianluca Prestianni

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

He will most likely be Benfica’s next big sale. Since the start of the season, Gianluca Prestianni has had a string of very high-level performances, to the point of being called up by Lionel Scaloni during this international break. With Nico Paz, Thiago Almada and Franco Mastuantuono, the 20-year-old winger is part of this generation which must embody the post-Messi era in Argentina. He has really taken off in recent months, as evidenced by his 12 goals and 6 assists in 20 matches in all competitions with his club.

His performances have not escaped the attention of various European recruiters. We learned yesterday in the Portuguese press that the 20-year-old player had entered the sights of PSG and Bayern Munich in particular. We will have to be able to give up €100 million, the sum that the Águias will demand in the months to come. His sale should not take place before the summer transfer window but his profile as a right-handed winger who can play on the left recalls the qualities of a certain Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, injured until the end of the season.

We’re not there yet. The Vinicius affair seems far away now. Under contract until 2029, Prestianni has been negotiating for several weeks now with his management to extend. The deal was on track, reports In Bolaonly the rumors about the player’s future have reshuffled the cards a little. The media even speaks of a “renewal process stalled.» However, there are points of agreement on most of the conditions, such as salary, different bonuses, and the duration of the new contract. The problem mainly concerns the signing bonus.

Benfica would like to pay this sum at the end of the season, where the Argentinian demands it upon his signing. His argument is to help his family and protect them from want as quickly as possible. For accounting and writing reasons, the Lisbon club is slowing down and prefers to postpone this payment until later. The management does not seem any more worried than that but an unexpected obstacle must be overcome. This situation does not completely reassure Marco Silva either. It promotes a risk of departure more quickly than expected.
