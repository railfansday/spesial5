Are stores open on Veterans Day 2026? Holiday hours for banks, stock markets, USPS, Walmart, Costco, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Since America’s creation 250 years ago, countless brave individuals have been willing to fight for it. Every November 11, the United States honors its veterans.

This year, the observance falls on a Wednesday, which is a little confusing. Here’s the rundown of why many get a day off in the middle of the week and what you can expect to be opened and closed for it.

Why isn’t Veterans Day always on a Monday?

Veterans Day is always on November 11 because of its links to World War I. The “Great War” was supposed to be the war to end all wars. If only that was the case.

On November 11, 1918, Germany and the Allied forces agreed to an armistice and stopped fighting. This agreement began on the eleventh hour of the eleventh day of the eleventh month. The Treaty of Versailles made the end of the war official about 14 months later.

The Uniform Monday Holiday Act of 1968 moved most holidays to Mondays to give workers a three-day weekend.

When it went into effect in 1971, Veterans Day was officially part of the shift. Backlash from veterans groups caused the holiday to be moved back to its historical roots.

Will the post office be open on Veterans Day? Will mail be delivered?

No and no. The United States Postal Service (USPS) will be closed and no regular mail will be delivered on Veterans Day because it is a federal holiday.

Are FedEx and UPS open on Veterans Day?

Physical FedEx locations will be open. However, deliveries and pick-ups will have some modifications. Early on-call pickups and drop box pickups may be impacted in some regions.

UPS is open for business as usual.

Are banks open on Veterans Day?

No, most banks will be closed. This includes major chains like Chase Bank, Bank of America, and Wells Fargo. Online banking and outdoor ATMs will be available for immediate needs.

Is the stock market open on Veterans Day?

Yes, both the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) and the Nasdaq will be open on Veterans Day.

However, the U.S. bond market follows the Securities Industry and Financial Markets Association (SIFMA) calendar and will be closed.

Are schools open on Veterans Day?

It is best to check in with your child’s school to see if it will be open or closed on Veterans Day. Schools are run at the local level, so it is location dependent in many cases. Private institutions also have greater freedom in scheduling.

Are pharmacies open on Veterans Day?

Yes. Most major pharmacies are open on Veterans Day, including major chains like CVS and Walgreens.

It is always a good practice to double check before heading out just in case so you don’t get an even bigger headache.

Are retail stores, groceries, and restaurants open on Veterans Day?

The majority of retail stores and restaurants will remain open on Veterans Day. Big-box chains like Walmart and Target will be open. Membership-based retailers like Costco are also open on Veterans Day.

Grocery store chains like Kroger and Whole Foods are also open on Veterans Day.

What about Veterans Day freebies and deals?

Some restaurants are offering freebies and discounts for those who have served. Applebee’s is offering vets who dine in a free entree with seven options to choose from and a $5 coupon for future visits.

Vets and active military personnel with a sweet tooth can head to BJ’s Restaurant and Brewhouse for a free Chocolate Chunk Pizookie. As an added bonus, the establishment will also present these brave souls with a free appetizer coupon.

Participating Farmer Boys locations are offering vets and active military personnel a free Big Cheese Burger with proof of service.
