The homepage has some rough edges on my older iPhone running 18.7.1.

For instance, there’s no spacing between the head and the ad right after.

The popular this month has a bit of a weird aspect ratio, making it look stretched compared to the cards. The title card is also slightly cut off at the top. I feel those cards just take up too much space as well, and the scroll-snap makes it tedious to go through them. I would also make the hover conditional via @media (pointer: fine) and (hover:hover) so they don’t jump around when dragging.

I also prefer when sites just take up the available width on mobile. Now you have the cream edge, then a container, again some padding and then the contents of the page. It would be simpler to just do away with one of them and give you some 20/30 pixels extra space for the content.
