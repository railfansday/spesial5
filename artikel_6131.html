Major AI platforms have a trust issue when it comes to travel bookings. Would cruisers trust an AI agent to book the most complex travel product there is? Still Nuitée, Boundless Labs, and AI agents could eventually pressure the incumbents in cruise distribution.

Nuitée is adding cruises to its travel distribution API through a partnership with business-to-business cruise booking platform Boundless Labs, a move that could eventually put cruise bookings within reach of the AI assistants its partners power. The companies will announce the deal Monday.

Companies that already use Nuitée Connect to sell hotels, flights or experiences will be able to search and book more than 30,000 sailings from 22 cruise lines through the same API. The cruise product will be available to partners starting in December.

Nuitée is adding cruise inventory to its travel distribution API through a partnership with Boundless Labs, the B2B cruise booking platform behind Cruisebound, with the product available to partners starting in December. Companies already using Nuitée Connect for hotels, flights, or experiences will be able to search and book more than 30,000 sailings from 22 cruise lines through the same API, with travel advisors and call centers getting the same inventory including cabin holds and deposit schedules. Through Nuitée Connect's Model Context Protocol integration, AI assistants will be able to search sailings, compare cabins, and book on a traveler's behalf, the companies said. Nuitée's partner list includes Marriott, Hilton, Accor, Duffel, Selfbook, Odessia, Revolut, and Grab — several of which power AI travel assistants — but none has publicly committed to selling cruises through Nuitée. Cruise distribution remains dominated by large sellers such as World Travel Holdings, Costco Travel, Expedia Cruises, Vacations to Go, Avoya, and AAA.
