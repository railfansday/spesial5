Beyond Miles: Earning Loyalty Through Proactive Care

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Katie Smigelski, General Manager, Travel Products, Delta Air LinesEmily Hartman, General Manager, North America, Allianz Partners

Moderated by Carolyn Kremins, President, Skift

Airlines can expand the value of loyalty currencies by allowing travelers to redeem rewards for services beyond flights and upgrades. Delta’s introduction of SkyMiles redemptions for trip protection offers an example of how broader redemption options can support ancillary revenue and deeper customer engagement.
