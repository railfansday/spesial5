Loyalty Unscripted: Relationship or Commodity?

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Tiffany Funk, Head of Flying Blue, Air France-KLMGilbert Ott, Director of Loyalty, oneworld

Moderated by Brian Sumers, Editor, The Airline Observer

Airline loyalty programs must make rewards easier to use while protecting the commercial value of the seats customers redeem. Air France-KLM’s Tiffany Funk and oneworld’s Gilbert Ott said closer coordination with revenue management, more flexible partner pricing, and clearer reward offers could help airlines balance customer expectations with sustainable program economics.
