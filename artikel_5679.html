Six new ways to simplify solar installs: Top Rack Technology brings mounting innovation to RE+ 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

By Marie DeFreitas, Contributor | November 18, 2026

From smarter waterproofing to 360-degree rail adjustment, Top Rack Technology is putting installer efficiency, rooftop flexibility, and cost control at the center of its latest product lineup.

As the solar industry continues to look for ways to reduce installation costs and streamline residential projects, mounting hardware is getting a fresh dose of innovation. Top Rack Technology, Inc. (TRT) is making that case at RE+ 2026, where the solar racking manufacturer is showcasing six new mounting solutions designed to simplify rooftop installations, improve flexibility, and help installers work more efficiently.

Its latest lineup reflects a practical approach to product development: addressing everyday installation challenges while keeping system performance, aesthetics, and affordability in focus.

Six Products, One Common Goal: Better Installation Efficiency

Among the new offerings is TRT’s Full-Coverage Skirt System, which targets an important consideration in residential solar: appearance. By concealing exposed array edges, the system delivers a cleaner, more finished look that can help installations better complement residential properties.

The new TRT-G2 Bracket takes aim at installation simplicity and competitive pricing. Designed around practical field requirements, the bracket can be paired with TRT’s ButyLok waterproofing component, creating an integrated attachment and roof-sealing solution.

Waterproofing is particularly important in rooftop solar, where roof penetrations are still a critical consideration for installers and property owners alike. ButyLok introduces a shoe-like component containing pre-applied, gel-like butyl material. When compressed by the G2 bracket, the material spreads across the intended sealing surface, potentially reducing manual sealant application and simplifying the installation process.

TRT is also introducing SAVRIX, another residential attachment option developed with manufacturing efficiency, installation practicality, and cost competitiveness top of mind.. Meanwhile, the RF-2 Rail Fastener is designed to simplify rail-to-bracket connections.

Top Rack Technology’s FreePivot Bracket features a 360-degree rotatable rail fastener and built-in height adjustment to accommodate varying rooftop conditions and simplify solar panel installations. Credit: Top Rack Technology

But perhaps the most versatile addition is the FreePivot Bracket. Its 360-degree rotatable rail fastener allows installers to accommodate attachment points that aren’t perfectly aligned. Built-in height adjustment helps compensate for uneven roof surfaces, while its all-metal construction supports durability. The one-orientation design and single-socket installation concept are also intended to reduce tool changes and keep crews moving.

Beyond the six new products, TRT is showcasing its established portfolio, including the TRT 01 Rail-less Base System, TRT 02 Rail-Base System, and TRT 03 Tile Hook System.

The company is also highlighting its Online BOM Design Tool, which supports project setup, structural design inputs, module layouts, engineering verification, and automated bill-of-materials generation. Connecting digital planning with physical mounting solutions could offer installers and distributors a more streamlined path from project design to field execution.

Installation hardware doesn’t need to be flashy to make a difference. Improvements in attachment flexibility, waterproofing, assembly time, and aesthetics can translate into meaningful operational advantages.
