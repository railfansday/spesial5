Netflix’s ‘The Body’ Sparks 126K-Strong Petition To Remove “Blasphemous Anti-Christian” Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

As Netflix‘s The Body surpasses 5M views in its first week, a petition to remove the show from the platform is also growing in numbers.

Criticizing the “blasphemous anti-Christian series” from creator Quinn Shephard ahead of its release last week, the CitizenGo petition has accumulated more than 126K signatures calling on Netflix to “stop defiling the faith of millions of Christian families and cancel The Body before it reaches our living rooms.” Deadline has reached out to Netflix.

“Once the show reaches your living room and your teenager’s phone, the damage is done. Nobody can take it back,” reads the petition addressed to Netflix’s Chief Content Officer Peter Rice.

It seems the alleged damage might already have been inflicted, with Netflix reporting that the coming-of-age horror series received 5.1M views in its first five days, landing at No. 3 on the streamer’s Global Top 10 English TV list, in addition to ranking in the Top 10 in 80 countries. Watch on Deadline

The petition takes issue with the depiction of “Christian schoolgirls, a dark initiation ritual, the Devil and ‘prophetic visions'” in the show’s trailer.

“This is a pattern. First Netflix mocked Jesus Christ Himself in The First Temptation of Christ. Then it sexualized eleven-year-old girls in Cuties. Now it has come for Christian schoolgirls,” it reads. “Each time, Netflix pushes one step further into the family home.

Shephard, whose grandmother was an Evangelical minister, previously told Deadline, “We weren’t trying to make a show that was hyper-critical of religion. Obviously, religion is a theme in the show, and it touches on it in a lot of ways.”

In The Body, all eight episodes now available to stream on Netflix, a group of badly-behaved Catholic school girls begin having prophetic visions that set off mass hysteria in town after a dance-team initiation goes wrong on Halloween night in 2004.
