Mike Flanagan’s Carrie Season 2 Hits Major Writing Milestone As 3-Season Plan Begins

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Mike Flanagan's Carrie season 2 is coming together well.

Flanagan dove back into the world of Stephen King for the fourth adaptation of the classic 1974 novel, starring Sumer H. Howell as the eponymous teenage outcast who grapples with the emotional rollercoaster of high school, all while developing telekinetic powers. Scoring largely positive reviews from critics and solid ratings on Prime Video, Carrie season 2 was officially confirmed to be in the works in late October 2026, just two weeks after its premiere.

Now, during a recent interview with The Hollywood Reporter, Flanagan offered an update on Carrie season 2's development. The creator/showrunner confirmed that he is "back to work" on the next chapter of the Stephen King adaptation, revealing that the "writers room is firing on all cylinders" and that he has already hit a major writing milestone in having "turned in the first episode script" the morning of the interview.

Flanagan went on to share that he and his team had been "working on it unofficially for a while" in the hopes of Carrie season 2 getting the greenlight from Prime Video and wanting to "be prepared in case it did." The creator further expressed that the hope is to "go into production next year" if the writing remains on schedule, though didn't specify what the timeline would look like for its production:

Mike Flanagan: The beautiful thing is that Carrie’s journey has always seemed to me to have a clear trajectory. And that was something, even though we were never taking for granted that it would happen, it was something that was always in the back of my mind. I know it was in conversations that I had with Summer about where she could go and with Steve too — King was very aware and excited about the potential for that. It’s always been in the room with us, but we didn’t really have permission to fully explore it until it was real because you don’t want to jinx it. But we are fully immersed in it.

When asked about his previous comments about a three-season plan for Carrie, Flanagan confirmed the plan is still in place for the series, seeing a "very clean arc" going through seasons 2 and 3. However, he does confess that there is "danger in the temptation to do more," and confesses it's "very presumptuous" he'll get a third season, and therefore the writers room is still approaching it as "we only know we're doing [season] 2."

In this new series adaptation of Stephen King's classic novel, Carrie White struggles through the daily torments of school life, her domineering mother, and a dormant  set of telekinetic powers.
