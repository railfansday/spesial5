These Are the Best Early Black Friday Deals on Samsung Devices

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Black Friday might be a couple of weeks away, but Samsung is rolling out the discounts early, with deals on its entire lineup of devices, including smartphones, tablets, laptops, and wearables. While not all of these early Black Friday offers are true discounts, you can find ways to save money on some devices through these promotions. Here's what Samsung's offering ahead of the official Black Friday discounts.

Samsung is offering some decent discounts on both its flagship smartphones and its midrange and budget lines. However, as part of its early Black Friday sale, the company is also offering increased trade-in credits towards these devices. While all of the devices here have trade-in credit options, only some have optional discounts for early Black Friday. Here's what you can expect:

Galaxy S26 FE: Up to $400 trade-in credit ($100 off without trade-in)

Galaxy S26: Up to $340 trade-in credit ($150 off without trade-in)

Galaxy S26+: Up to $440 trade-in credit ($200 off without trade-in)

Galaxy S26 Ultra: Up to $750 trade-in credit ($250 off without trade-in); Up to $150 off add-ons, 10% off Buds 4 or Buds 4 Pro

Galaxy Z Fold 8: Up to $800 trade-in credit

Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra: Up to $800 trade-in credit

Galaxy Z Flip 8: Up to $400 trade-in credit

Galaxy A27 5G: Up to $120 trade-in credit ($100 off without trade-in)

Galaxy A37 5G: Up to $205 trade-in credit

Galaxy A57 5G: Up to $205 trade-in credit

Samsung's wearable deals are much like its smartphone deals: mostly trade-in offers, but some traditional discounts if you don't have a trade-in as well:

Galaxy Watch Ultra 2: Up to $250 trade-in credit ($100 off without trade-in)

Galaxy Watch 9: Up to $250 trade-in credit ($50 off without trade-in)

Galaxy Watch 8 Classic: Up to $150 trade-in credit

Galaxy Watch 8: Up to $150 trade-in credit

Galaxy Watch 7: Up to $100 trade-in credit

Galaxy Ring: Up to $150 trade-in credit (out of stock as of this article)

Galaxy XR: Up to $1,030 off (plus three months of YouTube TV at $1 per month)

For some of its tablets this early Black Friday, Samsung is advertising that it will take off a certain amount in "total savings." That's a wiggily way of saying that to get the best deal, you'll need to stack together multiple offers at once: certain trade-in values, per-device discounts, as well as discounts from the Samsung Offer Program.

Galaxy Tab A11+ Kids Edition: Up to $75 trade-in credit

In short, yes, Black Friday still offers discounts that can be rare throughout the rest of the year. If there’s something you want to buy, or you’re shopping for gifts, it’s a good time to look for discounts on what you need, especially tech sales, home improvement supplies, and fitness tech.
