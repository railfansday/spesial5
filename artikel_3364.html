Carrie-Anne Moss Joins Halsey In Psycho-Sexual Horror ‘Replacer’

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

EXCLUSIVE: Carrie-Anne Moss (The Matrix) has joined the cast of Halsey and Avan Jogia’s psycho-sexual horror Replacer.

The Montreal-set horror stars Halsey as troubled DJ Proxy, who meets an alluring artist and his group of friends who run an underground radio station.

The station’s broadcast becomes warped by a mysterious signal hidden deep beneath the city’s subway system. Proxy must escape before the sound turns her, and her new friends, into something raw, primal, and unrecognizable.

The picture, written by Halsey and directed by Jogia, recently wrapped production in Montreal. Producer and composer Boys Noize (Alex Ridha) will score the film. It also stars Karl Glusman, Juliette Gariépy, with additional cast including Antoine Olivier Pilon, Landon Liboiron, Nhi Do, Gabriel Carter, Audrey Roger, Estelle Fournier, Joephillip Lafortune, and Martin Debreuil.

Moss found globl fame internationally for her defining performance as Trinity in The Matrix franchise, and has appeared more recently as Jedi Master Indara in Lucasfilm’s Star Wars: The Acolyte and 2024 post-apocalyptic thriller Die Alone.

She has also forged a career in TV, recently appearing in the second season of Netflix show FUBAR alongside Arnold Schwarzenegger, and can currently be seen leading the mystery thriller Yaga, alongside Noah Reid, Clark Backo, and Hudson Williams, which premiered in October 2026 on AMC+ in the U.S.

Her role in Replacer reunites her vicariously with The Matrix writer and director Lilly Wachowski who is an executive producer on the film.

Replacer is produced by Kyle Mann of Independent Edge, Isabelle Deluce of Soft Focus Films, and Olivier Picard and David Pierrat of Parce Que Films, with Damiano Tucci, in association with Doom and Gloom Inc, Anarchists United, Monarch Kaleidoscope, Aska Ventures, and Cray LA.

Further executive producers include Colin Kelty, Ronnie Rouge, Anthony Li, Lawrence Mattis, Sarah Flores, Emily McCann Lesser, Keshi and Aaron Dadoo, Clay Epstein, Christopher Renteria, Nicolas Jacques-Bouchard, Antoine Chaume, Mathio Pellerin, Hugues Vaillancourt, Daniel Dubuc, Catherine Duguay-Blackburn and Pierre Philippe Côte.

UTA Independent Film Group and CAA Media Finance represent North American rights.

Carrie-Anne Moss is represented by The Burstein Company, Verve, and Kleinberg Lange Cuddy & Carlo. Halsey is represented by UTA and Anthony Li. Jogia is represented by CAA, Berwick & Kovacik and The Characters Talent Agency. Glusman is repped by Paradigm, Ilene Feldman Management, and Ziffren Brittenham. Juliette Gariépy is repped by Agence La Suite, alongside Adéquat and Curate.
