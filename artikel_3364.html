Netflix Officially Confirms ‘The Body’ Star’s Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Netflix has delivered yet another viral smash hit, and two weeks into its release, it remains the talk of town. Data shows that its new psychological horror thriller, The Body, is the number 1 show with the most online engagement across multiple platforms, surpassing Prime Video's The Lord of the Rings: Rings of Power and Disney+ VisionQuest combined. Now, Netflix has begun to capitalize on that popularity.

It might be tempting to dismiss The Body's success due to its religious backlash, but that would be unfair, and perhaps even insulting to the efforts of its brilliant cast and crew. The show, which follows a Catholic school dance team whose Halloween night hazing ritual takes a dangerous turn, features a predominantly young cast, which also happens to be the best part of the show. As Collider's Gabrielle Moss describes in her glowing review, these young talents are "the show's best asset" and, needless to say, they're ones to watch. Everyone brings their A-game in what is arguably one of the best performances from a young cast this year.

Among the main cast is Sara Boustany, who plays the fan-favorite Lions co-captain Elise Zakaria in her first major starring role. New reports now reveal that Boustany is set to make her Netflix return in a new indie drama that has already wrapped filming. The movie titled Between Us and the River is a coming-of-age drama that is produced by The Body creator, Quinn Shepherd. Nika Fehmiu, directs for her feature directorial debut. Per the synopsis, the movie is "set in 2005 against the backdrop of the Iraq War, and follows a first-generation Iraqi American college student and aspiring dancer who forms a relationship with a young Mexican American classmate, only to make a shattering discovery that forces them to confront grief, identity and the devastating ways war has shaped their lives."

Four Netflix characters, four completely different ways of showing up for somebody. This is the one who fits how you actually do friendship, and what having them in your life would be like.

You do not do friendship at half volume, and neither does he. What you want is somebody who says yes to the plan before you have finished describing the plan.

You are the friend who makes things happen, and so is she. Together you would be mildly exhausting to everyone around you and completely delighted with yourselves.

You have no patience for pleasant lies, and she has no ability to tell them. What looks like coldness from the outside is the most reliable friendship on this list.

You look after whoever is having the worst day. So does he. It is the least strategic way to move through the world and the best possible quality in a friend.

The Body fans are waiting with bated breath for Netflix to make the Season 2 announcement, with some even optimistic about a dual renewal. While Netflix is yet to make it official, it's almost now a matter of when rather than if, given the show's updated performance stats. The Body drew just over 5 million views within mere days of its release and, in its second week, saw an 86% jump in viewership, bringing its two-week viewership to about 15 million, which now pretty much falls within the safety net category for Netflix renewals.

Netflix's Between Us and the River will see Boustany star opposite Alejandro Akara (Landman). Also starring is Amélie Hoeferle, the breakout star of Prime Video's hit coming-of-age drama series Sterling Point. Others are Sarah Himadeh (The Last of Us), Laughton Royce (Clean Hands), and Sean Luc Rogers.

Between Us and the River doesn't have a release date yet. Stay tuned for future updates.
