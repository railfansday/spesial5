OG Katniss, Peeta & Haymitch's New Hunger Games Cameos Clarified By Sunrise On The Reaping Director

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

EXCLUSIVE: The Hunger Games' latest cameos have been addressed.

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping director Francis Lawrence has unpacked the reasoning behind Katniss, Peeta, and an older Haymitch making cameo appearances in the latest movie. Based on Suzanne Collins' book of the same name, Sunrise on the Reaping's story follows Haymitch (Joseph Zada) competing in the 50th Hunger Games and Second Quarter Quell, which sees the number of participants doubled. The movie also features cameo appearances from Jennifer Lawrence as Katniss Everdeen, Josh Hutcherton as Peeta Mellark, and Woody Harrelson as an older Haymitch.

In an interview with ScreenRant's Ben Gibbons about the new prequel film, Lawrence unpacked the importance of featuring Katniss, Peeta, and an older Haymitch in Sunrise on the Reaping. The director explained how, when finding out the trio would appear in the epilogue of Collins' book, it made sense to try and get everyone back one last time. Since the book and adaptation were under wraps for a while, it was quite some time before the trio were asked to come back to the big screen together.

Check out what Lawrence had to say below:

Francis Lawrence: Well, it was great to have them back. I knew that they were present in the epilogue of the book, and so it was always a goal of ours to get them all to come back.

When we started working on the screenplay, when we first started working and actually working with Suzanne [Collins] and adapting, the book hadn't even been announced, so we had to keep everything really quiet. We weren't even really allowed to mention that we were working on Hunger Games, anything. So we couldn't tell the cast or anybody for a really long time, and so we waited for a really long time to call Jen [Lawrence] and Josh [Hutcherson] and and Woody [Harrelson] to tell them about the book. I mean, Woody certainly found out that the story was about him very very early on with the announcement of the book, and so he knew about that, but nobody knew about their involvement for a very long time.

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is the sixth movie in the Hunger Games franchise and the second prequel. Following the success of The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes in 2023, Collins' prequel novel was announced in June 2024. The book was released on March 18, 2025, while the movie adaptation hits theaters on November 20, 2026. In the novel, an epilogue features Katniss, Peeta, and Haymitch after the events of Mockingjay, living in relative peace after everything they'd been through.

The trio's cameo appearance is a small reference to the original four movies, while also staying faithful to Collins' book in the process. For the most part, Sunrise on the Reaping sets its sights on new Hunger Games characters that are participants in the 50th Games. As viewers know from previous movies, Haymitch was the only survivor of the Games, though acted against the Capitol during them in hopes of rebelling and putting an end to them once and for all.

This, of course, didn't happen, with Katniss and Peeta being the ones to ultimatel usher in an end to the Games. However, Sunrise on the Reaping shows how Haymitch's personal rebellion began, and how what he went through made him the man viewers meet at the start of the first Hunger Games movie. So far, this has paid off, with the prequel movie scoring a Certified Fresh 71% on Rotten Tomatoes based on 109 reviews.

Cameos from Katniss, Peeta, and an older Haymitch act as strong bookends to their stories as well. By showcasing what their lives are like at the very end of The Hunger Games timeline, Sunrise on the Reaping makes itself a vital part of the franchise's larger narrative. The direct ties to Haymitch's story makes the movie's cameos all the more vital, adding an important synergy between the movie and the book it's based on.
