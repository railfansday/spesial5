Novaya Gazeta, Russia’s Bravest Newspaper, Closes

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

For more than 30 years, the paper covered an increasingly repressive Russia. “The process of silencing us started many years ago,” one of its journalists said.

Outside a five-story office building on Potapovsky Lane in Moscow, a group of journalists took down a metal plaque and installed a plastic one in its place: Novaya Gazeta Lived Here. White carnations, the flower that Russians associate with mourning, were placed on top of it.

Novaya Gazeta, Russia’s most daring independent newspaper for decades, closed its doors on Monday, the latest blow to Russians who value freedom of expression and human rights.

Its forced closure is emblematic of the Kremlin’s erasure of opinions, ideas and people that do not conform to its views. That process has been underway for years, but it has dramatically accelerated since Russia launched its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022.

Novaya Gazeta’s journalists were packing up their offices when I visited on Monday. Its co-founder Dmitri A. Muratov, who shared the Nobel Peace Prize in 2021 for defending press freedom, was giving away personal items to colleagues and well-wishers.

He handed his emerald banker’s lamp to one of the paper’s last recruits, a video journalist. He kept for himself a fire evacuation floor plan, on which he asked staff members to sign their names where their offices had been.
