French Mayors Conference: 2027 Elysée hopeful Lisnard reelected association president

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Mayor of Cannes, David Lisnard, has been re-elected president of the Association of Mayors of France, securing a comfortable victory in a vote announced on Wednesday. Lisnard is also a candidate in next year's French presidential election. For more on what his re-election could mean, FRANCE 24's James André reports from the Association of Mayors' convention in Paris.
