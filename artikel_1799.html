Lukaku bows out of international football

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Belgium's record goalscorer Romelu Lukaku has announced his retirement from international football.

Lukaku made his debut for Belgium in 2010, and went on to score 94 times in 135 appearances for the Red Devils.

He bows out as Belgium's third-most-capped player, behind Axel Witsel (140 caps) and Jan Vertonghen (157).

Lukaku was an unused substitute as Belgium hammered Italy 4-1 in the Nations League on Sunday to secure their place in the quarter-finals.

It means the 33-year-old's last appearance for his country came as he captained them in a 4-1 loss to France last month.

"These 16 years I gave all I had for the national team. I went through every emotion possible," Lukaku posted on Instagram.

"I played with amazing team-mates that I will always be grateful for. I want to thank them all. I want to thank all the coaches as well for pushing and trusting me to help the team. It was a nice ride, and I enjoyed every single moment.

"To the Belgium fans, thank you for the support you gave our team. I am proud to have represented my country in four World Cups and three European Championships.

"I will never forget those moments. I'm at peace with my decision to stop here. It was amazing. It was enjoyable. I'm grateful. Thank you."

Only four men have scored more international goals than Lukaku: Cristiano Ronaldo (146), Lionel Messi (126), Ali Daei (108) and Sunil Chhetri (95).

The Royal Belgian Football Association said of Lukaku: "Through his personality, leadership, dedication and strong connection to the national team, he has earned a special place in the history of the Belgian Red Devils.

"His journey, from making his debut at just 16 years old to competing on the biggest international stages, remains a tremendous source of inspiration for countless young footballers and children who dream of one day wearing the Belgium shirt themselves."
