Footy Headlines: Puma to deliver ‘pure and traditional’ final home shirt for Milan in 2027-28

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The recent talk about AC Milan and kits has involved Adidas and a report has taken us back a year to Puma’s final shirt.

Next year, Puma will release their final home shirt for Milan, ending a 10-year stint as the club’s technical partner. It’s been an up-and-down period for the Rossoneri, but their return to Adidas will hopefully help to bring around more consistent, better times.

In a recent update, Footy Headlines have provided ‘practically a final look’ at next season’s kit, which will mark the end of the Puma era. Provided to them by kit insider JMFK, the new shirt is expected to feature a polo collar and the classic Rossoneri stripes, as you’d expect.

The German brand are expected to embrace a ‘pure, traditional approach’ across the whole set, rather than being experimental with wild graphics. There’ll be no gradients, patterns or overcomplicated gimmicks, so it should be simple red and black stripes.

With thinner stripes than the current home shirt – modelled on the 1998-99 Scudetto shirt – it’ll also feature a collar for the first time since the 2018-19 season.

With this set to be the last home shirt before the return to Adidas, it certainly does Puma a lot of credit and it will hopefully round off the 10-year stint in style.
