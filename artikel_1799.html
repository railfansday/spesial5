England host Croatia at Wembley tonight, a month on from thumping Slaven Bilic’s side 7-0 in a dominant performance in Rijeka.

Thomas Tuchel’s side were in seventh heaven during the reverse fixture, dishing out the heaviest defeat in Croatian football history to help their chances of advancing to the quarter-finals of the Nations League.

Heading into Thursday’s game, England have three wins from four fixtures, placing them second in Group A3.

England will get the chance to enact revenge on Spain - the only side to beat them in the Nations League this year - in their final game, but Tuchel’s team will likely need three points against Croatia to have any hope of leapfrogging the Spaniards and finishing top of the pile.

Tuchel’s initial squad announcement has been impacted by injury, with defensive duo Marc Guehi and Ezri Konsa replaced by James Hill and Dan Burn.

Bournemouth defender Hill is one of three outfielders that could potentially make their international debut against Croatia - midfielders Josh King and Jobe Bellingham other candidates for a first appearance with the Three Lions on their chest.

Harry Kane, meanwhile, is set for a record-breaking night as it has been confirmed he will start on the occasion of his 126th cap for the senior national team.
