Rochdale next manager: Ex-Sheffield Wednesday, York City and Doncaster Rovers men emerge as early favourites

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The early favourites to land the Rochdale job - including ex-Sheffield Wednesday, York City and Doncaster Rovers men

Figures familiar with fans of Sheffield Wednesday, York City and Doncaster Rovers are among the early favourites for the Rochdale job.

Jimmy McNulty led the Dale to promotion via the National League play-offs last season, only to then depart for League One outfit Stockport County.

He was replaced at the helm by Ian Watson, who has overseen a tough start to life back in the fourth tier. With the club sat 22nd in the table after a 3-2 defeat to Northampton Town, Watson has left his role as head coach.

A club spokesperson said: “Rochdale AFC can confirm that it has parted company with head coach Ian Watson.

“We would like to thank Ian for his hard work and commitment during his time at the Crown Oil Arena and wish him every success in the future.

“The process to appoint a successor is already underway, and the club will give a further update in due course.”
