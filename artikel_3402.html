’Pride and Prejudice’s 10-Part Spin-Off Is the Perfect Binge Before Its Christmas Special

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Taylor Vollman is a contributor at Collider. She has long quipped, “T.V. is my name, TV is my game.” Fortuitous initials aside, at the heart of a tired joke is a simple truth — she has a lifelong passion for television. And along with it, an affinity for movies, music and pop culture.

In a childhood time capsule, she had listed Friends, PB&J Otter, and Days of Our Lives among her favorite shows. But her tastes have not narrowed with age. Her viewing habits span TV Land classics, network hits, buzzy reality shows and streamer critical darlings.

Hoping to merge this passion with her love of writing, Taylor earned a bachelor’s in media arts and design with a minor in film studies from James Madison University. After working in marketing for years, she wanted to sharpen her skills and went on to graduate with a master’s in journalism from Georgetown University. Taylor has since pursued entertainment writing and reporting.

Jane Austen is a preeminent romance author, with an impressive catalog of novels that are still relevant centuries later. Pride and Prejudice was her second novel published in 1813 and continues to be her most successful, repeatedly adapted for stage, screen, and page. It documents the at-times fraught romance of Elizabeth Bennett and Mr. Darcy, who must overcome misconceptions and differing stations in life to find happiness. But BritBox’s breakout hit of 2026 shifted its focus, instead turning to Elizabeth’s overlooked middle sister, Mary.

The Other Bennet Sister is based on Janice Hadlow’s 2020 novel of the same name, adapted for screen by Sarah Quintrell. Ella Bruccoleri stars as the socially awkward, but endearing titular character as she ventures to define herself separately from her critical family. With just 10, roughly half-hour-long episodes, it is the perfect weekend binge ahead of the upcoming release of its Christmas special.

As the last unmarried sibling, The Other Bennet Sister follows Mary Bennet as she sets out on a journey of self-discovery and romance. She is the bespectacled middle sister of Pride and Prejudice’s Elizabeth Bennet, often finding herself as the odd-woman out in their sibling set of five. Lacking the same wit, beauty, and coordination as her siblings, her negative self-image is reinforced by the highly critical Mrs. Bennet (Ruth Jones). Not only does she vocalize her worry that Mary could hinder proposals for her other daughters, but she actively thwarts perfectly serviceable matches. Mary explains her central issue in the opening lines of the series, which mimic the opening lines of the book. “It is a sad fact of life that if a young woman is unlucky enough to come into the world without expectations, she had better do all she can to ensure that she is beautiful. To be poor and handsome is misfortune enough, but to be penniless and plain is a hard fate, indeed,” says Bruccoleri.

With the assistance of some helpful extended family members and newfound friends, she journeys to London for work before finding herself caught up in a love triangle between Laurie Davidson as Mr. Ryder and Dónal Finn as Mr. Tom Hayward. Other Pride and Prejudice characters continue to reappear, including Charlotte Lucas (Anna Fenton-Garvey) and Mr. Collins (Ryan Sampson). Mary finds herself at odds with Caroline Bingley, played by Ted Lasso’s newest star Tanya Reynolds, who sees her as a rival in a dwindling marriage market.

The Other Bennet Sister struck a chord with audiences, recontextualizing characters from a beloved novel and giving a long-neglected figure her due. Mary’s constant pursuit of identity feels modern, despite the period setting. And with her family so often overlooking her, her ability to claim her own happiness feels like a true underdog story.

The Other Bennet Sister will show what comes after love and marriage in its upcoming three-episode Christmas special. BritBox released the trailer for the new installment, catching up with Mary as she struggles with her new identity as a married woman and the accompanying expectations. “As Mary Bennet, I’d often spoke my mind freely,” she says in the trailer, before adding she now feels “lost.” This backslide into insecurity comes as a shock to audiences who watched her work so diligently to become self-assured throughout Season 1. She’s not the only one feeling lost amid big life changes, with Mary’s frenemy Caroline Bingley seeking help ahead of giving birth to her first child.

Caroline isn’t the only returning character. The trailer shows not only Mrs. Bennet, but Mary reuniting with her sisters played by Poppy Gilbert, Maddie Close, Grace Hogg-Robinson, and Molly Wright. Mr. Ryder, Charlotte, and Varada Sethu as Ann Baxter are also set to return, with Nerys Amber Stocks joining as Lucy, Mary's new housemaid.

After the success of The Other Bennet Sister, BritBox announced it will apply the same treatment to other Jane Austen novels in its Austen Untold anthology. The Other Elliot Sister is up next, shifting the focus to Persuasion’s Elizabeth Elliot. Austen’s posthumously published final novel focused on Elizabeth’s younger sister Anne as she reconnected with her first love. Similarly told across 10 episodes, Quintrell is returning to adapt Hadlow’s upcoming novel of the same name.

Production on the series is expected to begin in 2027. “Elizabeth Elliot is an intriguing figure at the edges of Persuasion - someone we know just enough about to wonder what might be going on beneath the surface,” Jane Tranter, CEO of Bad Wolf, explained in a press release. “We’re delighted to be returning to the world of Jane Austen with the BBC and BritBox, and to bring another overlooked character to the centre of her own story as part of Austen Untold - an exciting new anthology that gives us the chance to explore Austen’s world through the eyes of characters whose stories have yet to be fully explored.”

There's currently a renewed interest in the works of Jane Austen, with the recently released film adaptation of Sense and Sensibility and Netflix's upcoming Pride & Prejudice miniseries. So now is the perfect time to binge The Other Bennet Sister, especially with its upcoming Christmas special just weeks away. The feel-good show will only take five hours to watch, so it is perfect for a weekend or even a long night in. And it’s just the start, with BritBox promising more stories centering Austen's overlooked characters.
