Avengers: Doomsday Final Trailer Breakdown - All 13 New Scenes Explained & Ranked By Importance

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

With less than a month before the biggest Marvel movie of all time arrives, Avengers: Doomsday finally lands in cinemas, the final trailer (hopefully) has arrived. After weeks of teasing, poster drops, and what has felt like yet more tidal waves of speculation, the final Doomsday teaser delivers more new footage.

Since the last trailer, Marvel's Avengers: Endgame Encore re-release added new footage, confirmed Hulk's return as Doctor Doom's seemingly reluctant ally, and the TVA, and VisionQuest's post-credits revealed Scarlet Witch's return. The Russos had promised there would be more surprises - as well as telling us all to keep refreshing our feeds every day until release - and while it'll be better for some spoilers to remain unspoiled, the trailer inevitably added more new footage. Thankfully, there's nothing particularly ruinous in terms of new secrets, and the trailer - which is great - is mostly focused on hype building and finalizing the story.

So, with the final Avengers: Doomsday trailer now here and more new scenes and extended shots to pick over, here's everything new, what it means, and which of the reveals is the most exciting.

Thanks to a last-minute cast announcement - which was presumably sped up by the decision to include her face in the new trailer - we already knew that Hailee Steinfeld's Kate Bishop is appearing in Avengers: Doomsday. It's hard to tell what she'll be doing, since she and Cassie Lang don't appear in any footage other than a shot of Cassie and Scott from an earlier trailer. It's probably safe to assume their roles will be relatively minor, but clearly they're active as heroes, as both are shown reacting to something in the sky while wearing their supersuits.

That's confirmed in a second emotional reaction shot from Cassie Lang in her Stature costume, which also looks a lot like the Young Avengers members are in the same place. Surely the other key member of this team who is - for some reason - unannounced is there with them, right?

And as for what the Young Avengers heroes could be reacting to? The trailer reveals what incursions now look like: which is a pretty big departure from how The Marvels showed the universes crashing together, but fits more with the image conjured by the graphics in Avengers: Endgame Encore's TVA scene. And it's a lot cooler this way.

Interestingly, the trailer confirms Steve and Peggy have kept their past lives secret from their son - who now appears to be in his teens - and he has no idea Steve was a superhero. That changes, of course, when Steve reveals the story of the Avengers, but it also settles the long-running rumor that he could have been operating as a hero in his new timeline.

Naturally, we also get some establishing shots of the Rogers' "stolen life" as the emotional heart of the trailer, including Steve teaching his son to ride a bike, and what would seem to be Peggy and Steve arriving at their new home after Loki interrupts their dance from the end of Avengers: Endgame to lead them to safety (presumably because their branch of the Sacred Timeline was about to be pruned). There are also some shots of Steve and Peggy about a decade later, when the arrival of the Fantastic Four disturbs their peace, which add to the picture of their family unit.

More Shang-Chi footage is always a good thing: in a brief shot, we see Simu Liu's hero arrive at Avengers Tower, using the Ten Rings as platforms to climb onto one of the Chrysler Building's iconic eagles. There's also a shot of Captain America and Falcon arriving, which seems to suggest this is the setup for the meeting between the two squabbling Avengers groups when Thor reveals what he knows about Doom.

There's also a shot of the Fantastic Four's ship arriving at Avengers Tower, with Steve Rogers and Thor aboard. It's very cool to see the Avengers logo spinning as the docking bay doors open.

The first Avengers: Doomsday trailer showed Doom either jumping or being thrown from a window of a mysterious building or ship, with further sleuthing suggesting Mjolnir may have burst out of the window after him. The new trailer confirms as much by adding more to the scene, and showing the hammer returning to Steve, presumably after he throws it at Doom to knock him through the window. This shot also looks a lot like the "building" is actually a ship, given the console Pedro Pascal's Reed Richards stands by.

We've seen a shot of Sue Storm using her powers in an earlier trailer, but new footage reveals that she's casting a gigantic force field in the ocean, showing, again, the level of power she already has in the MCU. There's nothing to confirm what she's actually attempting to contain, and it seems unlikely that she'd be able to keep an incursion at bay single-handedly, but it's still a great shot of her at full power.

Doctor Doom doesn't really play a significant part in the trailer, and there's not a lot of new footage, but there is one sequence showing Robert Downey Jr's villain flanked by three of his key underlings and surrounded by a huge number of his so-called Latverian witches. This is very much aura farming, but it works, and it gives a better suggestion of the size of Doom's army.

Interestingly, the witch known as "Boris" is missing, and there's no obvious sign that one of them is the Hulk. The red one being Scarlet Witch would make sense though, in aesthetic terms at the very least.

After Captain America's Avengers and the New Avengers come together at the latter's headquarters, Thor arrives with the Fantastic Four and Steve Rogers from Earth-828 to lay out the threat Doctor Doom poses, giving a speech that positions him as the leader of the unified group. This is a continuation of the warning he gives about Doom from the earlier trailer, with new dialog, including Steve's plea for the warring factions to put their faith in him, even if they can't set aside their differences. Here's what he originally said:

“I fought with many warriors in my time. They were far stronger than all of us put together, they died. They died facing enemies and threats that scared me far less than this one. Everything they sacrificed for will be for nothing if we do not stand together... If you return, you will return as brothers and sisters.”

Beyond his new speech, which again urges unity, we see a longer version of the scene in which Steve asks Thor if him wielding Mjolnir bothers him, which starts with Steve complimenting him on his rousing speech. As an indication that there will still be some levity even with the darker tone, Thor calls Steve's own speech (which we presumably only hear part of) "average by Cap's standards."

Incidentally, that wig on Sebastian Stan is a disaster. It's also interesting that the scriptwriters chose to have Thor say he doesn't know half of the heroes in front of him, given the MCU's issues with establishing new legacy heroes in the wake of Avengers: Endgame. Is it playful shade, or just an accidental turn of phrase? Here's the new part of his speech:

"I don't know what half of you do. I don't even know who half of you are. Put aside your petty squabbles. The time has come. Team up and stand together."

The opening tranquility of the trailer is interrupted when the Fantastic Four arrive at Steve and Peggy's new family home, asking if he is Steve Rogers. As had been widely speculated, this seemingly confirms that Loki takes Steve and Peggy to Earth-828 as their new home. Their son's reaction to seeing the Fantastic Four in his living room also adds evidence to this, since he clearly recognizes them and makes the leap to assuming his father used to be a superhero too.

Interestingly, the trailer also confirms that Steve actually leaves Peggy and his son at their new home, which suggests this universe may not be immediately destroyed by an incursion. It also explains why Peggy is so absent from most of the other trailers: it would seem she's actually only in the opening act (unless she returns at the end when Doom's victory is inevitably revealed).

After their undernourished farewell in Avengers: Endgame's ending, it's great to see that Doomsday will feature a proper reunion for the oldest friends in the MCU. It may not be the most narratively important reveal from the trailer, but in terms of emotional depth, there's not much that can outstrip seeing Bucky and Steve brought back together. And Bucky's reaction is beautifully on point.
