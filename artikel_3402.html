GTA 6 Players Officially Getting Free Reward Worth $70

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Devin Coull is a Toronto-based writer, producer, and on-camera host. He spent over six years working in gaming and esports, most recently at theScore Esports, created content for millions of viewers around the world.

In case you needed literally any other reason to pre-order Grand Theft Auto 6, Rockstar Games is sweetening the deal with access to another huge title. The GTA series is only one of many IPs under the umbrella of parent company Take-Two Interactive, and the publisher will apparently take advantage of that library to further incentivize sales.

The offer is part of an upcoming crossover between GTA 6 and NBA 2K27, the latest release in the popular basketball franchise from 2K Games. Players will need an active GTA+ membership to take advantage of it, but that comes courtesy of a GTA 6 purchase. Publisher 2K has announced a limited-time collaboration between NBA 2K27 and GTA 6, coinciding with the basketball game's upcoming season and, of course, the release of Rockstar's highly anticipated open-world blockbuster.

According to 2K, GTA+ members on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S will be able to play NBA 2K27 Standard Edition from November 27, the start of the new season, through January 15 at no additional cost. And here's the thing: pre-ordering GTA 6 gets you one month of GTA+ for free, meaning you won't need to pay anything extra to take advantage of the offer initially.

That being said, since the promotion lasts 50 days, you'll need to extend your membership once the free month included with your GTA 6 pre-order expires. For those curious, one month of GTA+ costs $7.99 USD, and the Standard Edition of NBA 2K27 retails for $69.99 (although it can currently be found on sale for $39.99).

This is all part of a broader crossover between the two titles, bringing Vice City to Season 3 of NBA 2K27. As seen in the trailer released earlier this week on the official NBA 2K YouTube channel, the crossover will introduce several Vice City-themed rewards, including clothing and other items inspired by the upcoming game.

If you don't know what GTA+ is, it's basically Rockstar's version of Xbox Game Pass but includes access to their catalog. So this offer doesn't just get you access to NBA 2K27 but also Red Dead Redemption, Bully, the GTA Definitive Edition, and more. On top of that, members also get access to a ton of different rewards in GTA Online, including in-game money, discounts on vehicles, along with other perks.

With GTA 6 on the horizon, I'm not sure how much use players will get out of these perks, but they're there for those who are interested. Still, there's no denying that Take-Two is still looking to drive people towards this subscription service with these offers, and the company's latest crossover with NBA 2K27 comes at an interesting time.

Take-Two recently confirmed to Bloomberg that NBA 2K27 had fallen short of the company's sales expectations amid growing anticipation for GTA 6. The collaboration could help bring more attention to the basketball franchise ahead of the highly anticipated launch of Rockstar's next game. But considering Grand Theft Auto 6 looks to have its own basketball minigame, even a free offer might not be enough to pull me out of Leonida.
