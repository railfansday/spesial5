Typically, the inner radiation belt is covered in a shroud of charged gas known as the plasmasphere. Charged gases do a great job of dampening high frequency electromagnetic waves, as they simply absorb their energy. But when the May 2024 storm hit, the plasmasphere was crushed back toward Earth, creating a void where it would normally be.

Which is the perfect situation for chorus waves. In a depleted plasma environment, these waves are able to transfer their energy very efficiently to the surrounding low energy electrons, boosting them from sub-million electron volt energies to multi-MeV ranges - essentially creating high energy electron radiation within what is normally an exclusion zone.

Over time this new high energy radiation spread out both towards and away from Earth - and the effect lasted for months. Its effects were visible to Arase until another solar storm hit five months later in October 2024, and changed the magnetic field situation up again.
