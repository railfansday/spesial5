Krispy Kreme Is Bringing <i><em>Rudolph</em></i> To Life With A Brand-New Christmas Doughnut Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Who needs a glowing red nose when you've got doughnuts this festive?

If you want to bring ho-ho-home some holiday cheer, Krispy Kreme has you covered with its brand-new Rudolph Christmas Collection. Inspired by the classic 1964 Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer film, the doughnut chain is whipping up festive, sugary treats featuring some of the movie’s most beloved characters.

The newcomers are the Rudolph, Bumble, and Sam the Snowman Doughnuts. Rudolph starts with an Original Glazed doughnut dipped in icing, then gets a glittery Christmas garland sprinkle blend, vanilla-flavored frosting, and a Rudolph wreath decoration.

The Bumble Doughnut has an unglazed shell filled with raspberry filling and dipped in vanilla icing. It's finished with a vanilla frosting garland, white glitter sprinkles, and a Bumble fondant piece. Finally, the Sam the Snowman Doughnut starts with an Original Glazed dipped in vanilla icing and covered in white chocolate curls, then gets a buttercreme Christmas tree and Sam the Snowman fondant piece.

“We love giving fans new ways to enjoy the traditions they grew up with, and this collection is all about bringing people together over something fun, festive and delicious,” Alison Holder, Chief Product and Brand Officer at Krispy Kreme, said in a statement.

The new collection arrives November 28 at participating Krispy Kreme locations nationwide, just two days after the chain wraps up its Thanksgiving doughnut collection. That lineup featured four holiday-inspired flavors: Berry Cobbler, Caramel Pumpkin, Double Chocolate Crumble, and Cinnamon Apple Filled.

As for the new Rudolph treats, you can pick them up individually or grab a dozen in a special collectible-style box featuring the beloved characters.
