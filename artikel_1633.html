‘For sure’: Xabi Alonso makes Emmanuel Emegha prediction after Chelsea impact

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Xabi Alonso has heaped praise on Emmanuel Emegha and believes the striker can help dig Chelsea out of their poor form.

The Blues lost 3-2 to Nottingham Forest at the City Ground on Friday night, which was their fourth defeat in their last five league games.

It came after last Saturday’s 4-1 defeat to Leeds in which Emegha scored Chelsea’s only goal on the occasion of his first start for the club.

The Dutch forward, who joined in the summer from Chelsea’s sister club Strasbourg, also scored both of Chelsea’s goals in the Forest defeat and was praised for two well-taken finishes.

Asked after the match whether the 23-year-old can be a key figure in lifting Chelsea in a difficult period, Alonso replied: “Yes, for sure. He is having a good impact in the last two games.”

Alonso told Sky Sports: “Today, in the second half, we knew that between Danny and him we had to manage the whole game. He has done well.

“The last few weeks he has got into rhythm and you can see his quality. Hopefully he can help us as a team.”
