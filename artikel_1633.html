Report: Liverpool enter transfer battle with Barcelona for €25m star

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Liverpool have been linked with a whole host of players ahead of the winter transfer window.

Many areas among their ranks need upgrading and, even though fans knew this during the summer, Liverpool still only signed four new players.

And, one of them (Jeremy Jacquet) was already confirmed all the way back in January.

Now that we're a few months into the season, it's clear that some cracks are starting to show among the Reds' ranks.

So, with the January transfer window now edging closer, the rumour mill is likely to go into overdrive.

Liverpool and Barcelona have both set their eyes on Juventus' Andrea Cambiaso

Liverpool's full-backs have been a major problem for them this term.

Left-back has been the main issue but, there have been times when some dodgy situations have occurred at right-back.

Indeed, Ronald Araujo has performed much better than Kostas Tsimikas or Milos Kerkez have all season but, it still feels like an upgrade could be beneficial, or, at least someone with a bit of a higher ceiling than the Uruguayan needs to be brought in.

Because, while the defender is good, he's going to be 28 years old by June 2027 which is usually when the performance levels of players start to drop.

So, that's why this latest news from TuttoJuve should pique the interest of fans.

"Barcelona aren't the only team to have included Cambiaso on their list of targets," they wrote.

"Liverpool and Manchester United have also reportedly expressed interest in the Italian full-back, who is particularly appreciated for his ability to cover multiple roles on the wings.

"In particular, Manchester United are reportedly evaluating reinforcements for the left side of defence, while Liverpool are reportedly continuing to monitor various profiles to broaden their options.

"Cambiaso's versatility, which allows him to play on both flanks and in different playing systems, represents a particularly attractive characteristic for major European clubs.

"But one question remains: would €20-25 million be enough to convince Juventus to give up on a player with these characteristics?"

Andrea Cambiaso's versatility is a major selling point for the Italian

Versatility has become a key part of football in recent years.

Clubs now need players to be able to play in multiple positions if needed.

Because, with the sheer number of injuries that they suffer, having just one back-up option for each position isn't really enough anymore.

Cambiaso has been a regular feature for Juventus over the last few years and his ability to play on either side of the pitch is massively beneficial to any club that signs him.

The Italy international is also only 26 years old so, while he already has a lot of experience under his belt, there is still room for him to improve.

Right now, it's unclear what the future holds for Cambiaso but, if Liverpool are serious about reigniting their interest in him, they're going to have to be quick, especially with Barcelona lurking.
