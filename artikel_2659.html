Rutgers Football vs Michigan State 2026 Preview

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

After pulling off a miraculous victory last week against Penn State 25-24 in Happy Valley, Rutgers football will return home this Saturday for its final game of the season against Michigan State. The Scarlet Knights will be attempting to end the season on a high note by beating the fellow Big Ten bottom-feeder Spartans on senior night.

Rutgers' defense stepped up in a major way last week, forcing two turnovers on downs and recovering two fumbles that they forced. They also held Penn State's offense to just 40 percent on third down and recorded two sacks. If the Knights can find a way to replicate that performance, it will provide them with a major boost to their efforts. Injuries might assist here as well, as Michigan State starting quarterback Alessio Milivojevic has been ruled out for this game, and backup quarterback Cam Fancher is listed as questionable. This means that freshman Kayd Coffman, who started last week, will likely get the start again. In his only start of the season, Coffman completed 10 of 19 passes for 106 yards, with no touchdowns and one interception against No.13 Oregon.

According to Oren Basse of Michigan State's SB Nation site, Chrishon McCray and Fredrick Moore are likely to be the main targets in the passing game. Their numbers don't jump off the page, but they seem fairly reliable, as they have combined for 70 catches, 764 yards, and three touchdowns. The Spartans have a weaker running game compared to the opponents Rutgers has played in the past. Jaziun Patterson leads Michigan State with 92 carries for 353 yards and six touchdowns on the season. Since the Spartans' most reliable group on offense seems to be their receivers, Rutgers defensive success hinges on disrupting the pass and taking their opponent's best weapon out of the game.

According to Basse, the two most noteworthy players on the Michigan State defense are defensive tackles, Ben Roberts and Eli Coenen. Roberts has recorded 27 tackles and two sacks so far this season, while Coenen has recorded 21 tackles and 3.5 sacks. These two could pose a bit of a problem for the Knights' offense, which has had shaky line play all season and will likely be leaning a lot on the running game due to their quarterbacks dealing with injuries. So one of the key objectives on offense will be to neutralize these two and prevent them from causing a disruption in the backfield.

On top of all of that, AJ Surace will need to continue the momentum he started last game with another consistent game this time around. He doesn't necessarily have to break the game open; however, he needs to be consistent and deliver when needed, like he did last game, to give the Knights their best chance to win. One interesting caveat that should be noted, however, is that Surace is listed as questionable on Wednesday's injury report while Dylan Lonergan, the team's starter at the beginning of the season, is listed as probable.

Rutgers and Michigan State will kick off on Saturday, Nov. 28 at 4:15 pm inside SHI Stadium. The game will also double as the Knights' senior night, with the team's seniors being honored before the game. Live coverage will be available on the Big Ten Network.
