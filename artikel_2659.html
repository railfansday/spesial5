WEEKEND SCHEDULE: NOVEMBER'S FINAL FIXTURES

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The final weekend of November brings another round of fixtures for the Rossoneri Youth Sector, at the PUMA House of Football and across Lombardy. After beating Lazio to secure a third consecutive win, the Men's Primavera hosts Lecce on Saturday. The U17s are in action on Sunday, welcoming Mantova after an away win over Udinese. The U18s travel to Frosinone, while both the U14s and U13s face Renate in Briosco. Among the women's teams, the U17s visit Folgore Caratese, while the U15s look to maintain momentum against Ospitaletto after beating Mantova.
