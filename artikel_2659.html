Highlights: Victor Wembanyama drops 44 points and 16 rebounds in Portland

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Coming off a shorthanded win in Sacramento, the Spurs traveled to Portland to take on the Trail Blazers in NBA Cup play. Victor Wembanyama returned after sitting out the Sacramento game, but De'Aaron Fox was ruled out again with a shin injury. The first half was defined by two different-paced quarters: the first quarter was a high-scoring track meet that ended with Portland leading 38-35, but in the second quarter, both teams struggled to shoot. After scoring 22 points each, the Blazers took a 60-57 lead into halftime. The lead changed back and forth throughout the third, with Devin Vassell beating the buzzer at the end of the quarter. With the Spurs up by three heading into the fourth, a wild quarter was about to take place. For every punch Portland threw at the Spurs, the Spurs would counter with Wemby being Wemby. Late in the game, the Spurs had multiple chances to tie it up. However, unforced turnovers and ill-advised shots gave Portland a chance to seal the game with under a minute left. After Deni Advija banked in a lucky three to beat the shot clock buzzer, the Spurs needed a miracle. Unfortunately, the miracle did not happen, and the Spurs lost 116-111.

Wemby continues his MVP campaign by dropping a whopping 44 points (17-30 FG, 7-10 FT), 16 rebounds, three assists, and two blocks. When someone says Wemby is being Wemby, they usually mean he does an unimaginable and unguardable act on the court that results in a made basket. The Alien's highlight tape contains multiple examples of no regard for human life. The following is a clinic on how to embarrass Donovan Clingan. First, Wemby gets Clingan to bite up on his pump fake, and then he drives to the basket with long strides for the easy one-handed jam.

HIGHLIGHT OF THE GAME: Wemby pulls out the street moves by going behind-the-back on the fast break and finishing with a scoop layup off the glass with his left hand.

Stephon Castle was the lead guard with Fox and Dylan Harper out, and he turned in a near triple-double performance: 20 points, nine assists, and six rebounds. Here, Castle and Wemby run a beautiful two-man game that results in an AREA 51 alley-oop connection.

And now, a series of and-ones converted on Clingan. First, a jump hook with the left in the paint? Through contact? Okay, Wemby.

Second, Wemby grabs the board, leads the fast break by himself, drives in on Clingan, goes with the hook shot with his right hand, and puts it in off the glass. Oh, plus the foul, too.

We interrupt this Wemby 1 on 1 masterclass with a bailout pass out of shot to a cutting Carter Bryant slam. Thank you for your patience.

Back to regularly scheduled programming, Wemby goes to work on Clingan and steps through side contact to finish strong at the rim for the and-one. If you're keeping score at home, that's three and-ones scored on Clingan from Wemby. If you were wondering, Clingan eventually fouled out and picked up a technical foul on his way out. He said something about how Wemby is soft and flopping? If that was the case, Micah Nori should've challenged one of his fouls.

Bonus highlight: Here is how Vassell beat the third-quarter buzzer: A leaning three from the wing in transition. Nothing but net.
