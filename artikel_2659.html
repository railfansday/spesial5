Seahawks QB Drew Lock is a sleeper fantasy football option for Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Seattle Seahawks quarterback Drew Lock is an under-the-radar fantasy football option in Week 10 as he prepares to face the Las Vegas Raiders.

Lock showed promise in Week 9, completing 19 of 28 passes for 175 yards and a touchdown. He did not throw an interception in the game and also added five rushing attempts for 16 yards, providing a modest boost to his fantasy production.

On the season, Lock has completed an impressive 71.1% of his passes for 597 yards and five touchdowns without any interceptions thrown. He has also recorded 29 rushing yards on seven attempts, offering additional fantasy appeal through his mobility.

The matchup against Las Vegas may create an opportunity for Lock to put forth another productive outing. The Raiders have allowed the fifth-most passing yards to opposing teams in the league this season with 2,238, as well as the most passing touchdowns with 20, while opponents have completed 65.6% of their passes.

Given the favorable matchup and his recent efficiency, Lock deserves consideration as a quarterback to add off of waivers wires, and start in two quarterback leagues, or in deep leagues.
