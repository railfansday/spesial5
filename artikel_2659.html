Green scores 22, Saint Louis takes down Northern Kentucky 125-63

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

ST. LOUIS (AP) — Trey Green had 22 points in Saint Louis' 125-63 win over Northern Kentucky on Tuesday.

Green shot 7 for 8 from beyond the arc for the Billikens (3-0). Kellen Thames and Amari McCottry each scored 18 points, and Thames added seven rebounds.

B.J. Davis-Ray led the way for the Norse (2-2) with 13 points and six rebounds. Northern Kentucky also got 10 points from Noah Pagotto.

Saint Louis took the lead with 18:17 remaining in the first half and did not trail again. Ishan Sharma led his team in scoring with 16 points in the first half to go up 56-24 at the break. Saint Louis extended its lead to 81-35 during the second half, fueled by a 9-0 scoring run. Green scored a team-high 12 points after the break to close out the win.

Saint Louis plays High Point at home on Sunday, and Northern Kentucky will host Central Michigan on Saturday.
