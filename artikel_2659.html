Arizona Cardinals Week 11 offensive snap counts, observations vs. Chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In the Arizona Cardinals' 34-7 loss to the Kansas City Chiefs, 16 players participated in at least one of the 56 offensive snaps.

How was the playing time divided? We have the numbers for everyone below.

The first number after each name is their snap count, and the second is the percentage. Players in bold were starters.

Injured reserve: Gardner Minshew II (eligible to return after Week 13, but won't)

Sunday's offensive performance wasn't all on Johnson's shoulders. After all, he did what he could to be professional after signing with the team on Nov. 10 and having one day of practice after Beck had back spasms late in Thursday's practice. He completed 18 of 31 passes for 155 yards with a touchdown and no interceptions. He was sacked three times.

Overall, the Cardinals managed to reach 205 yards because of an 11-play, 72-yard drive with the score 34-7 in the fourth quarter. Prior to that, the offense totaled 129 yards on 41 plays (3.1 average), including the touchdown drive that gained 63 yards on 10 plays. Their other six possessions totaled 48 yards on 24 plays.

It might be Johnson again next Sunday against the Commanders if Beck doesn't improve enough to play. Johnson's teams have won only two of his 12 career starts and both were against Washington in 2018 and 2025.

Tyler Allgeier (30/54), Bam Knight (26/46)

Did not play: Evan Hull (played only on special teams)

Injured reserve: Jeremiyah Love (eligible to return after Week 12), James Conner (eligible to return), Trey Benson

It was another lackluster outing for the runners. Knight had seven carries for 35 yards, but only three for six yards prior to that 72-yard drive. Allgeier was 7-for-23 and 6-for-17 before the long possession.

Simi Fehoko (52/93), Trey Wallace III (36/64), Zay Jones (32/57, elevated from practice squad), Reggie Virgil (2/4), Devin Duvernay (2/4)

Injured reserve: Jalen Brooks (eligible to return, but won't), Kendrick Bourne (eligible to return, but won't), Michael Wilson (eligible to return, but won't)

Wallace was the leading wide receiver, catching four of seven targets for 57 yards. That includes a 23-yard play on the only drive that resulted in a touchdown. Jones had one catch for 17 yards and Fehoko one for two yards. Fehoko rushed for seven yards on three attempts, including an ill-advised "jet" sweep that gained nothing on fourth-and-1. Another "jet" sweep to Jones gained one yard.

Trey McBride (45/80), Elijah Higgins (32/57), Hunter Long (23/41)

Did not play: Teagan Quitoriano (played only on special teams)

PUP reserve: Tip Reiman (eligible to practice)

McBride had 14 of Johnson's targets and caught nine, but for only 54 yards (6.0 average). Long had a reception for 11 and Higgins one for four yards.

LT Josh Fryar (56/100), LG Isaac Seumalo (56/100), C Hjalte Froholdt (56/100), RG Isaiah Adams (56/100), RT Jayden Williams (56/100)

Did not play: T Jedrick Wills Jr., T Elijah Wilkinson, G/C Hayden Conner (all played only on special teams)

Inactive: G Jon Gaines II (injured), C Kingsley Equakun

Injured reserve: LT Paris Johnson Jr. (eligible to return, but won't), G Chase Bisontis

Williams, returning from a knee injury after starting four games at left tackle, made his right tackle debut replacing the benched Wilkinson. Froholdt was back at center after missing the game against the Rams while in concussion protocol. The starting lineup Sunday was the seventh different line combination in 11 games.

Only Seumalo has started every game. The Cardinals have started three left tackles, three right guards, two center and two right tackles.
