Studs and duds from Jaguars' Week 11 loss vs. Giants

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Jacksonville Jaguars dropped Sunday's game against New York, losing 23-18 to the Giants.

With the dust having settled on this game, here are the studs and duds from the Jaguars' performance.

As Liam Coen said afterward, if you hold a team to 23 points, you should win the game. Despite the Giants being in control for much of Sunday, moving the ball didn't come easily. New York totaled just 238 yards of offense, averaging only 3.7 yards per play.

Over the last several weeks, the play in the red area has been up and down, but on Sunday, it was mostly down. The good news is that the Jaguars' offense made six red zone visits -- that's a lot! -- however, they left with only one touchdown. For all the issues the Jaguars had on offense at times, they left a lot of points on the field.

No surprise here, but Little was again excellent. He was 4-for-4 on field goal attempts in this game, accounting for 12 of the Jaguars' 18 points.

Adding to the issues that the Jaguars faced, penalties were again a big problem. Jacksonville was penalized 13 times in this game for 107 yards. For a chunk of the game, they had more penalties than points and finished with more penalty yardage than rushing yards.

Johnson did a bit of everything on Sunday. He had two QB pressures and was in on seven total tackles. In coverage, he allowed only two receptions on seven targets, surrendering 12 total yards. Johnson also recovered a fumble and had a near interception.
