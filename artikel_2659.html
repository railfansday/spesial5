Eagles claim Cowboys draft pick off waivers

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Philadelphia Eagles claimed Ajani Cornelius off waivers from the Dallas Cowboys, according to an official team announcement.

Cornelius, 24, is a 6-5, 318 pound offensive lineman who was selected with a sixth-round pick (No. 204 overall) in the 2025 NFL Draft.

Playing college football for Rhode Island and Oregon, Cornelius almost exclusively played at right tackle. Pre-draft scouting report via NFL.com:

A durable lineman with NFL traits and four years of starting experience, Cornelius lacks standout flashes on tape. He has been a full-time right tackle but lacks protection talent to stay outside as a pro. He has access to physical tools but needs to play with better bend and hand quickness to help stabilize his play. He can cover up defenders with size but is frequently out-leveraged at the point of attack. His average body control and slide quickness should be mitigated in smaller spaces but those issues will still be concerning against explosive interior rushers. The traits should make him a Day 3 target, but his ceiling might be as a backup.

During his time with Dallas, Cornelius logged zero offensive snaps and 21 special teams snaps over four regular season games played. He played at right tackle during the 2025 preseason before the Cowboys moved him to right guard in the 2026 preseason.

The Eagles seem to think Cornelius has some developmental potential. It also makes sense for them to add some more offensive tackle depth after waiving Fred Johnson.

As an added bonus, the Eagles might've snagged a player the Cowboys were hoping to get back on their practice squad.
