Which Browns receiver did Rams reportedly try to trade for?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

There are reports that the Los Angeles Rams tried to trade for Jerry Jeudy from the Cleveland Browns. However, the Browns apparently wanted "premium picks" and the Rams weren't willing to part with them.

I have also seen some rumblings that the Rams maybe wanted to trade for Ladd McConkey from the Los Angeles Chargers. That didn't happen either.

So, rumors, reports and speculation suggest the Rams tried to trade for a receiver, but it didn't work out. Either other teams were asking for too much, or the Rams didn't want to give up what was being asked. Both things can be true.

Remember, the Rams made their moves in their offseason. They traded for Myles Garrett and Trent McDuffie. They made a move for Tutu Atwell too. So, the Rams pushed their chips before the season started, and now they are holding what chips they have left, and hoping their current pot is big enough to make a push.

It's not that the Rams didn't try to make a move at the deadline, it's more like a move didn't happen.

"The Rams created $3 million in cap space by restructuring the contracts of Pro Bowl running back Kyren Williams and safety Kamren Curl, ESPN's Field Yates reported on X.

The details came from Spotrac. Los Angeles turned $2 million of Williams' salary into a signing bonus and added two void years to his deal, which freed up $1.7 million. The Rams did the same with $1.7 million of Curl's salary, which cleared another $1.3 million.

Williams and Curl are both under contract through 2028. With the void years, both will now stay on the Rams' books through 2030.
