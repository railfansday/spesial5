Best fantasy football waiver wire pickups: 12 players to add for NFL Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

What a high-scoring week fantasy football managers just witnessed. Three players scored more than 40 PPR points, eight topped 30 and 19 more had at least 20.

Just don't expect a repeat performance. Especially this week, with six NFL teams on bye.

Fantasy managers will likely be scrambling to fill holes in their starting lineups − and some of those fill-ins might need to come from the waiver wire. Those who made proactive moves a couple weeks ago to add Chiefs running back Emmett Johnson were rewarded with his 35-point outburst in Week 10.

Could lightning strike twice before season's end?

ON BYE: Atlanta, Cleveland, Green Bay, L.A. Rams, New England, Seattle

Due to the wide variance in types of leagues and individual team needs, the players listed here include their availability in Yahoo leagues, which may or may not match roster rates on other platforms. Suggested bid values based on $100 free agent acquisition budget (FAAB) for the season.

NOTE: At this point in the season, feel free to overbid on players with the potential to help you win each week. You can't take those FAAB dollars with you into the offseason!

If there's a parallel to Johnson's situation in Kansas City, it's with Black in San Francisco. The rookie has been a reliable backup to Christian McCaffrey all season, occasionally turning in fantasy-friendly performances despite limited opportunities. If anything should happen to McCaffrey where he'd need to sit out a week, Black is fully capable of turning in a Johnson-like performance. This is probably your last chance to get out of the blue and into the Black. (Recommended FAAB bid: $37)

There won't be many opportunities the rest of the season to grab a difference-maker off the waiver wire, but Tyson could be one of those gems. In most leagues, he's not available ... but be sure to check just in case. The Saints' first-round pick (No. 8 overall) in this year's draft, Tyson spent the first eight weeks on injured reserve. However, it didn't take long for him to have a breakout game, with 11 catches for 80 yards last week vs. Carolina. (FAAB bid: $35)

The injury status of star TE Brock Bowers will play a major role in how valuable Mayer is going forward. Bowers is considered day-to-day after exiting Sunday's game with what coach Klint Kubiak called a "significant" ankle injury. With rookie QB Fernando Mendoza making his second NFL start this week in Denver, Mayer could serve as a frequent safety valve against the Broncos' pass rush. (FAAB bid: $9)

The McGowan momentum stalled once the Colts decided they weren't trading star RB Jonathan Taylor. But a funny thing happened this week: Taylor and McGowan both had similar stat lines against the Dolphins. Though McGowan played just 17 snaps to Taylor's 40, he still carried the ball on 12 of those, gaining 77 yards and scoring a touchdown for the second time in two weeks. (FAAB bid: $7)

A confluence of events in Minnesota has Price as one of this week's top pickups. First off, Kirk Cousins' return to rescue a flailing Vikings offense has already been a tremendous success. Price was one of the beneficiaries on Sunday, catching four passes for 49 yards and a touchdown. A multiweek injury to fellow wideout Jordan Addison should keep Price and Tai Felton heavily involved in the passing game for the near future. (FAAB bids: $7 on Price, $4 on Felton)

Injuries played a role in Ayomanor's additional opportunities as well in Week 10. Carnell Tate missed his second consecutive game with a shoulder injury, and Wan'Dale Robinson exited early after tweaking his ankle. That opened the door to (stately) Ayomanor setting career highs in catches (five) and receiving yards (79) vs. Jacksonville. With the porous Cowboys defense up next, Ayomanor could be worth eyeing on the waiver wire. (FAAB bid: $5)

Not to pick on the Cowboys ... ehhh, what the heck, let's pick on them. (Did you see how bad their defense was against the 49ers last week?) With six quarterbacks going on bye, Ward is clearly the best option of the ones available in most leagues. He's had solid games back-to-back, throwing for a total of 535 yards and four TDs. And now he takes aim at the Cowboys, who just had Brock Purdy light them up for 389 passing yards and three touchdowns. (FAAB bid: $5)

Miami's passing attack is the NFL's fourth-worst this season. But Washington has somehow managed to be fantasy-relevant on occasion. He did it in unconventional fashion last week, throwing a touchdown pass to wideout Jalen Tolbert while also catching three for 50 yards himself. The Bills give up the 10th-most fantasy points per game to wide receivers so Washington could be a one-week flex option. (FAAB bid: $4)

A much less exciting receiver pairing than we have in Minnesota, Treadwell and Dulin seem to be the primary pass-catchers in Indy now that Josh Downs and Keenan Allen are no longer around. Treadwell caught three passes, one of them for a score, on Sunday. And the 2016 first-rounder has the more prominent pedigree. Then again, they're relying on Daniel Jones to get them the ball. At least this week they get to face the Texans, who've yielded the third-most fantasy points per game to wide receivers. (FAAB bids: $2 apiece)

Meyers represents a tangential attempt to grab a piece of the Bengals' league-leading passing offense. The air attack runs almost exclusively through WRs Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins, but occasionally some scraps will fall on the floor. Meyers caght a TD pass from Burrow in Week 10 and could find a few more opportunities this week in Washington. (FAAB bid: $2)

Demercado is a "break glass in case of emergency" option. Woody Marks left Sunday's game early and has entered the concussion protocol. David Montgomery may not be ready to return for the Thursday night clash against the Colts. Demercado got the majority of work once Marks left, though he didn't go much with his opportunities (seven carries, 16 yards). (FAAB bid: $1)
