NFC West Recap: 49ers hang 46 on Dallas; Rams rout Cardinals, Seahawks win

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The San Francisco 49ers put up the most points and yards in franchise history for a road game, which is also the most yards the Dallas Cowboys have given up at home.

I'm just getting started on stats. We'll begin with the 49ers averaging 9.4 yards per play. That do anything for you? How about Brock Purdy going 22-of-28 for 389 yards passing and three touchdowns, finishing with a passer rating of 154.5. Then there's running back Christian McCaffrey, who had 223 yards from scrimmage and three scores.

And yet, they still, STILL had to recover an onside kick to win the game.

Cowboys quarterback Dak Prescott threw five touchdown passes, while cornerback Deommodore Lenoir spent most of the afternoon chasing Cowboys wide receiver George Pickens. Renardo Green left with the same back injury he dealt with all week. There hasn't been much about Green's status for next week as of this writing, but Kyle Shanahan said he was already playing hurt.

Regardless of whether we want to be optimistic or skeptical, the 49ers stay the NFC's No. 1 seed, a game ahead of the 7-2 pack of the Detroit Lions, Minnesota Vikings and Chicago Bears. The Seahawks and Rams both won, so the division lead stays at 2.5 games. The 49ers face a tough gauntlet, starting with the Vikings. Any questions you have about this team are about to be answered over the next four games.

After trading quarterback Kirk Cousins to the Vikings on Tuesday, the Las Vegas Raiders gave Fernando Mendoza his first NFL start. At first it looked like an upset, but then the Seahawks had to spoil the party.

There were moments, however. On Seahawks quarterback Drew Lock's first dropback, he tried to shovel the ball away to avoid a sack and fumbled. Mendoza scored on a quarterback draw a few plays later, and the Raiders led 10-7 at halftime. The second half belonged to the Seahawks, with highlights like rookie running back Jadarian Price scoring his first career touchdown to take the lead, and a 71-yard Jaxon Smith-Njigba score.

Final: 21-13. Smith-Njigba had 144 of his 171 yards receiving in the third quarter alone. Just like against the 49ers last week, the Raiders had a terrible second half with negative yards until their final possession. Mendoza took six sacks as well. The score could have been worse. Lock lost a second fumble when Zach Charbonnet ran into him on a play-action fake, and Jason Myers missed a 44-yarder. Right tackle Abe Lucas left with an ankle injury, which is worth watching before Seattle comes to Levi's Stadium in Week 12.

It was a lot of Seattle things, and then a lot of Raider-ing. Unfortunately, Seattle things usually don't end in losses the way Raider-ing does.

Minshew Magic is gone to IR. That led us to Carson Beck starting for Arizona. A rookie, Carson Beck.

So what happens? The Cardinals tried doing what worked last time they played the Rams: field-goal-ing them to death. This time, it wasn't as successful, as they managed only six points throughout the game. Besides Matthew Stafford's early-game interception to linebacker Cody Simon, it was all Los Angeles. Beck threw an interception just before halftime, and the Rams turned it into a 17-6 lead at the break.

Final: 34-6 in Glendale, the Rams' fourth straight win. Arizona finished with 123 yards of total offense: 89 net passing and 34 rushing. Beck was sacked five times, 1.5 of them by Aaron Donald. Stafford threw three touchdown passes. The Cardinals need to get through 2026 and make a football team next year.

Credit where it's due: the Rams look like a team again. They're 1-2 in the division, and their bye week comes before a Thanksgiving Eve game against the Packers. No credit is given to them for getting a team like the Cardinals, a team that beat them solely on field goals a few weeks back. Luckily, the Rams also have a tough stretch coming up: the Green Bay Packers and the Kansas City Chiefs.

The 49ers "host" the Minnesota Vikings (7-2) at Estadio Banorte in Mexico City on Sunday Night Football. The Vikings traded a 2027 third-round pick for Cousins, and he went 21-of-31 for 207 yards passing and two touchdowns in a 30-10 win at Lambeau. So they got the offense figured out; what about the defense? Well, they sacked Jordan Love eight times.

Just a reminder, that's not the Cowboys defense: Dallas sacked Purdy five times, and the 49ers still scored 46. This time the 49ers' defense has to carry its share. The No. 1 seed is on the line, and Minnesota won the last two meetings. Cousins also became the first quarterback since at least 1950 to start for two different teams in consecutive weeks, and last week's start was against the 49ers, when Jaden Dugger returned his pass for a touchdown. At least the game isn't in Minnesota.

The Rams and Seahawks are on bye. So, bye.

The Cardinals visit the Kansas City Chiefs, who beat Atlanta 35-13 behind five Patrick Mahomes touchdown passes. Gotta love it when the 49ers get Atlanta's best, but then they go back to doing Falcons things.
