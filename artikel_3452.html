Henry Cavill Officially Reveals His Superman Future Following James Gunn’s Exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

This has been a roller coaster of a week for DC fans now that the company is once again under new leadership and seemingly headed in a different creative direction. News broke on Monday that James Gunn and Peter Safran are out as the co-CEOs of DC Studios, with former Marvel Studios producer Jeremy Latcham in line to take their place as the new creative head of the studio. This news comes while Gunn is still hard at work in the post-production phase of his first Superman sequel, Man of Tomorrow, which is due in theaters on July 9, 2027. In addition to David Corenswet, who was back as Superman for the third time during his DCU tenure, the film is also set to star Nicholas Hoult as Lex Luthor, Aaron Pierre as John Stewart, Milly Alcock as Supergirl, and Adria Arjona.

David Corenswet took over the role of Clark Kent from Henry Cavill, who last played the character in the 2022 Dwayne Johnson-led blockbuster, Black Adam. Fans were led to believe after the ending of Black Adam that Henry Cavill was returning as Superman for at least another project, but when Gunn and Safran took over, one of their first announcements was that a new actor would take over the role. Cavill has been busy in the last few years with other movies like Enola Holmes 3 and In The Grey, but fans would still welcome him back into the DCU as Superman with open arms. During a recent sit down with Inverse, Cavill was asked about the potential of coming back to play Superman in the future now that the DCU is under new leadership, and his answer might surprise you:

Four teams, four completely different reasons to put yourself in danger for strangers. This is the roster you actually belong on, what they would want from you, and what it would cost you to stay.

You belong where the stakes are largest and the personalities are loudest. This is a team of people who are all used to being the most important person in the room, which is exactly why it works and exactly why it keeps nearly falling apart.

You have never had the luxury of being thanked for this. The X-Men protect a world that has never once been comfortable with them existing, and something in you understands exactly why they keep doing it anyway.

You do not want colleagues, you want your people. The Titans are the team where the friendship came first and the world-saving turned up afterwards as an inconvenient side effect.

You are not asking who to trust. You worked out the answer a while ago and it was nobody in a cape. This is where you go once you have decided the people in charge need watching, and that nobody else is going to do it.

"Look, there's lots of fantastic storylines. There's always a lot of opportunity... I’ve had such a wonderful time playing Superman in the past. I would never say never now."

The reality is that, right now, we don't know much about what the future of the DCU is going to look like. There are reports that Gunn will continue to write and direct movies at DC, but also reports that the studio is looking to take things in a different direction. It won't be until Skydance, the new name for the merged company formed by Warner Bros and Paramount, comes out with a public statement that we'll have some idea of what the future at DC holds.
