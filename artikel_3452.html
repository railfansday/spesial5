Inside Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando's 'Intimate' and Untraditional Wedding

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Miley Cyrus and husband Maxx Morando decided to forgo a “go big or go home” attitude for their wedding, opting for a smaller ceremony with loved ones.

“It was a really intimate wedding, and there weren’t any big celebrity guests outside of her family,” a source exclusively tells Us of the couple’s Saturday, November 21, nuptials.

The insider notes, “It wasn’t a typical Hollywood or traditional wedding, but was everything Miley wanted.”

The Hannah Montana alum, 34, has grown up under the bright lights of both Hollywood and Nashville, but for her special day she chose the quieter backdrop of Palm Springs, California.

“She wanted it small and more relaxed,” the source says. “It was mostly just family and their closest friends.”

Cyrus was in great spirits on her big day, according to the insider, who tells Us, “Miley was super emotional and genuinely so happy the entire weekend. She had the biggest smile on her face the whole time.”

Whether it’s their wedding day or just another Tuesday, the source adds that Morando, 28, “really balances” Cyrus out and “brings a sense of calm to her life.”

“They both just light up around each other, and you can tell how happy they are together,” the insider says.

Us has reached out to reps for Cyrus and Morando for comment.

Cyrus and Morando began dating after meeting on a blind date in the summer of 2021.

Us confirmed in December 2025 that the couple were engaged after the “Flowers” singer was spotted wearing a cushion cut diamond ring at the Avatar: Fire and Ash premiere in Los Angeles. An insider told Us at the time that Morando popped the question one month prior.

One year after their engagement, Cyrus and Morando said “I do” in Palm Springs on Saturday, with Miley’s mom, Tish Cyrus, and sisters Brandi and Noah Cyrus among those in attendance.

The “Let’s Get Married” singer’s father, Billy Ray Cyrus, was also on hand to celebrate the occasion with girlfriend Elizabeth Hurley. Tish, 59, walked her daughter down the aisle, while Miley enjoyed a special dance with Billy Ray, 65, according to reports.

Miley and Morando apparently enlisted her frequent collaborator Wayne Coyne, frontman of the Flaming Lips, to officiate the poolside ceremony at a private residence in California’s desert.

Miley’s late godmother Dolly Parton’s 1974 song “Love Is Like a Butterfly” was played during the wedding, according to Entertainment Tonight. (Parton died in August at the age of 80.)

The bride shared the first glimpse at her special day via social media on Sunday, November 22, posting a photo of the newlyweds.

“M + M 🤍 11.21.26,” Miley captioned the black-and-white photo, which showed off her white long-sleeved bridal gown with a dramatic open back and Chopard jewelry. The groom looked handsome in a classic tuxedo.

Both Miley and Morando’s wedding looks were Saint Laurent by Anthony Vaccarello.
