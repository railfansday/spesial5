3 Best Prime Video Movies To Watch This Weekend (Nov 14-15)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

November is already proving to be a popular month in theaters. As A24's viral documentary sensation, You Can See Everything, LAIKA's new animated masterpiece, Wildwood, and Olivia Colman's bonkers fantasy film Wicker continued to prove popular on the big screen last weekend, Alessandro Carloni and Erica Rivinoja's new Cat in the Hat movie and writer/director Takashi Yamazaki's Godzilla Minus Zero crashed onto the box office scene and stole the top two spots in the North American ranks. As another weekend approaches, you needn't get your movie fix with a trip to the theater. Here's a look at three Prime Video movies you should watch this weekend.

For more recommendations, check out our list of the best shows and movies on Prime Video.

Halloween might be over, but Stephen King's presence in your streaming rotation shouldn't be going anywhere. With that in mind, why not check out 2007's The Mist this weekend, based on King's 1980 novella, which was first included in the horror anthology Dark Forces. Set in the small town of Bridgton, Maine, the film follows a group of locals, including artist David Drayton (Thomas Jane), who become trapped when a sinister mist descends.

Written and directed by Frank Darabont and starring the likes of Marcia Gay Harden, Laurie Holden, Andre Braugher, and Toby Jones, this mid-noughties horror gem will chill you to your core as this politically relevant tale twists and turns towards a jaw-dropping finish. Featuring monsters designed by horror comics artist Bernie Wrightson and heaps of tension, you won't forget The Mist anytime soon.

For the perfect popcorn flick to add sparkle to your November weekend, look no further than one of the greatest adventure films of all time. Raiders of the Lost Ark, created by the winning combination of Steven Spielberg and George Lucas, stars Harrison Ford as Indiana Jones, who must race against both time and the Nazis to recover the biblical Ark of the Covenant.

Packed with typically Spielbergian ambition and heart, this iconic family favorite will never fail to brighten any weekend. One of Ford's most beloved performances features a smorgasbord of memorable moments, from the gun in a sword fight to the boulder chase. Raiders of the Lost Ark was the recipient of four Academy Awards, including Best Sound, Best Film Editing, and Best Visual Effects.

On weekends, we wear pink. This weekend, don't spend hours scrolling for what to watch when the most iconic chick flick of all time is ready and waiting for you on Prime Video. Mean Girls, written by Tina Fey and directed by Mark Waters, follows Lindsay Lohan’s Cady Heron as she joins a new school and immediately makes an impact with the A-list girl clique. However, when a mistake is made, all hell breaks loose.

Arguably one of the most quotable movies ever, Mean Girls is jam-packed with memorable moments that have been lovingly parodied countless times since. Rachel McAdams, Amanda Seyfried, Lacey Chabert, and more join Lohan in a perfectly assembled cast, as they combine to not just craft one of the best teen films of this century, but one that will forever define the genre.
