Spider-Man’s Dark Reboot Universe Unveils Twisted New Design For Classic Villain

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Warning! This post contains SPOILERS for Midnight Spider-Man #2

Marvel's new Midnight Universe has begun, featuring dark and horrific new takes on classic heroes in an alt universe unlike anything we've seen before. However, the latest issue of Midnight Spider-Man has confirmed that villains are receiving just as terrifying redesigns as well.

As seen in the first issue of Midnight Spider-Man, this dark new continuity's Peter Parker has become a truly grotesque David Cronenberg-style body-horror version of a human-spider hybrid, the result of twisted Oscorp experiments in the pursuit of eternal life. Contrasting with the classic Spider-Man motto, "With great power comes great responsibility", the darker world and circumstances surrounding Peter have instead instilled the phrase: "power makes monsters." Now that Peter has become a monster himself, his future looks to be all kinds of horrifying.

Now, Midnight Spider-Man #2 by Phillip Kennedy Johnson and Scietronc has debuted a brand-new version of Doctor Octopus, one that's easily more disturbing than any version seen before in the pages of Marvel Comics.

The story in Midnight Spider-Man #2 is aptly titled "The Eight-Handed Man," giving readers our first look at Otto Octavius and his role as a lead researcher at Oscorp behind the Augustine Project and the company's mission to achieve eternal life via twisted experiments involving animal-human hybrids. Initially shown as a reclusive germaphobe who hates interacting with foreign contaminants and individuals outside his lab, an incredibly diminutive Otto keeps himself isolated behind glass with various metal pins sticking out of his head.

The lab itself is full of dissection tables with various genetically mutated specimens that have gruesomely been ripped apart by his "assistants". Rather than having his traditional four robotic arms, this dark new version of Doctor Octopus controls four human beings through cybernetic brain implants somewhat resembling the classic tentacles. All of their individual humanity looks to have been stripped away, leaving them as nothing more than remote-controlled extensions of Octavius. Likewise, Octavius even threatens to turn Felicia Hardy into his next assistant should she fail to recover the spider that escaped in the new series' first issue.

Having wound up in Peter's backpack, the large radioactive spider ultimately fused with Peter and embedded itself in his chest. Currently unaware of this monstrous transformation, Otto desperately wants the spider back, and for Felicia to ensure that Peter signs a contract of employment to start working at Oscorp itself.

Ultimately, this super dark intro for Midnight Doc Ock makes it clear that as monstrous as Peter Parker might have just become in this dark new universe, Oscorp is the true and much bigger monster behind it all.

Nevertheless, it's extremely unsettling to see "Spider-Man" launching himself at Felicia Hardy (perhaps a future Midnight Black Cat?) and her men when they come to Peter's house, ripping the security guards to shreds with all kinds of blood and gore, leaving the house with only Felicia alive and paralyzed with fear. Clearly, this is going to be one of the scariest and most violent Spider-Man ever seen.

With the first issue strongly implying that the spider killed Uncle Ben before fusing with Peter, the grief and horror that will come once Peter discovers this will likely be what fully turns him against Oscorp (he had been more optimistic about joining them and turning them into a force for good from within, despite Uncle Ben suing them for their involvement in the deaths of Peter's parents).

Assuming Peter will find a way to control or at least direct his monstrous impulses toward those responsible, it follows that Midnight Spider-Man will likely wage a one-man war against Oscorp in future issues. As such, his new status as a human-spider hybrid will no doubt be seen as a major breakthrough by Octavius, making a confrontation between the two more than inevitable in the issues to come.

Midnight Spider-Man #2 is on sale now from Marvel Comics.
