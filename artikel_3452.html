LEGO’s 5,363-Piece ‘Godzilla’ Set Officially Arrives for Black Friday With a Free Gift

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Given how much of Godzilla's appeal over the last seven decades has been predicated on the big guy's ability to reduce entire cities to rubble, there's something quite amusing about the prospect of spending hours building him out of little plastic bricks. We've seen LEGO give us some wonderfully elaborate recreations of locations from our favorite movies over the years, although in this instance, it's probably best not to display your latest acquisition too close to any of those buildings. And if you've been wondering what to get the Godzilla fan in your life for Christmas, LEGO has come up with something that should keep them occupied well into the new year.

As of today, November 27, you can get your hands on LEGO's 5,363-piece Godzilla set for $449.99, exclusively through the LEGO Store. Based on the version of Godzilla we've seen in the MonsterVerse movies, the finished model stands more than 27.5 inches tall, and there are a few different ways you can pose him once you've put him together. You can even have him firing his atomic breath, while everything from his tail to his individual fingers can be repositioned.

What's particularly impressive about the design is the amount of detail that's gone into recreating Godzilla's scales, given that so much of the creature is essentially the same color. The blue dorsal plates running all the way down his back and along his tail give the model a little more color, and if you'd prefer to have Godzilla on display without his atomic breath, you can replace the piece protruding from his mouth with a red tongue. The set is designed for builders aged 18 and over.

As has become something of a tradition with LEGO's major Black Friday releases, there's an additional set available to those who purchase Godzilla over the course of the holiday weekend. The 359-piece Godzilla Logo & Footprint set is available as a gift with purchase between November 27 and November 30, while supplies last. Designed to be displayed alongside the considerably larger Godzilla model, the accompanying set won't be available to purchase separately.

Of course, given that Godzilla himself measures more than two feet tall, 10.5 inches wide, and 15.5 inches long, prospective buyers might want to spend a little time figuring out where they're going to put him before they start building. Between the two sets, those who manage to get their hands on the Black Friday exclusive will have a combined 5,722 pieces to work their way through over Christmas.
