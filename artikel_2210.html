How Dave Challinor has reacted to shock Sheffield Wednesday loss to Oldham

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Dave Challinor’s reaction to Sheffield Wednesday’s FA Cup defeat to Oldham Athletic has received a big reaction from supporters.

The new Owls boss oversaw his first game in charge on Sunday, with the team suffering defeat away to the League Two side.

Calum Kavanagh put the Latics ahead in the 66th minute, but Davis Keillor-Dunn responded to bring the game to extra-time.

But Will Sutton’s strike in the second half of the extended period gave the hosts a 2-1 advantage that Challinor’s side were unable to overcome.

The Yorkshire outfit have been eliminated from the FA Cup in the first round, highlighting the size of the task at hand for the new head coach.

The League One side will be aiming to compete for promotion back to the Championship this season, having brought in the former Stockport County boss in place of Henrik Pedersen.

Challinor has expressed his disappointment at the performance from his Sheffield Wednesday side, even though he knew that there was never going to be immediate progress after just a few days in charge.

He criticised the first half performance for being too passive, before praising the improvement in the second half, despite ultimately conceding two goals to suffer defeat.

“Really disappointed,” said Challinor, via the club’s official X account.

“It was always going to be the case, it was never going to be linear in terms of what progress looks like.

“Probably knew things coming into the game, and coming into the club, and that’s reinforced those things, and the things that need to change.

“We’re in a position, we’re a little bit hamstrung in terms of the team we can pick, and who can play certain minutes, and all those types of things, which is almost like pre-season, not middle of November in the middle of a League One season.

“First half, miles too passive, like a testimonial.

“And players maybe being a little bit protective because of what the back end of the game looks like.

“Second half, much, much better, having our best spell and obviously concede two really poor goals, but that’s nothing to take away from Oldham, who deservedly go through.”

Challinor highlighted a particular issue with fitness within the squad, suggesting that standards have to improve if players are going to reach the goals they’ve set out for their careers.

“Great reaction to that, and we’re on the front foot, and like I say there will be pleasing stuff from the second half,” he continued.

“The extra time period, you know what it’s like, it can go either way.

“Physically, we’ve got to be miles better.

“You look at, I’ll use Barry [Bannan] as an example, but he’s 36-years-old and he’s running around all over the place, and we’ve got players that will be aspirational in terms of where they want to go and play that are getting cramps left, right and centre.

“Dish has probably got a good excuse for that, he’s not played, but the rest, if we want to play like what we all agree a Sheffield Wednesday team should look like, and be front-foot, we need to have those behaviours every single day.

“In order when we come out here and play, the supporters see and we represent them in a better form than what we did tonight.”

Sheffield Wednesday are now winless in their last five games in all competitions, with their last victory coming against AFC Wimbledon on 17 October.

Sheffield Wednesday fans have backed Challinor’s assessment of the game and the squad’s fitness issues.

Supporters are behind the new manager, suggesting that they think he’s surprised at the fitness levels of the players.

Fans are optimistic that the 51-year-old is going to raise standards, believing that the players have had it easy for far too long.

The supporters are also pleased to see that the new manager is giving an honest assessment of the game, instead of offering empty platitudes.

Many are hopeful that Challinor will now work on improving the overall fitness level of the squad, with some wondering if the issue has caused the injury problems of the recent past.

Fans are willing to give the former Stockport boss time to turn things around, claiming that it will be a slow process to fix the problems with the team.

Supporters believe that Challinor knows what he’s talking about, and are encouraged by his angry reaction to the loss.

Next up for the Owls in the league is a home clash against Plymouth Argyle on 14 November in a 3pm kick-off.

The defeat to Oldham has revealed to Challinor the size of the task at hand, and he knows it won’t be a quick process in turning things around.

Sheffield Wednesday reached a significant low point last season, and it was always naive to think that it could all be undone in just one year.

But Challinor is an exciting appointment because he knows what it takes to compete to a high standard at this level, and he can reintroduce that to the club after a challenging year in which the Owls came out of administration.

Despite the loss, fans seem optimistic about the future under the new manager, and these comments have backed that belief.
