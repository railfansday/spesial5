Derby County need to target winter swoop for 25-year-old after Blackburn Rovers summer interest: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Derby County are in the Championship relegation zone and have struggled for points. DCFC will get the chance to bolster their squad when the January transfer window opens up for business.

Dundee United left-back Will Ferry was heavily linked with a move to Blackburn Rovers in the summer. However, nothing materialised on that front in the end and he remained in Scotland beyond the deadline in September.

The Rams should now turn their attention to the defender and try and lure him to Pride Park this winter. Ferry, 25, is out of contract at the end of the season and his current club risk losing him for free in June 2027 if they don’t sell him in January, assuming he doesn’t pen an extension anytime soon.

The Bury-born man has made 91 appearances for Dundee United in all competitions to date, eight of which have come in this campaign, and he has chipped in with five goals and an eye-catching 15 assists from the back. He has a nice left foot and should move back down to England soon for a new challenge in his career.

Derby County currently have Craig Forsyth and Derry Murkin in the treatment room. Therefore, DCFC could do with another option in their position on the left flank of defence.

The Rams have had to play the likes of Ryan Hedges and Ryan Nyambe there over recent times. Ferry would be ideal for them now and could plug a gap for the time being.

The Republic of Ireland international, who has one cap to his name, has proved himself up in Scotland. He likes to put crosses into the box as much as possible and attacking players like Carlton Morris and Patrick Agyemang (when he’s fit) will benefit from his delivery.

Ferry rose up through Southampton’s academy before he was loaned out to Crawley Town as a youngster to get some experience under his belt. He then had two permanent years at Cheltenham Town before Dundee United came calling for his services.

Dundee United will want to receive a fee of some sort for Ferry and not lose him for nothing next year. Derby County should try and test their resolve.

The Rams can’t afford to get their recruitment wrong in January and need the right type of characters. At 25, Ferry’s next move is a big one and he needs to go somewhere he is guaranteed regular minutes.

Up next for Derby County is a clash away against Ferry’s old team Southampton after the international break. The Saints are eyeing promotion to the Premier League under Tonda Eckert.
