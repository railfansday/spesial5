D2B: Draftsperson / Construction Drawings Specialist

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Position : Draftsperson / Construction Drawings Specialist

Working Hours & Conditions : 8 Hours per day 5 days per week

Holidays : Applicants local holidays apply

The company is a Melbourne-based landscape design studio founded by Drew Burns, specialising in native and climate-adaptive gardens for residential clients across Melbourne and the Mornington Peninsula. The client's philosophy is simple: every project starts with a clear plan that resolves layout, levels, materials, and planting before anything is built - so construction happens without guesswork, miscommunication, or costly rework.

The studio's process runs in two stages: a concept report developed locally with the client, followed by detailed construction drawings. We're growing our team to bring the construction drawing stage in-house on an offshore basis, freeing up our local design team to focus on new client work while drawings progress in the background.

We're looking for a skilled CAD draftsperson to take finalised landscape design concepts and turn them into accurate, buildable construction drawing sets. This is a genuine long-term team role, not a one-off gig - we're investing in someone who wants to grow with the business, not just complete tasks.

The ideal candidate is confident in producing both construction drawings and photorealistic renders, giving us a single point of contact who can carry a project from concept through to construction-ready documentation.

Technical skill matters, but it's not the only thing we're hiring for. We value:

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
