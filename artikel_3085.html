United Microelectronics: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Higher margins depend on navigating depreciation and raw material headwinds that could pressure profitability even as pricing power improves.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 28, 2026, United Microelectronics (UMC +6.92%) announced an additional $3.2 billion capital expenditure budget to accelerate facility expansion. For long-term investors, expanding production capacity for specialized semiconductor nodes positions the business to capture growing artificial intelligence demand.

Customer demand is accelerating while manufacturing capacity stays tight across key production nodes. Higher value per wafer and an improved technology mix will drive higher average selling prices (ASP, the average revenue earned per manufactured wafer).

Customer qualifications for silicon photonics (optical networking technology using light on silicon) and advanced packaging (methods for connecting multiple semiconductor dies) are moving faster than management expected. The company is directing capital expenditure (CapEx, money spent on physical infrastructure) toward facilities in Singapore and Taiwan to meet customer commitments.

Demand for specialized power management and optical connectivity chips is rising alongside next-generation server architectures. Management projects that artificial intelligence (AI, computer systems performing complex tasks) exposure will expand significantly from its current $300 million base.

Factory utilization reached 92% as core node demand remained steady. The company relies on its specialized 22-nanometer nodes to win customer commitments. Management is watching higher depreciation costs and rising raw material expenses.

Targeted investments in optical connectivity position United Microelectronics to expand its manufacturing share among leading chip designers.
