Why GeneDx Holdings Stock Just Dropped 16.6%

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

GeneDx met Wall Street's revenue target but missed on test volume and trimmed its volume growth outlook, and a stock priced on growth is paying for it.

GeneDx Holdings (WGS -16.57%) stock fell 16.6% on Tuesday, Nov. 10, after the biotech company lowered its growth targets for the year.

The S&P 500 and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 0.4% and 0.7%, respectively.

The genetic testing company GeneDx reported Q3 results on Monday after the bell. Sales jumped 5% to $123.1 million -- in line with what Wall Street was looking for -- and margins improved slightly. Unfortunately, testing volume lagged expectations. The company delivered 32,134 exome and genome results -- the DNA tests that are its core business -- up 25%. The company had promised 29.2% or more.

Management says that certain upfront paperwork is screening out some lower-value orders in favor of ones that pay better. The company kept its revenue forecast intact but cut its volume growth target to a minimum of 28%.

This is a good example of why expectations are everything in the stock market. The company is still growing, but not as quickly as expected. With two downward growth revisions this year now, I understand why investors are pricing in a third.
