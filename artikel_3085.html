Stereotaxis, Inc. (STXS -9.65%) reported third quarter results that focused on two structural transformations: a shift toward more accessible robotic capital systems and the expansion of its recurring revenue through a proprietary portfolio of interventional catheters. Management reported that revenue growth was driven by the initial commercial impact of the GenesisX and Synchrony systems, even as supply constraints for the MAGIC catheter created a $1.9 million order backlog. The company stated that the acquisition of Robocath has closed, adding a mechanical robotic system designed for cardiology and neurointerventional fields to its portfolio. To support these transitions, the company secured up to $24 million in convertible note financing, providing a pro-forma cash balance of $16 million and a total liquidity of $28 million. Management indicated that it expects a pathway to cash-flow profitability within the next year, supported by stabilized operating expenses of approximately $8 million per quarter.
