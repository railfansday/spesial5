What does the "best mattress deals" mean?

Before I delve into each of the best Black Friday mattress deals, I want to start by clarifying what I mean by the "best mattress deals".

I consider the best mattress deals to A) feature a mattress that was awarded three stars or above by our expert team or online customers and B) meet at least one of the following criteria:

1. It isn't the usual discount — it's better. For example, a mattress that is usually 20% in the regular monthly mattress sales suddenly dropping to 30% off during the Black Friday period.

2. It's the usual discount, but with a free gift as a Black Friday bonus. For instance, a mattress stuck at the usual price, but with a free gift (such as a bedding bundle or gift card) worth $50 or more.

3. It's the usual discount, but still outstanding value for money. For example, a mattress in a price tier that's beneath its actual performance (such as a luxury mattress regularly sold at a mid-range price).
