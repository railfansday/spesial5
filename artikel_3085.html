Sezzle Profit Jumps 79% as Credit Costs Ease: Rapid Earnings Reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The buy now, pay later lender is squeezing more profit out of each transaction. Here's what's driving it and the one number to track through the holidays.

Sezzle (SEZL +11.65%) grew third-quarter net income 79% to $47.8 million, or $1.38 per diluted share. Shares rose about 10% in after-hours trading. The bigger story is cost: Sezzle now earns far more on each dollar it handles, because revenue is growing faster than volume while the costs tied to each loan barely grow.

Management raised its full-year outlook for the fourth time and set a first 2027 target of $7.20 in adjusted earnings per share. The release notes that older guidance wasn't recast for a change in tax treatment, so the raises aren't directly comparable.

Watch whether credit losses hold near the new target of about 2.5% of volume through the holiday quarter. SezzleCash cash advances are a newer, less proven product, and their growth will test that number.
