Nvidia Stock Is Down 35% From Its High. History Suggests a $5,000 Investment Now Will Be Worth This Much by 2028.

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Nvidia has had a rough few days, but the future is still bright.

By Keithen Drury – Nov 21, 2026 at 4:55AM EST

Nvidia (NVDA -14.01%) stock has gotten slammed over the past few weeks. With former CEO and co-founder Jensen Huang resigning from his position, the market has grown uncertain about Nvidia's future. Furthermore, some of Nvidia's practices have drawn increased scrutiny, which is also adding to fears.

After the sell-off, Nvidia is down over 35% from its all-time high. Since the AI arms race kicked off in 2023, this is now the deepest sell-off Nvidia has seen, just surpassing the tariff-fear sell-off in April 2025. The market has made its opinion known, but does Nvidia actually deserve to be sold off this much? Or is this a generational buying opportunity?

Let's take a look at where the stock could be by 2028, and if now is the perfect time to buy.

With Nvidia's sell-off, it's now down over $2 trillion from its all-time high market cap. So, is Jensen Huang really worth $2 trillion to Nvidia's market cap?

I think the market has way overblown this sell-off. Nvidia’s visionary leader definitely helped shape and drive the company. However, there are still thousands of employees who run the day-to-day operations and designs that Huang had nothing to do with. The real risk is if these employees start to quit or resign from their roles and that knowledge is lost. If there is a mass exodus from Nvidia alongside Huang, that could represent a huge risk for investors.

Until we're a few months past Huang's resignation, the market is likely to stay cautious, which is one reason why Nvidia's stock price is down.

Another is the future. Huang's vision for where AI computing and GPUs will go placed Nvidia in a leadership role in the AI build-out. That will have driven product development over the next couple of years, but what comes after that is unknown. This is where a new CEO will need to have a great technical mind and vision for what's coming next. The wrong person at the helm could cause Nvidia's leadership position to collapse in the future if they cannot replicate Huang’s success.

As a result, the market is once again discounting the stock until a new leader is selected.

Lastly, there are concerns that Nvidia’s chips are potentially circumventing export restrictions to China, with some restricted products funneled through Southeast Asian countries, before eventually making their way into China. These are all allegations right now, but Nvidia hasn't been cleared of any wrongdoing yet, so once again, the market is discounting the stock.

Nvidia has a lot of unknowns right now, and the market has kept it in a low-valued spot as a result. The question is, if these are resolved, what could it be worth?

Nvidia has guided for 75% revenue growth in fiscal year (FY) 2028 (ending January 2028), thanks to the strength of the AI build-out. That's up from the 70% guidance given last quarter. With Nvidia estimated to generate around $424 billion in revenue this year, that would indicate revenue of $742 billion next year. At Nvidia's 62% profit margin, the company would generate $460 billion in profits.

Even if the stock traded at the same trailing earnings multiple it does now, that would give it a market cap of $7.4 trillion -- more than doubling today's levels and turning $5,000 into at least $10,000.

However, I think that is a dirt cheap price tag in and of itself, and most of Nvidia's problems will be solved by the end of next year. As a result, the market could value it like any other big tech stock and price it around 20 to 25 times earnings. That would result in an even larger market cap and make Nvidia a no-brainer buy today.
