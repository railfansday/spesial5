Roborock’s Qrevo Edge 3 Pro delivers room-to-room cleaning. It climbs up to 1.77 inches over single-layer thresholds and 3.46 inches over double-layer thresholds, and handles carpets up to 1.81 inches thick with automatic mop detachment. With 3.13 inches of clearance, it also easily reaches under beds and sofas. Plus, it’s designed with advanced obstacle avoidance, 36,000 of suction power and a dual zero-tangle system.

The Qrevo Edge 2 is Roborock's vacuum and mop combo combines powerful suction (25,000 Pa), advanced edge cleaning, and intelligent navigation with reactive AI Obstacle Recognition to deliver the ultimate in home vacuums. The upgraded mopping system with 8N downward pressure tackles tough dried stains and its fast-absorbing design leaves minimal water for quick drying, making it safe and gentle on hardwood floors.

The Roborocks Qrevo S Pro robot vacuum and mop delivers 18,500 of suction power, plus dual spinning mop pads that automatically lift off carpet. The Roborock Q7 M5+ delivers 10,000 Pa of HyperForce suction to lift pet hair and debris from every surface, including the edges, and the Dual-Anti-Tangle system keeps hair wrap to a minimum.
