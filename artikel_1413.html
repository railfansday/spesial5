Corinthians executive discusses Roma’s interest in Samuel Lino: “I can’t say much, but it will require a high offer.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Flamengo’s sporting director José Boto may be back in talks with Roma.

Following Wesley’s transfer from the Rossoneri to the capital, the same scenario could happen with Samuel Lino.

The Roman club, seeking an attacking winger, has set its sights on the former Atlético Madrid player.

The 1999-born player made a name for himself in Europe with the Madrid club and Valencia, before returning to his homeland where he achieved impressive results: in 2026, he scored 16 goals and provided 13 assists.

Gian Piero Gasperini is looking for a clinical, high-performance player, and the Brazilian fits the bill.

The Rio club’s director commented on the Giallorossi’s interest in his player to Tuttomercatoweb.com.

“There is indeed interest from several clubs in Samuel Lino. I won’t say whether Roma is among them or not, but I can confirm that the interest surrounding him is real and that requests for information about the player continue to arrive every day. Lino is a player we acquired when he was still at a very favorable age for a possible resale. We paid €25 million for him, and it’s obvious we won’t sell him for anything less. I don’t even know if he’ll be possible to sell unless a very high offer arrives.”
