OM-Levski Sofia, the match broadcast on a free channel?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Marseille must continue. Winner in Leverkusen of their first match of the season in the Europa League (1-0), after three defeats in a row against Besiktas (1-4), Olympiakos (0-1) and Sturm Graz (0-1), OM still have their chances in the race for the top 24 in the league phase, synonymous with qualification for the play-offs. But he must now pass the second.

And it’s a great opportunity to achieve additional success that presents itself to Bruno Genesio’s players. Thursday evening, the Olympians host a team from Levski Sofia which, on paper, seems affordable. However, be wary because the Bulgarian club is tenth in the standings with 7 points and remains on two wins ahead of Plzen (2-1) and Jagiellonia (1-0).

The Ciel et Blanc, who lost on Sunday evening in Ligue 1 against Le Mans (2-3), know it: they have no room for error. “It’s a European Cup match, they are always close matches, difficult to windeclared the Marseille coach. We must not fear this adversary. We respect him but we must be able to impose our game.”

However, due to its schedule, this poster will not be broadcast free of charge on the group’s new digital channel, Sport+. In its multi-live, accessible by creating an account on the Canal+ application, this platform will only offer matches starting at 9 p.m., in particular Lyon-Lilleström and Nijmegen-Rennes.
