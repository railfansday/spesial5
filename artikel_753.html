Florence Pugh Says It’s “Disturbing Having My Body Dissected,” Slams Surgery and Ozempic Accusations: “Be Mindful”

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Oscar nominee said that it's been "so stressful waking up and have thousands of people talking about you and your body in ways that you can’t even comprehend."

Florence Pugh is speaking out about recent conversations that have emerged online about her body, pertaining to speculation that she’s had surgery and is on Ozempic.

The East of Eden star took to Instagram on Wednesday, writing that she finds it “so stressful waking up and have thousands of people talking about you and your body in ways that you can’t even comprehend. I’m aware that the culture on socials currently is very interested in talking about people’s surgery and ozempic changes, it’s clearly a part of our time right now, that there’s this frenzy of people picking apart what’s truth and what’s fake.” Related Stories News Carrie Coon Calls for Women to Start Their Own Studio as Elle Women in Hollywood Honorees Push for Change Movies Florence Pugh Celebrates Robert Pattinson's Bond Casting: "He's Been the No. 1 Vote in My Household"

“I woke up the other morning and quite a lot of you were discussing my face and body in such a disturbing way. I learnt online that I had been on ozempic? I learnt that I’d had upper bleph? Lower bleph? Filler? Face lipo? Buccal fat remover? Chin surgery? Nose surgery?!” Pugh continued, slamming the accusations.

The actress said she is “disturbed” by “having my body dissected and pointed at again,” and that she has “spent my whole career defending and protecting bodies that just grow the way they grow” while also experiencing fat shaming “from the moment I started and I have loved everytime I get to tell someone to fuck off when they are being inappropriate about my weight, and I’m not changing that now just because you’ve all said so.”

In a recent Instagram video shared by Evie magazine, a woman analyzed Pugh’s face, placing a side-by-side image of her a few years past beside her recent appearance at the 2026 Governors Awards, saying, “People are saying Florence Pugh has fallen victim to Hollywood’s war on round faces. Don’t get me wrong, Florence Pugh still looks beautiful, but I think we can all collectively agree that Hollywood seems to have this thing against softer features.”

The woman referenced Pugh’s “sculpted, snatched look,” and said that the actress used to have “full, beautiful cheeks,” but now that her “jaw is more defined, her chin is more pointier, her jaws more are sharp, it makes sense why people are wondering if she’s had any work done.” The actress herself commented on Evie’s Instagram post, writing, “Guys. I turned 30. I’m a woman. Body’s and faces change. It’s not that deep. The fact that people can’t even tell between what’s aging and what’s surgery is disturbing. Sadly puppy fluff doesn’t last forever.”

The Oscar nominee denied having surgery or taking the weight-loss drug in her Instagram post. “I have never been on ozempic. I have NEVER done any face surgery. And don’t EVER say that I didn’t make my own nose, I’ve always had that beautiful ski slope. Even as a baby!” Pugh shared a photo of herself as a child on her Instagram Story, writing, “I’ve had that baby since day one. No surgery please and thank you very much.”

She further explained that after turning 30, her New Year’s resolution was “to change my relationship with alcohol, and to be able to do at least one pull up in the gym.” The actress also said it’s been “funny to see you all use reference images from times where I was really mentally unhappy and abusing my body and put it next to the image from the governors awards the other night where I am happy and truly respecting my body.”

The Thunderbolts star said that “for the first time in my ENTIRE career, I have had the opportunity to be rested, to sleep, to wake up and work out and to eat regularly and healthily. I’ve WANTED to move my body and not take it for granted. Guess how much weight I’ve lost in all of that? 2 pounds.”

She also explained that she’s been prioritizing growing muscle because “I think it’s the most important thing a woman can do. Build muscle and be strong. I can now do 6 pull ups and lift 110kg. Which I’m sure to many of you isn’t a lot.. but to me?! It’s everything.” On her Instagram Story, Pugh also posted a few videos of her training in the gym.

“I’ve fucking worked so hard to clean my head and clean my body because I don’t want to allow space for negativity in my mind to grow. I’m looking after myself, and that’s literally it,” Pugh wrote, urging her followers to “be mindful” as she’s also previously received negative comments in the past.

“I also want to point out that over the years I have been in the public eye, I have received comments or messages daily about how I look like a pig, that I’m fat and should go on a run, that I shouldn’t be proud of my body, that I SHOULD go on ozempic, that I shouldn’t show my midriff because I shouldn’t be proud of it. (The size of my thighs even made it into a movie review after I played Saraya the WWE wrestler which will never not be mind blowing),” Pugh added.

“I’ve always been aware that seeing a confident woman be happy in her skin is triggering to a lot of people and for long time just accepted that I will receive commentary like that. But now this..? I’m asking us all to just be mindful. We are all real people, living real lives,” she concluded.
