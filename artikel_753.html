Russell Brand Selling “Free Russell” Merch Via His Wellness Business While in Prison

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

He is also receiving backlash for sharing a message from behind bars, delivered by his wife, Laura Brand, on his YouTube channel on Nov. 15 where he claimed he was innocent.

Russell Brand is selling merchandise calling for his freedom while he’s behind bars after being sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison after being found guilty of rape and sexual assault.

It is unclear how long the items have been available for purchase on Reborn, however the earliest reviews on the varying products date to Nov. 16 (he was sentenced Nov. 9). Brand recently received backlash for sharing a message from prison, delivered by his wife, Laura Brand, on his YouTube channel where he claimed he was innocent. “So Russell has asked me to read something to you today,” the actor’s wife said at the start of the video, before looking to a notebook to read her husband’s message. “Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, God bless you for your support. As you know, I am not guilty for the offenses I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening.”

Brand was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison after being found guilty of rape and sexual assault. The trial lasted four weeks at Southwark Crown Court, with the jury finding him guilty on one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault (he was found not guilty on two other rape charges and not guilty of attempted indecent assault).

The actor has three items featuring “Free Russell” available for purchase on the website for Reborn, his wellness business. Every item is available for $34.99; a shirt with the words “Free Russell Brand“; a hat with the same text; and another shirt that reads “Free Russell” with an image of himself.

Upon visiting the Reborn website, images of the merch are displayed on its homepage with text that reads, “Stand with Russell against injustice” and “silenced for speaking out.” After clicking on the “Free Russell” section of the site, the items are labeled as “fundraiser apparel,” with a descriptor that additionally reads, “Silenced for speaking out against injustuce and corruption.”

After the YouTube video was shared, Google, the parent company of YouTube, told BBC News it was “aware of the new content and our teams are investigating,” adding, “We suspended Russell Brand’s channel from the YouTube Partner Program in September 2023. The channel has not been able to monetise on YouTube since then.”

BBC News also reported that Google declined a request from the Ministry of Justice to remove the video, saying none of its rules had been broken.

His message, delivered by his wife in the YouTube video, drew criticism, with Britain’s Justice Secretary Alex Norris calling the broadcast of a statement from Brand while he is behind bars “completely unacceptable.”

“Prisoners are rightly banned from making statements that cause distress to victims,” Norris wrote on X. “This is being urgently investigated, and I expect action to be taken if he is found to have broken the rules.”
