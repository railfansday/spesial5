Lyon dropped by Australia for Test series vs New Zealand

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Nathan Lyon was left out of Australia's 13-player squad for their Test series against New Zealand on December 9 in Perth; Uncapped off-spinner Corey Rocchiccioli comes into the squad; Lyon was omitted from Australia's second Test vs South Africa

Nathan Lyon has been dropped by Australia for their upcoming Test series against New Zealand.

The 39-year-old off-spinner, who has been a mainstay for the Baggy Greens for 13 years, will be replaced by Western Australia's Corey Rocchiccioli.

Lyon has not spoken publicly since Usman Khawaja's explosive Sandpapergate claims were published in his autobiography.

Australia selection chair George Bailey confirmed that his omission was not related to the ongoing saga, and was based on performances.

"Nathan has not quite had the impact we would like with the ball in recent matches," said Bailey.

"We also know he has an extraordinary record and his experience, knowledge and skill can still be valuable to the program we have moving forward."

Other notable omissions include Marnus Labuschagne, Cooper Connolly and Beau Webster, with middle-order batter Jack Clayton being drafted into the squad and is in line to make his debut during the series.

In recent years, Lyon has seen his role with the Test side diminished, with a right hamstring injury, sustained during day five of the third Ashes Test in 2025, ruling him out of Test selection for eight months.

Lyon, who ranks second as Australia's all-time leading wicket-taker behind Shane Warne with 577 wickets from 145 Tests, had previously been dropped for the second Ashes Test at The Gabba and spoke openly about the omission, saying he was "filthy" and "gutted" about being dropped.

Lyon had also been omitted from Australia's second Test against South Africa in October.

Prior to last year's Test at The Gabba, the off-spinner had remarkably not been left out of a Test squad since he made his debut in 2011.

Uncapped off-spinner Rocchiccioli will likely go head-to-head with Scott Boland for a spot in the side to play New Zealand on the fiery Perth pitch in the first Test which starts on December 9.

"Corey has been a strong performer in domestic cricket for the past few seasons and has traits that are well suited to Australian conditions," Bailey said.

Under-scrutiny skipper Pat Cummins will captain the side, while Josh Hazlewood, Mitchell Starc and Steve Smith, who have all found themselves at the centre of Australia's recent Sandpapergate saga, were also named in the team.

Smith had missed Australia's final Test against South Africa in Cape Town with a neck injury.

Australia will add one final player after the sixth round of Sheffield Shield matches.

Pat Cummins (c), Scott Boland, Alex Carey, Jack Clayton, Cameron Green, Josh Hazlewood, Travis Head, Josh Inglis, Michael Neser, Matthew Renshaw, Corey Rocchiccioli, Steve Smith, Mitchell Starc.

Second Test: December 17-21, Adelaide Oval

Third Test: December 26-30, MCG, Melbourne
