A subscription to Philo Essential, which now comes with Bravo, gets you access to live TV from cable channels for just $25 per month.

In fact, the live TV streaming platform features more than 70 cable networks, such as AMC, A&E, Animal Planet, BBC America, Cartoon Network, CMT, Comedy Central, Discovery Channel, EarthX, Food Network, Game Show Network, Hallmark Channel, HGTV, History Channel, Investigation Discovery, IFC, Lifetime, Logo, MTV, MotorTrends, Nickelodeon, OWN, Paramount Network, Pop TV, Revolt, Smithsonian Channel, Sundance TV, TLC, UPtv, VH1, Vice, We TV and many others. Plus, Philo has more than 130 FAST channels (Free Ad-Supported Streaming Television) available to watch too.

Meanwhile, AMC+, Discovery+ and HBO Max are available with Philo Bundle+, which goes for $33 per month. It comes with access to over 70 cable networks, as well as even more movies and TV shows on AMC+ which features Mad Men, Breaking Bad, Better Call Saul, Gangs of London, Interview with the Vampire, Show Me More, Deadstream and more.

Meanwhile, HBO Max also features originals, such as Hacks, House of the Dragon, The Rehearsal and The Last of Us; all things HBO, like Succession, Veep and Industry; popular movies, including Sinners, One Battle After Another, Dune and Dune 2, Barbie, Wonka and others. It’s the home to exclusive Music Box documentaries, such as Yacht Rock: A DOCKumentary, Woodstock ’99: Peace, Love and Rage, Juice WLRD: Into The Abyss, DMX: Don’t Try to Understand and much more.

Starting on Thursday (Nov. 5), you can now watch Bravo on Philo. The network is available to stream on Philo Essential with the service’s seven-day free trial.
