How to produce Huge Nutrifruits in Probably Stolen

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

While trying to make a living in Probably Stolen, players can unlock the Green Thumb achievement and get a good sum of money at a low cost by producing Huge Nutrifruits.

But as you can already imagine, doing that will require more than just unlocking the Hydroponic System. Here’s how to produce Huge Nutrifruits in Probably Stolen.

You can produce Huge Nutrifruits in Probably Stolen by growing their seeds in a Hydroponic System featuring a bottle of Pure Water infused with a couple of Hydroponic Nutrient Tablets. Just keep in mind that the Pure Water label is only given to containers featuring 99.9% purity.

The Hydroponic System can be acquired via the Retired Farmer Favor (250 Credits), which also gives you two sets of Nutrifruit Seeds and Tablets. It’s also possible to start a run with all the base Hydroponic resources by picking the Hydroponic Farmer specialization.

Upon unlocking the system in any way, an NPC will also begin stopping by your store weekly, offering both extra machinery and a source for both Nutrifruit Seeds and Tablets. The seeds will be pricey and cost around 350 Credits per pack.
