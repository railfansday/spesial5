How to dance on a driveable car in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Weekly quests will have you attempting to check off all kinds of strange tasks in Fortnite. One of the weirder ones you’ll encounter in Treasure Hunters asks you to dance on a driveable car.

Quests like this one can be tough to complete, as you’re tasked with seeking out a specific item around the vast island. If you’re struggling to get this mission done, here’s how to dance on a driveable car in Fortnite.

Drievable cars can be found all around the Fortnite island. Overall, though, they’re most commonly found sitting on roads and parked near main, named POIs. Instead, they only have a chance of appearing in each match, similar to how the spawning for chests works.

To complete the dance on a driveable car quest in Fortnite, you need to locate a vehicle that can be driven, hop on top of it, and perform any dancing emote. As soon as you do, this quest will be complete.

For this mission, you can either simply jump on top of any driveable car, or instead actually enter a Reboot Van, switch to a roof seat, and perform a dancing emote while situated there. Both options work for this quest as long as you’re technically dancing on top of the vehicle.
