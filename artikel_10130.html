The Honor Magic 9 Pro Max is making its global debut today with huge preorder bonuses

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Honor Magic 9 Pro Max launch ushers the company into the world of cinema with an ARRI camera partnership and cutting-edge specs.

The Honor Magic 9 Pro Max is Honor’s most cutting-edge flagship phone to date, and preorders for the global model begin today, November 24, 2026, starting in Malaysia. The phone goes for a retail price of RM5,999 and comes in Shadow Black, Glacier Silver, and Moss Green colorways. Preorders are available at Honor Experience Stores, on Honor Malaysia’s online store, and at a ton of official Honor shops across TikTok, Shopee, Lazada, and more, with further global availability to be announced soon.

That’s a heck of a lot of bonuses. The Magic 9 Pro Max is Honor’s first phone to ship with ARRI camera licensing, and the company has been adamant that this is the most cutting-edge camera experience you’ll find on any smartphone to date. Honor’s phones have had great photography prowess, especially the Magic 7 and 8 series, but the Magic 9 Pro Max improves that with dual 200MP sensors, a fully overhauled video recording experience, and ARRI cinema modes.

The Honor Magic 9 Pro Max also ships with the Honor Imaging Chip H1, a first for the company, as it employs ARRI imaging-based AI algorithms into the RAW domain in real time. Honor also touts a class-leading CIPA 8.0 stabilization score for the 200MP telephoto camera and 200MP main camera, so shaky hands should still produce abundantly clear images and stable video.

The rest of the spec list reads mostly like a power user’s dream come true. It’s powered by a Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 SoC, 12GB of RAM with an additional 12GB of virtual RAM, an 8,000mAh silicon-carbon battery, 100W wired and 80W wireless charging, a third-gen Honor C3 RF chip, a 6.8-inch LTPO OLED display with anti-reflective coating, an anti-scratch NanoCrystal shield, 10,000 nits peak brightness, and 3840Hz PWM dimming. Plus, that fancy new design sports IP68, IP69, and IP69K water and dust-resistance ratings, and the whole thing ships with Magic OS 11 (based on Android 17).
