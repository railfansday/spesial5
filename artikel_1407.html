Marquinhos greets Roma fans in away section

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Giallorossi drew 2-2 at the Parc des Princes against the team that has won the last two Champions League titles. De Roon expressed his satisfaction at the end of the match: “After the 2-1 win, we could have relaxed, but instead we played more. Great performance.”

Gasperini also commented: “I’m proud of the team’s growth; we’re on the right track.”

Among the many former players in the match, in addition to Luis Enrique, was Marquinhos. The Brazilian defender remained on the bench and didn’t play a single minute. However, the 1994-born defender made a point of greeting the Roma fans at the end of the match.

At the end of PSG-Roma (2-2), Marquinhos made a point of greeting the Giallorossi fans who filled the away section of the Parc des Princes.

The former Giallorossi defender hasn’t forgotten his past in the capital. The gesture was particularly appreciated by the Roma fans present, who reciprocated the affection of the 1992-born player: “Marquinhos is one of us.”

Luis Enrique: “This was by far the hardest game so far.”

Gasperini after PSG draw: “I will rewatch it, we were great. Soule is becoming a top player.”

Gasperini: “I’m happy and proud of my team. Castro deserved a chance, Hermoso was brilliant.”
