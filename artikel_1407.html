Correction to: Nature https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11007-3 Published online 23 September 2026

In the version of the article initially published, in the Abstract, “Biogeography” section and Conclusion, the epoch in which bats originated was given as the “late Palaeocene” but should have been “late Cretaceous/early Palaeocene”. This has now been corrected in the HTML and PDF versions of this article.
