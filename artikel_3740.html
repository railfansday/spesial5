'Mormon Wives' Couples Who've Split, Divorced Since Appearing on the Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The cast of The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives has become synonymous with messy breakups, and the drama hasn’t stayed on screen.

Since the Hulu series debuted in 2024, multiple MomTok stars have seen their marriages and relationships unravel in very public ways — complete with divorce filings, custody battles, cheating allegations and tearful TikTok confessionals.

From finalized divorces to ongoing separations, scroll below to see which Mormon Wives couples have called it quits since the series’ premiere:

Jordan Ngatikaura filed for divorce from Jessi Draper in March 2026, months after her emotional cheating scandal with fellow Hulu star Marciano Brunette while filming Vanderpump Villa.

Jessi and Jordan, who share son Jagger and daughter Jovi, signed off on their divorce paperwork in September 2026, closing the book on five years of marriage.

“Now that our divorce has been signed, I’m grateful to have some closure and the opportunity to move forward,” Jordan told Us in a statement. “Jessi and I shared a meaningful chapter of our lives together, and no matter how that chapter ended, I will always be grateful for the good that came from it — especially our children.”

Jessi has since confirmed she’s dating Marciano, who is expected to play a larger role on Mormon Wives season 6.

Taylor Frankie Paul and Dakota Mortensen’s on-again, off-again relationship exploded into a custody war in 2026. The exes, who welcomed son Ever in March 2024, split for good in fall 2025 before Taylor filmed The Bachelorette season 22. (Taylor’s season was scrapped by ABC over resurfaced footage from her 2023 arrest.)

In March 2026, Dakota filed a temporary restraining order, alleging Taylor choked him and shoved him into a window during a February altercation. A judge granted him temporary sole custody of Ever.

Taylor, who denied the accusations, and Dakota were each issued mutual protective orders. She also received visitation rights of Ever. (Taylor also shares two older children with her ex-husband, Tate Paul, with whom she’s also been in a custody battle since June 2026.)

Layla Taylor tearfully confirmed her breakup from boyfriend Mason McWhorter in February 2026 after seven months of dating, insisting via social media there was “no bad blood” between them.

“I genuinely wish and want the best for Mason. Things just weren’t working out,” Layla explained. “I think we were just on two different paths right now. He felt in order for him to grow, he had to be on his own. I just really thought that I was gonna get the family that I wanted so badly.

Layla, who shares two sons with her ex-husband, later publicly came out as bisexual and is now dating Kelsie Chesser.

Mikayla Matthews and husband Jace Terry are still figuring things out after separating on Mormon Wives season 4, which premiered in March 2026.

“I think it’s tricky in this environment, because I also need a lot of support from him going through all of it,” Mikayla exclusively told Us. “It’s kind of one of those things that we’re still navigating, and it still has its ups and still has its downs.”

Season 3 saw the couple, who share four children, open up about intimacy issues that remained unresolved in season 4. Newly postpartum and battling chronic illness, Mikayla was shown packing up her belongings from their home.

“Unfortunately for me, it’s like I’m stuck in this place where my health is out of my control,” she explained. “I think it’s something we’re still working through, and we’re still going to be working through for a while.”

Jen Affleck reportedly filed for divorce from husband Zac Affleck in November 2026, just days before her The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County premiere.

According to court documents obtained by TMZ, Jen filed a petition for divorce in Utah. Jen, who is pregnant with baby No. 4, also submitted a proposed parenting plan.
