Rockstar brought weather back to Leonida.

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

In my first 30 hours of Grand Theft Auto 6, I think I saw it rain once. And that was during a particularly gloomy main story mission where it felt like it was intentionally flipped on and not a serendipitous moment created by the game's weather system.

I was not alone in wondering what was up with the weather in Leonida. GTA 6 players have been confused by the lack of rain and fog since day one, especially after Rockstar boasted about the kind of storms and hurricanes it can create to Game Informer in September.

Apparently, the questioning was justified: The weather system was broken and should now be fixed in a new patch that went out today.

"Fixed an issue that resulted in weather not changing for extended periods," the patch notes say, confirming that, while some players witnessed changes in weather, it was not properly happening at the rate it was intended.

You don't partner with The Weather Channel to post exclusive pre-release screenshots of your game if the forecast is mostly sunny and not actually representative of Florida, the state with the most unpredictable weather in the US. GTA 6 has realistically detailed radio towers that left a TikTok guy who's worked on them in real life gobsmacked. It would've been bizarre if Rockstar neglected to put that kind of effort into a system normies like me would notice within a few hours.

The sun's reign is over and all the other meteorological conditions can take the spotlight. As someone who takes their time doing all the silly little daily routines in Jason and Lucia's life and abiding by traffic laws for the vibes, I'm excited to see how much the weather affects the mood. I'll just have to remember to be a little more discerning when chasing down one of GTA 6's cars to make sure I don't absolutely ruin the interior from a surprise storm.
