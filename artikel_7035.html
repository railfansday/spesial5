Senior Software Engineer, Full Stack (iOS & Backend) at Raya

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

We are seeking a Senior Full Stack Engineer with experience in iOS and backend services to join the Places team to build and elevate member-facing experiences across the Places iOS app. Places is a separate consumer iOS app within Raya. You will work on the whole app, not one part of it: Swift and UIKit on the client, TypeScript and Node.js on the server. You will own features end to end from the UI components to the backend services serving the data.

This is a senior engineering role with deep technical impact across our mobile application and backend. The ideal candidate has built and operated high-scale consumer iOS applications end to end, showing strong ownership from architecture to production execution. You will collaborate closely with product, design, and engineering teams to deliver robust features, maintain software health, and drive technical excellence across the client and server codebases.
