Every Pair of Levi’s Is 40% off for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The best jeans money can buy, and they’re at the best price of the year.

As a woman who writes about men's fashion, guys I meet on dating apps love to ask me what jeans to buy—and I always recommend Levi’s. Specifically, Levi’s 505 jeans. “Not 501s?” one asked frantically. As Luke Guillory, Esquire’s commerce editor, poignantly noted in his Levi’s denim guide, the fit and materials of Levi’s infamous 501s have changed over the years, and “the most straightforward Levi's fit is not that straightforward, nor is it consistent across era.” By comparison, the slightly more modern 505 cut is a safer bet, especially when shopping online during Black Friday.

“I prefer my jeans to hug up top, drape down low. A 505 does that,” Guillory wrote, declaring them Esquire’s pick for the best men’s jeans. “They're universally flattering and classic cut, so you don't have to worry about them going out of style. As a cheap pair of jeans go, it's the best way to go.”

Like many good things, Levi’s 505 jeans are on sale for Black Friday and Cyber Monday. This year, the brand’s entire site (minus a few exclusions) will be 40 percent off from November 23 to December 1, with early access starting November 19 for Red Tab Members. While you can find Levi’s markdowns at Amazon and other major retailers, you’ll find the widest range of washes, inseam lengths, and specialized fits by going straight to the source.

If you haven’t yet, it’s worth shopping Levi’s beyond jeans. The Sherpa Trucker Esquire editors have relied on for years now comes in a fresh corduroy fabric. For reliable workwear pants, try the rugged 568 Carpenter. And don’t sleep on Levi’s Classic Western Denim Shirt, which also makes a great gift.
