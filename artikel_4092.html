Fatal Fury: City Of The Wolves Adds Laocorn From The Motion Picture

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Reminder: The Fatal Fury movie antagonist is now available

Update [Sun 22nd Nov 2026, 4am]: Here's your reminder that Laocorn is now available in Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves on the Switch 2 and other platforms. He rolls out alongside the latest game update (Version 3.4.1).

"From Fatal Fury: The Motion Picture, Laocorn Gaudeamus joins the battle! Discover more about the sinister movie villain who once pushed Terry to his limits in his new Arcade and EOST stories! Now, players can finally wield the awesome power of Mars themselves, only in FATAL FURY: City of the Wolves!"

You can also check out the animated trailer for the relase of this character in the video above. Here's some backstory:

"Still haunted by Sulia's death, Terry finds himself confronted by the ghosts of his past when the Triumvirate suddenly returns. Drawn into a new battle over the power residing within the Armor of Mars, Terry and his companions must fight once again."

Original Story: [Sun 15th Nov, 2026 08:30 GMT]:

SNK's expansion of the Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves roster will continue with the release of Laocorn next week on 19th November 2026.

This character, who was the primary antagonist in the 1994 anime movie Fatal Fury: The Motion Picture, is part of the game's paid Season 3 DLC line up, and is voiced by Branden Loera.

"Primary antagonist in the 1994 anime film Fatal Fury: The Motion Picture. Laocorn and his twin sister Sulia are direct descendants of Gaudeamus, a mighty warrior from antiquity. With his ever-loyal Triumvirate in tow, he relentlessly pursues the legendary Armor of Mars—intent on transcending humanity and ascending to godhood.

"When he discovers the armor's final piece is part of the prize for the KOF tournament, he enters the competition himself. But will claiming the armor bring him the power he craves—or a fate beyond his control?"

You can check out the trailer above (note: the YouTube video uses auto-dubbing, which does not reflect the voice acting in the game). SNK has also released a "digest movie" of the motion picture to help set the scene:

"Fatal Fury: The Motion Picture (1994) Determined to stop her brother, Laocorn Gaudeamus, from completing the forbidden Armor of Mars, Sulia Gaudeamus ventures alone to South Town in search of Terry Bogard, the hero she hopes can thwart Laocorn's dark ambitions."

Laocorn joins Rick Strowd, Duck King, Kim Kaphwan and Manjiro Sano & Ken Ryuguji as part of Season 3. In December, SNK is also teaming up with Tekken 8, which will see the arrival of Reina in the game. If you haven't already played City of the Wolves on Switch 2, there's a demo you can check out on the eShop.
