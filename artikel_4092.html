This weekend I started a game everyone Recognizes

One of many Sony exclusives that I needed to finally try. I've only been playing for a couple of hours and so far my impressions are beyond brilliant. I've gone hunting with the boy, taken a giant stone totem to the face from a troll, punched a man (who wouldn't shut up) through a mountain, thrown him down into the depths of who knows where and even done some flower picking. The story so far feels really special, the bond between Father and son isn't something I get to see in the kind of games I usually play and not being solo makes the journey that much more interesting.

I really like throwing the axe at monsters, the feel of Kratos's raw unrestrained rage as he pounds on things with his big fists, and all while the score booms and echoes in my eardrums. The voice acting is fantastic too, Christopher Judge sounds so intense and gravely, he was made for the role. His delivery is flawless, I wish I could say the same for my combat skills but circle not being dodge is tricky to adjust to. The upgrade system is satisfying, the environments aren't too big or too small. I can't imagine how much of a leap forward it must have been on PS4. The combat is very responsive, everything looks sharp, the textures like skin and fabric really pop, and the vistas themselves are stunning. It's going to be a busy weekend though so the chances of finishing the game so soon are unlikely. Then there's my newest Steamdeck game, my other travel companion.

It's a store simulator with a difference. The aesthetic is similar to Coffee Talk. At night you scavenge for junk and trinkets then during the day you try to flog them to whoever happens to drop by. Other scavengers try to sell and barter/scam you. All manner of gypsys, tramps and thieves make up the customer base and the humour is great too. You can turn wanted felons in for reward money, learn to appraise items for some extra easy money, fleece tourists, or opt out of contraband completely and try to run an above board business (not happening) The upgrade system is the best I've seen in the genre. It's not on console and it needs to be. I also finished The Midnight Walk and loved every minute of it, the story was so moving, the score is now a favourite and the sculpting was so unique. So that's about it really I think Kratos will be good company for a couple of days, Tuesday I'll be surviving Warhammer style and doing something raccoon related to a coin machine for yet even more dopamine doses.
