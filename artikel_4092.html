Nintendo's 'Switch 2 Holiday Experience' Might Be Coming To A City Near You Next Month (US)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Nintendo's making sure that everyone has a Switch 2 on their wishlist this Christmas, as it has today announced the 'Nintendo Switch 2 Holiday Experience', which will be coming to select cities in the US over the next month.

Running from 25th November to 23rd December, the pop-up will feature playable demos for a bunch of Switch 2's finest (Pokopia, Mario Kart World, Ocarina of Time, Splatoon Raiders, and more), and give you a chance to see them all in both handheld and docked mode.

The event will also feature a Nintendo Holiday Gift Guide — because you know kids will leave this one wanting a console of their own — and all attendees can 'check in' at the My Nintendo station to redeem a gift tag set and 100 Platinum Points.
