Hollow Knight: Silksong Forced Indie Dev To Delay, Now They’re Clashing Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Hollow Knight: Silksong was practically the GTA 6 for indie games. Nearly a decade of development, enough hype to send an entire community into an ouroboros of gaslighting, and so many copies sold at launch that it crashed Steam and the Nintendo eShop; releasing a game on the same day, especially a metroidvania, was a death sentence.

So, Aeternum Game Studios delayed Aeterna Lucis by an entire year to steer clear, only to end up launching on the same day as Cherry Time — the showcase dedicated to Silksong's upcoming DLC.

"Dear Team Cherry. A little over a year ago, just as we were about to launch our game, you announced Silksong," the studio said. "We stepped aside, pushed the whole project back a year and, honestly, it was one of the best decisions we've ever made: the genre had an incredible year, and we got to enjoy Silksong as fans and polish Aeterna Lucis into the game we always dreamed it would be.

"Now here we are again, just days from launch. Aeterna Lucis, our new metroidvania, comes out on December 3... and you announce Cherry Time," it continued. "This time we're staying put. Not to go against you, but because a lot of people have been waiting for the Queen of Light for a long time, and we don't want to make them wait any longer. Whatever happens on Thursday, we'll be watching like any other fan. And if Sea of Sorrow ends up landing on the same day, the real winners will be metroidvania fans."

There's been a bit of hubbub on Twitter about whether Team Cherry should offer a courtesy to indie developers privately due to the sheer popularity of its games, but Aeternum has shut down those kinds of comments and assured that this statement is all in good fun. "Thanks to everyone who took this with the same humor we wrote it with," they commented. "If fate insists, let the king and the queen go to the ball together, we're ready."

Silksong could sell half as well as its predecessor, and the series will still outsell all 17 Metroid games combined.

And, as some fans pointed out, Team Cherry did tease post-launch content stretching into 2026, but the timing almost makes me think that somebody put a curse on Aeternum Game Studios. Almost. "We have to ask, with all the love in the world: is it just us, or does the universe have it in for us?" the team mused. "And since you're about to wreck our lives all over again, the least we can do is make a bundle together."

I'd be remiss not to mention that you can wishlist Aeterna Lucis here. There's even a demo available right now if you want to give the game a go before buying it. Sea of Sorrow is completely free, so if you have a spare chunk of change, Aeternum Game Studio's newest release is at least worth considering — especially with how rotten their luck has been.
