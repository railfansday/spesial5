How To Unlock The Snap Gun In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

If you want to be a professional criminal in GTA 6, the Snap Gun is your best friend. Here's how to get and use it.

Robberies are one of the most important aspects of Grand Theft Auto 6's gameplay loop, and they can be quite difficult if you don't approach them carefully and with a bit of planning.

In the early game, Jason and Lucia are small-time criminals who make their lack of experience known, but you can quickly turn them into stealthy professionals by unlocking a key item. Here's what to do.

Grand Theft Auto 6's Leonida has a surprising number of buildings that the player can actually enter, and many of them are teeming with robbery opportunities. In the early hours of the game, however, most buildings are very difficult to quietly enter, as locked doors prevent Jason and Lucia from doing whatever they please.

The most lucrative robberies in GTA 6 are locked behind a key item. Here's how to unlock it.

At first, your only choice for locked doors is to noisily break them open, which draws a lot of attention and makes stealth a no-go in most cases. That changes when you unlock the Snap Gun, which lets you pick the lock of any door in the game and gain access to locations without making a sound.

Like several helpful items in GTA 6, the Snap Gun is given to you as part of the main story, although it's a little more involved than others. To start with, you'll need to complete Chapter 3's Boobie Time mission, which will introduce you to Yasmani, a fence who specialises in jewellery. Yasmani will call you after the mission and task you with completing three jewellery store heists.

Once you complete any one of those three missions and return to Yasmani, he'll give you the Snap Gun as a reward. This device unlocks a new option for locked doors, as you can now choose to use the Snap Gun to unlock them through a minigame that's similar to hotwiring. You'll need to find the sweet spot with the left stick and hit R2 on PlayStation or RT on Xbox to pick the door, which adds a whole new layer to robberies.

The Snap Gun's minigame is unique since you can really just point anywhere and quickly mash R2/RT without any trouble.
