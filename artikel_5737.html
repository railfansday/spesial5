Thornova Solar and Nextpower strengthen strategic cooperation

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Solar module manufacturer Thornova Solar and Nextpower, a provider of advanced solar tracker and structural solutions, marked a milestone in their growing strategic cooperation with the signing of a Master Supply Agreement (MSA).

The agreement, announced at RE+ 2026, further strengthens the collaboration between the two companies, combining Thornova Solar’s U.S.-made solar modules with Nextpower’s U.S.-manufactured steel frame technology. The expanded effort is designed to deliver increased domestic content opportunities and enhanced structural performance, providing greater value to customers developing utility-scale and commercial and industrial (C&I) solar projects across the United States.

According to the companies, integrating advanced steel frame solutions into selected Thornova Solar module series addresses two increasingly important priorities in the U.S. solar market: maximizing domestic content and improving module resilience under demanding environmental conditions.

The solution is particularly relevant for projects in demanding U.S. environments, including high-wind regions across the Midwest and Southeast, where structural reliability and long-term module performance are critical considerations.

“The signing of this Master Supply Agreement represents an important step in strengthening our relationship with Nextpower and expanding the value we deliver to U.S. customers,” said Andrea Bodenhagen, global marketing director of Thornova Solar. “By bringing together U.S.-made solar modules and domestically manufactured steel frame technology, we are helping our customers increase domestic content while benefiting from stronger, more resilient module solutions. This is exactly the kind of collaboration needed to advance a competitive U.S. solar manufacturing ecosystem.”

“We’re excited to deepen our strategic collaboration with Thornova and bring modules featuring Nextpower’s domestically manufactured steel frames to the U.S. market,” said Jenya Meydbray, vice president and general manager of Nextpower. “NX CoreFrame offers a significant opportunity to advance module strength and reliability, addressing industry challenges such as glass breakage and demanding environmental loads while delivering greater confidence to solar developers and owners.”

The MSA reflects the shared commitment of both companies to strengthening domestic manufacturing and delivering solutions that respond to the evolving technical and commercial requirements of the U.S. solar market.
