Use Goodbudget’s free plan if you budget with envelopes, alone or with a partner, and will record purchases yourself, since it covers 20 envelopes, one account, and two mobile devices. Pay $80 a year for Premium only once you need a second account or want your bank’s transactions imported, which you’ll still confirm one by one.

In Goodbudget, you fill digital envelopes such as groceries, rent, and dining out from your income, and every purchase you record comes out of one, so each envelope shows what’s left. It suits households that plan spending together, since partners log in with the same email and password and see each other’s purchases on their own phones.

Households that record purchases as they spend get a shared envelope budget free for as long as 20 envelopes and one account cover them. Budgeters who want imported transactions in a zero-based budget, which plans every dollar they have, do better with YNAB at $109 a year.
