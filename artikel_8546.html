- Going out to eat - not just meat/potatoes. Some microbreweries as well as international cuisines.

With that in mind, we thought Coeur D'Alene in Idaho would be a great spot since it is easy driving distance to Spokane.
Take a look at visiting the Palouse. It is between Spokane and Idaho, a bit to the South, has very nice rolling hills, with crop stripes that change colors over the year. Moscow ID has a nice arboretum. Idaho is more than just Oregon - Idaho Or-Ida potatoes, but there are towns that smell like French fries from the potatoes processing plants. If you are going to Montana or Glacier NP, stop in Wallace, ID along the way, even if you don't visit the Oasis Bordello Museum or the Center of the Universe. There is a 15 mile Route of the Hiawatha rails to trails with "10 train tunnels and 7 sky-high trestles", bicycle downhill with shuttle bus. I always visit Northwest Museum of Arts & Culture in Spokane, if you like regional art or Tom Otterness. I would save Spokane falls and the Expo 74 riverfront area for the last night.
