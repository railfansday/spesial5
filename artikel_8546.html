"'We’re Contacting You About Fraud' Is Itself a Red Flag for Fraud." One of the major themes of the book is to be suspicious of any inbound communications.
A family member has fallen for the fraudulent line twice. She'd taken the bait, but she handed me the phone before giving out any numbers. I listened for a moment and told the silver toungued fraudster, that we'd call the number on the back of the credit card; then I disconnected. She's slowly learning but thinks that she has to answer all phone calls and all door bells.

I prefer:
Let it go to voicemail; no voicemail, block.
Don't open the door for door bell ringers unless they've called first.
Door bell cams are a plus.
Nice guys finish last.
