Celtics Loss To Warriors Marks First Four-Game Losing Streak Since 2021

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Celtics Loss To Warriors Marks First Four-Game Losing Streak Since 2021 originally appeared on NESN. Add NESN as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Boston's West Coast Trip has not been kind to Joe Mazzulla's crew.

The Celtics went into Sunday coming off a horrific loss to the 1-14 Sacramento Kings, needing a bounceback performance to end the weekend on a high note.

Instead, Jayson Tatum and company put up a season-low in points, dropping an 88-87 contest to a Stephen Curry-less Warriors team. The loss was the team's fourth in a row, their first slump of four consecutive games since May 12, 2021.

Tatum led the way with 27 points on 8-of-20 shooting, adding 10 rebounds and six assists. However, he was one of just three Celtics players with double-digit points, joining Derrick White (14 points) and Luka Garza (10 points) in that category.

The 30-year-old Celtics star had a chance to give Boston the lead in the dying seconds; however, he was unable to sink the potential game-winning shot over Draymond Green.

As a team, the C's shot an ugly 39% from the field, a slight improvement from the 34% recorded against the Kings, but still well below the team's season average of 44.5%.

"Yeah, I mean, you look at it as inefficient from the standpoint of shot-making and execution," Mazzulla said after the loss. "So, you have to go back and take a look at those things. Where can we execute better? Where can we run better plays to put guys in better positions, and where can we get better shots? So, we just have to take a look at that. Obviously, the result of it isn't going well, but from a long-term perspective, the process, we'll take a look at what the efficiency of our offense was, and [then] there's an aspect that you have to trust through an 82-game season."

Along with the inefficient offense, Mazzulla called out the rebounding as well.

"It's our inefficient offense and our ability to finish possessions with a rebound. Those are the two trends that have stuck throughout the four games."

The Celtics have one more game away from TD Garden to close out the road trip, a matchup on Tuesday against the Washington Wizards. If Boston drops another contest, it will mark the team's first five-game losing streak since 2014.
