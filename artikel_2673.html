Luka Dončić gave a ‘strong endorsement’ of Quentin Grimes before free agency

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Two seasons ago, Quentin Grimes and Luka Dončić shared the floor for 21 games and 228 minutes as members of the Dallas Mavericks before Dončić got injured and the two were ultimately both traded away in separate deals. During those minutes, Dallas outscored its opponents by 37 points.

Apparently, that was all the time and success necessary for Grimes to earn Dončić's trust as the Slovenian superstar pushed for the Los Angeles Lakers to sign him in free agency this past summer, according to Marc Stein.

"League sources say that Grimes, like new Lakers big man Walker Kessler, received a very strong endorsement from Dončić when the Lakers were assembling their offseason plans to make the 26-year-old one of their primary free agent targets," Stein wrote in his Substack, The Stein Line.

Aside from having Dončić in his corner, Grimes was also backed by head coach JJ Redick. Those are a couple of pretty powerful allies.

"And that gave Grimes two significant purple-and-gold advocates: Face of the Franchise and Lakers coach JJ Redick," Stein wrote. "There was confidence within the organization that Grimes could not only bring a trusted catch-and shoot prowess to Lakerland but also play more consistently closer to the level that resulted in the 6 foot-4 guard averaging 21.9 points per game for Philadelphia across the final 28 games of the 2024-25 season."

Thus far, that confidence has been rewarded. Following a slower start to the year, Grimes is playing excellently, averaging 12.7 points, 3.5 rebounds and 2.9 assists. He's shooting 41.2% beyond the arc and 56.9% on 2-pointers, and dropped a career-high 47 points in a win over the Charlotte Hornets on Tuesday.

Since joining the starting lineup in place of an injured Austin Reaves four games ago, he's averaging 25.5 points, 6.0 rebounds and 3.8 assists while shooting 57.1% from deep. That ability to scale up offensively is affirming the organization's confidence in him replicating "the level that resulted in the 6 foot-4 guard averaging 21.9 points per game for Philadelphia across the final 28 games of the 2024-25 season," as Stein reported.

Grimes and the Lakers return to the floor Monday night against the Utah Jazz at 7 p.m.
