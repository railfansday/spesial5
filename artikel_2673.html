Nets’ Michael Porter Jr. returning vs. 76ers after missing Grizzlies loss for personal reasons

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Michael Porter Jr. will return to the Brooklyn Nets' lineup on Monday vs. the Philadelphia 76ers after missing Saturday's loss to the Memphis Grizzlies for personal reasons. Porter was a late scratch on Saturday but sat on the Nets' bench during the 129-110 loss.

The Nets, who were coming off their best win of the season, couldn't find a rhythm on Saturday with Porter sidelined. Memphis was on the second night of a back-to-back, with travel, and missing Ty Jerome, Zach Edey and Jerami Grant. Still, the Nets allowed the Grizzlies to score 71 first-half points en route to a blowout victory.

Without Porter, the Nets dug a 17-point hole at the half that they couldn't climb out of. They shot 20-of-47 (42.6 percent) from the field and 7-of-21 (33.3 percent) from three, with 13 assists and seven turnovers in the first half.

Fernandez criticized his team's effort during the loss following two days off.

"It's frustrating they played harder than us," the Nets coach said. "We cut it to seven at some point, and you should've been in the game, but we're not mature enough. We played against a team that played on the second [night] of a back-to-back with the same travel that we had, and they played the right way, played really hard. We're not there yet."

Porter's co-star, Julius Randle, led Brooklyn with 22 points on 8-of-16 shooting from the field and 3-of-6 from three.

Starting center Day'Ron Sharpe added 16 points, 11 rebounds and five assists on 7-of-14 shooting. The rest of the Nets combined to shoot 26-of-63 (41.3 percent).

Meanwhile, Brooklyn allowed Memphis to shoot 46-of-87 from the field (52.9 percent) and 15-of-35 (42.9 percent) from three, with 34 assists and 12 turnovers.

"It was things that we can control," Randle said. "But we didn't get enough stops. They made like nine threes in the first quarter. It's tough when a team gets it going that early. We didn't punch first. We didn't come out with a sense of urgency. And they took advantage of that. It's frustrating because we want to see our habits translate, and they didn't [Saturday]. So we've got to be better."

Fernandez started Noah Clowney in place of Porter alongside Egor Demin and Danny Wolf in the backcourt. All three players struggled to get going offensively without a true point guard in the lineup, and their size didn't help Brooklyn's defense as their head coach had hoped. Point guards Nolan Traore and Mikel Brown Jr. each played only 20 minutes during the loss.

The loss dropped the Nets to 3-9. Brooklyn will look to get back on track on Monday against a 76ers team on the second night of a back-to-back.

The 76ers beat the Indiana Pacers 131-124 at home on Sunday. Nick Nurse's squad is 11-3 and has posted the NBA's 12th-best net rating (2.0).

Joel Embiid sat on the front end of the 76ers' back-to-back on Sunday, while LeBron James played 33 minutes. Embiid is expected to be available on Monday vs. the Nets, while James will likely sit.

Brooklyn has a clean injury report for the matchup.
