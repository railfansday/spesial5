Cook, McCaffrey lead Week 10 fantasy football running back rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Nov. 12 (UPI) -- James Cook and Christian McCaffrey lead my Top-50 fantasy football running back rankings for Week 10.

Jahmyr Gibbs, Bijan Robinson and Kyren Williams also are inside the Top 5 of my rankings, which are listed below. Kenneth Walker, Chuba Hubbard, Derrick Henry, Jonathan Taylor and Bhayshul Tuten are inside my Top 10.

Players from the Chicago Bears, Denver Broncos, Philadelphia Eagles and Tampa Bay Buccaneers, who are on bye, should be benched -- or dropped in some cases. Players from Thursday's New York Giants-Washington Commanders game were removed from my rankings.

My Week 10 waiver wire/free agent targets can be found here. My Week 10 quarterback and wide receiver rankings also are available.
