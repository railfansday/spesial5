Raiders vs. Broncos live updates, score: Fernando Mendoza throws first career TD pass to RB Ashton Jeanty

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Las Vegas Raiders and Denver Broncos will face off for the first time this season in Week 11's afternoon window, with kickoff set for 4:25 p.m. ET at Empower Field at Mile High in Denver.

After starting the season 3-0, the Raiders have been on a slide, going 1-6 over their past seven games. No. 1 overall pick rookie Fernando Mendoza is set to make his second career start following the trade of Kirk Cousins ahead of the deadline this week, up against another challenging defense after facing the Seattle Seahawks in Week 11. All-Pro tight end Brock Bowers is inactive for this matchup, leaving Mendoza without his top receiving option.

The Broncos entered the week tied with the Kansas City Chiefs for the best record in the AFC West at 7-2. They're 10.5-point home favorites in this contest and are on a five-game winning streak coming off the Week 10 bye. That win streak began as head coach Sean Payton resumed offensive play-calling from OC Davis Webb after a slow start on offense.
