David Bailey is built different — and it makes Jets star root for him even more

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

LOS ANGELES — David Bailey took another giant step toward NFL Defensive Rookie of the Year in Sunday's 27-24 Jets win over the Chargers when he recorded two sacks and a forced fumble.

Bailey strip-sacked Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert in the first quarter, but L.A. tackle Travis Burke recovered it. In the second quarter, he ended another Chargers drive with a sack of Herbert.

Bailey has 9 ½ sacks and an NFL-leading six forced fumbles. He's one sack away from breaking the Jets rookie record for sacks, set by Hugh Douglas in 1995.

And he did it in front of family and friends, having grown up in nearby Orange County, Calif.

"It meant a lot, man, it meant a lot to the team," the soft-spoken Bailey said afterward. "Coach AG [Aaron Glenn] put me in some good spots.''

Jets receiver Garrett Wilson marveled at how low-key Bailey is, refusing to celebrate great plays and take credit.

"I love him, bro," Wilson said. "Every time I watch him make a play, I get so happy and excited because he's a guy who doesn't really get excited or happy for himself. So I've got to do it for him.

"He makes a couple of plays and doesn't celebrate. No gloves, no sleeves. I tell him his drip is raw, bro. I know he was back home; he's from out here. He got to make some plays, but he's been doing that all season.

Los Angeles Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert (10) is tackled by New York Jets safety Dane Belton (6) and linebacker David Bailey (31) on Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Gregory Bull

"He's all about his business, and that's what he was brought in to do. And man, he's delivering. He's a baller, and I can't wait to watch him keep growing. He doesn't say much either; it's what he does. There's a lot of purpose to it, and I just like the way he goes about his business…. I feel like he's going to be one of the great ones."

Glenn was almost matter-of-fact when speaking about Bailey, saying, "That's what he does. I'm not surprised. You guys ask me about it all the time, and I keep telling you the same thing: We expect that from him.

"What's really sticking out to me is that he's doing everything he can to get the ball back to the offense. He has maybe six or seven forced fumbles this year. … So, any time he's on the field, he gives us a chance for something big to happen. He's a big-time playmaker."

The Jets also got sacks from DT Harrison Phillips and Jowon Briggs, who had 1 ½. Minkah Fitzpatrick, Darrell Jackson and Jack Heflin each had a half sack.

There was a familiar face for the Jets inside the Chargers coaching booth — Adam Gase. Gase, who was the Jets head coach from 2019-20 and compiled a 9-23 record, is a passing game specialist for the Chargers.

The only Jets player on the current roster who played for Gase is long snapper Thomas Hennessy.

When the Jets defeated the Titans earlier this season, they beat their former head coach, Robert Saleh.

Glenn faced a familiar face in the game Sunday — Chargers safety RJ Mickens, who's the son of one of Glenn's best friends, fellow former Jets cornerback Ray Mickens. So, Glenn has seen RJ, who was born in 2001 when Mickens and Glenn were Jets teammates, grow up since he was a child.

RJ Mickens made one of the deciding defensive plays in the Chargers win over the Ravens on Monday night with a big pass breakup.

"He's a really solid … he's not a kid anymore, but a solid man," Glenn said before the game. "It's good to see him out there living his dream."

The inactive players for the Jets were WRs Omar Cooper Jr. and Isaiah Williams, CB Jarvis Brownlee Jr., TE Jelani Woods, LB Trevin Wallace and DE Nate Lynn. Woods and Brownlee, who's been supplanted at nickel back by rookie D'Angelo Ponds, were healthy scratches.
