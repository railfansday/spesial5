Bears RB Kyle Monangai trending in right direction for Saints clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Chicago Bears running back Kyle Monangai returned to practice Monday as the team began preparing for Sunday's home matchup vs. the New Orleans Saints.

The second-year back worked at Halas Hall following Chicago's Week 10 bye. He has missed five games while recovering from turf toe in his right foot and a thumb injury.

Monangai last played October 4 against the New York Jets, rushing for 146 yards and two touchdowns in a 23-12 victory. The Bears have relied on D'Andre Swift and other backfield options during his absence.

ESPN's Adam Schefter shared an update Monday on X, formerly Twitter, citing Courtney Cronin's report that Monangai said he is "on track" to return against the Saints.

Before the injury, Monangai accumulated 324 rushing yards and three touchdowns on 60 carries, averaging 5.4 yards per attempt.

He returned to practice on November 6, participating on a limited basis before remaining inactive against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. Monday's session gave him another opportunity to work on the field after additional recovery time.

Monangai said the bye helped his progress, although he expects to manage the toe injury for the remainder of the season. He plans to wear plates and orthotics inside his cleats, with fuller healing left for the offseason.

Chicago has used Roschon Johnson and Salvon Ahmed alongside Swift while Monangai has been unavailable. A return would restore another rushing option for coach Ben Johnson.

The seventh-round pick from Rutgers is targeting Sunday's Week 11 clash at Soldier Field, though Monday's update does not confirm his availability or expected workload.

Monangai expects to play Sunday, with his game status still undetermined as the Bears continue preparations.
