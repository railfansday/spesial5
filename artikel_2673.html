Injured Warriors guard making good progress, to be re-evaluated this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Warriors desperately need Steph Curry back.

But Golden State could be getting another reinforcement as the superstar nurses an oblique injury.

Golden State Warriors guard Stephen Curry, right, confers with head coach Steve Kerr during an NBA game against the Denver Nuggets on Monday, Oct. 26, 2026. AP Photo/David Zalubowski

Reserve guard Brandon Williams, who was sidelined for the last three games after suffering from a left hamstring strain, is set to be evaluated Saturday.

The team ruled Williams out for the final two games of their current road trip, but his return appears imminent. The absence of Curry has made Williams a necessity for the Warriors on offense.

Brandon Williams of the Golden State Warriors handles the ball during the game against the Phoenix Suns on Oct. 24, 2026 at Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona. NBAE via Getty Images

The latest update on the 26-year-old bodes well for Golden State as the team stated: "The re-evaluation indicated that Williams is making good progress and has progressed to on-court activities. He will be re-evaluated again on Saturday, Nov. 21."

This season, Williams has averaged 10.4 points, 2.3 assists and 2.1 rebounds across 10 games (one start) with the Warriors.

Yaxel Lendeborg #1 and Brandon Williams #12 of the Golden State Warriors high five during the game against the Los Angeles Lakers on Oct. 21, 2026 at Chase Center in San Francisco, California. NBAE via Getty Images

The Warriors could be without Curry for as long as a month. Life without the veteran has been tough.

Golden State has lost three straight games in embarrassing fashion, but Williams returning could aid the team in its efforts to stay competitive.
