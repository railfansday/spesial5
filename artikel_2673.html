Titans have only defense in the NFL giving up scores on most of its drives

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Titans’ defense allowed the Cowboys’ offense to score on every drive on Sunday, with the exception of a kneeldown at the end of the game. That put the Titans into some very bad territory.

Tennessee’s defense has now given up a score on 50.5 percent of opposing possessions this season. The Titans are the only defense in the league that gives up points most of the time it takes the field.

Over the last two games, the Titans’ defense went on an incredible run: In addition to giving up scores on the Cowboys’ first eight offensive possessions in Week 11, the Titans gave up points on the Jaguars’ last five possessions in Week 10. That streak of 13 consecutive drives giving up scores is the longest streak of consecutive drives in which any NFL defense has given up points, for as far back as full drive data is available.

The Titans’ defense’s last 14 drives ended in 10 touchdowns, three field goals and a kneeldown.

Titans coach Robert Saleh has had success as a defensive coordinator, but he went 20-36 in his previous stint as head coach of the Jets, and he’s now 1-9 with the Titans. When he’s the head coach, his defenses don’t get the job done.
