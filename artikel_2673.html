Brittany Mahomes Unleashes Heated Rant After Husband Patrick’s Chiefs Defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Thanksgiving was far from a happy occasion at the Mahomes household after Patrick Mahomes and his NFL team, the Kansas City Chiefs, lost 13-10 to the Buffalo Bills following a contentious refereeing decision.

Patrick's wife, former soccer player Brittany Mahomes, took to social media on Thursday, November 26, to blast the officials. "Guess we can't even play football without getting it called against us… not sure what an actual catch is after that I guess?" she ranted on her Instagram Story.

She added, "I don't wanna hear s*** anymore from anyone."

Brittany went on to share several reactions from fans and pundits who also questioned the referee's decision. The controversy centered on a late fourth-quarter play when officials overturned the initial ruling that Chiefs wide receiver Rashee Rice had caught a pass from Patrick.

One of the posts Brittany reposted called the decision "highway robbery on a fantastic catch," while another exclaimed, "THIS IS ONE OF THE WORST CALLS OF ALL TIME."

Another commenter described the reversal as "beyond ridiculous," while one viewer said, "That was absolutely a catch by Rice. The lowest IQ person you know will still say it's rigged by the Chiefs."

Away from the drama on the field, Brittany and Patrick are also preparing to welcome their fourth child together. The couple, who married in March 2022, are already parents to three children: daughters Sterling, 5, and Golden, 1, and son Bronze, 3. In a gender reveal shared on November 18, the pair announced they are expecting another boy.

During a press conference earlier this month, Patrick revealed that he was hoping for another son. "I'm hoping that me and Bronze can even up the score," he said when asked about his growing family. "Obviously, it's a blessing to have another baby on the way. I'm excited for it."

Another reporter pointed out an interesting coincidence, noting that the Chiefs have won the Super Bowl in every season Patrick and Brittany have announced they were expecting a child.

"I'm hoping [to continue the trend]," Patrick replied. "I don't know if Brittany is, but I'm hoping, if we can keep that going and get another Super Bowl win with a baby on the way, that'd be awesome for sure."
