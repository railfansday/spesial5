Klopp planning two changes to Germany's starting XI for Netherlands clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Following Germany's 3-1 victory over Serbia on Friday, Jürgen Klopp is planning to make two changes to his side for Monday's final 2026/27 UEFA Nations League Group A2 fixture against the Netherlands.

According to Bild, Nico Schlotterbeck and Nathaniel Brown will be drafted into the starting line-up in defence, just as Bulinews predicted in our match preview.

Brown is in line to come in at left-back at the expense of Maximilian Mittelstädt, while Schlotterbeck will partner Jonathan Tah in central defence, with Malick Thiaw dropping to the bench.

Klopp's side can secure their place in the UEFA Nations League quarter-finals with a victory in Berlin on Monday.
