Witcher 3 Geralt actor approves AI use for his return after stroke changed his voice

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Geralt’s Polish voice actor has approved AI assistance for his return in The Witcher 3’s new expansion after a stroke changed the sound of his voice.

Jacek Rozenek has voiced the monster hunter in Polish since CD Projekt Red’s first Witcher game, recording around 10,000 lines for The Witcher 3 before returning for its Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine expansions.

However, a stroke in 2019 left Rozenek unable to speak for months, and although he eventually returned to acting, changes to his voice presented a challenge for the upcoming Songs of the Past expansion.

This ultimately led CD Projekt Red and Rozenek to seek help from AI, with the aim of making his new dialogue sound consistent with his original performance as Geralt.

Speaking to Game File, CD Projekt Red localization producer Mikołaj Szwed explained that the team needed to hear how Rozenek’s voice had changed following both his stroke and the years since his original recordings.

According to the report, Rozenek’s voice sounded much lower and gruffer following his stroke, which created a potential mismatch between Geralt’s existing dialogue and his new lines.

That difference would be particularly noticeable because Songs of the Past takes place around the same period as The Witcher 3’s main story. Players would also hear new battle cries and reactions while exploring existing areas, making the difference between Rozenek’s original recordings and his new performance more noticeable.

With Rozenek’s blessing, CD Projekt Red decided to contact an AI company it had worked with previously to help address the difference.

His return follows years of rehabilitation, during which he trained his voice for six or seven hours a day. Rozenek previously recorded dialogue in 2021 for a quest included in The Witcher 3’s 2022 next-gen update, although even that smaller recording session proved demanding.

“I was so exhausted after this recording,” he told Game File. “The point is that this voice should be as close as possible to my voice from 12 years ago,” he says. “I have great trust…for the creators of this game.”

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt – Songs of the Past expansion is set to be released in 2027.
