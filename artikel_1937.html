Hassocks downed by Dockers for second time this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Three times Hassocks have lost in the Southern Combination Premier Division so far this season. Two of those defeats have come at the hands of Newhaven.

The 2-1 scoreline at the Trafalgar Ground was the same as when the Dockers sailed back from Mid Sussex with all three points in August. The Robins must be delighted to see the back of Newhaven in the league.

So often the scourge of Hassocks in the past, Lee Robinson scored both the Dockers goals in the space of seven second half minutes.

Lewis Finney pulled one back with 13 minutes remaining and although the Robins threw the kitchen sink at Newhaven, they were unable to find another way through.

The Dockers defence were outstanding and in particular ex-Hassocks centre back Matt Gunn, who deservedly picked up the host's man-of-the-match award afterwards.

Gunn was also a threat at the other end and had two chances to score twice himself inside the opening three minutes. He first saw a flicked header from a corner sensationally tipped around the post by Ed Cooper.

That resulted in another corner and although the Robins cleared this one, Newhaven were able to recycle possession and get a delivery from Ezra Roeg into the box. Gunn remained forward and met it with a powerful headed effort just over the bar.

Hassocks were unable to cope with the relentless tempo at which the Dockers had started. A situation summed up when Harry Reed and Tom Sekyere converged rapidly on Jack Troak to snare the ball from him.

The intense press got Tyler Capon in down the right flank for a cross which Cooper and Harvey Blake combined to clear in somewhat unorthodox, acrobatic style to prevent Robinson from having a tap-in.

Troak managed to lead an attack which finally broke the Robins out of their own half. His cross hung up into the middle was met by Dan Perry for a header just over.

Perry was making his Hassocks debut after signing from East Grinstead Town in midweek. The Hitman has netted 11 times this season for the Wasps and looks to be a quality addition.

Dan Turner then saw a shot from the edge of the box blocked by the nether regions of Elliott Bresciani. The defender putting his body on the line was an early sign of the sort of defending Newhaven would deploy to good effect to get them over the line in the second half.

With the Robins now a willing participant in the game, things began to ebb and flow from end-to-end. Capon was millimetres away from connecting with a Reed centre and Cooper did just enough to put off Sekyere into placing a one-on-one wide.

Perry looked the most likely source of goals for Hassocks. He drew a routine save from Jake Rhodes when heading a Harvey Blake cross on target.

Rhodes then pulled off a much more difficult reaction stop at close range when Big Alex Fair delivered and Perry stooped to direct a header across the Dockers goalkeeper.

Cooper saved well at his near post when Matt Feeney-Hill cut inside before Newhaven faded a little in the final 10 minutes of the half, probably because of the speed at which they played the previous 35.

As a result, Hassocks were on top in the period leading into the break. Finney saw a shot blocked by Gunn and some nifty footwork from Fair followed by a clever back heel teed up Omar Abdsami for a shot just a little too high.

The second half was a slow-burner in comparison to the first until Robinson fired the Dockers ahead on 63 minutes. Sekyere hooked a hopeful ball over the top of the Hassocks defence which the veteran forward showed a remarkable turn of pace to latch onto.

It carried Robinson clear of Turner and enabled him to curl a fine finish with his side foot around Cooper and into the far corner.

Newhaven doubled their lead with 70 on the clock after the Robins were unconvincing dealing with a corner. The delivery from Feeney-Hill was only half cleared to the edge of the box, allowing Ross Barclay to return an up-and-under into the area.

Gunn won a flick on and it found the unmarked Robinson, who had taken advantage of a slapdash Hassocks offside line to move front and centre of goal where he volleyed home unmarked.

Half-hearted appeals for the goal to be disallowed were easily dismissed. Robinson had stayed in a legal position, thanks in part to the Robins being completely out of shape.

Hassocks now had no choice but to lay siege on the Newhaven goal. Liam Benson came on as James Westlake changed formation and went with two up front.

The adjustment helped the Robins. Tall striker Jamie Wilkes put an awkward header wide and Devon Fender rattled the post with a good volley from inside the D.

An excellent move halved the deficit on 77 minutes. Abdsami was patient in waiting for a gap to appear into which he could play a forward pass.

When it came, his ball into the feet of Benson was superbly flicked around the corner by the substitute and into the path of Finney.

The finish from Finney was cool as you like as he picked out the far corner with aplomb to move onto double figures for the season.

Hassocks now had 13 minutes in which to try and find an equaliser. Unfortunately, their shooting from that point on did not match the calmness or precision with which Finney had pulled one back.

Finney came closest to providing a leveller on 88. Abdsami crossed and Perry directed a clever downwards header into the penalty box. The arriving Finney met it with a rasping volley which Rhodes somehow flung himself upwards to block.

Bresciani produced an overhead kick to clear the loose ball off the line and prevent Benson putting away the rebound. In a moment which summed the afternoon up really for Hassocks, that clearance gave another chance to Blake.

The Robins captain could only blaze so high as to pose more of a threat to Newhaven Fort all the way up the hill than the Dockers goal.

Other than the quality of the Finney finish, there was one other positive for the Robins to take home at least. Raging Joe Bull made his 300th senior appearance for the club, becoming the first Robin to pass the triple century landmark since Phil Gault a decade ago.

Hassocks: Cooper; Blake, D Turner, Hendy, Bull; Wilkes, Finney; Fair, Abdsami, Troak; Perry.

Subs: Fender, Benson, Budd (used), Croal, Brodie (unused).
