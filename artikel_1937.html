Bradford City chief Michael Duff's admission regarding Nick Powell

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Namely, the amount of match minutes given to Nick Powell, whose quality has been there for all to see during his game-time so far in 2026-27.

Powell plundered his eighth goal of the campaign after coming off the bench in Tuesday's EFL Trophy cruise against York City at Valley Parade.

It's the 33-year-old's best haul since his Stoke City days in 2020-21-, with two-thirds of the campaign still to go. Duff remains wary.

The City chief said: "Everyone can see that (his quality) but it's a risk every time. Hopefully he gets through this because people are going, ‘what are you playing him in that game for, he's a dead rubber (versus York).’

“He needs to hit certain metrics and certain things during the week and what is the best environment for him to do that in.

“That’s the thing with Nick, he's in a good place at the minute, he's enjoying his football, he's fit.

“So, we need to harness that and keep him fit for as long as possible because when you put him on the pitch, he's going to score goals.”

City, who return to action next Saturday with a Yorkshire derby at home to Doncaster Rovers, have emphatically answered the charge regarding their perceived lack of goals with a 12-goal haul in two big Valley Parade wins over Marine and York City respectively.

Duff continued: "Since the first day we came in, we've definitely created a lot more chances.

“I go back to the Burton game where we've had the keeper made a 'worldie', we've had two off the line and had some good chances in it.

“The Oxford game, we’ve missed three or four gilt-edged chances in that and you look at the run that they’re on.

“The fact that the ball's actually hitting the back of the net, the opposition is irrelevant. We had five big chances in the first half (on Tuesday) and it's 1-0.

“From a coaching point of view, you can't kick the ball in the net for them. But the fact that we're getting opportunities and they're now starting to take their chances, hopefully it sort of just releases the noise.

“All you can do is stay consistent with the way that we work, the way we behave, the way we train.

“No one thinks we don't have quality. Hopefully the confidence starts going, they don't overthink it and then it becomes more natural.”
