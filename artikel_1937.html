Championship table predicted with Sheffield United, Middlesbrough FC, Birmingham City, Wolves & West Ham verdicts

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Opta have forecasted Championship finishes for Sheffield United, Middlesbrough, West Ham United, Birmingham City and more

FA Cup fever swept the lower divisions of the EFL but clubs in England’s second tier had league action to focus on.

Sheffield United visited the capital to take on Charlton Athletic only to leave The Valley empty-handed, losing 2-1 to Nathan Jones’ men. Middlesbrough also endured a frustrating afternoon and were held to a goalless draw by Watford at Vicarage Road.

Away from Yorkshire, Derby County secured a vital 3-1 win over Bolton Wanderers and West Ham United were beaten by Blackburn Rovers in Scott Parker’s first game at the Hammers helm. Cardiff City defeated Burnley 5-4 in a topsy-turvy affair, Birmingham City edged past Swansea City and Wrexham drew with Wolverhampton Wanderers.

Struggling Preston North End threatened to turn a corner as they edged past Southampton, while Queens Park Rangers clinched a 2-1 win over Lincoln City and West Bromwich Albion swept Millwall aside at The Hawthorns.

Following the latest twists and turns, data experts Opta have deployed their supercomptuer to predict how the Championship table will look at the end of the 2026/27 season.
