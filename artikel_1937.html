Di Marzio: Endrick and Tresoldi deals ‘not a financial issue’ for Milan – the situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

AC Milan have the financial capability to go out and sign their top targets in January, according to Gianluca Di Marzio.

Several reports and even the words of owner Gerry Cardinale have made it very clear that Milan plan to be busy in the winter mercato. The main target seems to be a forward to complete the attack, as well as a centre-back to help out the defensive department too.

Among the names linked have been Real Madrid’s Endrick, Club Brugge’s Nicolo Tresoldi and Sporting CP’s Goncalo Inacio. It’s one thing having the list of names, but it is another having the money and the possibility of doing a deal mid-season with the selling club.

During last night’s segment on Sky, Gianluca Di Marzio (via MilanNews) was asked whether Milan can realistically pursue names like Endrick and Tresoldi, who are likely to cost over €50m each.

“That they has the strength to do these kinds of deals, yes. They demonstrated it over the summer, so I don’t think there’s a financial issue,” he began.

“Whether they manage to sign one of the two… Endrick, as far as we know, José Mourinho hasn’t changed his mind and therefore doesn’t intend to part with him in January. But Endrick is a Cardinale obsession, just as he was a Roma obsession during the summer.

“We’ll have to see if Mourinho changes his mind in the month and a half before the transfer window opens. He hasn’t changed his mind at the moment, so I see the situation as complicated at the moment.

“Just as it’s complicated for Brugge to sell Tresoldi. Brugge are a club that doesn’t need to sell and always sells at least one key player in the summer, precisely to try to get to the end of the season with its players.

“Milan are looking for a striker, looking for a back-up for Ramos. The figures I’ve read, 60-70 million, are very, very high figures to try to convince Brugge to sell him. It’s a deal that – given the player’s objective value – I think is around 50 million. In my opinion.

“But Brugge are unlikely to sell him in January. Above all, Milan is looking for a striker to be a back-up for Ramos; it’s not like Ramos and Tresoldi will play together. In the sense that Milan has always played with a centre-forward.

“Above all, they need to provide this center forward with plenty of playable balls rather than buy another. Because if Ramos doesn’t receive balls in the penalty area, he struggles to score, and Tresoldi struggles to score either.

“They need a striker to get Camarda playing. I think Camarda will go and play and therefore an investment will be needed in a player who can be a suitable deputy for Ramos.”
