Albion's Sweden ace Yasin Yasin Ayari was case in point this week as Fotbollskanalen addressed recent transfer rumours that have swirled around the midfielder, including links with Manchester United.

Ayari is out of contract with Brighton this summer but Albion are believed to have an option of a further year. The 23-year-old has also been linked with AC Milan, AS Roma, Galatasaray and Fenerbahçe.

Ayari said: “I’m at Brighton and I’m happy where I am. Things are going well for me right now. Then we’ll see what’s to come.”

Ayari has been a key man for Fabian Hurzeler's as they sit second in the Premier League table. They resume action this Sunday at Hull City

Ayari added: “We have a clear identity and everyone follows it to the letter. We are like a big family. Everyone loves to play football and everyone has fun. Everyone has fantastic qualities. Then, the coach has done a good job. Getting all the qualities out on the pitch.”

Another report to emerge this week was around German playmaker Brajan Gruda. The Brighton ace - currently on loan at RB Leipzig - impressed for Jurgen Klopp's Germany against Serbia and The Netherlands.

the 22-year-old scored a fine individual goal on debut against the Serbs and then followed that with a goal and two assists in a 5-0 win against the Dutch.

Transfer expert Florian Plettenberg posted: “The obligation to buy for Brajan Gruda will be activated in 2027 due to accounting reasons. The total package for RB Leipzig will amount to €30m, including bonuses. Gruda will then get a contract until 2031, which has already been agreed.

“Bayern have always had him on their list, even before his move to Brighton. However, he is currently not a concrete transfer target for Bayern – unless something extraordinary happens, such as the sale of Michael Olise.”

Meanwhile Brighton have been linked with a move for young QPR winger Leon Scarlett. Sky Sports also claimed that AC Milan and Eintracht Frankfurt are also keen on the 17-year-old.

QPR boss Julien Stephan said: "The player is aware that the most important thing is to stay focused on the pitch, what he has to do every day to continue to develop his qualities.

“If I feel that I need to have a conversation with him, I will have a conversation. We just continue to manage him like before, with the same desire and the same passion to help him to develop his qualities.”
