Chelsea v Leeds United: Daniel Farke challenges Whites to continue proving Premier League credentials

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

And if Leeds United are performing and carrying themselves like an established Premier League team, perhaps it is time to start treating them as one.

A growing number of pundits are viewing Leeds in those terms after being impressed by the way the club have taken to life back in the top flight.

United's physicality and intensity have marked them out as one of the hardest teams to beat. They have lost just seven league games since the start of 2026, while their defence ranks among the best in the division this season – an improvement underpinned by summer signings James Trafford and Tarik Muharemovic.

Farke himself feels his players have done enough to earn the respect of pundits, although he has cautioned them against believing the hype.

"It's a credit to our work in the last years," he said. "I would also agree, based on our performances, that we deserve a bit the label and praise.

"But we also don't go into the trap where we think that we are already the finished product or better than we are. We know that we have to fight for each and every point because other clubs are more established.

"We have an unbelievable tradition and are a huge club in the UK. Everyone knows that this club belongs at the top level.

"We have a clear vision for our short, mid-term and long-term but it's also fair to say that in the last 25 years we haven't spent too many years at the Premier League level. Our best finish in that time is exactly one top-half finish in position nine.

"For that, we don't go into the trap and believe all the praise. We know that we are good and are not doubting ourselves too much; we play with confidence.

"But we don't go into the trap of thinking that we can win games with even 95 per cent effort. We're still a side in our second year after promotion and have to show some craft and fight in order to become an established Premier League side."

Chelsea provide another measure of Leeds' progress today, the first of three challenging away assignments before Christmas.

Farke, whose side travel to Manchester City and Liverpool in early December, is bidding to become the first United manager to mastermind a win at Stamford Bridge since David O'Leary in 1999.

"I know it's a while ago since we travelled away from Stamford Bridge with three points," said Farke.

"It will be difficult but if we are at our very best, we also know we're capable of winning points wherever we go. We have to make sure we are on it.

"The preparations have been difficult this week but it's also very similar for Chelsea. We're looking forward to this game."

Leeds return from the international break hoping to build on their impressive victory over in-form Tottenham Hotspur.

Chelsea, meanwhile, are looking to respond to their disappointing defeat at Sunderland last time out.

Xabi Alonso's side have shown their attacking potential in flashes – not least when they hammered Leeds 6-3 in the League Cup – but a record of five wins, four defeats and a solitary draw in the league hints at a team still finding their way under the new manager.

Farke, however, has warned his players not to read too much into Chelsea's inconsistency.

"I expect top-class players and also a top manager," he said.

"It's no secret that they have world-class potential in their squad. That's why they've spent so much money in the last years – to build a squad with unbelievable individual quality, especially up front.

"It's difficult. It's never guaranteed that if you have so many quality players that you play each and every opponent out of the park.

"Some of their last opponents have made their life difficult but it's still important to be aware of their quality and strength. We have to make sure we don't allow them to develop that strength.

"It's also important that we take part in this game in terms of having as much possession as possible and that we also dominate the ball because when they have the ball, they have so much talent, skill and potential to make something happen.

"For Leeds United, it's always a bit of a special fixture to travel to London and play Chelsea. We are very respectful but definitely not fearful."

Leeds took four points off the Blues last season on their way to a 14th-place finish.

They have made an encouraging start to their second campaign back at the top level but Farke is staying grounded.

The Leeds boss expects the coming weeks to offer a clearer indication of how the table is shaping up as clubs brace themselves for a long, uninterrupted run of fixtures.

"Until November, it's the starting period of the Premier League," said Farke. "It's important to put yourselves into a good position. We have done this with good performances and are sitting on 14 points in the top half of the table.

"There will be many, many games and challenges due to workloads, injuries, illnesses and perhaps suspensions. For that, it's not the moment to be too carried away. It's a period where we have to be really on it and fight for everything.

"After this period, we can have a look and see where we are in the table for the run-in. This upcoming month is quite important for the final table."
