'Dealer's Choice' mission choices and outcomes in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Choosing between pure or cut drugs in Grand Theft Auto 6 is tough; on the one hand, it'll be nice to make extra money… But on the other hand, shady dealings often end in shootouts. More than other GTA 6 missions, the "Dealer's Choice" mission in Chapter 3 has a "best" and a "worst" way to handle things.

Here's what happens depending on whether you pick the pure or the cut drugs in the GTA 6 "Dealer's Choice" mission.

"Dealer's Choice" is one of the first main missions in Chapter 3. Meet up with Dre'Quan, and he'll take you to Tyrone, who will give you some drugs to sell. It's up to you, however, whether you take the pure drugs, which are of higher value but less profitable, or the cut drugs, which have been mixed with baby powder and should therefore generate more profit.

Here's what happens next in "Dealer's Choice," depending on your pick.

If you sell the pure drugs, the deal will go smoothly. The buyers will test the quality and give you the money. Completing the deal this way adds to a "professional" criminal profile and yields $1,500. Since nothing should go wrong, this is arguably the best way to complete the "Dealer's Choice" mission in GTA 6.
