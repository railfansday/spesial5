Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Sombr has a knack for chipping his teeth while onstage -- and last night was no different at his concert in Wisconsin -- and it was partly caught on video.

The singer was performing in Milwaukee on Tuesday evening when he addressed the crowd about an issue he just had with a water bottle.
