Bowen Yang Apologizes for Calling Regina Spektor Antisemitic Slur

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bowen Yang has issued an apology for calling Regina Spektor an antisemitic slur on his “Las Culturistas” podcast, saying he was unaware of the word’s origins.

“I want to genuinely apologize for using a word on the podcast without knowing its antisemitic history, especially since we’ve repudiated antisemitism on the show as recently as the last two weeks,” Yang wrote in an Instagram statement Friday. “Particularly upsetting to realize that I adopted language popularized by far-right movements whose rhetoric we should be wary of and act against.”

“At the same time, Free Palestine,” the comedian added in a follow-up Instagram Story.

Yang drew backlash this week when a clip of an October episode of his podcast with Matt Rogers began circulating on social media. Featuring guest, actress and comedian Edy Modica, the conversation turned to Spektor, and the trio wondered what the “Samson” singer has been up to since the release of her 2022 album Home, Before and After.

Modica remarked that Spektor had been “just, like, a little Israel hardcore,” referring to Spektor’s vocal pro-Israel advocacy. Yang added, “yeah, she’s Zio.”

Short for Zionist, the derogatory term was popularized by Ku Klux Klan leader David Duke in the early 2000s and has been picked up by white supremacist groups.

The Anti-Defamation League recognizes the word as a hostile phrase when referring to Jewish people or Israelis.
