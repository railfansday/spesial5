10 of the Best 'GTA 6' Cheat Codes, and How to Use Them

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Cheat codes have long been a part of the Grand Theft Auto video game franchise, and the tradition continues with GTA 6. Not only does GTA 6 support cheats, but it also has a menu that lists all of them as well as instructions for unlocking them. However, you cannot fire up the game and start using cheats from the beginning. In fact, it requires quite a bit of work on your part. Here's how to unlock cheat codes in GTA 6, how to see the list of cheats, as well as the 10 best cheat codes in GTA 6.

Gone are the good old days when you could fire up GTA, enable god mode, unlimited ammo, no reload, spawn a jetpack, and run around the entire city destroying things at will. In GTA 6, you'll need to complete the main story to unlock the ability to use cheat codes. On top of that, you'll have to complete challenges of various difficulty levels to access specific cheat codes. Some of these cheats unlock the moment you finish the main story. Others, however, are quite challenging or time-consuming. For instance, unlimited reload requires you to win 25 gold medals at shooting ranges.

Once you complete the main story in GTA 6, just go to in-game settings, and you'll see a new Cheats tab. This lists all 61 cheats and how to unlock them. You'll see the actual button combo for each cheat right after you complete the unlock requirement.

Unfortunately, there are some serious gameplay ramifications to using cheat codes in GTA 6. For starters, you can't save the game once you input even one cheat code. That's why it's best to save before you start using cheats, to avoid losing progress. Using cheat codes will also disable trophies and achievements in GTA 6.

To start using cheat codes in GTA 6, you'll have to get your character to pull up the smartphone by pressing the up arrow on the D-Pad. Then, hold L1+R1, and press the cheat's key combination on the D-Pad.
