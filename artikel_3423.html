Pokémon’s Hyped Countdown Might Be Leading to a Letdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In October and early November, The Pokémon Company started counting down toward the end of the month, as noted by Serebii webmaster Joe Merrick. Using Poké Ball-themed logos created for the franchise's 30th anniversary, Pokémon teased some sort of announcement for Nov. 30 at 14:00 UTC.

The Pokémon Company has a new video set to premiere at that time and, well, it isn't a new Pokémon Winds and Waves trailer. A Pokémon music video called "Evergreen" will premiere on Nov. 30 at 14:00 UTC from the band Official Hige Dandism .

Several Pokémon social media accounts, like those for TCG Pocket and Champions, are promoting the music video as well.

A music video certainly isn't the major franchise announcement or Winds and Waves trailer some fans were hoping for. However, it's possible Pokémon is keeping its cards close to its chest. Who's to say the music video won't include teases of what's to come for Pokémon? After all, Pokémon has used the phrase "To be continued with Pokémon" to promote the video. However, at this time, all indications are that it will just be a music video celebrating 30 years of the franchise.

At the very least, Pokémon fans should expect more Pokémon news to come in February at the latest. For the past few years, the company has hosted a Pokémon Presents stream on Feb. 27 full of exciting announcements; the 2026 stream revealed Pokémon Winds and Waves and introduced their cute starters.
