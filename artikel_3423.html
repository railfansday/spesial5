Harrison Ford’s 10/10 Action Thriller Is Leaving Streaming Soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Indiana Jones star plays a president fighting terrorists at 30,000 feet in this ’90s thriller

Harrison Ford has played plenty of memorable action heroes throughout his career, from Indiana Jones to Jack Ryan. But one of his most entertaining roles puts him in an especially unusual position: the Oval Office. In 1997's Air Force One, Ford plays a U.S. president who must take matters into his own hands when terrorists seize control of the plane carrying him and his family.

With its high-stakes premise, memorable villain, and Ford playing a president who isn't afraid to throw a few punches, Air Force One remains an entertaining slice of '90s action cinema.

Air Force One follows President James Marshall (Ford), who finds himself aboard the presidential aircraft when a group of Russian nationalists hijacks the plane. The terrorists are led by Ivan Korshunov (Gary Oldman), who demands the release of a captured Russian general.

Marshall has publicly declared that he will never negotiate with terrorists, but the hijacking puts that promise to the ultimate test. With his wife, Grace (Wendy Crewson), and daughter, Alice (Liesel Matthews), among the hostages, the president must find a way to outsmart the hijackers and save everyone aboard without giving in to their demands. Rather than simply waiting for help from the ground, Marshall uses his military experience to fight back from inside the aircraft. Meanwhile, Vice President Kathryn Bennett (Glenn Close) faces the difficult task of managing the crisis from the White House.

Air Force One was generally well received by critics, earning a 78% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes, alongside a 66% audience score. Reviews praised its suspenseful action and Ford's performance, even if some of the film's more outlandish moments stretch believability.
