Reminder: Zelda: Ocarina Of Time - Playable Ocarina Available Now On The Nintendo Store

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

When Nintendo lifted the lid on the Ocarina of Time remake, it also announced the release of electronic and playable ocarinas.

HORI's electronic ocarina has already sold out in a lot of locations around the globe, and now alongside the release of the anticipated Switch 2 title this week, Nintendo's playable ocarina has officially gone on sale in certain parts of the world.

It's now live on the Nintendo Store in Japan for 5,500 yen and in Australia for $54.85 AUD. We'll share an update when it's made available in the US and UK. Although it's not like HORI's electronic one, you can still use it within the range of the Switch 2's built in mic to play songs in the game.

"This is a playable version for the musically inclined! In The Legend of Zelda™: Ocarina of Time, play (or simply hum) one of Link's learned ocarina melodies within range of the Nintendo Switch 2 console's built in micophone, and he will perform the song in the game."

As noted, this playable ocarina also includes a leaflet with sheet music. Here are some additional details and the dimensions:

Tuning (Key): Alto C tuning, Materials: ABS, Polyurethane synthetic leather, Polyester, Dimensions: 8.3cm x 15cm x 4.5cm (approx.)

So, if you want this ocarina, you'll want to check your local store ASAP, as it may sell out fast. The Nintendo Store in the US is also currently offering an exclusive Zelda 40th anniversary pin set. You can find out more details in our previous post:
