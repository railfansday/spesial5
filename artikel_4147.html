In a shocking turn, Firaxis, the team behind massive hits like XCOM and Civilization, has been gutted with the owning company, Take-Two, laying off a whopping third of the studio. With 60 major players ousted, including the head of the studio, the surviving team has been left reeling, still working on long-awaited titles that had yet to turn around.

Firaxis is a very specialized team, working entirely on their series XCOM and Civilization to deliver different kinds of large-scale tactics gameplay to millions across the decades. Now, right after the middling release of Civilization 7 and years into the newest entry in XCOM, losing members is the exact opposite of what the team needs.

Despite this, a spokesperson for Take-Two, Firaxis’ parent company, described it as “restructuring and recentering around the studio’s core strength.” The shrinking team is meant to allow them to operate faster, with a bit of a sleeker ship doctrine to keep things tight as they continue development.

Even so, the team is clearly going to see some major hiccups both in morale and speed, right as the current XCOM project had “...faced development struggles and missed a significant milestone over the summer.” Without major senior staff in the picture, especially the loss of studio head Heather Hazen, I can’t imagine they’ll suddenly be able to hit the ground running.

Firaxis had suffered layoffs as recently as this last September, making this new blow almost feel like a punishment for Civilization 7’s mixed reception. The studio has really struggled to keep up with the constant graphical pushes set by the rest of the industry, with many of their most recent titles suffering from frame issues and performance stutters.

Even with their recent hiccups, Firaxis is an absolute titan of strategy gaming, and seeing them continue to spiral is a major blow to the industry. Especially following Take-Two’s major financial win through GTA 6 preorders and the absolute truckload they’re about to make off of the game’s full release, it feels like Firaxis doesn’t need to be hung over the fire like this.

Firaxis is truly one of the original greats, boasting over 30 years of history and genre-defining development that has established what people want from both civilization-building and squad-based tactics. For what it's worth, Firaxis’s mixed launch of Civilization 7 is a major speed bump they needed to be a win.

Their work is some of the most important in the space, bringing tactics titles to millions who wouldn’t have otherwise touched them, giving them an in on a niche but thriving genre that needs the players now more than ever. It feels like the long dev times that have plagued Firaxis have let indie teams and more specialized titles steal their lunch, leaving them out to dry.

It’s hard to say, because performance definitely matters in the gaming industry’s job paths, but this most recent massive cutdown feels shortsighted, to say the least. How is a team that is already struggling to complete a title supposed to speed up after a 33% staff cut, after all? Take-Two has more than enough breathing room, and it feels like they might be getting a little greedy as Grand Theft Auto 6 approaches.
