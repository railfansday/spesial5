Monad faces $190 million insider token unlock larger than its market cap as MON hits record low

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The 16.61 billion MON release equals 140.5% of circulating supply, threatening fresh selling pressure and a potential shake-up in validator voting power.

Monad is preparing to unlock 16.61 billion MON tokens on Nov. 24, exceeding its circulating supply as the cryptocurrency hits record lows.

The release represents 140.5% of the 11.83 billion MON currently circulating and marks the first major insider distribution since the blockchain launched in November 2025.

According to DefiLlama, approximately 10.7 billion MON will go to team members, 4.925 billion to investors, and 987.5 million to the Category Labs treasury. This means its team and investor allocations account for 94% of the release.

The unlock was valued at approximately $226 million in Tokenomist's earlier estimate, but MON's decline has reduced its value to roughly $190 million, still exceeding the token's $137 million market capitalization.

CryptoSlate's data shows MON plunged around 11% over the past 24 hours, touching an all-time low of $0.01152 ahead of the release. It is trading at $0.012 as of press time.

The incoming supply adds to existing market pressure, with further insider distributions scheduled through 2029.

Historical data from Alea Research shows that 16 comparable token unlocks recorded median declines of 25.2% in the preceding month and 6.9% in the following month.

Category Labs has authorized up to $80 million in MON repurchases through Dec. 31, although Alea found no disclosed purchases under the program.

Beyond potential selling, the release could alter how Monad distributes voting power and staking rewards.

The network had 20.73 billion MON backing validator votes across 197 operators on Nov. 22, according to Valopers.

If insiders stake just 2.3 billion MON, approximately 14% of their newly available allocation, those tokens would account for 10% of the resulting voting power, assuming existing stake remains unchanged.

At 25% participation, approximately 4.15 billion MON would enter active staking, representing 16.7% of the expanded total.

That scenario would also reduce gross inflationary rewards per staked MON by 16.7%, assuming issuance and validator commissions remain constant.

The potential redistribution comes amid substantial concentration of Foundation-linked staking allocations.

Alea estimated that Foundation-linked wallets accounted for 78% of active stake on Nov. 1, while Backpack controlled 10.82% of validator voting weight on Nov. 22.

Monad initially prohibited staking locked insider tokens to direct more early rewards toward public holders. The restriction expires with the unlock, allowing newly eligible tokens to compete for staking rewards and validator influence.

The first post-unlock delegations will show whether insiders reinforce the largest validators or spread voting power across smaller operators, while trading activity will reveal how much of the new supply reaches exchanges.
