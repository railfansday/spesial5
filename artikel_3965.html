Try Hellraiser: Revival in This New PS5 Demo, Out Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Saber Interactive is letting you sample the opening moments of Hellraiser: Revival via a new PS5 demo that's been put on the PS Store today. This isn't one that's tied to PS Plus Premium; it's a free download for everyone.

You'll get to play through the game's prologue, and then a "portion" of the first chapter. This means you'll get to see a lot of what the title is all about: gore, action, sex... and more gore. The first hour or so lets you enter the Labyrinth, and then chapter one is more of a stealth sequence where you have to escape your captors.

Since the demo breaks up a piece of the full game, I don't believe that your progress would carry over if you were to buy it afterwards. Still, it's a nice sample of a horror game I had a lot of fun with a few weeks ago.

Opinion Hey, That Hellraiser: Revival Game Is Pretty Good

If you like what you play, Hellraiser: Revival is full price at the moment: £34.99 / $39.99 on the PS Store. I expect the game will go on sale at some point before the year is out, though, so it might be worth waiting and keeping an eye on our coverage of the PS Store sales to come.
