Wolverine PS5's Sales Absolutely Plunged in October

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Marvel’s Wolverine may have started strong in September’s Circana sales report, but it plunged in October.

Sony’s big PS5 exclusive for 2026, which attracted reasonable if utterly unremarkable reviews, ranked 14th in the latest US best-sellers list – dropping dramatically from first position the month of its release.

The game is now 15th overall for 2026 as a whole, down from 11th position in September. It’ll continue to fall with juggernauts like GTA 6 now officially out.

Couple other notes: Marvel's Wolverine dropped from 1st in Sept to 14th in Oct, ranks in top 15 YTD. On the YTD charts, less than $10M separates 8th place from 13th - it's a tight cluster.

The exclusive’s lore accurate lack of legs can likely be attributed to the game’s worse than expected reviews.

While it’s not universally disliked – we awarded it an 8/10, after all – fans understandably expect better from dev Insomniac.

Sony’s running discounts on the game during Black Friday to try and stimulate some enthusiasm around it, and I suspect this may be one of those titles that ends up in a lot of hardware bundles in the future.

To be fair, the platform holder seemed relatively satisfied with the title’s 2.4 million sales during its recent financial report.

I suppose it’s possible the launch numbers really did meet its expectations, but it may now be disappointed with the title’s long-term momentum – or lack thereof.

We’ll probably have to wait until its next investor update before we get more information.
