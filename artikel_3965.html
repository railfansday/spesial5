The Hori Ocarina of Time accessory was slated to launch on November 5, 2026, so it should be available to buy now. However, it isn't necessarily appearing everywhere at the same time; Hori UK is claiming it doesn't hit shelves until November 27, for instance.

Rather than being an instrument in its own right, the Hori Ocarina of Time is a controller that connects with the Switch 2 and lets you play Ocarina songs in-game with buttons placed where the air holes would be. Because it's an exact match to the ocarina Link is using on-screen, it provides a far more immersive experience.

Hori's Ocarina of Time accessory lets you play songs in-game by connecting with the Switch 2, and there are two distinct modes: free play lets you press the finger hole buttons and figure out the tune yourself, while auto-play mode allows you to press one of 12 songs when you hit a button.
