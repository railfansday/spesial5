GTA 6 Differences Between Paint Quality For Vehicle Customization

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

While it might have taken over a decade to develop, Rockstar's work on Grand Theft Auto 6 seems to have more than paid off if its record-breaking sales figures are anything to go by, leaving players absolutely stunned by the sheer level of detail offered by its world. The improvements have also been a bit of a double-edged sword, with GTA 6 facing some complaints of being bloated with too many features and details.

Despite the concerns, however, even players like percoquette_ on X that previously complained about Rockstar's dedication for realism, like how Red Dead Redemption 2's "horse companion's balls shrivel up when its cold," are now "gawking at the level of detail" shown with GTA 6's newest discovery.

As shared by PapaRPG on X, in addition to havin the most realistic cars in the franchise, GTA 6 players have recently discovered that depending on if you take your vehicle to a Pay n Spray compared to a fully-fledged vehicle customization garage, there will be a noticeable difference in quality between the paint jobs with the cheaper one being blistered and less reflective.

While this feature might not have much of a gameplay difference, fans have been absolutely flabbergasted that Rockstar even bothered to consider such a small detail, which actually gives incentives to opt for a more expensive paint job when you want to deck out your vehicle, compared to simply stopping at a cheap garage to quickly change colors to escape your wanted level.

Although not everyone is onboard with GTA 6's gaming takeover, as evidenced by its current review bombing on Metacritic, a growing number of players seem to be coming around on Rockstar's choice to spend so much time polishing the game with extra details, while also filling the world with numerous Easter eggs, locations, and other details to discover.
