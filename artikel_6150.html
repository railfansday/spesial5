The airport lounge wars aren't heating up only among premium credit card issuers. Airlines are stepping up their own lounge investments, too.

American Airlines has been on a tear of its own lately, including upgrades and a huge new Admirals Club at Dallas Fort Worth International Airport (DFW) Terminal C, a massive (and much-needed) new lounge complex at its Charlotte Douglas International Airport (CLT) hub and the reopening of its Flagship Dining offering at Los Angeles International Airport (LAX).

Now, Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport (PHX) is the airline's next base scheduled for a glow-up.

American Airlines currently fields three Admirals Clubs at PHX — two in Concourse A and one in Concourse B at Terminal 4 — all of which are being updated.

The first lounge where flyers will see changes is this diminutive lounge in Concourse A. Effective immediately, there will be a new food service area and expanded food and beverage offerings.

More importantly, though, the airline plans to nearly triple the combined lounge footprint of its Concourse A lounges, which include another Admirals Club near gates A7 and A9; it will increase seating capacity by around 150%, and add new furniture and finishes. Hopefully those will reflect the more sophisticated aesthetics American Airlines has been pivoting toward, starting with its Ronald Reagan National Airport (DCA) outpost.

Over in Concourse B, one of the airline's other Admirals Clubs will see a new self-service food and beverage area (aka a buffet and bar), a 20% increase in seating capacity and new interiors.

“Phoenix continues to be an important hub in our network, and these investments reflect our commitment to giving customers more space, comfort and convenience when they travel through PHX,” American’s chief customer officer Heather Garboden said in an online statement. “While we look forward to the development of the new West Terminal, these enhancements will help create an even more seamless travel experience for the millions of customers who pass through Phoenix each year.”

The updates make sense and are badly needed at an American Airlines hub where the Admirals Clubs frequently become overcrowded.

The airline carries over 20 million passengers through PHX each year and operates over 300 peak daily departures, so expanding and refreshing its lounges there is a priority to continue attracting premium passengers.

Though details are still scant, American Airlines is updating its three Admirals Clubs at PHX. Passengers should see immediate changes at its Concourse A club near gates A19 and A21, but travelers will have to wait for the larger changes, which will include an expanded footprint and updated interiors. It remains to be seen what other amenities might be available.
