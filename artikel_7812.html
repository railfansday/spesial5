We’re looking for a Manager of GTM Engineering to help build the data and activation engine behind Tebra’s next generation of outbound growth. You’ll help create and evolve a comprehensive Total Addressable Market database of independent healthcare practices and related businesses, combining public, proprietary, and third-party data into actionable segments, signals, and outbound workflows. This role sits at the intersection of data engineering, Clay, GTM systems, and outbound strategy—and is ideal for someone who can both architect the system and get hands-on building the workflows that turn market intelligence into a pipeline.
