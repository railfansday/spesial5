The Best Beauty Gifts From Target, According to an ELLE Editor

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The retailer is stocked with the season’s most-wanted brands, such as SACHEU, Saltair, and Morphe—here’s what to add to your list.

Gift-giving comes naturally to associate beauty commerce writer Emma Aerin Becker; in fact, it’s her love language. “There’s nothing I enjoy more than surprising a loved one with a little token of appreciation—whether it’s a luxe perfume they’ve been talking about or a small accessory they pointed out while window shopping,” she says. “I like to hunt down the most personalized presents for everyone on my list.”

When Becker shops for fellow beauty lovers, Target is her go-to destination: “The new Beauty Studio is like an adult playground. From press-on nails to perfumes, the aisles are stocked with just about everything.” To help inspire your own shopping list, she’s sharing the must-gift finds she’s treating her friends and family to, from a sandalwood-scented body butter from Saltair to a SACHEU lip-liner set.

“For a skin care obsessive, there’s nothing quite like lathering up with a moisturizing lotion from head to toe—especially in the winter months when your skin can feel drier than ever. This body butter from Saltair is creamy and deeply hydrating, and smells incredible.”

“Is there anything better than a man who smells good? Gift the guy in your life this Papatui kit. It allows him to test several scents and determine which notes work best for him.”

“I’ve recently fallen in love with shave oils, and this one from Athena Club is one of my favorites. The vanilla scent is delicious, and the hydrating formula leaves behind a smooth, bump-free finish every time.”

“Eye masks elevate any beauty routine. And the adorable ribbon detail on this one makes it feel extra special. It’s a thoughtful pick for the friend who loves a good self-care moment, or anyone who could use a little more beauty sleep.”

“Claw clips are one of the easiest ways to accessorize any hair look. This six-piece set is adorably festive, featuring red, green, and white clips adorned with bows. The mini size makes them great for anyone, of any age, who loves getting into the holiday spirit.”

“Lip liner is essential to any makeup look. However, between lattes and lots of chatter, it’s often the first product to disappear. But SACHEU’s STAY-N formula stains skin with a wash of color, so they’ll never have to worry about constantly reapplying.”

“Featuring three limited-edition shades—Matte Bone, Matte Rose, and Cranberry Shimmer—this set lets them create endless eye looks. Plus, each silky-smooth shadow delivers all-day pigment and crease-proof wear.”

“Cleansing wipes are every girl’s best friend. Whoever you give these to will think, ‘thank you,’ when they get home from a long day.”
