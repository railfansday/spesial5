Here’s What an Athleisure-Obsessed Editor Would Shop From lululemon’s Rare Black Friday Sale

As a lululemon stan since my tween years (I’ve been rocking my Aligns for over a decade), I know that the brand’s workout gear and loungewear are worth the money—but that doesn’t necessarily mean I want to pay full price. Thankfully, lululemon’s rare Black Friday sale is here, bringing savings on the brand’s most popular products.

Head Straight to lululemon’s Black Friday Sale

If you’re as lululemon-obsessed as I am, you know that the brand pretty much never hosts sales (aside from Black Friday and New Year’s)—meaning now is the time to finally invest in the viral Define jacket or stock up on Align leggings (a Cosmo editor favorite) while they’re as low as $59. I also found a handful of gift-worthy stocking stuffers for the lululemon fanatic on your list, like mini belt bags and clippable card pouches. Just be sure to act fast if you see something you like—stock is already selling out.

You don't need to spend a fortune to score some Lulu presents, and these finds are proof. The Everywhere Belt Bag is one of our editors’ all-time favorite gifts, and the adorable Nano size is on sale for just $14. If your underwear drawer could use a refresh, this pack of seamless thongs nets out to just $7 apiece. And I even spotted some apparel for under $30, like this classic tank top.

If your sports bras have seen better days, now’s your chance to invest in some new ones while they’re up to 35% off. My personal favorite is the Like a Cloud bra, which is made from a ridiculously lightweight, breathable fabric that keeps boob sweat at bay. What’s more, I spotted a pair of high-rise leggings for a whopping 67% off—the best deal I’ve ever seen.

lululemon Scuba Oversized Full-Zip Hoodie

Leggings are always a coveted find during lululemon’s sales, and this year didn’t disappoint. Need help deciding between styles? Aligns are ideal for yoga and low-impact workouts with their stretchy, whisper-soft fabric, while the Wunder Trains are durable enough for high-impact activities. Plus, the cult-favorite ABC pants are on sale in select colors right now, and they make a great gift for the man in your life (wink, wink).

Looking to spoil your giftee (or yourself) this season? Rest assured, I spotted plenty of splurge-worthy finds, including coats and bags in lululemon's We Made Too Much section. Down jackets and vests are particularly worth the investment right now—their insulative fill keeps you warm without weighing you down.
