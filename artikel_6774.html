On the Supreme Court, I'm all in favor of them. Those are appointed positions, not elected ones, and, back in the day, justices regularly returned to private life and their old clients back home. For example, Justice Benjamin Curtis was on the Court for six years, long enough for him to write his classic dissent in Dred Scott v. Sandford. He then quit over money and because he hated Chief Justice Roger Taney. (Good plan.) He went back to Boston and his previous client list.

(In a very odd twist of fate, Curtis returned to Washington as lead counsel for President Andrew Johnson in the latter's impeachment trial before the Senate. He won that one.)
