The Best Early Black Friday Apple Deals, According to Our Editors

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

The latest AirTags, MacBooks, and AirPods are up to 23 percent off.

Our editors look forward to rare discounts on Apple devices every Black Friday, and this year is no exception. Leading up to Amazon’s official sale (which runs from November 19 to 30), we’re already seeing up to 23 percent off Apple’s most coveted tech.

The items we deem worthy of shopping right away? AirTag tracking devices are on sale for their lowest price ever, and select AirPods headphones like the Pro 3 and Max 2 are up to 20 percent off. That said, discounts on iPads and Apple Watches are worth waiting a little longer for (save for a record-low price on the Watch Ultra 4), but we expect better deals once the official sale begins.

All to say, if you want to get a head start on shopping, there are plenty of options available now. Ahead, see what we’re adding to our carts from Apple this Black Friday.

Lost luggage is a thing of the past with Apple’s ingenious AirTags, which give you peace of mind by tracking the location of your prized possessions. Snag one for a record-low price of $23 ahead of Black Friday.

Apple’s AirPods Pro are worth the hype. Complete with features like Active Noise Cancellation and transparency modes, a water-resistant design, and silicone ear tips for a customizable fit, they’re bound to become your daily headphones. Currently, they’re at a typical sale price of $199, but they’ve been as low as $169 during past sales.

Get $250 off the latest version of the MacBook Air. This 13-inch computer weighs just 2.7 pounds and boasts up to 18 hours of battery life, making it easy to tote to and from work, class, your local coffee shop—you name it.

Track your steps, check your notifications, and play music right from your wrist with the 2026 Apple Watch Ultra 4. Designed specifically with the athlete in mind, it features Apple’s highest-tech heart rate sensor, a full suite of health and wellness metrics, a GPS monitor, and water resistance up to 100 meters—making it the perfect gift. It just returned to a record-low price of $700, and it could drop even further on Black Friday.

Everyone deserves a pair of headphones that are as chic as they are functional. If you prefer an over-ear style, add this highly covetable pair to your cart, as they tend to sell out quickly when they’re on sale. We expect even steeper savings once Black Friday arrives, but you’ve got a better chance of securing a pair if you shop now.

The 2026 MacBook Pro, Apple’s most sophisticated laptop, is a worthy splurge, even at full price. Updated with an ultra-quick M5 chip, this computer boasts enhanced graphics and a speedier performance than past models. Plus, it boasts a Liquid Retina display for crystal-clear imagery, an all-day battery, and built-in AI tech.

Ahead of Black Friday, the standard iPad is $49 off. Though this is an entry-level option, it boasts features like Touch ID, a 10-hour battery life, and an impressive 11.2-inch Liquid Retina display.

Whether you’re new to the world of wearables or need to upgrade an older device, the Apple Watch SE makes it easy to do so without breaking the bank. It’s not on sale yet, but it was at a record-low price of $199 in July, so we’re expecting a similar price once November 27 comes.

Don’t let this tablet’s tiny size fool you: This is the perfect device for watching Netflix on the go, devouring your latest literary fiction read, or jotting down notes during flights. It’s only $30 off right now, but it was on sale for a record-low price of $349 last Black Friday.

The perfect companion to the Apple iPad, the Pencil Pro allows for seamless note-taking, sketching, and coloring. Just like a real pencil, this updated model is equipped with angle and pressure sensitivity for precise lines.

If you haven’t jumped over to team Wireless Charging, now’s the time to do so. Not only does this MagSafe charger juice up your phone quickly, but it’s longer than your average charging cord. What’s more, it’s also compatible with your AirPods, letting it serve dual purposes on your nightstand.

This case turns your iPad into a laptop while also giving your investment some welcome protection. It comes complete with a smooth Apple keyboard and trackpad.
