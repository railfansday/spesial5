"Worth Your Time" - Digital Foundry Delivers Its Tech Review For Zelda: Ocarina Of Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Reviews for The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time are live, and that includes a tech-focused assessment from John Linneman over at Digital Foundry.

Naturally, Linneman heaps praise upon Nintendo's remake, with really only a couple of minor nitpicks. To start, resolution ranges from 1080p up to 1440p when docked and from 720p to 1080p during handheld mode. It's noted that handheld mode does implement some tweaks such as more aggressive level-of-detail distances and texture downgrades, but it's overall a very crisp, handsome game.

As for upscaling, Nintendo has seemingly implemented FSR over DLSS. While this isn't a particularly disappointing development, Ocarina of Time does suffer from instances of shimmering, which we noted in our review.

Frame rate, however, is very impressive. While the original was a paltry 20fps (which, to clear, was absolutely fine at the time) with the 3DS remake upping this to 30fps, the Switch 2 remake guns for 60fps and hits it consistently. There may be some "very tiny loading hiccups" here and there, but it otherwise remains stable throughout.

Linneman highlights a bunch of other neat little flourishes, including the fog effects seen in environments along with the lighting from the lava in the Fire Temple. Overall, it's a stunning game, and certainly one of Nintendo's best efforts when it comes to raw presentation and performance.
