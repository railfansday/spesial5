Ubisoft Is Laying Off Staff From Two Of Its Biggest Studios

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Ubisoft has confirmed that it is laying off a total of 166 members of staff from its Canadian offices.

The teams affected, Ubisoft Montréal and Ubisoft Québec, are arguably two of the most prominent studios in the corporation. The former has been responsible for some of the most well-regarded entries in the Assassin's Creed series along with key entries in the Far Cry franchise.

The latter, meanwhile, worked on the excellent Assassin's Creed Shadows and also developed the underrated Switch gem Immortals Fenyx Rising.

GamesIndustry.biz states that the layoffs account for less than 4% of the total workforce at the studios, and that the employees affected were working on projects that had either been cancelled or significantly changed.

Here's the statement provided by Ubisoft:

"Ubisoft is reducing its workforce by 166 positions across its Montréal and Québec City operations. As our portfolio evolves, our production needs are changing, requiring us to adjust the size of some teams to ensure our resources are aligned with current production priorities and business needs.

"We deeply value the contributions of these teams and recognise the impact this may have on our colleagues. We are committed to supporting them through this process with care and respect. Those affected are being provided with severance, extended health care benefits, career transition services and other resources."

Ubisoft's next major release is Rayman Legends Retold, a reimagined remake of the 2013 classic Rayman Legends, developed in collaboration by Ubisoft Milan and Montpellier.
