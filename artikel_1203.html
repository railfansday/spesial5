Nico Paz’s stance on potential Man Utd move and Real Madrid return revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

According to The Athletic, Como star Nico Paz does not currently favour rejoining Real Madrid this summer, and Manchester United may not be his first choice, especially if the Red Devils don’t qualify for the Champions League.

Como star Nico Paz inevitably continues to grab headlines worldwide thanks to his fantastic performances under Cesc Fabregas.

Paz has six goals and eight assists in 16 appearances across all competitions this season, and Real Madrid have an option to re-sign him for €80m in the summer.

According to The Athletic, however, the Argentine currently does not favour a return to the Spanish capital.

“This stance might alter if he is assured of a prominent role — that has not been the case,” The Athletic clarifies.

According to the report, Manchester United see the Argentine as a potential replacement for Bruno Fernandes, whose contract expires at the end of the season.

The Athletic explains that the Red Devils “may not be Nico Paz’s first-choice destination, and Champions League qualification is a major factor.”

Manchester United saw Nico Paz’s quality firsthand in October, when the Argentine scored two goals and provided one assist in Como’s 4-1 victory in a Champions League match at the Stadio Sinigaglia.

This is Nico Paz’s third season in Como. The Azzurri re-signed him from Real Madrid last summer for €60m.
