Guardiola ‘honour’ at Italy national team talks: ‘I probably would have accepted’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Pep Guardiola says that it was ‘one of the greatest honours’ to be considered for the role of Italy national team head coach during Paolo Maldini’s brief reign as technical director of the FIGC and says that he ‘probably would have accepted’ had the offer come a year or two later down the line.

Guardiola, along with Carlo Ancelotti and then later Andrea Pirlo, were among the names that Maldini had sought to appoint as head coach of the Italy national team during his brief reign as technical director of the FIGC over the summer.

In the end, neither Guardiola nor Ancelotti were available, Pirlo’s appointment was blocked, and Maldini decided to step down from his position with the Italian FA after just 16 days at the helm. Maldini was then replaced by Claudio Ranieri and Roberto Mancini has since returned as CT.

Guardiola, speaking in an interview his former Barcelona teammate Romario on his eponymous YouTube channel, said that it was one of the biggest honours of his career to be considered for the Italy job and admitted that had he not needed a sabbatical, he ‘probably would have accepted’ the Azzurri offer.

“It’s the first time I’ve spoken about it, but yes, I spoke with them,” Guardiola told Romario. “It was an honour. I felt embarrassed saying no to a country or squad like Italy. It was one of the greatest honours I’ve ever had, that they thought of me to be the coach of Italy, but I realised I had to stop.

“If they’d approached me a year or two later, I probably would have accepted.

“They approached, with Paolo Maldini and Leonardo, whom I love dearly, at a time where I needed to take a break and come to Bahia to spend more time for myself. It’s been 18 years of coaching games every three days, three days, three days. You have to win trebles, play every three days, play every three days. And then there was a moment when I said ‘oof’ [enough].”

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
