Report Celtic talent Nygren targeted by Atalanta and Bologna

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Serie A sides Atalanta and Bologna are reportedly keeping a close eye on the situation of Celtic midfielder Benjamin Nygren, who is becoming a transfer target for January.

According to TEAMtalk and Sky Sport Italia transfer pundit Gianluca Di Marzio, the Sweden international has already attracted attention from Italian clubs for a potential swoop.

He only joined Celtic in the summer of 2025 from Nordsjaelland for €2.5m, and was very impressive as the Hoops secured the Scottish Premiership title and Scottish Cup.

The player scored 21 goals in all competition during his debut campaign for the club, and so far this season found the net six times in 14 league games.

It is reported that while both Bologna and Atalanta have Nygren on their radar, no formal negotiations have yet been opened.

His contract runs to June 2030, so it would take a very strong proposal to convince Celtic into an early sale.

The asking price is believed to be in the region of €24m and many contend this will be his last season in Scotland.
