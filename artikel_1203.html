Report Robinio Vaz could return to Roma just 6 months into flop Hull City loan

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Hull City are reportedly ready to pull the plug early on the Robinio Vaz loan, so Roma must find him another club in the January transfer window.

The striker is still only 19 years old and was a big investment when the Giallorossi paid €22m to sign him from Olympique Marseille in January 2026.

However, he struggled for playing time at the Stadio Olimpico and was sent out on loan to Hull City for €2m, with an option to make it permanent at the end of the season for another €30m.

The Brazilian’s situation has not improved there either, making just seven competitive appearances for the Tigers and only one senior start.

According to Tuttomercatoweb, Hull City are ready to interrupt the loan move early and send Vaz back to Roma.

There is little room for him under Gian Piero Gasperini’s tenure right now either, so another loan elsewhere seems the most likely option.

Gasperini never disguised that he saw little chance for Vaz to flourish in his style of football, and the purchase was one of the biggest causes of tension between the coach and former director of sport Frederic Massara.
