Pi Network price under pressure – Can PI defend $0.065 support?

Pi Network [PI] has been under continued selling pressure with no real sign of a near-term recovery. At the time of writing, the token was down 9.50% over 24 hours, trading at $0.0660.

In general, the wider crypto market has been down 1.98% in the same period. However, PI’s price slide has been steeper than the market as a whole, indicating very thin liquidity and negative sentiment surrounding the token.

The ongoing outflows from the U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs have also contributed to a risk-off environment, which has put further pressure on the altcoins.

The daily chart highlighted the extent of PI’s bearish move. At the time of writing, PI was trading at $0.066, within the lower 0%–23.6% Fibonacci retracement zone. This made $0.065 a critical, immediate support level that traders need to watch for. The latest daily candle already tested this level before recovering slightly, although the recovery was limited. This left buyers with little room to absorb further selling pressure.

Additionally, the momentum indicators offered little encouragement for a recovery. The MACD remained below both its signal and zero lines, with the negative histogram expanding over the latest sessions. Furthermore, the OBV recorded a marked decline, a clear sign that buyers could not beat the sell pressure.

As a result, for traders, $0.065 is a key level to watch. Breaking below this level would cause PI to crash toward the -23.6% Fibonacci extension, near $0.061.
