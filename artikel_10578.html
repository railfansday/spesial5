World of Warcraft Forever Officially Hits Player Peak it Hasn't Seen in 15 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The last time this many people were playing WoW, GTA 5 wasn't even out

It was already clear that World of Warcraft: Forever, Blizzard's reimagining of the early days of the MMO, was a smash hit. During early access, before the game's full release, it brought back plenty of lapsed players and garnered praise. Now, with the dust having settled slightly two weeks after release, it is becoming clear that Forever is returning World of Warcraft to a level of popularity it hasn't seen in years.

To celebrate Forever's continued success, the official World of Warcraft X account shared a handful of fun statistics ranging from how many characters and guilds have been created to how many times players have died. But it's a statistic shared by Holly Longdale, the game's executive producer and vice president, that really puts things into perspective. According to Longdale, World of Warcraft has more weekly players than at any point in the past 15 years thanks to Forever.

That means the last time this many people were playing World of Warcraft, Cataclysm was the game's most recent expansion. That last popularity spike was also closer to the actual release of the MMO in 2004 than it is to Forever's release this year. Even World of Warcraft Classic, Blizzard's playable trip down memory lane, failed to ignite this much interest after launching in 2019.​​​​​​​

While Longdale's reveal is perhaps the most noteworthy​​​​​​​, there are some cool tidbits in the statistics Blizzard posted about Forever's first two weeks. Over 15 million characters have been created, and they've died nearly 200 million times in total​​​​​​​. That would average out to just over 13 deaths per character. Nearly 30 million deaths were due to PvP kills, though the deadliest boss in the game is Shadowfang Keep's​​​​​​​ Archmage Arugal.

Forever is still fresh, though, and only time will tell if Blizzard can keep the game's momentum going as the weeks go on. The developer has consistently updated and tweaked​​​​​​​ the game to better address fan feedback and pain points. What would also be interesting to know is whether​​​​​​​ Forever has brought enough lapsed players back into the fold for them to return to the MMO's main retail version as well​​​​​​​. When the game's next expansion, The Last Titan, releases next year, perhaps​​​​​​​ the game will see a markedly​​​​​​​ larger player count​​​​​​​ as well.
