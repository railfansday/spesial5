British PM Burnham ‘Won’t Be Playing’ GTA 6 Despite It Being ‘a British Success Story’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Covering the video games industry since 2017, with experience in news, articles, and reviews. Currently a writer at DualShockers and gaming magazines.

Grand Theft Auto 6 might take players to a fictional version of Florida, but its success is apparently something the British government is more than happy to claim! After more than 10 years of anticipation, Rockstar’s latest blockbuster has finally arrived in the UK, and Downing Street has already found a reason to celebrate.

Oh, but there's just one small problem: Prime Minister Andy Burnham won't be joining the party, as his schedule is apparently a little too packed for a trip to Vice City.

Speaking to reporters on Thursday (thanks, The Independent), a Downing Street spokesperson described Rockstar Games as a "British success story made in Edinburgh," adding that many fans were already saying GTA 6 had been worth the wait.

Rockstar’s latest release has already attracted millions of viewers.

When asked whether Burnham would be playing the game himself, however, the response was considerably less enthusiastic: "His diary is very full. He won't be playing it."

It's a pretty amusing contrast, especially considering just how many people in the UK have been making room in their schedules for Rockstar's new release! But hey, I get him – we’re talking about an experience that easily will get hold of 100+ hours of your life.

Although GTA 6 is set in the fictional state of Leonida, inspired by Florida, a significant part of the game's development took place in Scotland. Rockstar North, the studio responsible for much of the franchise's development, is based in Edinburgh, making the government's description of the game as a British success story more than just a bit of patriotic enthusiasm.

When asked whether Burnham would be playing the game himself, however, the response was considerably less enthusiastic: "His diary is very full. He won't be playing it."

The studio has been one of the UK's most recognizable names in the gaming industry for years, and GTA 6's launch is another major achievement for the country's game development scene. After all, it's not every day that one of the most anticipated entertainment releases in the world comes from a studio with such a strong presence in the UK, right?

Still, Burnham's absence from the player count might be a little disappointing for anyone hoping to see the Prime Minister share his first impressions of Vice City. At least Downing Street has made its position clear: the game is worth celebrating, even if its leader won't be playing it.

Rockstar’s UK staff will vote on union recognition from November 12 to 26, with the voting period spanning GTA 6’s November 19 launch.

While Burnham apparently has more pressing matters to attend to, plenty of other people in the UK have found a way to squeeze GTA 6 into their day! A poll commissioned by Uswitch earlier this month estimated that around 2.8 million UK adults would play the game on launch day alone, with many expected to take annual leave or even call in sick to work.

The game's arrival has also prompted schools to remind students that its release isn't an excuse to skip lessons. GTA 6 carries an 18 age rating, and some schools have even introduced prize draws for vouchers among students who attend classes, hoping to keep attendance numbers up during the launch frenzy!

Honestly, it's hard to imagine a better illustration of just how big this release has become. Rockstar has managed to turn a video game launch into an event significant enough to reach Downing Street, spark discussions about school attendance, and even inspire millions of people to rearrange their schedules.

Burnham might not be joining Jason and Lucia on their adventures, but at least he can take comfort in knowing that a major British studio is behind one of the biggest games of the year. And, judging by the number of people taking the day off, his diary might be the only thing keeping him away from Vice City.
