GTA 6 Could Leave Millions of Desks Empty on Launch Day, Survey Suggests

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Covering the video games industry since 2017, with experience in news, articles, and reviews. Currently a writer at DualShockers and gaming magazines.

If you were thinking about calling in sick on November 19 to spend the day exploring Vice City, don't worry: you're definitely not alone. In fact, a new gaming survey suggests that millions of Americans are planning to rearrange their work or school schedules for the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6.

And while some of us might have to get creative with our excuses, one company has decided to make things considerably easier for its employees.

According to a survey commissioned by PCMag and conducted by YouGov, approximately 12.5 million US gamers intend to take paid time off or adjust their schedules to play Rockstar's highly anticipated open-world adventure – that's around 8% of adult gamers in the country, which is quite a number for a game that hasn't even hit store shelves yet!

The survey questioned 2,024 American adults, including 1,154 people who identified as gamers, between October 23 and 27. Among those planning to make room for GTA 6, 3% intend to take at least two days off, another 3% are planning to take a full day, and 2% will either take a half-day or adjust their working hours.

As GTA 6's launch approaches, everyone wants part of the PR pie, including internet ISPs.

Interestingly, Gen Z appears to be the most committed group, with 13% of respondents in that generation planning to change their schedules. So, if your younger colleague suddenly disappears next Thursday, you might already know what's going on.

While most players will probably have to negotiate with their bosses to have some free time to play Rockstar’s title, Worldworthy has decided to take a different approach. The company, which publishes the Worldworthy 100 global rankings, actually announced that employees who want to play GTA 6 can take up to three paid days off starting November 19. No doctor's notes, questionable excuses, or suspiciously timed dentist appointments required!

"We decided not to fight it," founder and editor Ivgeni Zarubinski said in the press release sent to the media. "Everyone knows where a large part of the world will be next Thursday. Nobody on our team has to invent a dentist appointment."

Logitech wants employees to reap the personal and professional benefits of gaming.

The offer is optional, meaning employees who would rather keep working can do so, and the company's rankings desk will remain open throughout launch week. Still, it's a pretty generous way to acknowledge just how big this release is expected to be. Zarubinski even joked that after spending the year ranking the world's best airports, pizzerias, and cities, his team would get three days to "rank the streets of Vice City instead." To be honest with you, that's probably a much more entertaining assignment!

Of course, the survey also shows that the excitement isn't universal. Around 68% of US adults said they don't plan to play GTA 6 when it launches, while 14% intend to fit the game into their regular routines. Another 4% plan to clear their evenings and weekends rather than take time off.

Still, with GTA 6 arriving on November 19, it's clear that plenty of players are already making room for Rockstar's next adventure. The game follows the success of Grand Theft Auto 5, which has sold more than 200 million copies since its release in 2013. And if you're lucky enough to have a boss who understands the importance of visiting Vice City on launch day, maybe it's time to start planning that extended weekend!
