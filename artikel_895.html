Wattimena wins hearts with win vs Littler: 'Beat him, the crowd loves you'

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Jermaine Wattimena put in a stunning performance to come up with a 10-8 win and dump Luke Littler out of the Grand Slam in the second round; you can watch the Grand Slam of Darts in Wolverhampton live on Sky Sports until November 22

Jermaine Wattimena was visibly emotional following his massive 10-8 win over Luke Littler in the last 16 at the Grand Slam of Darts, taking in the moment as the Wolverhampton crowd sang his name.

An English crowd chanting so heartily for a Dutch player is somewhat of a rarity, especially when they are taking on an Englishman, but it was the sensational manner of his victory over the world No 1 and back-to-back Grand Slam champion that won them over.

Littler was aiming for a Grand Slam three-peat after his wins in 2024 and 2025 but with the Dutchman prevailing, it is still only Phil Taylor and Michael van Gerwen who have achieved such a feat.

Wattimena led 3-2 before running away to get 7-2 up against Littler. He then held on as Littler fought back to 9-8 and pinned 77 on tops for what is now the biggest win of his career.

It was made all the sweeter given the fact Wattimena has felt the pain of a massive Littler comeback before. He was 7-2 up against 'The Nuke' at the Matchplay in 2025 before eventually being beaten.

"Brilliant. One word needed, brilliant," Wattimena said.

"To be honest, I was thinking about the Matchplay in 2025 when I was 7-2 up. Now I am feeling that again but then the 77 goes in.

"I beat Luke Humphries in the European Championship and this is a bigger win than Luke Humphries then.

"I think you have seen me playing and I played with a lot of confidence. I was really concentrating in the match and I think I can go further than this.

"To be honest, I always play well against Luke Littler but to finally beat him on stage, I am really happy with that.

"I think my scoring power was better than his scoring power. He had to do it against me as well, but I don't think he did.

"They sang my name and I was emotional. That was really brilliant.

"If you beat Luke Littler then the crowd loves you."

Wattimena beat Littler back in August on the way to picking up a Players Championship title.

It was that win that reinforced for Wattimena that he could do the same again on the TV stage.

"I think (that win) gave me some confidence and then you know that he is not unbeatable as well. He is a human, he is not a robot," he added.

"He is a good player, he's an amazing player, everyone knows it but I am more than happy to beat him here at the Grand Slam.

"He got me here two years ago with a high score, but now I got one back.

"When you go to a tournament, you have to think that you can win the title, to be honest.

"You play tournaments to win something. It is a long way to go, but I am here for a win.

"I have got a day off tomorrow, so I have got nothing to do. I will be jumping in the jacuzzi, jumping in the swimming pool, so I'm all relaxed tomorrow."
