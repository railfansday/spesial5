Rangers edge Aberdeen in five-goal thriller to cut Celtic's Premiership lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Rangers vs Aberdeen. Scottish Premiership.

Ibrox StadiumAttendance: Attendance50,738.

Rangers are just one point behind in the Premiership title race after a dramatic fightback against a rejuvenated Aberdeen.

Derek McInnes' side needed to win their game in hand to cut Celtic's lead at the top of the table, but did not get it all their own way on a rainy night in Govan.

After Dan Neil and Ryan Naderi came close, Aberdeen took a shock lead to silence Ibrox. Nico Raskin thought he had cleared Brad Lyons header, but VAR confirmed the ball was already over the line.

Ivor Pandur's brilliant stop denied Ayoub Mouloua a second for the Dons before a triple half-time change sent the hosts on their way to victory.

Minsu Kim's first involvement resulted in the equaliser as Marius Muller spilled his shot into Ryan Naderi's path and he made no mistake.

Just 65 seconds later, Rangers were in front when Djeidi Gassama fired Naderi's ball into the net. Nico Raskin slotted in a third, but Findlay Marshall's wonderful strike pulled Mario Frick's side back into the game with Pandur pulling off another brilliant stop to make sure the three points stayed in Glasgow.

Rangers can move top of the Premiership on Saturday if they can halt Falkirk's winning run. Aberdeen remain eighth.

"But our naivety with the second goal is beyond me, because I'm not sure it was a free-kick in the first place, but that said, we turned back, they allowed the extra man.
