Why Airlines Are Suddenly Racing To Launch Media Networks

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

At least four airlines launched advertising businesses this year in search of new revenue as fuel costs have challenged profit margins across aviation. Better in-flight internet has also enabled richer audience targeting to sell to advertisers.

While airlines have pitched advertising for a couple of years, a crop of new ad networks have popped up in the past six months.

Some of these new networks are already trying to differentiate themselves through unique audiences or different formats like experiential ads.

In June, retail adtech firm Kevel partnered with Scandinavian Airlines to launch a commerce media network, pitching personalized ads across mobile apps and the web.
