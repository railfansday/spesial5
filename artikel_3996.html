GTA 6 First-Person Glitch Discovered, And It Works On Xbox & PS5

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

As you probably know, GTA 6 is not a game that's designed to let you freely play in first-person (at least not at launch), but thanks to some sleuthing from various GTA fans, it's been discovered that you can actually play this way.

This is thanks to a glitch that occurs when you change a specific setting and then spam the "View" button (formerly known as the "Back" button) on Xbox to trick the game into forcing you into first-person mode.

Here's how you do it (at your own risk - this is not an intended feature!):

The same instructions apply if you're on PS5, but it's the touchpad rather than the View button.

I've only been messing with this glitch a little bit so far, but it's pretty insane at first glance. You can run around, drive vehicles... do whatever you want to do. The only problem is that you can't actually back out of first-person once you're in it, although it seems some situations (like being arrested or going into a hair salon) will force you back into third-person.

So, why is Rockstar hiding this feature from us? It's probably because it's still in a work-in-progress, and there are some items that don't seem to function properly with the first-person viewpoint yet. This does seem to indicate a first-person perspective is in the works though, similar to how it was added into GTA 5 later down the line.
