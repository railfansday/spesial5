"We Saw Your Hopeful Requests" - Fabraz Announces Demon Tides Switch 2 Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Launching January 2027 alongside the Switch version

Critically-acclaimed developer Fabraz has announced it will be releasing its 3D platformer Demon Tides for the Switch on 21st January 2027.

As part of this news, it's also been revealed Demon Tides is launching for the Switch 2 (and PlayStation 5) on the same date! These versions of the game are done in partnership with PM Studios and will get the physical treatment at some point "later" next year.

"Demon Tides will launch on the Switch, Switch 2 and PS5 on January 21st 2027! Demon Tides had a critically acclaimed PC launch with a 95% Overwhelmingly Positive rating on Steam, 84 on metacritic and 86 on opencritic."

Fangamer's physical version of Demon Turf + Demon Tides will also be going into production "soon", with pre-orders for this scheduled to go live in early 2027. This version will also come bundled with Neon Splash.

Fabraz has also confirmed the Switch 1 version of the game will feature an option to uncap the frame rate, meaning you play the Switch 1 version in 60fps on Switch 2.

As for how each version of the game performs on its respective system, you can expect 30fps at 720p/1080p (docked and handheld) on Switch. And on Switch 2, the game runs at 60fps in performance mode, 30fps in quality mode, and displays at a 1080p resolution.

In some other news, Fabraz recently rolled out a patch for Atari's revival of Bubsy, bumping his 4D outing up to Version 1.2.1.
