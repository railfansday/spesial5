Pierre Sage’s Lyon return ends in disappointment for Crystal Palace

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Pierre Sage felt Crystal Palace were “really far off” his expectations after suffering a 3-2 away Europa League defeat by his former side Lyon.

Corentin Tolisso opened the scoring for the hosts and Ernest Nuamah doubled Lyon’s advantage late in a first half devoid of any shots on target for the visitors.

Yéremy Pino halved the deficit five minutes after the break, before Pavel Sulc restored the two-goal lead, and substitute Daichi Kamada’s late goal proved a mere consolation.

“At the end, we were frustrated by the result, but in the first half we have to be frustrated in the way we play,” said Sage, speaking to PalaceTV.

“I think we didn’t play this first half. We are really far off our standards in the mental side of the play, in the tactical side of the play, because the players are too high between our lines and we really struggled a lot with that.

“When you give back the ball to your opponent, you suffer a lot and we were better in the second half. You cannot win this kind of game by playing only one half.”

Sage’s side got the better of his predecessor, Oliver Glasner, after they bounced back to beat Nottingham Forest 3-1 at Selhurst Park on Sunday.

After that victory, the Frenchman called for more consistency from his team, but on Thursday night was left ruing missed opportunities and goals conceded too easily by last season’s Conference League winners.

Sage was sacked by Lyon in January 2025 after a 14-month spell with the Ligue 1 outfit. Like Glasner’s, his own reunion ended in disappointment.

He told Uefa: “It’s a very disappointing return because we lost. I can’t take satisfaction from coming back home or seeing familiar faces when the result isn’t there.

“The scoreline may have been close, but there was a big difference in the way the two teams played.

“We improved considerably after the break and gave ourselves a chance, but conceding that third goal made things difficult.

“I think our second-half efforts deserved an equaliser, although winning would have been a generous reward given that we only really played [to our level] for one half.”

The veteran captain said: “It has been a long time coming. The fans were up for it, the players were up for it so we’re very happy with the performance.”

When asked about Evanilson’s header, which Smith supplied the assist for, he replied: “It wasn’t my best cross but I put it in an area, what a finish from him and I think we deserved that little bit of luck.

“From there on we went on to win the game but in the second half we got a little bit tired but the quality that came off the bench is great and we look forward to Sunday now.

“It’s a great header, he deserved it because he has worked very hard this season and many more to come from him.”
