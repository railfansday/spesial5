Serie A Liveblog: Juventus-Venezia, Milan-Frosinone, Atalanta-Inter and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from today’s five Serie A games, Juventus-Venezia, Bologna-Udinese, Sassuolo-Genoa, Milan-Frosinone and Atalanta-Inter.

We begin at 11.30 GMT (12.30 CET) with Juventus in dire need of a victory as they host revitalised Venezia.

At 14.00 GMT (15.00 CET), new Udinese coach Paolo Zanetti visits on form Bologna, while Sassuolo welcome Roberto D’Aversa for his debut against Genoa.

Milan under pressure take on impressive high-flyers Frosinone at 17.00 GMT (18.00 CET), then Inter visit Maurizio Sarri’s Atalanta at 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET).

Players and coaches have a red mark on their cheek as part of the annual Serie A campaign highlighting the scourge of violence against women.

Cristian Chivu warns Inter ‘failed the exam’ in their 4-4 draw with Atalanta, criticising some of his players. ‘We were too soft, need to be more determined in the duels.’

Maurizio Sarri hails Atalanta’s ‘character’ to fight back from 4-2 down for a 4-4 draw with Inter, but warns he will not abandon his tactical ideas. ‘If they want man-marking, then hire someone else.’
