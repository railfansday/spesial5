Liverpool’s 2027-28 Home, Away and Third Kits ‘Leaked’

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The 2026-27 Premier League season is only 10 games old, but predictions are already being made about Liverpool's kits for next term.

Andoni Iraola's men look unlikely to cement this season's kit in the history books by posing with the Premier League trophy while wearing it after taking just 18 points from a possible 30.

However, there is every chance the Reds will be back in contention for the biggest prize in English football next season. That said, should we take a look at the projected home, away and third shirts, which have been based on 'leaked' information?

Footy Headlines have shared images which show what kit experts believe the kits will look like. It's important to stress that these are designs based on information that has already been revealed, and the final shirts won't look exactly like this.

FumlerRawk on X, a Liverpool kit expert, has already confirmed that the home shirt will feature a checkered pattern based on the flags that can often be seen on The Kop. Of course, Turkish Airlines will become the club's front-of-shirt sponsor from 27/28 onwards, which is another significant change.

The new Premier League campaign brings a whole host of new kits, designs and historical references to view - and this year is certainly impressive.

Early reviews seem to be overwhelmingly positive. One supporter said: "Think this looks much better than previous predictions.​​​​​​​"

"That’s a title-winning kit, mate," another added.

Elsewhere, the away kit concept looks very clean and eye-catching. Liverpool often go with a black second kit, and that's the case here, as JMFK used the info that an argyle pattern will be used on this shirt to mock up a design.

Coupled with bright pink highlights on the sleeves and Turkish Airlines sponsorship, the top really stands out as something different.

Finally, a football club's third kit is always an opportunity for more creativity. JMFK's design features predominantly white, with black and green highlights. All are colours that have been seen on Liverpool shirts of the past.

If these designs even come close to reality, then Liverpool will have three very different kits for the 2027-28 season.
