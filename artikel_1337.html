HIBERNIAN 2 DUNDEE 1: Hibs enjoy some long lost home comfort as Leith men win the battle of the wounded

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Published: 18:46 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 18:51 EST, 24 November 2026

Hibernian ended their long wait for a Premiership home win after overcoming Dundee in a hard-fought contest at Easter Road.

Martin Boyle and Nicky Cadden – who curled home a stunning effort from outside the box – had the hosts in command before the interval.

But Dundee, still licking their wounds after an 8-0 savaging at the hands of Hearts on Saturday, showed plenty of fight after the break and pulled one back through Joe Wright.

The visitors tried in vain to grab an equaliser and it was Hibs who were left satisfied by their first three points at home since beating Kilmarnock on April 4.

Manager Marink Reedijk was thrilled to have bounced back from their 4-0 weekend home defeat at the hands of Dundee United, saying: ‘I think it’s a good reaction to what we have done against Dundee United.

‘It’s important that you get the three points at home. Again, that’s a big win. I saw a front foot team again, I saw togetherness again, I saw a team who fought for every ball.

Martin Boyle takes the acclaim after scoring the all-important second goal for Hibernian

‘That’s not easy, they have to be patient, you have to be secure, you have to wait for your moment and we took these moments and that was really good to see.

‘The goal of Nicky [Cadden] is one to, we say, put in a frame and put on your wall as a painting. The one of Martin [Boyle] is why Martin is so important for us. He can disrupt an opponent if they are so organised and so close to each other and so deep.

‘As long as we keep ourselves, I think these are good signs.’

Hibs played on the front foot as they looked to start repairing the damage from the weekend. On a rare start, Togo international Thibault Klidje was lively and saw a header crash into the side-netting before hitting a harmless snap-shot straight at Dundee goalkeeper Owen Goodman.

From a short corner routine Hibs worked space for Cadden on the edge of the box from Josh Mulligan’s lay-off and his driving effort whistled just past a post.

Dundee were desperate to keep it tight at the back but offered little going forward. Just when it looked like they were beginning to settle Hibs hit them with two goals in three minutes before the break.

The first came in the 40th minute when Boyle fired a penalty past Goodman after being tripped by Ryan Finnigan.

Nicky Cadden unleashes an absolute pearler into the top corner to put Hibs in front

Cadden then found the top corner with a sublime curling effort from his left foot just outside the box. It was another short corner routine that saw Miguel Chaiwa set up the wing-back.

Despite the hammer blow of losing two goals in quick succession before the break, Dundee demonstrated at the start of the second half that they were not going to roll over.

Steven Pressley’s side upped their intensity as they searched for a goal to get themselves back in the game and that arrived in the 67th minute courtesy of Wright.

Substitute Callum Jones’ free-kick should have been put away by Ryan Astley but the ball broke back to Wright and he slammed the ball high into the net from six yards.

That set up a tense finish but Hibs desperately held on for a rare home victory.

For Pressley, who changed half of his outfield XI following his side’s weekend humiliation, there were also positives to take.

The Dark Blues boss said: ‘We’re disappointed to not come away with anything and to obviously lose the game. But I also thought we restored some degree of pride tonight.

‘Going into half-time 2-0 down, losing two late goals in the half, can be a little bit demoralising, especially for a team that has a bit of fragility on the back of the weekend’s result.

‘I thought we responded brilliantly. We showed a lot of courage, a lot of determination, a lot of spirit and, as I’ve said, I think we certainly restored some level of pride that allows us a foundation to move into the weekend.’
