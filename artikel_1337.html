Report Napoli contract with Manna extended to 2032, Allegri until 2029

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Napoli have extended their contract with director of sport Giovanni Manna to June 2032, while Max Allegri’s contract runs to June 2029.

The club has not formally announced the length the agreement, but the details were spotted in the release of the club’s accounts for the year ending June 30, 2026.

When Allegri was hired as the new Napoli coach on July 3, it was confirmed the contract would run to June 2029.

It is often the case for these to be one or two-year deals, so it was somewhat surprising to learn the former Milan boss had put pen to paper on a three-year agreement at the Stadio Diego Armando Maradona.

That is particularly important to note amid the calls from some fans to sack Allegri, because terminating that deal early would be extraordinarily expensive for President Aurelio De Laurentiis.

Meanwhile, the same report also confirmed that director of sport Manna has already extended his contract to June 2032.

That means a new deal was signed without any formal statement being given to the media, as his previous contract was meant to expire in June 2029.
