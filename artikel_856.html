Jamie Dornan & Anthony Mackie Share Hilarious Lesson From Fatherhood

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Jamie Dornan and Anthony Mackie—who are costarring in the thriller series 12 12 12—exclusively told E! News about their kids' reactions to growing up with movie star dads.

Jamie Dornan and Anthony Mackie's most important critics are their kids.

The actors—who first starred in 2019's Synchronic before reuniting for upcoming TV series 12 12 12—confessed that amid their many roles as action stars over the years, earning cool points from their children has been no easy feat.

"If they don't think I'm cool after Lord of the Rings, I'm done," Dornan, who plays Aragorn in the upcoming prequel, The Hunt for Gollum, joked in an exclusive interview with E! News’ Will Marfuggi. "If that's not good enough for my kids, then I don't know what else to offer them."

The 50 Shades of Grey alum brought his three kids Dulcie, 13, Elva, 10 and Alberta, 7, with wife Amelia Warner to stay with him in New Zealand during the fantasy shoot, where they were thankfully impressed by his performance on set.

"Usually, they ask to leave. Like, they come to set, and after half an hour, they're like, 'You're just saying the same thing over and over. This is so boring,'" Dornan admitted. "They came to set one night when I was fighting and beheading orcs and stuff, and they were like, 'It was cool.'"

That said, the 44-year-old was pleased that his little ones found it "good to see Daddy all heroic like that" because, as he put it, "that'll be as cool as it gets for me."

Mackie's brood, on the other hand—which includes four sons with ex-wife Sheletta Chapital—weren't so easily won over by their dad moonlighting as a soldier with supernatural powers, but for good reason. The Captain America actor is already a superhero at home.

"My boys could care less," Mackie remarked with a laugh. "At the end of the day, I realized they just want me to be dad."

And the 48-year-old understands why his family unit is more content with quality time at home, including playing pickleball.

"I love the fact that all they want from me is me," he continued. "But then when I come home, we get to be one big family."

Still, Mackie admitted bringing the crew as his plus-fours to a red carpet was more than a typical 'bring your kids to work' day, even for them.

"They went to the premiere of my movie," Mackie recounted. "They had never seen anything of that scale, like of that magnitude."

And Mackie and Dornan's latest project is yet another grand production. The Marvel alum credited the art department when remarking, "The scale of the show, I feel, is kind of like another character."

The Belfast native agreed the ambitious approach paid off for the series, premiering Nov. 13 on Apple TV, about a meticulously planned fictional robbery of a bank in Zurich.

"It's like the biggest heist in European history," Dornan said, using the British slang for cheap when adding, "It couldn't be pony. It needed to be big."

New episodes drop on Fridays through Dec. 25.
