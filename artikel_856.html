Chris Hemsworth Has 3 Cars Stolen From Australia Home in Armed Robbery

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Chris Hemsworth and Elsa Pataky's home in Australia was burglarized Nov. 11 by armed robbers who took three vehicles while the Fast & Furious actress was sleeping.

Chris Hemsworth and Elsa Pataky are handling a scary situation at home.

The Avengers star and the Fast & Furious actress' Australia home was targeted by armed robbers who stole three of their vehicles, which have since been recovered, police said.

"Officers attended and were told four unknown people had entered the property and stole three vehicles earlier that day," law enforcement told 7News of the Nov 11 theft. "No injuries were reported."

The incident occurred around 2 a.m., when Pataky was sleeping at their home near Byron Bay and woke to the sound of their dogs barking, according to the news service.

The model reportedly went back to sleep, but discovered in the morning that the family's BYD electric vehicle, Ram truck and Ford Ranger Raptor Ute—all of which were parked with the keys inside—were missing from the garage.

Upon checking security footage, investigators learned that a group of men wearing balaclava head coverings—with two holding knives—gain access to the garage by scaling a fence, according to the news outlet.

Police were able to locate two of the cars hours away from the couple’s beachside residence the same day, per 7News. The third vehicle was found on Nov. 12, according to TMZ.

Earlier this year, Hemsworth—dad to daughter India, 14, as well as twins Sasha and Tristan, 12, with Pataky—detailed his family's 2015 move from Los Angeles back to his home country of Australia, calling it "the greatest decision."

"You know when you come back from work, you wanna go on a holiday?" he said during a February episode of the SmartLess podcast. "Coming home for me, it feels like a holiday. We have a big farm and horses and motorbikes and surf."

Hemsworth added that the relocation was largely inspired by his own upbringing in rural Australia with brothers Liam Hemsworth and Luke Hemsworth.

"We lived about 20 minutes from the main sort of suburban areas in Melbourne up in the hills," he remembered. "And the nearest neighbor was a kilometer or two away."
