Are Traitors: New Blood’s Katie and Victor Dating? They Say…

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Traitors: New Blood contestants Katie Fites and Victor Vollbrechthausen addressed speculation about their relationship after the two went head-to-head in the Nov. 19 season finale.

Spoilers ahead for the finale of Traitors: New Blood.

Katie Fites and Victor Vollbrechthausen are not banishing the possibility of a future romance.

During the two-part Traitors: New Blood finale, the pair confronted each other several times as Victor, 26, wrestled with whether he could trust Katie, 23. As he said in a confessional, “This would be so much more fun if we were Bonnie and Clyde instead of Romeo and Juliet."

So what’s next for the duo, who both lost the $246,300 prize pot to Shane Beatty?

“That is a great question,” Victor told E! News in a an exclusive interview. “I mean, can a Traitor and a Faithful figure it out?”

Chimed in Katie, “I guess it’s star-crossed lovers."

And while they did go on a date after the show, they’re still, understandably, working out some trust issues.

“Funny enough, I always tell him, like, I feel like I can’t trust him,” Katie shared. "And then he’ll always be like, ‘You’re the Traitor. I don’t think you seem to remember that.’”

Joked Victor, perhaps one of the show’s greatest Traitor hunters, “Outside of the castle, she has the trust issues."

Of course, Katie wasn’t the only contestant to catch Victor's eye. Earlier in the season, he confessed he enjoyed “talking to pretty girls” and even sparked romance rumors with fellow Faithful Madeline Kostopulos.

But their castle connection didn’t last. “He’s got some wandering eyes,” Madeline, who was banished at the first Round Table, told E!’s Hot Goss in October. “I don’t think he stayed loyal to me once I left.”

The next season of The Traitors premieres Jan. 14, 2027 on Peacock and will return to an all-celebrity cast, including The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives’ Whitney Leavitt, Love Island narrator Iain Stirling, and Bachelorette runner-up Joey Graziadei.
