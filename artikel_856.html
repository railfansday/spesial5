Taylor Swift, Karlie Kloss Prove There's No Bad Blood at Chiefs Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Taylor Swift and Karlie Kloss put feud rumors to rest as they cheered on the Kansas City Chiefs Nov. 8, just months after the supermodel attended the Grammy winner’s wedding to Travis Kelce.

If Taylor Swift and Karlie Kloss had problems, it looks like they were able to solve ‘em.

Four months after the model surprised fans with her attendance at the Grammy winner’s wedding to Travis Kelce, Swift and Kloss confirmed their friendship is far from ruined when they reunited at the Kansas City Chiefs game against the New York Jets at Arrowhead Stadium on Nov. 8.

To cheer on her guy on the Chiefs, Swift—who donned a striped red sweater—was joined in her suite by Kloss as well as her and Joshua Kushner's 5-year-old son Levi. Also stopping everything now to support Kelce were the "Opalite" singer’s parents Andrea Swift and Scott Swift, plus her mother-in-law Donna Kelce.

Swift, 36, and Kloss, 33, were all smiles as they supported the “Love Story” artist’s husband during his milestone 200th game in the NFL, with the pair even standing up to applaud and share a celebratory hug at one point, according to fan footage shared on social media.

And while Swift has been known to bring her celebrity pals to Chiefs games, including Tom Cruise and Caitlin Clark to name a few, her spending time with Kloss is particularly notable as they were rumored to have grown apart after being best friends in the mid 2010s.

Speculation surrounding their friendship began after the former Victoria’s Secret Angel’s name was noticeably left off a T-shirt that Swift wore in her 2017 music video for “Look What You Made Me Do”, which featured a list of her girl squad.

And though Kloss attempted to calm down the speculation by sharing a photo with Swift backstage at the Reputation tour in 2018, the “I Knew It, I Knew You” singer did not attend Kloss’ wedding to Kushner—with whom she also shares kids Elijah, 3, and Rae, 13 months—a few months later.

But the two seemingly confirmed that any past bad blood had been washed away when Kloss and her husband attended Swift and Kelce’s white veiled nuptials at New York City’s Madison Square Garden in July.

And just a few weeks after the star-studded occasion, Kloss appeared to prove all was well while shedding light on Swift’s special day.
