Aaron Paul, Jobi McAnuff and Tommy Smith discuss the biggest stories in the EFL. EFL legend Tony Mowbray has stepped back from his role as manager of Blackburn due to health issues - the panel talk about how that might affect Blackburn ahead of their trip to Lancashire rivals Burnley. 6th placed Swansea boss Vitor Matos looks set for Strasbourg - where should they turn next? Shrewsbury's new Sporting Director Brad Friedel has laid out his ambitions for the club. And with all 3 leagues being led by managers without professional playing experience, is the landscape of football management changing? There's a Wimbledon stunner in 72PLUS 72MINUS as well as a very unhappy Steve Evans.
