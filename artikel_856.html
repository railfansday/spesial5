Huddersfield level at the top after defying red card to beat Doncaster

Ten-man Huddersfield moved level at the top of League One with a 3-2 win at Doncaster.

Goals from Cameron Humphreys, Joe Taylor and Ilias Bronkhorst delivered the win despite Town seeing Bali Mumba dismissed before half-time for two bookable offences. The second came when conceding a penalty from which Isaac Hutchinson netted to give Doncaster hope.

Captain Humphreys slotted in from an Ashley Fletcher pass to give Huddersfield a fifth-minute lead.

Town were dominant and took advantage of more poor Doncaster defending with Taylor volleying in from 15 yards on 21 minutes.

Mumba hauled down Jamie Sterry in the box on 33 minutes to gift Doncaster a route back in, with Hutchinson scoring the penalty.

Rovers controlled possession from then on but struggled to fashion opportunities and were punished on 59 minutes when Bronkhorst lashed in from a corner, moments after Derensili Sanches Fernandes struck the bar with a fierce effort.

A scruffy finish from Owen Bailey on 89 minutes threatened a late Rovers fightback but Town hung on to draw level with leaders Stockport.
