Celtic's Baur out for 10 weeks, says O'Neill

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Mika Baur is unlikely to feature until the new year

Mika Baur will be out for around 10 weeks with ankle ligament damage, Celtic manager Martin O'Neill has revealed.

The 22-year-old twice-capped Germany midfielder went off in the recent 1-0 win at home to Kilmarnock and withdrew from his nation's under-21 squad for this month's international break.

Baur, who moved to Glasgow from Paderborn in August, has made 19 appearances so far this season, scoring four times,

"He'll be out for some considerable time," O'Neill explained.

"It's a real shame - he was playing great for us. There's talk about 10 weeks. There's things that he can't do for a while. Foot's in the boot.

"He was on fire. It is a blow. It seemed so innocuous at the time.

"I thought that he'd been kicked, but it was the other foot he landed on. He's just gone over on it."

Benjamin Nygren missed the second of Sweden's recent double-header and O'Neill cast doubt on the midfielder's participation in Saturday's home Scottish Premiership meeting with St Mirren (15:00 GMT).

"Nygren didn't train today, but we'll see how he is in the morning - he's got a sore heel," the manager said on Friday. "He's been feeling it for a couple of weeks."

O'Neill's reigning champions are four points clear at the top of the league, having played a game more than nearest challengers Rangers.

The manager says his side have "got to play better" and "be more aggressive going forward".

On the prospect of January additions, O'Neill added: "We're definitely looking at players, wanting to strengthen the squad, which I think we would have to do.

"When people are fit, we will have numbers. We need a bit of quality.

"It would be lovely if, by January, we still had a chance of qualifying for the knockouts of the Europa League. The next couple of games will probably make that decision for us. Hopefully, it's not the breaking point of getting players in.

"I think that we would be doing something. We're absolutely looking at players now.

"I suppose every single club is doing that. The quicker you could get somebody in the better."
