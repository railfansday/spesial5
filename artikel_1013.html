Buurman scores late winner for Chelsea while Man City earn back-to-back Champions League victories

Veerle Buurman struck the winning goal three minutes from time as Chelsea narrowly beat Paris Saint Germain 1-0 to ensure automatic qualification for the Women’s Champions League quarter-final remains in their own hands.

Sonia Bompastor’s team had had by far the better of things at Plough Lane but created too little and were set to go into their final match in December needing favours from elsewhere, until in the 87th minute, Buurman found space at the near post to bundle a corner over the line.

It was a scrappy way to win it but Chelsea had deserved little better. With several of the teams above them not playing until Thursday, this was their opportunity to make a statement in the race for qualification but instead they lacked killer instinct.

Substitutes Lauren Hemp and Khadija Shaw struck to give Manchester City back-to-back wins in the Women’s Champions League with a 2-0 victory over Hacken in Gothenburg.

After three consecutive draws, City earned their second straight away European victory and boosted their chances of automatic qualification for the knockout phase.

Hemp and Shaw were introduced at the break and the former made the breakthrough 10 minutes after the interval after a defensive clearance ricocheted off her shin and into the net.

City thought they had doubled their lead shortly after through Mary Fowler, but it was ruled out for offside after Yui Hasegawa was stood in front of keeper Falk.

Hemp was then denied her second when she struck an effort off the post. But City were not to be denied, this time Hemp turned creator by giving it to an onrushing Shaw and she was able to tuck home in composed fashion.

City could have had a third but Falk guessed the right way to deny Fowler from the penalty spot after VAR adjudged Aivi Luik to have fouled Shaw inside the box.

Signe Bruun’s late double helped Real Madrid to a 3-1 win against 10-man Inter Milan. The Italian team looked to be hanging on for a credible draw after Michela Cambiaghi cancelled out Lineth Beerensteyn’s 28th-minute opener.

Cambiaghi’s leveller in first-half added time came just three minutes after they had Karolina Lea Vilhjalmsdottir sent off, with an initial yellow card upgraded to red by VAR.

Inter defended resolutely after the break but their hearts were broken in the 85th minute when Bruun struck a rebound into an empty net. She sealed the win in stoppage time when she converted a penalty, which was awarded by VAR.

Juventus left it late to beat OH Leuven 2-0.

The game was heading towards stalemate until substitutes Annamaria Serturini and Matilde Pavan turned the game in the final five minutes and secured three vital points for the Bianconere.

Serturini powered home in the 86th minute and then Pavan sealed the game in added time with a cool finish.
