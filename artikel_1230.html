Wesley Fofana Blasted For ‘Disgraceful’ Comments in Interview Not Arranged By Chelsea

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Chelsea fans have hit out at Wesley Fofana over his ‘disgraceful’ comments in an interview he took part in on Tuesday afternoon, which was not arranged by the club.

It is no secret that the Blues have struggled in recent weeks, and their humiliating 4-1 home defeat to Leeds United has once again brought Xabi Alonso's side's defensive frailties into the spotlight, with Fofana and Levi Colwill bearing the brunt of criticism from supporters online.

The worrying result means the west London outfit have now kept just three clean sheets in their last 30 matches across all competitions, while only four Premier League clubs have conceded more than their 20 goals this season.

Sitting eighth in the table, already 12 points adrift of league leaders Arsenal and having won just one of their previous five outings, Chelsea are in desperate need of a response. However, it is Fofana who has now sparked further controversy with comments that have left supporters questioning his attitude and commitment to playing for the club.

Fofana has told YouTuber Zack Nani that thigh injuries under Enzo Maresca, soon after his recovery from an ACL, made him briefly want to quit football. “I sat down with my agents and told them, ‘That’s it, I’m done with football.’”

The Frenchman, still just 25 years old, also spoke on his penalty shootout miss against Liverpool in the Carabao Cup, which has sparked outrage online. He said:

“If I miss it's not serious, it's definitely annoying, but if someone attacks me, it's not the first time, I don't care. I know there's another match in three days.

"If you win, you forget about your penalty. It's how football works. I missed. I had a beautiful shot, did you see it? I was shooting very high. The roof of Anfield. I remember a supporter very high up caught the ball. Yeah, I missed, now I laugh about it because it's part of football. That's how it is."

The Blues have slipped into eighth in the Premier League following a 4-1 home rout.

The interview was not arranged by Chelsea, and reacting to his ill-timed comments, one X user wrote: "Get this fool out of my club." A second added: "Fofana is a disgrace to the club," as a third remarked: "Gosh I hate this guy so much."

In his seven league appearances this season, Fofana has conceded 12 goals, made just nine tackles, while also having picked up two yellow cards. He has become a scapegoat among the Stamford Bridge faithful, with another user summarising a widely shared sentiment by concluding: "Terminate his contract now."

Jamie Carragher reckons Chelsea have “no chance of achieving anything” without a dominant centre-half. The Sky Sports pundit's big statement came on Monday night football amid stinging criticism of Colwill and Fofana – the centre-backs that started the defeat to Sunderland immediately before the international break, and the defeat to Leeds directly after it.

While the former Liverpool defender once saw the club's goalkeeper issue being the main thing holding them back, the results of signing Emiliano Martinez have not been as transformative as he expected. Nor were the arrivals of Maxence Lacroix and Tosin Adarabioyo over the summer, who have failed to bring some stability to the backline.
