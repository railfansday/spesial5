Flex Wins 2026 Automotive News PACE Award for Fully Symmetric HV DC/DC Architecture

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

AUSTIN, Texas, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Flex (NASDAQ: FLEX) today announced that its HV DC/DC Converter has been named a 2026 Automotive News PACE Award winner, recognizing its innovative approach to high-voltage power conversion designed for the evolving electrical demands of electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles.

As vehicle architectures become more electrified and software-defined, automakers face growing demands on the systems that power safety-critical and high-performance electrical functions. The Flex HV DC/DC Converter addresses these challenges with a scalable, bidirectional architecture that supports both 400V and 800V traction battery systems and responds quickly to dynamic vehicle loads.

"Winning a third PACE Award in two years is an honor and a testament to Flex's continued innovation in automotive power and compute," said Andreas Heim, Vice President of Automotive at Flex. "This latest recognition highlights our expanding capabilities across both high- and low-voltage power systems as automakers move toward increasingly sophisticated electrified vehicle architectures."

The DC/DC platform uses a dual active bridge-based architecture to provide wide input-voltage capability and bidirectional energy flow. By responding dynamically to changing electrical demands, it can help automakers simplify electrical architectures, reduce reliance on auxiliary low-voltage energy storage, and improve system reliability. It also supports higher-power applications and integration with onboard chargers, inverters, and software-defined power management.

The award builds on Flex's 2025 PACE Award for its Backup DC/DC Converter, which provides power continuity for essential drive-by-wire systems. The Automotive News PACE Awards recognize automotive supplier innovations that demonstrate significant technological advancement and business impact, as selected by an independent panel of judges.
