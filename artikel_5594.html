In a standard bull market, a sustained breakout above $1.60–$1.80 would confirm intermediate strength and open the path toward a retest of the prior all-time high near $3.65–$3.66.

Under the extreme corporate treasury and trade-finance adoption scenario outlined above, clearing that previous high would likely trigger powerful measured-move extensions and multi-cycle Fibonacci projections from the long-term base.

Momentum indicators currently sit in neutral-to-corrective territory with significant room to run into the extreme overbought levels typical of parabolic advances.

Key nearer-term supports sit near $1.18–$1.22 and $1.10–$1.15; holding those levels would keep the broader recovery structure intact.

Binance has announced it will resume phase one of withdrawals on November 1, 2026, in a controlled, phased manner after completing security assessments.

The exchange has reiterated that user funds remain safe, that it will absorb the full financial impact of the stolen assets (rather than passing losses to users), and that it has already covered approximately 95% of the impacted amount.

Deposits remained open throughout, and the company continues working with partners and law enforcement on recovery efforts. The relatively orderly response, combined with the market’s ability to absorb the largest exchange hack on record without a cascading collapse, suggests a constructive signal of growing industry resilience.

For risk assets more broadly, including XRP, a successful, transparent restoration of services and containment of the incident may help restore confidence and reduce one major overhang on sentiment as the market looks ahead.
