DC Gym Shooter Kills His Building Manager After HOA Dispute Over Mold Issues

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

A Washington, DC, doctor who had been locked in a legal battle with his homeowners association over mold problems fatally shot the manager of his condominium during a shooting spree on Thursday, police said.

The fatal shooting incident was reported around 8 a.m. in the Logan Circle neighborhood in Northwest DC, about a mile from the White House, drawing dozens of heavily armed police officers to the area.

Matt Wade Lambert, 55, a physician living in a penthouse apartment at the Metropole Condos building on 15th Street Northwest, was identified by the authorities as the shooter who killed one and injured four others before taking his own life.

Lambert was suspected of going down to the luxury VIDA Fitness center housed inside the building, locating the building manager, identified las 46-year-old James Stuart, next to the treadmills and shooting him with a handgun from a point-blank range, DC Councilmember Brooke Pinto told reporters, according to WUSA9.

"Within seconds there was a man who fell to the ground, and a pool of blood came out," gymgoer James Spellman told The Associated Press. "One gym member rushed to help him. He was instantly dead. He had been shot in the neck."

Dozens of people were in the middle of their morning workout in the gym at the time of the deadly attack, which triggered an evacuation and an order to shelter in place in the area.

While law enforcement officials have not revealed an official motive for the shooting, Pinto said that the building manager was "targeted" by the suspect.

During a press conference Thursday evening, Interim DC Police Chief Jeff Carroll said that Lambert then ran out of the gym, went up to his seventh-floor patio and opened fire from a rifle, striking two men and two women who were downstairs. Earlier reports said three of them were construction workers.

One of the victims was hospitalized in critical condition and three others sustained non-life-threatening injuries.

Lambert eventually barricaded himself inside his condo at Metropole Circle and was on the phone with police negotiators when he ended his own life with a shotgun blast.

When officers breached the door, they found all three firearms used during the shootings, along with containers of flammable liquid.

DC Mayor Muriel Bowser described the incident as a "senseless act of gun violence."

Court records reviewed by Realtor.com® indicate that Lambert had been in a long-standing feud with his condo’s management and filed a lawsuit in March 2025. In it he claimed that he had not been able to live in his seventh-floor penthouse since Nov. 11, 2024, because of mold, moisture, foul odor, and related damage.

Lambert argued in the 12-page complaint that the problems in his unit were caused by repairs to the building’s facade that had been performed by a contractor, for which he paid over $15,000 as part of a special assessment.

According to the filing, Lambert had incurred damages topping $100,000 as a result of the HOA's "failure to take corrective action to remedy the moisture accumulation."

A mediation in the case had been scheduled for March 2027.

Attorney Joel Braithewaite, who represented Lambert in the case, told NBC News that he spoke with him just yesterday or the day before and said that the 55-year-old was "in a good place" and about to start a new job on Monday.
