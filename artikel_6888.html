Zoë Kravitz and Harry Styles Had Very Different Costumes for His Harryween Concerts

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Harry Styles celebrated his Harryween shows this weekend, and he and his fiancée, Zoë Kravitz, got into the spirit.

On October 30, the singer performed the first of his two Halloween-themed concerts at New York City’s Madison Square Garden, where he wrapped up his 30-show residency on Saturday. The singer used his annual Harryween concert as an opportunity to pay homage to Elvis Presley and a past version of himself.

For his Presley-inspired look, Styles took the stage wearing a white bedazzled jumpsuit with colorful embellishments that featured a cobalt blue cape attachment and gold-trimmed sunglasses.

Kravitz watched from the audience and embraced a more traditional look in a red jacket and matching skirt, tights, and devil horns. She was joined by actress Riley Keough, who is Presley’s granddaughter. Keough was spotted filming the performance and dancing along to 2019’s “Watermelon Sugar.”

In addition to paying tribute to Presley’s stage wardrobe, Styles recreated his 2010 X Factor audition look, honoring the moment that launched his career. He was just 16 when he was on the show.

For his second Harryween show on October 31, Styles and Kravitz decided to pay homage to New York City. Styles wore a giant, fuzzy rat costume, complete with a giant foam slice of pizza, a nod to the city’s viral pizza rat.

Kravitz, meanwhile, dressed up as a takeout bag. The actress wore a white mini bubble dress with the instantly recognizable smiley and slogan printed on it. She finished the look with black kitten heels.

Styles first introduced Harryween in 2021 during his Love on Tour run, when he dressed as Dorothy from The Wizard of Oz for the first night and a Pierrot clown for the second. The following year, he channeled Danny Zuko from Grease for a Halloween performance in Los Angeles.
