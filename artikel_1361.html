Manchester United have suffered a major setback in their pursuit of Dominic Calvert-Lewin, with Leeds United determined to hold on to the striker and talks over a new contract expected to accelerate.

According to a report from TEAMtalk, the Red Devils have been monitoring the England international as they explore options to strengthen their attacking department ahead of the January transfer window. However, Leeds are increasingly confident they can retain their leading forward, with both the player and the club keen to continue their relationship.

Calvert-Lewin has enjoyed an impressive resurgence since joining Leeds, establishing himself as an important figure under Daniel Farke. The 29-year-old has scored 20 goals in 51 appearances for the Yorkshire outfit, while his improved fitness and consistency have helped him return to the England setup.

Manchester United’s interest comes at a difficult time for Michael Carrick, who is looking to address his team’s lack of firepower. Benjamin Sesko’s injury has left the manager short of reliable options at centre-forward, making the recruitment of another striker a priority for the winter window.

However, convincing Leeds to part ways with their prized asset was always going to be difficult, particularly given the historic rivalry between the two clubs. The Whites are now preparing to offer Calvert-Lewin a two-year contract extension that would keep him at Elland Road until 2030.

The latest developments represent a significant blow for United, who could now be forced to consider alternative options in the market. Calvert-Lewin’s Premier League experience, physical presence and improved goalscoring form would have made him an attractive addition to Carrick’s squad.

With Leeds and the player seemingly aligned over a new agreement, a January move to Old Trafford looks increasingly unlikely.

United must therefore continue assessing other centre-forward targets if they are to provide adequate competition and support for Sesko during the second half of the campaign.
