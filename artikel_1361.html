Man City Fans Officially Give Up on ‘Useless’ Enzo Fernandez After Forest Loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Manchester City suffered a shock 3-1 win away to Nottingham Forest and one of their star players has been called out for a disappointing performance at the City Ground.

Forest raced into a two-nil lead within twenty minutes when Jair Cunha struck in the sixth minute and Liam Delap produced a moment of magic for the second in the 18th minute. Enzo Maresca's men looked shell shocked as the two sides headed in for half-time.

A different City emerged for the second half and Rayan Cherki got them back into the game in the 52nd minute with a thumping effort. The visitors pushed for a vital equaliser, but it wouldn't come and their former academy product Delap bagged a brace with an almighty finish in the 73rd minute.

Whether games like tonight matter for City in the long run remains to be seen, with the likelihood being that they are relegated after being found guilty of breaching the Premier League's financial rules. The club have maintained their innocence and their spending in the summer suggested all was well at the Etihad.

Their most expensive arrival, Enzo Fernandez, came with huge expectations after becoming somewhat of a talisman at Chelsea. A joint-Premier League record transfer fee of £125 million allowed the Argentine midfielder to reunite with Enzo Maresca.

Fernandez had made a strong start to life at the Etihad but has come off the boil in recent weeks. His display tonight has concerned his club's fans as he looked lost at times including in the second half when Murillo easily picked the ball off him and only the crossbar spared his blushes.

The 25-year-old should be a leader in this City team but he went missing, hooked in the 77th minute for Phil Foden. Not only did he struggle against Forest's impressive midfield, but he lacked creativity going forward.

As is always the case when a player moves for an astronomical fee, they are judged by each and every performance. One slip up immediately puts you under the microscope and that's the case for Fernandez tonight.

Fans on X have vented their frustrations with the Argentine and one supporter is unsure he's a good fit in the City midfield: "Enzo Fernandez isn’t creative enough to be a 10, not physical enough to be a DM, not smart enough to dictate play. He’s one of the most useless profile in football."

Another feels Fernandez has lost his touch since leaving Stamford Bridge: "Enzo fell off so badly."

One added: "Enzo Fernandez has cost a total of £1/4 Billion. Million miles off it. Not good enough. Not better than Oreily or Bouaddi in CDM."

Another went as far as to suggest Maresca could be made to pay for the Argentine's struggles: "This Enzo transfer is gonna be the end of Maresca. Our midfield is non-existent yet again and he can’t drop him because the money he forced the club the spend. I am done with him."

One added: "But honestly what does Enzo Fernandez do?"

Benjamin Mendy left Manchester City in 2023, with his career moving in a different direction since his exit.

Fernandez will have the international break to recharge and try and get back to his best ahead of the winter period. His side's defeat to Forest means they now trail league leaders Arsenal by six points and have lost three on the bounce.
