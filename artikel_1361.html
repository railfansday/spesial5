Mancini message for Italy: ‘On the right track, will be stronger in March’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Italy coach Roberto Mancini sent out a statement following the Nations League elimination. ‘We are on the right track. We will be stronger in March’ when going into the EURO 2028 qualifiers.

The Azzurri had a rollercoaster of a Nations League campaign, as they started with a 2-0 home defeat to Belgium that had some fearing for potential relegation into League B.

That was followed by back-to-back wins over Turkiye, a 1-1 draw in Paris, and stunning 3-1 victory at San Siro over France to go top of the group.

However, a 4-1 loss in Belgium on Sunday saw them finish third and fail to reach the quarter-finals.

“Well done lads,” wrote Mancini on his Instagram account with a picture of him standing with the players in training.

“Despite the disappointment of elimination, we are on the right track. Congratulations to the youngsters.

A post shared by Roberto Mancini (@mrmancini10)

That is when Italy will next have competitive fixtures, as they head into the European Championship qualifiers.

When Mancini took the job, his second stint on the Azzurri bench after winning the EURO 2020 trophy, he said that the real target was not the Nations League, but to be ready for the European Championship in 2028.
