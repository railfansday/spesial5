The top 10 goalscorers worldwide in 2026: Harry Kane 3rd but outscored by forgotten England man

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Barcelona, Bayern Munich, Al-Ahli and Dinamo Zagreb are among the clubs represented in the worldwide top 10 goalscorers of 2026.

There are some familiar superstar names alongside prolific forwards from leagues you might struggle to find on a map, with a few rather unexpected players outscoring some of Europe’s biggest stars. Lionel Messi has scored 30 goals for Inter Miami in 2026, but he just misses out on the top 10.

Here are the 10 most prolific club goalscorers in world football this calendar year, across all competitions.

A player you’ve (probably) never heard of. Get used to that.

Gambian midfielder Ousman Gai averages a goal every 60 minutes in 2026, which is the best ratio of any player in this top 10.

He finished as the top scorer in the Philippines Football League last season, firing Manila Digger to the league title, a return that earned him a move to Cambodian outfit Visakha in the summer.

We all love a journeyman Brazilian playing in a far-flung league, don’t we?

Chigor is a classic of the genre, having developed his skills in the Red Bull system and gone on to play for the energy brand’s Bragantino club.

After representing eight clubs in Brazil, the forward moved to Thailand in 2024 and is currently leading the line for Malaysian outfit Selangor. He’s scored 34 goals in just 33 appearances for the club in 2026.

The most prolific goalscorer you’ve never heard of.

After some short-lived loans in South Korea and Portugal as a youngster, Bergson spent most of his twenties moving around Brazil without making much of an impression.

But he found a settled home at Malaysian giants Johor Darul Ta’zim since signing for the club as a 30-year-old back in 2021. He’s notched an insane tally of 203 goals in 200 appearances for the club and shows little sign of slowing down, with 34 in 2026 so far.

The former Brazil youth international was recently cleared by FIFA to switch his allegiance to represent Malaysia, and he’s already scored a hat-trick for the national team against Singapore.

Not that one. The former Barcelona and Liverpool man has scored a few for Inter Miami this year, but he’s slowing down considerably in his twilight years.

No, this is the Colombian one, once of Watford, who is now taking Viktor Gyokeres’ place in scoring for fun at Manchester United.

He’d be even higher if he’d converted both of the penalties he took against Manchester United in midweek.

You remember him. The World Cup cult hero and surprise 2026 Ballon d’Or nominee won the Saudi Pro League Golden Boot last season, having notched five more goals than Al-Nassr title-winner Cristiano Ronaldo.

Quinoses has scored 36 goals for Al-Qadsiah this calendar year, although he’s not quite the top Saudi-based goalscorer of 2026. It’s not Ronaldo – can you guess who?

Keep this up and you wonder if a European club might take a punt on the 29-year-old Mexico international.

The 23-year-old Puerto Rican has recently moved to DS Edusoccer after an outrageous goalscoring return for Academia Quintana.

Seven(!) goals in one game help out quite a bit here. He scored them in an 18-1(!!) victory over FC Mayaguez.

The oldest player in this top 10, Zampedri earned his first two senior Chile caps last year after blossoming in his mid-thirties with Universidad Catolica.

He was born and raised in Argentina but switched international allegiance after thriving in the country’s top flight over the past few years.

The 38-year-old has fired Universidad Catolica to two Primera Division de Chile league titles and won the Golden Boot in each of the last six seasons, with a seventh pretty much in the bag for 2026.

You knew he’d be here. Comfortably the top goalscorer when it comes to major European football in 2026. And he’s even further ahead if you count his bounty of goals for England.

Kane isn’t the England international with the most club goals in 2026, somehow.

Toney was pipped to the Saudi Pro League by Quinoses last season, but he’s the early frontrunner this season with 14 goals from just 10 appearances this season.

That takes his overall tally to 44 goals this calendar year for Al Ahli. Every chance he tops this list when all is said and done come New Year’s Day.

It’ll be interesting to see whether he has another Premier League stint in him. Given his goalscoring output, you’d take a punt, wouldn’t you? Whether he’d be willing to take a wage cut is another question – but maybe when his current deal expires in 2028.

We expected the leader to be a total unknown playing in the middle of nowhere, but Beljo is actually somewhat established.

The 24-year-old is a full Croatia international with experience playing in a top European league, although he failed to pull up any trees in the Bundesliga with Augsburg.

He’s been outrageously prolific since returning to Croatia with the perennial champions Dinamo Zagreb in 2025. He notched 38 goals in all competitions in his debut season and appears set to better that tally in year two.

Beljo recently scored against Sunderland in the Europa League. Keep this up, and another opportunity at a higher level will surely come calling. Leeds United are among a host of clubs who have been linked.
