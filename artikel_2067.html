The question we’re asking Newcastle United fans today: Are Newcastle United in a relegation fight this 2026-27 Premier League season?

We just wanted to get a snapshot of opinion from the Newcastle United fans.

As Newcastle United fans, do you see this season as one where NUFC are in a relegation fight?

Or is that too dramatic a take and the reality is something different?

Maybe you see Newcastle United as one of those Premier League clubs that in the 2026-27 season is realistically in no danger of ending up in the top four, nor the relegation zone.

Just a case of which mid-table position will be occupied in between those two extremes?

The last four league campaigns saw Eddie Howe and his Newcastle players finish the season in 4th, 7th, 5th and 12th places.

As we go into this international break, Newcastle United 12th in the Premier League table with 12 points from the opening 10 league matches. If keeping that average of points per game (1.20) up, it would see United end up with around 45 or 46 points.
