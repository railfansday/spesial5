Barry Bannan to change career? Sheffield Wednesday star’s exploits have caused a reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Sheffield Wednesday are rebuilding themselves as a club and institution on the pitch this season but their rebuild goes deeper and further than that off it, needing to reconnect with supporters and get back in touch with their history.

Having gone into administration last season, Sheffield Wednesday eventually succumbed to an inevitable relegation, albeit a defeat of West Bromwich Albion on the final day of the season at Hillsborough did help them finish the season on zero points rather than in the minuses.

The Owls have since started this season in a mixed manner, leading to the departure of manager Henrik Pedersen and the subsequent appointment of Dave Challinor.

Such was the mess the club had got into, on-pitch improvement would naturally always have to be subject to so-called ‘teething issues’, while the off-the-pitch commercial aspect of the club also needed improvement.

As a part of that potential rebuild off the pitch and increased commercialisation of the club as it seeks to reconnect with supporters, Wednesday have launched a collaboration with esteemed artist Pete McKee.

60-year-old McKee, born in Sheffield and a cartoonist for the Sheffield Telegraph, has built his artistic reputation on depicting working class elements of society and specifically northern culture – and even more specifically, Sheffield culture.

Within this new collaboration, it has been launched at Riverside Fish and Chip shop on Langsett Road in Hillsborough, nearby to the famous Hillsborough Tap pub and just around the corner from the home ground of Sheffield Wednesday.

As a part of the launch, club legend, or at the very least cult hero, Barry Bannan has featured in the photoshoot dressed as a chippy worker behind the counter.

The actual collaboration centres on the launch of a range of clothing such as hoodies and t-shirts with McKee’s Wednesday-focused art as the design.

McKee has explained: “I’ve supported Wednesday ever since I was a nipper, with my two elder brothers taking it in turns to take me until I was old enough to go by myself with my mates.

“The club has been a part of my DNA throughout my life and career, so it’s with immense pride that I now have the opportunity to work directly with the club on this range.

“I hope the fans take it to their hearts, as well all share the same love of following the Wednesday.”

Naturally, there has been quite the response from Sheffield Wednesday supporters to the comically produced involvement of Bannan in the photoshoot.

Many have expressed their amusement with the 36-year-old, soon to be 37, getting involved to such a degree with The Owls’ social media post nudging him to ensure he doesn’t give up his ‘day job’.

However, given the mixed performances of Bannan on the pitch, one Wednesday supporter did quip that perhaps the Scotsman may be best suited to the role he has been pictured in.

Having spent nine and a half years at Hillsborough, making over 475 appearances for the club in his first stint in South Yorkshire, Bannan moved to Millwall in January.

More of a bit-part player for The Lions as they finished third in the Championship before losing to eventual play-off winners Hull City in the play-off semi-final, Bannan returned from the capital city with Wednesday set to relaunch themselves as an entity this summer and this season.

18 new signings came through the door, including Bannan, and there were some standout performances and moments in the early couple of months, such as a 7-2 victory over Bromley and a 5-0 shellacking of AFC Wimbledon.

Inconsistency in terms of performances and results getting worse on the pitch, especially away from home, led to the departure of Henrik Pedersen with former Stockport County boss Dave Challinor now tasked with ensuring a title challenge in League One.

That inconsistency and general level of frustration felt by supporters has perhaps best been represented by the dip of Bannan’s own level; not quite as protective of possession nor as dynamic with or without the ball as he was in the past.

The arrival of Challinor could and perhaps should spark a Wednesday surge – and there will have to be improvement from their still talismanic figure in the middle of the park to help that along.
