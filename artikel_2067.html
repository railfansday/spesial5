Your 'loud and crazy' supporters won't stop us winning at Celtic Park

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Former Arsenal star Leandro Trossard has warned Celtic that Besiktas are coming to Parkhead determined to take three points.

The Belgian international believes his side can handle the famous Celtic Park atmosphere as they look to continue their impressive Europa League campaign. Trossard joined the Turkish giants from Arsenal in the summer and has set his sights on going deep into the competition.

Celtic are still searching for their first points of the league phase following a defeat to Celta Vigo last time out, while Besiktas have enjoyed a strong start.

And Trossard insists the visitors will not be intimidated by the Parkhead crowd. He said: “We want to get three points from the Celtic match. It will be very tough. They have loud and crazy fans, but if we can translate what we want onto the pitch, then we will win. [SunSport]

“Our fans should continue their support. It would be great if they increased this amazing backing. People don’t pay any attention to it, but the support from the fans is incredibly effective in difficult times. We have won matches with their support.

“They can be sure that if they continue to get behind us, we will win even the toughest matches.”

Besiktas sit fourth in the Turkish league but have made an impressive start in Europe, collecting seven points from their opening three matches against Marseille, Hoffenheim and Crystal Palace.

Trossard believes there is even more to come as they target a place among the Europa League’s top eight. He added: “I’m very excited and we want to reach the top in Europe. The decisive factor is the draw and the team’s performance during these next few games.

“Our current goal is to be in the top eight. After that, we just need to see how things progress. Every player wants to win trophies and to be champions. They want to be at the top at the end of the league.

“We want to reach the best possible position in Europe and there’s also the Turkish Cup. Besiktas aims for trophies in every competition. We have got this potential and we can do it.

"There were matches where we could have played better, but there’s a new coach, a new team, and an adaptation process - I know we can do better things.”
