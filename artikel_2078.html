All 24 EFL Championship clubs ranked by how successful they've been in the last 25 years

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

There has been a lot of success to go around the 24 teams currently competing in the Championship over the last 25 years.

What defines success to a club is different for everyone, and the wider context of a team’s history can make a National League title more significant than coming second in a higher division.

From Birmingham City to Wrexham, all of the teams in the second division this year have achieved a piece of history for their club.

But not all success is created equal, from winning the EFL and FA Cups, to simply achieving promotion through the play-offs.

Here we rank the 24 Championship sides based on how successful they’ve been since 2001/02…

Blackburn Rovers won the Premier League title in 1995, but that was over 30 years ago now, and they have yet to come close to that kind of success since.

In just the last 25 years, the Lancashire outfit have a second place in League One in 2018 as their greatest success, with their highest league finish being sixth in the Premier League in 2003 and 2006.

Preston North End were one of the most successful clubs in the very early days of organised football in the UK.

However, the Lilywhites have not many major achievements to celebrate over the last 25 years, with their greatest success in this period being their 2015 play-off victory in League One.

Stoke City came close to achieving the greatest success in their history in 2011 when they lost the FA Cup final to Manchester City.

Instead, the Potters have had to settle for play-off victory as their greatest moment of success in the last 25 years, earning promotion to the second tier in 2002.

Millwall came close to automatic promotion to the Premier League in 2025, but had to settle for the play-offs, where they lost to Hull City in the semi-finals.

But they have achieved play-off success in the last 25 years, twice winning the competition at a League One level (2010 & 2017).

Charlton Athletic were competing in the Premier League 25 years ago, and have fallen to League One in the time since.

But their 2012 title victory in the third tier remains an impressive achievement, and they have also won the play-offs at that level twice in this period (2019 & 2025).

Watford won the Championship play-offs in 2006 under Aidy Boothroyd to earn their place in the Premier League.

But their closest brush with real success came in the 2019 FA Cup final, where they lost to Manchester City in a 6-0 defeat.

Southampton have brushed up against big success in the last 25 years, reaching both an FA Cup and EFL Cup final in this period, as well as coming second in the Championship in 2011.

But their greatest achievement since 2001 was their 2024 play-off final victory, which earned Russell Martin’s side a place in the Premier League.

Bolton Wanderers’ decline from Premier League side to League Two outfit occurred within the last 25 years, which meant a lot of difficult campaigns for supporters.

The most recent season saw Steven Schumacher’s side win the League One play-offs, which may be their greatest achievement of this entire period, although the 2023 EFL Trophy victory is also a moment fans will remember fondly for years to come.

Wrexham spent several seasons competing at a non-league level during this 25-year period, but they’ve enjoyed a remarkable rise under their Hollywood ownership in the few campaigns.

The Red Dragons’ greatest success in this timeframe was their National League title victory in 2023, which they won with a staggering 111 points, before going on to earn two more automatic promotions in the following two years.

Derby County’s greatest successes all came in the post-World War years, with the high watermark being two league titles in the 1970s.

But over the last 25 years, the Rams’ biggest achievement was their 2007 play-off final victory under Billy Davies, which earned them a place in the Premier League.
