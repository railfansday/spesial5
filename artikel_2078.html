Martin O'Neill gushes over 'class' Celtic star after European heroics

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Camilo Duran earned glowing praise from Martin O’Neill after coming off the bench to fire Celtic to a dramatic Europa League victory over Besiktas.

The Colombian struck twice in a thrilling 3-2 win at Celtic Park as the Hoops finally got off the mark in the competition on their fourth attempt.

Duran was introduced alongside Callum Osmand and Colby Donovan shortly after the hour mark with Celtic trailing 1-0 and struggling to seriously threaten the visitors. The changes transformed the contest.

Osmand equalised with his first touch just 49 seconds after entering the action before setting up Duran to put Celtic ahead. Besiktas hit back to make it 2-2, but Duran struck again late on to complete his double and seal a huge European victory.

O’Neill was delighted with the impact of the forward and revealed he had always intended to introduce him from the bench. “Duran has been fabulous for us all season and got his two goals,” O’Neill said.

“He has played a lot of games and looked a bit tired in the last couple. But he’s a class player, and he was always going to come on.

“I’m delighted with the performance. It was fantastic.”

The victory brought back memories of O’Neill’s first spell in charge of Celtic as a raucous home crowd roared their side to victory. “That was a special European night,” he said. “It reminded me of the olden days.

“The subs re-energised us and played a big part in the win.”

O’Neill was particularly pleased with the character his players showed after falling behind and then being pegged back at 2-2 late in the game. “I told the players afterwards that they are a credit to the club,” he added.

“You can’t get a bigger accolade than that, but we put our heart and soul into the game. We played really good stuff, particularly in the second half.

"At 2-2, it looked like we would drop two points but we came roaring back to win the game. There was so much spirit and desire to get the three points. The character shone through big time.

"The winners came to the fore, as did the new boys.”

Duran also earned praise from fellow substitute Osmand following his match-winning display. “Camilo did amazing; he’s such a poacher,” He said. “Credit to him.

“The team dug in, stayed strong and we got our rewards. I was so happy to come on, score a goal and get the win.”

The victory gave Celtic renewed hope of progressing in the Europa League ahead of their meeting with Portuguese second-tier side Torreense on November 26. O’Neill said: “We rose to the occasion and we have given ourselves a chance.

“We are off the mark now. We have points on the board, and we can park it for a few weeks now. There is still a thought about qualifying, but that is a long way off.

“Hopefully it gives us a platform to charge on, but it’s not going to be easy.”
