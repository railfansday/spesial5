Zelda Playable & Electric Ocarinas Will Initially Be Sold On An Invite-Only Basis (Europe)

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

And you'll need to pass the criteria to register interest

We've been keeping an eye out for news of Nintendo's playable and electric ocarinas going on sale in Europe for a good while now. It looks like sales are just around the corner, but you're going to need to jump through some hoops if you want to get your hands on one.

Both the Nintendo playable model and HORI electric version will initially be sold on an invite-only basis in Europe. You can register your interest in getting either model now on the Nintendo website, but there are some criteria that you'll have to pass first.

Firstly, you'll need to have a Nintendo account registered in Europe (excluding Norway, Russia and South Africa), have allowed Nintendo to process your usage information to customise services, offers and recommendations in your Nintendo Account settings, and have allowed Nintendo to send email recommendations about games, events, special offers and more. You'll need to have done all of that by 22nd November.

You can only register your interest if you have "reached at least 10 hours of play time in the Nintendo Switch 2 game The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time by 18/11/2026" using the same Nintendo account you use to register.

And that's just to get an invite. On the day that the products become available, those who are invited will receive an email with a link to purchase either ocarina (limited to one each per customer).

The playable ocarina will set you back £29.99, bundling the instrument with a wrist strap and sheet music for all of the in-game songs. HORI's electric option is a little more expensive at £41.99, but also comes with a dedicated stand.
