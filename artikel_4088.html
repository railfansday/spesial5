New Details Revealed About The Physical Edition Of GTA 6 For Xbox Series X|S

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Double-sided poster, shipping dates & more

Everyone already knows about the code-in-a-box situation for GTA 6's physical edition on Xbox Series X|S and PS5, but we've also come to learn a few extra details in recent days about what to expect from the boxed copy of the game.

So, when you open up GTA 6 for Xbox Series X|S in physical form, you'll be presented with a code that will allow you to redeem the game on the Microsoft Store - essentially converting your physical purchase into a digital one.

Rockstar Games mentioned a shipping date of November 12th for the physical version a few days ago, but we've spotted some retailers saying they'll start shipping on the 11th instead, while a very small number of people even seem to have their copy already.

Once you've got the physical version in your hands, you'll be able to preload GTA 6 in full, although you can technically also do that from tonight by installing the game via the Xbox mobile app.

As you can also see from the tweet above, the physical edition is now confirmed to come with a double-sided A3 poster (at least if you buy from certain retailers). This poster appears to include the game's main artwork along with some character art on the back, so that's at least a nice little bonus to ease the pain of a disc-less release.

But what about a map? Well, Rockstar Games is handling the map a bit differently this time around, making a digital version available to download for free at launch. Alternatively, physical maps will be available to buy from the Rockstar Store "shortly after launch" for $0.99, but we reckon those will be in extreme demand and quickly sell out.

Of course, none of this really changes the fact that the physical edition of GTA 6 is kinda pointless for any platform as things stand, but it does at least make for a good display piece, and it'll be popular for gifting purposes this Christmas.

What's even weirder about this is that Rockstar is supporting GTA 6's launch with various other physical releases, such as an official soundtrack album and all kinds of merch, but it seems there's little hope for a disc version right now.
