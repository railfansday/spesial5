The Pokémon Company Is Giving Away Free In-Game Pokopia Furniture, And It's Adorable

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

What's that? You wanted more Pokémon Mystery Gift codes? Yes, after already lifting the lid on Champions and Legends: Z-A distributions today, The Pokémon Company has another up its sleeve, but this one is for Pokopia.

Starting today until 7th January, you can use the Mystery Gift code CELEBRATE30TH in the game to unlock the 'Stylish Furniture Set'. For free. Here are all of the items included in the set:

For anyone looking to add a bit of sweetness to their in-game interiors, this one looks like it'll fit the bill perfectly — heck, all of our designs are in desperate need of some hot pink.

Much like with the latest Legends: Z-A code, this distribution will move into its second phase from 10th December, when you'll be able to input a new code to redeem the 'Warm Cozy Furniture Set'. It packs a slightly more rustic vibe, but the fireplace and stained-glass windows have us already dreaming up a British pub build...
