MTG Star Trek Card Featuring Patrick Stewart Sells At $26K

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Magic: The Gathering isn't exactly a stranger when it comes to ridiculously expensive cards, and while resellers aren't quite as rabid as some other TCGs, even somewhat less popular sets like Star Trek are still reaching hefty prices on the second-hand market. While the price might not compete with Lord of the Rings' over $2M One Ring card, getting one of MTG's new serialized cards will still cost you quite a bit.

While the seven hand-signed card variants introduced in MTG's Star Trek Collector's Boosters were already going to be a massive hit due to their limited availability, one of them has already broken the $20,000 mark, albeit for a bit of a morbid reason.

As shared by moombahh on Reddit, while most autographed Star Trek cards go for around $5-10K USD, a recent eBay listing for none other than Captain Jean-Luc Picard reportedly sold for a whopping $26K. While it's entirely possible the buyer sent a lower offer, it might even end up on the cheaper side of the spectrum if the other eBay listings are anything to go by.

With more recent listings having shot up all the way to $38K and an even more massive $45K, the price could wind up being well above the last sold price, and it's not hard to see why either.

For the first time ever, you can pack signed MTG cards, with seven to collect in total, but the first two revealed are of Captains Kirk and Janeway.

Dwarfing the Secret Lair card prices, the main reason players have been pricing their cards so high is that, since signing the cards, Jean-Luc Picard's actor, Patrick Stewart, recently passed away, leaving the serialized cards among the last items signed by the legendary actor.

While fans have pointed out that it's more than a little depressing that others are trying to capitalize on the late actor's passing by selling the cards for absurd prices, it's unlikely that sellers will be slowing down anytime soon, considering how limited their availability will continue to be.
