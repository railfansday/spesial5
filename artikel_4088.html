Dead By Daylight Teases A Godzilla Collaboration In Latest Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Dead by Daylight has an absolutely massive roster if iconic killers and survivors from horror media that just keeps getting bigger each year, which is getting more ambitious ahead of its gameplay overhaul. Having already shattered its player count this year with the release of Jason, DBD is seemingly teasing yet another collaboration that could bring in heaps of new players, especially overseas.

Taking things overseas again after the Nier Automata collaboration event, DBD's developers just posted a teaser advertising the new Hartshorne Mall map release on the 24th of November, which offers an unmistakable reference to none other than Toho's Godzilla.

Although the trailer posted to Dead by Daylight's X account doesn't explicitly confirm that Godzilla is being added as a new killer or as themed cosmetics, the trailer shows Dwight's childhood of stealing a familiar-looking Kaiju toy, before being haunted by a sound that fans instantly recognized as an edited version of Godzilla's iconic roar.

While some fans have speculated that it could have been added for fun or to throw off players, Dead by Daylight is well known for its vague teasers before announcing official collaborations, which has already spawned countless memes about facing off against a building-sized killer camping generators.

More likely being added in the form of a new line of in-game cosmetics, some fans like Winter__s have also analyzed the audio to note that the two roars included in the clip include one from the Millennium era of Godzilla, and another used in all three Shin Godzilla, Godzilla: Minus One, and Godzilla (1954) films, possibly hinting towards which adaptations will make it into the game.

Even though it's hard to imagine Godzilla fitting in as a killer, other players have proposed the idea of the collaboration possibly adapting the internet analogue horror series The Man in the Suit, which follows the story of an enraged Godzilla actor who merges with his suit and terrorizes others.

One of GTA 6's only contenders is doubling down with the well-timed release of a free demo.

While a body-horror-inspired take on Godzilla would certainly be an interesting way to adapt the legendary monster into the world of Dead by Daylight, the most likely opportunity would likely come from more humanoid skins that pay homage to iconic eras from the titular series, or possibly even thin bodysuits for a few killers, similar to the previous Attack on Titan collaboration.
