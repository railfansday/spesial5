The Oura Ring 5 is Oura's latest offering, and a few upgrades earned it a spot on our Best Smart Rings list. This version has no noticeable sensors, while previous models had three interior nodes that could be a bit uncomfortable. It still collects health data seamlessly.

The Oura Ring 5 is lighter and slimmer than any previous models. The ring provides real-time stress tracking, continuous heart-rate monitoring, and more precise temperature sensing. All of this is designed to give you more precise, helpful feedback throughout the day. The ring’s interior has also changed from an epoxy to all-over titanium for a lighter feel.

The charging case is another great upgrade available to Oura Ring 5 users. This case lets you store a charge so you can power up your ring without being limited by nearby outlets. This means you can keep an extra charge for your ring while you're traveling or on the move so you don't miss out on any insights. The new charging dock is also improved and can charge your ring in about an hour.

Oura recommends wearing the ring on your forefinger, since that's where your pulse is strongest, which can mean slightly better data, but any finger works. I know a few people who use their Oura Ring as their wedding band.

If you're unsure about your size, it could be a good idea to order one of Oura's sizing kits. It’s now $10 on Amazon, and that gets applied to your future ring purchase, so technically it's "free." And it allows you to get an early jump on the sale.

When you purchase on ouraring.com the process of getting a sizing kit is a little more involved. You order the ring first, and then they send you a sizing kit to use to get your correct size in the system. Depending on which route you choose for the sizing kit, you'll use that same route to purchase your ring when you decide to buy.

You might not be looking for a super-slim smart ring. If you’re used to wearing rings with some weight, or you just like something with a little more heft, the Oura Ring 4 is the first version I think is worth keeping, even if you’re buying a new Oura Ring 5.
