The 8 Best Sweater Polos for Men, According to Style Editors

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Your next best-dressed moment starts from the collar down.

I CONSIDER MYSELF the king of sweaters. I love a good collared shirt, so polos are my go-to in the spring and summer. But once fall and winter roll around, I’m all about sweaters. Even better: a sweater polo. It’s the perfect mashup that combines the collar and button placket of a classic polo with the knit construction of a sweater, giving you a little more texture and warmth than your standard cotton pique. The best sweater polos offer that same versatility. You can dress things up or lean super casual depending on the day and occasion. And not all have to be long-sleeved either.

Short-sleeve versions are also great for tropical vacations or layers under a blazer for the office. Either way, they’re an easy way to switch up your usual sweater rotation from the same ol’ crews or turtlenecks. The Men’s Health fashion crew and I have tried more than 30 over the last three years, paying close attention to fit, fabric, comfort, and versatility to find ones that deserve a spot in your closet.

For the past five years, I’ve tested more than 25 sweater polos, but Uniqlo is easily one of my favorites. It checks all the boxes when it comes to excellent quality, fit, versatility and what really seals the deal—affordability. At just $50, you’re getting a premium knit that could pass for something more expensive. It’s made from a cotton-blend yarn in a structured Milano rib knit that looks and feels more like woven cloth than a stretchy tee. This one feels dense yet comfortable on the skin. I especially love the buttonless, open V-neck collar, which gives some breathing room and makes the whole thing feel a little relaxed.

In terms of fit, it’s roomy through the chest and flexible through the arms without looking oversized. I’ve worn it with jeans and dressed it up with tailored trousers and loafers for dinner. Plus, it comes in plenty of colors that you can rotate between office days and the weekend. After more than three wash cycles, it’s held up nicely. Just be sure to toss it in a mesh laundry bag to keep the knit in good shape.

You don’t have to spend a fortune to get your hands on a good sweater polo. Check out this one from Coofandy. It’s one of the lighter knits that I’ve tested, so it’s wearable through all the seasons. Yes, even in the summertime when the temps stayed around 80 degrees, I didn’t break a sweat at the backyard party. The polyester fabric feels soft against the skin and has a little stretch, which makes it easy to move around in.

It also has a V-neck polo collar to give it that laid-back vibe, while the ribbed cuffs and hem pull everything in for a more fitted, body-contouring look. Styling-wise, you’ve got plenty of options. I’d wear it untucked with jeans or chinos for an everyday fit, but you can also tuck it in pants with a belt for a day on the golf course. This one is not machine washable, so I’d recommend taking a trip to the dry cleaners.

I love mixing different textures in my polo sweater collection, so things don’t start looking too plain. That’s when I add in pieces like Charles Tyrwhitt’s textured knit to give some depth and dimension to my outfit without going overboard with loud patterns or prints. It’s made from 100 percent cotton that’s been garment-washed for a softer, broken-in feel, so I’m not dealing with a stiff, scratchy sweater.

The midweight material is substantial enough to wear on its own during cooler days but breathable enough to layer under a jacket without feeling too bundled up. I’d wear the petrol-blue shade with cream trousers and brown suede loafers for a sharp office look, or pair it with dark denim and boots for dinner. I’ve washed it more than four times and haven’t seen it shrink or pill, but my advice is to let it air dry.

When my brain can’t function in the morning to put an outfit together, I just rely on Abercrombie’s knit. It’s made from 100 percent merino wool, which sounds like it might be a recipe for itchiness, but surprisingly, it feels soft and smooth against the skin. I also dig the classic three-button collar, which gives it a slightly dressier edge than your average crewneck. The fit is also a nice balance, with enough room in the chest and stomach, in case I wolfed down a burger.

Now, I will say that the wool can get a little toasty, especially if you’re moving around a lot. But if your office blasts AC like it’s the middle of Antarctica, you should be just fine. Styling-wise, I’d wear it with straight-leg jeans and sneakers for a casual Friday, or swap in chinos and loafers when I want to look a little more put-together. It only comes in neutrals, which should be easy to pair with anything in your wardrobe.

There’s something about cashmere that makes even the simplest outfit feel a little more luxurious. And if you’re going to invest in a cashmere sweater polo, Quince is a great place to start. This one is made from 100 percent Grade-A Mongolian cashmere, with fine fibers that give the knit an incredibly soft, smooth feel. The 12-gauge construction gives it a refined look without making it feel overly chunky, and the ribbed cuffs and hem help keep its shape. And when I don’t want to wear a dress shirt, this is a great alternative. Throw it on with tailored trousers and loafers for the office, or pair it with dark jeans and boots for dinner.

I’m a big fan of merino wool because it gives you the warmth of a sweater minus all the extra bulk. Banana Republic uses it for this polo, which is a solid option that can transition from the office straight to dinner. No matter how toasty a room gets, I’m never sweating in it. The fabric stays lightweight and breathable, and has enough warmth to come in handy when temperatures drop.

I also like that the fit is pretty straightforward, with slightly dropped shoulders and ribbed cuffs and hem that give it a relaxed but clean finish. The buttoned collar makes it feel a little dressier than your average knit, especially when paired with chinos or tailored trousers. And unlike some wool sweaters that require a trip to the dry cleaner, this one is machine washable, which is always a plus in my book.

Cos gave the classic sweater polo a completely different spin with a buttonless collar that sits closed rather than opening into the usual V-neck. It’s a subtle detail, but one that makes people take a second look at my outfit. This neckline creates a cleaner, more sophisticated look without being overly flashy. I’d pair it with wide-leg trousers and leather loafers for a sharp office fit, or throw it on with relaxed jeans and sneakers for the weekend.

I especially like this kind of silhouette for guys who prefer a minimalist wardrobe but still want something with a little personality. Now, at around $130, it’s definitely on the pricier side, especially when you can find sweater polos for half that amount. But I’d argue you’re paying for a design that’s a refreshing departure from the sweater polos you’re used to seeing, and chances are, you won’t spot five other guys wearing the same thing.

Not every sweater polo needs long sleeves, especially when you can rock one under a blazer. Take this retro-inspired short-sleeve style from Bonobos, which I can wear year-round. My favorite way to style it is under a structured blazer with tailored trousers. It’s dressy enough to skip the traditional button-down shirt, whether you’re heading to an important meeting or a cocktail reception.

It’s also a great piece to pack for vacation, especially if you’re traveling somewhere tropical and need something sharp for a resort dinner. In that case, ditch the blazer and pair it with linen pants or even shorts for a more laid-back look. Plus, the breathable cotton fabric won’t make scorching temps feel even more unbearable. I wore it on a recent trip to Cancún and didn’t break much of a sweat. Depending on your personal style, Bonobos offers this sweater polo in a variety of colors, prints, and textures, so there’s plenty to choose from.
