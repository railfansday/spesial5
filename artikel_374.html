Amazon has the all-in-one Shark FlexFusion on sale for a record-low price ahead of Black Friday — save $130

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

SAVE $130.99: As of Nov. 10, the all-in-one Shark FlexFusion Air + Ceramic Styling & Drying System is on sale on Amazon for only $299 (normally $429.99). That's an unprecedented 30% savings.

We adore the Shark FlexStyle here at Mashable (it's our top Dyson Airwrap dupe). But if your budget has a little wiggle room, consider upgrading to Shark's more versatile FlexFusion multi-styler.

Released in 2024, the FlexFusion has the same ingenious twisting base as the FlexStyle, but it utilizes heat styling (not just air styling) and adds attachments for both wet and dry hair. It's basically a prototype of the Shark Glam with a slightly bulkier build, no idling sensor, and — as of now — a much cheaper price.
