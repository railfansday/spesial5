‘Apology FC’: Manchester United fans make clear demand after Sporting referee ‘robbery’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Manchester United fans have it out at referee Szymon Marciniak and UEFA over a host of controversial decisions during their 2-2 draw with Sporting in the Champions League.

The 2022 World Cup final ref pointed to the spot for a soft Sporting penalty in the first half, though Luis Suarez’s spot kick was saved by Senne Lammens. Minutes later Lammens went from hero to zero when rushing out of his goal and taking out Maximiliano Araujo for another penalty. Suarez made amends from 12 yards.

Amad Diallo thought he had equalised, only for his goal to be ruled out for a foul by Youri Tielemans in the build-up, which earned the midfielder a yellow card after some vigorous protests.

Man Utd received a penalty of their own early in the second half also bordered on soft; Lisandro Martinez going down very easily after Eduardo Quaresma’s clumsy challenge.

Bruno Fernandes converted and then two minutes later benefitted from a divot of his own creation, albeit inadvertently, to hand Man Utd the lead, when he low, driven shot flicked up off the pitch to beat keeper Kaique Pereira.

A chaos continued when a Lammens howler saw Sporting equaliser, the keeper failing to deal with a cross to allow Suarez to instinctively, and acrobatically, finish from close range.

And then, at the other end of the pitch, an extremely similar scenario occurred as Pereira palmed the ball into his own net at a Man Utd corner for what appeared to be the winner.

Referee Marciniak initially awarded the goal, but VAR ordered a pitchside review which saw Martinez and Patrick Dorgu penalised for a foul on the keeper. There was still time for a red card as Tielemans was shown a second yellow for a late, studs up challenge.

The performance stirred up plenty of frustrations among the fanbase, especially in light of Leny Yoro’s red card against Liverpool which was later overturned by the Premier League.

There were plenty of fans, both with an affiliation to Man utd and not, claiming a “robbery”.

A Man Utd fan said one of the decisions “boiled his blood”, while others were left waiting for another apology after the Yoro debacle.

But one Man City fan saw things slightly differently, insisting very decision was “spot on”...
