This early Black Friday YouTube TV streaming deal saves you $75 on 5 months of live TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

If the excessive streaming price increases across the board have bummed you out to the point where you're reconsidering cable, YouTube TV's early Black Friday deal is worth checking out.

The cable alternative recently debuted a more affordable Sports + News plan, but it costs $71.99 per month for 50 live channels. The Base Plan includes over 100 channels, and you can lock in five months for only $67.99 per month ahead of Black Friday. Compared to its usual $82.99 per month cost, that's a savings of $75 total. YouTube TV's Base Plan unlocks access to all of your local broadcast stations, news channels like CNN and Fox News, specialty networks like Discovery and Bravo, sports channels like FS1, ESPN, Golf, and NFL Network, and kids networks like Nickelodeon and Disney. You can enter your zip code on the website to check out your local lineup before you commit. If you've been looking for a way to watch your local NFL team on Sundays, as well as other live sports and events, this is it.

It works pretty much just like old-school cable, except that it uses an internet connection and there's no contract. You can still browse channels like the good old days and tune into new episodes of TV shows as they air live. You'll also get unlimited DVR space, special features like Key Plays View, six different household accounts, and three simultaneous streams.

Just remember that after the five-month promotion ends, you'll automatically be charged full price ($82.99 per month), unless you cancel. And be sure to sign up before Black Friday, when the deal expires.
