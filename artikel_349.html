FIFA confirms cyberattack that accessed personal and ​business information

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

FIFA has experienced a cyberattack, the football association first confirmed to Reuters and later Mashable.

On Thursday, the Fédération Internationale de Football Association said an external entity had accessed "a limited set of personal and ​business information" within the organisation. However, FIFA did not detail how many people or files were affected, or when it occurred.

"FIFA can confirm that it has been ​the victim of a criminal cyberattack resulting in ⁠unauthorised access to a limited set of personal and ​business information," a FIFA spokesperson told Mashable in a statement. "FIFA is working closely with the relevant authorities, including law enforcement."

A group called the Handsome Boy Hacking Club has claimed responsibility for the cyberattack, according to the BBC, reportedly posting screenshots of FIFA documents online last week (the group has several posts on X declaring themselves the hackers). The news outlet said the group's claimed 12GB of accessed files amounts to less than one percent of FIFA's data.
