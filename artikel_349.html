The Apple Watch SE 3 has dropped in price again — save $70 in Amazons Black Friday sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

SAVE $70: As of Nov. 23, the Apple Watch SE 3 is on sale for $179 at Amazon. That's a 28% discount on the list price.

Amazon's Black Friday sale officially kicked off on Nov. 19, and the early deals have been impressive.

Last week, the retailer kicked $50 off the price of the Apple Watch SE 3, making it the lowest price we'd seen for this model. Following the weekend, Amazon has reduced the price of this Apple fitness tracker again, bringing it down by a further $20 to $179. This discount applies to both the starlight aluminum case with a sand sport band and the midnight aluminum case with a navy blue sport band colorways. Despite being one of Apple's more affordable smartwatches, the SE 3 doesn't skimp on features. It can track your daily activity, monitor your sleep, and record workouts ranging from running and swimming to strength training. You'll also get temperature sensing, insights through the Vitals and Cycle Tracking apps, and sleep apnea notifications.

As for battery life, you can expect up to 18 hours on a full charge. And when you're short on time, just 15 minutes of charging can provide up to eight hours of use. Built-in Siri makes it easy to get things done hands-free, while fall and crash detection add an extra layer of safety by helping you contact emergency services when needed.

Head to Amazon to get the Apple Watch SE 3 for even less.
