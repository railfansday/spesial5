Liverpool XI vs Club Brugge: Predicted lineup, confirmed team news, injury latest for Champions League today

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Standard's journalism is supported by our readers. If you click links to other sites on this page, we will earn commission.

Liverpool will still be missing Cody Gakpo and Ryan Gravenberch for their Champions League clash with Club Brugge tonight.

The Dutch duo were both absent for Sunday’s 4-0 thrashing of fierce rivals Manchester United in the Premier League and won’t return as Andoni Iraola’s side head to Belgium on Wednesday.

"I think with Cody it's a soleus injury, it's a muscle injury,” Iraola said at his pre-match press conference. "It's not a big thing but I think we expected that he was out. He's going to be out even for the weekend [against Everton]. It's more predictable.

"With Ryan, it's difficult to say. I think he is having pain. These things in the fascia are difficult to solve.

"You know sometimes it's about managing the pain, but now it's not in a good place and let's see if we can find ways to solve a little bit the problem. But injuries [like this] are not easy to go normally."

Jeremie Frimpong and Virgil van Dijk were welcome returners against United following respective injury scares, while Rio Ngumoha played a starring role in the demolition job on Sunday in place of Gakpo on the left flank.

You would expect Iraola to opt for a similar side in Europe, with Ronald Araujo at right-back, Jeremy Jacquet partnering Van Dijk and Milos Kerkez completing the back four ahead of Alisson.

In the engine room, Alexis Mac Allister was joined against United by Dominik Szoboszlai, who himself had been an injury concern, and that should be the partnership once more unless young Trey Nyoni is given another opportunity.

Ahead of them, Florian Wirtz’s good form continued as he registered a goal in the thrashing of United, and he was flanked by the dangerous Ngumoha and summer signing Bradley Barcola.

Alexander Isak led the line by example on Sunday, and should get the nod once more here, with Hugo Ekitike still out with his Achilles rupture.

There could be returns to the squad for defenders Giovanni Leoni and Conor Bradley, with the pair finally making their comebacks to training after serious knee injuries.
