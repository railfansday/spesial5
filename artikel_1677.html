Opinion: Liverpool lining up surprise star to replace Alisson Becker

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Liverpool are reportedly lining up a surprise move to replace Alisson Becker.

Everything comes to an end. That is the reality of life. It's why life is both beautiful and tragic at the same time. Endings make you appreciate the present, the moments for what they really were - poignant periods of our lives that had a profound impact on us.

Experiences are the greatest tokens we have as humans.

For Liverpool supporters, Alisson brought so many of these profound moments. From incredible saves to standing up in the face of tragedy.

That goal against West Brom. Where he leaped from the sky and thundered the ball into the back of the net with his forehead merely days after losing his father will live long in the memory.

It was during the darkest period of the Covid-19 Pandemic and it brought fans a split-second relief from the doom and gloom.

Alisson's time is coming to an end at LiverpoolWe've seen a lot of legends leave Anfield over the last few years, and Alisson is likely going to be the next one who follows suit. The Brazilian could have left Liverpool last summer, but in the end, the club decided to keep him. He now has less than a year left on his contract with Liverpool, which expires at the end of the season. It's hard to see him being retained any further. He's 34-years-old and he is no longer the incredible goalkeeper that he once was. Liverpool have also very clearly paved the way for his departure. They signed Giorgi Mamardashvili two years ago, brought in Armin Pecsi a year ago and also broke the world record for an U23 goalkeeper when they agreed to sign Lucca Brughmann last summer.
