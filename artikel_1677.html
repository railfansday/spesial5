Bruno Fernandes boosts Milan’s hopes with update on Man Utd talks: “No negotiations ongoing”

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Bruno Fernandes has provided an update on his contract situation with Manchester United, as AC Milan watch on closely.

During an interview with Sky Sports, Fernandes admitted that Manchester United have offered him a new deal, with his current one expiring at the end of the season. However, he has put the talks on hold as he wants to see what their ambitions are.

Sky add that the Portuguese playmaker has rejected two contract offers from United. The Red Devils’ initial proposal was made in the summer when discussions first began, but it was turned down. United do have a one-year option that they can trigger.

“No, there’s no negotiations ongoing, but I’ve seen people saying that ‘he’s asked for more money, he’s asked for more years’. If I wanted more money, I wouldn’t be here. I had somewhere to go to get much more money than being at Manchester United,” he said.

“I could have been in a place that is much warmer than Manchester! Two summers ago, three summers ago, last summer, I always had the chance to leave and I always chose to be here.

“The thing is, I’ve always showed how much I care about the club and how much I want to win at this club. And I always said, in the moment the club thinks it’s time for me to move on, I will move on.

“I’m not going to be the player that I’ve seen many in this time at Manchester United. They’re going to stay here just by staying, just finish my contract and then we will see. In the moment the club says, ‘Bruno, it’s time to part ways’, I’m going to be the first one walking out the door.

“Yes, the club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay. And as you said, there are no negotiations going on.

“We spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side because I don’t think it’s the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most important thing now is that the club gets back to win games.

“My aim will always be to win the Premier League and the Champions League within the club I am. I will never hide from that, I will never say, no, our position at the moment needs to be to get top four.”

Recently, Fernandes’ name has been mentioned in conjunction with Milan, who are looking to boost the level of their squad with quality signings. The former Sporting CP man knows head coach Ruben Amorim well, and could help fill the void if Luka Modric retires/departs.

Fabrizio Romano even spoke of the idea being ‘more than a dream’ for the Rossoneri management, while The Athletic also reported that the 32-year-old is an option Milan are exploring after he turned down United’s second offer.

Since joining Manchester United in January 2020, Bruno Fernandes has recorded 113 goals and 111 assists in 340 appearances across all competitions
