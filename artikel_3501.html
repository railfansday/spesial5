Lego’s First Marvel Rivals Set Is A 495-Piece Tribute To The Game’s Most Adorable Terror

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Next month marks the second anniversary of Marvel Rivals, and to celebrate, a new Lego set is on the way that lets you build the game's unofficial mascot, Jeff the Land Shark. While we regularly see Lego Marvel sets roll out, this is the first-ever Marvel Rivals kit, and it'll go on sale starting January 1, 2027, priced at $50. GameSpot can also exclusively reveal the first images of Jeff below, showing the completed build alongside his box art.

See at The Lego Store

The 495-piece set captures the adorable strategist in all his glory, including his big eyes, toothy smile, huge tongue, and even his collar. This won't be a static figure either: once fully assembled, Jeff has a movable head, legs, tail, and fins that let builders recreate his joyful trot, swimming, and battle poses. For added touches, his collar includes his signature name tag, his tongue can be removed to close his mouth, and his fin can be detached so builders can replicate Jeff's Hide-and-Seek movement ability from the game.

"Jeff brings together everything players love about a breakout gaming character: an unforgettable silhouette, a personality you understand instantly, and the ability to turn any match into a story," Lego Marvel design lead Jesper C. Nielsen said in a press release. "We wanted this Lego model to capture both sides of him, from the lovable companion fans want beside their screen to the unpredictable strategist they meet in battle."

Meanwhile, Jeff and the rest of the Marvel Rivals roster are preparing to cap off an entire year of Avengers-centric events that have been building up to the game's big Avengers: Doomsday tie-in. Season 11.5 kicks off on December 11. Nightcrawler was recently added to the game, bringing mutant energy, swords, and teleportation strategies, and he'll soon be followed by Marvel's unique take on Dracula, who has spent the last few months hibernating and healing for the battles ahead.
