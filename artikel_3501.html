Brother of Hayden Panettiere's Ex Recalls Her Last Moments Before Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The brother of Hayden Panettiere’s on-off boyfriend Brian Hickerson is detailing the late actress’ final moments before her tragic death.

“I was not even supposed to be in Greenville that Sunday. I was supposed to go down to Atlanta to play golf,” Zach Hickerson began a Wednesday, November 25, interview with TMZ. “Upon learning that Hayden was like, ‘Absolutely not.’”

Walking into the South Carolina Airbnb where Panettiere and Brian, 37, were staying in August, Zach, 39, initially didn’t notice anything out of the ordinary.

“I didn’t think it was abnormal that she was [sitting], kind of, Indian-style and laid over,” he recalled. “Her hair was longer, so it was covering. I just assumed that she would pop up and be like, ‘Zachary, what are we doing today?’”

According to Zach, he realized something felt “odd” when Panettiere didn’t eventually wake up.

“[Brian] took his hand and went, ‘Hayden,’ and when she didn’t immediately respond or make a noise or react, I stood up and moved to the side,” Zach told the outlet. “Brian … pulled her onto his chest, and when he did, I had a clear view of her face, [which] was pale, lips were purple. I immediately knew something was incredibly wrong.”

While Zach wasn’t aware that the Nashville alum was unconscious at the time, he decided to call 911 for medical assistance.

“We did CPR for, gosh I don’t even know how long it was. I just did it until the EMS and the cops and the fire and whomever else came in,” he said. “I was like, ‘Oh my God, thank God, they’re here.’ Brian and I got up, got out of the way.”

Watching the first responders provide aid, Zach said he was “praying and crying” that the officials would be able to resuscitate Panettiere. Their attempts were ultimately unsuccessful and the actress died on the scene. (Panettiere is survived by her parents and her 11-year-old daughter, Kaya, whom she shared with ex Wladimir Klitschko.)

“When they called it, I was kneeling at her feet almost in front of her,” Zach said. “I just remember them saying, ‘We have to call it,’ and they named the time. I was crying, I put my hand on her leg and said goodbye.”

Zach further revealed how he was “so in the moment” of praying for a recovery that he didn’t notice Brian’s reaction. (Brian and the former Heroes star dated on and off from 2018 to 2020. She wrote about the demise of their relationship in her memoir, This Is Me: A Reckoning, published in May.)

“I remember him being in more of a distraught, disbelief, kind of, shock the whole time,” Zach claimed. “I don’t know what was going through his mind or what his reaction is, but to me he looked just in disbelief.”

Panettiere’s death was ultimately ruled as “toxic effects of fentanyl, 4-ANPP, Alprazolam, Methocarbamol, and Quetiapine” and an accident.
