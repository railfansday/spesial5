The Best GTA 6 Easter Eggs We’ve Seen So Far

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Familiar faces (and voices) in unexpected places

Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally in the wild, and there are a mind-boggling number of things to do in Leonida. Many of them are terribly dangerous, like stealing cars from the police impound lot, getting into high-speed chases, and laundering stolen goods. But GTA 6 has plenty of wild and wacky optional activities too, many of which include references to pop-culture icons, past Rockstar games, or well-known memes. Here are a few of the coolest Easter eggs we've discovered in our own playthroughs and from other players on social media.

GTA's radio stations have become famous for their celebrity hosts over the years, and GTA 6 doubles down on that tradition. Weird Al is a host on Vice City FM, which plays 1980s hits from artists like Hall and Oates, Phil Collins, and Flock of Seagulls. He confirmed his role in the game in a recent Bluesky post, where he wrote, "I’m the host of GTA 6’s “non-stop ‘80s” radio channel Vice City FM. I also wrote some in-game commercials, and I’m the voice of the Burger Shot ads as well! As a long-time fan of the Grand Theft Auto franchise, I couldn’t be more excited about this. Okay, gotta get back to playing the game!"

During Jason's final days as a bachelor early in the game, one of the most relaxing activities you can get up to is swimming near his house. If you manage to venture out far enough, you'll come across an unusual watersports enthusiast: a German Shepherd riding a jet ski all by himself. This is likely a reference to a real dog in Fort Lauderdale, a Golden Retriever who's become a local attraction due to his love of riding the waves. The breeds may not be the same, but the shout-out is clear.

In Brian's office at the Marina, you can find a copy of a book called The Cowboy's Prayer Is to Die Young. It contains several references to the events of 2018's Red Dead Redemption 2, the previous game from GTA 6 developer Rockstar Games. The book follows protagonist Rust McCreary, a clear stand-in for Arthur Morgan, as he "rides across the final days of the West in a bloody quest for inner peace." If you flip through the pages, you'll find references to New Austin, Blackwater, and Ambarino. There's even a pretty detailed rundown of a main-story mission from RDR2, where Arthur tracks down gunslinger Flaco Hernandez to a cabin on Cairn Lake.

Once you make it to Port Gellhorn, you can find a convincing replica of Arthur's hat at a yard sale. The house is located about two blocks south of Moses' garage, so keep an eye out for signs.

Dexter, the beloved Showtime crime drama that aired between 2006 and 2013, takes place in Miami. So it's a natural fit for some kind of cameo in GTA 6. Near the top of Washington Island in Vice City, you'll find the Trinity Apartments, named in homage to one of the show's most memorable villains. The building itself is also a near-exact dupe of Dexter's own residence. If you venture into the building, you'll discover "Dexter's apartment" right where you'd expect it to be based on its location in the show. From there, explore the rooftop to find some of the noble serial killer's tools hidden up there, including a rope, a knife, and a syringe.

Leonida's radio stations feature plenty of recognizable voices, but there's one that's easy to miss. The Kaleidoscope channel features retro synth-pop, European disco, non-English covers of iconic '80s tunes, and plenty of deep cuts from Germany. The station is hosted by "DJ Dicky," but that velvety British baritone comes from none other than Jeremy Irons, the voice of Scar in The Lion King and the dastardly Simon Gruber in Die Hard With a Vengeance, along with countless other roles in film and television.

The Fast and the Furious series has no shortage of incredibly cool cars, but few are quite as memorable as the neon-green Mitsubishi Eclipse Paul Walker's Brian drives in the film that started it all. It has a sky-high rear spoiler and eye-catching blue body art, and you can get one of your own in GTA 6 by breaking into the garage on Vespucci Island. The location can be found in the middle of the Rialto Islands and west of Vice Beach.

Fans of Miami Vice director Michael Mann are probably already pretty pumped to spend dozens of hours in GTA 6, and now they've got another reason to celebrate. Rockstar's latest also includes an optional encounter with an NPC inspired by Tom Cruise's character Vincent from the 2004 film Collateral. Vincent takes a cab driver hostage and forces him to drive across Los Angeles to take out five assassination targets in a single night. Unlike the film, Jason doesn't have to drive this gun-toting guy around all night, but he does sound conspicuously Cruise-like.

Red Dead 2 isn't the only entry in Rockstar's back catalog to make a cameo in GTA 6. In addition to a dedicated episode all about the main story of GTA 5, there are plenty of references in Rockstar's latest to previous installments of its marquee series, especially on the radio. In an episode of the podcast "Fuck, Marry, Murder," the two hosts discuss the many murders of Eddie Low, a serial killer who appears in GTA 4 and GTA 5.

Fans of deeply upsetting horror films, it's time to live out your worst nightmares. If you explore or swim around Hyde Island as Jason or Lucia, you'll potentially trigger an ambush. You'll be kidnapped and knocked out, temporarily lose all your items, and discover your captor has put a Saw-style detonation device on your neck. You'll awaken in a storage container while a voice speaks over a TV set. After an ominous countdown, you'll be hunted in an arena by a man with night-vision goggles, a sniper rifle, and a ferocious dog. You'll need to sneak around in the darkness to gather as much ammo as possible to take the hunter down, get your stuff back, and escape the island.

An optional side mission in Gullet Marsh includes a reference to one of the most memorable video game memes of all time, as Jason is abruptly attacked by a "giant enemy crab." You can read more about this deep cut here, which was inspired by E3 2006 and has been referenced in countless video games since.
