Jason Voorhees Officially Returns For First Friday The 13th Movie In Nearly 20 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Jason Voorhees is back to haunt Crystal Lake.

The Friday the 13th franchise began in the 80s with the original slasher film, written by Victor Miller and directed by Sean S. Cunningham. Following the success of 1978's Halloween, the beloved horror series has grown with 12 theatrical releases, a TV series, novels, comic books, video games, and more, grossing over $468 million worldwide. Now, the terrifying tale of Jason Voorhees returns with its 13th installment.

A new Friday the 13th movie is officially confirmed for 2027, with franchise veteran Steve Miner directing the 13th entry, marking the first film in nearly two decades. Following the successful A24 and Peacock series Crystal Lake, a new movie is making its way onto the franchise, and it will be the 13th addition. The movie is said to be arriving next year, with screenwriter David Fliegel attached, as well as being financed and produced by the Barsamian family.

Executive Vice President of Horror, Inc., Robbie Barsamian stated, "For generations of fans, Jason’s identity is inseparable from the creative choices Steve Miner brought to the screen,” said Robbie Barsamian, Executive Vice President of Horror, Inc. “Reuniting with Steve under a fully independent model allows us the creative control to honor what makes Jason the greatest slasher of all time and deliver the definitive theatrical event that today’s audiences expect."

Miner first introduced the machete to Jason Voorhees in Friday the 13th Part II and gave the horrifying character the iconic hockey mask in Part III. When discussing the upcoming release, Miner shared his thoughts on returning to the franchise: "When I put the hockey mask on Jason for Friday the 13th Part III, I knew it would create a truly scary image that would work for that movie. I had no idea that it would live on as one of the most iconic symbols in horror films. I’m thrilled to come back and continue the legacy."

The last movie, Friday the 13th, was released in 2009 and starred Supernatural's Jared Padalecki alongside Danielle Panabaker, Julianna Guill, and Derek Mears with Marcus Nispel attached as director. However, the movie only achieved a 27% rating on Rotten Tomatoes, with critics stating, "A best case scenario for a horror remake, Friday The 13th reveres the Jason character and brings him back to his basic roots, but adds intelligence and speed to his arsenal."

Jason Voorhees returned to everyone's screens with the release of Crystal Lake starring Linda Cardellini as the notorious serial killer's mother, Pamela Voorhees. The series was greeted with a much warmer reception and achieved a 77% rating on Rotten Tomatoes, as ScreenRant's Kara Hedash stated that "Linda Cardellini is brilliant in depicting Pamela Voorhees and the character's complex makeover."

Friday the 13th and Jason Voorhees will return to haunt theaters reportedly sometime in 2027.

The Friday the 13th film franchise is a chilling saga centered around the cursed Camp Crystal Lake and the supernatural serial killer Jason Voorhees. Known for his iconic hockey mask and relentless brutality, Jason is a nigh-indestructible force driven by vengeance.
