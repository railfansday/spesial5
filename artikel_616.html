HoverAir made a pocket gimbal that transforms into a drone — where to buy

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

TL;DR: HoverAir launched the Versa, a 2-in-1 hybrid camera that converts from a 163g 3-axis handheld gimbal into a 230g autonomous flying camera in seconds. It is available to order now.

Packing for a hike or weekend trip usually means deciding which piece of gear you can fit into your bag. If you want buttery walking footage or close-up selfies, you reach for a handheld gimbal; if you want third-person follow shots and sweeping views from above, you end up hauling a separate drone, a dedicated flight controller, and extra batteries.

The HoverAir Versa combines both setups into a single pocket-sized rig that transforms between handheld and aerial modes in seconds.

In your hand, the device weighs 163 grams and relies on a 3-axis mechanical gimbal to keep first-person trail footage and vlogs smooth without cropping your video frame. Built around a 1/1.3-inch sensor, it captures up to 17.5 stops of dynamic range, 4K video at 60 fps, 4K HDR at 30 fps, and native 4K vertical clips for social feeds. Advanced low-light image processing and 10-bit H-Log profiles help keep high-contrast scenes — from mid-day mountain glare to neon-lit city streets — from blowing out highlights or losing subtle shadow details.

When you want an overhead view, you snap on the 67-gram flight kit. With two clicks, it becomes a 230-gram autonomous flying camera that launches and lands on your palm. It tracks and films you at speeds up to 36 km/h (about 22 mph), with no piloting experience required, freeing you to focus on the trail or the conversation.

If you want more creative angles, the device packs over 10 smart shooting modes, including Dolly Zoom, Orbit, Inception Mode, and a 3D Worlds feature that flies a 360-degree loop to map and reconstruct surroundings in three dimensions.

Piloting remains flexible whether you prefer to go hands-free or fly manually. You can tweak compositions mid-air using simple hand gesture controls, use the optional 35-gram MiCo Mini Controller for quick one-handed adjustments, or pair the device with Beacon and JoySticks for traditional stick control.

The HoverAir Versa is available in black and white starting on HoverAir's web store, Amazon, and select retail partners.
