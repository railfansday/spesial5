TV Ratings: ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Records Another Huge Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Early ratings are at a 12-year high among adults 18-49 and even with last year in total viewers.

The season 35 finale of Dancing With the Stars capped off a season of big ratings with the show’s best numbers of the fall.

Early Nielsen ratings have the finale — which aired live coast to coast, a first for DWTS — averaging 9.2 million viewers, on par with preliminary numbers for last season’s final episode. The season 34 finale ended up at 9.43 million viewers after the addition of Nielsen’s big data component; final same-day numbers for Tuesday’s show will be available after Thanksgiving.

The DWTS finale also scored the show’s best rating among adults 18-49 in more than a decade: It’s currently at 2.38 (equivalent to about 3.24 million people in the demographic), subject to adjustments. That’s the highest rating for a Dancing With the Stars episode since the season 19 finale in 2014 — when current host Alfonso Ribeiro won the mirror ball trophy — and the highest mark for a regularly scheduled network entertainment show (i.e., not a special like the Oscars or Grammys) since an episode of The Masked Singer in April 2020.

The finale also scored a 15-year high among adults under 35 with a 2.86 rating — an almost unheard-of number for anything outside of live sports in the current broadcast environment.

Pending updates for the finale, season 35 of DWTS averaged 7.19 million viewers, up about 11 percent from last year. The show’s 1.59 rating among adults 18-49 is up by almost 20 percent year to year. The latter number ranks first among all network shows this fall, excluding live sports, and leads second-place Survivor (0.86 rating) by a canyon-sized 86 percent margin. Should that hold, it would be the largest gap between a first- and second-place show in the 18-49 demo since the beginning of Nielsen’s electronic measurement in 1991.

ABC says viewers cast a record-setting 86 million votes during the finale, up from 72 million for the season 34 closer. DWTS also generated the most social media engagements of any primetime program Tuesday.
