11 Gorgeous US Ski Vacation Homes Built for Fresh Powder and Easy Slope Access

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

I’ve been skiing my entire life, venturing out for nostalgic winter weekends on the East Coast and fresh powder-chasing adventures out West. Yet somehow, the anticipation of a new ski season never gets old. Like clockwork, every winter brings a new mountain to explore, a favorite run to revisit, and plenty of reasons to start dreaming about the next snowfall. With El Niño in the conversation and ski resorts across the US gearing up for opening day, there’s no better time to get your winter plans in order.

After all, a memorable ski vacation isn’t just about finding the right mountain (though I take my terrain very seriously). It’s also about the cozy ski towns, the lively après-ski scenes, the snow-covered scenery, and of course, the splendid place to retreat to when it’s time for boots to come off. When it comes to the best ski vacation homes, there are some amenities that really do take these trips to the next level: a hot tub to soothe your muscles, an in-unit washer and dryer to freshen up base layers, a nice fireplace or firepit to cozy up around, and a kitchen for easy meals at home—especially ideal after working up a big appetite on the mountain.

Some of the best ski vacation rentals in the US are every bit as enticing as the mountains themselves. Think glass-walled chalets, design-forward A-frames, and tucked-away cabins with easy access to the lifts. From New England favorites to Western powder havens, these 11 standout stays are worth building your next ski or snowboard getaway around.

Which ski resorts in the US are on the Epic Pass?

The Epic Pass offers skiing and snowboarding at a sprawling collection of USresorts. Major destinations include Vail, Beaver Creek, Breckenridge, Keystone, Crested Butte, Park City, Heavenly, Northstar, Kirkwood, Stevens Pass, and Stowe. There are also regional and partner resorts with more limited access, so check the specific pass tier before booking a trip. Do note that blackout dates and access limits vary by pass, so read the fine print!

Which ski resorts in the US are on the Ikon Pass?

The Ikon Pass has a tempting lineup as well, especially if your winter plans involve chasing snow across the Rockies and Sierras. Its US destinations include Mammoth Mountain, Palisades Tahoe, Steamboat, Winter Park, Aspen Snowmass, Copper Mountain, Big Sky, Jackson Hole, Taos Ski Valley, Deer Valley, Alta, Snowbird, and Sun Valley. Out east, you can head to Killington, Sugarbush, Stratton, Loon Mountain, Sugarloaf, or Sunday River.

Where is El Niño predicted to bring snowfall this season?

For the 2026-27 season, a strong El Niño is shaping the forecast, with the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) expecting wetter-than-average conditions across the southern US, from California and the Southwest through the Southeast, as well as along the East Coast. This could mean more opportunities for snowfall in higher-elevation ski destinations, particularly when temperatures cooperate.

However, wetter doesn't automatically mean snowier: NOAA also expects warmer-than-average conditions across much of the West and northern US Seasonal outlooks predict precipitation patterns, not specific snowfall totals, so keep an eye on shorter-range forecasts before planning a powder chase.
