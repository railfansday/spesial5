50 Travel Gifts Under $50 They'll Actually Use on Their Next Trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Travel buffs can be surprisingly difficult to shop for. They probably already have a suitcase they love, noise-canceling headphones, and enough packing cubes to last them a lifetime. Instead of adding to their collection of travel essentials, consider something they wouldn't necessarily buy for themselves. It doesn't have to be expensive, either. The best travel gifts will upgrade every journey, whether it’s a smart gadget to help make a red-eye flight less miserable or a sweet reminder of their favorite destination—all without breaking the bank.

With this in mind, I’ve rounded up 50 thoughtful gifts under $50 to give this season, from personalized accessories to luxe skincare sets. Whether you're shopping for a frequent flier, an avid road tripper, or a friend with a growing list of places to visit, there's something here for every traveler on your list—including yourself.

The adjustable straps can even be clasped over your chest, making it more secure and comfortable.
