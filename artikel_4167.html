Pokémon GO Players Can Claim Free Pikachu Crocs Avatar Item

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Scott Baird is a Contributor from the UK with over a decade of video game writing experience.

Halloween might be over, but Pokémon Go is giving the chance to unleash horrors upon its world, as the worst clothing item ever produced by human hands is coming to the game, and fans can acquire it for free.

It's quite refreshing to be able to make some actual progress in a mobile title without opening my wallet. Thanks, Pokemon GO.

Pokémon Go has had multiple controversies regarding player avatars; back when Niantic was running the game, there was a massive downgrade in character quality. Suddenly, characters lacked curves, and everyone became a generic outline, with detail plummeting across the board.

The backlash against the bad Pokémon Go avatars has diminished over time, but that hasn’t stopped the game from receiving some terrible outfits. The next one involves a type of shoe that is an abomination upon humanity, and one set of them is available in Pokémon Go for free.

Crocs are the worst shoes ever made, being marginally better than leather boots filled with thumb tacks worn by masochists. However, people buy them, and this has led to geek crossovers such as Yu-Gi-Oh! Crocs, and recently, Pokémon.

The official Pokémon Go website has announced that the Crocs crossover has arrived in the game and will run until December 31, 2027, giving players ample time to acquire them. One of the sets is completely free, being the Pokémon Pikachu Classic Clog item, which can be claimed by using the code POKÉMONGOxCROCSxELECTRIFYING in the game.

Pokemon Go's Scattered to the Winds event is bringing new Shiny forms and expanding the Gift limit for players.

Two other sets of Pokémon Go Crocs can be claimed: Pokémon Gengar Classic Clog and Pokémon Jigglypuff Classic Clog. These can only be claimed by the sickos who purchased them in real life, and they’ll be sent a free code to download them.

The player avatar in Pokémon Go is pretty small, and they don’t appear on the screen all that much, as the game is mostly focused on catching Pokémon and battles. That being said, players care about how their in-game avatar looks, often going for attractive, cool, or downright weird models to stand out from the crowd. This is why the in-game cosmetics industry is so profitable.

Niantic has revealed that one of the Pokemon Go March 2025 events is a Fuecoco-filled Community Day.

Pokémon Go has plenty of paid avatar items, some of which are tied to limited events. There are also lots of free avatar items, unlocked by completing tasks, giving players a reason to log in every day. All of them, without fail, are better-looking than the Pokémon Crocs collection.

Then again, there’s a certain kind of fan that loves the items everyone hates. Indeed, some people wear Crocs for this exact reason, imagining themselves as some kind of Cyberpunk-era James Dean rebel, wearing their awful plastic shoes with holes in them, rather than the sad, soulless wretches they truly are.

Perhaps the idea behind this crossover is to attract the Pokémon Go whales who buy everything associated with the game. They’ll have to buy some Gengar and Jigglypuff Crocs to stay in the loop and ensure they have every avatar item at their fingertips. Whether such fans remain after years of lackluster Pokémon Go updates remains to be seen.

In the end, it’s hard to be mad at a company giving away goodies for free. Maybe there’s a fan out there who is still stuck with the basic Pokémon Go avatar shoes and wants something a bit nicer for their little character to strut around the overworld in. Maybe that’s the point of all this, as walking around in Crocs for long enough can make any player reach for their wallet and buy some premium cosmetics.
