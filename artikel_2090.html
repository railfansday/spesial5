West Ham should be keeping tabs on situation of out-of-favour Leeds United striker: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

West Ham have a chance to propel themselves into title-winning contention in the January transfer window. WHU have been impressive to start the year and are the clear top scorers in the Championship, despite struggling to nail down a number nine.

West Ham United should be looking at Mateo Joseph as a potential January addition, with the Leeds United forward offering an interesting opportunity for Scott Parker as he looks to guide the Hammers back into the Premier League.

Parker has inherited a side with plenty of attacking quality, but the centre-forward position remains an area that could benefit from strengthening. With Joseph looking to be available in January, West Ham must be paying attention to his situation.

Joel Piroe has scored around four league goals since arriving on loan from Leeds, while Taty Castellanos has struggled to establish himself as a consistent goalscoring threat. West Ham are scoring freely, with 37 goals in their opening 15 Championship fixtures, but another option could prove important during the second half of the campaign.

Joseph would offer something different. The 23-year-old spent last season on loan at Mallorca, making 29 La Liga appearances, including 19 starts.

He registered two goals and three assists, with his underlying numbers suggesting there could be more to come from him in front of goal. His 4.92 expected goals compared with two actual strikes indicates that his finishing did not necessarily reflect the opportunities he found, according to FotMob.

Versatility is another attraction. Primarily a centre-forward or right winger, Joseph can operate across the frontline, giving Parker additional tactical flexibility. He also has experience of winning promotion from the Championship, having featured 39 times for Leeds during their successful 2024/25 campaign, recording three goals and three assists.

There is, however, an obvious complication. Joseph suffered an ACL injury while at Mallorca in April and has been recovering at Leeds. He is targeting a return to competitive action around January or February 2027, meaning any potential move would carry considerable fitness risk.

His modest senior goalscoring record also means West Ham would be taking a chance rather than signing an established Championship marksman. Nevertheless, that could make him an attainable target. Leeds are reportedly open to moving him on, while his injury situation could create an opportunity for a loan agreement with a potential obligation to buy.

West Ham need to be careful with their January recruitment, particularly with promotion within reach. However, Joseph’s age, versatility and previous Championship experience make him an intriguing option. If his recovery progresses as hoped, he could provide valuable competition for Piroe without requiring a significant initial investment.
