Hit or Miss : West Ham fans voice their opinion loud and clear on Scott Parker appointment

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

2026 is becoming something of a landmark year for West Ham United. Relegation, explosive allegations, departures, resignations, board room battles, a takeover foiled and now a manager sacked by telephone: It isn’t dull, is it.

Few fans would have expected the West Ham leadership to act so decisively, having grown accustomed to the slow painful, managerial death of a thousand ‘last games’ which had become West Ham’s preferred method of drawing out their head coach sackings.

Appointing a former three-time Hammer of the Year as head coach has certainly energised a fan base desperate for the end of the no-no-Nuno era.

Here we’ve several guest posts by Claret and Hugh readers voicing their own approval – or distaste for – the arrival of West Ham’s twenty third permanent managerial appointment:

Not my first choice either.. but I too am getting excited about this past hero running the team. Concerns seem to be with his Premier League record, but we are a long way from that at the moment. If he succeeds he will be taking up the biggest club he has managed by some way and his transfer budget should reflect that and increase his ability to stay up next time.

Scott needs to hit the ground running. A pragmatic approach to getting the best out of the squad we have is vitally important.

Nuno was still playing around with nuanced differences and his plan was to get some out and get some more in in January to continue building ‘his’ team.

We did not have the time for this plan. We need the team to perform now. Come on Scotty!

I am glad that this whole sorry saga of Lopetegui (although I felt a bit sorry for L), Potter & megalomaniac NES is finally over. To have a manager who internally divides a squad and ostracizes people beggars belief. Parker, Noble, Potts, Prenderville are not this type…..

Whatever Parker’s reputation with Burnley, this is a pretty good squad. He was willing to use youngsters in the past and we have plenty to hand along with some excellent experienced players. Parker & Noble were hard workers on a pitch but also very talented and if any of that is reflected by the team it will be a good start.

I know everyone talks about promotion, but personally, I want to see a blend of youth and experience, playing well, taking the game to the opposition and playing for each other – Let’s get to mid-season first of all. The players’ responsibility is to play well for 90+ minutes and play as a team, not get the club promoted – those are tactics, squad rotation, recruitment, etc…….

Good luck to them and will now look forward to the rest of the season in the Championship…..”

“Wont be attending games this season or writing comments on here now .Lots of talented managers out there so what do we do? Same old same old. Had enough.”

From the old Bobby Moore Upper to the Billy Bonds' stand these days - I've been watching since '03 and a supporter since about 1970.. Favourite player - Dean Ashton: Curse you Shaun Wright-Phillips. I still watch YouTube repeats of the Cup Final of 2006 hoping in vain that Shaka Hislop grows another six inches and stops Steven Gerrard's injury time equaliser. Can tell I'm getting old knowing I watched both Mark Noble's debut and his last game at West Ham. Pulling on a Claret and Blue replica shirt still makes me feel the same butterflies as when I was seven years old. Magic.
