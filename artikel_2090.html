Keith Wyness reveals Rangers have 'two options' over Lyall Cameron

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Rangers are being helped dramatically by the form of midfielder Lyall Cameron out on loan at Dundee United.

That is according to former Aberdeen chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Gers have "two options" to handle the 24-year-old's future long-term.

Cameron was only loaned out to Dundee United this summer, but has already impressed with two goals and two assists in his opening six games for his loan club.

Wyness believes that his form will be music to the ears of Derek McInnes and Co., as it gives them the "luxury" of a choice when his loan expires.

Cameron has been firmly down the pecking order since joining Rangers on a free transfer back in 2025, and also enjoyed a brief loan stint at Aberdeen last term.

However, his latest loan is providing much more success than his time at Pittodrie, which in turn is giving Rangers serious food for thought.

Aberdeen's former chief Wyness - who served as CEO at Pittodrie between 2000 and 2004 and now runs a consultancy business advising elite clubs - believes that Cameron's form is seriously boosting his value for Rangers.

He pointed out that the Ibrox outfit will either reintegrate him into the squad or sell him at the end of the season, with either appearing to be a decent option right now.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how Cameron is boosting his transfer value with every passing game.

"Well, in this loan, he's doing exactly what it's supposed to do. He's putting himself in the shop window, and he's performing," he said.

"There are two options: either he comes back to Rangers and they can sell him, and he's now got some value, or he can come back and get minutes. The worst thing Rangers could do is just hold him and do nothing again.

"He's showing talent, and it gives Rangers that luxury of an option. A few things are starting to go right for Rangers in terms of making football decisions behind the scenes.

"For the kid's benefit, he needs to know where his future is going to be. I think there'll be a market for him if they want to sell."

Football Insider revealed last weekend that Dundee United are keen to try and sign Cameron permanently when his loan deal expires.

The Tannadice outfit are hugely impressed by the impact the midfielder has made, and are hoping to be able to keep him long-term.

Sources say that Rangers won't decide on Cameron's future until the end of the campaign, which is set to give United an anxious wait.

They will be desperately hoping that they are not priced out by the Gers' final valuation, given their clear desire to sign Cameron already.
