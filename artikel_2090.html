Scotland are back in action on Monday evening as Graham eyes his third appearance. They face a tricky away trip to Switzerland as they target another positive result.

Stoke City landed Graham in the last transfer window to bolster their defensive department. He has since become a key player for SCFC.

At the age of 22, he has potential to grow and develop further down the line. He battles it out with the likes of Ben Wilmot, Bosun Lawal and Maksym Talovierov for game time with the Potters.

Graham has made 15 appearances for Mark Robins’ side in all competitions in this campaign, 13 of which have come in the league, and he has chipped in with one goal. He made his debut for Scotland off the bench against North Macedonia in early October.

The ex-St Johnstone academy man rose into Dundee’s first-team in 2024 after loan spells away from Dens Park at Lochee United, Albion Rovers and Montrose as a youngster to get some experience and to boost his development. He went on to make 46 outings for his previous team and found the net on three occasions before heading down the border to England when Stoke City came calling.

Stoke City’s swoop for Graham earlier this year has worked out well for all parties involved. SCFC’s summer recruit is now gaining international recognition with Scotland.

The Potters are joint-top of the table along with Birmingham City and Middlesbrough. They won 2-1 away at Portsmouth last time out.

Robins’ men are unbeaten in their last five games. They are back in action next weekend with a home match against Birmingham City.
