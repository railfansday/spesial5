Europa and Conference League Liveblog: Olympiacos-Milan, Juventus-Omonia, Borac-Atalanta

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from the Europa League and Conference League, with Milan visiting Olympiacos, Juventus hosting Omonia Nicosia, and Atalanta away to Borac.

We begin at 17.45 GMT (18.45 CET) with Milan in Greece hoping to turn their European fortunes around with Olympiacos.

At 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET), Juventus welcome Omonia Nicosia to stay in the top spots, while Atalanta are in Bosnia and Herzegovina against borac.

Maurizio Sarri warns Atalanta ‘cannot always expect to resolve games with these comebacks’ after a 3-2 away to Borac in the Conference League, while Gianluca Scamacca is ‘lacking confidence.’

Luciano Spalletti admits Andrea Cambiaso’s stunning goal ‘got Juventus back on track’ for a 4-1 Europa League win over Omonia Nicosia, and Jeff Ekhator provides different solutions in attack.

Atalanta just do not know when they’re beaten.

Has to be said that a) they were against 9 men, but b) had missed at least 3 open goals before that.

Atalanta wasted so many chances in Bosnia, but after Borac went down to nine men, completed another incredible comeback to win 3-2 with Nikola Krstovic, Sead Kolasinac and Davide Zappacosta.

Juventus recovered from a Neto howler to beat Omonia Nicosia 4-1 with Jeff Ekhator, Andrea Cambiaso, Jeremie Boga and Francisco Conceicao on target.

At the 93rd minute, Zappacosta down the left, cuts inside and fires the right-foot finish into the far bottom corner!

Woltemade might be offside, he hesitated and the defender cut him off.

Seven minutes of added time… Did we mention Borac are down to 9 men?

Zalewski free kick to the back post, deflection off a defender hits the upright, but Kolasinac manages to bundle the rebound over the line!

Absolutely horrible Herrera tackle from behind on Zalewski, not just the first, but the follow-through as well…

Ewandro header is on target, but also straight into the arms of Neto.

It’s always him! Bellanova floats a cross to the back post for the Krstovic header, still hope for Atalanta!

Zappacosta scorcher deflected over the bar for a corner.

Conceicao tripped after getting away, Balkovic booked.

Ewandro attempt deflected, could go anywhere, Neto quite pleased to see it sail over for a corner.

Jurkas actually parried that with his face.. Brave goalkeeping, now a little shaken.

Another huge chance for Atalanta on the free kick, but it falls to Rowe, who fires straight at the on-rushing goalkeeper at the near post!

Dinked pass finds Montnor for a volley, but it’s well over from 12 yards.

Rowe gets away on the Krstovic flick and is brought down just outside the box by Abel Pascual! Inevitable red card.

That is another absolutely cracking finish!

Koopmeiners sends Cambiaso through the centre, lay off for Boga down the left, cuts inside and after a few little dummies, smashes the right-foot finish into the top corner from just inside the area!

How does the finishing keep getting worse?

De Ketelaere this time incredibly turns over from close range after the Pasalic attempt is parried into his path! Ridiculous.
