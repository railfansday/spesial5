Man City have a striker who will not be stopped and a defence that cannot be trusted

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Manchester City surely cannot win the Champions League with a defence leaking goals and a goalkeeper making errors. But when Erling Haaland is in the team, anything seems possible.

This thrilling victory for City perfectly sums up their season: needless goals conceded, Gianluigi Donnarumma making a mistake, followed by Haaland saving the day with a winning goal.

“How can you stop Haaland?” read the headline in the local Volkszeitung newspaper on the morning of this Champions League clash. The answer is that it has been impossible for RB Leipzig.

Leipzig roll out the Red Bull carpet when they face the Norweigan. He has now scored 13 goals against them in nine matches, the most he has scored against a single team during his career. Enzo Maresca gave him the armband here and his winning goal masked more worries for the City head coach in his back line.

For all their travails on the road – RB Leipzig have also lost at Werder Bremen, Bayer Leverkusen and Bayern Munich – they have won all their home Bundesliga matches as well as a 1-0 victory over PSV in this competition. Their total of 22 league goals is second only to Bayern’s 27 and Antonio Nusa, a Norway team-mate of Erling Haaland, is in sparkling form flitting behind strikers Christopher Nkunku and Romulo.
