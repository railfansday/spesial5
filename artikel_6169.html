Which Airlines Fly To The Most Countries? One Carrier Has A Huge Lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

I don’t want to bury the lede — in posts, I often reference how Turkish Airlines flies to more countries than any other airline in the world. However, how many countries does the airline actually fly to, and how far ahead of competitors is Turkish Airlines? I thought that would be interesting to explore…

Turkish Airlines has the record for flying to more countries than any other airline in the world. That has been the case for a long time, and in late 2024, the Star Alliance carrier made it official, with a Guinness World Record in the category of “most countries flown to by an airline.” The airline has flown to more countries than any other airline since 2012, and has a sizable lead over competitors.

Just how many countries does Turkish Airlines fly to? This is where it always gets tricky as to what you count. If you count active flights operating at the moment, the airline flies to 117 countries. However:

As you’d expect, when you’re an airline serving virtually every corner of the globe, there will always be some suspended services due to geopolitical factors, etc. What’s cool is that Turkish Airlines is also far from done with its expansion, as the airline still has big growth plans. It’ll be fascinating to see just how many countries the airline eventually serves.

What’s perhaps most impressive about Turkish Airlines’ record for serving the most countries is just how far ahead of competitors the airline is. Turkish Airlines is 30+ countries ahead of the next airline when it comes to countries served.

As you can see, that means Qatar Airways serves the most countries after Turkish Airlines, while Air France, British Airways, and Ethiopian Airlines, are currently tied for third place.

Meanwhile it’s impressive that Emirates serves so many countries when you consider that the airline just flies wide body jets. If you combined the networks of Emirates and flydubai (both are owned by the government, but the airlines are run separately), then the combined airline would have close to as many destinations as Turkish Airlines.

Let me of course also acknowledge that the number of countries served and number of destinations served is very different, and US carriers would do much better by the latter metric. And if the question is the number of premium destinations served, then there’s only one right answer. 😉

Turkish Airlines sets the world record for serving the most countries in the world. The airline actively flies to 117 countries, but including suspended routes, serves 130+ countries. That puts the airline way ahead of the next carriers by this metric, which include Qatar Airways at 86 countries, followed by a tie between Air France, British Airways, and Ethiopian Airlines, at 80 countries.
