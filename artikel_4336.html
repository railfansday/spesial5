Phantom Blade Zero: Here's How To Get The Perfect Ending, Which Is The Only Satisfying One

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Phantom Blade Zero is a sprawling Wuxia adventure, the best action game of the year, and one of my favorite action games ever. It offers a lot to do, from main story quests to dozens of side quests, collectibles, hidden bosses, secret areas, and more. I highly recommend checking out Game Informer's list of Phantom Blade Zero tips and tricks before jumping in. It mentions some tasks required to achieve the perfect ending, but here I'll break down exactly what you need to see it.

As a note, getting the perfect ending in Phantom Blade Zero isn't that unreasonable. It requires doing a lot of the game's side content, but it's not shrouded in mystery or puzzles; you just need to complete certain things before heading into the finale. I'm going to keep these requirements relatively spoiler-free, and honestly, without the wider story context, they'll be a lot of mumbo jumbo proper nouns that don't really mean anything. If you're set on getting the perfect ending, I'd bookmark this page, and as you play Phantom Blade Zero, more and more of these requirements will start to make sense. However, if you think even a name or proper noun is a spoiler, be careful reading these requirements.

The following requirements don't necessarily have a set order. You could technically ignore all of this and then go after everything right before the game's "last chance to get things done" warning near the finale. So don't sweat about picking up this or that at a certain time in your journey – do it when you want to. Just make sure you have the following requirements completed before heading past the "last chance" warning.
