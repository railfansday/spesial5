Zoomlion shows off SIXTY electrified, AI equipment assets at bauma 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Wit more than sixty (60!) new-energy models spanning everything from driverless excavators to cabless loaders and hybrid-electric mining trucks, the next generation of Zoomlion machinery is wowing everyone at bauma 2026.

Closing out a breakout year for the Chinese equipment brand, Zoomlion arrived at bauma China 2026 with more than 60 intelligent, new-energy heavy equipment options spanning eight product categories, highlighting both the company’s manufacturing prowess and establishing it as a leader in what it’s calling, “physical AI.”

And, while the company brought along a few examples of the Z01 and Z03 humanoid robots that are currently building a new equipment asset every six minutes, the real headliner was the cabless Zoomlion excavator robot shown, above.

Zoomlion says its proprietary RobotOps embodied AI operating system built around a vision-language-action (VLA) model that uses a comprehensive suite of sensors to give it “a 540-degree view” of the world around it.

Basically marketing-speak for, “better than just 360 degree camera views,” the sensors helps the Zoomlion machine correctly identify objects in a variety of conditions, determining their positions, orientations, and dimensions, then using the company’s Zbrain platform to translate that information into physical machine movements – and those movements are “just” being calculated for the excavator.

That 540-degree 3D vision system gives the autonomous excavator a much more complete picture of its surroundings, using multiple sensors to identify obstacles, understand distances, and navigate a constantly changing worksite.

The company says its intelligent Zbrain platform has been trained on millions of hours of operational data and tens of thousands of real-world scenarios, and allows Zoomlion’s autonomous machines to interpret natural-language job instructions, autonomously deconstructing complex tasks into simple step-by-step workflows before scanning the surrounding terrain, classifying the materials, and determining the right digging and gripping paths to deliver fully automate excavation, loading, slope dressing, brick paving, or material handling.

The bipedal humanoid Z01 robots will be at the show to guide visitors through the sprawling Zoomlion display, and will reportedly be using facial and gesture recognition to identify and remember individuals, while executing force-compliant motion planning and real-time obstacle avoidance to perform interactive gestures such as fist bumps and waves.
