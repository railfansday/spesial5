LayerZero locks early ZRO deposits behind validators users cannot choose

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

LayerZero’s launch validators will share the voting power of early ZRO deposits, with withdrawals locked until 2027.

LayerZero named Kakao Pay and ZAN among the first validators for its Zero blockchain on Nov. 25 and said Four Pillars would run validator operations on behalf of Samsung Card.

The operators are slated to help confirm blocks when Zero opens to the public, a launch planned for Nov. 30.

The appointments show who will exercise some of the voting weight tied to early deposits of ZRO, the token that backs validator votes on Zero.

Under LayerZero’s staking terms, the portal automatically spreads early stake equally across the initial validators. Holders have no say in which validators receive it and no way to withdraw early.

LayerZero says its launch validators will span Asia, Europe and North America. Its Nov. 25 announcement names the Asia-Pacific participants and identifies Four Pillars as the operator for Samsung Card.

Validators check proofs that transactions were processed correctly and vote on blocks. A validator’s vote carries more weight when more ZRO is staked behind it, according to LayerZero’s staking guide.

The project’s February technical paper describes a design in which operators can validate without putting up their own tokens, and the network selects its consensus committee in proportion to stake.

LayerZero’s staking terms define genesis as the private mainnet launch, which the terms expected on Nov. 23, and that was also the date LayerZero gave for the opening of early staking. For public mainnet, the company says staked ZRO will be visible in Zero Explorer once the network goes live.

On Nov. 24, LayerZero said the LayerZero Foundation had completed 100 million ZRO in pre-genesis staking in two transactions of 50 million ZRO each.

The portal’s rewards calculator shows 300 million ZRO in an illustrative scenario that assumes 30% participation.

A holder who disagrees with a validator during the lock has no option in the portal to redirect the stake or exit the position, and the lock covers the staked tokens and any rewards.

LayerZero expects unstaking to open by March 31, 2027, and says that date may change. Once unstaking opens, it can take up to seven days, so moving ZRO off Zero to another chain may require a later upgrade.

LayerZero describes a 1% annual issuance target as the basis for the reward pool, and the terms set an initial validator commission of 10% on rewards. A holder’s share depends on how much ZRO they stake, how long they stake it, and how much others commit.

Any rewards compound into the locked position, and the staking guide says validators will set their own commission after the initial period.

Until unstaking opens, stakers can track their balances, but the selected validator set exercises the consensus votes backed by those deposits. The public launch is the next chance to see which of the announced validators come online.
