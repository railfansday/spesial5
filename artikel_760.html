IDA Documentary Awards Nominations: ‘Concrete Land,’ ‘To Hold a Mountain,’ ‘You Can See Everything’ Among Top Nominees

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Other films up for multiple prizes include 'Rehearsals for a Revolution' and 'A Fox Under a Pink Moon.'

The International Documentary Association has revealed its nominees for the 2026 IDA Documentary Awards.

Concrete Land is the top nominee, up for six awards, including best feature documentary, followed by fellow best feature nominees To Hold a Mountain with five nods and A Fox Under a Pink Moon, Rehearsals for a Revolution and You Can See Everything with four nods each.

“It’s a challenging time for documentary makers, but also a really inspiring moment for IDA and for the field,” IDA board president Ina Fichman said in part in a statement. “This year we see nominees whose deeply personal stories share narratives of resistance and exile, and we see artful, innovative filmmaking that is capturing new and robust audiences. This year, IDA received 650 entries from 95 countries, an increase from last year in the number of entries and countries represented. Thirty two countries are represented among the nominees. IDA members will vote for the winners in the best feature and short documentary through Nov. 29. All other category winners are selected by juries representing the international documentary community.

The 42nd annual IDA Documentary Awards are set to take place Dec. 4 at The Ebell of Los Angeles and will be hosted for the second year in a row by Tabitha Jackson.
