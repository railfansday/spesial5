Dancing With the Stars Eliminates Frontrunner Before Semi-Finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Dancing With the Stars' Jenna Dewan and pro Val Chmerkovskiy became the last season 35 couple to be eliminated before the semi-finals on week nine's Grammy Night.

A frontrunner has fallen out of the competition on Dancing With the Stars.

Jenna Dewan's Nov. 10 performance with pro Val Chmerkovskiy became her last on the show—despite receiving the second-highest scores from the judges on Grammy Night.

For the evening featuring winning hits from music's biggest night, the duo teamed up for a Jazz routine to Janet Jackson’s "All for You," earning 28 out of 30 points. They also won their dance battle against Tatyana Ali and pro Jan Ravnik, which earned Dewan an extra two points.

After revealing the eliminated pair who will not move on to the Nov. 17 semi-finals, cohost Julianne Hough was nearly speechless, saying, "You've made it this far—it's shocking, to say the least."

But even after a controversial season—including a weeklong suspension for Chmerkovskiy over comments to judge Carrie Ann Inaba—Dewan shared how she is taking away positive memories.

"Dancing again, getting to share my joy with my family, who's over here every single night—thank you, my baby—I had a great time," she said, referring to fiancé Steve Kazee. "And I loved getting to share that with the world. Honestly, all love."

Meanwhile, other frontrunners—Paralympian Ezra Frech and pro Daniella Karagach—took the audience back to 2003 with an Argentine tango set to Beyoncé's "Crazy in Love," a single that contributed two Grammys to the artist's record as the award show's all-time champion.

Frech and Karagach's moves were rivaled by Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson's salsa, as the Grey's Anatomy alum spun his partner around the dance floor to Lady Gaga and Bruno Mars' romantic duet, "Die With a Smile."

It was the final showdown in the ballroom before season 35 headed to the semi-finals. The momentous year has previously said goodbye to Summer House star Ciara Miller, The Bachelor's Tyler Cameron and most recently, Julia Stiles.

"It's been an incredible experience. I pulled things out of myself that I didn't realize were there," the Save The Last Dance star said through tears while standing next to partner Ezra Sosa. "Ezra is just an amazing person. I'm going to be sad not to see him every day."

Yet the Prince & Me star still walked away from the show with an incredible memento: matching ink with her ballroom bestie.

In a tattoo tour shared to TikTok after their Nov. 2 departure, Sosa explained they added eight miniature stars on their wrists, symbolizing "one star for every time we got to dance."
