Jessica Biel, Justin Timberlake Share Rare Look Into 14-Year Marriage

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In Justin Timberlake’s upcoming Apple TV documentary Timberlake, his wife of 14 years Jessica Biel shared some rare insight into their romance.

Jessica Biel is holding up a mirror to her romance with Justin Timberlake.

In the trailer for the *NSYNC alum’s new Apple TV documentary Timberlake, the 7th Heaven actress shared rare insight into their relationship.

“When I met him,” Biel—who first started dating Timberlake in 2007—explained in the new documentary trailer released Nov. 23, “he was just not what I thought at all.”

And while Biel and Timberlake—who share kids Silas, 11, and Phineas, 6—are notoriously private about their relationship, the Matchbox: The Movie actress admitted in a separate clip from the doc, out Dec. 11, “I think we gotta address some things.”

Footage in the documentary appears to confirm that Biel was referring to some of Timberlake’s public career blunders, including his 2024 DUI arrest amid his The Forget Tomorrow World Tour. (At the time, Timberlake took a plea deal, pleading guilty to a non-criminal traffic violation of driving while impaired and agreed to pay a minimal fine in addition to 40 hours of community service.)

Meanwhile, Timberlake owned up to his past mistakes, highlighting that he can’t stop the feeling of gratitude for his nearly 15-year marriage with Biel.

“She’s the best thing that ever happened to me,” Timberlake said. “That’s one thing that I did right.”

And the Grammy winner previously shared that Biel proved she was his mirror from the first moment he laid eyes on her.

"When I met Jess, it was undeniable how beautiful she is," the musician wrote in his 2018 memoir Hindsight & All the Things I Can't See in Front of Me. "I made some sort of sarcastic comment, really dry. Nobody got it except her. She laughed, and I noticed, all of a sudden, and in the way where you wonder if a person's like you, if they have a very dry, dark sense of humor, too.”

Although the pair had a winding road of romance ahead of them—they dated from 2007 to 2011 before splitting briefly and went on to tie the knot in 2012—Timberlake thought even then that when it came to Biel’s choice for a husband, “It’s gonna be me.”

“Thoughts kept leaping into my mind: I'm kind of really interested in that girl,” he mused. “There was just something about that girl.”
