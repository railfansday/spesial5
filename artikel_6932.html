Job Title: Credential Compliance Specialist Salary: Classified Salary Schedule, Range 1 Reports to: Director of Accountability, Senior CALPADS Manager FLSA Status: Exempt

The Credential Compliance Specialist serves as the primary resource for credential monitoring, assignment compliance, and educator authorization requirements for the California Virtual Academies. This position ensures certificated employees are appropriately credentialed, assignments align with California credentialing requirements, and organizational practices remain compliant with Commission on Teacher Credentialing (CTC), California Statewide Assignment Accountability System (CalSAAS), and other applicable state requirements.

The Credential Compliance Specialist collaborates closely with Accountability, Human Resources, Talent Acquisition, school leadership, and certificated staff to support credential compliance, assignment monitoring, regulatory reporting, and continuous process improvement.

Serves as the school's primary resource for credential compliance, assignment monitoring, and educator authorization requirements.

Supports credentialing compliance across all CAVA schools.

Collaborates with Accountability, Human Resources, Talent Acquisition, and school leadership regarding credential and assignment matters.

Maintains compliance records, reporting systems, and audit-ready documentation.

Supports organizational compliance with Commission on Teacher Credentialing (CTC), CalSAAS, and related state requirements.

Assists leadership in identifying and resolving credentialing and assignment compliance concerns.

Contributes to organizational audit readiness and reporting accuracy.

Monitor and verify teaching credentials, permits, authorizations, waivers, and certifications for certificated employees.

Track credential expiration dates, renewal requirements, and compliance deadlines.

Monitor changes in credentialing laws, regulations, and Commission on Teacher Credentialing requirements.

Communicate credentialing updates, deadlines, and compliance requirements to employees and administrators.

Maintain credential records and supporting documentation within established systems.

Review certificated assignments to ensure alignment between credentials, authorizations, and assigned duties.

Collaborate with Human Resources and school administrators to verify assignment eligibility and proper authorization.

Support administrators in resolving credentialing and assignment discrepancies.

Collaborate with the CALPADS Administrator to validate certificated staff, credential, course and assignment information used in state reporting.

Support CalSAAS assignment monitoring by researching potential misassignments, gathering documentation and coordinating resolution efforts with appropriate staff.

Assist with preparing credential and assignment compliance documentation while coordinating with the CALPADs Administrator.

Serve as a primary point of contact for credential-related questions from certificated staff and administrators.

Provide guidance regarding authorization requirements, credential pathways, and assignment compliance.

Support Talent Acquisition and Human Resources during hiring and onboarding of certificated staff.

Assist administrators with substitute placement reviews, leave-of-absence coverage, and assignment planning.

Develop and maintain positive working relationships with internal and external stakeholders.

Maintain organized, accurate, and audit-ready credential records.

Prepare reports related to credential status, assignment compliance, and regulatory requirements.

Provide periodic credential compliance updates to leadership, highlighting upcoming deadlines and areas requiring administrative attention.

Develop and maintain a centralized credential compliance tracking system to monitor credential status, expiration dates, authorizations, induction requirements and potential compliance concerns.

Ensure required records are maintained in accordance with organizational standards and applicable regulations.

Support Accountability Department initiatives related to credentialing, compliance, and state reporting.

Maintain accurate information in credentialing, reporting, and accountability systems.

Assist with data validation, compliance reviews, and reporting activities.

Develop and maintain procedures, tracking tools, and process documentation.

Identify opportunities to improve credentialing workflows, compliance processes, and organizational efficiency.

Associate degree or higher in Education, Human Resources, Public Administration, Business Administration, or a related field preferred.

Knowledge of California credentialing requirements and CTC resources preferred; willingness and ability to learn credentialing regulations required.

Strong understanding of compliance processes, documentation requirements, and regulatory standards.

Strong organizational, analytical, and problem-solving skills.

Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

Ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines with a high degree of accuracy.

Proficiency with Microsoft Excel, Google Workspace, databases, and reporting systems.

Ability to work independently, take initiative, and collaborate effectively across departments.

Ability to maintain confidentiality and handle sensitive information appropriately.
