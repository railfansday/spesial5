Pat Freiermuth says Steelers still believe in DK Metcalf

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

DK Metcalf hasn't quite lived up to expectations during his time in Pittsburgh, but that hasn't shaken the confidence of his teammates. Pat Freiermuth opened up to the media today about the embattled wideout's time in the Steel City.

"Obviously he's had some struggles but if you look at his effort on the run game or downfield blocking, he doesn't let his situation impact his ability to get other guys open," said Freiermuth on Thursday afternoon.

"I think that's something that you can appreciate as a teammate and as a guy you can tell a lot about his character. You know, we're with him until the end. He's going to get this figured out. We've got a lot of trust in DK and a lot of belief in him. We paid him for a reason, and the Steelers have backed him for a reason."

Metcalf's struggles have been a combination of costly drops, difficulty creating separation against physical coverage and inconsistency in the Steelers' passing game. Opposing defenses have increasingly focused on limiting his opportunities, while Pittsburgh has leaned heavily on its running game.

The Steelers acquired Metcalf to be their No. 1 receiving threat, and his physical tools and big-play ability remain undeniable. He previously established himself as one of the NFL's more productive receivers in Seattle, recording 6,324 receiving yards and 48 touchdowns from 2019-2024.

While the production hasn't consistently followed him to Pittsburgh, Freiermuth's confidence suggests the locker room still believes Metcalf can turn things around. The challenge now is translating his talent into more consistent results on the field.
