What To Watch For: Pitt Panthers

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

PITT WILL BE EVEN MORE AGGRESSIVE THAN NORMAL ON DEFENSE

Pitt has the best run defense in the country, but I expect them to turn up the dial this weekend in how they defend the run. Pitt will come into this game fully aware that Louisville has struggled with allowing tackles for loss this year. They will also be aware of the fact that they have been much better in this area throughout their five-game winning streak. UofL is ranked 71st in tackles for loss allowed after being in the 130s a few weeks ago.

The thing about the Cards being so bad when it comes to negative plays is that they still lead the country in yards per play. However, the Cards haven't been overly explosive over the last few games. They've just been more consistent. Pitt will look to force those negative plays, because that's what has been the key to the wins for the Cards. The Panthers will be bringing linebackers more often to force those negative plays this weekend.

LOUISVILLE SHOULD LOOK TO LAYER ROUTES FOR RECEIVERS

Teams haven't had a ton of success passing the ball against Pitt this year, but the two teams that did worked hard to get their most dynamic receivers into space down the field. The game that really stood out to me was the Virginia Tech game where Que'Sean Brown went off for 147 yards. Tech did a good job of running off coverage on the outside, with Brown running underneath on shorter routes in the voided space.

I would love to see Brian and Jeff Brohm work in some of the deep crossing routes we've seen from this offense over the last few years. Pitt has diversified their defensive calls over the years, but when it comes down to it, they want to run quarters, which opens them up for a receiver leaking into open space when a receiver runs off that coverage. I think the Cards can take advantage of this on Saturday night.

LINCOLN KEINHOLZ'S LEGS WILL BE A BIG FACTOR

As I pointed out above, Pitt is going to really get after the Louisville running game in an effort to pile up negative plays. That will make it that much more important that Lincoln Keinholz makes plays with his legs. He has shown this year that he has a unique ability to extend plays outside of the pocket when he gets pressure. He has also been very good at finding receivers down the field in those situations. Those types of plays will be important in this game.

What will be more important, in my opinion, is his ability to get chunk gains on option runs. He has been contained, for the most part, this season on plays where he has to make a read and then get up the field. That has to improve in this game. I don't see Pitt giving up some of the longer runs he's had on QB Sweeps because of their team speed and discipline. But give me Linc on some inverted veer plays with Isaac Brown running towards the boundary. I think things could open up for him.

THE GAMEPLAN SHOULD BE THE SAME AS LAST WEEK FOR LOUISVILLE'S DEFENSE

Louisville held UNC to 293 yards, 5 yards per play, and one play of more than 18 yards. Just, like, do that again?
