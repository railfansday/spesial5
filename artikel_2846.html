Who’s the Detroit News Girls High School Team of the Week? Vote now!

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Every week, Michigan high school teams across the state are winning championships, breaking records, reaching milestones and putting together seasons worth celebrating.

The Detroit News is recognizing those standout squads as Team of the Week. From nominations submitted by coaches, parents and fans, we've selected five girls teams as this week's finalists.

Any girls team, in any sport and from any region of Michigan, is eligible. Now it's your turn to decide who deserves the honor.

Voting is open from 10 a.m. Monday through 5 p.m. Thursday. Readers can vote multiple times, so rally your school community and get everyone involved.

The winner will be announced Friday morning in a special Detroit News post celebrating its accomplishments.

Who deserves to be named the Detroit News Girls Team of the Week? Cast your vote below.

Ann Arbor Pioneer didn't just win its second consecutive Division 1 girls cross country state championship Saturday at Michigan International Speedway. It swept the top three individual spots, too.

Pioneer finished with 87 points, led by junior Natasza Dudek, who captured her second straight individual state title in 16:26.6. That's the second-fastest time in state finals history.

Sophomore Keira Von Blon finished runner-up in 16:49.8, while junior Sienna Klemmer took third in 16:54.7. Their times rank fifth and seventh, respectively, on the all-time state finals list.

It was a historic day for Pioneer, which continues to set the standard in Division 1.

Otsego finally got its championship moment.

After finishing runner-up in 2023 and 2025, Otsego captured the Division 2 girls cross country state championship Saturday at Michigan International Speedway with 84 points. No girls team scored better at the state finals.

Sophomore twin sisters Alayna and Adelyn Verhage led the way, finishing 13th and 26th, respectively. Both recorded personal-best times on the biggest stage of the season.

Sophomore Addelyn Sabatke added another top-30 finish, placing 28th in 18:34.1.

Otsego had three All-State runners finish in the top 30, securing the program's fourth state championship.

Ann Arbor Pioneer has made winning state championships look routine.

Pioneer finished 18-3 and captured its second consecutive Michigan High School Athletic Association field hockey state championship, continuing a remarkable run under coach Jane Nixon.

The team didn't lose to a Michigan opponent all season. All three losses came against out-of-state competition. And when the postseason arrived, Pioneer was nearly untouchable, shutting out opponents in all five playoff games while outscoring them 20-0.

That included a 1-0 victory over Saline in the state championship game Oct. 31 at Michigan State's Ralph Young Field.

Pioneer has now won the last five Michigan state field hockey titles, including three when the sport was still played at the club level. Nixon has guided the program to 20 state championships since 1998.

Northville spent most of the season ranked No. 1 in Michigan. The team made sure there was no debate when it came time to crown a state champion.

Northville captured its third consecutive Division 1 girls golf state championship, finishing 18 strokes ahead of Macomb Dakota. It was the program's seventh state title in the past nine seasons.

Cam Baker, an MIGCA Super Team selection, led the way and also won the individual regional championship. Teammates Naaz Gill and McKenzie Stevens earned MIGCA First Team honors, while Kate Lis received honorable mention recognition. That gave Northville four all-state selections in its five-player lineup.

The team also won the KLAA championship, adding another trophy to an already impressive season.

Co-head coaches Kate Schultz and Trish Murray have overseen all three consecutive state titles, continuing one of Michigan's most successful girls golf programs.

Grand Rapids Forest Hills Central didn't leave much doubt about who belonged at the top of Division 2 girls golf.

The team captured the state championship with a 45-stroke victory over runner-up South Lyon after dominating regionals by 51 strokes.

Junior Alivia Offer added an individual state championship to the team's trophy haul after also winning the regional title. She earned MIGCA Super Team honors, while Caroline VanderVelde, Camille Tierney and Abigail Offer made the MIGCA First Team. Glenna Rinckey received honorable mention recognition.

Forest Hills Central was ranked No. 1 in Division 2 in every weekly state ranking this season. The team backed up that billing with a dominant postseason run.

The championship also made for a special family celebration. Head coach Ann Offer is the mother of Alivia and Abigail, giving the state title an extra personal touch.
