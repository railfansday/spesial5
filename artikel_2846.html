Player Grades: Cavs at Knicks – Another fourth quarter implosion

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Cleveland Cavaliers took another painful loss to the New York Knicks after going off the rails in the fourth quarter.

Grades are based on our usual expectations for each player.

30 points, 4 rebounds, 4 assists, 4 steals, 2 blocks, 2 turnovers

Mitchell was lights out in the first half, storming downhill and busting New York's point-of-attack defense. The Knicks had no answer for him as Mitchell began the game 9-for-10 from inside of two-point range.

His defensive activity was strong, as well, with Mitchell racking up stocks throughout the game.

The fourth quarter was less efficient. Mitchell's drives became erratic, including one play where he was lost in the trees twice in the same possession. There's value in putting pressure on the rim, but Mitchell has to do a better job of moving away from pressure when the time calls for it.

It was hard to choose between Mitchell and Harden for Player of the Game. Mitchell's defense tipped the scales.

28 points, 5 assists, 1 rebound, 4 turnovers

Good things happen when Harden touches the paint. He's looked more spry and youthful in recent games, dancing with his dribble to work into the restricted area and create from there. Whether it was shooting a floater, kicking to the perimeter, or dropping it into Jarrett Allen's lap — Harden put on a clinic on how to drive the ball.

Unfortunately, Harden shot 1-of-8 from downtown and wasn't able to sprint through the finish line as everything went wrong in the fourth quarter.

10 points, 5 rebounds, 1 assist, 2 steals

Allen was able to roam freely on defense while Harden accepted the challenge of guarding Karl-Anthony Towns on the perimeter. This strategy worked throughout the night as Cleveland was able to safely navigate New York's off-ball actions with Allen standing in the paint to clean up any mistakes. It wasn't until the wheels came off in the fourth quarter, when the Knicks were able to sprint in transition and break the mundanity of the half-court, that Allen became nonconsequential.

8 points, 5 rebounds, 2 assists, 2 steals, 2 blocks

It feels like I'm trying to figure out what to expect from Watson. He hasn't been featured enough in the offense for me to know where he fits. Watson only attempted four shots tonight and didn't spend much time with the ball in his hands.

Proctor had a rough shooting night (1-for-6) but was able to bolster the Cavaliers' defense by being aggressive away from the ball. Proctor does a good job of using his body and bumping guys who try to cut around him. The Cavs could use more of that mentality.

Sometimes, Thomas plays basketball like an adrenaline junkie. He bursts around the court and tries to get involved in every defensive play. That's helped him cause a handful of turnovers, like when he jump-scared Josh Hart, forcing him to lose the ball out of bounds in the second half.

This was not a standout game for Hezonja. He only attempted one shot in 10 minutes and finished as a minus-14.

Merrill hasn't had it going this season. He shot 1-of-7 from downtown tonight and is shooting 32% from deep this season.

6 points, 6 rebounds, 4 assists, 4 turnovers

Tyson is in a funk. It's that simple. He hasn't looked as confident or spry as he was last season. The Cavs will have to figure out how to correct this if they want to reach their ceiling this season. Tyson is too important as a potential sixth man for this to continue.

His last shot attempt was swatted into the stands.

Bryant gave the Cavs good minutes tonight by boxing out and cleaning the glass. I'm glad we were able to end this on a positive note.
