How the Knicks can advance in the NBA Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

After the Knicks' valiant comeback effort at Gainbridge Fieldhouse came up empty on November 13, they fell to 6-6 on the season and 0-2 in NBA Cup play. After an incredible run the year prior to claim the organization's first trophy of any kind in 27 years (foreshadowing), they seemed fated to become the third consecutive NBA Cup champion to be bounced in the group stage.

Yet, one six-game winning streak and an incredible fourth quarter in a Cup game against the Cavs later, and the Knicks are mathematically alive heading into Black Friday against Giannis Antetokounmpo and the red-hot Miami Heat.

Now, after winning the Treble last year, experiencing the fatigue from last year's Cup run that led to the 2-9 stretch from hell, and, you know, defending a real championship, a tournament that already had limited interest from Knicks fans seems to have the opposite intended effect, to the point where it might be beneficial to miss the knockout round altogether.

But the Knicks are currently the only team to have never been grouped in Cup play, and since these games count toward the team's 82-game record, there's plenty to play for as the Knicks look to defend the first of their three trophies.

Here's a look at the current standings in East Group B and the East's wild card, both of which are still in play:

The simplest path is to win the group, which simply requires two things:

Knicks to beat the Heat by 7 points in regulation

What helps the Knicks here is their point differential. They lost the games to Philly and Indiana by five and two points respectively, which allowed their 19-point win over Cleveland to put them in a decent spot.

Winning an NBA game by seven is the easy part; relying on an injured and struggling Pacers team to defeat the No. 1 seed in the East is another. Now, the Sixers look like a paper tiger with their clutch reliance (their Cup PD is negative because of this), but this reads like a game that would be a blowout.

The current injury report has Jaylen Brown out with hamstring management and Joel Embiid questionable with an illness for the Sixers, while Tyrese Haliburton, Ben Sheppard, and Johnny Furphy are still out for Indiana. TJ McConnell, Larry Nance Jr., and Kelly Oubre Jr. are questionable.

If the Sixers win, they clinch Group B. If they lose, the Knicks will get a shot at being the first 2-loss NBA Cup group winner in an unlikely five-way tie.

In the event of a Sixers win, things get a lot more complicated.

First of all, the Knicks would need there to be zero 3-1 non-group winners for obvious reasons. That means they'll need losses from the Hawks, Pistons, and Raptors. Toronto already has a lower point differential, so margin wouldn't matter there.

Margin would matter with the other two. The Hawks are at +21 and the Pistons are at +31, so the Knicks are going to need to win and win by a lot to guarantee they get in.

The margin of victory would likely not need to be that big, as the more the Pistons and Hawks lose by, the more it knocks that down. The absolute minimum the Knicks would have to win by is seven to pass the Heat in point differential, but to only have to win by that much, they'd need the Bucks to win by 13 and the Celtics to win by 3.

Which brings the difficulty aspect. You're asking two of the worst teams in the league to win games against significantly better teams, one of which is a matchup between a 2-win Bucks team and the reigning No. 1 seed. That's probably not happening.

Also, there is the spoiler aspect that could theoretically damage both of these scenarios. If the Pacers (-27 PD) or Nets (-11 PD) win by such ridiculous margins, they would have a chance to steal a spot in the knockout round.

Now, personally, a Hali-less Pacers team winning by damn near 50 or the Nets beating the Raptors by 30+ would be pretty stunning, but we're already asking for stunning things to happen to get this far.

In layman's terms, a fourth consecutive trip to the knockout round is still unlikely, but possible.

It'll make for a fun night of scoreboard watching, that's for sure.
