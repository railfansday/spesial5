Stock Market Midday, Nov. 23: Nasdaq Surges Nearly 7% as Jensen Huang's Return Sparks Historic AI Rebound

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Wall Street is staging a comeback following last week's brutal sell-off, with semiconductor stocks helping major indexes rise sharply.

By RJ Fulton – Nov 23, 2026 at 12:12PM EST

Wall Street staged a strong rebound on Monday, November 23, as investors returned to equities following last week's steep sell-off. Renewed enthusiasm for artificial intelligence (AI) stocks, fueled by Jensen Huang's reinstatement as Nvidia's (NVDA +32.42%) CEO, helped propel major indexes sharply higher, reversing some of the losses suffered during the recent market downturn.

As of 11:31 a.m. ET, the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +6.78%) had surged 6.79% to 24,152, while the S&P 500 (^GSPC +4.92%) jumped 5.00% to 7,256. The Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI +3.04%) gained 3.26% to 49,715, reflecting a broad recovery in investor sentiment.

The rebound extended beyond equities, with gold climbing 4.40% to $3,670. Meanwhile, the 10-year Treasury yield declined 12 basis points to 4.92%, providing some relief to investors concerned about elevated borrowing costs.

Nvidia led Monday's gains, surging 32.05% following the board's decision to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO. The announcement fueled a broader rally across AI-related stocks, with Micron Technology (NASDAQ: MU) climbing 19.50% amid renewed investor enthusiasm for semiconductor companies.

Meanwhile, Zscaler (ZS +15.18%) jumped 14.62% following the announcement that CrowdStrike Holdings (CRWD -10.72%) had agreed to acquire the cybersecurity company in a $43.9 billion deal. However, CrowdStrike shares declined 11.00% as investors weighed the financial implications of the acquisition.

Monday's sharp rebound provides some relief following last week's sell-off, but investors should distinguish between a recovery driven by improving fundamentals and one fueled by the reversal of excessive pessimism. Jensen Huang's reinstatement removes a significant source of uncertainty for Nvidia, although the broader semiconductor industry still faces questions surrounding AI valuations, regulatory restrictions, and the sustainability of infrastructure spending.

Looking ahead, Wednesday's personal consumption expenditures (PCE) inflation report could prove particularly influential. With the 10-year Treasury yield still near 5%, persistently elevated inflation could reinforce expectations that interest rates will remain restrictive, putting renewed pressure on equity valuations. For Monday's rally to develop into a more sustainable recovery, investors should look for broader market participation, stabilizing earnings expectations, and continued improvement in financial conditions.
