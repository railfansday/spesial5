4 predictions for Bills vs. Chiefs on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Is there anything bold to predict about a powerhouse matchup between the Buffalo Bills and Kansas City Chiefs? Sure, given enough imagination and luck. Bills-Chiefs on Thanksgiving night is truly must-see TV.

You can bet that most of the country will be tuned in this evening to see what quarterbacks Josh Allen and Patrick Mahomes have in store for Round 11. Through 10 games (includes playoffs), Allen and Mahomes are each 5-5. The key difference between them is that Allen has gone 4-1 in the regular season, while Mahomes has gone 4-0 in the postseason.

That said, this is a regular season with huge stakes. Buffalo is currently 9-1 and in first place in the AFC, but this game's winner takes the driver's seat over the loser in the chase for the one seed come playoff time. Well that is, unless they finish in a tie.

As you might expect, there are a lot of similarities between the Bills and Chiefs. Beyond the well-known star players, however, the rosters are a bit different. Where they run parallel is at quarterback — thanks to the two best players of this generation. Buffalo and Kansas City also boast nasty rushing attacks, and the Bills have the league's top-ranked unit behind lead running back James Cook III.

These are two teams that like to bleed clock, but can strike 80 yards or more down the field for six in a moment's notice. Tonight, the recipe might involve a bit more of the running game from both sides, at least until it's safer to attack downfield in the passing game.

Here are four bold predictions I have for the Bills on Thanksgiving…

You may have heard by now that the Bills are a run-first offense. That fact hasn't necessarily limited what Allen can accomplish as a passer, but head coach Joe Brady is intent on establishing dominance in the trenches.

Somewhere along the line, Cook must have been told that he'd be a fantastic runner between the tackles, despite his somewhat small size for the position. That, or his instincts know ball. Maybe it's both, and it might also be true that Buffalo's offensive line opens up some tasty running lanes between the tackles.

Whatever the case may be, Cook is a dangerous one-cut, take-it-to-the-house runner who, in ways, recalls Terrel Davis. Last season's rushing yardage champion has taken on an even bigger role in the Bills' offense this season, and he's currently second in rushing yards.

I predict a heavy workload for Cook tonight, with him setting a new personal best in carries. That number to beat is 32, which he set almost one year ago against the Pittsburgh Steelers. Cook has already come close to breaking that number, having totaled 31 carries against the Minnesota Vikings this season (Week 9).

It may not seem like a bold prediction, given the expected weather and Cook's role. However, if it comes to be, that may mean that the Bills thoroughly dominated the trenches and stole away the game clock. Buffalo is 14-2 in games that Cook carries the ball 20 or more times, so there's reason to expect they lean on him in bad weather.

FanDuel Sportsbook has James Cook's rushing attempts set at over 18.5 (-138)/under 18.5 (+104). I'd be shocked if he see the ball less than 20 times.

New Bills safety Amani Hooker has fit right in and taken every snap on defense since Buffalo brought him in via trade ahead of Week 10. Hooker has five interceptions on the season, which includes a hat trick against Cincinnati Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow.

The Bills may ask Hooker to spend a lot of time shadowing tight end Travis Kelce, which could mean ample opportunities to steal a ball from Mahomes. Kelce loves to ad-lib routes, and you can bet Hooker has studied his tendencies to see when and where jumping the route may lead to six the other way.

Mahomes has thrown only four interceptions this season, and none in the past four games. He's due. You can't try to cash in on his odds for a pick-six at FanDuel Sportsbook, but Amani Hooker is currently listed at +1200 to record an interception.

Try as they might, One Bills Drive can't seem to shake Shakir's role as Buffalo's most consistently productive receiver. He isn't always the one with the most yards or catches, but Shakir's efficiency is nearly unmatched.

If anything, Brady and Allen should be working to get Shakir more involved on game day. There were encouraging signs in Week 11 against the Miami Dolphins, when Shakir was involved early, even catching a 38-yard pass downfield while leading the team in receiving yards (101).

I often refer to Shakir as The Pinball Wizard thanks to his insane agility in both space and traffic. He's the best of both worlds as a shifty wide receiver with traits that rival the best running backs.

Brady probably understands that it makes sense to attack the middle of Kansas City's defense, and who better to lead that effort than Shakir? Maybe you prefer tight end Dalton Kincaid? Sure, that's fair game, if he plays through a pair of injuries.

Shakir is currently listed at +600 with FanDuel Sportsbook to finish with the most receiving yards among all players from both teams in the game. Again, with nasty weather expected, it seems reasonable to count on Allen looking to his most reliable receiver. Repeat after me: In Shakir We Trust.

It's Allen and Mahomes, and both are surgical dual-threats. You already trust that Allen's running for at least one touchdown. You might scoff at Mahomes doing it simply because the Chiefs never use him in short-yardage situations. It doesn't matter. Mahomes is probably going to end up keeping it himself for six from the red zone once this evening.
