How to watch Steelers vs. Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Pittsburgh Steelers will try to avoid losing two consecutive games for the first time this season on Sunday. To do so, they'll have to win in a place they haven't won in over 60 years as they travel to Philadelphia to face the Eagles.

The Eagles enter the week losers of seven straight following a myriad of injuries, including losing quarterback Jalen Hurts for the year due to blood clots. Pittsburgh is hoping to avoid being the team that ends the Eagles' skid, as a win of their own would put them at 7-3 and keep pace with the rest of the leaders in the AFC.

Where: Lincoln Financial Field, Philadelphia, PA
