Vega Ioane set to play for Ravens Thursday night, Jaylinn Hawkins ruled out

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Ravens are set to have right guard Vega Ioane back in the lineup on Thursday night.

Ioane missed last Sunday’s loss to the Bills with a knee injury, but he was listed as a full participant in practice for the second straight day on Wednesday and has no injury designation for the home game against the Jaguars.

Safety Jaylinn Hawkins will not be joining him in the lineup. Hawkins was ruled out with a knee injury.

Quarterbacks Lamar Jackson (thumb) and Tyler Huntley (toe) are the only other players ruled out. Both of them are destined for the injured reserve list. Undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano will start and the Ravens are finalizing a deal to acquire Jacoby Brissett from the Cardinals, although he will not join the team in time for Thursday’s game.

Cornerback Nate Wiggins (stinger, illness) is listed as questionable along with running back Adam Randall (neck). Randall will need to be activated from injured reserve to play against Jacksonville.
