Raiders vs. Browns odds: Opening lines for Week 12 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Las Vegas Raiders have lost 7 out of 8 after another loss on Sunday. It was a heartbreaker to the Denver Broncos, 38-24, with a late pick-six to seal the win for their division rival.

Now they head to Cleveland to face the Cleveland Browns. The Browns are 6-4 and looking to push for the Wild Card. The Raiders just want to get another win after their 3-0 start has gone to the wind. Hopefully Klint Kubak and his coaching staff can get the team ready

Fanduel Sportsbook posted odds for all the games of the 1oth week of the season. The Raiders are 4.5-point underdogs against the Cleveland Browns, with an over/under (O/U) of 41.5.

With teams on a bye in Week 12, plenty of NFL action remains. FanDuel lists the Jacksonville Jaguars as the biggest favorites in the upcoming games, at -13.5 over the Tennessee Titans.
