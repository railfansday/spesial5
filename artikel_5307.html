Trump says he convinced Nvidia's board to bring Jensen Huang back as CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Nvidia's board voted unanimously to reinstate Huang, citing input from senior U.S. government officials on national security

President Donald Trump said he persuaded Nvidia $NVDA's board to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO. "I convinced them," Trump told CNBC on Monday. "I made the deal."

Nvidia announced Huang's reinstatement as president, CEO and board member on Monday, effective immediately. The company said the board's vote was unanimous and came after several conversations and sit-downs with him. It also said the board heard from high-ranking U.S. government officials and is confident that bringing him back does not endanger national security. The company's statement did not mention Trump.

Trump told CNBC the board "did ask for assurance on the national security threat." "And we gave them assurances at least with everything we know, and we know a lot," he said. He added that Jensen Huang "did nothing wrong."

Trump also said he consulted Vice President JD Vance, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, according to CNN. Trump told CNBC, "Probably so, but the bigger impact was the long term," when asked if the previous week's sharp market-wide drop had influenced his lobbying.

Trump said he called Jensen Huang after the board's deal was done and asked him to "work hard." Another CNBC report put that call on Sunday.

Nvidia stock climbed upward of 30% on Monday once news of the move broke, according to CNBC.

Nvidia reinstated Huang less than two weeks after he resigned on Nov. 12 amid reports of multiple affairs. U.S. officials suspect one of the women, Alice Huang, who is not related to him, of relaying information to China's intelligence services. Board Chair Harvey C. Jones said on Nvidia's earnings call last week that directors had accepted the resignation and were not weighing a return for Jensen Huang.

In a statement, Jones said the board "believes Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers." Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress, who served as interim CEO, will continue as CFO.

Nvidia said the independent board committee examining reports that Jensen Huang was targeted by foreign intelligence operatives will see its work through to the end. Huang supports the investigation and has pledged to cooperate with it, the company said.

Jensen Huang is scheduled to give a closed-door transcribed interview to three House committees on Dec. 1, according to CNN. A congressional source told CNN the appearance was reconfirmed after the reinstatement announcement.
