How to Turn Your Shower Into a Spa-Worthy Ritual

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

This may be the ultimate way to get a break from the holiday rush

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Between gift shopping, family gatherings, and holiday parties, the season can quickly fill every moment of your schedule. An everything shower offers a chance to slow down and indulge in a little self-care, transforming an everyday rinse into a more immersive beauty ritual. Think of it as a moment to reconnect with yourself, recharge, and savor a little calm amid the holiday rush.

The right combination of sensorial fragrances and thoughtfully formulated products from Ulta Beauty can elevate an everything shower into a spa-worthy ritual. From skin-nourishing essentials to indulgent finishing touches, these picks will help you create a routine that feels uniquely your own.

An exfoliating body scrub helps buff away dead skin cells, leaving skin feeling smoother and softer. Tree Hut’s sugar-based formulas pair gentle exfoliation with shea butter and hydrating oils, all wrapped in an inviting fragrance. For a little variety, the Tree Hut Very Vanilla Trio Set features three vanilla-inspired scents: Golden Vanilla, Vanilla, and Tahitian Vanilla.

Take your shower up a notch with a body wash that feels as indulgent as it smells. Fenty Beauty’s Big Dripp’r Body Wash has a rich, velvety texture that lathers into a soft foam, while glycerin helps hydrate skin. Its Lil Miss Wonderful fragrance blends floral and gourmand notes, including candied apple and peony. Find it in the Fenty Skin Body The Driplets Mini Body Wash, Milk + Mist Set, which also includes the matching Butta Drop Hydrating Body Milk and Allover Body Mist for a coordinated scent experience.

After cleansing, a body serum adds another layer of care to your routine. The Uni 24-Hour Hydrating Body Serum is especially worth considering when winter weather leaves skin feeling rough or dry. Its lightweight formula absorbs quickly, with aloe vera and bladderwrack seaweed to help hydrate, while Australian caviar lime and kakadu plum offer exfoliating and antioxidant benefits.

Once you apply your serum, add body oil to help lock in moisture. The Osea Undaria Uplift Trio includes the brand’s bestselling Undaria Algae Body Oil, which has a velvety feel and absorbs quickly without leaving a greasy residue. Formulated with antioxidant-rich Undaria seaweed and a blend of passion fruit, açai, babassu, and rice bran oils, it helps soften skin and replenish moisture. The fresh, citrusy fragrance, with notes of grapefruit, lime, mandarin, and cypress, adds a sensory finishing touch. The set also includes a matching body wash and mood oil, so you can carry the scent through your routine.

Complete your ritual with a final touch of fragrance. The Sol de Janeiro Cheirosa Anywhere Jelly Perfume Balm & Mist Gift Set pairs perfume mists with solid balms designed to last up to 10 hours. Choose Cheirosa 62, a warm gourmand blend of pistachio, almond, and vanilla, or Cheirosa 68, a brighter, more floral composition featuring pink dragonfruit, jasmine, and sun musk. Either way, you’ll carry a little of that just-stepped-out-of-the-spa feeling with you long after your shower.
