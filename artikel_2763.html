Ravens offensive line coach chucking tablet in pouring rain sums up dreadful TNF performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Ravens offensive line coach chucking tablet in pouring rain sums up dreadful TNF performance appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Emergency Baltimore Ravens quarterback Joe Fagnano threw only 46 yards in three quarters. But the football wasn't the only thing tossed in the rain during the Thursday letdown against the Jacksonville Jaguars. Offensive line coach Dwayne Ledford showed his arm strength.

By taking it out on a tablet during this viral moment.

Ledford scolded his players for the offensive inefficiency. Fagnano not only struggled getting into a rhythm with the air attack. He took one sack as well as Jacksonville controlled the tempo throughout the night.

Fagnano averaged a dismal 2.9 yards per play in the inclement weather. Jacksonville picked him off twice on the evening as well. But he wasn't the only Raven who struggled against Jacksonville as he took over for both Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley. Plus handled the QB duties on the same day Baltimore added Jacoby Brissett via the Arizona Cardinals.

Star running back Derrick Henry couldn't find much running lanes amid this offensive line play. Henry settled for 55 yards through three quarters but averaged just 3.4 yards per carry. Wide receiver Zay Flowers even handled some carries too, but hit 17 yards on three carries. Baltimore as a whole only hit 85 rushing yards entering the fourth quarter.

The visiting Jaguars dominated across the line of scrimmage too, hitting three tackles for a loss. Baltimore matched the same output but again, the offensive line struggles rose as the story of this TNF showdown.

Jacksonville turned to TNF to show it belongs in the conversation for one of the AFC's best teams. They showed just that even if it meant Baltimore taking it out on a tablet.
