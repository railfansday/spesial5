Micah Parsons has limited practice Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Packers edge rusher Micah Parsons had limited work in Wednesday’s practice. He is listed with an illness in addition to the knee injury.

Parsons is in the second week of his 21-day return-to-practice window as he works his way back from the torn ACL that ended his 2025 season in December. He was limited in all three practices last week.

The plan is for him to get team work this week.

The Packers practiced without safety Javon Bullard (ankle/knee), linebacker Zaire Franklin (shin), offensive lineman Sean Rhyan (ankle), edge rusher Lukas Van Ness (back) and tight end Josh Whyle (concussion).

Offensive guard Aaron Banks (knee/hip), running back Chris Brooks (ankle), offensive lineman Travis Glover (knee), linebacker Ty’Ron Hopper (illness), wide receiver Skyy Moore (ankle), kicker Trey Smack (right calf), wide receiver Savion Williams (groin) and offensive guard Kevin Zeitler (ankle) were limited.
