🚨 Breaking: Las Palmas-Eldense postponed due to severe weather

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The match between UD Las Palmas and CD Eldense, part of matchday 13 of LaLiga Hypermotion, has been officially postponed due to the severe weather forecast for Gran Canaria.

The arrival of Storm Bruna prompted the island council to issue an alert for strong wind gusts and heavy rainfall, ordering the suspension of all outdoor sporting activities.

In light of this warning, LaLiga and both clubs agreed to call off the fixture to safeguard fans, players and staff at the Estadio de Gran Canaria.

The Alicante-based side will have to rearrange its travel plans to the archipelago while waiting for the storm to subside and normal conditions to return.

The Competition Committee will set a new date for the match in the coming days, while island authorities urge the public to exercise extreme caution.
