Chiefs News: Nikko Remigio signs with the Texans

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Kansas City Chiefs cut their starting punt returner of three years, Nikko Remigio, earlier this week. He posted a thoughtful goodbye message to Chiefs Kingdom on Instagram, ending with a note that he is signing with the Houston Texans' active roster.

On Thursday, special teams coordinator Dave Toub told local media that he and Remigio shared a tearful goodbye and said Remigio was like a son to him.

Texans Sign WR/KR Nikko Remigio | Pro Football Rumors

The Texans have signed wide receiver/returner Nikko Remigio, Aaron Wilson of KPRC 2 reports. Remigio will join the Texans' 53-man roster.

After going undrafted out of Fresno State in 2023, Remigio signed with the Chiefs and went on to spend his first three-plus seasons in the organization. Even though Remigio had been the Chiefs' primary returner since 2024, they cut him on Wednesday to open up a roster spot for newly acquired receiver Josh Downs.

Kansas City Chiefs Tap Clark-AECOM JV for New Stadium | ENR

The National Football League's Kansas City Chiefs franchise has named a joint venture of Clark Construction Group and AECOM as construction team for its planned stadium in Wyandotte County, Kan.

The team in July selected MANICA as lead architect, HNTB as architect of record and CAA ICON as owner's representative for the $3-billion enclosed stadium. The 70,000-seat venue is envisioned as the anchor of a mixed-use development and entertainment destination.

"As we finish the overall design, Clark | AECOM will help us advance the project further by identifying more certainty around project scope, budget and scheduling," said Chiefs President Mark Donovan in a news release.
