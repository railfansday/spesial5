Broncos activated TE Caleb Lohner from the IR

According to 9NEWS Denver's Mike Klis, the Denver Broncos have activated tight end Caleb Lohner from the injured reserve. Lohner will be at practice today, and this opens his 21-day practice window, during which the Broncos have to decide whether or not to add him to the active roster or keep him on the injured reserve for the remainder of the year.
