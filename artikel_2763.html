Live Discussion: Charlotte Hornets at Brooklyn Nets, 7:30 PM ET

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Nets finally have a win. Now comes the next step: getting one at home.

Brooklyn returns to Barclays Center on Wednesday night following a loss to the Knicks at Madison Square Garden. They had a chance to steal this game, but they collapsed in the fourth quarter and suffered a 15th consecutive defeat against the Knicks. Ouch.

At 1-6, Brooklyn isn't exactly where it wants to be in the standings, which is currently last in the Eastern Conference. Still, the Hornets have dropped four straight — including a 33-point loss to Detroit on Monday.

The Nets have lost three straight at Barclays Center, including their first meeting with Charlotte, a 110-103 defeat. They'll get another crack at the Hornets tonight, this time with the home crowd behind them.
