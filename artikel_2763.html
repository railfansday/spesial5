‘We Squashed It’: Hamilton Denies Demands as Leclerc Calls Ferrari Saga Closed

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Lewis Hamilton says there were no demands from either side in his talks with Charles Leclerc, as both Ferrari drivers try to move on from their Mexico clash.

The Ferrari saga should now come to an end after the latest comments from Hamilton and Leclerc, following their meeting on Thursday. Team boss Frederic Vasseur reminded both that the F1 team should always come first at all costs.

There are still no set rules of engagement, as he feels it is self-explanatory that the two teammates have to be careful if they are on track together. In the meantime, Italian media reported demands from Hamilton's side.

The reports said Hamilton wanted preference for the rest of the 2026 F1 season since he is ahead of Leclerc in the championship. But the Brit denied any such demand, saying he and Leclerc had talked it through.

"Me and Charles sat and spoke, and we squashed it, and we're moving on," Hamilton told Sky Sports F1. "We still have a huge amount of respect for each other, and I'm glad we had a chat, and now we move on.

"We've been getting along just fine as normal. Sometimes, in those scenarios, it takes having a conversation, and there's been no demands from either side; it's both just giving our points and areas we can do better and support each other. I'm hoping to have better races moving forward."

Lewis Hamilton in qualifying at Interlagos. Image Credit: Scuderia Ferrari

Ferrari teammate Leclerc, meanwhile, took some time away for the birth of his daughter. He arrived in Brazil late and went straight into the meeting. For him, it is a closed chapter after good discussions with Hamilton and even Vasseur.

"I mean, from my perspective, I was very straightforward, of course, and I think it was good to speak," Leclerc told the media. "I think this is always needed after a moment like this. It took proportions that are way too big, and it has been around also for way too long.

"At the same time, it was a very special week for me, so I couldn't really have those discussions before. And it was important to have those discussions, but now I consider that a closed chapter, and I think it will be important to look ahead."

A Ferrari in the wet at Interlagos. Image Credit: Scuderia Ferrari

Leclerc feels it is okay to disagree, but better to speak your mind, as they did. "I really believe that when we speak, things are much better straight away," he said.

"Of course, there is tension; of course, we want to finish ahead of each other, and sometimes we are not going to do things right. And it's okay sometimes to disagree on the way you see things, but at the same time, discussing about those things helps a whole lot.

"I mean, history speaks for itself, and maybe, yeah, looking back, it's maybe something needed. At the same time as I said, sometimes you disagree, and you've got to find common ground."

Asked about Hamilton saying that Leclerc races harder against him than anyone else, the Monegasque didn't want to add fuel. "I wouldn't want to comment on that," he said. "Honestly, there have been way too many comments, and again way too much fuss about it for way too long, so I don't really want to get into those kinds of comments."
