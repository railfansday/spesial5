NBA News: Donte DiVincenzo Cleared for Practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Minnesota Timberwolves officially announced on Tuesday morning that Donte DiVincenzo has been cleared for team basketball activities. As further detailed by The Athletic's Jon Krawczynski, a source told him that DiVincenzo is eyeing a return in January 2027.

ESPN's Shams Charania also shared his update on DiVincenzo's potential timeline, going on record as saying closed to around the turn of the new year.

This may not be a surprise to those of you who have been following along his rehab journey. As mentioned earlier this month, the Wolves have been providing a mini-docuseries on their YouTube channel following him along as he progresses towards a return titled "Day Zero." It's an incredible series that I highly recommend you check out if you haven't already done so.

During those updates, fans could only read between the lines and choose to believe the edited content that the media team was putting out about DiVincenzo's recovery. However, today's headline is an official update. Though the team's official press release didn't mention a specific timetable, it seems awfully realistic to expect him back within two months from now. It would be the quickest recovery from a torn Achilles tendon in NBA history.

In Krawczynski's article, he shared a quote from Donte's teammate Jaylen Clark, who also suffered a torn Achilles in college.

"He's light years ahead of me," Clark said in October. "Like, it's crazy to watch him on a daily basis. It's just crazy how far technology and all that stuff (has come), because I don't know, he's at six, seven months. The stuff he's doing, I was nowhere near. It took me a year and three months, four months. And then at that point, I was still out of shape, fat, barely could jump and everything. He's flying around."

Until we actually see Donte's name off the daily injury report, we can't quite say he's making history yet. But it's becoming clear that his hard work is setting things up for him to completely reshape what it means when a NBA player suffers what many used to consider a career-ending injury. January looks realistic, but what if it's even December? Sleigh bells? Presents under the tree? I hear what the comments section is whispering.

This update is certainly something Wolves fans should be happy to hear, considering the meltdown that many had regarding Anthony Edwards' media availability last week. Couple that with the hamstring issue Jaylen Clark recently suffered, getting some reinforcements back would be amazing. The second half of December features a slew of games against Western Conference playoff teams like the Lakers, Nuggets, and Thunder. Minnesota has been able to stay afloat despite missing several rotation players, but will that be sustainable moving forward?

Donte plays a style that is incredibly easy to fit in no matter who's on the floor or what the system is. He's a high energy "do-shit" player that will hustle on both ends of the court. His propensity to dive around the court fits Minnesota's style of defense like a glove. Meanwhile, on the offensive end, adding another movement shooter will make an already deadly bench deadlier. It's no wonder why many in the organization, front office and players, view him as the "heart and soul" of the team.

I'm going to take a second to gloat about my prediction resumé from earlier this month. Before there was an official update on DiVincenzo's return to practice status, I bravely put my name on the line.

I'll wager 690 Hoopus Points that Donte is back on the court before the All-Star break. I'll even call my shot and say he makes his debut on January 27th, 2027 against the team he suffered his injury against, the Denver Nuggets.
