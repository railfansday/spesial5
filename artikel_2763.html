Lions' coach defends player celebrations after Aidan Hutchinson ACL tear

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Detroit Lions will be without their biggest star on defense for the rest of the year.

Lions head coach Dan Campbell confirmed that edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson's knee injury, which he sustained celebrating a sack on Sunday, Nov. 15, was a torn ACL. Hutchinson will miss the remainder of the season as he recovers.

"Man, what a gut punch," Campbell said on Detroit radio station 97.1 The Ticket on Tuesday, Nov. 17. "He's such a phenomenal player. This guy's an impact player, but more importantly he's an unbelievable teammate. And he's one of our captains. It just stinks, man."

The coach also said that Hutchinson's injury will not create any discussions with the Lions' players when it comes to celebrating. Campbell said he doesn't want the team to stop playing with emotion just because Hutchinson's injury came while celebrating.

"I think what happened speaks for itself," Campbell said. "We're not talking to 8-year-olds here."

He explained that he once felt as though he tore a bicep while pumping his arm in celebration, but it hasn't stopped him from showing emotion.

"This game is built off of emotion," Campbell said. "And if you have to contain yourself after a play is made – I'm not talking about in the moment, in the game, because you gotta keep your head – then you're really becoming something you're not."

"I'm not going to say, 'Nobody celebrates anymore.'"

Campbell confirmed that Hutchinson tore his ACL while celebrating his sack of Drake Maye. The Nov. 15 injury will knock the edge rusher out for the remainder of the season.

"I hate it, I know he hates it," Campbell said. "But I mean, the number of moves that I've seen Hutch make and the number of plyometrics, explosive movements, the spins, the one leg lateral movements, lateral balances, I'm just like, it was one of those things, man," Campbell said.

Hutchinson sustained the injury when he landed after a celebratory leap. The edge rusher's right knee buckled when his legs hit the ground again, and he fell over and grabbed at his knee.
