Panthers QB Bryce Young earns solid grade for clutch Week 11 performance vs. Ravens

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Carolina Panthers quarterback Bryce Young is back on the upswing as his breakout campaign turns towards the stretch run.

The fourth-year passer led the 14th game-winning drive of his career in Sunday's 35-33 win over the Baltimore Ravens. Other than a rough end-zone interception, Young played a clean game—completing 22 of his 30 throws for 254 yards and two touchdowns to complement another big day from running back Chuba Hubbard.

For his efforts, Brent Sobleski at Bleacher Report has given Young a solid 'B' grade for Week 11. Sobleski writes:

While another game-winning drive is impressive, the Carolina quarterback's ability to respond while under constant duress became the more remarkable aspect of Young's performance. According to NFL Next Gen Stats, Young completed 66.7 percent of his passes for a career-higher 173 yards when pressured.

Young's best asset remains his ability to throw well under pressure. As opposed to crumbling to the heat like in years past, he's kept his eyes up, his feet moving and his decision-making sharp.

Barring a dramatic drop-off in December, the Panthers should plan to give Young a very large extension at some point this coming offseason.
