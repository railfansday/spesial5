Wizards vs Magic Preview: Washington returns home to face Magic

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Kyshawn George played a strong all-around game in the Washington Wizards win over the Orlando Magic. | Getty Images

When: Sunday, November 22nd at 7:00 p.m. ET

How to watch: Monumental Sports Network, League Pass

Magic: Jonathan Issac – Questionable (Knee), Izaiyah Nelson – Out (Ankle), Noah Penda – Out (Hamstring), Paolo Banchero – Out (Ankle)

Wizards: Jamir Watkins – Out (Knee), Tristan Vukcevic – Out (Hip), Reece Beekman – Out (Shoulder), Justin Champagnie – Out (Foot)

Riding on a two-game winning streak, the Wizards return home to face their Southeast Division foe, the Orlando Magic. Like the first matchup, this game will not feature Orlando star Paolo Banchero who is still out with an ankle injury. In that matchup the Wizards swarmed the Magic, holding Orlando to just 94 points scoring.

That formula was similar to what the Wizards needed to win their game on Friday night in Chicago, which featured Washington holding the Chicago Bulls to 94 points as well. The defensive intensity is key to keep the Wizards in these games. Currently they are ranked in the bottom 3rd of the league in offensive rating. This team simply has not figured out a consistent flow on offense.

Magic are not much better, especially without Banchero. Both teams average close to the same amount in points per game, while the Magic play at a slightly slower pace.

What's the key to this one? Wizards need to win the battle on the boards. Both teams are physically similar, using long athletic players at many positions to create mismatches, but neither has generated enough offense to allow themselves to win many games. When you have teams who struggle offensively, the last thing you want to do is continually give them 2nd chances to score. Let's see if the Wizards can push their winning streak to 3.
