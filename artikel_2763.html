Troy Aikman adds a little more to the lingering Joe Buck feud

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

When ESPN president Jimmy Pitaro said this week that the media coverage of the Joe Buck and Troy Aikman situation has “been disappointing,” the obvious takeaway was that Pitaro should be disappointed with those ESPN employees who chose to speak privately and/or publicly about a sensitive internal situation.

He gave it more oxygen on Thursday, in his weekly appearance on 96.7 The Ticket in Dallas.

Two of the usual three hosts of the show — Craig Miller and Gordon Keith — were out. It left George Dunham to do the segment on his own.

“Yeah, you’re flying solo this morning, huh?” Aikman said, via Brandon Contes of Awful Announcing. "[Y]ou know, I know that’s what — Joe’s always wanted a one-man booth. I’m wondering if you’ve always wanted a one-man radio show.”

Dunham later mentioned that he saw Buck on the 2026 CMA Awards and that “he’s everywhere.”

“Yeah, everywhere,” Aikman said. “Ubiquitous.”

But for the broader context of their well-publicized interpersonal issues, Aikman’s comments wouldn’t have registered. Given what’s known about their relationship, it adds a log to the fire.

And it gives Pitaro even more reason to be disappointed.
