Daily Norseman Reacts Survey Week 9: Do We Like That?

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year we ask questions of the most plugged-in Vikings fans and fans across the country.

It is, once again, time for us to take the pulse of our readers with our SB Nation Reacts poll. We have a couple of questions for you, the readers of the biggest and, if we might say so, the best Minnesota Vikings website on the interwebs to see how you're feeling about our favorite football team as we hit the halfway point of the 2026 NFL season.

We've got a couple of questions for you this week. As always, the first one has to do with whether or not you think the purple are moving in the right direction. Yes, Monday Night Football was a bit of an ugly one for our favorite side, as the Vikings fell to the Buffalo Bills in a relatively one-sided affair. The defense, once again, did everything it could, but when you play nearly three full quarters of football because you get no help from the offense. . .well, "everything you could" can only be so much. But the team is still 6-2, which is pretty good.

We had a pretty difficult time coming up with a second question for this week, but we eventually settled on asking everyone how they feel about the return of a certain player. . .who plays a certain very important position. . .and wears a certain number that looks a lot like a snowman. Yes, folks, Kirk Cousins is back and is, hopefully, going to drag this offense back to relevance somehow. So, we want to know what you think of our favorite squad bringing Kirk Cousins back for the amazingly. . .shockingly, really. . .reasonable price of a third-round pick in the 2027 NFL Draft.

As always, we invite you to participate in our survey for this week by casting your votes in our poll questions, whether by doing so below or over in the Feed section of our main page, and by sounding off in the comments section.

Have at it, everybody! We'll have the answers to this week's questions for you before things kick off at Lambeau on Sunday afternoon.
