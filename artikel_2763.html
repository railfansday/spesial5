How long will Rams’ Terrance Ferguson be sidelined?

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Terrance Ferguson had a setback in practice last week and the details coming out are that Ferguson is expected to miss a few weeks. Will Ferguson be able to stay healthy enough to play again for the Los Angeles Rams in 2026? The expectation is, yes, but he was expected to play in Week 9 and that didn't happen. That's concerning.

Add in that Puka Nacua could miss Week 10, and if there is no Ferguson, the Rams may not have a reliable pass catcher. Will the Rams make a trade before Tuesday's deadline? If so, is there a receiver who can join and truly help the Rams less than a week later against the Arizona Cardinals?

The Rams may have to roll with who they have, and after Nacua, the Rams passing game is a question mark, and that's not a great look halfway into the NFL season.

""Pulled his hamstring. You can't make it up, can you?" McVay said, visibly disappointed. "He was running just a standard out-cut, felt his hamstring pull and probably be another couple weeks for him."

The Rams play the Cardinals next week, a game that Ferguson is almost certain to miss. After that, they have a bye in Week 11 before returning to face the Packers in Week 12 on Thanksgiving Eve. The Rams are going to take it a week at a time with Ferguson, as McVay wants to avoid any scenario where he isn't activated before the 21-day window closes."
