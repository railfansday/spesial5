Veteran guard Gabe Vincent reportedly finalizing free-agent contract with Kings

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Veteran guard Gabe Vincent reportedly finalizing free-agent contract with Kings originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

Sacramento is finalizing a contract with veteran free-agent guard Gabe Vincent, ESPN's Shams Charania reported Sunday, citing sources.

The 30-year-old Vincent went undrafted in 2018 before signing with the Miami Heat, where he spent the first four seasons of his NBA career before playing for the Los Angeles Lakers (2023-2026), who traded him to the Atlanta Hawks midway through the 2025-26 NBA season.

Over seven NBA seasons, Vincent has averaged 6.7 points, 1.5 rebounds and 1.9 assists per game on 39.2-percent shooting from the field and 33.8 percent from 3-point range.

He now will provide coach Doug Christie with important veteran depth at the guard position behind star rookie Darius Acuff Jr.
