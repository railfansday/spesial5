Netflix's 'Haunted Hotel' Just Broke a Major Streaming Curse

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Jeffrey is a freelance features writer at Collider. He is an MPA-accredited entertainment journalist and a Tomatometer-approved critic based in the LA area. He graduated from the University of Texas at Austin with a Bachelor of Science in Radio, TV, & Film and a Bachelor of Arts in Theater.

Editor's note: The below contains minor spoilers for Haunted Hotel Season 2.Netflix's breakout animated hit, Haunted Hotel, has just wrapped up its second season. While the series first premiered to little fanfare last year, it's now building momentum and a sizable organic fanbase. With the news that the show has officially been renewed for Season 3 — right before Halloween, no less — Haunted Hotel just broke a streaming curse that's killed many promising shows in the past.

Over the past several years, some of Netflix's buzziest shows have debuted, garnered a sizable following, and were then suddenly and unceremoniously cancelled, leaving viewers on a huge cliffhanger. One of the streamer's biggest casualties was the animated series Inside Job, which was renewed for a second season in June 2022. Six months later, creator Shion Takeuchi revealed that Netflix had cancelled the series, rescinding the initial renewal.

The show has already been renewed for Season 2!

Thankfully, Haunted Hotel hasn't suffered the same fate as Inside Job, Sense8, The Boroughs, and so many other great Netflix shows that were cancelled before their respective stories could reach a proper conclusion. This Season 3 renewal likely comes as a big relief to the show's dedicated fanbase, who have been creating their own art and video edits. More than that, the announcement that Haunted Hotel will return for another season emphasizes the power of the show, world, and characters Matt Roller has created.

Haunted Hotel follows the Freeling family, who run the Undervale Hotel that single mom Katherine (Eliza Coupe) has inherited from her late brother, Nathan (Will Forte). Joined by her kids, the devious Esther (Natalie Palamides) and the studious Ben (Skyler Gisondo), Katherine tries to make the best of the family's situation and save their failing hotel. However, the Undervale is actually haunted by hundreds of ghosts — including Nathan, whom everyone can see. Dealing with the hotel's spectral residents is no walk in the park, and hijinks ensue.

Haunted Hotel lovingly parodies the horror genre and its many subgenres while also telling a surprisingly poignant story about a struggling family picking up the pieces and trying to move forward in their lives. While Season 1 builds to a shocking yet organic reveal about Nathan's death that belies his optimistic personality, the series also highlights the wonderfully weird and endearing friendship between Esther and Abaddon (Jimmi Simpson), an immortal and mischievous demon now cursed to wander the Earth in powerless human form. Abaddon essentially becomes the Freelings' unofficially adopted child, and his storyline pays off with one fantastic Season 2 episode.

While Haunted Hotel's animation is much more vibrant, elaborate, and exciting in Season 2, one episode in particular cements the Netflix series as an animated masterpiece. "Ghost Day Out" involves the Undervale's ghosts breaking out of the confines of the hotel due to the strength of a once-in-a-generation full moon. Finally able to wander the town of Midwich, Nathan meets and forms a connection with another ghost, Bridget (Zooey Deschanel), who haunts the neighboring lighthouse. Haunted Hotel is already hilarious, but this episode is surprisingly emotional and romantic.

After meeting Bridget, Nathan finally confronts his fears about his death and the afterlife, throwing caution to the wind and experiencing a pure, joyous moment set to an incredible cover of Simple Minds' "Don’t You (Forget About Me)." Despite the show's horror-centric premise and macabre themes, the story possesses genuine heart and sweetness. If there's one episode that single-handedly earns the show's third-season renewal, it's Ghost Day Out," but anyone new to Haunted Hotel will quickly discover it to be a surprising yet rewarding binge.
