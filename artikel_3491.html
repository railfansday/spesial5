Mila Kunis Jokes She's 'Made Out' With 2 of the Same Women as Ashton Kutcher

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Mila Kunis is reminding fans that she and husband Ashton Kutcher shared kisses with a few of the same costars.

“There’s two women that both my husband and I have made out with,” Kunis, 43, said during a Wednesday, November 25, appearance on Kelly Ripa’s “Let’s Talk Off Camera” podcast. “Natalie Portman and Zoe Saldaña.”

“Those are good women to make out with,” Ripa, 56, quipped.

Kutcher and Saldaña, both 48, costarred in the romantic comedy Guess Who in 2005, sharing an onscreen kiss. The actor later locked lips with Portman, 45, in the 2011 rom-com No Strings Attached.

As for Kunis, the actress kissed Saldaña and Portman in the 2007 film After Sex and the 2010 psychological thriller Black Swan, respectively.

“Nat and I were really good friends,” Kunis said on Wednesday. “It’s uncomfortable because you want to laugh at the insanity. But you’re so comfortable with one another because you’re such close girlfriends. So there’s that weird level where you’re like, ‘What are we doing? We look like idiots.’”

Kunis began dating Kutcher in early 2012, more than a decade after they starred together in That ’70s Show. They got engaged in February 2014 and welcomed daughter Wyatt that October. The couple tied the knot in July 2015 before welcoming their son, Dimitri, in November 2016.

Elsewhere on the podcast, Kunis recalled giving Kutcher a “second look” at the 2012 Golden Globes.

“I looked at him the first time in how many years and went, ‘Oh, he’s kinda hot,’” she shared.

Kunis and Kutcher stayed in touch after their Golden Globes run-in, with the latter inviting the former to a housewarming party.

“I was watching him smoke. I had just quit … and I was like, ‘I just want him to breathe it on me for a second,’” she recalled. “Throughout the night, as he would smoke, he would get closer and closer until … there was no more place to go.”

Kunis and Kutcher celebrated 10 years of marriage in July 2025 and opted to give each other gifts made out of tin. (Tin and aluminum are considered traditional gifts for a 10th wedding anniversary.)

“Ashton loves the Chicago Bears,” Kunis revealed on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon that December. “So for the anniversary, I got him a Tin Man from Wizard of Oz made of, like, sardine cans and pea cans, and it’s all of Chicago Bears. It’s really cute. It’s really good.”

As for the gift Kutcher got her, Kunis said, “Because I love sardines and he loves that I love sardines, he got me a custom-made sardine box. It’s just a giant tin sardine box. It’s really cute. I loved it. I was like, ‘I do love sardines!’”
