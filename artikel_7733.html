University of California law school offers intensive course on AI legal tool building

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

LexLab, the legal technology hub at the University of California College of the Law at San Francisco, is launching a three-day artificial intelligence legal tool building course, Law & AI Certificate Lab. (Photo by inkoly/Getty Images)

LexLab, the legal technology hub at the University of California College of the Law at San Francisco, is launching a three-day artificial intelligence legal tool building course, Law & AI Certificate Lab.

The intensive LAIC Lab course, offered Dec. 1-3, is a condensed, intensive version of one offered to law students over the course of a semester, offering lawyers and leaders a deep dive into the logic underlying legal AI while offering hands-on exploration of AI systems.

The course allows students to work with AI company Legora and AI platform Harvey as well as Replit, an app and website builder.

At the end of the certificate program, students should be able to move beyond using “chat” to build repeatable playbooks, checklists and other workflows; evaluate AI tools and vendors; determine whether tech should be bought or built; and manage privilege, confidentiality, conflicts and competence issues in AI-augmented practice.
