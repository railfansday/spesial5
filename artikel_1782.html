“I honestly didn’t expect it” – Journalist claims Milan have made €70m offer for Endrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It seems AC Milan have made an offer for Endrick in recent days, with a Spanish journalist shocked at the situation.

Talk of Endrick arriving at San Siro is dominating the news, and it’s clear that Milan are not messing around here. Indeed, they are looking to beat off interest from other European clubs and they may well be close to doing so.

Jose Mourinho is still opposing a deal, but the Brazilian’s entourage was in Italy last week and it seems that a move to San Siro is becoming less of a hypothetical and more of a tangible possibility.

Speaking to SER Radio, Anton Meana looked at the reporting from Italy about the Rossoneri’s interest in Endrick and how the situation was actually unfolding.

“AC Milan’s offer is formal and they want to sign him in January. The Rossoneri have already informed the player, and if he can’t leave in January, they’ll see about negotiations in the summer. But AC Milan’s firm offer is now on the table.

“The owner of AC Milan wants to offer close to 70 million euros to sign Endrick now. The problem is that Mourinho prefers to keep him because he knows there wouldn’t be a signing that could match or surpass the Brazilian.

“It’s good for him to have Endrick because he’s good, because he provides competition for Mbappé, and because he trains hard for every match. I want to see how the next few weeks unfold. I’d say Endrick isn’t going to leave, but the player’s entourage, his agents, will be in Madrid this week or early next week seeking talks and exploring the striker’s immediate future.

“I thought it was just the typical bluff from the Italian transfer press, who constantly promote players and see them as being in Serie A all the time, but this offer from Milan, I honestly didn’t expect it. The interest, as Cerruti told you, is very serious, and if they can, they want to sign Endrick this winter transfer window.”

It’s safe to say that the Diavolo’s interest is more than speculative and with an official offer on the table, things may begin to move between Milan and Madrid.
