Can GTA 6 download codes be seen through sealed boxes? Warning goes viral days before launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

GTA 6 players are raising concerns over a potential security flaw that could allow people to see download codes through sealed physical copies of the game without ever opening the box.

With Grand Theft Auto 6 finally launching on November 19, physical editions are now reaching customers, but some fans have spotted what they believe could be a serious problem.

As Rockstar previously confirmed, physical copies of GTA 6 don’t actually contain a disc. Instead, buyers receive a download code that can be redeemed to install the game.

However, viral posts suggest it might be possible to read that code without breaking the packaging, potentially allowing someone to redeem a copy they haven’t purchased.

The concern spread on November 13 through posts on X and Reddit discussions showing what appears to be a download code visible through a sealed GTA 6 case.

One Reddit user claimed they managed to make the code visible using a phone flashlight, raising questions about whether someone could redeem a game without purchasing it.

Fans have discovered the entire GTA 6 redeem code on the physical copy can be seen without opening the box by shining a powerful flashlight through the side at an angle. pic.twitter.com/YtN5QhC81R

“If the gta 6 box on the shelf is the same as the pre order, then all shelf codes can be read and activated?” they questioned.

The user said they had already redeemed their own code and censored several characters in the image.

“I immediately thought of this when I saw where the code was on mine,” another chimed in. “I think with a powerful flashlight and a little time, people can totally get the code without unsealing it.”

Others were skeptical, with one commenter challenging the claim: “Read and type the code from outside the box. Then we’ll talk.”

Fans claim a new trick can reveal the GTA 6 download code without opening the physical box. By shining a flashlight through the plastic at an angle, they say the text inside becomes visible. pic.twitter.com/sNfHNITmjj

As reported by French outlet Journal du Geek, the method hasn’t worked consistently for players attempting to replicate it.

The publication noted that codes in France are printed in dark gray against a black background, making them particularly difficult to read through the packaging.

There are also differences between the PS5 and Xbox versions, with the placement of the codes and included materials potentially making them harder to see.

Some have even suggested that the original viral image was edited to exaggerate how clearly the code could be seen.

Nevertheless, the situation has sparked fresh debate over Rockstar’s decision to abandon traditional discs, which previously led some game stores to refuse to stock GTA 6.
