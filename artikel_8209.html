Top 10 Resorts in the East for Challenge (2027)

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

East Coasters swear the conditions they ski make them better skiers, and it’s hard to argue with them. Skiing in the East means handling variable snow and, yes, ice. Lower elevations and cold, wet weather make for demanding days.

The runs may be shorter than those out West, but that doesn’t make them easier. Pair them with unpredictable conditions, and the East can give any Western purist a run for their money. So, if you’re looking for terrain that will challenge even the most skilled rippers out there, keep scrolling: SKI readers voted the following ski areas as the 10 best resorts for hard chargers.
