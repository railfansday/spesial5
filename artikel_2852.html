Kawhi Leonard making strides in recovery in latest injury update

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Kawhi Leonard making strides in recovery in latest injury update appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Toronto Raptors started off the 2026-2027 season at 2-0 amid the return of All-Star forward Kawhi Leonard, but since then he's been sidelined with a knee injury and the team has sputtered in the standings. They've gone only 3-8 since their start and currently hold a four-game losing streak. But help could be on the way soon via the latest injury update for Leonard.

Kawhi Leonard is reportedly progressing in his injury rehab, and he is set to be re-evaluated by the Raptors next week, as per ClutchPoints' NBA insider Brett Siegel. He has been participating in a strength and conditioning program amid swelling in his left knee.

Leonard last played during the team's win against the Washington Wizards on Oct. 23. He finished that game with 16 points, nine rebounds, one assist and three steals in a little over 27 minutes.

Through the Raptors' first two games of the season, Leonard was averaging 19.5 points, 7.5 rebounds, 1.5 assists and 3.5 steals with splits of 32.4 percent shooting from the field, 28.6 percent shooting from the 3-point line and 100 percent shooting from the free-throw line.

Leonard has dealt with injury issues throughout his career, but he's coming off one of his healthiest seasons in some time, having appeared in 65 games for the LA Clippers in 2025-26. This offseason, he returned via trade to the franchise he helped lead to the 2019 NBA title. Leonard spent only one season with the Raptors before opting to sign with the Clippers in free agency.

The No. 15 overall pick in the 2011 NBA Draft, the 35-year-old is in his 15th season in the NBA.
