Giants vs. Jaguars odds: Opening lines for Week 11 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The New York Giants will open Week 11 as 6.5-point underdogs at home against the Jacksonville Jaguars, per FanDuel.

This game has been penciled in as a loss for the Giants for weeks now, and it shouldn't be much of a surprise to anyone paying attention that the 5-3-1 Giants are (nearly) touchdown underdogs to the 7-2 Jaguars.

The Giants are coming off of a disappointing, and frustrating, loss to the Washington Commanders, squandering a golden opportunity to put the NFC East in a strangle hold and cement their position as a playoff contender. The Giants had multiple opportunities to put the game away, and instead played to not-lose on offense and defense — only to lose anyway.

The Jaguars, meanwhile, are coming off of a 40-23 win over the Tennessee Titans, though that game was closer than the final score until the final 5 minutes or so.

One of the biggest questions in the upcoming game is how the Jaguars will incorporate their newly acquired defensive players. The Jags elected to rest NT Jordan Davis and S Jesse Bates, both of whom were acquired in trades last week. DT Devon Godcheaux (acquired from the New Orleans Saints) did play, and ended the game with a tackle.

The Giants can find comfort in the fact that they were 10.5-point underdogs on the road to the Houston Texans and came away with a win. However, the Jaguars have been far better than the Texans were when the Giants played them in Week 7.

The Giants can win this game, but they'll certainly have their work cut out for them.
