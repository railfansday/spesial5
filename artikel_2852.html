Saints captain Justin Reid wants team to release him with ‘resolution’ expected soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A prized Saints 2025 free-agent acquisition wants out.

Defensive captain Justin Reid has requested his release despite committing to the team for three years just 20 months ago.

"I think that's going to come to a resolution here shortly," defensive coordinator Brandon Staley said Thursday.

Justin Reid of the Saints runs onto the pitch before a game against the Steelers at Stade de France in Paris, Oct. 25, 2026. Getty Images

Reid signed a three-year deal worth $31.5 million in March 2025, with $22.25 million fully guaranteed, making this a surprising turn of events.

Staley added that the Saints and Reid are "working through some things" as they work on the terms of his release.

"Justin's been a professional since he's been here and I know that I've really enjoyed working with him," Staley said ahead of a Week 11 road clash vs. the Bears. "There's some things that we all want a little bit more of from a productivity standpoint. But in terms of the fit, in terms of how he's been here, he's been a pleasure to coach. So, we'll just leave it at that."

It will be interesting to see whether Reid gets claimed should he be released, with perhaps the Chiefs as a suitor given he spent the three seasons prior with them.

Reid restructured his contract going into the 2026 season, per OverTheCap, converting most of his salary to bonuses, so he's only owed roughly $578,000 through the end of the season, and his 2027 season turned into a club option, giving any claiming team an out after this year.

The star safety who won two Super Bowls with the Chiefs has had a rough season in New Orleans.

New Orleans Saints safety Justin Reid (21) participates in an NFL football practice in Versailles, near Paris, Wednesday, Oct. 21, 2026. AP Photo/Aurelien Morissard

Reid has a 44.4 rating on Pro Football Focus, 86th out of 91 qualified safeties, after being among the league's best with the Chiefs.

Perhaps a change in scenery this late in the season will help Reid rebound to form.
