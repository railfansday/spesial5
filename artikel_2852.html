Iraola wants in-form Alexander Isak to keep improving for Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Alexander Isak has scored four goals in as many Liverpool matches having taken his tally to double figures for the season, but head coach Andoni Iraola believes he can still improve.

In September Iraola spoke about how his "fights" with the Sweden international were about the work he needed to do off the ball, but in the subsequent two months there has been a marked change.

That was highlighted in Wednesday's Champions League win against Club Brugge, where the striker's pressure on home Jan Sommer saw him rewarded with the opening goal after a mistake by the experienced goalkeeper.

"He has definitely improved a lot on the ball and without the ball," said Iraola.

"He is understanding better the press and the better he understands the press the less he has to run also.

"Also on the ball we are using him much better in possession, he is linking up play very well, technically he is a very nice player so I think we have to use him not just in the finishing side, he can be very important in our build up.

"Against (Manchester) United he didn't score a goal but was really good and the other day against Brugge he was very good.

"He helps us in the build-up, and sometimes those balls will finish on him and he will help us to progress so after we can provide him with better assists.

"I think he is understanding everything and is ready to learn and even improve more and it is a good sign."

The form of Isak, who now has 10 goals in all competitions, and Florian Wirtz (three goals in his last three games) has taken some of the pressure off other forwards like Bradley Barcola.

That will remain important while options up front continue to be limited, with Cody Gakpo still sidelined having missed the last two matches with a calf problem and Victor Munoz a doubt for Sunday's 249th Merseyside derby at Everton after injuring a knee midweek.

"Cody is definitely not (available). He has a muscle injury so we have to wait a little bit until the process goes on and it heals," added Iraola.

"This kind of injury is normally about two or three weeks. He did it in the last game against Germany so normally it is this timeline.

"I don't like to say timelines with injury because every injury is different and some of them go quicker than others. Normally, this isn't a big injury but there is something there and you have to give it the time to heal.

"With Victor, I think the good news is after assessing him, there is no structural damage in the knee but he has a big knock. He hasn't trained today but he wants to try tomorrow so we will see. I cannot tell you right now."

Midfielder Ryan Gravenberch, who has missed the last four matches with a heel problem, also remains unavailable.
