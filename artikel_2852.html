3 Cardinals ruled out for Seattle contest

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The rubber match for the Arizona Cardinals against the Seattle Seahawks will be played this weekend as Arizona plays on the road. The Seahawks thumped the Cardinals 31-7 in Week 2.

The Cardinals roster is fairly healthy, but three players aren't making the trip to the State of Washington.

RELATED: CARDINALS & SEAHAWKS WEEK 9 INJURY REPORTS

Two offensive linemen, OG Jon Gaines and OT Jayden Williams, are ruled out along with RB Bam Knight.

Williams began the season as the backup to OT Paris Johnson at left tackle, who is now on IR. Williams has played in all eight games with four starts. He suffered a knee injury against the Dallas Cowboys.

Gaines competed with Isaiah Adams during training camp for the starting right guard spot, and Adams was named the Week 1 starter. Adams then injured an ankle and was placed on IR, but had his 21-day window open and is now ruled as available to play. Most likely, Adams will start, with Hayden Conner as the backup.

Knight is in concussion protocol, which usually takes 10 days to clear. The running back room now consists of Tyler Allgeier and Evan Hull. The practice squad lists Jermar Jefferson and veteran Jerome Ford. It is assumed one will be elevated against the Seahawks.
