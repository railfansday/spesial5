Draymond Green on Yaxel Lendeborg: ‘It’s unfair to put him in Steph Curry’s role’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Golden State Warriors rookie Yaxel Lendeborg already has a lot on his plate, especially with Steph Curry sidelined and Jimmy Butler still in the middle of rehabbing his repaired ACL.

Lendeborg has been the de facto first option ever since Curry tore his right oblique against the Houston Rockets. Defenses have responded accordingly by sending aggressive coverages against him and tilting the entirety of their defensive machinery towards him.

Lendeborg visibly struggled against the San Antonio Spurs, while he was passive and listless to start against the New Orleans Pelicans. He upped the aggression slider during the second half and finished the game with 28 points. However, it was clear that the frustration he felt during and after the Spurs loss carried over to this game against the Pelicans.

After the game, Draymond Green was asked about Lendeborg and his struggles in trying to be the Warriors' first option.

Lendeborg is still an imperfect player with flaws that need to be either eliminated or minimized — all while making sure his strengths are maximized to their fullest extent. The Warriors have no choice but to rely on him while waiting for Curry to recover.
