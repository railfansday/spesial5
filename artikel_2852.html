Tyson Fury vs Anthony Joshua: Joe Joyce makes his pick after years of deliberation

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

On December 11 at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff, Tyson Fury vs Anthony Joshua is set to finally take place. The entire card will stream live and exclusively around the world on Netflix. It's often felt like this fight would never happen, but everything is set for these two titans of British boxing to meet for the WBA "super" heavyweight title. As we edge closer to fight night, fellow British heavyweight Joe Joyce has spoken to The Ring, revealing his pick for Fury-Joshua.

Fury (36-2-1, 25 KOs) has been brilliant at times during his career. The game plan against Wladimir Klitschko was perfect, and later on, the tactics in the trilogy with Deontay Wilder were perfectly executed by "The Gypsy King." He later tackled Oleksandr Usyk, losing twice to the Ukrainian. After losing the rematch to Oleksandr Usyk, Fury retired once again before returning to the ring earlier this year, beating Arslanbek Makhmudov and Mariusz Wach.

Joshua (30-4, 27 KOs) has been beloved by much of the British public since winning the gold medal at the 2012 Olympics. During his professional career, his best wins have come against Wladimir Klitschko, Alexander Povetkin, and Joseph Parker. In his most recent bout, AJ was floored twice in the opening round against Kristian Prenga, before stopping him in the second round.

Joe Joyce (16-5, 15 KOs) was considered by many to be on the verge of a world title shot, before losing back-to-back fights to Zhilei Zhang. When speaking to The Ring, the "Juggernaut" revealed his pick for Fury vs Joshua:

"I suppose throughout the years it was nip-and-tuck in my mind and I could make an argument for both to win," he told The Ring. "But now I'm going Fury. Just thinking skills-wise and he is the bigger man out of the two. Also, off the back of their most recent fights, that's my pick.

"It reminds me of [Floyd] Mayweather and [Manny] Pacquiao because it has been brewing for so long and it's finally happening. But I think heavyweights can go on later so this is shaping up to be a great fight."

Tyson Fury vs Anthony Joshua is just around the corner. Not to be a pessimist, but I, like many, will not be fully invested in this fight until the referee gives the fighters their final instructions on December 11.

It remains to be seen who has the most left, but looking at their most recent fights, it's hard not to favour Fury. Can Joshua knock Fury out? I firmly believe he has the power to do this; however, Fury seemingly still has the ability to box and move. Is Fury on the decline? Yes, his performance against Wach showed that. Joshua's punch resistance has seemingly gone, so it feels as though AJ must knock out Fury to win. "The Gypsy King" will be wary of this, and that's why we are backing him to win by decision.
