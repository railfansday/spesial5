The Pokémon TCGs Delta Reign Booster Bundle is down to a new record low on Amazon — save almost $10

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Delta Reign Booster Bundle is on sale for $49.94 at Amazon, down from its $58.50 list price.

The Pokémon TCG's Delta Reign expansion has only been out since Nov. 6, but we've already seen the Mega Evolution expansion’s six-pack Booster Bundle get cheaper than ever on Amazon — just in time for Black Friday.

As of Nov. 12, the Pokémon TCG Delta Reign Booster Bundle can be bought for as low as $49.94 at Amazon, with $8.56 knocked off its typical $58.50 price. With 15% off, the cost of each of its six booster packs comes to $8.32. That's a sizeable drop from the $57.95 price we covered on launch day. This is officially the lowest-ever price on Amazon, confirmed with price tracker camelcamelcamel.

This new offer means Amazon’s price is only $9.62 above its estimated $40.32 market price on TCGplayer.

This deal is coming from third-party seller TOYSandmore, with delivery times ranging from Nov. 19-25 at the time of writing. However, there are other listings with speedier delivery times for just a cent more at $49.95.

You’re getting six titular Pokémon TCG packs with 10 trading cards each, with a Basic Energy and Pokémon TCG Live code card. Some of the big pulls from Delta Reign's 135-plus-card expansion include Mega Rayquaza ex, Mega Golurk ex, Mega Malamar ex, and Mega Golisopod ex.
