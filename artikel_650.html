Following Sony’s April price hike that pushed the PS5 Pro’s standard MSRP to $899.99, legitimate retail stock has dried up across PlayStation Direct, Best Buy, Target, and GameStop.

Instead of falling for inflated reseller markups, bookmark this tracker to monitor official retail pages directly. It all comes down to GTA 6 arriving next week. Since Rockstar is launching the game exclusively on consoles with no PC version available at launch, anyone who wants the best-looking trip to Leonida is rushing to grab Sony's flagship hardware.

Rockstar confirmed this week that while GTA 6 runs at 30 frames per second across all consoles, the PS5 Pro is the single best place to play on day one. It is the only console getting sharper 4K visuals through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and upgraded ray-traced shadows. Throw in faster load times, DualSense controller haptics, and a built-in 2TB drive for massive game files, and it makes total sense why players are upgrading right now.

Besides that, supply has been tight for months. Component shortages and rising memory costs have slowed down console production throughout 2026, leaving stores without enough stock to handle the pre-launch rush. Because production won't catch up to GTA 6 hype anytime soon, any standard list-price units that hit store sites will vanish in minutes. If you are debating whether to hunt down a PS5 Pro instead of playing GTA 6 on a standard PlayStation 5, Rockstar just confirmed exactly what the extra hardware gets you on day one.

Even though frame rates remain the same across all consoles, the PS5 Pro is the only system to unlock two major visual upgrades. First is PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR), Sony’s AI upscaling tech that sharpens lower-resolution images on the fly so Vice City’s neon skylines, crowded beaches, and distant horizons look noticeably crisper on a 4K TV.

You also get exclusive ray-traced shadows powered by the Pro’s faster graphics chip, giving sunlight, headlights, and towering cityscapes far more realistic depth and lighting than you will see on a base PS5 or Xbox Series X. Plus, with GTA 6 taking up over 210GB, the PS5 Pro’s built-in 2TB SSD gives you plenty of breathing room without having to delete half your library just to install it.

Catching a surprise drop before GTA 6 launches comes down to speed and a little bit of prep:

Dodge third-party marketplace sellers: Sites like Walmart and Amazon let outside merchants charge whatever they want. Always check the "Sold and shipped by" label to ensure you are buying from the actual retailer at standard list price.

Save your checkout information beforehand: Taking the time to type in a security code or shipping address during a drop will cost you your cart. Keep your profiles logged in with default payment methods saved.

Do not refresh Best Buy's waiting line: When Best Buy runs a drop, it puts shoppers into an automated queue. Hitting refresh resets your spot and sends you to the very back of the line. Meanwhile, refreshing PlayStation Direct directly on weekday mornings often catches small waves before stock-alert apps fire.

Check physical stores in the morning: Online checkout bots can wipe out digital inventory in seconds. Visiting Target, Best Buy, or GameStop when doors open often turns up fresh shipment boxes that never made it onto website listings.
