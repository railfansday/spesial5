CMA Awards See a 55% Ratings Bump in Key Adults Demo With Taylor Swift’s Return to the Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Senior Music Writer and Chief Music Critic

The CMA Awards‘ producers knew it; they knew her. What they undoubtedly knew was that a performance by Taylor Swift would bring a ratings bonanza to the 60th annual telecast, with the only question being how big a boost. It was a substantial one, as ratings released Friday showed the 2026 show being up 26% in total viewers and an even more impressive 55% gain in the key 18-49 demo, versus the previous year’s tallies.

With help from Swift’s performance of a “I Knew It, I Knew You”/”Our Song” medley, the CMAs had their highest overall viewership in seven years, ABC announced. The show drew 7.62 total viewers, versus 6.03 million in 2025, for that 26% increase. The 18-49 share of the audience amounted to a 1.01 rating, compared to a 0.65 share last year. Not every bit of that increase necessarily had to do with Swift. America has certainly been high on Ella Langley, who did at least as well as anticipated in the awards, sweeping nearly every category she was up for to win a record six CMA Awards. Her “Choosin’ Texas” song is currently enjoying its 28th week atop Billboard’s Hot 100 chart, an all-time record — although Langley chose to sing a different song on the telecast, her latest single, “Loving Life Again.” (The singer’s performance of the then-new “Choosin’ Texas” on the CMAs last year has been cited by her label as giving the hit its biggest turbo boost.)

Also possibly a factor in viewer anticipation for the show was the promise of a Dolly Parton tribute. The show opened with host Lainey Wilson and Vince Gill dueting on “I Will Always Love You,” a scaled-down but still very well-received homage, in comparison to the all-star Parton tribute special the Country Music Association and ABC put on in October. Other stars on the show included country drawing powers like Kacey Musgraves, Miranda Lambert, Tucker Wetmore, Chris Stapleton, Megan Moroney, Luke Combs and what turned out to be a controversial hard-rock salute to Brooks & Dunn.

But without a doubt, the increase would not have been in such remarkable double-digits range without Swift. A previous Variety report, “Taylor Swift’s CMA Return Helped Disney Sell More Ads,” noted that ABC even re-opened its previously closed advertising space for the three-hour telecast after Swift officially signed on with a performance.

The superstar had announced that she would be attending the show as soon as the nominations came in, with Swift landing one for her Disney/Pixar “Toy Story 5” closing theme song. But the fact that she would be performing was not revealed until days before the show. Swift last performed on the CMA Awards telecast in 2013; she last made an appearance on the show in 2016.

Swift made her performance an all-star affair, beginning the number with a pre-taped and highly choreographed trip through the backstage area that had her meticulously interacting with Brooks & Dunn, Lainey Wilson, Eric Church, Sugarland, Little Big Town and Brad Paisley, all of whom she has some kind of history with. Then, with a symbolic toss back and forth with Bill Anderson, the longest-serving Grand Ole Opry member, it kicked to the live performance and she took to a “Toy Story”-themed set to complete the number, with Keith Urban on ganjo, throwing in a snippet of her first country No. 1 from 20 years ago, “Our Song.”

Later in the show, she presented a lifetime achievement award to Brooks & Dunn.

The CMAs telecast on Wednesday night was preceded the evening before on ABC by a preview show, “Darius, Dolly and 60 Years of Celebration: Countdown to the CMAs with Robin Roberts.” That broadcast picked up 2.99 million total viewers, getting a 0.29 rating among adults 18-49 and a 0.38 among adults 25-54.

The CMAs marked Swift’s first public performance of “I Knew It, I Knew You,” after she’d previously sung it at the Motion Picture Academy’s governors ball and at private awards-circuit showcases. It may not be the last the public sees and hears of Swift doing it. She was just nominated this week for a Grammy for song of the year for the film theme, and is hoping to be nominated for a best song Oscar for the first time. It’s likely both Grammys producers and Oscar producers will be hoping for their own Swift mashups, although a possible nomination for the Academy Awards is still two months away.
