Peacock Is $2.99 a Month With This Black Friday Deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Variety may receive an affiliate commission.

After sitting out Black Friday last year, Peacock is back with a deal that brings down the price of a subscription to $2.99 a month.

The deal arrives just in time for a packed holiday programming slate on the NBCUniversal-owned streamer, which will be home to the 100th annual Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade, the National Dog Show and the Kansas City Chiefs-Buffalo Bills Thanksgiving night matchup on Thursday.

Select, the lower-priced subscription option, is best suited for Bravo devotees and fans of NBC programming, with next-day access to network staples such as the “Real Housewives” franchise, “Below Deck,” “Saturday Night Live” and “Law & Order.”

However, those looking to tune into Peacock originals such as “The Traitors,” “The Paper,” “The ‘Burbs” and “All Her Fault” will want to opt for the Premium plan, which also unlocks the streamer’s film library and live sports programming.

Peacock has bolstered its original programming slate this fall with “The Good Daughter,” the adaptation of Karin Slaughter’s bestselling novel starring Meghann Fahy and Rose Byrne, and “Dig,” a comedy series that reunites “Parks and Recreation” collaborators Amy Poehler and Mike Schur. December will see the arrival of “Ted: The Animated Series” and a two-part streaming event celebrating Ariana Grande’s “Eternal Sunshine” tour.

For sports fans, a Premium subscription also includes access to the NBA, NFL’s “Sunday Night Football,” Premier League soccer and college football, in addition to exclusive live sporting events throughout the year.

Shop Peacock’s Black Friday streaming deal through Nov. 30.
