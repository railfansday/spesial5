JPMorgan and Hyundai Card are testing AI agents that can pay without humans

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The proof-of-concept used a simulated foreign exchange trade, and the companies target the first half of 2027 for a multi-agent phase

Michael Nagle / Bloomberg via Getty Images

JPMorgan $JPM Chase and South Korea's Hyundai Card have completed a first-phase test of an AI agent handling a foreign exchange transaction without direct human intervention. Kinexys by J.P. Morgan, the bank's blockchain business unit, ran the proof-of-concept with Hyundai Card.

JPMorgan said the test took place in a test environment with no live value movement. Under a digitally signed mandate, a Hyundai Card AI agent was tasked with managing euro liquidity in the company's Kinexys Blockchain Deposit Account in Frankfurt. The agent was also to request foreign exchange executions when needed to maintain liquidity levels.

The Hyundai Card agent monitored the account balance, identified a euro shortfall and determined that a pound-to-euro conversion was required. It requested quotes and engaged Kinexys for execution. A Kinexys AI agent verified the Hyundai Card agent's identity and authority and checked the request against the mandate and applicable controls. That agent could have auto-executed the request, escalated it to a human or denied it. It auto-executed the request, and a simulated foreign exchange transaction was booked. The reasoning and decision data were captured in an audit trail that cannot be altered, JPMorgan said.

A Hyundai Card spokesperson said the test validated a model in which AI agents carry out corporate treasury management and foreign exchange transactions without direct human intervention. Hyundai Card plans to expand its work with Kinexys into new areas, such as payments in vehicles.

"Agentic commerce will not scale on autonomy alone. It will require trusted autonomy," said Wee Kee Toh, global head of Kinexys Insights at Kinexys by J.P. Morgan.

Hyundai Card wrote the scenario for the transaction and set up the framework for authorizing and controlling the agents, according to PYMNTS. Kinexys built agents for each of the two firms and layered in ways to verify identity, authorize actions and validate requests.

The companies aim to begin a more complex phase, with several AI agents working together, in the first half of 2027, according to CNBC. Toh told CNBC the team's next move is to observe how agents behave once they start dealing with one another across an ecosystem. JPMorgan has not yet picked a date for bringing the technology to market.

Toh told CNBC that the technical build was comparatively easy, while getting oversight and internal safeguards right was the tougher part. He said Kinexys confines agents to transaction limits that are set in advance. "You are going to draw a box around the agent. The agent will never be able to do anything out of the box," he said. An agent cleared to transact as much as $5 million, for example, could not carry out anything larger, Toh told CNBC.

JPMorgan and Hyundai Card are looking at whether the partnership could reach into other units of Hyundai Motor Group, Toh told CNBC. The companies have not named any particular projects.
