XRP dipped sharply overnight into last week’s value area low, the bottom of the price range where most trading happened. Buyers stepped in quickly. The token then broke above its 7-day rolling volume-weighted average price (VWAP) and is now testing last week’s VWAP.

For now, XRP’s short-term outlook depends on whether it can close above $1.29. A sustained breakout would open the way to $1.34, and possibly higher on longer timeframes. A rejection would keep XRP stuck in its current range.
