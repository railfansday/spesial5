Im super impressed by Black Friday MacBook deals: $999 Airs and $599 Neos are absolutely major

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Black Friday MacBook deals are live. And as someone who keeps an eye on MacBook prices year-round, I'm sure I speak for most shoppers when I say: finally. Between Apple's summer price hikes on the MacBook Air and MacBook Neo, and its expensive new "Pro" releases this fall, these holiday savings are a sight for sore eyes — and bank accounts.

So far, some of the MacBook deals I'm seeing are even better than I expected. Black Friday discounts on the MacBook Air peaked at $250 in 2025, but this year I've already found two configurations of Apple's ultraportable on sale for $300 to $400 off. Of special note, the 13-inch base model has dipped under $1,000 for only the second time ever since June.

Then there's the budget MacBook Neo, which is $100 off right now in every color — its biggest discount yet.

For their parts, the new M6 MacBook Pro and MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display are both on sale for the first time ahead of the holidays. Based on last year's numbers, I suspect they might see further discounts as we head into Cyber Weekend, but it's up to you to decide if you're willing to wait.

Below, I've rounded up all of the best Black Friday MacBook deals that I've spotted across the web. To make sure I'm only recommending the lowest prices available, I've cross-checked every featured model across several different retailers, including Amazon, Best Buy, B&H Photo, and Costco.

The Mashable Choice Award-winning MacBook Neo is our favorite budget laptop on the market. It has the same premium build quality as Apple's other MacBooks, and its A18 Pro processor — a pared-down iPhone chip, wouldn't you know it — makes it much faster than competing Windows PCs and Chromebooks. "For students, young people, and casual users, there's nothing else like it," said Mashable Tech Editor Timothy Beck Werth, who rated it a 4.7/5.
