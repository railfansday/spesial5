Ariana Grande Delivers Dark Old Hollywood Romance at the <i><em>Focker-in-Law </em></i>Premiere in London

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

There’s a new member of the Focker family, and she’s introducing her in-laws to some serious style. Last night, at the London premiere of Focker-in-Law at the Odeon Luxe Leicester Square theater, Ariana Grande hit the red carpet in a custom gothic look from Vivienne Westwood.

For her latest silver-screen venture, Grande stars in the newest installment of the Fockers film series, where she plays the girlfriend and soon-to-be fiancée of Henry (Skyler Gisondo), the son of Greg (Ben Stiller) and Pam Focker (Teri Polo).

In the past few years, Grande most notably starred in the world-dominating Wicked film series, which earned her an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actress. And she supported those films with fashion-heavy press tours including head-turning looks from Balmain, Giambattista Valli, vintage Yves Saint Laurent, and more.

Now, as she enters her Focker era, it appears Grande is not letting up on the red-carpet magic.

She delivered Old Hollywood glamour at the London premiere on Wednesday, in a timeless black design with a pulled-back hairdo that called to mind icons like Audrey Hepburn (a starlet she has certainly channeled before).

The Vivienne Westwood dress featured a corseted bodice and a bell skirt in smoky lace, with inky florals embroidered throughout. Up top, the neckline was draped in a sheer black shawl that drifted around Grande like vapor, and her stylist, Law Roach, completed the monochromatic look with inky patent-leather heels from Christian Louboutin.

Finally, the Swarovski ambassador turned to the aforementioned jewelry house for a pair of diamond stud earrings that served as a classy finishing touch.

You can catch Focker-in-Law in theaters in the U.S. beginning Thanksgiving Day, November 25.
