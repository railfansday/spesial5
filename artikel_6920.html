With my Luxury Firm mattress, I can feel myself sinking into the pillow top as soon as I lie down, while the supportive layers underneath help realign my body and take the pressure off my back and shoulders. I can comfortably sleep on my back or sides, and it has helped reduce body pains.

Beyond the popular Classic mattress, Saatva has a range of specialized models that cater to specific sleep concerns or lifestyle preferences, including mattresses made from organic latex and cooling memory foam options.
