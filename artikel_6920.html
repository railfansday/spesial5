Lululemon’s Black Friday Sale Started Early, and It’s Stacked

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Time to start checking off that gift list.

Of all of the brands we frequently shop, Lululemon might just be one of the most sale averse—so when the retailer announces an honest-to-goodness Black Friday sale, it’s worth paying attention. Sure, you can often score pieces from Lululemon’s “We Made Too Much” section, but that’s mostly limited to “get it before it’s gone” colors and styles.

Starting November 19, through Cyber Monday on November 30, Lululemon is offering up to half off editor-approved styles, whether you’re shopping for holiday gifts, want to replace your worn-out joggers, or are already looking ahead to your “Work out more” New Year’s resolution and need to update your gym wardrobe accordingly.

At some point, Lululemon morphed from solely being the retailer we’d turn to for workout wear to a place where you could feasibly handle most of your wardrobe needs, courtesy of the brand’s fan-favorite ABC pants, comfortable button-downs, and solid range of outerwear, all of which are included in the brand’s Black Friday markdowns.

We’ve gone so far as to dub these pants “iconic,” thanks to their versatility. They’re almost like denim in that way—easy to wear with casual pieces but also able to be dressed up in a pinch. Per Jonathan Evans, style director, the ABCs are a “smart, performance-minded essential that you can comfortably pull on every day.” Need we say more?

Of course, Lululemon’s true bread and butter will always be athleisure. Right now, editor-endorsed joggers, T-shirts, and shorts—for those who are partial to showing a little leg year-round—are available for a fraction of the price.

They’re made from abrasion-resistant, water-repellent fabric—an essential for anyone who trains outdoors. And if you’re in need of a new coat, everything from puffers to windbreakers and vests starts at $99.

And that’s not to mention gift shopping. We’re willing to bet there are multiple folks in your life who have Lululemon at the top of their wish list (especially the Gen Z crowd; you’re welcome). Shop for them early and pat yourself on the back for getting their size and color before it’s sold out.
