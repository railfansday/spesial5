Get the PenFed Gold Visa® Card if you’ll use its 0% intro rate for 15 months to pay off a big purchase or a balance you transfer within 60 days of opening the account. After that it charges 17.99%, the same rate as PenFed’s Power Cash Rewards card, which pays cash back.

The PenFed Gold Visa Card is a no-rewards card from PenFed Credit Union, and you don’t need a military tie to get it. Anyone of age can join PenFed, which opens your membership as part of the card application.

A borrower who transfers $5,000 when opening the card and pays about $344 a month clears the balance before the 0% ends, for nothing more than the $150 transfer fee. If you pay in full every month, you’d pay no interest anyway, so choose a credit card on its rewards, such as the Power Cash Rewards card.
