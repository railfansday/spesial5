Promising action-RPG, Showa American Story, has been delayed into late 2027 in order to feel "cohesive, responsive and complete" at launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

"The game's content is already completely finished".

Publisher 4Divinity and developer Nekcom Entertainment have announced that upcoming action-RPG Showa American Story will now launch on 28th October, 2027. This comes after various sites noticed a Steam page update that listed the game as launching in 2027, slipping from the previous, vague window that suggested the game would come out this year. The announcement was made on the developer's Twitter account.

"Hello everyone!" reads the message. "We finally have an official release date to share: Showa American Story will launch on 28th October, 2027. Reaching this milestone after five years of development means a lot to our entire team. With core game content now complete, we're dedicating our full focus to polishing and optimizing the experience so that when you step into Showa American Story, it feels cohesive, responsive, and complete."

The devs go on to thank interested gamers for their support, enthusiasm and patience. It's an interesting delay, because just last month, game director Xiangyu Luo told Automaton that the "game's content is already completely finished" and that the team is now in the process of "polishing its overall quality." So a year for polish? I'm pleased the team has the resources for that kind of investment.

The most recent update we had from the game was at Gamescom Opening Night live (see above), back in August. Here, the developer revealed a bit more about the game's background and gameplay, showing off the Japanese voice cast for the first time. "Showa American Story follows the journey of Choko, a young woman who suddenly revives from the dead to discover a wild world filled with zombies, monsters, and an eccentric cast of human survivors - some friendly, and others downright bad," reads a blurb for that trailer.

"Players will travel across the United States in search of answers and discover the extent of Choko's mysterious and frightening powers. This unique RPG features a rich arsenal of melee and ranged weaponry, with Choko able to turn a variety of objects into tools of carnage. Choko will face hordes of enemies in fast-paced real-time combat, leveraging weapon-specific attacks, dodges, counters, and executions with fluid precision." In other words, you can expect a game that's pitched in a similar way to the Yakuza series, albeit from a greenhorn developer without the satirical nous.
