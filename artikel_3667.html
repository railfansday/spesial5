If you’re stuck in a footwear rut, we get it. Chelsea boots are tired, sporty sneakers are frumpy and oxfords are too stiff. It’s time for a refresh, and thankfully, rich girls offer plenty of inspiration. They’re wearing these 13 fall shoe styles on repeat, and even though they look expensive, they start at only $5!

These luxe loafers, beautiful boots, must-have mules and more are incredibly polished, with elements like buttery vegan leather, intricate stitching and sleek, minimal profiles that channel old-money vibes. Whether you’re searching for the perfect fall shoes for in-office days, weekend brunches or errand runs, or if you want a wear-everywhere pair for all of the above, you’ll find it below. No matter which you pick, you’ll look like you inherited millions . . . at least.

1. Our Favorite: If sophistication is your goal, you can’t go wrong with these polished Mary Janes. They balance classic and modern, featuring a cute square toe, rich chestnut hue and dainty top strap.

2. Classy Crocodile: Everyone’s wearing croc-embossed everything these days, so naturally, these $35 loafers fit right in. They could totally pass for designer, especially with the shiny hardware embellishment.

3. Tall Boots: Dress like a California socialite in these timeless tall riding boots accented with buckles, straps and a chic mini heel. Meghan Markle and Jessica Alba are just a few rich moms embracing the silhouette this fall!

4. Hybrid Slip-ons: Easy to slip on and even easier to style, these luxe-looking shoes are a savior on busy mornings. They combine the penny strap and ruched toe of a loafer with the ease of a mule.

5. Cap-Toe Queen: Nothing says ‘Chanel’ like these slingback flats. The two-tone look elevates even a tee and jeans, so you can only imagine what it does to a blouse and trousers. Consider your office outfits covered.

7. Charleston Charm: Not only are these suede ankle booties incredibly charming, but also lengthening, thanks to the chunky block heel. They pair especially well with cropped jeans.

8. Leopard Print: When neutrals feel a little too, well, neutral, these leopard-print mules add a pop of personality. They’re fun without being tacky, and the pointed toe adds a sexy twist.

9. Polarizing Pick: Hear Us out. Even if you don’t like clogs, these cool-girl shoes will change your mind. The wooden platform is lined with shiny metal rivets for an effortlessly rich aesthetic. Memory foam is just a bonus.

11. Burgundy Babe: Nail multiple trends with these croc-embossed ankle booties. They come in a refined color that blends chocolate brown and burgundy, two of this season’s most popular hues.

12. Total Steal: It’s hard to believe these pointed-toe ballet flats are only $5, but we’re not complaining! The cream color channels quiet-luxury vibes, as do the low profile and decorative bow.

13. Sporty Style: Whether you’re walking around Florence or running around the grocery store, these Mary Jane sneakers pack support and style in one. They’re a great alternative to the traditional style and an even better swap for sneakers.
