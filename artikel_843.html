RHOP’s Karen Huger Shares Update on Ray Huger Marriage After Season 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Real Housewives of Potomac's Karen Huger exclusively told E! News at the Hunting Wives season two premiere where she stands with husband Ray Huger as they plan the holidays with daughter Rayvin.

Karen Huger has no illusions of grandeur about the future of her marriage.

While the Real House of Potomac's Grand Dame admitted viewers would have to stay tuned to see how she and husband Ray Huger continue to work on their relationship during season 11, she confessed issues with her partner of 32 years haven't gone away.

"We're going through a rough patch," Karen exclusively told E! News at the Hunting Wives season two premiere Nov. 12, crediting her ability to face their struggles to the fact that "Ray and I have always been honest."

And despite the difficulties in her relationship since her return home after serving six months in prison due to a 2024 DUI conviction, the reality star has a positive outlook about their family unit with daughter Rayvin Huger.

As Karen explained, "The one thing is, we will always be a family, and we will always be friends."

And because of the couple's unbreakable bond, their plans for the holidays remain a family affair where the motto is the more the merrier.

"We're going to celebrate as a family," Karen shared. "In fact, we are going down to Virginia, near the guest house that I just renovated, to celebrate with our entire family."

As for how the 63-year-old feels about her equally complicated relationship with costar Wendy Osefo?

"I'm hoping for the best for Wendy. I'm always in her corner," Karen remarked. "We are supporting her 100 percent and wishing for the best."

Aside from her Bravo friend group, Karen is also a big fan of the women of Maple Brook, Texas, with the second season of Hunting Wives arriving on Netflix Nov. 26. In fact, she effused about her favorite actress in the drama, Brittany Snow.

As Karen put it, "Brittany Snow? Love, love, love. Sign me up. I'm ready to kill someone, OK."

With that in mind, Karen is open to trying out her acting chops alongside the Hunting Wives cast, musing when it comes to a future cameo, "All things are possible."
