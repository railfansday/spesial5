Maya Jama Steps Down As Love Island UK Host After 9 Seasons

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Love Island UK host Maya Jama announced she’s exiting the dating reality series after nine seasons, sharing the news in a lengthy statement: “All good things must come to an end.”

The most iconic bombshell is leaving the Villa.

Love Island UK host Maya Jama revealed she’s decided not to return to the dating series after nine seasons, reflecting on her journey on the ITV reality show in a heartfelt statement.

“What an incredible era and what an unforgettable time I’ve had, it’s been far too much fun to put into words or a video,” the British TV presenter wrote in a Nov. 8 Instagram post, “but as all good things must come to an end, it’s my time to step down as the host of my beloved Love Island.”

Alongside a video compilation of memorable moments over the years, Jama—who served as host on the ITV series and the companion show Love Island Aftersun, along with spinoffs Love Island Games and Love Island: All-Stars—expressed her gratitude toward those who supported her throughout her experience.

“I want to thank all of the people who rooted so hard for me to host the show in the first place. I wouldn’t change a thing,” she wrote. “To the fans of the show, thank you for every supportive message and every single time you’ve tuned in during my time as host, I’m forever grateful to you all.”

Jama added, “The show really does belong to the people who watch it and embrace it year after year.”

After thanking the “incredible production team,” the 32-year-old—who took over hosting duties in 2023 after Laura Whitmore’s exit—shared insight into her years with the “iconic” reality TV show franchise.

“I started hosting the show aged 28 and during the 9 incredible series met the most wonderful humans along the way,” Jama recalled. “I’ve loved every slow-mo walk, fire pit and live show. I’ll forever be grateful for the experience.”

While the Celebrity Traitors star didn’t go into detail about her decision to step away from Love Island, she gave a glimpse at her mindset.

“I’ve always believed that to truly grow, you have to take a leap every now and then,” she wrote, “and now is my time to throw myself into something new.”

Jama concluded her goodbye message with a touching sentiment, emphasizing that she’ll continue to be a fan.

“While my own Love Island journey has come to an end,” she wrote, “I wish the show and the team all the success in the world, I’ll be cheering you lot on from the sidelines forever.”

The Gentlemen actress summarized her thoughts while reposting her Reel on her Instagram Stories, writing, “I feel emotional,” adding that it's an “end of an era.”
