Freeman and Smith expected to be fit for All Blacks rematch

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Tommy Freeman and Fin Smith have been included in England's squad to face New Zealand at Twickenham; Freeman was ruled out of England's 37-12 victory against the All Blacks on Saturday with a quad issue but is expected to have recovered in time for Saturday's game

Tommy Freeman and Fin Smith are expected to be fit for Nations Championship finals weekend, having been included in England's squad to face New Zealand at Twickenham on Saturday.

Freeman was ruled out of last weekend's 37-12 victory against the same opposition shortly before kick-off after pulling up with a quad issue, but is expected to be in contention for a starting spot in the third-place match on Saturday.

Smith was seen putting ice on his legs after being substituted, but is also expected to retain his place at No 10 after recovering from cramp.

Elsewhere, head coach Steve Borthwick has added Saracens' Noah Caluori to the squad, with the winger potentially in line to make his third Test appearance for England.

England will hope for a repeat performance to seal third place in the inaugural Nations Championship, having proved far too strong for New Zealand at the weekend. The match takes place a day before France play South Africa at Twickenham in the final.

England's dominant win last time out has impacted the global rankings, with Borthwick's side climbing from fifth to fourth and the All Blacks plummeting from second to fifth.

Borthwick is set to name his 15-man starting lineup on Wednesday, with a sixth successive Test victory the target as England look to confirm their emergence as World Cup contenders next year.
