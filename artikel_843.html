The NFL's biggest game of the season is here - it could break records

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Watch the NFL's Thanksgiving triple-header live on Sky Sports from 5pm on Thursday, starting with Chicago Bears at Detroit Lions, followed by the Philadelphia Eagles against the Dallas Cowboys and Patrick Mahomes' Kansas City Chiefs vs Josh Allen's Buffalo Bills from 1.20am on Friday

It has become an era-defining rivalry in the NFL, and now the Kansas City Chiefs and Buffalo Bills will meet yet again in the biggest game of the regular season on one of football's most-watched stages.

Patrick Mahomes and Josh Allen will face off for the 11th time when they headline the NFL's Thanksgiving schedule on Thursday night, live on Sky Sports from 1.20am in the early hours of Friday.

Once more they are positioned neck and neck at the forefront of the NFL's playoff race, once more they will trade the greatness that has governed football for the best part of a decade.

Together they have forged modern football's answer to Tom Brady vs Peyton Manning. Two generational quarterbacks, dictating one another's story, time and time again, although Allen would prefer if the outcome had been a little more even by now.

Mahomes, the driver of a Chiefs dynasty and so often the AFC's Super Bowl gateway; and Allen, the starved nearly man banging down on the Championship door only to see dominant talent blunted by his Kansas City counterpart.

Last season's Thanksgiving Day match-up between the Chiefs and the Dallas Cowboys became the most-viewed regular-season NFL game in history with an audience of 57.230 million. This might well shatter that.

Allen arrives 5-1 in their regular-season head-to-head record, while Mahomes leads the rivalry 4-0 where it matters most - in the playoffs. Five of their last six match-ups have also been decided by one score, their most recent seeing the Bills win 28-21 in November 2025.

Mahomes and the Chiefs defeated Allen's Bills in the postseason four times from 2020-2024, including two AFC Championship Games and two Divisional Round match-ups. And when the Chiefs missed out on the playoffs for the first time since 2014 last season, Buffalo failed to capitalise.

Football swings in waves by way of a league that craves parity and a system structured for the purpose of emerging contenders, new storylines, fresh obstacles, unpredictable risers and fallers.

But every once in a while sport throws in a notion of consistency, an unwavering narrative that shall assert itself as the spine of an entire chapter in its history; Mahomes and Allen remain that constant at the forefront of league discussion and debate.

Who is the best? Who is No 1? Mahomes boasts the resume with three Super Bowl rings, five Super Bowl appearances, seven straight AFC Championship Game appearances from 2018-2024 and the angle-defiant, off-script absurdity to arm talent challenging every boundary in the game during that period.

But while Allen lacks the same accolades, the frequency at which he has carried his Bills outfit through the air and on the ground in critical moments as football's most obvious decider, equaliser and game-wrecker has tossed him into perennial MVP status; there may be no team who struggles as dearly were they to lose their quarterback than the Bills with Allen.

Their rivalry is perhaps best epitomised by January 2022's iconic 13-second miracle. With 1.54 remaining Mahomes connected with Tyreek Hill for a 64-yard touchdown, before Allen hit back with a scoring strike to Gabriel Davis that would see Buffalo lead 36-33 with... 13 seconds left.

The game was over. Until it wasn't. Until teams across the NFL were frightened into ever believing they had beaten Mahomes and his Chiefs until the clock hit zero. It was the greatest lesson in the importance of aggression against a team that, at its peak, thrived in the face of shoot-outs.

Mahomes connected on passes to both Hill and Travis Kelce to put the Chiefs in field goal range, where Harrison Butker broke Bills hearts with a 49-yard kick to send the game to overtime. Kansas City would win the toss and then the game as Mahomes returned to Kelce for the deciding touchdown. Allen had been 13 seconds from the AFC Championship Game, but for those pesky Chiefs.

The Bills are 9-1 as they prepare to host the first game between the two teams at Buffalo's new Highmark Stadium, where the Chiefs will arrive 8-2 in their bounce-back season after last January's playoff omission.

Mahomes and the Chiefs have rediscovered the offensive spark absent for the last two or three years, their quarterback feasting on the back of last December's season-ending surgery-requiring knee injury and Super Bowl MVP Kenneth Walker III having ignited Andy Reid's attack since signing in free agency.

The Chiefs offense ranks No 1 in yards per game through 10 weeks, while sitting fourth in passing, fifth in rushing and seventh in scoring following Sunday's 34-7 win over the Arizona Cardinals.

Mahomes has thrown for a sixth-most 2,709 yards for 22 touchdowns to just four interceptions with a third-ranked passer rating of 108.3. With Sunday's performance he has now gone four straight games without throwing a pick, while combining for 1,235 yards and 13 touchdown passes.

He has looked a scary reminder of his former self with tight-window aggression, off-platform invention and out-of-structure magic to enter the Comeback Player of the Year race.

Allen is, meanwhile, riding a six-game winning streak without, in his eyes, playing his best football. He has gone 194 of 302 passing (64.2 per cent) for 2,527 yards and 14 touchdowns to nine interceptions with a passer rating of 93.5, having thrown two picks in Sunday's 26-9 win over the Miami Dolphins.

Allen also leads all quarterbacks with 290 rushing yards and 11 touchdowns on the ground, though he played a full game without attempting to run the ball for the first time in his career at the weekend.

While it will be the two quarterbacks who take centre stage, defining factors lie elsewhere. Particularly on the ground.

Kenneth Walker III has missed the last two games through injury after spearheading the Chiefs with 872 rushing yards and seven touchdowns in eight games. But in his absence fifth-round rookie Emmett Johnson has emerged as a key contributor with 56 touches, 314 yards and three touchdowns over two weeks.

In the opposite backfield James Cook remains instrumental to Joe Brady's Bills attack with 1,060 rushing yards (second in the NFL) for 10 touchdowns from 209 carries. He enters Thursday having rushed for at least 75 yards and a touchdown in an NFL-record nine successive games, and has now surpassed 1,000 yards in four straight campaigns.

Buffalo's prime question marks this season came on defense and whether they had the unit and the personnel to stop football's best teams in the playoffs.

They hired Jim Leonhard as their new defensive coordinator during the offseason and currently rank 11th in total yards allowed and ninth in scoring having not allowed a touchdown in 43 drives since Derrick Henry's rushing score in their win over the Ravens on November 1, albeit having played the Vikings, Jets and Dolphins since then. Still, it can be considered progress for a defense that shipped 28.5 points on average across the first six games.

Steve Spagnuolo's Chiefs defense is, meanwhile, fifth in yards allowed and second in scoring to underline what is currently one of football's most complete teams.

With that being said, the Chiefs' only win against a team with a winning record through 10 weeks was back in Week One against the now 8-2 Denver Broncos.

The Bills have played just two teams sitting among the 14 available playoff spots in the Detroit Lions and the Minnesota Vikings, while their last four outings have seen them encounter back-up or third-string quarterbacks in Miami's Kyle McCord, the New York Jets' Cade Klubnik, Minnesota's Carson Wentz and Max Brosmer and Baltimore's Joe Fagnano, who replaced Lamar Jackson following his thumb injury on the first play from scrimmage in Buffalo's 36-10 win.

"You can see Mahomes stand with more conviction in the pocket. He's not as skittish in the pocket as he was initially, which is natural when you're coming back from a lower leg injury like he had," said Sky Sports NFL's Jeff Reinebold.

"Greg Rousseau and Ed Oliver are going to have to dominate the line of scrimmage. That's one place on the field that I would say Buffalo's defense has an edge against Kansas City. Rousseau is having a tremendous year as an edge rusher. They're going to have to find answers for him. And then Ed Oliver gives them that inside push that you always want to have against Patrick Mahomes.

"I think that's going to be the key because I don't know if Buffalo's back end is built to cover the Kansas City receivers and primarily I think Kelsey's going to be the biggest factor because Buffalo plays a lot of zone and when you play a lot of zone, Kelsey can have a night against you."

Kansas City's wake-up call was in Week Seven when they suffered a 20-3 loss to the Super Bowl champion Seattle Seahawks. Mahomes and co now enter a defining period of their own campaign with Matthew Stafford and Myles Garrett's Los Angeles Rams, Joe Burrow's Cincinnati Bengals and Brock Purdy's San Francisco 49ers coming up before the end of the year.

Mahomes leads the NFL with 33,267 passing yards since Allen's first full season as starter in 2019, with the Bills quarterback ranked third behind Jared Goff with 2,640. In that same period Mahomes is first with 239 passing touchdowns followed by Allen's 224, while Allen tops all quarterbacks with 92 wins (126 games) to Mahomes' 90 (119 games).

It is perfectly-scheduled and perfectly-poised as a test of who both teams are entering the home stretch towards the playoffs. It could be the best Thanksgiving Day game ever. It might be the most-watched regular-season NFL game in history.
