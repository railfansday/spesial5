Man arrested after 26 “critically endangered” turtles found in baggage at Amsterdam airport

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

A man has been arrested at Amsterdam’s Schiphol Airport after customs officers found over 20 critically endangered turtles in his checked luggage.

According to the BBC, the man was flying from Mexico to Hong Kong via Amsterdam when officers discovered 26 critically endangered Vallarta mud turtles.

The Netherlands Food and Consumer Product Safety Authority said most of the tiny turtles were found in a box with a toy car, while the others were tucked inside a sock in a suitcase.

Every turtle had been wrapped in duct tape to keep them from moving, but they have since been taken to a specialized rescue center, where staff carefully removed the tape.

Dutch authorities said the turtles were “presumably stolen” from the wild in their native home of Mexico. Vallarta mud turtles are thought to be the smallest freshwater turtles in the world, growing only as big as four inches.

The Netherlands Food and Consumer Product Safety Authority (NVWA) said the wild population may have fallen by nearly 10% if all 26 turtles had been taken.

As of 2025, it’s believed that only 300 Vallarta turtles remain in the wild.

According to Reuters, the U.S.-based Center for Biological Diversity said in October that Vallarta mud turtles can sell for up to $30,000 each, as it called on Mexico’s government to do more to protect the species.

This isn’t the only time that Amsterdam’s Schiphol Airport has made headlines this year, as back in May six poison dart frogs were found abandoned in a bathroom and handed over to the same Dutch agency that seized the turtles.
