Opinion: Julian Ward has found him, Liverpool's perfect left-back is finally here and he's unreal

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

This African sensation is bound to be Liverpool's next left-back.

When you glance upon the pantheon of greats at Anfield, Andy Robertson deserves a place right at the very top of the list.

For almost a decade he was a reliable servant, a staunch and passionate fighter for the badge and sensationally electrifying as he sped past his opponents with ease.

Robertson was the kind of player no attacker really wanted to face. In his prime, he was the best left-back in the world. He deserves an honour to be along the best left-backs to have every played the game - especially in the Premier League.

It's crazy that just a few months after his departure, Liverpool are already yearning for a player of his ilk to return to the club.
