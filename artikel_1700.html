Transfer news LIVE: Arsenal told Kroupi decision; Liverpool done deal; Man Utd twist; Chelsea latest

The January transfer window is approaching fast, with Arsenal, Manchester United, Chelsea, Liverpool and Tottenham all striving to get some early signings over the line. Arsenal have been told Bournemouth’s decision over selling top target Eli Junior Kroupi, while they continue to scout Antonio Nusa amid new links to the likes of Carlos Forbs and Tornike Kvaratskhelia.

Liverpool have agreed a deal to sign Senegal wonderkid Sidy Barhama Ndiaye, while they are also said to be contemplating a surprise return to Anfield for Jarell Quansah. Manchester United are looking at both Calvin Bassey and Nathan Collins as they try to strengthen their dreadful defence, with a new striker also high on Mauricio Pochettino’s shortlist with a rumoured £50million budget - including Dominic Calvert-Lewin, who has agreed a new contract with Leeds.

Chelsea are renewing their push to win the fierce battle for Alex Scott as Paris Saint-Germain pursue Estevao, while Tottenham have signed former West Ham youngster Isaac Allen ahead of Arsenal as they also scout Vangelis Pavlidis and Hakan Calhanoglu. Follow the latest transfer news, rumours and gossip live below!

Richarlison is open to moving to Saudi Arabia to end his Tottenham exile.

The striker has been frozen out at Spurs by manager Roberto De Zerbi after failing to agree a summer exit.

Everton, Vasco da Gama and Trabzonspor were all said to have explored deals for Richarlison, who ended up staying put at Spurs despite being left out of De Zerbi's Premier League squad.

Manchester United fans will be dismayed by reports that Mauricio Pochettino will have just £50million to improve his squad during the January transfer window.

A leaky defence has led to calls from ex-players such as Gary Neville for significant investment, but it appears Man Utd will try to do their business on a tight budget in January.

The second window of the season often leads to inflated prices, with clubs capitalising upon other teams’ struggles and desperate need for new players.

Omar Marmoush has hailed Luca Williams-Barnett as “unbelievable” and believes the teen sensation has a “very promising future ahead of him”.

The 18-year-old scored one, and set up another, on his full debut for Tottenham in their 2-0 home win against Ipswich last weekend.

Williams-Barnett has since signed a new deal to commit his future to Spurs until 2031.

Tosin Adarabioyo has said that “no-one can chat s***” about his former Chelsea team-mate Cole Palmer despite the England international’s struggles this season.

Palmer has scored five times in his 13 appearances across the Premier League and Carabao Cup this term, adding three assists in the top flight.

Sandro Tonali has hailed Tottenham team-mate Destiny Udogie as “one of the best” left-backs.

The Italian pair were already familiar with one another before Tonali’s big-money move to north London from Newcastle United in the summer for a fee worth £100million.

They had first played together in at Under-21 level on the international stage, before reuniting for the senior Azzurri side and at club level.

Dominic Calvert-Lewin has shut down all rumours of a Leeds United exit by signing a new contract at Elland Road.

The striker had been linked with a move away from the Yorkshire club - with several outlets identifying him as a potential target for Manchester United - but he will remain a White after penning a new two-and-a-half-year deal.

Paris Saint-Germain could push to sign Chelsea star Estevao or rival Arsenal for Antonio Nusa, reports claim.

Reigning back-to-back European champions PSG are on the lookout for further attacking reinforcements in the January transfer window as they seek to ensure another trophy-laden campaign under Luis Enrique.

According to Diario AS in Spain, the French giants are interested in offering the little-used Estevao an escape route from Stamford Bridge.

The Brazilian is said to feature on a shortlist along with Arsenal target Nusa - of RB Leipzig - and Bayern Munich’s Luis Diaz.

Jarell Quansah could make a surprise return to Liverpool.

The versatile defender has continued to flourish since leaving Anfield for Bayer Leverkusen in a £35m deal in the summer of 2025, impressing in the Bundesliga and becoming a favoured option of England boss Thomas Tuchel.

That has led to reported interest in a swift Premier League comeback from the likes of Arsenal and Chelsea.

According to TEAMtalk, Liverpool are keeping a close eye on Quansah’s progress in Germany and haven’t ruled out activating a buy-back clause that is valid until next summer and believed to be valued at just over £50m.

Teenage defender Isaac Allen has confirmed that he has joined Tottenham after leaving rivals West Ham in the summer, writes Sam Tabuteau.

In August, reports claimed that Spurs had beaten Arsenal to Allen’s signature, and the 15-year-old has now announced the move on social media.

The England U15s captain wrote in a post on Instagram: “Happy to be signed with Tottenham.”

Allen, who can operate either at centre-back or in defensive midfield, is regarded as one of the most promising young talents in England and his arrival continues Spurs’ busy youth recruitment strategy.

Tottenham look to be keeping a close eye on Benfica striker Vangelis Pavlidis.

Spurs are known to be on the lookout for a new forward ahead of the January transfer window to help boost Roberto De Zerbi’s inconsistent attack.

They have been linked with a swoop for Pavlidis for some time now, with Portuguese outlet Record reporting that they dispatched scouts to watch the Greek international in action again this week.
