Opinion: The next Michael Olise could now be coming to Liverpool, he is extraordinary

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Liverpool could be signing the next Michael Olise, he is an incredibly special player.

In the evolution of the Reds, the giant gaping hole in the squad is obvious for everyone to see.

Liverpool frantically tried to plug the gap in the summer yearning for the commitment of Yan Diomande before seeking out the likes of Yankuba Minteh and Ismaila Sarr to no avail.

With the banishment of Mo Salah to pastures new, the Reds no longer have a recognised left-footed attacker in the squad.

Liverpool have four wingers. But all of them are inclined to use their right foot and prefer to play on the left-wing.

It is why Liverpool's attack has looked so one dimensional under Andoni Iraola so far. The Reds' attack is far too predictable and the way to change it is by adding a left-footed attacker.

Who could be Liverpool's Mo Salah heir?Minteh is an option for the Reds. The only obstacle in the club's way over the summer was Brighton's obdurate stance to demand an £80m fee for a player who was still out injured for the next few months. Minteh has since returned to action and has even excelled for The Gambia scoring in a 2-2 draw with the Ivory Coast. Naturally, he is not the only left-footed option for Liverpool though. There's the dream target. Michael Olise. Liverpool fans would do a lot to try and lure him to Anfield and if he Reds can cough up the money and make a convincing appeal there is no reason as to why he would not be open to a move. But there is another avenue for Julian Ward and his recruitment team. They could be signing the next Olise instead, who looks quite special.
