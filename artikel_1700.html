Sorloth important to Atletico’s vision, insists Simeone

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Diego Simeone insisted Alexander Sorloth is important to Atletico Madrid's vision, despite a slow start to the 2026-27 season.

Sorloth has made just seven appearances across all competitions this term, with only one of those coming as a starter, after missing the start of the campaign through injury.

The 30-year-old is yet to score or assist for Atleti in 2026-27; he has scored 20+ goals in each of his last three seasons.

Sorloth came on as a substitute in Atleti's 3-0 LaLiga win over Getafe at the weekend, but missed two Opta-defined big chances in his 15-minute cameo, though he did win a penalty late on which Alejandro Grimaldo converted.

Nevertheless, ahead of a must-win Champions League encounter with Viking on Wednesday, Simeone backed the Atleti striker to start scoring soon.

"Sorloth's future isn't for me to decide; he has to," said Simeone. I see the plans for him; we don't have another player with his characteristics.

"When he's fit, he's a huge asset to the team – his power in open spaces, his ability to create scoring opportunities.

"The other day he could have scored two or three goals in 20 minutes. I don't mind if he plays 20 minutes or 70; he'd probably prefer to play 70, but we try to manage things as best we can because he's important to our vision for the team.

"Alex is used to situations where he scores more goals and others where he scores fewer.

"He's training well, with enthusiasm and excitement; [Viking] will be important because he's hosting a team from his country, and hopefully he can do well."

Atleti won their first six European games against Norwegian opposition but did lose the seventh and most recent in January of this year, at home to Bodo/Glimt in the Champions League.

Simeone's side have won just one of their last six group stage matches in the Champions League (D2 L3), having won nine of their previous 11 (L2).

Their three defeats in their last five group games are only one fewer than in their previous 21 (W14 D3 L4).

"There are still four games left. We need to start picking up points to try and finish in the top eight, or at least as high up as possible," added Simeone.

"Both teams need to win, we're playing at home, and we'll have to try to take advantage of that situation with our fans to help and support us, and play the game knowing what we need."
