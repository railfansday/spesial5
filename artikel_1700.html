Watch: Milan starlet Akram Jadid scores five-minute hat-trick for Morocco U17s

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

AC Milan talent Akram Jadid is making a name for himself on the international stage, with a hat-trick for Morocco U17s.

At the Stadio Olimpico in Sousse, the Milan U18s striker scored a hat-trick in the 5-1 win over Tunisia. Not only that, but he got his three goals in just five minutes. It was a performance that quickly gained traction on social media, where videos of his goals are circulating widely.

The match opened with Morocco taking the lead through Hatim Dabdoubi in the 24th minute. After half-time, Mohamed Aziz Abdelbaki levelled things on the hour mark, before Aymane Aït Hammou restored the lead with six minutes to go.

Then Jadid entered the scene. He netted 89th minute with his left foot, converted a penalty to make it 4-1 in the 92nd minute and completed his hat-trick in the 94th minute, scoring with his right foot.

He is one to look out for in the future, with promotion to the Primavera and eventually the Futuro seeming like a certainty at this point. He has trained with the first team, and went viral for having scored over 700 goals in the youth teams to this point.
