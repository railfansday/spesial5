Buffalo Bills elevate WR Trent Sherfield Sr., S Geno Stone for Week 9 of ‘MNF’

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Buffalo Bills have announced a series of roster moves in the hours leading up to their Week 9 game in primetime against the Minnesota Vikings. Buffalo has elevated wide receiver Trent Sherfield Sr. and safety Geno Stone.

This is the second consecutive elevation for Stone, who played 19 defensive snaps (37%) in Week 8 against the Baltimore Ravens. Stone made six tackles, which included two solo tackles and one tackle for loss. He also contributed on special teams, logging 16 snaps (59%) against Baltimore.

Sherfield also sees his second elevation this season, having previously been elevated Week 5 for Buffalo's road game against the Los Angeles Rams. He logged seven offensive snaps (11%), but didn't register a reception or yards. Sherfield made a bigger impact on special teams, where he was on the field for 17 snaps (59%), making one solo tackle and one combined tackle.

One Bills Drive also placed wide receiver Joshua Palmer on Injured Reserve (IR), which will sideline him for at least the next four regular-season games. With Palmer heading to IR before the trade deadline, it means he won't be subjected to an in-season trade.

Palmer was injured in action against the Ravens in Week 8. Through six games in 2026, Palmer has managed just four receptions (12 targets) for 117 yards, with two touchdowns and three of his receptions going for first-down yardage.
