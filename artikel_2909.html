Milwaukee Bucks vs. Philadelphia 76ers Preview & Game Thread: Slay the dragon?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Milwaukee Bucks are still hunting their second win of the season and are facing a monster in the Philadelphia 76ers. The Bucks are on a 12-game losing streak heading into this game, and the 76ers are on a four-game winning streak. That being said, the first matchup of the season between these teams saw the Bucks put up a solid performance, with the game as close as four points in the final two minutes. Unfortunately, the Bucks let it slip late, falling 127-116. The 76ers have also been pushed in games recently and haven't been winning as dominantly as you'd expect, given their roster or ranking.

The 76ers are a good team, but I don't think they are as good as their record says. They've found themselves in a lot of close games against teams they should have breezed past. Philly relies heavily on their star power, and the key will be to slow down those stars. If the Bucks can share the load, run a deep lineup, and play a team-centric brand of basketball, this one could go the distance. With Jaylen Brown out and Tyrese Maxey potentially out, the task becomes more achievable. Milwaukee won't be able to beat Philadelphia on pure isolation basketball—that's their thing—but sharing the load and wearing the 76ers down throughout the game will create issues.
