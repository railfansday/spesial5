Wolverines in the NFL: Lions’ Derrick Moore steps up for Aidan Hutchinson

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Former Michigan Wolverines delivered touchdowns, another multi-sack performance and contributions across special teams in Week 10 of the NFL season. But the biggest news was a difficult development for one of the program's most recognizable NFL stars.

Here's how Michigan's former players fared through Sunday night.

Blake Corum turned 17 carries into 100 yards and a touchdown in the Rams' 34-6 victory over the Arizona Cardinals. He averaged 5.9 yards per carry and added three receptions for 19 yards, finishing with 119 total yards. It was his sixth game in a row with a touchdown.

It was the kind of productive, balanced afternoon Michigan fans grew accustomed to seeing during his time in Ann Arbor. Corum handled more carries than fellow Rams running back Kyren Williams, who finished with 12 attempts for 23 yards.

Two other former Wolverines contributed to the victory: Josaiah Stewart recorded one tackle, and Josh Wallace finished with two.

Edge rusher Derrick Moore recorded two sacks for the second consecutive game in Detroit's 35-10 win over New England in Munich, Germany. The rookie now has four sacks across his last two outings, giving the Lions another productive option up front.

Unfortunately, Detroit's victory came with a major loss. Aidan Hutchinson recorded a first-quarter sack before injuring his knee while celebrating. Initial testing revealed a torn ACL that will end his 2026 season.

The injury cuts short another season after his broken leg in 2024. But for Moore, his recent production takes on added significance as Detroit moves forward without its established pass rusher.

Nico Collins caught nine passes for 94 yards and a touchdown in Houston's 27-24 loss to Cleveland. He drew a career-high 16 targets and reached the end zone for the second consecutive game, following his touchdown against the Chargers a week earlier.

Texans tight end Marlin Klein played 17 snaps across offense and special teams. For Cleveland, Mason Graham made one tackle, and Zak Zinter played six special teams snaps.

Elsewhere, on Thursday Night Football, J.J. McCarthy completed 23-of-36 passes for 293 yards, one touchdown and one interception in the Giants' 24-21 loss to Washington. He added 34 rushing yards on six attempts. Jon Runyan played 65 offensive snaps, while Dominic Zvada converted all three extra points and missed his lone field-goal attempt. Washington's Mike Sainristil played 14 special teams snaps.

Cincinnati's 38-18 victory over Pittsburgh featured three tackles and a pass breakup from Daxton Hill, three tackles from Kris Jenkins, and two from DJ Turner. Long snapper William Wagner handled two field goals, five extra points and two punts.

Jake Moody made both field-goal attempts, with a long of 38 yards, and his only extra point in Atlanta's 35-13 loss to Kansas City.

In Seattle's 21-13 win in Las Vegas, AJ Barner caught three passes for 19 yards. Mike Morris also saw action, while Kwity Paye finished with five tackles for the Raiders.

Josh Metellus made five tackles in Minnesota's 30-10 win over Green Bay, with Max Bredeson also taking the field for the Vikings.

Josh Uche recorded four tackles in Miami's 24-22 loss to Indianapolis, and Kenneth Grant had two tackles for the Dolphins.

Jaishawn Barham had two tackles and a quarterback hit, and Luke Schoonmaker had a catch for 12 yards in Dallas' 46-40 loss to San Francisco.

Jourdan Lewis had three tackles in Jacksonville's 40-23 win over Tennessee.
