Steve Kerr shuts down idea of Draymond Green coming off Warriors' bench

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Steve Kerr shuts down idea of Draymond Green coming off Warriors' bench originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

The Warriors are reeling, and searching for answers to snap a four-game losing streak while their superstar, Steph Curry, is sidelined for at least another week.

Coach Steve Kerr is evaluating all options to aid his team after Golden State's 116-94 loss to the Dallas Mavericks on Wednesday, except for taking Draymond Green out of the starting lineup.

"No, no. He's our best defensive player," Kerr said when asked about the possibility. "And that's not even a thought."

Of Green's 801 games played since Kerr took over before the 2014-15 NBA season, he only has come off the bench in seven of them.

The Warriors have struggled offensively since Curry's injury. In the first 12 games, Golden State averaged 119.3 points per game, third-best in the NBA during that span. In the last three games without Curry, the Warriors have not scored more than 94 points.

Green is averaging just 3.2 points per game, his lowest average since his rookie season. Despite that, he's still formidable in his typical offensive role as facilitator, leading the team with 6.5 assists per game.

And even if Green isn't scoring, Kerr does not want to forfeit his defensive ability in his starting group. Because of that, Kerr has to find solutions elsewhere.

"You have to experiment when you're in a state that we are right now; missing so many guys and trying to find combinations that haven't played together before," Kerr explained. "So, you're trying to find things that click."

Kerr inserted center Charles Bassey into the starting lineup Wednesday. It worked, in the sense that the Warriors only were down 10 points after the first quarter, as opposed to the 25- and 24-point deficits they faced after one quarter in their previous two games.

"We wanted to go bigger tonight with Charles and give Yaxel [Lendeborg] that lob threat and give Draymond help at the rim. He was going to guard Flagg and so we needed some help behind him," Kerr said, explaining his lineup decision. "And so, yeah, we tried something different, but this is just where we are right now."

Green's low scoring with Curry sidelined isn't a new trend. The Warriors weren't relying on Green to score during their eight-game win streak.

The two top scorers behind Curry, Brandin Podziemski and Lendeborg, have struggled in the star's absence. Podziemski and Lendeborg are shooting 32.3 and 29.6 percent from the field, respectively, in their last three games.

Kerr will continue to look for options to get the offense flowing more efficiently through Lendeborg and Podziemski, aside from sitting the team's best passer and defensive anchor.

"We're wiped out with injuries and the guys are really, really competing and playing hard and staying together, and that's all we can do," Kerr said. "Things are going to turn, but we've got to give ourselves the best chance to help them turn."

The Warriors will have their next chance to turn their skid quickly, as they prepare to face the Memphis Grizzlies Thursday night on the back end of a back-to-back to wrap up their tumultuous five-game road trip.
