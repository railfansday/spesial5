Nate Sheppard's last hurrah at home? Duke faces that reality | Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Manny Diaz has already confirmed that he will return to Duke for a fourth season, continuing the success that he has helped bring to the Blue Devils' program.

The massive question facing Diaz and all of Duke is this: will Nate Sheppard be back as well?

In reality, Friday night's matchup against Clemson could be the last time Sheppard suits up in a Duke uniform in Wallace Wade Stadium. The nation's leader in all-purpose yards and, in many people's opinion, including mine, the best running back in the country, could be bidding farewell to the Blue Devils following the season, as top programs will throw NIL money at him that Duke can't match. We saw it happen this past offseason with Darian Mensah.

Of course, Sheppard could return to Durham and continue building his legacy, one that continues to rise in the ranks as one of the all-time greats in Duke history. He already owns plenty of program records, but with one more season before the NFL eventually comes calling, Sheppard could ensure his name is etched into Duke football lore forever. That could be motive enough for a player who seems to have a great head on his young shoulders and might be thankful to a program that gave him a chance when others wouldn't.

But this is the new era of college football, and whether you like it or not, Sheppard has earned the right to seek his maximum value on the market, and after this season, that has risen to a massive number. Don't think for a second that Lane Kiffin and LSU have already thought of poaching the Louisiana native away from Duke.

So, if this is Sheppard's last game in front of home fans, they should enjoy every carry, cut, broken tackle and touchdown dash that he is bound to do against Clemson on Friday night. If Miami's top-ranked rushing defense has no answer for him, then it's possible that no one does. And if he's stuffed in the running game, he'll still be a receiving threat. Just ask Mario Cristobal and his Hurricanes. When Sheppard gets going, he's unstoppable.

If Duke fans want to encourage him to return, chants of "one more year!" would be appropriate. So would a standing ovation after every touchdown or highlight-reel run. The Blue Devils haven't seen many running backs, if any, as talented, hard-working and magical as Sheppard.

If this is his last hurrah, appreciate it. I know I will.
