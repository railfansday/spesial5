CIA hit by cyber breach that exposed unclassified data on intelligence analysis network: sources

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Fox News chief national security correspondent Jennifer Griffin has the latest on a cybersecurity breach involving an unclassified system on 'The Story.'

A cyber breach exposed unclassified information on a CIA network last week, the same day the FBI was hit by a cyberattack on a database used by law enforcement agencies across the country.

Multiple sources told Fox News that the CIA, the Office of the Director of National Intelligence and the FBI were breached on the same day.

The New York Times first reported that the CIA and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence discovered the breach.

FBI PROBES SECOND CYBERATTACK IN MONTHS AS HACKERS BREACH KEY CRIME DATABASE, SOURCES SAY

A cyber breach hit the CIA the same day the FBI last week. (Saul Loeb/AFP via Getty Images, File)

The CIA system was used to analyze open-source intelligence, which draws on publicly available information. No classified systems were affected, and the issue has been resolved, sources said.

RETIRED FBI AGENT WARNS NCIC BREACH COULD THREATEN POLICE SAFETY DURING TRAFFIC STOPS

It remains unclear who was behind the CIA breach or whether the intrusions were connected.  (Samuel Corum/AFP via Getty Images, File)

Fox News chief national security correspondent Jennifer Griffin reported that the FBI breach was more serious because it affected the National Crime Information Center, a database law enforcement agencies use to share and look up information.

TECH OUTAGE CASCADE EXPOSES US VULNERABILITIES TO FOREIGN CYBER THREATS

It remains unclear who was behind the CIA breach or whether the intrusions were connected.

Two Pentagon officials told Fox News that the Department of War was not breached.

Griffin also reported that a separate outage affected an internal classified system at the National Security Agency for several days last week. That outage stemmed from an internal technology upgrade.
