Jurgen Klopp loves him but Brajan Gruda may never play for Brighton again

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Brajan Gruda arrived at Brighton in 2024 as one of Europe's hottest prospects.

Albion paid around £25m to Mainz for the German playmaker as he joined on a four-year contract.

"I saw the big impact he made in the Bundesliga and he's a player I admire a lot," said Brighton’s German head coach Fabian Hurzeler at the time.

Hurzeler also admired Gruda's style of play and describe the then 20-year-old as a "street footballer."

Jurgen Klopp used the same "street baller" term this week as Gruda - now 22 - made an impressive start to his senior Germany career.

He netted a fine individual goal on his full debut against Serbia last week and then grabbed a goal and two assists as Germany walloped the Netherlands 5-0 last night. Brighton's Bart Verbruggen was in goal for the Dutch.

After Gruda's excellent debut against Serbia, Klopp said he felt sorry for their left back Petar Sukacev. "That left-back, he's a poor lad!" said the former Liverpool boss Klopp.

Opta statistics backed up Klopp’s claim as they stated nine of Gruda's 14 dribbles against Serbia were successful.

"He's a fantastic player who loves to take people on and hasn't yet fulfilled his potential. He doesn't need to at his age, but he's shown an awful lot of positive signs," Klopp said.

So will he play for Brighton again and why did the Seagulls loan him out? Gruda made 45 appearances for Albion under Hurzeler in all competitions and showed glimpses of his potential with four goals and eight assists.

Niggling injuries and the fitness demands of the Premier League saw Gruda struggle to make a sustained impact. At times he featured in the No 10 role but largely he was behind the powerful Yankuba Minteh for the right wing slot.

Minteh joined for £30m in the same window as Gruda but the Gambian adapted faster. Gruda made just eight Premier League starts in the 2025-26 season and a change of environment was deemed beneficial by all parties and he joined RB Leipzig on loan for the second half of last campaign

“Brajan wants regular football, and we are not able to guarantee him that in the second half of the season," said Hurzeler.

Three goals and three assists for Leipzig in the Bundesliga convinced them to extend the loan for another season and Brighton were happy to sanction another temporary deal.

Sporting director Mike Cave said, “Brajan had a good second half of the season with Leipzig and was keen to explore the opportunity of remaining there for the coming year.

“This is a move that allows Brajan to continue to secure regular playing time at a good level, in an environment he knows well.”

Brighton are pretty sharp on their loan agreements but no doubt they will now be double checking the small print of the loan contract.

Leipzig are believed to have an obligation to buy at the end of the season, albeit with certain conditions - but the size of any potential transfer fee remains unclear.

His value is clearly going upwards and the loan agreement will likely allow Brighton to cash in, should they decide to sell. Further high-profile displays for Klopp's Germany will see that increase.

Gruda remains contracted with Albion until 2028 and this week Bayern Munich and Bayer Leverkusen were also credited with interest.

Transfer expert Florian Plettenberg posted: “The obligation to buy for Brajan Gruda will be activated in 2027 due to accounting reasons. The total package for RB Leipzig will amount to €30m, including bonuses. Gruda will then get a contract until 2031, which has already been agreed.

“Bayern have always had him on their list, even before his move to Brighton. However, he is currently not a concrete transfer target for Bayern – unless something extraordinary happens, such as the sale of Michael Olise.”

Gruda seems to prefer living and playing his football in Germany and whether he even considers moving back to Brighton remains to be seen. The Seagulls - who are flying high in the Premier League - also have a number of options on the right flank.

Minteh - who almost joined Liverpool last summer - is just returning from injury, while Paraguayan international Diego Gomez has also excelled in the wide right position this term.

Zadok Yohanna, Femi Azeez, Malick Yalcouye are all available, while the likes of Amario Cozier-Duberry and Harry Howell are out on loan.

Gruda's form is impressive but whether he returns to the Premier League with Brighton anytime soon is debatable. Attention will now likely turn to maximising their fee.
