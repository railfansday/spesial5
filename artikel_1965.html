Man City have 'certain' Gianluigi Donnarumma exit route, hurdle must be overcome - Expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Manchester City could potentially offload struggling goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma to the Saudi Pro League next summer.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Citizens' options are limited in terms of where they can move Donnarumma.

The 27-year-old has faced scrutiny for his performances of late, with his current 65.8 per cent save percentage standing him 13th in the ranking of Premier League starting goalkeepers.

Wyness believes that the Saudi Pro League could provide a genuine option for City to cash in on the Italian, whose wages are likely to pose an issue for several European clubs.

Donnarumma was widely regarded as one of the world's leading shot-stoppers when he arrived at the Etihad Stadium back in 2025, but is seeing his reputation damaged a little of late.

The former PSG ace is on a significant contract with the Citizens, but is not producing the goods for them at the moment.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that a move to Saudi Arabia could be an option for Donnarumma, but is clear that City would have to play the market carefully.

The Pro League recently introduced a new budget cap which prevents clubs from spending over 75 per cent of their revenue on their first-team squad.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how that has created a hurdle for City to overcome with Donnarumma.

"Yeah, I do (think he could be sold). I mean, if this would have been just a year ago, I'd have said the Saudi market would probably have taken him up," he said.

"But, you know, the budget restrictions may be starting to bite down there slightly. So it's not quite the option for clubs that it once was.

"Although, in Donnarumma's case, I still think it could be one option, certainly."
