Keith Wyness reveals what Aston Villa fans contacted him to say about 'big' transfers

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Aston Villa chose the perfect time to revamp their squad with an overhaul ahead of this season.

That is according to former Villa chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who expects a lot of people to be eating their words about his former club's summer business.

The Villans netted over £300million worth of summer sales, including notable exits for the likes of Morgan Rogers, Ezri Konsa, Ollie Watkins and Emiliano Martinez.

However, Wyness has been impressed by Unai Emery's new-look group, and feels that their promising form in recent weeks has changed the opinions of many fans.

The second city outfit are beginning to rise up the Premier League table of late, with a run of good league form building some confidence around the club.

Their poor start was akin to the early-season struggles that they endured last term, but they are already showing clear signs of progression once again.

Villa's former chief Wyness - who served as CEO at Villa Park between 2016 and 2018 and now runs a consultancy business advising elite clubs - outlined that he was contacted by several fans in the summer who were disappointed by Villa's business.

However, he feels that several of the new additions, including attacking midfielder Johan Manzambi, are already proving themselves to be "perfect" fits for the club.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness made it clear that the "financial consequence" of Villa's summer sales was undoubtedly necessary.

"I had a lot of Villa fans contact me about all the sales in the summer, moaning and saying we're going to be nothing at all, and here they are, they're still producing the goods," he said.

"They've replaced some big players and look, I'm very pleased with the way that it's working out for Villa because I think Manzambi is an exciting talent.

"He's good to watch, and I think he's a perfect type of Villa player.

"But that financial consequence of the sales was exactly what Villa needed, and they did it, so it can be done in football, and full marks to them for getting it there."

Former Manchester City financial advisor Stefan Borson told Football Insider earlier this week that Emery's imminent new contract at Villa is likely to make him one of the highest paid managers in Europe.

The Spanish boss has been heavily linked with Manchester United of late, but Villa are now on the verge of tying him down to a new deal.

Borson expects that Emery will earn between £10m and £15m a year from his new deal, which he also feels will include a hefty release clause.

Villa view the former Arsenal chief as their strongest asset at the moment, and are doing everything they can to protect him long-term.
