CM: Milan begin initial discussions over renewal for Musah – the situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Yunus Musah has been one of the surprises of the season for AC Milan. Now, the management have begun talks over his contract.

Calciomercato.com begin a report on Musah by recalling what Sky pundit Alessandro Florenzi – a former Milan player himself – said to the midfielder live on air after the Rossoneri’s derby win over Inter.

“I congratulate you because you’ve had two difficult years. I know you’re a guy who’s suffered a lot, but you’ve never given up,” the former full-back said.

“I can assure you of this because I’ve watched you work together for years. It’s truly been a great pleasure, so congratulations, and don’t get too big for your boots, but I’m sure you won’t because you’re a good guy.”

These were sincere words from someone who has experienced the Milan dressing room and knows how difficult it can be to succeed.

One of the key figures behind Musah’s new-found form is Ruben Amorim. The Portuguese coach completely revitalised him after two very difficult seasons, relying on him from day one and receiving a lot of hard work and dedication in return.

Amorim’s vision demands a player with Musah’s characteristics: a powerful engine, a remarkable ability to transform a passive game into an active one, and excellent transition skills. Versatility helps too, given that against Genoa the manager even deployed him as a left-back.

Musah’s season so far has seen him make 11 league appearances, with a total of 759 minutes played, plus three in the Europa League, with 222 minutes played. Going into preseason, that kind of reliance seemed impossible.

It’s not just Amorim who’s satisfied with Musah’s performances but the entire management as well, so much so that in recent days they’ve even discussed the possibility of extending his contract, which expires in 2028.

Recent contacts with the player’s entourage have helped them begin evaluating the possibility of renewing his deal. Musah currently earns around €2m per season and – if he maintains this level – he will sign new terms before the end of the season.
