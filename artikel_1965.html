Worthing Women: Make Mackay-ne a double!

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Nicole Mackay’s brace made it three goals in two games for her and a second successive win for Worthing Women, as they saw off Aylesford at the Sussex Transport Community Stadium.

After last week’s epic cup comeback at Camden, teenage starlet Hattie Rainey made her full debut while Ruby Murdock also came into the starting eleven; meaning that Skye Birkinshaw and Cristie Pritchard began on the bench.

Barely had supporters sat in their seats than the aforementioned Mackay got the home team off and running, just three minutes in.

Seizing on  a loose ball on the edge of the eighteen yard area, the in-form striker drilled low and hard into Aylesford ‘keeper Sade Rider’s bottom right-hand corner, to open the scoring in emphatic fashion.

Although a very different picture might have been painted had the Rebels's netminder Sian Woods not denied a clean through Leigha Bradding-Butler not once but twice, to deny the visitor’s top scorer a seventh strike of a prolific campaign.

Still inside the opening ten minutes, Mackay came the width of the crossbar away from extending the host’s advantage. Dusting herself down and stepping up to take a free-kick she herself had won, the ball cannoned back off the frame of the goal, with Jess Richardson landing the rebound on top of the net.

The game settled into a more even contest for some time after that, as Worthing pressed forward in search of a second before the break, with Aylesford looking dangerous on the counter-attack.

Approaching the half-time interval, an audible sigh of relief reverberated around Woodside Road via Immy Armstrong - a threat from her set-pieces - firing narrowly over the target from inside the penalty area.

The sun then broke through the clouds on a pleasant November afternoon; similar to the way in which Aliyah Sheridan did when gifted possession by the homester’s otherwise solid last line of defence. Woods redeemed herself via a strong parry to her left of Sheridan’s subsequent shot.

One final opportunity before the change of ends fell to visiting Skipper Ami Burnham, only for Woods to keep her out by way of another excellent stop.

It’s tight at the bottom of the London & South East Prem but it was the Reds who headed into the interval with a precious one-goal lead.

Worthing continued to probe and were rewarded just shy of the hour mark, thanks to the persistence of Mackay. Although it was centre-half Ella Terry who got the ball rolling, courtesy of a wonderful switch of play from the middle of the park out to the roving Richardson on the right flank. The winger then found a well-placed Mackay to test Rider in the travellers’ net, with the Kent club’s custodian unable to prevent the Rebel’s number nineteen from overtaking Amber Hazlewood as leading markswoman in a red (occasionally lilac) shirt.

Some fine build-up laid the foundations for a possible third not long afterwards, when a Hattie Rainey pass went from a central position to wide on the right again, releasing the rapid Richardson to, ultimately, find Mackay once more, with the forward teeing up Neve McEvaddy for a long-range piledriver which saw Rider pull off a terrific save.

Goalscorer nearly turned provider three-quarters of the way into the contest but neither Richardson nor Annie Gracey could apply the finishing  touch to a dangerous-looking delivery.

Almost instantly, the visiting Blues hit back via a neat move of their own, culminating in half-time substitute Kara Richardson forcing Woods to hold on.

Ten to go and another excellent long ball from Terry resulted in some equally impressive hold-up play by the recently-introduced Paige Baker-Carroll, providing Richardson with an opening that she couldn’t quite keep down.

Not over the line yet though, the home side were reminded of the threat carried by Aylesford when Bradding-Bulter’s corner eventually led to Kara Richardson drawing a one-handed stop out of Woods, at the (in)expense of another flag-kick.

Time was running out for the girls from the Garden of England and Armstrong was quick to pounce on Pritchard's pass to fellow replacement Megan Gates; although the final flourish from twenty yards failed to test Woods between the home posts.

Fittingly, Worthing’s brightly-clad netminder had the last word on proceedings, as she added to her collection of wonder saves by frustrating Sheridan with only a few short minutes of normal time to play.

That would, indeed, be that and three vital points stayed at the Sussex Transport Community Stadium to move the Rebels up to ninth in the table, ahead of another tough test at bottom-of-the-table and fellow Sussex side Eastbourne Borough next Sunday.
