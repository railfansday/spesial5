Ateez’s Yeosang Shares His Anti-Acne Routine

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Acne: It happens to the best of us. And that includes, believe it or not, K-pop megastars. When Yeosang, a member of the Korean boy band Ateez, invited Vogue into his bathroom to spill his Beauty Secrets, breakouts were a major topic.

“It used to be a massive source of stress for me,” the singer confesses. “I would constantly agonize over how I could get rid of all of these breakouts on my face.” That’s where a regimented skin-care routine came in. First, he researched ingredients and products—and luckily for him, he had access to one of the world’s best beauty markets.

His routine includes some products you may recognize (“This [product] helps dissolve dead skin cells and tighten pores,” he says about Stridex pads), along with some new discoveries. By the end of the video, though, you’ll probably have discovered a new product or two, plus a trick for using it.

“Doing this video with Vogue made me realize I use quite a lot of products compared to others,” he jokes. Whatever it is, it’s working for him.
