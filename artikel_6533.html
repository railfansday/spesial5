14 Appetizers That You Can (&amp; Should!) Eat For Dinner

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

An apps-olutely delicious way to do dinner.

If you’re like me, sometimes you don’t want to make a whole big meal for dinner. A homemade dinner can feel daunting, but I’ll never turn down the opportunity to indulge in appetizers. While it may be frowned upon (though I’d argue wrongfully so) to fill up on pigs in a blanket, stuffed mushrooms, or other finger-food favorites, there are a ton of fantastic appetizers that verge on dinner. It’s those beauties that I turn to for my favorite kinds of evenings: appetizers for dinner! With that in mind, I gathered 14 of our best apps that can (and should) be eaten for dinner. Plus, if you make a few of these on the same night, you’ve got a veritable feast.

Aside from the buttery biscuits, melty provolone cheese, and salty roast beef combo, we’re obsessed with these French dip stuffed biscuits because of how easy (emphasis on easy) they are to make.

These sliders are inspired by a classic fried chicken Caesar salad. They have all the nostalgic flavor notes you love, from crispy fried chicken to a tangy buttermilk dressing.

We love chicken wings in all shapes, sizes, and flavors, but there’s something extra special about the saucy, sweet, savory, and slightly sticky teriyaki wing. Our version skips the store-bought sauce in favor of a few pantry staples that come together in just a few minutes to create a gingery, garlicky soy-based sauce that clings to oven-baked wings and never falls short on flavor.

These old-school sausage pinwheels are a classic for a reason. They feature crescent dough with bits of sausage and red pepper layered within a creamy garlic cheese spread. Bell pepper + cream cheese + cheddar cheese make for a pimento-inspired bite.

What’s better than a tray of cheesy chicken nachos? Four cheese chicken nachos! Don’t worry, though, a couple cans of chopped green chiles cut the richness of all that cheese. We’ll leave the toppings up to you, but we suggest The Works, aka red onion, radishes, avocado, jalapeño, cilantro, and hot sauce.

This recipe has everything you could ever want in the seafood classic: They're golden, crispy, and chock-full of crab flavor. Ready in 30 minutes and served with homemade tartar sauce, they are the perfect easy main or low-effort appetizer.

Sheet-pan quesadillas are easy to assemble and fun to customize to your mood. We were inspired here by our French onion pot roast. In both recipes, we’re utilizing French onion soup packets to get that iconic flavor—no caramelizing onions required.

If you aren’t familiar with the muffuletta sandwich, you should be. It’s filled with three different cured meats, a plethora of olives, roasted red peppers, and other brined ingredients. It’s big and proud, and this dip is the perfect scoopable version of it.

If the best wing flavor and sauce combination is hot Buffalo wings and ranch dip, then it stands to reason that a ranch wing with hot Buffalo dip would be equally delicious—right? Damn right. We flipped the classic combo to create a ranch-flavored chicken wing served with a side of zesty, spicy Buffalo sauce.

This Nashville hot chicken dip brings the heat and MORE. Beware—this party dip is for major spice fans only. A cream cheese, mayo, and sour cream base meets hot sauce and breaded chicken for a spicy, creamy dip that is also perfectly filling.

These sliders are the crème de la crème. Ham, Swiss, and jammy caramelized onions are layered between the star of the show: buttery and soft Hawaiian rolls. You simply split the rolls, layer them, bake, and in a little over 10 minutes, you'll have a dozen hot, mind-blowing sandwiches ready to serve.

Caul us crazy, but we believe that these cauliflower wings are just as good as regular wings. This recipe transforms this staple veggie into crispy, golden bites that were made to be drenched in your favorite sauce.

These vegan potstickers are easy to put together and most of your time is spent roasting your squash until really tender, which can be done ahead of time. Crimping takes a little practice, but the good news is that even if they look a little wonky they are still going to taste great!

In these Buffalo chicken meatballs, ground chicken is formed into meatballs with bread crumbs and plenty of seasoning, before being drenched in the signature hot Buffalo sauce. Ready in 30 minutes, these meatballs are a flavor-packed app you can have ready in no time.
