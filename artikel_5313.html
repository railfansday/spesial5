BOXED Launches Mobile App, Inviting Everyone to "Collect Your Way" with NBA All-Star Karl-Anthony Towns and NFL Legend Richard Sherman

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The New York Knicks champion reveals the newly launched app as BOXED serves up a stylish ad running on live sports and on connected TV services

SEATTLE, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- BOXED, the most fun and rewarding way to collect the trading cards you want, is thrilled to celebrate the launch of a new mobile app that brings the hit collectibles experience to mobile for the first time. To celebrate this exciting milestone, BOXED partnered with New York Knicks star Karl Anthony-Towns and Seattle Seahawks legend and twenty-five-year Pokemon card collector Richard Sherman in an ad where the NBA champ faces off with a community member in a pack-ripping showdown in the heart of New York City.

BOXED is built to provide trading card fans with the ultimate collectibles experience. The BOXED online community space, which is now available as an app for Android and iOS devices, is a place where users can rip packs and swap real cards as they build their dream collection. Bolstered by an engaging and growing suite of community features, BOXED has welcomed more than 150,000 passionate collectors to the platform, including star athletes like Towns, Sherman, NFL sack specialist Maxx Crosby, Top 10 tennis ATP tennis player Taylor Fritz, and many others.

"I connected with the BOXED team from day one," said Richard Sherman. "They are passionate about building a great experience for collectors, and genuinely care about the Trading Card community. As a long-time collector, I really appreciate this approach."

The commercial spot, broken into :60, :30 and :15 edits, delivers an epic introduction to BOXED that speaks directly to mainstream audiences. The heart of New York City becomes the battleground for a friendly pack-ripping showdown between Towns and a BOXED community member. Meanwhile, a gathered crowd explores the app's collector-first experience, which offers easy home shipping, packs created by the community, and a stack of social features that celebrate the joy of building a collection.

"We set out to craft a highly visual story that makes the BOXED app launch feel as big as possible, while simultaneously connecting to the core trading card community and educating new users," said Craig Howe, CEO of Rebel Ventures, BOXED's creative agency. "It's all about celebrating the joy of collecting, the anticipation of ripping a fresh pack with friends, and celebrating wins."
