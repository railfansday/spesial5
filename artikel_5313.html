AI bubble fears are rattling global markets as investors question infrastructure spending, valuations and the profitability of massive technology investments.

AI tokens face mounting selling pressure as weakening investor confidence and broader market uncertainty weigh on cryptocurrency sentiment.

Global financial markets are facing renewed turbulence as fears surrounding an AI bubble intensify, triggering a sharp sell-off across technology stocks and semiconductor companies. The correction, which began on Wall Street, has spread across Asia, with major indices in Japan, South Korea and Taiwan recording substantial losses as investors reassess the sustainability of massive AI investments and their potential returns.

The growing uncertainty has also spilt into the crypto market, where Bitcoin and several altcoins are facing renewed selling pressure. AI-based tokens like NEAR, TAO, RENDER, FET, etc., and a few more are struggling, raising questions about whether weakening confidence in the broader AI investment narrative is beginning to weigh on digital assets.

The growing concerns surrounding AI investments have extended beyond Wall Street, triggering a sharp correction across global equity markets. Technology and semiconductor stocks have faced significant selling pressure as investors reassess AI valuations. The losses were concentrated in semiconductor companies, which have benefitted significantly from rising demand for AI processors and data-centre infrastructure.

Key US market movements included the Nasdaq Composite with a drop of 5.5%, the S&P 500 with 4.4% and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index with a 12.2% loss. Selling pressure then spread across Asia, where markets with significant exposure to the semiconductor & AI hardware industries posted substantial losses. South Korea’s KOSPI plunged 8.8% intraday, triggering a temporary halt, while Japan’s Nikkei and Taiwan’s TAIEX also recorded losses of 6% and 4.5%, respectively.

The latest correction highlights how closely global technology markets are tied to the AI investment cycle.

Bitcoin and major altcoins faced extensive selling pressure that erased the recent gains. The BTC price quickly fell below $76,000 after regaining $80,000, while the ETH price re-entered the consolidation zone around $2,225. The broader crypto market also experienced a significant correction, with losses extending beyond Bitcoin & Ethereum. The sell-off extended to AI-based cryptos that declined by 4.79% in the past 24 hours, with 86 of 106 tokens trading in negative territory.

The weakness highlights how the broader reassessment of AI-related investments is coinciding with declining confidence across digital assets.

The global sell-off has raised concerns about whether weakening confidence in AI investments could extend beyond technology stocks and cryptocurrencies. With semiconductor companies, major equity indices and AI-related tokens facing selling pressure, investors are becoming increasingly cautious about risk-sensitive assets.

The next few trading sessions will be crucial in determining whether this is a temporary correction or a prolonged market downturn. Continued weakness in AI investments, semiconductor demand and institutional risk appetite could keep pressure on global equities and cryptocurrencies. The bigger question is whether fears surrounding the AI bubble will remain confined to technology markets or trigger a wider risk-off environment.
