After testing more than 50 sweatpants over the past two years, I can confidently say today’s best sweatpants for women are no longer reserved for bed rotting on lazy Sundays. I’ve worn top-rated styles with a white t-shirts, styled them with my go-to wool coat for running errands, and paired them with an oversized hoodie while WFH. The difference between frumpy and fashionable comes down to fabric, fit, and style: today’s standouts are made with soft, stretchy, and moisture-wicking materials that are a cinch to move in and hold their shape well. Sweatpants are meant to be oversized, so you don’t have to worry as much about fit, as, say, jeans. But it’s important to make sure the pair you choose is still flattering. Look for a body-skimming fit that doesn’t cling too tightly to your thighs while sitting comfortably at your waist or hips. Also, pay attention to length. You don’t want your pant leg to drag on the ground while you walk, so consider options that come in petite, regular, and tall lengths, depending on your height.
