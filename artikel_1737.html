David Raya misses Arsenal training with new injury update imminent

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

David Raya was not involved in Arsenal’s open training session on Monday morning.

The Spaniard was forced off in the second half of Arsenal’s 2-2 draw with Newcastle in the Premier League on Saturday evening, as the Gunners dropped two points at St James’ Park.

Raya suffered what looked like a shoulder injury during a seemingly innocuous collision with Newcastle striker William Osula.

He went down hurt and required treatment, and had to be replaced by deputy Kepa Arrizabalaga.

Raya appeared to be in significant pain as he came off and Arsenal boss Mikel Arteta admitted after the match that it was “not good news”.

Raya was sent for scans on Sunday and while the results of those have not yet been confirmed by the club, he did not train with his team-mates on Monday morning.

Kepa and Illan Meslier went through the usual goalkeeping drills without their team-mate at London Colney.

It leaves Raya as a major doubt for Arsenal’s Champions League match at home to Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday night.

Arteta will provide an update on Raya’s injury and any potential spell on the sidelines at his pre-match press conference later on Monday.

The big matches come thick and fast for the Gunners in the coming weeks.

Arsenal host Manchester City in the Premier League at the weekend, before making the short trip across north London to take on arch-rivals Tottenham seven days later.

There are also showdowns with both Real Madrid and Manchester United coming up before Christmas.
