Tottenham injury update: Savio, Sandro Tonali, Xavi Simons latest news and return dates

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Tottenham are once again sweating over the availability of Savio after the winger picked up another injury shortly after brushing off a previous issue.

The Brazilian winger had returned from a month-long absence, coming off the bench in the second-half of Spurs’ win over Ipswich at the weekend.

But he lasted just 25 minutes before being forced off again.

It remains uncertain whether Savio will be available to play Sunderland this weekend, but it is hoped Spurs can recover a few players for Sunday’s game.

With that in mind, here is all the latest Tottenham injury news and potential return dates...

Summer signing and long-term target Savio was forced off after coming on as a substitute against Ipswich - his first appearance in a month for Spurs.

After the game, De Zerbi confirmed it was a recurrence of the same issue that has plagued the Brazilian in recent weeks.

The head coach said: "It is the same injury. And it is the worst thing from today."

No timeline has yet been provided for a return.

Illness forced Sandro Tonali to miss the victory over Ipswich, but the midfield maestro returned to training on Tuesday.

De Zerbi had stated Tonali “came back (from international duty) not in the right condition. (Sandro) Tonali was sick for a couple of days.”

Potential return date: Sunday, November 29 vs Sunderland (A)

Mathys Tel remains sidelined after a harsh tackle from Leeds United defender Jayden Bogle before the international break.

The Dutchman was then spotted in a protective boot before withdrawing from international duty, with the latest timeframe another ‘two or three weeks’ for a return.

Speaking before the Ipswich match, De Zerbi said of Tel: “Mathys Tel we need another two, three weeks.”

That could end up meaning Tel returns for the north London derby.

Potential return date: Sunday, December 6 vs Arsenal (H)

Tosin Adarabioyo has returned from injury, but the centre-back needs to build up fitness before he can be given minutes by De Zerbi.

“Tosin is already okay, but it is not the right moment to take any risks,” he said ahead of the Ipswich game.

Adarabioyo missed full first-team training on Tuesday, so it remains to be seen whether or not he will feature against Sunderland.

Potential return date: Sunday, November 29 vs Sunderland (A)

Mykhailo Mudryk’s loan switch to Spurs has been forgettable so far, to say the least. The Ukrainian has made just one appearance for Tottenham since the north Londoners borrowed the winger from Chelsea, and De Zerbi has already confirmed he will miss the trip to Sunderland.

“[The] plan was to play Ipswich or Sunderland, and then he felt no pain, but not comfortable last week. They take two weeks more,” he said. “Step by step.”

The latest from De Zerbi suggests Mudryk could be available to play Arsenal - a team he controversially admitted a soft spot for during the international break.

Potential return date: Sunday, December 6 vs Arsenal (H)

There has been a lot of positive noise surrounding Xavi Simons in recent weeks, but no concrete timeframe has been given for his return.

The Dutchman is still rehabilitating an ACL rupture suffered in April. In November, Simons started incorporating contact into his training, but as of yet he has not returned to first-team training at Hotspur Way.

Wilson Odobert is another Spurs player recovering from an ACL injury. The winger’s timeline is believed to be a bit further along, having sustained his issue a few months earlier than Simons, with Odobert injured in February.
