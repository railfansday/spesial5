Mauricio Pochettino issues Bruno Fernandes update after Man Utd star misses training

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Bruno Fernandes is a major doubt for Manchester United’s Premier League clash with Brentford on Saturday.

Fernandes scored twice midweek in the Champions League against former club Sporting CP but was absent from training on Friday morning.

The captain missed the session through illness with United confirming Fernandes was ‘feeling a bit under the weather and is, therefore, being protected ahead of the clash with the Bees.’

United head into the weekend second from bottom in the Premier League having won just one of their opening 11 matches.

Mauricio Pochettino is searching for his first win since taking over and hopes to have his captain available for his first game in charge at Old Trafford.
