Marmoush backs Spurs youngster Williams-Barnett to have ‘very big future’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Omar Marmoush has backed his "unbelievable" Tottenham team-mate Luca Williams-Barnett to have a "very big future".

The teenager has impressed under Roberto De Zerbi in recent weeks, most notably scoring and assisting in their Premier League win over Ipswich Town last weekend.

Aged 18 years and 51 days, Williams-Barnett became the second-youngest player to score and assist in a Premier League game, behind Michael Owen.

He also became the fourth-youngest player to score on his first Premier League start, behind Daniel Jebbison in May 2021 (17y 276d), Danny Cadamarteri in September 1997 (17y 343d) and Mark Cullen in May 2010 (18y 12d).

Should he start and score against Sunderland on Sunday, he'd be the youngest player to score in his first two starts (18 years, 59 days).

The forward's breakthrough into the Spurs' first team was rewarded with a new contract, which is reported to be a five-year deal, and Marmoush believes the only way is up for the exciting winger.

"He is unbelievable," Marmoush told Sky sports about Williams-Barnett.

"He is a great, great talent and what I like most about him, of course, his talent is something that everyone sees, is that he is very brave, too.

"And when he came on in his first game, he just took the ball and tried things. He is very direct, tries to beat his opponent and he has a very, very promising future ahead of him.

"He has got it, and you can see that. One of the big traits of a big talent, and a big player, is the personality and the character and that is what stands out most for me, his courage and to be brave.

"As a young lad, to come into Spurs' first team and perform like that is not easy, and just to believe in yourself and do the actions on your pitch is great.

"I hope he is enjoying it, and he will have a very, very big future, that's for sure."
