DC's Next Batman Was Never Going To Be Nightwing

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

On the surface, Dick Grayson's Nightwing may seem like an obvious choice to one day succeed Batman and don the cape and cowl. After all, he was The Dark Knight's very first Robin and oldest adopted son. However, DC Comics has spent quite some time establishing a much different trajectory for Grayson, while setting up someone else entirely to one day become the next Caped Crusader.

In the past, Dick Grayson had indeed filled in as Batman in Bruce Wayne's absence. However, it's one thing to become a temporary Dark Knight or for the comics to showcase an alt-reality "What If?" type scenario (see DC K.O. Knightfight), and quite another to suggest that Nightwing will one day become a full-time Batman for the foreseeable future. That's never going to be in the cards for several reasons.

Ever since he left Robin behind, Dick's evolution into Nightwing has long been punctuated by the desire to be more than Batman and step out of his shadow. Conversely, DC Comics clearly has someone else in mind to become the next Batman, and he's already been wearing the cape and cowl: Jace Fox.

Dick Grayson has successfully taken over as Batman before, most notably after Bruce Wayne's apparent death in Final Crisis. This era saw Dick becoming a new Batman alongside Damian Wayne as his Robin in the critically acclaimed Batman and Robin run. As such, it's been proven that Dick can become The Dark Knight when needed (but that doesn't mean he should long-term).

When he left his role as Robin behind, Dick Grayson's decision to become Nightwing was all about creating a new identity all his own, beyond Bruce. This included his move to Gotham's sister city of Blüdhaven. In the several years that have followed, Dick has become an A-list DC hero in his own right, leading the Titans and protecting his own city without needing Batman's name or reputation.

More importantly, Bruce would likely want something better for his eldest son than simply becoming another Batman. Bruce's own experiences have shown him the loneliness and sacrifices that come with the cowl. Dick has managed to retain more of his optimism and personal connections while still becoming an exceptional hero. Rather than asking him to take his place, Bruce would have every reason to want Dick to continue being Nightwing and prove that the next generation can become something greater than Batman.

Timothy "Jace" Fox, the estranged son of Lucius Fox, offers a different possibility. First introduced as the next Batman in DC's Future State initiative, Jace received a proper origin in The Next Batman: Second Son, which explored his troubled history and years away from his family. Following his return to Gotham, he eventually took up the Batman mantle, using advanced armor to fight the Magistrate during the events of Fear State.

Jace eventually relocated to New York City, making him the city's first ongoing Batman in decades. His I Am Batman series explored what the mantle could mean outside Gotham, giving him the opportunity to establish his own methods and reputation rather than simply imitating Bruce. His connection to Lucius also gives him a unique relationship with the technology and resources associated with the Wayne legacy.

That makes the upcoming Batman: Reign of Assassins series particularly exciting. Launching February 17, 2027, the five-issue series reunites writer John Ridley and artist Olivier Coipel, bringing Jace and Bruce together for their first team-up. When dangerous forces from Jace's past resurface, he turns to Katana and Gotham's Batman for help, sending the two Dark Knights on a globe-spanning adventure.

Keeping that in mind, it's clear that DC can use Jace to explore what the Batman legacy looks like in a new generation, and it will be much better than Dick Grayson becoming the next Dark Knight.​​​​​​​

Batman: Reign of Assassins #1 is available February 27 from DC Comics
