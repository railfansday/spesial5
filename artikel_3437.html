Hugh Laurie's New 10-Part Bestselling Series Debuts To Stellar Rotten Tomatoes Score

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Hugh Laure’s new 10-part TV show has already proven itself to be a hit, as evidenced by its Rotten Tomatoes score.

From House to Tehran, Laurie has starred in many acclaimed TV shows over the course of his career. He’s now back on the small screen for Peacock’s Dig, which is based off author Kate Myers’ 2023 novel Excavations, and follows four archaeologists who discover something mysterious at an excavation site in Greece. While the public still has a few days to go until they can judge Dig for themselves, the early reviews for this new comedy series from professional critics are overwhelmingly positive.

Dig currently ranks at 88% on Rotten Tomatoes’ Tomatometer, based on eight reviews so far. With that score, it ties Laurie's RT profile under the Critics Highest category with The Night Manager (which returned for season 2 earlier this year), and just one point below House (which ran for eight seasons). While that score will be subject to change as more reviews come in, Dig is nonetheless off to an impressive start on the critical front.

ScreenRant’s review of Dig rated the new show seven out of 10 stars, stating that the show excels in “taking the familiar humor of sitcoms and elevating it alongside gripping narrative drama.” Laurie’s performance in particular is described as “brilliant,” and the review ends by declaring that Dig is “an entertaining story with well-defined characters and a core premise that keeps the story moving at pace” thanks to its humor and well-rounded cast.

Laurie stars in Dig as Professor Neville, who’s been an archaeologist for decades and serves as a mentor to those who work at the dig site, including Amy Poehler’s Elise. While the Dig trailer shows Neville informing Elise that he intends to retire, she points out that he’s said that before, yet continued working, leaving it unclear if this will be the time it finally sticks. Laurie and Poehler previously crossed paths when the former hosted Saturday Night Live in 2006 and 2008, when the latter was still a cast member.

Peacock has crafted standout original series, elevating the streaming landscape with top-tier talent and storytelling deserving of TV acclaim.

Dig’s other main cast members include Thunderbolts*' Geraldine Viswanathan as Dylan, Paper Girls’ Fina Strazza as Patty, and The Good Doctor’s Antonia Thomas as Clare. Jason Matzoukas, Noma Dumezweni, and Brennan Lee Mulligan appear in recurring roles, and the show’s guest stars include Kayvan Novak, Jon Pointing, Bonita Friedericy, Max Greenfield, and Dan Bucatinsky.

Poehler co-created Dig with Mike Schur, with this marking their first reunion since Parks and Recreation. Schur’s other notable TV credits include Brooklyn Nine-Nine, The Good Place, and A Man on the Inside. Myers is also involved in Dig as one of the executive producers. The show’s arrival comes one month ahead of Ted: The Animated Series’ premiere, which will be Peacock’s final show of 2026. The streamer’s currently-running original programming includes fellow comedies Twisted Metal, The Paper, The ‘Burbs, and The Miniature Wife, and the dramas The Five-Star Weekend and Crystal Lake.

All 10 episodes of Dig premiere Monday, November 23 on Peacock.
