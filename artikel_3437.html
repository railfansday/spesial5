Directed by Claire Scanlon and written by Sarah Rothschild, The Love Hypothesis is a movie adaptation of the 2021 novel of the same name by Ali Hazelwood, which was first published online as a Star Wars romance fanfiction about the "Reylo" ship between Rey and Kylo Ren. The film follows a biology student who fakes a relationship with a professor, Dr. Adam Carlsen, to prove their theories about love, but their pretensions soon give way to real feelings. Tom Bateman and Lili Reinhart star as the lovers, with Rachel Marsh, Jaboukie Young-White, and Nicholas Duvernay as other notable characters.

As a modern rom-com, The Love Hypothesis does not offer anything new for genre fans and repeats the popular fake-dating trope without exactly reinventing the formula. However, one cannot deny the generous dose of humor that the movie offers, alongside great chemistry between the leading lovers. When it premiered in September 2026, The Love Hypothesis received mixed reviews from fans and critics alike, who equally found the film formulaic and familiar but praised Bateman’s complex performance and his chemistry with Reinhart.

Written, directed by, and starring Sylvester Stallone in his follow-up to 1979’s Rocky II, and the second sequel to the legendary Rocky, Rocky III continues the pursuits of the titular world boxing star. In the third chapter of his story, Rocky Balboa faces stiff competition from James “Clubber” Lang, a young and powerful contender rising the ranks, and turns to his old adversary, Apollo Creed, to help him train for his new fight. Talia Shire, Burt Young, Carl Weathers, and Burgess Meredith reprise their roles from the previous films, with Mr. T and Hulk Hogan appearing as new characters.

At the time of its release, Rocky III was criticized for its story, but it grew into a cult favorite and is one of the most popular and culturally significant of the Rocky films. The film is mostly remembered for Carl Weathers’ performance and the Oscar-winning theme song, “Eye of the Tiger,” which has​ since become synonymous with sports/training montages in films. Undoubtedly a flashy, high-energy blockbuster, Rocky III transformed the franchise from a grounded underdog drama into a larger-than-life 1980s pop-culture phenomenon.

Not to be confused with the 1991 live-action film, 2019’s The Addams Family is an animated adaptation of Charles Addams’ comic strip featuring the creepy and kooky titular family. The supernatural comedy film follows the Addams as they relocate to a humble suburban town in New Jersey, where their peaceful, macabre life is threatened once again when a reality TV show host tries to get rid of them. The ensemble voice cast includes Oscar Isaac, Charlize Theron, Chloë Grace Moretz, Finn Wolfhard, Snoop Dogg, and Bette Midler as the core family members.

Directed by Conrad Vernon and Greg Tiernan, The Addams Family puts a colorful spin on the deliciously dark original material, transforming Charles Addams’s demure world into a dazzling animated extravaganza. The film not only shifts the tone and style of its origin but also the storytelling, while keeping the eccentricity of the characters intact. When it was released, the film did not fare well among critics but was a box office success and earned praise for its animation style and quirky reinterpretation of the original characters.
