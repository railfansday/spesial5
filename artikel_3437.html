Glen Powell’s Savage Thriller Officially Takes Over Streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

2026 has been a tale of two halves for Glen Powell. On the one hand, he lent his voice to one of the biggest box office successes of the year. Powell joined Chris Pratt, Charlie Day, Anya Taylor-Joy, and Jack Black in the video game adaptation sequel, The Super Mario Galaxy Movie, which managed to perfectly follow up on the smash-hit triumph of 2023's The Super Mario Bros. Movie. The movie made an impressive $1.012 billion against a reported budget of $110 million, and currently ranks as the fifth-highest-grossing film of 2026.

Feedback for the film wasn't as glowing as many might've hoped. The Super Mario Galaxy Movie earned just 42% on the review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes, but Collider's Ross Bonaime was at least positive, saying that "The Super Mario Galaxy Movie remains a charming, often hilarious, and playful testament to the greatest video game franchise of all time." These mixed reviews were an afterthought compared to the billion-dollar success of the sequel, something that sadly can't be said for Powell's other 2026 film.

Released the week before The Super Mario Galaxy Movie, Powell's How to Make a Killing was met with a similar critical response. Earning just 45% on Rotten Tomatoes, the film was considered "tonally bizarre," with Bonaime's review for Collider writing, "How to Make a Killing just doesn't bring much new to the table, and that's a shame." Despite the ensemble talent of Topher Grace, Zach Woods, Jessica Henwick, Ed Harris, and Margaret Qualley, this blend of Succession and American Psycho couldn't live up to either. Thankfully, streaming has provided a warm home for the film, as it currently ranks as one of the ten most-watched movies on HBO Max in the world.

Four criminal masterminds. One of them was always going to be yours. Here is who comes for you, why they picked you out of everyone available, and what they want from the encounter.

Your nemesis does not want to beat you. He wants to know you completely, and he is patient enough to wait for it. The frightening part is how good the conversation is.

Your nemesis is not after money, territory or revenge. He is after a demonstration, and you are the person he has chosen to prove his point on.

Your nemesis respects you, which sounds flattering until you work out what it means. He chose you because nobody else was worth his time.

Your nemesis has no personal interest in you whatsoever, and that is precisely what makes him unsurvivable. You are not a rival. You are a stop on the way.

There was plenty of hope and expectation that, with a star like Powell at the helm, and as an A24 movie, How to Make Killing would live up to its name at the box office. Alas, these hopes were quickly dashed, as the film flopped almost upon arrival. Against a reported budget of $15 million, How to Make Killing earned just $20 million in global revenue, split between a domestic haul of $7.8 million and a further $12.6 million from overseas markets.
