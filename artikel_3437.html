Dashboard Confessional Announce 2027 Unplugged: 25th Anniversary Tour

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Dashboard Confessional will celebrate the 25th anniversary of their seminal Unplugged performance with a 15-date North American tour. The trek kicks off in Chicago on March 12 and continues through April 3, when it wraps in San Francisco. It will hit cities like New York, Boston, Nashville, and Los Angeles.

“Dashboard Confessional’s Unplugged was a pivotal and unifying moment for the band and our fans,” frontman Chris Carrabba said in a statement. “I’m so excited to celebrate that moment in time. I’ve recently done some thrillingly small solo acoustic shows in Nashville, New York and Los Angeles — the energy around those shows was, fittingly, electric. I’m excited to carry that spirit into bigger venues with the band arrangements from our original Unplugged performance.”

Dashboard Confessional performed on MTV’s Unplugged series on April 24, 2002. It was released as an album, MTV Unplugged 2.0, that December, a year ahead of their 2003 breakout LP, A Mark, a Mission, a Brand, a Scar.

Ticket information for the tour is available on the band’s website. The Saints & Sailors Fan Club presale begins Nov. 12 at 9 a.m. local.
