Everything New On Netflix In December 2026: Full List Of Movies & TV Shows

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

New month, new stuff to stream on Netflix. And the streamer's December 2026 offerings are robust, with the service looking to end the year on a high note thanks to a fresh batch of original programming, as well as some familiar favorites. Movies, TV shows, documentaries, and even live sporting events, the streamer's got it all.

To be sure, Netflix has several big original movies coming our way that month, including director Brad Bird's animated sci-fi noir flick "Ray Gunn" (which boasts a star-studded voice cast that's led by Sam Rockwell and Scarlett Johansson). Then there's "The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth," which brings Brad Pitt back as his character from Quentin Tarantino's "Once Upon a Time in Hollywood" in a new spin-off film written by Tarantino and directed by David Fincher. Early reactions to "Cliff Booth" have been largely positive, too, so that bodes well for everyone.

Finally, in terms of familiar favorites, horror fans will be able to enjoy Nicolas Cage's wild turn in "Longlegs." The "Shrek" movies are also being added to Netflix, which means viewers can revisit one of the best sequels of all time in "Shrek 2." Meanwhile, on the TV side, Netflix's new version of "Pride and Prejudice" is hitting the service, along with returning series like "Emily in Paris."
