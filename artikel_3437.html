Sam Raimi’s Latest Nightmare Officially Becomes a Massive Streaming Hit on HBO Max

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

2026 has been loaded with great horror movies. From YouTuber Mark Edward Fischbach (Markiplier) surprising the world with the acclaimed Iron Lung and Severance star Adam Scott's spine-chilling Hokum, to Nia DaCosta's superb horror sequel 28 Years Later: The Bone Temple and Kane Parsons' record-breaking directorial debut Backrooms, there's been more than enough for horror fans to feast upon this year. This is without mentioning the all-conquering Obsession, which defined the genre not just for 2026 but for the decade so far.

This October, the biggest horror hit was Rob Savage's Other Mommy, which fought off terrible reviews to stamp itself as the spooky season favorite. Based on the 2024 novel Incidents Around the House by Josh Malerman, the film follows young Bela (Arabella Olivia Clark) as she is haunted by an evil entity that resembles her mother (Jessica Chastain). Also starring Jay Duplass, Dichen Lachman, Karen Allen, and more, the movie turned a $19 million budget into an impressive $109 million worldwide and is still returning each weekend.

But Other Mommy's competition isn't just in theaters, as several big-name horror movies make the move to streaming. Right now on HBO Max, one of the most-watched films in the U.S. is Sébastien Vaniček's Evil Dead Burn, a truly chilling gem that many have cited as this year's scariest film. Starring Souheila Yacoub as Alice, the film is the sixth movie in the Evil Dead franchise and, of course, benefits from a Bruce Campbell cameo. The movie scored a global haul of $72 million against a reported budget of $20 million during a quietly impressive theatrical run.

The Evil Dead franchise's mastermind, director Sam Raimi, returned to the horror chair earlier this year after 17 years away. Send Help, a Rachel McAdams-led survival story that blends the gory greatness of Raimi's best with the social satire of Triangle of Sadness, made just shy of $100 million against a reported $40 million at a difficult time for the 2026 box office. McAdams' lead performance is cited by many as one of this year's most underrated.
