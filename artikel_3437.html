Ridley Scott's Apple TV Crime Thriller Is a 10/10 Binge-Watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

A crime series needs certain qualities to work. Of course, there have to be twisty plots, plenty of exciting and suspenseful moments, and characters that you become instantly drawn to. Recent shows like True Detective, Task, and MobLand have all introduced viewers to thrilling episodes that provide an inside look at dark, mysterious criminal underworlds. One series in particular flew under the radar when it was released, but remains every bit as worthy of a binge-watch as the other shows in this genre.

The Ridley Scott-produced drama Dope Thief premiered on Apple TV in March 2025. Over a succinct eight episodes, the Peter Craig-created series never shies away from creating an intense and brutally violent yet completely captivating story. The series was adapted from a 2009 novel by Dennis Tafoya, and follows two best friends named Manny Carvalho (Wagner Moura) and Ray Driscoll (Brian Tyree Henry), who are trying to survive on the rough streets of Philadelphia. They've created a small-time robbery scheme where they pose as DEA agents and steal from local drug dealers.

After you watch 'Dope Thief,' go back and watch these shows!

Their con works pretty well until they take on a job that brings their whole lives crashing down. Even though there are plenty of red flags from the start, Ray and Manny rob a drug operation that is actually already being surveilled by real DEA officers. From there, the two men fall deeper and deeper into a massive criminal enterprise that puts them in danger at every turn. Can Manny and Ray outrun their fates, and will their friendship survive the danger that's coming for them? Each episode is fast-paced and drama-packed, but Dope Thief also manages to infuse its story with a wry humor that makes all the bloodshed a tiny bit easier to stomach.

Dope Thief stands apart from other crime shows because of its impressive blend of genres. One moment, you'll be covering your eyes at the violence, while a moment later, you'll be chuckling at the silly antics Manny and Ray are forced to perform to escape danger. Despite the humor and levity, Dope Thief never shies away from emphasizing the story's high-stakes circumstances. The series also impressively emphasizes the importance of found family. The bonds between many characters are compelling and authentic, and although most aren't related by blood, their connections are just as powerful. Dope Thief also successfully portrays the long-lasting effects of trauma and the heartbreak of knowing someone struggling with addiction, weaving these elements to make each episode better than the last.

Much of Dope Thief's success is tied to Wagner and Henry's performances. Their chemistry is both electric and effortless, and each actor proves why they're considered among the best in the industry right now. It's no surprise that Henry was nominated for an Emmy for his work as Ray. Marin Ireland is also incredible as Mina, a beautifully broken undercover agent who becomes entangled in Ray and Manny's lives. We'd be remiss if we didn't mention the stellar performances of Kate Mulgrew as Ray's tough-as-nails adoptive mother and Ving Rhames as Ray's gruff, embattled father.

For more than a year, fans wondered if there would be future episodes of Dope Thief. However, a few weeks ago, it was announced that Apple TV wouldn't be moving forward with the series. Although it's a shame that there'll be no Season 2, you should still check out this exceptional show. With an impressive 86% Rotten Tomatoes score and edge-of-your-seat storytelling, Dope Thief is the perfect addition to the crime thriller genre.
