iOS 27.3 adds new features in six iPhone apps, details here

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

iOS 27.3 is coming soon, and it’s a big update for new iPhone features, including additions that span six built-in Apple apps. Here’s what’s new.

Apple Music adds a pair of upgrades in iOS 27.3. Selfie Sing is an all-new feature that lets you record short videos of yourself singing along to your favorite songs, aided by onscreen lyrics. It’s perfect for sharing on TikTok or other social platforms.

If you’re not the type to share karaoke videos online, another nice iOS 27.3 change is that the lyrics view for songs has gotten an iPhone Duo-inspired upgrade in landscape mode. Now, artwork for the song you’re listening to blends beautifully into the stream of lyrics.

Apple’s Health app gets a major overhaul in iOS 27.3, though only for Apple Intelligence-compatible iPhones.

The new Health app has an Insights tab for personalized data visualization and guidance, Longevity tab for tracking your health age, and there are new features like educational videos, movement evaluations, and the ability to order labs that get automatically imported into the app.

Vertical video is coming with a new Clips feature that lets you browse short samples of Apple TV shows and movies in a TikTok-style vertically scrolling format.

Video podcasts are also coming to the TV app, with the option to automatically sync playback from Apple Podcasts inside the TV app.

Apple Fitness+ is getting a big expansion in iOS 27.3 with a new Gym Strength program.

Gym Strength lets you build a strength program that fits your schedule and the equipment you have on hand. It’s fully customizable and provides Apple’s best offering yet for traditional strength training.

Whether you’re brand new to strength training or just benefit from some additional structure, Gym Strength is a great reason to try out Fitness+.

Apple’s Freeform app gets a handful of improvements in iOS 27.3, including new text styling options and preset background colors to choose from. You can also generate custom shapes using Apple Intelligence.
