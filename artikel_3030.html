Hadrian Targets Critical Bottleneck in US Shipbuilding

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Defense manufacturer Hadrian is investing $300 million to acquire and modernize a historic Pennsylvania foundry that produces critical components for US Navy ships and submarines. CEO Chris Power discusses why domestic casting capacity has become a major bottleneck for America’s defense industrial base and how AI and advanced manufacturing could boost production. He joins Ed Ludlow on "Bloomberg Tech." (Source: Bloomberg)
