Opera (OPRA -7.99%) reported third-quarter financial results that met or exceeded management's previous guidance, driven by growth in both advertising and search query segments. The company is transitioning its listing from American Depositary Shares to ordinary shares on the NASDAQ to lower holder fees and improve eligibility for various equity indices. Strategic focus remains on integrating artificial intelligence natively into the browser architecture, specifically through local inference capabilities and agentic tools. Management indicated that the advertising business is diversifying its vertical mix, with gaming and travel increasingly contributing to the top line alongside the primary e-commerce segment.
