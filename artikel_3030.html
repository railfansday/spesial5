LTC Properties, Inc. (LTC +1.16%) reported a significant strategic transition as its Seniors Housing Operating Portfolio (SHOP) reached 45% of total annualized net operating income (NOI), with a target to hit 50% by year-end. Management indicated that the company is actively recycling capital from lower-growth triple-net lease assets into the higher-growth SHOP segment, which now includes 50 communities and 14 operators. The company increased its 2026 investment guidance to $1 billion following record acquisition activity and maintains a pro forma liquidity position of over $900 million. Management stated that the current strategy triples the pro forma internal growth rate of the portfolio as it moves toward a long-term goal of generating 75% of NOI from SHOP assets by 2028.
