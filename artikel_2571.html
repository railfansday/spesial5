Gauntlet over, Vikings have five of their last seven games at home

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Vikings managed to win their first six games, fueled by a dominant defense and an offense that was barely able to dog paddle. Then came the gauntlet.

At Detroit, Buffalo, at Green Bay, 49ers in Mexico City.

Minnesota lost three of those games, dropping them to 7-3 and, for now, third place in the NFC North. Still, despite the Sunday night blowout loss to San Francisco, the table is set for a possible late-season run.

Five of the last seven contests are at home, with the road games coming at New England in Week 14 and, three weeks later, at the Jets.

They could win all seven. Falcons, Panthers, at Patriots, Lions, Commanders, at Jets, Bears. The toughest of the bunch will be the division games, but both will be at home. Even if they split that pair and win the rest, that’s potentially 13-4.

It won’t be easy, but it won’t be nearly as challenging as the last four games. The offense has improved and the defense (assuming the 49ers haven’t provided the rest of the league with a blueprint for beating the Brian Flores blitz) continues to be among the best in the league.

It won’t be easy, but it’s hardly impossible. One game at a time, they can resume stacking wins.

And they may inevitably cross paths with the 49ers again in January, where it would begin not at 35-10 but 0-0. Without altitude being an issue. And with a chance to have a handful of key moments that went against them in the first meeting possibly go their way in the rematch, where the pressure will be on the team that made quick and easy work of the Vikings the first time around.
