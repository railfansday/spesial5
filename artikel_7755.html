Maryland Brothers Sentenced for Tax Evasion

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Baltimore, Maryland – A federal judge handed down prison terms to Maryland twin brothers, today, in connection with tax-evasion crimes. Judge Deborah K. Chasanow sentenced Dennis March, 55, and Greg March, 55, both of Berlin, to six months in prison each, followed by three years of supervised release — with a special condition of 12 months of home detention as part of their supervised-release term — for tax evasion. Judge Chasanow also ordered the March brothers to each pay restitution of nearly $1.8 million.
