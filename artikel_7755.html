Plastic surgeon sentenced to death for killing ‘beloved’ lawyer

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Lawyer Steven Cozzi, 41, who worked for Blanchard Law, a small law firm in Largo, Florida, went missing in 2023. Dr. Tomasz Kosowski of Florida was convicted of killing Cozzi and sentenced to death Friday. (Photo courtesy of the Largo Police Department)

A Florida judge has sentenced a prominent plastic surgeon to death for the murder of a “beloved” lawyer who went missing in 2023, saying the doctor committed the crime in a “cold, calculated and premeditated manner.”

Pinellas County Circuit Judge Joseph A. Bulone handed down the death sentence Friday to Dr. Tomasz Kosowski of Florida, who was convicted in September of murdering attorney Steven Cozzi, 41, whom he faced in a contentious lawsuit, Law360 reports.

Prosecutors said Cozzi was last seen entering the restroom of his law office in Largo, Florida, in March 2023. Surveillance video allegedly captured Kosowski walking into the law office’s main entrance on the morning that Cozzi went missing, showing the surgeon carrying a backpack and a large box. About two hours later, the video shows him walking out of the building with a different clothes and pulling a large cart covered with a red blanket.

Cozzi, described as a “beloved” person, worked for Blanchard Law, a small law firm specializing in business litigation, bankruptcies and contract disputes. At the time of his disappearance, Cozzi was representing a defendant being sued by Kosowski over medical billing disputes. His body was never found.
