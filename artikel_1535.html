MU will have to pay a fortune in the United States to recruit Pochettino

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Manchester United will have to put their hands in their pockets if they wish to recruit Mauricio Pochettino. While the future of Michael Carrick on the bench of the Red Devils seems more and more compromised, the United States coach appears to be the big favorite of the Glazer family to succeed him. But according to information from The i Paperthe Mancunian club will have to pay 9 million euros to release the Argentine coach from his contract with the American selection. A significant sum for a 54-year-old technician who recently signed a new four-year contract. Manchester United leaders are due to meet this Wednesday in Monaco to make a decision regarding Michael Carrick, whose departure could be announced as early as Thursday. With only one victory in the first ten days of the Premier League, a first in 53 years for the club, the former midfielder would not have convinced his management to give him more time. Already approached by Manchester United in 2018 and 2022, Pochettino finally saw Ole Gunnar Solskjaer and Erik ten Hag being preferred to him. This time, Joel and Avram Glazer would be determined to make the Argentinian their priority.

However, it remains to convince Mauricio Pochettino to leave the United States, where he seems fully invested in his project. The former Tottenham and Chelsea coach is said to be happy with his current situation, but would agree to listen to a possible Mancunian offer. No official approach has yet been made, and several people close to the matter doubt his desire to join Manchester United in such a complicated sporting context. The Glazers could nevertheless benefit from their relationship with American billionaire Ken Griffin, who helped finance Pochettino’s contract with the American selection and maintains historic financial ties with the owners of the Red Devils. At the same time, two other candidates remain seriously considered by management. Eddie Howe, former Newcastle manager, is said to be the favorite of director of football Jason Wilcox, while Julian Nagelsmann, former Germany manager, is said to be favored by head of recruitment Christopher Vivell. The choice of the future coach could thus depend as much on financial negotiations as on Manchester United’s ability to convince its priority candidate.
