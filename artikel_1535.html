David Raya injury: The Arsenal numbers that might surprise you as Arteta awaits scan results

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Arsenal are sweating over an injury concern for David Raya after their reliable goalkeeper was forced off against Newcastle United.

Raya has been Arsenal’s first-choice goalkeeper since September 2023 after a short settling in period after his move from Brentford. Remember when Mikel Arteta tried telling us he had two number ones and that it’d be a straight battle between Raya and Aaron Ramsdale every week? That didn’t last long.

Since making his league debut for Arsenal, Raya has only missed three games in the top flight. Two were against his parent club Brentford in his debut season, when he was still on loan and therefore ineligible. The third was on the final day of last season, when Arsenal were already home and dry as Premier League champions.

In other words, when Arsenal need Raya, Raya plays. But depending on the results of his scan, they might have to do without him for a bit.

Kepa Arrizabalaga will be on standby and ready to step up in his absence, with Illan Meslier next in line after.

They normally only get their chances in cup competitions, which can give some insight into how Arsenal do in Raya’s absence.

Kepa has been backing Raya up since the start of last season, while Meslier was added in the summer. Before that, Raya had others like Ramsdale keeping him on his toes.

But for the duration of his Arsenal career so far, how have Arsenal done when Raya has been playing and when he hasn’t?

A closer look at the stats shows, surprisingly, that Arsenal actually have some better ratios when Raya isn’t playing. But it’s one thing for his backups to come in and do a job every now and then, and another entirely if he’ll be out for a prolonged stretch of time.

“Unfortunately, he had to come off, which is never good news, and he will get assessed now,” Arteta said on Saturday. “He will probably get an MRI scan tomorrow, to see what exactly it is. At the moment, I don’t know.”

As Arsenal wait for more certainty over Raya’s availability, here’s how they’ve done with and without him since he joined the club.

Goals against: 122 Goals against per game: 0.75 Clean sheets: 76

Points per game: 2.15 Win percentage: 64.4%

Goals against: 27 Goals against per game: 1.04 Clean sheets: 8

Points per game: 2.23 Win percentage: 69.23%
