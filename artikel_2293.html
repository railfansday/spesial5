Ajax go second in Eredivisie behind PSV as Feyenoord woes deepen

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

See more DutchNews articles in your Google search results

Ajax moved into a clear second place in the Eredivisie with a routine 3-0 win against ADO Den Haag, as Feyenood slipped to fifth after losing to local rivals Excelsior.

Davy Klaassen took just three minutes to stroke home the opening goal for Ajax and added a second just before half-time.

By then ADO had been reduced to 10 men following the dismissal of Matteo Waem for hauling down Tolu Arakodare.

But there was a blow for Ajax ahead of next weekend’s visit to Feyenoord when Jorthy Mokio, scorer of the second goal, was taken off injured after an accidental collision with Sékou Sylla.

Feyenoord, who started the month top of the Eredivisie, suffered their first home defeat in the derby against Excelsior since 1980 to continue a slump in form that has seen them draw two and lose four of their last six games.

Givairo Read’s blistering shot gave Feyenoord the lead after just two minutes, but Excelsior were ahead by half-time and made it 3-1 when Casper Windell headed in a corner after 58 minutes. Despite an own goal by Jan Plug, on loan from Feyenoord, Excelsior were not to be denied.

PSV remain a point clear of Ajax at the top of the table after they cruised to a 4-1 win at NEC, with Filip Kostic opening the scoring with his first goal for PSV and his first Eredivisie goal since 2014, when he was playing for Groningen.

Utrecht sacked their managed Anthony Correia after just 14 matches after sinking to 15th place in the table with a 3-2 home defeat to Cambuur.

Correia won just four games after leaving Telstar, the club he led to promotion two seasons ago and where he spent his entire 15-year playing career. Youth team coach Mark Otten will take charge of the first team while Utrecht search for a replacement.

Willem II, searching for their first Eredivisie win in 30 matches, took a shock lead away to Twente despite being reduced to 10 men in the first half when Devin Haen was given space in the area to shoot past Joël Drommel.

The home side struggled to turn their superiority into goals, but an equaliser by Ramiz Zerrouki and a late header by Marko Pjaca gave them the points they needed to rise to third place above Feyenoord.

AZ also overtook Feyenoord as they beat Sparta 5-1 with a dominant second-half display, while Fortuna Sittard kept in touch with the leading group with a typically gritty 1-0 win at Go Ahead Eagles.
