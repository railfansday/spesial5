3 players who Birmingham City should offload in January, including Lee Buchanan

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Birmingham City have enjoyed a fine season to date after a slightly different approach in the summer transfer window. BCFC made seven new signings, two of whom had already been at the club, so it was a window of evolution not revolution as opposed to previous squad rebuilds.

But, with Chris Davies‘ side firmly in the Championship promotion conversation, the January window offers a chance to strengthen. The Blues can shift out some fringe players to make way for additions who can push them over the line in the bid to make the Premier League.

With that in mind, we put forward three players who should be leaving Birmingham City in the winter…

Somewhat surprisingly, Scottish winger Scott Wright stayed at St Andrew’s in the summer. The 29-year-old spent the second half of last season on loan at Dundee and the strong competition for minutes on the Birmingham City wings meant minutes were bound to be limited.

That has proven to be the case with Wright making only two Championship matchday squads and playing just one minute of senior football in a brief substitute appearance against Swansea City in the Carabao Cup.

Wright sees his contract expire in 2027, but to get back to playing regularly, he should leave BCFC in the winter. A return to Scotland or drop to League One seem like his most likely options.

Another player who has been on the fringes at Birmingham City is Lee Buchanan, although his situation is a little different. There was talk of a potential summer loan move as the left-back rebuilt his fitness after a serious knee injury, but he ended up staying put.

Alex Cochrane‘s emergence in the starting XI and the signing of Kristoffer Lund have left Buchanan as third choice in his favoured position. The Blues have been eager to be careful with his minutes given how long he was out, but by the time January comes around, he should be ready to play more regualrly.

There may be hesitance to cash in right away, so a loan feels more likely for January. Then, his situation can be reassessed in the summer, as he has shown he can be a Championship option before.

Last but not least is Marvin Ducksch, who has made eight Championship appearances off the bench. The German striker showed signs of promise in his debut campaign at Birmingham City, but a drink driving charge brought doubts over his future.

A summer move failed to materialise, but it is still widely expected that the 32-year-old moves on this season. A switch back to Germany is most likely, and could see Ducksch return to impressive form given his record of 71 goals in 138 2. Bundesliga appearances, and 34 in 130 in the Bundesliga.

August Priske is the lead striker at BCFC with Jay Stansfield and Luis Vasquez as the other options. Another centre forward may be needed if Ducksch is sold, but that seems like a logical switch.
