Arsenal handed injury boost as William Saliba plan revealed: 'No need to panic'

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

William Saliba came through his appearance for Arsenal’s Under-21s without any fresh injury concerns.

The Frenchman started against Aston Villa on Sunday, as he continues to step up his comeback from a serious back injury.

Saliba had been out since the World Cup semi-finals in the summer but he is now closing in on a return to first-team action.

He made Arsenal’s matchday squad against Slavia Prague in the Champions League last week, though manager Mikel Arteta then opted against having Saliba involved against Hull in the Premier League on Saturday.

It was decided that minutes for the U21s would be more beneficial and Saliba - who was not named in the latest France squad by Zinedine Zidane - started alongside captain Marli Salmon in a 5-2 Premier League 2 win over Villa at Boreham Wood’s Meadow Park.

Max Dowman - who is serving a three-match suspension at senior level after his sending off in the shock Carabao Cup defeat by Fleetwood last month - was among the scorers as he starred in a playmaker role.

Saliba was replaced at half-time but Arsenal’s U21s head coach David Horseman confirmed that was always the intention.

“He’s fine, by the way, it was all pre-planned, so nobody needs to panic,” Horseman said.

Saliba was at the Emirates Stadium on Saturday to watch Arsenal beat Hull 1-0 and extend their lead at the top of the Premier League table to five points.

He then joined up with the U21s on Sunday morning and impressed Horseman with his attitude and impact on the young group.

“Just the way he was in the meeting, everything about him, the way he prepares on the bus, the lads probably won’t notice it, but they should,” Horseman said.
