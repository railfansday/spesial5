Gary Neville sends Shea Lacey warning to Michael Carrick ahead of Man Utd vs Aston Villa

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Gary Neville has urged Michael Carrick to ignore the clamour to start Shea Lacey against Aston Villa.

The struggling Red Devils dropped into the relegation zone on Saturday as a consequence of a Liam Delap inspired Nottingham Forest condemning Manchester City to a third straight defeat.

United showed signs of life in midweek after they came back from 2-0 down to produce a rousing second half comeback against Roma in the Champions League.

Lacey’s late cameo proved key in helping Carrick’s struggling side earn a first victory in any competition since September, and the young winger is pushing for a start against Villa.

Neville, however, believes United finding themselves in such a precarious situation means today’s game at Old Trafford is an occasion for wise old heads.
