Arsenal XI vs Slavia Prague: Confirmed team news, predicted lineup and injury latest for Champions League

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Max Dowman could make his first start in the Champions League when Arsenal travel to face Slavia Prague on Wednesday night, as Mikel Arteta also weights up the return of William Saliba.

Dowman was absent from the Gunners’ win over Liverpool at Anfield as he started a three-match suspension.

That ban only applies domestically and so Dowman is in contention to feature in the Czech Republic, where Arteta is expected to rotate his side.

Saliba travelled with the squad on Tuesday afternoon as he pushes to show he is ready to make his first appearance of the season.

The Frenchman is back in training and has completed his rehabilitation from the back injury that has kept him out for the entire campaign so far, and Arteta said at his pre-match press conference that Saliba is “ready to go”.

"Now you're in or not," the Spaniard said.

"Now he's in. He's in like anybody else. All the preparation has been done to make sure all the load and all the steps have been done to minimise the risk.

"He's in a really good place, he's built the capacity that we needed. Now it's about giving him minutes.”

Ben White is likely to start, as Arteta continues to split the right-back minutes, while Cristhian Mosquera could come in to allow Ezri Konsa a rest.

The same goes for Piero Hincapie at left-back, with Arsenal keen to manage Riccardo Calafiori's workload.

In midfield, Myles Lewis-Skelly and Mikel Merino will hope to start and there is a decision for Arteta to make over whether to rest Declan Rice.
