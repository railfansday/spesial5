Bills vs Jets Week 10 NFL tickets are under $150, see latest prices

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Following their game against the Minnesota Vikings on Monday night, the Buffalo Bills have a quick turnaround as they'll continue their road trip down to New Jersey when they face the Jets next Sunday.

Ahead of their Week 9 matchup, the Bills are 6-1 with a strong offense led by quarterback Josh Allen and running back James Cook.

The Jets on the other hand, have been in a downward spiral after kicking off the season 1-0 as they're now 2-7. Their latest loss came on the road against the Kansas City Chiefs.

If you're looking to watch this historic AFC East rivalry game in person, tickets are still available for under $150, making it one of the more affordable Bills games to attend this year.

Below, we break down ticket prices and how to secure your seat today.

The Jets look to end their losing streak against their division rival Buffalo Bills next Sunday, Nov. 15. As of publication, ticket prices start at $143.
