NFL fans rip ‘convenient’ timing of Puka Nacua’s suspension as Rams star sidelined

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Puka Nacua was suspended three games for violating the NFL's personal conduct policy.

Nacua will serve the suspension while on injured reserve, provoking a hostile response from football fans.

After the initial news was reported, social media exploded with vehement rebuttals of the league's suspension.

"So not even a suspension since he's going to miss the next 3 games on IR anyway

"Lol the league waiting for him to be on IR to suspend him is crazy," @ProfessorO_NFL posted to X.

@DiBonaNFL added: "It's absolutely insane that Puka Nacua can serve his suspension while on injured reserve. Reminder: The incident happened on December 31, 2025… Yet the NFL didn't come to a ruling until over nine months later, suspending Nacua for three games when he conveniently happens to have three weeks remaining on injured reserve. The NFL continues to be a self-serving joke."

"Suspending a guy who is already hurt and on IR and whose suspension will expire before he is off IR? That's like the bartender cutting you off after you already paid and literally walking out," @JBRBracketology responded to the Nacua news.

"So he's suspended zero games then lmao," another user wrote on X.

"@nflcommish what a freaking joke you're doing to protect this creep & the @RamsNFL to keep him playing. Why didn't you suspend him when he was playing?" @Mikeg_1972 said on X.

Nacua is eligible for reinstatement December 14, giving the Rams their top receiver for both regular season matchups against the Seahawks and the postseason.

The Rams entered the 2026 season as Super Bowl favorites, but the looming suspensions of several star players hung in the balance.

It appears in Nacua's case, the injured Rams receiver will complete the suspension during his time on IR.

Nacua was given a stern warning by the league as any future violation of the NFL's personal conduct policy could trigger even harsher punishment, including a permanent banishment from the league.
