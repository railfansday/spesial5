Jimmy Garoppolo’s reunion may not be with the Rams

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

If the Los Angeles Rams lost Matthew Stafford for any amount of time this season, would you trust the current backup quarterback options?

Would you feel any better if an experienced veteran, like Jimmy Garoppolo, could step in?

There's nothing to indicate Stafford is at any increased risk of an injury or missing snaps for any reason. But as the NFL playoffs approach and the margin for error shrinks closer to zero, it's hard not to think about protecting yourself against calamity. That's especially true for a Rams team that has dealt with tons of injuries this year, including another serious one to a starting offensive lineman just this week.

This is relevant now because Garoppolo, who spent the 2024 and 2025 seasons in Los Angeles, is suddenly being linked to other playoff contenders, like another one of his former teams, the New England Patriots.

After initial reporting on the matter by Jordan Schultz, ESPN's Jeremy Fowler discussed the situation this week and threw some cold water on the topic but stopped short of completely killing the idea. Specifically, he mentioned that there are questions about whether or not the 12-year veteran has any actual desire to play again.

He also mentioned, however, that Garoppolo enjoyed playing on the west coast. That could work in the Rams' favor, obviously.

Don't get it twisted, this is not an earth-shattering kind of hypothetical personnel scenario. This is a quarterback who had good moments in several years as a starter, but isn't exactly a Hall of Fame candidate. He would purely be an insurance policy if Los Angeles wanted to pursue that route. And realistically, if Stafford missed more than a game or a couple games at the absolute most, things would get dire in a hurry for the Rams.

Still, the alternatives currently are Stetson Bennett, who's taken a whopping 10 offensive snaps over four seasons (all of which have come this year) and Ty Simpson, the first-round pick who still hasn't seen the field and who is a known project player.

This idea isn't completely out of left field, either, as it was discussed over the offseason as the backup job was still in flux in the wake of the Simpson pick in the NFL draft. There isn't much that's controversial about re-signing a generally inoffensive backup quarterback.

The Rams have enough personnel concerns that they likely don't have to go full bore after another reserve quarterback right now. But if push came to shove and they had to sign one out of desperation, Garoppolo still makes sense. That's the situation the Patriots are quite literally in right now with Drake Maye injured.

It would be a terrible scenario regardless, but a slightly less devastating one with a more reliable backup in the fray.
