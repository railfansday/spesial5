Houston Rockets vs. Denver Nuggets game preview

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Tonight is the third game of Houston's NBA Cup journey. A loss here eliminates Houston from moving to the next round (I think technically it doesn't but in reality it does). A win would put Houston in a decent spot to advance but would still need some help. Basically, you shouldn't be looking at NBA Cup standings at this point. Just think of tonight as a regular NBA game because it is also one of those.

The Rockets will probably be without Alperen Sengun, who has a corneal abrasion in one eye and swelling in his other eye. The Rockets have two days off after tonight, so it behooves Houston to rest their star center even if he's able to go.

When these teams met last week, the Denver Nuggets were coming off a trip to Mexico City and had to spend an extra night south of the Texas border. That meant they were tired for the game in Toyota Center, but they still decided to play everyone. In this situation, it's Houston coming in at the tail end of a four-game road trip. I'd expect the game to go in Denver's favor with that fact, especially with Houston having potentially zero big bodies to pester Nikola Jokic defensively or make him work on the other end of the court.

Just in case anyone cares, Houston has two other road trips longer than three games this season: a five-game road trip in January and a four-game trip in March. Houston does not play another back-to-back until December 13 and 14 (at Chicago and New York at home), so the Steven Adams and Fred VanVleet back-to-back decisions won't need to be addressed for another few weeks.
