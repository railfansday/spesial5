Cisse discusses desire to add assists, not even being at 50% and why he must thank Gabbia

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Alphadjo Cisse has revealed that he would like to add more assists to his game, as well as why he has Matteo Gabbia to thank above all.

Milan put on a ruthless display at San Siro on Sunday night, beating Frosinone 5-1 to move back into the top four. Calvani’s own-goal started the rout, while Goncalo Ramos, Ardon Jashari and Christian Pulisic all scored.

Cisse got his name on the scoresheet again just minutes after coming on, netting his ninth goal of the season. Diego Moreira set him up with a nice through ball in transition, and though the offside flag went up initially, VAR overturned it to award the goal.

After the game, Cisse spoke to both Sky and DAZN to give his thoughts on what was another very positive night for him personally and for the team. His comments are via MilanNews.

“I definitely always try to contribute in all positions, and I definitely prefer to play in my own position, but I’m always available in all roles. Which position? I won’t say (laughs).”

Where can you still grow and what does Amorim ask of you?

“I have to improve in many other areas. As always, scoring is always great. I’d also like to add a few assists for some of my team-mates, but the most important thing was always to bring home three points, and today we did that.”

Is there a team-mate you have to thank in particular?

“I definitely think Gabbia, because I was initially coming from Serie B, and settling into that kind of environment is never easy. But I had teammates who welcomed me very well, him especially, so for me everything was much easier.”

We also see you as a possible centre-forward…

“I’ll tell you the truth, come on, I’ll tell you. I prefer to play in the attacking midfield because, as I’ve always done, I always want to try to make plays, to entertain people. But maybe, who knows, I’ll also play as a striker, I don’t know. Let’s see…”

“At first I thought it was. Then, as they were checking it a bit, I thought it wasn’t.”

You’re Milan’s top scorer. Will we see a bicycle kick soon?

“The overhead kick used to be my specialty, but now it’s not. Let’s hope it happens. It’s always a great feeling to score, I want to add a few assists too. The important thing was the three points, and we got them.”

How far along are you in your development?

“Right now, I’m not even halfway there. Maybe even lower. There’s still a lot of work to do to get there. But I’m in the right place, and hopefully we can get there.”

Have you ever thought about what would have happened if you had gone to PSV?

“Better not to think about it. Let’s just say that I’ve always made the right decisions with my family, and thanks go to them, to the coach, to my team-mates, and I’m in the right place and on the right path.”

“Progress is the right word, because we’ve changed coaches, and it’s never easy. We’re a great team, with many champions. We still have a lot of work to do. Milan always has our backs, and that’s very important to me.”
