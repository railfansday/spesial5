Brahim Hemdani kicked out after Togo-Algeria!

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

“I am primarily responsible for this defeat. » Brahim Hemdani did not show up after the humiliation suffered by Algeria against Togo (0-3), Tuesday in Lomé, during the 4e day of the CAN 2027 qualifiers. “We are a coach and in defeat we are exposed, it’s natural”he added at a press conference. Does he still feel in danger?

“This question is not my responsibilityreplied the Fennecs coach. Today, we were not up to par, we conceded a defeat, we did not meet expectations which were to come back from here with a positive result. In any case, we continue our work. What’s happening around, the noise around, we try to get past that. »

Despite everything, it’s difficult not to hear the anger rising. On social networks, many Greens supporters are calling for his departure. And they should be heard. According to journalist Romain Molina, the former OM midfielder “will no longer be the Algerian coach” at the end of this gathering. The FAF would have already made its decision.

“The federation seeks to calm discontent by using a fuse that it placed itself in the hope of creating a (Djamel) Belmadi without resolving the other fundamental problems”he reveals on Facebook. If he had been saved until now by his results, with three victories and a draw before this heavy setback, Vladimir Petkovic’s successor will therefore already hand over the reins.
