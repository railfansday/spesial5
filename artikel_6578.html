Demellier Black Friday Sale 2026, Buy A-Lister Handbags for Less

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

There are certain brands that have claimed pride of place in our wardrobes without the gimmicks of internet-breaking campaigns or showy logos, and Demellier is one such example. Launching in its current state in 2017, the London-based brand has become a constant in the wardrobes of fashion editors and A-listers alike, including Emily Ratajkowski, Anne Hathaway, and Katie Holmes. And while the brand rarely discounts its goods, Black Friday is one of the few exceptions, and this year the sale is looking better than ever.

Through November 30, Demellier is offering shoppers 20% off sitewide—the discount automatically applied at checkout. It’s a one-week window of being able to nab the brand’s sellout styles—from the capacious Stockholm to the compact, Katie Holmes-approved Siena—for less.

“I felt there were many shortcuts taken in the fashion industry, and I wanted to do things differently – to design handbags that are modern and of the highest quality, ethically and sustainably made but also attainable and socially conscious,” DeMellier’s founder, Mireia Llusia-Lindh, previously told Vogue. “It’s so important to understand your customer. Our design process is deeply collaborative, and with a large female team of different ages, backgrounds and countries, we know that if we all love a new bag, it’s very likely to be a success, as we are customers too.”

In many ways, this is what makes DeMellier such a safe bet when it comes to shopping the Black Friday sales: you know that these bags won’t be rendered obsolete by trending silhouettes or hype-generating colorways. The brand’s commitment to hard-wearing fabrications, classic shapes and utility means that, not only will they stay relevant season to season, but they’ll work for every stage of life: whether you’re a student in need of a sturdy book bag or a working mum craving a sophisticated, one-and-done office tote.

And then there’s the fact that, full price, the entire range comes in under $750, making them more affordable than the average designer handbag, many of which run into the four figures.

From the capacious New York and the supple Miami, to the structured silhouette of the Hudson and the crossbody versatility of the Siena, it’s fair to say that DeMellier’s accessory line-up has something for everyone. Scroll on to see the bestselling styles we’re adding to basket this Black Friday.
