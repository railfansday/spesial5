Ryan Friedkin to arrive in Rome for transfer summit

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The main question Roma fans have been asking themselves in the last few hours is clear: how far can Gasperini’s team go with a major signing in the January transfer window?

For some, the answer is simple: they can win the Scudetto. This question is fueling the thoughts of Gasperini and the Giallorossi management, starting with Tony D’Amico, who will be extremely busy meeting the manager’s demands.

They’re also waiting for a signal from the Friedkins. In the coming weeks, the American ownership will be grappling with the final decisive steps for the stadium in Pietralata, UEFA’s ruling on the settlement agreement, and the January transfer window itself.

Gasperini’s mission to strengthen Roma through the January transfer window will involve the Friedkins.

As reported by Corriere dello Sport, Ryan Friedkin is expected in the capital in the coming days to discuss strategies for the upcoming transfer campaign.

A plane is ready to take off from Houston, but it’s not out of the question that it could be carrying Corbin Friedkin. The ownership’s presence in Trigoria means they’re ready to go. Gasperini doesn’t want to waste time and needs a complete squad to maintain the team’s competitiveness.
