Mora to start alongside Dybala vs Parma

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The emergency is behind us. Gasperini finally has almost his entire squad available.

Rensch and Pellegrini will be missing in Parma, but the Dutchman’s return isn’t far off. He’s targeting Paris, while Lorenzo will have to wait until 2027, writes Daniele Aloisi in Il Messaggero.

Svilar, Koné, and Dybala are back at full strength. They’re the backbone of Roma. Manu was already back against Sassuolo and took advantage of the break to get back to peak form. Today he’ll play in midfield alongside Cristante.

Mile will return to goalkeeping after two months. He’s recovered from a problem with his right toe and is 100% fit, as is Paulo, who hasn’t played since October 14th. La Joya will lead the attack alongside the usual Malen.

The third member of the attack is in doubt. Mora and Soulé are in the running. Gasp never gives up on Matias when he can, but the long trip and especially the limited training sessions with the team are putting his starting spot in doubt, as was the case in Como. He returned to Rome on Thursday and completed his final training session yesterday.

This morning, the Giallorossi won’t even take to the field for their usual “stretch” session just hours before the match. This is why the Portuguese has the upper hand. He was also called up, but didn’t play a single minute in the matches against Denmark and Wales.

And the attacking midfielder’s current form is one to be remembered for. He’s scored three goals in a row (Cagliari, Udinese, and Sassuolo) including a perfect assist for Soulé against Manchester United. Mora is looking for his fourth consecutive goal and could become the third player under 20 to do so in Serie A in the three-point era after Pato and Kean.
