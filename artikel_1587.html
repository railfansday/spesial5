Lookman’s latest injury to alter Roma’s transfer plans

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Bad news for Atlético Madrid, who risk losing Ademola Lookman for the remainder of the 2026 season.

The Nigerian striker suffered a serious knee injury during the match against Tanzania on Friday, November 13, forcing him to end his international career.

The player returned to Madrid on Tuesday, landing at Barajas Airport, to undergo tests requested by the Spanish club.

The tests revealed a more serious problem than initially thought by Nigeria’s medical staff, raising alarm at the club.

At first, the African national team’s doctors had diagnosed a mild sprain, suggesting a relatively quick recovery time.

However, the assessments carried out by Atlético Madrid have significantly altered the clinical picture. The club’s statement read: “Ademola Lookman has suffered a severe knee sprain during Nigeria’s match against Tanzania on Friday, November 13.”

The recovery program will begin today, when Lookman will begin his first physiotherapy sessions and rehabilitation work in the gym.

For a serious sprain, the minimum recovery period is generally estimated at around six weeks, although the actual duration will depend on the knee’s response to treatment. Atlético Madrid will therefore need to carefully monitor the player’s progress, without rushing it.

The possibility that the Nigerian won’t return to action before 2027 is real, given the severity of the injury and the calendar now approaching the end of the calendar year.

Roma and sporting director D’Amico remain vigilant ahead of the January transfer window: in addition to their strong support for Endrick, the Giallorossi management is keen to sign Gian Piero Gasperini, and Lookman fits the profile the Grugliasco-born coach is seeking perfectly. In the coming weeks, it will be clearer whether the former Atalanta player’s injury will pose an obstacle to this.
