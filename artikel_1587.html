CorSport: Moreira becomes irreplaceable – Belgian stars again to silence old debates

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

AC Milan paid a large sum to bring Diego Moreira to San Siro in the summer, and it hasn’t always looked like a smart signing. Now it’s impossible to say that.

After a summer of familiar names, Moreira entered the Milan sphere out of nowhere and there was excitement about what he could bring. However, his debut against Juventus reeled the line in somewhat.

Since then, he’s taken off and the game at the Allianz looks like a distant memory of a distant player.

Against Frosinone, as Corriere dello Sport (via MilanNews) writes, Moreira was one of the best players on the pitch again. It’s become an almost certainty now, his form is quite simply undeniable.

He played a major part in the second goal, launching the counter-attack with his speed. Then, he set up the fourth, assisting Alphadjo Cisse, whose rise is similar.

It’s difficult to remember that just a few months ago there were questions about the Belgian and whether the Rossoneri had wasted €50 million.

Now, though, it’s difficult to watch the Diavolo and not be drawn to his performances for the right reasons. In fact, he has become a player that Ruben Amorim can no longer do without. So, today’s headline makes complete sense: “The Moreira Effect. In three months, he’s become irreplaceable”.
