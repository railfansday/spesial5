Investing $500 Per Month in VOO Starting at Age 25 Could Build a Portfolio Worth Over $3 Million by Retirement

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Consistency and discipline are the keys to accumulating that level of wealth.

Can $500 a month really turn into a seven-figure portfolio by the time you retire? The answer is a clear yes. But you have to start early enough and target the right investment.

Not only does this show how much a simple $500 monthly investment can grow to under the right conditions, but it also demonstrates how the long-term power of compounding accelerates the longer you remain invested.

The examples above assume that an investor continues contributing through the listed age. But that doesn't necessarily have to be the case in order to hit the $3 million mark. Someone who contributes steadily through age 55 and then stops still makes it to $3 million by age 65 even with that extra 10 years of zero additional investments.

That 10% average annual return is based on historical data and is not a guarantee of future results. But it does show that years of consistency and steady monthly investing can produce big results.
