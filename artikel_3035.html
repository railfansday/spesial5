Steelers vs Eagles betting guide: Odds, props and picks

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Steelers vs Eagles betting guide: Odds, props and picks originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Steelers (6-3) vs Eagles (2-7), Sunday 1:00pm

Point spread/ML: Steelers -4.5/-225 Eagles +4.5/+188

Analysis: The Steelers have had a strong season, coming in at 6-3. They had won four straight before being embarrassed on national television by the Bengals, 38-18. The Eagles are no strangers to embarrassment this season, but for the first time in several weeks, the injury report isn't looking like a CVS receipt. WR DeVonta Smith practiced in full Thursday, as did RB Saquon Barkley, and edge rushers Jonathan Greenard and Jalyx Hunt.

HC Mike McCarthy is in his first season coaching Pittsburgh, but has plenty of experience coaching against the Eagles from his days in Green Bay and Dallas. He is 3-5 lifetime in games at the Linc. Nick Sirianni is 5-0 after a bye week, but I'm not sure any amount of time will give his team enough of an advantage.

Aaron Rodgers: 211.5 passing yards, 1.5 TD (+140/-188)

Joe Milton: 187.5 passing yards, 0.5 TD (-205/+152)

Analysis: Milton did his best with the hand he was dealt two weeks ago against the Giants (18/34/169), considering he spent much of the afternoon running for his life. He'll get help with Smith – and very possibly Tay Wicks – returning to the lineup. He should also get a boost from a Steelers pass D ranked 30th in the league.

Rodgers has had an up-and-down season; he had a four-week run where he looked bulletproof, and followed it up with two games with subpar numbers: a blowout win over Cleveland, where they didn't need much from him, and their blowout loss to the Bengals, where they didn't get much.

Picks: Milton OVER yards, Rodgers OVER yards

Analysis: I feel a lot of the game script could be determined by which Jaylen Warren shows up. He has had five games with 93+ yards this season, and four with 50 or fewer, the most recent last week vs Cincy (7/15). Dowdle had three straight games of 40+ yards before last week's 5/26.

I like Milton to make more plays with his legs, as he did vs NYG (9/64). It may be a better strategy than running Barkley between the tackles. Barkley may finally be healthy, but he can't block for himself.

Picks: Barkley UNDER yards, Milton OVER yards

DeVonta Smith – 48.5 yards, 4.5 receptions

Dallas Goedert – 30.5 yards, 3.5 receptions

Germie Bernard – 29.5 yards, 3.5 receptions

Pat Freiermuth – 20.5 yards, 2.5 receptions

Darnell Washington 20.5 yards, 2.5 receptions

Analysis: One would think Smith will want to put on a show after missing 5 of the last 6 games, and a change in QB from Tanner McKee to Milton shouldn't put a wrinkle in that. Goedert has caught 4+ passes in L5.

I can't explain what happened to DK Metcalf. 35 catches in nine games is far less than I expected, and I think Steelers fans would agree. Five catches on 28 targets over the last 3 weeks is shocking. Meanwhile, Bernard had a four-game run of 25 catches before being shutout last week. Warren has 3 catches in 6/9 this season, and 26+ yards in 8/9, he's Rodgers' main checkdown option.

Picks: Smith OVER yards, OVER receptions; Warren OVER receptions, OVER yards

Analysis: Either Steelers TE has scored in six straight. Play them both, just not in a parlay.

Picks: Barkley, Milton, Washington, Freiermuth
