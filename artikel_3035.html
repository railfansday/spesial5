Lakers survive in ugly game to down Kings

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Another sloppy game saw the Lakers struggle to the finish line before coming away victorious against the Kings on Friday, 106-104.

LA remained in a funk not dissimilar from Wednesday's loss to the Jazz, trailing by as many as 11 points. The bench helped spark a run in the third quarter to get back into the game.

A clutch three from Quentin Grimes late pushed LA ahead by three with a minute remaining in the game. Zach LaVine had a chance to win it for the Kings at the buzzer but missed.

Matisse Thybulle got off to a hot start with two triples. Everyone in the starting lineup, minus Ziarie Williams, scored a bucket. Keegan Murray and Domantas Sabonis combined for all 12 of Sacramento's first points.

LA struggled with turnovers early, committing four. Luka Dončić started scoring and was already close to double figures with nine points. Sabonis and Murray were still leading the Kings with eight points each.

At the end of the first, the game was tied. Los Angeles was shooting better than the Kings, 52% to 44%. Luka ended the quarter with 13 points.

Walker Kessler started the second period strong, scoring four points. Zach LaVine started to heat up for the Kings, scoring four points. It was Sabonis, though, who continued to keep Sacramento in the game with 15 points.

Quentin Grimes missed his first four shots in the half, but finally scored on a layup. He had six points, mostly thanks to free throws.

The game was tied again with six minutes left in the half.

Cameron Carr had an immediate impact off the bench, scoring five points. Los Angeles struggled with their offense. They were 7-20 from the field in the quarter. Sacramento went on a 9-0 scoring run.

As a result, the Kings were up by eight at halftime.

De'Andre Hunter scored first to start the third period for Sacramento. The Kings seemed to have all the momentum as Acuff Jr. also drained a triple. LA, though, had some life as they scored six in a row, forcing Sacramento to call a timeout.

As soon as it looked like the Lakers were gaining momentum, Sacramento struck back almost immediately. Thybulle drained a three to make it a one-point game before the Kings responded with a 6-0 run.

LA started gaining more defensive momentum thanks to Jarred Vanderbilt's extra effort plays. That included a sequence in which he got three rebounds in a row that resulted in Sandro Mamukelashvili converting on a three-point play.

At the end of the third, the purple and gold were only down two.

The final frame started with a 3-pointer from Kessler that received a nice crowd pop. Collin Sexton also began to take over offensively, scoring four points. Los Angeles had kicked up their defense, and in return, their offense was flowing more smoothly.

Thybulle knocked down his fourth triple of the game, giving the team a five-point lead with 7:36 left.

Williams finally got a 3-pointer to fall after several games of struggling from behind the arc. Sexton was now the only player off the bench in double figures with 10. Hunter continued his strong play for the Kings, converting on a three-point play. He had 25 points.

At the 3:30 mark, the purple and gold were up by five.

The teams swapped big shots to either tie or take the lead. Luka was poked in the eye by Murray, but remained in the game.

The Lakers went up by two after Grimes made a pair of free throws. Hunter responded with a jumper on the other end to tie it again.

With 1:12 left, Grimes knocked down a much-needed three to put the team up by three.

Sacramento missed a shot. Luka then had the ball and turned it over. The Lakers intentionally fouled up three to put LaVine on the line and he split the pair. Kessler was then fouled after rebounding the miss but missed both free throws.

LaVine had the opportunity to win with a deep three and missed.

Luka finished with 25 points, four rebounds and eight assists. Kessler ended with 15 points and three blocks. Thybulle pitched in with 14 points. Grimes logged 13 points with five rebounds and seven assists.

Carr had five points off the bench. Vanderbilt scored four points with four rebounds and two assists. Mamu finished with nine points, nine rebounds and two assists. Sexton and Jake LaRavia combined for 16 points.

The Lakers' next game will be against the Utah Jazz on Monday at 7:00 PM PT.
