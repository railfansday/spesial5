Voters Put the AI Data Center Boom on Notice

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Bloomberg News tech reporter Brody Ford explains how voter backlash against data centers could disrupt the rapid buildout of infrastructure powering the AI boom. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Ford discusses the risks for heavily indebted AI companies and whether tougher promises from newly elected officials will translate into meaningful restrictions on development.
