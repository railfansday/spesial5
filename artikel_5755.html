These Cars Look Best On Aftermarket Wheels

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Sometimes, a car's factory wheels just aren't right. Too small, too big, wrong color, wrong design — automakers get it right once in a while, but more often than not they're boring at best. Luckily, we have aftermarket wheels. Unluckily, those don't always work either, and in some cases they're worse than the OEM wheel they're there to replace. That's why, earlier this week, I asked you all to tell me the cars you think look best on aftermarket wheels. Today, we're seeing what you said.

This is a great one, because your answers are truly across the board. We've got cars from Japan, Europe, and the U.S.; luxury sedans and tiny roadsters and everything in between. Plenty of you even submitted cars you've owned and put aftermarket wheels on in the past, which shows a level of confidence in your own decisions that I respect. Let's dive into all of your answers.

As some have said, most cars look great on aftermarket wheels if the wheels have been chosen correctly and fit the car well. I still shake my head when I see wheels that stick out that clearly were not intended for that car but were probably a Facebook Marketplace deal, they should have turned down the deal instead of looking ridiculous.

If I had to pick though, a VW GTI on some aftermarket BBS wheels is always a classic that cannot be beat. Looking almost OEM, but not quite and goes together like peanut butter and jelly. Nothing better than a clean GTI on some BBS wheels that bring out the lines of the car.....chefs kiss.

A Miata. They look great with Watanabes, Heliums, BBS wirebaskets, and just about anything else. Stock wheels are okay on NC and ND, but in my opinion the early stock wheels were not very attractive.

1990s Thunderbirds looked great with Cobra 5-spoke wheels.

I believe base C8 Corvettes need aftermarket wheels to look good. The Z06, ZR1, Grand Sport and E-Ray look fine without them but the base NEEDS them!
