Falcons vs. Vikings injury report: Matthew Bergeron, four others ruled out

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Greetings, Falcoholics! We hope that you all have had a wonderful Thanksgiving! This week the Atlanta Falcons are facing one of the toughest defenses in the league, the Minnesota Vikings. We have been monitoring the health of both teams all week, and we now have details on some players who will not suit up on Sunday.

Looking at the Falcons first, they ruled out some key players for Sunday, with the most notable likely being left guard Matthew Bergeron. With Bergeron sitting, Kyle Hinton is technically the next man up. Also, the team has ruled out both Zachariah Branch and Jahan Dotson for this weekend. Expect to see a lot more Olamide Zaccheaus and Chris Blair in the lineup.

The Vikings also have some notable players ruled out in: receiver Jordan Addison, offensive tackle Christian Darrisaw, and corner Isaiah Rodgers. Their veteran safety Harrison Smith is dealing with a thigh injury and is listed as questionable to play.
