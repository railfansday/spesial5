JD Vance says he'll decide 'sometime next year' about 2028 presidential run

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

U.S. Vice President JD Vance said Monday he will make a decision next year about whether to run for president in 2028.

"We'll figure it out sometime next year," he told Fox News host Laura Ingraham in an interview that aired Monday evening.

"I'm not an expert in this but my understanding with a presidential campaign is there's a lot of infrastructure that you have to set up, fundraising, all that stuff and so you can't declare a month before the Iowa caucus that you're going to run for president," he said. "So, you know, I haven't made a decision."

Vance has yet to openly declare any ambition to succeed President Donald Trump when his current term ends, but speculation that the vice president has his sights set on the White House has been mounting for some time. In the wake of the GOP's crushing defeat in the U.S. midterm election on Nov. 3, anonymous sources involved in campaigns told Reuters Vance was among the high-profile Republicans who had begun early preparations for a 2028 run.

Candidates typically announce their intentions to run in the election in the spring of 2027, with parties holding nominating conventions to formally select their presidential candidates between July and September.

Polymarket currently slates Vance as the frontrunner to win the Republican presidential nomination for the 2028 election, with the prediction market giving him a 37% chance of becoming the nominee. Pressed on 2028 in Monday's interview, Vance said: "When I make a decision, I will be all in on whatever that decision is."

Candidates vying for the Republican nomination face the challenge of convincing voters the party can veer away from unpopular Trump-era policies without alienating its MAGA base.

Vance himself has blamed the Iran war for the midterm election outcome.

"I remain hopeful about our country. To the people who are pissed off and want to say, "I told you so": you're right, you did tell us so," he said in a lengthy X post as the results were coming in. "Take that energy into electing Republicans who share your worldview. To the people who are just pissed at the results, you should be. Let's take that anger and get back to the work of saving our country."

In his interview with Fox News on Monday, Vance said the Republicans got "shellacked" in the midterms.

"We have to take messages from that, but the message that we have to take is that the American people want prosperity, they want freedom, they want sovereignty within their own country and they want us to do more of the good things that we've done," he said.

He added that lawmakers needed to double down on "economic nationalism" and bring "prosperity to the homeland" to deal with "economic pain" in the United States.
