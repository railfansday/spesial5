Sixers get lucky with their 14th win as Herro can’t get off game-tying three in time

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Sixers just outlasted the Milwaukee Bucks by a literal second, hanging on to win 132-129 Saturday afternoon, notching their fifth win in a row and improving to 14-3 on the season.

Joel Embiid did a lot to make up for a subpar shooting night, finishing with a team-high 26 points, a season-high 17 rebounds, and nine assists shooting 7-of-17 from the floor. After missing last game with a foot sprain, Tyrese Maxey had one of his quietest games of the year finishing with 17 points on 4-of-11 shooting.

LeBron James played quarterback for much of the day, finishing with 21 points, six rebounds and six assists going 9-of-14. VJ Edgecombe shot 5-of-12 from the floor to finish with 16 points with three rebounds and six assists. Tyler Herro led all scorers with 50 but his three to tie the game just didn't beat the buzzer.

Jaylen Brown and Dean Wade missed their second straight game, both dealing with hamstring strains, along with Adem Bona, who's missed the last eight. Milwaukee was without Ousmane Dieng.

The Sixers got two of their easiest buckets all season to open the game off of cuts. James went down the baseline and Edgecombe flashed to the middle of the paint. Their pull-up jump shooting was a different story early. Embiid even had a midrange blocked by Myles Turner as the Sixers struggled to capitalize on a 1-of-8 start for the Bucks.

Embiid also had a layup attempt blocked by Turner as he started the game 0-of-4 from the field. His other two jumpers falling just off the rim. On the other end the Sixers allowed two offensive rebounds to turn into second-chance points as the Bucks' offense was the first to find a rhythm.

The Sixers' offense settled in after an early timeout from Nick Nurse. They got a couple of open threes from Anfernee Simons and Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, Edgecombe broke Turner down off the dribble, and LeBron opened up plenty of space on a drive to hit Embiid with a lob. James made a great one-handed pass cross court to find KCP in the corner a few possessions later but the three rimmed out. KCP and Embiid also both missed fast break layups that halted a run.

Jabari Walker checked in next to Embiid at the four like he has in recent games and was rewarded for cutting by Embiid shortly after checking in. Dominick Barlow made his season debut a few minutes later, relieving Embiid. Labaron Philon Jr. remained in the rotation and picked up a floater, but also fouled Herro attempting a three right before the quarter ended. Herro made all three to give the Bucks a one-point lead after one.

Barlow's first big impact was tipping back a missed Maxey floater for Philly's first points of the second. On the next possession he ran down the floor to catch a lob after LeBron had just about singlehandedly created. It wasn't all perfect — the Bucks scored six straight field goals in the paint, but it was a fine first shift back for Barlow.

LeBron was easily the best player on the floor for the Sixers. His ability to lead people open gave them a lot of easy baskets that were much needed for an offense struggling to hit jumpers. They were also struggling to finish off defensive possessions as the Bucks picked up their 10th second-chance point on eight offensive rebounds.

Maxey got downhill quick and drew a foul which gave Nurse a chance to use a timeout. Coming out of that break, it seemed like there was an ever bigger focus to run quickly, whether if it was after a make or a miss. After Maxey zoomed through for a layup, Edgecombe got down the floor twice, setting up Walker and Embiid for buckets at the rim.

It was weird because the Sixers weren't playing great defense by any means, but the Bucks still had a bunch of airballs in the first half, more than any NBA team should. The misses the Sixers got were ugly, but they hadn't made life hard on Herro at all — he was able to get to just about every spot on the floor.

The first trouble Herro ran into all night was when he tried to contest an Embiid dunk in transition and took a shot to the face while unsuccessfully doing so. The dunk seemed to finally get Embiid going as he was able to bully past Turner for an and-1 after trying all half to do so. That capped off an 8-0 run that gave the Sixers a one-point lead at the break.

Sandwiched in between a pair of free throws from Maxey and Embiid, LeBron got to the basket for another big dunk. A few possessoins later, Embiid finally got a midrange to go over Turner. Not much had changed defensively as the Bucks scored on their first four possessions of the second half.

The Sixers definitley got more comfortable in their half court offense as the game went on. After paint buckets from Maxey and Edgecombe, KCP knocked a three to give the Sixers a lead they were able to hang on to for more than one possession. Aside from losing Herro once on the perimeter as they came down the floor, the defense had improved as well. Embiid had started to do a good job of finishing possessions as he passed his season high in rebounds.

Simons and KCP were the only Sixers who had made a three to this point in the game, so it was a good thing they were both feeling it. Simons ripped eight straight points to give the Sixers their first double-digit lead of the night.

Embiid threw a nice one-handed pass into the corner for Edgecombe who finally became the third Sixer of the night to hit a three. Edgecombe tried to run quickly off a made free throw, but had his layup attempt blocked by Kel'el Ware. Simons got a chance for yet another buzzer-beater, but his midrange attempt didn't fall, neither did Embiid's humorous attempt to get a shot off with 0.2 seconds remaining. The Sixers held a six-point lead after the third.

Barlow did get up a corner three in his first shift and airballed it pretty bad. He got another chance in his second shift and got plenty of arc to bury it this time. LeBron found him from the high-post for a layup on the next possession to prompt an early Bucks timeout.

Shortly after James found Barlow cutting to the basket again and hit him for the layup, drawing a foul in the process. The Sixers lost their grip on Herro coming out of the timeout though, letting him get to the basket a couple of times. The Sixers nearly forced a turnover, but left AJ Green open in the process, who was able to hit a three to cut it back to a one-possession game.

After calling a timeout, the Sixers put the ball in LeBron's hands, who was able to back in his way in for a bucket, putting back his own missed floater. Thanks to another offensive rebound from Barlow on the next possession, Maxey was able to scoot to the basket for two more. James bullied his way for two more baskets, drawing a foul on one, to restore order a bit.

Milwaukee was able to respond with a basket nearly every time the Sixers scored a big one, but they couldn't get stops with LeBron locked in. They ran the same play just about every time down the floor, having Maxey come up to screen for LeBron while Embiid had the ball, and it got LeBron open every time.

Herro missed an open floater down six with a minute, which seemed like it would put the Bucks away for good. Not only did Embiid come up empty on both Sixers possessions, but the Bucks scored quick off those misses, cutting it back down to two with 12 seconds left. The Sixers hurried to beat the Bucks down the floor and Turner had to foul Embiid, picking up his sixth of the game. Embiid buried both free throws, but Brayden Burries stepped back into the corner, burying the open three to cut it to one with 4.8 to go.

Embiid went way into the backcourt on the inbound to take three seconds off the clock before being fouled. It was hard to tell from press row, but he definitely stepped out of bounds running towards the sideline and got lucky the refs missed it. He made both free throws to make it a three-point game with 1.5 seconds left and the Bucks had one timeout left to advance. The Sixers put Barlow in for Embiid. The Bucks sent Herro down the other end of the court who took a dribble before getting his three up. He buried the shot, silencing the crowd, but replay showed that he just didn't get it off in time, making the Sixers very lucky to escape this one in regulation.
