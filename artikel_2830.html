Week 11 injury report for Commanders

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Washington Commanders OC Lucas Patrick (concussion) practiced in full this week and is listed as questionable on Saturday, Nov. 21's final injury report for Week 11's game against the Cincinnati Bengals. CB Amik Robertson (ankle) is questionable after practicing in a limited fashion on Saturday. S Quan Martin (shoulder) practiced in a limited fashion on Thursday and Friday, but practiced in full on Saturday and has no injury designation. WR Treylon Burks (foot) did not practice on Friday but practiced on Thursday and Saturday and has no injury designation. DE Deatrich Wise Jr. (NIR-Rest) did not practice on Saturday and has no injury designation. OG Nick Allegretti (chest), TE John Bates (shoulder), QB Jayden Daniels (left elbow), TE Chig Okonkwo (back), S Tyler Owens (ankle), OT Andrew Wylie (hip), and TE Colson Yankoff (knee) all practiced in full for all three days of practice and have no injury designation.
