Francisco Lindor Reacts To Mets Landing Tarik Skubal

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The New York Mets have reportedly agreed to a nine-year, $450 million deal with Tarik Skubal.

Like many Mets fans, their star shortstop, Francisco Lindor, is excited.

Lindor posting smiley faces is pretty much how all Mets fans will be feeling.

Skubal was the top free agent available this offseason and arguably the best pitcher in baseball. His reported deal will make him the highest-paid pitcher in history in terms of average annual value and total value. The Mets were desperate for an ace, and they have one now.

The Mets now have Juan Soto, Lindor and Skubal. That's three legitimate superstars who have a case to be the best players at their positions.

However, they have to be able to perform like superstars when the time comes. Lindor did not have a great season after getting MVP votes in the previous four years. If the Mets want to get to the World Series, Lindor will have to play like the star he is being paid to be.

Lindor is signed to a $341 million deal and Soto is signed to a $765 million deal. Adding Skubal to the payroll now will just put more pressure on the team to produce a championship.
