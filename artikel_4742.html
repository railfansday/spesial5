30 High-Protein Meals That Are Just as Easy to Whip Up as They Are Delicious

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

So much more exciting than grilled chicken breast.

As a society, we’re in our protein era, and that’s a good thing. The macronutrient is super important for building and repairing muscles, everyday body functionality, and immune and hormone health. Plus, it helps keep you full.

Since it's so important, it's also no surprise that you need quite a bit of it, even though pulling that off might get a little tricky sometimes. While the recommended dietary allowance for protein has remained the same for 30 years—at 0.36 grams per pound of total body weight, or 0.8 grams per kilogram—research now suggests that we need more like one gram per pound. (Psst: that higher number is especially important for anyone who regularly hits the gym.) One easy way to make sure you get there? Following high-protein recipes, where all the calculations are already done for you.

The Women's Health kitchen has you covered. Every single one of the high-protein recipes below packs in at least 20 grams of the macro. These high-protein meals offer a variety of beyond-delicious ways to get more of it into your diet. From high-protein breakfast recipes to steak dinners and meatless options, there's something for everyone. Ready to get cooking?
