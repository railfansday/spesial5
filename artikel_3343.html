Blade Runner 2099 Season 2 - Renewal Status & Everything We Know

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Warning! This article contains SPOILERS for Blade Runner 2099.After a somewhat ambiguous ending, there's room for Blade Runner 2099 to continue, but there's no guarantee that it will get a second season yet. The ending of Blade Runner 2099 season 1 was, paradoxically, both definitive and ambiguous. The fate of characters like Olwen, who went off-world, and Elias, who decided to lead the human revolution, are set in stone, but Cora's future and the fate of replicant lifespans as a whole were still up in the air.

At the moment, it seems like the story of Blade Runner 2099 still has room to continue. Cora could continue her work as a Blade Runner and possibly feed inside information to Elias, the Tyrell Corporation's plans are interesting enough to warrant a new season, and it seems like there's a revolution brewing. Any number of these plot threads could be meaty enough to base a second season around, even if it means Olwen will be left out.

Despite some mixed reviews for Blade Runner 2099, the franchise itself is also quite strong. Prime Video likely wants to continue using the Blade Runner IP, especially since Ridley Scott himself worked on the show as an executive producer. It would probably be profitable to renew Blade Runner 2099 season 2, and there's plenty of space to do so, but there's even more reason to believe the series will end with its first season.

Unfortunately for viewers who wanted a more definitive ending for Cora and the Nexus-1 metamorphosis procedure, Blade Runner 2099 creator Silka Luisa said the show was envisioned as a limited series. Luisa said she wanted 2099 to have a definitive beginning, middle, and end, and to be a complete, standalone series, not unlike a longer version of a Blade Runner movie.

It was always considered as a limited series. Part of making the transition from film to TV is a really big jump for something that’s as cinematic as Blade Runner, and so, in that sense, it was really important to us that we told a story that, in a way, is like a movie in the sense that there’s a beginning, there’s a middle, and there’s an end. So we went into it with Alcon, with all of our producing partners, knowing we really want to tell a complete story. Eight episodes—I mean, eight’s my lucky number, that’s the number I would have picked, but it felt like the right amount for the story we were planning on telling.

Evidently, the ambiguity surrounding Cora and replicant lifespans is how Luisa planned to end Blade Runner 2099. The actual ending also supports that idea; Cora's future is uncertain and, frankly, not very important to the story of empathy and humanity that Luisa was trying to tell. As such, Blade Runner 2099 season 2 probably isn't happening because Silka Luisa doesn't want to extend it beyond the ending she already wrote.

There is, however, a chance that Blade Runner 2099 isn't over just yet. Executive producer Andrew Kosove said that Blade Runner 2099 season 2 could happen, if "the fans clamor loud enough." Kosove also mentioned that there are other stories to tell in the Blade Runner universe in the same year, which could be an indication that Cora's story could continue. Given the show's mixed reception so far, however, that doesn't seem likely.

Though Blade Runner 2099 is most likely ending, that doesn't mean Blade Runner as a franchise has reached its conclusion. As previously mentioned, 2099 created quite a bit of ambiguity surrounding the state of the world, replicant lifespans, and the burgeoning human revolution started by Elias. There's plenty of room for another sequel set later on in the Blade Runner timeline, albeit not directly connected to 2099.

Alcon producers Broderick Johnson and Andrew Kosove offer a confident update on Blade Runner's future beyond the new Prime Video series.

Andrew Kosove also expressly stated that "at some point in the future, there will be the next iteration of Blade Runner. You can be sure of it." That's about as definitive as it gets, and fairly sure proof that Blade Runner isn't over yet. The only questions remaining, then, is what the next Blade Runner project will be, when it will take place, and when it could come to streaming or movie theaters.

Unfortunately, the future of Blade Runner after 2099 is very much so up in the air. There are plenty of possibilities, like focusing on the human uprising against the replicants or focusing on replicants working off-world, that could go further into the future of the timeline. The franchise could also do an "in-betweenquel" set in the years between the movies and shows. For now, fans will just have to settle for one season of Blade Runner 2099.
