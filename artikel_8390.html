Host on Airbnb if travelers book stays near you and your city, lease, or homeowners association allows short-term rentals. Price each night to leave a profit after Airbnb’s 15.5% fee and your costs for cleaning, supplies, utilities, insurance, and income tax, or rent the space long-term instead.

Airbnb pays you to host travelers in a spare room or whole home, and it suits owners in places people visit who can clean between stays. A room booked 120 nights a year at $90 a night brings in $10,800, and Airbnb’s 15.5% fee leaves $9,126 before cleaning, supplies, utilities, insurance, and income tax.

Hosting on Airbnb pays for owners whose city allows short stays and whose space books often enough to cover cleaning, supplies, and insurance after Airbnb’s fee. If your city bans short stays or you can’t clean between guests, sign a long-term tenant or rent the spare room to a lodger instead.
