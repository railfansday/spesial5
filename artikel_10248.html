Every single pair of Xreal smart glasses is now on sale for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Xreal offers an impressively wide range of smart display glasses that give you a massive virtual display without the massive price tag.

Xreal makes my favorite pairs of smart display glasses, and all of the company’s standalone pairs are on sale for the big weekend. Xreal glasses plug into any device that supports USB-C display out (your phone, handheld gaming system, laptop, etc.) and deliver a giant virtual screen only visible inside the glasses, giving you a personal home theater to enjoy your content on. Here’s the Black Friday sale in ascending price order:

Personally, I would recommend the least expensive of the bunch, the Xreal xbx a01+. It’s on sale for $249 ($50 off) and sports the most stylish looks of the three options, plus the lightest weight at 62g, the brightest display at 1600 nits, and class-leading 3,840Hz PWM dimming. It’s even got smooth tracking that’ll keep you from feeling sick when using them in a moving vehicle. I find these the most comfortable, by far, and have taken them with me on every flight since they launched a few months ago in the summer.

Choosing between the Xreal 1S and Xreal One Pro is a little harder, so let’s cover the pricing and differences that you’ll care most about. Xreal 1S is on sale for $379 ($70 off) and offers true native 3DoF spatial tracking, built-in real-time 3D conversion of 2D content, and a fully adjustable screen size and distance. Those are all upgrades over the xbx a01+ model.

Xreal One Pro is on sale for $499 ($100 off) and upgrades the Xreal 1S experience with prism lenses and a wider field of view. There are no other functional differences between One Pro and 1S other than the wider field of view and flatter lenses, so if those aren’t a big deal to you, the Xreal 1S is the “Goldilocks” pick because of its price and the range of features it offers.

Xreal 1 Pro+, on sale for the first time, upgrades the experience even further, offering a peak 1,200-nit brightness, a new AI HDR option, upgrading the 2D-to-3D conversion to 60FPS, adding extra viewing modes, and swapping out the nose pad for a more comfortable design.

Lastly, is Xreal Asus ROG R1 glasses, which are also on sale for the first time. You can choose between the normal style or the 20th anniversary style for the same $749 price. This one comes with a 240Hz display, an entire dock that connects to your PC or console of choice, more settings than any other Xreal glasses to date (making it feel like a proper monitor replacement), and that trademark Asus ROG style.

If you haven’t used a pair of smart display glasses from Xreal before, the setup is simple. Just plug the USB-C cable into your phone, laptop, or gaming handheld, and you’ll be greeted with a giant virtual screen that floats in front of your face. It’s like having a huge TV on the go, and it always looks great thanks to the micro-OLED image quality and tinted lenses. Only Xreal Aura differs from this setup, as it’s a completely standalone, VR headset-like experience in a significantly smaller form factor.
