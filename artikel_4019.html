Eight Games Are Leaving Xbox Game Pass In Late November 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Microsoft has revealed the next eight games leaving Xbox Game Pass in late November 2026, with all of these being highlighted as "leaving soon" on the official Xbox mobile app.

Two of them particularly stand out to us - Aliens: Dark Descent and Banishers: Ghosts of New Eden. Both of these are well worth a play before they leave the service, as are the likes of Moving Out 2 and Totally Reliable Delivery Service.

As is always the case with Xbox Game Pass leavers, you'll find discounts of at least 20% on most if not all of these titles over the next few weeks, and some may even be reduced by a lot more in the upcoming Xbox Black Friday Sale.

There's obviously plenty of good stuff still to come to Game Pass before the year is out as well, including quite a few December titles that have already been announced. We're definitely not done yet for XGP in 2026, folks!
