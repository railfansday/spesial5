Frontier Airlines Passenger Refuses To Leave, Demands Flight Attendant Apologize To Her 4-Year Old Over Toilet Dispute

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A Frontier Airlines passenger says she refused to get off her San Francisco–Denver flight until a flight attendant apologized to her four year old son. The argument began with a request to flush the toilet after the frightened boy had left the lavatory. The mother says things escalated when she overheard the crew member making fun of his fear. I don’t think asking for a little help with a frightened child is outrageous. If the flight attendant mocked him, an apology was appropriate. But the mother wasn’t entitled to make getting off the aircraft conditional on receiving one. She says her son was afraid of loud toilet flushes and needed the bathroom about 40 minutes before landing. She tried offering headphones and offering to flush for him. Eventually she reassured him that they could ask a flight attendant to help. She says she asked the flight attendant near the lavatory to flush after they’d washed their hands and left. The response was, He can flush it. It’s not that loud. The flight attendant added that this was a new plane. The mother says she dropped the request, but while she and her son were inside the lavatory, she heard the crew member talking to a colleague: Can you believe that mom? Scared of a toilet? Come on. She says her son heard it too and asked whether they were talking about him. The mother flushed the toilet herself, with her son waiting outside and the door closed. At deplaning she confronted the flight attendant and demanded an apology to the boy. She says the crewmember responded “Have a good day,” so she announced: I’m not getting off this airplane until you apologize to my son for literally making fun of him for being afraid of something. A pilot emerged from the cockpit, the flight attendant apologized, and she left. She also says a couple of passengers applauded and another mother praised her. She’s since posted a clarification: she says she also asked the flight attendant to stand with her son while she flushed. She also says her analytics show viewers watched an average of 23% of the original video and that’s why so many people seem to have missed that she did, in fact, flush the toilet herself. She wanted help pressing a button once they were away, or standing beside the child briefly. A polite refusal would be reasonable. The claim that it’s a new airplane doesn’t mean the toilet isn’t loud. Aircraft vacuum toilets can be startling.
