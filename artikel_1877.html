Unai Emery's 'man-management' crucial to Aston Villa star's new deal - Keith Wyness

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Aston Villa centre-back Victor Lindelof is proving to be another example of Unai Emery's brilliant man-management.

That is according to former Villa chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Spanish boss has the squad in the palm of his hand at Villa Park.

This comes after the news earlier this week that Lindelof has signed a one-year extension with the Villans, with his previous deal having been due to expire this summer.

Wyness believes that the 32-year-old is yet another example of Emery polishing a "rough diamond" and getting the player back to his best.

The Swedish international had been cast aside at Manchester United when Villa picked him up back in 2025, and he has been one of the first names on the team sheet this season.

Lindelof has played every single minute in the Premier League so far this season, and has now been rewarded with a one-year extension to his deal.

Villa's former chief Wyness - who served as CEO at Villa Park between 2016 and 2018 and now runs a consultancy business advising elite clubs - believes that Lindelof's rise is similar to the one that Ross Barkley has enjoyed at Villa.

Both players arrived at the club as veterans and were expected to make a limited impact, but have ended up being heavily involved this term.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness outlined that Lindelof's new deal is yet more evidence of Emery's "man-management" skills.

"It is a good deal for Villa, and it's exactly the same as Barkley, and that's the whole thing of picking up a rough diamond and polishing it again and getting it going. That's one of the most cost-effective ways to do it," he said.

"Obviously Emery's man-management has worked extremely well. He's getting the best out of both of them, and they're both obviously talented professionals who still have something to give.

"I think there are a lot of these players around that need that second life or third life, often in some cases. But yes, look, he's performing, he's obviously enjoying his football and that's all about the environment he's in.

"The atmosphere around the squad: people are responding to Emery; he's obviously leading, and his leadership is accepted fully, and Lindelof and the senior pros are all enjoying it. So why would you want to leave a happy situation? And Lindelof's obviously feeding off it."

Former Villa scout Mick Brown recently told Football Insider that his former club "won't entertain" any offers for midfielder Boubacar Kamara ahead of the January window.

The Frenchman has emerged as a surprise target for Chelsea, as Xabi Alonso's side continue their efforts to rebuild their midfield.

However, Brown does not believe Villa will consider selling Kamara in the middle of the campaign, with injuries badly hindered their midfield depth at the moment.

Amadou Onana is currently sidelined with a long-term injury, and Emery is desperate to avoid leaving himself short of options.
