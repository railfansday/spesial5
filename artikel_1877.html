Arsenal wonderkid Max Dowman smashes Jude Bellingham England Under-21 record

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Arsenal wonderkid Max Dowman has broken two England Under-21 records previously held by Jude Bellingham in a matter of days.

The 16-year-old became the youngest player ever to play for the Three Lions at Under-21 level when he came off the bench on Friday in a 1-0 defeat to Portugal.

Dowman then started against France at Aston Gate on Monday night, scoring a dramatic late winner in a 3-2 victory.

His appearance against Portugal broke Bellingham’s record as youngest England Under-21 player and his goal against France broke the Real Madrid superstar’s record as youngest to score at this level.

Dowman was 16 years and 320 days when he scored in Bristol on Monday, while Bellingham was 17 years and 67 days old when he made his debut against Kosovo in 2020, scoring on his first appearance for England Under-21s.

Also on the scoresheet for England in the friendly win over France were Tottenham’s Mikey Moore and Manchester City’s Divin Mubama, who is on loan at Southampton this season.

Manchester City’s Rico Lewis captained Lee Carsley’s side, while the likes of Brighton’s Jack Hinshelwood and Liverpool’s Trey Nyoni were also in the starting XI.

It was Dowman who was making yet more history, though, having already become the youngest player to score in the Premier League and to make an appearance in the Champions League.

When Arsenal won the title last season, he also became the youngest player to lift the Premier League trophy.

The incredible talent does not turn 17 until December 31 and will hope to get more run outs for Arsenal before then, although he has only had one very brief substitute appearance for the Gunners since his red card against Fleetwood Town in the Carabao Cup on October 27.

Arsenal boss Mikel Arteta was clearly fuming with Dowman when he was shown a straight red card for reacting to a heavy challenge against Fleetwood, giving the youngster stern words as he left the field.

Arteta said: ‘I haven’t seen the reaction to my reaction, whether it is positive or negative. But you need to understand the context and the relationship that I have with him and what we discussed just before we went out.

‘But, I don’t know. You can have another reaction. If you see the reaction of my father and my mother with me some days you might say “I don’t like the reaction”. For me, it was the best reaction to be who I am.’

He added: ‘The reaction when I don’t win, you want to know what my reaction is? I am [angry] big time.

‘So, we need to be ready for that. This is the environment and this is the reality, otherwise we are in Disneyland. We are not in Disneyland, we are in the Premier League and this level.

‘We look after him so much that you cannot imagine. But at the same time we are going to demand from him, like everybody else, because he needs to earn the right to play. Because the other boys, what are they going to tell me? You need to be fair.’
