Man Utd told Chelsea star would be ‘good signing’ for Mauricio Pochettino

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Mikael Silvestre has encouraged Manchester United to explore a move for Estevao – but doubts the forward could be coaxed to the Theatre of Dreams as he appears intent on ‘fighting for his place’ at Chelsea.

United spent in the region of £160million on new signings in the summer, with club chiefs showing they were prepared to back Michael Carrick following an impressive third-place finish.

But any optimism that had grown amongst the fanbase in the back-end of last season rapidly faded as a 2-0 defeat away to Chelsea last month extended the Red Devils’ miserable winless run to nine matches in all competitions.

A stunning comeback victory over Roma followed by a 4-4 thriller at home to Aston Villa in the Premier League were ultimately not enough to save Carrick, who was relieved of his duties last Thursday.

Former Tottenham and Chelsea head coach Mauricio Pochettino was subsequently announced as Carrick’s successor, with the Argentine officially vacating his position with the United States national team to start life in the role later this week.

Naturally, attentions have quickly turned towards the January transfer window, when United will be expected to splash the cash in the hope of boosting their prospects for the second half of the campaign.

United chiefs are set to resurrect their pursuit of a left-back as a priority to provide support for Luke Shaw after missing out on Newcastle’s Lewis Hall in the summer.

It’s understood the Old Trafford giants will also be in the market for attacking reinforcements on the back of a desperately disappointing start to the season for the likes of Matheus Cunha, Bryan Mbeumo and Benjamin Sesko.

According to Silvestre, Chelsea winger Estevao could represent a ‘good signing’ for United, with the Brazilian having made just one start in the Premier League under Xabi Alonso.

That start happened to come against United, though, and the 19-year-old was widely praised for his performance as goals from Morgan Rogers and Cole Palmer proved decisive at Stamford Bridge.

‘Well, Estevao played against United for Chelsea, and I think he’s shown why he should feature more,’ Silvestre told Betarades.gr.

‘It looks like he’s going to fight for his place in the team, so I don’t anticipate United making a move for him.

‘But he’s a good player for sure. He’s playing for the national team of Brazil, and I like his style. If he were available, he would be a good signing for United.’

Asked why Newcastle and England star Hall may ‘make sense’ for United at left-back, Silvestre replied: ‘Lewis Hall had an impressive season with Newcastle, and he’s been on the club’s radar for sure.

‘The club made an attempt to sign him and other fullbacks, but couldn’t get it done.

‘They now have to rely on Luke Shaw, Diego Dalot, on Noussair Mazraoui, and on Patrick Dorgu, who has also been injured for quite some time. So that’s not been helpful either.’

Another high-profile name heavily linked with United in recent weeks has been Dominic Calvert-Lewin, who has scored five goals in his opening nine appearances for Leeds this term.

But Silvestre, who won five Premier League titles with the Red Devils, believes the England striker would only be brought in as a stop-gap for his former club up front.

‘I think Dominic Calvert-Lewin would be a stop-gap,’ the ex-United and France defender explained.

‘I don’t think it’s a long-term. He doesn’t belong in a Manchester United squad. So I don’t know where this is coming from.

‘Yes, you need to have a look around for strikers because of the Sesko situation with his injury, and we are short in that department, but I wouldn’t consider Calvert-Lewin as an option.’

All eyes will be on Anfield on Sunday when Pochettino takes charge of United for the first time against bitter rivals Liverpool.

Pochettino takes over the reins from Carrick with the club languishing down in 18th place in the Premier League, just point better off than rock-bottom Crystal Palace.
