Leeds United and Hull City’s international travels compared to Premier League rivals including Arsenal and Everton

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A ranking of the international break miles travelled by players on the books of Leeds United, Hull City, Arsenal, Everton and more

The international break is coming to a close and the return of Premier League football is on the horizon.

Both of the division’s Yorkshire clubs face stern tests this weekend, with Leeds United set to visit Chelsea and Hull City gearing up to host Brighton & Hove Albion.

Each of the league’s 20 clubs have seen players jet off to represent their respective countries over the last week and will be hoping they report back without injury issues.

They will also be mindful of the miles travelled and minutes played in the midst of a gruelling fixture schedule.

Select Car Leasing have complied data to assess which Premier League club’s players have done the most travelling.
