The 22s, 10 mad minutes, Chivu’s confusion: DAZN reveal unseen details from Milan-Inter

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

After AC Milan’s win against Inter, DAZN have published the latest Bordocam episode, offering a complete view of the game.

A fourth win in a row for Milan, the most recent Derby della Madonnina will be remembered fondly for several reasons. Not only was Ricardo Kaka inducted into the Hall of Fame, but the Rossoneri dominated their biggest rivals.

As MilanVibes relays from the most recent episode of Bordocam, a behind-the-scenes look at the Derby della Madonnina was offered. Of course, the result will certainly make Rossonero want to watch it back.

First and foremost, it showed the night of the 22s. Starting with Ricardo Kaka’s induction into the Hall of Fame before moving to an encounter between referee Guida and Nicolo Barella, reminding him that Luka Modric would be in the middle with him.

After the first goal, it shows the bench celebrating, but not Mike Maignan or Mario Gila; the former remained in his box, whilst the latter had his shirt replaced. On the Inter bench, there was frustration, and a meeting with Yann Bisseck, who mismarked for the goal.

For the second, it was a focus on Carlos Fernandes, who was the focal point of celebrations for Adrien Rabiot’s goal.

After Alessandro Bastoni’s header, there were several confused reactions to Mike Maignan’s saves, with members of the Inter staff repeating questions to themselves.

Diego Moreira’s free-kick brought around calls for calmness from Maignan, but Chivu instantly wanted his team to attack with more intensity, admitting that his team needed to ‘raise the bar’. His team had a few chances in the second period, and changed systems a few times, but nothing could be done to change the scoreline.
