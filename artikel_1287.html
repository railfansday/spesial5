Spain vs England: What Happened to Spark Tunnel Bust-Up

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The world champions Spain welcomed England to the Metropolitano for a UEFA Nations League group game on Sunday and tempers flared between the two sides.

Thomas Tuchel's men dominated the first half, pressing aggressively and affording the 2026 World Cup winners no time or space. Jude Bellingham came within inches of opening the scoring early on.

But Harry Kane netted his 94th England goal from the penalty spot shortly before half-time after the Bayern Munich forward was brought down inside the area. With the visitors leading 1-0 going into the break, a scuffle broke out just before the players reached the tunnel.

Clement Turpin blew the half-time whistle, but the players couldn't even reach the tunnel before things got out of hand. It appeared that England's Ben White and Spain's Fabian Ruiz were at the centre of the flare-up.

Almost everyone else quickly got involved, either to have their say in the disagreement or to act as peacemakers. Kane was seen asking the referee to step in. Watch the video below.

During ITV's half-time segment, presenter Mark Pougatch gave a small insight into what had happened. "You might have noticed as the players were coming off, Fabian Ruiz and Ben White were having a frank exchange of views."

Ian Wright, on the punditry panel alongside Roy Keane, appeared delighted to see the passion in the game. The former England striker replied: "Nice one, Ben White. Yeah, good."

Footage from the stands showed the likes of Dan Burn and David Raya stepping in to keep the two men apart very quickly to prevent anything escalating. Marc Cucurella was among those looking to get involved.

One thing that didn't appear to get picked up originally was a brief squabble between midfielders Bellingham and Martin Zubimendi. The latter, who started the game for Spain, appeared to push a member of England's coaching staff.

Bellingham tried his best to get to Zubimendi, but there were too many bodies in the way. The 23-year-old still appeared to shout something in the Arsenal star's direction. Watch from another angle below.

Many people took to social media to point out the tension between Bellingham and Zubimendi, which ITV's commentary and punditry teams appeared to miss.
