Forget Lankshear: Man Utd Must Sign Nicolo Tresoldi Instead

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Mauricio Pochettino is already devising transfer plans at Manchester United ahead of the January window, with a move for a striker understood to be on the cards.

The Red Devils are in a perilous position in the Premier League — they sit in the relegation zone after ten matches, a return which prompted a change in the dugout. Michael Carrick has made way for Pochettino, whose tenure officially begins after the international break when United visit Anfield.

Clearly, a managerial change alone will not solve all the north-west club's issues. A significant squad overhaul will need to be enacted over the next year or so, starting this January.

Alongside a left-back, United are understood to be scouring the market for potential centre-forward signings. Benjamin Sesko hasn't played a minute of football since mid-November as he recovers from an injury that will keep him out for another month or so. Meanwhile, Joshua Zirkzee continues to flatter to deceive when given an opportunity.

Using Bryan Mbeumo as a makeshift number nine is unlikely to persist in Pochettino's era, and thus, it's clear that this should be a priority area for the Red Devils heading into the winter window.

Will Lankshear has emerged as an option for United. The Middlesbrough striker has enjoyed a spectacular start to the new season, scoring 13 goals in his first 13 Championship appearances this term.

However, the extensive list of potential suitors for the Englishman, combined with Boro's likely reluctance to sanction the departure of their talisman midway through a promotion-chasing season, means the price could be hefty.

Instead, United should look elsewhere at a potential bargain on the market.

There are a select number of strikers scoring bucket loads of goals in the top divisions in Europe, most of whom are unattainable for United at the moment. The Red Devils will need to look beneath the surface for a centre-forward, scouting some of the less-talked-about markets, and their supposed interest in Lankshear suggests that they are prepared to do this.

However, for the aforementioned reasons regarding the former Tottenham star's prospective price tag, they should steer clear and instead focus on Club Brugge's Nicolo Tresoldi.

Unlike Lankshear, Tresoldi has showcased his ability to score goals over a full season, rather than just a few months. He scored 23 goals across all competitions for Brugge last season, and has started this term with 14 in 17 matches. His league record stands at 17 goals in his previous 23 top-flight appearances for Brugge.

He has also performed in European competition, which cannot be said of Lankshear. Adding to his four Champions League strikes last season, Tresoldi already has five this time around, including a hat-trick at the San Siro against Inter Milan.

Boasting smart off-the-ball movement, neat combination play and efficient finishing, the German has all the attributes needed to thrive in the Premier League. To add to this, he's only a year older than Lankshear, so his signing would have the same upside as the Boro striker but with less risk given there is a larger sample size of him excelling, and that is before price is even considered.

According to Goal, Brugge are reportedly only looking for around €60 million (£50 million) for Tresoldi — a very reasonable fee for a player of his pedigree and age.

As shown by the data above, Tresoldi is putting up outstanding numbers that point to him being a seasoned poacher. He gets into dangerous goal-scoring positions all the time, is a constant presence in the box, and he almost always manages to get his shots off.

He certainly wouldn't arrive at Old Trafford as the finished product, and would need time to acclimatise to the demands of the Premier League.

However, there is enough in his numbers and in the scouting to make £50 million a smart investment, particularly with Sesko available in United's squad as a fallback option.

The Red Devils desperately need a striker - and there are some big names they can sign for nothing right now.
