Which interactions between the players went unnoticed by the cameras? And what exactly took place in the crucial moments before Sebastiano Esposito stepped up for his penalty in the second half?

Join us for exclusive insight, behind-the-scenes details, and a comprehensive look at the stories emerging from Italy’s latest triumph.
