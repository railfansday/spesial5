11 Non-Traditional Thanksgiving Turkey Recipes That Everyone Will Love (Even The Haters)

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Whether you love it or hate it, we can all admit that a classic Thanksgiving turkey is divisive. An overcooked, dry turkey can be a serious bummer, so this year, try a little something different. These 11 non-traditional Thanksgiving turkey recipes bring all the benefits of a juicy, well-seasoned turkey, with more flavor and flair. They’re so good that even the biggest skeptics will go back for seconds.

Looking for sides to serve alongside your newest turkey tradition? We’ve got plenty of incredible Thanksgiving side dishes that everyone will love. Plus, we’ve got plant-based alternatives to all your Thanksgiving favorites too, with our best vegetarian Thanksgiving recipes.

Friendsgiving nachos are the ultimate way to enjoy Thanksgiving (with minimal effort). It’s all the elements of Thanksgiving—turkey, mashed potatoes, stuffing, gravy, and cranberry sauce—PLUS cheese served on top of a bed of crunchy nacho chips.

When done wrong, roast turkey can lean on the dry and bland side—this garlic-herb turkey roulade is the solution. The garlic-herb mixture not only adds heaps of flavor into our turkey breast, but when cut into, it creates a stunning swirl worthy of your Thanksgiving table.

This is the turkey recipe you make for folks who think they don’t like turkey. A savory mushroom and bacon filling keeps this roasted turkey breast juicy while making it fancy and hearty enough to take the place of a whole bird on the holiday table.

Turkey breast can get a bad rap for being bland and overcooked. No longer, thanks to this easy tandoori turkey breast recipe, which relies on a few key steps to lock in flavor and moisture.

This sheet-pan dinner is as cozy as it is convenient, bringing together the classics with a few flavorful twists. The beauty of this meal lies in its simplicity—everything roasts together on a single pan, making cleanup a breeze.

Sure, looking over a sprawling table of Thanksgiving turkey, sides, rolls, salads, and more can be extremely impressive. But you know what’s even more spectacular? These Thanksgiving tacos.

Simple to make and adaptable to what you have on hand, this baked pasta dinner is not only the perfect holiday meal but a great staple weeknight dinner to make all year long.

Looking for a comforting dish that’s royally good? It’s high time you try Turkey À La King—it tastes just like a rich, savory chicken pot pie filling, all without having to mess around with a fussy pie crust. It’s the ultimate one-skillet meal to pull together as a low-key holiday main.

This meatloaf is moist and LOADED with flavor, thanks to Worcestershire sauce, fresh herbs, and a simple two-ingredient glaze on top. Whether you’re looking for a lighter alternative or just want to mix things up, this recipe should have a place on your Thanksgiving table.

Alright, it may not actually be able to take the place of turkey on your table, but this Rice Krispies turkey is too cute not to include. It’s the perfect way to wrap up dinner and make all your guests smile.
