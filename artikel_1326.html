Official: Sassuolo have fired Aquilani after five straight defeats

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Sassuolo have officially announced the dismissal of coach Alberto Aquilani after five consecutive Serie A defeats, with Roberto D’Aversa the favourite to take over ahead of Vincenzo Montella.

The decision had been in the air following the 3-0 loss to his old club Roma, a result that took his total record on the bench this season to four wins, two draws and seven defeats between Serie A and the Coppa Italia.

This was his first top flight experience as a coach, following roles in Serie B with Pisa and Catanzaro, while before that he was a coach at the Fiorentina youth academy.

Called in to replace Fabio Grosso over the summer, Aquilani had signed a contract to June 2028 and started quite well, the highlight being the 3-2 win over Juventus on September 13.

That was also the last moment of joy, followed by a 1-1 draw against Milan and six defeats.

It is widely reported that Roberto D’Aversa is the favourite to take over, but so far Sassuolo have only announced that Aquilani has been removed from his post.

Another potential candidate is former Turkiye international coach Montella.
