Danny Dyer Would Let Jarrod Bowen Leave West Ham For Two Clubs

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Jarrod Bowen has been nothing short of incredible since joining West Ham United from Hull City in January 2020. The Englishman has been an extremely loyal servant at the club, even after the Hammers were relegated from the Premier League and into the Championship.

Since his £22 million transfer six years ago, Bowen has netted 94 goals in over 297 appearances and has become the talisman at the London Stadium. His place in history is already sealed, after he netted a dramatic late winner in the UEFA Europa Conference League final in 2023.

That delicate chipped effort secured the east London club's first piece of major silverware since the 1980 FA Cup. Bowen hasn't just racked up strong achievements on the pitch, but he's gained family ties to West Ham off it, too.

It certainly makes leaving a lot more difficult. But given the continued struggles the Iron find themselves in, he may be tempted to go against his father-in-law's ideals in search for a career more attuned to his quality.

Bowen married into one of the most well-known east London families he possibly could've when he tied the knot with reality TV star Dani Dyer in 2025. Of course, that now means Bowen calls notorious actor Danny Dyer his father-in-law.

Dyer is a massive West Ham fan and has often spoken about his delight at the Hammers' captain now being part of his family. Adding to that, the entire Dyer clan live in east London, and Bowen would have to think hard about relocating.

There was one big player who stood out as the hardest for Danny Dyer...

Speaking to talkSPORT in 2025, The Football Factory star was quizzed over whether he'd accept the West Ham captain jumping ship to any other club. At first, he shut down the idea of an Arsenal move, saying: "He’s never going to do that. Why would he? What’s in it for him?"

The prospect of another cross-London switch was quickly denied, too. This time, Dyer ruled out Chelsea as a possible option: "Why would he go there?"

However, two teams did come up in the conversation that Dyer claimed he could 'suffer' seeing his son-in-law join. First was European giants, and possibly the biggest club in the world, Real Madrid: "Real Madrid? Yeah, for sure."

Interestingly, it appears only one Premier League club would leave Dyer 'over the moon' for Bowen. Manchester City were given as the other acceptable destination. Dyer said:

"Listen, it’s his career. City? I wouldn’t mind that, I’d suffer that.

"I’m not going to lie, it would hurt, but he’s a grown man and he makes his own decisions. It would obviously hurt, but of course I would be over the moon for him."

With West Ham suffering relegation under Nuno Espirito Santo in 2026, Bowen and his teammates had to face the reality that they would be lining up in the second-tier of English football. However, the winger has stayed loyal to the Irons so far, despite the demotion, and the subsequent poor form to start the new campaign.

However, there will continue to be no shortage of clubs interested in the forward, who can play across the frontline. Reports over the summer suggested London rivals Chelsea were among the front-runners to secure his signature.

The Blues would likely be another team on Dyer's no-list. But with Bowen having racked up 22 England caps, he will surely have a pick of the club he desires should he decide to part ways with the east London side.
