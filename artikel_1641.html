Courtois: ‘Leader’ Bellingham key to future success at Real Madrid

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Thibaut Courtois described Jude Bellingham as a "leader" on the pitch for Real Madrid, saying Los Blancos' chances of silverware this season may rest with the midfielder.

Courtois' comments came ahead of Madrid's Champions League clash with PSV on Tuesday, with Jose Mourinho's side winning all four of their games in Europe so far.

Madrid are on a nine-game winning run across all competitions, the latest of which came in dramatic fashion against Celta Vigo at the weekend, with Carlos Espi coming off the bench to save his team once again with an 85th-minute winner.

Bellingham was also on target in what was his 100th LaLiga appearance. He has now scored three goals in his last four games, having failed to net in his previous seven.

Since joining from Borussia Dortmund in 2023, Bellingham has played 182 times for Madrid in all competitions, scoring 60 goals as well as providing 41 assists.

He has six goals and three assists so far in 2026-27, and Courtois believes Bellingham is one of Madrid's most important players despite only being 23 years of age.

"Jude is a young guy but when you see him, it feels like he's a few years older," Courtois told reporters when asked about Bellingham's most impressive qualities.

"In the beginning, when you compare him with Arda [Guler], Arda was a quieter kid. With Jude, you already feel like he had a presence, but there isn't much difference in age.

"Jude is someone who really wants to win. That's why on the field he swears a lot! He's really a winner, and when he's not happy, he will make it known.

"He's also a leader on the field. He's a great player, a great mentality. You need those kinds of people like that in your team that go for everything.

"It seems like he has an engine to play 200 minutes. He keeps on going, running with the ball, it's really hard to dispossess him.

"And on the outside, he just works hard, he goes to the gym. With a good Jude, we play better."

It was confirmed earlier this week that Courtois had agreed to fresh terms in the Spanish capital, signing a new deal that will keep him with Madrid until the summer of 2028.

"I'm very proud and happy," Courtois said. "My objective is to end my career here. I want to keep playing and stay healthy. I'm grateful for the club's confidence in me."

And Courtois will be hoping to play his part against PSV in the Champions League, with this the first meeting between the two sides in 37 years.

Under Mourinho, Real have won 17 of their 20 Champions League matches at the Estadio Bernabeu (D2 L1), with their only defeat coming against Barcelona in the 2010-11 semi-final first leg. It's the best home win percentage (85%) for a manager at a club (min. 20 games) in Champions League history.

"We've had to find how the boss wanted to play, to understand each other better," Courtois said of Mourinho's start to life back in Madrid.

"Our two defeats, against [Real] Betis we didn't deserve to lose, against Atletico [Madrid] the circumstances that led to that result.

"I knew [Mourinho] when I was 22, that was a long time ago. He has a lot more experience. Over the years you change a bit.

"He's calm, and when he has to speak clearly he speaks clearly. He's a great boss. As a team, we want to keep winning and lift trophies at the end of the season."
