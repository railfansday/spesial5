Gibbs-White ‘trusting the process’ under Glasner at Forest

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Morgan Gibbs-White has called for calm around Nottingham Forest as they are "trusting the process" under Oliver Glasner.

The Tricky Trees secured another crucial home Premier League win this season on Friday after defeating Chelsea 3-2 at the City Ground.

Igor Jesus gave Forest an early lead before Gibbs-White and Emmanuel Emegha scored braces at either end in the second half.

Gibbs-White has scored 15 Premier League goals in 2026; only Erling Haaland (17) has scored more.

Against Chelsea, he scored more than once in a match in the competition for just the second time in his 202 appearances, after his hat-trick against Burnley in April last season.

Forest have won back-to-back home games in the Premier League for the first time since April 2025.

Before their wins over Manchester City and now Chelsea, the Tricky Trees had won just one of their previous 15 league matches at the City Ground (D8 L6).

Gibbs-White believes Glasner deserves time to prove himself in the Forest dugout after a slow start to the season.

"I think there's going to be drama no matter what – it's the Premier League, there's always going to be drama," the Forest captain told Sky Sports.

"But I feel we need to take a bit of realisation that we have a new manager, we're sticking with him, we're trusting the process, believing in him and trusting what he wants.

"Not every week is it going to be pretty or is it going to be nice, but when times like this happen, we've got to make the most of it and enjoy it.

"We showed that grit, we showed that determination, and we got the job done and I think deservedly as well.

"Delighted to go up in the first half and we said we knew the second half would be difficult and they'd be fighting but we stuck in there and got the job done."
