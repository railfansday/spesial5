TIDAL will delete 29 million AI-generated songs from its catalogue

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The streaming service says labels and cut royalties were not enough, as AI tracks made up 43% of recent uploads.

TIDAL will begin deleting all wholly AI-generated tracks on December 9, about 29 million songs, and expects to reject 80,000 to 85,000 more a day. It had already labelled and demonetised AI music in July, but says 43% of tracks it received in the past 90 days were AI-made.

TIDAL will start deleting every wholly AI-generated track from its catalogue on December 9, removing about 29 million songs. Robert Andersen, who leads TIDAL at parent company Block, announced the plan on X on Tuesday. TIDAL’s own account confirmed the date.

Andersen said the songs should be gone by the start of 2027. TIDAL also expects to turn away 80,000 to 85,000 AI-made tracks a day. He called it the first streaming service to take this step and urged rivals to follow.

In July, TIDAL began labelling AI tracks and cutting off their royalties. Andersen said that had not been enough.

“43% of tracks distributed to us in the last 90 days have been detected as AI-generated. Something more drastic needs to be done,” Andersen wrote.

Most of that music finds no audience. Andersen said 96% of AI-generated songs on TIDAL get zero plays.

Rivals face the same flood. More than half the music uploaded to Deezer in June was made by AI.

Others have stopped short of deleting it. Spotify has pulled 50M spam tracks from its recommendations, but left them on the service. Apple Music has made AI tags mandatory for record companies.

The cut covers only songs made wholly by AI, which TIDAL finds with detection tools. Its terms of service warn those tools can wrongly tag tracks, and users can ask support to fix a label.
