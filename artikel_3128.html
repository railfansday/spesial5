Ambarella: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Customers are delaying orders as memory shortages persist, but Ambarella's new 2nm, 4nm, and 5nm chips are set to carry second-half fiscal 2028 growth.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 25, 2026 at 11:16PM EST

On its third-quarter fiscal year 2027 earnings call, held on Nov. 24, 2026, Ambarella (AMBA -19.77%) announced quarterly revenue of $115.2 million. Industrywide shortages of dynamic random-access memory (DRAM), a type of computer memory chip, are forcing customers to delay orders and rethink product timing. Three things management said stand out.

Memory price spikes hit consumer markets hard, driving subseasonal guidance for the fourth quarter. Management pruned customer forecasts to exclude unconfirmable shipments while customers rationed available memory for high-priority products.

Management projects full-year revenue growth in fiscal 2028, propelled by new 5-nanometer, 4-nanometer, and 2-nanometer system-on-chips (SoCs) -- integrated microchips containing primary system components. These devices command a higher average selling price (ASP), or baseline unit price, than legacy products.

Ambarella added global design house Sigma Connectivity to its partner network to support complex artificial intelligence (AI) chip integrations. The company is leveraging partners to reach small and mid-sized enterprise accounts in emerging robotics and infrastructure markets.

Industrywide memory headwinds are biting, but management isn't wavering on its long-term strategy.

Ambarella holds a $22.9 billion serviceable addressable market (SAM), or total potential market revenue, for fiscal 2032. Management is scrutinizing discretionary expenses while expanding its open software platform.
