The New ‘Hunger Games’ Popcorn Buckets Are Here—And Fans Are Divided Over The Designs

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The 50th Hunger Games are almost here, and apparently, the competition has already begun at the concession stand.

With The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping arriving in theaters November 20, AMC, Regal, and Cinemark have unveiled their movie-themed collectibles, including popcorn buckets, drink cups, blankets, and more. And while each theater chain has its own designs, fans are already making it pretty clear which collection they’re most excited about.

Regal is leaning into the film’s floral aesthetic with a collection featuring a popcorn bucket, tumbler, fleece blanket, and an exclusive book-shaped popcorn tin.

The pink tin resembles a hardcover copy of Sunrise on the Reaping, complete with floral artwork and the film’s snake and mockingjay imagery. It also comes with a large popcorn, although I have a feeling most fans are buying it for the tin itself.

And judging by the Instagram comments, Regal might have already won.

“You guys hit it out of the park with the book tin omg,” one fan wrote.

“OMG THE PINK TIN AND THE BLANKET,” another exclaimed, while someone else added, “I VOLUNTEER FOR ALL THIS.”

The collection will be available at select Regal locations while supplies last.

AMC is taking a more elaborate approach with its Flint Striker Tin, which features a gold-and-purple snake and mockingjay design that wraps around the bucket. The chain is also offering a plush throw blanket featuring the film’s artwork.

But unlike Regal’s collection, AMC’s popcorn bucket isn’t exactly receiving universal praise.

“Regal was miles better than this popcorn bucket,” one commenter wrote.

Another was even more direct: “Regal clears with theirs, this is ugly lol.”

Cinemark is also releasing a collection featuring a popcorn bucket with a Flint Striker keychain, a separate collectible popcorn bucket, a matching tumbler, and a blanket.

The chain’s promotional materials advertise free same-day refills with its popcorn buckets and tumbler, with merchandise available at select locations while supplies last.

Cinemark is also offering a $40 Super Ticket package that includes admission to a Cinemark XD screening, a 130-ounce collectible popcorn tin filled with popcorn, a medium drink, and a Flint Striker pin.

Between all three chains, there’s certainly no shortage of ways to spend money at the movies this November.

But if the early fan reactions are any indication, Regal’s book-shaped tin might be the one to beat.
