Louis Vuitton Takes Its Place at the Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix Finish Line

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

A handcrafted trunk awaited the winner, Max Verstappen.

When Max Verstappen crossed the finish line at the Las Vegas Formula 1 Grand Prix on Saturday night, after a gripping race that saw the championship driver battle to maintain the tightest of leads against rivals George Russell and Kimi Antonelli, his trophy came in very special packaging: a bespoke Louis Vuitton trunk. For the past two seasons, Trophy Trunks have been presented to the winner of the annual Las Vegas event, which sees drivers race on a thrilling course that winds along the famous Strip. They have also been presented to the winners of the World Cup, America’s Cup, and the NBA Finals. Louis Vuitton had a strong presence across the whole race weekend, hosting a suite where guests including Teyana Taylor watched the events from above the track and were able to get an early look at the trunk.

The Las Vegas trunk was, like all Louis Vuitton custom pieces, made at Asnières-sur-Seine, a commune in northern France. It was handcrafted by 25 artisans, made to the specific dimensions of the trophy. The Trophy Trunks are a remarkable mingling of the old and the new. They use the traditional craftsmanship techniques that have defined the house for over 170 years. All construction of the trunks’ wood, leather, and locks was done with the same techniques LV has used since the beginning, meaning that in many ways the Trophy Trunks differ little from its trunks from a century ago. The modern energy of motor racing comes through via subtle touches like the two stripes across the trunk that make a large V, a nod to both Vuitton and victory—they’re black-and-white checkered as a nod to the Formula 1 finish line. A small badge that calls back to the early days of trunk travel and bears the winning driver’s initials was added this year.

Louis Vuitton’s relationship with Formula 1 began with the Grand Prix de Monaco, which the brand began creating Trophy Trunks for in 2021. In 2025, it became an official partner of the league, and this year, LV created trunks for all 24 of the season’s races. In early December, the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix will mark the final 2026 race.
