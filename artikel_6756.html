Shop the Mules Celebrities Can’t Get Enough Of

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Mules are making a comeback as the surprise hit of fall footwear, with celebrities wearing the minimalist silhouette on repeat in their everyday wardrobes. If summer was all about the wedge mule, this season flatter styles are taking center stage, with fans like Kendall Jenner and Hailey Bieber leading the charge. Meanwhile, ‘90s-inspired kitten-heel mules are also gaining traction among those who prefer a little extra height—a nod, perhaps, to the enduring Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy effect, further fueled by the TV series Love Story.

One brand celebrities continue to gravitate toward is The Row, whose refined flat designs are well suited for day-to-night wear. Bieber is a fan of the pared-back Penelope patent slippers, while Jenner favors the June mules, another take that fits right into her minimalist -leaning aesthetic. Elsewhere, Jennifer Lawrence makes a case for the chunkier Hudson slip-ons in brown suede, a comfortable option with a little more weight for walking.

Of course, there are plenty of alternatives beyond The Row. Lawrence has also been seen in a leopard-print pair by Ancient Greek Sandals on multiple occasions, adding a welcome dose of pattern to her modern boho looks. Julianne Moore, meanwhile, gave the trend a little lift in a pair of kitten heels by Bottega Veneta—a chic alternative to the season’s flatter styles. Whether you prefer a pared-back leather slipper, a statement-making print, or a touch of height, there’s a mule for just about every wardrobe.

Ahead, shop the season’s best mules, with styling inspiration straight from the wardrobes of your favorite celebrities.

Hailey Bieber is loyal to The Row’s Penelope mules, styling the carefree slippers with everything from lady jackets and jeans to a cobalt trench coat, black baby tee, and cropped kick-flare trousers, as she recently demonstrated on a night out in Los Angeles. Add a burgundy shoulder bag, and you get an easy day-to-night formula for transitional weather and beyond.

Easy, comfortable footwear is a staple for Jennifer Lawrence. As a New Yorker, she racks up plenty of miles on foot and bike, making The Row’s brown suede Hudson slides a practical choice, designed with a chunkier sole that can handle the steps. Style them as she did with an oversized navy crewneck sweater, a black T-shirt, and light-wash straight-leg jeans for effortless off-duty flair, then finish with a bold handbag for an unexpected pop of color.

Kendall Jenner is another die-hard The Row fan, her mule of choice being the June style. Keep your own wardrobe in a minimalist vein by borrowing her understated formula: a black bateau-neck top and navy slip skirt. (Yes, the two shades work together!) The combination feels instantly elevated with sleek mules, The Row’s camel Terrasse shoulder bag, silver hoop earrings, and Jenner’s favorite Chimi sunglasses.

Lawrence also has a soft spot for statement shoes, and this leopard-print pair from Ancient Greek Sandals brings a playful dose of pattern to her fall wardrobe. For a recent night out, she styled them with a sheer open-knit duster, white T-shirt, and floral Dries Van Noten skirt for an unexpected pattern clash that plays up her bohemian side.

Julianne Moore went for height in Bottega Veneta’s kitten-heel mules while promoting her new film, The Debut. The ’90s-inspired silhouette felt right at home alongside an inky-hued cardigan, white long-sleeve T-shirt, and the house’s statement skirt for a fashion-forward finish. A suede clutch and silver jewelry complete the look, adding just the right amount of polish.
