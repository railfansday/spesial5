3 players who Wrexham should offload in January, including Ryan Longman

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Wrexham boss Phil Parkinson will be keen to add to his ranks, but inconsistent results have been a driving factor behind the Red Dragons’ lowly league position of 15th place.

The North Welsg have several players they could offload in January to free up funds to strengthen the squad, as they aim to close the six-point gap to QPR in the final play-off spot. A hefty outlay in the summer means Wrexham will need to be shrewd in the transfer market, with new arrivals likely requiring some departures to balance the squad.

Here, we take a look at three players Wrexham should look to offload in the winter to freshen up the squad and make way for new arrivals…

Yet to feature in a matchday squad this season, the former Plymouth Argyle man may be enticed by the prospect of regular first-team football.

Ryan Longman has only had one Championship start this season and may seek a new challenge in January.  He asserted himself as a regular at right wing-back, making 21 League One appearances in the second half of the 2024/25 campaign as Wrexham secured promotion.

The 26-year-old was one of their most consistent performers when called upon last season too, deputising on both flanks, with his relentless work rate hailed by the Racecourse Ground faithful.

However, following the permanent acquisition of former loan favourite Issa Kabore from Manchester City in the summer and Danny Imray’s arrival, former Hull City ace Longman has been limited to substitute appearances. He is certainly capable of playing a more prominent role at another club.

Last but not least is Sebastian Revan, who has endured a torrid spell on the sidelines with injury. The defender could benefit from an EFL loan move upon his expected January comeback.

The Aston Villa academy graduate suffered a serious hamstring injury in February during his loan spell at Burton Albion, having made a positive impression under Gary Bowyer after playing a bit-part role in Wrexham’s promotion to the Championship.

His time at the Pirelli Stadium was cut short as he began a long-term rehabilitation programme with his parent club’s medical staff and is nearing a return to action.

Standing in the way of a starting spot is Jacques Ekomie, signed from Angers in the summer and in fine form. It means a loan or permanent exit for Revan looks increasingly likely.
