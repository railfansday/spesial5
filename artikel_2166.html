QPR’s Top 3 players of the 2026/27 season so far, including resurgent 6-goal/assist star

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

QPR sit 8th in the Championship table with the latest international break upon us. The Hoops have had some patchy form in recent weeks, but they enter the break off the back of a 2-1 win over Lincoln City.

Julien Stephan and co will be pleased with their efforts to date, but there’s room for improvement. The time without league football offers the R’s a chance to put some work in on the training ground, while we can assess the season so far and pick out some standout stars.

In no particular order, we put forward QPR’s top three players of the 2026/27 season so far…

Ronnie Edwards’ fine loan at QPR in the 2024/25 season meant they knew what to expect when he returned permanently earlier this year. He reclaimed a key role swiftly, and the 23-year-old has gone from strength to strength this term.

Used in defensive midfield and centre-back by Stephan, Edwards is one of the Hoops’ most influential players. As per FotMob‘s data, he stands out in possession as he ranks among the Championship’s best centre-backs for successful passes (767), long ball completion rate (62.1%), interceptions (20) and recoveries (65).

His best displays include the big home wins over Swansea City and Derby County, and the 0-0 draw at Charlton Athletic. Edwards has been consistent all season though, proving his star quality at this level.

Throughout his second stint at QPR, Paul Smyth has endured some tough spells. After three years, some may have expected the Northern Irishman to be phased out of the first XI by the likes of Harvey Vale, Karamoko Dembele, Kwame Poku or Koki Saito, but that has not been the case.

Smyth has retained a regular role in Stephan’s side, and it is not hard to see why. The resurgent 29-year-old shows the commitment and endeavour Stephan desires from his attackers, and he’s added more end product to his game with three goals and three assists in his 14 league appearances this season.

He is tied with Richard Kone at the top of the goal contribution charts at Loftus Road and ranks in the top three of Hoops players for big chances created (3), xG on target (2.0) and expected assists (1.6). Smyth has also won three penalties – more than any other player in the Championship (FotMob).

Last but not least is Pierce Charles, whose influence from goalkeeper cannot be overrstated. The Northern Irish prospect arrived as a highly-regarded talent on loan from Manchester City after his Sheffield Wednesday breakthrough, and he has not disappointed.

Charles has work to do with his shot-stopping, as shown by an xG prevented of -2.47 and a save percentage of 67.9%. However, he makes this list because of how his abilities with the ball have changed the way QPR play.

His distribution ability makes Stephan’s side so much more comfortable at the back and they have more routes to the attack thanks to his range of passing. No Championship goalkeeper has made more than his 564 successful passes (FotMob) and he has fantastic numbers in his pass completion rate (77.7%) and long pass accuracy (46.6%).

It has been a real team effort from QPR to start the season in strong fashion. There were some impressive performers overlooked for inclusion here.

Captain Jimmy Dunne has been key with his physical presence and leadership from the back. Midfielder Glen Kamara has made a positive impact since his arrival as well, and if he continues in his recent form, he will be firmly in the player of the season conversation at Loftus Road.

Ilias Chair and Boy Kemper deserve early mentions too, but injuries have limited their involvement compared to others. Kealey Adamson has been one of the most improved players when stepping in at full-back for the R’s.
