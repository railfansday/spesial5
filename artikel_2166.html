Crawley Town set to complete free transfer swoop for 31-year-old

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Crawley Town are a side in trouble this season. They sit bottom of League Two and are still without a permanent manager after appointing Kevin Blackwell on an interim basis nearly a month ago.

The Red Dragons have not played a League Two game in November but have lost back-to-back cup matches, including an FA Cup upset away at Wimborne Town. Former Sunderland goalkeeper Vito Mannone was in goal for that fixture as they lost 2-0.

Now though, Ben Jacobs states Crawley Town are set to take action by signing free agent shot-stopper Dejan Iliev. The 31-year-old was last on the books of Romanian club Rapid Bucharest.

The EFL’s bottom side have three games in hand on Rochdale in 22nd. However, they sit six points adrift of safety and uncertainty remains over their managerial position with Colin Kazim-Richards yet to be permanently replaced.

Alongside the reported arrival of Iliev, Crawley Town have recently made two other free agent swoops. Experienced striker Connor Wickham has joined the side, as well as English midfielder Ayman Benarous.

The Red Devils have scored 13 goals so far this season, with only Exeter City and Northampton Town netting less frequently. Midfielder Dylan Morgan has been a standout performer netting five goals, but current No. 9 Danilo Orsi has only netted twice in the league.

Wickham joins in the hope he can provide a focal point up front for his side. The 33-year-old is known for his physical and aerial ability and brings experience to his new club.

Benarous is more youthful at 23, but he offers a decent amount of experience from his spells at Bristol City and Plymouth Argyle. He is a reliable player in possession and can cover on the left-hand side alongside his usual central midfield role.

Crawley Town currently have three goalkeepers, all of whom have featured more than once in the league this season. Iliev would be a fourth option, and potentially the strongest.

Nicolas Michalski is currently on loan from Blackburn Rovers, and Jackson Izquierdo signed from Crystal Palace in the summer. Izquierdo has played the least of the three goalkeepers, so perhaps a loan away awaits as he bids to develop.

Mannone has featured six times in League Two for Crawley Town this season. However, like the other goalkeepers, he has failed to make the position his own.

Dejan Iliev has only played three senior matches in English football, with Shrewsbury Town. However, it is hoped that his top-flight experience in European football will help him adapt to League Two well and become the starting shot-stopper at Broadfield Stadium.
