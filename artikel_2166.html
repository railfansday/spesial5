AC Milan among European sides keen on 17-year-old QPR breakout star

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

QPR have made a fine start to this season and currently occupy the final Championship play-off spot. The likes of Pierce Charles and Ronnie Edwards have earned the plaudits, while Richard Kone is finding his form again in attack, but a real breakout star has been 17-year-old Leon Scarlett.

Scarlett joined the club from Arsenal in April 2025, and so far this season he has made six appearances and clubs are starting to notice. Anthony Joseph has said that European giant AC Milan are tracking his progress after his start to the campaign.

The Italian side are not the only side keen, with Eintracht Frankfurt also keeping tabs on Scarlett’s situation. Both sides have expressed interest in the winger, who is already a Wales under-21 international.

QPR youngster Scarlett is attracting growing attention after breaking into Julien Stephan’s first-team plans this season, with AC Milan and Eintracht Frankfurt reportedly monitoring his progress.

The 17-year-old Welsh winger joined QPR in January 2025 after spending around a decade in Arsenal’s academy, having been released by the Gunners. Primarily operating on the left, Scarlett is known for his explosive acceleration, close control and ability to beat defenders in one-on-one situations.

He made his senior debut aged 16 in May 2026 and has featured regularly from the bench, but he did start against Preston back in September, accumulating around 130 minutes. He has also represented Wales at multiple youth levels, including the U21s.

Manager Stephan has praised his personality and willingness to express himself in first-team training, with QPR viewing him as an exciting prospect. He is playing ahead of his age right now and QPR fans have every right to be excited about Scarlett’s potential.

QPR must resist interest from AC Milan and Eintracht Frankfurt in Scarlett, with the 17-year-old better off continuing his development at Loftus Road.

Scarlett has enjoyed a rapid rise since leaving Arsenal’s academy, earning first-team opportunities under Stephan. However, with only around 130 Championship minutes to his name and no senior goal or assist yet, a move abroad would arguably come too soon.

Stephan has a proven record of developing young talent, while QPR are placing increasing emphasis on their academy pathway. Scarlett’s explosive pace, close control and ability to beat defenders make him an exciting prospect, but he needs regular exposure to senior football to refine his game.

A move to Milan or Frankfurt could leave him fighting for opportunities in youth sides rather than progressing at first-team level. QPR should keep hold of Scarlett, gradually increase his involvement and allow his performances and value to grow before considering a big-money departure.
