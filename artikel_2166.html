Birmingham City told to consider 'no-brainer' transfer reunion with £15m man

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Birmingham City have been told to consider a move for former St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park favourite, Che Adams, ahead of the looming January transfer window.

Despite a lesser amount of activity in the summer market, Blues are firmly among the early-season candidates for promotion, with Chris Davies' tactical reshuffles in recent months certainly paying dividends.

As well as having a squad which has now adapted to the roughness of the Championship after coasting to the League One title two seasons ago, such changes have clearly got the best out of Davies' attackers.

August Priske has become one of the most feared strikers in the division based on his early-season form after signing in January, whilst Jay Stansfield looks far more assured in an attacking-midfield role at present.

There are question marks over what the future holds for Marvin Ducksch, though, after extended exit talk in the summer, whilst Luis Vazquez's start in B9 hasn't been overly convincing.

Adams, 30, is currently in his third season with Torino in Serie A, having left Southampton in 2024 following their return to the Premier League.

It has been seven years since the forward departed Birmingham for Saints in a £15m deal, though, but he hasn't lost sight of where the goal is, posting over 20 strikes for his current employers in just over 80 outings.

Given the experience the striker has got under his belt since leaving St. Andrew's, Football League World's Birmingham City fan pundit, Jason Moore, has urged the deal to be explored.

He told FLW: "I can probably sit and say, is he not going to play enough? Is he going to be happy with that?

"But, if we convinced him, and you could make the move happen, you'd be silly not to bring him in.

"He's a proven Premier League striker, granted he doesn't score tons. He's played in a very decent league in Italy. It'd be an absolute no-brainer to get Che Adams in," Moore stated.

"You need two decent players in every position and Jay is predominantly playing as a 10, so if you have Priske and someone like Che Adams as your strikers to get you up, it puts you in pretty good stead going forward, doesn't it?

"I'd be 100% up for bringing Che Adams back and any Blues fan that wouldn't, I just think it would be silly. If you get to January, and you're still in the position of 'it looks like we might come up', every single team in the past few years have added that bit of power just to make sure they get up.

"Ipswich got Mehmeti in January just to make sure they went up. I think about when they brought Kieffer Moore in at the end of the season when they first went up, Benik Afobe at Wolves was another one. Clubs always do it, just add a little bit more firepower to get up," he explained.

"If we can go and get Che Adams in January to make sure we go up, I'm all for it."

The thought of bringing Adams back to the West Midlands is one which would certainly excite many Birmingham fans, especially after his recent sighting with former strike partner, Lukas Jutkiewicz.

However, speculation between the club and the 50-time Scotland international under Knighthead's ownership is nothing new at this point.

Indeed, it was claimed in January that Birmingham staff were not against the idea of a sentimental transfer, given the impact which Demarai Gray has had since returning to his home city on a free transfer.

As Moore mentions, sourcing a move for a forward who has scored 49 Championship goals in 156 appearances and won promotion would be a smart move for Blues if an agreement could be reached.

That said, Adams is featuring regularly for Torino this campaign, meaning a stumbling block may arise regardless of his feelings towards the Midlands club.

However, if a deal was reached, it could have some of their divisional rivals scratching their heads, as Wolves were linked with his services in the summer, and are currently lacking in attacking depth following an injury to Raul Jimenez two months ago.
