Martin Zubimendi stance on Chelsea transfer as Arsenal set asking price

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Martin Zubimendi is seriously considering leaving Arsenal for the first time and has refused to rule out a move to Chelsea, according to reports in Spain.

Zubimendi is yet to start a Premier League game for Arsenal this season, with the concerning lack of game-time sparking speculation over his future at the Emirates Stadium.

The Spanish midfielder enjoyed a bright start to his Gunners career following a £60m move from Real Sociedad, earning rave reviews from Mikel Arteta this time last year.

But Zubimendi had lost his place in the Arsenal team by the end of the season, with a teenage Myles Lewis-Skelly instead starting the Champions League final against PSG.

The 27-year-old has continued to struggle for minutes this season and is now, for the first time, considering his future at the club.

Spanish outlet AS say Zubimendi has his eye on a January exit and is ‘open to everything’ following reports linking him with Chelsea, Real Madrid and Atletico Madrid.

Xabi Alonso is keen to strengthen his midfield options and knows Zubimendi well from his previous spell at Real Sociedad.

Zubimendi is contracted to Arsenal until 2030 but reports in Spain say the north London club will consider offers for the World Cup winner of at least £60m.

Last week, Zubimendi was forced to address speculation linking him with a move away from Arsenal while on international duty with Spain.

‘To be honest, I’m sometimes surprised by the press too,’ he told Marca. ‘I understand that the winter transfer window is approaching and rumours can surface, but I think we need to be a bit more careful with what we publish.

‘Ultimately, everyone has a team they play for, and we need to respect that. I’ve also read that I might be close, but not at all.

‘Often a news story comes out and it seems that if it doesn’t come true, it’s quickly forgotten and nothing happens.

‘I’m learning how this world works and getting used to it, but sometimes I could avoid a lot of the messages I receive because of this kind of news.’

Zubimendi also revealed he was warned about a possible lack of minutes by Arteta ahead of the 2026/27 campaign.

Arteta bolstered his midfield options over the summer, with Arsenal spending £75m to sign Brazil international Bruno Guimaraes from Newcastle United.

‘I spoke with Mikel at the beginning of the season,’ Zubimendi added. ‘As he does with all the players, he explained the plan to me.

‘He warned me that there were new signings and that there would be playing time for everyone. And here we are, fighting for it.

‘I understand because, for me, we’re among the five best teams in Europe. There are some fantastic players. New signings have arrived in my position, and there were already some very good players.

‘I’m also part of that competition that makes it so difficult to get playing time. I’m calm and happy with how I’m handling this situation.

‘It’s something I’ve never experienced before, and I think you learn from these situations too. Besides, it’s in moments like these that you need the people around you the most, and they’re there for you.

‘I’m patient. When you’ve been playing a lot of minutes and your role changes to not playing as much, I’m able to see the positive side.

‘I think everything passes, that just like last year the opposite happened to me and I played practically everything, this can change very quickly.’
