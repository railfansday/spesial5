Haywards Heath Town players give perfect response to Senior Cup hammering

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Blues were thumped in their home cup clash with higher-division Broadbridge Heath nine days ago but responded with a 2-0 SCFL Premier win at Roffey thanks to Josh Wright’s double.

Boss Green said: “It was a very important win. They’re up there and competing so it was similar to the East Grinstead game – it’s nice to get the wins against the teams up there with you.

"It was a cagey game, first half. First 20 minutes I thought they were the better team without really creating any chances.

"We slowly grew into the game and scored our first through Josh and we were pleased to go in 1-0 up at half-time after what was probably a 50/50 half.

"Second half I thought we went up a gear, I can’t really remember Roffey having a chance of note in the second half.

"The most important things were three points and another clean sheet – I think it was deserved over the 90 minutes.”

Green said of the cup loss: "Broadbridge Heath are a good side but we made it way too easy for them, which is very unlike us. We said to the boys ‘you’ve got to bounce back’ – and on Saturday they did.”

Leaders Haywards Heath host Billingshurst on Saturday.
