Former Chelsea man makes Xabi Alonso sack prediction after ‘disgusting’ Leeds loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Chelsea’s performance in a 4-1 loss at home to Leeds United on Saturday has been labelled ‘disgusting’ and Xabi Alonso’s future is now in doubt, reckons former Blues player Jason Cundy.

Emmanuel Emegha had given the home side the lead at Stamford Bridge, but it was all downhill from there for the Londoners.

Noah Okafor (2), Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Brenden Aaronson scored the goals as Leeds romped to a memorable win.

Daniel Farke’s team climbed above Alonso’s in the Premier League table thanks to the victory, with Chelsea losing five of their 11 top flight games so far this campaign.

The loss comes after a 1-0 defeat to Sunderland in their last outing and Cundy is increasingly concerned by what he is seeing from his former club.

Despite a seemingly impressive appointment over the summer, Cundy reckons Alonso will be out of work soon if that level of display is repeated.

‘We are rotten. We were so bad, it was so bad… I’m in disbelief at what I’ve just witnessed,’ Cundy said on talkSPORT.

‘That was a worrying performance, worrying. If that carries on, Alonso won’t keep his job, I’m telling you that for nothing.

‘You can’t do what you did against Sunderland, you can’t do what you did against Leeds today and lose those two games.’

He added: ‘We were disgusting today, absolutely disgusting. I’m still trying to find the words… as every minute ticked by, we got worse, and Leeds got better.

‘Genuinely now, I’m worried… I am worried about what this season looks like for us. I’m being completely honest here, I don’t know whether we’re going to finish… we look like bottom half fodder, easily bottom half fodder.’

The Blues have not had a disastrous campaign so far, sitting eighth in the table, but back-to-back defeats have rung alarm bells and Alonso admits his team is showing weakness.

‘We are in a fragile moment mentally,’ said the former Real Madrid boss. ‘When we receive a punch, we didn’t recover that strong, that tough enough and today that was my feeling.’

Moises Caicedo went off injured in the first half and Alonso pointed to this as a turning point for his side.

‘After the equaliser, after the change (Caicedo), we lost the momentum completely,’ he said. ‘Until then, we were doing OK. We were creating, playing a lot on the pitch, we were coming out from the pressure, but from that moment they started believing and we started not believing and it changed totally.

‘And after that, we never got the good feeling mentality in any moment, and we were not able to cope with the pressure, and we didn’t get close to the goal, so it completely changed it.

‘We need to find solutions for sure because there will be setbacks during any game and to be tougher and to be able to go through that and to know that it’s still so long to play, it’s something mandatory in this competition.’
