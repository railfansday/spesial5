A Trump-Vance Fissure

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Being in a different place on a hugely important policy question is particularly treacherous for the VP.

Vice President JD Vance and President Donald Trump attend a meeting with oil industry executives at the White House in Washington, D.C., January 9, 2026.

JD Vance may have many ambitions, but probably being the next Hubert Humphrey isn’t one of them.

During his 1968 presidential campaign, the Democratic vice president couldn’t escape the shadow of the Vietnam War vigorously prosecuted by the incumbent, Lyndon Baines Johnson, despite his VP’s private doubts about it.
