If you’re considering applying for , you'll want to pay close attention.

The Business Platinum has an $895 annual fee (see rates and fees). That makes it among the most expensive business cards on the market.

With an annual fee that high, securing the best possible welcome offer is important. Amex now offers personalized welcome bonuses for the Business Platinum and eligible applicants could receive one of the card's richest offers to date.

So, here's what you need to know about the current offer on the Amex Business Platinum.

New cardmembers can find out their offer and see if they are eligible for as high as 300,000 bonus points after spending $20,000 on purchases within the first three months of card membership. Welcome offers vary and you may not be eligible for an offer.

If you're offered the full 300,000 bonus points, that's by far the highest-ever offer we've seen on this card. So, it's worth filling out an application to see which offer you receive.

Amex will display your personalized offer during the application process, allowing you to review it before deciding whether to proceed.

Based on our October 2026 valuations, this welcome offer is worth up to $6,000. That's fantastic value if you're targeted for the full 300,000 points and can meet the spending requirements.

It's possible to get even more value if you redeem your Amex Membership Rewards points for lucrative redemptions, like first class flights on transfer partner Singapore Airlines.

To earn the welcome offer, your business must spend a lot on the card quickly. With its hefty initial spending requirement and statement credits (which we’ll talk about below), this card is targeted toward businesses with employees and significant expenses.

Keep in mind that Amex only allows cardmembers to earn one welcome bonus per card in a lifetime. That's why we encourage prospective cardmembers to be patient and wait for the best offer they can get.
