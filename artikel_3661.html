Kris Jenner and Khloe Kardashian Walked in on Lamar Odom Having an 'Orgy'

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Kris Jenner and Khloé Kardashian revealed new details about how they caught Lamar Odom having an orgy during the height of his substance abuse issues.

During the Thursday, November 5, episode of The Kardashians, Jenner, 70, and Kardashian, 42, recalled a night years ago when they went searching for Odom. They got “intel” about where Odom, 46, was located and tracked him down to a “certain hotel.”

“We’re going to extract him from the situation because he’s being held hostage by these hookers,” Jenner, who had taken weed gummies while filming the Hulu series, recalled of their mindset at the time.

Kardashian, for her part, was surprised Jenner brought up the “crack cocaine story” on screen. (Odom suffered six heart attacks and 12 strokes in 2015 and was in a coma for three days and later required months of rehabilitation. Kardashian, to whom Odom was married from 2009 to 2016, assisted with his medical care after pausing their divorce amid his addiction issues.)

While reflecting on that time in her life, Kardashian and Jenner detailed how they found their way to Odom’s room in an effort to help him.

“Khloé’s security burst into this room,” Jenner said before Kardashian added, “And all I saw [was] they were literally having a circle jerk. There were 20 people in the room.”

Kardashian said she urged everyone to leave while Jenner recalled “swiping all of the drugs” into the trash.

“This is solidifying how I will never date a freak again,” Kardashian told cameras.  ”I needed to put this on my manifest list [for a man]. Maybe I should add no circle jerks and no orgies. Also maybe no hardcore drugs.”

Kardashian previously got brutally honest about what she was manifesting in a future partner. In an October episode of The Kardashians, Kardashian expressed interest in a suitor who is “a man of God” and is “successful in his own right.”

The Good American cofounder listed physical attributes as well.

“He can’t have a micropenis,” she quipped. “Just be open to any beauty treatments, or don’t judge me for doing what I gotta do. He must be OK with plastic surgery. … I do want someone to be my biggest supporter and cheerleader. Because I wanna do the same for that person. I want my friends and family to love him.

Kardashian mentioned someone who is “not a cheater and not a liar.”

“I want someone that’s honest and makes me feel safe. First and foremost, I need to know that I can trust you,” she continued. “I don’t think I need calm because I’m not really calm. I think we could take off patience for mine because I’m not testing people’s patients.”

She concluded: “I don’t know what tomorrow holds. But then again, I never do. I don’t think I’m closed off. When the right person comes, I’m sure I will feel open. At the moment, I’m not even looking. But I imagine that someone great will come into my life one day and I will feel safe, and … we will talk about the next steps.”
