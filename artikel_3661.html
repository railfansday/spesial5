A Guide to Aspyn Ovard's Family Amid Throuple: Meet Her Kids and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Aspyn Ovard is giving fans a glimpse of her family dynamic on The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County.

Aspyn gained a following on YouTube while documenting her lifestyle as a teenager, including her relationship with now ex-husband Parker Ferris. The pair welcomed three children and split in 2024. Aspyn has made a conscious effort to keep her children’s faces off of social media and will not feature them on the Hulu reality show, which premieres in November 2026.

Following her split from Parker, Aspyn came out as queer and sparked a connection with Bri Davis — who is married to husband Billy Davis. The trio are now in a polyamorous relationship.

Aspyn and Parker tied the knot in October 2015 and share three kids. In 2024, Aspyn filed for divorce from Parker after nearly nine years of marriage. People reported at the time that the divorce documents were filed the same day Aspyn announced that she and Parker had welcomed their third baby.

“One time I had a baby and then she was in the NICU and then I filed for divorce,” Aspyn said in a 2024 TikTok video. “People somehow found the records from it and so they started talking about it online and posting it even though I hadn’t said anything and I literally just had a baby and she was premature and she was in the NICU.”

She continued, “Then like literally a thousand articles were written about it and then I just didn’t say anything about it because I just didn’t want to. And then, I said something about it like months later when I wanted attention.”

Aspyn has been candid about the beginning of her relationship with Billy and Bri, explaining that it didn’t originally begin as a polyamorous relationship.

“It didn’t start off like that,” she said during a July 2026 appearance on Becca Moore’s “For the Girls” podcast. “It was just like … me and Bri. They had an open relationship so that she could explore her sexuality, and then I met her, and then I met Billy, not with the intention of … they weren’t looking for a third person.”

Following her divorce from Parker, Aspyn swore off men and didn’t think she would be interested in joining a “throuple” — but Billy changed her perspective.

“He literally is the best. I didn’t know guys like him could exist,” she said on the “For the Girls” podcast. “He just literally changed my life.”

Aspyn and Parker welcomed their first daughter, Cove, in 2019.

The pair added their second daughter, Lola, to their family in 2021.

Aspyn and Parker welcomed their third daughter, Elle, in 2024. “You complete us, Elle 🤍,” the reality star wrote via Instagram at the time.
