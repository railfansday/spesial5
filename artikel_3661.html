GTA 6’s Mandy Could Be Hiding A Killer Secret

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

One of the first characters you meet in the opening chapter of Rockstar's GTA 6 is Mandy, and people online are thirsting hard for her. However, fans have also discovered evidence that they believe points to Mandy being a serial killer.

While Jason is fixing Mandy's sink, he discovers an object--a severed finger--was the cause of the blockage. Mandy snaps it away from him quickly, presumably to hide the evidence. Players who stick around after fixing the sink to explore Mandy's place found other clues that point to Mandy being a killer.

Jason can find a meat cleaver and tape in the trunk of her car, and items someone like Dexter Morgan might use to get rid of a body--acid and saws--can be found in her yard. Beyond all of this, a newly dug grave can be seen next to Mandy's home.

Could there be another explanation here? Of course, but the signs are pointing to Mandy not being all that she seems.
