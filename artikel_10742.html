Jimmy John’s reveals Christmas Vacation meal with exclusive Cousin Eddie collectibles

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Jimmy John’s is celebrating the holiday season with a limited-time meal inspired by National Lampoon’s Christmas Vacation, featuring new menu items and exclusive collectibles based on the classic comedy.

The sandwich chain has partnered with Warner Bros. to launch the Christmas Vacation Feast, bringing several references from the 1989 holiday film to its restaurants nationwide.

Beginning November 23, customers can order the themed meal at participating Jimmy John’s locations, with the promotion also extending beyond the menu through exclusive merchandise and a holiday-themed restaurant makeover in Chicago.

The Christmas Vacation Feast features three holiday-inspired menu items, custom packaging based on the movie, and a collectible turtleneck dickie inspired by Cousin Eddie.

A post shared by Markie_devo (@markie_devo)

Those interested in collecting all four Cousin Eddie-inspired dickies will be able to purchase them individually without ordering the complete meal.

“When it comes to the holidays, Clark Griswold set the bar impossibly high,” said Suzanne DeRouchie, Vice President of Integrated Marketing Communications at Jimmy John’s.

“We wanted to capture that same over-the-top energy in a way only Jimmy John’s could. From festive new menu items to the Cousin Eddie-inspired dickie, the Christmas Vacation Feast gives fans a delicious excuse to lean into a little holiday chaos.”

Jimmy John’s is also transforming its restaurant at 501 N. LaSalle Drive in Chicago with 25,000 holiday lights, recreating one of the film’s most recognizable scenes.

The decorated restaurant will be on display from November 21 through December 26, paying tribute to the movie’s Chicago-area setting and the sandwich chain’s origins.

Additionally, a limited-edition merchandise collection will launch November 23 through the Jimmy Store, featuring a squirrel plushie, a Clark Griswold-inspired hockey jersey, and a Cousin Eddie-inspired trapper hat.

Jimmy John’s is the latest fast food chain to embrace holiday nostalgia, following McDonald’s Home Alone Meal, which features pizza-flavored fries and collectible socks, and Burger King’s Peanuts collaboration, which includes Snoopy-themed meals for adults and kids alongside exclusive holiday collectibles.
