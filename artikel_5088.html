Bitcoin Whales Buy Over 121,000 BTC Despite 29.5% Price Crash

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Bitcoin whales have defied the negative trend seen in the Q4s of the past nine years as they have accumulated billions of dollars' worth of Bitcoin since Q4 2026 began.

Even though Bitcoin has faced severe price corrections during the period, with its price retesting levels not seen this year, Bitcoin whales have continued to buy the asset.

Onchain data from crypto analytics platform Cryptoquant shows that Bitcoin wallet addresses holding between 1,000 and 10,000 BTC have increased their holdings by 121,117 BTC in Q4 2026.

This defies the trend seen in the last nine Q4s, as Bitcoin whales have historically reduced their holdings during Q4 of each of the last nine years.

The source shared historic data from 2017, revealing that the Bitcoin whales recorded balance declines in seven of the last nine Q4 periods, with only 2019 and 2020 recording increases.

This means that the increase seen this month marks a major reversal of that historical trend, especially as Bitcoin has suffered even bigger price losses this Q4, dropping by about 29.5% over the same 52-day period.

Following the increase in Bitcoin whale accumulation seen in this quarter, further data shows that the whales now hold about 2.947 million BTC as of November 21, 2026.

Despite the increase in the holdings of Bitcoin whales this Q4, data also showed that these large holders have seen their unrealized profits drop significantly.

As such, unrealized profits among addresses holding at least 1,000 BTC have fallen to about $23.8 billion. Notably, this represents a massive decrease from the $124.7 billion recorded on September 30 and the record $294.4 billion in gains achieved on August 14, 2025.
