T. Rowe Price’s Blue Macellari: Institutions “Not Backing Away” From Bitcoin

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

For institutions, Blue Macellari says Bitcoin is no longer about whether to own it or whether it is real.

For institutions, Blue Macellari says Bitcoin is no longer about whether to own it or whether it is real. It is about sizing and how to manage it inside a broader portfolio. She tells BMTV that her institutional clients are long-term investors who are not backing away after two months of volatility, and that most major wire houses are already having that allocation conversation.
