Rob Run Is Surging On Steam As PC Players Look For A GTA 6 Alternative

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Rockstar still hasn't confirmed when GTA 6 will launch on PC, and judging by past releases, it could take anywhere from 7 to 19 months. However, one game is already filling the void.

Rob Run, a cops-and-robbers "crime sandbox" where every single cop and robber is played by a "living breathing human," launched on Steam earlier this month. It was a pretty successful release for Last Day on Earth and Frostborn developer Kefir, with a 23,000 peak on November 4 rising to 43,000 within just a few days — a number that only skyrocketed as GTA 6 got closer and closer.

On November 15, Rob Run hit a peak of over half-a-million players with a 549,661 CCU, and has continued to hold firm while PC players watch from the sidelines as every single social media feed is flooded with posts about GTA 6.

To put those numbers into perspective, it's the fourth most-played game on the platform right now, competing with heavy-hitters like CS2 and Apex Legends:

Admittedly, Rob Run is closer to something like GTA Online (specifically, RP) than GTA 6, but it has all the hallmarks: bank robberies, police chases, convenience store stick-ups, and the rags-to-riches criminal enterprise story that defines so many Grand Theft Auto games. "In just one week, you can go from being a regular thug to the boss of the server's largest organization," the description reads. "And then start over — with new experience, allies, and a more ambitious plan to seize power."

All we had to do was stop the damn train, CJ!

It sounds like a solid alternative to GTA 6 on paper, even if it doesn't quite measure up to the charm of NotGTAV, the Snake-like parody where you drive around in a Tesco car park that kept us going while we waited for Los Santos, but there's a big asterisk here. Developer Kefir admits to using AI "in the creation of certain graphic elements," and the 'Mixed' reviews paint a stark picture of cheaters running rampant and a completely unwelcoming community that have made the game impenetrable for newcomers.

"Half enjoyable AI slop rendered completely unplayable due to the worst cheating crisis I've seen since Counter-Strike," reads one review.

"Great concept, ruined by the community," says another. "Cops just kill on sight (KOS) the moment they see you wearing a mask. Criminals aren't any better. Both sides constantly camp selling areas and extraction points, making it almost pointless to even try playing the game as intended."

"Every store you try to rob is already robbed or you get shot by another player just getting out of your car," warns one reviewer. "Tried to live an honest life and play as a cop but everyone is already playing as a cop so it will not let you. As a new player, all I could do is drive around and return to Lenny's cause any time I slowed down I got rammed by some other robber."

Plenty of reviews raise eyebrows that AI appears to have been used more than the disclosure lets on, while complaining of connection issues and long maintenance windows interrupting the experience. Kefir is already working to address some of these problems, adding a more overt report button, improving servers, and limiting voice chat to target toxicity, while also adding more methods to detect cheaters, but the negative reviews only continue to pour in.

"I've never experienced this many hackers in a game before," a review from earlier today claimed. "Within 30 minutes of a server going live, people are already using aimbot and pulling bank heists. Servers not being region locked makes things even worse. The game is also practically unplayable for solo players. Winning a 1v2 is possible, but a 1v3 is impossible [...] The game is fun and has potential, but between hackers, camping, and the huge advantage groups have over solo players, it's honestly more frustrating than enjoyable. Not worth the headache in its current state."

So, while it might be a solid free-to-play alternative to GTA 6 right now, the reality of Rob Run is a game plagued by overt AI use, cheaters, and a KOS problem that makes DayZ look tame.
