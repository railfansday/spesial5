FaZe crash out of PGL Major Singapore in Stage 1 as HEROIC beat them again 11 days on

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

HEROIC have sent FaZe Clan home from the PGL Major Singapore 2026 before Stage 2. A side that reached a Major final in 2024 now faces questions about its roster heading into 2027, with captain Twistzz admitting the team has gone backwards.

Rio 2022 was the last time FaZe Clan fared worse at a Major.

The campaign started badly. FaZe opened on 24 November with a 9-13 best-of-one defeat to Wildcard, Dot Esports reported. Their only win in Singapore came that same day, a comfortable result against BC.Game on Mirage. PARIVISION then beat them 13-10 on 25 November. That defeat dropped FaZe into the 1-2 elimination pool, where HEROIC were waiting.

Singapore marked the second time in under two weeks that HEROIC ended a FaZe campaign. Mirage and Ancient had decided the earlier sweep. FaZe had won the meeting before that, knocking HEROIC out of DraculaN in October. HEROIC have now won the two most recent encounters, both with elimination at stake.

The roster has changed shape this year. Under karrigan, the organisation won its first Major title. FaZe then missed the IEM Cologne 2026 Major, and karrigan left for Team Falcons.

Twistzz took over as in-game leader. The early signs were encouraging. FaZe finished in the top four at XSE Pro League Guangzhou and BLAST Bounty Season 2. Results then fell away. The team failed to get beyond the top 16 at the Esports World Cup, FISSURE Playground 3 and IEM Beijing.

Twistzz was frank on PGL’s broadcast after the defeat. He said FaZe had “regressed a lot as a team” over the season, and perhaps individually too. He also described himself as not the most experienced captain. In his view, he does his best work with a second voice to bounce ideas off. That points to a structural question for the side, not just a bad week in Singapore.

The decline is stark against recent history. FaZe reached the final of the Perfect World Shanghai Major 2024. Spirit beat them there to take the title and US$500,000, leaving FaZe with US$170,000.

PGL announced the event as the second CS2 Major of 2026 and the first Counter-Strike 2 Major in Southeast Asia. It carries a US$1,250,000 prize pool. The tournament runs from 24 November to 13 December with 32 teams.

HEROIC play Lynn Vision on 27 November for one of the last three places in Stage 2. The playoffs follow from 10 to 13 December at the Singapore Indoor Stadium, which holds more than 12,000 people.
