GTA 6 fans in Japan disappointed after discovering censored content

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

GTA 6 players in Japan have voiced disappointment over the game’s censored content, which tones down nudity and gore compared with the Western release.

Japan is no stranger when it comes to censoring video games, with recent examples like Resident Evil Requiem famously featuring black textures over decapitated body parts and less blood.

However, on November 10, it was revealed that the Japanese version of GTA 6 was 1 GB smaller than its overseas brother. This quickly sparked fears among players that the game would be missing content to make it pass Japan’s ratings board. Well, it turns out that those fears were indeed true.

A screenshot of protagonist Jason taking a shower in the Japanese version of the game has gone viral, and no, it’s not because he’s getting all soapy. Rather, it’s because he’s showering in what can only be described as a tiny black Speedo.

For contrast, the overseas release has Jason showering in his birthday suit, with there being no steam or swimwear to obstruct his bare buttocks from view. As you can imagine, many fans aren’t exactly happy with these watered-down scenes.

“Isn’t it strange that in a country where people go completely naked in public baths and hot springs, they make people wear swimsuits to take a shower?” wrote one Japanese player.

“If they’re going out of their way to give it a CERO Z rating for 18+, what’s the point of censoring the content? I don’t get it,” replied another commenter.

Nudity isn’t the only thing that has been toned down, as Japanese gaming website Game*Spark found that the Japanese version has no option to adjust the intensity of blood and gore.

This is in comparison to the overseas version that comes with three adjustable settings, which allow players to tweak how much of a bloody mess they want to see when shooting at NPCs. The Japanese version also has less blood left on the ground after gunfights, although dismemberment remains in the Japanese release.

“They could just use parental controls and the like to restrict content that’s harmful to children, but instead, decent adults get content shoved at them that’s been watered down to the point of having its teeth pulled,” added another angry fan.

GTA 6 was released on November 19 and is now the joint best-reviewed game of all time, sitting alongside The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.
