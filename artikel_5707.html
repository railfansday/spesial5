Kim Kardashian’s Former Beverly Hills Tuscan Villa From Early Seasons of ‘Keeping Up With the Kardashians’ Hits the Market for $8.5 Million

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Kim Kardashian’s former Beverly Hills home has hit the market, with an asking price of $8.495 million for a piece of reality television history.

The five-bedroom, five-bathroom home was featured heavily in the earliest seasons of “Keeping Up With the Kardashians.” Remember when Kris Jenner and Khloé Kardashian went out on the town, and after a few too many margaritas, decided to toilet-paper the Tuscan-style villa?

Fortunately, the defaced rose bushes have been cleaned up since that 2013 episode.

Kim purchased the 4,027-square-foot home for $3.4 million in 2010. Three years later, she sold it fully-furnished, and it went for $5.12 million after a bidding war, according to Robb Report.

Later, in 2024, Kim visited the property with Khloé and celebrity broker Josh Altman, to find that it was still nearly exactly as she’d left it. Recognizable details such as a “KK” logo on the wall of her iconic glam room and her custom bedding were still there, prompting the star to share a tour of the time capsule on her Snapchat profile.

Today, the famous family’s connection to the estate is more of an “if you know, you know” situation. It’s undergone a complete transformation by AD100 designer Sebastian Zuchowicki, and now boasts a more sleek, contemporary look.

With that said, many of the structural features that made the home a gem in the first place are still present.

The gourmet kitchen has been redone in a European country style, with custom cabinetry and a spacious center island. Charming windows line one wall, while a double-sided fireplace connects the breakfast room and lounge. New, top-of-the-line appliances have also been added.

The primary suite is certainly luxurious, with a fireside seating area, two fabulous and generously-sized walk-in closets, and a spa-inspired Italian-tiled bathroom that features double vanities, a soothing shower, and a unique soaking tub.

Additionally, the second floor also includes three more en suite bedrooms, which each have a distinct personality. From floral wallpaper to chic striped walls, these rooms manage to be neutral and statement-making at the same time.

The home is perfectly primed for entertaining, especially considering the private theater.

Indoor-outdoor living is encouraged, with a covered fireplace lounge and a fully equipped outdoor kitchen.

The resort-style pool and spa makes the backyard feel like a true getaway. There’s also a tranquil fountain, a hillside sun terrace, a dedicated bathroom, and a fire pit. A gazebo and cabana serves as a spot to watch television without being cooped up inside.

The gourmet kitchen is appointed with custom cabinetry, premium appliances, a spacious center island, and a double-sided fireplace connecting the breakfast room and lounge. A private theater provides an additional space for entertainment.

Today, Kim holds an impressive collection of properties in California and Idaho. Most notably, she’s spent years renovating her $60 million Hidden Hills home. The home has been undergoing major construction since 2024, and during a recent episode of “The Kardashians,” it was revealed that the Skims founder is creating an 8,000-square-foot closet.

Kim continues to share her real estate updates with viewers, and during the Nov. 5 episode of the family’s Hulu show, she purchased a new Palm Springs, CA, estate on what appeared to be a whim.

The Kardashian-Jenners have a series of homes in La Quinta, with hidden pathways connecting the properties. When Kim realized the house located next door to Kylie Jenner’s abode was up for grabs, she sneaked onto the property to see if she liked it better than her existing home.

Later in the episode, Khloé confirmed that her older sister had, in fact, made the purchase.
