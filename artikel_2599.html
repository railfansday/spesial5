Jalen McMillan to return to practice for the Buccaneers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Wide receiver Jalen McMillan is on his way back to the Buccaneers lineup.

Greg Auman of Fox Sports reports that McMillan will return to practice on Wednesday. The move will start the clock on his 21-day window to practice before he must be activated from injured reserve or shut down for the year.

McMillan can be activated at any point in that window. He has been out since injuring his knee in Week 3 and he did not catch any passes in his two appearances this season.

Auman reports that linebacker Christian Rozeboom is also being designated for return after missing five games with a finger injury. He had 11 tackles in the first five games of the year.
