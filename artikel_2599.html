NFL predictions: Broncos vs. Steelers score predictions for Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Denver Broncos seem to be right back on the same track they found themselves on last year riding a six game win streak heading into December. They will take on the Pittsburgh Steelers who are fighting to hold onto a playoff spot after Aaron Rodgers was run out of town due to a fallout with head coach Mike McCarthy. That situation makes this game incredibly unpredictable for the visiting team.
