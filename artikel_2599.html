Jesse Minter: The only thing you can do is get up off the mat again

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

It has been a rough stretch for first-year head coach Jesse Minter and the Ravens, who have now lost six games in a row after falling to the Panthers on Sunday.

After a back-and-forth game that ended up in a loss, Minter said that every loss is tough.

“The last two weeks, our team has battled their butts off and put the team in position to have a chance to win. We haven’t gotten it done,” Minter said, via transcript from the team. “And so again, you’re going to look back. We’re going to come in tomorrow. We’re going to look at all these different plays and say, '[It] could’ve been this play, or it could’ve been that play. Can’t do that.’ It’s a one-play-at-a-time game, and we didn’t get it done.”

“The only thing you can do is get up off the mat again,” Minter added. “[It was] two really tough, gut-wrenching type of games, but again, it’s an opportunity. Every day’s an opportunity to bounce back. Every day is an opportunity to improve. We’ll go right back to work.”

Now a few games behind in the AFC North, the Ravens will be on the road again to face the Texans in Week 12 before a Week 13 bye.
