"All signs point towards" exit for 17-year-old Bayern talent amid Chelsea and Juventus interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

About a month ago, Sky Germany reported that Bayern Munich were at risk of losing 17-year-old goalkeeper Leonard Prescott on a free transfer in the summer of 2027, when his contract expires.

Judging by a fresh report from Kicker, the situation doesn't appear to have changed, with the German football outlet reporting that “all signs point towards” the youngster leaving the club.

The report adds that the expectations of Prescott and Bayern Munich “differ” in their contract talks.

With Jonas Urbig seen as the club’s long-term number one, the German champions are unable to offer Prescott a clear path to first-team football.

Chelsea and Juventus have previously been linked with Prescott, who has made nine appearances for the German U17 national team.

The talented goalkeeper, who is eligible to represent both Germany and the United States, has yet to feature this season due to a meniscus injury.
