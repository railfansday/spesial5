For the person who already has the workout routine down, this is a gift that takes their home recovery setup to another level.

For the athlete who wants to move better, not just lift heavier

Strong glutes are essential for everything from squats and deadlifts to running, jumping, and everyday movement. But long hours spent sitting can leave these muscles underactive, potentially affecting movement patterns and athletic performance.

The Align Athlete Glute Activator is designed to help users incorporate a simple glute activation technique into their daily routine.

Developed by an exercise physiologist with more than 25 years of experience, the tool is designed for a 60-second application. Users lie face down, position the device over the sacrum (the triangular bone at the base of the spine), and apply gentle traction toward their feet.

The technique is intended to support glute activation and neuromuscular control, making it an interesting addition to a warm-up or movement-preparation routine.

Whether they’re preparing for leg day, getting back into training, or simply trying to offset hours behind a desk, this is a practical gift for someone who wants to prioritize how their body moves.
