Switch to a Safety Razor – Its Blades Cost Cents, Not Dollars

A double-edge safety razor costs about $30 up front, but its blades cost about 15 cents each in a 100-pack, while name-brand cartridges cost dollars apiece. Buy the razor and 100 blades, learn a light touch, and if you shave daily, they pay for themselves within a few months.

The American Academy of Dermatology advises changing your blade every five to seven shaves, whatever razor you use. Shave daily and change the blade every seventh shave, and you’ll go through 52 blades or cartridges a year.

At $3.75 apiece, 52 of Gillette’s Fusion5 cartridges cost $195 a year. A $30 double-edge razor plus a $15 pack of 100 blades costs $45, enough for 100 blade changes, almost two years at that pace. The same 100 changes would cost $375 in cartridges instead of $45. By your 12th change, about three months in, the cartridges have already cost $45, as much as the razor and all 100 blades.

Shave your legs or underarms twice a week instead, and the payback takes more weeks but still comes by the 12th change. Any standard double-edge blade fits a modern double-edge razor, including one marketed to women, so one 100-pack can serve everyone in the house.

The catch is a learning curve. Lather up, with a cream labeled for sensitive skin if yours is, then hold the razor so the blade meets your skin at about 30 degrees. Use short strokes in the direction your hair grows, press lightly to avoid nicks, and rinse the blade after each stroke.

Loose blades can’t go in a carry-on bag. TSA lets a safety razor through security only with the blade removed and asks that sharp items in checked bags be wrapped, so pack the blades that way. On carry-on-only trips, bring a cartridge razor, which TSA’s travel checklist permits.

At home, collect used blades in a metal tin with a slot or a lid, so they can’t cut anyone who handles your trash.

Blades differ in sharpness from brand to brand, so start with a sampler pack that holds blades from several brands, and buy 100 of the one that irritates your skin least.
