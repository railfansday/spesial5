Remote jobs run from transcription, which a course can get you into, to nursing and radiology, which need a license valid in each patient’s state. Choose the one that fits the skills and credentials you already have, take its first step, and confirm the employer hires in your state before you apply.

In October 2026, 22.1% of people at work teleworked or worked from home for pay, according to the Current Population Survey from the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS). Over the year to March 2026, the rate ranged from 21.5% to 23.0%, BLS reported in The Economics Daily.

Working from anywhere has limits, though. Licensed health care workers can treat only patients in states where their license is valid, many employers hire remote workers only in certain states, and software is shrinking demand for some of this work, such as medical transcription.
