Decrypt Money Account Gets Confidential Transfers With Solana Privacy Firm Helius

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Decrypt is partnering with Solana infrastructure company Helius to bring confidential transfers to the Decrypt Money Account.

The partnership addresses the issue of publicly visible balances and transactions on Solana, said Tilo Palfner, product lead at Helius. "Public wallets can reveal what someone holds, sends and how they trade," he explained. "Once a wallet is linked to a person, that history can expose their finances to anyone on the internet. That matters for everyday users as much as it does for companies."

Rings are programmable, onchain environments for encrypting money, markets, and finance - without ever leaving Solana

The core programs are live on Solana mainnet and have been audited by security firms Certora, OtterSec and ZK Security, according to Helius. The default ring is permissionless, with no global auditor key, and users can recover their private balances directly from Solana without relying on a separate provider to store their encrypted data. Helius is also building Custom Rings, which will let institutions set their own compliance controls and auditor access, with release planned for the fourth quarter of 2026.
