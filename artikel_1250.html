Forget Barcola: Liverpool's Biggest Problem Was Made Clear in Palace Win

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Florian Wirtz and Bradley Barcola have faced scrutiny for recent performances, but both players ultimately stepped up for Liverpool on Sunday afternoon at Selhurst Park.

The Reds strolled to a 3-1 victory in South London, courtesy of goals from Alexis Mac Allister, Wirtz and Alexander Isak, cancelling out Eddie Nketiah's late consolation. Andoni Iraola's men were fluid and dynamic in attack, while they relied on some Alisson Becker heroics to prevent them from conceding multiple first-half goals.

The result sees the Merseysiders move above Chelsea and into the Premier League's top four, rounding off a promising few weeks for Iraola ahead of the international break.

Liverpool invested heavily in Wirtz last summer, and again in Barcola this summer. Both players' performances are subsequently under a microscope, and understandably so.

However, few can have genuine complaints about either player's contributions against Palace. The pair combined for Wirtz's goal, before the German showed composure to lay the ball off to Isak inside the Eagles' box for the Swede's strike.

Wirtz and Barcola's Statistics vs Crystal Palace

Both players were bright and showed moments of quality that impacted the match. They'll rightfully be out of the firing line for the time being, although the victory shouldn't prevent Liverpool supporters from shining the spotlight on another player, who certainly didn't do himself justice against Palace, and generally hasn't this season.

Alongside Wirtz and Barcola, Cody Gakpo was deployed in Liverpool's trio behind Alexander Isak against Palace.

On paper, this is the strongest front four available to Iraola, but unlike the other three, Gakpo did very little to justify his inclusion. The likes of Victor Munoz and Rio Ngumoha were introduced late on, and both were able to showcase their attributes much more than the former PSV Eindhoven star was.

The bizarre nature of Liverpool's squad-building has seen them end up without a natural right-sided forward, so Gakpo has, perhaps unfairly, been shunted out to this side. However, this ploy was so ineffective in the first half at Selhurst Park on Sunday, that Iraola swapped his two wingers at the interval.

This did little to improve Gakpo's performance. In the opening 45 minutes, he was struggling when receiving the ball on the right, with the angles that playing on this side created not conducive to him being able to carry out dangerous attacking actions. In the second half, playing on the left solved this issue, but he still wasn't able to make much of the moments when he found himself driving inside onto his stronger foot.

The Netherlands international was ponderous and inefficient with his final action, and this resulted in an incredibly profligate display on the numbers.

He created just one chance, took no shots and accumulated just 0.05 expected assists. He also only beat his man once, whilst winning four of his eleven ground and aerial duels.

This was a completely non-existent showing from Gakpo, who is typically busy and industrious, even if not always polished in the final third.

Unfortunately for the player, this is a bit of a recurring theme this season. He's failed to contribute to a goal in each of his last five Premier League appearances, letting his minutes pass him by without impacting the game.

Liverpool have identified the perfect Van Dijk replacement, and they're ready to pay up to £90m to bring him in.

While Barcola and Wirtz continue to face the brunt of the Liverpool censure when things don't go well, and understandably so given the fees the Reds outlaid for the duo, Gakpo's quiet performances are going under the radar.

Iraola needs to start considering handing more minutes to his promising young wingers, Ngumoha and Munoz, instead of the 27-year-old.
