Serie A official line-ups: Milan vs. Frosinone

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Milan rely on Goncalo Ramos, Christian Pulisic, Diego Moreira and Luka Modric as they host high-flying Frosinone’s Fares Ghedjemis and Giorgi Kvernadze.

It kicks off at San Siro at 17.00 GMT (18.00 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from today’s five Serie A games on the Liveblog.

The Rossoneri remain an enigma this season, able to lurch from victory over Inter to defeat in the Europa League, going into the break for international duty with a 2-1 result away to Genoa.

Mike Maignan has shaken off a knock sustained playing for France and is able to start in goal, but Matteo Gabbia, Samuel Chukwueze, Fikayo Tomori, Ruben Loftus-Cheek and Alexis Saelemaekers are still out of action.

Strahinja Pavlovic is still not at 100 per cent, so Sankhoun Diawara moves into central defence and Davide Bartesaghi is preferred to Pervis Estupinan.

Modric and Adrien Rabiot keep the midfield reins, behind a trio of Moreira, Goncalo Ramos and Pulisic.

Frosinone have had an amazing campaign after promotion from Serie B and sit in sixth place, just four points behind Milan.

They had won three of the last four before the 1-0 defeat at Cagliari, scoring a remarkable 16 goals in four rounds.

Star striker Antonio Raimondo is still out with a sprained ankle, along with Florian Grillitsch, while Gabriele Bracaglia is suspended and here replaced by Cittadini on the left flank.

Ghedjemis, Romano Schmid and Georgia international Kvernadze therefore start together with the Austrian as a False 9.

There are also reshuffles in defence, seemingly moving to a 3-4-3 formation, rather than the usual 4-2-3-1.

Milan: Maignan; Gila, De Winter, Diawara, Bartesaghi; Musah, Modric, Rabiot; Moreira, Ramos, Pulisic

Frosinone: Palmisani; Calvani, Monterisi, Cittadini; Oyono, Masini, Calò, Tchato; Ghedjemis, Schmid, Kvernadze
