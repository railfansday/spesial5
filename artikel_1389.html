Pedro Porro puts it back on Barça and Real

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Since the end of last season, Pedro Porro (27 years old) is often cited to come and strengthen FC Barcelona or Real Madrid. Holder with Spain and author of a major World Cup, the right back attracts the attention of the big leagues. It will still be necessary to reach an agreement with Tottenham, where it extended only a few weeks ago, until 2031. The English club will demand at least €70 million to let it go. “I have a contract with my club and I am very happy there. I have to respect that. Of course, it’s football: there are always unforeseen events, rumors circulating, but for now, I’m focusing on the national team and then we’ll see», he responds to Sport during this international break.

He is of course not insensitive to the rumors circulating, particularly those leading him to the Blaugrana. “It’s flattering. When one of the best clubs in the world is interested in you, it’s always a good sign, because it probably means I’m doing my job well. I must continue on this path», he continues. Porro was even praised by Joan Laporta himself during an interview a few weeks ago, saying he was “one of his favorite players.» «I hadn’t seen it. It’s also a compliment from him. As I said previously, it’s a good sign when, for example, the president of a club other than mine appreciates my work. This definitely means I’m on the right track.» To the wise.
