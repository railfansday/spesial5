Get the Aven Home Equity Visa® Card if you want a home equity rate, with no annual fee, on a planned cost like a renovation or card debt you’ll repay on a schedule. Don’t use it for everyday spending, because your house secures every charge.

The Aven Home Equity Visa Card lets you draw on a home equity line of credit (HELOC) with a Visa card, so every purchase, balance transfer, and cash transfer to your bank account borrows against your house. It’s built for homeowners who want a low variable rate on borrowing they’ll repay on a schedule.

Homeowners who’ll repay a renovation, or card debt moved to a home equity line, within a few years get a low rate here with no annual fee. Anyone who keeps a card balance month after month does better with a 0% intro APR balance transfer card, which doesn’t put the house at risk.
