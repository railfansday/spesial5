Your Midterm Guide to Voting for the Arts

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

We rounded up key races and where candidates stand on culture funding, AI, and more.

Happy Election Day. Today, voters across the country are casting their ballots on races that could not only impact the balance of power in Congress, but also decide the future of democracy in the United States. If you’re an artist or cultural worker, there are several key states to watch, where issues including arts funding, artistic freedom and censorship, and the incursion of AI on creative professions are now at stake. We compiled a dedicated Midterm Election Guide highlighting House, Senate, and Gubernatorial races that may prove decisive for arts and culture, especially as Trump and his allies ramp up their attacks on history, heritage, and diverse communities.

But as we’ve always emphasized here at Hyperallergic, the policies that most benefit artists are also what benefit the majority: affordable healthcare and housing, a better education, the right to speech and expression. Candidates including Claire Valdez in New York and Jonathan Nez in Arizona have voiced that very sentiment in interviews with us as part of our robust coverage ahead of the Midterms over the last six months — we’re re-sharing many of those articles below.

One last note: We’re only able to produce our in-depth, unbiased reporting because we are completely independent, not beholden to donors or investors. Please consider supporting us as a member so we can continue this work. And, should you feel so inspired, we love seeing your “I Voted” selfies … :)

Midterms are tomorrow, marking the first nationwide election since President Donald Trump started his second term in the Oval Office. In the wake of Trump’s financial and ideological crackdowns on Smithsonian, unapproved pet projects around the US Capitol, devastation to cultural landscapes through border construction, and dozens of other policies targeting history and heritage, many will be lining up at the polls with arts and culture policies on their minds.
