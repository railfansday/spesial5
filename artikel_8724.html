The 10 Best Wellness Gifts for the Most Mindful Person in Your Life

Self-care might be a personal undertaking, but that doesn’t mean you can’t give your people tools they’ll find useful along the way. A well-chosen wellness gift is typically a blend of thoughtfulness and innovation, something that delivers support, creativity, and a little extra mindfulness to everyday routines.

Our chosen gifts of the year are YJ editor-tested and approved, buzzed about by friends and family, and generally coveted by wellness enthusiasts. They include analog tools that encourage presence as well as gadgets that support your body and mind. And because we’re all about self-care, it’s okay if one of your recipients is you.

Here, 10 wellness gifts to help make your holiday shopping a touch more intentional.

Yoga blocks are not one-size-fits-all, or at least they don’t have to be. With its lower-than-usual profile, Manduka’s slender block is perfect for those who want slightly deeper, but still supported stretches. And for some, the elongated shape is just a better fit for hand size and posture. The blocks are made of lightweight, grippy cork, and might just be the perfect gift for the yogi that has everything (but also knows they need nothing).

This smart ring supports anyone who is so over any sort of tracking technology. I tested the Pulse Mindfulness Ring—which pulses to remind you to breathe deeply, focus, or whatever other intention you choose—and found the lightweight, stylish accessory to be a helpful and minimalistic tool for maintaining presence throughout the day. If your giftee’s goal is less about personal optimization and more about simple reminders to enjoy life, Pulse is worth slipping on.

Any tool that helps the body feel relieved, relaxed, and de-puffed is a gift we can get behind. Sliding this oversized gua sha tool over lotioned or oiled limbs is one of my favorite self-care moments. Lymphatic drainage is an actual thing, but even if all that this giant gua sha is doing is giving me a good old-fashioned massage, I offer it my gratitude. If you or someone you know enjoys a multi-step evening routine, add this to the holiday list.

Yes, you can give the gift of a regulated nervous system. The Open app is a wonderland of breathwork, meditation, mindfulness, even yoga and movement classes. It’s also really, really pretty, which let’s be honest, is an important aspect of an app that’s designed to calm you down. I’ve admittedly fallen off my streak, but for a few months I was meditating daily (and happily) with Open. An Open credit pack allows you to gift that to someone else. Basically, for the person who wants to elevate their mindfulness routine but isn’t sure where to start, there’s an app for that.

I hold a lot of tension in my shoulders; I also work at a laptop all day everyday, which might have something to do with my tightness and pain. At the end of a massage a few years ago, the masseur very seriously told me to invest in a Theragun to help loosen some of my long-held knots. The mini version of the tool is lightweight and easy to grip, making at-home massage sessions simple. For what it’s worth, my shoulders have definitely loosened up.

Sitting in stillness, tuning in to your breath, and letting your thoughts float by is the work of a lifetime—and notoriously difficult. If a regular yoga practice aims to limber up your body so you can sit for longer stretches, a good set of meditation pillows can help make the prospect even more comfortable. This pretty, eco-friendly cushion makes meditation all the more aspirational. Who knows? This could be the start of your giftee’s mindfulness journey, or the thing that helps the sometimes-meditator develop a more consistent sitting session.

An oracle deck that doubles as an astrology primer turns a daily card pull into a lesson in the stars. Cards such as “Cusp,” “Fifth House,” “The Moon,” and “Solar Eclipse” speak to life experience and celestial events, a welcome reminder of the interconnectedness of it all. Whether your loved one is a deck collector or just getting into their spiritual card game, they’ll appreciate learning something new (or cementing their astrological knowledge) on the daily. Plus, the psychedelic illustrations are enough to compel your giftee to pick a card, any card.

A new journal is a fresh start. So, why not invest in a journal that’s been raved about by social media (particularly the JournalTok community, which is very much a thing)? This journal comes with a generous page count, allowing it to be whatever your loved one needs it to be—and challenging new owners to actually fill its 411 pages rather than getting a shiny, new notebook every few months. And for the organizational freaks among us, the notebook’s table of contents and numbered pages make it easier to actually keep track of your thoughts.

We are all obsessed with our smartphones. But if there’s a particular person in your life who could use a little extra help curbing their tech use, Brick is a solid gift. This little gizmo is tantamount to a physical wall between phone users and their current content obsessions by restricting access to selected apps. Rather than skipping self-imposed limits while you’re out and about—or working at your desk—Brick requires physical contact with the phone to lift restrictions. The gift of technological boundaries is truly one that keeps on giving.

I have been cozying up in this absurd (and I must admit, fairly cumbersome) red light bag for a couple of months now. Sure, the price tag is steep, but I find the resulting relaxation of mind, body, and spirit to be very real. My skin is glowing, certain aches and pains have subsided, and I often fall asleep in it. Is this entirely due to this therapy bag? Probably not. But if there’s a lover of wellness and innovation in your life, I guarantee they will appreciate taking in this at-home, full-body red light at their leisure.
