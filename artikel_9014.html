Urbanism for what? Professional practice in an expanded field

The Faculty of Architecture at the Federal University of Bahia (FAUFBA), through the research groups LAB20 and Laboratório Urbano, and the State University of Bahia (UNEB) invite researchers, faculty, and professionals to the seventh edition of the Seminar on Urbanism and Urbanists in Brazil, which aims to discuss professional practice in urbanism within an expanded field.
