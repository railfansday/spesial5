Lift recliners are essentially recliners engineered to lift the user with minimal manual effort required. Designed for those who have a hard time sitting down or standing up from a seated position, they use a power mechanism to raise the chair up. They look the same as traditional recliners, but the lifting mechanism makes them a popular choice for seniors.

Lift recliners come in single-motor or dual-motor designs. As the name suggests, single-motor chairs have one motor that controls the chair’s entire range of movement, powering both the lifting mechanism and reclining mechanism. These chairs are less adjustable but tend to be more affordable than dual-motor chairs. Most single-motor lift chairs have one button that operates the lift and one that operates both the recliner and the footrest as one piece, so when you recline the chair, the footrest follows in a preset position.

Dual-motor chairs have two motors that power different parts of the chair. One motor controls the recline angle, while the other controls the footrest and lifting mechanism. This design allows for infinite positioning, so you can adjust each independently to find your perfect lounging position.

Recliners can take up a fair amount of space in a room, so it’s important to consider the chair dimensions when shopping. Space is especially a concern for lift recliners, which tend to be larger and heavier than standard recliners on account of their motors and parts.

Due to their reclining design, recliners also need enough space behind and in front of them to fully extend. Before you start shopping, measure your space to make sure the recliner can fit without hitting the wall or any other chairs or tables.

Besides considering the room space, make sure the recliner is a suitable size for your needs. Most recliners will advertise their weight capacity, which should not be exceeded, especially in the case of lift recliners. Tall users may opt for a longer chair for better comfort. Choose a recliner that is suitable for both the user’s height and weight.
