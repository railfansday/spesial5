Bournemouth’s Adams lauds ‘incredible manager’ Rose

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Tyler Adams lauded Marco Rose as an "incredible manager" as Bournemouth prepare to return from the international break and keep up their sparkling form.

Bournemouth went into the break on the back of a 2-1 win over Ipswich Town, which left them seventh in the Premier League.

Since a 5-1 defeat to Chelsea on October 10, the Cherries have reeled off eight straight wins across all competitions.

Only Arsenal (67), Manchester City (59), and Brighton (50) have collected more Premier League points in 2026 than Bournemouth (49 – W12 D13 L4), with the Cherries aiming to win five successive games in the top flight for the very first time when they take on Nottingham Forest on Saturday.

And though Rose had a slow start to his tenure after he replaced Andoni Iraola, Bournemouth have now hit their groove.

Rose deserves all the credit, so says Adams, who told Bournemouth's media channels: "He's an incredible manager. I'm not just saying that because he’s my manager now. The way that he looks at the game, the way that he breaks it down, I think we're going through a period of evolution, which at times takes a period of adjustment and adaptation.

"But you can see the things that we’re starting to change and the things that are starting to click at the same time.

"With the ball, we have a lot of ideas that we’re trying to execute and get guys into good positions to score goals. Some guys are playing in different positions that they weren’t accustomed to playing last year, and there's a little bit of a different formation as well. But the ideas are starting to click, and he has a lot of good ideas."

Bournemouth are competing in Europe for the first time, and Adams says the squad are learning to cope with a hectic fixture list.

He said: "It's extremely difficult. The most important thing is that you need a full squad of players, guys who are always ready to play and compete.

"When you just have Premier League games and there's one game a week, sometimes the same players play every single week. But balancing that competition, it's nearly impossible. From top to bottom, we need every single player.

"We're in a good place. The morale of the team is good. It's about finding that balance and being able to put in those Thursday, Sunday, Thursday performances over and over again, and repeat it. That's the level of consistency that we have to chase."

While Bournemouth are in fantastic form, Forest beat Manchester City before the international break to move out of the relegation zone.

No player has more goal involvements in the Premier League this season than Evanilson (nine – five goals, four assists), while only Elliot Anderson (30), John McGinn (25), and Boubacar Kamara (24) have won more fouls than his 22.

Delap leads the Premier League this season for both shot-ending carries (14) and goal-ending carries (three), while, among players with over 50% of their minutes played as a central striker this season, only Matheus Cunha (33) has recorded more 10+ metre progressive carries than his 26.

His double propelled Oliver Glasner's team to that much-needed win over Man City on November 7.

Glasner is set to take charge of his 100th Premier League game (W33 D32 L34), though he has yet to beat Bournemouth in the competition (D3 L2), the joint-most he has faced a side without tasting victory.

Furthermore, in all competitions against sides managed by Rose, Glasner has just two wins in 16 meetings (D5 L9), losing each of the last four.

Bournemouth are first and joint-first, respectively, for shot-ending high turnovers (20) and goal-ending high turnovers (three) in the Premier League this season, while they have had the joint-most shots following opposition errors (18) and scored the most goals from those situations (eight), four more than any other side.

Forest are looking to win successive Premier League games for the second time this calendar year, after a run of three victories in April/May last season. Indeed, in 2026, 60% of their points have come away from home (21/35), the highest proportion in the competition.

However, Forest are winless in their last six away league games against Bournemouth (D2 L4), since a 2-1 victory in the 2014-15 Championship campaign.

Bournemouth are unbeaten in all eight of their Premier League games against Forest (W4 D4).
