Gemini Reveals 3 Biggest Altcoin Losers, XRP Included

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Home » Ripple (XRP) » Gemini Reveals 3 Biggest Altcoin Losers, XRP Included

One of America’s most-popular crypto platforms takes note of the altcoin bloodbath, naming 10 most affected ones.

Gemini, one of the largest American crypto exchanges led by the Winklevoss brothers, just posted their take on the current market dip, calculating which altcoins have taken in the biggest deficits. XRP makes the top four with a 43.43% drawdown since the start of 2026, while Bitcoin Cash (BCH) is figuratively leading with 61.76% in the red.

Even though slipping slightly softer than XRP, other competing altcoins such as Solana (SOL) & Hedera Hashgraph (HBAR) were also named on the list, soaking up a 34.02% and 38.13% deficit, respectively. These declines occurred amid a crypto market cap near $2.5 trillion on  November 11, 2026, with Bitcoin dominance around 60%.

There’s a couple of key factors that established such a trend: liquidity slimming, the weakening speculative interest, lackluster institutional interest and general macroeconomic conditions are all major factors. However, that could provide the right setup for a bounce back rally if the retail interest returns along with the big-time player purchases.

Ripple continues its expansion in the institutional payments sector, stablecoin and tokenization areas, but that hasn’t necessarily translated into sustained buying interest for XRP. On the other hand, XRP’s involvement in Ripple’s native stablecoin trades on the decentralized exchange (DEX) has refuted the broader claims about XRP being pushed out of the ecosystem.

On the contrary, Ripple’s native XRP coin accounted for 92% of Ripple’s RLUSD swaps on the exchange, the latest Evernorth research paper states. However, the backtracking towards $1.10 this week comes after a similar setup witnessed last Summer, when XRP’s price bounced off the same liquidity pockets prior to securing $1.67 as the quarterly high, CoinGecko says.

XRP bulls had to resort to $1.058, a level falling hard below all four moving-average thresholds,  following a $1.21 million liquidation event on Perpetuals. This has pushed the long versus short ratio to 0.9912, meaning short-sellers still have a slight advantage over the XRP bulls. Liquidity pockets around $0.98 to $1.05 will be of crucial importance for the near-term direction.
