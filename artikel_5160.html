PEPE Sits 25% From Overtaking SHIB By Market Cap

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Home » Altcoins » PEPE Sits 25% From Overtaking SHIB By Market Cap

Pepe’s lagging behind Shiba Inu by a historically-near distance, but the broader market takes a toll on both.

The old-timer meme coins Shiba Inu (SHIB) & PEPE Token (PEPE) have been competing for the top spot in the global meme currency charts. Presently, PEPE Coin’s (PEPE) market cap stands at $1.86 billion, while Shiba Inu’s (SHIB) hovers around $2.53 billion, says SoSoValue.

There’s a huge discrepancy between the trading volumes, as PEPE Coin (PEPE) currently edges Shiba Inu’s daily turnover by a ten-fold distance. Despite today’s 11.29% downturn for the frog-embossed memecoin, PEPE garnered $470 million in contrast to SHIB’s $43.8 million.

Two days ago, PEPE Token (PEPE) leapfrogged to its monthly peak at $0.00000562, scoring a 14.4% rally while the broader market carried on gradually dipping. Amid those gains, PEPE emerged as the biggest meme coin by trading volumes, even overtaking Dogecoin (DOGE).

Pepe has a market cap of about $2.11 billion against $2.61 billion for Shiba Inu per @coingecko, and $PEPE would need a 24% to 25% rise to pass it. pic.twitter.com/yYuZOU4ehv

The speculative interest on Perpetual markets has also witnessed a considerable spike. With the general market soaking in fear at ‘29’ on Crypto’s Fear & Greed Index, PEPE coin’s price positions have flipped towards bearish levels. The long versus short ratio now flashes 0.90.

That means the short-sellers are outnumbering the market participants betting on the meme coin’s price ascension. The $2.26 million in excessively-leveraged position liquidations hints at PEPE’s holders expecting a PEPE price rally, while short-sellers incurred just $187,000 of it.

Conversely, the top-trader accounts on Derivatives have kept strongly above 1, registering a 1.089 ratio on OKX exchange. The strong belief came weeks after Canary Capital amended their first-ever PEPE ETF, tracking the Spot-market price of the cult-like meme currency.

The previous rally was partly sparked by the news of Justin Sun becoming the top PEPE Coin holder. Arkham data links $51.25 million of PEPE, about 2.82% of supply, to crypto wallets tied to Justin Sun, making him the largest individual holder as PEPE sparked up a 33.5% rally.

🚨 NEW: Justin Sun holds more $PEPE than any other individual, Arkham reports.His official and Arkham-predicted wallets collectively hold nearly 3% of PEPE’s supply, worth $51.25 MILLION, following massive inflows over the past 10 days.Two withdrawals from Poloniex alone… pic.twitter.com/f8Q61TBiCk

In spite of that, PEPE Coin (PEPE) now stands out as the biggest loser on Thursday, driving the price lower by more than 11% on the daily time-frame. The ability to stay above $0.00000444 is of key importance: with much of last month’s rally tracing back, PEPE relies on demand areas.

These are usually captured by the crypto whales, big-time investors who tend to enter during a local price bottom. This time, the Chaikin Money Flow (CMF) points to hefty selling going on, as the CMF index hovers between -0.17 to -0.30 on time-frames ranging from one-hour to one-day.
