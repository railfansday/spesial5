Quantum software startup Q-Ctrl raised $290 million at more than $1 billion valuation

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Salesforce Ventures led the Series C round for the quantum software company, which makes GPS-free navigation tools

Q-Ctrl, a quantum software company, raised $290 million in a Series C round led by Salesforce $CRM Ventures, the venture arm of Salesforce, the company said on Thursday. The round values Q-Ctrl at more than $1 billion, according to Bloomberg.

Q-Ctrl said it will expand its software work and scale globally to meet customer demand across sectors from finance to energy. It also plans to continue autonomy and aerospace programs.

The company's main product, Ironstone Opal, is a quantum navigation system that works when GPS is unavailable or denied. Q-Ctrl said the system is being deployed on commercial aircraft with Airbus and in autonomous platforms with Lockheed Martin.

Q-Ctrl counts DARPA, a U.S. military agency, the navies of Australia and Britain, and Airbus among its contract customers, Chief Executive Michael J. Biercuk told Bloomberg. The technology can fix its location with an error of no more than 10 meters (about 33 feet), according to Bloomberg. Jamming and spoofing have degraded satellite navigation signals amid the conflicts in Ukraine and the Middle East.

Q-Ctrl's software can also make quantum computers more stable by cutting their error rates, according to Bloomberg. "Think of our software as the equivalent of noise-canceling headphones," Biercuk told Bloomberg.

"The hard problem in quantum is not building the machine, it is getting useful output from it," Robert Keith, a partner at Salesforce Ventures, said in a statement. Keith said Q-Ctrl's control software is the reason Salesforce Ventures led the round.

"Q-CTRL is one of the few quantum companies with real customers and real revenue," Per Roman, founding partner of Bullhound Capital and a Q-Ctrl board member, said in a statement.
