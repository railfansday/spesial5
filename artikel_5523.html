Tesla is winding down Cybertruck production to make room for more Cybercabs

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Free daily briefing on global business news.

CEO Elon Musk said the truck sold 70,000 units over three years and is set to wind down with a net project loss of $1.9 billion

Tesla $TSLA will begin phasing out Cybertruck production at Gigafactory Texas around the middle of 2027 so the space can go toward expanding Cybercab output, CEO Elon Musk said in a post on X $TWTR on Friday.

Musk said Tesla installed tooling to build 125,000 Cybertrucks a year but sold 70,000 over three years. He said the company spent $2 billion on research and development and made $100 million in gross profit from sales, for a net project loss of $1.9 billion.

The Cybertruck has suffered from slow demand, according to The Wall Street Journal. Without it, Tesla's lineup consists of the Model Y and Model 3.

Tesla first announced the Cybertruck in 2019, and the first trucks went to customers in late 2023, according to TechCrunch. The base price rose to around $60,000, versus the under-$40,000 starting price Tesla cited at the 2019 reveal.

Tesla has put Cybercabs on the streets of Austin, Texas, for the past two months with no major incidents, according to TechCrunch. The company plans to expand Cybercab operations to Dallas later this month.

Tesla began commercial deployment of a small number of Cybercabs in Austin in September, the same day the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration opened an audit query into whether the vehicle was properly certified for public roads. Tesla began manufacturing the Cybercab at Giga Texas earlier this year. Musk has said material revenue from the Cybercab is unlikely before at least 2027.

The Cybertruck is the third Tesla offering to be axed as the automaker shifts toward artificial intelligence and robotics, according to TechCrunch. This year, the company stopped making the Model S sedan and Model X SUV, then gave that factory space over to Optimus humanoid robot production.
