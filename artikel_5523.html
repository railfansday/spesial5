Alibaba is launching a new flagship AI model, claiming to rival U.S. leaders

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Qwen4-Max cut average task costs 62% versus Qwen3.8-Max in Alibaba's Work360 evaluation, and smaller versions are planned

Alibaba introduced Qwen4-Max, a new flagship AI model, on Monday. Users can reach the model through QwenCloud with either a Token Plan or an API, and through QwenWork, Qoder and QwenStudio.

Alibaba is asserting that its new model matches the capabilities of systems from Anthropic and OpenAI, according to Bloomberg. The company said the model placed close to the best results on coding tests and beat Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.1 and Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro.

Qwen4-Max is built on a new Qwen4 architecture with 2.4 trillion parameters, native vision capabilities and a one-million-token context window, Alibaba said.

On Alibaba's Work360 benchmark for industry tasks, Qwen4-Max scored 52.69, up from 47.74 for Qwen3.8-Max, the company said. The average cost per task in that evaluation fell from $4.43 to $1.67, a 62% reduction. Alibaba said it lowered API prices compared with Qwen3.8-Max, with large cuts in cached-input pricing.

Alibaba said it added code security checks to the model's software engineering training. The share of code generated securely at the function, file and repository levels rose from 8% to 14.7%, the company said. For cybersecurity, it kept training data for defensive tasks such as vulnerability detection and remediation and removed data for offensive tasks. Alibaba said an internal expert review found no capabilities posing large-scale cybersecurity risks.

Alibaba released Qwen4-Max without open weights, which would let outside users download the model or tailor it to their own needs, according to Bloomberg. The company said smaller models in the Qwen4 family will come to open source.

The Qwen series has been downloaded more often than any other open-weight model family worldwide, according to Bloomberg. Moonshot and DeepSeek are among the other Chinese firms putting out models that have quickly grown stronger at programming and logical problem-solving.
