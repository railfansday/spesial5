AMD beat earnings estimates as data center revenue nearly doubled

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The chipmaker forecast fourth-quarter revenue of about $17 billion, plus or minus 3%, and its stock rose in extended trading

AMD $AMD reported third-quarter revenue of $13.4 billion, up 45% from a year earlier, as Data Center segment revenue rose 97% to a record $8.5 billion. Adjusted earnings were $2.09 a share, up from $1.20 a year earlier, the company said.

Analysts had expected revenue of $13.1 billion, according to The Wall Street Journal. They had expected adjusted earnings of $1.95 a share, according to CNBC, or $1.97, according to Bloomberg. Bloomberg said analysts had predicted data center revenue of $8.2 billion.

AMD stock rose in extended trading on Tuesday.

"We delivered a record quarter with revenue exceeding the high end of our guidance as Data Center segment sales nearly doubled year-over-year," AMD Chair and CEO Lisa Su said in a statement. She said the company expects growth to accelerate in the fourth quarter as it scales production of its Helios rack-scale systems and "Venice" processors.

On a GAAP basis, net income was $2.4 billion, or $1.46 a diluted share, compared with $0.75 a year earlier. The results included a $1.1 billion charge from the change in fair value of warrants issued to certain customers. The charge stems from AMD's agreement with OpenAI, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Client and Gaming segment revenue fell 8% to $3.7 billion. Gaming revenue dropped 40% to $773 million on lower semi-custom and graphics sales, while Client revenue rose 7% to $2.9 billion. Embedded segment revenue rose 29% to $1.1 billion.

Anthropic, Meta $META, Microsoft $MSFT, OpenAI and Oracle $ORCL Cloud Infrastructure are running models and workloads on the first Helios systems, which use AMD Instinct MI450 Series GPUs and 6th Gen AMD EPYC CPUs, the company said.

For the fourth quarter, AMD expects revenue of about $17 billion, plus or minus 3%. The midpoint implies growth of about 66% from a year earlier and 27% from the third quarter. The company expects non-GAAP gross margin of about 55%, compared with 56% in the third quarter.

Analysts had expected fourth-quarter revenue of $16.2 billion, according to CNBC, or $16.3 billion, according to Bloomberg.

AMD stock slid last week as investors reacted to Intel $INTC's results, which pointed to weak PC sales and a shortage of server chips. Intel reported that data center and AI revenue rose 78% to $7.3 billion. Its stock dropped more than 22% in after-hours trading Thursday.

The higher cost of memory chips has hampered PC manufacturing and sapped buyer interest, according to Bloomberg.
