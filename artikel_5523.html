Japan's ARK Signs MOU with U.S.-Based UMBC/IMET to Advance Grouper Hatchery Development

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

BALTIMORE, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- ARK Inc. (ARK), a Japan-based aquaculture technology company developing modular recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS) to make land-based fish farming more accessible, announced today that it has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the University of Maryland Baltimore County (UMBC)Institute of Marine and Environmental Technology (IMET), a marine and environmental research institute based in Baltimore, Maryland, United States, to advance sustainable aquaculture.

Under the agreement, ARK and UMBC/IMET will collaborate on research, technology development, and demonstration activities related to grouper hatchery production in Baltimore, Maryland, leveraging ARK's recirculating aquaculture technology and IMET's aquaculture research expertise.

The collaboration will also explore the establishment of grouper hatchery facilities in Maryland, as well as opportunities for student training and workforce development aquatic resource conservation, and future commercialization. Both organizations will further examine the potential application of research outcomes in the United States, Japan, and other international markets.

The United States imports approximately 80% of the seafood it consumes, resulting in an annual seafood trade deficit of approximately USD 20 billion. In April 2025, a U.S. executive order aimed at strengthening the competitiveness of the domestic seafood industry highlighted the expansion of domestic aquaculture as a national priority.

Against this backdrop, ARK and IMET have been exchanging technical expertise and research insights to explore potential solutions to challenges facing the aquaculture industry.

In particular, the two organizations have been evaluating the potential application of ARK's modular recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS) to support stable grouper hatchery production. Discussions have also focused on joint U.S.-Japan research into hatchery technologies and the potential development of the first commercial grouper hatchery in Maryland, United States.

The signing of this MOU marks a further step in these discussions, establishing a framework for cooperation in research, technology development, education, training and demonstration activities.

By combining ARK's aquaculture technology with IMET's aquaculture scientific expertise, the collaboration aims to advance grouper hatchery development in Maryland and the broader U.S. market, while exploring opportunities for future commercialization and international applications.

Under the MOU, ARK and UMBC/IMET will explore cooperation in the following areas:

Professor and Chair, Department of Marine Biotechnology and Institute of Marine and Environmental Technology (IMET), University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC)

"This MOU establishes a groundbreaking partnership between global aquaculture leaders at UMBC/IMET and in Japan to overcome critical bottlenecks in farming the grouper, one of the most heavily fished and high-demand marine species. By closing the grouper's life cycle and enabling year-round juvenile production, this initiative will make commercial hatchery-based grouper aquaculture viable. Integrating basic and translational research across academia and industry, our work will culminate in the establishment of the world's first large-scale grouper hatchery in Maryland."

Co-founder/ Representative Director/, CEO, ARK Inc.

"I am deeply moved that several years of friendship with Professor Yoni Zohar and our friends at IMET have finally borne fruit today. Both the United States and Japan share a major challenge: we import far too much of our seafood. Under this five-year MOU, we see tremendous potential in combining ARK's RAS technology with grouper hatchery production here in Baltimore. Grouper is prized as a delicacy across many food cultures, from Japanese sashimi to Chinese and Middle Eastern cuisine. By working with IMET to build commercial hatcheries and train future talent, I hope this fish will serve as a bridge, fostering strong, warm relationships between local communities in the U.S., Japan, and around the world"

About the Institute of Marine and Environmental Technology (IMET)

The Institute of Marine and Environmental Technology (IMET), based in Baltimore, Maryland, United States, is a world renown research institute specializing in marine and environmental sciences.

Bringing together multiple universities within the University System of Maryland, IMET conducts research across a wide range of fields, including innovative aquaculture, marine ecosystems, environmental science, and marine biotechnology.

ARK Inc. is a Japan-based aquaculture technology company that develops and sells modular land-based aquaculture systems, including recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS), as well as seafood produced through land-based aquaculture. With business activities in Japan and international markets, ARK aims to contribute to food security by expanding opportunities for seafood production on land, complementing conventional fisheries and marine aquaculture.
