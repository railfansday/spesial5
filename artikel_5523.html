Former Century Communities CFO David Messenger Named CEO of Villa

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Offsite home builder Villa has named David Messenger, former chief financial officer of Century Communities, as CEO. In the role, Messenger will lead the company as it seeks to expand into larger residential communities and grow partnerships with home builders, land developers, and multifamily owners.

Messenger spent more than a decade as CFO of Century Communities, helping scale the company from 150 to 1,800 employees across 18 states. During his tenure, he led the builder’s initial public offering and seven acquisitions, oversaw more than $3 billion in debt and equity capital raises, and helped launch its mortgage, title, and insurance businesses.

“I see great potential to build on that foundation by bringing a large-scale home builder’s operating discipline to Villa, with an emphasis on predictable execution and profitable growth,” Messenger says. “We look forward to partnering with homeowners, multifamily operators, land developers, home builders and capital partners who share that vision and want to bring their projects to life.”

Villa, founded in 2019, says it has delivered more than 425 homes using offsite construction. Its work spans accessory dwelling units, primary homes, multifamily properties, and infill development. The company is also developing the 25-home Acacia Village in Santa Rosa, California, and Alpine Village in Westminster, Colorado.
