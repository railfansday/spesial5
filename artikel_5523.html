The Famous ‘Orange County Choppers’ Compound Just Got a $100K Price Cut—and It’s a Gearhead’s Dream

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

A 38.3-acre ultimate collector’s playground, formerly owned by master mechanic Paul Teutul Sr. of the popular reality TV show "American Choppers," has received a $100K price chop.

Located in Montgomery, NY, the adventure estate has a pond with a waterfall, fenced paddocks, animal run-in sheds, a two-story auto showroom, and a 6,000-square-foot commercial-grade mechanic’s garage and workshop.

Teutul sold the iconic Hudson Valley retreat to the current owners for $1,500,000 in 2019 after originally listing it for $2,895,000 in 2017.

The farm, garage, and family compound, known locally as the “Orange County Choppers House,” would be ideal for an equestrian, auto enthusiast, or investor seeking a legacy compound.

“It’s a substantial property that offers a lifestyle beyond the main house that feels like its own retreat,” said listing agent Danielle Bartula of Howard Hanna | Rand Realty. “The Orange County [Teutul] family had goats, llamas, and horses.”

A gated entrance opens to meadows that lead to the primary log home. The 3,448-square-foot house has a gourmet eat-in kitchen with high-end appliances, custom wood cabinetry, granite countertops, and an island with a prep sink.

The wood-clad interior also includes a two-story great room with vaulted wood ceilings, skylights, a floor-to-ceiling stone fireplace, and French doors that open to a wraparound covered porch.

“This part of Montgomery is known for hiking, mountain biking, and horse farms,” Bartula added. “It’s in the heart of the Hudson Valley with a wide range of everything nearby.”

The main level offers an executive office and a primary suite with a walk-in closet and a spa-inspired bath with a jetted soaking tub, oversized shower, and double vanity.

An upstairs loft that could be used as additional living space overlooks the great room. A partially finished lower level with a bar area awaits a buyer’s finishing touches.

“I could see a family who loves to entertain with family and friends buying this property or an athlete who has to go back and forth from the city,” she said. “It’s only an hour and 20 minutes away to New York City but offers a true retreat setting.”

Other highlights include a mudroom with a dog washing station, a whole-home generator, manicured gardens, and koi ponds.

“The current owners used it as a secondary residence but for the previous owners, it was their primary,” Bartula noted. “I think it would be great to have it used as a primary but would certainly work well either way because it is very private yet very convenient.”

Outdoors, a multi-level heated pool has a slide and wading area. It is surrounded by a sundeck for lounging, plus an outdoor kitchen and shaded pergola for entertaining. The nearby pool house has a tiki bar, full bath, and outdoor shower.

A spectacular heated 7,000-square-foot garage features two guest quarters that could also be used for staff or extended family.

The two-story barn can accommodate up to five horses and has a hay loft and “room for expansion.”

A mechanic’s garage and workshop provides additional flexible space for hobbyists, collectors, or business pursuits.

The 1-acre pond has docks and a grilling patio, ideal for fishing, relaxing, or entertaining.

“There is no way you could rebuild this for that price,” she said. “We have shown it to a lot of people and they are all enamored. It’s a really special property but the hardest part is finding someone who wants to maintain all of this.”
