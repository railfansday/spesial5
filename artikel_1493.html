Manchester United fire Michael Carrick!

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Michael Carrick’s debut during the second half of last season was very encouraging. With him at the helm, Manchester United finished the Premier League at a brisk pace of 2.25 points per game, even qualifying for the Champions League. Unfortunately for the former midfielder, the off-season has passed and the first results in this new exercise have once again plunged the Red Devils into full doubt. In 12 matches, there were only two victories for four draws and six defeats. In this type of situation, the fuse is all found. The management of the English club has decided to crack down and fire Carrick with immediate effect.

“Manchester United confirm that Michael Carrick has stepped down as head coach of the men’s first team. Michael joined the club in January and led the team to third place in the Premier League by winning 12 of the remaining 17 matches in the 2025/26 season, accumulating the most points in the Championship during that period. After a disappointing start to the season, the club came to the extremely difficult conclusion that change was necessary to realize our ambitions. Michael is, and will forever be, a Manchester United legend. Just like when he was a player, he has conducted himself with the utmost dedication and professionalism throughout his return to the club. His leadership and exploits last season will forever be remembered. The club intends to appoint a permanent successor as soon as possible. »

Successor to Ruben Amorim on the bench, the 43-year-old technician lasted 10 months. He arrived last January. The new poor performance against Aston Villa in the Premier League was one too many. Humiliated at home by Bournemouth (0-3), miraculous winner against AS Roma in the Champions League, Manchester United is bogged down in a situation it knows too well. The club is once again facing a series of poor results. With only one success in the Premier League, against promoted Ipswich on the 2nd day (5-2), the Red Devils occupy 18th place in the championship and do not seem able to fight with the best.

The situation is undoubtedly even more complex. Anger took over the locker room. After the setback against Atlético de Madrid in the Champions League (2-0), Lisandro Martinez criticized the lack of resources put on the table during the summer. MU, however, invested €173 million on midfielders Carlos Baleba alone (€75 million from Brighton), Andrey Santos (€56 million from Chelsea) and Youri Tielemans, recruited from Aston Villa for €41 million. After Como (4-1 defeat), it was the captain, Bruno Fernandes, who criticized Carrick’s tactical choices. Without the support of the locker room, it was time for it to end.
