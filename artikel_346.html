I Found the 20 Best Black Friday Tech Deals, Up to 50% Off (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Why not take a break from your uncle’s MAGA monolog, put down your turkey-laden fork, and embrace a more modern American Thanksgiving tradition: shopping for the best Black Friday deals. This weekend used to be about retailers dumping old stock at big discounts to make room for the new, but now the deals on tech come thick and fast, often on the latest gear (though it’s not always clear which discounts are real).

Welcome to WIRED’s guide to the best Black Friday tech deals. I can promise you that everything here is worth buying (we’ve tested and recommended all of it), and these are real discounts (not the year-round price). If you need an upgrade, a pick-me-up, or some great techy gifts, you’re in the right place. For more Black Friday deals, check out our other guides, like the Best Black Friday TV Deals or the Best Black Friday Mattress Deals.

Perhaps the most exciting release of the year (unless you're an Apple person), the Z Fold8 is our top pick of the best folding phones. Samsung’s experience shows with a fresh form factor that feels revelatory. Both the 5.5-inch cover screen and the 7.7-inch inner screen are bright, sharp, and genuinely useful. There's a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset and plenty of stamina, thanks to the 4,800-mAh silicon-carbon battery. A $350 discount brings it to the lowest price we’ve seen yet.

After testing many, many Apple 3-in-1 wireless chargers, this is the one that claimed a permanent place on my bedside table. It looks classy, and I love the weighted base, enabling you to grab your iPhone without pulling the whole charger off the nightstand. The pad also tilts and holds your iPhone vertically or horizontally, with support for StandBy mode. It can fast-charge capable iPhone and Apple Watch models and charge any AirPods with a wireless charging case. The 5-foot braided cable is permanently attached, and there’s a 36-watt wall charger in the box.

Handy as they are for keeping you connected when your phone dies unexpectedly, portable chargers can be very same-y. One of the reasons the Nimble Champ is in our Best Power Banks guide is Nimble’s focus on the environment. It’s made from 90 percent certified recycled plastic and comes in fully biodegradable packaging. It also works well, with capacities starting from 5,200 mAh, with USB-A and USB-C ports, and up to 15-watt charging.

While it's no longer my top pick of the best travel adapters, this is still a solid option and will keep all of your gadgetry running wherever you travel. There's a classic slider design for the plug that covers more than 200 countries, but you also get one USB-C port, two USB-A ports, and a handy retractable USB-C cable (perfect if you're forgetful).

It’s the thoughtful design that elevates the Backbone One above the rest of the best mobile controllers out there. Slot your phone into the compact cradle, with a USB-C jack for speedy connectivity, and you get satisfyingly clicky and responsive controls plus a 3.5-mm headphone port. You can also customize it for different games, or even use Backbone’s software as a one-stop gaming hub (if you’re willing to pay a subscription).

If you want a virtual screen for gaming that you can take anywhere, the Viture Beast XR are my favorite smart glasses for gaming. These glasses can plug directly into your phone (and many other gadgets) via USB-C to give you a bright 174-inch virtual screen. It's also worth snagging the excellent 8BitDo controller. If these are still too pricey, the Viture Luma Pro for $339 ($160 off) are a good alternative.

Already the cheapest Apple laptop, an extra $50 off the MacBook Neo is not to be sneezed at. In fact, it's a great deal for anyone seeking an affordable laptop that feels premium, with a metal chassis and quality display. The limited 8 GB of RAM is a definite compromise, but for folks with modest needs, the MacBook Neo should serve just fine.

Don't ask me why they keep taking away our ports. God forbid you should want to plug something into your laptop. Well, you can stick it to those minimalist designers with one of the best laptop docks. Despite the small size, this one boasts powerful ports, including 96-watt power delivery and support for up to three 4K displays at a time (or a single 4K monitor at 120 Hz using the USB-C port). The card slots, USB, and headphone jack are up front, with the display, power, and Ethernet ports around back.

The Asus RT-BE58U is perhaps the ideal Wi-Fi 7 upgrade for modest homes and apartments still struggling with the crappy router their internet service provider sent, and that's why it tops our Best Wi-Fi Routers guide. It’s easy to set up and use, can cover up to 2,000 square feet, and has plenty of ports. As a dual-band router, it lacks the 6-GHz band, but it has all the other advantages of Wi-Fi 7. There’s also support for VPN service, separate IoT or guest networks, and Ai Mesh.

We love Amazon's Eero mesh systems for being easy to set up and use, but they also double as smart home hubs with support for Matter, Thread, and Zigbee. The Eero Pro 7 is a tri-band mesh router that adds the 6-GHz band to the familiar 2.4-GHz and 5-GHz bands. For busy households with many devices and a 1-Gbps or faster connection, this mesh system is ideal. Just bear in mind that the 6-GHz band is short-range. The pricey Eero Plus subscription is an extra $10 per month (or $100 per year), but it adds a ton of value with parental controls, advanced security, ad blocking, and even a password manager and VPN service.

This is a rare and tasty deal on my favorite Xmas lights. They work indoors or out, can be scheduled, and support a bunch of lovely animated effects. While I'm on the topic of Philips Hue and its excellent but horribly expensive lights, you might want to check out some of its other Black Friday deals (I would go for the wall washers and the TV lightstrip).

You know what I don't miss? Mowing the lawn. A good robot mower, like this relatively affordable one from TerraMow, will do it for you, quietly. No wire required; it recharges itself. You just set a schedule and relax. OK, it sometimes leaves a verge, but the only model I've tried that doesn't is more than twice the price.

These wireless noise-canceling headphones may not be the latest release from Sony, but they are still an excellent pair of cans with a far deeper discount. The Sony WH-1000XM5 are relatively light and comfortable, producing accomplished sound in every scenario, and have great control options.

The JBL Flip 7 is the Bluetooth speaker that has it all. It's durable, it has stamina, it produces a punchy sound, and it comes in fun colors. As the best Bluetooth speaker you can buy, this deal is for real.

Yes, you should read more, and Amazon’s Kindle e-readers make it easier to do just that. Our current favorite is the Kindle Paperwhite (12th generation). It has a sharp 7-inch display, auto-adjusting warm light, three-month battery life, and snappy performance, and it’s slim and light. It even has integration with Overdrive for your library books and support for several languages.

Our favorite budget Android tablet has a 12.2-inch, 2400 x 1600 pixel LCD panel that’s sharp, colorful, and bright, with a 120-Hz refresh rate. It's perfect for web browsing or kicking back with a movie or sports game, but the aging Qualcomm Snapdragon 4 Gen 2 chipset makes it not the best option for gamers.

This deal on LG’s 65-inch C6 OLED TV caught my eye. At 48 percent off the regular price, this is the cheapest it has ever been, making it a better option than the entry-level LG B6 we just reviewed. Released this year, the Samsung S95H OLED TV has never been priced lower than this. This TV promises accurate color and deep blacks. It also sports a snazzy silver bezel that’s recessed behind the main OLED panel that resembles a metal picture frame.
