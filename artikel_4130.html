Xbox Brings Its Free Tier Of Cloud Gaming To Loads More Players Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

You may recall that back in July, Xbox announced a new free version of Xbox Cloud Gaming that would allow you to play games at no charge, but you'd be forced to deal with ads as a compromise.

That tier of Xbox Cloud Gaming was in testing for months, but as of today (November 11th), it's rolled out publicly - meaning the vast majority of Xbox users around the world should now have access to it.

Xbox has been very quiet about it today so far, but based on what we've seen around the web, the free tier of Xbox Cloud Gaming will provide you with a 30-minute slot to stream "select games from your library" as well as select free-to-play games, after which time you'll be kicked off and will have to start a new session.

You'll see ads before the session begins, and seemingly never again throughout your 30-minute playtime.

"You can stream select games for free by watching ads before each gaming session. Session time limits apply. After a session ends, you can watch another set of ads to start a new session."

Despite the rapid session length, this could prove a popular option for anyone who wants to play GTA 6 on Xbox One (for example) without being forced to pay anything for Xbox Cloud Gaming. We'd urge some caution for now though, as Xbox has yet to confirm whether GTA 6 will be one of the "select" games that supports this feature.

Meanwhile, the cloud playtime limits that were announced a while ago for Xbox Game Pass members have now started kicking in, which also means you can now purchase "Cloud Playtime" to top it up if you run out.
