Stocks Waver Ahead of Nvidia Earnings; Trump Kicks Off Asia Trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

US equity futures waver ahead of Nvidia’s earnings as the chip giant forms a new committee to oversee national security policy and export control following US semiconductor curbs. President Trump arrives in Manila to meet the leaders of India, Australia, and Japan ahead of the summit with President Xi Jinping. Alexandra Mousavizadeh, CEO of Evident, joins “Bloomberg Brief” to look ahead to Nvidia’s earnings. Lauren van Biljon of Allspring discusses fiscal risks in 2027. (Source: Bloomberg)
