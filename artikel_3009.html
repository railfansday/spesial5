NFL predictions: 3 bold takes on the Cowboys vs Titans game

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Dallas Cowboys got close to upsetting the San Francisco 49ers late in the game last week, but were unable to close the deal. That has led to chaos among the defensive coaching ranks.

The Cowboys are at home for this week's game against the 1-8 Tennessee Titans, and are 7.5-point favorites according to our friends at FanDuel. The over/under is 54.5 points.

Let's get into three bold predictions for the Titans game.

Tony Pollard returns to his old home as one of the main cogs in the Titans offense. Pollard has 628 yards rushing this year with six rushing touchdowns, along with 136 yards receiving an an additional touchdown.

Against Dallas, who has trouble containing running backs, especially dual-threat ones, Pollard will have a day. He will amass over 100 yards from scrimmage and will score twice.

This is more hope than anything else. The Cowboys defense needs something good to happen, and this week the Titans will oblige. While struggling with Pollard, they will frustrate Cam Ward and the passing attack with pressure and tight coverage on the Titans' short passing game. Things will go so well that they will actually score a touchdown on defense.

Last week, it was George Pickens who kept the Cowboys in the game against the 49ers. CeeDee Lamb came on late to help out, but spent much of the game on the wrong page with Dak Prescott. That gets smoothed out this week as Lamb goes off for over 150 yards receiving and a receiving touchdown.
