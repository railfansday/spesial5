Eagles castoff Fred Johnson joins Andy Reid on Chiefs practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Eagles castoff Fred Johnson joins Andy Reid on Chiefs practice squad originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Veteran offensive lineman Fred Johnson, released by the Eagles Friday, just added seven wins to his resume.

Johnson has signed with the Chiefs' practice squad, so he goes from a 2-7 team that's lost seven straight games and is buried in last place in the NFC East to a Chiefs team that's 7-2 and tied with the Broncos atop the AFC West.

Johnson, 29, started six games – five at left tackle, one at right tackle – during the Eagles' 2024 Super Bowl championship and then eight last year – seven at right tackle, one at left tackle.

But this year, he was passed over for the right tackle spot when Lane Johnson retired in favor of rookie 3rd-round pick Markel Bell. He played only 24 snaps on offense this year after playing 481 in 2024 and 626 in 2025.

In Kansas City, Johnson joins a team that has the 6th-best odds to win the Super Bowl and is coached by Andy Reid, who has won three of them.

The Chiefs list Johnson as a guard on their roster, although he hasn't played guard regularly since he was with the Buccaneers in 2022.

Johnson entered the NFL as an undrafted rookie with the Bengals in 2019 and spent three years in Cincinnati, starting eight games. After a year in Tampa the Eagles signed him in 2023 and he wound up a valuable backup during the Super Bowl run.

He signed with the Jaguars as a free agent in March of 2025 but when that didn't work the Eagles re-aquired him in a trade just after training camp ended. He hit free agency this past spring before re-signing with the Eagles two weeks into free agency.

In his seven-year career, Johnson has played in 92 games with 23 starts. He's played 1,986 career snaps.

The Eagles haven't won a game since Lane Johnson retired. With both Lane and Fred Johnson now gone, the Eagles' swing tackle becomes John Ojukwu, a former undrafted free agent out of Boise State who started nine games for the Titans from 2023 through 2025. The Titans released Ojukwu five times over that three-year period.

The Eagles signed Ojukwu as a futures player in January, waived him at final cuts, then signed him to the practice squad the next day. They signed him to the 53-man roster on Monday.
