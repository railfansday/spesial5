It's been eight years since the two collaborated on the Apple iPhone 8.

If you’ve been waiting for an excuse to upgrade your home audio while supporting a good cause, here’s your chance. Apple is planning to revive its Product Red charity partnership with new versions of the HomePod Mini 2 and AirPods Max 2, according to a Bloomberg report. The $149 HomePod Mini 2 came out in early November and will now be offered with a red mesh cover as part of this collaboration. The AirPods Max 2 will come in silver, instead of red, but will feature a red digital crown.

While Apple has released red phone cases in the meantime, the last hardware collaboration came in 2018, when the iPhone 8 and 8 Plus were released in Product Red colors. Red cases were also released.

A representative for Apple didn’t immediately respond to a request for comment.

The release would coincide with the 20th anniversary of the first Product Red collaboration, the iPod Nano Red Special Edition. For that event, Apple contributed $10 from the sale of each device to the Global Fund to help fight HIV/AIDS in Africa. Product Red was founded in 2006 by politician Bobby Shriver and Bono of the band U2, and the iPod Nano Red special edition included music from U2.

Apple has yet to announce pricing and availability for the two devices. But according to the report, the product could be available Dec. 1, which is designated as World AIDS Day.
