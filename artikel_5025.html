Mutsamudu, Comoros, November 23, 2026 – MEXC, a pioneer in 0-fee digital asset trading, has launched the limited-time MEXC Earn Plus Event. The event is designed to offer users more diverse and flexible asset allocation options. During the event, eligible USDT holdings (capped at 100,000 USDT) can enjoy promotional APRs of up to 13%.

MEXC Earn Plus is a flexible-term savings product. Users can stake USDT to earn daily interest based on the real-time APR shown on the product page, which may adjust with market conditions. MEXC allocates staked assets to underlying products, whose returns generate interest. Unlike traditional fixed-term savings products, Earn Plus has no lock-up period, allowing users to earn interest on idle USDT while keeping it readily accessible for trading.

Key highlights of MEXC Earn Plus include:

Since November 12, the maximum APR on USDT in Earn Plus has been raised from the previous 11% to 13%, and the event is set to run for at least 20 days. The promotional rates are tiered, with eligible USDT holdings earning a 12% APR on the first 50,000 USDT and a 13% APR on the next 50,000 USDT.

For event details, applicable APR tiers, eligibility requirements and risk information, please visit the MEXC Earn Plus event page.
