14 Best Early Black Friday Rug Deals to Style Every Room (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Rugs are one of those niche investment pieces that can cost a fortune but will have little to no resale value after your dog has done a number on it (guess which one) or dust and hair have built up a layer of filth on top. All of which is to say, if you’re going to invest in a rug, you might as well get a good deal. Dear readers, now is that time.

Black Friday and Cyber Monday are when all the rug brands converge to roll out their biggest markdowns of the year, with deals from retail giants like Amazon and popular rug-exclusive retailers like Ruggable that make the business of underfoot fabrics its specialty. Below, a list of some of our favorite early Black Friday rug deals (and their makers). We’re tempted to bring these rugs home ourselves since we’re already seeing some designer pieces up to 50% off.

Stay tuned for more news on Black Friday events; we’ll update this guide as more sales are announced.

Design Within Reach is a go-to for iconic modern furniture and decor from brands like Herman Miller and Fritz Hansen. Its Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales are among the few opportunities each year to score investment pieces at more approachable prices. Ahead of this year’s event, you can take advantage of up to 50% off clearance items, up to 40% off markdowns, and up to 25% off Herman Miller products. In the current bargain bin, you’ll find rugs spanning the shape, color, and pattern spectrum (like these two numbers, one from Beni Rugs and another direct from the retailer itself).

Ruggable is one of our favorite destinations for durable and washable rugs, whether you’re shopping for outdoor rugs, kids rugs, or even a doormat for your entryway. The brand’s ongoing Last Chance Hosting Sale rug sale includes up to 40% off select styles, many of which you can throw in the washing machine. Shameless plug: Our AD rug collection is also featured in the sale, including this Hema Persad design inspired by Moroccan textiles and architecture. “It fits perfectly in my space and was super easy to set up—the velcro underlay is game changing since I have an energetic puppy at home and it really holds the rug in place, even when she’s being rough with it,” says Stefanie Lindenbaum, director of project management and activation at Condé Nast, who tested out the rug. She also found it easy to clean after a puppy accident, noting a little soap had it looking as good as new. Other designer collaborations are discounted too, like this Scalamandré tiger-print piece and modern graphic style from Jonathan Adler, available in nine colorways.

Lulu & Georgia’s textured, high-pile, and solid color accent rugs come in rich shades of amber and green, with tufted 100% wool constructions that look and feel luxurious. More than a hundred products have been added to the sale section, like this editor-approved rug designed in partnership with Sarah Sherman Samuel (which you might recognize from Mandy Moore’s Altadena home). “Its contemporary look is neutral enough to align with lots of different aesthetics, whether you’re looking to add some whimsy to a midcentury decor scheme or bring some edge to a cottagecore setup,” shopping editor Audrey Lee shares in her review, adding that it hardly sheds. “The material has an elegant sheen to it that looks and feels almost like velvet, so it’s a dream to walk across, and it even brightens up the room a bit.” Another AD100 designer worth browsing is Jake Arnold, whose hand-loomed wool rug combines modern and traditional elements with tonal color-blocking and scalloped corners.

Known for its easy-care styles and collections sourced from design hubs like Morocco, India, and Turkey, direct-to-consumer brand Revival is one of our favorites when it comes to vintage and new rugs. Right now, you can get 15% off when you buy two rugs, with the offer extending to everything except for custom orders, rug pads, and swatches. If we could steer you toward two, we’d start with this washable vintage-inspired, lived-in rug, which fit right into the existing interiors of senior shopping editor Nashia Baker’s “moody midcentury” Brooklyn apartment. “I went with the ‘ruby’ option, which looks just as rich in person as it does online,” Baker says. “The floral details—my favorite feature—give the rug a modern romance that I don’t see as often in the market.” For something more understated, shopping producer Lauren Arzbaecher recommends the Mori Washable Wool Rug, calling it the “perfect neutral.” “The warm-toned stripes aren’t too loud, and play well against the brown velvet of my corner sectional,” she says, adding that it’s easy to clean and doesn’t shed as much as a typical wool rug.

The internet’s superstore has no shortage of quality transitional rugs for indoors and out, including celebrity-led and interior designer-backed collections. Joanna Gaines’ Magnolia Home collab with Loloi is on sale for a limited time, with deals on pieces like this indoor/outdoor striped rug in a versatile neutral palette. We’re also partial to Safavieh’s natural rug and the nuLOOM’s earthy tones and geometric patten.

We are always here for a Serena & Lily sale, and this year’s is no exception. Self-dubbed the Sale of the Year, its well curated, coastal vibes are up to 50% off across the site, including 30% off or more on all things floor coverings and free shipping on everything. We found great deals on natural fiber rugs and personality-packed picks like this charming floral number. We highly recommend a deep dive to find the piece that’s right for your vibe, whether that’s a vintage-inspired traditional rug, a breezy indigo blue design, or a hand-knotted wool option.
