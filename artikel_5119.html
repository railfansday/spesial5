Not every blockchain is built for finance or NFTs. Some are trying to connect blockchain directly to the physical world.

IoTeX is a Layer-1 blockchain designed to connect real-world devices, sensors, and machines to decentralized networks. The project operates at the intersection of IoT, AI, and blockchain, aiming to create a trusted data layer that securely transmits real-world information to decentralized applications.

The native token IOTX powers the ecosystem by securing the network through staking, paying transaction fees, and enabling device registration. As of now, IOTX is trading around $0.002329.

Will the project remain relevant in the coming year?

Let’s explore CoinPedia’s IoTeX price prediction for 2026, 2027, and 2030.

IoTeX (IOTX) is trading around $0.00233, down more than 1%, as weak market sentiment and lower liquidity continue to pressure smaller altcoins. The decline appears to be part of the broader market downturn rather than the result of one specific event.

Extreme fear across the crypto market has added selling pressure, while IOTX remains below its short-term moving averages.

IOTX is finding support around $0.0022–$0.00225, but a stronger recovery may need higher trading volume and improved market sentiment.

Meanwhile, IoTeX is holding a governance vote on IIP-65, known as “Bunker Mode,” which aims to protect staking against future quantum-computing risks. The vote runs until November 28, 2026. However, this update has not yet led to a clear price recovery.

IoTeX is expected to strengthen its position in the Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) sector as it expands its ecosystem throughout 2026.

One of its biggest strengths is the Burn Drop mechanism, which reduces the circulating supply as new devices join the network. A portion of IOTX is permanently burned, while the remaining tokens are distributed to long-term stakers, encouraging users to hold their tokens.

The expansion of W3bstream is another major catalyst, allowing real-world data from smart devices to connect with leading blockchains such as Ethereum, Solana, and Polygon.

At the same time, enterprise partners deploying DePIN infrastructure must purchase and lock IOTX, helping reduce the available supply. With new developer grants expected to bring more projects to the network, higher on-chain activity could further support IOTX’s price growth throughout 2026.

IoTeX is still in a long-term downtrend, but the chart shows that the selling pressure has slowed. Since mid-2026, IOTX has been moving mostly sideways around the $0.0022–$0.0040 area, suggesting the market is building a base.

The most important support is $0.00217. IOTX is currently trading around $0.00233, just above this level. As long as buyers defend $0.00217, the coin has a chance to start recovering.

The first major resistance is at $0.00549. IOTX needs to break above this level with strong buying volume to confirm that the sideways phase is ending and a fresh upward move is beginning.

If that breakout happens, the next major target on the chart is $0.01344, which would represent a much stronger recovery from current levels.
