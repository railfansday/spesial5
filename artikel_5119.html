Bitcoin can move sharply overnight, during a weekend or while a trader is away from their screen. By the time they check the price again, a level they were watching may already have been broken.

That is why alerts, technical indicators and tools for testing strategies have become important for monitoring price movements, analysing volatility and making informed trading decisions. As crypto markets have matured, traders have gained access to more sophisticated technology to navigate this 24/7 environment.

PrimeXBT, a global multi-asset broker and crypto asset service provider, has now integrated with TradingView, giving its clients access to these tools alongside the ability to place Crypto Futures and CFD trades directly from the TradingView platform, while benefiting from top-tier trading conditions and the flexibility to use crypto as trading capital.

Early crypto exchanges generally offered basic charts and order books, which meant traders looking for more detailed analysis often had to use separate charting tools. Today, they can examine multiple timeframes, add technical indicators and set price alerts, while historical testing tools help them assess strategies against earlier market conditions.

Traders often follow several coins at once, and price alerts notify them when a market reaches a chosen level. Historical charts also show how a strategy performed during previous rallies and sell-offs. With hundreds of coins and a growing range of derivatives, screeners let traders scan by price move, volume and technical conditions.

TradingView brings these capabilities together in a single analytical environment. Used by more than 100 million traders and investors worldwide, the platform covers cryptocurrencies alongside shares, forex, commodities and indices, allowing traders to analyse different markets using the same tools.

It offers 400+ built-in indicators and strategies, more than 100,000 public community indicators, and Pine Script for building custom indicators, strategies and alerts. Bar Replay rewinds the market to test an idea against past conditions, and its screeners cover crypto coins as well as pairs on centralised and decentralised exchanges.

Together, these tools help crypto traders monitor multiple markets, respond to price movements and evaluate strategies in an environment where volatility can emerge at any time.

As a verified TradingView broker, PrimeXBT allows clients to connect an existing account or create one through the Trade panel and place orders directly from the TradingView platform. This connects advanced market analysis with trade execution in a single environment.

For example, a trader monitoring Bitcoin on a four-hour chart might identify a trading range and set alerts at both edges. When an alert is triggered, they can review the price movement, compare it with developments in gold and the US 500, and decide whether the conditions support a potential trade. If they choose to act, they can place the order through their connected PrimeXBT account without leaving TradingView.

PrimeXBT’s authorisation screen lists what TradingView can do on the account: open and manage orders, close active positions and check the balance, but it has no permission to withdraw funds.
