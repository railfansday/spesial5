Mom Begged Boyfriend to ‘Put the Gun Down’ Before He Killed Her, Her Daughter

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A Florida man was arrested after he allegedly shot his girlfriend and her daughter to death and as one of his alleged victims begged him to “put the gun down.”

The Broward County Sheriff’s Office announced in a press release that David Keith Thornton has been charged with two counts of first-degree murder for his role in the deaths of his girlfriend, Suzanne Travers Courtade, and her daughter, Meghan Courtade.

Authorities were made aware of the incident on Thursday, November 12, when deputies received a report of a shooting around 7:21 p.m. at a Pompano Beach, Florida, home.

Suzanne, 66, called 911 and told dispatchers that her “daughter was shot by a pellet gun and is unconscious.” She then asked a neighbor for help before she screamed, “Dave put the gun down, put the gun down,” according to a probable cause arrest affidavit viewed by Law & Crime.

The dispatcher then heard a weapon fire multiple times. The same neighbor who was called for help later contacted 911 to report the alleged incident.

Once deputies responded, they discovered both Suzanne and Meghan, 23, dead in the street. Five spent .380-caliber shell casings were recovered near Thornton’s vehicle, while two additional 9 mm casings were located near another home.

Thornton, 64, had his hands up as he exited the home and said there were weapons inside, according to the affidavit.

The responding officers then entered the home, where they reportedly found weapons that matched the caliber of the recovered shell casings.

Investigators proceeded to speak to neighbors, with one person saying she thought the gunshots were loud fireworks. Soon after, Suzanne knocked on her door and explained that her daughter was unconscious and not breathing. The neighbor said she then went to Meghan and found her lying on the ground.

“While trying to assist Meghan, Thornton approached her and Suzanne and told [the neighbor], ‘This has nothing to do with you, go back inside,'” officers wrote in the affidavit.

The neighbor told officers that Thornton was holding a “dark object” in his hand, which she believed was a gun. She then ran to her home before she heard several gunshots.

Following his arrest, Thornton refused to speak with investigators. He is currently in custody at the Broward County Jail, where he is being held without bond.

Not much is known about Thornton’s dynamic with the victims, though police said that he, his girlfriend and her daughter had lived in the neighborhood for nearly two decades.

It’s not currently clear if Thornton has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following his arrest. The Broward County Sheriff’s Office did not immediately respond to Us’ request for comment regarding updates in the case.
