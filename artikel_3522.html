Don’t Expect To See Vice City For A Long Time In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

For those still waiting for GTA 6 to unlock, it's possible you have something of a mistaken impression of what the early hours will look like. Despite partially sharing a setting with GTA Vice City, you should not expect to be walking up the game's equivalent of Ocean Drive and Miami Beach in the opening minutes, as in the 2002 game. Far from it.

As we noted in our video discussion on why we gave GTA 6 a 10/10 in our review, Tamoor and Jake did some expectation-setting. Rather than starting the game in Vice City, you'll instead spend the opening hours in a smaller corner of the state of Leonida, Key Lento. Vice City is undoubtedly the crown jewel of the game's world, but it could be as much as 20 hours before you reach it, depending on how you play.

This seems to be a deliberate decision that, as Tam says, allows you to progress through the world at roughly the same pace as the ambitions of co-stars Lucia and Jason. The opening area may not be as glamorous as Vice City, but it is no less detailed than the rest of the game. It's possible it's only meant to last you around 5-6 hours, but we found it easy to get lost spending far more time than that, which resulted in Tam not reaching Vice City until after 15 or 16 hours.

Key Lento provides a good look at how the game and its simulation work, letting you eventually progress to a much larger space (seriously, the map is huge) with a good understanding of how things work.

All of this is to say, the glitz and glamor of Vice City are waiting for you--eventually. To make the most of the opening hours, be sure to check out our guide on what to do in GTA 6 first.
