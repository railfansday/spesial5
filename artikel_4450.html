Toyota expands C-HR EV recall to 19,000 globally

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Toyota is recalling 19,000 electric C-HR SUVs globally, with about 10,000 affected in the US.

In September, Toyota issued a safety recall involving certain model-year 2026 C-HR electric SUVs in North America.

Toyota initially said about 10,000 models were involved, but later confirmed the number was 8,521 in a letter to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA).

According to Germany’s federal motor authority, Kraftfahrt-Bundesamt (KBA), the recall has now expanded to 19,000 C-HR vehicles globally. In Germany, the recall impacts 1,096 vehicles and is still under investigation by the KBA.

The issue stems from faulty software programming in the EV-ECU. While it’s intended to limit the battery’s charge level, the software can cause it to overcharge under certain conditions.

Toyota said the issue occurs if the battery is already at a high state of charge and the driver continues using the regenerative braking system.

When this happens, warning lights and a sound will alert the driver, and the electric drive system can shut down, causing the vehicle to lose power while driving.

Following an internal investigation, Toyota said in the NHTSA report that the issue was caused by incorrect software that disrupted the charging-limit function, allowing the battery to overcharge while driving.

The Toyota C-HR vehicles included in the recall were produced between June 17, 2025, and July 9, 2026.

In Europe and other overseas markets, Toyota sells the C-HR EV as the C-HR+ to distinguish it from the gas- or hybrid-powered version.

Owners of affected vehicles in North America can take them to a Toyota dealership, where the battery ECU software will be updated free of charge.

Toyota mailed owner notification letters out on October 18, 2026. Owners can contact Toyota’s customer service at 1-800-331-4331. The recall number is 26TA17.

Other than saying the EV-ECU is being reprogrammed, Toyota has not yet announced next steps for owners of affected vehicles outside North America. The KBA reference number is 17116R.
