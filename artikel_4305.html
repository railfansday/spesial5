Certain titles are destined to be blockbusters, from their announcement to the day the launch finally arrives. There are always easy hits, remakes of absolute classics like Ocarina of Time or Resident Evil 4, but there are also new titles, originals and sequels that punch through and give players exactly what they want, making absolute bank for the people behind them.

As GTA 6's launch approaches, everyone wants part of the PR pie, including internet ISPs.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to be one such title, based on word of mouth alone. Off a resounding success in their Q2 fiscal report, Take-Two is estimating Grand Theft Auto 6’s revenue to help it net a whopping 3.5 billion dollars in the near future, a titanic amount of cash that would set Rockstar up for the foreseeable future. That number may sound comedic, but if any game can make it, it’s GTA 6.

Rockstar has been a household name since the PS2 era, absolutely crushing the competition with constant self-improvement within their cinematic, satisfying formula. They’re the perfect popcorn-flick game for casual players, while offering plenty of narrative and gameplay spice for those well-versed in the field. All in all, the complete package, and Take-Two knows it.

After a massive surge in Grand Theft Auto franchise sales and a record number of preorders, Take-Two is sitting pretty, entering the next financial quarter with enough money to pursue whatever gaming endeavor it wants. With Grand Theft Auto as massive as it is, it’s likely Take-Two is looking to keep Rockstar active, working on the much-anticipated multiplayer aspect of GTA 6, as well as potential remakes of older titles.

This amount of cash off of a single title is unprecedented, but games like Grand Theft Auto 6 don’t come around too often. We won’t know the exact profits until we hear the actual budget Grand Theft Auto 6 is working with, something I’m sure Take-Two will be all too eager to share once the game finally launches- anything to run circles around the competition and flaunt their success while doing it.

Their other usual money maker, their various sports franchises, have been in a bit of a downward spiral the last few years, and this is a major ticket to potentially revamp them for a modern audience. With money to burn, Take-Two has full opening touch up anything and everything they want, getting a bit more niche while still using their success as an even bigger selling point. After all, why not buy from the winner?

While the Game Awards are right around the corner, big financial wins mean that Take-Two and Rockstar can both make the absolute most out of every little opening to advertise. With Grand Theft Auto 6 all but a shoo-in for a Game of the Year nomination, I’ve got to wonder how they plan to wow the crowd. They break the bank and everyone’s expectations for Red Dead Redemption 2’s win, after all.

By winning both casual players and hardcore consumers over, Take-Two is in the best spot of maybe any individual studio in the industry, able to secure the positions of their successful employees while prepping them for the next big thing, alongside the mountains of new blood they can afford to bring onto the squad.

The actual development of Grant Theft Auto 6 is a story I can’t wait to hear, and is one Take-Two is surely eager to tell. I almost expect a never-before-seen press tour from the company, allowing eager fans to absorb every inch of GTA 6 both behind and in front of the curtain. A game like Grand Theft Auto 6 only comes once in a generation, and Take-Two is eager to milk it for every single cent.
