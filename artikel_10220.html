Michigan clears Soaring Eagle to swap GAN for White Hat Gaming’s platform

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Michigan's regulator has approved the Saginaw Chippewa Indian Tribe's move of its Soaring Eagle online casino and sportsbook from GAN to White Hat Gaming. The rare platform swap gives White Hat a full platform role in a top-three US market while GAN's owner restructures the business.
