Motorola MA2 wireless Android Auto adapter just vanished days after finally going on sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The long-awaited Motorola MA2 wireless Android Auto adapter is gone just days after it went on sale.

Motorola MA2 is a wireless Android Auto adapter designed with Google. It was announced way back in March 2026 as the sequel to MA1, which itself launched back in 2022. Needless to say, it’s debut has been long-awaited, and that arrived earlier this week.

But, just as quickly as it came, it’s gone.

We noticed today that Motorola MA2 has vanished on Amazon. The listing is still live, but doesn’t appear in search and is completely sold out. You can try your luck, but odds are it’ll be out of stock for a little while to come.

This isn’t a huge surprise. The listing showed a delivery date in mid-late December, implying that stock hasn’t hit Amazon’s warehouses just yet. It’s not particularly uncommon for listings like these to be taken down briefly as stock is on its way.

More than likely, MA2 will return sometime in the next few weeks. Stay tuned, we’ll be keeping a close eye on it!
