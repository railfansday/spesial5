‘Dancing With The Stars’ Season 35 Finale Scores Series’ Best Performance Among Young Adults In 15 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Dancing with the Stars wrapped its 35th season with another ratings win.

The finale scored 9.2 million total live + same-day viewers across ABC and Disney+, according to early Nielsen data, making it the most-watched episode of the season.

It was on par with last year’s finale, when Witney Carson and Robert Irwin took home the mirrorball, in terms of total viewers. However, since this is preliminary data, the final number is expected to go up.

Impressively, the episode, during which Daniella Karagach and Ezra Frech were crowned the champs of Season 35, saw a hefty surge in interest from adults 18-49, rising 11% over the week prior to a 2.38 rating, per Nielsen. That is the best rating in that demo the show has managed in 12 years — since Carson and her partner-turned-host Alfonso Ribeiro won the trophy.

The ratings were driven by an influx of young viewers to the series, as illustrated by the fact that the episode put up a 2.86 rating among adults 18-34. That is its strongest performance in the younger adult demo in 15 years, since the Season 12 finale. Watch on Deadline

The episode over indexed among women 18-34, delivering the top series telecast in that demo in more than a decade with a 4.46 rating.

The Dancing with the Stars Season 35 finale is the top entertainment telecast in the 18-49 demo since ABC’s presentation of the Oscars earlier this year. Nielsen data shows it’s also the highest-rated regularly scheduled entertainment telecast in the key demo in over six years.

For the third consecutive season, Dancing with the Stars takes the cake as the No. 1 entertainment series among adults 18-49.

Tuesday night’s finale was one for the history books as Frech became not only the youngest winner ever on DWTS (eking past Irwin by just a few months) but also the first amputee and the first Paralympian to take the crown.
