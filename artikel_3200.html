Donald Trump Jr.’s Outrage Gifts Netflix’s ‘Hunting Wives’ Some Priceless Streisand Effect PR

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Executive Editor, Legal, Labor & Politics

Christmas came on Thanksgiving for Netflix‘s The Hunting Wives, thanks to a fatuous Donald Trump Jr.

Never slow to put his silver foot in his big mouth, the oldest and most ambitious son of El Presidente got very hot under the digital collar early on Turkey Day over a passionate promo for the soapy and streamy MAGA themed series of two women kissing.

In proclaiming “What is wrong with you??? It’s Thanksgiving” on X on November 26 in reply to Netflix, the no stranger to controversy and speculation Don Jr. looks to have handed the Season 2 launch of the Rebecca Cutter created show the kind of publicity even Ted Sarandos and Greg Peters‘ well-funded team can’t buy. What is wrong with you??? It’s Thanksgiving.

While the First Louche’s outrage may have struck some as reeking of hypocrisy, or at least lacking all self-awareness, the fact is Don Jr’s tweet has been viewed over 7.5 million times since Thanksgiving morning. Add to that, a flood of media coverage from the highbrow to the gutter press and (we see you Fox News) everything in-between and you have yourself a good old fashion water cooler moment. Watch on Deadline

Starring Brittany Snow and Malin Åkerman as Sophie and Margo, a East Coastnewcomer to an East Texas town and the town’s leading lady, The Hunting Wives, which is based on the May Cobb novel of the same name. With guns, booze, short leather skirts, cowboy boots, MAGA, murder and lot of sex, the show follows Sophie as loses more and more of her old Boston life and more intimate with the scheming Margo, who’s Dermot Mulroney portrayed oil tycoon husband is running for Lone Star State Governor.

Hunting Wives scribe Brandon Zuck took out a shiv of his own against Don Jr. on Instagram, as well as delivering a backhanded compliment. “Just wait until season 3,” he wrote on his IG Story next to a screen shot of Junior’s tweet. That echoes what Cutter said when Netflix announced Season 3 last month. “Buckle up, because Season 3 will be even more scandalous and twisted,” the showrunner stated.

Further add to Jr’s ill-considered pearl clutching, the fact the actual online clip of Snow and Åkerman’s characters making out has itself claimed 36 million views on the (MAGA? Not MAGA?) Elon Musk-owned platform. All of which means the Streisand effect of Little Don’s post cannot have been helpful in pushing Hunting Wives Season 2’s eight episode debt to number #3 in the Netflix charts in less than 24 hours.

Likely not wanting to throw cold water on a good firestorm, Netflix did not respond to Deadline’s request for comment on Little Don’s tweet and the backlash it’s received. But, why would they? As WME Ari’s brother, former White House Chief of Staff and budding 2028 candidate Rahm Emanuel once said: “You never want a serious crisis to go to waste.”

The seriousness of it all aside, for a streamer that has been short of late of big breakout hits, Don Jr’s foot stamping may have proved the spark to set the already well-watched Hunting Wives on fire — in the best way.

Or as Netflix posted on their official feed later on Thanksgiving: “It’s messy is what it is!”

BTW – The Streisand effect comes from the superstar taking photographer Kenneth Adelman to court for $50 million in 2003 for a pic her took of her Malibu home. Barely noticed before the lawsuit, the photo became widely viewed in the aftermath — thereby destroying the whole point of the Funny Girl star’s effort. Fact: No matter how famous you are, or who your Daddy is, when you tell people not to pay attention to something you don’t like — they always will.
