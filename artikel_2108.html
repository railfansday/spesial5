Coventry City twist emerges in Nottingham Forest race for Birmingham City's Neumann

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

A fresh twist has emerged in the latest transfer saga concerning the future of Phil Neumann at Championship leaders, Birmingham City.

According to TEAMtalk, the newly-promoted trio of Coventry City, Ipswich Town and Hull City have all identified the German centre-back as a potential January signing, whilst a stance on such developments becoming a reality has firmly been set from within the hierarchy at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park.

Following an intriguing wave of summer transfer activity, Chris Davies' side currently lead the Championship after 15 matches, accumulating 29 points after winning five of their last six games.

Birmingham currently lead the way in the second tier ahead of Middlesbrough and Stoke City by virtue of a superior plus-14 goal difference, largely attributed to their defensive work, with a tally of 14 concessions thus far being the division's finest record.

Given the ambition associated with Blues at this moment in time, attention will certainly be generated when it comes to the club's January transfer plans.

However, they are also hoping to fend off the aforementioned top-flight interest in Neumann, who has been nothing short of a revelation this campaign.

Neumann was one of 14 arrivals in this part of the West Midlands last summer, having signed a pre-contract agreement with Birmingham ahead of the expiration of his Hannover 96 contract.

The 6"3 man endured a mixed first season in the Championship, making 32 league appearances and scoring three goals, although he was the subject of reported January interest from Sassuolo.

Having been an ever-present this term, it emerged earlier on Tuesday that, for a fee between the regions of €5m and €10m, an exit as early as January could be possible for Neumann to one of Nottingham Forest and Fiorentina.

TEAMtalk reference the interest from the Italian giants, but have also claimed that Coventry, Ipswich and Hull have identified the centre-back on their pending January shortlists ahead of what is expected to be a gruelling second half of the season.

Neumann was also linked with the Tigers in August, before the Blues man reaffirmed his desire to help Davies' side end their own lengthy exile from the top-flight.

Neumann's contract at St. Andrew's is currently set to expire in June 2028, and many Birmingham fans have already voiced their opinions as to what fee would be deemed acceptable for the "colossal" defender to depart so soon.

It is, therefore, rather unsurprising that, within the aforementioned report, it has been claimed that the league leaders have very little desire to entertain the thought of losing such a key performer in the upcoming window.

It has been suggested that Blues could be prepared to wait until the summer window to look at moving Neumann on for a substantial profit, as he would enter the final months of said deal and the club's promotion ambitions would have either been realised or not.

Neumann's physicality and strong leadership alongside captain Christoph Klarer, largely in front of James Beadle, has been key to such an impressive start for Birmingham.

The defender's brace against Wrexham in August also backed up his threat in attacking situations, with Davies looking to see his side net more from set-pieces this campaign following the addition of Scott Fry to his backroom team.

However, Davies only has one other senior centre-back option to call upon in the form of Dael Fry, who is yet to have a run of games for the club since his summer switch after leaving Middlesbrough.

Fry's experience cannot be undervalued, but finding adequate cover at the same level as Neumann would prove extremely difficult to do, and if an exit occurred for the German youth international, the Second City side would risk leaving themselves short-changed.
