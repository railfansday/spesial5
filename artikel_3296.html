YouTuber Topper Guild Teams With Toonstar For Animated Series

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

YouTube creator Topper Guild is working with kids animation company Toonstar on an original animated series.

The project, working title, The Guild, will launch on a dedicated YouTube channel and aim to leverage Guild’s 125 million+ followers. Plot details were scarce today, but will blend Guild’s online persona with animated tropes and storylines will be “refined as audiences respond.”

“Animation is a medium I’ve always wanted to work in, but I didn’t think it was possible to maintain that same pace my fans expect, or retain the creative control I have with my live-action content,” he said. “But Toonstar changed that. They’re unlocking animation for the creator economy. They bring incredible development, production and digital distribution capabilities and keep me in the driver’s seat.”

Toonstar’s existing slate includes StEvEn & Parker, which has garnered more than 10 billion lifetime views across five languages and has its own graphic novel series published by Penguin Random House. Watch on Deadline

“Topper is the kind of creator who understands exactly what his generation wants,” said John Attanasio, co-founder and CEO of Toonstar. “Working with us, he can bring his unique storytelling style to animation and expand his reach with a show that feels like him and also has a path to becoming franchise IP.”

WME represented Toonstar and Guild on the deal.
