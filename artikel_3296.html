Percy Jackson's 21-Year-Old Ares Mystery Answered By Star

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Warning: There are spoilers ahead for Percy Jackson and the Olympians season 3, episodes 1 and 2.EXCLUSIVE: A Percy Jackson and the Olympians mystery gets an answer 21 years later.

Rick Riordan's The Lightning Thief, which was published in 2005, establishes that demigod Clarisse La Rue is a daughter of Ares, but the identity of her mortal mother has been a longtime mystery. The character has never been named, and little is known about her other than that she lives in Phoenix, Arizona, and Clarisse found Chris Rodriguez near her mother's house after he escaped the Labyrinth.

In an interview with ScreenRant's Liam Crowley, Dior Goodjohn, who plays Clarisse in the Disney+ series, shared that she has had extensive discussions with the show's on-set acting instructor Andrew McIlroy about what her character's mother is like, ultimately believing that she is similar to Aphrodite. The actor imagines her as being "traditionally, soft and caring and kind and just magical," along with reflecting on what this relationship means for Clarisse and her feelings toward Camp Half-Blood. Check out Goodjohn's comments below:

Andrew [McIlroy] and I have talked about it so much, and you kind of try to put together, who is her mother? What would she look like? All of this and that. And I think she would be someone very similar to Aphrodite, traditionally, soft and caring and kind and just magical. And when you think about what you go through in this, it's like seeing the one thing that she really cares about, which is camp, because it has saved her and kept her safe and given her purpose. And then her mom, it's making the choice of, am I going to back out of this or am I going to go for it? Because if I save camp, I might hurt everybody else.

Goodjohn's answer has to do with her own interpretation of her character, so it is not necessarily canon, but it does align with why Ares would have fallen in love with Clarisse's mother. The Greek god of war loves Aphrodite, who is the goddess of love, despite their innate differences. This would explain why he was drawn to a mortal woman with a personality similar to Aphrodite, even though these details have never been revealed in Riordan's Percy Jackson books.

Based on the source material, Ares and Aphrodite's relationship is one that viewers will get a glimpse of in season 3. Professional wrestler and actor Adam Copeland has been playing Ares since season 1, while Saturday Night Live alum Kate McKinnon is making her series debut as Aphrodite, and all 12 Olympians are confirmed to be in the show for the first time this season.

Clarisse does have a brief appearance at Camp Half-Blood in Percy Jackson season 3, episode 2, where she mentions to Percy (Walker Scobell) that she is heading out on a top-secret mission for Chiron (Glynn Turman). This is a shift from season 2, where she is one of the central characters, but consistent with her not being part of the main quest in the book that season 3 is based on, The Titan's Curse.

Disney+ has not officially renewed the series yet, although Scobell addressed Percy Jackson season 4's fate in a recent interview: "Let's say we don't get another season. I'm just going to binge all of Percy just to watch it. But since, like, I know we're coming back next season, I can't."

If seasons 4 and 5 do happen, per the novels, Clarisse is expected to have a larger role again. Since the show does make some changes from the Percy Jackson books, there is the possibility of Clarisse's mother being introduced, which would prove whether Goodjohn's ideas about the character are canonically accurate, although no plans for this have been confirmed or suggested by anyone who works on the show.

As for the story of the current season, it sees Percy determined to find Annabeth Chase (Leah Sava Jeffries) after she is taken by a Manticore named Dr. Thorn (David Costabile). Her being kidnapped ties into a larger plan formed by the Titan Atlas (Holt McCallany) and the demigod Luke Castellan (Charlie Bushnell), along with the goddess Artemis (Dafne Keen) being taken. Thalia Grace (Tamara Smart), Grover Underwood (Aryan Simhadri), Bianca di Angelo (Olive Abercrombie), Zoë Nightshade (Saara Chaudry), and Phoebe (Sophie Carriere) are chosen by the Oracle to undertake a quest in response.

With a 100% Tomatometer score and 97% Popcornmeter score on Rotten Tomatoes, along with eight out of 10 stars from ScreenRant's El Kuiper, Percy Jackson and the Olympians season 3 has been well-received thus far, and it is Disney+'s number one show on the platform's worldwide streaming charts as of this writing.

Percy Jackson and the Olympians season 3 releases new episodes on Fridays at 3 a.m. ET/12 a.m. PT.
