TikTok adds new brand safety resources

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The company offered brands protection features designed to help identify potential risks, including copyright and counterfeit detection tools.

TikTok launched some new brand safety resources last week, including a new brand safety center website, which will provide easy access to the platform’s advice and guidance tools.

TikTok’s brand safety center includes a range of videos featuring the company’s internal experts discussing key considerations for marketers.

It also includes links to various guides, including its new Brand Protection Playbook and its AI Safety Essentials overview.

TikTok also announced new brand protection tools that will help brands identify potential areas of risk. Those include a brand perception insights tool located in TikTok’s business center, as well as copyright and trademark infringement reporting options and counterfeit protections for sellers.

TikTok announced its updated brand safety resources at its APAC Responsible Media Forum, held in Singapore last week.
