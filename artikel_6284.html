A Wonderfully Relaxing “Afternoon Matinée” In SAS A330 Business Class

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Our SAS A330-300 Business Class flight from Copenhagen to Chicago could not have been better and it really was one of the most relaxing flights I’ve had in a long time, during which I enjoyed great food and watched two excellent movies when I could not sleep.

Unless I’m flying on a plane with Starlink, I assume that the internet is not going to work onboard (especially on SAS). If it does, it is a pleasant surprise. If not, in most cases I will have already completed essential tasks so that I’m not spending my flight dreading unfinished work.

We flew home on Sunday afternoon, also a day off for me, and I decided that I’d just relax and watch some movies during the flight if I could not sleep between meals.

Lunch, by the way, was delicious. I pre-ordered “ASC” salmon with tomato vinaigrette, summer cabbage with brown butter, Jerusalem artichoke soufflé, and chive oil while Heidi pre-ordered beef with osso buco sauce, polenta cake, butter-and-thyme carrot, grilled artichoke and gremolata. We shared. Both dishes were perfect.

The appetizer and dessert were also excellent, as was the coffee:

I find the catering consistently excellent on SAS.

I did try to sleep, but could not, but that turned out to be a blessing in disguise on this flight. First I watched a Japanese movie called A Pale View of Hills, which I found riveting and very well-done. It trace the story of Etsuko and has a beautiful twist that left me profoundly moved. Highly recommended.

Meanwhile, Heidi was watching a German movie called Sound of Falling, a psychological drama spanning a century at a farmhouse in rural Germany. I then watched that while she watched the Japanese move. Wow, also excellent. Riveting and thought-provoking.

We don’t have a TV at home and I do not watch movies or anything at all unless I’m on a plane, but watching movies like this makes me think that I may be missing out…both were so well done and absolutely worth the time. I honestly could not stop thinking about either one for a couple days.

Before a landing, a delicious open-face sandwich was served with eggs and shrimp.

The flight crew was excellent and the kids were again very well-behaved, so I really don’t think it could have been a better flight. SAS does not have a cutting edge business class seat, but between my six hours of sleep on the way to Copenhagen and my restful flight on the way back, I was reminded again how much I love fly SAS Business Class and hope that I can find a way to replenish my EuroBonus account once we spend down the rest of the two million points.

I’m not sure whether I will offer a full review of this flight…I took a lot more pictures, but I’ve reviewed this product recently and little has changed. Bottom line: I do strongly recommend SAS for intercontinental travel.
