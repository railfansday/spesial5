If you are working toward American AAdvantage elite status this qualification year, you may be looking for more ways to earn Loyalty Points to secure your desired status. Here's what you need to know about AAdvantage elite status requirements and five ways to close your Loyalty Point gap before the end of February.

The status qualification year for American AAdvantage elite status runs from March 1 to the end of February. You need the following number of Loyalty Points to qualify for each status tier:

Your Loyalty Points reset to zero each year on March 1. However, once you earn AAdvantage elite status, you'll keep it through March 31 of the year following the end of your qualification year.

Related: American AAdvantage miles vs. Loyalty Points: What's the difference?

Loyalty Points are the sole metric for AAdvantage elite status. As a reminder, you generally earn 1 Loyalty Point for every eligible AAdvantage mile you earn, including miles earned through flights, credit card spending and select partners.

However, some AAdvantage miles — including those earned through credit card welcome offers and certain promotional bonuses — don't earn Loyalty Points.

Here are five straightforward ways to earn more Loyalty Points before the current elite qualification year ends on Feb. 28.

You can earn Loyalty Points on eligible flights with American Airlines and its partners. Flights marketed by American and select partners, including British Airways and Iberia, earn 5 Loyalty Points per dollar spent on eligible fares, plus elite status bonuses. Other partners, including Alaska Airlines, Finnair and Japan Airlines, award Loyalty Points based on fare class and distance flown.

Basic economy tickets usually don't earn Loyalty Points.

If you have the (see rates and fees), you can also earn 5,000 bonus Loyalty Points after every four qualifying American Airlines flight segments, up to three times per qualification year.

Related: How to earn American Airlines AAdvantage miles

You can earn Loyalty Points by shopping through the AAdvantage eShopping portal or using eligible SimplyMiles offers with an enrolled Mastercard. However, Loyalty Points generally won't post until the qualifying transaction is completed and processed, which may be long after you purchase back-ordered items and hotel stays.

Related: Earn points, miles or cash back: How to maximize online shopping portals for your purchases

When you book your stay through AAdvantage Hotels, you can earn up to 15,000 Loyalty Points per reservation once you complete your stay. However, keep in mind that you usually won't get hotel elite status earning or benefits when you book through AAdvantage Hotels.

Related: How I earned 29,000 Loyalty Points and 23,000 AAdvantage miles on a single $800 hotel stay

You can earn Loyalty Points at participating AAdvantage Dining restaurants. Just be sure to register your favorite dining rewards cards in the AAdvantage Dining program — which will unregister them from most other dining rewards programs — and then use an enrolled card to pay at participating restaurants.

Related: How to double-, triple- or even quadruple-dip on dining rewards

You can also earn Loyalty Points when you use AAdvantage credit cards for purchases. Specifically, you'll generally earn 1 Loyalty Point per dollar spent on eligible purchases with an AAdvantage credit card, regardless of whether the card earns bonus miles in certain spending categories. Purchases made through Feb. 28, 2027, will earn Loyalty Points that count toward the status qualification year ending on Feb. 28, 2027, even if your statement closes in March.

The (see rates and fees) also awards 10,000 bonus Loyalty Points at each of four thresholds — 50,000, 90,000, 165,000 and 240,000 Loyalty Points — for up to 40,000 additional Loyalty Points per qualification year. These thresholds include Loyalty Points earned through all eligible methods, not just card spending.

American AAdvantage has yet to announce whether it will change its elite status tiers or qualification requirements for the next qualification year, which will run from March 1, 2027, to Feb. 29, 2028.

If you're short on Loyalty Points for your desired AAdvantage elite status, you still have time to close the gap before Feb. 28, 2027. Whether you fly, shop, dine, book hotels or spend on an AAdvantage credit card, be sure to understand when your activity will count toward status qualification.
