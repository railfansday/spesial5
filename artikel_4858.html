CHRISTMAS IS STILL weeks away, but getting a head start gives you time to find a gift for her that shows you care. Maybe she’s mentioned something she wants, or you’ve noticed what she uses every day. Those are good places to start. We’ve got plenty of ideas to help you fill out your list.

We pulled from our team’s favorite finds and products our Women’s Health colleagues recommend after testing. That means gifts with more going for them than pretty packaging, including beauty products she’ll keep using after the holidays and gear that fits her favorite hobbies. We’ve also included affordable stocking stuffers because a thoughtful gift doesn’t have to eat up your December budget.

Whether you’re shopping for your partner or finding something for your sister, there’s plenty here to work with. Read on for the best Christmas gifts for her in 2026.
