Jamie Carragher delivers damning Chelsea transfer verdict as £75m signing slammed

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Jamie Carragher believes that Chelsea have “absolutely no chance” of winning silverware with their current crop of central defenders.

The Blues suffered a heavy 4-1 home defeat by Leeds United on Saturday as fans began to turn on Xabi Alonso and his players at Stamford Bridge.

It was a result that leaves them eighth in the Premier League table and five points off the top four.

Levi Colwill and Wesley Fofana started in a back four for the west Londoners against their Yorkshire rivals, while summer signing Maxence Lacroix is out injured with a hamstring injury that could keep him out until the turn of the year.

"Chelsea have got a massive problem at centre-back and if they don't change it in January,”Carragher said on Sky Sports.

“They're going to have a big problem in terms of where they feel they should be this season with the Champions League.

"Colwill at Sunderland against [Brian] Brobbey gave the penalty away, and the manager took him off at half time. Fofana's performance against Man United, especially in the first half, was one of the worst you'll see from a centre-back this season.

"As a partnership, this will never work because they're not physical enough and there's no real leader there.

"It almost feels like you've got two novices playing, but one cost £75million, and Colwill has been talked up a lot not just at Chelsea but at international level as well."

The former Liverpool defender continued: "Those two can't play in a back four as a two, but it reminds me of myself at Liverpool.

"We didn't fix it defensively and we were seen as weak, conceding from set pieces and long throws. It wasn't until we signed Sami Hyypia, 6’4”, dominant centre-back, leader, great player, that it changed for us.

"This is not going to change with what they have. Lacroix is a decent player, that's a sticking plaster until the next transfer window in January or the end of the season.
