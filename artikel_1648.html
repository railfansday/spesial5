Arsenal selection boost confirmed ahead of Man City showdown after Mikel Arteta update

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta will have his attacking options bolstered for the visit of Manchester City in the Premier League this weekend.

The reigning Premier League champions welcome an out-of-form Man City side to the Emirates Stadium on Sunday afternoon and will be looking to avoid their winless run stretching to three games.

While Arsenal have lead in both games against Newcastle and Borussia Dortmund in the past week, they have failed to win any of their games since returning from the international break.

Missing from the matchday squad at St James' Park last weekend was Max Dowman as he served the final game of a three-match suspension after being sent-off in the shock Carabao Cup fourth round loss to Fleetwood Town.

The Gunners unsuccessfully appealed for the red card to be overturned, meaning the teenager had to sit out wins over Liverpool and Hull, as well as the 2-2 draw against Newcastle.

Dowman was on the bench against Dortmund but did not come on, though he has now been cleared to feature in domestic matches for Arsenal and is an option as Arteta aims to get his attack firing again.

“When the margin is by one goal, especially with the level of the opponent that we play every three days, how difficult it is; they just need a moment, and it happened with Dortmund,” Arteta said in the pre-match press conference.

“They broke the press after eliminating one of our front players, and they have the capacity to pick out that player, that player has an individual action, and then it’s a goal.

“They didn’t do much more than that, but that was enough to get a point and you have to assume that. But again, overall, if we continue to generate what we’re generating, I’m very positive that things are going to be good.”

In addition to Dowman, David Raya is on course to be available against Man City as Arteta revealed the goalkeeper is back in training after missing the Dortmund game with a shoulder issue.
