Opinion: Why Florian Wirtz could be Liverpool's best player

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Many wrote off Florian Wirtz after his first season with Liverpool, but that would have been a mistake.

The German arrived to plenty of fanfare for a then-British transfer record £115 million in the summer of 2025, but his first season at Anfield was nothing short of disappointing.

Seven goals and 10 assists wasn't a bad return, but for the price Liverpool paid many expected more, and Wirtz's displays often left supporters wanting more.

This season Wirtz has been a different beast however, and Andoni Iraola seems to have figured out exactly how to get the best out of the German.

Wirtz could be Liverpool's best playerSunday's win over Manchester United was arguably Wirtz's best performance in a Liverpool shirt, and the 23-year-old announced to the Premier League that he means business. For the second league game in a row the German scored a goal and provided an assist, taking his total to three goals and six assists in all competitions this season. Liverpool's no.7 also created 10 chances and covered the most distance in the game and has now created more chances than any other player in the Premier League this season.

Wirtz has hit his best form since his first international break under Jurgen Klopp, something that may have kickstarted his turn around at Liverpool.

Klopp can't take all the credit though, Wirtz has clearly worked hard and Iraola's system has allowed the playmaker to flourish.

Many supporters had written of Wirtz, but they are being proven wrong and the maestro is absolutely key to Liverpool's upturn in form.

If Iraola can continue to get the best out of his £115 million man then Liverpool may not be as far off competing on all fronts as they appeared just a few weeks ago.
