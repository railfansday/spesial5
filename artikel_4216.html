All Correct Answers For Vascal During The Broken Silence Quest In Crimson Desert

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

We'll show you how to deduce the culprit by answering Vascal's questions correctly.

Crimson Desert offers dozens of quests to complete and disputes to resolve, including a few investigations that see you taking on the role of expert detective. One such investigation can be found on the far eastern side of the map, and requires you to help find a thief.

The Redfox Trading Post is experiencing a crisis, as a valuable piece of ceramic has mysteriously disappeared, and it’s up to you to help solve the mystery. Put on your hat, Sherlock; it’s time to question the suspects, gather the evidence, and deduce the conclusion as you help Vascal solve the mystery of Redfox Trading Post in the Broken Silence quest.

Updated on November 12, 2026, by Kaitlyn Peterson: This article has been updated and reformatted to help you find the right answers for Vascal and finish this quest as quickly as possible!

To begin the Broken Silence quest chain, you must reach the Redfox Trading Post in the northeastern area of Delesyia, east of Gorthak.

When you arrive at the post, speak to the Trading Post Manager marked on your map, who will give you the Investigative Log Broken Silence book. This will give you information about the suspects of the theft.

Finally, Vascal will put all the evidence together with you and leave you with a vague conclusion. However, you can go to the marker on your map to apprehend the culprit.

As you approach the marker, you'll spot Tarbo making a shady deal with a wagon driver, revealing that he was the culprit. Once the cutscene plays out, tie Tarbo up and take him back to the marked location.

After a short cutscene with Vascal, you'll receive your reward: a Kinetic Circle, which is a piece of artwork. However, if you'd prefer silver instead, it can be sold for 43.70 silver.
