GTA 6 Gives Players 62 Reasons to Take Up Birdwatching

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Most of us already have a plan for our first few hours in GTA 6. Steal a car, see how the police react, explore Vice City, and maybe get around to the story eventually. But Rockstar is also giving us a good reason to leave the car parked and pull out a pair of binoculars instead.

GTA 6 will have 62 different bird species to discover, and you can record them in a wildlife logbook. The detail emerged during The Guardian's visit to Rockstar North, when journalist Keza MacDonald noticed a gull sitting on a post near the beach. Jason pulled out his binoculars, and the bird was added to the logbook.

It's a small interaction, but it suggests there will be plenty to occupy us in Leonida besides robberies and police chases.

GTA VI will be one of the biggest video game releases in our lifetimes, and it will be talked about for years to come.

We already knew Rockstar was putting a lot of effort into Leonida's wildlife. Earlier reports pointed to more than 170 animal species across the state, including over 60 birds, roughly 30 land mammals, and a variety of reptiles and aquatic creatures. The exact count of 62 birds isn't a massive surprise, then. What's more interesting is that we now know you can actively look for them and keep a record of what you've spotted.

It's one thing to see birds flying overhead, but a logbook gives you a reason to stop and identify them instead of driving straight past.

Leonida has plenty of places where this could be fun. We've seen coastal areas, the wetlands of the Grassrivers, and the tropical scenery of the Leonida Keys. Looking for wildlife could be a good excuse to explore places that might otherwise get overlooked in favor of Vice City's streets. Rockstar hasn't said whether certain species are tied to particular habitats, though. A logbook might be all it takes to get players to stop and appreciate the wildlife.

We don't know whether finding all 62 species earns a reward, whether some birds are particularly rare, or whether spotting them depends on the time of day. The game's dynamic weather system raises another question, but there's no confirmation that weather affects wildlife sightings.

Actual birdwatchers are already looking forward to GTA 6 for this exact reason. In a November 15 thread on Reddit's r/birding community, one user asked whether anyone else was excited to catalog the game's birds. They added that their planned crime spree would have to wait.

Some commenters hope there's an achievement for spotting every species. Others brought up Red Dead Redemption 2, which got a few of them interested in birdwatching in real life.

If you've spent much time in Rockstar's western, you can probably relate. You could lose an afternoon studying animals, watching how they behaved, and filling out the compendium instead of doing whatever Arthur Morgan was supposed to be doing. A trip through the woods often turned into something completely different from what you'd originally planned.

In fact, this is one of several reasons why I never got around to finishing Red Dead Redemption 2 despite multiple attempts. I made it a priority to complete every hunting-related achievement and log every single wild animal present in the game.

And considering how much time I spent documenting wildlife in Red Dead Redemption 2, I can only imagine how much more distracting GTA 6 will be. After all, we're moving from the relatively sparse world of 1899 to a densely populated, modern-day Leonida, with far more going on around us. Between all the wildlife, activities, and countless other details Rockstar has packed into the game, I wouldn't be surprised if I ended up neglecting the story all over again.

Vice City's roads, crowds, and nightlife will probably take up most of our time at first. But I can easily imagine heading out to explore, spotting a bird I haven't logged yet, and forgetting what I was supposed to be doing in the first place.

GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The robberies can wait when there's a gull on the beach that needs identifying.
