Targets second KPop Demon Hunters drop turns your PC setup into the ultimate tribute

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

TL;DR: Target’s second KPop Demon Hunters x heyday drop arrived on Nov. 8, bringing mechanical keyboards, wireless headsets, and Switch accessories starting at just $4.99.

Target's first team-up with Netflix’s KPop Demon Hunters was an instant hit when it landed on Oct. 11. The initial drop leaned heavily into quirky, everyday desk pieces, like the viral ramen charging kit, soda-can Bluetooth speakers, and tap-on night lights, all for $20 each. But if you wanted real peripherals for your PC or console, Target's heyday brand isn't done yet.

Arriving in stores and online starting Nov. 8, the second wave focuses directly on PC and console setups. Just like the first drop, the whole idea is to turn the show's characters, emblems, and artwork into things you can play with, show off on a shelf, and collect. The big difference is that this lineup is tailor-made for gamers and fans who want a fun desk upgrade without paying crazy prices.

You get a little bit of everything here, from quick little cosmetic add-ons to full-blown setup staples. While the previous drop kept everything at a flat twenty bucks, this round gives you a wider range — starting at $4.99 for small accessories and maxing out at $49.99 for the keyboard and wireless audio.

Heyday x KPop Demon Hunters Figural Key Caps ($4.99) — Fun little character caps you can pop onto your favorite keyboard keys.

Heyday x KPop Demon Hunters Joycon Thumb Grips ($7.99) — Cute analog stick caps repping both HuntrX and the rival Saja Boys.

Heyday x KPop Demon Hunters Wrist Rests ($7.99 to $12.99) — Comfy foam rests sporting Derpy Cat and Soda Pop designs.

Heyday x KPop Demon Hunters Desk Mats ($9.99 to $19.99) — Standard long mats in Derpy Cat and HuntrX artwork for $9.99, or an edge-lit LED version for $19.99 that adds a soft glow around your setup.

Heyday x KPop Demon Hunters Switch Case ($17.99) — Travel-ready protective cases available in Demon Markings, Derpy Cat, and HuntrX prints.

Heyday x KPop Demon Hunters Ramen Light ($19.99) — A soft glowing desk light celebrating Rumi, Mira, and Zoey's go-to snack.

Heyday x KPop Demon Hunters Wired Headsets ($22.99) — A simple plug-and-play wired option themed after HuntrX and Soda Pop.

Heyday x KPop Demon Hunters Mouse ($29.99) — Made to pair right alongside the mechanical keyboard for a clean, matching look.

Heyday x KPop Demon Hunters Gaming Mechanical Keyboard ($49.99) — The main star of the desk, finished with custom character artwork and clicky keys.

Heyday x KPop Demon Hunters Wireless Headsets ($49.99) — Cord-free gaming audio coming in Derpy Cat and Demon Markings styles.

If the first release was any indication, the most popular character pieces won't sit on shelves for long, so it's smart to grab what you want the day it goes live. Have your cart ready on Nov. 8 to grab your favorite pieces at Target while you still can.
