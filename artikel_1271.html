Report Milan have €70m Endrick offer ready for Real Madrid

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Spanish reports claim Milan are ready to offer €70m in January for Real Madrid and Brazil striker Endrick, with Jose Mourinho the biggest obstacle to a sale.

Rossoneri patron Gerry Cardinale has made no secret of the fact he wants to bring in Endrick, repeatedly praising the youngster in the media.

Cadena SER suggest that the proposal is very much real, with €70m on the table for a January transfer.

Considering the lack of playing time he is getting at Real Madrid, the club is more than open to the potential exit, having invested €47.5m in buying him from Palmeiras in the summer of 2024.

However, Mourinho is adamant it would be a mistake to sell the 20-year-old so soon into his career, fearing he can flourish elsewhere.

We already saw glimpses of his capabilities during the loan experience at Olympique Lyonnais, but the report in Spain suggests Endrick is not prepared to take on another short-term move.

He wants some certainty in his career, and that means Milan could be the favourites.

It could ultimately come down to what Mourinho can promise, and whether Endrick believes it will be enough.
