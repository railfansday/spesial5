Arsenal late show keeps heat on Man City

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

This season’s Premier League title race may be locked in a paralysed state of uncertainty but Arsenal, the defending champions, are determined to keep it interesting.

While the painful delay on Manchester City’s battle against their guilty verdict threatens to drag deep into next year, Arsenal are remaining hot on their tails.

City’s lead at the top is back to three points but Arsenal’s win here was unlikely to have sent any shivers down the spines of Enzo Maresca and his players in this phoney mess of a season.

It was not until the 80th minute that Arsenal finally registered their first shot on target, and that was the moment which ended their cycle of frustration at the City Ground.

Just when it appeared Mikel Arteta was heading for a third successive goalless draw at the home of Nottingham Forest, substitute Noni Madueke’s deflected strike proved the difference.

Trips to the City Ground have rarely been easy for Arsenal, with an FA Cup defeat in January 2022, at a time when Forest were still in the Championship, sparking a famous Arteta rant in the dressing room which was included in the All Or Nothing documentary.

They were far from fluent here and have now failed to score in the first half of their last six Premier League and Champions League matches.

Eberechi Eze endured a particularly difficult afternoon in what was a missed opportunity for the England international, with Madueke replacing him at half-time. Yet Arteta will argue that finding a way to win games when not at their best is a sign of champions.

“We have played 11 competitive matches and won 10 of them, we are in a good place and will improve,” Arteta said. “Since losing at Brighton we have won three games in a row so that is the response that I want.

“The level of the league has gone up again and there will be more and more surprises. We have to go again at Bayern Munich this week [in the Champions League on Wednesday night] and we will have to adapt to that one too.

“I have a smile on my face as Noni is a special guy. He’s in a good moment and after the [international] break he’s shown another level. The team needs him and he’s doing well.”

Arsenal often struggled against teams who operate with a compact midfield and defend deeply last season and this was another case in point.

When Declan Rice wastefully lifted a shot over the crossbar 18 minutes from the end, after a rare opening in the Forest area, it did appear that another draw was inevitable.

Yet Madueke provided the crucial contribution, driving a right-footed shot towards goal which took a heavy deflection off Forest defender Jair Cunha to beat Matz Sels.

Madueke has struggled for consistency and minutes since his £52m move from Chelsea, so this felt like a big moment for the winger.

“If you look at the quality of our starting XI and bench it’s absurd,” Madueke said. “We have elite players of the highest level and it’s great to have that type of competition. You know on the bench that you’re ready to have an impact. We showed grit and determination and showed a champion spirit.”

Forest’s problems at home are increasing and Oliver Glasner is now the club’s fifth manager in a year to experience such problems. His team have now gone almost eight and a half hours without scoring in front of their own supporters. They have only recorded two home wins in the league over the past 11 months which, quite frankly, is relegation form.

They did not deserve to lose this match. Without their injured talisman Morgan Gibbs-White, Forest defended resolutely and in the second half displayed far more attacking impetus without ever truly testing Arsenal goalkeeper David Raya.

Liam Delap, their £50m record signing, produced a fine centre-forward performance and never allowed Arsenal’s centre-backs Ezri Konsa and Gabriel to feel comfortable. Goals, however, continue to elude this team.

Murillo had Forest’s best opportunity two minutes into injury time after cutting inside the penalty area but could not find the top corner.

Glasner said: “I feel really sorry for the players because we didn’t deserve that.

“It feels unfair but I don’t want to be complaining as we are the only ones who can change it.

“It is a tough moment and we need to stick together and stay positive. Sometimes it takes a while – during my first year at Palace we didn’t start well but we had a turnaround.

“We are playing better right now but we also concede how competitive the Premier League is because every mistake you get punished.”

We knew the margins would be really small. They are extremely well coached and organised. The longer the game goes on they gain more belief. We had to be so patient - and clinical when we got the chance. The way they were defending, they were more comfortable. But we have players with different qualities and can adapt to what the game is delivering.

We have another three big chances that should be goals. That is the difference, you have to be clinical. If we keep generating them, they will come.

Let’s enjoy the win, because it was a very tough one. And then, on Wednesday, we have an incredible game against Bayern Munich away [in the Champions League].

When you see their goal it’s deflected otherwise, it’s straight to the gloves of Matz Sels. You have periods in football where you feel everything going against you and then there are periods when things are on your side.

Now it is how we deal with the situation and react as a team and just how we can change this is by staying positive, sticking together and working hard.

And having this coolness, not to worry too much because I can see good performances and not good results. The results will come if the performances stay good.

Noni [Madueke], Jurrien [Timber], Mikel [Merino] was brilliant when he came on and Viktor [Gyokeres] really changed the game - all of them did and that’s what we need. Noni obviously the highlight because he scored the goal and that’s the difference.

Sometimes you can get frustrated with teams who are difficult to break down. First half we didn’t have a shot on target and that’s something we discussed. We had another two big ones in the second half but the only one on target we had was a little deflection and it’s in.

Great reaction [after scoring goal], to have the will to defend a really open and difficult and stressful situation.

We knew it would be a tough game, it always is against Arsenal. They have a good team. But we feel we can change the result, maybe a draw, maybe a win. Now we have to put it in the past and focus on the next match.

We have a good manager and I think we are still getting his ideas. We changed everything in our system this season. It is difficult but after three or four months we keep improving and I think we are close to reaching our best.

If you look at the quality of our starting XI and bench it’s absurd so we know that we’re going to start some and finish some and for me it’s the same mentality every time I just want to create or score.

Amazing feeling [to score] but I feel better about the win.

We have elite players, all 20-something players are elite and of the highest level. Great to have that type of competition. You know on the bench you’re ready to have an impact.

In the ordinary course of a Premier League season, you would say Manchester City have applied pressure on Arsenal by opening up a six-point lead before the champions play at Nottingham Forest this afternoon.

This is not a normal Premier League season though, and we await to discover if the top of the league table is simply a mirage.

Regardless, Mikel Arteta and his Arsenal players have their own motivations to accumulate as many points as possible before January as they seek to retain the title for the first time since the 1930s.

Arsenal have struggled at the City Ground, drawing 0-0 on their last two Premier League visits. They also lost at Forest in May 2023, a result which confirmed Manchester City as champions at Arsenal’s expense, and were knocked out of the FA Cup by Forest in January 2022.

Forest are yet to score a home league goal under Oliver Glasner, another data point suggesting this will be a low-scoring game.

That might not be the full story though, with Forest creating a hatful of chances in their defeat at Crystal Palace last week and Arsenal also playing a more inventive, technical game with the ball so far this season.

Arsenal have yet to score from a non-penalty set-piece, and their percentage of goals from open play is now the highest in the Premier League.

Morgan Gibbs-White is a doubt after sustaining what Glasner described as a “minor muscle injury” at Palace and his involvement in the game will be a last-minute call. With the exception of Christos Tzolis, Piero Hincapie and long-term absentee William Saliba, Arsenal’s squad is in pretty good health. Bukayo Saka, Martin Odegaard, Kai Havertz, Riccardo Calafiori and Ben White could be recalled to Arteta’s starting XI after starting the Champions League win over Lille on the bench.
