All correct Dice locations in Dice Hoarder (Complete Inventory map)

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Dice Hoarder might seem like a casual game about storing different dice in your shop, but it’s deeper than that. When you restore the dice in your shop, you have to follow the pre-determined order.

Returning to your computer is quite inconvenient. Instead, you can use the image I have added below to know the correct locations.

Restoring the dice to their correct locations has two key aspects. First, you have to separate them based on their base materials. There are seven different base materials.

The idea is separate the dice based on their materials first. Then, you have to place them according to their colors. That’s why I have color coded the map image below.

So, how do you use the image and store the dice? When you pick a die, you’ll find the exact color and the base material. Refer to the inventory image above, and you’ll be able to store it in the correct location.
