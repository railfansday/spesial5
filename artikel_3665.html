Olivia Culpo Brings Newborn Son to 1st 49ers Game Weeks After Giving Birth

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Olivia Culpo is making sure husband Christian McCaffrey feels the love from their whole family this NFL season.

Culpo, 34, took to Instagram on Sunday, November 8, to document her experience taking both daughter Colette, 15 months, and newborn son Roman to Levi’s Stadium to watch McCaffrey, 30, and the San Francisco 49ers take on the Las Vegas Raiders.

“Go daddy, go,” Culpo captioned an Instagram Story clip of Colette walking while wearing a jersey that read “Daddy” and had McCaffrey’s No. 23 on the back.

She was also seen tending to Roman in a stroller, captioning the snap, “Roman’s first game.”

Culpo, who wore an oversized 49ers jacket, also shared an image of her and Colette looking across the stadium from inside their suite. In another video, Culpo smiled as she was breast-feeding Roman.

“I’m not a regular mom,” she wrote, “I’m a cool mom.”

Down on the field, Culpo embraced McCaffrey as the NFL player held up their two children. Roman was seen wearing a tiny 49ers jersey with his dad’s name printed across the back.

“A little more love coming along for the game this season,” Culpo wrote over the Instagram video, which she captioned, “We’re back with one more little fan in the stands this season ❤️.”

In another snap, Culpo smiled as she stood beside McCaffrey in the stands and gazed down at Roman. “Niners win!” she wrote.

Culpo and McCaffrey, who tied the knot in 2024, welcomed their daughter in July 2025 and their son in September. Prior to Roman’s arrival, Culpo gushed over watching McCaffrey take on parenthood.

“I always knew he would be a really good dad because he’s a really good person,” Culpo exclusively told Us in January. “He has a really good example because his parents are great.”

McCaffrey’s father-daughter relationship with Colette is what specifically warmed her heart.

“He will be such a good example and protector to her. I know that she’ll always be able to feel like she has someone who loves her more than anything,” Culpo told Us. “I feel like everybody deserves to feel that.”

In Culpo’s eyes, McCaffrey’s bond with Colette is also reminiscent of her own relationship with her father.

“I’m also so close with my dad, and I appreciate what a great father he is for me, and I really do feel like I’m a daddy’s girl,” Culpo explained. “I just feel like that’s what they’re going to be like, too. It’s a really beautiful thing to see so early on. She loves her dad, and little girls really do. You see the way that they light up with their dad.”
