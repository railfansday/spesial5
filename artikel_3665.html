GTA 6 Is A British Success Story, Prime Minister Says, But He Won’t Play It

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Prime Minister of the UK, Andy Burnham, has labeled Rockstar Games a "British success story" amid GTA 6's rave reviews and huge sales milestones.

A spokesperson for his office told The Independent, "Rockstar Games is a British success story made in Edinburgh, and many fans are already saying GTA has been worth the wait."

Rockstar North is based in Edinburgh, Scotland, but GTA 6 was made by thousands of people across the globe. GTA is also historically linked to the UK, as the original game, 1997's Grand Theft Auto, was made by DMA Design in Edinburgh.

Asked if the PM himself would play GTA 6, his office said Burnham is too busy right now. "He won't be playing it," the spokesperson said.

GTA 6 launched on November 19 and reportedly sold nearly 25 million copies and earned $2 billion in revenue by launch day. The game boasts a mightily impressive 99 on GameSpot sister site Metacritic, meaning it's tied with the original Ocarina of Times for the highest aggregate review score for any game ever.

GTA 6 is a strictly single-player game, but many are expecting Rockstar to eventually announce an online mode for the game. According to reports, the new version of GTA Online for GTA 6 could arrive in 2027, with a PC edition also coming that year.
