Report: Liverpool learn asking price for Jurgen Klopp favourite

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

There has been a focus on youth recruitment at Liverpool over the past few years.

Over £650 million has been spent on adding players to the first team squad since winning the Premier League in 2025, but several truly elite prospects have been added to the academy in that time too.

The Reds' recruitment strategy stretches back beyond their current rebuild though, with players like Rio Ngumoha, Trey Nyoni and Amara Nallo all being signed as teenagers into the Kirkby academy.

Julian Ward and Liverpool's recruitment team are looking to bring another outstanding teenager to L4, and they may have just learned an asking price.

A winter move seems unlikely for the forward, who now finds himself a regular at Borussia Monchengladbach, but clubs are queuing up nonetheless.

The German side insist that a mid-season sale is off the table, but the club could be persuaded to change their stance in the summer.

That's according to an update from Ekrem Konur, which claims a fee of around €25-30 million (~£21-25m) could be enough to secure Mohya’s signature in the summer, with Bayer Leverkusen also interested.

Several reports have also linked Brighton to the German wonderkid, but Liverpool's ability to offer a clear pathway to first-team football could give them a cutting edge in negotiations with the teenager.

Klopp's clear admiration for the youngster could also help sway Liverpool into the lead in the race for his signature, and competition is expected to heat up as the forward continues to develop.

At just 17, Mohya is already a versatile asset comfortable playing in several positions, and Ward will no doubt be watching on closely as the situation continues to develop.
