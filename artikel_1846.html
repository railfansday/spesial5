Bruno Guimaraes breaks silence on Newcastle boos and Arsenal ‘frustration’

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Bruno Guimaraes has reacted for the first time since receiving a hostile reception on his return to Newcastle following his £75m move to Arsenal.

The Brazilian felt Newcastle in the summer, ending a four-and-a-half-year spell which saw him named captain and win the Carabao Cup.

The midfielder’s exit was somewhat acrimonious, so Saturday evening’s return to St James’ Park was greeted to boos every time he touched the ball, in what proved to be an underwhelming performance as the Gunners dropped points in a 2-2 draw.

Bruno Guimaraes is known to be an emotional character, and the reception appeared visibly affect him and was substituted by Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta in the 70th minute, being replaced by Myles Lewis-Skelly.

Guimaraes later took to social media to reflect on an emotional evening and his frustration at Arsenal's failure to secure all three points.

"A different experience for me today, returning to a place where I’ve shared so many happy memories over the past five years," he posted.

"A game of two very different halves, and the frustration of not being able to take all three points at a place where it’s always tough to play.

"There's still a lot ahead of us. We keep moving forward together, always!"

Reflecting on the performance, which Standard Sport rated as the worst from an Arsenal player, Arteta said: “We spoke about that before the game because it is emotional when you have so much going on, especially in the way that he feels connected to this club, being the captain and everything that they did together.

“But I think he was very composed. He was very, very, very focused on what he had to do in the game. I think for the time that he was on the pitch, he had a very good performance.”
