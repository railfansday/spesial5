‘No values, no spirit’ – Xavi blasts mentality of Netherlands squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Xavi attacked the mentality of his Netherlands squad after they fell to a humbling 5-0 loss against Germany in the Nations League on Monday.

Netherlands only needed a point to advance to the quarter-finals of the Nations League but suffered a heavy loss at the Olympiastadion that saw Germany win Group B.

Derry Scherhant's fine strike put Germany in front four minutes before half-time, and they added four goals in the space of 17 minutes during a frantic second-half period – Nico Schlotterbeck and Brajan Gruda scoring either side of Florian Wirtz's second goal.

The result was a bitter pill for Xavi to swallow having seen his team defeat Greece 7-0 on Friday, and he blasted his players for their performance.

"First of all, we have to say sorry for the supporters because we were not playing football – no values, no spirit, no anything," Xavi told NOS.

"As a coach, I cannot understand because we played an incredible game against Greece, with the spirit, values, intensity and passion. This was completely the opposite.

"Maybe because I'm new here, and I'm still trying to understand the spirit of the players – maybe it's a lack of mentality as well.

"I was talking to them, they talked as well, so I think it's good that we have had the communication and now we know the scenario.

"We have the capacity to do amazing things, but we did the complete opposite so it is a pity. I'm really disappointed, really sad because until [this match] I was really proud of the team, of the players – each one of them, but it was a shock for me as a coach.

"We're going to build something starting from now. I talked to the players honestly that we need to change the mentality and the values they have on the pitch even if we win, draw or lose. But it was impossible to complete [when] playing without values."

Xavi did not feel the absences of star duo Virgil van Dijk and Frenkie de Jong played a role in the result.

The Netherlands boss added: "It was 99% because of the values.

"It's embarrassing. We had the chances. We created things. We suffered because they played so well with the values that we wanted and that we had in the first five games.

"I'm trying to understand what happened, but it's difficult because three days ago we did an incredible game in terms of values and football-wise. But here the values were zero."
