Thomas Tuchel tells Cole Palmer he 'misses too many opportunities' to play for England after leaving Chelsea star out of his latest squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

By JAMES DUTTON, DEPUTY SPORTS NEWS EDITOR and KIERAN GILL, MAIL SPORT REPORTER

Published: 06:46 EST, 6 November 2026 | Updated: 09:39 EST, 6 November 2026

Thomas Tuchel has warned Cole Palmer he 'misses too many opportunities' to play for England, after leaving him out of his latest squad.

The Chelsea star is a high-profile omission ahead of upcoming Nations League games against Croatia and Spain, having withdrawn from duty during the last international break with a minor muscular injury.

England were assured Palmer is 100 per cent fit and ready when they contacted Chelsea this week – with his omission purely down to a selection decision from Tuchel and not to do with any fitness issues.

The German said on Friday: 'I guess everyone is desperate to play for England and Cole is no exception.

'We are speaking now about a player that is not in camp. It counts when you are in camp and I just said last time that he misses too many opportunities to impress.

'It was a long camp, it was two and a half weeks and the players and the boys who were in camp built something extraordinary.

'That's why the credit goes to them, the trust is for them. If you are in camp, this is your chance to impress.

'So it's not personal and of course we will never assume that Cole is not in the mindset to come and desperate to play for his country, but the competition is on and the players who were in camp did an amazing job and kept their place.'

Last month Tuchel appeared to question Palmer's desire to represent his country, warning he 'misses way too many camps' as the 24-year-old skipped all four England fixtures in September and October.

Palmer had played a full 90 for Chelsea away at Bournemouth before that break, which he then used to fly abroad to the Caribbean with his girlfriend, Olivia Holder, after withdrawing from England duty.

He was accompanied by a personal rehab coach on that trip, and was provided with an injury-specific training and recovery plan by his club.

Palmer started in Chelsea's first fixture when the Premier League returned at home to Brentford with Blues boss Xabi Alonso insisting they were right to take care of their star player themselves.

It has been described as standard procedure for England to make medical checks on players with clubs. While it does not guarantee a call-up, it indicates how Tuchel has been weighing up offering Palmer another chance as he names his next squad on Friday morning.

Chelsea boss Xabi Alonso (right) said he had not spoken with Palmer about his England omission

Asked if he had spoken with Palmer about his England omission, Alonso said: 'We have not spoken about that. We have been talking about our game on Sunday. We are very happy with Cole.

'He's enjoying his game, he's connecting very well with the other attackers, he's helping the team. I'm very happy with his quality, his attitude, and his impact.

'We have not talked about that (England). We are focused on Chelsea things, how to do better, how to help him, how he can become more and more influential in the game.

'We speak about Chelsea. We speak about us. We have enough on our plate. My impact area is my control area, and that's what we talk about.

'He needs to focus on his thing. His thing is to play football, to enjoy his game, with us, with Chelsea, to connect with his team-mates.

'I would never tell (a player to withdraw). When a player is fully fit, he is selectable. When he is not fit, we try to give the national team the reasonable information to decide together, because sometimes it's not good for the player.

'For example, Anselmino, he has been called up for Argentina, they asked us, he has been training for the last 10 days better, and for him, it's very important to have his first call-up.'
