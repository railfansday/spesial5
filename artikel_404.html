Starbucks just unveiled a limited-edition FriendsWithYou merch collection — how to buy

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Starbucks has been incredibly busy lately. Between launching an incredibly cute Winnie the Pooh collection and all their holiday drops going live, they have somehow found the time to launch an ultra-colorful artist collaboration.

Starbucks is teaming up with Los Angeles-based art duo FriendsWithYou (Samuel Borkson and Arturo Sandoval III) on a limited-edition, six-piece drinkware collection arriving at select U.S. and Canadian locations from Nov. 24, while supplies last. Built around the duo’s signature Little Cloud Boy character and his new barista sidekick, Fluffy, the lineup spans ceramic mugs, insulated tumblers, and charm-adorned bottles.

There's a lot to like about this latest collection:

FriendsWithYou Ceramic Mug 16 oz — White and teal ceramic mug featuring a 3D Little Cloud Boy figurine perched on the handle ($24.95).

FriendsWithYou Cold Cup 24 oz — Plastic iced venti cup topped with Fluffy and Little Cloud Boy straw toppers ($29.95).

FriendsWithYou Glass Bottle 24 oz — Glass water bottle wrapped in a protective blue silicone sleeve with dangling FriendsWithYou character charms ($29.95).

FriendsWithYou Demi Mug Set 3 oz pair — Set of two coordinating blue and green ceramic espresso mugs featuring Little Cloud Boy and Fluffy ($29.95).

FriendsWithYou Stainless Steel Tumbler 13 oz — Insulated stainless steel tumbler crowned with a bright yellow star lid ($32.95).

FriendsWithYou Stainless Steel Bottle 14 oz — Rainbow-motif insulated stainless steel bottle with a rainbow character topper and blue carrying strap ($32.95).

The limited-edition Starbucks x FriendsWithYou collection arrives exclusively in participating U.S. and Canadian coffeehouses starting Nov. 24, while supplies last. Because Starbucks does not sell seasonal artist collaborations online or via mobile order-ahead in the app, your only move is to visit a standalone or licensed store in person.

Just keep in mind that the 14-ounce rainbow stainless steel bottle is a U.S. exclusive, meaning Canadian shoppers will only find five of the six pieces on shelves. And if your local store sells out by the afternoon of launch day, skip the inevitable secondary-market markups on eBay and check out the live Black Friday deals on Owala and Yeti Rambler tumblers instead.
