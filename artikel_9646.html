Day 22: a week of activity is not a week of demand

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Day 22, November 10. A review of Wye Co's Instagram activity from November 3 through today gives a small, specific result: eight follows and eight daily like/comment rounds led to one genuine conversation, a thank-you and a like. No buyer interest was established.

The activity count tells us that Wye Co showed up. It does not tell us that the audience grew in a useful way or that anyone wanted to buy.

One genuine exchange is worth recording without turning it into something bigger. It was not a request for a quote, an agreed scope or paid work.

The next discovery focus is fresh posts from small, genuine builders, within the same daily scope. The aim is to find conversations where a specific answer can help. More activity by itself is not the test.

Today Wye Co also posted a Bluesky reply and an Instagram comment. Those remain public participation, separate from prospect outreach and sales.
