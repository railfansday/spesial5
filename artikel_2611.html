Dylan Harper injury update: Guard goes down late in Spurs' loss to Pistons

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Not only did the San Antonio Spurs have their 12-game winning streak snapped, the loss may have come with a significant cost, too.

Second-year guard Dylan Harper, a rising star in the NBA, suffered an injury late in San Antonio's 105-93 loss on Tuesday, Nov. 17 against the Detroit Pistons.

The play happened with a little more than a minute left, with the Spurs trying to overcome a seven-point deficit. As San Antonio was heading down the court, guard Stephon Castle looked to pass the ball to Harper, who had slipped when he planted his right foot, perhaps because of a slick spot on the court. Harper's legs then splayed awkwardly as he went to the floor. The pass resulted in a turnover, and Harper remained on the floor, clutching his groin area.

He was in obvious discomfort and was eventually helped up off the floor, before limping toward the locker room.

Given that there was not much time left, the Spurs did not issue any update on his injury during the game.

After the loss, head coach Mitch Johnson addressed the incident in his postgame press conference.

"Looked like he slipped, obviously, so hoping for the best," he said, per ClutchPoints reporter Hector Ledesma, "but don't know anything yet."

Victor Wembanyama, who led the Spurs with 25 points and 18 rebounds, also commented on his teammate's injury.

"I know as much as you guys, so I hope he's fine. But, yeah, I just hate this," he said, according to Sports Illustrated's Matt Guzman.

Harper, 20, had been in the middle of a breakout season, his first as a starter. He entered the night averaging 18.3 points, 5.5 rebounds and 4.7 assists per game.

Tuesday night against the Pistons, he scored 20 points on 8-of-14 shooting and added 4 rebounds and 2 assists.
