Report: Mike McCarthy "has the full support" of the Steelers organization

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Aaron Rodgers took a full-windup parting shot at Mike McCarthy, in an apparent effort to take him out of his job.

Via ESPNFL, McCarthy “has the full support of the organization,” and "[t]hey are standing by him and not planning to make any immediate changes.”

The use of the term “immediate” is curious. Is this just a matter of getting through the season before taking a closer look at the situation?

Regardless, Rodgers dropped a fairly sizable turd into the confluence of the Ohio, Allegheny, and Monongahela Rivers. If his claims about McCarthy are true, McCarthy is objectively unfit to coach the team.

At this point, there’s not much that can be done. They play a game on Friday afternoon. Besides, they’re 6-4 and fighting for a spot in the playoffs.

While the situation is far from ideal, the Steelers are opting to stay the course for now. Likely because it’s too late to change direction.

The real question is whether, after the season, McCarthy will be given a chance to step down. And, if not, whether he’ll be fired.

Barring a postseason berth and the first playoff win since the 2016 season, it’s hard to envision the situation being sustainable. Which could result in a team that had three coaches from 1969 through 2025 having two in two years.
