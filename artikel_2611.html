Steelers release veteran wide receiver

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Pittsburgh Steelers have released wide receiver Isaiah Hodgins from their practice squad, the team announced today.

Pittsburgh signed Hodgins on September 22nd, but he was never elevated to the active roster. He spent time with the Steelers in 2025, as well, before being claimed by the New York Giants. He had 10 catches for 115 yards and a touchdown in seven games in New York.

The Steelers seem to have a pair of wide receivers getting close to return to the lineup, as Michael Pittman Jr and Roman Wilson have had their 21-day windows opened. Pittsburgh will travel to Philadelphia to face the Eagles in Week 11.
