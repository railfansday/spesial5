Nets vs. Timberwolves predictions: Odds, recent stats, trends and best bets for November 23

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Julius Randle returns to Minnesota tonight for the first time since being traded in the offseason as the Brooklyn Nets (3-12) head to Target Center to take on the Timberwolves (12-5). The Nets are looking to snap a four-game losing streak against a Minnesota Timberwolves (12-5) team that has been without Anthony Edwards since their fourth game and yet has established itself as a contender in the Western Conference. The Timberwolves' strength has been their defense. Minnesota is allowing just 105.2 points per game. To put that number in perspective, Brooklyn is giving up 118.8 points per game.

Brooklyn nearly ended its skid Friday but came up just short in a 113-111 loss to the Orlando Magic. The performance resulted in a loss but as a consolation prize, at least it was close after losses of 30 points to Chicago and 12 to Philadelphia in Brooklyn's previous two games. Randle came away encouraged by the Nets' effort, pointing to their improved connectivity on both ends while acknowledging that small possessions and mistakes ultimately decided the game.

Minnesota comes in off a 104-95 victory over the Memphis Grizzlies on Saturday, handing Memphis its first road loss after the Grizzlies had opened 8-0 away from home. Jaden McDaniels poured in a season-high 25 points on 11-of-17 shooting, continuing to give the Wolves an important two-way presence alongside Rudy Gobert. Minnesota has had to spread the offensive workload around without Edwards, with Ayo Dosunmu averaging a team-best 17.9 points among the healthy regulars. The key, though, has been their defense which is anchored by Rudy Gobert.

The teams last met on December 27, 2025, when Brooklyn pulled off a 123-107 victory in Minnesota, avenging a 125-109 Timberwolves win earlier that season. Monday's matchup has an entirely different centerpiece, though, with Randle making his first trip back to Target Center since Minnesota dealt him to Brooklyn after two seasons in which he averaged 20.0 points and 6.9 rebounds.
