Bears DB will miss Thanksgiving clash vs. Lions with concussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Bears DB will miss Thanksgiving clash vs. Lions with concussion appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The Chicago Bears are gearing up for a key matchup on Thanksgiving Day against NFC North rivals Detroit Lions, but they have already ruled out one defensive back for that contest.

"Per Bears head coach Ben Johnson, S Elijah Hicks has been placed in the concussion protocol. He's out for Thursday," Kevin Fishbain of The Athletic shared via a social media post on X, formerly Twitter.

Hicks saw action in the Bears' 21-13 win at Soldier Field in Chicago on Sunday against the visiting New Orleans Saints, recording just a tackle. The former California Golden Bears defensive back has been a depth guy on defense for most of the 2026 campaign, appearing in only 12 percent of defensive snaps, while seeing 78 percent of special teams snaps. In 10 games played so far in the 2026 NFL campaign, the 27-year-old Hicks has recorded a total of 12 combined tackles, along with a quarterback hit.

The Bears have also recently signed defensive back Marlen Sewell to their practice squad, perhaps as a contingency move, with Hicks already ruled out and Kyler Gordon appearing on the injury report with a shoulder issue.

These developments on Hicks and Gordon are not ideal for the Bears heading into a showdown against a Detroit team that is powered by a high-flying attack. Through 11 weeks of football, the Lions are top five in the league in both scoring offense and total offense with averages of 28.8 points per game and 391.8 total yards per outing.

Conversely, the Bears' defense is sixth in the NFL with 19.7 points allowed per contest and eighth with 310.5 total yards surrendered per week.
