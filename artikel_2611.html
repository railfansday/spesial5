Could punch machine have role in Tyson Fury vs. Anthony Joshua?

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

A start time of 1:30 a.m. might not be the strangest thing about the fight between Tyson Fury and Anthony Joshua scheduled for Dec. 11 in Cardiff, Wales.

If it's up to Eddie Hearn, Joshua's promoter, which boxer walks into the ring first will be determined in a novel fashion: by which fighter can hit a punching machine harder.

Hearn said Saudi Arabian entertainment company Sela – a key player in the fight -- is on board.

"We agreed with Sela, and this was AJ's request," Hearn told reporters Friday Nov. 27 according to ESPN, "And Sela thought it would be great for TV. I believe Netflix loved the idea as well, that there would be, on fight week, a punch machine.

"And the person that scored the highest punch on the punch machine would walk second." (The "A-side" fighter traditionally walks second. But there is no clear "A-side" fighter between Fury and Joshua, who are both popular former champions who no longer hold titles.)

One snag: Fury doesn't like the idea and wants the order of the walkout determined by a coin toss, according to Hearn.

Joshua's punching power is immense. Almost a year ago, Joshua broke Jake Paul's jaw in two places during a sixth-round knockout blow.

Regardless, there is more logic to the unusual time for the bout than to the use of a punch machine. The scheduled start of 1:30 a.m. in Wales will be 8:30 p.m. ET.
