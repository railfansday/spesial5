TP-Link’s Tapo smart home brand announced a new indoor security camera that can help keep an eye on your house while you’re away or connect you to family and friends while you’re at home. The C825 upgrades a 2K pan-and-tilt camera with a 4-inch touchscreen, so when it’s not watching for intruders or misbehaving pets, it can be used to make hands-free video calls.

The Tapo C825 is available starting today for $149.99. That’s a little expensive for a 2K camera, but its tracking capabilities could make up for it not being 4K. Using a rotating base, the C825 can pan 360 degrees while its camera module on top can tilt up and down up to 156 degrees. When paired with the camera’s 114.5-degree field of view, every corner of a room should be covered, including in the middle of the night using its infrared night vision capabilities.

When used as a security camera, the C825 leverages AI to differentiate between subjects, including pets and different people, through facial recognition. It can also recognize sounds and send alerts when a crying baby or barking dog is heard. The detection processing happens on-device, and videos of events are stored locally on a microSD card up to 512GB in size. No subscription is required, but you can upgrade to Tapo Care for a monthly fee, which adds cloud storage and features like video summaries and weekly reports.

While the C825’s two-way audio communication isn’t unique, expanding that to include two-way video is. Calls can be made to the C825 using the Tapo mobile app, but two of them can call each other directly by tapping a preconfigured onscreen contact. The device feels like another way for aging parents and grandparents to keep in touch with family without having to familiarize themselves with apps, phones, or tablets, but it’s also a useful hands-free video calling solution that can track and keep people in frame as they move around a room.
