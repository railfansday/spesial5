Plenty of folks make larger financial mistakes with home buying. We have bought homes with and without brokers and sold homes with and without brokers - for someone that has little experience with a home purchase making the key metric a 'discount broker' is likely a poor primary concern.
Given the very small market share held by discount buy-side brokers, most of the cohort who made a mistake bought with a full-commission realtor. That's not exactly an endorsement for full-commission buy-side realtors.

There's a very good probability that a full-commission buy-side realtor would simply not be worth the commission. Based on my experiences (self and with family, 10+ interactions), only about a quarter are actually actually worth it. The OP could sign up for a full-commission realtor, but likelihood that higher payment results in a better service isn't high.
