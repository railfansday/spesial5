Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Moving everyday banking away from Fidelity for security. Vanguard Cash Plus or another bank?

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

I am retired and I keep a few months expenses at a local regional bank which has lots of branches in my area where I have a no fee checking account and an ATM card which is not a debit card.

Maybe once a year I will have something where it is very convenient to be able to go into a branch to deal with something in person especially if it involves cash.

One huge thing for me is that I have some old paper i-Bonds which will mature in a few years and they will still redeem paper i-bonds. This will allow me to avoid dealing with Treasury Direct which can be difficult. Apparently at some banks you need to have had your account opened for a year to deposit a significant amount of i-bonds.

I do all my banking there which I keep seperate from my investment accounts because if it is compromised there is a lot less money there.
