Ahmed Juraybi reigned supreme in Romania, topping a packed Open category in Bucharest, and qualifying for the 2027 Mr. Olympia final in Las Vegas. Here are your winners from a busy weekend in Bucharest.

The 2026 IFBB Pro Romania Muscle Fest Pro and expo took place November 7-8 and was presented by Wings of Strength. Winning his first title since turning pro, the Saudi Arabia-born Juraybi  proved that he is now a force to be reckoned with on the world stage, as he bested the packed 15-man Open category to stamp his ticket to Sin City. Juraybi, who also works as a dentist, was understandably elated to be Olympia bound.

“No chance to beat me,” said Juraybi of his participation at next year’s Olympia In fact, he is the first bodybuilder from Saudi Arabia ever to qualify for a shot at becoming Mr. Olympia, so he has already made his way into the history books. The up-and-coming star impressed the judges with a combination of dense muscle and superior conditioning to beat the rest in  Bucharest.

In addition to Ahmed Juraybi, Anastasia Korableva was among the athletes who qualified for next year’s Olympia, after her massive size helped secure a third straight Romania Pro win. Also qualifying at the show were Roman Iushchenko, Beatriz Almeida, Julia Cabarcos, Ana Alexandra Petreanu, and Sidy Pouye. While Spanish Open bodybuilder, Pablo Llopis picked up the award for best poser.
