Jim Cummings Wakes Up to a Post-One-Night-Stand Crisis in ‘DISC’ — Watch the Short

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It’s rare for a short film to be named one of IndieWire’s favorite movies of the fall festival season. But last year, we couldn’t resist adding “DISC” to our roundup of must-see films. Directed by rising filmmaker Blake Winston Rice (who previously made “Tea” with Michael Gandolfini, another short that we championed) and co-written by Victoria Ratermanis, the film premiered at the 2025 Toronto International Film Festival before screening at more than 50 festivals worldwide. Now, just in time for the Best Live Action Short race, IndieWire can debut the full film as this month’s Vimeo Staff Pick.

“DISC” stars Ratermanis and indie filmmaker Jim Cummings as two conference attendees whose drunken hookup turns them into unexpected partners in crime when an inexplicable twist of fate turns their world upside down the following morning. We won’t spoil the unique premise, but an official synopsis reads: “After a one-night stand during a work conference, a man and woman wake to an unexpected crisis. Following its TIFF premiere, “DISC” went on to play at the 2026 Tribeca Festival, where it reportedly caused audiences to faint in multiple screenings. Other festival bows included the Clermont-Ferrand International Short Film Festival, Woodstock Film Festival, Indy Shorts, and the Palm Springs International ShortsFest.

Cummings is best known for writing, directing, and starring in the 2018 SXSW breakout “Thunder Road,” which he followed with the features “The Wolf of Snow Hollow” and “The Beta Test.” He also continues to act in projects for other filmmakers, including recent roles in “The Last Stop in Yuma County” and “Redux Redux.” He can also be seen in theaters later this month as Oliver Babcock in “The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth.”

Rice’s other short film credits include “Sawdust,” “Steps,” and “The Recovery Call.” He is currently developing his first feature, “The Empty Lot.”
