Percy Jackson Star Reveals Encouraging Season 4 News Amid Cancellation Fears

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Percy himself seems to have confirmed the news fans have been waiting for

The third season of Percy Jackson & the Olympians, Disney's adaptation of the Rick Riordan novels, has finally premiered, and it may just be the show's best. Yet the months leading up to the premiere have been overshadowed by growing anxiety among the fanbase that this season could be its last, as there has still been no official confirmation that the show is being renewed​​​​​​​. While we don't have official news, Percy Jackson himself may have just let slip that fans can breathe a sigh of relief.

In an interview on the Empire State Building's social media pages (the iconic New York City skyscraper plays a major role in the series), Walker Scobell (Percy) seemingly confirmed that season 4 is happening. About halfway through the video, Scobell says that he can't rewatch old seasons of the show "since, like, I know we're coming back next season." The brief comment comes as part of an answer about not wanting to be in the MCU because it would ruin the experience of being a fan of the movies. It's an almost blink-and-you'll-miss-it moment, but it is the most concrete indication we've had about Percy Jackson season 4​​​​​​​.

Despite Scobell's tease, Disney has yet to officially announce a renewal​​​​​​​. Rick Riordan has even weighed in on the cancellation rumors, saying that he suspects season 4 will "come down to viewer numbers for season 3." The current season isn't set to conclude until Dec. 11, so Disney likely won't have the information it needs to make such a decision for a while.​​​​​​​ Disney is currently developing an adaptation of Riordan's Kane Chronicles as well, the author says that news has "no bearing one way or another on the future of Percy Jackson."

The current season adapts The Titan's Curse, a novel that marks a major pivot for the series.​​​​​​​ As the third of five books​​​​​​​, it marks the beginning of the end and sets up much of the show's finale. It is also a much darker story than anything Percy has faced in the first two seasons​​​​​​​, showing that the series can mature over time​​​​​​​.

With the end in sight, fans are especially worried about the show's cancellation. Percy Jackson fans have already gone through one failed attempt to adapt the entire series. Fox previously released two Percy Jackson films, in 2010 and 2013, that adapted the same two novels as the Disney Plus show's first two seasons.​​​​​​​ Fans hoping for the series to get an official renewal from Disney are encouraging each other to stream each new episode of season 3 on Disney Plus​​​​​​​ to boost those numbers.
