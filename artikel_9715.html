GITEX GLOBAL 2026 Puts AI Security, Cyber Resilience and Post-Quantum Readiness in Focus

As artificial intelligence moves from experimentation into critical business and government infrastructure, cybersecurity is facing a new set of challenges. Autonomous AI agents, increasingly interconnected systems, and growing concerns around data sovereignty are changing how organizations approach digital security. These developments will take center stage at GITEX GLOBAL 2026, taking place December 7–11 in Dubai, UAE. With a dedicated cybersecurity showcase, GITEX is bringing together global cybersecurity companies, government institutions, technology leaders, and industry experts to explore the next generation of digital defense.

One of the key cybersecurity highlights of this year's event is GITEX Cyber Valley, which will focus on AI security, dual-use cyber technologies, and post-quantum readiness.

As organizations increasingly deploy AI agents capable of making decisions and executing tasks, questions around accountability, access control, and operational security are becoming more urgent.

The challenge is no longer limited to protecting networks and endpoints. Organizations must also secure the data, infrastructure, and autonomous systems that increasingly power their operations.

GITEX Cyber Valley will bring these conversations together through tailored business meetings and private briefings for global cybersecurity leaders, creating opportunities to examine how organizations can scale AI capabilities while protecting the systems and information they depend on.

GITEX GLOBAL 2026 will feature an extensive lineup of cybersecurity companies showcasing technologies and solutions across the security ecosystem.

Confirmed cybersecurity exhibitors include Acronis, AMIVIZ, Cohesity, Commvault, Forcepoint, Forescout, Kaspersky, ManageEngine, NETSCOUT, Netskope, NinjaOne, Proofpoint, SentinelOne / Sentinel Labs, and Trellix.

Their participation reflects the breadth of the cybersecurity landscape, spanning data protection, endpoint security, network defense, threat detection, and enterprise resilience.

The UAE Cybersecurity Council will also participate, alongside other government institutions and public-sector entities, highlighting the importance of collaboration between national cybersecurity strategies and private-sector innovation.

The growing adoption of AI is creating new opportunities for businesses, but it is also expanding the attack surface.

AI-powered applications, autonomous agents, cloud infrastructure, and connected systems introduce new security considerations. Organizations must account for how AI systems access information, interact with other applications, and operate within critical business environments. At the same time, the emergence of quantum computing is prompting organizations to consider the long-term resilience of their encryption and data protection strategies.

These developments make AI security and post-quantum readiness increasingly important areas of discussion for cybersecurity professionals, technology providers, and government decision-makers.

GITEX GLOBAL's wider focus on sovereign AI, frontier models, agentic AI, and sovereign security provides a broader context for these discussions, connecting cybersecurity with the infrastructure and technologies shaping the next phase of digital transformation.

Beyond its cybersecurity programme, GITEX GLOBAL 2026 will bring together more than 6,000 global technology enterprises, government entities, and startups, alongside 1000+ investors representing nearly US$2 trillion in assets under management.

The 2026 edition also introduces a new venue and format. The GITEX Summit will take place on December 7 at Dubai World Trade Centre, followed by four days of exhibitions and conferences from December 8–11 at Dubai Exhibition Centre, Expo City Dubai. Expand North Star, the co-located startup and investor showcase, will run at the same venue.

For cybersecurity professionals, enterprise technology buyers, government representatives, and security innovators, the event offers an opportunity to connect with the companies and decision-makers shaping the future of digital security. As organizations prepare for a new era of AI-driven operations, the conversations at GITEX GLOBAL 2026 will be particularly relevant to those working to balance technological progress with security, resilience, and trust.
