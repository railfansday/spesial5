Napoli discover referee for Manchester City trip in the Champions League

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Romanian official István Kovács will referee Napoli’s Champions League trip to Manchester City on Tuesday.

According to UEFA, Kovács will be assisted by compatriots Mihai Marica and Ferencz Tunyogi, with Florin Andrei as fourth official. Poland’s Tomasz Kwiatkowski will be on VAR, assisted by Dennis Higler of the Netherlands. The game kicks off at 21:00 CET [20:00 GMT] at the Etihad Stadium.

Kovács is one of Europe’s top referees. He’s from Carei in Romania and he has refereed three major European finals in four years.

He refereed the 2021-22 Conference League final won by José Mourinho’s Roma against Feyenoord in Tirana, then the 2024 Europa League final in Dublin, when Atalanta beat Bayer Leverkusen.

Most recently, UEFA picked him for the 2025 Champions League final in Munich, which PSG won 5-0 against an Inter side that were completely outplayed.

Napoli, however, have mixed memories of him. Napoli felt hard done by in the first leg of their Champions League knockout match with Milan, the 2023 quarter-final.

Massimiliano Allegri’s side head to Manchester with a seemingly ever-worsening injury crisis after Antonio Vergara limped off against Torino on Saturday.
