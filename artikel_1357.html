'One to Watch' - Ben Jacobs: Man Utd Could Move to Sign Gusto for Pochettino

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Manchester United could potentially be one of the busiest clubs in the January transfer window as they look to give new manager Mauricio Pochettino what he needs to turn the club's season around, with GIVEMESPORT senior correspondent Ben Jacobs naming a France international as one possible target.

Hopes were high of another strong season for Man United under the permanent leadership of Michael Carrick after their rise up the Premier League table in the first half of 2026, but instead they have made a miserable start to the campaign.

The Red Devils have won just one of their opening 10 Premier League matches this term, while losing three of their five fixtures in the EFL Cup and Champions League too, which has left them in the relegation places in the top flight.

It could get even worse for them this weekend, as they could be bottom of the table by the time they kick off in Pochettino's first match against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday afternoon.

That will happen if Crystal Palace win in Thomas Frank's first game in charge against Coventry City, and Sunderland draw with Aston Villa.

Pochettino knows he has a big task ahead to turn United's fortunes around, and especially from a defensive point of view, where they have conceded four goals against Como and Aston Villa, and three against Bournemouth, in their previous five fixtures.

GIVEMESPORT sources have revealed that club chiefs will look at signing a new striker in January, with Leeds United's Dominic Calvert-Lewin one player they are keen on, although the England international looks likely to sign a new contract at Elland Road instead.

Meanwhile, in his latest GMS newsletter, Jacobs has confirmed that United are also looking at the centre-back and full-back positions, with Chelsea's Malo Gusto 'one to watch'.

On the right hand side, even though maybe a right-sided full-back is not as high a priority as a left-sided one, Malo Gusto could be one to watch.

"There’s been no contact between Manchester United and Gusto yet, and Manchester City have looked in the past as well. But Chelsea are prepared to sell at the right price.

“Over the summer, that number was £75million and it would have to be dropped significantly, you would imagine anyway, if anyone’s going to come in for the France International, but more broadly, Gusto’s future is one to watch in this January window."

While United may not want to pay £75m to sign Gusto, especially given Chelsea are willing to sell him if the price is right, a move for the 23-year-old would also make a lot of sense.

The France international has already played under Pochettino, with the duo together at Stamford Bridge from the summer of 2023 to when the Argentine was sacked in May 2024.

Gusto knows how the 54-year-old wants to play and should be able to make an immediate impact in a Pochettino team as a result, while he also boasts 100 Premier League appearances and Champions League experience too.

Most impressive though is Gusto's versatility, as while his primary position is as a right-back or right wing-back, he has also been used as a left-back and even in midfield during his career.

It's no secret that United were looking for left-back competition for Luke Shaw in the summer, having shown strong interest in Newcastle United's Lewis Hall and Barcelona's Alejandro Balde, although neither move materialised as they prioritised signing three central midfielders.

Signing Gusto could kill two birds with one stone if he is seen as a player who can perform well in both roles, although there will of course be some caution given Diogo Dalot is a player who can play left-back and right-back, but has largely ultimately struggled when deployed on his weaker side.

Gusto has 17 caps for France and a big future ahead, and if United were able to secure him for a lower fee than the summer asking price, it could be a smart addition to help Pochettino.
