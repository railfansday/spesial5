Inter eye deal for Lazio defender following Stones struggles

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

La Gazzetta dello Sport reports that John Stones’ fitness issues have sparked Inter’s interest in Lazio defender Oliver Provstgaard for next summer.

Inter have set their sights on 23-year-old Lazio defender Provstgaard, according to Gazzetta.

The report claims the defending Serie A champions are already planning to add a new centre-back for next season, also due to Stones’ physical issues.

Lazio’s asking price for the Denmark international is close to €40m, a fee the Nerazzurri will only be able to afford at the end of the season, not in the January window.

According to Gazzetta, Davide Frattesi’s move to Lazio over the summer might even help the Nerazzurri to lower Lazio’s demands.

Frattesi joined Lazio in a €5m loan deal last summer, and the Biancocelesti hold an option to buy for a further €10m.

Inter have a 50% sell-on clause, and re-discussing the terms of the agreement might help them get a better deal for Provstgaard in the summer.

Inter saw Stefan de Vrij, Matteo Darmian and Francesco Acerbi leave the club at the end of their contracts in June 2026, replacing them by signing Stones as a free agent from Manchester City and retaining Benjamin Pavard after his return from a one-season loan spell at Marseille.

Stones has struggled with injuries this season and has just returned to training after nearly two months out. The England international suffered a muscle strain in the flexor muscles of his left thigh in mid-September and was an unused substitute in Inter’s latest Serie A match against Como, just before the break.

The England international has made four appearances across all competitions this season, and his contract at the Stadio Meazza expires in June 2028.
