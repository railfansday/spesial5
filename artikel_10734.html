GTA 6’s new dangerous driving and doomscrolling feature spawns hilarious memes

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

GTA 6 fans are discovering that driving while checking their in-game phone drastically changes the way their vehicle handles, spawning a wave of viral memes and videos that highlight how dangerous it is.

Driving while looking at your mobile phone is never a good idea, as it drastically impairs your ability to react to your surroundings and can even lead to fatal accidents.

Rockstar has also reflected the IRL issues that come with doomscrolling while behind the wheel by having both Jason and Lucia’s driving skills take a complete nosedive.

Just like GTA 5, players can whip out their phone while driving to check messages and interact with apps to their heart’s content. However, GTA 6 takes things a step further by making things even more dangerous. While Jason and Lucia’s smartphone doesn’t take up the entire screen, it does now block out more of your view.

This alone isn’t the only danger to any unfortunate NPC that happens to stray into your general direction, but the car also becomes noticeably harder to control. In fact, it feels like fighting with your controller when it comes to making those smooth maneuvers.

As you can imagine, it’s not exactly the optimal way to drive, especially if you want to keep your car from becoming a total wreck or avoid any pedestrian-related accidents. Well, unless you’re after some run-ins with the cops (it is a GTA game after all).
