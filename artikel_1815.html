Arsenal injury update: Martin Zubimendi, William Saliba, Bukayo Saka latest return dates

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal do not have many fitness concerns ahead of their return to Premier League action, but Martin Zubimendi is a big doubt to face Newcastle this weekend.

The Gunners head to St James’ Park looking to build on victories over Liverpool and Hull prior to the international break, with manager Mikel Arteta expected to have a near fully fit senior squad to choose from.

Bukayo Saka and Max Dowman are guaranteed to miss out through injury and suspension respectively, while Zubimendi’s involvement is up in the air after he limped off during Spain’s 3-2 loss to England in the Nations League.

That same game saw Ezri Konsa shake off a knock to start - and score - thus proving his fitness as he bid to keep his place in the Arsenal defence now that William Saliba has recovered from a back injury.

There was also a mini scare involving Christos Tzolis as he was sent for an MRI while on international duty with Greece, but any fears were immediately allayed and the winger played 88 minutes of the 1-0 win over Serbia.

With that in mind, here is the latest Arsenal injury news and return dates…

The Spaniard just is not having much luck this season. Having gone from the first choice midfield partner for Declan Rice to fourth choice, Zubimendi was already finding opportunities limited prior to this latest knock.

Keep in mind that Zubimendi has been playing with a protective face mask in recent weeks due to suffering a broken nose against Everton nearly a month ago, he suffered a further blow when forced off in the second half for Spain against England.

After the match, Luis de la Fuente confirmed the Arsenal star had sustained a muscle injury, though its severity remains unknown as he will have returned to London Colney for further tests.

A further update should be provided by Arteta in the pre-Newcastle press conference, but it would be a surprise if Zubimendi is able to play again before the end of the month.

Saliba should be challenging Konsa and Gabriel for a place in the Arsenal defence this weekend, following a delayed start to the season due to a long-term back injury that he exacerbated while on duty for France at the World Cup.

An unused substitute in the Champions League victory over Slavia Prague, Saliba and was then left out completely against Hull, before getting some minutes under his belt for the U21s as he played one half of a 5-2 win over Aston Villa.

It has since been reported that the Frenchman played in another behind-closed-doors friendly during the week after not being selected by Zinedine Zidane for the Nations League double-header.

With Arsenal entering a hectic period in terms of fixtures, it should not be too long before Saliba is back in first team action.

Though, the form of Konsa and Gabriel does mean Arteta can ease the defender in.

Potential return date: Saturday November 21, vs Newcastle (A)

Saka has now missed five Arsenal games in a row with a quad injury sustained after just 10 minutes against Bayern Munich last month.

He was also be sidelined for England’s Nations League double-header against Croatia and Spain during the international break.

Amid fears Saka could be out for action for up to eight weeks, Arteta confirmed that the winger’s recovery was going well ahead of the Hull fixture, but it will still be some weeks until he can make a full comeback.

”The way it happened, it wasn't a really aggressive action,” Arteta said before the 1-0 win over Hull.

“So that gives you some hope as well that the tissue will repair in a more efficient and faster way as well and he has some very good symptoms right now. So we have to wait and see."
