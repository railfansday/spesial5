Crystal Palace sweating over Oscar Mingueza injury as defender sent for scans

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Crystal Palace defender Oscar Mingueza is a doubt for Thursday’s Europa League clash with Real Sociedad after missing out through injury against Coventry.

Mingueza was absent from the squad that travelled to Coventry for Thomas Frank’s first game in charge of Palace, and Standard Sport understands that the Spanish full-back has suffered an adductor overload.

The 27-year-old, who arrived at Palace on a free transfer over the summer, has been for a scan, and while his injury is not believed to be serious, it is unclear whether he will be fit to face Real Sociedad at Selhurst Park on Thursday evening.

Mingueza has played nine games in all competitions for Palace this season, but has started just twice in the Premier League, with fellow summer signing Anan Khalaili preferred on the right-hand side of defence.

Speaking about Mingueza’s injury before kick-off against Coventry, Frank said he was hopeful that he could be available against Sociedad.

“Oscar Mingueza unfortunately picked up the smallest of knocks, keeping him out of this game. But we’re hopeful for Thursday,” Frank said.

Palace tasted defeat in their first game under Frank as Dwight McNeil’s 85th-minute own-goal handed Coventry a 3-2 win at the CBS Arena.

They remain rooted to the bottom of the Premier League table, having won two of their opening 11 league games.
