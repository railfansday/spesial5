Why Manchester United kit selection vs Liverpool could be a good omen - with one glaring exception

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Mauricio Pochettino has potentially been handed a positive omen for his first game in charge of Manchester United.

The Argentine begins his tenure as Red Devils boss with the daunting prospect of facing the club’s biggest rivals away from home, with the two sides in contrasting form.

Pochettino arrives at a club currently in the bottom three of the Premier League, while Andoni Iraola has Liverpool fourth in the Premier League table after 10 games.

For Sunday’s trip to Anfield, Man United have confirmed they will wear this season’s third kit which consists of white shirts and black shorts.

Interestingly, Man Utd’s four most recent victories at the home of their arch rivals have come with the players wearing white shirts, including the away half of last season’s double.

Harry Maguire’s header late on gave Man United a 2-1 win at Anfield, ending a run of 10 games without a win for Man United at Anfield.

To find further wins in white for Manchester United at Anfield you would have to go back to Louis van Gaal’s reign.

In 2016 Wayne Rooney’s strike sealed a smash-and-grab 1-0 win against Jurgen Klopp’s Liverpool, with Juan Mata starring the year prior.

However, fans will recall that Man United wore white when they were embarrassed 7-0 at Anfield in March 2023.

Coincidentally, Man Utd are undefeated at Anfield since that humbling loss, picking up two draws and a win in their last three visits to Liverpool. On those occasions Man Utd wore all white.

There was a large gap between white strips for Manchester United on trips to Anfield, with a six-year wait between a 2-0 defeat in the Europa League in 2016 and a 4-0 hammering in 2022.
