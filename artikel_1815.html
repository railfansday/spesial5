Ref Watch: Three Alejandro Jose Hernandez mistakes in Club Brugge vs Liverpool

Alejandro Jose Hernandez caused controversy as the man in the middle of Liverpool's 3-0 win at Club Brugge in the Champions League.

The Reds took an early lead as Alexander Isak continued his strong start to the season by tapping home Dominik Szoboszlai’s pass across the box.

Florian Wirtz doubled the visitors’ lead 11 minutes from time with a superb strike, while Isak grabbed his brace shortly after.

Liverpool are now fifth in the Champions League table with four wins from their first five league stage games.

Hernandez was not at his best as the referee of the Bruges clash on Wednesday evening.

Football Insider have picked out three decisions he clearly got wrong on the night.

Kerkez has built a reputation for rash tackling and being caught out of position throughout his short Liverpool career so far.

Wednesday night was no different as he took to body-checking Brugge winger Forbs in the 21st minute to stop him getting down the hosts' right flank.

Hernandez was lenient in his decision to only award a free-kick to Brugge, with the Reds full-back clearly getting away with one because it was still the first half.

The Spanish referee's mistake continued before the break as he got the simplest of decisions completely wrong.

Brugge playmaker Hugo Vetlesen struck a shot goalwards in the 29th minute that was clearly deflected behind for a corner by a visiting defender.

Hernandez somehow saw fit to give Liverpool a goal kick, and he was set to go through with the decision until an assistant referee corrected it.

It is widely known that play must now come to an immediate halt when a player goes down clutching their head.

That can lead to unfortunate time-wasting, but Brugge defender Seys had no reason to do that, and was clearly hurt after being caught by Rio Ngumoha's boot as he fell to the floor in the second half.

Hernandez somehow failed to spot the injury and kept play going for at least another 30 seconds before the ball found touch and home physios could come on to apply treatment.
