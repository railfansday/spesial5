‘They had faith’ – Moyes reflects on Everton legacy ahead of milestone match

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

David Moyes credited Everton with giving him his first big break in management as he prepares to oversee a record-breaking 593rd match in charge of the club.

Moyes is set to surpass Harry Catterick's record of 592 games managed as Everton boss when his side make the trip to face Brentford in the Premier League.

He first made the move to Merseyside in 2002 from Preston North End and remained in the Everton dugout for 11 years before replacing Alex Ferguson at Manchester United.

The 63-year-old went on to have spells at Real Sociedad, Sunderland and West Ham before returning to Everton in January 2025 to replace Sean Dyche.

During his two stints with the Toffees, Moyes has overseen 250 wins in all competitions (D155 L187), and his Everton side have started the 2026-27 campaign brightly.

Everton are seventh after signing off for the international break with a win over Coventry City, and Moyes wants that upward trajectory to continue in his milestone match.

"I'm really thrilled about it, but if I'm honest, it's not something I really think about," Moyes told reporters ahead of the trip to the Gtech Community Stadium.

"I've suddenly become the manager who has managed more Everton games than anyone before. That's a privilege and an honour to be put in that position.

"I have to thank the people who brought me to the club. When you take a chance on a young manager, you never know if it'll work, but they had faith and believed in me.

"I'm so thankful for the people around Everton because they've been so good for me."

Brentford boss Keith Andrews paid respect to the Scot.

"I played against his Everton team. I've always had a lot of respect for him, even when I was a player," Andrews said of the Everton boss.

"He's now an iconic manager of English football, with how long he's been involved and how successful he's been."

Brentford know a win will see them leapfrog Everton in the Premier League, but they must do so without Igor Thiago, who was sent off against Brighton last time out.

Thiago was shown a red card for violent conduct in an incident with Chema Andres after a VAR review, with an appeal against the decision unanimously rejected.

Put to him that Callum Wilson could come in as a direct replacement in attack, Andrews added: "That would be your natural thinking.

"We've got three players that have played that position and can play that position; wide players could become more central options. We have Fabio Carvalho coming back as well.

"There are different ways that we can play, but Callum has obviously been champing at the bit. I do not doubt that he is going to get a decent number of minutes."

With Thiago suspended, Andrews looks set to name Wilson as his starting striker against Everton, and it is not a bad replacement to have.

Wilson has failed to score in the Premier League this season, but he does have a good record against Everton. Only against West Ham (12) has he scored more top-flight goals against than the Toffees (nine).

Having won their first Premier League home game against Everton 1-0 in November 2021, Brentford are winless in their last four against them at the Gtech (D3 L1).

Andrews' side have, however, won four of their last seven Premier League games at the Gtech (D2 L1), and are looking for back-to-back home league wins for the first time in 2026, having last done so in November 2025 (three in a row).

But the Bees have won just one of their last 11 Premier League games that have come the matchday following an international break (D5 L5) – although that was the only such match they played on a Monday in this period, beating West Ham 2-0 in October 2025.

Everton, meanwhile, have lost just one of their last eight Premier League games against Brentford (W3 D4), though they are winless in their last four against them (D3 L1).

This will be Everton's ninth top-flight game played on a Monday since the start of last season, three more than any other side in this time (Manchester United six). The Toffees have lost just one of their last seven on this day of the week (W2 D4), winning their last two away from home.

Moyes' men have won back-to-back Premier League games for the first time since between February and March, and are looking to win three in a row in the competition for the first time since May 2025.
