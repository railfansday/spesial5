Arsenal handed Ezri Konsa injury update after unusual squad announcement

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal defender Ezri Konsa has been ruled out of England’s Nations League match against Croatia, with James Hill called up to the senior squad for the first time.

The Three Lions host Croatia on Thursday night before concluding their Nations League group with a trip to Spain on Sunday.

Konsa will play no part in the first of those matches and he is training away from Thomas Tuchel’s main group as he follows his own programme.

The expectation is that Konsa will return to full training later this week and be in contention to face Spain this weekend.

Konsa has been managing a minor issue in recent weeks, one that led to him coming off when Arsenal played Slavia Prague.

However, the defender has still been fit enough to start each of Arsenal’s last 12 matches across the Premier League and Champions League.

He has been one of the Gunners’ best players this season, slotting in alongside Gabriel in defence and filling the void left by the injured William Saliba.

With Saliba now fit again and closing in on a return to first-team action, Konsa faces a fight to keep his place in Mikel Arteta’s side.

Despite that fitness concern in Prague, Konsa was passed fit to face Hull on Sunday and he played the full 90 minutes.

He appeared to come through that without any further issues, but it has been decided that he should miss the Croatia match with a view to being available for a clash with Spain that could well decide top spot in the Nations League group.
