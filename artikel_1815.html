‘It didn’t sit very well’ – Baena admits Alvarez transfer request irked Atleti team-mates

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Atletico Madrid midfielder Alex Baena admits Julian Alvarez's attempts to join Barcelona this summer "annoyed" the rest of Diego Simeone's squad.

Alvarez was at the heart of one of the most high-profile sagas during the summer transfer window, after saying it was his "dream" to join Barca.

Barca president Joan Laporta was not shy about the club's desire to acquire the former World Cup winner, but the Atleti hierarchy responded by saying they would never do a deal with their domestic rivals.

Ultimately, Alvarez stayed put, with Barca moving for Gabriel Jesus at the end of the window, to provide competition for Raphinha, Lamine Yamal, Fermin Lopez, Anthony Gordon and Karim Adeyemi.

The Argentine's return to the fold saw him booed by his own fans during a draw with Villarreal in August, but he has since been fully reintegrated by head coach Diego Simeone.

Yet Baena said the way Alvarez approached the speculation did not earn him much support in Atleti's dressing room.

"It feels a bit strange when a team-mate says publicly that he wants to leave," the Spain international said in an interview with Cadena SER.

"I can understand a player having doubts if a club like Barca or Real Madrid comes in for you, but, ultimately, it was the way he went about it that really annoyed us at Atletico."

Now, though, Baena says all has been forgiven and Alvarez deserves to be supported by everyone at the club.

"We would have supported him regardless of what he decided to do," Baena added.

"The important thing is that he is with us now, and we need to show him all the affection in the world."

Alvarez has netted just twice in all competitions this season while providing three assists, having teed up Giuliano Simeone's opening goal in Sunday's 2-1 defeat to Barca.

Alvarez led all Atletico players for shots (four), touches in the opposition box (seven) and successful dribbles (three) in that game, while also creating two chances.

Atletico's defeat snapped their seven-game winning run in LaLiga. They sit third in the table, two points behind second-place Real Madrid and five adrift of leaders Barca.

Alvarez could feature for Argentina during the international break, as they face Uruguay and Costa Rica in friendlies, while Baena and his Spain team-mates will take on Czechia and England in the Nations League.
