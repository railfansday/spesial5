Prediction: Bayern Munich vs Koln

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Marking their return from November’s international break on Friday night, Bayern Munich will welcome Koln to the Allianz.

With the rest of Europe’s elite domestic leagues making their respective returns this weekend, there are plenty of eye-catching betting promotions available.

Ahead of Friday’s Bundesliga battle, you can find all of the key Bayern Munich vs Koln H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, welcoming back Vincent Kompany to the touchline this weekend, Bayern Munich will be excited to make a return from November’s fortnight interval. Last seen heading into the final international break of 2026 with a 2-1 victory away at Mainz, it is no secret that the German giants are universally regarded as the standout contender to once again defend their Bundesliga crown this season. Marking their return sat at the top of the table, Bayern will be looking to make another public statement here. Getting their Champions League adventure underway with a real flurry and yet to suffer a single competitive defeat across all competitions so far this season, Kompany’s superstar squad have also gained a fearsome reputation for their free-scoring performances in the final third. In fact, the former Burnley boss has seen his squad notch 29 goals in their first nine Bundesliga appearances.

Along with welcoming Kompany back to the Allianz dugout this weekend, German international Jamal Musiala is also expected to make a first Bundesliga appearance since the middle of September. However, suffering from a muscle injury prior to November’s international break, former Liverpool star Luis Diaz remains on the sidelines.

Crowned as the new Ballon d’Or winner during an emotional ceremony in London last month, talismanic number nine Harry Kane will lead the line in Munich. Registering nine direct goal contributions in as many Bundesliga appearances this season (six goals, three assists), Michael Olise will partner Kane in the final third.

As for the visitors, while Koln might have eventually maintained their Bundesliga status last season, the Billy Goats appear destined for another relegation scrap this time around. Marking their respective return from November’s international break holding just a two-point buffer over the drop zone, Rene Wagner’s side couldn’t have asked for a tougher examination when they travel to Munich this weekend. Last seen heading into the fortnight interval with a 2-1 defeat at home against Bayer Leverkusen, Koln have mustered just a sole win from any of their previous eight Bundesliga appearances. Along with failing to find any kind of clinical edge in the final third, Friday’s guests will be concerned by a nightmare record against Bayern. Including a 5-1 drumming when they last travelled to the Allianz back on May 16th, they have lost each of their last six straight battles by an aggregate score of 19-4.

Yet to make a maiden Bundesliga appearance this season, German midfielder Timo Hubers remains on the sidelines with a serious knee injury. However, Wagner’s side could welcome back one-time Sparta Rotterdam youngster Ragnar Ache for the first time since the start of October.

While Tottenham loanee Mikey Moore might still be waiting to open his Bundesliga account, the England U21 international will feature in Munich. Bagging what turned out to be a consolation strike against Bayer Leverkusen last time out and featuring for Germany over the past fortnight, 20-year-old Said El Mala will lead the line on Friday night.

Excited to welcome Kompany back to the Allianz dugout on Friday night, Bayern Munich will be confident of laying down another domestic marker. Marking their return from November’s fortnight interval once again leading the chase at the top of the Bundesliga standings, the reigning German champions have yet to suffer a single defeat across any competition since opening the new campaign. Cruising their way to a routine 5-1 victory when they last welcomed Koln to the Allianz back in May, Bayern hold a dominant record against Friday’s guests. Winning their previous six consecutive Bundesliga battles by an aggregate score of 14-4, a free-scoring Bayern will be confident of putting on a show.
