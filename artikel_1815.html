Prediction: Croatia vs Czech Republic

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Verdict: Over 2.5 goals Best odds: 59/100 Bookmaker: 1xBet

UEFA Nations League, League A, Group 3 | Sunday, November 15, 2026 | Opus Arena, Osijek | 7:45 PM GMT

Tomáš Souček has scored on this ground before, although he might prefer to forget what happened next. His equalizer put the Czech Republic level with Croatia in a World Cup qualifier in June 2025. Seventeen minutes later, Croatia led 5-1. Souček is back in the Czech squad for Sunday’s Nations League finale, and September’s much closer meeting in Prague offers another reason to like the goals market here.

Slaven Bilić’s second spell as Croatia coach began with a 2-1 win in Prague. His side created chances in a scoreless first half, then took the lead two minutes after the break. Igor Matanović won the ball for Luka Modrić, who finished from the edge of the box. After the Czechs equalized, Bilić found the winner on his bench. Marco Pašalić had been on the field for about 15 minutes when he headed in Ivan Perišić’s cross.

What followed explains why the home win is a less straightforward call than that September result might suggest. Croatia lost 4-1 in Spain and 7-0 at home to England. Bilić got a much closer game out of his side in the return against Spain, but a 2-1 defeat still left the win in Prague as Croatia’s only points from the opening four rounds.

Andrej Kramarić, who scored twice in that 5-1 win in Osijek, misses the November games through injury. Franjo Ivanović is also out. Injuries to Josip Šutalo and Marin Pongračić leave Bilić shorter at center back, where Dinamo Zagreb’s Niko Galešić earned his first senior call-up.

The Czech Republic lost its opening four group games, but Croatia saw more of its attack than England did across two meetings. Adam Hložek supplied the cutback for Adam Karabec’s equalizer in Prague. Even after Pašalić had restored Croatia’s lead, the hosts found the net again through Matěj Radosta, only for a VAR review to rule the goal out.

That passage of play matters for Sunday. Santi Denia’s side already found a way behind Croatia’s defense, and Souček gives Hložek another target when he gets into a crossing position.

Souček missed the first four rounds after injuring ankle ligaments at the World Cup. He returns for the November fixtures, while Karabec and Pavel Šulc miss out through injury. Teplice winger Idris Hopi and Mladá Boleslav midfielder David Kozel received their first senior call-ups.

Croatia found two different ways through the Czech defense in Prague, and Hložek caused enough trouble at the other end to make a home clean sheet a difficult sell. Another 2-1 Croatia win looks plausible. Over 2.5 goals also covers the visitors making more of their chances this time.

Verdict: Over 2.5 goals Best odds: 59/100 Bookmaker: 1xBet
