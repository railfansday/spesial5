The 63 Best New Perfumes and Fragrances of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

We sniffed, we spritzed, we sprayed. Only the perfumes below left a lingering impression, earning them a spot on our annual best-of list.

Everything smells like vanilla. At least, that’s what perfume naysayers will tell you. And it’s somewhat true: If you’re judging by what’s popular, ubiquitous, or cool with the youth, then sure—vanilla scents and sweet gourmands are still trending.

But hundreds of other fragrance launches in 2026 deserve a spot on your vanity and a spritz on your skin, and many are far less sweet. Gourmands took a turn for the weird and fruity this year, with notes like lychee and rice milk popping up in otherwise innocuous formulas. Woods and ambers continued to reign, as did spice-forward and citrus scents with unexpected depth. Perfumers are meeting our demand for newness in increasingly interesting, artistic ways, and we smelled hundreds of their creations to choose the 63 winners of our annual fragrance awards.

See all the winners of the 2026 Harper’s Bazaar Fragrance Awards, plus quotes from our beauty editors on what makes these formulas worthy of your next blind buy.
