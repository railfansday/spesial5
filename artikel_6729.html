Meet ‘The Altruists’ Cast and Characters

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

You know a true story hates to see them coming.

The fact that the characters of Netflix’s The Altruists feel like one big family is what the computer science community would call a bug, not a feature. Based on real people and led by two true story mavens, Julia Garner and Anthony Boyle, the series documents how one of the many reasons the collapse of the cryptocurrency exchange company FTX happened is that the employees were all friends, lovers, and emotionally tangled in ways that made it hard to do business ethically. That and the blatant fraud. As you get sucked into the series, and the many romances at the center of all that crime, let me provide you with a cheat sheet for the cast and characters of The Altruists.

Most of the actors on the Netflix series are young, because the people they are portraying were young when this all went down. The adult cast includes some pretty recognizable names, like Jennifer Grey (Dirty Dancing), as well as character actors like Robin Weigart (Deadwood), Paul Reiser (Mad About You), and William Mapother (Lost). But let’s get to know the kids’ table, because unfortunately they were and still are running this world even after the events of The Altruists.

Caroline is the co-CEO of Alameda Research and Sam’s on/off girlfriend.

Garner previously played Anna Delvey, a very different type of scammer, in the miniseries Inventing Anna. But she’s best known for playing Ruth on Ozark for four seasons. The role earned her a Golden Globe and three Emmy Awards. Prior to that, she had a recurring role on The Americans. Her other television credits include Maniac, Dirty John, Waco and The Get Down. She played the villain in an MCU movie last year, Fantastic Four: First Steps, NBD. Some of her other film credits include Weapons, Wolf Man, The Assistant, and Martha Marcy May Marlene.

Sam is the CEO of FTX, and Caroline’s on/off girlfriend.

Since originating the role of Scorpius Malfoy in Harry Potter and the Cursed Child, for which he won a freakin’ Olivier Award, Boyle has been dominating the based-on-a-true-story genre. On television, that includes: House of Guinness, Say Nothing, Manhunt, and Masters of the Air. In film, that includes Tetris, Danny Boy, and Tolkien. You’ve also seen him in Shardlake, The Plot Against America, Ordeal by Innocence and two episodes of Derry Girls.

Trabucco is the co-CEO of Alameda Research and Sam’s friend from MIT.

Lawther is a stage and screen actor best known for starring in The End of the F***ing World on Netflix and Andor on Disney+. You may also recognize him from Alien: Earth, Howards End, Black Mirror, The Last Duel, The French Dispatch, and The Imitation Game. His involvement in The Jungle off-Broadway earned him an Obie Award, and you can also see him on NT Live in the play Summerfolk.

Ryan became the CEO of FTX Digital Markets after working at Alameda Reserch.

Rife is a standup comedian with a smattering of acting experiences, including shows like Gamer's Guide to Pretty Much Everything, Fresh Off The Boat, That ‘90s Show, and the Scrubs reboot. You’ve also seen him in films like The Curse of Wolf Mountain and I Live Here Now.

Hu is a former child actress you know from Best Friends Whenever and Bizaardvark. More recently, her credits include The Brothers Sun, Man on the Inside, and Rooster. She has also appeared in several thriller films, like Voyagers, The Boogeyman, and Night Shift.

Nishad is FTX head of engineering and Sam’s childhood friend.

Soni is best known for playing Dopinder in the Deadpool movies and Pavitr Prabhakar in the Spider-Verse movies. But if you haven’t seen A Nice Indian Boy, the romantic comedy he stars in opposite Jonathan Groff, be sure to check that out! He had a major role on Miracle Workers and other comedies like Other Space and The Goldbergs. His filmography also includes Safety Not Guaranteed, Rough Night, Always Be My Maybe, Unicorn Store, and People We Hate At The Wedding.

Gary is FTX’s CTO and, you guessed it, Sam’s childhood friend.

Young played Reggie on Veep and had a major role on the Korean period drama The King’s Affection. He also had a recurring role on Extraordinary Attorney Woo and the popular dystopian Netflix series Squid Game.

Claire is a software engineer at FTX and, eventually, Nishad’s girlfriend.

Okada is a Juilliard graduate whose only other screen credit, according to IMDb at least, is a web-series called Q Train.

Also known as CZ, Changpeng Zhao is the allegedly shady CEO of Binance.

Chen is best known for television shows like Continuum, The Lake, Jessica Jones, A Million Little Things, UnREAL, Bates Motel, and House of Cards. His film credits include Almost Famous, The Cabin in the Woods, and Snakes on a Plane.

Maddie Hasson plays Lauren Remmington Platt

Lauren Platt is a former model and FTX’s head of Global Luxury Partnerships. She also appears to do some PR management.

Hasson played the female lead in Twisted alongside Avan Jogia. You might also know her from shows like The Recruit, Mr. Mercedes, The Finder, and Impulse or films like Malignant, Novitiate, and Bone Lake.

Duncan is the rich boyfriend of Lily Zhang, played by Marianna Phung (Y: The Last Man). Duncan and Lily are the co-founders of Modulo Capital, a crypto-trading firm.

Williams broke out at the end of 2025 as one of the co-leads of Heated Rivalry. You can also catch him on the Canadian series The Assembly and Yaga. Up next, he’ll appear in a handful of films including The Apparatus and Tyrant, which also stars Garner.
