GTA 6 fans convinced iconic GTA 4 character’s cameo has been revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Grand Theft Auto 6 might feature a cameo from an iconic character that has appeared in GTA 4 and GTA Online, after fans think they’ve spotted them in a new clip.

Over the years, a number of characters from Grand Theft Auto’s past have popped up in newer games. Johnny Klebitz met an unfortunate end when players were introduced to Trevor in GTA 5, for example.

With GTA 6 just on the horizon, fans have long speculated about similar cameos. Now, following the newest small ‘trailer’ from Rockstar on November 14, fans think they’ve spotted another: Tony Prince.

Prince, who was a part of GTA 4: The Ballad of Gay Tony and appears in GTA Online as part of the nightclub update, appears to turn up during a brief two-second moment in the clip based in the Leonida Keys.

Grand Theft Auto VI pre-loading is now available for everyone who has pre-ordered. If you still have yet to pre-order, now’s the time to make sure you're prepared for November 19: https://t.co/LUoPLobocD pic.twitter.com/TLtJUSFBwN

YouTuber TGG noted that it is a “crazy” theory from fans, but he is “buying into it” due to the voice of the character in the clip.

“Why does this guy actually kinda look and sound like Tony? I’m losing my mind, similar glasses, beard, and very similar voice,” he posted on X (formerly Twitter). “There’s no way he let himself go and moved to the keys, right?”

The creator also pointed out that the newest ‘trailer’ is based on nightclubs, so it could be a more than subtle hint about Prince’s potential involvement.

As noted, other fans are on board with the theory too. “I’m ngl, it could be? It’s insanely similar,” one said.

“The whole trailer does have a very BoGT vibe to it,” another commented. “It’d be quite the fall from grace based off his appearance in the trailer,” another added.
