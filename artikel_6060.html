Hegseth demands $350B for military, fires back at Dem impeachment push

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth urged Congress to approve at least $350 billion in additional funding, arguing the investment is needed to rebuild the military and keep the U.S. ahead in a dangerous world. (Credit: @SecWar)

War Secretary Pete Hegseth urged Congress Thursday to approve at least $350 billion in defense funding while pushing back against efforts to impeach him.

In a video posted to X, Hegseth defended his Pentagon overhaul, including eliminating DEI initiatives, removing military leaders and rebuilding recruitment.

Hegseth dismissed the impeachment push as political theater while defending his efforts to overhaul the Pentagon and advance President Donald Trump's military agenda.

HEGSETH FACES BIPARTISAN IMPEACHMENT THREAT AFTER IRAN WAR GETS BLAME FOR GOP MIDTERM SLAUGHTER

Secretary of War Pete Hegseth testifies before the Senate Armed Services Committee on Capitol Hill, in Washington, D.C., April 30. (Cliff Owen/AP Photo, File)

"We need every vote from every patriotic member of Congress," Hegseth said, calling for a reconciliation bill of at least $350 billion to strengthen the military and defense industrial base.

The impeachment effort is being led by Rep. Thomas Massie, R-Ky., who introduced eight articles against Hegseth in September, accusing him of violating congressional war powers during the Iran conflict and abusing his authority in other military operations.

Massie, who lost his Republican primary in May, plans to renew the effort when Congress returns next week. The House has not voted on the articles.

Trump defended Hegseth on Thursday, telling ABC News he intends to keep him in his Cabinet despite the impeachment push.

GOP REP THOMAS MASSIE INTRODUCES 8 ARTICLES OF IMPEACHMENT AGAINST WAR SECRETARY PETE HEGSETH

Rep. Thomas Massie, R-Ky., introduced eight articles of impeachment against Hegseth in September. (Tom Williams/CQ-Roll Call, Inc via Getty Images, File)

Hegseth dismissed the effort as politically motivated.

"A disgruntled, defeated congressman along with all his Democrat allies, has also dropped sham articles of impeachment against yours truly," Hegseth said.

He also took aim at former President Joe Biden and former Defense Secretary Lloyd Austin, accusing them of leaving the Pentagon in disarray and forcing the Trump administration to rebuild the military.

"We ended the weird and the woke and elevated patriots in strength," Hegseth said, defending his efforts to eliminate diversity initiatives and change military personnel policies.

HEGSETH'S LEADERSHIP QUESTIONED BY GOP MILITARY VETERAN AS DEMOCRATS TALK IMPEACHMENT

Trump defended Hegseth on Thursday, telling ABC News he intends to keep him in his Cabinet despite the impeachment push. (Aaron Schwartz/AFP via Getty Images, File)

Hegseth also defended the administration's military operations against Iran, describing the campaign as a historic effort to prevent Tehran from acquiring nuclear weapons.

The war secretary closed with a message to troops amid the political fight in Washington.

"Don't blink. Ignore the noise. Never hesitate," Hegseth said."We will not back down. We put America and the warfighter first, always and without apology."
