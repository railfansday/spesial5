Booking a table can be the least exciting part of a night out. However, the platform you use to do it can now earn you rewards worth anything from a few cents to a delicious discount on your bill.

OpenTable, DoorDash, Seated and now Resy all reward you for dining out, though each takes a different approach. OpenTable awards its own points, DoorDash issues credits for future orders, Seated offers rewards that can be redeemed for gift cards and Resy awards American Express Membership Rewards points.

As with your favorite coffee chain's loyalty program or a food delivery app subscription, the best option depends on where, how often and how much you eat out. Whichever you choose, you'll generally still earn rewards on eligible restaurant purchases when paying with a dining credit card, making your meal even sweeter.

In this guide, we compare what each program earns, what those rewards are worth and the fine print to watch so you can decide which one deserves a seat at your table.

OpenTable Regulars is free to join, and you'll earn 100 dining points for each reservation you book directly on OpenTable while signed in to your account and then complete. Reservations made through restaurant websites or third-party booking partners generally don't qualify. Points are posted only after you've dined, so canceled reservations and no-shows earn nothing.

Some restaurants in the program also offer bonus-point reservations worth 500, 750 or even 1,000 points at certain times. To find them, turn on the "bonus points filter" under special features in the OpenTable app.

That means a standard 100-point reservation is only worth 80 cents toward a future meal, and you'll need 20 of them to earn a $16 dining credit, which isn't very rewarding.

Dining credits work at participating OpenTable restaurants (look for the "Dining Credit Accepted" filter). After you redeem your points, you can add the credit to a reservation, which provides a digital Mastercard you can use toward eligible bills. Experience credits work similarly but only apply to certain prepaid experiences, such as prix-fixe menus and cocktail classes. Dining credits expire 12 months after you redeem them.

If you complete six reservations on OpenTable within 12 months, you'll then unlock Gold status for a year. Gold members get priority notifications when tables open up, a three-month complimentary Uber One trial for eligible new or returning members who do not have an active subscription and access to "Gold Tables," which promises harder-to-book tables at more than 500 participating restaurants in over 50 U.S. cities.

OpenTable also partners with Chase, Uber and Visa. Uber One members can book Uber One Exclusive Tables, eligible Visa cardholders can access tables and special events through the Visa Dining Collection and select Chase cardholders get exclusive tables and statement credits (more on that below).

Make sure you're actually turning up for your bookings, though. OpenTable will close your Regulars account and cancel all your points if you record four no-shows within 12 months. Your points also expire after 12 months of inactivity or after 3 years from the date you earn them, whichever comes first.

DoorDash is best known for delivery, but reservations are now available in select cities across the U.S., Australia and Canada. Its current market page lists 17 U.S. cities, plus Melbourne and Sydney in Australia and Toronto and Vancouver in Canada.

Rather than earn points, you earn promotional DoorDash credits when you book and complete an eligible reservation in the DoorDash app or on its website. The amount is shown before you book and now varies by restaurant, reservation time and membership status. Current U.S. listings commonly advertise $5 in credits, while some times display $10 or $12. Not every reservation earns a credit.

Credits are posted after you've paid for your meal and can be used toward eligible future DoorDash delivery or pickup orders. They typically can't be used to pay for taxes, fees or tips, expire six months after they're issued unless the offer says otherwise, have no cash value and can't be used to buy gift cards or alcohol.

Since the reward is a fixed amount, you don't earn more rewards for a higher food bill. A $5 credit is a 5% return on a $100 dinner but about 1.7% on a $300 tasting menu. A $10 credit would deliver returns of 10% and about 3.3%, respectively. That makes DoorDash Reservations especially appealing for smaller restaurant bills if you already use DoorDash.

DashPass members can earn more on eligible reservations, plus access exclusive prime-time tables and discounts on Lyft Black rides. However, the elevated reservation reward is no longer a simple fixed $2 boost across every listing, so check the offer shown before booking rather than assuming your membership guarantees a particular amount.

Check the restaurant's cancellation policy when you book. Late cancellations and no-shows may incur fees set by the restaurant, and DoorDash says excessive no-shows can result in suspension of your access to reservations.

Outside its reservation cities, DoorDash's "Going Out" tab also lists in-store offers at certain restaurants, with DashPass members earning extra rewards for repeat visits in some locations.

What is unique about this program is that you don't need to book through Seated: You link an eligible credit or debit card, then tap to activate the restaurants where you want to earn a higher reward rate before paying with your linked card. Eligible rewards then post automatically, with no receipts to upload.

Activations last until the end of the calendar month, and you can activate up to 25 restaurants per month (plus any monthly specials). Seated's app also advertises 5% back at participating restaurants, even without an activation, provided you open the app at least once every 30 days. Higher reward rates generally require activation, so it's worth activating your favorites at the start of each month and checking the offer terms in the app.

Rewards vary by restaurant but can be up to a generous 35% back, with the rate shown on each restaurant's page before you choose to activate it. Rewards are calculated on your eligible food-and-beverage total before tax and tip, so a restaurant offering 10% back would earn you $10 in Seated rewards on a $100 meal. Since each diner earns on the share they pay with their own linked card, splitting the bill doesn't cost anyone their individual rewards.

Seated partners with more than 2,500 restaurants in certain U.S. markets. Availability varies by city and neighborhood, so check the app for participating locations and current rewards before dining, rather than relying on a fixed city list.

You can then redeem Seated rewards for gift cards in the app's store tab, with available brands to choose from including Airbnb, Amazon, Dunkin', Nordstrom, Sephora, Starbucks, Target and Uber. Because rewards can then be converted to eligible gift cards at face value, TPG values them at $1 per $1 redeemed, provided you choose a brand you'd spend with anyway and meet any minimum redemption requirement.

The catch is that Seated rewards may expire after more than 30 days without qualifying account activity, so check the app's activity and expiration rules rather than assuming opening it alone preserves your balance. Seated also says its rewards can't be combined with other restaurant promotions, third-party deals (such as those available through Groupon) or discount offers.

If you're a new member, keep an eye out for a first-meal bonus offer of up to $10 through an eligible referral link or promotional offer, though this is not guaranteed for every member.

Resy Rewards takes a different approach than the other three programs. It does not issue a stand-alone Resy currency. Instead, eligible U.S. American Express cardmembers earn 4 additional Membership Rewards points per dollar spent at participating restaurants, on top of the points their enrolled card already earns.

To participate, you must first enroll an eligible U.S. Amex card that earns Membership Rewards points in the Rewards tab of the Resy iOS app.

You must then book a participating restaurant through the Rewards tab, complete the meal and pay with the enrolled card. Merely dining at a participating restaurant or booking it through another part of Resy will not trigger the 4 bonus points per dollar. At launch, the program is only available through the Resy iOS app, meaning Android users cannot currently use it.

The initial New York City list includes restaurants like Carne Mare, Le Crocodile, Hearth, Kiko, Opto and Le Pavillon. American Express plans to expand Resy Rewards to additional U.S. cities and select Tock venues in 2027.

On a $100 eligible restaurant purchase, Resy Rewards would provide 400 bonus Membership Rewards points. Based on our valuation of 2 cents per Membership Rewards point, that is an estimated $8 in value (or an 8% return) before accounting for the card's standard earning rate.

If you spend $200 on a meal rather than $100, the OpenTable and DoorDash rewards stay the same, while the Seated rewards as a percentage of spending will quickly increase. Resy would also double to 800 bonus points, worth an estimated $16 with TPG's valuation of Amex points.

Seated can be the most rewarding option for larger restaurant bills, especially at restaurants that offer higher reward rates. However, its value depends on the percentage offered. DoorDash's fixed credits may still provide you with a better return on smaller bills or at restaurants that offer lower Seated rewards. Resy provides a consistent 8% estimated return from its bonus points — but only at its small number of participating restaurants at launch.

Resy Rewards offers a strong, straightforward points return at participating restaurants. However, the program is launching at only a handful of New York City restaurants and is available through the Resy iOS app, so most diners will not be able to use it regularly yet.

Seated has the highest potential return for larger dining bills. A restaurant offering 15% back would earn you $30 in rewards redeemable for gift cards on a $200 dinner, while a 35% offer would earn $70. Know, though, that higher rates generally require monthly activation, and you'll need to keep your rewards account active.

DoorDash Reservations can still be the best option for smaller meals when the booking carries a strong credit. A $10 or $12 reward can beat percentage-based programs on a modest check. The catch is that rewards now vary by reservation, availability is limited to select cities and the credits are less useful if you rarely order through DoorDash.

OpenTable Regulars is the least rewarding of the four for most standard reservations, with a 100-point booking worth just 80 cents at the best U.S. redemption rate. Still, it is the most broadly available of the four in the markets where its Dining Points program operates and offers useful extras, including bonus-point tables, Gold Tables and a three-month Uber One trial for eligible Gold members. Select Chase cardholders can also receive statement credits at eligible restaurants.

There's no single best dining rewards platform for everyone, as the right choice depends on where you live, how much you spend and how often you dine out.

Resy offers a compelling points return at participating venues, but its limited launch footprint and lack of an Android app make it more of a promising option than an everyday solution right now.

Seated can deliver the biggest reward on an expensive meal, while DoorDash can be particularly rewarding on a smaller bill when the booking offers a decent credit.

As for OpenTable, it remains the most broadly available of the four in the markets where its Dining Points program operates. Its nationwide coverage, Gold perks and Chase partnership make it worth considering, especially when you travel.

Since all four cost nothing to join, you may as well sign up for them all so you are ready the next time you want to book a meal. Just remember to show up for reservations or cancel within the restaurant's deadline, as no-shows can cost you points, credits or even access.

That's some food for thought before your next night out.
