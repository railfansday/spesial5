Outback Steakhouse reveals massive Thor’s hammer steak in yet another Avengers: Doomsday menu

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Outback Steakhouse is the latest restaurant chain to join the Avengers: Doomsday food craze with an enormous steak inspired by Thor’s iconic hammer, Mjolnir.

With Marvel’s next blockbuster fast approaching, brands have been rolling out increasingly ridiculous promotional menus. We’ve already seen black burgers, green mayonnaise and Doctor Doom-themed donuts, but Outback might have just taken things to another level.

The steakhouse’s Korean accounts announced its own collaboration with Marvel Studios, showing a massive bone-in steak designed to resemble Thor’s hammer.

The enormous cut of beef uses its bone to recreate the weapon’s handle, resulting in a meal that looks more like something the God of Thunder would wield in battle than something you’d order for dinner.

Outback first teased its Marvel collaboration earlier in November with a mysterious video featuring Doctor Doom’s signature green imagery.

The steak also features a Thor-inspired handle, just in case you wanted to wield the piece of meat as a weapon. Likely super effective against vegans, but we’re not sure how well it would do against Doom.

The Thor-inspired steak isn’t the only item on Outback’s Avengers: Doomsday menu arriving on November 10. The lineup also features shrimp pasta, a tomato-and-cheese dish, and a chocolate dome dessert decorated with Doctor Doom’s emblem.

There’s even a layered green-and-red iced drink to round out the superhero-themed feast.

Outback is far from the only restaurant getting involved in Marvel’s latest marketing blitz.

Earlier this week, KFC revealed its own Avengers: Doomsday menu, featuring black burgers, Smoky Green Mayo and a bizarre seasoning called Doom Dust.

Not to be outdone, Burger King launched an equally wild menu in India, complete with green-covered Whoppers, Thor Thunder Nuggets and a mysterious Sprite-based drink.

Meanwhile, Dunkin’ assembled its own superhero lineup with character-inspired coffees, donuts and collectibles, including a Doctor Doom MUNCHKINS tin modeled after the villain’s mask.

Even Dave & Buster’s has joined the chaos, temporarily rebranding itself as Doom & Buster’s with exclusive Marvel games and drinks.

With Avengers: Doomsday hitting theaters on December 18, 2026, there’s still plenty of time for more brands to get involved.

For now, though, Outback might have the most ridiculous offering of the bunch. Whether customers will need to prove themselves worthy before lifting Thor’s steak remains to be seen. But steak lovers may have to make the trek to Korea to try it for themselves as it’s unclear if the menu will be coming to other countries just yet.
