Viral Cowboy who took his horse on bus responds after Detroit launches investigation

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

A horse owner has responded after a viral video showed them taking their horse onto a bus, and even the city is investigating.

When you’re using public transport, it isn’t a shock to see a dog sitting next to its owner, or other pets being transported in carry cases. These are pretty common sights for regular bus and train riders.

However, on November 9, the internet’s attention was caught by a viral video of two horses being led onto a bus in Detroit. In the clip, which was filmed from across the street, the two riders approached the door while holding their horses on a leash.

Both horses and riders were able to get on board the bus briefly, but they didn’t stick around for long. That left the woman recording the scene, Elessia Drew, in disbelief. “This is crazy,” she said.

The video has been reposted across social media since Drew first posted it to Facebook, attracting millions of views.

Jordan Williams, the horse’s owner, told WXYZ that he helped his horse on board for a funny video, but has taken them on buses before. “We walked on, and I must tell you the buses are a lot smaller than they used to be, or I may have been a little bigger,” he said.

“The point is just to bring light to… like there are cowboys that look like us.”

However, the Detroit Department of Transport has said they are taking the “taking appropriate steps to address this incident” as it is “not consistent with DDOT policy and potentially creates an unsafe and uncomfortable ride for our passengers.”

Williams told WXYZ that he hopes they “look at it like a joke like everyone else,” rather than taking any action.
