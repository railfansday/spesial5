Amazon announces $25 “complete” Thanksgiving meal with same-day delivery

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Amazon is getting into the Thanksgiving dinner game with a surprisingly affordable $25 holiday meal that includes a turkey, sides, dessert, and even same-day delivery.

With Thanksgiving just weeks away, retailers are rolling out deals to make feeding the family a little easier on the wallet.

Now, Amazon has announced its own Thanksgiving meal deal, offering an entire holiday spread for just $25 through Amazon Grocery.

The promotion is available in more than 2,300 US cities and towns, with eligible customers able to have their groceries delivered within hours.

The centerpiece of Amazon’s Thanksgiving bundle is an eight-pound frozen Butterball turkey, accompanied by a surprisingly extensive selection of sides.

Customers will receive Ocean Spray cranberry sauce, two packets of McCormick turkey gravy, two boxes of Kraft Mac & Cheese, Stove Top stuffing, and ingredients for green bean casserole.

There’s also five pounds of russet potatoes, carrots, corn, crescent rolls, and everything from pumpkin puree to pie crusts for dessert.

Of course, there’s a catch to Amazon’s definition of a “complete” meal. Unlike a fully prepared Thanksgiving dinner, customers will need to cook everything themselves, and certain pantry staples may need to be purchased separately.

The deal comes just days after Costco unveiled its massive 30-pound Thanksgiving dinner for $200, which includes enough food to feed eight people.

Amazon is offering additional Thanksgiving savings throughout November, including frozen turkeys for as little as $0.79 per pound and fresh turkeys starting at $0.99 per pound.

Shoppers can also save 40% on premade sides between November 15 and November 26, potentially making things easier for anyone who doesn’t want to spend the holiday cooking.

Amazon’s $25 Thanksgiving meal is available to order online through November 26, while supplies last. The company is advertising free same-day delivery on qualifying orders of $25 or more in eligible areas.
