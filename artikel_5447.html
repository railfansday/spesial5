A KFC franchisee has closed dozens of stores and filed for bankruptcy

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Tasty Chick'n filed for Chapter 11 owing $15.6 million in unpaid royalties and plans to exit additional leases

Tasty Chick'n, a franchisee of Yum Brands $YUM' KFC chain, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection on Monday after closing 44 restaurants since the start of the year, according to Bloomberg.

The company has 75 KFC stores and nine dual-brand KFC/Taco Bell sites spread over 10 states, according to Bloomberg. In court papers, it said it needs Chapter 11 protection to keep Yum from ending its franchise agreement and plans to use the case to give up more leases.

Tasty Chick'n pointed to declining revenue and climbing expenses for food, labor, day-to-day operations and rent, as well as required remodels and upgrades under its KFC franchise agreement, according to Newsweek.

The franchisee owes $15.6 million in unpaid royalties, according to The Washington Times. After the two sides failed to agree on a forbearance deal, Yum sent notice on Sept. 15 that it intended to terminate the agreements, according to The Washington Times.

Tasty Chick'n reported that sales fell 11.4% in fiscal 2024 and 1.4% in fiscal 2025, with a further 2.8% drop so far in 2026, according to The Washington Times. By this year, restaurant margins had dropped to 4% from 11.8% in fiscal 2023, according to the same outlet.

The company intends to sell its assets in a single package, market by market, or in other groupings, according to The Washington Times. It is seeking $20 million in debtor-in-possession financing from MidCap Financial Trust. The company and three affiliates reported assets of $50 million to $100 million, against liabilities of $100 million to $500 million.

Court filings identify nine restaurants whose leases Tasty Chick'n will give up, according to Newsweek. Three are in Memphis, Tennessee, three are in Kentucky, two are in Arkansas and one is in Roanoke, Virginia. Newsweek's state-by-state breakdown of closures before the filing includes 19 in Tennessee and 10 in Florida.

A Yum affiliate said in a court filing on Tuesday that sudden store closures "caused significant harm to the KFC brand," according to Bloomberg. The affiliate said it had worked with Tasty Chick'n for many months.

Tasty Chick'n employs more than 1,600 people, most of them full-time, according to Bloomberg. The company filed motions to keep paying wages along with its other day-to-day expenses. The case, number 26-90831, is in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas in Houston.
