Milton Hershey School Hosts Exclusive HERSHEY Movie Premiere

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Founders Week Event Offers Students a First Look at the Film Through a Gold-Carpet Premiere Featuring Surprise Hollywood Guests, Immersive Exhibits, and Historic Artifacts

HERSHEY, Pa., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Milton Hershey School (MHS) students became the first large audience to watch the HERSHEY movie during an exclusive premiere at Founders Hall on Sunday, Nov. 15. The milestone screening placed the students who carry Milton and Catherine Hershey's legacy forward at the center of a one-of-a-kind event that concluded the school's 2026 Founders Week.

"Our students are the Hersheys' heirs and the living embodiment of Milton and Catherine's dream."

"Our students were not simply invited to see the HERSHEY movie. They were given the distinction of experiencing it first," said MHS President Peter G. Gurt. "That made the premiere especially meaningful because our students are the Hersheys' heirs and the living embodiment of Milton and Catherine's dream. There could be no more fitting audience with whom to share this story."

The premiere transformed the MHS campus into a movie extravaganza designed specifically for students. They walked a 739-foot gold carpet reflecting one of the school's signature colors, explored immersive exhibits about Milton and Catherine Hershey's lives and contributions, viewed rarely shown artifacts and costumes from the film, and took part in a custom-designed LED floor activation connected to the MHS Alma Mater. The event also featured appearances by influencers, celebrities, and actors, including Alexandra Daddario and Candace Cameron Bure, before students gathered for the screening.

The premiere capped a weeklong celebration rooted in the Hersheys' signing of the Deed of Trust in 1909 that established the school. Founders Week also included a special chapel service; a tree-planting ceremony for high school seniors; Founders Feast, a meal of gratitude served by MHS staff to all 2,200 students; a Founders Day Assembly and parade; fireworks above Founders Hall; and a Student Government Association Leadership Conference featuring Hershey entity executives.

"Being at Milton Hershey School and seeing firsthand the impact that Milton and Catherine Hershey's vision continues to have on young people is incredibly moving," said Daddario, who portrays Catherine Hershey in the HERSHEY film. "MHS is an amazing home and school for children and brings to life the very best of the Hersheys' ever-inspiring influence on the world."

Founded 117 years ago, MHS has a mission to nurture and educate children in social and financial need to lead fulfilling and productive lives. Honoring the Hersheys' wishes, its students live on campus in family-style homes led by married houseparent couples.
