Promise David and Femi Azeez decision: Fabian Hurzeler’s Brighton XI to face Craiova predicted

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Brighton boss Fabian Hurzeler has a few selection headscratchers ahead of the Conference League clash with Craiova.

Injuries continue to bite Brighton with midfielder Malick Yalcouye expected to miss "several weeks" with a knee issue. Albion are currently exploring whether to bring a replacement into the European squad. Mats Wieffer is the most likely candidate.

Olivier Boscagli, Diego Gomez and Jaouen Hadjam are also sidelined for this one.

The Seagulls are looking to hit back after their painful 2-0 Premier League loss at Hull but Hurzeler also has to manage his squad for Sunday's top flight clash against Newcastle.

Bart Verbruggen - GK: Conceded twice at Hull but was not at fault for either of McBurnie’s close range efforts. Hurzeler has the option of resting Verbruggen for Jason Steele... but the Dutch keeper has started each Conference League match so far.

Costinha - RB: The Portuguese right back is available for the Conference League despite his dismissal at Hull. He will serve his one-match suspension against Newcastle.

Pascal Struijk - CB: The £18m arrival from Leeds United is fit again after thigh issues and will likely start if Lewis Dunk is rested.

Michael Svoboda - CB: The Austrian international has impressed with his previous outings and will likely get another chance to shine against the Romanians. Regular centre backs Luka Vuskovic and Dunk could be rested.

Ferdi Kadioglu - LB: The injuries to Boscagli and Hadjam will likely see Kadioglu start on the left tonight. I think he would have played on the right ahead of Costinha, had Hadjam been fit.

Mats Wieffer - CM: The Dutchman could be a late addition to the Uefa squad following the injury to Yalcouye. Wieffer is fit again after a knee issue and needs the minutes as well.

Pascal Gross - CM: To rest or not to rest? Hurzeler said: “He's a clear team player. If he feels he can't play at his highest level because of his physical condition or tired in certain moments, he will speak up.” He starts!

Matt O’Riley - RM: The Denmark international started against Kauno Žalgiris and Jablonec and will hope to do so again.

Jack Hinshelwood - No 10: The Brighton academy graduate loves an European night at the Amex and will likely start in the No 10 role.

Femi Azeez - LW: The summer signing from Millwall could make his long-awaited debut on the left flank. Ibrahim Osman is also an option for Hurzeler.

Georginio - CF: The striker started at Hull as Charalampos Kostoulas missed training last Friday due to “a personal issue.” Expect Georginio to start this one with Promise David pushing for minutes from the bench.
