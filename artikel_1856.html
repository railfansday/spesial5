Barnsley FC 1 Sheffield Wednesday 2: Headstart costs Reds against clinical Owls

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

On a night dedicated to Weston Park's cancer work, the Reds extended that charity to their defending, and within 15 minutes, Wednesday already looked uncatchable.

Vimal Yoganathan's sixth goal in eight games ensured a proper South Yorkshire derby broke out, but the Reds are getting rather too good at nearly or semi-performances for their own comfort down in the League One relegation zone.

After Yoganathan's strike, the home side showed the fight they should be a given in games against the near-neighbours, but it was already too late.

For the visitors, it was a character test passed in front of 5,700 invigilators in the away end, Jamal Lowe scoring from their only two shots on target on a night that felt like a big step forward for their new manager, Dave Challinor.

"Three points away ole, ole," sang the packed away end and more pertinently, "Wednesday are back."

Funny how one week can totally change the mood of a football club.

The Reds actually started the better, but they are getting good at looking good in ineffectual parts of the field, and awful at the business ends.

Their fans had been in mutinous mood before the game, hundreds gathering outside the ground before kick-off to protest about the way things are being run, but there was no such defiance when they needed it early on.

Lowe – playing more of an orthodox winger role with the balance of Wednesday's unchanged side titled more towards a back four than a three than it had been the previous week – was ruthless in exploiting it.

When Barry Bannan lofted a free-kick to the back post in the ninth minute, Liam Cooper was in far too much space there. The defender headed the ball across goal, and Lowe pounced for the Owls' third set-piece goal in a week.

Five minutes later, two passes took the visitors from the halfway line to a second goal.

Callum Slattery's pass to Bannan was almost cut out but when he found Lowe, but Jubril Okedina stood off, almost daring the forward to score, so he did.

Barnsley's early work lacked that conviction.

Reyes Cleary's second-minute snapshot lacked the power to beat Joe Lumley, and Ryan Brownlie's also allowed the goalkeeper to save. Makenzie Kirk miskicked a shot when Cleary picked him out, and when Patrick Kelly ran onto a knockdown by the centre-forward, his shot lacked power.

Cleary put a shot well wide and Luca Connell hammered one into Billy Mitchell.

Ziyad Larkeche controlled a fairly routine Cieran Slicker pass over the touchline.

But a side with seven starters under the age of 23 plugged away and got back into it courtesy of one of their young leaders.

Like Okedina, Yan Valery stood off Cleary and when the winger delivered a low cross, Yoganathan arrived onto it like a centre-forward to pull a goal back.

With both sides attacking their own noisy ends, it made for an entertaining second half.

Like Barnsley at the start of the first half, Wednesday pushed the harder, without making it count.

Bannan had a shot blocked and when Slicker came out of his goal to stop former Red Davis Keillor-Dunn finishing a Bannan pass, Lowe lifted his shot onto the roof of the net.

At the other end, Barnsley centre-back Sam Gale forced his way to the byline, but his cross cleared those in the area. Larkeche put an acrobatic effort wide.

It was Wednesday who were throwing on the substitutes - a sign of their deeper resources, but also off their keenness to finally add to Lowe's second shot on target, 14 minutes in.

Despite one of those substitutes, Harry Gray, accelerating past Gale for a chance Slattery had blocked, it never came, but it did not need to because hard as Barnsley pushed – throwing centre-back Connor O'Riordan on as a late emergency striker and sending Slicker up for a stoppage-time corner – they could not find the next goal either.

Luca Connell came the closest, his free-kick tipped over in the 93rd minute.

His team are just not good enough to gift promotion wannabes like Wednesday a two-goal headstart and get away with it. Only just, you might say, but game after game that is enough.

Barnsley: Slicker; Okedina (Reid 86), Gale, Kay, Larkeche; Connell, Kelly (Johnson 86); Brownlie (O'Riordan 90), Yoganathan (Noslin 81), Cleary; Kirk (Toure 81).

Sheffield Wednesday: Lumley; Valery, Bernard (Santos 86), Cooper, Swinkels (Palmer 70); Heskey, Mitchell (Barry 70), Slattery, Bannan (Gray 78), J Lowe (Liongola 70); Keillor-Dunn.
