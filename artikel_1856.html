Arsenal: Declan Rice agrees new long-term contract as Gunners target period of dominance

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Declan Rice has agreed a new long-term contract with Arsenal.

The midfielder began talks with the club over improved terms earlier in the year and those have accelerated in recent weeks.

It is understood that a verbal agreement has now been reached and Rice will soon put pen to paper on the deal.

The long-term extension will see Rice receive a huge pay rise, taking him up to bracket of Bukayo Saka as the club’s best-paid player on around £350,000-a-week.

Rice has established himself as a central figure in Mikel Arteta’s side ever since he arrived for £105million from West Ham in 2023.

The 27-year-old secured his first piece of silverware for the Gunners last season, winning the Premier League in a campaign in which he played 69 matches for club and country.

Rice’s previous deal was set to expire in 2028 but both parties have been relaxed about the situation, even after he entered the final two years of that contract.

The England international made it clear that he wanted to stay at Arsenal, while the club never had any intention of losing him.

Rice follows Arteta in committing his future to the club. Bukayo Saka, William Saliba and Gabriel are among those to have also signed new long-term contracts.

Arsenal are keen to tie down the core of their first-team squad as they target a period of dominance over the next few years.

Discussions have been ongoing with Jurrien Timber, who like Rice would have been out of contract in 2028.

Those talks are also believed to be nearing a conclusion after an agreement was improved on an improved contract for the Dutchman.
