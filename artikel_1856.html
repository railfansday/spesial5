Prediction: Athletic Bilbao vs Espanyol

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Two camps who will make their respective returns from November’s international break on Friday night, Athletic Bilbao will welcome Espanyol to San Mames.

With the rest of Europe’s elite domestic leagues stealing centre stage this weekend, there are plenty of eye-catching betting promotions available.

Ahead of Friday’s La Liga showdown from the Basque Country, you can find all of the key Athletic Bilbao vs Espanyol H2H stats here.

Verdict: Athletic Bilbao win & Under 4.5 goals

Starting with the hosts, while Athletic Bilbao might still be searching for some recent stability, the Lions mark their return on Friday night at the heart of a European scrap. Suffering last-gasp heartbreak away at Osasuna prior to November’s international break as they eventually settled for a 1-1 stalemate in Navarre, Edin Terzic’s men will be seeking an immediate response at San Mames. Signing off October with a season-best performance as they stormed their way to an emphatic 4-0 victory against Getafe, the former Borussia Dortmund boss has seen his squad register seven points from their previous three La Liga appearances. Clinching local bragging rights against arch-rivals Real Sociedad earlier in the month as they opened November with a dramatic 2-1 victory, Bilbao know that three points on Friday night would temporarily skyrocket them into the European spots.

Both still recovering from respective knee injuries, Athletic Bilbao will once again be without the academy graduate duo of Unai Egiluz and Gorka Guruzeta. Likewise, sustaining a reported hamstring injury in the Basque derby against Real Sociedad earlier in the month, Moroccan international Maroan Sannadi remains on the sidelines.

Bagging a first La Liga strike since the start of September against Osasuna last time out, attacking talisman Nico Williams will spearhead Bilbao’s charge. In possession of Spain’s captains armband over the past fortnight, Unai Simon will start between the sticks at San Mames.

As for the visitors, while Espanyol might have signed off October with an essential 1-0 victory away at Villarreal, Eder Sarabia’s camp have shown plenty of cause for concern since opening up the 2026/27 campaign. Marking their return from November’s international break holding just a two-point buffer over the drop zone, the Parakeets fear that they could be at the heart of a relegation scrap this season. Watching their Copa del Rey adventure come to a remarkable end at the hands of fourth-tier outfit Sestao last month, Espanyol look set for what will be another stern examination when they travel to the Basque Country this weekend. In fact, their shock 1-0 win against Villarreal on October 30th is the only domestic victory that Sarabia’s side have mustered from their previous eight appearances across all competitions.

Yet to make a maiden appearance this season after undergoing hamstring surgery back in August, Espanyol will once again be without veteran marksman Kike Garcia here. However, serving a one-match La Liga suspension prior to November’s international break, 21-year-old Roger Hinojo will return to Sarabia’s travelling squad.

Completing a season-long loan switch from Burnley over the summer, Dutch defender Quilindschy Hartman will feature at the back. Notching six La Liga strikes so far this season, one-time Barcelona youngster Roberto Fernandez will lead Espanyol’s attack at San Mames.

Registering seven points in their final three La Liga appearances prior to November’s fortnight interval, Athletic Bilbao will now have their sights set on finding a route into the European spots. Gaining a reputation for their rugged success at San Mames and unbeaten in all of their previous five consecutive domestic battles in the Basque Country, Friday’s hosts should be smelling a potential opportunity here. It should also be highlighted, Bilbao have registered back-to-back wins in front of their own supporters for the first time this calendar year.

Verdict: Athletic Bilbao win & Under 4.5 goals
