Rocket Alumni Solutions Puts Recognition at the Center of School Community Engagement

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Interactive storytelling gives students, families and alumni a way to discover the people, achievements and traditions that shape their school.

BOSTON, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Rocket Alumni Solutions helps schools turn their history and achievements into a shared community experience. Serving 1,500+ schools and organizations, the company provides interactive recognition software that brings alumni stories, student accomplishments, school records and yearbooks together on campus touchscreens and the web.

A school's story includes the students who set records, the teachers who shaped generations, the volunteers who supported a program and the graduates who carried its traditions forward. Rocket gives schools a place to tell those stories with the photographs, biographies and historical context behind each name.

The company's approach centers community engagement on something personal: giving people a way to find their connection to a school and explore the contributions of others. Its recognition engine makes room for both established traditions and the achievements being added to that history today.

Recognition that reflects the whole school

Rocket's History Showcase supports recognition across academics, athletics, the arts, alumni, staff, donors and community service. Schools can organize collections around their own priorities, from a hall of fame or scholarship program to an archive of student leadership and performing arts.

Searchable profiles let visitors explore the people behind an award. Team collections can bring together rosters, photographs and season stories. Donor recognition can explain the people behind a scholarship, building or program, while alumni profiles can show students where a graduate's path began.

This flexibility gives school leaders a practical way to broaden the stories they share. An athletic induction can sit alongside academic honors, an educator tribute or a volunteer's contribution, with each collection reflecting a different part of the same community.

Rocket makes recognition available where people gather and where they live. A compatible touchscreen gives visitors a place to explore during a campus visit, reunion or induction ceremony. The web-based showcase lets alumni and families revisit those stories without returning to the building.

Digital yearbooks and photo archives add another way to discover a school's past. A returning graduate can look for a class photograph, a student can explore an earlier generation's activities, and a family can browse the history of a team or tradition. Those possibilities give schools concrete ways to use their collections throughout the year.

The archive also gives an event a lasting home. Schools can prepare profiles for an induction or assemble historical material for an anniversary, then keep the collection available after the celebration ends.

A collection that grows with the community

Rocket lets staff maintain recognition content through a browser, adding new honorees, updating records and building out historical collections over time. Unlimited content gives schools room to keep expanding the archive as more material becomes available.

A project does not require every decade of school history to be organized before work begins. Schools can start with one meaningful collection, such as an upcoming induction class, a set of yearbooks or a group of alumni profiles, and establish a plan for what comes next.

Rocket offers complimentary data entry, content preparation and project support to help bring that first collection into use. Its team works with schools on source materials, responsibilities and display setup, with 24/7 support available as the recognition program continues.

Recognition is part of Rocket's wider School Engagement System. Alongside History Showcase, Rocket offers Media Management to organize source material, Digital Signage for timely campus messages and Social Media Graphics for branded visual communications. Hardware and Data Entry & Project Management support the work of bringing a project to campus.

These offerings address different parts of a school's storytelling effort: maintaining the archive, finding the right photograph, sharing a current achievement and giving visitors a place to explore. Schools can choose a starting point around the people they want to recognize and the material they already have.

Rocket Alumni Solutions partners with more than 1,500 schools and organizations, including the PGA Tour, New York University, Arizona State University and Virginia Tech. The company helps communities celebrate achievements, preserve their history and recognize the people who shaped them.

Rocket Alumni Solutions' touchscreen software maintains an 80 Net Promoter Score, reflecting exceptional ease of use and customer satisfaction. Its interactive touchscreen software helps schools create digital Halls of Fame, donor recognition walls, record boards and campus recognition displays that celebrate community achievements and preserve institutional history.

Rocket Alumni Solutions serves schools and organizations of all sizes. The platform offers pre-built templates and remote content management. Schools receive ongoing support as their recognition collections grow.
