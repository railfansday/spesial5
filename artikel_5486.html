ModuleMD Continues to Strengthen Its Position in Allergy EHR Innovation with a New Generation of AI-Powered Technology

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Following strong client interest in its new WISE Touch AI experience, ModuleMD showcases its expanding technology ecosystem at ACAAI 2026, bringing clinical intelligence, automated workflows and specialty-focused innovation together for allergy practices.

PHOENIX, Nov. 21, 2026 /PRNewswire/ --  Allergy practices are facing a new set of expectations. Clinical documentation needs to move faster. Front-office teams are managing increasingly complex administrative workflows. Patients expect greater transparency, while practices need technology that understands the demands of specialty care.

For more than 25 years, ModuleMD has built its technology around these realities. At the 2026 American College of Allergy, Asthma & Immunology (ACAAI) Annual Scientific Meeting in Phoenix, Arizona, the company brought that commitment into focus, showcasing a new generation of AI-powered capabilities designed to advance how allergy and immunology practices deliver care and manage their operations.

At Booth #1713, the conversation centered on the continued development of the ModuleMD EHR platform, particularly its latest WISE Touch AI experience. The showcase also highlighted innovations in automated skin testing, clinical documentation, conversational practice intelligence and AI-powered front-office operations.

The momentum extends beyond the conference floor. Following its recent WISE Touch first-look webinar, ModuleMD has seen considerable interest and positive feedback from existing clients, reflecting growing enthusiasm for an EHR experience designed around the way specialty practices actually work.

An EHR Built Around Allergy. Now, With AI at Its Core.

Specialty healthcare technology requires more than a general-purpose EHR with a few specialty features added to it. Allergy practices have distinct clinical, operational and revenue cycle requirements, from immunotherapy and skin testing to biologics management and recurring patient workflows.

ModuleMD's approach has been to build around these requirements from the ground up.

Its latest development, WISE Touch AI, brings a redesigned clinical experience and multiple AI-powered capabilities into one connected platform. Rather than treating artificial intelligence as a separate application, ModuleMD is integrating it into the everyday work of clinicians and practice teams.

The result is an EHR experience built around three important areas: clinical productivity, administrative automation and access to practice intelligence.

A redesigned clinical experience brings AI-assisted documentation, clinical decision support and CPT suggestions closer to the point of care. Designed around simpler interactions and fewer manual steps, WISE Touch AI aims to reduce the administrative effort involved in documenting and managing patient encounters.

AI-powered clinical documentation with JOSHâ¢ takes this further. JOSH converts natural conversations into structured clinical notes, helping clinicians spend less time typing and more time with patients. Supporting more than 75 languages, the technology brings conversational documentation into the clinical workflow.

ModuleMD Front Desk extends AI into practice operations through five specialized agents working across patient intake, scheduling, insurance eligibility and estimates, authorizations and referrals, coordinated by a supervisor agent. The objective is to reduce repetitive administrative work while keeping essential front-office processes connected.

ModuleMDQâ¢ adds another dimension through conversational practice intelligence. Instead of navigating multiple screens to locate information, practice teams can interact with their practice data using natural language and get answers that help them move work forward.

Together, these capabilities reflect a broader direction for the ModuleMD EHR: clinical workflows, administrative operations and practice intelligence working as one system.

Innovation at ModuleMD also extends into some of the most specific workflows in allergy care.

Skin testing remains a fundamental part of allergy diagnostics, but measuring and documenting wheal and flare reactions can involve repetitive manual effort. ModuleMD's SkinSightâ¢ technology introduces image-based AI measurement into this process.

Using images captured on a device already available to the practice, SkinSight detects and measures wheal and flare reactions, with structured results and timestamped images transferred directly into the patient's chart.

The integration of measurement, documentation and clinical records helps reduce manual data entry while giving clinicians the ability to review and interpret results. It also supports documentation for CPT 95004.

Developed with allergy-specific workflows in mind, SkinSight reflects an important distinction in ModuleMD's approach to AI: technology should address actual clinical tasks, not simply introduce another layer of software.

Client Interest Signals a New Chapter for the ModuleMD EHR

Product innovation is only meaningful when it makes a difference to the people using it.

ModuleMD's recent WISE Touch first-look webinar gave existing clients an opportunity to explore the new clinical experience and its connected AI capabilities. The response reflected strong interest in the direction of the platform, particularly the opportunity to bring clinical documentation, front-office automation and practice intelligence closer together.

For ModuleMD, this engagement is part of an ongoing relationship with the specialty practices that use its technology every day.

The company continues to develop its EHR around client needs, evolving clinical workflows and the operational realities of independent specialty practices. That means looking beyond individual features to how different parts of a practice work together.

The objective isn't simply to add more functionality. It is to make the technology more useful at every point where clinicians and their teams interact with it.

More Than an EHR: A Connected Platform for Allergy Practices

ModuleMD's platform brings together Electronic Health Records, Practice Management, Patient Engagement, Revenue Cycle Management, Allergy Immunotherapy, Biologics and Infusion Management.

This connected foundation is particularly important for allergy practices, where clinical care and practice operations are closely linked. From patient encounters and skin testing to immunotherapy workflows, biologics administration and revenue cycle management, the technology needs to support the full operational picture.

With more than 25 years of specialty healthcare experience, continued investment in AI and a platform shaped by the needs of its clients, ModuleMD is building on its position in allergy-focused healthcare technology.

Its presence at ACAAI 2026 offered a closer look at that direction: an EHR that continues to develop, AI that works within real practice workflows, and technology designed with the specific demands of allergy care at its center.

As specialty healthcare moves toward more connected and intelligent systems, ModuleMD's focus remains on making technology work better for the practices that depend on it.
