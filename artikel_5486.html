Dow futures rise 323 points as Nvidia rebounds and wholesale inflation comes in light

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The October producer price index rose 0.3% on the month, while the three major indexes are on track for weekly gains

Nvidia $NVDA stock climbed over 2% in Friday's premarket session, while Dow Jones Industrial Average futures gained 323 points, or 0.6%. S&P 500 futures and Nasdaq $NDAQ-100 futures each added 0.7%.

The October producer price index, which gauges inflation at the wholesale level, rose 0.3% from September and 6.5% from a year earlier. Economists surveyed by Dow Jones had forecast a 0.4% increase in October's headline PPI, according to CNBC.

Nvidia's gain followed a 4.3% drop on Thursday after CEO Jensen Huang stepped down. Huang departed after reports of extramarital affairs, one of them reportedly with a woman whom U.S. intelligence officials suspect of being a spy for China, according to CNBC.

Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO, according to CNBC. The company's board has begun a search for a permanent replacement, drawing on candidates from within and beyond its ranks, CNBC said in the same report.

The Dow, S&P 500 and Nasdaq each declined on Thursday. A jump in oil prices also weighed on stocks after President Donald Trump posted on Truth Social, "We don't need a deal with Iran." The remark raised concerns that the U.S.-Iran war might be prolonged.

Oil prices held roughly steady on Friday. West Texas Intermediate crude futures changed hands at more than $88 a barrel, while Brent crude futures were priced over $96.

Despite Thursday's decline, the major averages remain on pace to finish the week higher. The Nasdaq is up 3.2% for the week, and the S&P 500 is tracking a 2.9% gain. The Dow is on track to add just under 2%. The Dow, S&P 500 and Nasdaq are each on track to post their strongest week since early August, according to CNBC.
