NHTSA is probing more than 1 million Hondas over stop-start engine failures

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The agency received 701 complaints about engines that failed to restart in 2024-2026 Odyssey, Passport, Ridgeline, Pilot and Acura MDX models

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration began a formal engineering analysis of 1,033,632 Honda $HMC vehicles in the U.S. on Monday, citing engines that would not start back up once the automatic stop-start system had cut them off, according to Reuters.

NHTSA has logged 701 complaints tied to the stop-start function on Honda's Odyssey, Passport, Ridgeline and Pilot from the 2024-2026 model years, along with the Acura MDX, according to Reuters.

The stop-start system aims to cut down on fuel consumption. It shuts the engine off once a vehicle has fully stopped, then is meant to fire it back up as soon as the driver eases a foot off the brake. Drivers have reported that their engines stayed dead once the system switched them off, according to Reuters.

NHTSA already has a separate inquiry open on comparable engine trouble tied to stop-start systems in certain Honda models, according to Reuters. That earlier probe encompasses all of the vehicles now under review apart from those from the 2026 model year.

The agency said the two groups exhibit comparable failures, yet the newly investigated vehicles seem to stem from distinct underlying problems and do not react to repairs in the same way, according to Reuters.

The probe follows a Honda recall of 635,808 vehicles in the U.S. to fix a software problem that can disable power steering assistance, leaving the vehicles harder to steer, according to Reuters.

Honda has issued a series of recalls this year. In June, the automaker recalled 1,049,883 U.S. vehicles over a flat tire repair kit defect that could cause a sealant bottle cap to detach and strike the user or bystanders. Honda also recalled more than 440,000 Honda Odyssey minivans in April over a software flaw that could cause side and curtain airbags to deploy without warning.
