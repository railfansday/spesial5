Dave & Buster’s changes its name to Doom & Buster’s with new Marvel games and drinks

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Dave & Buster’s is temporarily changing its name to Doom & Buster’s as part of a nationwide takeover celebrating the upcoming release of Marvel Studios’ Avengers: Doomsday.

The arcade and entertainment chain announced the promotion on November 4, revealing plans to transform its locations with Marvel-themed games, exclusive merchandise, collectible prizes, and a new lineup of cocktails and mocktails.

The promotion begins rolling out this week before the full takeover launches on November 16. It will run through January 31, 2027, giving fans a chance to experience the themed attractions before and after Avengers: Doomsday arrives in theaters on December 18.

As part of the promotion, participating Dave & Buster’s locations will feature dedicated Doomsday Zones, complete with themed entrances, Marvel games, signage, and interactive experiences.

Several attractions begin rolling out November 4, including MARVEL Cosmic Invasion, a four-player beat ’em up that lets players select two Marvel superheroes and switch between their abilities during combat.

Avengers: Roller Race & Battle is another four-player experience that challenges guests to race against each other while defeating a villain.

The promotion also introduces limited-time Avengers-inspired cotton candy flavors, themed merchandise, and collectible Power Cards featuring characters such as Thor, Doctor Doom, and Yelena.

Alongside the new games, Doom & Buster’s is introducing five limited-time beverages, including three alcoholic options and two mocktails.

The cocktail lineup features Doomsday’s Dominion, made with Smirnoff vodka, melon liqueur, pineapple, lemon-lime soda, and a Black Magic sidecar.

The Multiversal Mule combines vodka, blue orange liqueur, dragon fruit, and ginger beer, complete with edible shimmer and an LED glow cube.

Guests can also order the Fantastic Flight, which includes four miniature cocktails featuring different flavors inspired by Marvel superheroes.

For those looking for alcohol-free options, Panther’s Potion combines cherry, ginger beer, and citrus sour with a blue shimmer sidecar, while Captain Punch features blue raspberry, coconut, pineapple, and lemon-lime soda.

All five beverages will be available from November 16 through January 31.

The promotion also includes a new event called DOOMHOUR, which gives D&B Rewards members the opportunity to earn double tickets on green-swipe games during randomly selected one-hour periods.

Members will receive a notification through the D&B Rewards app 24 hours before the next DOOMHOUR, followed by another notification revealing the exact time on the day of the event.

“When one of the biggest movies of the year is coming, you don’t put up a poster. You bend the knee,” said Jeremy Tucker, Chief Marketing Officer at Dave & Buster’s.

“We gave Doom our name. We gave him the floor. We gave him the bar. Doom & Buster’s is a world fans can only walk into with us, and it’s built for the thing we do better than anyone: bringing people together to play, compete, and be part of the action.”

Dave & Buster’s isn’t the only chain celebrating the upcoming Marvel movie. KFC also announced an Avengers: Doomsday collaboration featuring black burgers, green mayo, themed drinks, and collectible figurines. However, while the KFC promotion will launch in more than 100 countries beginning November 9, it won’t be available in the US.
