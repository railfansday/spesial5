Air India Cancels 17 Nonstop Routes: See Full List Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Air India has made several changes to its network this year. The Indian flag carrier launched a three-weekly service between Mumbai and Toronto and plans to add Osaka Kansai to its international network next year.

However, rising fuel prices and operational constraints stemming from the closure of Pakistani airspace have forced the airline to scale back parts of its network. At the same time, it has also transferred several routes (mostly domestic or short- to medium-haul) to its low-cost subsidiary, Air India Express.

Unsurprisingly, the list includes several European routes from Delhi's Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL), which Simple Flying has previously reported on. These include the Star Alliance member’s services to Birmingham Airport (BHX), which ended earlier this month, Vienna International Airport (VIE) in Austria, and Copenhagen Airport (CPH) in Denmark.

Some of the other international cuts are in the Middle East, mostly on routes to Dubai International Airport (DXB) and one to King Khalid International Airport (RUH) in Riyadh. As for the domestic cuts, the Indian flag carrier has largely handed over these routes to its subsidiary. The Tata Group consolidated its four airlines into two in 2024, merging Vistara with Air India and AIX Connect with Air India Express. Under this structure, the Star Alliance member appears to be focusing more on its full-service operations.

Meanwhile, it has been handing over several price-sensitive, point-to-point routes to its low-cost subsidiary. Air India Express has taken over the group's operations at several non-metro airports, including Aurangabad, Bhopal, Coimbatore, Jamnagar, Jodhpur, Madurai and Tirupati. The airline now serves 48 domestic and 16 international destinations.

For the current winter season, according to schedule data from aviation analytics company Cirium, the low-cost carrier has scheduled 78,202 flights, up 5.1% from the 74,374 flights it offered last winter. It has more than 70 daily international flights on its schedule and offers up to 390 daily flights across its domestic network.

Three of these are international long haul routes.

Nevertheless, we have seen some interesting additions to Air India's international network this year. In May, the airline launched five weekly flights between Delhi and Hanoi's Noi Bai International Airport (HAN) in Vietnam. The following month, it introduced four weekly flights between Mumbai's Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) and Tokyo Haneda International Airport (HND) using its Boeing 787-8s.

In addition, last month, it started a new winter seasonal service between Mumbai and Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ). From February 2, 2027, it will also operate four weekly flights between Delhi and Osaka's Kansai International Airport (KIX) using its Boeing 787-8s. It will become the only airline offering nonstop flights between India and Osaka.
