Galaxy Z Fold 8 is $1,499 for Black Friday; its new lowest price ever, just not for everyone

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

A strange deal over on Samsung.com is offering the Galaxy Z Fold 8 for $1,499, a new low price for the hit foldable, but it’s not showing up for all users.

Samsung’s new Galaxy Z Fold 8 is usually sold for $1,899 which, in itself, isn’t a bad price for the device. But it’s been available with some kind of discount at basically all times since it launched. And, this week, we’re seeing a new low price that brings the foldable down to $1,499.

The discount is available on Samsung.com and cuts $400 off of the price of a new Galaxy Z Fold 8 in any configuration. That brings the 256GB model down to $1,499.99, as pictured below.

Firstly, this is a discount if you buy the Fold 8 without a trade-in. Moving to a trade charges the fully $1,899 and applies your credit to that.

The other is that, strangely, this $400 discount isn’t showing for everyone. As noted when we covered the previous all-time-low earlier this week, $1,549, Samsung.com only shows a $350 discount to some users, and there’s no rhyme or reason to it. As for why this is the case, we’re not sure, but it’s unfortunately not uncommon for Samsung.com sales to act a bit inconsistently like this.

Whatever the case, if you’re hunting for a Galaxy Z Fold 8, now is very much the time. Whether you’re paying $1,549 or $1,499, it’s still a killer discount. Notably, the Amazon deal still lets you stack a trade-in on top for up to $980 off.
