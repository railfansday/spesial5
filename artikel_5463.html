2026 BCDA Grand: Collection at R Street

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

In Washington, D.C.’s historic Shaw neighborhood, The Collection at R Street reveals how thoughtful infill development and adaptive reuse can expand housing opportunities while preserving neighborhood character. The 23-unit project embraces a “missing middle” housing strategy, bridging the gap between single-family homes and large multifamily developments through a mix of housing types within a single urban block.

At its center is the adaptive reuse of a 19th-century schoolhouse that had most recently served as offices. Rather than demolish the vacant structure, the team transformed it into 12 condominium residences, preserving key historic features while introducing modern living spaces. Original masonry, window openings, and roof forms were restored, while high ceilings, exposed roof trusses, and even historic blackboard walls were incorporated into the new design. Upgraded building systems and an enhanced envelope improve energy performance and ensure the building’s long-term viability.

The development also includes four new rowhouses on a former parking lot and a freestanding duplex on a triangular parcel. Both new-construction components draw inspiration from Shaw’s historic residential fabric through their scale, materiality, and relationship to the street.

By repurposing an existing landmark and converting underutilized land into housing, the project shows how preservation and sustainable development can work hand in hand. It delivers a diverse mix of homes in a transit-rich neighborhood while demonstrating how adaptive reuse, contextual design, and environmental stewardship can extend a building’s civic legacy for generations to come
