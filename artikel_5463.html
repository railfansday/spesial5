Qantas is said to be picking Boeing for most of its next long-range jet order

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The airline is close to a decision that would include some Airbus A350s, but Qantas said no decisions have been made

Qantas Airways intends to give Boeing $BA the majority of a coming purchase of roughly 20 long-haul aircraft, according to Bloomberg.

The Australian flag carrier has nearly settled on a choice that would span Boeing 787s and some Airbus A350s, according to Bloomberg. The decision is not yet final, and the situation could shift, Bloomberg reports.

"We are still in discussion with the relevant aircraft manufacturers as part of our widebody fleet planning and no decisions have been made," Qantas said in a statement, according to Bloomberg. Boeing referred questions to Qantas, and an Airbus spokesperson said the company does not discuss its commercial talks with customers.

The order fits into a broader Qantas effort to modernize its fleet, which involves phasing out its Airbus A380s and acquiring jets that burn less fuel, according to Bloomberg. Fuel prices have surged because of the Iran war, which has given the plan renewed momentum.

Qantas builds its long-range fleet around the A350, a family that features an ultra-long-range version engineered to fly for 22 hours, according to Bloomberg. The airline is scaling up its operations with the 787 Dreamliner.

In September, Qantas announced nonstop Sydney-New York service starting in mid-2028. The flights are to use A350-1000ULR aircraft from a 12-plane order, with the first delivery expected in April 2027, subject to regulatory approvals and aircraft certification.

Boeing has also drawn interest from other carriers. Malaysia Aviation Group, the parent of Malaysia Airlines, announced an order for 10 Boeing 787-10 Dreamliners on Wednesday, with options for six more. Boeing said the purchase is to be finalized. The order is the group's first purchase of widebodies directly from Boeing since 1995, and deliveries are scheduled between 2032 and 2035.
