Take-Two stock jumps more than 6% as GTA VI launches to near-perfect reviews

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Take-Two stock closed at $216.61 on Wednesday but remains down about 15% for 2026 as Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI reaches players

Take-Two Interactive $TTWO stock gained over 6% in Wednesday trading and finished the session at $216.61, with the first critic verdicts on “Grand Theft Auto VI,” the newest release from its Rockstar Games label, now public.

Rockstar Games, a Take-Two publishing label, announced the launch of “Grand Theft Auto VI” on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X $TWTR|S. The game is sold through the PlayStation Store, Microsoft $MSFT Store, Rockstar Games Store and other retailers.

The game earned a Metacritic score of 99, tying Nintendo’s 1998 title “The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time” for the top spot on the site’s all-time leaderboard, according to Fox Business, citing Reuters.

Rollout began at midnight in international time zones, ahead of the U.S. release on Thursday, according to Fox Business, citing Reuters. Analytics firm Sensor Tower estimates sales of about 17 million copies, worth more than $1.65 billion, through Wednesday, according to Fox Business.

Take-Two stock remains down about 15% for 2026 despite Wednesday’s gain, according to Fox Business.

Joost van Dreunen, an adjunct professor at NYU Stern School of Business, compared the launch to a major film release. "It's the Star Wars of this generation, right?" he told Reuters, according to Fox Business. "It's a synchronized, shared event. So, millions of people will all want to be there the first couple of days, the first few weeks of experiencing it as it goes live."

"We are incredibly grateful for everyone's support, and we look forward to many more years of sharing these experiences with you," Sam Houser, president and founder of Rockstar Games, said in a statement.

On Nov. 9, Take-Two reported fiscal second-quarter net bookings of $1.60 billion, down 18% from a year earlier, and reiterated its fiscal 2027 outlook for net bookings of $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion. The company listed Nov. 19 as the release date for “Grand Theft Auto VI” in its lineup of announced titles. Fiscal 2027 ends March 31, 2027.
