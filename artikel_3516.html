Telegram CEO Says The ‘Least Realistic’ Thing About GTA 6 Is That Nobody Uses His App To Commit Crimes

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The lawyers for Pavel Durov have got to be going through it right now. The CEO of Telegram is one of the many millions of people who seem to be enjoying GTA 6. However, he has a few quibbles about the game’s realism. Late last week he posted on X, “The least realistic thing about GTA VI is that Jason and Lucia run a whole criminal operation without a single Telegram group.”

Telegram is a popular messaging app used worldwide. It functions similarly to Signal and allows for encrypted messaging, making it an attractive chat service for people who need to communicate privately including journalists, protestors, and labor organizers. Those features also make Telegram a hotspot for illegal activity.

Telegram has an infamous reputation for being the go-to app for crimes. Scratch some kind of criminal network of unsavory people like white nationalists or cryptoscammers, and you’re likely going to find a Telegram group chat. The app is so notorious for being Crime Facebook that in 2024 Durov was indicted in France on 12 chargesÂ for allegedly not doing enough to curb the drug trafficking and distribution of child sexual assault abuse materials taking place on Telegram. Earlier this year, Russia charged Durov with facilitating terrorism for failing to remove information allegedly used to target Russian soldiers, infrastructure, and civilians in the Russia-Ukraine war.

Durov has said Telegram actively works to remove illegal content from the app and has denied claims that it’s some kind of haven for criminals. Which is why it’s really weird that he’s salty-posting on X about how the two most famous fictional criminals in the world right now aren’t using his app to do their criming. He’s facing all this litigation for allegedly not doing enough about his app being the crime place and he just tweets an acknowledgement that he can’t even conceive of fictional crime being done without Telegram’s help.
