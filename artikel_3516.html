Pokémon Go Wild Area 2026 event schedule, habitat spawns, and bonuses

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Everything you need to know for the Wild Area 2026 event

Everything you need to know for the Wild Area 2026 event

Pokémon Go's big global Wild Area 2026 event is finally here, running on Nov. 14 and 15 from 10 a.m. until 6 p.m. in your local time. This event features the debut of Gigantamax Duraludon, as well as Dynamax Dialga and Palkia. Safari Balls and mighty Pokémon also make a return from the previous years' Wild Area events, which means you can find some powerful Pokémon roaming about.

Additionally, you can find a costumed Machamp in a pretty nifty jacket in three-star raids, and quite a few legacy moves make their return for a limited time during this event. Black Kyurem and White Kyurem will also be making a return during this event, so it's a good chance to stock up on their Fusion Energy. As a reminder, these are powerful Pokémon with Adventure Effects, so it's definitely worth having at least one of each.
