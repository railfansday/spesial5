After her serial killer antics in the previous outing, Rhiannon is on a cover up mission, but with a copycat killer on the loose, no sooner is one body hidden than another drops. But her biggest enemy is her own conscience, as looking at deeds similar to hers from afar causes Rhiannon to question her supposedly righteous crusade of violence.

As if that wasn’t enough to deal with, there’s also problems at work thanks to a new, demanding boss (Taj Atwal), while Rhiannon’s sister (Alexandra Dowling) is pestering her to hurry up with her part in selling the family home. But it’s not all bad, there’s a dishy new doctor (Rish Shah) on the scene giving the deadly avenger plenty of attention.

But, in typical fashion, just as Rhiannon is ready to make a fresh start, her boyfriend AJ’s mother (Tamsin Greig) arrives in town. That’s the same AJ that Rhiannon murdered in the season 1 finale. And she’s not the only one sniffing around, with detective Marina (Leah Harvey) more dogged than ever in her determination to pin Rhiannon down.

It’s set to be another great outing of pitch black comedy and utter carnage, and with Purnell at the top of her game following Fallout, it's sure to be a thriller of a season.

Season 2 of Sweetpea arrives on Thursday, November 26 in the UK and Australia. A release date for North America is TBC.

Sweetpea is based on the book series of the same name by author CJ Skuse. While season 2 may share some elements with book 2, In Bloom, the show has diverged from the text to tell its own story.
