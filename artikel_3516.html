GTA 6 Has A Hilarious Riff On Easter Eggs

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Now that Rockstar's GTA 6 is available, people are discovering all manner of things in the game's gigantic open world. One such finding is that GTA 6 has its own take on Easter eggs, and Rockstar was quite literal.

Twitter user xGarbett posted a video of Jason climbing to the top of a cell tower where there is a bird's nest with eggs in it. Except these aren't normal eggs. They're painted to look like Easter eggs, and Jason can throw one of them (with an impressively tight spiral), sending it hurtling through the air until it crashes down on a scummy-sounding real estate agent or lawyer of some type.

xGarbett said in a follow-up post that it took about seven minutes to climb the tower, so unlocking this Easter egg will take some persistence.

Given the scale of GTA 6's world, there are no doubt going to be countless more Easter eggs that players discover as they make their way through Leonida.

Another secret that players have already discovered is that Bigfoot is back, and he's very smelly.

GTA 6 officially launches for everyone on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Meanwhile, you can check out our GTA 6 guides hub for more details on how to make the most out of your trip to Vice City.
