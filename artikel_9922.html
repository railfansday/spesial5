It's OK to get excited about a second remake of a 60 year old TV series considered one of the greatest sci-fi fantasy series of all time.

The original was created and starred Patrick McGoohan, who also directed five of the episodes.

One man with a vision created a work of art that has outlasted countless other series.

Note that the first episode is basically setup, some of the episodes are better than others, and about a third are completely kickass.

The sci-fi setting has aged a little, but at the time the technology was amazing for it's creativity and imagination. Cordless phone handsets that you could pick up and talk into, automatic doors and lights, universal surveillance supporting visible and infrared, non-torture drug-based interrogation... and a "retrieval mechanism" that was so bizarre it was terrifying.

Most of the good episodes rely on psychological drama, and completely knock it out of the park. The series finale is open to multiple interpretations.

It's well worth it to see an episode or two in the evenings at home, on a big TV over the course of several days. (And not, for example, on your phone while you're on the subway.)
