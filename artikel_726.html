Ella Langley Wins Entertainer of the Year, Capping a Dominant Night at 2026 CMA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Langley officially went five-for-five on the CMA Awards' live broadcast after taking home the evening's top prize.

It was a nonstop parade to and from the podium for Ella Langley at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena on Wednesday night (Nov. 18) — as the crossover country star won in all five televised categories where she was nominated, including the evening’s final prize, entertainer of the year.

“At this point, I’m sick of me,” she began her fifth and final speech of the evening, acknowledging her ubiquity at the ceremonies. She went on to explain how much the award meant to her: “I have watched these awards since I was a little kid, and the award I cared most about was song of the year and entertainer of the year… [there] hasn’t been a song on the radio or in my ears at all where I wasn’t imagining what that song would sound like in front of a crowd, what I would want to do with that guitar solo, maybe I could play it — we’re figuring it out still…”

While trying to figure out something new to say following her four previous speeches, she decided to thank her live band, specifically singling out her lead guitarist and musical director Ben Flanders. “He got in the van for $150 a show,” she explained of Flanders. “I took him from a full-time touring gig with a salary, and… I still don’t know why he decided to do it but I’m grateful he did. I’m grateful for my tour managers, every person that has been out on the road… ”

As she continued raving about what a special night these CMA Awards were for her, she was interrupted by host Lainey Wilson pushing around a shimmering broom on stage. “What did I tell you?” she remarked to Langley. “I told you I was gonna bring you a bedazzled broom, because I knew you was gonna sweep, girl!” (“I love how weird she is,” was Langley’s response.)

All in all, Langley took home six prizes for her troubles: entertainer of the year, album of the year (Dandelion), female vocalist of the year, and single, song and music video of the year (all for “Choosin’ Texas”). She also performed an unplugged version of her current Billboard Hot 100 top 20 hit “Loving Life Again,” and teamed up with her Dandelion collaborator Miranda Lambert for a performance of their duet “Snakeskin Boots.”
