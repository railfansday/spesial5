Dua Lipa, Tame Impala, SZA and Daddy Yankee to Headline 2027 Coachella Festival

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The music festival takes place April 9-11 and April 16-18, 2027.

The mystery is over: the lineup and headliners have been revealed for next year’s Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival in Indio, California.

The headliners set to hit the stage for two weekends, April 9-11 and April 16-18, 2027, are Dua Lipa on Friday, Tame Impala on Saturday and SZA on Sunday. Daddy Yankee has also been tapped as a fourth top-billed headliner to showcase a special production.

Other artists for Friday include Madonna, Zara Larsson, Steve Lacey, JISOO, Kehlani and Leon Thomas. As for Saturday, Phoebe Bridgers, Weezer, Tiësto, Martin Garrix, Role Model and Adéla are set to take to the Coachella stages. And finally, on Sunday, other performers include Don Toliver, Dom Dolla, Kygo, The Neighbourhood and Becky G. See the full lineup below. The official Coachella Instagram account wrote in the post’s caption, “The return to innocence.”

The 2027 headliners follow the chart-topping artists who headlined the 2026 iteration of the famed music festival, which included Justin Bieber, Sabrina Carpenter, Karol G and Anyma. Last year, Carpenter delivered a theatrical production that featured a dazzling, vintage Hollywood-style set and several celebrity cameos. As for Bieber, he brought a more intimate set, leaning heavily on his Swag era while also playing some classic hits via YouTube clips from his laptop onstage. Karol G, who became the first Latina solo artist to headline the festival’s main stage, brought the energy back up on Sunday with a visually stunning and electric performance.

Passes for Coachella 2027 go on sale Nov. 13 at 12 p.m. PT. YouTube also returns for its 15th year as the exclusive livestream partner for both weekends.
