How to Watch ‘Southern Charm’ Season 12 Online: The Bravo Reality Series Returns Tonight

Bravo is heading back to Charleston. “Southern Charm” returns for its 12th season on Thursday, Nov. 12, bringing another round of romantic entanglements, fractured friendships and Southern social drama.

Season 12 sees the return of longtime cast members Craig Conover, Austen Kroll, Shep Rose and Madison LeCroy, alongside Venita Aspen, Salley Carson, Molly O’Connell, Rodrigo Reyes and Whitner Slagsvol. Newcomer Allie Newman joins the group, while Patricia Altschul, Whitney Sudler-Smith, Charley Manley and Taylor Ann Green are also expected to make appearances.

The Season 12 trailer also teases a potentially explosive revelation involving rumors that one of the cast members has a secret child. Meanwhile, the group’s increasingly complicated romantic dynamics threaten to derail their attempts at reconciliation.

The premiere arrives amid a broader expansion of Bravo’s “Southern Charm” universe. Earlier this month, the network launched “Charmed by a Southern Monster,” its first original scripted microdrama, starring Conover and Nicole Mattox. The short-form series, which debuted Nov. 9 exclusively on the Peacock mobile app, follows a corporate fixer who becomes romantically entangled with a mysterious heir accused of murder.

New episodes of “Southern Charm” air Thursdays at 9 p.m. ET/PT on Bravo, with next-day streaming on Peacock. Stream the premiere tonight on Philo.
