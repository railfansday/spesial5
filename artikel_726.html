Penge claims stunning maiden PGA Tour win at RSM Classic

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Three-time DP World Tour winner Marco Penge was in danger of losing his PGA Tour card after a 2026 season disrupted by a viral illness; the 28-year-old Englishman secures his card in stunning fashion with his maiden triumph at year-ending RSM Classic in thrilling four-way playoff finish

English golfer Marco Penge claimed a stunning maiden win on the PGA Tour as he emerged victorious from a four-way playoff at the season-ending RSM Classic.

Penge, a three-time winner on the DP World Tour, had gone into the tournament at risk of losing his PGA Tour card for next year after a rookie season disrupted by a viral illness placed him outside of the top 100.

With a top-four finish required to retain his spot, the 28-year-old found form at the best possible time at the Sea Island Golf Club this week, and a final-round 65 earned him a spot alongside Haotong Li of China and Americans Michael Thorbjornsen and Max Greyserman in a playoff decider - all four locked at 16 under.

Penge's five-under 65 on the Seaside Course included six birdies, including one at the last, and a single bogey, while he birdied 18 again to clinch victory in the playoff after nailing his approach to 10 feet.

"I always seem to play my best golf when my back's up against the wall," Penge said following his stunning win.

"I've been through this before on the DP World Tour, keeping my card in the last event and dealing with adversity and coming back from health issues, all that stuff.

"So I knew I could do it, I just wasn't sure I ever had the game this week to be able to do it and thankfully I did."

Rasmus Hojgaard, who had held a share of the lead - on 13 under with Thorbjornsen - heading into the weather-delayed final round on Monday, ultimately finish tied fifth.

The Ryder Cup star posted a disappointing final round of 69 to end two shots off the pace on 14 under, level with Jackson Koivun.

Fellow Americans Harris English, Nick Dunlap and five-time major winner Brooks Koepka finished tied-seventh, ninth and tied-10th, respectively.

Denmark's Rasmus Neergaard-Petersen also finished in the top 10.
