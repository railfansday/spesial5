Geico unites brand icons in holiday ad mixing human craft, nostalgia

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Let Marketing Dive's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

A stop-motion narrative video complements the insurance firm’s first appearance with a float at the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade.

Geico is making a more concentrated push around the holidays with a festive holiday ad and Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade float. The insurance firm is celebrating its 90th anniversary this year, and both the float and the narrative-driven video bring together a cast of characters drawn from a legacy of memorable TV commercials. The Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade itself is promoting its centennial, which has attracted additional brand interest this year.

“How 15% Saved Christmas” aligns with a larger trend of marketers focusing on content that seeks to stoke a sense of nostalgia in viewers. The effort uses stop-motion animation recalling Rankin/Bass holiday classics while leaning into fondness for mascots such as the Gecko and Caveman. Following the Thanksgiving Day Parade, the ad will air in theaters across 12 markets through Dec. 27. Geico plans to run additional holiday creative, including a seasonal spin on its “Hump Day” ad, during streaming and football programming.

Geico’s team of animators sketched the setting and core characters for “How 15% Saved Christmas” and the puppets were made by hand. AI was used in the pre-visualization phase while the background environments were produced with a hybrid approach combining AI generated-imagery with practical sets to create the appearance of a matte painting.

The renewed emphasis on human craft among marketers has been interpreted as a response to growing AI backlash. A number of other brands, including Chewy, Prada and Levi’s, are also leveraging old-school animation techniques for their holiday campaigns this year. Coca-Cola, which ran AI-generated campaigns the past two years, is pivoting back to ads featuring real humans.

This is the first time the Geico characters have appeared together in a holiday campaign, which tells the story of a town that has seen its Christmas spirit hit an all-time low. Santa is able to reach Maxwell the Pig over a ham radio — as in, a radio shaped like a literal leg of ham — with a message around saving Christmas and 15%, a key part of Geico’s messaging. There are also 15 hidden Easter eggs in “How 15% Saved Christmas” that nod to past Geico work.

Martin, the brand’s longtime agency, developed the campaign, which was created in partnership with production company Passion Pictures Animation, stop-motion specialist studio Screen Novelties and Mackinnon & Saunders. The float was unveiled in September and has been promoted through a 10-week, cross-country tour in the lead up to Thanksgiving.

"Geico has always been an entertainment brand, and people still quote our characters years after they were first introduced," said Geico CMO Arianna Orpello in a press statement. "This season, we wanted to give people something they could simply enjoy.”

Orpello became CMO of Geico in January, joining the insurance marketer from Goldman Sachs.
