Reddit publishes guide to Super Bowl engagement trends

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

Brands looking to engage with subreddit communities should begin well ahead of the 2027 game in order to map out effective approaches, per the platform.

Reddit published a new report which examines key NFL Super Bowl engagement stats and opportunities, and provides tips on how brands can tap into Super Bowl hype to maximize ad approaches.

The 12-page guide includes notes on Super Bowl and NFL engagement on Reddit, with overviews of how fans engage with the game across a range of related subreddit communities.

The guide also provides notes on how brands can build their Reddit presence, and get a handle on key trends in order to better target their approaches.

Reddit’s advice for brands essentially centers on choosing a specific content approach for their Super Bowl campaign, then honing in on the related questions will drive the most engagement.

Reddit said that brands should consider what feelings they want to evoke with their Super Bowl campaigns, and what rituals or language they want to use in order to relate to niche communities. Within this, brands should also consider how they can tell an authentic, meaningful story.

These elements link back to how Redditors engage, and by mapping out a strategic approach early that aligns with this, Reddit said that brands will be able to construct a more effective approach ahead of the Super Bowl hype in 2027.

Reddit’s guide includes a range of more specific notes on how to do this, and it could be a valuable planning tool for brands that plan to use the platform for their marketing efforts.
