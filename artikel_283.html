GTA 6's cops will remember you thanks to the 'Heat' system

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It’s a fun evolution of the iconic Wanted mechanic.

With Grand Theft Auto 6 finally starting to roll out to players after more than a decade of development, people are beginning to discover the ways developer Rockstar Games has tweaked the GTA formula. One of the more interesting examples involves the franchise's well-known Wanted system.

As before, the game's police will respond to the player as they commit crimes across Vice City and the fictional state of Leonida. If you've played any GTA game in the past, you know to look to the top of the interface to see your Wanted level, represented here by up to six stars. As your Wanted level increases, the police will respond with more force. At one star, for example, a couple of patrol cars will start chasing you, while at four stars and higher the VCPD will begin deploying helicopters and armored cars to take down protagonists Jason and Lucia.

To this system, Rockstar has added a new "Heat" mechanic that makes it more difficult for players to lose the police. Here's how that system works.

The Heat system consists of three related mechanics. First, there are the Wanted stars themselves, which, for the first time in the series, will change color depending on whether the police know if you're the suspect they're after.

Below your Wanted level, you will see a new set of tracking icons. So far Engadget has seen six in total, and they're designed to communicate how the police know you're the suspect they're after. The more of these icons you see at the same time, the easier it is for the police to identify you and start a new chase.

In short, unless you want to get into a gun fight with the police, the game encourages you to hide Jason and Lucia's identity. A bit of preparation goes a long way. Lastly, there's a regional element to the Heat system. As you commit more crimes in the same area, the police will respond with more patrols. They will stay in an area even after you lose your Wanted level. You can see if an area is "Hot" by looking at your map. It will have a purple overlay over it. Over time, an area's Heat level will cool down if you don't commit any crimes there.
