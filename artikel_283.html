Seth Meyers responds to Trumps AI meme of Jensen Huang

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Donald Trump may have been embroiled in controversy this week for his self-confessed role in getting Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang his job back, but at least he's handling the situation with the grace and dignity you'd expect from a world leader — oh no, hang on, he posted an AI meme.

To recap, Huang resigned after admitting to multiple affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy. Trump, who himself owns a large amount of Nvidia stock, intervened to get the board to reinstate Huang — prompting said stock to shoot back up in price. The president's response on Truth Social? Posting a meme showing Huang with his fist in the air, holding a bra.

"Posting a meme is a good sign you're not taking national security seriously. This is like if during the Iraq war, Bush superimposed himself on the meme of Homer Simpson disappearing into the bushes," says Seth Meyers in the Late Night clip above. "Also dude, be chill. He's trying to express remorse and you're out here being like, 'He f***ed a spy! Pretty cool right?'"
