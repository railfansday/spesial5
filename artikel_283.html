Everything to know about Targets Black Friday 2026 sale: Store hours, early access info, best deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Target's Black Friday 2026 sale is coming in hot, folks. Things kicked off extra early this year, with the first official shopping event of the season on Nov. 5.

While most holiday shopping is done online these days, Target still offers that classic Black Friday in-store shopping experience for those who want to soak it all in. The deals will really start to heat up on Black Friday proper, but you can start checking off your lists as soon as today.

With every retailer holding major Black Friday events, it's easy to get your wires crossed. To help you keep all the details straight and get ready to shop at Target on Black Friday 2026, here's everything you need to know, including store hours, what will be on sale, and how to get early access to the best deals.

Black Friday proper falls on Nov. 27 in 2026, but most retailers start dropping deals well before that. Target's first early Black Friday sale kicked off on Nov. 5, followed by new limited-time deal drops every weekend throughout the holiday season. The official Black Friday sale will run from Nov. 22 through 29, with new weekend deals available Nov. 26 through 29.

Target's Deal of the Day program also returned on Nov. 1. Shoppers can score a new featured deal each day until Dec. 24, with a one-day preview of the offers.

You can shop Target's Black Friday sale both online and in stores. Most Target stores will open their doors at 6 a.m. local time on Black Friday, Nov. 27. There may be exceptions to those hours, so double-check the exact opening hours for your local store. Target stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day.

Target has made waking up early on Black Friday worth it once again, as the first 100 guests in line will receive a Target gift card valued from $25 to $100. One lucky guest will even receive a a $1,000 Target gift card. Visit Target.com/BlackFridayGiveaway for official rules and details.

Target Circle 360 members can enjoy 24 hours of early access to select deals starting on Wednesday, Nov. 25 at 3 a.m. ET.

Target Circle 360 is the retailer's paid membership program (separate from its free Circle membership). It costs $99 annually or $10.99 per month and offers perks like same-day delivery, free shipping, monthly freebies, early access to sales, and an extra 30 days for returns.

Target has a wide assortment of Black Friday deals, ranging from toys to tech. We expect to see some steep discounts on toys and will definitely be keeping an eye on Lego sets and Pokémon 30th Anniversary products. In years past, we've also seen great deals on small appliances, TVs, headphones, floor care, and gift cards. Standout deals from 2025 included $69 AirPods, $600 off TCL's The Frame TV dupe, 20% off gift cards, and a $49 air fryer.
