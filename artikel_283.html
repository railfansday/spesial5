Shop Magic: The Gatherings Avatar The Last Airbender Play Booster Box for its best-ever price on Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

TL;DR: The Magic: The Gathering Avatar: The Last Airbender Play Booster Box is on sale for $117.95 at Amazon, down from its $132.99 list price.

It's a particularly good week for trading card collectors to stock up on Magic: The Gathering, with Amazon's latest batch of Black Friday deals making the Avatar: The Last Airbender Play Booster Box cheaper than ever.

As of Nov. 24, the Avatar: The Last Airbender Play Booster Box is going for just $117.95 at Amazon — putting $15.04 back in your pocket and bringing the cost per booster down to around $3.93 — shipped and sold directly by Amazon (instead of a third-party seller).

That's $5 cheaper than the previous record-low Amazon deal we covered in October. It's the lowest-ever price on Amazon and a whole $11.01 under the estimated $128.96 market price at TCGplayer.

Across the display's 30 Play Boosters, you'll find 420 cards celebrating everything from the streets of Ba Sing Se to the Fire Nation, with elemental bending mechanics to experiment with in your decks.

There's at least one Traditional Foil and up to four Rare or higher-rarity cards in every booster, plus the chance of snagging a borderless Mythic Rare in foil.
