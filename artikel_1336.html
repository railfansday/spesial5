Roma race to extend Malen’s contract after four-goal show at Parma

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Roma want to tie Donyell Malen down to a new contract as soon as possible, after his four-goal performance against Parma took him clear at the top of the Serie A scoring charts.

According to TuttoMercatoWeb, talks with the Dutch striker have already started, and the Giallorossi are keen to finalise a deal to take him off the market.

Malen scored all but one of Roma’s goals in Saturday’s 5-1 win at the Tardini. That gives him 12 goals in 11 league games this season, three more than Monza’s Gustavo Varela, and more than 30 in 35 games since he joined from Aston Villa.

Roma also want to reward the commitment Malen showed in coming to the club. He accepted a pay cut to leave Villa and work under Gian Piero Gasperini, and he has been outstanding ever since.

A new deal would make him the club’s highest earner. He would receive more than €4m a season, overtaking goalkeeper Mile Svilar.

The Giallorossi paid a total of €27m for Malen, including the loan fee and the cost of making the deal permanent. He’ll turn 28 in January, and his value has risen considerably since then, which could make him a major asset if a big offer ever arrived. For now, though, Roma and Gasperini have no intention of letting him go.
