Jose Mourinho’s 12-Word Comment to Valencia Boss After Real Madrid’s Win Goes Viral

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

José Mourinho has never been one to tiptoe around the truth. Over more than two decades in management, the Portuguese legend has built a reputation for saying exactly what he thinks, whoever happens to be on the receiving end.

So when he was asked about his post-match chat with the opposing manager after Real Madrid’s win at Mestalla, few should have been surprised that he didn’t hold back.

His side are hitting their stride and confidence is flowing through the side, and it's clearly found its way to the man in the dugout, who has begun to find the unnerving confidence that defined him in his early days at the top.

It was a night full of flashpoints in Valencia, tensions, arguments, on and off-pitch incidents. Notably, defender Pablo Maffeo’s half-time outburst during his DAZN interview went viral, while reports of a tunnel brawl involving Madrid players and stadium security dominated the fallout.

On the pitch, though, there was only going to be one winner. Fede Valverde opened the scoring after Kylian Mbappé’s free-kick came back off the wall, Arda Güler doubled the lead before the break, and Mbappé wrapped up a 3-0 win in the second half.

The result extended Madrid’s winning run to eight games in all competitions and moved Mourinho to the top of La Liga’s all-time list for win percentage among managers with at least 100 matches as his side went level on points with rivals Barcelona.

Speaking at his post-match press conference, Mourinho revealed exactly what he had told Valencia boss Javier Aguirre, a man he describes as a friend, at full-time. He said:

“What I said to Aguirre after the game was: ‘Don’t be sad, because my players are much better than yours'. And it’s the truth.

“The problem isn’t Aguirre, because he’s a coach with a lot of experience. But the truth is that Real Madrid’s players are very good, and coaches who have players like mine are closer to winning.”

Mourinho also had a word for the hostile Mestalla atmosphere, describing the stadium as one built on “passion”. He added that playing against a far superior Madrid side only increases the frustration of the home crowd and players.

Reaction on social media was mixed, to say the least, with one fan calling it ‘a crazy thing to say’. Another questioned why Mourinho would share it at all: ‘Why would you even tell him that.’

Others were simply delighted to see the Special One back to his brutally honest best. ‘Peak Jose is back,’ wrote one, while another added: ‘Football will miss Mourinho when he’s done.’

The Valencia defender gave a very strong response to one reporter at half-time as he was forced into an interview during his clash with Real Madrid.

Whether Aguirre took it as consolation or a jibe, only he knows. But one thing is certain: Mourinho’s second spell at the Bernabeu is giving the Spanish media plenty to talk about.

As Madrid grows stronger, Mourinho is proving, for the time being, he is capable of managing the superstars, the expectations and the big moments as they head into the international break with 14 wins and two defeats as Jose looks to prove everyone wrong once again.
