Elon Musk, tech leaders push to reinstate Jensen Huang as Nvidia CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

A petition with 175,000 names asks the board to bring back the chipmaker's founder, while Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent urges an outside hire

Elon Musk and other prominent tech executives are backing an online petition that asks Nvidia $NVDA's board to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO. The petition stood at 175,000 signatures by Monday morning, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Musk said on X $TWTR on Friday that he signed the petition, called "Stand With Jensen," according to CNBC. On Saturday, he said on X that without Huang, some "critical deals will be lost."

Confirmed signers include Uber $UBER co-founder Travis Kalanick, Shopify $SHOP CEO Tobias Lütke and Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, according to The Wall Street Journal. Y Combinator President Garry Tan is among the signers, according to The Guardian. Kalanick wrote that Huang had “erred, yes – to err is to be human – but I do not believe anything he has done rises to the bar of separating him from his company.”

More than 7,500 signers listed themselves as Nvidia staff, a group that makes up 18% of Nvidia’s total headcount, according to The Guardian. Some people said they never signed the petition even though their names were listed on it, among them prominent Nvidia employees, according to The Wall Street Journal. Some employees have floated a rally for Huang on the Santa Clara, California, campus over the next several days.

Huang resigned last week amid reports that he had been unfaithful with at least two women. U.S. officials suspect one of them, Alice Huang, of having relayed information to China's intelligence services, according to The Wall Street Journal. Nvidia named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO.

The board said Friday it was enlisting a firm that specializes in executive recruiting, and that no candidates have been engaged yet, according to The Wall Street Journal. Nvidia told CNBC in a statement that chatter over specific names, from inside or outside the company, was premature.

Altimeter Capital CEO Brad Gerstner and Gavin Baker of Atreides Management, both Nvidia shareholders, wrote a letter on Friday urging the board to weigh all options, including "the potential return of Jensen Huang," according to CNBC. The letter stopped short of demanding that Huang be brought back right away.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Fox News on Sunday that the board should hire an outside CEO. "They need a dramatic shake-up," he said. Bessent said he wants answers from the board and outside counsel within 10 days.

Nvidia stock traded lower in premarket trading Monday.

Musk had told a financial blog on X that hiring Huang would be "an honor" last week, before the resignation. The board opened an independent investigation on Nov. 8, the day Huang admitted to infidelity in a note to employees.

Nvidia employees had urged the board to bring Huang back in an internal suggestion-box thread that had drawn more than 1,600 replies as of Nov. 13.
