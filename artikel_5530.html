NinjaCat Introduces Enterprise Marketing AGI, A New Category of AI Technology to Run Marketing at Enterprise Scale

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

AI martech leader moves marketing from isolated tools and dashboards to governed AI that drives outcomes across accounts, brands, and markets

NEW YORK, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- NinjaCat, the AI Operating System for Enterprise Marketing, today introduced Enterprise Marketing AGI, a new category of AI technology designed to change the way marketing is run and scaled at the enterprise level. Enterprise Marketing AGI moves AI beyond individual tools and fragmented point solutions to a governed, enterprise-wide system that connects marketing data, intelligence, and workflows across an organization. This marks a shift from AI-assisted marketing tasks toward AI-powered operations that drive marketing outcomes at scale.

As AI becomes ubiquitous across organizations, enterprises face a new problem: too much AI. Whether a marketing team uses a "frankenstack" of disconnected platforms, or individuals build their own siloed AI assistants and tools, today's marketing AI solutions weren't built to scale across an entire marketing organization.  And without a way to enforce brand, legal, and budget rules automatically, most AI pilots stay read-only, because no one will let an agent touch spend without approvals and audit trails. Even organizations that deploy agents can run into inconsistent governance and approval processes, preventing AI from moving beyond recommendations to coordinated execution.

"The strength of the model was never the issue. The modern enterprise needs AI that can not only analyze but actually execute. DIY data pipelines and messy in-house marketing data stacks have made it almost impossible for marketing leaders to manage AI organization-wide, let alone deliver results at scale," says Paul Deraval, CEO of NinjaCat. "Enterprise Marketing AGI is about creating an infrastructure with clean, unified data and the right organizational context to make better decisions and take action across the enterprise. It positions AI to make the invisible visible, and drive real marketing outcomes at every level."

NinjaCat's solution for Enterprise Marketing AGI is built for enterprises making this shift. It combines trusted marketing data with governed agents to deliver the infrastructure to deploy AI without adding complexity or sacrificing control. Its unified marketing data foundation gives agents clean, consistent data to reason and act on, with a scoped organizational memory that gives AI context and compounds with use. This creates a complete system of record that delivers shared intelligence to drive marketing results for every brand, account, and team.

Within this operating system, policy-driven agents can evaluate proposed actions against an organization's established brand, legal, media-plan, and budget rules, allowing AI to act within defined controls and escalate exceptions for human review.

"We have an incredible opportunity to rethink how marketing gets done today. AI is moving from an individual assistant mindset to an organization-wide asset, with agents that can work across channels, accounts, and locations," said Brian Lee, COO at NinjaCat. "Marketing is quickly moving beyond copilots and toward AI agents that can orchestrate work across the entire organization. The companies that embrace this shift will be able to move from simply understanding what's happening in marketing to having AI actively help run it."
