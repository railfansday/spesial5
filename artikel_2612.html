Eagles make a game of it with blocked punt for touchdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Eagles aren't going down without a fight in Dallas today.

After falling behind 31-10 late in the third quarter, it appeared that Philadelphia was out of it. But late in the third quarter, Joe Milton hit DeVonta Smith for a 45-yard touchdown pass that made it 31-17. And then early in the fourth quarter, Philadelphia's Ty Robinson blocked a Dallas punt, and Kenny Moore picked it up and walked in to the end zone for a touchdown.

The Eagles' defense has had trouble containing the Cowboys' offense, but this game is far from over.
