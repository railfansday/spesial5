Man Utd impress in draw against PSG but injury worries mount

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Michael Carrick said he had to be careful with Mason Mount after the England midfielder handed Manchester United an injury scare in their friendly draw with Paris St Germain.

Mount has had a torrid time with injuries since signing from Chelsea in a £55m-deal three years ago, starting only 25 Premier League games for United in that time.

The 27-year-old has been successfully deployed by Carrick in a central midfield role during pre-season, leading to speculation that United may not make another expected signing in that department.

But Mount played only 18 minutes of Saturday’s 1-1 draw against the European champions in Gothenburg before being replaced by Scotland international Tyler Fletcher.

“He [Mount] got kicked. We just wanted to be careful and look after him,” Carrick, the United head coach, told reporters in Sweden.

“We’ve been lucky with injuries so far, so we wanted to be careful.”

Post-match video footage showed Mount walking freely on to the United bus without the aid of a protective boot or knee support.

United went behind within two minutes at the Ullevi Stadium when Liverpool target Ibrahim Mbaye punished slack marking to put PSG ahead.

Bryan Mbeumo fired United level after 32 minutes following good work from Amad Diallo, the Cameroon forward’s third goal in two games after his match-winning double against Atletico Madrid.

United meet Leeds in a Dublin friendly on Wednesday before coming up against AC Milan and their former manager Ruben Amorim next Saturday.

United open their Premier League campaign at Hull on August 22.

Ready to face PSG in our latest pre-season test ✊

Manchester United continue their Sweden sojourn with match against Paris St-Germain in Gothenburg.

Clearly a clash against the back-to-back Champions League winners is a step up from their previous two pre-season friendlies (victories against Rosenborg and Atletico Madrid), but at the same time this is more a chance for players to get miles in the legs and see how new signings and possibly some young guns fit in.

One of those new signings is Youri Tielemans, and the Belgian international is in line to make his debut after his move from Aston Villa. Other team news is that Bruno Fernandes is back and ready to captain the team.

Michael Carrick has also been boosted by the returns of Matheus Cunha, Diogo Dalot and Noussair Mazraoui after their post-World Cup breaks.

Cunha, however, will not play a part in today’s match because of a “minor illness” according to the MUTV website. He is expected to link up with the squad in Ireland, where they face Leeds United at Croke Park, after the match.

This will be Man Utd’s first outing since the 2-1 win over Atletico, a performance and result that pleased Carrick.

“I enjoyed it, I thought there was a lot of good in things that we are looking to improve on,” the United manager said. “Building on what we did for the last seven months, I think there is a lot of good signs for us, it’s never going to be the exact stage we want to be at. But I thought there was really a lot of positives to take out of the game, individual, collectively as a team, senior players looking strong and fit, and the younger players doing really well.

“The spirit and the camaraderie is so important for the team, it’s great to see the boys getting on so naturally and enjoying each other’s company. Collectively we looked strong as a team, which is always the most important thing, and individual performances seeing the young players come on to have an impact.”
