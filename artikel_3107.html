Xiaomi’s profit falls 68% as memory costs hit its phones and car sales slow

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Phone shipments fell 29%, but record prices kept phone revenue nearly flat. The car and AI unit lost 3.4 billion yuan, and Xiaomi’s president expects high memory prices through the first half of 2027.

Xiaomi’s revenue fell for a third quarter in a row as rising memory prices squeezed its phones and its car sales slowed. Revenue dropped 5.2% to 107.2 billion yuan ($16.1bn) in the three months to September. The company reported the figures in its results filing on Tuesday.

Profit fell faster. Net profit dropped 68.4% to 3.9 billion yuan. Adjusted net profit, a non-IFRS measure, fell 56.8% to 4.9 billion yuan. Gross margin slipped to 20.2%, from 22.9% a year earlier.

Xiaomi said it kept raising its spending on AI, and described the quarter’s conditions in the filing:

“The costs of key components, including memory, continued to rise and industry competition further intensified,” Xiaomi wrote.

Xiaomi shipped 30.7 million smartphones, down 29.1% from 43.3 million a year earlier. It said it cut shipments of low-end phones on purpose. Global smartphone shipments fell 20% in the quarter, according to Omdia data cited by Xiaomi.

The average selling price rose 39.8% to a record 1,486 yuan. That offset most of the drop, and phone revenue fell 0.8%, to 45.6 billion yuan. The phone gross margin fell to 9.2%, from 11.1%, which Xiaomi put down mainly to higher component prices. Its Xiaomi 18 Fold, launched in September from 10,999 yuan, runs on the company’s own Xring O3 chip.

Company president Lu Weibing said on the earnings call that memory prices would likely stay high through the first half of 2027. Peggy Ye and Howard Liu reported his comments for the South China Morning Post.

The unit that now covers cars, AI and other new businesses saw revenue fall 17.7%, to 23.9 billion yuan. Car revenue alone dropped 19.9%, to 22.7 billion yuan. Xiaomi delivered 100,975 vehicles, down 7% from a year earlier, Bloomberg reported. China’s passenger car retail market shrank 24% in the period, the filing said.

The unit posted an operating loss of 3.4 billion yuan. Executives put it down to growing investment in AI, according to Bloomberg. The filing also blamed fewer deliveries of the high-end SU7 Ultra for a lower margin.

Xiaomi pointed to its new SkyNomad range of extended-range SUVs, priced from 209,900 yuan. It said orders passed 70,000 in the first month of sales. Deliveries in the quarter included more than 10,000 SkyNomads.

On the call, executives kept the plan to enter Germany in the third quarter of 2027, Bloomberg reported. They said recent EU trade barriers would not greatly change its strategy for Europe. Xiaomi has signed agreements with eight German dealer groups.

Research and development costs reached 9.2 billion yuan in the quarter. Over nine months they rose 16.5%, to 27.4 billion yuan. In September, Xiaomi released its MiMo-V2.6 models as open source. It also showed the design behind its next model, MiMo-V3.

The results follow a second-quarter profit drop in August. Xiaomi has also bought back about 431.5 million of its own shares this year, worth about HK$13.1bn.
