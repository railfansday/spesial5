Grand Canyon Education: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Common App access brought over 5,600 applications in two months, a channel the company had never used before its ground growth push.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 4, 2026, Grand Canyon Education (LOPE -1.07%) announced total enrollment increased 5.2% year over year. Long-term investors care because student retention and new campus formats drive scalable service revenue across education partners.

Three key takeaways from the call highlight where the business is headed.

Ground campus enrollment fell three percentage points below management expectations due to flat incoming student growth. Management launched four targeted programs to expand student reach and reignite campus growth over the next five years.

Grand Canyon University, the company's primary university partner, previously operated without access to the Common App, an online college application platform. Joining the platform generated over 5,600 applications during September and October, giving the institution direct access to high school applicants across the nation.

Off-campus hybrid locations deliver healthcare and nursing programs that generate more than three times the revenue per student compared to standard online programs. Growth in these specialized healthcare sites provides a higher-margin revenue stream as campus capacity expands across target regional markets.

Online enrollment growth remains steady as high student retention rates balance shifts in advertising spending and lower reentry counts.

The company relies on high program completion rates and expanding specialized degree offerings to maintain pricing power across its university partners. Management is watching site-level profit margins and capital allocation through share repurchases to compound long-term shareholder value.
