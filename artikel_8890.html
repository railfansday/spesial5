Kindergarten Aegelmatt / Brandenberger Kloter

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Text description provided by the architects. Since the start of the 2026 school year, children in Pratteln, Switzerland, have been learning and playing in a new timber building by Brandenberger Kloter Architekten. Kindergarten Aegelmatt brings a kindergarten and a day-care centre together under one roof. The façades of untreated timber change with the weather, the light and the seasons, making the passing of time visible to the children.
