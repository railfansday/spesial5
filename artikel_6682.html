That means the unimpeachable parkas, the marvelous mid-layers, the underrated knits and more are all far more accessible than they were last week, and just in time for the weather to get nasty too. This year in the US, Black Friday is on Friday, November 27, a day after Thanksgiving. While that’s the official date, many retailers roll out deals throughout November.
