Google Nest Wifi router and point get October 2026 update

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The second update this year for the Nest Wifi router and point rolled out last week with two improvements.

Following the April update, the Nest Wifi router is now on software version 14150.883.1264, while the Nest Wifi point is running 1.56.539812.

The changelog dated October 20, 2026 lists two updates:

Google Wifi and Nest Wifi devices update automatically overnight. To manually check, select your Nest Wifi router from the “All devices” top tab in the Google Home app and tap the settings gear icon. You can find “Software information” under the “Wi-Fi information” section.

The Nest Wifi Pro received its last notable update in March.
