A good throw is all about getting your seed as far down the canyon as possible. Every zone it passes adds luck (so you get more chance to grow rarer plants). Lucky Throws are especially useful here since each one gives 10 times the luck for a single throw. Luck Potions and Mega Luck Potions also increase odds but only temporarily so save them until you’re ready to launch several seeds at once.

Other rewards support the rest of your progression. Training Boosts support pull-up sessions (strength needed to throw farther), Cash and Money Boosts build up your garden income so you can afford better seeds; better seeds give you more luck (another way to improve the plants you grow).
