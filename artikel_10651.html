Stranger Things prequel Other Worlds revealed with 1950s setting and 2028 release

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Netflix has revealed its long-awaited Stranger Things live-action spinoff is called Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure, with the 1950s-set prequel arriving in 2028.

First announced in July 2022, the project will bring creators Matt and Ross Duffer together with longtime Stranger Things writer Kate Trefry for a story featuring new heroes, villains, and a different setting.

Netflix’s November 6 announcement for Stranger Things Day has revealed more about the series, which takes place decades before the Hawkins gang uncovered their town’s supernatural secrets.

In Netflix’s reveal, the Duffer Brothers teased an adventure that retains the feel of Stranger Things while introducing a different group of characters.

“We’ve been dreaming of Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure for many years now, and to see it become a reality is surreal and exhilarating,” they said.

“It’s Stranger Things, but also thrillingly fresh, with a new setting, new heroes and villains, and new … actually, we’re not allowed to say any more. Now, on to casting. In the meantime, watch the skies, nerds!”

Netflix hasn’t revealed the characters or exact location of the story, although production will take place in New Mexico.

Trefry, who wrote The First Shadow alongside her work on Stranger Things, will serve as showrunner, co-creator, writer, and executive producer. Joe Wright, whose films include Pride & Prejudice, Atonement and Darkest Hour, will direct the first three episodes and executive produce.

According to Trefry, the prequel will return to the mix of horror, wonder and mystery that helped define the original show, while telling a separate story.

“With the support of Netflix and 21 Laps, we have had the time of our lives returning to all the horror, wonder and mystery of the flagship, while also telling a wholly new story, one we hope will blow your minds and break your hearts all over again,” she said. “There are so many more mysteries to explore. Welcome to Other Worlds.”

Stranger Things producer Shawn Levy is also returning as an executive producer, and teased that the series will explore a new era, setting, and mythology. While Netflix has yet to explain how its story connects to Hawkins, it has released a brief 57-second teaser that shows an abandoned bike with an old-timey radio playing (it’s pretty eerie stuff).

Other Worlds is scheduled to arrive in 2028, with an exact premiere date and cast yet to be announced.
