Which 2026 Samsung Galaxy Tab should you buy?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Samsung has a huge lineup of Galaxy tablets to choose from right now, and they’re all a little different.

The Galaxy Tab S12+ is the Samsung tablet to choose for a premium mix of entertainment, creativity, and productivity. The Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra gives you an even bigger canvas, while the Galaxy Tab S11 brings flagship features to a more portable size. For everyday value, start with the Galaxy Tab S10 FE series or the affordable Galaxy Tab A11+.

Samsung’s tablet lineup covers everything from watching a show on the couch to sketching your next project or getting work done between meetings. The right choice comes down to what you want that extra screen to do.

With options across a wide range of price points, Samsung has a Galaxy tablet for different needs and budgets this holiday season.

Here’s a list of the main Galaxy Tab options available in 2026, from family-friendly entertainment to Samsung’s largest creative workspace:

Screen size is only one part of the whole story. The Galaxy Tab S11, S12+, and S12 Ultra have AMOLED displays that offer rich contrast for movies and photos, while the FE models provide useful everyday features at a more accessible price.

The Galaxy Tab S12+ is the sweet spot if you want a serious upgrade over a basic tablet without moving to Samsung’s biggest screen.

As we covered in our Galaxy Tab S12 launch story, it combines a 12.6-inch 120Hz AMOLED display with a MediaTek Dimensity 9500 processor, 12GB of RAM, and an included S Pen, with four months of complimentary Adobe Photoshop access and a six-month Notability trial, both of which support the stylus.

The S12+ is easy to use and appreciate in daily life. It puts reference material beside your notes during a course, sketch ideas for a presentation, while making it easy to settle into a movie after finishing your email. It has room to spread out without requiring the Ultra’s larger footprint.

The Galaxy Tab S12+ also offers a 5G version, making it worth considering if you regularly work away from Wi-Fi.

Buy the Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra when the screen itself is the upgrade you’ll enjoy every day.

The Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra is made for creative professionals and families seeking premium entertainment. It moves naturally between focused creative work and immersive movie nights at home.

Its 14.6-inch AMOLED display gives photos, drawings, and multiple app windows more breathing room. Picture reviewing a document while keeping your handwritten notes visible, or drawing with reference images alongside your canvas.

The Ultra shares the S12+’s Dimensity 9500 processor but offers configurations up to 16GB of RAM and 1TB of storage. It’s available for $1,399.99, but last year’s Tab S11 Ultra is on sale during Black Friday for just $899.99.

Both S12 tablets ship with Android 17-based One UI 9 and seven years of OS and security updates. Photoshop comes pre-installed with a four-month access offer, giving you a way to explore S Pen-assisted photo editing.

The Ultra is especially appealing with a keyboard at a desk; Samsung’s Ultra Book Cover Keyboard Slim is the perfect companion to the S12 Ultra, and is on sale for $146.99 during Best Buy’s Black Friday Deal event.

Yes, the Galaxy Tab S11 is the premium Galaxy tablet to choose when portability comes first.

Our Galaxy Tab S11 launch coverage highlighted the return of Samsung’s smaller flagship tablet, with a Dimensity 9400+ processor, 12GB of RAM, and an included S Pen. Its 11-inch AMOLED screen makes it an attractive companion for reading, travel, and handwritten notes.

The Galaxy Tab S11 is the sort of tablet you can bring to a meeting or pull out on a train without needing a large work surface. You still get premium display quality, just in a size that’s easier to carry, and it comes in at a lower price.

A discounted Tab S11 Ultra can also be worth considering if you want a 14.6-inch screen. Compare the actual price with the S12 Ultra rather than dismissing an older flagship automatically.

The Galaxy Tab S10 FE and FE+ are compelling choices for people who want an included S Pen without flagship pricing.

Choose the 10.9-inch FE for a study companion that travels easily. Choose the 13.1-inch FE+ when you’d rather put a lecture, document, or recipe beside your notes. Both offer IP68 water and dust resistance, so they’re great kitchen companions.

Galaxy Tab S10 Lite is for students, children, and emerging creators. It is a practical, encouraging gift for handwritten learning, organization, drawing, and experimentation.

The Galaxy Tab A11+ is the straightforward entertainment choice. At $199, it’s the cheapest of the Galaxy Tab options, but it doesn’t support the S Pen, so choose an S-series model if handwriting matters.

Choose the Galaxy Tab S12+ for a premium all-rounder, the S12 Ultra for a spacious creative workspace, or the S11 for flagship features in a travel-friendly size.

For school, home projects, and everyday notes, the FE series delivers the features many people actually need. For family streaming and browsing, the A11+ keeps things simple and affordable.

The best Galaxy Tab isn’t automatically the biggest or newest. It’s the one that makes the things you already love doing easier — and gives you a few new possibilities along the way.
