Zelda Ocarina of Time remake sells 5.2 million copies in four days to break Switch 2 record

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Nintendo’s Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake has sold over 5.2 million copies in its first four days, giving it the biggest four-day launch of any Nintendo-published Switch 2 game.

Almost three decades after its Nintendo 64 debut, Ocarina of Time has brought players back to Hyrule with a remake of Link’s time-traveling adventure.

Released on November 5, the Switch 2 exclusive has earned widespread praise from players and critics alike. Our own review gave it a whopping 5 stars, while its current 96 Metacritic rating makes it the best-rated game of 2026.

Now, Nintendo has revealed that the Ocarina of Time remake has sold a massive 5.2 million copies in less than a week.

In its November 11 announcement, Nintendo confirmed that Ocarina of Time sold over 5.2 million units worldwide during its opening four days, including 810,000 copies in Japan.

According to the company, that’s the highest global sales total achieved by any Nintendo-published Switch 2 game in its first four days.

For comparison, the original Ocarina of Time reached 7.6 million all-time sales on Nintendo 64, while Ocarina of Time 3D reached 6.44 million on 3DS. Not bad for a remake of an old-school remake of its beloved Zelda game.

Ocarina of Time remake’s sales success follows two enormous Zelda releases on the original Switch, when Tears of the Kingdom famously sold over 10 million copies in just three days, becoming the fastest-selling entry in the series to date.

Meanwhile, Breath of the Wild sold 3.84 million units across Switch and Wii U during its March 2017 launch month. Ocarina of Time is still early in its release, but its opening four days have already made it one of Switch 2’s biggest launches.
