Rockstar reportedly seeking $100M for Red Dead Redemption movie and TV rights

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Rockstar Games is reportedly open to selling the movie and television rights to Red Dead Redemption, but Hollywood studios will need a lot more money than Dutch van der Linde ever dreamed of getting his hands on.

Despite being responsible for some of the most cinematic experiences in gaming, Rockstar has historically been reluctant to let Hollywood adapt its biggest franchises.

While the studio is reportedly still unwilling to bring Grand Theft Auto to the big screen, it appears that Arthur Morgan and John Marston could be saddling up for Hollywood.

According to a report from The Ankler, sources claim Rockstar is now more receptive to the idea of adapting Red Dead Redemption into a movie or television series.

However, the licensing rights alone could come with a nine-figure price tag.

“At one point, they were throwing around a purchase price of more than $100 million minimum for licensing rights,” an agent who works with video game writers told the outlet.

The news marks a potential change in direction for Rockstar. In 2024, company co-founder Dan Houser revealed why previous pitches for GTA and Red Dead movies were rejected, citing financial risks and concerns over creative control.

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick also expressed caution about adapting its franchises following the disappointing reception to the Borderlands movie.

There certainly wouldn’t be a shortage of Hollywood stars willing to join the Van der Linde gang, though.

Jeffrey Dean Morgan, Henry Cavill, and Jack Black have all expressed interest in seeing Red Dead adapted, with Morgan even offering to play Arthur for free.

Arthur’s original actor, Roger Clark, has also expressed interest in reprising his role in live-action.

Rockstar has yet to publicly confirm its plans, and no studio has been announced as acquiring the rights. It’s also unclear whether an adaptation would retell the events of either game or explore a completely original story set in the same universe.

It’s also unclear what effect, if any, this could have on a possible RDR3.

For now, Red Dead fans will just have to have some goddamn faith that Hollywood can get the money together. Perhaps they’ll turn to GTA 6‘s incredible bank robberies for some inspiration.
