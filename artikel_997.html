Prince George braves rain with Prince William as they watch Champions League game

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Prince of Wales and Prince George braved torrential rain to watch Aston Villa play away to Barcelona in a Champions League clash on Tuesday night (3 November).

Prince William, who is a passionate Villa supporter and a patron of the Football Association, often attends the Birmingham team’s matches.

The father and son were seated in the director's box as they witnessed Barcelona beat the future King's beloved side 3-0.

Villa boss Unai Emery said the defeat shows his side is not yet ready to compete with Europe’s elite.
