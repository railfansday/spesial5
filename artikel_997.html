CME Group Tour Championship: Can Hull and Woad stop Korda?

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Key questions about the CME Group Tour Championship, as the LPGA Tour’s 2026 season-long points race comes to an end; watch all four rounds live on Sky Sports

Who will be crowned the LPGA Tour’s Race to the CME Globe Champion? Who can stop Nelly Korda from winning? Can Brits Charley Hull and Lottie Woad claim victory? We get you up to speed ahead of this week’s LPGA Tour 2026 season finale, live on Sky Sports.

The CME Group Tour Championship is the final event in the LPGA Tour's season-long points race.

Throughout 2026, players have competed in the LPGA Tour's points competition, the Race to CME Globe.

The top 60 players on the Race to CME Globe Points List are invited to feature at this weekend's event and the player who wins the 2026 CME Group Tour Championship will be crowned the Race to CME Globe champion.

The tournament is held at the Tiburon Golf Club at the Ritz-Carlton Golf Resort in Naples, Florida.

The course has hosted the tournament since 2013.

It is the second time in consecutive years that Korda has arrived at the CME Group Tour Championship sitting at the top of the Race to CME Globe standings.

The American has already been crowned the Rolex Player of the Year, with three events to spare, but has never won the Race to CME Group Tour Championship. A first title this week would see her qualify for the LPGA Hall of Fame.

Standing in her way is a field of the LPGA Tour's best players, including two-time CME Group Tour Championship winner Jeeno Thitikul, who arrives in Florida having won four times on the LPGA Tour this season.

Miyu Yamashita, who has three wins on the LPGA Tour in 2026, enjoyed a strong finish to her season, winning twice while placing inside the top 10 three times in her last six events.

Haeran Ryu, who clinched back-to-back majors at The Amundi Evian Championship and the KPMG Women's PGA Championship, also comes into this event off the back of a fine vein of form, finishing tied-second and tied-third in her last two starts.

Hull clinched the first LPGA Tour title of her career at this event back in 2016, so she certainly knows how to win at Tiburon Golf Club.

It's been a great year for Hull, who won the Solheim Cup with Team Europe in September and placed inside the top 10 at five LPGA Tour events. She'll no doubt want to end the season in style with a win.

Woad is the only other English player in the field. She too has enjoyed an excellent year, winning the Kroger Queen City Championship in May and finishing tied-ninth at last week's The ANNIKA, marking her seventh top-10 finish of the season.

A total prize purse of £8.33m ($11m) is up for grabs, with the winner set to take home a £3.03m ($4m) cheque, the biggest cash prize in the history of women's golf.

The runner-up is also set to receive £757,423 ($1m).

The LPGA Tour will also award the golfer with the lowest scoring average across the 2026 season with The Vare Trophy.

While Korda holds the lowest average score for the year, she has not played enough rounds to qualify for the award, meaning Yamashita, who has a scoring average of 69.50 and Thitikul, who follows her at 69.87, will battle it out to win The Vare Trophy.

The first two rounds of the tournament will be shown live on Sky Sports Mix from 7pm on Thursday and Friday, with the action also live from 8pm on both days.
