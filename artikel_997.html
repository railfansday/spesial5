Mauricio Pochettino fumes over red card decision after nightmare start to life at Manchester United

New Manchester United manager Mauricio Pochettino has denied that Leny Yoro should have been sent off in his side’s 4-0 loss to Liverpool, calling it an “unfair situation”.

Yoro was given a straight red card in the eighth minute of the game at Anfield to give Pochettino the worst possible start to his life as United boss, with the young Frenchman fouling the Liverpool striker after he had gone clean through as last man.

As there was no attempt to play the ball, referee Sam Barrott showed Yoro a straight red card, with VAR checking and confirming the decision “for denial of an obvious goal-scoring opportunity (DOGSO)”.

It went from bad to worse for United too, with Dominik Szoboszlai scoring from 19 yards out from the ensuing free-kick.

But while little was made of the incident at the time, some – including Roy Keane in the Sky Sports studio – pointed out that the challenge may not have been worthy of a red card as Isak was unlikely to stay in control of the ball after taking a poor touch.

And Pochettino agreed, stating that “the ball was for the goalkeeper”.

“In that situation, if you review the VAR you can see for sure, he [Yoro] never had the intention to connect with Isak,” explained Pochettino on Sky Sports after full-time.

“When the control was long with the striker, the ball was going to the goalkeeper, he runs and goes down. In Leny's case, it wasn't intentional. It was an unusual situation that is normal, everyone who plays football can feel it.

"We have to take responsibility, I take the responsibility. We keep pushing. It's not going to change what happened. Being respectful is the most important. At the same time, I have to defend my club. And tell what I feel. That was an action that everyone who plays football, it was clear that a situation that was not a red card,” he added.

Kobbie Mainoo agreed with his manager, saying there is “no chance” it deserved to be a sending off.

“The ball's already with the goalkeeper. They're both running full speed. What can we do? It is what it is,” said the United midfielder.

The result leaves United in 19th after 11 games, with just eight points and one win in the league all season. As for Liverpool, they rise to fourth with 21 points, just seven behind leaders Arsenal.
