Is McIlroy Europe's greatest golfer ever?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Rory McIlroy shares the record with Colin Montgomerie on eight Race to Dubai titles but faces a tougher challenge for a ninth in 2027, with Tyrrell Hatton and Jon Rahm set to return full-time to the DP World Tour; Paul McGinley says McIlroy remains "very important" to the European Tour

Rory McIlroy's quest to become the greatest European player in golfing history continued as he topped the DP World Tour's season-long Order of Merit for a record-equalling eighth time.

McIlroy has emulated Colin Montgomerie's tally and will look to usurp the Scot with a ninth triumph in 2027 - although is aware that will be quite the feat with LIV alumni Tyrrell Hatton and Jon Rahm set to make full-time returns to the European-based circuit.

The position of the continent's golfing GOAT is obviously a subjective one, but McIlroy's numbers mean he definitely deserves to be in that conversation.

No European has spent as many weeks at world No 1 as his 122. Only one European - Harry Vardon, whom the DP World Tour Order of Merit silverware is named after - has won more men's majors, with Vardon scooping seven to McIlroy's six (and possibly counting).

Should the Northern Irishman claim a ninth Harry Vardon Trophy next year - he has already publicly said he is targeting double figures in time - and perhaps also furnish his cabinet with more major titles, he may be considered Europe's unequivocal kingpin.

Speaking after a second-placed finish to Hatton at the DP World Tour Championship, McIlroy said: "Some things mean more to me than other things.

"I feel a theme of my career over the last few years is trying to get to a point where other people may consider me as the best European that's ever played.

"I'm a proud European. That's something that I would be very proud to achieve. Getting to eight Order of Merits and matching Monty is probably a step to doing that.

"Next year will be tough because you've got the likes of Jon [Rahm] and Tyrrell [Hatton] and some of these guys coming back playing a full-time DP World Tour schedule, [whereas] I'll be bouncing around a little bit on both the DP World Tour and the PGA Tour.

"It's certainly a big incentive [to win six Race to Dubai titles in succession]. Monty [Montgomerie] won seven in a row. It will be incredibly competitive, a little more difficult.

"I'll just have to go out there and play well and accumulate points early. If I have to beef up my schedule at the end of next year compete with them, if I'm in with a close enough chance of doing it, I'll do that."

Rahm and Hatton are currently unable to play on the PGA Tour following their previous switches to LIV Golf, which recently announced its intention to wind down after failing to secure funding.

Sky Sports pundit and Ryder Cup-winning captain Paul McGinley said of McIlroy: "To be the best European of all time, you have got to win more Order of Merits, be supportive of the DP World Tour and obviously there are major championships as well.

"Those three things really push him. If you look at all the things that he does in golf, those three things are kind of what motivates him the most.

"Is the world ranking No 1 position really that important to him? I don't think so.

"It's going to be difficult next year to overtake the likes of Rahm, who will play double the tournaments that will count for Race to Dubai points than Rory. That doesn't mean he can't do it.

"I think Rory is a very important guy for the tour in a lot of areas. He's really important for his negotiations with the PGA Tour, because of his loyalty over here. The PGA Tour want a big piece of him too, but his loyalty is very much over here.

"I think he's a big ally for Guy Kinnings [DP World Tour CEO], when it comes to these negotiations that are going on.

"Let's not discount the role that he's playing for the DP World Tour going forward.

McIlroy says his immediate target is victory at the Australian Open next month, a tournament he previously won in 2013.

He added, of an event live on Sky Sports from December 3: "That's really the next goal, get that next win. I've played a lot of good golf this year. I've only won once [at The Masters] but it was a big one.

"It would be nice to cap the year off with a victory."
