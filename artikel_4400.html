Still partnered with and owned by XBOX, the title of Bethesda’s newest fantasy epic has been a bit of an open secret, one finally confirmed by the biggest names at XBOX. In a Twitter post, Asha Sharma herself announced the title of Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel. Continuing the theme of subtitles marking the game’s major locale, it’s easy to assume ES6 will take place in the capital of the same name.

Elder Scrolls 6 won’t be the first time series long players have entered the opulent city of Sentinel, as the desert-flanked capital also appeared in Elder Scrolls Online, and as far back as the original The Elder Scrolls: Arena.

Sentinel has a rich history for both players and the setting itself, standing apart as a city flanked by opposites, in both politics and environment. The split between ocean and desert does wonders to make it feel truly fantastical, while Bethesda has taken care to use real-world analogues to make the title location feel genuine.

After Skyrim’s focus on ice-cold mountains and Norse-themed culture, the Redguard housing Sentinel is set to be a major visual shift, one primed to breathe new life into the franchise. Bethesda is kind of playing with fire here, as many are expecting Sentinel to be an absolute blockbuster rivaling Skyrim’s universal acclaim.
