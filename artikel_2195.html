3 midfielders Blackburn Rovers should target in January after Mowbray admission, including 2018/19 loan star

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Blackburn Rovers only signed three players in the summer transfer window, and sights are already set on January targets. Tony Mowbray has voiced a need to bolster in central midfield, with Adam Forshaw’s absence leaving only Kristi Montgomery, Sam Morsy, Moussa Baradji and Jake Garrett to pick from.

Rovers aren’t flush for cash, so they will likely have to be shrewd in their winter business. A lack of experience in the absences of Morsy and Forshaw mean some extra pedigree would be welcomed too.

Here, we put forward three options for the recruitment team at Ewood Park to consider…

First up is someone who would be a highly popular signing at Blackburn Rovers. Midfielder Harrison Reed spent a highly successful loan spell at Ewood Park in the 2018/19 season, providing three goals and seven assists in 36 games while away from former club Southampton.

He would join Fulham off the back of his Rovers stay, and he has played 212 times for the club since. However, Reed is down the pecking order under Alvaro Arbeloa having failed to make a Premier League matchday squad since August, despite being fit and available.

A drop to the Championship would give him the game time he deserves. Reed would bring experience, leadership and a well-rounded skillset to Tony Mowbray’s side and would be a very impressive addition.

A player in a similar position to Reed is Alan Browne, who has bags of Championship experience to his name but is currently struggling for minutes in the Premier League with Sunderland. The Irishman has only played in cup competitions and it seems highly unlikely that he makes a resurgence at the Stadium of Light.

Browne has been a player of interest to Blackburn Rovers before, and his vast second tier experience would make him an influential player on and off the pitch. The 31-year-old would bring leadership in the dressing room, offering an energetic box-to-box presence in midfield.

This paired with a strong technical game and versatility made Browne a regular in Middlesbrough’s push for the Championship play-offs while on loan last season.

Last but not least is a more left-field option, but Julius Magnusson would be a great player for Blackburn Rovers and potentially a real hit in the Championship. The 28-year-old Iceland international is a key man for Swedish side Elfsborg, and he has stood out over their 2026 season.

Magnusson is a very well-rounded player, chipping in with six goals and three assists in 30 games across all competitions this year, as per Fotmob. Amongst Allsvenskan midfielders, he ranks amongst the best in defensive contributions (200 – top 1%), interceptions (53 – top 3%), aerial duels won (55 – top 1%), line-breaking passes (217 – top 13%) and touches (1,659 – top 8%).

Those stats reflect his wide skillset, and at his age, he likely wouldn’t break the bank. Magnusson could come in and be a real hit for Rovers if they are happy to pay a fee to pry him out of his contract until 2029.
