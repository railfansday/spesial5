New Sheffield Wednesday training ground footage has got Owls fans excited

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Footage has emerged of new first team assistant coach at Sheffield Wednesday Antony Sweeney making a strong impression with the players at training.

Dave Challinor was officially appointed as the new manager by the Owls hierarchy earlier this week after the decision to dismiss Henrik Pedersen.

The Yorkshire outfit will be hoping that the 51-year-old makes a positive impact, with the club targeting a promotion push this season.

New owners Arise Capital have made a big impact since taking over at Hillsborough last May, and have overseen fresh appointments at CEO and sporting director, with Simon Wilson taking over in the latter role during the summer.

Wilson previously worked with Challinor at Stockport County, with the manager overseeing two promotions, as well as consecutive play-off finishes in League One in the last two campaigns.

He has brought Sweeney and former QPR defender Clint Hill with him as part of his backroom staff, with his first game in charge coming up this weekend.

Footage has emerged of new coach Sweeney addressing the players, emphasising the high standards that are now being expected under this new management staff.

With an authoritative voice, the 43-year-old claimed that straightforward passes into the channel will not be missed at Sheffield Wednesday, as he commands the attention of the first team squad.

“It’s a four-yard channel, we do not miss that channel at this football club,” said Sweeney, via Liam Norcliffe on X.

“We’re too good to do that, standards are higher.”

Sweeney previously worked with Challinor at Stockport, joining the club in August 2025 after their play-off disappointment in League One.

He was an important figure as a first team coach as the Hatters finished third in the table, again reaching the play-offs, as they lost to Bolton Wanderers in the final at Wembley Stadium.

The pair also worked together at Hartlepool United, and have reunited at Hillsborough alongside Hill, who was also at both of Challinor’s former clubs.

Sheffield Wednesday fans have all had a very similar reaction to seeing Sweeney’s demands about higher standards, suggesting it’s long overdue.

Supporters are excited by the prospect of this new coaching staff getting to work with the players.

The Owls will be hoping that the desire for higher standards will actually bear fruit on the pitch.

Standards was the key word that almost every Sheffield Wednesday supporter noticed from this new footage, highlighting where they feel the team needs to improve under this new regime.

Challinor’s first game in charge will come away to Oldham Athletic on 8 November in a 5.15pm kick-off.

The removal of Pedersen as head coach at Sheffield Wednesday is essentially the last remnant of the old regime leaving.

Challinor’s arrival makes him Arise Capital’s first managerial appointment, and means that this is now their vision for the future of the club.

Speaking about higher standards in training is the kind of strong first impression the new staff will want to make with the squad, and the fans.

But the proof will be in the pudding, and Sunday’s trip to Oldham will be everyone’s first opportunity to see if that can translate into improved performances and results in League One.
