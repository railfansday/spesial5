Charlie Austin tells QPR what they must do to be taken seriously

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Former QPR striker Charlie Austin believes Julien Stephan’s side must prove their top eight credentials by beating Millwall on Saturday.

It has been a good season for the R’s so far, with the Londoners currently occupying the final play-off spot in the new extended format.

There have been lots of positives for QPR to take in the opening third of the campaign as well, as they’ve been hard to beat, losing just three times, and they have had some very impressive results.

However, there are certainly ways that QPR can improve, and Stephan will want his team to show a ruthless streak as they compete for promotion.

A trip to The Den to take on Millwall on Saturday represents a chance for the R’s to end a five-game winless run on the road.

And, speaking on his Chaz Kicks Off podcast, popular former QPR man Austin cooled talk of Stephan’s side being genuine top two contenders, and he explained why beating a team like Millwall could give the group extra belief as they chase the play-offs.

“Millwall have had a drop off, so the R’s could go there, and it wouldn’t be an upset. It’s way too early to cement yourself (as play-off contenders), but if you want to be taken seriously, of being involved in the play-offs, forget the automatics, then you’ve got to go and take points off teams that are there come May.

“I expect Millwall to be around it in May, but they’re not hitting the heights of last season. So I expect the R’s to go there all guns blazing.

“Ilias Chair is back which is a massive boost. You’ve got Charles coming on the back of two fantastic games for Northern Ireland, so for me, I think everything is there to keep momentum going - and I expect them to do that.”

Even though Millwall have had a difficult campaign so far, most would agree that they are a very good team at this level, as injuries have really impacted their start.

Alex Neil’s side will still fancy their chances of reaching the top eight, but they know they must start to show some consistency, which they haven’t done.

So, QPR will be expecting a difficult game, and it’s fair for Austin to state that this would be a statement win for the R’s if they could head home with all three points.

Millwall did the double over QPR last season, and they also knocked Stephan’s side out of the League Cup back in August.

QPR have won at Portsmouth and Blackburn, who are above Millwall in the table, so Stephan’s side won’t head to The Den with any fear.

But, with the Lions having finished third last season, it’s fair to say that this is a game against an opponent with a lot of quality.
