Bristol City must eye ex-Premier League boss if they sack Michael Skubala: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Bristol City managed to lure the 44-year-old away from Lincoln City in the summer on a three-year deal after he guided the Imps to the League One title. However, he has struggled to adapt to life in the Championship and his side’s recent form hasn’t been great.

The Robins are reportedly sizing up potential replacements as they aim to rise up the table. With that in mind, they should target a move for Steve Cooper if they decide to part ways with Skubala in the near future.

Cooper, 46, is available following his exit from Brondby back in May and he will be weighing up his next move in the game. He has recently been linked with Derby County amid uncertainty over John Eustace’s position, whilst his name was also in the frame for the Burnley job back in June following their relegation from the Premier League.

The Welshman would be ideal for Bristol City and he knows the EFL inside out. They are currently positioned in 17th place in the table after 15 fixtures.

Bristol City will have aspirations of reaching the top flight in the coming years. They have great facilities and a big catchment area.

However, the Robins haven’t been able to get it right yet and need someone like Cooper to lead them forward. He knows what it takes to get out of the second tier after guiding Nottingham Forest up back in 2022.

Prior to his switch to the City Ground, he got Swansea City into the play-offs as well. The Pontypridd-born man is a proven performer at Bristol City and landing him would be a big statement of intent.

Cooper’s more recent spells at Leicester City and Brondby haven’t quite gone to plan. Therefore, he will have a point to prove now and the Robins can provide him with a platform to show what he can do again and give him an exciting project to lead.

Bristol City supporters are becoming increasingly frustrated and the heat is on Skubala after their 5-0 home loss to Norwich City last time out. They are back in action on Saturday away at Middlesbrough.

If the Robins’ hierarchy plan to dismiss Skubala, they would have probably done it during this international break. It appears they are sticking with him for now.

If they do sack him eventually, Cooper should be top of their list of replacements. Other available names consist of Steven Gerrard, Sean Dyche, Rob Edwards and Paul Heckingbottom.
