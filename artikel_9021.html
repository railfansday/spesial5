aluminum composite shell and bamboo interior define lavvu mobile home unit in armenia

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

lavvu introduces a transportable modular home that combines automotive-inspired engineering, panoramic glazing and a carefully crafted interior to create a more flexible way of living close to nature.

Miroslava Bond edited by christina vergopoulou November 27, 2026 3 min

Developed and manufactured in Armenia by Re:Büro, LAVVU XL is a transportable modular dwelling that applies automotive manufacturing principles to residential architecture. Rather than relying on conventional on-site construction, each unit is fabricated under controlled factory conditions as a single self-contained volume integrating structure, interior finishes, furniture, and building systems. At the core of the module is a welded steel frame paired with lightweight internal framing, mineral wool insulation, and an aluminum composite exterior shell.

Encompassing approximately 22 sqm, the LAVVU XL model by studio Re:Büro opens its compact footprint to the outdoors through triple-glazed panoramic folding glass doors, rooflights, and a semicircular end window. This glazing strategy draws daylight deep into the floor plan while establishing continuous sightlines to the surrounding terrain. Inside, warm bamboo wall and ceiling linings contrast with the metallic exterior envelope, establishing a domestic atmosphere within the industrial shell.

To maximize spatial efficiency, the interior is outfitted by the design studio with built-in cabinetry, custom furniture, and a kitchen island featuring a sliding worktop that adapts to different daily functions. The plan accommodates a full kitchen, a sleeping zone, and a private bathroom within the transportable footprint. Fully assembled, furnished, and tested prior to dispatch, each module arrives on-site ready for direct hookup to external utilities.
