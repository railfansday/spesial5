X tests adding professional histories to user profiles

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The platform’s latest experiment with a work experience element is reminiscent of its previous attempt to build out X Jobs two years ago.

X may be gearing up to take on LinkedIn once again, with the platform currently testing professional profiles that let users list their career history.

As shown in these example images, posted on Threads by app researcher Radu Oncescu, X is experimenting with a new “work experience” element, which allows users to add their work history to their X profile.

This is no real surprise, given that X has been working to build out its job listings and professional insights graph for some time. That said, X hasn’t done much on this front for the last couple of years.

In May 2023, X owner Elon Musk hinted that X might be looking to integrate job listings as part of his broader “everything app” vision for the platform. X then launched job ad listings for verified organizations in the app in August that year, which was viewed as the platform’s first step towards facilitating job ads and candidate discovery.

In June 2024, X reported that more than 750,000 open roles had been listed in the app, less than six months after X Jobs was launched.

But two years later, the X Hiring profile in the app has gone dormant, and its dedicated job search option no longer functions.

Evidence would suggest that X simply lost interest in this element. However, with a new push to add professional histories to user profiles, maybe the platform is looking to revive that effort and re-engage job seekers in order to connect them with brands that had previously shown interest in its job market options.

The more skeptical view could be that X is only adding job listings on user profiles so it can extract more data insights, which could then be fed into xAI’s servers for expanded audience insight.

X hasn’t made any official announcement of the option either way, but it is interesting to note the platform’s apparent resurgence in interest on this front.
