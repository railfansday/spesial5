Patrick Mahomes and Pregnant Wife Brittany’s Cutest Family Photos

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Patrick Mahomes and Brittany Mahomes have embraced their roles as parents.

The couple, who started dating in high school, got engaged in August 2020 after Patrick’s Super Bowl LIV win earlier that year. One month after the quarterback proposed, the pair announced that Brittany was pregnant with their first child.

Patrick and Brittany welcomed their eldest daughter, Sterling, in February 2021. They proceeded to tie the knot in March 2022 and three months later, the twosome announced baby No. 2 was on its way. Their son, Bronze, was born in November 2022 followed by daughter Golden in January 2025.

The pair announced in November 2026 that baby No. 4 is on the way.
