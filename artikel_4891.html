Bitmine edges toward 5% of ETH supply as treasuries report buys amid market slump

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Strategy, Strive, Bitmine and Cypherpunk disclosed fresh additions to their crypto treasuries, spanning Bitcoin, Ether and Zcash.

Publicly traded crypto treasury companies Bitmine, Strive and Cypherpunk Technologies disclosed increases in their cryptocurrency holdings on Monday, joining Strategy in accumulating digital assets despite a broader market downturn.

Bitmine, the largest publicly traded Ether treasury company, said it added 7,653 Ether (ETH) over the past week, bringing its total holdings to 6.08 million ETH, about 4.97% of its total supply and bringing it ever closer to its stated aim of accumulating 5% of the ETH supply, according to a Monday announcement.

Top Bitcoin treasury companies Strategy and Strive also reported purchases. Strategy acquired 485 Bitcoin (BTC) for $33.4 million, while Strive bought 251 BTC for $17.5 million, bringing its holdings to 43,404 BTC — just 110 BTC behind Twenty One Capital, the third-largest publicly traded Bitcoin holder, according to BitcoinTreasuries.net data.

Zcash (ZEC) treasury and mining company Cypherpunk Technologies reported buying 3,775.72 ZEC for $3.48 million since September and mining another 5,721.35 ZEC in October. The additions brought its holdings to 341,858.75 ZEC, or around 2% of circulating supply.

The purchases come amid a broader cryptocurrency sell-off. Bitcoin was trading around $65,017, down 12.9% over the past seven days and 20% over the past month. ETH fell 14.2% and ZEC dropped 22.9% over the past week, according to CoinGecko data as of 8:08 pm UTC on Monday.
