Was Vladimir Putin 'snubbed' by Xi Jinping? Viral handshake video is AI fake

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

A viral video appearing to show China's Xi Jinping "snubbing" Vladimir Putin at the APEC summit has racked up millions of views, fuelling speculation about tensions between Beijing and Moscow. But the footage is fake. FRANCE 24 traced the original video to an earlier summit, where the two leaders did shake hands. The creator, who runs a satire account, admitted to manipulating the footage using Grok Imagine, an AI tool developed by Elon Musk's xAI, as Vedika Bahl explains.
