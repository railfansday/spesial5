Rwanda's malaria cases on the rise: Climate change leads to more mosquitoes

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In Rwanda, malaria cases are on the rise due to climate change. Higher temperatures and prolonged rainfall are creating more breeding grounds for mosquitoes. According to researchers, the risk of infection could increase by as much as 20 percent by 2100 in the Rift Valley. Our correspondent Aurore Bayoud reports on Rwanda's efforts to tackle the problem.
