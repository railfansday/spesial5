Philippine star Carmelle wins first Asian edition of Eurovision song contest

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Philippines has won the first Asian edition of the Eurovision Song Contest. The Filipino singer beat ten other acts to take the title in Bangkok, with a song celebrating women's empowerment. A taste of the winning song with Jack Pownall.
