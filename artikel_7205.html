LinkedIn adds monetization tab to profile analytics

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The new element will allow creators to access opportunities and see data on their profile performance in the platform’s Creator Marketplace.

LinkedIn is taking the next step in its creator monetization push by adding monetization to its profile analytics.

Right now, LinkedIn’s analytics section has two parts: content analytics and audience analytics. But soon, LinkedIn will roll out a separate monetization tab, where creators will be able to access information on various potential income streams.

As shown in this example screen, which LinkedIn shared last month within a larger announcement about evolving creator opportunities, LinkedIn’s monetization tab will provide users with easy access to the platform’s monetization opportunities. The section will also offer guides and advice on how to sign up.

In addition, the monetization tab will show daily prospect suggestions and info on in-stream ads and related opportunities, as well as data on profile performance in LinkedIn’s Creator Marketplace.

Over the past year, LinkedIn has been developing its creator monetization options, which include affiliate marketing opportunities and brand sponsorships for selected creator content.

Last month, LinkedIn launched initial testing of pre-roll ads on creator content. This will give marketers the opportunity to display promotions alongside creator and publisher videos.

As the platform continues to add users and overall usage and engagement levels rise, LinkedIn is now exploring more ways to keep its top creators active and keep users coming back to the app.

The launch of a monetization dashboard suggests that this will become an even bigger focus in 2027.
