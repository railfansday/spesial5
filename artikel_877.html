Tanggal: 2026-11-26

By the end of another utterly chaotic 90 minutes in the Manchester United soap opera, there was only one person who had endured a worse night than Senne Lammens.

Watching this game back will make for grim viewing for the United goalkeeper who, having been one of the club’s shining lights last season, is steadily becoming enveloped in a crisis of his own in this most torrid of campaigns.

But Roberto Rosetti, Uefa’s chief refereeing officer, might also want to sit Szymon Marciniak down after this and pick over the Polish official’s own nightmare performance. Marciniak denied United two goals, both of which should have stood, and in doing so ended up shining an even more brutal lens on Lammens’s calamitous display.

United’s Belgian goalkeeper was responsible for both of Sporting’s goals. He needlessly conceded a penalty in the first half, after a rush of blood to the head, that was converted by Luis Suárez, just 75 seconds after he had saved a spot-kick from the Sporting striker.

United had recovered from those bewildering few minutes to lead early in the second half, courtesy of two goals from captain Bruno Fernandes against his old club.

But, 10 minutes from time, Lammens committed the kind of howler that becomes worse and worse with every viewing. In doing so, he handed Sporting an equaliser and ensured United remain without an away win this season.
