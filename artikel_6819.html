69 of the Best Kendall Jenner Street Style Looks

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Kendall Jenner was first thrust into the public eye nearly two decades ago, as the quiet, tomboyish 11-year-old younger sister on Keeping Up With the Kardashians. But in 2014, the second-youngest member of the Kar-Jenner dynasty struck out to forge her own path as a model. Since her runway debut for Marc Jacobs’ fall 2014 show, Jenner has become a ubiquitous presence, not only on our TV screens, but on the runway and in magazines, too. With such a jam-packed schedule, the budding model became a regular street style subject, as she bounced from show to show.

Early photos captured Jenner’s style at the beginning of its evolution. Even at 19, she shows the same fashion DNA that she has today: a love of simplicity. As she’s become more entrenched in the fashion world, her interest in fashion history has broadened, as has her wardrobe. Case in point: these days, you can find Jenner mixing Matthieu Blazy’s Chanel with The Row or showing off her latest vintage Mugler grail.

Over the last 10 years, Kendall Jenner has gone from nascent model to fashion fiend. Below, in honor of the model’s birthday, revisit some of her best street style looks from 2014 until now.
