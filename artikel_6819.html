Nicole Kidman Brings Her Youngest Daughter, Faith Margaret, to the Starry Chanel Cruise Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The pair looked so in sync while showing off their individual styles

When it comes to fashion events, Nicole Kidman tends to brings her eldest biological daughter, Sunday Rose, 18, as her plus-one. This past May, the two walked the red carpet together at the Met Gala in fabulous designer gowns, mere months after Sunday hit the runway for the Dior Spring/Summer 2026 haute couture show. Not to mention, Sunday has also accompanied her mother to events ranging from private Miu Miu parties to the 2024 Paris Summer Olympics.

So, it came as a bit of a surprise when, at the Chanel Cruise 2026-27 show in Sydney, Australia, this morning, Kidman decided instead to bring along her youngest daughter, Faith Margaret Kidman Urban, 15.

At the show, Kidman went classic in a black suit look that was like a deconstructed tuxedo. The ensemble consisted of a cropped black jacket with coattails around back, which she paired with matching straight-leg slacks and layered over contrasting white bases.

Kidman left her jacket open to reveal a thin white vest, which itself was layered over a white tank top. It was a simple, but elegant fit, and the Oscar winner wore a pair of inky satin pumps to round things out.

Faith, meanwhile, looked a bit cozier in a ribbed zip-up jacket in cream, paired with a matching mini skirt. The jacket featured two tiers of gold-studded pockets in front, and it was closed all the way up to its pointy collar. As for accessories, the 15-year-old carried a suede, camel-colored handbag, and she slipped into a pair of strappy silver mules down low.

While in years past Kidman may have been opting to shield her youngest child from the spotlight, Faith has been accompanying her mother to more and more events as of late.

Back in September, the two were seen attending the US Open together, and just last month, Faith sat front row alongside her mom at the Chanel Spring/Summer 2027 ready-to-wear show.

Don’t be surprised if you see more of this high-profile teen at future events.
