iOS 27.3 adds Siri AI usage limits, here’s what counts toward your limit

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Earlier this week, Apple revealed that Siri AI usage limits will be implemented in iOS 27.3 and explained how iCloud+ increases those limits. Now we have a real-world example of what it looks like when limits are hit, and details on what counts toward those limits.

Siri AI and select other AI features will begin having usage limits in iOS 27.3 for advanced tasks.

This is standard practice with other AI services like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude because these tools rely on server-based models rather than running entirely on-device.

Here’s an example from the iOS 27.3 beta showing what it looks like when you hit your Siri AI daily usage limit. Per Ben in a post on X:

In a follow-up post, Ben clarifies that complex questions did stop working shortly after posting. As expected though, basic requests continued to work as usual—they don’t have a usage limit.
