Mohya dropped by Germany rumours labelled "simply nonsense" by Klopp

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Jürgen Klopp vented his frustration over the headlines today that Wael Mohya was 'dropped' from Germany's squad to face Serbia tomorrow.

"I'd like to say something about Wael Mohya, because that's been turned into a headline [of being] ‘dropped from the squad.’

“That's completely missing the point. It's simply nonsense,” Klopp fumed in his press conference tonight ahead of Friday's game.

"We obviously haven't dropped him. I spoke to Wael beforehand. Unless something changes, he won't be in the squad.

"We want him to experience everything. If I imagine being 17 myself and someone telling me I could be part of a team like this.

"I would have taken absolutely any opportunity to make that happen. That's exactly how it is.

“He's training brilliantly and he's in a great place, but he hasn't been dropped. He's here with us,” Klopp concluded on the topic.
