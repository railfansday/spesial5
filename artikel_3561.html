Russell Brand Accuser Calls Out 'Absolute Lies' After Prison Statement

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A woman who accused Russell Brand of raping her when she was 16 has slammed the actor’s recent statement maintaining his innocence following his sexual assault conviction.

“I feel like at this point, I shouldn’t really be shocked by anything that he does because he was making videos every day after being in court all day,” Mia Latter, who recently revealed her identity while detailing her allegations against Brand, 51, said on BBC Radio 4’s Today on Tuesday, November 17.

“But this was really shocking to me … to have this Christian message alongside absolute lies, it was just such a contradiction,” Latter, 37, continued.

“It was appalling, frankly, it was upsetting to watch,” she added of Brand’s message.

Brand’s wife, Laura Gallacher, issued a statement on his behalf via YouTube on Sunday, November 15.

“Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, God bless you for your support. As you know, I am not guilty for the offenses I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening,” the statement read.

A spokesperson for U.K. Prime Minister Keir Starmer announced on Monday, November 16, that Brand’s message is being investigated as a possible violation of a law that prohibits prisoners from making statements that may be upsetting to victims.

Brand was found guilty earlier this month on one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault against four women between 2004 and 2009. He was acquitted of three other charges. Brand was subsequently sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison on November 9.

Latter, who was not a complainant in Brand’s criminal case, said she “felt for those women that stood trial and everything they went through.”

“For him to say, ‘I’m innocent, I’m not guilty,’ well, you are. You’re a convicted rapist now,” she said on Tuesday. “There’s no arguing with that.”

Though Latter was not involved in Brand’s criminal trial, she previously accused the comedian of rape alongside five other women in a Times, Sunday Times and Channel 4 investigation in 2023.

Latter waived her right to anonymity and spoke publicly for the first time on Saturday, November 14, to accuse Brand of raping her when she was 16 and he was 30. She is now campaigning for the U.K. to introduce a staggered age of consent that would make relationships between 16- and 17-year-olds and adults illegal.

“It’s shocking to me time and time again that whenever the news comes up again about him, it’s always in the comments about the relationship with a 16-year-old,” she told The Times on Saturday. “That’s not the most heinous bit. But why is that the thing that everybody collectively gets stuck on? I think we all sort of agree that … adults shouldn’t be allowed to have sexual relationships with children, with somebody under the age of 18. That really doesn’t seem like a gigantic leap to take.”
