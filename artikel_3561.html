Just Like GTA 6, The Witcher 4 Has A PlayStation Deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

CD Projekt Red has announced that just today, November 24, the Polish studio signed new deals with Sony Interactive Entertainment for The Witcher 4's upcoming marketing campaign, among other efforts.

CFO Piotr Nielubowicz told investors that the deals cover CD Projekt Red's games on PlayStation and in the PlayStation Plus catalog, as well as "our collaboration during the upcoming Witcher 4 marketing campaign."

“This cooperation will support our business and financial outlook in this and future years," he said, according to VGC.

No specifics were made available, but the partnership for The Witcher 4's marketing campaign has people theorizing that it could be similar to Rockstar's co-marketing deal with Sony for GTA 6.

Under that agreement, GTA 6 was marketed as a game that "plays best on PS5," even if the jury is out on that being true. Sony also created special GTA 6 DualSense controllers and even custom consoles for NBA players. The PlayStation branding was prioritized over Xbox to give people the impression that they should get it on PlayStation.

The Witcher 4 is in the works now and is expected to launch in 2028 for console and PC. The game kicks off a new trilogy of The Witcher games, which will also include The Witcher 5 and The Witcher 6. These will all be released within a six-year window, so the whole new trilogy could be out by 2034.

The studio is “targeting” a 2028 release for The Witcher 4, so it could be delayed like The Witcher 3 and Cyberpunk 2077 were before it.
