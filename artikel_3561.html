Brittany Mahomes Pops in Red at Patrick's Game After Revealing Pregnancy

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Pregnant Brittany Mahomes brought the whole brood to her husband Patrick Mahomes’ Kansas City Chiefs’s Sunday, November 15, away game against the Atlanta Falcons.

Brittany, 31, sizzled in a buttoned-up red coat and oversized sunglasses as she watched Patrick, also 31, lead the Chiefs in what the teams hopes to be a second consecutive win. For glam, she styled her signature blonde tresses in a wavy ponytail with face-framing tendrils.

Before kickoff, Brittany brought the couple’s three children down to the field. (Patrick and Brittany are parents of daughters Sterling, 5, and Golden, 22 months, and son Bronze, 3.)

“This child literally walked into the stadium with [a] waffle in hand,” Brittany quipped in footage shared to her Instagram Stories, referring to Golden’s pregame snack of choice. “No waffle left behind!”

Golden, just like her mom, was equally decked out to cheer on her dad in a white puffer coat with “Mahomes No. 15” on the back, a nod to Patrick’s jersey number. Her jacket coordinated with a pair of red leggings, much like older sister Sterling who matched hers with an adorable pink dress. Bronze, for his part, wore black.

Just days before Sunday’s game, Patrick and Brittany revealed that they are expecting baby No. 4 — and, yes, the influencer is aware of the viral link between her pregnancies and Patrick’s Super Bowl appearances.

“Glad someone finally figured this out, just doing my part over here,” she joked via her Instagram Stories on Thursday, November 12, after fans pointed out that every year Brittany has been pregnant the Chiefs have clinched a Super Bowl berth.

Brittany had been pregnant with Sterling when the Chiefs lost to the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in Super Bowl LV. The former soccer star gave birth to her second child, Bronze in November 2022 just months after the Chiefs defeated the Philadelphia Eagles at Super Bowl LVII. The Mahomeses most recently added Golden to their family in January 2025, shortly before the Eagles defeated the Chiefs in Super Bowl LIX.

While it’s too soon to tell whether Brittany’s pregnancies are indeed the Chiefs’ lucky charm, she and Patrick are just excited about becoming a family of six.

“Obviously, it’s a blessing to have another baby on the way. I’m excited for it,” Patrick said in a Wednesday, November 11, press conference. “I’m hoping that me and Bronze can even up the score [with another baby boy]. I’m just praying for a healthy baby at the end of the day.”

As for the viral link between Brittany’s pregnancy and the Chiefs’ Super Bowl odds, Patrick is aware of the coincidence.

“I’m hoping,” he quipped. “I’m hoping if we can keep that going and get another Super Bowl win with a baby on the way that’d be awesome for sure.”
