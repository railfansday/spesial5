Jennifer Lawrence Reunites With 'Hunger Games' Costars at NYC Premiere

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The odds are in Us’ favor because Jennifer Lawrence just staged an epic Hunger Games reunion.

The Oscar winner was joined by cast members from the original Hunger Games movies — including Josh Hutcherson and Woody Harrelson — at the New York City premiere of the latest installment in the franchise, The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, on Monday, November 16.

Lawrence, 36, who played protagonist Katniss Everdeen, was photographed posing with Hutcherson (who played Peeta Mellark), Harrelson (Haymitch Abernathy) and Elizabeth Banks (Effie Trinket), all of whom first brought Panem, the devious Capitol and heroic District 12 to life in the first four Hunger Games released between 2012 and 2015.

The actress wore a long black coat with a fur trim and floral detailing, in keeping with the movie’s floral promotional artwork.

Joining the family reunion were fellow Hunger Games OGs Willow Shields, who played Katniss’ sister Primrose Everdeen, Jena Malone, who played Johanna Mason, and Amandla Stenberg, who shot to fame with her epic turn as Rue in the first film.

Sadly, there was no Liam Hemsworth — who played Hutcherson’s love rival Gale Hawthorne — on the red carpet to complete this epic reunion.

However, the original cast was joined by the stars of the 2023 prequel The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes: Rachel Zegler, Tom Blyth, and Josh Andrés Rivera.

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is a sequel set after the events of The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes and features an all-new cast taking the deadly Hunger Games, led by Mckenna Grace as Maysilee Donner. Joseph Zada plays a younger version of Haymitch, while Elle Fanning takes over the role of a younger version of irreverent stylist Effie Trinket.

Lawrence, Hutcherson and Harrelson will also reprise their respective roles in the movie more than a decade after they were last seen together in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 2.

The actress spoke about her cameo during an October appearance on the “Call Her Daddy” podcast.

“Oh my, unbelievable. It was so crazy because it was the same crew. It was the same camera guys, the same AD who I’ve worked with a million times too, because I love him, and Josh and Woody,” she said. “It was so emotional. It was like, Josh and I didn’t walk to the bathroom without our arms around each other. It was so beautiful.”

As for Harrelson, Lawrence joked, “And Woody obviously came through the window of my hotel room uninvited because it would not be the same. It wouldn’t be the same if he listened to boundaries and used a door.”

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is in theaters on Friday, November 20
