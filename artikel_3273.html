Skyward Specialty Insurance Group, Inc. (SKWD -5.50%) reported third quarter results characterized by significant growth in fee-based income and specialty niches, partially offset by targeted pullbacks in softening property and energy markets. Management reported that the company's Rule Our Niche strategy allowed for the reallocation of capital toward high-return segments like accident and health and strategic distribution partnerships. The integration of the Apollo acquisition has provided a capital-light growth engine, with managed premiums now exceeding $1.3 billion. The company continues to utilize its Bionic Underwriting platform to drive down expense ratios while maintaining a record high IBNR reserve level to protect against casualty market volatility.
