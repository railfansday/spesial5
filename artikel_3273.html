Halestorm, Brothers Osborne, Hardy Sing for Brooks & Dunn at Ferocious 2026 CMA Tribute

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The 2026 CMA Awards received a hefty dose of hard rock when Halestorm kicked off a tribute to Brooks & Dunn, this year’s recipients of the Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award.

Led by dynamic lead singer Lzzy Hale, the Nashville-based band delivered an amped-up take on “Boot Scootin’ Boogie.” The song wasn’t a stretch for the group: They recorded a version of track, originally on B&D’s 1991 debut album Brand New Man, for the 2024 Reboot II tribute LP.

Halestorm didn’t go it alone: The Grammy-winning band, with both Hale and Joe Hottinger on guitar, Josh Smith on bass, and Tommy Clufetos on drums (Arejay Hale, the group’s regular drummer had a scheduling conflict), backed up the rest of the guests. Marcus King tackled “Rock My World (Little Country Girl),” Brothers Osborne recreated “Hard Workin’ Man,” and Hardy howled his way through “Hillbilly Deluxe.” (Each of those artists previously recorded those songs on either the Reboot or Reboot II albums.)

The whole spectacle ended with Luke Bryan taking lead on “Play Something Country,” with Brad Paisley on guitar. Following the performance, Taylor Swift emerged to present the duo of Ronnie Dunn and Kix Brooks with their lifetime achievement honors. Past recipients include Vince Gill, George Strait, Alan Jackson, Loretta Lynn, and, of course, the award’s namesake, Willie Nelson.

The 60th CMA Awards were hosted by Lainey Wilson and featured performances by top nominee Ella Langley, Stephen Wilson Jr. with Chris Stapleton, Red Clay Strays, and Swift, making her first CMA Awards performance in 13 years.
