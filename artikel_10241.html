Storage is expensive right now, but these deals are worth it if you’re desperate for space

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

We can blame AI for many things, least of all the steep increase in the cost of integral storage and memory we need for PCs, Macs, phones, and consoles.

I don’t know if you’re aware – I really hope you are – but prices for SSDs, USB storage, SD cards, and yet more important storage components are exponentially higher than ever before. If, like me, you need these to keep a camera running, or maybe store important files, you’ll know the pain of buying any type of storage right now.

Thanks to Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and more, we’re going to be stuck with shockingly bad prices for a while to come.

I’ve tried tracking some of the best prices, and while some are still eye-watering, brands like Lexar, PNY, SanDisk, and even Samsung have discounted some of the most popular storage and memory options. I’ve been tracking a few for a while, and sadly, we’re not going to get insane savings, but some are up to 50% off in very rare cases.

Another thing I would advise is to avoid going for huge drives at this stage unless you desperately need it. A 4TB NVMe SSD is going to be steep, and the price per GB starts to get ridiculous. The best deals start to sour once you get past the 1TB range. I wouldn’t recommend an HDD given the speed discrepancies, but maybe for long-term storage you could pick up a larger, slower drive and back up the important content in your life.

My personal pick for quick, easily accessible storage is the 128GB SanDisk dual USB thumb drive. I’ve used one for several years to transfer files from my phones to PCs and Macs. Fits in your pocket, and although it’s not the fastest, it’s just useful to have a sizable storage device on hand wherever you are. I used to move installation files between my devices, but I’ve even used it as a boot drive when setting up Linux distros on my PCs. It works with Android phones and iPhones too because of the USB-C port. I can’t recommend that one highly enough.
