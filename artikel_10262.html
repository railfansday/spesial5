Black Friday is approaching, and some companies are already rolling out the discounts ahead of the big day. If you’re hunting for PlayStation 5 accessories, we’ve already rounded up some of our favorite controllers and headsets from Sony’s ongoing promotion. However, if you’re looking to expand your library, some of the PS5’s biggest titles are also currently on sale, in some cases by 50 percent or more, until November 30th.

We’ve gathered some of the best games below, but sale standouts include 007 First Light for $34.99 ($35), The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered for $19.99 ($30 off), and NBA 2K27 for $31.49 (about $39 off). You can view the full list of titles on the PlayStation Store, many of which are also currently available at Amazon and other retailers for the same price (or less).
