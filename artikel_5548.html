CoreWeave is planning to raise at least $4 billion in a new high-grade loan

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Morgan Stanley and MUFG are leading the potential financing, which could reach the market as soon as next week

CoreWeave is seeking an investment-grade loan of at least $4 billion, and Morgan Stanley $MS and Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group are heading up the possible deal, according to Bloomberg.

The loan could come to market as soon as next week and would be sold to investors in phases, though the plans are not final and could change, according to Bloomberg. Spokespeople for CoreWeave and Morgan Stanley would not comment, and MUFG did not reply to an inquiry.

The financing is expected to use chips and related contracts as backing, a structure CoreWeave has used to secure cheaper borrowing costs than it gets in the high-yield market, according to Bloomberg. CoreWeave is rated below investment grade.

Early discussions point to a yield of 7% to 7.5% on the new loan, according to Bloomberg, which cited CoreWeave's earnings call on Tuesday. That works out to roughly half of what CoreWeave's junk bonds currently pay.

CoreWeave reported third-quarter results on Tuesday, with revenue of $3.661 billion, up from $1.365 billion a year earlier. The company raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to a range of $13.0 billion to $13.3 billion. Wall Street had predicted an average of $12.9 billion, according to Bloomberg.

The company posted a net loss of $709 million for the quarter and net interest expense of $846 million. It expects interest expense of $1.05 billion to $1.15 billion in the fourth quarter.

CoreWeave stock soared on Wednesday, and its bonds rallied.

In March, CoreWeave closed an $8.5 billion financing under an investment-grade facility supported by its customer agreements with Meta $META Platforms, according to Bloomberg. The March facility is split into two pieces: a floating-rate portion priced at 2.25 percentage points above SOFR, the Secured Overnight Financing Rate, and a fixed-rate portion at roughly 5.9%.
