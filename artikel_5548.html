Bitcoin Price Crashes as Iran Strike and Crypto Hack Spree Shake Markets

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Bitcoin price fell sharply as renewed Iran conflict fears collided with a wave of crypto hacks, giving us a fresh market shock. Reports of US strikes on Iran’s Qeshm Island revived concerns about oil supplies, while a string of security breaches continued to undermine confidence across the digital asset industry.

The sell-off comes as we are grappling with weak crypto sentiment, falling ETF flows and uncertainty surrounding global markets. Bitcoin is now caught between geopolitical risk and a growing security crisis within its own industry.

The latest reported US strikes added uncertainty to an eight-month conflict that has disrupted shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. Oil prices edged higher after the reports, with Brent crude trading near $105 per barrel today.

The Iran narrative returned to the forefront after reports that US forces struck Qeshm Island, near the Strait of Hormuz. The US officials themself described the strikes as defensive, but the development threatens to complicate diplomatic efforts between Washington and Tehran. The two sides have continued contacts aimed at ending the conflict, but no breakthrough has emerged.

The Strait of Hormuz remains a major concern for global markets. The waterway previously carried around one-fifth of global oil and natural gas supplies, making any disruption a potential threat to energy prices and inflation.

Higher oil price could complicate the outlook for central banks and keep financial conditions tight. That would leave risk assets, including Bitcoin, vulnerable if investors shift toward cash and safer investments.

Asian equities also retreated today as investors weighed geopolitical risks, technology-sector weakness and uncertainty ahead of US midterm elections. Reuters reported that MSCI’s Asia-Pacific index excluding Japan fell 1.4%, while South Korean shares dropped as much as 4.1%.

Rather than acting as a safe haven, BTC has continued to trade like a risk-sensitive asset during periods of market stress.

The geopolitical shock arrives as crypto investors are still processing one of the industry’s largest security incidents. Binance suffered a breach involving $6.3 billion in crypto assets. Customers withdrew billions of dollars following the incident, raising questions about confidence in centralized trading platforms.

The exchange said it would cover the losses, while withdrawals later began stabilizing. However, the incident has added another source of uncertainty for traders already navigating a fragile market.

The Binance breach is not an isolated case. Recent attacks have also hit Mercado Bitcoin, Polymarket and infrastructure providers serving crypto platforms.

Mercado Bitcoin disclosed a security incident involving customer-related systems, while Polymarket experienced unauthorized withdrawals through a compromised third-party provider. The incidents have renewed concerns about the security of exchanges, wallets, and services connected to crypto applications.

A wave of attacks has also targeted older BTCPay Server installations and a vulnerable WordPress crypto-payment plugin, according to recent security reports.

The timing is particularly damaging. Crypto markets need fresh capital and confidence to sustain a recovery, but repeated breaches risk pushing investors toward self-custody or away from the sector altogether.

Bitcoin price now depends on whether geopolitical tensions ease and crypto-specific fears begin to fade. A diplomatic breakthrough between the US and Iran could reduce pressure on oil markets and improve appetite for risk assets. However, another escalation around the Strait of Hormuz could renew inflation concerns and trigger further selling across equities and crypto.

Bitcoin also needs to show that the recent security incidents have not triggered a deeper withdrawal of capital from the industry. Binance’s outflows and the latest exchange breaches will remain important sentiment indicators.

For now, the Iran story is unfolding alongside a wider Bitcoin and crypto confidence test. If the sell-off continues, Bitcoin could face another challenge to its recovery. If geopolitical fears cool and the hacking fallout remains contained, buyers may get an opportunity to step back in.

Can BTC stabilize despite pressure from both global markets and the industry’s own security failures?
