Build-to-Rent Deliveries Expected to Slow Further

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Following a wave of new supply, build-to-rent (BTR) deliveries have moved lower. Zonda’s third-quarter 2026 Rental Housing Outlook shows quarterly deliveries totaled roughly 17,000 units in the first quarter of 2025 before declining to just over 10,000 units in the third quarter of 2026. The near-term forecast points to additional slowing, reflecting a pullback from the elevated levels of new supply seen in recent years.

Specifically, deliveries are projected to fall below 10,000 units in the fourth quarter before declining again in the first quarter of 2027. The slowdown in overall deliveries could gradually reshape competitive dynamics in markets that have experienced significant rental development. However, the impact will depend on how quickly existing inventory is absorbed and whether renter demand keeps pace with new supply. Looking ahead to 2027, these dynamics will be an important factor shaping lease-up activity, concessions, and future development opportunities.
