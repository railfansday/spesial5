Newrez Launches Personal Loans, Powered by Upgrade

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Newrez—the residential mortgage lender and servicer—announced the launch of Newrez Personal Loans, powered by Upgrade. The new offering expands the financial tools available to eligible Newrez homeowners, providing access to funds for a variety of financial needs, such as debt consolidation, home improvements and unexpected expenses.

According to a release, personal loans are available through a partnership with Upgrade—a financial technology company that offers consumer credit products—and are issued by Upgrade’s bank partner, Cross River Bank.

“Our relationship with homeowners doesn’t begin and end with a mortgage,” Newrez CCO Leslie Gillin stated. “Homeowners’ financial needs often extend beyond their monthly mortgage payment, and we’re proud to broaden the ways we can support them, including helping identify opportunities for savings as they navigate important financial decisions. Partnering with Upgrade is another example of us delivering on our mission to do everything possible to make home happen.”

“Consumers increasingly expect financial solutions to be fast, transparent and easy to access,” said Avia Kay, Upgrade’s senior vice president of marketing. “We’re excited to partner with Newrez to help homeowners explore personal loan options through a simple online experience, supported by the trust Newrez has earned with its customers. Together, we’re making it easier for eligible homeowners to access funding quickly when they need it.”
