Powerhouse Parramatta / Moreau Kusunoki + Genton

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Text description provided by the architects. Funded by the NSW Government and located on Dharug land, Powerhouse Parramatta is a major new landmark for Australia, making Western Sydney a global cultural destination. Its opening marks the most significant cultural infrastructure project since the Sydney Opera House. Powerhouse Parramatta, combined with the relocation of the Collection to Castle Hill, is a $A915 million project, including $A75 million in philanthropic contributions.
