MKBHD orders Tesla Cybercab after Elon Musk calls him a “chicken” over head-shaving bet

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

YouTuber Marques Brownlee, also known as MKBHD, has ordered a Tesla Cybercab after Elon Musk called him a “chicken” over a longstanding bet that involves him shaving his head.

Tesla first unveiled its Cybercab in October 2024, promising a fully autonomous vehicle without a steering wheel or pedals for under $30,000 by the end of 2026. The announcement prompted Brownlee to bet that he would shave his head on camera if Tesla delivered on that promise.

On November 13, Tesla opened Cybercab pre-orders to select customers, with prices starting at $29,990 and deliveries expected in December, as per TechCrunch. However, buyers must pay a $299 monthly subscription, take delivery in select Texas cities, and operate the vehicle in designated areas.

Following the announcement, Brownlee posted on X, arguing that his hair was still safe, pointing out that no vehicles had been delivered and that pre-orders were currently invite-only rather than publicly available.

As detailed by Mashable, he also highlighted the vehicle’s geographical restrictions, joking that it would effectively become a “paperweight” if he took it home, although he acknowledged this wasn’t technically part of the original bet.

Musk subsequently responded to a Tesla supporter criticizing Brownlee for refusing to concede the wager, writing: “He’s a chicken.” However, Brownlee appeared to call Musk’s bluff, sharing a screenshot confirming that he had ordered a Cybercab.

Despite placing an order, Brownlee has yet to concede defeat, with Tesla still needing to deliver the vehicle before the end of 2026, along with questions remaining over whether its invite-only rollout satisfies the original bet.

Whether Brownlee will ultimately have to shave his head, it remains to be seen.
