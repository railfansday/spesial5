McDonald’s new chicken sandwiches are so popular they’re being pulled from menus

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

McDonald’s Japan is temporarily pulling several chicken sandwiches from its menu after demand for its new lineup exceeded expectations, leaving the fast food chain struggling to maintain supplies.

The company announced on November 10 that sales of its Zakuzaku Juicy Mac lineup will be suspended nationwide beginning November 13.

The decision comes after the sandwiches proved more popular than anticipated, creating shortages of the chicken patties used to make them.

The suspension affects several permanent menu items, while a separate limited-time chicken sandwich will disappear earlier than originally planned.

In its announcement, McDonald’s Japan explained that stronger-than-expected sales have made it difficult to consistently secure enough chicken patties for its new menu items.

The company confirmed that seven products will be temporarily removed from menus across Japan, including three standard sandwiches, three double-patty versions available through its Night Mac menu, and a chicken side dish.

McDonald’s also confirmed that its limited-time Cheese Cheese Teriyaki Chicken Juicy Mac will end its run earlier than originally scheduled.

The company warned that individual restaurants could run out of affected products before November 13, depending on their remaining inventory.

Despite the shortages, other chicken products will remain available, including Chicken McNuggets, the McChicken, and the Chicken McMuffin. Several other Night Mac offerings will also continue to be sold as usual.

McDonald’s expects to resume sales of the suspended permanent menu items by the end of November, although an exact return date has not been announced.

The company said it will provide further updates through its official website once preparations to resume sales are complete.

McDonald’s Japan apologized to customers affected by the temporary suspension and thanked them for their understanding as it works to restore supplies.

The temporary suspension comes as McDonald’s Japan prepares to launch a new Genshin Impact collaboration on November 18, featuring special packaging inspired by characters Varesa and Escoffier.

The promotion, which runs through December 8, will feature themed packaging for the Teriyaki Mac Burger and Double Teriyaki Mac Burger, alongside exclusive in-game rewards that customers can redeem through the My McDonald’s Rewards program.
