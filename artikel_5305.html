Since the start of Q4, crypto markets have remained volatile and continued to weaken, with major assets retreating from their highs and market participants becoming more cautious. Against this backdrop, HTX Earn recently launched an APY Boost Promotion as part of the HTX Winter Campaign. The promotion offers APY boosts on 7-day Fixed products available on HTX Earn for seven assets, with APYs of up to 10%. It aims to provide users with a relatively stable way to earn returns amid a market downturn.

Event details: https://www.htx.net.ht/en-us/support/85047689167878/

APY Boosts Available on Both Major Cryptos and Stablecoins

Assets eligible for APY boosts in this promotion include three major cryptocurrencies—BTC, ETH, and TRX—and four stablecoins—USDT, USDC, USDS, and USDD. The promotion is designed to reach a broader range of users. Long-term holders of BTC, ETH, and TRX can earn interest in their respective assets after subscribing, without selling or converting their holdings. They can keep their existing positions while earning returns.  The stablecoin selection includes widely recognized and heavily used assets, giving users more options.

Quota Limit for Boosted APY: Up to $14 Million per User

Repeat Subscriptions Available Upon Maturity

The crypto industry remains in a cyclical adjustment phase. Against this backdrop, HTX is offering APY boosts across multiple assets with generous quota limits, aiming to deliver tangible benefits to users and stand by them through the crypto winter. Putting users first has always been a core principle at HTX. The platform will continue to focus on users’ needs and offer more promotions and benefits, including APY boost campaigns on HTX Earn.
