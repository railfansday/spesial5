Ex-refs chief drops verdict on 'nailed-on' Vanja Dragojevic controversy away from Rangers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Rangers' Vanja Dragojevic was unfortunate to be sent off during Serbia's 3-1 defeat to Germany in the Nations League.

That is according to former PGMOL chief and ex-FIFA referee, Keith Hackett, who exclusively told Football Insider that a yellow card would have sufficed on Friday, 13 November.

The 20-year-old midfielder, much like his time at Ibrox so far, was forced to watch from the bench until being subbed on at the half-time break.

In the 74th minute, however, after a challenge on Joshua Kimmich and a VAR review, he was given his marching orders as Serbia fell to defeat.

Bayern Munich's star was left in a lot of pain following the collision, but upon review, it didn't look nearly as dangerous as it did initially.

Nonetheless, Serbia were forced to finish the game with nine men after another sending-off, but the Rangers man can feel hard done by.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Hackett expected a simple caution to be enough, rather than sending Dragojevic off.

"Challenges where the landing foot lands on the Achilles of the opponent, that does endanger the safety of the opponent," he said.

"For me, there doesn't appear to be the excessive force to meet the criteria for serious foul play, and I think that's where the judgment of the referee has come in.

"I'd question that as a red card. I think it's a nailed-on, reckless card, for sure. I don't think I can fully criticise him for issuing a red card.

"But I think from an English point of view, we'd expect that to be yellow."

Dragojevic has made eight appearances for the Gers since signing from Partizan Beograd on a £4.6million deal.

Only three of them have been starts, but Football Insider understands that there are no current plans to send him out on loan in January.

However, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Keith Wyness believes that it is simply a matter of time before the player seeks an exit already.

Dragojevic would have wanted regular time when signing across the dotted line at Ibrox, but his spell has been anything but positive.
