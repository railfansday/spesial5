A look at how Barnsley and Sheffield Wednesday could line up for their South Yorkshire derby clash

Barnsley and Sheffield Wednesday are set to do battle in a South Yorkshire derby under the Oakwell lights.

The former are languishing in the League One relegation zone, while the latter are sitting fifth and aiming to break into the top two.

Wednesday will be fancied to claim three points at the expense of their neighbours and rivals, but form does tend to go out of the window in derbies.

Barnsley boss Daniel Stendel said: “What I would like is that we have a big support in the game. Not just because it's a derby but also these young guys, without the captain (Cameron McGeehan), need the push from the stands.”

There are plenty of subplots heading into the fixture and one of them concerns Davis Keillor-Dunn. The 28-year-old was a talismanic figure at the top end of the pitch for Barnsley and could face them tonight in the colours of Wednesday.
