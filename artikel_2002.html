West Ham 1 – 1 Preston | Final score, first impression as more points slip away

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

West Ham’s familiar profligacy in front of goal came back to haunt them, as did their propensity to go behind in the first half of games as the points were shared at London Stadium this afternoon.

From Preston’s first corner, the fanbase was understandably apprehensive, knowing West Ham’s weaknesses from dead balls earlier in the season. Again the curse struck, with Piroe heading the ball over his own oncoming goal keeper’s fists, the ball then looping onto the post and allowing a gentle tap-in for Preston to take the lead.

Herrick rallied well after conceding early

The wheels could have come off for West Ham’s young goal keeper at that point: However, all credit to Herrick. It would have been very easy for his confidence to crumble after that early combi-cock-up, but he rallied well and even appeared to be smiling as the game went on, with several catches and a couple of really good saves surely meaning the shirt is his for the time being.

West Ham hit the post four times during a frenetic period in the first half when their intensity was high, but it seemed as though the ball simply would not go between the posts.

Familiar problems surfaced in the second half as the pace dropped and West Ham ran out of ideas, notably after Austbø had been substituted. Manor Solomon, looking very one-footed, simply ran inside and passed square. Frustratingly, the game slowed in pace in the last twenty minutes with the Hammers looking devoid of new ideas just at the time when they should have been upping the tempo.

It is clear that there’s still a shortage of pace in this side and with a build up that got slower as the game went on, West Ham’s bench looked as if it desperately needed an injection of Antonio-like pace to scare opponents and perhaps take on a shot!

A disappointment for the West Ham supporters, who booed their team off in the now time-honoured tradition as another two points went west. Against the bottom side.
