One selection choice boosts January transfer fund by millions | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Switching several key players after the international break was a brave move designed to revitalise West Ham’s fortunes. Had the ball not crashed against the woodwork four times but nestled in the corner just once we’d all be proclaiming his changes as astute: Bringing back Alvarez and switching Morato to a centre back role weren’t rocket science but shored up a hitherto leaky defence.

However, bringing Fin Herrick into the side – ostensibly ‘resting’ (a.k.a.’dropping’) Hermansen – paid off . With Herrick now tried and tested, his elevation to credible first team keeper surely puts paid to any interest West Ham were showing in young Swansea ‘keeper Joe Duncan.

Herrick’s emergence means the budget doesn’t have to be diluted: Koppen can call off the search for a ‘keeper and find a goal scorer instead.

As Matt’s Claret and Hugh article last week predicted: “West Ham Could Save Themselves A Transfer Fee” by promoting Herrick rather than forking out millions to bring another promising young goal keeper to West Ham – when there’s one in situ already.

Exactly as it has seemed to pan out. Whilst Parker claims Hermansen has been strategically withdrawn for ‘some air’, there’s every chance that Herrick will get more match time whilst Areola remains unfit. And good luck to him. As he proved yesterday he’s got what it takes to keep a modest attack at bay.

A sterner test will come at Portsmouth but saving Koppen’s transfer budget for the acquisition of a striker who can actually hit the target looks to be the 100% absolute stone-cold priority in January, if it isn’t already too late.

And Herrick’s emergence has probably boosted the funds available to Koppen significantly.
