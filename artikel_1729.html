‘Wake up!’: Gary Neville slaps himself in the face TWICE during Manchester United rant

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Manchester United’s heavy defeat by Liverpool led to Gary Neville slapping himself in the face twice on live television as he took aim at their dreadful defending.

Mauricio Pochettino’s debut as Man Utd boss ended in a 4-0 loss at Anfield, with the Red Devils collapsing after Leny Yoro’s early red card.

Again criticising United’s defence, Neville called out the players’ body language, accusing them of being asleep.

He said on Sky Sports: “I never see any of them on their toes, alert, looking like they’ve got something fixed in here,” before pointing to his head.

“I never see any of them look like that. They look like that,” Neville added, providing a gormless look and pausing for several seconds, before stating “wake up” and slapping himself in the face twice.

With fellow pundit Jamie Carragher bursting into uncontrollable laughter, Neville finished his impassioned rant by adding: “You play for Man United!”

Roy Keane also fumed over United’s latest capitulation and called out Neville for initially suggesting that things could have been worse after Yoro’s early dismissal.

He said: “If I hear that one more time from the lads on commentary saying 4-0 is not too bad and the team were down to 10 men - has a team ever got a result before with 10 men? It can be done.

“I thought United were desperate, I thought they were so bad, and Neville who has played for United hundreds of times said he’s okay with four nil? Obviously it [the red card] doesn’t help.

“But are you telling me they couldn’t have done better? At least show a bit more fight, a bit more quality.

“I can’t believe what I’m hearing from people who played for the club, saying 4-0 is not too bad. Wow.”

Keane then criticised the United fans at Anfield, claiming that they were “applauding rubbish”.

“Fans were applauding the players. Applauding what? Applauding rubbish,” Keane continued.

“This team two years ago finished 15th. The last year or two we were blaming the board, blaming ownership, blaming managers. When do the players get responsibility for some of the decision-making?

“Liverpool should have scored eight. Probably eight or nine, unbelievable chances they had. And everyone’s coming off the pitch, saying ‘four is not too bad’.

“I feel like swearing. I’ll try not to, but how can anyone say four is not too bad?

“Just ’cause they went down to 10 men? That obviously made it a lot more difficult, but if you go down to 10 men, is that it? You just down tools and throw the towel in?”

United remain in the Premier League relegation zone after their loss to Liverpool, with just one win and eight points from their opening 11 games.
