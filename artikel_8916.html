Rare Artist-Designed Louis Vuitton Handbags Included in Upcoming Christie’s Upcoming Online Sale

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Starting November 25, or the day before Thanksgiving in the US and two days before Black Friday, Christie’s will offer a collection of some 100 rare Louis Vuitton handbags, featuring collaborations with artists like Takashi Murakami, Frank Gehry, and Yayoi Kusama. The “Scarcity & Infinity” sale will be offered online during the house’s luxury week from November 25 to December 10, with a selection of handbags on view at Christie’s Rockefeller Center headquarters from December 3–9.

“This collection celebrates an extraordinary intersection of art, fashion, and design, and the idea that a work of art can be something we carry with us,” Rachel Koffsky Parker, Christie’s international head of handbags and accessories, said in a statement. Highlights include several examples from Murakami’s many Artycapucines collaborations with Louis Vuitton, from a Swarovski crystal-encrusted grinning, wide-eyed panda purse (estimated at $20,000–$30,000) to a semicircular bag covered with rainbow mink fur ($10,000–$15,000) to a pink calfskin handbag covered with octopus tentacles ($10,000–$15,000). The Swarovski panda is the sale’s top lot.

“Scarcity & Infinity” also includes a limited-edition yellow leather pumpkin-shaped bag designed by Kusama in 2023; here, her trademark polka dots are replaced by black LV logos.

Both Kusama and Murakami have long histories of collaboration with Louis Vuitton, but that is not as much the case with another designer included in the sale. The late architect Frank Gehry, known for his sweeping, shimmering designs for museums like the Guggenheim Bilbao and the Fondation Louis Vuitton in Paris, notably worked with the fashion house in 2023 on a line of 11 handbags that were presented at Art Basel Miami Beach that year. Several of those bags, including a metallic bear ($10,000–$15,000) and a white crocodile Capucines bag emblazoned with a red fish ($6,000–$8,000), are also part of the sale.

Handbags from other artist collaborations that will be offered by Christie’s include ones by Pharrell Williams, Stephen Sprouse, Jeff Koons, Nigel Peake, Tom Sachs, and Yohei Yama.
