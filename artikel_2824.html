The Rams swap out another pair of receivers following latest injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Los Angeles Rams have placed another receiver on injured reserve this week, as Jordan Whittington will miss at least four games with a back injury. Whittington was in line to have serious reps moving forward with Puka Nacua joining Davante Adams on IR this week, but now that opportunity will go to someone else.

Perhaps Xavier Smith, who the Rams re-signed on Tuesday in a corresponding move. Smith joins Konata Mumpfield, Tutu Atwell, and C.J. Daniels in a receivers room that looks a lot different than it did a few weeks ago.

Despite rumors like that L.A. inquired about Stefon Diggs, the Rams didn't trade for any receivers at Tuesday's deadline. This is the group they are going with.

Whittington had just four targets this season, catching two passes for 12 yards. The 22 catches for 293 yards he had as a rookie in 2024 may end up being his best career year when it's all said and done.

Instead, the Rams look to Mumpfield, last year's seventh-round pick.

Mumpfield leads all active Rams receivers with 11 catches for 162 yards. Tyler Higbee leads the team at any position among non-injured players, with 28 catches for 287 yards. Kyren Williams has 36 catches for 258 yards.

Outside of a 48-yard catch against the Broncos in which Mumpfield was practically uncovered, L.A.'s new number one receiver hasn't been productive. Aside from that play, Mumpfield has 10 catches for 114 yards and zero touchdowns in the other eight games. He had two catches for 20 yards last week.

Atwell, who the team traded for at the start of the season, has been targeted nine times in the past two games, catching four passes for 68 yards. C.J. Daniels has yet to be targeted.

So, the Rams bring back Xavier Smith, who had one target and no catches prior to his release. Smith had 18 catches for 303 yards in 2025, however.

Other notable moves on Tuesday had Josh Downs going to the Kansas City Chiefs, Keenan Allen going to the San Francisco 49ers and Brian Thomas Jr. staying with the Jasonville Jaguars. As it stands, adding Smith is the biggest move the Rams made on the NFL's trade deadline day.

Smith's biggest moment with the Rams came in the 2025 NFC Championship game when he fumbled after halftime against the Seattle Seahawks. Seattle would go on to score a touchdown and ultimately win the game. Was that play the sole reason the Rams lost? No, but it didn't help.

Entering the 2026 season, the Rams brought Smith back as their kick returner and punt returner before waiving him a few weeks into the year. Smith then went to the 49ers, who have since traded for Allen, and decided to move on from Smith. As the Rams enter Week 10, Smith is back in L.A.

Is Smith the player that the Rams needed to make up for the losses of Puka Nacua and Davante Adams? Right or wrong it's what the Rams just did, and for now, that's all they did to address their wide receiver room as another trade deadline comes to its end.
