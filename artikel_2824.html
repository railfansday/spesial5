Darien Porter returns to practice ahead of Las Vegas Raiders-San Francisco 49ers Week 9 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Las Vegas Raiders cornerback Darien Porter was spotted at Thursday's practice ahead of the Week 9 matchup against the San Francisco 49ers, according to ESPN's Ryan McFadden.

Previously, Porter exited last Sunday's game against the New York Jets after suffering a groin injury on the first play from scrimmage, and he missed Wednesday's practice with the same injury. The Raiders' injury report listed him as limited for Thursday's session, a step toward being able to play this weekend.

Porter has played in seven out of eight games this season, missing Week 2 against the Los Angeles Chargers with a foot injury, and has started four contests. He's been rotating with rookie Hezekiah Masses. However, the team's other rookie corner, Jermod McCoy, originally replaced Porter when Porter went down in New York. Masses and McCoy ended up splitting reps, though, with the former recording a late pass breakup to help seal Las Vegas' win. Presumably, he'd be the starter if Porter can't go or isn't healthy enough to start.

Additionally, The Athletic's Sam Warren reported that defensive tackle Thomas Booker IV wasn't seen during Thursday's practice. Booker was limited the day before with a foot injury. He's primarily played a backup/rotational role this season. If Booker is sidelined for the game, his absence against the 49ers shouldn't change much for the Raiders' defense.

The Raiders' injury report had a few other updates as well. Defensive tackle Foley Fatukasi (shoulder/elbow) and tight end Ian Thomas (ankle) went from limited during the first practice of the week to full participants in the second. Also, wide receiver Dont'e Thornton Jr. (back) wasn't on Wednesday's report but popped up on Thursday and was limited.

Meanwhile, there's also some key injury news coming out of San Francisco. General manager John Lynch told Bay Area radio station KNBR that wide receivers Mike Evans (Achilles) and Deebo Samuel (hamstring) will not be available this week (h/t NBC Bay Area's Matt Maiocco). But the 49ers are expecting to have wideouts Demarcus Robinson and KhaDarel Hodge back this week.

The 49ers also had wide receiver Christian Kirk (knee) go from limited to did not participate, while running back Christian McCaffrey (hamstring) was upgraded to limited, and offensive tackle Trent Williams (rest) was a full participant on Thursday.
