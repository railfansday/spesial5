Manhattan wins Class B football title on 2-point stop

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

WORDEN — Manhattan quarterback Tyson Pavlik scored two touchdowns, one in double overtime, and the Tigers stopped Huntley Project's Blake Ramaeker on a two-point PAT to win their second straight State B football championship Friday, 21-19.

Both teams scored in the first quarter — the 230-pound Pavlik had a 4-yard run at the 4:14 mark and Huntley Project's Tucker Kaczmarek scored on a 16-yard run 3 minutes later — but were shut out for the rest of regulation.

The Tigers (11-1) had a long clock drive at the end, driving from their own 14 to Project's 3-yard line. But with under 10 seconds left Pavlik threw a pass that Brayden Zikmund caught for no gain and Manhattan couldn't get off another play before time ran out.

The Red Devils (12-1) had the ball first in overtime and jumped ahead when Ramaeker broke four tackles on a 9-yard touchdown scramble. Their PAT kick missed, leaving it 13-7.

Manhattan answered with a 10-yard option pass from Zikmund to Matthew Fenno — the Montana State commit catching a TD from the Montana recruit — but then had its PAT kick blocked.

In the second OT — teams start first-and-goal from the 10 — Pavlik scored on fourth down from the 1, then pitched to Easton DeHaan for the successful 2-point PAT and a 21-13 lead.

The Red Devils' Huntly Harp matched Pavlik, scoring on fourth down from the 1. On the PAT, the slippery Ramaeker scrambled left but was wrapped up by Taysen Meech and Zikmund at the 1-yard line.

It was the third football championship and the fourth straight title appearance for Manhattan, which lost to Florence in 2023 and Malta in 2024. Last year the Tigers beat Three Forks, their neighbor 12 miles down I-90, for their second state title. Their first came in 2020.

It was Project's eighth title game appearance and first since a loss to Fairfield in 2015. The Red Devils won State B titles in 1998 and 2008-09.
