Titans WR Calvin Ridley was involved in an incident at Nashville International Airport

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Titans had a bye in Week 9. One of their players made a headline anyway.

Via WKRN, receiver Calvin Ridley was involved in an incident over the weekend at Nashville International Airport.

“We are aware of the incident and are in communication with Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority,” the team said in the statement. “We will not be able to comment any further at this time.”

No details regarding the nature of the incident have been released.

The news comes at a time when Ridley has been the subject of trade rumors and speculation. He’s in the third year of a four-year, $93 million contract with the Titans.

A first-round pick of the Falcons in 2018, Ridley was suspended for all of the 2022 season for betting on games — including games involving his team.

He was traded to the Jaguars in November 2022. He spent 2023 in Jacksonville before signing with the Titans as a free agent in 2024.

In seven 2026 games, Ridley has six catches for 91 yards.
