Miami reportedly to host NBA All-Star Game in 2028 for first time since 1990

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The NBA All-Star Game is returning to Miami.

Per ESPN's Shams Charania, the NBA is finalizing Miami as the host city of the league's 2028 All-Star Weekend.

Despite being a prime destination for a February party, this will mark just the second time that Miami has hosted the NBA's All-Star weekend. The first and only other time was in 1990, the second season of the Heat's existence.

Magic Johnson won MVP in that game, while David Robinson, Reggie Miller, Dennis Rodman and Scottie Pippen all made their All-Star debuts.

Miami will host the NBA All-Star Game in 2028 for the second time.

Since Miami last hosted, 10 NBA cities have hosted multiple All-Star weekends: Los Angeles (4 times), New Orleans (3 times), Atlanta, Charlotte, Cleveland, Houston, New York, Orlando, Phoenix and Salt Lake City (2 times each). The Bay Area has also hosted twice since 1990 with games in Oakland and in San Francisco.

Phoenix will host again in February from the 19-21.

As currently iterated, the NBA All-Star weekend officially consists of the Rising Stars Game on Friday night, the skills contest, 3-point contest and dunk contest on All-Star Saturday and the All-Star Game itself on Sunday.
