Sounds unlikely Bills face Drake Maye vs. Patriots in early December

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Patriots fell to 5-5 in a blowout against the Lions in Germany hours before the Bills moved to 8-1 with a win over the Jets in New Jersey, and in New England's defeat, quarterback Drake Maye suffered a leg injury.

Said injury might be rather serious based on a report from NFL Network insider Ian Rapoport:

Appearing on Get Up on ESPN Monday morning, Adam Schefter reported Maye suffered a fractured ankle and the Patriots are trying to determine if he needs surgery.

The Bills play the Patriots in New England on December 6 at 4:25pm ET. The tweet doesn't make it sound like Maye is likely to be available for that contest. Schefter's comments make it all but official he won't play.

We'll continue to monitor this as we get closer to that Week 13 showdown.

Suddenly, it feels like there's a better chance the Bills see Patriots backup Tommy DeVito than Maye in that AFC East clash.
