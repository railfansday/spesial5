Florida football report card, grading Gators after Oklahoma game

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Florida football was a gracious host in Oklahoma's first-ever trip to The Swamp.

The Florida Gators (5-4, 3-4 SEC) struggled on offense and couldn't come up with enough third down stops on defense in a 23-7 loss to the Sooners before 90,442 at The Swamp.

Not even former Hall of Fame former UF head coach Urban Meyer being inducted into the stadium ring of honor could inspire the Gators, who put forth another flat effort. Florida has now dropped four of its last five under first-year coach Jon Sumrall.

Oklahoma (5-4. 3-3 SEC) improved to 2-1 all-time against UF in the first SEC meeting between the two programs and Oklahoma's first-ever game in Gainesville.

Florid fans started heading to the exits after an eight-yard TD pass from quarterback John Mateer to receiver Isaiah Setenga III put the Sooners up 20-7 with 4:56 remaining.

Here's how UF graded out against the Sooners:

The move to Tramell Jones Jr. as starting quarterback didn't provide the intended spark. Jones went 10 for 22 for 133 yards and two interceptions, including one in the red zone for a second straight week. Back-to-back holding penalties by right tackle Caden Jones killed a potential first quarter scoring drive for UF. Jadan Baugh scored UF's lone TD on a 15-yard run but failed to reach 100 yards for fifth straight game. Florida finished the game a combined 2-of-11 on third and fourth down conversions.

Florida's defense generated pressure on Oklahoma quarterback John Mateer throughout the game finishing with one sacks and six quarterback hurries. But the Gators didn't do a good enough job covering Oklahoma's tight ends, though, as Rocky Beers and Hayden Hansen combined for five catches for 75 yards and a TD. When Florida needed a stop on third down, it couldn't get one in the fourth quarter during a 20-play, 89-yard Oklahoma TD drive that chewed up 12:45. Overall, Florida allowed an Oklahoma offense under a new coordinator, Jason Witten, to go 10 of 17 on third down.

A 31-yard punt return by Bailey Stockton late in the second quarter helped set up a short field for UF's first TD drive. Alec Clark averaged 42.4 yards on five punts with three downed inside the 20-yard line.

Sumrall was unable to solve the Georgia hangover, as UF dropped to 9-15 after losses to UGA since 2021. Florida played hard but not smart, which is a reflection of coaching. Sumrall decided not to take the points down 13-7 with 6:22 left in the third quarter, going for it on 4th-and-2 instead of going for a chip shot field goal. The move backfired when Jones threw an interception in the end zone.
