Iowa football lands commitment from 2028 kicker Dima Petrov

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

IOWA CITY — Class of 2028 kicker Dima Petrov has committed to Iowa football, he announced Nov. 16.

Petrov, who attends Hanover High School in New Hampshire, is the 2nd-ranked kicker nationally in the 2028 class, according to Chris Sailer Kicking.

Iowa offered Petrov a full scholarship in June. He is listed at 6-foot-2 and 200 pounds, according to social media.

Here is his evaluation on Chris Sailer Kicking:

"Dima is a big time high school kicking prospect. He is a great looking athlete with a live leg. Dima does an outstanding job on field goal. He hits a pure, accurate ball off the ground and has 55+ yard range. His kickoffs are excellent, D1 ready. Dima drives the ball 70+ yards, with 4.0+ hang time. Also a very talented punter. Dima is a competitor who thrives under pressure. I look forward to seeing what the future holds. Dima has all the tools to be special at the next level. He is a fine young man who is always a great pleasure to work with."

By the time Petrov officially joins Iowa, the Hawkeyes are still projected to have kicker Caden Buhr on the roster. Buhr, who is in his first season as Iowa's starting kicker, is 14-of-19 on field goals so far in 2026. Petrov could be Iowa's next kicker after the eventual departure of Buhr.

Petrov is Iowa's fourth verbal commitment in the 2028 high school class, joining defensive back Dwayle Smith, offensive lineman James Barnett and quarterback Chase Grove.
