Open Thread: Jeremy Sochan’s first press conference after being drafted first overall in the G-League

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

What a ride Jeremy Sochan has had, and the new season is just getting underway. After nearly four seasons with the San Antonio Spurs, he was waived and signed with the Knicks, picking up a championship ring when New York beat the Spurs in the 2026 Finals.

After New York, it appeared Sochan was spending the 2026-2027 season in Portland, but was waived after four preseason games. The Polish-American power forward declared for the G-League draft and one week later was chosen first overall by the Texas Legends.

Here he is earlier this week in his first press conference as a member of the Legends.

"I think the way I grew up, my family, my parents; it's always been like that. I could look at it negatively (the Portland situation). But when I look back at that situation, I think the two months I was there, I grew a lot. I was healthy throughout the whole training camp, preseason and open gym. So I think that was a blessing. When we got into preseason games, the minutes I got I think I used them to the best of my ability. I had good games. So what would be the point of being negative and angry for getting waived? Of course, you can look at it as that's the NBA and I have a contract. But at the same time I have good momentum. So, why would I hold weight on something that I can't control? And instead, be in this moment and do what I can do and keep that momentum going."

According to Marc Spears, Sochan originally found out he was being waived by the Spurs through another player:

"I thought I was going to be there for the rest of the season at least. And a few days after the trade deadline, it's funny. I don't want to be messy or anything, but I found out from one of my teammates that they wanted to waive me, and it definitely hit me in a way. But again, I kind of had the mentality of, 'It is what it is.' I heard it was pretty possible from someone else, and then officially found out through my agent. So, that was definitely new to me."

Texas Legends season kicks off tonight in Mexico City against the Capitanes.
