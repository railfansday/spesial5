Patriots RB Rhamondre Stevenson will miss multiple weeks with a knee issue

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Patriots running back Rhamondre Stevenson will miss multiple weeks with a knee issue.

Ian Rapoport of NFL Media reports that Stevenson has a plantaris tendon issue in the back of his knee, which won’t require surgery but will require rest and rehab.

Stevenson was injured in Sunday’s 45-27 loss to the Packers. He had six carries for 32 yards and three catches for 46 yards and a touchdown.

The Patriots were already down their top two receivers, A.J. Brown and Romeo Doubs, top two guards Michael Onwenu and Alijah Vera-Tucker, and backup running back TreVeyon Henderson.

Corey Kiner and Lan Larison are the only healthy running backs on the 53-player roster but signed Terrell Jennings to the practice squad last week.
