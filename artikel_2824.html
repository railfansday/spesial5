Vikings explored all options before deciding to stay in Minnesota this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

As Sunday’s Mexico City game between the Vikings and 49ers approaches, one of the big issues is the decision of the 49ers to spend the week at altitude in Colorado Springs while the Vikings opted to stay in Minnesota.

On that point, multiple 49ers players have claimed that the Vikings tried to secure the Air Force Academy facility at Colorado Springs, but the 49ers had beaten them to the punch.

The Vikings, we’re told, explored all options — including going to Air Force Acadamey facility and going to Mexico City early. They ultimately decided to implement a plan premised on staying in Minnesota as long as possible.

Obviously, the Vikings could have figured out something that would have allowed them to spend the week somewhere that would have allowed for full acclimation. There are other practice facilities in Colorado. And, again, early arrival in Mexico was always a possibility.

The issue has likely become a focal point of the days preceding the game because of the dramatically different approaches used by the 49ers and Rams before their Week 1 game in Australia. San Francisco went early; the Rams arrived late. And the 49ers stunned the Rams, 27-7.

In that case, it was about time and distance traveled. Here, it’s the same time zone, and the flight is roughly four hours — not much farther for the Vikings than a trip to Seattle, San Francisco, or L.A.

Teams that play in Denver don’t spend the week in Colorado. And while the altitude results in more sideline shots of players with oxygen masks during games, no one ever says, “Well, that team lost to the Broncos because they weren’t acclimated to the altitude.”

The Vikings-49ers game won’t turn on that. If the 49ers win, it will be because they were the better team during those 60 minutes of clock time. And, if the Vikings win, it won’t be because they someone cracked the code on playing at altitude.
