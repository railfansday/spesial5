Pau Cubarsi explains how Barcelona ‘fixed things’ at half-time against Atletico

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Pau Cubarsi spoke about how Barcelona turned things around against Atletico Madrid on Sunday after playing his part in a 2-1 win at the Metropolitano Stadium.

Guiliano Simeone put the hosts ahead after just 10 minutes but Barcelona hit back through a Fermin Lopez double to seal an important win.

Cubarsi revealed afterwards how Barca made changes at the break that helped to swing the match in their favor.

"In the first half we came out a bit disconnected. They had a great first half, but at half-time we were able to fix the things we weren't doing well and we took the victory," he said.

"We needed to apply better high pressure, and in the second half we positioned ourselves a bit better. They weren't as comfortable, and we had a presence up front. If we're playing well and give our all in every match, we're going to have a great league season."

Hansi Flick sent on Frenkie de Jong at half-time at right-back in place of Brian Farinas, while Gavi, Eric Garcia, Andreas Christensen and Karim Adeyemi also featured in the second half of a big win.
