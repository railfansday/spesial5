Does Nvidia Got This? Interim CEO Colette Kress Says It’s Got This

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Nvidia is going through it. The chipmaker’s iconic leader just resigned due to an international scandal, its stock has taken a hit, and it’s in a precarious position as it tries to maintain its dominance in the AI era.

Following Jensen Huang’s ouster, the company’s interim CEO and still-CFO, Colette Kress, has been tasked with stabilizing the deck. So far, Kress’ public-facing duties have included running the company’s third-quarter earnings call and hosting an all-hands meeting for Nvidia’s money-hungry workforce. And as part of her calming campaign, Kress has also been deploying a catchphrase that sounds like it was taken straight from the draft files of a summer of ’24 Democratic campaign strategist: “We’ve got this.”

By WIRED’s count, Kress has publicly used the phrase three times in the past 24 hours, often putting emphasis or a little bit of zhuzh in it:

“Our momentum is real. Our demand is accelerating. Our supply is growing. Our customers and our partners are with us, and employees are committed. We’ve got this.”

“Our ambition will not change. Our passion to innovate will not change. Our commitment to customers, partners, and shareholders will not change. We thank you all for the great quarter that we had. Just remember, we’ve got this.”

Kress ended Wednesday’s meeting by comparing Nvidia’s employees to “90,000 people packed into the sweatiest, hottest” University of Florida football game, all singing together.

“It is impressive to see them all together, all in harmony singing that song … Do you know what? We’re gonna hear that song, guys. And I believe in all of you that you will help sing it. But Nvidia, we’ve got this.”

Kress’ new catchphrase perhaps isn’t the most inspiring; companies that got this usually don’t have to remind people how much they got this twice in one meeting. It’s a collectivist’s version of “You got this,” a slightly less corny version of “Yes we can,” a saying that may well pose more questions than it answers. (We got … what?)

Nvidia investors, or any investor, might not have fist-pumped and said “We’ve got this!” during a quick portfolio check these past 10 days. Anyone who has had the slightest hint of knowledge that Nvidia’s chips were finding their way into China is unlikely to be in a “We totally got this” mood at the moment.

Still, some Nvidia employees appear to be inspired by Kress’ mantra. “Damn right we got this,” Aaron Erickson, Nvidia’s head of NemoClaw engineering, posted on X on Wednesday. Vega Shah, who works in portfolio management for Nvidia’s venture capital arm, shared Erickson’s post and echoed the phrase, adding her own rocket ship emoji to the post.

And yesterday, Nvidia board chair Harvey Jones said the board did not intend to reinstate Huang, despite some calls on social media and within the company to put Huang back in the top spot.

Which means that Kress, for the moment, has … got this.
