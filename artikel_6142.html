Delta Air Lines and Hyatt have announced the much-anticipated details of their new loyalty partnership.

The new arrangement will allow certain SkyMiles and World of Hyatt members to “double dip” their earnings on flights and hotel stays.

That means you could earn both Delta miles and Hyatt points on a flight — or a hotel booking. The setup is reminiscent of what Hyatt previously shared with American Airlines.

However, there are two big rules to know about this Delta-Hyatt arrangement:

For Delta Medallion and World of Hyatt elite members, this partnership will open up more ways to earn points and miles.

Plus, the partnership includes new loyalty perks that members can unlock as they climb the status ladder.

For instance, Delta will add Hyatt free-night certificates to its menu of choice benefits available at the Platinum and Diamond Medallion levels.

Hyatt elites can opt for Delta benefits like e-certificates and Sky Club passes as they reach certain milestones.

Let's get to the details of how to link your account, and how the earnings will work.

To start earning SkyMiles on Hyatt stays and World of Hyatt points on Delta flights, you’ll need to link your two loyalty accounts.

You can do that through this page on Delta’s website or this one via Hyatt’s.

You’ll start earning right away on eligible flights and hotel stays.

There are bonus points on the table for members who act quickly.

All members can earn those bonus points, whether they have elite status or not.

Even sooner, though, members who link their World of Hyatt and Delta SkyMiles accounts can register to earn one additional elite night credit on their first qualifying stay completed between Nov. 17 and Dec. 31, 2026. Eligible stays are those booked as paid or award nights.

Delta flyers who earn Medallion elite status will be eligible to earn SkyMiles on eligible Hyatt bookings.

Medallions will earn 1 mile per $1 of eligible spend at participating Hyatt properties.

You’ll earn those Delta miles on top of the standard points (and elite night credits) that you’d earn as a World of Hyatt member.

Similarly, World of Hyatt elite members can earn points on Delta flights.

That’s on top of the Delta miles (and Medallion Qualifying Dollars) you’d earn for your booking.

While Delta’s coach fares are not eligible for World of Hyatt earnings, you can still earn Hyatt points on the carrier’s basic premium fares like Comfort Basic, Basic business, etc.

This new partnership between Delta SkyMiles and World of Hyatt certainly unlocks more earning opportunities for loyalty members across both programs.

The setup is clearly tilted heavily toward travelers who have status — and those who are willing to pay more for a premium ticket. You have to be “elite” to take full advantage of the tie-up, and can’t earn Hyatt points on a regular Delta main cabin booking.

However, travelers who are already committed Delta and Hyatt loyalists will come out ahead, gaining the ability to stack their earnings on both flights and hotel stays.

The SkyMiles-World of Hyatt partnership also includes a host of new benefits that members of both programs can select as they earn higher status.

Those options will go live on Feb. 1, when Delta starts its new Medallion qualification year.

Before opting for a free night award, you'll obviously want to weigh that perk against the other options you have, which range from Sky Club memberships to travel vouchers on Delta.

Hyatt is also layering in Delta benefits to its menu of Milestone Rewards, which members can select upon hitting certain elite thresholds.

Still, for those travelers to whom this applies, this is a great new way to unlock additional rewards over the course of your annual travels.
