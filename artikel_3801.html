How to buy the store in Probably Stolen

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Whether you are tired of dealing with the landlord and his oppressive price hikes or just want to unlock one of the many endings in Probably Stolen, saving enough to buy the store should be among your end-game goals.

But how much does the store cost? And most importantly, how can you unlock the ability to purchase it in the first place? Here’s how to buy the store in Probably Stolen.

You can buy the store in Probably Stolen by amassing any of the sums below before accepting the landlord’s offer at the end of the week. Don’t take the prices as a guarantee, though, as the store will progressively increase in value once you pass the 50-day mark, no matter your difficulty of choice.

If you don’t have the necessary Credits, a Loan Shark will keep his promise and visit your store a few days after you break past the 5.000 mark. He will come with a proposition, allowing you to purchase the store via a down payment and multiple weekly deposits. Upon making all payments, you will receive the documentation and close the deal.

Even if using the Loan Shark might be a good alternative for those looking to get the store early and unlock the Prodigious achievement in Probably Stolen, most of the player base considers it to be a bad deal in the long run, with investing in the right foundations and getting it done in a single payment being the safest option.

That rings even more true considering how, although inviting, all of the weekly payments part of the deal come with heavy interest and usually demand triple the amount of your rent at twice the risk.
