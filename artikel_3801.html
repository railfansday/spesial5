Disney Dreamlight Valley The Keepsake Sea Deluxe Edition – Price, exclusive bonus rewards, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Another big expansion for Disney Dreamlight Valley has arrived, which means it’s time to purchase another optional add-on if you’re hoping to experience The Keepsake Sea. When buying this Expansion Pass, you’ll be given the choice between the Standard and Deluxe Editions.

If you’re unsure whether this upgraded version of the DLC is right for you, carefully reviewing everything it includes is a helpful way to make this decision. Here’s a complete breakdown of The Keepsake Sea Deluxe Edition, including the price, exclusive bonus rewards, and other key details to know about it in Disney Dreamlight Valley.

The Deluxe Edition of The Keepsake Sea Expansion Pass is an optional, pricier version of the DLC that comes with some extra exclusive items in DDV. When choosing a version of The Keepsake Sea to add on to the base game, you can either buy the Standard Edition or the Deluxe Edition. You cannot pay the difference to upgrade your choice later on, so choosing wisely at the start is crucial.
