Fans don’t know what to think of the alleged kit

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

The most precious boy from Marvel Rivals is apparently getting a Lego set if leaks are to be believed, but fans are seriously divided on how Lego Jeff the Land Shark looks.

Popping up via reliable source Unibricks on Instagram, Lego Jeff the Land Shark looks to be a mid-size figure that's roughly on par with Lego Eevee. Much like his inspiration from Marvel Rivals and the comics, he's all toothy grins, big cartoon eyes, and stubby little legs… but the result has received mixed reactions from fans who aren't entirely sold on the idea. The comments on Unibrick's post trend toward the negative, while responses on a Reddit post sharing a video of the set from New Elementary are split down the middle – they basically veer from "aw, so cute!" to "oh man, that's hideous."

Want more Jeff in your life? There's plenty of merch based on his appearance in Marvel Rivals, from tees to action figures. You can find it all at Amazon, where a lot is getting a discount ahead of Black Friday.

I understand this mixed reception. While the alleged model does capture that signature smile and happy-go-lucky vibe, it doesn't quite replicate Jeff's squishy, rotund look. The tail on this leaked set feels a little sparse too, as if it's missing pieces. (I assume this is so the fin can 'wag' thanks to a ball joint, but it looks odd at a glance.)

Still, I think the team's done the best they can with the scale they're working at – and I'm not sure I'd call it 'bad' by any means. Soft and rounded characters are really hard to recreate in Lego brick form at this size due to the toy's angular nature. While a few of the best Lego sets manage it through some eldritch sorcery (Munchlax or Wallace & Gromit come to mind), I can only imagine how tricky it is with a shark that is basically a spongy football with legs.

There's no word on when this kit would launch, but if it's January, that month is looking very busy - a host of Marvel kits are also hitting the Lego store then.

Either way, this leaked kit is just one of many Jeff-themed pieces of merch coming out; although the recent Hasbro Marvel Rivals action figures are cool, I only have eyes for the Jeff the Land Shark action figure.
