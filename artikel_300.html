However, I do have have my complaints. Mainly, the price of iPads. Luckily, Black Friday has swept in to ease the pain of of this year's price increases, in part thanks to RAMageddon.

And the good news is, every iPad model is on sale including the recently released iPad Mini with A20 Pro chip, which makes it actually worth buying.

I have a new favorite iPad deal headed into Black Friday. The standard iPad with A16 chip just dropped in price to $379, bringing it $20 below the October Prime Day price I reported on. Keep in mind this price is available on the silver and blue iPads only.

This iPad is great for anyone. While we prefer the iPad Air's computing power, the basic iPad is far more affordable. And don't get us wrong, the basic iPad has plenty of power for streaming, playing games, or reading. And with its lower price point, it's also a great option for kids.

My favorite iPad, the 11-inch iPad Air with M4, is now down to $649, saving you $100 off of its $749 price tag. The iPad Air is the best iPad — and tablet — that you can buy. It's incredibly powerful for a tablet, with more computing power than many laptops.

$649 isn't technically the lowest price ever on the device. But given that iPad prices have increased so dramatically over the past year, these are still savings worth shopping.

Apple just announced the 8th Gen iPad mini with big upgrades, but if you're interested in an older (and less expensive) model, the iPad mini with the A17 Pro chip is already on sale. The 7th Gen iPad mini is a great option if you're looking for a versatile tablet that's great for streaming shows and catching up on emails. It's also the best iPad if you're looking for a model that'll double as an e-reader, thanks to its compact size.

But if you want the most up to date tech, luckily the new iPad Mini finally went on sale and is now $100 off.
