Santa may be missing with the Macys Thanksgiving parade postponed, but Lego Santa is on sale at Amazon

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

SAVE $18: As of Nov. 26, the Lego Up-Scaled Santa Minifigure is on offer for $41.99 as part of Amazon's Black Friday sale. That's a 30% discount on the regular list price of $59.99.

It's the time of year when we'd usually celebrate Thanksgiving Day with the Macy's Parade. Unfortunately, the parade has been postponed until Saturday, Nov. 28, due to bad weather. Not only does it mean a serious disruption to the usual festivities — you know, turkey, football, and the parade on TV — but it means we'll have to wait to see Santa, whose arrival always marks the beginning of Christmas.

But thanks to Black Friday, you can see Santa a little earlier than Saturday. A Lego Santa. As of Nov. 26, the Lego Up-Scaled Santa Minifigure is available for $41.99, an $18 discount off the regular list price of $59.99.

This 10-inch Santa Claus figure comprises 761 pieces and is based on the classic Lego minifigure design. Not just great fun to put together, it also works as a Christmas ornament that you can build, display, and break apart every year. You could call it the gift that keeps on giving.

Get the Lego Up-Scaled Santa Minifigure for $41.99 and kick off the Christmas season before the Macy's Thanksgiving Parade even gets started.
