Ariana Grande and Ricky Alvarez Share a Glimpse of Their <i><em>Focker-in-Law</em></i> Premiere Date Night

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Last night, Ariana Grande and Ricky Alvarez made their first red-carpet appearance since getting back together this past summer at the New York City premiere of Grande’s film Focker-in-Law. While photos of them posing together quickly spread on social media, the couple also gave fans a personal look at their date night.

Grande reposted a selfie Alvarez shared of them inside the theater. “Seeing a movie,” he wrote with a white heart emoji, adding in handwriting below, “Fockers.”

Grande has been similarly supportive of Alvarez’s photography, writing on his October 20 Instagram post, “I love your beautiful work so much.”

For the uninitiated, Alvarez was a backup dancer on the singer’s 2015 Honeymoon Tour, and the pair originally dated from 2015 to 2016. On July 14, People reported that they had rekindled their romance 10 years later, with a source sharing that they were “taking things very slowly.”

“Ariana has always considered Ricky a friend,” the insider explained. “She’s not held ill will against him [since their split]. Ricky is dependable, trustworthy and very supportive. Ariana feels she can be herself around Ricky.”

The source added that they “have a similar, smart, dry sense of humor” but are being prudent with their new relationship. “They’re not jumping into anything serious right away, but Ari is happy to have him back in her life.”

In August, Grande went Instagram official with Alvarez, tucking two photos of him into an Instagram carousel.

Last week, the two gave the most revealing look at their relationship dynamic yet when they participated in a kissing challenge for her R.E.M. Beauty brand.
