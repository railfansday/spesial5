Grafana Labs: Engineering Manager - Grafana Infrastructure (Remote, UK)

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Grafana Labs is the company behind Grafana Cloud, the fully managed observability platform trusted by more than 10,000 organizations to ensure reliability, resolve incidents faster, and optimize telemetry at scale. Built on open source and open standards and designed for interoperability across any stack, Grafana Cloud brings AI to observability and observability to AI, giving teams (and their agents) unified visibility so they can see, understand, and act on all their disparate data, wherever it lives, and move at the speed of their ambitions. Customers, including Anthropic, Bloomberg, NVIDIA, Microsoft, and Salesforce, rely on Grafana Labs. We are a 100% remote company with team members across 40+ countries, backed by leading investors including Lightspeed Venture Partners, Sequoia Capital, GIC, Coatue, J.P. Morgan, CapitalG, and Lead Edge Capital. Learn more at grafana.com and follow us on LinkedIn and X.

We’re scaling fast and staying true to what makes us different: an open-source legacy, a global collaborative culture, and a passion for meaningful work. Our team thrives in an innovation-driven environment where transparency, autonomy, and trust fuel everything we do.

You may not meet every requirement, and that’s okay. If this role excites you, we’d love you to raise your hand for what could be a truly career-defining opportunity.

This is a remote position and we're considering candidates in the UK, Spain or Ireland.

Grafana Cloud moves millions of metrics, log lines, and traces per second from our customers' environments into a highly available, low-latency stack that processes and stores this data, and serves it to dashboards and alerting tools. We aim to grow this to hundreds of millions per second, and it's critical that as we grow, we improve our performance, increase our reliability, and do it efficiently and securely.

The Foundations department owns the Internal Engineering Platform (IEP): the Kubernetes clusters, cloud service provider resources (CSP), hardware, identity, delivery, and observability systems that Grafana Labs's application engineers use to build, deploy, and run their workloads.

We're hiring an engineering manager for the Foundations Infrastructure group, which owns the cloud infrastructure Grafana Cloud runs on. The group provisions and operates Kubernetes clusters and CSP resources across GCP, AWS, and Azure; enforces identity, access, and security controls across those environments; and runs the observability and cost-visibility systems that keep the infrastructure measurable and accountable.

You'll lead one of the squads of platform engineers that comprise the group.

We're platform engineers, and our application teams are our users. Our job is to give them leverage: self-service building blocks that take care of the hard parts of infrastructure, so they can focus on what they're building. We treat the platform as a product and value the experience of the people who use it. When a change is well communicated and understood, teams welcome it, and it lands as an improvement that makes the right way the easy way. You'll lead a squad that makes that happen.

Foundations is a global team that works asynchronously. The people who own a component or problem make the decisions about it, once stakeholders have had a chance to weigh in. Application teams build and run their own services; we give them a self-service platform to do it on.

Every engineer is on-call for the components their squad provides. We plan on-call, tactical work, and time off into every quarter, so roadmap work is roughly half of an engineer's time rather than all of it.

We invest heavily in developer productivity. You can use modern AI coding assistants as part of your daily workflow (your choice of tools, within security guidelines), backed by a company-funded usage budget so you can iterate quickly without unnecessary friction.

We encourage pragmatic AI-assisted development: faster prototyping, test generation, refactors, documentation, and incident follow-ups, always paired with strong code review and quality standards.
