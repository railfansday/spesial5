As Corporate Counsel, Commercial, you will be the legal partner behind Carrot’s customer and vendor contracts. Day to day, you will negotiate, draft, and review a high volume of commercial agreements, including master services agreements, service level agreements, professional services agreements, and NDAs. You will partner closely with Sales and other business teams to resolve redlines and customer questions quickly, manage most matters from start to finish, and escalate high-risk issues when the moment calls for it.

This role is built for an attorney who pairs sharp legal judgment with practical business thinking. You will help move deals toward close while managing risk thoughtfully, supporting employers, health plans, and health systems around the world. You will join a collaborative Legal team and have support as you grow into greater independence and take on work with limited oversight.
