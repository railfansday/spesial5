Only 11% of the 42.6 million HSAs held investments at midyear 2026, though invested money made up 52% of all HSA assets, according to Devenir’s 2026 Midyear HSA Research Report. An HSA’s triple tax advantage means no federal tax on money going in, on its growth, or on withdrawals for medical care, and it does the most for money you leave invested for years. So every provider here charges no maintenance fee and has no minimum to start investing.

Payroll deposits into a work HSA generally skip Social Security and Medicare taxes, so keep that account for them and move money from it to an HSA you choose. The IRS sets no limit on transfers between HSAs. But some providers close the account when you transfer out, and many send only cash, which means selling any investments first. Ask your work HSA whether you can move part of the balance and keep the account open, and what it charges.
