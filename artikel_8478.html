Open an Alliant Kids Savings account for a child 12 or younger if you’ll keep at least $100 in it and bank by app. It pays a 3.01% annual percentage yield (APY) only in months the balance averages $100 or more. Alliant has no branches, so money goes in by transfer, mobile deposit, or ATM.

The 3.01% APY is the same rate Alliant pays on the High-Rate Savings account every member gets, while the FDIC puts the national savings average at 0.38%. The child owns the account jointly with a parent, grandparent, or guardian, who has to be an Alliant Credit Union member.

Families who keep $100 or more in it and add money at least once a year get Alliant’s full rate and never see its $10 inactivity fee. A child who wants to deposit coins in person is better off at a bank or credit union with a branch nearby.
