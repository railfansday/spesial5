Get the Capital One Spark Cash over the Spark Cash Plus unless your business can charge $30,000 in the Plus’s first 3 months for its $2,000 bonus, or $150,000 a year to get its fee refunded. Both pay 2% cash back, and this card costs $95 a year after a free first year, against $150 for the Plus.

The Capital One Spark Cash is a standard credit card with a credit line, while the Spark Cash Plus is a charge card designed to be paid in full. It suits a business that borrows on that line only now and then, since carrying $100 of purchases for a month at the 24.74% variable APR costs about $2 in interest, the same as their cash back.

Unless your business will earn the Plus’s bonus or its fee refund, this card pays the same 2% for $150 less in year one and $55 less each year after. Owners who pay in full each month and want business spending off their personal credit reports should get the Plus instead.
