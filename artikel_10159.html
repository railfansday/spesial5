I finalized porting the compression lib zxc (libzxc.org) to native go with the help of Claude opus 5.5. We had to rewrite a big part of it in AMD64 and ARM64 assembly to reach the C lib speed.

ZXC is a compression format allowing ultra fast decompression (faster than zstd) which might be of interest for game development to provide compact ressources.

The Go package is available here: https://github.com/chmike/zxc-go.
