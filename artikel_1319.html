Every Premier League Goalkeeper Ranked Based on 2026-27 Statistics

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The 2026-27 Premier League season is now in full swing, and opinions are already being formed on certain players. Manchester United goalkeeper Senne Lammens has been a big talking point in the opening few months.

The Belgian appears to be suffering from a crisis of confidence after his blunder saw his country eliminated from the 2026 World Cup. Lammens was named the Premier League's signing of the season last term but has been at fault for several goals United, currently 18th in the table, have conceded this term.

But where does he rank among every other Premier League club's goalkeeper this season?

To answer that question, let's take a closer look at each team's first-choice goalkeeper based on the goals prevented statistic. For clarity:

Every Premier League Goalkeeper Ranked by Stats (20-11)

Hull City, the favourites to finish rock-bottom in many people's eyes before a ball was kicked, have exceeded expectations during the opening months of the season. The opening-day win over Man United was a glimpse into how vital Konstantino Tzolakis could be for the Tigers. His imposing frame and calmness in goal are key qualities behind his 0.5 goals-prevented stat.

Liverpool could've had the perfect replacement for Alisson Becker had Caoimhin Kelleher been willing to stick around at Anfield. The Irishman has been an unbelievable replacement for David Raya at Brentford. Kelleher continues to impress for the Bees, preventing 0.6 goals his side should've conceded, according to the data.

Jordan Pickford, England's No.1, has consistently been a top performer for Everton over the years. The 32-year-old hasn't been able to do too much about the 10 goals David Moyes' side have conceded so far. He's also prevented 1.0 goals this term.

Chelsea's glaring issue heading into the new season under Xabi Alonso was the goalkeeper situation. Robert Sanchez had a couple of howlers in the first game against Fulham, prompting the Spanish coach to sign a new 'keeper. Emiliano Martinez will be disappointed with some of the goals he's conceded so far, but the Argentine is still among the best in the league.

Alisson turned 34 in October but remains one of the best goalkeepers in the business. Highly dependable, the Brazilian has been worth his weight in gold for the Reds following his move from AS Roma in 2018. He's conceded 10 Premier League goals but prevented 1.8 during Liverpool's opening 10 games of the 2026-27 season.

Many will be surprised that David Raya - arguably the Premier League's best goalkeeper right now - is only fifth on this list. The Spaniard has prevented 2.3 Premier League goals so far this season, but four goalkeepers have prevented more.

Zion Suzuki was handed the daunting task of replacing Emiliano Martinez at Aston Villa after an impressive World Cup for Japan. The 24-year-old shot-stopper has enjoyed an impressive start to life at Villa Park, preventing 3.4 goals.

Brighton are currently flying high in second place, thanks to a supreme team effort. No team has scored more Premier League goals (26) than the Seagulls, while Bart Verbruggen is one of three goalkeepers who has prevented an impressive 4.4 goals.

James Trafford left Manchester City looking for first-team football in the summer, and has proven to be worth the hefty £40 million price tag for Leeds United so far. The Lilywhites have been rock-solid at the back, only conceding eight goals so far.

Newcastle United looked like a team that could struggle with several key players moving on and an overhaul of the first-team. However, Lukas Hornicek has proven to be one of the best signings of the summer so far. Along with Verbruggen and Trafford, the Czech Republic international has prevented 4.4 goals.
