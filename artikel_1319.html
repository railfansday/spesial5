Aston Villa are reportedly preparing a move for River Plate goalkeeper Santiago Beltran as they look to strengthen their options between the posts following Emiliano Martinez’s departure.

The Premier League side have identified the highly-rated Argentine as a potential long-term addition, although they face competition from several European clubs ahead of the January transfer window.

According to reports from Soy Del Millo, Aston Villa are among the clubs monitoring the 22-year-old, with Fiorentina and Atletico Madrid also keen on securing his services.

Journalist Sebastián Srur has confirmed that Unai Emery’s side are keeping a close eye on the River Plate goalkeeper. Their interest comes after Martinez completed his move to Chelsea, leaving Villa to find a suitable replacement for the Argentine international.

Beltran has established himself as a key figure at River Plate, and his impressive development has attracted considerable attention across Europe. His performances have convinced several clubs that he could be a valuable long-term investment.

River Plate reportedly value their young goalkeeper at around €15 million. However, the Argentine giants are determined to protect their asset and are working to extend his contract while raising his release clause to €100 million.

River Plate president Stefano Di Carlo understands that holding on to the youngster could be challenging, particularly as established European clubs show serious interest.

The Argentine outfit would prefer to secure a new agreement before considering any potential departure, but the growing competition could complicate their plans.

Meanwhile, River Plate have already started considering potential replacements should Beltrán eventually leave. Flamengo’s Agustín Rossi and Al-Hilal goalkeeper Yassine Bounou are reportedly among the names the club’s hierarchy has approved.

It remains to be seen whether Aston Villa will formalise their interest with an offer in January. However, Emery’s side could have a promising opportunity to secure a talented young goalkeeper for a relatively affordable fee.
