Pochettino's Fernandes Test Laid Bare by 'Isolated' 5/10 display

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Mauricio Pochettino has his work cut out to solve one of Manchester United's biggest issues - Bruno Fernandes. There is no denying the importance of Fernandes to United, with the Portuguese star having carried the Red Devils for much of his time at the club, and he certainly did a lot of the heavy lifting last season.

But it's clear that Fernandes has not been himself this season, and Pochettino is going to have to find a way to get him back on board. Fernandes has managed four goals in eight Premier League games, also providing two assists, so in terms of actual performance, he is not United's biggest problem by any means, but those returns are just a baseline for the veteran playmaker.

"He was the best player in the Premier League last season; they need him, but they've got to set some standards." - Owen Hargreaves

Pochettino needs to get the very best out of his star man, and that means ensuring Fernandes is fully engaged and fully believing in the system he is playing in. That wasn't the case under Michael Carrick, with Fernandes making his frustration known on multiple occasions, be it in interviews or with subtle hints on social media.

His most notable comments came after the Como game, when he said; “It's a decision from the staff to not press man-to-man and sometimes leave a little bit of space in behind and try to get the ball in the first moment, to wait for them, to give them the ball. Sometimes when you give too much of the ball to these teams they get confidence. They have so many bodies behind the ball that make you run a lot, make people get frustrated.”

To add fuel to the Fernandes fire, the midfielder struggled for Portugal in their surprise 4-1 defeat to Denmark on Saturday evening. Portugal could have wrapped up their Nations League group with a draw but were battered by the Danes, and the scoreline could have looked worse had Bernardo Silva not scored four minutes into added time.

Portugal are now left needing to avoid defeat in their last game or for Denmark to fail to beat Wales to win their group, but they will progress to the final round either way.

But it was another tough night for Fernandes, who produced a 5/10 performance, according to GOAL, who reasoned in their player ratings: "The captain tried to pull the strings and created several chances, but he was often isolated as Denmark’s double-pivot of Hjulmand and Hojbjerg squeezed the space. He was substituted in the 79th minute as Portugal looked for fresh legs."

That doesn't bode well for Fernandes amid his current run, and new United boss Pochettino is going to have to get a handle of the situation. This United side doesn't have enough talent to compete for a top four finish, or indeed for trophies, without Fernandes at his best. Pochettino will understand that, and he will know the importance of getting the United skipper on side, feeling confident and back in form.

Mauricio Pochettino will take charge of Manchester United from November 18.
