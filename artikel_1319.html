Carrick's Key Changes Needed for Man Utd vs Aston Villa

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Manchester United manager Michael Carrick might be considering some changes to his side as he looks to turn things around after a disappointing start to the season.

The Red Devils did secure an important 3-2 victory over Roma in the Champions League during the week, but that's only papered over some of the cracks.

United were still disappointing for large portions of that game and were even 2-0 down after just 18 minutes.

Their Premier League form has been the major disappointment, though, and there's no doubt they drastically need to improve if Carrick wants to save his job, with United's preferred candidate to replace him already named.

Heading towards the game against Chelsea, United have dropped into the relegation zone, having secured just one win all season.

Despite the win over Roma during the week, Carrick is still under pressure at Old Trafford, so it'll be interesting to see whether he makes any major changes to his side.

Many have been calling for Carrick to give a start to young winger Shea Lacey after he came off the bench against Roma to produce an assist.

Starting a young talent like that against Aston Villa does feel unlikely, even though his fearlessness could be what United need.

Lacey might not be the player to make all the difference for United, though.

One player that Carrick needs to bring into the starting eleven is right-back Noussair Mazraoui, who has been barely seen this season, particularly in the Premier League.

Mazraoui's last appearance for United came in the Champions League against Como, but in the Premier League, he's played just 143 minutes.

Mazraoui might not be the difference maker for United, but it could help solve their defensive issues.

The Moroccan international is perhaps being underrated by Carrick, while it would allow Leny Yoro to come back into the centre of defence.

Yoro has been playing in his unfavored position of right-back recently, and although he's performing well there, United need to strengthen the central defence.

Of course, Carrick sees all of these players every single day, but everyone else sees what they produce on the pitch.

It's simply not been good enough for United, and Carrick needs to do something to try to save his job.

The change in defence will allow Carrick to drop centre-back Ayden Heaven from the starting eleven. The young defender conceded a penalty against Roma, and he's generally struggled for the Red Devils.

Heaven is clearly not ready to be playing week in, week out for United, and bringing Mazraoui in will allow the centre of United's defence to look stronger.

There's no doubt Heaven can be a key figure for United in terms of his long-term future, but for now, he shouldn't be a starter.

United will be without Lisandro Martinez for the game against Chelsea due to suspension, but the Mazraoui change could still allow Carrick to drop Heaven.
