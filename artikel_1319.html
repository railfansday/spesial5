Report: Real Madrid have already made decision on €80m Nico Paz buy-back option

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Reports in Spain claim that Real Madrid have already decided that they will activate their buy-back option for Como star Nico Paz, whose stocks continue to rise despite already being valued at €120m during the summer.

Nico Paz has gone from strength to strength at Como: He provided 12 goals and seven assists from 35 league outings last term and has already come up with six goals and eight assists from 14 matches at club level in 2026-27, not including the five goals in five games he has for Argentina this term.

Back in Argentina, he is being tipped to inherit Lionel Messi’s iconic no.10 shirt with the national team, and Juan Sebastian Veron claims that the Como star ‘does things I’ve only seen Messi and Diego Maradona do’.

For the last two years, Nico Paz has found himself in the middle of a tug-of-war between his current and former employers, Como and Real Madrid.

Nico Paz joined Como at a cost of just €6m during the summer of 2024, but this deal included a 50% sell-on clause for Madrid, as well as a series of low-cost buy-back options beginning at just €9m.

Como and Real Madrid were able to renegotiate the terms of the Nico Paz deal during the most recent summer transfer window: The Serie A side agreed to pay an additional €60m to cancel Madrid’s 50% sell-on clause.

This does not mean that Como have full control over Nico Paz’s next steps, however, as the new deal also includes another buy-back option for Real Madrid, which is set at €80m and is only valid in the summer of 2027.

According to reports from Spanish journalist Melchor Ruiz on Deportes COPE, Madrid have already decided that they will exercise their €80m buy-back option for Nico Paz this summer.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
