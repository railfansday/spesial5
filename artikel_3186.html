Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Gucci exit brings $250 million upfront as cash flow climbs.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Coty Inc. (COTY +12.68%) reported first-quarter results that exceeded management's internal expectations for sales and adjusted EBITDA, despite a modest reported revenue decline of 1%. Management characterized the current period as a transition year focused on the Coty.Curated strategic framework, which prioritizes resources toward 10 core brands that represent the majority of segment revenues. The company reported a narrowing of like-for-like sales declines in its Consumer Beauty division and growth in its primary Prestige brands, particularly in the U.S. and China. Strategic focus remains on organizational simplification, debt reduction, and identifying fixed-cost savings to fund disciplined reinvestment in brand advocacy and influencer marketing.
