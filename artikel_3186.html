What it's about: No-nonsense Hannah Brookes (Leighton Meester) is hired as the bodyguard for action-movie star Jack Stapleton (Jared Padalecki) as the A-lister tries to avoid a relentless stalker over the holiday season. To keep Stapleton's family from worrying about his safety, the two pose as a couple, and predictably, the fake romance becomes a real love affair.

Why you should watch it: Netflix's new Christmas original is based on the novel "The Bodyguard" by Katherine Center and has a distinctive Hallmark vibe. Whether or not that's a selling point is a matter of personal preference, but the holiday flick looks like a charming, cozy watch for the winter season.

Watch "Guarding Stars" on Netflix starting November 18

What it's about: Netflix's latest glossy, overedited, competitive reality series is called "Win the Mall." Hosted by Haley Baylee, it sees 10 so-called "expert shoppers" battle for a huge cash prize in a shopping mall. Expect shopping-themed challenges and an urge for some retail therapy after watching. Special guests include Jonathan Van Ness, Salish Matter, and Lilly Singh

Why you should watch it: Netflix has been bolstering its competitive game show roster in recent years, and "Win the Mall" is the latest entry in the disposable stable. It doesn't look great, but it'll probably make for suitable background noise if you need a Netflix show to watch while doing chores.

What it's about: Based on the true story of financial fraud, "The Altruists" follows lovebirds Caroline Ellison (Julia Garner) and Sam Bankman-Fried (Anthony Boyle) as they create crypto exchange platform FTX, which collapsed in 2022 and was revealed to be a front for wire fraud, money laundering, and a litany of other white-collar crimes.

Why you should watch it: Netflix will hope "The Altruists" is met with a warmer reception than recent box office bomb "The Social Reckoning," which proved public interest in dramatic retellings of online wrongdoing isn't exactly high. Fortunately, this Netflix limited series appears to be hooked on the relationship between Ellison and Bankman-Fried, which might help it stand out. At least it'll be more evidence of what we all know: crypto bros suck.

What it's about: A new twist on the "Cinderella" story, "Steps" reframes the fairy tale around Lilith, one of Cinder's stepsisters. When she accidentally transforms her other sister into a frog and throws the fantasy Kingdom into chaos, Lilith has to team up with Cinderella and a troll to save the day.

Why you should watch it: There's nothing novel about an animated movie putting a new spin on a classic story, but "Steps" looks like good family fun this Thanksgiving, and a solid streaming alternative to taking the whole family to see Disney's "Hexed" in theaters. It features a strong voice cast with Ali Wong, Amanda Seyfried, Daniel Radcliffe, Peter Dinklage and Bette Midler.
