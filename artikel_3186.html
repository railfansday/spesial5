Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Credit stress and buybacks defined Golub's fourth quarter, with leverage falling.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 18, 2026 at 4:08PM EST

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Golub Capital BDC (GBDC +0.41%) reported a fourth fiscal quarter impacted by elevated credit stress and significant unrealized losses concentrated in specific problem credits. Management stated that nearly all of the unrealized losses related to three previously identified investments, while 85% of the total portfolio remained in the highest internal performance categories. The company prioritized deleveraging and share repurchases over new originations, resulting in a decrease in net debt-to-equity to 1.17x. Chief Executive Officer David Golub noted that while industry-wide credit stress is running at approximately twice normal levels, the company expects future net asset value upside from the potential reversal of prior-period fair value adjustments on performing loans.
