Jesse Eisenberg Says “There’s No Sexuality To My Work” & Why Life Is “Different” When Fans “Want To Sleep With A Person Onscreen”

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Jesse Eisenberg is reflecting on his work in Hollywood and why he has a very different experience with fans in public than some other actors.

In a new interview with The New Yorker, the actor, director, writer, and producer of The Debut said fans of his work are “mostly fine” when they see him in public, as his roles are not sexualized.

Eisenberg said in the interview that he goes to the New York Public Library, where he writes scripts. “It’s because there’s no sexuality to my work,” he added. “I think once the public is asked to want to sleep with a person onscreen, that person’s life is fundamentally different.” Watch on Deadline

Eisenberg, who earned an Oscar nomination for his role in Aaron Sorkin’s The Social Network as Facebook founder Mark Zuckerberg, recalled an interaction with a fan.

“I’d be out at a bar, and a drunk guy would say, ‘Hey, I love your website,’” he said. “And as I’m pretending to laugh, I’d just wish all of the oxygen would leave my lungs, and that I would collapse on the floor in front of this guy and show him what he’s doing to me.”

The actor didn’t return for the sequel, The Social Reckoning, and has been outspoken about his decision. Succession alum Jeremy Strong took on the role of Zuckerberg after Eisenberg declined.

Eisenberg’s The Debut opens in theaters on December 11, and the cast also includes Julianne Moore, Paul Giamatti, Halley Bailey, Colton Ryan, and Bernadette Peters, among others.
