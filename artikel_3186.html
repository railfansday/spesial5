Remove your personal info from the web (and stop it coming back)

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

It seems incredible that anyone can legally create a business which involves scraping your personal data from the web and then selling it, yet here we are. There are literally hundreds of data brokers doing just this.

Personal data bought and sold includes everything from your web-browsing habits and interests to your location history. The least-worst case is that your personal data is purchased by companies wanting to target you with ads. In the worst case, hackers gain access to that data, where it is made available to scammers and identity thieves.

You can fight back using what’s known as “takedown requests.” By law, data brokers have to remove your data if you tell them to. However, this is much easier said than done.

First, you would have to contact literally hundreds of different companies. Second, many of those companies deliberately make takedown links hard to find, then force you to jump through all sorts of hoops in order to issue the requests. Some of them even break the law by not providing the links in the first place.

But even if you succeed, the results won’t last for long. Data brokers are constantly buying new data and adding it to your profile. It’s a constant game of whack-a-mole.
