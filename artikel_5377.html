Scribd releases first transparency report on trust and safety

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Report provides new visibility into content enforcement across platform spanning more than 400 million documents

SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Scribd published its first transparency report, providing new data on content moderation, copyright enforcement, and user safety. Covering the first half of 2026, the report details more than 47M content moderation actions, including 42M uploads blocked before publication and 5M published items removed afterward.

The first in its nearly 20-year history, the report marks a new step in sharing how Scribd, Inc. enforces its policies across knowledge platform Scribd and sister presentation platform Slideshare. Between those two, the scale now spans more than 400M pieces of content, 250M monthly users, and 50 languages. 84M new pieces of content uploaded by users were published in the first half of 2026 alone.

Key findings from Scribd's first transparency reportContent moderation: During the first half of 2026, automated tools blocked 42M pieces of content during upload time, preventing them from appearing on the platform. Content appeals: Scribd received and reviewed 3,539 appeals of content removal decisions, upholding 3,296 (~93%) of original decisions.

Community reporting: Community acts as another arm helping keep Scribd safe. More than 48,000 users submitted 108,638 reports covering 52,359 pieces of content.

Account removals: Scribd terminated 15,355 accounts as part of its repeat infringer policy or for other copyright infringement reasons.

Our Community Rules define what people can upload and publish. Scribd combines human and automated methods to review content against those rules.

What's next for trust and safety at ScribdThis first transparency report establishes a baseline for how to measure and share this work, and compare future reports against. The next report is expected in 2027.

Scribd will continue to invest in trust and safety, including expanding its team, refining content policies, implementing new technology, and improving content classification.

Note to editors: This report covers Scribd.com, with some information about presentation-sharing platform, Slideshare. It does not cover the other Scribd, Inc. products, Everand and Fable.

MethodologyThis is Scribd's first transparency report. It covers the first half of 2026 (January 1 to June 30, 2026). The data includes all content uploaded to or moderated on Scribd and Slideshare worldwide during that period, and every action taken by our automated moderation systems and human review teams.
