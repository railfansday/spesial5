Fable Quest Trailer Ratio’d And Compared To Dragon Age

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Xbox has just released a new gameplay trailer for Fable showing one of the game’s quests, but it might not have been the best showcase since it’s being bombarded with dislikes.

Ever since the initial reveal trailer more than six years ago, Fable has easily been one of Xbox’s most highly anticipated games. That excitement has only increased with each gameplay showcase and trailer, which have shown just how far Playground Games has pushed the original trilogy’s mechanics, even if the reboot is leaving some things behind.

Of course, while most people are looking forward to Fable, it’s also been a target for online hate campaigns ever since the second trailer. Recent showings of Fable, including one that was focused on combat, have notably had a lot of backlash, and that drama has all come to a head with the latest trailer for the game.

Earlier this week, the official Fable YouTube account shared a new trailer for the game that highlights a quest where the Hero and Arden investigate what’s happening to Oakshire. The gameplay admittedly isn’t the most exciting thing in the world, since it mostly just shows the Hero walking through the area, getting in a fight, and talking to Arden before a big plot tease at the end.

Fable will let us hit on as many townsfolk as we want, but am I the only one who liked getting at least one proper romance option in Fable 3?

While it’s not a great showcase, the quest trailer is pretty harmless overall in my opinion and not that noteworthy. People don’t seem to agree with that, though, as the trailer is being ratio’d on YouTube with 4,200 dislikes compared to 3,400 likes as of the time of writing and an incredibly negative comments section with some brutal reactions.

One notable trend in the comments section is comparisons to Dragon Age: The Veilguard, with one popular comment calling it “Fable Age: The Veilguard”. Others are simply calling it out as a “disappointing” trailer that ended too soon and didn’t really show anything of substance. As one user puts it, “Is that really the best they can show?”

The Fable account is definitely seeing these negative comments too, as it’s heart-reacted the few positive ones praising the voice acting and graphics.

It’s not just the YouTube video that’s being slammed, either, as Xbox CEO Asha Sharma’s post about the trailer on Twitter is also full of backlash and the usual “Sweet Baby Inc” comments over two female characters. Some of it is clearly bad faith, but I’d be very hard-pressed to say the footage was worthy of a trailer.

It's been a long, hard road back to success for Sports Interactive, but FM 27 is the game I hoped it would be and so much more.
