Spirit Crossing is a cosy life sim with some meat on its bones, now available on iOS

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

When I say 'life sim,' your first thought would be a laid-back, slow-paced, relaxing experience with little-to-no action. Your second would be to get your hearing checked because I haven't said a word. And the third is whether or not the more action-focused life sim Spirit Crossing is finally out on iOS, which it is!

Spirit Crossing: Cozy Life Sim was previously a Netflix exclusive, but has slowly begun to expand out into other platforms, including the iOS App Store. Spirit Crossing sees you explore the world of the Crosslands, a place wracked by storms that leave spirits in need of healing floating adrift, and it's up to you to help them out.

And the 'action' focus isn't slim either, as Spirit Crossing offers traversal around a vast, sprawling world on your hunt to help out the spirits. It's not exactly dark soul, but there's definite fun and excitement to be had here beyond just relaxing and farming

With over five years of development under its belt, not to mention a good period on Netflix, Spirit Crossing is well worth checking out to see what it has to offer after all that time in the oven!
