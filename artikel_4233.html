Grand Theft Auto 6 has been out for 24-36 hours now, depending on where you live. Players are only just scratching the surface, but they're already making some incredible discoveries in the game's early sections. References to GTA 5's Trevor, Franklin, and Michael have already been spotted, there have been some Dexter Easter eggs, and we could be on our way to the next Mount Chiliad mystery.

Players have also been Googling some very suspect phrases...

The game's simulation mechanics have been taken to the next level this time around, and serious effort has gone into the health and fitness elements. However, while some players like their protagonists buff, others prefer a chunkier, more realistic version.

Within 24 hours, players have min/maxxed their characters' fitness and weight levels, trying to make them as plump as possible. Unfortunately, there are many complaints that the biggest just ain't big enough.

On Reddit, GTA 6 player TheKey32 showed off their max-weight, low-muscle Jason. While there are signs of a double chin emerging, and a bit of a dad bod poking through his shirt, Jason is still buff, and those extra pounds kinda ​​​​​​suit him. There have, naturally, been calls to make him fatter.

"That is nowhere near chunky enough. I want my Jason to NEEED a mobility scooter to get around," said wrghf. finnjakefionnacake added, "I cannot believe that the devs thought, in America, in Florida, in 2026, that this was enough for max fat. I was honestly flabbergasted."

The discontent continued throughout the thread, with many pointing to Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas' CJ as an example of the heft they'd have liked to see.

While much of the discussion on Reddit focused on Jason, Twitter skewed more towards Lucia. The sentiment, however, remained the same.

I'd argue that max-weight Lucia could just about pass herself off as thicc, but she's not quite at the weight that many were hoping for.

"That is NOT the body of a girl with 'low muscle', you can see ab definition and collarbones," said one user. "Is that all? Is that the max? How disappointing, Rockstar..." added another.

The perceived lack of weight has, in a way, amped up excitement for GTA 6's PC version, with many exclaiming that their first port of call will be to mod the game with XXL protagonists.
