24 No-Bake Desserts To Make For Thanksgiving When There's No Room In The Oven

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Now you just have to find space in the fridge!

Thanksgiving is a beautiful time with family, but just like figuring out how to fit all your loved ones around the dinner table, you're also bound to struggle to find space in your oven for all the amazing foods you want to cook. By the time you’ve made your roasted turkey, stuffing, green bean casserole, and sweet potato casserole with marshmallows, there’s no more space or time left for dessert. The Delish team is here to say we’ve got dessert covered —and it requires *zero* time in the oven, so you won’t have to play oven Jenga with all your delicious Thanksgiving sides. These 24 dessert recipes won’t take up an inch of that coveted oven space.
