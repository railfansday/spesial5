GTA 6 causes surge in UK physical game sales, despite there being no disc in the box

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Grand Theft Auto 6 has had a huge effect on physical game sales in the UK, despite not actually containing a disc in the box.

NielsenIQ data shows that GTA 6 drove boxed software sales up an enormous 409 percent for the launch period ending 21st November, compared to the previous week, resulting in the biggest week for UK physical game sales since Christmas 2018.

GTA 6 alone accounted for £32m of the total £43m boxed software sales, apparently - around 74 percent, with 88 percent of its sales generated on day-one. But it's not a champion or record-breaker in this regard. In fact, GTA 6 places 17th in the all-time table of first-week boxed revenue, which is refreshingly humbling given the money-making records it's been breaking elsewhere. This is to do with the overwhelming shift towards buying games digitally, of course.

Nevertheless, Rockstar is well represented in the all-time list (which stretches back to 1995), because it's none other than GTA 5 that sits at the top, and GTA 4 at 11th.

But the GTA 6 effect is that of a rising tide, with console hardware sales rising 38 percent, to their highest level since 2025. Specifically, PS5 sales rose 46 percent and Xbox Series sales jumped a considerable 75 percent. And it's been a similar result in the US, where PS5 and Xbox Series sales have more than doubled.

Gaming accessory sales also got a boost, with PS5 controller sales up 355 percent, gaming headset sales up 94 percent, and - more nichely - gaming chair sales up 32 percent, which is apparently in line with the 40 percent increase they experienced around GTA 5.

"While the physical games market looks very different today compared with previous blockbuster launches, GTA 6 has still made a huge impact," said NielsenIQ's Dorian Bloch. "Back in 2008, boxed software accounted for half of all games retail revenue for the combined sectors of software, hardware, and gaming accessories, compared with 15 percent so far in 2026. This is a reflection of the market increasingly shifting towards digital downloads."

It's against this backdrop, of course, that Sony made its hugely controversial decision to wind down production of PlayStation game discs starting January 2028. This has led to boycotts and backlash and much angry sentiment towards the company, but Grand Theft Auto 6's enormous digital success is likely to be a hard example to ignore.
