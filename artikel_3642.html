One of the cars Randy wants in GTA 6 is the Ocelot Bloodless Jugular—and don't let the name fool you, its a slick and speedy ride that'll you'll be tempted to keep for yourself.

But Randy doesn't know the locations of many of the cars on his wishlist, and can't point you to the Ocelot Bloodless Jugular: you just need to keep an eye out for it. Luckily, we spotted it ourselves, so just follow the simple steps below to find it.

Head to Belville: It's a town in Leonard County, in the central eastern part of the map, a bit south of Lake Leonida and north of La Perle.

In the northern edge of Belville there's a little cluster of four homes laid out almost in an X pattern. The southernmost home of that cluster has a garage you can break into. Once you open the garage door, you'll see the white Ocelot Bloodless Jugular.

You'll need the key cloner to get in, which I assume you already have since you're doing jobs for Randy. No need to worry about the tracker, it's a standard one so it won't transmit your location to the cops. You still don't want to dawdle, because the owner of the car has a gun and he's not afraid to use it. He'll start shooting if he sees you boost the car.

Randy will pay you $10,615 for the Ocelot Bloodless Jugular. He'll pay an extra $5,000 if you bring it to him in pristine condition.
