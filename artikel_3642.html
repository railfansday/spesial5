Modders already have GTA 6 running on PC, after CyberLeek released a full Xbox build of the game

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

A PC version of Grand Theft Auto 6 - one from unofficial sources, at least - may be closer than we thought. After suddenly returning last week with a near-full build of the Xbox version, notorious leaker CyberLeek has now posted the missing piece they intentionally held back: the game's executable (.exe) file.

"The exe is out. Always free. An early Christmas gift from CL," CyberLeek reportedly wrote, as relayed on Reddit. "The torch passes to the open source re-comp and emulation projects."

Hours later, the recompilation modders and hackers part of the Project Leonida Discord server - the current hub for all GTA 6 recompilation efforts - posted footage of the game booting up on PC.

In just a matter of hours, modders - with the help of Claude AI - managed to turn the leaked Xbox build into a PC port, of sorts. In this Reddit video, you can see the game boot up to the initial setup before hitting a black screen. The build is clearly not yet playable, though Eurogamer has not directly verified those claims.

Shayder, one of the lead developers on Project Leonida, said in a since-deleted post on X that we should "expect a lot of static recompilation work to be done soon." On the project's Discord server, they later clarified that it could take about a week for the build to reach a playable state on PC.

As for CyberLeek, the leaker is still soliciting donations for a secret project "for the gamers", adding in the build's readme file, "Nobody has to give anything, and every release stays free either way. But every donation is a vote for more of this, and so is every trade of the token."

Rockstar has not announced a PC version of GTA 6, but the company has historically released its games on PC several months after their console debut. In October, a report said GTA 6's PC version would arrive in the first quarter of 2027, an unusually short gap between the console and PC releases.

These latest recompilation efforts, however, may force Rockstar to speed up development of the PC version. Though the recompiled build may never reach the level of quality or stability of an official port, many of those eager to play the game on PC likely wouldn't care.
