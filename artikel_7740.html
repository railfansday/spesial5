Serial Armed Robber Sentenced to 30 Years in Federal Prison

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Tampa, Florida – Devon Floyd (33, Tampa) has been sentenced by U.S. District Judge Thomas P. Barber to 30 years in federal prison for robbing four convenience stores during the summer of 2023. A federal jury found Floyd guilty in July 2026. U.S. Attorney Gregory W. Kehoe made the announcement.
