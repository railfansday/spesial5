All of these limit what you can convert efficiently.

You might want to consider holding bonds in tIRA. There is a section in the wiki that describes the process of holding equities in taxable and bonds in tax-deferred. When you need money sell equities in taxable (generally pay less tax and just on the gain) and buy the same equities in tax-deferred (after selling the bonds or using the income from them). This does transfer some equities to tax-deferred, so in some cases it might not be the best solution. But, it will give you more low marginal rate room for Roth conversions.
