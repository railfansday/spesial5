Use Flashfood if a grocery store near you sells through it. You get meat, produce, and dairy near their best-by dates at up to half off, paid for in the app and picked up in the store. It pays off only when you’d have bought that food anyway and can cook or freeze it before its date.

Partner stores post their surplus fresh food in the app each day, so what you can buy depends on what your store has left. Flashfood suits shoppers who cook most nights and can plan meals around that day’s deals.

Shoppers who buy that beef and chicken at half off every week save about $400 a year, even after a $2.99 fee on each order. Anyone whose groceries already spoil before they’re eaten should stop wasting food first, and shoppers with no partner store nearby can try Too Good To Go’s surprise bags at half price or less.
