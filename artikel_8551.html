25 Legit Ways to Make Money From Home – and What Each Pays

Legit ways to make money from home don’t pay alike. Remote jobs and freelance work pay a real hourly rate, while paid surveys add up to pocket change. Pick the one that fits the hours, skills, and space you have, and walk away from any job that asks you to pay up front.

In the Federal Reserve’s survey of household finances for 2024, 20% of adults had done some gig work in the month before, most often selling things. Only 21% of them called it their main job.

Which way fits depends on what you have most of: spare hours, a skill, or space and things to rent out. The kind of work sets the pay. A remote job or a freelance skill pays a real hourly rate, renting earns from space or a car you already own, selling pays item by item, and online gigs like paid surveys pay pocket change.

Freelance and gig pay arrives with no tax taken out, so set aside part of every payment for taxes on your self-employment side income.

Airbnb says the typical U.S. host earned about $15,600 in extra income in 2025. If you’d rather not host guests, Neighbor says an empty garage can bring in $100 to $600 a month, rented for storage or parking. Neighbor keeps 4.9% plus 30 cents of each payout. Before you list either one, confirm that your lease, your homeowners association, and your city allow it.

Neighbor says an empty parking space can rent for $50 to $300 a month, and listing one costs nothing. Price yours by checking what garages and lots nearby charge by the month.

Turo puts its U.S. hosts’ average at $10,489 a year per car. In most U.S. markets, hosts keep 70%, 80%, or 90% of each trip’s price, depending on the protection plan they choose. Run your car through Turo’s Carculator to see what yours could earn in your market.

Selling pays once, not every month. On eBay, the fee in most categories is 13.6% of the sale’s total plus 40 cents for an order over $10, and your first 250 listings each month are free. That makes the fee on a $50 sale $7.20. Start with the item that would sell for the most.

Etsy charges 20 cents to list an item and 6.5% of each sale, shipping included, plus a payment processing fee. On a $30 piece with free shipping, the first two fees come to $2.15. Price each piece to cover materials, those fees, and a fair rate for your hours.

Retail arbitrage means buying discounted products in stores and reselling them online for more. You earn only the gap between the two prices after the resale site’s fees, and your cash sits in stock until it sells. Before you buy, look up what the same item has actually sold for online.

In an Institute for Justice survey of 775 people who sell food made in their home kitchens, median profit was $500 for all of 2016, while producers averaged 15 hours of work a week. Check your state’s cottage food law, which sets the foods you may sell from a home kitchen.

Editorial freelancers charge median rates of $65 to $75 an hour for business and marketing writing and $50 to $60 for copyediting it, according to the Editorial Freelancers Association’s 2026 rate chart. Win your first clients on Upwork, which takes 0% to 15% of each contract.

Interpreters and translators earned a median $28.93 an hour in May 2025, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS). Translate between the two languages you know best, starting with documents in a field you already work in, such as law or medicine.

A virtual assistant does an administrative assistant’s work from home. Secretaries and administrative assistants earned a median $23.23 an hour in May 2025, the BLS reports. Offer two or three tasks you already do well, such as scheduling, email, and travel booking.

Management consultants, whom the BLS counts as management analysts, earned a median $48.97 an hour in May 2025. Pitch your first project to a former employer or colleague who already knows your work.

Tutors earned a median $20.84 an hour in May 2025, according to BLS wage data. On Wyzant, tutors keep 75% of the hourly rate they post. Apply in the one or two subjects you know best.

Animal care and service workers earned a median $17.08 an hour in May 2025, per the BLS. On Rover, you set your own prices and, outside California, take home 80% of each booking. Offer to watch pets in your own home, so the work fits around the rest of your day.

These are jobs with an employer, and remote job websites list the openings you can do from home.

Customer service representatives earned a median $21.53 an hour in May 2025, and some employers let them work from home, the BLS says. Apply for remote openings listed on the company’s own careers page.

Computer support specialists earned a median $30.24 an hour in May 2025, and some can work from home, per the BLS. Look for remote help desk openings, and list every system and program you’ve fixed for others.

Data entry keyers earned a median $19.88 an hour in May 2025, per BLS wage data, and staffing agencies employ more of them than any other industry. Register with a staffing agency that places office workers.

Medical transcriptionists earned a median $19.43 an hour in May 2025, but the BLS expects jobs in the field to fall 4% by 2035 as speech recognition reduces the need for them. Start with a certificate program beyond high school, which the job typically requires.

These earn nothing until people find what you made, and the money grows with your audience.

A blog earns from ads and affiliate links once it has readers. The ad network Mediavine takes small blogs into its Journey program once they draw 1,000 visits in 30 days from countries such as the U.S. and Canada. Its main program waits until a site makes $5,000 a year from ads. Pick one topic you know well and publish on a schedule you can keep.

Amazon’s Kindle Direct Publishing offers a 70% royalty option on e-books priced $2.99 to $12.99. Audiobook narrators on ACX, Audible’s marketplace for them, are paid by the finished hour of audio, a share of royalties, or both. ACX says most narrators need 5 to 7 hours of work for each finished hour, so a rate of $100 per finished hour comes to $14 to $20 for each hour you work. Start with a short e-book on something you know well, priced inside that 70% band.

Udemy pays instructors 97% of the revenue from students who buy through the instructor’s own coupon or referral link and 37% from every other sale. Outline a course on a skill people already pay to learn, and send your own audience to it first.

Flippa suggests pricing a site at 2 to 2.5 times its net profit over the past 12 months, so a site netting $500 a month would list for $12,000 to $15,000. Build one that earns steadily for a year before you sell it.

These pay per task, and the work comes only when a company needs testers or survey takers.

Branded Surveys says members can expect $5 to $100 a month, depending on how often they take surveys and how many are available, which is pocket change. Most of its surveys run 5 to 20 minutes, so take them in spare minutes, and cash out once you reach 500 points, or $5.

User Interviews, which pays people for surveys, focus groups, and user tests, says its average study pays over $45, with payment within a few days of each session. Sign up and apply to studies about products you already use.

PlaytestCloud pays $5 to $9 for a typical 15-minute playtest and survey, and its onboarding session pays nothing. Sign up, finish the onboarding, and watch your email for invitations.

A real employer never charges you to get hired, the Federal Trade Commission warns, so anyone who does is running a scam. In a work-from-home scam, the charge comes as a starter kit, training, or a certification you have to buy first. Search the company’s name plus the word “scam” before you hand over money or personal details.
