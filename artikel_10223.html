You can get a new phone plan for as low as $6.50/month for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Phone plans are incredibly expensive, but they don’t always have to be. With Black Friday deals in full swing, you can currently score a new phone plan for as little as $6.50 per month.

Both running on T-Mobile’s network, Ultra Mobile and Mint Mobile have unveiled their Black Friday deals, and they’re great ways to save on your monthly phone bill.

Starting with Ultra Mobile, plans are available starting at just $6.50 per month, with every plan 50% off of the usual price when you sign up for 12 months of service upfront. Here’s the breakdown:

You can also save on the “Ultra Unlimited” plans which include unlimited data on your phone and up to 10GB or 25GB of mobile hotspot data, respectively.

All of these plans support physical SIMs or eSIMs, with these discounted prices valid for the first year of service.

Meanwhile, Mint Mobile also has a pretty great offer available.

Every Mint plan is $15/month for the first year. You can choose from 3-month, 6-month, or 12-month packages with 6GB, 17GB, 23GB, or Unlimited monthly data. So, regardless of how much data you want, you’re paying $45 for a 3-month plan, $90 for a 6-month plan, or $180 for a 12-month plan.

After the initial plan ends, you’ll go back to Mint’s normal pricing, which varies by billing period.
