MotorCity Casino Gains 15.6% as MGM and Greektown Slip in Detroit’s October Revenue

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Growth at MotorCity Casino outweighed declines at MGM Grand Detroit and Hollywood Casino at Greektown, lifting Detroit's October table and slot revenue from a year earlier. Retail sports betting also rebounded sharply from September, adding to the month's gains.
