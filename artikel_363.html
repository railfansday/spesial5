175+ major websites affected by malware hack, report says: What we know

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The past several days have seen non-stop outages at Microsoft, Chase Bank, and Apple, to name a few.

Fitting with that theme, cybersecurity researcher Scott Helme published a report on Monday detailing a malware infection that he said affected more than 175 major websites, including CNN, Zoom, Equifax, Ikea, and Peacock. You can check out Helme's full report at Report URI for all of the granular details, but the hackers were reportedly able to compromise those sites with malware through a third-party platform called Optimizely, which creates popular tools for testing websites.

The headline news is alarming — malware reportedly made its way to popular websites people visit every day — but the researcher found that the attackers' crypto wallets only contained about $7,000, all of which was transferred during a nine-hour time period on Nov. 16. And just because malware appeared on a site, that doesn't necessarily mean that hackers successfully exploited visitors to that sites. According to a statement from Optimizely, the situation has already been taken care of.

"Earlier today, Optimizely identified a security incident in which an unauthorized party exploited a vulnerability in Experimentation and used it to add malicious JavaScript to some customer projects," Optimizely said. "We've fixed the vulnerability and removed the injected code from affected projects. We've also taken steps to secure affected user accounts and put additional protections in place to prevent unauthorized code from being delivered."

So, not quite as catastrophic as the big Outlook outage, but it's another reminder that all of the platforms we use every day are vulnerable to some extent — as if we needed another reminder.
