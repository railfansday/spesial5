Bayern plotted swoop for Vušković last summer as Liverpool remain interested

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

As Sky Germany understands, Bayern Munich had considered making a potential move for Luka Vušković last summer.

However, in the end the German record champions decided against it because Kim Min-Jae ended up staying in Munich.

Bayern are still on pursuing Vušković, but any move has become unrealistic due to the exorbitant transfer fee.

As noted by the pay-TV channel, the 19-year old talent is already attracting interest from several top clubs within the Premier League.

One of which – Liverpool FC – are regularly monitoring Vušković and even have him on their shortlist for next summer.

With his contract at Brighton is valid until 2031 and no release clause available, the Croatian could cost a sum of up to €100million.

Ultimately, Vušković is deemed to be very happy under Fabian Hürzeler. However, his dream is to join La Liga giants Real Madrid one day.
