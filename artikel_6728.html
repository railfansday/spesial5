Tyler Hawkins Says ‘SLOMW: OC’ Is a “Tough” Watch Because He and Bobbi Althoff Quietly Split 2 Months Ago

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County has brought a bunch of fresh faces to the franchise, including podcaster Bobbi Althoff. Of course, that includes her personal life and her relationship with basketball player Tyler “Ty” Hawkins.

While we saw him accompany Bobbi to several events throughout the spin-off’s inaugural season, the pair is unfortunately no longer together after filming. After months of breakup speculation, Ty revealed he and Bobbi quietly split in September due to “different” lifestyles.

“Bobbi and I split about two months ago,” he said in a TikTok posted on Monday, November 16. “It wasn’t a bad breakup. There wasn’t one thing that led to us splitting.”

He continued, “I just think that we were two very different people, and I think that over time we realized we weren’t as compatible as we thought we were.”

After posting the breakup announcement, Ty spoke with People about the split—and admitted it’s been “tough” watching SLOMW: OC knowing they’re no longer a couple.

“Actually seeing us as a couple knowing that we aren’t together was tough. I miss her and everything and the life that we had,” he told the outlet, emphasizing that they ended things amicably.

“She’s been getting great feedback, which is all I can hope for,” he said of his ex’s involvement on the Hulu reality series. “It’s great to see her flourish.”

Bobbi and Tyler dated for nearly two years, marking her first public relationship after her split from ex-husband Cory Althoff in 2024.

She has yet to comment on Tyler’s breakup comments.
