<b><strong>A Little Blue Box for Every Holiday Wish</strong></b>

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Tiffany & Co. promises that good things come in small packages this season and beyond

Along with cooler temperatures and shorter days, colorful cues signal the holiday season drawing close: silver bells, white ribbons, and evergreen trees are obvious signs of the most festive time of the year. But two more hues make the moment more merry and bright: gold jewelry in Tiffany blue gift boxes. Both have been hallmarks of the heritage brand since its earliest days. The same brilliant robin’s egg blue tone adorned the cover of the first Tiffany Blue Book, a catalog of the quintessential American jeweler’s most exceptional items, beginning in 1845. From historic French crown jewels to modern cuffs by famed designers, gold jewelry has been indispensable to the Tiffany identity then and now. And because you’ve certainly got a special someone or many who would delight in a treasure from the elevated house, we’ve curated the collection to tailor a selection based on their personality. Keep reading for a holiday list built on a legacy of innovative design and craftsmanship.

Tiffany HardWear earrings with a dash of turquoise are powerful yet playful. They will captivate the power broker (or aspiring one) on your gift list. Their strong, classic silhouette means business, while the colored stone accents add whimsy to the equation.

When you’re gift hunting for someone who always sees the silver lining (or needs a reminder to), the Bird on a Rock pendant is the answer. The diamond-bedecked gold and platinum necklace features a wing motif first created by legendary designer Jean Schlumberger for Tiffany in 1965. It is a dose of optimism to wear every single day.

For someone who means the world to you, give a gift that celebrates the ties you share. Inspired by an archival bow, the graceful knot of the Tiffany Knot wire bangle is a lasting symbol of love and connection. Crafted in 18-karat yellow gold, the bracelet is hand polished for a brilliant finish and set with round brilliant diamonds.

A ring with a little lift is made for free spirits who like their jewelry to make an entrance. The Bird on a Rock by Tiffany, Wings Band Ring reimagines the collection’s iconic motif in sculptural feathers of 18-karat yellow gold and platinum, accented with pavé diamonds. Worn alone or stacked, its graceful silhouette brings a bold, artful finish to any look.

Even the person who has everything will agree it’s impossible to have too many gold necklaces—especially one with a pop of color. Give the gift of a Tiffany HardWear pendant in 18-karat yellow gold and turquoise, and it may be the one they wear ever after. Its sculptural gold silhouette and vibrant stone bring a fresh take to the collection’s signature links.
