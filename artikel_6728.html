Your Taurus Monthly Horoscope for December 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Share your secrets with someone you trust, Taurus.

Wear your heart on your sleeve, Taurus! The month begins with the Sun moving through Sagittarius, encouraging you to explore trust, intimacy and vulnerability in your close relationships. You’re also more aware of reciprocity—or a lack there of—in certain connections and situations in your life. This can also invite you to spend more time with your shadow side and emotions, processing feelings you’d rather ignore and choosing to invest in your own healing and personal growth. Security is also a theme, and important financial developments and emotional developments that impact your sense of grounding can unfold.

It’s all or nothing when it comes to love once Venus enters Scorpio on December 4. This is the season for testing your relationship and your ability to be vulnerable, honest, and intimate with the important people in your life. Some might strengthen their connection and take the next step, while others are disrupting unhealthy power dynamics and might part ways. This is also a great time for collaboration, you can attract more opportunities, clients, and people into your world who appreciate your creativity and what you bring to the table.

You’re moving through a period of self-acceptance once Mercury enters Sagittarius on December 6. The next few weeks push you in a direction that helps you heal and become more self-aware. This can be a tender and sensitive time, as you’ve navigating old wounds and choosing a new way forward. Practically, you’re also thinking more about your longterm investments. You’re ready to have conversations about shared finances and resources, and also ask for your worth in any negotiations.

A New Moon in Sagittarius on December 8 brings new financial developments into your world. You could learn about a new contract or opportunity that feels expansive or where you could learn something new or pick up a new skill in the process. Emotionally, you might also experience a turning point, finding the silver lining in a situation that helps you break free from the past or vices that have kept you stuck.

You’re ready to be accountable in new ways and break bad habits once Saturn stations direct in Aries on December 10. You’re probably already aware of what you need to change and the ways in which you’ve been getting in your own way. The bad news: This work isn’t easy and it can bring up feelings of grief and fear. The good news: You have the structure and foundation necessary to make a longterm change. The time is now—you’re turning a new leaf. Say goodbye to the person, habit, or situation in your life that’s keeping you repeating cycles you’ve outgrown.

It’s time to commit to a spiritual practice once Neptune stations direct in Aries on December 12. Spending more time connecting with your intuition, grounding exercises, or learning more about ancestral spiritual practices can bring revelations and healing. You might also feel called to get involved and give back to the collective somehow—by sharing your creative gifts, volunteering, or finding some type of more meaningful work. Divine downloads and insights find you through your dreams and other symbols that pop up during your day, too.

There are seismic changes in your private life when Juptier stations retrograde in Leo, also on December 12. You might make a decision about your living situation—finding a new roommate, expanding your family and adopting a fur baby, introducing someone special to your loved ones, or confronting an issue with a family member that can spark healing. The energy is supportive, but it can also expand and amplify feelings you’ve hidden. You might reflect more of your comfort level with being seen vs. keeping some things private, just for you, as well.

The Sun enters Capricorn and the Winter Solstice takes place on December 21, bringing important news your way. You can experience revelations when you choose to navigate boundaries and be direct with others. This can also give you the structure necessary to study something new, go back to school, or plan a new trip or adventure in your life. There is more movement around communication over the coming weeks, so share your thoughts and feelings widely!

A Full Moon in Cancer on December 23 helps you speak your truth. An ongoing issue or conversation reaches a boiling point. You might need to confront a family member or share something from your past. This can also help you make an important decision about your future—zooming out and thinking about what is meaningful and how to invest your time so you can reach some of your larger goals.

The month wraps up with Mercury moving into Capricorn on December 25, encouraging you to learn something new and expand your knowledge. You could gain more attention for your skills, writing, or even get something published. You’re also more focused on your mindset, which can help you break limiting beliefs and come up with habits that are supportive. If there’s a skill or language you want to brush up on, this is your season to study and adopt a beginner’s mindset. You’re craving new experiences and knowledge!
