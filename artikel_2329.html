Bayern said to be in talks to sign French goalkeeper

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Bayern Munich have reportedly made contact over a potential move for 21-year-old RC Lens goalkeeper Robin Risser.

According to Gianluca Di Marzio, the German champions have approached the young goalkeeper as they look to fend off competition from the Premier League.

The proposed plan would be to sign Risser in January, loan him back to RC Lens for the remainder of the season and then integrate him into Vincent Kompany's squad for the 2027/28 campaign.

However, such a move would go against what German media have previously reported about Bayern's goalkeeping plans.

With Jonas Urbig expected to step up as Bayern's first-choice goalkeeper if Manuel Neuer retires as anticipated this summer, reports have suggested that the German champions could bring in an experienced goalkeeper to provide backup.

Oliver Baumann, Loris Karius and Bernd Leno are among the experienced options who have been mentioned.
