Stock Market Today, Nov. 3: Grab Holdings Jumps 9% on Q3 Revenue Beat and Raised 2026 Outlook

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

On Nov. 3, 2026, shares of the Southeast Asian super-app climbed as profitability improved across all three core segments for the first time.

Grab Holdings (GRAB +9.09%), a Southeast Asian super-app for ride-hailing, delivery, and fintech services, closed at $3.12, up 9.09%. The stock rose after Grab reported Q3 revenue above estimates and raised its full-year 2026 outlook. Investors are watching whether the higher guidance and improved profitability extend the move. Trading volume reached 144.8M shares, coming in about 143% above its three-month average of 59.7M shares. Grab Holdings IPO'd in 2020 and has fallen 74% since going public.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC +0.36%) rose 0.36% to 7,569, and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +0.83%) gained 0.83% to 25,372. In internet services: ride-hailing, delivery, and digital financial services, Uber Technologies (UBER -0.17%) closed at $66.61, down 0.15%, while DoorDash (DASH -3.55%) closed at $183.32, down 3.55%, as delivery-platform peers digested growth and margin updates.
