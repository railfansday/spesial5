ChatGPT expands GPT-6.2 Sol as OpenAI sets retirement date for two models

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

OpenAI is bringing GPT-6.2 Sol to the Chat tab in ChatGPT today, less than a week after the model debuted in Work and Codex. The company also set a retirement date for two older models.

OpenAI says it tuned this version of Sol for everyday conversation. It’s better at picking out details in photos, like the symbols on a parking sign, and following your instructions when searching the web.

If you’re comparing hotels for a weekend trip, for example, ChatGPT can weigh a shorter walk against a lower nightly rate. It can also offer to alert you when something you’re shopping for drops in price.

The global rollout starts today for ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users.

As for the outgoing models, OpenAI says it will retire GPT-5.6 Sol from Chat, Work, and Codex on December 23.

GPT-5.6 Terra will also leave ChatGPT Work and Codex on the same day next month.
