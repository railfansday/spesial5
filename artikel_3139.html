CEMIG (CIG) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Tax reversal and a BRL 7 billion investment plan shape this quarter.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Companhia Energética de Minas Gerais - CEMIG (CIG +4.35%) reported a third quarter characterized by the execution of its multiyear BRL 7 billion investment plan and the renewal of core energy concessions. Management confirmed the renewal of the Sa Carvalho plant for 30 years and noted positive expectations regarding the renewal processes for the Nova Ponte and Emborcacao plants currently with the Ministry of Mines and Energy. The company achieved a AAA credit rating from major agencies, which management indicated supports its strategy to finance growth through the capital markets. Financial results were significantly influenced by a reversal of tax provisions and a tariff adjustment that contributed to cash generation in the distribution segment.
