Sofia Carson, Taylor John Smith & Charlie Hall Board NFL Rom-Com ‘The Christmas Game’ From Netflix

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

EXCLUSIVE: Sofia Carson, star of such juggernaut Netflix rom-coms as My Oxford Year and The Life List, is set to star in The Christmas Game, a new NFL-themed holiday rom-com from the streamer. Up-and-comers Taylor John Smith (Where the Crawdads Sing) and Charlie Hall (Maximum Pleasure Guaranteed) complete the film’s love triangle.

Directed by Laura Terruso, who helmed Netflix’s Sabrina Carpenter dance-crew comedy Work It, The Christmas Game revolves around a blunt, big-deal sports agent who is publicly dumped by her star client. She must then land an even bigger one to save her career — her NFL quarterback ex-boyfriend — before her rival signs him by Christmas. Notably, The Christmas Game marks Netflix’s first narrative feature in partnership with the NFL and Skydance Sports Entertainment. While Christmas Day football has been a holiday staple at Netflix since 2024, the project brings that tradition onto the holiday movie slate for 2027. Watch on Deadline

The script for The Christmas Game is by Danielle Morrow and Vanessa Marano, who previously penned the NFL rom-coms Holiday Touchdown: A Bills Love Story and A Make or Break Holiday for Hallmark Channel. Producers are Jesse Sisgold, Jason Reed and Sandino Moya Smith for Skydance Sports Entertainment. Carson and Jessica Matthews are exec producing.

News of the film comes following a big first weekend for Guarding Stars, Netflix’s holiday rom-com starring Leighton Meester and Jared Padalecki, which racked up 27.6M views in its first three days.

In addition to the projects aforementioned, Carson has found major success at Netflix with the romantic drama Purple Hearts, as well as Carry-On, the action-thriller from Jaume Collet-Serra, where she starred opposite Taron Egerton. The latter film is Netflix’s third most popular of all time, behind KPop Demon Hunters and Red Notice. The actress is repped by WME, Ziffren Brittenham and Kleinberg Lange Cuddy & Carlo.

Previously seen in films such as Where the Crawdads Sing and Warfare, as well as HBO’s Sharp Objects, Smith is repped by CAA and Vybe Trybe.

Hall stars opposite Tatiana Maslany in Apple’s acclaimed thriller Maximum Pleasure Guaranteed, heading into its second season. He’ll next be seen checking into The White Lotus Season 4 and is also set to write and star in a new college fraternity drug ring series, executive produced by Barry‘s Alec Berg, for Netflix. He is repped by Untitled and CAA.

Terruso most recently directed About My Father, the Lionsgate comedy starring Robert De Niro and Sebastian Maniscalco. She is repped by Verve and Artists First.

Morrow is repped by Eris Talent Agency and AKA Talent Agency; Marano by Marano Entertainment, Untitled and Hansen, Jacobson, Teller.
