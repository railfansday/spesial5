The countdown to Black Friday is on, and many discounts have already arrived. Although the shopping holiday isn’t officially until Friday, November 27th, retailers are already dropping early deals on some of our favorite tech. Of course, with prices rising across the board, a good discount doesn’t necessarily mean you’re getting a historically low price. But we’re still seeing worthwhile discounts on a wide variety of tried-and-tested gear, with more expected later this week.

Below, we’ve rounded up the best early Black Friday deals we’ve found so far. Prices could drop further as Black Friday approaches, as is always the case, but these deals are worth considering if you’d rather shop early and beat the holiday rush. We’ll continue to update this guide as more retailers launch their holiday sales, too, so be sure to check back regularly as we get closer to the main event.

Apple’s AirPods Pro 3 deliver the best noise cancellation and sound quality of any AirPods yet, along with a more comfortable, secure fit and improved water resistance. They also feature built-in heart rate tracking for workouts and support live translations for a handful of languages. Read our review.

The Nintendo Switch 2 features a 7.9-inch LCD display, magnetically attached Joy-Con 2 controllers, backward compatibility with most original Switch games, and exclusive titles like Donkey Kong Bananza and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Read our review.

Amazon’s last-gen Fire TV Stick 4K Plus streams in 4K and supports Dolby Atmos, Dolby Vision, and Alexa Plus. It also ships with the redesigned Fire OS and, unlike more recent models, still lets you sideload Android TV apps.

Do you ever wish you could turn on your coffee maker or hit the light switch without getting up? The SwitchBot Bot lets you do that. All you need to do is stick the inexpensive contraption on a standard button or rocker switch, which will allow you to control or schedule your pet feeder, garage door, and a wide range of other devices from your smartphone.

Sony’s last-last-gen noise-canceling headphones were so good, the company brought them back with a few minor improvements. They still offer great ANC, a timeless foldable design, and a 10-band EQ, delivering performance nearly on par with Sony’s newer flagships at a fraction of the cost.

Lego’s miniature bonsai trees can bring a sense of calm to even the most cluttered home. Building each tree — whether it be the black pine, the ginkgo, or the lilac-adorned wisteria — is designed to be a mindfulness practice in itself, centering your giftee as they go. They also make for a great piece of decor once finished, adding a touch of zen to any desk or shelf.

Rechargeable AA batteries are handy, until it’s time to charge them. Most wall chargers let you refill four, maybe six, at once. Zepath’s clever solution is to ship them in a easy-to-use USB-C charging case that charges eight simultaneously. The batteries themselves are long-lasting, too.

Sony’s DualSense Wireless Controller for the PlayStation 5 features immersive haptic feedback and resistance triggers, which feel great to use. It offers a built-in mic, a 3.5mm headphone jack, and a mute button; it also works with PC, macOS, and mobile devices via Bluetooth or USB-C.

The Apple Watch Series 12 introduces AI-powered Audio Intelligence features, including sound recognition alerts, Live Rewind, and Siri Recap. It can also track your heart rate every five seconds, even when you’re not exercising, and offers faster charging than prior models. Read our review.

With 12.4mm drivers, active noise cancellation, and an IP54 rating, the CMF Buds 2A offer high-end features at an affordable price. You can also customize the sound, adjust ANC levels, and find the earbuds using the Nothing X app.

Anker’s durable travel adapter delivers up to 100W of power and works in over 200 countries. It doesn’t support AC plugs, but it does offer a trio of USB-C ports and a nifty LCD display to keep tabs on the charge percentage.

007 First Light, a third-person stealth action game from the studio behind Hitman, is arguably the best action game we’ve played this year. It features a wide variety of missions that have you sneaking through enemy territory, engaging in hand-to-hand combat, and taking part in high-speed car chases — you know, classic Bond stuff. Read our review.

Fanttik’s rechargeable electric screwdriver features three adjustable torque settings, a built-in LED light, and 16 S2 hardened steel bits for tackling everything from furniture assembly to small electronics repairs. It also comes with a magnetic storage case and supports USB-C.

Apple’s most affordable laptop uses the same A18 Pro chip found in the iPhone 16 Pro. It features a vibrant 13-inch display, surprisingly good speakers, and 8GB of RAM, making it a solid option for students and anyone who needs a laptop for everyday tasks. Read our review.

No one should have to deal with a rat’s nest behind their desk when devices like Anker’s 2-in-1 USB-C cable exist. The handy USB-C cable splits at the end, giving you the ability to charge up to two devices from a single port. That means you can split power between a phone and tablet or juice a 16-inch MacBook Pro to 50 percent in just 20 minutes using a single port.

The Nintendo Switch 2 has 256GB of storage, but that won’t get you very far if you’ve built up a medium-sized digital game library. Samsung’s P9 256GB microSD express card doubles the console’s available storage.

You likely won’t find wireless earbuds with a more striking appearance than Nothing’s Ear 3A. They also sound fantastic, boast active noise cancellation, and offer robust EQ settings, making them a great pick for the price. Read our review.

Ember’s latest smart mug is designed to keep your coffee at the ideal temperature for up to three hours, with five temp settings between 120 and 145 degrees Fahrenheit. The third-gen version is similar to prior models; however, you can now adjust the heat settings directly on the mug and throw it in the dishwasher, which wasn’t the case before.

Despite being an entry-level variant of the B6 OLED, this model features four HDMI 2.1 ports, a 120Hz refresh rate, a capable processor, and pixel-perfect image quality and contrast for any content you want to watch. Lower brightness is likely the biggest compromise with getting a cheaper OLED.

JBL’s Tour One M3 noise-canceling headphones come with a touchscreen-equipped wireless transmitter that lets you wirelessly listen to audio from in-flight entertainment systems and other devices without Bluetooth. They also offer solid active noise cancellation and last up to 70 hours on a charge.

Hisense’s 2026 CanvasTV is a 4K QLED TV that doubles as wall art when you’re not watching. It features a matte, anti-glare display and runs on Google’s excellent TV software; it’s also a great pick for gamers thanks to its snappy 144Hz variable refresh rate and a pair of HDMI 2.1 ports.

The Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2 Combo is a beginner-friendly 3D printer that supports four filament spools. It features an enclosed build chamber, automatic bed leveling, and a large touchscreen, making it easy to produce reliable prints.

Moft’s Trackable Tripod Wallet is a MagSafe-compatible wallet that holds two cards and folds into an adjustable phone stand or small tripod. It also supports both Apple’s Find My network and Google’s Find Hub, offers Qi wireless charging, and includes a detachable shutter remote for taking photos.

Govee’s Table Lamp 2 is way more fun than your average table lamp. The color-changing device boasts built-in scheduling and more than 60 dynamic lighting effects, including preset scenes for various holidays. You can even create custom color combos for when “Dandelion” and other presets just aren’t cutting it.

Birdfy’s metal smart bird feeder features a wide-angle 4K camera that lets you get an up-close look at visiting birds, along with a 1.8-liter seed capacity and a built-in solar panel for charging. It also offers free AI-powered recognition of more than 6,000 bird species.

Bambu Lab’s P2S Combo is a 3D printer that can print in multiple colors and automatically dry its materials to help improve print quality. It also features a 5-inch touchscreen, a built-in camera for monitoring prints remotely, and AI-powered tools that can detect printing problems.

Headspace makes it easier to build mindfulness into your everyday life, whether you’re looking to manage stress, improve focus, or get a better night’s sleep. An annual subscription gives you access to hundreds of guided meditations, breathing exercises, and other tools for beginners and experienced users alike.

The Dreame X60 Ultra is a powerful robot vacuum and mop that does a great job of cleaning carpets, thanks to its dual roller brushes and 35,000Pa of suction. It can climb over tall thresholds, squeeze under low furniture, and automatically detach its mop pads when vacuuming carpets.

Saros is a third-person sci-fi shooter in which players fight alien enemies on a mysterious planet trapped in a time loop. The PS5 exclusive features fast-paced combat, a variety of powerful weapons, and challenging boss fights. Read our review.

Bose’s second-gen QuietComfort Headphones build on the original with better sound quality and support for lossless audio over USB-C. They also offer improved noise cancellation and are more comfortable thanks to their lack of “eardrum suck.”

Garmin’s midrange Forerunner watch has an OLED display, a week’s worth of battery life, and dual-frequency GPS for better accuracy. Read our review.

The Matic robot vacuum and mop is ideal for cluttered homes with pets, multiple floors, and a mixture of carpets and hardwood. It offers almost human-like navigation, tackling high-pile rugs, and mopping up wet spills. The quiet bot also requires less maintenance than many rivals thanks to its onboard water tank and dust bag. Read our review.

Azul is a game in which you arrange patterned tiles in order to create the most beautiful tapestry possible. Arranging tiles in a strategic sequence will help you build your score while limiting your opponents’ ability to grow theirs.

The Eufy Omni C28 combines excellent mopping performance with a small self-cleaning dock that’s well suited for apartments and other smaller spaces. It navigates around furniture and into tight areas with ease, while its roller mop does a great job cleaning hard floors.

Arguably the best thing to come out of The Book of Boba Fett was the Mandalorian’s N-1 Starfighter, which Lego has recreated here in miniature. It’s not quite as impressive as the 1,802-piece version, but the cheaper set still packs plenty of detail, including a spring-loaded shooter.

Nothing’s first pair of over-ear headphones feature a retro transparent design and high-end features like active noise cancellation, spatial audio with head tracking, and plush ear pads. They also feature a paddle button that allows you to control music playback. Read our review.

Amazon’s 12.3-inch Alexa Tablet 12 Pro runs Android with access to the Google Play Store, unlike the company’s Fire tablets. It also features built-in Alexa Plus integration, a dedicated Kindle reading mode, and up to 16 hours of battery life. Read our hands-on impressions.

Samsung’s last-gen Frame is a 4K QLED TV that displays art on its anti-glare matte screen when not in use. It also supports Dolby Atmos, HDR10 Plus, and popular streaming apps, with refresh rates up to 144Hz on larger models.

TP-Link’s smart smart plug lets you remotely control lamps, fans, and other appliances using your phone or voice commands. It supports Matter, so it works with all major smart home platforms, and lets you set schedules and timers to automatically turn devices on or off.

Google’s latest pair of wireless earbuds feature crisp sound quality, a comfortable design, and solid active noise cancellation, which is great considering the budget-friendly price tag. They even offer built-in support for Gemini and, unlike the entry-level AirPods, a purple option! Read our review.

Roku now makes some of the most affordable, yet feature-packed 4K OLED TVs available for gamers or movie lovers looking for a big TV upgrade under $1,000. The Pro Series LX with better brightness, faster refresh rate, and a polarizer to reduce reflections starts at $200 more.

If you’re on a tight budget, but want spectacular contrast and brightness control that only an OLED can provide, here’s a deal for you. Alienware’s 1440p 27-inch QD-OLED monitor with 240Hz refresh rate is selling at its lowest price yet. Read our hands-on.

Soundcore makes some of the best wireless earbuds you’ve never heard of — and the Liberty 5 Pro may be its best pair yet. The sleek buds deliver powerful noise cancellation, good sound, and surprisingly fabulous call quality for less than Apple’s latest AirPods.

A great first portable music player/speaker for kids. It uses insertable NFC cards (sold separately) to play music and stories to keep kiddos occupied and entertained without zoning out in front of the TV. This model supports Bluetooth and 3.5mm for private listening, too.

The latest entry in the long-running survival horror series returned in 2026, with two different styles of gameplay (tense first-person and guns-blazing third-person perspectives) via its dual protagonists. Read our review.

The latest Powerbeats Pro are a no-brainer for athletes. They pack fantastic sound and thumping bass, along with active noise cancellation, IPX4 water resistance, and heart rate monitoring. Read our review.

The big difference between the Superstrike and other gaming mice lies beneath its main two mouse buttons. It uses haptic actuators instead of mechanical switches, allowing for lower latency and more longevity.

The Nex Playground’s motion-controlled gameplay is designed for ages five and up. It comes with games like Fruit Ninja and Whack-a-Mole Deluxe, but many more titles are available via a subscription. It’s a ton of fun for kids. This is the lowest price so far in 2026. Read our review.

With a 4.3-inch E Ink screen, the Xteink X4 is smaller and more pocketable than any Kindle and Kobo model currently available. It has some drawbacks — it’s limited to TXT and EPUB files — but it’s great for tinkering; it also has a magnetic mount so you can stick it to the back of MagSafe-ready phones.

The Ring 5 is a fantastic entry point for new users. While short on improvements over its thicker predecessor, it’s hard not to appreciate that it manages to deliver similar battery life despite being thinner. Read our review.

Nothing has changed in terms of aesthetics, but the Max 2 deliver better sound and some new features. Not exactly worth upgrading for, but fantastic for those who are considering them now. Read our full review.

It’s hard to find a cooler lamp than Govee’s Uplighter floor lamp at this price. It can light a room, and display a mesmerizing pattern up your wall and onto your ceiling.

The Meta Quest 3S is a battery-powered standalone VR headset that has the same chipset as the pricier Quest 3, but with the same lenses and screen resolution as the Quest 2. In addition to being able to play many games from the Meta Store, it can also be connected to a gaming PC to play VR games like Half-Life Alyx. Read our review.

Nintendo’s latest Switch 2 exclusive is the best way to relive what is arguably the best Zelda game of all time. The revamped visuals look gorgeous, and while the game’s improved controls might annoy some purists who still cling to the heyday of ’98, we reckon most folks won’t bat an eye.

Compared to the last-gen base Kindle model (currently $119.99), the Paperwhite is a much better value. It features waterproofing, better battery life, faster page-turning speeds, and a higher pixel density screen that’s slightly larger (7 inches vs. 6 inches)

The water-resistant Sonos Play sits between the Roam 2 and Move 2, offering a balance of portability and room-filling sound. It delivers up to 24 hours of battery life, with a built-in loop for easy carrying. You can also use it as a power bank and pair it with other Sonos speakers over Bluetooth when Wi-Fi isn’t available. Read our review.

LIFX’s dimmable, color-changing bulbs offer Wi-Fi connectivity, simple setup, and Matter compatibility, so they fit seamlessly into any major smart home platform. Best of all, the smart features come at a price that makes buying multipacks more affordable than other brands.

Iniu’s P55-E2 Power Bank is an ultra-slim three-port USB charger that’s capable of fully recharging an iPhone 16 twice. It supports charging speeds of up to 45W via a USB-C port, which can deliver fast recharging for a range of gadgets.

The Echo Dot Max features a flat design and a redesigned sound system, which delivers nearly three times the bass as its predecessor. It also offers improved conversation detection and better noise filtering, thanks to Amazon’s new AZ3 chip. Read our review.

Donkey Kong Bananza is one of the Switch 2’s first exclusive must-have titles. Its destructible environment allows you to smash your way through each of its 17 levels, and the paths you make will remain intact unless you specifically choose to reset the world. Your companion Paulene’s singing allows you to transform into animals that have abilities that allow you to reach previously inaccessible parts of levels. Read our review.

Whether you primarily drive a car, motorcycle, or a bicycle, the one thing in common with all three modes of transport is the need for a well-inflated tire. This rechargeable 150psi inflator might just save you from a flat (or from being dangerously close to one) when you least expect it.

Google wants to sell you on the Pro’s software features, but it’s the underlying hardware that makes it a stellar Android handset. The build quality is incredible, the larger telephoto sensor is an improvement, and it now supports faster wired speeds and Qi2.2 for up to 25W wireless charging.

In Catan, up to four players strategically compete and trade for resources. The iconic board game ends when one player reaches 10 “victory points,” which is achieved by constructing a sprawling civilization of roads, settlements, and cities using sheep, ore, wheat, and wood.

A 10-inch anti-glare touchscreen that comes in a few design options. It’s a frame that features Alexa, and doubles as a smart speaker. It works with Amazon Photos, which offers unlimited storage for Prime members.

Glocusent’s clip-in reading light features five brightness levels and multiple color temperature settings, ranging from a soft, warm glow that’s great for late-night reading to a brighter light that’s perfect for daytime use. The slim USB light easily slips into your pocket, too, so you can read anywhere, from a cozy bed to a dimly lit train.

This innovative new smart lock uses the U1 ultra-wideband (UWB) chip in many iPhones and Apple Watches to automatically unlock your door as you approach. It will also work with the new Aliro standard and be compatible with Android phones for hands-free unlocking.

Few plants are as festive to have around the house during the holidays as poinsettia. Lego’s 608-piece recreation stands 8 inches tall, includes a “woven-basket” flowerpot, and — unlike the one you undoubtedly received as a gift in the past — requires absolutely no maintenance.

Roborock’s first roller-mop robot vacuum, the Curv 2 Flow, features flagship specs at a midrange price, including excellent mopping, a retractable mop cover, 20,000Pa suction, AI-powered obstacle avoidance, and a compact design.

Twelve South’s entry-level Bluetooth transmitter delivers up to 25 hours of continuous use and lets you connect up to two pairs of headphones to an in-flight entertainment system, making it a great travel companion no matter where you’re going.

The Chamera is not a good camera, but that’s not the point. Kodak’s charming, keychain-friendly tchotchke lets you snap lo-fi memories on a whim, with an ultra-small 1.6-megapixel sensor, a built-in LED flash, and the tiniest amount of storage.

Sucker Punch’s follow-up to Ghost of Tsushima is everything you could want in a sequel. The open-world revenge epic brims with fluid combat and a host of welcome narrative twists, all set against the gorgeous backdrop of Japan’s Edo period. It’s precisely the type of game photo modes were made for, even if you never use them.

The Wuben G5 flashlight is as small as a Zippo lighter, yet far more versatile, with a bright, rotatable light that can shine in numerous colors. It features USB-C charging and has an adjustable clip.

The last-gen JBL Go 4 has been around for a bit, but the pocket-sized Bluetooth speaker still delivers with up to seven hours of runtime, dual-device connectivity, and an IP67 rating for water and dust resistance. You can also pair it with other Go 4 speakers for bigger stereo sound, if desired.
