Chicago Med Officially Ends Its Big Season 12 Story At The Perfect Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Laura Hurley has been writing about the entertainment industry for more than twelve years, after a decade as a content producer and editor at CinemaBlend after writing for WhatCulture and Examiner. Attending events like SCAD TVfest and San Diego Comic-Con over the course of her career, she has been immersed in the world of network, streaming, and cable TV.

Warning: this article contains spoilers for Chicago Med season 12, episode 7, "Borders and Battle Lines."Chicago Med started season 12 with the ED in disarray, thanks to Lenox's absence and the demotion of Sharon Goodwin from her position as the executive director at Gaffney Med. S. Epatha Goodwin was still part of the cast in NBC's 2026 fall schedule thanks to the season's biggest ongoing storyline: Goodwin's fight to regain her job. Now, as Chicago Med quickly approaches its fall finale, One Chicago's medical drama wrapped up the story, and the timing couldn't be better.

Goodwin spent the majority of season 12 in scrubs and sneakers, working in Gaffney's Emergency Department as a nurse. She didn't go back to her old job just to keep working, however. She was determined to take down board member Miranda Lewis from inside. Goodwin couldn't get any dirt on Miranda for the first several episodes of her crusade to oust her from the hospital, but the discovery that Miranda was illegally prioritizing privately insured patience gave her what she needed.

In "Borders and Battle Lines," Goodwin had the support of Dr. Charles in the final scheme to restore balance to the hospital by getting rid of Miranda. They had to recruit help from some unlikely sources, and they had very little time to spare before Miranda got full control as CEO. The clock was ticking, but Chicago Med managed to finish the storyline before the final credits rolled.

Goodwin and Dr. Charles managed to keep their scheme to get hard proof of Miranda's sketchy policy so secret that even viewers didn't know what they were up to until the end. They recruited Frederick Lambert, the husband of Dr. Charles' late mother, to fake an ailment. He was disregarded by Miranda's algorithm until he produced his private insurance card, and then was immediately prioritized when it became clear that Gaffney could profit from him. Then, all they needed to do was talk Mallory into flipping on Miranda, and her recent crisis of conscience was enough to convince her.

Between the evidence from Lambert and the inside information from Mallory, Chicago Med finally delivered the inevitable cast exit. Miranda was fired in the middle of a board meeting, to the barely suppressed glee of Peter Kalmick, the hospital's long-suffering lawyer. She was walked out of the hospital by Gaffney's security rather than anybody from Chicago P.D., so it's not clear yet if she'll face legal repercussions for her insurance scheme, but Goodwin got the last laugh.

With Miranda fired and Mallory likely on the way out after how she gathered the evidence for Goodwin and Dr. Charles, the storyline that started in the tumultuous season 11 finale has ended.

While Goodwin undoubtedly would have been happy to oust Miranda much earlier than episode 7, the timing is actually perfect for how season 12 is going to shake out before the end of the year. Episode 8, which will be the fall finale, features the return of none other than Colin Donnell as Dr. Connor Rhodes, who has been absent from Chicago Med since his bloody exit in season 5.

The earliest reports about Donnell's return revealed that Goodwin would be responsible for recruiting Connor, and the twist wouldn't have been as satisfying if she had to do so while still dodging Miranda's oversight. The preview for the fall finale even shows that Goodwin came up with the idea to bring him back, and she's definitely still not wearing her scrubs in the footage.

Chicago Med revealed how Sharon Goodwin can stay on board at Gaffney despite an ultimatum, and it's already clear how long the twist will last.

Thanks to Miranda's storyline ending, Goodwin can be the one to bring Connor back without the board member getting in her way, and Chicago Med has one less arc to focus on in the fall finale. It would be a shame if any plot twists took time away from Connor after nearly a decade away, and episode 7 settled on the one that could have taken up the majority of the fall finale.

The only downside is that Chicago Med is now going on a break. NBC's final One Chicago episodes of 2026 will air Wednesday, December 9. In the meantime, fans can always revisit Connor's best moments with a Peacock subscription.

Chicago Med is a medical drama series that explores the high-stakes environment of a bustling Chicago hospital. Set against the backdrop of chaotic emergency room scenarios, the series follows a team of dedicated doctors and nurses as they confront challenging cases inspired by real-world issues, while navigating complex relationships.
