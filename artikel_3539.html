Musician Ryan Adams, 52, Is Engaged to Kate Farr, 22, After 6 Months of Dating

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Country music star Ryan Adams is reportedly engaged to fellow musician Kate Farr.

“I’ve never been happier in my life. Kate is the most fascinating, kindest nerd I’ve ever met. She’s my best friend, and I’ve never been happier. Here’s to love,” Adams told TMZ on Tuesday, November 17. The outlet noted that Adams, 52, and Farr, 22, got engaged after six months of dating.

Us has reached out to a representative for Adams for comment.

The outlet also reported that Adams, who is the ex-husband of Mandy Moore, and Farr, a writer and producer who now works in the music industry, met through mutual friends earlier this year. “While the two started out as friends, things quickly turned romantic,” the outlet stated.

A photo of two hands was also published by the outlet, seemingly of Farr’s atop Adams,’ with the top donning a diamond set against gold on its ring finger.

Less than one month again, Adams shared his adoration for Farr in the comments section of an Instagram post she shared. The October 21 carousel, which included three close-up snaps of Farr demurely gazing off camera, attracted Adams’ comment, “Just stopping by to tell you I love you so very much and I hear you rattling around upstairs. ❤️.”

Farr wrote in response, “WEIRDO!!! jk I love you 💛,” which drew a string of red heart emojis from Adams.

TMZ also reported that Farr “moved to Los Angeles to be with Ryan … and the two are now living together,” with plans to leave California one day to move “back to the Midwest … to be closer to family.”

Adams was married to Moore, 42, from March 2009. In January 2015, the former couple separated and their divorce was finalized in June the following year. In the aftermath of her marriage breakdown, Moore alleged in an interview with The New York Times that she was the victim of psychological abuse at the hands of Adams.

The article, which included seven women’s allegations of emotional abuse and sexual misconduct against Adams, including Moore, stated that she was at a pivotal point in her own musical career.

“Music was a point of control for him,” she was quoted as saying in the February 2019 article. “He would always tell me, ‘You’re not a real musician, because you don’t play an instrument.’”

Moore, who is now married to fellow musician Taylor Goldsmith and shares three children with him, also noted that she would once write songs with Adams, which he promised to record but never did.

“His controlling behavior essentially did block my ability to make new connections in the industry during a very pivotal and potentially lucrative time — my entire mid-to-late 20s,” Moore said at the time.

The outlet noted at the time that Adams, through his lawyer, referred to Moore’s depiction of their relationship to be “completely inconsistent with his view of the relationship,” adding that he supported her “well-deserved professional success.”

He issued a further statement to Us in the hours following the Times article going live.

“I am not a perfect man and I have made many mistakes. To anyone I have ever hurt, however unintentionally, I apologize deeply and unreservedly,” the statement to Us read at the time. “But the picture that this article paints is upsettingly inaccurate. Some of its details are misrepresented; some are exaggerated; some are outright false. I would never have inappropriate interactions with someone I thought was underage. Period. As someone who has always tried to spread joy through my music and my life, hearing that some people believe I caused them pain saddens me greatly. I am resolved to work to be the best man I can be. And I wish everyone compassion, understanding and healing.”
