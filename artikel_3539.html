Every Free Game You Can Keep on Steam, Epic, PlayStation Plus, and Xbox (Nov. 13-19)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Next week the world will stop for many players with the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6. If you've already preloaded GTA 6, then you'll be ready to hit "Play" as soon as it becomes available on Nov. 19. In the meantime, you have to find a game that'll keep you busy.

This week, there aren't a lot of giveaways, but there are a handful of games that you can try for free on console and PC. We're halfway through the month, and there are still a few active offers that you might've missed, though. So, be sure to claim the games that we featured in our previous article for week 1 and week 2 of November.

Whether you just want to unwind and play a few runs or want a game you can get completely sucked into, Knightica might just be what you're looking for. While you have the objective of conquering the kingdom, you can also pop in for a few runs at any time. It's the perfect game to play before GTA 6, as it doesn't require a strong emotional investment in a main story.

In this turn-based roguelike game, you have to ensure that unit synergy is at its best in order to create the strongest army possible. As you mix and match units and try different items and enchantments, you will become more familiar with each option and successfully progress to increasingly hard levels.

Game Pass members can play four different games this weekend. This is a great way to try out games that you're not certain you would like. If you find out that any of them are right for you, you can even consider picking them up later and continue where you left off.

Sumerian Six is a stealth, real-time strategy game that gives you the opportunity to command your own team of specialists against Nazi forces in World War II. As you progress and explore real world locations, you will uncover occult weapons, ancient tech and artifacts. You must perfect your strategy to ensure that you're able to defeat increasingly harder enemy groups.

Hypnospace Outlaw is a 90s internet simulator where you play as a virtual enforcer. Your job is to track down criminals and trolls that are set on disturbing the peace online. If you end up falling in love with the game, you can pick it up at 75% off this weekend.

Choose one of six classes (arcanist, bard, berserker, pyromancer, ranger, rogue) and make your journey into the Sunderlands with a friend beside you in Sunderfolk for turn-based couch co-op adventure. Here, you must build a strategy not only around your character's specialty abilities, but also with the notion that you never know who will strike first in battle, as there is no set order of play.

A unique first-person shooter where you play as a fully customizable teddy bear and try to defend your owner's bedroom from evil, nightmarish beasts that come out to play. You can improve your arsenal by finding new household items and building fun concoctions.

Steam has three free-to-try games available this weekend, and they come in all shapes and sizes. Whether you like co-op games, single-player city builders, or tactical action games, there's something for everybody. Be sure to check these games out soon, as they're only available until Nov. 16.

It's time to hit the mines with Deep Rock Galactic: Rogue Core. Become a guardian, falconer, retcon, spotter, or slicer, and work with your team to ensure the security of the mines. To protect them, you'll have to do a lot of shooting and killing, as they're infested with hostile aliens. This co-op game has roguelike elements, which means every time players go down there, they'll find different weapons and equipment, making every Rogue Core run different.

If you prefer a more realistic and tactical approach to combat rather than fast-paced chaos, then perhaps you'll want to try Company of Heroes 3 instead. In this tactical-strategy game, you can opt for a Full Tactical Pause, which grants you full control of the battlefield. There's an array of factions and units to choose from, and it is up to you to decide how you want to approach each battle.

If you enjoyed playing Flotsam on the Epic Games Store last week, you should also give Cities: Skylines 2 a shot. The graphics are more on the realistic side, and the infrastructure that you build can be as complex as you wish to make it. You can stick to making a small town for a laid-back cozy experience, or you can choose to build a megacity riddled with skyscrapers, complex public transportation systems, and much more.
