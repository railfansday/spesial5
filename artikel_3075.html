Apple tested a ‘complication column’ for the iPhone Duo Lock Screen, per leak

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Apple made a bunch of changes to iOS to optimize it for the iPhone Duo’s form factor, including revamping the Lock Screen. According to a new leak today, the company has tested another change, referred to as a “complication column.” Here’s what it looks like.

On the iPhone Duo’s Lock Screen right now, you can add widgets along the bottom of the interface. On the bottom right, you have two controls that default to the flashlight and camera. You can customize these and link them to other controls from Apple and third-party apps.

Cellular and Wi-Fi information is located along the top right-hand side of the iPhone Duo’s Lock Screen.

According to the spelunking account pdfu on X, Apple has tested a “complication column” that would appear along the right edge of the Lock Screen. The column has room for two widgets, filling the space between the flashlight/camera controls and the Wi-Fi and cellular status icons. These widgets are the same size as the widgets that appear along the bottom.

The “complication column” for iPhone Duo was first spotted in iOS 27.1 code, the account says. No code changes have been made since, so it’s unclear whether the feature is still in active development or Apple has scrapped it.
