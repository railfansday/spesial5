Nvidia Sales Double Under an Interim CEO: Rapid Earnings Reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Nvidia's third quarter set another sales record, yet the release raises fresh questions about leadership, customer payments, and how fast growth is really running.

Nvidia (NVDA -0.44%) reported third-quarter revenue of about $115.8 billion, more than double its total a year ago. Shares rose about 2% in after-hours trading. AI demand is still accelerating, but Nvidia is meeting it with an interim CEO, an open board investigation, and a growing pile of unpaid customer bills.

The biggest change isn't in the income statement. This is Nvidia's first report since Jensen Huang resigned as CEO on Nov. 12. CFO Colette Kress is now also interim CEO. On Nov. 8, the board opened an independent investigation into reports that Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. The release gives no update on it.

Growth is also tying up cash. Receivables rose about $18 billion in the quarter, nearly matching the revenue increase from the second quarter. Late-quarter shipments of a new product can cause that. If it persists, Nvidia is effectively financing its customers' buildouts.

Guidance is softer than it looks. Spread across 14 weeks instead of 13, it implies weekly revenue up only about 4% from the third quarter. Gross margin is guided lower too. The release doesn't say why, but new chips like Vera Rubin often cost more to build early on. The outlook also assumes no data center compute sales to China.

Watch whether Nvidia names a permanent CEO and what the board's investigation finds. Then check whether receivables keep growing as fast as revenue.
