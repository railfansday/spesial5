St Mirren vs Rangers: TV channel, live stream and kick-off time

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Rangers return to Premiership action tonight with a trip to Paisley to face St Mirren.

Derek McInnes’ side head to The Marbill Coaches Stadium looking to maintain their momentum and pick up another important three points, following their Premier Sports Cup semi-final win over Aberdeen last week

The match has been selected for live television coverage in the UK, with supporters able to watch the entire contest.

St Mirren host Rangers on Saturday, November 7.

The Premiership encounter kicks off at 8pm at The Marbill Coaches Stadium in Paisley.

Yes. The match will be shown live on Sky Sports Football and Sky Sports Main Event.

Coverage on Sky Sports Football is scheduled to begin at 7.30pm, half an hour before kick-off, with Sky Sports Main Event coverage starting at 7.45pm.

The match is also available to stream through NOW for supporters with the appropriate sports membership.

Match: St Mirren v Rangers Competition: Scottish Premiership Date: Saturday, November 7 Kick-off: 8pm Venue: The Marbill Coaches Stadium, Paisley TV: Sky Sports Football and Sky Sports Main Event Live stream: Sky Go and NOW
