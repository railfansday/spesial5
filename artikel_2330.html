Fantasy Bundesliga: Fixture Difficulty Planner Matchday 10-14

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Fixture Planner 1 – 2025/26 Season Table:

The first of our fixture difficulty planners is based on how the 2025-26 Bundesliga season ended. Red marks the sides who finished in the top three and are deemed the most difficult, with orange indicating teams who finished in 4-7th place. Midtable clubs who finished in 8-11th place are yellow, while sides from 12-15th are deemed an easier light green fixture. All three newly promoted teams are the optimal dark green shade.

Fixture Planner 2 – Current Season Table:

In our second table, fixture difficulty is categorised based on how the current 2026-27 Bundesliga table looks. The colour coding system remains the same 1st-3rd (red), 4th-7th (orange), 8-11th (yellow), 12-15th (light green), 16-18th (dark green). However, it’s worth noting that this is only based on the nine games played to date. This style of table becomes more effective the longer the season goes on when teams' true form is established.
