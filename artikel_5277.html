Bitcoin (BTC) price continues to fall as the sell-off from the $86,000 - $87,000 region in October is gathering momentum. The sell-off has accelerated in recent days, and BTC price has slipped to roughly $61,900, moving closer to the next technical support level at $60,000.

The daily chart shows several concerning technical developments for Bitcoin. Shorter-term moving averages continue to fall below longer-term moving averages, forming a technical pattern commonly known as a death cross. The death cross has now formed twice with 100 and 200 EMAs, indicating a worsening technical setup for Bitcoin, commonly known as a double death cross.

Several key moving averages continue to cluster in the $75,000 - $76,000 region, adding resistance for Bitcoin price action.

Relative Strength Index (RSI) has started to move closer to the oversold region, indicating that momentum has started to slow for Bitcoin.

Taken together, this technical setup indicates that selling pressure for Bitcoin has been increasing on multiple time frames. We are monitoring $60,000 as a key support level. This level was also an area of interest for buyers during the June correction in Bitcoin.

Should BTC price continue to move lower and break out below $60,000, the next level of support to watch will be $58,000, followed by $56,000.

For Bitcoin price to reverse course and show signs of a meaningful short-term rebound, it will need to continue higher and break out above $64,000 - $65,000.

Breaking out above $68,000 would be a strong move that could help to stabilize Bitcoin in the short term. A positive move for Bitcoin would need to continue higher and reclaim several key moving averages. Until then, Bitcoin will continue to trade in a more bearish technical setup.

Bitcoin price action continues to move lower on the daily chart. All technical indicators on this chart continue to deteriorate, indicating that Bitcoin remains vulnerable to another leg lower before buyers can show signs of a robust short-term recovery.
