Dimont & Associates, LLC: Hazard Claims Administrator (REMOTE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Claims Administrator – Hazard Claims (Remote)

DIMONT is seeking a Claims Administrator to join our fully remote claims operations team supporting hazard insurance claims for mortgage lenders, servicers, and investors nationwide. In this role, you will help keep claims moving efficiently by ensuring files are accurate, documentation is complete, and required information is gathered and communicated throughout the claims process.

This position is a great fit for someone with experience in hazard claims, property preservation, mortgage servicing, or related insurance operations who enjoys working in a structured, fast-paced environment where organization, accuracy, and follow-through make a direct impact.

Founded in 1996, DIMONT is a Dallas-based provider of insurance claims management and adjustment services supporting mortgage lenders, servicers, and investors across the United States. Since 2010, DIMONT has helped clients recover more than $2 billion in insurance claim proceeds and mitigate losses on mortgage collateral through technology-enabled services, borrower insurance claims management, and FHA, GSE, MI, VA, and USDA claims processing.

As a growing company, DIMONT offers employees the opportunity to make a meaningful impact while supporting critical services within the mortgage servicing industry. We are small enough that your work has real visibility, yet established enough to provide stability, flexibility, and opportunities to contribute to our continued success.

We take pride in delivering exceptional service to our clients and fostering a workplace where every team member is valued, respected, and supported.
