For some skiers, the day doesn’t end when the lifts stop spinning. It ends when the band plays its last song, the bar announces last call, or the kids finally crash after one too many rounds of hot chocolate and s’mores.

Whether your idea of après involves tossing back beers in ski boots, belting out karaoke with friends, catching some live music, or settling in for a family-friendly evening, a little fun after the final run is part of what makes a ski vacation memorable. And these 10 resorts give you plenty of ways to keep the good times going.
