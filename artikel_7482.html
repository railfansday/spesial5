Have you heard? Ethan and Hila’s divorce, Harvard’s creator summit, and Brandon Herrera’s defeat.

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Each week, we handpick a selection of stories to give you a snapshot of trends, updates, business moves, and more from around the creator industry.

This week, infidelity is bringing an end to a high-profile creator marriage, the Ivy League is hosting a new kind of summit, and we’re still waiting for the first creator in Congress.

Hila Klein files for divorce as Ethan’s affair becomes public. The Kleins were well known as co-hosts of a podcast and YouTube channel, but Ethan is being removed from his positions after his indiscretions came to light. And that’s not all — Hila wasted no time filing for divorce. She had already made that decision before the news became public, and she will seek joint custody of the couple’s kids while also restoring her maiden name.

Sour Patch Kids owner sues Ryan Trahan over candy brand Joyride. Consumer goods giant Mondelez is arguing that Trahan’s candies were designed to be confused with Sour Patch Kids, because they’re human-shaped and wrapped in colorful packaging. The actual case might be complicated, but this is yet another roadblock for a creator-led consumable product.

Alex Cooper heads to CAA after UTA exit. The Call Her Daddy host will look to turn over a new leaf as she brings a new talent firm into her corner. CAA is choosing to represent Cooper, her husband Matt Kaplan, and their media company Unwell, so the L.A.-based agency is making a big bet on a major Gen Z tastemaker.

Harvard University’s School of Public Health hosted a creator summit. More than a dozen creators were in attendance as Harvard looked to improve the quality of health information of social media. Platforms like TikTok are increasingly popular as sources of medical advice for Gen Z users, so the summit is an important step in the right direction as healthcare professionals look to counteract the spread of misinformation.

YouTube is bringing a Grand Theft Auto “creator collective” to London. On October 17, two days before the arrival of the much-anticipated next chapter in Rockstar Games’ open world franchise, YouTube will gather gamers and creators in West London for an event that will celebrate the launch of GTA VI. The game is expected to be the biggest gaming tentpole of the year, so it’s no surprise to see platforms pulling out all the stops to generate excitement.

Can Paris Creator Week become the Cannes of the creator economy? That question was on everyone’s mind as 8,000 attendees flocked to the French capital for two days of typical convention fare. All of the major social media players were represented, as were European content producers like Banijay. All of a sudden, France has become a major hotbed for creator events.

Firearms YouTuber Brandon Herrera lost his Congressional bid. Running for a House seat in a deep-red district, Herrera was favored to become the first Congressman whose career began on social media. But the blue wave that crashed down in Texas swept Herrera aside, as Democrat Katy Padilla Stout earned a comfortable victory that will send Herrera back to YouTube.

TikTok edits might not be the right choice for political ads. That was one takeaway from a recent study that examined the social media messaging of Kamala Harris’ 2024 presidential run. The researchers concluded that traditional ads work better on social media than posts that try to adopt those platforms’ stylistic conventions. Translation: Promote your campaign on TikTok, but keep it simple, stupid.

Jubilee follows up election stream with short-form push. The media company delivered its first election special on the night of the 2026 midterms, and days later, it announced its first two short-form originals. Dateable and People Spotting will help Jubilee break into new territory as it builds on its reputation as a wide-reaching content producer aimed at Gen Z.

The financials of Instagram’s move into long-form content are drawing questions. Creators want to buy into Instagram’s plan for 90-minute videos, but without a robust revenue stream attached, some are skeptical. “Exposure as a form of payment and with the intention of cutting into YouTube’s market share and cutting into the amount of views people are getting on YouTube — it’s just gross,” digital strategist and creator Gabriella Zutrau told Digiday. “Instagram has never valued creators.”

YouTube’s app redesign has proven to be perplexing. The new version of the YouTube app moves the Subscriptions tab to a menu that goes away once the user starts scrolling through their feed. YouTube redesigns are always unpopular at first, but the platform’s repeated minimizations of its famous subscriptions is definitely a curious choice.

Snapchat’s subscription offerings now have 30 million customers. As Snap Inc. revealed its earnings for the third quarter of 2026, it touted the continued success of products like Snapchat+ and Lens+. By offering premium features in exchange for a cost-effective monthly price, subscriptions have become a key part of Snap’s business model and monetization suite.

Why watch YouTube when you can build your own in two hours. That’s what Andrej Karpathy did with a little help from a fellow named Claude. The result of that effort is HackerTube, which contains thousands of videos related to the titular subject and shows what can be accomplished with AI-assisted coding.

Why is ‘Sacrifice’ the first Elton John music video with one billion YouTube views? ‘Sacrifice’ is a 1989 single that peaked at #18 on the charts, but it gave John his first entry in the Billion Views Club. You would think that ‘Tiny Dancer,’ ‘Your Song,’ or ‘Benny and the Jets’ would have broken through first, but it’s going to be a long, long time before those singles can match ‘Sacrifice’ on YouTube.

Humanoid robots put to work at a China supermarket are “slacking off.” Why bust your circuits when you can just quiet quit like all those humans who programmed you? The bots’ questionable work ethic is just smart — that’s why they call it artificial intelligence.
