Jimmy Kimmel trolls Melania Trump with a parody trailer for her new series

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

It's been less than a year since Amazon's Melania documentary came out, and we're already getting another one, with Prime Video set to release a two-part series this November.

"Melania is about to become the first First Lady with her own TV show while in office. Amazon, which as you know produced Melania's $75 million bribe-umentary, is using the leftovers from that to make a new two-part docu-series called Melania: The Series," explains Jimmy Kimmel in the clip above. "Wouldn't it be great if she spent a month living with Nathan Fielder?"

Kimmel goes on to unveil a parody trailer, which makes the motivations for said documentary abundantly clear. This isn't the first parody of the upcoming doc, either — The Daily Show released its own back in September.
