Open SoFi Checking and Savings if you’re paid by direct deposit and want checking and savings together with no account fees. Direct deposit of any amount raises its savings rate from 0.80% to 3.30%, and your first direct deposits can earn up to $400. If you only want the top savings rate or often deposit cash, look elsewhere.

Pibank pays 4.40% annual percentage yield (APY) with no deposit rules, more than SoFi’s 3.30% with direct deposit, so the rate alone won’t justify moving your paycheck. SoFi’s two welcome offers make a better case, with up to $400 for $5,000 in direct deposits within 25 days of your first and 1.10% added to the savings rate for up to 6 months.

Workers paid by direct deposit get the higher rate, early paychecks, and overdraft coverage without a monthly fee. Anyone who deposits cash often does better at Capital One 360 Checking, which takes cash free at Walgreens, CVS, and other stores, while a SoFi cash deposit at a store can cost up to $4.95.
