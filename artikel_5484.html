OpenAI's quarterly revenue crossed $10 billion, but so did its losses

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

OpenAI told investors its adjusted operating margin improved to -109% from -150% in the prior quarter, according to Bloomberg

OpenAI generated $10 billion in revenue in the three months ended in September, its first quarter at that level, according to Bloomberg. The ChatGPT maker’s operating loss for the period was $10.9 billion.

Revenue rose about 50% from the second quarter and was nearly three times the year-earlier level, according to Bloomberg. OpenAI shared the figures with investors on a call on Tuesday. President Greg Brockman and Chief Financial Officer Sarah Friar spoke on the call.

OpenAI's adjusted operating margin stood at -109% in the third quarter, a smaller loss than the -150% recorded in the second quarter, according to Bloomberg. The company also informed investors that its annualized revenue stood at roughly $50 billion at the end of September. OpenAI declined to comment.

Bloomberg attributed the revenue increase to demand for OpenAI's latest AI software. It said OpenAI has won back traction thanks to newer models, lower prices and a push toward AI agents built to take over routine tasks for customers.

Rival Anthropic posted revenue of roughly $17 billion for the third quarter, according to Bloomberg. Its growth from the second quarter slowed to about 47%, versus a jump of over 140% in the prior quarter. The two companies have historically used different methods to arrive at their revenue numbers.

The latest figures follow a second quarter in which Anthropic surpassed OpenAI in quarterly revenue for the first time, with $11.6 billion against OpenAI's $6.7 billion. OpenAI's annualized revenue of about $50 billion in late September also fell short of a $68 billion figure that had been widely cited. That higher number included gross revenue from partner companies.

OpenAI is finalizing a fresh funding round that values it at $1.25 trillion, after pushing its public-listing timeline back to next year, according to Bloomberg. OpenAI recently closed an initial $11 billion part of that round. Anthropic is aiming to be valued at $1.4 trillion when it lists publicly later this month.
