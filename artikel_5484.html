Eli Lilly is buying a rare blood cancer biotech for up to $1.5 billion

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The deal covers Calytrix's preclinical antibody for essential thrombocythemia and myelofibrosis, with milestone payments included

Eli Lilly $LLY agreed to acquire Calytrix Bio, a privately held developer of therapies for myeloproliferative neoplasms, for up to $1.5 billion in cash, the company said on Monday.

Under the definitive agreement, Calytrix shareholders could receive up to $1.5 billion, made up of an upfront payment and further payments that depend on clinical and regulatory milestones being met, the company said. Calytrix did not say how large the upfront payment will be or when it expects the transaction to close, according to Reuters.

Calytrix's lead asset is an investigational antibody for essential thrombocythemia (ET) and myelofibrosis (MF) driven by mutant calreticulin. The antibody is preclinical and targets both Type 1 and Type 2 mutant calreticulin, the company said. Current drug therapies for the two myeloproliferative neoplasms, or MPNs, primarily manage symptoms and complications, according to the company.

The acquisition broadens Lilly’s presence in the MPN field, the company said. Lilly’s hematology business already counts Jaypirca, a blood cancer drug cleared by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, among its products, according to Reuters.

"Calytrix was founded on the belief that patients with MPNs deserve therapies that do more than manage symptoms," said Brad Prosek, Calytrix's president and chief executive officer, in a statement. Prosek said the company looks forward to Lilly advancing the program through the clinic.

Lilly agreed in August to acquire Merida Biosciences for up to $2.875 billion, adding a developer of therapies that target disease-causing antibodies. The Calytrix transaction adds to a run of Lilly acquisitions as the Indianapolis-based drugmaker seeks to lessen its reliance on diabetes and weight-loss products, according to Reuters.

J.P. Morgan Life Sciences Private Capital founded Calytrix in 2025. The company launched with a Series A financing co-led by J.P. Morgan Life Sciences Private Capital and Westlake BioPartners, followed by a Series B financing led by Avoro Ventures.

Centerview Partners is exclusive financial adviser to Calytrix, and Goodwin Procter is its legal counsel. Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison is legal counsel to Lilly.
