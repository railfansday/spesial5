Trump Memecoin Dinner? Investors Wait for a Miracle

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The CryptoNews editorial team is composed of seasoned writers specializing in cryptocurrency and blockchain technology. Their expertise ensures comprehensive, accurate, and insightful content for...

Donald Trump is preparing another exclusive dinner for his biggest memecoin supporters, but the invitation is becoming cheaper as his token continues to collapse. The TRUMP token is down almost 99% from its peak, and this time, the presidential dinner contest closed with just 130 participating wallets holding $8.6 million, despite organizers offering 185 places.

The dinner will welcome the largest holders at Trump’s Virginia golf club on November 22. The top 29 receive an additional VIP reception, while other token holders are left watching from outside.

Just last year, wealthy buyers competed for access to Trump with millions of dollars in token purchases. This time, more than 5,000 eligible holders reportedly declined to register, while one successful entrant qualified with just $518 in purchases.

The upcoming celebration offers little reason to celebrate, as holders watched millions disappear from their holdings during the competition.

The shrinking competition exposes a problem that another presidential appearance cannot easily fix. TRUMP first memecoin dinner generated considerable excitement around the token, attracting buyers who wanted proximity to Trump and exposure to his political brand. That enthusiasm has since faded alongside the price.

Trump’s family has reportedly earned at least $635 million from the memecoin through April, according to financial disclosures and reported estimates. Meanwhile, outside investors have suffered substantial losses across several Trump-linked crypto ventures.

So, what happens to everyone who bought the token but never gets an invitation?

There is no comparable VIP experience for ordinary investors who purchased during the rally and are now sitting on losses. They cannot simply exchange their depreciated holdings for access to the president.

The frustration extends beyond TRUMP. MELANIA memecoin has also suffered a dramatic collapse from its launch-era highs, weakening confidence in the wider family-branded token ecosystem. Investors who bought into the political momentum now face a difficult question about what sustains demand once the initial excitement disappears.

World Liberty Financial is another broken painting. Its WLFI governance token has fallen towards $0.045, close to its all-time low of $0.0437. A substantial retreat from its September 2025 peak near $0.33.

Its USD1 stablecoin has also seen its market cap retreat from $4.4 billion in early October to around $2.9 billion today.

These are different products with different purposes, but their declining market valuations put pressure on the Trump-linked crypto narrative.

From bad comes to worse. Justin Sun, Tron founder, was among the most prominent early supporters of World Liberty Financial and attended Trump’s inaugural memecoin dinner. His WLFI holdings were frozen by the project, prompting him to file a lawsuit against the company just a few months ago.

The concern is no longer simply if all of Trump’s linked tokens can recover.

The market environment is hardly helping speculative assets. Iran war continues to disrupt commercial shipping and keep geopolitical risk in financial markets. Renewed attacks around Riyadh and reported tanker incidents in the Strait of Hormuz pushed oil prices higher on Friday.

Brent crude hovers at $92, while US West Texas Intermediate reached $83. Both benchmarks were nevertheless heading for another weekly decline as expectations of a global supply surplus offset renewed geopolitical concerns.

For us, the problem extends beyond oil prices. Energy disruptions can feed inflation concerns, complicate interest-rate expectations and undermine appetite for riskier investments.

Memecoins have little protection when speculative demand disappears. The political branding behind the TRUMP memecoin cannot guarantee buyers will return after the dinner, especially when holders are dealing with substantial losses.

Trump can still host another dinner and welcome his biggest memecoin supporters, but the event cannot answer the question facing thousands of ordinary holders.
