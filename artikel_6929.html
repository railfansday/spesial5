The Black Friday Apple Deals to Shop Now, According to Our Editors

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

The latest AirTags, MacBooks, and AirPods are up to 28 percent off.

Our editors look forward to rare discounts on Apple devices every Black Friday, and this year is no exception. Now that Amazon’s official sale is here, we’re seeing up to 28 percent off Apple’s most coveted tech.

The items that pose a serious sellout risk? AirTag tracking devices and the Apple Watch Series 12 are on sale for their best prices ever. Select AirPods headphones (like the AirPods Pro 3 and AirPods Max 2) are up to 28 percent off. That said, deals on iPads are less impressive, save for a $100 discount on the iPad Mini.

All to say, if you want to get a head start on holiday shopping, there are plenty of options available now. Ahead, see what we’re adding to our carts from Apple this Black Friday.

Lost luggage is a thing of the past with Apple’s ingenious AirTags, which give you peace of mind by tracking the location of your prized possessions. Snag one for a record-low price of $23 during Black Friday.

Apple’s AirPods Pro are worth the hype. Complete with features like Active Noise Cancellation and Transparency mode, a water-resistant design, and silicone ear tips for a customizable fit, they’re bound to become your daily headphones. Currently, they’re hovering $10 above their record-low price.

This 2026 Apple Watch model is on sale for its lowest price since release. Powered by the S11 chip, it boasts a 24-hour battery life and advanced health features like blood oxygen monitoring and hypertension alerts.

Apple’s MacBook Neo hasn’t gone on sale since it was released earlier this year. In fact, the price increased just a few months after, due to rising costs of RAM. It’s only a $50 discount, but it’s still, by far, the most affordable Apple computer money can buy.

Get $250 off the latest version of the MacBook Air. This 13-inch computer weighs just 2.7 pounds and boasts up to 18 hours of battery life, making it easy to tote to and from work, class, your local coffee shop—you name it.

Everyone deserves a pair of headphones that are as chic as they are functional. If you prefer an over-ear style, add this highly covetable pair to your cart, as they tend to sell out quickly when they’re on sale. We’ve seen prices as low as $399 during Prime Day, but this current deal is still impressive.

Whether you’re new to the world of wearables or need to upgrade an older device, the Apple Watch SE makes it easy to do so without breaking the bank. It just returned to a record-low price of $199, so it’s worth scooping up now.

The 2026 MacBook Pro, Apple’s most sophisticated laptop, is a worthy splurge. Updated with an ultra-quick M5 chip, this computer boasts enhanced graphics and a speedier performance than past models. Plus, it has an all-day battery (literally up to 24 hours).

Don’t let this tablet’s tiny size fool you: This is the perfect device for watching Netflix on the go, devouring your latest literary fiction read, or jotting down notes during flights. It’s $100 off right now, but it was on sale for a record-low price of $349 last Black Friday.

Currently, the standard iPad is $49 off. Though this is an entry-level option, it still has updated features like Touch ID, a 10-hour battery life, and an impressive 11.2-inch Liquid Retina display.

The perfect companion to the Apple iPad, the Pencil Pro allows for seamless note-taking, sketching, and coloring. Just like a real pencil, this updated model is equipped with angle and pressure sensitivity for precise lines.

If you haven’t jumped over to team wireless charging, now’s the time to do so. Not only does this MagSafe charger juice up your phone quickly, but it’s longer than your average charging cord. What’s more, it’s also compatible with your AirPods, letting it serve dual purposes on your nightstand.

This case turns your iPad into a laptop while also giving your investment some welcome protection. It comes complete with a smooth Apple keyboard and trackpad.
