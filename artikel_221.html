Apple is working on its own AI agent that can perform a range of tasks for you, including browsing the web, working across apps, writing code, and calling services. That’s according to a job listing spotted by Bloomberg, which says Apple is looking for an engineer to lead the creation of an agentic AI assistant.

An AI agent from Apple would put the iPhone maker in the running to compete with the likes of Meta’s Muse, OpenAI’s Dots, Anthropic’s Duet, and Google’s Tasks. Muse, ChatGPT, and Perplexity can already take control of a computer through their Mac apps, while Google just started letting Gemini do the same.

Apple began rolling out its AI-upgraded Siri in September, which you can use to ask questions about what’s on your screen, as well as perform actions across apps. It looks like Apple is planning to give Siri more agentic capabilities, as a report from Bloomberg suggests the company could add the ability to rotate and crop images in Photos, or organize Safari tabs. This lines up with the company’s new job listing, which says the engineer will work closely with teams across its Siri, UI Frameworks, and Apple Foundation Models divisions.

“People already do their most valuable work by moving fluidly between tools: a browser, a document, a terminal, an editor, a service,” the job listing says. “We’re building AI agents that can do the same… These agents plan and carry out multi-step tasks across apps, browsers, command lines, code, and APIs, and they can coordinate specialized sub-agents along the way.”

With its upcoming iOS 27.3 update, Apple is also giving developers the ability to integrate Siri AI into “all apps.” This is rolling out as part of a Developer Preview and will eventually allow Siri to perform tasks across a wider range of apps.
