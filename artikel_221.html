As for the company’s broader earnings release, Snap’s shares fell 12% after missing ad revenue and user growth estimates.

Snap reported 495 million daily active users for the quarter, up from 493 million in the previous quarter. However, the figure fell short of analysts’ expectations of 499 million.

“Daily engagement has historically been stable as a percentage of monthly engagement, but we do not intend to optimize Snapchat around daily use at the expense of long-term value for our community,” the investor letter said. “A longer-term horizon better aligns our reported metrics with the product and safety decisions that strengthen relationships, create lasting utility, and support broader, more durable audience growth.”

Spiegel said in the letter than while advertising revenue growth remains below the company’s target, the company plans to prioritize improving advertiser performance and expanding the ways businesses can reach its users.
