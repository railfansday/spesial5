I suggest that 2% withdrawal at age 50 is about right - especially if you are comfortable with your lifestyle.

You might be retired for 50 years.

At just 3% inflation, the cost of everything doubles in just 24 years (the years go by fast).

As we age, a lot of our costs exceed inflation, and inflation could be a lot higher than 3% going forward.

We have been retired for more than 20 years, and the costs of just a modest retirement have increased significantly.

We have no need or desire to live more lavishly, and we continue to invest several times as much as we spend each year.

We hope to remain in our modest home right up until the end, knowing we can afford whatever it costs to stay here.

At the same time, we derive great satisfaction knowing we could afford the absolute best continuing care, if we ever need it.
