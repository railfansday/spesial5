florim stone broadens design possibilities with full-body technology and expressive material finishes

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

florim stone introduces DeepSoul technology to embed color and texture throughout selected porcelain slabs for uninterrupted edges, alongside tactile stone and marble finishes across the wider collection.

mariana rodrigues November 23, 2026 4 min

Through its specialized brand FLORIM stone, Italian ceramic manufacturer Florim transforms porcelain stoneware into a versatile medium for countertops, custom furniture, and interior architecture. Leading the latest release is DeepSoul, a dry digital powder technology applied to selected surfaces such as Sandscape Beige and Sandscape Grey. Because this process builds color and texture throughout the entire thickness of the slab without surface glazes or inks, these specific designs maintain uninterrupted patterns and continuous edges even when cut, beveled, or carved. Alongside the DeepSoul slabs, additional stone and marble finishes expand the wider collection with tactile surface options for architects and designers.

With oversized 320×160 cm slabs available in 6, 12, and 20 mm thicknesses, FLORIM stone gives architects and furniture designers a single modular system adaptable to residential, hospitality, retail, and commercial interiors. Combining structural strength with subtle studies of light, color, and texture, the surfaces coordinate naturally with Florim’s broader architectural ranges.

The FLORIM stone collection is organized around marble, concrete, stone, and essential black and white tones, all brought to life through three distinct surface finishes. Matte Leather combines a textured, anti-reflective appearance with a soft, natural stone feel. Matte Silk uses fine mineral granules to create a smooth, honed surface, while Glossy introduces a mirror-like finish that deepens color saturation and highlights dramatic veining.

Among the latest surfaces from FLORIM stone, Sandscape Beige and Sandscape Grey rely on DeepSoul technology to echo the subtle layers of sedimentary stone, combining neutral tones with a 6 or 12 mm Matte Leather finish. Expanding the stone palette, Travertine Nut reproduces the warm, linear grain of Turkish Walnut travertine in a Matte Silk finish across 6, 12, and 20 mm thicknesses. Adapted from the Rinascenza collection, Arena Conchiglia turns compact sandstone into a light-reactive 12 mm slab in Matte Leather. For darker monochrome interiors, Solid Black features a custom-formulated body designed for intense color depth and tactile countertops in a 12 mm Matte Leather finish.

Alongside these natural stone textures, FLORIM stone introduces marble and breccia surfaces inspired by classic European quarries. Apuano Oro brings the golden and grey veining of Apuan Macchia Vecchia marble to kitchen countertops and surfaces in 6 and 12 mm Matte Silk and Glossy finishes. For stronger color accents, Baroque Red channels Greek Rosso Lepanto marble through saturated red-violet and pink-violet tones in a 12 mm Glossy or Matte Leather slab. Dusty Rose translates Apuan Fior di Pesco marble into gentle light pink and grey hues in 12 mm Glossy and Matte Leather finishes. Rounding out the range, Bronze Fantasy recreates the warm, multicolored mineral fragments of Verona’s Breccia Pernice in a 12 mm Matte Silk or Matte Leather surface.
