New York hosts spy lookalike contest inspired by Nvidia CEO controversy

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A “Chinese spy” lookalike contest took over New York City’s Washington Square Park, with participants performing mock espionage acts for a cash prize.

The competition took place on November 21 and was organized by FactMachine and Prez, drawing a crowd of spectators who were given the opportunity to vote for their favorite contestants.

The event was inspired by the ongoing controversy surrounding Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang, who had resigned as CEO just over a week before the competition took place.

Around 10 women participated in the competition, dressing up as spies and performing various espionage-inspired acts in front of the crowd.

Some contestants pretended to pull classified documents from their pockets, while another person dressed up as Jensen Huang joined in with the performances.

Spectators were able to decide the winner by scanning QR codes displayed at the venue and voting for their favorite participant, with a $50 cash prize up for grabs.

A post shared by Keri | NYC Content Creator (@keri_nyc)

The contest came after Nvidia launched an independent investigation on November 8 into reports that its CEO had been targeted by a foreign intelligence operation. Huang also admitted to being unfaithful to his wife in a message sent to employees.

On November 12, he resigned as president, CEO, and a member of Nvidia’s board of directors, ending his 33-year tenure leading the company. Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress was appointed interim CEO.

However, just two days after the lookalike contest, Nvidia announced that Huang had been reinstated following a unanimous vote by its board of directors.

The company said it had consulted senior US government officials and was confident that his return posed no national security risk. Its independent investigation remains ongoing, with Huang committing to cooperate.
