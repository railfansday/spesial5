How to compete in the $100,000 November Fortnite PlayStation Cup

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Fortnite’s PlayStation Cup is back in November, giving PlayStation players a chance to compete for a share of $100,000 in cash prizes.

The cup qualifiers start on November 23, with separate competitions for Battle Royale and Zero Build. Both are Solo events, so you can jump in without having to find a duo partner or put a squad together.

Here’s how to enter the Fortnite PlayStation Cup on your PlayStation, when each round takes place, and what you can win.

Before the Cup starts, make sure your PlayStation and Epic Games accounts are linked and that you’ve enabled two-factor authentication. Your Epic account needs to be in good standing and at least level 50, which you can check in the Career tab. You’ll also need to be at least 13 and meet the eligibility requirements for your country.

The Cup starts on Monday, November 23, with qualifiers for both Battle Royale and Zero Build. Top 80 players who advance in Zero Build will compete in the finals on Tuesday, November 24, while the Battle Royale finals take place on Wednesday, November 25.

This gives you just one day to book a spot in the finals, so you’ll have to rack up those wins as quickly as possible.

Each mode has $50,000 in prize money split across seven regions. In Europe, the top 50 finishers in each final earn cash, with $4,000 going to first place. North America Central also pays out to the top 50, with $2,500 for the winner.

In North America West, Brazil, the Middle East, Oceania, and Asia, the top 25 players in each final earn a cash prize, with $750 for first place.
