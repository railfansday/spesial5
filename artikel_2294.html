"We had to adapt" – Klopp breaks down tactical approach to stun the Netherlands

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Putting the Netherlands to the sword 5-0 in Berlin this evening, Jürgen Klopp was bowled over by his team's ruthless performance which saw them top Nations League Group A2

“It was really extraordinary. The match was exceptionally difficult. Against a really good opponent, you have to find a way,” the DFB head coach said in an interview with ARD.

"We had to adapt, had to change a few things — actually, some big things — and we made those changes, took a lot away from them and then scored some unbelievable goals.

“It was madness. A great football match and a fantastic result. To be able to do that here in Berlin, in the last international match of the year, I wouldn't have dared to wish for that,” he said.

Regarding the national team's tactical approach against the Netherlands this evening, the 59-year-old said his side had to tweak some tactics midway through the game.

"In all the other matches, we were incredibly dominant. Today was intense in a completely different way. Both teams wanted to press high, and neither team did the other any favours.

"Instead, they were playing the ball over each other's heads.We tried to keep things simple. Whenever we could play out quickly, that's what we wanted to do.

"Otherwise, there was no point driving ourselves crazy. I thought we could leave the perfect build-up play against high pressing for another time. We'll do that when we have two weeks.

"So we wanted to play towards Kai [Havertz] and compete for the second balls. When the ball was on the ground, they caused problems with Reijnders operating as a number ten.

"Meanwhile, Malen occupied both centre-backs. They made us run. You have to be braver in those situations. You have to allow a defender to step out."

“Either Jona [Tah] or Schlotterbeck had to step forward into midfield to defend. At that point, we started causing them problems,” Klopp explained with great detail.

Two of Germany's least experienced attackers, Derry Scherhant and Brajan Gruda, were both singled out for praise by Klopp. He felt the duo put in a remarkable shift all night.

"The work they put in, the work they had to do today, was brutal. That's what makes a team in the end, because other players can play football too.

"But it's about what you put into it in terms of commitment and passion. That's what matters in the end. These lads are still surprising themselves."

“Today we showed that we can also be devastating when attacking quickly. That's something we hadn't shown before,” Klopp concluded.
