Doncaster Rovers boss on quadruple injury boost ahead of Bradford City and Huddersfield Town derbies

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

McCann, whose fourth-from-bottom side headed into the international break on the back of an embarrassing 3-0 FA Cup exit at Kidderminster Harriers - seven days on from a similarly awful 6-1 reverse at MK Dons at league level—reports some much-needed positive news on the squad front.

Experienced duo Matty Pearson and James Collins are back in training.

The former has made just two appearances this term; the 33-year-old defender underwent double hernia surgery shortly after returning from a calf complaint which afflicted him early in the campaign.

Loan forward Collins, meanwhile, is yet to make his debut after joining at the end of the summer window until January from Championship outfit Lincoln City.

The 35-year-old has been recuperating from a long-term knee injury sustained last season, with Rovers conscious that it would take time for him to complete his recovery and return to full fitness.

Regarding team news ahead of Saturday's trip to Bradford, McCann said: "It's been actually really positive and beneficial.

"We've managed to have Matty Pearson, James Collins, James Husband, Tommi O'Reilly (back) not too far away. Whether they make the game on Saturday, we'll see.

"But having those four in and around the training ground over the last ten days has been really beneficial for us.

"We managed to get an in-house game last week on Saturday, which James Collins scored a hat-trick in, so it was good to see him back firing. But we're going to have to be a little bit careful with him.

"Of course, he's only just come back from a long-term injury but he looks really, really good.

"We want to have as much of the team available so that can have an issue and a problem to pick from. We've still got obviously Frankie (Okoronkwo) to come back and Will Keane and Harry Clifton to come back but we'll never use anything like that as an excuse.

"We're not going to use injuries as an excuse. We know that we have to improve in what we're doing and this last 10 days has given us an opportunity to do that. We're looking forward to the games coming up.

"There couldn't be any better games to be involved in. We're at Bradford and then have another Yorkshire derby on Thursday night at our stadium. There are tremendous games for us as a staff and the players to look forward to."
