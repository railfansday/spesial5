I hold my hands up that I was guilty of a bit of an oversight during The Mag Podcast last week.

“Those who do not learn from the past are doomed to repeat it” – George Santayana

I half remembered the quote above and threw it to my co-host Hutch on The Mag Pod to try and recite it verbatim, as he likes history and stuff like that.

There were two errors involved to be fair, me for putting him on the spot like that, and Hutch for being as thick as mince.

Jamie puts Hutch on the spot over Newcastle United’s relegation history

Anyway, I’ve since found the appropriate wording and replicated as a starting point here, as a way of conveying what we’ve been doing on The Mag Pod as we tried to fill the ongoing international break. Basically, there has been some chatter and paranoia that the issues this Newcastle United side are facing this season could well lead to a relegation battle. If it does turn out that way, it won’t be any of our first rodeos.

However, it’s fair to say that many of those Mike Ashley era struggles ended fairly benignly, with the drop avoided. On occasions this was comfortably, at times it was squeaky, but it didn’t really matter as long as you lived to fight another day. Unfortunately, there were exceptions.

So, to try and learn the lessons of history, we have used this round of podcasts to look at the Mike Ashley seasons where it went terribly wrong.

Late last week the first instalment (Episode 16) dropped, as we took a walk through the chaotic 2008-09 season. The follow-up, reliving the relegation of 2015-16 (Episode 17), which is out now.

These serve as both a wander through times gone by and a study in what we can learn.

Ideally, listeners may feel better about current circumstances in comparison with the poor decisions, awful form and careless business that led to highly avoidable seasons away from the Premier League under the previous regime.

Unfortunately there may be times when certain things ring true and we appear in real danger of breaking Mr Santayana’s above rule.

There is a third instalment to come as well, but alas we are a bit wet behind the lugs to fully recount the demotion of 1988-89.

Instead, a slight change of tack is incoming where Thursday’s edition (Episode 18) will see a review of the 2020-21 season, the final year before the Newcastle United takeover. This is to give a perspective on how far we have come, the impact of the considerable changes since and to hopefully make you all feel better you’re no longer looking at Steve Bruce.

Spoiler alert, but the general overall conclusion of these editions is that more time and consideration is needed in the current circumstances, to really have a full and proper idea of how badly it may compare to some of our darker times. However, there are things in there that may very well resonate.
