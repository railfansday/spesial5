Barry Bannan & Josh Windass react to major Di’Shon Bernard, Sheffield Wednesday moment v Plymouth Argyle

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Josh Windass and Barry Bannan have reacted on social media to Di'Shon Bernard scoring a stunner to make the points safe during a 3-0 Sheffield Wednesday win against Plymouth Argyle on Saturday.

Dave Challinor lost to Oldham Athletic in the FA Cup during his first game in charge of Sheffield Wednesday, while a young Wednesday team also lost 4-0 in the EFL Trophy to Accrington Stanley.

The Plymouth Argyle game, therefore, was his first in charge in the league. Promotion from League One has to be a priority above all else this season, with Billy Mitchell, Davis Keillor-Dunn, and Di'Shon Bernard winning Challinor's team the game against Tom Cleverley's side.

It was not the best of starts from Wednesday, with Plymouth having the better of the opening exchanges prior to Mitchell's goal on the stroke of half-time from a corner.

Cleverley and co. were not able to fight their way back into the contest, with Keillor-Dunn's goal on 80 minutes making three points at Hillsborough more likely before Bernard strode forwards minutes later to fire into the bottom corner from range.

Multiple knee issues and surgeries had kept Bernard out for much of the last couple of seasons, with the defender making just six appearances since February 2025. All of those games have come this season, too.

The 26-year-old has started four of the last seven league games now, but scored his first since a 2-2 draw with Bristol City in January 2025 here, which saw Barry Bannan and Josh Windass send messages to Bernard on social media.

Speaking via his Instagram story, former Sheffield Wednesday man Windass said: "Man like @dishonbernard 😂🙌🏻🙌🏻"

Bannan, who wore the captain's armband for much of the match, also posted to his Instagram story, stating: "What a guy 💙💙"

There was a time when Windass and Bannan were quite the double act, with the pair absolutely central to everything Sheffield Wednesday did, becoming two of the most important figures in the club's modern history.

Both played enormous roles in getting the Owls out of League One, providing the quality, experience, and leadership needed to navigate a difficult period and eventually return to the Championship.

They then helped keep Wednesday competitive at that level, often carrying a side that lacked the resources and consistency to properly establish itself. Bannan was the heartbeat of the midfield, while Windass provided goals, moments of quality, and genuine match-winning ability.

Their importance to Wednesday should never be underestimated. The latest posts sent to Bernard from that duo will have many feeling nostalgic about the last team to be promoted, but they will hope Challinor can replicate Darren Moore's team by gaining promotion one way or another.

Bernard's return to fitness could be hugely important for Sheffield Wednesday, particularly given the quality he can bring to their defensive options. When fit and firing, Bernard should be among the best central defenders in English football's third tier.

He possesses the physical attributes, composure on the ball, and also plenty of experience in League One and the Championship to make a real difference at this level. An injury-free run could therefore be pivotal to Wednesday's hopes of building some consistency during the remainder of the campaign.
