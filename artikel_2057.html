Watch: Milan deliver dreadful display in latest European loss to Ferencvaros – the highlights

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

AC Milan had another night to forget in the Europa League on Thursday as they were beaten 2-0 by high-flying Ferencvaros.

Milan needed to bounce back having lose two of their first three games in the competition, and despite being buoyed by Saturday’s derby win over Inter, they managed to deliver their worst performance of the season.
