Tajik National Convicted of Providing Material Support to ISIS and Firearms Offenses

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

BROOKLYN, NY –  A federal jury in Brooklyn today convicted Mansuri Manuchekhri, also known as “Mansuri Manchekhri” and “Manysher,” on all four counts of an indictment charging him with conspiring to provide material support to the Islamic State of Iraq and al-Sham (ISIS) and the Islamic State-Khorasan Province (ISIS-K), conspiring to possess and possessing firearms and ammunition while unlawfully in the United States, and making false statements to federal agents.  The verdict was returned following a two-week trial before United States District Judge Hector Gonzalez.  Previously, on October 30, 2026, Manuchekhri pleaded guilty to immigration fraud relating to a sham marriage between him and a U.S. citizen.When sentenced on all those crimes, the defendant faces up to 55 years in prison.
