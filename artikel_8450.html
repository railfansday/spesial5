You can travel the world for free if you’ll trade something other than cash for it, whether that’s your time and skills, your home or schedule, or spending you already do. Before you sign up, count what each trade asks of you in hours, fees, and months, and pick the ones that fit your life.

The Peace Corps offers the biggest version of that trade.

It pays volunteers’ flights to and from their country of service, provides free housing, health care, and a monthly allowance that covers a modest lifestyle, and asks for 27 months in return, training included.

The other 14 ways ask for less time than the Peace Corps, and most cover less. Their price comes as hours of work, membership fees, flexibility with your dates, or spending you’d do anyway, so choose by which of those you have to spare.

Most of these trades pay for a place to sleep or a way to get there, not both. Pair a house-sit or home swap for the bed with card points or a voucher from giving up your seat for the flight.
