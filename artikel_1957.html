Who can England face in Nations League draw? Dream and nightmare scenario revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

England will learn their opponents for the Nations League quarter-finals on Friday morning.

The Three Lions beat Spain in dramatic circumstances in Madrid to top their group last week, and can now avoid the likes of France, Germany and Portugal in the quarter-finals as a result.

But the draw might still throw up some tricky fixtures as England look to improve on their best-ever finish in this competition, which came when they crossed the line in third in 2019.

Here is all you need to know about their potential opponents...

England are in Pot 1 for the Nations League draw, along with France, Germany and Portugal.

The teams in Pot 2 are Belgium, Spain, the Netherlands and Norway.

The Nations League draw will pit each of the four teams in Pot 1 against one of those in Pot 2, for a two-legged quarter-final.

There is one provision, though: teams from the same group cannot be drawn against each other, meaning England cannot face Spain at this stage of the competition. They could, however, come across them in the semi-finals or final.

As such, England could draw Belgium, the Netherlands, or Norway in the quarter-final.

Semi-final matchups will be determined by another draw following the quarter-finals.

England’s dream opponent at this stage of the Nations League is Norway.

FIFA sees Martin Odegaard and Erling Haaland’s side as comfortably the lowest-ranked of England’s three potential opponents.

That is in spite of their impressive World Cup campaign - where they were knocked out in the quarter-finals by England - and the fact they turned over both Denmark and Wales in the November international break.

England’s nightmare draw, meanwhile, would see them pitted against Belgium.

It is tough to say whether Belgium or the Netherlands would be the tougher opponent at this stage - FIFA ranks them at eighth and ninth in the world, respectively - but the Red Devils just pip their neighbours.

They finished second in their group, just a single point off winners France. A pair of one-sided victories over Turkiye and Italy got them to this stage.

The quarter-final first-legs are scheduled for March 25-27, with the return legs scheduled for March 28-30.

The semi-finals and final will be played in June 2027.
