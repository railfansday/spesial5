Ex-Celtic hero reveals impact of ‘all night’ gaming on former teammate as GTA VI launch comes with warning

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Former Celtic winger Aiden McGeady has revealed that a former Parkhead teammate became so addicted to computer games that he was rated as one of the world’s highest-ranked players.

McGeady’s comments arrive on the back of the highly anticipated launch of Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA VI), which was released on November 19 and is expected to be the best-selling action-adventure video game of all time, having already shattered launch records.

Generating an estimated $1.65 billion across over 17 million pre-order copies before its release, GTA VI is the first release in the franchise from Rockstar Games, which has a flagship development studio based in Edinburgh, in 13 years, and has several celebrity fans that include footballers Erling Haaland, Declan Rice and Thibaut Courtois.

"Tommy Gravesen would play Call of Duty when I was at Celtic,” said McGeady. “We had a group, so we'd go home from training and play it. Tommy would never give us his gamertag. I don't know why, but I don't think he wanted us playing with him. But apparently he was like third or fourth in the world at Call of Duty on team deathmatch.

"Somebody managed to get his gamertag and added him and he had 45 days of playing time. 45 days of playing time! You look down the list of your friends and the next one after him was like two days and Tommy had 45 days. So we were like, Tommy must have been going home and just sitting there all day and all night. So yeah, I mean it is addictive isn't it? I have seen it in football before, lads staying up until like three or four in the morning before a game to play Call of Duty and stuff like that. Not ideal, but what are you going to do?"

However, despite the popularity of GTA VI, the 40-year-old doesn’t expect any current professionals to follow in the footsteps Celtic, Everton and Real Madrid. McGeady admits he has seen several players take portable games consoles on the team coach when headed to away games, and the ex-Celtic and Hibs man has warned the younger generation to not follow Gravesen’s example.

“I don't think GTA VI would be a problem because anybody that brings a portable PlayStation or a portable Xbox, they're obsessed with it anyway, aren't they?” McGeady said, speaking to BOYLE Sports. I've been on buses before and it's like lads will get the PlayStations out and it's like FIFA or Call of Duty or it's Fortnite, whatever it is. Lads do that anyway, they're obsessed with it anyway.

"So I don't think GTA VI is going to create a new breed of a new market of players who are just going to start playing PlayStation. I think the people that already play it will play it anyway. I think most footballers are fairly responsible and most Premier League footballers are fairly responsible. I don't think it's going to take over their life. Maybe for the younger players it could be an issue because they can be addicted to these games.”
