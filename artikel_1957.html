Jashari, Athekame, Camarda and Bartesaghi all start on international duty – their numbers

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

There were more AC Milan players in action for their national team on Monday night, with largely positive results.

Switzerland celebrated qualifying for Group A of next season’s Nations League with a resounding 4-0 victory over Scotland. Ardon Jashari and Zachary Athekame both started the game in Murat Yakin’s 3-4-2-1 system, with the former in the double pivot and the latter at right wing-back.

Jashari played the full 90 minutes and put in a solid performance. The midfielder ensured precision and order in possession, finishing the match with a 95% pass accuracy rate: 74 completed out of 78 attempted passes, plus four successful long balls out of six attempted.

Athekame remained on the pitch until the 86th minute. He recorded 42 of 44 passes completed – equal to 95% accuracy – plus four crosses, and four defensive blocks. The wing-back role gave him license to get forward, amid rumours he could return to Milan early.

Meanwhile, Italy U21s were in action against Belgium. After their 2-1 victory over France in Laval, Silvio Baldini’s side made five changes to their starting line-up, but the coach still fielded Davide Bartesaghi and Francesco Camarda from the start in a 2-2 draw.

Camarda had one shot which was classed as a ‘big chance missed’ by Sofascore. He completed 10/12 passes and one was a key pass, while he had 19 touches overall and lost possession 3 times, drawing 2 fouls. The striker also chipped in with 2 clearances, 1 recovery, 2 ground duels won (out of 2) and 1 aerial duel won (out of one).

Bartesaghi meanwhile completed 2 of 7 crosses, 36/43 passes, 4/7 long balls, had 70 touches, 2 successful dribbles, lost possession 17 times, had 2 interceptions, 3 clearances, 7 recoveries, 1 blocked shot, 5 ground duels won (out of 9) and 1 aerial duel won.
