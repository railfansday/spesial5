McCurtain County Resident Pleads Guilty To 2022 New Year's Eve Shooting

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

MUSKOGEE, OKLAHOMA – The United States Attorney’s Office for the Eastern District of Oklahoma announced that Kendel Reese Wolfe, age 19, of Broken Bow, Oklahoma, entered a guilty plea to five counts of Assault with a Dangerous Weapon with Intent to do Bodily Harm in Indian Country, each punishable by up to ten years in prison and a $250,000 fine, and two counts of Causing the Death of a Person in the Course of a Violation of Title 18 U.S.C. Section 924(c), punishable by any term of years up to Life in prison, and a $250,000 fine.At the plea hearing, Wolfe admitted that on December 31, 2022, he fired an assault rifle into an occupied truck as it exited a business in Idabel.  Wolfe’s shooting injured one and killed two of the seven people who were passengers in the truck. The shooting constituted an assault with a dangerous weapon intending to do bodily harm of five of the victims and causing the death of two of the victims while using a firearm in the commission of a crime of violence.
