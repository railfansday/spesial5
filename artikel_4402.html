2028 Honda CR-V Hybrid

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The CR-V has been a fixture in the Honda lineup for 30 years, and for the 2028 model year, it’s entering its 7th generation with a front-to-back redesign and a hybrid powertrain on all models. Few details have been shared so far, but the 2028 CR-V will feature the company’s new Honda Hybrid powertrain.

Honda says the 2028 CR-V will arrive in 2027 wearing all-new sheetmetal. A thoroughly revamped cabin is also likely. As for the decision to go all-hybrid this time around, Honda says that currently over half of all CR-Vs sold are hybrid—which is the powertrain we’ve been recommending for some time.

Expert Tip: The new CR-V’s platform and hybrid powertrain are expected to be shared with the new Acura RDX, which will serve as the luxury alternative to Honda’s mainstream version.

The CR-V Hybrid enters a new generation for the 2028 model year. Everything is new from tip to tail.
