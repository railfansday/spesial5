OR Royalties Inc. (OR -2.16%) reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026 and confirmed its annual guidance for gold equivalent production. Management stated that three development projects commenced production during the year, increasing the total count of producing assets to 25. The company reported revenue growth driven by higher precious metal prices and indicated that the business model maintained a cash margin of 96.8%. Strategic developments during the period included the expansion of the revolving credit facility and the completion of a share repurchase program. Management also detailed a long-term growth trajectory that projects a 50% increase in gold equivalent production by 2030 through the development of fully funded assets.
