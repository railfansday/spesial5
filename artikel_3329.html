Perez Hilton’s Family Says Blogger Is Making ‘Encouraging Progress’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Months after Perez Hilton was hospitalized after briefly livestreaming acts of self-harm on TikTok, the blogger’s family says Hilton is slowly making the next steps of his recovery and has made “encouraging progress.”

In an update Wednesday, Hilton’s family announced that the celebrity commentator finished a partial hospitalization program in October and has moved back in with family. Hilton will continue an intensive outpatient program that includes mental health support and therapy, but his family says he is moving “gradually” toward a full-return to his life.

“Over the past few weeks, Perez has enjoyed several successful weekend visits with his mom and children. Being reunited with his loved ones has meant so much to everyone, especially after these past few difficult months,” the statement reads. “With Thanksgiving approaching, we’re looking forward to spending some much-needed quality time together as a family and enjoying a little peace and quiet during the holiday.”

Hilton was hospitalized on Aug. 4 following a TikTok livestream from his Miami home where he engaged in acts of self-harm. (TikTok disabled the livestream but not before the videos spread across social media.) Representatives for the Miami-Dade Sheriff’s Office confirmed to Rolling Stone at the time that a fire rescue team “began treatment on scene for multiple lacerations and an overdose” on an individual before hospitalizing him under Florida’s Baker Act, legislation that allows for mental health holds for people deemed in active crisis. His mother began taking care of his three children, who Hilton lived with in Miami at the time of the incident. During his time in inpatient treatment, Hilton’s family continually struck down what they called “false reports” and online rumours about the blogger’s health and mental status, urging fans to only trust the information from official family updates.

“Perez continues to make encouraging progress, and we’re taking things one day at a time,” his family added Wednesday. “If and when he’s ready, we look forward to Perez being able to share an update with you personally.”
