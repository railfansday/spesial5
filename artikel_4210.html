The Treasure Chest Game in the Zelda Ocarina of Time remake has Link forced to pick between a pair of chests five times in a row, with a heart container as the potential reward. If you pick incorrectly at any point you lose, but there is a way (two ways, in fact) to beat the system and get this substantial reward early in the Switch 2 OOT remake. The Treasure Chest Shop in OOT is located on the West Side of Hyrule Castle Town, at the spot shown above. However, it can only be accessed at Night.

If it's daytime and you don't want to wait, head North along the alley to the building with the blue door, which will have a bed you can rest in to change the time of day.

The Treasure Chest Game is simple: you'll be shown a pair of chests, one of which is right, and one of which is wrong. If you pick correctly, you move forward to the next round. If you pick wrong, you lose the game. If you can pass five rounds, you'll get a heart container. The problem is, the chests looks identical, and each attempt costs you 10 Rupees.
