14 Best Early Black Friday Bedding Deals: Tested & Reviewed by AD

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Your bed might be the hardest-working piece of furniture in your home, so why not give it a little extra attention? Black Friday doesn’t officially arrive until November 27, but you don't have to wait to score a deal on new bedding. From crisp cotton sheets to cozy comforters and decorative quilts, early holiday sales are already giving shoppers plenty of reasons to give their bedroom a refresh.

Maybe your current sheets have seen better days, or you just want to add a few stylish finishing touches to your sleep space. Brooklinen and Boll & Branch have breathable everyday essentials covered, while Nickey Kehoe brings a more playful approach to bedding with colorful patterns and unexpected designs. For something a little more opulent, Sferra and Rebecca Udall offer elegant options that make bedtime feel like a treat.

Ahead, we've rounded up six bedding brands with early Black Friday deals worth exploring. AD-reviewed favorites, statement designs, and plenty of cozy finds are all on the list, with prices that leave a little extra room in your holiday gift budget.

A comfortable bed is only as good as the layers you put on it, and Vesta has a few worth considering at a discount. Its PlumZen Goose Down Pillow is currently 20% off, bringing the price down to $119 from $149. Filled with Hungarian goose down and a vegan down core, the pillow uses a baffle-box construction that helps it maintain its fluffy shape while supporting your head and neck. Contributor Blake Bakkila, who tested the low-to-mid-loft version, described the experience as "sleeping on a cloud, if that cloud was well-supported and fluffy." She also appreciated that the pillow didn’t completely flatten overnight.

If you're after something to snuggle beneath, the Merino Wool Comforter is a top choice, currently 70% off on Shop at $229.50, down from $765. Its moisture-wicking wool fill is made to regulate temperature, while sewn-in chambers distribute the material evenly for consistent warmth without excessive bulk. For the less glamorous but equally important task of protecting your mattress, Vesta's Waterproof Mattress Protector is marked down 50% to $74.50 from $149. Made with a breathable bamboo-viscose surface and waterproof barrier, it's designed to protect against spills without the uncomfortable crinkling of traditional mattress covers. Consider this your chance to improve your entire bed's comfort for less.

Rebecca Udall's brand has caught our attention because of the bedding's delicate scalloped edges, ornate embroidery, and elegant designs that make sleep feel a little more luxurious. The Annabelle Scalloped Top Sheet in taupe is currently 61% off at $37, down from $95. Made in Portugal from crisp, breathable Egyptian cotton percale, it has a softly embroidered scalloped border that looks particularly lovely folded over your duvet. Its lightweight construction also makes it a welcome layer on warmer nights when a comforter might feel like too much.

For another dose of understated elegance, consider the Georgiana Ladderstitch Boudoir Pillowcase in sage, now 52% off at $30 from $62. Made from 600-thread-count Italian-woven Supima cotton, this petite accent features hand-embroidered ladderstitch detailing for an heirloom-worthy feel. At just under 12 by 16 inches, it's perfectly sized to sit in front of your everyday pillows. Pair the sage with Annabelle's soft taupe for a subtle color combination that looks thoughtfully collected.

A five-star hotel experience without leaving your bedroom? We’ll take it. If you've been dreaming of a high-end makeover for your bed, Sferra's current markdowns make that indulgence more attainable. The Italian heritage brand is known for its beautifully crafted linens, and the Costa Throw is a great place to start. Currently 30% off at $257, down from $367 on Shop, the lightweight, Italian-made cotton throw features a colorful herringbone weave and delicate fringe detailing. Senior shopping editor Nashia Baker has it and recommends draping it over your favorite reading chair or at the foot of your bed for an easy dose of texture.

If your taste leans more vintage, the Alice Coverlet is also 30% off, down to $286 from $408. Its soft cotton matelassé construction creates a raised floral-and-vine pattern, complete with scalloped edges for a subtly textured layer. And if you're after a little glamour, the Coriano Flat Sheet is currently 40% off at $213, down from $355. Made from smooth, long-staple cotton sateen, the crisp white sheet has an embroidered golden checked border that adds just the right amount of shine.

Whether your bedroom leans more romantic or eclectic, Nickey Kehoe has something for both design sensibilities. Better yet, the Los Angeles–based brand's Eden Quilt in midnight is currently 40% off, bringing its price down to $285 from $475. Inspired by traditional Indian block prints discovered during the founders' travels, the richly patterned quilt is reversible, so switching up your bedroom's look is as simple as flipping it over. Handmade in India from 100% cotton, its lightweight construction is breathable enough for warmer nights while supplying a bold, textured layer when temperatures dip.

The Hazel Ruffled Coverlet in natural is also tempting at 40% off, bringing its price down to $180 from $300. Made in Portugal from soft cotton, it has delicate floral detailing and a generous 19-inch ruffle that drapes elegantly over the sides of your bed. Its subdued neutral hue makes it equally charming on its own or layered with more colorful bedding. At these prices, a few new layers can go a long way.

Few things are better than climbing into a freshly made bed, especially when the sheets feel as good as they look. If your linen closet could use a little TLC, Brooklinen's Last Call sale is giving you a pretty good excuse to stock up, with select bedding marked down as much as 75%.

First up is the Classic Percale Core Sheet Set, currently 40% off at $107.40, down from $179. Made from 270-thread-count long-staple cotton, the sheets have a crisp, breathable feel that's especially attractive to hot sleepers who prefer something lighter than silky sateen. The set includes a fitted sheet, flat sheet, and pillowcases, with select striped and floral patterns that bring a little personality to your bedroom. Contributor Yelena Moroz Alpert, who's tested both the Classic Percale and its washed counterpart, notes that the latter has "a slightly brushed handfeel." Meanwhile, the original model offers a lighter, more matte finish.

The Sunwashed Stripe Sham Set is an easy way to switch up your bedding, especially at 75% off ($22.25 from $89). Made from cotton with muted, lived-in stripes and a crisp percale reverse, the shams bring a relaxed, coastal-inspired touch to your bed. Pair them with your everyday sheets for an effortlessly styled look. Just remember that these Last Call finds are final sale, so double-check your preferred colors and sizes before checking out.

Alpert also tested Boll & Branch's Waffle Bed Blanket, comparing it to "a tiny, cushy egg crate for your body," and now you can experience that same cozy texture for less. The organic cotton blanket is currently 25% off in deep plum, bringing its price down to $194.25 from $259. Made from 100% long-staple organic cotton, its airy, springy waffle weave provides breathable warmth without feeling overly heavy. The rich plum color also makes a lovely accent against neutral bedding.

For a more polished finishing touch, consider the Signature Hemmed Duvet Set in camel, currently 40% off at $209.40 from $349. Made from buttery-soft organic cotton sateen with a subtle sheen, the set includes a duvet cover and two matching shams. Its hidden zipper and interior corner ties help keep everything neatly in place, while the warm camel hue pairs beautifully with the deeper plum of the blanket. Together, the two create a thoughtfully-layered look that feels as inviting as it is stylish.
