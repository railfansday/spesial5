Sean Hagan revisits the Bitcoin-in-gold chart BMTV first ran in late September, when one Bitcoin bought about 19.7 ounces. Back then, he said to watch whether Bitcoin turned green on the year against gold before it did against the dollar. It did that by October 3, peaking at 20.9 ounces the next day. That breakout didn’t hold, but the higher-low trend did, and it has continued up to today.
