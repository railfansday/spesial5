SLING BAGS ARE here to stay, gentlemen, and to be honest, they’re a lighter and smarter way to commute with your EDC when backpacks and duffel bags feel like overkill. Worn across the body (unlike a fanny pack, which is designed to sit around the waist), sling bags are easy to carry, super practical, and able to transport all your daily essentials using a bunch of accessible compartments, zippers, and pockets. The best sling bags for men offer a range of styles for every kind of guy, whether you’re commuting, traveling, or heading into the outdoors. Some are minimal and sleek, while others favor the rugged and technical.

The best sling bags for men should offer features that cater to your needs. Typically smaller than a messenger bag, they can be worn across the body, over a shoulder, or even around the waist. The sling bags in our guide range from 1.5 liters of capacity to 30 liters.

When looking for one, consider the layout and accessibility of the compartments, pockets, and dedicated spaces for laptops, smartphones, tablets, or water bottles. All in all, there should be ample storage capacity (usually measured and listed in liters) that allows for smart organization and easy reach.

Since sling bags are often worn over one shoulder or across the body, the best sling bags for men need to have adjustable straps and padding to distribute weight evenly across your shoulders and back—a properly fitted sling bag should feel comfortable and not strain you even when worn for extended periods. Therefore, pay attention to the strap’s material, width, and adjustability.

A lightweight sling bag is usually better since it is easier to carry and feels better on your body by preventing unnecessary strain on your shoulders and back, especially if you plan to use the sling bag for hours on end, or carry it while hiking. Ultimately, the weight of a sling bag will depend on the bag's capacity, design, and intended uses.

To find the best sling bags for men, myself and Joseph Cheatham, led by our Senior Gear and Reviews Editor Ryan Brower, put them through real-life situations. We loaded them up with our usual gear like our phone, wallet, keys, snacks, even a water bottle to see how they handle the weight. The ones we tested have been worn at outdoor festivals, during day hikes, through long-haul flights, and of course, out and about running weekend errands. We paid close attention to the pockets and zippers, but really made sure to assess how comfortable the adjustable straps lie on our shoulders.

Many sling bags are actually designed to accommodate laptops or tablets. These sling bags usually fall on the larger, more functional side, with a large capacity and spacious interior compartment, as well as dedicated padded compartments or sleeves that offer protection and secure storage for your electronic devices.

Sling bags are versatile just like backpacks, hence, with the right style and design, they can work in a variety of places. A sleek, leather option looks professional enough for the office and goes well with your office fits. A rugged, weather-resistant one is great for outdoor activities—in fact, there are sling bags designed for purposes like hiking and traveling. For your casual outings, a small, stylish sling bag will work wonders.
