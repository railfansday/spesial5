The Best Resorts in Turks and Caicos: Readers’ Choice Awards 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Trends come and go, but here at Condé Nast Traveler, one thing never changes: your insatiable appetite for seeing the world. This year our readers cast an almighty 798,867 votes in our 39th Readers’ Choice Awards to tell us about the planet’s best travel experiences: You fell hard for palace hotels in Morocco, cruised down the Amazon, and got swept up in New York City’s exhilarating energy. Now, read on to find out where your favorites fall among the winners—and get inspired for where to go next, whether it's to a top-rated hotel or resort, or centered around a worthy cruise, spa, or island. These are the best hotels in the world as voted by you, our readers.

View all of the Readers’ Choice Awards winners for 2026 here, and the best resorts in the Caribbean here.

Turks and Caicos’s bustling yet beautiful island of Providenciales (affectionately dubbed “Provo”) has had its moment in the proverbial sun. Consider venturing southeast to South Caicos for a more intimate and undeveloped look at what are arguably the Caribbean’s most jaw-dropping beaches. The first property to put down literal roots with nearly 800 acres of primarily local vegetation is Sailrock South Caicos: a stunning, 40-unit property on the southeastern island’s northern tip where the Atlantic Ocean and Caicos Bank converge to house the area’s latest and greatest escape. Travel to South Caicos is also more convenient than ever with two direct flights from Miami on Wednesdays and Sundays via American Airlines. This means guests can now bypass a layover in Providenciales and reach paradise in less than two hours.

At the Ritz-Carlton Turks and Caicos, guests can sandwich sun-soaked days of lounging in a private cabana or on Grace Bay’s idyllic slice of beach with plenty of on-site experiences. A favorite for its white-sand beachfront and unrivaled centrality on famed Grace Bay, there’s no shortage of pursuits here—it’s a choose-your-own-adventure property with great amenities for young kids. 147 luxury ocean-view rooms and suites—with the Ritz-Carlton standard—awe and delight. There’s also a collection of luxury residences and penthouses for longer stays, each, of course, with stunning floor-to-ceiling views of Grace Bay’s turquoise waters. Interiors are all creams and whites with gentle pops of beachy colors, large windows, and pristine linens. As the most central property in Grace Bay, everything is within walking distance: the best restaurants, shops, and even a grocery store. For families with little ones, the Ritz Kids club is the place to be for guests ages 4–12. Drop the kids off and embark on the resort’s luxury sailing catamaran, the Lady Grace.

Basking on a coveted stretch of Grace Bay, this well-appointed resort is as lax as it is luxe. While there’s plenty of opportunities for R&R at the blissful on-site spa and for romance to blossom over candlelit dinners, the Sands at Grace Bay also know a thing or two about picking up speed. It wouldn’t be a proper Turks and Caicos vacation without hitting the water, and the resort provides complimentary access to a variety of water sport equipment ranging from sea kayaks to snorkeling gear to explore the mirror-clear seas. If you’re traveling with little ones who aren’t quite ready for the open ocean yet, there’s also kid-friendly pools and sprawling perfect for their next adventure. If you’re looking for a resort that grows up alongside your family, look no further.

Wymara Resort + Villas sits on the western curve of Grace Bay in Providenciales, just a 10-minute drive from the airport. It’s a place for travelers who like their luxury understated and favor the clean lines of minimalist design. Rooms and suites open to sea views and sun-washed balconies, while the villas—set apart from the main resort on a 15-acre peninsula—offer private pools, over-water decks, and personal hosts. The food is sustainably sourced: Indigo serves refined Caribbean dishes, and the Pink Bar is a magnet for sunset watchers. The spa, using island-grown botanicals and 111SKIN products, is intimate and calm. Service is polished yet relaxed, with staff who are friendly and will remember your preferences. Eco credentials are strong, from solar-powered electricity to refillable amenities. Complimentary wellness activities include yoga, pilates, HIIT, and sound baths, while evening entertainment leans local and cultural.

This mega-resort sets itself apart from other all-inclusive properties throughout the region with its family-friendly activities. There is a 45,000-square-foot waterpark and kids clubs for different ages, including one with Caribbean-ified Sesame Street characters, the Xbox Play Lounge, and a teen disco. For the rest of the family, there are 10 swimming pools, 21 restaurants, 15 bars (some of them swim-up), and access to the 12-mile Grace Bay Beach. The 758 rooms and suites are spread across five themed villages, with architecture that represents different regions: France, Italy, the Caribbean, and Key West. With 43 room categories, the resort truly offers something for everyone.

Grace Bay Club offers not one, but three distinct accommodations to its discerning guests: the adults-only Hotel building, the family-friendly Villas Suites, and the Estate, a resort within a resort, with exclusive access to dedicated amenities, such as a separate pool, cabanas, and more. Despite catering to different crowds, they do have a few common features, including spectacular oceanfront views and personal concierge service. Head to the oceanfront Infiniti Restaurant & Bar for bites of such fresh local fare as a warm South Caicos lobster salad, garnished with shaved carrots and crispy basil, or the Mahi Mahi carpaccio, with a kick of jalapeño, served to the sound of breaking waves. Looking to stake your umbrella in the sand a little while longer? Rent one of the homes in the Private Villa Collection, a cluster of five freestanding private beachfront residences that come with all the amenities and services of a superior hotel stay, including roundtrip airport transfers, daily housekeeping, and complimentary breakfast.

A fan-shaped trio of seven-story buildings curve around a beachfront pool and patio at this Grace Bay resort, close to Providenciales's shopping district. Enormous rooms in soft colors have marble baths and soaking tubs, proving that bigger can mean better. Staff members always serve with a smile and make a point of remembering names. The restaurant Seven dishes up Caribbean-inspired cuisine, with entrees like house-made stone crab ravioli, or you can opt for private beachfront dining, complete with a personal butler and a playlist of your choosing. If you're traveling with the family, the kids camp provides diversions such as lizard hunting and sand-castle building—or you can all go for a dip in the island's only heated saltwater pool.
