United Airlines Launches 9 New Routes From Major West Coast Hub: See Full List Now

The world's largest airline by fleet size, United Airlines is gearing up for a busy 2027, with the airline already announcing a total of nine new routes from San Francisco International Airport (SFO) that will take flight in the New Year.

United, a Star Alliance partner, is the largest operator at San Francisco and maintains the hub as a strategic port for its West Coast and Pacific operations. United will add two new international routes from San Francisco in 2027, along with seven new domestic links.

With all of these new routes beginning in 2027, the vast majority will fly between the US East and West Coasts. Beginning on March 28, a total of four domestic routes will take flight, connecting San Francisco to Hartford (BDL), Charleston (CHS), Oklahoma City (OKC), and Richmond (RIC).

All of these routes are set to be operated year-round. Hartford, Charleston, and Richmond will be operated with the airline's Boeing 737 MAX 8 aircraft, while services to Oklahoma City are operated by United Express on the Embraer E175.

United has expanded its domestic network from one of its busiest airports, primarily to capture growing demand and rebuild its domestic footprint to feed passengers through its West Coast hub.

March 28 is set to be a busy day for United route resumptions at San Francisco, with the airline also preparing to resume its nonstop flights to Tel Aviv (TLV). The airline historically served the route, due to heavy demand from tech and startup business at both ends. The airline had suspended the route due to ongoing conflict in the Middle East and Israel, and continued security assessments.

As the conditions have stabilized, this has permitted the airline to restore its long-haul corridor to Israel. The route will be served three times a week on the airline's Boeing 787-9.

San Francisco will become United's fourth direct route to Tel Aviv when it comes back online in March, complementing the airline's existing services from Chicago (ORD), Newark (EWR) and Washington Dulles (IAD).

Among the most notable additions are 14 gates and a new United Club lounge.

The final four new routes for United from San Francisco in 2027 include the airline's exciting seasonal service to Okinawa (OKA). This popular tourist destination in Japan becomes the airline's sixth Japanese gateway, after Haneda (HND), Narita (NRT), Nagoya (NGO), Sapporo (CTS) and Osaka (KIX). The route will operate seasonally with the Boeing 777-200ER.

Services to Jacksonville (JAX) and Traverse City (TVC) have been recently announced, with the service from San Francisco to Florida operating year-round from March 29, while services to the 'Cherry Capital of the World', in Michigan, beginning on June 19 and will run seasonally.

Flights from San Francisco to Norfolk (ORF) will begin on March 31, operating year-round on the 737 MAX 8.
