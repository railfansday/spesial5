‘Love Is Blind’ Reunion Trailer Spoils Cast Member’s Cheating

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Fans are calling out the trailer for including huge spoilers.

The Love Is Blind Season 11 reunion is incoming this weekend, and if you’ve been following spoilers, you know what to expect. Actually, even if you haven’t been following spoilers, you probably know what to expect thanks to the trailer revealing a cheating scandal.

The trailer—which airs directly after the final episode on Netflix—shows host Nick Lachey saying to someone off-camera, “I’ll ask you point blank: Did you cheat on your wife?” We also see Sarah crying on the couch as her friends and fellow contestants comfort her.

Considering just one couple got married during the Love Is Blind finale, the reunion trailer makes it abundantly clear that Sarah and John split up post-wedding and that he’s the one who potentially cheated. Though, at this point the news is public due to TMZ getting their hands on court documents and confirming that Sarah filed for divorce on June 4, 2026 (just over a year after their March 27, 2025, wedding) due to “an irretrievable breakdown” of the marriage. The outlet also confirmed that Sarah asked to split their finances and requested that a judge “bar John from restricting her freedom.”

As for the question of cheating, Reality Receipts podcaster Rikkii Wise (@storytimewithrikkii) dropped spoilers claiming that John had an “emotional affair” with an escort during the marriage. He hasn’t confirmed this report just yet (presumably because the cast has been asked not to speak out amid the reunion dropping), but stay tuned.
