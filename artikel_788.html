We Predict Ballet Makeup Will Be the Viral Trend This Holiday Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

While ballet flats and sneakers are having their moment, beauty brands are bringing the Nutcracker and Swan Lake mood to makeup.

This holiday season, it's all about ballet-inspired makeup. Think Nutcracker and Swan Lake, but make it beauty. So many brands have been launching their limited-edition holiday collections recently with products inspired by ballet and all its iconic motifs.

We're talking everything from Ariana Grande's r.e.m. Beauty and its en pointe collection to C-Beauty brand Flower Knows and its new snow ballet line. These products don't just use a ballet aesthetic, though. The looks depicted are ones we associate with the performance. They're full of soft shimmers, flushed cheeks, delicate blue eyeshadows and glass-like skin.

We don't think we're reaching here by predicting this will become a trend during the holiday season. This makeup look will probably last throughout the rest of winter, too. Ballet flats and sneakers are already trending; so why not extend it to beauty?

It'll make it the perfect gift for the makeup-obsessed friend in your life, too! So, whether you're shopping for them or for yourself, keep scrolling to shop our favorite products to recreate this ballet-inspired trend.

Ballet is all about how the body moves, so why not make it as beautiful as possible? This affordable (and glowy) body balm highlighter gives a glassy sheen and a pearlescent finish to the collarbone, shoulders, and other high points of your body.

Featuring ballet-blue packaging and a silver pointe shoe charm, this lip mask from r.e.m. beauty's new holiday collection is incredibly dreamy. It's infused with lactic acid, tripeptide, and shea butter to leave your lips soft, glossy, and smelling deliciously gourmand.

Prefer something a little softer and blurred? This viral lip stain in the shade Romance is a cool-toned rose color that's perfect for the stage and lasts through the entire performance.

When it comes to the skin, keep it light and fresh. Cushion foundations are a perfect option for ballet makeup since they look like your real skin. While there are so many to choose from, my favorite is YSL's Soft Glow Cushion Foundation—which has a weightless, hydrated feel with medium coverage.

Highlighter is key for this look. This adorable heart-shaped one from Kimchi Chic Beauty leaves your cheekbones with a pinkish, pearlescent glow that has us absolutely obsessed.

You can't go wrong with any product from Flower Knows! The C-Beauty brand is known for its gorgeously intricate packaging, and its latest Snow Ballet collection is no exception. This blush (specifically in the shade Chiffon Ballet) glides on your cheeks, giving you a silky flush fit for a ballerina.

When it comes to eyes, opt for a sheer wash of color with some shimmer on the lid and the inner corner. This K-Beauty brand offers an eyeshadow palette with 18 shimmering and velvety soft shades.

Dior is the best designer beauty brand to choose if you're trying to recreate the ballet-inspired makeup trend. Infused with cherry oil, the Lip Glow Oil is iconic for its mirror-like, glossy shine, plumping effect,  and variety of pH-changing shades. In our opinion, though, the clear sparkly shade Cupcake is perfection.

Want a cheek palette that gives you options? This one offers blushes and highlighters in a blendable cream and powder textures that leave your cheeks soft, flushed, and naturally radiant.

Talk about a throwback! This shadow palette from Physicians Formula is arguably the first ballet-inspired makeup product, with its soft nude shades and lace packaging with a pink bow to finish it off.
