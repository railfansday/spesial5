Simple Plan, Hoobastank Give Surprise Flight Performance Amid FAA Stop

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Simple Plan, Hoobastank and more artists headed for the Warped Tour surprised passengers with a mid-flight performance as flights were grounded due to the nationwide ground stop.

Life can be a nightmare and not fair, but Simple Plan, Hoobastank and The Used made sure travelers weren’t alone and were having fun on a flight.

That’s why the rock bands surprised people on a Southwest flight from Los Angeles to Florida with impromptu performances amid the nationwide ground stop on Nov. 13.

While the groups were on their way to Orlando for Vans Warped Tour 2026, they made 35,000 feet in the air their stage, with the bands performing a handful of their respective greatest hits, according to social footage shared by passengers, along with videos posted on the Warped Tour’s official Instagram account.

And though the reason for the FAA’s ground stop is still up in the air, Hoobastank’s lead singer Doug Robb and guitarist Dan Estrin found a way to show a side of the band fans didn’t know, performing their hits “The Reason” and “Crawling in the Dark,” per social videos.

As singer Lolo—who was also en route to Warped Tour and performed in the air—wrote alongside footage of Hoobastank’s surprise performance on Instagram, “Every plane was grounded today, but the emo gods made sure this one flew.”

Meanwhile, for Simple Plan’s mid-flight gig, lead singer Pierre Bouvier took up the mic and his guitar, warming millennials' hearts everywhere as he sang several of the punk band’s hits, such as the songs from their 2002 debut album, “I’m Just a Kid” and “Perfect,” along with the 2004 track “Welcome to Life,” according to footage shared by Warped Tour and passengers.

As for The Used’s performance, vocalist Bert McCraken and guitarist Joey Bradford took passengers—who sang along—on a trip down memory lane, performing the rock band’s songs “All That I’ve Got” and “The Taste of Ink,” per social videos, including from one traveler, who praised the “in-flight entertainment.”

And though the flight was later revealed to be a part of Southwest’s “Live at 35” surprise plane concert series, the bands were grateful to bring joy to passengers on the plane, which Robb told Rocksound Nov. 14 was “one of the last” to depart before the shutdown.

“[It] was just an extremely awesome and hilarious coincidence,” he added, “because we're on the plane going, ‘Are we okay? Is everything okay?’ But everything seems fine, so let's start singing.”

And the love was felt throughout the flight. As Robb put it, “It felt like a tube full of happy people.”

Australian rockers 5 Seconds of Summer first started on YouTube before singles "She Looks So Perfect" and "Girls Talk Boys" got radio play. Eventually 5SOS opened on tour for One Direction, and the rest is history.

Still performing as a trio, Boyz II Men's Nathan Morris, Shawn Stockman and Wanya Morris have been rocking the charts with hits like "I'll Make Love to You" and "One Sweet Day" for over 30 years.

The bad boy counterpart to One Direction, U.K.-based The Wanted hit it big stateside with 2012 banger "Glad You Came," before starring in their own E! reality series, The Wanted Life. The group went on hiatus in 2014 before reuniting in 2021, but sadly, founding member Tom Parker died of brain cancer the following year.

CNCO formed in 2016 with members Joel Pimentel, Richard Camacho, Erick Brian Colón, Christopher Vélez and Zabdiel De Jesús but gave their last performance as a five-some on May 14 after Pimental announced he was exiting the group.

From Nickelodeon to national tours, Big Time Rush rocked hard for five years before parting ways in 2014. Good thing all four members—Kendall Schmidt, Logan Henderson, Carlos PenaVega and James Maslow—reunited for two new shows in Dec. 2021.

British pop group Girls Aloud made history as one of the first bands formed from a reality competition series. The year was 2002, the show was Popstars: The Rivals and the powerhouse vocalists were Sarah Harding, Cheryl (then known as Cheryl Cole), Nadine Coyle, Nicola Roberts and Kimberley Walsh. Girls Aloud recorded 20 consecutive top 10 singles and four number ones, including "Sound of the Underground," during their reign.
