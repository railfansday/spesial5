Bryan Cranston, Marianne Jean-Baptiste to Star in ‘All My Sons’ on Broadway

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The two reprise their roles from the Olivier Award-winning London production.

Bryan Cranston and Marianne Jean-Baptiste will star in All My Sons on Broadway this spring.

The two reprise their roles as Joe Keller and Kate Keller after the play’s Olivier Award-winning run in London last season. Ivo van Hove directs the Arthur Miller revival, which is set to begin performances March 30, 2027 at Broadway’s August Wilson Theatre, ahead of an April 13 opening night.

Additional casting will be announced at a later date. Paapa Essiedu also starred in the London cast and won an Olivier Award for his performance, in addition to the play’s win for best revival.

Cranston returns to Broadway after last starring in van Hove’s Network in 2018 and winning a Tony Award for his role. He made his debut as President Lyndon B. Johnson in All The Way, for which he also won a Tony Award.

This is the Broadway debut for Jean-Baptiste, who received an Academy Award nomination for her performance in Mike Leigh’s Secrets & Lies, and recently appeared in his 2025 feature Hard Truths.

Van Hove, known for his spare, innovative style that typically involves the use of cameras, was last on Broadway with his updated take on West Side Story, which saw its run cut short by the pandemic.

All My Sons follows businessman Joe Keller as he grapples with the fallout of World War II, including the loss of a son and his own actions during the war. Originally premiering in 1947, the play was last on Broadway in 2019, starring Tracy Letts and Annette Bening.

“We are thrilled to bring this Olivier Award-winning production to Broadway following its acclaimed run in London, where audiences connected with Arthur Miller’s masterpiece in powerful and deeply resonant ways,” said producers Wessex Grove and Gavin Kalin Productions. “Led by the extraordinary Bryan Cranston and Marianne Jean-Baptiste and brought to life by Ivo Van Hove and an exceptional design team, this production offers a compelling examination of the American Dream and the cost of putting self-interest above collective responsibility. At a moment when Miller’s questions of accountability, truth, and moral responsibility feel more urgent than ever, we look forward to sharing this timely and deeply moving production with New York audiences.”
