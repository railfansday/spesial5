Manchester City’s WSL title bid hit by Spurs after Vinberg and Naz’s late double

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

This most unpredictable and evenly contested Women’s Super League campaign took another unexpected twist as Manchester City missed the chance to return to the top of the table as they suffered a second successive defeat, by virtue of a terrific performance from a Tottenham side who moved within three points of the European places.

The shock result came just 24 hours after City’s title rivals Chelsea had dropped points at Liverpool. It was the first time City had failed to win a home league match during Andrée Jeglertz’s 16 months in charge, let alone lose.

Nevertheless, the victory was fully deserved for Tottenham, who made Khadija “Bunny” Shaw wait to equal her teammate Vivianne Miedema’s all-time WSL goalscoring record of 97 goals.

Jeglertz described the defeat as a “wake-up call” for his team, adding: “Tottenham were a little bit more urgent than us. Each duel counts, each moment counts, each ball counts. That was something that I missed with our players, especially in the second half where we needed to score and we needed to beat them in one-versus-one situations. I missed a little bit of that urgency, which I think is the most important in a team, and if we drop a little bit in that, this league is so tight and tough and the teams are so good, you will drop points and lose games.”

Before the game, the anticipated storyline felt almost too obvious. Shaw, needing three goals to surpass Miedema, was facing a club against whom she had scored four hat-tricks, including on Tottenham’s most recent visit to this stadium in March, when she recorded the WSL’s fastest treble. Persistently their tormentor, Shaw has scored more goals against Spurs than any other player has managed against a single opponent in the competition.

When Shaw opened the scoring inside 10 minutes, everything seemed to be following the script. Jade Rose, pressing very high up the pitch for a centre-back, won the ball and played a precise low pass across to Shaw, who tucked into the bottom corner from inside the box to record her 96th WSL goal and her 16th against Tottenham.

That, however, proved to be the only effort at goal – on or off target – that City would register during the opening 45 minutes, as Tottenham impressed. Kirsty Hanson drew a good save from Ayaka Yamashita and Hanson also felt she had a very strong claim for a penalty when Carlotta Wamser appeared to foul her inside the area, but it was not long before the visitors were deservedly level as Cathinka Tandberg expertly turned home Hanson’s cross with a clinical near-post finish.

Tottenham continued to threaten after the break, although Rose scooped over the bar to spurn a huge City chance on the rebound after a Lauren Hemp free-kick had been parried by Lize Kop. Some very effective counterattacking football then deservedly won the game for Tottenham as they scored twice to stun the defending champions.

First, in the 74th minute, an exquisite Drew Spence through ball found the run of Matilda Vinberg – who was a late addition to the starting side after the withdrawal of Olivia Holdt during the warm-up because of stomach pains – and Vinberg showed her quality to turn Rose and fire past Yamashita. Vinberg turned provider five minutes later, to set up Jessica Naz for another good finish.

Martin Ho, the Tottenham head coach, hailed his team’s “outstanding” display, saying: “Every time we play a team who has been in the top three consistently, the conversation is: ‘Can you get one over the line?’ And, for the players, they need to feel what that feels like and enjoy that. Today we nullified a lot of their threats. That will give us a lot of confidence.”

Manchester City had won 12 of their 13 previous WSL games against Tottenham, and all of their past 17 WSL home games, but this defeat added a further dent to their title challenge after their loss at Stamford Bridge last weekend. Chelsea, held to a goalless draw by Liverpool on Saturday, remain two points clear.

Arsenal remain winless at home in the WSL this season after being held to another frustrating draw at the Emirates, this time by West Ham. Arsenal had 22 shots at goal but were unable to break the deadlock, as their under-pressure head coach, Renée Slegers, oversaw a sixth home league draw out of six, leaving Arsenal eight points behind the top three.

Manchester United left it late to come from behind and beat Birmingham 2-1 but Simi Awujo’s last-gasp header from a corner gave them a valuable win and extended Eva Olid’s side’s club-record unbeaten run to 14 matches in all competitions. Birmingham had led inside four minutes through Lee Geum-min’s expert volley but Elisabeth Terland’s close-range finish with 10 minutes to go and Awujo’s 94th-minute winner snatched it, moving Olid’s fourth-placed team just six points off the top.

Elsewhere, the bottom side Charlton collected their first point of the season as they drew 1-1 with Aston Villa, while Brighton moved into the top half with a 1-0 victory at Everton, courtesy of Maz Pacheco’s early own goal. Tom Garry

Jeglertz continued: “We will bounce back, I’m not worried about that. I know the qualities in this group. But it’s the good habits, the small details that matter. Those are the things that win titles – this was actually the first time that I missed that in a game and I’m pretty sure it’s not going to happen again.” With just seven points now separating the top five, his team can ill-afford for it to happen again.
