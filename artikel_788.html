Corey Gamble Calls Kris Jenner His "Wife" in Eyebrow-Raising Tribute

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Corey Gamble shared a loving Instagram tribute about Kris Jenner, whom he fondly described as his “lil wife.”

Corey Gamble is giving some love to his sweetie.

After all, the talent manager couldn't help but share a touching message to his "lil wife" Kris Jenner for her 71st birthday.

"Happiest Birthday to the Holy Grail …. Love you forever," Corey captioned his Nov. 5 Instagram post. "Keep being Great. You're the greatest mother, Lovey & Honey to all your kids and im blessed to have you in my life lil wife."

He concluded his tribute, "To many more great years ….. Love you much."

Kris reacted to the post on her Instagram Stories, resharing it and adding, “Love you.

And Khloe Kardashian proved she's always keeping up with her mom's life, sending a message of support in the comments, "Awww that's sweet Corey.”

While the couple didn't clarify if they've actually tied the knot, Kris addressed whether she'd consider walking down the aisle once again.

"You just never know. I'm full of surprises," she teased on her Khloe in Wonder Land podcast in April. "You could be a flower girl."

But Kris isn't focusing on the outside noise.

"I don't really have time to dwell on things because I'm so busy,” she added, “and I have such natural distractions with my real life and the things that really matter to me, my grandchildren, my children, me, my mom, CeCe, Corey, all of us."

Still, Corey and Kris—mom to kids Khloe, 42, Kourtney Kardashian, 47, Kim Kardashian, 46, and Rob Kardashian Jr., 39, with ex Robert Kardashian Sr., as well as Kendall Jenner, 31, and Kylie Jenner, 29, with ex Caitlyn Jenner—have proven they do amazing together. In fact, the momager shared her own loving tribute for his 45th birthday.

"Thank you for being my rock, my ride or die, and the most incredible partner," she wrote on Instagram in November 2025. "Thank you for all you do every single day, for the love you give, and for always being there for all of us. I'm so blessed to have you by my side. I love you!!!"

And Kris—who began dating Corey in 2014—is grateful they both put in the work to keep their relationship strong.

"We have the best time together," Kris shared during a March 2025 episode of The Kardashians. "We've been together over a decade—never thought I'd say that again—and we just have a lot of fun together."

Corey—who used to work as Justin Bieber's tour manager—feels love from the entire family, with Khloe commending him for always supporting the matriarch.

"He hasn't let yet, with all of our craziness," she playfully teased to Kris during her Khloe in Wonder Land podcast in March 2025. "He doesn't try to control you. He lets you be you."

As Kris put it, "And he makes it easier to be me."
