Slayyyter, Phoebe Bridgers, Billy Strings to Headline Iowa’s 2027 Hinterland Festival

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The July 30-Aug. 1 event in St. Charles, Iowa will also feature sets from Remi Wolf, Violet Grohl, Malcolm Todd, Zach Top and bbno$, among others.

The 2027 Hinterland Music Festival will feature headliners Slayyyter, Phoebe Bridgers and Billy Strings, who will take the stage at the July 30-Aug. 1 event in St. Charles, Iowa. Among the other acts booked for the three-day event are Remi Wolf, Caroline Polachek, bbno$, Tiffany Day, Florence Road, Violet Grohl, Sophia Stel and Judith on day one. The July 31 roster will also include Malcolm Todd, The Backseat Lovers, Joshua Slone, underscores, Emma Ogier, Westside Cowboy and Kinji, with Zach Top, Stephen Wilson Jr., Mon Rovia, Wade Forster and Medium Build rounding out the Aug. 1 lineup. There will also be a special performance by Haywire in a Cornfield. Camping is once again at the center of the 12th edition of the music fest at Roaming Hills in St. Charles, just outside Des Moines, with fans able to choose from tent, car or RV options for the weekend. A release announcing this year’s roster notes that after the final main stage set of each night, the action moves to the Campfire Stage for late-night sets in the campground that are open to all attendees.

In addition, the Basecamp campground hub will once again feature food vendors and bars, along with the HinterMarket showcasing regional artists and makers, alongside immersive art installations, interactive experiences, miles of scenic trails and the family-friendly Miniland.

Last year’s lineup featured Lorde, KATSEYE, Mumford & Sons, Ninajirachi, Geese, Kali Uchis, Oklou, Jane Remover, Audrey Hobert, Audrey Nuna, beabadoobee, CMAT, Julia Wolf and Santigold.
