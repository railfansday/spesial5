Rob Kardashian Poses in Rare Family Photos for Daughter Dream's B-Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Rob Kardashian posed for rare family photos at his daughter Dream Kardashian’s leopard-themed 10th birthday party, including one where he was all smiles with sister Khloe Kardashian.

Rob Kardashian is doing amazing, sweetie.

The Keeping Up With the Kardashians alum shared a rare photo posing with his and Blac Chyna’s daughter Dream Kardashian while celebrating her 10th birthday with an extravagant leopard-themed bash.

“Birthday fun yay yay,” Rob captioned a Nov. 21 Instagram post. “I love you Dreammmmmmmmmm.”

In one snap shared to the carousel of photos, the 39-year-old—donning a blue hat and black Nike shirt—was all smiles as his little girl leaned in close to pose for the camera.

And while the pic marks a rare sighting for the social media star, one common occurrence in Rob’s life is how he shows up for his daughter—and her special day was no different.

Elsewhere in his post, Dream—wearing a leopard-print top, matching shorts, Uggs and a chain around her neck—couldn’t contain her excitement as she smiled behind her elaborate leopard-print cake.

Sharing a closer look into the soirée, Rob’s sister Khloe Kardashian proved in her Nov. 21 Instagram post that keeping up with Dream’s cousins—including her and Tristan Thompson’s kids True Thompson, 8, and Tatum Thompson, 4—might have been difficult if it weren’t for the on-theme arts and crafts, sweet treats and dance party to keep the family’s little ones entertained.

In fact, Khloe—who wore a figure-hugging leopard ensemble to the event—and Rob were able to snag their own moment together as they also posed for a rare photo while sitting at an outdoor table.

But the backyard bash wouldn’t be the only way Rob made his little girl’s milestone birthday one to remember.

After all, he pulled out all the stop for her golden birthday Nov. 10, with the tween waking up to a bouquet of flowers, huge balloons that read “Happy 10th Birthday Dream” and donuts to commemorate the occasion.

"Happy golden Birthday to my Dream girl," Rob wrote on Instagram, referencing the one year when a person's age and date of birth align. "I can’t believe 10 years has gone by already goodness Lord please slow down time."

And while it’s true that the years are flying by, Rob is just grateful to rise and shine every day as Dream’s dad.

“Being your dad is the greatest gift of my life!” he added. “You make me proud every single day! I love you more than you’ll ever know!!”
