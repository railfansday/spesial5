The Lego Shrek, Donkey, and Puss in Boots set has never been cheaper at Amazon — save over $25

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

SAVE OVER $20: As of Nov. 18, the Lego Shrek, Donkey, and Puss in Boots set has hit a new low price of $103.95 at Amazon. That's $26.04 off its full cost of $129.99.

Amazon's Black Friday sale may start tomorrow, but it's already dropping solid early Lego deals we think are worth checking out. This includes price cuts on some newer builds from this year, such as the Lego Shrek, Donkey, and Puss in Boots set, which has dropped to a record low.

As of Nov. 18, this brick-built model of the iconic trio is on sale for $103.95, $26.04 off its usual price of $129.99. This marks the first major discount we've seen on this set, which is a nice surprise before Black Friday sales officially start. If you've been hoping to grab this 1,403-piece build, now is a great time to do it.

This Lego set is quite a recent release, launching just a few months ago. It's recommended for adult builders, with an age rating of 18+, and lets you piece together Shrek, Donkey, and Puss in Boots, who stand atop a little swamp setup with the classic 'Beware Ogre!' sign, some sunflowers, and more. There are even some fun accessories, like an onion and a blue flower with red thorns.

If the Lego Shrek, Donkey, and Puss in Boots set has been on your wishlist, there's no need to wait for Black Friday to buy it. Act fast to save at Amazon.
