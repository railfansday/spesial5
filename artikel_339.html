YouTubes official GTA 6 release day numbers are staggering

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

If it feels like everyone and their dog is watching or streaming Grand Theft Auto VI content, YouTube has the official numbers to prove it.

In a blog post Tuesday, the company detailed the colossal online impact of Rockstar Games' critically acclaimed blockbuster, announcing that YouTube saw over 3.5 billion global views of videos with GTA 6 in the title on Nov. 19's release day.

To date, the Grand Theft Auto series has over 900 billion views of related content on YouTube, according to the data, with videos specifically about GTA 6 gaining 40 billion views before release day.

Along with YouTubers, streamers across Twitch and Kick broke streaming records with GTA 6 content on launch day. Data from streaming analytics platform Streams Charts said Twitch saw 2.50 million views while Kick had 1.31 million (the platform clocked YouTube at 3.22 million views, less than the company now reports), marking a reported total of 7.91 million concurrent GTA 6 launch day viewers.

In context, the 24-hour record for most views on one YouTube clip remains with K-pop superstars BTS, whose 2021 "Butter" video saw 108.2 million views in one day, smashing their own 101.1 million-view first-day record for previous single "Dynamite."

However, GTA 6 can claim the record for most views in a 24-hour debut of a non-music video for the very first trailer released in Dec. 2023: 93 million views in 24 hours.

To mark the streaming news, YouTube added a few GTA 6 themed features to YouTube Live including onscreen Wanted Stars (the stars that pop-up in game if there's police heat on protagonists Jason and Lucia), and a Shorts effect that lets creators use Vice City backgrounds.
