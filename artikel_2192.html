Dave Challinor's private Sheffield Wednesday notes exposed during Oldham defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Dave Challinor's debut as Sheffield Wednesday manager didn't go to plan, as the Owls were dumped out of the FA Cup by League Two opposition in Oldham Athletic, and there were parts of the performance he was especially angered by.

Oldham were good value for their win after extra time, with Wednesday putting in a tired performance that not many were expecting from Challinor's first game in charge. It's the first time that the 51-year-old has opened up a dugout role without a win.

The first half, especially, wasn't good enough by the new manager's standards. Wednesday dominated possession, but only had one shot and just three touches in the Latics' box. It was quite reminiscent of what angered Owls fans about Henrik Pedersen's setup.

Challinor wasn't going to let this become the norm, though, and was seen writing plenty of notes in his notebook for what was going to be quite the angry team talk at the break.

Oldham's win over Wednesday was shown live on TNT Sports on Sunday evening, and with the TV cameras out, the Owls were unable to put on a show in the first half of Dave Challinor's reign.

The cameras caught an over-the-shoulder shot of the Wednesday boss making some notes midway through the first half, and he'd already had a full page's worth of grievances.

Amongst some more expletive expressions to describe parts of that first half, there were the words "WEAK," "compete !!" and "Stupid !"

Immediately, Wednesday fans took to X to try and see if anyone could make out what was being written by their new boss midway through the first half, which led to many screenshots, identical to the one above, being tweeted.

"Not a happy boy. Quite rightly," one Wednesday fan said in response to those notes being relayed on X during the game. "Hahahahhahaa that’s class. He’s impressed I see," another commented.

"This is hilarious btw. It took him 2 minutes to see how dire Palmer is," was also among the comments, as was "That result might just be what Challinor needed to witness."

Sheffield Wednesday fans soured very quickly on Henrik Pedersen when results weren't going their way, but despite bowing out of the FA Cup at the first hurdle to a side a division below, the majority of reaction during and after the game was directed at the performance on the pitch, rather than in the dugout.

After the game, Challinor continued to cut a frustrated figure, with the majority of fans agreeing with what he was saying, with the overarching feeling that some of the players aren't where they should be physically at this stage in the campaign.

Most Wednesday supporters felt disappointed with how Pedersen would protect players after poor results, whereas the new boss seems content with outlining where things aren't good enough, which in turn is winning fans over.

While it would have been an ideal scenario for Dave Challinor to walk in, tweak one or two things and Wednesday go from strength to strength, the first 45 minutes of his tenure in charge highlight that this definitely won't be the case.

Challinor is coming into a side that, by his own admission, isn't there physically and is struggling massively with injury.

Mason Burstow is the latest casualty, while Ashley Maynard-Brewer was forced off during the game and Harry Gray was removed from the squad at the last minute after failing to get through the warm-up.

The 51-year-old, evidently, is going to need time, but it feels that the Wednesday fanbase, or at least the most vocal parts, are happy to give him that. At least, more so than they were with Challinor's predecessor.

To many, Challinor was the number one option to take Wednesday forward at the start of the season, and now he's in post; those in the stands at Hillsborough are willing to give him enough time to take them forward. In the meantime, his ruthless nature is helping them get onside, even if the results take some time to follow suit.
