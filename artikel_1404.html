Inter: Chivu’s big rant after the draw against Atalanta

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

While AC Milan played against Frosinone (5-1) earlier in the day, Inter Milan failed to keep pace by conceding a spectacular, but frustrating draw on the lawn of Atalanta Bergamo (4-4) this Sunday evening. Despite a successful offensive performance and a new sparkling hat-trick from Pio Esposito, which confirms his rise in power at the forefront of the Nerazzurra attack, Cristian Chivu’s men squandered their lead on the scoreboard three times. A cruel scenario which deeply annoyed the Romanian coach at the microphone of DAZN after the meeting: “we led three times, that’s the regret. We could have done much better in management, we managed to take a two-goal lead and we failed this test of maturity through sheer thoughtlessness. »

Particularly angry at the defensive errors of his team, the Interist technician did not mince his words to point out the culpable slackness of his players in key moments: “congratulations to Atalanta, they knew how to put us in difficulty by occupying the surface better. These are two points offered, let’s learn the lesson. Defense? You have to know how to read specific game situations. It’s always a question of attitude and determination to display. (…) The number of goals conceded is far too high for a team with such ambitions. I’m not looking back, but we all have to remember to give it our all until the final whistle. »
