'Hang on a minute' - Ex-Spurs employee can't believe Tottenham's response to Leeds defeat

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Tottenham Hotspur should focus on their team's failure to break down Leeds United instead of blaming Saturday's 1-0 defeat on the referee.

That is the opinion of former senior Tottenham scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The Lilywhites have submitted a formal complaint to the refereeing body Pro Ref after Jayden Bogle remained on the pitch following a challenge on Mathys Tel.

The Leeds defender appeared to step on the back of the Tottenham man's Achilles, eventually forcing him off the pitch injured.

Referee John Brooks did not even award a yellow card, with the decision eventually upheld after a lengthy review by VAR Tony Harrington.

Leeds would go on to dominate much of the remaining action, finding the breakthrough 11 minutes from time when Noah Okafor scored.

In their last two games before Saturday's trip to Elland Road, Tottenham had benefitted from red cards handed to their opponents.

Cole Palmer was sent off for two bookable offences when they won at Stamford Bridge, and it took just 13 minutes for Jean-Philippe Mateta to be dismissed when Crystal Palace visited North London.

VAR looked certain to make it three in a row when Bogle's challenge was reviewed, but Tottenham were ultimately made to play against 11 men.

"Don't start blaming officials for tackles or offside decisions or VAR for this, that and the other. Tottenham, for me, didn't deserve anything on Saturday," King told Football Insider.

"You're looking for somebody to get a red card for a tackle, which 20 years ago, you would have laughed at. I thought Leeds were better.

"They looked a far better team. They were more aggressive. They just looked a better team than Tottenham.

"This has been Spurs' problem for me. You need a team that are going to start looking and upsetting teams because, at the moment, Tottenham seem to be getting manhandled out of results.

"And if you start looking to blame the referee - hang on a minute, this is a 60-second incident. Look at the 85 minutes that your team played poorly."

Tottenham were clearly angered by the decision not to send Bogle off on Saturday, but Roberto De Zerbi stayed away from controversy after the game.

"I can't say something more because I didn't see clearly the situation," he said. "I was surprised because in the same position in the second half, two different fouls and two yellow cards.

"I don't know if it was a red card or not on Mathys Tel. And why I'm telling like this, because the parameter of the referee was not good enough, I think, for this both situation."

It is unclear how Bogle managed to get away without even a yellow card, but De Zerbi will have plenty more to think about given his team's display.
