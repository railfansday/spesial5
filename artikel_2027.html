Rangers-mad pundit names the Aberdeen figures who must be 'held accountable'

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Kris Boyd insists Aberdeen are “miles off” where they should be as pressure continues to mount on Stephen Robinson.

The Dons have endured a difficult start to the campaign, with their latest struggles placing the manager firmly under the spotlight.

— Sky Sports Scotland (@ScotlandSky) November 9, 2026

However, former Scotland striker Boyd believes responsibility for Aberdeen’s problems should not rest solely with Robinson.

Speaking on Sky Sports, Boyd acknowledged that the manager has struggled to win over sections of the support but pointed towards other influential figures associated with Aberdeen who he believes should also face scrutiny.

He said: “It’s not worked out the way that he would have thought it would have.

“A lot of it was down to coming in last year, and keeping Aberdeen in the league has got to be considered as a success, but obviously lost out in the semi-final last week.

“It’s not been the start to the season that Aberdeen would have expected. I don’t think the fans have taken to him, but there’s a lot of other factors for me that the Stephen Robinson regime at Aberdeen hasn’t worked.

"I said it on Saturday after the match as well: there are a lot of individuals at Aberdeen. You go back to Alan Burrows, Dave Cormack, Lutz Pfannenstiel, Darren Mowbray as well, the head of recruitment.

"There’s other people in there who have got to be held accountable as well because it’s not been great for Aberdeen.

“They will tell you they’re the third biggest team in the country, but right now they are miles off it, and they’re going to have to start getting results.”

Aberdeen will now be desperate to find an upturn in form as Robinson attempts to ease the growing pressure surrounding his position.
