'I've looked at it a few times' - Ex-refs chief shares verdict after analysing Patrick Dorgu footage vs Aston Villa

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Patrick Dorgu's equaliser against Aston Villa was rightfully awarded to Manchester United.

That is the opinion of former head of PGMOL and FIFA referee Keith Hackett, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

Aston Villa and the Red Devils played out an enthralling eight-goal thriller that was not short of contentious decisions.

Two penalties were awarded on the night, and complaints were made by the Villa's defence after conceding in the closing minutes.

Zion Suzuki led those efforts to get the goal checked over by VAR, but in the end, it was allowed to stand.

With Hackett delivering his thoughts on the decision.

Goalkeepers are extremely protected between the sticks, and often any infringement on them is put forward as a possible foul.

When Suzuki looked to claim the ball from United's last-gasp corner, Senne Lammens positioned himself right in front of his opposing counterpart.

The Belgian did enough to both put off the Japanese shot-stopper, while also avoiding a foul, at least according to Hackett.

Hackett said: "I think when you look at it, and I looked at it a couple of times last night, I think the goalkeeper's [Lammens] actions really have taken place after the goalkeeper [Suzuki] has missed going for the ball.

"And, you know, when a goalkeeper comes out, he's got a fair amount of protection, but he's got to effectively win the ball and win it cleanly. And I think this is poor goalkeeping rather than actually a foul that should be awarded."

"So I'm saying that I'm allowing the goal. I'm happy with the goal because I think that action by the opposing player was after the event, not during the course of it."

Hackett was also left impressed with how Kavanagh generally officiated the 4-4 draw seeing as there were a number of incidents.

He said: "So, look, I thought Kavanagh officiated quite well yesterday. I thought it was one of his better performances.

"I think the game has to recognise that we have two UEFA elite referees. We might have an opinion about those two, Michael Oliver and Chris Kavanagh, but they've satisfied UEFA that they are elite officials.

"And therefore, as such, a game of this nature needed a top-class referee on it."

Villa fans may not agree, and there will be plenty of United fans who were left unhappy as well.

But two sets of unhappy fans with the referee is often a sign he was fair in his judgements.
