Leeds 'set sights' on deal for 'top-class' Will Lankshear - transfer expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Leeds United have joined a number of Premier League clubs in the race to sign Middlesbrough star Will Lankshear in January.

The 21-year-old has made headlines since his move to Boro from Tottenham during the summer, and has established himself as one of the best strikers in the second tier.

He could now be offered the chance to step up into the Premier League, with scouts keeping a close eye on his performances, and many believed to have been impressed.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Lankshear is a "top-class" striker and could be the ideal target for Leeds as they step up their transfer plans.

Daniel Farke is determined to sign a new centre-forwrad in the January transfer window, and is keen on the striker who has scoerd 15 goals in 16 appearances this season.

Whether Leeds or any of their Premier League rivals could stump up the funds to lure Lankshear away from Middlesbrough remains to be seen, but they are weighing things up.

Football Insider revealed on 11 November that Middlesbrough are extremely reluctant to lose Lankshear, but must win promotion to the Premier League to do so.

A January move is currently seen as unlikely given his importance to Kim Hellberg's side, but clubs have not ruled out making offers in an attempt to lure him away.

Given Leeds' desire to sign a new striker, they are likely to be among them, and Lankshear is believed to tick a number of boxes for Farke as he pushes ambitious transfer plans.

"Will Lankshear is obviously going to have a lot of Premier League clubs looking at him," Brown told Football Insider.

"He's proving already he's a top-class striker, it's not like he's just come out of nowehere, he's somebody these clubs have been aware of even when he was at Tottenham.

"But now he's showing he can do it at a high level, there's even been talk of an England call-up, so maybe he is ready for the Premier League.

"Leeds, for example, will be watching him closely because everybody knows how badly they want to sign a new striker and he would tick a number of boxes for them.

"He's a certain type of striker, a penalty box striker, somebody who gets on the end of moves and scores goals rather than getting involved too much, and Leeds could do with a player like that.

"Whether or not he'll be allowed to leave in January, there could be offers to tempt him away, because why not? Why not test their resolve?

"Leeds should be one of those clubs interested, but obviously they won't be the only one, so it's going to be something to keep an eye on."

As previously mentioned, Leeds are far from the only club keeping a close eye on Lankshear's situation, as a number of Premier League clubs are keen to lure him away.

The striker's recent form has placed a number of potential suitors on high alert, and every club looking to sign a new striker are bound to be watching.

Football Insider revealed on 10 November that Newcastle are among the clubs who could make a move for Lankshear amid growing concerns about their current options.

However, the Magpies have been warned they would have to part with a fee of around £30million for Middlesbrough to entertain offers just months after he joined the club.

Sources say that the starlet's price tag is increasing weekly at the moment, and has already tripled from the initial £10m that he moved for in the summer.
