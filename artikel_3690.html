While the internet broke when Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson and Woody Harrelson reunited at the premiere of Sunrise on the Reaping, there was another moment happening that’s gotten a hilarious amount of attention. I’m, of course, talking about the bird on Whitney Peak’s bold dress getting in the way of her co-star Jesse Plemons during the group photo.

Now, the Lenore Dove actress has responded to all the completely unfettered Internet users who posted about her bird hilariously getting in the Plutarch actor’s face.

Overall, there was a lot of love for the celebrity fashion moments at this premiere for this book-to-screen adaptation on the 2026 movie schedule. I even personally like Peak's deep blue gown!

However, Plemons politely leaning out of Whitney Peak's bird's way with a funny look on his face (as you can see in the group image above) was a little less well-reserved. People called out the funny moment, as influencers like ideservecouture posted reactions on Instagram saying:

I don’t think that she thought about the group picture, because I cannot look at [laughs]. I cannot look at this picture. [Laughs] I love [Jesse Plemons]. I mean, can you blame him? My favorite thing is the head tilt, little to the side. I find that hilarious. The whole premiere was worth it just for this picture. I am sorry; this is the funniest thing today.

The comments on that viral video (and others) couldn’t get over how the white bird got into the actor’s face and how he reacted to it by awkwardly leaning out of the way. This is what the Internet was made for:

Now, Peak has seen the memes and the jokes, and she’s responded to all of it with the same amount of humor.

Following the premiere of Sunrise on the Reaping, Peak posted a photo dump on Instagram about it and her dress. Seriously, the floating shoulder on the blue Robert Wun gown and the white bird flying off of it are stunning, and as the actress wrote in one of her captions about the garment, “she is the moment.” However, she also knows that pesky but legendary white bird got in Digger actor’s face, as she captioned the image of her and Plemons with:

Honestly, this whole hilarious incident just made this fashion moment even better. Already, the floating shoulder and the bird were iconic to me, as it was an avant-garde piece that was perfect for this Hunger Games prequel. But this is the sort of viral moment that's funny without anyone getting seriously offended, and it's the perfect thing for the Internet to latch onto.

Plus, while people are poking fun at what happened between Peak and Plemons, there’s also so much overwhelming love for the dress. In fact, A-listers came out of the woodwork to comment things like this on the Gossip Girl actress’s post:

Ultimately, I think this bird dress will go down alongside Mckenna Grace’s Hunger Games method dressing, Elle Fanning’s Effie looks and Joseph Zada’s pajamas as one of the most memorable fashion moments from the Sunrise on the Reaping press tour. Seriously, it was so beautiful, but what happened with Jesse Plemons and Whitney Peak’s reaction to it all made it even better.

Now, with all that said, if you’d like to see the movie that caused all of this hilarious fuss, you can see Sunrise on the Reaping in theaters now.
