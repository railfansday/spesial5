Jensen Huang Resigns From Nvidia

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Jensen Huang founded Nvidia back in 1993 and called each of the company’s major shots along the way, from its focus on gaming graphics to its latest pivot to AI. He’s now resigned as CEO following reports of multiple affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy.

“Board of Directors announced today that Jensen Huang has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately,” reads a new press release from the tech giant on November 12. Colette Kress, Nvidia’s current chief financial officer, has been named as interim CEO to replace Huang while the board of directors initiates a search for a permanent replacement. Investor Harvey Jones has been appointed chair of the board following Huang’s departure.

Huang’s resignation comes after a report in The New York Times of a second extramarital affair, this time with Nvidia’s VP of healthcare technology. Earlier in the week, the 63-year-old executive admitted to a separate romantic relationship with an alleged Chinese spy. Nvidia’s stock price has shed hundreds of billions in the intervening days, and the company faces at least one lawsuit from shareholders.

Once a humble graphics card company lobbying video game console makers for contracts to supply them with chips, Nvidia has become the epicenter for the current boom in AI tech, rising to a multi-trillion-dollar market cap in just a couple of years as it shifted from servicing the PC gaming market to feeding an insatiable demand for new data center buildout.
