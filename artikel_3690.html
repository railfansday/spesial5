All edible dice locations in Dice Hoarder

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Dice Hoarder has bucket loads of dice to collect and store, including 10 hidden edible dice you can find in specific locations. These are different from your standard dice, since you can’t store them. Instead, you’ll have to find and consume them.

Let’s look at all the locations where you can find these edible dice.

While there are only 10 edible dice, there are 13 possible locations you can find them, so you might need to look around a bit.

Some dice locations for you may be different. What’s not different is the store inventory and where you keep all your ordinary dice. Going to the computer every time you pick up a die isn’t convenient. You can check out this map instead to make your life easier.
