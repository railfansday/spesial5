How Meghan Markle and Prince Harry Are Coping After Her Dad Thomas Markle's Arrest

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Meghan Markle and Prince Harry are trying to focus on their new life in the U.K. after the Duchess of Sussex’s father Thomas Markle Sr.‘s arrest on a child pornography charge.

“Meghan is really beside herself over everything going on with her father,” a source exclusively tells Us. “She is extremely upset and emotional over it. It’s obviously been a lot for her to process.”

Thomas, 82, was charged on November 6 with one count of possession of child pornography after federal agents conducted a raid on two of his Los Angeles storage containers. A separate investigation is taking place in the Philippines — where Thomas Sr. lived until he had part of his left leg amputated in December 2025 — after his son, Thomas Markle Jr., allegedly found child sex abuse material (CSAM) in his father’s former home and turned over evidence to local police.

The retired TV lighting director was subsequently re-arrested by officers from Florida’s Pinellas County Sheriff’s Office on November 10 after allegedly cutting off an ankle monitor that he was required to wear as part of his release on bond pending trial. (Thomas Sr. has maintained his innocence, calling the claims “sick lies.”)

“[Meghan] knew about the allegations before it was made public, and it has been very hard on her,” the insider shares with Us. “Although she is estranged from her father, it affects her deeply, and she is disturbed by it. ”

Meghan, 45, broke her silence about the allegations against her father on October 23, revealing that she’d first been warned in a letter obtained by London’s Metropolitan Police and Royalty and Specialist Protection in 2018.

“In 2018, a letter was sent to the Metropolitan Police and Royalty and Specialist Protection. That letter was subsequently shared with me,” she said in a statement. “It made troubling and disturbing allegations against my father and I was given assurances that these were being investigated.”

The duchess went on to say, “I hope it can now be understood that, despite certain media publications’ distortion over the years, any distance from my father and my choice to not allow him to have contact with our children, was for one reason: to protect them. I trusted that the authorities were doing the same for other families.”

Meghan closed her statement by suggesting the current allegations against her father were “more sickening than one could ever imagine” while expressing her “sincerest hope that justice will be fully served.”

It was subsequently reported that Meghan had sent a private note to Thomas Sr.’s bedside while he was hospitalized in 2025 to tell him she would never try to contact him again and ban him from ever meeting his grandchildren Prince Archie, 7, and Princess Lilibet, 5.

The fresh allegations against Thomas Sr. emerged mere weeks after Meghan and Harry, 42, moved with their two children from Montecito, California, back to the U.K. The family arrived back in England in time for the start of the new school year in late August and have reportedly settled into a home in the Cotswolds.

“Meghan and Harry have been focused on settling into their life in the U.K.,” the insider tells Us. “They’re actually in a really good place right now and are happy there. They wanted a change of pace and to get away from everything for a bit, and they’re feeling really settled and happy with that decision.”

Us has reached out to representatives for the duke and duchess for comment.
