Sephoras Fall Savings Event drops a rare deal on the Dyson HushJet Mini Cool: Save up to 20% with code

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

SAVE WITH CODE ALLONSALE: Beauty Insider members can snag the Dyson HushJet Mini Cool on sale for $89.10 during the Sephora Savings Event (normally $99). Those with VIB and Rouge status will pay as little as $79.20.

Having trouble finding the right Christmas gift for an outdoorsy person? Don't sweat it: We just spotted a rare deal on Dyson's fancy new portable fan, the HushJet Mini Cool.

During Sephora's fall Savings Event, live now through Nov. 9, members of its free-to-join Beauty Insiders rewards program can get the HushJet Mini Cool on sale for just $89.10, or 10% off. You'll pay even less if you're a Beauty Insider member with VIB or Rouge status, meaning you've spent at least $350 or $1,000 at Sephora in the past year (respectively). Those tiers unlock 15% and 20% discounts with ALLONSALE.

I tested the HushJet Mini Cool during two heatwaves this past summer and was impressed by its power. To quote my review: "There are five standard airflow speed settings to choose from, plus a Boost mode ... At settings two and up, it gusts." I also liked that it's very easy to stash in a purse or fanny pack. Just be aware that it gets very noisy at its upper settings — I wouldn't use it indoors or in a tightly packed crowd.

If you have a little more wiggle room in your budget, note that the rival Shark ChillPill is also on sale for up to 20% off (depending on your membership status) during the Sephora Savings Event. It's pricier, but it adds swappable misting and cooling attachments.
