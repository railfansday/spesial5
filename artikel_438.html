Jimmy Kimmel reacts to Trump insulting yet another female reporter

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Federal Aviation Administration's (FAA) nationwide ground stop over the weekend may have stranded thousands due to canceled and diverted flights, but Donald Trump is determined to avoid any responsibility himself — instead attempting to divert blame towards the Biden administration.

"Trump met with the CEOs of airlines and vowed to modernise these systems," Jimmy Kimmel explains in the clip above from Monday night's show. "Unfortunately he did it back in 2017 and never, ever mentioned it again. This wasn't Biden's fault. This was the president before Biden's fault, that's who Trump should be blaming. What was that guy's name?"

Kimmel goes on to recap a tense exchange between Trump and NBC News White House correspondent Yamiche Alcindor, who questioned the president's spending on D.C. construction projects "while our airline systems were crumbling" — the president responded by hurling insults at the journalist.

"What a mean, obnoxious creep," says Kimmel. "He hates female reporters so much. How dare they challenge his gift for redecorating?"

You certainly don't have to look too hard for more examples of the president insulting female reporters doing their jobs.
