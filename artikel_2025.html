Victor Osimhen flattered by Manchester United transfer links, report claims

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Galatasaray striker Victor Osimhen is reportedly open to a move to Manchester United, although any potential transfer would depend on an agreement with the Turkish champions.

The Nigeria international, 27, has been linked with United as new head coach Mauricio Pochettino considers his attacking options. According to The Sun, Osimhen is flattered by the reported interest and remains keen to play in the Premier League during his career. However, there has been no confirmation of a formal approach or negotiations between the clubs.

Osimhen is under contract until June 2029 and reportedly earns around £13m a year. The Sun says Galatasaray value the former Napoli forward at between £80m and £100m, with the club reluctant to lose him midway through the season.

The report also suggests there is no release clause in his contract, meaning United would need to agree a fee with Galatasaray. A source described as close to Osimhen told the newspaper that the striker was flattered by the interest but remained loyal to his current club. Any January move could therefore prove difficult, with a summer transfer potentially more realistic.

Osimhen has scored 10 goals in 11 appearances across all competitions this season, following campaigns of 37 and 22 goals for Galatasaray. His reported interest in a Premier League move comes as Pochettino prepares for his first match in charge of United against Liverpool on Sunday.

Benjamin Sesko’s injury has reduced United’s attacking options, while Joshua Zirkzee has struggled to establish himself as a regular goalscorer. The speculation was also included in Sky Sports’ transfer paper talk, which attributed the claims to The Sun. For now, Osimhen remains a Galatasaray player, with no indication that a transfer is close.
