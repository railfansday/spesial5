Viktor Gyökeres’ Sweden resurgence raises Arsenal questions, but the January exit is far from certain

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Viktor Gyökeres is reminding Arsenal of his prolific goal scoring capabilities.

The striker found the back of the net twice in Sweden’s 3-2 Nations League win against Bosnia and Herzegovina on November 14, both times from the spot to take his total to four in the Nations League this season.

The win also means they have booked their place in League A with one match left to play in the group, and Gyökeres has certainly played his part in this excellent run.

However, the performance will raise questions about his future at the club. The idea that he may be leaving in January is a possibility that needs to be looked at.

There is a stark difference between his international and club form.

Gyökeres has only played 181 minutes in seven Premier League games this season, starting just once as Kai Havertz has usually been preferred in central areas.

However, he has had more opportunities to play in European competition. Gyökeres has started three of Arsenal’s four matches in the Champions League, scoring one goal and providing two assists.

Gyökeres is not totally out of Arteta’s plans, but he will not play much domestically.

He has been in fine form internationally recently. Sweden are building their attack around his movement and penalty area presence, meaning he will have chances to regularly perform.

However, just because he is doing well internationally does not mean he will start regularly for the club. Arteta has other attacking options and tactics to consider.

There have been suggestions surrounding Gyökeres’ future, with reports that Arsenal may look to add more attacking flair to their squad.

AC Milan and Juventus have also been linked, but there is no agreement or suggestion that either club is looking to make a January move for the forward.

Gyökeres has opened up about the speculation.

In an interview with Swedish media outlet Fotbollskanalen during the international break, he stated that he would like to stay at the club. The player also denied any issues with his relationship with Arteta, stating that they have good communication.

However, the player may not stay forever, as he has not played much in the Premier League so far. If this continues, and Arsenal sign another forward, it could cause an issue.

For now, though, there is little evidence that Gyökeres is trying to leave the club.

He’ll be looking to get regular game time at 28 after finding his scoring touch with Sweden. Arsenal will also need to work out how to use him best as a goalscoring striker.

But for now the January speculation seems a little early. He has stated he wants to stay at Arsenal, and the next few weeks may prove to be more important.
