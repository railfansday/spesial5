Unai Emery could soon put pen to paper on a new Aston Villa contract after the club dropped a big hint today.

The Spaniard has been simply sensational ever since stepping through the Villa Park doors in 2022.

That all culminated in Europa League glory last campaign and has continued with success to begin a new Champions League season.

As a result, no one has been surprised that links to Manchester United have been reignited.

That hasn't gone down well within the Villa hierarchy as Jamie Carragher was threatened with legal action for even suggesting it.

The claret and blue outfit have now made their stance clear, with all eyes now on a potential new contract for Emery.

The B6 faithful have never truly believed that their head coach would be on the way to Old Trafford.

With all the control that he has at Villa Park, it would be utterly bizarre for him to depart for the Red Devils.

Fans at Old Trafford don't seem to understand that.

Any noise now looks set to have been put to bed once and for all as Emery met with Nassef Sawiris on Wednesday.

Villa released a statement to X, reading: "Aston Villa held an important working meeting today to plan the club's future and lay the foundations for its long-term sporting and institutional project.

"The meeting brought together Nassef Sawiris, the club's Chairman and Unai Emery alongside President of Football Operations Roberto Olabe and Director of Football Operations Damian Vidagany.

"The discussions focused on jointly planning the club's long-term strategic direction, with positive discussions establishing a clear commitment beyond 2029 for the joint development of the club under the sporting leadership of Unai Emery based on the shared ambitions and common goal to reach excellency and keep growing during the next years.

"The objective is clear: to build an ambitious, competitive Aston Villa capable of taking on the biggest challenges and achievements."

That serves as a major hint that Emery could soon pen a new contract, but what could that mean for Manchester United?

This seems like a direct address from the Villans to the Red Devils.

It proves just the ambition that Sawiris has for the claret and blue outfit both on and off the pitch.

Emery is a major part of that and looks set to be sticking around for the foreseeable.

It isn't a new contract officially, but it is good as.

That leaves Man United without their favoured managerial target should they part ways with Michael Carrick.

It instead opens the door for the likes of Julian Nagelsmann or Eddie Howe to potentially move up the pecking order.

This is great news for Aston Villa and a bad day at Old Trafford.
