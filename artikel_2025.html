Why England fans booed Arsenal defender Ben White again after Croatia win

Ben White returned to the England senior squad on Thursday night but received a hostile reception at Wembley.

The Arsenal defender came on as a substitute in the 78th minute of England’s 2-1 Nations League win against Croatia but was met with boos from a section of the crowd.

Thomas Tuchel has since defended White, stating that he should be in the squad. However, this raises questions as to why some England supporters have turned on the defender after he has continued to perform well for Arsenal.

The answer lies with the 2022 World Cup in Qatar.

He did not play in the first two group games for England and missed the last group game against Wales due to illness.

There were reports that White had fallen out with assistant manager Steve Holland, but neither the FA nor England management confirmed this was the reason he left.

Gareth Southgate later stated there were no problems between White and Holland and that White had simply withdrawn himself from selection after the tournament.

This meant that White would never play for England again while Southgate was still manager. Some fans were clearly upset by this, as they felt White was shunning his country.

When White returned under Tuchel in March 2026, he was met with a negative reaction. He was booed by the crowd when he scored his first-ever international goal against Uruguay. White was also booed in the same fashion against Japan.

He has since explained that supporters might view the situation differently if they knew the full story. However, he has chosen not to reveal the private details behind his departure.

Tuchel has shown little concern about White being affected by the controversy.

The England boss said after the win on Thursday that he had not heard any boos and praised White’s qualities, work ethic and desire to play for his country.

He believes White can help England win games and added that he has the backing of the coaching staff.

Arsenal fans will be unsurprised to learn that White is still a valuable asset for Mikel Arteta, offering defensive consistency and being able to play as a right or centre-back.

The bad press that White is receiving from England fans will not go away any time soon. However, he has not done anything to hurt his reputation at club level. The reasons why he left for the World Cup are personal.

The main focus for Arsenal will be getting him match fit again. As for the England fans, we shall see.
