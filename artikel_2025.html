‘So valuable’ - Brighton confirm major contract breakthrough for club legend ahead of Hull City

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Brighton have confirmed Pascal Gross has agreed a new contract until June 2028.

The Germany international, who made 261 appearances during his first spell with Albion, rejoined the club in January after 18 months in the Bundesliga with Borussia Dortmund.

The 35-year-old is poised to make his 300th Albion appearance in Sunday's Premier League match against Hull City.

Head coach Fabian Hurzeler said: "We are really pleased that Pascal has signed a new contract.

"We can talk about his many qualities on the pitch - his goals, assists and football intelligence - but he is also a great leader within the group.

"He connects different generations and different nationalities in our squad. He's so valuable because he helps everyone understand what it takes to be successful."

Pascal joined Albion from Ingolstadt in 2017 and played a key role in establishing the club in the Premier League, becoming one of its most influential players during his first seven seasons.

The midfielder helped Albion secure its highest-ever league finishes and qualification for the Europa League in 2023 and the Conference League last season.

He has been involved in 87 Premier League goals, scoring 35 times and providing 52 assists, the joint-highest total of goal involvements by a German player in the competition's history.

His performances for Albion earned him a first senior Germany call-up in 2023. He has won 19 caps and featured at this summer's FIFA World Cup.

Hurzeler, speaking ahead of Brighton’s trip to Hull, said: “He connects different generations, and we have different generations in our team.

“He connects different nationalities, and we have a lot of different nationalities in our team. He's very adaptive to every personality, and he finds access to every person.

“I think that's something we shouldn't underestimate, and therefore he's so valuable because he gives everyone an understanding of what it needs to be successful.

“No matter if you are 19, 20 years old, no matter if you are 27 or 32, and no matter where you come from, it's all about our standards.

“He's a great standard driver, culture driver, together with Jason (Steele), together with Lewis (Dunk). They are very helpful in driving these things, and therefore I think that's his biggest impact.

“Of course, we can speak about all his goals and assists and football understanding, but being that connector beside the pitch is very valuable.”
