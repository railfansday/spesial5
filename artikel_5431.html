The U.S. has let Nvidia and AMD sell hundreds of thousands of AI chips to China

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

House committee records show BIS approved more than 879,000 AI chips, out of 3.4 million requested as of May

House Foreign Affairs Committee Chairman Brian Mast released export license information on Thursday showing the Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security approved more than 879,000 Nvidia $NVDA and AMD $AMD AI chips for sale to China.

The findings show requested sales of 3.4 million AI chips to China, and the approvals include exports to Chinese Military Companies designated by the Department of War, such as Alibaba and Tencent, according to the committee. The figures run through May 2026, according to CNBC.

The documents also include requests by Nvidia to rely on possible chip-smuggling fronts to facilitate sales of advanced AI chips to Chinese companies, the committee said. Mast said at Thursday's committee meeting that Maginfra and New Matrix, which appear in Nvidia's license applications, are believed to be fronts for smuggling, according to CNBC.

"Both are fake companies," Mast said. "If BIS approved the sale of these chips to these firms, obviously that is of tremendous concern to us."

Rep. Gregory Meeks, the committee's ranking member, said the panel approved the disclosure by unanimous consent at a business meeting. The release covers license information on Nvidia H200 chips and equivalent AMD chips.

"The record shows a troubling contradiction," Meeks said. "Even as there were massive delays in license reviews at BIS, the agency seemed to approve licenses for advanced chips to China with little scrutiny."

Meeks said the committee's oversight must include public hearings with Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and BIS Undersecretary Jeffrey Kessler. The Commerce Department did not immediately respond to a request for comment, according to CNBC.

Nvidia has stopped shipping licensed Hopper data center chips to China, interim CEO Colette Kress said on an earnings call on Tuesday. The Hopper family includes the H200. Nvidia booked $2.4 billion in China sales of licensed Hopper hardware in its fiscal third quarter, which ended Oct. 25. Kress gave no reason for the halt.

Washington had cleared 10 Chinese firms to buy the H200, among them Alibaba, Tencent and ByteDance, according to the House Foreign Affairs Committee. Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as CEO on Nov. 12 after admitting to an affair with a former executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based Nvidia customer. U.S. intelligence officials suspect she is an agent of Beijing's spy agencies.

Lawmakers from both parties in Congress have urged new legislation that would tighten restrictions on exports of cutting-edge AI processors to China and other rival nations, according to CNBC.
