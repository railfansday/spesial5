Aneha Calls Out ‘Love Is Blind’ Reunion Editing for Being “Egregiously Awful,” “Rude,” and “Disrespectful”

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Anyone else watch the Love Is Blind reunion and have their jaw absolutely drop from the drama? Beekeeper Matt, we did not see you coming! The episode was messy, to say the least, but apparently it was also pretty heavily edited—at least according to Aneha, who hopped onto Instagram to call out the show.

“I’m assuming at this point most of you have watched the reunion, and I have something I got to say,” Aneha said. “First of all, what the hell was that? Cam and I had the shortest segment out of all the couples, yet we’re going to make it look like I’m on this long-winded monologue and can’t shut up and I’m annoying, and pan to everybody being disinterested in what I have to say? How incredibly rude and disrespectful.”

She added, “I was asked to go on that state to tell my side, to say why Cam and I had broken up. And to make it look like no one gives an absolute flying anything about what I have to say is incredibly disrespectful, rude, and ridiculous. What the hell? Every single person on that stage, I had the decency, patience, and time to listen to them.”

“Make it make sense. Hello?” Aneha continued. “The editing for that is incredibly egregiously awful and genuinely, I think they did me a favor. If they could edit me to make it look like whatever it was, and that I’m so annoying and I just keep talking, whereas making everyone else who talked for probably two to three times as long as I, and make them look like they’re succinct and direct, to the point? But make me look crazy? Just think about how editing went for the rest of the season.”
