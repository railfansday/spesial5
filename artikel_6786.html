Meet the Husbands, Partners, and Exes of the ‘Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County’ Cast

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Exactly one week before the premiere of The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County, OG MomTok member Jen Affleck shocked fans by filing for divorce from her husband of seven years, Zac Affleck. Of course, if you’ve been paying attention, then this update isn’t all that surprising…save for the fact that Jen is currently pregnant with her and Zac’s fourth child. And while fans have been predicting this split since season 1 of SLOMW, chances are that there will be even more tension between Jen and Zac in this new spinoff series.

Wondering what other kind of relationship drama SLOMW:OC will bring? While most of the new cast members are in long-term, committed marriages, you can look forward to seeing the dynamic of Aspyn Ovard’s buzzy throuple with Billy and Bri Davis firsthand, as well as Bobbi Althoff’s budding romance with basketball player Tyler Hawkins. Here’s a rundown on everyone’s relationships ahead of the November 12 premiere.

Current relationship status: Married to Nathan Pease

Ashleigh tied the knot with Nate in September 2013, and four kids later, they still seem rock-solid.

Current relationship status: Dating Billy Davis and Bri Davis

Just about two years filing for divorce from Parker Ferris in April 2024, Aspyn made headlines when she revealed she was in a relationship with married couple Billy and Bri Davis, who practice consensual non-monogamy. She later explained that the relationship started with just Bri before she became involved with Billy as well.

Current relationship status: Married to David Woods

Avery married former police officer David back in June 2016 and they still seem very much in love. David also has two kids from a previous relationship.

Current relationship status: Dating Tyler Hawkins

Once her divorce from Cory Althoff was finalized in August 2024, Bobbi struck up a romance with basketball player Tyler Hawkins in November 2024, per People, and two years later, they’re still going strong.

Current relationship status: Married to Dylan Higginson

Despite getting her role reduced since the initial cast announcement, it seems like Chandler and her husband Dylan (who she’s been married to since February 2016) will still make some appearances.

Current relationship status: Divorcing Zac Affleck

Number of kids: 3 (with a fourth on the way)

After seven years of marriage (and MONTHS of breakup rumors), TMZ broke the news that Jen had filed for divorce from Zac. The two tied the knot in June 2019 after meeting on the Mormon dating app Mutual the year before.

Current relationship status: Married to Corey McClanahan

Jordan and her firefighter husband Corey (who were high school sweethearts!) have been married since February 2017 and are seemingly still head-over-heels for each other.

Current relationship status: Married to Kyler Steven Fisher

Madison got married to Kyler back in April 2013 after meeting on Facebook, and now they share five kids and a wildly popular YouTube channel.

Current relationship status: Married to Kirk DaPron

McCall and Kirk have been married since September 2011 and (though they recently faced backlash for their controversial family Halloween costume) are still loved up 15 years later.

Current relationship status: Married to Aaron Brimhall

Number of kids: 2 (with a third on the way)

Despite breakup rumors, Salomé and her photographer husband Aaron (who she married in December 2012) are still together and gearing up to welcome their third child.
