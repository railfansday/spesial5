Kylie Jenner and Timothée Chalamet Swap Courtside Maximalism for Sushi Casual

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Kylie Jenner and Timothée Chalamet made something of an art form out of courtside couple dressing. Between their coordinating looks and unabashed PDA, the pair were as much a spectacle as the triumphant basketball games that played out during the Knicks’s historic run. But for a rare date night in Los Angeles, they traded the satsuma orange and Chrome Hearts theatrics for much more low-key.

The couple were spotted heading into Sushi Park, the famously unassuming West Hollywood restaurant whose second-floor strip-mall walkway has become an unofficial paparazzi runway. Kendall Jenner, Hailey Bieber, and Zoë Kravitz are among the famous faces who have turned the short walk from the parking lot to the restaurant into a street-style moment. Jenner and Chalamet, however, kept things casual. Jenner wore an oversized black leather jacket over a simple white tank top, paired with relaxed blue jeans and Khaite’s minimal Sophie sandals in patent leather. Her long, dark hair fell in loose waves, while her makeup was similarly pared back. Chalamet opted for a black zip-up jacket from Adon (a London-based brand the actor looks to frequently for slick suede and leather bombers, as does Robert Pattinson) layered over a dark T-shirt, loose black suede trousers also from Adon, and a fine gold chain at his neck.

It's a considerable departure from their more exuberant recent public appearances, particularly their courtside outings, where Chalamet embraced his playful and eccentric approach to getting dressed thanks to a close partnership with Chrome Hearts and his enduring penchant for oversized streetwear.

For Jenner, the pared-back ensemble also comes hot on the heels of one of her most theatrical fashion moments yet. Just weeks ago, she made a surprise appearance at the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show, donned the famed Fantasy Bra in a glittering, jewel-encrusted look complete with feathered wings for her official runway debut. Of course, in recent years Jenner has relied on a more laidback day-to-day uniform, whether in her own KHY leather pieces or new denim launches.

But remember: we’re hurtling toward the festive and awards seasons (plus, the Dune: Part Three press tour), where Kylie Jenner and Timothée Chalamet will certainly find their maximalist groove once again.
