Who wins EU public contracts in your niche? Build a competitor list from TED award notices

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Disclosure: I'm Lindenbyte, the small studio that built the Apify Actor used below, and this post was drafted with AI assistance. TED's own search and free API work too; this is a no-code shortcut.

Most people use TED (Tenders Electronic Daily) to find tenders to bid on. The other half of the data is award notices: who won, how big they are, and for how much. That is free competitor research.

Award rows include the usual fields (title, buyer, country, date, CPV, link) plus winners, winnerCountries, winnerSize and awardedValue. Result on our check (run 2026-11-13): 200 award notices (the maxResults cap; published 17 Aug - 30 Sep 2026), 133 of them with an awarded value. Winner size split: 79 medium, 62 large, 44 small, 14 micro - so roughly 29% of winners were small or micro firms.

Export as JSON (keeps winners as a list) and a few lines of Python do the ranking:

In our sample, value was dominated by a few large frameworks (the top four awards were EUR 182M, 82M, 59M and 32M), while counting wins instead surfaced repeat winners (one firm won 9 lots, another 4). Rank both ways: by value to see who owns the market, by count to see who wins consistently.

Check three things: who wins repeatedly, whether winners are SMEs (winnerSize) - proof small firms can win in this segment - and which buyers keep awarding, who are your best outreach targets.

Not every award notice lists the value; below-threshold national tenders are not on TED; winner names come as published (no company-registry matching).

Cost: about $3 per 1,000 results plus half a cent per run, so 200 awards is roughly $0.60.
