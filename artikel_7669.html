Washington and Lee University law students demand resignation of dean

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

An investigation last week by the Washington Post reported that Tate Weston, a graduate of Tulane University, was admitted to the Washington and Lee University School of Law after accusations of sexual assault of a fellow student. (Photo from Shutterstock)

Students at the Washington and Lee University School of Law are demanding that the law dean and other administrators resign after the school admitted a man with an open sexual assault case and an alleged cover-up during his time on campus.

An investigation last week by the Washington Post reported that Tate Weston, a graduate of Tulane University, was admitted to the law school after accusations of sexual assault of a fellow student. He pleaded guilty this month of felony second-degree battery and misdemeanor sexual battery.

Despite the university president and the board of trustees knowing about the situation, Weston was not put on leave until last month.

Now, 38 law students are calling for the immediate resignation of law dean Melanie D. Wilson—along with the associate dean of law student affairs, community and belonging; the assistant dean of law admissions; and the Title IX coordinator.
