Endrick deals blow to Milan with declaration of Real Madrid love: “Spend my life there”

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

AC Milan have been dealt a blow in their pursuit of Endrick by the words of the Real Madrid forward himself.

Overnight, TNT Sports Brazil reported that Milan were readying an offer for Endrick. With a loan deal viewed as being ‘off the table’, Gerry Cardinale is apparently willing to sanction a bid of €60m plus €10m in bonuses to try land the Brazilian on a permanent.

The 20-year-old scored in Brazil’s dominant 7-0 friendly win against Singapore, one that ensured that his national team finished the calendar year on a positive note under the guidance of former Milan boss Carlo Ancelotti, someone who has urged him to try and play more regularly.

As relayed by GOAL, the former Palmeiras starlet spoke after the match to reporters and he refuted any notion that he is unhappy with his lack of regular starts at Real Madrid.

“I am always available to help Real Madrid, which is the biggest club in the world. I hope to be able to spend my life there to help Real and my team-mates,” he said.

“It was a good game [the win over Singapore]. We knew what kind of game it would be and it was very important for us to close the year. We were able to apply what we practiced, and now it’s about continuing that.”
