Opinion: Awful Liverpool star leaves Andoni Iraola smiling, this was brilliant

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

One Liverpool star was pretty awful against Manchester United, all things considered. But that will only leave Andoni Iraola smiling.

Liverpool were wonderful against Manchester United on Sunday. They tore them to shreds at Anfield, dominating the fixture and enjoying some incredible performances.

Florian Wirtz and Rio Ngumoha stole the show in most peoples' eyes. Both finished with a goal and assist, leading the attack beautifully as United struggled badly to cope with either of them.

They were fantastic on and off the ball - it was their movement to receive passes that troubled the visitors as much as anything. Of course, what they did with it ultimately won the match.

It was a good thing, too, as other parts of the attack didn't really show up. One pretty awful display will have given Andoni Iraola reason to smile, however.

This might have been the worst Alexander Isak has looked in front of goal for Liverpool. The no.9 had plenty of chances but spurned them all, some very badly.

And yet, that's a good thing. Or at least, the good thing is that it didn't matter one bit because Liverpool weren't reliant on Isak for goals at Anfield - and that's certainly not been the case this season.

Isak has scored plenty to kick off this Premier League season and they've been important goals, too. He had a goal against Nottingham Forest that ultimately secured a point, and two against Ipswich Town that would have been a 0-0 otherwise.

His goals against Brentford and Bournemouth were both winners, too. Liverpool's 2-0 win over Brighton was secured his Isak's goal - the other was an own goal from Pascal Gross.

Really, the 89th-minute goal against Crystal Palace in a 3-1 win is the only time Liverpool had a goal from Isak that didn't strictly matter. All his other ones have been incredibly important.

In fact, that win against United is Liverpool's first in the Premier League under Iraola that didn't feature a goal from Isak. That is certainly a good thing - great, even - as it's evidence of the squad not having an over-reliance on a forward.

Over-reliance is a dangerous thing, after all. It can leave teams paralysed when they're missing their star goalscorer but Liverpool were able to comfortably cope without Isak's shooting boots.

That's necessary to move forward as a team under Iraola.
