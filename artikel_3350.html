Julien Baker Reveals ‘This World or the Next,’ Their First New Album in Six Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Julien Baker fans, rejoice: the musician will release This World or the Next, their first new album in six years, on Feb. 12, 2027.

Baker revealed the news alongside the new single “Hound,” which is also the opening track on This World or the Next. It kicks off with bare piano, before erupting in shredding guitars and chaos. “Love is the separation between yourself and others finally disintegrating,” they sing. Check out the video below, directed by Joshua Cannon.

This World follows 2021’s Little Oblivions, and their massive full-length debut with boygenius in 2024. The new album is a return to Baker’s hardcore roots, where they reunited with their collaborators Matt Gilliam and Calvin Lauber (Baker and Gilliam used to play in the band Forrister). They self-produced the LP, and cut it at Lucy’s Meat Market in Los Angeles.

The announcement arrives as Baker, Gilliam, and Lauber are on their month-long rock club residency tour, which will conclude with three nights at Seattle’s Neumos on Nov. 19 through 21. They’ve been performing This World songs on tour, opening each set with “Lathe.”

Prior to this trek, Baker played a surprise pop-up show at Jack White’s Third Man Records in Nashville on Sept 9, and a solo show in Nantucket, Massachusetts, in late August. They’ve also been performing with their boygenius bandmates this year, joining Lucy Dacus onstage during her Outside Lands set, and Bridgers on her Lost Tour (alongside Dacus) in New York and L.A.
