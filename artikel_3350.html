Bruce Dickinson Already Has a New Solo Album and Tour Dates on Deck for Iron Maiden’s Break

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Bruce Dickinson must be determined to be the hardest-working man in hard rock and metal. He’s currently on tour in Australia with Iron Maiden — frankly too busy to attend the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, which inducted Maiden on Saturday — but here he is announcing a new solo album, PsychoGod, with a cinematic music video that doubles as an allegory about the prison industrial complex for the single “Speaking in Tongues.” The album will come out March 12, and he has solo tour dates scheduled all around Europe in between Iron Maiden’s North American trek. And it’s only been two years since Dickinson released his last solo album, The Mandrake Project.

Dickinson sings the PsychoGod song “Speaking in Tongues” with his trademark urgency. Its intro recalls Maiden’s “Fear of the Dark,” but it soon becomes a unique plea for understanding all leading to the revelation: “Tell the truth, and the world will know just what you mean.”

“‘Speaking in tongues’ is an expression used to describe a form of religious or sacred ecstasy in which celebrants often lose control of normal speech and bodily functions,” Dickinson said in a statement. “Their search for truth at the expense of sanity contrasts sharply with the shallow world of the Internet, social media and the narcissist political classes that seem to dominate our lives.”

The video, which Dickinson co-directed with Leo Liberti, shows the singer both shouting at the heavens in a Nevada desert as his band plays and under arrest in orange prison gear as he’s accused of trafficking drugs in L.A. There are cartoons of people wagging their tongues (including tongue-touching nuns) all as bigwigs laugh at his misfortune.

Dickinson said in a statement that the video is the first of five interconnected stories, “a karmic journey” connected by the album’s songs. “As a first video, ‘Speaking in Tongues’ is brutal, violent and [it] holds a mirror to the ugly global world of accusation, incarceration, conspiracy, and lies. Dickinson and his band recorded the album live at Dave Grohl’s Studio 606 with a guest list that includes Devin Townsend and Sepultura’s Andreas Kisser and Derrick Green. Grohl, Slash, and members of Anthrax and Angra all stopped by the sessions as well.

The album, which the singer says “has some very personal moments,” will be available on CD, double-LP vinyl, and a CD-Blu-ray combo that includes a Dolby Atmos mix and three music videos. The singer had to obtain permission to use the names of Lord of the Rings characters from J.R.R. Tolkien’s estate for “Eye of Sauron.” “It is an allegorical use of the Lord of the Rings characters to illuminate the tragic world of strife that exists all around us in our supposedly civilised world, in particular the numerous wars started in the name of conquest and greed,” he explained in a statement.
