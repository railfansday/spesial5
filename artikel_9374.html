The Vitamin D Schedule That Came Out Ahead For Sleep

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Almost 90 adults tried two vitamin D schedules.

Almost 90 adults tried two vitamin D schedules.

Daily vitamin D supplementation may offer an advantage over weekly treatment for sleep quality in adults with a deficiency.

Scores for sleep duration and daytime functioning were better in the daily group after eight weeks.

Overall sleep quality scores also favoured daily treatment, although the weekly group had better scores for sleep medication use.

Vitamin D could influence sleep through pathways involved in regulating the body’s sleep–wake cycle.

A previous review found some evidence of improved sleep quality with supplementation, while results for individual sleep problems were less consistent (Abboud, 2022).

“Daily vitamin D3 supplementation is more effective than weekly supplementation in improving overall sleep quality, sleep duration, and daytime dysfunction in vitamin D3-deficient adults.”

The trial involved almost 90 adults with vitamin D deficiency, whose average age was around 39.

Participants were randomly assigned to receive either 6,000 IU daily or 60,000 IU weekly for eight weeks.

The daily regimen provided 42,000 IU per week, so the groups differed in both dosing frequency and total dose.

Sleep timing is influenced by an internal body clock that follows a rhythm of roughly 24 hours.

Light reaching the eyes helps keep that clock aligned with the cycle of day and night.

As evening approaches, the brain normally releases more melatonin, helping the body prepare for sleep.

Bright artificial light late at night can interfere with this signal, while time spent outdoors and physical activity can also affect these rhythms.

Sunlight has another role in the body, enabling exposed skin to produce vitamin D.

People who spend little time outdoors may have more difficulty obtaining enough of the vitamin, although food and supplements provide additional sources.

Oily fish, including salmon and mackerel, are among the best natural food sources, while egg yolks provide smaller amounts.

The study was published in Neurology India (Lalitha et al., 2026).
