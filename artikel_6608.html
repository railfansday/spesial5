BEC (Bacon, Egg &amp; Cheese) Puffs

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A tang from Grey Poupon Dijon Mustard livens these custardy and crunchy bite-size snacks.

Landing somewhere in between a New York-style bacon, egg, and cheese (BEC) and a quiche Lorraine, these bite-size appetizers are sure to be party-pleasers. Tangy Grey Poupon Dijon Mustard, creamy Gruyère, and smoky bacon are whisked with egg and poured into prepared mini phyllo shells for a simple starter that comes together in a half-hour.
