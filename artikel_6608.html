The New Bon Appétit Cooking App Is Here!

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

This exciting new tool is more than a digital version of our extensive archive of recipes. The app is designed to help you plan and pull off great meals, whether you’re a novice cook or a seasoned chef. You’ll be able to explore curated menu collections from our editors, personalize your homepage by noting dietary preferences, and save your favorites to build your own collection. It also includes videos from the BA Test Kitchen, teaching you techniques so every recipe comes out perfectly.

The new Bon Appétit app also puts recipes from our sister brands right at your fingertips. You’ll be able to access thousands of recipes from Epicurious, classics from Gourmet magazine, as well as excerpts from beloved chefs and cookbook authors including José Andrés and Samin Nosrat.

We hope our new app will inspire you to make many great meals.

Bon appétit,Jamila RobinsonEditor in chief
