42 Bridal Clutches, Purses, and Handbags to Carry on Your Big Day

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

All products featured on Vogue are independently selected by our editors. However, we may earn affiliate revenue on this article and commission when you buy something.

The star of any wedding-day look is undeniably the dress—however, the right accessories are an easy way to sneak in a little personality. Take, for example, the bridal clutch. It’s by no means an essential, but if you do choose to carry one, you might as well use it to add an extra-special touch to your day. From pearlescent minaudières befitting a beach ceremony to elegant envelope clutches ideally suited for City Hall, the best wedding bags both complement your carefully put-together outfit and act as an emergency catch-all for the little things you’ll want on hand, like lipstick and handwritten vows. (It’s never a bad idea to have an extra safety pin within reach, too.)

To narrow down a potential style for your bridal handbag, look for something that fits the aesthetic and theme of the wedding overall. For a city venue, this might mean a sleek, elongated clutch by Hunting Season or a structured mini tote by Le Sundial. Let your creativity run wild if you’re planning a destination wedding, perhaps with a hand-embellished piece, or keep things classic a leather envelope. Still looking for your something blue? A powder-blue clutch is a subtle way to honor the age-old adage. And if you want your purse to make more of a statement, consider metallics—gold and chainmail offer fun twists. It’s your day; you make the rules.

Ahead, discover 42 options designed to delight every type of bride.

Tasseled bags are having a moment, and the trend has even made its way down the aisle. Opt for a pared-back style with Marlies Grace, embrace your vintage-loving side with Anna Sui, or lean into opulence with JuJu Vera’s golden clutch.

Think of a metallic clutch as one of your most important pieces of jewelry. Liffner’s golden design and Savette’s polished silver style are bound to catch the light just as a statement ring would. They’re also a great way to veer from traditional bridal white purses.

There’s no easier way to embrace the something blue tradition than via a clutch. Whether you prefer a paler blue from Self-Portrait, a cornflower-hued drawstring pouch by Prada, or a navy piece from Staud, a blue bag brings a necessary pop of color to your bridal wardrobe.

A satin clutch is perhaps the most classic handbags you can carry as a bride; its subtle shine flatters nearly every wedding dress fabric, from crepe to tulle. Choose a modern design by Attersee or a softly gathered pouch with floral detailing by Sophie Buhai—you can’t go wrong either way.

A clutch can be as much an objet d’art as an accessory. Simone Rocha’s micro egg clutch and Erdem’s mini bag with a rose-shaped handle both feel more like sculptures than purses. Let either take center stage against a minimalist gown for maximum impact.

Of all the bridal clutches, leather is the one you’ll likely reach for long after the honeymoon. Porto and J.Crew keep things minimal; Dior and Métier add a hint of gold detailing. Best of all, each slips easily into your everyday rotation, so consider it an investment that will earn its keep.

Looking to add an extra dose of glamour to your wedding-day outfit? An embellished clutch is an easy way to introduce drama. Altuzarra and Manolo Blahnik’s designs sparkle with allover crystals, while pearls on pieces by Valentino and Simkhai offer a more romantic alternative.

What kind of bag do you need for your wedding?

Something small will suffice here: You’ll have other people keeping tabs on the essentials, so think of this as a little extra accessory you can use to elevate your ensemble on the big day. White and ivory are classic choices, but gold, silver, or a pop of blue work just as well.

Since this is an optional piece (many people tend to hand off things like cell phones and other bulkier items to their bridesmaids or members of the bridal party), it’s really up to you. Use it to hold the things you might need for touch-ups throughout the day, like lipstick or breath mints, or pop in a disposable camera for the after-party. The contents of your wedding purse are really up to you.

A wedding clutch can be many things—and it doesn’t have to be a clutch at all. For functionality, pick something with a strap (like a tiny crossbody bag) to keep your hands free. If you do go the clutch bag route, choose something with embellishments to add some sparkle. Whether it’s shimmering pearls, iridescent appliqués, or gleaming sequins, these evening bags are your chance to go bolder on a smaller scale.
