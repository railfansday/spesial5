Even Realities glasses are coming to a job site near you

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Even Realities is making its first big push into the enterprise business world with a new Enterprise SDK release.

Even Realities’ G2 glasses have been a hit for the company, making up 11% of the AR glasses market as of September. Now that consumers are clearly interested in what they can do, Even Realities is branching out to the business world with the release of the company’s first Enterprise SDK (software development kit).

The SDK works well with Even Realities’ app-based software, which lets users browse a bespoke app store filled with games, utilities, and more, all of which are designed to be used on the Even Realities G2’s in-lens displays. In our Even Realities G2 review, reviewer Andrew Romero said the glasses are “for the tech minimalist who wants to stay connected to notifications, while keeping a healthy distance away.”

In the business world, that actually makes a lot of sense. XR glasses and other types of smart glasses with displays are used in fields where you might need more information in front of you but can’t be on a device like a phone. Even Realities glasses are also extremely useful for keeping up with notifications when they come through, something that would be very handy on a job site.

“We built Even G2 to help people access useful information while staying present in their everyday lives, and we believe that principle matters just as much at work. With the Enterprise SDK, we’re giving companies the flexibility to build around the way their teams already work, rather than asking them to change their workflows to fit the technology,”– Will Wang, Founder and CEO of Even Realities

The SDK will allow companies to build custom interfaces, directly integrate glasses’ inputs (microphone and motion data) into apps, and handle other traditional IT management needs like managing device connections and updates. Companies can sign up on the Even Realities enterprise page.
