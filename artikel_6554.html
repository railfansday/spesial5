These Panera Snowman Cups Are Only Available for a Day—Here’s How to Get One

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Panera Snowman Cup is giving the Starbucks Bearista Cup some cozy competition. Here's what you need to know about the limited-edition mystery boxes.

I don’t generally need an incentive to stop at Panera in December. Any of those delicious soups in a bread bowl is a fairly convincing argument on its own. But this year, the chain is giving us another reason to visit: collectible glass snowman cups, with a chance to get one free.

If you’ve been admiring the Starbucks Bearista cup, these little snowmen might also deserve a spot on your wish list. Panera’s version comes in a mystery box containing one of two designs, naughty or nice. The free cup offer runs on Dec. 3, and with only 12 available at each participating cafe, there are a few details to keep in mind before you go.

The limited-edition glass cups come in two designs inspired by the naughty-and-nice theme of Panera’s holiday packaging. Each mystery box holds one snowman cup, so you won’t know which you’ve received until you open it.

There are also a few new things to drink while you admire your cute cup. Panera’s new holiday menu, rolling out this week, includes peppermint white mocha and pistachio white mocha beverages. Both flavors are available as a latte, iced coffee or Frozen Java, finished with peppermint bark pieces or pistachio pieces. The iced coffees have sweet cream cold foam, and the lattes and Frozen Java drinks get whipped cream.

On Dec. 3, the first 12 customers at each participating U.S. cafe who make a qualifying purchase of $25 or more will receive a free Snowman Cup, while supplies last. There’s a limit of one per person, so you may want to make that Panera outing a breakfast date.

You’ll need to order inside the cafe at the cashier. Kiosk, Rapid Pick-Up, drive-thru, catering and delivery orders—including those placed through third-party sites—don’t qualify.

If you don’t get your hands on one of those first 12 boxes, you’ll have another way to find a snowman. A limited number of cups will also be available through the Panera Shop for $22 each. An online sale date hasn’t been announced yet, so keep an eye on the shop if you’re hoping to grab one.
