Meet the Stylists Who Shaped Vogue’s Best-Dressed List

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

In case you missed it, Vogue’s 2026 best-dressed list has officially arrived—and on it are plenty of well-clad celebrities driving fashion forward.

While all of these A-listers come with their own distinct sense of style, their masterful stylists have played an important role in making them true red-carpet stars. As they say, “it takes a village”—so let’s pour one out for the amazing fashion minds who help craft all of the glamorous moments.

Clients on Vogue’s best dressed list: Kendall Jenner, Carey Mulligan, Greta Lee, Ayo Edebiri.

Styling approach: “My approach is always client-first. It’s important to me that what they wear on the red carpet feels authentic to who they are.”

Favored silhouette: “I’m really drawn to silhouettes that feel a little unexpected and challenge convention, but still translate naturally to the red carpet.”

Most-pulled accessory: “A Sophie Buhai teardrop pouch.”

Clients on Vogue’s best dressed list: Connor Storrie.

Styling approach: “I always start with the person rather than the clothes. For me, great styling is about understanding someone’s personality, proportions, and how they carry themselves, then building on that. With Connor, I’ve always drawn heavily on Old Hollywood, particularly the 1920s and 1930s, bringing those references into something that feels contemporary and a little unexpected. I want the clothes to enhance the person rather than overpower them. There’s nothing better than a beautifully executed look that feels completely effortless.”

Favored silhouette: “I’m really drawn to a strong shoulder, a slightly higher waist, and a beautiful, long, wide-leg trouser. There’s something incredibly flattering and seductive about those proportions. I love the elegance of traditional menswear, but with a slightly louche, almost 1970s sensibility. It’s masculine without feeling rigid, and there’s a real sense of movement and ease to it.”

Signature touch: “I think my strength is understanding proportion and knowing when to hold back. I’m much more interested in personal style than chasing trends. Sometimes the smallest adjustments to a jacket, the way a trouser falls or how a shirt sits against the body can completely transform a look. I also love finding that balance between classic elegance and a little sex appeal. Nothing should ever feel forced or as though someone’s wearing a costume.”

Most-pulled accessory: “Pinky rings. I know they’re having a real moment on the red carpet, particularly in menswear, but they’re something I find myself coming back to. Even for Connor’s most recent red carpet at the Academy Museum Gala, convention might suggest wearing just one, but we loved both Tiffany & Co. rings so much that we decided to wear one on each pinky. One was a platinum ring featuring a cat’s-eye alexandrite surrounded by diamonds, while the other was a platinum ring set with a diamond of over eight carats. Two insanely beautiful pieces, and rather than choosing between them, we thought, why not wear both? I love those little moments where you go against the grain. Sometimes breaking an unwritten fashion rule creates something far more interesting and unexpected, and therein lies the fun of styling!”

Clients on Vogue’s best dressed list: Teyana Taylor, Colman Domingo.

Styling approach: “Firstly, we consider the event and where our client is at that time in their career to decide what feels right for the moment. It’s also dependent on what our clients feel best in—making sure that they feel their truest and most confident self. Then, between the two of us, we also have to have synergy and feel good about a look, which we almost never disagree on.”

Favored silhouette: “As we like to say—never waste a waist! We always love cinched-in jackets, corsets, and waistlines and are so happy to see them being modernized on both the runway and red carpet at the moment. Right now we are seeing waistlines highlighted through accentuated peplums, exaggerated hips, and snatched bodices.”

Signature touch: “Jewelry is beyond important to us and always makes a look feel finished. Whether it’s diamonds, colored stones, or metal-based pieces, we love to use jewelry to elevate or put a twist on a look that might otherwise feel expected.”

Most-pulled accessory: “We always gravitate towards a Christian Louboutin So Kate pump for our female clients, or the Christian Louboutin Ziggissimo heeled boot for our male clients.”

Clients on Vogue’s best dressed list: Tessa Thompson, Whitney Peak.

Styling approach: “I think about the project, the event, and what we are communicating. But ultimately, I’m thinking about the woman I’m dressing, and what we have established as a fashion identity. And what makes them feel amazing.”

Favored silhouette: “I’m not sure this defines my approach. When I see a look, it’s more that I connect it to a person. It’s much more organic and romantic than just saying, ‘Minis for spring!’”

Signature touch: “My signature touch is that my clients look like they all have the best style, and that the clothes never wear them.”

Most-pulled accessory: “I love a mega earring. And a perfect little sack clutch. I just want to be Mrs. Prada!”

Clients on Vogue’s best dressed list: Olivia Rodrigo.

Styling approach: “Our approach is rooted in the individuality of each client, creating looks that feel authentic yet unexpected. We love refined and timeless silhouettes but always need a little edge, or elements that aren’t so perfect. Ultimately, we want every look to tell a story, feel effortless, and leave an impression.”

Favored silhouette: “We are really drawn to the two extremes right now—either something incredibly sculpted and structured, or fluid with beautiful draping. How a look moves, especially on the carpet, is so impactful.”

Signature touch: “Our signature touch is finding the balance between femininity and edge—we love taking something traditionally beautiful and introducing an unexpected element via a shoe, accessory, fabric, or silhouette.”

Most-pulled accessory: “Our most pulled accessory is often a rare or untraditional shoe! We don’t always use them, but we are obsessed with interesting shoes.”

Clients on Vogue’s best dressed list: Rebecca Hall, Morgan Spector.

Styling approach: “I always start with the person rather than the clothes. Understanding who they are, what makes them feel confident, and how they want to be perceived is essential. When putting together a look, I’m already considering the final image. The clothes, accessories, and glam all play a part, as does the way the client carries the look, how they pose, and how it’s photographed. The best looks are a creative collaboration between stylist, glam, and the client.”

Favored silhouette: “Long column silhouette with a strong neckline.”

Signature touch: “Clean lines with a touch of drama or tension.”

Most-pulled accessory: “Black pointed toe pumps.”

Clients on Vogue’s best dressed list: Daisy Edgar-Jones, Kendall Jenner

Styling approach: “I always say I try to see my client’s style, just through my eyes. It always starts and ends with them, and that excitement when our visions align is when the relationship is magic.”

Favored silhouette: “Knee-length skirts (even better if it’s a skirt suit), medium-sized bags, and square-toe heels—I can never get enough.”

Signature touch: “Chic and cool. That combo is always what underwrites my vision. I’m always drawn to looks that say something but are still timeless—that make a moment in one part being really interesting, and the rest being really fluid, classic, or refined.”

Most-pulled accessory: “Black satin slingback pumps and great sunglasses.”

Clients on Vogue’s best dressed list: Louisa Jacobson.

Styling approach: “Thinking about who is being dressed, and the event they are attending, is paramount. Being ‘appropriately dressed’ (whatever that may mean) is important, but also keeping in mind who the client is and what they want to convey with their look. From there, as confirmations begin to roll in, I start to think about the type of jewelry that will complement the look, and I continue onwards with shoes and a bag. I really like to keep a tight edit of things, but make sure that the pieces all work well.”

Favored silhouette: “I’m all about tailoring. A beautifully-tailored suit is timeless in my opinion. However, it’s fun playing with different proportions and materials to keep it fresh.”

Signature touch: “I don’t know that it is unique per se, but I really want to make sure whomever I am dressing feels comfortable and super confident in their look. That is not to say I won’t suggest things that may be slightly out of their comfort zone—because I think that it’s important to try new things.”

Most-pulled accessory: “I tend to favor a simple shoe shape. I am a big fan of Gianvito Rossi’s Portofino and Elle styles. I tend to have both styles at just about every fitting, because they work so well.”
