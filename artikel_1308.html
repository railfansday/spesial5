Tottenham have retained their first-option matching rights on Harry Kane, despite the England captain signing a new mega-contract with Bayern Munich that makes him Europe’s highest-paid player.

The 33-year-old striker has committed his future to the Bundesliga giants following his historic Ballon d’Or win, with his new deal reportedly making him the highest-paid footballer in Europe.

Despite Kane tying his immediate future to Bayern, The Telegraph reveals that Tottenham’s leverage has not changed.

The report explains that as part of Kane’s £100 million transfer to Bayern in 2023, Spurs secured first-option matching rights that would allow them to match an offer accepted by Bayern from another Premier League club.

More importantly, the agreement is between the two clubs and understood to apply throughout Kane’s time at Bayern, rather than being tied to the duration of his original contract.

That means the striker’s latest extension does not automatically invalidate Tottenham’s rights.

While media continues to link the player with a return at every opportunity, the immediate chances of Kane returning to North London remain slim given his success and comfort in Munich.

However, the dream of a sensational one last ride in English football remains alive.

Kane sits on 213 Premier League goals, just 47 goals behind Alan Shearer’s all-time record of 260.
