Pochettino Must Make Lewis Hall First Man Utd Signing After 10/10 Display

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Now Mauricio Pochettino has been confirmed as the new Manchester United manager, attentions are already turning to who could follow him to Old Trafford during the January transfer window.

Fabrizio Romano has already confirmed that Man United are expected to make at least one signing in the New Year, and it could even be more depending on the opportunities available to them, with centre-back and striker seen as two priority positions for the winter market.

Until then, Pochettino must work with the players he has, and he will be looking to pick up the win needed to take the Red Devils out of the Premier League relegation zone when they face rivals Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday in his first match in charge.

The Argentine won't have too long to work with his players on the training ground ahead of the trip to Merseyside, and he will be hoping to have some of his experienced players available to face Andoni Iraola's men.

There is currently a chance that the likes of Marcus Rashford, who Pochettino is said to love, Harry Maguire and Luke Shaw could be fit to face the Reds after fitness issues.

The left-back position was a big problem for Carrick in the latter stages of his reign at Old Trafford, with Shaw's absence seeing the 45-year-old use Patrick Dorgu and also Diogo Dalot there out of position, as United struggled majorly from a defensive point of view.

The position was one that United wanted to strengthen during the summer window, but they were unable to find the right deal and instead focused their attentions on signing three central midfield players.

They would have hoped that would have been a safe decision after Shaw played all 38 Premier League matches last term, but his injury issues have surfaced again to cause the Red Devils problems.

One of United's key left-back targets during the summer window was Newcastle United star Lewis Hall, and he looks to be the perfect long-term replacement for Luke Shaw at Old Trafford.

Hall's price tag and the fact that they had already sold Anthony Gordon, Sandro Tonali and Bruno Guimaraes during the summer made a deal impossible, before the 22-year-old signed a new five-year deal to extend his stay at St James' Park.

That could make any move for Hall even more difficult, but he produced a reminder of why he would be the perfect signing for United during England's brilliant 3-2 win against Spain on Sunday night.

Pochettino loved Shaw's attributes as a young left-back at Southampton, and Hall continues to display the same traits as he continues to establish himself as one of the best young left-backs in Europe.

England's full-backs were both outstanding in Madrid as Thomas Tuchel's men produced one of their best wins in years, ending Spain's 43-match unbeaten run as a result.

Hall was up against Lamine Yamal in the Metropolitano Stadium, but he dealt with the Barcelona star with ease, also providing a big threat from an attacking point of view too.

Those are the exact traits Pochettino wants from his full-backs, and the Magpies star was labelled 'world-class' and handed a 10/10 rating by talkSPORT pundit Adrian Durham for his performance in the Spanish capital.

If United really intend to make big strides under the Argentine manager, they need to make another big investment in their defence in 2027, and Hall should be their number-one left-back target.

Newcastle may see Hall as a player worth around £100m now, having sold Tonali for a similar fee to Tottenham, but that shouldn't dissuade the Red Devils' hierarchy moving for a player who can be their left-back for many years.
