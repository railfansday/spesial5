Frustrated Cole Palmer shares frosty exchange with Joao Pedro after upsetting Chelsea staff member before walking off down the tunnel after 4-1 drubbing by Leeds

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Published: 16:00 EST, 21 November 2026 | Updated: 19:04 EST, 21 November 2026

Cole Palmer was involved in a frustrated exchange with team-mate Joao Pedro after Chelsea crashed to a 4-1 home defeat against Leeds.

The Blues were beaten by Leeds at Stamford Bridge for the first time since 1999, despite having taken the lead through Emmanuel Emegha's first goal for the club.

Noah Okafor scored twice for Leeds either side of a Dominic Calvert-Lewin penalty, with Brenden Aaronson adding a fourth in stoppage time.

Palmer, who opted against going around the pitch applauding the remaining fans at full-time, was captured approaching Joao Pedro following the match.

The Chelsea star could be seen covering his mouth while speaking to his team-mate, who had been left to watch from the sidelines as he continues his recovery from injury.

Joao Pedro put his hand on Palmer's shoulder as he responded to his team-mate, with the Brazilian then shrugging his shoulders as a frustrated Palmer went to walk down the tunnel.

Chelsea boss Xabi Alonso was asked post-match about the exchange, amid a suggestion Joao Pedro and Willie Isa, Chelsea's player support and development officer, had attempted to persuade Palmer to applaud the fans.

‘No, we were disappointed, the fans were disappointed because the expectations from them are high,' Alonso responded.

'We feel sorry for them and we need to go through together and to find solutions to that and now, as I said, for sure it’s not the easiest moment.

‘It’s a fragile moment but we need to work together and stick together because there is still such a long way to go.'

Alonso's side have now lost three of their last four Premier League matches, having been beaten at home by rivals Tottenham and falling to defeat at Sunderland prior to the international break.

The Blues have now dropped to eighth in the table, with Alonso's side having won five, drawn one and lost five of their 11 matches to date.

Palmer had begun the season brightly under Alonso by scoring in their opening two fixtures but has only scored one league goal since, netting with a penalty in a 2-0 win over Man United.

Chelsea will look to bounce back when they face struggling Nottingham Forest and Crystal Palace in their next two matches, before blockbuster clashes with Liverpool and Man City.
