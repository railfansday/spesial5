Calvin Bassey features on Mauricio Pochettino’s January target list

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

While Mauricio Pochettino might not have officially arrived at Old Trafford since putting pen to paper on a two-and-a-half-year deal on Thursday, the new Manchester United boss is already putting together his plans ahead of the January transfer window.

Already shipping 19 Premier League strikes so far this season and with only the duo of Crystal Palace and top-flight newcomers Ipswich holding a worst defensive tally, United are desperately seeking reinforcements when the window reopens.

Watching Argentinian international Lisandro Martinez unleash a remarkable attack on the Old Trafford hierarchy last month, Pochettino has a new central defender at the top of his January wishlist.

With the new Man United boss expected to be heavily backed, The Telegraph reported on Sunday that Fulham’s Calvin Bassey will feature on Pochettino’s potential targets.

Enjoying an essential resurge prior to November’s international break and finding a route out of the Premier League’s drop zone, Bassey was appointed as Fulham’s club captain by new boss Alvaro Arbeloa.

However, with his current deal at Craven Cottage set to expire next summer and failing to agree on a contract extension in west London, the Nigerian international reportedly has an admirer in Pochettino.

Previously linked with a potential move to Manchester United last summer and with it suggested that the Old Trafford hierarchy have long since been keeping tabs on the ex-Rangers star, Fulham will be bracing themselves for a January assault.
