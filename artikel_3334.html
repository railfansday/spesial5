‘90210’ Alums Tori Spelling & Brian Austin Green To Star In Vertical Microdrama For VertTV

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Tori Spelling and Brian Austin Green have been tapped to star in a new vertical microdrama for vertTV.

The Beverly Hills, 90210 stars will reunite in the vertical series, which recently wrapped production in Los Angeles.

Carol Potter, who played Cindy Walsh, the mom of twins Brenda and Brandon Walsh in the Fox series, will also be featured in the microdrama.

The as-yet untitled vertTV thriller is written and directed by Theo Buckwald and produced by Barry Barnholtz, Peter Sullivan, Ben Rosenthal, Brian Nolan, and Jeffrey Schenck. Plot details are being kept under wraps

VertTV’s most recent original series, the supernatural thriller Bite Club, premiered on the app last week following its world premiere at the ALZA Festival in New York City earlier this month. It reunites horror icons Barbara Crampton and Russell Todd on the 40th anniversary of their cult classic, Chopping Mall.. Watch on Deadline

Other shows on the vertical streaming app include Finding Hope (reuniting Fuller House favorites Jodie Sweetin, John Brotherton, and Marla Sokoloff alongside Richard Karn) and Chonda (starring Chonda Pierce and Regan Burns), which broke ground as the vertical microseries format’s first multicam sitcom.

Based in Los Angeles, vertTV continues to elevate mobile entertainment by pairing traditional L.A. production infrastructure, experienced local crews, and veteran Hollywood screenwriters with fast-paced vertical storytelling. Powered by human intelligence, the studio remains deeply committed to authentic artistry, shooting every series with real actors on real sets without the use of generative AI.

Vertical microdramas continue to gain traction as they meet viewers where they spend most of their time on — mobile phones. Peacock recently announced the premiere of Bravo’s first scripted microdrama titled Charmed by a Southern Monster, starring Craig Conover, star of the reality series Southern Charm.

The Bravo Original scripted vertical microdrama premieres Monday, November 9, on the Peacock mobile app, with Nicole Mattox co-starring alongside Conover. In Charmed by a Southern Monster, Conover plays Hunter Caldwell while Mattox plays Maeve Marchant. The series blends soapy romance, workplace rivalry, and small-town mystery.
