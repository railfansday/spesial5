Stock Market Today, Nov. 12: Nvidia CEO's Stunning Exit Sends Wall Street Lower as Rivals Surge

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Markets retreated Thursday following Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation, raising new questions about the AI industry's leadership as investors shifted toward competing chipmakers.

Wall Street retreated on Thursday, November 12, as the sudden resignation of Nvidia (NVDA -4.34%) CEO Jensen Huang rattled investors and raised uncertainty surrounding the artificial intelligence industry. The S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.35%) declined 0.36% to 7,592, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -0.39%) fell 0.39% to 25,366. The Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI -0.36%) also slipped 0.36% to 52,297, reversing some of Wednesday's gains.

Selling pressure extended beyond equities as gold prices tumbled 3.02% to $3,879.60, while the benchmark 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 4.98%, approaching the psychologically important 5% threshold. Market performance was mixed across sectors, with industrials leading gains and healthcare lagging. The divergence highlighted growing investor caution as rising borrowing costs and uncertainty surrounding the AI industry's leadership weighed on market sentiment.

Advanced Micro Devices (AMD +5.86%) surged 5.86% as investors assessed potential market share opportunities following Nvidia's leadership shakeup. Broadcom (AVGO +5.54%) also rallied, gaining 5.54%, as semiconductor investors weighed the implications of heightened uncertainty surrounding Nvidia and its leadership change.

Meanwhile, Global Payments (GPN -12.01%) plunged 12.01%, ranking among the session's worst performers, while Super Micro Computer (SMCI -11.30%) tumbled 11.30%.

Nvidia's leadership shakeup introduces significant uncertainty into one of the market's most influential companies. Jensen Huang played a central role in establishing Nvidia's dominance in AI computing, and his departure raises questions about the company's long-term strategy, product development, and customer relationships. The strong gains in AMD and Broadcom suggest investors are already reassessing the competitive landscape, although Nvidia's established technology ecosystem and market position remain formidable. Its upcoming earnings report will be an important test of whether the underlying business can maintain momentum through the leadership transition.

Beyond the semiconductor industry, rising Treasury yields and signs of weakening consumer demand present additional challenges for equities. With the 10-year yield approaching 5% and housing affordability under pressure, investors face the prospect of tighter financial conditions heading into 2027. While corporate earnings have remained relatively resilient, elevated valuations leave little room for disappointment. The sustainability of any year-end rally will likely depend on stronger earnings, stabilizing bond yields, and greater confidence in the Federal Reserve's policy outlook.
