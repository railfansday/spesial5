'Tracker' Is Finally Giving Colter What He Needs After 2 Seasons | Review

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It's wild to think that after three seasons on the road, Tracker Season 4 is the first time we see Colter Shaw (Justin Hartley) united with the entire cast. Seriously. Even in the first season, Colter's former handlers never appeared on-screen alongside our favorite rewardist, and the one time Colter met up with one of them in Season 2, his former tech expert was still further east in the Windy City. But now that the rest of the cast has consolidated to Denver, "Blind Spot" offers Colter the opportunity to share some screen time with everyone currently on his roster — and it's about time.

At a nice home somewhere in Denver, Colorado, a man we later learn is named Emelio Chavez (J. Anthony Pena) leaves his wife, Marie (Mercedes Colon), indoors to meet two guys named Hector Cruz (Rafael Cabrera) and Freddie (Mike Miller) in his garage. They speak briefly about a car before Chavez invites his buddies inside for some dessert his wife made — of course, they're only welcome after they dispose of a dead body wrapped in a sheet on the floor. The next day at Reenie Greene's (Fiona Rene) law office, Colter arrives and reunites with his lawyer in the flesh. It's been a while since they've seen each other, and nothing is said about her date with Russell (Jensen Ackles), so those hoping for answers will be disappointed. Colter also meets Mel Day (Cassady McClincy Zhang) for the first time, who awkwardly steps over herself during the introduction. However, Colter is there to work, and he's soon brought before Reenie's mentor, Judge Gail McGill (Amy Aquino), whose daughter, Erica (Daniela Nieves), appears to be missing. Although they haven’t spoken in years, Gail’s terminal diagnosis has led her to make amends.

Tracker works because the case changes every week and the four people solving it never do. Here is which of them you are, what it says about you, and the case for yours being the best of the four.

You would rather handle it yourself and you are usually capable of doing so. Colter is a survivalist who lives in an Airstream and takes reward money for finding missing people, which is a premise that only works because he is clearly running from something of his own.

You know somebody, and that somebody owes you a favour. Reenie is the lawyer Colter calls when a case needs a door opened legally, and she is the only person on the show who can get further with a phone call than he can on foot.

You are two steps ahead and you rarely mention it. Bobby is the one who finds the thing that unlocks the case, usually from a desk, usually about ninety seconds before anybody realises they needed it.

You turn up, change the temperature of the room, and leave people arguing about you. Russell is Colter's brother, ex-military, and the show has never once let the audience settle on whether he is a help or a problem, which is exactly why he keeps coming back.

Speaking to Gail, she admits that she wasn’t really there for her growing up after Erica's father died. Colter asks about what led to their estrangement, and Gail reveals that it was a combination of her own stubbornness and cutting her daughter off. It's been three years since they've spoken, thus far, but she hopes to rectify that. With no social media or current contacts, there's little to go on, but Gail offers Colter a letter to pass to her daughter if he does find her. “I’m going to do everything I can to find her for you.” Meeting with Randy (Chris Lee), the hacker reveals he’s unable to find literally anything — credit cards included — on Erica. The only thing that may give them an edge is an old photo from a deleted social account that leads them to Mile High Taproom, a local brewery where Erica once worked alongside a friend, Ashley Cox (Carina Conti), who appears in several photos.

Arriving at the brewery, Colter speaks with Ashley, who reveals that she thought Erica just wanted a clean break from life. However, the two have since had a falling out of their own after a dispute regarding a guy Erica had been seeing named Jeb Boyd (Jesse Luken). “I have no idea where they landed,” she tells Colter. This gives Randy some extra material to work with, however, and leads Colter to a towing company Boyd owns across town. When he arrives, the door is ajar, and Boyd has been knocked out on the floor. Upon waking up, he tells Colter that three guys beat him up over a Cadillac that Erica had repossessed the night before. Of course, when they try to call her, they have no luck.

Instead, Colter heads over to Erica's place and looks around. Randy calls to reveal that the car itself belongs to a woman named Paloma Nava, a local churchgoer with a clean record. Poking around inside, Colter doesn’t find much other than the fact that her bed comforter is missing, as is a missing curtain. That leads him to a bullet hole in the window frame and, given that the place smells like bleach, it doesn’t look too good for Erica. That's when he has Randy hack into the neighboring camera, where he sees Erica gunned down and her body taken by several goons. Later, when the cops show up, Detective Zahn (Rolando Boyce) reveals that they believe the man involved is Emelio Chavez, who headlines a mean narcotics operation in town. After being told by the detective to stand down for the time being, Reenie shows up and asks what she should tell Gail — Colter asks for more time. He just wants to find answers for her before they tell her that Erica is dead. For a minute here, it really looks as if Colter has failed, but there's, of course, more to the story.

Just then, Randy calls to reveal that Hector Cruz, Nava’s nephew, would have had access to the Cadillac. He further connects Hector to Chavez by linking him to a construction company owned by the outlaw. Colter believes that they probably have Erica’s body in the car, and so heads out to the last spot where Hector's phone pinged — which just so happens to be one of Chavez’s construction sites. Colter comes upon the goons involved, creates a distraction, and takes him out. Looking around, he finds the breach and bloody materials but not the body itself. Breaking into the office, he holds a gun at Hector and starts firing off both questions and bullets — anything to get answers. Apparently, the body they have in the car is a federal agent named Mark Sutton, an undercover DEA agent who was killed by Chavez. But Hector messed up. Having left the car long enough for Erica to repo it, they took the fight to her. Hector tells him that they took the bodies to the gas fields ten miles north, and he won't have enough time to stop them. As usual, don't tell Colter Shaw the odds.

After taking out the federal agent first, Freddie goes back for Erica's body, only for her to fight back. She survived her gunshot wound and does all she can to escape, though Chavez tackles and strangles her. Barely beating him with a rock, she runs off, still wounded. By the time Colter arrives, however, he finds the scene empty. The car is there (trunk open) and Freddie is dead, but Chavez and Erica are missing. When Colter sees the bloody handprint on the car and a trail leading away from the scene, he realizes that Erica is alive. Unfortunately, he's not the only one following her trail toward the nearby power plant. Although she flags down some help, Chavez kills the worker before he can do anything but slow him down, which signals Colter to their location.

With Chavez on her tail, Erica escapes into the plant and hides. Chavez's other men show up, and they invade the plant, looking everywhere for their loose end. Fortunately, Colter arrives in the nick of time to help, dressing Erica's wound and leading her toward the roof. When Chavez and his men enter, he takes out one of the guys and a firefight ensues. Colter beats down Chavez in a one-on-one fight, but before he can take him out to the police, Erica shoots and kills her captor. When Zahn and the rest of the reinforcements arrive, the detective thanks Colter for his help, though notes that the prints on the gun that killed the drug lord have been wiped clean.

That night, Colter visits Erica in her hospital room, who has been reading over her mother's letter. After everything, she decides that she wants to see her mother after all, prompting Gail to walk in. The pair have a heartfelt reunion as Erica finally forgives her mother, and Colter slips out to let them reconcile. Back at Reenie's office, Colter enjoys a few drinks with Reenie, Randy, and Mel. For the first time, the Tracker cast is all together, but, as per usual, Colter does his best to skip out before things get too personal — this was the same technique he used to get out of spending time with Reenie and Randy's cousin Bobby (Eric Graise) back in Season 1's "Chicago."

But before he can perform his Irish goodbye, Colter is cornered by Mel, who asks him to look into what happened to her mother. She still has questions about how exactly her mother died, and Colter agrees to take a look. It's a bit strange that Reenie hasn't brought up what she found to Colter before, but the reward seeker will no doubt be clued in soon enough. “It was nice meeting you,” he says, taking off without looking back. Far be it from Tracker to allow Colter to bond with his allies...
