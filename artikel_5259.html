Origin Protocol is one of the oldest DeFi projects on Ethereum and has been building in the crypto space for many years. The project focuses on helping users earn passive income through staking and stablecoin products in a simple, flexible way.

Its main products, Origin Ether and Origin Dollar, allow users to earn yield while still keeping control of their funds.

Origin also aims to make DeFi and NFTs easier for more people to use, with the help of its native token, OGN. The project focuses on long-term growth, user rewards, and building a sustainable system.

But many investors are now wondering about one important question: what could be the future price of OGN?

So, let’s explore the Origin Protocol price prediction for 2026, 2027, and 2030.

Origin Protocol is down 1.29% to $0.0247 over the past 24 hours, despite a broader market rally. Bitcoin gained 2.83% to trade above $80,500, supported by a post-election rally and renewed inflows into spot Bitcoin ETFs.

OGN moved in the opposite direction, this suggests some investors may be taking profits or moving funds into larger cryptocurrencies.

OGN’s 24-hour trading volume also increased 8.28% to $4.15 million. However, higher trading activity has not helped the price recover.

If OGN holds above $0.0240, it could trade sideways. A break below this level could push the price toward $0.0230. Bitcoin’s ability to stay above $80,000 may also affect OGN’s next move.

As 2026 enters its final quarter, it will be an important year for Origin Protocol. The coming months will test whether its deflationary token model can help OGN recover. Unlike inflationary DeFi tokens, OGN’s supply is shrinking while staking rewards continue, revenue increases, and TVL grows.

If more people start using OETH across DeFi and more users deposit into OUSD, the protocol’s revenue could increase, which would allow more OGN buybacks.

This could create a positive cycle where more TVL brings more fees, more fees lead to more buybacks, buybacks reduce supply, and lower supply can push the price upward.

However, this whole model depends on people actually using Origin’s yield products, not just market hype or speculation.

Origin Protocol (OGN) remains in a long-term downtrend, but the recent price action shows an attempt to build a base around the $0.02 level. OGN is currently trading near $0.02458 after a sharp September rally pushed the price briefly above $0.05, but the move was rejected, and the token quickly retraced.

Since then, the price has been consolidating between roughly $0.020 and $0.032, suggesting that the market is still undecided after the spike.

Holding the $0.020–$0.022 zone could allow OGN to recover toward the upper range near $0.032, while a breakout above this area would put the first major resistance at $0.04416 in focus.

If buyers reclaim $0.04416 with strong volume, the next major level is $0.06913. However, a breakdown below $0.020 would weaken the current base and could bring the price back toward its recent lows

If buybacks accelerate and OETH deposits rise, reduced circulating supply could push OGN toward $0.06913.

Origin Protocol believes that once OGN TVL crosses the billion-dollar milestone, staking demand will increase. Thus, the protocol revenue growth may drive the price to $0.152.

Expansion of OUSD yield integrations and multi-chain liquidity partnerships could strengthen demand, pushing OGN near $0.30.

By 2029, sustained buybacks, rising DeFi yields, and institutional liquid staking adoption may support OGN around $0.89.

If Origin evolves into a major yield infrastructure with strong TVL growth, OGN could approach $2.21.
