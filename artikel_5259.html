Arthur Hayes Says Anthropic IPO Could Determine Bitcoin’s Next Move

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Maelstrom CEO Arthur Hayes projected that the outcome of Anthropic’s IPO could impact digital assets above the current baseline. This follows a string of analysts citing fund rotation as a reason for capital flight over the last three months.

The crypto executive hinted that the much-anticipated IPO’s performance would be felt throughout the sector. Artificial Intelligence (AI) has dominated financial markets, raising expectations for the company’s stock price and related projects.

According to Hayes, a weak IPO would negatively impact digital assets and drag Bitcoin’s price lower. The top crypto dipped into the red zone, plunging trader sentiment after its price hit a new three-month low.

Conversely, a strong IPO would be bullish for crypto assets, especially Bitcoin, by boosting market confidence. Hayes’ view may stem from the year-long correlation between Bitcoin and the United States stock market, though risks remain.

“If Anthropic struggles or treads water, then it’s trouble for the entire market… I want to see Anthropic IPO perform, but what matters is the sentiment around AI, can Anthropic pull off a successful IPO. This is the bellwether for the whole story… Massive pop off the IPO, it’s game back on, it’s long everything, risk on.”

For bears, although institutional investors going bullish on tech stocks results in crypto’s win, this year has been different. Capital rotation to tech stocks occasioned mass crypto liquidations. Basically, investors dumped assets and jumped on the AI bandwagon, creating different views on the IPO’s impact.

Binance founder Changpeng Zhao cited talks around Anthropic IPO as a factor behind Bitcoin’s decline. The top crypto trades under $62,000, making a consistent decline amid a slew of macro and industry factors.

Strive CEO Matt Cole explained that Bitcoin fell 19% during the SpaceX IPO and is down 18% heading into the second-largest IPO. “What happened after the SpaceX IPO is the notable part. Bitcoin bottomed 18 days after the listing, then recovered all the way. By Day 100, it was above where it started.”

Crypto users expect a slow turnaround because institutional sentiment needed for a rebound is at sharp lows due to infamous hacks and tighter regulations.
