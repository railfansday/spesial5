BW House / Studio S+AO

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Text description provided by the architects. BW House is a renovated and extended residence designed by Studio S+AO as a private sanctuary within the city. Set on a 900-square-meter site, the house has a total building area of 1,500 square meters across three floors. Designed in an artistic modern tropical style, BW House combines minimalist architecture with a warm and contemporary character.
