Sharjah Architecture Triennial 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The third edition of the Sharjah Architecture Triennial, Architecture Otherwise: Building Civic Infrastructure for Collective Futures, curated by Vyjayanthi Rao with Associate Curator Tau Tavengwa, takes place across the Emirate of Sharjah from November 14, 2026, to April 14, 2027. The opening programme (November 14–19) brings together 35 international participants to explore how architecture can foster collective life through social relationships, shared knowledge, and civic engagement. The six-day programme features installations, participatory projects, performances, film screenings, lectures, and panel discussions, culminating in a two-day international conference on November 18–19, 2026.
