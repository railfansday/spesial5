Rams get final Micah Parsons decision from Packers ahead of Thanksgiving Eve

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Los Angeles Rams could be in for a rude awakening for their Thanksgiving Eve game against the Green Bay Packers.

With the game set to kick off on Wednesday night, teams sent in their final injury reports and the Packers revealed that edge rusher Micah Parsons will be making his season debut against the Rams.

Green Bay Packers linebacker Micah Parsons speaks with Los Angeles Dodgers staff members before a game at Dodger Stadium on Monday, April 27, 2026. Kevork Djansezian for CA Post

It's been nearly 12 months since Parsons tore his left ACL against the Denver Broncos in December of last season, and the three-time All-Pro has been steadily working his way back by thoroughly rehabbing his injured knee.

Since his rookie debut in 2021, Parsons has been a force to be reckoned with on opposing offensive lines, with five consecutive seasons where he registered over 12 sacks.

Parsons' highest number of sacks in a season came in 2023, when he tallied 14 for the Dallas Cowboys.

After a contract dispute just before the 2025 NFL season began, Parsons was traded from Dallas to Green Bay in exchange for a first-round pick in 2026 and 2027, as well as defensive tackle Kenny Clark.

Rams offensive lineman Dylan McMahon walking off the field against the Cardinals. Getty Images

Before his injury last year, Parsons had 12.5 through 14 games, and won 23 percent of his pass rushes, finishing the year third behind Nik Bonito and Nick Herbig.

Parsons will likely be monitored on how many snaps he appears in, but for the Rams, his return comes at an inconvenient time, as the team lost starting and backup centers, Coleman Shelton and Dylan McMahon — both of whom are on the team's injured reserve.

Arizona Cardinals linebacker Zaven Collins (25) sacks Los Angeles Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford (9) in Glendale, Ariz., Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/Ross D. Franklin

With the two out, Beaux Limmer, who was filling in for Steve Avila at left guard, was moved to center during the Rams' Week 10 game against the Cardinals.

Ironically, the Rams got their running game going in the second half when Limmer moved over, and finished the game running for 202 yards and were led by Blake Corum who had 17 carries for 100 yards and a touchdown.
