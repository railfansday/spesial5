Mavericks vs Warriors Preview and Injury Report: Can home cooking help Dallas figure things out?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Dallas Mavericks (7-7) host the Golden State Warriors (8-6) on Wednesday night for what was going to be an ESPN headliner that got downgraded to local television. Dallas is on a three-game skid where they've been annihilated on repeat, most recently getting walloped by the Celtics. The Warriors are on a three-game losing streak of their own, most recently getting waxed by the New Orleans Pelicans. Here's the main things to know:

WHO: Dallas Mavericks vs Golden State Warriors

WHERE: American Airlines Arena, Dallas, Texas

It's a busy injury report for the Mavericks. As of this writing (3pm CST) Santi Aldama and Walter Clayton Jr. are each listed as probable with ankle sprains. Max Christie is questionable with an ankle sprain. Tobi Lawal is doubtful with a groin injury. Sergio De Larrea, Tarik Biberovic, and Dereck Lively are all out. On the Warriors end, Steph Curry is missing time with an abdominal injury. Jimmy Butler is recovering from his ACL tear. Kristaps Porzingis is out with POTS. Brandon Williams is dealing with a hamstring issue, something that reared its head often in Dallas.

Dallas should win this one, without Curry the Warriors have looked LOST, even worse than Dallas. But I've said this about the Mavericks recently (Saturday!) and they embarrassed me. Let's hope everyone's ready to get this one over with and go play GTA6.
