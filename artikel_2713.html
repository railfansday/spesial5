Belichick and Kraft named finalists for Hall of Fame Class of 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

New England Patriots owner and longtime Patriots head coach and general manager Bill Belichick has advanced in the selection process for the Pro Football Hall of Fame Class of 2027, making them finalists for the second year in a row.

Last year was Belichick's first year of eligibility. Both were finalists but were not selected for the Hall of Fame Class of 2026. Belichick's decision not to be a first-ballot Hall-of-Famer drew severe backlash and led to significant changes in the selection process, including cutting the voting panel in half and eliminating the senior category.

During his 24 years as the head coach for New England, Belichick led the Patriots to nine Super Bowls, winning six of them. New England dominated the AFC for most of his tenure, making the playoffs in 19 of his 24 seasons. He won 31 playoff games, including one as the Cleveland Browns head coach in 1994. Kraft was the owner during Belichick's tenure.

Some have questioned whether Belichick's success was more due to quarterback Tom Brady, who will be eligible for the Hall of Fame in 2028. Regardless of whether Brady or Belichick was more responsible for their success, Belichick is still one of the most successful head coaches in NFL history with a total of 302 regular-season wins, which is the third-most in NFL history. His 333 total wins are the second-most in NFL history, behind only Don Shula. He has also coached the second-most playoff games in history, behind Andy Reid with the Kansas City Chiefs.

Despite Belichick not being a first-ballot Hall of Famer, he should surely get in this time, especially with all of the backlash over him not getting in back in 2026.
