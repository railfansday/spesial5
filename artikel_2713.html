Austin Reaves questionable on Lakers injury report for NBA Cup clash vs. Spurs

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Los Angeles Lakers took another step towards full strength as star guard Austin Reaves was officially listed as questionable on the team's injury report of their big NBA Cup clash against the San Antonio Spurs.

Austin Reaves has missed the last five games de to a heel injury, but following Lakers' practice on Wednesday, the prevailing thought was that he would try and give it a go against the Spurs. He was a full participant during the team's live portion of practice, and he acknowledged that it felt like he had broken his foot after landing awkwardly during the team's win against the Milwaukee Bucks back on Nov. 11.

"I tried to play through it and then I drove baseline, planted on it and was like, 'no I can't do this.' So I walked to the back and was in some pretty bad pain. I didn't think I broke my foot but it sure felt like I broke my foot," Reaves said following practice on Wednesday. "Not much you can really do. Try to stay off of it as much as possible. There's not really a certain thing you can do to make the process speed up. You kind of just let time take care of that stuff."

With Reaves potentially back in the lineup, the Lakers' fate for the knockout round of the NBA Cup hangs in the balance. Should the Lakers defeat the Spurs, they would need a little help from the Portland Trail Blazers to advance to the NBA Cup quarterfinals.

Even if they beat the Spurs, should the Warriors defeat the Blazers, the Warriors would have the advantage of claiming the West Group C's automatic berth. The Lakers lost to the Warriors in head-to-head cup matchups and both would be tied at 3-1 in the standings.

Reaves acknowledged the significance of the matchup, but affirmed that his primary goal was to be healthy and feel good regardless of who is next on the schedule.

"I want to play … I didn't get hurt that night in Milwaukee and look at the schedule and be like, 'I need to be back before the San Antonio game,'" Reaves said. "If I'm back, good, we'll go to war. But I got to listen to my body and take care of myself."
