How to watch Warriors vs. Pelicans

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Golden State Warriors will conclude their six-game homestand with Monday night's matchup against the New Orleans Pelicans. Tip-off is scheduled for 7:00 PM PT in San Francisco and can be watched on NBA TV and NBC Sports Bay Area.

Warriors fans were treated to another vintage performance from Stephen Curry on Saturday night. The 38-year-old superstar scored 41 points on 13-of-19 shooting from the field as he led Golden State to a 126-119 overtime victory against the Orlando Magic. Curry was especially brilliant down the stretch, scoring 17 points in the final seven minutes of regulation and overtime, as he carried the Warriors to their seventh win in a row.

After starting the season 0-3, the Warriors have flipped the script with seven consecutive wins, improving their record to 7-3. Much of that success has come during their current six-game homestand, which Golden State now has an opportunity to sweep with a win over the Pelicans on Monday night.

However, in order to do that, one area the Warriors will need to clean up is their work on the glass. Despite Saturday's victory over Orlando, Golden State struggled to control the glass, particularly on second-chance opportunities, as the Magic held a staggering 24-3 advantage in offensive rebounds.

With the Pelicans' 43.5 rebounds per game ranking just behind the Warriors (43.7), Monday's matchup should present an opportunity for the Dubs to address their shortcomings on that end, especially with New Orleans playing without stars Zion Williamson and Trey Murphy.

Enjoy the game Dub Nation. GO WARRIORS!!!

Warriors: Stephen Curry, Brandin Podziemski, Yaxel Lendeborg, Gui Santos, Draymond Green

Pelicans: Dejounte Murray, Jeremiah Fears, Saddiq Bey, Herbert Jones, Karlo Matkovic

Who: Golden State Warriors (7 – 3) vs. New Orleans (4 – 6)

When: Monday, November 9th, at 7:00 p.m. PT

Where: Chase Center — San Francisco, California

TV and Streaming: NBA TV and NBC Sports Bay Area
