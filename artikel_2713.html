“I Thought Stephen A. Smith Said he Wasn’t Beefing with LeBron James” – NBA Fans Call Out ESPN Analyst For Saying He Would’ve Confronted Ex-Lakers Star If Not For New Contract

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Stephen A. Smith addressed his March 2025 incident with LeBron James again on Tuesday's debut episode of the "KG and Stephen A" show with Kevin Garnett. Smith has spoken about it on several podcasts, but on his latest show, he said something he hadn't disclosed before.

The ESPN analyst said he wanted to confront James that night for pressing him mid-game, but avoided it because he had just landed a new contract from his employers.

"He wanted to make a scene," Smith said. "And I'm going like this, 'Remember, it's the first day of my new contract; if I do something right now, it might affect my money… I wanted to confront him, but I knew it was gonna be a problem."

According to The Athletic, Smith reportedly signed a lucrative $100 million contract extension with ESPN that would also allow him to talk more about politics.

He was at the Lakers game to celebrate after reports of his new contract emerged on Mar. 6, 2025, when James spotted him during a timeout and pressed Smith about his comments on parenting Bronny James.

Since then, both have publicly criticized each other. Smith has done it more. After months of discussion, the ESPN analyst said he was over his issues with James on Monday.

However, Stephen A. Smith recalled plans to confront LeBron James, and several fans called out the ESPN analyst for dragging the issue again.

"I thought Stephen A said he wasn't beefing with LeBron anymore 😂😂😂," one fan said.

Stephen A. Smith is not just over his beef with LeBron James, but he's also rooting for him to win a chip in Philadelphia. Here's what the ESPN analyst said on the Stephen A. Smith Show on Monday (via NBA Central's X):

"I wish him nothing but the best and I'm looking forward to covering him as his career comes to an end over the next two years. Hopefully, he'll win a championship for Philadelphia."

LeBron James' tenure in Philadelphia has looked positive. The Sixers are 9-3, and despite playing as the fourth or even fifth option at times, James has looked comfortable. If the Sixers can stay healthy as a group, they have a legitimate chance to make the Finals.
