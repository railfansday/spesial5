Warriors won’t have to face Damian Lillard tonight

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Portland Trail Blazers will be without their star Damian Lillard tonight due to right calf tightness, as reported by Trail Blazers PR on X, The Everything (Bad) App. The full injury report, though, is really something:

That's the same right calf that suffered a deep vein thrombosis a year and a half ago. Here's hoping that there's no reoccurrence or long-term complication from that condition for this latest injury. Now, having gotten the deserved respect for human health out of the way, this is a break for the Warriors on the court tonight.

Lillard plays no better than when he's against the Warriors. And a Steph-less squad might have elevated his career average 28 points and 6 assist-night against them. He's played 38 games against the Warriors and his 28.3 PPG is his highest average against any opponent. Compare his Warriors record against his career averages and it really stands out:

Career (918 G): 25.0 PPG, 43.8 FG%, 37.0 3P%, 89.9 FT% vs. Warriors (38G): 28.3 PPG, 44.2 FG%, 40.2 3P%, 90.2 FT%

He always strongly represents his hometown, but for a battered Golden State team that's trying to survive the next month without Steph Curry, this is a break. Although, we should be getting a Curry health update today. I'll update this post with that info.

Ja Morant, who has his own history with the Warriors, was perhaps on the way back to the point where he might've been questionable tonight, but that's not the case.
