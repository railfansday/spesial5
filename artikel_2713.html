Here is where the 49ers are in the NFC playoff picture after Sunday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A matchup between two of the top teams in the NFC did not live up to the hype. The San Francisco 49ers made the Minnesota Vikings look like a team that should be drafting in the top 10, not competing for a playoff spot.

Here's a look at the current standings through Week 10.

8. Seattle Seahawks (6-4, 2-1 NFCW, 2-3 NFC)

11. Washington Commanders (3-5-1) (play on Monday Night Football)

12. Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-7, 1-0 vs ATL+NO)

14. New Orleans Saints (3-7, 0-1 vs TB+ATL, 1-5 NFC)

The Lions hold the tiebreaker over the Bears since they are 2-0 in the NFC North, while Chicago is 0-2. Those two and the 49ers look like the conference's three best teams.

The Giants surprised a lot of people with a convincing win over the Jacksonville Jaguars. They might be better than most are willing to admit.

The Panthers escaped with a win, while the Buccaneers couldn't keep pace with Detroit. Carolina has a commanding lead in the division, even though they have a .500 record.

The Packers are the last team in the playoff field. They have the same record as the Seahawks and the Rams, but Green Bay has a better win percentage in the NFC.

Seattle has a better winning percentage in the division, which is why they are ahead of the Rams, who likely won't have Puka Nacua for the remainder of the season.

The Cowboys are hoping the Giants can slip up one or two more times, so they can get into the playoffs. But the hole might be too deep for Dallas to climb out of.
