Cowboys lose fourth game while scoring 30 points

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Greatest Offense There Is needs to be a little greater.

The Cowboys have scored 30 or more points in eight games this year. They’re 4-4 in those games.

Thursday’s 39-38 loss to the Eagles became the fourth loss for Dallas while scoring 30 or more points. That matches the all-time single-season record, set by the 2002 Chiefs and matched by the 2024 Bengals.

And the Cowboys have six chances to do it a fifth time.

This one was the most stunning. Up 31-10 with 3:35 to play in the third quarter, the Cowboys should have won the game. To the Eagles’ credit, they didn’t give up. They didn’t accept the outcome.

And, at some point, a “here we go again” vibe may have infected the Dallas sideline. Because they’ve seen this movie. They’ve lived this nightmare. Over and over again.

They’ve also extended a franchise-record 19-game streak of allowing 20 or more points. Which is bad but not nearly as bad as losing four times while scoring at least 30 points.

Or while losing twice at home in the same season while leading by 14 or more points in the fourth quarter. Via Doug Clawson of CBS, that had never happened for any NFL team — until this year.

What will owner and G.M. Jerry Jones do about it? He’ll do what he always does. He’ll shed a crocodile tear or two, wipe his eyes, and then count his money.
