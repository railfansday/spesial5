Nebraska volleyball vs. Washington live updates, stats and highlights

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

No. 1 Nebraska volleyball hits the road for a Big Ten showdown with the No. 18 Washington Huskies. This will be the 11th meeting between the two schools, with Nebraska leading the series 8-2.

The Huskers were ranked No. 1 in the latest AVCA Coaches Poll released on Nov. 2. It's Nebraska's 134th week all-time at No. 1 in the AVCA Coaches Poll, the most of any program in the nation.

Senior Bergen Reilly was named Big Ten Setter of the Week for the third time this season. Reilly set the Huskers to a .380 hitting percentage on the weekend, leading the Big Ten and ranking second nationally.

Nebraska is on a 55-match regular-season winning streak that dates back to the 2024 season. It's the longest regular-season winning streak in program history in the NCAA era (since 1981).
