Chicago Bears Injury Update for Kyle Monangai, Luther Burden III, and others

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Chicago Bears won't issue an official injury report until Wednesday, but after the bye week, they held an extra practice today at Halas Hall, and several injured players returned to the field. Again, there will be no official practice participation designation until Wednesday, but the Chicago Tribune's Brad Biggs shared that the "only player not spotted during the portion of practice open to media was (cornerback) Kyler Gordon."

Gordon left their win over the Tampa Bay Buccaneers with an undisclosed injury — it looked like a left arm issue after landing awkwardly following a tackle — but returned to finish the game and appeared in 76% of the defensive snaps (57 total). His absence may not be injury-related, but we'll know more on Wednesday.

With Gordon the only Bear missing, that means running back Kyle Monangai (thumb/toe), cornerbacks Malik Muhammad II (toe) and Tyrique Steevenson (hand), and wideout Luther Burden III (concussion) were all on the field.

Monangai hasn't played since Week 4, and Muhammad has been out since Week 7. Stevenson and Burden both exited the Week 9 win over the Bucs. Burden's participation doesn't necessarily mean he's cleared concussion protocol, but we'll know more on Wednesday.

Defensive lineman Dayo Odeyingbo is eligible to come off injured reserve this week, and if he's ready, the Bears could open his 21-day practice window on Wednesday.

The Bears host the New Orleans Saints this Sunday at Noon (CT).
