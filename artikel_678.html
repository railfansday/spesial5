Taylor Swift Performs “Our Song” and “I Knew It, I Knew You” at the CMAs

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The pop star, whose 'Toy Story 5' track marked a return to the country music world, is up for single of the year at the award show.

By Ethan Millman, Carly Thomas November 18, 2026 6:35pm

Taylor Swift played a mashup of “Our Song” and “I Knew It, I Knew You” during her first CMAs performance in 13 years on Wednesday night.

To kick off the performance, the global superstar started backstage as she danced past other country icons — including Brad Paisley, Little Big Town, Sugarland, Brooks & Dunn, Lainey Wilson and Eric Church — while singing her single from the Toy Story 5 soundtrack. She then grabbed her guitar and made her way onstage, where she was joined by Keith Urban on banjo.

Swift later mixed in one of her first-ever hit tracks, “Our Song,” from her 2006 self-titled debut studio album before concluding her performance with the ending of “I Knew It, I Knew You,” which is also up for single of the year at the CMAs. Swift’s CMAs appearance reflects the pop superstar’s return to the country scene she first came up in 20 years ago. Last month, she became the youngest-ever inductee into the Country Music Hall of Fame.

Her CMAs appearance is the latest in a busy year for Swift, who was also inducted into the broader Songwriters Hall of Fame over the summer and is currently a front-runner in the Oscar race for best original song.

She also earned five Grammy nominations earlier this week, with Life of a Showgirl getting an album of the year nod, while its single “The Fate of Ophelia” is nominated for record of the year and “I Knew It, I Knew You” is up for song of the year.
