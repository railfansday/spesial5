Jay-Z’s 2027 Super Bowl Halftime Show Coming to IMAX Theaters

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

“This ain’t a movie, dog,” Jay-Z says on “Public Service Announcement (Interlude)” — but this time around, it is.

The NFL, Disney and ESPN collectively announced on Monday (Nov. 23) that Hov’s Super Bowl LXI Halftime Show will be getting the IMAX treatment.

With the Super Bowl broadcasting on ESPN for the first time in its history, the big game is set to air in IMAX theaters across the U.S. and select international markets.

According to Variety, tickets will go on sale in January, with Super Bowl LXI slated for Feb. 14 at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles.

Theaters hosting the Super Bowl in IMAX will also be announced in the coming weeks. There will be a four-hour presentation of the game’s entirety, including Hov’s halftime show, as well as a pre and post-game show.

“Watching the Super Bowl with your fellow fans is one of the great shared experiences in sports, and this first-of-its-kind collaboration with the NFL and IMAX takes it to a historic new scale,” said Ken Potrock, who serves as the Walt Disney Company’s president of major event integration, per Variety. “With ESPN’s first Super Bowl, we’re reaching fans across an unprecedented range of platforms and meeting them wherever they want to be, with unique moments that only Disney and ESPN can deliver.”

Jay-Z was announced as the 2027 Super Bowl headliner during a Monday Night Football broadcast on Oct. 26, just days after he performed a pair of concerts at SoFi Stadium.

The Brooklyn rap deity has been back outside this year. He returned to the stage in May at Roots Picnic and hosted a blockbuster three-night stay at Yankee Stadium in July, before heading across the pond for dates in London and Paris.

Jay and Roc Nation partnered with the NFL in 2019 and have served as the league’s live music entertainment strategist since 2019.

Hov’s upcoming Super Bowl debut has come with fan-driven rumors of an album in tow. Jay’s last solo effort, 4:44, arrived in June 2017 and debuted atop the Billboard 200 with 262,000 total album-equivalent units earned.
