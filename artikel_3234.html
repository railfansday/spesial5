Paramount+ Orders ‘Clandestine’ Drama Series Starring Brit Marling & Tilda Swinton From ‘The OA’ Creators

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

EXCLUSIVE: Clandestine, a new series from The OA and A Murder At the End of the World creators Brit Marling and Zal Batmanglij, is a go at Paramount+ with Marling and Tilda Swinton starring, Deadline has learned. A high-stakes family drama set in the world of D.C.’s political elite, Clandestine is produced by independent studio Sister Pictures, part of Sister Group.

The series had been developed and greenlighted by the previous Paramount+ regime under former Paramount Chair of Direct-to-Consumer Cindy Holland, I hear. The process of Swinton’s casting was well underway when the October close of the Paramount-Warner Bros. Discovery merger brought along a leadership change, with Paramount+ added to the purview of HBO and HBO Max content chief Casey Bloys who became Co-Chair and Chief Content Officer, Direct-To-Consumer, for the combined Skydance company. Holland exited on the eve of the deal close, with her close associate, Paramount+ Head of Originals Jane Wiseman, who had steered the development on Clandestine, departing a couple of weeks later. Clandestine is now being overseen by Francesca Orsi, President, HBO Drama Series and Films, I hear. Watch on Deadline

In the new structure, Wiseman’s team reports to former HBO Max Head of Originals Aubrey, who became Head of Paramount+, Max Originals and International Programming, adding oversight of all Paramount+ programming.

As Deadline reported last month, Aubrey and HBO veteran Orsi have been reviewing Paramount+’s drama slate, with Orsi taking on projects that have a prestige drama feel similar to HBO’s brand. In addition to Clandestine, I hear Orsi also is overseeing Paramount+’s legal thriller Discretion, starring Nicole Kidman and Elle Fanning, and 9/11 first responders limited series The Best of Us, headlined by Succession‘s Jeremy Strong.

There has been talk about some Paramount+ drama series potentially moving to HBO but, at least as of now, all of the streamer’s shows are staying put.

According to sources, Marling and Batmanglij started developing the project at Sister while Holland was Global CEO there. Holland left the indie studio in October 2024 to become a senior adviser to then-Skydance CEO David Ellison before becoming DTC Chair for Paramount Skydance post-merger in August 2025.

Marling and Batmanglij took out their script this past spring with Marling attached to star. Paramount+ landed Clandestine in a bidding war with FX, and HBO, I hear. Paramount+ and FX vying for Clandestine made sense as their top executives had commissioned Marling and Batmanglij’s two previous series: Paramount+’s Holland had developed and ordered The OA while running English-language scripted originals at Netflix (in addition to being involved in Clandestine early on), and FX chief John Landgraf and his team developed and picked up A Murder At the End of the World.

This also marks a full-circle moment for Orsi who is now overseeing the series she once bid on.

Clandestine is believed to have received an informal green light as part of Paramount+’s winning bid. While I hear the pickup was not cast-contingent, casting key roles was considered crucial, which explains why the streamer did not announce the series order but instead proceeded with securing the other leads opposite Marling. The process, which started over the summer, is ongoing.

Clandestine is written and executive produced by Marling, Batmanglij, Rory Haines and Sohrab Noshirvani (Informer, The Mauritanian). Jane Featherstone (Chernobyl, Black Doves) executive produces for Sister Pictures. Blake Holland (The OA, A Murder at the End of the World) and Alice Tyler (Coldwater, The Following Events are Based on a Pack of Lies) are co-executive producers; Callum Devrell-Cameron (Lord of the Flies, Black Doves S2) a producer.

This marks the biggest TV series commitment to date for Oscar winner Swinton who starred in two British miniseries at the start of her career. Her recent feature credits include Pedro Almodóvar’s The Room Next Door, Edward Berger’s Ballad of a Small Player and Joshua Oppenheimer’s The End. She is repped by CAA, UK’s Hamilton Hodell and Peikoff Mahan Law.

Marling and Batmanglij broke out with their 2011 indie movie Sound of My Voice, which the two wrote, Batmanglij directed and Marling co-starred in. Premiering at Sundance, it earned Indie Spirit Award nominations for Best First Feature and Best Supporting Actress (Marling). They are repped by CAA and Hansen Jacobson Teller.
