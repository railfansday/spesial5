FTC Solar (FTCI) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Revenue climbed 26% as the company eyes positive adjusted EBITDA by 2027.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

FTC Solar, Inc. (FTCI -4.83%) reported sequential revenue growth and an improvement in gross margins, driven by increased product deliveries and operational efficiencies. Management highlighted a strategic focus on 'constructability,' a design philosophy aimed at reducing site labor requirements and accommodating challenging soil and terrain conditions. The company is expanding its international presence, specifically in India, Australia, and the United Kingdom, while maintaining its domestic manufacturing capabilities to leverage local content requirements. Management indicated that the company is on a path to reach positive adjusted EBITDA by the first quarter of 2027, supported by a contracted backlog of $640 million and an accelerating revenue ramp expected in the final quarter of 2026.
