Ocean Spray Just Gave Canned Cranberry Sauce A Chocolate Makeover

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Someone finally saw the potential in those jiggly, ridged slices.

Delish editors handpick every product we feature. We may earn commission from the links on this page.

Canned cranberry sauce has some pretty stiff competition at Thanksgiving. Between the mashed potatoes, stuffing, and mac and cheese, that jiggly little side dish often gets treated like the ugly stepsister of the dinner table. And it probably doesn't help that it comes out looking exactly like the can it was packaged in.

But perhaps a little chocolate could improve its reputation.

Ocean Spray and Los Angeles chocolatier Compartés have teamed up with recipe developer Carolina Gelen on a new treat called Chocolate Covered Cranberry Sauce, taking the familiar Ocean Spray stuff and turning it into chocolate-coated discs, sold in packs of four.

If that sounds vaguely familiar, you may have seen Gelen's original Instagram video from 2023, when she experimented with dipping slices of canned cranberry sauce in chocolate (the recipe has since racked up more than 10 million views).

Ocean Spray's Kelvin Vuong said Gelen's recipe was exactly the kind of unexpected use for cranberry sauce the company wanted to share with more people. The brand eventually partnered with her and Compartés to turn the homemade recipe into something people could purchase—and Gelen is pretty thrilled to see her original experiment come this far.

"Getting to work with Ocean Spray and Compartés to turn that idea into something people across the country can try is incredibly exciting," she said in the announcement.

As for why this combination works, Compartés' creative director and chief chocolatier, Jonathan Grahm, pointed to the contrast between the cranberries' sharp, sour flavor and the richness of the chocolate. It's the same reason tart fruit and chocolate can work so well together, even if cranberry sauce isn't exactly the first thing you'd think to dip.

The limited-edition chocolates are available exclusively at Compartés for $24.95 per four-pack, while supplies last, with the gourmet chocolate brand also making chocolate turkeys filled with Ocean Spray's whole-berry cranberry sauce in both milk and dark chocolate options.

Cranberry sauce might never get the same love as mashed potatoes, but chocolate certainly gives it a fighting chance.
