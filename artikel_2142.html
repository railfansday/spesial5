Swindon Town tipped to take Ian Holloway action after Grimsby Town taunts

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Swindon Town have been tipped to potentially part ways with experienced manager, Ian Holloway, after the Robins were defeated heavily by his former employers, Grimsby Town, at the County Ground on Saturday afternoon.

The Robins are currently 16th in League Two having taken 19 points from their first 15 matches, but have won just twice in their last 14 encounters across all competitions.

Saturday's 4-1 loss against the high-flying Mariners was also Swindon's third on the spin, having been eliminated from the FA Cup against League One side, MK Dons, as well as losing in the EFL Trophy to Plymouth Argyle.

Supporters in Wiltshire have grown increasingly frustrated with the club's recent situation, as the club have continued to stagnate in the fourth tier in the five years of Clem Morfuni's ownership.

Holloway recently surpassed two years in charge at the County Ground, but after finishing 12th in his first full season last term, there are concerns as to whether his team can show any signs of progress based on their recent form.

Holloway is, arguably, the EFL's most experienced manager at present, having taken charge at matches across all levels for 30 years, including Premier League spells with Blackpool and Crystal Palace.

The 63-year-old has gained a strong reputation for various incidents over time, and this season, that has been no exception for the Swindon boss.

After slamming Manchester City for their alleged financial misconduct, Holloway claimed last month he was a "Dead. Man. Walking," within a viral interview clip, which he was reminded about by the supporters of his former club as David Artell's side ran riot, with Aaron Drinan's goal for the hosts a mere consolation after strikes from Andy Cook, Josh Andrews and a brace from Iwan Morgan.

Hollowaytook charge of Grimsby between December 2019 and 2020, winning 13 of his 39 matches in charge, accumulating a 33 percent win ratio in the process.

The experienced figure resigned at Blundell Park following a 2-1 loss to Bradford City which had the club lying 20th in League Two at the time, and the Mariners were eventually relegated back to the National League months later, albeit for a solitary season.

As such, the North Lincolnshire fanbase are not particularly fond of Holloway, who, in return, criticised their actions in his post-match interview.

“On that performance, it is what we deserved, and that hurts me bitterly, because that was a club that I used to work for," he said.

“You wouldn’t think so, would you? The use of language [from their fans] was disgusting, in my opinion, but that is what happens in modern-day football.

“They didn’t say a word when we beat them 4-0 up there two years ago, did they? It is what it is," he added.

Many notable teams have been emotionally exhausting to support in League Two, but which club tops our list at this moment in time?

Discussing the action in League One and League Two from across the weekend, EFL pundits, Tom Garratt and Callum Fowler, were amongst those debating whether Holloway's future in the Swindon job is currently jeopardised.

"Another man who should be under pressure, Ian Holloway. Swindon 1-4 Grimsby," said Garratt on the Pyramid podcast.

"He's just a 'dead man walking', that's what the Grimsby fans were singing to him," Fowler immediately responded.

"Swindon's four-game unbeaten run ends at the hands of Grimsby who have won their last three, scoring 13 goals in that time."

Later discussing Holloway's immediate future, Garratt asked: "is he under pressure?" to which Fowler added: "100%. He's a nutter."

"I'm amazed he's still got a job. Get him gone," the former then stated.
