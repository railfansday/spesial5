Director confirms ‘the next step’ with talks over deal for West Ham man eyed

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

West Ham loaned out flop striker Niclas Fullkrug in the summer, and his remarkable form since rejoining Werder Bremen has been well documented. The 33-year-old has 12 goals in 11 games this season, earning a recall to Jurgen Klopp’s Germany squad and prompting swift talk over a permanent WHUFC exit.

Now, Werder Bremen sporting director Clemens Fritz has spoken publicly about the matter, telling Sky Sports Germany (as relayed by the Hamburger Morgenpost) that the plan is to hold formal talks with the Hammers over a permanent move for Fullkrug.

“The next step is to enter into dialogue with West Ham,” Fritz said. “We will, of course, hold talks about this. Ultimately, we also have to see what the financial package looks like.”

West Ham signed Fullkrug for a hefty fee worth up to £27m in 2024. He had been a prolific scorer in Germany, but there were eyebrows raised over the amount paid given that he was aged 31 at the time.

Those concerns were justified, as Fullkrug failed to make a positive impact in English football. Across all competitions, he managed three goals and two assists in 29 games for the Hammers, so his summer exit to Werder Bremen was a much-needed one.

The German has been a prolific scorer in his home country across his career, but few expected him to find the form of his life on the return to Werder Bremen. Fullkrug has 12 goals in 11 games for the club, with Saturday’s 1-0 loss to VfB Stuttgart marking the first Bundesliga game he has failed to score in since August 30th.

His call-up to Klopp’s Germany squad shows just how well he has been doing. Few WHUFC fans would have expected such success given the standard of his performances over two years in East London.

When players out on loan hit impressive form, it is often met with clamour for their return. However, that is highly unlikely to be the case for Fullkrug.

His circumstances are different. Not only did West Ham pay out a big transfer fee and sizeable wages, but he turns 34 later this season, so will likely have eyes on seeing out the twilight years of his career enjoying his football back in his home country.

Fullkrug’s stint at WHUFC is one best forgotten, and neither he nor the club should have a problem with bringing their affiliation to a permanent end. He can focus on maintaining his form and happily seeing out his career in Germany while the Hammers get his wages off the books for good.
