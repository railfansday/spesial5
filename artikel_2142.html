Celtic sweat on Mika Baur as star turn set for scan after update out of dressing room

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Celtic face an injury sweat over Mika Baur after the £6m summer signing hobbled off in the final minutes.

The German was at his all-action best in the 1-0 win over Kilmarnock but now appears set to be an absentee for his country’s U21s after needing help off the pitch.

Now Martin O’Neill admits the 22-year-old will be heading for a scan to discover the full extent of the damage.

Speaking to BBC Sport after the game, O’Neill said: “Yeah, we’ll have to see about him. He’s in pain at the minute and I think he’ll be going for some scans very shortly. But we’ll see how he is.”

O’Neill then offered a further update to Celtic Player, revealing the initial assessment from the club’s medical staff.

He said: “We’ve all gone in to see Mika Baur. The physio tells me it might be a heavy ankle sprain. That’s what we’re hoping it is, rather than anything else, but it’s too early to say.”

Baur had been heavily involved before his late withdrawal, with the midfielder finding the net only to see his effort ruled out for offside.

Liam Scales’ first-half strike ultimately proved enough for Celtic to earn all three points.
