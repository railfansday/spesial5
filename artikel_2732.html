Deshaun Watson found it "definitely special" to beat the Texans

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Browns quarterback Deshaun Watson led a game-winning drive in the fourth quarter on Sunday, which isn’t surprising given the way the 2026 season has unfolded in Cleveland.

All six of the team’s wins have come thanks to Watson’s efforts in the final minutes and the Browns are the first team in league history to win its first six games of a season by four points or fewer. Sunday’s 27-24 win did have a different wrinkle for the quarterback, however. It came against the Texans, who drafted Watson in 2017 and then traded him to the Browns in 2022 after Watson sat out the 2021 season amid sexual misconduct allegations and a trade request.

Watson said that the opponent meant that beating his former team was “definitely special” for him.

“I think people know the history behind it,” Watson said, via a transcript from the team. “Didn’t leave on a good note, but it’s all love for the city of Houston, the fans there, the players there, some of the staff there, most definitely, that I got to see today. And even the older ownership. So, it was good to see a lot of people, familiar faces, but it’s still love. But it’s definitely a good win.”

Watson’s time with the Texans is in the past and the present is exciting for the Browns. They head into their bye week with a 6-4 record that has them firmly in playoff contention and Sunday’s win gave them their first 4-0 home start to a season since 1963. Faster starts would likely help their chances of both staying unbeaten at home and making it to the postseason, but knowing that they’re never out of a game isn’t a bad lesson to take from the first 10 weeks of the season.
