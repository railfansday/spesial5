Panthers vs Buccaneers: The run game is the big storyline this week

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Hello CSR! Happy Black Friday and I hope you all got to enjoy Thanksgiving! I hope the Panthers did too, but didn't eat too much and are hyped and ready for a pivotal NFC South matchup against the Buccaneers for Monday Night Football. The last time around, the Bucs ran the football down their throats and played excellent defense, en route to a 43-13 win over the Panthers in Charlotte. Now, the Panthers travel to Tampa in what could be a division defining showdown. How do the Panthers stack up? Hopefully, not like last time.

The Bucs have been pretty transparent about their offensive gameplay this season; run the football. The Bucs are averaging a whopping 159.6 yards per game which is 1st in the league, and averaging running the ball 51.44% of the time, also 1st in the league. We all know Bucky Irving is good, but they are riding him and Kenneth Gainwell hard this season. The duo has already racked up 1,200 yards between them as Baker Mayfield hasn't been relied on too heavily as a passer in an injury riddled season. The Panthers defense meanwhile has been a tale of two defenses this season, sometimes giving up 300+ yards on the ground but also shutting down some of the more prominent running backs in guys like Jahmyr Gibbs and Bijan Robinson. Last week, the Panthers did win with many turnovers, but they still allowed 157 yards on the ground for an average of 7.1 yards per carry, and that average was dragged downward by a 13 yard loss on a rush by WR Zay Flowers. There's not much to be said here, the trenches are going to be the storyline in this game. They have to come out playing fast early on defense.

The Panthers offense on the other side has settled down from the explosive passing attack they were to start the season. They are still taking the shots down the field, but for the most part they are staying on schedule by getting the ball to Chuba Hubbard, and last week they sprinkled in a healthy mix of Trevor Etienne. The last time these teams met, the two only carried the ball 13 times between the two, and Chuba only carried the rock 7 of those times. The game had gotten out of hand fast, so that was understandable, but that can't happen again. I do worry about the Buccaneers jumping out ahead early, as the same thing happened last week. The offense can't start slow this week and allow the Bucs to jump out to a multi-score lead like last week and the first round against the Bucs, because that only favors the Bucs plan of attack. A healthy amount of Chuba Hubbard to start the game should allow the Panthers to get on schedule early. The Bucs' rushing defense is very good, so this could very well be a challenge, but the offense has to rise to the occasion here.

Last week, the Panthers probably only picked up the win against the Ravens due to their pass rush coming up big in huge passing downs. They didn't get a ton of pressure consistently, but when they sent Princely opposite of Jaelan Phillips, good things happened. Umanmielen had 1.5 sacks and a tackle for a loss, along with two QB hits. This Buccaneers offensive line is very good, and they've kept a beat up Baker Mayfield upright for the most part when he does have to throw. The Panthers can't let Baker Mayfield scramble like he can to keep drives alive, so I want a healthy Thanksgiving portion of Princely thrown at him.

What are you watching for this week, Panthers fans?
