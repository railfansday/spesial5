LeBron James reveals what the 76ers have done right to start strong this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

LeBron James reveals what the 76ers have done right to start strong this season originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Across the first 17 games of the season, the Philadelphia 76ers have cemented their place as the top team in the Eastern Conference. They have a 14-3 record, which is the best in the East, compared to the second-seeded Boston Celtics.

It has been an impressive run for the 76ers because they have elite firepower across the board. While a lot of people thought the main problem for this team heading into the season was that there is only one ball, Philly has not faced that issue so far in the 2026-27 NBA season.

It is easy to get caught up in narratives during the offseason. While a lot of people were hyped about James and Jaylen Brown joining the 76ers, there were concerns regarding how the stacked roster will work together.

So far this season, it seems that it has not been an issue. James has been a big part of that success because he has been the leader for Philadelphia as both a playmaker and a vocal presence.

Unselfishness has been a big part of the 76ers' success. Tyrese Maxey and Brown are the leading scorers for Philly, but they are not playing selfish basketball, which is why they have been such a successful team this season.

"We've bought into the pass. We have so many guys that can score at elite levels, but the power of the pass helps everyone get into a rhythm and feel confident. We've done that from day one, and it's gotten better and better," James said about the secret behind the 76ers' success.

Moving forward, it will be quite a challenging time for the 76ers. However, they are playing the right type of basketball that is helping them get past those issues, as their offense has been quite fun with an unselfish style.
