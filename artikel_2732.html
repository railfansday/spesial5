Wizards at Pistons Discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Washington Wizards (4-6) will look to snap a three-game losing streak when they travel to Detroit on Monday for the third game of a four-game road trip. Washington is coming off its worst showing of the season — a 114-75 loss to the Oklahoma City Thunder on Saturday. It was their lowest scoring output since January 2018.

The Pistons (6-3) are on the second night of a back-to-back after losing to the Los Angeles Lakers on Sunday. Washington went 1-3 against Detroit last season and could've won twice if they didn't blow a 13-point fourth-quarter lead.

Anthony Davis is AVAILABLE after missing the previous two games with an ankle sprain.

WAS: Bilal Coulibaly (hand soreness), Justin Champagnie (foot surgery), Tristan Vukcevic (hip impingement), Jamir Watkins (ACL tear) and Reece Beekman (AC joint) are OUT.

DET: Jalen Duren (hamstring), Isaiah Joe (hip), Elijah Harkless (G League), Isaac Jones (G League) and Ugonna Onyenso (G League) are OUT.
