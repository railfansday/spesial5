YouTube Gold: The Evolution Of Cameron Boozer

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Most fans tend to think of Cameron Boozer as a finished problem when he was at Duke and now as an NBA rookie, and just see him as a 6-9, 250 lb. problem for everyone.

But he was once a skinny kid like anyone else, as this video demonstrates.

When we first see young Boozer, he's wearing goggles, which is an interesting look (of course, they could be prescription sports glasses).

As he grows, you see him going from a skinny kid to a taller, thicker guy, but it's quickly clear that he has an unusually mature grasp of the game.
