Liam Coen: Everything said kick FG late in fourth, going for it a "hope play"

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Jaguars head coach Liam Coen opted to kick a field goal from the 9-yard-line and cut the Giants’ lead to 23-18 with just over three minutes to play in Sunday’s game, but hopes of winning the game with a touchdown went up in smoke when the Giants picked up two first downs and ran out the clock.

Coen was asked about the decision to kick rather than try for the touchdown in that situation.

“We talked about it a few times,” Coen said. “Our defense — they hadn’t done anything up until that point. Everything about the charts and everything talked about it said kick the field goal, get within five points. Go get a stop and you feel pretty good about where you’re at. Just executing that down-and-distance on a fourth down is kinda challenging. They’re zero blitzing you every play, the ball’s gonna come out. It’s like a hope play.”

The Giants had gone four straight drives without picking up a first down, so some would argue in favor of the hope play while using the same argument about getting a stop in the event you score and don’t get the two-point conversion or fail to convert. The defensive stop would have remained crucial if the Jaguars had tied the game and the failure to get one at the biggest moment of the game left the Jaguars with their third loss of the season.
