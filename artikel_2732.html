Cam Skattebo gives Giants a 14-10 lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Cam Skattebo has his second touchdown, and the Giants lead for the second time.

The Giants, who trailed 10-7 at halftime, lead 14-10 after the running back's 13-yard touchdown run.

The Commanders took the second-half kickoff and went three-and-out. Rachaad White lost 1 yard, and Shelby Harris sacked Jayden Daniels for a 14-yard loss. The Commanders then had a false start to face a third-and-28.

After the Commanders punted, the Giants went 55 yards in five plays.

Skattebo has 10 carries for 55 yards and two catches for 37 yards.

Giants safety Jason Pinnock is questionable to return with a back injury.
