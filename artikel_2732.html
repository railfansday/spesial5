VIkings player fined for taunting in big win vs. Packers in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Just one Vikings player was fined in the aftermath of Vikings vs. Packers in Week 10, and it was linebacker Dallas Turner.

The fine stems from Turner's actions during the Vikings' huge win over the Packers. Turner said something to Packers running back Kaleb Johnson, and he was immediately flagged. Now he owes the NFL $11,941 for whatever he said.

On the Packers' side of things, linebacker Ty'Ron Hopper was also fined for unnecessary roughness, specifically striking, kicking, or kneeing. He'll owe the league $8,325.

The NFL deducts fine money directly from players' game checks, and the funds are donated to the NFL Foundation to support the health, safety and wellness of athletes at all levels of football and the Professional Athletes Foundation to support NFL Legends in need.

Josh Metellus, $23,882: Unnecessary roughness, use of the helmet, Week 3

Dallas Turner, $11,941: Unnecessary roughness, face mask, Week 3

Dallas Turner, $11,941: Unsportsmanlike conduct, taunting, Week 10
