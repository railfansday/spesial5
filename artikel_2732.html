Panthers come back to beat Ravens in a wild game in Carolina

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Panthers and Ravens played a highly entertaining game today at Carolina, going back and forth until the closing seconds, when an ill-advised Jacoby Brissett interception ended the Ravens’ hopes.

Brissett played well for most of the game, but when he attempted to make a play in the final 30 seconds, he scrambled around before heaving the ball deep downfield, where it was intercepted by the Panthers, sealing a 35-33 win.

Other than that interception, Brissett was solid, completing 24 of 37 passes for 276 yards and a touchdown. Derrick Henry and Justice Hill both had rushing touchdowns for the Ravens, but Henry went into the locker room late in the game for a concussion evaluation.

Panthers quarterback Bryce Young shook off some early struggles and finished 22 of 30 for 254 yards, with two touchdowns and an interception. Chuba Hubbard had an excellent game for the Panthers, running 27 times for 120 yards and two touchdowns.

With the win, the Panthers improve to 5-5 and remain atop the NFC South. The Ravens fall to 4-7.
