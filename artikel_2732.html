Jacob Rodriguez coming for Dolphins record held by Zach Thomas

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Great linebacker play has been a staple of the Miami Dolphins throughout the history of the franchise. From Nick Buoniconti, A.J. Duhe, John Offerdahl and Bryan Cox, all the way up to Zach Thomas, Junior Seau, Joey Porter and now Jordyn Brooks, Dolphins fans have been blessed to witness the play of some of the greats of the game.

Keeping with tradition, the season that 2026 second-round pick Jacob Rodriguez is having is giving the youngster a chance to possibly etch his name in the record books.

During his rookie season in 1996, future Hall-of-Fame linebacker Zach Thomas finished his first campaign as a professional with 154 combined tackles. That mark set a rookie record for Miami — one that has yet to be broken… yet. However, that record is in danger of being surpassed by Rodriguez this season.

Through ten games, Rodriguez ranks 8th in the National Football League with 93 combined tackles — 9.3 per game. If Miami's sensational rookie can avoid injury and the daunting "rookie wall," he'll finish the year with roughly 158 tackles — four more than Thomas had in the greatest rookie year a Dolphins' linebacker has ever had. The accomplishment would be a bright spot in an otherwise difficult year for the South Florida franchise.

Now, it's worth noting that Thomas' record was set when the NFL played just 16 regular season games before the playoffs started — one less than the 17 games the league currently plays, so if Rodriguez wants to lay claim to the record without an asterisk, he'll need to average a little over 10 tackles per game from here on out.

Can he do it? Absolutely, but the achievement won't come easy.

In a season that has seen the team secure just one single victory, Rodriguez's development — plus his potential to set a new Dolphins record — is something fans of the franchise can cheer for as the year nears its conclusion.
