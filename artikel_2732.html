State Swim: Martin County's Charlotte Mitchell tops field for Class 3A backstroke title

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

OCALA — Charlotte Mitchell needed just one season to enter the Martin County High School record books.

The freshman became the fourth Tigers girls swimmer to capture an individual state title. Mitchell placed first in the 100-yard backstroke at the FHSAA Class 3A state championships Friday night at the Florida Aquatics Swimming and Training facility in Ocala.

The program's previous individual state champions were Tina McIntyre and Tori Richmond, who won the 100 freestyle and 100 breaststroke, respectively, in 2005, along with Teresa Anderson, who captured three state titles from 1981-82.

It is the Martin County girls team's first state championship of any kind since Hannah Burdge, Aubrey Murray, Sara Lacusky and Macey Arnold won the 200 freestyle relay in 2012.

In what may be the first of many state titles to come, Mitchell posted a winning time of 57.82 seconds.

The Tigers freshman cruised to district and regional titles in the 100 backstroke before entering the state meet as the event's No. 1 seed. Mitchell followed that by recording the top preliminary time (57.96) before winning the final, nearly a second ahead of runner-up Bogdana Kuptcova of Miami Beach (58.65).

She wasn't done there. Mitchell placed fifth in the 100 butterfly (58.94) before teaming with Chloe Morgenstern, Julia Murphy and Martina Saralegui Blanco for a fifth-place finish in the 200 freestyle relay (1:41.32). Mitchell, Morgenstern, Murphy and Noelle Bussom also finished 12th in the 200 medley relay (1:55.17).

Behind the freshman's efforts, Martin County placed an area-best 12th overall with 73 points. Jensen Beach tied for 23rd with 19 points, while Sebastian River tied for 27th with 16.

Martin County also was the area's top boys team, placing eighth overall with 105 points. Roman Watson emerged as the Tigers' top performer.

The junior finished sixth in the 50 freestyle (21.11) and fifth in the 100 breaststroke (58.57). He also teamed with Daniel Romero, Bradon Fiore and Manny Gonzalez for a fifth-place finish in the 200 freestyle relay (1:26.50). The same quartet tied for fourth in the 200 medley relay (1:35.97).

Other Tigers highlights included Romero winning the B final in the 100 backstroke (52.96) and Samuel Shiflett placing seventh in the 1-meter diving competition.

As well as the Tigers performed in Ocala, the area's best individual boys showing came from South Fork.

Gavin Thompson closed out his prep career with a pair of top-three finishes. The Bulldogs senior was runner-up in the 100 breaststroke and placed third in the 200 individual medley (1:52.43).

With Thompson leading the way, South Fork finished 12th overall with 63 points.
