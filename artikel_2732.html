Liverpool enter race for €30 million-rated teenage defender wanted by Barcelona &#8211; report

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

According to SPORT, Barcelona face fresh competition in their pursuit of Arthur Dias, with Liverpool entering the race for the highly-rated Athletico Paranaense defender.

The Blaugrana have been closely monitoring the 19-year-old Brazilian starlet for a while now but could face serious competition from the Premier League giants.

Barcelona have decided to enter the market for a new central defender ahead of the January transfer window.

The recent injuries to players like Eric Garcia, Andreas Christensen, and Jules Kounde exposed the lack of depth in the defensive line, prompting Hansi Flick, Deco & co. to scour the market for options.

And, Dias has emerged as one of the options on the Blaugrana's radar. The 19-year-old is held in high regard by the Blaugrana sporting department and is seen as someone who would fit the profile they are looking for in the market.

Arthur Dias is in demand. (Photo by Heuler Andrey/Getty Images)

A left-footed centre-back, comfortable with playing out from the back and physically imposing, the Brazilian is also versatile enough to operate at left-back.

Despite being only 19, he already has over 50 senior appearances for Athletico Paranaense, while he has also featured three times for the Brazilian national team.

All these aspects make Dias an attractive target for Deco and Barcelona as they look for defensive reinforcements.

However, Liverpool have now entered the fray for Dias' signing and could complicate matters for Barcelona.

The Reds have sent scouts to watch the Brazilian centre-back and are assessing the possibility of bringing him to Anfield in a future transfer window.

Liverpool set to compete with Barcelona for Dias. (Photo by Koji Watanabe/Getty Images)

Another Premier League side, Tottenham Hotspur, had already explored the possibility of signing the teenager in the summer.

However, their offer of €20 million was shot down by Athletico, who are said to be seeking a figure of around €30 million for their young talent.

But, Spurs are understood to be still monitoring the 19-year-old starlet, along with Bundesliga giants RB Leipzig and Bayer Leverkusen.

The growing competition could force Barcelona to make a decision sooner rather than later over their pursuit of Dias, whose contract at the Brazilian outfit runs until December 2028.
