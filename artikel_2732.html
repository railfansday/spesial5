Steelers insider believes Drew Allar will be 2027 Week 1 starter

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Pittsburgh Steelers selected quarterback Drew Allar out of Penn State 76th overall in the 2026 NFL Draft. The selection surprised some, as it was widely known that Aaron Rodgers would return to the team despite not officially signing by the time the Steelers selected Allar. The Steelers had also brought back longtime backup quarterback Mason Rudolph and had selected Will Howard in the fifth round just one year prior.

Now, it seems like Allar might be in pole position to be the Steelers starter come Week 1 of the 2027 NFL season. Mark Kaboly of The Pat McAfee Show took to his X account to say he believes the chances of Allar being the starter are higher than anyone wants to admit.

"At this moment, I would say that Drew Allar has an 87.9% chance of being the Steelers' starting quarterback in Week 1 of 2027," Kaboly said on his X account.

Allar went 30/54 passing for 351 yards and two touchdown passes in his three preseason appearances. The biggest takeaway from his preseason performance was how well Allar took care of the ball while also making noticeable strides with his footwork.

With Aaron Rodgers retiring after the season, and Mason Rudolph on an expiring contract, it leaves the team with just Allar and Howard under contract for 2027. With the Steelers firmly in playoff position, the likelihood of them selecting a top quarterback in a weird 2027 quarterback draft class is low.

Given the fact that Mike McCarthy picked Allar himself, his strong preseason showing, and all of the progress being made behind the scenes, insiders are starting to believe we could very well he headed for number 16 to be under center for the Steelers come Week 1 of the 2027 NFL season.
