Anthropic IPO could create thousands of multimillionaire employees

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A version of this article appeared in CNBC's Inside Wealth newsletter, a weekly guide to the high-net-worth investor and consumer. Sign up to receive future editions, straight to your inbox.

The average Anthropic employee could be worth over $40 million after the company's IPO today, with over 400 employees likely to be worth over $100 million, according to SEC filings and investors.

At a share price of $83, Anthropic's market value would top $1.4 trillion, making it one of the 10 most valuable companies in the U.S. It will not only be the largest IPO in history by dollar amounts, but it is also shaping up to be one of the most rapid wealth creation events in history.

Anthropic was founded just five years ago and has only 5,300 employees. According to SEC filings, the artificial intelligence company has generated 1.77 billion options for employees, with a strike price of $2.91 a share. It has also paid out over 1 billion restricted stock units.

If the stock trades at $83 a share, the combined wealth of Anthropic employees will be around $232 billion, based on their share count in the filings. The total works out to an average of $44 million per employee.

Yet because many of the employees are later hires, earlier employees could have supersized gains since they've accumulated more stock. According to investors in the company, at least 400 early employees will be worth $100 million or more at a $83 share price. Several could become billionaires, they said.

The employee total doesn't include the seven founders, who each own between 230 million and 240 million shares, which will each be worth around $20 billion based on a $83 share price.

While employees face complex lockup periods and vesting schedules, the wealth is likely to start making its way into the economy quickly, economists say. The first two areas will be the San Francisco real estate market and philanthropy.

Brokers say San Francisco's already red hot real-estate market will get a boost from hundreds of Anthropic employees who now have more liquid stock they can borrow against. An analysis by Redfin found that Anthropic's current and former employees will have enough post-IPO wealth to buy 9% of all the homes in San Francisco.

Anthropic's culture of effective altruism, along with its generous matching program for charitable gifts of employee shares, are expected to spark a wave of giving, especially to donor-advised funds. Employees hired before May 17, 2024 can pledge up to 50% of their vested shares for a 3-to-1 match by the company. Later hires can pledge up to 25% for a 1-to-1 match.

The seven co-founders have also pledged 80% of their wealth to charity.

"Philanthropy could be the piece that's different this time," said Michael Faust, president of wealth management at Bailard Inc. "Some of the companies going public now really encourage their employees to give, and they match it. That could make a huge difference for Bay Area nonprofits for a long time to come."
