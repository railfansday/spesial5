Open Mitten Savings if you’ll keep $10,000 or more in it. At that balance, every dollar earns a 4.41% annual percentage yield (APY), and a new account’s rates can’t drop for its first 180 days. Under $10,000, the whole balance earns 3.01%, or 0.05% below $2,500, so put smaller savings in an account without balance tiers.

Mitten Savings is the online-only savings account of Community Choice Credit Union, a Michigan credit union that opened it to U.S. citizens nationwide in November 2026. Each tier’s rate applies to the whole balance, not just the dollars past its threshold, so a balance of $9,500 earns 3.01% on all of it.

Mitten Savings is worth opening for savers who will keep $10,000 or more in it, since every dollar then earns one of the top savings rates. Anyone keeping less earns more at Pibank Savings, which pays 4.40% on any balance against Mitten’s 3.01% or less, though Pibank sends withdrawals only by wire.
