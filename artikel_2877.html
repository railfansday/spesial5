Capitals’ Alex Ovechkin pays tribute to Nicklas Backstrom after breaking his record

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Alex Ovechkin made even more franchise history on Thursday night, passing longtime teammate Nicklas Backstrom for the most assists in Washington Capitals history.

Although it came in a losing effort — a 6-3 defeat at the hands of the Buffalo Sabres at Capital One Arena — his 763rd career assist marked an impressive milestone. The Great Eight has now scored the most goals in NHL history and recorded the most assists in Capitals history.

Ovechkin downplayed the record when speaking to reporters after the game.

"Yeah, it's nice, but if 'Backy' is still playing, he would probably have 1,000 right now or however many assists he can get," the Russian said, per NHL.com's Tom Gulitti.

Both Ovechkin and the Capitals have struggled in 2026-27. The former has managed just a goal and six points in 20 games, while the latter is 10-10-0 and in sixth place in the Metropolitan Division.

Washington is outside the Eastern Conference playoff picture, a disappointing position considering the club made some key additions over the summer, bringing in Alex Tuch, Jordan Kyrou and Boone Jenner.

"We just have to keep fighting," Ovechkin said, per Gulitti. "Sometimes things don't go your way, but we just have to keep fighting and stay with it.

"Chances are there. It's just sometimes you hold the stick too tight. Just stick with it and keep working."

Ovechkin is in the midst of the longest goal drought of his illustrious 22-year career, going 15 games without finding the back of the net.

It's been a difficult start to the 41-year-old's farewell season. He has already announced that 2026-27 will be his final NHL campaign, but there is still plenty of hockey left to be played, and the Capitals are seven points out of a playoff spot.

"I thought he was doing a lot of good things with his line and, listen, he's working," head coach Spencer Carbery said of Ovechkin. "He's just like everybody else in our group, everybody else on our staff. Things are not going our way as a group and individually right now and he is fighting like hell to try to score a goal to help our team win."

Even amid Washington's four-game losing streak, Thursday's milestone is another reminder of how much Ovechkin has meant to the franchise.

The Caps will look to right the ship against the Boston Bruins at TD Garden on Saturday night.
