Ravens elevate WR Chris Moore, TE Nick Vannett; sign OT Chuma Edoga

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Ravens added three players to their roster ahead of Monday night's game against the Chargers.

Wide receiver Chris Moore and tight end Nick Vannett are temporary additions after being elevated from the practice squad. Offensive tackle Chuma Edoga has been signed to the 53-man roster from the practice squad.

Moore had three catches for 63 yards and a touchdown in four games this season, but was released in mid-October and returned to the practice squad a couple of days later. Vannett appeared in five games before going through the same change in roster status this month.

Edoga has appeared in two games this season as a temporary elevation.
