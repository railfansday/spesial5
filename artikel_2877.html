Cowboys roasted by birds on Thanksgiving with latest coaching meltdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The largest comeback in NFL Thanksgiving football history. The most points the Philadelphia Eagles have scored all season. Another meltdown at home, complete with Eagles head man Nick Sirianni getting his swagger back and celebrating on the sidelines and behind the benches after his team completed the improbable comeback. A hated division rival smiling and eating turkey legs on your field.

These are all things the Dallas Cowboys allowed to happen in their own stadium on Thanksgiving, with nobody else to blame. AT&T Stadium was lit up red all week, and left the faithful fans sitting inside of it seeing red by Thursday evening as the Cowboys continue to find ways to blow games in more and more spectacular fashion.

The chance for the Cowboys to get to .500 and remain in the playoff conversation int0 December was right there for the taking. Needing to hold onto a 31-10 lead against an Eagles team led by a backup QB, they failed. The collapses the Cowboys have had against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers and Arizona Cardinals already moved the goalposts of what this season was supposed to be, but finding a way to top these losses instead of finally winning back-to-back games sinks this team into an even deeper hole.

This latest loss was so bad it sucked up all the oxygen needed to talk about another good-enough-to-win performance from Dak Prescott, with no room to do so after Joe Milton joins the likes of UDFA rookie Jalon Daniels and Gardner Minshew to also have head-to-head wins against Prescott this season. Thanksgiving cemented Prescott's status as the quarterback who was letdown the most by his own coaching, defense, and front office in the league.

Let's get to some notes on how the Cowboys gave away another game.

Even when the Cowboys won in Philadelphia back in week seven against Tanner McKee at QB for the Eagles, they still allowed more yards, points, and big plays than the Eagles had been getting across multiple McKee starts in the few weeks prior. Still, the Cowboys electing to go up ten with a red zone field goal was a decision that earned Schotty some praise when it came to feel for the game. The Eagles hadn't scored in the entire second half to that point, so daring them to score twice in under ten minutes was sound game management.

Five weeks and a quarterback change to the much more competent and skilled Joe Milton is all it took for a rival team to flip this on its head and hand Schottenheimer his latest dumbfounding loss. The Cowboys defense was already hapless against Milton by the time their offense went conservative this time around in a few crucial spots, and the latest coaching missteps for the Cowboys at home again proved fatal to their chances of winning.

The Cowboys offense was moving the ball with some of the same ease they had against the Titans on Sunday, this time adding the same physical run game they had in the last meeting against the Eagles to really separate on the scoreboard early. They even continued to find new wrinkles like getting big plays on throws into the flats, such as KaVontae Turpin's touchdown to cash in on Quinnen Williams making a fourth down stop to go up 14-0.

The same way it felt against the Titans that more of the Cowboys scoring drives were truly necessary than needed if the defense could bow up just a little more, this Eagles game slowly but surely took on that feeling too. A 31-10 lead would quickly shrink to 31-17 when the contain on Milton was blown yet again, and he rolled out to hit Devonta Smith deep downfield for a touchdown against Malik Hooker. Now, it was really necessity time for the offense to keep the pressure on, and they came out and ran the ball three times in a row, lined up to punt, and had it blocked and returned for a touchdown to instantly turn the game into a dogfight.

At times the play-call menu was as deep as the Salvation Army kettle for Schotty against this Vic Fangio defense, and the Cowboys used it to stabilize the game thanks to Prescott putting the team on his back and scrambling for a touchdown to go up 38-24.

Unfortunately, more costly special teams play meant the Cowboys needed to go even deeper into the kettle offensively, and this is around the time it inexcusably started to run dry. The Eagles cashed in a QB rushing touchdown of their own from Milton to make it an eight point game.

Dallas had a chance a drive later to ice the game, but once again, Schotty had no feel for the game and went conservative once he got into field goal range. Brandon Aubrey's 40-yard field goal was no good, and the door remained open for the Eagles.

A drive later, Schottenheimer's offense needed to move the ball up by two points to either score or run out the clock. Inexplicably, on a fourth down with one yard to go for a first down to ice the game, Schottenheimer called a pass with no play-action instead of using Javonte Williams. After a disastrous sack they gave the Eagles the ball at nearly midfield. The defense could not keep the Eagles off the board on a drive that started at the Eagles 48, and the collapse was complete on Jake Elliott's 60-yard winning field goal.

The lost feel for the game was exemplified on that fourth down attempt. Of all the ways the Cowboys could get into slant patterns and high percentage throws for Prescott, if throwing the ball on 4th-and-1 was even the best decision, they did none of them on this sack play. No stack release for CeeDee Lamb who was the primary target, no play-action or disguise indicating they could run, and nowhere else for Dak to throw.

No winning streak this season is something Schottenheimer has to wear on his record. Whatever the multi-layered reasons are that the Cowboys have arrived at another December like a ship lost at sea, it falls on the coaching and scheming.

The week of drama around the NFL started with the last Cowboys head coach Mike McCarthy being put on blast by his own QB, but it didn't take long for the Cowboys to say "hold our turkey leg" and lose a game in such spectacular fashion that even their own former backup QB put them on blast, which they absolutely deserve.

This Cowboys loss goes a long way in setting up the Giants in year one of John Harbaugh to win the NFC East for the first time since 2011, rewarding a franchise that wasn't afraid to empower a head coach to make real changes and implant a real winning culture instantly with a playoff berth.
