Why Warriors' Yaxel Lendeborg calls Rookie of the Year possibility a ‘lose-lose'

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Why Warriors' Yaxel Lendeborg calls Rookie of the Year possibility a 'lose-lose' originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

Yaxel Lendeborg has a theory about potentially winning NBA Rookie of the Year: no matter what happens, somebody will have a complaint.

Lendeborg, the 11th overall pick in June's 2026 NBA draft, is averaging 21.7 points, 7.4 rebounds and 3.7 assists through his first 12 NBA games, and he was No. 1 on the NBA's Kia Rookie Ladder this week. Asked how badly he wants the award in an interview with NBA on Prime's Taylor Rooks, the Warriors rookie said he wants it, but he does not think it can be a clean win.

"Man, that right there, I mean, I want it pretty badly, but when you think about it, it's kind of like a lose-lose for me, you know?" Lendeborg told Rooks. "If I don't get it, 'Ah, he's 24, he couldn't get Rookie of the Year.' If I do get it, 'Ah, he won it because he's 24 years old.'"

The age question follows him because it is real. Lendeborg is 24 and arrived after a college career at Michigan, where he won an NCAA championship, a path that is older than the typical rookies. He did not dodge it, but he did not let it set the ceiling either.

"So it's like the award would be nice, but I'm trying to be an All-Star this year," Lendeborg said.

That is a rare goal for a rookie. Only 45 rookies have ever made the NBA All-Star Game, and only 10 have done it since the 1985 game, with Blake Griffin in 2011 the most recent.

The next few weeks might help Lendeborg make the case. Through 11 games, he was the only rookie averaging at least 20 points, seven rebounds and four assists.

Now Golden State needs him more. An MRI confirmed that Steph Curry has an oblique abdominal tear, and the Warriors said he will be re-evaluated in two weeks, with ESPN's Shams Charania reporting the team expects him to miss closer to a month. The Warriors have said Lendeborg already is their primary option in minutes without Curry.

Lendeborg scored 25 points with 10 rebounds in Wednesday's loss to the Houston Rockets after Curry left early. If he wants to settle the age debate, the next month without Curry might be his best chance.
