Justin Reid posts farewell message to Saints, New Orleans

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Saints have not announced any move involving safety Justin Reid, but it seems one is coming soon.

Reid asked for his release this week and he posted a farewell message to the team and the city of New Orleans on Friday.

“In life and sports, things don’t always go the way you planned or hoped they would,” Reid wrote. “Unfortunately, this is one of those moments. But that doesn’t change the love and respect I have for this organization or city. I truly want to see this team and this city thrive. No one deserves it more than New Orleans and the Saints.”

Reid joined the Saints as a free agent in 2025 and he started all 24 games he played for the team. He had 118 tackles, an interception and a half-sack in those appearances.
