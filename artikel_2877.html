Blue Cain splashes late 3, has something to say about it in beating Georgia Tech

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

ATLANTA—Blue Cain planned to play basketball for Georgia Tech before a coaching change in March 2023.

He ended up at Georgia, where he is now 4-0 against Georgia Tech after an 88-82 victory over the Yellow Jackets in McCamish Pavilion on Friday, Nov. 20.

The senior guard scored 15 points and sank 3 of 6 3-pointers ― including a shot-clock beating, long 3 with 44 seconds to go from in front of the Georgia bench after the Yellow Jackets closed to 83-78.

Cain picked up a loose ball near the GT midcourt logo, dribbled a few steps toward the hoop and launched a shot over Colby Garland.

"The shot clock was winding down so I just had to shoot it," he said. "I tried to get a look and just trusting myself."

Cain yelled a few words in an exchange with a Georgia Tech fan sitting on the other side of the court after the play.

"He was talking to me, but I mean I was talking back to him so I can't really blame him," Cain said.

Coach Mike White pulled Cain from behind and got in his face after the verbal back-and-forth with the fan.

Cain said White told him to show more maturity.

"In that situation, we could have got a technical if the ref wanted to," Cain said. "I'm a senior so I just can't respond like that."

Said White: "We showed a lot of positive emotion throughout the game, especially in the second half when we made a big individual play and didn't do a good job of just moving onto the next play. That's something that we've already addressed the last couple of timeouts. In the locker room after the game. … 'Let's move on. Let's celebrate after the game.' That said, huge shot, you know, and a lot of swag."

White had a good view of the shot and thought, as soon as it left Cain's hands, it had a good chance to go in.

"At first when he shot it, I was like, 'Oh," and I saw it go in and I was like 'Oh, (sh**)," said freshman forward Mading Kunay, who scored all 10 of his points in one first-half stretch. "Once he released it, I was like, 'Damn, that's Blue Cain.'"

After Georgia ran its record to 6-0 on the season, someone wearing Bulldog garb snuck into the postgame press conference and asked Tech's Tylis Jordan and Victor Valdes if they remembered the final score.

"Next thing, who runs this state?" the man said. "Let's go Dawgs!"

Security was asked to call the police as the person exited the room.

Georgia led by as much as 19 with 59 seconds to go in the first half, but the Yellow Jackets pulled within 3 during a half it shot 20-for-29 from the field.

Georgia has had 11 players in their career go 4-0 against Georgia Tech, according to the school. Cain could return for a fifth year under changes in NCAA eligibility rules.

"I just look at it as any other game," Cain said, "but it's definitely cool to go 4-0 against them."
