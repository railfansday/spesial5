All Disney Dreamlight Valley The Keepsake Sea biomes and critters

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Keepsake Sea is calling, and it promises quite a few new places to explore, with some adorable critters to meet along the way in Disney Dreamlight Valley. This world is quite unique, and there’s plenty to discover as you explore it.

Having a solid understanding of what to expect in this DLC before you dive in is crucial for preparing to play and also a great way to help you decide whether or not buying this optional add-on is right for you. To help you get acquainted with this pack, here are all of the biomes and critters featured in Disney Dreamlight Valley The Keepsake Sea.

There are three biomes included in The Keepsake Sea Expansion Pass, all of which are exclusive to owning this DLC in DDV. All of the regions you can visit around this special world are as follows.

Each of the biomes you can explore in The Keepsake Sea features a unique critter Companion for you to befriend. In total, there are three critters you can unlock in this DLC, all of which come in a variety of different colors.

All three types of critters are exclusive to The Keepsake Sea Expansion Pass, so purchasing it is the only way to find, meet, and befriend them. The only exception to this rule is that special versions of them may appear in the Premium Shop and be purchasable using Moonstones in the future, but if this happens, it will only be different versions of them, never the DLC exclusive ones.

If you’re looking for more critters to befriend, there are plenty to find around other DLC worlds. Some critters you may want to work on meeting and unlocking next are Hedgehogs, Sweet Bees, Geese, and Skunks.
