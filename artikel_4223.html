February 2027 is set to be a massive release window, with remakes and new releases of all kinds of major series hitting one after another. Because of this, a few titles have pushed their release dates back, opting instead to give February releases their time and allowing gamers' wallets a bit of time to recover.

Wuthering Waves is one of the biggest Gachas on the planet, and they do far better than most at answering player complaints. Let's go over exactly how!

Santa Monica Studio joins the teams leaning away from February 2027, as the previously planned God of War: Laufey has been delayed to May 20th, 2027. With how important Laufey’s individual performance needs to be, Santa Monica Studio is making the most of the time they have, refusing to fall for the age-old pull of rushing release in order to present the best game possible.

Santa Monica Studio is easily one of the most prestigious teams under Sony, releasing banger after banger with the God of War series. Coming off the announcement of remakes of the original trilogy, Santa Monica revealed a major gamble with God of War Laufey, the first title in the franchise without Kratos as the central playable character.

The game focuses on Kratos’ wife and Atreus’s mother, Laufey, one of the last Norse giants present in the new God of War’s setting. Trapped within a mystical realm of the afterlife, Laufey seems to be taking major notes from the PS2/PS3 God of War titles, pitting its protagonist against a truly magical set of foes with only one way out.

After some of the more grounded presentation and general combat of the last two God of War titles, Laufey seems like a perfect return to form, one that the team is making sure hits as hard as possible. The release date change is disappointing for eager fans, but it does wonders to dodge some other heavy hitters dropping in February.

If Laufey stuck to its original release plan, the game would be battling titans like Fable, Persona 4 Revival, and Metro 2039, all major releases gamers are setting aside cash and time for. Not that God of War Laufey wouldn’t be able to hang, but it's worth taking a step back if the team could afford to.

Being one of Sony’s flagship studios doesn’t mean that Santa Monica is free of danger, if their recent treatment of Suckerpunch is any indication, but ensuring a good release is best both for the team and the consumers. Laufey doesn’t have the immediate pull for long-time players who haven’t kept up with the new God of War titles, so it needs to stand on its own as strongly as possible.

Santa Monica is a team known for absolute polish, and their refusal to be rushed is a great sign that maintaining this quality is a top priority. Too many games have been released in recent years only to be cannibalized by post-launch patches that add functions that should already be there, a mistake Santa Monica Studio is being careful to avoid.

May is also a much cushier release month in terms of competing releases, with no major drops ready to clash with God of War Laufey’s acclaim. The new title by Santa Monica Studio may feel like a spinoff meant to buy time, but the opportunity to learn more about Laufey, such an impactful character in the new God of War series, is incredibly exciting for any fans of that saga.

Finally getting her side of the story is a phenomenal way of closing the saga for now, allowing Kratos to rest on his laurels while expanding the wildly fantastical side of not only the Norse saga, but the entire worldwide mythos of God of War.
