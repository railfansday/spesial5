GTA 6's Physical Launch Has Been An Astronomical Success, Despite No Discs

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

All eyes have been on the launch of GTA 6 this month, with the game's so-called physical launch being a particularly interesting litmus test for the future of the industry.

It turns out that even without any discs, GTA 6 has proven to be a monumental success in terms of physical sales. This comes via Christopher Dring at The Game Business, who states that the game enjoyed the largest physical launch since 2019 in the UK, generating £32 million in revenue in just three days.

He goes on to state that GTA 6 is the "No.17 biggest physical game launch in the UK since records began in 1995".

In France, it's estimated that over 1 million physical copies were sold in the first three days, a figure that Dring states is comparable to GTA 5 back in 2013. In Spain, meanwhile, the game achieved the largest physical launch in the chart's history.

It's safe to say then that this has comfortably dwarfed pretty much anything that Nintendo has put out in recent years. Normally of course, we'd be celebrating such success – even if the game isn't actually available on Nintendo systems – but the fact that it's a code-in-a-box release raises a few eyebrows.

With GTA 6 being such a success, Sony will no doubt view this as validation that it made the right decision in ceasing the production of discs from 2028. Other large companies will also likely see it as proof that customers don't care about discs anymore, and will likely lean toward download codes much sooner than Sony's cut-off date.

Nintendo itself, meanwhile, probably won't do anything different – at least for the time being. Stats from recent financial reports indicate that Switch and Switch 2 players still value cartridges, with physical sales still making up close to half of the company's total first-party sales.

Still, it's a troubling result for folks who are still all-in on physical games. It certainly indicates that while there are plenty of folks who begrudge Sony's decision to stop the production of discs, there are many, many more people who just don't give a hoot.
