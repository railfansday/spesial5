GTA 6 Preloading Is Causing Major Download Traffic Spikes in Europe

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

If you needed another clue as to how big GTA 6 is going to be, internet service providers in a number of countries are reporting inflated traffic figures in the wake of preload access.

Millions of eager fans have pre-ordered the open world action title on PS Store — in fact, it's the most pre-ordered game on the digital storefront ever — and ahead of its launch, they're able to download the game to their PS5s to ensure it's ready to roll come 19th November.

This process, which sees people trying to download a game that's roughly 212 GB in size, is causing inflated traffic in several European countries.

A German ISP, 1&1, has said (thanks Golem) that connections to PlayStation Network and Xbox Live were 10 times higher than usual "during the night of November 9th to 10th." Vodafone also reported a spike in the same period.

Over in Sweden, Philip Wagrowski, business manager at GlobalConnect Sweden, has stated an increase in traffic of 50 per cent on the night when preloading began. Danish and Estonian ISPs report similar findings too.

In the UK, Virgin Media O2 reports that the 10th November saw record-breaking data volume and traffic thanks to preloading GTA 6.

In short, then, a lot of people have been downloading the game this week, and it's made a pretty significant impact on the ISPs.

Over in the United States, Spectrum is currently running a promotion in which qualifying users can take advantage of a temporary boost to 1Gbps, or 2Gbps if they're already on the 1Gbps plan. This comes at no extra cost, and is expressly for the purpose of downloading GTA 6 as fast as possible.

It's pretty interesting to see the reactions from global ISPs; the game really is setting up to be a massive moment when it finally drops next week.
