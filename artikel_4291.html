Zelda: Ocarina Of Time's Director Was Chosen For His Ability To "Solve Problems Very Aggressively"

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for Switch 2 gained a new director in HokWai Ng, a developer at Nintendo who started his career with Wii Fit Plus before becoming involved with planning on Tears of the Kingdom for the Switch.

In a new interview with Eiji Aonuma and Hidemaro Fujibayashi over on Polygon, Aonuma commented on the reason why Ng was chosen to lead the development on Ocarina of Time. He cited Ng's ability to "solve problems very aggressively, very proactively" during his time on Tears of the Kingdom, while his idea for future projects made Aonuma believe that he would be the right fit for a remake.

"He was involved with the planning on Tears of the Kingdom, and he was someone on that project that I saw find and solve problems very aggressively, very proactively. And so I knew that would be a great opportunity to see him do more. I thought it'd be really interesting to use him on this project. In fact, at the end of Tears of the Kingdom, he started to present some ideas for future projects that made me think, "Okay, yeah, you're interested in something like a remake. I think you've got your own ideas here. Perhaps you should work on this as a director." And this has been a really good experience. I think [it was] a good decision."

It's honestly so lovely to see Nintendo continue to work with younger generations of developers and provide them with such opportunities. It's certainly not something we're used to seeing here in the west, that's for sure.

Needless to say, we think HokWai Ng has done a remarkable job on Ocarina of Time, and we're keen to see what else he might do with the Legend of Zelda franchise in the future.
