Fortnite Faces Gambling Backlash Over Pirate Shop Tokens

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

There was a lot of buzz when Fortnite introduced the Pirate Shop tokens last week. Everyone piled onto the servers in a disorderly queue hoping to reap weeks of free Item Shop cosmetics.

But now that the honeymoon phase is over, and we've been forced to listen to that opening One Piece riff a billion times, players are accusing Epic Games of using predatory practices just to compete with GTA 6.

The problem is that Epic Games hasn't disclosed the odds of finding a token, meaning that every single chest has essentially been turned into a free loot box. And just like with loot boxes, there's only a slim chance that you win big. Most of the time, you'll get a few throwaway consumables that let you fire your name into the sky. Best case, you get a new pickaxe.

"I think the idea is to introduce a gambling addiction to the game," @RealGojifan01 argued. "And, unfortunately, it's working on a lot of people. This is why I am eternally grateful I don't care enough about the token because there's nothing I want from the Item Shop right now."

"I hate making tweets like this, but I truly think there’s no coming back for Fortnite honestly," added @kymanji. "If one of the most popular games in the world has to rely on straight gambling directed towards children just to keep retention rates up, it won’t last against GTA 6."

It's not strictly gambling in the legal sense, as players aren't paying to open chests, but the similarities to randomized reward systems are hard to ignore. The real issue with this mechanic is that it preys on FOMO, incentivizing people to play nothing else because that next game, that next chest, could be the one.

Every single chest is a roll of the dice, and the lucky few out of the millions playing who get a Pirate Shop token will inevitably boast about it online and go viral, making it feel more common than it is—intensifying the urge to keep playing. All of which is compounded by the invisible timer ticking away while you hunt, as the tokens are only available for one season.

"Epic has resorted more and more to gambling, FOMO, using more hostile engagement & retention metrics to keep us playing (harder challenges, longer grinds, more exclusivity)," said @LazerLyss. "Epic has really lowered their standards and is willing to do anything in order to get sales."

"Epic Games added a new 'Pirate Token' which doesn't have any public odds of getting it, which creates anti-consumer field/FOMO," @QuantSet posted, tagging the FTC. "The game is majorly played by children, they can do anything if they wouldn't get it, so take action against Epic Games please."

"Fortnite has just turned into a giant retention bait game. It doesn't blow up for anything actually cool or fun, it only blows up for mechanics specifically made to hold retention," said @ImNitr0h. "Sprites were bloat made to exploit FOMO, and now the pirate shop exists as basically gambling."

People are poking fun at this next post, but it really sums up exactly why players are critical of the Pirate Shop tokens, and demanding that Epic release the odds and move away from these kinds of FOMO tactics. The hunt is fun for some, but risks becoming addictive for others.

Some are even trying to sell tokens for $9,000 - or offering to open thousands of chests in exchange for thousands of dollars.

"I'm gonna have to uninstall Fortnite because this has genuinely turned into hell," @MacaroniButSoup said. "This stupid token is literally such a shi**y way to get [people] to play. This is causing many, including myself, mental health issues not being able to get this once in a lifetime token. I can't do it.Even when I want to play a different mode or map, I can't help but think that i probably would have gotten the token had i just kept going - I can't enjoy Fortnite at all in general cus it keeps circling back to grinding for the token. I'm unwell."

Epic has confirmed that everyone has an equal opportunity to find Pirate Shop tokens, and that drops work like any other loot, but we don't know the specifics beyond that. There have been countless debates over whether breaking open chests works, whether worse players have better odds, and whether tokens are being removed from the loot pool altogether once too many have been acquired, leaving the community hunting for ghosts.

However they work, it's obvious that the Pirate Shop is Epic's way to keep the community playing Fortnite when GTA 6 drops, but as Epic Partner @magickbones said, it probably won't work anyway. "Everyone is getting burnt out," they said. "More people will quit in frustration and switch to GTA. What was the point."

Epic points out gambling is against its development rules, but that "paid random mechanics" do not fall under that same umbrella.
