Hegseth Impeachment Vote Imminent

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

There is a significant chance that one or more articles will be approved.

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth testifies about the Pentagon’s fiscal year 2027 budget request before the House Armed Services Committee in Washington, D.C., April 29, 2026.

Eight articles of impeachment against Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth have been proposed in the House by Representative Thomas Massie, a libertarian Republican from Kentucky who lost his seat when the president pushed for his ouster in a primary earlier this year. (The Trump-endorsed candidate, Ed Gallrein, retained the seat for the GOP last week despite the wave that will give Democrats solid majorities in both congressional chambers come January.)

Though still narrowly controlled by Republicans for the next two lame duck months, the House will be forced to consider Massie’s proposal. Impeachment is a privileged motion under House rules; action of some kind must be taken on it within two days after it is filed. Impeachments can stretch beyond a single session of Congress, so if the House impeaches Hegseth, a Senate trial could be postponed until January, when Democrats will have a commanding majority in the upper chamber.
