The 10 Best Jones Road Beauty Products, According to <i><em>Harper’s Bazaar </em></i>Editors

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

From creamy gel eyeliners to plumping lip treatments, Bazaar editors swear by these products

Celebrity makeup artist Bobbi Brown wears many hats: author, no-makeup makeup pioneer, and, of course, creator of viral beauty brands. Along with the eponymous Bobbi Brown line she founded in 1991, Brown counts launching Jones Road Beauty among her proudest moments in the industry.

“Jones Road was born from a search for something that didn't yet exist. Because it didn't exist, I had to create it,” the founder previously told Harper’s Bazaar. “What I wanted was the makeup equivalent of a Swiss Army Knife: easy, cool, multi-purpose products that could be used to nail any look, whether it be no-makeup makeup or something with more drama.”

To assemble our list of the best Jones Road Beauty products, Harper’s Bazaar editors searched deep in their makeup bags to narrow down the top eyeshadows, concealers, blushes, and more. Read on for the ones that deserve a place in your collection.

“Dark circles and I have become well-acquainted in the four years since I had my son. This new brightening concealer from Jones Road is one of the best I’ve tried to wipe them out. It’s a thick formula, so only a small amount is needed to provide crease-free coverage that makes you look like you actually slept.” —Jenna Rosenstein, beauty director

“If you’re looking for a mess-free concealer to add to your beauty collection, allow me to introduce you to the Face Pencil. It practically melts into my skin on contact, and unlike other concealers I’ve used in the past, the Face Pencil’s sharpener tip makes targeted application a breeze.” —Tiffany Dodson Davis, beauty commerce editor

“Not only is this blush stick easy to use and travel-friendly (I often toss it in my work bag to apply on-the-go), but its buttery formula is ideal for building and blending, whether I’m going for a gentle flush or full-on blush blindness.” —TDD

“This eyeliner has become my everyday staple. It’s ultra-creamy and pigmented in the most perfect, rich espresso brown. And it’s easy to apply with precision using the Jones Road Beauty eyeliner brush. I achieve the best smoked winged liner look every time.” —Olivia Alchek, senior designer

“Granted, it’s not for everyone (especially those with oily skin), but I love the natural, dewy glow Jones Road Beauty’s Miracle Balm imparts on highlight zones like my cheekbones, the bridge of my nose, and my brow bone. The Miami color is my ideal luminous peach shade. It gives me a subtle sun-kissed look, especially since it’s so sheer.” —Katie Intner, beauty market and commerce editor

“Just like their face pencils, the brand’s lip pencils glide on smoothly without awkward skips. I like wearing them alone, topped with a clear gloss, or to set the stage for a blurred lip look. It’s truly hard to go wrong with a staple that’s so versatile and easy, especially since the pencil comes in 12 different shade options.” —TDD

“I have tried a hundred thousand lip treatments—and so many of them are disappointing when it comes to making a noticeable difference for dry, cracked, chapped lips. This one from Jones Road really delivers on all fronts: it’s glossy, it’s pretty, and it softens and smooths my lips like nothing else. The metal applicator is deliciously cooling, and the formula is rich in plumping peptides and skin-protecting antioxidants. I wear a fat layer before bed, and I use it as a mask before doing my makeup (though I always wipe it off before lipliner if I want my lip color to last; it’s that hydrating).” —JR

“For moments when I don’t have a ton of time to spend on my eye look, I reach for the Jones Road Beauty Just A Sec shadow pot, and the shade Bronze in particular. It provides just enough shimmer without being over the top, making the shadow easy to transition from day to night.” —TDD

“I’m a big fan of multi-use products, and the Hippie Stick definitely fits the bill. It’s a buttery balm that I apply anywhere that needs a little extra moisture, from my lips and cuticles to my elbows and heels—which is why I usually keep two on hand. Plus, its fragrant blend of orange peel oil, lavandin oil, and spearmint leaf oil smells absolutely incredible.” —TDD

“’Tis the season for a good body oil. This lightweight, fast-absorbing option is the perfect addition to my body care routine in the colder months, with or without my go-to lotion. Its formula leaves my skin looking glossy and feeling super soft, especially right after the shower.” —TDD
