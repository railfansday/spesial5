You Can Now Steal Jeremiah Brent’s Signature Style With His New Rug Collection on Amazon

The muted tones will work seamlessly in any room.

A rug can be one of a designer's most important, transformative tools: it can anchor furniture, establish a color palette, and make a room feel inherently cozier. If you doubt a rug’s superpowers, Jeremiah Brent might have you thinking otherwise. The celebrity designer just teamed up with Loloi on a collection of rugs that bring the focus underfoot.

While most rug collections are packed with stripy or floral-fueled layers, Brent found inspiration in architecture: If you look closely, you’ll spot rugs with tile-inspired repeats, “carved” silhouettes, and strong structural lines. Take the Obelisk collection, for example, where a plush, hand-knotted wool surface is expertly paired with notched corners. You know, the kind you might see in a historic home on a vintage wooden chair.

If you want to command more attention, check out the Fori Collection. The assortment of meticulously uniform patterns evokes the soothing repetition of mosaic tiles. Or, for something down the straight and narrow, the angular lines of the Axis Collection will look great in modern homes. Plus, the Axis’s 100 percent polyester construction is easy to clean, making it a great choice for high-trafficked areas like a hallway or playroom.

Not only are all three rug collections available on Amazon, so you can add them to your next Prime order, but they all come in muted nature tones. Slightly autumnal, yet versatile enough to look good year-round.

While Brent’s Loloi line is reasonably priced—it’s rare to find a five-foot by seven-foot option for under $250—adding a rug to your space can be both a big financial and spatial undertaking. If you’re not willing to move your furniture or buy a new layer, there are also decorative throw pillows for $100 a pop.

Regardless of which layers you add to your cart, one thing’s for sure: This drop is poised to beautify your home from the ground up.
