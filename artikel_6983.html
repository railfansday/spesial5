Outmarket AI: Head of Content

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Outmarket AI · Remote (US) · Full-time · Reports to the SVP of Marketing

Insurance still runs on manual work. Most policies are sold through agents and brokers, and the people who know the work best are buried in the documents: checking policies line by line, comparing quotes, building proposals, on nights and weekends. We think that's backwards. The best people in insurance should spend their time advising clients.

Outmarket, the AI platform built for insurance, makes that possible. Account teams drop in their documents and get back finished work to review, with every finding linked to the page it came from. Outmarket connects to the systems they already use. The judgment stays with the person; the groundwork doesn't.

It's working. More than 10,000 people at over 300 agencies use Outmarket, including 30 of the top 100 U.S. insurance brokers. Most of those agencies found us through other customers. We raised a $34.5M Series B led by SignalFire four months after announcing our Series A because customers were pulling us forward faster than we planned.

Customers who want to talk. About 300 agencies use Outmarket, and most will go on record about it. Few content leaders start with that many stories ready to tell.

First in the seat. You'll be our first content hire, joining alongside a new Head of Product Marketing and Head of Growth. You'll decide what Outmarket sounds and looks like.

Real creative room, and a budget to use it. There's budget for specialist contractors such as editors, shooters and designers, and you'll have room to make work that doesn't look like typical B2B content.

A direct line. You'll work with the SVP of Marketing, the founders and customers, and you'll see what your work does for pipeline within weeks.

You own video, social, customer stories and written content. You'll build the function hands-on: you script, interview, direct and write yourself, and you work with a small group of specialist contractors instead of an in-house crew. Pipeline is the team's main number, so success means content that brings brokers into sales conversations, not just views.

Our buyers are owners, producers and operations leaders at commercial insurance agencies. It's a relationship-driven industry, and we're still learning where these buyers spend their attention online. Part of your job is finding out, then making the content that reaches them there.

Run the customer story program. Choose which customers to feature with our client services team, plan each story before the shoot, run the interview, and cut one day of filming into pieces for the website, ads, sales and events. Make it easy for busy customers to say yes, and never over-ask the same ones.

Ship every week. Build a steady publishing rhythm with a bench of specialist contractors. Find them, brief them clearly, know what things should cost, and review closely so quality holds as volume grows.

Turn our events into content. Work with our Director of Industry Events & Programs to plan what to capture at the conferences and customer dinners we already run, and publish soon after.

Find where brokers pay attention, whether that's LinkedIn, YouTube, industry media or events. Put our effort where the audience is, not where content teams usually start.

Build leaders' voices. Help our founders and other leaders post every week, each in their own voice.

Own written content, including the site, social and long-form pieces, and keep everything we publish clear and sharp.

Measure what your content does for pipeline and use that to decide what to make next.

5+ years in B2B content, brand or creative, including time owning a content program end to end.

You've personally made content that grew an audience or produced demos and pipeline, and you can explain the numbers and your part in them.

You're a hands-on maker. You've scripted, directed, interviewed and edited video and social content yourself, not only managed an agency or writers.

You've produced customer stories people actually watch, and you know how to run a great interview.

You've shipped on a small budget with contractors and can say what each piece cost.

You've built content at an early-stage company, ideally Series A to C, and want to build from scratch again.

High agency: you find the work instead of waiting for it.

Ownership: you own the outcome, not a task list.

Startup pace: we ship weekly instead of planning quarterly.

You don't need to know insurance. Curiosity about brokers is the requirement, and becoming fluent is part of the job.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
