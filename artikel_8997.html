balzar arquitectos pairs 19th-century modernist interiors with wabi-sabi materiality in valencia apartment renovation

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

the restoration of the 1890 residence balances original nolla mosaics, wrought iron, and plaster moldings with lime wash, travertine, and curated contemporary furnishings.

Studio edited by christina vergopoulou November 9, 2026 3 min

Located in the historic center of Valencia, Spain, Ciutat Vella Apartment is an interior renovation by Balzar Arquitectos set within an 1890 residential building. Sited in an urban context characterized by layered historical traces, the project approaches preservation not as a static museum display, but as an active framework for contemporary domestic life. Original architectural elements, including geometric Nolla mosaic tile floors, wrought ironwork, decorative ceiling moldings, solid timber doors, and surviving polychrome wall fragments, were retained and restored to guide the layout and spatial rhythm.

Guided by a wabi-sabi philosophy prioritizing material patina and natural imperfection, the intervention by Balzar Architectural Studio introduces a restrained palette of lime plaster, unfilled travertine, solid wood, linen, and wool. Surface textures take precedence over color, complemented by diffused daylight filtering through historic apertures. The interior blends vintage pieces sourced from Nekonato Gallery with artworks from CLC Arte, custom site-specific joinery by Balzar Arquitectos, and contemporary furnishings, including seating by New Works and luminaires by Venicem, establishing a balanced conversation between 19th-century craft and modern minimalism.
