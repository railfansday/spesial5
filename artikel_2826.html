Week 11 power rankings: 49ers a consensus top-3 team

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The San Francisco 49ers had a bit of a scare in Week 10, but managed to win their eighth game of the season and improve to 8-1. The team's stranglehold on the NFC West remains intact, as well as Brock Purdy's MVP-caliber season.

As such, the 49ers didn't move much in most power rankings heading into Week 11. They were already a top-three team by most experts, and that stayed firm. Two outlets put the 49ers at No. 1, while the rest oscillated between Nos. 2 and 3.

No matter how you slice it, the 49ers are one of the elite teams in the NFL heading into the home stretch. They'll look to secure their place at the top of the league against arguably the best defensive team in the NFL next week — the Minnesota Vikings.

Here is a roundup of the NFL power rankings heading into Week 11.

The defensive performance wasn't all that inspiring Sunday, but the Niners are re-incorporating players on that side of the ball. They're still not completely whole on offense, either, WRs Mike Evans and De'Zhaun Stribling the most notable absentees right now. But when you're averaging 9.4 yards per play anyway, then you're a pretty dangerous operation − maybe a Super one, though the upcoming matchups with Minnesota and Seattle will be much truer litmus tests than Dallas was.

The 49ers put on some offensive show in Dallas, running up 584 yards and scoring six touchdowns against the Cowboys. Then again, given how soft the Dallas defense has been this season, maybe that's not as impressive as it sounds. Besides, everyone knew the 49ers were explosive on offense. We all just thought they were better on defense. They were torn apart for 343 yards and five touchdown passes by Dak Prescott, who kept getting the Cowboys back into striking distance of a miracle comeback. If Dallas' defense was even mediocre, with the ability to make just one more stop, the 49ers would have lost.

The offense was great in the victory over the Cowboys, but the defense gave up 40. That can't happen. They have to be better on that side of the ball.

The San Francisco 49ers continue to look like the best team in the NFC, though they did have some hiccups against the Cowboys on Sunday.

Brock Purdy had another MVP-caliber outing, as Christian McCaffrey, Deebo Samuel, and other 49ers playmakers repeatedly knifed through Dallas' suspect defense. The deadline trade for receiver Keenan Allen paid immediate dividends, and it's fun to think about how potent the offense could be once Allen is fully integrated into the game plan and Mike Evans is healthy again.

However, San Francisco's pass rush continues to underwhelm, and its defense struggled overall in Dallas. Next week's matchup with the Kirk Cousins-led Vikings will be a massive test.

The 49ers cooked the Cowboys' defense, putting up 46 points and 584 yards. Dallas' defense is dreadful but it's still hard to pile up close to 600 yards in any NFL game.

The way the 49ers have climbed to the top of the NFL in yards per game is worth studying. Brock Purdy was famously Mr. Irrelevant in the draft and now he's an MVP candidate. Christian McCaffrey was at a point in which age and touches should have started to chip away at his effectiveness, but he is tied for the NFL lead with 13 total touchdowns. Draft experts laughed at the Kaelon Black pick in the third round; he's one of the best backup running backs in the league. Deebo Samuel Sr.'s career looked like it might be over after last season, but he has five touchdowns in six games. The 49ers had to give up only a sixth-round draft pick for Keenan Allen in a trade and he had five catches for 61 yards on Sunday.

The conclusion: Kyle Shanahan is your NFL Coach of the Year.

It was incredibly fun to see this team against a JV defense. The gap between the best offensive playcaller and the worst defensive playcaller in the NFL is absolutely off-the-cliff steep. A reminder that if you're going to bring anything but your A-game against Kyle Shanahan, you're better off not showing up at all.
