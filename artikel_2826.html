Eagles injury report: Zack Baun, Dontayvion Wicks ruled out vs. Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Eagles injury report: Zack Baun, Dontayvion Wicks ruled out vs. Steelers originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Eagles linebacker Zack Baun (back) and receiver Dontayvion Wicks (ankle/foot) have been ruled out for the Week 11 game against the Steelers.

Baun missed practice all week with his back injury. Wicks was a limited participant all week but that foot injury was added to the report on Friday.

The good news is that DeVonta Smith (hamstring) does not have a game status and is going to make his return after missing three games before the bye week.

Out: LB Zack Baun (back), WR Dontayvion Wicks (ankle/foot)

Questionable: RB Will Shipley (foot/illness)

Expect Jihaad Campbell and Jeremiah Trotter Jr. to start at linebacker against the Steelers in Week 11. Campbell and Trotter had been in a rotation earlier in the year but Campbell missed the two games before the bye week. In addition to Baun, the Eagles were also without Mondon (ankle) on Thursday and Friday and he's doubtful going into the weekend.

Edge rusher Jonathan Greenard (ankle) did not practice on Friday as he got a bit of rest. But he's good to go against the Steelers.

Shipley is questionable because he is still on Injured Reserve. His practice window was opened for this week so he would need to be activated by 4 p.m. on Saturday to be eligible to play on Sunday.
