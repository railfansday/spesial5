Rookie TE Marlin Klein scores first career TD, Texans lead 26-3

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

C.J. Stroud threw his third touchdown of the night, giving the Texans a 26-3 lead on the Colts with 4:19 left in the third quarter.

Stroud hit rookie tight end Marlin Klein for a 13-yard score. It was Klein’s first career touchdown.

Klein had only one catch this season but has four for 24 yards tonight.

Stroud is 18-of-23 for 179 yards with three touchdowns and an interception.

The Texans led 17-3 at halftime but got a safety when Will Anderson sacked Daniel Jones in the end zone. Anderson leads the NFL with 11.5 sacks, a half sack short of his career high from last season.

The Colts have lost running back Seth McGowan, who was ruled out with a hamstring injury. Tight end Tyler Warren left the game late in the first half and remains on the sideline, but the Colts have not provided an update.
