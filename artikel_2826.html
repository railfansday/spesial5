Ravens rule out Kyle Hamilton with a hip injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Ravens safety Kyle Hamilton was injured in the second quarter and briefly returned after halftime. His night, though, is finished.

The Ravens ruled out Hamilton with a hip injury.

He has three tackles, including a tackle for loss, and a pass defensed.

The Jaguars lead 23-3 in the fourth quarter after Cam Little’s second-half field goals of 45 and 47 yards.

The Ravens had 25 yards and no first downs in the third quarter and have only 126 yards and seven first downs for the game.
