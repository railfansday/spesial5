Luther Burden scores 56-yard touchdown, Bears lead Bucs 14-0

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The last time the Bears played at home, Luther Burden went off for 130 yards.

Caleb Williams connected with Burden on third-and-8 with a short pass over the middle, and Burden turned on the jets to outrun the Tampa Bay defense and score a 56-yard touchdown.

It's the fourth touchdown reception of the season for Burden.

So far, the Bears have converted all three of their third-down attempts on their first two drives.

The Buccaneers went three-and-out on their opening possession. After going 0-of-3 passing last week in heavy rain, Baker Mayfield completed his first passing attempt of the day for a 3-yard gain.
