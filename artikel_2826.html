Yankees news: Luis Gil signs with Detroit Tigers

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

MLB.com | Jason Beck: Obviously the big news of the day on Sunday league-wide was that Tarik Skubal would be signing with the Mets for a monster nine-year, $450 million deal, but he wasn't the only 2024 award winner who came to terms on a new contract.

Two days after the Yankees non-tendered him, Luis Gil has a new home. The 28-year-old andd former American League Rookie of the Year inked a one-year pact with the Detroit Tigers yesterday afternoon, worth $2.3 million. Jeff Passan at ESPN broke the news. Gil's injury-abbreviated 2026 season in New York was rough, with the right-hander pitching to a 4.61 ERA with more walks (18) than strikeouts, and the dynamite stuff that led him to success two years ago had faded already. Given the Yankees' rotation depth, it makes sense Gil would look elsewhere for more opportunities. We do sincerely wish him the best of luck; he was a fun Yankee at his peak.

MLB Trade Rumors | Erik Beaston: Depending on how you feel about free agent outfielder Randy Arozarena, MLB Trade Rumors has good news or bad news for you. Arozarena would theoretically fit for the Yankees as a right-handed bit to offset a lot of lefty thump. But Philadelphia may also be in pursuit of the former Ray and Mariner, and that's where the author predicts Arozarena will end up. Remember, Arozarena has at least been on the Yankees' radar already.

Denver Post | Sean Keeler: Five years after the Rockies dealt franchise cornerstone Nolan Arenado, the trauma (understandably) lingers. I'm not surprised. I was in Colorado when the Rockies dealt the third baseman and the entire state was gutted (OK, that might be a slight exaggeration, but only slight). So how does this relate to the Yankees, you ask?

Well, drawing on the Arenado trade as a blueprint of what not to do, Keeler takes a look at what a theoretical haul for Colorado All-Star thumper Hunter Goodman should look like if the Rox were to deal their superstar backstop to the Bronx. How does Dax Kilby, Carlos Lagrange, and Bryce Cunningham sound to Yankee fans? The club's No. 1 prospect, its No. 1 pitching prospect, and another top-1o organizational prospect for Goodman.
