Jaguars vs. Giants: How to Watch Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Today, the Jacksonville Jaguars (7-2) will take on the New York Giants (5-3-1) in Week 11.

Giants head coach John Harbaugh had some bizarre comments about the Jaguars on Thursday. When asked about Jacksonville's recent rise to becoming a top team, Harbaugh said he "could care less" (although he probably meant "couldn't care less"). He could've taken the high road and said, "Yeah, they're good, we're in for a tough matchup," and moved on. He quoted Bill Parcells instead.

"It was interesting listening to Coach Harbaugh's media," Jaguars head coach Liam Coen said Friday. "It's like, yeah, you are what your record is, that's a good thing. But any given Sunday—the NFL is the NFL. I was with the Rams in '22 when you lost to the 0-13 Jets. It is any given Sunday, anything can happen. And I do think our group believes that. They know that. I think they're a little bit ticked, especially on the defensive side from some of the things that happened on Sunday that wasn't up to our standard. So, I think when you have a hungry group that feels as if they have not done anything yet. Nothing. We're in the second half of the season. A lot to prove, a lot of work, more games to be played than we even just did and went through. We have new additions now where this is our football team. This is an opportunity as we move forward throughout these next few weeks to go and put it on tape with this team, our final football team of who we are for the rest of the season. Let's go put together four quarters of our best because we have yet to do it yet."
