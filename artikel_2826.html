Packers Injuries: Entire roster healthy enough to practice on Saturday

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Green Bay Packers have an odd schedule this week, as the team prepares for a Wednesday night game against the Los Angeles Rams, coming off a bye week. The Packers' players got back on Friday, following three days off after the team "flushed," in the words of head coach Matt LaFleur, their game against the Minnesota Vikings on Monday.

Here's the good news: Every single player on the Packers' 53-man roster was active in practice today, at least in limited fashion. Yes, that means receivers Skyy Moore and Bo Melton and cornerback Brandon Cisse, who were unable to finish the game against the Vikings, were suited up for action on Saturday.

On top of that, players like running back Josh Jacobs, center Sean Rhyan, guard Kevin Zeitler and linebacker Zaire Franklin were also able to get looks, after not playing at all against the Vikings. The Packers are not releasing an official injury report today — meaning we'll only get two of these before the team travels to Los Angeles on Tuesday.

Jacobs, coming off surgery for a fractured finger, is wearing a cast around his fingers in practice right now.

Left tackle Jordan Morgan also appears to have a cast on his left wrist, based on practice video.

Based on the individual drills portion of practice that the media is available for, it looks like the open right guard competition is starting out with both Aaron Banks and Kevin Zeitler sharing reps at the position this week. Last week, Banks was on the bench, as he was dealing with two injuries, while Zeitler was injured during the team's Thursday practice and couldn't go against Minnesota. So Travis Glover, who made the start at right guard last week, appears to be the third option at the position going into Rams week. It's also worth noting that on the offensive line John Williams, the team's third-string center, got the start, but Sean Rhyan is now in that role in practice — a sign he'll be able to play in the upcoming game.
