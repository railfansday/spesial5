Which free agents could help the Diamondbacks against right-handed pitching?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

PHOENIX — The Arizona Diamondbacks reportedly have a clear offensive need they want to address this offseason: their production against right-handed pitching.

According to MLB insider Jim Bowden, the D-Backs will "explore" ways to better balance the lineup against righties, especially after ranking 24th in MLB with a .235 batting average against right-handed pitching and 26th with a .697 OPS. In comparison, Arizona ranked first in the league against left-handed pitchers with a .275 batting average.
