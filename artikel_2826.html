NBA champ blasts Luka Doncic, Lakers ahead of clash with Spurs for best in West

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Lakers' early-season schedule has been a point of contention, but the team's biggest test of the season is on Friday when Los Angeles takes on the Spurs.

San Antonio earned its 15th win of the season Monday, as did the Lakers. The two West rivals boast the best record in the conference, but Spurs color commentator Sean Elliott isn't convinced by Los Angeles' strong start to the campaign.

Jacob Tobey and Sean Elliott smiles during the game between the Utah Jazz and San Antonio Spurs at the Frost Bank Center in San Antonio, Texas on October 9, 2024. NBAE via Getty Images

"Well, the Lakers aren't gonna win the NBA championship. They're not," Elliott said bluntly on the game broadcast Monday as San Antonio defeated the Kings.

"I mean, they'll be fun to watch. They have really good players. I like Mamu in a Laker uniform. Plus, your best player always, he's gotta be a stalwart on the defensive end of the floor. I firmly believe that."

Elliott, a former NBA champion with the Spurs in 1999, built a reputation for being a solid two-way player during his career, particularly on the defensive end of the floor. Clearly, he is not a believer that Doncic has made many strides in that area, despite what many people say.

He went on to list the Spurs, Thunder, Knicks and 76ers as potential championship teams. In spite of the mass amount of injuries for Oklahoma City, the former NBA champion believes they have a better chance at this year's title than the Lakers do.

Luka Doncic #77 of the Los Angeles Lakers looks on during the game against the Utah Jazz on November 23, 2026 at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, California. NBAE via Getty Images

San Antonio Spurs center Victor Wembanyama walks up court between plays in the second half of an NBA basketball game against the New Orleans Pelicans, Saturday, Nov. 21, 2026, in San Antonio. AP Photo/Darren Abate

The heavy usage of Luka Doncic has also been a sticking point for skeptics. Doncic is central to helping the Lakers make a deep playoff run, but many argue his workload is unsustainable throughout the regular season and beyond.

Earlier on the broadcast, Elliott praised Doncic for his otherworldly performance this season, but noted that his usage rate quickly puts the purple and gold out of championship contention.

He is averaging just over 36 minutes per game. But that is nothing new for the Slovenian sharpshooter. Doncic has averaged between 32 and 37 minutes every season of his nine-year career.

Nevertheless, Friday's game should be a fair litmus test for the Lakers and their skeptics.
