NFL makes surprising ‘Sunday Night Football’ change for Week 12 featuring unexpected teams

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Raiders and Browns will get primetime treatment in a couple of weeks.

In the first primetime flex of the 2026 NFL season, the league bumped the Patriots-Chargers matchup out of "Sunday Night Football," slotting Las Vegas' Week 12 visit to Cleveland into the coveted window on Nov. 29.

The Patriots and Chargers will instead kick off at 4:05 p.m. ET on CBS, while the Titans-Jaguars game, originally scheduled for that time slot, will move to 1 p.m. ET on Fox.

Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Fernando Mendoza (15) passes against the Seattle Seahawks during an NFL football game in Las Vegas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/John Locher

The move comes as New England's season appears to be teetering following a blowout loss to the Lions in Munich, Germany, on Sunday to fall to 5-5 on the year.

Patriots quarterback Drake Maye left that game during the first quarter after going down with an ankle injury, and it's still unclear when he will return to the field.

And although the Chargers got their first win of the season on Monday night, they have been the most disappointing team in football, sitting at 1-8.

The flex will give No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza his first primetime start.

Cleveland Browns quarterback Deshaun Watson (4) celebrates a touchdown against the Houston Texans on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, in Cleveland. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

The Indiana product made his first career start Sunday following the trade of Kirk Cousins to Minnesota, throwing for 143 yards with a rushing touchdown during a loss to the Seahawks.

Cleveland, meanwhile, is surprisingly in playoff contention, giving the NFL a more compelling matchup to showcase in primetime.

The Browns head into their Week 11 bye at 6-4, having upset the Texans with Deshaun Watson's sixth game-winning drive of the season.

It will mark the Browns' first home game on "SNF" since 2019, while the Raiders had no primetime games on their schedule before the flex move.
