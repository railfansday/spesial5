Skippa on ‘WYFL,’ Viral Dancehall Hits & His Rise in Jamaican Music | Billboard Up & Coming

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

As Caribbean music charts its next era with a new class of stars and crossover hits, fast-rising dancehall artist Skippa is making his mark on the global stage. He joins Billboard Up & Coming host Kyle Denis to discuss his journey from St. Ann, Jamaica, his early musical influences, the story and woman behind his ‘4Granny’ project and the viral success of his track “WYFL.” Skippa also offers his perspective on the evolution of dancehall and the new generation of Jamaican artists, while discussing his approach to making music as his fanbase continues to grow.
