Kieran Culkin Says He’s Reuniting With ‘Succession’ Creator Jesse Armstrong on New Movie

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Kieran Culkin has let slip that he’s reuniting with Jesse Armstrong for the first time since “Succession” came to an end three years ago — but that it’s got nothing to do with Roman Roy.

“I’m about to work with Jesse on a movie — he wrote something,” he told the U.K.’s Heart Breakfast radio on Friday, without revealing any further details.

Armstrong doesn’t currently have any announced films in development. His last feature “Mountainhead” — which he wrote and was also his directorial debut — released on Apple, while A24’s “Jonty,” which he co-wrote with his “Peep Show” co-creator Sam Bain and stars Jesse Plemons and Cole Escola, is currently in post-production. Since losing out in the battle for Waystar at the end of “Succession” and saying goodbye to his Emmy-winning spell as the Roy family’s most foul-mouthed and emotionally chaotic heir, Culkin has become a major movie force. He won the best supporting Oscar for Jesse Eisenberg’s “A Real Pain” and is currently in cinemas as the younger Caesar Flickerman for franchise prequel “The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.” Next year, he’s due to appear in Warner Bros.’ untitled Nancy Meyers rom-com alongside Erin Doherty and Jude Law.

For all his move into starry, studio tentpoles, “Succession” fans will likely relish the chance to see Culkin back with the writer who gave him his most memorable role, a role he said he himself missed.

“It was a great show. I miss that whole thing,” he said. “It was a lot of fun because I decided early on, this is a guy who’s never had to suffer consequences in his life. He can just talk his way in and out of any situation, like how fun is that to play! Completely unhinged.”

Returning to what he told Variety in an interview in 2023, Culkin also noted that Armstrong had discussed another season of “Succession” with him.

“He pitched me three different of what season 5 could be, they all sounded good,” he said. “But it ended perfectly. The way it was supposed to.”
