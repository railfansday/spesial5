Guy Fieri Debuts Shocking Gray Hair Transformation

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Guy Fieri’s new, naturally gray hair stole the show on Thanksgiving, leaving many fans stunned after he debuted the look on Instagram.

Guy Fieri served up a major makeover alongside his turkey.

While the Food Network chef’s spiked, bleach blonde hair and goatee have long been his signature look, Fieri shocked fans with naturally gray hair in a Thanksgiving photo carousel.

In fact, while the 58-year-old pointed to his plate full of delicious looking food in the first slide, it was his hair that truly gobbled up fans’ attention.

“Your hair looks so good grey!!!! Dont [sic] go back to blonde,” one user wrote in the comments, while another added, “PLEASE TELL ME YOU'RE KEEPING THE GRAY HAIR!!!!”

But though Fieri’s bleached locks are now a natural shade, a second photo showing the Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives star helping himself to food revealed that his signature spikes are still firmly intact.

As for his Turkey Day celebrations, Fieri was joined by his and wife Lori Fieri’s sons Hunter Fieri and Ryder Fieri, as well as a whole other host of loved ones as they dined together in a covered pergola outside.

“Hope you all had an amazing Thanksgiving!!” Fieri captioned his Nov. 26 post, encouraging fans to “leave a comment sharing something you’re thankful for.”

Of course, those who have been staying tuned in to the chef’s journey through Flavortown over the years will know this isn’t the first time Fieri has stunned fans by cooking up a completely new look.

Back in January, Fieri shared a video to Instagram in which he’d lost both his facial hair and his blonde locks, instead appearing cleanshaven with brown hair upon his head, hair that was styled into a much more classic look than his usual.

“Hey there,” Fieri said in the Jan. 22 video clip as he stood before a lit birthday cake. “After so many years of celebrating my birthday as Guy, I thought this year I’d celebrate it as ‘just a guy.’ Happy birthday to me.”

But while the video sent shockwaves throughout the internet—with many users decrying the clip as AI—it was later revealed to be part of Fieri’s Super Bowl partnership with Bosch.

"My commitment to doing this was, 'Okay, we're playing big ball. And I'll play,’" he told Entertainment Weekly of the transformation. "What it turned out to be, what people are going to see, I'm just gonna turn my phone off for about three weeks after this, because people are gonna hammer me."

Meanwhile, his family wanted front row seats to the transformation.

“My wife said to me, 'I gotta see this,’” Fieri revealed. “‘I gotta be there for this.’”
