Lakers’ Official History Book Gets $9,600 Limited Edition With Magic Johnson Handprints, Staples Center Hardwood and Rare Artwork

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, Variety may receive an affiliate commission.

The Los Angeles Lakers’ $100 coffee-table book is getting a five-figure upgrade.

Luxury art-book publisher Taschen and the Lakers have unveiled two limited-edition versions of “Lakers: An Official History of the Purple & Gold,” featuring original handprints from Showtime-era legends, authenticated hardwood from the former Staples Center court and exclusive artwork signed by Magic Johnson and Kareem Abdul-Jabbar. The new releases, announced Thursday, are limited to 1,100 copies worldwide, with 1,000 Collector’s Editions priced at $2,400 each and just 100 Art Editions retailing for $9,600.

Both versions include the original 528-page book, housed in a custom leather clamshell box designed by Jeff Hamilton, the fashion designer known for his elaborate leather jackets commemorating the Lakers’ championship victories in 2000, 2001, 2002 and 2020.

Each box also contains an authenticated piece of hardwood from the original Staples Center floor, where the Lakers won five championships and Kobe Bryant played his final NBA game. Collectors will receive an original handprint from one of seven Showtime-era figures — Johnson, Abdul-Jabbar, James Worthy, Michael Cooper, Kurt Rambis, Byron Scott or Pat Riley — with the identity revealed upon delivery.

The $9,600 Art Edition takes things a step further, adding a large-format print of Ernie Barnes’ “Fastbreak,” originally commissioned by the Lakers in 1987. The 41-by-24.5-inch artwork depicts the team in the midst of its Showtime era and features seven signature plates hand-signed by the players and coach portrayed in the painting. Each print is numbered and authenticated by the Estate of Ernie Barnes.

The limited editions arrive nearly two months after Taschen released the standard version of the book in September. Abdul-Jabbar wrote the foreword, Lakers governor Jeanie Buss contributed the introduction and former coach Phil Jackson penned the afterword. Other contributors include Johnson, O’Neal, Pau Gasol, LeBron James, Luka Dončić, Jack Nicholson, Paula Abdul, Flea and Lou Adler.

“The Lakers have had an extraordinary impact on basketball and pop culture, and we wanted this book to capture the full scope of that influence, from the game itself to art, fashion, music, and beyond,” Tobak said in a statement. “The Limited Edition takes that vision a step further, bringing together rare memorabilia, artwork, and personal contributions from the people who helped shape the team’s history.”

Both limited editions are available now through Taschen’s website.
