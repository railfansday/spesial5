Here’s How to Watch ESPN Online for Free

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Catch the latest games and other live sporting events without having to drop hundreds of dollars on cable.

Live sports is bigger than ever with more and more fans eager to watch their favorite teams and players in action.

With various networks that broadcast live sports, ESPN is the most notable with a wide range and variety of leagues to watch, like NFL (for Monday Night Football), NCAA Football, NBA, NHL, WNBA and others, and 24/7 sports news, commentary, analysis and highlights available at a click of a button.

A majority of the games and events are broadcast on ESPN, ESPN2 and ESN on ABC, while others are spread across ESPNU, ESPNEWS, ESPN Deportes, ESPN8: The Ocho, SEC Network, ACC Network, ESPN+, SEC Network+ and ACC Network Extra. There are a variety of affordable streaming options that can give you access to watch ESPN online. For cord-cutters, DirecTV has a five-day free trial, while other streaming services, such as Hulu + Live TV and Fubo, also offer free trials.

A subscription to DirecTV gets you access to live TV, local and cable channels, including ESPN, ESPN2 and ESPN on ABC, at $49.99 per month for the first two month of service ($94.99 per month afterward). The service even offers a five-day free trial to watch for free, if you sign up now. It comes with access to ESPN Unlimited too for even more ESPN networks.

Sling Orange & Blue comes with 50+ broadcast and cable channels.

Sling TV offers the Orange & Blue plan, which comes with ESPN, ESPN2 and ESPN on ABC. With more than 50 channels, the streaming plan goes for $69.99 per month. All plans come with a free cloud DVR and an extra 600 or so free channels too. Please note: Pricing and channel availability vary from market-to-market.

Fubo is an affordable option offering a five-day free trial for new users who sign up.

Fubo starts at $63.99 for the first month ($88.99 per month afterward) with more than 230 channels, including local and cable, like ESPN, ESPN2, ESPN on ABC, ESPNU, ESPNEWS, ESPN Deportes, ESPN8: The Ocho, SEC Network and ACC Network that are streamable on smart TVs, smartphones, tablets and on web browsers. And with a five-day free trial, you can watch for free, if you act fast and sign up now.

Comes with Hulu, Disney+ and ESPN Unlimited.

Broadcast and cable networks ESPN, ESPN2, ESPN on ABC, ESPNU, ESPNEWS, SEC Network and ACC Network are available to watch with Hulu + Live TV. Prices for the cable alternative start at $99.99 per month for more than 100 live channels, while each plan comes with Hulu, Disney+ and ESPN Unlimited at no additional cost. Hulu + Live TV might be best for those who want all of these streaming services together in one package.

ESPN Unlimited is the official streaming platform for ESPN. A subscription includes instant access for $31.99 per month. However, you can save almost 17% off by purchasing an annual subscription for $319.99 per year. It features every ESPN network, including ESPN, ESPN2, ESPNU, ESPNEWS, ESPN Deportes, ESPN8: The Ocho, SEC Network, ACC Network, ESPN on ABC, ESPN+, SEC Network+ and ACC Network Extra — all in one streaming service. It even comes with CW Sports for more livestream games.

In addition to live sports, ESPN Unlimited has original programming to stream on demand. Although the service doesn’t offer a free trial, you can still watch ESPN Unlimited with a clever workaround to watch online for free. Learn more about how to get a free trial to access ESPN Unlimited here.

ESPN is also good to watch for surprise celebrity appearances. Every now and then, musicians have been know to drop in and give their thoughts and opinions on the latest going in the sports world. Famous recording artists, like Kendrick Lamar, Lizzo, J. Cole, John Legend, Imagine Dragons, Fall Out Boy, Drake and others.
