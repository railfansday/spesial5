‘Hunger Games’ Series Returns to No. 1 on Soundtracks Chart After More Than a Decade

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping — with music by Noah Kahan, Ella Langley & more — bows atop the Soundtracks chart.

The Hunger Games is back on top of Billboard’s Soundtracks chart, as the latest soundtrack from the long-running film series, The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, debuts at No. 1 on the list dated Nov. 28. The set includes tracks from Noah Kahan, Ella Langley, Kacey Musgraves and Stevie Nicks.

It’s the first time an album from the franchise has topped the list since the soundtrack from The Hunger Games: Catching Fire, spent one week at No. 1 on the ranking dated Dec. 7, 2013. That film was the second in the series, following The Hunger Games: Songs From District 12 and Beyond, which ruled for five weeks in 2012.

Sunrise On the Reaping is the second prequel of film series, with its events taking place 24 years before those in the first The Hunger Games film.
