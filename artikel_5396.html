uReport & KOIN 6 Launch $20K Portland DMA Student Reporting Grant

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The $20,000 grant fund supports 40 school awards in the Portland DMA as uReport continues to expand across the market

CLEVELAND, Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- DistrictWON's uReport and KOIN 6 announced a $20,000 student storytelling grant fund for Portland DMA schools.

The fund will provide 40 awards of $500 each to schools on behalf of students who contribute to school and community storytelling. The awards recognize work in student journalism, media, communications, broadcasting, photography, videography, digital content creation, and related fields.

Students use uReport to create and publish stories about athletics, academics, arts, student activities and community events. The grant fund will recognize schools and students contributing to those efforts.

"At KOIN 6, we believe strongly in the value of local storytelling by creating opportunities for the next generation of communicators," said Tom Keeler, vice president and general manager of KOIN 6. "This initiative allows Portland-area students to develop media skills while sharing the stories of their schools and communities."

KOIN 6 & uReport will have visibility into all student-produced content and will be sharing specific "how-to's" and other information about the grants within the platform once schools activate their accounts.  Over 100+ high schools have already enrolled to use the platform, which has been fully-funded by KOIN 6 for the entire 2026-27 year.  For schools not currently enrolled, but are located within the Portland DMA, there is plenty of time to get involved as winter season activities begin to ramp up.  School administrators can activate school accounts here: Activate KOIN 6 uReport Account

The ISTE Seal Provides Strength & Educator Comfort

uReport was recently awarded the prestigious ISTE Seal, a validation program recognizing high-quality educational tools.

"The ISTE Seal is the gold standard that very few companies have achieved. Portland area schools know that they are getting a world-class platform through the partnership with KOIN 6.  It's amazing to see what some of the schools are already doing." says Peter Fitzpatrick, CEO of uReport
