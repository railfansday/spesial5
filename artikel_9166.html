John Tree forms sculptural Hoop lamp from pine for Vaarni

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Designer John Tree has created the monolithic Hoop Table Lamp for Finnish brand Vaarni, combining an “almost brutalistic block of solid pine” with a hooped shade of thin pine veneer.

Vaarni, which is known for its pine furniture and accessories, used Finnish pine from a sustainably managed forest for the lamp.

Tree‘s design for the light, which was made locally in Jurva, Finland, combines two different pieces of pine to create a sculptural shape.

“One is this heavy, almost brutalistic block of solid pine that forms a stable structural element through which a single cut is made to support the second element – a hoop of thin veneer which forms a diffuser and allows light to come through in an almost magical way,” he told Dezeen.

The hoop has an integrated light source to create a diffused glow, with Tree basing the thickness of the pine used on its ability to let the light through.

The designer also let the materials lead the way when it came to the size of the shade.

“It was in some ways an exercise in reducing the two elements to their simplest geometric forms, yet governed very much by what material would allow,” he said.

“For example, we discovered that if the thin veneer hoop became any bigger, it started to distort under its own weight,” he continued.

“Thickening it was not an option, as this stopped light from going through so we naturally arrived at an optimum form defined by the material.”

While Tree has worked with pine before, he said he hasn’t previously had the opportunity to explore the material’s properties to the extent he did for this project.

“The expertise that Vaarnii have with this material meant that we could do some very precise and complex technical details,” Tree said.

“It was interesting to see how much a material can be elevated with care and precision in its manufacture,” he continued.

“Hoop is a little celebration of pine, a humble material that can do some amazing things.”

Vaarni has previously showcased “brutal and sophisticated” furniture at 3 Days of Design and designed a chair and footstool with Faye Toogood.
