Chelsea star Emmanuel Emegha ROBBED by phone snatcher in London after leaving Stamford Bridge following 4-1 hammering by Leeds

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Published: 18:28 EST, 22 November 2026 | Updated: 09:19 EST, 23 November 2026

Chelsea striker Emmanuel Emegha was robbed after leaving Stamford Bridge on Saturday evening, with the 23-year-old Dutchman having scored in his first start for the club.

Despite grabbing the game's first goal while filling in for the injured Joao Pedro, the Blues were beaten 4-1 by Leeds United and booed both at half-time and full-time.

Daily Mail Sport approached Chelsea and the Metropolitan Police on Sunday morning after hearing that the forward's mobile phone had been stolen after he left the stadium the night before.

Neither stood up the information, but Emegha has now confirmed there was an incident, writing in an Instagram post on Sunday night: 'Unfortunately after the game I was robbed. I am okay and thankfully no one was hurt. Thanks for the messages of support.'

Emegha signed for Chelsea from Strasbourg in the summer on a pre-agreement. The transfer fee between the sister sides has never been disclosed. He has won two caps for the Netherlands national team to date.

An interview with Emegha was included in Saturday's match-day programme in which he said: 'I'm a winner, I'm a fighter. I would do anything to win. If I need to block a ball with the front of my face, I will do it.

Emmanuel Emegha has revealed he was robbed on Saturday after Chelsea's 4-1 loss by Leeds

On Sunday, the Chelsea striker revealed the incident had happened to him on Instagram

He put the Blues ahead at Stamford Bridge on Saturday afternoon - his first goal for the club

'I'm quick, I know how to make runs in behind, and I know that I can be a nightmare for all the defenders. Nobody has seen me yet. When my time comes, I will be ready, and I know that the stadium will love my energy.'

It coincided with him being handed his first start for the Blues and scoring his first goal via a nice left-footed finish. However, Chelsea's 1-0 lead lasted only three minutes as Leeds capitalised on suspect defending to equalise, and then deservedly won.

Noah Okafor scored twice, while Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Brenden Aaronson got one goal each.
