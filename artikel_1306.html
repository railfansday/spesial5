Leoni finally returns to Liverpool training after 14-month injury nightmare

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Italian talent Giovanni Leoni has finally returned to training with the Liverpool first team after missing the last 14 months with a complicated knee injury that he picked up on his first appearance after joining from Parma.

Leoni moved to Liverpool from Parma aged 18 in the summer of 2025, joining the Premier League side at a cost of €35m plus add-ons.

He has only been able to make one appearance for Liverpool since then: The teenage centre-back was stretchered off 80 minutes into his first start for Liverpool in a 2-1 victory over Souuthampton in the EFL Cup third round last September.

He was originally expected to miss nine months of action following reports of a cruciate ligament (ACL) tear, but complications in his recovery have meant that Leoni has not appeared for Liverpool in 14 months.

Last month, Liverpool head coach Andoni Iraola admitted that there had been a set-back, but that Leoni was expected to return to first-team training during the November international break.

“It’s taking more time than we wanted,” Iraola admitted last month. “The planning was to train with the team in this past international break [but] probably it’s going to be in the next international break, so we’ve lost one month in the process.

“Giovanni’s injury, I wasn’t here but talking to the medical department, it was not just an ACL – it was a bad injury, a lot of things around the knee were involved apart from the ACL.

“He has to be very confident, the rehab process has to be very careful with him and we want to make sure once he comes to the team, he stays there in a good place.

“It’s true that we’ve had, not a setback, but a little bit of a delay, if you want.

“The plan now is he starts in the next break. It’s not that far but it’s a little bit later than we expected, yes.”

According to updates from the Liverpool Echo on Wednesday, Leoni has recently returned to training with the Liverpool first team.

Liverpool have confirmed the news with a few clips of the young Italian defender warming up and taking part in passing drills with his teammates.

Given the extent of Leoni’s injury and the length of time it has taken him to recover, he is not expected to be immediately available when Liverpool  face Manchester United in the Premier League this weekend.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
