To Fund Nusa: Arsenal Must Sell Zubimendi in January After England Disaster

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Arsenal are expected to make a move or two in the January transfer window, as they look to bolster Mikel Arteta's attacking options ahead of the second half of the season.

The Gunners have enjoyed an impressive start to the new campaign, winning nine of their first ten Premier League matches, whilst conceding just five goals in the process. However, Arteta's side have only scored 15 goals, and thus, there is a recognition from the Premier League champions' coaching staff and hierarchy that they need more offensive firepower to get them over the line later in the season.

Antonio Nusa has been heavily linked with a switch to North London, with Arsenal reportedly weighing up a potential move for the Norwegian. Nusa has scored six goals and provided eight assists in 15 appearances already this season for RB Leipzig, so the German club are unlikely to sanction his departure mid-way through the campaign for a bargain.

Arsenal will likely need to fork out a substantial sum to get their hands on the 21-year-old, and they may even need to sell a player or two to raise funds and create squad space for the player to arrive.

Several Gunners players could be considered for a sale, although there is one who is particularly vulnerable in Arteta's squad at the moment.

After a largely impressive debut season in English football faded out due to fatigue, Martin Zubimendi has been relegated to the periphery of Arteta's squad this term.

The Spaniard finds himself behind the likes of Declan Rice, Bruno Guimaraes, Myles Lewis-Skelly and Mikel Merino in the pecking order at the Emirates, sparking speculation that he could be on his way out in January.

This uncertainty surrounding his future appears to have affected the midfielder, whose form shows no signs of improving his standing in the Arsenal squad.

Starting in midfield for Spain against England on Sunday evening, Zubimendi had an opportunity to remind the world of the levels he can show. He failed to do so.

Hooked with 20 minutes left with an injury, Spanish media were not happy with the former Real Sociedad man's contributions.

Marca argued that Zubimendi was 'nowhere to be seen' on the night, while AS described the player's showing as 'disastrous'.

"His second consecutive appearance as Rodri's replacement was a disastrous night for him. He struggled to control the game against [Declan] Rice's aggressive marking, suffering several dangerous turnovers and rarely finding the Spanish second-to-last line of defense. To make matters worse, he had to leave the field injured in the 71st minute." - Marca on Zubimendi vs England

Given his paucity of minutes currently at Arsenal, a below-par display against England could be detrimental to Zubimendi's chances of rediscovering his important role in North London.

With Zubimendi looking somewhat redundant in Arsenal's squad, it makes sense for the Gunners to cash in on him in January whilst he still has an extensive list of suitors. Atletico Madrid, Real Madrid and Chelsea are all said to be interested in landing the 27-year-old.

While he might generate in excess of £50 million, which can go towards signing Nusa, his departure would also make sense regarding the Norway international's arrival, from a pure squad-building standpoint.

The Atletico Madrid star was desperate to leave in the summer, but was unable to secure a move.

Eberechi Eze has so far been used primarily as a left-winger this season by Arteta. If Zubimendi leaves, the Englishman will be moved centrally again, creating a spot in the squad for a new left-sided forward.

The jigsaw pieces fit, while the finances also make sense. Zubimendi is doing very little with his performances to quieten the noise around a potential exit, and the convenience around the potential deal makes it too difficult for Arsenal to ignore this winter.
