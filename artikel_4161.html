The problem with releasing your game at the same time as GTA 6 will be that whilst yes, they will be forever on the Store to be bought, there is never a better time to sell your game than within the first month. That is when there is the most buzz around your game. Occasionally, we see a game build momentum, but the norm is that occurs almost straight away.

The other issue is that whilst your game is always available to buy, it only really sells at full price upon release. A month or more after release day and people are looking for a bargain, meaning you will make less money on each sale.

So even though these other games will be available for years to come, the only time they are likely to make the most of their revenue is in that first month, and if you are releasing it at the same time as a behemoth like GTA 6 then you are likely to miss out on that first month bonus.
