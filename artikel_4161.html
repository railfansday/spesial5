Pokémon GO's Duolingo Crossover Gives Language Learners a Shot at Shiny Lucario

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Duolingo's green owl already has a reputation for making sure you don't forget your lessons. Now, it's offering Pokémon GO players a different incentive to open the app – a chance at Shiny Lucario.

Redeem a code for free Pikachu Crocs in Pokémon GO. Gengar and Jigglypuff avatar items are available with purchases of the corresponding Crocs.

Pokémon GO and Duolingo have teamed up for Duo's Pokémon Party, a limited-time event that rewards language practice with Pokémon-themed avatar outfits and special Timed Research. You won't get Lucario just for finishing a lesson, but completing lessons on seven different days will earn you a chance to encounter one.

The crossover began on November 17, according to the official Pokémon GO announcement, and you can take part regardless of which Duolingo course you're studying.

First, update Duolingo and join Duo's Pokémon Party in the app. Completing a lesson in any course earns your first reward, with more rewards available for studying on additional days. These include up to four Pokémon-themed suits for your Duolingo avatar. The suits are cosmetic rewards within Duolingo, not outfits for your Pokémon GO trainer. To get something in Pokémon GO itself, you'll need the research code.

The outfits include Poké Ball, Pokémon GO, Chatot, and Pikachu suits, so there's something to collect even if you aren't interested in the Timed Research. There's a catch with those rewards, though – they won't stay in your Duolingo wardrobe forever. Duolingo says the suits will expire on March 31, 2027, meaning you'll lose access to them even if you've already unlocked all four.

For the Pokémon GO reward, you'll need to complete at least one lesson on seven different days. They don't have to be consecutive, but seven lessons in one sitting won't count. Once you've met the requirement, you'll receive a code for the collaboration's Timed Research.

Redeem that code on the Pokémon GO Web Store's redemption page using the account linked to your game. The Timed Research will then become available in Pokémon GO.

You don't have to begin a new language course, either. If you've been working through one already, those lessons count toward the seven-day requirement. The important part is showing up on seven separate days.

The research carries the language-learning idea into Pokémon GO. You'll choose a language for the tasks, which will appear in that language rather than the one you normally use in-game. Professor Willow will offer hints if you get stuck.

Completing the tasks rewards you with XP, Stardust, and encounters with Pikachu, Meowth, Chatot, and Lucario. Each Pokémon comes with a special background and can appear in its Shiny form. A Shiny Lucario isn't guaranteed, of course, but it's a pretty tempting reward for keeping up with your lessons.

There's also a language tag for certain Pokémon caught through the research. Tapping it on their summary page shows their names in different languages. It's a small detail, but at least the partnership brings some language learning into Pokémon GO rather than stopping at a promotional code.

There are three deadlines to remember. You must join Duo's Pokémon Party by December 21, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. local time. After joining, you have until December 29 at 11:59 p.m. local time to complete lessons on seven different days and claim your Duolingo rewards.

You'll also need to finish the Pokémon GO Timed Research and claim its rewards by December 31, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. local time. Redeeming the code alone isn't enough, so don't leave the research until the last minute.

I haven't kept up with my Duolingo lessons lately, but this might finally get me to resume them. Apparently, the chance of a Shiny Lucario is more motivating than another reminder from Duo.

And even if the Shiny hunt doesn't work out, seven days of lessons isn't a huge commitment. Whether I'll manage to keep the habit going after the event is another matter.

It's quite refreshing to be able to make some actual progress in a mobile title without opening my wallet. Thanks, Pokemon GO.
