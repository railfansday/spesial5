Dunk City Dynasty introduces Tim Duncan to the line up in time for its holiday update

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

As we head into the winter, the holidays are upon us. That time of year when, depending on where you live in the world and where you're from, it's Guy Fawkes Night, Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's practically back-to-back. So, maybe it's time to unwind with Dunk City Dynasty as they add legendary player Tim Duncan to the roster?

Tim Duncan, a five-time NBA Champion and widely known as The Big Fundamental, spent the entirety of his nearly 20-year career with the San Antonio Spurs. But don't let that fool you, because on the court Duncan will challenge you to master the fundamentals of pro basketball with footwork, defence and bank shots galore.

Meanwhile, you can armour up with a brand-new outfit in the form of the Backdown Rhino outfit, alongside Demon Hunter Treasure Hunt and Mr Zombie. All of which let you bring some fresh and interesting looks to the court in this newest update.

I may not know much about NBA basketball (tautology, I know), but it's always interesting learning more from the Dunk City Dynasty about some of these players and their achievements. And with Tim Duncan, it presents an interesting new playstyle based less on flashy moves and more on grasping the core fundamentals of basketball that make him an exciting new addition to the roster.
