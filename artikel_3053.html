Data Center Buildout Could Help Energy Prices

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Data centers can be assets to energy resources and the power grid, says Arushi Sharma Frank, founder and CEO of Luminary Strategies. The former US energy market policy lead at Tesla discusses how the midterm results could impact the AI buildout, and why Elon Musk's plan to put data centers in space offers diversification for the chip sector. She joins Carol Massar on "Bloomberg Tech." (Source: Bloomberg)
