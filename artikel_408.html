AMC is giving moviegoers a Wrapped. Heres how to get yours.

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Year-end recaps have become an end of year ritual. Spotify Wrapped tallies listening minutes, Apple Music Replay ranks the songs people played most, Duolingo counts language lessons, and even Letterboxd gives movie fans a look back at the films they logged.

Now AMC is putting its own spin on the movie recap. On Wednesday, Nov. 25, the theater chain announced AMC Stubs "That's A Wrap," a year-end look back at members' trips to the movies.

In the comments on its announcement, AMC shared three steps:

Open the AMC Theatres app and go to Account Settings.

Check "Email me about promotions, offers, and more."

AMC Stubs includes both the free Insider tier and the paid A-List subscription.

AMC hasn't said when the email will land or which stats it'll include; but this isn't the first recap the movie theater chain has done. According to some Reddit threads, in 2024, some Stubs members reported getting a year-in-review email that counted their theater visits. Other members said they received a recap in December 2025.

In the Instagram comments, one moviegoer was already bracing for a "time spent in a theater" stat. Another said they didn't need a wrap, they'd been keeping a spreadsheet all year.

For now, anyone itching to see how many times they went back for the same movie can find their past tickets in their AMC Stubs account.
