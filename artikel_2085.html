The official Newcastle United Hall of Fame has now grown to 36 members.

The Newcastle United Foundation annual awards dinner on Tuesday (17 November 2026) unveiling the latest two new members.

Kieran Trippier and Paul Gascoigne the latest new boys to be awarded the honour.

Kevin Keegan was also inducted into the official Newcastle United Hall of Fame last night.

However, for King Kev this was for a second time, so he has been doubly honoured but isn’t a ‘new’ name.
