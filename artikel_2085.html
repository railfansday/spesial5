Southampton loanee has chance to kickstart his spell after Keven Schlotterbeck injury: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Southampton had a mixed summer transfer window but one major blow was the sale of Taylor Harwood-Bellis. The Saints brought in multiple defenders in a bid to replace him and one of which was Zach Abbott who joined on loan.

Abbott has a chance to change the course of his Southampton loan spell following Keven Schlotterbeck’s shoulder injury. The Nottingham Forest youngster was introduced during the 1-0 defeat at Preston North End and delivered a composed performance across 79 minutes at centre-back, earning a 6.8 FotMob rating.

It was only his second Championship appearance since arriving on loan in September, but the circumstances have created an opportunity he desperately needs. At 20, Abbott is still developing, and regular first-team football was the obvious attraction of his temporary move to St Mary’s.

Abbott has been honest about his frustration at the lack of minutes, admitting to BBC Radio Solent that he has been “itching for a lot more”. His response to limited opportunities has been to remain patient, work hard in training and take advice from his experienced teammates.

The numbers underline how little football Abbott has played. He has accumulated just 124 Championship minutes across two appearances, completing 74 of his 84 passes at an impressive 88.1% accuracy. He has also won two of his three aerial duels, with his success rate ranking in the 86th percentile among full-backs.

Although the sample size is small, his Preston performance offered encouragement that he can handle the demands of Championship football. His versatility is another advantage, having previously demonstrated an ability to operate at both centre-back and right-back.

Southampton are 7th after 15 games, with 24 points, and remain firmly in the promotion conversation. However, Schlotterbeck’s expected six-week absence leaves Tonda Eckert needing to find a reliable defensive replacement. Nathan Wood and Jack Stephens provide competition, but Abbott has already shown he can step in without looking out of place.

This is an important period for Abbott and both clubs. He joined Southampton to gain the senior experience that was difficult to secure at Nottingham Forest, but his lack of involvement has reportedly raised the possibility of a January recall.

A sustained run of appearances before the new year would change that picture considerably. It would provide Forest with the Championship experience they wanted him to gain and give Southampton another capable defensive option during a crucial period of their campaign.

The upcoming home fixture against Derby County after the international break could be particularly important. Abbott has already reached an FA Cup semi-final and featured in the Europa League, so his potential is clear.

Now he needs regular opportunities to demonstrate it at Championship level. Schlotterbeck’s injury is unfortunate, but it could be the opening Abbott has been waiting for. Southampton should give him the chance to make the most of it.
