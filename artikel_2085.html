Blackburn Rovers officially confirm how many away fans will head to Bolton Wanderers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Blackburn Rovers have officially confirmed that their side will be backed by a sell-out travelling contingent for the upcoming Lancashire derby against Bolton Wanderers at the Toughsheet Community Stadium.

It has been a campaign of mixed fortunes for Rovers, with the side who many tipped for relegation at the beginning of the campaign currently 12th.

A 1-0 victory against Watford at Vicarage Road on Wednesday night saw Blackburn reach 25 points after 17 games, just three points outside the play-offs.

Such feats come after the news that Tony Mowbray would be stepping back from his role due to health reasons, with Damien Johnson and Mike Dodds currently overseeing matters on the touchline.

And it's fair to say the interim duo will not be short of backing against Steven Schumacher's side on Saturday week.

As the crow flies, there are just 10 miles which separate Ewood Park and the Toughsheet Community Stadium, with this region of the country known for several historic rivalries.

Rovers fans, of course, will not quite have the same feelings of animosity towards the Whites as they displayed against Burnley on November 22nd, but it is one of three eye-catching fixtures from their perspective - the other being against Preston North End.

Bolton, of course, returned to the Championship for the first time in seven years in May, renewing this particular Lancashire rivalry in the process.

Yet, whilst attendances at Ewood, largely due to Blackburn's ownership situation, remain heavily criticised, not many will do the same after Rovers confirmed that over 5,000 supporters will make the short journey for next weekend's early kick-off.

It was confirmed on November 20th that away supporters wanting to purchase a ticket for Blackburn's clash with Bolton could do so on a general sale basis, after those with 4,500 or more points on the club's loyalty scheme were given a two-day headstart.

Even if the journey is straightforward, those in the away end still had to part with a sizeable sum of cash, particularly if they are an adult-paying supporter.

Indeed, tickets in such brackets cost £36, whilst an adult wanting to take one junior supporter with them to the match still had to part with a combined £45 for the two tickets.

However, the aforementioned bundle was only available in the upper tier of the Toughsheet Community Stadium's away allocation.

This may come as little surprise too, as Bolton rank sixth in the Championship when it comes to the average price of a ticket for visiting supporters.

Given their recent four-point haul, Rovers now sit level with five other Championship sides in this particular metric, having taken 10 points from a possible 27 on the road.

Bolton, meanwhile, have failed to make their home ground the fortress it needs to be in order to consolidate, winning just two of their first nine matches at the Toughsheet.

Blackburn will look to pile on the misery in that regard, especially with the recent form displayed by Andri Gudjohnsen, whose father, Eidur, enjoyed two spells with the Whites.
