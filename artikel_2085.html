'Fantastic Business' - Keith Wyness hails Aston Villa for landing extra '£58m'

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Aston Villa deserve huge praise for their moves to sell Morgan Rogers and buy Johan Manzambi during the summer transfer window.

That is according to former Villa chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that his old club found the perfect time to cash in on their star.

Rogers moved to join Chelsea in a £117million deal, while Manzambi came in as his replacement from Freiburg for £59m.

Wyness believes that the exchange is already proving to be "fantastic business" for the Villans, who have seen their season kick-started in recent weeks.

Manzambi has already chipped in with two goals and three assists from just six Premier League games, and is already becoming a favourite among the fans.

Perhaps most notably, he is also close to matching Rogers' performance in almost every key attacking stat, proving that he is already making up for the loss of the England international.

Villa's former chief Wyness - who served as CEO at Villa Park between 2016 and 2018 and now runs a consultancy business advising elite clubs - believes that Villa unlocked their summer spending with the deals for Rogers and Manzambi.

Unai Emery's side faced tight SCR constraints once again, but were able to "liberate" around £58m to reinvest into their squad.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained why Manzambi's arrival has proven to be a masterstroke.

"Full credit to Villa for Manzambi; they've uncovered a gem and not only that, as you say, they've liberated £58m. I mean, it's a fantastic piece of business," he said.

"Suddenly, Villa found value for Morgan as well as a replacement. It's great and hopefully he carries on, but it's still early days.

"He (Manzambi) certainly looks to be the real deal, and I think full marks to Villa for finding him and getting him in, and also to Unai Emery for giving him that safety net to go and perform."

Former Villa scout Mick Brown told Football Insider earlier this week that a new contract for Emery is "unlikely" to end the risk of him departing for another job.

The Spanish boss has been heavily linked with both Real Madrid and Barcelona of late, and continues to be tipped to potentially take the reins at Manchester United.

Brown believes that a new contract will protect Villa with a "huge compensation fee" for Emery, which they hope will ward off any interest.

However, his achievements have been so impressive, that the imminent contract extension is unlikely to quash the talk altogether.
