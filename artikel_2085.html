Valencia vs. Real Madrid: Head-to-head record and past meetings ahead of La Liga clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

By Matt Law | 05 Nov 2026 20:38, Last updated: 05 Nov 2026 20:38

Real Madrid will continue their 2026-27 La Liga campaign with a clash against Valencia at Mestalla on Saturday evening.

Jose Mourinho's side have been victorious in each of their last seven matches in all competitions during an excellent run of form, including a 3-2 success over AEK Athens in the Champions League last time out.

Los Blancos have won their last four league games, meanwhile, responding impressively to their defeat to Atletico Madrid before the September/October international break.

Real Madrid are currently third in the La Liga table, three points behind the leaders Barcelona, and they posted a 2-1 victory over Hansi Flick's side in the recent El Clasico.

Valencia, meanwhile, are 19th in the division, collecting nine points from 11 matches.

Los Che will enter this match off the back of a win, though, beating Elche on Sunday 1-0 to post just their second league win of the season.

Here, Sports Mole looks at the head-to-head record and past meetings between the two teams ahead of their contest at Mestalla.

These two teams have locked horns on 216 occasions throughout history, and it is Real Madrid that lead the head-to-head record, with Los Blancos winning 114 times to Valencia's 59, while there have also been 43 draws.

As expected, the vast majority of their contests have taken place in La Liga, with Real Madrid winning 96 of their 182 games in Spain's top flight, suffering 48 defeats and drawing the other 38.

The last meeting between the two sides at Mestalla took place in February 2026, with Real Madrid recording a 2-0 win.

However, Valencia have a recent victory over Real Madrid to their name, with Hugo Duro hitting a last-gasp winner, as Los Che ran out 2-1 winners in the Spanish capital in April 2025.

Valencia also beat Real Madrid 4-1 when the pair locked horns at Mestalla in November 2020, so they have enjoyed some impressive results against Los Blancos in recent years.

Karim Benzema and Raul are the joint-leading goalscorers in this fixture, having netted 16 times against Valencia during their time at Real Madrid, while Cristiano Ronaldo managed 15 for Los Blancos against Los Che.

Each of the last eight meetings between the two sides have come in La Liga, with Real Madrid posting five wins, suffering two defeats in the process.
