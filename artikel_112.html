EU sanctions Wildberries CEO Tatyana Kim, Russia's richest woman

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Wildberries, Russia's largest online retailer, has been the target of Ukrainian attacks for its role in supplying the Russian military. The EU has now frozen its owner's assets and banned her from travel to the bloc.
