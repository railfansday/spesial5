Electric minivans could be the ultimate in utility, with room for passengers, gear, and huge batteries. While VW’s ID Buzz only gave us 200 miles of real-world driving on a charge in 2023, China’s BYD has just announced a flagship purple peoplemover that can go an estimated 559 miles (900 km) on electricity.

It’s called the BYD Whale, and some back-of-the-napkin math suggests it lives up to the name. The van would be bigger than a Honda Odyssey, bigger than a Chrysler Pacifica, and bigger than a Toyota Sienna, at 17.3 feet (5,270mm) long, 6.5 feet (1,996mm) wide, 6 feet (1,815mm) tall, and with a 123-inch (3,130mm) wheelbase.

According to an auto-translated press release, the vehicle should have room for seven passengers and seven suitcases at a time, not that the company has shown off its interior yet. You can see a prominent sensor cluster on its forehead, though, and it’ll come equipped with LiDAR for advanced hands-free driving.

CarNewsChina points out it’s actually not the first with 900 km of CLTC battery range on board — Geely’s all electric Zeekr 009 minivan also claims to offer up to 900 km of range in one configuration. But BYD’s also claiming the can charge its battery from 10 percent to 97 percent in just nine minutes, and it get you from 10 percent to 70 percent in five minutes, according to Electrek.

The Whale will also come in a 248-mile (400 km) plug-in hybrid version as well, and the company’s planning to ship it in China in Q1 of 2027 for around 300,000 yuan. That’s roughly $45,000. As we’ve reported, Chinese cars are becoming quite competitive. Will they make it to the US?
