All endings in Queen’s Domain and how to unlock them

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Queen’s Domain is not an easy game by any means, and your decision-making will determine the ending you unlock. Yes, there are multiple ones (four, to be precise), and the ending depends on certain crucial junctures and the ensuing decision.

Let’s find out the requirements to unlock each of the four endings.

If you want to unlock all the endings, you should look for the turning points in the following sections. Create a backup so that you can reload and try out a different ending.

This is the least complicated ending to reach in the game. You have to complete the quests and take the ordinary weapon route to finish the game. The ending is actually boring, but it’s easy, and takes the least effort.

To reach this ending, wait until you complete Endu’s evolution by meeting the movement requirements. Then, you’ll need to get the lens/ideal setup. Once done, you’ll now have to take the Akunra route to finish the story.

To get this ending, you must pursue the Okbujo route once you complete its late-game quests. This is a relatively hard route, so you’ll need to put in some extra effort.

This is the hardest ending, and for a good reason. Once you finish Petrified Garden, you’ll need to first find 69 Silver Skulls. These skulls can be found in different locations, so it’s not an easy job. Once done, you’ll need to repeat all the steps that you do for the previous ending (Ending three).
