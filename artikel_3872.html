The Legend of Heroes: Trails beyond the Horizon sequel to launch by September 2027 in Japan

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Falcom will release a sequel to The Legend of Heroes: Trails beyond the Horizon, the latest entry in its long-running Trails series, by the end of September 2027, the company said in its financial report for the fiscal year ended September 2026.

The company anticipates a decline in both revenue and profit for the fiscal year ending September 2027, as it plans to release only one new title. Several ports are also planned.

The Legend of Heroes: Trails beyond the Horizon launched in September 2024. Since then, Falcom has released remakes Trails in the Sky 1st Chapter and Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter as its annual major releases. A remake of The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky the 3rd has yet to be announced, but if such a remake exists, it seemingly will not launch before September 2027.
