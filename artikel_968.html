Who could England play in Nations League quarter-finals?

England produced a statement win over world champions Spain in Madrid in their final Nations League group stage game to top group A3.

The Three Lions had already confirmed their place in the quarter-finals in March with victory over Croatia earlier in this window, but have given themselves the best chances of a favourable draw by beating Spain 3-2 on Sunday and proven they are one of the teams to beat.

Thomas Tuchel’s side will be glued to next Friday’s draw, with League A still to be completed across the international break.

But here’s everything you need to know about the next stages of the competition:

The draw will take place at 11am GMT on Friday 20 November.

England will feature in the last eight of the Nations League 2026/27, with a two-legged tie to decide who will play in the semi-finals and final, both to be played across a ‘finals’ week.

As well as the Nations League quarter-finals, the League A/B and League B/C promotion/relegation play-offs will be drawn, set to be held in March 2027. While the final tournament is scheduled to take place across 9–13 June 2027.

England's potential opponents in the last eight of the Uefa Nations League as things stand are Belgium, Greece or Denmark.

Beating Spain ensured the Three Lions will face a second-placed team in their quarter-final; Belgium have finished second in their group, while Greece could be pipped by Germany and Denmark could still be replaced by either Norway or Portugal.

England topping the group also means they will play the second leg of their quarter-final at home.
