Inside Prince Harry and Meghan Markle’s Real Estate Portfolio

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The couple recently returned to the UK. Here’s where they’re living now.

Since they married in May 2018, hardly a day has gone by when Meghan and Harry, the Duke and Duchess of Sussex, haven’t made news. Now that they’ve returned to the UK after six years in Meghan’s native California, that’s gone into overdrive. This week, they made their first formal public appearance together, with a day of events in Nottingham, a city about 100 miles from their new home in the Cotswolds, the idyllic, posh region in Oxfordshire. The rest of their family is living more royally, with King Charles, Queen Camilla, Prince William, and Princess Catherine in palaces and castles in London and Windsor.

But it wasn’t so long ago that they were living right beside them. After their wedding, the couple lived in a cottage at Kensington Palace, where Prince Harry had lived in his bachelor days (when Meghan was still splitting her time between Los Angeles and Toronto). Their worlds collided in 2016, thanks to a mutual friend and a now-legendary Instagram post (Dalmatian filter, if you must know). After a whirlwind of transatlantic dates, Botswana adventures, and a roast chicken proposal, soon the prince and the actress became the Duke and Duchess of Sussex, rewriting royal tradition as they went. After stepping back from official duties, launching new ventures, and growing their family, Harry and Meghan’s journey has been anything but ordinary.

But if you really want to understand the Sussexes, look at where they’ve lived. Each home—quirky, grand, or sun-soaked—marks a turning point in their story.

Before Montecito and million-dollar manses, Prince Harry and Meghan Markle’s first home together in 2017 was a far more modest affair: Nottingham Cottage, also affectionately known as “Nott Cott.” Situated within Kensington Palace’s grounds, this 17th-century red-brick charmer designed by Sir Christopher Wren included all the grandeur of a royal shoebox—1,300 square feet, two bedrooms, and ultra-low ceilings. Meghan herself described it as “so small,” and Harry said that the whole cottage was “on a slight lean” in their Netflix documentary, Harry & Meghan.

Yet, what Nott Cott lacked in size, it made up for in intimacy. The checkerboard-tiled kitchen, cozy living room, and petite dining area became the backdrop for the couple’s early romance and Meghan’s transition from Toronto to royal life. It was here that they celebrated their engagement, navigated the end of Meghan’s Suits era, and planned their next chapter. But as their family grew (and perhaps their need for headroom), the Sussexes bid farewell to their first shared sanctuary, relocating just before Archie’s arrival. After they left, Prince Harry’s cousin Princess Beatrice reportedly moved in with her husband, Edo Mapelli Mozzi.

Frogmore Cottage was a wedding gift from Queen Elizabeth II and a testament to the power of a good renovation budget. Situated on the grounds of Windsor Castle, the property was transformed from a five-unit dwelling into a five-bedroom single-family home at a reported cost of $3 million. The upgrades included a yoga studio for Meghan, a sleek kitchen, a guest wing, and a nursery in soothing neutrals for baby Archie. They weren’t always popular.

If Nott Cott was cozy and Frogmore was stately, Montecito is pure California fantasy, at least it seemed that way when the couple moved in 2020. When they first arrived in the U.S., their home was a loan from mogul and friend Tyler Perry, but in July 2020, Harry and Meghan snapped up a place of their own, a Mediterranean-style estate in Santa Barbara County for a reported $14.65 million. Sprawling over 7.4 acres, the mansion includes nine bedrooms, 16 bathrooms, a guesthouse, teahouse, tennis court, swimming pool, rose gardens, and even a chicken coop for those farm-to-table eggs, frequently present in Meghan’s Netflix series, With Love, Meghan (note that they actually filmed the show at the Montecito Orchard Private Estate a few miles away).

The rooms were characterized by wooden beams, sun-drenched rooms, and a palette equal parts Tuscan villa and Napa vineyard. There’s a library, shared office, sauna, gym, games room, arcade, wine cellar, and a five-car garage. The kitchen has undergone a major upgrade, though the couple have kept it mostly private, as Meghan prefers to film her lifestyle series in a lookalike rental to preserve their “safe haven.”

Though it once seemed to be their forever home, they recently left it behind for the UK. However, they still own the house, and Meghan and the children are reportedly returning to spend the holidays there.

Prince Harry’s family has a special connection to the CostaTerra Golf & Ocean Club in southwest Portugal, where it’s believed they own a beachy retreat. The club, which is in the seaside region of Melides, is also home to Harry’s cousin Princess Eugenie and her family. The princess’s husband, British executive Jack Brooksbank, is in marketing for the company that owns the development. The sprawling property, which is reportedly between 700 and 900 acres along the coast, features about 300 units. Harry and Meghan are believed to have bought theirs for £6.3m in 2023.
