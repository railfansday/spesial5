Ellipsis®: Strategy Lead (Remote, UK/EU -- £60-65k)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ellipsis is the market leader in AI search for B2B technology companies, on a mission to be the best in the world. We make B2B technology companies most-recommended by AI (ChatGPT and Google). Our 15-person remote team combines ambition with real results.

Ellipsis is fast becoming the market leader in "GEO" for B2B technology companies. This is a senior, hands-on role in a fast-moving and ambitious agency.

The move to AI search has put a lot of the SEO industry on the back foot. Ellipsis has always been consultative and strategic in our approach. We started building AI search products in 2021 on GPT-2, so this has been a time for us to thrive.

We think we understand AI search better than almost anyone. We have our own tracking product, ~25 million AI answers to analyze (with more tracked every week), and execution end-to-end: strategy, writing, in-house design, optimization, and performance reporting.

Marketing teams everywhere are working out what AI search means for them, and we're one of the few firms with credible answers backed by work we've already done. That's taking us beyond B2B tech scale-ups into conversations with global retailers, banks, and consumer brands, and into work well beyond search: positioning, websites, and how buyers choose between suppliers.

As Strategy Lead, you'll decide what work we do for every client, and why, and shape how our latest research on AI Search feeds into this decision. You'll run the Strategy team, set the strategy for our biggest accounts yourself, and build a strategy service that holds its quality as we take on much larger projects.

You'll own strategy across Ellipsis: what we recommend for each client, in what order, and why. You’ll ensure that all work we do for clients is adapted based on our constantly growing understanding of AI Search. You'll report directly to the MD/Founder and sit on the leadership team alongside the Accounts Lead, Head of Operations, and Chief of Staff.

Ellipsis is at the forefront of demand as SEO turns into GEO. We are growing very quickly as buyers are deciding now whom to trust with AI search, and some are bringing us in at board level as part of their AI strategy. The firms that earn those relationships over the next year will be hard to replace.

Our view is that AI makes yesterday's expertise cheap, so the job is to keep producing today's expertise and applying it well. Every client project should teach us more about what works, and that learning should make the next project faster and better. You’ll work closely with our Research and Development analyst to shape overall strategy, work with the team to ensure this is effectively used in practice, and ensure we have an effective feedback loop of findings <> implementation <> upskilling the team to ask better questions <> identifying gaps and new research.

Your job is to provide strategic judgement and ensure work gets done across the whole team. We have more data, tooling and ideas than any single client needs! And so your role will be identifying the leverage points across our customers; what can be done that makes performance better across the board? How can we deliver more of what our clients need? How can we build or improve automations that help us deliver more value? How do we apply findings, data, and research that actually lands?

You will own a couple of (high level or at risk – depending where you are most needed) clients and will need to be comfortable presenting, answering hard questions from clients, and building trust with our customers. You’ll need to be doing client work in order to understand how we do Strategy, what works, etc.; but client work is a max of 50% of the role.

You will work closely with other senior members of the team: our Accounts Lead owns client relationships, and you will need to collaborate closely; our Head of Operations owns capacity and process; our Founder&MD leads the business and is hands-on in the weeds with clients; and we’re currently hiring a Research and Development analyst to own the research (currently this is split across the team). You’ll also need to work closely with our Writing team and Content team to ensure questions are answered, strategy is understood, and everyone is doing the right work!

You will lead and line manage our Strategy team (currently 3); their personal development and progression is very important and you will have the chance to shape their up-skilling and growth! Search is changing quickly, and you’ll help our ambitious team progress rapidly.

We want to see progress in the key metrics we and our clients care about across the board – clients recommended, and picked, more often on the buying questions that matter to them, and that showing up in their pipeline. Alongside it, we want to see progress in the whole Strategy team's output: more clients and larger programmes carried by the same people, without quality slipping, and progress / progression for the team members too. Our understanding of AI Search continues to develop, and you’ll be instrumental in ensuring the work we do for clients adapts quickly to our latest research.

You'll see it working day to day! Every client has a plan they can explain in a sentence! Work lands when we said it would! Strategists spend most of their week doing strategy instead of preparing for meetings about it. And client calls never need the founder to step in to answer a question or rescue a number.

Decide the work for every client. You own the plan for each account: what we do month on month, what we don't, and why. We have far more data and tooling than any one client needs, so most of your value is in choosing / discerning the two or three things that will move the result and saying no to the rest. You’ll need to be confident in understanding industry shifts and our latest research, and feeding that into the work we do for clients.

Make strategy simple. Turn complicated analysis into a recommendation a client, a Content Manager or a writer can act on first time, without coming back to you. Lead with the answer, and caveat only when it changes the decision. A one-page plan the client agrees with beats a 40-slide deck they have to decode.

Front strategy on the biggest accounts. You set the strategy for a handful of our largest, most complex accounts yourself and present it on calls with the Accounts Lead. You know the numbers, you answer the hard questions in the room, and can confidently and clearly present to senior stakeholders.

You'll need to be effective on these calls from your first few weeks. As the team grows, you'll do less of this day-to-day, but you'll still step in confidently whenever a client needs you.

Build strategy that scales to enterprise. Our newer projects span several markets and, in some cases, several brands. You design strategy that holds up across all of them: one approach per portfolio, adapted locally, with quality checks that don't depend on one senior person reading everything.

Take strategy closer to the sale. Clients increasingly bring us into positioning, website decisions and commercial performance reviews. Point our strategy at the decisions that move pipeline, using what buyers ask AI and what clients' sales teams hear to shape what clients say about themselves.

Run the Strategy team. You lead and develop a small, growing team of strategists, and upskill them so they can handle accounts at your level. You review their plans before clients see them, coach their judgment, and protect their time. You make it easy for them to ask for help early, and you notice when they haven't. Their progression is extremely important.

Plan to capacity. Our tooling generates more good ideas than any team can deliver. You make sure the plan fits the hours, working with Operations so what we promise each client is what Content, Writing and Design can ship on time. When something has to slip, you decide what and say so early.

Own our playbook and how we measure it. AI search changes weekly. You decide which new findings change how we work, update the playbook, and get the team using it. You also work with our Account Lead to define success for clients -- which questions count, what counts as a recommendation, how we weight them -- so every number in a client report means something commercially.

Shape what our tooling and agents do. Ellipsis builds its own AI search technology, and more of our delivery is moving to agents. You decide which strategy work they take on, check their output is right, and feed that back to our tech team, who build it. They own the tooling; you shape what it does for strategy.

Back up sales. Give our sales team a sharp strategic view on prospects quickly: what they need and what we'd do first. A clear view early in the process is often what wins the work.

You've led strategy for a portfolio of client accounts and managed strategists doing the same. In UK agencies this is typically a Head of SEO, SEO Director or Strategy Director, with 6-10 years in organic search and at least two of those managing people. Experience with enterprise or multi-market clients is a strong advantage. We're open to other routes if you can show the judgment.

You'll need a strong grasp of organic search fundamentals and a working understanding of AI search. We'll teach you how we think about AI search; we can't teach judgment. That means making a clear call from imperfect data, defending it calmly, and changing it when the evidence says so.

This is very much a client facing role. You will be expected to interact with the clients in tandem with the Accounts Lead regularly in meetings. Having experience handling strategic conversations, managing high level discussions and presentations with clients, from junior to C-suite is essential to your success.

You simplify. You can explain a client's strategy in one sentence and defend it for ten minutes. Your decks got shorter as you got more senior.

Your priorities hold. You decide what matters this week, it stays decided, and when something will slip you say so before anyone has to ask.

You take feedback without ego. When you're wrong, you change course. At Ellipsis, continuous improvement matters more than being right! Especially as so much is changing now.

You use AI for speed, not slop or sprawl. You use Claude or ChatGPT daily, and you know the difference between work AI made faster and work AI just made bigger.

You want to drive things forward. You'd rather design a strategy service that works for 50 clients than be the hero on five.

You're a doer. You'd rather get the right work live for a client than spend a week building a tool to do it, and you're at your best in the room, with clients and with your team.

Shadow the Founder and the Accounts Lead on the main accounts, review strategy, data, etc and see how we work; and meet each strategist one-to-one. Get up to speed over the first month, and be effective on client calls for those accounts from early on.

By day 60, you're presenting strategy on those accounts, and every client plan is sized against the team's real capacity. By day 90, you own client strategy across the business, plans land on time, you've started building a way of working that lets the team carry more clients, strategists need you less on their accounts, and the founder joins strategy calls by exception only.

By six months, you're hitting your stride: adapting the work we do for clients as our understanding of AI Search changes, with less of your week spent on day-to-day client strategy.

In the follow-up interview we'll give you direct feedback on it, and how you respond is part of the assessment.
