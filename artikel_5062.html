Revolut Lists GoMining Token for UK and European Users

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Home » Exchanges » Revolut Lists GoMining Token for UK and European Users

The move brings the niche utility token to more than 15 million fintech app users under the EU’s MiCA regulatory framework.

Retail fintech giant Revolut has added GOMINING, the native utility token of the digital Bitcoin mining platform GoMining, to its in-app cryptocurrency trading service. The listing enables over 15 million Revolut crypto users across the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area (EEA) to buy, hold, and trade the token alongside everyday banking services.

Prior to this integration, GOMINING was primarily accessible through crypto-native exchanges such as Kraken, KuCoin, and Bitget.

The listing builds on an existing payment integration established in 2024 that allowed European users to fund GoMining accounts via Revolut Pay. It also adds GOMINING to Revolut’s cryptocurrency trading service.

Revolut reviews digital assets before listing them, assessing their risks, compliance requirements and potential financial crime exposure. It also monitors listed tokens on an ongoing basis as part of its procedures under the EU’s Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA).

“For years, there has been a clear line between crypto and traditional finance, and most people stayed on one side of it,” said Mark Zalan, CEO of GoMining, in a statement announcing the listing. “For GoMining, this listing means our token is no longer only for people who already live in the crypto world.”

GOMINING has several uses within the GoMining platform. Token holders can use it to pay digital miner maintenance fees, with discounts of up to 20%, buy digital miners on the platform’s marketplace and purchase boosts in Miner Wars, GoMining’s mining game.

The token also has a governance function. Users can lock their GOMINING tokens to receive veGOMINING, which gives them voting rights on the allocation of ecosystem rewards.

Revolut customers can now buy GOMINING directly through the app. To use the token on GoMining, they must first transfer it to an external GoMining account.

The Revolut listing gives GOMINING exposure to a wider retail audience through a mainstream financial app. It also provides another way for users to acquire the token without first opening an account on a crypto-native exchange.

However, buying GOMINING through Revolut does not provide direct access to GoMining’s platform features. Users must transfer their tokens to an external wallet before using them within the GoMining ecosystem.
