Bitcoin Price Trades Near $64,000 After Weekend Plunge to $62,000

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Bitcoin price traded near $64,000 on Monday morning, down about 2% from Sunday, according to Bitcoin Magazine Pro data. The world’s largest cryptocurrency is trying to hold its ground after a weekend plunge to $62,000, as tech stocks slid ahead of Nvidia’s earnings and traders braced for a Federal Reserve rate hike next month.

The selling that broke Friday’s low on Saturday morning did not stop there. Bitcoin slipped under $64,000 Saturday afternoon and dropped below $62,000 in the 8 p.m. ET hour on some exchanges, its lowest level since July 14. It bounced back above $64,800 within the hour and reached about $66,100 early Sunday before sellers returned. The bitcoin price has spent most of the past day between $64,000 and $65,000.

The slide has been punishing for leveraged traders. About $2 billion of crypto positions were liquidated over a 24-hour period during the weekend, the vast majority of them long bets. Bitcoin is now down 17% in November and has posted six straight weekly losses.

Stock futures offered little help on Monday. Contracts on the Nasdaq 100 dropped 1.5% and S&P 500 futures slipped 0.7%, while Nvidia fell 3% in premarket trading. Investors weighed a Financial Times report that former CEO Jensen Huang and his allies are discussing a campaign to reinstate him, a week after his resignation sent bitcoin below $72,000.

Nvidia reports third-quarter results on Tuesday, and options markets imply an earnings-day move of about 7% in either direction, Pepperstone’s Chris Weston told Reuters. Bitcoin has traded in step with chip stocks for much of the past week, so a weak report could add to the pressure.

Rates remain the other weight on the bitcoin price. Traders fully price a quarter-point hike at the Fed’s December meeting, with odds of a half-point move near 8%, CME FedWatch data show. The 10-year Treasury yield eased to about 5.12% on Monday after jumping 29 basis points last week, and higher yields have lifted the dollar to its strongest level since April 2025. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh speaks in San Francisco later Monday, and minutes from the last Fed meeting are due Wednesday.

Bitcoin-linked stocks fell with the coin. Strategy (MSTR) was down 8% in premarket trading and Strive (ASST) fell 9%. Strategy is also facing index-fund selling after MSCI removed it from its USA index, forcing index funds to sell by Nov. 30.

Spot bitcoin ETFs shed another $185 million on Friday, their seventh straight day of outflows, bringing losses since Nov. 5 to about $1.91 billion. That run follows BlackRock’s iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) bleeding $2 billion over nine straight sessions.

Analysts are now focused on the $60,000 level, which marked the lows of February and June. John Roque, technical strategist at 22V Research, said bitcoin is close to completing a head-and-shoulders top. “Bitcoin has not broken below its neckline at 60,000,” he wrote in a note Saturday, adding that such a breach “would then imply risk to $38,000.”

Matt Maley, chief market strategist at Miller Tabak + Co., wrote that bitcoin “NEEDS to hold the $60k level,” warning that “any material break below that level would be quite bearish.” A gauge of bitcoin’s 30-day implied volatility climbed to 49.75 over the weekend, its highest since June.

With Nvidia’s report, Fed speakers and the Fed minutes all due in the next three days, the bitcoin price faces a busy week. A move back above $66,000 would recover Sunday’s bounce high, while a break below the weekend low near $62,000 would put the $60,000 floor in play.

At the time of writing, the bitcoin price is near $64,100.
