Arsenal handed David Raya injury boost as Mikel Arteta reveals huge new update

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Mikel Arteta has confirmed that David Raya‘s shoulder injury is “not too serious”, but he will miss Arsenal’s Champions League clash with Borussia Dortmund.

The influential Gunners goalkeeper came off in the second half of Arsenal’s 2-2 draw with Newcastle in the Premier League on Saturday evening.

Raya’s late collision with Magpies striker William Osula did not initially appear to be a serious one but it was enough for the Spaniard to be rendered unable to continue and he looked in considerable pain as he walked off the pitch.

He was sent for scans on Sunday in order to learn the severity of the injury and Raya then took no part in Arsenal’s open training session on Monday morning.

Deputies Kepa Arrizabalaga and Illan Meslier were out on the pitch without their team-mate at London Colney and the pair now look set to battle it out to start against Dortmund on Tuesday night, with Kepa having replaced Raya for the closing stages at St James’ Park.

Speaking at his pre-match press conference on Monday afternoon, Arteta confirmed that it was indeed a shoulder injury for Raya, who was pictured wearing a sling on his left arm on social media on Sunday while out in London.

“He’s not fit for tomorrow unfortunately, but it doesn’t look too serious,” Arteta said. “We need some more tests tomorrow but it’s quite encouraging.

“With the pain that he was showing immediately after the action, we were quite worried but it seems to be better than what we expected.

“The doctor explained but it’s quite technical. When you look at the action, it doesn’t seem to be much. The extent of the pain that he had and how much he was struggling, it was quite worrying.”

He added: “We expected something much worse because he could almost not handle the amount of pain that he had at the moment. The first tests that we have done showed something that is much more encouraging.

“Tomorrow we need to reaffirm that to be able to give you some more specific information.

“There was no need for surgery, which was the main fear especially because that would have have ruled him out for months. Let’s see.”

With the last international break of 2026 having already taken place, club football will continue relentlessly for the next few months and Raya’s injury has come at an inopportune time for Arsenal.

Arteta’s side face big Champions League matches against Dortmund and Real Madrid in the next couple of weeks, as well as Premier League games against rivals Manchester City and Tottenham.
