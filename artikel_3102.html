Cellebrite DI Ltd. (CLBT -14.25%) reported third-quarter results characterized by the accelerating adoption of its AI-powered investigative tools and a strategic initiative to redomicile in the United States. Management stated that the move to establish a U.S. parent company is intended to eliminate sales friction associated with its foreign entity status and qualify the company for major U.S. stock indices. The company reported that its newest product, Genesis, reached a revenue milestone in its first full quarter, with adoption primarily driven by law enforcement agencies seeking to automate evidence analysis and reduce investigative backlogs. Management indicated that the business model is shifting toward more ratable revenue as cloud and AI solutions become a larger portion of the mix, leading to an updated commercial structure that will consolidate multiple customer contracts into a single platform agreement starting in 2027.
