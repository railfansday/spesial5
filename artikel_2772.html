Celtic Football Club announces exciting new strategic partnership with Pluto Data Analytics

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Celtic Football Club is delighted to announce a new strategic partnership with Pluto Data Analytics, a leading football analytics and predictive modelling company.

The partnership will initially run for three years, with scope for extension at the end of this period.

It will support the Club's continuing commitment to innovation and excellence in football operations by enhancing recruitment analysis, talent identification, squad planning and broader football decision-making processes.

The partnership forms part of Celtic's ongoing investment in modern football infrastructure and its commitment to ensuring that football decisions are informed by the best available intelligence and expertise.

By working with Pluto Data Analytics, Celtic will gain access to enhanced analytical tools and insights to support its existing recruitment and football operations functions.

The objective is clear: to improve decision-making, identify emerging opportunities and support the Club's long-term football strategy.

Celtic Chief Executive, Michael Nicholson, said: "We are always looking for ways to strengthen our football operation and ensure that we continue to evolve in an increasingly competitive environment. We have been reviewing the options in this area in order to select the optimum model for our Club.

"Our partnership with Pluto Data Analytics, an organisation with real quality and pedigree within this field, reflects our commitment to innovation and continuous improvement.

"Recruitment remains one of the most important areas of any football club. Our aim is to identify talent as effectively as possible, reduce uncertainty where we can and support our football staff with the best available information.

"We believe this partnership will strengthen our ability to support the Club's ambitions both domestically and in European competition."

Celtic manager, Martin O'Neill added: "I am very pleased that Celtic and Pluto are joining together to give the Club an extra level of insight and analysis which is becoming more prominent in football.

"While football is not an exact science, if the club can access and utilise new tools and new details, ultimately, we hope that such data will help us deliver the success we are all striving for.

"Our recruitment and wider football operations teams have already engaged with Pluto. Our plans in the short, medium and longer term are being developed and we are confident that our relationship will deliver real tangible benefit to the club over the next few years."

Jon Godfrey, CEO of Pluto Data Analytics, said: "Today is a big day for Pluto and we are incredibly excited to be working with Celtic, one of the biggest clubs in world football.

"We believe that predictive data modelling combined with the allure and resources of such a famous institution will allow us to achieve great things in this partnership.

"Celtic has the capacity to compete at the highest levels of European football and it is an honour and a fantastic challenge to work relentlessly with the club on achieving the exciting goals we have put in place."
