Is the NFC East really winnable by the Cowboys?

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Dallas Cowboys kept their hopes alive for the 2026 season this past Sunday. They managed to actually snatch a victory from the jaws of defeat instead of the other way around. The 27-23 victory over the Indianapolis Colts wasn't exactly high-quality football, but a win is a win when it comes to making the playoffs.

And that is what we are here to discuss – do you believe the Cowboys can still make the playoffs? Obviously, technically, they can. They are a long way from being mathematically eliminated.

But do you believe in them, or have you lost hope?

At 4-5, they trail the 5-2-1 New York Giants who are currently going through QB issues due to injury. The Giants keep winning by beating bad teams, something the Cowboys have not been as good at. The Cowboys wasted some of their soft schedule while the Giants mostly made good on theirs.

So do you believe the Cowboys can win the NFC East and make the playoffs? The second half of the season gets harder for the Cowboys, but the same can be said of the Giants.

What's your call BTB? Do you still believe?
