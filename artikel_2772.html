The Spurs had a nice night off for Victor Wembanyama and De'Aaron Fox on Monday, as Steph Castle and Julian Champagnie played well while Luke Kornet and the backups stepped up with excellent performances in a 123-107 win on the road over the Sacramento Kings. Tarris Reed has some run in the game, and Ja'Kobi Gillespie had a solid performance in his first extended minutes in the NBA. Sacramento had just come off a big win over the Celtics, but couldn't solve the Spurs, despite taking a 12 point lead early in the game. Dylan Harper was also out with a hip injury so it was a banner night for the Silver and Black despite not having three of their best players on the court. Damontas Sabonis achieved an unusual statistical feat by fouling one second into the game, eventually fouling out with three minutes left.

Fox and Wembanyama are listed as questionable tonight, but they should play with advancement in the NBA Cup tournament at stake. The Spurs are the only undefeated team in West Group C play, and a victory tonight would put them one win away from winning the group outright by defeating the Lakers on Friday night. They would still have a chance of advancing by winning one of the two games, but it would be a lot cooler if they removed all doubt by winning both games.

The Spurs defeated Portland 125-103 on November 7, and since then, Scoot Henderson has been replaced with former Spur Sidy Cissoko in the starting lineup. I kind of wish that the Spurs could have kept him around, but he was pretty raw in his first couple of seasons in the league. I'm happy with his success in Portland. Dame Lillard is always dangerous, and Deni Avdija is like a somewhat more annoying Luka, so this is a team that the Spurs cannot take lightly. The home crowd will by hyped for the NBA Cup, and it'll be a hostile atmosphere. It'll be a good opportunity for the Spurs to experience playoff intensity without all of the playoff pressure. Let's GO SPURS!! De'Aaron must dominate Dame.
