How Mount Gilead's late kick led to a third state championship run

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

OBETZ — It wasn't looking good for top-ranked Mount Gilead in the Division IV boys cross country race during the OHSAA state championships Nov. 7.

The team was fifth by more than 100 points behind the leaders just 450 meters into the race at Fortress Obetz. At the mile marker, the race pace settled, and MG found itself in second place, still trailing second-ranked Russia by a substantial margin. They continued to close the gap with a little over a mile to go but were still behind at the 3200-meter mark by 10 points.

"A lot of praying and a lot of hoping," MG coach Jake Hayes said.

Mount Gilead came from behind to earn its third team state championship in the last five years, scoring 84 points to beat Russia by 15 points with its 99 points. Botkins was third with 155 points, while Legacy Christian was fourth with 158 and Tinora fifth with 214. Northmor finished 22nd with 540 points in the 24-team race.

"All those guys knew before they hit the hill that we were down by 10 at the 2-mile," Hayes said. "They knew and they had a look in their eyes that I was hopeful for. I'm glad we were able to sneak it out."

Mount Gilead likely would have won it going away down the stretch, but top runner Henry Bartlett faltered, stumbled and went down roughly 50 meters from the finish. The junior managed to right himself and get across the finish line in 45th place, clocking a 16:43.4, which still made him an All-Ohio runner by cracking the top 48.

Picking up the slack for their fallen teammate was junior Brycen Gabriel who finished on the top 24 podium with a 15th in 16:16.6, sophomore Aiden McLain who ran 28th in 16:31.4, senior Ethan Turner who was 37th in 16:38 and junior Grayson Smith who was 39th in 16:40.

That's five Mount Gilead runners earning All-Ohio honors, showing how dominant they were at the finish. And right behind their top five were sophomore Andrew Gannon in 63rd in 16:59.9 and senior Liam Stalnaker in 81st in 17:09.4.

"I was trying to do what I could for my team," Turner said. "Even though Henry didn't have his best race, we were all able to be there and pick up for him. I'm really happy."

The program won state championships in 2022 and 2024, and it took home state runner-up trophies in 2019, 2021 and 2023. Mount Gilead was making its 18th trip to state and its 17th since 2003. It has made a dozen appearances since 2014.

"It feels absolutely amazing. I can't remember the last time I was this happy," Gabriel said.

Gabriel's 15th in 16:16.6 was the best result of anyone from the Marion area, but joining him on the podium was Ridgedale junior Elliot Weston who finished the D-IV boys race in 16:18 for 17th. He was Marion County's lone podium finisher, despite competing with a lingering hip injury.

"It was a good day, even if I didn't run my best. I ran better than I have been able to, so I'm thankful for that," Weston said.

Besides the five MG runners and Weston, Northmor sophomore Jack Dean was an All-Ohioan in the boys D-IV race, clocking a 16:42.1 for 42nd.

In girls Division III, Pleasant freshman Madisyn Ruhe was 27th in 18:18.6 for 27th, and Northmor freshman Vada Walters was 32nd in 19:28.

River Valley senior Kael Swartz was 32nd in 16:19.8 in boys Division III, while Pleasant senior Joey Dietsch took the final All-Ohio spot in the race with a 48th in 16:29.6.

Cardington-Lincoln sophomore Mickenzie White passed some runners late to end in 47th in 19:40.8 to get one of the last All-Ohio honors in girls Division IV.

Northmor's girls were 19th with 438 points in girls Division III. Behind Walters were Delaney Landin (130th, 20:44.7), Bella Smith (144th, 20:56.5), Macie Witherell (154th, 21:06.6), Janey Creswell (171st, 21:24.9) and Veronica Kanagy (198th, 22:44.1).

Also in D-III girls, North Union's Kailin McElroy was 81st in 20:02.6 and Highland's Veronica Ballard 89th in 20:14.9.

Mount Gilead's girls were 16th with 399 points in D-IV. Gwen Hershner led them with a 94th in 20:23.3 followed by Sophie Mosher (108th, 20:40.9), Hayden Combs (114th, 20:45.7), Leona Schwartz (115th, 20:45.9), Paisley Douglas (183rd, 22:20), Callie Ziemba (191st, 23:15.9) and Jazmyne Floyd (207th, 24:09.5).

Also running for the Northmor boys in D-IV were Carter Thomas (142nd, 17:49), Oliver Huddleston (164th, 18:04.6), Colton Randolph (197th, 19:04.4), Andreh Abdelmassih (207th, 20:00.9) and Colby Kent (208th, 20:01.2).

Cardington-Lincoln's Brayden Alwood was 68th in 17:01.2 in the D-IV boys race.
