Player Grades: Cavs at Bulls – Poor effort results in a rough loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Cleveland Cavaliers fell behind by 22 points in the first half of this game as they blew an easy one against the Chicago Bulls.

Grades are based on our usual expectations for each player.

28 points, 3 rebounds, 5 assists, 2 blocks, 1 steal

Mitchell was largely absent for most of this game. His impact wasn't felt until the third and fourth quarters, when he grew more aggressive in trying to will his team back into the game. His scoring was too little, too late, as the Cavs lost this game when Chicago outscored them 45-20 in the second quarter.

Still, he takes our player of the game for getting to the line 12 times and converting all but one of those free throw attempts.

21 points, 6 rebounds, 5 assists, 7 turnovers

Harden got the Cavs back into this game through his downhill drives in the third quarter. He was as important as anyone in turning the tide after that ugly second quarter.

With that said, Harden shares just as much blame for their collapse in the first half. Turnovers and missed shots led to easy transition buckets for Chicago. And his half-court defense wasn't making up for anything.

Cleveland could have done a better job of finding Allen. He shot 6-for-7 from the floor but wasn't fed the ball enough for my liking. We know that Allen can quickly build momentum when he's being featured in the offense, and the Cavs never really tapped into that tonight.

Still, Allen failed to seize the one aspect of this that is within his control — offensive rebounding. Allen could have had more touches tonight if he had cleaned the offensive glass. Instead, he finishes with only 4 rebounds (1 offensive).

8 points, 10 rebounds, 3 assists, 6 blocks

The Cavs have something real in Watson's ability to block shots as a weakside defender. He's been a genuinely impactful defender in these scenarios. But there are more holes in the ship than just that, and so far, Watson hasn't been enough to stop the sinking.

Cleveland will need to get better support at the point-of-attack and in transition before getting the most out of Watson's defense.

Proctor is readjusting to his role now that Harden and Mitchell have taken back full control of the offense. He hasn't been as prominently featured as a creator, and that's led to quieter performances as of late. He's done well in this smaller role, but the Cavs should look for more ways to enable Proctor as this feels like they are currently selling him short.

Thomas looked like a stud tonight. His one-legged jumper from the baseline was the highlight, but for me, his inside finishes have been just as impressive. I wasn't expecting Thomas to be such a dynamic player at the rim this early into his career — but he's shown a level of touch and creativity that has won me over.

His grade takes a slight bump for being backdoor cut on defense throughout the first half.

Hezonja struggled with his shot and wasn't a great fit in any lineup tonight. He was on the wrong end of multiple defensive breakdowns, and didn't have the mobility to play the run-and-gun style that this game required.

12 points, 3 rebounds, 2 assists, 2 steals

Sam shot the ball well (3-for-6 overall) and was able to turn the corner against cheating defenders to get clean looks at the rim. He wasn't a difference maker on defense, but I don't think he was the biggest culprit, either.

The Cavs are still waiting for Tyson to figure things out. He's been in and out of the lineup recently, so that doesn't help. But if we're being honest, Tyson hasn't looked comfortable offensively for more than a game at a time since last season's All-Star break.

There's a tangible bump in intensity every time Omier is on the floor. He plays each game more like a sumo wrestler than a basketball player. His willingness to bury his defender beneath the rim on every possession is a big reason why.

Every other defender is trying to play defense, while Omier's man is trying to fend off a grizzly bear.
