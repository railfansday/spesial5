David Pollack predicts winner, score of Georgia-Missouri game

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Former Georgia Bulldogs All-American defensive end David Pollack correctly predicted that Georgia would win on the road at the Ole Miss Rebels last week. Pollack predicts how the No. 1 Georgia versus No. 22 Missouri Tigers game will unfold on Saturday, Nov. 14 at 3:30 p.m. ET.

"I'm not going against Georgia," Pollack said on his podcast "See Ball Get Ball." He predicts that Georgia will win and cover in a 34-17 home victory for the Bulldogs, who lost their last home game.

"(Missouri quarterback) Austin Simmons, I will say this, like the rhythm passing game, getting the ball out, like he's really good at it," Pollack said. Simmons has 18 passing touchdowns and two interceptions this season, but only has 20 rushing yards, so he is not much of a threat on the ground.

Speaking of ground game, Georgia will look to dominate rushing the football again. "But Georgia just can they keep this going on the offensive line?" Pollack said. "Can they keep just pounding people? Like last week was a clinic."

Pollack notes that Simmons along with leading receiver Cayden Lee both are banged up after the Texas game. However, neither player appears on the SEC availability report.

"Missouri's got a good front," Pollack adds when discussing ways the Tigers can have a chance to beat Georgia.
