Wild blow first three-goal lead at home since 2019 in loss to Avalanche

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Minnesota Wild didn't have a great Thanksgiving week in St. Paul. A shutout loss to the Avalanche on Friday night marks their first losing streak at home all season. The way it happened makes it even more frustrating.

Minnesota remains without superstar defenseman Quinn Hughes, who is currently on IR with an upper-body injury. That didn't stop the Wild from taking a commanding 3-0 lead into the third period behind two goals from Joel Eriksson Ek and one from Blake Coleman.

Things fell apart for the Wild in the third period. Devon Toews was the first Avalanche player to find the back of the net with 6:23 to go. Artturi Lehkonen scored one minute later, and T.J. Hughes tied the game with less than one minute left to force overtime.

Neither team scored in overtime, which forced a shootout. Minnesota lost the shootout and moved to 0-7 in shootouts this season. The disappointing result was also the first time the Wild blew a three-goal lead at home since 2019.

Minnesota is now 18-4-6 on the season. The Wild are off Saturday night before hitting the road to face Chicago at 1 p.m. CT on Sunday.
