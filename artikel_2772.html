Christian McCaffrey, 49ers run past Cowboys 46-40

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

49ers fans took over AT&T Stadium on Sunday, out-drawing and out-yelling Cowboys fans. They booed the Cowboys running onto their own field, and they loudly celebrated a 46-40 San Francisco win.

In an entertaining game, the 49ers moved to 8-1, and the Cowboys fell to 4-6.

Christian McCaffrey had a career-high 161 receiving yards on four catches, two of them touchdowns, and ran for 62 yards and a touchdown on 13 carries. He is the first player in the Super Bowl era with at least 150 receiving yards, two receiving touchdowns, 50-plus rushing yards and at least one rushing touchdown.

The 49ers led 38-19 with 11:26 remaining in the fourth quarter. But George Pickens caught a 5-yard touchdown pass, which was followed by a missed 2-point conversion. Ryan Flournoy’s 11-yard score with 3:42 left cut the Cowboys’ deficit to 38-32. The 49ers burned only 2:26 on their possession in between, punting after four plays.

The 49ers showed the Cowboys who they are, though, when Brock Purdy hit McCaffrey across the middle of the field. Von Miller, who was covering McCaffrey, and his teammates had no chance of catching the running back, who went 66 yards to the end zone.

The Cowboys didn’t go away quietly, scoring once more on CeeDee Lamb’s 1-yard reception. Dak Prescott ran in the 2-point conversion.

Dallas tried an onside kick despite having three timeouts and did not convert.

Kaelon Black, who had 11 carries for 53 yards and a touchdown, fumbled on the second-down play. Quinnen Williams forced it, but Black was able to get back on the ball. The 49ers punted, and the Cowboys were left with only 9 seconds and in need of 91 yards and a miracle.

McCaffrey’s other touchdowns were a 74-yard reception and a 16-yard run.

The 49ers had 584 yards, the third-most the Cowboys have allowed in their history. It was not far from the 49ers’ team record of 621.

Purdy set a career-high with 389 passing yards and threw three touchdowns, completing 22 of 28 passes.

George Kittle had six catches for 74 yards, and new addition Keenan Allen five for 61. Deebo Samuel had three catches for 31 yards and a touchdown and three carries for 78 yards and a touchdown.

The Cowboys couldn’t keep up, even with 439 yards.

Prescott was 30-of-51 for 343 yards with five touchdowns and an interception. Pickens had 11 catches for 145 yards and three touchdowns, and Lamb seven for 72 yards and a touchdown. Flournoy added six catches for 71 yards and a touchdown.
