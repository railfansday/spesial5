Cam Skattebo misses practice due to a hamstring injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Giants running back Cam Skattebo said he felt something in his hamstring near the end of the team's Week 7 win over the Texans and it's still an issue coming out of the team's bye week.

Skattebo was not spotted taking part in the team's bonus practice earlier this week and he was officially listed as out of practice on Wednesday. Head coach John Harbaugh said "we'll see" when he was asked if Skattebo's availability for Sunday's game against the Eagles could be impacted by the injury.

Linebacker Abdul Carter (illness) also missed practice, but Harbaugh indicated there was no concern about his status for the game.

Right tackle Jermaine Eluemunor (calf), linebacker Malik Harrison (groin), cornerback Colton Hood (illness), wide receiver Charlie Jones (hand), and left tackle Andrew Thomas (groin) were limited participants. Tight end Theo Johnson (neck), offensive tackle Marcus Mbow (knee), and defensive lineman DJ Reader (knee) were all full participants for the Giants.
