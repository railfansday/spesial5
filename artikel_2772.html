Who plays on Thanksgiving 2026? NFL slate, channels, halftime shows

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Thanksgiving Day is almost here, which means it's time to give thanks, feast on turkey and watch some football with the family.

Traditionally, the Detroit Lions and Dallas Cowboys host games every Thanksgiving. For the 21st consecutive year, three NFL games will be played on the holiday.

The Detroit Lions and Chicago Bears kick off the festivities, followed by the Dallas Cowboys and Philadelphia Eagles. The Buffalo Bills and Kansas City Chiefs close out the Thanksgiving slate in prime time.

Here's what to know about the 2026 NFL Thanksgiving games, including kickoff times, TV channels and halftime performers.

Detroit Lions vs. Chicago Bears, 1 p.m. ET.

Dallas Cowboys vs. Philadelphia Eagles, 4:25 p.m. ET.

Buffalo Bills vs. Kansas City Chiefs, 8:20 p.m. ET.

The Lions will play their 86th Thanksgiving game in 2026 against the Bears. The Cowboys are set to host the Eagles in 2026 for their 59th game on Turkey Day.

To cap off the Thanksgiving slate, the Buffalo Bills will host the Kansas City Chiefs in primetime. It is the 12th time the Bills are playing on Thanksgiving and the 12th time for the Chiefs – and second year in a row.

The Detroit Lions vs. Chicago Bears Thanksgiving game will air on CBS.

The Dallas Cowboys vs. Philadelphia Eagles Thanksgiving game will air on Fox.

The Buffalo Bills vs. Kansas City Chiefs Thanksgiving game will air on NBC.

Legendary hip-hop group Wu-Tang will perform at the Lions' Thanksgiving game against the Chicago Bears, which is slated for Thursday, Nov. 26, at 1 p.m. ET. The game and halftime show will air on CBS.

Rapper and Detroit native Eminem and his manager, Paul Rosenberg, are the executive producers of the halftime show.

Country superstar Miranda Lambert is set to be the Cowboys' Thanksgiving halftime show performer as the team takes on the Philadelphia Eagles at 4:25 p.m. ET. The show will air on Fox.

The Goo Goo Dolls, an iconic rock band from Buffalo, New York, will perform the final halftime show for the NFL's Thanksgiving slate in 2026 – taking the stage during the Bills-Chiefs intermission. The Bills and Chiefs take the field at 8:20 p.m. ET. The halftime show will air on NBC.
