Jacoby Brissett: The last week has definitely been a crash course

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Jacoby Brissett has been a member of the Ravens for less than a week, but he’s set to start at quarterback in the team’s game against the Chargers on Monday night.

The Ravens lost Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley to injuries in Week 8 and got the wheels moving on their trade for Brissett ahead of their loss to the Jaguars last Thursday night, but the deal didn’t become official until Friday. That left Brissett with a short window to pick up the offense for a 4-5 team that’s hoping to stay afloat in the second half of the season.

“It’s definitely been a crash course. No question,” Brissett said.

Brissett said it has “been a long, but fast, week” and said he’s confident in the work he’s done with the coaching staff to digest the offense. That work will continue — Brissett joked that the press conference was interrupting his crash course — and it will likely keep going until close to kickoff on Monday night.
