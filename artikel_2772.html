Which teams are you rooting for during Rams’ bye week?

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The article below lists a few NFC teams to root for as the Los Angeles Rams sit this Sunday out for their bye week. Among those teams to root for include the New Orleans Saints, the Tampa Bay Buccaneers and the Minnesota Vikings. The Saints, Bucs and Vikings play the Chicago Bears, the Detroit Lions and the San Francisco 49ers respectively.

In theory, and in reality, as we enter Week 11 the Bears, Lions and 49ers are a threat to the Rams and their chances to make the playoffs. Of course, it's still early enough in that season that nothing is certain, yet.

If the Rams can gain some ground by others losing on their week off then that is great, but the Rams mostly need to worry about winning. The fan can scoreboard watch, while the Rams need to win, baby, win.

"The Los Angeles Rams won't be in action this week, but it won't be long before they take the field again on Thanksgiving Eve next Wednesday. During their brief bye, it'll be hard for them to gain much ground on a playoff berth. The Green Bay Packers and Seattle Seahawks, with whom the Rams are tied at 6-4, are both on their bye, as well.

The San Francisco 49ers are playing, but they're going up against the Minnesota Vikings – a team the Rams could be battling for a playoff spot come January.

Still, there are a couple of games that can help the Rams' playoff odds if they go in Los Angeles' favor. According to The Athletic, the Rams currently have a 47% chance to make the playoffs. Using The Athletic's playoff predictor, we can get a better idea of which games can help L.A. this weekend."

"The Rams are currently set to have $75.27 million in cap space during the 2027 offseason. That could be enough to extend Nacua and still fill out the roster. However, it could mean letting another talented player walk in free agency.

Graziano also cited Nacua's injury history as another reason for concern. Puka is on IR with a knee injury and has dealt with nagging injuries throughout his career. It is fair to question if longevity will be a long-term concern for the superstar receiver.

"He's 25 years old and a brilliant player, but his play style makes you wonder about his longevity," Graziano argued. "All of that is to say: I could at least imagine a scenario in which the Rams let him hit free agency, or franchise him and listen to offers."

"At the end of the day, 11-6 should be enough to get the Rams into the playoffs. If they beat the Packers out of the bye week, they'll hold the head-to-head tiebreaker over Green Bay and will have two opportunities to earn it against Seattle. The Rams may have the most difficult schedule in the NFL going forward, but at 6-4 and on a four-game win streak, they are in a good spot to make a playoff push in December."

"Let's break it all down to show why fans may be correct in anticipating some healthy players returning to the fray:

Center Dylan McMahon to IR – Frees up a roster spot

Wide receiver Brennan Presley waived – Frees up a roster spot

Nose tackle Jalen Hunt contract terminated – Frees up a practice squad roster spot

Signed wide receiver Brennan Presley – Takes up a practice squad roster spot

By the numbers, LA's active roster has freed up two spots. And the team is confident enough at receiver and defensive tackle to shrink its options going forward. With an extended period until LA resumes competition, the time to activate players from injured reserve is now."

"Among running backs who recorded at least 100 carries and averaged eight attempts per game in 2025, Corum ranked fifth in yards per carry and second in rushing success rate, trailing only Williams.

Corum also is top 10 in missed tackles, gains of 15 yards or more, and is one of the NFL's most efficient running backs. Oh, and he's only fumbled twice in two years.

Think about that. Two running backs sharing the same backfield finished first and second in a category measuring how consistently they keep an offense moving down the field.

And somehow, they're still being overlooked."

"The Rams-Packers game isn't just the first game ever on Thanksgiving Eve. It'll be the first time in NFL history that teams will play on a Wednesday after a bye. In fact, no team has even played a Thursday game after a bye since the 2001 season, according to Stathead. From 1990 to 2001, there were 28 games played on a Thursday after a bye. But since 2002, there hasn't been a single one.

Since 2002, the quickest turnaround was when the Colts played the Vikings on a Saturday in Week 15 of the 2022 season, and when Indianapolis faced the Patriots on Saturday in 2021. Other than that, every team has played a Sunday or Monday game following a bye since 2002."

If anyone is capable of adjusting and following a new game plan, it's Stafford; just don't expect another MVP season from him.
