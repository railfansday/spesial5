What the Lions have said about Jack Campbell's injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

After Lions linebacker Jack Campbell was taken off the field on a backboard early in the fourth quarter against the Vikings and transported to a local hospital on Sunday, Nov. 1, the team said he was being evaluated for a neck injury. They have provided few specific medical updates since, citing respect for Campbell's family. After being off Tuesday, Lions players return to practice Wednesday, Nov. 4. Head coach Dan Campbell is scheduled to have a press conference at 1 p.m.

Here is what the Lions and Dan Campbell have said about Jack Campbell's injury:

➤ In a postgame radio interview with Lions Radio Network's Brad Galli, Dan Campbell said Jack Campbell was alert and breathing on his way off the field.

➤ In his postgame press conference, Campbell said: "Thoughts and prayers are with Jack and his family. He's being taken care of. We don't have any new information. We'll keep you updated as this goes, but we're thinking about him."

➤ At 7:08 p.m. on Sunday night, the team released this statement: "Jack Campbell is resting and will remain in a local hospital tonight, for continued evaluation and monitoring. The team will provide further updates as we have them."

➤ At 2:31 p.m. on Monday, the Lions announced: "Jack Campbell continues to rest and recover at a local hospital. Preliminary indications are positive and out of respect for Jack and his family we will not share further information at this time."

➤ At his press conference about half an hour later, Dan Campbell referred to the team's statement and said, "It's positive. So your prayers are working. Please continue to do that and we'll leave it at that. So, just continue to pray for him, but it is positive news on Jack."

➤ Asked if he could say any more on Jack Campbell's injury, the coach said, "I can't, so I'm not going to." He added, "The team knows what's going on. I've given it to the captains."

➤ At his Monday press conference, Dan Campbell also said he visited Jack Campbell and his family at the hospital on Sunday night: "It was good. It was positive. Got to see his parents, talk to his wife. And everybody's in good spirits. And it's about the recovery and it's a small setback, that's the family. They're very positive."

➤ On "Costa & Jansen with Heather" radio show on Tuesday, Dan Campbell said of Jack Campbell, "The good news is he is improving." Dan Campbell added that he's kept the team captains updated on the linebacker's status and that the idea that he is "doing better" is "good news" for the players who are concerned about his health.
