Blake Lively Gets Ready for the Holidays in Archival Midnight-Blue Chanel Sequins

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Blake Lively is making a grand return to the New York City scene. After nearly two years privately navigating her high-profile legal battle against It Ends With Us costar Justin Baldoni, the actor stepped out in a shimmering midnight-blue minidress that seemed to signal the start of a sparkling new chapter.

Last night, the Gossip Girl star, whose appearances this year have primarily been limited to major cultural events—namely May’s Met Gala and the summer’s World Cup games—attended NYC-based jewelry designer Brent Neale’s holiday capsule launch party. Full of smiles, she was photographed wearing an archival look from Chanel’s Karl Lagerfeld days: a strapless sequin number with a scalloped lace neckline and black satin bows. More specifically, the piece was designed for the Autumn/Winter 1994 collection, and walked the runway on Christy Turlington with a floral brooch pinned at the neckline.

Putting her own stamp on the vintage mini, Lively updated the ’90s look with a black bucket bag from a more recent Chanel collection. The accessory, introduced for the Fall/Winter 2026 season, was crafted from black leather, with a rounded shape and top closure. Gold hardware detailing and a matching chain added a little pop of shine, and for some contrast, Lively selected a pair of silver Brent Neale bow earrings and mixed-metal rings.

The actor added a pair of red-bottomed PVC Christian Louboutin heels with a black cap-toe accent, ankle strap, and ultra-high stiletto. And of course, she finished things off with her signature bouncy blonde blowout, parted at the side. ’Tis the holiday season!
