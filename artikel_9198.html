Top features of the M2 Color Craft include the dual cameras and ACS (Auto-Creation System), which simplify the creative process, while its enclosed Class 1 design makes it suitable and safe to use for home studios.

Released 27 May 2026, the xTool M2 earned pretty uniformed positive reviews as a desktop laser and printer for crafters and small businesses. And I can see why – it's got dual cameras, air assist, and 600 mm/s speed (pretty fast) across a 426x320mm work area (fairly small size) with 10W or 20W diode lasers (a strong beam). You've got the option of CMYK printing and 3W infrared engraving to cut, mark, and colour wood, acrylic, leather, metal, and stickers.

What is clear from reading a few of the online reviews is that users really liked the M2's accurate camera alignment, true black ink, and simple setup. They didn't particularly like the lack of a blade cutter, a smaller print area and expensive non-refillable cartridges.
