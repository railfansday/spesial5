Digital Extremes says Warframe's imminent Tau update is practically big enough to be a sequel as it reveals new details, including dog walking

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Warframe's latest devstream has just gone live, revealing more about the new Tau solar system players will be able to explore as part of December's "massive" new update, which arrives on 9th December. Additions include a two-hour long quest, new explorable areas, and even chill nighttime dog walking.

In a press pre-briefing ahead of the Devstream, Warframe creative director Rebecca Ford and community director Megan Everett discussed the scale of the Tau update, revealing just how much of an ambitious undertaking it's been for the team. "This is a huge update, it is massive," Ford said. "It is a second solar system ... Alone, by now, this would have been Warframe two. We're just going for it: we're building a new solar system brick-by-brick."

That begins with a "two-hours-ish" story quest, according to Ford, but there's plenty more besides. Tau brings a new navigation system, for instance, and will feature two playable regions at launch. The World-Seeder Oasis is one: full of environmental hazards, thanks to damaging terraforming efforts, and various missions to complete. Here, players can go bug-collecting for an NPC called Catulast. Doing so increases your standing with him, and provides new rewards.

Then there's the ringed city of Fornax. Unlike previous regions in Warframe, this features three hubs: the Lastbloom Lounge, Manifold 90-BF, and the Elder Rustgarden. All have their own playable missions, some brand-new and others returning from the original star system. This includes Spy, a returning mode that has players breaking into terminals while trying to remain undetected.

Spy hasn't been shown a lot of love for a while, and it seems like this new iteration will bring new custom puzzles for players to take on. Ford notes it'll include codes to memorise, passwords to figure out, plus gameplay and an aesthetic that alights with the new Fornax setting.

Steel Path - the optional, more difficult variant of regular missions - also returns in Tau, although the Accolite mini-bosses who would hunt you down in previous Steel Path missions will not be present. Instead, the Hokra brothers will take that role: giant sentient casino goons who look quite daunting.

We were also shown more of the Last Bloom Lounge. Here you'll be given bounties that'll always bounce you between the three Fornax hubs. So, if you take one bounty in the Lounge, you'll complete it while out on a mission, and end up in either Manifold or the Rustgarden (which is still being kept secret for now). That way you'll be naturally circling between the three locations.

However, rather than there being three separate syndicate reputations to grind out, one single syndicate connects them all: the Fornax Informer, a newspaper run by kids obsessed with breaking stories across the ringed city. Print lives! By progressing through the ranks with this syndicate, you'll unlock clues for detective work that reveal a special cinematic within the Lounge, though this too is being kept secret for now.

By completing bounties, you'll also gain access to Fornax's own progression system which provides still-mysterious buffs and other beneficial effects within Tau. There's also a card game in the Lounge, which you can either play against the house or with other players.

As for navigating between the hubs, each one has its own garage. The Tau update comes with a driving minigame: an on-rails opportunity to gather resources and fend off other drivers along the way. In the demo shown, we're brought from the Lounge to the Manifold, dodging barriers and blowing up other sentient vehicles in the process.
