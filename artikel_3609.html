Cassie Randolph Is Pregnant With 1st Baby With Husband Brighton Reinhardt

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Bachelor alum, 31, and her husband, Brighton Reinhardt, shared the news via an Instagram Reel posted on Thursday, November 26.

“It is Thanksgiving 2026 and we have had the hardest time keeping this secret,” Randolph said as she showed off her baby bump.

As music played, Reinhardt, 29, said, “Growing very fast every week, every day.”

The couple could barely contain their happiness about starting a family. Randolph added, “We’re so excited!”

The Reel also contained a montage of video clips of the pregnancy journey, including the pair’s excitement over a pregnancy test returning a positive result as well as footage of Randolph getting pregnancy scans and Reinhardt being a supportive partner as he gears up to be a dad.

In the post’s caption, the couple shared that their baby is due in May 2027.

Earlier this year, Randolph hinted that she was ready to begin to start trying for a family in the near future.

“Deciding whether to sip my way through the holidays or get pregnant,” she wrote in a September 29 TikTok video, which she captioned, “Decisions decisions.”

In the clip, Randolph took a sip out of a glass as she filmed herself and lifted her hands to create an invisible baby bump. After pretending to cradle a baby, Randolph’s eyes got wide as she leaned into the camera and took another sip.

In the comments section, Reinhardt quipped, “Both options scare me.”

Randolph and Reinhardt tied the knot in Costa Rica in June 2025, less than a year after getting engaged in November 2024 at Crystal Cove Cottages in Newport Beach, California.

Randolph told People at the time of their engagement that she had no hesitations when it came to accepting.

“When Brighton dropped to a knee and asked ‘Will you marry me?,’ as he opened the box, my reaction was immediately, ‘Oh, my gosh, yes!’” the reality star told the magazine.

One year after tying the knot, Randolph took to social media to look back at their special relationship step.

“6.3.2025 🤍,” Randolph wrote via Instagram in June. “One year ago today, we had a sweet, intimate ceremony in Laguna Beach with our grandparents who weren’t able to make it to our Costa Rica wedding (and parents too!). It was such a special day that we’ll cherish forever.”

She continued, “Our wedding bands have this date engraved inside them, my bouquet was made by my mom and grandma and wrapped with a sleeve from my grandma’s wedding dress, and Brighton’s grandpa married us. The day was sunny and beautiful, ending with a little rain shower that felt like good luck. Forever one of the most special days of our lives. Happy ‘official’ one year baby. 🤍.”

Randolph and Reinhardt began dating in 2020 after several years of friendship. One year prior, Randolph appeared as a contestant on Colton Underwood’s season of The Bachelor. The pair ended the show together, without an engagement, before calling it quits in May 2020.
