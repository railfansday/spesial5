Over 2 million Xbox players in the UK and US reportedly used the 'New Zealand trick' to play GTA 6 early - that's more than a third of the country's population

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

We already knew there'd be a frantic clamour for GTA 6 this week, and we also knew plenty of players would be eyeing up Xbox's 'New Zealand trick' to get in on the action early. But it's now being reported that a whopping 2m UK and US players took advantage of the trick - which, for context, equals more than a third of New Zealand's actual population.

Here's a quick bit of background: Rockstar's regional release schedule meant GTA 6 launched at midnight on 19th November everywhere, local time. As such, New Zealand was the first country in the world to see the hugely anticipated game go live, thanks to timezones. And because Xbox players can make purchases on any regional store easily, simply by changing their console's country, understandably impatient sorts opted to become temporary virtual New Zealand residents in their droves (on PlayStation, the process requires a regional account and payment in local currency so is more finicky).

In fact, according to data analytics firm Ampere Analysis, the early demand was such that 1.5m Xbox users in the US and 600,000 UK Xbox players briefly went Kiwi for the occasion of GTA 6's launch. Other countries highlighted by Ampere include Canada, where 200,000 Xbox players took the New Zealand plunge, Brazil (200,000 players), and Mexico (100,000 players).

By contrast, just 100,000 PlayStation players in the US opted to dive in early, reflecting, as Ampere notes, "the ease of being able to switch country in the account settings on Xbox consoles." And here's all that info in graph form:

Amusingly, New Zealand's tourist board tipped a wink to the sudden influx of virtual 'visitors', telling them "We'd love to have you". It also cheekily offered a video of cheerful tourists admiring the country's resplendent natural beauty "Life," read the accompanying caption, "because we're getting GTA 6 first."

Some hours later and the rest of the world can now finally, officially join in the GTA 6 fun, and it's a game worth playing. Eurogamer's Chris Tapsell called it an open world of "awe, minute detail and impossible scale" in his five star GTA 6 review.
