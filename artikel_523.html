Sharks CryoGlow Plus face mask gets first-ever discount for Sephora Savings Event

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

SAVE UP TO 20% WITH CODE ALLONSALE: The Shark CryoGlow Plus is on sale for $341.10 (normally $379) for Beauty Insider members during Sephora's Savings Event — its first discount ever. Members with VIB and Rouge status save up to $37.90 extra.

The Shark CryoGlow LED face mask is one of Mashable's Gifts of the Year — and the upgraded version is now on sale for the first time ever.

Through Nov. 9, Sephora shoppers can save up to 20% on the Shark CryoGlow Plus using the coupon code ALLONSALE. This deal comes as part of Sephora's fall Savings Event for members of its Beauty Insiders rewards program, which is free to join.

Standard Beauty Insider members will get 10% off the $379 CryoGlow Plus, which brings it down to $341.10. Those who have unlocked VIB status (by spending at least $350 at Sephora in the past year) save 15% and pay $322.15.

Beauty Insider Rouge members who have spent at least $1,000 at Sephora this year earn the most savings. For them, the CryoGlow Plus is only $303.20 — a 20% discount.

Unlike other red light masks we've tested, Shark CryoGlow can emit red, near-infrared, and blue light to tackle different skin concerns, from wrinkles to acne. It also features de-puffing cooling eye pads, which feel "amazing on groggy eyes in the morning or post-shower on a summer night," according to our reviewer.

The "Plus" version of the CryoGlow, which launched this summer, has more LED bulbs and a longer battery life than the original model.
