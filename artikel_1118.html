Liverpool vs Manchester United - Premier League: Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of the Red Devils for the very first time as the rivals go head-to-head at Anfield

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

By HARRY BAMFORTH, LIVE SPORTS COVERAGE REPORTER

Follow Daily Mail Sport's live coverage for the latest updates from Anfield as Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of Manchester United for the first time at rivals Liverpool in the Premier League.

Consistency is something Liverpool need to find, and soon.

While the Reds have been much improved under Andoni Iraola this season, they have struggled to put any sort of run together. It's almost like two steps forward, one step back.

Their recent results in all competitions pretty much show that: a real mixed bag.

Why Liverpool have a point to prove in Man United showdown

LEWIS STEELE: Since thrashing United 7-0 in 2023, Liverpool have beaten their friends in Manchester just once in seven meetings, including two draws and a defeat at Anfield.

Andoni Iraola, Liverpool boss, speaking ahead of the game:

On the topic of Anfield, we have to talk about Liverpool's form there.

The Reds used to be almost unbeatable under Jurgen Klopp at home; he only lost six games in the Premier League at Anfield when there were fans in the stadium. He was in charge for almost a decade...

Don't forget Arne Slot, too. While last year Anfield was the opposite of intimidating, during the Dutchman's title-winning season his side only lost once in the league here, to Nottingham Forest.

But this season? Well, Liverpool have really struggled.

While Andoni Iraola's side have been almost perfect away from home, winning four of their five league games on the road and drawing the other, at Anfield they have only picked up five points.

Their only home win this season was an impressive one against Brighton, but when the big boys have come to play, Arsenal and Manchester City, they have crumbled, failing to score a goal in either match while conceding four across both.

In fact, they have only netted four goals in total at Anfield in the league this campaign.

It's simply a trend that can't continue if Liverpool are going to challenge for the big trophies this season. They need to make this place a fortress again, starting today.

Just look at Anfield right now, so peaceful.

In just three hours, this place will be pitch black and the noise will be so loud you won't even be able to hear yourself think.

But for now it's tranquil and empty, and there is just something so beautiful about that.

Alan Shearer, former Newcastle striker, speaking to Betfair:

The Premier League table is not good reading for Manchester United.

To be 10 games into a campaign and sitting 19th in the league is inconceivable for a club of United's size. It's simply embarrassing.

By winning at Anfield today, they can climb up to 16th and relieve some of the pressure on them.

But that's easier said than done, they know that.

This afternoon's clash with Liverpool is the first of a crucial five games for Mauricio Pochettino and Manchester United.

They simply must start picking up points to climb out of the relegation zone. Yes... they are in the bottom three.

Tantrums, tears and a new dawn... story of Poch's whirlwind two weeks

When Mauricio Pochettino boarded a flight from London to Atlanta a little over two weeks ago, there was little indication that by the time he landed back in the UK it would be as the head coach of United.

Over the international break, Manchester United rid of their head coach, Michael Carrick, after what has been a, quite frankly, abysmal set of results, and drafted in a man they have been eyeing up for almost a decade.

That's right, that man is Mauricio Pochettino. It feels like United have been chasing Poch since his days at Tottenham, but the timing never seemed right to bring him in.

Well, now it is. They splashed the cash to pry him away the US men's national team, making the Argentine their 13th manager in as many years since Sir Alex Ferguson retired from duty. Quite remarkable.

Pochettino couldn't have asked for a much harder start: Liverpool at Anfield. But in a way, the pressure is off. He's only had half a week to work with his players and this almost feels like a free hit.

But this will be the only free hit. Regardless of what happens today, he simply needs to pick up results, and quick. Otherwise, United may, quite possibly, find themselves in a battle against the drop.

We had an absolutely stacked Saturday of action, which isn't very common these days, so our Sunday fixture list looks rather thin.
