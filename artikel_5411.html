BTC Price vs. PCE Shock: 21Shares Warns of $53,250

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

BTC price faces another potential sell-off as it awaits today’s inflation report after weeks of heavy selling. Core PCE is expected to rise 0.3% month over month, while 21Shares puts Bitcoin’s realized price at $53,250. A hotter reading could bring that level closer than we want.

The Binance hack initially sent Bitcoin lower, but the price recovered most of its losses by November 4. The sell-off picked up again afterward, driven by ETF withdrawals, shrinking stablecoin balances, and traders being forced out of leveraged positions.

Core PCE is forecast to rise 0.3% month over month, with annual inflation expected at 3.2%. The figure matters as markets reassess the outlook for interest rates and how much room the Federal Reserve has to ease policy.

Bitcoin has already suffered a sharp reversal this month. The latest drop followed a brief Nvidia-tied recovery that brought BTC back above $60,000, but momentum quickly faded. A hotter inflation reading could put that recovery further out of reach, as Bitcoin is currently struggling to attract sustained buying.

A softer report could help prices recover, but markets are looking for more than a single green candle after several failed attempts to reverse the decline.

Bitcoin Hyper is down to its final day before launch, and the presale price is still low at just $0.0136. That’s the price buyers can get today, with tomorrow’s launch bringing the presale to a close and exchange trading expected to follow.

More than $33.2 million has already gone into the project. That’s a lot of money for a presale, especially with the deadline now just hours away. Buyers also have 35% APY staking rewards on offer, adding another reason some investors aren’t waiting around for the token to hit exchanges.

The timing is what makes this interesting. Tomorrow, the presale price disappears. Anyone planning to buy after launch will have to enter through the market, where the price could move either way. If demand carries over from the presale, waiting could cost buyers a cheaper entry.

$0.0136 is the presale price today, not tomorrow. With $33.2 million raised and launch just around the corner, anyone still considering HYPER is running out of time.

21Shares puts Bitcoin’s realized price at $53,250, based on Glassnode data from November 22. The figure tracks the average value of Bitcoin based on the price at which coins last moved on-chain.

Bitcoin falling toward that level would put more holders underwater. The firm also outlined a deeper scenario near $43,800, a potential 18% drop below realized price. It considers that outcome less likely, given the institutional presence and spot Bitcoin ETFs that were missing during earlier bear markets.

The report also points to fading demand and forced liquidations as major contributors to BTC price decline. The Binance hack may have triggered the initial selloff, but 21Shares argues that the subsequent weakness was driven by deteriorating market demand.

Bitcoin now needs to reclaim $60,000 and hold it. Otherwise, $53,250 could become the next level as the market searches for a floor.
