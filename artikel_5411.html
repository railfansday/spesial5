By offering, the platforms segment is expected to hold the largest share of the global AIoT Market during the forecast period.

In the AIoT Market, platforms help develop and deploy AI solutions, enhancing IoT systems' performance and functionality. These platforms serve several roles and are divided into various subcategories. IoT device management platforms manage the entire usage lifecycle, from installation and operations to maintenance and upgrades of IoT devices. This process aims to ensure devices are used effectively and safely for the period they are intended to serve. IoT application enablement platforms collect the requirements needed to develop and monetize AIoT applications. This enables the integration of various devices and data, making it easier for application developers to create and manage applications that handle data generated by IoT devices. IoT connectivity management platforms and services are designed to manage data flow within the network and between IoT devices and the cloud. An IoT cloud is a service that provides a flexible architecture for storing, managing, and analyzing large quantities of IoT data to support faster, better business decisions. IoT advanced analytics use big data analytics and AI techniques to scrutinize performance data from IoT devices and provide actionable information to improve operations.

By deployment type, the edge-based AIoT segment is expected to witness the highest growth rate during the forecast period.

Edge-based AIoT implementations exploit the advantage of processing data closer to IoT devices, thereby reducing dependence on high bandwidth and the latency experienced during data analysis. AIoT systems with edge characteristics are organized into three major layers: the collection terminal, connectivity, and edge layer, each performing its designated functions. Hardware components in the collection terminal layer include sensors, vehicles, embedded systems, tags, and active mobile components wired to gateways through existing electricity lines. In this case, the connectivity layer also includes field gateways that connect to the collection terminal layer through these power transmission lines. Finally, the edge layer includes data warehouses, data processing resources, and insight generators.
