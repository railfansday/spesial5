George & Armstrong Start In England Under-21s Friendly

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Harrison Armstrong and Tyrique George both played an hour of England Under-21s’ 1-0 defeat to Portugal at Ashton Gate Stadium.

The Young Lions went behind to an Afonso Moreira goal in the 28th minute and were unable to find a way past keeper João Carvalho from there.

This was Armstrong’s first start for the Under-21s after making his debut as a substitute against Ireland in the previous international window, while George was making his 10th appearance at this level.

Lee Carsley’s side had already secured qualification for next summer’s UEFA U21 Euro finals in October, and are using these friendlies in preparation for the tournament in Albania and Serbia.

Armstrong and George return to action in Bristol on Monday 16 November, taking on France (7.30pm kick-off).
