Data Centers, AI, & the Growing Demand for Energy: Impacts, Risks, & Solutions

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Learn how the rapid growth of data centers is affecting electricity bills, public health, communities, and the environment and what can be done about it.

Data centers, the physical infrastructure powering the rapid growth of artificial intelligence and our increasingly digital lives, are expanding at an unprecedented pace and scale around the world. This growth is creating enormous new demand for electricity and other resources, with consequences for communities and the environment. In the United States, where data center development is surging, unchecked growth could raise electricity bills for families and small businesses, strain power grids and water supplies, increase pollution, and slow progress toward a clean energy future.

Communities should not be left to bear the costs of this expansion without a meaningful voice in the decisions that affect them. People deserve transparency and a real say in whether and how data centers are built. The wealthy technology companies driving this growth should pay for the infrastructure their facilities require rather than shifting those costs onto the public, bring new clean energy to meet new electricity demand, and meet strong protections for public health and the environment.
