Fresh Oldham Athletic development sparks fierce backlash online

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Oldham Athletic have had their appeals against Kai Payne and Kane Drummond's red cards in Saturday's defeat to Crawley Town rejected, which has not gone down well with the Latics fanbase.

Despite being tipped as one of the potential promotion contenders, it has been a poor start to the season for Oldham in League Two, and it was another frustrating afternoon for Micky Mellon's men against Crawley on Saturday.

Oldham had won just one of their previous nine league games prior to Saturday's meeting with bottom side Crawley, and their dismal form continued as they slumped to a 1-0 defeat to the Red Devils at Boundary Park, but the scoreline alone does not tell the full story of the dramatic encounter.

The Latics were reduced to 10 men in the 14th minute when Payne was sent off for a challenge on Danilo Orsi, and their afternoon went from bad to worse when Hammond saw red for a high boot on Kai Jennings just 16 minutes later.

Oldham battled valiantly despite their numerical disadvantage, but Crawley took the lead in the 52nd minute when Orsi bundled the ball home from close range after Charlie Barker to hit the crossbar, and they held on for just their second victory of the season.

The Latics currently sit 21st in the League Two table, and they are only outside the relegation zone on goal difference, but they have a chance to pull clear of danger when they face fellow strugglers Northampton Town at Sixfields on Tuesday night.

With many feeling that Payne and Drummond's red cards were harsh, Oldham submitted appeals against the decisions in an attempt to get their suspensions overturned, but it was confirmed on Tuesday that both had been rejected.

That means that Payne and Drummond will be unavailable for the game at Northampton on Tuesday night, as well as Sunday's FA Cup tie against League One side Sheffield Wednesday at Boundary Park and next Saturday's trip to Shrewsbury Town.

After returning for a third loan spell with the Latics this summer, Payne has scored one goal in eight appearances so far this season, while Drummond has netted two goals and provided one assist in 16 appearances.

However, Mellon will now be without the pair for three of the next four games, although they will be able to feature in next Tuesday's EFL Trophy clash at Barnsley as suspensions from the league do not carry over to that competition.

Oldham are not the only club to have failed in an appeal against a double red card in recent weeks, with National League side Carlisle United also having their appeals against Miguel Azeez and Harvey Bunker's dismissals in their 3-0 win over Harrogate Town last month turned down.

With plenty of Oldham supporters still feeling angry about the refereeing decisions in Saturday's defeat to Crawley, it is fair to say that the news that the appeals against Payne and Drummond's red cards had been rejected was not well-received, with many taking to social media to vent their fury in response to the club's announcement on X.

The incidents continued Oldham's long-standing frustration with referee David Rock, who sent off two Latics players last season, Ryan Woods and Mike Fondop, with the latter's red card later being rescinded after a successful appeal.
