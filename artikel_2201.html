Liverpool ‘showing concrete interest’ in Bundesliga wonderkid Jurgen Klopp likes

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Liverpool are reportedly showing ‘concrete interest’ in highly-rated Gladbach talent Wael Mohya.

The Bundesliga teenager is high up on the list at German giants Bayern Munich, with the Bavarians expected to face competition from Bayer Leverkusen for his signature.

The attacking midfielder has registered four goal contributions in 11 games (across all competitions) this season.

Christian Falk told CF Bayern Insider: “However, Bayern Munich definitely have him on the list. And we should note that both Liverpool and Brighton in the Premier League are showing concrete interest in the 17-year-old attacking talent.

“This is why Gladbach would be well-advised to try and offer him a new contract; that way, they can have some influence over how long the player stays at the club, or, at the very least, ensure that they get a sizeable sum from a permanent transfer.

“I heard that there has already been contact with Mohya’s agent, Thies Bliemeister, and they talked about a move to Leverkusen. The next step is to talk to the player, his family and then the club. He doesn’t have a long-term contract at Gladbach, so there is a chance to make this deal happen.”

With a contract currently set to expire in the summer of 2028, Mohya represents a genuine market opportunity for clubs looking to invest in a high-potential option.

On that note, Brighton makes a great deal of sense as the next stop in the 17-year-old’s career (if not his final destination), similarly to Leverkusen in the German top-flight.

Liverpool, it has to be said, would benefit greatly from a player with the Gladbach star’s profile, given they lack a viable backup option to Florian Wirtz in the ten position. Not to mention the fact that the footballer would provide further reassurance on the flanks.

Falk went on to add: “The 17-year-old was invited to the national team by Klopp during the international break. He wasn’t officially part of the squad; it was more just to give the player a taste of what international football looks like. He’s not totally clear yet about where he wants to commit, but Germany is putting up a strong fight to ensure that he pledges his international future to Die Nationalelf.”

The big issue facing Mohya in the coming weeks and months, of course, is whether a move to the likes of FC Bayern and Liverpool are actually in his best interest at this stage in his career.

Bayern Munich have Ismael Saibari and Jamal Musiala behind Harry Kane, albeit there are question marks over the latter’s long-term future in Munich in light of ongoing concerns over his health.

The Reds, of course, have Wirtz, in whom they invested a British transfer record fee (the total package being worth £116.5m). He’s played a significant percentage of the total minutes on offer in 2026/27 under new boss Andoni Iraola, and at 23 years of age, he’s hardly at any risk of sinking past his peak in the near future.

We’ve no doubt Liverpool would welcome having a rotational option of Mohya’s quality, whom they could develop on their own time, and they can supply evidence of how well they’re managing Rio Ngumoha’s development despite the presence of more senior figures in Bradley Barcola and Cody Gakpo.

But if Wael Mohya’s looking for an absolute guarantee of regular minutes, you could forgive him for either staying put in Germany or joining the talent factory line in Brighton.
