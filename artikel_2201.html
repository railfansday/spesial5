Five-goal West Ham prospect set to complete National League loan move

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

West Ham poached Daniel Cummings from Scottish giants Celtic in the summer of 2025. He had enjoyed a fine youth career with the Bhoys, and after 11 goals in 37 games for WHUFC’s U21s, the Scot is set for a first senior loan.

Writing on X, Pete O’Rourke states that Cummings is set to join Aldershot Town in the National League. The expectation will be that the 20-year-old’s deal runs until the January transfer window, when his situation will be reassessed at the London Stadium.

Cummings’ sole first-team experience to date came back at Celtic. He is yet to make a senior matchday squad with the Hammers, so this serves as a much-needed chance for the striker to make an impact at a higher level.

As highlighted before, Cummings was a star in his youth at Celtic. He scored 39 times in 62 games for their B-team and was prolific in the UEFA Youth League with six goals in 12 outings.

Doubts over his future ultimately limited him to one senior appearance and brought around his West Ham move. There was hope that he could progress through the setup in East London, but a lack of first-team opportunities have seen his trajectory slow somewhat.

This loan to Aldershot Town marks a first chance for Cummings to go out and play senior football on a regular basis. He will be determined to carry his form from youth level into the first-team game, having managed five goals in 15 outings so far this season.

In fact, one of those goals came in a National League Cup win over Aldershot. Perhaps it was that performance that caught their eye, and prompted them to give Cummings the chance to prove his abilities in senior football.

West Ham’s drop to the Championship has brought some increased exposure for young players. The likes of Airidas Golambeckis, Ezra Mayers and Lewis Orford have all found opportunities in the first-team.

However, for prospects like Cummings who are aged 20 and still on the fringes, loans offer the best opportunity to stake a claim for a place in the senior setup. If he can emulate the form he found in his early Celtic days, he could be one to keep an eye on.

A strong stint with Aldershot Town could earn Cummings a chance higher up the pyramid over the second half of the season, so it will be intriguing to see just how he fares.
