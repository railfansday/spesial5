Nottingham Forest plot move for 'colossal' Birmingham City ace

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Birmingham City's ascent to the Championship summit has led to a fresh round of transfer speculation involving German centre-back, Phil Neumann.

According to Sky Deutschland, Nottingham Forest and Fiorentina are casting eyes over the centre-back's strong performances at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park.

Blues currently top the table on goal difference, ahead of Middlesbrough and Stoke City with 29 points from their first 15 matches, conceding just 14 goals - the Championship's strongest defensive record.

Neumann, unsurprisingly, has been a key aspect of such an improvement in this part of the West Midlands, having endured an indifferent first season in English football after arriving at Birmingham last summer following a pre-contract agreement during his final months with Hannover 96.

Transfer speculation involving the 29-year-old isn't exactly new at this stage, as the 6'3" figure was the subject of reported interest in the previous January window and then again in the summer.

However, it would be an untimely blow for Chris Davies if Neumann was to depart B9 in the coming months as the ambitious club stand a chance of realising their promotion dream.

Neumann has already surpassed the 50-appearance mark for Birmingham, with the imperious defender being an ever-present alongside Christoph Klarer in the early weeks of the season.

Having scored three times in all competitions last season, his brace against Wrexham on August 28th sent Davies' side on their way to the first of eight victories so far, and he has continued to impress defensively.

Registering 181 defensive contributions thus far, as well as a duel success rate of 60.9%, such performance levels have attracted the attention of Forest, who have conceded 13 goals in their first 10 league games under Oliver Glasner and sit 16th in the Premier League.

Having been linked with Sassuolo in January, the defender has also been monitored by Fiorentina, who are currently 15th in Serie A after 11 games, despite conceding 25 goals in the process.

It has also been claimed that a deal could be struck as early as January for the player, who has never featured at the top level in any of Europe's major footballing nations.

Neumann was recently described as "colossal" by former EFL striker, Sam Parkin, and Birmingham fans will be hoping the defender stays true to his previous words during the summer transfer window.

Indeed, the former German youth international was linked with Hull City and Borussia Mönchengladbach, although a move to either club failed to materialise.

Neumann also distanced himself from such transfer calls, saying: "It’s not on me, my agent is getting these calls but I don’t care.

"My focus is on Birmingham and to do good in the season," he added.

Given his importance to this promotion-chasing Blues side, many supporters have voiced their immediate thoughts on the speculation surrounding Neumann.

With figures of between €5m and €10m mooted, one wrote: "Firstly, he won’t leave this winter, we’re in a promotion push. Secondly you’ve massively undervalued him. Minimum £18 million."

Another Blues fan added: €5m euros. Might send him out on loan for a couple of games for that."

"One of the best defenders in the league, he shouldn't go for anything less than 15m and hopefully if we're up for promotion he'd stay till the end of the season," was another viewpoint adding to the consensus.

This stance was hammered home by the comment: "If anyone thinks Wagner would take £10m I think they are very much mistaken. Put another 5 on and they may get to have a conversation."

"Some crazy figures being quoted in the responses here. Would agree if he was 5 years younger. Would be surprised if he went for anymore than £8m-£10m. Don’t think we need to sell tbh," was another strong reaction.

Some associated with Forest are also not willing to entertain such speculation, with a Reds fan posting: "Utter rubbish. Brum won't sell and he does not get ahead of Cunha, Murillo, Diomande or Milenkovic."

Neumann's contract at St. Andrew's is said to expire in June 2028, meaning in January, the defender will be entering the final 18 months of his deal.

As such, Birmingham will still hold plenty of power when it comes to negotiations, although it remains to be seen whether either Forest or Fiorentina are to press forward with such advances between now and then.
