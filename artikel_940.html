Anthropic trading tests investors' appetite for AI

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Anthropic was valued at $2 trillion after shares started trading on the Nasdaq following a record $81 billion IPO. Its performance will put investor confidence in AI to the test.

Shares of US artificial intelligence (AI) giant Anthropic saw their first day of trading on Thursday on the US tech-heavy Nasdaq stock exchange.

Anthropic is one of the global leaders in AI technology and is best known for its Claude chatbot.

The stock opened at $111.50 (€101.7) — that's 34% higher than the IPO price of $83 and puts the company's valuation at $2 trillion. Anthropic stock ended the first day of trading at $97 per share.

The company's share performance is being seen as a key test of investor appetite for AI, as stock prices have swung wildly in recent months.

The San Francisco-based company behind the Claude AI tool has offered 980 million shares at $83 per share, raising a record $81 billion.

This valued the firm at $1.5 trillion, well above market estimates of around $1.3 trillion.

Anthropic is the first big AI company to go public. Its main competitor, ChatGPT-maker OpenAI,  has reportedly pushed its IPO to 2027.

Issuing shares on the stock market is essentially a way to raise money and avoid taking on debt.

Anthropic has already taken on $6.2 billion in debt and is not yet making a profit. At the same time, it faces massive bills for computer power and data centers.

That specific financial situation is one of the reasons for persistent concern that the AI boom might be a bubble that will eventually burst.
