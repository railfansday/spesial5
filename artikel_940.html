This is the third match between Japan and Brazil in exactly 13 months. Last October, Hajime Moriyasu made history by becoming the first manager to lead a Japan team to a senior international win over the Selecao.

Takumi Minamino, Keito Nakamura and Ayase Ueda scored the goals as Japan overturned a 2-0 deficit in the second half of a friendly match in Tokyo.

Moriyasu is still in post, but not for long. He was due to step aside after the Asian Cup, handing the reins to former Kashima Antlers boss Go Oiwa. The confederation championship in January and February has been postponed, and Moriyasu's number of remaining matches significantly reduced.

Brazil went on to lose to Norway in the last 16 after their victory over Japan in July. This is their fourth match in a row against AFC opposition after they spent the last international break in Australia and India.

Carlo Ancelotti has named a squad that's missing a handful of experienced players, not least injured skipper Marquinhos, but the attacking talent at his disposal is no joke. Vinicius Jr and Raphinha lead the way.

The postponement of the Asian Cup has left Japan in an uncertain spot. FourFourTwo predicts that Ancelotti's men will have the edge.
