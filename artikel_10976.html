AI fire detection system sprays food booth’s fake flames in bizarre viral video

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

An automated fire detection system at a ski resort in China has gone viral after repeatedly spraying water at a food booth’s sign, leaving viewers convinced it mistook pictures of flames for a real fire.

Footage of the bizarre incident shows a ceiling-mounted water cannon blasting a barbecue stall’s flame-covered sign as confused onlookers watch.

The incident occurred on November 19 at Wanlong Ski Resort in Zhangjiakou, Hebei province.

As shown in footage, the automated system fires a powerful stream of water directly toward a sign decorated with images of burning charcoal.

The video quickly spread online, with viewers joking that the supposedly intelligent system couldn’t distinguish between a picture of fire and the real thing.

However, despite the apparent AI blunder, resort officials have offered a different explanation.

According to Yangtse Evening News, a visitor said she had ordered lamb skewers shortly before the system activated, with water beginning to spray not long after cooking started.

Resort officials claimed the system was actually triggered by heat from a charcoal grill positioned behind the sign, rather than mistaking the flame graphics for a genuine blaze.

Staff quickly shut down the equipment and cleaned the affected area, reopening it approximately 20 minutes later. No injuries were reported, and the resort has since adjusted the grill’s position to prevent another incident.

The footage comes amid growing scrutiny of automated detection systems and their mistakes.

In October 2025, a teenager was swarmed by armed police after an AI security system mistook a bag of Doritos for a gun.

More recently, a Pennsylvania high school was forced to scrap its AI security system after ordinary student belongings repeatedly triggered weapon alerts.

While the Chinese resort maintains that its system was responding to a genuine heat source, the footage has become another viral example of automated technology producing unintended results.

And although the equipment may not have mistaken the fake flames for a real fire, that didn’t stop it from giving the barbecue stall an unexpected soaking.
