The Best Advent Calendars for Crafters, Makers and Artists

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Advent calendars for crafters come in a huge variety of options spanning crochet, coloring, clay and any other creative hobby you can think of!

For folks who love to spend a snowy day exploring their creativity, a craft Advent calendar is the perfect gift. Unlike classic Advent calendars, which contain chocolate or tea, these clever countdowns feature 12 to 24 days of fun activities to toil through when the brisk weather keeps you bundled up inside. And whether you’re shopping for men, women or kids, there’s an option out there for every hobby.

So grab your favorite faux fur blanket, pull up a seat, and turn on that imagination! Below are the best craft Advent calendars for artists, innovators and casual makers, tested by our editors.

I’m already a huge fan of the pottery kits at Pottd, so when I saw they offered a crafting Advent calendar, I just had to snag one. And let me tell you—testing the Pottd Advent calendar has been the highlight of my holiday season so far. From the outside in, the Pottd Advent Calendar is a truly magical 24-day experience. This year’s theme is Gingerbread Bakery. Inside the adorable gingerbread house calendar are 24 bakery-themed boxes, each containing a small craft.

While I won’t spoil all the surprises, inside is an assortment of fun hands-on projects, including air-dry clay creations, polymer clay miniatures, foam dough decorations and stands for displaying your creations. It also comes with all the tools, paints and accessories you need for each project. And if you need a little creative inspiration, each project also includes a QR code linking to a guided instructional video.

At $100, it’s definitely a bit of a splurge. But considering the craftsmanship and thought put into each project, I’d say the price is well worth it. This is one stunning Advent calendar—and the projects inside are bound to spark the spirit of the season!

Looking for a fiber-focused option? Snag the legendary The Woobles Advent calendar. This Advent calendar for crafters has everything you need to keep calm and stitch on. And no worries for newbies—The Woobles offer easy-to-follow video instructions for each kit, so you can learn a new skill as they craft some cute friends!

One of my favorite aspects of the yearly Woobles Advent calendar is its reusability. The calendar boxes themselves are historically cute and sturdy—I still keep craft items in my two boxes from previous years—but this year is even better! The holiday Victorian home design is absolutely swoon-worthy. Pulling out the day’s box reveals a cozy backdrop printed on the inside of the box, so you get a bonus surprise at the end of the countdown!

While the calendar has offered 24 days in previous years, this 12-day Advent calendar is still chock full of surprises. It’s also a lot more manageable to tackle eight projects instead of a full fleet of 20. Your recipient can open the first day on December 1 and take their time working through the projects instead of rushing through them, which I really appreciate.

Additionally, the boxes are printed with furniture for the home, so they double as play items. And as always, one of the days offers a collectible 2026 snow globe to add to their collection!

The Coco Wyo coloring book Advent calendar is your ticket to a cozy afternoon! With 24 mini coloring books stuffed with over 500 pages of adorable illustrations, your recipient is bound to enjoy hours of creative coloring magic.

Behind each door of this 24-day calendar is a themed coloring book, each filled with classic and newly drawn designs from Coco Wyo. Images feature calming activities, winter traditions and—of course—cute animals. The paper is nice and thick and the prints are clear, giving each book a high-quality feel.

The best part? Because they’re tiny, the coloring books are perfect for holiday travels. Just pick up a pack of markers or crayons and you have a cozy activity to tap into all season long!

This seasonally-appropriate Winter Sports Advent calendar from KiwiCo includes 12 DIY wooden craft ornament kits, offering a new activity every couple of days. Each one is a different Christmas character participating in a winter sport. Editor Mary Henn enjoyed putting together the ornaments with her daughter as a cute holiday bonding activity.

“Okay, this Advent calendar is such a unique and fun idea,” raves Mary. “My kiddo loved helping assemble a few of the ornaments during testing. Although the suggested age range is 5+ (likely due to the small parts), the instructions were simple to follow, and the assembly was easy enough for her to help with some of the projects!”

The bright, bold colors also make it fun and appealing for kids. Each kit is also individually wrapped, so there’s no loose pieces or missing materials. The packaging is thoughtfully designed with hidden doors, colorful pictures, and descriptions on the back. While it’s a little pricey at $50, the ornaments are well-made and should last for years.

“Each little wooden craft ornament is adorable,” raves Mary. “I especially love that some have movable parts, like spinning tops and sliding limbs, which make them even more fun and interactive.”

For folks who still appreciate the art of sending letters and documenting memos on paper, the Papier stationery Advent calendar is well worth the splurge. This luxury countdown is loaded with 24 desk delights that make crafting snail mail a dream. It truly checks all the boxes: luxurious, cohesive, joyful and, a personal favorite, practical.

“I loved last year’s letter-shaped calendar, but the design of the 2026 countdown is even better,” says Deputy Editor Katie Bandurski. “It’s a little more streamlined yet still oh-so impressive.”

When you lift the lid, you’re greeted with three drawers of perfectly packaged mini presents to open each day until Christmas. Each gift is exquisitely wrapped in thick paper in an array of cohesive yet distinct patterns.

“If you want an Advent calendar that feels like you’re truly opening a gift, rather than just pulling open a drawer, this is the best option I’ve seen in my years of testing,” says Katie.

This luxury calendar sells out almost every year, so we recommend ordering it as soon as you can. And if you miss out on the full-sized version (or want to spend a little less), Papier offers a 12-day version new for 2026!

LEGO Advent calendars are some of the best Advent calendars, hands down—and that’s not just my nostalgia talking. Unpacking this year’s LEGO City Advent calendar offered me the same magical experience I remember as a kid.

Behind each door is a variety of buildable toys, figures and accessories. Each and every day was fun to open, especially the days with builds inside them. The instructions are printed on the inside of the doors, so they’re rather simplistic. However, I found them overall thorough enough to figure things out.

Both my notoriously hard-to-please nephew and my millennial husband worked on the projects together. Their verdict? This was the best Advent calendar either had ever received! Believe me—there’s no better endorsement than that.

Here’s a gift for crafters, young and mature. This DIY ornament Advent calendar includes all the bells and whistles for designing their own felt Christmas tree ornaments. While it’s not set up like a traditional calendar—you won’t be opening any doors—the numbered craft kids prove to be exciting enough on their own.

Tester Katie Bandurski tried this Advent calendar and confirms that it’s fun for almost any age, but tots especially will benefit from the easy instructions. Plus—and this is a big one for parents—there’s no mess!

Kids craft 25 ornaments using felt and stickers—no sewing or glue required at all. And while each ornament has its own set of picture instructions, crafters can follow their heart and create something truly unique. The only thing to consider? They might need their own mini Christmas tree to show off their creations!
