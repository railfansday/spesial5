Why Aspyn Ovard Changed Her Mind About 3rd Baby Amid Older Child’s Cancer

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County star Aspyn Ovard opened up about welcoming her third baby while one of her older daughters was battling terminal cancer.

“I didn’t want my other child to grow up alone,” Aspyn, 30, said in a Saturday, November 21, YouTube vlog. “I did decide to have another baby. I did do it on kind of a crazy timeline, like, it was a lot.”

She continued, “It was a lot to be doing all of that with everything we were going through, but I just knew that that was like what was right for our family. I didn’t want a really big age gap, so that was something that I put myself through and did for my family and for my daughters. I have never felt like I shouldn’t have done it like on that timeline. I’m very glad that I did.”

Aspyn and ex-husband Parker Ferris welcomed daughters Cove, Lola and Elle in 2019, 2021 and 2024, respectively. Before Aspyn’s pregnancy with Elle, the reality TV star found out that one of their older daughters was diagnosed with a rare form of childhood cancer.

“That was, like, the one thing I could focus on [and] the one thing that was in my control,” she recalled on Saturday. “I did make the decision to do that. And honestly, I feel like it kept me going. …I do think that being pregnant saved my life. I don’t know how I would have had the strength to keep going and stay here if I was not growing that baby, so that also was a huge reason why I felt like I needed to do that in that time.”

Aspyn and Parker split after Elle’s birth, often opting to keep their child’s cancer diagnosis private.

“I’ve been very private for the last few years so I was thinking that maybe I could get away with doing this and still staying private,” Aspyn exclusively told Us earlier this month of opening up about her family’s ordeal on the Mormon Wives spinoff. “I think that this is definitely just a different experience than me sharing my life through my own content. It was kind of like going big or going home in a way.”

She continued, “I had a lot of reassurance from production that they would be very respectful of my life and my story. I got to have a hand in crafting my story and the way that they put everything together. So they made me feel very comfortable and OK with sharing everything that I did.”
