'Boston Blue' Recasts Another 'Blue Bloods' Role After Surprise Cast Changes

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Blue Bloods‘ spinoff, Boston Blue, is recasting another role from the original show.

Amaury Nolasco has been cast as Arturo Muñoz in an upcoming episode that will reintroduce the former cartel leader — who is also the biological father of Baez’s (Marisa Ramirez) adopted daughter, Elena. He was previously portrayed by Juan Francisco Villa in two episodes of Blue Bloods before the Boston Blue shakeup.

According to the official synopsis, Nolasco, 55, will appear in the December 11 episode when Baez’s daughter, Elena, “is kidnapped the morning of Sarah’s wedding,” and the Reagans and Silvers “launch a desperate search to bring her home.”

“As Danny and Lena pursue a lead involving Arturo Muñoz, Elena’s biological father and former head of the Mano Sangriento cartel, the investigation takes an unexpected turn,” the description continued.

This isn’t the first time Boston Blue has recast a role that originated on Blue Bloods. Danny’s (Donnie Wahlberg) journey in New York was featured on CBS’ Blue Bloods, which ran from 2010 to 2024. The spinoff, which premiered in October 2025, kicked off with Danny visiting Boston after Sean (Mika Amonsen) ended up in a coma. This version of Sean looked a little different because the role was previously played by Andrew Terraciano.

“It was a difficult decision — certainly for me — and it’s one of the decisions that made me uncertain about doing the show. It’s like, ‘How do we do this with a different [actor]?'” Wahlberg, 57, admitted to Us in February. “I have to say, Mika has the same level of grace and class that Andrew showed. Mika has great respect for Andrew. He stepped into the role very mindfully and carefully and he took his time. He was very thoughtful about how he went about playing this character.”

Terraciano, 23, also broke his silence on the onscreen swap.

“It’s beautiful because you’ll always remember every single line and every day that you had the opportunity to be that character. It doesn’t mean for even a second that somebody else can’t go and do something beautiful with it,” he shared with Us in December 2025. “I got the joy of playing Sean, and I got to send him off into the world. Now somebody else gets that joy. We’re both getting to show what we do and then send Sean off into the world.”

Terraciano specially mentioned Amonsen’s performance, adding, “I appreciate it because not only has there been such an outcry of love from the community, but there’s also a very clear love coming to the show.”

He explained: “It’s hundreds if not thousands of people who depend on their livelihood for a show to be made. It really is amazing that part of that character remains. Somebody can look back and say, ‘I want to pay homage to that. I want to respect that and I want to work on making something beautiful.'”

For Amonsen, 24, the transition has since gotten easier.

“That initial pressure from fans and making sure that I represented Sean in a way that Andrew would appreciate, fans would appreciate and have them understand that I wasn’t just coming into this creating whatever I wanted [was hard],” Amonsen exclusively told Us in March. “It’s been reciprocated well and I’m happy about that. But I think that that was the biggest challenge. At this point, I’ve slipped into comfortability.”

Boston Blue airs on CBS Fridays at 10 p.m. ET.
