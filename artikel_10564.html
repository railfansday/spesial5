Among Us player & his mom reveal how they helped save kidnapped girl he met in-game

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A 16-year-old boy has revealed how a chance encounter in Among Us led to him and his mother helping rescue a teenage girl who had allegedly been held captive for more than 500 days.

Earlier in November, 17-year-old Kylie Arellano was rescued after secretly asking another player for help while playing Innersloth’s popular social deduction game.

Arellano had been missing since May 2025, when she disappeared from her home in Utah at the age of 15. She was allegedly held against her will in the basement of a home in Huntington, Oregon.

For Colorado teenager Landon Tresp, what began as an ordinary gaming session on October 15 would end with him helping a girl hundreds of miles away escape her alleged captor.

After meeting in Among Us, Landon and Arellano struck up a friendship and began communicating through Discord.

Over the following two weeks, Arellano began opening up about her situation, eventually sending Landon news articles covering her disappearance.

Realizing something was seriously wrong, Landon turned to his mother, Khristina, for help on Halloween. Neither knew that within 24 hours, the girl would finally be free.

Khristina began speaking with Arellano through Discord, but locating the teenager proved challenging as she didn’t know the address where she was being held.

“I knew I had to do everything I could to make sure she was brought to safety and had a second chance,” Khristina recalled.

Determined to find her, Khristina searched online property records before turning to real estate website Zillow. After being sent photos of two potential houses, Arellano recognized the second property.

Khristina contacted the girl’s family and began working with the FBI to bring her home.

Speaking with KOAA, Khristina described how devastating it was to learn just how long Arellano had been missing.

“Just heartbreaking to think that she had been going through what she had for over 520 days and without anyone that she could reach a hand out to and not knowing where you’re at,” she said.

On November 1, authorities arrived at the Oregon residence. Khristina stayed on a video call with Arellano, encouraging her to make the final few steps toward freedom.

“I was on the video chat with her when I told her to face those basement stairs and run up them and down that hallway,” Khristina recalled. “And grab that front door because that was her freedom.”

After more than 500 days away from home, Arellano was finally in the care of law enforcement and was taken for medical treatment and evaluation.

For Landon, seeing the friend he had met just weeks earlier finally reach safety was an overwhelming moment.

“Oh my God, just pure bliss. I was so happy she was safe in that car. I was so happy she was out of that horrible monster’s house,” he said.

Adam Hunter Harmel, 39, has since been indicted on a federal charge of transportation with intent to engage in criminal sexual activity. Prosecutors allege he met the girl online before traveling to Utah and taking her across state lines.

Despite everything that happened, Landon and Arellano haven’t stopped playing video games together.

The pair remain friends, while Khristina continues to speak with Arellano every day. The family is also hoping to meet her in person.

What began with two strangers meeting in Among Us has become a friendship that extends far beyond the game, with Landon and his mother giving Arellano the help she had desperately needed for more than a year.
