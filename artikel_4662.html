'Forever' chemicals? Don't be so sure, says engineer

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Inside the disinfection room of Baltimore's Ashburton water filtration plant, 14 University of Maryland students clad in hard hats peered up at six towering vats of bleach used to treat water that flows from the distant Liberty Reservoir into the facility, killing bacteria and other pathogens before it's routed to the region's kitchen sinks.
