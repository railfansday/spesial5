Rove Gives You 1,500 Free Miles Just For Signing Up—Plus 5,000 More On Your First $500 Hotel Booking

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Rove is offering 1,500 free miles just for signing up. You don’t have to book a trip, buy anything, or complete any other transaction. I specifically asked about an activity requirement, and Rove confirmed that new members get the miles instantly. I’ve written quite a bit about Rove, and asked them to improve the offer available through my link. This is three times the standard 500-mile referral signup bonus. Just like normal referrals, I receive Rove Miles when someone signs up through this link. It doesn’t cost you anything. In fact it gets you much more than not using this link. They’ve also created a hotel booking code for readers: 1,500 miles for joining: Use this link to create your new account. There’s no purchase requirement. 5,000 additional miles on your first hotel booking of $500 or more: Enter promo code VFTW when booking through Rove. A new member who uses both offers would earn 6,500 bonus miles, on top of the normal miles from their qualifying hotel booking. You can also just take the signup miles. And 5,000 miles on a $500 booking is twice what Rove offered at that spending level during its May promotion. What You Can Do With These Miles Rove is a free rewards program that lets you earn miles from hotel and flight bookings, online shopping, dining and gift cards. You don’t have to apply for a credit card, and you can earn rewards from the card you use to pay as well. Their transfer partners include familiar programs like Air Canada Aeroplan, Air France KLM Flying Blue, Qatar Airways Privilege Club and Virgin Atlantic Flying Club. They also have programs that are harder to access through the major bank currencies, including Lufthansa Miles & More (for better availability of business and first class awards), Japan Airlines Mileage Bank (better availability of awards, lower prices on partner awards), Air India Maharaja Club (United awards starting at just 3,500 points) and Copa ConnectMiles (Emirates awards with no fuel surcharges). Rove has a 2,000-mile minimum for partner transfers. You don’t need a hotel stay to earn additional miles. I’ve covered Rove’s dining program and instant gift card rewards, and just wrote about its personalized shopping offers of up to 25 miles per dollar. When The Hotel Bonus Makes Sense You can also redeem Rove Miles directly for travel. As I wrote last month, I can usually get around 1.5 cents apiece redeeming them for hotel stays that still earn hotel points and elite status credit. Using that benchmark, the 5,000-mile hotel bonus is worth about $75 toward travel. On a qualifying $500 booking, that’s an extra 15% in value before the booking’s regular rewards. Expressed another way, the promo adds 10 miles per dollar at exactly $500 in qualifying spend. I’ve been particularly interested in Rove’s Loyalty Eligible hotel rates. There are two different kinds of bookings to compare: Loyalty Eligible rates generally earn 5 Rove miles per dollar, while preserving eligibility for the hotel’s own points, elite night credit and status benefits. The hotel charges your card, which can make paying with that chain’s credit card attractive. Rove Rates can offer more Rove miles, with the site advertising up to 25 per dollar, but generally give up the hotel loyalty program earnings and benefits. That tradeoff is easier at an independent hotel where you wouldn’t earn chain points anyway. I’d take the free signup miles. For the hotel offer, enter VFTW and consider the rate you’re selecting before booking for the 5,000-mile bonus.
