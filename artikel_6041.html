The Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card is a great choice for beginners and experienced points-and-miles enthusiasts who want an easy way to earn miles — all for an affordable $95 annual fee.

The Venture Rewards card's current welcome offer is especially compelling — but this is your last chance to take advantage of it.

You can earn 75,000 bonus miles after spending $4,000 on purchases within the first three months from account opening. Plus, you'll receive a $300 credit upon approval to use on hotel and vacation rentals booked through Capital One Travel in the first cardholder year.

Here's what the offer is worth and what to know before applying.

Apply now for a limited-time offer on the Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card: Earn more than $1,000 in travel bonuses with a limited-time $300 Capital One Travel stays credit, plus earn 75,000 bonus miles once you spend $4,000 on purchases within the first three months of account opening.

New Capital One Venture Rewards cardholders can earn 75,000 bonus miles after spending $4,000 on purchases within the first three months from account opening. Plus, you'll receive a $300 credit upon approval to use on hotel and vacation rentals booked through Capital One Travel in the first cardholder year. This offer ends soon.

Including the credit, this offer is worth $1,688, per TPG’s November 2026 valuations.

In terms of miles alone, this is the standard welcome bonus on the card. However, the inclusion of the $300 credit makes this offer among the most valuable we've seen.

For comparison, previous welcome offers have gone as high as 100,000 miles after meeting the minimum spending requirements (no longer available), but with no credit.

It’s possible to squeeze even more from that bonus if you redeem Capital One miles for maximum value, thanks to their impressive roster of 15-plus transfer partners.

There are some eligibility requirements to keep in mind before applying. You don't want to waste a hard pull on your credit report if you aren't eligible for the current offer.

First, if you've previously earned a welcome bonus on the Venture Rewards, you won't be able to earn another one for 48 months. Note that this is 48 months from the date you received your bonus, not from the date you were approved.

In addition, if you previously earned a welcome bonus on the Capital One Venture X Rewards Credit Card, you will more than likely not be eligible to earn a welcome bonus on the Venture Rewards.

Capital One may consider multiple factors when deciding whether to approve you; for a deeper dive into its application requirements, check out our full guide.

This card earns at least 2 miles per dollar spent on all purchases. So, by spending $4,000 on it to earn the welcome bonus, you’d earn at least 83,000 miles in the first few months, including the welcome bonus.

And since the Venture Rewards earns 5 miles per dollar spent on car rentals, hotels and vacation rentals booked through Capital One Travel, it’s possible to earn even more miles while working toward your welcome bonus.

While some travel credit cards may earn slightly higher rates on select categories such as groceries or dining, the draw here is the simplicity and guarantee of earning at least 2 miles per dollar spent on anything you buy without the need to memorize multiple bonus spending categories.

Your total value from this welcome bonus depends largely on how you redeem your miles. There are two key options:

When converting your miles to hotel points or airline miles, most partners offer a 1:1 transfer ratio, though a couple of partners have different ratios. You can get great sweet spot redemptions by transferring miles to programs like Air Canada Aeroplan and Air France-KLM Flying Blue.

For example, TPG's editor-in-chief, Nick Ewen, transferred 117,000 Capital One miles to Emirates Skywards to upgrade himself and two family members to Emirates first class. This broke down to 39,000 miles per person, a reasonable cost for the upgrade.

If you don’t want to transfer your miles, you can redeem them by booking a trip through Capital One Travel. These redemptions can be very handy in a world where you may be renting recreational vehicles, booking vacation home rentals or securing a cabin close to home more often than hopping on a flight to the other side of the world.

The Capital One Venture Rewards remains a strong option for travelers who value simplicity. It earns at least 2 miles per dollar spent on purchases and gives cardholders access to Capital One's airline and hotel transfer partners, all for a $95 annual fee.

Plus, the current welcome offer — which ends soon — includes a solid haul of miles and a $300 credit for Capital One Travel, one of the best offers we've seen. Just remember to use your credit in the first cardholder year, or you'll forfeit the value.
