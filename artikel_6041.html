Democrat Cindy Holscher wins Kansas governor's race, extending party's hold on governor's mansion

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Kansas elected Democrat Cindy Holscher as governor, marking the party's third consecutive gubernatorial victory despite the state's Republican presidential streak.

Democratic state Sen. Cindy Holscher won the Kansas governor’s race, defeating Republican Kansas Senate President Ty Masterson in the open contest to succeed term-limited Democratic Gov. Laura Kelly, The Associated Press projected.The victory keeps the governor’s mansion in Democratic hands and marks the third consecutive Kansas gubernatorial election won by a Democrat. Kelly won the office in 2018 and secured a second term in 2022.

The result underscores Democrats’ continued success in Kansas gubernatorial races despite Republicans’ longstanding dominance in the state’s presidential elections. The GOP has carried Kansas in every presidential election since 1968.

Kansas Gov.-elect Cindy Holscher, a Democrat, campaigns in Lenexa on Nov. 2, 2026, ahead of her victory over Republican Ty Masterson.  (Nick Oxford/Bloomberg via Getty Images)

Holscher has served in the Kansas Senate since 2021 after previously spending four years in the state House. She secured the Democratic nomination after defeating fellow state Sen. Ethan Corson, who was endorsed by Kelly and former Democratic Gov. Kathleen Sebelius, in the August primary.Holscher faced scrutiny during the campaign over resurfaced social media posts from 2023 in which she suggested Jesus Christ could have been transgender. The comments came during a debate over proposed Kansas legislation restricting drag performances in front of children.

Holscher celebrated her victory in a post on X, thanking Kansas voters as she prepared to take office.

Democratic state Sen. Cindy Holscher will become Kansas’ next governor after defeating Republican Ty Masterson in the Nov. 3 election, The Associated Press projected Thursday. (REUTERS)

"Thank you, Kansas! It’s the honor of a lifetime to serve as your next Governor," Holscher wrote.

Masterson, who was endorsed by President Donald Trump in the crowded GOP primary, conceded the race in a post on X and said he had called Holscher to congratulate her.

"It looks like we will come up just short, but the things we fought for are no less important today than they were yesterday."

Masterson, who has served as Kansas Senate president since 2021, also pointed to Republicans' continued control of the state Legislature and said he would return to the Senate in January to focus on property taxes, public safety, affordability and schools.
