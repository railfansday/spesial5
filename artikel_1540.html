GdS: ‘Numbers like Kaka’ – why Rabiot is so important to Milan with renewal imminent

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Adrien Rabiot has had an outstanding start to the 2026-27 season, and he is showing absolutely no signs of slowing down.

La Gazzetta dello Sport (seen below) begin a report this morning by pointing out what Rabiot said after Milan’s 5-1 demolition of Frosinone on Sunday: “An assist is better than a goal.” He was the man of the match against Frosinone: five goals were scored, yet none bore his name.

Still, the Frenchman made himself the stand-out performer, thanks to his tireless running, leadership, and a series of decisive passes, not to mention his role in the opening goal, which came via an own-goal.

It was a summer of doubts for Rabiot, given that his extended vacation was interpreted by some as a sign of discontent and a longing to reunite with his former mentor – Massimiliano Allegri – at Napoli. Yet, he has transformed into a cornerstone of this new era.

The former PSG man is now a decisive force on the pitch, integral to Ruben Amorim’s tactical framework, and a true leader. The numbers demonstrate this perfectly: Rabiot is the first midfielder to contribute to at least one goal (scoring or assisting) in seven consecutive Serie A appearances since Kaká in 2004.

With five goals and six assists, he recently became the first midfielder in the last 20 years of Serie A to record more than 10 goal contributions within the first 10 matches of a single season. In total, Rabiot has surpassed the milestone of 50 direct goal involvements in Serie A (29 goals and 22 assists).

Milan trust him, and the feeling is mutual. As previously mentioned, the very fact that he remained with the Rossoneri over the summer – publicly reaffirming his commitment to the club – is reflected in his attitude, both on and off the field.

He is always smiling and highly active on social media; his numbers on Instagram are as impressive as his stats on the field. He posts photos of his goals, his celebrations and embraces with Diego Moreira, with whom he is clearly building a close bond.

Amorim said after the game: “Adrien can play as a ’10,’ an attacking midfielder, an ‘8,’ or even as a playmaker – though he doesn’t particularly like that role. He is certainly one of the best players I have ever coached, and one of the best midfielders in Europe.”

Speaking to Telefoot, Rabiot addressed the topic of his excellent start statistically: “It’s a positive thing to have players capable of being decisive, even if they aren’t forwards. It also serves as an encouragement to those around us.

“At Milan, we have quite a few young players, and it’s important for them to have role models in their day-to-day lives. I had only been back from vacation for a week when he was already talking to me about a contract renewal.

“I told him, ‘Coach, take it easy!’ (laughs). But, naturally, it’s significant for a player to feel that kind of trust—the coach relies on us, holds us in high regard, and makes us feel important.”

Further significant remarks regarding Rabiot were made by Gerry Cardinale on the eve of the most recent league match, specifically concerning his imminent contract renewal.

“I love Adrien, he’s one of my favourite players. He’s fantastic. I have enormous respect for him and his family. We are in talks, and I hope we reach a happy conclusion soon. I believe he should be a signature player for Milan. Could it be a Christmas gift for the fans? Absolutely, and for me, too.”

The renewal will happen, and it will happen soon. Any delay until the new year should not be interpreted as a negative sign. Rather, it reflects the fact that everything is already settled, and there is no rush to finalise the signature.

Adrien’s current deal – which runs until 2028 – will be extended to 2030, with an additional one-year option. From his current age of 31, he will remain a fixture in the Rossoneri midfield until he is 33 or 34, the prime of his footballing maturity.

Now into his thirties, he will also earn a more lucrative contract than his current one, which pays him €3.5m net per season. It is worth noting that in the summer of 2025, Adrien was acquired from Marseille for €7m after a training-ground altercation with Jonathan Rowe.

It was, by any definition, a fantastic deal. As for the future, Rabiot has a role model he can observe from very close quarters: “Modrić trains as if he were 21, he’s an example to follow. The closer you get to the end of your career, the more you start to savor every moment. I try to do the same.”

With the imminent contract extension, Adrien Rabiot is also set to conclude his career in the red and black colours, and that would make everyone happy.
