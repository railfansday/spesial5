Mercato: Ibrahim Maza drops a little bomb on PSG!

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

For the moment, Paris Saint-Germain assures that it will not seek to compensate for the long unavailability of Khvicha Kvaratskhelia by strengthening itself during the next winter transfer window. “No, not especially. No injuries mean signing new players. At the start of the season, I said that we could endure anything even if there will be significant injuries. But we remain attentive to opportunities”Luis Enrique recently confided at a press conference.

However, rumors are rife. Recently, the English press assured that the French champions had reactivated the track leading to the young Frenchman from Bournemouth, Eli Junior Kroupi (20 years old). Barely back from injury, the former Lorient player is one of the most coveted attackers of the moment. In Germany, sky assured that the Algerian international from Bayer Leverkusen, Ibrahim Maza, was also being courted.

The only downsides are RMC denied any desire of PSG to initiate negotiations for the 20-year-old nugget, whose market value is estimated at €50 million by the site Transfermarktand his club does not intend to part with it immediately. Maza is seen as the next Florian Wirtz of the team and its managers already want to extend a contract which runs until 2030. But this Wednesday, the former Hertha Berlin attacking midfielder dropped a small bomb at the microphone of Sky Germany by declaring that he would like to wear the Parisian jersey one day.

“Of course, it would be a huge dream to one day play for such a prestigious club. I think we all know what a great club Paris Saint-Germain is. It is one of the best in the world, along with Real Madrid, Barcelona and Arsenal. Playing for a club like that is a goal I strive towards, like many other footballers. » A statement that will not go unnoticed across the Rhine… and in the French capital.
