Witcher 4 Won’t Be A PS5 Exclusive After New Deal With PlayStation

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

CD Projekt Red and Sony Interactive Entertainment just announced a marketing deal for The Witcher 4, but that doesn't mean the game will be exclusive to PlayStation.

CD Projekt Red's Michał Nowakowski said on social media, "Just to be clear: The Witcher 4 and our other upcoming titles will remain multiplatform."

The marketing deal could be similar to the one between Rockstar and Sony for GTA 6, where GTA 6 was given preferential treatment on PS5 in a number of ways in the marketing campaign.

Beyond The Witcher 4, CD Projekt Red is making Cyberpunk 2, a new IP codenamed Hadar, a new game believed to be a multiplayer The Witcher title, and a remake of the original Witcher. On the mobile side, CD Projekt Red is working with Scopely on a new game that hasn't been announced yet.

The Witcher 4 now has more than 500 people working on it, and the game is expected to release in 2028. Given that timing, The Witcher 4 likely won't be on a disc because Sony won't support game discs beginning in January 2028.

That being said, CD Projekt Red is promising that The Witcher 4 will have some kind of physical goodies for fans.

In other news about The Witcher 4, CD Projekt Red recently discussed the game in comparison to GTA 6.

"Of course we did look at the game; we did see the differences. We believe there’s going to be a comparable leap between The Witcher 3 and The Witcher 4–I think so, yes," Nowakowski said. "It’s been 11 years; we are working on The Witcher 4 to make it significantly better looking and improved in terms of pretty much every possible facet of the game, so yes, you should expect us to make a much bigger jump between Witcher 3 and Witcher 4.”
