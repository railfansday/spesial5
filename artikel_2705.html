NFL news, live updates: Lions LB Jack Campbell says he has 'full movement' in lower body after neck injury; Cardinals reportedly trading Jacoby Brissett to Ravens

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

We're gearing up for Week 9 of the NFL season (and the looming NFL trade deadline), where teams will have to make a decision on the intended course of the remainder of their season — are they going all in? Or is it time to tear it all down and look toward the future?

One of those teams will be the Baltimore Ravens (4-4), whose quarterback, Lamar Jackson, suffered a thumb injury that will require surgery and has landed him on injured reserve. Backup Tyler Huntley then suffered what is reportedly a season-ending turf toe injury in relief of Jackson, putting undrafted free agent Joe Fagnano into action as the emergency third quarterback.

Now, Fagnano will get the start as the Jacksonville Jaguars travel to face the Ravens on "Thursday Night Football" in Week 9. The 25-year-old, who played his college ball at Maine and then UConn, will be the second undrafted rookie to start under center for Baltimore in the Ravens' 32-year history, per ESPN's Jamison Hensley.

NFL fans also await news on the fate of star running back Jonathan Taylor, who has been touted as a potential trade-deadline mover as the Indianapolis Colts kicked off their franchise reset with a fire sale. They've already traded away two foundational players, Quenton Nelson and DeForest Buckner, for several draft picks as they look to the future, leaving us to wonder whether their star running back could be next.

Follow along with Yahoo Sports for the latest NFL news, injury updates and more ahead of Week 9.

Jacksonville Jaguars running back Bhayshul Tuten will be a game-time decision for Thursday's game against the Baltimore Ravens, per NFL Network.

If the Indianapolis Colts are going to trade running back Jonathan Taylor, it won't be cheap. The Colts are seeking a Christian McCaffrey-type package for Taylor, according to NFL Network.

McCaffrey was traded from the Carolina Panthers to the San Francisco 49ers for four draft picks, including a second, third and fourth-round selection.

Taylor is in the midst of another excellent year. Through eight games, he has 610 rushing yards and eight touchdowns.

That could lead to a ton of activity before the NFL's trade deadline, which is on Nov. 10. The precise organizational term for the current state of the Baltimore Ravens is: screwed. It's a fate that likely awaits every other team that doesn't already have a quarterback succession plan in place, right this very moment. But Baltimore is uniquely beset by injury.

The Ravens lost all-everything quarterback Lamar Jackson on the very first play of Sunday's game against the Buffalo Bills to a thumb injury that could cost him the remainder of the season. Making matters infinitely worse, Baltimore subsequently lost backup Tyler Huntley to a toe injury that could cost him time. And time is not a luxury Baltimore has right now; the Ravens are scheduled to play the Jaguars on Thursday night.

Oh, and just to add to the joy, undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano, who leaped from emergency No. 3 quarterback into the lineup on Sunday, is himself dealing with an ankle injury. It rains, it pours, and sometimes, it Biblically floods.

As for Baltimore's quarterback options now, well … how's your arm feeling? Can you get to Charm City by Thursday?

It's been over two years since San Francisco 49ers wide receiver Brandon Aiyuk played in an NFL game. After sustaining an ACL and MCL injury during the 2024 season, Aiyuk was initially placed on the PUP list ahead of 2025. But his relationship with the 49ers deteriorated, leading Aiyuk to not appear in a game despite still being under contract with the team.

Now the wideout is looking to change that. In an attempt to get back in the NFL, the 28-year-old Aiyuk hired agent Drew Rosenhaus, per multiple media reports. Rosenhaus told reporters he is tasked with "working on getting [Aiyuk] back to work and playing again in the NFL."

Rosenhaus got to work quickly on making that a reality, as it was reported Wednesday that Aiyuk filed paperwork to be reinstated. He plans to report to the 49ers on Friday. At that point, the team has a few options. It can keep him under his current deal, release him or trade him ahead of the deadline, per ESPN.
