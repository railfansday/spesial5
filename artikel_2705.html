Kickers made the difference as Harrison Butker missed, Tyler Bass won the game

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Thanksgiving night game between the Bills and Chiefs was billed as the battle of two great quarterbacks, Josh Allen and Patrick Mahomes. What proved to matter even more was the contrast of two kickers, as Buffalo’s Tyler Bass was great and Kansas City’s Harrison Butker was not.

On a rainy day in Buffalo, the fourth quarter featured Bass making a 54-yard field goal to tie the game 10-10, Butker missing a 46-yard field goal that would have given the lead back to the Chiefs, and then Bass making a 57-yard field goal as time expired to win the game, 13-10.

Bass has been excellent this season. He’s now 26-for-27 on field goals in 2026, with his only miss coming from 54 yards in last week’s win over the Dolphins.

Butker has been terrible this season. He’s now 15-for-23 on field goals in 2026, and his eight misses are the most for any kicker in the league. He also hasn’t made a single kick longer than 50 yards this season; 31 other NFL kickers have made at least one from beyond 50.

Chiefs coach Andy Reid said after the loss that he wasn’t ready to say whether Butker will remain the kicker in Kansas City.

“I’ll go back and go through all of it again,” Reid said immediately after the game. “Not gonna make any decisions right this minute.”

It’s hard to see how the Chiefs can stick with Butker down the stretch. A kicker’s job is to make his kicks, and Butker simply isn’t performing.
