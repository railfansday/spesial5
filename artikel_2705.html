Cardinals workout 7 offensive players

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Arizona Cardinals continue to update their free agent database, and have brought in seven players for a workout on Tuesday. Sometimes, this is just to update, and then again, position groups which are thin will need to evaluate which players to sign to the roster.

All of the players the coaching staff worked out are on the offensive side of the ball.

The players are: QB Carter Bradley, RBs Elijah Tau-Tolliver, Jermar Jefferson, and Ulysses Bentley, and WRs Tyler Johnson, Joseph Manjack, and Jamari Thrash.

Bradley (6'-3", 213 pounds) went undrafted in 2024 after playing for South Alabama where he was named Second Team All-Sun Belt Conference. He then signed with the Las Vegas Raiders and had a good training camp, but was waived on the final cutdown, then signed to the practice squad. He was promoted to the active roster before Week 15 and stayed on the roster for the remaining four games. He did not play any snaps.

Tau-Tolliver was undrafted this year after playing for Michigan State. He signed with the Baltimore Ravens and was a final-day cut. Jefferson was a seventh-round selection by the Detroit Lions in 2021. He played for the Lions for four years and played in nine games. He was injured all of 2022 and was cut before the 2025 season; he signed with the Tennessee Titans, was released, and signed with the Cardinals before Week 5 last year. He also spent time with the San Francisco 49ers and Minnesota Vikings.

Bentley went undrafted in 2025 after spending his college days at Ole Miss and SMU. He was the AAC Co-Rookie of the Year in 2020 and was named First Team All-AAC. He signed with the Indianapolis Colts, was a last-day cut, signed to the practice squad, and then was elevated to the main roster, then reverted back to the practice squad. This year, he was again a last-day cut, signed to the practice squad, and waived in early September.

Johnson was a fifth-round selection of the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in 2020. As a rookie, he played in 14 games with three starts and scored two touchdowns. He was a final cut after his second training camp and claimed off waivers by the Houston Texans, then released. He had stints with the New York Jets (twice), Buccaneers (again), Raiders, Los Angeles Rams, and Dallas Cowboys.

Manjack went undrafted out of TCU this year. He then signed with the Denver Broncos and was a last day cut.

Thrash (6'-0", 190 pounds) has game experience. He was a fifth-round draft pick by the Cleveland Browns in 2024, and the thought process at one point was that Thrash was going to become their WR3. He was a roster member for two seasons after being a Senior Bowl invitee and named First Team All-Sun Belt. He has played in 18 games with one start, 22 targets, 13 receptions for 129 yards, 7 first-down conversions, 359 offensive snaps, and 0 touchdowns. He also played on special teams and runs a 4.46.
