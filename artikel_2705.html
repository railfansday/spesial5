Hapless Chargers tickets now cheaper than a stadium cheeseburger

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A SoFi Stadium cheeseburger now costs more than tickets to watch the lowly Los Angeles Chargers play.

Astute sports fans noticed resale tickets for the Chargers-New York Jets game on Sunday going for as low as $14 at one point on TickPick, less than the $16.50 in-stadium cost for a cheeseburger.

Whether that says something about the hapless Chargers, who are 1-8 this season, or the cost of burgers, is up to fans.

Most online are embarrassed about their team and complaining about the expensive nature of going to a game outside of admission — the cheapest resale tickets on other sites like StubHub were just under $30.

Fans gather at SoFi Stadium during a timeout in an NFL game between the Los Angeles Chargers and the Los Angeles Rams in Inglewood, Calif., Nov. 1, 2026. Icon Sportswire via Getty Images

"Chargers should return back to San Diego," one person wrote.

"Don't forget parking $150," another added.

But there's no question the Nov. 22 game is fairly inconsequential to the NFL playoff picture as both teams sit firmly out of the race for a Super Bowl title.

The 2-8 Jets have lost five straight games, including their last two bouts by 19 and 23 points, after a 2-3 start. Starting quarterback Geno Smith is also dealing with an ankle injury, though he expects to play Sunday, he told ESPN.

There is an unusual amount of optimism surrounding head coach Jim Harbaugh's team after they upset the Baltimore Ravens on the road Monday night — finally breaking speculation that they could go on to be the NFL's first winless team over a full season since the 2017 Cleveland Browns after starting 0-8.

Fans have booed the team's horrific start, which comes in part due to paltry quarterback play from starter Justin Herbert. Herbert has thrown just six touchdowns in nine games with 11 picks. No matter what happens in the second half of the season, the Chargers will underperform their 2025 record of 11-6.

The Los Angeles Rams, who share the stadium with the Chargers, have seen substantially more success on the field. The 6-4 Rams are just a game out of a Wild Card spot with the help of a lockdown secondary and veteran quarterback Matthew Stafford.

Therefore, their ticket prices are much, much more expensive. Two standing-room-only tickets for their Thanksgiving Eve game against the Green Bay Packers cost more than $500 total on TickPick.

The Chargers playing against a fellow AFC bottom-dweller in the Jets has opened the matchup to plenty of mockery.

"If there was ever a game that both teams could lose, it's this one. Unfortunately, I am being dragged to the game against my will and could witness history," a football fan wrote on X.

And there could be at least one fan who paid more for his lunch than to watch the lowly teams spar.
