Andy Reid: It looks like Josh Downs, Patrick Mahomes have a little connection going

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The early returns from the Chiefs’ trade for receiver Josh Downs were undoubtedly positive, as the wideout caught four passes on five targets for 53 yards with an 18-yard touchdown.

Downs was on the field for just 11 of Kansas City’s 65 offensive snaps. But he clearly made the most of them.

“I thought Josh — for only being here for two, three days, we’d have him narrowed down to somewhere below 15-20 reps,” Reid said. “I thought, could he have had a few more? Yes. But, for what we asked him to do, he sure handled it well.

“It looks like Pat [Mahomes] and he have a little connection thing going there, so that’s something we can build on.”

Downs’ touchdown came during a two-minute drive in the second quarter, showing the trust the club had already developed in the receiver.

“He said he felt comfortable with it, so we were able to put him in there and let him go,” Reid said. “And again, that’s a narrow package, there’s not a whole lot in that package to do. But he’s a smart kid — I’m stating the obvious here, just from our standpoint, that he was able to pick, really, the whole game plan up and handle it.

“Those are things you don’t know until you get him here, though. … You might hear it, but once you get him here, you get the reality of it.”

The Chiefs will be back home in Week 11 to face the Cardinals. We’ll see if and by how much Downs’ production increases with more time in the building.
