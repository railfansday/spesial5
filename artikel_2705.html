The Commanders brief blackout is a great omen for the Bengals (and fans had jokes)

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A blackout is a good omen for an AFC North team. Take the Baltimore Ravens, for example. The lights went out at the Mercedes-Benz Superdome at Super Bowl XLVII, stemming a San Francisco 49ers rally and allowing Ray Lewis to retire with a second world championship under his belt.

Thus, the sudden lack of lights at Northwest Stadium for Monday night's game between the Cincinnati Bengals and Washington Commanders was a good thing for Joe Burrow -- even if it gave the home team a little extra time to plan before a key third-and-14 inside the Bengals' red zone. The two teams had played just over two quarters of football on a rainy night in Maryland when, all of the sudden, the main stadium lights went out leaving only the LED boards that ring the club levels to illuminate the field below.

Unlike Super Bowl XLVII, the delay was brief. The broadcast team of Joe Buck and Troy Aikman relayed word it was a power issue across the area and not a glitch limited to the stadium. After a few minutes of extended commercial breaks, the lights flickered back on and the Commanders were free to dial up a third down incompletion before tying the game 10-all with a field goal.

Of course, folks on the internet had jokes to get us through that harrowing seven minutes without football.

As the prophecy foretold, the Bengals went on a 66-yard touchdown drive soon after to take a 17-10 lead.
