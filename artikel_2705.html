Jonah Coleman's Three TDs Lifts Broncos to 38-24 Victory Vs Raiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Former Washington Huskies star running back Jonah Coleman doubled his career touchdown total and recorded his first 100-yard game in the NFL on Sunday to lead the Denver Broncos to a 38-24 win at home over the Las Vegas Raiders.

The 5-foot-8, 220-pound bruising runner finished with 111 yards on the ground and three rushing touchdowns on 21 carries, the third rookie in franchise history to boast a trio of scores and rush for 100-plus yards in a single game since Clinton Portis (2002 vs Kansas City Chiefs) and Mike Anderson (2000 vs New Orleans Saints).

Coleman's dominant Week 11 performance got started on the first play of the game as fourth-year Broncos' coach Sean Payton made a concerted effort to feed the 2026 fourth-round pick the ball early and often, both as a runner and pass catcher out of the backfield for third-year quarterback Bo Nix.

The Stockton, California native was directly involved in three of Denver's first four plays and added four more touches of 5-plus yards on the Broncos' first touchdown drive early in the second quarter to spark an early two-possession advantage for the home team at Empower Field at Mile High Stadium.

Coleman plowed his way into the end zone for his fourth rushing touchdown of the season and first of the night against Las Vegas through a 1-yard run to give Denver a commanding 17-0 lead with 10 minutes, 35 seconds left in the first half.

After the Raiders responded with consecutive scoring drives of their own before halftime and added another score midway through the third quarter to tie the game at 17-17, it was Coleman twice more that got the Broncos back on top and allowed the team to run away with its eighth win of the season.

His second touchdown of the afternoon, a 4-yard run that capped off a 10-play, 72-yard drive just before the end of the third quarter, put Denver ahead 24-17 and gave Coleman his first multi-TD game of his budding professional career with still more to come.

On the Broncos' following possession with under 10 minutes remaining in the the fourth quarter Coleman used his perfect mix of patience, vision, and physicality to brush off Raiders rookie cornerback Jermod McCoy as he ran in for a 7-yard TD to mark his third score of the game, doubling the three rushing touchdowns the ex-Arizona and UW star running back had coming into the Week 11 contest over his first four games of the season.

It also gave Coleman an even 100 yards rushing against the Raiders, which smashed his prior career-high of 57 rushing yards set back on November 8 against the Carolina Panthers two weeks ago. Coleman nearly doubled his performance against the Panthers with 11 more yards on three carries over the final two Broncos possessions to end the game three yards shy of doubling his rushing yard total in the 26-21 road win.

Although it fell well short Anderson's' single-game rookie record of 251 rushing yards and four touchdowns in a 38-23 road win against the New Orleans Saints back in 2000, Coleman proved he can be the bell cow back for the team as he stakes his claim to be the NFL Offensive Rookie of the Year, an honor Anderson and Portis both earned in their first season with the franchise two decades ago in 2000 and 2002 respectively.

In addition to his dominance as a traditional running back, Coleman also tied his career-high with four receptions on five targets in the 12-point win over Las Vegas, his third consecutive game with at least four catches and a touchdown run, as he added 18 receiving yards to go with his 111 rushing yards for a grand total of 129 yards of offense.
