NFL Week 12 betting: 5 biggest storylines, including Thanksgiving week spreads

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Home-field advantage hadn't really shown itself often during this NFL season, but Week 11 corrected that.

Home teams were great in Week 11. Washington's win straight up and against the spread on Monday night meant home teams won 11 of 12 games (that doesn't count Sunday night's neutral-site game at Mexico City). The Chargers were the only home team to lose. Home teams went 9-3 against the spread as well. Before Week 11, home teams were just 72-78-1 according to Covers.com. After a big Week 11, home teams are now above .500 for the season.

Here are the five biggest betting storylines for Week 12 of the NFL season:

The Rams are favored over the Packers, but there's some skepticism about them baked into the line.

Los Angeles is a 2.5-point favorite for its Wednesday game against Green Bay. Oddsmakers didn't want to have the Rams laying a full field goal, and it's understandable. The Rams are without Puka Nacua and Davante Adams, which severely limits the passing game. The Packers also get edge rusher Micah Parsons, who tore his ACL last December, in the lineup for the first time this season. It's a huge game in the NFC wild-card race. Heading into the game, the Rams are -130 to miss the playoffs at BetMGM.

Matthew Stafford of the Los Angeles Rams last faced the Packers in 2024. (Photo by Sean M. Haffey/Getty Images)

The Chicago Bears are a hot team, especially in the betting market. It's rare to see an 8-2 team be an underdog at home, but that's the case for the Detroit Lions on Thanksgiving. The Bears are 2.5-point favorites for the first game on Thursday. That's not a lot of respect for the Lions.

That's a surprising contrast with the NFC North odds. The Lions are favored at most sportsbooks to take the division. That's mostly due to remaining strength of schedule. The Bears have the third-toughest remaining schedule, via DVOA. The Lions' remaining schedule is the 11th easiest. Still, if the Bears can win on Thursday, it would give them a nice one-game lead in the division with a leg up on clinching the head-to-head tiebreaker.

The Kansas City Chiefs aren't underdogs often, but oddsmakers had little choice this week. In the third Thanksgiving game, the Chiefs travel to face the Buffalo Bills. The Bills are one of the NFL's top two teams, along with the 49ers, and at 9-1 they had to be favored. And it's not a surprise that at most sportsbooks the Bills are full field-goal favorites at -3.

It is the third time the Chiefs have been underdogs this season. They were underdogs at the Seahawks and at the Broncos, and lost both games. They didn't cover the spread in either game.

For the record, the other Thanksgiving game has a spread of Dallas -8.5 vs. Philadelphia. Four of five good matchups for the weekday games isn't a bad rate.

Since the NFL introduced a game the day after Thanksgiving, they have mostly been throwaway matchups. This season it's between two teams that would be in the playoff field if the season ended now.

The Denver Broncos travel to face the Pittsburgh Steelers on Friday. The Broncos are 2.5-point road favorites, which is a reflection of how bad Pittsburgh has looked the last two weeks. They were blown out by the Bengals and lost to the Eagles on Sunday as a 3.5-point favorite.

While it could be a close game, oddsmakers don't expect it to be a high-scoring one. The total of 37.5 is the second-lowest of the week, with the Patriots-Chargers game at 35.5 coming in lower.

Week 11 had five double-digit spreads. And the big favorites covered in four of those five games, with the Bears' 21-13 win over the Saints being the only time a double-digit underdog covered.

This week there aren't so many big spreads. Jacksonville is a 14.5-point favorite against the Titans, the Bengals are 10-point favorites over the Saints and that's it for double-digit spreads. Eagles-Cowboys is the only other spread of more than 7.5 points. It's a better week of matchups around the NFL.
