Minnesota game week: David Braun presser notes

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Northwestern head coach David Braun took time Monday afternoon to speak on last week's matchup with No. 20 Ohio State and NU's upcoming game at Minnesota on Saturday Nov. 21 at 11:00 a.m. CST on FS1. Some of the quotes below have been edited for brevity or clarity:

Braun on the loss to Ohio State: "We're not okay with the result. There's no moral victories here."

Braun on preparation for the week: "Typically, we reserve our time to start the process for our next opponent until our guys come back in the afternoon. This morning, we took a quick evaluation of the Ohio State tape, took the things that we needed to learn from and immediately shifted our focus to Minnesota."

Braun on Minnesota: "Coach Fleck has done an outstanding job for a long time at Minnesota. He has built something that's really sustainable, really successful, really impressive. He has a really good football team this year with some really impressive wins along the way."

Braun on Minnesota's offense: "A quarterback that's really dangerous as a thrower. Two really talented backs. They built their offense as an O-line-centric offense, and they got some weapons out on the perimeter."

Braun on facing a new offensive coordinator: "It can be a unique challenge. Like we've talked about, I think it's remaining true to your process, and then being objective in terms of things that we can put together in terms of what adjustments we may see."

Braun on tight end Luke Dehnicke: "He's certainly very skilled. A commitment to learning the offense and not being someone that's gonna ease into this opportunity in the Big Ten. Just showing up and saying 'I'm going to put my best out there every single day of practice to prepare myself for the arena.'"

Braun on Jack Sadowsky V starting in place of Kobie McKinzie: "There were some opportunities these last couple weeks that felt like Kobie wasn't playing at his best, and Jack had been improving. As a program, when you truly talk about earned opportunities, Jack had earned an opportunity to get a chance to go out there in the arena and show what he could do."

Braun on quarterback Aidan Chiles: "Going into the Iowa game, it felt very day-to-day. This feels now a little bit more week-to-week, and after some more downloads this morning and into this afternoon, as we approach our walkthrough tonight, I think we'll have a lot more clarity."

Braun on preventing injuries: "The way that we're practicing right now is dramatically different than we did last year. I think it's much more mindful, much more strategic, much more dialed in. And we need to continue to understand [the question of] how do you build the guys' engine to the point that they can go compete and sustain that throughout an entire Big Ten schedule?"

Braun on letting players try to play through injury: "This game is inherently dangerous and has risk and requires warriors and gladiators to go play it week in and week out…is this young man potentially exposing his overall long-term health of his football career, and if those things are serious concerns, well then you can't put that guy back out there."

Braun on what he has seen from quarterback Nicco Marchiol: "It's him continuing to be more and more comfortable in the system. It's doing a great job with our protection, a great job with the depth of our routes and our timing, and then also an opportunity for Nicco to really trust it and let it rip."

Braun on Marchiol's completion to Joseph Himon II: "Really good protection. Ball doesn't come out on time, but Nicco does a good job of extending. Joe does a good job in scramble mechanics. It's so easy to look at the quarterback or it's so easy to look at the offensive line. It all interconnects. We did some good things on Saturday and there's room for improvement as well."

Braun on the first punt of the game: 'That first punt where they they leaked for a little bit of return, Niki's done a great job for us, but we need a better kick. If it's going to be that short of a kick, it needs more hang time. That is almost an impossible punt to cover. [But] Niki bounced back and did some really good things."

Braun on punt return unit: "What we saw in our punt return game is what we need to see showing up in our kickoff game. Gosh, there were two returns that were huge plays in the game when it was still still a one-score game that positioned us for success. Objectively, there's really good stuff on that unit."

Braun on Drew Wagner's dropped punt: "If I could select anyone in the country to be back in that situation [to win], I'm picking Drew Wagner every single time…Now we're sitting at 12 o'clock on Monday. That opinion hasn't changed. If there's one person in the country that I'd pick to put back there and catch that punt, it's still Drew Wagner. So I know Drew will bounce back."

Braun on state of the team: "Not only can we compete with anyone in the country, but we can beat anyone in the country, and we're also at at a point where you can objectively say that when the opportunities are out there, we need to capitalize on those opportunities."

Braun on Jeremiah Smith's incompletion that was overturned: "Yeah, they did get it right on the field. So credit to the officials on the field. There's been conversations and we'll continue those conversations. I'm really curious and I got a lot of questions around this, so I'll keep asking those questions."

Braun on Mason Mayne: "There's some flashes of like, 'oh yeah!' and then there's levels of consistency and continued development that's needed. But what I do know is that dude's going to show up every single day and give it his absolute all, going to be super dialed in. He's the type of young man that you expect between partnership between myself, staff and position coaches to continue to develop not only the player, but the man. A guy that I fully anticipate is going to be a captain on this football team someday."

Braun on the offensive line allowing five sacks: "You never want to give away your ownership on something by saying, 'Well, hey, they're just really good.' We got guys that are in there and battling and improving. We got an understanding that the last three defensive fronts that we faced are are a pretty talented group, and it's really easy to [determine that] when the number of sacks comes up. There's a lot of factors that go into [the offensive line] in terms of the ball coming on time, the backs involved in the protection, play calling, you can list it off, but what I will say is we got a coaching staff that's going to evaluate that every single week, make decisions that are based on us truly believing it's positioning us for success and winning football games."

Braun extending on Minnesota's offense: "You know that quarterback's a really good player. His backs are really good players. They've always been O-line centric, and they got weapons on the perimeter. That tangibly shows up very clearly on the tape from last year."

Braun on Nicco Marchiol's physicality and scoring his first touchdown as a Wildcat: "That dude is a competitor, he's a warrior. I know that can be over-utilized. I'm guilty of it. But some of those shots that he took, the roughing the passer flag that came out late in the game: unfazed. Stand up, get back in the huddle, go to work with your teammates. If you want a tangible piece of evidence of a guy that's laying it all out on the line for his teammates, all you got to do is look at Nicco."

Braun on improvement for Northwestern beyond this season: "Everything that we evaluate and assess, does it align with winning a Big Ten championship? And if the answer is yes, well, there's still room for improvement. So let's continue to look for those improvements. But if the answer is no, it's my job to address it and find the solution. And that that's a 365-day-a-year responsibility that comes with this role that I'm proud to hold, but constantly evaluating those things."
