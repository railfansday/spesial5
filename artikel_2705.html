Deion Sanders shrugs at talk of being on the hot seat in Colorado

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

As he concludes his fourth season at Colorado, there are real questions as to whether coach Deion Sanders will have a fifth.

“Do you think that bothers me?” Sanders said at a Tuesday press conference, via the Associated Press. “Do you think that causes me to lose sleep or rest? Do you think that affects or impacts my life?

“That don’t affect me like it may affect you or may affect some others. Because I’m in the third quarter of life. I may be losing games, but I’m winning life.”

He’d win again, even if he’s fired. Colorado would owe him a buyout of $25.5 million. And he’d then be available to coach elsewhere.

Sanders has a record of 20-28 at Colorado. The Buffaloes have qualified for a bowl in one of his four seasons. The 2026 team fell to 4-7 on Saturday with a loss at Cincinnati. The season concludes on Saturday with a home game against UCF.
