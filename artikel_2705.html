Pistons vs. Spurs Discussion: Game Time, TV, Odds, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

If ever a night felt like David vs. Goliath, this is it. Detroit Pistons fans love being the underdog, and so do many storied franchise players who have a chip on their shoulder. When we're counted out, we perform best. But sometimes the odds are against you, and you simply have to say, in the words of one of my favorite pieces of human writing ever, "The haters said I couldn't do it. And they were correct. Honestly great call from the haters."

Jalen Duren remains out. The big man's absence has been sorely felt in every Pistons game this season, and that includes when Detroit is going up against the likes of Bam Adebayo and Walker Kessler. Now they face Victor Wembanyama. If you told me Wemby dropped 60 points tonight, I would not be shocked.

Detroit is also without Ron Holland and Duncan Robinson. And still without Isaiah Joe. It feels like it could be an ugly night. And yet! Maybe they will shock the world (including me) tonight.
