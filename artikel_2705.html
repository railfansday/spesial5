Bills-Chiefs injury tracker: Good news on both sides ahead of Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Week 12 is going to be a wild one. Weather alerts are already out there. Practice schedules are already skewed. And injury reports are just educated guesses at this point.

The Buffalo Bills will host the Kansas City Chiefs on Thanksgiving night, but as they prepare for a game that could determine when – and where – they will play in January, they haven't had a full practice this week. After no practice Monday, they held a walk-through practice today, which means injury reports are estimates. Good estimates, made by qualified medical staff, but estimates just the same.

With that in mind, we're going to look at who we think is trending toward playing versus who can probably safely eat an early Thanksgiving meal on Thursday.
