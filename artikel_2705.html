USA vs. Haiti: How to watch, stream Concacaf Nations League quarterfinal Leg 2

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Head coach Mauricio Pochettino's swan song arrives Tuesday when the United States host Haiti in the second leg of the 2026-27 Concacaf Nations League quarterfinals.

The USMNT head to TQL Stadium, home of FC Cincinnati, with a commanding 6-0 lead after dominating the opening leg at Inter Miami's Nu Stadium on Saturday.

The aggregate winner advances to the 2026-27 Concacaf Nations League Finals in March and qualifies directly for the 2027 Concacaf Gold Cup.

The USMNT flexed their muscles in their first competitive match since co-hosting the 2026 FIFA World Cup, cruising to a 6-0 victory over Haiti in the opening leg.

In Pochettino's penultimate match before managing storied English Premier League side Manchester United, the Yanks turned a one-goal halftime lead into an impressive rout with five second-half goals.

Red Bull New York forward Julian Hall continued his torrid pace with the USMNT, scoring his fourth goal in five appearances and his third goal in three starts.

Established veterans Christian Pulisic (brace), Weston McKennie, Folarin Balogun and Auston Trusty also found the back of the net.

Haiti's first Concacaf Nations League quarterfinal trip will be an unsuccessful one, barring a miraculous comeback in Cincinnati.

Les Grenadiers' best moment in Miami was fleeting, with Lenny Joseph's goal called back due to offside following Video Review.

Philadelphia Union midfielder Danley Jean Jacques, Sporting Kansas City product Kayden Pierre and ex-FC Dallas forward Louicius Deedson were all in head coach David Badía Cequier's starting lineup.

Meanwhile, Atlanta United veteran Fafà Picault came on as a substitute.
