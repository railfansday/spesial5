Report – Juventus Already Considering French Star’s Exit as Vlahovic Comparison Raises Questions

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Juventus are once again facing questions over their attacking recruitment, with Randal Kolo Muani struggling to justify the investment that brought him to Turin last summer. According to Gazzetta dello Sport, his future at the Allianz Stadium is already hanging by a thread.

The Frenchman has yet to establish himself as a reliable goalscoring option, while comparisons with former Bianconeri striker Dusan Vlahovic have done little to improve his standing.

Kolo Muani arrived from Paris Saint-Germain in a deal worth €38 million, potentially rising to €50m with bonuses. Juventus viewed the 27-year-old as the ideal candidate to lead their attack, handing him the iconic number nine shirt previously worn by Vlahovic.

However, Kolo Muani's performances have fallen short of expectations, raising doubts over whether the club made the right decision. Meanwhile, the statistical comparison with the Serbian is particularly interesting.

Vlahovic endured an inconsistent spell towards the end of his Juventus career, but his goalscoring numbers still offered a level of productivity that Kolo Muani has struggled to replicate. The Frenchman has also failed to consistently influence games through his movement, link-up play, and ability to create opportunities for teammates.

Luciano Spalletti now faces the challenge of getting more out of an attacking department that has struggled to deliver consistently. Juventus have already seen their investment in center-forwards come under scrutiny, with the lack of goals placing additional pressure on the rest of the squad.

Unless Kolo Muani rediscovers his scoring touch, questions over Juventus' decision to prioritise him over Vlahovic are unlikely to disappear anytime soon. Furthermore, there's a chance he could leave Turin already at the end of this season.
