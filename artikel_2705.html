Brian Schottenheimer: Christian Parker still has a bright future with the Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Brian Schottenheimer confirmed Mike Pettine will take over as the Cowboys’ defensive play-caller on Sunday. He replaces defensive coordinator Christian Parker, who will retain his title.

“Christian Parker is a huge part of what we’re trying to build here and that he is our defensive coordinator,” Schottenheimer said Wednesday, via Todd Archer of ESPN. “He is our leader. He is not changing any of those things, but I do feel that in the best interest of winning that Pet’s calling the game is going to help us get to where we want to get to.”

Schottenheimer said Parker would continue running meetings and leading walkthroughs, but the Cowboys needed “a different perspective” on game day.

Parker, 34, arrived in January as a first-time play caller, leaving Philadelphia after two seasons as the Eagles’ defensive passing game coordinator. The Cowboys, though, rank last in yards (393.8), last in run defense (154.1) and last in third-down defense (48.8 percent). They are 30th in scoring (28.2).

Pettine arrived before Week 5 as a senior defensive assistant to help Parker, whom he brought into the NFL with the Packers in 2019. He was defensive coordinator with the Jets, when Schottenheimer was on staff, and also served in that role with the Bills and Packers.

Pettine was head coach of the Browns from 2014-15.

“When I look at this situation, we’re all committed to trying to get it right,” Schottenheimer said, “and so we’re making adjustments like we’ve made. We’ve made adjustments. We’ve adjusted some scheme. We’ve adjusted some personnel. We’ve adjusted some play time. This is just another adjustment where we’re trying to get right.”

Schottenheimer made clear several times that he still believes in Parker and still believes Parker has a future with the Cowboys.

“CP has a very, very bright future as a coach, but more specifically, a very bright future here as our defense coordinator,” Schottenheimer said.
