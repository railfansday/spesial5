There are tons of unique and collectible weapons in GTA 6 but these are the best and easiest to snap up early in the game.

GTA 6 has a ton of unique weapon variants all with their own names and mods. They're all special custom builds of stock guns and weapons with unique skins, but some of them are way more worth the trouble to hunt down than others. You'll find some of them easily—every Ammu-Nation has a unique gun sitting out for you to buy. The best unique weapons are a little more hidden. There are treasure hunts and easter eggs and a whole lot that you'll grab by fighting your way through a gang hideout.

The Assassin Nipper is at the south tip of the eastern island in the Permit Keys.

Use Focus Mode to find the hidden cache for the Assassin Nipper .38

This is the easiest unique weapon to grab right at the start of the game and totally worth the boat ride to grab it. There isn't even any combat to get to it, like many of these other uniques. The Assassin Nipper comes with a suppressor attachment, which is a really expensive mod to buy early in the game. Pick it up as soon as you've got free range in Key Lento from an old shipwreck in the Permit Keys near the military base location.

Another great Chapter 1 snag is this sniper rifle at a Kingfisher hideout. The combat to get it is minimal (don't get between that tiger and its prey) so it's easy to pick up early. It's a bolt action that can't equip a silencer, so it isn't exactly a stealth tool, but it can help Jason pick off gangsters in a hideout before even going inside. It comes with a stock and a basic dot sight.

A variant of the Vom Feur V5FC, found in Port Gellhorn at a Kolt gang hideout. It's up on the top floor of the motel by the La Cascara Motel safe, so you pretty much have to complete this illegal operation to grab it. I recommend coming up the south stairwell that's less guarded and hugging the outer walkways to get most of the way to the loot room by stealth. It's worth it though. The Scrapper is a silenced longarm SMG fairly early in the game with an extended mag and a flashy paint job.

This Duke Carbine variant doesn't have a suppressor but it is quite high accuracy for a full auto assault rifle with a heavy stock and 7" pistol barrel mod. The Kolt 556 is easier to smash and grab than the Scrapper, though. You've got to fight in and out of the Kolt stash house, but it's a small joint so it's not too hard to get away clean. It's in a gun case on a kitchen table near the safe full of pills.
