Apple Calendar got two feature upgrades it really needed in iOS 27

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Apple Calendar keeps getting better, including some design updates coming in iOS 27.3. But there are two standout feature upgrades added recently in iOS 27 that Apple’s Calendar app really needed.

I’ve used Apple’s Calendar app for years, and there’s an elegant simplicity to it. But one key area the app has long lagged behind competitors is event creation.

When you need to create a new event, the Calendar app historically has required manually filling in various fields for the title, date, time, invitees, etc. By contrast, the excellent third-party app Fantastical lets you type in natural language and it automatically fills in relevant parameters based on what you type.

In iOS 27, Apple finally introduces its own natural language input for Calendar events. You can type out your event details and certain recognized parameters will be used to automatically fill in the detail fields.

While the feature still isn’t on par with what Fantastical offers, since it doesn’t support as many data points as the third-party alternative, it’s very much a step in the right direction.
