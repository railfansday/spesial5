American Healthcare REIT, Inc. (AHR +3.34%) reported a transition into what management termed Phase 2 of its public lifespan, emphasizing platform enhancements and data-driven operational support for its regional partners. The company completed 2.1 billion in investments year to date, specifically targeting newer vintage senior housing assets in infill markets with high barriers to entry. Management increased its full-year 2026 earnings guidance based on sequential occupancy gains and expanded operating margins within its primary senior housing segments. The company also improved its leverage profile, reporting a net debt to annualized adjusted EBITDA ratio of 1.9x as it settled 1.88 billion in forward equity agreements during the quarter.
