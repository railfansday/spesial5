Burger King is bringing back its miniature restaurant advent calendar for a third year

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The 12-door calendar costs $19.54, with 110 available each day from Nov. 16 through Nov. 20 on BKAdventCalendar.com

Burger King is bringing back its advent calendar for a third year, with 12 miniature pieces that assemble into a model Burger King restaurant, the company said. The 2026 calendar will be available starting Nov. 16 at $19.54, a figure that nods to the chain's founding year.

Each of the calendar’s 12 doors holds a miniature piece, the company said. The items include a Whopper in its new box packaging, nuggets, the King’s Booth, a soda fountain, a reindeer and a holiday tree. On the 12th day, the pieces fit together as a finished tiny Burger King restaurant. The final door also yields a pint-sized $5 digital coupon that Royal Perks members can redeem.

“We know Guests will be excited that for the third year in a row, the Advent Calendar is back,” Joel Yashinsky, chief marketing officer of Burger King U.S. and Canada, said in a statement.

Burger King will make 110 calendars available at noon ET on each of five days, from Monday, Nov. 16, to Friday, Nov. 20, or until inventory runs out, the company said. BKAdventCalendar.com is the sole sales channel. That schedule totals 550 calendars nationwide, according to Attractions Magazine.

Each order, household, address, name and email address is limited to one calendar, the company said. Shipping is free and standard to the 48 contiguous states and Washington, D.C., excluding Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories and P.O. boxes. Calendars are scheduled to ship the week of Nov. 30.

Burger King said each of its past two calendars was gone within five minutes of going on sale. The 2025 version came stocked with an inflatable chair, holiday ornaments and plush toys, according to Allrecipes.
