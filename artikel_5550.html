The acting director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases, John Powers III, will become the permanent director, the agency announced Monday.

NIAID has been without a permanent director since March 2025, when the Trump administration ousted Jeanne Marrazzo. Powers has been serving in an acting capacity since June. A physician-scientist, Powers has worked with NIAID since 1999 in several roles focused on clinical research.

The second Trump administration has brought a renewed spotlight to the infectious disease institute, in large part because of the president’s ire for former director Anthony Fauci, and because of unsubstantiated accusations that research funded by an institute in China played a role in creating the SARS-CoV-2 virus. It has also seen tumult in its leadership. After Marrazzo was ousted, Jeffery Taubenberger was appointed acting director. He unexpectedly stepped down, and several other leaders at NIAID were reassigned to other institutes or asked to resign.
