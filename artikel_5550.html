Inc.-Recognized LuggageToShip.com Helps Travelers Ship Luggage, Sports Equipment and Boxes Worldwide

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Award-winning AI-powered platform simplifies holiday travel, ski season, study abroad and relocations across 220+ countries

NEW YORK, Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- LuggageToShip.com, an AI-enabled TravelTech platform that helps customers compare shipping rates and arrange delivery for luggage, boxes, sports equipment, and personal belongings, today announced it has been named a 2026 Inc. Power Partner Award winner in AI, Technology & Development.

The recognition highlights the company's use of artificial intelligence, automation, and digital logistics technology to simplify shipping and help travelers, students, families, and businesses compare available carrier services through one connected platform. LuggageToShip.com appears on Inc.'s 2026 AI, Technology & Development list alongside recognized technology companies including Canva and Intuit.

"Travelers already compare flights, hotels, and rental cars before they book. We believe shipping luggage should be just as easy," said Mike Ulker, CEO of LuggageToShip.com. "Customers can compare carrier options, book online, track shipments, and travel lighter during some of the busiest travel periods of the year."

Meeting Growing Demand for Travel and Mobility

As holiday travel, ski season, study-abroad programs, relocations, and long-term assignments continue to increase, more consumers are shipping belongings ahead of their trip rather than carrying oversized luggage through airports.

Depending on carrier and route availability, customers may arrange door-to-door service or use convenient carrier drop-off locations across more than 220 countries and territories.

Built for Students, Travelers and Businesses

The platform supports recurring use cases across travel, education, relocation, and business.

Students can coordinate college moves and semester transitions. Families can ship luggage ahead of holiday travel. Skiers and snowboarders can arrange transportation of winter sports equipment to mountain destinations. Businesses can coordinate shipments for meetings, conferences, events, and trade shows.

Customers can compare available services through carrier networks including DHL, FedEx, UPS, USPS, and Aramex where supported.

LuggageToShip.com combines online quotes, multi-carrier comparisons, booking, payment processing, customs documentation, shipment tracking, and customer communication into one experience.

Powered by PayToMe.co, the platform supports Text-to-Pay checkout, automation-assisted booking workflows, shipment alerts, customs documentation, and customer service operations.

The company reports serving more than 100,000 customers worldwide and maintains a 4.7 Trustpilot rating based on thousands of reviews.

Beyond consumer shipping, LuggageToShip.com supports travel agencies, hotels, resorts, universities, study-abroad programs, relocation providers, corporate mobility teams, conference organizers, and logistics partners. The platform's integration and white-label capabilities help organizations add shipping, storage, and customer-service solutions to their own customer experience.
