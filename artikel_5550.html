Kase Celebrates Terra Kaffe's Selection for Oprah's Favorite Things 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Longstanding partnership helps premium coffee brand scale fulfillment operations and prepare for increased holiday demand

FONTANA, Calif., Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Kase, a national third-party logistics (3PL) provider, is celebrating a major milestone for customer Terra Kaffe, whose Demi espresso machine has been selected for Oprah's Favorite Things 2026. As Oprah says on oprahdaily.com, "I added this to my kitchenâmine's the Dune color. It grinds whole beans to make an Americano, drip coffee, and more, so everyone in the house gets what they like best."

The selection marks a significant achievement for Terra Kaffe and reflects the brand's growth in the home espresso market. "We knew we needed to partner with an organization that valued customer experience as much as we do. We've absolutely found that in the Kase team," said Cate Marques, Chief Experience Officer at Terra Kaffe. "Kase has helped our organization grow in a way that's truly scalable, which has been essential as our customer base has grown exponentially over the years."

Today, Kase supports Terra Kaffe's omnichannel fulfillment operations from its Sunnyvale, Texas facility, handling deluxe coffee equipment, accessories, and retail orders. The partnership also includes specialized services such as protective return packaging.

"For Terra Kaffe, ensuring we can trust our warehouse partner is paramount, as our customers are trusting us to deliver their high-value orders efficiently and safely," said Marques. "We know the Kase team has it covered on the fulfillment and shipping side, and cares just as much about our customers getting an unparalleled experience as we do."

From January through September 2026, Kase exceeded target service-level agreements (SLAs) for order accuracy and damage-free shipments for Terra Kaffe. "We're thrilled that even icons like Oprah are recognizing Terra Kaffe's quality and craftsmanship," said Peter Davis, President of Kase. "We're proud to play a role in delivering the experience their customers expect."

As Terra Kaffe enters the holiday shopping season, its fulfillment partnership with Kase will continue to support the brand. "We're ready for this milestone, and all it entails, thanks in no small part to having the Kase team in our corner, ready to support us through this massive growth moment," said Marques.
