Once Upon a Farm is cutting its outlook after a toddler snack bar recall

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The toddler snack maker lowered its full-year net sales forecast to $290 million-$300 million from $327 million-$335 million

Once Upon a Farm lowered its full-year 2026 outlook on Thursday to reflect the voluntary recall of its Tractor Wheels product lines. The company expects net sales of $290 million to $300 million, down from the $327 million to $335 million it had forecast.

The company expects an adjusted EBITDA loss of $4.5 million to $7 million, compared with prior guidance of adjusted EBITDA of $3 million to $4.5 million. Adjusted EBITDA is earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, with additional adjustments. The company said the recall's effect on the fourth quarter drove the update.

For the third quarter, net sales rose 34.9% to $89.1 million from $66.0 million a year earlier. Net loss narrowed to $3.8 million from $11.3 million. Adjusted EBITDA was a loss of $1.0 million, compared with a profit of $0.9 million a year earlier.

Gross margin fell to 36.1% from 42.1%. The company cited trade investment for a national club program, product mix and cooler placement costs. As of Sept. 30, it held $106.4 million in cash and cash equivalents and no debt.

"We are committed to resolving the Tractor Wheels situation correctly, continuing to earn the trust of families and retailers, and executing against the significant growth opportunity ahead of us," CEO and co-founder John Foraker said in a statement.

Foraker told Bloomberg the company anticipates as much as $30 million in recall expenses, spanning written-off inventory, legal fees and product returns. For 2027, Foraker expects the company to reach breakeven or better on an adjusted EBITDA basis, with net sales no lower than $330 million, according to Bloomberg.

The company plans to change the bars' recipe and packaging before bringing them back to store shelves, Foraker told Bloomberg. It has brought in outside specialists in food safety to review the product, according to Bloomberg.

Once Upon a Farm announced the voluntary recall of its Tractor Wheels soft-baked bars on Oct. 30, citing three consumer complaints about a choking hazard. The bars are labeled for toddlers ages 1.5 to 2.5, and the recall covers all lots across three varieties.

Tractor Wheels accounted for about 18% of the company's revenue this year as of the earlier coverage. Once Upon a Farm stock has lost over 40% of its value since it began trading in February.
