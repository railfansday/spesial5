Amazons cute new Echo Time smart clock is 50% off for Prime members, but not for long

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Updated on Nov. 16 at 2:25 p.m. ET — For a limited time, Prime members can get the new Amazon Echo Time smart clock for 50% off. Part of Amazon's early Black Friday sale, this discount makes the smart gadget a lot easier to recommend. Through Nov. 17, Prime members (and only Prime members) can take advantage of an on-page coupon that drops the price to $99.99. On the product page, look for a checkbox that reads, "Exclusive: Get 50% off."

The Echo Time was just released on Oct. 28 alongside a host of new Echo devices, which are getting their first discounts for early Black Friday shoppers.

Keep scrolling to read our original story on the Amazon Echo Time.

Having good taste is expensive. Alas, it has always been thus.

But $200 for a clock is pushing it, even for a snob like me.

That's unfortunate, because I love the design of Amazon's new Echo Time smart alarm.

Earlier this week, Amazon launched a bunch of new Echo smart home devices, and Mashable was there for the unveiling. The new Echo devices have less of a Death Star vibe, and the Echo Time stands out to me for its friendly design. It's exactly the kind of product I can imagine installing on my nightstand.

I really do love the design of this smart alarm. It doesn't have quite the same appeal as the new Apple HomeView for me (I'm not a huge fan of the knitted fabric cover on Amazon smart devices), but the round shape and pleasant, customizable clock faces work extremely well together. I even like the optional stand.

Why does everything have to be so expensive right now? Whether you're booking a hotel, ordering food, buying a phone, or looking for an alarm clock to break your morning phone habits, prices are painful. Amazon's last smart alarm, the Echo Spot, was priced at a much more reasonable $79.99. That is, until Amazon raised prices on the Echo Spot to $109.99 in August. The Echo Time isn't even the only pricey alarm clock I'm lusting after in 2026. Stylish, Instagram-famous analog alarms like the Nanu Arc Alarm Clock cost even more, at $299.

The Echo Time does work really hard to justify the price tag. And, for the record, I really wouldn't be mad about getting this as a Christmas gift this year. I just don't know that I can justify the money myself.

Billed as "your Alexa smart clock," the Echo Time has hundreds of clock faces for its 3.2-inch circular touchscreen, and with the AI-powered Alexa+, you can even use it to control smart lights, set the perfect sleep temperature, or open your blinds. The Echo Time also doubles as a noise machine, playing soothing sounds as you fall asleep at night or meditate in the morning. Amazon says its built-in speaker offers lossless high-definition audio for listening to music.

I've long since learned that having good taste is expensive, but in 2026, having any kind of taste is expensive. This, despite the fact that President Donald Trump promised to bring down prices at the pump, grocery stores, and beyond, telling crowds, "Starting on day one, we will end inflation and make America affordable again." Obviously, that's not what happened. Tech companies, in particular, keep raising prices as they pass along the higher costs of the memory squeeze spurred by the AI industry.

So, you'll have to decide if your budget can bear a $199.99 smart alarm.

My verdict: Wait for Black Friday and see how much the Echo Time is going to cost when it inevitably goes on sale.
