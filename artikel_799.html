Inside Kendall Jenner's Horse-Themed 31st Birthday Celebration

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Kendall Jenner, an avid horse rider, paid homage to her favorite animal during her low-key 31st birthday celebration.

Kendall Jenner’s 31st birthday celebration is the most exciting to look at.

That’s because the supermodel’s special day revolved around her one true love—and we’re not talking about Jacob Elordi. Rather, the low-key soirée paid homage to her close connection to horses.

In one photo shared to her Instagram Nov. 6, Kendall showed off a butter yellow birthday cake that featured a design of the animal made of white frosting. Meanwhile, 31 lit birthday candles framed the oval dessert.

The Kardashians star also gave fans a look at her birthday get-up, which included a dramatic fur-lined robe and matching silk top underneath. And it’s clear Kenny wasn’t horsing around when it came to refreshments on her big day as she held onto an empty wine glass while focused on the restaurant’s menu.

Though the reality TV star also shared a glimpse into the various floral arrangements she received for her big day, it’s clear all she really wanted to do was keep up with her favorite hobby as she included a snap of herself riding a brown horse on the beach at sunset.

Though Kendall, whose birthday is Nov. 3, didn’t share who she celebrated with, her family took to social media to show her some birthday love. And you can bet her mom Kris Jenner doted on her second-youngest, sweetie.

“I am so proud of everything you’ve accomplished, but even more proud of the amazing woman you are and the life you’re creating for yourself,” Kris wrote on Instagram Nov. 3. “You are kind, passionate, patient, funny, loving, smart, creative, gentle, loyal, and you give 100% of yourself to everything you do and to everyone you love. We are all so blessed to be loved by you, my sweet girl.”

Meanwhile, Kylie Jenner also dedicated a sweet post to her sister, writing, “I am who I am because of who you’ve been to me.”

And while Khloe Kardashian wrote on Instagram that she hopes “this next year surprises you in the most wonderful ways,” one aspect of the Keeping Up With the Kardashians alum’s life is sure to stay the same—her passion for horses.

“I think I've spent many lifetimes with horses,” she said on Jay Shetty’s podcast in September. “I was always around horses, and I just was deeply in love with them, and I have no explanation, and that's why I say I feel like in past lives I have just been around horses.

Indeed, she's certain her special bond with the animal runs deep. As she put it, “I think I've spent many lifetimes with horses.”
