Foo Fighters Announce 2027 U.K. & European Summer Stadium Tour With Support From QOTSA, Iggy Pop & More

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Foos are extending their Take Cover dates into Europe next summer

Foo Fighters announced a run of U.K. stadium headline performances as part of a European tour in 2027 on Monday (Nov. 16).

The rockers will headline 15 dates across the region, kicking off in Reykjavík, Iceland on June 9. In the U.K., they’ll perform two shows at London’s Wembley Stadium (Sept. 9, 11), alongside dates at The Darlington Arena in Country Durham (Sept. 14), and Edinburgh’s Murrayfield Stadium (Sept. 16). They’ll close the tour with a date at Croke Park in Dublin, Ireland on Sept. 18.

Elsewhere they’ll head to a number of major European cities: Helsinki, Finland; Copenhagen, Denmark; Dusseldorf, Germany; Sofia, Bulgaria; and more dates across the continents. On a number of European dates, they’ll be supported by The Hives, Iggy Pop, Slowdive, Architects and more. See the full run below. At the U.K. shows they’ll be joined by Garbage (Sept. 9, 16), St. Vincent (Sept. 11, 14), Yard Act (Sept. 14), Florence Road (Sept. 11), Reclus.e (Sept. 9), and The Vaselines (Sept. 16).

Tickets for the U.K. dates will go on sale this Friday (Nov. 20) at 10 a.m. GMT from Gigs and Tours and Ticketmaster.

Foo Fighters last performed in the U.K. earlier this summer with a two-night stand at Liverpool’s Anfield Stadium back in June. The band is currently on the Australian leg of its Take Cover tour, performing at Melbourne’s Marvel Stadium on Saturday night (Nov. 14).

The band released their 12th studio album Your Favorite Toy in April, which landed at No. 23 on the Billboard 200 and No. 2 on the U.K.’s Official Albums Chart.
