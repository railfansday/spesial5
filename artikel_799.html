Jessica Simpson Will Take Your Breath Away With Hair Transformation

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Jessica Simpson showed off her darkest hair in years as she debuted a new brunette hair transformation on Instagram Nov. 21: "She says brunettes have fun and then some."

Jessica Simpson is rocking an irresistible new look.

The “With You” singer debuted her darkest hair color in years, trading her signature blonde locks for a rich chocolate brown.

Simpson revealed her new ‘do by resharing a photo with hairstylist Ken Paves to her Instagram Stories Nov. 20.

“They say blondes have more fun,” Paves wrote over the shot. “She says brunettes have fun and then some.”

The photo featured Simpson, 46, riding in a car with Paves as she posed for the camera, with her long brown hair styled in loose waves.

Although “Take My Breath Away” artist’s latest hair color marks a major transformation as she’s known for her honey blonde looks, she’s been dabbling with darker hues throughout the past year.

After all, Simpson—mom to Maxwell, 14, Ace, 13, and Birdie, 7, with ex Eric Johnson—kicked off 2026 by rocking what her colorist Rita Hazan described as “hot chocolate brunette,” a light, yet warm brown with blonde highlights.

“I didn’t want to go too dark at once, so we gradually increased the hue to ensure that we got the most perfect tone,” Hazan told People last December. “This shade is pretty dark for her but maybe we could go a little darker. Her hair color still has to be sexy and soft.”

A few months later, Simpson and her longtime colorist gave a closer look at the warm winter shade in social media photos, with Hazan sharing a shot of the Dukes of Hazzard actress showing off her side profile and her tossed waves.

Alongside the Feb. 9 Instagram photo, she wrote, “Hazelnut brown is my new favorite shade on Jessica.”

And Simpson’s transformation at the time was a perfect fit for her latest era in music after the March 2025 release of her two-part Nashville Canyon EP.

“I think her music is very moody,” Hazan told People. “It’s a full vibe, so the hair has to match: very deep and multi layered and dimensional with the color.”

As Simpson embraces change with her new brunette hair, she’s preparing to pull back the curtain into her personal life, with the fashion designer confirming Netflix will be producing a documentary about her career and influencer.

“It’s time to open a new chapter,” Simpson wrote in an Aug. 23 Instagram post alongside a shot of herself sitting by a camera crew. “@Netflix, 2027.”
