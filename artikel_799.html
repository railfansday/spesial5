Jenna Dewan Shares Her, Val Chmerkovskiy’s DWTS Plans Before Exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dancing With the Stars’ Jenna Dewan shed light on her and Val Chmerkovskiy’s planned performances prior to their elimination in an exclusive interview with E! News at the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala.

Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy were ready to take their dances a step up in the ballroom.

Nearly a week after her and her pro partner were eliminated on Dancing With the Stars, the Rookie actress shared insight into the “really great numbers” she and Chmerkovskiy’s had planned for the semi-finals and beyond.

“We were starting next week's routine, which was going to be a really beautiful foxtrot,” Dewan exclusively told E! News’ Erin Lim Rhodes at the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala presented by Paul Mitchell Nov. 14, “which I was really excited about, and a salsa.”

The Step Up star—mom to Everly, 13, with ex Channing Tatum as well as Callum, 6, and Rhiannon, 2, with fiancé Steve Kazee—added that the Latin dance “was going to be fierce and absolutely insane.”

But they weren’t going to stop there. As Dewan revealed, she and Chmerkovskiy were already also planning their freestyle routine for if they made it into the finals.

As the 45-year-old teased, the performance “was obviously going to be full out and fun.”

But Dewan shared that she’s open to finding a way for fans to get the chance to see the freestyle dance, pointing to how season 34 pairing Whitney Leavitt and Mark Ballas revealed their freestyle routine on the Call Her Daddy podcast after their elimination.

“What was beautiful about Whitney and Mark is they really paved the way,” Dewan shared. “Listen, I never say never. Maybe we can put it out there into the world in our own way you never know.

And although the mom of three is “sad” that her official time in the DWTS ballroom came to an end, she’s looking back at the experience with gratitude.

“I always keep coming back to the good of this whole journey, and I got to share it with my family, Steve and my kids, even Evie had never seen me dance live that she remembered.”

“So that experience was really beautiful to be able to end a dance and look out and see them happy and cheering for me and excited,” she continued, “and just to be in my mid-40s with my kids and be able to be like, ‘I can still do it and like there are so many good feelings that I take from it.”
