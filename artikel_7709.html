Attorney General Todd Blanche Honors Southern District of Florida Senior Litigation Counsel Christopher J. Clark at 2026 Director’s Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Senior Litigation Counsel (SLC) Christopher J. Clark of the U.S. Attorney’s Office for the Southern District of Florida was among 183 Department of Justice employees recognized by Attorney General Todd Blanche and Executive Office for U.S. Attorneys (EOUSA) Director Francey Hakes at the 2026 Director’s Awards Ceremony held Oct. 28 in the Great Hall of the Robert F. Kennedy Department of Justice Building in Washington, D.C. The awards recognize outstanding achievements by employees of the U.S. Attorney’s Offices.
