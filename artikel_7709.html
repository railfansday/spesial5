Kiwibit is dropping prices on three of its 4K smart bird feeders for Black Friday, just as cardinals, chickadees, and juncos settle in at backyard feeders for the winter. The Kiwibit Bird Feeder 2 Pro for $199.99 (was $259.99) leads the lineup, with phase-detection autofocus that keeps a bird sharp on the perch or farther back.

For families, check out the Kiwibit Bird Feeder 2 for $219.99 (was $289.99). It includes lifetime AI bird ID with no monthly fee, so every visitor gets identified on top of live view and visit alerts. When the sunniest spot isn’t the best viewing spot, consider the Kiwibit Bird Feeder 4K AI Camera With Solar Panel Kit for $129.99 (was $189.99). It puts the solar panel on its own mount. Kiwibit says these are its lowest prices of the year, and once the offers end on Dec. 27, the next chance at these prices won’t come until next year.

This 4K autofocus bird feeder camera records 1080p slow motion at up to 120 fps, slow enough to catch wingbeats. A 5 W solar roof charges the removable battery, and the 1.7 L dual hopper holds two kinds of seed.

This solar bird feeder camera includes lifetime AI bird ID, so species alerts come with no monthly fee. A 4.4 W solar roof, infrared night vision, and a 1.5 L dual hopper keep upkeep low.

The 4K bird feeder camera kit pairs the feeder with a separate 3 W adjustable solar panel, so the panel can sit in full sun while the feeder hangs closer to the house. It mounts on a wall, pole, or tree strap and records infrared video after dark.

All three feeders shoot 4K HDR video, run on a removable 5,200 mAh battery with solar charging, and carry an IP65 weather rating with an operating range down to -4°F. Live view, motion alerts, local recording, and family sharing do not require a membership.

AI species ID is the add-on: Kiwibit Premium currently runs $9.99 a month or $89.99 a year, while the Lifetime AI versions cost $50 to $60 more up front.
