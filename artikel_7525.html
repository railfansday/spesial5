In-house lawyers return to BigLaw as firms search for senior talent

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

BigLaw firms are increasingly hiring lawyers with in-house legal department experience. (Image from Shutterstock)

BigLaw firms, hunting for senior-level talent as they try to expand client relationships, are increasingly hiring lawyers with in-house legal department experience.

Over the past five years, Am Law 200 law firms hired about 740 lawyers from in-house positions. These in-house lawyers began their careers in firms, according to a story by Law.com.

Lawyers who have switched from firms to in-house and then back to firms are being calling “boomerangs” in the legal industry, according to a FirmProspects report this month.

Firms’ increasing investment in artificial intelligence also has them searching for lateral partners who have an expertise in AI or are open to increasing their understanding and use of it, according to Law.com.
