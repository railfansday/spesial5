Olympiacos 2-4 AC Milan: Hutchinson inspires remarkable comeback in Greece

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

AC Milan produced a stunning come-from-behind victory against Olympiacos, scoring four times unanswered to win 4-2.

It looked like being another miserable European night for Milan, as they fell two goals behind inside 50 minutes. The first was given as a Mike Maignan own-goal after Lorenzo Pirola’s header from a corner hit the crossbar then the goalkeeper, then Jota Silva doubled the advantage.

Out of nowhere, Milan mounted a comeback inside 15 second-half minutes. Omari Hutchinson inspired it, scoring a wonderful solo goal to halve the deficit, then setting up Alphadjo Cisse for the equaliser, and also providing the assist for Christian Pulisic’s goal off the bench.

Diego Moreira finished a counter-attack right at the end of the game to make it 4-2 and seal the result, one that was very much needed for Milan given their start in the competition. The play-off hopes have been dealt a big boost, while the top eight could still be a possibility too.

Amorim made four changes to the starting line-up from the team that beat Frosinone on Sunday. Two were enforced due to injuries, with Ardon Jashari replacing Luka Modric and Pervis Estupinan taking Davide Bartesaghi’s spot. Meanwhile, Alphadjo Cisse and Omari Hutchinson came into the attack.

The first shot of the game took less than two minutes to come and it provided an example of the intense pressing that the home side have become known for, though Freuler’s effort from distance was a speculative one that sailed comfortably over.

Milan began to string some nice moves together, and they had a couple of attempts of their own from just beyond the edge of the box miss the target. The first came from Cisse, who cut inside and curled one well over and wide, then Jashari concluded a promising attack with a wild shot.

There were two moments of panic in quick succession for Milan. The first was when a cross from the right evaded everyone and fell to Martins, though thankfully his touch let him down in a dangerous area. Then, Goncalo Ramos had a rough fall and seemed to jar his knee, but he got up and played on with a grimace.

The first shot on target fell to the home side and it came after Sankhoun Diawara lost possession inside his own half. Jota Silva picked up the ball and fed Pedersen, whose attempt was thankfully down the middle and caught by Mike Maignan.

Olympiacos continued to have the better of the scoring chances, as Martins began to find his range. Firstly he shot from distance but weakly and straight into Maignan’s arms, but then the Frenchman had to made a brilliant save at full-stretch to keep out the Portuguese’s strike.

The trend of tame efforts from around 20 yards out continued midway through the first half up at the other end. Ramos won an aerial duel and collected it to try and gallop in behind, eventually laying it off for Moreira who didn’t really test Ortega.

Right on the half-hour mark, the home side took the lead. A corner kick from the left was defended awfully in truth, with Pirola getting the better of Diawara in a central area to head off the crossbar, off Maignan and in from six yards out. Going behind in the Europa League is by this point an all-too-familiar feeling.

Milan were inches away from getting themselves level quickly as Pirola turned from the hero in one goalmouth to the hero at the other. A ball over the top found Cisse’s run in behind and Ortega came darting off his line but the ball squirmed through. The Italian somehow got a shot off from the floor that was rolling into the net but for Lirola’s last-ditch sliding clearance for a corner.

There was a flash point towards the end of the half which saw De Winter and Jota Silva both go into the box. A long ball over the top was eventually followed by play being blown dead for offside, yet the pair had a stand-up wrestling match for about 20 yards, until they both even ended up off the field.

In the first of two minutes added on at the end of the half, Pirola had a chance to actually get his name on the scoresheet after the opener was labelled an own-goal. Some dire defending down the right ended with a cut-back that the former Inter youth academy man fired over from inside the box.

Amorim chose to make two changes at the break, as the American duo Christian Pulisic and Yunus Musah came on. Ramos and Estupinan were the ones to make way, with Cisse going up front and Moreira switching over to left wing-back.

Within four minutes of the restart, Olympiacos doubled their lead after yet more calamitous defending. De Winter continued his trend of failing to strong together multiple games without errors as he played a stray pass out from the back, allowing Freuler to play though Jota Silva, who fired past Maignan first time.

De Winter then gave away a sloppy foul when it looked like he had brought a ball under his control and Amorim had seen enough, bringing Fikayo Tomori on for the Belgian.

Just as it looked like getting out of hand for Milan, Hutchinson scored a goal from nothing. Having seized possession inside his own half, the Englishman went on a brilliant solo run down the right where he was nearly clipped down twice, before jinking inside onto his preferred left foot and finding the bottom corner with a precise low shot.

Milan built on the goal that got them back into it, and six minutes later they drew themselves level. A sustained period of possession inside the Olympiacos half saw the ball worked nicely down the right side for Hutchinson to cross perfectly onto the head of Cisse at the far post, who headed in from a few yards out for his 10th of the campaign.

As if things couldn’t get any more dramatic, Milan completed the comeback as they made it 3-2 with 20 minutes left on the clock. Hutchinson was again the man who did all of the hard work, getting the ball on the touchline and sitting his marker down before driving to the right edge of the box and standing up a cross for Pulisic. The substitute struck his volley perfectly, nestling it inside the post.

Milan made their final two changes either side of the third goal, with Filippo Terracciano replacing the cautioned Diawara, then Christian Comotto coming on for Cisse.

It was the Omari Hutchinson show in the second half, and the on-loan Nottingham Forest man should have had yet another assist when he darted past his man to fire in a low cross that squirmed to Moreira but was skewed wide.

With just a couple of minutes left there were huge penalty shouts from the Greek side after a header struck Tomori, though replays showed it hit his shoulder. The Rossoneri broke from that corner and had a four-on-one, however Hutchinson stumbled when playing the final pass, and Pulisic sent Moreira’s square pass into the stands.

The finale to the game saw Milan finally kill it off. Olympiacos had a corner that produced a scramble inside the box, which eventually saw Hutchinson emerge with it and lead another counter. He found Pulisic down the left who slid it across for Moreira on the right, and the former Strasbourg man settle it well, cut inside and fired into the bottom corner for the final act of a dramatic night.
