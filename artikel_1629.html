Bayern Munich have some of the best attacking talent in the world.

Hary Kane has just won the Ballon d'Or, Michael Olise is one of the most sought-after players in the world and Luis Diaz has continued his brilliance since moving on from Merseyside.

The German giants have concerns over the long-term futures of Olise and Jamal Musiala however, and as a result are targeting a double swoop for Florian Wirtz and Rio Ngumoha.

That's according to an article from TEAMtalk, which states the German side are eying the Liverpool pair as well as Manchester City's Jeremy Doku as contingency planning begins for Olise and Musiala's potential exits.

The club's interest in both of the Liverpool pair extends back some way, with Bayern being heavily linked with both players in the past, but news of a potential move for both is new.

Any offer would have to be astronomical for Liverpool to consider the sale of either star, so the notion that they may sell both players to the same club is almost unthinkable.

There may have been a willingness to listen to offers for Wirtz in the past, but the German has turned a corner in the last six weeks and has a clear place in Iraola's plans.

Bayern may well try and sign the Liverpool duo, but it will likely be a firm no from Ward and the Anfield hierarchy.
