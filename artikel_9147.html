Frieze Los Angeles reveals 102 exhibitors for 2027 edition

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Frieze Los Angeles will return to the Santa Monica Airport in February 2027 Courtesy Casey Kelbaugh and Frieze

The eighth edition of Frieze Los Angeles will welcome 102 exhibitors under the sprawling white tent on the grounds of the Santa Monica Airport. The exhibitor list, as in previous years, includes heavy local representation—with more than one-third of participants operating a space in Los Angeles.

The roster includes established Los Angeles galleries such as Jeffrey Deitch, Regen Projects, David Kordansky, Château Shatto, Commonwealth and Council, Anat Ebgi, Night Gallery, the Pit and Roberts Projects. Four younger Los Angeles galleries will make their debuts in the Focus section for emerging galleries: Megan Mulrooney, Rajiv Menon Contemporary, House of Seiko and Zodiac Pictures. Erin Christovale from the Hammer Museum will curate this section.

International blue-chip galleries returning to Frieze Los Angeles include Gagosian, Hauser & Wirth, David Zwirner, Pace, White Cube, Perrotin, Gladstone, Almine Rech, Thaddaeus Ropac and Sprüth Magers. First-time exhibitors include Gallery Wendi Norris from San Francisco, Franklin Parrasch in New York, G Gallery in Seoul, Newchild in Antwerp, and Yehudi Hollander-Pappi and Danielian in Brazil.

A handful of prominent galleries will not be returning after taking part in recent editions, like Berggruen Gallery from San Francisco, Ortuzar and Tina Kim Gallery in New York, Mendes Wood DM and Nara Roesler from Brazil, Southern Guild from Cape Town and Timothy Taylor from London. Turnover year-over-year remains around 28% for a second consecutive year, amid stable overall exhibitor numbers.

Visitors to the fair will be greeted at the entrance by a new work by the Los Angeles-based artist rafa esparza called Queercancha, painted across two adobe panels mounted on chrome steel, drawing on Southern California’s culture of lowrider cruising.

Amid a boom following the onset of the Covid-19 pandemic, art dealers from all over the world began setting up shop in the Los Angeles area. However, many of those outposts have since closed alongside local dealerships—most recently, the Landing on Jefferson Boulevard. Along with a global market downturn, many local businesses were hurt by the devastating 2025 wildfires that nearly saw that year’s edition of Frieze Los Angeles cancelled. A handful of galleries have closed their local spaces and shifted their attentions to New York, including Southern Guild and Pietro Alexander.

This may be one of the final years the fair will be held at the Santa Monica Airport; the facility is scheduled to close permanently to aviation at the end of 2028 for conversion into a public park.
