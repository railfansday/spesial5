biote: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Data analytics now target lagging clinics, while medical training aims to keep practitioners engaged across the network.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 4, 2026, biote (BTMD -9.83%) announced full-year 2026 revenue guidance above $175 million despite a 9.6% year-over-year quarterly revenue decline to $43.3 million. Revenue contracted after a January 2026 voluntary product recall delayed new clinic onboarding and clinical procedure volume.

Three takeaways from the call stand out for long-term investors.

Management is deploying data analytics to identify clinics needing operational support and to enforce sales team accountability. Directing field representatives toward lagging practices aims to reduce clinic attrition and rebuild procedure volume.

Retaining healthcare providers requires ongoing clinical education beyond initial onboarding. Management is structuring science-based resources to address common clinical questions, aiming to cement long-term practitioner trust across its network.

Gross margin, the percentage of revenue left after direct production costs, dropped to 67.7% due to temporary reliance on third-party suppliers. Production at Xteria Health, the company's internal manufacturing facility, has recovered to pre-recall levels to supply over 50% of shipped pellets.

Procedure volume shows early signs of stabilizing across clinics. The company relies on internal manufacturing, expanded field representatives, and clinical education to rebuild procedure counts while tracking clinic retention and pricing discipline.

Long-term performance hinges on converting clinic relationships into consistent procedure volume across the network.
