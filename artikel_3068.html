Dolphin Entertainment, Inc. (DLPN -6.07%) reported record quarterly revenue for the third quarter of 2026, citing organic growth across its marketing and publicity agencies. Management stated that the company is transitioning toward a period of increased free cash flow as major office leases expire and acquisition-related debt matures over the next two years. The company indicated that its entertainment marketing segment is benefiting from cross-selling initiatives and a growing talent roster in its influencer marketing division. Additionally, management detailed plans for its content production segment, including the launch of its first creator-led feature film and a pipeline of four new ventures scheduled for the 2027 calendar year.
