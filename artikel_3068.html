Prediction: Rocket Lab Will Triple Its Revenue by 2028. Here’s What That Would Mean for the Stock.

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

What sounds outlandish on the surface actually makes a lot of sense once you dive deeper into the details of this space-launch company’s efforts.

By James Brumley – Nov 20, 2026 at 6:30PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Rocket Lab ›

Plenty of companies are growing incredibly well right now, including several from the artificial intelligence business. A company that's set to triple its top line over the course of the next couple of years, though? That seems outrageous.

Except for orbital launch and satellite tech specialist Rocket Lab (RKLB -10.92%), it isn't. This future business is already lined up. These paying customers are just waiting for one pivotal milestone that's likely to materialize sometime next year. Then the floodgates open.

Rocket Lab offers satellite launch solutions. Its relatively small Electron rocket design has now been launched 100 times, putting 273 satellites into low earth orbit.

The organization also provides a range of satellite components and construction services, which accounts for more than half of last quarter's revenue of $268 million. Rocket Lab expects to do around $980 billion in business in fiscal 2026, well up from last year's $602 billion.

This is just a taste of what's to come, though. As the company announced with its third-quarter report posted earlier this month, Rocket Lab's backlog grew 52% from Q2's $2.36 billion to an incredible $3.6 billion. The organization just needs more capacity, but that's coming. Rocket Lab can now manufacture an Electron rocket every eight days.

Then there's the proverbial 800-pound gorilla in the room. That's the company's larger Neutron rocket, which has seen its inaugural flight pushed back several times, with the first flight now expected sometime in 2027.

Despite the delays so far, once Neutron is flying, everything takes a turn for the better. As CFO Adam Spice commented during the third-quarter's earnings conference call, adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) "will turn positive within six months of that first successful test flight," with cash flow likely to turn positive shortly after that.

Neutron isn't the big profit center in and of itself, to be clear. It's just a means to an end. Being able to put larger, heavier satellites into orbit also opens up its satellite tech business. Of its new backlog of $3.6 billion, $1.7 billion of it reflects space systems rather than launch services, up 194% year over year.

To this end, while analysts aren't yet looking for Rocket Lab's revenue to triple between now and 2028, with recent failures of similar rockets like ULA's Vulcan and one earlier in the year from Blue Origin, Rocket Lab is quickly becoming a preferred launch option. It may well exceed 2028's expectations.

As for the impact on the stock, it's not a complicated prediction to make. If the company's top line comes anywhere close to tripling by 2028, the bullish argument firms up enormously.

While it may not triple in value in step with revenue, $3 billion in sales could turn this high-growth $34 billion outfit into a $60 billion outfit with relative ease. And given that the company isn't likely to raise money by issuing more stock anytime soon, that translates into a near-doubling of the stock's price.
