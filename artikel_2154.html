Bradford City TV decision announced and it's causing a reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Bradford City fans have been left extremely frustrated by a television broadcast decision impacting their FA Cup tie against non-league Wimborne Town next month.

The Bantams have endured a dramatic opening to the new League One season, with Michael Duff recently replacing Graham Alexander as the man in the Valley Parade hotseat.

Duff has only overseen one league victory in West Yorkshire so far, but his new employers are certainly going well in cup competitions, yet to lose in the EFL Trophy whilst also making the EFL Cup quarter-finals and reaching the FA Cup second round.

Bradford comfortably dispatched Marine 7-0 on Saturday afternoon, with said victory being rewarded with a high-profile tie against Wimborne, the lowest-ranked side now left in the competition, after the Dorset club defeated League Two side Crawley Town 2-0 on Sunday.

There are, of course, plenty of former Premier League sides competing in the FA Cup's early rounds, and the likelihood of them competing against non-league sides increases the chances of television coverage.

Eight ties were selected for broadcast in the first round proper, and the same number have been chosen again for the second round between December 4th and 7th.

All 24 League One clubs have been ranked in order of size.

Bradford are one of six League One sides who will see their ties televised, with their trip to the Wyatt Homes Stadium kicking off the weekend on Friday, December 4th, with coverage beginning on TNT Sports 1 at 7PM ahead of a 7:30PM kick-off.

Other televised ties include 2021 FA Cup winners, Leicester City's trip to Dover Athletic, which now kicks off at 5:15PM on Saturday, December 5th on BBC Two.

It is approximately five hours' worth of travel from the West Yorkshire city to the town of Wimborne Minster, meaning that, at the best of times, an arduous journey would be in store for the Bradford fans.

Crawley supporters received an allocation of 500 tickets in the 3,250-capacity ground last weekend, and a similar number will be expected to make the trip to support Duff's men.

However, whilst City fans are likely to sell out such a figure, the decision to now play the game on a Friday night just three weeks before Christmas hasn't gone down well at all, with one supporter saying: "Asking people to take a day off work to go do a 10 hour round trip to Winbourne is crazy. Deserves boosted loyalty points imo."

Another said of the decision: "Friday night on the south coast. Seeing as there will only be about 200 tickets it should reduce demand."

"Did we really expect anything else - well actually the way the schedulers don't give a damn about travel times I had a feeling it might have been Monday!," one displeased City fan wrote.

"Who makes these decisions," questioned another Bantams supporter.

Another verdict read: "Teed up perfectly for #bcafc to be on the receiving end of an FA Cup shock. Friday night, tight ground, locals right up for it, live tv....all the ingredients right there!"

"Honestly mind boggling who makes these decisions. Fair play to anyone who makes that journey on a Friday night," backed up the general feeling amongst Bradford's fanbase.

Wimborne are currently ninth in the Southern Football League Premier Division South, with four steps in the English pyramid separating themselves from Bradford.

As such, the wider public will certainly be hoping the non-league side reach the third round and potentially pluck out a 'glamour tie' involving a Premier League or high-flying Championship outfit.
