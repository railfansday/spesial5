Former Man Utd Ace Looks Completely Unrecognisable, 11 Years After Retiring

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

A former Manchester United ace, who was named in the legendary 1999 Champions League-winning final team as the Red Devils completed a historic treble following triumphs in the Premier League and FA Cup, now looks completely different after having hung up his boots 11 years ago.

Indeed, the player in question was never the biggest name in Sir Alex Ferguson's side, but had the privilege of sharing a dressing room with the likes of Roy Keane, David Beckham and Paul Scholes during his time at Old Trafford.

Now managing a football club in England, the 47-year-old cuts a very different figure on the touchline. Sporting a new haircut and facial hair, he's undergone quite the transformation and could easily pass you by in the street without a second glance.

His career also took him to the likes of Middlesbrough, West Brom, Fulham, and Nottingham Forest - but it's at Old Trafford where Jonathan Greening could dream of becoming one of the very brightest stars in world football.

Of course, it was never meant to be, as he only ever turned out 27 times for Fergie's trophy-laden side. But that's not to say he didn't have success elsewhere, and continues to be involved in English football even now.

But while he remains a constant in football, not many would recognise him after he decided to get rid of his luscious locks and instead don a quiff and a beard. He also went through a period where he was bald, having taken part in a fundraiser back in 2014.

Sir Alex Ferguson tipped seven Man United youngsters to become stars in 2001, but not all of them developed as he would have hoped.

Greening is the manager of Scarborough Athletic, a role he took on in 2021 after retiring from playing in 2017. Since moving into management, the 47-year-old - who also turned out for England Under-21s during his youth career - has guided Scarborough to promotion and a cup win, though the side are currently struggling in 21st in the National League North for the 2026/27 season.

Under his leadership, the Seadogs have finished 8th and 13th twice, and he etched his name in the annals of the 2023/24 FA Cup, when his side advanced to the first round for the first time following a victory over Oxford City.

Greening joined United from York City back in 1998 for an initial fee of £500,000. He signed a new contract the following season, but sought a move to secure more first-team opportunities and subsequently left for Steve McClaren's Middlesbrough after failing to make a real stamp on the Red Devils.

Greening reflected on his time at the club in 2001, stating, as per the Manchester Evening News: "The club is more likely to spend millions than give fringe players the time to establish themselves.

"I have a Champions League winner's medal after sitting on the bench when we beat Bayern. But I didn't play a single minute of European football that season. I felt a bit of a fraud when I got the medal."

Following his stint at Middlesbrough and five seasons with West Brom, Greening moved to Fulham where he played a key role in their journey to the final of the first-ever Europa League.

After spells with Nottingham Forest and Barnsley, he descended the leagues with Tadcaster Albion and York City before retiring in 2017 at the age of 38 and transitioning into management.
