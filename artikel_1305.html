Tarkowski On Individual Form & Hopes For Everton Response

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

James Tarkowski has commended Everton’s ability to bounce back from defeat as his side look to rediscover their strong form following Saturday’s 2-0 loss against Chelsea.

That result marked the end to Everton’s undefeated start to the campaign, and the Blues face a tough text next, travelling to north London to take on reigning Premier League champions Arsenal.

Tarkowski told evertontv: “I always felt last year that anytime we suffered a defeat, we responded pretty well. So, hopefully that happens [this weekend].

“Hopefully, the team can keep picking up points. [Chelsea] was a tough day, a setback.

“We dealt with them pretty well last year, so we’re looking forward to [the next match].”

The Toffees skipper has enjoyed a strong season so far, often putting in standout showings from the Blues backline.

He tops the Premier League for successful duels (57) while ranking second in the division for interceptions (22).

Discussing his positive performances this term, Tarkowski added: “I feel good, I feel fit, I feel strong.

“It’s been nice to have a full pre-season behind me to build into the season. The team’s in a good place, I’m feeling good.

“I just want to continue playing week in and week out – that’s the most-important thing for me.”
