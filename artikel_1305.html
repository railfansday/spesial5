Manchester United fans have been reacting on social media during the Red Devils’ Champions League clash against AS Roma.

Following last weekend’s 2-0 defeat against Chelsea, United arrived at Old Trafford on Tuesday night under massive pressure to win against Roma in the Champions League.

Under the lights at Old Trafford, United were poor from the get-go once again as Matias Soule opened the scoring with just 11 minutes on the clock.

Less than 10 minutes later, Roma were awarded a penalty after Ayden Heaven was adjudged to have handled the ball, with Donyell Malen making it 2-0.

After being booed off at half-time, United were handed a lifeline in the 57th minute when Matheus Cunha was fouled inside the penalty area, with Bruno Fernandes making it 2-1.

From there, the Red Devils pushed for the equaliser, which came in the 83rd minute when Cunha made it 2-2.

Cunha then lifted the roof off Old Trafford when he managed to power a shot into the back of the Roma net to complete the comeback, putting United 3-2 up. In the end, Michael Carrick's men held on for a much-needed victory.

During the match, United fans were less than happy with the performance of Diogo Dalot, who started at left-back as Luke Shaw continues to nurse an injury.

The full-back was partly at fault for Roma’s opening goal, having left acres of space after going up the field, leaving Soule free to slot home and make it 1-0.

In total, the 27-year-old recorded a pass success rate of 83.8 per cent, made two successful tackles and made two interceptions.

Taking to social media, one fan said: “I just do not think I've seen a positive impact from Dalot at LB. He's like 10x worse than at RB & that is saying something. He just hasn't got a clue what to do or where to be.”

Another tweeted: “Diogo Dalot, get as far away as humanly possible from this football club.”

Manchester United, Sunderland, and Crystal Palace are all drastically underperforming in the Premier League this season.

A third fan added: “That’s disgraceful from Diogo Dalot.” Someone else wrote: “It’s Dalot that will sink Carrick.”

A fifth commented: “Never question his work ethic though, he’s an integral part of the 'Leadership group'."

Another fan said: “This Diogo Dalot performance is up there with one of his worst and the list is quite long. Unforgivable. Imagine considering renewing his contract.”

One United added: “Dalot has to go man, why is he so high up the pitch? It’s like he wants Carrick sacked.”

Looking ahead, United will be back in Premier League action this weekend when they take on Aston Villa.
