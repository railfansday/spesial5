Matchday Guide: Everton v Liverpool

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Everton host Liverpool in the Merseyside derby on Sunday 29 November (12pm KO).

Some key information follows.

A comprehensive matchday guide to help supporters plan their matchday, including our extensive Travel Hub and more on food and drink, digital tickets, accessibility and what’s happening in and around Hill Dickinson Stadium, is available at evertonfc.com/matchday.

HELP US MAKE MATCHDAY ENJOYABLE FOR EVERYONE

We want everyone visiting Hill Dickinson Stadium to enjoy their matchday experience in a safe, welcoming and inclusive environment. All supporters have a part to play in making that happen.

Support with passion and respect

We encourage supporters to get behind the team, create a fantastic atmosphere and show respect for those around them.

Discrimination, tragedy chanting, abusive behaviour, entering the field of play, throwing objects and the possession or use of pyrotechnics, including smoke bombs and flares, have no place at our matches. Those responsible face lengthy stadium bans, potential police investigation and further legal action.

Supporters bringing flags, banners or other items into the stadium, or wearing clothing displaying messages, are reminded that these must not feature offensive, discriminatory or inflammatory content, including politically motivated or nationalistic messaging.

See it. Report it.

If you witness any anti-social behaviour, harassment, abuse or discrimination, please let us know. Supporters can report incidents confidentially by:

  • Speaking to a steward or police officer.
  • Texting HELP to 84300, along with details of the incident and its location.
  • Emailing reportit@evertonfc.com.

Reporting incidents at the time they occur helps our Stadium Safety Team respond more effectively.

Alcohol restrictions on Regent Road

Supporters attending Sunday’s fixture should be aware that a Public Spaces Protection Order issued by Liverpool City Council will be in place, prohibiting the consumption of alcohol in public spaces on Regent Road.

Keep your belongings safe

If you are driving to the match, please take a moment to ensure no valuables, cash, mobile devices or chargers are left on display in your vehicle.

Merseyside Police have advised that thieves are known to target vehicles parked by football supporters on matchdays at both Hill Dickinson Stadium, Goodison Park and Anfield. Taking a few simple precautions before heading to the stadium can help reduce the risk of becoming a victim of crime.

1878s FLAG AND BANNER DISPLAY

A flag and banner display will take place in the South Stand ahead of kick-off, with flags placed on selected seats across both the upper and lower tiers. Supporters are encouraged to wave them as the teams emerge from the tunnel.

Banners will also be draped from the North Stand upper.

After the game, please leave the flags under your seat so they can be collected by the fan volunteers and reused at future fixtures.

KEY TIMINGS

9am - Access to the stadium site opens via the Regent Road wall, with food and drink outlets on the Budweiser Plaza*. All available gates (A–E) in the Regent Road wall will be open and supporters are encouraged to use the full range of entrances to help avoid unnecessary congestion. 

9am - The Ticket Office also opens until half-time. The away ticket office, located outside the North Stand turnstiles, will also open at 9am.

10am - Stadium turnstiles open, with food and drink available across all concourses, including Early Bird offers until 11am. Please ensure your digital match ticket has been downloaded to your mobile device before arriving at the stadium. A step-by-step digital ticketing guide and stadium map can be found HERE.

12pm - Kick-off.

AFTER THE FULL-TIME WHISTLE

1.50pm - Following the end of the game, the South Stand Bar will remain open for two hours for home supporters, with Level 0 open to for 30 minutes (see below for more details).

In addition to the South Stand, bars on the Budweiser Plaza will remain open for approximately one hour after full-time, giving supporters the opportunity to stay for a drink before beginning their journey home.

NB: Supporters should leave Hill Dickinson Stadium using the designated exit gates (A–E) on the Regent Road wall. The Nelson Dock exit, accessed from the Budweiser Plaza, is reserved exclusively for supporters with allocated parking in this area, including supporters using accessible parking. Please note, there will be no pedestrian access from Nelson Dock onto Regent Road after the game. 

SOUTH STAND BAR OPENING (POST-MATCH)

The Upper South Stand bar will remain open for two hours for Evertonians after the final whistle.

All supporters on Level 2 can access this bar after the game and, subject to capacity and operational requirements, home supporters from lower areas will also be able to access the bar post-match. This will be via the Lower South escalators for fans on Level 0, or via pass gates at Entrance 7a if coming from other areas of the stadium.

Please note, supporters on Level 0 will not be allowed to access Level 2 until it is safe to do so. A bar on Level 0 will be open for supporters for 30 minutes while they wait for the Upper Tier crowd to disperse and for access to be granted.

HELP KEEP EVERYONE SAFE

We hope you enjoy your visit to Hill Dickinson Stadium.

If you see or hear anything that does not feel right, please report it to a steward or police officer. In an emergency, call 999.
