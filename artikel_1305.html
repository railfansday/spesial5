Serie A Liveblog: Como-Cagliari, Lazio-Lecce, Parma-Roma and Napoli-Torino

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from today’s four Serie A games, Como-Cagliari, leaders Lazio against Lecce, Roma visiting Parma, and Napoli hosting Torino.

We begin our Week 12 coverage at 14.00 GMT (15.00 CET) with Lazio hoping to keep top spot as they welcome Lecce, and Como taking on Cagliari.

At 17.00 GMT (18.00 CET), Roma’s injury crisis is calming down for a visit to Parma, then stuttering Napoli host Torino at 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET).

This weekend, all players have a red mark on their cheeks as part of an annual Serie A campaign highlighting the scourge of violence against women.

Lazio corner finds Diogo Leite for a very difficult volley, it is on target, but Falcone has it under control.

Cagliari chance, Grandu knock-down from the by-line is scrambled clear with difficulty by Como defenders. Mendy then caught offside as the move continues.

Quite a sluggish tempo so far, Como trying to break through the brick wall of this Cagliari side.

Noslin breaks on the counter down the left, but is blocked off by Siebert for a Lazio corner.

Como midfield, might be Ricci as a trequartista, might be Liberali, they might swap around.

The Lazio ultras are still boycotting home games, but there are a few more than usual here today.

Serie A players and coaches will be donning a splash red paint across their cheeks during the 12th round of league fixtures coming up this weekend to raise awareness for the ‘Un Rosso alla Violenza (‘a red card to violence’) campaign.

The Aquile are the surprise outfit of the season, flying high at the top of the table and one of only two sides still unbeaten. Mattia Zaccagni, Nuno Tavares, Josip Sutalo and Adam Marusic are out injured, but Davide Frattesi shakes off a knock on international duty to start.

Lecce went into the break with one point from four rounds, missing Santiago Pierotti and Ivan Ilic, but Medon Berisha and Joel Monteiro return to the bench from injury.

The Lariani saw their run of four straight wins ended by defeat to Inter going into the break for international duty, with Moise Kean, Assane Diao, Jesus Rodriguez and Jayden Addai still long-term injuries, but Trevoh Chalobah returns to the bench. Nico Paz is rested after bagging a brace for Argentina.

The Sardinians had the opposite, ending four defeats in a row with victory over Frosinone. Yerry Mina, Harry Winks, Joseph Liteta and Mattia Felici miss out.
