GTA 6 Launch Stats Are In: Over 50% Of Active Console Users Played

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

GTA 6 has already outgrossed the entire box office run of Avengers: Endgame, the highest-grossing movie in history — and the sheer scale of that success is represented in the launch day stats.

According to Mat Piscatella, senior director and video game industry advisor at Circana, over 50% of all active consoler users played GTA 6 at launch, averaging 494–513 minutes of playtime, or roughly 8 hours.

That does not mean 50% of all console users played it.

"For players active on November 19th, 61% of US PlayStation 5 and 56% of global Xbox players engaged with Grand Theft Auto 6," he posted to Bluesky. "PS5 GTA 6 players averaged 513 minutes that day, Xbox players 494. Both the DAU and minutes played engagement rates are absolutely bonkers."

Piscatella doesn't have the exact stats to hand, explaining that it's "not exactly easy to go back and sort daily active engagement rates," but to his recollection, during his time at Circana, there has been "nothing near this."

The only game second to GTA 6 on launch was Fortnite, accounting for 10% of all active players across both platforms. Clearly, the Pirate Shop Access tokens are working.

$3.7 billion in revenue within 24 hours, "the biggest entertainment launch of all time," accounting for over 50% of all active console users — those numbers are nothing to scoff at.

In fact, in less than a week, with 32.5 million copies sold, GTA 6 is already one of the best-selling games of all-time. It currently sits on the edge of the top 30 after overtaking Super Mario Odyssey, Borderlands 2, and Monster Hunter: World.

A lot of people felt compelled to buy something they could play GTA 6 on after being shown a lot more of it.

Considering that GTA 5 is the third best-selling game of all-time, behind only Minecraft and Tetris (clearly, the secret to selling hundreds of millions of copies is blocks), it's easy to imagine GTA 6 landing somewhere near the top.

Almost six years after the PS5 launched, almost two million people are still playing GTA on PS4.
