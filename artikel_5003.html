XRP’s shrinking exchange supply may be telling investors the wrong story

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Uphold accounts for 82% of the weekly drop in tracked XRP exchange balances, with 270 million XRP sent to unlabeled wallets.

Wallets attributed to Uphold accounted for about 82% of the 545.3 million XRP decline in tracked exchange balances in the seven days to Nov. 24.

The weekly balance breakdown from XRP Insights compares the same addresses on Nov. 17 and Nov. 24. At the end of the week, the tracker counted about 1.25 billion XRP in 24 Uphold-attributed wallets.

Three payments from Uphold-labeled addresses sent about 270 million XRP to three unlabeled wallets on Nov. 23. At follow-up checks the next day, each still held roughly 90 million tokens.

The broader snapshot at 08:01 UTC on Nov. 24 put exchange-attributed holdings at 21.69 billion XRP across 708 addresses. The weekly calculation used the 707 wallets already tracked on Nov. 17.

Coinbase-attributed balances fell by 35.9 million XRP, and Bithumb’s fell by 24.7 million, while Binance-attributed wallets added about 181,000.

Nov. 17–24, 2026 comparison across the same 707 XRP Ledger wallets tracked by XRP Insights.

Uphold-attributed wallets accounted for about 82% of the net decline across the 24 tracked exchanges.

The Uphold share is 446,819,576.34 divided by 545,288,925.25 XRP. The other exchanges’ combined decline is the difference, 98,469,348.91 XRP. Bar lengths use the unrounded proportion. XRP amounts are rounded to one decimal million and the share to a whole percent.

Between 16:21:22 and 16:21:42 UTC on Nov. 23, the XRP Ledger recorded three successful payments from three separate addresses labeled Uphold by XRPSCAN.

The first payment, second payment, and third payment each delivered just over 90 million XRP. Each sending address was left with 1 token, and each receiving address held about 40 tokens before the transfers.

The account histories for rhDoUm, rP2Sxx and rKB4EP show one subsequent incoming payment of 0.000010 XRP to each wallet. No outgoing transaction appeared by the time XRPSCAN balances were checked between 13:12 and 13:13 UTC on Nov. 24.

On Nov. 20, one unlabeled address sent 50 XRP to each of the three. Later that day, each sent 10 tokens to the same Uphold-labeled address.

Those payments establish a connection among the addresses, but Uphold’s public transparency report names none of the three recipients.

XRPSONAR’s Nov. 23 flow series counted 271.6 million XRP sent from its known Uphold wallets and 2.4 million received, leaving a net outflow of 269.2 million tokens that day.

The same series showed a separate 178.7 million XRP net outflow on Nov. 20.

In October, XRP Insights attributed most of an earlier monthly decline to Uphold moving tokens into newly used wallets outside the tracked set. The labeled balance can fall even when the exchange still controls the coins.

A useful test would compare how much was offered within the same distance of the market midpoint on the same spot pairs and venues at comparable times before and after Nov. 23.

Measuring buy orders over the same price range would show how much a seller could trade near the quoted price.

Repeated readings would show whether any difference in quoted depth persisted beyond a single snapshot. A thinner set of sell orders in that range would show that buyers could get less XRP close to the prevailing quote on those markets.
