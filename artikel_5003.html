Nvidia Stock Has a 20% Rebound in Sight—If Bulls Can Clear a Hurdle

If you were looking to buy Nvidia stock at a discount, last week may have been the best time to shop. Shares tanked following the Jensen Huang scandal involving an affair with an alleged Chinese spy, falling as low as $148.80.

On that drop, Nvidia shares touched a major support and then bounced after dropping 38% from its all-time high.

But if you didn’t buy the bottom, the second best time may be this week. The stock is trading at around $159, already up 8% from the lowest point last week. For traders relying on reading the charts, the current price of NVDA is running into another level that could decide whether the recovery has legs.

At around $159 on Monday, its weekly chart now places the $164.40 zone directly in the bulls' way.

That level previously acted as support, a price where buyers stepped in. After the breakdown, it is turning into a level to watch as investors who bought earlier may look to sell once they get their money back. But it could mark a bounce point based on the longer timeframes—as it has for at least three times since September 2025.

If Nvidia can reclaim $165 and hold above it, the next prominent resistance on the chart sits around $188 to $190. That would represent almost 20% upside from Monday's $159 price, although only about 16% from the breakout level, with possible selling pressure along the way.

Failure to clear $164.40 would put $152.62 back on the radar. Another break below that yellow support would weaken the case that last week's bounce marked a bottom.

The exponential moving averages, or EMAs, still offer some encouragement. Nvidia's 50-week EMA remains above its 200-week EMA, a bullish long-term configuration that gives more weight to recent prices but hasn't yet flipped bearish.

The shorter-term signals look less friendly. The Average Directional Index, or ADX, is around 23, suggesting a weakened trend (which is natural after the FUD episode). Its directional readings favor sellers. ADX measures trend strength, not direction by itself.

The Relative Strength Index, or RSI, is near 31.6, barely above the conventional oversold threshold of 30. That can make a relief bounce more plausible, but oversold stocks can keep falling. Neither indicator confirms a reversal yet.

Nvidia's underlying business hasn't suddenly stopped growing. The chipmaker reported $96.2 billion in quarterly revenue in August, up 106% year over year, including $89 billion from its data center business.

Investors will also be watching profit margins and the enormous financing commitments tying Nvidia to the wider AI infrastructure boom.

Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang resigned on November 12 amid reports of extramarital affairs involving Alice Huang, a businesswoman U.S. intelligence suspects of spying for China, and Nvidia executive Kimberly Powell. Reuters subsequently reported a third alleged relationship with another employee.

Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress is serving as interim CEO while an independent investigation into possible foreign-intelligence targeting continues. The espionage allegations have not been established by an independent investigation, much less in court, but were powerful enough to oust the man who led Nvidia for 33 years in record time.

The Financial Times reported that Huang has spoken with allies about returning, while a petition calling for his reinstatement has attracted more than 178,000 signatures, including Elon Musk. The board has not announced plans to bring him back.

The argument sounds familiar: Huang may be too important to Nvidia to keep out. Sam Altman returned to OpenAI days after being ousted in 2023.

Other cases of CEOs too big to let go have been Steve Jobs with Apple, Jack Dorsey with Twitter and Starbuck’s Howard Schultz. Many of these leaders have turned into the face of their companies, making it hard for the markets to trust in any successor.

For bulls, $164.40 remains the line to beat, and charts point to this outcome being quite possible. Assuming, of course, Nvidia's next big reveal is about chips and not a fourth love affair.
