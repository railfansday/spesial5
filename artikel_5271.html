The Microsoft 365 outage is not completely fixed, but services have improved steadily over the past few hours, according to Microsoft’s latest update. The company said availability is now showing lasting gains across affected services, though some users may still face patchy connections or delayed emails. Microsoft said its work is now focused on clearing the remaining problems and making sure service is consistent for everyone. It is testing recovery across different access methods, such as Outlook on the web, desktop and mobile, and watching closely to confirm the improvements hold. Some features linked to Exchange Online, including Copilot’s access to work data, Teams calendar functions and external file sharing in SharePoint, may still be affected for some users. Microsoft again said there is no evidence of malicious activity and will publish a full report on the cause once the issue is fully resolved. Its next update is due at 4 pm UTC, or 9:30 pm IST on Friday.
