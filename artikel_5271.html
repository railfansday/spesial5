TRON, Tether, and Chainalysis Launch Task Force to Combat Illicit USDT Activity on TRON Network

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Following the disbandment of the T3 FCU, TRON and Tether have launched a new initiative to tackle criminal activity in USDT on Tron.

Tron and Tether have formed a strategic partnership with blockchain intelligence platform Chainalysis. The coalition aims to crack down on criminal activity involving Tether’s USDT stablecoin on the Tron blockchain, according to data Tron CEO Justin Sun shared today.

In a Thursday announcement, Tron, Tether, and Chainalysis formed a digital crime task force (DCTF), a new initiative designed to effectively uncover and clamp down on illicit activity involving USDT on the TRON network.

The DCTF brings together three industry leaders – a stablecoin issuer, a crypto network, and a blockchain intelligence firm – aimed at building investigative capability, with support from global law enforcement authorities. The key driver behind the collaboration is to better combat illegal USDT transactions on Tron amid a surge in AI-enabled crime.

The DCTF takes effect after the T3 Financial Crime Unit (T3 FCU), formed by Tether, Tron, and TRM Labs, ended its role in August. The T3 FCU program, which was rolled out in September 2024, was tasked with disrupting unlawful USDT activity on Tron. Its track record has been good. By mid-2025, it had blocked around $250 million in illicit USDT assets, and by May 2026, it had also frozen over $450 million. It had collaborated with law enforcement agencies in 23 jurisdictions.

In the new announcement issued today, Tether and Tron have launched a new initiative to make the crackdown more efficient with Chainalysis’ support. With the recent massive Binance hack, the timing of the fresh partnership is critical.

The Binance exploit highlights serious security risks for crypto platforms. Reports indicate that the hacker sold around $325 million USDT to market makers within minutes of obtaining the stolen funds.

The formation of the DCTF aims to swiftly unearth and dismantle illegal USDT transactions on Tron. With swift responses and coordination among the parties, the alliance seeks to minimize the time between uncovering criminal activity and freezing assets, particularly as AI-assisted crimes are on the increase.
