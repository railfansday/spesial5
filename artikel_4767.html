WHEN IT COMES to athleisure, I’m usually torn between two brands: Vuori and Lululemon. And now that Black Friday is here, the two are copeting for my wallet just as much as they are for space in my closet. Both have become staples in my wardrobe, whether I’m heading to the gym or catching a flight. And I don’t care what anyone says about dressing up for the airport—I’m always going to travel in sweats. Comfort is nonnegotiable when I’m sitting on a plane for hours, and both brands make some of the best joggers and sweatshirts I’ve put on my body. But what I appreciate most is that neither brand is limited to workout clothes. They’ve got plenty of everyday pieces, from sleek trousers and versatile jackets to tees and button-downs, that look just as fly outside the gym.

For the biggest shopping day of the year, Vuori and Lululemon are rolling out almost 50 percent off deals on everything from performance gear to casual wardrobe staples, so it’s a great time to refresh your workout rotation or grab something comfortable for your next trip. But which brand has the better Black Friday deals? I’ve rounded up the styles worth shopping from the two giants to help you decide where to spend your money.

Starting with Lululemon’s Black Friday sale, we’re seeing deals up to 50 percent off, with plenty of workout gear and outerwear worth adding to your cart. The biggest markdown that caught my eye is the Always Down Puffer Jacket, set at 49 percent off. It’s a water-resistant and windproof piece, packed with insulation and plenty of room for chunky layers. I would pick this up just for winter days ahead when the temps dip below 30 degrees. There’s also a solid selection of hoodies and gym shorts on sale. Speaking of, if you’re not a fan of built-in liners, the Zeroed In Linerless 7-Inch Short is a great option for a freer feeling during running and training. It has a relaxed fit through the glutes and thighs, giving you plenty of breathing room, plus sweat-wicking, quick-drying fabric to keep you comfortable when things heat up. Beyond that, you’ll find workout tanks, compression shirts, and even comfortable joggers and pants that are for running errands or casual meet-ups with friends.

Over at Vuori’s Black Friday sale, deals are low as 40 percent off. The Sunday Performance Jogger, one of the best joggers we’ve tested, is 40 percent off. I’d reach for it whether I’m catching a flight or spending a lazy Sunday at home. The Kore Short, another editor favorite from our testing, is also marked down by 21 percent. It’s a solid pick for lifting weights and tackling a sweaty cardio session. You’ll also find deals on training jackets, quarter-zips, and workout tees, along with pants that are sharp enough to wear to the office followed by a nice dinner.

As Black Friday shopping has began, popular sizes and colors are disappearing fast, so if something catches your eye, I wouldn’t wait around. Your size might not be there when you come back.
