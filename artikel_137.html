Writing seen in PHerc. 306B for the first time in 2,000 years

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Where Readers Share Great Links. Join Free

Researchers and volunteers identified readable text in PHerc. 306B, part of a carbonized Herculaneum scroll buried by the eruption of Mount Vesuvius in AD 79. The Vesuvius Challenge team awarded a $50K First Letters Prize after submissions from Liam Warner and Nicola Bodill revealed previously unreadable ink and helped recover the first clear characters from the scroll.
