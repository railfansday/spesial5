Dodgers Finally Move On From Former Top Prospect Bobby Miller

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Los Angeles Dodgers, in their trade for Minnesota Twins' former top prospect Royce Lewis, parted ways with right-hander Bobby Miller, the first notable move of the offseason for LA.

The full trade between the Twins and Dodgers is now: Miller and catcher Francisco Espinoza to the Twins for Royce Lewis, the former No. 1 overall pick of the 2017 MLB Draft.

Espinoza is a 19-year-old minor league catcher. Lewis had two great years at the start of his major-league career, but his performance has declined.

Miller, who battled a ton of injuries over the offseason, made some spot appearances for the Dodgers last year, but he wasn't available until the second half of the season.

The flamethrower has the chance to start anew now, and the Dodgers get a needed infield, but it is bittersweet since Miller was once supposed to be an elite member of the rotation.

Miller was a former top prospect for the Dodgers, peaking at No. 20 overall for Baseball America. He had an elite fastball with great movement, a quality slider, changeup, and curveball with good enough control to be a starter.

He debuted in 2023, pitching 124.1 innings and posting a 3.76 ERA. He had a 98th-percentile fastball, which made it hard for hitters to make hard contact.

However, after that season, his fastball lost some movement, and his command slipped, leading to more walks and hittable pitches.

Also, he had two shoulder issues pop up over the last three years, along with a concussion after getting hit by a comebacker in Spring Training.

May 23, 2023; Cumberland, Georgia, USA; Los Angeles Dodgers starting pitcher Bobby Miller (70) is interviewed after the game against the Atlanta Braves at Truist Park. Mandatory Credit: Dale Zanine-USA TODAY Sports

With all of those ailments, Miller's mechanics got out of whack; he lost confidence in his delivery, and as a result, he spent 2024 and 2025 yo-yoing between the minors and the majors.

In Triple-A this year, he had a 5.66 ERA with 61 walks and just 83 strikeouts. He made three appearances with the big-league team, posting a 3.68 ERA in 7.1 innings, but he did not make the postseason roster.

There is still a chance Miller becomes something, and plenty of fans will be rooting for him since he has battled a lot, but he remains committed to making the most of his potential and career.
