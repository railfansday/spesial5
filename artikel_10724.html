DJ Khaled is giving Xbox players doctor’s notes so they can skip work for GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Xbox has teamed up with DJ Khaled to give gamers an excuse to skip work and spend the day playing video games, complete with personalized doctor’s notes.

The rapper and producer has taken on the role of “Dr. XBOX” as part of a new promotional campaign encouraging players to take time off during a major week for gaming.

Announced on November 16, the campaign allows fans to generate their own doctor’s notes online, although Xbox has made it clear that the documents aren’t medically certified and won’t necessarily convince employers to approve a day off.

Players interested in getting a personalized doctor’s note can visit the website, where Xbox has launched a dedicated website for the promotion.

The notes are presented as prescriptions from DJ Khaled, who claims to have discovered a widespread condition called “Vitamin G deficiency” after observing symptoms including controller cramps, late-night gaming dreams, and excitement surrounding new releases.

“After extensive research (and a whole lot of gaming), I’ve come to one conclusion: there’s a serious case of Vitamin G deficiency around the world. And I’m here to tell you that the cure is simple,” Khaled explained.

A big week in gaming is coming, and there's a serious case of Vitamin G deficiency around the world. Luckily, Dr. XBOX has the cure – it’s time to take the day off and play XBOX.https://t.co/MpwW4SM87V

The musician encourages players to use their personalized notes however they like, whether that means displaying them at home, sharing them with friends, or showing them to their bosses.

“Your very own prescription for more gaming. Put it on your fridge. Show your boss. Send it to your group chat. Let the people know that Dr. XBOX has spoken,” he said.

However, the promotion comes with a disclaimer confirming that the notes have no medical validity.
