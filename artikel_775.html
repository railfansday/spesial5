Khelif still set for pro bout in Paris despite opponent withdrawal

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Imane Khelif was the subject of a fierce gender eligibility row at the Olympic Games; she hasn't boxed since winning gold at Paris 2024; Algerian is due to fight as a professional in Paris on December 15

Imane Khelif is still set to box for the first time since the Olympic Games even though her scheduled opponent has withdrawn.

Algeria's Khelif won Olympic welterweight gold in Paris in 2024, while a fierce gender eligibility row overshadowed her participation.

Khelif was cleared to compete by the International Olympic Committee (IOC), despite the International Boxing Association (an organisation that itself has been banned from Olympic boxing) having banned her from the previous year's World Championships for allegedly failing to meet gender eligibility criteria.

Khelif hasn't boxed since beating Liu Yang in that Olympic final, but is due to have a professional bout in Paris on December 15.

That fight next month will go ahead although Khelif's original opponent Clarida Sarabia has withdrawn and Khelif is now looking for a replacement.

"The opponent did have to withdraw from the fight for administrative reasons," a spokesperson ⁠for promoter Stair Event ​told Reuters.

"But the bout ​is still scheduled for December ​15. We'll announce the opponent ⁠soon."

In February, Khelif said she was open to genetic testing ⁠only if administered by ​the IOC.
