Everything We Know About Coachella 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

It’s the most wonderful time of the year: Coachella has officially dropped the list of performers for its 2027 festival, and as usual, it offers a stacked lineup across genres, with power players in pop, alternative, R&B, and more on the bill.

The headliners include Dua Lipa, SZA, and Tame Impala. Madonna will also be at the festival following the release of her most recent album, Confessions II, but will perform in the dance tent instead of a main stage—a cheeky nod to the exact same billing she received when she performed at the festival as the first dance act in 2006.

Here’s everything we know about Coachella 2027 so far, should you plan on trekking to the desert next spring.

The 2027 festival will once again take place at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California. Coachella will run from April 9 to 11 and April 16 to 18, 2027.

In addition to Dua, Madonna, SZA, and Tame Impala, other performers include Zara Larsson, Jisoo, Kehlani, Kelela, Haute & Freddy, Phoebe Bridgers, Robyn, Adéla, Tiffany Day, Underscores, Becky G, Babymonster, Muna, Magdalena Bay, Shygirl, Victoria Monét, Caroline Polachek, and many more.

Passes for Coachella go on sale on November 13 at 12 P.M. PT. There will be different tiers—from GA to VIP and more—as well as options that include shuttle transportation to and from the festival. Passes for both weekends can be purchased here, with more information available here.

Not heading to the desert this year? No problem—YouTube plans to partner with the festival again as the exclusive livestream partner for both weekends of Coachella. Fans can watch performances on the official YouTube channel.
