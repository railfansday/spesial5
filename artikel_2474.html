Three takeaways from Gotham’s narrow quarterfinal win over Reign

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

(Un)lucky goal, strong defense and the value of experience

NJ/NY Gotham FC narrowly defeated the Seattle Reign in the National Women’s Soccer League quarterfinals Sunday morning, 1-0. Despite one of their worse offensive performances of the season, Gotham got the lucky bounce on the game-changing goal, then relied on their defense and experience to see out the gritty victory.

The deciding goal in this game came less than five minutes in. Jaedyn Shaw’s corner kick service was met by Reign defender Jordyn Bugg at the front post and, in her attempt to clear the ball, it sliced off her shin to squeak past goalkeeper Claudia Dickey. The week before, Bugg had scored Seattle’s Decision Day game-winner that got them into the playoffs in the first place.

Outside of the own goal, Gotham created just 0.17 expected goals (xG) and had zero shots on target. Dickey was not forced into any saves. On the other end, Seattle’s xG amounted to 0.54. Not included in that was putting the ball in the back of the net in the 59th minute; Coco Mondésir finished off a Jess Fishlock assist, but it was called back because Fishlock was inches offside. The Reign also had multiple chances shot barely wide of the post. There is something to be said, of course, about failing to take advantage of what they did create.
