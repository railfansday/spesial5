While this higher-temperature heat can be radiated away with a relatively compact radiator, it would have to be separate from the thermal sinks cooling the processors below their 320 Kelvin ideal temperature. Ultimately, a nuclear-powered cluster would only need around 6% less low temperature radiator area than a fully solar-powered version.
