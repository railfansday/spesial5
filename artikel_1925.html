CM: ‘In the coming hours’ – Milan and Rabiot on the verge of renewal with one matter to solve

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Ruben Amorim sees Adrien Rabiot as key to his squad and the Frenchman is on the verge of extending his deal with AC Milan.

This season, few players have been more impactful than Rabiot and it’s not exactly a new thing. Last year, he was vital to Massimiliano Allegri and whilst his Portuguese replacement sees things much differently, he doesn’t when it comes to Rabiot. So, a renewal has been sought for a while now.

As Calciomercato.com writes, the desire to move forward together is shared, and Gerry Cardinale is hoping to close the deal soon. Indeed, talks with his mother and agent, Veronique, are progressing well and are at an advanced stage.

His performances deserve a raise and Amorim has been keen to offer a renewal to one of the biggest pillars in his squad. After the questions during the summer, it’s a huge statement of intent from all parties that he wants to spend the remainder of his best years at San Siro.

The deal will see him call the Rossoneri home until 2030 – a two-year extension – and then there will be an option of a further year, which the club can utilise when they want. The report states that this is the final thing to confirm and it could be done in the ‘coming hours’.
