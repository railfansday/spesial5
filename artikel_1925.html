Maldini admits career could’ve been different without father and Liedholm: “Liked playing goalkeeper”

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Things could’ve been much different for Paolo Maldini at AC Milan without two major figures in his life and career.

Maldini has two faces at Milan, but both remain adored by the fanbase. Firstly, you have the legendary defender, whose number was retired with him, a sign of respect and a chance to honour a career and legacy which surpasses many in the game.

Then, he returned as the sporting director, ending the Scudetto drought. Of course, that story didn’t have an ending as romantic as his playing career, but his legacy lives on. In fact, he may be voted into the Hall of Fame in the very near future.

Speaking to Tuttomercatoweb, via PianetaMilan, Maldini looked at the earliest stages of his career, as well as when he really took off for the Rossoneri.

“When my father took me to try out for AC Milan, I liked playing goalkeeper, but luckily I ended up choosing to be a defender. I played football with my friends in the park and at the youth club: I didn’t feel like I was destined for success at all, and until the day I made my debut, I never thought I could reach the highest levels.

“My dad always told me that the most important thing is to have fun, because if you treat it only as a job, it loses its magic.”

“There’s one person in particular who believed in me and wanted me to play when I was 15: Nils Liedholm, then AC Milan’s coach. He loved showing his faith in young players, and once, during a break, he asked me whether I preferred to play on the right or left. I replied that it was obviously his choice, but it was a gesture that helped me a lot.”

As they say, one change and the rest is history. With Maldini, that was certainly the case.
