Mauricio Pochettino contract clause explained as Manchester United 'learn price to appoint'

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Manchester United have finally decided to sack Michael Carrick, with Mauricio Pochettino the frontrunner for the Old Trafford job.

Reports emerged on Monday evening that a decision has been made to dismiss the under-pressure boss with the club in the Premier League relegation zone.

As many of the squad fly out for international duty with their respective nations, work will now begin at the club’s Carrington training ground to hire a new manager.

And former Tottenham and Chelsea manager Pochettino is the favourite.

ESPN report that Pochettino is the favoured candidate among Man Utd chiefs, but he will not come cheap.

Pochettino has not been involved in club management since his decision to leave Chelsea, by mutual contest, two and a half years ago.

The Argentine had spent just one year in west London, and was soon after lured by the USA national team to guide them into their home World Cup where they were knocked out at the last-16 stage.

Pochettino was heavily linked with a return to Tottenham before the World Cup, and was widely expected to leave the USMNT setup after the tournament.

However, in August, Pochettino signed a new contract to guide the national team into the 2030 World Cup.

But, with Man Utd now set to come calling, Pochettino is ready to head back to the Premier League.

BBC Sport report that Pochettino is open to the Old Trafford job and Man Utd will have to cough up to appoint him.

According to The Athletic, there is a buyout clause in Pochettino’s contract worth $10million (£7.44m) which Man Utd can trigger to bring back him to England.

However, the buyout clause does not cover Pochettino’s coaching staff, many of who - such as assistant Jesus Perez - has been with him most the majority of his career.
