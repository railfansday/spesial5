Manchester United shortlist £26m Spurs target to solve Mauricio Pochettino issue

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Manchester United are considering a move for Freiburg striker Igor Matanovic in the January transfer window, according to reports.

Mauricio Pochettino arrived at United this week having replaced Michael Carrick, who was sacked during the international break.

But the Argentine begins his tenure at Old Trafford without a recognised striker as Benjamin Sesko is sidelined until next year due to a shin injury.

United have made funds available to strengthen Pochettino’s squad in January and the club are targeting a new striker.

According to The i Paper, Matanovic is on United’s striker shortlist alongside Folarin Balogun, who Monaco are open to selling in the January transfer window.

The report adds that United are also looking to bolster Pochettino’s defence with Fulham centre-back Calvin Bassey and left-back Antonee Robinson under consideration.

Matanovic, meanwhile, was signed by Freiburg in an €8 million (£6.8m) deal from Eintracht Frankfurt last year.

The Croatia international, who offers a physical presence in attack at 6ft 5in, has scored 23 goals in 64 games for Freiburg so far.

At international level, Matanovic has scored two goals in 17 appearances for Croatia and came off the bench in three games at the World Cup in the summer.

Matanovic, who is now valued at around €30m (£26m), has also been linked with a move to Tottenham.

When asked about his future in September, Matanovic insisted he had not received an approach to leave Freiburg.

‘As for me, I’m currently in Freiburg. There has been absolutely no contact with anyone from my side,’ the striker said.
