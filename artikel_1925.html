Joao Pedro injury update with five Chelsea stars to miss Leeds clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Joao Pedro has been ruled out of Chelsea’s Premier League clash with Leeds United this weekend.

The Brazil international has missed his side’s last four games as a result of a thigh injury he suffered in training in October.

The former Brighton star sat out of games against Tottenham, Manchester United, Sunderland and Liverpool in the Carabao Cup with the Blues losing three of those four in his absence.

There was hope he would be available to return after the international break but Xabi Alonso has confirmed his striker is still not ready ahead of the visit of Daniel Farke’s side on Saturday afternoon.

Pedro is still to return to training with his manager unable to put a timeline on his return.

‘Not yet,’ Alonso said when asked about his availability. ‘He’s not fit yet to train with the team. He’s not going to be available for Leeds. We need to see game by game how he’s feeling.’

When asked how confident he is to find a solution in attack in Pedro’s absence, Alonso replied: ’We need the whole team for that.

’It’s not just the strikers who can score. We can score from set-pieces, defenders, midfielders, so it’s teamwork, for sure.’He’s an important player but we need to deal this time without him. So far we have tried different options and we need to keep trying.’They [Danny Welbeck and Emmanuel Emegha] are both ready, both have different qualities, both at different stages of their career. Danny has played so many games, has great experience, has quality, Emman has this power, this physicality, he’s waiting for his chance and we have enough games to try.’

Chelsea have other concerns with Maxence Lacroix still sidelined along with Jorrel Hato.

Lacroix, almost ever-present at the back since his summer arrival from Crystal Palace, came off with a hamstring injury in the Carabao Cup defeat to Liverpool while Hato missing the last three games with a back issue.

‘He’s [Lacroix] not fit to play tomorrow and he’s a similar situation with Joao,’ Alonso said.

Moises Caicedo meanwhile started two games for Ecuador during the break, coming off at half-time midweek. Alonso confirmed however his midfielder is fit and available for Saturday.

Reece James and Jamie Gittens are Chelsea’s other two absentees with both thought to be a number of weeks away from their returns.
