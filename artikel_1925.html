Vitalii Mykolenko is finished under David Moyes after rotten 0% Everton display

Vitalii Mykolenko delivered a dismal display as Everton were smashed 4-0 by Brentford in London on Monday.

Keane Lewis-Potter scored two goals within two minutes to hand the home side the lead in the first half, before Vitaly Janelt and Fabio Carvalho added the third and fourth against the 10-man Toffees after the break.

James Tarkowski was controversially sent off just before the break for a high challenge, despite having comfortably won the ball.

Monday's performance will certainly go down as a set-back for Everton, who had started the season excellently.

The Toffees had been on a run of three consecutive wins across all competitions, and will now face the visit of Liverpool on Sunday.

Moyes will have a key decision to make on Mykolenko, who certainly did not advertise his quality on Monday.

Mykolenko signed a new contract in June, and has been a key part of the Everton back-line for several years now.

And Moyes clearly trusts him, with the Ukraine international essentially a guaranteed starter when he is fit.

But Monday was a clear example of what the 27-year-old can leave Everton without on the left.

Mykolenko completed just 76 per cent of his passes, misplacing seven and completing only three into the final third.

He did not create a chance, take a shot or even deliver a single cross into the penalty box - an astonishing stat considering Everton played with 11 men for almost the entire first half.

Mykolenko was strong in the ground duel and did notch 11 defensive contributions as the Toffees were pinned in, but he also gave away a foul and lost all three of the aerials he contested.

Moyes is not inundated with options in his defence, but there is certainly scope to replace Mykolenko.

James Garner played at left-back when the Ukrainian was injured last season and excelled there, able to drop into midfield in an inverted role.

Merlin Rohl, who has appeared regularly at right-back this season, can likely do a similar job on the left.

And of course, there is Ainsley Maitland-Niles, who started his Everton career superbly but has not even appeared off the bench in the league since defeat to Chelsea.

Moyes is stubborn, but he must surpass some of his own barriers if Everton are to make history this season.
