Verra / MVRDV

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Text description provided by the architects. MVRDV has completed construction of Verra, a residential complex providing 343 apartments at the new Enterprise Research Campus (ERC) in Boston. Surrounded by a mixture of laboratories, conference facilities, and a hotel, Verra's ground floor layer of locally owned shops, cafés, and restaurants help to integrate it into Allstonway, the new public park that forms the central spine of the ERC. The project thus places residents at the heart of a vibrant district that brings together the international science and innovation community with the Allston-Brighton community. Meanwhile, behind the green façade a diverse collection of apartments offer expansive views through the design's many corner windows, giving residents a high quality of life both at home and out in the neighbourhood.
