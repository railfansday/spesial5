Caleb Downs has no injury designation, George Pickens listed as questionable

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Cowboys safety Caleb Downs is set to play against the Colts on Sunday.

Downs moved up to full practice on Friday and has no injury designation for Sunday, which means he's cleared concussion protocol and will not be sidelined by an ankle injury. Safety P.J. Locke is also without a designation after his own trip through the protocol.

Wide receiver George Pickens (hamstring) remained limited in practice and is listed as questionable. Cornerback Joey Porter Jr. (ankle), tight end Luke Schoonmaker (groin), tight end Jake Ferguson (ankle), and linebacker DeMarvion Overshown (hamstring, back) are also listed as questionable, Defensive tackle Jonathan Bullard (calf) and linebacker Marist Liufau (forearm) are both listed as questionable to be activated from injured reserve.

Linebacker Malachi Lawrence (ankle) and wide receiver KaVontae Turpin (ribs) have both been ruled out.
