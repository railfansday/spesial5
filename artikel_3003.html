UiPath vs. Palantir Technologies: Comparing Modest Revenue Growth vs. Rapid Expansion

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Palantir's quarterly revenue has more than doubled over the past eight quarters, while UiPath's has stayed largely flat with recurring seasonal swings.

By Robert Izquierdo – Nov 18, 2026 at 7:25PM EST

UiPath (PATH -5.33%) primarily generates revenue by delivering an integrated suite of enterprise software applications that empowers corporate entities, financial institutions, and government organizations to independently design, deploy, evaluate, and continuously manage automated business processes across their operations.

It recently reorganized its executive leadership team, formally appointing a new chief financial officer, and simultaneously introduced several advanced developer tools specifically designed to help large global enterprises dynamically map and orchestrate their various internal organizational workflows.

Palantir Technologies (PLTR +0.07%) primarily earns revenue by engineering and deploying specialized software systems that enable government intelligence agencies, allied military branches, and large commercial enterprises to integrate, analyze, and gain insights from massive amounts of fragmented information.

It recently secured a major tactical ground station production agreement with the United States Army, while simultaneously establishing multiple new commercial partnerships explicitly aimed at supporting both its global international expansion and its ongoing modular data center infrastructure initiatives.

Revenue represents the total amount of money a business brings in from its core operations before any expenses are subtracted, serving as a fundamental baseline measure of overall customer demand and business growth..

UiPath and Palantir's revenue trajectories illustrate their sharp divergence in enterprise artificial intelligence adoption. Palantir is experiencing compounding hyper-growth fueled by broad commercial adoption of its artificial intelligence platform (AIP), whereas UiPath has stabilized into moderate, seasonally cyclical expansion.

This distinct difference in sales trends reveals the massive customer exuberance for Palantir's AIP solution, as demonstrated by the consistent quarter-over-quarter revenue growth. Meanwhile UiPath's AI offerings are not finding as much market demand despite the AI boom, with sales volatility from quarter to quarter, although the company is still maintaining year-over-year increases.

That said, UiPath's growth appears to be decelerating. Its fiscal second quarter revenue of $410.3 million was a 13% year-over-year jump, but down from the 17% growth seen in Q1. This decline extends into its fiscal Q3, with the company forecasting sales of at least $440 million, which is about a 7% increase over 2025's $411 million, raising concerns as to whether the company can continue to grow over the long term.

Palantir's soaring sales are due to AIP attracting commercial customers, and successfully expanding beyond its government roots. The company's $2.3 billion in Q3 marked the first time its U.S. commercial sales surpassed U.S. government revenue, signaling a key inflection point in its business.
