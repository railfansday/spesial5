Senior housing now drives 18% of income, but summer occupancy fell short of plans.

National Health Investors, Inc. (NHI -4.83%) reported a third quarter defined by the completion of its $541.6 million skilled nursing portfolio sale and the subsequent rotation of capital into the senior housing operating segment. Management indicated that the Senior Housing Operating Portfolio now contributes 18% of total net operating income, doubling its share since the beginning of the year. While the same-store portfolio met growth objectives, recently acquired properties experienced operational delays and occupancy growth that trailed management's initial expectations. The company maintained a leverage ratio below its target range while executing $396.2 million in year-to-date investments, positioning the firm for its second largest investment year in company history.
