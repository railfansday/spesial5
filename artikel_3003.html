McDonald's: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The margin story rests on a 98% franchised target, and whether baseline traffic can carry that model through 2028 is still open.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 4, 2026, McDonald's (MCD +6.53%) announced global comparable sales growth of 1.5% for the quarter. For long-term investors, customer visit frequency and menu pricing power remain critical factors for sustainable profit growth. Three things management said on the call stand out.

Management is testing new everyday affordable meal options across 40% of U.S. restaurants to address entry-level pricing. Fixing baseline guest traffic remains key for franchise profitability and store volume.

Specialty beverages are expanding across global markets to capture non-peak customer traffic. Orders containing these beverages generate check totals that are larger than the full-day average check.

Refranchising efforts in international operated markets (IOM, company-operated international stores) continue as the business aims for a 98% franchised portfolio by 2028. This asset-light model protects operating margins and capital efficiency.

Baseline guest traffic is turning positive heading into the fourth quarter following targeted promotional interventions. Management is watching pricing discipline and store execution across international markets.

The company maintains a vast global store footprint, strong brand equity, and unmatched supply chain scale. Future value depends on executing its multi-year growth plan, McDonald's > NEXT, through digital engagement and modern store remodels.
