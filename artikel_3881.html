■ An Update to the PlayStation Plus App on PC

We wanted to let you know that from December 10, we will be retiring the PlayStation Plus app on PC.

PlayStation believes in providing high-quality experiences to our players, and we regularly review offerings to ensure we are focusing on experiences our members value most. With a very small number of eligible PlayStation Plus Premium members (less than 0.4 percent) using the PlayStation Plus app on PC, making this change allows us to focus on enhancing high-quality experiences that more of our members want to use. As a result, the PlayStation Plus cloud streaming app on PC will no longer be available.

This change does not affect PlayStation Plus memberships or benefits available on PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 4 consoles. We appreciate everyone who used the app on PC and will be notifying affected users directly.
