Tubi wants more creator content. The Kickstarter-backed “Fandom Fund” is here to help.

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

As Tubi looks to bring more creators to its shores, it is helping them solve a prevalent problem by providing easier access to capital. That’s the gist of the Tubi x Kickstarter Fandom Fund, which unites a Fox-owned streaming service and a 17-year-old crowdfunding platform.

The Fandom Fund follows up last year’s FilmStream Collective, a partnership between Tubi and Kickstarter that delivered support to more than 20 independent films. The new collab between the companies will function similarly; Tubi will throw its weight behind active Kickstarter campaigns, which it will distribute exclusively.

Tubi won’t just pick any Kickstarter project; instead, as it has done with its other incubator programs, it will hone in on the genres that have the widest appeal among its 110 million monthly active users. The categories fitting that description include horror, YA, Black entertainment, true crime, LGBTQ+, comedy, and reality.

For Kickstarter, the Fandom Fund represents an increasing commitment to the creator economy. It is part of the three-month-old Culture Fund, which builds on Kickstarter’s enduring status as a key tool for indie artists. In 2024 alone, the platform generated over $700 million of fundraising across more than 35,000 projects.

Indie creators don’t always get distribution deals with brand-name platforms, but Tubi is eager to support the next generation of storytellers — whom it believes are just as capable as traditional TV voices. To that end, Tubi has premiered a handful of shows and movies led by social media standouts. One of the projects in the Fandom Fund will get a similar treatment by receiving additional backing, allowing it to be developed into a full-length series or feature film.

“We believe great talent can come from anywhere,” said Tubi GM of Creator Programs and EVP of Business Development Rich Bloom in a statement. “The Fandom Fund gives emerging storytellers the resources to bring those ambitious ideas to life on a bigger canvas while preserving the creative voice, vision and ownership that made their work resonate.”

Kickstarter will provide updates about the Fandom Fund through its film-focused newsletter. Participation in the fund requires an active campaign, so if you want Tubi’s backing, get your crowdfunding game on.
