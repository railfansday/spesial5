Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando’s Complete Relationship Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Miley Cyrus can buy herself flowers, but we have a feeling that her beau, Maxx Morando, is buying them for her.

Since 2021, the Disney Channel alum has been linked to Morando, a fellow musician who is perhaps best known as being the drummer for the rock band Liily. He was also previously part of another group, called the Regrettes. In 2022, Lilly was invited to play on Cyrus’s TV special, Miley’s New Year’s Eve Party.

Throughout their years-long relationship, the two have preferred to keep their romance largely out of the spotlight. But Cyrus offered a rare glimpse into their life together during her interview for the Harper’s Bazaar’s December 2024/January 2025 cover story. “He’s very similar to me,” she said. “We just don’t take life too seriously.”

Throughout their time together, they’ve also collaborated numerous times in the studio, with Morando serving as a producer and songwriter on Cyrus’s Something Beautiful and Bass Persuades records. In her interview with Bazaar, Cyrus revealed that music was actually how she and Morando first got to know each other. “I’ve always worked with the people that I love,” Cyrus said. “And Maxx just inspires me so much.”

Now, the pair are engaged and reported to be getting married this weekend with an intimate ceremony in Southern California. However, other details are being kept under wraps—for now.

If you’re wondering what else there is to know about Cyrus and Morando’s low-key romance, then look no further. Below, we’ve tracked the couple’s entire relationship from start to finish.

For Cyrus, it was love at first sight, but for Morando, he already had an idea of who he was meeting long before the date even happened.

“We got put on a blind date,” Cyrus reveals in an interview with British Vogue years after the fact, in May 2023. “Well, it was blind for me and not really for him. I thought, ‘The worst that can happen is I leave.’ ”

When he calls mid-interview, her phone starts playing “Tyrone” by Erykah Badu. “I call that ‘boyfriend sexy ringtone,’ ” she says, smiling.

In an interview with Vogue, Cyrus discusses the outfits that she wore for her sets at Summerfest in Milwaukee and Atlanta’s Music Midtown festival. Turns out, one of the pieces—a faux-leather set crafted by Shane Kastl—includes cartoon monsters sketched by Morando.

During the interview, Cyrus comments on the custom design, saying, “This look is a one-of-a-kind collaboration between two of my favorite emerging artists, Maxx Morando and Shane Kastl. Shane used existing pieces I’ve collected over time and customized Maxx’s cartoon characters [within] his construction. Their collaboration is the perfect example of sustainability becoming the next fashion phase, and it proves that anything can be reinvented, which resonates with me deeply.”

The pair spark dating rumors when they make their first public appearance at the Gucci Love Parade fashion show in November.

For the event, Cyrus wears a metallic blue fringe dress with a contrasting cream feather drop waist, which she pairs with strappy golden sandals. Meanwhile, Morando sports a burgundy suit over a G-logo top, with white sneakers featuring the signature Gucci red and green stripes.

Shortly after the event, Cyrus shares a slideshow where the third photo sees her and Morando posing together on the red carpet. In her caption, the singer writes, “LOVE PARADE ❤️💕 @gucci @alessandro_michele.”

A fan captures a sweet video of Morando supporting Cyrus backstage for her NBC holiday special, Miley’s New Year’s Eve Party. In the clip, the two can be seen smiling and dancing together.

Days later, paparazzi snap photos of the two enjoying a vacation together in Miami, where the special was filmed.

Cyrus and Morando make a rare joint appearance together for Versace’s Fall/Winter 2023 fashion show in West Hollywood.

Later in the day, the two attend the launch party for Cyrus’s eighth studio album, Endless Summer Vacation, in Beverly Hills. Paparazzi photos—which you can see here—show Cyrus dressed to the nines in a sparkly black one-shoulder minidress and a vibrant green fur jacket. Morando looks equally stylish in a navy-blue velvet suit.

A source tells People where Cyrus’s head is at amid her relationship with Morando.

“She’s the healthiest and happiest she’s been in a long time,” the source says, then goes on to describe how Cyrus has healed following her 2020 divorce from Liam Hemsworth: “Everyone was blaming her for the divorce and calling her this wild child, but that wasn’t fair. Their relationship and marriage was toxic, and she was heartbroken.”

A second insider tells the outlet that Morando is a “cool guy.”

“He has no interest in being a huge celebrity; he’s very low-key. Miley loves these qualities,” the source explains. “Miley can be herself. They are supportive of each other’s careers. Miley is enjoying life and things are great.”

Morando supports Cyrus at the 66th-annual Grammy Awards ceremony, where the singer wins her first-ever golden gramophone.

During the ceremony, the couple is joined by Cyrus’s family members, mom Tish Cyrus and sister Brandi Cyrus.

A source for People opens up about Cyrus and Morando’s joyful home life together.

“She is very happy with him,” the insider claims. “Miley has changed a lot in the past couple of years. She seems more calm and harmonious. Everyone loves Maxx. He’s a great guy. She is very happy with him. He’s private and doesn’t like being in the press. It’s been good for her.”

In a new Dolce & Gabbana campaign video, Cyrus poses in a sultry all-black look and shares the song “Imitate,” a track from Morando’s band, Liily.

Cyrus offers rare details on her love story with Morando in an interview for Bazaar’s December 2024/January 2025 cover story.

“He’s very similar to me. We just don’t take life too seriously,” she says.

While the two have a small age gap—Cyrus is six years Morando’s senior—she reflects on how their different upbringings have made them approach life differently.

“He looks at life really differently than I do,” she explains. “He grew up with a laptop. I had a desktop computer that I shared with my brothers and sisters. … Honestly, he’s raised our dog off Reddit. I’m like, Are you sure we’re supposed to be doing this? And he’s like, On Reddit it says blah, blah, blah.”

She also opens up about working with Morando on musical projects and how collaboration is her love language. “I worked with my dad forever. That’s how me and my ex-husband met each other. I’ve always worked with the people that I love,” Cyrus says. “And Maxx just inspires me so much.”

Morando and Cyrus are photographed behind-the-scenes at the 67th Grammy Awards, where Cyrus snags her third Grammy for her collaboration on Beyoncé’s “II Most Wanted.” The appearance marks a rare joint public outing by the couple, who match in all-black ensembles.

Morando wears his usual red-carpet uniform to stand by his partner at the 97th Academy Awards. Cyrus wears a black gown with a distressed bodice embellished with sequins, which she wears with a curly hairdo and bleached brows.

Cyrus and Morando walk the red carpet for the Hollywood premiere of Avatar: Fire and Ash, and there, the singer debuts a chunky gold ring with a large, square diamond at the center.

Hours later, People confirms the two are engaged.

The engaged pair wear cool all-black looks to the show and look so in love as they embrace at their star-studded table during the ceremony.

Cyrus is sleek in a moto-inspired fit from Celine, while Morando goes low-key in a black T-shirt and pants.

Following their engagement news, a source tells People that the couple wants a “smaller and meaningful” wedding rather than a “huge, traditional” event.

“Maxx is extremely private, and that’s just not who he is,” the source says. “When they do get married, it will be something smaller and meaningful to them. Not a big Hollywood production.”

While it’s unclear when the Hannah Montana singer plans to wed her drummer fiancé, the source says Cyrus is enjoying this era and her engagement: “Miley really loves her life right now. Everything just feels like it’s falling into place for her.”

At the world premiere of the Hannah Montana 20th Anniversary Special in Los Angeles, Cyrus walks the red carpet alongside her fiancé.

For the occasion, the Disney Channel alum wears a plunging silver Rabanne dress layered over a bedazzled Hannah Montana tee, while her beau opts for a white tee and black jeans, which he pairs with black-and-white sneakers from Celine.

Cyrus is honored with a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame, thanks to her “lasting impact on the entertainment industry.” In reference to Cyrus’s work on Hannah Montana and more, Walk of Fame producer Ana Martinez notes: “Generations of young girls have grown up watching her evolve, finding inspiration in her journey and fearless creativity.”

At the ceremony, Cyrus is supported by members of her family like mom Tish and her older half-sister, Brandi. Meanwhile, Morando shows his support too, posing with Cyrus in front of the plaque.

Nearly a year after the pair reveal their engagement, TMZ reports that they will tie the knot with a secret ceremony in Southern California, scheduled to take place on the weekend of November 20.

Although other details are kept under wraps, Cyrus is seen running errands—like like getting her nails done—ahead of the ceremony.

She seemed to have been teasing the nuptials a month prior, when she brought out Morando during her Hollywood Bowl shows to do a surprise rendition of “Let’s Get Married.”
