Your Horoscope for the Week of November 22

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

After a rather quiet horoscope last week, this week brings lots of cosmic action. First, on Sunday, Sagittarius season begins, changing Scorpio's scheming energy for a more playful, impulsive tone. This will last for the next month and affect Sagittarius placements the most, as well as fellow fire signs Aries and Leo.

Also this week: On Tuesday, a Full Moon in Gemini brings a moment of culmination when it comes to communication. The next day, Mars, the planet of desire and action, moves from exuberant Leo into careful Virgo. For the rest of the year into February 2027, this transit encourages you to stop and think things over before acting, approaching conflict with a plan.

Read your Sun/Rising sign horoscope for the week ahead:

Get out there, Aries. Sagittarius season begins on Sunday and asks you to spend the next month exploring—both literally, through travel (even if it's only a day trip to a nearby town), or metaphorically, through learning something new. On Tuesday, a Full Moon in Gemini asks you to share a truth with someone who needs to hear it. And on Wednesday, Mars moves into Virgo and helps you get your day-to-day schedule in order.

Meet the new Taurus. Sagittarius season helps you discover a whole new you, so spend the next few weeks thinking about—and working towards—who you want to become. Tuesday's Full Moon in Gemini shines on your chart's financial sector, helping you come to a realization about your money habits. And on Wednesday, Mars steps into Virgo and brings order to your creativity. Now's the time to take that great idea you have and turn it into something real.

Turn date night into date month, Gemini. Sagittarius season begins on Sunday and encourages you to focus on your "other half," whether that's a romantic partner, best friend, or creative collaborator. On Tuesday, the annual Full Moon in your sign asks you to celebrate how far you've come since last year. Then, on Wednesday, Mars moves into Virgo, helping you approach any family conflict from a calm, cool, and collected place.

Be well, Cancer. The holidays are often a time for indulging, but Sagittarius season begins on Sunday and reminds you to focus on your health. Meaning that maybe you should stick to one glass of wine at Thanksgiving instead of a whole bottle. On Tuesday, the Full Moon in Gemini helps you let go of an idea that's no longer working for you. Finally, on Wednesday, Mars enters Virgo and helps you talk through a disagreement with care.

Get creative, Leo. Sagittarius season begins on Sunday, giving you a burst of inspiration that lasts all month. Put these vibes to good use and experiment artistically through painting, writing, photography, dancing, etc—whatever seems like fun. Tuesday's Full Moon in Gemini gives you an opportunity to catch up with your friends. Make some time for happy hour or at least a phone call. Finally, on Wednesday, Mars moves into Virgo and helps you get your budget in order in time for the holidays.

Remember your childhood, Virgo. The Sun moves into Sagittarius on Sunday, and for the next month, you're feeling nostalgic. Return to your favorite activities, books, movies, and songs from childhood, and of course, this is a good time to catch up with family or old friends, too. On Tuesday, the Full Moon in Gemini is a great opportunity to ask for a promotion or apply to a new job. Finally, on Wednesday, Mars enters your sign and gives you an extra boost of confidence that'll last til February.

Don't put your foot in your mouth, Libra. Sagittarius season begins on Sunday and makes you more outspoken for the next month—but you might accidentally say the wrong thing the wrong way. Tuesday's Full Moon in Gemini is the perfect time to share those photos or post that TikTok video from your last vacation. Then, on Wednesday, Mars enters Virgo and helps you cut someone or something off for good.

How's your budget, Scorpio? Sagittarius season begins on Sunday and puts the cosmic spotlight on your finances. You might be tempted to make impulse purchases right now, so keep a close eye on your spending habits. On Tuesday, a Full Moon in Gemini offers you a moment to make a change. Finally, on Wednesday, Mars moves into Virgo and helps you connect with your friends—but watch out for group chat drama over the next few months.

Happy birthday, Sagittarius! It's your season, so spend the next month celebrating you. You can also treat Sunday—the day the Sun moves into your sign—like a mini New Year. Tell the universe your resolutions. On Tuesday, a Full Moon in Gemini shines on your closest relationship, whether it's with a romantic partner or best friend. Spend some time with them and let them know how much you care. On Wednesday, Mars moves into Virgo and helps you approach your career with a renewed sense of focus and determination. Go get that promotion/new job/raise/bonus/etc.

Look inside, Capricorn. Sagittarius season begins on Sunday, and your season is next. That means that this month is a time for rest, release, and quiet introspection before you become the main character. Tuesday's Full Moon in Gemini gives you a realization about changes you can make to your daily routine. Finally, on Wednesday, Mars enters Virgo and helps you plan your next vacation—or go on it.

Need an excuse to throw a party, Aquarius? Beginning on Sunday, Sagittarius season wants you to focus on your friends, and not just your bestie, but your whole social circle. Friendsgiving, a holiday party, or just a regular old happy hour—make sure you have some fun socializing on your schedule this month. On Tuesday, a Full Moon in Gemini gives you a burst of creative inspiration. And on Wednesday, Mars slides into Virgo and powers your personal transformation.

Work hard, Pisces. Sagittarius begins on Sunday and for the next month, the cosmic spotlight is on your career. If you do well, you could be rewarded with a promotion, a raise, or a bonus. But if you don't, that'll be noticed, too. Tuesday's Full Moon in Gemini has you feeling nostalgic. Go ahead and revisit your favorite memories tonight. Finally, on Wednesday, Mars moves into Virgo. If you're partnered, you might be feeling an extra surge of desire for your partner. If you're not, this isn't a good time for casual hookups; instead, spend some quality (platonic) time with your best friend.
