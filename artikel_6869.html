The 15 Beauty Products Our Editors Loved in November

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Including Bazaar-approved hair combs and our beauty director’s favorite $10 drugstore find

I’m always asked, “What are your favorite beauty products?”—and honestly, I find that question hard to answer. My role as Bazaar’s beauty director requires me to test hundreds, if not thousands, of launches yearly, so my favorites change daily as I discover what’s new. I’m not alone in this. My colleagues also have access to the best that beauty has to offer, not to mention that they have exceptional taste, which makes them keepers of so many must-have recommendations across skincare, haircare, body care, makeup, and more. The list below represents the top beauty products we all tried and loved in November, organized in one handy list. Happy shopping!

“I posted this hair mask on Instagram stories last week for pure love of the game. It’s a fantastic hair mask—it smells like the rose scent from the ‘90s, has a cute rose-shaped twist-up applicator, and makes my hair feel smoother than ever. Days later, I got dozens of DMs and texts from people who bought it because of me, thanking me for the recommendation. It’s really the best $10 you’ll ever spend.” —Jenna Rosenstein, beauty director

“Most lip masks are too thick and gloopy to want to wear anywhere but my bedroom. NYX’s version is not only adorable, but it’s a rich, moisturizing formula that clings to your lips, leaving a sheer rose tint you can wear out of the house like any other balm. It’s a winter staple for those of us who are chronically chapped.” —JR

“I love any body scrub that comes in a tube and not a jar. This is no exception. It gently scrubs away dead skin and hydrates at the same time, thanks to the glycerin-rich serum. It’s my favorite pre-shave treatment!” —JR

“I'm super picky with eye creams, but this lightweight serum leaves my eye area feeling nourished, brighter, and less puffy, which has been a godsend as I've been sleeping less with the busy holiday season.” —Katie Intner, beauty commerce and market editor

“In a sea of scalp serums, I love this serum specifically designed to nourish and strengthen strands. The bi-phase formula, a mix of water-based ingredients and oils, is super lightweight yet delivers long-lasting, protective moisture that withstands multiple days of hot-tool use. The formula contains nourishing oils, so I can easily skip a daily hair oil when lazy or in a rush. Plus, it works on wet or dry hair and doesn’t mess up my style when I use it the latter way.” —KI

“My skin drinks up this silky body lotion from U Beauty, and I will be using it on repeat until spring. The lotion first appears thick and tacky, but it melts into a buttery texture that leaves a gorgeous, dewy sheen on the skin.” —KI

“This beautiful brand, Machete, should be a go-to this holiday season. The barrettes, toothbrushes, and claw clips make perfect stocking stuffers. I am going to gift my friends this really chic comb that they can keep on their vanity or in their handbags.” —Lynette Nylander, executive digital director

“Mr. Cloud, Nette's first-ever fragrance collaboration, isn't your traditional floral scent. Along with tuberose, the sensual fragrance also contains notes of cold milk accord, blonde woods, cut grass, and skin musks that lend to its well-rounded freshness that smells familiar yet completely different from any perfume in my collection." —Tiffany Dodson Davis, beauty commerce editor

“From slick backs to wash-and-gos, The Doux's Honey Dip Honey Butter Gel is one of my hairstyling mainstays. Not only is its formula extremely flexible and hydrating, but it also has a medium hold that keeps frizz to a minimum, thanks to its blend of jojoba oil and cationic honey." —TDD

"Dior's tinted moisturizer is infused with SPF 20 and fragmented collagen for ultimate skin plumping and features a natural, skinlike texture that doesn't make it obvious you're wearing makeup. Whether I'm going for a no-makeup makeup look or full glam, I always find myself reaching for the Backstage Glow-Up Tint nowadays, especially since its blurring and practically mess-free formula also happens to be travel-friendly." —TDD

"There's rarely a day when I'm not double cleansing, so I'm very nitpicky when it comes to my cleansing balm or oil of choice—that first step makes all the difference. Thankfully, Prequel's cleansing balm makes the process simple, since a little goes a long way to break down the day's dirt, oil, and makeup without feeling heavy on my combination skin. Since the formula is optimized for sensitive skin, it's also noncomedogenic and fragrance-free, and it nicely leaves my skin feeling hydrated once I wash it off." —TDD

“I love adding a little luxury to my beauty routines to make them feel a little more special. This simple tortoise comb is so chic and elevates my hair care regimen. It looks pretty on my vanity and also makes a fabulous stocking stuffer!” —Olivia Alchek, senior designer

“I believe that your feet are too often an overlooked body part when thinking about a self-care routine. Pedicures aren’t the only way to give your little piggies some love! I feel like my feet are at their driest and most cracked in the winter, and this foot mask set is the easiest way to target that, all from the comfort of my home. I use the first mask to exfoliate and remove dry skin and calluses. Then, about two weeks later, I use the second mask for a boost of hydration, leaving my feet silky smooth. No salon required!” —OA

“There’s something about moisturizing my entire body daily that feels like a hassle or a daunting task. That’s why I appreciate any product that helps with my lazy beauty routine. A moisturizer in a spray bottle? Obsessed! I’m much more willing to use it because it's so easy to apply. The lotion itself is luxurious and leaves my skin soft and hydrated without feeling greasy. Plus, the scent is subtle but sexy. It’s become a new staple in my nighttime routine.” —OA

“It's really hard to do something new or something that stands out with candles. Every gorgeous concoction has already been done—or so I thought. Nicolehollis 1972 candle combines burnt rosewood, smoked patchouli, and geosmin. It's so beautiful now, flickering in my living room as the temperatures drop.” —LN
