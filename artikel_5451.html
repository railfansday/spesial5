Hyperliquid (HYPE) momentum is bullish again, trading at $90.36 following a recovery from its October correction. The latest price movement indicates buyers are regaining control, with HYPE breaking above several key moving averages and approaching crucial resistance levels.

After reaching approximately $97.50 in late September, HYPE experienced a correction toward the $75–$77 support zone. Buyers successfully defended this region, establishing a potential higher low within the broader bullish trend. The subsequent recovery has pushed prices above the $82–$85 resistance area, reinforcing the possibility of further upside continuation.

Technical indicators support the improving outlook. HYPE is trading above its short- and medium-term moving averages, while longer-term averages maintain an upward trajectory. The Relative Strength Index (RSI) has recovered toward 60–65, indicating strengthening buying pressure without signaling extreme overbought conditions.

Immediate resistance is located between $92.50 and $95, followed by the previous high near $97.50. A sustained breakout above these levels could open the path toward the psychological $100 milestone. On the downside, initial support stands around $85–$87, with additional demand expected near $80–$82.50.

The overall technical structure remains constructive, especially as HYPE continues to outperform its recent consolidation range. However, confirmation through sustained trading volume and daily closes above resistance will be essential. As long as HYPE maintains support above $85, the short-term outlook favors recovery. A rejection near $95 could trigger temporary profit-taking, but the broader bullish structure would remain intact unless prices fall decisively below $80.

XRP continues to experience technical weakness, trading near $1.2904 as the asset struggles to recover from its recent decline. Despite several attempts to establish bullish momentum, XRP remains below crucial moving averages, suggesting sellers continue to dominate the broader market structure.

Following a strong rally in late August that briefly pushed prices toward $1.70, XRP entered a corrective phase characterized by progressively lower highs. September and October trading activity revealed repeated failures to sustain upward momentum, with the latest rejection near $1.55 reinforcing the prevailing bearish structure.

The current price consolidation around $1.25–$1.30 represents a critical technical area. Buyers have repeatedly defended this region, but their inability to reclaim overhead resistance suggests limited conviction.

Technical indicators reflect this uncertainty. XRP trades below several important moving averages clustered around $1.34–$1.40, creating a significant resistance barrier. Meanwhile, the RSI remains near 40, indicating subdued momentum and relatively weak buying pressure. Trading volume has also declined compared with previous breakout periods, suggesting reduced participation.

Immediate resistance stands near $1.32–$1.35, followed by stronger resistance around $1.38–$1.42. A decisive breakout above these levels could improve market sentiment and potentially support a recovery toward $1.48–$1.55. However, losing the $1.25 support level would increase downside risks, exposing XRP to potential declines toward $1.20 and eventually $1.10.

The short-term outlook remains neutral to bearish until XRP successfully reclaims its major moving averages. While the current consolidation could provide a foundation for recovery, stronger buying volume and a confirmed breakout above $1.40 are vital to establish a more convincing bullish reversal.

Cardano remains under considerable bearish pressure, trading near $0.2015 following a sustained decline throughout October. The latest price action shows weakening buyer participation, with ADA approaching a critical support area after failing to hold its September recovery.

Following an extended downtrend during the first half of 2026, ADA established a local bottom near $0.14 in June before recovering toward $0.27 in early October. However, this bullish momentum proved unsustainable, as sellers regained control and pushed prices below several critical moving averages.

ADA currently trades below its major moving averages, with immediate resistance centered around $0.22–$0.23. The longer-term moving average near $0.24 reinforces the bearish structure, suggesting that any recovery could encounter significant selling pressure.

The RSI has declined toward the 30–35 area, approaching oversold territory. While this may create conditions for a short-term technical rebound, the indicator has yet to confirm a meaningful reversal. Meanwhile, declining trading volume indicates limited buying interest at current levels.

The immediate support zone stands between $0.195 and $0.20. A decisive breakdown below this area could expose ADA to further downside toward $0.18, followed by stronger historical support around $0.16–$0.17. Conversely, reclaiming $0.22 would represent an initial improvement in short-term momentum, while a sustained breakout above $0.24 would be necessary to challenge the prevailing bearish trend.

Overall, ADA maintains a cautious technical outlook. Although oversold conditions may encourage short-term recovery attempts, the wider market structure remains bearish until buyers establish higher lows and reclaim key resistance levels.

However, the subsequent correction toward $1,100 highlighted increased volatility and considerable profit-taking. Since then, ZEC has entered a consolidation phase, fluctuating mostly between $1,150 and $1,350 as buyers and sellers try to establish a new equilibrium.

This positioning indicates that the recent correction may represent consolidation within a broader bullish cycle rather than a full trend reversal. The RSI has stabilized around the neutral 50 level, reflecting balanced momentum and providing room for another potential upward movement.

On the downside, initial support stands near $1,200, followed by the $1,100–$1,150 demand zone. A breakdown below these levels could trigger a deeper correction toward $1,000.

Overall, ZEC maintains a cautiously bullish technical outlook. Holding above $1,200 would support further consolidation and potential recovery, while reclaiming $1,350 would provide stronger confirmation that buyers are regaining control.
