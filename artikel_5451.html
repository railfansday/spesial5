Midwest Transplant Network and InVita Partner to Advance Organ, Eye and Tissue Donation and Transplantation with implementation of iTransplant

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

WESTWOOD, Kan., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Midwest Transplant Network (MTN), a federally designated organ procurement organization (OPO), and InVita Healthcare Technologies today announced a new partnership following MTN's selection of iTransplantÂ®, InVita's comprehensive software platform for organ, eye and tissue donation operations. The partnership brings together two organizations committed to advancing donation and transplantation through technology, innovation and operational excellence.

MTN serves Kansas and the western two-thirds of Missouri, partnering with more than 240 hospitals and five transplant centers to facilitate organ and tissue donation throughout its service area.

iTransplant will provide MTN with a market-leading platform that centralizes donation workflows and critical information from referral through recovery and donor family aftercare, helping streamline operations and improve efficiency across its organ, eye and tissue donation programs. The platform's configurable, interoperable design connects systems, workflows and data across the donation process, giving teams and healthcare partners the visibility and secure access needed to make timely, informed decisions. By strengthening coordination across MTN's donation network, the platform will modernize partner collaboration to help accelerate its lifesaving work.

The partnership builds on MTN's long-standing record of innovation and results. Consistently among the nation's top-performing OPOs, MTN is a Tier 1A organ procurement organization, rated by the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services. In 2025, MTN recovered 1,080 lifesaving organs for transplant from 371 donor heroes, and recovered eyes and tissue from 1,682 donor heroes.

"Midwest Transplant Network's success is because of our commitment to organ, eye and tissue donation, the care we give to our donor heroes and their families, and trust and collaboration with the partners that help us provide safe, high-quality care," said Lori Markham, RN, MSN, CCRN, CPTC, President and Chief Executive Officer, Midwest Transplant Network. "We chose InVita because they understand our work and the people behind it. Together, we can better serve our hospital partners and the donor heroes and families whose generosity gives hope and shares life."

As a new InVita partner, MTN is the latest leading OPO to select iTransplant, joining a growing community of donation organizations using the platform to support mission-critical operations. Used by more than 25,000 medical professionals globally, iTransplant continues to evolve through peer collaboration, best-practice sharing and co-development with the donation and transplantation community.

"We're so honored to partner with MTN, an organization that has continuously set a high standard for what an OPO can achieve," said Wade Liu, President, Human Biologics, InVita Healthcare Technologies. "We look forward to working alongside MTN's team to build on that record and reach new heights together in our shared mission to save and heal more lives."

InVita Healthcare Technologies delivers the world's most comprehensive human biologics software solutions managing blood, organ, and tissue workflows, enabling unmatched visibility and compliance to improve patient outcomes and save lives. Every day, healthcare organizations rely on InVita's software to carry out their missions and support the communities they serve. For more information, visit invitahealth.com.

Midwest Transplant Network saves lives through organ, eye and tissue donation. Serving Kansas and western Missouri since 1973, Midwest Transplant Network is a federally certified, not-for-profit organ procurement organization. Midwest Transplant Network works with organ, eye and tissue donors, their families, hospitals, and other professional partners to extend legacies, provide hope and give life. Its central office is in Westwood, Kansas, with satellite offices in Wichita, Kansas, and Columbia and Joplin, Missouri. Midwest Transplant Network commemorated 50 years in 2023, and consistently ranks in the top 10 of organ procurement organizations in the country.
