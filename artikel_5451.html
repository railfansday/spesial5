A Seattle Startup Brings Your Pet to Life on Your Phone and Laptop With the Avatar Mirroring Their Every Move in Real Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The pet AI startup will demonstrate PettiLive! at CES 2027.

SEATTLE, Nov. 21, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- PettiChat, the Seattle-based pet AI startup behind the world's first real-time AI pet translator, today announced the launch of PettiLive!, a digital version of each pet that lives in the PettiChat app and on the computer, mirroring the pet's real life as it happens.

PettiLive! is a digital twin of your pet. Your pet wears a small PettiChat device that tracks what it does throughout the day â running, walking, standing, eating, sleeping, jumping, cleaning itself, and calling out. PettiLive! brings those behaviors to life as a digital pet on your screen, mirroring them in real time. When your pet naps, PettiLive! naps. When your pet runs across the room, PettiLive! runs too. Every movement is driven by your pet's actual behavior. It is your own pet â its real life, brought into a digital presence.

Because PettiLive! is built around your pet's real behavior, every digital twin is unique. It continuously learns your pet's habits: the longer your pet wears the device, the more PettiLive! behaves like your pet. Open the app to see what your pet is doing right now and how its day is unfolding â including activity, meals, rest, and sleep, all measured against its own normal patterns. Whether you are at work, traveling, or simply in the next room, your pet's daily life is right there on your screen.

PettiChat's vision: the AI gateway for pets

PettiChat is building the AI gateway for pets, powered by its own pet AI model, PettiWorld. Translation is the first feature PettiChat delivers.

But the vision extends beyond translation: Pet Wellness, built around the pet itself. By tracking eating, playing, sleeping, and daily activity over time, Pet Wellness aims to surface meaningful changes in routines and behavior that owners should pay attention to. PettiLive! is a step toward that vision. The same always-on record of a pet's life that powers PettiLive! also lays the foundation for longitudinal pet wellness.

One model powers both experiences. PettiWorld, PettiChat's self-developed multimodal model for pets, translates pet vocalizations and behaviors into human language while also powering PettiLive!. Trained on 60M+ behavior data points and 10M+ vocalization data points, the model continues to improve â allowing both translation and PettiLive! to get better after launch.

"Owners have always wondered what their pets' days look like when they are not there," said Anodime Wen, co-founder of PettiChat. "A camera shows you a room; a tracker shows you a dot. PettiLive! shows you your pet, living its day. That is what it means to build the future of pet care."
