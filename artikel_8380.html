2% inflation adjusted being safe has many fans, including Dr Bernstein. That does not mean it is guaranteed. If the portfolio is a conventional combination of stocks and bonds there is risk. How much risk going forward is impossible to quantify. We know probabilities based on the past, we don't know the probabilities going forward.

Whether taking more risk than a US government guarantee is a good idea is a matter of individual choice.
Getting a guarantee on a portion of a portfolio can produce more risk than not getting a guarantee.

Fixating on the guarantee is a mistake.

I can't repeat the words used for people that think 2% is not safe, but you should go read them - straight from the source.
