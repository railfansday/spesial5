Reddit’s holiday goals guide offers helpful ad checklist

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The platform’s overview includes ideas on how to focus a seasonal campaign and insight into effective promotions.

Is Reddit on the radar for your brand marketing efforts this holiday season?

This could help. Reddit’s holiday guidance for marketers, which it published earlier in the year, offers helpful tips for brands looking to run ad campaigns this holiday season. The platform’s 16-page overview provides several pointers on the main requirements for Reddit promotions.

The report is a complement to Reddit’s Holiday Planning Guide, with the two covering all the key elements of effective Reddit campaigns.

Reddit’s Holiday Goals report includes insights into discovery trends in the app, and notes on the growth of Reddit as a conversion driver.

There’s also month-by-month breakdowns of what marketers should be focused on with their Reddit holiday campaigns.

As well as a checklist of key steps that advertisers need to take to run effective Reddit promotions.

These are some handy notes, which could help to guide more effective promotions on Reddit this holiday season.

And it could be worth considering. Reddit is now up to 515.2 million weekly active users, with many of these people coming to the app seeking out specific product insight.
