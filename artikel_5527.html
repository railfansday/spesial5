Goldman Sachs lowered its gold price forecast as Fed turns hawkish

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The bank's end-2026 target drops to $4,150 an ounce from $4,650 while traders see a 64% chance of a December Fed rate hike

Goldman Sachs $GS cut its gold outlook for the end of 2026 to $4,150 a troy ounce, down from $4,650, on Thursday, according to CNBC. The bank pointed to the possibility of additional rate increases from the Federal Reserve, according to MarketWatch.

Spot gold was down 0.3% at $4,076.91 an ounce on Friday, leaving it 5% higher on the week. U.S. gold futures with December delivery were off 0.6% at $4,088. Gold had slid as low as $3,869.85 an ounce on Oct. 30.

On Thursday, two Fed officials voiced support for modest, incremental rate hikes as a way to bring inflation, which remains above target, back under control, according to CNBC. Traders put the odds of a Fed rate increase in December at 64%.

Elevated energy prices can justify a tougher policy stance, which makes gold more expensive to own because the metal pays no yield, according to CNBC. Brent crude was trading near $100 a barrel on Friday.

Giovanni Staunovo, a UBS analyst, described an "extremely strong correlation again at the moment" linking crude, inflation and bullion, according to CNBC.

The U.S. nonfarm payrolls report for October is due at 8:30 a.m. ET on Friday. Investors are looking to the data for hints about where the Fed will take interest rates next, according to CNBC. A weaker labor market takes some of the heat out of the case for raising rates, Staunovo said.
