The IRS is raising 401(k) limits for 2027. But IRA savers get no higher limit

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The 401(k) catch-up limit for workers 50 and older rises too, while income ranges for IRA deductions and Roth eligibility increase

The Internal Revenue Service raised the 401(k) employee contribution limit by $1,000 for 2027, setting the cap at $25,500 versus the $24,500 that applies this year, the agency said on Thursday.

The same limit covers 403(b) plans, governmental 457 plans and the federal Thrift Savings Plan. The IRS issued technical guidance on cost-of-living adjustments for 2027 in Notice 2026-66.

The limit on annual contributions to an Individual Retirement Account remains $7,500, the IRS said. The IRA catch-up limit for people 50 and older remains $1,100.

The catch-up limit for most 401(k), 403(b), governmental 457 and Thrift Savings Plan participants 50 and older rises to $8,500 from $8,000. Those participants can contribute up to $34,000 a year starting in 2027, the IRS said. Under SECURE 2.0, employees aged 60 to 63 in these plans have a higher catch-up limit of $11,750 for 2027.

"Along with the Working Families Tax Cuts, this increase in the pension limitations will help American families keep more of their hard-earned income," IRS Chief Executive Officer Frank J. Bisignano said in a statement.

Income ranges that determine eligibility for deductible traditional IRA contributions, Roth IRA contributions and the Saver's Credit all increased for 2027. For single taxpayers covered by a workplace retirement plan, the traditional IRA deduction phases out between $83,000 and $93,000, up from $81,000 to $91,000 for 2026. For married couples filing jointly, where the spouse making the IRA contribution has a workplace plan, the range is $133,000 to $153,000, up from $129,000 to $149,000.

The Roth IRA contribution phase-out range for singles and heads of household rises to $158,000 to $173,000, from $153,000 to $168,000. For married couples filing jointly, it rises to $250,000 to $260,000, from $242,000 to $252,000.

The general contribution limit for SIMPLE retirement accounts increases to $17,500 from $17,000. The general SIMPLE catch-up limit for employees 50 and older remains $4,000.

The announcement arrives a week after the agency published a separate batch of cost-of-living updates for 2027, among them the tax brackets for federal income, according to CNBC.
