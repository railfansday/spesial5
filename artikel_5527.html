Schoox Named to Training Industry's Top 20 LMS Companies List for a Fifth Consecutive Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Training Industry again selects Schoox, the Intelligent Learning & Growth Platform for frontline enterprises as the LMS market evolves toward intelligent, connected platforms

AUSTIN, Texas, Nov. 5, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Schoox, the Intelligent Learning & Growth Platform for business performance, earned a place on Training Industry's 2026 Top Learning Management Systems (LMS) Companies list. It marks the fifth consecutive year Schoox has made the list. Training Industry evaluates providers on four criteria: features and analytics, market presence, client portfolio strength and business performance and growth.

"Frontline employees don't need a library of courses from headquarters. They need training that builds the skills they need to confidently perform in their positions," said Sandra Moran, Chief Marketing Officer of Schoox. "Recommending a learning platform is a career bet. A five-year record gives a learning leader precedent to cite, and our ROI forecast gives them a number to defend."

"This year's Top 20 Learning Management Systems (LMS) Companies illustrate how the LMS market is evolving beyond traditional learning delivery toward more intelligent, connected and action-oriented platforms," said Jalen Banks, market research analyst at Training Industry, Inc. "Leading providers are embedding AI throughout the learning lifecycle to automate workflows, deliver conversational experiences and support data-driven decision-making. At the same time, they are strengthening skills intelligence, expanding personalized and accessible user experiences, advancing analytics that connect learning to business outcomes, and building more open ecosystems that integrate learning into the flow of work."
