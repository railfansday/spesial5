Middlesbrough FC chief's Will Lankshear vow, injury update on midfielder and message to Tony Mowbray

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Lankshear's form has been the talk of the Championship this term and the 15-goal forward was badly missed in Boro's last two fixtures with a hamstring concern.

He is back in training and could return against Bristol City this weekend.

On Lankshear, Hellberg said: "I am not worried about him being distracted by that.

"Of course, there will always be interest from clubs for a striker with what he has done.

"But I think he is very committed to the work he's done here and I think his only aim is to try to finish as high as possible in the table and score as many goals as possible here for this year (season).

"You can see his commitment to try and do everything for Middlesbrough. He's been handling every question asked of him since the first day in a brilliant way and nothing has affected him."

Meanwhile, Hellberg confirmed that midfielder Rokas Pukstas will be out for 'probably 10 to 12 weeks' after undergoing hamstring surgery.

The Boro boss has issued a message of support to Boro legend Tony Mowbray, who has stepped down from his managerial duties at Blackburn Rovers amid health issues.

He said: "He's a brilliant guy. It's been good to meet him and he's been absolutely brilliant every time I've talked to him.

"We are wishing him all the best and hopefully he's soon back in terms of watching games, coaching again or whatever."
