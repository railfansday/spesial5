A face-off, France and Croatia – five Milan stars in international action tonight

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Tonight, AC Milan will have their eyes on the Nations League, with several of their representatives expected to take to the field.

It’s been a solid week for Milan in the international camps, with a few solid performances to note so far. Of course, the headline – at least currently – will be Christian Pulisic’s brace against Haiti last night, but Francesco Camarda has also impressed.

Tonight, the Rossoneri will hope to see more from their stars, with one game expected to earn more attention than the rest, especially if a debut is given.

The biggest night of the game will be Belgium vs. Italy, where Alphadjo Cisse has been called up for the game. If he makes a senior debut, it would add another crowning moment to an impressive season. Of course, he will face Diego Moreira too, who was fantastic in Belgium’s first game of the break.

Mike Maignan and Adrien Rabiot will also be hoping to improve their international break record, with a win against Turkey. After their loss to Italy, they need a big result in Bordeaux.

Finally, Luka Modric’s Croatia side face the Czech Republic in a game that they must win if they want to progress in the competition.
