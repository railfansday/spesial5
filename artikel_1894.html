Mauricio Pochettino has already had one wish granted as Manchester United manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Mauricio Pochettino has already had one of his first wishes granted as Manchester United manager.

The Argentine arrived in the north west on Wednesday, immediately boarding a plane from Cincinnati after his final match in charge of the United States national team to begin work at Carrington as soon as he touched down.

The former Tottenham and Chelsea head coach takes his new side to Anfield this afternoon to face Liverpool in a tricky first match at the helm.

The 54-year-old revealed in his introductory press conference on Friday that he wanted input in big decisions at United as his tenure wore on, and he has already had one particular request granted in his short spell in the hotseat.

In a round of quickfire questions with the club’s media team upon his arrival, Pochettino was asked whether he preferred team lineups to be formatted in shirt number or position order, with clubs having differing approaches to their pre-match graphics.

United have traditionally plumped for a shirt number-first approach, but when Pochettino’s first team selection was revealed ahead of the game against Liverpool, the Red Devils’ first XI was listed in position order.

It outlined that their new boss had gone for a classic 4-2-3-1 shape, listing Leny Yoro and Diogo Dalot as full-backs, and indicating that Matheus Cunha would play up front rather than Bryan Mbeumo.

United travel to Merseyside this afternoon mired in the Premier League relegation zone, winning just once in their last 11 games.

It was a streak that lost Michael Carrick his job, with the former midfielder sacked a week and a half ago after 303 days in charge.
