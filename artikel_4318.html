10 Pokémon That Are the Biggest Green Flags

There have been many Pokémon over the years. When you combine all the generations, there are over 1,000 different Pokémon, and each one is bound to be somebody’s favorite.

Of course, not every Pokémon conveys a positive message. There are some Pokémon, who, when mentioned as someone’s favorite, give off some major red flags.

On the other hand, there are Pokémon that also give off some major green flags. These are Pokémon that show you can trust a person. Maybe they are motherly, maybe they are a great friend, or maybe they are as innocent as can be. Either way, they are great to have around.

Mew is a classic Pokémon that is usually loved by everyone in the fan base, with few people considering it a negative sign. The reason for this is that Mew is just a little guy who is generally sweet towards others.

If you take one look at Mew, you can easily tell that it is a green flag. It looks absolutely harmless, and wouldn’t hurt a fly unless it was specifically required to do so.

With Mew being so harmless, anyone who chooses this Pokémon as their favorite is considered to be a sweet and empathetic individual who just wants to be around others because it makes them happy.

Mimikyu somewhat tows the line between a red flag and a green flag, but I am here to tell you that this Pokémon is for sure a green flag. The argument about it being a red flag is mostly because it is trying too hard to be Pikachu.

However, if you choose Mimikyu as your favorite Pokémon, it is probably because you have the emotional intelligence to identify with Mimikyu and recognize that it just wants to be loved.

It is a misunderstood creature who dresses up as Pikachu to hopefully one day get the love it rightfully deserves, which makes those who love this Pokémon people who recognize it for what it is, and realize that it is a lonesome creature who just wants to be seen.

Some Pokedex entries in the Pokemon series are pretty brutal, and these are the saddest.

The legendary beast trio from generation two are some of the most recognizable legendary Pokémon from the franchise. While each of these Pokémon are a green flag, I chose Entei to represent them because I feel like it is the most well-liked.

The legendary beasts are a great pull from the second generation of Pokémon that are well-known enough that it isn’t weird, and they aren’t obscure enough that it feels like you are trying too hard.

Each of the legendary beasts are known for being protectors, but Entei has the greatest appearance of them, coming off as more of a large fluffy lion, giving it more of the guardian vibes that people who love this Pokémon may themselves embody.

Wooloo is a more recent pull from generation eight, as the Pokémon made its first appearance in Pokémon Sword and Shield. Those who have played these games know that Wooloo are found in plenty of places early in the game, with its white wool covering the fields of Paldea.

Wooloo is an innocent little buddy who is calm, doesn’t want to go into a fight, and would rather just chill. Its little pigtails that hang along the sides of its face make it even more approachable.

Those who love this Pokémon are perceived as loving, calm, and big softies who wouldn’t want to fight unless it's absolutely necessary. The only one that is a red flag is Hop’s Wooloo, and that’s only because it beat me in the game that one time.

Every pocket monster in Pokopia is beautiful in its own way, but some are cuter than others.

Teddiursa has been around for a long time. In fact, it has been in the games since generation two, meaning fans have had a long time with this Pokémon, and there are some who consider it their favorite.

You’ll notice that many of the Pokémon on this list match the theme of “little guys,” and that is because they are so sweet and innocent that you don’t need to worry about them. Teddiursa is one of them, where it just wants to play around and eat some honey.

Those who love Teddiursa have a cute and playful side. They are also generally viewed as non-toxic because they would rather just have fun and cozy up by the fire with a good book.

There is a theme of Pokémon who are considered the “derpy” ones, and Fuecoco falls very specifically into that category. It has a face that screams, “I just want to sit around and snack all day without worrying about stressful things.”

Fuecoco is the definition of someone who is ignorant to the world around them and is blissfully going about life at their own pace. They have little care in the world and that is perfectly fine because it makes them happy.

There’s little that could be considered wrong with someone who likes Fuecoco. Maybe they are a bit too childish at times, but they are overall just a good person who lives the way they want.

Lapras is one of Ash’s Pokémon from the anime, and a Pokémon that stands out from the first generation. It is cute, calm, and collected, meaning it is definitely a green flag Pokémon.

If you judge the Pokémon from its Pokédex entry, you will find that it has a gentle heart, which led to many of them being caught because they rarely fight. Plus, it loves to ferry people around on its back, showing that you pride yourself on being there for others.

Additionally, Lapras is an overall intelligent Pokémon that is capable of understanding human speech, and those who choose it as their favorite Pokémon are often showing that they are caring, intelligent, and emotionally available friends who just want to be there for their pals.

There are some Pokémon where it feels like you chose it to be cool or to follow a trend. Cough. Charizard. Cough. Then there are some Pokémon where you love them despite the popularity, accepting the fact that they aren’t the best in terms of stats, but rather, they are just cute.

Eevee is one of those cute Pokémon that is beloved by everyone, and some choose Eevee as their favorite because of how adorable it is. Also, an argument can be made about its potential, with those who love this Pokémon showing that they believe they have great potential in life in the same way Eevee does through its evolutions.

This is a safe, friendly, and easy pick for many. It may be a bit too basic for some, but the cute factor solidifies it as a top-tier green flag.

Psyduck is a great choice for a favorite Pokémon, and is a major green flag. I am totally not saying that because it is one of my favorites, but rather because it is relatable.

This Pokémon suffers from constant headaches, which many can relate to because of the stresses of life constantly feeling like they're weighing them down. Psyduck feels like the embodiment of what is in people’s minds when dealing with stress, and it feels like an outlet in a sense.

Plus, Psyduck has some of the cutest expressions, with it often conveying confusions, annoyance, and more. If you love Psyduck, you probably aren’t toxic, have a decent sense of humor, and can be a bit childish. Now, I will pat myself on the back.

The biggest green flag of the entire Pokédex belongs to two Pokémon, and they are Wooper and Quagsire. Since I can only choose one, I went with Quagsire. After all, it is just a bigger version of Wooper.

We need to give those who love Quagsire a big hug and thank them for being some of the most chill and down to Earth people out there, as this choice of favorite is one that shows that they have the ability to just go with the flow, accepting life as it is.

Quagsire is the definition of a Pokémon that just doesn’t care about the stresses of life and lets them slide off its body and into the water it floats around in. Thanks for being such a special soul Quagsire, and keep up that chill lifestyle.
