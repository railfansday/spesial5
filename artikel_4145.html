This JRPG Series Lightly Inspired by the Console Wars Is Finally Getting a New PS5 Sequel

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Hyperdimension Neptunia intrigued me when it first released on the PS3, because its cast of characters were loosely inspired by the various console platforms, like PlayStation, Xbox, and (at the time) Wii.

The series gained a cult following and spawned dozens of sequels, spin-offs, and re-releases. But there hasn’t been a new mainline entry since 2015.

That changes in 2027 with the release of Hyperspace Neptunia: Unlimited on the PS5 and PS4, a full-blown sequel which first debuted in Japan earlier this year.

This version promises the most “expansive” magical girl action ever, with new mechanics and deeper dungeons.

“A whole new adventure has opened up for the Goddesses where dungeons are more expansive than ever. Goddesses can seamlessly switch from running to flight to explore new heights. Explore waterways, rooftops, and other crevices to duke it out with enemies and even find special items that can help you along the way.”

The press release also notes that there’ll be more opportunities for personalisation than in past titles, allowing you to “change your favorite Goddesses’ colors, attach fun accessories wherever you like, strengthen Processor Unit equipment found in dungeons that boost stats, and craft with the classic Disc Make that helps Goddesses achieve new abilities”.

There’s no specific date for when this title will deploy in the west outside of a vague 2027 window, but physical versions will be available for both PlayStation consoles in North America. (In Europe, only the PS5 version will be getting a boxed release.)

Do we have any NepNep fans in our ranks? I actually only ever played a little bit of the first one which was undoubtedly scruffy, but I know there’s a really strong following for this series which has kept it active for so long.
