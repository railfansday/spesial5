perkins&will and estúdio campana wrap são paulo airport lounge in amazon river-inspired timber installation

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

inside terminal 3 of são paulo’s guarulhos international airport, perkins&will and estúdio campana design the 1,400-sqm pedra preta lounge, anchored by a curved timber entrance installation modeled on the amazon’s juruá river and espírito santo stone interiors.

Perkins&Will edited by christina vergopoulou November 19, 2026 3 min

The spatial progression begins at the entrance, where a sculptural timber installation by Brazilian practice Estúdio Campana, modeled on the meandering curves of the Amazon’s Juruá River, wraps the reception area to form a physical and acoustic threshold from the surrounding terminal. Inside, Perkins&Will studio employs curved transitions, layered lighting, and framed sightlines to delineate zones while maintaining orientation toward departure gates. The material palette pairs natural stone quarried in Espírito Santo with warm timber, textured wall finishes, and custom-designed furnishings that integrate built-in architecture with furniture. A curated selection of Brazilian modern and contemporary artworks is embedded throughout the circulation path rather than isolated in gallery displays, complemented by a hospitality program featuring menus by chef Luiz Filipe Souza and wine pairings curated by sommelier Juliana Carani.
