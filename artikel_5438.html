Hyperliquid (HYPE) Adds 680% in Volume: Analyzing Record-Breaking Surge

Hyperliquid (HYPE) has seen a sharp rise in trading activity, with volumes jumping more than 680% across major exchanges. The spike was followed by a price recovery that pushed HYPE above $91, raising questions about whether the cryptocurrency is gearing up for another major rally.

According to CoinGlass data, HYPE is trading around $91.41, up 15.22% over the past 24 hours. Futures trading volume has reached $13.47 billion, while spot volume sits at roughly $1 billion.

Binance leads trading activity with $3.89 billion in daily volume, a 789.13% increase. Meanwhile, Hyperliquid's native exchange recorded $3.06 billion, reflecting a 738.72% jump.

Open interest currently stands at $4.02 billion, pointing to heavy participation in the derivatives market.

That futures activity could bring liquidation risks if prices suddenly reverse.

The daily TradingView chart shows a strong recovery from recent lows near $75, with HYPE reclaiming several key moving averages.

The latest bullish candle comes with a significant volume spike, suggesting growing market participation.

Meanwhile, the Relative Strength Index (RSI) has climbed toward 65, signaling strengthening momentum without entering traditionally overbought territory.

Immediate resistance sits between $94 and $98, where selling pressure appeared previously. A decisive breakout above this zone could open the door toward the psychologically important $100 level. On the other hand, failure to hold momentum could trigger a pullback toward $85, followed by stronger support around $80.

Despite the impressive price performance, derivatives flows aren't as healthy as they might appear. CoinGlass data shows roughly $32.57 million in liquidations over 24 hours, while futures net outflows reached $50.28 million over 12 hours.

HYPE's combination of rising volume, improving technical indicators, and renewed market interest supports a cautiously bullish outlook.

The $94–98 resistance zone remains the critical level to watch, with a confirmed breakout potentially determining HYPE's next directional move.
