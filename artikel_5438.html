AnnaLynne McCord Looks Back on the ‘Days of Our Lives’ Role That ‘Saved’ Her—Months After Her Backyard Wedding at Her L.A. Home

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

AnnaLynne McCord was still processing a call about her finances when her phone rang again. This time, it was the casting director of “Days of Our Lives” with a job offer.

In an interview with Hollywood Hills Magazine, the actress, 39, said joining the soap “saved” her. McCord had spent five seasons starring on “90210” after appearing on “Ugly Betty” and “Greek.”

By 2024, when the “Days” offer came along, she was worried about how she would cover her bills.

“I was told that I could not pay my credit cards. You work in this industry, and it’s sobering because you can be at the highest highs, and then things shift,” McCord told the magazine.

McCord said she had devoted much of her time to charity work. That morning, she found herself asking a question about her own future: “Am I going to be okay?”

“That afternoon, Marnie, the casting director of Days of our Lives, called me asking if I want to come on the show, and I said, ‘Yes, I do’, in more ways than she would ever know,” McCord recalled.

Looking back, McCord described that call as a “foundational moment of what Days would go on to offer me.” The job came along when she needed the work.

What surprised her over time was how much the people she worked with would come to mean to her.

“I feel at home here. I feel family here. I feel that people genuinely care about your well-being,” she told Hollywood Hills Magazine.

That sense of belonging has carried over into her life away from the set. McCord credited her castmates with showing her “genuine support,” including when she recently married British rugby player Danny Cipriani.

Five months before the pair said "I do" bohemian" wedding held in the backyard of the property, they purchased a picturesque $1.66 million Los Angeles home.

McCord and Cipriani, who had been in an on-again, off-again romance since 2016, tied the knot in front of 81 guests at their California property, telling People magazine at the time that they chose the intimate setting because it is "where we get to start our new life together."

As it turns out, the quaint two-bedroom, two-bathroom dwelling marks a fresh start for both the actress and the athlete. Records show that McCord closed on the home at the start of April—just under five months before her wedding and around four months before she faced bitter backlash for asking fans to contribute funds to their nuptials.

The couple, who have an estimated combined net worth of $8 million, created a crowdfunding website in July, which asked fans to donate money for different wedding essentials, including $100 for their marriage license and $1,200 for floral arrangements. There was also an option to gift the duo any amount between $10 and $5,000.

However, just a few weeks before that website went live, the couple made a rather large purchase of their own in the form of the property that would ultimately serve as the venue for their nuptials, which took place on Aug. 22.

The deed, according to records, is registered under only McCord's name.

Images shared with People magazine show the proud newlyweds posing in front of their new marital home, which was described in its listing as being located in a highly sought-after neighborhood in L.A.

The backyard, which was hailed as an "oasis," served as the perfect spot for the intimate wedding ceremony thanks to the high hedges that border the property, helping to shield it from prying eyes.

McCord confirmed the happy news of her marriage in an Instagram Reel posted on Aug. 24, in which she could be seen lying in bed alongside her new husband while gushing that marrying the "love of my life" felt like a "dream."

"I married the love of my life yesterday. And it’s real it wasn’t a dream. This love is a grace upon my being," she began.

"Every moment of this creation of this ceremony, every moment of ritual, every stolen glance, until we could just stand there holding hands, looking in each other’s eyes for a frozen moment that held the current of infinity," she continued.

The bride went on to describe the celebration as "incredibly timeless," while marveling at how perfectly the day had come together—and introducing herself by her new married name, AnnaLynne Cipriani.

"It’s all a blessing. The energies swirling around this wedding are all completely perfect. Not a foot put wrong. No missed step. Happy first day being AnnaLynne Cipriani to me," she said at the end of the video.

The joyous occasion also drew a star-studded guest list, with actors Josh Bowman, Emily VanCamp, Linsey Godfrey, Emily O’Brien, Mieko Hillman, and Jessica Minhas among those in attendance.

McCord's siblings, Rachel McCord and Angel McCord, were also on hand to celebrate the couple as they exchanged vows at home.
