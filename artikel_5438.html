Actian's Approach to Always-on Governance

Traditional governance relies on manual checks and centralized controls. This reactive framework identifies issues after the data is created and moved, which increases the risk of errors reaching downstream users.

Actian's contract-first governance approach embeds policies into data products with agentic stewardship to reduce manual enforcement work. As a result, policies are carried out at the pace of delivery, analytics teams and AI agents work from the same governed data, and business teams get consistent answers they can rely on.
