France team: Kylian Mbappé called up for the match against Italy

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

This Thursday, France challenges Italy in Milan for the 5th day of the Nations League at 8:45 p.m. A hell of a shock which had to happen without Kylian Mbappé. “Yes Kylian will be out tomorrow, he felt pain and we won’t take any risks. It’s unfortunate to lose players and that’s part of the game. I hope it’s not very serious and that we will have it on Sunday.announced his coach Zinedine Zidane at a press conference last night.

And if we shouldn’t expect to see him, Kylian Mbappé will be at the Giuseppe-Meazza stadium this evening. Indeed, Sky Italia reveals that the Real Madrid striker will be in the French group and should start on the bench. This does not mean, however, that he will come into play because he is slightly injured. Zinedine Zidane hoped yesterday to see him instead this Sunday against Turkey.
