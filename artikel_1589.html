Cisse claims he is ‘just getting back into it’ and responds to idea of 20-goal target

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Alphadjo Cisse has praised head coach Ruben Amorim, and he insisted that he is ‘just getting back into it’ after his injury.

Things did not start well for Milan against Olympiacos as they fell two goals behind, something that they had already done on three occasions in the Europa League. A Lorenzo Pirola header (given as a Mike Maignan own-goal) and a Jota Silva strike put the hosts in command.

Then came 15 minutes of madness. Omari Hutchinson scored a lovely solo goal to get the Rossoneri back into it, then he set up both Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic to complete the turnaround, before Diego Moreira sealed the deal in the dying seconds.

Cisse spoke to the reporters in the mixed zone following the conclusion of the game to shed some light on what was another positive personal night in a winning effort, boosting the hopes of knockout round qualification. MilanNews relayed his words.

“We didn’t have a good first half. Coming back from half-time with a deficit wasn’t easy, but we had a good second half and we’re happy to have brought home these three points.”

“Scoring a goal is definitely always nice. I’m not happy with my performance today, but we’re taking home the goal and the victory. I could have done much better in the first half.”

What was the morale like after their second goal and now at the end of the match?

“It’s never easy to be behind, but we have a strong team: turning things around isn’t easy, but it’s always nice. We’re continuing the work we’ve been doing.

“We’re very happy, let’s say we’ll be traveling a little more relaxed, but starting Monday we have another battle we need to win.”

“It’s November. But I’m still coming off a season where I was sidelined for four or five months, so let’s just say getting back into it, even though it’s November, isn’t easy. But I’m working towards it.”

“The Europa League is definitely a great competition. We’re a strong team and we hope to score even more.”

“No, I don’t have that target. Maybe I won’t tell you, we’ll see at the end of the season and take stock.”

“It’s great working with the coach and his staff. They help us a lot, we’re growing, and his work, let’s say, is coming through.”

Do you believe you’ll qualify? Are you more relaxed?

“No, definitely not relaxed. We believed in it even before this match and we have to believe in it again, and we also have other matches where we definitely need to get points.”

Have you unlocked headers in your repertoire?

“It’s something new, let’s just say I’ve never done it in the past. I might have scored one headed goal in my career, this year there are already two, and we’ll see.”
