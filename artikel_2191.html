Celtic star’s agonising goal drought inspires ruthless online game

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Kasper Hogh’s struggles in front of goal have now inspired an online game where the aim is simple: try to make the Celtic striker score.

The only problem is it appears almost impossible.

Hogh is enduring a torrid dry spell for the Hoops, having failed to find the net in his last 15 appearances. It has now been 1,053 minutes since the £11 million striker last scored, against Kilmarnock back in August.

And someone has decided to make light of his misfortune by creating Hogh’s Hotshots: The Impossible Finisher.

Players take control of Hogh and attempt to fire the ball beyond the goalkeeper, only for an increasingly ridiculous selection of obstacles to get in the way.

Efforts can be saved by the goalkeeper, crash back off the woodwork, or fly harmlessly into row Z. At one stage, Hogh simply falls over while attempting to shoot. Even finding the back of the net does not necessarily guarantee success, with VAR capable of intervening to chalk the goal off.

Things have hardly been going Hogh’s way recently, although the timing of the game is perhaps unfortunate given his improved display against Kilmarnock on Sunday.

Hogh earned Celtic’s Man of the Match award after producing one of his more encouraging performances in recent weeks, impressing with his hold-up and link-up play.

The striker even thought he had finally ended his agonising wait for a goal with a well-taken finish at Celtic Park. His celebrations were cut short, however, when VAR intervened, and the goal was disallowed because of a foul in the build-up.

The drought therefore continues, but Sunday provided genuine encouragement that Hogh could finally be edging closer to ending it.

After 15 games without a goal, Celtic will hope the real-life Hogh proves a lot easier to get scoring than his virtual counterpart.
