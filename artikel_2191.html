Why James Morrison and West Brom fans are tipping 26-year-old star for a Premier League rise as impeccable form continues

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

West Brom sit 5th in the table after a 3-0 win over Millwall on Saturday made it six games without defeat. Isaac Price and Jayson Molumby made the headlines as they both scored one and set up another, but it was a fantastic team performance from WBA who strengthen their promotion credentials with every passing match.

Although not a regular on the scoresheet, one man never far away from praise is Ousmane Diakite. And, once again, the Malian midfielder was excellent on Saturday, playing the full 90 minutes in midfield as the Baggies stormed to victory.

Focusing on the win over Millwall, Diakite was key in his usual role of winning duels, guarding the backline and keeping the ball moving. Primarily operating on the left of the midfield two, the West Brom man completed 51 of his 58 passes, five of which were into the final third while one created a chance.

In duels is where the 26-year-old found most of his success though, as has been the case all season. Diakite won seven duels in total: six on the ground and one in the air. He also completed three tackles, made two interceptions and four recoveries (FotMob).

This latest effort contributed to his eye-catching totals across the Championship campaign to date. No central midfielder has won more than his 92 duels, and he ranks amongst the league’s best in his position for aerial duels won (28 – top 2%), clearances (44 – top 2%) and defensive contributions (98 – top 3%).

On the ball, WBA star Diakite has completed 595 passes (top 9%), made 86 line-breaking passes (top 23%), made 13 successful dribbles (top 8%) and won 23 fouls (top 4%). As far as his stats go, he has been one of the Championship’s most effective midfielders of the season so far in all key areas apart from chance creation and goalscoring with those responsibilities falling to Albion stars Price, Jimmy-Jay Morgan, Aune Heggebo and more.

After a challenging start in a West Brom shirt, Diakite proved his worth in last season’s relegation fight under James Morrison and deservedly earned a new deal. He has got better and better in the 2026/27 campaign, and is a crucial part of an Albion side that looks ready for a promotion challenge.

In the build up to the Millwall game, Baggies boss Morrison was asked whether Diakite was a Premier League player in the making. Speaking to reporter Steve Tudgay, he said: “Yes he is. With us!”

Similarly, former West Brom man Paul Robinson recently told the Express and Star: “He has Premier League attributes, when you look at him”.

Not only do Diakite’s stats back this up, but so do the rave reviews of WBA supporters. Over the past week or so, numerous fans have shared their belief in his potential on X:

Ousmane Diakite WILL be a premier league footballer. Hopefully with us but time will tell #wba

Diakite walks into a bottom half Prem team all day for me #wba

Diakite could get into the Man united midfield all day, his improvement since joining has been unreal to watch #wba

West Brom fans will be hoping Diakite’s form continues. If it does, he may well get his deserved step up to the top flight at The Hawthorns.
