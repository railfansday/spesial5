Sarah Pidgeon Ticks Off Two of Winter’s Biggest Outerwear Trends in One Viral Coat

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It’s been a banner year for Sarah Pidgeon’s outerwear wardrobe. The actor, who made her debut on Vogue’s Best Dressed list this week, has spent much of 2026 investing in a really good collection of coats. And while playing Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy in Love Story has furnished Pidgeon with an extensive knowledge of the late fashion publicist’s preferred ’90s silhouettes, her own tastes are often a little more irreverent and varied.

Taking in a Knicks and Cavaliers game at Madison Square Garden in New York this week, Pidgeon ticked off two of the season’s biggest outerwear trends—animal print and flourishes of faux fur, as well as the sophisticated standing collar shape—in one covetable coat. The actor wore the Dolly Maxi by Carelli, an elongated iteration of the label’s cult jacket, with cream-and-black spotted, pony hair-effect fabric, a jaw-grazing collar, and fluffy chocolate-brown cuffs. Pidgeon let the coat do most of the talking, adding straight-leg blue jeans, pointy black boots, and her trusty black Balenciaga Le City bag, which she clutched under her arm.

Carelli is one of the runaway fashion success stories of 2026. Founded by Anna Carels in early 2025, the brand has amassed a celebrity clientele that includes Dua Lipa, Olivia Dean, and Peggy Gou, as well as legions of fans on TikTok. As Carels told British Vogue earlier this month, the original Dolly sold out in under an hour. The designer’s penchant for old-school glamour chimes with a broader appetite for rather louche, vintage-inflected outerwear: See Conner Ives’s decadent fur-trimmed creations and Charlotte Simone’s always sell-out fuzzy numbers, as well as the proliferation of vintage silk opera coats on everyone from Kendall Jenner to Hailey Bieber and Jennifer Lawrence. After several seasons of austere and minimalist coats, we’re all in the mood for something a little more flamboyant. (Vogue’s predictions for the hottest coats of the coldest months says just as much.)

Pidgeon is no stranger to the timeless pleasures of a leopard-print coat. During her Love Story press tour, the actor stepped out in Adam Lippes’s leopard chenille Margaux coat in a subtle nod to Bessette-Kennedy’s own affinity for animal print. She’s also made a habit of punctuating her more pared-back outfits with leopard-print accessories.

Elsewhere in her enviable outerwear rotation? A collarless leather trench from Aflalo, a sculptural peplum jacket from Celine, and the supple Prada leathers she sported during fashion month.

Working with stylist Emma Jade Morrison, Pidgeon has struck an interesting balance between the restraint that characterized CBK’s wardrobe and her own more adventurous inclinations. She has an eye for younger labels, too, and a knack for assimilating their more idiosyncratic designs into an otherwise unfussy wardrobe of great denim, white tees, and ’90s-coded accessories. The Carelli coat feels especially at home among them. Consider Sarah Pidgeon’s latest outing some inspiration: swap out that sensible black winter coat in favor of something with a little more personality. Match point!
