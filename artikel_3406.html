3 Best New HBO Max Series To Binge-Watch This Weekend (November 20–22, 2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The biggest new release this weekend on HBO Max is also the most popular on the prestige streaming platform right now, delivering what critics are calling its best season yet, as evidenced by its near-perfect 96% Rotten Tomatoes score. What's more, ScreenRant has updated its binge-watching guides for major platforms like Netflix, Prime Video, and Paramount+, keeping you entertained all weekend long.

Crown of Deceit: Inside the Miss Universe Scandal is one of HBO Max's most-viewed new docuseries heading into the weekend, currently ranking #9 on the streamer's U.S. streaming chart. Consisting of just 3 episodes, all 42 or 43 minutes each, this docuseries has a runtime of just 2 hours and 8 minutes, making it easy to start and finish in just one sitting. Crown of Deceit: Inside the Miss Universe Scandal arrived on HBO Max last Thursday, November 12, and is trending ahead of the 75th Miss Universe pageant ​​​​​taking place on November 24.

As its title indicates, Crown of Deceit: Inside the Miss Universe Scandal follows several prominent contestants in previous Miss Universe and Miss USA pageants, who collectively reveal instances of misconduct, including rigged outcomes and elaborate NDA agreements, along with industry insiders and whistleblowers. The series also examines the headline-making 2025 Miss Universe pageant that was held in Thailand, which included one contestant and one judge withdrawing from the competition. If you're planning on watching the 2026 Miss Universe pageant next week, this is a can't-miss series on HBO Max this weekend.

War is once again one of the best new series to binge-watch on HBO Max heading into this weekend, with its season 1 finale episode arriving on Thursday, November 19. Currently ranking #3 on HBO Max's worldwide viewership chart, War is one of HBO's biggest hit series of Fall 2026 and has gotten better with each new episode, with its most recent two episodes soaring to all-time highs on IMDb. There's no better time to binge War than this weekend now that all eight episodes of the acclaimed legal thriller are available to binge.

The ongoing divorce battle between Sienna Miller's Hollywood royalty character Carla and Dominic West's tech billionaire character Morgan finally reaches a resolution in this week's episode of War, as the already-confirmed season 2 will feature a new slate of characters who battle over a different legal dispute. With its 85% Rotten Tomatoes score, strong ensemble cast, and sophisticated tone that is similar to past HBO corporate dramas like Succession and Industry, War is HBO Max's best new series to binge from start to finish this weekend.

The Gilded Age is by far the most critically acclaimed new show to watch on HBO Max this weekend, given its 91% overall Rotten Tomatoes score across four seasons and its 15 Emmy nominations with one win. The Gilded Age season 4 has tied season 3 as the top-rated season in the franchise's history with an individual RT score of 96%, ensuring that the 34-part historical drama series is well worth the long-term investment. It's also HBO Max's most-watched series worldwide right now and is already confirmed for a fifth season.

HBO's acclaimed nine-episode sci-fi thriller delivers a complete, visually stunning mystery with exceptional performances and a must-watch conclusion.

The Gilded Age has some of the best production and costume design on streaming right now, set in 1880s New York City and centering on the clashing social warfare between old and new money classes. Carrie Coon delivers one of her career-best performances as Bertha Russell, the wife of a railroad tycoon who disrupts the status quo with aggressive financial moves that alter the framework of Manhattan's high society. Despite her ruthlessness toward the established social order, Bertha is incredibly likable, influential, and easy to root for, making The Gilded Age must-watch television this weekend when its fourth of eight season 4 episodes arrives this Sunday on HBO Max.
