Sorry Jason And Lucia, These 5 GTA 6 Side Characters Steal Every Scene They're In

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

From Brian to Yago, we loved interacting with these eclectic side characters

Although Jason and Lucia are the stars of Grand Theft Auto 6 and amazing characters in their own right, there are lots of side characters who steal the show whenever they show up. From folks critical to the main story path to weirdos you meet if you take the time to stop and chat with someone on the side of the road, GTA 6 constantly introduces wild, memorable characters almost every hour.

These side characters make the world of GTA 6 feel more fleshed out, lived in, and representative of all different kinds of kooky folk. They’re a big part of what makes the game special, and we’re thankful that Rockstar Games took the time to make us care about people other than Jason and Lucia. In particular, these five characters emerged as some of the Polygon staff’s favorites.

Brian is the single biggest scene-stealer in GTA 6 for one reason: Stephen freakin’ Root! For years, Root has been one of Hollywood’s ultimate “Hey, it's that guy” character actors. Now, fresh off an extremely well-deserved Emmy win for his role in Widow’s Bay, he brings the same kinetic, frenetic energy to Brian in GTA 6.

The eternal brilliance of Root’s acting comes from the humanity he injects into every character — often people who would be impossible to believe in lesser hands. Like many of Root’s best roles, Brian is a little too wacky for the real world, but there is a clear and nuanced sense of humanity underneath all of that eccentricity. That ethos is reflected throughout the entirety of Grand Theft Auto, and Brian is the key to selling it from the start. —Ari Notis

Of all the side characters Rockstar has introduced in GTA 6, Raul Bautista (voiced by Manny Pérez) is my personal favorite. He commands attention — not through loud bravado, but through quiet, seasoned authority. Raul carries himself around with a sun-bleached cool reminiscent of Don Johnson’s Sonny Crockett mixed with the criminal smarts of Robert De Niro’s Nick Wells from The Score. Raul isn’t the sort of guy who needs to announce himself or make a show of what he can do. As a seasoned bank heister who knows Vice City like the back of his hand, Raul operates with understated confidence and stays deliberately out of the spotlight, treating high-stakes scores like a craft rather than a spectacle.

Living on a boat also shows he’s calculated down to his quarters and can pull anchor and disappear on a dime whenever the heat closes in. What makes him especially intriguing is how little we know about his past. Who knows? He could’ve easily crossed paths with Michael Townley and Trevor Philips during their early heisting days — and maybe even learned a thing or two from them. — Ryan Epps

Midway through GTA 6, just as the heists are heating up, Jason becomes an accidental hip-hop mogul. In a series of missions, Jason is called upon to help promote Real Dimez, an up-and-coming rap duo managed by Only Raw Records. The group immediately steals the show the moment they first appear on screen. Played by real Miami rappers Hood Brat and Krissy Celess, Bae-Luxe and Roxy add some welcome levity to the story as they try to get their big break with a ridiculous single about “booty holes.”

Their missions have Jason doing promo photo shoots, breaking into house parties, and shooting music videos as Real Dimez rise to stardom. In a game that’s almost entirely about complex criminal enterprises, it’s a reprieve to get a side-tale about two musical misfits looking for their moment the old-fashioned way. — Giovanni Colantonio

Jason’s Key Lento neighbor Shirley is a good, kind soul, if a slightly eccentric one. She has her own way of viewing the world, and while it’s certainly centered around a brand of logic unique to Shirley, it’s not a bad thing. She cares about people as much as she cares about her obnoxiously yappy little dog Arugula, and she’s genuinely hurt by the unnecessarily rude comments from Peggy (of “steal her scooter” fame).

Shirley is basically your odd-but-sweet neighbor who you might dread catching the attention of on your way home, but would never dream of showing that dread or turning down a chance to talk to, if only briefly. Hurting her feelings? Inconceivable. And it’s to GTA 6's credit that it encourages you to meet Shirley halfway and be kind to her without judging her oddities. — Josh Broadwell

When I pulled up to my first street race in Vice City, I was not expecting to kick off a whole Fast & Furious-like sidequest in the game. Early on, you meet Yago, a talkative and ambitious racer who just has one problem: he can’t drive very well. Yago takes an instant liking to Jason, who guides him through being a better street racer and drug dealer. What ensues is one of the best male friendships in the game — and one that you can entirely miss.

Yago and Jason’s dynamic is equal parts hilarious and endearing, as Yago constantly teeters on annoying but is so gung-ho and into Jason that you can’t help but love him. He also exposes a softer side of Jason that you otherwise only see through his interactions with Lucia. I won’t spoil how Yago’s sidequests ultimately play out, but that event ultimately recontextualizes his entire relationship with Jason and ends up making him one of the game’s more nuanced characters. — Tomas Franzese
