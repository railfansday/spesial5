Rams more likely to miss the playoffs than make the playoffs, according to betting odds

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Rams entered the season as the overwhelming favorites to win the Super Bowl. As the dust settles on the NFL trade deadline, the Rams are now not even favored to make the playoffs.

The latest betting odds have the Rams as -130 favorites to miss the playoffs, and +110 underdogs to make the playoffs.

That represents a massive shift from before the season, when the Rams were -500 favorites to make the playoffs, the best playoff odds of any team in the league.

The Rams are a disappointing 5-4, and if the playoffs started today they’d be a game behind the Packers for the third and final NFC playoff spot. They’re also 2.5 games behind the 49ers in the NFC West race.

Perhaps more importantly, injuries are mounting for the Rams, with the latest big one being that wide receiver Puka Nacua is heading to injured reserve, where he’ll join Davante Adams, as Matthew Stafford is running out of healthy receivers.

If the Rams get healthy and make the playoffs, they’ll be a major threat to any postseason opponent, which is why they still have the seventh-best odds in the league to win the Super Bowl at +1300.

But as the 49ers pull away in the NFC West, and the NFC wild card race looks increasingly competitive, the Rams’ chances of being in it in January are dwindling.
