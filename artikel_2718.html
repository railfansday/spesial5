Kyler Murray off injury report, likely to be No. 2 QB vs. Falcons

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Vikings quarterback Kyler Murray is officially off the injury report for Sunday’s game against the Falcons.

While he’s not going back to his starting role after Minnesota traded for Kirk Cousins, he probably going to be a snap away.

“Kyler got some good work in today. Took quite a bit of the reps this week, look-team wise, and feels pretty good,” head coach Kevin O’Connell said in his Friday press conference. “We’ll assess him where he’s at, but [he’s] likely to be the backup. Carson [Wentz] would be the No. 3.”

In his first season with the Vikings, Murray has completed 65 percent of his passes for 1,022 yards with five touchdowns and three interceptions this season in his six starts.
