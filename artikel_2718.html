David Njoku limited at Thursday's practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Los Angeles Chargers TE David Njoku (fibula) was a limited participant in practice Thursday, Nov. 12.

Why it Matters: Njoku has been sidelined since suffering an injury in Week 2, but he did have five catches for 58 yards in the season opener. If he does return to action in Week 10, his performance will be worth monitoring, but he is probably too risky to trust him after such along layoff.
