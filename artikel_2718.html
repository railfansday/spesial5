Week 12 NFL Power Rankings: Did the Vikings blowout move the 49ers up to No. 1?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The San Francisco 49ers routed the Minnesota Vikings 35-10 on Sunday Night Football in Mexico City, improving to 9-1 as they retained control of the No. 1 seed in the NFC.

In a battle between the No. 1 offense and the No. 1 defense in the NFL, the 49ers were well-prepared and came out firing in one of their most complete performances of the season.

Last week, in the Week 11 NFL Power Rankings, the 49ers were between No. 1 and No. 2 on most major outlets with the Buffalo Bills, who won once again this week, beating the Miami Dolphins 26-9 to improve to 9-1 as well.

Buffalo had beaten Minnesota 23-6 earlier this month on primetime. Will the 49ers dominant win sway opinions this week, or will the status quo remain the same? Let's see where San Francisco falls in the Week 12 power rankings.

The 49ers moved up to No. 1 in last week's ESPN NFL power rankings, as they beat the Cowboys while the Bills were on bye. They remained there this week, while the Bills stayed at No. 2.

Behind them, the Kansas City Chiefs jumped from No. 5 to No. 3 after their beatdown of the Arizona Cardinals. The Chicago Bears stayed at No. 4 on their bye week, while the Denver Broncos jumped from No. 7 to No. 5 with a 14-point win over the Raiders.

Just missing out on the top five were the Detroit Lions, who jumped four spots from No. 10 to No. 6 after beating the Tampa Bay Buccaneers by 10.

The 49ers moved up to No. 1 last week in the NFL's Week 11 power rankings and stayed there this week following the win over the Vikings.

"Of the 49ers' nine victories in 10 games, this rout of the Vikings was undoubtedly their most impressive," NFL.com's Nick Shook wrote. "Brock Purdy knew when to get rid of the ball and where to throw it against a Minnesota defense that had caused problems for every other quarterback it faced, delivering a pristine performance on the Sunday night stage in Mexico City.

"Mike Evans' return upgraded this offense and came at the perfect time, and San Francisco's defense is quietly becoming a bit of a monster up front thanks to the return of some previously injured members and the deadline addition of DeForest Buckner. This is unquestionably the best team in football through 11 weeks."

The Bills remained at No. 2, followed by the Broncos (who jumped up four spots), the Chiefs (who jumped up five spots), and the Lions (who jumped up three spots).

The 49ers jumped up from No. 2 to No. 1 on these power rankings after their win over the Vikings, as the Jacksonville Jaguars fell from No. 1 to No. 4.

"The 49ers have faced the No. 1 defense in DVOA (Seattle) and the No. 1 defense in EPA allowed per play (Minnesota)," Yahoo's Frank Schwab wrote. "They scored 32 points against the Seahawks and 35 against the Vikings. They got a defensive touchdown in each game but it seems like no defense can hold down the 49ers this season.

"While the offense has been fantastic, the 49ers wouldn't be the best team in football through Week 11 without their defense, which has excelled despite Nick Bosa not having a sack all season (he did have two quarterback hits on Sunday night). The 49ers have given up 17 or fewer points in six of their 10 games. They have given up fewer than 300 yards in six games too. Defensive coordinator Raheem Morris, who replaced Robert Saleh, is doing an excellent job."

Following the 49ers were the Bills, Bears, Jaguars, and Chiefs in the top five.

There was no movement on Pete Prisco's power rankings once again, as the Bills stayed at No. 1, while the No. 2 remained at No. 2.

"They are clearly the team to beat in the NFC and maybe the league. They get better each and every week. The loss of right tackle Colton McKivitz will be felt," Prisco wrote.

The Bears, Broncos, and Chiefs rounded out the top five, with each moving up one spot as the Jaguars fell from No. 3 to No. 8.

The Vikings win moved the 49ers up from No. 2 to No. 1 here, with the Bills falling.

"Sunday night's Mexico City beatdown was a statement win in every way—chief among them the statement that the 49ers are the NFL's best team," Bleacher Report's Gary Davenport wrote.

"This was supposed to be a titanic showdown between two of the NFC's top teams. The Niners apparently didn't get that memo, because they took control immediately and never gave Minnesota a chance to recover. On the first drive of the game, it took San Francisco less than four minutes to drive the length of the field on one of the NFL's best defenses. Just before halftime a pick-six by the 49ers defense put the game on ice.

"Brock Purdy is the NFL's MVP after 11 weeks. Given all the injuries the team has had to endure on both sides of the ball, Kyle Shanahan is the Coach of the Year."

Here, the Bears, Broncos, and Chiefs rounded out the top five as well.
