Bears at Lions: 3 Props to Play

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It's a short week, and we are jumping back on after a rough performance on Sunday against the Saints. We believed that the return of Kyle Monangai was going to spark the offense into some significant production. Every key player for Chicago went under, which puts us at another 0-3 mark. After finally rebounding and getting above water, we fall back to .500 at 15-15.

This week it's a short week against an excellent Detroit Lions team. How does this one play out? Let's take a look at 3 props to play this week. As always, odds courtesy of FanDuel.

This one might be a little risky, but I think this game is going to be close, and I'm counting on the Bears defensive line to heat up Goff a little bit. I don't think the Bears have a big lead where Goff totals like 150 4th-quarter passing yards. I think it'll be hard-fought, and Goff will get some pressure from Jeffery Simmons and company, and while I think he will throw for plenty of yards, I think 250 or 260 is probably closer to what we see from Goff on Sunday. But make no mistake, Amon-Ra or Jammo could have a couple of 50+ yard explosives, and this number will blow up in my face.

The Detroit Lions have struggled shutting down tight ends this year, and it makes sense with both their preseason starting linebackers on IR. The Lions are one of the worst teams defending tight ends, and while they have done better against tight ends the last couple of weeks, I think that's more about playing teams like the Dolphins and Bucs than about covering the position better. Loveland has a big number to go over this week, but I think he clears it. Ben Johnson knows how to exploit weaknesses, and I think we might see Loveland have his best game of the season.

I'm not sure how the running game for Chicago will look overall, but the Lions do a far better job containing runs between the tackles than outside. I think if Monangai is used inside and Swift outside like the Bears have done frequently over the last year and a half, I think Kyle might have another slow day, but Swift could have some success running off tackle and attacking the edges of this Lions defense. Swift's number is much lower than it's been due to the Lions' fairly stingy rushing attack, but I think Ben Johnson will stay committed to the run on Thursday to try to keep the Lions offense off the field, and Swift feels like the better matchup.
