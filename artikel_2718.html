4 takeaways from Broncos' snap counts in Week 11, including a change at guard

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Denver Broncos moved to 8-2 following their Week 11 win over the Las Vegas Raiders. Though it finished as a 14-point victory, it didn't come easily. The Raiders pushed the Broncos hard in the second half and Denver was able to come up with big plays when they needed them.

Jonah Coleman powered the Broncos' offense with a 100-yard rushing performance and three touchdowns, while a late pick-six by Ja'Quan McMillian provided the game's biggest play.

Looking at the snap counts from this game, the Broncos continued to do a great job of mixing in the right players while doing a good job of replacing players who are still out of the lineup with injuries. However, the biggest development of the day came along the offensive line.

The Broncos at point this season appeared to be slowly easing Jonah Coleman into the offensive game plan, but now, they can't shy away from riding the hot hand and turning the entire backfield over to him. Coleman saw almost every single snap on Sunday, 62, while J.K. Dobbins was only on the field for one. Cody Schrader had two.

Jahdae Barron has only seen significant work when either Pat Surtain II, Riley Moss, or both, have missed time this season, being relegated to a spectator when they're healthy. But the team gave Barron 13 defensive snaps against the Raiders, trying to get him out on the field as much as it could.

While the Broncos got one starting safety, Talanoa Hufanga, back on the field for this game, Brandon Jones still wasn't ready to return from a hamstring injury. In his place, the Broncos rotated between Devon Key (37 snaps) and Taylor Rapp (33 snaps) to replace him.

JL Skinner did not play any defense and was just in on special teams in this game.

Ben Powers has had his struggles as one of the Broncos' starting guards this season and on Sunday, the team chose to experiment with a partial change, getting Matt Peart out on the field in his spot. Peart saw more snaps (42) in the game than Powers (31), but what was equally surprising is that the Broncos chose to go with Peart over Alex Palczewski, the player they normally put into the starting lineup when someone is out.

It was an interesting move by the team, and it will be an interesting situation to keep tabs on, starting with the team's road game this Friday against the Pittsburgh Steelers.
