Auburn football's bowl projections entering Week 13

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Auburn Tigers have already clinched their bowl eligibility, it is just a matter of where they will be playing and who they will be facing. They have a chance to give their resume a major boost on Saturday when they play the Alabama Crimson Tide.

It will be a tough game for the Tigers, who have not won a road Iron Bowl since 2010. They will be coming into the game with some momentum, winning their last three games to improve to 7-4 overall and 4-4 in SEC play. It is their most conference wins since the 2020 season, when they only played SEC teams.

Alabama (9-2, 6-2) is on a four-game winning streak of its own and is poised to make the College Football Playoffs. The Crimson Tide are 5-1 at home this season and have won the last six Iron Bowls, improving their all-time record against Auburn to 52-37-1.

Here is a look at Auburn football's latest bowl projections ahead of the final weekend of the regular season.

USA TODAY Sports' Erick Smith is once again projecting Auburn to play the Baylor Bears in the Texas Bowl, setting up a rematch of the season-opener.

The Tigers started the Golesh era with a 17-16 win over the Bears, who elected to go for two and the win after scoring a touchdown with nine seconds remaining instead of kicking the extra point to likely send the game to overtime. While Baylor does not have a head coach, they would love to get revenge in their home state on Dec. 31.

ESPN's Kyle Bonagura and Mark Schlabach are split on what bowl Auburn will play in.

Bonagura, for the sixth week in a row, is projecting Auburn to play the USF Bulls in the Gasparilla Bowl in Tampa on Dec. 18 in Raymond James Stadium. It would be a exciting matchup, with Alex Golesh and several Auburn players getting to play their former team and teammates in their former stadium.

Schlabach projects Auburn to play the UCF Knights in the Liberty Bowl on Jan. 2 in Simmons Bank Liberty Stadium in Memphis, Tennessee, the home of the Memphis Tigers. Auburn is 1-1 in the Liberty Bowl, with their last appearance coming in 1984.

Brad Crawford of CBS Sports is projecting Auburn to stay in Alabama, sending them to the Birmingham Bowl where they would face the Minnesota Golden Gophers. Auburn is 1-1 in the Birmingham Bowl and 0-1 against Minnesota all-time.

Steven Lassan of Athlon Sports is also expecting Auburn to end up in the Gasparilla Bowl, but he projects them to play the Virginia Cavaliers. The Tigers have never played in the Gasparilla Bowl and are 2-1 against the Cavaliers, with the last win coming in the 2011 Peach Bowl.

Brett McMurphy of On3 is also expecting Auburn to play an ACC opponent, but he has them playing the Clemson Tigers in the Gator Bowl. It would be Auburn's seventh appearance in the Gator Bowl, making it their most common bowl destination in program history.

They are 4-2 in their six appearances, winning the last three. The two Tigers have played 51 times, with Auburn leading the series 34-15-2. Clemson has won the last four meetings, with the last game coming in 2017.
