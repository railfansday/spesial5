Recap: Wizards fall to the Celtics, 131-116

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

This one started out ugly and it got ugly in a fast way. As mentioned during the broadcast, the Wizards had perhaps the biggest spotlight on their team since this young group has been assembled and this was a chance to potentially advanced to the semifinals of the NBA Cup for the first time as a franchise.

With all of this in mind, the team was flat to start this. The defense was porous, giving up 80 points in the first half, versus a team that had just scored 87 points for an entire game the last time they were on the floor. Celtics led by as many as 34 points in this one and walked into the locker at halftime up, 80-49.

For the half, the Celtics shot 15 for 26 behind the arc. Too many open shots for a team, struggling to generate consistent offense, is not a recipe for winning.

Fortunately, the Wizards woke up in the 2nd half and made a game of it. The Wizards were able to get as close as seven points, but then the Celtics hit back to back three towards the end of the 4th quarter to put the Wizards out of it.

Besides the poor overall team effort, AJ Dybansta (20 points, 11 rebounds) and Kyshawn George (24 points, 9 rebounds) both had good games. There were some encouraging parts to this one especially in the 2nd half, but the Wizards dug themselves too big of a hole to climb out of and now their 3-game winning streak has been snapped.

Washington will take a few days off and then host the Orlando Magic on Saturday evening in our Nation's Capital.
