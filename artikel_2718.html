Mexico vs Jamaica live stream, start time, TV info for CONCACAF Nations League

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Rafa Marquez is looking for his first win as Mexico soccer coach when El Tri travels for the first leg of the CONCACAF Nations League quarterfinals against Jamaica on Saturday, November 14, at National Stadium in Kingston.

The second leg is scheduled for Tuesday, November 17, in Monterrey, with the aggregate winner advancing to the tournament semifinal, which will take place at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, in March.

El Tri will be looking to repeat as Nations League Champions after winning the 2025 title, while the Reggae Boyz are looking to pull off an upset. Jamaica reached the quarterfinals after finishing second in Group B play.

Kickoff between the two teams is set for 8:00 p.m. ET on Univision. All of the information you need to watch Saturday night's showdown can be seen below.
