Lane Kiffin falls to 0-6 against Alabama despite pink locker room trick

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Alabama Crimson Tide dressed in Lane Kiffin's pink visiting locker room at Tiger Stadium on Saturday and walked out with a 31-13 win over the LSU Tigers. No visitor had won in Baton Rouge this season, and Alabama became the first to win after using the room Kiffin had painted this fall.

Kiffin took the idea from Iowa, where Hayden Fry painted the Kinnick Stadium visitors' quarters pink after arriving in 1979, and he used it at Ole Miss before coming to LSU. He explained the choice at his Oct. 12 news conference, which 247Sports transcribed.

"We did that at the last place, and I think we did it about two years ago. We never lost a home game. We haven't lost a home game yet. Maybe it works. That's why," Kiffin said.

LSU outgained Alabama 457-285 and led with 16 minutes to play. It lost by 18 because it went 0-for-4 on fourth down, missed a field goal and turned the ball over in Alabama territory, and Alabama turned those empty trips into a 14-0 fourth quarter.

The most debated call came with eight minutes left, when Kiffin kept his offense on the field on fourth-and-10 from the LSU 25.

"And then at the end of the game, you can play to just keep the score low when you're on your own 20, but you have no chance to win by punting really, analytically, so you go for it," Kiffin said.

Alabama's version of the afternoon centered on those same stops. Keelon Russell played through an ankle injury and threw three touchdown passes, and Kalen DeBoer opened his postgame news conference with the defense, in remarks On3 transcribed.

"Just super proud of the guys. Team win all around. Defense coming up with some big stops. I believe they were 0-for-4 on fourth down and got us some field position, and offense taking advantage of that when we did," DeBoer said.

Alabama Crimson Tide head coach Kalen Deboer talks on the sideline Thursday, Jan. 1, 2026, during the Rose Bowl and quarterfinal game of the College Football Playoff against Indiana Hoosiers at Rose Bowl Stadium in Pasadena, Calif.

Alabama has now won seven of its last eight games at Tiger Stadium, including a 42-13 rout there in 2024 before any of the walls changed color. The paint gave Kiffin a talking point and his visitors a strange pregame setting, and Saturday showed how little either one counts once a team comes up empty on four fourth downs.

The loss dropped LSU to 6-3 and 5-1 at home, and its playoff hopes now depend on help from elsewhere with the Texas Longhorns coming to Baton Rouge next. Kiffin is now 0-6 against Alabama as a head coach, across his stops at Tennessee, Ole Miss and LSU.
