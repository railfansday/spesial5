Aryna Sabalenka flexes her acting chops in holiday ad with Tom Brady, David Beckham, Kevin Hart

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

WTA World No. 2 tennis player Aryna Sabalenka is flexing her acting chops in a new holiday commercial for Shark Ninja. Sabalenka is joined by athletic legends Tom Brady and David Beckham as well as actor/comedian Kevin Hart.

The premise is that Hart is housesitting for Beckham over the holidays. His friends Sabalenka and Brady stop by Beckham's house for a visit, proceeding to wreck the house all the while using the Shark Ninja products.

No one wants to see Sabalenka exit professional tennis anytime soon, but she could easily be an actress when she chooses to do so. She has personality and flair like Sofia Vergara of Modern Family fame.

In the meantime, Sabalenka is in Indian Wells preparing for her sixth consecutive WTA Finals singles campaign. She is the reigning Indian Wells champion and loves playing in the desert conditions. It is also a magical place for her because she got engaged there earlier this year to longtime boyfriend Georgios Frangulis. The couple plan to marry after the 2027 US Open.

Sabalenka has never won the WTA Finals in either singles or doubles (playing with Elise Mertens in 2019). In addition, there is a Mercedes at stake for this year's WTA Finals champion. She has never won Stuttgart but has always coveted the first prize Porsche.

Could this be Sabalenka's year to achieve both career milestones, a WTA Finals title and a prize-winning car?
