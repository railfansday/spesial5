Dolphins fan confidence stumbles after Colts loss with Bills looming in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Miami Dolphins fell 24-22 to the Indianapolis Colts in Week 10, dropping them to 1-8 for the year. They now face an uphill battle as they start their home-and-home series with the Buffalo Bills, beginning in Week 11 with a visit to western New York. Whereas Miami is in a battle for the top spot in the 2027 NFL Draft, the Bills are positioning themselves to be the best team in the AFC playoff picture.

This week's game is one where just about everything favors Buffalo. Can Miami be the David to the Bills' Goliath? The latest FanDuel Sportsbook odds do not think so, with Buffalo favored by 16.5 points as of Saturday afternoon.

Miami's 2026 season is clearly a rebuild, with the team stripping the roster to the bare bones in the offseason, then restocking it with rookies, first- and second-year players, and journeyman veterans on one-year prove-it contracts. They are reframing the team and the philosophy surrounding it, shaking the finesse persona that dominated the Mike McDaniel era and taking on a new one under head coach Jeff Hafley.

The fans have, for the most part this season, recognized that the wins and losses in 2026 do not matter, as long as the team seems to be growing and developing. This season will put Miami in a position to compete in 2027 and beyond. One year of pain before, hopefully, starting to see the fruits of the team's labor.

Last week, heading into the game against the Colts, our weekly SB Nation Reacts fan confidence poll showed 91 percent of the fans believed Miami was headed in the right direction. After the Colts loss, and with the Bills looming in Week 11, that number was not quite as high.

The vast majority of the fans still trust the Dolphins' direction, but it the losing is clearly having some impact on the confidence. Our Week 11 poll shows a stumble down to 84 percent.
