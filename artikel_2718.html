Arch Manning, Texas football saved by strange fumble call against Missouri

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Texas football appeared to catch a break late in the third quarter of its latest SEC game.

The Longhorns trailed Missouri 20-13 at Memorial Stadium in Columbia, Missouri, and faced a third-and-10 at the Missouri 39-yard line. Quarterback Arch Manning's arm was hit on what looked to be an attempted pass, which fell to the ground roughly 20 yards downfield. But the play wasn't ruled an incompletion when the ball hit the ground. Referees didn't blow their whistle, and Manning scampered to pick up what was ultimately deemed a fumble recovered by the offense.

Replay review ensued. The call on the field was upheld, and boos reigned down in a combination of frustration and confusion from the Missouri crowd. Manning added to their frustration three plays later with a 25-yard touchdown pass to wide receiver Jermaine Bishop to tie the game at 20-20.

Texas caught a break on its recovered fumble. It was on the unfortunate side of a different officiating decision in the second quarter. Texas appeared to rip the ball from Missouri wide receiver Cayden Lee as he fought for a first-down near the Missouri 35-yard line. But a turnover did not ensue, as officials ruled they awarded Lee forward progress and blew the play dead before the ball was taken from his hands.

Both Texas and Missouri entered Saturday at 6-2, 3-2 SEC. Saturday marks Texas' second true road game this season after Texas' 20-17 win at Tennessee in September.
