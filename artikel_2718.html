Blake Corum scores for sixth consecutive game, Rams lead 24-6

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Blake Corum won’t stop scoring, and the Rams have increased their lead early in the second half.

Corum’s 1-yard touchdown run gave the Rams a 24-6 lead in the third quarter.

It’s the sixth consecutive game with a touchdown for Corum, who now has five rushing TDs and six total touchdowns on the season.

With Los Angeles’ offense missing several key players, Corum leads with 67 yards on 12 carries. He also has three catches for 19 yards.

L.A. has now scored touchdowns on three consecutive possessions to take control of the game.

On the injury front, Cardinals linebacker Jordan Burch (knee), Rams center Coleman Shelton (hamstring), and Rams backup center Dylan McMahon (knee) have all been ruled out.
