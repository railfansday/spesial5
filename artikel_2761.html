Indianapolis Colts Injury Report: Week 10 Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Indianapolis Colts released their Wednesday injury's report ahead of Week 10 of the NFL season against the Miami Dolphins.

Linebackers Jaylon Carlies (neck) and Akeem Davis-Gaither (knee) both missed practice today. Carlies left Sundays game early and was replaced by rookie Bryce Boettcher, Davis-Gaithers missed the game all together and led to CJ Allen getting the start at the MIKE, which he will continue to do so moving forward.

Defensive end Arden Key (hamstring) was a full participant today. Key has been out injured for the past few weeks, last played against the Commanders in London. A return for let will help with the defensive end rotations moving forward.

Cornerback Justin Walley (knee) was a full participant today. Walley had missed last Sundays. Walley looks set to become available this week and will be a huge gain to a secondary that is struggling. Right tackle Jalen Travis (knee) missed practice today. Travis left the game early on Sunday and was replaced by Luke Tenuta. The offensive line has struggled this year in pass protection especially at the tackle positions. It could be a first start for Tenuta this week if Travis can't go Sunday.

Safety AJ Haulcy (ankle) missed practice today. Haulcy was unavailable for Sundays game against the cowboys after injuring his ankle late in the week. Haulcy looks like he could miss back to back games too.
