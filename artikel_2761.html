Sixers injury updates: Nick Nurse provides the latest on Jaylen Brown

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

ORLANDO, Fla. -- The Philadelphia 76ers will take on the Orlando Magic on Wednesday to end a quick 2-game road trip and they will do so without the services of Jaylen Brown. The 5-time All-Star suffered a left hamstring strain in Monday's win over the Brooklyn Nets and the Sixers are going to take it easy with him.

As far as what the next steps are for Brown, coach Nick Nurse did explain what the plan is for Brown as the team ends the road trip.

"He's gonna get re-evaluated when we get back home," Nurse stated. "So. just know he's out tonight and we'll look at him when we get back."

The Sixers will have two days off before they play host to the Milwaukee Bucks on Saturday afternoon. One has to wonder what his status will be for that game as Philadelphia will look to welcome back a player who's averaging 22.6 points, 6.6 rebounds, and 3.6 assists on the season.

As for other injuries, Dean Wade is out against the Magic due to his own left hamstring issue. Tyrese Maxey was thrown onto the injury as questionable due to right foot soreness, but he has been downgraded to out for Philadelphia's 7 p.m. EST tip off in Orlando.
