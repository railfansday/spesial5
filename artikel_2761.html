Barcelona loanee Casado expresses sympathy over Balde&#8217;s situation, defends Lamine: &#8216;Makes me laugh&#8217;

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Speaking in an interview with Jijantes FC (h/t SPORT), Barcelona loanee Marc Casado has spoken about Alejandro Balde's difficult situation at the club and defended Lamine Yamal against the criticism directed at the young winger.

The La Masia graduate, currently on loan at Deportivo de A Coruna, will face his parent club this weekend in La Liga, and took time to discuss things related to the Blaugrana ahead of the clash.

"I was able to share a few things with the coach that could help us. We're ready, and now it's time to prove it on the field," he explained.

However, he was under no illusions about the challenge facing his current team against Hansi Flick's Barcelona.

"In a football match, anyone can beat anyone. Barça is one of the best teams in the world, and it will be very tough," Casado noted.

Casado was particularly sympathetic when asked about Alejandro Balde, with whom he shares a close relationship from their time together in Barcelona's dressing room.

The left-back has fallen out of favour this season and has found minutes hard to come by, leading to speculations about a January exit.

Struggling for prominence. (Photo by Alex Caparros/Getty Images)

"I think Balde is the one I'm closest to. I feel bad for him because of what he's going through right now," Casado said.

"When he gets playing time, I wish him all the best with all my heart, and I think he's been able to show what he's capable of," he added.

Casado was also dismissive of the the criticism aimed at Barcelona superstar Lamine Yamal and his willingness to attempt individual actions during matches.

"It makes me laugh that people criticise Lamine Yamal for trying so many different things and then complain that neighbourhood football is a thing of the past," Casado said.

"They call for players like Neymar and then criticise Lamine. Have they ever been so harsh on Messi…?"

Casado defended Lamine against criticism. (Photo by Alex Caparros/Getty Images)

Casado also addressed the reaction to a video in which he had commented on Deportivo's facilities, claiming that they were better than Barcelona's.

"I was disappointed by the reaction to the video of Dépor's training ground. I always wish Barça the best. I think it was an unfortunate comment; I was disappointed that it was misinterpreted," he admitted.

Casado also spoke positively about his adaptation to Deportivo since joining in the summer, as he said:

"The move has been straightforward and smooth. The new signings have all come in to help the team perform as well as possible, and you can tell the club appreciates that."
