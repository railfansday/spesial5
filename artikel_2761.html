Will Steelers WR Michael Pittman Jr. play in Week 11 vs. Eagles?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Michael Pittman Jr. is a 5,000-plus-yard wide receiver who held a special place in the hearts of Indianapolis Colts fans for six years. Traded to the Steelers in March 2026, Pittman has had his early tenure with Pittsburgh hampered by nagging health setbacks — but he has a legitimate possibility of suiting up this Sunday against the Philadelphia Eagles.

Using his 2026 injury history and the Week 11 practice report to help identify whether or not Pittman can return to action this week, here's everything fans need to know about the veteran wide receiver's potential availability.

Pittman has dealt with a lengthy 2026 injury history, as the Steelers receiver suffered a foot setback which forced him to miss significant time. He returned in Week 3 but reaggravated the injury during Week 5 practice and was subsequently placed on injured reserve. In total, Pittman has missed six games due to this foot injury.

Steelers fans may have a lot to celebrate this Sunday, as Pittman was able to begin practice on Wednesday as a limited participant before upgrading to full participation on Thursday and Friday. While he is still listed as questionable to play in Week 11, finishing practice as a full participant on the final two days of practice points towards him making his return in Week 11 against the Eagles.
