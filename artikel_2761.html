Brock Bowers, Brandon Jones both inactive for Raiders-Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Fernando Mendoza will not have his top pass-catcher for his second career start.

Tight end Brock Bowers is officially inactive for the Raiders’ matchup against the Broncos on Sunday.

Bowers is dealing with ankle and quad injuries. While he didn’t practice on Wednesday, he was limited on Thursday and Friday.

Bowers has 50 receptions for 676 yards with six touchdowns so far in 2026.

Receiver Dont’e Thornton, receiver Malik Benson, safety Tristin McCollum, safety Dalton Johnson, guard Bryce Cabeldue, and defensive tackle Tonka Hemingway are also inactive for Las Vegas.

For Denver, safety Brandon Jones is inactive due to a hamstring injury. Quarterback Sam Ehlinger, defensive lineman Jordan Jackson, receiver Lil’Jordan Humphrey, offensive lineman Kage Casey, tight end Dallen Bentley, and offensive lineman Gavin Ortega are also inactive for Denver.
