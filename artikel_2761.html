South Anchorage and Monroe Catholic pick up gritty wins on Day 1 of 3A/4A state volleyball tournament

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Nov. 13—Each of the first two matches during the opening round of the 2026 ASAA 3A/4A State Volleyball Tournament at the Alaska Airlines Center on Thursday ended in decisive three-set sweeps.

However, fans and spectators didn't have to wait much longer to be treated to a couple more entertaining games happening at the same time.

On the 3A side of the court, Monroe Catholic got the better of Sitka in four sets, taking the first handily, dropping the second and winning the third and fourth to advance to the quarterfinals.

"The second set didn't go exactly how we planned, but we just kept pushing through it, and the outcome was what we wanted, so it was really good," senior Rory Morgan said.

She was awarded the Rams' Player of the Game honors for her standout contributions, but she is especially proud of how the team came together to prevail over the Wolves.

"We've worked hard all this season, so it's nice to have won, obviously, but we still have a lot to strive for," Morgan said. "We've had a roller coaster, a lot of challenges we've had to overcome, but we've had a goal this entire year and we're going to keep shooting for that goal."

While the stands weren't necessarily packed with fans from the Interior, Monroe Catholic got plenty of energy from one super fan who made the trek to Anchorage.

Senior Anya Chernich's dad sported a dazzling suit jacket covered in glittering silver sequins and barely sat in his seat as he stood and cheered for every point and rally the Rams made, with his homemade double-sided sign over his head.

"He's the best," Morgan said. "His team spirit is amazing and he's got signs every time. He's awesome."

At the 4A level, South Anchorage dropped its first set to Soldotna and rallied to win each of the next three to advance.

"In the first set, we were kind of just getting in a rhythm and got the nerves out," senior outside hitter Jessica Gieser said. "In the last three, we just got in it and there were some ups and downs, but we fought for it."

She was awarded Player of the Game honors for the role she played in securing the Wolverines' victory.

"It feels so good," Gieser said. "This year, people thought we were going to be rebuilding, but obviously, we've shown that we are not. We're a force to be reckoned with, and that's exciting."

While it felt good to receive individual recognition, she's more excited about what the rest of the weekend holds since one loss doesn't spoil a team's chance to still win a championship in the tournament's double elimination format.

"It's great and our crowd is great," Gieser said. "I love my team and I'm just excited to be here."

Both South and Monroe Catholic went on to lose their respective quarterfinal matches in four sets later in the day but are still alive to qualify for the finals.
