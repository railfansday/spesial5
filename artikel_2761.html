George Kittle's second TD gives 49ers 14-7 lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Once again, George Kittle has made it into the end zone.

Kittle caught his second touchdown of the night on an 8-yard pass from Brock Purdy, putting the 49ers back ahead 14-7 with 14:13 left in the second quarter.

It took San Francisco just five plays to go 67 yards and score.

The big play on the drive came on second-and-10 from the Minnesota 48, with Purdy beating a blitz to fire a strike down the middle to Mike Evans for a 34-yard gain.

After a Christian McCaffrey 6-yard run, Purdy connected with Kittle for the tight end’s seventh touchdown of the season.

Notably, Kittle has now passed Terrell Owens to become No. 2 all-time in 49ers franchise history in receiving yards. Only Jerry Rice has more receiving yards than Kittle, as the star tight end continues to thrive in his long career.
