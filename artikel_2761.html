College Football Playoff: Texas No.3 in latest rankings before regular season ends

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The No. 3 Texas Longhorns are effectively guaranteed a place in the College Football Playoff and now, if they take care of business against Texas A&M, look to be a lock for a first-round bye.

After slipping from the No. 1 spot with two early SEC losses, the Longhorns have slowly climbed back to the top of the rankings, fueled by hard-fought wins against strong opponents. The strength of the SEC schedule continues to be rewarded, with two of the top three teams and four of the top six teams hailing from the conference. One of the biggest benefits Texas receives is its win over No. 5 Tennessee, combined with the continued ascent of Oklahoma and Florida, both of which cracked the top-20 this week.

Texas is currently heavy favorites over Texas A&M for Friday's rivalry matchup, and a win would likely put them in the SEC Championship game. If Texas wins in that game against the Georgia Bulldogs, they would be guaranteed a top-two spot and a bye. Conversely, if Texas loses that game, the committee has signaled in the past that it won't punish teams for a loss in the extra game, so they won't see much of a change. While they have made weird decisions in the past, it would be hard to see them dropping Texas behind Tennessee, a team they beat on the road, or the Alabama Crimson Tide, who lost to the Volunteers.

For a year in which it felt like the wheels fell off with losses to unranked teams early in the season, the Longhorns sit in an enviable position — prepared for their third postseason berth in four seasons.
