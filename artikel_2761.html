‘This kid’: Draymond Green says he just cannot allow Yaxel Lendeborg to do one thing during Warriors games

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Draymond Green is having to be a good older brother to his Golden State Warriors teammate Yaxel Lendeborg on and off the court.

The 36-year-old is one of the most experienced players on the roster, and along with Steph Curry, he is a leader in the locker room.

Lendeborg, who is still a rookie, has a lot to learn, not just about the game but how to behave in heated moments as well.

Green is doing his best to teach that to his young teammate.

Lendeborg wears his heart on his sleeve every time, and that gets him into altercations.

What happened with Luka Doncic made the headlines, and he has since had a few scuffles with other players too in the middle of games.

Green, who has seen and been involved in such incidents more than most others, has claimed that he cannot allow Lendeborg to get into fights and has urged the rookie to leave them for players like him.

Green said on his podcast: "I've got to stay on the court with him because that kid is about to be a star. I can't be having him fighting out there. I take on those battles. I can't be letting my young fella (get into fights).

"I love that you on that type of time, but that's why we're here, you know. Guys out there, when they come touch you, we're coming to do that, you know? I got to get him to understand that, too, like: 'Hey, man, always keep that attitude, but let us come handle that, man. You get over here.'

"Like, we ain't letting Steph fight. Steph got to get over here. Here we go. You know, so like move over here, young fella. Let us handle that. That's why some of us are here, young fella, so you've got to reel it in a little bit now."

Lendeborg really is a star in the making, and he has all the talent and ability in the world to become one of the biggest names in the NBA.

But right now, the 24-year-old needs to be protected, and experienced players like Green and Curry are doing what they need to do to keep him on the right path.

That really is great to see, and it should help Lendeborg focus on what's important, which is fulfilling his massive potential.
