The missing Gen-Z reaction that told JJ Redick this Lakers team was special

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Long before the Lakers were the best story in the NBA, JJ Redick had an inkling that this team was different.

It clicked for him after the Lakers had a team dinner at Carbone Riviera in Las Vegas in July.

After general manager Rob Pelinka paid the bill, Redick became hyperaware of what had not happened that evening.

"I get up to leave and it struck me," he said. "Nobody had left. Nobody had been on their phone."

But they were indicative of the character of the group. The players wanted to bond. They wanted to be around one another.

Los Angeles Lakers guard Luka Doncic reacts to a charging foul during a game against the Charlotte Hornets, Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, in Los Angeles. Getty Images

They were in Sin City, surrounded by a million distractions, but they chose to spend their time with one another.

Now a team that was expected to be a mile behind the Thunder and Spurs in the Western Conference has the best record in the league at 13-2. They've won nine games in a row. They've announced themselves as real championship contenders.

The Lakers have surpassed expectations. They're leading the league in offensive rating (123.1) and steals (10.8) and they're third in blocks (6.1).

Los Angeles Lakers head coach JJ Redick calls out directions against the Portland Trail Blazers in Los Angeles on Friday, Nov. 6, 2026. Getty Images

Luka Doncic and Austin Reaves constantly tease each other as though they were brothers. Their affinity for one another has spilled onto the court, where they're the top-scoring duo in the league, averaging a combined 63.5 points per game.

Walker Kessler has a contagious positivity. He's also a beast down low and a double-double machine, averaging 14.5 points and 10.9 rebounds per game.

And the Lakers' role players are starring in their roles.

That was never more obvious than in the Lakers' 118-114 overtime win over the Hornets on Tuesday. The Lakers were without Kessler (right lower leg soreness) and Reaves (left heel bruise). Doncic was in foul trouble. The Lakers trailed by as much as 18 points.

Los Angeles Lakers guard Luka Doncic, left, slaps hands with head coach JJ Redick during an NBA game Friday, Nov. 6, 2026, in Los Angeles. AP Photo/Mark J. Terrill

Doncic realized Quentin Grimes had a hot hand. He started waving off Redick's play calls in the fourth quarter, so he could feed his teammate. Grimes went on to finish with a career-high 47 points, becoming the only Laker to score 10 or more 3-pointers in a game other than Kobe Bryant in the Lakers' win.

Things have just come together for the Lakers.

There's a good feeling in the locker room. The guys genuinely like each other. And that's dangerous for the rest of the league.

"I think at the end of the day we have great chemistry, which is in my opinion very important," Doncic said. "But I think this group is very special."

It's interesting because the Lakers weren't supposed to jell this quickly.

Los Angeles Lakers head coach JJ Redick looks on against the Atlanta Hawks during the fourth quarter at State Farm Arena in Atlanta on Monday, Nov. 9, 2026. Getty Images

They have nine new players. Doncic has never been known as a vocal leader. This was supposed to be a transition year after LeBron James left in free agency and the Lakers overhauled their roster around the Slovenian superstar.

But something happened over the summer that changed things.

A seed was planted in Vegas. And it flourished after Doncic flew his teammates to Slovenia to host a mini camp, something he had never done before in his career.

They laughed. They sightsaw. They bonded with each other and grew a much deeper understanding of their biggest star.

Redick believes what's happening right now is a magical coalescence of opportunity meeting the right personnel.

It has resulted in a locker room with good vibes. A team that's soaring. And a coach who is deeply grateful that his gut feeling was correct.

He first saw it months ago, long before they ever stepped foot on the court.

"I don't know what this is going to look like after 82 games," Redick said. "I don't know what it's going to look like after 50. But I do know that there's something with this group. Every special group doesn't mean you win a championship.
