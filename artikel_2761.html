What game are you most looking forward to in Week 11?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

With the calendar inching its way toward Thanksgiving, December football in the NFL is just around the corner. And with it, many division and playoff races begin to really heat up. As for the NFC North, things may be warmer than any other division in football. The Detroit Lions hold possession of first place as we head into Week 11, with the Minnesota Vikings and Chicago Bears right behind them, each with seven wins of their own. Even the fourth-place Green Bay Packers have six wins.

For Detroit, they will host the Tampa Bay Buccaneers at Ford Field this Sunday. Despite their 3-6 record on the year, Tampa Bay is firmly in the NFC South race and only one game behind the division-leading Carolina Panthers. Overall, Week 11 is a bit light in terms of games where both teams have playoff aspirations, but there are still plenty of games with intriguing storylines. That brings us to today's question of the day—

My answer: It feels like the obvious choice to me, but I am going with "Sunday Night Football" featuring the Minnesota Vikings and the San Francisco 49ers. The 49ers have been one of the best teams in the league for most of the year, while Minnesota is hoping they found the cure to their offensive struggles with the return of veteran quarterback Kirk Cousins.

With a win, San Francisco would give themselves a nice inside-track towards the number one overall seed in the NFC, while the Vikings would love to keep pace with a Detroit team that is hoping to extend their winning streak to six against Tampa Bay.

What about you? Are you going with the heavyweight fight in the NFC in primetime, or do you have another game in mind that you want to keep an eye on? Let us know in the comments below.
