Giants vs. Jaguars: Preview, prediction, what to watch for in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

An inside look at Sunday's Giants-Jaguars matchup at MetLife Stadium:

Jaguars CB Travis Hunter vs. Giants WR Malik Nabers

Remember when Hunter won the Heisman Trophy, went No. 2 overall in the 2025 draft then all you heard about was how he was going to be a unicorn able to play both sides of the ball — cornerback and wide receiver?

Hunter this season has played just 48 snaps on offense.

He is a darn good corner, though — ranked No. 8 out of 105 cornerbacks in coverage by Pro Football Focus.

Hunter does not shadow the top target, but he will get to go against Nabers plenty.

Nabers had a stretch of four consecutive games topping 100 receiving yards, but the past two games has just 87 receiving yards and in his most recent game lost his first fumble in the NFL.

Malik Nabers Bill Kostroun for New York Post

+9: The Jaguars lead the league in turnover differential.

The Giants are tied for 20th at minus-2. Taking care of the ball could decide this game.

This would be a significant upset if the Giants can pull it off.

The likelihood of rain (what else is new?) could be an equalizer.

Putting points up on the Jacksonville defense is problematic, meaning there can be no turnovers or wasted plays. It's a tall order.
