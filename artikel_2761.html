NFL Week 11 picks, predictions: Colts vs. Texans on TNF

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Believe it or not, we're already in Week 11 of the NFL. Better appreciate the season while it's still here.

Thursday night could be a tough night to be thankful for the NFL. Not only is it a matchup that doesn't impact the Detroit Lions in any meaningful way, but it's also a game that shouldn't be all that close or interesting.

The 3-6 Houston Texans are hosting the 3-7 Indianapolis Colts.

It's been a massively disappointing season for both teams. Both went into the season with Super Bowl aspirations. Now, they have a combined 5 percent chance to make the playoffs, according to RBSDM. Indianapolis has gone completely off the rails, while the Texans are a little harder to figure out. They've got a positive point differential, rank 11th in DVOA, and don't look like a complete joke when watching. Yet, they've lost to the Colts, Browns, Cowboys and Giants this year.
