Is the Rams 2027 draft set to be one of their worst ever?

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Los Angeles Rams are only projected to have one selection in the first 110 picks of the 2027 NFL Draft, and right now that is set at 49th overall. The Rams do not have a first-round pick because of the Myles Garrett trade, and do not have a third-round pick because of the Trent McDuffie trade. In addition, L.A. is not projected to have a single compensatory pick in 2027.

Without a trade and/or getting a third-round comp pick from the NFL's minority hiring incentive program, the Rams will only have one fairly mediocre draft pick until the fourth round. As of Week 12, that is pick 49 in the second round.

Not only are the Cleveland Browns projected to make the playoffs, they also hold L.A.'s first-round pick in 2027, which could end up being in the top-20, if not higher.

The Rams are shutting down players left and right, similar to 2022 when Matthew Stafford, Cooper Kupp and Aaron Donald landed on IR, as both Puka Nacua and Terrance Ferguson have been announced to not be returning this season. Jaylen Watson, whom as a free agency signing may have cost L.A. their only potential 2027 comp pick, is also on season-ending IR. So is Poona Ford. And Davante Adams won't be back soon.

But there is no incentive for the Rams to throw in the towel for a higher draft pick. Coincidentally, there also wasn't any incentive like that in 2022 and the Rams ended up sending pick 6 to the Detroit Lions to complete the Matthew Stafford trade.

The Lions used that pick to trade down and then they selected Jahmyr Gibbs, the frontrunner for Offensive Player of the Year in 2026.

Even after this year, the Rams will still owe the Browns a second-round pick in 2028, and a third-round pick in 2029. The 2029 pick becomes a first-round pick if Garrett is traded to an AFC North team. The 2027 draft will complete L.A.'s trade for McDuffie.

So L.A.'s first pick is projected to be 49th and their second pick is projected to be their original fourth-rounder. Right now that's "113" but that's before we add in compensatory pick. It may actually end up being 120th.

The Rams have their own picks in rounds 5 and 6, but traded their 7th for Tre'Davious White.

So unless the Rams make some trades and/or a team hires Nate Scheelhaase as head coach, L.A. will end up with picks in rounds 2, 4, 5, and 6. This could be their most challenging draft since 2022 when the first pick was Logan Bruss.
