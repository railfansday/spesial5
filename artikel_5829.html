FDA Approves First Treatment for Neurologic Symptoms of Type 3 Gaucher Disease

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

— Oral venglustat led to improvements in symptoms related to ataxia and cognitive function

The FDA approved venglustat (Infilbray) tablets as the first treatment for neurologic symptoms of type 3 Gaucher disease in adults and children weighing at least 15 kg, the agency announced Wednesday.

Gaucher disease is a rare, inherited lysosomal storage disorder caused by a deficiency of glucocerebrosidase. Without this enzyme, glycosphingolipids build up, mainly in macrophages in the spleen, liver, bone marrow, and lungs. In type 3 Gaucher disease, these lipids also accumulate in the central nervous system and lead to neurologic symptoms including cognitive deficits or problems with coordination and balance.

Venglustat is a novel oral glucosylceramide synthase inhibitor designed to cross the blood-brain barrier. By limiting abnormal glycosphingolipid buildup and its effects, it may help slow the progression of type 3 Gaucher disease.

"We have been able to treat the systemic manifestations of type 3 Gaucher disease, while the neurological manifestations remained beyond the reach of available therapies," Pramod Mistry, MD, PhD, of the Yale Lysosomal Disease Center and Gaucher Disease Treatment Center in New Haven, Connecticut, said in a news release. "The availability of a medicine capable of addressing the neurological manifestations of this devastating disorder represents a major milestone for patients, families, and the global type 3 Gaucher disease community."

The FDA's decision was based on data from the phase III LEAP2MONO trial, which compared daily oral venglustat versus intravenous infusions of the enzyme replacement therapy imiglucerase (Cerezyme) in 43 patients ages 12 and older. Study participants had previously achieved stabilization of their systemic manifestations with enzyme replacement therapy. At 52 weeks, a global test of multiple neurologic endpoints showed that venglustat achieved significant improvements in symptoms related to ataxia and cognitive function (P=0.0137).

The most frequently reported side effects with venglustat included gastrointestinal symptoms and adverse effects on mood, the FDA said. "Serious psychiatric risks, including suicidal ideation and behavior, have been reported," the agency pointed out. "Potential risks identified from animal studies include potential clouding of vision in younger patients and adverse effects on fetal development when drug is taken during pregnancy."

Prescribing information includes warnings and precautions for suicidal ideation and behavior, lens opacification in pediatric patients, and embryo-fetal toxicity.

The open-label phase of the LEAP2MONO study is ongoing and results will be presented when available, drugmaker Sanofi said.
