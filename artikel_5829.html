Trump is in Asia ahead of Xi, Putin summit. And, tech leaders want Huang back at Nvidia

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A technology issue with United Airlines delayed departing flights yesterday evening, just as air travel was returning to normal after the Federal Aviation Administration ordered a nationwide ground stop on Friday. President Trump said yesterday that he hopes this incident will prompt Congress to allocate more funds to upgrade the outdated air traffic control system.

President Trump is in Manila today to meet with allies at the ASEAN summit. Tomorrow, he travels to Shenzhen, China, for a joint summit with Chinese leader Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin. It's the first time those three have had a joint summit. The meeting will happen on the sidelines of the APEC summit that Xi is hosting in Shenzhen.

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation has shaken Silicon Valley, prompting calls from some tech leaders for the board to reinstate him. Trump said he agreed with them. Meanwhile, the U.S. government introduced a rule to block remote access to advanced AI chips and servers, closing a loophole that could allow adversarial governments to access American AI technology through overseas data centers. Nvidia is the world's dominant producer of AI chips.

Prosecutors are moving to retry Lindsay Clancy for the deaths of her three children. The new trial is set for March 29, 2027. Although there was speculation about potentially reducing the charges to manslaughter or second-degree murder after her previous trial ended in a mistrial, no changes were made during Monday's court hearing. Judge William Sullivan, who presided over the first trial, will oversee the new proceedings. He denied Clancy's defense team's request to dismiss the charges based on double jeopardy. Clancy's defense argues that she shouldn't be held criminally responsible for killing her children because she was suffering from severe postpartum psychosis.

One year ago, New Mexico became the first state to offer free childcare to all families regardless of income, as long as adults are working, seeking work, in school, or unable to work. Many parents have found this initiative life-changing, but capacity was limited. Only 50% of the some 67,000 children ages 5 and under are enrolled in the universal childcare program. Some of the remaining families participate in other programs, such as PreK and Head Start. New Mexico officials say the program must expand to meet demand. The issues observed in New Mexico are mirrored across the nation, prompting policymakers and childcare advocates to closely monitor the state's initiative.

Millions of shorebirds embark on epic migrations to Alaska each year to breed. They often travel thousands of miles from places like New Zealand, Asia and South America. But researchers have found a concerning trend: Nearly half of the 41 shorebird species that breed in Alaska are in decline. These birds time their journeys to coincide with peak insect availability, and climate change is throwing off their schedules. Warmer springs lead to earlier insect emergence, creating a mismatch that could leave shorebirds without enough food supply. Scientists are now racing to understand if these migratory birds can adapt to the changing Arctic landscape.
