The 82 best Walmart early Black Friday deals include $89 AirPods 4 and a $328 75-inch TV

Walmart has launched a full bombardment of Black Friday deals with weeks to go before the holiday even hits. While I’m not entirely sure how I feel about the shopping holiday creeping ever earlier in the month, I’ll certainly never turn down a good deal. It’s the first of three Walmart sales this month, with Black Friday deals on November 23 and Cyber Monday deals on November 30. Walmart+ members get first access to a set of hot Deal drops, and whatever is left opens to everyone at 12:01 a.m. ET on Friday, November 13. We checked every price in this post on Walmart.com at 1:30 p.m. ET on Thursday, November 12. Walmart doesn’t publish price history, so we list each sale price with Walmart’s crossed-out price.

Apple’s redesigned everyday earbuds for less than $100

The Apple AirPods 4 are $89.00 (was $129.99) in new condition, sold and shipped by Walmart. Make sure the product page says New: Walmart’s listing also has an Open Box version at a lower price. Apple says these use its H2 chip, run up to 5 hours per charge and up to 30 hours with the USB-C case, and carry an IP54 rating for dust, sweat and water. This is the standard model without active noise cancellation. For help choosing between Apple’s earbuds, see our guide to the best AirPods.

The Hisense 75-inch H5 Series 4K Roku TV (75H5R1) is $328.00 (was $668.00). It’s a 2026 model, and Walmart’s listing says it supports Dolby Vision, HDR10 and HLG, runs Roku OS and has three HDMI ports. The panel refreshes at 60Hz, so console gamers who want 120Hz should look elsewhere. If you’re comparing bigger, brighter sets, start with our guide to the best 4K TVs.

One-button espresso and coffee from a compact pod machine

The Nespresso Vertuo Pop by De’Longhi is $69.00 (was $119.00). Nespresso says it brews four cup sizes, from espresso up to a full cup, and reads a barcode on each Vertuo capsule to set the brew. It only takes Vertuo pods, so plan on buying those for as long as you own it. For other options, see our guide to the best Nespresso machines.

A full-size cordless saw with the battery and charger included

The Greenworks Pro 24V Brushless 7-1/4-inch Circular Saw Kit is $129.00 (was $217.44), and that includes a 4Ah battery and charger. Greenworks rates the brushless motor at 6,000 RPM with a 2-5/8-inch cut depth at 90 degrees, and says the USB-C battery can also charge your phone. Greenworks backs it with a five-year warranty. For how it stacks up, see our guide to the best circular saws.
