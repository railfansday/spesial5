TSSAA cross country state champs. Vote for Ponce Law Girls Athlete of Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

There was no lack of excitement this week at the TSSAA girls cross country state championships.

Who do you think should be the Ponce Law Girls Athlete of the Week? Vote now!

Editor's note: The Tennessean will remove nominees from the poll if there are evident signs of vote tampering through bots or other means. Please help us maintain the integrity of the poll by not cheating.

Chaiti Ainapure, Liberty Creek: Ainapure won the AA girls individual state championship with a time of 18:49.81.

Lorelai Whitten, Summit: Whitten finished as runner-up in the AAA girls state championship race with a time of 18:10.35.

Breah Crocker, Cornersville: Crocker finished third overall in the Class A cross-country state championships in a time of 19:35.53.

More: Ponce Law is honored to offer the 2026 Perspectives Scholarship to support students' educational journey. Learn more.

Scotlyn Szabo, Brentwood: Szabo finished in third place in the AAA girls state championship race with a time of 18:30.38.

Rachel Benefield, Central Magnet: Benefield took fourth in the AA state championships in a time of 19:01.22.

Violet Evans, Hume-Fogg: Evans took home ninth place in the AA state meet in a time of 19:31.00.

Keira Stegall, Nolensville: Stegall finished 21st in a time of 19:14.08 in the AAA state championship race.

Lydia Brunner, Father Ryan: Brunner helped Father Ryan girls capture the Division II-AA state championship as a time after she finished runner-up, individually, in a time of 18:15.17.

Annabel Farringer, Harpeth Hall: Farringer took fourth in the Division II-AA meet in a time of 18:43.90.

Kiera Bray, Battle Ground Academy: Bray took 25th in a time of 21:47.76 in the DII-A state championship race.
