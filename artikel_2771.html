Bills lead Dolphins 20-6 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Bills have a big game against the Chiefs on Thanksgiving night, but they haven’t gotten caught looking ahead against the Dolphins this Sunday.

Josh Allen hit wide receiver DJ Moore for a touchdown and running back James Cook ran for another score as the Bills extended their lead to 20-3 in the second quarter. The Dolphins capitalized on a Josh Allen interception to cut the lead to 20-6 and the Bills’ bid to pad their lead appeared to end with an interception by safety Zayne Anderson with 18 seconds to play in the half.

Dolphins quarterback Kyle McCord returned the favor one play later, however. His pass to wide receiver Chris Bell was picked off by safety Damar Hamlin and the play proved to be doubly painful for the Dolphins when Bell needed medical attention after a big collision with Hamlin.

The Bills got the ball near midfield and Allen threw his third interception of the half, but it was wiped off the board by a defensive holding penalty. The Bills completed a short pass to Moore on the untimed final down of the second quarter.

Allen’s first interception came when Dolphins defensive end Chop Robinson tipped a pass that fluttered into the hands of rookie linebacker Jacob Rodriguez and Rodriguez took the ball back to the Buffalo 22-yard-line, but Greg Rousseau’s second sack of the game and his 10th of the season meant that they wouldn’t be able to turn it into six points.

It was an erratic half for Allen, who went into the break 15-of-30 for 173 yards, but a two-touchdown lead attests to the difference between the overall quality of the two teams on the field in Buffalo.
