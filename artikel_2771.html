Victor Wembanyama, NBA's dunk king, turns to the 'shunk' due to painful blister on hand

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Victor Wembanyama has found a new way to shoot in order to give his hands a rest and prevent future blisters.

It's called the "shunk," as San Antonio Spurs color commentator Sean Elliott put it. It's basically the 7-foot-4 Wembanyama "dunking" the ball through the basket like a jump shot.

Wembanyama showed off the "shunk" during the Spurs' 124-112 win over the Los Angeles Clippers on Thursday. The two-time All-Star posted 32 points, 11 rebounds and 1 "shunk."

"I'm not going to lie," Wembanyama said afterward via ESPN. "Actually, I've had this blister forever, and it's killing me. And every time I dunk, it kills me. I've been dragging this blister for I don't know how long because of dunking. So, I did not [quit dunking], but if I can avoid it, I would."

Through 16 games, Wembanyama leads all players with 72 dunks — Thursday's "shunk" was counted as a dunk by the NBA. The next closest is Giannis Antetokounmpo of the Miami Heat, who has 49. The Spurs star is on pace for 369 dunks this season, which would pass Rudy Gobert's record of 306 he amassed during the 2018-19 season.
