The Packers’ get some help from the 49ers in the playoff picture

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Green Bay Packers weren't the only team on a bye this week, with the Seattle Seahawks and Los Angeles Rams also taking their rest week. All the 6-4 teams in the conference on a bye meant nothing much was going to change for the Packers in the standings. Green Bay is still seventh.

But the Packers did gain a half-game on the team directly in front of them. The 7-2 Minnesota Vikings lost to the San Francisco 49ers, so Green Bay is now just one game (plus a tiebreaker) back of Minny.

Green Bay plays the Rams on Wednesday night which will be very important in the long run of the Wild Card chase. The Rams have a better NFC record than Green Bay, too, so even a three-way tie won't help the Packers.

In the rest of the NFC North, the Chicago Bears and Detroit Lions both won to move to 8-2 with the Lions' NFC North record providing the tiebreaker.
