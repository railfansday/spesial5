Ravens list Rashod Bateman, Vega Ioane as limited in Thursday's practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Everyone on the Ravens’ active roster practiced on Thursday, but a handful of them were limited participants.

Wide receiver Rashod Bateman was part of that group. Bateman, who has 20 catches for 294 yards and a score on the season, is dealing with a calf injury ahead of Monday’s game against the Chargers.

Right guard Vega Ioane (knee, shoulder) and safety Jaylinn Hawkins (knee) were also limited. Hawkins did not play in last Thursday’s loss to the Jaguars due to his injury.

Running back Rasheen Ali (ankle), safety Kyle Hamilton (hip), tight end Matthew Hibner (hand), running back Adam Randall (neck, hamstring), and cornerback Nate Wiggins (illness) were all listed as full participants.
