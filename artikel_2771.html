Here’s where the Bears sit in the NFC playoff standings after Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

With the Chicago Bears' epic 39 to 35 comeback win over the Detroit Lions, they've taken over first place in the NFC North. The victory also shot them up to the second seed in the NFC behind the 49ers, while the Lions dropped to fifth in the conference.

The Bears and Lions both still control their own postseason destiny, but since this was Chicago's first NFC North victory, they'll need to keep stacking wins to avoid any tiebreaker scenarios atop the division.

The Bears can now relax, enjoy the long holiday weekend, and catch their breath before playing the Jacksonville Jaguars on December 6.
