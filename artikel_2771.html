No Aaron Rodgers? No problem. Steelers crush Broncos with dominant defense

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Aaron Rodgers was the story of the week in the NFL, but the story of today's game in Pittsburgh was the Steelers' defense.

Broncos quarterback Bo Nix could do nothing in Pittsburgh, as the Steelers' defense dominated in a 27-5 win.

Steelers quarterback Mason Rudolph, starting for the first time since Aaron Rodgers was benched and released, played reasonably well, completing 9 of 15 passes for 142 yards, with one touchdown and no interceptions. The Steelers didn't ask Rudolph to do a lot, but what they asked him to do, he did.

But the Steelers' defense was outstanding. T.J. Watt recovered a Nix fumble in the end zone for one touchdown, Jalen Ramsey returned a Nix interception for another touchdown, and the Steelers' defense had a great day on a rainy Black Friday in Pittsburgh.

The win improves the Steelers' record to 7-4 and erases any doubt that head coach Mike McCarthy can lead them to the playoffs this season. The loss drops the Broncos to 8-3, and raises questions about how far they can go with Nix at quarterback.
