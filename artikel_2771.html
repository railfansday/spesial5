Predators Forward Ranked 7th on The Athletic's Trade Board

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Ryan O'Reilly's future in Nashville has been surrounded by question marks for quite a while, considering the fact that he'll become an unrestricted free agent at the end of the ongoing season.

More fuel has been added to that fire, as The Athletic's Chris Johnston listed O'Reilly seventh on his fourth iteration of the annual "Trade Board." "As a pending unrestricted free agent, O'Reilly will be back in demand from contenders who prize his two-way diligence and vast experience, which includes a Conn Smythe Trophy and Stanley Cup season in St. Louis," Johnston wrote.

While the Predators are likely to hang on to O'Reilly as long as their current push for the playoffs continues, if their level of play begins to slip, even slightly, it would be surprising if opposing general managers didn't pick up their intensity in pursuing the 35-year-old. Carrying a $4.5 million cap hit, he'll undoubtedly be one of the most cost-effective options on the market and his two-way game would help nearly every contending team in the NHL.

As Johnston put it, "As much as he may want to stay in Nashville, he represents a valuable trade piece for new general manager Chris MacFarland."
