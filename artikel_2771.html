Week 11 goes perfectly for Browns: AFC North within reach, updated playoff seeds

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

We spent much of Monday going over different pieces of the Cleveland Browns playoff scenarios, which has bled a bit into Tuesday already. Week 12 versus the Las Vegas Raiders will have a big impact on both teams' AFC playoff odds, including potentially dropping the visitors out of the small list of teams from the conference who could make the postseason.

Week 11 ended up going perfectly for the Browns while they were on their bye week. The coaches got to attack some of the weaknesses in their planning, while the roster was able to get healthy before Tuesday's return to practice. On top of that, everyone else in the AFC North lost games, including the Cincinnati Bengals falling to the Washington Commanders on Monday Night Football.

The Pittsburgh Steelers' loss put Cleveland into the second spot in the division. The Baltimore Ravens' loss all but eliminated them from playoff contention, while the Bengals' defeat opened up the possibility of the Browns taking the AFC North for the first time, now just one game back with two games against their in-state rivals.

Cincinnati's loss also dropped them down in the AFC playoff standings as well, while Cleveland remains in the sixth seed heading into Week 12.

A big Week 11 for the Browns overall, who now have a more realistic shot at the division, jumped up a spot in the AFC playoff standings, and saw some more distance between themselves and a couple of teams that are now holding on to postseason hope for dear life.
