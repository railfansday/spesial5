Lions, Bucs Week 11 injury report: D.J. Reed returns to practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Detroit Lions (7-2) are back home in Week 11 and preparing to host the Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-6). As the Lions work to reacclimate after returning from Germany earlier in the week, they elected to hold a walkthrough on Wednesday.

A walkthrough practice means that all participation levels are estimates from the coaching staff based on how the player would have participated in a traditional practice. So, with that in mind, let's look at Wednesday's injury report and get you up to date on what we know.

Note: Any changes from the most recent injury report will be bolded.

RB Isiah Pacheco (MCL/Back, IR) — expected to be out until December

OL Giovanni Manu (undisclosed, NFI) — eligible to begin practicing at any time

OL Ben Bartch (foot, IR) — eligible to begin practicing at any time

EDGE Aidan Hutchinson (ACL, IR) — requires surgery; his season is over

LB Devin White (forearm, IR) — eligible to begin practicing at any time

LB Jack Campbell (neck, IR) — determined to play football again

S Thomas Harper (ankle, IR) — eligible to begin practicing at any time

Earlier in the week, Lions coach Dan Campbell said he wasn't sure if Mays would be able to return to play this week, and while an estimated no practice to start the week is not ideal, he could still return to practice this week, even if the plan is not to play him.

CB D.J. Reed (hip) — UPGRADED from no practice last week

CB Ennis Rakestraw (shoulder) — New Injury

Vaki missed two practices last week with an ankle injury, returned to practice on Friday in Germany, and played in the game. An estimated limited practice is a step in the right direction as he looks to stay on the field this weekend.

Lacy did not appear to be limited by this injury in Germany, and Campbell has noted that the big end is in the plans to help replace Hutchinson, so an estimated limited practice is worth monitoring but with some optimism.

Bachie didn't play many defensive snaps last Sunday, but he's been a consistent special teams contributor. Hopefully this injury won't keep him sidelined.

Reed's status is still up in the air, as Campbell said he wasn't sure whether the Lions' top corner would be available against the Bucs. That being said, an estimated limited practice is a good sign that even if he can't return this Sunday, he could be on track to return against the Bears on Thanksgiving.

Dorsey was notably injured against the Patriots and required trainers' assistance on the field in Germany. The Lions never assigned him an in-game injury designation, which suggests they were trying to see if he could return. Couple that with an estimated limited practice, and he'll have a chance to play on Sunday.

Rakestraw played only a limited number of snaps in Germany, though it's not clear whether that was the plan ahead of time or whether this shoulder injury changed things. We'll have to see how he progresses this week before we know his availability.

Joseph has officially been activated from the PUP list, and while his knee is still being monitored, he is expected to return to the starting lineup this Sunday against the Bucs.

"Barring anything else here negatively—which there hasn't been, he's progressed nicely here—the plan would be to get him going this week. Get him going and get him into Tampa Bay and let him play," Campbell said.

The players in this section have improved enough to no longer be on the injury list. Goff's chest injury, St. Brown's ribs, and Branch's hip were considered minor; Saylors is wearing a neck brace for added protection; and Wonnum has cleared the NFL's concussion protocol.
