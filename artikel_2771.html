Clear Fork, Ontario boys soccer players earn All-MOAC honors

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Mid-Ohio Athletic Conference announced its all-league selections for the 2026 boys soccer season.

The Clear Fork Colts saw Kade Beachy, AJ Swendal, Owen Weikle and Finn Mcauley earn first team honors after leading the team to the MOAC championship. Beachy led the team with 21 goals and 13 assists while Swendal had 16 goals and four assists, Weikle had eight goals and 13 assists and Mcauley had 13 goals and seven assists. Teammates Wes Bunnell, Coy Bieri and Jace Feeman were second team selections while Kabuto Setser and Jude Gailey were honorable mentions.

The Ontario Warriors put two players on first team in Gavin Wagner and Ian Morrison. Wagner had 29 goals and four assists while Morrison added 11 goals and 10 assists. Teammates Henry Yugovich and Trevor Breitinger were second team selections while Aiden Kirst and Sage Hawks were honorable mentions.

Here is the complete list of All-MOAC boys soccer teams for the 2026 season:

FIRST TEAM: Kade Beachy, Clear Fork; AJ Swendal, Clear Fork; Owen Weikle, Clear Fork; Finn McAuley, Clear Fork; Christian Mason, Highland; Atticus Wilson, Highland; Coleman Chamberlain, Highland; Angel De La Cruz, Marion Harding; Gavin Wagner, Ontario; Ian Morrison, Ontario; Cooper Halpin, Pleasant; Rodric Gomez, Pleasant; Cooper Mullins, River Valley.

SECOND TEAM: Wes Bunnell, Clear Fork; Coy Bieri, Clear Fork; Jace Feeman, Clear Fork; Liam Cahill, Highland; Braxton Sqrow, Highland; Jose Baltazar De Dios, Marion Harding; Jeffrey Stimson, Marion Harding; Henry Yugovich, Ontario; Trevor Breitinger, Ontario; Jair Orrostieta, Pleasant; Coben Reser, River Valley; Caleb Strother, River Valley.

HONORABLE MENTION: Kabuto Setser, Clear Fork; Jude Gailey, Clear Fork; Kasey Clark, Highland; Logan Elverson, Highland; Harrison Keith, Marion Harding; Joedan Contreras, Marion Harding; Aiden Kirst, Ontario; Sage Hawks, Ontario; Jose Salinas, Pleasant; Hudson Severns, Pleasant; Riley Hummel, River Valley.
