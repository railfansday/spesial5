Scottish Premiership roundup: Dundee left reeling after 8-0 mauling by Hearts

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Steven Pressley: ‘I’m embarrassed … I apologise’

Celtic 4-1 St Mirren; Rangers 2-1 Motherwell

Wouter Vrancken said Hearts’ remarkable 8-0 demolition of 10-man Dundee was his team’s best performance since he took charge at Tynecastle.

Sabah Kerjota and Pierre Landry Kaboré both scored twice with Claudio Braga, Sabri Guendouz, Stuart Findlay and a Ryan Finnigan own goal completing the rout. Vrancken was naturally delighted that his players had ended a run of three defeats in a row in such spectacular style.

“Our best performance? Of course,” he said. “When you have the confidence of one moment and then you feel the score is getting bigger, you see the players with extra confidence. You see the dynamics there and it’s good they feel it works.

“It was ruthless and I like to see it this way. That was the message at half-time – that and keeping the clean sheet. But it’s only the first step, only one game and we have to try to bring more.”

Steven Pressley admitted he was embarrassed by the result and Dundee’s performance, saying: “It’s a night I need to own, a night where I can only apologise for the performance. I never expected that.

“I had a little sense we haven’t been quite on it over the last couple of weeks but I take ownership and I mean that. We talk about the culture and environment that we set and part of that is making sure we have a fully motivated team, a team which works hard and shows grit. We didn’t show that tonight but that is on me and I mean that. I’m embarrassed by it and I accept it.”

The recalled midfielders Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain and Luke McCowan both scored as Celtic beat St Mirren 4-1. The champions brushed off the loss of midfield pair Mika Baur and Benjamin Nygren to injury to consolidate their place at the top of the Premiership.

The centre-forward Camilo Duran was also on target as Celtic killed the game late in the first half with three goals in nine minutes. Callum McGregor grabbed his first league goal of the season before Killian Phillips scored a consolation from the spot.

With Baur facing up to 10 weeks out with an ankle injury and Nygren nursing a heel problem, Martin O’Neill would have been delighted with how his midfield functioned.

Oxlade-Chamberlain made his fifth start of the season and McCowan his second. The pair had never started a game together before. O’Neill also introduced the former PSV Eindhoven midfielder Joël van den Berg for a debut in the second half and the 19-year-old showed some good touches.

Ryan Naderi hit a deflected 88th-minute winner at Fir Park as Rangers stayed four points behind Celtic by beating Motherwell 2-1. Naderi’s effort went in off the unfortunate Motherwell defender Martin Moorman as Rangers finally made the most of some late pressure to win.

The visitors looked as if they might be held to a third draw in four away games after Jake Girdwood-Reich had cancelled out an early opener from Rangers defender Emmanuel Fernandez. Rangers had 29 shots at goal but found Motherwell goalkeeper Alex Paulsen in top form and the New Zealand international pulled off one of the saves of the season to deny Dujon Sterling with five minutes left.

However, they netted after a corner when Naderi’s shot from eight yards went in off Moorman after the Austrian had blocked a shot from Badredine Bouanani.

Mario Frick enjoyed a dream start to life as Aberdeen manager by edging a six-goal thriller with a 4-2 win at Kilmarnock. Kevin Nisbet’s double along with Brad Lyons and Findlay Marshall strikes secured three priceless points to leave the Rugby Park side rooted to the bottom

The visitors climbed to eighth spot after a remarkable afternoon where the Pittodrie side hit a much-needed scoring streak. Aberdeen opted to dispense with the services of Stephen Robinson after a run of one win from seven which saw them sink to second bottom and Frick was appointed as his replacement last Sunday.

Dundee United recorded their biggest ever win at Easter Road with an impressive 4-0 thrashing of Hibernian. Zac Sapsford’s first-half double laid the foundations before Lyall Cameron and Jesse Randall found the net after the break.

The visitors were well worth the three points as they cemented their place in the top six and picked up their first win in Leith since 2021. It was a humbling afternoon for Hibs, who thought they had turned a corner under their new head coach, Marink Reedijk, after back-to-back victories before the international break.

Falkirk clung on to clock up six top-flight wins in a row for the first time since 1966 with a 2-1 home victory against St Johnstone. John McGlynn’s team scored early in each half to remain third.

Jacob Murrell’s eighth goal of a superb debut season sent Falkirk on their way inside six minutes. Finley Barbrook’s second goal in as many games on 48 minutes doubled the advantage. Josh Campbell nodded in a late reply to serve up a scare but the hosts held firm.
