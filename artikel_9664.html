17 apple dessert recipes for fall baking: Embrace the season with apple tarts, cookies, cakes, and more.

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

There’s an apple dessert for everyone. For the purists, there are lattice-topped pies and shingled galettes. For the non-traditionalists, there are caramel-frosted cakes and fritter-inspired loaves. And for apple lovers that hate cinnamon? Well, there are custardy cakes and classic French tarts. Ready to start baking with fall’s best fruit? Read on for our best apple dessert recipes.
