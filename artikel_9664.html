A week after our first audit, the broadened index held

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Posted on Nov 24 • Originally published at openjob.softprobe.ai

Originally published at https://openjob.softprobe.ai/articles/week-one-steady/

One week after OpenJob's first audit, the index held the breadth it gained. The top 10 employers are 43.7% of sampled rows — down from 48.2% on November 18 and flat against 43.8% yesterday — the sample now covers 99 employers (was 81 a week ago), and the manifest grew to 33,438 rows. The honest live-jobs figure still holds at about 14,000 posted in the last 60 days.

Day to day, almost nothing moved — and that is the point of re-running the same count. Five of the six sampled shards are byte-identical to yesterday; only one shard changed, by eight rows. A job index that churns wildly every morning is an index you cannot trust to answer the same question twice.

In the sample, 2,290 of 5,438 rows were posted in the last 60 days (42.1%). Weighted across the full dataset that is about 13,650 of 33,438 — the live figure we quote as about 14,000. The freshest pipe is still Ashby, at 66.3% of its sampled rows posted in the last 60 days; Greenhouse is at 47.0% and Moka at 27.8%.

The AI-agent roles are still there, and still named: 64 roles on the narrow keyword set (1.2% of the sample), 33 of them fresh — Cloudflare's Agentic Commerce and Agentic Discovery teams, Reddit's Agentic Ads, Hugging Face's Voice Agents, Scale AI's agent work, and the rest. The broad AI/ML set is 6.4% (346 roles).

The narrow AI-agent count fell from 76 to 64 over the week — not because agent hiring stopped, but because the sample broadened toward Moka rows, where agent-titled roles are rarer. The count is honest either way: same six shards, same definitions, same word-boundary keyword match. A note on method: a quick substring match overcounts this number badly — it returns 83, counting "Fulfillment" (it contains "llm") and Chinese titles with no word boundaries. We publish the word-boundary count, 64.

Try it yourself: paste "Read https://openjob.softprobe.ai/llms.txt and follow it. Find me a job posted in the last 30 days." into the agent you already use — Muse, ChatGPT/Codex, Cursor, Hermes, or Instinct.
