GdS: Wins, consistency and goals for Ramos – the next steps Amorim wants Milan to take

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Ruben Amorim wants to see more from his AC Milan side, starting this evening against Frosinone, and he believes they are capable.

As La Gazzetta dello Sport (seen below) report, the aim for today’s match is to ensure the break didn’t disrupt the momentum, ashat happened following the September-October pause when Milan drew away to Sassuolo.

This time, the opponent will be even tougher (at least based on league position), and Amorim knows it. For this reason, he has demanded the utmost focus from his players and has sought to fire up Gonçalo Ramos, who has only scored twice in the first 11 league matches so far.

The first issue the Portuguese forward needs to address is his finishing. In four matches – against Juventus, Lecce, Bologna, and Inter – he failed to register a single shot, while in two others (against Lazio and Genoa), he didn’t manage to hit the target.

That is simply not enough for a striker who cost €70m, plus bonuses and commissions. However, according to the former Manchester United manager, the fault does not lie with Gonçalo and instead is a team issue.

“Having another forward to assist Ramos and Camarda would certainly be an extra option, but our real problem is that we aren’t reaching the final 30 metres. We are among the worst teams in Serie A when it comes to shots taken and chances created,” he said in his press conference.

“As for Gonçalo, we aren’t putting him in a position to score. Cissé gets into the box with his runs, while Ramos relies on the team’s build-up play. In Genoa, both of our goals came thanks to him. We need to support him better through more possession and quick passing combinations in the final third.

“My goal is to win matches while helping the players perform at their best. We signed Ramos to score goals, but right now, he is helping me more than I am helping him.” A candid admission in defence of the striker he personally pushed to sign.

Compared to the win in Genoa two Sundays ago, Amorim is leaning toward not fielding four attacking players (Moreira, Cisse, Pulisic, and Ramos) from the start.

In yesterday’s training sessions, Cisse was left out of the starting line-up, which featured Musah and Modric in the middle, with Rabiot pushed further forward behind Gonçalo. Pavlovic has been called up and is eager to start, though he is not yet at 100% fitness.

“The choice of formation depends on the opponent and the team’s current state. I don’t believe that scoring more goals or winning more matches is necessarily linked to fielding more attacking players. Frosinone is an attractive side to watch, and speed is their primary weapon,” Amorim added.

“We rank second in Serie A for the number of sprints, and we have the tools to counter both their pace and their one-on-one ability on the flanks. We’ll need to be focused, as they commit more players to the final third than we do and are more clinical than us when winning the ball back.

“As for our season so far? I’m not satisfied with fourth place or all of our performances. We need to play better and achieve more in Europe, but we have grown as a team and in our overall attitude. We must keep moving in this direction.”

He concluded by sharing a Christmas wish regarding the transfer market: “January signings shouldn’t be based on the current league table, but rather on long-term planning. My Christmas wish is for us to win our next seven matches and get our injured players back. That is what I’d like.”
