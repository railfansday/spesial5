Arsenal player ratings vs Hull: 8/10 masterclass in jittery Gunners victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Martin Odegaard came to the rescue as Arsenal battled to a 1-0 win over Hull in north London.

It was a hard-fought occasion for the Gunners, who found Hull a tough nut to crack and for more than 70 minutes were kept at bay.

But Odegaard followed in after Noni Madueke’s shot was saved and that proved enough for victory, as the champions moved six points clear at the top of the Premier League for the time being.

Matt Verri was at the Emirates Stadium to rate the Arsenal players...
