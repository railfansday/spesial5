Gomes scores for Aston Villa in defeat by Bayern Munich in Hong Kong

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Aston Villa’s tour of the Far East ended in defeat as they lost 2-1 to German champions Bayern Munich in a pre-season friendly at the Kai Tak Sports Park in Hong Kong on Friday.

Bayern took the lead inside the final 10 minutes of the first half, with defender Kim Min-jae heading in from Tom Bischof’s sublime free-kick. Villa goalkeeper Marco Bizot made a string of saves in the second half to keep Bayern’s lead at one goal until former Liverpool forward Luis Díaz smashed in Bayern’s second off the underside of the bar.

Just as the game appeared to be out of reach for Aston Villa, who had struggled to create much going forward for most of the game, Unai Emery’s side showed some life late on. Summer signing João Gomes’s goal from the edge of the box gave them hope before Tammy Abraham nearly brought them back onto level terms, only to be denied by a fine save from 40-year-old Manuel Neuer.

Aston Villa are next in action in six days’ time when they take on Paris St-Germain in the Uefa Super Cup before kicking off their Premier League campaign at Brighton. Bayern have one more friendly to come against RB Leipzig before the DFL-Supercup against Borussia Dortmund. They then kick off the defence of their Bundesliga crown against Stuttgart at home on August 28.

For 80 minutes, Aston Villa barely looked like scoring but come so close to getting two goals in just a few minutes. Abraham meets a cross from the right and must think he has scored but his header is brilliantly saved by the 40-year-old Neuer.

Maybe this game is not quite done and dusted yet. A cross from the right picks out Gomes on the edge of the box. The Brazilian takes one touch before toe poking past Neuer and into the Bayern net. Gomes meant that finish and Villa have some hope.

Aston Villa’s preparations for the new season continue out in Hong Kong as they take on Bundesliga champions Bayern Munich at the Kai Tak Stadium. These sides have met twice before; Aston Villa beat Bayern 1-0 to win the European Cup in 1981/82 before then meeting in the Champions League group stages during the 2024/25 season, with Jhon Duran’s long-range screamer giving Aston Villa a 1-0 win at Villa Park.

After losing two of their first three pre-season friendlies, Aston Villa have won their first two games on Asian soil and this game marks the end of their tour of the Far East. It has been a summer of significant change at Aston Villa thus far with some key members of their squad departing and some new faces arriving. Youri Tielemans has departed after Manchester United triggered his release clause and they then sold Morgan Rogers to Chelsea for a British transfer record £117m. Unai Emery’s side have brought in 20-year-old Swiss midfielder Johan Manzambi, Brazilian midfielder Joao Gomes and Alejandro Garnacho, with the deal for Garnacho being an initial loan. Manzambi is currently on the sidelines due to a knee injury.

Unai Emery’s side were dealt a blow during the World Cup as Belgian midfielder Amadou Onana picked up a serious knee injury. They were though boosted by the return of Boubacar Kamara after seven months out as he scored in the 3-1 friendly victory over BG Parthum. As one midfielder returned from injury in that game, another picked up a fresh injury as John McGinn suffered a knock to his knee. The likes of Ollie Watkins, Ezri Konsa and Emi Martinez are yet to return after their world Cup exploits. They begin their Premier League campaign with an away game on the south coast against Brighton on August 23 but will be involved in the Uefa Super Cup against PSG next week having lifted the Europa League last season.

Bayern Munich head into this season looking to win a third straight Bundesliga title and have won two of their three friendlies, losing once. They have added Moroccan attacker Ismael Saibari from PSV and German left-back Nathaniel Brown from Bundesliga rivals Eintracht Frankfurt to their ranks this summer. The likes of Harry Kane, Dayot Upamecano and Michael Olise are not available as they are still yet to return after World Cup duty but Vincent Kompany has confirmed that Bayern’s German stars are set to return.

After today, Bayern have one more friendly against RB Leipzig before the DFL-Supercup against Borussia Dortmund. They then kick off the defence of their Bundesliga crown against Stuttgart at home on August 28.
