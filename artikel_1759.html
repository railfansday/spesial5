David Raya injury update as Mikel Arteta admits it’s ‘not good news’ for Arsenal star

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Arsenal have been handed a huge injury scare after David Raya was forced off in the second-half of their Premier League clash against Newcastle.

The Gunners, who led at the break, were held to a 2-2 draw at St James’ Park as a resilient Newcastle fought their way back into the contest.

While chasing an equaliser, Raya was involved in an innocuous coming together with William Osula in the penalty area.

The Spaniard appeared to be in real pain as he clutched his left arm, using his shirt as a makeshift sling as he left the pitch to be replaced by Kepa Arrizabalaga.

Raya was visibly distressed and at one stage appeared close to tears as he made his way off, sparking huge concern for the Gunners.

The 31-year-old has grown into a crucial figure at Arsenal, vital in their Premier League success last season having established himself as the best goalkeeper in the league.

Speaking after the draw, Arteta admitted the sight of his shot stopper in pain was ‘not good news’ with Raya to undergo an MRI scan on Sunday.

‘Unfortunately, he had to come off, which is not good news,’ Arteta said in his post-match press conference.

‘He will have an MRI assessment tomorrow, but at the moment I don’t know.’
