Chelsea XI vs Leeds: Confirmed team news, predicted lineup, Joao Pedro injury latest for Premier League

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso has a decision to make over who to start in goal against Leeds.

The Blues return to action at Stamford Bridge on Saturday and have both Mike Penders and Emi Martinez available and pushing to start.

Penders is seen as the long-term Chelsea No1 and deputised reasonably well while Martinez was out recovering following jaw surgery, but the veteran is now back fit and over the international break broke the record for the goalkeeper with the most clean sheets in history of Argentina’s national team.

Alonso cannot call on Joao Pedro, Maxence Lacroix, Jorrel Hato or Reece James after ruling all four out of the Leeds game at his press conference on Friday.

But Chelsea do have Josh Acheampong available, and Jamie Gittens is now back in training.

Alonso provided Chelsea a major boost by insisting Moises Caicedo is available against Leeds. The midfielder had asked to be substituted at half time of Ecuador’s match against Bolivia on Tuesday but the Chelsea manager has revealed he is fine.

Alonso will choose between Caicedo, Romeo Lavia and Jordan Henderson as he decides which midfield pair to start against Leeds.

Chelsea will be hoping to bounce back after defeat in their last game against Sunderland, who sat bottom of the Premier League table at the time.
