Steelers QB Aaron Rodgers’ fading powers highlighted by stunning SNF stat vs. Bengals

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Aaron Rodgers is old now. At 42 years old, the Steelers quarterback is no longer the MVP-winning superstar in his prime. Rodgers' performance to start Sunday Night Football against the Cincinnati Bengals is another perfect illustration of how much Rodgers' star has faded.

Rodgers went 0-of-7 to start the game against the Bengals, which, per ESPN Insights, is the most passes he's thrown without a completion to start a game in his illustrious 22-year career. His previous high before Sunday night was a mere four straight incompletions.

Rodgers eventually did complete a pass, but he went just 4-of-12 for 25 yards in the first half. On the other side, Bengals QB Joe Burrow was busy putting up vintage Rodgers numbers, going 26-of-34 for 302 yards with three touchdown passes to give the Bengals a commanding 24-3 lead.

Amid a 6-2 start to the season, Rodgers has been the clear weak link in the Steelers' chain. He's completing a career-worst 60.8% of his passes for 1,871 yards with 13 touchdowns and six interceptions.

That's just the sad reality of aging. Rodgers should be commended for still being a somewhat competent starting NFL quarterback in his early 40s. But not even an all-time great like him can ward off Father Time like Tom Brady seemingly did after playing until he was 45.

For the Steelers, they will have to come to grips with Rodgers' limitations if they are to remain successful this season. They need to continue to rely on the combination of defense and the running game to keep the wins coming.
