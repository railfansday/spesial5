Photos: Best images from Thunder's 117-112 loss to Hawks

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Oklahoma City Thunder fell short in a 117-112 loss to the Atlanta Hawks on Wednesday. The fourth-quarter offense once again struggled. Lu Dort and Aaron Wiggins one-upped their old NBA squads in their emotional returns.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander led the way with 34 points and five rebounds. Jared McCain helped with 18 points and three rebounds. Ajay Mitchell only had 13 points and six assists.

Meanwhile, Onyeka Okongwu stole the show with 28 points and nine rebounds. Nickeil Alexander-Walker finished with 19 points and seven assists. Jalen Johnson had a 16-point, 12-rebound and 11-assist triple-double. Dort had 16 points and Wiggins had 14 points.
