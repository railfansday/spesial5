Spurs Dylan Harper avoids major injury, likely out 2-3 weeks with hip strain

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

This could have been much worse. After further testing, the San Antonio Spurs have diagnosed guard Dylan Harper with a left hip external rotator strain, a story first reported by Jeff McDonald of the San Antonio Express-News.

There is no official timeline for a return, but it is likely a 1-3 week injury, according to reports. That feels like dodging a bullet for San Antonio.

Harper, a second-year guard, slipped on a wet spot on the floor and went down with just more than a minute left in the Spurs' loss to the Pistons on Tuesday. He clearly was in pain and stayed down while play continued on the other end, and he was eventually helped off the court and back to the locker room.

Harper, 20, has taken a step forward this season, averaging 18.4 points on 50.5% shooting, plus 5.4 rebounds and 4.5 assists per game, while playing high-level defense — he might already be the second-best Spurs player. The Spurs have a +22.3 net rating when he is on the court.

With Harper out, expect to see more Julian Champagnie, Jordan McLaughlin and Keldon Johnson — San Antonio has the depth to handle this loss. That said, this would be easier for the Spurs if Tobias Harris were healthy, but he remains unavailable.
