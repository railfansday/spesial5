Player Grades: Lakers vs. Jazz

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Thankfully for the Lakers, the Jazz almost certainly won't be a playoff team because that would be a grueling series.

Utah may be 3-16 this season, but they've given the purple and gold three separate tough games. And as a quick aside, what is going on with the schedule this season? All three games against the Jazz before Thanksgiving?

This ends a four-game homestand that was supposed to be a springboard for the Lakers but became something of an anchor. Instead of heading into a Black Friday showdown against the Spurs with momentum, they instead are looking for a reset of sorts, even after going 3-1 at Crypto.com Arena.

So, let's dive into the win. As always, grades are based on expectations for each player. A "B" grade represents the average performance for that player.

26 minutes, 14 points, 4 rebounds, 2 steals, 2 blocks, 1 foul, 6-9 FG, 2-5 3PT, +3

We've reached the intersection of Thybulle regularly making threes again and no longer exclusively shooting no-dip shots. His recent play has been one of the encouraging takeaways from this homestand.

37 minutes, 15 points, 10 rebounds, 2 steals, 1 assist, 4 fouls, 1 turnover, 6-10 FG, 1-4 3PT, 2-2 FT, +9

LaRavia returned to the starting lineup and looked like he didn't miss a beat. Even as well as Grimes has played in as a starter of late, it's hard to argue any lineup change right now once Reaves does return.

35 minutes, 10 points, 12 rebounds, 1 steal, 2 blocks, 1 foul, 4-5 FG, 2-5 FT, +16

No one is happier to see the Jazz in the rearview mirror than Kessler. What a frustrating string of games against his former team this season.

29 minutes, 9 points, 3 rebounds, 8 assists, 1 block, 3 fouls, 2 turnovers, 1-8 FG, 1-4 3PT, 6-6 FT

In most ways, Monday was a step back for Grimes, save for his playmaking. He was at the center of that fourth quarter run with five points and three assists in that 15-3 run.

32 minutes, 28 points, 4 rebounds, 8 assists, 1 steal, 4 fouls, 4 turnovers, 8-24 FG, 3-14 3PT, 9-10 FT, +14

No one is going to be more excited to have Austin back in the lineup than Luka, which is an obvious take but a very apparent one, too. He is shooting 7-35 from three in his last three games.

On Monday, he was hunting a bit too much for fouls on jumpers and missing the shot. The decision to try to draw a charge in the first half with three fouls was a really bad one, too.

13 minutes, 2 points, 4 rebounds, 2 assists, 2 steals, 1 foul, 1 turnover, 1-4 FG, 0-2 3PT, +10

It's unfortunate that Mamu will never make another 3-pointer. It really limits his offensive game. His turnover was also a pretty bad decision that he was immediately punished for.

10 minutes, 9 points, 1 foul, 1 turnover, 3-6 FG, 1-3 3PT, 2-2 FT, +0

Another strong showing from Carr, who is playing his way into minutes. His athleticism and shooting fit into any lineup.

19 minutes, 9 points, 4 rebounds, 1 foul, 3-5 FG, 2-4 3PT, 1-3 FT, +6

Ziaire moved back to the bench and looked solid again. His four-point play was big as he found his shot again.

11 minutes, 1 point, 2 rebounds, 1 steal, 2 fouls, 1-2 FT, -1

If Austin does return on Friday, it'll be interesting to see how the minutes play out. Head coach JJ Redick said he wants to find minutes for Carr and Vando always seems to be at the fringe of the rotation. Will he be the odd man out?

23 minutes, 12 points, 2 rebounds, 4 assists, 1 steal, 1 block, 4 fouls, 2 turnovers, 3-10 FG, 2-6 3PT, 4-5 FT, +14

Reaves' absence has helped Sexton play his way back into shape after missing the preseason. It wasn't an efficient night for Sexton, but he was big in the fourth quarter to help LA pull away.

Redick is committed, for now, on the zone defense that has it's strengths and weaknesses. Kyle Filipowski had a great night tearing it up at the free throw line, but LA also held Utah to 18.8% shooting from range and forced 16 turnovers.

He has also increasingly given Carr more minutes, and deservedly so.

Monday's DNPs: Carson Cooper, Jaden Hardy, Bronny James, Kevon Looney, Adou Thiero

Monday's inactives: Austin Reaves, Arthur Kaluma, Chris Mañon
