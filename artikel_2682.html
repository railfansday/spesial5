Jacory Croskey-Merritt returns to Commanders practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Commanders could get running back Jacory Croskey-Merritt back in the lineup as they try to win for the second straight week.

The team announced that Croskey-Merritt has been designated to return from injured reserve on Tuesday. He has missed the last four games with a groin injury.

Croskey-Merritt had 67 carries for 239 yards and two touchdowns in the first five games of the season. Rachaad White and Austin Ekeler have handled the backfield duties for Washington in his absence.

The Commanders have a 21-day window to activate Croskey-Merritt, but a good practice week could have him back in action against the Bengals in Week 11.
