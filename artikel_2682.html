49ers lose right tackle Colton McKivitz for season with fractured, dislocated ankle

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The 49ers lost starting right tackle Colton McKivitz for the season on Sunday night with a serious ankle injury.

Coach Kyle Shanahan confirmed the injury after the game and said it was particularly hard for the 49ers to see McKivitz get hurt because of how well respected he is within the locker room.

“That was really crushing. Colton’s had an unbelievable year. We care about all our guys, but it was tough to see Colton go down like that,” Shanahan said.

Shanahan said McKivitz appears to have the same injury that 49ers linebacker Fred Warner suffered last year. Warner had that injury in Week 6 and missed the rest of the season, so McKivitz will certainly not be able to play again this season after suffering the injury in Week 11.

“We think it’s broken and dislocated, similar to Fred’s last year,” Shanahan said.

McKivitz suffered the injury midway through the second quarter and was replaced by Vederian Lowe. Shanahan said Lowe played well for the rest of the game and the 49ers will be counting on him to play well for the rest of the season.
