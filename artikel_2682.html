Warriors rookie’s meteoric rise comes with glaring defensive concern

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Warriors have turned to rookie Yaxel Lendeborg while Steph Curry is sidelined.

The 24-year-old quickly put together a dominant stretch of games with Curry on the floor, but without the superstar, Golden State has struggled. Evidently, so has Lendeborg and the biggest concern for the No. 11 overall draft pick is on defense.

Yaxel Lendeborg #1 of the Golden State Warriors dribbles during a game against the New Orleans Pelicans on Monday, Nov. 16, 2026, in New Orleans. NBAE via Getty Images

ESPN's Zach Kram broke down a glaring concern regarding the rookie's play.

"And the second is his extremely negative on/off data. The Warriors have a 121.0 defensive rating with Lendeborg on the court versus a 99.3 mark when he's off, per Cleaning the Glass. That difference ranks him in the first percentile league wide. Opponents have gotten to the rim way more, committed fewer turnovers and run much more in transition with Lendeborg on the other end."

Golden State Warriors player Yaxel Lendeborg looks on during a game against the San Antonio Spurs in San Antonio, Texas, Friday, Nov. 13, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

Lendeborg admitted there needs to be defensive improvement as the Warriors continue to sink in the Western Conference. While the team has a record over .500, Golden State is only ahead of the 5-14 Pelicans, 4-12 Clippers, 3-16 Jazz and 2-15 Kings.

Regardless, Lendeborg is still regarded as a favorite to win Rookie of the Year. The forward has his sights set on another award, though.

Yaxel Lendeborg #1 of the Golden State Warriors plays defense on Victor Wembanyama #1 of the San Antonio Spurs during the 2026 Emirates NBA Cup on November 13, 2026 at the Frost Bank Center in San Antonio, Texas. NBAE via Getty Images

"The award would be nice, but I'm trying to be an All-Star this year," Lendeborg said earlier this month.
