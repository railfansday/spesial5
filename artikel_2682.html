Thanksgiving week features extra episodes of #PFTPM

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Thanksgiving week typically alters the usual PFT Live schedule. This year, it will include expanded #PFTPM offerings.

Like every year, PFT Live shuts down on Thanksgiving and Black Friday. After weeks of five two-hour shows every Monday through Friday, the crew deserves a break on the two days that most of the rest of the world takes a break.

Enter #PFTPM. If you’ve noticed (or if you haven’t), we’ve significantly increased #PFTPM this season. It’s a YouTube exclusive, with some of the clips landing in PFT posts.

This week, new #PFTPM episodes will land on Wednesday afternoon (the usual weekly nuggets episode), on Friday (a review of the three Thanksgiving games plus anything else of interest), the usual Saturday episode, and the pair of Sunday editions reviewing the early afternoon and late afternoon windows.

We’ll also be trying something new. With the NFL staging a Thanksgiving Eve game for the first time (Packers-Rams), I’ll be taping a reaction to the game immediately after it ends at or about 11:30 p.m. ET.

If nothing else, it will keep me from consuming excessive amounts of adult beverages during the game. Or not, which will make the episode even more interesting.
