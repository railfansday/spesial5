Report: Nuggets, Magic showing interest in free agent Kevin Porter Jr.

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Free agent veteran guard Kevin Porter Jr. is drawing some interest from around the league, specifically two teams in need of guard depth, Denver and Orlando, reports Michael Scotto of Hoopshype.

As always, take this with a grain of salt (I don't know Scotto's source, he's a very good reporter, but it very well could be Porter's agent, who will want to spin things a certain way). The fact that the Bucks didn't bring him back and no team snapped him up is worth noting.

Porter can get buckets, in 38 games with the Bucks last season he averaged 17.4 points, 7.4 assists and 5.2 rebounds per game. He can help a team.

Denver expected Tyus Jones to thrive as Jamal Murray's backup at point guard, but he has struggled with his shot and isn't having the impact the Nuggets hoped for. That said, Denver is already operating over the second apron, so adding a player, even on a minimum contract, becomes a massive expense. That will factor in.

Orlando has struggled with guard depth after Jalen Suggs' injury, but with Suggs back the Magic may be patient before making any moves (and they also face a healthy tax bill right now).
