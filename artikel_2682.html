Richard Childress Racing Loses Longtime NASCAR Sponsor Ahead of 2027 Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

One of the most familiar brands to grace Richard Childress Racing's No. 8 Chevrolet in recent years won't be returning in 2027.

Cheddar's Scratch Kitchen is ending its sponsorship of the NASCAR organization following the conclusion of the 2026 Cup Series season, the company confirmed in a statement to Sports Business Journal.

The departure brings an end to a partnership that lasted more than six years, spanned multiple drivers and grew into a significant commercial relationship for the two-car Cup Series operation.

It also comes as Richard Childress Racing prepares for a new chapter with Austin Hill behind the wheel of the No. 8 Chevrolet.

Cheddar's first entered into a sponsorship agreement with Richard Childress Racing in May 2020, when Tyler Reddick was in his rookie Cup Series season.

What began as an introduction to NASCAR team sponsorship eventually expanded into a deal that included more than six races as a primary sponsor of the No. 8 Chevrolet.

The restaurant chain remained with RCR when Reddick departed for 23XI Racing and two-time Cup Series champion Kyle Busch took over the No. 8 entry in 2023.

Cheddar's subsequently renewed its agreement with the organization ahead of the 2024 season.

The restaurant chain is owned by Darden Restaurants, whose portfolio also includes Olive Garden, Ruth's Chris Steak House and Yard House.

Cheddar's isn't the only familiar name that will be missing from Richard Childress Racing's sponsorship lineup next season.

The organization confirmed to Sports Business Journal on Monday that several sponsors whose marketing programs were tied to Busch and the No. 8 Chevrolet would not return in 2027. RCR did not identify those companies.

However, the team indicated that other commercial relationships were expanding ahead of Hill's first full-time Cup Series campaign.

"At the same time, several long-standing partners are increasing their investment and expanding their reach into the Cup Series in 2027 as RCR continues to build activations around Austin Hill," the organization said.

Hill brings existing sponsor relationships from his time competing for Richard Childress Racing in NASCAR's second-tier national series, giving the organization a foundation on which to build its 2027 commercial program.

For RCR, the offseason represents a period of transition both on and off the racetrack. Along with ushering in a new era for its No. 8 Chevrolet, the organization is reshaping the sponsorship portfolio that will support it.
