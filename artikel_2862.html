‘Kind of sad to see’: Aaron Rodgers brother laments the way things unfolded

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Steelers have been the focus of the NFL world this week for all of the wrong reasons. After the team announced late on Tuesday night that Aaron Rodgers would be benched and made inactive for Friday's game against the Denver Broncos, the four-time NFL MVP called into The Pat McAfee Show and went scorched earth on head coach Mike McCarthy.

Rodgers accused McCarthy of falling asleep during team meetings, being largely unprepared for practices and walkthroughs, and even ending a rambling rant during halftime of the Steelers' loss to the Philadelphia Eagles by saying, "1, 2, 3, Packers!"

Rodgers was, unsurprisingly, released by the Steelers later that day. While the sports world has been torn over whether Rodgers was right in his actions or not, his brother, ESPN analyst Jordan Rodgers, was scathing in his criticisms of the Steelers coach.

"I think what's getting lost in this is just how unacceptable and unprecedented some of the behavior that McCarthy has displayed and how unique that is," Jordan said on Good Morning America on Friday.

"I think I would be surprised if he continued to be the head coach moving forward, whether that's short term, long term, whatever it is. I think having been in locker rooms, having been a third-string quarterback on a practice squad, somebody who knew if I even looked like I was nodding off, I'd be cut in a second and to have that happen from the head coach, the play-caller, you can't play football that way and there's too many jobs at stake to put up with that type of behavior.

"I think the Steelers are looking into this, but seemingly the accusations, allegations are confirmed in a lot of ways, and he didn't rebut them. Crazy world we're living in but, yeah, weird way to see it end."

Jordan also made it clear how sad he was to see his brother's career, in all likelihood, end in such an ugly way.

"It's just kind of sad to see it end this way, in Pittsburgh," he said. "There was so much good vibes and hope about the reuniting of McCarthy and Rodgers and the potential to make a playoff run, one last dance. Unfortunately, it didn't happen that way."

While Aaron has been adamant that this will be his final season in the NFL, he cleared waivers following his release, making him a free agent. So, as long as a team is willing to take on the baggage of recent events, he still has a chance to join a quarterback-hungry team and make one more run.
