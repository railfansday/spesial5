Saints activate Kelvin Banks, Jordyn Tyson

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Saints offense will be getting a couple of reinforcements for Sunday’s game against the Browns.

They announced that they have activated left tackle Kelvin Banks and wide receiver Jordyn Tyson from injured reserve. Banks has been out since suffering an ankle injury in Week 2 and Tyson has yet to play this season due to a hamstring injury. Tyson was a first-round pick this season, so Sunday’s game will be his NFL debut.

As previously reported, the Saints released running back Kendre Miller in a corresponding move. They also released tight end Treyton Welch.

Kicker Charlie Smyth and running back Zamir White have been elevated from the practice squad on a temporary basis.
