NBA trade rumors 2026-27: Dejounte Murray update (or, non-update), Kyrie Irving, Trey Murphy, more

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Don't expect any NBA trades before Dec. 15, when many of the players who signed new contracts or extensions this summer become eligible to be traded. Even then, it's more likely we'll get closer to the February trade deadline before we see any notable names moved. That includes the guy in the sub-headline below.

While the insatiable NBA rumor mill keeps trying to find clickable updates on Dejounte Murray trades — "The Pelicans talked to the Suns!!!" — the reality is there is no traction toward any kind of deal for the New Orleans guard. Grant Liffman and Chris Mannix break it down well in the video above, but here is the latest.

• Murray, through his agents at Klutch Sports, made a trade request, and the Pelicans publicly acknowledged that, saying, "We will evaluate potential trade opportunities that are in the best interest of our organization." New Orleans hasn't found that yet. Meanwhile, Murray's body language on the court betrays how unhappy he is, and he is shooting 29.6% from 3-point range this season, which doesn't help his trade value.

• To be blunt, teams around the league view Murray as a negative value contract. Those are hard to trade. The fact he is making $32.8 million this season and has a $30.8 million player option for next season also gives teams pause. He is viewed as a 30-year-old guard who's shot 41.9% overall and 30% from 3 in the last two-plus seasons.

• While Murray's camp seems to continue to push the Suns as a destination, league sources have told NBC Sports the Suns have minimal interest, unless the Pelicans want to attach sweetener, such as a first-round pick. That's been reported a lot of other places as well, it's no secret. While the Suns could use a point guard and have done background work on Murray (according to Brian Windhorst of ESPN), they aren't sold on Murray being the All-Star player he was five years ago, and he hasn't looked the same coming off his torn Achilles. How much interest the Pelicans have in Jalen Green should be another question.

• When teams do call the Pelicans, they are more likely to ask about Trey Murphy III and Herb Jones. But Joe Dumars is keeping the price ridiculously high — the Pelicans turned down a trade that would have netted them at least two first-round picks for Murphy over the summer, Michael Scotto of Hoopshype reports.

• Jake Fischer of The Stein Line reports the Suns are more interested in adding a big man with Mark Williams out for the season.

• Utah is one of the teams giving "internal consideration" a potential Kyrie Irving trade, if (or, more likely when) the Mavericks start listening to offers, reports Ian Begley of SNY.TV.

It's worth noting that teams have internal discussions about a lot of players and deals that they never even bother to reach out to the other team about in a serious manner, and where Irving fits in the young timeline in Utah is a good question, but the rumor is out there.

• Multiple reports have coach Brian Keefe's seat in Washington getting hot. This was a team that made moves for veterans in Anthony Davis (playing well) and Trae Young (not playing well) to try and win now, and with the team off to a 5-11 start, someone may be the scapegoat. Washington should have just pivoted to building around AJ Dybantsa, but they are going to be stuck with that horrible four-year, $212 million extension they gave Young this summer for a while. That is the most untradable contract in the league right now.

• Multiple reports have teams interested in trading for Bulls guard Tre Jones. Here is what Zach Lowe said on his Ringer podcast: "Teams are already calling the Bulls about Tre Jones... if he's not the best backup point guard in the NBA, he's very close. If they trade him, they're gonna get real stuff."
