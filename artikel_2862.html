Saints release RB Kendre Miller

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Saints, on the last day before their first game since Week 7, have made a change.

Via Nick Underhill of NewOrleans.football, the Saints have waived running back Kendre Miller.

Miller arrived as a third-round pick (71st overall) out of TCU in 2023. He has 184 career carries for 656 yards, a per-carry average of 3.56 yards. He also has 29 career catches for 217 yards.

The Saints presumably tried to trade Miller but found no takers. He'll now go to waivers. If he clears waivers, he'll become a free agent.

Travis Etienne, who arrived in free agency, remains on injured reserve with a hamstring injury. The Saints currently have Alvin Kamara, C.J. Donaldson, and Audric Estimé on the depth chart. Estimé arrived recently on waivers from the Browns.

The 2-5 Saints host the 5-3 Browns on Sunday. The Saints hope to avoid their first 0-4 start at home since 1980 (not including the "home" game in Paris). That year, the Saints went 0-8 at home.
