Small 'advantage' may give Verstappen a head start in battle with Russell

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Starting from pole position, Max Verstappen has a chance to put George Russell on the back foot early in Las Vegas, as Pirelli observed that Red Bull Racing got its tyres up to temperature faster than Mercedes on the cold asphalt. Whether Verstappen can make the most of that advantage remains uncertain after a difficult final practice session. If Russell stays close behind him, the Briton will get the slipstream on The Strip that helped Verstappen so much in qualifying.

After qualifying, Verstappen said he had "been struggling with a few things in Las Vegas that were already in the car in Brazil." Earlier in the day, he had complained about the handling of his RB22 during the third practice session. Thanks to a successful first attempt in Q3 and Kimi Antonelli's grid penalty, he will start from P1 on Sunday, but it remains to be seen where Red Bull stands in terms of race pace.

The main question concerns how his long run unfolded. In fact, there is nothing clear to take away from Verstappen's side of the garage in FP3. He did not complete the kind of long run we have come to expect from him. Hadjar, on the same soft tyre, showed that Red Bull has found some consistency. With Verstappen, the question remains how his car would have held up if he had continued for longer.

The table below shows the long runs from FP3, with Verstappen's shorter one also included. Mercedes, McLaren and Ferrari did complete long runs, with Mercedes using the medium, Ferrari the soft, and McLaren splitting its running between the medium and soft tyres.

For Verstappen, the pace of the drivers directly behind him is especially important. Russell needed a little time to get going on the medium, then found a rhythm he maintained until the end of his run. Lando Norris, who starts behind Verstappen and Russell, was quick straight away on the soft.

McLaren also had the best long-run pace among the drivers in the table on both compounds. Fuel loads are unknown, but Verstappen will therefore also have to keep Norris in mind, especially in the early stages. Speaking to, among others, GPblog, tyre supplier Pirelli said that it believed Red Bull and McLaren had got their tyres into the right temperature window quickly.

In qualifying, Verstappen himself saw just how much it helps to have someone ahead of you on the long straights. "My first lap in Q3 was pretty much as good as it can be, just with the tow effect as well around the lap," he said of the attempt he made behind the McLarens. Hadjar saw in the data how much time he was losing on the straights and admitted he had underestimated the power of the tow.

If Verstappen holds the lead at the start, Russell could in turn benefit from having the Red Bull ahead of him as they head towards the end of The Strip. This is especially true when the battery starts delivering less electrical power on that long straight: the lower drag of running in another car's wake then matters and the chasing driver can carry more speed towards the braking zone. According to Pirelli, the slipstream or tow will have little effect on the tyres during the race given the cool conditions.

Pirelli expects some graining on the soft tyre on Sunday, particularly at the front. The rubber particles that form on the tread are difficult to clear in Las Vegas because the circuit has few corners that put the tyres under sustained lateral load. The tyre supplier believes the drivers will be able to manage it. In the front-wheel lock-ups seen during the sessions, the cold was the main factor. Until the front tyres are up to temperature, drivers lack grip under braking.

This also affects the strategy. Pirelli expects most drivers to start on softs and sees an opportunity from lap 22 to make it to the finish on mediums. An early stop to try to get past Verstappen through the pits is not particularly appealing for Russell. While he brings his new tyres up to temperature, Verstappen could still put in a quick lap on his warm set. If graining is indeed limited, Russell will probably benefit more from staying close as they head onto The Strip than from Mercedes calling him in first.
