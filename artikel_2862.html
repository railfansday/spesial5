Can Cameron Carr and Adou Thiero stay in the Lakers’ rotation?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Did I jinx the Lakers with my headline on the latest mailbag? At the very least, it wasn't a great look on my part.

My defense is that the Lakers were overdue for a loss and the energy and emotions in Tuesday's win over the Hornets eventually came back to bite them. They haven't been convincing in their current homestand and can certainly use a return from Austin Reaves, but things could be worse.

So, let's dive into your questions for this week.

We got a peek at the answer to this when the Lakers were in Milwaukee as head coach JJ Redick and the team were playing an 11-man rotation prior to Austin Reaves' injury.

That feels like stretching things a bit much unless it's at the end of a stretch of three games in four days or a long road trip. It's more likely the team settles on a 10-man rotation once Reaves returns, with either Cameron Carr or Adou Thiero as the swing player. When they went to 11 guys, both youngsters played.

I think the Lakers will maintain a 10-man rotation and keep Austin's minutes low once he returns. Right now, I wouldn't be surprised if Carr continues to get minutes after his strong showing against the Kings.

But as Redick recently stated, too, it's going to be very rare that the team has every player healthy and available for any significant stretch. Having a deep team allows you to absorb those injuries and continue to win as much as it allows you to extend the rotation.

The Lakers have had Luka and Austin on the floor together for 3/4 of a game in the last seven contests and are 6-1 in that span. That's because of the depth.

The next step in the transition will be the final step. It's just a matter of whether it will include the Buss family or not.

Based on the timeline for the sale from the Buss family to Mark Walter, the NBA finalizing the terms of the deal should come in the next month or two. However, the Buss family is set for a court date on Dec. 8. The only way it feels like they'd be included in the transition from Walter to Josh Kusher and Bob Iger is if the siblings come to an agreement at or before that date.

The more likely path is that the majority of the team is sold and the Buss family share is potentially included at a later date.

When it comes to any other positions being filled, that likely won't come until the sale is finalized. The hiring of Eric Amsler as assistant general manager filled the biggest opening.

Once the sale is official, it's really a blank slate for what positions to hire. The team should continue to look to expand out the analytics and scouting departments. But there isn't a limit to how many people they can hire and they absolutely take advantage of that.
