49ers midseason awards: Who is the most improved player on the roster?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The San Francisco 49ers lead the NFC, with a critical Sunday Night Football matchup in Mexico City against the Minnesota Vikings. Through nine games, the 49ers slipped up once in Atlanta, and a win over the Vikings could decide the all-important bye week in the Wild Card round. While it isn't quite "midseason," with only eight games remaining, this is close enough to hand out midseason awards for the 49ers.

While I was tempted to hand Purdy the MVP and OPOY award, I figured I'd take a page from the NFL voting process and give the MVP to the quarterback, with OPOY going to a skill position player. Pick a metric, and if Purdy isn't number one, he's at the very least top five. As for the actual MVP race, it feels unlikely that voters will place their ballot for Purdy over flashier names like Josh Allen or Joe Burrow.

Having said that, the 49ers aren't 8-1 without Purdy's improved play in 2026. According to SumerSports, Purdy leads in EPA/play (0.29), success % (56.9%), EPA/pass (0.29), pass success % (54.9%), YPA (8.97), rush success % (70.7%), and is second in total EPA behind Burrow (93.69) at 93.09.

On 364 snaps, McCaffrey leads all running backs with a total EPA of 40.06. Adding in his ten rushing touchdowns, 40 catches, 431 yards, and three receiving touchdowns, McCaffrey is producing a hugely efficient season. The addition of Kaelon Black has only spared McCaffrey hits and kept him fresh. His long touchdowns on Sunday in Dallas show that McCaffrey still has burst.

An incredible season following his high touch count last season has been the driving force for the 49ers offense, with the injuries to other skill position players.

Unfortunately, Gracen Halton's season was cut short, and he would have been a leading candidate for this award, but the uptick in Kaelon Black's play can't be ignored. Black has 79 rushes, 356 rushing yards, two rushing touchdowns, averages 4.51 YPC, and 3.38 yards after contact.

As the battering ram in the backfield, the 49ers boast one of the best running back duos in the NFL. His running style will translate well in January.

Warner has returned from his injury and been a forced-fumble machine. Accounting for an 11.3% run-stop percentage, the linebacker has helped anchor the stingiest run defense in the NFL. On 549 snaps, Warner has missed just eight tackles and has 22 impact plays.

The best linebacker in the NFL proves it day in and day out. His 2026 season is all the more impressive considering his ankle injury from last season. Truly one of a kind.

Last season was a down season for Puni as he dealt with an injury suffered in the preseason. Once he fully recovered, his play improved, but it was a step down from his stellar rookie season. With a 5.4% pressure rate allowed, Puni allowed 34 pressures and two sacks, with a pass-block win rate of 91.2%.

Puni has allowed 12 pressures in nine games with a pass-block win rate of 95.3%. Finally healthy, Puni is helping anchor the right side of the offensive line with Colton McKivitz. Overall, the offensive line has been very good in 2026, but Puni looks like he's taking a third-year leap.
