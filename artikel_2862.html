TV broadcast map for NFL Week 11: Find every game on Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

NFL Week 11 kicked off on Thursday night but there are still 12 games left to be played, including 11 total coming up on Sunday. Since this week's International Series matchup will air on Sunday Night Football, there are no early games to worry about.

Instead, we've got a ton of 1 p.m. ET kickoffs, starting with Arizona Cardinals facing the Kansas City Chiefs. Other 1 p.m. ET games include a Pennsylvania showdown between the Pittsburgh Steelers and the Philadelphia Eagles, an AFC East duel between the Miami Dolphins and the Buffalo Bills, and the Chicago Bears vs. the New Orleans Saints.

In the late-afternoon slot, the Las Vegas Raiders will face the Denver Broncos, while the Jacksonville Jaguars will face the New York Giants (both games at 4:25 p.m. ET). The New York Jets will also face the Los Angeles Chargers at 4:05 p.m. ET.

Find every remaining Week 11 game on one of the three TV maps below (via 506 Sports).

Arizona Cardinals At Kansas City Chiefs: RED (Ian Eagle, Ross Tucker)

Pittsburgh Steelers At Philadelphia Eagles: BLUE (Kevin Harlan, Trent Green)

Miami Dolphins At Buffalo Bills: GREEN (Spero Dedes, Adam Archuleta)

Jacksonville Jaguars At New York Giants: RED (Jim Nantz, J.J. Watt)

Las Vegas Raiders At Denver Broncos: BLUE (Andrew Catalon, Logan Ryan)

Tennessee Titans At Dallas Cowboys: RED (Joe Davis, Greg Olsen)

New Orleans Saints At Chicago Bears: BLUE (Kevin Burkhardt, Tom Brady)

Baltimore Ravens At Carolina Panthers: ORANGE (Kenny Albert, Jonathan Vilma)

Tampa Bay Buccaneers At Detroit Lions: YELLOW (Kevin Kugler, Daryl Johnston)

New York Jets At Los Angeles Chargers (4:05 p.m. ET): GREEN (Adam Amin, Drew Brees)

For the nightcap, the Minnesota Vikings (7-2) will face the San Francisco 49ers (8-1) on Sunday Night Football. Tonight's game starts at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC. This Week 11 showdown will be played in Mexico City. The Vikings acquired quarterback Kirk Cousins at the trade deadline, while 49ers QB Brock Purdy is having an MVP-caliber season.

Last but not least, the red-hot Cincinnati Bengals will face the Washington Commanders on Monday Night Football to close out NFL Week 11. The Bengals have won five straight games to take over first place in the AFC North. The Commanders have been a disappointment this season at 3-5-1. If it feels like you've seen the Commanders a lot lately, you're right. Monday's game will be the Commanders' fourth prime-time matchup in the last six weeks.

The Atlanta Falcons, Cleveland Browns, Green Bay Packers, Los Angeles Rams, New England Patriots and Seattle Seahawks are off for their bye this week. The Falcons will return from their bye in Week 12 to face the Minnesota Vikings on the road.
