Birmingham City pair may now regret summer transfer moves to the Blues: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Birmingham City delved into the European market to lure the pair over to England in the last transfer window. However, neither have been able to make the impact they would have wanted to at BCFC so far this season.

Lund, 24, was brought in after the Blues sold Kai Wagner back to Philadelphia Union and he has struggled to get into Chris Davies’ starting XI due to strong competition from the in-form Alex Cochrane. He has been limited to only three appearances in all competitions since his move, two of which have come in the Championship.

As for Vazquez, 25, he hasn’t been able to find his shooting boots and has failed to score in 14 outings altogether. The striker, who penned a contract running until June 2029, is down the pecking order behind August Priske is his position and faces a serious battle for game time.

Birmingham City are sat top of the Championship table above Middlesbrough and Stoke City on goal difference so you can understand why Davies is reluctant to change a winning team. They beat Swansea City 2-1 at home before the international break.

Birmingham City signed Lund to boost their defensive department. BCFC head coach Davies doesn’t seem to fancy him though and much prefers Cochrane.

Prior to his switch to the Blues, he had a loan spell at FC Koln in the Bundesliga. He put pen-to-paper on a long-term deal running until 2030 so he still has plenty of time to turn things around at St Andrew’s and force his way into the picture.

Vazquez provides cover for Priske but his confidence will be low at the moment. The ex-Boca Juniors man missed an open net against Derby County back in mid-September.

The former Argentina youth international desperately need to find the net but the longer his goal drought goes on, the more pressure he will be under to perform. He spent time on loan at Getafe last term in La Liga from Anderlecht before his previous team sold him permanently.

Birmingham City seem unlikely to offload Lund in January, despite his lack of minutes. If Cochrane picked up an injury, he would be needed by BCFC.

It wouldn’t be a surprise to see the Blues consider Vazquez’s future though. His transfer hasn’t worked out and if the price is right or a loan opportunity arises, they may be tempted to cut ties.

Davies would benefit from another attacker this winter and if they were able to get someone else in, they could do with freeing some space and funds by letting Vazquez depart. Birmingham City are back in action this weekend away at Stoke City.
