Revealed – when Nuno lost the West Ham dressing room

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

There’s a sense of deja vu for those reading of West Ham’s latest managerial casualty – the fourth since 2023- and what seems an overriding sense of relief amongst supporters that the end of the Nuno era has been delivered in a timely, swift, ruthless and businesslike manner.

Jacob Steinberg writing in the Guardian has confirmed how the dissatisfaction inside the club led to Daniel Kretinsky choosing to act decisively over dissatisfaction with Nuno’s performance, and the truth behind last weekend’s whispers that Nuno had lost the confidence of his players:

“Kretinsky has assumed more power and is said to have been unhappy with Nuno after attending West Ham’s 3-2 defeat by Southampton at the London Stadium.

Daniel Kretinsky decided to at decisively and with customary swiftness

There was further disquiet in the directors’ box when West Brom handed West ham a second consecutive home defeat. Kretinsky has recognised it was a mistake to stick with Nuno, who is due £3m in compensation…

While West Ham’s squad is far from perfect, the internal view is results should be better this season, especially as Nuno was able to break the EFL transfer record by spending £22m on Arne Engels from Celtic last summer. It is understood Nuno lost support in the dressing room in recent weeks. Nils Koppen, the director of recruitment, was especially unhappy with him”

Other sources claim the West Bromwich defeat last weekend when ‘the mood darkened’ was the moment the dressing room turned – along with the fan base, judging from the boos at London Stadium.

Credit to West Ham for acting swiftly and decisively whilst thirty three games remain to make good on the head start they’ve given their Championship rivals.
