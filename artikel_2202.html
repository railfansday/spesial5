Tony Mowbray’s words offer recall stance hint as Blackburn Rovers loanee continues to impress – it’s the right move

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Blackburn Rovers saw George Pratt break into the first-team squad last season. A shortage of defenders saw him brought into the senior ranks after a loan spell at Chorley, and he made a good impression before ultimately dropping back down the pecking order after the January window.

Many expected Pratt to be loaned out for the 2026/27 season as a result, and a move to Bradford City would follow. The 23-year-old had to bide his time a little as he watched on from the bench for his first two matches, but he has locked down a starting spot in a watertight Bantams defence since.

Pratt’s form and Rovers’ limited depth in defence have raised inevitable questions over a potential winter recall. Tony Mowbray recently spoke to the Lancashire Telegraph about the defender’s situation, and although there was no definite answer, it appears the plan is for Pratt to stay put.

Mowbray said he’s been keeping a close eye on Pratt’s performances and has been impressed. But, he acknowledges that there is room for improvement, and that the important thing is that the Bradford-born defender just keeps playing.

The Blackburn Rovers boss said: “I don’t sit and look at the game and think, wow, it’s Terry Butcher out there. He does what he does in certain parts of his game. They are developing a nice approach with the new manager of Bradford.

“We’re now at looking at, of course, what he needs to get better at. Otherwise, he’s not going to play for us. I can see he’s developing it and working on things. It’s great for us that he’s doing so well. And let’s see. At this moment, we’ve got four fit men to play centre-half at the moment.”

Pratt has been a mainstay in a Bradford City backline that has kept seven clean sheets in nine League One matches. They have conceded four goals in that run, three of which came in the 3-0 loss to Huddersfield Town before the international break.

He has played 40 accurate long passes, completed 47 recoveries and made 71 defensive contributions (FotMob).

Given the players available at centre-back and Mowbray’s belief that game time is of paramount importance, it feels as though the Blackburn Rovers boss is not minded to recall Pratt at the moment.

Watching him, Mowbray still feels there are strides to be made for Pratt, and he is only going to make the progress needed by playing regularly. Doing so in such a strong backline under a former defender in Michael Duff will be ideal for the 23-year-old’s growth.

It would indicate that, at present, Rovers are not planning on recalling Pratt. That stance may change if injuries hit at Ewood Park, or if departures await in the January transfer window.

At the moment though, it feels like Pratt is in the ideal place to keep progressing and Rovers do not have an urgent need to bring him back. Nonetheless, Mowbray and co will remain open and keep monitoring the situation in case a change of heart is required.
