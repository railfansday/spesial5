Trent McDuffie active for Rams-Cardinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Rams will have one of their star defenders back for Sunday’s game against the Cardinals.

Cornerback Trent McDuffie is active for the contest after he was listed as questionable with a back injury.

McDuffie did not play last week. He was listed as a non-participant on Wednesday and Thursday before being limited in Friday’s practice.

The Rams will also have safety Kam Curl (toe), who was listed as questionable.

With quarterback Carson Beck making his first start, the Cardinals will not have left tackle Jayden Williams (knee), who is inactive after being listed as questionable.

But defensive back Andrew Wingard (groin), defensive back Max Melton (knee/hand), defensive lineman Roy Lopez (toe/ankle), and defensive lineman Darius Robinson (wrist) are active.

Los Angeles’ inactives are quarterback Ty Simpson, safety Maximus Pulley, long snapper Joe Cardona, outside linebacker Darryl Peterson, offensive lineman Steve Avila, offensive lineman Keagen Trost, and outside linebacker Desjuan Johnson.

Arizona’s inactives are quarterback Joey Aguilar, cornerback Starling Thomas, linebacker Eku Leota, offensive lineman Jon Gaines, offensive lineman Hjalte Froholdt, and Williams.
