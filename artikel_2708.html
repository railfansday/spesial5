Yahoo Perfect 5: Victor Wembanyama, Giannis Antetokounmpo lead lineup with piece of every team for Friday's Prime doubleheader

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Yahoo Sports is giving fans another way to play fantasy basketball with Perfect 5, a free-to-play game featuring each week's NBA on Prime doubleheader. The rules are simple: pick five players, stay within a $15 budget and rack up fantasy points through scoring, rebounds, assists, defensive stats and 3s.

Friday's slate is an NBA Cup Group Play doubleheader: Heat at Knicks (7 p.m. ET) and Lakers at Spurs (9:30 p.m. ET). The injury report is busy again. Karl-Anthony Towns was downgraded to questionable with an abdominal contusion, Andre Drummond is out in concussion protocol and OG Anunoby is out with a calf strain. Giannis Antetokounmpo is probable with groin tendinitis, Austin Reaves is back from a heel bruise and De'Aaron Fox is questionable with a shin injury.

Using Yahoo projections, each player's last five games, the betting lines and the latest injury news, I ran 20,000 simulations of the slate against a simulated Yahoo field built from this week's pick rates. I'm pairing the two best fantasy players on the board and spreading the rest of the lineup across all four teams, so I have a stake in every game.

This group finished in the top 1% of the simulated field 23.4% of the time and beat every entry in the field in 1.49% of sims, and nobody in the simulated field had these exact five. It holds up whether Towns plays or not: 24.3% of the time in the sims where he played and 21.6% when he sat.

Wembanyama is the top projection on the board at 58.17 fantasy points. Over his last five games, he's averaged about 68 fantasy points, including 50 points, 18 rebounds, 4 steals and 5 blocks against the Pelicans last Friday, and 44 points with 16 rebounds at Portland on Wednesday. The blocks are what separate him: he's averaging 3.8 a game, and every one is worth three points.

You'll want some star exposure in a matchup featuring two of the NBA's best teams. San Antonio is a 6.5-point home favorite, and the 226.5 total is the higher of the two games. Wemby was in the single best lineup in 58% of my sims, more than anyone else. Yes, 61% of the field has him, but fading a player this far ahead of everyone else isn't worth it.

This is the pick that makes the lineup. Giannis is projected for 53.67 fantasy points, essentially even with Luka Dončić, but he's on 7% of rosters to Dončić's 58%. He's been a beast in Miami — scoring about 59 fantasy points a game over his last five, including 34 points, 14 rebounds, 8 assists, and 3 blocks against Detroit, and 32 points and 14 rebounds at Philadelphia on Wednesday.

He came into this game probable with a groin injury, but that shouldn't be an issue since he played 36 minutes against Philly a couple of days ago. And the Knicks' frontcourt looks weaker than usual: Drummond is out, Anunoby is doubtful and Towns is a game-time call.

Thybulle is the next best defensive-stats play on the slate to Wemby. He's averaging 2.3 steals and a block, and he's turned that into about 26 fantasy points a game over his last five, with at least 2 steals in four of them. He's added some offense too, scoring 14 points in each of his last two games. Reaves's return could trim his minutes a little, but his value comes from defense, not shots, and that holds up even in a smaller role.

Shamet gets this lineup into the early game without paying for a Knicks star whose status is up in the air (Towns). Shamet's averaging about 19 fantasy points over his last five, right on his 19.50 projection. He's played 30-plus minutes in three of his last four games. He should be in more Sixth Man of the Year conversations and, at 11% picked, he's also a cheaper way to get leverage than Klay Thompson, who's on 58% of rosters at the same $2 cost.

Conwell is the value that makes two $5 stars work. He's projected for 19.25 fantasy points at $1, the best points-per-dollar mark on the slate, and he's played 25-30 minutes in four of his last five games. He doesn't need a big night; 15 to 20 fantasy points from your $1 slot is plenty. He showed up in the optimal lineup in 51% of my sims, more than any player except Wembanyama. The rookie is gaining confidence by the game, so I'll keep riding the wave.

Dončić is projected for 53.95 fantasy points, but that number was built during a stretch where he's carried the Lakers by himself. Reaves is back Friday after missing five games, and some of the shots and touches Dončić has been soaking up will go back to his running mate. I trimmed his projection about 5% in the sims to account for that. Even at full strength, he'd be a tough sell: he costs the same $5 as Giannis, projects for about the same and is on eight times as many rosters.

Very close. Using Yahoo's projections, the highest-scoring possible lineup is Wembanyama, Dončić, Thompson, Thybulle and Conwell at 173.75 fantasy points. Mine comes in just 2.04 points behind it.

The max lineup finished in the top 1% only about 12% of the time, roughly half my lineup's rate. Swapping Dončić for Giannis and Thompson for Shamet gives up two projected points and gets you out of the game's most crowded build.

You can start building your Perfect 5 roster for Friday's slate now, including going up against me in my Perfect 5 group.
