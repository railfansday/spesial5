Ja’Marr Chase has spectacular showing in Bengals’ big win vs. Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Ja'Marr Chase said he loves primetime games. On Sunday night, he showed everyone why.

Chase caught 11 passes for 231 yards in the Cincinnati Bengals' 38-18 win over Pittsburgh at Paycor. It was the fourth 200-yard game of his career and the third-best yardage total he's ever had, and it came against a Steelers team that walked in tied for first in the AFC North and walked out a game behind.

Chase didn't find the end zone, and it didn't matter. He was the engine of the drives that buried Pittsburgh early. His two deep shots, a 53-yarder and a 43-yarder, each set up Bengals touchdowns. The Bengals scored on four of their five first-half possessions and took a 24-3 lead into the locker room, while the Steelers managed just 40 total yards and four first downs before halftime.

The only thing that stopped Chase was the scoreboard. With the game in hand, the Bengals eased off in the fourth quarter and brought in Joe Flacco for mop-up duty.

Sunday was Chase's fourth regular-season game with at least 200 receiving yards.

The headline record of the night belonged to his quarterback. The Bengals also completed 21 passes before Aaron Rodgers completed his first, tying the most by one team before its opponent's first completion since 1978.

None of that happens without No. 1 stretching the defense. When Chase is getting behind safeties like he did Sunday night, everything underneath opens up for Chase Brown, Drew Sample, and Dohnte Meyers, which is exactly how all four touchdown passes were scored.

The Bengals are 7-2 and alone in first place in the AFC North. And their best player just reminded everyone that the bigger the stage, the better he gets.
