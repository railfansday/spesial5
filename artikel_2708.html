Brittany Mahomes shreds referees after crucial call harms Chiefs in tough Bills loss: What a joke’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Brittany Mahomes and the Chiefs are not thankful for NFL referees this year (insert your Chiefs-referees jokes here).

The wife of Kansas City star quarterback Patrick Mahomes shredded the zebras after a controversial call went against her husband's team in the 13-10 last-second road loss to the Bills on Thursday night that could have massive implications in the AFC playoff race.

The Chiefs' (8-3) penultimate drive featured an 11-yard third-down catch by Rashee Rice that would have gone for a first down being overruled in the final two minutes.

"HAHAHAHAHAHA WHAT A JOKE," Brittany posted late Thursday night to her Instagram story. "Guess we can't even play football without getting it called against us… not sure what an actual catch is after that I guess? I don't wanna hear s–t anymore from anyone."

Brittany Mahomes criticizes referees after a call against the Kansas City Chiefs during their loss to the Buffalo Bills.

Brittany also posted several comments of people saying they believe Rice's catch should have stood as called, plus one fan calling out the referees for a bizarre sideline scene during which one of the officials stood around a heater on the Bills' sideline while surrounded by Buffalo's players.

It's not a given the overturned call would have affected the ultimate outcome but it stalled a late Kansas City drive that had potential in a game that could affect the Super Bowl.

Facing a third-and-10 from their own 9-yard line, Mahomes found Rice in traffic right beyond the marker to seemingly convert a critical third down.

When the referees reviewed the play, they determined that the ball hit the ground while Rice fell to the grass and ruled it an incomplete pass.

Rice told reporters, "Yes, I did," when asked if he believed he caught the pass, and Patrick said he thought they were reviewing the spot, not the catch, and believed Rice made the play, per ESPN.

Mark Butterworth, the NFL's vice president of instant replay, said they believed that Rice did not maintain possession.

The tip of the ball hitting the ground during Rashee Rice's attempted catch. @MySportsUpdate/X

"As the receiver [was] going to the ground, he had the ball in his right hand and when he contacted the ground, his hand slid up the ball and we deemed that a loss of control," Butterworth said, per ESPN. "The hand moving up the ball is what we deemed the loss of control."

That call not only mattered since it ended the possession, but it helped swing the field-position game in a game affected so much by the weather.

The Bills began their next possession at the Chiefs' 47-yard line after a punt but did nothing with that possession, resulting in another punt.

Patrick Mahomes, left, and Brittany Mahomes attend the Sports Illustrated Swim Issue Launch Party at the Hard Rock Hotel in New York, Thursday, May 16, 2024. WWD via Getty Images

The Chiefs went three-and-out again before another punt, and a 17-yard return set up Tyler Bass' 57-yarder as time expired.

Kansas City coach Andy Reid did not agree with the decision that factored into his team falling a half-game behind the Broncos in the AFC West before Denver faces the Aaron Rodgers-less Steelers on Friday.

"I'm not quite sure what they saw," Reid said, per ESPN. "I saw he had control of the ball and his elbow hit and then the ball hit [the turf] but he had control. Normally in those situations, it's got to be clear and obvious. To me, that was not clear and obvious."
