Houston Rockets vs. Phoenix Suns game preview

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Phoenix Suns were going to be bad (but not too bad!) and land somewhere between the fourth and tenth worst team in the NBA. Then the Houston Rockets would get their three ping pong balls and whatever the Brooklyn Nets deemed to give them—likely two—and the Rockets would land a couple of top ten picks. Certainly not the top pick. That's never happening again in my lifetime. But a couple of good picks!

And the Suns started 1-5! That wasn't a great record with the new lottery system, but you figured they would turn things around and win some more games and settle in comfortably in the three-ball area. Tri-ball? There's a joke here but I can't find it.

The problem is that no one bothered to tell the Suns about this plan. Phoenix is 8-2 since that point and have slingshot past the field of bad teams into the play-in spots. They're just two back in the loss column from Minnesota, Denver, Memphis, and Houston. Tonight marks the end of a five-game road trip for Phoenix, and the Suns are 4-0 so far. Yes, three of those games were Sacramento, Utah, and New Orleans, but the first game was the second night of a back-to-back in Denver. And in case you've forgotten, Houston has lost to both Denver and Phoenix. So you don't get to mention their schedule.

This is a big game with massive implications for both teams. Or at least, as massive as a game in November can be. The Rockets want to keep moving onward and upward, much like the Suns. The Rockets would like to knock down the Suns a peg or two to remove competition for the top 6 spots in the West and to improve their draft outlook come June. And on a personal level, it's clear that Dillon Brooks and Jalen Green take this matchup extremely seriously.

Oh, and this is technically Houston's last NBA Cup game. Remember that the schedule will update soon with two more games during that current lull in early December. We'll talk about that when it happens.

AK note: I just ran a lottery simulation on Tankathon and the Suns picked moved all the way up to 2. Is there anything more Rockets than getting the #2 overall pick in a one-player draft?

Happy Thanksgiving to those who celebrate!

Saturday night at home against the Philadelphia 76ers
