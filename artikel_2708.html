Tom Brady takes dig at Aaron Rodgers amid Mike McCarthy drama

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Tom Brady had a message for Aaron Rodgers as the controversy surrounding the Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback and head coach Mike McCarthy continues to be the biggest talking point across the NFL. The seven-time Super Bowl champion shared his thoughts while calling the Thanksgiving matchup between the Philadelphia Eagles and Dallas Cowboys on Fox, discussing the situation with Kevin Burkhardt.

Brady focused on the value of the NFL locker room and why players should be careful about allowing private matters within a team to become public.

"The sanctity of the NFL locker room is a very sacred place and we shouldn't ever lose track of that as players," Brady said. "It's a privilege to be in that locker room. You're fighting with your teammates, you're fighting with your coaches, you're always acting as a team together. Shouldn't always be the place for, you know, private things to become public."

He also explained that his experience playing alongside several great teammates shaped how he viewed the responsibility that comes with being part of an NFL team and stressed that individual interests should never take priority over the collective goal.

"I was fortunate to be around a lot of great teammates over the years. And what made a great teammate to me was you cared about your teammates and your coaches and you cared about what the team was trying to accomplish. And that supersedes any individual aspect of your game that you want to promote."

He also reflected on his own career, pointing to the team-first mentality that defined his time in the league.

"I played for my teammates. I played for my coaches. There's nobody wins when the family feuds."

Brady's remarks came after Rodgers publicly criticized McCarthy following his benching in Pittsburgh. Appearing on "The Pat McAfee Show" on Wednesday, Rodgers accused the coach of falling asleep during meetings, missing team activities and struggling to connect with players.

He also described McCarthy mistakenly ending a halftime huddle with "1-2-3 Packers" during Pittsburgh's loss to Philadelphia. The Steelers subsequently released Rodgers, bringing their brief reunion to an abrupt end. The two previously spent 13 seasons together in Green Bay, winning Super Bowl XLV.
