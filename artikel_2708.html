AFC North Preview, Week 11: Bengals look to keep separating from the pack

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Matchup: @ Philadelphia Eagles, 1:00pm EST

The Steelers came out flat last week despite coming off their bye week. In a primetime spot with the chance to take a first-place lead in the division, the Steelers punted on four of their first five drives. They didn't score a touchdown until the fourth quarter, but it meant little as they were trailing by 28 points. Pittsburgh still has six wins on the season, though, and is set up well to earn a seventh victory in Week 11.

A matchup with the Eagles is much less imposing now than it was several weeks ago, as Philadelphia has been riddled by injuries and traded away several core players. They have lost seven straight games and the last four have come by eight or more points. To avoid a trap game on the road, the Steelers need to avoid turning the ball over and giving the Eagles' offense — which ranks bottom-10 in both the pass and run — any short fields.

The Eagles have vulnerable defending the run this season, allowing 126.1 rushing yards per game. That affords the Steelers an opportunity to get their rushing attack back on track after Jaylen Warren and Rico Dowdle combined for 41 yards on 12 carries last week. Pittsburgh's passing game continues to be inconsistent as well with D.K. Metcalf struggling as the lead No. 1 wideout. Any potential big play that Aaron Rodgers can connect on through the air will be a bonus.

Following a fifth-straight loss this past Monday to the previously winless Los Angeles Chargers, the Ravens' season is officially on life support heading into Week 11. The Ravens were unable to capitalize on turnover opportunities, struggled to defend the run, and committed two costly turnovers of their own. They're now two games under .500 and, in order to keep any semi-possible playoff chances alive, they can't afford to lose any of their next few games.

This week's matchup against the Panthers profiles as perhaps their most favorable contest remaining on the schedule. There are a couple particular areas where the Ravens match up well and need to take advantage. On offense, it's their wide receivers versus Carolina's cornerbacks. The Panthers have been without their starting cornerbacks for several weeks now. They've managed to hold up and rank Top 10 in passing yards allowed, but Zay Flowers, Rashod Bateman and Ja'Kobi Lane will still have opportunities to win their individual matchups and get open consistently. Given the team's struggles run blocking lately, the Ravens may be better off leaning on Jacoby Brissett's arm and spreading out the defense.

Defensively, the Ravens need to tighten up their coverage over the middle of the field. Bryce Young's biggest strength throwing the ball is targeting that area and the Panthers have been effective doing so this year. They have several tall, athletic pass-catchers that have to be accounted for. It will help if the Ravens can create some pressure and convert sacks. The Panthers' offensive line, particularly their tackles on both sides, has been a weak point. This is a prime spot for Trey Hendrickson to get back in the sack column and for Mike Green to continue his strong run of play recently.

Matchup: @ Washington Commanders, 8:20pm EST

The Bengals continued rolling last week with another convincing divisional win, defeating the Steelers handedly by 20 points (38-18). Led by another MVP-caliber performance from Joe Burrow and signature showing from Ja'Marr Chase, the Bengals led from start to finish — using a 21-point second quarter to quickly pull away. They will hit the road in Week 11 for a second straight primetime outing, this time against the Commanders.

On paper, the Bengals should continue to find success throwing the ball in this game. The Commanders allowed the second-most passing yards per game at over 244 and have a vulnerable secondary. They are much better against the run, but running the ball has not mattered much at all for the Bengals this season. Burrow has been unstoppable airing the ball out to Chase and Tee Higgins, which they will likely continue to lean on here.

The Commanders have been one of the league's most turnover-adverse offenses this season, although Jayden Daniels did throw two fourth-quarter interceptions last week. If the Bengals can force a takeaway or two, it will only strengthen their chances of winning by giving their offense shorter fields. The Bengals match up well defensively with the Commanders but should not let their guard down. Washington has shown an ability to hang around in games and put together late scoring drives.
