Patriots place TreVeyon Henderson on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Patriots running back TreVeyon Henderson’s absence from the lineup will stretch through at least Week 15.

The Patriots placed Henderson on injured reserve Saturday as he continues to deal with a neurological issue that kept him out of the team’s last two games. Henderson was ruled out for this Sunday ahead of the team’s trip to Germany to face the Lions.

Rhamondre Stevenson is listed as questionable for that matchup with a knee injury, which leaves Corey Kiner and Lan Larison as the healthy running backs on New England’s roster.

In addition to the Henderson move, the Patriots activated fullback Reggie Gilliam and guard Greg Van Roten from injured reserve. They also elevated guard Andrew Rupcich from injured reserve. Van Roten and Rupcich help fill out a position group that will be missing Ben Brown against the Lions. Walter Rouse is listed as questionable due to a concussion as well.
