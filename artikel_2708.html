Charles Barkley calls out Kyle Kuzma for reckless play that hurt Luka Doncic

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Los Angeles Lakers beat the Milwaukee Bucks, 123-112, on Wednesday night. The NBA's leading scorer, Luka Doncic, had 32 points in 32 minutes. Doncic started the fourth quarter on the bench with the Lakers up 21. When the Bucks cut the lead to 20 with 6:51 remaining Doncic checked back in.

Doncic scored six points before he checked out with 4:25 remaining. He left the game because he hurt his ankle on a reckless closeout from Kyle Kuzma. Everything about the play was unnecessary, from Kuzma getting in Doncic's landing zone to Doncic kicking out his leg to the fact that he was even in the game.

With Austin Reaves and Colin Sexton also getting hurt in the game, coach JJ Redick didn't even mention the incident after the game and simply noted that Doncic was "fine." Despite the fact that the Lakers didn't seem upset and the officials took a look at the play and decided not to upgrade the personal foul to a flagrant, it still drew criticism on "Inside the NBA." While showing highlights Charles Barkley called out Kuzma in passing.
