Fernando Mendoza's second TD pass tied Raiders-Broncos at 17-17

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

After the Broncos failed to convert on fourth-and-1 in their own territory, quarterback Fernando Mendoza connected with Ian Thomas for a game-tying, 19-yard touchdown midway through the third quarter.

Facing fourth down at their own 39-yard line, the Broncos elected to go for it with a QB sneak. But Bo Nix wasn’t pushed across the line to gain, resulting in a turnover on downs. The officials reviewed it and the call was upheld.

With great field position, Las Vegas took full advantage of the opportunity, largely with the run game. Mike Washington took four consecutive carries before Mendoza faked it to him, rolled to the right, and hit Thomas in stride down the field for his second career passing touchdown.

Mendoza is now 8-of-15 for 150 yards with two touchdowns.

On the injury front, Raiders defensive tackle Adam Butler (concussion) and defensive tackle Tim Keenan (knee) have been downgraded to out.
