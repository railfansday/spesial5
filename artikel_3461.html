8 Biggest Snubs in the 2026 Game Awards Nominations

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Game Awards has announced its official list of nominees for this year’s prizes. Among the nominees are plenty of obvious contenders, such as Grand Theft Auto 6, Resident Evil Requiem, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. There was stiff competition across several categories, meaning that some worthy games — and Polygon favorites — didn’t make the shortlist for certain prizes. These are the biggest TGA nomination snubs of 2026.

As one eagle-eyed Resetera user pointed out, Sony just broke a streak that dates all the way back to 2015. This marks the first time in 11 years that a first-party PlayStation release hasn't received a Game of the Year nomination. During that 11-year streak, Sony took home three GOTY trophies: God of War (2018), The Last of Us Part 2 (2020), and Astro Bot (2024). This year's big first-party game from PlayStation was Marvel's Wolverine, which is currently sitting at a 75 on Metacritic and incited a whole lot of debate about difficulty and UI design. The game was nominated in two categories: Best Performance for Liam McIntyre and Innovation in Accessibility.

This outcome won't come as a surprise to Polygon's Oli Welsh, who predicted Marvel's Wolverine missing out on a GOTY nomination and snapping Sony's impressive streak all the way back in September.

There were a ton of fantastic video game performances this year, and somebody’s favorite was bound to get left out. Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave fan favorite Jimmie Yamaguchi gave an outstanding performance as the oddball scholar Fabio, and by every measure, it deserved a nomination. In less capable hands, Fabio’s starry-eyed devotion to Fortune’s Weave co-protagonist Dietrich could have been played for laughs, but Yamaguchi makes the budding romance between these men believable and heartfelt.

Manni L. Perez was nominated for GTA 6, presumably for portraying Lucia Caminos. (Funnily enough, developer Rockstar Games has not yet officially confirmed Perez is actually playing Lucia, despite longstanding online rumors.) Apparently, the actor who played co-lead Jason Duval didn’t make enough of an impact with players to score a nomination.

Blizzard’s World of Warcraft has had a massive year, with the launch of its reimagined Classic experience, World of Warcraft: Forever. The team also released major updates for the modern version of the beloved MMORPG, like March’s Midnight expansion. Those changes weren’t enough to move the needle with voters, and WoW was also left out of the Best Community Support category.

The popular farming sim, which entered early access in 2024 before its full release this year, may have more than 26,000 “overwhelmingly positive” reviews on Steam, but that wasn’t enough for it to snag a nomination in any category. Best Debut Indie might have been Fields of Mistria’s best shot at a prize, but it wouldn’t have been out of place in Best Sim/Strategy or the broader Best Indie category, either.

Despite a wobbly third act, Fortune’s Weave is one of our favorite games of 2026, one of the best games available for the Nintendo Switch 2, and one of the best-reviewed games of the year. Surprisingly, developer Intelligent Systems’ epic strategy RPG only managed to pick up one nomination, for Best Role-Playing Game. It stands a decent chance of taking this one home: other nominees include Final Fantasy Resonance, Monster Hunter Stories 3, The Blood of Dawnwalker, and Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter.

Pearl Abyss’s open-world adventure game was an early-year frontrunner for a GOTY nom, even after middling reviews and a rocky launch. While it wasn’t a critical darling, it did have a pretty massive online following that made the prospect of several nominations seem likely. Surprisingly, Crimson Desert will compete in just one category: Best Community Support.

Bungie’s extraction shooter managed to snag a nomination for Best Multiplayer Game, which it frankly stands little chance of winning against a field that includes two indie chart-toppers and a well-regarded Call of Duty. However, the stylish game was overlooked in the Best Art Direction category, perhaps partly due to the shadow cast by a 2025 plagiarism scandal where Bungie admitted to using an artist’s designs without proper credit. (Bungie later credited her as a Visual Design Consultant.) Marathon’s retro-future meets Y2K cyberpunk aesthetic truly stands out in a sea of Unreal Engine 5 homogeneity, and it’s a shame to see it overlooked here.

Both the Best Indie and Best Debut Indie categories have some stiff competition this year. However, no independent game managed to get a nomination for Game of the Year in 2026. While there weren’t a ton of indie contenders for the top category this year, Polygon’s Oli Welsh had the co-op adventure Big Walk pegged as a dark horse, though developer House House’s recent AI controversy likely didn’t help matters.

The lack of an indie GOTY nominee represents a pretty massive shift from the 2025 crop, which included a record-setting three games developed by independent studios: Hades 2, Hollow Knight: Silksong, and Clair Obscur: Expedition 33. Sandfall Interactive’s much-loved RPG managed to take home the top prize and a total of nine Game Awards, the most wins by a single game. 2024 also saw one indie crack the category, Balatro, though the top honors went to Astro Bot.

The Game Awards 2026 will stream live on Disney+, YouTube, and Twitch, on Thursday, Dec. 10, starting at 7:30 p.m. EST / 4:30 p.m. PST.
