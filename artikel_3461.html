Spoiler alert for the post-credits scene of VisionQuest

Fan reactions are officially in for Marvel's post-credit scenes of VisionQuest.

The Disney+ series concludes the WandaVision trilogy, following White Vision's journey to reconnect with his humanity. However, the finale saved its biggest surprise for last.

Fans have taken to social media to celebrate the return of one of the MCU's most beloved characters, Elizabeth Olsen's Scarlet Witch. The second post-credits scene reveals Doctor Doom resurrecting Wanda Maximoff, potentially setting up her return in Avengers: Doomsday.

Fans are not holding back their excitement. Taking to X, @POPin4k exclaimed:

"WANDA IS ALIVE OMG WE ARE SO BACK... MOTHER RETURNS TO THE MCU"

Similarly, @siilllver celebrated the reveal, writing:

"LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE QUEEN HAS RETURNED"

The excitement continued, with @syriangeekvibes declaring, "WE ARE SO BEYOND BACK," while @oyemnassxo celebrated Wanda's return following Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness.

Meanwhile, @bekhayalime expressed their loyalty to the character, writing:

"SCARLETT WITCH YOU HAVE MY LOYALTY FOREVER"

The reveal has also fueled excitement for Avengers: Doomsday. @AvengersUpdated hinted at a potential connection, writing:

"The ‘AVENGERS: DOOMSDAY’ clock is now glowing red following the Scarlet Witch’s return."

Rebooted after the events that restored his body, Vision has gone into hiding from the people who want to weaponize him. Now searching for purpose, he consults the artificial intelligence personalities embedded in his programming, but a bounty on his head forces him to go on the run with Thomas Shepherd, a mysterious boy who may be the reincarnation of his son.
