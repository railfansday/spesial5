Waller Thinks Fed Should Consider Half-Point Rate Hike

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller urged the central bank to consider a larger, half-percentage-point rate hike at its December meeting. Waller said inflation remains headed in the wrong direction in comments Wednesday on CNBC. Michael McKee reports on Bloomberg Television. (Source: Bloomberg)
