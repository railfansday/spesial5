Homeward cash-offer product launches nationwide for agents

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Homeward expanded its agent-only cash-offer program from 13 states to all 48 contiguous states. The company says sellers can close in as little as 14 days and receive any resale upside back, while agents gain more certainty and timeline control.  AI Summary

Homeward has expanded its cash-offer product from 13 states to all 48 contiguous states, giving real estate agents nationwide a way to offer sellers a cash sale, the company announced on Wednesday.

The Austin-based company, which works only through real estate agents, said agents in every contiguous state can now bring a Homeward cash offer to their listings. The product is designed to reduce the risk of failed transactions tied to buyer financing or last-minute cancellations.

The move comes as contract fallout remains a concern. In a low-inventory, high-mortgage rate market, a canceled contract often means agents must re-market the property while managing client frustration and timing pressures around the seller’s next move.

“Agents are doing everything right and still watching deals fall apart at the finish line,” Tim Heyl, founder and CEO of the HousingWire Tech100 firm, said in the announcement. “A cash offer fixes that, but most cash buyers make their money by underpaying the seller. We don’t. Now agents everywhere can give their clients a sale they can count on, without asking them to leave money on the table.”

Through the cash-offer program, homeowners can sell to Homeward for cash in as little as 14 days and skip traditional showings, according to the company. Unlike many investor or iBuyer models, Homeward said any upside when the home is later resold is passed back to the seller rather than retained by the buyer.

For listing agents, the pitch centers on certainty of close and timeline control. A cash buyer eliminates the need to navigate financing contingencies, re-negotiations after appraisal issues and the risk of a buyer walking away late in the process. That stability can be critical for sellers who must coordinate a purchase on their next home or who need proceeds on a specific schedule.

For housing professionals, products that convert financed buyers into effective cash buyers or that provide institutional cash to sellers have become more prominent as higher mortgage rates, tighter credit conditions and affordability constraints increase fallout risk. Homeward’s nationwide expansion gives brokerages and teams in smaller and midsize markets access to a tool that had been limited to 13 states.

Alongside the nationwide cash-offer launch, Homeward said its Buy Before You Sell program is on track to be available in most states by the first quarter of 2027.

The program is designed for move-up or move-down buyers who need to tap their existing equity but want to avoid a home sale contingency. Homeward pairs short-term bridge financing with a guaranteed backup offer on the customer’s existing home, allowing agents’ clients to write offers on a new property without first selling their current one.

Buy Before You Sell is currently available in 16 states: Arizona, California, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Idaho, Iowa, Maryland, North Carolina, Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, South Carolina, Tennessee, Texas and Washington.

Bridge-style and buy-before-sell products have gained traction over the past several years as a way to unlock trapped equity for existing homeowners. For agents, they can shorten transaction timelines, reduce dependence on contingent offers and help keep purchase deals intact if the client’s sale is delayed.
