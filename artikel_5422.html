Mission Telecom Partners with Kajeet to Deliver Managed Wireless Connectivity for Schools, Libraries, and Nonprofits

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Schools, libraries, and nonprofits will now have access to affordable connectivity plans, education-ready hotspots, and flexible and secure management tools

ENGLEWOOD, Colo., Nov. 18, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Mission Telecom, a nonprofit telecommunications provider and grantmaker dedicated to expanding digital equity, today announced that its affordable wireless broadband plans are now available on Kajeet's award-winning Sentinel platform. The integration combines Mission Telecom's low-cost 4G and 5G connectivity with Kajeet's advanced device management, analytics, and security tools, giving schools, libraries, and nonprofits a cost-effective way to expand and manage internet access in their communities.

The integration enables new and affordable "smart" broadband plans for mobile hotspots, tablets, and fixed wireless internet. Through Sentinel's self-service management tools, real-time reporting, customizable controls, and CIPA-compliant content filtering, organizations gain greater visibility and control over their connectivity programs. These capabilities simplify day-to-day operations, help organizations make the most of limited budgets, and support the growth of connectivity programs.

"Affordable internet is essential, but it's only part of the solution," said Mark Colwell, Executive Director at Mission Telecom. "Schools, libraries, and nonprofits are being asked to do more with less. By bringing our affordable wireless plans to the Sentinel platform, we're making it easier for organizations to deliver secure, reliable connectivity without adding complexity or cost. That means less time managing technology and more time connecting people to opportunity. This is a true 'win-win' that supports our partners and the communities they serve."

Interested schools, libraries, and nonprofits can sign up for service by visiting the Mission Telecom website.

Mission Telecom is a nonprofit telecommunications provider and grantmaker dedicated to expanding digital equity and driving systemic change. We are closing the digital divide with best-in-class, affordable wireless broadband services and equipping schools, libraries, and nonprofits with the connectivity and resources to power access and opportunity at every level.
