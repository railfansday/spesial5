Suns star Devin Booker’s message to ‘my favorite rookie’ Darryn Peterson on signed jersey

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

On Saturday night, in NBA Cup action, the Phoenix Suns faced the Utah Jazz for the first time this season. With the Jazz being heavily shorthanded amid injuries to Lauri Markkanen and Jaren Jackson Jr., the Suns handily took care of business in a 111-101 victory.

With the Jazz's veteran All-Stars out and Keyonte George struggling to the tune of a 4-of-17 shooting night, they turned to rookie guard Darryn Peterson to lead their offense. Peterson did not have the best of shooting nights, as he made just seven of his 20 field-goal attempts, but he did lead the Jazz in scoring with 20 points.

It certainly looks like Peterson has a huge fan in Suns star Devin Booker, who didn't just give the Jazz guard a signed jersey of his after their contest, but also inscribed on it a message that the 19-year-old will surely hold on to for his entire career.

"To: DP, stay on the gas! My fav rook!" Booker wrote on the signed jersey he gave to Peterson, via Fullcourtpress on X.

Perhaps, when Booker looks at Peterson's game, he is reminded of a younger version of himself. The Jazz guard, much like Booker, can light up the scoreboard and generate buckets in bunches. And like the Suns back in Booker's first few years in the league, the Jazz have struggled to put together wins as well.

Peterson has had moments as well where he's been lost in the shuffle as the Jazz integrate new pieces and figure out their identity as a team. Nonetheless, the Suns star knows just how talented Peterson is, and it should only be a matter of time before he figures it out.

As for the Suns, this win over the Jazz kept them in contention of a place in the NBA Cup knockout stage. Booker, on the season, is averaging 24.7 points and 5.3 assists per contest.
