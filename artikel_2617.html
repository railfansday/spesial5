Sophie Cunningham isn't crazy about the Caitlin Clark 'enforcer' label that fans love

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It didn't take long after the Indiana Fever acquired Sophie Cunningham via trade from the Phoenix Mercury for fans to jump on board with her approach to looking out for fellow teammates. This was especially notable when Cunningham spoke with players who had exchanged words (or more) with Fever star Caitlin Clark. In turn, Cunningham was quickly dubbed the Fever and Clark's "enforcer" on the floor.

The fans haven't let that nickname disappear, largely because Cunningham has shown she's always ready and willing to step into the chaos to help her teammates. And while that is unlikely to ever change, as it sounds like that's simply how she's built, Cunningham admitted in a recent interview with Maxim that she won't be embracing the "enforcer" reputation.

The good news? Cunningham confirmed that even though she doesn't see herself that way, the "passionate and protective" side will still shine through.

"Nope. I don't really know what I'm enforcing," Cunningham said of the reputation. "I get it, and maybe I feel that a little bit because of how I was raised; we were always taught to protect the ones you love. You go to war for the people to the left and right of you. So I do have a sense of protecting myself and my teammates. But to call me the enforcer, I know people love it, but I just don't view myself as that. It's become a fun storyline on the internet."

"All my family and friends, they get why people are saying that because they know how passionate and protective I am about my people. But they also know I'm the biggest goofball, just trying to laugh, smile, and truly enjoy the game of basketball."

Cunningham explained that off the court (or in general), she's a "very chill, down-to-earth person," which may not seem to match the "enforcer" mentality. But you could still argue that her protective side comes out on the court, and that's essentially what she pointed to.

"Of course, when I'm on the court, I'm gonna do whatever it takes for my team to win," Cunningham added. "I'm the type of person who can flip the switch and also flip it off. I think a lot of people aren't used to people who can do that. A lot of people who don't know me think that I'm like this 24/7, but really, I'm just a very goofy, down-to-earth, go-with-the-flow, chill type of human."
