Vikings-49ers in Mexico City averaged 23.8 million on Sunday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Sunday night's game between the Vikings and 49ers had the potential to be a great one. It wasn't, due in large part to the failure of the Vikings to show up for the game.

The 35-10 game nevertheless generated 23.8 million viewers on average for NBC and Peacock.

It ended up being the most-watched Week 11 Sunday night game since 2013. And, if the game had been more competitive, the final number would have been higher.

Indeed, the game averaged 27.6 million during the 9:00 p.m. ET to 9:15 p.m. ET window, before the game was essentially over.

This week, the Browns host the Raiders on Sunday night, thanks to a flex by the NFL that removed Patriots-Chargers from the prime-time window.
