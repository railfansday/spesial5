Dolphins sign P Jack Browning to their 53-man roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Dolphins added a second punter to their 53-man roster on Wednesday.

They announced that they have signed Jack Browning off of their practice squad. The Dolphins had an open spot, so there was no corresponding move needed to sign Browning.

Browning was elevated for last Sunday’s loss to the Bills because Bradley Pinion was out with a groin injury. Wednesday’s move suggests he’ll be handling the punting and holding duties against the Jets this weekend as well.

Browning punted four times for 165 yards against Buffalo. One of those punts landed inside the 20-yard-line.

The Dolphins also announced that they have signed defensive end Max Llewellyn to the practice squad. Llewellyn was waived off the 53-man roster earlier this week.
