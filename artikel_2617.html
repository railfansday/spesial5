Report: Ravens to sign K Riley Patterson

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Ravens have reportedly settled on their next kicker.

Mike Garafolo of NFL Media reports that the Dolphins will sign Riley Patterson. Patterson worked out for the team on Wednesday.

The Ravens found themselves in need of a new kicker because Jake Moody opted to sign with the Falcons after being elevated from Baltimore's practice squad in Week 9. Moody became the choice because Tyler Loop is on the team's injured reserve list.

Patterson opened the season as the Dolphins' kicker, but was released in October after going 7-of-9 on field goals and 5-of-6 on extra points. He is 97-of-112 on field goals and 148-of-154 on extra points over his entire career.
