Eagles make major QB decision ahead of Week 11 vs. Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Philadelphia Eagles just announced a major quarterback decision ahead of their Week 11 matchup against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

According to Eagles head coach Nick Sirianni, quarterback Joe Milton III, who made his first career start in Week 9 of the 2026 season, was once again named the starter for Sunday's matchup against the Steelers.

In Hurts' absence, the Eagles initially leaned on backup quarterback Tanner McKee but decided to make the change to Milton in Week 9.
