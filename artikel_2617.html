RB Jayden Limar to Face Former School During Rivalry Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

During Jayden Limar's three seasons at Oregon, he never took a single carry against Washington.

This Saturday, when the Huskies travel down Interstate 5 to wrap up the 2026 regular season at Autzen Stadium, the fourth-year running back is expected to be a key factor in Jedd Fisch's game plan against the Ducks as he aims to notch his first victory against Dan Lanning having lost his first four times, three at UW and once when he was at Arizona, against the fifth-year coach.

"He's a talented player," Lanning said of the now-UW runner. "He plays with great violence and emotion. He runs hard. He's smart. He obviously made a lot of big plays while he was here in his time here. So, excited to see what he's able to do. You're kind of sick to your stomach when you see a guy like that get hurt early in the season. Glad he's back and able to contribute."

The 5-foot-10, 210-pound Lake Stevens, Washington native has taken a career-high 50 carries this season, over 30 percent of which came last Saturday in the Huskies' 27-24 overtime win against then-No. 8-ranked Indiana, with the fourth-year running back only 32 yards shy of tying his single-season career-best in rushing yardage set back in 2025 with 230 to his name in just five games.

Since Limar returned from an elbow injury in late October, the veteran has eclipsed that total twice, first in a narrow 28-25 loss at home to Penn State and most recently against the Hoosiers, but it's not just about accruing yards that will be the difference Saturday against the Ducks.

For just the second time in his career, Limar has found the end zone in consecutive games at Michigan State and at Husky Stadium against IU, both crucial touchdowns that without either score the Huskies could've had two more losses on their record instead of a pair of victories.

If the former four-star recruit in the 2023 class is able to extend that streak to three games this Saturday and tie his longest consecutive streak with at least one touchdown, it could be the difference in another upset victory for Fisch as UW enters the conference finale as double-digit underdogs against Lanning and the Ducks.

The last time Limar recorded a rushing touchdown in three straight games was last season in Oregon's first three games against Montana State, Oklahoma State, and at Northwestern. While important in their own right as it marked three of the Ducks' 13 en route to a College Football Playoff semifinal appearance against Indiana, to do it in a rivalry game that has so much at stake for both programs would mean significantly more.

The Huskies are in search of their first win at Autzen since 2022. In order for the program to beat Oregon that night in Eugene, then-coach Kalen DeBoer relied on a pair of TD runs by Virginia transfer Wayne Taulapapa and Cameron Davis to keep the team in contention early, before Michael Penix Jr. delivered two touchdown passes in the second half and opened the door for Peyton Henry to nail what proved to be a 43-yard game-winning field goal with under a minute remaining to secure a 37-34 win.

Despite his previous three years as a Duck, Limar would write himself into Husky history if he can help be a reason why UW added its fourth straight road victory in 2026 this Saturday when the rivalry game commences in Eugene.
