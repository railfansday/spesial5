Mike McCarthy confirms he said "1-2-3 Packers" at halftime last Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Aaron Rodgers said a number of things that caught the attention of the football world on Wednesday and one of the comments that drew a big response had to do with something that happened in the locker room at halftime of last Sunday’s loss to the Eagles.

After saying head coach Mike McCarthy fell asleep in meetings and missed other ones altogether in his first comments since being benched on Tuesday night, Rodgers said that McCarthy broke down the team in the locker room with “1-2-3 Packers” in reference to the team he coached through the 2018 season. In a press conference later in the day, McCarthy confirmed that happened but said he took issue with how Rodgers characterized other things that included his commitment to the job.

“The Philadelphia situation at halftime was very emotional. . . . Embarrassing moment, but I just think we work a lot of hours so definitely a difference of opinion from what I was told about the comments,” McCarthy said.

The Steelers have released Rodgers and McCarthy stressed the need for the team to focus on Friday’s game against the Broncos, although it feels like that’s going to be difficult based on how the last 18 hours have played out in Pittsburgh.
