FPL Captain Picks This Week (Gameweek 12)

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Having the right FPL Captain can make all the difference in a tight race. The additional points on offer make the decision one of the most important that fantasy managers will make in any given gameweek. Looking at the fixtures for Gameweek 12, these are some picks who could come through and deliver for your team.

After scoring last weekend and being rested against Borussia Dortmund, Eberechi Eze is one to watch as the Gunners take on Manchester City. Arsenal beat the Cityzens by a number of goals in the Community Shield and Enzo Maresca's side have only seen their defensive woes get worse since then. Back post defending is notably one of the manager's big problems.

This opens the door for Eze to score some goals, either by exploiting the line, moving to the back post, or getting more central and playing off the striker.

Jaidon Anthony is one of Brentford's assist leaders so far in this campaign and he has a prime opportunity to add to that tally against Manchester United. Having given up six goals in the first two matches of Mauricio Pochettino's reign, the Red Devils are an easy target for any attacker. With no clear goalscorer in the lineup for the Bees, the better option to utilise is a creator like Anthony who could definitely exploit an unconfident back line.

A rather predictable option but Erling Haaland has a great goalscoring record against Arsenal. He averages one goal contribution per appearance in the last eight outings in the league against the Gunners. He's a solid option to captain as he could easily grab a goal or assist and get twice the fantasy points scoring.
