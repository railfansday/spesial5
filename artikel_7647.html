Parker Man Sentenced For COVID-19 Related Fraud

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

DENVER – The United States Attorney’s Office for the District of Colorado announces that Tarek Mohamad Kassem, 66, Parker, Colorado, was sentenced to 18 months in federal prison, three years of supervised release, restitution totaling $2,041,649.51, and a forfeiture money judgement of $1,776,792.00 after pleading guilty to one count of wire fraud and one count of money laundering.
