Here’s Everything We Know About ‘Campus Drivers’ Season 2 (and Which Couple It’ll Be About)

Hello to everyone who watched all of Campus Drivers in one day and now urgently need to know what’s going on with season 2: We have answers about what comes next for Lois and Lane and their delightfully trope-y love story.

The first season of Campus Drivers saw Lois break up with her longtime boyfriend and have no choice but to move in with “campus bad boy” Lane in his ultra-fancy apartment. At first Lois can’t stand him, then they become fast friends, then there’s the whole “let’s make out in front of your ex to make him jealous” phase, and finally the season ends with Lane and Lois fully getting together...only for Lane’s mom to enroll him in a new school. Lois literally comes home to a bunch of movers and literally no information from her boyfriend, so yeah, obviously season 2 can’t come fast enough! Here’s what we know.

Not yet. Prime is likely in the “wait and see how it performs” stage of things, but the show is in their top 10, so a renewal seems pretty inevitable at this point.

Lane and Lois obviously have a lot of issues to resolve, but reminder: Campus Drivers is based on a book series. And if season 2 is based on book 2 (aka Crash Course), then a brand-new couple will take center stage: “college bad boy trying to change gears” Donovan and “romance-loving good girl” Carrie. Their general vibe? Let’s dust off the book description.“When Donovan, basketball star and notorious heartthrob of the Campus Drivers, realizes the damage his womanizing behavior has caused, he decides to become the perfect boyfriend. And who better to coach him than romance-obsessed, all-around good girl Carrie? Carrie wants nothing to do with Donovan. She’d much rather lose herself in the heroes from her romance novels. They’re uncomplicated and drama-free, just the way she likes it. If Donovan wants her help, he’ll have to endure a tough, no-nonsense crash course in romance. But one can’t just become a romance hero overnight—especially when reality refuses to stick to the script.”

Time to do some math. According to IMDb, Campus Drivers’ first season was filmed from October 2025 to January 2026. It then dropped in November. This is a pretty fast turnaround, so assuming the show is renewed and filming takes place in early 2027, we could be looking at a late 2027 or early 2028 release date. So, about a year from now (or a little longer).
