While we love a showstopping main, appetizers are almost always the course that excites us the most. Especially if you’re hosting, it’s a nonnegotiable that appetizers impress. Perfect apps have to be easy, fast, and above all, delicious. Puff pastry dough makes hitting those benchmarks a cinch. Though making it from scratch is a lengthy process that includes folding tons of butter into dough (kind of like making croissants), store-bought frozen puff pastry is just as good (maybe better, considering the convenience!). Check out our 26 best puff pastry appetizers to see how versatile the dough can be, and we bet you’ll stock up, too.
