Florence Pugh Wants to Play the Villain in Robert Pattinson’s James Bond Movie

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Robert Pattinson is the next James Bond, and Florence Pugh is throwing her hat in the ring for the opportunity to star alongside him—not as a Bond girl, but as the villain.

The East of Eden actress attended ELLE’s 2026 Women in Hollywood event on November 16 and was introduced by Dune: Part Three director Denis Villeneuve, who is also helming the next Bond film starring Pattinson. During her acceptance speech, Pugh took a brief moment to shoot her shot at starring in the next 007 movie.

“I sent Denis a message this morning. I said, ‘Congrats on finding your Bond. Nice catch. Any villains with the size of 5’4”?’” she told the audience, who laughed at the joke. “Anyway, I’m reminding you just in case!”

The joke was a timely one—just earlier that morning, Pattinson was announced as the lead of the next Bond film, after several outlets reported that actors like Josh O’Connor, Nicolas Hoult, Callum Turner, and Jack Lowden auditioned for the role. Now, the speculation has turned to who will star opposite him as the female lead, a.k.a. the Bond girl. Pugh’s name has been on some prediction lists, but she seems to have her sights set on a different character.

Elsewhere during Pugh’s Women in Hollywood speech, she thanked all the people who assisted her journey as an actress, telling those in attendance, “I think of the crews I’ve been lucky enough to work with, who create the space and the stage so I can do what I do comfortably. I think of the casting directors at the beginning of my career, who took a risk arguing for me, claiming that it didn’t matter that I had no qualifications and only one film credit. Or for the dressers, who would fib and say there was a costume malfunction when they could see I hadn’t had enough time to eat my lunch—this was pre-SAG membership. So don’t panic, SAG. Or to the glam teams all over the world who join because they believe in you and what you do. The makeup artists and hair stylists who wash and clean brushes well into the night just for you, tomorrow at 5 A.M.”

“I think of all of these people who protected me on my journey,” Pugh added. “And it would not have been possible without every single one of them. Just in case you’re listening now and you’re not aware how grateful we are and how important you are, I just received an ELLE Women of the Year honor, and I didn’t get here on my own. I only took stock in myself because I could see you doing it for me first.”
