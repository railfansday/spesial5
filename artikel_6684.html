7 Sneaker Outfits That Put a Fresh Spin on Your Favorite Kicks

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

I’m what you might call a sneaker hoarder. But after building a capsule wardrobe I currently swear by, I’ve found that three pairs have become the foundation of most of my fall outfits: my black Adidas Superstars, which tap into the retro sneaker revival and rising noir sneaker trend I’m loving lately; my earth-toned Salomon XT-Whispers, perfect for an easy gorpcore moment; and my lilac Adidas x Wales Bonner Japan shoes, which add a subtle splash of color to neutral looks while complementing the rich hues in my autumn lineup. With those themes in mind, and inspired by some of this year’s most viral celebrity getups and standout street-style looks, I’ve gathered a quick guide to seven exciting sneaker outfits below.

Maybe you’re eager to invest in a new staple pair, or maybe you already have one of these favorites tucked away at the back of your closet. Either way, there’s plenty of fresh inspiration to copy and paste this season. Ahead, discover key pairs to shop (if you don’t already own them) and the chic pieces to wear with them.

Y2K icons like Vans and Converse are quietly making a comeback, stylishly co-signed by stars like Bella Hadid and Margot Robbie. Here, Robbie nails the cold-weather off-duty formula, swapping classic booties for black All Star high-tops to give her cozy gray polo, leather trench, and straight-leg jeans a more casual edge. A zesty red beanie completes the look, breaking up the muted tones.

By Anthropologie 100 Percent Brushed Cashmere Beanie

Even a few years after Adidas Sambas surged in popularity, the legendary indoor soccer-inspired shoe has cemented its status as a bona fide modern classic. Zoë Kravitz was recently spotted wearing a black-and-white pair while filming her new rom-com, Die Alive, in New York, styling them with flowy black trousers and a burgundy sweater and scarf. It’s a polished yet easygoing ensemble perfect for everything from bakery runs to overdue weekend catch-ups.

Saks Basic Cashmere Easy Crewneck Sweater

Hollister High-Rise Pleated Wide-Leg Pants

Or, as aforementioned, try switching up a neutral outfit with a colorful pair of kicks. As Jennifer Lawrence shows, an emerald- or forest-green sneaker feels just right for fall and winter, easily styled back to baggy jeans, a chunky turtleneck, and a navy coat.

The coolest outfits often hinge on contrast, mixing different textures, proportions, and aesthetics. This Paris Fashion Week showgoer went polished on top with a striped button-down and plaid blazer (also creating a nice pattern play) and sporty on bottom with classic track pants and chunky black Asics sneakers. An oversized denim tote introduces yet another interesting element.

Favorite Daughter The Dean Plaid Notch Lapel Blazer

Speaking of contrast, I love the idea of using a chunky sneaker to give tailored separates a cool, nonchalant feel. A relaxed-fit tuxedo blazer and drapey wide-leg trousers are a match made in street-style heaven when teamed with a boxy white tee and metallic trainers, like the celebrity-favorite New Balance 530s.

Banana Republic Factory Double Breasted Tuxedo Blazer

Lauren Ralph Lauren Pleated Stretch Jersey Wide-Leg Pant

PerfectWhiteTee Harley Cotton Boxy Crew Tee

Retro runner silhouettes, with their slim, streamlined uppers and slightly thicker soles, are another IYKYK trend to keep on your radar. Taking a cue from this Paris Fashion Week attendee, don’t be afraid to work traditionally spring-ready colors into your look: a baby-blue cable-knit sweater and soft yellow track jacket look lovely—and still feel relevant—alongside a chocolate-brown maxi skirt, matching sneakers, and a rich burgundy handbag.

Vineyard Vines Cashmere Cable Crewneck Sweater

Just like a go-to pair of low-profile kicks, a good ol’ graphic tee can be styled with just about anything in your closet—and it’s the kind of playful, sentimental piece that’ll spark joy every time you reach for it. For a unique combo, pair your favorite artist tee with sporty pieces like a nylon skirt and track jacket. An oversized briefcase-style bag adds a sleek touch.
