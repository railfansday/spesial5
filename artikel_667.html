E.D.I. Mean Says 2Pac Was Planning to Squash Beef With Biggie: ‘I’m Deading Everything’

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

He also revealed that 'Pac's conversations with his mother Afeni Shakur had started to change his perspective.

Apparently, 2Pac was over the back-and-forth with The Notorious B.I.G., especially the East Coast/West Coast narrative.

E.D.I. Mean, member of the Outlawz and the slain rapper’s friend and collaborator, recently sat down with with Drink Champs and revealed that 2Pac was contemplating ending his vitriolic beef with his former friend.

“When ‘Pac came back from New York, he said to us before we went to Vegas, he was like, ‘I’m squashing the sh–. I’m deading everything,'” E.D.I. Mean told N.O.R.E. after being asked if the two would’ve buried the hatchet eventually. “When he came back, we had a conversation about the trip and just about where he was and how he felt about it.”

He then said that Pac was planning on making an album that would’ve featured rappers from across the country in order to ease the tension between the two rivaling coasts. And so, prior to that, and before we started on Makaveli, we was working on One Nation,” he continued. “I was privy to a lot of conversations him and his mother had. We all was. He had a plan to do songs with everybody from all over the country because he didn’t like the fact that this had turned into an East Coast/West Coast thing.”

E.D.I. Mean also said that it was ‘Pac’s mom Afeni Shakur that helped him start to see things from a different perspective and used her experience dealing with the U.S. government as a Black Panther as a reference point. “His mother was telling him about how COINTELPRO was able to wedge a separation in the Panthers by selling letters from the East Coast to the West Coast and vice versa, which led to the killings of people like Bunchy Carter and on the UCLA campus,” he said. “And so she was telling ‘Pac, ‘This is what they did to us.'”

He added: “I noticed after that conversation, he started to make some changes as far as where he was with the whole sh–.”
