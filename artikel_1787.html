Graham Potter defends Dejan Kulusevski after Tottenham star faces criticism on Sweden duty

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Sweden boss Graham Potter was quick to defend Dejan Kulusevski after the forward’s form in this international break was criticised.

Tottenham forward Kulusevski only recently returned from an extended injury layoff. In May 2025, he suffered a complex patella injury and endured a lengthy recovery pockmarked with various setbacks and multiple surgical procedures.

The 26-year-old made a long-awaited comeback earlier this autumn, though, and has now made four cameo appearances for Tottenham.

Those showings were enough to earn him a recall to Potter’s Sweden squad for the November international break, where he has enjoyed substitute appearances totalling 56 minutes in a victory over Bosnia & Herzegovina and a draw with Poland, his first appearances for his nation since November 2024.

His performances this month, though, have been criticised by some in the Swedish press. The outlet TVmatchen dubbed his performance against Bosnia “miserable”, adding: “For obvious reasons not in absolute match form, but looked slow and was never in the game. Was he really ready for this already?”

The news site Svenskafans then described him as “eager” but “rusty”.

Potter, though, has leapt to Kulusevski’s defence, slamming what he considers unwarranted criticism.

“I think the criticism is completely unfair, to be honest,” he told Viaplay following Tuesday’s draw with Poland.

He continued, explaining why he felt Kulusevski was ready to return to the Blagult fold: “He has trained very, very well. You have to judge someone based on a match like that, like it was against Bosnia. It wasn't easy for the substitutes to come into that match.

“Now he just needs to get playing time, and that's completely normal. It's completely normal for anyone who's been out with an injury and is now getting back into match form.”

Kulusevski has yet to score or assist since returning to first-team action, but is only expected to increase his involvement for club and country as he builds back his fitness in the coming weeks.

Speaking last week, Potter acknowledged the need to be gentle with Kulusevski, and he worked with Tottenham to ensure he was not overexerted during the November break.
