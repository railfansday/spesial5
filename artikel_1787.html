Mauricio Pochettino has already delivered verdict on Premier League return amid Manchester United links

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Mauricio Pochettino has previously revealed the Premier League is the “ideal place” for him to work, amid links to becoming the next Manchester United manager.

The Argentine, who managed both Tottenham and Chelsea in the Premier League, has emerged as the new favourite to replace Michael Carrick - who is reportedly expected to be relieved of his duties as Man Utd head coach.

Following a 4-4 draw at home to Aston Villa, United are in the relegation zone and will remain there throughout the November international break.

According to reports, Man United are anticipating a managerial change before facing bitter rivals Liverpool at Anfield a week on Sunday and Pochettino is the new leading contender, ahead of Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann.

It is not the first time Pochettino has been linked with the Old Trafford post as it was almost a regular occurrence during his time at Tottenham.

But would he be open to a move now, should the vacancy become available in the near future? Judging by comments made in an interview with L’Equipe earlier in the year, Pochettino would be keen to manage in the Premier League again.

“I miss the football scene in England. I love the country, its culture, the football culture,” he said.

“For anyone with a competitive spirit who wants to measure themselves against others and test their limits, it's the ideal place. Here, you have to give your best constantly.”

While Pochettino misses English football, his commitments to the USMNT could prove a stumbling block if Man United do end up making an approach for the 54-year-old.

After guiding USA to a respectable round of 16 finish at the 2026 World Cup, Pochettino signed a new contract in August to stay on with the national team and his deal runs through until the 2030 World Cup.
