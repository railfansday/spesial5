Flick lauds ‘unbelievable’ Barcelona turnaround against Villarreal

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Hansi Flick hailed Barcelona's "unbelievable" comeback win over Villarreal but took issue with some of the referee's decisions.

Villarreal made a stunning start against Barcelona, scoring twice in the first seven minutes through Tajon Buchanan and Tani Oluwaseyi to shock Camp Nou.

Lamine Yamal then missed a first-half penalty to send Barcelona into the break 2-0 down, but they completed a remarkable 10-minute turnaround to claim all three points.

Gerard Martin and Fermin Lopez – who had made a mistake that led to Villarreal's opener – pulled Barcelona level before Pau Navarro's own goal finished off the comeback.

This was the first match in which Barcelona had conceded twice in the first seven minutes of a LaLiga match since November 1981 against Real Zaragoza, but Flick was impressed with the response his team mustered.

"When you see the first 10 minutes, we don't start so good," Flick said.

"We took too long with the ball, made two mistakes, and they scored two goals.

"The reaction was unbelievable. I think also in the first half it was top. In the first half, we created a lot of chances, the big opportunity with the penalty.

"In the second half, we looked at what we want to do better: better structure, a clear structure. If we play like this, the opponent gets tired. We scored, I think, three goals in 10 minutes; it was amazing."

Flick was angered with the officials in the match, though, starting with the fact that VAR needed to step in to award the penalty Yamal missed after he was fouled by Oluwaseyi.

Fermin was also sent off while on the bench after being substituted for a comment he made to the fourth official when Anthony Gordon had gone down in search of a penalty.

"If it was another result, maybe I wouldn't say anything," Flick added.

"But seeing this referee, we expected [decisions] like that. I am really happy with the referees, but with this referee I am not.

"Also, the linesman, the fourth official, they're always looking to us. They could handle it differently, speak with us, explain things …But they say nothing.

"I told the team that, if that happens, we just have to play better. And, in the second half, it was unbelievable.

"The hunger to come back and to score the goals, it was fantastic. We played against a Champions League-level team. It was an unbelievable game."
