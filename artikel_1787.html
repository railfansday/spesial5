How to watch Sunderland vs Chelsea: TV channel and live stream for Premier League today

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Chelsea make the trip to the Stadium of Light to take on Sunderland today.

The Blues are looking to keep their momentum going from the 2-0 win over Manchester United last time out, which followed two straight defeats by Tottenham and Liverpool in the Premier League and Carabao Cup respectively a fortnight ago.

Xabi Alonso’s men were back on song last weekend, with Morgan Rogers and Cole Palmer both on target against the out-of-sorts and ten-man Red Devils, who had Lisandro Martinez sent off.

The west Londoners, sat fourth in the table, now visit Wearside to face Regis Le Bris’ struggling Black Cats.

The Frenchman has been given a vote of confidence from the club hierarchy in recent days despite his side lying at the foot of the top-flight rankings.

They have won just once in their nine games so far this campaign, and they come off the back of a shock 2-0 loss at the hands of Dinamo Zagreb in the Europa League.

TV channel: In the UK, you can watch this match live on Sky Sports Main Event and Sky Sports Premier League. Coverage starts at 12.30pm GMT ahead of the 2pm GMT kick-off.
