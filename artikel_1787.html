Paul Scholes blasts Man Utd star for ‘cheating’ his teammates in Liverpool thrashing

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Paul Scholes has accused one of Manchester United’s most senior players of ‘cheating’ his teammates during Sunday’s demoralising defeat at Anfield.

Mauricio Pochettino’s tenure got off to the worst possible start with Michael Carrick’s successor handed an instant reality check as his side crashed to a 4-0 defeat.

While United’s cause was hardly helped by the early sending off of Leny Yoro, Pochettino would have hoped the players he has inherited would’ve been capable of offering sterner ristence.

Instead, the Red Devils crumbled and capitulated in a fashion that has become all too familiar under a succession of different managers.

Defensively, United were an utter shambles and the four goals they shipped means they now possess the leakiest rearguard in any of Europe’s top five leagues this season.

Scholes reserved particular criticism for Lisandro Martinez who has hardly been backwards in coming forwards when it comes to being critical of members of United’s boardroom for failing to bolster the squad with the necessary talent.

The Argentina international was badly at fault for Rio Ngumoha’s second half goal which essentially killed off any remote hope of the visitors launchig a comeback when he put in a half-hearted attempt to halt the run of Liverpool flying machine Bradley Barcola near the centre circle.

‘The big issue we have is the physicality and the athleticism of this group of players compared to everybody else,’ Scholes told The Good, The Bad & The Football.

‘I said the other week when I watched Brighton play, now they are a top end team right now. They should’ve won again yesterday with the chances they had but I’ve never seen a team that fit.
