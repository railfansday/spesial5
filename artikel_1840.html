Alonso admits Chelsea are ‘in a fragile moment’ after defeat to Leeds

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Xabi Alonso admitted Chelsea are "in a fragile moment" after their Premier League defeat to Leeds United on Saturday.

The Blues took the lead through Emmanuel Emegha in the 25th minute, but ended up losing 4-1 at Stamford Bridge after goals from Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Brenden Aaronson, along with a brace from Noah Okafor.

Chelsea lost by 3+ goals while conceding 4+ goals at home in the Premier League for just the fourth time, last doing so against Brentford in April 2022 (also 4-1).

It also marked Alonso's joint-biggest losing margin at home to any side as a manager (also 3-0 v Porto with Bayer Leverkusen in the 2022-23 Champions League), while it was the Blues' 100th home defeat in the Premier League (658th home match).

Chelsea have now lost three of their last four league matches, and Alonso acknowledged things are looking bleak at Stamford Bridge after his side were booed off at full-time.

"We are disappointed and sad. It was tough to deal with the defeat, and we're in a fragile moment," Alonso told BBC Match of the Day.

"We have lost a bit of the confidence which we were playing with. It's a delicate moment, but we have to go through it together and find solutions.

"The first half an hour was good, but we conceded the equaliser and weren't too comfortable and lost some momentum.

"We could have done more in the second half, but we didn't regain the confidence to have a real go and couldn't push hard enough to score one goal, which would have changed the momentum.

"Everything is related. It's the confidence, this fragility at the moment, the demand of the game, the physicality – everything is related."

Chelsea also saw key midfielder Moises Caicedo taken off in the 22nd minute due to injury, and Alonso felt the substitution made a negative impact.

"It's a knock, and we need to wait until tomorrow to see how he is," said Alonso, when asked if Caicedo's injury was serious after the game.

"He was in pain and let's see how bad it is. What went wrong was after their equaliser, after the change with Moi, we completely lost momentum.

"Until then, we were doing okay. I think that we were creating, playing a lot on the pitch.

"We were coming out of the pressure, but from that moment, they started believing, we started not believing."
