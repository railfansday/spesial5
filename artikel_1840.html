Brighton fans face double travel headache after Amex journey chaos and Sunday rail closures

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

And things are unlikely to be much easier for Seagulls fans heading to the Amex on Sunday (November 29), when major engineering work will close a section of the Brighton Main Line, affecting train services across Sussex.

Thursday's fixture kicked off at 5.45pm, but some supporters were reporting difficulties getting to the stadium as road and rail problems affected journeys across the area.

One supporter, posting on X under the name North Stand Seagull, warned fans to avoid Lewes railway station.

Writing at 5.07pm, they said: "Don't bother coming to Lewes station it’s carnage massive queue lucky to make ko at this rate."

Another supporter, MatsMotion, also reported problems travelling from Lewes to the stadium via the A27.

They wrote: "Can’t get to the Amex via A27 from Lewes either, road closed due to car fire."

The A27 was closed westbound between the A26 Southerham Roundabout and the A277 Brighton Road (Ashcombe Roundabout) following a car fire, with traffic queueing in the area.

There were also complaints about road conditions for supporters travelling from the A23.

Meanwhile, Southern had warned passengers to expect busy trains and stations across the Brighton area ahead of the match.

In a post on X at 10am, the rail operator said a queueing system would be in place at Brighton station to safely manage crowds.

It warned that entering and leaving stations could take longer than usual and urged supporters to allow extra time for their journeys.

Southern also advised passengers to plan ahead and check National Rail for the latest travel information.

Brighton Main Line closure will affect Newcastle match travel

Albion fans heading to Sunday's home game against Newcastle United face another challenge, with major engineering work closing the Brighton Main Line on November 29.

Network Rail and train operators are urging passengers to plan ahead and check before travelling, with services affected between Three Bridges and Brighton, Hove and Lewes.

No Southern train services will run between Three Bridges and Brighton, Hove or Lewes on Sunday.

Direct Southern trains will operate between London Victoria and Brighton, diverted via Horsham, Littlehampton and Hove, meaning extended journey times.

Replacement buses will operate on several routes, including: Three Bridges to Preston Park; Three Bridges to Lewes; Balcombe to Brighton; Haywards Heath to Plumpton; Plumpton to Lewes.

Thameslink services will also be affected, with replacement buses running between Three Bridges and Brighton throughout the day. A reduced Thameslink service will operate between Gatwick Airport and Three Bridges.

Southern tickets will also be accepted on Southeastern trains between Hastings and London at no extra cost.

With Albion welcoming Newcastle to the Amex on Sunday, supporters travelling by rail are being urged to check their journeys carefully and allow plenty of extra time.

The engineering work means another potentially difficult journey for fans following Thursday's travel problems, with both road and rail disruption having already caused headaches for supporters getting to the stadium.
