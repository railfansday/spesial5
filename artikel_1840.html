Ukrainian FA demands apology after Mykhailo Mudryk points finger for doping ban

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Andriy Shevchenko, the president of the Ukrainian FA (UAF), has demanded Mykhailo Mudryk apologise for “baseless slander” after he blamed the national team for his failed drugs test.

Chelsea winger Mudryk, presently on loan at Tottenham, was suspended from football between November 2024 and July 2026 after testing positive for the banned substance meldonium in a routine doping examination.

He was slapped with a four-year ban by the FA, which was lifted in August after a successful appea’.

Speaking this week in an interview with the ‘Guest of Gordon’ YouTube channel, Mudryk revealed the belief that meldonium entered his system while representing the Ukraine national team.

“I had never heard of a drug like meldonium,” he said, asked who he blamed for his suspension. “It could only have got into my body in Ukraine. There is no such drug in England... it points to the fact it was in a national camp.”

He added that he felt he did not receive sufficient support from the UAF or Shevchenko himself while suspended.

But Shevchenko has now hit back at Mudryk’s claims, demanding the player retract and apologise for his accusations.

Shevchenko said in a statement to Tribuna that Mudryk was never given any medication by the national team, as per a request from Chelsea.

“This is a baseless slander against our people, who do their job honestly and professionally,” said Shevchenko.

“I know people who have been working in the national team for many years. I trust them, because during all this time there have been no incidents in the team that could make one doubt their professionalism.
