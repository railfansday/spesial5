Travel YouTuber dies after being struck by truck in hit-and-run during overseas trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

A South Korean travel YouTuber has died at the age of 33 after being struck by a truck while cycling through Bolivia, with the driver reportedly fleeing the scene.

According to Bolivian news outlet UNITEL, the accident occurred at approximately 9:30 AM on November 4 in the city of El Alto.

Witnesses rushed to help the YouTuber after he was struck by a large cargo truck, but his injuries were too severe, and he died at the scene.

The truck driver fled following the collision but was apprehended by police several hours later.

According to SBS News, the driver told investigators he had not seen the bicycle. Police also confirmed that a breathalyzer test detected no alcohol in his system.

Authorities are continuing to investigate the circumstances surrounding the fatal collision.

JinsTravel launched his YouTube channel in 2020, sharing videos of his cycling adventures across Europe, Africa, North America, and beyond.

He had recently been traveling through South America after beginning an ambitious journey from Mexico, with his final video uploaded on October 30 documenting his travels through Peru’s Andes Mountains.

The acquaintance explained that Cho had spent years preparing for his dream journey, filming his travels and sharing them with viewers around the world.

Funeral arrangements are being prepared in Bolivia with assistance from the South Korean Embassy. The family has also requested that people refrain from sharing unverified speculation or footage of the accident.
