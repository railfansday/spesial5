Nathan Fillion’s The Rookie Spinoff Officially Hits Major Filming Milestone Ahead Of January Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The star of The Rookie: North just shared a major production milestone ahead of the police procedural's January series premiere on ABC.

The Rookie: North will be the third show in the franchise, which began in 2018 with Nathan Fillion playing rookie John Nolan at the Los Angeles Police Department. The original series has aired eight seasons and continues delivering strong ratings for ABC and Hulu. In fact, The Rookie season 8 finale, which aired in May, set a new streaming record.

In early 2027, ABC will use The Rookie to launch a brand-new spinoff, The Rookie: North. Lead star Jay Ellis shared a video on TikTok to celebrate the end of filming for season 1.

In his caption, the actor expressed gratitude and pride for all that he, his co-stars and the crew accomplished over the past few months. "I have so much more to say," he teased, "but for now I'll let this video speak for itself."

The video features a montage of behind-the-scenes clips of Ellis and the rest of the cast on the set of The Rookie: North, including taking part in a table read, walking to set, goofing around, dancing and posing for the camera.

It ends with Ellis delivering a speech to the cast and crew after filming the final scene. He admits that he never imagined he could have an experience like the one he's had on The Rookie: North.

Ellis also gives a shout-out to creator and showrunner Alexi Hawley. "Thank you so much for trusting me," he says. "Thank you for your spirit, your collaboration, for leading this team." The actor ends his speech by saying he doesn't care that he's the first name listed on the call sheet because The Rookie: North is "really every single person's."

Jay Ellis: "I don't know that I ever could have imagined or even had the capacity to dream for an experience like this, to be honest with you. I'm so grateful for every single one of you. Alexi Hawley, thank you so much for trusting me. Thank you for your spirit, your collaboration, for leading this team. I might be number one on the call sheet, but I don't really give a f*** about that. This show is really every single person's. Thank you so much for bringing you to it every single day, guys."

Before being cast as Alex Holland on The Rookie: North, Ellis appeared in movies like Top Gun: Maverick and Escape Room, and the TV shows The Game, Insecure, Mrs. America, Running Point and All Her Fault.

Now he's back on the small screen in another series regular role on The Rookie: North, which is executive-produced by Hawley, Fillion, Bill Norcross, Michelle Chapman, Mark Gordon, Brett Mahoney and Mary Leah Sutton.

Ellis' character, Alex Holland, joins the Pierce County Police Department after experiencing a terrifying home invasion. His co-stars include Chris Sullivan, Karen Fukuhara, Froy Gutierrez, Janet Montgomery, Mya Lowe and Malik Watson.

When The Rookie: North premieres in January, it will have the original Rookie as a lead-in on Wednesday nights.

ABC first expanded the franchise in 2022 with the spinoff The Rookie: Feds, which starred Niecy Nash-Betts as a school counselor-turned-FBI agent. Despite strong reviews from critics, The Rookie: Feds only had one season due to underwhelming ratings and structural changes at ABC. Now the network is giving it another shot with The Rookie: North.

The Rookie: North premieres Wednesday, January 6, 2027, at 10 p.m. EST on ABC.
