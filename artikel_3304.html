BBC Dancing Competition Pranked With Sex Noises

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A recording of what definitely sounded like sex noises interrupted a recent episode of a British dancing competition called — and presented totally without comment — Strictly Come Dancing.

The BBC series was broadcasting live from the Tower Ballroom in Blackpool on Saturday, Nov. 21. While judge Motsi Mabuse was delivering feedback to two contestants, the faint strains of some vigorous and ecstatic moaning could be heard emanating from somewhere behind her. While there appeared to be some awkward looks and throat-clearing from the other judges, Mabuse handled the moment like a pro, quipping, “It’s not me!”

At the end of the episode, Strictly Come Dancing (again, presented without comment) host Emma Willis addressed the audio malfunction, saying, “Before we see the judges’ leaderboard, we’d like to apologize if you heard any audio disruptions earlier in tonight’s show and for any offense caused.”

The BBC also apologized to “any viewers offended during the live broadcast of Strictly,” and said an investigation was underway. Police in Lancashire have already received a formal complaint and seized a mobile phone, and are looking into whether there are criminal charges to pursue.

As for who might have been behind this coitus interruptus, early signs point to Daniel Jarvis, a content creator and known prankster with the extremely likely name of Jarvo69. As detailed in The Standard, Jarvis was livestreaming Saturday night and not-so-subtly suggested he thought one of the other Strictly Come Dancing judges, Craig Revel Horwood, was about to be the target of a “naughty noises” prank. When Horwood appeared on screen, Jarvis pointed and said, “This is for all the people you keep bullying. Don’t bully people.”

Jarvis then allegedly made a phone call, soon after which the sex noises were heard on the broadcast. While Jarvis appeared elated that the prank came off — “I can hear it!” he allegedly exclaimed — he did seem to acknowledge a delay had caused him to miss his intended target. “Sorry, I tried to get the other one!” he said. If it was Jarvis, this wouldn’t be the first time he’s pulled such a prank. Back in 2023, he claimed he was responsible for sneaking sex noises onto an episode of the BBC’s popular soccer highlights show, Match of the Day.

Jarvis did not immediately return a request for comment on whether he was behind the prank on Strictly Come Dancing. He has, however, posted a clip of the incident to social media with a statement of totally believable incredulity, “Who would have done this!”
