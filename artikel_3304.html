‘Percy Jackson And The Olympians’ Season 3 Release Schedule: When Do New Episodes Come Out?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A new tidal wave of Percy Jackson and the Olympians episodes will soon break on Disney+.

The third season of the television series adaptation based on Rick Riordan’s book series arrives the same day as The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping hits theaters. Deriving from the third book in the series, specifically, Season 3 charts new territory because Riordan’s The Titan’s Curse has never been adapted for the screen before.

The cast have teased a much darker season, and plenty of new faces will join as a new quest emerges for Percy (Walker Schobell) and Thalia (Tamara Smart). Read on for all the details about the Percy Jackson and the Olympians Season 3 release schedule:

When does Season 3 of Percy Jackson and the Olympians come out? Watch on Deadline

Percy Jackson and the Olympians Season 3 premieres Friday, November 20.

How many episodes are in Season 3 of Percy Jackson and the Olympians? Will they release weekly or all at once?

Percy Jackson and the Olympians Season 3 will unfold with two episodes arriving each week for a total of four weeks. The first two episodes arrive at premiere on Nov. 20, and the finale and penultimate episode will arrive on Friday, December 11.

Who is in the cast of Percy Jackson and the Olympians Season 3?

Walker Scobell portrays Percy, son of Poseidon. Tamara Smart plays Thalia Grace, daughter of Zeus. Aryan Simhadri portrays Grover, the satyr and protector of Percy, Thalia and Annabeth. Leah Sava Jeffries portrays Annabeth Chase, daughter of Athena. Charlie Busnhell plays Luke Castellan, son of Hermes and turncoat. Glynn Turman portrays Chiron, centaur and son of Kronos. Virginia Kull portrays Sally Jackson, Percy’s mother. Dior Goodjohn plays Clarisse La Rue. Jason Mantzoukas appears as Dionysus, the god of wine.

Daniel Diemer portrayed Percy’s half brother Tyson, a cyclops, in Season 2. Timothy Simons also appeared in Season 2 as Tantalus. Beatrice Kitsos portrayed Alison Simms. Adam Copeland appears as Ares.

Season 3 will also welcome Olive Abercrombie and Levi Chrisopulos as Bianca and Nico di Angelo, children of Hades. Dafne Keen will portray Artemis, goddess of the hunt, the moon and maidens. Saara Chadry, (The Mysterious Benedict Society, PAW Patrol: The Movie) will play Zoë Nightshade, Artemis’ lieutenant and an immortal hunter.

Kate McKinnon will appear as Aphrodite, goddess of love and beauty, who can change looks based on who she is encountering. Holt McCallany will portray the Titan Atlas, also known as The General of Kronos’ army. David Costabile will play Dr. Thorn, who readers meet at the very beginning of The Titan’s Curse. Jesse L. Martin will portray Annabeth’s mortal father, Frederick Chase.

Ming-Na Wen will play Hera, and Jennifer Beals will portray Demeter. Hubert Smielecki will play Artemis’ twin brother Apollo, god of the sun, archery and poetry as well as prophecy.

What is Percy Jackson and the Olympians Season 3 about?

The titular Titan’s curse refers to the burden Atlas usually bears by holding up the weight of the world or sky on his shoulders. Season 3 of the show will see Annabeth be kidnapped by Dr. Thorn, who is a manticore, as part of Atlas’ and Kronos’ plans.

Thus, Percy and Thalia must set aside their differences and join forces with the Hunters of Artemis to embark on a quest to save her and try once more to put a stop to Luke resurrecting the Titan Lord Kronos, who sired all of the gods and has tried to put an end to their reign before.
