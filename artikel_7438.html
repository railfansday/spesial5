Mark Vins’ ‘Reef to Ridge’ premiered at TIFF. Now pocket.watch is on board as its distributor.

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

After building brands alongside some of YouTube’s biggest kid-friendly stars, pocket.watch is increasing its presence in the world of film distribution. The creator media company has come on board as the exclusive worldwide distributor and sales agent for Reef to Ridge, a nature documentary directed by Brave Wilderness Co-Founder and CEO Mark Vins.

Reef to Ridge takes viewers to the Galapagos Islands, where Vins and his crew explore unique habitats, encounter endangered species, and discuss the challenges that hinder conservation efforts around the world. The project premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival (TIFF) where it was part of a showcase curated by YouTube. By highlighting projects from creators like Vins and Ziwe, YouTube showed that its biggest draws were ready for Hollywood close-ups.

By working with Vins to give Reef to Ridge a streaming premiere, pocket.watch is solving a problem that has stunted progress for some creators with Hollywood ambitions. Major studios are eager to turn YouTube stars into feature directors, but the distributors attached to those projects have often been film industry firms like A24 and Neon. Meanwhile, creators who eschew traditional distribution can encounter some headaches, as Markiplier discovered when he attempted to bring his video game adaptation Iron Lung to YouTube.

As a creator media company, pocket.watch can pursue global distribution for a project like Reef to Ridge while also understanding the needs of a director like Vins. “Creators have built extraordinary relationships with their audiences, and there is a real opportunity to bring that connection to feature films,” said pocket.watch Founder and CEO Chris M. Williams in a statement. “Mark Vins and his team have made a visually striking, cinematic film with the ambition and scale to reach well beyond their established fanbase. We’re bringing our distribution expertise and platform partnerships to that opportunity, with more creator-led film announcements to come.”

As Williams alluded to in his statement, Reef to Ridge is just the start for pocket.watch. The media company is going to help creators realize their feature film ambitions. According to a press release, more announcements of this nature will arrive “by year’s end.”
