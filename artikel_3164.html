Among iOS 27’s many new features is a great change for anyone with multiple iPhones: you can now keep the same phone number on two iPhones via iPhone Handoff.

With iOS 27, Apple introduces big AI upgrades like Siri AI, plus exciting new features for apps like Wallet, Messages, Notes, and also CarPlay and AirPods.

But the update also has a lot of smaller, more niche improvements.

One such addition is iPhone Handoff, which enables switching seamlessly between two iPhones using the same phone number.

After iPhone Handoff is set up, one iPhone becomes the main iPhone and the other iPhone becomes the secondary iPhone that shares the number. Your main iPhone should be the one you use most often. Some carrier services, like Call Forwarding and Caller ID, and carrier account management can only be managed on your main iPhone. Contact your carrier for more details.

To move between your main iPhone and secondary iPhone, simply unlock the device that you want to start using — your phone number will automatically become active on that device. The message “Switched to this iPhone” will display at the top of your screen to confirm it’s now active. Your phone number will only be active on one iPhone at a time when using iPhone Handoff.

Most users will never need this feature. But if you’re someone who, for whatever reason, has separate devices that you want on the same number, iOS 27 has the solution.

I feel like I’m exactly who this feature was made for. After years of running dual SIM, I now have a personal line on my Duo and work line on an 18 Pro. Going out with friends, family, etc, I’d love to bring one phone again while bringing both numbers with me, but keeping work system access to a specific, separate device. Only current problem is waiting for Verizon to activate the feature and then see if it actually works well.

If you’re trying to decide between an iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro, perhaps iPhone Handoff is Apple’s subtle way of saying: “Why choose?”

So far carrier support is limited (here’s the latest list), but Apple plans to expand iPhone Handoff to more carriers soon.
