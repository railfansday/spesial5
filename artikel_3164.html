Navitas Revenue Up 38%, Q4 Spending to Jump: Rapid Earnings Reaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Navitas is shifting toward power chips for AI data centers and has just bought another business to support that move. Its fourth-quarter outlook shows what that costs.

Navitas Semiconductor (NVTS +7.95%) grew third-quarter revenue 38% to $14.0 million, its first year-over-year gain since 2024. Shares rose about 5% in after-hours trading. The growth is real. Fourth-quarter guidance, though, has costs rising much faster than revenue, so losses should widen before they narrow.

Fourth-quarter guidance puts non-GAAP operating expenses about 50% above this quarter, and the release says that includes the newly acquired Claros business. An acquisition adds its costs on day one, while its revenue usually takes longer to arrive. Revenue is guided up much less, so the non-GAAP operating loss should grow at the midpoint.

The jump in GAAP gross margin is mostly an accounting effect. Some acquired technology finished amortizing, which cut a charge that sits in cost of revenue. On a non-GAAP basis, gross margin barely moved, at 40%.

Cash more than doubled since year-end, but the release has no cash flow statement, so it does not show where the money came from. Weighted shares outstanding are up 23% from a year ago.

Watch whether fourth-quarter revenue lands in the guided range while non-GAAP operating expenses stay near $25 million. The release also gives no current AI infrastructure revenue figure, so check whether that share is on track for the 40% target.
