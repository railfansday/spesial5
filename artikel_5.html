Huang's return fails to dispel doubts as Nvidia shares drop

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

A rally sparked by Jensen Huang's return as Nvidia's chief executive faded on Tuesday, with the company's shares falling as doubts persisted over the probes he faces and the fate of the AI investment boom. After vaulting more than 30 percent on Monday following Huang's shock comeback, Nvidia fell almost six percent Tuesday. Huang resigned on November 12 over extramarital affairs, including one with a suspected Chinese spy.
