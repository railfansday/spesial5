Is Spain's snap election being rigged? Debunking viral claims

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ahead of Spain's snap election on Sunday, allegations of vote rigging - known in Spanish as "pucherazo" - are spreading widely on social media. From claims that undocumented migrants are being illegally registered to vote, to allegations that postal ballots and vote counts could be manipulated, internet users are accusing Pedro Sánchez's government of attempting to rig the election. But these claims misrepresent how Spain's electoral system works, as Vedika Bahl explains in Truth or Fake.
