Jaguars RB Bhayshul Tuten likely to play vs. Ravens on TNF

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Jaguars RB Bhayshul Tuten likely to play vs. Ravens on TNF appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Jacksonville Jaguars running back Bhayshul Tuten appears likely to play Thursday night against the Baltimore Ravens despite ankle and foot injuries that kept him out of practice all week.

Tuten, the Jaguars' leading rusher, carried a questionable designation into Thursday after head coach Liam Coen labeled him a game-time decision. Jacksonville planned to evaluate how he responded during a pregame workout before making a final call on his availability.

ESPN's Jeremy Fowler provided the strongest indication yet on X, formerly Twitter, that Tuten is trending toward playing.

"Jaguars RB Bhayshul Tuten (questionable, ankle/foot) 'should be' good for TNF vs Ravens barring any pregame setbacks, per source."

Tuten injured his ankle and foot during Jacksonville's 42-7 Week 8 win over the Indianapolis Colts but still delivered one of his best performances of the season. He finished with 159 scrimmage yards and two touchdowns, including 104 rushing yards on 16 carries.

The performance made Tuten the first Jaguars player since Maurice Jones-Drew in 2007 to record at least 100 rushing yards, 50 receiving yards and multiple touchdowns in the same game.

Jacksonville kept Tuten out of practice throughout the short week, creating uncertainty around his status. If Tuten suffers a setback before kickoff or Jacksonville limits his workload, Chris Rodriguez Jr. could take on a larger backfield role.

The Jaguars enter Thursday night at 5-2 and atop the AFC South after consecutive wins. The Ravens enter Thursday at 4-4 and plan to start rookie quarterback Joe Fagnano.

Kickoff is scheduled for 8:15 p.m. ET/5:15 p.m. PT at M&T Bank Stadium.
