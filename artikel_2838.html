Rob Pelinka on Lakers: 'We’ll be active at the trade deadline'

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Los Angeles Lakers sit on top of the Western Conference at 14-3, with the third-best offense in the NBA — behind an MVP-level start to the season from Luka Doncic — and a solid defense. Walker Kessler has been exactly what the Lakers hoped for as a big in the paint, and their moves for role players that better fit around Doncic — Matisse Thybulle, Sandro Mamukelashvili, Quentin Grimes, Quentin Grimes — have largely worked out.

Lakers general manager Rob Pelinka has seen it, too, and thinks this team has a deep playoff run in them. Which is why he said in an interview on the Lakers pregame show that the team would be "active" at the trade deadline (hat tip Real GM).

"We'll be active at the trade deadline to shore up things that we see. We think this team has a real chance to make a run, so we want to give the group as much talent as we can. Don't want to get too far over our skis with a hot start, because you don't want to get big-headed, but clearly the way it's gone so far, it's been great, and we hope it continues."

Active is a broad term. The Lakers will be active talking to teams, whether they make a deal will depend on the players available, and the price.

The Lakers' most critical need is to improve at backup center behind Kessler. Kevon Looney has largely fallen out of the rotation, leaving the Lakers playing small ball with Sandro Mamukelashvili or Jarred Vanderbilt at the five, or leaning into two-way big Carson Cooper. There may be better options available.

The Lakers don't have draft picks of note to trade, but they have players, and while they are hard-capped at the first apron, they are a little more than $8 million below that line and have room to work.
