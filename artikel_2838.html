Titans vs. Cowboys odds: Opening lines for Week 11 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

For the 100th straight week*, the Tennessee Titans have opened as underdogs. This week, they are a 7.5-point underdog at the Dallas Cowboys. This could be a really fun game to watch. Neither team is good on defense. Both teams, dare I say it, can put up points on offense. I wouldn't touch the spread in this one, but the over 52.5 looks like a pretty good bet.

Speaking of bad defense, they said on Titans Talk Back on 104.5 The Zone that the Titans have given up more yards over the past three games than any three-game span in the history of the franchise. It would be great if there were any hope of improvement there, but there isn't. This unit can't stop the run and can't rush the passer.

Cam Ward's play over the last two weeks has been very encouraging. He gets another good matchup this week against a middle-of-the-road Dallas defense that gives up 225.2 passing yards per game. Hopefully, he can put together another solid week.
