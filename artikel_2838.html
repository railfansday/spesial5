Titans put Wan'Dale Robinson on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Titans have been happy with quarterback Cam Ward's recent play, but he'll have to keep it going without his most frequent target in the passing game.

Wide receiver Wan'Dale Robinson has been placed on injured reserve. Robinson injured his ankle during last Sunday's loss to the Jaguars.

Robinson had 38 catches for 272 yards and three touchdowns while being targeted 54 times this season. Rookie Carnell Tate has missed the team's last two games with a shoulder injury, but head coach Robert Saleh said on Monday that they hope the first-round pick can return in Week 11.

The Titans filled Robinson's roster spot by signing wide receiver Xavier Restrepo off of the practice squad. Restrepo was Ward's college teammate and he had three catches for 31 yards as a temporary elevation in Week 10.

They also announced that they have signed wide receiver Jamari Thrash to their practice squad.
