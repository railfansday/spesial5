The biggest weekend of the 2026 college football season is here.

The Week 10 Saturday slate is loaded with ranked-on-ranked matchups, including a top-10 battle in the Holy War and a critical showdown between Miami and Notre Dame. The SEC features several ranked games, with Texas trying to stay alive in the CFP race against Missouri, as Ole Miss will look to do at home against Georgia.

LSU and Alabama also play what could prove to be a de facto College Football Playoff elimination game.
