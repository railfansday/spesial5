Chargers vs. Ravens Opening Odds: Bolts face primetime showdown in Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Los Angeles Chargers are 0-8 after falling to the Houston Texans at home in Week 9. Now the Chargers will have a chance to face off against the Baltimore Ravens on the road in Week 10's edition of Monday Night Football.

The the Ravens are fresh off a loss against the Jacksonville Jaguars on Thursday Night Football, in which they started third-string rookie Joe Fagnano due to injuries plaguing Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley. The Ravens are expected to start Jacoby Brissett after acquiring him in a trade with the Arizona Cardinals for a fourth-round pick.

The Bolts entered this week as 3.5-point underdogs, per FanDuel Sportsbook. The over/under is currently set at 38.5 points.

The Chargers hit a new low in their latest contest against the Texans, who were 2-5 going into the game. The Chargers surrendered a pair of touchdowns right before the end of the first half, which gave the Texans a 15-point lead going into the locker room. It was part of a 28-point unanswered run by the Texans that stemmed all the way into the early part of the fourth quarter.

The Chargers did end their long streak of not having a touchdown late in the fourth quarter, with wide receiver Quentin Johnston breaking off a 58-yard score. Still, there was not a lot to like from their 44-22 loss at home.

The game against the Ravens will mark the team's first game facing off against former defensive coordinator Jesse Minter, who became the Ravens' head coach during the offseason.
