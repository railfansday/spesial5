Bears stun Lions on late Rome Odunze TD

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Bears suffered an agonizing defeat in Detroit on Thanksgiving a couple of years ago, but they found themselves on the other side of things this time around.

Caleb Williams hit wide receiver Rome Odunze in the back corner of the end zone for a six-yard touchdown with three seconds left on the clock. The extra point gave the Bears a 39-35 lead and the game came to an end after the Lions' attempt to lateral down the field failed to produce a miracle.

It was the first time the Bears led all day, but that's all it took for them to end the day in sole possession of first place in the NFC North. Odunze's score capped a wild second half that saw the two teams put up 55 combined points in one of the most entertaining games of the season.

The game-winning touchdown drive came after Lions head coach Dan Campbell passed on a 51-yard field goal with 2:22 to play in the game and went for it on fourth-and-4. A pass to running back Jahmyr Gibbs was complete, but he was yanked down by linebacker T.J. Edwards and stopped short of a first down.

Campbell's decisions on the previous possession will also merit a second look. The Lions chose to have Jared Goff roll left to throw on a third-and-1 while in field goal territory and Goff's pass behind Jameson Williams wound up being intercepted by Bears safety Dillon Thieneman. The Lions chose to run with Sione Vaki rather than Gibbs on the previous play, which was a curious choice on a day when Gibbs ran 21 times for 205 yards and three touchdowns.

That aggressiveness has been a hallmark of the Campbell era, but the calls backfired on a day when the Lions led by as many as 11 points. The defense coming up with big plays to thwart Campbell's gambles deserves credit for the ultimate result and so does Williams, who finished the day 30-of-44 for 345 yards and two scores despite losing tight end Colston Loveland to a knee injury early in the first quarter.

Williams proved his mettle in big spots several times last season and Thursday's win is the latest reminder why the Bears are building their future around him. They'll return home next week for a date with another division leader in the Jaguars and these two teams will do it again in Chicago in Week 17.

The Lions will try to rebound in Atlanta next Sunday and they can't afford too many more stumbles in a jam-packed NFC playoff race.
