NFL moves Jaguars-Giants game in Week 11 to a 4:25 p.m. ET kickoff

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The NFL announced a couple of changes to kickoff times for Week 11 of the 2026 season.

The Jaguars and Giants were originally scheduled to start their game at MetLife Stadium at 1 p.m. ET on November 22, but the game will now get underway at 4:25 p.m. ET. The Steelers’ visit to Philadelphia to face the Eagles was set for that time and it will now take over the 1 p.m. slot.

Both games will still be broadcast by CBS and the Jaguars-Giants contest is now set to be the network’s national game during that window. It’s a sensible change as both teams are leading their divisions while the Eagles, who have a bye in Week 10, will carry a seven-game losing streak into their matchup with the Steelers.

A Raiders-Broncos game in Denver will also start at 4:25 p.m. ET and the Jets-Chargers game in Los Angeles will be the lone 4:05 p.m. ET start that weekend.
