Milwaukee Bucks vs. Brooklyn Nets Preview & Game Thread: Start stackin’ wins!

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Fresh off their second win of the season and with the 13-game losing streak in the rearview mirror, the Milwaukee Bucks will try to make it two in a row tonight as they host the Brooklyn Nets at home in what will also be their third NBA Cup game. The Nets are slightly ahead of the Bucks in the standings with three wins themselves, though frustrations have been mounting for Julius Randle and company. Brooklyn is coming off a 142-116 blowout loss in Minnesota, where Mikel Brown Jr. got his first start of the season and put up 17 points and seven assists on 6/12 shooting.

While the Nets have one more win than the Bucks, things have arguably been bleaker for them over the last two weeks; during that stretch, they have the worst defense in the league, averaging 130.9 points per 100 possessions. They are currently on a five-game losing skid, and four of those losses have been by double digits, including a 30-point rout against Chicago; in that game, fans saw offseason addition Julius Randle tell his teammates they were competing with "no pride." Brooklyn then lost their next game at home to the Magic, 113-111, after a Franz Wagner go-ahead jumper—brutal. As if that wasn't bad enough, Nolan Traore, who had been settling into a nice rhythm, injured his hamstring during the first quarter of that game and will miss 8-10 weeks.

While things seem to be going against the Nets, the vibes around the Deer District are steadily improving: if not for a missed call in the Saturday matinee game, the Bucks may have pulled off the biggest upset of the year by beating Philadelphia on the road. While there is no such thing as a moral victory in sports, Milwaukee can at least look back at this 18-game stretch and take a lot away from it. For starters, they have had far and away the toughest schedule of any Eastern Conference team and the toughest November schedule in the entire league. But despite the brutal schedule, the Bucks have kept things close, playing in seven clutch-time games this year (and making a habit of it over the last five games). This is largely due to a burgeoning new bench mob—the Bucks currently rank second in the NBA in bench points at 45.2. While Milwaukee cannot afford to take anything for granted at 2-16, it feels as though they enter this game in the driver's seat.

Joshua Jefferson: Out (G League assignment)

With Nolan Traore out, Mikel Brown Jr. will likely get the start again, and the sixth overall pick in this year's draft is looking to build on his last game in Minnesota. Turnovers (2.4 per game to just 3.8 assists per game) have been an issue, and the shooting has been streaky (36.4% from the field), but if Brown can put things together, it will help energize an offense that could match the pace the Bucks have played at over the last few games.

Who: Milwaukee Bucks (2-16) vs. Brooklyn Nets (3-13).

Kel'el Ware will finally have his breakout game. Brooklyn ranks 22nd overall in paint points allowed at 48.9. If Ware's three-point shooting is on, it could spell doom for the Nets by opening lanes for the Bucks' crafty backcourt; if not, the Kryptonian will make his presence felt near the rim. 15 and 10 with five blocks as the Bucks notch their third victory of the year!
