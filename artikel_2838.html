NBA Star Tari Eason Sues Ex, Claims at Least $92K in Losses Over Missing Rolex and Bleached Designer Clothes

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Houston Rockets forward Tari Eason is suing a former girlfriend, claiming at least $92,000 in losses involving his personal belongings.

The 25-year-old's lawsuit names Marilyn Maritza Melo Rodriguez as the defendant. His claim covers both missing possessions and damage to his wardrobe.

According to court documents reported by TMZ on November 19, Eason alleges Rodriguez entered his rental property without permission in June.

Eason says he and his attorney repeatedly asked Rodriguez to return his belongings, but she refused. His complaint seeks their return or financial compensation.

The items identified in the filing include a Rolex watch, Chrome Hearts glasses and a Louis Vuitton duffel bag Eason values at $15,000. He also accuses Rodriguez of pouring bleach over his designer clothing.

The complaint seeks up to three times his claimed losses under California law, along with additional compensation and attorney fees.

Eason petitioned for a domestic violence restraining order in Los Angeles County in July. As Chron reported, he alleged that Rodriguez repeatedly struck him in the face during an argument.

He also claimed that one of her associates called and threatened to break his legs and kill him. Eason said the threats left him fearing for his safety.

A source close to Rodriguez disputed the earlier account in TMZ's July 31 update, describing it as false and misleading. The source said the portrayal was intended to damage her reputation and divert attention from her own allegations of harassment and emotional distress.

A judge granted the temporary restraining order on July 30. Its restrictions barred Rodriguez from approaching Eason, his home, workplace and vehicle, as well as NBA facilities where he plays or practices.

TMZ's latest report said the order remained in effect, with another hearing scheduled for July 29, 2027.
