Hawks overcome 22-point first half deficit, defeat Bucks 113-106

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Atlanta Hawks were in action on Sunday night to face the Milwaukee Bucks. This was the second game of the Hawks' three-game road trip, and they got things started on a bad note, losing to the Chicago Bulls on Friday. This was a good game to bounce back, as the Bucks are near the bottom of the Eastern Conference, but so were the Bulls, and they still lost.

The Hawks also came into this game with injuries, with Kingston Flemings and Jock Landale ruled out. That meant CJ McCollum was inserted into the starting lineup, and there would be minutes for Zuby Ejiofor coming off the bench.

McCollum and Jalen Johnson got things going early for the Hawks.

Similar to the Bulls game, the Bucks came out knocking down almost every 3-pointer they took, while the Hawks struggled on offense. Onyeka Okongwu was the recepient of some easy looks in the paint, including this one from Johnson.

The Hawks still weren't able to cover much ground in the first, and the Bucks had most of the momentum. They carried on that momentum until the end of the quarter, as they forced six turnovers and scored 12 points off of them.

Going into the second, the Hawks trailed 37-18.

Things didn't get better for the Hawks to start the second, as they found themselves down by 22 points. The good news is that the 3-pointers started to fall, courtesy of RayJ Dennis and Corey Kispert.

What was once a 22-point deficit turned into single digits as the quarter continued, and it was due to the Hawks being able to get stops and get out in transition. Nickeil Alexander-Walker got this strip on Tyler Herro and took to the other end for two.

Herro once again turned the ball over and the Hawks were able to score on the other end.

The Hawks were able to tie the game late in the quarter, and going into halftime, they pushed the pace so Dyson Daniels could help them take a two-point lead. It was a 37-13 run for the Hawks to end the half, and that was all due to their defense and the Bucks coming back down to earth with their shooting.

The Hawks caught a rhythm in the third quarter, and they extended their lead. As for the Bucks, they struggled to make a shot, a completely different story from the first half.

Alexander-Walker knocked down a few shots in the quarter to help the Hawks.

The transition buckets continued to pile up for the Hawks, and Johnson got this breakaway dunk.

McCollum happened to be in the right place at the right time for this offensive rebound, and he put it back up for the layup plus the foul.

The Bucks did find some life down the stretch of the third and cut into their deficit, but the Hawks quickly regrouped and bought their lead to double digits. Wiggins helped make that happen.

Going into the fourth, the Hawks led 87-80.

The Bucks made a run to start the fourth, which helped them cut their deficit again. Okongwu helped the Hawks stay afloat with a 3-pointer.

Dennis got this and-one to extend the Hawks' lead.

The Bucks kept fighting, but the Hawks continued to hit timely shots to stay in front.

The Hawks needed some big buckets down the stretch, and it was no other McCollum who stepped up.

After both teams failed to make any shots and the Hawks were only up three points, McCollum walked down and got to his sweet spot to put them up five with 17 seconds left.

That ultimately sealed the game for the Hawks, and they walked away with a win.

McCollum and Alexander-Walker finished with 20 points, Okongwu finished with 16 points and 11 rebounds, and Johnson finished with 14 points and 11 rebounds.

The Hawks will be back in action on Wednesday against the Oklahoma City Thunder.
