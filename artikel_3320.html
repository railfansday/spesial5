If you have a DIY enthusiast on your list—or if you just want some new tools for your own projects—Black Friday sales are a good time to snag low prices on quality tools. Lots of big box stores are looking to thin out their inventory and make room for next year’s upgrades, so in addition to encouraging a holiday rush, retailers are motivated to price things to move. If you know what to look for, you can get up to 65% off on trusted brands. Here are a few of the best deals out there right now.

If you’re building a new cordless tool set, or updating your battery system, getting a cordless tool set can save you some money because these combos usually include batteries and a charger, which can be the most expensive part of your tool set. Here are a few of the best early Black Friday deals on cordless tool sets.

The Ryobi 6-tool 18-volt combo set is on sale for $199, 33% off its regular price at Home Depot. The set comes with a drill, an impact driver, a circular saw, a sawzall, an oscillating multi-tool, a work light, a 1.5-amp-hour battery, a 4-amp-hour battery, a charger, and a tool bag. This is a good deal on a trusted home tool brand.

The Ryobi 18-volt drill and driver set is on sale for $99, 34% off its usual price at Home Depot. The set comes with a drill, an impact driver, two 1.5-amp-hour batteries, a charger, and a tool bag. This is a great starter set because it has the first two tools you need for a cordless kit and it comes with batteries so you can expand your set for cheaper as you develop your skills.

The Ryobi 4-tool, 4-volt project set is on sale for $99, 61% off its typical price at Home Depot. This set comes with a rotary tool, a project light, a glue pen, a mini drill, two rechargeable batteries, a charging cable, a set of starter rotary bits, and three glue sticks. This set is handy for DIY projects that require fine detail, like wood carving, craft projects, or framing pictures.

The DeWalt drill and driver combo set is on sale for $149, 38% off its regular price on Amazon. This set includes a drill, an impact driver, 2 1.5-amp-hour batteries, a charger, and a tool bag. This is a good deal on a trusted brand for drills and drivers, and a good start for a cordless DIY tool set.

If you have a cordless tool set already and you want to expand your kit, or if you have a DIYer in your life who already has a particular brand of battery and you want to get them something they can use, a single tool deal can be just the thing.

The DeWalt 20-volt compact drill/driver is on sale at Home Depot for $99, 45% off its usual price. This tool comes with a 20-volt, 2-amp-hour battery and a charger, so it’s ready to use out of the box. It’s a good first tool for a set.

The Milwaukee 18-volt impact wrench is on sale at Home Depot for $199, 68% off its typical price. This tool comes with two 5-amp-hour batteries and a charger, so it’s ready to use out of the box. This is a good tool for driving bolts through tough materials and for automotive projects.

The DeWalt 20-volt oscillating multi-tool is on sale at Home Depot for $99, 50% off its regular price. This tool comes with a 2-amp-hour battery and a charger, so it's ready to use out of the box. This is one of the most versatile cordless tools, allowing you to cut a wide range of materials and use a sanding attachment to do finishing work as well.

The Milwaukee 18-volt tire inflator kit is on sale at Home Depot for $199, 65% off its usual price. This tool comes with two 5-amp-hour batteries and a charger, so it’s ready to go right out of the box. This is an excellent addition to your emergency roadside tool set because you can use it to inflate a car tire with the charged 18-volt batteries.

The Makita 18-volt cordless electric 2-inch brad nailer is on sale on Amazon for $324, 38% off its regular price. This is a tool-only deal, so you’ll need a Makita 18-volt battery and charger to use it. This is a good tool for attaching trim or paneling.
