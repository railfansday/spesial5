The Best Amazon Prime Benefits You Probably Don't Know About

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

If you’re going to commit to an Amazon Prime membership, you might as well squeeze as much as you can out of the deal. And there's a good amount of juice to squeeze there. The price you pay for Amazon depends on many factors, but regardless of how much you're paying, it's worth it for most people (we've done the math).

If you're on the fence about signing up, consider that Amazon's Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale is underway, and the company is hosting some amazing tech deals. It's a great time to sell your soul—or take advantage of the 30-day free trial—and there's a lot more to your membership than just deals and free shipping.

The standard Prime membership costs $14.99 per month or $139 per year, but there are other tiers that some people might qualify for. For example, the Amazon Prime for Young Adults program allows young adults between the ages of 18 and 24 to get all of the Prime perks for half the cost—$7.49 per month or $69 annually.

I'll be the first to admit I didn't know all of the benefits that Amazon Prime offers to its members. There are a lot more perks than people think. Here are the most impressive ones (and check out the full list):

Fast free shipping on millions of items: Depending on where you live, shipping can take one or two days, and some even come on the same day for orders of $25 or more in most areas—and it’s always free (if being shipped by the Amazon store).

Select pre-ordered items can be delivered on their release date for free (for most areas).

The option to have items delivered inside your garage with a smart garage device (depending on where you live) for $1.99 per order, or for free if you combine it with Amazon Day delivery.

Earn rewards for future purchases when you select free no-rush shipping.

The option to have all your weekly orders delivered on a specific day, to reduce waste from boxes.

Amazon Prime Visa card: Cardholders get 5% back on Amazon, Whole Foods, Chase Travel, and other purchases. Prime members who use this card extensively can earn rewards that offset the cost of membership.

A Grubhub+ and Uber Eats membership, normally $9.99 per month, which gets you free delivery on eligible orders, lower service fees, exclusive deals, and other perks as long as you remain a Prime Member (it used to be only for a year). The Uber Eats membership gets you free delivery on eligible restaurants of $25 or more.

Sharing with family or friends: Amazon Family is the existing program for sharing Prime benefits, and it allows you to add separate profiles for one other adult who lives in the same household as the primary account holder, up to four teens, and up to four child accounts. This grants access to all the standard benefits, such as free delivery, Prime Video with ads, Prime Reading, third-party benefits like Grubhub, and access to audiobooks, e-books, certain games, and Amazon Music.

Amazon Health: Prime users get One Medical access for an additional $9 per month or $99 per year. Through your devices, you’ll have 24/7 access to on-demand virtual care, plus real-life access to primary care offices in a number of U.S. cities. It accepts most major health insurance plans, too.

Amazon Pharmacy: Access to free two-day delivery and other savings on medications.

Prime Video: Prime includes unlimited streaming of movies, TV shows, and Amazon Originals with ads, which normally costs $9 per month.

Amazon Music Prime: Access to Amazon's Spotify-esque music streaming service (not to be confused with Amazon's other music streaming subscriptions).

Amazon Luna: A cloud gaming platform that offers new free games every month, free downloadable PC games, content, a monthly Twitch channel subscription, and a free channel subscription on Twitch each month for Prime Members.

Amazon Photos: Unlimited photo storage plus 5 GBs for videos and other files (which extends to members of your Family Vault).

Prime Reading: Free access to a rotating selection of e-books and magazines for Kindle or Fire tablets and Kindle apps.

Amazon's First Reads: You get up to two free e-books every month from a curated list of books handpicked by editors.
