Pierce Brosnan Welcomes Robert Pattinson Into James Bond Role: ‘Enjoy the Ride’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Former 007 Pierce Brosnan has officially welcomed Robert Pattinson into the James Bond club. The actor, who play the spy in four films from 1995-2002, shared a photo of Pattison on Instagram along with a personal message for his former co-star from 2010 romantic drama Remember Me.

“Dear Robert, Welcome to the world of Bond,” Brosnan wrote. “You are going to be magnificent. I have fond memories of our time together making ‘Remember Me.’ It has been a joy to watch you create a remarkable body of work and to go from strength to strength. Enjoy the ride. Grá mor, Pierce.”

Brosnan is not the first former Bond to congratulate Pattinson. George Lazenby, who played Bond in 1969’s On Her Majesty’s Secret Service, also reminded the Batman actor, “Remember to enjoy yourself.”

“I hear they’ve announced the new James Bond @007,” Lazenby wrote on X. “Robert, welcome to the most exclusive club in the world. But remember to enjoy yourself. Let’s see if you’ll have ‘this never happened to the other fella’ moment. All the best mate, George.”

Pattinson’s casting as the next 007 was announced earlier this week after months of heavy speculation. In a statement, Pattinson called Bond “one of the most legendary characters in cinema” and said securing the role was “the honor of a lifetime.” He continued: “To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. The actor will next appear in Dune: Part Three, and is set to partner again with Dune filmmaker Denis Villeneuve, who was announced as the next Bond director last year. The forthcoming Bond movie is being written by Steven Knight, best known as the creator of Peaky Blinders.

“I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond,” Villeneuve said. “He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin.”
