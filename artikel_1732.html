Luis Enrique backs PSG’s resilience amid attacking injuries

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Luis Enrique believes his Paris Saint-Germain side have already "shown their ability to be resilient" as they are set to be without two key attackers for the rest of the season.

After Khvicha Kvaratskhelia suffered an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury in October against Barcelona, Desire Doue went down with the same injury against Nice on the weekend.

Both forwards have been ruled out for the remainder of 2026-27, placing more impetus on the likes of Mika Godts, Maghnes Akliouche and Ferran Torres.

PSG did receive a boost ahead of their Champions League encounter with Roma on Wednesday, with Ousmane Dembele making his return from injury against Nice.

Luis Enrique believes his attacking players now have an opportunity to showcase their talents after the injuries to Kvaratskhelia and Doue.

"I feel sad for the players, but it's a wonderful opportunity to show our level. It's a great challenge to take on," said the Spaniard.

"This is normal. All teams have injured players. In this case, it's not normal to have injuries as serious as those of Kvara and Des'. We remain focused on the players' recovery."

"This team have shown their ability to be resilient; it's a great challenge to take on, to think about improving, to find solutions, whether in the tactical or technical aspect."

PSG have faced seven different Italian opponents in European competition, but this will be their first ever meeting with Roma.

PSG have had at least 10 shots on target in all four of their Champions League games so far this season – no team on record (since 2003-04) has ever done so in five in a row.

However, they will need to keep a close eye on Donyell Malen, who has been directly involved in six goals in the Champions League this season (four goals, two assists).

The only Roma player with more goal involvements in a single edition is Edin Dzeko (10 in 2017-18 and eight in 2018-19).

"We all know the AS Roma players, especially the forwards. Malen used to play on the wing, and when he moved into the centre, he showed his ability to score goals," added Luis Enrique.

"His goal tally is incredible. We'll have to be careful with the forwards, but also with all the players, because mobility is important for Roma.

"They deserved more points against Real Madrid and Manchester United. I hope we can overcome their pressure and take all three points."
