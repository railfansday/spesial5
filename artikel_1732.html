Opinion: Fabrizio Romano confirms Liverpool have path to complete bombshell Arsenal deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Fabrizio Romano's latest comments suggest Liverpool have a clear path to a bombshell deal with Arsenal. Could this actually happen?

Liverpool's midfield project needs a rethink and we're already starting to see the beginning of that. They invested an incredible amount of money in two players this year, handing out new contracts to Ryan Gravenberch and Dominik Szoboszlai.

However, neither appear to be first-choice under new head coach Andoni Iraola. He's opting for Trey Nyoni and Alexis Mac Allister - which certainly wasn't the plan this summer.

But it quickly became clear that Liverpool lacked balance and control through the middle. That's led to repurposing Nyoni as a holding player and going with Mac Allister, who's arguable the best all-around midfielder (if not player) in the Reds' squad.

Nyoni, though, is still relatively untested. Liverpool won't want him to be the only holding midfielder in the squad and signing someone to ease the pressure on him still feels like the sensible option.

We're seeing moves in this direction. Mac Allister looks set for contract talks, finally, while the club are constantly linked with defensive midfielders. One of whom looks like he might have a clear pathway.

And it sounds as though they've got a path to signing him. Arsenal are reportedly open to selling as they look to fund a big-money move themselves, with Michael Olise linked to them.

Now Fabrizio Romano has discussed the situation on his YouTube, claiming that both Atlético Madrid and Chelsea are unlikely landing spots. Atleti simply can't afford him, while Chelsea have Alex Scott on their radar.

So Real Madrid might be the only proper suitor. Though, they've just renewed Aurelien Tchouameni's contract - they might not have room for Zubimendi.

TeamTalk suggest that intermediaries have discusssed a potential move with Liverpool and it increasingly looks like the Reds are the best landing spot here. An opportunity that shouldn't be missed.
