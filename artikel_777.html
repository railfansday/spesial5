Frank earns first Palace win with late comeback against Sociedad

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Crystal Palace vs Real Sociedad. UEFA Europa League Round 1.

Selhurst ParkAttendance: Attendance20,053.

Match report as Thomas Frank earns his first win as Crystal Palace manager; Mamadou Sarr gave Real Sociedad the lead after a dominant opening hour; Jon Martin was sent off and Jean-Philippe Mateta scored from the penalty spot to level; Dwight McNeill score the winner

Thomas Frank earned his first win as Crystal Palace manager, coming from behind to beat Real Sociedad 2-1 in the Europa League.

The Dane lost his opening game to Coventry at the weekend, but saw the heart and fight he will need from his side as they picked up their second win in the competition this season.

For 70 minutes, Sociedad dominated at Selhurst Park, with Dean Henderson needed to make a string of saves. They finally made the breakthrough when Chelsea loanee Mamadou Sarr tapped home from Job Ochieng's cross.

But the game turned out of nowhere as Crystal Palace were awarded a penalty and Jon Martin was sent off. He picked up a second yellow card for holding Jean-Philippe Mateta round the middle, with the striker expertly dispatching the spot kick to level for the Eagles.

The confidence surged in the hosts - backed by the famous atmosphere at Selhurst Park - and it was a well-worked second that put them ahead.

Yeremy Pino's cross was cleared to Jorgen Strand Larsen at the back post, who hit an overhead kick. It wasn't on target, but it did bobble into the six-yard box for the incoming Dwight McNeil, who slid to poke home.

Crystal Palace could have wrapped up the points in a topsy-turvy end after they were awarded another penalty. Carlos Soler was penalised for handball, but Pino's effort was well-saved by Unai Marrero.

The win catapults Crystal Palace into 12th place in the league phase table, just two from the top eight, and gives them a huge boost ahead of another key clash against Hull on Super Sunday. Real Sociedad have slid down into 27th.

Crystal Palace manager Thomas Frank to TNT Sport on if the win can be a turning point: "I surely hope so. You can't underestimate a win, what that gives the group, what they're feeling.

"I think there was more positives than negatives from this performance, but still a lot we need to work on, and to keep working on.

"It's a fantastic feeling to get the win in every aspect here at home and in the circumstances that it's a season that hasn't been living up to everything we expected, so the confidence is maybe not at the highest.

"The ability to come from 1-0 behind and then go to win the game, the mentality and the belief in the players was really good.

"We're playing a very good team, they're fifth in LaLiga, they're very good on the ball. Not everything was perfect in the first half, but the willingness to suffer together and defend together was there, that was good, then we built a little bit more into the second half."

Crystal Palace goalscorer Dwight McNeil to TNT Sport on what Frank has bought to the club: "It's like an energy, a belief. He keeps telling us how good as players we are, but now we're just putting it out on the pitch and we did that tonight.

"We knew it was going to be a tough game but we stuck in there, especially going down 1-0 and then the big man [Mateta] scoring to get us back into the game and then we go on from there."

In response, Frank said: "It's easy to bring energy and the confidence, but what really moves the needle is that you get over the line and get some wins.

"In the last five minutes, I think we all almost were about to get a heart attack because it went both ways, back-to-back. That's also part of the confidence and also we should have controlled that game against 10, but it's football, it's emotions, it's everything."

Analysis from Sky Sports' Charlotte Marsh: "Perhaps one of the most surprising aspects of Crystal Palace this season has been a lack of fight and desire.

"Under Pierre Sage, the team looked disjointed and out of sorts. You did not always see the same determination and sheer-mindedness that has seen the Eagles achieve beyond their wildest dreams over the last few years.

"But we saw that again on Thursday - the Palace of last season, of 'old' perhaps, is still there. To come from behind against a team of Real Sociedad's quality is impressive, and it hinged on one moment. Some fans might say it is about time things started turning in Palace's favour this season.

"They had certainly ridden their luck until that point. Frank very quickly must eradicate the mistakes in and out of possession. Too many sloppy moments allowed Sociedad onto the ball and to have a shot. They were certainly victims of their own lack of precision - Palace might not be so fortunate next time.

"But what the final 20 minutes and result will bring them is a huge boost in confidence that must be channelled towards the Premier League. Frank said in his pre-match press conference that he knows he will be judged on league results and he's right - now it's time to start getting some."
