El Coyote Earns Third Regional Mexican Airplay No. 1 With ‘Ranchero Enamorado’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It's the second chart-topper from the group's album Nacimos Pa' Ser Historia.

Sr. Charts & Data Analyst (Latin & Billboard Español)

El Coyote y Su Banda Tierra Santa earns its third No. 1 on Billboard’s Regional Mexican Airplay chart as “Ranchero Enamorado” jumps 2-1 on the list dated Nov. 21. The song is the second chart-topper from the group’s album Nacimos Pa’ Ser Historia, following “Te Dije” in July.

Released on Andaluz/FOMO/UMLE, “Ranchero Enamorado” climbs from No. 2 with 6.9 million audience impressions earned in the United States, up 6%, during the Nov. 6-12 tracking week, according to Luminate.

The song switches places with Los Ángeles Azules and Tini’s “Pa’ Él,” as the latter cedes the summit, dipping 1-2, after an 11% dip in audience impressions, down to 6.4 million.

El Coyote y Su Banda Tierra Santa’s ascent to No. 1 is the fifth time this year a group has taken the top spot from a collaborative track. Banda El Recodo de Cruz de Lizárraga’s “Sólo Tú” replaced La Arrolladora Banda El Limón and Juanes’ “Una Noche Contigo” at No. 1 on the Jan. 24-dated chart. Los Dos de Tamaulipas’ “El Chavalón” then took the crown from Xavi and Carín León’s “La Morrita” on the March 7 chart. Three weeks later, Calibre 50’s “Me Enamoré Solo” pushed “La Morrita” from the lead on the March 28-dated ranking. Meanwhile, Banda Los Recoditos followed on May 23, when “En Coma Inducido” took No. 1 from Banda Los Sebastianes de Saúl Plata and El Jerry Gerardo Coronel’s “Al Chile No Sé.”

Notably, before 2026, El Coyote y Su Banda Tierra last topped Regional Mexican Airplay in 2000, with “Te Soñé.”

“Ranchero Enamorado” also rises to a new No. 3 peak on the overall Latin Airplay chart, nearly matching its highest rank to date —among 22 entries— through the No. 2-peaking “Te Dije” last July.

Elsewhere, “Ranchero” gives the group its highest rank on the Hot Regional Mexican Songs list, where it rises 10-7.
