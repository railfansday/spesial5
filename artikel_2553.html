Mikel Brown Jr. impresses in first start during Nets night to forget against Timberwolves

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

MINNEAPOLIS — Nolan Traore's hamstring injury is a tough break for the Nets and their second-year pro. But a golden opportunity for rookie Mikel Brown Jr.

With Traore sidelined for at least 8-to-10 weeks, Brown finally got his first NBA start Monday at Minnesota. It may not have been a winning one for the lottery pick, but it was an impressive one.

The Nets got drubbed 142-116 by the Timberwolves before 18,329 at Target Center. But Brown's debut in the lineup was one of the few bright spots.

Brooklyn Nets guard Mikel Brown Jr. (0) drives past Minnesota Timberwolves center Rudy Gobert (27) during a game in Minneapolis, Monday, Nov. 23, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

"Just a normal game. It's another game for me," Brown told The Post beforehand. "Whether I come off the bench or whether I start, it's all about playing hard for four quarters and getting a [win]. So it's a great opportunity."

Traore was diagnosed with a right hamstring strain, opening the door for Brown, who had heretofore been on a minutes limit and treated with kid gloves.

But Monday the training wheels finally came off. Brown finished with 17 points, a career-high seven assists and five rebounds, albeit six turnovers for a Nets team that looked surprisingly fluid but shockingly cavalier and careless.

The Nets gave away 21 turnovers for 31 points. Combined with allowing 58 percent shooting and 17-of-39 from deep, they gave themselves no chance to win.

They allowed 34 points to Jaden McDaniels and offered no defensive resistance, while Michael Porter Jr. disappeared for long stretches.

But Brown was a silver lining. He poured in all of his points in the first half, and finished shooting 4-for-6 from behind the arc, showing deep range and a hair-trigger release.

Grant Nelson (16) of the Brooklyn Nets drives to the basket against the Minnesota Timberwolves at Target Center in Minneapolis on Monday, Nov. 23, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

The rookie also combined well with Julius Randle, who had a team-high 29 points in his first clash with the Timberwolves (13-5) since they dealt him to Brooklyn this summer.

With Brown at the helm, the Nets shot 52.6 percent and hit 18-for-36 from deep.

As much as the Nets will miss Traore — who had leveled up from his rookie season — it was at least an encouraging first start for Brown.

Brooklyn Nets guard Mikel Brown Jr. (0) dribbles against Minnesota Timberwolves guard Ayo Dosunmu (13) during a game in Minneapolis, Monday, Nov. 23, 2026. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

"It's unfortunate for the kid because he's had a great summer," coach Jordi Fernández said of Traore. "He's about the right things. He's worked really hard. He's improved his body, his game. You just saw his confidence out there and his personality and how he communicated [with] his teammates. Everything has been great. Now, it's not the end of the world. Obviously it's an injury that we feel for him; he's already started working and he'll be back at some point.

"Life goes on and we want him around as soon as he can be with the team. And that's important because his energy was so good, his voice. So, next man mentality. Mikel will have the opportunity to start, and I think that's also great and it's exciting. And to [the] question about what his minute restriction is — which I'm not going to tell you — he'll be able to play starters minutes, whatever that means."

The Nets fell behind 25-12 with 3:54 left in the first on a Jonathan Kuminga layup. They then gave up 10 unanswered to turn a six-point deficit into a 16-point hole. They trailed 74-61 going into the break, and couldn't rally.
