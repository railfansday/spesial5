Lions vs. Patriots prediction: Detroit seeks fifth straight victory, while New England looks to bounce back

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

The New England Patriots and Detroit Lions will kick off Sunday's action with a 9:30 a.m. game at Allianz Arena in Munich, Germany on Sunday, Nov. 15. The Lions come into the international matchup at 6-2 and are coming off a 19-9 win over the Miami Dolphins. The victory extended their win streak to four games.

Detroit's 6-2 record is strong, but because the NFC North is so good, they are currently narrowly in second place in the division, tied record-wise with the Minnesota Vikings.

The Patriots story is a bit different, coming off a bad loss to the Green Bay Packers. In the 45-27 defeat, quarterback Drake Maye threw three interceptions, tying him with winless Los Angeles Chargers QB Justin Herbert for most picks in the league (11).

The Patriots are 2-2 in their last four games and sit in second place in the AFC East.

Both teams can make big plays, but it's what happens between those big gains that may determine the winner of this matchup between two coaches, Mike Vrabel and Dan Campbell, who tend to be aggressive in their play-calling.

I think the Patriots will make some adjustments from last week and come out stronger in Week 10. This game could really go either way and we will know if the Patriots offense has the juice to win right away, as their first few drives tend to establish the rest of the game. If New England's defense can bounce back and Maye can stop giving the ball away, they can keep themselves in the playoff conversation with a win.  Pick: Patriots 33, Lions 30
