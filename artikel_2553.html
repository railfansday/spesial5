Packers vs. Rams live updates, inactives, where to watch: Micah Parsons makes season debut as Jordan Love, Matthew Stafford open Week 12 on Thanksgiving Eve

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Green Bay Packers and Los Angeles Rams will kick off the Week 12 game slate with a Thanksgiving Eve showdown in a rare Wednesday matchup. Kickoff is set for 8:15 p.m. ET at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, and the game will air on Netflix.

The Packers head into this game with a 6-4 record and would sit as a wild card team if the season ended today in an ultra-competitive NFC North where each team currently sits above .500. They'll get some superstar power back on defense this week, as star defensive end Micah Parsons makes his season debut following an ACL tear suffered last December.

The Rams also sit at 6-4 heading into this matchup, but sit just outside the playoff picture, currently three games back from the San Francisco 49ers for the lead in the NFC West. A win on Wednesday night would go a long way toward their playoff hopes — but even if they keep those hopes alive, they'll be moving forward without All-Pro wide receiver Puka Nacua, who is out for the rest of the season with a knee injury.

Micah Parsons will officially make his season debut on Wednesday night against the Rams.

Green Bay released its inactives for the game, and Parsons was not listed. The defensive end will play in his first game since tearing his ACL and meniscus in Week 15 last year against the Broncos.

This will be his 15th game with the franchise after he was acquired ahead of the 2025 regular season.

Parsons finished last season with 12.5 sacks — the seventh-most in the league.
