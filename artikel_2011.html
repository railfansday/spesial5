Tonda Eckert’s new deal could have implications for 3 Southampton FC players

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Southampton are propelling themselves back up the Championship table and Tonda Eckert, who had doubts over his future for off-field reasons, is now set to stay for the long-term at St Mary’s – but that could affect three Saints players.

Having been kicked out of the Championship play-offs ahead of the final last season due to ‘spygate’, Saints began this season with a four-point deduction in the second-tier and the prospect of a big ban coming for manager Eckert.

However, Eckert managed to escape with just a suspended six-match ban and so the club have now been able to tie him down to a new contract, too, ensuring the German is contracted to the south coast outfit until the summer of 2029.

Southampton eased past Derby County by two goals to nil at the weekend to leave them sixth in the table and just five points behind Middlesbrough, even with those four points taking off them at the start of the campaign.

Eckert is proving himself to be an excellent coach and having committed his future to the club, three players will now be looking for a permanent route out soon.

Having scored just two goals in 35 top-flight appearances for Saints as they succumbed to a humiliating relegation in which they collected just 12 points, Archer only notched four times in 30 Championship games last season – albeit with a slight improvement in his overall performance level before the end of the campaign before being an unused substitute in both legs of their play-off semi-final against ‘Boro.

Over the summer, he reunited with Will Still at Auxerre and has shone in Ligue 1 with six goals in nine appearances and, with no long-term future at Southampton, especially under Eckert, a permanent move in January or next summer feels inevitable.

Another loanee now destined to leave St Mary’s on a permanent basis is versatile Japan international full-back Yukinari Sugawara, who is currently out at Cagliari in Serie A.

Sugawara has been largely on the periphery of things in terms of the first-team picture in Sardinia, where he now a teammate of former Leicester City midfielder Harry Winks, and that is where he ended up at Southampton, too.

Having spent last season on loan at Werder Bremen in the Bundesliga, Sugawara’s permanent exit from Southampton has been expected for a while but, after a summer in which Eckert again sanctioned his departure, that is now inevitable. He is contracted to the club until the summer of 2028.

Upon their return to the Championship last summer, Southampton shelled out around £7 million on American forward Damion Downs but, from the off, he appeared to be surplus to requirements.

Injuries and generally average form eventually led to Downs being loaned out to Hamburg in the Bundesliga for the second half of last season, despite coming off the bench in Eckert’s first four matches in charge of Saints last November before his injury.

An indifferent time of it at the Volksparkstadion was then followed by the 22-year-old being shipped out to St Louis City on loan this summer, where he has failed to score in ten MLS appearances as the expansion franchise finished third in the Western Conference and now head into the play-offs.

Downs is contracted until the summer of 2029 but a permanent move away in the winter after the close of the 2026 MLS campaign feels inevitable, even if he hasn’t done enough for St Louis to take the plunge on him just yet.
