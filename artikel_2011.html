“Erased” – Media explanation for one star’s waning at West Ham United

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

West Ham’s Nuno- divided squad was rallied midweek by new manager Scott Parker with his declaration that the slate had been wiped clean and that everyone had a chance to impress. Indeed as if to prove the point his first match day squad included Soungoutou Magassa, one of the summer want-aways who didn’t get his way.

Although the French midfielder was understandably rusty and hooked at half time, Parker had proved his point. Images of the head coach in training alongside-even- Maxi Cornet showed nobody is beyond redemption in the Coach’s search for the right eleven.

However, anxious voices across the Atlantic continue to berate West Ham for ruining the career of Mexico’s national team captain Edson Alvarez.

Few in Central America seem able to understand el Machin’s fall from grace, despite both injury and scandal dogging the midfielder during the summer. Rather than accepting that the West Ham management simply don’t rate their skipper, the understandably patriotic local media indulge themselves in all manner of excuses for their star’s waning in east London.

Edson Alvarez pictured back in training in Parker’s pre-Blackburn session.

As futnsoccer.com report, the Mexican football sites are claiming he’s been rendered virtually invisible by successive managers:

“91 days in oblivion, with Alvarez still erased from West Ham.

Hispanic Post went further, arguing in an opinion piece that the exclusion points to a deep fracture between the player and the London club’s project.”

I’d suggest after yesterday’s desperate effort without Edson anywhere near the squad, there might be one or two who started the game against Blackburn who might find their positions coming under scrutiny in the next two weeks as Parker gets to grips with the challenge he faces implanting some spine into his chosen line up.

Whereas the first two sides picked may well have been a ‘committee’ selection involving his coaching team, since the new manager only rocked up on Wednesday, two weeks with virtually his whole squad including the likes of Magassa and Alvarez will allow Parker to judge for himself who has the cojones to give his West Ham line up some much-needed moral fibre.

Should Alvarez still not feature, then the Mexican media will simply have to drop the conspiracy theories and face the fact that successive West Ham managers just don’t think he’s good enough for second tier English football.
