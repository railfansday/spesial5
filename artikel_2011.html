Sources: Jack Grealish keen to join Premier League rival if Everton deal collapses

Jack Grealish is keen to remain in the Premier League even if he move to Everton falls through, sources have told Football Insider.

The 31-year-old has been widely expected to make a permanent swtich to the Hill Dickinson Stadium following back-to-back loan spells, but that has been put into doubt.

Despite his imminent availability as a free agent, with his Man City contract expiring, the wide midfielder's recent conduct has cast doubts over his future on Merseyside.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Grealish will be looking for a new club next summer but wants to stay in the Premier League.

There has been interest from abroad in the England international, including speculative links with a move to Celtic, but he is not interested in a move outside of England.

Instead, should Everton decide against making his stay permanent, he will attempt to drum up interest from some of his long-term admirers in the Premier League.

Football Insider revealed on 8 November that Grealish is now all but finished at Everton, with his chances of a permanent move dashed by his recent diciplinary breach.

The midfielder allegedly failed to report for training, and was subsequently left out of their three games ahead of the international break.

While David Moyes has confessed there is a way back for Grealish, and he could feature in their next match against Brentford, a permanent deal may now be ruled out.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Jack Grealish himself, if he doesn't win a permanent move to Everton, would ideally want to stay in the Premier League.

"He'd prefer to stay playing in England, obivously elsewhere other than Everton, than make a move into Europe or up to Scotland at this stage.

"The wages that he's on would be difficult for clubs outside of the English top flight to match, so that would be a sticking point for a lot of clubs as well.

"So I would say it's a bit of a no-goer at the moment. I think the finances involved, even on a free transfer, would make it probably outside Celtic's price range."

Despite Everton seemingly backing away from their plans to sign Grealish permanently, there are still a number of clubs in the Premier League believed to be keen.

Football Insider revealed back in September that English top-flight clubs will be keeping a close eye on the England international's performances at Everton this season.

Among those suitors are Sunderland and Aston Villa, after both clubs made late attempts to sign him before he completed a return to Everton on loan.

His departure from Man City on a free transfer is now inevitable, sparking renewed interest from his potential suitors, alongside the doubts over his future at Everton.
