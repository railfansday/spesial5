Liverpool working on multiple south American signings, expert predicts likely success rate

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Liverpool are taking a major risk by switching their focus towards exploiting the market for young South American talent in the coming years.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Merseysiders will begin working to “unearth gems” from South America.

This comes after the news that the Reds have appointed Flamengo scouting chief Andrii Fedchenkov as their new head of scouting operations.

However, Wyness believes that the Merseysiders’ new strategy is fraught with risk, as young starlets from outside Europe can often find it difficult to adapt to the drastic change of lifestyle that comes with a big move.

Fedchenkov is set to take the reins at Anfield in January, but Wyness believes the true test of his ability will not come straight away.

Instead, he believes that the 30-year-old’s work cannot be judged until the summer of 2028, when he will have had multiple transfer windows to implement his ideas.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that it is often a “50-50” chance as to whether a young South American player will be able to adapt to the Premier League.

The Scottish businessman feels that Liverpool are taking something of a gamble with their new approach, but it could potentially pay off for them with major upside.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how Fedchenkov’s imminent arrival could change things at Anfield.

“I’ve bought South American players before and I've seen the issues, is how they settle when they come over. And it's 50-50 in many cases, despite the talent that they show in South America,” he said.

“So hopefully he (Fedchenkov) will be a good judge of character, because that will be important in terms of that.

“Obviously that's where Liverpool are looking in terms of trying to unearth some gems. So look, I don't know the guy personally, but hopefully he fits in well, and he’s got all the promise.

“It probably won't be until the summer of 2028 when we can really judge him. I hope he gets that long. It's going to be a long-term burn because there's a lot of tidying up in the Liverpool squad to do first.”

Football Insider revealed earlier this week that Liverpool are set to prioritise a move for a new left-back next summer.

Kostas Tsimikas is out of contract at the end of the season, and with talks over an extension yet to take place, the club are planning for him to depart.

Sources say that the Liverpool recruitment team are already exploring their options for a replacement, and are keen to avoid leaving Milos Kerkez as the only left-back option.

They have begun to overhaul their squad for Andoni Iraola already, and that is expected to continue across the upcoming transfer windows.
