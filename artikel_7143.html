Ritz Returns for Super Bowl 61 in a Play for Casual Snackers

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Mondelēz has announced that it will advertise its Ritz brand in Super Bowl XLI, marking the third consecutive year in the game for the venerable cracker with the scalloped edge.

The company is keeping most details of its campaign close to the vest for now. But category president for savory snacking, Steven Saenen, told ADWEEK that the goal is to maintain the momentum begun in 2025 when the 90 year old brand appeared in a Super Bowl ad for the first time.

“The brand is perceived as more modern, more fun than it was two years ago, [and] market share gains have been pretty strong over the past two years,” Saenen said.

Robert Klara is the senior editor of brands at Adweek, specializing in the evolution and impact of brands.
