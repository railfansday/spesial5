The Associate Product Marketing Manager, GRC will support the go-to-market strategy and execution for select products in Mitratech's GRC portfolio. This role will focus on positioning and messaging, competitive intelligence, product launches, and sales enablement, working closely with Product Marketing, Product Management, Sales, Demand Generation, and Customer Marketing. We're looking for a hands-on marketer who can own defined projects, turn complex product and market information into clear, customer-facing messaging, and collaborate effectively across teams. The ideal candidate is curious, proactive, organized, and comfortable using data and AI tools to make marketing work more efficient.
