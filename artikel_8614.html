Get the Ink Business Premier® Credit Card if your business pays the balance in full each month and makes purchases of $5,000 or more, which earn 2.5% cash back. Once those purchases pass $19,500 a year, the extra 1% they earn over the no-fee Ink Business Unlimited’s flat 1.5% more than pays the $195 annual fee.

Chase issues the Ink Business Premier to sole proprietors as well as companies, so a side business can apply, and the owner who applies is personally liable for the balance. Purchases go first to Flex for Business, a part of the credit line you can repay over time with interest, and anything past its limit must be paid in full each month.

Compared with the no-fee Ink Business Unlimited’s 1.5%, the extra cash back here covers the $195 fee at $19,500 a year in purchases of $5,000 or more.

Without big purchases or travel booked through Chase Travel, covering the fee takes $39,000 a year in spending, and a business that spends less earns more with the Ink Business Unlimited.
