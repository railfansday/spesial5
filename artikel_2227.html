Ex-Celtic star’s £22m move takes fresh twist as manager who signed him axed

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Arne Engels faces a fresh period of uncertainty at West Ham after the club sacked Nuno Espirito Santo just months after his £22 million move from Celtic.

The former Hoops midfielder has started every Championship game since making the switch to the London Stadium, with Nuno having been a major backer of the Belgian.

But West Ham have now decided to make a change following back-to-back home defeats, leaving the club eighth in the Championship.

Saturday’s 3-1 loss to West Brom prompted what the Hammers described as a “thorough review” of their start to life in the second tier.

The club had opted to stick with Nuno in May despite his failure to preserve their Premier League status, but the Portuguese coach has now been removed from his position.

The managerial change provides an intriguing twist for Arne Engels, who only left Celtic for West Ham in August.

The Belgian made the move in a £22m deal after Celtic rejected two earlier bids, with Martin O’Neill admitting at the time that Engels wanted the transfer and did not want to risk injury by playing in the Hoops’ Champions League play-off.

Martin O’Neill also described the midfielder’s departure as “disappointing” and conceded his running power would be difficult for Celtic to replace.

Arne Engels has since been a regular under Nuno, starting every league match following his arrival, but will now have to impress whoever takes charge next.

West Ham said: “West Ham United can confirm that men’s first-team head coach Nuno Espírito Santo has left his position with immediate effect.

“Following a thorough review of the start to the EFL Championship season, the board of directors believe that a change is necessary at this time in order to ensure that the club’s objectives and expectations remain on track.

“Assistant head coach Rui Pedro Silva and goalkeeper coach Rui Barbosa have also departed.

“West Ham United would like to thank Nuno, Rui and Rui for their hard work and commitment during their time at the club, and wish them all the very best for the future.

“The process of recruiting a new head coach is already underway.”

Mark Noble, Steve Potts and Gerard Prenderville will take temporary charge of preparations for Wednesday night’s Championship trip to Cardiff City.
