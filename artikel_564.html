Producer of Oscar-Nominated ‘A Friend Of Dorothy’ to Make Directorial Debut With Disability Drama ‘Hidden Creatures,’ Easyjet Founder Boards as EP (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Max Marlow has cast actors with lived experience for his directorial debut.

Max Marlow, who produced this year’s Oscar-nominated short film “A Friend of Dorothy” starring Miriam Margolyes and Stephen Fry, is set to make his directorial debut.

“Hidden Creatures” is a magical-realist short drama exploring invisible disability through what’s described as an “unconventional coming-of-age story.”

Set in a cinema, the film follows 17-year-old Rafi on a date; nervous not because he’s with a girl, but because of the creature he’s hiding from her under his shirt. Newcomer Oliver Kaye will make his screen debut in the lead role, bringing personal lived experience to a character navigating the complexities of a situation that the filmmakers say remains largely unseen by those around him.

Kaye, known online to his 18K+ followers as @thekidwithabag, is a medical student and U.K. advocate for invisible illnesses, who shares his life living with a stoma bag following surgery to remove his large intestine due to ulcerative colitis in 2020. Marlow, having undergone the same surgery almost 10 years prior, was drawn to casting Kaye after an initial introduction through mutual family friends to offer advice led him to discover Ollie’s camera-facing social media presence.

“I see a lot of myself, in Rafi, particularly in the emotional and mental challenges of navigating life with a stoma and an invisible condition,” says Marlow. “It was especially important to me that his story reflected the realities that so many people face and are often misunderstood.”

On his first time acting, Kaye said: “Making my screen debut brought a whole range of emotions, from nerves and the uncertainty of stepping into something completely new, to excitement at being part of a project, bigger than myself, with such an important and powerful message. I’m incredibly grateful to Max, the entire team and all the cast for supporting me throughout to draw on both the lived experiences I and people living with invisible illnesses have and the acting side of the role.”

Also joining the cast is actor Adam Rhazali (“Shetland”), who plays James and brings his own lived experience of alopecia to the production. His casting reflects the film’s wider commitment to authentic representation.

“After conversations with experts and others with similar lived experience, I knew authentic casting would be essential on this film, which at its core explores the parts of ourselves we hide to fit in with everyone else,” said Marlow, who wrote the screenplay drawing on his own experiences.

“Having Ollie and Adam bring parts of their own lives to Rafi and James is something I don’t take for granted. There’s a vulnerability and understanding they bring to their characters that simply can’t be manufactured. Although this is a story told through metaphor, I hope the experiences it represents feel recognisable and honest, and that the film opens up conversations that continue long after its festival run and wider release.”

Behind the camera, “Hidden Creatures” marks the first film credit for Sir Stelios Haji-Ioannou, better known as the founder and former CEO of budget airline EasyJet, who serves as executive producer. The film is being produced by Marlow through his A Marlow’s Production banner, in association with Blue Lobster Productions, the Nick Maughan Foundation, the Sandhu Charitable Foundation and the Stelios Philanthropic Foundation. Alongside Haji-Ioannou, executive producers are Jai Sandhu and Nick Maughan. Charities Alopecia UK and RAiISE have each expressed support for the project.

The short film completed principal photography at South London’s Peckhamplex Cinema in November, shot on Kodak 35mm by BIFA-nominated cinematographer Jermaine Canute Edwards (“My Father’s Shadow”). The film also features practical, Henson-inspired in-camera creature effects, with puppets designed and created by Andy Heath of Talk to the Hand Puppets.

“Hidden Creatures” is intended to premiere in 2027. Further casting announcements will follow.
