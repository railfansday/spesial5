New York Jets News: Toilet Bowl Between NYJ, Chargers has Huge Implications

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The New York Jets versus Los Angeles Chargers matchup is a huge contest in the Week 11 slate. How do I know? They're the only game scheduled for 4:05 pm ET on Sunday, November 22. Is that because the NFL wanted to put them in a temporary standalone window? No, it's probably only because this game is being played on the West Coast — but it's still important!

That is because of the implications for the 2027 NFL Draft. The Jets are 2-8 and the Chargers are 1-8.

The Athletic's Austin Mock explained how a win or loss would impact both teams' odds of landing the No. 1 overall pick:

"A Jets loss would bump their odds of getting the No. 1 pick to 10 percent, while a win would almost certainly knock them out of that race. Meanwhile, a Chargers loss would put them firmly back in contention for the top pick, at 23 percent, whereas a win would leave them at just 7 percent. That's the biggest result this week for draft purposes."

So this is a must-lose game for the Jets? Are we even allowed to call it that?
