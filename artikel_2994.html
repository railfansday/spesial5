Why Sandisk Stock Just Dropped Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Sandisk dropped again. And it's TrendForce's fault.

By Rich Smith – Nov 25, 2026 at 10:16AM EST

Sandisk (SNDK -13.28%) stock just might be the cheapest stock ever... that investors think is overpriced.

Shares of the specialist in NAND flash memory sell for a tiny 4.6 times trailing earnings, and are even cheaper when valued on forward earnings. Regardless, investors have cut Sandisk's share price literally in half since the start of November. At $72 and change this morning, Sandisk stock is down another 7.4% as of 10 a.m.

And once again, I blame TrendForce for that.

Five days ago, if you recall, a report from TrendForce sparked a sell-off in Sandisk stock. According to TrendForce, Chinese companies were cutting back on purchases of NAND flash memory, and spot prices were already down 20% -- and heading lower.

Five days later, TrendForce just published a new report: "Chinese [cloud service providers] have reduced their 2027 memory orders," says the memory-market research firm. "The NAND Flash market is expected to enter a downward pricing cycle as supply continues to expand," and so TrendForce is cutting its forecast for memory sales in 2027 even more than it already had -- to $419.3 billion.

That sounds like bad news for Sandisk, but here's where things get curious:

According to TrendForce, the reason why China is cutting memory purchases isn't what you might think. It's not that China is suddenly uninterested in buying memory to help build artificial intelligence. Rather, TrendForce says Chinese companies have been unable to buy all of the GPUs they want to build AI. And lacking sufficient GPUs with which to pair memory chips, they don't need to buy as many memory chips.

The "yet" here is implied, but reading between the lines, the implication seems to be that if China could access more GPUs, it would happily buy more NAND, too. And that means what we have here isn't really a demand problem (despite appearances), but a different sort of supply problem.

Viewed in that light, this news actually sounds long-term bullish for Sandisk.
