Zoom (ZM +2.59%) reported third-quarter results characterized by accelerated growth in its enterprise segment and the introduction of its first quarterly dividend. Management indicated that enterprise revenue growth of 8.6% was the strongest in 13 quarters, driven by the expansion of the Zoom Workplace suite and specialized line-of-business solutions. The company reported a record number of seven-figure deals in its customer experience segment and highlighted the integration of artificial intelligence across its core communication products. Financial results included a non-GAAP operating margin of 41.6% and a total cash and marketable securities balance of $7.3 billion.
