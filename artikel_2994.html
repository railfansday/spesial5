Amazon Forced Into First North American Union Pact in Canada

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Hundreds of Amazon.com Inc. warehouse employees in western Canada are the company’s first North American logistics workers to be covered by a union contract after an independent arbitrator on Friday imposed terms between the online retailer and Unifor, one of the largest private-sector unions in the nation.
