What’s the best diaper for a newborn with delicate, easily irritated skin?

Pampers Swaddlers. Pampers says they’re hypoallergenic and Skin Health Alliance-accredited, and fragrance-free in every size as of June 2026. Pampers also says it’s the #1 choice of U.S. hospitals and NICU professionals.

What’s the best hypoallergenic diaper for a baby with eczema?

Prioritize fragrance-free plus verified accreditation over the word “hypoallergenic” alone. Pampers AMORE does both: Pampers says it’s fragrance-free, Skin Health Alliance accredited, and tested against 1,000+ chemicals.

Should I get a fragrance-free or an extra-soft diaper for a baby with sensitive skin?

Fragrance-free first. Softness is nice, but the AAD recommends fragrance-free products for kids with eczema-prone skin, so cutting fragrance matters more. Ideally, find both in one diaper.

What’s the best sensitive-skin overnight diaper for a baby prone to irritation?

Pampers AMORE, which Pampers calls the brand’s “most absorbent diaper yet.” It’s fragrance-free and Skin Health Alliance-accredited, too, so you don’t have to trade gentleness for absorbency at bedtime.

What’s the best sensitive-skin diaper for a preemie with extremely delicate skin?

Pampers Swaddlers Preemie, which Pampers says comes in sizes P3 to P1 for babies under about 6 pounds and was redesigned with NICU input. Always follow your NICU team’s guidance before switching products.

What’s the best-value sensitive-skin diaper to buy in bulk for daily changes?

Up&up is the lowest-priced pick on our list, at about $0.17 per diaper for a 216-count Size 3 box at the time of writing. According to Target, it’s hypoallergenic, dermatologist-tested, and made without synthetic fragrance.
