Football-Oranje's Michael and Abdul review round 11 of the Eredivisie ...

Netherlands edged out Serbia 2-1 in Eindhoven to move top of their Nations League group.

Xavi decided to name Teun Koopmeiners in the starting eleven but at centre-back alongside Virgil van Dijk, who was earning his 100th cap. Ryan Gravenberch, Tijjani Reijnders and Noa Lang all came into the eleven.

Netherlands made a strong start and missed two good chances to take the lead within the first two minutes. They then dominated possession but it took until the 31st minute for Oranje to take the lead. Koopmeiners free kick evaded everyone and nestled in the corner.

Oranje were only ahead for four minutes as Mihaijo Cvetkovic was allowed far too much space in the Netherlands box to loop in a header. It could have been worse for the Netherlands before the break but Pavlovic squandered a chance when in on goal.

Xavi brought on Joshua Zirkzee for Mexx Meerdink at the break but the best chances early in the second half were for Serbia. Cvetkovic should have put the visitors ahead.

Xavi turned to the bench again with Gjivai Zechiel, Ruben van Bommel and Jorrel Hato all appearing. Reijnders should have made it 2-1 but when through on goal he scooped the ball over the bar.

Netherlands retook the lead after 72 minutes with Denzel Dumfries crossing for Reijnders to square it to an unmarked Zirkzee, who tapped in. It should have been 3-1 straight away but Reijnders was denied through on goal.

The Netherlands sat back and that allowed Serbia to put on pressure. That pressure increased when Micky van de Ven was forced off and Oranje played the last five minutes with only ten men.

Bart Verbruggen made a big save to deny Nemanja Gudelj and Netherlands managed to hold on for the three points.

Netherlands first four games under Xavi see them win two and draw two. They will finish the Nations League at home to Germany and then away to Greece.
