UEFA Nations League: Dortmund youngster Karetsas starts against Serbia as Greece await Germany's result

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Greece and Germany set to battle on Monday for the remaining quarter-final spot from Group A2 in the UEFA Nations League. Although Greece started the day in second place behind Netherlands, who already have 99.99% of qualification after a 7-0 win over Ethniki, all the top three teams could be level on 11 points by the end of the group stage action.

While Germany host historic rivals Netherlands at Olympiastadion, Greece play at home against Serbia, who have lost all five games so far in the group. If both sides won their respective games, Germany will finish top ahead of Netherlands on the head-to-head table involving the three national teams.

Borussia Dortmund's winger Konstantinos Karetsas keeps his starting berth for the hosts, while former Eintracht Frankfurt striker Luka Jovic starts up front for Serbia.

Here are the lineups for tonight's game at Volos.

Greece: Mandas; Rota, Mavropanos, Koulierakis, Tsimikas; Karetsas, Zafeiris, Androutsos, Tzolis; Pavlidis ©, Douvikas

Serbia: Petrovic; Nedeljkovic, Erakovic, Milenkovic, Babic, Bukinac; Lukic, Maksimovic, S. Milinkovic-Savic; Jovic, Mitrovic ©
