’14th’ Trailer: First Look at Ava DuVernay’s Haunting Political Documentary (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Netflix has released the trailer for the political documentary “14th,” analyzing the history of the U.S. Constitution’s amendment and the risks of its weakening power.

Directed by Ava DuVernay, the film follows her “13th” documentary, which examined racial injustices within the American prison system. “Written in the wreckage of the Civil War, the 14th Amendment was meant to close the door on the hierarchy of human worth,” reads the logline. “Instead, it has become a permanent argument.” Related Stories Glenn Close Says Her 'Career Is to Create Characters for Gay Halloween Parades' Hollywood Still Faces Weekend Disruptions After FAA Lifts Flight Ground Stop

The main tenet of the 14th Amendment grants citizenship to all persons born or naturalized in the United States. The documentary explores the potential consequences of removing or diminishing this right. Congresswomen Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and Anna Paulina Luna, former Senator Jeff Flake and current Senator Alex Padilla, plus legal minds Sherrilyn Ifill, Robert Chang and Stacey Abrams are among the historians, lawyers, scholars, curators and politicians lending their voice to the documentary as DuVernay chronicles the vicious battle over the amendment that has raged for over 150 years. “14th” brings together voices in direct conversation with a question America still hasn’t settled: Who gets to belong?

Speaking with Variety before the election which saw Democrats win control of both the House and Senate, DuVernay said, “Folks need to really understand what it means to be a citizen, what it means to fight and to really know your rights.”

Variety’s senior film critic Owen Gleiberman wrote that the documentary “exerts the same kind of historical/topical force that ‘13th’ did. DuVernay, while she works in an extremely accessible and mainstream way, has a singular gift for making the nuances of history come alive.”

The documentary releases in select theaters on Nov. 27 before releasing globally on Netflix on Dec. 4. DuVernay also serves as a producer along with Spencer Averick, Tammy Garnes and Paul Garnes. Spencer Averick is the editor, and Michael Cambio Fernandez and Clifford Charles are the directors of photography. The score is composed by Jason Moran.
