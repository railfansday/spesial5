The Brit List: Coming-of-Age Drama and Cornwall Crime Series Top 2026 Lineup of Best Unproduced Scripts

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Brit List, the annual list of unproduced British film and TV screenplays, has revealed its 2026 lineup.

Compiled from recommendations by the British industry, 13 projects — each requiring a minimum of 10 recommendations — made it onto this year’s list. Leading the pack were Gemma Addy’s coming-of-age drama feature “Dig for Victory” and Archie Thomson’s family crime sage series “Petroc,” both with 25. Previous writers on the list have included the likes of Emerald Fennell, James Graham, Abi Morgan, Jack Thorne and Peter Straughan, while the projects from the screenplays have gone on to make $2 billion at the international box office and generate more than 130 award nominations, including 6 Oscar, 16 BAFTA and 5 Golden Globe wins.

Alongside the lineup, this year The Brit List has also announced Disney+ as its headline sponsor.

“In 2026, The Brit List and Disney+ will go further than ever before to support the great British writing talent of the future,” said Brit List founder and director Alexandra Arlango. “We look forward to introducing this year’s writers to the team at Disney+ and to our returning partners, Audible and the Film Distributors Association.”

But when a violent incident interrupts a new friendship with an older woman, she uncovers a series of secrets that will shape a deadly future.
