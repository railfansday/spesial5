Dane Cook's Wife Kelsi Taylor Is Pregnant, Expecting First Baby

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Dane Cook and Kelsi Taylor—who tied the knot in 2023—shared that she is pregnant with their first baby: “We’ve been bursting with excitement to share this news!”

Dane Cook is preparing for his chapter as dad of the month.

The comedian and wife Kelsi Taylor shared they are expecting their first baby together.

Alongside black-and-white maternity photos, Cook, 54, and Taylor, 28, wrote in a joint Nov. 24 Instagram post that they’ve “been bursting with excitement to share this news!”

They added in reference to a bible verse about an answered prayer for a baby, “1 Samuel 1:27.”

In the first snap, the Good Luck Chuck star and fitness inductor stood beneath a tree as they looked lovingly into each other’s eyes. Meanwhile, Taylor held up a sonagram of their baby as Cook laid a gentle hand on her wrist.

Showing off their playful side, another photo showed the couple cozied up on a couch, while a third picture captured a close-up of Taylor’s baby bump.

Reposting the carousel to her Instagram Stories, Taylor wrote, “So grateful for this beautiful chapter of our lives.”

And Cook would have to agree, sharing on his own Instagram Stories that he’s “feeling so incredible.”

The Employee of the Month alum and Taylor tied the knot in September 2023 at a private estate in O'ahu, Hawaii, after nearly six years of dating.

"Last night I married the girl of my dreams here in Hawaii," Cook shared the day after the wedding on X. "The greatest feeling in the world is knowing you've got the strongest person you could ever imagine by your side and giving them all of your love in return."

He continued, "The years of laughs, adventures and accomplishments we've shared were solidified in front of some of our dearest friends and family during an emotional ceremony. I can't wait to share more but for now just know this. I've never felt this way. It's so wonderful."

It’s also wonderful that the couple can poke fun at their 27-year age gap. In fact, the My Best Friend’s Girl actor included a few wisecracks about their relationship in his 2022 stand-up special Dane Cook: Above It All.

"She saw everything that I was working on for the show," Cook exclusively told E! News at the special’s premiere. "And she was like, ‘Keep that in. I think that part will be great.' There's even some fun at our own expense."

As for why he wanted to draw inspiration from their age difference? "If we can't laugh at it,” he said, "then nobody else is allowed."

And that wasn’t going to fly with Cook. After all, he hopes to keep laughing at every twist and turn his life takes.

"I think it'll change it in the way that I would always hope growing up with a generation of comedy fans,” he explained. “I was a college kid talking to college-age students. And to grow up with a generation of comedy fans, their kids are now coming to my show. I'm family oriented and to be looking at that with Kelsey and our future and family. It all feels like the right timing."
