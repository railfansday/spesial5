Patten ends Grand Slam-winning doubles pairing to partner fellow Brit Skupski

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Henry Patten and Harri Heliovaara were crowned the best men's doubles team of the year at last week's ATP Finals after winning Wimbledon for a second time in July; the pair have won three Grand Slam doubles titles together but will go their separate ways in 2027

Henry Patten has ended his world No 1 doubles partnership with Harri Heliovaara in shock fashion in favour of teaming up with British compatriot Neal Skupski for 2027.

Patten and Heliovaara celebrated being crowned the best men's doubles team of the year at last week's ATP Finals after they claimed a second Wimbledon success together back in July.

However, in a post on Heliovaara's blog, the Finnish player revealed Patten phoned him on Friday to say their three-time Grand Slam-winning partnership was over - despite a previous agreement they would continue.

"After the trip to Turin, I didn't even have time to get into the holiday mood when the weekend took a rather unexpected turn," Heliovaara said on his own blog website. "Henry completely unexpectedly announced that he had changed his mind about next year.

"I picked up the kids from daycare on Friday afternoon and we were just opening presents when I got a call from Henry. Without going into too much detail, the message was clear.

"He didn't want to play together again next year, even though we had agreed to do so during the fall. The announcement came as completely as it could come and I couldn't help but listen with my mouth open in horror and say that I don't agree at all.

"I'm unlikely to ever fully understand what and why this ultimately happened.

"The emotional scale in my head was quite violent at first, a shocking situation in any case.

"The two-and-a-half years with Henry have been by far the best time of my career, for which I am eternally grateful to him, but now the main feeling was heavy disappointment and even anger."

Heliovaara confirmed in his blog that he would team up with another British player Julian Cash, while Patten is set to partner Skupski in 2027.
