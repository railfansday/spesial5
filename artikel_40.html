Live: At least 14 feared trapped after Russian strike in Kyiv region, Zelensky says

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said at least 14 people were missing after a Russian drone struck a five-story residential building in Dymer, in the Kyiv region, injuring two others.
