Photographer Federico Veronesi: 'I've been in love with African wildlife since I was three'

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The winner of this year's Wildlife Photographer of the Year award has told FRANCE 24 that his winning picture reveals a lot about the complexity of an elephant's soul and feelings. At first glance the image seems to show a mother elephant playing with her calf, but in fact it is a female carrying another elephant's dead calf. Photographer Federico Veronesi told us how he fell in love in Kenya as a six-year-old child and ended up moving there to fulfil his photography dream. He spoke to us in Perspective.
