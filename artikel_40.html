Xi-Trump-Putin trilateral meeting: Trump's recognition of China and Russia as 'peers of the US'

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Genie Godula is pleased to welcome Olivia Cheung. The trilateral meeting between Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin at the APEC summit was significant despite the absence of concrete agreements, according to Cheung. “The significance is seen in the precedence of the United States seeing and giving recognition that China and Russia are peers of the United States,” she explained, adding that “the image of a G3 is very significant.”
