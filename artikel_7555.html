TikTok’s next microdrama is an 81-episode musical series from Barlow & Bear

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Four and a half years after winning a Grammy for The Unofficial Bridgerton Musical, the songwriting duo known as Barlow & Bear has something new in store for TikTok viewers.

Abigail Barlow and Emily Bear are two of the creators behind To Die For Love, an 81-episode musical microdrama co-produced by TikTok and Ampersand. According to TikTok, the series follows “an ambitious half-witch vying for her coven’s crown. When she falls for a mortal, their romance takes an unexpected turn, forcing an impossible choice between family and the love of her life.”

Barlow & Bear created an original score for To Die For Love, while Barlow co-wrote the script alongside Oscar-nominated writer/director, actor, and opera singer Julia Aks.

Barlow also stars in the series opposite Broadway vet Derek Klena. Executive producers include Ampersand CEO Agnes Chu, Benj Pasek, Justin Paul, Danielle Perelman, Chris Crema, Matthew Ko, and Kevin Weaver.

“Making To Die For Love has been one of the most rewarding creative experiences of our careers,” Barlow and Bear said. “We love a pressure cooker, and building an entire musical in under five months proved that meaningful storytelling isn’t about time—it’s about the story and the people bringing it to life.”

This isn’t the first creator-driven microdrama TikTok has brought to life. Earlier this year, Issa Rae’s media company, HOORAE, struck a deal with the platform to produce short-form web series for both TikTok and its companion app PineDrama. The first vertical series to emerge from that partnership, Screen Time, had racked up over 620 million views on TikTok and PineDrama as of late October, making it both platforms’ most-viewed show to date.

TikTok doubled down on that success last month when it announced a new microseries-focused partnership with Made By Us Studios, which describes itself as a “first-of-its-kind, Hollywood-grade studio designed for the creator economy and powered by creator management company MADE BY ALL.”

Now, TikTok is forging ahead into the world of musical microdramas. To Die For Love will be available exclusively on TikTok and PineDrama, with all 81 episodes premiering December 21. The original soundtrack will be released by Atlantic Records.
