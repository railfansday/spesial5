Former Epstein lawyer publicly censured over antisemitic slurs, aggression at courthouse protest

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

An attorney who displayed physical aggression and directed antisemitic language at pro-Palestine demonstrators outside a Manhattan, New York City, courthouse is being publicly censured, a New York appeals court ruled Thursday. (Image from Shutterstock)

An attorney who displayed physical aggression and directed antisemitic language at pro-Palestine demonstrators outside a Manhattan, New York City, courthouse is being publicly censured, a New York appeals court ruled Thursday.

Criminal defense attorney Marc A. Fernich, whose high-profile clients included multimillionaire financier Jeffrey Epstein and drug kingpin Joaquin “El Chapo” Guzman Loera, did not try and justify his conduct displayed against protesters outside the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York in 2025.

The Appellate Division’s First Judicial Department of the New York Supreme Court approved a joint motion for a public censure because of the fact that it was an isolated incident, noting that Fernich’s actions warranted public discipline, according to coverage on Law.com.

The five-judge panel considered Fernich’s remorse for his actions, his disciplinary record and other health-related factors. The appeals court’s opinion did mention cellphone recordings of the courthouse confrontation in which Fernich grabbed demonstrators and made references to the Holocaust.

“[Fernich] is thankful for the Appellate Division’s compassion and looks forward to putting this episode behind him,” Michael S. Ross, Fernich’s lawyer, told Law.com.
