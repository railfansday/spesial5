Fabrizio Romano confirms Liverpool 'delighted', currently in talks

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Fabrizio Romano has confirmed that a ‘delighted' Liverpool are currently holding discussions. Things could change from here.

Liverpool saw off Club Brugge on Wednesday evening to get their UEFA Champions League campaign back on track. The Reds were comfortable with their win, too - 3-0, two goals from Alexander Isak and another from Florian Wirtz.

It’s impressive and it comes on the back of a 4-0 win over Manchester United. It’s the best-possible return from the international break and suddenly this Liverpool side is looking special.

Special enough to justify the enormous spend on it? We’d need to see a lot more consistency from the side before saying that but Liverpool do appear to be on that path. That’s fantastic to see, given how negative things felt across last season and in the summer.

This squad isn’t even complete, either, with some wild imbalances across it. The fact that the Reds are on an upward turn is something to celebrate, then.

Delighted with Andoni IraolaFabrizio Romano reports that Liverpool are ‘delighted’ with Andoni Iraola and his start to life as their head coach. Iraola was something of a gamble in the summer - a coach who hasn’t operated at the absolute highest level before and with no history of winning things. But the club gave him the keys and Iraola is currently proving the decision correct. Fabrizio Romano reports that Liverpool are ‘delighted’ with Andoni Iraola and his start to life as their head coach. Iraola was something of a gamble in the summer - a coach who hasn’t operated at the absolute highest level before and with no history of winning things.

But the club gave him the keys and Iraola is currently proving the decision correct. Iraola is, per Romano, also delighted with how things are going and the connections with the players are said to be very good.

That’s all led to Iraola being in the mix as Liverpool plan out their future. That’s a major discussion for the club after Julian Ward returned to the sporting director role in the summer, replacing Richard Hughes - the man who actually hired Iraola.

Liverpool are in talks with Iraola over what to do next, then. We imagine that means grabbing a couple of players who fit his way of doing things - essentially looking for gaps in the squad and filling them in ways the Spaniard needs.

That’s not something they did back in the transfer window, for instance. Liverpool instead signed who they signed and asked Iraola to make something of it. We suspect there will be more of a cohesive plan this time around.
