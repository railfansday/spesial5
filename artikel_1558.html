Liverpool lining up Ajax teenager for January

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Liverpool transfer news from Goal says Ajax teenager Abdellah Ouazane is a January target as attacking cover for Florian Wirtz. Julian Ward, Liverpool’s recruitment chief, has reportedly sped up plans to support the £116m playmaker, and Ouazane has become a serious option before the winter window opens. Wael Mohya and Gilberto Mora sit on the same shortlist.

The 17-year-old grew up supporting Liverpool and badly wants to play at Anfield. Ajax hold the leverage, though: his contract runs to 2028, and persuading them to sell a prized academy graduate mid-season will take careful negotiation. De Telegraaf also reported earlier this season that sporting director Jordi Cruyff wants to give him a new deal when he turns 18 on 15 January.

Ouazane is mainly an attacking midfielder who also plays wide or up front. FotMob‘s 2026/27 numbers show two goals and one assist in 12 Eredivisie games, only four of them starts, across 441 minutes. His goals and shot attempts rank in the 88th and 86th percentiles among comparable attackers.

The gaps are just as clear: he sits in the 19th percentile for defensive contributions and the 15th for aerial duels won, and a 45.7% dribble success rate suggests he still loses the ball too often against senior opponents. Real Madrid pulled out of a 2025 deal after his medical, though Ajax’s own checks cleared him.

Iraola’s Liverpool press harder and play more direct than they did under Arne Slot, so that defensive profile matters. Wirtz works as the central creator with Bradley Barcola, Victor Munoz and Rio Ngumoha out wide, which leaves the number 10 role as Ouazane’s only route in.

Yes, but as a planned investment rather than genuine cover. Wirtz missed several matches with a back problem last February, so depth behind him is a fair concern. Yet Liverpool sit fourth, a point behind second-placed Brighton after Sunday’s 4-0 win over Manchester United, and only eighth in the Champions League table after losing to Fenerbahce. January minutes will decide those races, and Ouazane has four Eredivisie starts and numbers that clash with Iraola’s pressing.

His lifelong Anfield dream and Ward’s record of targeting elite teenagers give Liverpool an edge, so they should push. If Ajax demand a premium for a mid-season exit, Ward should wait for the summer, when Liverpool can judge a full Eredivisie campaign. Four starts make him a bet on 2028, not insurance for February.
