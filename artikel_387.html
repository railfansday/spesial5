Samsungs S90H OLED TV just dropped to its lowest price of the year ahead of Black Friday

The S90H is Samsung's mid-range OLED from its 2026 lineup — just a step down from our favorite OLED TV of the year (the S95H) and a step above the entry-level S85H. While most TV upgrades from one year to the next are generally pretty minimal, the S90H was a decent jump from its predecessor, as Ty Pendlebury from Mashable's sister site CNET points out. "The real reason to buy the S90H over the previous S90F is the addition of the antireflective coating," he writes.

The anti-reflective coating helps eliminate those pesky glares and reflections while watching TV in aggressive lighting. It also bumped up the brightness from the previous generation, improved lag times, and upgraded the refresh rate to 165Hz.

If you don't feel like waiting around for Black Friday, you can rest assured you're getting the best price to date on the Samsung 55-inch S90H OLED TV. Plus, if you upgrade now, you can start watching all of your favorite holiday movies in top-notch quality.
