Medtronic Is About to Report Its First Earnings Since Spinning Off MiniMed. Should You Buy the Stock Before Nov. 24?

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The medical device giant is expected to post another quarter of stabilizing sales.

10 stocks we like better than Medtronic ›

Medtronic (MDT -0.67%), one of the world's largest medical device makers, will post its next earnings report on Nov. 24. It will mark its first earnings report after it spun off its diabetes unit, MiniMed (MMED -1.54%), and fully removed its business from its balance sheet.

In fiscal 2023 and fiscal 2024 (which ended in April 2024), Medtronic's earnings declined as it struggled with supply chain constraints, inflation, forex headwinds, regulatory challenges for its diabetes business, and lumpy post-pandemic recovery in elective procedures. Tougher competition, quality control issues, and new GLP-1 drugs exacerbated that pressure.

But in fiscal 2025 and fiscal 2026, its earnings grew again as it stabilized its costs, expanded its higher-growth cardiovascular and neuroscience businesses, and spun off MiniMed. In fiscal 2026, its revenue grew 5.8% organically, marking its strongest top-line growth in a decade. For fiscal 2027, it expects its revenue to rise another 7.25%-7.75%.

For the third quarter of fiscal 2027, analysts expect Medtronic's revenue to rise 6% year over year but for its adjusted EPS to dip 2%. Its cardiovascular and neuroscience businesses should remain strong. Still, the completion of its MiniMed spin-off (which concluded on Oct. 15), its reinvestment in higher-growth platforms, early-stage manufacturing costs for newer cardiovascular devices, and a few recent acquisitions are squeezing its margins.

Medtronic's stock looks cheap at 17 times forward earnings and pays a forward yield of 2.9%. However, I'd wait to see how well it grows organically -- and how it's handling the margin pressure -- before buying the stock and assuming it's a safe blue chip dividend play.
