Webull: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Acquiring Pi Securities gave Webull a foothold in Thailand, a move that tests whether its global platform can scale beyond U.S. retail.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 21, 2026 at 5:27PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 19, 2026, Webull (BULL -9.19%) announced record total revenue of $219 million, up 39% year over year. Client assets reached $32.4 billion during the period. Expanding operating margins show how higher user activity builds long-term earnings power. Three things management said stand out.

The relaxation of the pattern day trader, or PDT, rule in June removed day-trading limits on accounts under $25,000. Webull capitalized on this shift as options and equities trading from smaller accounts surged without dampening overall user retention.

Webull completed its acquisition of Pi Securities, a brokerage operating in Thailand, to accelerate its international expansion. The transaction added $3 billion in customer assets while expanding access to high-balance accounts in Thailand.

Management launched crypto perpetual futures (perps), which are derivative contracts allowing traders to speculate on asset prices without an expiration date. Adding these products diversifies revenue away from traditional equity trading and expands options for active traders.

Trading momentum continued through October across active retail accounts, and Webull is capturing market share in U.S. options. It relies on its global technology platform, multi-market compliance infrastructure, and product innovation to drive customer growth while maintaining expense discipline.

With international expansion and new asset classes scaling, leadership points to significant long-term runway across global markets.
