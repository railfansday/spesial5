All The Songs In Disney’s ‘Hexed’: Queen, Dua Lipa & More

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Disney’s Hexed tells the story of a family trio of witches, disconnected from themselves, each other and their powers.

Billie Doe (voiced by Hailee Steinfeld), a rebellious teen in Toledo, Ohio who does anything to get out from under her controlling mother’s (Rashida Jones) thumb, discovers a hidden world full of witches when her long dormant magical powers awaken and transfigure a portal that takes her to Hexe, where her estranged grandmother (Jodie Foster) is queen and her mother was a princess. Adventures ensue with the help of Beef Rogers (Walton Goggins) as Billie uncovers her true family history.

Original music composed by Kris Bowers (Bridgerton), is accompanied by several themed needle drops throughout the film. Each song fits emotional moments as well, from the parenting themes in the opening needle drop to the rebellion of the one that follows. Dua Lipa’s “Levitating” is a natural selection, as is Demi Lovato’s “Confident,” a string cover of which was used in Bridgerton Season 3 Part 2. Star Hailee Steinfeld also lends her vocals to a cover of a very appropriate song by The Cars, which plays during the film’s credits.
