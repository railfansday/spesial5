This two-year Norton VPN Standard plan costs $2.59 per month ($69.93 upfront for 27 months) and comes with an industry-leading 60-day money-back guarantee so you can test it out risk-free.

Norton VPN does a good job in pretty much every area that matters, but it’s hands down the best streaming VPN we’ve tested. In our latest tests, Norton VPN unblocked Netflix libraries in every location we tried, along with Disney+, Prime Video, BBC iPlayer, ITV, Channel 4, and TVNZ+. Its unblocking power beat big hitters like NordVPN, Proton VPN, and Surfshark.

There are also apps for big screens, including Apple TV, Google TV, Android TV, and Amazon Fire TV. Admittedly, it isn't as fast as Proton VPN or ExpressVPN, two of the fastest VPNs we've tested. But at over 1,000 Mbps in our tests, it's more than fast enough for seamless, lag-free 4K streaming and anything else you need it for.

Its Plus plan, at $2.99 per month, packs a lot under one roof and is actually better value than Proton VPN’s $2.49 plan, which has no extras like these, or ExpressVPN Basic at $2.79 per month, which includes ad blockers and a private AI assistant but no password manager or cloud backup.

Norton VPN isn't without its shortcomings, though. Its device limit counts installed devices rather than active connections, so once you hit the cap you'll need to remove a device before adding a new one. If you need unlimited simultaneous connections, go for Surfshark ($2.29 per month), or Private Internet Access ($1.49 a month) if you're on a tighter budget.

All in all, Norton VPN is excellent value for money, particularly if streaming is high on your list. Plus at $2.99 per month is the sweet spot, above all if you’re after an all-in-one internet security suite. You also get a 60-day money-back guarantee, which is double the industry standard, so you can try it risk-free.
