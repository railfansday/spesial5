McIlroy misses out on PGA Tour player of the year shortlist - who is in?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Wyndham Clark, Matt Fitzpatrick, Chris Gotterup, Scottie Scheffler and Cameron Young have been shortlisted for the PGA Tour's Player of the Year award; Scheffler looks to break Tiger Woods' record by winning the Jack Nicklaus Award for a fifth consecutive season

Rory McIlroy has missed out on being shortlisted for the PGA Tour's Player of the Year award, despite successfully defending his title at The Masters.

McIlroy became the first player since Tiger Woods to win at Augusta National in successive years, following his one-shot victory over Scottie Scheffler, with the world No 2 adding three further top-four finishes on the PGA Tour during the season.

The six-time major champion is a three-time winner of the Jack Nicklaus Award but a noticeable absentee on the five-man shortlist for the 2026 season, announced Tuesday after being finalised by the PGA Tour Player Directors and the Player Advisory Council (PAC).

Wyndham Clark, Matt Fitzpatrick, Chris Gotterup, Scottie Scheffler and Cameron Young were the five players nominated, with the Player of the Year award then decided by votes from PGA Tour members who have played at least 15 tournaments over the season.

Scheffler won three times on the PGA Tour this season, including The American Express and two of the three FedExCup Playoffs, with the world No 1 looking to break another Woods record by winning the award for a fifth consecutive year.

The 30-year-old added five runner-up finishes over the campaign and won the Byron Nelson Award for the low scoring average, with Scheffler a strong contender despite failing to add to his major tally in 2026.

Clark won the US Open for a second time and added two further victories during the campaign, lifting him inside the world's top five, while Fitzpatrick also enjoyed three wins in his best PGA Tour season to-date.

Gotterup also picked up three wins and contended in multiple other events, with Presidents Cup team-mate Cameron Young completing the list after winning The Players and the Cadillac Championship.

Voting will close December 4, with the winners of the award and the Arnold Palmer Award for the PGA Tour Rookie of the Year then announced before the start of the 2027 season.

Viktor Hovland won the Travelers Championship and finished runner-up at the season-ending Tour Championship, while major winners Aaron Rai and Ryan Fox were among the others to miss out on the shortlist.

English duo Alex Fitzpatrick and Marco Penge are among the five nominees for the Arnold Palmer Award for Rookie of the Year, having both enjoyed breakthrough wins on their maiden PGA Tour seasons.

Fitzpatrick partnered older brother Matt to victory at the Zurich Classic of New Orleans and was one of only two rookies to qualify for the Tour Championship, finishing fourth in the FedExCup rankings.

Penge retained his PGA Tour playing status in dramatic fashion at the RSM Classic, winning the final event of the FedExCup Fall in a play-off to break inside the top 100 in the season-long standings.

Norway's Kristoffer Reitan won the Truist Championship and rising star Jackson Koivun claimed the 3M Open in just his third professional start, while Ben James - a two-time runner-up in 2026 - complete the rookie quintet nominated.
