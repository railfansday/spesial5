Joseph retains place as England change nine for All Blacks and drop Pollock

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Steve Borthwick makes nine changes to England XV for visit of All Blacks in Nations Championship on Saturday (2.10pm); Fin Smith, George Furbank, Tommy Freeman, Seb Atkinson, Jamie George, Fin Baxter, Joe Heyes, Ollie Chessum, Ben Curry all return; Will Joseph keeps place on wing

Will Joseph has retained his place on the wing for England's Nations Championship Test against New Zealand on Saturday, with Fin Smith among those restored and Henry Pollock dropped to the bench.

Joseph, who missed out on a place in England's matchday 23 for their 45-22 defeat of Australia a fortnight ago, started in last week's crushing 78-15 success over Japan, scoring five tries.

The 24-year-old picked up just his second Test cap in that victory at Twickenham, and his first since July 2022 under Eddie Jones. He moves from right to left wing for the visit of the All Blacks.

Elsewhere fly-half Smith, full-back George Furbank, wing Tommy Freeman, centre Seb Atkinson, hooker Jamie George, props Fin Baxter and Joe Heyes and back-rowers Ollie Chessum and Ben Curry all return as part of nine changes by Steve Borthwick.

Pollock, who made just his second Test start for England in 15 caps last week, drops down to the replacements bench.

Outside-centre Henry Slade, scrum-half Jack van Poortvliet, lock and captain Maro Itoje, second-row Alex Coles - who shifts back alongside Itoje from blindside flanker - and No 8 Ben Earl keep their places.

Alongside Pollock, the replacements bench is made up of hooker Luke Cowan-Dickie, loosehead Ellis Genge, tighthead Will Stuart, lock George Martin, back-row Greg Fisilau, scrum-half Ben Spencer and fly-half/full-back Marcus Smith.

"There are few bigger occasions in international rugby than facing New Zealand," said Borthwick.

"It's a fixture that always carries a special significance, and one that the players are hugely looking forward to.

"We know the quality New Zealand have in their team. We've prepared for the very best version of them, knowing the challenge they will bring and the level we'll need to reach to get the result we want."
