Altimeter Capital is aiming to invest $5 billion in the Anthropic IPO

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Anthropic, valued at about $1.4 trillion in the offering, is expected to begin trading on Nasdaq on Thursday

Altimeter Capital plans to commit $5 billion to Anthropic's stock market debut, Brad Gerstner said Monday during an appearance on CNBC's "Halftime Report."

"I think this is a cornerstone compounder for a long time," Gerstner said. He said the amount would be five times the most he has put into an IPO. "It's where the trade is going."

Anthropic is expected to list on Nasdaq $NDAQ on Thursday. It would mark the first public listing by a leading AI lab, according to CNBC, coming before OpenAI, which has said it aims to go public next year.

The IPO price is expected to fall between $70 and $78 a share, with a valuation of about $1.4 trillion, according to CNBC. Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom $AVGO, Google $GOOGL, Micron $MU, Microsoft $MSFT, Nvidia $NVDA and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. are together committing $26 billion to buy shares at the offering’s set price, CNBC said. Many of those companies count Anthropic as a customer.

The offering follows a downturn in semiconductors and a swing against AI in Washington, according to CNBC. Gerstner said he remains optimistic.

"They have done such an excellent job presenting this company, they're in such an excellent position on the financials," Gerstner said, "that folks like me are making this a major order."

Nvidia announced last week that Jensen Huang had resigned as president, chief executive and board member, days before the planned offering. Colette Kress, the chief financial officer, is serving as interim CEO. Nvidia has committed roughly $10 billion to buy Anthropic stock in the $26 billion private placement, which is set to close alongside the IPO.

Anthropic plans to price the IPO on Wednesday and expects trading to begin Thursday on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol ANT. The IPO and private placement together would raise as much as $90.4 billion if the IPO prices at the top of the range. That would top the $86.2 billion SpaceX raised in its June listing. Goldman Sachs $GS and Morgan Stanley $MS are leading the IPO with 20 other banks.
