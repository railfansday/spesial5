The Champions League always manages to deliver.

Slavia Prague and the Champions League are a match made in... hell.

By suffering defeat to Arsenal tonight the Czech side have equalled a rather bad record.

They are now on a 23-game winless run in the Champions League (and European Cup). Both AEK Athens (1989-2006) and FCSB (2006-2013) share that record with Slavia Prague.
