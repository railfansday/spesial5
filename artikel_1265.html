Ex-Milan director on Cisse pursuit, Amorim ‘courage’ and 20-year-old talent

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Former Milan director Geoffrey Moncada reveals how long they had been tracking Alphadjo Cisse, convincing him to choose them rather than PSV Eindhoven, and Ruben Amorim’s ‘courage.’

The versatile figure just turned 20 last month and received his senior Italy debut in last night’s 4-1 Nations League defeat to Belgium.

He earned that opportunity thanks to his club form at San Siro, scoring eight goals in 13 appearances between Serie A and the Europa League so far this season.

“We had a scout in Verona and he told us about this young lad playing in their Under-17 team, and he was completely different to the usual profile you get in Italian youth academies,” Moncada told Calcio e Pepe podcast.

“He could play as a support striker, a winger, a mezz’ala in midfield, a Number 10. He seemed to change position in practically every match. His ability to change direction was remarkable, he could also cross with his left or right foot, it’s so hard to find players who can do all that.”

Verona loaned Cisse to Catanzaro in Serie B and his performances there prompted interest from other clubs, above all PSV Eindhoven, who were very close to an agreement.

“We’d been working on Cisse for several years, so Verona knew that well, but timing can be the most important thing in transfers. We had to rush, otherwise PSV Eindhoven would’ve got him, and if he had then played in the Champions League, the price tag would go up to €30-40m,” explained Moncada.

“It wasn’t easy, because we couldn’t promise him regular playing time, but fortunately we have the name AC Milan and that has a certain effect, especially for an Italian player. So PSV could promise he’d be a regular starter, but it’s not Milan, it’s not the same for his family, for the player, or his agent.

“As soon as he heard our name, he made his decision. He knew what he wanted.”

Nonetheless, few expected Cisse to make such an immediate impact at Milan, and that was thanks to coach Amorim deciding to bet on him from pre-season.

“When he was at Manchester United, Amorim had done something interesting with Patrick Dorgu,” continued Moncada.

“Initially, the player was used as a left-back, but then was used on the right wing in the Premier League. Whether it works or not, this is a coach with courage, he is not afraid of making decisions or changing positions.

“Considering Milan missed out on the Champions League last season, getting a player who was at Catanzaro and throwing him into the starting XI at Milan, that takes courage.”
