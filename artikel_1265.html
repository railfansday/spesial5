Gasperini: ‘A great moment for Roma to face the best’ ahead of PSG test

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Gian Piero Gasperini says Roma’s trip to Paris Saint-Germain is “a great moment” to test themselves against the best, and admits the Lisbon defeat with Atalanta still leaves a bitter taste.

With quotes via TuttoMercatoWeb, the Roma coach was speaking at his pre-match press conference at the Parc des Princes, alongside Paulo Dybala, ahead of Wednesday’s Champions League clash. Kick-off is at 21:00 CET [20:00 GMT].

“It’s definitely a very, very good moment for us to be able to play this game against the strongest team,” Gasperini said. “What we’re thinking about most at the moment is putting in a high-level performance.

“It’s clear we have to measure ourselves against this team. It’s the first time we’ve faced them with Roma, so we’ll have to understand straight away on the pitch what their level is. It’s certainly very high, and we need to see how much we can counter their technique and their speed. Our aim is to reduce their effectiveness a little so that we can also play our own game.”

Roma go to Paris with four points from four games after dramatic defeats to Real Madrid and Manchester United. PSG, the holders, have won all four of theirs.

Gasperini insisted those losses shouldn’t affect his side’s confidence.

“This is what the Champions League is like. It’s the most important competition in the world, with the highest level,” he said. “You can put in very good performances, like we did against Real and Manchester, but then there are key moments, and the best players in the world are on the pitch and can decide games.

“We have to focus on producing that type of performance, because it gives you confidence, makes you grow and carries over into the league.”

This is not Gasperini’s first big night against PSG. In the 2020 quarter-final in Lisbon, his Atalanta side were leading going into the final minutes, but lost 2-1 to two late goals.

“That’s a game that’s still a bit hard to swallow, because of the ending,” he admitted. “We were down to 10 men in the final minutes because of Freuler’s injury and had no substitutions left. PSG brought on Mbappé, and he was decisive. But it’s still one of the highest moments in Atalanta’s history.

“This is a different type of game, because PSG are different. They’re much more of a team now, and that’s thanks to Luis Enrique. Roma are coming into this competition after several years away, which is different for us. It’s a league-phase game, and for us it’s a measure of what we’re doing.”
