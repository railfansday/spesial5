Chiellini offers Yildiz update for Juventus: ‘It will still take some time’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Giorgio Chiellini has confirmed that Kenan Yildiz has recently begun ‘training with the ball’ but has also urged for patience, warning that it will ‘still take some time’ before the Turkey international returns to his best form.

Yildiz has been out of action since August and has only appeared once for Juventus so far this season, following an injury to his foot that he sustained during his first Serie A match of the season, a 1-0 victory away against Frosinone.

It was later confirmed that Yildiz had suffered a fracture to the fifth metatarsal in his left foot and that he required surgery to fix the issue.

Reports at the time indicated that it would take around two to three months for Yildiz to recover from the injury and subsequent surgery. Recent updates claim that he is targeting a comeback in December, although in the worst case, he might have to wait until the new year.

He has missed 20 matches for club and country since the injury occurred in August: 10 in Serie A, four in the Europa League and all six of Turkey’s Nations League group stage matches.

Juventus executive Chiellini has provided a brief update on Yildiz’s condition at a Fans’ Club event in Belgium on Wednesday.

“Kenan is recovering. He has resumed training with the ball, but it will still take some time,” Chiellini confirmed (via Gianluca Di Marzio). “He’s a player who can help us make the leap in quality.”

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
