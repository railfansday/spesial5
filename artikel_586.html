Daily Shows Desi Lydic reacts to Trump insulting CNNs Kaitlan Collins

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Donald Trump's sexist treatment of female journalists has not stopped, with the U.S. president once again insulting CNN's chief White House correspondent Kaitlan Collins during a press briefing.

Doing her job, the journalist asked Trump about Rep. Thomas Massie's resolution to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth. The President of the United States responded, "Massey's a loser, just like you're a loser at CNN, just like your ratings are a loser."

The Daily Show's Desi Lydic, who has previously called out the president's continued insults to female journalists, reacted to Trump's latest attack by mocking his response.

"Yeah, your ratings are a loser. Your shirt's a loser. Your hair's a loser. I'm a loser. Oh god, it's just hitting me. I just lost so bad," Lydic said during Wednesday night's show. "By the way, can we just give props to Kaitlan Collins for not even flinching during that tantrum?"
