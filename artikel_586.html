Justin Timberlake Rolls Back the Years on Silly, Sexy and Sincere Appearance on ‘Tonight Show’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The singer performed a medley of his hits, took part in some dumb sketches and bared his soul about why he agreed to take part in an upcoming Apple TV docuseries.

A game Justin Timberlake gave a welcome reminder of his varied talents on The Tonight Show on Thursday night, variously dressing up as a “sexy” turkey, doing a spirited Scottish accent and of course dancing and singing like only he can.

In what felt like a throwback to the innocence and goofiness of the Obama years, Timberlake was on top form, taking part in sketches, dance bits and sitting down for an interview to promote the 20th anniversary of his classic album FutureSex/LoveSounds and the four-part documentary series Timberlake that begins streaming on Apple TV on Dec. 11. With the Roots backing them up, Fallon opened the show with Timberlake for a song and dance number, with the duo going through The History of Party Songs. In a segment that is catnip for 90s kids, the tracks covered included Soul II Soul’s “Back to Life (However Do You Want Me),” Ini Kamoze’s “Here Comes the Hotstepper,” Mark Morrison’s “Return of the Mack,” Montell Jordan’s “This Is How We Do It,” Spice Girls “Wannabe,” Bell Biv DeVoe’s “Poison,” Timberlake’s own “Rock Your Body” and more.

In the interview section, Timberlake opened up about the making of FutureSex/LoveSounds, reminding the audience that that his influences on the album included “Bowie, David Byrne, Talking Heads, Freddie Mercury, Queen, Billy Idol, James Brown, Eurythmics and INXS.” He said that on the song “SexyBack” he was channeling Bowie doing “Rebel Rebel” and he had to fight the recording company on the production. “They fought me on it. Well, I brought it in and it was like, ‘It’s too fast.’ I was like, ‘That’s why it’s gonna work.’ … [And they said], ‘You don’t sound like you, your signature falsetto.’ I was like, ‘That’s why it’s gonna work.'”

On the infamous lyrics to “SexyBack”, Timberlake added, “That song does not make any sense. I’ve said this. It makes zero sense. But, it’s why it makes sense.”

In a particularly poignant part of the interview, Timberlake discussed his love for and influence of the late D’Angelo, describing him as “my guy.” “D’Angelo, for me, he changed everything. You know, he was one of those artists… rest in peace. What a brilliant, brilliant, brilliant artist. But I remember when he came out, it just like everything changed. [He] was a once-in-a-generation artist.”

On why he decided to take part in the Apple docuseries — which Fallon said tackled Timberlake’s career highpoints and personal low points — the Grammy-winner said that initially he “was really reluctant to do it” agreeing with Fallon that he was “sort of famously private.” His relationship with director Fisher Stevens softened his stance and that the doc he has “mustered up some courage to talk about some things that I’ve never talked about before, that, you know, things that even those that are closest to me don’t know.”

Timberlake said he found the doc experience “cathartic” and he hoped it would help others to share their troubles. “Everybody’s going through something. And I think I’ve gone through a lot in the last five years that I feel like have made me a better human and a better father, a better husband, a better friend.”

In the recent past, Timberlake has had a number of public incidents including being arrested for DWI in the Hamptons in June 2024 and revealing in July 2025 that he was diagnosed with Lyme disease. There was also the cheating rumors in 2019, and subsequent public ridicule, after the singer was caught holding hands with co-star Alisha Wainwright during the filming of Palmer. Timberlake had to issue a public apology about the Palmer incident on Instagram, calling it a “strong lapse in judgment.”

One sketch that’s likely to get a lot of play on social media was “Sexy Butterball Hotline,” where Fallon (Rocky Drumstick) and Timberlake (Pecker Spatchcock) played two very sexy turkeys — “the dirtiest birds in the coop” no less — manning an after dark Thanksgiving helpline. A sample of some of the lines from Rocky and Pecker in this bit: “Who says the turkey can’t be the one who does the stuffing?” “Is it Black Friday? ‘Cause my clothes are 100% off.” “I can’t wait till you lay me down on the table right in front of Grandma.” Good clean fun.

It wouldn’t be a complete Timberlake Tonight Show appearance without a “Camp Winnipesaukee” sketch. Fallon and Timberlake once more donned the retainers and wigs to relive their awkward teenage years and play the annoying campers trying to sing a popular song much to the annoyance of the camp counselor. On Thursday’s episode, the pair decided to take on The Proclaimers’ 1988 hit “I’m Gonna Be (500 Miles)” with Timberlake and Fallon’s attempt at Scottish brogue worthy of commendation but ultimately short of passable.

Proving he’s still one of the greatest singers and dancers around, Timberlake rounded his appearance with two medleys of his greatest hits. In the first clip he performed a medley of “FutureSex / LoveSound,” “SexyBack,” “Summer Love” and “My Love.”

In a second clip he performed “LoveStoned,” “Until the End of Time” and “What Goes Around…/ … Comes Around.”
