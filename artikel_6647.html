We Tried Starbucks’ All-New Holiday Drinks—Here’s What We’d Order Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

I don’t care whether your annual traditions call for the start of the Christmas season on November 1 or you’re a total Scrooge until after Thanksgiving, Starbucks’ holiday menu is officially here—which means it’s time to retire the Pumpkin Spice Lattes (for now) and embrace your holly-er, jollier alter ego.

Naturally, I had to get my hands on the new flavors—and reacquaint myself with the classics—in the name of journalism and the holiday spirit! From the dessert-inspired tiramisu drinks to the light and bright Refreshers (there are two, by the way!), I put Starbucks’ latest creations to the test. Here’s which ones I’d order again—and what I’d skip.

If you want your coffee to taste like a straight-up dessert, good news: I found the morning pick-me-up for you. Starbucks has created the ultimate holiday treat in this Tiramisu Velvet Latte. Inspired by the classic Italian treat, the new signature drink combines espresso, milk, and vanilla syrup with a fluffy Velvet Mascarpone Cold Foam and cocoa dusting that mimics the OG. It’s sweet without being overly sweet, and the cold foam adds a rich, creamy layer like the actual dessert. Whether you get it hot or iced, it’s a fun departure from your typical Peppermint Mocha order.

Cold brew purists, this one’s for you. Starbucks is also giving its classic Cold Brew a holiday makeover with the same formula as its latte: vanilla syrup, Velvet Mascarpone Cold Foam, and a dusting of cocoa and coffee. Unlike the latte, this format allows the coffee to do more of the talking, and the creamy mascarpone foam adds just a touch of dessert energy.

Now, there are actually two ways to try the refresher: the classic version, a.k.a. the Apple Pomegranate Refresher, with crisp apple flavors and popping pomegranate-flavored pearls; and the Snowy Apple Drink, which features the same ingredients with coconut milk for a creamier take.

The classic refresher is definitely brighter and fruitier, with a tart-sweet apple flavor and sugary pops of pomegranate, while the Snowy Apple Drink is more mellowed out thanks to the coconut milk. The result still captures the key flavors, but delivers them with a smoother, creamier finish.

You can customize the refreshers even more by adding a citrusy twist of lemonade or adjusting the caffeine levels—anywhere from no caffeine to light caffeine to full-blown Energy Refresher.

While I genuinely enjoyed trying all three new beverages, I probably wouldn’t order the Tiramisu Velvet Latte again—but that’s only because the Tiramisu Velvet Cold Brew exists. I’m always going to gravitate toward a more coffee-forward drink. And as for the Apple Pomegranate Refreshers, they’re a fun addition to the lineup—light, fruity, and the perfect pick-me-up for after a long day of Christmas shopping (especially if you add the caffeine boost).
