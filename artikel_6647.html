Thanksgiving Dinner For Less Than $4 Per Person? Some Grocery Deals Make It Possible

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Have a delicious (and affordable!) holiday.

Thanksgiving means cooking all the cozy fall dishes imaginable, which can fill our stomachs but also put a dent in our wallets. Between homemade stuffing, mashed potatoes, and a classic roasted turkey, there's quite a spread to put on the table.

With just about everything getting more expensive, luckily, some grocery stores are offering Thanksgiving meal deals that can help cut costs while making your holiday cooking a whole lot easier.

But with so many options, how do you know which one gives you the most bang for your buck? I looked through the latest meal deals to find out where you can get the most food for the least money.

Target's Thanksgiving meal is arguably the best deal you'll find this holiday season. Here's everything you get in the kit, which the company says feeds six people for just $30:

Walmart has some of the best offerings, too. Its $35 Thanksgiving meal deal includes a 13.5-pound turkey and 25 additional items. The spread includes ingredients to prepare sides like green beans, mashed potatoes, and cranberry sauce, plus a pumpkin pie for dessert.

Keep in mind that while the price of the meal deal has dropped from $57 in 2024, the number of food offerings has decreased, too. Walmart says the meal serves 10 people, but we suggest planning more conservatively.

Aldi's take on Thanksgiving dinner is one of the best values for your money. It's the only company offering a hefty 16-pound turkey in its meal deal, which costs just $39 total. The package includes stuffing, sweet potatoes, dinner rolls, cranberries, peas, carrots, celery, pumpkin pie ingredients, milk, and whipping cream. Aldi says the meal will feed 10 people, bringing the cost to less than $4 per person.

The Thanksgiving meal from Sam's Club is pricier than its competitors’, but the $160 package is advertised to serve 16 people. If you’re planning a big holiday gathering, this might be your best bet. Along with a turkey, you’ll get a ham, mashed potatoes, turkey gravy, prepared green beans, mac and cheese, sweet corn with garlic butter, cranberry crunch salad, yeast dinner rolls, a charcuterie entertainment tray, and two 12-inch pumpkin pies.

Costco's Fortune Gourmet Thanksgiving Turkey Dinner serves eight people and costs $199.99 with a $70 manufacturer's coupon. It's available for pre-order and includes 5-pound all-natural, ABF, free-range, Amish-raised turkey breast, three pounds of mashed potatoes, three pounds of mac and cheese, three pounds of green bean casserole, three pounds of sweet corn, three pounds of stuffing, and gourmet dinner rolls. It even includes dessert: Apple Cobbler and Pumpkin Pie.

Lidl's 10-person $34.40 meal deal—which comes out to just $3.44 a person—has ingredients to make turkey, stuffing, gravy, mashed potatoes, sweet potato casserole, green bean casserole, macaroni and cheese, rolls, and pumpkin pie.
