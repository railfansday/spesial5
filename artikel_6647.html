Turkey Cheese Ball

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The turkey may be the main event of Thanksgiving dinner, but it can be a snack, too! Whether you're hosting Thanksgiving for your community or celebrating with family, this fun appetizer is the perfect way to get everyone excited for the big turkey dinner with another smaller turkey! This cheese ball is not only the most eye-catching appetizer you can add to your holiday menu, but it’s surprisingly easy to create.

In a food processor, pulse the cream cheese, goat cheese, butter, and garlic powder until the mixture is smooth. Add parsley, chives, and thyme and pulse until well combined. Then season with salt and black pepper.

Transfer the cream cheese mixture to a piece of plastic wrap and gather up the wrap to form a ball. Then transfer the mixture into a large bowl and refrigerate until it’s firm, which should take at least three hours and up to one day.

In a clean food processor, pulse 3/4 cup pecan halves until they are finely chopped.

Roll the cheese ball in nuts and arrange it all on a platter.

Start by cutting a large triangle from a red bell pepper—this will be the face. Next, cut a smaller triangle from a yellow bell pepper for the beak. For the eyes, slice two small rounds from a small carrot. Take a larger carrot and slice it into diagonal ovals to make the feet, then carve little triangles at one end to look like toes. Finally, thinly slice the rest of the carrots into matchsticks for the tail.

Grab some toothpicks and stick the carrot eyes onto the red triangle face. Then, use another toothpick to add the yellow beak to the face—just make sure all the toothpicks point toward the back so you can attach everything to the cheese ball later. Now, press the face onto the front of the cheese ball, and slide the carrot feet into the base.

Insert matchstick carrots, pretzel sticks, and mini pretzels into the back of the ball to create the tail. Using the remaining 1/2 cup pecan halves, create wings by overlapping them along each side of the ball. Serve with crackers alongside!

Store leftover cheese ball in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. If possible, remove the pretzels and vegetable decorations before storing, as they can soften over time.
