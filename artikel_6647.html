The Best Holiday Gifts That Are Actually on Tweens’ and Teens’ Wish Lists

Can’t keep up with the trends? We’ve done the work for you.

We know what’s trending within our own circles, but keeping up with what’s cool among the younger crowd? That can be quite the challenge. And with the holiday gifting season officially upon us, finding perfect presents for the teens and tweens on your list can feel surprisingly daunting. Luckily, I’ve done some serious sleuthing to uncover exactly what’s topping their wish lists this year, from the latest must-have Ugg styles and coveted Lululemon finds to beauty essentials from cult-favorite brands like Summer Fridays and Rhode.

Whether she’s a fashion lover, just starting to experiment with makeup and nails, or looking to give her bedroom a refresh, there’s something here to suit every interest and budget. I’ve taken the guesswork out of it all, so you won’t have to spend hours deciphering which colorways are coveted or which trends have already had their moment. And while the latest viral finds are sure to earn you some major points, there’s also room for practical presents she’ll put to good use throughout the school year, like a stylish backpack she’ll actually be excited to carry or an insulated water bottle in a pretty print.

Ahead, discover the best gifts for tween and teen girls that they’ll be thrilled to unwrap this holiday season.
