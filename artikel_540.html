Crunchyroll drops 6-month subscriptions by up to 60% for Black Friday, plus get free anime collectibles

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

TL;DR: Crunchyroll is offering up to 60% off six months of streaming for new and eligible returning members — dropping the Fan plan to $3.99/month (down from $9.99/month), Mega Fan to $5.99/month (down from $13.99/month), and Ultimate Fan to $8.99/month (down from $17.99/month).

Anime has never been bigger, and if you have been meaning to catch up on fall simulcasts or plow through a backlog of classics, Crunchyroll just kicked off a rare Black Friday sale that makes signing up a whole lot cheaper.

Unlike streaming promos that only discount the cheapest base tier, Crunchyroll has slashed U.S. prices across all three of its membership levels — Fan, Mega Fan, and Ultimate Fan.

Normally $9.99 per month, the entry-level Fan tier drops to $3.99 per month for your first six months — saving you $6 every month, or $36 total.

It covers all the essentials if you are only watching for yourself: ad-free streaming across Crunchyroll's anime library (note that plans may still include brief trailers or promotional messages before playback), new episodes shortly after they air in Japan, HD downloads for offline viewing, and streaming on 1 device at a time.

If you share an account or want extra gaming and shopping perks, Mega Fan drops from its usual $13.99 per month down to $5.99 per month for six months — saving you $8 a month, or $48 over the promo period.

Mega Fan includes everything in the Fan tier, bumps simultaneous streaming up to 4 devices at once, and unlocks access to Crunchyroll Game Vault (a library of 100+ mobile games with no ads or in-app purchases) besides access to exclusive Crunchyroll Store drops and free U.S. shipping on orders of $50 or more.

Crunchyroll's top-tier Ultimate Fan plan is also cut in half for six months, dropping from $17.99 per month to $8.99 per month — saving you $9 each month, or $54 total.

Along with everything in Mega Fan, Ultimate Fan lets you stream on up to 6 devices at the same time, unlocks Crunchyroll Manga access in select countries (with ad-free reading on iOS, Android, and crunchyroll.com/manga for series like The Apothecary Diaries, My Dress-Up Darling, and Re:ZERO -Starting Life in Another World-, which Mega Fan members can also add separately), gives you free shipping on all Crunchyroll Store orders with no minimum, grants early access to drops, and includes an exclusive swag bag after 12 consecutive months of membership.

Alongside the subscription discounts, Crunchyroll is running several U.S. holiday promotions through the Crunchyroll Store throughout November (most geared toward Mega Fan and Ultimate Fan members, though gift cards are open to everyone):

Discounted subscription gift cards (Nov. 19-30) — Available to both members and non-members if you want to gift a membership to someone else.

Home video savings (beginning Nov. 19) — Special markdowns on Blu-rays and home video releases (following Aniplex home video savings on Nov. 18).

Free Bleach Funko Pop (Nov. 19-24) — Included with Crunchyroll Store purchases over $100, while supplies last.

Free Frieren collectible (Nov. 25-30) — Included with Crunchyroll Store purchases over $100, while supplies last.
