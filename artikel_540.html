How Janelle James talked her way into GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

One of the best parts of driving around Vice City in Grand Theft Auto VI is seeing what's on the radio. On Cocoteo FM, Bad Bunny and RaiNao play everyone from Don Omar to Rosalía. Flip over to WKTT and there's Jason Sudeikis hosting The Richard Bastion Show. And on The Wipe, Anitta plays the host of an advice show called Ask Dr. Nita.

That fake radio channel is also where you'll hear very real comedian Janelle James, better known to Abbott Elementary fans as Principal Ava Coleman. In the GTA universe, she plays Tish, the host of She Got Assets, much to her fans — and her son's — surprise.

After speculation on social media ensued, James took to Instagram Stories to explain herself.

Hi guys. It’s very important to me that everybody knows that not only is that me, I’m not simply "voice acting." I pursued, conceptualized, pitched, scripted AND voice acted my radio show.

Rockstar didn’t "choose" me, I bullied my way to them. I've been playing this game since Vice City (was previously overcommitted to Metal Gear) and this is a literal dream come true!! 😭😭 I'm told the response is already great, TY. Hope to catch y'all in these online streets where I will promptly kick you square in your back then put 2 in your dome. (PS5) Cheers!!!

She also added how she got to bring two friends along, too: Comedian Zainab Johnson plays Kendra St. James, and comedian Liza Treyger plays Destiny Stallion. Fans are already sharing clips and rave reviews on her channel: "Janelle James is living her LIFE in GTA6!" wrote one user. "Janelle James has one of those voices you could listen to all day. Perfect for GTA!" wrote another.

In a follow up Instagram Story, she told her followers how her son found out while playing the game. "Oh yea i forgot to tell you," she said.
