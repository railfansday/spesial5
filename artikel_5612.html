Presley Gerber’s Hollywood Hills Home Hits the Market for $3.6 Million—Nearly 2 Months After His Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Presley Gerber’s Hollywood Hills home has hit the market for $3.59 million, less than two months after the model’s death at age 27.

The son of Cindy Crawford and Rande Gerber, who passed away on Sept. 20, snapped up the property for $3.4 million in March 2023.

Now, nearly two months after his tragic death, the property has hit the market for $3.6 million with real estate agent Cindy Ambuehl of Christie's AKG, as first reported by TMZ.

The dwelling is described as "beautifully reimagined mid-century architecture meets modern luxury in the heart of the Hollywood Hills."

The home features four bedrooms, three bathrooms, and sprawls across 3,000 square feet.

Built in 1955, the home sits behind gates in the Hollywood Hills. Canyon views frame the property, while the living spaces connect to outdoor areas through large sliding glass doors.

Inside, the main gathering space combines wood floors and a fireplace with access to the kitchen and dining areas.

The primary suite and guest bedrooms also open onto the pool and patio, allowing residents to step outside directly from their rooms.

The guest accommodation is attached to the house, according to the listing. It contains one bedroom and one bathroom, along with its own kitchen, laundry facilities, and private entrance.

That arrangement offers visitors a place to stay with access to everyday essentials beyond a bedroom and bathroom. The listing also presents the space as an option for extended stays or additional living quarters.

Outside, the pool shares the property’s roster of amenities with a Jacuzzi, cold plunge, and sauna. A dedicated gym area adds another fitness feature.

The sales description identifies updates to the roof, heating and air-conditioning system, floors, lighting, and sliding doors. Landscaping and outdoor lighting have also been improved.

Other advertised features include reverse osmosis water filtration and smart entry technology. Parking consists of a gated motor court and an oversized garage with epoxy flooring.

The property is marketed near Runyon Canyon, with access to Hollywood and West Hollywood.

"Offering true tranquility amid the surrounding hills, this architectural retreat is just moments from Runyon Canyon, the best of West Hollywood, Hollywood, and beyond," the listing adds.

Gerber died Sept. 20 while staying at a rehabilitation facility in Santa Monica. Police investigated his death as a suspected overdose, and the medical examiner initially deferred the cause of death while further investigation was underway.

The reported suspicion should not be confused with a confirmed medical finding. Us Weekly’s account cited a police statement indicating that there was no evidence of foul play at that time.

Following his death, Crawford and Rande shared a touching tribute remembering their son’s kindness and vulnerability and thanking those who had offered support.

“Presley Walker Gerber. July 2, 1999-September 20, 2026. We are beyond heartbroken to lose our beloved son and brother. We feel so blessed to have had Presley in our lives for 27 years. He is a sweet, beautiful soul who loved and is loved, now and forever. We are so proud of how he showed up in the world, with kindness and vulnerability. We are grateful for the outpouring of love and support from so many who know and love our family and even strangers who feel our pain. Til we meet again our sweet boy," they shared in the joint statement.

His sister, model and actress Kaia Gerber, also shared an October tribute reflecting on her grief and their close relationship.

“Prez, I don’t have the words, and maybe the tiny mercy is that we didn’t need them all along. Words fail and words subtract and words summarize and words always lack truth. And how naive of me to believe that language could transcend grief. I’ve read all of the books and I have none of the answers. My articulation is no match against this, the magnitude of losing you. I am, quite literally, down on my f—ing knees," she penned.

Now publicly offered for sale, the Hollywood Hills property brings together a midcentury home, guest accommodation, and extensive outdoor amenities at an asking price just below $3.6 million.
