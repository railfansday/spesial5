King Crimson Original Drummer Michael Giles Dies at 84

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Michael Giles, whose drumming on King Crimson’s legendary debut album brought fire and abandon to an already wild and innovative sound, has died, according to a post on the band’s website. No cause of death was announced; he was 84.

“His extraordinary rhythmic imagination, musical sensitivity and highly inventive approach to drumming were fundamental to the band’s pioneering sound,” the announcement reads in part. “Michael’s originality, musical curiosity and uncompromising commitment to creative freedom challenged conventions and inspired generations of musicians around the world. Born in 1942, Giles hailed from Bournemouth on England’s south coast, along with Crimson cofounders Robert Fripp and Greg Lake. In 1967, he formed a group with his brother Peter and Fripp called, naturally enough, Giles, Giles and Fripp. The trio released an album titled “The Cheerful Insanity of Giles, Giles and Fripp” the following year, to extremely modest commercial results. However, the album laid the groundwork for King Crimson, which Giles formed late in 1968 with Fripp, vocalist/ bassist Lake, multi-instrumentalist Ian McDonald and lyricist Peter Sinfield.

Crimson’s early concerts electrified the British rock scene, to the extend that the band was invited to open for the Rolling Stones at its massive concert in London’s Hyde Park in July of 1969. The group’s innovative sound, as captured on its debut album, “In the Court of the Crimson King,” combined rock, jazz, classical and avant-garde music into a concoction that many consider to be the first true progressive-rock album; certainly nothing like it had been heard during the rock era. (A telling example can be found in this December 1969 live recording of the album’s title track, in which Fripp gives a four-count and an eruption of sound ensues.)

Its influence was profound, and can be heard everywhere from the music of fellow prog-rock travelers Yes and Genesis as well as David Bowie, Roxy Music and countless later acolytes like Tool and the Mars Volta; its song “21st Century Schizoid Man” was famously sampled by Kanye West on his 2010 single “Power.”

Giles’ wild drumming was key to the band’s first lineup, embellishing its classical-tinged sound with innovative, thunderous drum rolls and lightning-fast rhythms.

However, in a trend that would follow throughout Crimson’s long existence, this lineup was short-lived: By early 1970, McDonald, Lake and Giles had all left the band, although the latter two did contribute to the band’s second album, “In the Wake of Poseidon.”

Giles subsequently recorded an album with McDonald under the name McDonald and Giles (naturally enough), and over the years performed on recordings with an unusually diverse array of artists, including erstwhile bandmates Lake and Sinfield as well as Steve Winwood, Bryan Ferry, Leo Sayer, original Genesis guitarist Anthony Phillips, Kevin Ayers, Neil Sedaka, Yvonne Elliman, Andy Summers and the Penguin Cafe Orchestra.

He also released a solo album titled “Progress, Ghost Dance” with later Crimson member Jamie Muir and David Cunningham, and the Michael Giles MAD Band, which featured yet another Crimson alumnus, Keith Tippett.

He and several other alums united in 2002 to form the 21st Century Schizoid Band, which toured performing the band’s early repertoire.

He is survived by his five children, Tina, Amanda, William, Rosie and Oliver, several grandchildren, his sister Marion and brother Peter.
