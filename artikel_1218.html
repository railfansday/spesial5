PSG coach Enrique pinpoints Roma threat and praises Gasperini for ‘clear playing identity’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Luis Enrique has warned Paris Saint-Germain that Roma will cause them problems in the Champions League on Wednesday, and praised Gian Piero Gasperini for building teams with a “very clear playing identity”.

With quotes via PSG’s official website, the Spaniard was speaking at his pre-match press conference ahead of Wednesday’s league-phase clash at the Parc des Princes. Kick-off is at 21:00 CET [20:00 GMT].

The two sides are heading in very different directions in Europe. PSG sit top of the league phase with a perfect record of 12 points from four matches, having scored 20 goals and conceded just five, while Roma are 20th with four points.

“It’s clear we’re happy to be back in the Champions League, and above all at home,” Luis Enrique said. “This match will be very different from what we’ve experienced so far. They’re a team that defends in a particular way, but we can’t wait.

“They’re a team that loses very few matches. We know it will be very difficult, and it’ll be a great challenge. If we win this game, it’ll be the first time we’ve won five matches in a row in the league phase, so that would be a first!”

Luis Enrique had plenty of praise for Gasperini. The two crossed paths in 2011, when the Spaniard was in charge at Roma and Gasperini lasted only a few weeks at Inter.

“He’s a coach with a lot of experience, and I like him a lot. His teams have a very clear playing identity, very different from what we’re used to coming up against,” he said.

“I remember he was at Inter when I was coaching Roma. It was complicated for him there, but he proved afterwards what he was capable of. We know they’ll cause us problems tomorrow, and it’s a great challenge.”

The holders will be without two of their biggest names. Khvicha Kvaratskhelia suffered a torn anterior cruciate ligament in his right knee during PSG’s win over Barcelona last month, and Désiré Doué also faces a lengthy spell on the sidelines with a suspected ACL injury.

“That’s football at the highest level. Every team and every coach goes through it. There are injuries,” Luis Enrique said. “It’s very sad to have this type of injury for players like Désiré and Kvara, but we’re focusing on their recovery and how we can improve our performance.

“This team has shown all its resilience over the last three years. We’ve always bounced back from everything.”

He also ruled out signing a short-term replacement in January.

“We don’t have to sign anyone in the transfer window, and it’s not our style to sign a player for six months. We’re happy with our squad. Above all, we’re not afraid of anything. We’re confident in our ability and our resilience.”

The former Roma coach was also asked about his time in the Italian capital.

“My time at Roma was very special for me, and I have great memories. It’s a magnificent city, I experienced some lovely things there as a coach, and I’m at peace with everything that happened to me there.”
