Brittany Mahomes Reacts to Link Between Pregnancies and Chiefs’ Super Bowl Odds

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Patrick and Brittany Mahomes might have more than just a new addition to the family to celebrate next year if history serves them right.

The couple announced they are expecting their fourth baby earlier this week, which is good news for fans of the Kansas City Chiefs. Every season Brittany, 31, has been pregnant, the Chiefs have made the Super Bowl.

“Glad someone finally figured this out, just doing my part over here 😂😝😆,” Brittany joked via her Instagram Story on Thursday, November 12.

Brittany and Patrick’s daughter Sterling was born in February 2021, less than three weeks after the Chiefs lost to the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in Super Bowl LV.

Son Bronze was born in November 2022, just months before the Chiefs defeated the Philadelphia Eagles in Super Bowl LVII.

The couple welcomed daughter Golden in January 2025, weeks before the Eagles got revenge on the Chiefs in Super Bowl LIX.

Brittany and Patrick announced their latest pregnancy Tuesday, November 10, on social media.

“Another blessing from God 🥹🙏🏼 & then there were 6 🤍,” Brittany shared via Instagram.

Patrick, 31, was asked whether he expects the Super Bowl connection to continue during a press conference after the pregnancy news was announced.

“I’m hoping, I’m hoping,” he said with a laugh. “I don’t know if Brittany is, but I’m hoping we can keep that going and get another Super Bowl win with a baby on the way. That’d be awesome for sure.”

The Chiefs currently hold a 6-2 record, right behind the 7-2 Denver Broncos in the AFC West. NFL Next Gen Stats puts their odds of making the playoffs at 86% heading into their matchup with the Atlanta Falcons on Sunday, November 15.

Super Bowl berth or not, Patrick said he’s hoping he and son Bronze can “even the score” and the family will welcome another boy.

“It’s obviously a blessing, another baby on the way,” Patrick told reporters on Tuesday. “I’m excited for it … I’m just praying for a healthy baby at the end of the day.”

Friends and family members showered the couple with well-wishes after their big announcement.

“I cannot wait!! Congratulations love yall ❤️❤️❤️❤️,” Patrick’s mom, Randi, commented on the post.

Patrick’s brother, Jackson, wrote, “Woohoooo! Uncle jack cant wait.”

“Beyond thrilled for you both!!!” said Tavia Hunt, wife of Chiefs owner Clark Hunt. “What an incredible blessing! Congratulations!! My heart is just bursting with joy for your growing family!
