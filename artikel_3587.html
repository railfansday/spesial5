007 First Light Might Be The End Of The Line For James Bond’s Favorite Watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

After months of searching and numerous potential frontrunners, Robert Pattinson has been cast as the next James Bond. It's lead to some questions about whether he's too famous or too old for the role, whether or not he looks like Bond, and just how different his portrayal might be to those that came before him. For some others, however, the questions is far more direct: What watch is he going to wear?

James Bond has long had recognizable brand associations, but one that has endured has been the partnership with luxury watch maker Omega. Bond was first seen wearing an Omega in 1995's GoldenEye, and was mostly recently seen sporting the latest iteration of the Seamaster Diver 300m in this year's 007 First Light. Although not a new film in the series, First Light still featured its own unique take on the Seamaster, the first ever created for a videogame, with Omega adding it to its bursting 007 collection that it makes available for purchase.

So given that it's been three decades, why could First Light be the last iteration of James Bond wearing an Omega? Simply put, Robert Pattinson doesn't wear one, and has very recently become a brand ambassador for one of Omega's Swiss competitors, Jaeger-LeCoultre. There's no confirmation that Pattinson's obligations will directly influence a brand deal between Omega and new Bond owners Amazon, but the omission of any watch at all in the announcement trailer sure has cause some speculation.

Daniel Craig, during his tenure as Bond, was Omega's chief ambassador, often wearing unreleased models to drum up interest for imminent releases. That might not be the case with Pattinson, which could suggest a change for what timepiece his Bond decides to switch to.

If Bond were to jump from Omega to JLC, it would be somewhat fitting. The watch maker manufactures some extremely elegant pieces with a rich history associated with each line. But none makes as much sense as the rectangular Reverso. The watch is distinct in its design, allowing the wearer to rotate the face and flip it 180 degrees, with a variety of designs on the back. Initially created for polo players to protect their prized wristwatches, you can clearly see a lot of potential for some weird and creative Bond gadgetry to intersect with a design like this.

Or maybe Pattinson will arrive on the block with yet another Seamaster Diver 300m, and all of this would've been wishful thinking. But if he doesn't, and this truly is the last we'll see of Bond with Omega, at least First Light introduced one of the better entries in the entire collection.
