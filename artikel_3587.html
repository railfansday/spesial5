Miranda Lambert Makes Rare Appearance With Husband at 2026 CMA Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Date night in Music City! Miranda Lambert had the support of her husband, Brendan McLoughlin, while attending the 2026 Country Music Association Awards.

The couple skipped the red carpet at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena before the Wednesday, November 18, awards show but later posed together inside the venue during a break in the action. They coordinated dark colors as McLoughlin, 34, lovingly wrapped his arm around his wife, 43, for the cameras. (The couple first showed off their CMAs fits via Instagram on Tuesday, November 18.)

The 2026 CMAs were a huge night for Lambert as she performed twice, with Ella Langley to premiere their song “Snakeskin Boots” and she also teamed up with Kacey Musgraves for a live performance of their duet “Horses and Divorces,” which was also nominated in the Musical Event of the Year category. (The award was ultimately won by HARDY, Eric Church, Morgan Wallen and Tim McGraw’s collaborative track, “McArthur.”)

Lambert — the most successful female artist in CMAs history with 14 career awards — and the former NYPD officer live a largely private life. They most recently stepped out for the 2026 Academy of Country Music Awards at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas in May, though they also skipped the red carpet that night.

McLoughlin was shown on camera rising to his feet and cheering for his wife when Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas” — which Lambert co-wrote and produced — was announced as Single of the Year. (The track was also named Song of the Year at the 2026 ACMs.)

The couple tied the knot in Davidson County, Tennessee, on January 26, 2019, just over two months after they met on the set of Good Morning America in New York City in November 2018. (Lambert was previously married to fellow country music star Blake Shelton from 2011 to 2015.)

McLoughlin has since been credited as a co-songwriter on a few of Lambert’s songs, including “Dammit Randy” as their first collaboration in 2024.

“We were at our house in Austin, and Brendan was watching football and [label co-founder Jon Randall] got the guitar out,” she exclusively told Us in 2024 about writing “Dammit Randy” with her husband. “Brendan was listening and kept piping in here and there with some ideas, and we’re like, “Are we cowriting? You’re going to have to turn the game off.” So he did, and he had some of my favorite lines in the song. It’s his first cut ever on an album. I’m actually really proud of him, and he’s super excited about it.”

Lambert has credited McLoughlin’s brutal honesty as a crucial aspect of why their marriage has been successful.

“My husband tells me the truth. He gives me harsh reality, and I love it,” Lambert revealed on a 2024 episode of the Tamron Hall Show. “I like to say he calls me on my s***, which I love, because everybody needs somebody in their life like a truth-teller.”

The 2026 CMA Awards aired live from Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena on ABC and Disney+ Wednesday.
