Rex Ryan Accused of Having ‘Small Penis’ by Pedestrians He Allegedly Ran Over

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Former NFL coach Rex Ryan had his manhood challenged by a Nashville pedestrian he allegedly ran over with his truck.

“My wife and I both got ran over by some a**hole on f***ing Broadway,” a man said in a 911 call released by TMZ on Tuesday, November 17. “I want to turn him in.”

The man added, “He literally ran us over. Ran over my feet!”

When asked by the dispatcher to describe the vehicle in question, the man described it as a “big red pickup truck with oversized wheels that said ‘small penis.’”

The man insisted he and his wife didn’t need an ambulance, but said, “My toes are going to be a mess tomorrow.”

“We ran him down so I could get the license plate number,” the caller said, which he then relayed to the dispatcher.

The caller insisted about the driver, “He f***ing needs to be arrested.”

Ryan, 63, was arrested on November 11 after turning himself in to authorities at the Metropolitan Nashville Police Department.

He was charged with two counts of leaving the scene of an accident, two counts of failing to give information/render aid and two counts of failing to obey traffic signals.

Ryan was released after posting a $6,000 bond. He is due for a court hearing on December 18.

Michael Holland, the man allegedly struck by Ryan, claimed he and his wife were “almost killed” in the incident.

“If we had been six inches further out, I’d be in a hospital right now, and so would my wife,” Holland told the California Post in a story published on Friday, November 13.

Holland added, “He decided he wanted to turn and didn’t care, and just ran over both of my feet, my wife’s foot and got her leg too.”

When Holland and his wife tracked Ryan down, he claimed the former coach “got out and confronted us.”

“He was told in no uncertain terms what he had done,” Holland alleged. “Instead of staying at the scene or even saying, ‘Sorry,’ he just got back in his vehicle and took off.”

Holland said he was concerned that Ryan “was really going to hurt somebody.”

“I mean, just another inch or two, and his mirror or bumper would have taken off [my wife’s] knee,” he said. “His tire literally was so close to her that it scraped her leg and ran over her feet and both of my feet. You just can’t do that.”
