Ex-refs chief drops Dan Burn disallowed goal verdict as Newcastle footage re-analysed

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Dan Burn's goal for Newcastle against Fulham was rightly disallowed for Yoane Wissa's foul on Timothy Castagne.

That is according to former PGMOL chief and FIFA referee, Keith Hackett, who has exclusively told Football Insider that Rob Jones made the correct call to stop Newcastle opening the scoring in the first half.

Newcastle were held to a 1-1 draw at Fulham on Saturday as Rodrigo Muniz's flicked goal earned a point shortly after Jacob Murphy's stunning 80th-minute opener.

The visitors felt they should have been ahead in the 33rd minute as Burn struck from Murphy's dangerous cross.

Newcastle's captain had peeled away in celebration against his old club before Jones blew his whistle to signify a foul in the build-up spotted by an assistant referee.

Magpies fans flocked to social media following the controversial incident, but seemingly did not have any grounds to complain.

Burn was able to drift into the box and poke Murphy's fizzed cross past Bernd Leno at the near post just after the half hour mark.

He celebrated for around 10 seconds before it was clear that the goal was not set to stand, as Jones insinuated that an assistant had flagged for an offence in the box.

Replays showed that Wissa had tussled with Castagne and guided him closer to the goal line so he would play Burn onside from the cross.

Former referees chief Hackett has insisted that, despite Newcastle players and fans' protests, the DR Congo striker did enough to warrant being penalised for a foul on his opponent.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, he said: "Yes, the number nine has fouled his opponent, therefore, it was correct to rule the goal out for that foul."

Magpies manager Jaissle has admitted that he was surprised at the call and did not feel Wissa did enough for it to be ruled out.

Speaking after the game, he said: "I learned that word here, a 'soft' decision. In my eyes, it was not right. Last week we had the situation where Fabian Schar was bleeding in the face (before Everton scored). That is difficult for me to understand."
