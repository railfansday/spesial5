Chaos appears to be following one West Ham want away as upheaval continues

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

There’s been another twist in the chaotic career of one of West Ham’s standout signings of the Tim Steidten era.

It would be fair to say that Jean Clair Todibo was largely unknown to Hammers fans when Tim Stediten whisked the former Barca defender to east London in his private jet for a VIP welcome in the summer of 2024.

Sadly its would also be fair to say that the defender has been part of a chaotic two and a half years during which he’s seen three West Ham managers – and fallen out with all of them – and declared himself unwilling to play for the club again.

Todibo – dressing room chaos never seems far away

Chaos appears to have followed Todibo to France when West Ham finally – gratefully – found a loan destination for their unhappy defender in the summer as Todibo was sent to Lens owing to his desire (apparently) to work with manager Dino Toppmoller.

No sooner had the wayward West Ham defender landed than Toppmoller was fired – and his successor Yannick Cahuzac fared little better, lasting just two months.

Todibo is surely the only player in the Lens dressing room who will have played under six managers in side three years, as his new head coach is revealed as Didier Digard.

Scott Parker can’t have missed the fact that Todibo is due to return to east London at the end of the season – putting the defender on course to test Parker’s own managerial credentials when his loan ends next May. And few West Ham fans will welcome him back after his turbulent and truculent career in Claret and Blue.
