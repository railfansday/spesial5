Tale of two Towns: Battle and Rye impress in East Sussex League

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It was another entertaining weekend, with plenty of goals, dramatic encounters and some outstanding individual performances across all four divisions.

Battle Town continued their excellent run of form with a convincing 4-1 home victory over SC Pass & Move Arrows. The sides were level at the interval, but Battle took control in the second half to secure another important three points.

Rye Town also maintained their winning momentum, although they were made to work hard for a narrow 1-0 victory over Sandhurst. Ben Reader scored the decisive goal to ensure Rye collected maximum points.

Ninfield were involved in one of the afternoon's most entertaining encounters as they edged past The Junior Club Tackleway 4-3. Joseph Trigwell was the star of the show, completing a superb hat-trick with goals in the 37th, 56th and 81st minutes. Ed Taylor had opened the scoring in the 29th minute, with Ninfield eventually doing enough to claim all three points.

Victoria Baptists also enjoyed a welcome victory, defeating Sidley United 2-1. Harrison Smith was on target for the visitors, but Baptists secured the points in a closely contested encounter.

Little Common II continued their impressive goalscoring form with a 5-2 away victory over Pevensey & Westham. Igor Wydra led the way with an excellent hat-trick, while Cyrus Abbott and Jacob McArthur also found the net to complete another productive afternoon for Common.

Bexhill Amateur Athletic Club II secured a hard-fought 2-1 away victory over Robertsbridge United, with Dexter Bawn scoring both goals for the visitors. Danny Turner replied for Robertsbridge in the 85th minute, but Bexhill held firm to take the points.

There was no shortage of entertainment at St Leonards Social, where the hosts emerged victorious from an extraordinary ten-goal encounter against Herstmonceux. Social eventually ran out 6-4 winners, with Allan McMinigal and Rhys Brockhurst both scoring twice. Anotidaishe Tanaka and Matt Syrett were also on target as the home side claimed a memorable victory.

Parkfield produced one of the standout performances of the weekend, recording a remarkable 8-0 away victory over Sedlescombe Rangers. Jamie Salvidge and Jack Lugg both completed superb hat-tricks, while Neale Blackham and Bobby Bowles added a goal apiece to complete the rout. The result continued Parkfield's outstanding recent goalscoring form.

Northiam 75 also enjoyed a successful afternoon, defeating Battle Town II 3-1. Ricky Rogers, Brandon Smith and Tyler Wilson were the goalscorers for Northiam, while Alex Southall replied for the visitors. SC Pass & Move Arrows II secured a narrow 1-0 away victory over Ticehurst. The sides were level at half-time before Beni Isaac struck the decisive goal to earn Arrows all three points.

Division Three produced plenty of excitement, with Herstmonceux II edging an extraordinary nine-goal thriller against Hollington United III. The sides were remarkably level at 4-4 at half-time, but Herstmonceux found the only goal of the second period to secure a dramatic 5-4 away victory.

Eduart Zotaj scored twice for the visitors, with Max Attrell, Charlie Mackay and Fenton Webster also finding the net. For Hollington, Rhys Smith scored twice, while Marley Streeter and Michael Woodcock were also on target in a remarkable opening 45 minutes.

Hawkhurst United and Icklesham Casuals shared six goals in an entertaining 3-3 draw. Alfie Hillman scored twice for Icklesham, with Callum Hadaway also on target as the visitors returned home with a point.

Hollington Hawks secured a 4-2 home victory over Robertsbridge United II, thanks largely to an outstanding hat-trick from Chris Sanders. James Winchester added the fourth goal to complete a fine afternoon for the hosts.

Little Common III continued their positive run of results with a narrow 1-0 home victory over Northiam 75 II. Jay Abib scored the only goal of the game to secure another valuable three points for Common.

Bexhill Amateur Athletic Club produced an emphatic performance in the Sussex Intermediate Challenge Cup, defeating Westfield Reserves 6-0. Leo Oliver and Simon Walton both scored twice, while Jayden Douglin and Daniel Goergen also found the net as Bexhill comfortably secured their place in the next round.

There was plenty of excitement in the Hastings FA Junior Cup, where Ninfield II claimed an impressive 4-3 away victory over Mountfield United in a seven-goal thriller. The Junior Club Tackleway II also progressed, securing a 3-1 home victory over Catsfield.
