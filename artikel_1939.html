Doncaster Rovers boss Grant McCann on when football talk is off the menu with son Bayley

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Rovers' worrying recent form may have provided food for thought for both, given their working relationship at the club as manager and player.

But from a family perspective, time together as father and son – away from a work environment - is also beneficial and important for both.

The Rovers boss, whose side makes the trip to Yorkshire rivals Bradford City on Saturday, said: "We don't really (talk about football). We just either go for something to eat or go out to the cinema or something like that and we just think about everything but (football) really.

"Just life and how he's getting on and all that sort of stuff.

"On most Thursday evenings, I'll take him out for a bit of food and just spend that little bit of time with him because I don't get to see him even though he's here every day.

"I don't get to spend that sort of time with him like I do with my two youngest boys.

"So we always go out and (usually) have something to eat. He's still hasn't paid yet!"

McCann's son (pictured) has made the breakthrough at first-team level this term at Rovers, having joined on a two-and-a-half year deal from South Yorkshire rivals Barnsley in the winter window.

The young midfielder, who turns 21 early next month, made his Rovers debut from the bench in the home game against Stevenage in late April.

He followed up with another substitute appearance at Peterborough United, a club both father and son know well, on the final day of the 2025-26 campaign.

McCann has kicked on this term, with his development recognised by an international call-up to Northern Ireland Under-21 side. He netted in the fixture against Malta in late September.

On the club front, McCann has made 13 appearances in all competitions for Rovers and scored his maiden goal for the club in the League One game at Luton Town on October 27.

According to reports, Glasgow Rangers recently sent scouts to monitor the form of McCann.

For McCann senior, it's very much business during working hours with his son, which is just the way that both want it.

He continued: "I want to be his manager when I'm in here and when we're away from here, I want to be his dad.

"That's just the relationship that we have and I think he prefers it like that.

"It's not like he gets any sort of special treatment or anything or I let him away (with anything).

"If anything, I'll probably speak to him less because I don't want to put any sort of pressure on him.

"I want him to just do his own thing. We still have phone calls now and again.

"He phoned me (recently) telling me his wi-fi was playing up at home, so me and my wife were trying to sort that out for him and it's things like that."
