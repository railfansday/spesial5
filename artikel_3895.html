Cyberpunk 2 Development Really Ramping Up as Team Targets Big 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

2027 will likely be a pivotal year for Cyberpunk 2 as it rockets towards full production, with CD Projekt Red anticipating significant expansion to the sequel's development team.

Since the summer, the number of people working on Cyberpunk 2 has increased from 184 to 199, which doesn't seem like much, but this is a steady process.

By the end of next year, management expects there to be a total of around 320 devs — a team size that would be comparable to the 500-strong studio that's currently preparing The Witcher 4 for its 2028 release.

In other words, we can probably assume Cyberpunk 2 will leave early production in the relatively near future — and that's a big deal, because it means that the bones of the project are basically locked in place.

CDPR's Karolina Gnaś, who maintains relationships with the company's investors, reiterates that "good progress" is being made with Cyberpunk 2 — with further comments suggesting that there have been no internal delays.

The thing is, we're probably not going to see the Cyberpunk 2077 successor until The Witcher 4 is out, which seems a rather daunting prospect.

But it's worth remembering that CDPR is now essentially split between two major studios — one in Poland (The Witcher 4) and one in the US (Cyberpunk 2). The company's grown massively since The Witcher 3 first launched over a decade ago.
