Poll: Are You Hyped for November's 10 New PS Plus Games?

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

I don't want to influence your opinion right out of the gate here, but November's PS Plus Extra lineup will surely go down as one of the service's strongest offerings in recent memory.

It's got a bit of everything, but it's also packed with highly rated titles. Death Stranding 2: On the Beach was a Game of the Year contender just 12 months back — a madcap but heartfelt sequel to Kojima Productions' impressively unique 'strand-type' debut.

And while Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii is far from being peak Yakuza, it's still a damn fun and endearingly stupid spin-off. Well worth checking out if you're looking for something a little less serious, without sacrificing the dramatic storytelling that the franchise is known for.

Elsewhere, Atomfall is a bit like if Fallout was set in rural England, and Dragon Quest 3 HD-2D Remake is old school JRPG bliss brought into the modern era.

We've even got two completely different shooters up for grabs: Trepang2 is an unhinged run-and-gunner with horror elements, and Hell Let Loose is a grounded World War 2 reenactment for up to 100 players.

You could probably argue that PS Plus Premium's having an uncharacteristically decent month as well. Soul Reaver 2 is an absolutely lore-soaked sequel to the beloved PS1 action adventure, and Freedom Wars Remastered gives one of the Vita's best action RPGs a welcome second chance.
