All songs in Zelda Ocarina of Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Songs allow you to change the time of day, call your horse, literally teleport, and much more in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake. They're essential to your progress, and they're just great melodies to listen to. However, all the songs are not available to you at the start of the game. Instead, you'll need to progress in the story, and scour all of Hyrule to unlock them all.

Here's all the songs in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake.

[Ed. note: This guide contains spoilers for The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake, so continue at your own risk! Feel free to follow along or bookmark this page and come back later.]

There are 13 songs in Ocarina of Time. Below, we list all the songs, their inputs, how to unlock them, and what they're used for.

[Ed. note: If you're viewing this guide on a mobile device, you may have to swipe left and right to see all the information on the tables below.]

Talk to Zelda in Hyrule Castle for the first time.

Talk to Malon at Lon Lon Ranch after you wake up Talon and he returns home.

Call Epona to your side (as an adult) or milk a cow.

Defeat the two ghosts at the east end of the Graveyard, and make it to the end of the crypt.

Make your way through the Lost Woods and the Sacred Grove, and talk to Saria.

After you collect all the Spiritual Stones, return to Hyrule Castle.

Play in front of time cubes to move their position.

Talk to the Music Man inside the windmill in Kakariko Village as an adult.

Make it to the entrance of the Forest Temple, and Sheik will teach you Minuet of the Forest.

Teleport to the entrance of the Forest Temple.

Return to the Temple of Time after completing a temple, and Sheik will teach you Prelude of Light.

Teach the scarecrows a song at Lake Hylia as young Link. Then, return to the scarecrows as adult Link, and play them the same song. Whatever inputs you use will become the Scarecrow Song.

Go to the Fire Temple, and Sheik will teach you Bolero of Fire.

Teleport to the entrance of the Fire Temple.

Collect the Iron Boots in the Ice Cavern, and Sheik will teach you Serenade of Water.

Teleport to the entrance of the Water Temple.

After you complete three temples, you'll see that Kakariko Village is on fire. Head there, and Sheik will teach you Nocturne of Shadow.

Teleport to the entrance of the Shadow Temple.

Go to the Spirit Temple, and Sheik will teach you Requiem of Spirit.

Teleport to the entrance of the Spirit Temple.
