Enjoy Christmas In Style With A Festive Night Out At Goodison Park

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Evertonians can enjoy a Christmas night out to remember at Goodison Park next month when Everton Women host Arsenal in the Barclays Women’s Super League.

Gather family, friends or colleagues on Friday 11 December (KO 7pm) and celebrate the festive season while taking in one of the most anticipated fixtures of the campaign from the best seats in the house.

There are a range of premium matchday experiences available that will allow you to enjoy the game in style, whether you're looking to treat yourself and loved ones, thank clients, or just find a good excuse to get together.

Our premium lounges and boxes, with matchday hospitality starting from just £30 per person, and with a festive menu offering available, offer the perfect way to elevate your matchday.

From exclusive lounge access and premium seating to opportunities to go behind-the-scenes, each experience has been designed to make your visit to Goodison Park even more memorable.

Be a part of matchday with Everton Women’s exclusive VIP experience. Enjoy a private box, as above, for up to eight guests, and experience the buzz of the home dressing room as you and your guests lay out the players’ kits ahead of the game. Then, after the game, hear from the key players as you watch their live interviews. An unforgettable, one-of-a-kind Everton matchday experience.

The Leasowe Pacific Lounge provides the perfect balance of comfort and atmosphere while giving guests the chance to rub shoulders with former players. Relax in an exclusive lounge setting before kick-off, where you’ll meet a former player from Everton Women’s past. Enjoy our festive menu offering and enjoy access to a private bar. Upgrade to bottomless drinks for just £25 and enjoy the action from premium padded seats.

Relax in the 1878 Lounge and enjoy a complimentary drink on us. Offering a paid bar, enhanced facilities, and live entertainment on select matchdays, the GA+ ticket is the perfect way to enjoy a more fun and comfortable matchday experience.

To book, and learn more about the experiences listed above, CLICK HERE, or use the enquiry form to secure your seats.
