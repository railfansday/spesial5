Everton Women Unwrap New Partnership With Nestlé Confectionery

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Everton Women has announced a new multi-year partnership with Nestlé Confectionery, bringing one of the world's most recognisable food and beverage companies into the Club's growing portfolio of commercial partners.

The agreement will see Nestlé Confectionery become the 'Official Chocolate and Grab Bag Sweet Partner of Everton Women' and an 'Official Supplier of Hill Dickinson Stadium', with household favourites including KitKat, Smarties, Rowntree's, Aero, Lion, Yorkie, Milkybar and Quality Street set to feature across both venues.

At Goodison Park, Nestlé Confectionery will have a prominent presence through pitchside LED advertising, big-screen exposure and matchday activations designed to engage supporters, alongside opportunities to showcase its products at food kiosks and points of sale.

Supporters and visitors to Hill Dickinson Stadium will also be able to enjoy Nestlé Confectionery's popular range of products, extending the brand's presence across both of Everton's iconic stadiums.

The agreement represents Everton Women's second dedicated commercial partnership, following last year's deal with GGM Gastro, with further Everton Women's-focused partnerships set to be announced.

The growing interest from major businesses reflects the opportunities being created around Everton Women, with the team's move to Goodison Park offering prospective partners a unique combination of footballing heritage, an iconic stadium and access to new audiences as the women's game continues to develop.

Andrew Middleton, President of Business Operations at Everton, said:  “We are delighted to welcome Nestlé Confectionery as a partner of Everton Women and an Official Supplier of Hill Dickinson Stadium.

“Like Everton, Nestlé’s brands have been part of family life for generations, making this a natural fit for our supporters and matchday experience. Together, we will create engaging moments across both venues and bring these iconic products closer to our audiences.

“This partnership is another important step in Everton Women’s continued growth, and we look forward to working with Nestlé to bring it to life.”

Gail Stove, OOH Confectionery Lead for Nestlé UK & Ireland, said: "Nestlé Confectionery is excited to be an Official Partner of Everton Women and Official Supplier of Hill Dickinson Stadium.

"We can’t wait to share our beloved brands with visitors at these brilliant venues.

“Working collaboratively with Everton Football Club, we’ll bring the No.1 confectionery single within the leisure channel, KitKat® 4 Finger, along with other iconic Nestlé Confectionery brands for everyone to enjoy.”

Further details of Nestlé Confectionery's planned matchday activations and opportunities for Everton supporters to engage with its brands will be announced in the coming months.
