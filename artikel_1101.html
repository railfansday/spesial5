How Leeds built the longest unbeaten home run in the Premier League

The Elland Road factor is no myth. Leeds are on the longest unbeaten home run of any top-flight side and since Farke took over only Arsenal, Man City and Liverpool have more points per game at home.

At Elland Road where it feels a significant game for both teams.

Two of Tottenham’s recent three wins have come at home to Coventry and 10-man Palace. Win here and the revival starts to look real.

Leeds are nearly always competitive but are without a league victory since beating Newcastle 4-1 here on September 14. Victory would perhaps pave the way for the club to announce a new deal for Daniel Farke.
