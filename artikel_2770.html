Is Ireland v Australia on TV? Channel, kick-off time and how to watch Nations Championship clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ireland and Australia battle for fifth place on the inaugural Nations Championship finals weekend at Twickenham.

The two sides finished third in their respective pools despite successes last weekend, with Ireland impressive in beating South Africa and the Wallabies securing their first win of November over Wales.

The two sides last met in this competition in July, with a late, potentially match-winning penalty from Ben Donaldson sliding by in a thrilling finish.

What more thrills might two rotated sides have in store under the Friday night lights?

Ireland vs Australia is due to kick off at 8.10pm GMT on Friday 27 November at Twickenham's Allianz Stadium.

Viewers in the United Kingdom can watch the match live on ITV4, with coverage from 7.15pm GMT. A live stream will be available via ITVX.

With captain Dan Sheehan suspended, Tadhg Beirne skippers the Ireland side and Nick Timoney comes in at No 8. Jacob Stockdale is given a chance to impress on the wing, while Ulster pair James McNabney and Nathan Doak are brought onto the bench.

Ireland XV: 1 Andrew Porter, 2 Ronan Kelleher, 3 Tadhg Furlong; 4 Joe McCarthy, 5 Tadhg Beirne (capt.); 6 Ryan Baird, 7 Josh van der Flier, 8 Nick Timoney; 9Jamison Gibson-Park, 10 Sam Prendergast; 11 Jacob Stockdale, 12 Stuart McCloskey, 13 Robbie Henshaw, 14 Robert Baloucoune; 15 Hugo Keenan.

Replacements: 16 Tom Stewart, 17 Tom O'Toole, 18 Thomas Clarkson, 19 James Ryan, 20 Nick Timoney; 21 Nathan Doak, 22 Ciaran Frawley, 23 Bundee Aki.

Australia hand a debut to former Northampton wing James Ramm, while Allan Alaalatoa wins his 100th cap at tighthead. He will lead the side with regular captain Harry Wilson on the bench.

Replacement scrum half Teddy Wilson is another potential debutant on the bench, while veteran Kurtley Beale is primed for a first apperance in five years.

Australia XV: 1 Isaac Kailea, 2 Brandon Paenga-Amosa, 3 Allan Alaalatoa (co-capt.); 4 Josh Canham, 5 Jeremy Williams; 6 Tom Hooper, Carlo Tizzano, 8 Rob Valetini; 9 Kalani Thomas, 10 Carter Gordon; 11 James Ramm, 12 Len Ikitau, 13 Joseph-Aukuso Suaalii, 14 Max Jorgensen; 15 Tom Wright.
