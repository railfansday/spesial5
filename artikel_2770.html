Carlos Alcaraz to stay in the ATP Finals after scan results are returned

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

After much deliberation, it has now been revealed that Carlos Alcaraz will not withdraw from the ATP Finals.

The tennis world will rejoice at such a revelation.

Having cruised through his opening two matches against Frances Tiafoe and Daniil Medvedev, it was during the latter showdown that fears started to build regarding his fitness.

After all, despite there being seemingly no on-court issues, he was forced to get brief treatment from the physio before seeing out the win.

Afterwards, the Spaniard revealed he had suffered a little discomfort in his hamstring, and it was revealed that Alcaraz would undergo an MRI scan to see the extent of the damage.

It has felt like forever since that news broke, and at last we have a positive update.

That is what is being reported by Spanish outlet El Partidazo de COPE, which revealed that the 23-year-old will not withdraw from the ATP Finals.

There had been fears he might have been forced to do so, due to a strange ATP Finals rule that was forcing him to play tomorrow.

Not to mention that Alcaraz had cancelled his practice session for today as well.

After all, his upcoming match with Flavio Cobolli is the quintessential dead rubber encounter, between a player already knocked out and another already qualified as the top seed.

Fortunately, all the medical tests have come back positively, reaffirming his intention to stay in the tournament and fight for the title.

Having lost in last year's final, Carlos Alcaraz will be desperate to bounce back and claim one of the few major events that he has yet to win in his career.

After all, if he were to win this year's ATP Finals, he would only really have the Canadian Open and Shanghai Masters left to effectively complete tennis.

It would be unwise to bet against him achieving this feat soon as well, with the seven-time major champion seemingly only getting better and better.

This trend is actually quantified in his steadily improving results at this end-of-year showcase.

During his debut at the ATP Finals in 2024, Alcaraz lost in the semi-finals to Novak Djokovic.

Then, the following year, he suffered a disappointing group stage exit, with Alexander Zverev and Casper Ruud both qualifying ahead of him.

Last year saw him defeated by his great rival Jannik Sinner, who is crucially not present at this year's event.

Alcaraz will arguably not get a better chance than this to stroll to the title; it would be so cruel for fitness to stop him from achieving his ultimate goal.
