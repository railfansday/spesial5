2026 Week 11 rooting guide for Cowboys fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Outside of Dallas dropping their game to the 49ers, a lot went right for the Cowboys in Week 10. We need that to keep going for the rest of the season, both for the NFC East contention and our admittedly razor-thin hopes of a Plan B in the wild card race.

A Cowboys upset over San Francisco sure would've been nice given how other games unfolded. The Giants getting upset by the Commanders and Green Bay getting crushed at home by Minnesota were not expected. These results helped keep a division title and the seventh seed in range, one of which is required for Dallas to still get into the playoffs.

Granted, that NFC East crown is far more reachable than a wild card spot. But a lot can happen over the next eight weeks, especially as some of these NFC North and West teams start beating each other up in divisional games. A lane could still open for the Cowboys, even if we're not good enough to use it.

The next two weeks are critical for Dallas, getting two gifts from the scheduling gods in the Titans and Eagles. At a combined 3-15, they're arguably the worst teams in each conference. If the Cowboys don't come out of this stretch at 6-6, then talking about anything but the next offseason becomes quite silly.

Aside from avoiding supreme embarrassment against Tennessee, what else do we need for the Cowboys from Week 11?

Obviously, we need another loss for the Giants. And while we appreciate Washington helping us out there last week, we don't need them going on some late run and becoming another problem in the division.

Philadelphia is different. They seem truly done for this season, so now it's more about not wanting them to get a top-tier draft pick. And this week, it'd be especially nice if they could upset Pittsburgh, who owes Dallas their fourth-round pick next April.

It'd be great for Dallas if one team was clearly running away with the NFC North, but this feels like it's going to be a tight battle through Week 18. As such, we need as many losses as possible for those teams so they stop monopolizing the wild card spots.

Green Bay is on their bye week, so no help there. But as long as this thing stays tight, there's a good chance that someone falls out of the playoff scene.

Of course, for Dallas to benefit, they'll also need to get past the Rams and Seahawks. Both of them are also on a bye, so we'll see if the Cowboys can inch closer this week and then take a fresh look at playoff implications for Thanksgiving weekend.

If the Cowboys do win the NFC East, it'd be nice to be the third seed. We don't really care who wins the NFC South, so long as they have a worse record than us. Carolina is the current leader, so that's an easy pick.

The Arizona-KC game is mostly meaningless. The Cardinals' head-to-head win over Dallas shouldn't matter by Week 18. At this point, more teams filling up the bottom of the standings, and potentially keeping the Eagles or Commanders away from the top picks, is something to root for.
