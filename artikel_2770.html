The numbers behind the Wizards’ three game win streak

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Yesterday, the Washington Wizards defeated the Orlando Magic and improved to a 7-11 record. They have also won three straight games after an eight game losing streak.

I'm no Kevin Broom, who wrote his analysis from last night. And Greg Finberg reported from Capital One Arena about Washington having their "players-only meeting" and stepping up defensively in the last week. But let's take a look at the numbers from Washington's last three games against the rest of the league:

4th in Defensive Rating: The Wizards have a defensive rating of 102.8 in their last three games which is fourth in the NBA among all teams' last three games.

4th in Rebounding Percentage: This is at 53.9 percent. When a team grabs more rebounds than their opponents, it increases the odds of victory.

2nd in Defensive Rebounding Percentage: 74.2 percent. That's all I need to say.

12th in Effective Field Goal Percentage: The Wizards do not have one of the NBA's top offenses. But when the defense is among the league's best and the offense is at least average, those teams should be winning most of the time.

Anthony Davis and AJ Dybantsa are both averaging double doubles: Davis averaging 27.7 points and 10.7 rebounds a game. Dybantsa is averaging 19.7 points, 10 rebounds and 4.7 assists per game.

Trae Young is in an overall shooting slump: He is averaging 8 assists per game but is also shooting just 28.1 percent from the field. That said, he shot 42.1 percent from three (and nearly all of his made shots were threes).

So far, the Wizards are starting to right the ship, but it's early. Also, it's just the beginning when it comes to dissecting the stats. Let us know your thoughts in the comments below.
