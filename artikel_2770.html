Dalton Kincaid a limited participant

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Buffalo Bills TE Dalton Kincaid (chest) was limited during practice Thursday, Nov. 5.

Why It Matters: Kincaid is coming off one of his better games of the season last week and should be available for Week 9. He should see a handful of targets during the game and can be a No. 2 tight end in fantasy leagues.
