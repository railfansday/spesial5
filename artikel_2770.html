Giants list J.J. McCarthy as limited on Monday's practice report

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Giants didn't hold a practice on Monday, but they say quarterback J.J. McCarthy would have been able to participate if they weren't coming off of a game on Sunday.

McCarthy was listed as a limited participant due to the hip injury that briefly forced him out of Sunday's 19-11 win over the Eagles. Head coach John Harbaugh said at a press conference earlier in the day that the team believes McCarthy will be well enough to play against the Commanders on Thursday night.

Linebacker Abdul Carter (knee, back), linebacker Malik Harrison (groin), wide receiver Charlie Jones (thumb), wide receiver Darnell Mooney (chest), and running back Cam Skattebo (hamstring) were also listed as limited. Harbaugh said he expects Skattebo to play after he missed the Eagles game.

Right tackle Jermaine Eluemunor (calf), tight end Theo Johnson (neck), wide receiver Malik Nabers (knee), and left tackle Andrew Thomas (groin) were listed as full participants.
