Game Discussion Thread: Knicks vs. Bulls, November 4, 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Knicks (3–4) host the Chicago Bulls (1–6) tonight at Madison Square Garden. A win would bring New York .500 and give them two wins over Chicago in eight days.
