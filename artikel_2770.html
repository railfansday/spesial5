Dylan Harper ruled out against Clippers with “soreness”, more to come tomorrow

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Spurs had mostly avoided the injury bug that has buring through the league, until now. With injury report due for Thursday's match-up against the Clippers, the Spurs listed guard Dylan Harper as out due to "left hip soreness", but they will not have more information until tomorrow, according to San Antonio Express News' Tom Orsborn.

Harper suffered the injury in the final minute of last night's loss to the Pistons when he was running up the court and slipped, falling awkwardly and appearing to pull his groin. He stayed down for a bit before limping back to the locker room.

The Spurs will release more information when they hear from the medical staff tomorrow, and the amount of time Harper could potentially miss will depend on the severity of the injury. (It's rarely useful to listen to online doctors who are basing their opinions of the same videos we see, but most I've seen have ranged anywhere from 2-6 weeks, for whatever that's worth.)

He's joined by Tobias Harris (calf) and Jayden Quiantance (knee). Head coach Mitch Johnson remains noncommittal on a potential return timeline for Harris, and Quaintance told reporters at a turkey handout earlier in the day that he does not have a timeline either, but the goal is to get back to 100% and not rush things.

It's easy to assume that Julian Champagnie will once again return to the starting lime-up, which would give them the same line-up that made the Finals last season. The question of course will be back-up point guard. De'Aaron Fox and Stephon Castle may get their minutes more staggered, and Jordan McLaughlin could get some regular minutes. Another option would be to call Ja'Kobi Gillespie up from Austin.
