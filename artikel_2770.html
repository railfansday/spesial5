Rockets (11-3) rout Wizards as Steven Adams makes return from injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Steven Adams returned from an 11-game absence with a right calf strain during Houston's 147-119 victory (box score) over the Washington Wizards on Sunday night.

The Rockets (11-3) have now won four straight games and remain at No. 3 in the Western Conference standings (one game back of No. 1). In a clear mismatch, the Wizards (4-10) have lost seven in a row.

As part of a "double big" configuration, Adams started next to Alperen Sengun and contributed 6 points and 7 rebounds in 12 minutes. Houston went +12 as a team in that time.

"It's gonna take some time to get his wind back," head coach Ime Udoka said postgame. "But those four-minute stints, even in those, you can see what he does for us."

With a swarming defense, the Rockets held Wizards guard Trae Young scoreless in a game (0-of-5 shooting) for the first time in his NBA career.

For Houston, individual standouts included:

Amen Thompson: 26 points, 7 assists, 7 rebounds; 9-of-11 shooting (81.8%), 3-of-3 on 3-pointers (!!!)

Kevin Durant: 23 points, 5 rebounds, 4 assists; 8-of-13 shooting (61.5%), 2-of-3 on 3-pointers (66.7%), 7-of-8 on free throws (87.5%)

Alperen Sengun: 19 points, 8 rebounds, 8 assists; 5-of-8 shooting (62.5%), 8-of-9 on free throws (88.9%)

Tari Eason: 25 points, 7 rebounds; 10-of-14 shooting (71.4%), 3-of-5 on 3-pointers (60.0%)

Veteran guards Fred VanVleet (maintenance on night one of a back-to-back) and Marcus Smart (three-game suspension) were both sidelined for the Rockets in Sunday's game.

VanVleet is expected to return to the starting lineup (replacing Adams) when Houston's back-to-back concludes on Monday at Miami. Tipoff versus the Heat (7-6) is at 6:30 p.m. Central, and the game will be televised and streamed via Space City Home Network (SCHN) and NBA League Pass.
