Aidan Hutchinson: That's the last time I go airborne

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Lions head coach Dan Campbell said he isn't going to tell players to stop celebrating big plays because of defensive end Aidan Hutchinson's torn ACL, but Hutchinson is going to be making some changes on his own.

In a post to his Instagram account, Hutchinson made his first public comments since suffering his injury last Sunday. Hutchinson leaped in the air after sacking Patriots quarterback Drake Maye and his knee went out upon landing.

Hutchinson wrote that he has "jumped hundreds of times on a football field and never in a million years did I think this would happen to me" and that it will "never make sense" why things played out the way that they did. He added that he does know he will come back stronger from the injury and "that's the last time I go airborne."

Hutchinson closed the post with a message asking his Lions teammates to "hold down the fort til I'm back." That won't be until 2027, but Hutchinson has shown once already that he can make a full recovery from this kind of injury so there is some light at the end of the tunnel in Detroit.
