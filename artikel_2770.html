Hardik Pandya-Arshdeep Singh trade: What do MI and PBKS stand to gain from the deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Hardik Pandya-Arshdeep Singh trade: What do MI and PBKS stand to gain from the deal originally appeared on Cricket News. Add Cricket News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

Hardik Pandya is set to join Punjab Kings in exchange for Arshdeep Singh, with the trade reportedly completed.

Mumbai Indians, who finished ninth in IPL 2026 with four wins from 14 matches, gain a left-arm new-ball and death-overs specialist to pair with Jasprit Bumrah.

Punjab Kings gain a fast-bowling all-rounder under Shreyas Iyer but take on Hardik's injury risk and lose their all-time leading wicket-taker.

After months of trade rumours and uncertainties, Hardik Pandya's home ahead of the 2027 IPL might be decided in the most shocking way possible. According to multiple reports, the Mumbai Indians and the Punjab Kings have agreed on one of the biggest trades in IPL history, with Hardik Pandya moving to Punjab and Arshdeep Singh going the other way ahead of IPL 2027.

An IPL source has confirmed that the trade has been completed and that Hardik is pleased to play under Shreyas Iyer, who continues as Punjab's captain. Hardik's three-year stint as Mumbai captain has been disappointing, with the franchise making the playoffs just once and losing 24 of their 39 matches.

In contrast, Arshdeep has been in excellent form in recent seasons, even helping Punjab reach the finals against RCB last year. His 111 wickets in 96 matches at an economy rate of 9.18 has also been a big part of his appeal.

Both teams have until the retention deadline on November 17, and the mini-auction takes place in Goa on December 16. Here is what each side stands to gain.

The clearest gain is bowling support for Bumrah. No Mumbai bowler took more than 15 wickets in IPL 2026, with Allah Ghazanfar leading the list with 15. Arshdeep took 14 wickets for Punjab in the same season and 21 in 2025, when he led their attack to a place in the final.

He also brings a skill set that complements Bumrah. In April, he took 3/22 against Mumbai by swinging the new ball, reversing the old one and bowling a quiet over in the middle. His record at the death is built on yorkers, and a Bumrah-Arshdeep pairing gives Mumbai two specialists for the last four overs.

The deal also resets the captaincy. Rohit Sharma is expected to regain the role, which ends a tenure that produced more defeats than wins.

Punjab finished fifth in 2026 with seven wins from 14 matches and were among the last teams to be eliminated, so the gap to the top four was small. A fast-bowling all-rounder is the rarest profile in T20 cricket, and Hardik was the first player in that category to score 1,000 runs and take 100 wickets in T20Is.

The fit is also clean off the field. Hardik joins purely as a player, with Iyer staying in charge, and the decision to trade for him came from the captain and the coach.

The risks are real, however. Hardik has suffered multiple injury breakdowns and has not played since the last IPL, and Punjab are giving up the bowler who is the franchise's all-time leading wicket-taker.

On paper, Mumbai has addressed a specific issue by bringing in a proven specialist. They needed a second front-line pace option beside Bumrah, and Arshdeep is a better fit for that role than any bowler on their 2026 wicket list. A fresh captain and a fit new-ball partner give a ninth-place side a clear route back into the top four.

Punjab's logic is harder to read. Their second-half bowling was a major issue last season, and they have traded away their best bowler to fix it. That only works if Hardik stays fit and bowls meaningful overs, because an all-rounder who misses games cannot make up for the loss of a leading wicket-taker.

A side that reached the 2025 final deserves the benefit of the doubt, but the deal puts all of the risk on Hardik's body.

The trade will be judged on two numbers: how many league games Hardik plays for Punjab and how well Arshdeep and Bumrah share the death overs for Mumbai. Punjab's answer to replacing Arshdeep's overs will be revealed at the Goa auction on December 16.
