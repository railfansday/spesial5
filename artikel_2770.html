Did Notre Dame football move up in this week's CFP rankings?

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

SOUTH BEND — Notre Dame football heads into the final week of its regular season with a first-round bye in the College Football Playoff tantalizingly close.

The CFP selection committee's latest rankings on Tuesday Nov. 24 placed the Irish (10-1) at No. 4, up two spots from last week's No. 6 ranking.

Should the current order hold, Notre Dame would face the winner of a 5-12 matchup between Tennessee (9-2) and the highest-ranked Group of 6 team. Boise State (9-2), ranked 17th last week, fell out of this week's rankings after a loss to San Diego State but is penciled in as that representative among teams outside the Top 25.

The top three spots this week went to Georgia (10-1), Utah (11-0) and Texas (9-2), with Alabama falling from third to sixth and UCLA dropping five spots to No. 9. Quarterfinal games will be held on New Year's Eve and New Year's Day.

Coming off a 24-3 win over SMU, Notre Dame ranks second among FBS teams in game control and the Football Power Index; fifth in strength of record and 51st in strength of schedule.

Riding a 14-game November winning streak that dates back to 2023, Notre Dame closes out the regular season on Nov. 28 at unranked Syracuse (5-6).

Notre Dame's first 11 opponents have a combined 61-59 record with five of them above .500 individually: Wisconsin (7-4), Stanford (6-5), BYU (9-2), Miami (10-1) and SMU (7-4).

Miami, whose only loss was at Notre Dame on Nov. 7, remained at No. 7 this week.

BYU, which handed the Irish their only loss on Oct. 17 in Provo, Utah, stayed put at No. 10 despite a triple-overtime scare at Kansas.

Through the first two years of the 12-team playoff, top-four seeds went a combined 1-7 with 2025 Indiana's Rose Bowl pasting of Alabama the only exception. However, the 2024 byes included No. 3 Boise State (highest-ranked Group of 6) and No. 4 Arizona State (Big 12 champion).

Those seeding restrictions went away in Year 2 of what will become a three-year run for the 12-team iteration.

Beginning in 2027, the CFP field will include 24 teams playing as many as five postseason games should they reach the final.

Selection Sunday is Dec. 6, with the first-round playoff games due to begin Friday night Dec. 18 at campus sites and the remaining three first-round games on Dec. 19.

Coach Marcus Freeman's team is ranked third in both the media and the US LBM Coaches Poll.
