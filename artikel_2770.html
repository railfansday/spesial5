Ladd McConkey will play against the Jets

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Chargers will have wide receiver Ladd McConkey in the lineup as they try to win their second game of the 2026 season.

McConkey was listed as questionable to face the Jets because of a back injury, but he avoided the inactive list 90 minutes ahead of kickoff in Los Angeles. McConkey has not played in the last two games.

Linebacker Daiyan Henley, cornerback Tarheeb Still, safety Kendall Williamson, guard Alex Harkey, wide receiver KeAndre Lambert-Smith, and wide receiver Brenen Thompson are inactive for the home team.

The Jets have scratched defensive back Jarvis Brownlee, wide receiver Omar Cooper, wide receiver Isaiah Williams, linebacker Trevin Wallace, tight end Jelani Woods, and linebacker Nate Lynn.
