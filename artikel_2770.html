Monday Night Football: No surprises among Chargers-Ravens inactives

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Chargers will officially see the return of tight end David Njoku and linebacker Denzel Perryman. The team activated them from injured reserve earlier Monday, and both are active for Monday Night Football.

Njoku (fibula) has not played since Week 2, and he has six catches for 67 yards.

Perryman (pectoral) will make his 2026 debut tonight.

The Chargers’ inactives are wide receiver Ladd McConkey (back), safety Kendall Williamson (calf), cornerback Isas Waxter (ankle), outside linebacker Bud Dupree, wide receiver Brenen Thompson (hamstring) and defensive lineman Justin Eboigbe (ankle).

The Ravens’ inactives are safety Jaylinn Hawkins (knee), wide receiver Elijah Sarratt, cornerback Chandler Rivers, offensive guard Emery Jones, defensive tackle Aeneas Peebles and quarterback Kedon Slovis. Slovis is the emergency third quarterback.
