DK Metcalf's 2025 suspension can be factor in discipline for Sunday's ejection

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Steelers receiver DK Metcalf was ejected from Sunday’s 19-16 loss to the Eagles. That may not be the end of the discipline imposed by the league.

The disqualification, per the league, was automatic. It resulted from a pair of fouls for unsportsmanlike conduct.

The official Game Book described the first penalty as “unnecessary roughness.” Regardless, the ejection was automatic.

The next step won’t be. The league office will review the situation on Monday, with a decision inevitably made on whether additional discipline will be imposed.

And Metcalf’s history becomes relevant to the evaluation. As one source explained it, Metcalf’s two-game suspension from 2025 after an altercation with a fan in Detroit can be taken into account.

Which raises the question of whether Metcalf will be suspended again.

If it’s going to happen, it will need to happen quickly. The Broncos play the Steelers on Friday.
