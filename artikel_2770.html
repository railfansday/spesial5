Vikings re-sign Odell Beckham Jr. to practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Wide receiver Odell Beckham Jr. is back with the Vikings.

The Vikings signed Beckham to their active roster ahead of Monday night’s loss to the Bills, but released him on Tuesday. Word at the time was that they planned to re-sign him to the practice squad and official announcement of Beckham’s return came on Thursday afternoon.

Beckham played two offensive snaps against the Bills and did not catch a pass. He also went without a reception in three appearances for the Giants earlier this season.

Jauan Jennings missed the Bills game with a calf injury and remained out of practice for the Vikings on Wednesday. Justin Jefferson, Jordan Addison, Tai Felton, and Myles Price are their other wideouts.
