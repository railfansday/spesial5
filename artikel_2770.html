Tech outages are causing people to panic buy. Costco is benefitting

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A series of tech outages has consumers stocking up on emergency supplies.

Costco sales are seeing a lift because of it, according to research firm Mizuho.

In the final week of October, Mizuho estimates Costco's U.S. comparable sales, excluding gasoline, rose 11% to 12%, up from the normal 6% to 7% the first three weeks in October. Costco reported an 8.1% increase for the month. The firm said the stronger pace is continuing through November as 21 of the 26 high-ticket generators it tracked on Costco's website sold out as of Sunday night.

Shares of Costco were roughly flat-to-lower in Wednesday morning trading around $1,107 apiece.

"Consumer anxiety following the Microsoft Outlook outage, the ground stoppage for all U.S. airline flights, and apocalyptic concerns flaring up across social media have all contributed to increased emergency prep sales at COST," said Mizuho analyst David Bellinger in a research note Monday.

The stockpiling intensified from a series of recent disruptions. Microsoft's Outlook and Teams outage on Thursday, the Federal Aviation Administration halted departures nation-wide on Friday. A separate software outage at JPMorgan Chase Monday caused banking transaction delays while a system outage delayed United Airlines flights Monday. The National Security Agency on Tuesday said the spy agency's communication network went down temporarily.

The various outages appear to come amid a rush to recover software vulnerabilities uncovered by AI tools and officials are saying there's no reason for panic.

Still, on TikTok and other social media platforms, posts urging people to stock up on food and supplies are gaining significant traction.

Mizuho raised its price target on Costco to $1,150 from $1,100 and kept its buy-equivalent outperform rating. The firm said U.S. comparable sales could grow more than 10% in November.

Mizuho also sees Walmart's Sam's Club benefiting from emergency preparedness sales as shoppers fill up their pantries, and for Home Depot and Lowe's as they buy home improvement emergency supplies.

At Costco, Mizuho will be watching whether the surge brought in new members sign-ups when the warehouse retailer reports its first quarter of fiscal 2027 on Dec. 10.
