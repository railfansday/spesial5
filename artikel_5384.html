Mark Zuckerberg Meta AI Predicts ‘Will Crypto Recover in November’

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Bitcoin (BTC) is trading near $75,800–$76,000 and Ethereum (ETH) is around $2,230–$2,235 as of November 8, 2026. Mark Zuckerberg’s Meta AI predicts, ‘Will crypto recover in November? ‘, which is the question on many traders’ lips heading into a big week across the markets.

Bitcoin sits roughly 40% below its October 2025 peak of about $126,000, while Ethereum remains well off its own highs. The market is asking whether crypto can stage a meaningful recovery this month. After the post-midterm bounce, both assets have consolidated.

Bitcoin has held the mid-$70,000s after testing higher levels earlier in the recovery, while Ethereum continues to lag, trading with a relatively weak ETH/BTC ratio near 0.0295.

This is typical in the early stages of a potential recovery, when Bitcoin usually leads, which is why all eyes should be on BTC USD over the coming week.

Bitcoin has formed a series of higher lows since the mid-year troughs and is defending the $74,800–$75,500 zone. A weekly close back above $81,000–$82,000 would strengthen the case for a durable base.

A break below $74,000 would reopen downside risk toward the low $70,000s. Ethereum remains softer. It is struggling to reclaim key moving averages and needs a sustained move through $2,400–$2,500 to show relative strength returning.

Until the ETH/BTC ratio turns higher, a full “altcoin season” style recovery remains unlikely, but a shift in market conditions can quickly change sentiment.

The most immediate event is the October CPI report on Tuesday, November 10. Consensus expects a cooling in the monthly rate (around +0.2–0.3% headline). A softer-than-expected print would ease rate-hike fears, support risk assets, and give Bitcoin and Ethereum room to push higher into the second half of the month.

A hotter reading would reinforce the “higher-for-longer” narrative, pressure yields higher, and likely stall or reverse the recent rebound.

Soft inflation and resilient but not overheating growth data would be constructive. Sticky inflation or stronger-than-expected economic prints that raise December rate-hike odds would act as a headwind. Spot Bitcoin ETF flows remain the marginal driver.

Recent weekly outflows have weighed on price, so a return to consistent net inflows would be an important confirmation of recovering institutional demand.

Stablecoin growth and on-chain activity continue to favor Ethereum over the longer term, but near-term price action is still dominated by macro and Bitcoin leadership.

A modest recovery is possible if the November 10 CPI comes in soft and ETF flows stabilize. In that scenario, Bitcoin could retest the $82,000–$85,000 area and Ethereum could push back toward $2,400–$2,500 by month-end.

A hotter inflation print or continued ETF outflows would likely keep the market range-bound or push prices back toward recent lows. A full-blown bull-market breakout to new all-time highs is unlikely in November.

The more realistic path is a grinding recovery that builds through the end of 2026 and into 2027, assuming inflation continues to cool and institutional demand returns.

For now, the November CPI print is the single most important near-term catalyst that will decide whether the post-midterm bounce has legs or fades.
