Bakole steps in for Opetaia to fight Wilder on Fury-AJ undercard

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Martin Bakole will replace Jai Opetaia and fight Deontay Wilder on the Tyson Fury vs Anthony Joshua undercard at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff on December 11; Bakole lost to Joseph Parker at short notice and has scored outstanding wins over Tony Yoka, Carlos Takam and Jared Anderson

Martin Bakole will fight Deontay Wilder on the undercard of Tyson Fury vs Anthony Joshua on December 11.

Fury and Joshua finally meet after a long-running rivalry in the main event at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff.

Wilder is a former WBC heavyweight world champion renowned as one of the hardest punchers in the sport.

He was due to box Australian cruiserweight star Jai Opetaia, who withdrew injured and will now remain in the 200lb division.

"Bakole is next, and then after that, it's going to be crazy," Wilder said in a post by @heavyweighttruth on Instagram.

"I ain't going to move forward with the information that I've received, but once I beat him, you're looking at the new heavyweight champion of the world.

"Martin, here we come, baby. I appreciate you for accepting the opportunity, and let's give the fans, let's give the people something to talk about for the rest of their lives."

Bakole was already scheduled to box on the card and is no stranger to taking a big fight on short notice. He travelled at just three days' notice to step in for Daniel Dubois and box Joseph Parker.

Prior to that Bakole scored outstanding wins over Tony Yoka, Carlos Takam and elite American hope Jared Anderson.

Opetaia's promoter Dana White said: "Jai Opetaia had to withdraw from his fight against Deontay Wilder next month. Stepping in is Martin Bakole, a six-foot-six, 300lb knockout artist who's ready to take on one of the hardest-hitting heavyweights in boxing.

"Wilder and Bakole have 66 wins and 59 knockouts, so you don't want to miss this fight."

Welsh heavyweight Moses Jolly also joins the card in a bout against Igor Adiel Macedo with more bouts still to be announced.
