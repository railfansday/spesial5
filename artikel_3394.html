6 Superhero Movies From the 2020s That Are 10/10 Masterpieces

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

With how prominent superhero movies have been for decades upon decades, one would think they'd eventually slow down—but no, they're still going and stronger than ever. With some amazing superhero movies released thus far in the 2020s, and some of them being among the highest-grossing movies of all time, it's hard to believe that superhero films will be going anywhere anytime soon.

Whether they come from the land of Marvel or DC Comics, there have been some astounding superhero adventures audiences have gone on in this new decade. While it may not be over just yet, what viewers have been treated to so far is more than worth noting. There are been some truly excellent projects that one could easily consider 10/10 masterpieces in one way or another.

If there's a superhero movie that flew under the radar of most, it is 2023's Blue Beetle. Directed by the excellent Angel Manuel Soto, starring the ever-lovable Xolo Maridueña, Blue Beetle ended up being a movie that—for those who went out to see it—was critically well-received. The title almost feels like Sam Raimi's Spider-Man for the DC universe, and that's helped it become very memorable.

The story of Jaime Reyes is such a strong one, filled to the brim with relatability. Following the young man as he comes back from college and struggles to find a job to help his family, he eventually comes across the Scarab through a freak incident, and dons the powers of the titular Blue Beetle. It's a true coming-of-age story that is a great watch from minute one to the final second. In a time of multiversal superhero movies releasing yearly, the likes of a blockbuster superhero movie like Blue Beetle felt like a true breath of fresh air in 2023.

James Gunn is a great filmmaker—this much has been clear for quite some time—and the general audience really became acquainted with his work in 2014 with the release of Guardians of the Galaxy. This Marvel trilogy became what he would most prominently be known for quite some time—until he became the co-head of DC Studios a short time after. So, with the release of this trilogy-capper, people knew Gunn had to really knock it out of the park to impress the audience.

Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 is a great example of how to satisfyingly wrap up a trilogy, and give almost all of the primary characters a genuinely happy ending. Everyone gets a good conclusion to their character arcs, and it leaves the fans feeling like there's a true full-circle to the story that began in Guardians of the Galaxy. While he teases major deaths a good couple of times throughout the runtime, the Guardians all get a happy ending of some sort. When the standard for finales is typically leaving viewers with a heartbreaking death, getting to see these fan-favorites make their way out and end their journeys with a happy celebration is heartwarming.

Four powers, and four completely different kinds of person behind them. Here is what you would wake up with, what it would cost you, and why the MCU keeps handing it to somebody exactly like you.

You get the power that is really about taking the hit so that nobody else has to. Strength in the MCU always lands on people who were already carrying too much, which is exactly why it suits you.

You get the power that never switches off. Spider-sense is a gift and a nervous system on permanent alert, and the people who get it in these films are always the ones who were already worrying about everybody.

You get the quietest power in the building, and quietly the most useful. Invisibility comes with force fields for a reason, because the people who prefer not to be seen are usually the ones holding a shield around everybody else.

You get the biggest and least comfortable power on the list, the one that runs directly on feeling. Chaos magic is not really a superpower in the MCU so much as an emotional state with consequences attached.

For quite some time, Batman in cinema has been portrayed in a couple different ways, but not one that really hones in on his detective side (he's considered The World's Greatest Detective for a reason). Fans of the Big Bat finally were treated to a film that does just that in 2022, thanks to director Matt Reeves and superstar Robert Pattinson. The Batman is a crime noir that follows The Dark Knight in his second year as he attempts to solve the crimes of a new serial killer known simply as the Riddler (Paul Dano).

The Batman being so detective-focused is something that pleased a plethora of fans, and it allowed the story to be beyond engaging. Reeves' direction bleeds style and personality, making the movie feel one of a kind, and like a Gotham City that audiences have never experienced in previous Batman media. Pattinson does a stellar job bringing this loner hero to life in a way that truly gives him an amazing duality.

It's safe to say that the 2020s have treated James Gunn pretty well (until recently, that is). Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 isn't the only smash hit he got to have in the 2020s. Another one of his best comes in the form of DC's The Suicide Squad. No one could have ever expected him to bring this franchise out from the grave it was exiled to after Suicide Squad, but he did.

This 2021 title is not only hilarious and visually vibrant, but has such a distinct heart and soul at the center of it. Much like Guardians of the Galaxy, James took these misfits and turned them into fully fleshed out, three-dimensional characters that are so easy to fall in love with. Granted, he managed to make the Guardians of the Galaxy MCU fan-favorites, so this seems in line with his previous superhero movie track record.

If one were to ask almost anyone, they'd say that Spider-Man: Brand New Day is the Spider-Man movie that people have been waiting about a decade for. After the conclusion of the trilogy that began with Spider-Man: Homecoming, fans were happy, but craving something different—a more grounded, street-level Spider-Man story. That is exactly what Spider-Man: Brand New Day is.

This project is so phenomenal and beloved that it has quickly become one of the highest-grossing movies of all time—landing in the current top three. The new director, Destin Daniel Cretton (known for Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings), did an exceptional job taking all the issues people had with the previous movies, and improving on as many of them as he could. Not only that, but it acts as the official introduction to the MCU's Jean Grey (Sadie Sink), and does so in a manner that feels natural and unforced.

There's absolutely no denying that Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse's release in 2018 changed the film medium forever. So, when it came to getting a sequel, it was hard to believe they could create something that could live up to the immense legacy that the 2018 title set. But, come 2023, audiences would soon see that Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse more than lives up to the legacy of the first film.

This sequel takes everything people loved about the first and expands upon those things. New art styles are introduced for new Spider-People, an incredible cast comes together to bring these characters to life, the attention to detail is off the charts, and the screenplay exceeds expectations. It is a living and breathing trolley problem that tests the protagonists in every single scene. There's no question that the trilogy-capper, Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse, has a gargantuant mountain to climb when it comes to following this film up.
