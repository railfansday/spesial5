Avengers: Doomsday Confirms A Massive Thor Power Change After Getting Beat By Doctor Doom

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Thor is confirmed to be getting a massive power upgrade in Avengers: Doomsday, and it seemingly all stems from Doctor Doom beating him. Thor is one of the strongest heroes in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, so if anyone was going to stand a chance against Doom in one-on-one combat, he would be considered one of the most likely candidates to hold his own, if not win.

However, Avengers: Doomsday's trailers have shown that the Asgardian's strength and weapons are no match for the Latverian sorcerer. Marvel Studios has not been shy of showing Thor leaping into battle against Doom, looking to deliver a lethal blow to the head courtesy of Stormbreaker. But to Thor's surprise, Doom stops his ax with only two fingers and sends the god flying backward.

If the Avengers are going to have any chance to stop Doom from winning Avengers: Doomsday, they'll need a lot more power than Thor can usually provide. He's not alone in this fight, but Doom has an army of his own, one we now know to include Hulk and Scarlet Witch. After giving Thor the Stormbreaker upgrade in Avengers: Infinity War, the Russo brothers have once again boosted his power to take on Doom.

The final Avengers: Doomsday trailer features an important moment where Thor is seen in the woods reaching his hand to the sky as lightning begins striking him, a standard powering-up sequence for the Asgardian prince. Instead of the lightning being blue, though, it is yellow, signaling the arrival of Odinforce Thor.

Odinforce is a power that is typically reserved for Asgard's kings and grants them immense abilities. The power has always been associated with yellow in the MCU, hence making it clear that Thor's lightning color change is connected to Chris Hemsworth's character receiving it.

This upgrade presumably comes after Doom beats Thor. He has his usual blue lightning in that sequence. While Marvel Studios has quite a history of manipulating trailer footage to throw fans off certain elements, this does not appear to be another example thanks to how the Odinforce upgrade is used in Thor's arc.

Thor's initial Avengers: Doomsday teaser trailer included him praying to Odin for "the strength of the All-Fathers" in the woods. It was thought this could come ahead of an initial fight with Doom, but now it appears the Asgardian is searching for a way to amplify his abilities ahead of a rematch.

His kneeling position and the woods surrounding him match the previous sequence, making it clear that Odin answers his son's plea. Thor will be able to make another third-act grand entrance and crash the final battle against Doom's army with more power than he's ever had.

Thor's initial attack in Doomsday is one way Marvel plans to show just how powerful Doctor Doom is thanks to his brilliant mind, indomitable will, mastery of the mystic arts, and more. And yet, Odinforce Thor will arguably be even stronger than Doom, depending on how the MCU portrays it. At worst, he'll be an equal to Doom.

Odinforce Thor should be treated as one of the most powerful beings in the entire multiverse based on the comics. He's nearly invincible and has a wealth of abilities the god of thunder typically does not, such as manipulating space, time, and minds, creating force fields, and teleportation. The Odinforce can even resurrect fallen Asgardians, which could come in handy when fighting Doom's army.

The Russos have rarely held back with Thor's powers throughout the MCU, so there is a good track record to go off of that they will do Odinforce Thor justice. Even if he is arguably destined to lose another fight with Doctor Doom even after the upgrade, the rematch should be much more evenly fought. The power of the All-Fathers coursing through Thor's veins in Avengers: Doomsday sets up an epic clash.
