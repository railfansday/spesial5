Last-Minute Costumes for Trail Runners

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

It’s Halloween week and you still have no idea what you’re going to be. Trail or ultrarunning is your entire personality, but you can only toss on some last-minute split shorts and a fake beard to go as Forrest Gump so many years in a row. Everyone is “pretty tired” of you responding to what you are with an exhausted, “I just felt like running” in a goofy accent. It’s OK; we’ve all been there. And Trail Runner’s resident doodler and creative character costume expert, Bobby Peavey, has your back. He’s put together a curated list of archetypes from your local run club and Instagram feed that are sure to be a hit this spooky season.

This costume is perfect for your most bonk-vulnerable friends.  Dress them up as Bonkenstein and enjoy a night of constantly bringing them back from the dead with salty and sweet snacks.

Do you have the patience of a saint and the multitasking skills of Leslie Knope? Then the Race Director costume is the perfect choice for you. Your night is going to consist of making sure there are ample snacks for all, that trick or treating/holiday party schedules stay on track, that everybody stays safe and healthy, and about a dozen other things that everyone else surely overlooks.

When you select this costume for the spookiest night of the year, the assignment is clear: only speak in the past tense for the entire night. You’re full of nostalgia and cynical disdain for anything modern. You’re basically the Uncle Rico of the evening.

Time to update your watch to the current model, recharge your heart rate strap, and cover every inch of exposed skin with wearable storage.  Because the funnest part of your Halloween is going to be teaching all your friends about the newest and techiest gear on the market.

For this costume, it is crucial that you log more photos than miles.  You are in this costume purely for the likes and kudos. If you exit Zone 1 at any point, you’re doing it wrong.

No pacer or muling needed with this costume. Thanks to your vampire diet and a steady dose of nipple chafing, you’ve got all the nutrition you need to conquer any ultra distance!
