'It's extremely aggressive' - Stefan Borson drops '£900m' Crystal Palace takeover verdict

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Crystal Palace's owners will struggle to find someone to spend £900million on a team at the bottom of the Premier League.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that now is not the right time for the Eagles to change owners.

Palace are in the midst of an enormous storm, having just sacked Pierre Sage after 16 games, leaving them 20th in the league standings.

After drawing 1-1 with Hoffenheim, the 2024-25 FA Cup winners are also sitting 21st in the Europa League table after four matches.

There is now a very big fear that the Eagles could be relegated this season, and it remains to be seen who could replace Sage in the dugout during this international break.

As well as failures on the pitch, the owners have also been unable to find any potential buyers for the club, having valued Palace at £900m.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson has sent some home truths to Selhurst Park after it was revealed that as many as eight Premier League clubs are up for sale, officially or unofficially.

"Some of the clubs that are on the list are not going to get deals. I think, you take Crystal Palace, for example..." he said.

"I don't think anybody's going to buy Crystal Palace for £900m at the bottom of the table. First of all, the price was always extremely aggressive.

"And secondly, I just don't think now is the right time for them. So I think it's more about who isn't going to get sold on that list."

It has been reported that the Eagles' owners are exploring a full sale, but it remains to be seen who else is on that newly emerging, and hidden, list.

It's impossible to put an exact figure on that question, in all fairness, but it does seem like Palace's owners are asking for above the market value.

To put it into perspective, Everton have a new stadium and are sixth in the Premier League, and The Friedkin Group (TFG) are asking for £1billion.

Borson, therefore, believes that Palace's owners should be asking in the region of £750m to £800m, and might attract more buyers this season.

Palace won trophies in back-to-back seasons, the FA Cup in 2024-25 and the Europa Conference League in 2025-26. Now, relegation is on the cards.
