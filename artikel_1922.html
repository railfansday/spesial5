Portsmouth site highlights the surprising ‘one to watch’ in West Ham’s line up

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After a lacklustre performance against Preston, Scott Parker’s West Ham side travel to the south coast for a game tomorrow under the lights and definitely under the cosh: No wins in five means mid-table mediocrity beckons if the new West Ham boss cannot get his team back to winning ways.

Ahead of the clash, Portsmouth’s Express FM have been running the rule over our claret and blue squad – and have come up with something quite surprising in their summary of who is likely to be the the West Ham game changer:

Belgian midfielder Arne Engels could cause Portsmouth a problem or two this time around.

Engels – rated as one to watch by supporters of tomorrow’s opponents

Wearing squad number 7, the 23-year-old has been putting in consistent standout performances over recent weeks despite West Ham’s dip in form.

An objective opinion on West Ham’s young midfielder who has shown flashes of excellence it may be, but, from most Hammers’ fans’ viewpoint, Engels hasn’t yet lived up to the price tag of being the Championship’s record signing.

Corners excepted, he’s pretty consistent at those. Interestingly though, he does appear to have been asked to operate in a more advanced role under Scott Parker so perhaps he’ll live up to the assessment made by tomorrows’ opposition fans, who clearly see him as more of a ‘weapon’ than those of us who’ve watched him week in week out.
