Mauricio Pochettino’s first Manchester United training session filmed by drone

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Manchester United have been left red-faced after a drone covertly filmed Mauricio Pochettino’s first training session and posted the footage to YouTube.

The Argentine, who signed up to replace Michael Carrick as United’s head coach last week, arrived at Carrington on Wednesday to start work with his new club.

With United languishing in the drop zone, Pochettino faces a brutal opening fixture of his reign, away at Liverpool.

But his preparations for that match have already been compromised in a concerning security breach at United’s training centre, after a drone secretly recorded 12 minutes of Pochettino’s first session with the club, with footage posted on YouTube for all to see.

The drone does not appear to have entered Carrington’s airspace at any point, but footage filmed with a zoom lens shows the session in clear detail.

The YouTube channel which posted the clip followed it up with a statement claiming they had broken no laws by recording the session as the airspace around Carrington is unrestricted.

No players or staff caught in the footage appeared to be aware of the fact they were being filmed. At the time of writing, the clip has accrued over 40,000 views.

The footage shows the United squad working in two groups. One was being put through a rondo on one pitch, while Pochettino led the other through what appeared to be a tactical session on another. Both groups appeared to comprise a blend of first-team and academy players.

Manchester United declined to comment when contacted by Standard Sport.
