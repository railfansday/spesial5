Your Weekly Horoscope, November 15-21, 2026: Discerning Your Next Step

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

This week channels themes of desire and identity as Mars and Jupiter come together in a conjunction and the Sun in Scorpio squares first the Lunar Nodes, then Jupiter, and finally Mars. Four distinct movements, one continued story. The days ahead act as a mirror, one asking for truthful self-reflection. Your weekly horoscope for November 15-21, 2016, helps you discern what, exactly, to ask yourself.

November 15 | Mars conjunct Jupiter November 17 | Sun square Lunar Nodes November 18 | Sun square Jupiter November 19 | Sun square Mars

As the week begins, Mars and Jupiter come together in a conjunction while held by Leo on November 15, 2026.

Mars resonates with desire, initiation, assertion, and direction. Jupiter connects us with belief, hope, expansion, and possibility. As they form a conjunction in the sign of the zodiac known for self-expression and creativity, their vibrations merge. Desire becomes infused with hope, direction with belief, initiation with possibility.

Together, they offer a ripple of boldness through our cosmos and through our bodies. They ignite courage and a willingness to take up more space and participate wholeheartedly in life. It is a conjunction of permission to desire, create, and act.

As Mars and Jupiter start to separate from one another, a square aspect forms between the Sun in Scorpio and the Lunar Nodes, made up of the North Node in Aquarius and the South Node in Leo, on November 17, 2026.

Scorpio is not a delicate energy. Squares hold an inherent intensity in their angles formed. The South Node speaks to the past and the familiar patterns that have gotten us to this moment and the North Node whispers of something new. Aquarius speaks to themes of reciprocity, belonging, freedom, and community.

So when the Sun, a symbol of selfhood, is influenced by the sign of brutal honesty and transformation as it comes into a 90-degree angle with the Nodes, the cosmos is asking us to notice the fears, attachments, or power dynamics underneath our decisions. Who have we been and who are we becoming? The square asks us to look at the comforts and familiarities that no longer align with our truth, and the emerging self that is still learning to find safety and trust within. What identity is asking to be released? What fears whisper in response to the changes emerging through us?

The next day, the Sun in Scorpio moves ahead and forms a square with Jupiter in Leo on November 18, 2026, calling for an exploration of our beliefs, desires, and expressions. Accompanying it is the question of whether these still matter to us.

As the Sun travels through Scorpio, we are in a space of unrelenting self-honesty. No longer will old stories that don’t speak truth align. No longer will what is simply noise, projection, or inherited stories fit. We want to access what lies beneath the surface— the origin, the source, and the real.

Jupiter is not a quiet planet. As it communicates with the Sun, it speaks to our visions, hopes, dreams, and desires for ourselves. Their meeting is a mirror. Does our vision have emotional substance? Can our confidence accommodate uncertainty, feedback, or discomfort? Are our desires held in truth or have they been built upon external expectations, ideas, projections, pressures, or fears?

Our final transit of the week takes place as the Sun enters a 90-degree square aspect with Mars in Leo on November 19, 2026.

Mars is the fiesty layer of us that moves, builds, acts, take steps according to an inner direction. The Sun in Scorpio invites us deep into our emotional world and asks: what matters? What do we want? Who are we, truly? Together these two bodies invite us to reconsider what is worthy of our energy, action, and life force. They bring our attention to the flow of desire into movement, where we trust ourselves to act, where we are still learning to express with authenticity.

Here we are supported in exploring our relationship with self-expression and action. How does emotion fuel and move us? When something matters to us, how do we create action? Are we able to transmute emotional intensity into deliberate action?

There is depth, intensity, and power inherent in the movements of the cosmos this week which challenge us to ponder our relationship with self-trust. Namely, how safe we feel in allowing what is alive in us to change our life trajectory. How safe we feel in loosening our attachments to what has been and creating space for what is emerging. And how safe we feel in looking beneath inherited expectations, familiar identities, and outside pressures so that we can desire, create, and self-express with  authenticity. Your weekly horoscope explains how.

This week invites an exploration of inner safety and self-trust. The safety within your body to express who you are with truth, to be seen with honesty, to live with authenticity. Can you allow yourself to bring strength, courage, and self-compassion to your  fear, hurt, and stories?

There are desires and whispers of your truth wanting to be heard. Where are you ready to be seen in greater honesty, intimacy, and courage with who you are and what you desire? Are the patterns that once protected you still required?

Can you integrate your expression with movement to create action? Let your desires embed themselves in your habits. Discern what truly matters and what is simply noise and allow your answers to shape the way you design your days, which are the building blocks of your life.

It’s a time to remember, own, and embody your inherent worthiness. As you encounter each experience, emotion, or fear that seems to suggest your desires are beyond you, meet them with the truth of your intrinsic value, the kind that’s not based on achievement. Then notice how your days feel different.

Old identities are falling away to create space for current layers of who you to emerge. Let patterns learned in childhood and emotions and stories inherited from family go through questioning. What lives within you? How much of it is truth? What of that is truly yours?

The way you see, think, believe, and express is experiencing a transition. Memories that surface do so for the purpose of healing, release, or integration. And as more unconscious material arises and filters through your consciousness, allow new ways of seeing to infiltrate your mind, perspective, and narratives.

Can you let yourself meet your hopes, your dreams, and your visions that you hold for your future? What feels aligned? What feels authentic, empowering, and alive? As old stories around fitting in, self-expression, and safety begin to whisper more loudly, let your desires be based on your truth rather than anyone else’s expectations.

This week sings with direction and identity, purpose and expression. Let your courage ground you and dispel your fears around authenticity, being seen, and where you want to take your life. There is a need for your innermost truth to emerge so that you can live wholly as yourself.

Allow your awareness to turn inward this week. There are stories and perspectives, ideas and pathways from both your conscious and unconscious mind wanting to be released. And beyond those, there are whispers within your body wanting to be heard and speaking to who you are becoming. These are desires wanting to come into your life.

This week supports you in rebalancing your energy. No longer will voices, perspectives, and expectations that are not yours speak more loudly than your own. No longer will your actions, movements, and belonging be guided by fitting in or finding yourself dependent upon approval and sameness. When you move in authenticity, the right people will find you.

There is a voice, an inner calling, a different aspect of yourself ready to emerge. Where is it guiding you? Let your desires speak. Let your emotions reveal who you are becoming and what wants to come alive in your life. A healing of your heart and your old patterns of relating are releasing.

This week holds space for a rearranging of your life. One in which your inner truths shape your everyday attitude. Where your desires, visions, and hopes for your future come through in your habits, actions, and self-talk. As you create space for reflection, allow your body to feel the emotions of what you believe and how you choose to see the world.
