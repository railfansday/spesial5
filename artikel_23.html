Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond: The surprise casting choice explained

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Robert Pattinson will be the new James Bond, taking over from Daniel Craig in one of cinema’s most iconic roles. Film critic Emma Jones joins Eve Jackson on the arts24 weekly film show to discuss the surprise choice, Pattinson's star power and what he could bring to 007. Plus, they review the latest "Hunger Games" prequel "Sunrise on the Reaping", Lukas Dhont's Cannes prize winner "Coward" and Germany's Oscar entry "Everytime".
