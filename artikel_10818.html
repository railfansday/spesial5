Spanish footballer dies aged 30 after collision with goalkeeper during match

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Spanish footballer Jesús de Miguel has died aged 30 after suffering a cardiac arrest following a collision with an opposing goalkeeper during a match.

The Tenerife forward was struck in the chest while challenging for the ball against Andorra on Monday, November 2, during a Spanish second division match at Estadio Heliodoro Rodríguez López.

De Miguel collapsed on the pitch, prompting players from both teams to urgently call for medical assistance.

Medical staff treated the 30-year-old for more than five minutes before he was rushed to hospital by ambulance. The match was subsequently abandoned.

Andorra goalkeeper Jesús Owono, who was involved in the collision, was shown a straight red card.

Tenerife confirmed that De Miguel’s neurological condition had deteriorated in the hours following the incident, before announcing his death on Tuesday morning.

“Racked with pain, with a lump in our throats and unable to believe that this is true, from CD Tenerife we communicate the tragic passing, this Tuesday, November 3, 2026, at 8:00 am, of Jesús de Miguel Alameda.” the club said in a statement, translated from Spanish.

“There are no words of comfort; there is absolutely nothing that can alleviate this feeling overflowing with sadness. We stand alongside his devastated family, alongside his loved ones, alongside all of tinerfeñismo that today weeps for this cruel outcome.”

De Miguel began his professional career with Leganés B in 2015 and went on to represent eight other Spanish clubs before joining Tenerife in 2025.

His death has prompted tributes from across Spanish football, including messages from Barcelona, Real Madrid, and Andorra.

Former Barca defender Gerard Piqué, who is the majority shareholder of Andorra, wrote on social media: “I am so deeply sorry. Rest in peace, Jesús.”

Andorra also offered its “most sincere condolences” to De Miguel’s family, teammates, and club.

Barcelona said: “Our thoughts and support are with his family, friends and loved ones during these difficult times.”

Real Madrid expressed its “condolences and affection to his family, his teammates, his club, and all his loved ones.”
