New Official Fallout-Inspired Game Announced, And It's Unlike Anything We'd Ever Expected

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

"A Wasteland Full of Cats, a Fallout-inspired adventure!"

In and amongst all of the GTA 6 news that's been happening this week, Bethesda has made a very quiet announcement about a new Fallout-inspired game, and it's one of the most bizarre crossovers we've seen in a while!

Simply put, Xbox and Bethesda are teaming up with the developers behind games such as A Building Full of Cats to create a Wasteland-themed game where you'll seemingly have to locate cats throughout the Fallout universe.

In other words, this would appear to be a hidden object game.

We'll throw some footage of one of this developer's Xbox titles down below so you can get an idea of what to expect. Their games are also always very inexpensive, and they also have some fantastic reviews on the Xbox Store.

There's obviously lots of other Fallout stuff in the works right now, including two remasters based on Fallout 3 and Fallout: New Vegas, as well as the much-anticipated Fallout 5. This new "inspired" game clearly just serves as a bit of fun to plug the gap, and we reckon it might actually do very well when it launches in the future.

It's cool to see Xbox partnering on lighthearted ideas like this! Long may it continue - do it with Halo and Gears next?
