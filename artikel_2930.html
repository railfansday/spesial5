Cleveland Browns news: It’s the most insufferable time of the year

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Cleveland Browns have entered the annual part of the calendar that everyone was hoping to avoid.

Welcome to Quarterback Controversy Season!

The Browns had barely left the field on Sunday following a loss to the two-win New Orleans Saints before head coach Todd Monken was asked who the starting quarterback will be for this week's game against the Houston Texans.

Monken rightly pointed out that, you know, the game had just ended, so maybe it wasn't the right time to discuss such things.

On Monday, presumably having had time to sleep on the matter, Monken had the opportunity to put the nonsense to bed before it started, but instead decided to start digging his own grave and state that he has still not made a decision.

Let's assume Monken and the coaching staff are not planning to pull the surprise of the season and start either Dillon Gabriel or Taylen Green against the Texans. So that leaves Monken with two options:

The quarterback he handpicked to start the season and, through some form of voodoo, played a role in helping the Browns to a 5-3 record and a spot to play meaningful games in the final two months of the season.

The backup quarterback, who was just 5-of-8 for 38 yards at halftime against the two-win Saints, but rallied the troops in the second half thanks to impressive YAC totals from the receivers in a one-point loss.

At this point it may not matter much as the defense is worn down after being overrun the past three weeks to the tune of 501 rushing yards. And for all the "explosive plays" on Sunday, the offense needed almost five full quarters of football to score 22 points on a New Orleans defense that was allowing 28 points a game.

All Monken needs to do is pick someone- it doesn't matter who- and stick with them the rest of the season. It's simply too exhausting to go through all this stupidity again.
