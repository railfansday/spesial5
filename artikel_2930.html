Mortgage demand turns lower, as interest rates shoot back up to the highest level in three weeks

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Mortgage demand had been improving, as interest rates fell for about a month, but that came to an abrupt end last week. Mortgage rates moved sharply higher, causing total mortgage application volume to drop 2.3% from the previous week, according to the Mortgage Bankers Association's seasonally adjusted index.

The average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances, $832,750 or less, increased last week to 7.35% from 7.21%, with points rising to 0.79 from 0.75, including the origination fee, for loans with a 20% down payment. That was its highest rate in three weeks.

"Treasury yields rose last week amid data showing inflation accelerated in October and Fed officials acknowledging that additional rate hikes may be needed in the coming months," said Joel Kan, vice president and deputy chief economist at the MBA.

Applications to refinance a home loan fell 2% for the week and were 54% lower than the same week one year ago. While overall refinance activity declined, Federal Housing Administration refinances increased almost 7%. The FHA offers low-down-payment government loans at lower interest rates but requires borrowers to pay mortgage insurance.

"The FHA rate was roughly 30 basis points below the conforming rate, which supported refinance opportunities for some homeowners," added Kan.

Applications for a mortgage to purchase a home fell 3% for the week and were 18% below where they were one year ago. Potential buyers are dealing with both affordability challenges as well as weaker confidence in the overall economy due to persistent inflation.

Mortgage rates haven't done much to start this week, staying essentially flat, according to a separate survey from Mortgage News Daily. There is more housing data set to be released this week with the Census report on monthly housing starts and building permits, as well as the monthly pending home sales index from the National Association of Realtors.
