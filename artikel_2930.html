Dolphins news: Rebuild working, Lions game review, trade deadline updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Miami Dolphins lost their seventh game of the 2026 season, having won just once this year. It has been a rough season according to the record, and we all know you are what your record says you are, but in the Dolphins case this year, they may be a little more than that 1-7 record.

The Dolphins are a rebuilding team, and this year's losses are understandable for a team that completely tore down its roster and is relying heavily on rookies and other younger players to build a foundation for the future. The 2026 season is not the goal for the Dolphins' front office and coaching staff. They are more concerned about making Miami a consistent threat year after year in the AFC playoffs, rather than making a one-year splash.

The Dolphins are playing a physical style of football this season. They have matched up with some of the league's top teams. They are also seeing flashes of potential from younger players who may promise a brighter future. The rebuild seems to be working.

The Palm Beach Post's Joe Schad agrees that, despite a 1-7 record, the Dolphins are proving on the field that the rebuild is working:

"A lot of stuff that we started off the year doing, we're not doing anymore," [quarterback Malik] Willis said [after Sunday's game].

Miami has the youngest team in the NFL and at times, it shows. But on this day, first-round cornerback Chris Johnson had his first career interception and second-round linebacker Jacob Rodriguez had his first career sack…

The Dolphins have not quit and that's way more important than their record.

Miami is 1-7 and yet the Dolphins are optimistic. Fans should be, too.
