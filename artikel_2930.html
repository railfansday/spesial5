Bears vs. Lions odds: Opening lines for Week 12 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The traditional Thanksgiving games in Detroit and Dallas don't always feature high stakes, but this Thursday's Noon kickoff in Motown between the Chicago Bears and the Detroit Lions will see the winner move into first place in the NFC North. Both teams won today to move to 8-2 to set up the critical NFC matchup, and with the Minnesota Vikings loss tonight, the winner on Thanksgiving will end the week as the two-seed in the NFC.

Our partners at FanDuel Sportsbook list the Bears as a road favorite (-2.5), with the Chicago moneyline at -132, and the point total over/under at 55.5.

The Bears haven't beaten the Lions since 2023, and they haven't won in Detroit since 2021, which was also the last time they won a Thanksgiving game.

In their two games a season ago, the Lions won big at Ford Field 52 to 21 in Week 2, then won on a last-second field goal in the season finale 19 to 16 at Soldier Field. So Bears head coach Ben Johnson is still looking for his first win against his former team, and you know that will be a big part of the narrative leading up to the game.

The Bears didn't cover today's 11.5-point spread against the New Orleans Saints, but they jumped out to a 16-0 lead, then held on and coasted to a 21 to 13 win. The Bears have won five in a row, and they never trailed once in any of those games.

The Lions are on a hot streak of their own, winning their sixth straight today, 31 to 21 against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. Running back Jahmyr Gibbs is having an outstanding year for the Lions and is among the leading candidates for the NFL Offensive Player of the Year Award, but he's just one of several weapons Chicago's defense will need to contend with.
