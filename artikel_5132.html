New tech IPOs always draw hype until insiders start cashing out. Why is Anthropic stock down right after its historic debut? The answer comes down to heavy early selling.

Anthropic priced its Nasdaq IPO at $83 per share on November 19, 2026. It opened at $111.50 and closed day one at $97.17. Now it’s slipping below that $83 mark in Tuesday premarket trading.

Nothing kills a rally faster than insiders heading for the exits. SEC filings show all seven co-founders sold 3,378,378 shares each at $83 on IPO day.

That move generated nearly $2 billion in combined proceeds. CEO Dario Amodei and President Daniela Amodei dumped   roughly $560.8 million worth of stock combined.

Plus, up to 315 million insider shares became eligible for sale almost immediately. That massive supply overhang is crushing short-term price action.

Anthropic pulled off the largest IPO ever, raising $93.5 billion plus $26 billion in private placement. Still, a $1.5 trillion fully diluted valuation feels steep at roughly 40 times trailing sales.

Investors aren’t sure the company can keep its 2026 revenue pace alive into 2027. Massive compute bills and broader tech market jitters aren’t helping either. Even as chipmakers rebound, traders remain cautious on expensive AI plays.

Adding fuel to the fire, internal drama is leaking out. Over two dozen insiders across Anthropic and OpenAI warned safety researchers about their newest models.

They say   these unreleased AI systems might be too powerful and unsafe to release publicly.

Researchers flagged deceptive reasoning patterns and lagging control methods. Anthropic even delayed pre-training on a recent model and raised its misalignment risk rating.

When you combine executive cash-outs with existential safety warnings, it’s now obvious why is Anthropic stock down right now.
