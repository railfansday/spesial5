Stellar (XLM) has seen a 1.63% price increase over the last 24 hours, recovering to its current price of $0.1295.

As data from DefiLlama suggests, Stellar had around $4.3 billion in active tokenized real-world assets as of Nov. 27. Trading on the network’s DEXs was going the other way, down 22% on the week, with 24-hour volume barely reaching $661,000. Stellar already has billions in tokenized assets on the network.

The Stellar (XLM) price appears to have stabilized at its previous support region between $0.12-$0.13. Although there are some signs of recovery, it has yet to reverse the broader downtrend that saw the token price fall from ~$0.23 in October to its current trading price of $0.1295.

All major moving averages are currently trading above the token's price. Resistance can be seen at $0.1299, $0.1314 with a larger resistance/sell wall at $0.1330. Support can be seen at $0.1291, $0.1277. Should this support fail to hold, Stellar price may see further selling. It will be interesting to see if Stellar can reverse its bearish trend or continue to trade sideways/downtrend.

Stellar trading activity has seen a 23.48% decrease in spot trading volume over the last 24 hours, per CoinGlass, trading at $23.23 million. Its futures volume has also seen a decrease of 35.21%, trading at $90.27 million. Open interest has seen a 1.84% decrease, trading at $145.65 million.

Its funding rate is slightly positive at 0.0005%, indicating that some bullishness still exists despite the decrease in trading volume/interest. Per DefiLlama, its decentralized finance (DeFi) trading volume has seen a decrease of 22% week-over-week. Its 24-hour DEX volume is currently at ~$660,804.

Its total value locked (TVL) is at $182.68 million as of Nov. 27, up 0.88% in the last 24 hours. Its stablecoin market cap is at $427.79 million, down 0.71% over the last 7 days.

It has 44,621 active addresses and its 24-hour chain fees are at $707. Overall, it appears that Stellar's network usage has seen a decrease in some aspects. With decreasing trading activity/volume, it will be interesting to see if Stellar's price can sustain its current recovery.
