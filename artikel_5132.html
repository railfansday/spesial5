A notable positioning imbalance has been spotted in the XRP market, leaving leveraged bulls facing a difficult question: add to their positions or fold as the price remains below their entry level?

According to Coinbase Markets, a significant cluster of leveraged XRP long positions sits below their entry prices, putting them at risk of liquidation.In an X post, Coinbase Markets observed that the largest cluster of XRP net long positions was opened around $0.982, while XRP's spot price stood at nearly $0.923. This leaves the cryptocurrency roughly 6% below the level at which the largest concentration of long positions entered the market.

The biggest cluster of $XRP net longs was opened at $0.982. Spot: $0.923, about 6% below them. Nearly all net long notional sits above the current price. Almost every long is underwater. Add or fold? https://t.co/g6KdSFkeQW

Coinbase noted that nearly all net long notional is concentrated above the current spot price, meaning almost every position in the highlighted cluster is currently underwater.

A recovery toward the $0.982 level could offer underwater traders an opportunity to reduce losses or return closer to breakeven.

The crypto market is facing fresh selling pressure early Wednesday, reversing a rise at the start of the week, with XRP trading in the red. Attention now turns to Wednesday’s US GDP and core PCE inflation data ahead of the Thanksgiving break, with the December Fed decision drawing closer.

Testnet and Devnet are scheduled for a reset on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, 5–6 PM PT to improve full history server stability. The resets affect Testnet and Devnet only. Other networks, including Mainnet, will continue to operate as usual.

The resets will delete all ledger data in Testnet and Devnet, including all accounts, transactions, balances, settings, offers, AMMs, escrows, and other data. Testnet will have its Amendment statuses restored to match Mainnet's.

15 million agentic payments have settled on the XRP Ledger over the past year, and volume is still climbing every week. Agents settle in both XRP and RLUSD on the rail through the x402 facilitator that t54 built and runs on XRPL.

XRP Ledger Foundation, together with Peersyst, has launched a new network monitoring tool for the XRP Ledger. XRPL version 3.5.0 is scheduled for release on December 3. This release contains only improvements and fixes, including a permanent fix for occasional slow ledger close times.
