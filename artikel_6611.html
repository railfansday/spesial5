Just Browsing: The H&M Studio Styles I’m Backing for Party Season and Beyond

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

With so much fashion existing online, there’s something refreshing about trying pieces on in store. Enter Just Browsing: We’re taking you into the changing room as we preview the newest collections from some of our favourite high-street brands and contemporary labels. We’ll get into fabric and quality, fit and feel, and so much more as we make our way through the shop floor. Happy scrolling!

H&M Studio has arrived just in time for party season, and there’s plenty to get excited by in this year’s installment. The latest collection is filled with pieces that’ll see you through any event in style: be it after-work drinks or a Christmas dinner with friends, but there are also options that’ll fit seamlessly into your everyday wardrobe.

“Personality dressing” has been a hot topic in fashion circles this season; from the latest runways to the street style galleries, there’s been an increase in appetite for outfits that feel unique. It’s all about finding unexpected ways to wear familiar pieces.

Take the collection’s accessories: the tasseled scarf and bejewelled, brooch-like clips could be introduced to an otherwise ordinary outfit to add a sense of individuality. Whether worn with a white tank and baggy jeans, or on a more elaborate evening look, these personal flourishes need not be saved for special occasions.

Over the years, the H&M’s Studio collections have become a reliable source of great outerwear, and this season, a cropped bomber and oversized, faux-shearling jacket were some of the first looks to catch my eye. I’m always on the lookout for new additions to my jacket-heavy winter wardrobe, and while both feel fresh for this season, they’re also pieces I can imagine wearing for many years to come.

Elsewhere, there are plenty of timeless pieces that lend themselves to a new-season switch-up. A statement skirt, for example, can be updated with a slouchy jumper and satin ballet flats; likewise, a tailored blazer can feel just as impactful styled with something understated as it can with a dazzling dress.

Scroll down to see and shop the H&M Studio styles I’m wearing now and into 2027.

The collection’s accessories provide endless styling potential. Tie this weighty tassel scarf around the waist to elevate even the most basic party-season outfit.

The appliqué detailing on this statement skirt really sings when paired with an oversized shirt. I’ve gone for an undone approach, finished with some sleek heels. Alternatively, for a more polished finish, try styling it with the velvet scarf, cummerbund-style.

If your RSVPs have a more strict dress-code, H&M’s twist on a classic tux is an instant win. I experimented with uneven collar pops and brooch embellishments to add personality for the season ahead.

After several seasons of oversized silhouettes, fitted cuts are working their way back into our wardrobes, and this textured zip top is an easy way to dip your toe into the trend. The peplum shape means it sits nicely over a pair of jeans, then the fluffy-collared bomber offers a nice cropped finishing touch.

No holiday collection is complete without a dress that will see you through multiple events. This LBD with a twist–featuring suit-like tailoring and pockets–ticks all the boxes.

Every event outfit needs a warm, oversized top layer, and H&M Studio’s shearling style will be the perfect finishing touch to your party season outfits. This particular style gets bonus points for being reversible, transforming into a fur coat when worn the other way around.
