Hoor Al Qasimi Named Curator of 2028 Venice Biennale

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Sheikha Hoor Al Qasimi has been named curator of the 2028 edition of the Venice Biennale, the world’s biggest recurring art exhibition running from April 8 to November 26, 2028. Al Qasimi is also president and director of the Sharjah Art Foundation, a member of the United Arab Emirates’s Sharjawi royal family and president of the Sharjah Museums Authority.

The board of La Biennale di Venezia said it met on November 20 to pick Al Qasimi on the recommendation of president Pietrangelo Buttafuoco. Al Qasimi will be tasked with curating the 62nd edition of the exhibition and will take on the title of director of the visual arts department. “La Biennale di Venezia is a place where the world converges with art through its diverse visions. Throughout her career, Hoor Al Qasimi has succeeded in creating a caravanserai of geographies and the Universal, giving voice to stories and sensibilities shaped by research and the unexpected,” said Buttafuoco in a statement on Thursday. “Her work brings together a profound attentiveness to deep-rooted memory and its legacies, an open perspective on the intricate complexities of the present, and a rare ability to listen to places and their people. I also recognize in her a quality of balance and composure, namely the vibrant presence of mīzān (inner harmony), which finds its strength in relationships.”

The taking of an art biennale is nothing new to Al Qasimi. In 2024, she was appointed by the Biennale of Sydney as the artistic director of the exhibition’s 25th edition, which ran from March 7 to June 8, 2026. However, the Venice Biennale is of a larger scale on an international stage. “The international art exhibition of La Biennale di Venezia has been an important platform for many of us around the world. I first visited the 50th edition in 2003 having recently stepped into my curatorial role at Sharjah Biennial,” said Al Qasimi in a statement on Monday. “For me, La Biennale continues to be a critical space to engage with the work of influential artists and curators. I am honored to have been invited to curate this exhibition and grateful to president Pietrangelo Buttafuoco, and the board for their confidence and trust.”
