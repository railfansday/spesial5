Yaxel Lendeborg hilariously states how he will honor ‘glorious king' Steph Curry

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Yaxel Lendeborg hilariously states how he will honor 'glorious king' Steph Curry originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

It's only has taken 11 games for Yaxel Lendeborg to become a full-fledged Steph Curry truther.

On the online platform Kick, where Lendeborg streams under the name LordLendeborg, the Golden State rookie humorously stated he'd change his room's decorations to celebrate Curry.

"Y'all see the GOAT over there," Lendeborg said, pointing to a poster on his wall of Michael Jordan shooting a free throw. "I'm gonna change it to a Steph Curry poster, guys. Don't worry, my real goat. My glorious king. My one and only."

Lendeborg, like nearly all of Curry's teammates, has heaped praise on the two-time MVP since getting on the court with him.

The 24-year-old said it would be an "honor" to watch Curry in person and added he "caught [him]self shaking [his] head, like 'wow' a couple times," after Curry scored 44 points in a five-point win over the Minnesota Timberwolves on Oct. 28.

Since getting selected with the No. 11 overall pick in the 2026 NBA Draft, Lendeborg's impact on the Warriors has been undeniable.

Lendeborg is averaging 21.4 points and 7.2 rebounds per game, shooting 52.3 percent from the field and 44.3 percent from the 3-point line, firmly positioning himself as a contender in the Rookie of the Year race.

The Michigan alumnus has rejuvenated Golden State's veteran core, with forward Draymond Green calling Lendeborg a "special player" and Curry labeling him the "full package."

The Warriors are in the midst of an eight-game winning streak that has pushed them to fourth in the Western Conference standings, and Lendeborg will continue to be a huge part of the team's success this season.
