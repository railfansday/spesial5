NFL Week 9 preview & bold predictions: can Tampa Bay stop Caleb Williams? Jordan Love vs. Drake Maye

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Nate Tice & Matt Harmon preview the biggest games of Week 9 of the NFL season. After reacting to the Baltimore Ravens trading for QB Jacoby Brissett in an effort to save their season, the guys deep dive on their three favorite games of Week 9, including the Green Bay Packers taking on the New England Patriots, the Tampa Bay Buccaneers battling the Chicago Bears (our Hoss Fight of the Week) and the Buffalo Bills taking on Carson Wentz & the Minnesota Vikings on Monday night.

Later, Nate & Matt predict a big afternoon for the San Francisco 49ers offense against the Las Vegas Raiders and give their thoughts on the Denver Broncos vs. Carolina Panthers. The guys wrap up with their Hail Mary bold predictions for the weekend featuring Justin Jefferson, Kyle Shanahan and Baker Mayfield.
