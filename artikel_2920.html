Detroit Lions' Cade Mays still not practicing in second Bucs injury update

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Detroit Lions starting center Cade Mays didn't practice again on Thursday. Mays was sidelined for each of the Lions' past two wins at the Miami Dolphins and over the New England Patriots in Munich as he nurses an ankle injury.

The 6-foot-6, 325-pound center hasn't practiced at any point over the past three weeks.

Lions head coach Dan Campbell wasn't optimistic that Mays would play against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers earlier this week.

"I don't see Cade being ready this week. There's a chance, but I'm not putting all my eggs in that basket," Campbell said of Mays on Tuesday.

In addition to Mays missing Thursday's practice, six other Lions were sidelined. That group included linebacker Joe Bachie (knee), cornerback Khalil Dorsey (ankle), defensive lineman Tyler Lacy (shoulder), cornerback Ennis Rakestraw Jr. (shoulder), cornerback D.J. Reed (hip), and running back Sione Vaki (ankle).

Safety Kerby Joseph was a full participant for the second-straight practice.
