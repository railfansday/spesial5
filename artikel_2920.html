OpenWorld (OPNW) Q4 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Real-world asset tokenization now drives most revenue as the platform scales.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

OpenWorld, Inc. (OPNW -5.24%) reported its first quarterly results following a reverse takeover, highlighting a strategic transition toward high-margin real-world asset (RWA) tokenization. Management reported that the company is shifting away from project-based activity to focus on long-term platform-oriented infrastructure and recurring revenue. The financial results were impacted by significant nonrecurring costs related to the public listing and noncash stock-based compensation, though underlying operations generated positive adjusted EBITDA. The company also confirmed it is advancing a pipeline of large-scale tokenized financing programs totaling more than $1.1 billion across the artificial intelligence, energy, and intellectual property sectors.
