49ers latest injuries update: Will Mike Evans play Sunday vs Vikings?

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The San Francisco 49ers' injury report is something to closely monitor as players become unavailable and others return to football activities for the game against the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City on Nov. 22.

The 49ers are still anticipating the return of Mike Evans, who sat out their Week 10 win against the Dallas Cowboys with right Achilles tendinitis.

Niners head coach Kyle Shanahan is hoping to have Evans back, who sat out Wednesday's practice, but his status remains "day-to-day", he said to reporters.

"Yeah, hopefully. We'll see," Shanahan said. "Can't go (Wednesday), but hopefully he'll be able to go (Thursday). I'm not sure yet but taking it day by day right now."

It's undetermined whether Evans will play, but Shanahan wanted to ensure that he traveled with the team, regardless of his game-time status.

"I haven't thought about that," Shanahan said about the possibility of Evans playing against Minnesota on Sunday. "Maybe, but he does have a chance. But, if he didn't, probably still would've brought him."

49ers linebacker Dre Greenlaw hurt his neck in their 46-40 win against the Cowboys on Nov. 15. He didn't practice Wednesday, Shanahan said, but expects him on the field on Thursday.

"Greenlaw, neck, won't practice," Shanahan said. "I think he's got a good chance to practice (Thursday). Hopefully it'll feel better though."

Greenlaw has been a complementary force for the Niners' defense. He returned to San Francisco in the offseason after spending a season with the Denver Broncos. Greenlaw has 31 tackles, 34 assisted tackles, 0.5 sacks and a forced fumble through 8 games played.

His presence is something that the 49ers have needed to help balance their rush defense, where San Francisco ranks 2nd in the NFL, allowing 82.6 rushing yards per game in the 2026 season.

At this juncture in the season, the injuries to the Niners' defensive backs are piling up.

Cornerback Renardo Green is still working his back from a lingering back injury that had him as questionable status going into Week 10 against the Cowboys.

He stuck it out, managing to get 7 tackles, but the secondary got torched by Dallas. His matchup against CeeDee Lamb went in the Cowboys' favor as Lamb added seven catches for 72 yards and a touchdown.

Shanahan hoped that Green would practice Wednesday, but he was still limited, he told reporters.

Other injuries to their coverage guys include cornerback Upton Stout who was limited in the Niners' Wednesday practice with a shoulder and neck injury, Shanahan said.

Defensive back Siran Neal has been dealing with a knee and foot injury. He was removed from the injury list and made active against the Cowboys in Week 10; however, Neal was limited during the 49ers' Wednesday practice.

The same designation went for safety Ji'Ayir Brown. Brown was limited with an ankle injury.

Shanahan said defensive lineman Khalid Kareem was also limited during their Wednesday practice.

Kareem, who played two seasons with the Atlanta Falcons, signed with the Niners just before the start of the 2026 season, reuniting with defensive coordinator Raheem Morris. He's recorded six tackles in eight games.
