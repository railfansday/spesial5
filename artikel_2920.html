Ripple’s Garlinghouse Sees Crypto Assets Diverging

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse expects crypto assets to increasingly diverge in performance, with utility and real demand separating winners from more speculative tokens. He remains bullish on the industry despite a difficult year for the broader crypto market. He speaks with Haslinda Amin from the sidelines of 'Singapore Fintech Festival 2026'. (Source: Bloomberg)
