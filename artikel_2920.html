Chiefs News: 3 Chiefs players labeled as ‘Day-to-Day’

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Fans of the Kansas City Chiefs do not take the injury status of "day-to-day" at face value. It's been that way since safety Eric Berry held that status for what felt like the entire 2018 season.

So it's hard to take much away from head coach Andy Reid labeling three Chiefs players — quarterback Patrick Mahomes (elbow), wide receiver Xavier Worthy (hamstring) and right tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) — as "day-to-day" during his press conference on Monday. Still, it's an update.

Chiefs QB Patrick Mahomes day-to-day with elbow injury | ESPN

Chiefs coach Andy Reid said Monday that quarterback Patrick Mahomes is considered day-to-day with his right elbow injury, an injury he sustained in Sunday's win over the New York Jets. Reid shared that Mahomes was in the Chiefs' training facility Monday morning to receive treatment for his injury.

Following the game, an MRI scan didn't reveal any major injury to Mahomes' elbow, sources told NFL Network's Ian Rapoport.

"It was more positive news than negative news on Patrick," Reid said Monday. "It looks like it's just a matter of [getting] some rehabbing in there and getting rid of some of the soreness. We'll just see where it goes from there. He's in good spirits."

Former Chiefs player was roasted for his take on kicker Harrison Butker | The Kansas City Star

At 11:21 a.m. on Sunday, roughly 40 minutes before kickoff of the Jets game, ex-Chiefs wideout Gehrig Dieter shared this message on X: "I'm calling it now. Butker going to go on a generational run. He won't miss another kick the entire season."

Ninety minutes later, that generational run ended before it began as Butker missed a 51-yard attempt — after missing from 56 before a penalty gave him another shot. That led fans to believe his tenure with the Chiefs was nearly over, although coach Andy Reid said after the game he wasn't ready to announce a change.
