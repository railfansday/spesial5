One of the paradoxes of being human is that often the things that are most painful to us in life are also the things that are most meaningful to us. When we engage in purposeful activities like parenting or owning a pet or service work or helping others, it often brings a lot of discomfort.

In this practice, we’re going to explore something that is painful to you and look underneath to see what it means about what you care about.
