Brazil football match sets record with 51 red cards after massive brawl involving chairs & stretchers

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

A football match in Brazil descended into absolute chaos after a massive brawl involving players, coaches, and even ball boys resulted in a staggering 51 red cards, with police later called to the stadium.

The incident occurred on November 1 during an Under-20 championship semifinal between Uberlândia and Mamoré at the Parque do Sabiá Stadium in Minas Gerais.

Mamoré was leading 3-1 deep into stoppage time when a goal celebration sparked a confrontation that quickly spiraled out of control.

According to Brazilian outlet O Tempo, Mamoré player Davi Fernandes Bittencourt made provocative gestures and shouted insults toward the opposing bench.

Uberlândia’s goalkeeping coach, Paulo Sérgio Dias dos Santos, then reportedly stormed onto the field and pushed Bittencourt, triggering an all-out brawl that looked more like something out of a hockey game than a football match.

Players and coaching staff began exchanging punches and kicks, with footage showing chairs and even stretchers being thrown during the violence.

The referee’s report also claimed that six ball boys joined the fighting, attacking players and staff with punches, kicks, and thrown objects.

Referee Fernando Patrick Drosghic Modesto ultimately abandoned the match, recording 48 additional dismissals in his official report because the chaos prevented him from showing the cards on the field.

As ge.globo reported, three players had already been sent off before the brawl, bringing the final total to 51.

Of those dismissed, 43 were players and eight were members of the coaching staffs.

The trouble didn’t end on the pitch, either.

Military police were called after the match following reports of vandalism in the visiting team’s locker room.

Officers discovered approximately 14 damaged doors, along with broken sinks, toilets, showers, electrical installations, and furniture. Two hot tubs were also reportedly damaged. No arrests were made.

Remarkably, the match had already been suspended for around 40 minutes earlier in the second half after Uberlândia officials confronted the referee over another dismissal. The official subsequently reported receiving threats.

The 51 dismissals comfortably surpass the previous Guinness World Record of 36 red cards, set in 2011 during an Argentine fifth-division match between Atlético Claypole and Victoriano Arenas.

However, Guinness has not yet officially updated its record to recognize the Brazilian incident.

It’s also not the first time football has produced bizarre scenes this year. In March, masked fans sabotaged a VAR monitor during a German match, while streamer IShowSpeed was forced to leave an Algerian football game after spectators began throwing objects at him.

Uberlândia and Mamoré are scheduled to meet again on November 8, although the unprecedented number of dismissals could create major complications for both teams.
