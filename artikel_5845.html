China's Xi surprises Trump at tarmac, reciprocating gesture shown months ago

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

President Donald Trump lands in Shenzhen, China, for the APEC summit, where Chinese President Xi Jinping greets him on the tarmac with an elaborate welcome ceremony. Bill Melugin reports on the high-stakes arrival and national security analyst Dr. Rebecca Grant analyzes the diplomatic significance of the meeting.

President Donald Trump said he expected a special reception from China's Xi Jinping, and Wednesday morning's reception did not disappoint.

After Trump's in-person welcome of Xi at Joint Base Andrews in September, Xi personally greeted Trump at Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport, a rare airport welcome ahead of a three-way meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin.

Putin, who has met with Xi dozens of times and arrived at the same Shenzhen airport earlier Wednesday, did not receive a personal greeting from the Chinese leader. Instead, senior Chinese Communist Party advisor Cai Qi welcomed Putin upon arrival.

Some Democratic and Republican lawmakers had criticized Trump over that September airport greeting, but it has brought Xi to reciprocate.

The Trump greeting marked a sharp contrast with Trump's previous visit to China in May, when Chinese Vice President Han Zheng met him at the airport in Beijing. Xi, who has led China for more than a decade, has rarely — if ever — appeared at an airport to welcome a visiting foreign leader, according to reports.

President Donald Trump speaks with China's President Xi Jinping as he arrives at Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport in Shenzhen, Guangdong Province, on Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026. (Mark Schiefelbein - Pool/Getty Images)

As Trump stepped off Air Force One, a handshake between the two leaders turned into a brief hug while military regiments and performers dressed in pink and turquoise stood at attention. A military band played the U.S. and Chinese national anthems as dancers waved flowers, pompoms and American and Chinese flags.

"China is a nation that values courtesy and has a tradition of returning goodwill with goodwill," a report in China's Xinhua read.

The leaders' exchange represents a new U.S.-China relationship of "respect, fairness, and reciprocity," Dean Wu Xinbo of Fudan University in Shanghai told The New York Times.

It showed China will "still treat him seriously and would like to continue to work with him," he added.

International Crisis Group senior analyst William Yang noted the gesture before potentially turbulent talks to come.

"It’s important to highlight the reciprocity that Xi gave to Trump by receiving him on the tarmac," Yang wrote Wednesday morning on X. "This gesture likely reflects Xi and Beijing’s desire to show respect to Trump, which they know the US President enjoys, and it is likely also an attempt to uphold the positive rapport between Xi and Trump ahead of a potentially turbulent period in U.S.-China relations in the coming months."

Trump, Xi and Putin are in Shenzhen for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit. The three leaders are scheduled to meet together Thursday on the sidelines of the economic gathering — an unprecedented three-way session.

Ahead of a one-on-one meeting with Xi on Wednesday, Putin said he expected "great potential benefits" from Thursday's three-way discussion. The session has drawn concern from officials in Ukraine, Iran and Taiwan, who worry the leaders of the world's three most powerful nations could make decisions affecting their futures without their participation.

During a briefing last week, a senior U.S. official said the three-way meeting is unlikely to produce any formal denuclearization agreement, calling it an "entry point" and "the first conversation."

Trump will meet Putin one-on-one tomorrow on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen, a U.S. official confirmed to Fox News.

Earlier Wednesday morning, Trump recapped his trip to the Philippines.

"I am now heading to China, from the Philippines, where we had a wonderful meeting on Trade, National Security, and other subjects," Trump wrote on Truth Social. "I am pleased to announce that we made a new and completely different Trade Deal with the Philippines, one that will be a true landmark and, in particular, a Deal that will be great for our Farmers and those in the business of 'Energy.'

"President Marcos is a powerful leader, and a man of high integrity and substance. It was an Honor to be with him, and his beautiful wife and son. See you in China!"
