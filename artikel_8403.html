Idea. Why? It's TEY in 12% has ~matched TBMs. Meaning I got equivalent after-tax income but remained in 15% fed back then (until other events pushed me into 25%---TSM/TISM distro + SS).

BND SEC yield: 5.03%; see: https://investor.vanguard.com/investmen ... rofile/bnd

Everyone need some bonds---you and your heirs. So if your heirs are in same or higher tax brackets, then their outcomes should be equivalent or better with VWLUX. And until then you can live on its dividends and expect to remain in 12%. You'll need a Vanguard account to buy VWLUX cheaply.
