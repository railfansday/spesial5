Kylie Cantrall Surprises Fans With Private Acoustic Set in Downtown L.A.

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The actress-singer, who broke out with her role as Red in Disney's 'Descendants' movies, took over Moxy Downtown Los Angeles to perform for fans on Tuesday night.

On the heels of her Valley Girl Problems Tour, Kylie Cantrall played a surprise intimate acoustic set in downtown Los Angeles on Tuesday night for a crowd of enthusiastic fans who sang along with all of her tunes.

Cantrall took over Moxy Downtown Los Angeles for the special event, which also celebrated her EP, Valley Girl Problems, which debuted in July. The event was part of Moxy Hotels’ ongoing music series, which brings emerging and buzzy artists together with fans for intimate experiences at Moxy properties around the world (other recent events have featured Ayra Starr in the U.K.). The actor-singer — who has starred in two of Disney’s Descendants movies, playing Red, daughter of the Queen of Hearts — noted onstage that Los Angeles was her hometown and she was “feeling the love” at Moxy DTLA that night.

After asking how many people were also at her recent tour — many in the audience were — she shared: “That show was a mix of emotions. I was so sad,” she said, and then a fan said she had cried during the show. “I made you cry? That should be a disclaimer on my website: If you’re coming to a Kylie show, there’s a chance you’re going to cry. But you’re going to shake a little ass too,” she quipped. “It’s a mix of both. That’s me in a nutshell honestly — crying and shaking ass at the same time.”

Cantrall said she was missing being on tour when Moxy Hotels reached out about the surprise pop-up L.A. show, and she said jumped at the chance to perform in the intimate venue. Addressing the fans, she continued, “Thank you guys for coming at the last minute.”

She performed four songs at the event, kicking off with “Carrie Bradshaw,” a tribute to Cantrall’s “favorite TV character,” the one played by Sarah Jessica Parker in Sex and the City. “She’s is a fashion icon; she’s kind of a mess as well, but we love her for it,” Cantrall said. (The bar at Moxy DTLA was serving special cocktails in honor of the event that included the Carrie Cosmo and the Valley Girl Martini.)

Next up was “Oversized,” from her previous EP, B.O.Y. (Deluxe). She revealed that she’s a “hoodie stealer,” joking that she had about five of her best friend’s hoodies at her house. In co-writing the song, “I was kind of inspired by this era I was in, dating and dealing with guys with hoodies. … I definitely still have two of (my ex’s) hoodies that I never gave back, but he never asked for them back. And they look better on me anyway,” she joked.

“You know we’re gonna get in our feels for a minute,” she said, introducing the next song, the emotional “Space Between” from Valley Girl Problems. “Now that I’ve been home, I’ve been writing the next album … but I haven’t written any ballads on it so far. That just goes to show you we’re healed. We’re healing slowly day by day. This song is very special to me. If you’re going through something right now, or maybe you’re healing through a situation, maybe even this song helped you heal a little bit.” After, a fan told her that song always makes her cry, to which Cantrall responded that it makes her emotional as well. She also gave the crowd a note of encouragement: “You are so entitled to feeling whatever you’re feeling … and I think the best thing we can do as humans is just try to embrace our imperfections and let that shit go, whether it’s writing it down, talking to someone, therapy, writing music, creating art. … You are never alone.” (Here she quipped that it was her first 18-and-older show, so she didn’t have to censor herself as when she was on tour.)

Cantrall closed the show with “Elastic,” which she said was so frequently requested during her tour that she wound up adding it to her setlist. She wrapped with an a capella sing-along to a few verses of “Valley Girl Problems” to the delight of those inside Moxy DTLA.

Indeed, many of those in attendance sang along with her songs throughout the performance, and Cantrall engaged in some fun banter with the fans. Afterward, she asked the crowd to take a group photo before graciously signing autographs and taking selfies with fans’ phones.

Cantrall recently hit 15 cities across North America as part of her first-ever headlining tour, which kicked off Sept. 25 in Minneapolis and wrapped up Oct. 22 in Los Angeles, her hometown. Next, she’s set to appear on three dates of the Jingle Ball tour (Dec. 1 in Dallas, Dec. 14 in Washington, D.C. and Dec. 15 in Philadelphia). In summer 2025, she toured North America as part of Disney’s Descendants/Zombies: Worlds Collide Tour.

Valley Girl Problems, released via Republic Records, follows on the heels of her debut EP, B.O.Y. The new nine-track EP was produced by Grammy-winning hitmaker Tricky Stewart (Rihanna, Tyla) and written alongside acclaimed songwriter Tayla Parx.

Moxy Hotels says that its music series aims to create a space where Gen Z can be connected with artists, events and moments shaping culture.
