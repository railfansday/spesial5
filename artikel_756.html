Sandra Bullock Shares Rare Photos of Kids During Disneyland Trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Sandra Bullock shared an inside look at a family trip to Disneyland with her kids Louis, 16, and Laila, 13: “Remind yourself of what it feels like to be a kid.”

Sandra Bullock is making sweet memories with her family.

The Practical Magic star shared a rare look at a magical outing with her kids Louis, 16, and Laila, 13, to Disneyland Resort in Anaheim, Calif., with Bullock posting photos and videos of her family enjoying amusement park rides, treats and holiday fun.

“Sometimes you just need to stop worrying and remind yourself of what it feels like to be a kid,” the Oscar winner wrote in a Nov. 22 Instagram post. “FYI There are no calories in ice cream if you ride the Guardians of the Galaxy ride and don’t cry.”

For the pixie-perfect day at Disneyland Park and Disneyland California Adventure, Bullock was joined by longtime pal Josh Holloway, his wife Yessica Holloway and their son Hunter, 12.

Tagging the couple in her post, the 62-year-old wrote “family and friends,” adding a heart emoji.

But the Proposal actress proved that her kids were at the front of her mind. After all, the first shot in the carousel featured the teens walking down Hollywood Boulevard in California Adventure.

And it’s safe to say Bullock and her loved ones were having a blast at the Happiest Place on Earth as she shared pics and videos of the group riding Radiator Springs Racers, Guardians of the Galaxy—Mission BREAKOUT! and Space Mountain.

The Blind Side actress also included an epic shot of her riding the rollercoaster with her kids, with Laila looking over at her mom with a grin as they rode in the front of the rocket.

Another photo in the carousel featured Bullock taking a selfie of her group from behind as they walked in the theme park, with the Miss Congeniality star sporting a Disneyland baseball cap.

And since a trip to Disneyland isn’t complete without a tasty treat, Bullock also shared a pic with a delicious cup of ice cream.

With Thanksgiving on the horizon, the Speed actress is feeling grateful.

Resharing photos from her post on her Instagram Stories, Bullock wrote, “Things I’m thankful for: family,” adding in follow-up slides that she's also thankful for “friends,” “life,” “ice cream” and ultimately, “EVERYTHING.”
