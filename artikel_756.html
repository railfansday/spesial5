Travis Kelce ‘Can’t Wait’ to Make New Traditions With Taylor Swift on His First Christmas ‘As a Married Man’

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The athlete also shouted out his favorite holiday treat mom Donna Kelce makes.

Travis Kelce is ready for his first Christmas as a newlywed. The Kansas City Chiefs tight end recently gushed about being pumped to spend the holidays with Taylor Swift, telling WWD in an article published Thursday (Nov. 5), “I start celebrating as that tree goes up.”

“I’m excited for my first holiday as a married man,” Kelce also said. “I love all the old traditions and can’t wait to start some new ones.”

The football player stars in Tommy Hilfiger’s holiday campaign alongside JISOO of BLACKPINK, New York Knicks player Josh Hart and more celebs. The group will continue sharing festive tidbits — while modeling the brand’s cozy winter line — in videos rolling out on social media, including one posted Thursday, in which Kelce says his “mother baking blueberry muffins” is one of his favorite Christmas traditions.

The New Heights podcaster is now married to another world class baker, tying the knot with Swift in July. Kelce has raved about her homemade treats multiple times, and we’re betting there will be at least one fresh loaf of sourdough on the Swift-Kelce table this year.

The 14-time Grammy winner has also whipped up her fair share of Christmas songs over the years, releasing a holiday EP in 2007 that featured original songs “Christmases When You Were Mine” and “Christmas Must Be Something More.” In 2019, she dropped seasonal single “Christmas Tree Farm,” reaching No. 59 on the Billboard Hot 100.

Kelce has been a Tommy Hilfiger partner since early September, when he first starred in a commercial that featured an adorable cameo from his and Swift’s pet dog, Wendy.
