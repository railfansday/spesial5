Gisele Bundchen Shares Rare Glimpse at 3 Kids and Husband on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Gisele Bündchen revealed her heart is full as she shared rare photos of her loved ones in honor of Thanksgiving.

“The amount of goodness we experience in life depends on our ability to notice it,” Bündchen, 46, wrote via Instagram on Thursday, November 26, sharing a glimpse at her life with her husband, Joaquim Valente, and her three children.

The supermodel noted, “Gratitude is contagious, and the more we can appreciate the beauty around us, the more joy we bring into our lives and the lives of others.”

Bündchen also sent a message to her followers, writing, “May we always find something to be grateful for, not just today, but every day. Happy Thanksgiving! Sending you all so much love. 🤍.”

In celebration of the U.S. holiday, Bündchen posted never-before-seen pictures of her family, including son Benjamin, 16, and daughter Vivian, 13, whom she shares with ex-husband Tom Brady. Bündchen and Valente’s 21-month-old son was also spotted in several photos.

In one snap, Bündchen and Valente, 39, shared a sweet moment while cuddling their baby boy on the beach.

The Lessons: My Path to a Meaningful Life author threw her youngest child up into the air while walking on the sand in a second picture. The toddler hugged his big sister in a third snap, as Vivian chatted with Ben on the steps of what appeared to be their home.

Among scenic sunset photos and mountain pictures, Bündchen sprinkled in a solo yoga snap and one of her laughing while spending time with her dog.

Vivian and Ben were also highlighted in separate photos with their baby brother, and the little man was seen feeding a bunny a carrot in another.

The family-filled upload comes just weeks after the Victoria’s Secret model celebrated Valente’s birthday with a heartfelt social media tribute.

“Today a great man was born, @joaquimvalente,” Bündchen wrote via Instagram on November 7, sharing a cozy picture of the lovebirds. “How lucky I am. I love you so much!”

Us confirmed in December 2025 that Bündchen and Valente had been granted a marriage license in Miami. Days later, news broke that the couple tied the knot after dating two years.

Their nuptials took place 10 months after they welcomed their first child together, whose name they have chosen to keep private.

Prior to her relationship with Valente, Bündchen was married to retired NFL quarterback Brady, 49, for 13 years. The exes, who share Ben and Vivian, announced their split in October 2022, finalizing their divorce the same month.

Bündchen is also the stepmother of Brady’s eldest son, Jack, 19, whom he shares with ex-girlfriend Bridget Moynahan.
