Amid rising demand for crypto assets, top cryptocurrencies with strong fundamentals gained bullish traction on Monday, November 9, 2026, while those with bearish structures plunged further.

As of press time, the top-performing crypto asset over the past 24 hours, among the leading 100 cryptocurrencies by market capitalization, was Pieverse (PIEVERSE), a Web3 payment compliance infrastructure project. PIEVERSE, with a market capitalization of $597.5 million and a 24-hour trading volume of nearly $80 million, had surged 48.7%, thereby trading at $2.21.

Top crypto gainers today in the top 100 cryptocurrencies by market capitalization. Source: CoinGecko

Jupiter (JUP), the governance token for a decentralized exchange (DEX) aggregator on the Solana (SOL) network, had spiked 6.9% over the past 24 hours to $0.2553. Other top-performing cryptocurrencies today were Ether.fi (ETHFI), Arbitrum (ARB), and Avalanche (AVAX).

Meanwhile, the most bullish memecoin among the top 100 cryptocurrencies by market capitalization was the frog-themed Pepe (PEPE).

Over the past 24 hours, Rain (RAIN), a decentralized prediction markets protocol, had fallen by 11.5% to $0.0046 at the time of reporting. Pearl (PEARL), a Layer 1 blockchain powered by the Proof-of-Useful-Work (PoUW) consensus mechanism, declined 12.4% to trade at $1.54.

Top crypto losers today in the top 100 cryptocurrencies by market capitalization. Source: CoinGecko

Bitway (BTW) and OKB (OKB) had plunged by roughly 10.1% and 5.6%, respectively.
