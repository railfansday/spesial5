Celtic set to receive 'letter from SFA' after latest fan controversy - Keith Wyness

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Celtic will not face punishment after their fans interrupted a minute's silence ahead of their home game against Kilmarnock last weekend.

That is according to former Aberdeen chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that no rules were broken as part of the protest.

Referee John Beaton was forced to cut the Remembrance Day commemoration short after around 12 seconds, with sections of the Parkhead crowd whistling and jeering.

However, Wyness believes that Celtic's sanction is not likely to go beyond a slap on the wrist from the SFA and the SPFL.

The issues at Celtic Park were the latest in a list of long-standing incidents regarding the Bhoys' fans and their response to Remembrance Day tributes.

Their Green Brigade ultras group also notably opted to stay out of the stadium while the pre-match silence took place.

Aberdeen's former chief Wyness - who served as CEO at Pittodrie between 2000 and 2004 and now runs a consultancy business advising elite clubs - believes that the SFA don't have the means to punish Celtic for the incident.

The Scottish businessman pointed out that it is a "difficult situation" in a political sense, due to the reputation of the British Army among those of Irish descent.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness outlined that Celtic can only expect to receive a letter of warning from the SFA.

"No, I don't think there'll be any punishment. I don't think the rules would prescribe anything for that," he said.

"I think breaking up a minute's silence is nothing that I've seen in the rule books that you can actually penalise. There may be a letter from the SFA or something like that, but there'll be no punishment.

"It's a difficult situation politically. You'd hope that there would be some decency shown to respect what some people would take as a very important moment.

"But there are certain parts of the fans, the whole situation, that are still very toxic."

Former Tottenham scout and Norwegian football expert Bryan King told Football Insider earlier this week that striker Kasper Hogh is "lost" at Celtic at the moment.

The club record buy hasn't scored since way back in August, and saw an effort ruled out during the win over Kilmarnock last weekend.

King believes that the service into the 25-year-old isn't good enough at the moment, and feels that it is damaging his confidence.

The Scandinavian expert believes that Hogh can still come good at Celtic Park, but needs to find a way of getting himself back into top form.
