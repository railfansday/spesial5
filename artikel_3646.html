Marathon’s Trailers Are Still The Best

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Marathon has a new cinematic ahead of its big Symbiosis update on December 8. The trailer is another masterclass in cool shit. It’s a reminder of how sound and compelling the underlying world building and presentation of the game has been despite all of its ups and downs.

The 90-second cinematic introduces the game’s newest character class: Bishop. Imagine a cyberpunk Samurai toting around a pump action shotgun with jetpacks installed on its ankles. The trailer is meant to show off the deadly precision and ferocity of the offense-minded subclass, but its Y2K riff on John Wick is also just really neat in its own right.
