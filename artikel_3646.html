Mario Kart World Switch 2 Bundle Black Friday Deal Brings The Price Back To 2025 Levels

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Nintendo has announced its 2026 Black Friday deals, with a Mario Kart World Switch 2 hardware bundle as the centerpiece for $500.

The Switch 2 launched in 2025 with a similar bundle for the same price, but it was discontinued at the end of the year. It later returned, but at a higher price point--$550. Now, the bundle is on "sale" again, though it's the same price as it was in 2025 before the price hikes. In addition to a digital copy of the game and the console, it comes with a three-month Nintendo Switch Online membership.

The bundle goes on sale November 22 and will be available through December 5. It's not the only hardware bundle that people can buy, as Nintendo also offers a system with a digital copy of Nintendo Switch Sports Resort and three months of Switch Online for $530. This bundle is not on sale for Black Friday, though.

Additionally, for just one week--November 22-29--Nintendo will offer games like Donkey Kong Bananza and Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2 for $20 off.

What's more, Nintendo announced that the Nintendo eShop and the Nintendo Store will offer a range of deals November 19-December 2, but it hasn’t yet detailed what you can expect.

Black Friday used to be just one day of deals--the day after Thanksgiving in the US--but now it's an entire season of savings. Keep checking back with GameSpot for more Black Friday deals in the days ahead.
