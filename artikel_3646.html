Man Allegedly Shot Sister to Death After Their Mom Died in Hospice Care

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Police believe that a Mississippi man fatally shot his sister after their mother apparently died of natural causes while in hospice care at their home.

Richard Timothy Ward was charged with three counts of attempted murder on Monday, November 16, after he shot at three deputies with the Escambia County Sheriff’s Office in Pensacola, Florida.

Officers in Long Beach, Mississippi, also said that Ward, 64, is the prime suspect in the killing of his sister, Kimberly Hernandez. However, he has not yet been charged with her murder.

Long Beach police reported to a home on the morning of Friday, November 13, after they received a report of a shooting after a hospice worker found two dead women in the home.

Hernandez, 63, and her mother, Doris Crimmins, were later identified as the victims, according to WLOX.

The coroner said that Hernandez was fatally shot, while Crimmins, 84, apparently died of natural causes while she was in hospice care. An autopsy is expected to be performed at a later date, which will determine Crimmins’ official cause of death. Both Hernandez and Crimmins lived at the home where they were found dead.

Ward was quickly named a suspect in the case and was tracked to the Sweet Dreams Inn in Pensacola, Florida, which is about 130 miles from where his mother and sister died.

Escambia County Sheriff Chip W. Simmons said in a video shared via Facebook that deputies located and approached Ward in his hotel room on Friday afternoon.

As he was approached, Ward allegedly pulled out a gun and refused to put it down despite police orders.

Simmons said that Ward then fired a shot at the deputies, and the deputies responded by opening fire on Ward. Ward was then taken to the hospital for gunshot wounds sustained in the shootout, and he has since been released and taken into custody.

Ward is currently being held at the Escambia County Jail without bond. It’s not currently clear if Ward has entered a plea or retained legal counsel following his arrest.

Authorities said that as he remains behind bars on the attempted murder charge, investigators in Mississippi are working to file charges against Ward for shooting his sister.

Despite naming him as a suspect, authorities have not disclosed a potential motive in why they believe Ward killed his sister and shot at the officers.

The Escambia County Sheriff’s Office and Long Beach City Police Department did not immediately respond to Us’ request for comment regarding updates in the case.
