‘An honour’: Pochettino up for challenge of leading Manchester United revival

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

New head coach faces tough opening match at Anfield

‘It’s a big challenge but at the same time it’s adrenaline’

Mauricio Pochettino said it was “an honour” and “a privilege” to be the new Manchester United head coach after arriving in England on Wednesday.

Pochettino flew from Cincinnati to Manchester overnight after overseeing the USA beating Haiti in his final game in charge. Members of Pochettino’s staff landed earlier in the week to begin work at Carrington, laying the foundations as United seek to turn their season around. His right-hand man, Jesús Pérez, the goalkeeping coach Toni Jiménez and the fitness coach Sebastiano Pochettino, who continues to work with his father, have already taken training with those not on international duty.

Those away with their national teams will return to the club on Thursday, when they are expected to meet Pochettino for the first time and hold meetings. Conversations have also been held with various departments within the club, as Pochettino sought to learn about the squad’s health as he seeks areas of improvement.

Pochettino’s first challenge will be at Anfield on Sunday, when 18th-placed United take on Liverpool. The 54-year-old has his eyes set on rattling Manchester City after being told on TNT Sports that “Manchester’s blue and always will be blue” by DaMarcus Beasley, who once had a loan spell with City. Pochettino responded: “No way. You will see. Give me time … give me time and that is going to start to change.”

Speaking about joining United, Pochettino said: “It’s a big challenge but at the same time, it’s adrenaline that you want to feel because I think we are going to have a different job. But I think one of the things that is important is that we have the experience to deal with that, and I think we need to add our common sense and the way we are going to approach all the things.

“We know that the situation is not a situation that a club like Manchester United deserves,” Pochettino added. “The fanbase is amazing and after we signed here in America, of every 10 people I crossed in the street, nine are Manchester United fans. It’s amazing.”
