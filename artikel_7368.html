TikTok highlights 2026 holiday trends

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

Popular hashtags including #diychristmas and #pinkchristmas speak to a shift away from last year’s “Ralph Lauren Christmas” toward a more whimsical season.

TikTok has shared an overview of key holiday trends based on in-app activity, which could help marketers align with the popular shifts of the season. Which could be especially helpful given the role that TikTok now plays in defining popular culture.

Indeed, TikTok is now a critical influencer of music and movie trends, which also makes it a valuable guide on broader cultural shifts.

According to TikTok, “Whimsy Christmas” is a key trend this year. The trend offers a colorful, DIY-based take on the season.

As per TikTok: “Posts with #diychristmas are up over 80%, and TikTok users in the US are almost twice as likely as non-users to make their own decorations this season.”

TikTok also said that “Cozy Christmas” is another key trend centered around holiday movie nights, quiet winter evenings and cozy bedding. Posts with #cozychristmas are up over 170%.

Pink Christmas, which the app also calls “Pinksmas” is another trend of note.

“Posts with #pinkchristmas are up over 210%, illustrating the creativity behind the TikTok community driving interest in a ‘girly’ Christmas.”

Underlining these broader trends, TikTok added:

Some of these seasonal trends are to be expected, but the growth data could highlight opportunities for tie-ins to boost brand promotions.

TikTok also said that shopping-specific trends like #stockingstufferideas and #beautyadventcalendar are up significantly this month.
