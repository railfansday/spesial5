Tatyana Ali Details Her Body Transformation & Weight Loss From DWTS

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

As Dancing With the Stars contestant Tatyana Ali has competed alongside pro partner Jan Ravnik, she’s now feeling “so free” inside her body and revealed she has lost weight throughout the competition.

Tatyana Ali is feeling the groove throughout her entire body.

After 10 weeks of rigorous training and performances on Dancing With the Stars with her pro dance partner Jan Ravnik, the Fresh Prince of Bel Air alum revealed she has undergone a “transformative” change, even saying cha cha to a few pounds.

“I was a different size when I came here,” Ali told Us Weekly in an interview published Nov. 18. “I liked my body before I got here, but it feels like I have—I don’t know how to describe it—I have room in my body.”

In fact, the 47-year-old added, “I feel so free inside my body. Freer than I felt in a long time. Like, there’s room in here.”

Still, Ali—mom to kids Edward Aszard, 10, and Alejandro Vaughn, 7, with husband Vaugh Rasberry—emphasized that she “loved” her body before the competition.

“She helped me bring my babies into this world,” she added of her body. “She went through a lot of stuff [and] trauma. She got me through it.”

Now, as Ali and Ravnik prepare to waltz their way into the winners’ circle during the season 35 finale Nov. 24, the Abbott Elementary actress has words of encouragement for fellow moms who want to find the same freedom in their bodies that she has found.

“My advice for them is dance,” she shared. “Get your girls together and your mom friends because we’re not supposed to be momming by ourselves. I think that’s unnatural. We’re supposed to actually have a village and be doing it together. But y’all need to dance.”

Beyond the positive impact to her body, Ali has also emphasized the nourishment she has found in her friendship with Ravnik.

“You are this experience,” she told Ravnik during an interview with E! News Nov. 17. “It couldn't have been with anyone else. I can't even imagine.”
