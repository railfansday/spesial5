In 2026, the Dolphins-Bills game broadcasts on TV on CBS, while the game livestreams on Paramount+.

The NFL is known for celebrity sightings, so it’s likely there may be a number of famous actors, actresses and recording artists in attendance during the game. Artists who have been known to appear at games in the past include Buffalo Bills fans Hailee Steinfeld (QB Josh Allen’s wife), Benny the Butcher, Westside Gunn and others.

For cord-cutters, there are a few ways to watch the NFL game if you don’t have cable, especially if you want to watch for free. DirecTV has a five-day free trial, while other streaming services also offer free trials, so you can watch the game for free.

A subscription to DirecTV gets you access to live TV, local and cable channels, including CBS, at $49.99 per month for the first two months of service ($94.99 per month afterward). The service even offers a five-day free trial to watch for free, if you sign up now. It comes with access to ESPN Unlimited too, for even more ESPN networks.

There are two plans to choose from: Paramount+ Essential and Paramount+ Premium (with Showtime).

Paramount+ has the Dolphins-Bills game on CBS streaming online (for those who live in the metro Miami and Buffalo areas), as well as past seasons. If you don’t already have a Paramount+ subscription, the streaming service plan goes for $8.99 per month (or $89.99 per year). With Paramount+, you’ll get access to a wide selection of TV shows and movies, live sports, 24-hour news with CBS News and more.

Fubo is an affordable option offering a five-day free trial for new users who sign up.

Fubo starts at $63.99 for the first month ($88.99 per month afterward) with more than 230 channels, including local and cable, like CBS, that are streamable on smart TVs, smartphones, tablets and on web browsers. And with a five-day free trial, you can watch for free if you act fast and sign up now.
