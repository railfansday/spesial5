Blink-182, Pierce the Veil, Limp Bizkit, Third Eye Blind Headlining 2027 When We Were Young Festival

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Next year's event will touch down on the Las Vegas Festival Grounds on Oct. 16.

The fifth edition of the throwback When We Were Young festival will touch down at the Las Vegas Festival Grounds on Oct. 16 next year with a lineup topped by Blink-182, Pierce the Veil, Limp Bizkit and Third Eye Blind. The roster of punk, emo, pop rock and pop-punk groups will also include Circa Survive, The Used, 5 Seconds of Summer, Hot Mulligan, Sleeping with Sirens, All Time Low, Thursday, Underoath, New Found Glory, Twin Atlantic and Bayside, as well as pop singers Michelle Branch, Natasha Bedingfield and Ashlee Simpson. A presale for the 2027 event begins on Friday (Nov. 20) at 10 a.m. PT, accessible through a passcode you can access here. A general on-sale will take place later that day at 11 a.m. PT if any tickets remain after the presale.

Among the other acts taking the stage this year are: Story of the Year, Thrice, Drain, Neon Trees, We the Kings, The Academy Is…, Senses Fail, From First to Last, Plain White Ts, Hawthorne Heights and more.

The 2025 edition of When We Were Young featured a reunion of Panic! At the Disco, along with Weezer, Blink-182, Avril Lavigne, The Offspring, All Time Low, The Used, Knocked Loose, Yellowcard, Simple Plan and more. Organizers announced they were taking a break in 2026, while assuring fans that they were not going anywhere.

“To our When We Were Young Family,” a statement on Instagram began. “The songs, the memories, the moments — none of it exists without you. After an unforgettable run in Las Vegas, we’ve decided to take 2026 off to give this festival the care it deserves and to make sure what comes next feels just as special as what came before.” Organizers did not provide any specific reason for skipping this year.
