Elliot Frances Flynn and Michael Paré to Star in Indie Supernatural Horror ‘Not Herself’ (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Elliot Frances Flynn (“Peacemaker“) will star alongside Michael Paré (“Streets of Fire”) in Jon Russell Cring and Tracy Nichole Cring’s supernatural horror film “Not Herself,” which recently wrapped production in New York.

The Crings co-wrote and co-directed “Not Herself,” which, according to an official description, “centers on Izmarie (Flynn), a guilt-ridden young woman haunted by a tragic accident in her past. Seeking a fresh start, she moves into a sprawling old house and begins offering psychic readings, exposing the secrets of those around her. Flynn recently appeared in Season 2 of HBO’s “Peacemaker.” Her other recent TV credits include “Long Bright River” and “Only Murders in the Building.” On the big screen, she’s appeared in “Shoplifters of the World,” “Linoleum,” “Unidentified Objects” and “Confession.” Flynn’s upcoming work includes the rom-com “Silent Retreat,” starring Isabella Rossellini.

Paré broke into Hollywood starring in “Eddie and the Cruisers” and “Streets of Fire,” both mid-’80s cult classics. His other big-screen credits include “Village of the Damned,” “Bad Moon,” “The Virgin Suicides,” “Bone Tomahawk” and “Puppet Master: The Littlest Reich.”

Jon Russell Cring and Tracy Nichole Cring are a husband-and-wife filmmaking duo that has produced 20 feature films together. Their other film releases include “Wheelies Inc.,” “Girl in the Palms” and “Darcy.” Their most recent project, “Sky Valley,” starring Morningstar Angeline, Irene Bedard and DJ Qualls, wrapped production in 2025.
