The 25 Best Christmas Gifts for Every Kind of Boyfriend, Whether He’s a Situationship or Soulmate

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Whether you just got together or you're practically married, there are gift ideas for every kind of partner.

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, The Hollywood Reporter may receive an affiliate commission.

Spare a thought for the humble boyfriend. Gift-giving for significant others can be tough, and “boyfriend” can mean a lot of things, from just-got-together to long-term partner. Do you go all out on your new boo and risk overdoing it, or play it cool and risk playing it too cool? Of course, there’s the happy middle, which is where we (hopefully) come in.

Below, you’ll find carefully chosen gifts for boyfriends of all stripes. There are gifts for the cinephile boyfriend who can rattle off his Letterboxd top four at any time (this is The Hollywood Reporter, after all), the fashion-forward guy with a mile-long Mr. Porter wishlist and even gifts for the guy who promises, year after year, that he doesn’t want anything. Check out our favorites below, and bookmark this list, as we’ll be updating it throughout the holiday season.

A movie-loving boyfriend will likely appreciate Criterion’s latest box set, which compiles all of the works of legendary filmmaker Stanley Kubrick. It’s on sale right now, too, but stock is limited.

A watch that watch nerds respect doesn’t have to cost thousands. The Seiko 5 is the ideal starter automatic, but it can also be the only watch you ever wear.

A good pair of headphones is always a good gift, and Bose’s QuietComfort headphones deliver top-tier noise-canceling, solid audio quality, and true all-day comfort.

If you haven’t heard, it’s an El Niño year. Get him a jacket that can keep up, like Arc’teryx’s Beta jacket, which features a GORE-TEX membrane that sheds water without sacrificing breathability.

Codenames has become one of the most popular board games thanks to its short learning curve and its replay value. Typically a four-player game, Duet turns it into a two-player game for competitive couples.

Sonic Editions makes high-quality prints that come framed, making them great gifts. They have photos from the worlds of film, music, sports, and more.

Gifting for a big reader, but not sure what he’s into?

An Audible gift membership lets him choose his own books to listen to at home or on the go, and Audible also includes original exclusive podcasts.

Another subscription idea is Trade, which offers some of the best quality coffee from unique roasters. You can choose between three-, six-, or 12-month subscriptions.

The Bialetti Moka pot is an icon for a reason. It’s the easiest way to make espresso-strength coffee at home, with an intuitive design that happens to be stylish, too.

Gifting for someone who’s more into tea than coffee? The Kinto teapot is stylish and easy to use, thanks to the lid with an integrated strainer. It’s also dishwasher safe.

A slipper is a classic gift for a reason, and UGG’s Tasman slipper tops lists thanks to its suede upper and genuine shearling lining. There are plenty of colors to choose from, too.

This collectible art book from Taschen is ideal for Laker superfans, and features essays from some of the franchise’s most famous names, including Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Magic Johnson, Luka Dončić and Phil Jackson.

Rugby polos are having a moment. Leave it to the preppy experts at J. Crew to nail the trend.

Got a boyfriend who’s constantly asking you where he left his keys or sunglasses? This stylish tray keeps his everyday things organized and easy to access.

Gifting for a guy who’s trying to get off his phone?

Leatherology’s spiral journal is a great way to get thoughts off the notes app and onto paper. It’s refillable too, so he doesn’t have to be too precious with it.

The North Face’s iconic Base Camp duffel can be carried in the hand or worn as a backpack, making it a versatile option for travel, road trips, and outdoor adventures.

CDs are making a comeback, and Fosi Audio’s reasonably priced CD player is one of the best ways to take advantage. It has a 3.5mm output, so it can plug into a variety of speakers.

If you’re shopping for someone who regularly travels with suits, this cleverly designed duffle bag features a built-in garment bag.

Is he still working out in running shoes? There’s a better way.

Nike’s Metcon sneakers are purpose-built for the gym, offering the support needed for strength training, while still offering flexibility for cardio.

Pizza ovens aren’t just for people with big backyards. The Ooni Volt 2 is safe for indoor use, but can reach temperatures of 850°F to quickly and evenly cook pizzas.

Gifting for a guy who prefers things practical? A drill is a good option.

While there are more powerful options, this is an affordable, entry-level pick that can make quick work of everyday tasks.

Don’t let its name and diminutive size fool you — the Theragun Mini gets plenty powerful. It’s travel-friendly and comes with three attachment heads.

Gifting for someone with an outdoorsy streak? Opinel’s stylish knife is great for cooking at the campgrounds, cutting rope, and yes, opening packages.

Cocktail enthusiasts will tell you that there’s no real substitute for Luxardo’s maraschino cherries. This two-pound tin will make a lot of Old Fashioneds.

This set of lead-free crystal glasses and decanter set is also a great gift idea for the whiskey enthusiast.
