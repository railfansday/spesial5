‘Glee’ Star Chris Colfer Says He Forgot Kurt Hummel ‘Meant So Much to People’ Because of the ‘Homophobia and Hatred’ He Experienced During the Show

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Chris Colfer is opening up about what it meant to play his now-beloved “Glee” character, Kurt Hummel, a month after reuniting on stage with his on-screen love interest and co-star, Darren Criss.

Criss (who played Blaine Anderson on the hit show) and Colfer surprised audiences by performing “Baby It’s Cold Outside,” an homage to their “Glee” characters, during the “American Crime Story” actor’s London concert on Oct. 18. Speaking with People about the evening, Colfer said it “was a really special night for me.” “We had been planning it for months. I was not expecting the reaction that we got,” he said. “I was expecting a few hoots and hollers, maybe a couple of gasps, but I was never expecting to get a standing ovation at Royal Albert Hall.”

Colfer added that the reception was “really healing, honestly.” He continued: “Because of the homophobia and hatred that I was exposed to at such a young age during the show, and because of the hatred that the show has received since it stopped airing, I had kind of forgotten that that character meant so much to people. So it was a really, really nice reminder, and I think it was a life highlight for me.”

Colfer has long been outspoken about the bullying he endured while on the show, and in 2024, even revealed he was warned not to come out as gay. “When I started filming the show, I had a lot of people tell me, ‘Do not come out whatever you do because it will ruin your career.’ So I hid for a little bit,” he said during an appearance on “The View.” “But I also told them, ‘I can’t hide it with my voice… I’m more effeminate than most people. I can’t hide it.’ And they said, ‘Don’t worry. As long as you never address it, you’ll be rewarded for it in the end.’”

Just days after Colfer and Criss’ performance, news broke that Disney+ and Hulu had greenlit a reboot of Ryan Murphy’s musical dramedy series. While Variety reported that original cast member Lea Michele is set to return as Rachel Berry, Colfer has been more ambiguous about his involvement, though he did recently confirm on The Daily Beast’s “Obsessed: The Podcast” that he is having conversations about reprising his role. “From what I heard, it sounded very funny, and it sounded like a good idea. So we’ll see,” he said.
