Bryan Freedman Sued Over Hush-Money Deals Shielding Michael Jackson Estate

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Cascio siblings claim the attorney had a conflict of interest when he brokered their settlements with the singer's estate.

Five siblings at the forefront of sexual assault allegations against Michael Jackson have sued Bryan Freedman, Hollywood’s favorite lawyer who was allegedly enlisted by the pop star’s estate to deceive them into signing hush-money deals.

In a lawsuit filed on Wednesday in California state court, Frank Cascio and his siblings Edward, Dominic, Aldo and Marie-Nicole accuse Freedman of legal malpractice, breach of fiduciary duty and fraud. They allege that he duped them into signing agreements barring them from publicly discussing Jackson’s alleged abuse that have emerged as obstacles in ongoing litigation against the estate, which Freedman allegedly worked for while representing the siblings when the deals were signed in 2019.

The siblings’ relationship with Freedman started five years earlier when a lawsuit was filed against Edward, who at the time was defending the singer from sexual abuse allegations, and the estate in connection to his work on a posthumous Jackson album. At the recommendation of the estate, which paid for legal fees, he retained Freedman, according to the complaint.

The lawsuit says Jackson’s estate again turned to Freedman to broker deals with the Cascio siblings in the wake of Leaving Neverland, which featured sexual abuse allegations against the singer. Freedman told the siblings that they didn’t need to hire their own lawyer and that the settlement was fair and nonnegotiable, according to the complaint.

“None of this was true,” writes Howard King, a lawyer for the siblings, in the filing. “At the time, Freedman represented the estate, including in connection with litigation against HBO, which had broadcast Leaving Neverland.”

The document the estate presented framed the settlement as an “acquisition and consulting agreement,” identifying the deal as a purchase of the family’s life story rights, the lawsuit says. Under the terms of the contract, the estate made five annual payments of $690,000 to each sibling. In exchange, they are barred from publicly discussing Jackson’s alleged sexual abuse and subject to mandatory arbitration provisions, among other things that include releasing the estate and its representatives from potential liability. It requires the entire family to return all payments if one member is violates the terms of the deal, according to the complaint.

The siblings say they learned of Freedman’s alleged conflict of interest when the lawyer in 2024 told them that the estate hadn’t paid enough money five years earlier. He allegedly called the deal “toilet paper” and that he “had enough on the estate” that would convince its representatives to “do right” by them, the lawsuit says. After Freedman instructed the siblings to hire him, they retained another lawyer, who told the estate that the 2019 deals would be challenged. The estate later filed an arbitration demand against the siblings for breaching the nondisparagement and confidentiality provisions of their deal.

“If Freedman had not fraudulently induced Plaintiffs into signing the agreement, they never would have been dragged into litigation for publicly discussing Jackson’s abuse,” states the complaint, which notes that a child sex trafficking lawsuit they had filed against the estate was forced into arbitration under the terms of the 2019 agreement.

The lawsuit was filed as Freedman, who didn’t respond to a request for comment, fields accusations of helping orchestrate online smear campaigns in public relations battles against his clients’ adversaries. A trial is scheduled to start next year involving Rebel Wilson and producer Amanda Ghost, who accuses the actress and her public relations team of creating a site that accused Ghost of sex trafficking.
