Dancing With the Stars Finale: Jan Ravnik's Censored Comment Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Dancing With the Stars pro Jan Ravnik surprised Julianne Hough when he swore during the finale—a major broadcast TV faux pas—alongside Tatyana Ali. See what was cut from the live show.

Jan Ravnik isn't dancing around his excitement—even if it comes at a cost.

As the Dancing With the Stars pro and partner Tatyana Ali celebrated their fresh freestyle routine during the Nov. 24 season 35 finale, he couldn't help but slip out an expletive on live television that had to be censored.

"I'm just extremely proud of her," Ravnik told co-host Julianne Hough after their final dance. "She f--king killed it."

Hough was sure to chastise the dancer—whose comments surprised everyone in the skybox—just a little bit, since the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) fines broadcast television stations for airing obscenities and indecent language between 6 a.m. and 10 p.m.

As the co-host teased before the judges announced their scores, "I think you're on somebody else's bad list now."

Nonetheless, Ravnik and Ali's dance to Judy Garland's "Somewhere Over the Rainbow" and Cab Calloway's "Minnie the Moocher," which earned them two 9s and a 10, was the perfect routine to close out their time together on the ABC show.

"Jan told me to tell him what my dream world was like," the Fresh Prince of Bel-Air alum explained to Hough. "He created this beautiful dance that's a metaphor for my experience here. So, it was fitting that it would be the last dance that we did."

While the pair celebrated their spot as finalists—Ezra French and Daniella Karagach ultimately took home the Mirrorball Trophy—Ravnik reacted to his on-air slip-up.

As he told Extra TV, "It was just the perfect way to describe the last dance."

Meanwhile, Ali—who made it to the finale alongside runner-up Amber Glenn, as well as fellow finalists Connor Wood and Maura Higgins—wasn't as taken aback as Hough.

"I wasn't that shocked," she admitted. "He was just being real."

However, she acknowledged, "It's live TV, though."

What did surprise the 47-year-old—mom to kids Edward, 10 and Alejandro, 7, with husband Vaugh Rasberry—though, was receiving a sweet message from Will Smith to cheer her on.

"I am amazed," Smith said in a video package that aired just before winners were announced, prompting Ali to fall to the ground in shock. "I am in awe. I am inspired. Keep doing what you're doing. I love you."
