Chelsea could block Man City’s move for Malo Gusto as price tag revealed

Manchester City's hopes of signing Malo Gusto in the January transfer window could be ended by Chelsea's £75million price tag, sources have told Football Insider.

The 23-year-old has told the Blues that he would like to explore a move away from the club in the window, amid a struggle to regularly feature in Xabi Alonso's team so far this season.

The Citizens explored a move for the Frenchman in the summer transfer window, but were put off then by the asking price.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Chelsea's determination to secure a mammoth fee for Gusto could kill a future move dead once again.

It remains to be seen if the Blues would be willing to budge on that price, however, with the right-back having started just three Premier League games this season.

With his current terms set to expire in 2030, there may be no better window to sell the defender.

Football Insider revealed in July that Chelsea had placed a £75m price tag on Gusto amid interest from Man City.

Enzo Maresca is keen to reunite with his former defender, but even in an inflated market, the fee is particularly sizable.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "They obviously looked at it in the summer window, but then it was the price tag that put them off going ahead with the move from Malo Gusto.

"Still, the right-back has been a bit of a problem area for Man City for a while now. Obviously [Matheus] Nunes has played there, [Abdukodir] Khusanov has played there this season as well.

"Enzo Maresca is well aware of Malo Gusto, having worked with him at Chelsea, and he is a big admirer him."

In recent years and under Pep Guardiola, Man City's full-backs were very much of a certain type.

Often centre-backs shifted out wide, they would both be capable of dropping inside and defending the touchline, too.

Nunes was initially a central midfielder, and has adapted well to his position change over the last couple of years.

Gusto is much more of a traditional and natural full-back, yet even he is capable of playing inside, having started in the midfield for Alonso this season.
