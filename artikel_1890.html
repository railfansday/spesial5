Sources: Rio Ngumoha’s updated Liverpool contract position

Rio Ngumoha is happy at Liverpool and keen to sign a new contract, sources have told Football Insider.

There have been fears that the 18-year-old could be set to leave Anfield in the coming months, with his contract set to expire in 2028.

The likes of Bayern Munich and Arsenal are interested in the talented forward, who would have his pick of the European giants if he decided to leave.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, however, both Ngumoha and Liverpool are relaxed about the situation and eager to reach an agreement.

The winger has taken steps forward under Andoni Iraola this season, and has been rewarded with his first caps for the senior England team.

Ngumoha is widely considered among the Three Lions' greatest prospects for the next generation, alongside the likes of Arsenal's Max Dowman.

Liverpool won the race to sign Ngumoha from Chelsea in 2024, beating off competition from the Premier League and Europe.

The Reds could eventually pay around £7million in compensation for the winger - a fee that pales in comparison to what they could sell him for.

But speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Ngumoha is happy at Liverpool. Liverpool are very happy with him.

"He came out in an interview earlier this week saying that his move to Liverpool was a no-brainer, that he was happy that he made that decision to leave Chelsea for Liverpool.

"Everything’s happy in his camp and Liverpool are obviously very happy with his progress as well. Liverpool absolutely want to keep hold of the player, they see him as a long-term part of their plan.

"So I would imagine there’s a willingness from all parties to get a new contract done. So I’d imagine Liverpool are quite optimistic that they will get this sorted."

Ngumoha does face competition at Liverpool, with Cody Gakpo, Victor Munoz and Bradley Barcola all preferring the left-wing.

The 18-year-old has often been deployed on the right of the attack this season, however, with Iraola keen to provide him with new challenges.

"I feel like if I stayed at Chelsea - not saying that I wouldn't have got to the first team as quick, because I believe in my ability, but I feel like Liverpool just gave me a new pathway," Ngumoha said this week.

"The plan that they set for me was straightforward really. For me, it was a no-brainer going to Liverpool, and they just said if I prove myself, because they know how much ability I had, then they know that I would definitely be a first-team player and definitely a star for them in the making.

"I think going to Liverpool so far has been the right choice.”
