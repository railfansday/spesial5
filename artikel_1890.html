Andrey Santos could be set to benefit from Mauricio Pochettino's appointment at Man United.

The Brazilian arrived in the summer from Chelsea but has so far failed to nail down a consistent starting spot.

When a new manager comes in, it is a chance for those on the fringes of the squad to start with a clean slate.

And performing well on international duty is certainly one way to go about things.

While Brazil lost 3-2 to Japan in their recent friendly, Santos' stats have made him stand out, with Pochettino likely to be impressed by the emerging data.

The midfielder has shown glimpses of his potential, and this latest game provides another reason for the new manager to take a closer look at him.

Pochettino will be looking for his midfielders to be comfortable on the ball and show a desire to win their individual battles.

The quality of a midfield can often dictate the tempo of the game, and Santos has a fresh chance to make himself a regular.

As per FotMob, Santos completed 95 per cent of his passes against Japan, misplacing just two out of 42 attempted.

The Brazilian also completed his one attempted dribble, while succeeding with his only attempted long pass too.

More importantly, Santos managed to win all three of his ground duels, which will certainly raise Pochettino's eyebrows.

A strong battling midfielder who is able to keep the ball ticking and remain safe in possession will be a helpful player for his starting XI.

Whether or not he can force his way in, after starting just four Premier League matches under Michael Carrick, remains to be seen, but his performance for Brazil is undoubtedly a great start for him.

Santos and Pochettino are already acquainted as the Brazilian was at Chelsea when the Argentine was manager.

Pochettino was able to witness him through the pre-season of 2023, but he would not end up staying at Chelsea.

He would be sent out on loan to Nottingham Forest, though, with the 54-year-old deciding he had no plans for him in his squad yet.

Now, Santos has developed into a more experienced and complete player since 2023.

And that past history could end up helping get his Man United career finally kickstarted.
