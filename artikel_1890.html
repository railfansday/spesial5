Thomas Tuchel speaks out on James Trafford vs Jordan Pickford battle for England spot

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Thomas Tuchel insists that England do not have a number one, after James Trafford made a brilliant case to be first choice goalkeeper in the win over Spain.

Trafford played the full game as the Three Lions pulled off a memorable 3-2 Nations League win over the world champions in Madrid.

It was a stunning first hour from Tuchel’s side and the visitors were 3-0 up on 57 minutes thanks to goals from Harry Kane, Ezri Konsa and Ben White.

Things started to unravel a bit from there as Elliot Anderson was shown a straight red card and Mikel Oyarzabal converted from the penalty spot.

Nico Williams then made it a very nervy climax for England as he scored on 75 minutes and the hosts pushed for an equaliser.

Trafford helped his team stand firm, though, including making a tremendous double save late on.

Everton’s Jordan Pickford has been first choice goalkeeper since 2018, winning 94 caps, but his place is now in doubt thanks to the emergence of Trafford.

James Trafford last-gasp double save 🤯🤯#UNLSaves | @Betano_PT pic.twitter.com/K3Rn995OEE

Tuchel was not ready to declare either stopper his number one, but was clearly very impressed by the Leeds United star.

‘There’s no number one,’ said the England boss. ‘It’s a good double save. That’s what we wanted. He had a brilliant match. It only works if you’re top from number one to 11.’

Speaking in his post-match press conference, Tuchel continued on Trafford: ‘Very good. That’s his job, to close the gap. We want competition in all positions. We need to see Trafford in high-pressure moments.

‘The obvious save, where he deserves full credit, is the double save at the very end. Absolutely brilliant.’

The German has also liked what he has seen from Trafford on the ball, saying: ‘There was a lot to do in the deep build-up as well, which he did very, very well, with everyone around him.

‘Never lose the concentration, never lose the focus, never lose the vision where to find the free men to overcome the press and put the game to the opponent’s half.

‘With everyone else, he was part of a fantastic team performance.’

At 24 years old, Trafford is eight years younger than Pickford and looks to be his successor, even if he has not entirely displaced him just yet.

The win in Spain meant England topped their Nations League group and will play a runner-up from another group in the quarter-finals of the competition.

Groups A2 and A4 come to an end on Monday and Tuesday respectively, which is when we will learn England’s possible opponents for Friday’s draw.

Belgium have already secured second place in group A1, so they are a possible opponent for the Three Lions in the quarter-finals that are played in March next year.
