Chris Van Hollen’s Anti-Israel Turn

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Maryland senator has become an unlikely opponent of the Jewish state — and that’s boosted his fortunes in the Democratic Party.

Senator Chris Van Hollen (D., Md.) speaks during a press conference at the U.S. Capitol in Washington, D.C, November 19, 2026.

It’s a well-established gambit. A politician starts criticizing Jews. He gains followers. His attacks grow, along with his political power.

Maryland Senator Chris Van Hollen (D.), who is challenging Chuck Schumer for the position of Senate majority leader, is adopting this playbook. Van Hollen, who alleged in a recent New York Times op-ed that Israelis have “increasingly undermined American interests and values,” has become one of the foremost critics of the Jewish state in Congress. Backed by progressives such as Bernie Sanders, he is basking in his newfound fame. His foray into the Senate leadership contest coincided with his push to strip provisions concerning U.S. security cooperation with Israel from the National Defense Authorization Act. These and other broadsides have made him a darling of the anti-Zionist left. Regardless of whether he supplants Schumer, Van Hollen is pushing the Senate ever leftward on its policy toward Israel.

Senator Chris Van Hollen (D., Md.) speaks during a press conference at the U.S. Capitol in Washington, D.C, November 19, 2026.
