Angie Katsanevas Fights for Mary Cosby Friendship in ‘RHOSLC’ Sneak Peek

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Angie Katsanevas is ready to do whatever it takes to repair her friendship with Mary Cosby.

“I hope we can find our way back to each other,” Angie, 52, says in Us’ exclusive reveal of The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City’s midseason trailer.

The camera quickly pans to Mary, 54, who appears to have her eyes set on another costar.

“What I don’t like, though, is you,” she says as Ashley Quai appears on the screen.

On the November 4 episode of RHOSLC, jaws dropped when once-BFFs Angie and Mary got into a heated argument.

At one point, Mary called Angie a “bad friend” who prioritized self-care over parenting her 15-year-old daughter, Elektra. Soon after, Angie mentioned Mary’s late son, Robert Jr., who died in February.

Although Angie quickly expressed regret for her words, Mary rejected any possible conversation and said she didn’t want to look at her costar again.

“I love Mary with all my heart,” Angie explained on the November 4 episode of Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen. “Elektra being brought up, I hit my breaking point. And I deeply regret it, obviously. I never would want to hurt Mary. There’s no excuse. And it’s nothing I can take back, and that’s the hardest thing about it, because I do love her.”

Ultimately, Mary and Angie’s bond isn’t the only friendship in trouble. In the midseason trailer, Lisa Barlow isn’t interested in building a bridge with Bronwyn Newport.

“You talk bad about your husband. You talk bad about all of us,” she says. “Bye, Bronwyn. We’re calling [your boyfriend] Brandon [Good].”

In another scene, Bronwyn, 41, goes after Lisa, 51, and Meredith Marks, suggesting she’s not exactly over the drama surrounding the song “Hypocrisy and Bulls***.”

“Lisa and Meredith can crawl up each other’s ass holes and f***ing die up there,” she says. “I’m f***ing over it.”

Believe it or not, there does appear to be some fun moments amongst the cast in the second half of season 7.

The ladies embark on an unforgettable cast trip to Maui where Meredith, 54, and Whitney Rose appear to finally be making amends.

“In friendship, you hear each other out, you take accountability, move the f*** on,” Whitney, 40, declares after sharing a hug with her frenemy.

Watch all the drama unfold when The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City airs on Bravo Wednesdays at 8 p.m. ET. Stream new episodes the next day on Peacock.
