So you've played through the GTA 6 intro, gotten to know Jason and Lucia a bit, and probably been taught how to drive, shoot, and change clothes. You finally reach that moment in an open world game where, for the first time, you're let off the rails to just be.

That's what I want to know, because I suspect we all have a different system or detail that we've been thinking about since the first GTA 6 trailers and leaks—a test we must conduct to determine if "Rockstar really thought of that." I'm talking about hoops shooting, snack eating, or going on a classic GTA rampage.

Sound off in the comments below (there's a button at the top of the page to skip straight to them) to share your night one plans.

I'm gonna punch someone in the face. I swear I'm not usually one to play Rockstar games like a psychopath, but a bit of random crime will answer a question I've had about GTA 6 for months now: Is evading cops actually interesting? Gameplay leaks suggest that Rockstar has implemented a proper stealth suite with threat indicators and search zones. We also learned that law enforcement will be able to identify Jason and Lucia based on what they were wearing and driving while raising a ruckus. That sounds super interesting, and I can't wait to put those systems through their paces.

I'm going shopping! Actually, I'll probably need some cash first. I will do some crime, and then I will go on a lovely shopping spree. Dressing up my digital counterparts is the main reason I play videogames, mostly because it means that, at least for a few hours, I'm not spending real money on real trainers or the high-quality fabrics that I love to drape over myself.

Going for a drive. I'm also a sucker for playing Barbie with my GTA protagonists, but something I'm really looking forward to is jumping in a car and going for a long drive while cycling through Leonida's radio stations. Rockstar always has an excellent ear for music curation, and there's something about speeding down a highway on a rainy night that hits different. With GTA 6's map being Rockstar's biggest yet, there should be a whole lotta asphalt for me to explore.

Walking off into the woods. I shall find the first tree line and B-line towards it. What interests me more than the cars or even the crime is finding out what kind of wildlife will populate the stretches of land in Leonida. Ever since I heard about the wildlife logbook that Jason (and presumably Lucia) has access to I've been planning my nature excursion, someone's going to have to find all those 62 birds and see just how snappy these alligators are. Deadass, I'm going to be like that penguin from Werner Herzog's Encounters at the End of the World: The PCG team explained even if they caught me and brought me back to the city, I would immediately head back for the woods.

Refueling my car. I'm not exactly well known for playing GTA games in a *normal* way (if there is such a thing), like I spent most of GTA 5 riding around the backcountry on a dirtbike listening to the radio, and not much time at all actually committing crimes. While I am curious about Leonida's dirtbiking potential, my first port of call is the gas station. Having to refuel your car in a GTA game is such an alien concept I just want to see how the whole process works. How much does it cost? Is there an animation? Do I have to go into the gas station? I'll drive around a little bit first to empty the tank and then that's where I'm heading. I know, I know, I live for thrills.
