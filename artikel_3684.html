Rockstar has made some big changes to the way GTA 6 handles, but you can switch some key buttons to go back to old controls

Classic controls in GTA 6 are a feature that many will have wanted to see, as it's been 13 long years since the release of Grand Theft Auto 5. Many of you have been dipping in and out of that world consistently throughout that time, and if that's the case, there's a couple of aspects of the 2026 release which will come as a bit of a culture shock – particularly the way criminal profiles and GTA 6 Wanted Levels function.

Still, one of the biggest and most jarring changes may well be the new default control scheme that Rockstar has introduced. If muscle memory is getting in the way of you having a good time, you can turn on a Classic control scheme in GTA 6 in the options menu.

If you'd like to have an experience that's close to that of GTA 5, you should activate the Options/Menu button on your controller and navigate to Settings to find the option to switch from the default scheme to the Classic control scheme in GTA 6.

This will make a few key changes to the way the game handles.

For starters, the GTA 6 default control scheme has running set to L3/LS (rather than tapping X/A, which is now assigned to jumping). Melee has also been shifted to Square/X.

While you're here, you should also check that you're happy with the way the HUD communicates information. Rockstar has introduced a more dynamic system for the GTA 6 HUD – pulling non-essential information off of the screen to better showcase the scale and fidelity of the world around you.

The standard Dynamic HUD means you need to press down on the D-pad to bring up key elements such as the minimap and your bank balances – not to mention your health, stamina, and focus bars. These can all be toggled on or off in the Settings menu.
