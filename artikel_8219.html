Pros and Cons + Ultralight + Ultra-packable + Nice feature set – Pricey

If you’re looking for a sleep system with the weight savings of a quilt but the security and ease of use of a sleeping bag, the Spark Pro 30F Down was made for you. Category manager Stephanie Pearson called it “a unicorn—a roomy, feature-rich mummy that can still get lost in your pack.” At just 1 pound 5.8 ounces for a regular, it’s nearly an ounce lighter than our favorite quilt of the year, the Neve Gear Waratah. That’s largely down to the high-quality 950-plus-fill goose down that Sea to Summit used, which also helps it compress down to about the size of a cantaloupe. Despite that low weight, the Spark Pro has all the features you’d expect out of a normal mummy—a form-fitting hood and draft collar among them—plus a few surprises, like side vents that let us both bleed heat and stick our arms out for in-tent tasks without having to uncover our entire torso.
