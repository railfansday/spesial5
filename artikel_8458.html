My wife needs medically necessary procedure (vein a lation to prevent recurring clots). It turns out my current plan (continuation of Cigna employer plan under COBRA) specifically excludes Vienna ablation from coverage. We appealed since our doctor says it is medically necessary, and waiting on the decision.
In the meantime I'm looking at ACA plans for 2027. I called Kaiser permanente enrollment dept with the medical codes to be covered and they told me to get the gold plan. When I asked for something in writing that the plan covered those code they told me they couldn't look them up and they didn't know if it would be covered.
Is there a way to make progress or is it just a gamble?
Well, it’s pretty hard unfortunately before signing up. Probably still difficult after signing up.

I had an ACA BC/BS plan. And in the BC/BS portal they had a tool for estimating what various things would cost using certain providers. I’d never seen anything like that before and thought it was pretty cool. It still didn’t give me 100% confidence, but it was something.
