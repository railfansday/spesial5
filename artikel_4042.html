Hard West II for PS5, Xbox Series launches November 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Wild West-themed turn-based strategy game sequel Hard West II will launch for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series on November 10, publisher Klabater and developer Ice Code Games announced.

Hard West II first launched for PC via Steam on August 4, 2022.

Here is an overview of the game, via Klabater:

Hard West II takes players deep into unforgiving wastelands, where revolver bullets collide with dark magic drawn straight from forgotten tales. Discover the story of Gin Carter, a legendary outlaw who sets out on a mission to rob the mysterious “Ghost Train.” However, it soon becomes clear that gold will not be your main objective—instead, you will face the task of reclaiming your own soul.

To achieve this, together with your posse you will travel across the diverse lands of the Weird West, fighting a variety of enemies and taking part in duels, train robberies, and horseback chases.

In Hard West II, the main focus is on tactical, turn-based combat, in which the player commands a group of gunslingers, each with unique abilities developed through creating special combinations of mysterious cards. An important gameplay element is the Bravado mechanic, which grants additional action points after defeating enemies and allows you to chain your companions’ actions into spectacular kill sequences.
