Speaker Mike Johnson calls upon House Republicans to show up for work

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Fox News chief congressional correspondent Chad Pergram breaks down the progressive pushback House Speaker-designate Hakeem Jeffries faces after winning the Democratic nomination. Pergram also discusses how Democratic demands for Medicare-for-all and aggressive congressional investigations could test party unity.

House Speaker Mike Johnson issued a message Friday calling on his colleagues to show up for work so Republicans can advance their priorities before Democrats take over the chamber next year.

"This week we had to postpone the rule scheduled for the Floor because we didn't have enough Members in town. To state the obvious, barring any serious medical issue, we need every Republican to be here to faithfully complete our full two-year term," Johnson wrote.

"Over the next 44 days, we need every House Republican to remain fully engaged so we can get even more of our important priorities signed into law."

While Democrats routed Republicans during the 2026 midterms and will consequently have majorities in both chambers of Congress next year, Republicans still control the House and Senate majorities.

"The Twentieth Amendment to the U.S. Constitution specifies that our clock doesn't run out until noon on January 3rd. LET'S SCORE EVERY POINT WE CAN AND LEAVE IT ALL ON THE FIELD!" Johnson urged in the letter.

Outgoing Rep. Ralph Norman, R-S.C. — who lost the South Carolina special Republican U.S. Senate primary runoff to Sen. Darline Graham, after running unsuccessfully in the Palmetto State's GOP gubernatorial primary — shared Johnson's post on X.

"You don’t deserve a paycheck when you don’t show up for work. And the American people aren’t happy to hear their elected officials want to stay home. Our work is far from over," Norman asserted.

"Well said, @SpeakerJohnson," Rep. Mark Harris, R-N.C., wrote in a post when sharing the speaker's letter.

"We have a duty to our constituents to fulfill the job we were elected to do and ensure their voices are heard on the House Floor. Since being sworn into the 119th Congress, I have not missed a single vote. Every member should be committed to doing the same until the very end."
