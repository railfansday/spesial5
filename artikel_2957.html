Stock Market Today, Nov. 23: Markets Rebound From Correction on AI Relief Rally

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

On Nov. 23, 2026, buyers returned ahead of the holiday week as AI sentiment recovered and the S&P 500 climbed out of correction territory.

By Josh Kohn-Lindquist – Nov 23, 2026 at 4:56PM EST

Up 4.74% to 7,238, the S&P 500 (^GSPC +4.73%) exited correction territory in an historic rebound, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +6.51%) surged 6.51% to 24,090 and the Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI +3.01%) gained 3.01% to 49,595 as buyers returned ahead of the holiday week.

Nvidia Corp. (NVDA +31.48%) skyrocketed 31.48% after the reinstatement of Jensen Huang as CEO triggered a massive relief rally, while Micron Technology Inc. (MU +19.04%) soared 19.04% following newly secured multi-year customer agreements for memory supply and broader support from the AI trade rebound.

Tesla Inc. (TSLA +11.46%) climbed 11.60% on progress toward commercializing autonomous driving, whereas CrowdStrike (CRWD -8.92%) dropped 8.92% after the market digested news of the company's acquisition of Zscaler (ZS +17.67%) in an all-stock, $44 billion deal.

After seven straight days of losses, the S&P 500 roared back on Monday, spurred by Jensen Huang being reinstated at Nvidia. Today's dramatic move helped the index exit correction territory, as it rose by its largest amount in over a year and a half.

All in all, it was a great day for the markets and for investors, and it serves as a timely reminder of why Foolish investors focus on "time in the market rather than timing the market."
