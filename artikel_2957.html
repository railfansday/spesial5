NFL Week 9 picks: Bills prevail over Vikings; Packers beat Patriots

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Los Angeles Times NFL writer Sam Farmer examines the matchups and makes his predictions for Week 9 of the NFL season.

All lines and over/under numbers are according to FanDuel Sportsbook.

Last week, Farmer posted an 11-3 (.786) record. Through the first seven weeks of the season, he is 74-74. (.612).

Using point spreads with the scores Farmer predicted, his record against the spread in Week 8 would have been 8-6 (.571). For the season, his record against the spread is 53-68 (.438).

All times are Pacific and TV reflects broadcasts in the Los Angeles area. The Steelers and Titans do not play this week.

Jacksonville Jaguars quarterback Trevor Lawrence hands the ball of to runningback Bhayshul Tuten during a win over the Indianapolis Colts on Nov. 1. (Kelly Jordan / Associated Press)

With Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley hurt and newly acquired quarterback Jacoby Brissett on the way, the Ravens will start third-string quarterback, undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano. Trevor Lawrence, meanwhile, is coming off a near-perfect passer rating against the Colts.

The Falcons had a great run defense — or seemed to — then got trampled by Tampa Bay. The Bengals needed overtime to beat one-win Tennessee, but Joe Burrow played great.

Rams players celebrates with wide receiver Puka Nacua, left, after his touchdown catch against the Chargers on Nov. 1. (Robert Gauthier / Los Angeles Times)

Rams got on track offensively on their final drive of the first half, then rolled the Chargers. Myles Garrett might be back. Every game is a must-win and Washington isn't going to derail them.

Who could pick Dallas against anyone these days, especially after that meltdown against Arizona? Well, maybe against the Colts, who are parting out their team.

This is a get-right game for the Chiefs, who have lost two in a row after a 5-0 start. The Jets are 1-5 in one-score games, so even if they can hang in there, their tendency is to watch the wheels come off.

Detroit Lions running back Jahmyr Gibbs celebrates after a run against the Minnesota Vikings on Nov. 1. (Paul Sancya / Associated Press)

The Lions handed the Vikings their first loss and Jahmyr Gibbs ran wild. They have too many weapons for 1-6 Miami, which had a 13-3 halftime lead against New England before being outscored, 21-6.

Whatever the Browns are doing to protect Deshaun Watson isn't working. He was sacked eight times against Pittsburgh. The Saints are coming off a bye, and they're playing teams close.

What happened to the Eagles? They have lost six in a row and are the NFL's only team to give up at least 100 yards rushing in every game. John Harbaugh is 15-3 after the bye.

The Broncos are coming off a huge win over Kansas City. Not easy to fly across the country and face the rested Panthers, although Carolina can have some breakdowns on defense.

Chargers offensive tackle Joe Alt looks on from the bench late during a loss to the Rams on Nov. 1. (Robert Gauthier / Los Angeles Times)

The Chargers are going to win a game at some point, and maybe this is the one. Weirdly, Houston's defense has been a huge disappointment. That said, the Texans have had their number, so…

Tom Brady is coming back to Foxborough to call this one, and Drake Maye put together a solid game against Miami. Jordan Love is playing at an elite level, and that could be enough.

San Francisco 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy looks to pass against the Atlanta Falcons on Oct. 25. (Danny Karnik / Associated Press)

The Raiders came back and beat the Jets to win for the first time in five weeks. But the 49ers are rested, just added DeForest Buckner and are a better team top to bottom.

The Seahawks didn't look like themselves against Chicago and got upstaged at home. That isn't likely to happen against the Cardinals, who reminded us against Dallas that they don't give up.

The Buccaneers have run for a combined 582 yards in two straight wins, but they will have to be more balanced to beat the Bears. Chicago added Jeffery Simmons on the defensive line.

Buffalo Bills quarterback Josh Allen passes against the Baltimore Ravens on Nov. 1. (Jeffrey T. Barnes / Associated Press)

The Bills are not only playing well, but they're having fun doing it. Josh Allen looks both relaxed and deadly. Minnesota was the last undefeated team but will get a big test with this one.
