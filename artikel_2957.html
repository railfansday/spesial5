If You Invest $1,200 a year in Kroger Stock, Here's the Dividend Income It Could Generate Over 20 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A seemingly slow, small start can turn into something very big, given enough time.

As the old adage goes, past performance is no guarantee of future results. If the reasons for that past performance are still in place, however, that past performance is a decent indication of what the future likely holds.

What sort of dividend income might a position in grocery store chain Kroger (KR +1.30%) generate over the course of two decades if you buy $100 worth of this dividend-paying ticker every month during that 20-year stretch? Assuming the past 10 years are an indication of what awaits, we can come up with a pretty decent idea.

Here's what we know: Since late 2016, Kroger's per-share quarterly dividend has improved from $0.12 per share to $0.39, while the stock's price has advanced from around $33 to its current price of $68.

With that, you have enough information to determine the number. This ever-growing position would have produced approximately $1,700 in dividends during this 10-year span, and it would be worth nearly $17,700 at the end of it. If you'd reinvested the dividends, however, you would have received roughly $1,850 in dividend income, and the position would be worth nearly $20,000.

This disparity widens as time goes on. Using the same inputs as above but adding another 10 years, without reinvesting, your position would be worth just over $57,000, and you would have only collected around $13,500 in dividends. With dividend reinvestment, though, you'd be sitting on a little over $81,000 worth of Kroger stock in 20 years' time, nearly $17,000 of which would have come from dividends.

Some investors are surprised at the difference that dividend reinvestment can make. They're even more surprised at the impact time can have. That's the power of compounding. Even seemingly small steps make a big difference when you start earning money on your past gains. The key is being consistent.
