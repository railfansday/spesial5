NFL Week 10 uniforms: Here are the helmets, jerseys and pants for every team

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

NFL Week 10 uniforms: Here are the helmets, jerseys and pants for every team originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

There's a little bit for everyone in the NFL wardrobe department this week.

If you like classics, the New York Giants and San Francisco 49ers are turning back the clock. If you like alternates, five teams are going with unique helmets or jerseys — including the newest Rivalries look.

Fans who don't want anything other than primary uniforms will be served, too. Legacy teams like the Pittsburgh Steelers, Green Bay Packers and Las Vegas Raiders, among others, are wearing their traditional sets.

What are all the uniform matchups for Week 10? Here's a game-by-game breakdown.

Commanders: Burgundy helmets, burgundy jerseys, burgundy pants

Giants: Navy blue helmets, white jerseys, white pants (throwback)

Patriots: Silver helmets, white jerseys, silver pants

Lions: Silver helmets, blue jerseys, silver pants

Bills: White helmets, white jerseys, white pants

Jets: Black helmets, black jerseys, black pants (alternate)

Panthers: Silver helmets, white jerseys, TBA pants

Saints: Black helmets, black jerseys, black pants (alternate)

Texans: Navy blue helmets, white jerseys, white pants

Browns: Brown helmets, brown jerseys, brown pants (alternate)

Jaguars: Black helmets, white jerseys, black pants

Titans: Light blue helmets, navy blue jerseys, navy blue pants (Rivalries)

Chiefs: Red helmets, white jerseys, TBA pants

Falcons: Black helmets, red jerseys, black pants

Dolphins: White helmets, white jerseys, TBA pants

Colts: White helmets, blue jerseys, white pants

Vikings: Purple helmets, white jerseys, TBA pants

Packers: Yellow helmets, green jerseys, yellow pants

Rams: Blue helmets, white jerseys, white pants

Cardinals: White helmets, red jerseys, red pants

Seahawks: Navy blue helmets, white jerseys, white pants

Raiders: Silver helmets, black jerseys, silver pants

49ers: Gold helmets, red jerseys, white pants (throwback)

Cowboys: Silver helmets, white jerseys, seafoam pants

Steelers: Black helmets, black jerseys, yellow pants

Bengals: White helmets, white jerseys, white pants (alternate)

Chargers: White helmets, white jerseys, powder blue pants

Ravens: Gray helmets, black jerseys, black pants (alternate)
