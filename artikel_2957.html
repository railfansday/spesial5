Anthropic sets IPO price range of $70 to $78, looks to start trading Nov. 19, sources say

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Anthropic said it will offer 825 million shares in its upcoming IPO at a price range between $70 and $78 per share, according to an updated filing on Tuesday, bringing the 5-year-old artificial intelligence lab a step closer to a potentially historic Nasdaq debut.

The company is planning to price on Nov. 18 and trade on Nov. 19, according to people familiar with the details who were granted anonymity because they were not authorized to speak publicly. Anthropic is targeting retail allocation of about 20%, the people said, a much higher percentage than the typical initial public offering, which is 5% to 10% for retail investors.

At the high end of the range, Anthropic would net up to $64.35 billion in proceeds through the IPO. The company could end up being worth up to $1.405 trillion on a fully diluted basis, rising from the $965 billion valuation it announced as part of a funding round in May.

Anthropic said it has entered into an agreement with Advanced Micro Devices, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. to purchase $26 billion of the company's Class N common stock in a private placement at a price per share equal to the IPO price. The shares sold in the private placement will not be registered in the offering and will not be subject to lock-up agreements with the underwriters, the company said.

As Anthropic executives get set to pitch the offering to investors, they have to contend with a tumultuous political environment, following last week's midterm elections that included a Democratic sweep in Congress and the passage of numerous statewide ballot measures that could slow the AI buildout. They're also facing a public market that's grown increasingly skeptical of the AI trade on concern that rising interest rates will make debt unaffordable for companies that need it for costly infrastructure purchases.

CNBC reported last week that some Anthropic investors were bracing for a lower valuation than had previously been expected. While Anthropic had been looking toward a $2 trillion valuation until recently, shareholders told CNBC that even $1.5 trillion is now in doubt, with one investor calling that number a "stretch."

Should Anthropic reach the Nasdaq this month as planned, under the ticker symbol "ANT," the debut would come roughly five months after SpaceX went public in a record offering that raised $85.7 billion. Elon Musk's reusable rocket and AI company has a market cap of just under $2 trillion, around its valuation at the time of the IPO.

Institutional demand for Anthropic is even stronger than it was for SpaceX at this point in the process, the people said. The company's book is already covered at the $70 to $78 per share range on an allocable basis, they said.

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei co-founded the AI lab in 2021 after he and a small group of researchers defected from OpenAI, now its chief rival. Anthropic is best known for developing a family of AI models called Claude, and the company primarily sells its products and services to other businesses.

On Monday, Anthropic introduced two new AI models, Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, the first time the company has released so-called frontier models since Amodei called to slow down the pace of AI development in September. Anthropic said that the models went through rigorous safety and alignment testing, and that the company developed new evaluations and post-launch monitoring tools ahead of the release.

For the nine months ended Sept. 30, Anthropic said it generated $33.24 billion in revenue up roughly 1,200% from the same period last year. It reported a $104 billion net loss during the nine-month period, though most of that was due to the change in fair value of convertible notes and derivative liabilities. Anthropic lost $25.1 billion over the same stretch in 2025, the filing said.

Anthropic said in its amended filing Tuesday that its run-rate revenue was roughly $81 billion at the end of October, up $10 billion from the previous month.

"Our aim is to help humankind chart a safe course through this technological evolution, open new frontiers of discovery, and bring forth an age of miracles," Anthropic's co-founders wrote in the prospectus. "Everyone who owns our stock, whether an institution or an individual, becomes part of that effort. We hope you will share our ambition to see it though."
