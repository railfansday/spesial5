DataOne Software Announces Build Data Licensing Agreement with Mercedes-Benz USA

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

DataOne's VIN Decoding Now Incorporates OEM Build Data for Mercedes-Benz Vehicles

BEVERLY, Mass., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- DataOne Software, a Dominion Enterprises company and a leading provider of automotive data and software solutions, today announced a new OEM build data licensing agreement with Mercedes-Benz USA (MBUSA). This agreement enables DataOne's VIN decoding services to provide OEM-sourced, option-level data for MBUSA's complete portfolio of vehicles, which includes Mercedes-Benz, Mercedes-AMG, Mercedes-Maybach and Mercedes-Benz Vans.

DataOne customers will now have the option to utilize Mercedes-Benz build data when decoding a vehicle with DataOne's VIN Decoder API. OEM build data is instrumental in identifying all the optional features and equipment present on the vehicle, including transmission, color, and trim level, in addition to factory-installed options, marketing packages, and safety features.

DataOne customers will now have the option to utilize Mercedes-Benz build data with DataOne's VIN Decoder API.

"We are extremely excited to announce this data collaboration with Mercedes-Benz USA, which will provide immediate benefits to our customer base across the automotive industry," states DataOne Software President Jake Maki. "DataOne users are now empowered to more easily identify the innovative vehicle technology and customization incorporated into Mercedes-Benz vehicles and to utilize this insight to better support their organization's needs."

DataOne Software's option-level VIN decoding returns both the manufacturer-specific terminology as well as DataOne's standard vehicle descriptors. The manufacturer-specific terminology is optimized for use in generating OEM marketing-compliant vehicle descriptions, while DataOne's standard vehicle descriptors make this information easy to integrate and use across all brands.

DataOne Software (www.dataonesoftware.com) is a premier automotive data source providing the data and software solutions needed to power modern businesses across all segments of the automotive industry. DataOne's comprehensive data solutions cover everything from VIN decoding and vehicle descriptions to specifications, optional equipment, and market pricing.
