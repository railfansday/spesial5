To become a licensed real estate agent in Texas, you need to complete 180 hours of prelicensing education, pass the state license exam, and work under a licensed broker. Following a full-time schedule, you can complete your prelicensing coursework in as little as six weeks, though on average people take about six months to ensure they are prepared for their license exam.

Online real estate school in Texas typically costs between $300 and $800. Basic course packages will cost you less, and will provide you with the coursework required to get your Texas real estate license. Upgrading your real estate schooling package will give you options to include useful tools such as textbooks, better instructor support or exam prep tools to prepare you for the license exam.

The fastest way to get your real estate license in Texas is through an online course. Online courses allow you to work at your own pace and complete the course quickly. Online courses also offer the flexibility to study from anywhere and at any time, making it the most convenient option.

It’s important to note that how long the licensing process takes overall depends on several factors, including the time it takes to find a sponsoring broker and the length of time it takes to schedule and pass the state licensing exam.
