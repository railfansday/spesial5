Anthropic is targeting up to $3.5 billion from Japanese investors in its IPO

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The Japanese tranche is part of an IPO in which Anthropic is offering up to $64.4 billion of stock at about a $1 trillion valuation

Anthropic aims to draw between $3 billion and $3.5 billion from investors in Japan through its IPO, according to Bloomberg.

Anthropic plans to place 38.5 million to 50 million shares in Japan, with a preliminary price of $70 to $78 a share, according to a filing the company submitted to Japan's Ministry of Finance. Mizuho Securities will run the Japan portion of the sale, the filing said. Goldman Sachs $GS, Mitsubishi UFJ Morgan Stanley $MS Securities, Morgan Stanley MUFG Securities, Rakuten Group and SBI Holdings are among the firms responsible for taking orders from investors.

The Japanese portion is a public offering without listing, according to Bloomberg. Bloomberg said Japan would be among the few markets where investors can buy directly into the IPO through this tranche, pointing to the country’s households and their ¥2.4 quadrillion (about $15 trillion) in financial assets.

The overall IPO covers up to $64.4 billion of Anthropic stock. Using the share count disclosed in a filing on Tuesday, that would put the company's worth at roughly $1 trillion, or about $1.4 trillion once all dilutive securities are included.

Anthropic is planning a Nasdaq $NDAQ debut under the ticker symbol “ANT,” according to CNBC. The company plans to price the offering on Nov. 18 and begin trading on Nov. 19. Anthropic is targeting about 20% of the offering for retail investors, compared with a typical 5% to 10% in IPOs, CNBC said.

Institutional demand is running ahead of SpaceX’s offering at the same stage, according to CNBC. Anthropic’s order book already holds enough demand to fill the deal between $70 and $78 a share, counting only allocable orders, CNBC said.

The $64.4 billion figure is the most Anthropic could raise, with any lower price meaning a smaller sum. Anthropic had been aiming for a valuation of $2 trillion up to a short time ago, according to CNBC. Some shareholders told CNBC they are no longer certain the company can reach even $1.5 trillion.
