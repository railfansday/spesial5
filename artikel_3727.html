Lifetime Star Says She Suffered 'Permanent' Disfigurement After Dog Attack

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Lifetime actress Natasha Wilson has filed a lawsuit against a man whose dog allegedly left her permanently disfigured after an attack at a dog park.

Wilson, who is known for her roles in Lifetime films including The Best Version of Love and Single and Ready to Jingle, alleged in her suit that her injuries have impacted her ability to work, according to court docs obtained by The New York Post on Wednesday, November 4.

The actress claimed that she was walking her French bulldog at the West Hollywood Dog Park around 9:40 p.m. on Nov. 7, 2024, when she crossed paths with defendant Gregory Marcel and his dog, Scruffy.

Wilson alleged that Scruffy approached her dog “and stood over it in an aggressive and dominant posture.” When she attempted to pick up her dog, Scruffy allegedly jumped on her and caused her to lose hold of her pup.

“Scruffy continued to attack, while Wilson was on the ground attempting to shield her dog with her body and hands,” the suit alleged, per The Post. “During the attack, Scruffy bit Wilson on the face, above her right eyebrow, causing a laceration and bleeding.”

After the incident, Wilson went to the emergency room at Cedars-Sinai with Marcel, where she received stitches and was prescribed antibiotics.

“As a result of the incident, Wilson suffered a laceration above her right eyebrow requiring sutures and has been left with a permanent, visible, and painful scar, an altered eyebrow, hair pattern, and impaired movement of the right brow,” the lawsuit claimed.

Wilson was allegedly told that she requires scar revision surgery and additional treatment.

“Wilson was prevented from attending her usual occupation and thereby sustained a loss of earnings and an impairment of her earning capacity,” the suit read.

She is seeking economic damages, including medical expenses, wage loss and loss of earnings capacity.

Us reached out to reps for Wilson and Marcel for comment.

Wilson last appeared in the 2024 film Trust in Love, according to her IMDb. Her other credits include 2023’s Her Study of a Killer, Vote One for Love and Crimson Point, 2022’s Dancer in Danger, 2018’s Crazy, Rich and Deadly and 2018’s Darc. She also appeared in 14 episodes of the TV series UnREAL from 2015 to 2018.

Wilson last posted on her Instagram, where she has 12,300 followers, in June 2025, sharing a clip from Vote One for Love.
