Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2's latest updates introduces a Weekly Clash mode that shakes up PvP with some constricting modifiers

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

In as booming a monster truck rally commentator voice as you can imagine: ARE YOU READY TO GET DECIMATED??? Yes? Yippee! Because I've got some good news for you. The latest update for Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 is here, appropriately dubbed the Decimation update, which adds in a new mode and more.

First up, that new mode! It's called Weekly Clash and it's for the competitive folks out there. "This new PvP shakes things up with the introduction of “Weekly Clash”, a new mode that brings unique new rules to Eternal War, like games restricted to a single class, a single weapon or even granting near-unlimited grenades," explains a Steam blog post. "Except more modifiers to be added in the future."

This update also introduces a new Chaos Siege sector, that new mode introduced last month, which adds in a "new playable sector bringing a renewed challenge between waves 11 and 15!" You'll also find six new heroic weapons in the armoury, like the omnissian axe, plasma incinerator and heavy bolter. This comes complete with some new heroic armour pieces, specifically four new helmets. On top of all of this, there's been a Vanguard rework: "Fight the enemies of Humanity with new ways to use your Grapnel Launcher! Attack cancellations, close-quarter kicks and a new Revenge mechanic make the Vanguard as bold and brutal as he’s ever been!"

Some other general tweaks have been made with this patch too. All PvP maps have seen the number and locations of ammo and equipment pickups adjusted, and conditions for the spawn position swap system have been improved. A level lock for the Absolute Siege and Absolute Stratagems mode has also been added; "The difficulty will be accessible to Classes that have reached level 25 or achieved Prestige at least once." And you'll also find that loading times and general connectivity should be a bit better!
