This week in PC games: Raise a psychedelic baby, invent terrible new religions, and run your own PC hardware business

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Fine Monday to ya, reader. In the interest of allowing Edwin more time to spend with his interview transcripts, you’ve got me on Maw duty this week. Not much chance of it going ungorged, in any case – while the continued bellows of Grand Theft Auto VI have frightened away most of the air-quotes "big" games from this late November habitat, many others remain, undaunted. Or because they probably thought the Venn diagram of GTA fans and grand strategy expansion fans was just two rings.

There’s still some Vice City-adjacent matters we’ll be covering this week – chief among them the Rockstar North unionisation vote, which is set to close in the coming days – but as I said to Edwin and Mark this morning, the cool thing about the continued lack of a PC version is that it leaves open a space to be filled with other things. Judging by what’s out this week, there’s certainly no shortage to choose from.

With the RPS 100 completed for another year – our version, at least – I’ll be turning my attention this week to spinning up the 2026 Advent Calendar. Surely, there’ll be no arguments about this one. Elsewhere, Edwin is pondering stealth game remakes and clanking RTSeses, Mark has been yanked off his PS5 long enough to do some newshounding, and Julian remains on the wrong side of the Earth.

Have I missed anything, in my premature Christmas spirit? Do let us know the games you’re looking out for in the comments.
