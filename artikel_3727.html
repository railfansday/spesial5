Burning Sword: Death Sun now available in Early Access

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Wuxia hack-and-slash action game Burning Sword: Death Sun is now available in Early Access for PC via Steam at a 10 percent-off launch discount price of $17.99, developer Nomadic Games announced.

The game will be updated throughout Early Access with more stages, bosses, and skills, before an eventual version 1.0 release.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:

Burning Sword: Death Sun tells the story of Zhang Yixian and his pursuit of power and vengeance for the death of his master. Hardcore action combat meets epic boss battles and careful customization, interwoven with subtle environmental storytelling and worldbuilding. Burning Sword: Death Sun emphasizes challenge, progression, and deep combat systems.

The story is told subtly, but is full of character, political intrigue, and power dynamics. Starting in the year 1139 during the Southern Song Dynasty, you play as Zhang Yixian just as he’s cast down and nearly killed by the Elder of the Northern Mountain. The martial arts emerged during moments in history like this, when tyranny and brutality must be met with strength and passion.
