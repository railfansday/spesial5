Morning Minute: Google’s Newest AI Model Tops Benchmarks Ahead of Anthropic IPO

Morning Minute is a daily newsletter written by Tyler Warner. The analysis and opinions expressed are his own and do not necessarily reflect those of Decrypt. And check out our daily news show 'FOMO HOUR' covering all of the top stories and market action.

Google shipped Gemini 4 Neon on Wednesday, its fourth frontier model release in three weeks, and on the eve of the heavily anticipated Anthropic IPO.

This isn’t just a normal model release. For one, it’s incredibly good. Neon posts 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index against 59 for Claude Opus 5.5 and 57 for GPT-6.2 Sol. It takes the Vals Index at 70.9%, AutomationBench at 56.3% against Opus 5.5’s 42.5%, and GraphWalks long-context retrieval at 95.2% against Opus 5.5’s 66.8%.

Beyond just the outperformance, Google has been releasing at a pace nobody else is matching. It dropped Argon in mid-October. Carbon on October 28. Hydrogen last week. Google owns its silicon, its data centers, and its distribution, and it is using all three to iterate faster than labs that rent compute. And it has some speculating that Google has already achieved RSI (recursive self-improvement), the final step ahead of super intelligence.

So Google has caught up at the top of the AI race. Just in time for the ANT IPO.

Anthropic will be priced at $83, 6.4% above the top of its $70 to $78 range, valuing the company around $1.5 trillion fully diluted. On the 825 million shares in the original terms, that’s roughly $68.5 billion raised, and about $94.5 billion counting the separate $26 billion private placement from its compute suppliers. It trades Thursday on Nasdaq as ANT. Notably, ANT has been trading on Hyperliquid in the $90-$100 range for the past week but popped to $105 this morning.

The valuation math is a 55% step-up from the $965 billion May round and about 25% below the $2 trillion Anthropic was reportedly chasing in September. Revenue ran $33.2 billion through nine months against $2.5 billion a year earlier, a 13x increase. Annualize that and $1.5 trillion is roughly 34 times sales.

That’s a steep raise given the macro backdrop and the Fed calling for more hikes for longer. AI stocks have been shaky over the past month and even moreso since Jensen Huang left Nvidia. Not the most confident market headed into the 2nd $1T+ IPO of the year.

So what does it mean for today’s price action? Personally, I expect a pop to the $95-$105 level that Hyperliquid has been pricing out for weeks, and it likely sees a spike higher. But there will be massive volatility, and it’s very likely to trade under that $83 level over the coming days and weeks. Especially given the new (surprise) headwinds from Google with its outperforming model.

We will find out soon enough. Good luck to everyone trading it today.
