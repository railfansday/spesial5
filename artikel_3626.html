Mia Thornton and More Former 'RHOP' Stars: Where Are They Now?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Over the years, the Real Housewives of Potomac series has parted ways with several cast members.

Viewers said goodbye to Monique Samuels after her altercation with Candiace Dillard-Bassett during season 5 made her reconsider her time on the show. After joining the series in 2017, Monique and Candiace initially formed a close friendship. Things took a turn, however, when a miscommunication escalated into a physical fight.

Cameras were rolling as Monique grabbed Candiace by the hair during October 2020 episode and then proceeded to pull her down. Later in the episode, the businesswoman attempted to run back into the building to continue her argument with Candiace.

Monique, who shares three kids with husband Chris Samuels, was later charged with second-degree assault. The “Not for Lazy Moms” podcast host filed a counterclaim against Candiace the same month. Both assault charges were dismissed in December 2019.

Following an appearance in the season 5 reunion, Monique announced her exit from RHOP. Although Monique seemingly has no plans to return to the franchise, her former costar Katie Rost has made cameos since her respective departure. After appearing in the first season of the show, Katie’s full-time role was reduced and she didn’t appear in season 3.

The model briefly returned in season 4 in a “friend” of status but later claimed she was not invited to the reunion. More recently, she popped up for a brief appearance during season 7 in October 2022.

After joining the cast of RHOP for season 8, Nneka announced she was stepping away from the show after one season. “As a true resident of Potomac proper, 20854, just know that I’ll be seeing you all again soon,” she told People in May 2024. Nneka explained that she was leaving the series so she could focus on starting a family with her husband, Dr. Ikenna Ihim, after “multiple failed fertility procedures.” Nneka revealed she was pregnant with the couple’s first child in January 2025.
