Patrick Mahomes and Wife Brittany Announce They're Expecting Baby No. 4

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Patrick Mahomes and his wife, Brittany Mahomes, are ready to expand their team in 2027.

“Another blessing from God🥹🙏🏼,” the couple wrote via Instagram on Tuesday, November 10, when announcing they are expecting baby No. 4. “& then there were 6🤍.”

In the announcement, Patrick, 31, and Brittany’s kids Sterling, 5, Bronze, 3, and Golden, 22 months, gathered around mom and placed their hands on her belly as they posed for family photos by the beach.

The pregnancy news may come as a surprise to fans of the couple, who remember Brittany, 31, previously telling reporters that she wasn’t sure if more children were in her future.

“We’re done, we’re done for a while,” she said in a June 2025 episode of the “Whoops” podcast. “I don’t know. I mean, I feel like you can never say you’re done. I think three is all I need.”

At the same time, the Kansas City Current women’s soccer team coowner couldn’t hide her love and joy of being a parent.

“Being a mom changed so many things in my life and changed how I looked at so many things,” Brittany told Yahoo Entertainment in June. “It really reshaped and redefined a lot of things in my life.”

She continued, “It’s made me more intentional, more purposeful and, honestly, more motivated to keep going and keep showing my kids that there are no boundaries in front of you. You can achieve anything. You can be a part of anything that you want to. You need to dream big and understand that you have to work hard and do the hard things to do the fun things.”

While sports fans know Patrick as the Super Bowl champion quarterback of the Kansas City Chiefs, the NFL stud also previously shared how much he loves being a dad.

During a July 2023 episode of Netflix’s Quarterback docuseries, Patrick acknowledged that everybody sees the game days. Very few, however, get a look into life at home.

“They don’t see how you have to manage playing football and being a dad and being a husband,” he shared. “It’s football. You’re gonna have highs. You’re gonna have the lows. You put so much into this that you want to win, you want to succeed. And you want to win that Super Bowl at the end of the year. But I have to be a dad too. I have to be a husband.”
