Raising Cane's Is Expanding In 10 States—See If Your City Made The List

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Your next Box Combo might be a whole lot closer to home.

If you're someone who would happily drive out of your way for Raising Cane's chicken fingers and a side of Cane's Sauce, your next order might be a whole lot closer to home.

The popular chicken chain is expanding in a pretty big way this November, with 12 new restaurants planned across 10 states. For some communities, it'll even be their first Raising Cane's location.

Nation's Restaurant News says the new markets include Brick, New Jersey; Fayetteville, North Carolina; and Sioux Falls, South Dakota, with the chain also adding restaurants in Alabama, California, Connecticut, Florida, Illinois, Michigan, and Utah.

For anyone unfamiliar with Raising Cane's, the chain has built its reputation around a fairly straightforward menu featuring chicken fingers, crinkle-cut fries, Texas toast, coleslaw, and its signature Cane's Sauce. Rather than offering dozens of different chicken sandwiches and menu combinations, Cane's keeps its focus on the essentials.

The chain is also picking up the pace with its openings. After adding seven restaurants in October, it's planning nearly twice as many for November, with the overall expansion as part of a much bigger growth push.

According to NRN, Raising Cane's generated $5.48 billion in sales in 2025, up 10.6 percent from the previous year. Founder Todd Graves has said his goal is to make Raising Cane's one of the 10 largest restaurant brands in the United States.

Personally, I'm just happy more people are getting easier access to that Texas toast and Cane's Sauce.
