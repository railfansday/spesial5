The Best Subscription Boxes for Women: Sure to Wow the Women in Your Life

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Tested by Katie Bandurski, Emily Way and Paul Rankin

We've tested dozens of subscription services over the years, and these are our favorite subscription boxes for women.

Our editors and experts handpick every product we feature. We may earn a commission from your purchases.Learn more.

We tested some of the best subscription boxes to find the ones truly worth gifting. Whether you’re shopping for a woman who has everything or treating yourself to something special, these thoughtfully curated subscriptions make it easy to discover new favorites and enjoy a delightful surprise each month.

Looking for more inspiration? Explore our 2026 Holiday Gift Guide for gifts she’ll love.

BloomsyBox is your one-stop shop when it comes to flower and plant subsctiption boxes. The brand offers a variety of eco-friendly blooms spanning seasonal bouquets, roses, tropical flowers and even houseplants for gifting. Choose from classic arrangements, large, mixed bouquets or collections of roses in monthly, weekly or bi-weekly deliveries.

“My BloomsyBox arrangement featuring red and white roses arrived expertly arranged, although I quickly trimmed the stems for maximum freshness and plopped them in a clear glass vase,” says tester Madi Koetting. “Despite the less-than-ideal winter weather, my bouquet remained intact for a whopping two and a half weeks. BloomsyBox is another go-to for show-stopping flower delivery.”

I re-tested BloomsyBox in 2026 to ensure quality and consistency. My Garden Soirée bouquet arrived via next-day delivery, with all 30 stems fresh and perfectly intact. BloomsyBox delivery is turly one of the freshest, fullest and most well-preserved flower delivery boxes I’ve ever received, and it would make a wonderful subscription gift for anyone who loves fresh blooms.

A candle subscription that’s affordable, flexible and totally giftable may seem too good to be true, but we found an option that has it all. Vellabox offers flexible subscription options, and each candle comes from a different candlemaker, so there’s no limit to the number of scents and brands she’ll get to try. Plus, regardless of size, each box comes with a curated gift to accompany the candle.

Deputy Shopping Editor Katie Bandurski has been a Vellabox fan ever since trying them for her Vellabox review. She has received a number of candle companion gifts, from lip balms to an eco-friendly scrub brush. “I’ve received many Vellabox boxes over the years, and they’re all high quality. I’ve found so many amazing scents, and I love to give them as gifts,” she says.

For the price point, we find Vellabox to be an incredible value for gifting, enjoying and exploring new scents. They’re also very well packaged to prevent breakage, and each comes in a pretty fabric pouch, sure to feel extra special to giftees.

Our best chocolate box winner offers our favorite chocolate subscription box, too. I tested the Compartés Chocolate of the Month Club box in February—and it wowed from the moment it arrived. Inside were two chocolate bars and a box of assorted chocolates.

The assortment featured strawberry cream and crème brûlée–filled chocolates. The strawberry cream was exactly what you’d expect—fruity and delicious. The crème brûlée, however, was a surprise, capturing the rich, warm vanilla flavor of the classic dessert with impressive accuracy.

February’s box included two chocolate bars: California Berries, a vegan dark chocolate full of dried berries, and Strawberry Shortcake, a white chocolate bar packed with strawberries and airy, crunchy shortcake pieces.

While the subscription does require a minimum of three deliveries (discounted to $180 from the $225 value), it would make a thoughtful gift, especially if you have a series of gifting occasions, like the holiday season aligning with someone’s birthday month.

After trying the most popular charcuterie clubs, we found that Appy Hour charcuterie kits offer a convenient, curated solution that takes the guesswork out of building a board—a perfect gift for casual hosts and aspiring entertainers alike.

An average subscription box from Appy Hour typically contains up to five cheeses, two meats, cracker packs and two or more accompaniments like dried fruits, olives, candied nuts, chocolate, spreads or pickled items. Tester Paul Rankin’s box included four packets of cured meats, three hard cheeses, one jar of sheep and goat cheese in oil, one sweet lemon ricotta, a box of artisan crisps, a bag of pralined pecans, a bag of dried cherries and, lastly, a tiny jar of blackberry balsamic jam.

“This entire box was exquisite,” says Paul. “I’m already a huge fan of a charcuterie board, so this was an easy winner. Each meat was different and delicious; the dried cherries and candied pecans tasted amazing. But the cheese, the cheese! The cheeses were the star, particularly the sweetened lemon ricotta, which was absolutely stunning.”

The ease and quality of this kit are hard to beat, and buying each item separately would likely cost more than the price of the box, making it an excellent value for giving and receiving alike.

If fruit is her snack of choice, The Fruit Company offers a wide selection of fresh fruit for delivery. There are multiple different monthly fruit clubs that include everything from pears and apples to more exotic choices like star fruit and champagne mangos. And you can choose different packaging options, from ribbons and bows to beautiful baskets.

“This is a solid fruit basket,” says Katie Bandurski of her Simply Organic Fruit Basket. “It’s beautifully arranged, shipped without any damage to the fruit and has a nice variety.”

You can choose from four different subscription options: HarvestClub Americana (oranges, peaches, pineapples, cherries), HarvestClub Medley (a mix of hard-to-find fruits and familiar favorites, like Rainier cherries and Fuji apples), HarvestClub Organic (seasonal organic fruit) and HarvestClub Exotica (exotic fruit, like champagne mangos and star fruit). All boxes ship free and are available on 3-, 6- and 12-month-long options.

Looking for the perfect gift for an experienced baker? Crumble Crate offers monthly boxes that include pre-portioned dry ingredients, a recipe, at least one quality baking tool and a QR code that leads to instructional videos, shopping lists and extra tips.

Katie Bandurski tested the apple rose tart, which arrived with a tart pan and pastry cutter, along with specialty ingredients like almond flour. Some basics, including apples, butter, eggs and lemon juice, needed to be supplied separately.

Even as an experienced baker, Katie found the project challenging. Forming the apple roses took some patience, and she had to reread a few of the more involved steps. Still, the finished tart looked impressive and tasted excellent. She described the experience as equal parts baking project and art project.

Crumble Crate may be frustrating for a true beginner, but if you’re gifting to a baker who already knows her way around the kitchen, it makes every delivery feel like a worthwhile challenge.

Winc is one of the best-rated wine clubs out there, and the subscription wine club includes diverse selections with a little bit of everything, from sake to rosé.

You can select individual wines or fill out a survey to have bottles specially curated for you. While it doesn’t come with tasting notes or other supplemental information in the box, all of that is easily available on their website.

Tea Runners is a top pick when it comes to tea subscriptions. Each box includes four sampler pouches of tea, 10 tea bags and a postcard with some basic info about the club, tea origins and steeping instructions.

The Original Box is a great gift for her to start experimenting and figuring out what types of tea she likes. If she already knows what kind of tea she likes, she can choose from four box options: Black, Pure, Herbal or Original.

Inside Katie’s box, each tea impressed with its freshness and flavor. She likens Moonlight Jasmine to “a warm summer night,” whereas Floral Nectar Black wowed with its subtle finishing florals. Even the Majestic Earl Grey impressed Katie, who isn’t a big fan of Earl Grey. “It was so delicious—much fresher than any Earl Grey I’ve had before,” she says.

We adore Jeni’s ice cream, and our rave review of Jeni’s Pint of the Month Club may just convince you that this is the food subscription box you need to get her.

Katie says, “I was impressed with the wide array of flavors in my ice cream subscription box. The mix had a little bit of everything—chocolaty, fruity, salty and even savory. Plus, many of these were flavors I would not have picked out myself. Talk about a great way to widen your ice cream palate!”

This one is definitely on the pricier side, but our editors absolutely love it.

The Partners Coffee Roaster’s choice subscription is one of the best ways to stay on top of the latest coffee. I tried Partners for the first time this year, and was blown away by the fresh, delicious flavor and quality of the brand’s whole bean coffee. The beans truly took my home latte to another level. Katie, who has also tried the brand, loves Partners for their bold packaging and both New York- and global-themed brews.

Each 12-ounce bag is hand-selected by a member of the Partners team. You’ll receive both single-origin beans and blends, depending on the month. Enjoy delivery every one, two, three or four weeks, starting at $20 a bag. As an added bonus, you get free shipping with the coffee subscription.

Let’s just say, I have Partner’s Coffee on my personal Christmas list this year, and I’m hoping Santa comes through!

Any bookworm will appreciate this curated book box. Choose between a 3-, 6- and 12-month subscription, and your giftee will receive a specialty-bound read each month for the duration of the subscription.

Plus, Book of the Month is totally customizable. They’ll get to choose one book out of a selection of five or six books each month, and can even skip a month if they need some more time to read their last pick.

I needed a gift for the family member who ‘has everything’ and also loves books—and BOTM was the perfect find. With BOTM, she can choose books she doesn’t already own from a curated list. Buying a subscription as a gift is super easy too. I opted for a physical card, which I tucked in a little gift basket with other goodies.

I think we can all name someone who would be thrilled to receive a puzzle of the month club as a gift. Once you sign up for the service, a new puzzle automatically ships to your door each month.

Jiggy is a puzzle company that focuses on partnering with artists through the Jiggy Puzzle Club. Members get an exclusive 500-piece puzzle each month, plus VIP perks like early access to new products and opportunities to interact with Jiggy artists. Each puzzle also comes with a reusable drawstring bag, making it highly giftable.
