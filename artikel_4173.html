Xbox PC App Receives Significant Upgrade Starting Today, Plus More Cloud Gaming Improvements

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Xbox is officially rolling out an updated version of the Xbox PC app to members of the Insider program as of today, aiming to deliver a "faster" and "simpler" experience compared to the version we've been using in recent years.

The new app is something that leaked a while ago, and adopts a cleaner visual style with loads of quality-of-life improvements for navigating the Microsoft Store, your library of games, the Xbox Game Pass section and beyond.

Here are just some of those improvements:

Another massive focus of the new Xbox PC app is making it run faster and be more responsive, with internal tests suggesting that it now launches up to 60% faster, while pages like the store and Game Pass load up to 50% faster.

"A faster, more responsive app: The app now launches up to 60% faster. Pages like Home, Store, and Game Pass load up to 50% faster, and in-app windows open up to 65% faster."

We were fortunate to get a sneak preview of the app from Xbox earlier this week, where it was explained to us that the team felt the old iteration was ready for a refresh, and the new version not only improves the experience in all kinds of ways, but makes it easier for them to continue adding new features in the future.

The app is also coming to Windows handhelds, and has been better optimised for controller support.

"There’s more to do, and your feedback will continue to shape what comes next."

Meanwhile, a new interface for Xbox Cloud Gaming on the web that's been available to test for some time is rolling out automatically to users over the next few weeks, again with the goal of making it "easier to find and play your games".

Here's what you can expect if you aren't using it already:

This refreshed look will begin appearing on supported Smart TVs and VR headsets as well, helping to provide a "familiar" feel across multiple devices rather than having to learn how to navigate each one separately.

Good stuff, then! All of this should lead to an improved experience across PC and Xbox Cloud Gaming in the weeks to come, and assuming all goes well for Insiders, we've been advised the PC app should roll out to everyone soon.
