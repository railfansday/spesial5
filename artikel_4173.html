Every Voice Actor Behind GTA 6's Main Cast

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Though there are hundreds of voices and talented actors in Grand Theft Auto 6, here are a few that you'll be hearing the most!

Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally here, and now that NDA's have expired, actors everywhere are finally taking a moment to confirm their roles on the project through social media. The full cast includes a mixture of well-known names, traditional actors making voice debuts, and even a few who will note GTA 6 as their first major project.

Of course, being as big a release as it is, it should come as no surprise that, in addition to the main cast, a massive number of actors and celebrities are featured in supporting roles and cameos throughout the game. So, keep an ear out, because you never know where you might run into a voice you recognize.

Taking on the role of bank-robbing professional Raul Batista is none other than Manny Perez. Raul's chaotic energy and simultaneously suave manner couldn't have asked for a better voice to back it than this legendary Dominican actor.

Unlike some of the lesser-known actors you'll see on this list, Manny has a long history of work, including Washington Heights, La Soga, El Rey de Najayo. He has also had supporting roles in shows like Law & Order: SVU, Blue Bloods, 100 Centre Street, and Hawaii Five-0.

Manny Perez has also served as a writer and producer on many of the projects he has acted in.

Here is how to get the legendary Kingfishers W-22 rifle early in GTA 6.

Real Dimez is the iconic rap duo, Bae-Luxe and Roxy Rashad, signed by Dre'Quan Priest at Only Raw Records. Voicing these talented women are actual Florida rappers, Hood Brat (Bae-Luxe) and Krissy Celess (Roxy). The duo has multiple tracks featured in the game, so needless to say, you'll be getting to know these voices quite well.

While Hood Brat and Krissy Celess aren't known for any other major acting roles and aren't a duo, you can find their individual music on major listening platforms like Spotify and Apple Music. So, if you're enjoying Real Dimez in GTA 6, check out some of their other work.

Dre'Quan Priest, voiced by Naiqui Macabroad, is a character you won't get to know until you've made it out to Vice City and engaged in the nightlife there, since he resides there while managing Only Raw Records and its newest talent, rap duo Real Dimez.

Though this is Macabroad's first major voice role, he recently made his debut as a prominent recurring character on Chicago P.D., so fans of the show will be getting to know his face well over the course of Season 14. Other notable projects include guest roles on shows like Power and Law & Order.

A face you'll see frequently alongside Dre'Quan is Boobie Ike, a Vice City legend. He runs several things around the city, including the adult entertainment club Jack of Hearts, while also backing the recording studio Dre'Quan manages.

Voicing Boobie Ike is Mike Forbs, whose work has primarily been supporting roles across TV and movies up to this point. That said, if his performance as Boobie Ike is any indicator, we'll likely be seeing Forbs' name on other major projects very soon.

Though you won't get to spend tons of time with Donnie, he leaves a pretty unforgettable first impression and plays a major role in helping Jason earn money early on. Voicing this man of chaos is Brad Carter, an actor with several guest appearances on shows like Sons of Anarchy, Criminal Minds, Dexter, and Ozark.

A Few Supporting Voices As GTA 6 Locals That You May Recognize:

Though you won't see Brian, Jason's boss, as often as some of the other supporting characters, you'll certainly be hearing his voice plenty when he calls with new orders. That voice belongs to Stephen Root, who you'll almost certainly recognize from one of his major roles. A few of those castings include Bill Dauterive in King of the Hill, Monroe Fuches in Barry, and Jim Hudson in Get Out.

While Lucia is a bit busy at the start of GTA 6, you'll quickly get to know Jason's closest friend and co-worker of sorts, Cal Hampton. Bringing Cal's laid-back persona to life is none other than Bobby Moynihan, who you may recognize from Saturday Night Live and a variety of voice work and traditional acting roles. Some of his most recent include Woody on NCIS: Origins, King George in Hoppers, and Louie in Ducktales.

Though you're introduced to Lucia right from the start, it'll be several hours into the game before she's a consistently present face and playable character. Voicing Lucia is Manni L. Perez, who you may not recognize from other voice roles, but might know from major guest roles on shows like Law & Order: SVU.

That said, Perez has voiced a couple of background characters in Grand Theft Auto V and GTA Online, so if you're a major fan of the franchise, you might find her voice a tad familiar.

While Lucia and Jason are both playable characters in GTA 6, Jason is widely considered the primary main character, since you spend the first several hours of the game playing as him, and this Florida man is voiced by none other than Dylan Rourke.

Though you may not recognize this uniquely deep voice from many previous major projects at the moment, it's safe to say this will put Rourke on everyone's radar.

GTA 6's first chapter has a brutal point of no return; make sure not to miss out on these things.
