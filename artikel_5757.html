Savannah Chrisley Reveals How She and Jason Galanis Pulled Off Their Secret Cayman Islands Wedding

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Savannah Chrisley has opened up about her secret wedding to her 56-year-old husband, Jason Galanis, to whom she said "I do" in the Cayman Islands months before announcing they were expecting their first child.

The 29-year-old real estate agent, who shot to fame alongside her parents, Todd and Julie Chrisley, on their reality TV show, "Chrisley Knows Best," announced on Oct. 27 that she was both pregnant and engaged to Galanis, who is 27 years her senior and served time behind bars alongside her father.

Days later, it was revealed that they are actually married, with People reporting that they said "I do" on July 14—just one year after beginning their romance.

Although the TV personality has stayed tight-lipped about the details surrounding their nuptials, she has now spilled on the special day while appearing on "The Golden Ticket."

Savannah Chrisley admitted that the couple avoided filing their marriage paperwork in the U.S. so that they could keep the news to themselves.

“I’m not public about anything. We ended up getting married in the Cayman Islands in July. So we did everything there, but we haven’t filed [in the U.S.] because then it will be public record, right?" she said.

Despite having already tied the knot, she revealed that they plan to have another celebration in May.

“The wedding is in May. The wedding was supposed to be in January, [but] I have a real-life wedding crasher,” she said as she motioned toward her baby bump.

It was previously revealed that the couple began putting roots down for their family home months before she announced her pregnancy, with the soon-to-be parents picking up a $2.2 million pad in February, as first reported by TMZ.

The 5,207-square-foot property was built in 2023 and comes with five bedrooms and 5.5 bathrooms. It includes two fireplaces, chic wood and marble flooring, walk-in closets, and a chef's kitchen with state-of-the-art appliances.

It also features a patio, balcony, and two-car garage.

The home was listed for $2.9 million in June 2025, meaning the expectant mother got over a half-million dollars off.

After her husband's identity was revealed, Chrisley opened up about their romance on her podcast, "Unlocked," telling her fans that she doesn't pay any mind to their large age gap.

"Yes, there is a huge age gap there, OK? And that’s probably part of the reason why I kept a lot of it quiet, because I know how brutal the public can be. But I genuinely do not care," she insisted.

"I have always said, ‘I would rather [have] 20 phenomenal, unbelievable years with someone than 50 ‘meh’ years.' Like, the amount of love and respect that we have, we have so many dreams, and we dream together, we work together ... we want to have life experiences with each other. And I think that’s when you know you have the right person, when you don’t feel like you have to escape them at times. We do everything together. ... And it’s just such an amazing, fulfilling feeling to finally find your person. And I’m there. So, yes, the age difference. So what?"

Chrisley also shed some light on his proposal, which came a mere five months into their relationship.

"When he gave me the ring, he was like, ‘Before I open this, I just want you to know that this is just an expression of love to you. This can be whatever you want it to be. This can be an engagement ring, a promise ring, a cocktail ring for all I care. I just want you to know how much I love you,'" she said. "And there was no pressure ever put on me to, like, ‘You have to accept this as this.'"

Although she acknowledged that the engagement took place fairly early in their relationship, she added, "timing means nothing."

Chrisley announced the news of her pregnancy on Oct. 27 an adorable Instagram post.

“I’ve prayed for this life. I’ve cried for this life. And there were moments I truly wondered if this life would ever be mine. And just when I thought my heart couldn’t hold any ›more love, God gave us our sweetest blessing yet ... we’re having a BABY!," Chrisley said in the social media post announcing the news, which saw her cradling her baby bump while sitting on her kitchen island and kissing Galanis.

“The past few years have taken me through fires I never imagined I’d have to walk through. Seasons of heartbreak, loss, and moments when I didn’t know if I would ever make it to the other side. I wondered if I would ever find my forever ... if I would ever feel safe enough to truly breathe again. And then God sent me J," she added.

“My protector. My fiercest fighter. My most loyal person. My lover. My safe place. A man with the patience of Job (and Lord knows he needs it). The one who makes me laugh even on my hardest days. For the first time in a long time, I feel safe. I feel fulfilled. I can finally exhale. I found my forever in you," Chrisley said.

The TV personality then took a moment to praise her partner for how caring he has been during her pregnancy: “J, watching the way you already love and protect our baby before he/she has even entered this world has made me fall in love with you in an entirely new way. Not a single doctor’s appointment missed. Constantly caring for me, talking about our baby, and reminding me how beautiful my growing bump is. I thank God every day that this baby gets to call you Daddy."

“I once prayed just to make it through another day. Now I get to spend forever with the love of my life, carrying a little miracle we already love more than words. From heartbreak to healing. From surviving to living. From praying for my forever to building a family with him. Only God could have written this story. Here’s to forever, J ... and the sweetest little blessing we can’t wait to meet," she added.

Chrisley had kept the identity of her partner under wraps until now, having previously revealed on her podcast, "Unlocked with Savannah Chrisley,"  that she wouldn't share his name "until after I'm married."

In the latest episode of her podcast, she revealed she and Jason began their romance last July, branding it a "dream relationship."

She revealed that Galanis proposed five months into their relationship when they were in the Cayman Islands. "The ring is insane and it’s everything I ever wanted."

She then revealed that the couple connected after Jason reached out to her to praise her for her efforts to get her parents out of prison.

Todd and Julie Chrisley were found guilty in 2022 of conspiring to defraud Georgia banks out of more than $36 million in personal loans and tax evasion, before being pardoned by President Donald Trump in May 2025.

Savannah Chrisley has frequently spoken out about the toll of her parents' incarceration.

Todd, 57, was sentenced to 12 years (later reduced to 10 years) in the Federal Correctional Institution Pensacola in Florida. Julie, 53, was sentenced to seven years in the Federal Medical Center in Lexington, KY.

Following her parents' incarceration, Savannah took on the care of her brother, Grayson, 19, and her niece, Chloe, 13, while also assuming responsibility for the care and maintenance of her parents' opulent dwelling.

At the same time, the burgeoning real estate agent was tirelessly campaigning for Todd and Julie's release, issuing public pleas for government support, while alleging that her parents were being subjected to "beyond inhumane" conditions behind bars.

On May 27, her prayers were answered when Trump revealed to her in a personal phone call that he planned to pardon her parents. Just a few days later, they were released from prison.

At the time, Savannah revealed she was ready to return the hefty upkeep of their home to Todd and Julie.
