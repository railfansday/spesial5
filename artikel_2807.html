Lakers vs. Jazz Preview: Seeking vengeance

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Lakers (14-3) will play the Utah Jazz (3-14) once again on Tuesday. This will be the final matchup between the two Western Conference teams, with the series tied 1-1.

The Lakers have a much friendlier schedule this week than they've had over the last two. They had two days off — which feels like a rarity this season — leading up to the Jazz game and will get a three-day break before facing the San Antonio Spurs in an NBA Cup matchup on Friday.

Needless to say, it's well deserved for Luka Dončić and the team, who have been grinding hard to stack up wins over the last few weeks. The one game that really got away from them was the most recent loss to the Jazz. L.A. has the opportunity to get Utah back and here are the x-factors to look out for:

AR has been badly missed in these last few games and it has caught up on the Lakers. His absence has put more pressure on Dončić to carry the offense which the superstar has done a stellar job in. Fortunately, Luka has gotten help from Quentin Grimes who has stepped up big time.

With Reaves expected to return in the next two games, the Lakers' offense balances out and that would be more trouble for the Jazz, who benefited from Luka having his worst offensive performance in their last meeting. Hopefully the Lakers get a much-needed boost with the return of their second-best player. They could use his scoring and playmaking against a Jazz team that didn't have an answer for him last time.

There is no team Kessler has struggled more against this season than the one he used to call his own the last couple of years. The center is averaging just 6.0 points in 29.6 minutes against the Jazz this season — which is a far cry from his usual 14.3 per game. It's as if Jazz head coach Will Hardy knows exactly how to contain him and that's something the Lakers and Kessler need to figure out ahead of Monday's game.

It would be nice to see Walker break out of his slump against the Jazz since that'll remove the pressure off Dončić and possibly Reaves. Remember, they still have the 20th-best defensive rating in the league and have no business keeping up with the purple and gold offensively. They also haven't had Lauri Markkanen and some of their other front court pieces, so it would be ideal if the Lakers can take advantage of that.

Not to take credit away from the Jazz but fatigue played a huge factor in the Lakers' recent loss to them. Yes, both teams were on the second night of a back-to-back, but it felt like the purple and gold were a step slower and it made sense because they played in overtime the night before. Hopefully, that won't be the case against the Jazz on Monday.

Having already played each other twice in a month, these two squads are quite familiar with each other now. The Lakers can't allow Keyonte George to drop more than 30 points on them again and have someone like Svi Mykhailiuk have his way. They'll be fighting hard for this one, hoping to get contributions from everyone on the team.

Let's see if the Lakers can indeed exact revenge against the Jazz on Monday.

Reaves will be out for the Lakers yet again with a heel bruise. He's the only player on the injury report.

As for the Jazz, Jaren Jackson Jr. (MCL tear), Lauri Markkanen (hand fracture) and Jusuf Nurkic (plantar plate tear) are all out.
