Commanders, Cowboys get help from schedule in effort to chase down the Giants

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The New York Giants are leading the NFC East after 10 weeks of play, but the gap is closing. The Dallas Cowboys and Washington Commanders are hoping to take them down.

There likely will only be one team coming out of the division for the playoffs, and the Giants want to make sure they are the ones that come out on top. Here's a look at each team's remaining schedule and how difficult it will be for each team to navigate.

Current standing: 4–6, second place in NFC East

Remaining opponents: Titans, Eagles, at Seahawks, at Rams, Jaguars, Giants, at Commanders

The Cowboys have one of the easier remaining schedules in the league, but they remain in second place in the division behind the Giants. They have very winnable games coming up, with the Titans and Eagles on deck, but then things get more difficult with the Seahawks, Rams, and Jaguars before the rematch against the Giants at AT&T Stadium later in the season.

That three-game stretch before that rematch against the Giants could knock them out. Some wins against some powerful teams will likely keep them in the hunt and give them some momentum before facing the Giants in their first game of the 2027 calendar year.

Current standing: 3-5-1, third place in NFC East

Remaining opponents: Bengals, at Cardinals, at Titans, Texans, Falcons, at Vikings, at Jaguars, Cowboys

The Commanders' win against the Giants on Thursday Night Football last week kept their season alive, and their schedule is about to get easier.

After this week's Monday Night Football game against the Bengals, the schedule gets much easier for the Commanders. They will play four consecutive games against teams that don't have a better record than them, which could lead into a run leading up to the end of the season.

The final three games are a bit more challenging, as they face two likely playoff teams on the road before finishing the season against the Cowboys in a game that could decide the division if the Giants fold in the second half of the year.

Remaining opponents: Jaguars, at Colts, 49ers, at Seahawks, Browns, at Lions, at Cowboys, Eagles

The Giants have one of the toughest schedules remaining in the league, as only three teams will face a more challenging gauntlet in the second half of the season. With seven games to go, four come against teams that are likely heading to the playoffs.

If they were to lose all four of those games, they would need to sweep the Colts, Cowboys, and Eagles in order to get to eight wins. That might be enough to win the NFC East this year, but it is far from a guarantee.

The Giants should want to beat some of these top teams to give them reason to believe that they can compete in the playoffs. That all starts with this weekend's contest against the Jaguars, which is arguably the team's toughest test to date.

Kickoff between the Jags and Giants is scheduled for Sunday at 4:25 p.m. inside MetLife Stadium. Fans can watch the game on CBS or stream it on the Paramount+ app.
