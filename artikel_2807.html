Highlights: Spurs rule once again on the road against the Kings

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The San Antonio Spurs kept the show rolling on the road for their 8th win. This time, the boys in Black and White notched a win without starters such as Victor Wembanyama, De'Aaron Fox, and Dylan Harper. Stephon Castle led the way with 28 points, 11 assists, and 8 rebounds. Julian Champagnie and Luke Kornet also played a huge role in the win without 22 points and 18 points scored, respectively. Kornet slotted in seamlessly and controlled the glass to the tune of 11 rebounds to achieve a double-double.

Jordan McLaughlin likes his turkey dry, but he provided plenty of sauce on this move to the bucket. The wily veteran guard (and former Sacramento King) stepped up for the short-staffed Spurs and contributed 6 points and 2 rebounds in 14 minutes of solid play.

Speaking of White Castle, they are opening up a White Castle (burger restaurant) in the DFW area, which is the only good thing about living up here as a Spurs fan away from my P. Terry's and Hawx Burger Bar (R.I.P.). I know P.Terry's is more Austin than San Antonio, but sometimes you want something like In 'N Out, but slightly less California-y and more Austin-y even though they're both the same type of burgers and fries with a small menu and similar vibe. If you don't agree, that's your right to do so, but it doesn't bring back my beloved Hawx Burger Bar.

I usually do not post highlights of three-pointers (unless they are really, really extraordinary like full-court shots or Victor Wembanyama bouncing one off the backboard before he takes a three (he WILL attempt this before it's all said and done)) because dunking and Euro-stepping through a sea of 7-foot-wing-spanned arms seems more extraordinary. That being said, I want to take a moment to appreciate Spurs rookie Ja'Kobi Gillispie's pretty shot from downtown. His shot was so pretty, I want to name it "Lucia" and dote on it like apparently one does on Grand Theft Auto VI.

Keldon Johnson flicked his wrist for this prompt alley-oop to Luke Kornet. Kornet, filling in for Victor Wembanyama, had a superb box score of 18 points and 11 rebounds. Perhaps just having extra minutes to get some burn is exactly what Kornet needs to get into a groove as the season wears on. The team and Spurs fans would certainly love to see the big man provide some punch from the bench, especially to give Wembanyama a breather either in minutes or entire games.

Watch closely below at that subtle move Kornet makes when he fakes a pass and then blows by his defender for the jam. That's exactly what you have to do with the Thanksgiving sides when you want to take more than your share after someone passes you the dish: you gotta fake passing it to the next person and dump the entire contents of smoked cabbage (w/bacon) onto your plate. If your daughter starts crying, the wife begins to contemplate divorce, and the dog looks at you with shame, well you just think of how Luke Kornet shook off his defender and you remind yourself that winning takes sacrifice.

Kornet off another alley-oop, once again reminding us that Thanksgiving mac and cheese is not Thanksgiving mac and cheese if you don't use smoked gouda cheese with sharp cheddar. Also, fried or smoked turkey is the preferred method if you have the setup.

Happy Thanksgiving, fellow Pounders! May your gravy boat flow over with gratitude and blessings.
