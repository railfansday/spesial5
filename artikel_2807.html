Dike leads list of former Florida Gators starring in NFL Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Florida football had several former Gators deliver standout performances during Week 11 in the NFL. Their presence was felt across the league on offense, defense and special teams as the regular season enters its final stretch.

Highlighted by franchise and career milestones, players who once donned the Orange and Blue made a mark for their respective teams.

Chimere Dike continued his emergence as a versatile offensive weapon for Tennessee, producing 200 all-purpose yards in the Titans' 51-30 loss to Dallas. As a receiver, Dike caught six passes for 69 yards but also contributed 127 yards as a kick returner — his lone rushing attempt netted four yards.

The No. 103 overall selection in the 2025 NFL draft surpassed Mel Gray for sixth place on the franchise's career kickoff return yardage list, reaching 2,700 yards.

In Carolina, Princely Umanmielen posted his best pass-rushing performance of the year, recording four tackles and a career-high 1.5 sacks in the Panthers' 35-33 victory over Baltimore. The former Gators' crunch-time sack helped stall the Ravens final drive. Pro Football Focus gave the 2025 third-round selection a 72.3 defensive grade, ranking third among all Panthers defenders.

Ventrell Miller remained an effective cog in Jacksonville's impressive defensive campaign, despite the Jaguars' 23-18 loss to the New York Giants. The fourth-year linebacker collected seven tackles, matching his second-highest single-game total this season. Miller has registered at least six tackles in seven games, fortifying himself as a steady presence in the Jaguars' defensive rotation.

Jonathan Greenard notched a sack in consecutive appearances for the first time since 2024, while Evan McPherson made both field goal attempts in the Bengals' 22-20 loss to the Commanders on Monday Night Football. His 56-yarder with 46 seconds remaining was the seventh of 50 yards or more this season.
