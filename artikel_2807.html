North Bay Haven softball players sign to continue athletic careers

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

LYNN HAVEN, Fla. — North Bay Haven senior Bailee Gladden will continue her softball career at Florida Gulf Coast University, putting a milestone on a journey that began when she joined the school as a sixth grader.

For Gladden, signing day represented both the excitement of reaching a goal and the realization that her time at North Bay Haven is beginning to wind down.

"I'm thinking about all the memories I've had with my coaches and all the past coaches, and now it's all kind of coming to an end," Gladden said. "I'm going to have to go to college, but it makes me really happy. I love my coaches, so I'm excited to go."

Gladden said playing college softball wasn't necessarily something she envisioned when she first arrived at North Bay Haven.

"Not at all," she said when asked if she imagined signing to play collegiately as a sixth grader. "I just always wanted to play. I never really thought about anything after, because I never really thought about it becoming an end."

Her decision to attend FGCU was influenced heavily by the coaching staff and the atmosphere surrounding the program.

"Honestly, the coaches," Gladden said when asked what attracted her to the school. "The coaches kind of brought me down there, and I'm glad they did, because I love being back here."

Gladden committed to FGCU in October and said she never felt overwhelmed by the recruiting process. Instead, she focused on playing her best and allowing things to fall into place.

"I kind of didn't think about it because I knew what was going to happen," she said. "Just let it all pan out and just do the best that I can."

With one final high school season remaining, Gladden wants to enjoy every moment with the teammates and friends who have been part of her journey.

"I just want to play this last season with fun," she said. "I can still be with my best friends that I've had for years. I kind of just want to play freely and have fun."

Although she is excited about the opportunity ahead, Gladden knows leaving North Bay Haven won't be easy.

"I think it's going to be hard to say goodbye," she said. "I just hope that I can come up to visit, and they'll come watch."

Senior Molly Jimenez will continue her playing career at Wallace Community College, a decision she said ultimately gave her the opportunity to find the right fit both on and off the field.

Although Wallace wasn't originally her first choice, Jimenez said the opportunity became the best option as her plans changed.

"It was not my first choice, but it was the best choice," Jimenez said. "I had other plans, and it just didn't work out. I felt like I had a better place somewhere else, so I decided to just sign with Wallace. I'm really excited. I'm happy to be going there."

Jimenez's commitment came together quickly. She said she had been committed to Wallace for less than a week before signing day, leaving little time to prepare for the occasion.

The short turnaround added some stress, but she was ultimately happy to make her decision official.

"I'm just really happy now to know that it's been said. I'm going to be going there," Jimenez said. "I'm happy. It was a good place for me, basically."

The signing represents another milestone in a journey that included a difficult transition to softball after spending much of her childhood competing in gymnastics.

Jimenez began gymnastics at approximately 5 years old and continued until she was 13, spending as many as 40 hours a week in the gym. The sport helped develop her mental toughness but also came with physical challenges, including back and knee injuries.

Leaving gymnastics for softball wasn't an easy decision, but she said the transition ultimately proved beneficial.

"It was like leaving something that I knew was comfortable to go into something that I might think is better and might be better for me," Jimenez said. "And it ended up being the best thing."

After experiencing some difficult years at a previous school, Jimenez said finding happiness and rediscovering her love for softball became her biggest priorities entering her senior season.

"The biggest thing for me was just to keep that love for softball instead of putting so much pressure on myself," she said.

Her first season at North Bay Haven has helped provide that opportunity. Jimenez credited the coaching staff and her teammates for creating a welcoming environment where she feels comfortable being herself.

As she prepares for the next step at Wallace, Jimenez is focused on enjoying her final high school season and making the most of the environment she has found at North Bay Haven.

"I wanted to go somewhere that I was happy and that I was loved and appreciated and all that stuff," she said. "And I know that I have that here."
