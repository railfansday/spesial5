Discussion: Is today’s game a must win for the Panthers?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It's rare that an interconference match-up in November has enough stakes to be considered a must win, but the Carolina Panthers might find themselves in a position where that's the case. They're currently just 4-5, but that's enough to lead the NFC South by a game over the Saints and Buccaneers with the Falcons just one extra loss behind. It's a tightly packed race, and by rule, someone has to win the division and make the playoffs.

The Panthers are coming off a key step towards winning the division with a road win over the Saints last week. Up next is the Ravens. Going into the season, this looked like a tough game, probably even penciled in as a loss for the Panthers. But now the Ravens are on their third string quarterback who they just traded for like two weeks ago. The Panthers are favorites, so they should win this game. But the question is, do they have to?

MY TAKE: Yes it is. The Panthers still could win the division with a loss, but their road will be a whole lot tougher for it. After this game, the Panthers will have seven games left on their schedule. Three of those are against NFC South rivals with one game apiece. The Panthers can win all of those. But winning all of those gets them to seven wins, and seven wins seems very unlikely to make the playoffs in a 17 game schedule, no matter how bad the division is. They have to get at least one or two more wins from outside the division from here on out, and this is by far the best chance for them to do it.

This is definitely the Panthers' chance to get a win, and it's very likely it's the only game they'll be favored in. The Steelers are gettable, but the other three teams on this schedule have legitimate Super Bowl aspirations. It's hard to see the Panthers stealing one of those games.

The best path for the Panthers to make the playoffs is to take care of their division games and steal at least one more game outside the division. The rest of the non-divisional schedule is brutal, so that makes today a must win game, in my opinion.
