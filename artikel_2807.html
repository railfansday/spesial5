Grizzlies, Giannis-led Heat take leaps in Post’s NBA Power Rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Each week, the California Post will power rank the NBA's 30 teams. Records are as of Saturday morning:

Victor Wembanyama dribbles up the court. Getty Images

The reigning Western Conference champions' 12-game winning streak may have ended with their loss to the Pistons on Tuesday. But they bounced back with a win over the Clippers and remain the only team ranked top five in offensive and defensive efficiency. But their status at the top of the league will be tested with Dylan Harper expected to be sidelined for a couple of weeks due to a strained left hip. (Last week, 1st)

Luka Dončić has been leading the Austin-Reaves Lakers over the last week. AP Photo/Caroline Brehman

The Lakers maintain their spot behind the Spurs despite Wednesday's home loss to the Jazz ending their winning streak at nine games and the overall struggles they've had during the homestand: a two-point victory over the one-win Kings on Friday and needing overtime to beat the Hornets earlier in the week. They're 6-1 in their last seven games despite Luka Dončić and Austin Reaves, who's missed the last four games, only being available at the same time for three-fourths of a game over the last two weeks. (Last week, 2nd)

Boston Celtics head coach Joe Mazzulla talks with forward Paul George (13) and forward Jayson Tatum (0). IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

The Celtics were rolling, winning six consecutive games and 11 of 12, before back-to-back losses to the Magic and Nuggets in which they struggled offensively. This past week was a reminder that even the league's best are vulnerable to off-nights. (Last week, 3rd)

Philadelphia 76ers center Joel Embiid (21) shoots over Brooklyn Nets center Day'Ron Sharpe (20). AP Photo/Yuki Iwamura

The 76ers are starting to normalize to their preseason expectations — their offense is trending up and their defense is regressing. But they entered the weekend winners of four straight games after an impressive midweek win over the Magic. (Last week, 4th)

Minnesota Timberwolves head coach Chris Finch speaks on the sideline as guard Bones Hyland looks on. AP Photo/Ellen Schmidt

Back-to-back wins over the Trail Blazers bumped the Timberwolves up a spot despite ending the week with a loss to the Knicks. The Timberwolves continue to thrive despite being without All-NBA Anthony Edwards. (Last week, 6th)

Kevin Durant #7 of the Houston Rockets brings the ball up court. Getty Images

A blowout loss to the Heat and another loss vs. the Nuggets, which prompted coach Ime Udoka to call out his team's defense, knocked the Rockets down a spot. But they remain one of the league's best offensive teams, with six players averaging at least 10 points and seven averaging at least nine. (Last week, 5th)

Cade Cunningham continues to lead the Jalen Duren-less Pistons. Getty Images

The Pistons picked up their best win of the season when they decisively beat the Spurs on Tuesday. They continue to hover around the top of the standings despite All-NBA center Jalen Duren being sidelined for the last month. (Last week, 8th)

The Memphis Grizzlies continue to win despite the absence of Ty Jerome. NBAE via Getty Images

Led by the league's best offense, the Grizzlies continue to be the early-season surprise by winning five consecutive games entering the weekend despite Ty Jerome being sidelined for the entire week, showcasing the depth of this team. (Last week, 11th)

Nikola Jokic is coming out of his early-season slump. Getty Images

Three-time league MVP Nikola Jokić is coming out of his early-season slump (at least by his lofty standards), and the Nuggets are starting to look like the team most expected to factor in the dark-horse contender conversations after beating the Celtics and Rockets this past week. (Last week, 10th)

New York Knicks' Jalen Brunson handles the ball. NBAE via Getty Images

The defending NBA champions are still searching for nightly consistency, but they won their last three games while two of their most important players in Mikal Bridges and Josh Hart stepped up their play. (Last week, 13th)

Portland Trail Blazers' Damian Lillard shoots a free throw. NBAE via Getty Images

Back-to-back losses to the Edwards-less Timberwolves — including one that came down to the wire on Tuesday — have the Trail Blazers outside of the top 10 since the first week of the season. (Last week, 7th)

Miami Heat forward Giannis Antetokounmpo reacts after scoring against the Houston Rockets. Sam Navarro-Imagn Images

The Heat continue to be the league's hottest team after a bad opening week. They picked up impressive wins over the Pistons and Rockets since the previous rankings, with Giannis Antetokounmpo (28.2 points, 12.6 rebounds, 5.5 assists, 1.3 blocked shots) showing he's still one of the league's best players. (Last week, 16th)

Hawks forward Jalen Johnson leads the team in assists. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

The Hawks' 3-0 week, including a win over the Thunder, moves them up a spot. With most of their wins in November coming against the league's bottom-feeders, matchups against the Pistons, Celtics, Knicks and Heat over the next week and a half will be good tests for how good they are. (Last week, 14th)

Oklahoma City Thunder guard Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (2). Daniel Dunn-Imagn Images

The Thunder are starting to feel the challenges of life without Jalen Williams, Chet Holmgren and Cason Wallace. They're 2-4 in their last six games, with their lone wins coming against the one-win Kings and struggling Pelicans. (Last week, 9th)

Cooper Flagg of the Dallas Mavericks. NBAE via Getty Images

The level of competition picked up for the Mavericks, which led to a three-game losing streak that ended with a blowout win over the Warriors on Wednesday. But Cooper Flagg being ready to lead a playoff-level team before his 20th birthday is the most important development of their season so far. (Last week, 15th)

Cleveland Cavaliers guard James Harden (1) turned back the clock against the Indiana Pacers. AP Photo/Tim Phillis

James Harden delivered the highlight of the Cavaliers' season with his 51-point performance in Friday's win over the Pacers, passing Kobe Bryant for the third-most 50-point games in NBA history. Maybe that'll give the Cavaliers the spark they need after hovering around .500 during the season's first month. (Last week, 17th)

Phoenix Suns' Devin Booker drives to the basket. NBAE via Getty Images

The Suns have won three straight games and seven of their last nine, including an impressive win over the Nuggets early in the week before the schedule lightened up again. Their bench continues to be the bright spot. (Last week, 18th)

Orlando Magic forward Franz Wagner (22) has been the biggest factor in his team's recent success. Robert Sabo for NY Post

Franz Wagner continues to make his case for being a first-time All-Star, averaging 31.5 points, 7.8 assists and 7.5 rebounds during the Magic's 6-1 stretch over their last seven games. (Last week, 20th)

Life without Steph Curry has been tough for the Golden State Warriors. AP Photo/David Zalubowski

Life without franchise icon Steph Curry has been treacherous for the Warriors, who are on a five-game losing streak. And they're not only losing but also getting blown out. They've lost their last four games without Curry by a combined 109 points. (Last week, 12th)

Anthony Davis of the Washington Wizards shoots a free throw. NBAE via Getty Images

Anthony Davis has been the biggest bright spot for a Wizards team that ended an eight-game losing streak this past week with two straight wins over the Raptors and Bulls. (Last week, 22nd)

Immanuel Quickley of the Toronto Raptors dribbles against the Detroit Pistons. NBAE via Getty Images

The Raptors ended a six-game losing streak with a victory over the Pistons on Friday. It's clear the availability of Kawhi Leonard, who's only played in two games, is a necessity and not a luxury. (Last week, 19th)

Pascal Siakam has been a bright spot for the Indiana Pacers. NBAE via Getty Images

A winless week drops the Pacers down the rankings. They desperately need Tyrese Haliburton back in the lineup. (Last week, 21st)

Utah Jazz guard Keyonte George had 30 points and 10 assists against the Lakers earlier in the week. NBAE via Getty Images

Beating the Clippers and Lakers on a back-to-back for an LA sweep bumps the Jazz up a few spots. Keyonte George is the real deal. (Last week, 27th)

Brook Lopez has been a bright spot for the Clippers. AP Photo/Mark J. Terrill

Salute to Brook Lopez, who's been the Clippers' leading scorer in three of their last four games. The 38-year-old Lopez is averaging 15.4 points for the season, which is his most since 2022-23 and second most since 2017. (Last week, 25th)

Norman Powell (24) of the Chicago Bulls drives to the basket against Julius Randle (30) of the Brooklyn Nets. Getty Images

The win over the Timberwolves was the surprise of the Bulls' 2-2 week. Norman Powell has been a bright spot since Caleb Wilson fractured his hand. (Last week, 28th)

Dejounte Murray #5 of the New Orleans Pelicans has requested a trade. Getty Images

The Pelicans are tumbling down the standings and rankings after losing eight of their last nine games. (Last week, 23rd)

Luka Doncic #77 of the Los Angeles Lakers battles Moussa Diabate #14 of the Charlotte Hornets. Getty Images

The Hornets lost every game of their four-game trip, but at least they showed life in the narrow loss to the Lakers. (Last week, 24th)

Julius Randle of the Brooklyn Nets. NBAE via Getty Images

Julius Randle's frustration, which was captured in a viral video after a blowout loss to the Bulls, sums up the Nets' season. They've lost their last four games. (Last week, 26th)

Dylan Cardwell of the Sacramento Kings plays defense on Walker Kessler of the Los Angeles Lakers. NBAE via Getty Images

The losses continue to pile up for the Kings, who've dropped eight straight games. (Last week, 30th)

Milwaukee Bucks' Tyler Herro (11) drives to the basket. NBAE via Getty Images

At this point, the Bucks and Kings continue to jockey for the bottom spot in the rankings. But at least the Kings have won a game in November. The Bucks can't say the same. (Last week, 29th)
