Aaron Rodgers goes unclaimed on waivers after explosive Steelers exit

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Aaron Rodgers is officially a free agent.

The veteran quarterback went unclaimed on waivers Thursday, Nov. 26, one day after the Pittsburgh Steelers parted ways with him following his incendiary public criticism of coach Mike McCarthy.

Rodgers had been subjected to waivers due to the release coming after the Nov. 10 trade deadline. Now, the four-time NFL MVP – who announced in the spring that he planed to retire following the season – is untethered, though it's unclear whether there's another move left to be made.

On Tuesday night, news broke that the Steelers would be benching Rodgers for Friday's game against the Denver Broncos. The team later confirmed that Mason Rudolph would take over as the starter and that Rodgers would be inactive for the contest.

In an appearance on "The Pat McAfee Show" on Wednesday, Rodgers alleged that McCarthy had fallen asleep during team meetings and missed others. He also said that the former Green Bay coach closed out a halftime speech during Sunday's 19-16 loss to the Philadelphia Eagles by saying, "one, two, three, Packers." McCarthy confirmed that account in a news conference, calling it an "embarrassing moment."

McCarthy said things came to a head with Rodgers when the quarterback made an "ultimatum" that the coach considered "clearly outside of (Rodgers') job description." Rodgers revealed that he told McCarthy that he should relinquish play-calling duties or have the team release him.

In making his explosive remarks, Rodgers acknowledged his time with the Steelers was over, saying "that ship has sailed." Shortly after, the team made his release official.

Rodgers, who turns 43 on Dec. 2, now faces an uncertain landscape, with the overwhelming majority of teams having settled their quarterback outlooks by the trade deadline.

"I didn't see this happening, and I didn't want this to be the way that it ended," Rodgers said Wednesday.
