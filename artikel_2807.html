Hunter Henry, Keisean Nixon are active for Patriots-Packers clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Patriots will be missing wide receivers A.J. Brown and Romeo Doubs on Sunday, but they will have tight end Hunter Henry as a target for quarterback Drake Maye.

Henry is active after being listed as questionable on Friday due to a foot injury that kept him out of practice for two days this week. Safety Kevin Byard, cornerback Carlton Davis, right tackle Morgan Moses, special teamer Brenden Schooler, and guard Greg Van Roten are also playing after drawing questionable tags.

Brown, cornerback Charles Woods, running back TreVeyon Henderson, offensive tackle Dametrious Crownover, and quarterback Behren Morton are inactive for New England.

Cornerback Keisean Nixon has a groin injury, just he is active for the Packers. Running back MarShawn Lloyd, linebacker Zaire Franklin, offensive lineman Donovan Jennings, center Sean Rhyan, offensive lineman Travis Glover, and tight end Josh Whyle are out for Green Bay.
