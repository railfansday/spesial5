If your credit is bad or fair and you can’t spare a deposit, start with a no-fee unsecured card like the Capital One Platinum. Check first whether you prequalify, since prequalifying won’t affect your credit score. Pay an annual fee only when its cash back covers the fee, or after pricing it against a secured card.

A secured card asks for a deposit up front, and you get it back when you close the account paid off. An upgrade to a regular card can return it too, as a statement credit. An annual fee is a charge, not a deposit you get back, so a no-fee secured card often costs less than any card here that charges one.
