Alanis Morissette Reveals She’s Pansexual During Podcast Interview

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Alanis Morissette has publicly revealed she is pansexual, sharing the news during a November 3 appearance on the Handsome podcast. The 52-year-old Grammy winner confirmed the disclosure after a joking question about whether heartbreak made her want to be a lesbian, replying, 'I'm pansexual, so yeah.' The revelation has drawn widespread attention and adds another layer of honesty to a career defined by emotional openness and songs like Jagged Little Pill.
