mara rethinks how we furnish modern, flexible office at orgatec 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

mara unveils an ecosystem of flexible office furniture at orgatec 2026, which aims to shape the future of dynamic, remote-friendly workspaces.

The past decade has seen countless changes in the working landscape, and Mara’s furniture has evolved alongside them. Orgatec 2026, the trade fair dedicated to workplace design, provides the setting for the brand to present some of the latest developments helping to shape this world.

First came digitalization. Then lockdown. Finally, the rise of digital nomads. Offices around the world have witnessed continuous changes in workflows and in the understanding of work itself. Today, teams have flatter structures, remote work is commonplace, and everyday working life often takes shape through dynamic, fluid relationships.

Offices have necessarily changed in the process. From height-adjustable desks that prioritize health over the rigidity of the past to meeting rooms equipped with tailored furniture and buildings that integrate outdoor spaces into daily life, the environments we work in reflect the way we work. This is why Orgatec helps us take the pulse of how we furnish our offices and how we inhabit them.

Aligned with this increasingly fluid and flexible world, the collection Mara presents at Orgatec 2026 stands out for its versatility, flexibility, and durability.

One of the most talked-about developments is the expansion of its Aera collection. Designed by Marcello Ziliani, the seating system adapts to new needs with the addition of a writing tablet for the conference version, suitable for training, education, and meeting environments. The brand also introduces a new swivel version on a five-star base with casters, designed specifically for offices and collaborative spaces where mobility is essential.

Meanwhile, the Otto collection of height-adjustable tables, designed by Christophe Pillet, expands with new fixed-height versions.

Alongside these additions, Mara presents a new interpretation of a classic: Aera Metal, which draws on the brand’s metalworking roots. Retaining Aera’s lightweight, stackable, and practical character, the new all-metal version naturally extends into outdoor settings, helping create fluid spaces without boundaries. This proposal builds on Mara’s more than sixty years of metalworking experience and its commitment to durable, recyclable materials.

The Elle collection, designed by Ferruccio Laviani, also evolves. Specifically, Elle Boxes and Elle Wardrobe take it from a storage system to a multifunctional solution that structures a room as part of its architecture. It combines display, storage, and organization flexibly, responding to the needs of contemporary workplaces.

Orgatec also makes room for celebrations. Ten years have passed since Mara designed its iconic Argo Libro table system, created to meet an emerging need for flexibility in working environments. To mark the anniversary, the brand introduces a special limited edition in polished steel, transforming the piece into a mirrored surface that tells a story.

Beyond its own exhibition, Mara also participates in the Innovation Award Architecture+ Office 2026 installation with its Typo Lounge Chair & Ottoman, designed by AMDL CIRCLE, the studio founded by Michele De Lucchi. Additionally, the brand furnishes the shared seating areas in Hall 6.1 with its Aera chairs, Typo tables, Follow Break, and Typo Lounge Chair & Ottoman.
