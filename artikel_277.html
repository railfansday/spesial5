If you’re tired of filament jams and clogged nozzles, perhaps it’s time for a new 3D printer. Through December 1st, the Bambu Lab P2S Combo, currently one of the best models that’s easy to use, is discounted. It’s $649 at Best Buy, as well as directly from Bambu Lab. That’s $100 off the list price for the bundle, which includes the Bambu P2S printer and the AMS 2 Pro multi-filament handler for multicolor prints.

Bambu Lab’s P2S Combo is a 3D printer that can print in multiple colors and automatically dry its materials to help improve print quality. It also features a 5-inch touchscreen, a built-in camera for monitoring prints remotely, and AI-powered tools that can detect printing problems.

My colleague Sean Hollister praised the P2S printer for its consistency and hands-off approach to 3D printing. Automatic bed leveling, including a warning if you forget to reinstall the plate, and a bevy of sensors helps ensure every print goes smoothly. There’s a live camera feed, too, so you can check in on your prints or share a timelapse. On its own, the P2S printer is usually $549, but is also currently $100 off.

The AMS 2 Pro’s filament handler’s fully enclosed setup saves your filament from absorbing moisture and clogging the nozzle or tubes. As a result, you can go weeks between prints and fire one off from your computer without checking on colors or manually swapping filaments. The AMS 2 Pro itself is typically $299, but is currently on sale for $229, and the P2S printer supports up to four AMS 2 Pro units simultaneously.
