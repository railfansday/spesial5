LeBron James sues sports betting startup over unauthorized use of ‘Bron’ nickname

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

NBA superstar LeBron James is suing the makers of sports betting app Bron, claiming that it is intentionally ripping off his nickname. (Photo from Shutterstock)

NBA superstar LeBron James is suing the makers of sports betting app Bron, claiming that it is intentionally ripping off his nickname.

In a federal complaint filed Tuesday in the Southern District of New York, James said he has been known as “Bron” since he was a young basketball standout in Ohio. He alleges that PlayVision and its co-founders, Anish Gupta and Marc Zoghby, seek “to trade on” his “valuable name and reputation” by naming their app Bron.

There is no other connection between the word and the defendants or any other feature of their app, James alleges. He also argues in the complaint that when the defendants launched a marketing campaign for the Bron app earlier this month, they used a video advertisement that incorporated an artificial intelligence-generated version of him.

“The AI-generated version of Mr. James sits in an NBA-style locker room wearing a basketball jersey bearing Mr. James’s nickname, ‘Bron,’ above the number ‘23’—a number associated with Mr. James and the one he currently wears on the Philadelphia 76ers—with physical traits that mimic Mr. James, down to his identifiable tattoos,” according to the complaint.

“Speaking in Mr. James’s voice, the AI-generated Mr. James scrolls through wagers on other NBA players in defendants’ Bron app,” the complaint continues. “The ad then urges viewers to ‘scroll to bet.’”

The lawsuit seeks damages and an injunction preventing the defendants from using the “Bron” name; James’ name, image and likeness; and voice.

Courthouse News Service has additional reporting.

A representative for Bron did not respond to the publication’s request for comment.
