The Best Leggings for Working Out in 2026, Tested by Style Editors

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A pair of workout leggings is the most important piece of activewear you’ll buy—but it’s also one of the hardest to get right. Too compressive and they dig in. Too soft and they stretch out after a few washes. And if they slide down mid-sprint or turn sheer in a squat, they’re done. Since Women’s Health was founded in 2005, our editors have sweat-tested workout leggings through strength training, long runs, Pilates, and HIIT to find pairs that stay opaque, supportive, and comfortable through every session.

To help you find the right leggings for every activity, I built the ultimate list, from great leggings for low-impact activities like yoga to the best compression leggings.

I’ve trusted Nike leggings since my middle school days, circa 2012, and this pair is no exception. I love that the waistband never moves, no matter how much I bend, jump, run, or sweat. I’ve worn the original pair for years, but I love that the newest version has no front seam, for an even more flattering look. Pictured here is the flared version (which I love for lounging), but I typically reach for the standard legging silhouette for most workouts to avoid the extra fabric getting in the way.

“I first tried these Nike leggings during the brand’s month-long running challenge in 2025, and they’ve stayed at the top of my extensive leggings drawer ever since,” says style contributor Lily Wohlner. “They allow me to move freely during all sorts of movements, they’re super durable, and are buttery soft for maximum comfort.”

I find these leggings to be extremely flattering—even my mom told me I look good in them (that’s how you know they’re good!). The second you slip these on, every curve on your legs and thighs magically tightens thanks to the body-hugging nylon and spandex in the fabric. I also appreciate that Nike left out the side pockets with this pair, so they don’t add any width to the thighs. But, there’s still a back pocket that’s big enough to hold your phone, which is a welcome plus.

These are one of my favorite leggings to run in because they stay in place. The high waistband never moves or slips down, so I never find myself adjusting it while in motion. At the gym, they’re stretchy enough to accommodate any move from deadlifts to downward dog, but they still have enough compression to make me feel held in and supported. I’ve done my fair share of squats in these leggings—I actually wore them during a few lower-body training sessions—and they pass the squat test every time. Another bonus? They’re super moisture-wicking, so I worry less about sweat stains peeking through at the gym.

They are super flattering, and they've garnered the most compliments I've ever received from a pair of leggings.

At $105 they are on the pricer side, but they’ve never once declined in quality since I’ve owned them, and I’ve probably washed them over 80 times.

I’ve been singing praises for Crz Yoga ever since I joined the team at Women’s Health over a year ago. The affordable brand makes some of the best workout clothes on Amazon with quality that actually competes with high-end labels. Think: smooth, stretchy fabric that holds up over tons of wear and wash cycles.

The 81% nylon and 19% elastane blend (aka the brand’s famed Butterluxe fabric) has the second-skin softness and light-to-moderate compression that feels good during just about any workout. I love the high-rise waistband that doesn’t slip down during HIIT workouts, weight lifting, or long walks.

This pair even earned a spot in our guide to the best leggings with pockets for the dual side pockets that securely hold small essentials like a keycard or headphones without adding too much bulk to the hips. Oh, and I love that it comes in a ton of colors (most of them with sports bras to match!), from classic neutrals to brighter, bolder shades.

My only con is that it only comes in a single length, which may bunch slightly at the ankles on petite frames, and while the pockets are handy, they’re not the most secure option for larger phones during high-impact workouts.

For a long time, I always opted for the softest, stretchiest leggings in my drawer, no matter the workout. However, in the past year, I’ve been doing a lot more strength training, and I’ve found myself swapping soft pairs for more compressive options like the Wunder Train to stay locked in and supported during moves like jump squats, burpees, and quick-burst movements. I never thought I’d be a compression leggings gal, but once I tried them, I never went back.

The Wunder Train Leggings are made out of 77 percent nylon and 23 percent elastane (a light, stretchy fabric). The higher elastane content makes them super stretchy. During my weekly Barry's HIIT workout class, these leggings stay put and keep me feeling secure, supported, and snatched. With softer leggings, I’m always adjusting them or feeling like they don’t offer enough support during deep bends and hinges. The hidden drawstring in the waistband of these leggings also helps.One of my top priorities when it comes to leggings is absolutely no sweat stains. And this pair nails it thanks to the sweat-wicking nylon, which is super durable and keeps you dry. I’ve had some seriously sweaty gym sessions in them, and while sweat does build up after intense workouts, it dries way faster than other leggings I’ve tried thanks to its high nylon content in the fabric. I also love how the fabric feels light, breathable, and cool against the skin, so it doesn’t cause overheating like some of my other leggings. That said, I only own these in darker colors, so I can’t speak to how well they hide sweat stains on lighter shades.

I’ve worn these leggings during many different types of workouts, and they always strike the perfect balance of support and comfort. These are soft enough for a stretching or yoga class, but really shine in any exercise that gets your heart rate pumping and the sweat flowing. Plus, the moisture-wicking fabric and its odor-fighting finish keep me feeling fresh, even when things get super sweaty.

I’m also a mega fan of the wide waistband that stays put through any exercise. And get this: If you’re out in the sun, these leggings add a great extra layer of defense to your sunscreen, as they’re equipped with UPF 50 for sun protection.

My one con with these leggings is that they run large, as does all of Rhone’s womenswear. I would size down if you’re looking for a normal fit with light compression.

Beyond Yoga leggings in their signature (super soft) Spacedye fabric have been my go-to for lounging and low-impact yoga for years. But recently, the activewear label dropped Glowzone, its new high-performance line with a snappy fabric designed to support intense, sweaty movement while still offering the comfort we know and love.

The Glowzone fabric is made of smooth nylon and a bit more elastane than the brand’s famed Spacedye fabric, giving it noticeably more compression. It’s snappy and compressive enough that it keeps me feeling supported and locked-in during my workouts without restricting movement or sliding down my waist (something I often have trouble with while wearing other compressive leggings).

Despite the more snappy fabric, it’s every bit as soft and comfortable as I’d expect from a pair of Beyond Yoga leggings. I still reach for mine on more chill days spent walking the boardwalk near the beach or running errands around town—but the material is just a touch too compressive to wear for lazy days spent couch rotting. Where it really shines is for working out: I’ve worn it to the gym for cardio and lifting sessions as well as mat Pilates classes, and I love that I can basically forget about what I’m wearing while I focus on getting my reps in.

Contributor Sidney Lee tested a few items from the Glowzone collection and agrees the fabric is every bit as soft as she hoped. “It’s slightly thinner than Spacedye, which makes Glowzone feel sleeker and more breathable against the skin,” she says. “Also, the four-way stretch and moisture-wicking technology of the new fabric offer more freedom of movement, especially during heated classes or summer workouts.”

Another huge bonus? This pair is basically seamless (the only seams are located on the inside of the legs and at the buttock) so there’s no rubbing or chafing. There’s no front seam (which helps keep the fit looking flattering, if you know what I mean), and it has raw edges at the bottom, which means the legging basically melts into your skin.
