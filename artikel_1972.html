Aberdeen could be searching for a new manager after the heats turns up on Stephen Robinson after heavy Hibs defeat.

Aberdeen head coach Stephen Robinson is facing mounting pressure to keep his job after his side’s 4-0 thrashing by Hibs at the weekend.

Have lost three of their last four league games, his side sit bottom of the table on 11 points and are bang in relegation trouble. Just two points ahead of rock bottom Kilmarnock, the former St Mirren boss saw his side booed off at the weekend.

Following the weekend loss, Robinson said: “I don't know [what the implications may be], I don't make that decision. I'm Aberdeen manager until told otherwise, I'll keep fighting until told otherwise. The performance isn't acceptable, the result isn't acceptable. It's the story of the season - if you don't take chances, you lose matches. They had five opportunities, scored four of them. That's the difference.”
