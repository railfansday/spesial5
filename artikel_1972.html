Duncan Ferguson red carded for first time as manager as unbeaten Glentoran start grinds to halt

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Duncan Ferguson was sent off as his unbeaten start as Glentoran manager came grinding to a halt in a 3-2 defeat at Cliftonville in the NIFL Premiership on Friday night.

The former Rangers and Everton striker was shown a straight red card and ordered from the touchline after losing his temper in the aftermath of his side falling behind at Solitude in north Belfast.

Ferguson had angrily protested against Cliftonville's opener believing there was a foul on defender Jarlath O'Rourke in the build-up and was subsequently sent to the stand by referee Ben McMaster.

Ironically, Glentoran had already equalised by the time the card was brandished with Cal Weatherup levelling the match at 1-1 just a minute after Joseph Sheridan had put the hosts in front.

The red card was Ferguson's first as a manager in his 80th appearance in the dugout but the 10th of his career overall after receiving his marching orders on nine occasions during his playing days.

Assistant manager Gary Bollan took charge for the remainder of the game as Glentoran fell behind to a Brian Healy strike in the 67th minute before equalising again through James Douglas with four minutes left.

However, Cliftonville took the three points thanks to a second goal of the night from Sheridan in the 88th minute.

Bollan handled post-match media duties as Ferguson kept his counsel.

"I think the manager got frustrated because it was a foul," Bollan said. "It changes the dynamic [the sending off], he's a leader. I've then got to step in and fill his boots and it's a big set of boots to fill."

In the NIFL Premiership, a manager or team official given a red card receives an automatic one-match suspension for the next competitive fixture, with longer bans possible depending on the offence.

It means the former Scotland international will not be in the dugout for the Northern Ireland League Cup match against Larne at The BetMcLean Oval on Tuesday with any further punishment dependent on the content of the referee’s report.

Ferguson had started his Glentoran tenure with two away draws against Coleraine and Carrick before winning his first home game in charge 2-1 against Bangor last Friday. The defeat leaves Glentoran 14 points behind league leaders Linfield.
