Jorrel Hato has just become even more important after Chelsea update

Jorrel Hato has missed Chelsea's last two fixtures through injury.

The Dutch defender has more or less been a regular for Xabi Alonso so far, with him starting six of the eight games he has been available for.

His absence has perhaps gone under the radar, but Hato has quietly become a core part of the Spaniard's defence.

The ability to use the 20-year-old anywhere across a defence, whether as a left-back, wingback, or as a centre-back is certainly an undervalued trait.

Hato has also offered Chelsea plenty going forward, rather than being a stalwart at the back.

With his underlying numbers showing how much he brings to the team, ranking among the best in several key stats.

As stated before, what makes Hato valuable is that his contribution extends well beyond his defensive work.

The Dutchman is able to positively influence attacking proceedings, and under Alonso that has been crucial.

Hato, though, may be most important when it comes to breaking down teams in low blocks.

As per Data.MB, in comparison to every Premier League fullback, Hato comes out high in quite a few important data points.

This season, he ranks per 90 in the 98th percentile for assists (0.4) and the 96th percentile for pass completion percentage to the opposition's penalty box (66.67%).

Additionally, in a defensive sense, he ranks in the 94th percentile for shots blocked this season (0.83).

While also being in the 92nd percentile for possession won versus lost difference with (2.06).

From those stats, it does portray that the Dutchman is a dual threat on both ends of the pitch.

And that was lacking from Chelsea's fullbacks at the Stadium of Light.

Alonso confirmed ahead of facing Sunderland that Hato would be out for a few more weeks.

Whether or not the international break will allow him to fully recover in time for the Stamford Bridge side's match against Leeds remains to be seen.

However, that may be the target for the defender.

Leeds are a tough side, with a strong defensive set-up, so Hato's accurate passing into the box may be a trait Alonso would appreciate having.

So, his return could prove particularly important as Chelsea look to bounce back from their recent defeat.
