Injured Manchester United pair spotted in training as Mauricio Pochettino leads first session

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Harry Maguire and Patrick Dorgu both shook off injury concerns to take part in Mauricio Pochettino’s first training session as Manchester United manager on Thursday.

Pochettino has been thrown in at the deep end with United. He joins a club sitting 18th in the Premier League, and must visit Liverpool in his first match this weekend.

His predecessor, Michael Carrick, was beset by a number of frustrating injuries in the latter stages of his tenure, but Pochettino is hoping to welcome a number of absentees back into the fold this weekend.

And it has been confirmed that both Maguire and Dorgu are back in training ahead of the fated visit to Anfield.

Maguire was a doubt for the 4-4 draw with Aston Villa before the international break and was fortunate to start, but only lasted 45 minutes before being forced off with a back spasm. Dorgu returned from injury to feature off the bench in that match, but was not considered fit enough to represent Denmark during the international break.

But the fact both have returned to training suggests they will be fit for Sunday’s trip across the north-west, with United confirming they took part in the full session.

United have released a slate of pictures from Pochettino’s first session, showing the Argentine getting to know the likes of Leny Yoro and Bryan Mbeumo.

Elsewhere, Lisandro Martinez will be available having served a suspension imposed after he was sent off at Stamford Bridge on Halloween.

Pochettino will also hope to be able to call upon longer-term absentees Luke Shaw and Marcus Rashford this weekend, with some reports suggesting they will finally be ready to return from muscle and hip problems, respectively. It is unclear whether either man trained on Thursday.

Pochettino will deliver a more detailed team news update when he faces the media for the first time as United boss on Friday afternoon.
