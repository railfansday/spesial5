Gallery: Shoreham and Oakwood end all square in SCFL Division 1

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A superb Ryan Ferrar free kick gave Alex Laing’s side the lead in the first half but Henry Chandler scored for Oakwood on 42 minutes.

That was the way it stayed but the point took the Musselmen off the bottom of the table, with Ringmer AFC replacing them.

Next up for Shoreham it’s a trip to Arundel next Saturday, while Ringmer go to Reigate Priory.
