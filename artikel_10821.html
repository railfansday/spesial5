GTA 6 has already made over $750 million in pre-orders: report

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

GTA 6 has reportedly generated more than $750 million in pre-orders weeks ahead of its release in November.

According to estimates from analytics firm Sensor Tower, Rockstar’s highly anticipated sequel has accumulated approximately 7.71 million digital pre-orders across PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, generating an estimated $751 million in revenue.

Figures published by the firm indicate that PlayStation accounts for 74% of pre-orders, compared with 26% for the Xbox Series X|S.

That translates to around 5.74 million copies on PlayStation, generating $559 million, while Xbox accounts for another 1.97 million copies and $192 million in revenue.

The latest estimates also suggest that GTA 6 has generated more than $100 million in additional pre-order revenue over the past two weeks.

For comparison, GTA 5 generated more than $800 million in worldwide sales during its first 24 hours in September 2013, according to official figures released by Take-Two Interactive. It went on to surpass $1 billion in just three days.

It’s worth noting that these figures are estimates rather than official sales data from Rockstar or parent company Take-Two Interactive. They also cover digital pre-orders, meaning physical copies aren’t included.

GTA 6’s estimated $751 million in digital preorders is already approaching that first-day figure, although the comparison isn’t exact. Sensor Tower’s numbers are unofficial estimates, while Take-Two’s figures cover sales after GTA 5 launched.

GTA 6 releases on November 19, 2026, for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with preloading set to begin on November 10.
