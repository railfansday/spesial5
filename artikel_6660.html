Skyler Gisondo Is Using His First Awards Show Circuit to Experiment With Style

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Last night, ahead of the 2026 Governors Awards, Primetime actor Skyler Gisondo put the finishing touches on his Louis Vuitton look in a rather unusual location: the projection room at the Aero Theater in Santa Monica.

The theater hosted a Gisondo-themed double feature: Primetime and Licorice Pizza (you may also know him from his scene-stealing performance in Booksmart). Still, this is all new to him. Though Gisondo has been a working actor since childhood, Primetime marks his first-ever awards season campaign. “It’s not even a dream come true because it’s almost like something I never even allowed myself to dream of,” Gisondo tells Vogue.

With an awards blitz comes extra attention to fashion. “I’m 30, and I feel like I just recently started to figure out what I like,” says Gisondo, who loves a vintage workman jacket and admits, “I seem unable to eat anything without spilling on myself, so that is kind of prohibitive for me wanting to get nicer shirts and stuff.”

Enter menswear maestro Jason Bolden, who is helping Gisondo achieve a look inspired by his favorite actors: Redford and Newman. “I think there’s a certain timelessness to it—it’s cool in any era at any time. That’s certainly the stuff that I find myself responding to,” he says.

While he’s kicking off awards season with a classic black suit (though the Vuitton tuxedo blouson adds a cool, unfussy touch), Bolden is nudging the actor toward bolder pieces. “I’m pretty tame in my fashion decisions,” Gisondo says. “I’m probably Jason’s most boring client, but to me, the stuff he has me in, I’m like, oh my God, this is crazy.” What is crazy, you may ask? “He’ll put me in a leather jacket or something, and I’ll look at him and say, ‘Jason, I don't know if I’m this guy, man.’ And he’ll say, ‘Oh, you’re this guy.’”

Bolden isn’t the only one encouraging Gisondo to broaden his fashion horizons. “My girlfriend is also lovingly pushing me to dress cooler,” he says. He recalls an instance when she suggested he buy a pair of baggy pants. “I was like, ‘I just can’t do it. They’re too big. This just isn’t me.’ Now they’re not even the biggest pants I own.”

Up next on Bolden’s agenda? To get Gisondo into a pair of leather pants.

“If I learned anything from Primetime, it’s that you just got to try things. You can’t be afraid to fail,” he says. “You can’t be afraid to fail at work, in your fashion choices. You only live once. Let’s try some things. Put on the leather pants. Let’s see how it goes.”
