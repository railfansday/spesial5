The 13 Best-Dressed Celebrities at the 2026 Governors Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Zendaya, Penélope Cruz, and more offered an early look at awards-season style.

Awards season’s biggest ceremonies don’t arrive until next year, but the Governors Awards offered an early look at the red-carpet style to come. On Sunday night, Hollywood gathered at the Ray Dolby Ballroom in Los Angeles to celebrate artists whose work has shaped film, and the stars dressed aptly for the occasion.

This year, the Academy honored actor Glenn Close, animator Floyd Norman, and director Ridley Scott with Honorary Awards. Producers Christine Vachon and Pamela Koffler received the Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award. But before the awards were presented, stars galore arrived on the red carpet in their finest finery.

Zendaya kick-started her Dune: Part Three press tour in a gilded amber gown from the archive of the British designer Giles Deacon. The actress paired the sculptural, hip-forward look with dazzling Chopard jewelry and a slim Rolex timepiece. Elsewhere, ELLE Women in Hollywood honorees Penélope Cruz, Julianne Moore, Florence Pugh, Carrie Coon, and Inde Navarette brought the fashion in Chanel, Bottega Veneta, Valentino, Balenciaga, and Dior, respectively. Primary hues—particularly a stark cherry red color—proved to be a popular choice among stars, most notably Charlize Theron, in Givenchy by Sarah Burton, and Lea Seydoux, who dazzled in a Louis Vuitton train gown. The evening also made room for menswear, with style gurus Pedro Pascal and Alexander Skarsgård rewriting the rules of black-tie dressing in unconventional looks by Chanel and Tom Ford.

Here, the 13 best-dressed stars at the 2026 Governors Awards.
