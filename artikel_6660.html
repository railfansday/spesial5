Daisy Edgar-Jones’s Favorite Smart-Crop Jeans Are the Fall Denim Trend to Try Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Known for her love of vintage Levi’s, the actor has shown no signs of abandoning her trusty denim.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

In recent years, press tours have become even buzzier than whatever’s being promoted. Not that we’re complaining—we were first in line to gush over the array of bright pink outfits Margot Robbie wore throughout her 2023 press appearances for Barbie, and again earlier this year when the actor was promoting Wuthering Heights, gracing our social feeds with enviable corsetry reminiscent of her character, Cathy.

As for another scene-stealing star who knows how to turn a look, Daisy Edgar-Jones has been out in force in a mixture of casual and formal outfits to promote her latest on-screen endeavor, Sense and Sensibility. Styled by Dani Michelle, Edgar-Jones has been on a run of wearing some of the most buzzy luxury labels, including Prada, Chloé, and Schiaparelli.

But this time, it’s not the statement pieces we’ve got our eye on. Instead, a humble pair of jeans.

Known for her love of vintage Levi’s, Edgar-Jones has been photographed wearing the same pair of straight, cropped jeans on two occasions during this ongoing press tour. Styling them on the first outing with an archive Galliano top with a cinched waist and blossoming collar, she finished the look with a Boucheron choker and gold peep-toe heels. The rigid, straight denim, although more casual than a tailored trouser, worked well to keep the look feeling elevated, without leaning into something overly formal. Likewise, the cropped leg gave the shoes a much-deserved moment in the spotlight.

For the second look of the day, the star simply swapped out her top and shoes, opting for a strapless corset and nude pumps. Here, the jeans added a relaxed edge to the feminine ensemble, allowing just a little ankle to show at the hem. Where other trending styles such as barrel-legs and wide jeans are great for everyday, Edgar-Jones proves that a pair of straight, cropped jeans is a smart alternative for when the dress code reads “elevated-casual.”
