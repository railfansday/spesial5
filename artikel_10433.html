Walmart raises prices on its entire Google TV streamer lineup

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Walmart’s ultra-cheap Google TV streamers were never going to remain cheap in the age of RAMageddon, and now we’re seeing the second big price hike of the year with the whole Onn Google TV lineup seeing higher prices today.

Quietly rolled out over the past few days, Walmart has raised the price of its Google TV streaming devices by $10 a piece. The lineup now starts at about $40, up from just $15 prior to this year. Walmart’s streamers started getting higher prices back in March.

The price hikes have hit everything from the base 1080p streaming stick up to the Onn 4K Pro that rivals Google’s own TV Streamer.

Walmart’s price hikes still leave every device in its lineup as a great deal in the broader scheme of things, but it’s a huge increase compared to past generations.

For the time being, Walmart’s Onn-branded Google TV sets remain at their launch price, starting at around $250.
