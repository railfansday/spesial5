All Hail the Non-TV Newsmen

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Or: the virtue of hiring interesting people.

President Donald Trump speaks with 60 Minutes correspondent Ross Douthat at the White House in a publicity image released November 6, 2026.

In the universe of “people who got it wrong,” the doubters who protested CBS News’s hiring of Ross Douthat rank right up there with the MAGA influencers who insisted, even late into the evening of November 3, that the Democrats were not on track to have an exceptionally good election cycle.

Some people are just professionally wrong.
