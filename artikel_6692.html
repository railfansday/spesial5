The 28 Best Hostess Gifts Guaranteed to Secure You an Invite Back

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

If one of life’s simple pleasures is going to a friend’s house for a home-cooked meal, one of life’s great conundrums is figuring out the art of the host gift. If you bring a dessert, should you poll for food allergies ahead of time? Does anyone really need more scented candles? And what about the much-maligned bottle of wine?

I host a pasta dinner nearly every Monday, and for what it’s worth, I’m always happy when someone shows up with a wine we can pour with the meal. I’ve had people bring a really great wheel of brie and I’ve had people bring a plant stand for a kumquat tree in my kitchen whose sad drooping they’d noticed at dinner the week before. Recently, one friend appeared with a calendar of Italian priests. Am I Catholic? No. Is it a newly prized possession, hanging prominently on the wall by the bread basket (it felt apropos)? Of course. The best hostess gifts don’t adhere to any strict guidelines; they’re the ones that show your friends know you, and put a little thought into what to get you when—to quote the cliché—they really shouldn’t have.

Whether your calendar is filling up with holiday parties or you’ve befriended someone with a weekend home Upstate, this time of year is rife with occasions that call for these sort of trinkets. And since there’s no such thing as a one-size-fits-all option, it’s worth taking a little time to pinpoint a present with a little personality.

The below is an assortment of the very best hostess gift ideas. If you’re looking for something small and sure to please, an edible treat is a safe bet; if you’re looking for a larger gift, say, for a birthday or a holiday, for your friend who loves to entertain, this is for you too.
