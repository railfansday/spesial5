Slow-Cooker Tuscan Chicken

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Creamy Tuscan chicken is a Delish classic—it’s by far one of our most-made meals. This slow-cooker variation is perfect for a busy fall night and has all of the flavors of the original—garlic, cherry tomatoes, oregano, and Parm—plus the added benefit of hands-off cooking. The other thing I love about this recipe (perhaps more than the original, but don't tell!) is that it uses bone-in chicken thighs, which are my favorite cut of chicken. Chicken breast is great for the OG stovetop version, but thighs are far more forgiving for low and slow heat, and they offer a little bit of extra flavor from the bone and skin.

The potatoes:When you have a creamy, delicious slow-cooker meal hot and ready to eat, the last thing you want to do is take out an extra pot to make a side dish, so we broke the Tuscan formula ever so slightly by adding baby potatoes to make this a well-rounded and hearty main and side all in one. It’s my new go-to on busy weeknights, and it provides all the comfort of the original, with even more oomph.

Storage:If you have any leftovers, store them in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
