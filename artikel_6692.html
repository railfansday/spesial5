‘Cancer Does Change You’: Kate Middleton Shares Her Healing Journey in ‘Deeply Personal’ Documentary

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

“Cancer does change you,” Kate Middleton says in the emotional trailer for the film, Heal. The Princess of Wales is to release a “deeply personal” documentary, in which she will discuss her cancer diagnosis, her journey towards healing, and navigating life after experiencing serious illness.

In the clip, we see cameras trailing the Princess as she completes the Three Peaks—one of the UK’s toughest hiking challenges—which happened earlier this year. “It changes your body, it changes you physically,” she continues. “But there’s a much, much deeper part to what a cancer diagnosis shifts in you.” In June, the mum-of-three scaled the highest mountains in Scotland (Ben Nevis), England (Scafell Pike), and Wales (Snowdon) within 24 hours. Kate, who just completed a visit to Mumbai, India, with the Prince of Wales for the 2026 Earthshot Prize ceremony, had announced that her cancer was in remission in January 2025.

Footage in the trailer shows the Princess approaching a summit in foggy conditions, and tearfully hugging her children, George, Charlotte, and Louis, and her husband, Prince William, after completing her challenge.

Through this documentary, the Princess hopes to help others navigate diagnosis, recovery, and life beyond cancer and transform access to holistic cancer care which supports wellbeing, resilience, and quality of life during and after diagnosis, Kensington Palace said in a release. All funds received through the documentary’s distribution have been directed to the Royal Marsden Cancer Charity.

The film will premiere on the BBC on November 29, before airing internationally.
