Halo Infinite Adds 10 New Maps In Surprise November 2026 Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

They're part of the "Halo Fest Showcase" playlist

Who would have thought Halo Infinite would still be getting updates in November 2026? That's exactly what's happened this week though, and it's getting us tempted to install the multiplayer and play some games all over again.

The 10 new maps that have been added to the game this week are part of a Halo Fest Forge Contest that took place earlier this year, and they've specifically been added to a "Halo Fest Showcase" playlist.

You can see all the maps in the video up above, the list of which is as follows:

All of these are available across anything from 2-4 modes depending on the map, with those modes including CTF: Arena, King of the Hill: Arena, Oddball: Arena and Slayer: Arena.

There's nothing you need to do in terms of downloading anything on your Xbox Series X, Series S, Xbox One or PC as far as we're aware - they've all just been added in the background and are ready to play right now!
