Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A life-long tea drinker, I made my first post to this forum as a request for recommendations for decaffeinated black tea. Upton Tea in Holliston, MA, was the winner after I tried several brands. I still buy some decaf products from Upton. Most of their teas are loose leaf, included all the decaf versions; periodic sales and/or free shipping offers help with the price, which is not exorbitant but not cheap. Upton is one of the few tea sources which sells only tea decaffeinated by carbon dioxide processing rather than by petrochemicals.

All that said, I prefer a robust black tea and drank Assam every morning until caffeine became an issue. I have tried all the decaf products from Upton and, while drinkable, they are not at all comparable to the "real deal." The process that removes caffeine also removes a lot of the elements that give tea its flavor and aroma.

I like roibos and many other caffeine-free beverages, and will even occasionally settle for hot water and lemon for my morning drink. I wish you success finding decaf teas you can enjoy.
