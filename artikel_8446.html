If you carry a balance, keep it on the card that will charge you least, judged by the rate you’d likely get rather than the low end of its range. For most readers that’s PSECU’s Visa Classic Card, at 9.90% for everyone PSECU approves, so join the credit union and apply.

Credit unions often charge less than big banks. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau found that small banks and credit unions charged typical rates 8 to 10 percentage points lower than the 25 largest card issuers in the first half of 2023, at every credit level. Federal credit unions also can’t charge more than 18% on most loans, cards included, which is under the 22.36% average rate banks charged on card accounts that paid interest in August 2026, the Federal Reserve reports. The National Credit Union Administration has extended that limit through September 10, 2027.

The cards here earned their spots on what their borrowers are likely to pay, since many approved applicants pay well over a card’s lowest rate. In the bureau’s credit card survey, USAA reported that half the Rate Advantage accounts it opened in the second half of 2025 for applicants with credit scores of 720 or higher carried 17.65% or more. The card’s lowest rate then was 10.65%. If you might pay in full most months, weigh a low rate against rewards before you pick a card here.

Borrowers with good but not great credit gain the most, since PSECU charges them the same 9.90% as everyone, while a card priced by credit would likely charge them well over its lowest rate.
