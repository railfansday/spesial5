Apple announces Black Friday deals on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Apple has officially announced this year’s Black Friday promotion. Starting Friday, you can score up to $300 in gift cards when you buy an eligible Apple product.

Apple’s announcement comes as Amazon kicks off its own Black Friday deals offering cash savings on AirPods Pro 3, AirPods Max, the brand-new M6 MacBook Pro, the Beats 360, and more. Our friends over at 9to5Toys have you covered.

As usual, Apple’s Black Friday promotion doesn’t actually offer you cash deals on any of its products. Instead, it’s centered on giving you an Apple gift card when you buy an eligible device.

This year, the promotion is slightly more lucrative. In previous years, the gift card maxed out at up to $250, but this year you can get up to a $300 gift card. Here’s how it works;

As you can see, you’ll need to buy a top-of-the-line MacBook Pro to get the full $300 gift card. A MacBook Air, meanwhile, nets you a $200 gift card. This is the first year the iPhone Air has been eligible for the Black Friday promotion. The latest Apple gear, like the iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro, is not eligible for the promotion.

The 2026 Apple Store Black Friday Event runs from November 27 at 12 a.m. PT through November 30 at 11:59 p.m. PT and is available online and in Apple Stores. You can’t use the gift card to reduce the product’s purchase price. It’s only valid for a future purchase from Apple, including hardware, software, and subscriptions.
