Ameris: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The bank's willingness to sacrifice market share for loan pricing power signals how disciplined management can sustain profitability across rate cycles.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Oct. 23, 2026, Ameris (ABCB +1.29%) reported a five-year record high earnings of $1.73 per diluted share. Disciplined balance sheet management and mid-single-digit asset growth allowed the bank to expand profitability without taking excessive credit risk. Three things management said stand out.

Net interest margin (the gap between interest earned on assets and paid on deposits) stays protected when lenders avoid price wars. Prioritizing profitability over market share keeps returns on equity high across rate cycles.

Banks require steady deposit growth to fund new loans without relying on expensive wholesale borrowings. Setting deposit growth as the ceiling for loan expansion preserves cheap funding and keeps net interest income expanding.

A low criticized loan ratio (borrowings flagged for potential credit weakness) protects earnings from sudden provisioning charges. Maintaining asset quality well above regional peers preserves regulatory capital and shareholder equity.

Operating efficiency (operating expenses measured as a percentage of revenue) remains under 50% as revenue growth outpaces overhead expansion across southeastern markets.

Core deposit relationships and conservative credit underwriting anchor future performance as leadership monitors deposit pricing competition and loan yields.
