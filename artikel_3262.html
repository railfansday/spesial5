Ari Aster’s ‘Scapegoat’ Casts Spenser Granese, Keen Ruffalo, Jared Abrahamson & More

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

EXCLUSIVE: Ari Aster has added six to the cast of his new A24 thriller, Scapegoat: Spenser Granese (Marty Supreme), Keen Ruffalo (Sterling Point), Jared Abrahamson (F.A.S.T.), Brock Yurich (Test), Indiana Affleck (Casey Affleck’s upcoming Company) and Finn Harry (Amazon’s upcoming Tyrant).

Character details are being kept under wraps, as is the film’s logline. Some have speculated that the film is about a doctor who operates on an internet-famous person. Others claim it’s a sci-fi romance, while others believe it’s more of a traditional drama for Aster.

Previously announced cast for Scapegoat includes Scarlett Johansson, Jacob Elordi and Jude Law, along with Ella Purnell, Caleb Landry Jones, Samba Schutte and Cory Michael Smith. Watch on Deadline

Aster is directing from his own script, with his partner Lars Knudsen producing via Square Peg.

Granese is repped by Untitled Entertainment and Jackoway Austen Tyerman; Ruffalo by CAA and Strand Entertainment; Abrahamson by Gersh, Lauren Levitt & Associates and Canopy Media Partners; Yurich by Arise Artists Agency and Red Letter Entertainment; Affleck by CAA and Brillstein Entertainment Partners; and Harry by CESD, Sol22 and Lichter Grossman Nichols.
