Walmart’s 2026 Black Friday and Cyber Monday Sales Are Now Live

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

If you're thinking of picking up any big-ticket items—whether for yourself or as a gift for someone on your list— I would advise you not to sleep on Walmart's sale. Walmart is the first major retailer to announce and kick off its Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale for 2026, and they're actually holding three major shopping events between now and the December holidays.

Walmart will hold three sales between Nov. 12 and Dec. 2, as outlined in a recent press release. The sales will be both online and in-store, and will target a mix of Walmart+ members and general shoppers.

The events will take place on three different dates:

Walmart's Black Friday Deals Event 1: Nov. 12–15

Available online and in stores starting at 6 a.m. local time

Walmart+ members get six hours of early access beginning online at 12:01 a.m. ET on Nov. 12

Walmart's Black Friday Deals Event 2: Nov. 23–29

Online only: Nov. 23 starting at 12:01 a.m. ET

Online and in stores: Nov. 27 starting at 6 a.m. local time

Walmart+ members get six hours of early access online beginning Nov. 23 at 12:01 a.m. ET

Walmart's Cyber Monday Deals Event: Nov. 30-Dec. 2

Walmart's physical stores will open at 6:00 a.m. local time on Nov. 27 for their Black Friday events. Of course, they will start online earlier, so make sure to check their website as well.

Walmart says that they will have discounts on electronics, toys, fashion, beauty, home goods, gifts, and everyday essentials. Although the company doesn't provide much in the way of specifics in its press release, last year's sales included discounts on TCL TVs, Dyson vacuums, Dreo appliances, and toys from LEGO.
