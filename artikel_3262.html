iOS 27.3 brings vertical video and video podcasts to Apple TV app

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

iOS 27.3 beta 2 debuted today and includes two new types of video in the Apple TV app: vertical ‘clips’ and video podcasts.

The Apple TV app is expanding in two key ways with iOS 27.3, which is now in beta and ships next month.

First, Apple is introducing vertical video through a new ‘clips’ feature.

Similar to what streaming services like Netflix and Disney+ offer, the feature is a way to let users sample short-form previews of Apple TV content in a TikTok-style interface.

Inside the TV app in iOS 27.3, the Home tab has a new section: ‘Explore Clip Collections.’ Currently the collections include themed titles like ‘Absolute Adrenaline,’ ‘Animal Action,’ ‘Close Calls,’ and ‘Mind-Bending Moments.’

Tap to open a collection and you’ll start playing a short-form clip from Apple Originals with the option to swipe vertically to watch other clips. As you’d expect, Apple provides convenient buttons for adding the TV show or movie to your Watchlist.

iOS 27.3 also brings video podcasts from the Apple Podcasts app into TV.

As we reported this morning, there’s a dedicated video podcasts section coming to the TV app. But you can also, via your Account Settings, choose to sync playback data from Apple Podcasts.

If you enable sync, then video podcasts played in one app will be added to the queue and playback history in both apps.
