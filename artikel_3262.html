Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Margin pressure and Brazil credit issues tempered a strong quarter.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

ScanSource, Inc. (SCSC -20.90%) reported a 30.1% increase in net sales for the first quarter, alongside margin pressure resulting from the sales mix. Management stated that broad-based hardware demand in North America led to the revenue growth, while profitability was impacted by a shift toward larger, lower-margin contracts and specific bad debt reserves in Brazil. The company completed the acquisition of MicroAge during the quarter to expand its footprint in cloud, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence solutions. CFO Jones attributed the margin dynamics to timing and specific reserves, expressing confidence in the underlying earning power of the business.
