Villa beaten late on at Galatasaray as winless run continues

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Galatasaray vs Aston Villa. UEFA Champions League Round 1.

Match report as Deniz Gul's strike beats Aston Villa in the Champions League league phase, with Galatasaray winning 1-0; the game lacked chances, with Villa failing to score for a second game in a row; Unai Emery's side have not won in six matches

Aston Villa's winless run extended to six games as a late Deniz Gul's header saw them beaten 1-0 by Galatasaray in the Champions League league phase.

It is the first time Villa have lost back-to-back league phase or group games under Unai Emery, putting them in a perilous position ahead of tough games against PSG and Borussia Dortmund in their next two European fixtures.

The Premier League side have not won since beating Manchester City on October 24, and have not scored since Leon Goretzka's strike against Manchester United on November 8.

It was a game that lacked chances, and it took until the 79th minute - Gul's goal - for a shot on target. Villa failed to deal with a corner, with Nicolas Jackson's poor attempted clearance landing to the Galatasaray forward, who nodded home for his first Champions League goal.

Aston Villa failed to attempt a shot on target in this game, it's the first time they failed to attempt a shot on target in a game in the UEFA Champions League (since at least 2004/2005).

He took a boot to the face from Jackson with the Villa forward attempting to clear. There was a VAR check for a possible red card, but nothing was given.

In contrast to Emery's side, Galatasaray extend their winning run to eight games in all competitions ahead of the Istanbul derby against Besiktas on Monday. They move up to 13th in the league phase table, and have now won three successive Champions League games - matching a club record.

Aston Villa dropped into ninth by the end of Tuesday's matches on goal difference and could do so further by the end of matchday five. They play away to Ipswich next, live on Saturday Night Football.

"Disappointed a little bit. After their goal, it was impossible to play. They wasted time. They added only five minutes. OK. Finish the match. We don't want to play more. I don't think the referee was fair there. It is like that.

"Two years ago we lost to Brugge and Monaco away. Today I can compare this match like those matches.

"Why? Because their supporters are pushing so much. The pitch was not helping. The match was difficult because they pressed intense.

"We did a fantastic job in the first half. We stopped them from keeping the ball and going long. We avoided transitions. It was not enough because we lacked offensively."

"You can be forgiven for losing away at Galatasaray. They have beaten the last four English opponents to travel to their stadium, but it was the performance that will worry Unai Emery.

"It was a similar story to the 0-0 against Sunderland. Villa had more possession than their opponents but struggled to create anything in the final third.

"They have the players to do so. Johan Manzambi, Nicolas Jackson, John McGinn and others are all capable of match-winning moments.

"But for some reason, since conceding two late goals to draw against Manchester United, Villa look lost for ideas in the final third.

"Successive defeats in the Champions League and the previous hold they had within the top eight of the table slipping away is a worry.

"But the real concern should lie with addressing this newfound crisis of confidence in the final third.

"It's Ipswich up next and that will likely be another game where they are given the majority share of possession and are tasked with breaking down a defence.

"At the minute, that feels like a mountain to climb against any opponent."
