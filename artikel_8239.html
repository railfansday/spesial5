The 20 Best Gifts from Yeti, According to Our Editors

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

We may earn revenue from the products available on this page and participate in affiliate programs. Learn more

Yeti has become one of the most popular outdoor gear brands—and for good reason. It’s no secret that Yeti coolers are the best of the best, but the brand’s tumblers, travel mugs, straw cups, and other drinkware are just as coveted. With that in mind, we’ve rounded up the best Yeti gifts this year, whether you’re shopping for an avid boater who could use the tough Yeti Tundra cooler, a hunter who needs a spot to stash all their gear (cough, the LoadOut GoBox), or a casual camper who would love a portable French press for campsite coffee.

Along with the classics, Yeti has also come out with a bunch of new stuff that we’re pretty excited about, including the rugged Cayo backpack for any adventure and the high-end Rambler bowls for backyard barbecues. Bonus: You can get almost any Yeti product customized at Yeti.com for an extra personal touch.

When we tested the best rotomolded coolers, we were impressed by the superior ice retention and durability of the Yeti Tundra 45. Not only did it hold ice for six days in 90-degree heat, it handled being dropped 8 feet off a ladder more than a dozen times with minimal signs of wear. With six colors to choose from, it’s the ideal cooler for camping and fishing any time of the year.

The iconic Rambler mug got a new upgrade with the DuraSip ceramic lining, which is a nonstick interior that won’t stain and keeps hot drinks hot and cold drinks cold. Your giftee can use it for a cup of coffee, a fireside glass of bourbon, or even a bowl of soup at camp.

New for 2026, the Camino Zip Carryall is everyone’s favorite tote but with a zippered top to keep all your gear safe and secure. With a waterproof exterior, built-in laptop sleeve, and convenient adjustable divider, it’s as good as a boat bag as it is a gym tote or commuter bag.

One of our favorite Yeti products this year, the Daytrip 6L lunch bag is the perfect soft cooler for exactly what the name suggests: day trips. Whether you’re heading out on the boat, packing lunch for work, or spending a day exploring the outdoors, this fold-top soft cooler is big enough to hold the essentials (and keep them cold) yet portable enough to not weigh you down or take up too much space.

Yeti tumblers feature a double-wall insulation that keeps hot drinks hot and cold drinks cold. The popular Rambler comes in three different sizes and 11 colors. You can even get it customized with a monogram or fun design to reflect your giftee’s favorite animals, sports teams, or National Parks.

We had the chance to test out the new Roadie 15 when it came out earlier last year—and it didn’t disappoint. It’s everything we love about the OG Yeti coolers, but in a more compact package. I love it for fishing trips or a lazy afternoon on the boat. It’s just the right size for a few cold beverages, and it keeps them nice and frosty all day long.

After testing a bunch of backpack coolers, our experts chose the Yeti Hopper M20 as the best overall. Aside from the amazing ice retention (this thing will keep your drinks cold), other highlights are the MagShield closure that’s both effective and convenient when you’re ready to grab a cold one and the padded straps that make it comfortable to tote around.

Every hunter, fisherman, and camper will accumulate a lot of gadgets and toys. The Yeti LoadOut GoBox—with its solid waterproof construction—will keep all your gear safe and protected, no matter the conditions. A series of internal trays and dividers allow you to customize the box to your specific needs. It comes in three sizes.

New in the past year, the Yeti Rambler bowls are just what you need for outdoor dining or entertaining. Available in a range of sizes, the stackable stainless steel bowls are well-insulted to keep hot food hot and cold food cold. We’ve tested them and they’re as heavy-duty as a Yeti cooler—perfect for everything from camping to backyard grilling.

For boating or camping, the Camino 35 carryall is an endlessly helpful tote bag that pairs well with a Yeti cooler to keep your dry gear (and snacks) organized. It features the same rugged waterproof exterior that’s used on Yeti backpack coolers, giving this bag a durability and toughness that’s hard to match for other totes.

This year, Yeti released a couple of new backpacks, our favorite of which is the Cayo. It’s as good for hiking and exploring as it is for commuting, thanks to the weather-resistant shell, comfortable and ergonomic design, and plenty of pockets for organizing your essentials. The HitchPoint Grid on the front allows you to attach extra accessories like water bottle slings or gear cases.

The coffee-obsessed will love Yeti’s French Press. It lets you brew flavorful coffee anywhere and everywhere, whether you’re in your kitchen or roughing it out in the woods. The double-wall vacuum insulation will keep your java hot (or cold) for hours, so you can enjoy a fresh cuppa whenever you need it.

No more lugging around a heavy cooler. The Yeti Wheeled Roadie takes all the beloved features of the brand’s other hard coolers and makes it more mobile. Now you can roll a 24-, 48-, or 60-can sized cooler just about anywhere thanks to a heavy-duty set of wheels and a comfortable-to-hold retractable handle.

The Hopper M30 remains one of Yeti’s most popular offerings. This stylish soft cooler utilizes magnets to stay securely closed and prevent ice water leaks. It’s one of the toughest soft coolers out there—perfect for outdoor enthusiasts who are rough on gear.

A rugged outdoor cocktail shaker that allows the making of mixed drinks just about anywhere was not what we expected from Yeti. But it’s a welcome addition to their impressive lineup. Whether you’re mixing up a celebratory drink on the back patio or taking your favorite adult beverage to the campsite, the Yeti cocktail shaker will keep your refreshments ice cold.

Yeti didn’t forget about man’s best friend. Made of rust-resistant stainless steel, the dog bowl can hold up to 8 cups and features a grip on the bottom that will keep even the sloppiest of pooches from making a mess. You can use it for water, kibble, or any other treats your pup loves.

Nothing makes a hot day more miserable than a drink that quickly goes warm. That’s where Yeti’s brilliant gasket-secured Colster can cooler comes into play. It’s a simple design that locks in the coldness, ensuring you never have to endure a warm beer ever again.

This Yeti outdoor blanket is made of a cozy yet durable polyester and rayon blend that can easily stand up to the rigors of camping or the beach. You can wrap up in the softer side on a crisp fall evening or lay it down as a picnic blanket on the waterproof bottom layer that repels away moisture.

If you’re going to serve drinks, these new Yeti lowball stackable 10-ounce cups are the perfect portable option. The vacuum-sealed insulation keeps drinks hot or cold for hours on end. They’re dishwasher-safe and stack neatly together for easy storage.

Yeti offers several bags and backpacks, but the Panga stands out thanks to an IPX7 waterproof rating—it’s completely waterproof even when fully submerged. Combine that with a tough puncture-resistant TPU lamination and nylon outer shell, and you’ve got a pack that’s built for the elements. The spacious 28L interior has more than enough room for all your gear, too.

If you want to enjoy a glass of your favorite pinot or merlot deep in the backcountry or lounging on the beach, you can do so with the highly rated Yeti wine tumbler. The 10-ounce travel mug—which resembles a stemless wine glass—keep drinks at the perfect temperature no matter how hot or cold it is outside.
