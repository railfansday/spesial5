GTA 6 Is Currently The Best-Selling Game In 63 PlayStation Stores

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After years of waiting, GTA 6 is now so close we can almost taste it. Anticipation for its arrival couldn’t possibly be any higher, and we already know it’s going to be a hit everywhere it releases thanks to the latest data tracking how well it’s charting in various countries.

That's according to PS Store DB, an unofficial site that tracks the chart positions of games on various PlayStation Stores around the world. Right now, of the 52 PS Stores from which the site collates data where GTA 6 is available to buy, Rockstar’s upcoming title is number one in all of them.

Turns out we might not have as long to wait for GTA 6 on PC than we might have expected.

That list of 52 countries includes the UK, the US, Canada, France, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Belgium, Japan, Costa Rica, and of course, New Zealand, where a shocking number of GTA 6 players will be residing at some point tomorrow. Definitely legitimate New Zealand residents and not anyone tricking their PS5 into thinking it’s there so they can start playing early.

It's also number one in South Korea, where the game came dangerously close to being banned for its graphic nudity.

PS Store DB has added on Twitter that GTA 6 is currently the best-selling game on PlayStation Stores in 11 other countries for which it doesn’t officially track figures, including Bolivia, Malta, Ecuador, and Panama. The only PS Stores where GTA 6 isn’t currently the best-selling game are Kuwait and China, where the game is officially unavailable, and Qatar, where its availability is a little more complex.

GTA 6 topping the charts the world over before it has even been released is further proof that this game is going to sell in ways that the industry has never seen before. Sure, GTA 5 has sold 235 million copies and is now one of the best-selling games of all time. However, it seems almost certain at this stage that the 11 million copies that game sold in its first 24 hours will be far outmatched by GTA 6.

According to Sensor Tower, as of November 10, roughly 9.69 million GTA 6 pre-orders had been placed. That number has been regularly updated since then and has not only breezed past ten million, but it has also surpassed that 11 million figure and is closer to the 15 million threshold. Again, a reminder that this game isn’t even out yet. As if you needed to be reminded.

It’s hard to believe that it’s almost here. After multiple delays and some of the most high-profile leaks in video game history, in less than 48 hours, we’re all going to finally be playing this game. Well, maybe not all of us, but judging by the figures being bandied about, a significant chunk of us will be treating Vice City like our home away from home from Thursday onwards.

We know it's been a long wait for GTA 6, but when you think about it, we've all lived so many lives since GTA 5.
