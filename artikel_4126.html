KFC is Inviting You to a Funeral so You Can Play GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Daniel is a lifelong gamer with a love for various genres and gameplay styles who enjoys exploring games and playing from various angles.

There are only two days left until the landmark launch of Grand Theft Auto 6, which promises to be immensely profitable for Take Two. The gears of society are already starting to grind to a halt as the world braces itself for a statistically-significant portion of the population to disappear into their homes for an indeterminate period. There’s probably going to be at least one guy in a small town out there somewhere who thinks he’s become the last man alive when he doesn’t see anyone on the streets.

It’s kind of an open secret that everyone who plans to play GTA6 will be ditching work or school on the 19th. Many workplaces around the world are aware of this, and some have even done the sporting thing and given their employees an unofficial holiday for a couple of days rather than trying to work without them. Unfortunately, not everyone has managed to secure an iron-clad excuse, but that’s where a certain purveyor of chicken is stepping in.

On the official Instagram account for the Australian arm of fast food chain KFC, it was announced that everyone’s totally real best friend “Ann Alibi” has tragically passed away, and by sheer coincidence, her funeral will be held on November 19. This isn’t just a marketing stunt; an actual funeral will be held, coincidentally providing the perfect out for anyone in need of a day off.

The post on the KFC Australia Instagram features a photo invite memorializing the passing of “Ann Alibi,” with an invite reading, “It is with heavy hearts, and a much lighter workload, that we announce Ann finally got her wings. She was always there for us when we needed her, in sickness, and in perfect health. By remarkable coincidence, her service will take place on the very same day something thirteen years in the making finally arrives.”

The invite adds that a service will be held from 11:00 AM, and urges attendees to “please let your manager know.”

By remarkable coincidence, her service will take place on the very same day something thirteen years in the making finally arrives.

The real-life funeral will be held at KFC’s Robina, Queensland location, and will include “a hearse, coffin, floral tributes, a book of condolences, an order of service and even a heartfelt eulogy delivered by a surprise guest."

Anyone who attends the service in person will be treated to some complementary chicken, but for everyone who can’t be there for, y’know, whatever reason, will be able to livestream the event online. Plus, KFC’s Australia locations will be offering free delivery all day on the 19th.

KFC is offering just about every semi-realistic avenue for turning this obviously-fake funeral into a genuine excuse for a day off, with the aforementioned book of condolences being the perfect way to create a paper trail for those in need of an excuse for a day off.

"We appreciate that Australians have responsibilities, but some occasions are simply too important to miss," a KFC Australia spokesperson said in a statement obtained by Australian business outlet Human Resources Director. "And in accordance with Ann's final wishes, we've made sure the funeral takes place during working hours."

Surveys suggest that around 12 million Americans are planning on either adjusting work schedules or taking PTO, though given the sheer size of this launch and its accompanying social movement, it wouldn’t be especially surprising if many players didn’t even bother to concoct an excuse.

Some workplaces know that trying to corral employees away from GTA6 would be a foolhardy endeavor, and as such, aren’t even bothering. Palworld developer Pocketpair recently announced that all of its employees are getting time off from Thursday to Monday, just to keep friction to an absolute minimum.
