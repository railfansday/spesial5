Sentinels complete 2027 VALORANT roster with British player keiko, reuniting him with brawk

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Sentinels have signed Briton Georgio 'keiko' Sanassy from NRG as the final starter in their 2027 VCT Americas lineup. The move pairs him again with former NRG teammate brawk as Sentinels try to return to international events after missing them all in 2026.

The Sentinels lineup for 2027 now reads johnqt, brawk, OXY, canezerra and keiko. Zellsis rounds out the group as sixth man. He came back to the organisation on 7 November in that role and as a content creator. Contract lengths were not disclosed.

Sentinels called keiko ‘bloody brilliant’ in its announcement. Riot’s VALORANT Esports account then shared the news and described him as ‘the finishing touch’ on the lineup.

Sheep Esports first reported the move on 8 November, citing sources, and said keiko was expected to play controller. The same report had brawk on initiator and johnqt on sentinel.

The signing ends a rapid rebuild. OXY arrived on 2 November and brawk a day later. canezerra followed on 6 November. Four days after that, cortezia and Reduxx were released.

Only a few hours before joining Sentinels, brawk had ruled out staying at NRG.

keiko’s NRG spell ran from 12 November 2025 to 21 October 2026. He finished third at Masters Santiago 2026. He also placed third in both VCT Americas Stage 1 and Stage 2. At Champions 2026 he finished joint fifth to sixth.

That spell ended once Champions was over. NRG told him he would not be kept for the following year, keiko said on X on 21 October. He was then free to look for a new team as a restricted free agent.

Sentinels reached no international event in 2026. The team went out of that year’s Kickoff in the bottom four. It then missed the playoffs in both Stage 1 and Stage 2. Adding a player with that run of results is consistent with a side trying to close the gap quickly.

keiko was born on 12 September 2003. He grew up in and around London. He played competitive Fortnite before switching to VALORANT. His VALORANT career began at UK sides WYLDE, from May to August 2022, and BONK, from October to December 2022. He then joined Apeks from January to November 2023. That year he won the Challengers Rising Stars award. Team Liquid followed, from November 2023 to November 2025. His move to Sentinels places a British player at the centre of a rebuilt Americas contender.

The new lineup’s next test is the Americas Kickoff. Partner teams are guaranteed a place only at Kickoff. The Americas has eight partner teams, and all of them receive direct invitations to its event. Four further places go to non-partner teams.

The 2027 Kickoff events will be played in person in each international region. One of the non-partner places in the Americas will be decided by a Last Chance Qualifier in São Paulo on 17–18 December.
