Guide: Xbox Adds More Perks For Game Pass Members (November 2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Xbox Game Pass Perks program is continuing to add a bunch of freebies for Game Pass members throughout November 2026, including for the likes of Skate, Sea of Thieves and much more!

Rare's Sea of Thieves one is perhaps the most notable so far - it's an emote celebrating the 25th anniversary of Xbox. Of course, with the anniversary itself taking place on November 15th, there may be more surprise perks on the way.

As we've mentioned before, it seems most Xbox Game Pass Perks are now accessible to Essential, Premium and Ultimate members, but there are exceptions where only Ultimate members can get access. Fortnite Crew and the Rocket League Rocket Pass are notable ones that are only available with Xbox Game Pass Ultimate.

To claim your free Xbox Perks on console, you'll need to navigate to the Xbox Game Pass app and look for the 'My Benefits' button.

To claim your Xbox Perks on PC, you'll need to open up the Xbox PC app, hit 'Game Pass' and then choose "Benefits".

The exception to the rule is for some of the free-to-play games where you'll find the Perks automatically included when you load them up. If you look for the "Recently Added" section in the Xbox Game Pass app on console, any free-to-play title in there should grant you a special Perk when you start up those games.
