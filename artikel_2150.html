Top 8 most famous footballers to ever play in the EFL Championship (Ranked)

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Championship is a fantastic league when it comes to producing talent, with many of Thomas Tuchel’s England squad having featured at this level in the past.

The competitive nature of the second tier, combined with the technical and physical demands it places on players, mean it’s a relentless division that will test individuals in different ways.

Some struggle to cope with that, although others flourish, and it can be the perfect environment for young players to show what they can do.

However, it’s not just up-and-coming players who enjoy the Championship, as some of the biggest names in the game have turned out in England’s second division in the past.

With that in mind, FLW has ranked the EIGHT most famous footballers to have played in the Championship, even if they weren’t necessarily the best…

The 39-year-old is a Leicester City legend, with the high point of his career coming when he helped the Foxes to the Premier League title.

Many people love Vardy for his rise to the top, which saw him make the jump from non-league Fleetwood Town to Leicester when they were in the Championship, and it was a move that kickstarted his career, which would eventually make him a household name.

Vardy is still playing in the Championship right now, turning out for Burnley, and is undoubtedly one of the most famous names in the league.

Walker is a teammate of Vardy’s at Turf Moor, and he is a player who has enjoyed a glittering career at the highest level.

During his time with Man City, the right-back won six Premier League titles, a Champions League and numerous domestic trophies. Walker was integral to City’s success under Pep Guardiola, and is in the discussion as the best right-back the league has seen.

When you add in 96 England caps, Walker has had a remarkable career, which started with Sheffield United in the Championship.

Many people consider Cole as the best left-back England have ever had, with the Arsenal academy graduate winning over 100 caps for the Three Lions, which is a fantastic achievement.

During his club career with the Gunners and Chelsea, Cole won every honour possible, which included lifting the Champions League with the Blues after beating Bayern Munich on their own patch in 2012.

Cole was a superb one-vs-one defender, and whilst he spent the majority of his career at the top, he ended up at Derby County, and whilst he didn’t make a huge impact at Pride Park, having a player of that calibre at the club would’ve benefited the younger players.

Bellingham is one of the most famous footballers on the planet right now, with the midfielder starring for Real Madrid and England.

But, it all started for the Stourbridge-born Bellingham at Birmingham City, who are a club that are still close to his heart. He broke through at Blues as a 16-year-old, and it became apparent very quickly that they had a special talent on their hands.

At 23, Bellingham still has so much more to offer, and there’s every chance that he could go on to become the most famous footballer on this list, as he will fancy his chances of winning the Ballon d’Or, as well as aiming for more success with Real Madrid, and leading England to a major trophy.

Terry is another Premier League legend, with the defender among the best centre-backs to have played at the top level in English football during his time with Chelsea.

He won five Premier League titles, five FA Cups, three League Cups and a Champions League in a brilliant career, with Terry also representing England 78 times, many of which were as captain.

Even though Terry is associated with Chelsea, he ended his playing career at Aston Villa in the Championship, with his final game a play-off final loss to Fulham at Wembley.

Bale is a legend of Welsh football, having been the driving force behind what has been their best period in the past decade, where they qualified for major tournaments and reached the semi-final of Euro 2016.

On the club stage, Bale was part of a Real Madrid team that dominated in Europe, and he ended his career having won the Champions League five times, which is an outstanding achievement.

It all started for Bale with Southampton, though, where he had one full breakout season before joining Spurs.

The ex-Man Utd forward is one of the best players that England have produced, and he is another player on the list that won every honour available during his club career.

Rooney also won 120 caps for England, and he was the Three Lions’ record goalscorer before he was beaten by Harry Kane, so he was a key figure for both club and country for the chunk of his time as a player.

The attacker was another player who ended his career in the Championship, with Rooney joining Derby County under Phillip Cocu, who he would then replace as head coach, to give him his first taste of management.

Kane may not have the trophy cabinet of many on this list, but as the only Ballon d’Or winner, we feel he has to go in as number one.

The striker is the all-time England record goalscorer and appearance maker, and he is now picking up silverware on a regular basis with Bayern Munich, having undoubtedly established himself as one of the best players in the world in the past few years.

That recognition as number one on the planet arrived earlier this year, and it capped off an incredible journey for Kane, who turned out for Millwall and Leicester in the Championship during loan spells earlier in his career, and for Leyton Orient in League One.
