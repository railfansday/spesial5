Apple Music debuts Labrinth’s new single, created live on the touchscreen MacBook Pro

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Following last week’s livestream, when Labrinth composed and recorded a song overnight at Apple Tower Theatre in Los Angeles on the new touchscreen MacBook Pro, the single is now available on Apple Music. Here are the details.

Last Wednesday evening, singer-songwriter Labrinth transformed Apple Tower Theatre in Downtown Los Angeles into his private studio as he composed a song from scratch using Logic Pro 13 on the new MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display.

The entire session was livestreamed on his YouTube channel, with Labrinth also answering questions from fans along the way.
