Anya Taylor-Joy’s $1.3B Fantasy Blockbuster Is Officially a 2026 Streaming Smash

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

2026 has been a busy year for Anya Taylor-Joy, who has returned to the TV scene with shows like Lucky but also maintained her streaming dominance with films such as Sacrifice. Lucky was particularly important earlier this year because it marked the first time that Taylor-Joy returned to Apple TV after the colossal success of The Gorge (co-starring Miles Teller).

Despite premiering last year on Valentine’s Day, The Gorge has still been one of the most-watched movies of the entire year on Apple TV, now amassing over 300 days on watchlists. Meanwhile, Sacrifice hasn’t been the hit that many fans were expecting, but the movie is still hanging around in the top 10 in a few countries. Marvel veteran Chris Evans, who returns this December in Avengers: Doomsday, stars alongside Taylor-Joy in Sacrifice.

However, amidst all these successful streaming projects, Anya Taylor-Joy also returned to the big screen with the release of The Super Mario Galaxy Movie, the sequel to the 2023 blockbuster co-starring Chris Pratt. The Super Mario Galaxy Movie was the first feature film of 2026 to reach $1 billion at the global box office, and after a run of success on Peacock, the movie is officially coming to Netflix soon.

The original Super Mario Bros. Movie from 2023 is still streaming on Peacock, but it’s now been in the top 10 on VOD platforms like Prime Video and Apple TV for over 200 days, which is enough to make it one of the most-watched movies of the entire year on streaming. Taylor-Joy is the queen of streaming with five hits in the top 10 around the world right now.

Nintendo is hard at work on a third Super Mario Galaxy movie, which isn’t all that surprising, considering the first two films have earned over $2 billion at the box office combined. However, star Keegan-Michael Key has cautioned fans against expecting the third film to be released anytime before 2029, citing that these things take time. Plot details about the third film are being kept under wraps for now, but more information will likely come out once it is closer to starting production. Both Taylor-Joy and Pratt are expected to return in the third movie.
