The Best Black Friday Deals on Smartwatches and Fitness Trackers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Black Friday sales are now in full swing. I've been keeping an eye on all the best fitness trackers and smartwatch deals so that you can snag the best possible price from now until the end of Cyber Monday (Nov. 30). Whether you want a full-featured fitness smartwatch or a more recovery-focused tracker, here are the discounts you can find right now, including on brands like Apple, Oura, Garmin, and more.

The Ultrahuman Ring Pro is a worthy competitor to the Oura Ring 5, with an included high-capacity charging case and no subscription. You can read Beth's comparison of the two here: most people would probably prefer the Oura, but the pros and cons are nuanced and you, dear reader, may be exactly the person the Ultrahuman is best for. The silver color is only $299 in this Black Friday deal, with other colors going for $379. The ring normally retails for $479, so that's $100 to $180 off, depending on color. That price includes a charging case and all the basic functionality of the ring; some extras require a subscription.

This is a rare deal from Pebble! An email from the company states "We don’t do sales. Black Friday is the one exception each year." The Pebble Time 2 is on sale for $200, down from $225 regular price. This is the brand's rectangular watch, with a heart rate sensor, and an always-on MIP (e-paper) screen. The Pebble 2 Duo (a "do-over" version of the Pebble of yore) is also on sale, for $124, down from $149.

The Oura Ring 5 is at its lowest ever price: $299 for black and silver, $399 for all other colors. These are the lowest prices ever for these rings, which usually go for $399 and $499, respectively. The Oura Ring 5 is the smallest and most comfortable smart ring I've tried, although it's worth noting it requires a $5.99/month subscription fee.

For an even cheaper deal, the older Oura Ring 4 is $249—a full $100 off its list price of $349. If you opt for "stealth" (matte black), the price is $279; for gold, it's $286.61, down from $399. While this model has since been replaced by the Oura Ring 5, it's still an accurate and comfortable smart ring—especially if tracking your sleep is your priority.

Whoop may be one of the most expensive smart bands out there, thanks to its subscription-only model. But that price is particularly low right now, since the Peak membership that normally goes for $239 is now $199. Peak is the middle tier, and it's the one Beth recommends. While you can get Whoop One for $189 in this sale, the $10 you save isn't really worth it given the features you'd be missing. We also wouldn't normally recommend Whoop Life, since its ECG readings and blood pressure estimates aren't really worth the extra cost. That said, if you do want it, the Life tier is $299 right now instead of $359.

The new Luna band is on sale for $119.20, down from a normal price of $149. The Luna band is made by Indian smartwatch company Noise, the same company behind the Luna Ring that Beth reviewed last year. The ring is no longer available in the U.S., but the band is. It's a Whoop-style screenless fitness tracker, potentially a worthy competitor to the Fitbit Air and other smart bands.

This is the brand-new Apple Watch model, and it's actually worth the upgrade. One of the biggest reasons is that the Series 12's new processor (called, confusingly, the S11 chip) supports some features older Apple Watches can't, and a good rule of thumb is that whenever there's a break in software compatibility, it makes sense to get the newer model. Right now, we're seeing it for as low as $299, down from the list price of $399. (And if you're stuck deciding between an Apple Watch and a Fitbit, Beth breaks it all down in her comparison of the two watches.)

The Google Pixel Watch 5 has been marked down by $100, starting at $299.99 for the version that normally retails for $399.99. Each model is $100 off its regular price, including the LTE and Wifi-only models of both sizes.

The CMF Watch 3 Pro is Nothing's latest smartwatch, and it incorporates AI features powered by ChatGPT; you can currently pick it up at a major discount. Originally $99, you can get the CMF Watch 3 Pro for $55. This is the lowest price this smartwatch has been.

If you're looking specifically for a sporty, outdoors watch, Garmin sets the standard. (You could definitely call Meredith something of a Garmin enthusiast.) Here are the best Black Friday deals on Garmin watches that we've seen so far:

The 44mm Garmin Instinct 3 is 249.99 (down from $449.99). The 1.2-inch color AMOLED display makes the screen more readable in bright daylight conditions, and battery life is best-in-class—a must for those going off-grid.

The Garmin Fēnix 8 in titanium with a gray band is $749.99 (down from $999.99). While this is last year's model, it's a perfectly solid option if you don't specifically need the titanium case or the latest mapping engine in the latest Fenix 9 Pro.

The Garmin Forerunner 265 is $299.99 (down from $449.99). Although it isn't the latest and greatest anymore, this is still a fan favorite among Garmin users. It's a step above entry-level options, with dual-band GPS, power meter compatibility, and advanced analytics (like training status).

Meredith's beloved Forerunner 970, the top-of-the-line Garmin for serious runners, is $649.99 right now, down from an original price of $749.99.

Yes, Garmin is one of the best brands when it comes to rugged sports watches and fitness trackers. The Forerunner line, especially, is a go-to pick for accurate and reliable GPS tracking for runners.

My pick would be the Garmin Forerunner 265 for $299.99. It's a slightly older model, but still works well and is packed with features for runners.

There are! Check out the Apple Watch deals above. If you can't decide, my pick would be the brand-new Series 12 at $299. This one has step counting and will soon be getting the slightly creepy Siri Recaps (which you can disable if you'd rather not deal with them).
