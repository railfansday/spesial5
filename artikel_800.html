Travis Kelce Plans to Consult Taylor Swift on This Business Venture: ‘I’ll See What My Wife Can Cook Up’

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The pop star knows a thing or two about putting on a good show.

Travis Kelce doesn’t need to look farther than his own house to find the perfect consultant for an upcoming business venture. The Kansas City Chiefs tight end noted on the latest episode of New Heights that Taylor Swift can help him out with a specific element of his and Jason Kelce’s next tailgate event.

On the podcast posted Wednesday (Nov. 11), the two brothers debriefed on the New Heights tailgate in Philadelphia three days prior. The elder Kelce brother called it the “best” one they’ve ever done and said that they should “re-create” the stage setup they had at that particular venue — but “the problem is most tailgates don’t have a stage.”

“I don’t know how we’re gonna do it … maybe build our own,” Jason continued before asking his co-host, “Do you know anyone who knows anything about stages?”

“Yeah, I actually do,” Travis replied with a laugh. “I’ll see what my wife can cook up.”

The Grotesquerie actor seemed to say it kind of as a joke, but also kind of not. After all, Swift — who’s been touring since she was a teenager and executed her billion-dollar Eras Tour trek in 2023 and 2024 — would definitely have some expertise to input, even if it’s for a tailgate rather than a stadium concert.

Jason, however, was definitely messing around. “‘The companies I deal with are not gonna f— around with you dumb f—s,'” he jokingly imagined Swift telling them.

Whether or not the 14-time Grammy winner — who last dropped music in September, reclaiming the No. 1 spot on the Billboard 200 with The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore — teams up with her husband and brother-in-law for the next New Heights tailgate, the Kelces will definitely figure it out one way or another. The siblings have pulled off numerous events together, both for the podcast and independently; Travis, for instance, has hosted two of his Kelce Jam festivals in honor of the Chiefs’ Super Bowl wins in 2023 and 2024, bringing together artists such as Lil Wayne, Diplo, Machine Gun Kelly and more for a night of music in Kansas City.

He also successfully planned his top-secret proposal to Swift in 2025, orchestrating it so that everything would be set up while the hitmaker was distracted recording her guest episode of New Heights last year.

“During that recording, the entire time I’m planning, like, ‘I’m gonna ask this woman to marry me after this,’” he previously recalled of his nerves leading up to popping the question.
