JoJo Siwa Unveils Her Darkest Hair Transformation Yet

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

JoJo Siwa debuted a new brunette look on social media, marking a significant change from her signature bright blonde locks.

JoJo Siwa proved that she can rock any hair color as she debuted her latest hair transformation on social media.

The Dance Moms alum's hairstylist @HairByKarla shared photos of Siwa's new 'do on Instagram Nov. 8 in which she showed off her lengthy locks in a gorgeous, multi-tone brown hue. While the undertones are certainly her darkest shade yet, the new style also features some highlights that echo her signature blonde look.

The post was captioned, "Welcome to the dark side @jojosiwa."

While her latest hair transformation is her latest move away from the classic big blonde ponytail that she sported throughout her rise to fame, fans of Dance Moms know that Siwa has long been open about the fact that she's a natural brunette.

"I have been dying my hair since I was, like, two—maybe one-and-a-half," she said on the reality show in a now-viral audio clip. "I'm not a natural blonde. I'm actually a natural brown. I get brown roots, so I have to dye it makes it look like I'm a natural blonde."

The Nickelodeon star first cut off her signature ponytail in favor of a drastic short 'do back in 2022, which she documented on social media at the time.

"So I just got my hair cut yesterday," she said on her Instagram Story at the time. "And I went for a down and curly moment. But today, I went for an up and back moment, and I don't know, the picture that I posted was from yesterday when it was down and kind of curled…"

In the years since, she has tried her hand at different styles and variations of her usual blonde locks—sometimes even with some colorful highlights—as she started to slowly grow her hair back out to a longer length.
