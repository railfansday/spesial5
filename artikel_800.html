Oliver Hudson's Son Wilder Deferring College Due to "So Much Stress"

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Oliver Hudson shared why his son Wilder is deferring his second semester of college, noting the teenager wanted to combat depression experiencing "so much stress" on campus.

Wilder Hudson has a new chapter on the books.

After all, his dad Oliver Hudson detailed how the 19-year-old—shared with actress Erinn Bartlett—dealt with depression on campus after a complicated wisdom teeth removal at home, ultimately leading him to take a break from college life.

"Going back to school was a bit of a task," Oliver said on his and sister Kate Hudson's Sibling Revelry podcast Nov. 6, explaining that after six weeks of dental issues, Wilder "felt a little depressed, like he didn’t belong there anymore."

According to the Grown-Ups 2 actor, the "false start" at the University of Colorado: Boulder caused his son to start "carrying so much stress.”

"Stress is a killer," the 50-year-old continued. "I'd rather you smoke a f--king pack of cigarettes than be stressed."

And after Wilder starred alongside his dad and Alicia Silverstone in Netflix's A Merry Little Ex-Mas, Oliver understood why his eldest felt he would be happier pursuing acting now since "college will still be there waiting for him."

As such, Oliver—also dad to Bodhi, 16, and Rio, 13, with Erinn—helped Wilder come up with the plan to defer his second semester and return to school for sophomore year.

"He wants to take a shot at his life, which means that, of course, he’ll still be living at home," Oliver clarified, "but he’s getting a job. He’s going to work on his auditions."

In addition, the upcoming actor will take a screenwriting class at a local community college to hone his craft because, while Oliver admitted nepotism "will get your foot in the door, no doubt about it," he's also learned that connections can't replace the ability to perform.
