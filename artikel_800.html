Mulder shreds Bangladesh as South Africa seal 2-0 sweep before England Tests

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

South Africa host England in a three-Test series live on Sky Sports from December 17, with matches at Johannesburg, Centurion and Cape Town; Proteas hammer Bangladesh inside three days at SuperSport Park to consolidate top spot in World Test Championship

South Africa's Wiaan Mulder followed his fifth Test century with career-best bowling figures of 7-31 as the Proteas routed Bangladesh by an innings and 219 runs inside three days to secure a 2-0 sweep ahead of their three-match series versus England.

Mulder's knock of 100 from 178 balls came in the hosts' 494-7 declared at Centurion, before the tourists were rolled for 184 in their second dig, having been blown away for 91 on day one of the game when Kagiso Rabada and Marco Jansen shared six wickets.

South Africa, who face England at home in Tests for the first time since 2020 when the opening game takes place in Johannesburg from December 17, consolidated their position at the top of the World Test Championship as they target back-to-back titles.

The Proteas beat Australia in the Lord's final in 2025, the same side they recently defeated 2-1 in a three-match assignment on home turf after rallying from 1-0 down.

South Africa then saw off Bangladesh by nine wickets in the opening Test at Wanderers, a game in which Mulder was out for a two-ball duck in his sole hit, before this drubbing at SuperSport Park ended when Mulder caught Tigers tailender Khaled Ahmed off Jansen.

Bangladesh battled to 58-0 in their second innings, only to lose two wickets for no runs and three for 20 once Tanzid Hasan (29) edged Mulder behind on the cut, with Mominul Haque (0) glancing the same bowler down the leg-side two overs later.

Once Rabada reduced the visitors to 158-5 by having top-scorer Towhid Hridoy (41) clip meekly to gully, Mulder shredded the middle and lower order.

The all-rounder bowled four other players, including Jaker Ali (2) and Shoriful Islam (0) from successive deliveries, and forced captain Najmul Hossain Shanto (34) to nick off as he found lavish swing movement while bagging a maiden Test five-for.

Mulder said: "I got the ball to go with the wind, ​into the right hander, away from the left hander, and I ​kind of just stuck with that. I tried to target top it off stump ​as often as I could.

"With a little bit of extra movement, I ⁠think I got them playing at balls that they shouldn't, and they created a gap in there to hit the stumps."

Shanto added: "We knew that conditions are tough. Our batters couldn't adapt, especially in the first innings."
