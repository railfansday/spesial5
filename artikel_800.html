Cole Swindell Signs With Make Wake Artists for Management

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Swindell joins a roster that includes Luke Combs, Stella Lefty and Jon Bellion.

Cole Swindell has signed with Nashville-based artist management company Make Wake Artists, which also reps Luke Combs.

Swindell will be managed by Make Wake founder and chief navigation officer Chris Kappy and partners Dylan Wright and Michael Gonzales. Make Wake, which launched in 2015, also represents artists Jon Bellion, Flatland Cavalry, Stella Lefty and Muscadine Bloodline.

Swindell has earned eight No. 1 hits on Billboard‘s Country Airplay chart to date. In 2023, he took home a trio of ACM Awards wins, including single of year and song of the year (as songwriter and artist), for his four-week Country Airplay No. 1 “She Had Me at Heads Carolina.”

In 2027, Swindell will serve as support on Morgan Wallen‘s Last Time I Checked Tour. He’ll also perform at C2C: Country to Country in March and Wallen’s Sand in My Boots Festival in May.

“I couldn’t be more excited to join the Make Wake family,” Swindell said in a statement. “I’m thankful for everything that’s happened in my career up to this point and I still feel like there’s so much more ahead. I’m fired up for this next chapter and can’t wait to get to work with the Make Wake team!”

“There are artists who achieve success and then there are artists who leave a lasting mark on country music. Cole has unquestionably done both,” Kappy said in a statement. “From his incredible catalog of hits and undeniable connection with fans to his extraordinary talent as a prolific songwriter, Cole has helped shape the sound and stories of modern country music. His ability to create songs that resonate with millions, both for himself and for other artists, is a testament to the depth of his artistry and the remarkable career he has built.

“Welcoming an artist of Cole’s stature to Make Wake is a monumental moment for our company and a testament to the caliber of talent we are committed to representing. What excites us most is that despite everything he has already accomplished, we believe his greatest chapters are still ahead. We take tremendous pride in the trust Cole has placed in us and we intend to match that trust with the creativity, passion, and relentless determination that define Make Wake Artists. Cole has built an extraordinary legacy and we couldn’t be more excited to help take it to even greater heights.”

“This is the kind of opportunity you don’t take lightly, and I’m grateful to be part of it,” Wright added. “My focus is simple: listen closely to Cole, understand what he wants next, and make sure our team is built around that every day. I’m excited to get to work with him, Kappy and Mike.”

Swindell is also signed to Warner Records Nashville, William Morris Endeavor (WME) for booking and OH Creative for publicity.
