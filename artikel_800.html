At $599.99 at Best Buy, the Sony ZV-1F Compact Digital Camera is one of the best entry-level cameras that’s an all-in-one for digital photography and video capture. It’s ideal for beginners and casual use.

The Sony Alpha 6100 APS-C Mirrorless Camera, which is priced at $949.99 at Best Buy, is a more advanced camera system for someone who wants to take their photography to the next level.

For rich and robust personal audio, the Sennheiser MOMENTUM 5 Noise-Canceling Headphones, which are priced at $399.99 at Best Buy, are a must, especially with Bluetooth wireless settings.

For a distraction-free TV watching experience, then go with the Sennheiser RS 275 TV Headphones, which are priced at $299.99 at Best Buy. The headphones offer clear and crisp audio, dialog enhanced settings and immersive listening when watching movies and TV shows.

Priced at $179.99 at Best Buy, the HP OfficeJet Pro 8125 Inkjet Printer is a pro-level printer that’s best for a small business or heavy-duty printing at home.

For a more entry-level printer, go with the HP Envy 6165 Wireless Inkjet Printer, which is on sale for $79.99 (was $159.99) at Best Buy — a 50% savings. This printer is best for casual use, like printing out the occasional tax form, term paper or shipping label.

If you’re getting a gift for a family, then the Amazon Echo Show 15, which is priced at $349.99 at Best Buy, is best for showing calendars, weather, recipes, schedules and more at a glance. It even comes with a remote control to watch YouTube and best placed in a central location in a home, like a family kitchen.

For something on the smaller end, like for an office, go with the Amazon Echo Show 5. It’s powered with the new Alexa+ AI assistant that’s designed to make life a little bit smoother. It’s priced at $99.99 at Best Buy.
