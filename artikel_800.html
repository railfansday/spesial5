Arteta says Arsenal need more luck to kill off games

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Viktor Gyokeres has scored three goals in all competitions this season for Arsenal.

It might be emerging more as a theme than as a major problem, but in recent weeks Arsenal cannot seem to finish off matches when they are on top.

In their past nine outings across all competitions, just once have the Gunners won a game by more than a single goal.

The latest frustration came in Tuesday's 1-1 draw with Borussia Dortmund, though perspective is needed. Arsenal might consider it as two points dropped having gone into half-time ahead, but they remain unbeaten in the current Champions League league phase and have not lost at this stage of the competition for more than two years.

But of late, despite dominating large parts of matches, Arsenal have had periods where they have dropped off and allowed opponents back into the game.

Against Slavia Prague away in the Champions League, after scoring twice in the first half, Arsenal's standards dropped. The same happened against Newcastle in the Premier League last weekend where they found themselves 2-1 behind in the second half having led at the break, before recovering to eventually draw 2-2.

They are producing periods of high-level football, but the struggles to consistently turn that into more goals can lead to nervy finishes.

In the draw with Bundesliga leaders Dortmund, Arsenal had 30 shots but just four of those attempts were on target.

Boss Mikel Arteta mentioned the failure to capitalise post match, though was keen to praise his side's "brilliant performance".

"The way we played, bearing in mind the opposition that we played today and what we've done out there, is just something to be very, very pleased [about] because there is very little more that you can demand from the team," Arteta said.

"But obviously, the feeling is that we should reward ourselves much more when you put that kind of performance in the amount of chances (and) situations.

"There is an element of luck; there is an element of us finishing the actions better. We need to insist because I think what the team has done and shown today, I think it's the right direction."

Arsenal held by Borussia Dortmund in Champions League

Arteta made six changes to the side that drew with Newcastle, with one of the biggest calls to rest striker Kai Havertz and bring in Viktor Gyokeres.

The striker has had a difficult season so far, with four of his seven starts coming in the Champions League against opposition where Arsenal have been favourites.

However, Gyokeres scored a powerful effort from outside the area to give Arsenal the lead against Dortmund.

Overall though, it ended up being a mixed night for the 28-year-old as he missed a header from inside the six-yard-box that would have surely won the game for the Gunners late on.

Arteta was asked if the chances his side are missing were down to mentality, misfortune or composure.

"There is an element of luck here because how some of the goals they haven't gone inside, I don't know.

"The execution can be better, but everything that is in between that, I think it was really, really good."

Arteta was also full of praise for Gyokeres, with particular emphasis on the striker's link-up play; an element of his game at times criticised.

"What I love the most is how connected he was to the team," Arteta said.

"We used him many times in different ways. His link-up play, the way he held the ball, the way he threaded in behind and stretched the pitch.

"There was a lot to like in that performance and that's the Viktor that we needed."

Noni Madueke has been Arsenal's best attacker in recent weeks.

The winger, who was left out of the last England squad, is enjoying a run in the Arsenal starting XI with Bukayo Saka out injured and is taking his opportunity.

Madueke has registered a goal and two assists in his past three games and was again the best Arsenal forward against Dortmund.

The Gunners host Manchester City on Sunday and are currently six points clear at the top of the Premier League table.

Madueke, who was frustrated with the performance against Dortmund, said the clash with third-placed City is a great chance to get back to winning ways and shake off any frustration.

"I think these are the best games. I'm really looking forward to it. Two draws on the bounce now and I just desperately want to win on the weekend and I know my team-mates feel the same," Madueke told Amazon Prime.

"We are going to do everything we have to win the three points. We're champions, we're at home. We have a duty to perform well at the Emirates.

"There are things we need to improve but all in all we are going in the right direction."
