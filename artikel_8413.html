You don't mention your other assets, but nothing here seems completely out of whack. You say you liquidated your taxable account to put $600k+ down. Do you know what you will owe by way of capital gains taxes and have you paid them yet? If you just recently did this you have until 1/15/27 to pay timely estimated taxes. I do think putting a big downpayment is prudent. You are estimating with a $600k down payment $4600 ongoing housing costs + $5k non-housing costs which is $9,600 out of your $10,000 monthly take-home. Not sure how you make it work by putting less down. Many would say just draw down savings to make the payment but that is nerve wracking month after month.

Nothing you point out sounds like a hair on fire emergency. If the roof, HVAC, and septic all work right now there's no reason to fix them today. If the kitchen is functional it doesn't need to be immediately renovated. Of course, a renovation before move-in would be minimally disruptive.

What I do see is someone who didn't really go through this the right way and now feels anxious about it -- dual agency, very high earnest money, few if any contingencies. That's okay, and once you move in and settle down that will all go away.
All taxes are paid fortunately. Take home is ~10k after tax, but ~3 months a year I usually get 3 paychecks (15k) + 30-50k in after tax bonus annually.
