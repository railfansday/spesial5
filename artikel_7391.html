The 35-page guide offers insights into seasonal trends and a list of major events for the coming year to help brands plan forward-facing strategies.

There are less than two months left in 2026, which means it’s time to start planning for 2027, and mapping out content approaches in line with major events in order to maximize performance.

To help with this, Reddit published its 2027 planning guide, which includes a range of notes on key dates to keep in mind, as well as usage trends that could provide additional guidance in planning.

The 35-page guide includes notes on Reddit’s rising influence as a product discovery platform and how people use the app for shopping-related insights.

There are also notes on seasonal trends and how they relate to Reddit engagement.

But the main focus of the guide is its listings of major events, including a full rundown of key U.S. dates to consider.
