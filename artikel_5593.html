KKR posted record quarterly profit as fee-related earnings climbed 27%

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Assets under management rose 11% to $804 billion, and KKR expects to return more than $40 billion to clients in 2026

KKR reported record adjusted net income per share of $1.78 for the third quarter on Monday as fee-related earnings rose 27% from a year earlier to $1.3 billion.

The adjusted figure beat analyst estimates of $1.58 per share, according to The Wall Street Journal, and of $1.59, according to Reuters.

Adjusted net income rose 29% to $1.6 billion. Total operating earnings climbed 25% to $1.7 billion. For the second consecutive quarter, KKR posted record fee-related earnings, total operating earnings and adjusted net income per share.

Management fees rose to $1.20 billion from $1.06 billion a year earlier. Fee-related performance revenues rose to $326 million from $73 million.

Net income attributable to common stockholders totaled $1.12 billion, equal to $1.19 per diluted share, up from $859.9 million, which worked out to 90 cents per share, in the year-earlier period. Total revenue dropped to $4.56 billion from $5.53 billion. Insurance revenue declined to $2.80 billion from $3.78 billion.

"We continue to monetize investments and return capital to our clients at record levels," Co-CEOs Joseph Bae and Scott Nuttall said in a statement, according to Reuters. KKR anticipates distributing over $40 billion back to clients in 2026, which would top its prior annual high by more than 50%.

Assets under management rose 11% from a year earlier to $804 billion. Fee-paying assets under management also rose 11%, to $648 billion. KKR brought in $25 billion of fresh capital in the quarter and deployed $20 billion.

Insurance operating earnings were $230 million, down from $305 million a year earlier. The latest figure included a $26 million charge tied to Global Atlantic's annual actuarial assumption review and related items. The year-earlier figure included a $41 million benefit from the same review.

KKR announced the sale of USI Insurance Services on Aug. 31. The deal is expected to be completed during the fourth quarter. Subject to closing, KKR expects it to generate about $2.0 billion of adjusted net income, or more than $2.00 per share, in the fourth quarter. The company said there is no assurance the transaction will be completed.

From June 30 through Nov. 4, KKR used $567 million to repurchase and retire 6.2 million shares at an average price of $92. On Nov. 6, it approved a $500 million increase to its share repurchase program. KKR declared a quarterly dividend of $0.195 per share, payable Dec. 3 to holders of record on Nov. 19.

KKR will hold a conference call on the results at 10 a.m. ET on Monday.
