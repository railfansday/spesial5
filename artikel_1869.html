Is Sheffield Wednesday v Accrington Stanley on TV? Channel and streaming details for EFL Trophy clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Sheffield Wednesday are set to host Accrington Stanley in the final game of their EFL Trophy group stage campaign.

The Owls have already booked their place in the next round of the competition but could surrender top spot in Group A to their fourth-tier opponents with a defeat.

Perhaps more pertinently, they need to give their fans something to cheer about after a humiliating FA Cup exit at the hands of Oldham Athletic.

The game was Dave Challinor’s first at the Hillsborough helm and tonight (Novemnber 11) he will be in the dugout at Wednesday’s home for the first time as Owls boss.

After the loss to the Latics, Challinor said: “I am really frustrated and disappointed not to have won. The first half was like a testimonial in being so slow – not like I want a team of mine to be.

“The second half was a whole lot better, but we conceded a goal during our best spell of the match. The substitutions changed the dynamics of the game – it would have been better if we had started that way.

“There is plenty of work to do – change won’t happen overnight but hopefully you saw some structure.”

Here are the key TV and streaming details ahead of Wednesday’s clash with Accrington.

Kick-off in South Yorkshire is scheduled for 7pm tonight (November 11).

Yes - those not heading to the game can watch the action unfold on TV. The fixture has not been given top billing on any of the main Sky Sports channels but can be viewed as an alternative broadcast via the Sky Sports+ service. Subscriptions can be purchased online.

Supporters can stream the action via the official Sky Sports app or on NOW TV with a valid subscription. There will also be live audio commentary on BBC Radio Sheffield.
