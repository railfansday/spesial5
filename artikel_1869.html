Italy vs. France: Head-to-head record and past meetings

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

By Jonathan O'Shea | 10 Nov 2026 17:02, Last updated: 10 Nov 2026 17:05

Longstanding rivals, Italy and France have been crowned global champions six times between them, with several crucial clashes deciding which side would go on to collect silverware.

Most notably, they met in the 2006 World Cup final, which was decided on penalties after France talisman Zinedine Zidane saw red for his infamous headbutt on Marco Materazzi. After one early goal apiece - including Zidane's pinpoint free kick - football's biggest game eventually went Italy's way.

Success marked revenge for the Azzurri, following their dramatic defeat to Les Bleus in the Euro 2000 decider. On that occasion, a late strike by Sylvain Wiltord sent the final into extra time, before David Trezeguet's 'golden goal' secured another trophy for the reigning world champions.

Looking further back, Italy were traditionally the dominant side - claiming 17 wins from the first 26 meetings - but France were ascendant from 1982 on.

Inspired by Juventus star Michel Platini, Les Bleus started to pick up regular victories, and they had gone nine games unbeaten against the Azzurri before that fateful night in June 2006.

The streak notably featured a penalty shootout triumph at the 1998 World Cup on home turf, which Zidane and co would ultimately go on to win.

More recently, the pair have played out three Nations League contests: in the 2024-25 campaign, either side won 3-1 away from home.

Then, in October 2026 - with Zidane now France boss - they settled for a 1-1 draw in Paris, where Alessandro Bastoni's strike cancelled out Michael Olise's opening goal.
