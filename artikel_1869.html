‘Brilliant human’ - Middlesbrough FC’s David Strelec hailed by Kim Hellberg after goal against Bristol City

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Middlesbrough head coach Kim Hellberg hailed forward David Strelec after the forward notched in a 2-0 win over Bristol City.

Adilson Malanda opened the scoring at the Riverside before Strelec made sure of victory with his first goal since May.

He was leading the line ahead of free-scoring Will Lankshear, who was on the bench after missing two games with a hamstring injury.

Hellberg said: “It was brilliant; breaking down a team we hadn’t beaten in nine games and a team which had lost only one of their last seven away games, I am happy.

“Breaking down the low block and scoring from a set-piece and from David makes me overall happy, with a clean sheet too.

“It was a massive moment for David. He has been very good since coming back, he’s shown how good he is in these two games. That goal was a beautiful team goal and he is a top player – you could see how happy he was for it.

“He is a brilliant human being, it’s easy for me to back him. I can see what he does every day. It is about having more quality players being ready to play.

“For Will we wanted to get him on, it’s important for him to play and have that feeling. Everyone knows how good he is, I am happy to have him back.”

Under-pressure Bristol City boss Michael Skubala was left frustrated at the way his side conceded the first goal, when Malanda was left unmarked in the area to head in a corner.

Once Strelec had added the second, City were on track to lose a third game in a row – meaning they have won just one of their last eight matches.

Skubala said: “This was a disappointing afternoon. We tried to nullify all their strengths so to concede from a set-piece when we did made for a difficult start, and the second goal killed us.

“And when you assess our finishing, it hurt us. We hit the post three times, and if we get one of those it would be a different end to the game.

“It is very difficult to take because if that first goal doesn’t happen then it is a different game.”
