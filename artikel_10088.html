How much CO₂ will stay? New study estimates long-term residual emissions under the EU Emissions Trading System

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Current CO₂ emissions covered by the EU Emissions Trading System (EU-ETS1) could be substantially reduced by 2040. A new study by the Oeko-Institut, commissioned by Carbon Market Watch, examines the level of emissions that could remain in 2040 and 2050. In 2040, installations covered by the EU-ETS1—including waste incineration—could still emit between 159 and 231 million metric tons of CO₂.
