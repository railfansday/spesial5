Photon Brings Prescribing to ChatGPT

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Clinicians can now securely draft, review, and send prescriptions from ChatGPT, while patients compare pharmacies by price and convenience.

BROOKLYN, N.Y., Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Today, Photon announced its prescribing plugin for ChatGPT, enabling licensed clinicians to securely draft, review, and send prescriptions directly from ChatGPT

Clinicians are already using ChatGPT regularly to explore treatment options for their patients. Now with Photon, they can carry that discussion into a real prescription. Patient and treatment context from the conversation helps create the draft, which the clinician reviews and authorizes before sending.

For clinicians, this connects the work of considering a treatment to putting that decision into action. The authorized patient context shared with ChatGPT can inform the prescription draft, and missing clinical details can be gathered as part of the conversation. The clinician remains responsible for the treatment decision and control over what gets sent.

"AI is quickly becoming where clinical decisions take shape, and the tools that support care should meet clinicians there," said Otto Sipe, CEO at Photon. "We're building toward a future where going from a treatment decision to a filled prescription is seamless, and where patients have real choice in how they get their medication. Bringing Photon into ChatGPT is an important step in that direction."

From treatment conversation to prescription

With Photon's plugin for ChatGPT, clinicians can name the medication they want to prescribe, review recommended dosing options, and draft a prescription informed by context shared with ChatGPT and authorized patient information. New patient details can be gathered while drafting the prescription, and patient records are saved for future prescriptions.

Patient details, allergies, and medication history are synced with Photon. Prescriptions are screened for interactions as they're drafted. Clinicians review the prescription details and authorize each prescription before it's sent.

To get started, clinicians add the Photon plugin in ChatGPT and connect their existing Photon account or create one during login. Clinician verification is required to sign and send prescriptions. Prescriptions written for controlled substances are not currently supported.

Photon offers patients a choice where to fill

The work of prescribing often continues after a prescription is sent, when patients encounter an unexpected price, an out-of-stock medication, or a closed pharmacy, and call their clinician for help. Photon gives patients the information, at the moment a prescription is written, to make that choice themselves. This in turn saves clinicians time on the administrative burden of rerouting prescriptions.

When the prescription is sent via Photon, patients receive a text link where they can compare pharmacies by price, location, and hours before choosing where to fill. If the selected pharmacy can't fill the prescription, patients can choose another pharmacy through Photon without asking their clinician to resend it.

Photon's plugin for ChatGPT is planned to launch November 16th, 2026, for eligible U.S. clinicians using ChatGPT for Clinicians or ChatGPT for Healthcare.

No existing Photon account is required. Access is free at launch through December 31, 2026

Clinicians can learn more, view the workflow, and get started at www.photonhealth.com/openai

Photon Health is an independent prescription network and pharmacy marketplace that puts the prescription in the patient's hands. When a clinician prescribes through Photon, the patient receives a link, branded to their provider, to compare pharmacies by price, stock and delivery, choose where to fill, and reroute if plans change. Because Photon runs its own network rather than relying on legacy rails, partners can launch in days and gain a parallel route to pharmacies for redundancy. Digital health companies use Photon to reduce prescription support volume, independent prescribers use it to prescribe without the pharmacy follow-up, and health systems use it to connect patients with their in-house pharmacy options. Founded in 2021 and based in Brooklyn, Photon is building a pharmacy marketplace where every patient can choose. Learn more at www.photonhealth.com.
