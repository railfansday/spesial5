Blend posts smaller Q3 loss, warns of steeper mortgage revenue drop ahead

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Blend Labs reported Q3 revenue of $32.9 million, up 1% year over year, and cut its GAAP operating loss to $2.6 million from $5.3 million. Mortgage suite revenue slipped 2% as funded loans on the platform fell 4% to 194,000 and value per loan reached $81.   AI Summary

Blend Labs narrowed its loss in the third quarter and logged its strongest period of sales in more than three years, even as rising mortgage rates pressured loan volumes and its core mortgage suite revenue declined.

Total revenue in the third quarter was $32.9 million, up 1% from the same period in 2025, the company reported Thursday. GAAP operating loss was $2.6 million, an improvement from a $5.3 million loss a year earlier.

“The market got harder, but we’re moving faster than ever,” Nima Ghamsari, co-founder and head of Blend, told analysts on an earnings call. “When we spoke in August, mortgage rates were about 6.8%. After the Fed raised rates in September, they hit a high of north of 7.5%. And as you can expect, applications dropped with them — purchase and refinance both.”

Blend closed 26 new deals and expansions during the quarter. In dollar terms, the company said, these moves represented more than twice the combined value of its first- and second-quarter deals.

Churn notices in the quarter represented about $2 million of annual revenue, an improvement from roughly $3 million in the second quarter. Based on a lighter churn environment in the first quarter and subsequent improvement, Blend said year-to-date gross revenue retention is in the mid-90% range.

“Three takeaways for today: One, We had our best sales quarter in more than three years; two, Autopilot went from six customers to 10 and the pipeline behind it is now big enough to size and growing; three, next year the market may not help us. Converting Autopilot pipeline is how we’re going to grow,” Ghamsari said.

Revenue from Blend’s mortgage suite totaled $17.4 million in the third quarter, down 2% year over year. The platform handled 194,000 funded mortgages, a 4% decline from the same period last year, and economic value per funded loan was $81.

In a compressed market, lenders are relying on Blend’s Rapid Mortgage and Rapid Home Equity solutions to lower per-loan costs and accelerate consumer-facing processes “when every basis point counts,” Ghamsari said.

For the fourth quarter, Blend guided total revenue to a range of $27.7 million to $29.7 million, a decline of about 8% to 15% year over year. Mortgage suite revenue is expected to fall 23% to 28% from the prior-year period, reflecting lower industry volumes and mix shifts.

Executives said potential fourth-quarter deals could contribute about $5 million in incremental revenue in 2027 as implementations go live. “Next year the market may not help us,” Ghamsari said.

Analysts at Keefe, Bruyette & Woods wrote that Blend is “executing on what it can control against a weak mortgage market” and that “early traction with budding AI products (Autopilot) is encouraging.”

“We continue to think upside in the shares will be capped until there is visibility into a more meaningful and sustained acceleration in revenue growth (thus driving more scaled profitability), which the weak mortgage backdrop pushes out,” the analysts wrote.

Blend ended the quarter with $44.2 million in cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities, and no debt.
