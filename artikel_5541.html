Lanvin Group Receives Continued Listing Standard Notice from NYSE

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

SHANGHAI, Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Lanvin Group Holdings Limited (NYSE: LANV) ("Lanvin Group" or the "Company"), a global luxury fashion group, today announced that it received a notice from the New York Stock Exchange (the "NYSE") on November 10, 2026 that the Company is not in compliance with the NYSE's continued listing standard requiring a minimum average closing price of US$1.00 per share for its ordinary shares over a consecutive 30 trading-day period.

The Company has notified the NYSE within ten business days of receipt of the notice of its intent to cure the deficiency. Pursuant to the NYSE's rules, the Company has a six-month period following receipt of the deficiency letter to regain compliance with the minimum share price requirement.

The Company intends to consider available alternatives, including but not limited to a reverse stock split that is subject to shareholder approval, if necessary to cure the stock price non-compliance.  The Company can regain compliance at any time during the six-month cure period if, on the last trading day of any calendar month during the cure period, the Ordinary Shares have a closing price of at least US$1.00 per share and an average closing price of at least US$1.00 per share over the 30 trading-day period ending on the last trading day of that month.

The NYSE notice has no immediate effect on the continued listing of Lanvin Group's ordinary shares on the NYSE and is not anticipated to impact the Company's ongoing business operations. The Company's ordinary shares will continue to be listed and trade on the NYSE under the symbol "LANV" during this period, subject to the Company's compliance with the NYSE's other continued listing requirements.

Lanvin Group is a leading global luxury fashion group headquartered in Shanghai, China, and Milan, Italy, managing iconic brands worldwide including Lanvin, Wolford, Sergio Rossi and St. John Knits. Leveraging its unique strategic alliances with industry-leading partners in the luxury fashion sector and its access to the fastest-growing luxury fashion markets, Lanvin Group strives to expand the global footprint of its portfolio brands and achieve sustainable growth through strategic investment and extensive operational know-how. Lanvin Group's ordinary shares are listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol "LANV".  For more information about Lanvin Group, please visit www.lanvin-group.com, and to view Lanvin Group's investor presentation, please visit https://ir.lanvin-group.com.
