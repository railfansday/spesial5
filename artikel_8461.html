Get the Bilt Palladium Card if you’ll book two hotel stays a year through Bilt’s travel site and spend its Bilt Cash, since those credits are worth $600 against its $495 annual fee. Skip it if you won’t, because its 2 points per dollar take heavy spending to earn back the fee over the no-fee Bilt Blue.

The Palladium is the priciest of Bilt’s three cards for renters and homeowners and the only one with airport lounge access and 2 points per dollar (2X) on purchases outside rent and mortgage. Column N.A. issues all three.

Travelers who’ll book two Bilt Travel hotel stays a year and spend the Bilt Cash come out ahead, since $600 in credits covers the $495 fee. Readers who’d skip the credits do better with the Blue unless they charge more than about $39,600 a year, or with the $95 Bilt Obsidian for 3X on dining or groceries.
