Adjusting for Credit Risk: Masters In Business with David Eichhorn

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Barry speaks with David Eichhorn, CEO and Head of Investment Strategies at NISA Investment Advisors. They discuss his early days as a quant at JPMorgan before returning home to St. Louis to work at NISA. They also discuss the risks and benefits of the private credit market, along with the dangers of private credit going into pension funds and 401Ks. They also discuss liability investment strategies for managing over $483 billion in client assets. (Source: Bloomberg)
