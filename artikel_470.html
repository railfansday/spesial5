Legos Pikachu and Poké Ball has dropped to a new low price at Amazon — save $40.04 before Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

SAVE OVER $40: As of Nov. 12, the Lego Pokémon Pikachu and Poké Ball set has dropped to its lowest-ever price of $159.95 at Amazon. That's $40.04 off its full price of $199.99.

Early Black Friday deals are already trickling in for Pokémon fans, including a few price cuts on select Lego sets.

If your eyes have been set on the delightful Pikachu and Poké Ball set as a must-have build for your collection, you'll want to grab your wallet: Amazon's knocked it down to a new low price ahead of its Black Friday sale.

As of Nov. 12, this set is available to shop for $159.95, just over $40 off its usual cost of $199.99. For a build that launched earlier this year, a deal like this is rare.

This Pikachu and Poké Ball set comes with a total of 2,050 bricks that create the iconic Electric-type Pokémon leaping from a Poké Ball atop a lightning bolt-shaped base. That's just one way you can have it modeled, though. Pikachu can also be set up in a resting pose with the Poké Ball closed, if you want to change it up. Plus, the Pikachu figure's ears and limbs are posable.

The display base also features a little nod to Pikachu's Pokédex number, with the number "25" formed from Lego studs. Keep in mind its age rating is 18+, meaning it's geared towards adults. Kids can still get in on the fun with help.

There's no need to wait for Black Friday to save on this build. Act fast to score the Lego Pikachu and Poké Ball set at its lowest-ever price on Amazon.
