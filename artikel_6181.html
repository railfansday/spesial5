It's that time of year again: Your last chance to plan your final flights or mattress runs of 2026 in order to snag last-minute elite status for next year.

If you hold Marriott Bonvoy elite status and aren't going to requalify for your status tier this year, there's good news: The program is extending its “soft landing” policy into 2027. With this policy, if you fail to requalify for your current status this year, you will only drop one tier from March 2027 until February 2028.

Related: Marriott elite status: What it is and how to earn it

Based on the program's elite-status policies, the Marriott Bonvoy status you earned in 2025 will expire at the end of February 2027 unless you requalify for your current level or higher in 2026. But what if you don't meet the threshold for the same or higher status?

That's where Marriott's generous soft landing policy comes in. Whether you qualify for a level far below your current status or don't qualify for status at all in 2026, you'll only drop one status tier in 2027.

According to Marriott's website, members who don't requalify for status will see their new, lower tier take effect in early March 2027, and they can enjoy this status through February 2028. Meanwhile, if you requalify for your existing tier or a higher tier, you will also hold this status through February 2028.

This soft landing policy, which Marriott has offered for years, is a win-win: It provides valuable benefits to elite members who weren't able to requalify for status, while giving them a reason to stay loyal to Marriott and perhaps try again for status next year.

Related: The complete guide to earning Marriott elite status with credit cards

Marriott Bonvoy elite status offers some excellent perks, such as room upgrades, late checkout and even lounge access. Thankfully, with this soft landing policy, mid- and upper-tier elite members who don’t requalify this year won’t be left empty-handed. You can still enjoy the benefits of the next-lower tier from March 2027 to February 2028.

For example, with Titanium Elite status, expiring Ambassador Elite members will enjoy many of the same perks of their current status, including complimentary breakfast at most brands, suite upgrades at check-in, 75% bonus points earned on stays and a status match to United MileagePlus Premier Silver status.

You should see your new Marriott status and updated expiration in early March.
