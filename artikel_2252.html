'There is a chance' - Sheffield Wednesday face Keillor-Dunn recall fear

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

A new era is underway at Sheffield Wednesday following the appointment of Dave Challinor as the club's new manager.

Challinor was named as Henrik Pedersen's replacement at Hillsborough this week, and while his immediate focus will be on improving Wednesday's form in League One, he may already have one eye on the January transfer window.

Wednesday made a total of 18 new signings during the summer transfer window as part of their rebuild under new owner David Storch, and Davis Keillor-Dunn was one of their standout additions, with the forward arriving on loan from Wrexham until January.

After scoring 35 goals in 78 games during a productive spell at League One side Barnsley, Keillor-Dunn joined Championship outfit Wrexham in January for a fee of around £2 million, but he made just seven substitute appearances for the Red Dragons in the second half of last season.

Having continued to struggle for game time at the SToK Cae Ras in the early stages of this season, Keillor-Dunn made the temporary move to Wednesday in September, and he has scored four goals and provided one assist in 10 appearances for the Owls so far, including netting the opener in his side's 1-1 draw with Blackpool at Hillsborough on Sunday.

However, Wednesday may be fearful of losing Keillor-Dunn in January after Wrexham striker Sam Smith was ruled out for the rest of the season with an ACL injury, meaning manager Phil Parkinson could look to bring the 29-year-old back to South Wales to provide extra cover in the attacking areas.

When asked if he was concerned that Keillor-Dunn could be recalled in January, FLW's Sheffield Wednesday fan pundit Patrick McKenna admitted there is a possibility the forward could return to Wrexham given their injury situation and their underwhelming start to the Championship season.

"I think it is kind of surprising that Wrexham have been so poor this season, so they may want to bring him back," Patrick told FLW.

"If Wrexham were having a decent season in the Championship, they wouldn't need him.

"There is a chance that they may want to buy someone else in the January transfer window and spend a bit of money, but there is a chance that they will want Keillor-Dunn back.

"I suppose we'll just have to wait and see on that one.

"Wrexham have been having a dreadful season, and with Josh Windass out as well, they may want to bring Keillor-Dunn back for some attacking prowess."

With Louie Barry out injured, Keillor-Dunn has been Wednesday's main attacking threat in recent weeks, so it would certainly be a major blow if they were unable to extend his loan beyond January.

Wrexham could opt to recall Keillor-Dunn in January after Smith's season-ending injury, but he has been deemed surplus to requirements for much of his time at the SToK Cae Ras, so it seems more likely that the Red Dragons will enter the market for a replacement.
