Sheffield Wednesday star could 'easily depart' to allow Wrexham transfer - Ipswich Town will watch

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Sheffield Wednesday went through a massive overhaul of their first team squad during the summer transfer window.

The Owls completed 18 deals in the aftermath of Arise Capital finalising their takeover of the club at the beginning of May.

One of the final deals that they completed was the loan of Davis Keillor-Dunn from Wrexham, with the forward arriving until January 2027.

The 29-year-old has contributed three goals and one assist from his first nine appearances in League One.

However, if the Yorkshire outfit want to keep him on loan for the remainder of the term, then it may require some reshuffling of the squad and their other loan options due to the terms of the business plan agreed to with the EFL.

Sheffield Wednesday’s other loan options include Harry Gray, Mason Burstow, Ali Al-Hamadi, Kieran Morrison and Jaden Heskey.

When asked which loan should be terminated to make room to keep Keillor-Dunn for the second half of the season, FLW’s Sheffield Wednesday fan pundit Patrick McKenna claimed that it’s between Ali Al-Hamadi and Mason Burstow.

However, the latter has started to perform well in the last few weeks, which has put Al-Hamadi’s position at Hillsborough under significant threat, if an exit will be necessary to retain Keillor-Dunn.

“Looking at the loan players we have, I think at the minute the choice I would make would be Al-Hamadi,” McKenna told Football League World.

“He hasn’t had a lot of game time, he’s injured at the minute, but he hasn’t really done much this season and I think he probably would be the one you would get rid of.

“With Burstow, a few games ago I probably would’ve said I’d be happy for him to go, but in the past few games he shown what he can offer us.

“Unfortunately, for Al-Hamadi, that’s just put him ahead on the list.

“The other three loan players, I definitely want to keep all of them, so it would be between Burstow and Al-Hamadi, so just at the minute it would be Al-Hamadi we could most easily depart back.”

Al-Hamadi arrived in the summer on loan from Premier League side Ipswich Town, but he has struggled for consistent game time.

The injury-plagued forward has made eight appearances in League One, with only one of those coming as a starter, and he has yet to contribute a goal or assist.

Meanwhile, Burstow has found the back of the net twice from 11 League One appearances, including in the 7-2 win against Bromley, and the recent 1-1 draw at home to Cambridge United.

Sheffield Wednesday have appointed Dave Challinor as their new manager this week, and he will be aiming to lead the club to promotion to the Championship.

Keillor-Dunn could be a key part of that ambition, with the forward already scoring on three occasions from nine appearances in League One for the Owls.

However, it remains to be seen whether a deal can be agreed with Wrexham to extend his loan spell beyond January, particularly with Sam Smith now injured for the Red Dragons.

Challinor’s first game in charge will take place on 8 November against Oldham Athletic on a 5.15pm kick-off.

Keillor-Dunn has looked dangerous for Sheffield Wednesday this season, and he is someone that Challnor will surely want to keep in the squad beyond January.

The financial situation at Hillsborough is precarious as a result of coming out of administration, and terminating a loan may be required as a result.
