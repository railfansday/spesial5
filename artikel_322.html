Fiona the hippo, one of the internets most beloved animal icons, has died

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Fiona, the beloved Cincinnati Zoo hippo who became an internet sensation after surviving a premature birth in 2017, has died at age 9. The zoo announced Thursday that Fiona died after accidentally swallowing a knit hat that fell into her enclosure earlier this month, despite days of intensive veterinary care.

Her death has prompted an outpouring of grief online from fans who watched her grow from a vulnerable 29-pound newborn into one of the internet's most recognizable animal celebrities.

Born six weeks premature, Fiona became a viral sensation as the Cincinnati Zoo documented her unlikely survival on social media. Millions followed her progress, celebrating everything from her first steps to her swimming lessons. Nearly a decade later, she remained a beloved fixture of internet culture, inspiring memes, merchandise, and a devoted online following.

Nearly a decade later, the internet is mourning her loss. The Cincinnati Zoo confirmed her death in a heartbreaking statement, explaining that its veterinary team had spent days attempting to help Fiona pass the foreign object. Despite round-the-clock care and multiple treatments, her condition deteriorated.

"We are in shock at the moment," Cincinnati Zoo director Dave Jenike said in a statement, as reported by the BBC. "We did not expect this outcome. She fought until she couldn't anymore."

According to the zoo, the hat entered Fiona's habitat in a freak accident involving a visitor and a ceiling fan. The zoo consulted veterinary and medical specialists, but surgery was not considered a viable option for the full-grown hippo.

"Our animal care team was heroic in their efforts to save her," Jenike said. "They did everything possible, but her body couldn't fight anymore."

Long before the internet fell in love with Moo Deng, there was Fiona.

When Fiona arrived on Jan. 24, 2017, she was the smallest premature hippo known to survive in human care. Unable to stand long enough to nurse from her mother, Bibi, she required intensive care from zoo staff, who worked alongside specialists from Cincinnati Children's Hospital to keep her alive.

Her recovery quickly became an online phenomenon. Through photos, videos, and regular updates from the Cincinnati Zoo, fans followed Fiona's earliest milestones, from learning to swim to growing strong enough to reunite with her family. The hashtag #TeamFiona became a rallying cry for people who had never visited Cincinnati but nevertheless felt invested in the little hippo's survival.

She eventually inspired children's books, merchandise, memes, and even an ice cream flavor. Her birthday became an occasion for celebration, and visitors traveled from around the world to see her in person.

For the past nine years, Fiona has been a familiar presence in people's social media feeds, an unlikely celebrity whose fans have watched her grow from a vulnerable newborn into a playful, 2,000-pound hippo.

And unlike so many fleeting internet sensations, Fiona never really disappeared from the public's affection.

News of Fiona's death prompted an outpouring of grief across social media Thursday, with longtime fans sharing memories, photographs, and tributes to the hippo who had become a symbol of resilience.

For many, the circumstances of her death made the news especially difficult to process. Social media users expressed anger that an accidentally discarded object could have such devastating consequences, while others urged compassion and discouraged directing harassment toward the family involved.

The loss also resonated with Cincinnati residents, for whom Fiona had become something of an unofficial mascot. Her unlikely survival story was woven into the city's identity, and her life had been celebrated by generations of zoo visitors.

"Fiona was such a gift," Cincinnati Zoo Executive Chair of the Board Thane Maynard said in a statement. "I have never known an animal so beloved in all my life."

For an animal whose earliest days were shared with the world, her death feels unusually personal. Millions of people watched her learn to swim, celebrated her birthdays, and cheered as she grew into a healthy adult. They knew her story because they had been following it almost from the beginning.

The internet helped make Fiona famous. But it was her unlikely survival — and the joy she brought people along the way — that made her unforgettable.
