Juventus to go for Gustavo Varela

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

According to Corriere dello Sport, Juventus have identified Gustavo Varela as one of their primary striker targets and have already made enquiries with Monza regarding a potential transfer.

The Portuguese forward has enjoyed a remarkable start to the season, scoring 11 goals in 11 appearances across all competitions and establishing himself as one of Serie A’s most prolific attackers.

Juventus are searching for a physically imposing centre-forward and view Varela as a player capable of making an immediate impact. His performances have attracted widespread attention, although Monza are believed to value the 24-year-old at around €35 million, a figure that could prove challenging given Juventus’ financial constraints.

The Bianconeri may therefore need to explore a creative deal structure, potentially involving a loan with an obligation to buy or a package spread across multiple payments.

Juventus are far from alone in the race. Napoli have also made enquiries, while interest from the Premier League is growing. Tottenham Hotspur, Brentford and Sunderland have all requested information on the striker, while Liverpool are reportedly sending scouts to monitor his performances on a regular basis.

An additional complication comes from Benfica. The Portuguese giants retain a 20 per cent sell-on clause and reportedly possess matching rights on any future offer.
