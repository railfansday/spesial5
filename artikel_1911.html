Stefan Borson reveals what new London Stadium plans mean for West Ham

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

West Ham United may struggle to see any upside as new plans emerge to make the most out of the London Stadium.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that financial benefit from the stadium's use should not be a priority for West Ham.

The 2026-27 campaign has been a rollercoaster in East London so far, with promotion back to the Premier League certainly no guarantee.

Nuno Espirito Santo's tenure came to a very abrupt ending, and Scott Parker is now in charge, though his reign has gotten off to a poor start.

There remains some focus behind the scenes, though. It has emerged that the London Stadium could be used as a venue for more boxing events.

Under proposals submitted to Newham Council, attendances would be allowed to increase from 60,000 to 85,000, helping the stadium to compete with the likes of Wembley and Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

However, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson has poured cold water on the idea of the Hammers benefiting in any way from this news.

"I actually think it's carved out of the deal. All the benefit goes to the London Authority, from memory," he said.

"I'm not sure that they would get any upside at all from the use of the stadium for a non-footballing event above 60,000. But we'd need to check the terms.

"So whatever it is, it's very limited upside for the club. It's just not going to move the dial significantly compared to everything else that's important for them, i.e. promotion.

"West Ham can't really be thinking about anything else right now other than getting back into Premier League."

The supporters will certainly agree with Borson's verdict when it comes to what is really important to the Hammers this season.

It's not about getting more cash from the London Stadium. It's about getting West Ham back to the Premier League at the first time of asking.

And according to Opta, Parker's side are on track to finish fifth in the Championship standings, with an 8.26 per cent chance of winning the title.

Chances of promotion have dropped to 17.68 per cent, while promotion via the play-offs is rated at 57.36 per cent right now. Things could change.
