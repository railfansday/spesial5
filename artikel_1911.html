Fabian Hurzeler issues major Brighton injury update with Kaoru Mitoma and Stefanos Tzimas latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Brighton boss Fabian Hurzeler still has a number of injury issues ahead of their Premier League clash against Hull City.

Brighton - who are second in the table - resume Premier League action this Sunday after the international break at Sergej Jakirovic’s 13th place team.

The match is expected to arrive too soon for the likes of Stefanos Tzimas and Kaoru Mitoma but Chema Andres, Pascal Stuijk, Femi Azeez and Yankuba Minteh are available.

Mitoma, 29 has been sidelined with a hamstring injury and is yet to feature this season and Tzimas continues his recovery after a serious ACL issue.

Hurzeler, speaking to Sussex World, said Mitoma is getting closer but remains “one or two weeks” away from a return.

Tzimas appears to to be further along and has been pictured training with the first team. “Stefanos is a special character,” said Hurzeler.

“We always try to help him and he is a great personality and everyone is happy for him.

“He fought hard for the comeback. How he looks very good after such a long injury, is very impressive. He will not be immediately an option for us but we are happy that he is back.”

Minteh is a available despite an injury scare while on international duty with Gambia and Chema trained on Thursday despite withdrawing from the Spain under-21 squad with a “knock.”

There was however bad news for Zadok Yohanna who picked up a “re-injury” while on duty with the Nigeria under-23 squad.

Yohanna had just returned to fitness following a muscle strain but now faces another spell on the sidelines.

“Zadok, unfortunately, he got a re-injury when he was with Nigeria under-21s,” Hurzeler explained. “Unfortunately, it wasn't perfectly managed and therefore he had a re-injury, which is very bad and very unlucky for Zadok, who is a young personality, a great personality.

“But we will help him now, we will support him now and try to get him back on pitch as quick as possible. But it's a tough period for him, but we are there to help him.”
