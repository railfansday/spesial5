Parker Drops Hint Over West Ham Goalkeeping Change

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

West Ham head coach Scott Parker has refused to commit to either Mads Hermansen or Fin Herrick ahead of Saturday’s Championship clash against Stoke City. The Hammers boss was asked about his goalkeeping selection during today’s pre-match press conference and was decidedly non-committal.

Speaking to the club’s official website, Parker said: “I’ve been hugely impressed with both Mads [Hermansen] and Fin [Herrick], so we’ll manage things, keep working hard and then make our decision.”

“As always, we’ll see. The goalkeeping position is probably one of the hardest on the pitch. You’re there for all eyes to see.”

Personally, I would start with Mads Hermansen in goal against Stoke City. I completely understand why Scott Parker chose to go with Fin Herrick over the last couple of games, but the truth is it hasn’t worked.

I’ve little doubt that the last couple of performances will have dented the young keeper’s confidence, and taking him out of the firing line might not be a bad thing.

Hermansen, whose recent performances have seen him dropped from the West Ham team

Unfortunately, we’re currently in a situation where it’s a case of picking whoever is the least worst, rather than selecting a player because they’ve done something impressive.

If Parker’s non-committal comments are anything to go by, I suspect we’ll see Hermansen reinstated to the team to face Stoke.
