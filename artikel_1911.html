‘Restricted’ - Mats Wieffer makes Brighton and Chema Andres claim as best position revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Brighton star Mats Wieffer admits he felt ‘restricted’ in the full back role as he targets a regular spot in central midfield.

The Dutch international joined Brighton as a midfielder for around £25m in 2024 but often played as a reluctant right full back.

It's a position Wieffer adapted to pretty well but now the former Feyenoord man is keen to make an impact in his preferred central midfield role.

Ferdi Kadioglu and Costinha are now the recognised right backs at Brighton, which means Wieffer can now compete for midfield minutes, alongside the likes of Pascal Gross, Yasin Ayari, Jack Hinshelwood, Diego Gomez and Chema Andres, who has impressed since his summer arrival.

Chema joined for around £20m from VfB Stuttgart and Wieffer feels they operate in a similar position.

Wieffer - who is fit once again following a recent knee injury - said to the club website: "I can still play both, but I'm happy to play in midfield.

"Now I need to keep the rhythm, train hard, and get all the principles there, because for the last two seasons I was more focused on full-back.

“I think Chema [Andres] and I are a little bit similar, but the rest are a little bit different. If I have to compare myself, I'm more like Chema than the others.”

He added: “You're always happy to play, but overall I prefer midfield because I've always done it. As a full-back, you're a little bit restricted, and I think I'm a better player as a midfielder overall.

“But last season went really well, and he always told me I was playing well and the system suited me. The stats were good, which gave me confidence that he put me in the starting XI.

“The team and playing are always the most important things for me. In the end I'm happy, but if I could choose I would play in midfield.”

Brighton are second in the table and resume Premier league action after the international break at Hull City.

It's been an impressive start to the season as Brighton have 22 points from 10 matches and are the division's leading scorers.

Wieffer said: “Last season we had the same kind of principles we have now, but this season we are doing it every game.

“When everybody sees that the things we train are working it gives the feeling that if we keep doing this it will pay off in points.

“Most importantly, it's because of the things we do like the pressing. Last season we didn't do it every game.

“In the bigger games we were always there but sometimes in the games we thought we needed to win it was a little bit less and opponents punished us.

“That was frustrating - getting points from a bigger team and then dropping points against a team we thought we were equals with.”
