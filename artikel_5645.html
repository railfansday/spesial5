Pennsylvania confirms 9th measles-related death

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Pennsylvania health officials on Monday confirmed the state’s ninth measles-related death this year, less than a week after the U.S. lost its measles elimination status after more than 25 years. The Pennsylvania Department of Health said an unvaccinated Centre County resident died after testing positive for the virus. The state has recorded 1,638 measles cases…
