ECHO Names Four Builders for 1,260-Lot Utah Community

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Cole West Land Co. has announced four home builders for ECHO, its 325-acre master-planned community in Erda, Utah. Century Communities, Cole West, David Weekley Homes, and AMH will deliver eight distinct home collections across the 1,260-lot development, offering a mix of for-sale townhomes and single-family homes as well as single-family rentals.

“Cole West Land Co. has thoughtfully planned ECHO to create a distinctive lifestyle that brings together connected neighborhoods, amenities, and a range of housing options for residents at every stage of life,” says Logan Fawcett, president of Cole West Land Co. “By partnering with an exceptional group of home builders, each with its own approach to home design, we’re creating a place where a variety of home styles and floor plans come together within a cohesive community vision.”

Century Communities will offer three single-family collections. Its Cottage homes will range from 1,679 to 2,149 square feet; Legacy homes from 1,852 to 2,855 square feet; and Estates homes from 2,330 to 3,326 square feet.

Cole West will introduce three collections, including Fairview townhomes starting in the upper $300,000s; Benton single-family homes starting in the mid-$400,000s; and Quartz single-family homes starting in the low $500,000s.

David Weekley Homes’ Echo Prairie single-family collection will range from 1,662 to 3,895 square feet, with two to five bedrooms.

AMH will add a rental component with its Lakeside Peaks collection of single-family homes. The three- to five-bedroom rentals will range from 1,600 to 2,300 square feet, with monthly rents expected between $2,400 and $2,700.

Cole West acquired the ECHO property in October 2025 in what the company described as one of the largest master-planned land acquisitions in Northern Utah. Cole West is serving as both master developer and one of the community’s home builders.

The approximately $600 million development is planned with parks, trails, and open space alongside future commercial and civic uses, church sites, and a future Erda City Office. Located in Tooele Valley, ECHO will provide access to downtown Tooele and the Salt Lake Valley via State Route 36. Model home grand openings are anticipated in spring 2027.
