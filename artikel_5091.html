A pattern seen before: XRP’s $1 restoration could turn the Elliott Wave-based price behavior thesis into reality.

The Elliott Wave-assisted technical analysis breaks down the latest XRP price drawdown to four main phases. First of all, the “hardest correction” happened at the ABC trajectory following the Wave 1 top, which counts as XRP’s all-time high at roughly $3.65.

After that was completed, the Wave 3 expanded, turning the regular flat into an extended one, according to Dark Defender. The technical chart analyst is now calling for XRP’s bounce back rally towards $1.6992, a measured move that aligns with the local high of a re-labeled Wave D.

In order to reach that, XRP’s price performance must sustain a series of Fib-based levels, such as 61.8% at $1.4742, 50% at $1.1388, and 38.2% at $0.8797). On that scale, $1.6992 is simply the local high of the re-labeled Wave D. The core condition is holding the $1 XRP base channel.

Meanwhile, the XRP chart’s Fibonacci extension targets up to 361.8% (around $18.23) from recent lows, preserving the larger Wave 2 framework while adjusting internal waves in this complex corrective phase ending around 2026-2027, according to Dark Defender’s thesis.

Regardless of the Elliot Waves, claiming back that $1.69 XRP price level would surely require a substantial recovery in activity. Relevant ongoing or near-term factors include macroeconomic liquidity, further regulatory signals, Futures market positioning squeezes & whale accumulation.
