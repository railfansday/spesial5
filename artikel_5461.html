CPC Biotech Launches the Quattroflow HTX All-in-One Pump Drive for Bioprocessing

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

DOWNERS GROVE, Ill., Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- CPC Biotech, part of PSG and Dover (NYSE: DOV) and a leading manufacturer of connectors, pumps, and flow sensors used in biopharmaceutical processing, today announced the launch of the Quattroflow HTX, an all-in-one pump drive featuring touchscreen control, flexible remote operation, and fluid-specific calibration.

The HTX pairs with the same four-piston diaphragm pump heads used across the Quattroflow range, in single-use and multiple-use configurations. The new drive includes an upgraded motor, drive electronics, software, control functions, and interfaces designed to enhance operations.

The HTX was created with the operator in mind. A five-inch capacitive touchscreen replaces the keypad and small display of the previous generation and has been tested with several types of gloves, including cleanroom gloves. Speed, flow rate, and dispense volume can be adjusted directly on the screen using the operator's preferred units, and the drive displays speed, temperature, run hours, alarms, and fault codes.

"Ease of use was the starting point for the HTX. Its intuitive touchscreen and integrated, space-saving design eliminate the need for a separate inverter, so it works as a standalone unit on a laboratory bench or in bioprocessing applications," said Andreas Frerix, Product Manager Quattroflow at CPC Biotech.

The HTX connects over Modbus TCP via Ethernet, with analog and digital inputs and outputs available as a hardware alternative. Firmware updates and pump configurations can be transferred by USB, allowing configurations to be replicated across pumps.

It also offers fluid-specific calibration to support accurate flow control across a range of viscosities and densities. Operators can perform a test dispense, measure volume using a scale or a flow meter, and enter the result, allowing the software to recalculate its internal curve.

"The accuracy of the Quattroflow pump is what makes the HTX so versatile. Because each motor revolution displaces a defined volume, the HTX can reliably calculate flow rates and dispense precise amounts of fluid, for example, when filling bottles or containers," added Frerix.

Dispensing mode enables repeatable, measured volume dispensing on demand. With a foot switch connected, the operator fills a container, moves the next one into place and fills again. The pumping principle is unchanged, producing low-pulse, low-shear transfer that supports the range's established use in tangential flow filtration and chromatography.

Additional features include motor fault and overpressure alarms, timestamped alarm logging, and configurable user access controls with PIN-protected permissions. The interface is available in English, German, and French. Applications include media and buffer transfer, precise dispensing and small-batch filling, and small-scale filtration.

The HTX represents the next generation of the HT platform introduced in 2016 and is available to order in three sizes. For more information about the performance and versatility of the Quattroflow HTX Drive Series, please visit the HTX webpage.
