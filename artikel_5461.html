E.U. leaders nominated a former Spanish central banker to lead the ECB

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Pablo Hernández de Cos is expected to succeed Christine Lagarde on Feb. 7 after the E.U. consults Parliament and the ECB

The European Council put forward Pablo Hernández de Cos, a former Bank of Spain governor, as the next president of the European Central Bank on Thursday.

Once confirmed, Hernández de Cos is expected to replace Christine Lagarde when she leaves office on Feb. 7, 2027, the European Council said. The ECB president serves an eight-year term that cannot be renewed.

The European Council also put forward Laurence Boone and Markus Brunnermeier as members of the ECB's executive board. They would replace Isabel Schnabel, who has announced she will step down on Jan. 3, 2027, and Philip Lane, whose term ends May 31, 2027.

The European Council will consult the European Parliament and the ECB's governing council on all three candidates before taking a formal decision. The Council of the European Union adopted its recommendations on the appointments to the European Council on Thursday.

Final approval is expected at the EU leaders' summit in December, according to Reuters. The Netherlands threw its support behind Hernández de Cos on Thursday, which in practice ended the candidacy of its own nominee, Klaas Knot, who previously led the Dutch central bank. Hernández de Cos would preside over his debut rate-setting session on March 18.

Hernández de Cos is general manager of the Bank for International Settlements. He served as governor of the Bank of Spain from 2018 to June 2024, according to CNBC.

Boone, a French national, would probably run the bank's market operations, while Brunnermeier, a German national, is anticipated to fill the chief economist post, according to Reuters.

Lagarde had announced last month that she would step down ahead of the scheduled end of her term, which had been set to expire on Oct. 31, 2027. Schnabel's departure and the end of Lane's term left two other top ECB posts open.

The handover comes as the ECB faces higher prices. Euro area inflation hit 4% in October, with the Iran war lifting the cost of oil and gas, according to Reuters.

Lagarde congratulated Hernández de Cos on Thursday and praised his "intellect, warmth and good humour," according to The Associated Press. "If he is confirmed, I know these qualities will serve him well as ECB President," she said, according to the AP.
