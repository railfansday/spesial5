PinnacleQM launches new enterprise website to help Australian organisations deliver more with confidence

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The new pinnacleqm.com brings PinnacleQM's quality engineering, AI assurance and automation platforms together in one place for Australian IT and business leaders

PERTH, Australia, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- PinnacleQM, the Australian-owned software testing, quality engineering and AI assurance consultancy, today launched its new enterprise website at https://www.pinnacleqm.com. The site replaces a six-page brochure with a structured library of more than 50 pages. Each service, enterprise platform, industry and proprietary product now has its own page, so CIOs, CTOs, programme directors and procurement teams can see exactly what PinnacleQM does, how it does it and what it has delivered.

"I have spent most of my career alongside CIOs and programme directors on IT change that simply cannot fail, and the question they ask has never changed: can you show me it works?" said Gary Jenn, Managing Director of PinnacleQM. "Our new website is our answer. It is backed by real results, so buyers can understand our capability before the first conversation, not after it. That kind of transparency is what our clients have always expected from us."

Visitors will find dedicated pages for every PinnacleQM service, from test automation, performance engineering and penetration testing to UAT, DevSecOps, independent assurance and managed testing-as-a-service, plus SAP, Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics, Oracle and IBM Maximo testing. A new AI assurance section at https://www.pinnacleqm.com/ai/ covers AI production readiness, agentic AI and AI governance, built around the Pinnacle AI Assurance Framework. Pages for the QMFactory, Enginuity and INsight platforms, industry pages, case studies and insights complete the site.

"This website is the work of many hands, and I am proud of every one of them," said Niranjan Limbachiya, Director of PinnacleQM. "Our consulting and delivery teams gave us the substance, and our design and development team, working closely with our partners, brought our vision to life. I thank each of them."

Visitors can explore services and client outcomes, book a demo or request a proposal at https://www.pinnacleqm.com/contact-us/.

PinnacleQM is an Australian-owned software testing, quality engineering and digital business assurance consultancy and part of the Pinnacle Group. Headquartered in Perth with offices in Brisbane, Melbourne and Sydney, it helps government and enterprise organisations reduce the risk of IT change through independent testing, test automation, AI assurance and managed testing services, supported by its own no-code AI automation platforms.Visit https://www.pinnacleqm.com.
