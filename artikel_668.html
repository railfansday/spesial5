Nominees for AARP Movies & TV for Grownups Awards Include Newly AARP-Eligible Andrew Scott and Keri Russell

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Winners will be announced at a ceremony at the Beverly Wilshire in Beverly Hills on Jan. 9, 2027, which will be hosted by Jane Lynch and subsequently broadcast on PBS's Great Performances on Feb. 26.

The AARP has announced its nominees for the 26th annual AARP Movies & TV for Grownups Awards, an event which aims to elevate experienced talent and challenge ageism by celebrating work by and about people at or above the age of 50 — the age at which people become AARP-eligible.

Multi-nominated films include The Odyssey, Wild Horse Nine and Tender Loving Care, and multi-nominated TV shows include Widow’s Bay, Hacks and DTF St. Louis. Meanwhile, individual nominees include Tom Cruise, Meryl Streep, Matt Damon and Jean Smart — and the newly AARP-eligible Andrew Scott and Keri Russell. Winners will be announced at a ceremony at the Beverly Wilshire in Beverly Hills on Jan. 9, 2027, which will be hosted by Jane Lynch and subsequently broadcast on PBS’s Great Performances on Feb. 26.

“This year’s nominees are proof that experience is one of the most powerful creative forces in film and television,” Dr. Myechia Minter-Jordan, the CEO of AARP, said in a statement. “From legends like Meryl Streep and Helen Mirren to acclaimed creators like Christopher Nolan and Chris Rock, these artists are doing some of the best work of their careers and reminding audiences and the industry alike that great stories have no age limit.”
