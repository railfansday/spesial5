Paramount+ Is Down to $2.99 a Month With This Black Friday Deal

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A lot has changed at Paramount since last Black Friday, but its streaming discounts are staying the same. The company is bringing back its popular Paramount+ Black Friday deal, offering both its Essential and Premium plans for $2.99 per month for the first two months.

The limited-time promotion, which launches ahead of Black Friday on Nov. 27, brings both Paramount+ Essential and Paramount+ Premium down to $2.99 per month for the first two months — a nearly 80% discount on the Premium plan (typically costs $13.99 per month) and a roughly 67% discount on the ad-supported Essential plan (regularly priced at $8.99 per month).
