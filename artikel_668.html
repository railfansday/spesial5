U2 Reveal North American Dates For 2027 Greatest Show on Earth World Tour

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The run is slated to kick off on Aug. 13 in Miami at Hard Rock Stadium and include shows in Georgia, Maryland, New Jersey, Illinois, Toronto and Massachusetts.

U2 rolled out the North American dates for their upcoming 2027 Carnaval De Luz presents: The Greatest Show on Earth World Tour outing on Tuesday morning (Nov. 24). The first run of shows in the U.S. and Canada, were announced after the veteran Irish rockers already revealed shows in Latin America, the U.K. and Europe slated for next year. The dates will pick up again on Sept. 27 at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, followed by a double-down at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles (Oct. 1-2) and stops in Las Vegas and Santa Clara, Calif. before wrapping things up on Oct. 18 at BC Place in Vancouver; the War on Drugs will support on the second run of North American shows.

The new dates come on top of previously announced 2027 gigs in Latin America, the U.K. and Europe (see below) in support of the group’s just-released 16th studio album, Carnaval de Luz.
