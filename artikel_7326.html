FELYRI CONSULTING LIMITED: Research Consultant

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

As a locally based consulting firm, we specialize in research on international trade, climate change, geopolitics, and strategic risks in the United Kingdom and around the world. We provide professional consulting services to businesses and organizations in areas such as policy compliance, climate risk, supply chain financing, and political risk. We monitor global news headlines and seek to identify potential business opportunities within them.

The Role:This position is a strategic consulting role responsible for providing professional risk consulting and risk solutions aimed at supporting the sound operations and strategic development of organizations. The incumbent will be required to identify, assess, and address various complex risks arising from both internal and external business environments, including policy risks, market risks, compliance risks, and operational risks.

Candidates are required to possess more than five years of in-depth policy analysis experience. The role focuses on forward-looking risk forecasting by systematically analyzing government policy announcements, international geopolitical events, and industry statistical data. The consultant must accurately identify potential emerging risks and external uncertainties in advance, formulate targeted and executable risk response strategies, and provide professional solution recommendations to prevent and reduce organizational risk losses.

We use a project-based billing system, with compensation for each report ranging from $500 to $1,000. The specific amount is determined based on a combination of factors, including the report’s complexity, comprehensiveness, and quality.
