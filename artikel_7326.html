You have a knack for process innovation and project management and are a true specialist in Benefits. When collaborating with stakeholders to define requirements related to our 401(k) MEP and Benefits Billing, you should serve as an expert who can build solutions from scratch and think creatively to enhance team productivity and processes. You possess an ability to perform complex analysis, develop findings, and improve project operations critical to Financial Benefits.

As the Financial Benefits Project Manager, you will be tasked with developing processes and strategies to ensure that larger projects function and operate as intended. Your responsibilities will encompass onboarding, overseeing, and managing the new and existing projects related to both the 401(k) MEP and additional benefits billing, including Medical, Dental and Vision, commuter, voluntary benefits and more. Your role will require engagement with stakeholders from various sectors, including external auditors and partners, fiduciaries and internal teams, including the Retirement teams, the Benefits Billing team, Product and Engineering, Internal Audit, and Finance.

The Financial Benefits Project Manager will help shape both the MEP Retirement benefit and Benefits Billing at Justworks by managing projects related to customer risk, contributions, compliance, payroll information, reconciliation and process automation. This position will impact the operating procedures of the team by understanding customer and policy data and recommending process changes to ensure customer and PEO compliance.
