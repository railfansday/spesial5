37 Best Housewarming Gifts to Get You Welcomed Back (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Yes, the best housewarming gifts should ideally be useful, but that doesn’t mean you should completely abandon elements of charm and whimsy when you’re selecting the perfect item. Whether you’re bestowing something special upon a new homeowner or a pal settling into a new apartment, a vibe-enhancing and aesthetically pleasing present goes a long way to add warmth and personality to any new space.

With that in mind, we’ve rounded up the best housewarming gifts—many of which have blessed our own spaces—at various price points. You’ll find practical presents, such as throw blankets and kitchen tools; home decor items, like chic glassware; and a few indulgences, like caviar. Conveniently, several options also work as hostess gifts.

Here are some of our absolute favorite housewarming gift ideas for kicking off a whole new era of feeling at home, in an elevated way.

This lightweight set of borosilicate glasses has a playful raised-spot pattern. They’re blessedly dishwasher-safe, which matters a lot for glasses in daily use.

This high-quality reprint of a 1902 guest book is shopping writer Julia Harrison’s go-to housewarming gift. The book instructs guests to draw a pig rather than the more traditional practice of writing a note. This is “for most guests, a great relief,” says Harrison.

It’s not often that we come across a toolbox that is actually attractive. This model from Japanese brand Toyo sure is, and it’s just the right size for essentials, like screwdrivers, a level, and a measuring tape. It’s sure to get used by the end of the first week in every new home.

Kitchen towels get used harder than almost anything else in a new home. These are heavy cotton terry made in Portugal, with a jacquard pattern on one side and loops on the other. They’re thick enough to stand in for a trivet on many surfaces, which most kitchen towels can’t claim.

Gifting someone beautiful custom stationery is a thoughtful and useful way to make their new chapter that much more official. It’s one of the easier housewarming gifts to personalize, and you’re sure to smile when you receive a handwritten thank-you note.

This isn’t your grandpa’s soap on a rope. Ash’s revamp involves a spicy yet floral scent and a pop of color in its elegant red string.

For those who prefer to sip their spirits neat, these hand-blown mezcal glasses are a vibrant way to toast to the new digs.

Do the eternal (or aspirational!) host or hostess a favor, and introduce this AD-favorite coffee table book into their collection. Its pages are full of dinner-party inspiration, including interviews with tastemakers, like Laura Gonzalez, about their entertaining philosophy.

Any coffee enthusiast will treasure a high-quality French press, and we’re partial to this design from Le Creuset. The kitchen must-have is available in a number of colorways to suit neutral tastes or a more maximalist style.

Feed their caffeine addiction with style by showing up to a housewarming with a sleek-looking coffee maker and a bag of their favorite French roast.

Help the amateur chef in your friend group get a head start on their new home’s kitchen essentials. Flamingo Estate is known for its homegrown pantry wares; this oil-and-vinegar gift set includes a California-grown olive oil and persimmon vinegar. In a rush? This gift set comes conveniently prewrapped.

Steak knives don’t feel like an essential until you’re sawing through prime rib with a butter knife. We love this set for its approachable price point and handsome riveted wood handle. (And don’t worry, though inspired by a 19th-century French design, these blades are dishwasher-safe.)

These hand-painted candles elevate the traditional taper. Choose between three floral motifs to best suit their style and personality.

Take the stress out of floral arrangements with a ruffled set of bud vases. Each varies in height to make a dramatic array, whether filled with single stems or small bunches.

Senior shopping editor Nashia Baker loves this blanket, which she says is “incredibly fluffy, warm, and easy to care for.” The faux mohair throw feels soft, has elegant-looking plush tassels at either end, and it’s light enough to stay out on the sofa all year. It comes in a variety of colorways that will accent and elevate any living room.

Anyone with a predilection for kitschy-chic home decor will swoon over these delicate shell napkin rings from Gohar World. Gifting this set will likely seal your invite to their upcoming dinner party.

A bouquet is a classic gift on its own, but you can level it up by presenting it arranged in a stunning vase. This one from Zara Home features a graphic black-and-white stripe that perfectly contrasts a soft floral display.

The best incense holders look like intentional objects even when you’re out of palo santo. Made from raw sand clay, this piece closely mimics the luxurious look of marble while maintaining a playful shape that transforms your tabletop into a micro sculpture garden.

Every kitchen needs a salt dish, and this handcrafted set moves from the counter to the tabletop with ease. Just add a loaf of fresh bread and a good cutting board for the ideal food-prep setup.

If they’ve finally retired their mismatched plates, this is the replacement they need. The 12-piece set from Villeroy & Boch serves four in white porcelain that’s textured to look like matte slate. It would make an equally good wedding gift.

Cheaper napkins exist, of course, but how many resemble 12th-century geometric stone mosaics? This set of four from Cabana will become an heirloom. While hand-embroidered napkins might be a particularly kind touch, purchasing these is perhaps the more sensible choice if you’re on a tight timeline (and don’t know how to stitch).

The hand-blown glass bottle arrives filled with Le Soir hand and body wash, scented with cedar and tangerine. But it will look just as good by the sink once it’s refilled with whatever they buy next.

A custom caviar tin is the fanciest way to celebrate new beginnings. This one from CY Kitchen comes with a mother-of-pearl serving spoon to complete the experience.

Ensure a new homeowner’s greenery thrives with a shiny new watering can and mister gift set. The sculptural design is great for those who still want to prioritize aesthetics when tending to their plant babies.

Cheese boards are a longstanding go-to housewarming gift, and this oyster platter is a surprising and luxurious take on the dishware. It looks like it was plucked right off Gwyneth Paltrow’s kitchen wall and will definitely get a ton of use from a lover of seafood.

Channel a French café vibe with this elevated cutlery set from Parisian label Sabre.

For your coziest friend, a luxe throw blanket is the gift that keeps on giving—for movie nights, late mornings, and everything in between.

Winding stainless steel creates a surrealist botanical motif across the handles of these salad servers. They’re so good, we’re gifting them to everyone we know as housewarming gifts and as a wedding gift when the registry has been picked over.

For the friend who moved solely for a better kitchen, a handblown glass cake stand can almost guarantee that you’ll be the first on call when it’s time to taste-test those new recipes.

For the fashionable host or hostess: This tablecloth takes plissé pleats out of their wardrobe and puts it onto their table.

Little comes in handy as much as a tabletop lighter. Suddenly, there’s no need to rifle through drawers, searching for matches to set the ambience. This sculptural ceramic option looks intentionally placed wherever it sits and makes for a great conversation piece.

For all the champagne toasts to come, SLJ’s coupe set feels special enough to mark any big occasion or milestone. Most people already move in with a set of wine glasses, making coupes the perfect gift.

Salt cellars have risen in popularity over the last few years, and the designs of these humble culinary staples have only gotten more imaginative. This version from AD100 designer Beata Heuman makes for a cute kitchen companion.

These art deco glasses are the perfect size for a short cocktail, whether a sophisticated pregame sip or a low-alc nightcap. The mini size makes them stand apart from the rest of your glassware.

North Carolina metalsmith Colby Shelton Byrd designed these spiral servers exclusively for East Fork. The stainless steel has real heft, and they’ll toss a salad or serve pasta at the first dinner in the new place.

Before thoughtfully gifting this generously sized cushion with a linen-and-cotton canvas front and a linen back, definitely inquire about the color of their sofa. But should you forget, this oceanic blue is an elegant yet unexpected take on a neutral.
