I raised my rates 40% and lost 2 of 11 clients. Here is the math.

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

IT consulting, Germany, one person, small businesses. I put off raising rates for two years because I was scared of losing everyone.

In January I did it. 11 clients, 40% increase, same scope.

2 left. 9 stayed. One of the 9 asked for a discount, I said no, they stayed anyway.

The math: I lost about 18% of revenue from the two who left, and gained 40% from the nine who stayed. Net was roughly +15% for the same hours. Plus two fewer clients to manage.

What I got wrong: I told everyone at once. The two who left were the two I liked least, which sounds like a win, but one of them was also the one who paid on time every month. Cash flow got lumpy for a quarter.

What I would do differently: raise on renewal dates, not all at once. And tell the long term clients first, in person, before the email goes out.

The part nobody mentions: the conversation is 10 minutes and the fear lasts two years. I rehearsed it in my head maybe 50 times. The actual calls were fine.

If you are sitting on a rate increase right now, the math is probably better than you think. Run the numbers on paper first, then do the calls.
