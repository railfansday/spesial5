SAN FRANCISCO, California, November 23rd, 2026/TechnologyWire/--Hyring, the AI hiring platform that has run more than 900,000 AI interviews, is now revolutionizing one of the oldest office practices: the timesheet. With Hyring Insight, employers can track work time and see where the day is actually going.

Conventional time tracking requires employees to remember and keep track of their work hours and managers to trust the data. Hyring Insight does things differently. Employees pick their start times, and that's when tracking begins. Once they finish their workday, they can stop the timer. Any meetings on Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Webex, and Slack huddles are detected and will be counted as meeting time without manual recording. If any time is missed, it can be submitted for approval with the reason.

“Nobody has ever created a company or a product because they loved filling out a timesheet every week,” said Adithyan RK, CEO, Hyring. “The timesheet came into existence because there was no better way at that time to validate and monitor a working day. But there is now. The timer keeps a record of time, and AI helps keep everyone informed about it.”

Enterprises can measure three things with Hyring Insight’s time tracking software: worked time, productivity, and activity. Productivity can be seen in the apps they use and the websites they surf, or Ask AI can go through the person's screenshots and give an analysis of work done.

Ask AI answers simple questions like, ‘What did I work on yesterday?’ or ‘What did the content team work on last month?’ and gives accurate data and charts. These conversations with Ask AI are completely confidential and stick to data without weighing in on any problem.

When there is suspicious activity, Hyring Insight instantly flags this and brings it to the admin's attention. This can include looking at unauthorized websites and unusual inputs caused by mouse activity that is being intentionally falsified, while only comparing the person's data with their previous data and never with a colleague. On the Pro and Enterprise plan, AI will explain why activity was flagged while objectively telling the admin what happened.

“Hours can always go either here or there,” said Adithyan RK, CEO, Hyring. “A manager never wants to know how many hours an employee worked; they want to know what got done and how time can be used more productively. When an AI gives data for this, the timesheet stops being relevant.”

“If AI is going to pinpoint data, that same data must be accessible to the employees too,” said Adithyan RK, CEO, Hyring. “There is a fine line between surveillance and intelligence, and the intention is to stay on the right side of it.”

Hyring Insight runs on a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux with a web portal. Companies can start for free with up to two users or test Pro for 14 days with no credit card needed.

Hyring is an AI hiring platform headquartered in Chennai, India, founded in 2022 by Adithyan RK and Surya N. Its products include the AI Video Interviewer, AI Resume Screener, AI Phone Screener, AI Coding Interviewer, and English Proficiency Test, and the platform has completed 900,000 plus interviews for early-stage startups and Fortune 500 companies.
