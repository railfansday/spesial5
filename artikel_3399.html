Johnny Depp Secured $5 Million Salary for Comeback Film 'Ebenezer': Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Johnny Depp is making his return to the big screen ... and has worked out a deal to make a ton of money if the movie is a box office success.

Variety reports the actor filmed "Ebenezer" for a $5 million salary -- which is less than the $20 million he received for several films.

Sources tell TMZ Depp, who is repped by The Agency Group, negotiated a deal that will pay him handsomely on the back end if it is a financial success.

The retelling of the Charles Dickens classic will be his first movie in 8 years -- following a scandalous stretch of controversy and legal battles.

Depp's big Hollywood appearance was in "Fantastic Beasts: The Crimes of Grindelwald" in 2018.
