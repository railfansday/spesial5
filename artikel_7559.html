Sheppard mandates 4-day-per-week return to office

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Sheppard, Mullin, Richter & Hampton will require all U.S.-based lawyers and staff to spend four days per week in the office starting in 2027. (Image from Shutterstock)

Sheppard, Mullin, Richter & Hampton has become the latest firm to reverse course on COVID-era hybrid work policies, confirming that in 2027, all U.S.-based lawyers and staff will be required to spend four days per week in the office.

The change adds a day to its current three-day requirement. The new policy becomes effective Jan. 1.

Other BigLaw firms—including Cooley; Dechert, Goodwin Procter; and Skadden, Arps, Slate, Meagher & Flom—have moved to require increased in-person attendance.

Other notable firms, including Duane Morris and Vinson & Elkins, are also requiring a four-day in-person attendance minimum, according to a report by Law.com.
