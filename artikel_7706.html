SCOTUS won’t disturb Texas-DOJ deal on deportation proceedings

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Type to search articles, cases, and authors.

Tomorrow at 1 p.m. EDT, Amy will join Briefly’s Adam Stofsky on LinkedIn Live to discuss what unfolded at the court this month. Register here to attend the virtual event.

On Tuesday, the Supreme Court denied a request from three cities and a group that provides legal services to immigrants to pause an agreement between Texas and the Department of Justice that prevents enforcement of a Biden-era rule allowing immigration judges to pause certain deportation proceedings. There were no noted dissents. For more on the dispute, see the On Site section below.

On Oct. 21, the Trump administration asked the justices to allow it to resume construction of its border wall in the Big Bend region of southwestern Texas. Responses to the application are due tomorrow by 4 p.m. EDT.
