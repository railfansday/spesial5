Three Foreign Nationals Arrested And Charged With Scheme To Use Super Intelligence To Defraud Companies Of $129 Million

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

United States Attorney for the Southern District of New York, Jamie McDonald, and Assistant Director in Charge of the New York Field Office of the Federal Bureau of Investigation (“FBI”), James C. Barnacle, Jr., announced today the unsealing of an Indictment charging JACOB SOALL, a/k/a “Seba Clarke,” a/k/a “Rocky Balboa,” SHARON HAZAN, a/k/a “Superman Cash,” a/k/a “Impossible Mission,” a/k/a “Fat Boy,” a/k/a “Ron,” and GUY SHEETRIT for their participation in a scheme to use super intelligence (“SI”) to defraud victim companies all over the world of more than $100 million.
