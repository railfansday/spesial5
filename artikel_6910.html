Zac Affleck Responds on TikTok After Jen Exposes His Awful Comments on Her Postpartum Body: “I Was Absolutely Shocked I Had Said That”

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Jen just filed for divorce amid her fourth pregnancy.

Zac Affleck has responded to multiple videos his ex Jen Affleck posted on TikTok exposing his behavior in their marriage—specifically, footage where Zac can be heard commenting on Jen’s postpartum body.

Replying to one follower who said, “As much as I don’t like Jen.. if my husband told me my boobs were ‘saggy’ 2 weeks after giving birth I’d never forgive him,” Zac said, “This was a video from 2022. Obviously horrible and should have never been said. When I saw the video today I was absolutely shocked I had said that. I said a lot of horrible and hurtful things but I have tried my best to learn and grow.”

Zac also responded to a person who wrote, “Degrading your postpartum wife is INSANE…” with “It was from 2022. And I think that world already knows I made a lot of mistakes and said a lot of absolutely horrible things.”

Reminder: Jen is currently expecting her and Zac’s fourth child, and filed for divorce just a few days ago. Zac allegedly heard about the filing through TMZ, which he confirmed in a TikTok video saying “I found out from a TMZ reporter about the filing. But regardless of that, this is something we've been talking about for a long time. Jen came to me about a year ago, after Dancing With the Stars, and told me she was unhappy and wanted a divorce.”
