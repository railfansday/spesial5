An Inside Look at <i><em>Cosmo’s</em></i> Acne Awards Celebration

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Founders, dermatologists, and estheticians joined the Cosmo team for the ultimate relaxing spa night in NYC.

Who doesn’t love a good sauna and breathwork session to wind down the week? On Thursday, November 19, a group of estheticians, dermatologists, and friends of Cosmo celebrated our 2026 Acne Awards at Akari Spa in NYC’s Lower East Side.

Upon arrival, our guests were greeted by the lovely owners of the spa (and us, of course!), and swapped out office loafers for comfy slippers. Then we stocked the locker room with gift bags filled with Acne Award-winning products, hand-selected by our very own beauty editors.

Everyone got to go home with ANFISA’s Skin ÂN-DEW Azelaic Acid Serum, Sofie Pavitt’s Studio-Smooth Microdermabrasion Scrub, OMAD’s Bio Cellulose Masks, Face Reality’s Omni Clear Mist, Paula’s Choice BHA Liquid Exfoliant, Starface’s Salicylic Acid Pimple Patches, and Ultra Violette Unblock Screen SPF 50.

And what’s a sauna session without a comfy robe? We provided custom ones from embroidery haven Abbode for each guest to wear and take home for their daily GRWM (and un-GRWM) routines.

Once everyone was bundled up, it was time to hit the saunas and cold plunge. Guests sipped on Happier Grocer green juices while sweating it out. Conversation about yoga and breathwork classes, skincare products, and wellness routines filled the rooms.

Upstairs, an even hotter sauna awaited those brave enough to break a major sweat. And of course, we stocked indoor and outdoor lounge areas for cool-off breaks with our latest winter issue. The night was a rejuvenating cycle of sweating, plunging, and relaxing ahead of the holidays.

As the evening wrapped up, we all gathered for the highly anticipated guided breathwork session, led by Alden Price. Safe to say we all ended the night with our peace restored and a lovely new skincare routine to indulge in.
