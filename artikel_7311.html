TRM Labs: Lead Product Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

TRM Labs provides AI-powered intelligence solutions that help public and private sector agencies investigate and disrupt crime. TRM's platforms enable investigators to trace illicit activity, build cases, and construct operating pictures of threat networks. Leading agencies and businesses worldwide rely on TRM to make the world safer and more secure.

What if your next product role wasn't just an opportunity to build a better product, but an opportunity to help define the future of an industry?

At TRM Labs, we're building technology that helps the world's leading financial institutions, governments and law enforcement agencies combat financial crime and safeguard the future of finance.

As a Lead Product Manager, you'll operate with an entrepreneurial mindset, identifying new market opportunities, shaping product strategy and building solutions to problems that don't yet have established answers.

We're looking for someone who thinks like a founder. Someone who can connect emerging technologies, customer needs and commercial opportunities to determine not only what we should build, but where we should go as a business.

You'll operate at the intersection of AI, blockchain, intelligence and financial technology, working alongside exceptional technical and domain experts to turn ambitious ideas into products with meaningful global impact.

This is an opportunity to expand your influence beyond traditional product management and develop the capabilities of a business and product leader.

Own the vision, strategy and outcomes of a strategically important product area.

Identify emerging market opportunities and develop conviction around where TRM should invest.

Lead zero-to-one product development and scale existing products into new markets and use cases.

Shape how AI and emerging technologies can fundamentally transform customer workflows and outcomes.

Build deep relationships with customers and industry leaders to anticipate needs before they become obvious.

Partner with executive leadership to influence strategic priorities and investment decisions.

Drive cross-functional alignment across Engineering, Design, Intelligence, Data Science and GTM.

Influence commercialization, product positioning, pricing and market expansion strategies.

Establish clear success metrics and take accountability for customer adoption, product performance and business impact.

Elevate the broader Product organization through exceptional product judgment, mentorship and leadership by example.

A demonstrated track record of building and scaling complex, high-impact technology products.

Experience owning product strategy and influencing significant business outcomes.

Evidence of identifying new market opportunities and translating them into successful products.

Exceptional strategic judgment, with the ability to navigate ambiguity and make high-conviction decisions with incomplete information.

Strong commercial acumen and an understanding of market dynamics, competitive positioning and business models.

Deep technical curiosity and the ability to partner with exceptional engineering and data science teams.

Demonstrated AI fluency, including the ability to identify opportunities where AI can fundamentally transform products and workflows.

The ability to influence senior stakeholders and drive alignment without relying on formal authority.

An entrepreneurial mindset, exceptional execution and a willingness to challenge conventional thinking.

Think like a founder. Help identify new opportunities, shape product strategy and build meaningful parts of a growing business.

Influence the trajectory of a company. Work on strategically important problems where your decisions can have an outsized impact.

Build AI with real-world consequences. Apply emerging technologies to complex, high-stakes problems where accuracy, trust and customer outcomes matter.

Expand your leadership capabilities. Develop the strategic, commercial and operational judgment of a future product executive, GM or founder.

Leave a meaningful legacy. Help build technology that protects people and institutions for generations to come.

We are building a safer world. That promise shows up in how we work every day.

TRM moves FAST. We are a high-velocity, high-ownership team that expects clarity, follow-through, and impact. People who thrive here are energized by hard problems, experimentation, and continuous feedback. If something takes months elsewhere, it will ship here in days.

Our work sits at the intersection of AI, national security, and fighting crime. The problems are complex, the stakes are real, and the environment evolves quickly. The pace and intensity of the work reflect the importance of the mission. As a result, the way we operate requires a high level of ownership, adaptability, collaboration, and creative problem-solving.

Operating with a high degree of ambiguity

A high level of personal ownership and accountability

Close collaboration across teams and functions

Creative problem-solving and out-of-the-box thinking

A pace that rewards urgency, adaptability, and outcomes

This environment is energizing for people who enjoy building, solving hard problems, and making progress in situations that are not always fully defined. It also requires thriving in ambiguity, adjusting course as new information emerges, and maintaining focus and positivity in a fast-moving and intense environment.

We also recognize that this style of operating is not for everyone. If you are primarily optimizing for predictability or a consistently balanced workload, we encourage you to use the interview process to pressure-test whether this environment is truly the right fit. We want teammates who thrive here, not just survive here.

If you are excited by meaningful problems, motivated by ambitious goals, and energized by working alongside mission-driven colleagues, there is a good chance you will find TRM to be an exceptional place to grow and contribute.

We hire and grow against three leadership principles. They’re the standards for how we operate, treat each other, and make decisions.

Impact-Oriented Trailblazer: We put customers first and move with speed, focus, and adaptability. We treat every plan like an experiment – test, ship, measure, and iterate quickly.

Master Craftsperson: We care deeply about our craft. We balance speed with high standards, own outcomes end‑to‑end, and invest in getting better everyday.

Inspiring Colleague: We add clarity and energy, not noise. We bring humility, candor, and a one‑team mindset — giving and receiving feedback to make the team stronger.

At TRM, we care deeply about our craft. We are looking for individuals who want their work to matter, who experiment with speed and rigor, and who take pride in building a safer world for billions of people. If you’re excited by TRM’s mission but don’t check every box, we encourage you to apply — we hire for slope, judgment, and the will to learn fast.
