Why single-region uptime monitors wake you up at 3 AM — and how we built a two-region monitor with a read-only OAuth 2.1 MCP server

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

If you have ever used a basic single-location uptime checker, you know the feeling: your phone buzzes at 03:14 with CRITICAL: example.com is DOWN, you jump out of bed, open the URL on your laptop, and it loads in 180 ms. Two minutes later: RESOLVED.

The internet between one probe datacenter and your origin has brief BGP reconvergences, packet loss spikes, and transient resolver timeouts every week. A single failed HTTP request from one datacenter is a local network observation, not proof that your website is down for users.

Beamfar stores snapshots of DNS records, TLS certificates, final redirect URLs, and SEO headers (noindex, robots.txt, canonicals), and accepts optional deploy markers from our GitHub Action or official WordPress plugin. When an incident opens, anything that changed in the previous hour is pinned right beside the incident timeline.
