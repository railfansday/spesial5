90 Day Fiancé Alum Sheila Mangubat Dead at 35

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days alum Sheila Mangubat has died at the age of 35, her former fiancé David Dangerfield confirmed: “I am devastated and still trying to process this loss.”

90 Day Fiancé fans are mourning a heartbreaking loss.

Sheila Mangubat—who appeared on 90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days in 2023—died in the Philippines on Nov. 21, her former fiancé David Dangerfield confirmed. She died just days before her 36th birthday, according to TMZ.

“It breaks my heart to share the news from her family that Sheila passed away on Saturday in the Philippines,” David wrote on Instagram Nov. 21 alongside a black-and-white photo of Sheila. “I am devastated and still trying to process this loss.”

“Please keep her son, her family, and me in your prayers,” the 45-year-old added. “Thank you for all your love and support.”

Sheila’s cause of death has not yet been confirmed.

As the reality star’s loved ones begin to grieve, her brother Jonathan Mangubat shared footage from Sheila’s funeral to his Facebook Stories Nov. 22, per TMZ, while her sister Grace Mangubat paid tribute to her late sibling on her own social media.

“Rest in peace my beloved sister,” she wrote on Facebook Nov. 21. “Sorry kaau layo kaau ko, dili ko kauli DHA.”

“Fly high my Sister,” she added. “We will miss you.”

Sheila and David’s romance was documented on season six of TLC’s 90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days in 2023. After meeting in a Facebook group for deaf singles in 2021, the pair sparked a long-distance romance, before becoming engaged. The former couple’s time on the show concluded with Sheila accepting David’s proposal.

In October 2025, Sheila and David broke up due to distance and K-1 visa issues, but rekindled their relationship in June, according to TMZ.

But David, who was born deaf, expressed that his love of Sheila—mom to son Jhonreil from a previous relationship—is eternal.

“I’m happy I never gave up because I found my love,” he shared on a June 2023 episode of Before the 90 Days. “I have a girlfriend whose name is Sheila, and she’s beautiful. She is 31 and lives in Cebu, Philippines. When I first saw Sheila’s picture, it got my heart beating.”

At the time, David saw forever in the TLC star.

“I will have a new family with Sheila in the future,” he vowed. “Sheila is better than any other woman I’ve dated because Sheila is a sweet person. She is the perfect match to me. I’ll love Sheila forever.”
