NZ skipper Latham laughs off Pant dominating local cricket under false name

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Footage emerged this week of India's Rishabh Pant scoring 54 off 21 balls for local Christchurch cricket club Open House Swingers under false name 'Stan Stan'; Pant played one day before second New Zealand vs India Test begins; Black Caps captain Tom Latham laughs it off as 'brilliant'

New Zealand captain Tom Latham has laughed off India's Rishabh Pant dominating a local Christchurch cricket match under a false name, a day before their second Test.

On Monday, Daryl Mitchell and Glenn Phillips held firm for 43 overs to earn the Black Caps a hard-fought draw in Wellington after India posted a record score of 720-9d.

Pant scored 128 in that Test, with the second Test between the sides beginning on Thursday at Hagley Oval.

Yet, footage has since emerged of Pant playing for club side Open House Swingers against The Chosen Ones on Wednesday, scoring 54 off 21 balls under the name 'Stan Stan' to seal a 16-run victory.

In an interview with The New Zealand Herald, Open House player Mohammad Safi said he had seen Pant on an electric bike in the city and asked if he would play, due to his club team being a player short.

"Did he play?! How'd he go?" Latham said at his New Zealand press conference. "That's brilliant!

"It's great. He's obviously a real character of the game, is Rishabh.

"It's great for him to be giving his experience back to LMS (Last Man Standing) teams!"

Pant has scored 3,853 runs in his 53 Tests for India, averaging just below of 45.

The 29-year-old also boasts a strike-rate of 74.43, which is bettered only by Virender Sehwag and Kapil Dev among players to have scored more than 2,000 Test runs for India.
