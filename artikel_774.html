Suki Waterhouse Teases Holiday Plans With Robert Pattinson & Daughter

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Suki Waterhouse shared insight into how she and fiancé Robert Pattinson are celebrating the holidays with their daughter during an exclusive E! News interview at the 2026 CFDA Awards.

Suki Waterhouse is ready to sleigh this winter.

After all, the "Good Looking" singer revealed how she and fiancé Robert Pattinson plan to celebrate the holiday season with their 2-year-old daughter.

"The tradition I'm most looking forward to is just dressing the tree," Waterhouse exclusively told E! News at the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards Presented by Amazon Fashion Nov. 2. "I'm really big into mulled wine. I'm excited to go back to London and start drinking a lot of mulled wine."

In fact, you can even call her a model, actress and winemaker.

"I will make my own mulled wine," she beamed. "I like it that much. It's dangerous how much I like mulled wine! I will be gifting my mulled wine."

Waterhouse and the Twilight star—who got engaged in December 2023 after five years of dating—are especially excited to celebrate the holiday season with their little spider monkey.

As she put it, "I go big at Christmas. I'm excited."

Though the couple is looking forward to breaking out the eggnog and blasting Christmas tunes, they're still learning how to navigate life as working parents.

"You're always finding the balance," the Daisy Jones & The Six alum admitted. "It's like you become a juggler, but we're all juggling. It feels good. I'm just having a great time with her."

And being a mom has made it so easy for the 34-year-old to love, to love, to love the most classic elements of her style.

"My staples are important, like great leather jackets," she explained. "You know me, I love my coats. I'm big into my coats, my boots, belts—just having the structure of an outfit there, and the day is good."

As for where she finds the inspo for her closet? Waterhouse traveled all the corners where destiny called her and it was in the '60s and '70s.

"My biggest fashion inspiration is probably people like Anita Pallenberg, Patti Smith, Pattie Boyd, Marianne Faithfull," she explained. "How it's translated to my own—I love boyish clothes, anything that, vintage sourcing, stuff like that."
