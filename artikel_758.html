Raya injury 'not too serious' says relieved Arteta

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

David Raya came off injured during Arsenal's Premier League draw with Newcastle; the Spaniard is the Gunners' undisputed No 1 goalkeeper with Kepa Arrizabalaga and Illan Meslier as back-up options; the 31-year-old got injured at a time where Arsenal have some big games coming up

Mikel Arteta has revealed that David Raya's shoulder injury is not as bad as first feared, but is unsure when the Arsenal goalkeeper will return to action.

Raya came off with a shoulder problem in Saturday's 2-2 draw against Newcastle and there were initial fears that the Spaniard could miss several months if he required surgery.

However, an MRI scan over the weekend has revealed that Raya does not require surgery and while he will miss Tuesday's match with Borussia Dortmund, more scans are needed on Tuesday to understand the full extent of the damage.

"He's not fit for [Dortmund] unfortunately, but it doesn't look too serious," said Arteta.

"We need some more tests tomorrow but it's quite encouraging with the pain he was showing. Immediately after the action, we were quite worried but it's not as bad as we expected.

"We expected something much worse because he could almost not handle the amount of pain he had. The first tests we have done showed something much more encouraging. [On Tuesday] we need to ratify that to give you more specifics.

"There was no need for surgery. That was the main fear, because that would have ruled him out for months. Let's see."

Asked if he could return for Arsenal's big Premier League home game with Manchester City on Sunday, live on Sky Sports, Arteta replied: "Tomorrow we will have more tests. Then you will know."

Raya's injury means Kepa Arrizabalaga and Illan Meslier will compete for the goalkeeper spot at Arsenal until the Spain international returns from his injury.

Kepa was widely regarded as Arsenal's No 2 goalkeeper and replaced Raya at Newcastle off the bench.

However, Meslier started against Fleetwood Town in the Carabao Cup fourth round defeat - which was the last time Raya was rotated out the Arsenal starting line-up.

"I think it's clear for us who is going to play tomorrow," said Arteta about who starts out of Kepa and Meslier.

When asked why Meslier started against Fleetwood, Arteta replied: "Because I want everybody to be part of it and to feel part of it. You need to play, and Illan has been really good with us.

"We believe that it was the right thing to share those minutes, the competition. To give a player that every single day is so consistent, so reliable, so supportive, with the rest of the boys, to have the feeling that we trust him as well."

"Raya's injury is a leveller for the other teams - not that much of a leveller because Arsenal still have some good players. When Chelsea miss a key player like that, that's it for them. But I don't think that's the case for Arsenal, they've got a very good squad.

"But that goalkeeper saves you points. That will be the problem for Arsenal.

"The next five or six games for Arsenal are decisive for them. If they come out of this run of games and they are five or six points clear of the pack, it's impossible for anybody to catch them. Every one of their next five or six games is a difficult football match.

"For me, Raya will be a miss. You'd be more comfortable going into those games with him in goal.

"He's the best goalkeeper in the world, by far, at making angles. He reads the game by always making the right angles.

"A lot of people don't see it. When the ball goes back to the goalkeeper, he'll always be on the right angle to get the ball - and then will always play it into the right player's foot.

"If Declan Rice comes short, he'll play it onto his right foot so the ball can go smoothly out to the right-back. All these little things help.

"And now it looks like Kepa is going to come in - and he's not played for a long time. And all these things have to work for him. That could be a worry for Arsenal."
