2027 Grammys: Madonna Lands Five Nods, Misses Out on Major Categories

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The pop superstar makes a long-awaited return to music's biggest night by nabbing four nods for 'Confessions II,' her 16-song follow up to the 2005 chart-topping original, ‘Confessions.’

Madonna is nominated for the 2027 Grammys in the dance/electronic categories for Confessions II, but her latest album didn’t make it to the biggest categories.

The Grammy nods, the first for the pop superstar in 16 years, follow the release earlier this year of the 16-song Confessions II as a follow-up to the 2005 chart-topping original, Confessions on a Dance Floor. Madonna, who has seven Grammys under her belt, netted from Recording Academy voters nominations for best dance/electronic album for Confessions II, best dance/electronic recording for “I Feel So Free,” and best dance pop recording for “Danceteria.” Related Stories Music Ariana Grande Lands Four Grammy Nominations, Including Song of the Year Nod, After "Taking a Step Back From Visibility" Music Harry Styles Shut Out of the 2027 Grammy Nominations

Madonna also scored a Grammy nomination for best remixed recording for “Love Sensation,” a collaboration on the Confessions II album with Kylie Minogue, and a best pop duo/group performance nomination along with Sabrina Carpenter for “Bring Your Love.” But despite buzz going into the Grammy nominations reveal on Monday morning, Confessions II was shut out of the all-important record of the year, song of the year and album of the year categories. The five nominations do, however, end a drought for Madonna as her last Grammy award came in 2010 with “Celebration” winning for best dance recording. Her 28 Grammy nominations and seven trophies over the course of her career have come mostly in the dance song and electronic album categories, where Madonna had her biggest haul on Monday.

Confessions on a Dance Floor won best dance/electronic album at the Grammys in 2007. The 2027 Grammys will will take place Feb. 7 at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, with this year’s ceremony airing live at 7 p.m. ET / 4 p.m. PT on the Disney-owned ABC and streaming on Disney+ and Hulu.
