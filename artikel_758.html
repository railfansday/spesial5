Duran scores late Celtic winner to keep Europa League hopes alive

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Torreense vs Celtic. UEFA Europa League Round 1.

Campo Manuel MarquesAttendance: Attendance4,971.

Report from the Europa League clash between Torreense and Celtic at Campo Manuel Marques; Javi Vazquez' own-goal put Celtic in front; Dany Jean levelled before the break; substitute Camilo Duran headed in a late winner for the Hoops; Martin O'Neill's side are up to 25th

Football Journalist @AlisonGConroy

Camilo Duran came off the bench to keep Celtic's Europa League hopes alive with a vital win against Torreense.

The Colombian striker peeled away to the back post to meet a cross from fellow substitute James Forrest, guiding a downward header into the net in the 85th minute.

Duran's 15th goal of the season secured a 2-1 victory over the Portuguese second-tier side and moved Celtic on to six points in the Europa League. His latest decisive contribution follows a match-winning double off the bench in the Hoops' comeback victory over Besiktas last time out.

Celtic took the lead in the ninth minute when Javi Vazquez turned Jota's cross into his own net, with Hyun-jun Yang lurking behind him.

However, the Portuguese Cup winners hit back 10 minutes later after some hesitant defending from the visitors. Alex Alfaro rattled the crossbar with a 22-yard effort, but neither Kieran Tierney nor Colby Donovan could deal with the resulting loose balls, allowing Haiti international Dany Jean to volley home the equaliser.

Celtic dominated possession after the break but struggled to create clear-cut opportunities until Duran's late intervention secured all three points.

The Hoops' attention now turns to Hearts on Sunday, live on Sky Sports, as they look to remain top of the Scottish Premiership.

Their next Europa League match is away to Omonia Nicosia on December 10.

Jota looked back to his best despite not scoring or providing an assist.

In his first European appearance for Celtic since 2025, the winger was a pest for the Portuguese side until he was replaced by James Forrest.

He created chances with some brilliant crossing with one turned in by Vazquez for the opening goal.

He has been sensational since arriving in the summer and he saved Celtic again.

But it was far from a perfect performance in Portugal.

Once again, slack defending and individual errors proved costly, allowing Torreense to remain well in the game.

Record signing Kasper Hogh started, but once again did not score. He is lacking in confidence, but how long do you stick with a striker who is not putting the ball in the net?

Celtic manager Martin O'Neill on TNT Sports:

"I'm just delighted to have won, it keeps us going if nothing else. We've got six points on the board and we've still a couple of games to go.

"From an almost impossible position, having lost the first three games, we've given ourselves some hope.

"Three points tonight were of vital importance. There's lots we can still do to improve our game."

Asked about Camilo Duran, he added: "He's just a magnificent footballer.

"15 goals and we're not into December, he's been flying. He's a terrific player, and that's two games he's come up big for us."

Celtic winger Jota speaking to TNT Sports after his first European appearance for almost two years:

"It was a long road and I did my maximum to be as fit as possible.

"Right now, it's all about enjoying life. No expectations, just be professional and having the responsibility of being a Celtic player.

"It wasn't easy. I think we just had to stick with our plan and in the end it worked out, that's the most important thing.

"We must believe [Celtic can qualify]. Every game is a different battle, we know what to do."
