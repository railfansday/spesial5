Brit List 2026: Cornwall Outlaw Series, Coming-of-Age Film Top List of Unproduced Film, TV Scripts

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Read up on the feature script 'Dig for Victory' by Gemma Addy; 'Petroc,' a series from Archie Thomson ('California Schemin''); and 11 other hot Brit scripts on the list, whose new headline sponsor is Disney+.

The Brit List, the platform for emerging British screenwriting talent, has unveiled its 2026 lineup of the best unproduced film and TV scripts from emerging creatives — 13 scripts from 13 writers.

Two projects received the highest number of recommendations: Dig for Victory by Gemma Addy, who has worked on the TV drama series Marie Antoinette, and Petroc by Archie Thomson, one of the writers on California Schemin’.

Established in 2007, The Brit List‘s mission is to “showcase outstanding British voices,” with its annual list compiled from recommendations by British production companies, talent agencies, sales companies, financiers, distributors, and broadcasters. As such, it has been compared to Hollywood’s The Black List. “For 19 years, The Brit List has showcased scripts that have achieved 140 nominations for major awards, including six Oscar and 16 BAFTA wins, and have earned over $2 billion at the international box office,” The Brit List team highlighted. “Nearly one in four of the feature scripts that have appeared on The Brit List have gone on to be produced. These include movies such as The King’s Speech, Paddington and The Favourite.”

And the team emphasized: “Our alumni feature many writers who have since had stellar careers: Moira Buffini, Aschlin Ditta, Emerald Fennell, James Graham, George Kay, Kelly Marcel, Abi Morgan, Ava Pickett, Jack Thorne and Peter Straughan.”

Since 2018, The Brit List has included TV scripts from writers who have gone on to create their own shows, including Industry creators Mickey Down and Konrad Kay, Curfew‘s Lydia Yeoman, and Gangs of London‘s Lauren Sequeira. “In 2025, after being featured on The Brit List, Dylan Brady’s script Discretion went on to be snapped up by A24, with Nicole Kidman attached to star,” the team also noted. This year’s list features “another strong showing from feature scripts, reaching near parity with TV pilots for the first time since the separate film and TV lists were integrated in 2018.”The Brit List offers writers not only a boost for their projects but also offers them a spot in a six-month development program of weekly seminars with top industry professionals — agents, producers, writers and showrunners — alongside specialists in personal finance, mental health, pitching technique and personal branding.Alexandra Arlango, founder and director of The Brit List, also unveiled a collaboration with Disney+ as the list’s new headline sponsor. “In 2026, The Brit List and Disney+ will go further than ever before to support the great British writing talent of the future,” she said. “We look forward to introducing this year’s writers to the team at Disney+ and to our returning partners, Audible and the Film Distributors Association.”

Angela Jain, head of content, Disney+ EMEA (Europe, Middle East and Africa), added: “We’re excited to see the names on this year’s list, as we’re committed to investing in British screenwriters and empowering the future storytellers in the industry. It’s vital to drive the local creative economy.”This year, 10 or more recommendations were needed for inclusion. So, without further ado, here is a look at the scripts on The Brit List 2026.
