West Brom battle to win at Bolton

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Bolton Wanderers vs West Bromwich Albion. EFL Championship.

Report and free match highlights from the Sky Bet Championship match between Bolton Wanderers and West Bromwich Albion at the Toughsheet Community Stadium on Wednesday night; Aune Heggebo nets twice in Baggies win.

Norwegian duo Aune Heggebo and Felix Horn Myhre scored the goals as West Brom boosted their promotion push with a 3-1 Sky Bet Championship win at Bolton.

Hot-shot Heggebo gave James Morrison's in-form visitors a 21st-minute lead which was cancelled out by substitute Ruben Rodrigues' 63rd-minute penalty.

But West Brom, without injured top scorer Isaac Price, finished strongly, with Myhre's fifth goal since his summer move from Brann restoring Albion's lead after 75 minutes.

And Heggebo, back in the starting line-up after last Saturday's draw with Swansea, sealed a fifth win in eight unbeaten games with a penalty seven minutes from time.

It all added up to only Albion's second success at the Toughsheet Community Stadium since 2000.

The Baggies quickly imposed themselves on their hosts, fashioning a brace of early shooting opportunities.

Goalkeeper Gavin Bazunu pushed away a third-minute Harry Whitwell effort and then kept out a low drive from Kareem Tunde.

Bolton have conceded more goals from crosses than any other Championship team this season, so it was little surprise to see Morrison's men exploit this weakness to take the lead.

Heggebo was involved in the build-up and then raced into the six-yard box to slide in Callum Styles' teasing centre from Bolton's right flank.

A slick training ground corner routine almost paid dividends for the Baggies after 37 minutes but the set-up proved better than Tunde's rising drive into the North Stand.

For all their domination, West Brom were almost caught out three minutes from half-time.

Former Baggies loanee Sam Iling-Junior ended a strong run with an inviting cross but Theo Bair - deputising for top scorer Sam Dalby - headed over. The Canadian striker then flicked another chance over the crossbar as Bolton finished the half strongly.

That was Bair's last action as he did not reappear for the second half, replaced by Dalby. Bolton also brought on defender Chris Forino and shortly afterwards swapped Xavier Simons for Rodrigues in a bid to get back into the contest.

Those changes did make a difference as Bolton levelled from the spot. George Campbell was adjudged to have handled Rodrigues' shot and the Portuguese midfielder drilled in his third goal of the season.

Iling-Junior had the ball in the net again eight minutes later but his celebrations were cut short by an assistant referee's flag.

The same player was then denied by Max O'Leary's fingertips after a stunning curling attempt with the outside of his boot.

Having survived the scare, West Brom imposed themselves once again, with Myhre and Heggebo taking them within three points of the top two.

"The lads did everything we asked of them at half-time by being braver and playing with more energy.

"But we wasted 40 minutes of the first half because we were not aggressive or tight enough.

"We spoke about how well West Brom pass the ball and they have good movement.

"So we couldn't afford to give them too much time and space, which they had in the first half.

"I felt it necessary to change at half-time and go more aggressive and change system.

"We were good for half-an-hour of the second half and were probably on top and then they got a second goal which was a blow.

"The third goal was another error, which was needless."

"I thought he (Heggebo) would be perfect for this game if we could get behind their high back line.

"We knew he would be in the space behind to arrive and be better suited in the box.

"Any team would miss big players and what they bring to the team. But it's not to cry about, it is about trying to find the best way and trust in the players who come in."
