Facebook shelves additional profile option

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

Facebook added the ability to create multiple personas in 2023, but is sunsetting the feature and deleting extra profiles by mid-December.

Meta is removing the option to create additional Facebook profiles that can be managed under a single user presence.

Which, it’s important to note, is different from creating a profile page and a business page. Users can still run business pages in addition to their individual presence. The change only relates to the alternate profile option Meta launched in 2023.

As explained by Meta: “Additional Facebook profiles are being permanently deleted after December 14, 2026. This does not affect your main Facebook profile, which is the first profile you create when you sign up for Facebook.”

As noted, in September 2023, Facebook added a multi-profile option, which enabled users to create additional Facebook profiles, in order to explore different interests or share different content with different audiences.

As Meta explained at launch: “Whether you’re new to Facebook or a longtime user, you may want to keep your personal and professional relationships separate, or you may want to keep one profile tied to a community you’re a part of and another profile just for friends. Creating multiple personal profiles lets you easily organize who you share with and what content you see for the various parts of your life. Think one profile for the foodie scene you love and another one to keep up with your friends and family.”

Though even when it was added, it did seem like a confusing and potentially harmful proposal.

As SMT reported at the time: “[Users] will have to switch over to another profile for each, which I just don’t see becoming a habitual behavior for most users, and really, it seems more likely to invite negative behaviors, like trolling, via a separate identity.”

Indeed, creating a new profile, and regularly switching between profiles, seemed more likely to add friction, while it could also be used as another means to target other people or groups via an alternate persona.

Either way, it seems that Facebook users weren’t overly interested, which is why the company is now shelving the option.

Meta said users can export their additional profile information, and those who joined groups under an alternate persona will need to rejoin under their main profile.

“If your additional profile manages a group, reassign group administration to a main profile,” Meta said. “You may also be able to add your additional profile’s connections to your main profile by adding friends, rejoining groups, or following Pages.”

Meta also said that if a user’s additional profile is an admin of a Facebook group, they’ll need to assign another profile as admin before December 14. “If no new admin is assigned, the group will be paused until an admin is added,” Meta said.
