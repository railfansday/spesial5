Sociable: Instagram adds Amazon affiliate links

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The partnership will enable creators to add up to 30 shoppable products per Reel or 20 per feed post.

“Sociable” is the latest commentary on important social media developments and trends from industry expert Andrew Hutchinson of Social Media Today.

Instagram has added another way for creators to make money, by partnering with Amazon to enable affiliate links for Amazon-listed products in Reels and feed posts.

As shown in these example images, creators can select from a range of products on Amazon to add to their posts. Clickable links to the relevant product listings on Amazon will then be added in the bottom left of a post or Reel display, allowing viewers to easily make a purchase.

All affiliate links will be connected to the creator’s account, and creators will earn a commission for any sales generated from those referrals.

Eligible creators can link their Instagram and Amazon Influencer accounts in their Professional Dashboard on IG in order to facilitate product linking.

As per an Instagram press release sent to SMT: “Once connected, Instagram automatically includes their affiliate links when they add Amazon products to content, with product links taking viewers directly to the Amazon Shopping App.”

Instagram also said creators will be able to add up to 30 products per Reel, or 20 per feed post. This should provide a heap of linking potential for affiliate opportunities.

Creators can also see the commission rate for each item, so they’ll know how much they’ll get from any resulting link clicks.

This is another way for Instagram to provide monetization pathways for its creators, while also giving Amazon another means to promote its products via creator endorsement.

Amazon has been steadily expanding its product display opportunities, with the shopping giant also inking similar affiliate partnerships with YouTube and Pinterest this year.

Though, interestingly, Amazon asked to be removed from the list of platforms that will work with Meta’s Muse agent Amazon said that was because the Muse system was in violation of Amazon’s automated shopping tool restrictions.

A partnership with Instagram, then, seems like an interesting development, which could also lead to Amazon eventually working with Meta’s other platforms more openly.
