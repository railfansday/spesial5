Florida man arrested for sending card full of peanut butter to highly allergic ex-friend

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A 68-year-old man from Florida has been arrested over a year after allegedly sending a card full of peanut butter to a former friend, who happens to be highly allergic to it.

There are plenty of people out there who, after falling out with friends, family, and partners, want to get their own back in some way. Plenty go on social media and make posts about the situation, while others are happy to throw clothes and other items away.

In the case of Joseph Gerard Cancellare Jr., the 68-year-old was arrested on November 12, more than a year after he allegedly sent a card full of peanut butter to a former friend.

As per a probable cause affidavit from the Plantation Police Department, the woman, who is highly allergic to peanut butter, received an envelope back in August 2025 and noticed that the card inside had been covered in peanut butter.

According to reports, the woman – who hasn’t been named – admitted to being a former friend of the 68-year-old and had spent time socially with him over the last two decades.

Additionally, some reports state that Cancellare Jr. blamed the woman for being the reason why his ex-wife decided to divorce him, hence wanting to get some payback.

He was taken into custody by the Plantation Police Department and now faces charges of aggravated battery with a deadly weapon and culpable negligence exposing another person to harm.

The woman told police that the 68-year-old was “well aware” of the allergy, and that even breathing in air around peanuts is “life-threatening” to her.

She developed hives on both arms and a tingling sensation after coming into contact with the peanut butter. This happened after the woman had washed her hands too.

In Florida, the charge of aggravated battery with a deadly weapon comes with a minimum of 21 months in prison. The charge of culpable negligence exposing another person to harm is less severe, however, with a fine of up to $500 and potentially 60 days in jail.

However, it remains to be seen if Cancellare Jr. will be jailed as a result of the incident.
