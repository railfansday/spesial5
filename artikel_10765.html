Dana White’s Power Slap threatens legal action against Power Spank parody competition

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Dana White’s Power Slap has threatened legal action against a parody competition called Power Spank, which sees women slap each other on the backside.

Launched in January 2023, Power Slap is a combat sports promotion founded and co-owned by UFC boss Dana White, in which competitors take turns delivering unprotected slaps to each other’s faces.

In October, the Lingerie Fighting Championships (LFC) debuted Power Spank in Clearwater, Florida, as a parody of White’s competition. The inaugural event featured eight women, including MMA fighters and exotic dancers, competing for a $500 grand prize, which was won by a contestant known as Bebe.

According to USA Today, attorneys representing Power Slap sent LFC a cease-and-desist letter in late October, demanding that the organization stop using the Power Spank name.

However, LFC CEO Shaun Donnelly has refused to comply, arguing that the competition constitutes parody protected under the First Amendment. “We respect Power Slap and its founders, but we will not be bullied into abandoning protected speech,” he said in an official statement.

“We will defend our right, and the right of every American, to free speech, and if that takes us to the Supreme Court, we are prepared to go,” he continued.

Donnelly also explained that Power Spank was conceived after an LFC athlete was reportedly hospitalized following an appearance in Power Slap. “Power Spank is our answer: same energy, same theatrics, none of the brain damage. And let’s be honest, a spank is a lot more fun to watch than a slap.”

LFC has confirmed it intends to continue running Power Spank at its US events while reviewing its legal options. At the time of writing, no formal lawsuit had been filed, and White had not publicly commented on the dispute.
