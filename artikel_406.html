Ali Wong and Nikki Glasers Hot Ones Versus is hilarious chaos

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Hot Ones Versus episodes tend to be more chaotic than regular Hot Ones, and Ali Wong going head-to-head with Nikki Glaser is of course no exception.

In the clip above the two comedians take part in what is essentially a game of truth or dare, where the punishment for refusing to answer a question is a spicy wing.

Highlights include a passionate Taylor Swift discussion (Glaser refused to roast her) and a final challenge in which the pair have to draw one another without laughing.
