Best Buy is selling a $100 gift card for $60 on Black Friday — how to claim free credit

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

TL;DR: On Black Friday (Nov. 27), My Best Buy members — including free-tier members — can buy a $100 Best Buy gift card for $60 in stores while supplies last (limit one per customer).

If you are planning to brave the brick-and-mortar crowds for Black Friday, Best Buy has one of the easiest doorbusters to justify waking up early for.

On Friday, Nov. 27, the retailer is selling a $100 Best Buy gift card for $60 — essentially handing out $40 in free spending money toward your next tech upgrade or holiday gift.

That works out to a 40% discount on future Best Buy purchases, which can easily stack with existing Black Friday sale prices. Because quantities are limited and there are a few rules in place, here is everything you need to know before you head out on Friday morning.

The $60 gift card deal is valid exclusively on Black Friday, Nov. 27, at standard Best Buy retail locations while supplies last. You cannot grab it online, in the Best Buy app, or at Best Buy Outlet and Pacific Sales stores. The offer is open to My Best Buy, My Best Buy Plus, and My Best Buy Total members. If you don't have an account yet, you can sign up for the free base My Best Buy tier online or in the app before heading to the register.

The promotion is strictly limited to one gift card per customer, and Best Buy is not offering rainchecks once a store's supply sells out. Best Buy stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day before reopening early on Friday morning (most locations open at 6 a.m. local time, though mall stores and local ordinances can vary), and individual stores may hand out tickets outside to manage lines for high-demand doorbusters.

You have to use the $100 gift card on a separate transaction, which just means buying the card first and immediately putting that $100 toward your next Black Friday pickup — like a new TV, laptop, or holiday gift.

Purchases made from Oct. 30 through Dec. 31, 2026 are covered by Best Buy's Holiday Price Match Guarantee and are eligible for extended returns through Jan. 15, 2027 (excluding cell phones, AppleCare+ plans, and holiday decor).

If you are mapping out a full-morning route, browse our rundown of Black Friday 2026 opening times before heading out (and confirm your local store's hours, just in case). You will also want to set up your free My Best Buy account ahead of time so checkout goes smoothly.
