Amazon’s Alan Moss on How Black Friday Sports Lineup, Shoppable Ads Are Winning Advertisers

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Thanksgiving week sees the return of Amazon Prime Video’s biggest sports day of the year on Black Friday, a day important not only to sports enthusiasts but also to advertisers.

Viewers will see a 15-hour marathon of top-notch sports programming, including the The Farmers Insurance Skins Game from the PGA Tour at 9 a.m. ET, the Black Friday Football game featuring the Denver Broncos and Pittsburgh Steelers at 3 p.m. ET, and two NBA games as part of the Emirates Cup: Miami Heat at New York Knicks at 7:00 p.m. ET and Los Angeles Lakers at San Antonio Spurs at 9:30 p.m.
