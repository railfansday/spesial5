Patriots Get Devastating News About QB Drake Maye’s Ankle Injury: Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The worst fears of the New England Patriots might have been realized after quarterback Drake Maye suffered an ankle injury while playing the Detroit Lions on Sunday, November 15.

Maye, 24, will miss “an extended period, and possibly the remainder of the season,” ESPN reported on Monday, November 16.

The quarterback left the game, which took place in Munich, Germany, during the first quarter after he was sacked by Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson. Hutchinson, 26, also suffered a knee injury on the play after celebrating the sack. There is fear Hutchinson tore his ACL and will miss the remainder of the season.

Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel explained that Maye did not immediately head home with the team.

“The update that I have is just out of caution, Drake stayed back,” Vrabel, 51, said Monday on WEEI’s The Greg Hill Show. “He’ll be traveling back here shortly, but [we] just wanted to make sure that everything was good for him to travel back and safely.”

Vrabel added, “Just wanted to make sure everything was in line for him to travel — and it seems like he is. He’ll be returning here, I think, shortly.”

Immediately after the game, Vrabel told reporters that Maye would “get more tests run” when he returned stateside.

“He couldn’t function, couldn’t play, couldn’t do whatever he needed to do to play quarterback, to protect himself, to execute or function at that level,” Vrabel explained.

Vrabel downplayed any malicious intent on Hutchinson’s behalf, telling reporters, “I don’t think it was anything intentional.”

“I’m sure that it wasn’t,” Vrabel added. “That’s part of the game.”

Backup quarterback Tommy DeVito stepped in and played the rest of the game for the Patriots. The Lions dominated New England in Maye’s absence, winning 35-10.

DeVito finished 12-for-24 passing for 81 yards, with one touchdown and one interception. The team has not announced if DeVito will remain the starting quarterback when they return to action against the Los Angeles Chargers on November 29.

“I’m going to continue the process I have been doing up to this point,” DeVito said after Sunday’s game. “Something that as a [backup], as cliche as it sounds, you have to be prepared to be a starter. So just keep doing what I’m doing, keep my head down, and then if something like that were to happen, you would have the reps and practice and the walkthroughs and everything going forward, so just keep on preparing and on to the next.”
