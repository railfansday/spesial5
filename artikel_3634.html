GTA 6 players are already pulling off high-flying tricks and other impressive stunts, just days after launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

One of the best things to emerge from Grand Theft Auto over the years has been the community's truly spectacular stunts: players soaring under bridges, leaping across buildings, and more. And with GTA 6's release this week, we've seen the first signs of the trend continuing in a brand-new world.

This TikTok video by user Gstitz from early in GTA 6, for instance, claims to be the "world's first" demonstration of a Mallard flying sideways between a narrow gap in a large building. It takes several attempts, but they eventually pull off the stunt without crashing or broken parts.

Another video from Hazardous is a more grounded (literally) look at Grand Theft Auto 6, and shows the YouTuber flinging motorbikes and cars around the map as they explore promising high-risk jumps. Some of these locations are tied to in-game challenges, so there are certainly breadcrumbs placed by Rockstar to encourage such ventures.

Granted, these are far from the truly absurd stunts we've seen in prior Grand Theft Auto games. But as a firing gun for the wider community, it's a great start. They're also among the most impressive things we've seen in GTA 6's opening week.

It's doubtless been an eventful few days though. Players have been entranced by the slapstick violence and in-game social media. Even UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham has praised the game, despite not having time to actually play it. Eurogamer editor-in-chief Chris Tapsell also came away impressed, lauding it in his five star GTA 6 review.
