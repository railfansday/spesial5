Candace Cameron Bure Explains How She Was ‘Duped’ Before Joining ‘The View’

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Candace Cameron Bure is getting real about why she signed on to cohost The View, despite her admitted lack of political knowledge.

“I was kind of duped. Raven will say the same thing,” Cameron Bure, 50, claimed during the Tuesday, November 24, episode of the “McBride Rewind” podcast, referring to former cohost Raven-Symoné.

The Full House alum alleged, “We were both told that the show was going to be pop culture and evergreen topics and not politics.”

While Cameron Bure said she had seen the talk show “in the past,” when she was approached to cohost The View it had just gotten a new producer and the team allegedly told her they were shifting the focus. (Cameron Bure was on the hosting panel for two seasons from 2015 to 2016.)

“I’d guest cohosted a couple of times. I didn’t realize I was actually auditioning for the seat,” Cameron Bure recalled of the process. “I came in a bit naive.”

After guest cohosting the ABC series, Cameron Bure said she seemingly nailed the audition but she wasn’t sure she could handle being a full-time member of the panel.

“I was like, ‘I’m not comfortable talking about politics — I actually don’t even know if I know enough, but to actually verbalize all of the opinions,’” she recalled. “They go, ‘Actually we’re steering away from politics. We’re going a new direction in the show and it’s going to be much more about social issues, pop culture.’”

Cameron Bure remembered the producers saying, “We might talk about politics here and there, but that’s not the focus of the show,” which is why she signed on.

The Special Forces: World’s Toughest Test season 5 winner claimed that what was presented to her before she joined soon shifted when she was in front of the camera.

“And then, it was like, it just turned into politics, and Donald Trump entered the [presidential] race, and, you know, all hell broke loose,” Cameron Bure said.

Outside of politics, Cameron Bure recalled talking with the powers at be about stepping into the shoes of Elisabeth Hasselbeck as the token conservative on the show.

“I knew I was going to be the conservative on the panel no matter what. Even with social topics, you can be morally conservative or not, but I know me,” Cameron Bure shared. “I’m me all the time. I’ve never changed who I am over the years. I’ve been very consistent my whole life and in my Christian faith and my beliefs.”

The actress noted, “So, I’m always going to represent that point of view, no matter what topic we’re talking about. But, once it turned very political, I was like, ‘I didn’t sign up for this.’”

Cameron Bure ultimately exited The View in 2016 after two seasons. Earlier this month, she returned as a guest after her Special Forces win and appeared to fit right back in with the women.

“They still have great producers over there and, off camera, everyone was lovely,” Cameron Bure said on the podcast, reflecting on the good times she had. “I loved Joy [Behar] so much, and I think she’s always the name that people are like, ‘How’s Joy?’ Joy loved me and I loved her. There was respect behind the cameras, even though there was a lot of disagreeing on camera.”

Us has reached out to The View for comment.
