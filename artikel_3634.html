90 Day Fiance's Sheila Mangubat Dead at 35, Ex-Fiance David Breaks Silence

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

90 Day Fiancé alum Sheila Mangubat has died. She was 35.

“It breaks my heart to share the news from her family that Sheila passed away on Saturday in the Philippines,” Sheila’s former fiancé, David Dangerfield, wrote via Instagram, announcing his ex’s death. “I am devastated and still trying to process this loss.”

He continued, “Please keep her son, her family and me in your prayers. Thank you for all your love and support. ❤️🕊️.”

A cause of death has not been publicly revealed at the time of publication.

“Rest in peace, my beloved sister shiela mangubat😥,” her sister, Grace Mangubat, wrote via Facebook. “Sorry kaau layo kaau ko, dili ko kauli DHA😥😥😥 fly high my Sister we will miss you♥️.”

David and Sheila’s fellow 90 Day alums have also offered their condolences.

“Waoo this so sad 😞,” Anny Francsico wrote via Instagram comment. “She was so young, sorry David😭😭😭.”

Sheila met David, 45, in a Facebook group for deaf singles in 2021, soon embarking on a long-distance relationship. Their engagement was featured on season 6 of TLC’s 90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days in 2023.

“I’ve lived in Omaha on my own for a long time now. I want to start a family and have a wife,” David, who was born deaf, said in a June 2023 episode. “I was open to dating a hearing, deaf or hard of hearing person. I was putting myself out there consistently and getting a lot of ‘no’s.’ It hurt my feelings because I wanted to be in a relationship.”

Eventually, David connected with Sheila, who used a hearing aid to help her partial deafness.

“I’m happy I never gave up because I found my love,” he said at the time. “I have a girlfriend who’s name is Sheila, and she’s beautiful. She is 31 and lives in Cebu, Philippines. When I first saw Sheila’s picture, it got my heart beating.”

David had been eager to build a family with Sheila and her son, Jhonreil, from a previous relationship.

“I will have a new family with Sheila in the future,” David mused of Jhonreil, who was 12 at the time. “Sheila is better than any other woman I’ve dated because Sheila is a sweet person. She is the perfect match to me. I’ll love Sheila forever.”

The couple called off their engagement in October 2025 before reportedly reconciling earlier this year.
