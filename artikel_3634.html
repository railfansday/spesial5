How Long Is GTA 6? Depends On How You Play

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

As I prepare to pick up and play Grand Theft Auto VI tonight, I’m bracing myself to be in Leonida pretty much non-stop for days, weeks, maybe even months. It just intuitivelyÂ feels like it’s going to be a doozy of a long game, huh?

After all its predecessor, GTA V, takes 32 hoursÂ just to rush through the main story (according to HowLongToBeat), and over 50 if you factor in side content. I’d expect its successor, especially with all the bells and whistles Rockstar is showing off, to take even longer.

With reviews out today, we now have a rough idea of what to expect, and yeah: this game’s gonna take time.

Our own reviewer, Zack, says he saw the credits roll at about 90 hours of gameplay. Granted, he was doing a lot of side content and wandering around and testing stuff, and not everyone will want to be anywhere near as thorough as he was. Zack suggests a speedier player might make it through in around 60 hours. Jeff Gerstmann, in his review, says he beat the game after about 45 hours, maybe 50 if you count some saves he had to reload throughout. Zack doesn’t think you can reasonably finish the story in much less than that.

For those looking for more to do, though, Zack has spent 150 hours in GTA VI and still hasn’t seen everything there is to see or done everything there is to do. Like past Rockstar games, GTA VI will let you keep playing after the credits roll (though in a slightly unusual way), so you’re free to turn Leonida inside out for days on end if that’s what you want to do.

Sorry to all the other games in my backlog, we might be here a while.
