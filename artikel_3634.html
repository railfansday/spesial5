In the wake of the first crossover of Fire Country and Sheriff Country that iconically brought Bode and Boone together as friends, I expected more game-changing character pairings in the new crossover that aired on the 2026 TV schedule. What I didn’t expect was the romantic implications that came with one of them. I’m, of course, talking about the connection between Mickey and Manny, which honestly has me spiraling. However, thankfully, someone asked Morena Baccarin, who plays Mickey, about this potential budding relationship.

During the crossover event, it’s revealed that Manny and Mickey went on a really, really bad date a while back. It went poorly because, as Manny was celebrating Gabriela becoming an Olympian, Mickey was looking for rehab facilities for Skye. They worked through that conflict in this crossover, and by the end of it, Mickey bid on Manny in the charity auction. It felt like something was set up through all this, and TV Line spoke to Morena Baccarin about the storyline. Here’s what she had to say about it:

They started off at odds, not really getting each other, and then through this epic adventure they go on, they really learn a lot about each other. They become vulnerable, and you see the possibility of two people who could maybe have a go at it. It's just bad timing. It was bad timing then, and it's perhaps bad timing now, but you never know.

Well, I'm relieved to know I read into this situation correctly, and I seem to have the same feelings as the Mickey actress. Truly, the timing is horrible. First of all, there are Manny and Camille to consider. Secondly, there are multiple relationships in Mickey’s life (I’m looking at you, Boone and Travis) that feel like they could turn romantic at any moment. However, it’s hard to deny the chemistry Manny and Mickey had, and I would be curious to see them explore what they might have had if their date didn’t go wrong.

In terms of whether that will ever be addressed in a more in-depth manner, Baccarin has a “let’s just see where this goes” mentality, explaining:

I don't know, we'd have to see where the stories all go. Sometimes in life, it's nice to not wrap something up with a bow, but just to see what could have been. The possibility just kind of lays there between them.

Well, the possibility is certainly lying there, and right now, it feels like a full-on elephant in the room. Luckily, with Fire Country having a shorter season this year, there’s actually more opportunity for its cast to appear on Sheriff Country. That means it’s possible for us to see more of Manny and Mickey as we navigate these murky potential romance waters that have been put in front of us.

Potentially, the timing could be better down the line. As Baccarin said, there’s for sure a “possibility” between the two when it comes to romance; now is just not the right time. While I’m not sure if any time will be, the shows have certainly set this story up to continue down a path that isn’t 100% platonic.

So, to see how this story between Manny and Mickey develops, you can catch new episodes of Fire Country and Sheriff Country on Fridays starting at 8 p.m. ET on CBS.
