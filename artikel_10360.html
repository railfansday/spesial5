Several Xiaomi, Redmi, and Poco phones are getting the Android 17 HyperOS 4 update starting today.

HyperOS 4 follows in the footsteps of several other Android 17-based updates, giving the UI a Liquid Glass-inspired overhaul, more lockscreen customization (and giant clocks.
