Google will let Android & iOS apps directly integrate Quick Share next year

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

At Android Dev Summit 2026, Google announced a Quick Share SDK that will let Android and iOS apps directly integrate the peer-to-peer file sharing capability.

The Quick Share SDK is coming in the first half of 2027. Developers will be able to integrate file share functionality directly into their apps instead of requiring users to first open the system Share sheet and launch the fullscreen Quick Share experience.

Google will also offer a Quick Share iOS SDK for apps on the iPhone, as seen in the example below.

In terms of the end-user interface, activating Quick Share in third-party apps launches a bottom sheet “where the users will be able to see the files that are being sent as [a] preview.”
