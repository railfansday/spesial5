Everything Nothing sells is up to 49% off today; Phone (3) for $499, $17 Buds, more

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Nothing is selling a lot of its products at huge discounts today, with up to half off select products, bringing devices like Phone (3) down to $499, Buds (3a) to $69.99, and more.

All available through Amazon, Nothing has essentially every product it sells on sale today – and no, it’s not Black Friday yet! The “Early Black Friday Deal” is stuffed with major discounts, but there are some clear standouts here.

Good smartphone deals are becoming increasingly rare in RAMageddon. Most discounts we’re seeing just bring the price back down to what it was at launch, as so many products have gotten price hikes in recent months.

That’s not the case with Nothing Phone deals today.

2025’s flagship-ish Nothing Phone (3) drops to $499 for the 12/256GB model and $599 for 16/512GB. Slightly out of date, sure, but a great deal nonetheless on a phone that’s still got plenty of life left to give.

The newer Nothing Phone (4a) Pro is also on sale; $398 for the 8/128GB model and $483.50 for the 12/256GB.

If you need cheap earbuds, Nothing’s CMF Buds Neo don’t suck, and they’re already a good deal at $25. Right now, though, they’re only $16.99 on Amazon, and that’s just a steal for what you’re getting.

Meanwhile, CMF Buds 2 are perhaps a better bang for your buck at $24.99. That’s a 49% discount for better overall earbuds and more quality-of-life features.
