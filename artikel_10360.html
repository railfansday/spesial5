Elon Musk is giving away nearly $18 billion in SpaceX stock. Here’s who can get it

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Your kid is becoming quite the capitalist.

Every U.S. child with an active Trump account will soon receive two shares of SpaceX, the space exploration company run by Elon Musk. This donation marks the latest in a string of philanthropic initiatives focused on these new types of tax-advantaged investment accounts.

SpaceX announced Wednesday that the company will donate one share of its Class A common stock—currently trading for more than $130 per share—to every currently active Trump account. In turn, CEO Musk will match that donation, which he later confirmed on X.

“Every child in America will receive two SpaceX shares, one from the company and one from me personally,” Musk posted. 

In its official announcement, SpaceX touted its future-focused mission of building new infrastructure that could achieve multiplanetary living. “We believe ownership of the company creating these systems should extend to the next generation of Americans for their long-term benefit.”

Here’s what we know about the donations.

MILLIONS OF NEW SHAREHOLDERS

The portfolios of the country’s youngest generation of investors keep getting fatter. Launched earlier this year, Trump accounts allow deposits of up to $5,000 per year for each child until the age of 18 and various billionaires have even thrown some money into the mix.

Musk now joins the likes of Michael Dell and Ray Dalio, who have similarly pledged donations to Trump accounts. The U.S. Treasury Department, too, will make a one-time $1,000 deposit to every child born between Jan. 1, 2025, and Dec. 31, 2028.

The SpaceX shares work a little differently in that the company will transfer one share of its stock to active Trump accounts in December, according to Musk’s post. Nearly 70 million accounts have been created thus far, the White House reported last month, though that’s largely a result of a change by the Treasury to allow for automatic enrollment. 

Musk’s matching donation will come later, he said on X, sometime after mid-June 2027. That’s when a 366-day lockup expires that he agreed to as part of the SpaceX initial public offering earlier this year.

The Starbase, Texas-based company noted that Gwynne Shotwell, its president and chief operating officer, previously pledged with her husband to donate more than 2 million shares to Trump accounts. But there are strict eligibility requirements associated with those donations, which parents can check online.

Combined, the gifts from the company, Musk, and Shotwell could have a value of more than $18 billion given the stock’s current price and if all eligible Trump accounts have been claimed. These donations also do something that’s delving into new territory: They guarantee that potentially millions of Americans will become long-term shareholders of SpaceX. 

YOU GET A STOCK!

Just as Oprah famously announced a new car to every person in her studio audience back in the early 2000s, Musk and other billionaires are saying the same to kids now: “You get a stock!”

But that could create a conundrum of sorts for parents as more and more money is likely to flow into these accounts from billionaires. 

The Treasury recently implemented new rules that allow companies and other donors to gift individual stocks to these accounts, while also limiting trading activity in them, as The Wall Street Journal reported. Children and their parents can neither pick nor reject stocks that are donated to a child’s account—and they can’t sell them for five years.

And yet, there’s free money on the line. If parents don’t claim an account on behalf of their kid, their kid misses out on a potentially lucrative portfolio. Despite the move to automatic enrollment, some manual steps are still required, which means parents need to claim an account by downloading an app from the Trump accounts website. 

Musk couldn’t help but tout the prospects of what that could mean to millions of prospective shareholders—something that resonated with investors, too, as the stock has jumped nearly 5% in the past two days of trading.

“If SpaceX succeeds in making life multiplanetary and harnessing even a millionth of the Sun’s energy, it will be worth about a million times the current global economy,” Musk posted. “These are obviously absurdly difficult goals, but there is a path to achieving them. I recommend holding on to your shares.”
