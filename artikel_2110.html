‘A crucial match for John Eustace’: Derby County vs Watford predictions

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Derby County lost 3-0 away at Wolves last time out at Molineux and slumped to another defeat. DCFC have won only two of their first 17 league fixtures so far this season and have been seriously struggling for points.

The Rams are in the Championship drop zone along with Burnley and Preston North End. They are five points from safety behind their upcoming opponents and Lincoln City as they aim to claw themselves to survival under John Eustace.

Watford lost 1-0 against Blackburn Rovers at Vicarage Road on Wednesday evening. Iker Bravo missed a penalty for the Hornets in the first-half.

Jordan Zemura was then sent off for the Hertfordshire outfit in the second-half and they went down to 10 men. They have appointed ex-Romania boss Edward Iordanescu as their new manager following their decision to sack Alessio Dionisi.

“Derby County can’t afford to lose this game against a side they can catch in the table. If they do, John Eustace’s position at DCFC could become untenable.

“The Rams haven’t scored in three out of their last four games now which is a big worry. The goals have dried up and they are seriously lacking a cutting edge at the top end of the pitch.

“Watford are on a downward spiral towards the bottom three and their new manager Edward Iordanescu has a very tough job on his hands. The fans are disillusioned and their players are not performing.

“The Hornets have won only one out of their last six and have slipped down the division. I think this weekend is a battle of two teams equally struggling though and they are likely to just cancel each other out.”

“This feels like a crucial match for John Eustace’s Derby County future. At the time of writing, he has not been sacked and will take charge against Watford, but given the fact this is a home game against a fellow Championship struggler, the boss simply has to win.

“Back-to-back defeats against Wolves and Southampton are more than excusable, but DCFC have now won just twice in 17 games. Injuries have been awful, however, they are now the second-lowest scorers in the league and have the joint-worst defence – that says all you need to know.

“Watford haven’t fared much better with only five points more, but they do come into this under new management. Edward Iordanescu will take charge for the first time and he will be determined to have an immediate impact by breathing life into a struggling attack and stopping his side being so easy to create chances against.

“With a new boss behind them, the Hornets could definitely win this, but they’ve lost seven games on the trot away. It means that I think Derby County might just nick a crucial win, with Eustace saving his job for now.”
