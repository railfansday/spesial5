The dates and times of three more Newcastle United matches have now been confirmed on Thursday.

Today’s announcement means that we now have confirmation of when the next 13 Newcastle United matches will be played in the Premier League.

The Premier League official release saw only one of three NUFC games chosen by the broadcasters.

The trip to Arsenal on Saturday 23 January 2027 stays on that day but Sky Sports have moved the kick-off time to 5.30pm (Newcastle United officially announced this on Wednesday but then deleted all mention of it from their official media).

However, the Newcastle United matches at home to Fulham and Brighton haven’t changed from the scheduled dates and times.

Newcastle v Fulham is 3pm on Saturday 16 January and Newcastle v Brighton is 3pm on Saturday 30 January.
