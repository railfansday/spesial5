Monaco summit adds unnecessary drama to Michael Carrick situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Michael Carrick arrived at Carrington for Manchester United training as normal on Tuesday morning, while his bosses were preparing to decide his future in talks on the Mediterranean.

Chief executive Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox are flying to Monaco to meet Sir Jim Ratcliffe, with the BBC reporting that the pair will recommend Carrick is sacked.The Guardian has indicated representatives of the Glazer family will also be involved.

The club insists the Monaco summit was pre-planned and is not solely about the head coach and while that may be true, it hardly matters.By allowing the meeting to become public knowledge, United have turned a straightforward football decision into unnecessary drama, compounding an already difficult situation.

What does is say about Man United in 2026 that a manager is given three games to secure his job, then left to train the squad during an international break while the executives are flying off to rule on his future? It’s farcical.

The latest chapter is another muddled process.Wilcox and Berrada met at Carrington the day after the 3-0 home defeat to Bournemouth on 25 October and agreed to give Carrick three more matches.A defeat at Chelsea, a win over Roma and a 4-4 draw with Aston Villa later, they appear to have reached the conclusion many expected a fortnight ago.

If the timeline holds, the decision will be made at Wednesday’s meeting, with an official announcement expected later in the week.United reportedly want to tell Carrick face to face, which means he could spend another day or two working under a cloud. In reality, it could be two days or two weeks.

The stakes could hardly be higher. United have won once in 10 league games, their worst-ever start to a Premier League season, and sit one point off the bottom in 18th place.

Should Carrick go, attention will turn quickly to his successor.ESPN reports that United States head coach Mauricio Pochettino is the favoured candidate, while Eddie Howe has been named as Wilcox’s preferred choice. Antonio Conte has also been mentioned.

Carrick deserves better than this.Whatever the outcome, a club that wants to be taken seriously should not be settling its manager’s future in the full glare of the media while he is still out on the training pitch.
