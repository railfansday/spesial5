Three Major New Games Have Added Free Xbox Trials And Demos

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

We don't tend to highlight demos and trials on the Xbox Store all that often, but when you get multiple recent releases (some of them AAA) suddenly getting freebies, they definitely can't go unmentioned!

The three games we're talking about in particular here are Control Resonant, Crimson Desert Enhanced and Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival. The first two have free trials (and we actually mentioned the Crimson Desert one in a separate article back in October), while Hellraiser: Revival has a free new demo.

You can access free trials by going to the game's official Xbox Store page and then hitting the "Free Trial" button, which will typically download the entire game and give you access to just a couple of hours of it. Demos, meanwhile, are separate downloads that are hidden away in the "Demos" section of the Xbox Store.

Control Resonant is probably the one that everyone's going to focus on here, as it literally only came out in late September and already has a way to try it for free. That's amazing news for anyone who might have been on the fence.

Don't sleep on the others as well though, especially considering how the "Enhanced" version of Crimson Desert refers to the updated August 2026 version rather than the one that first released in March, while Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival has been getting "Very Positive" feedback on Steam and is only a few weeks old!
