PC Gaming Show 2026: Most Wanted set for December 3

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

PC Gamer will host PC Gaming Show 2026: Most Wanted on December 3 at 12:00 a.m. PT / 3:00 p.m. ET, the company announced. You will be able to watch it on YouTube, Twitch, Twitter, and more.

The broadcast will be hosted by Frankie Ward (esports and gaming presenter), with a pre-show hosted by Future channel presenters Midas and Jules Gill. “Planning for the PC Gaming Show takes place all year as we confer with our industry council on the games they are most hyped for,” said PC Gaming Show editorial director Jake Tucker in a press release. “I’m excited about the top 20 we’ll be revealing on December 3. Make sure you have your wishlists at the ready!”
