Video: Need A Break Between GTA Reviews? Watch Us Rave About Zelda's Switch 2 Changes

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Now yes, things have been a little Vice City-focused today, haven't they? The GTA 6 reviews are out, and it is, by all accounts, bloody brilliant.

But you know what else is brilliant? The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

While most of the internet is off Leonida dreamin' today (and honestly, fair enough), we've still got Hyrule on the mind. Last week, we asked what your favourite tiny tweak in the Switch 2 remake is, and now our wonderful video producers, Mai and Alex, are talking about some of their top changes, too.

In the above video, you'll hear the pair talk about all things Zelda. They touch on tiny tweaks like new NPCs, dialogue and physics, up to larger additions like the Fairy Messages and additional HUD options.

So, if you're eager to take a break from all the GTA reviews and relax with something a little more Zelda-focused for a while (shall we say, 37 minutes?), then this could be the perfect opportunity to do so.
