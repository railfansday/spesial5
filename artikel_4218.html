UnGodly brings brutal hack 'n slash action to mobile in the fine Frazetta tradition, out now

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Time to put aside flashy, colourful martial arts moves and get right to the hacking and slashing, as UnGodly, the brutal new fantasy RPG from Mark Otero (one of the key people behind Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes), lands today on iOS and Android.

Set in the world of Dunmiir, a world ravaged by The Devouring, you play as gifted warriors called Primals who offer the only hope to defy fate and save the world. To do so, you'll need to hack 'n slash your way through foes big and small, wielding crude but effective weaponry, the power of magic and more. Think Diablo, but with a touch of Genshin and a sprinkle of Frazetta's famous paintings to wrap the world together.

Equally, the core gameplay thoroughly delivers on the Sword & Sorcery promises of UnGodly, as you blast apart enemies with lightning bolts in suitably gory fashion. And with their upcoming collab featuring Conan the Barbarian, it may be that UnGodly satisfies a sword & sorcery itch yet to be scratched.

So if you're looking to skip the more sanitised combat of other RPGs, and instead get right to the decapitation and limb-lopping action you'd associate with characters such as Conan, then check out UnGodly on iOS and Android.
