concrete, lime-washed brick, and color-blocked zones organize movebon boutique training studio

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

designed by jotajota+, movebon is a 126-sqm boutique training studio organized around an elliptical tubular lighting fixture, a concrete block focal wall, color-blocked zones, and custom galvanized steel and timber storage units.

LINKA News edited by christina vergopoulou November 26, 2026 3 min

Occupying 126 sqm, Movebon is a boutique training studio in Madrid designed by JOTAJOTA+ around a clear spatial division between an open workout area and private service facilities. Rather than altering the building’s primary structure, the studio organizes circulation fluidly around existing structural elements while facing the street through two large glazed panels that admit daylight and expose interior activity to the sidewalk. A suspended elliptical loop of tubular lighting runs across the ceiling to guide movement and reinforce visual continuity throughout the main training room.

Chromatic blocking and raw industrial materials define the two functional zones. In the main workout space, both the floor and ceiling are coated in brown-red (RAL 3011), framing sightlines toward a newly built concrete block wall at the rear. Along the lower perimeter, existing brickwork is coated in lime paint to introduce surface texture and balance the saturated red planes. Moving into the service zone, a concrete block entry threshold transitions into the changing rooms, where a traffic-blue palette (RAL 5017) marks a quieter shift for preparation and recovery.

The interior is completed by a custom modular furniture system engineered by the design studio JOTAJOTA+ to integrate storage and training equipment. Combining timber, galvanized steel, metal grating, and standardized AUER plastic containers, the custom fixtures extend the project’s utilitarian material vocabulary across both public and private zones.
