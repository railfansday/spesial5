IntelliGRC Launches Trust Center, Giving MSPs and MSSPs a Controlled Way to Share Security Documentation for Every Client

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Service providers can now publish a Trust Center for each tenant, gate documents behind access approval, and keep a record of who had access and when.

FAIRFAX, Va., Nov. 25, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- IntelliGRC, the cyber governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) platform built for managed service providers (MSPs) and managed security service providers (MSSPs), today announced Trust Center. The new capability lets customers design and publish a public web page for each tenant, where external organizations can find security documentation and request access to gated content. Trust Center is now available and is included with every IntelliGRC license at no added cost.

Trust Center is built to end the practice of sending security documents one at a time. Each Trust Center is hosted on IntelliGRC's domain and gives customers and prospects a single place to find certifications and audit artifacts. When a visitor requests access to gated content, an admin or designated user approves or denies the request. Approval sends the requester a magic link by email. Access can be revoked at any time, and the platform keeps a record of who had access and when. Customers can also add their own NDA for requesters to sign once they are provisioned access.

Service providers can now publish a Trust Center for each tenant and keep a record of who had access and when.

The feature is designed for providers that manage many clients at once. From the IntelliGRC Admin App, a provider can set defaults that establish a baseline for every client Trust Center under its license, upload documents once to a Shared Library and deploy them to all Trust Centers or only specific ones, and review pending access requests across every Trust Center from a single Approvals tab. Notifications alert admins to access requests and other Trust Center events.

"A compliance program only counts if you can prove it to the people asking," said Ozzie Saeed, Founder and CEO of IntelliGRC. "Our customers should not have to email documents one at a time and then lose track of who has them. Trust Center gives every tenant one page to point to. It puts the provider in charge of who gets in and lets them revoke that access at any time. For an MSP managing many clients, proof becomes something they can deliver the same way every time."

Trust Center shipped in IntelliGRC release 3.52.0 on November 10, 2026. Existing customers can find it in the Trust Centers section of the Admin App. New to IntelliGRC? Schedule a discovery call with us to see it live.
