Stocks fall as Nvidia slides, giving back part of Monday's rally

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Anthropic, Micron and Advanced Micro Devices also fell on Tuesday, a day after the Nasdaq closed 6.5% higher

Nvidia $NVDA stock fell more than 3% on Tuesday, giving back part of the 31% gain it posted on Monday after Jensen Huang returned as the company's CEO.

The Nasdaq $NDAQ Composite was down 1.4%, and the S&P 500 was off 1.1%. The Dow Jones Industrial Average was lower by 280 points, or 0.6%.

The Nasdaq ended Monday's session with a 6.5% gain, the S&P 500 advanced 4.7% and the Dow added 3%. That marked the strongest one-day performance since April 9, 2025, for both the Nasdaq and the S&P 500, according to CNBC. Huang's return lifted Nvidia's market value by close to $1 trillion.

Other AI-linked stocks also sank as Nvidia retreated. Anthropic stock lost over 6% and, at one stage, traded under its IPO price. Micron $MU Technology stock dropped more than 6%, and Meta $META Platforms stock lost nearly 2%. Shares of Advanced Micro Devices and Broadcom $AVGO lost 5% and 4%, in that order.

The U.S. is readying higher duties on dozens of nations, China among them, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Tom Hulick, CEO of Strategy Asset Managers, told CNBC that Nvidia has "a lot of things to worry about" on corporate governance.

In Asia, the Nikkei in Japan was up 4%, the Kospi in South Korea was up 6% and the Taiex in Taiwan was up 9%.

The S&P 500 and the Dow closed in correction on Friday during a sell-off in AI-linked stocks.

Dell $DELL, Analog Devices $ADI and Autodesk $ADSK are among the companies scheduled to report earnings on Tuesday, according to The Wall Street Journal. Home-price and consumer-confidence readings are also due.
