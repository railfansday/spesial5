Building in Barca and the Jim Reaper strikes again

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Greetings, Tim here on blog duty today and I am going right back to Arseblog’s roots by writing from Barcelona. I am here of course because Arsenal Women earned a very creditable (and slightly unexpected given how this season has gone for them) draw against the current Champions League holders Barcelona.

Of course, Renee Slegers’ side did beat Barca the last time the teams met in the 2025 Final and that was only slightly less of an upset than this result. As expected, Arsenal rose their luck a little with Barca putting up 24 shots and 2.4XG. But Arsenal have put up similar numbers in games this season roughly 176 times and not won so I am happy to be on the receiving end of a little serendipity.

Frida Maanum gave Arsenal a first half lead before her compatriot Caroline Graham Hansen equalised in the second half. You can read Renee Slegers’ post-match press conference here and my take on the match here if you are so inclined. Up next is a six pointer for Champions League qualification at home to London City Lionesses on Sunday which Arsenal just have to win. Obviously we will have all the usual coverage of that game for you.

I also had the pleasure of meeting fellow Arseblog / Arsenal Vision colleague Phil Costa for lunch and in Barcelona, for a seafood and beer lover, a long lunch in balmy 20 degree conditions was very welcome indeed!

Elsewhere, there were plenty of Arsenal players in action for their countries. Martin Zubimendi played the full 90 minutes for Spain in their 2-0 away win at Czechia. Usually I would wince at a sentence that featured ‘Arsenal player x’ and ‘full 90 minutes’ but I think Zubimendi could use those minutes for physical reasons and for psychological reasons as he tries to plot his way back into a very crowded Arsenal midfield.

Mikel Merino came on for the final 24 minutes of that game and David Raya kept his gloves clean on the bench. Declan Rice played 77 minutes of England’s 2-1 win over Croatia at Wembley in which Harry Kane scored his 417th goal for England, 461 of which have been penalties (citation needed). Eze was an unused sub and he’s another I wouldn’t have minded seeing get some minutes to build rhythm and confidence.

Riccardo Calafiori played the full 90 of Italy’s 3-1 win over France. Jurrien Timber and Kai Havertz could be in action tonight as Netherlands play Greece and Germany play away at Serbia. Fingers crossed for both. These are two players I would not mind seeing unused but I doubt that will be the case.

Meanwhile, the big news elsewhere was that Michael Carrick was finally officially sacked by Manchester United. He had still been taking training this week despite everyone knowing that he was for the chop. I hope he filled his coat pockets with as many of the milk and ketchup sachets as he could from the canteen.

He was clearly out of his depth and had been stalking the United dugout looking like a haunted Noel Gallagher for what feels like months. Steve Holland has also left and Ben White’s recent recall to the England team resurfaced old wounds as he was asked about his reasons for making himself unavailable for a while for his country. White refuses to be drawn on it.

I am biased, of course, but I know who I think has emerged better from this sorry saga and his surname is a colour not a country. Having taken in Spurs and Chelsea already, it looks like Mauricio Pochettino is about to go three for three on his tour of the Premier League’s most evil institutions. If hell really does exist, this man will regard an eternal stint of fire and brimstone as something of a vacation. Maybe working for the Jim Reaper at United is his penance for accepting work from Chelsea and Spurs.
