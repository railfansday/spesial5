Arsenal are set to make a late decision on the fitness of David Raya for Manchester City’s trip to north London on Sunday.

The goalkeeper missed Tuesday’s Champions League draw with Borussia Dortmund due to a shoulder injury he picked up against Newcastle last weekend.

However, while there were fears that was a significant problem, scans have since showed that Raya avoided serious injury and his spell on the sidelines was not expected to be a lengthy one.

It remains to be seen whether Raya recovers in time to face City in the Premier League on Sunday, with Kepa Arrizabalaga ready to fill in again if required.

“I don’t know,” Arsenal boss Mikel Arteta said of Raya’s availability at his pre-match press conference on Friday. “We still have a few hours before the game. We’re just trying to get everybody back.”

Asked whether there was hope that Raya would be available, Arteta added bluntly: “Yes.”

Ezri Konsa appears set to keep his place at centre-back alongside Gabriel, as Arsenal continue to cautiously manage William Saliba’s fitness.

Saliba was included in the matchday squad against both Newcastle and Dortmund but is yet to make a first-team appearance this season. It would be a surprise if he came straight in to face City.

It is expected that Riccardo Calafiori will return to the Arsenal starting lineup on Sunday after being rested in midweek, while Eberechi Eze could well get the nod in front of him on the left wing after impressing at St James’ Park.

Viktor Gyokeres scored against Dortmund but rival Kai Havertz is usually trusted in these biggest games by Arteta.

The German was given a full rest in that draw with Dortmund and he looks set to lead the line against City. Bukayo Saka and Martin Zubimendi both remain out with thigh and hamstring injuries respectively.

Predicted Arsenal XI vs Man City (4-2-3-1): Raya; Timber, Konsa, Gabriel, Calafiori; Rice, Guimaraes; Madueke, Odegaard, Eze; Havertz
