Meta has begun testing a prediction-market app with employees, allowing them to wager points on the outcomes of events rather than real money, The Wall Street Journal reported.

The U.S. Department of Justice obtained court approval to seize or restrain more than $1.2 billion stolen from Binance and disrupted a separate North Korean crypto theft scheme.

Two Bitcoin addresses that had been inactive for more than 16 years transferred a combined 100 BTC on Friday, worth around $6.9 million.

JPMorgan increased its Strategy share holdings by 36% in the third quarter to 848,932 shares as of Sept. 30, according to its latest SEC filing.

The White House nominated four individuals to fill vacant seats at the Securities and Exchange Commission and Commodity Futures Trading Commission.
