How to earn and spend Legacy points in WoW Forever?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Legacy System in WoW: Forever turns your account into quite literally a record of your achievements.

The Legacy System in World of Warcraft: Forever replaces the Achievements in other versions of WoW, and this time everything you achieve in one character is another toon’s to enjoy. Milestones you ordinarily just gloss by become moments that can improve your next playthrough, so the Legacy System is Blizzard’s way of motivating you to get up and get to leveling.

But the Legacy System goes beyond just achieving things. When you finish an objective within the Legacy System, you also earn Legacy Points. And this is spent on buffs all your characters can enjoy. So how exactly should you approach this?
