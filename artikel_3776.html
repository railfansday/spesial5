Marvel Rivals’ Doomsday-themed season 11 brings Nightcrawler into the fold, and Dracula soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Doom is here. But first, a blue, religious mutant who can teleport all over the battlefield.

Nightcrawler has been confirmed as the newest Marvel Rivals hero, launching alongside season 11 this Friday. The X-Men member looks to be a Strategist who can use his teleportation ability to get teammates out of harm’s way.

“Two timelines, the present and the future, are on a course for an Incursion,” the update teases. “New York and Nueva York are about to collide head-on! Will they find a solution in separation, or will each look out for its own interests? How will the heroes’ actions affect the ambitions of the two Doctor Dooms? How will a pious saint with the visage of a demon and the exiled king trapped in a foreign land shoulder the burden of saving two worlds?”

Here’s everything we know so far about the season 11 update hitting Marvel Rivals on Nov. 13.

BAMF! Kurt Wagner, otherwise known as the X-Men team member Nightcrawler, is the 55th hero to join Marvel Rivals, and the game’s 13th Strategist (healer). He looks to be a very agile hero, teleporting in and out of battle, healing allies and dealing damage with swords and his tail.

The end of the trailer teases that Dracula, waking from his slumber, will be the new hero in the 11.5 update in December, refuting leaks that it would be Doctor Doom.

It’s all about Doom these days, and rightfully so, as the iconic villain gets his shine in Avengers: Doomsday next month. Considering the season is called Doomsday and teases conflict between Doctor Doom and Doom 2099, it’s a head-scratcher that he’s not included in this season, but it’s likely he will be coming soon. Maybe next year!

Another 18-vs-18 map is coming on Nov. 26th for the new Chrono-Annihilation mode, themed around Doomsday’s Incursion taking place between New York and Nueva York 2099.

This is the second birthday of Marvel Rivals, coming up in December, including an area in Times Square to play mini-games, exclusive anniversary-themed costumes, and more.

“Severe offenders” will face a year-long ban and account suspensions when it comes to hate speech in-game, according to NetEase. The team is cracking down on the community.
