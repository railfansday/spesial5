Brad Pitt Wants to Play a James Bond Villain

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The announcement finally arrived this week: Robert Pattinson is James Bond. But before we could even come up for air about Amazon MGM and director Denis Villeneuve’s decision to have arguably the busiest movie star in the business today follow Daniel Craig as 007, the Internet had already moved on. Fans started asking more questions. Who will costar as the next Bond girl? Who’s performing the song? And, most importantly, who would play opposite Pattinson as the villain?

Well, how about Brad Pitt? The F1 star likely didn’t expect a James Bond question when he stepped on the red carpet for the premiere of his latest film, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth. The spinoff sequel of Quentin Tarantino’s Once Upon a Time... in Hollywood sees Pitt return as stuntman Cliff Booth under the direction of David Fincher, which couldn’t be further from the life of a British MI6 hitman. Still, Pitt was excited to hear for the first time that Pattinson landed the role when a reporter for ExtraTV shared the news. “I love this idea,” Pitt said. “He’s the real deal.”

Would he ever think about playing the villain? Pitt winked at the camera and said, “Robert? Denis? You know what, I’d like to do that. My man Javier [Bardem] did it so well [in Skyfall] It’s tall shoes, but I’d take that on.”

Later down the red carpet, Pitt answered the question again when Complex asked if he had any advice for Pattinson. “Sign me for the villain,” he said, coming around to the idea even more. “Get Denis Villeneuve on the flip phone. Let’s go!”

Elsewhere, Pattinson’s fellow Dune: Part Three costar Florence Pugh is already pitching herself as a potential villain and/or Bond girl. When Villeneuve presented her with an honor at Elle's Women in Hollywood event on Monday, Pugh congratulated the director on finding the next James Bond. “Nice catch,” she said. “Any villains the size of a five-foot-four-inch female?" I’m sure this is just the beginning.

I’ll admit, Pitt wasn’t my first choice for the next Bond villain. If anything, Pattinson was. But I love how enthusiastic he is about the possibility. Now, I kind of feel like I need to see it.
