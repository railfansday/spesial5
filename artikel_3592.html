Each campaign in Towers of Babble begins with a handful of letter tiles. Spell a noun and you can build a unit on the map. Like larvae in an anthill, words are predeterminedly assigned to harvesters or defensive units. Spell out verbs and modifiers to create power-ups for your nouns. Use your harvested resources to purchase upgrades, such as a larger selection of tiles or adding premium stats to certain letters.
