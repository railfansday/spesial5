World of Warcraft: Forever just achieved a player high that WoW hasn’t seen in 15 years, according to WoW VP Holly Longdale.

Apparently, WoW: Forever has more people playing weekly than have played WoW weekly in 15 years. Granted, that’s a bit of a wobbly record, because WoW: Forever has only been out for two weeks so far, so I don’t really know what a weekly average looks like or what sort of stats one might compare it to. But I do believe Longdale that an awful lot of folks are playing WoW: Forever right now. And there are still three weeks left before WoW: Forever‘s raids unlock and things really get spicy!

We knew WoW: Forever was going to be big. As a remixed version of the original world of WoW, with brand new content built onto the vanilla foundation, it appeals to a lot of people who burned out on the game over the years as well as new players who find the retail version overwhelming. Its beta was “the largest beta in franchise history,” and so far its critical reception is pretty positive as well (including from us). It remains to be seen whether Blizzard can hold that interest long-term as it now juggles three different versions of World of Warcraft, including the retail version, which is in a bit of a rough spot at the moment both in terms of story and gameplay.
