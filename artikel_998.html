Sykes ready to go 'all the way' on debut at Grand Slam after stunning Humphries

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Tom Sykes came up with a thrilling 10-9 victory over Luke Humphries at the Grand Slam of Darts; It sees him through to the quarter-finals on debut; you can watch the Grand Slam of Darts in Wolverhampton live on Sky Sports until November 22

Tom Sykes pulled off the biggest win of his darting career with a 10-9 victory over Luke Humphries in the last 16 at the Grand Slam of Darts - but he does not want to stop there.

The 'Castleford Tiger', who is making his debut in Wolverhampton, put in an incredible performance against the world No 2 in which he raced away before 'Cool Hand' fought back to take the game to a last-leg decider.

Sykes scored amazingly in that winner-takes-all leg and with Humphries unable to finish off the 128 checkout, he clinched D18 to set up a quarter-final showdown with Michael van Gerwen after his 10-8 win over James Wade in the final match of the night.

Sykes is ranked 72nd in the world in what is his first year on the PDC Pro Tour but, with the way he is playing, he sees no reason why he cannot go on a real run at the Grand Slam.

"I think so. I know I have got the scoring game," Sykes said.

"I know I have got the scoring game to live with anyone in the world, and I have shown it tonight with the 180s and the 140s that I have been hitting.

"If the doubles go in, then I'm a dangerous player."

Humphries was suffering with a shoulder injury throughout the contest and Sykes did not realise until they were practicing and was gingerly picking up his darts.

Despite the clear pain Humphries was in, Sykes still feels he was up against the best darts player in the world playing some of the best darts in the world.

"I can't believe it, honestly. Luke didn't tell me till the end, but he said he had a bit of an injury there. I could tell, to be fair," he added.

"In the practice darts at the beginning, he was dropping his darts and he could barely bend over to pick them up.

"He still played really well and I got off to a really good start. To get 4-1 up against, in my opinion, the best player in the world at the minute, I'm absolutely buzzing with that.

"For me, he is still one of the best players in the world, even if he's injured or not. I am just buzzing to get the win over him."

Many a player would be overawed with the experiences that Sykes is now having but, as someone who enjoys the big stages, he is revelling in rubbing shoulders with the top stars of the sport.

"When I first started on the Pro Tour and I'm walking in, you see Luke Humphries, Luke Littler, Michael van Gerwen, James Wade. It is a bit daunting walking in and seeing all these guys who you've watched on TV," Sykes said.

"I have been in there tonight and I've been playing with Gian van Veen and with James Wade. You are like, 'what am I doing in here with these lot?'

"Now I am mixing it with these on the big stages, it is mad.

"I have always said that I am a big stage player, I like playing on big stages.

"I massively put the hard work in for the last year, and I ended up getting my Tour card last year, and now I'm reaping the rewards of it."
