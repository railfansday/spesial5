At Surest, we are passionate about improving the health of our members!

The Clinical Advocate - Behavioral Health Specialist is responsible for supporting members in navigating their Surest benefits to support improved health and well-being. In this role, the Clinical Advocate - Behavioral Health Specialist will focus on members and their families with mental health challenges and substance use disorders. Through telephonic coaching, resources, and referrals to applicable programs, the Clinical Advocate - Behavioral Health Specialist will work with members and their families and providers to establish a personal plan to improve their health. This position reports to the Clinical Operations Manager and has no direct reports.

If you reside within PST time zone, you’ll enjoy the flexibility to work remotely * as you take on some tough challenges.

*** Candidates must be willing to work 8:00 am – 5:00 PM PST ***

You’ll be rewarded and recognized for your performance in an environment that will challenge you and give you clear direction on what it takes to succeed in your role as well as provide development for other roles you may be interested in.
