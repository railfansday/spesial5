How to get the Mini Miku Sprite in Fortnite

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Mini Miku Sprite is now available in Fortnite Treasure Hunters, and you can now add it to your collection. Like all previously released Sprites, the Mini Miku Sprite has special variants and unique in-game abilities.

Without further ado, let’s find out how to get the Sprite and how it works.

Unlike most sprites in the game, there is a guaranteed drop spot for Mini Miku. First, head to Friendship Villa, which is located in the north of the island. There, you will have to interact with Hatsune Miku, who allows you to select a new Sprite.

This is the easiest way to collect the Sprite. Aside from the method mentioned above, you can also find Mini Miku from the wild. Try opening as many chests as possible to have higher chances. You can also try your luck at the Vault since the Rare Chests have better chances at providing you with the Sprite.

If you want to find the Fortune, Cursed, or Gold variants, the method remains the same. Their odds are significantly less that the base variant.

You can spawn a Shield Bubble around you and your allies. However, you and your buddies will have to perform an emote together to do so. This makes the Sprite truly unique since I can’t remember any other variant that has such requirements.

The Fortune, Cursed, and Gold variants all offer the same base effect, but you get additional in-game abilities from them to trigger.
