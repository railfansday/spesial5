CNN Calls GTA 6 The ‘Most Intense Version Of Pac-Man‘

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Folks, I’m not sure if you are aware of this, but video games, like Grand Theft Auto 6, have come a long way since Pac-Man. I’m aware of that, but perhaps the people at CNN might want to play a video game made in the last 20 years.

Today, on CNN, three adults who are paid money to report on the events of the world were asked to talk about the just-launched Grand Theft Auto 6 for a bit, and it was extremely embarrassing and sad for all involved. For some reason, in an effort to explain what GTA 6 is to people who aren’t big gamers, they decided to compare it to Pac-Man. Seriously.

"… think of it as the most intense version of Pac-Man…" – CNN on GTA VI

Come on, man. This industry made over 200 Billion dollars in revenue last year alone. What are we doing here?

— Raj Patel (@rajio.bsky.social) 2026-11-19T21:36:55.884Z

“If you’re not a gamer, think of it as the most intense version of Pac-Man where you are being chased, and you’re chasing with the most lifelike graphics possible.”

Wow! What a terrible way to describe GTA 6. Or really any popular video game made in the last 20 years. Look, I’m not suggesting these people need to be gaming experts. But video games aren’t a tiny niche only played by kids down at the arcade. They’re a massively popular, successful, and influential medium. Some of the biggest shows and movies of the last few years are direct adaptations of games like The Last of Us and Fallout. And GTA 6 is the biggest of the big. You could maybe not waste everyone’s time with such a lazy and useless description.

It’s also just weird because most people on this planet have a basic understanding of what GTA is in 2026. It’s that game where you steal cars and run around and shoot cops. That’s not a perfect description, but it’s much better than the crap CNN is sharing on the air when the network is suddenly forced to talk about video games due to a really, really big one dropping.

Anyway, I can’t wait to be in my 70s and hear reporters in the mainstream media describe GTA 12 or whatever as the best version of Pac-Man you’ve ever seen. Video games really have come a long way, folks…
