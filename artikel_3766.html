Updated Xbox PC app promises 60% faster loading and easier access to games, but CEO Asha Sharma says there's "plenty more to do"

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Xbox PC app hasn't had the best track record in recent years, but Microsoft is promising that it's now much faster and easier to use than before.

Starting today, anyone part of the Xbox Insiders program can try out the new improvements. According to Microsoft, the updated Xbox PC app now boots up 60% faster, several pages can launch up to 50% faster, and in-app windows can open up to 65% faster. The company acknowledged that performance can vary based on your set up, internet connection, device, and so on.

The rest of the update involves "simpler navigation, updated filters, and new ways to organise your games," according to Xbox Wire, while social features are now included in the main navigation menu so you can start playing or chatting with friends quicker.

A faster Xbox PC app is certainly nice for players who regularly use it, though it's still missing a lot of the features that make Steam the go-to place to buy PC games. CEO Asha Sharma called the update "a meaningful improvement," but acknowledged there's still "plenty more to do."

Microsoft also said "it's easier to find what you're looking for" using the app's search function, and refreshed game cards and animations should give the platform "a cleaner look and feel."

Elsewhere, the revamped cloud gaming setup will roll out to even more people using web browsers, and more Xbox Insiders should hear about the program over the next few weeks. "The updated experience also makes it easier to find and play your games," the company said while bringing attention to its new light and dark modes. Supported smart TVs and VR headsets will get the updated cloud experience, as well.

Earlier this year, the console maker brought a selection of 'classic' games to PC for the first time ever - Blinx: The Time Sweeper, Conker: Live and Reloaded, Crimson Skies: High Road to Revenge and Fuzion Frenzy led the first batch. Mythic Achievements - the Platinum Trophy equivalent - went live for all Xbox players, too.
