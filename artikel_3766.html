Deborah Ann Woll said she felt "totally unbothered" by the small but vocal subset of gamers who were upset by the upcoming God of War: Laufey, and apparently she meant it—because today Amazon MGM Studios announced that she's going to play the same role in the live-action God of War series coming to Prime.

Just as in God of War: Laufey and its predecessor, God of War: Ragnarok, Woll—best known for playing Karen Page in Marvel's Daredevil, Defenders, and Punisher TV series, as well as a lengthy stint on True Blood as Jessica Hamby—will portray Faye, wife of Kratos and mother of Atreus.

"Faye’s abrupt passing sets in motion the events of God of War," the announcement says. "Along their journey, Kratos and Atreus discover secrets about Faye that make them wonder how well they truly knew her at all."

It's been a bumpy road for the God of War series. It was first reported in 2022, but started over from scratch two years later after the showrunner and executive producers quit. We got our first look at Ryan Hurst as Kratos earlier this year—not very impressive, but we all had the same initial impression of Henry Cavill as Geralt and that worked out well—but then Hurst had to be recast because of an on-set injury; Dave Bautista now has the lead role. Child actors Callum Vinson and Island Austin, who played Atreus and Thrud, were also recast.

God of War: Laufey is set to launch on May 20, 2027, but sadly is a PlayStation 5 exclusive, a state of affairs that seems likely to endure given Sony's recent strategy shift. The God of War live action series on Prime has been ordered for two seasons, but a release target hasn't been announced.
