‘DWTS’ Grammy Night Taps Sheryl Crow, Maren Morris, Brandy and Monica: Where to Watch Online

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The season's long-awaited dance-offs will feature live performances by each of the Grammy-winning artists.

Dancing With the Stars debuts its first-ever Grammy Night with a stacked lineup of live performances by award-winning artists and a powerhouse playlist of nominated hits.

The episode airs on Tuesday, Nov. 10 on ABC, available to stream live via DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), Fubo (with a five-day free trial), Sling and Hulu + Live TV. It will also stream on Disney+, which doesn’t offer a free trial of its own, but is included in any of DirecTV’s Signature packages, which do offer the aforementioned free trial.

Grammy Night 2026 airs live on Tuesday, Nov. 10 at 8 p.m. PT/ET on ABC (carried on live TV streamers like DirecTV, Fubo, Sling and Hulu + Live TV) and will be simulcast on Disney+.

Since the competition show airs on ABC, it’s also available to watch live on any streaming service that carries said network, including DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), Fubo (also with a five-day free trial), Sling and Hulu + Live TV.

The episode will also be available to stream live on Disney+, and while the streamer doesn’t offer a free trial directly, fans can get free access through any of DirecTV’s Signature packages, which include the Disney+ and Hulu bundle and offer a five-day free trial.

Learn more about each plan option, including how to build your own channel lineup (starting at just $20.99 per month), at directv.com.

In select markets, ABC is included in Sling’s budget-friendly Select plan, from $19.99 per month; Sling’s Blue plan, from $45.99 per month; and Sling’s Orange & Blue plan, from $60.99 per month. Visit Sling.com to check station availability.

Watch ABC with Hulu + Live TV. Subscriptions come bundled with Disney+ and ESPN+, and start at $89.99 per month.

Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson received immunity from this week’s dance-offs. The remainder of the pairings include Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov vs. Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach; Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy vs. Tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik; and Connor Wood and Rylee Arnold vs. Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas.

The first pairing will dance a rumba to “The Boy Is Mine,” which Brandy and Monica will perform live; the second will dance a cha cha to “The Middle,” performed live by Maren Morris; and the final matchup will dance a cha cha to “All I Wanna Do,” sung live by Sheryl Crow.
