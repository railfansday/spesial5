Dakota Johnson Is 50 Shades Sleeker With "Off Black" Hair Makeover

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Dakota Johnson darkened her hair to a shade of "off black" thanks to colorist Tracey Cunningham before stepping out for a romantic date night with MGK.

Dakota Johnson just said "laters, baby" to her dirty blonde hair.

Seven months after lightening her signature brunette locks, the Fifty shades of Grey alum went back to her dark roots with what her colorist Tracey Cunningham described as an "off black" hair makeover.

To keep her blackish brown tresses glossy, the hairstylist used products from Schwarzkopf Professional's IGORA VIBRANCE line, according to a press release from the brand.

"@dakotajohnson went lighter for a movie—then came home and shot the cover of Vogue," Cunningham wrote in an Instagram post last month. "And as soon as the Vogue shoot was done, we took her darker again for her next project!"

And Johnson knew just where to show off her new 'do: a date night with Machine Gun Kelly. On Nov. 11, the 37-year-old stepped out in Los Angeles with her raven-colored hair and an oversized tiger-striped coat with a black fur collar.

As she exited a recording studio, MGK—clad in baggy jeans, a white T-shirt and a bright red beanie—proved to be a true gentleman as he took her hand and helped her down the stairs.

But while Johnson is down to switch up her hair color once in a while, she'll never get rid of her iconic fringe.

“I've had bangs since I was a child,” she told Vogue last year. “I cut them myself. I’ve had the same haircut my entire life, apart from between the ages of about 10 and 14. I just feel like myself with bangs.”

However, the Verity star would love to go "without having to trim it all the time."

"I always travel with scissors and love to drink a martini while trimming my own bangs," she quipped, adding that there have been times when she accidentally messed up. "Not every time, but yes."
