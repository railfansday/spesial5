Price casts doubt over accepting Premier League Darts spot

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Grand Slam of Darts champion Gerwyn Price hints at missing next year's Premier League Darts; you can watch the World Darts Championship live on Sky Sports from December 11

Gerwyn Price has cast doubt over accepting a Premier League Darts spot in 2027.

Price ended his major trophy drought by winning the Grand Slam of Darts with a brilliant 16-11 victory over Gian van Veen in Wolverhampton on Sunday.

Price claimed Grand Slam glory for the fourth time as he celebrated his first televised ranking title since November 2021.

The Welshman will be eyeing further televised success as the Ladbrokes Players Championship Finals takes place at Butlin's Minehead Resort from November 27-29 before the start of the World Championship on December 11 - live on Sky Sports.

Then all eyes turn to the Premier League where PDC chief Matt Porter recently teased a change to the format.

Price, a finalist in 2023, previously suggested he might decline an invite to the tournament, which runs across 16 weeks, after a difficult 2024 season before reversing his decision and accepting his selection.

The 41-year-old, who has hit five nine-dart finishes in Premier League Darts, the most by any player in the tournament's history, has now hinted at missing the 2027 roadshow.

Speaking on Sky Sports News, Price said: "My daughter's pregnant. I've got a lot of things on. It is difficult in the Premier League with World Series and how many events the PDC are putting on these days.

"It's a couple of months before things are decided for that. So we'll see how things go."

Price also spoke about Luke Littler facing repeated booing and whistling from crowds at major tournaments, with 'The Iceman' suggesting it will eventually "go away".

Price has been subjected to boos and he even wore ear defenders to block out jeers from the crowd at the World Championship in 2023.

"The crowd like to see the underdog win, and I think that's what Luke is going through at the minute," Price reflected.

"Obviously, being the best player in the world is what he is at the minute, and I think a lot of people are a little bit jealous and jump on his back because they want to see him fail because he is so successful. But that'll go away.

"I never thought it would go away with me, but it does eventually, and it will with him as well.

"He's a great lad, he's a great player, he's great for the sport, and people should just get off his back."
