Kourtney Kardashian Shares Gift From Lauren Sánchez Bezos to Her Kids

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Kourtney Kardashian revealed the "special delivery" Jeff Bezos' wife Lauren Sánchez Bezos gave to her kids Mason, 16, Penelope, 14, Reign, 11, and Rocky, 3.

Christmas is starting a little early for Kourtney Kardashian.

The Kardashians star revealed the festive package from family friend Lauren Sánchez Bezos gave to her and ex Scott Disick's kids Mason, 16, Penelope, 14, and Reign, 11, as well as her 3-year-old son Rocky with husband Travis Barker.

Alongside a photo of the gift, which was wrapped in a large Santa Clause-themed sack, Kourtney wrote on her Instagram Story Nov. 11, "Special delivery."

The present was attached with a handwritten note bearing the names of Kourtney and her kids, with the 47-year-old tagging Lauren and the official Instagram account for the journalist's recently released children's book The Fly Who Flew to the North Pole.

That Lauren and Kourtney are getting a jumpstart on the holidays with some sweet exchanges is no surprise as the former journalist has long been keeping up with the Lemme founder and her family. After all, the 56-year-old first met Kourtney's mom Kris Jenner back in the '90s before reconnecting with the Keeping Up With the Kardashians alum through mutual friends of husband Jeff Bezos.

"We know they will be in our lives forever," Kris said of the Bezos family in a 2023 interview with Vogue, adding that Lauren will always "make sure everyone is up to speed about what’s happening in the world."

The momager jokingly added, "She has more energy than I do, which is really annoying."

Lauren is also close with Kris' daughters Kim Kardashian, Khloe Kardashian, Kylie Jenner and Kendall Jenner, all of whom attended her wedding to Jeff, 62, in Venice, Italy, last year.

“Lauren and I are always sending DMs building each other up,” Kim raved to Vogue in 2023. “Every time there’s a look that we like, she’ll say, ‘WOW,’ or, ‘OMG you look amazing.’ She’s such a girl’s girl.”

In fact, their styles are so similar that they had unintentionally bid against each other during a fashion auction that year.

"We’ll share it!" Kim, 46, recalled telling Lauren realizing that they were competing against a Balenciaga couture piece. “I thought, you wear it once, I’ll wear it once, it’ll be so cute!"
