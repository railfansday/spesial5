Miley Cyrus Reacts to Shared Grammy Nod With Dolly Parton: ‘A Beautiful Way to Honor Her Enormous Heart’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

"My Aunt Dolly is one of the greatest songwriters the world has ever known," the pop star wrote.

Miley Cyrus is channeling Dolly Parton‘s light as she reacts to their new Grammy nomination on behalf of them both.

Shortly after the 2027 Grammy nominations went live on Monday (Nov. 16), revealing that the late Queen of Country and her famous goddaughter are up for best country duo/group performance — alongside Lainey Wilson, Queen Latifah and Reba McEntire for their collective cover of “Light of a Clear Blue Morning” — Cyrus shared a black-and-white photo of a New Harvest … First Gathering vinyl on Instagram. The album was released by Parton in 1976 and featured the original version of the song on the tracklist.

“A couple Christmases ago Dolly gifted me this vinyl knowing how much I’d love an original,” the singer-actress wrote. “My Aunt Dolly is one of the greatest songwriters the world has ever known. She never made music for recognition but to make the world a happier place through her songs.”

Cyrus also shared a clip of her in the studio recording vocals for the 2026 version of “Light of a Clear Blue Morning,” which Parton and her collaborators released in January in honor of the icon’s 80th birthday. She’d pass away about eight months later following a brief cancer battle.

“To be nominated alongside her, Lainey Wilson, Queen Latifah and Reba McEntire for Light of a Clear Blue Morning is a beautiful way to honor her enormous heart,” Cyrus continued in her post. “This cover supported pediatric cancer research at Vanderbilt’s Monroe Carell Jr. Children’s Hospital a cause close to her heart.”

The Hannah Montana alum added: “I miss her calling on big days like this but feel her in every moment. Love you Aunt Dolly. Congratulations to these beautiful women and all the other nominees.”

Parton is actually nominated twice in the best country duo/group performance category, earning a nod for her guest appearance on Zac Brown Band’s “Butterfly.” Her narration of her book Star of the Show: My Life on Stage also got a spot in the best Audio Book, narration and storytelling recording race.

The Dollywood founder won a total of 10 Grammys in her lifetime out of 58 nominations. “Light of a Clear Blue Morning” — which debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard Country Digital Song Sales chart — was just one of several songs on which Parton collaborated with Cyrus. The pair also collaborated on a joint version of Cyrus’ hit “Wrecking Ball” as well as “Rainbowland” from Cyrus’ album Younger Now.

The 2027 Grammys will take place on Feb. 7. RAYE leads the upcoming ceremony’s nominations with 11 total, followed by Rosalia with nine.
