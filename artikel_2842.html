Eagles roster news: 3 moves announced ahead of Cowboys game

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Philadelphia Eagles officially announced three roster moves ahead of their Week 12 Thanksgiving game against the Dallas Cowboys.

DT Gabe Hall was signed from the practice squad to the active roster.

EDGE Mason Reiger and S Cole Wisniewski were temporarily elevated from the practice squad to the roster.

Note: teams are permitted to temporarily elevate up to two practice squad players per game. A player can be elevated up to three times in the regular season and an unlimited number of times in the postseason.

Let's sort through this news on a player-by-player basis.

The Eagles previously temporarily elevated Hall for their Week 11 game against the Pittsburgh Steelers. Despite playing just eight snaps on defense, Hall logged one sack (potentially making him the last player to ever sack Aaron Rodgers) and one tackle for loss.

One can wonder if Hall's performance caught attention from other teams, prompting the Eagles to put him on their roster so that he couldn't be poached from the practice squad.

The Eagles now have five defensive tackles on their roster: Jalen Carter, Moro Ojomo, Byron Young, Ty Robinson, and Hall. They also still have Leki Fotu and Zion Wilson on the practice squad.

With Arnold Ebiketie ruled out, the Eagles elevated Reiger to have more edge rusher depth. Reiger will likely only play on special teams, just like he did the last time the team temporarily elevated him. He has one remaining elevation this season, as does fellow rookie edge defender Keyshawn James-Newby.

This marks Wisniewski's third and final elevation for the 2026 season. He's set to contribute on special teams in addition to providing depth at safety.
