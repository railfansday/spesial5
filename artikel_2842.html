JJ Redick hopeful Austin Reaves will return in next two games

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

While the Lakers have been fairly calm about Austin Reaves' heel bruise injury, the team has also now gone over a week without getting him back on the court.

Head coach JJ Redick has stated there is no structural damage and it's a matter of pain tolerance, which is encouraging long-term. However, it must be a particularly painful injury because, while Reaves has gotten on the court in recent days, he still has, obviously, yet to return for a game.

Ahead of Friday's contest against the Kings, Redick provided an encouraging update on Reaves and a potential timeline for a return.

"Hopefully we'll get AR back [soon], we assume based on the progress he's made," Redick said. "He had a great day on the court today and we've made a lot of progress, I think, today…so, hopefully he'll be back in one of the next two games."

The Lakers have an extra day off before playing the Jazz again on Monday. Then, they have an extended break before kicking off a road trip in San Antonio.

Having him back for the game against the Spurs would be ideal, but even better would be getting him back on the court before then. Returning after two weeks out to play one of the best teams in the league is a tall task.

The best-case scenario is getting him on the floor for a run against the Jazz to get back to speed before a big measuring-stick game against the Spurs.

Either way, it feels like Reaves is very close to a return, which is great news.
