Lions sign LBs K.J. Britt, Joe Bachie to 53-man roster

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Lions have made a few roster moves at linebacker.

Detroit announced the club has signed K.J. Britt to the 53-man roster off of New England’s practice squad. The Lions have also signed Joe Bachie to the 53-man roster from their own practice squad.

Britt, 27, has appeared in one game this season. He played all 17 for Miami last year, recording 35 total tackles.

Bachie, 28, has appeared in two games for Detroit this year, playing exclusively on special teams.

Additionally, the Lions have signed linebacker Owen Pappoe to their practice squad.
