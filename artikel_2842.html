As Anthropic shares tank, investors scramble to hedge. It could be very expensive

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

As Anthropic shares tumble toward their IPO price of $83, investors are looking for ways to hedge. Unfortunately, they may have to wait until Monday, when options on the AI giant are expected to list.

If Anthropic breaks its IPO price, it will represent a 27% decline from Thursday's intraday high of $113. The wild price swings offer clues on how expensive it might be to buy protection on the new tech giant once options begin trading.A 20% intraday move points to an implied volatility somewhere between 100 and 150 for Anthropic options, according to estimates from single-stock market-makers on the Cboe trading floor in Chicago.

Options traders are also looking at how SpaceX traded in the days following its offering for clues on how options might price.

Implied volatility in SpaceX started at 116, but that didn't deter options traders big and small from piling into the contracts tracking the stock, with about 1.7 million contracts traded on the first day for a total options premium of about $2.5 billion. Far out-of-the-money call contracts were some of the most popular trades as the stock soared 40% in its first three sessions.One key difference between now and then? Anthropic is trading against the backdrop of S&P 500 volatility close to 30, compared to 17.3 when SpaceX debuted. "I expect ANT options to be super active," said Tom Sosnoff, co-founder of the trading platforms ThinkOrSwim and TastyTrade. "I expect heavy options volume."One thing Anthropic and SpaceX have in common is heavy retail involvement in both IPOs, with retail brokerages receiving a 20% allocation from SpaceX and reports pointing to between a 15-20% allocation from Anthropic on Thursday. "Round one of the Anthropic IPO easily goes to the individual investor," Sosnoff added. "Retail participants bailed early at the best prices of the day while institutional investors paid up to top off their underwhelming allocations. I believe retail was aggressively reloading in the mid-90s."Further signs of retail demand will become evident when options likely begin trading on Monday. One thing to watch: the spread between out-of-the-money call and put contracts, which was heavily skewed toward calls in the case of SpaceX. "I would be careful comparing Anthropic to SpaceX," said Dennis Davitt, CIO and Co-Founder of Millbank Dartmoor Portsmouth. "From a market-structure perspective, the two are dramatically different. The availability and cost of borrowing will be different, the companies are at very different stages of maturity, and SpaceX has allowed employees to monetize portions of their holdings over time. All of those factors matter when pricing options."
