3 Wide Receivers Rams Should Target After Failing to Make Deadline Trade

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Los Angeles Rams, much to fans' chagrin, did not make a deadline deal for a wide receiver despite injuries to Puka Nacua and Devante Adams, leaving the team limited in options.

However, the Rams do have some free-agent options to add depth and stay afloat until they get healthy.

Here are the three best options for the Rams:

Hill is still one of the best receivers when healthy, but his devastating leg injury has him sidelined after the Miami Dolphins waived him.

He has been working out on his own and rehabbing diligently, and while there are questions about his cultural fit, the need for pass-catchers may outweigh other considerations.

His agent, Drew Rosenhaus, said Hill is hopeful he'll be medically cleared in the next few weeks.

If he does become available soon, the Rams will be sniffing around to see how much he costs and what his health is. With a win-now year in danger of being derailed before the postseason, the Rams may need to make a move for Hill.

Samuel has been a free agent all season long after getting released by the Buffalo Bills. He has failed to produce consistently despite his electric speed and dynamic yardage after the catch.

However, he can help offer some depth and an experienced pair of hands with more size than Tutu Atwell.

His speed means Sean McVay could get creative and use him as a gadget player, unlocking yards the team needs without their lead guys.

The Rams could use a physical receiver like Smith-Schuster, with a modern play-caller like McVay to scheme things for him.
