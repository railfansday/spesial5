Broncos vs. Raiders: How to watch, game time, TV schedule, game preview and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Denver Broncos took first place in the AFC West from the Kansas City Chiefs, but they took it back while Denver was relaxing on their bye week. They do control their destiny, however, so all they have to do is keep winning and that starts in Week 11 at home against the Las Vegas Raiders today.

From where I sit, it sure looks like the Raiders organization may finally be on the cusp of breaking a nearly three decade run of mediocrity — but the turnaround isn't quite there yet. They had a good start, but then began losing games and ultimately shipped Kirk Cousins off to Minnesota and decided to go all-in on their number one overall pick in Fernando Mendoza.

Mendoza's NFL career will decide the Raiders attempt to turn things around finally. If he is a bust, they'll be send back into the the realm they have dominated for decades: last place.

The thing about this game against the Denver Broncos is that the Raiders seem to know how to play them tough. Both games last season were decided by a total of 10 points. Bo Nix is a perfect 4-0 against them in his career, but before he came to town the Raiders had won eight straight and 10 out of the last 11. As fans, I am sure we'd all like to keep this fledgling win stream alive and extended another two games in 2026.
