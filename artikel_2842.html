Lakers’ rising star joins Kobe Bryant in team history after dropping 47

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Quentin Grimes lifted the Lakers with a big-name performance Tuesday night.

LA kicked off its four-game home stand with an overtime thriller against the Hornets and Grimes led the Lakers in scoring with a 47-point performance. The 26-year-old joined Kobe Bryant as the only players in Lakers franchise history with 10-plus three pointers made in a single game.

Quentin Grimes of the Los Angeles Lakers dribbles the ball against the Charlotte Hornets on Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026, at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

"It's an honor to wear this jersey and be mentioned with a guy like that," Grimes said.

There had been a lot of discourse surrounding the Lakers and the team's ability to take the lead, but not maintain it throughout the game. Against Charlotte, a different tale was told.

Quentin Grimes #5 and Carson Cooper #20 of the Los Angeles Lakers high five during the game against the Charlotte Hornets on Nov. 17, 2026 at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, California. NBAE via Getty Images

The Lakers rallied back from an 18-point deficit to win their ninth straight game. It was a gutsy performance led by Grimes, who dominated the floor on a night where the team was shorthanded with both Austin Reaves and Walker Kessler out.

The game-tying three from Grimes to force overtime caused a loud uproar throughout Crypto.com Arena.

Guard Luka Doncic #77 reacts after being called for a charging foul as guard Quentin Grimes #5, and forward Jake LaRavia #12 of the Los Angeles Lakers look on during the first half of a game against the Charlotte Hornets at Crypto.com Arena on Nov. 17, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. Getty Images

Luka Doncic also mustered 36 points, seven assists and six rebounds, despite struggling throughout the night on 13-of-34 shooting. But the Lakers remain in the win column thanks to a career-high performance by Grimes.

Entering Tuesday's game, the guard was off to a slow start this season but evidently he was ready to put on a show when the Lakers needed him most.
