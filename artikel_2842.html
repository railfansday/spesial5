The Western Conference stat that should have New York Knicks and Philadelphia 76ers terrified

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The New York Knicks and the Philadelphia 76ers face the daunting task of emerging from the Eastern Conference, but the challenge might be much bigger than they expected.

The 76ers have the best record in the Eastern Conference with 9-2, while the Knicks are the reigning and defending NBA champions, even though their 5-4 record isn't the strongest.

Both sides have a great chance to emerge from the East and head to the NBA Finals, but they might not like who they face on the other side.

For the first time since the NBA implemented conferences, the top five teams in the Western Conference are all on winning streaks of four or more games.

The Spurs are on an eight-game winning streak, the Warriors are on a seven-game streak, the Lakers are on a five-game streak, and the Wolves and Thunder are both on four-game winning streaks.

The Western Conference is stacked more than it has been in years past, with the top six all being considered possible contenders to win the NBA championship.

Players like Luka Doncic, Victor Wembanyama, and Stephen Curry are all making serious cases to win the MVP award this season with the way they've performed this season.

As for the Eastern Conference, only the 76ers are putting up a comparable record, as their 9-2 record would be good enough to be tied for second with the Thunder and Spurs in the West.

To get a good idea of how big the gulf is between the East and the West, we can take a look at the Knicks. The Knicks are fifth in the East with a 5-4 record.

That would be good enough for the 10th seed in the Western Conference, tied with the Denver Nuggets who have the same record.
