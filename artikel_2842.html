New York Jets vs. Buffalo Bills odds: Opening lines for Week 10 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The New York Jets lost their fourth straight game and seventh of their last eight on Sunday. The Jets are now 2-7 on the season. All playoff hopes have evaporated. In one and a half seasons under Aaron Glenn the Jets have a 5-21 record. We are reaching Kotite-ish levels of ineptitude.

This week the Jets visited the Kansas City Chiefs. They kept it competitve for a half. In the first half the Jets outgained the Chiefs. They won the turnover battle. They had more than twice the time of possession as the Chiefs. They scored on four out of five possessions. The Chiefs scored only twice. And still the Jets trailed at halftime, 14 – 12. It was an ill omen.

The second half was all Chiefs. They scored 21 unanswered points and cruised to a 35 – 12 blowout over the Jets. It was just one more humiliation in a decade filled with them.

The embarrassing Jets loss left the Jets with a 2-7 record to start the season, in 3rd place in the AFC East. Though mathematical elimination is still probably a month or so away, it has become obvious the Jets playoff drought will extend to 16 years and counting by season's end.

Now the Jets limp home to face the first place Buffalo Bills in East Rutherford, New Jersey next Sunday. The Bills have won three straight games and six of seven on the year. Josh Allen is playing like an MVP candidate, as usual. The Bills offense is humming, leading the NFL in points scored. The Bills defense has had some issues, but they held the Baltimore Ravens to 144 yards of offense and just 10 points last week. This will be a formidable challenge for a reeling Jets team just trying to stop the bleeding.

Let's take a look at the current odds for this game according to FanDuel Sportsbook. Unsurprisingly the oddsmakers think the Bills will easily handle the Jets. The current line has the Jets home underdogs by 8.5 points. The game has an over/under of 51.5 points. On the moneyline the Jets are +400 while the Bills are -520.

Any given Sunday and all that jazz, but an upset here seems like a bridge too far for the Jets. Perhaps they can keep the game competitive for a while as they did against the Chiefs for a half. Regardless, if the Jets don't exit next week with their eighth loss of the season it will be a major surprise.
