Pat Bryant practices in full, ready for Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Denver Broncos wide receiver Pat Bryant (ankle) practiced in full for the third straight day Friday, Nov. 6, and he is listed as questionable for the Week 9 game against the Carolina Panthers.

Why it Matters: Head coach Sean Payton said Bryant is expected to come off the reserve/injured list to play Sunday. Prior to his injury, he had at least two receptions, and 44 or more yards, in his two games prior to injury, while posting a TD. He can be a useful WR4 or WR5 in most fantasy leagues when he gets back up to speed.
