Knicks’ Josh Hart is the cover athlete of SLAM 265

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After an off-season full of celebrations and media appearances, the Knicks are back to being fully concentrated on their pursuit of another championship. Their fans aren't far behind them as they've reverted to dissecting every game as if it was game seven of the NBA Finals. Or better yet, life and death. But that hasn't stopped the media from capitalizing on the post championship hype surrounding the Knicks.

There's still plenty of odd articles and television segments covering the Knicks, their owner, their slow start, and extension-less stars. Jalen Brunson is just weeks removed from a Law & Order cameo. And reports surfaced earlier this week that the defending champions have been the most watched NBA team this season. But it won't end there.

It was announced on Tuesday morning that Josh Hart will be featured on the cover of the upcoming SLAM magazine issue. This is now the fifth time a Knick has been on the cover of the publication since May. OG Anunoby was the latest to dawn the cover in SLAM 263, with Brunson being featured on the cover of the Knicks' championship issue. And just a few weeks before that, the Knicks were on the cover of back-to-back issues featuring both the Eastern Conference Championship and Anunoby's game-winning tip-in.

"We sat down with JHart to talk winning, the sacrifices that come with it, putting your teammates first and what it really takes to be a champion," SLAM said.
