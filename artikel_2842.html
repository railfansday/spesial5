Mike Evans limited in his return to practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

49ers wide receiver Mike Evans was a limited participant in his return to practice Thursday.

He missed the past three games with Achilles tendinitis.

Evans has 27 catches for 420 yards and three touchdowns in six appearances this season.

Linebacker Dre Greenlaw (neck/rest) also returned to practice as a limited participant.

Running back Christian McCaffrey (rest), cornerback Renardo Green (back), safety Siran Neal (knee/foot) and left tackle Trent Williams (rest) were back as full participants. Neal and Green were limited Wednesday, while McCaffrey and Williams took veteran rest days.

Quarterback Mac Jones (illness) was downgraded to a non-participant after limited work Wednesday.

The 49ers added running back Jordan James (calf) as a limited participant.
