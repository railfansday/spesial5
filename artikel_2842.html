Pistons vs Spurs preview: No Duncan or Duren, others must step up

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Detroit Pistons are down four rotation players as they match up with Tobias Harris and the San Antonio Spurs. Harris has not made his season debut due to a nagging calf injury. That hasn't affected the Spurs in the slightest.

The absences of Duncan Robinson, Ron Holland, Isaiah Joe, and Jalen Duren surely make a difference for Detroit. The shooters will be extremely missed, with Victor Wembanyama's presence shrinking the floor.

Duren has some eye-popping moments against the MVP candidate, but we won't see that big man clash until late March, unless they line it up in the NBA cup Finals.

Where: Frost Bank Center, San Antonio, TX

There aren't too many games where Cade Cunningham doesn't step on the floor as the guy. He can be him in any matchup, but it will take a lot to top Wemby. Considering how small Detroit is currently, their hands are full with the 7-foot-4 Frenchman.

John Collins started at the 5 over the last three games. With Robinson out, don't be surprised if Paul Reed re-enters the starting group. That isn't a Goliath pairing, but some teams opt for as much size as possible when defending Wemby.

The "put the small on Wemby" philosophy hasn't been fruitful this year. Wemby is dunking everything. Per Basketball-Reference, 27 percent of his shots are slams. That's way up from 18 percent last year. Detroit could have success "forcing" him to shoot. He's shooting 32 percent on triples and 39 percent on all middies.

That's a tough ask. Detroit's defense came up short against Giannis Antetokounmpo and the Heat Saturday night. Giannis scored 24 points in the paint. That same type of shots-at-the-rim challenge is up tonight.

Here's a glass-half-full takeaway from Collins. He's made at least two 3s in each of the past three games. Yes, glass half full for sure. Plus, he is shooting 38 percent from 3 on the season. Teams haven't cared, though. They still treat him like a maybe shooter. San Antonio will absolutely dare him to shoot from the wings tonight.

Detroit needs the open ones. Collins has to start hitting enough for teams to feel they need to stay attached. The defensive rotations can get better. The rim protection will not get better. That's not who he is. He's been disappointing defensively and on the glass. The shooting has to be feared enough to aid the spacing — high percentages on low volume won't shift defenses.

The Spurs are the second-best defense in the league, only trailing the Minnesota Timberwolves. While Wemby suffocates you, the "smalls" relentlessly hound.

Stephon Castle and Dylan Harper are big guards who get after it. Castle especially is an All-Defense caliber guy. Cade plays to win, but he'll want to let loose against Castle, who gave him trouble in the second February game last year.

Cade was better in the first one and led Detroit to the dub. He'll try to do that again tonight with an undermanned roster.

Ausar Thompson will do some leading too, as he's had the ball in his hands a lot more. According to Databallr, here's his on-ball percentage by year:

Ausar got his first triple-double a few games ago. He was so involved, zipping up and down the floor. Detroit needs all of that and then some tonight. He has to stay on the floor.

In the dawg category, Daniss Jenkins is in the 100th percentile. He steps up whenever Detroit is counted out. No Robinson, so he'll surely start. The Spurs don't allow many 3s, so Jenkins needs to be ready to knock down when he gets his chances.

Jenkins creates a lot of chances with his I don't care who's guarding me mentality, so expect him to get them up, with the paint being limited.

You can't fault his philosophy either. Jenkins is shooting 42 percent on five 3s a game. He makes them count. Detroit will need every ounce of his fearlessness as they travel to San Antonio. The Spurs are winners of 12 in a row. Detroit will rarely be nine-point underdogs. Let's see how they come out in this position.

Detroit Pistons (8-4): Cade Cunningham, Daniss Jenkins, Ausar Thompson, John Collins, Paul Reed

San Antonio Spurs (12-2): De'Aaron Fox, Dylan Harper, Stephon Castle, Devin Vassell, Victor Wembanyama
