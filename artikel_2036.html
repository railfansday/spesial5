Cardinale urges Milan fans to ‘come together’ to win: “It has to be everybody”

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Yesterday evening, Gerry Cardinale was stopped by Carlo Pellegatti for an impromptu interview and he urged the AC Milan fans to ‘come together’.

Between the two defeats in the Europa League and the three draws in the league, it has been a so-so start to the season for Milan. In that sense, tonight’s derby against Inter could act as a catalyst for a good period, as a win would take them within four points of the Nerazzurri.

Ruben Amorim knows it and the players know it, so it will be very interesting to see if we will get a good response tonight or another disappointing performance. The pressure is certainly on, even if the support from the fans will be amazing too.

Speaking to Carlo Pellegatti last night in what was an impromptu interview, Cardinale urged the Milan fans to ‘come together’ as winning isn’t just about the players performing well. It needs to be a perfect ecosystem in which everyone plays their part.

“Listen, we have to come together. If we want to win, it has to be everybody. The fans, us, the press, anybody who supports AC Milan. Let’s come together and all make this possible. Ruben is doing a great job, I made the right decision on Ruben.

“I watched him today at Milanello and he really cares about his training, his managing, his coaching, and his mentoring. I met with every single one of our players and they want to win. It’s only a matter of time.

“You either want to win or you don’t want to win. You can’t fake that. I want to win, the players want to win and Amorim wants to win. I’m telling you, that I got right. Now, football is up and down, but I promise you that they want to win,” he stated.
