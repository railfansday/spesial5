Celtic ‘disaster’ fuels Hearts trophy quest as Jambos’ Mr Versatile names ex-Liverpool hero inspiring dream

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Hearts’ versatile attacker Rogers Mato says the Tynecastle dressing room is determined to end the club’s 14-year wait for a major trophy after seeing the title slip through their fingers in the final minutes of last season.

Mato, 23, played just three league games for Hearts after arriving on an initial loan from North Macedonian outfit FK Vardar last January, but has featured regularly for the Gorgie side this season after making his move permanent in the summer by penning a three-year contract in July.

Originally signed as a wide forward, the Ugandan international was deployed as a left-back against Turkish outfit Trabzonspor in the Europa League earlier this month, with Hearts’ injury crisis seeing Mato pitted against former Liverpool icon, Mo Salah. However, he admits it’s another ex-Anfield hero, Senegal international Sadio Mane, that he takes inspiration from as he looks ahead to the African Cup of Nations in his homeland next summer.

“I grew up just watching the other players on TV and sneaking in the stadiums,” Mato told RG. “I didn't even have a chance to be like a ball boy to give them the balls in the national team. I only did that on the club level. But wearing the national team jersey is a big responsibility, we're representing our families, our dream, and millions of Ugandans.

“Mane is my all-time favourite African player. I would love to wear the captain's armband. I would love to lead my country. We grow by responsibilities, not by age. So, that would have a massive impact and would be a massive confidence booster for me to be leading the nation to glory.”

The 23-year-old says he is using the pain of defeat to fuel another of his major dreams - bringing silverware at Tynecastle. Refusing to concede his side are out of the title race this season despite being 11 points behind league leaders Celtic, he admits the crushing way that a first top flight title win since 1960 trophy was ripped from their grasp by two late Hoops goal on the final day last season still stings.

“It's been ups and downs [since joining Hearts],” he admits. “Coming from Macedonia, playing 90-plus minutes and then coming on for Hearts and playing for 13 minutes in three games and 13 minutes in the second half of the season. It's been ups and downs, but with patience, everything will come to an end.

“I just had to come here and wait for my time, but every time we just have to be patient. I needed to work hard on myself, work hard for the team, so I can get more game time. It's all about mentality. You have to adapt to any coach that comes your way. If you don't adapt, you're dead as a footballer. Last season was hectic and brutal for the team, for the fans. Those last minutes [vs Celtic], it was a heartbreak for everyone, for every Hearts fan. It was a total disaster.

“But we're looking forward to give it all this season. But suddenly we got a lot of injuries here and there, but those who are still fit, we're trying our best to still keep [the title race] alive for our friends, our brothers who are injured, so they can come and join us [when they're back]. Because we can't let it down, we still have to keep the hopes for the first place."
