Rangers have no intention of selling left-back Tuur Rommens any time soon, sources have told Football Insider.

The Belgian is currently behind James Penrice in the pecking order and has started just two games in the Scottish Premiership so far this season.

"All expectations" at Ibrox are that Penrice's loan move from AEK Athens will be made permanent, which could potentially leave Rommens playing second fiddle.

However, sources say that Derek McInnes still views him as a valuable part of the project at Ibrox, and still expects him to play a significant part long-term.

Rommens has faced an injury-hit start to the season, and is currently focusing on working his way back towards full fitness and match sharpness.

He started in the recent Premier Sports Cup semi-final victory over Aberdeen, and there are no doubts surrounding his future at the moment.

Rangers have steadily transformed their squad across the last few transfer windows, and Rommens made a big impact when he arrived last January.

The 23-year-old quickly became one of the first names on the teamsheet for the Gers, and also established himself as a firm favourite among the fans.

It has been a totally different story for him this time around, but the club are not prepared to make a snap decision to cash in on him.

McInnes still has significant plans for the former Westerlo ace as the campaign wears on, and he is set to compete with Penrice for the left-back spot.

Any injuries in the defence are expected to see Rommens slot straight into the backline, which will then give him the chance to impress once again.

Rangers are keen to avoid carrying unnecessary depth in their squad long-term, but Rommens is not someone who they are looking to offload any time soon.
