Rafa Benitez has been talking about making a return to the Premier League.

The Spanish manager having previously been in charge at Liverpool, Chelsea, Newcastle United and Everton.

Rafa Benitez revealing that he has turned down a number of national team jobs in recent months, as well as clubs in both Mexico and Brazil.

The one-time Real Madrid boss telling The Athletic that now his priority is a Premier League job next.

Now 66, Rafa Benitez was sacked by Everton in 2022 and since then has only managed Celta Vigo and Panathinaikos.

The former Newcastle United manager lasting only nine months in Spain and eight months in Greece before getting the sack both times.

Rafa Benitez explaining to The Athletic: “For me, the priority is the Premier League. After you go Italy, Spain, Germany and France. In general, the Premier League has the most money and is able to buy the best players. You have some fantastic teams in Spain and Italy, but I like the atmosphere in the Premier League. I’m not just watching games; I’m analysing with a tactical vision, trying to learn something new which I could implement. I don’t just want to be in a job; I want to be sure I can compete for something — whether that’s staying in the division, qualifying for Europe or winning something. I want to stay in Europe. Maybe I’ll have to go to China but that’s not the idea. There will come a point where if you cannot get what you want, you have to make a decision.”

Rafa Benitez on how his future club would ideally look: “It has to be somewhere they respect your vision and experience…Are you creating something for the future? Is everyone on the same page? You need a clear vision and communication across the club, and time to improve a team. When you have had success in different countries, when you’ve had to handle difficult situations, when you know the mentality of players, you can manage in a different way and get the best out of them. Young people, especially, don’t attach too much value to experience, but after all these years, I’m a better coach than I’ve ever been.”

During the interview, Rafa Benitez talked about his previous Premier League jobs and said about his time at Newcastle United under Mike Ashley: “We achieved something very difficult. We made a £30m profit (on player transfers) and won the Championship. Then we finished 10th and 13th in the Premier League without spending big money. That was a great achievement.”
