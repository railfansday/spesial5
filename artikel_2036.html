'When you look at the video...' - Ex-refs chief shares Cole Palmer penalty verdict after re-analysing Chelsea vs Sunderland footage

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Cole Palmer and Chelsea should have been awarded a penalty in the first half of Sunday's 1-0 defeat to Sunderland at the Stadium of Light.

That is according to former head of PGMOL and FIFA referee, Keith Hackett, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The incident came mid-way through the first half just minutes before Levi Colwill was adjudged to have brought down Brian Brobbey in the penalty area.

Enzo Le Fee converted the spot kick and put Sunderland ahead, with Chelsea unable to fight their way back into the game.

Xabi Alonso felt that his team should have had an earlier penalty, however, with Palmer felled by Luke O'Nien as he looked to drive into the box.

Jarred Gillett initially ignored the incident, with VAR Nick Hopton eventually deeming that the contact came outside the penalty box.

Alonso will feel that Chelsea had the worst of it by VAR in Saturday's game, and rightly so.

Sunderland's penalty was an extremely harsh decision, with Brobbey clearly having hold of Levi Colwill's shirt before he went down.

And Palmer was clearly fouled, with Gillett inexplicably failing to even award a free-kick.

VAR did determine that O'Nien had gone through the back and fouled Palmer, but was happy to confirm that contact took place outside of the box.

Replays have called that decision into question, however, with the challenge seemingly arriving when the Chelsea man is on the line of the penalty box.

"When you look at the video, it appears that Cole Palmer has been fouled outside of the penalty area," Hackett told Football Insider.

"However, the still image shows that Palmer's foot is on the line. The line forms part of the penalty area, and therefore Chelsea are rather unfortunate not to be awarded a penalty kick."

While both controversial decisions on Sunday would have affected the game, there is little doubt that Chelsea did not deserve three points.

The Blues were bettered in every area, and were ultimately fortunate not to concede more.

"I haven’t seen Cole’s actions, but there could be a chance that it could be given," said Alonso after the game.

"We didn’t produce enough to deserve to win," he added, keen not to find excuses.
