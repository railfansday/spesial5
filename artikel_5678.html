NEW: Sierra Club Report Shows Data Centers’ Potential to Erase 15 Years of Climate Progress

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Despite Americans’ Disapproval, Data Centers Rush to Build Climate-Threatening Projects

Washington, D.C. — Sierra Club has released a new report that shows that without swift, critical guardrails, artificial intelligence data centers in the United States could undo 15 years of climate progress.

The power demand brought on by data centers combined with utility companies’ rush to fossil fuels has severe and threatening climate impacts. The Artificial Intelligence, Real Pollution report shows that between data centers connected to the electrical grid and those that are building their own on-site power supply, these projects could add 680 million tons of greenhouse gas emissions into the atmosphere.

Sierra Club’s Dirty Truth Report, released in September 2026, reflected the alarming rate at which utility companies are building new methane gas power plants to power artificial intelligence data centers. Today’s report shows that additionally, data center projects are building massive amounts of on-site power sources that are subject to more lenient guardrails and reporting standards.

This report comes on the heels of the 2026 midterm elections, in which voters across the country overwhelmingly supported candidates who promised more guardrails and accountability on data center projects. Voters also passed nearly every ballot measure that would slow the breakneck speed at which data centers are developing. Artificial Intelligence, Real Pollution does not just outline the real climate risk that the industry’s current growth trajectory poses, it shares substantive and impactful strategies that states can implement right now to prevent harmful emissions from gas plants while protecting ratepayers from rising costs caused by data center demand.

In response, Sierra Club Chief Program Officer Holly Bender issued the following response:

“For twenty years, the Sierra Club has led the environmental movement’s most successful campaign—the Beyond Coal Campaign—with a clear-eyed vision that a clean power grid is the backbone of any climate strategy. Millions of people have contributed to this work, advocating for clean air, clean water, and investments in clean energy across the country. We face an unprecedented risk to our hard-won progress, as the picture of the climate impact of artificial intelligence data centers and their power demand becomes clear. We now know that as currently planned, this industry could put all of our progress at risk.

“Artificial Intelligence, Real Pollution shows the staggering amount of greenhouse gases that would be emitted into our air if we allow data centers and utility companies to continue their rush to fossil fuels. More pollution will worsen the extreme weather families are facing, cause illnesses, and drive up the cost of healthcare and disaster recovery. Just two weeks ago, Americans unequivocally demanded more accountability, more transparency, and more guardrails on AI data centers. With this report, the Sierra Club has not only clarified what’s at stake, but shared a menu of strategies that can be implemented right now to protect consumers, communities, and the climate.”

The Sierra Club is America’s largest and most influential grassroots environmental organization, with millions of members and supporters. In addition to protecting every person's right to get outdoors and access the healing power of nature, the Sierra Club works to promote clean energy, safeguard the health of our communities, protect wildlife, and preserve our remaining wild places through grassroots activism, public education, lobbying, and legal action.
