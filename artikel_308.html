Black Friday streaming deals 2026: The best deals live now and what to realistically expect

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

It's been yet another rough year in the streaming world. Many of our favorite streamers raised costs once again, including Peacock, Apple TV, Disney+, Hulu, Netflix, ESPN, and Paramount+. Bank accounts everywhere are pleading for some relief, and this year's Black Friday streaming deals are slowly rolling in to deliver it.

Black Friday is famously the best time of year to sign up for streaming services. Typically, we see some serious savings on monthly or annual subscription fees. And so far, it appears that this year is no different. I highly recommend taking advantage of these prices if you're looking to diversify your streaming this season, as we're unlikely to see discounts this steep for a while.

We're still expecting more streaming deals to roll in throughout Cyber Week. But since Amazon's official Black Friday sale kicked off last week, its add-on subscription deals are now live (and some of them are very good). Roku also joined in on the fun with subscription deals of its own.

I'm keeping a watchful eye on all the heavy hitters and will update this list as any other noteworthy streaming deals go live. In the meantime, here are all the best Black Friday streaming deals live.

HBO Max's Black Friday deal is top dog once again in the streaming world. This year, you can secure an entire year of streaming for half the usual cost. That drops it down to just $5.49 per month or $65.88 total for the entire year. Better yet, the offer is available as a standalone deal, a Prime Video add-on, or a Roku subscription.

Yes, you'll have to watch with ads, but for the price, it's easy to look past that. With a Basic subscription, you also won't get access to live sports. Everything else is yours to enjoy — including recent box office gems like Backrooms, Wuthering Heights, and The Drama, as well as HBO Max's many excellent original series like The Pitt, The Comeback, A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, and Industry.

Paramount+ offers two plans: Paramount+ Essential, which includes the standard Paramount+ library with ads for $8.99 per month, and Paramount+ Premium, which removes ads and includes the additional Showtime library for $13.99 per month.

The new Black Friday deal (which is available as a standalone deal or add-on) drops both plans to $2.99 for the first two months. So there's really no reason to not go with Paramount+ Premium. You save 67% with the Essential deal, compared to almost 80% with the Premium deal. You’ll gain access to hit shows like Yellowjackets and Tulsa King with Paramount+ Premium, and you won’t have to deal with ads. Not to mention, with the Premium subscription, you'll also unlock access to Showtime in its entirety and your local CBS channel live. It's a no-brainer.

Apple TV's outstanding library is well worth the subscription, even if you cancel after the six-month promotional period. It's stacked with Emmy-winning TV series like Widow's Bay, Pluribus, and Severance, as well as Oscar-winning and nominated films, including Coda, F1: The Movie, and Killers of the Flower Moon.

I'm going to hold your hand when I say this: most streaming deals aren't going to blow you away. In fact, over the last few years, Black Friday streaming deals have gotten significantly less impressive. Still, any and every discount is welcome in a time where prices keep rising.

Last year, the Disney+ and Hulu bundle Black Friday deal was one of my favorites at $4.99/month for 12 months. I expect to see something similar this year, but likely a couple of dollars more. And before you ask, yes. It will be the ad-supported version.

Peacock used to offer the best Black Friday streaming deal around, but last year it gave us nothing. And that was after raising costs from $7.99 to $10.99 per month. This year, the price jumped up to $12.99 per month, and I'm not holding out hope to see any kind of deal.

Netflix has never offered a Black Friday discount, or any kind of discount for that matter. I don't expect this year to be any different.
