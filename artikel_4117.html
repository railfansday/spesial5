GTA 6 Mystery Sparks Over Strange Symbols Near Manifest Canyon

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Even before GTA 5 hit store shelves, fans were speculating about Mount Chiliad, starting a years-long mystery that would intensify at launch when players spotted a mural showing secret chambers beneath the surface, with crude drawings showing an all-seeing eye, a stickman with a jetpack, UFO, and a cracked egg.

Players spent years squabbling over what it meant until eventually, a former Rockstar Games employee flat out told us. The egg was a hint that you need to 100% the game, the jetpack was telling you to fly as high as you could into the sky, and the UFO was alluding to the alien saucers you would see if you did both. Now, with GTA 6 firmly in hand (or at least, the code), fans are convinced they've found the beginnings of another Easter Egg hunt.

Though GTA Online would later add an underground Mount Chiliad facility and jetpack.

Reddit user u/akspree spotted a few strange symbols at a monument near Manifest Canyon, with a placard dedicated to "Leonidan Supremacy."

Upon approaching the memorial, their control began to vibrate, and the statue itself started beeping in what could be Morse code. "Looking around at the four stallions circling the statue itself, they each have different symbols on their respective podiums & heads (with one even wearing a traffic cone)," OP explained. "Even more strange, there is a paragraph written on the statue talking about 'Leonidafication.' I have no idea what this means or where to even begin starting to solve this."

It's not as overt as a map of a mountain with secret chambers, UFOs, and jetpacks, but beeps that could represent Morse code, strange symbols, and the promise of 'Leonidafication' are all conspiratorial enough to raise an eyebrow. It's at the very least not a run-of-the-mill monument.

You might wanna keep Jason and Lucia away from the fursuits.

"Just as America leads the world, so too does Leonida lead America," the placard reads. "Built in 1982 this statue commemorates our state's god-given place at the vanguard of human civilization. The four majestic stallions represent the cardinal components of Leonidan culture. As enshrined in our state's noble constitution wildlife, corruption, humanity and yeyo. The pillar and globe symbolize inevitable worldwide Leonidafication, whereby every person, no matter how geographically remote or culturally inferior, will eventually embrace, embody, and evoke these values, and one day call themselves a proud Leonidan."

It almost sounds like the murmurs of a long-forgotten cult, ready to turn everyone into a Floridian. Nobody is quite sure what the symbols mean, and theories are currently slim, but I'm sure as the millions of players pour in today, we'll start to see speculation ramp up as the search begins. Hopefully, this time the answer isn't hidden away in a GTA Online update. Then again, Rockstar could be messing with people. That's what designer ben Hinchcliffe thought he Mount Chiliad mystery was, after all.

The most anticipated video game of all time is a triumph on every level that exceeds years of unbelievable expectations. You're not ready for Leonida.
