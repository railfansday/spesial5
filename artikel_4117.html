It seems that Ruaridh Mollica is hopeful for a Young Avengers movie

Ruaridh Mollica is absolutely down for a Young Avengers movie... especially after the big reveal in the final episode of VisionQuest.

Warning: there are massive spoilers for VisionQuest episode 8 ahead!

In the season finale of VisionQuest, Tommy finally reunites with his brother Billy – and it all becomes a very sweet family reunion. After Vision saves the world from a vengeful Ultron (again), Billy appears and is pulled in for a rather heartwarming group hug.

"Do the words 'young' and 'Avengers' mean anything to you?" Iman Vellani asked Mollica during a special VisionQuest episode of the Marvel podcast she hosts with Frank Alvarez.

"They mean a dream I would love to come true," he replied.

Now that Tommy aka Speed has arrived, and has finally teamed up with Billy aka Wiccan, this means the following comic book-accurate Young Avengers members are currently in the MCU: Speed, Wiccan, Kamala Khan aka Ms. Marvel, Kate Bishop, Cassie Lang, Skaar, Lisa Molinari, and America Chavez. I think those are enough characters for a standalone movie, wouldn't you say?

There is also the fact that the extra post-credits scene at the end of VisionQuest implies that Tommy and Billy could be in Avengers: Doomsday, as the scene sees Wanda resurrected by Doom... but seemingly put under his spell. There's a Young Avengers comic book arc where Wanda goes missing, loses her memory, and becomes Doom's bride. But we'll just have to see on December 18.
