Lea Michele Wants a <i><em>Scream Queens </em></i>Reboot, Too

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The star of Kiehl’s new “Gift Like a Local” campaign also reflects on the legacy of Glee.

With multiple projects on the horizon, all eyes are on Lea Michele. Glee is getting a reboot; fans are begging for Scream Queens to come back, and the actress and singer is about to embark on a tour with Chess co-star Aaron Tveit. She also just starred in Kiehl’s new “Gift Like a Local” campaign. In partnership with Uber, Michele traveled around to small businesses in New York City and doled out Kiehl’s products. The Glee star even picked most of the stops herself. “Kiehl’s had emailed me and they said, ‘We really want to feature some local businesses in this campaign. Can you send us some of your thoughts?’” she says. “It took me about 30 seconds. I had an email with 50 places.”

It’s a natural collaboration, as the Bronx-born Michele is the perfect shepherd for a curated NYC experience. In many ways, she breathes New York. She’s starred on Broadway multiple times, most recently in Chess, which just received a Grammy nomination for Best Musical Theater Album. In the later seasons of Glee, her character, Rachel Berry, moved to New York to chase her Broadway dreams (Michele is also rumored to be returning to the show’s reboot). She currently lives in New York with her husband and two children. On the morning of our interview, fresh off a sold-out show with Tveit at the Beacon Theater, she’s having a classic New York morning. “I took my kids to school. I was walking in the sunshine, I had my matcha. I’m getting ready for an event right now,” she says. “It just is a great day.”

Below, Michele talks about Glee, her collaboration with Kiehl’s, and whether she’d ever come back for a Scream Queens reboot.

Aaron [Tveit] would love my personal favorite, the Grapefruit Body Cleanser. That’s just my tried-and-true. I used it this morning. If you’re feeling a little tired, it helps you to wake up and feel good. But even if you’re not, if you’re feeling like how I felt this morning, energized and happy, it’s just one of my favorite ways to start the day. Love, love, love their body lotion. It’s one of the only body lotions that I feel like actually works for me and helps to really moisturize my skin. They have all these amazing little kits as well over the holidays. I’ve given my dad Kiehl’s lip balm and eye cream in his stockings every year for the holidays.

I’m really excited to be a part of the Kiehl’s family now. I love our commercial that we shot and the fact that we had the opportunity to support some of the local small businesses.

I grew up here. I was born in the Bronx. My father owned a deli here in the city. We had ice cream parlors and candy shops. We featured Élan Flowers in my neighborhood. Seasons in Times Square is somewhere that I go all the time and buy flowers for people in Broadway shows. I love Onda, it’s an amazing little facial and wellness store in Tribeca. Obviously, so many little coffee shops. I mean, the list is quite long.

I just love everything white. I love all-white. My house is all white, but peonies are my favorite flowers. I love hydrangeas. I love white roses. I just love a big white bouquet of flowers.

[Aaron Tveit] and I just found out this morning that almost the entire tour sold out. [It] took less than 24 hours. At our show last night, I think we learned so much about what the show is and what the show could be. We had to cut 10 songs from the concert because we just have so much material. I don’t think that we’re going to do the same show every night. I think that each city is going to get their own show. We’re going to do some of what I would say are our greatest hits together, and some of our individual hits that I think that I will always do in concert, but I really just feel like there’s so much that we can do.

I want Radio City to be epic. We haven’t really talked about having some friends join us, but that’s also something that we could definitely do if we wanted to and bring out some friends to sing some songs with us. The options are endless.

[Aaron is] one of my dearest friends. His wife is one of my closest friends. He’s friends with my husband. Our children are friends. [It’s] amazing that I get to work with someone that I just respect and care about so much.

It feels awesome. Normally I know when these things are happening, but my publicist texted me in the morning. She was like, “You got a Grammy nomination.” I cried. My reaction was so, even for me, surprising and unexpected. I just broke down. My husband was walking our kids out the door to take them to school, and I was like, “I can’t believe this. We got nominated for a Grammy.” It means so much. I can’t tell you the talent of the cast of Chess. Every single cast member, the most extraordinary talent, our ensemble, the band. To have the cast get this incredible honor, it’s beyond well deserved. I think every single person who saw Chess would agree.

We are constantly one-upping each other in the wellness world. He’ll be like, “Have you seen this? Do you have this?” He had some sort of, I don’t know if it was an infrared mat, and I was like, “Okay, I need that. What’s that?” He saw that I had an amazing water filter in my house, and he’s like, “Okay, what’s that? I need that.” He is probably one of the only people who could rival me in my biohacking, wellness, and taking care of myself.

Most of the time during Chess, if we weren’t talking about Summer House and all of the drama, we were talking about what are all of the wellness things that we’re doing and to try to stay healthy.

I’ve been doing Pilates for years. Emma Roberts took me to my first Pilates class when we were living in New Orleans together, shooting Scream Queens. We went to Romney Studios and did a private lesson. I fell in love with it. Since then, I’ve done mat Pilates and hot Pilates and power Pilates. Everything. I felt pretty good going into this, being like, “I know what I need to know.”

It wasn’t my first time playing a killer on television, which was also kind of funny. It was so awesome to return to television last night after so many years, and I just had a really great time.

I met a little girl last night at the meet-and-greet. She was probably 7 years old, and she came to see the concert with her two dads. Her dads were like, “We just are starting to show her some of the musical numbers.” I was like, “Look at you, you’re a little Rachel Berry.” She had her headband on and her cute little penny loafers, and it was just the sweetest thing. What we all created, and what Ryan and Brad and Ian created with Glee, was just so amazing. The things that people say to me about how much it affected their life and brought them so much joy means the world to me.

I hear stories like that from people all the time, and I know that it really means so much to all of us.

The love for Scream Queens has always been there. Emma [Roberts] and I love that show. We talk about it all the time. Ryan knows, too. Ryan and I talk about it always and I beg him all the time. I’m like, “Can we please bring Scream Queens back?” Ryan should do it. It would be awesome.

It’s so beautiful. I talked a lot about Spring Awakening during my concert last night. We are celebrating 20 years of Spring Awakening opening on Broadway this December. We have a group chat with the original cast. Myself, Lauren Pritchard, and Jonathan Groff are planning a really special private night just for the original cast, a little celebration. I’m so excited that it’s coming to Off-Broadway. My dear, dear, dear sister, Lilli Cooper, is going to be in it. Spring Awakening is such a beautiful and important show, and so anytime that it has the opportunity for people to see it, it should be there. It’s the most beautiful piece of art.

Oh, yeah. I’ve had the opportunity and the real honor to be a part of some incredible original Broadway productions, both with Ragtime and Spring Awakening. It’s an incredible, incredible show.

There are so many. I want to do everything, I really do. I want to do it all. If I am lucky enough to perform on Broadway for the rest of my life, then that would just be the greatest dream. There are so many great shows that have been on Broadway that could be revived and amazing pieces and incredibly talented artists that are writing original pieces as well. So, I’m ready and available for whatever is to come.
