Everything We Know About Jennifer Lawrence and Emma Stone’s Miss Piggy Movie

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A new leading role is on the horizon for Miss Piggy. Half a century after her on-screen debut, the Muppets’ most glamorous star is set to headline her first solo film, with Jennifer Lawrence, Emma Stone, and Cole Escola bringing the project to life.

The film is moving forward, at least on the page. During a Nov. 19, 2026 appearance on Watch What Happens Live with Andy Cohen, Lawrence offered a rare update on the project when a fan asked about its progress. “I don’t know, I always get in trouble for saying stuff,” she joked, before revealing, “We have a first draft!”

During a November 2025 appearance on Las Culturistas, Lawrence revealed that she and Stone are developing a Miss Piggy movie for Disney, with Oh, Mary! playwright and Tony Award winner Escola attached as the screenwriter. It marks the first collaboration between the two Oscar-winning actresses and the first time Miss Piggy will take center stage in her own feature.

“I don’t know if I can announce this, but I’m just going to,” Lawrence said on the podcast. “Emma Stone and I are producing a Miss Piggy movie and Cole is writing it.” When asked if she and Stone plan to appear on-screen, she added, “I think so. We have to.”

Plot details haven’t been shared yet, but the project is in early development at Disney, which owns the Muppets franchise.

While the storyline is still under wraps, the pairing of Escola’s wit with Miss Piggy’s flair for dramatics suggests a distinctly self-aware take on the beloved character could be coming.

Lawrence and Stone will produce the film, marking the first time the two have worked together professionally.

Since first appearing on-screen in The Muppet’s Wizard of Oz in 1974, Miss Piggy has been one of the franchise’s most recognizable characters, serving as an enduring symbol of self-confidence, ambition, and unapologetic glamour. Despite starring in multiple ensemble Muppet films, she’s never led a movie of her own until now.

The new project follows a recent wave of reimagined icons from Barbie to Wicked that has brought nostalgic characters back with modern points of view.

Ariana Grande hasn’t confirmed yet, but she has an invitation. In an interview with Entertainment Tonight at the 2026 Golden Globes, Lawrence addressed speculation that the Wicked actress would be in the film.

After a reporter told Lawrence that Grande was asked about the project earlier in the night, she responded that it was “the first time [Grande] was hearing” about a potential role. Lawrence added, “I guess it was one of those Twitter rumors. They’re good at those.”

But Lawrence seemed on board with Grande’s involvement, though she didn’t say which part the “7 Rings” singer would be cast in. “I mean, if she wants to be in the movie, I would love for her to be in the movie. Please be in Miss Piggy,” she said.
