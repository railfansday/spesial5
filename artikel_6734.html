Prince William and George Go on a Fun Father-Son Date to Barcelona

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The duo cheered on their favorite football club, Aston Villa, at a UEFA Champions League match

Prince William and Prince George love a football match. The royal father-son duo has made it somewhat of a tradition to travel together to cheer on their favorite football club, Aston Villa. Last April, they went from Paris to Birmingham, England, within days to watch their players in the quarter finals. And yesterday, they watched Aston Villa go up against FC Barcelona at the Spanish team’s home base, Spotify Camp Nou in Barcelona.

The royals coordinated in cool, easy game-day looks for the UEFA Champions League match. William traded his go-to navy suit for a navy windbreaker and a black baseball cap. Meanwhile, his eldest son looked so grown up in a black Aston Villa x Adidas track jacket.

Growing up, most of William’s friends supported powerhouse football clubs like Manchester United or Chelsea, but the royal has said he chose to put his faith on a smaller, less popular team—and Aston Villa won him over early. Now, the Prince of Wales has passed his allegiance to his children, particularly 13-year-old George, who has been going to football matches and big sporting events with his parents since he was a little kid (see this throwback from 2021).

“I’m kind of hoping they’ll all find their own teams in time,” William once said. “They don’t all have to be Villa fans. I’m trying not to be biased, but obviously they see how passionate I am about it and they watch the matches with me.”

Wills and George’s fun reunion came as the teenager is on his half-term break from Eton College, the prestigious boarding school in Berkshire that both his dad, William, and uncle Prince Harry previously attended.
