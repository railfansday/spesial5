What Really Happened Between Sarah and John That Caused Their Divorce After ‘Love Is Blind’? A Much-Needed and Very Shocking Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Love Is Blind Season 11 didn’t exactly go as planned for most of the engaged couples, with pretty much everyone breaking up before saying “I do.” But one pair managed to pull through! Sarah and John exchanged vows and got married (legally, I might add) during the Love Is Blind season finale.

“You are beautiful, you are brilliant, you are thoughtful, and I love you,” John said in his vows, while Sarah gushed “I love that you are sensitive and considerate and I find you funny.” Honestly, it was sweet!

The thing is...this finale was filmed over a year ago. Meaning, we’re in desperate need of an update on where Sarah and John stand today. Enter: the reunion! Netflix filmed the Love Is Blind reunion pretty recently, and there’s a shocking update on Sarah and John.

During an early reunion viewing on Tudum, John revealed he had an emotional affair about a year into the marriage, which led to their separation. "Unfortunately Sarah and I are no longer together, which is my fault,” John said. “We had a wonderful year of marriage, the two of us. Shared a lot of memories together. We had a wonderful year of marriage together, but unfortunately I made a mistake and there was a major breach of trust on my part. It's hard to talk about. There was a boys’ night I went on with a bunch of my friends and I just acted un-husbandly.”

He added that “nothing physical happened," and when pressed said “I texted one of the people we met out that night, and nothing happened, but she saw the texts and they were inappropriate for me to be sending as her husband. It was like three [texts], but they were bad.”

As for Sarah, she said the situation was “very sad,” that she'd struggled with “how John spoke to me” in the marriage, and that his behavior on the “boys’ night” was the "final thing that made me want to leave."

We don’t have information on where Sarah and John stand circa this moment, but it seems safe to assume they aren’t about to get back together given how the relationship ended.
