Jacob Elordi Tries to Hide a Major Hair Change During 2 NYC Dates With Kendall Jenner

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Kendall Jenner and Jacob Elordi spent their weekend in New York City, where they were photographed at Paranormal Activity on Broadway on Saturday and cozied up outside in stylish fall ensembles on Sunday. The Australian actor drew extra attention for how he tried to conceal a major hair change, though. During both outings, Elordi wore a beanie. He added a hoodie to his look for extra coverage on Sunday.

But he couldn’t keep his dye job completely out of sight. A bit of blond hair peeked out of the back of his hat in photos that surfaced of him talking with Jenner at Paranormal Activity. Elordi hasn’t shared why he went blond himself or the full look. He is, however, starring in Outer Dark with Lily-Rose Depp. In May 2025, Deadline reported that the dark fairy-tale film was “being lined up to shoot in 2026.”

Jenner was first romantically linked to the Australian actor this spring. In July, a source told Entertainment Tonight that she was falling for him. “Kendall has been telling her closest friends that she’s in love with Jacob,” the insider said. “She feels like Jacob is everything she’s been looking for, and the past few months have felt like something out of a movie. She’s happier than she’s been in a long time. He brings out a side of her that even surprises her.”

“With him, everything feels different,” the source shared. “He’s thoughtful in all the little ways. He’s always carrying her bag, opening doors, and making sure she feels taken care of.”
