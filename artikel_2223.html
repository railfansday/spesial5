Julien Stephan's major QPR, Richard Kone boost confirmed pre-Lincoln City

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

QPR boss Julien Stephan has received a new boost following his side's frustrating 1-1 draw with Burnley involving the fitness of Richard Kone, who was initially hauled off against the Clarets.

The Hoops would fail to return to winning ways at home to Burnley after Saturday's bruising 4-1 away defeat in Southampton over the weekend, with the two results now leaving Stephan's men positioned in 11th.

14 games have now been played and QPR, for the large part, have appeared capable of challenging for a play-off spot, albeit contending with both the inconsistent form and difficult injury luck for star performers that has plagued the West Londoners in recent campaigns.

Much to the relief of QPR, though, one injury boost has now emerged in preparation for their return to action against Lincoln City at Loftus Road this weekend, swiftly allaying fears among the club's fanbase.

History looked to be repeating itself during the second-half of QPR's draw with the Clarets when Kone, who has battled through some testing injury setbacks already this term, was forced off in visible discomfort just before the hour-mark before heading down the tunnel instead of joining his teammates on the substitutes bench.

The Ivorian forward had been moving awkwardly and, after picking up a string of injuries in both pre-season and the early stages of the campaign, it was feared that another spell on the sidelines was looming as Rayan Kolli came on in his absence.

However, a new update from the London Evening Standard has revealed a major boost for the Championship play-off aspirants by confirming that Kone made it through Burnley's visit unscathed and will be available to face off against Lincoln on Saturday afternoon.

Perhaps, then, it was merely a precaution from Stephan, and the Frenchman will know that he has to carefully manage Kone's fitness in order to keep QPR's leading goalscorer firing on all cylinders.

QPR will be keeping a close eye on the situation involving Kieran Morgan's Bradford City game time as January approaches

It will now only add to the Hoops' confidence of getting back on winning terms against a Lincoln side who have lost each of their last two encounters against Birmingham City and Watford and currently sit six points shy of their next opponents in 18th position.

Kone's availability for the visit of the Imps represents positive news for QPR, who will feel even more optimistic of their chances against last season's League One title winners with his firepower to call upon if he builds on his most recent performance.

The 23-year-old has contended with some difficult months in West London and endured a stop-start introduction to the campaign blighted by injury and fitness issues coupled with a loss of form, although he now appears to have rediscovered his goalscoring boots with devastating effect.

Kone finally opened his account for the season just two minutes into QPR's shock 5-0 rout of promotion hopefuls Swansea City towards the end of last month. By the fifteenth minute, of course, the ex-Wycombe Wanderers sensation had scored a history-making hattrick, and even set up Jimmy Dunne's second of the afternoon to wrap up an unprecedented five-star display at Loftus Road.

It's disappointing that QPR have fallen short of building on that momentum, although Kone did find the back of the net with a little over 20 minutes to spare in the aforementioned defeat away to Southampton before looking visibly off the pace when Burnley came to town.

Either way, Kone's clean bill of health is a significant boost for a club often accustomed to dealing with unfortunate injury setbacks to key performers, and perhaps nobody in Stephan's camp is more important than the top-scoring forward.

He is, after all, his side's highest goalscorer with four strikes from nine appearances, six starts and just 471 minutes of Championship football, working out to 0.76 goals per 90 minutes at this moment in time.

Kone did, however, fail to maintain his white-hot goalscoring streak against Fabio Celestini's outfit, and a largely-anonymous individual display saw the talismanic frontman register just 24 touches, merely four in the opposition box and not a single shot on goal before behind hauled off ahead of Jonathan Varane's all-important equaliser six minutes later.

He wasn't able to get himself into valuable shooting positions, leaving QPR supporters to slam the striker's positioning, movement and even fitness.

Visibly off the pace, this one fan joked how Kone's fitness resembled that of a veteran striker in the form of Burnley's Jamie Vardy, citing the need to build up greater physical conditioning and workload management.

Kone's athleticism and technical ability came in for criticism from another fan in a scathing assessment of the Hoops' attacking options, which always appear strong on paper and are undeniably talented, but left the home crowd frustrated against a relegation-threatened Burnley side.

The same punter was eager for Kone's substitution before he was dragged off by Stephan, with wingers Koki Saito and Harvey Vale having also earned his ire.

And one supporter was particularly vocal about his criticisms of Kone, slamming the ex-Wycombe starlet's movement, position and technique in a damning verdict.

All in all, it remains difficult to deny the importance of Kone's fitness being intact for QPR's next match at home to Lincoln, as his scoring rate in limited minutes shows just how vital he can be under Stephan when operating at full throttle.
