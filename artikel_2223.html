Sheffield Wednesday team news and predicted XI as Dave Challinor takes charge vs Oldham Athletic

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Sheffield Wednesday travel to the North West for their first game under Dave Challinor‘s management. An FA Cup first round clash against Oldham Athletic awaits SWFC, and the new boss will be eager to make a winning start.

Challinor is tasked with turning around some poor form of late as the Owls have gone four without a win, including consecutive draws under Matt Hamshaw’s temporary lead. Wednesday have a good history in this prestigious competition though, with 2021/22 the last time they got knocked out in the first round.

Oldham Athletic meanwhile sit 18th in League Two, so will be hoping for some cup joy. In their last game, Micky Mellon’s Latics won 1-0 against Northampton Town thanks to a second-half goal from Ollie Norburn.

Challinor has a handful of injury issues to deal with as he prepares to make his Sheffield Wednesday bow. Before the game, the new boss confirmed the Owls will be without Liam Cooper, Joe Lumley and Ali Al-Hamadi as their recent absences continue.

Cooper is a precautionary call, while Lumley and Al-Hamadi are expected to step up their returns next week.

Elsewhere, Billy Mitchell and Reece James are set to miss out, as are long-term absentees Louie Barry and Tyler Onynago. Sil Swinkels is suspended after his sending off against Blackpool, but Jamal Lowe will be available for selection having served his ban.

In better news, Di’Shon Bernard has been deemed fit to return for SWFC. Challinor is wary that his minutes still need to be managed but the former Manchester United youngster’s availability serves as a welcome boost.

Centre-back Ricardo Santos returned off the bench against Blackpool and is set to be in contention again.

Maynard-Brewer; Bernard, Santos, Weaver; Valery, Heskey, Bannan, Lowe; Slattery, Keillor-Dunn; Burstow

Challinor has mainly played with a 3-4-2-1 in his managerial career to date, so that is what formation is anticipated in his first Sheffield Wednesday game. However, he has used varied three-back setups as well as a 4-2-3-1 in the past.

Lumley’s absence means Ashley Maynard-Brewer continues in goal. He will be guarded by Ernie Weaver, and the fit-again Bernard and Santos in the back three with Yan Valery and Max Lowe selected as right and left wing-backs.

The fairly limited midfield depth means SWFC will likely persist with Barry Bannan and Jaden Heskey in the middle. Callum Slattery continues in a more advanced role alongside Davis Keillor-Dunn, who has now scored four goals in 10 games for the club.

Mason Burstow continues at striker despite Lowe’s return. The experienced striker will be hoping to impact the game off the bench though, alongside the likes of Sean Fusire, Jordi Liongola, Kieran Morrison and Harry Gray.

Oldham Athletic vs Sheffield Wednesday kicks off at 17:15 on Sunday evening and will be broadcast on TNT Sports 1.
