Ex-Rangers star handed bumper new contract despite being injured for 10 months

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Former Rangers striker Hamza Igamane has been handed a bumper new contract at Lille...despite being injured for ten months.

The Moroccan international striker joined the Ligue 1 side last year in a £11 million deal after impressing for Rangers in his one season at the club.

He made an excellent start for Lille after scoring nine goals in 18 matches, but suffered a rupture of the anterior cruciate ligament in his right knee during the Africa Cup of Nations final against Senegal in January.
