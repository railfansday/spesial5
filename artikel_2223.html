Chelsea teenager Reggie Watson captains England and scores as Germany snatch victory with late comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Graduate Sports Journalist with a love for football.

Chelsea midfielder Reggie Watson scored while captaining England Under-17s on Sunday, although Germany’s late comeback ended his international window with a 3-2 defeat.

The 16-year-old equalised from the penalty spot after Oscar Johnson drew a foul inside the area.

According to England’s official match report, Watson beat goalkeeper Nick Hartung with a finish into the left-hand corner.

Mylo Bernard put England ahead on the hour, but Linus Güther equalised seven minutes later. Niko Ilicevic then struck the winner in the 83rd minute.

Barry Lewtas’ side finished the three-match tournament with two victories and one defeat, having previously beaten Israel and Venezuela.

Watson’s involvement offers Chelsea supporters another opportunity to track a teenager already introduced to senior football by Xabi Alonso.

As covered in our report on Watson’s first-team progress, he started the Carabao Cup victory over Leeds United and played the opening 45 minutes.

That appearance, aged 16 years and 226 days, placed him fourth among Chelsea’s youngest competitive debutants.

His England captaincy and goal add a concrete international development to that emerging club story.
