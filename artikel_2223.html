Charlie Austin identifies 'big Jay Stansfield 'problem' at Birmingham City

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Birmingham City have been issued a warning involving Jay Stansfield ahead of their crucial away trip to Stoke City on Saturday afternoon by modern-day Championship goalscoring supremo, Charlie Austin.

Chris Davies' side had won six of their seven games between the final international breaks of 2026, with a 2-1 success against Swansea City at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park on November 7th sending them top ahead of Middlesbrough and the Potters on goal difference.

After finishing 10th in 2025/26, Davies oversaw a relatively quiet summer transfer window in comparison to the two previous years of his tenure, and such a decision has clearly paid off.

The main question concerning Blues right now is whether they have the capability to sustain their promotion challenge for the remaining 31 games, as well as how they seek to add further quality in the January transfer window.

Stansfield's exploits in B9 are often analysed due to the club-record fee Birmingham parted with to sign the forward on a seven-year contract from Fulham in August 2024.

The 23-year-old has received mixed reviews over time, largely down to a decrease in output despite his overall importance within Davies' setup never being questioned.

His brace last time out saw him surpass 50 goals in all competitions for Blues, and is one of several difference-makers on show in Saturday's game at the Bet365 Stadium.

Discussing the clash with Stoke, Austin said: "The clubs that have been in those positions are wanting to get back. It's nice to see the usual names up there and performing.

"We're having this conversation in November. The way the Championship is, give it the week before Christmas and you could be at the opposite end.

"It's going to be a fascinating game," the former striker claimed. "The thing is with Birmingham, for me, they heavily rely on Jay Stansfield.

"That's my only issue with Birmingham. If anything happens to him, they're in big trouble."

Stansfield has been viewed as the figurehead of Knighthead's ambitious project at Birmingham, but many supporters are likely to disagree with Austin's claim on the England youth international.

In the past three seasons, the striker may have hit double-figures, emphasising his importance whilst also adjusting to his current 'number 10' role, but there certainly doesn't appear to be an over-reliance on his services anymore.

Birmingham have scored three or more goals in four of their last six encounters, with the goals very much shared around during that timeframe as the likes of Alex Cochrane, Max Bird, Liam Millar, Demarai Gray and August Priske have all netted as well as Stansfield.

Davies will be pleased with how well the attacker has led the line in the victories over Millwall and Swansea in Priske's absence, which, in itself, creates a selection headache ahead of the matchup with Mark Robins' side.

With Stoke and Birmingham level on points, even with 30 matches to play afterwards, the potential victors in North Staffordshire have the chance to use the game as a springboard for future success.

The Potters have only lost once since August, which came in a narrow 3-2 defeat to Middlesbrough on October 13th, although both of Blues' two defeats came on the road against Norwich City and Cardiff City.

As Austin alluded to, a fascinating contest lies in wait with both squads strong in defence and possessing attacking flair. Davies will be hoping Stansfield continues his impressive form whatever role he is assigned, whilst also needing his 10 teammates to step up to the plate as they have so often done.
