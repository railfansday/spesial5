‘Adds quality’: Loan manager keen to keep Derby County player

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The youngster is due to return to Derby County at the end of the month. DCFC have a decision to make about whether to let him stay in Yorkshire or to bring him back to Pride Park.

Allen, 19, first linked up with FC Halifax Town back in August and he has since made a positive impression with the National League side. He has made 12 appearances for the Shaymen in all competitions and he has chipped in with two goals and one assist.

His current manager McGrath, who played in the EFL for Doncaster Rovers and Burton Albion, has shared this update on his situation at The Shay, as per a report by the Halifax Courier: “Yeah, I’d love to keep Cruz as well. He adds quality, I think you saw that for the 20 minutes he came on on Saturday, he did really well, albeit in a deeper-lying role, which is something he can do.”

FC Halifax Town lost 2-0 to Cheltenham Town away in the FA Cup first round over the weekend. They are eyeing a place in the fifth tier play-offs this season.

Derby County will be keeping tabs on Allen’s development at FC Halifax Town. DCFC will be pleased with the amount of game time he is getting.

The teenager is highly thought of by the Rams and is being tipped for a bright future in the game. He is under contract until June 2028 meaning he has just under two years left to run down on his deal.

Allen, who is a Wales youth international, has been on the books of Derby County since joining them as a child from Burton Albion. He has since risen up through their academy and has been a regular at various different youth levels.

He was handed his first-team debut back in November 2023 at the age of just 16 against Wolves U21s in the EFL Trophy. The prospect has since made three more senior outings.

Allen is unlikely to get many minutes at Derby County right now. DCFC are scrapping for points as they aim to claw themselves out of the bottom three.

Therefore, the Rams should let him stay at FC Halifax Town until January. They can then reassess things later this winter and could even look at finding him a League Two team as the next step for his development.

In the meantime, John Eustace‘s side are back in Championship action after the international break against Southampton away. They won 3-1 at home against Bolton Wanderers last time out and needed that victory.
