Clean Port Coalition Urges EU To Accelerate Port Electrification

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Clean Port Coalition has written to the European Commission calling for ambitious EU rules to accelerate port electrification and shore power ahead of the AFIR review.

Ahead of the upcoming Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Regulation (AFIR) review, the Clean Port Coalition (CPC), an alliance of industry, civil society, and maritime and energy stakeholders, has sent a joint letter to the European Commission calling for ambitious rules to scale up port electrification and shore power.
