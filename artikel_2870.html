Open Thread: Enter to win the Classic on the Court Giveaway

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

This season the Spurs unveiled their City Edition jerseys with a throwback video:

The reveal, which featured Spurs 2025 draftees Carter Bryant and Dylan Harper, starts in an animated form while the voice of Spurs legend George "The Iceman" Gervin narrates. The duo, who played in the NBA Finals as rookies, eventually appear in place of the animation.

The spot goes on to show Bryant and harper pulled the iconic red, white, and blue ABA balls from the trunk of a classic lowrider, eventually showcasing this years jersey.

Now you can enter to win the Classic on the Court Giveaway.

Per a Spurs press release:"Straight from the HemisFair Arena days, a Spurs classic could be yours! Enter the Classic on the Court Giveaway pres. by Ledger for your chance to win a Classic Edition jersey inspired by the original home uniforms worn in downtown San Antonio, plus tickets and more."Prize Includes:🎟️(2) tickets to Spurs vs. Pacers on Jan. 6th👕 Classic Edition jersey🏀 ABA basketball🔐 Ledger device

Sean Elliott has promoted the idea that the Spurs should play with the ABA tri-colored ball when they host games in the City Edition jerseys. Perhaps he will get his wish.
