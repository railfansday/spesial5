Brandon Williams’ hamstring strain lingers, will miss at least two more games

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Brandon Williams will miss at least 5 games here in this early part of the season as the Golden State Warriors announced this morning that he'll be reevaluated again on Sunday before the team hosts the Boston Celtics in primetime.

Of course, it's good news that he's making "good progress," and it certainly sounds like the team is penciling him to return for that Celtics game, but that's scant good given the heap of bad the team and fans have been staring at for the entire season. To recap:

Draymond Green is questionable for tonight's game against the Mavericks

Al Horford is questionable for tonight's game

Steph Curry is out indefinitely with an oblique abdominal tear

Kristaps Porzingis is out probably forever with chronic medical issues

Moses Moody is out until probably January

Jimmy Butler III is out until probably February

On a per capita basis, the Warriors are suffering from a 49ers-level of injuries, the key difference being that there is no 49ers equivalent of Wardell Stephen Curry II.

The team figures to lose triples digits to double digits for the foreseeable future, as spirited as the depth players were to start the season and turn an 0-3 start into an 8-3 run. I'm sure Brandon Williams would've loved to ahve been ready in time for tonigt's game against is former team, too; but, the sooner e returns, the better in terms of stanching the bleeding. In that scenario, the Warriors might get to 10 wins before 20 losses.
