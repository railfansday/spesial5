Rams playoff picture sunnier than forecast six weeks ago amid win streak

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Rams have spent the better part of the past two months clawing their way out of a hole. Now they're back in the NFC playoff picture, and the view is considerably better than anyone could have imagined six weeks ago.

With Wednesday night's 26-13 victory over the Packers at SoFi Stadium, the Rams improved to 7-4, extended their winning streak to five games and potentially seized the seventh and final NFC wild-card spot.

Byron Young (0) of the Los Angeles Rams celebrates sacking Jordan Love (10) of the Green Bay Packers on Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026, in Inglewood, Calif. Getty Images

Equally important, Los Angeles secured the head-to-head tiebreaker over Green Bay (6-5), giving the Rams the advantage should the teams finish with identical records in a two-team tiebreaker.

But unfortunately there's no time to exhale.

The Seahawks (6-4) could reclaim the final playoff spot with a victory over the 49ers on Sunday at Levi's Stadium, leapfrogging Los Angeles by virtue of their superior division record.

The Rams should root for San Francisco this weekend, even if doing so comes at the expense of their division title aspirations. A 49ers victory would give San Francisco a commanding 3 ½-game lead over Los Angeles in the NFC West heading into their Week 14 showdown.

Considering San Francisco's more favorable remaining schedule, the wild-card route remains the Rams' most realistic avenue into January.

The defending champion Seahawks hope to reach the playoffs. Getty Images

However, simply being satisfied with the seventh seed shouldn't be the extent of their ambitions.

The Rams are just a half-game behind the Vikings (7-3) and one game behind the Lions (8-3) following Chicago's last-second Thanksgiving victory over Detroit.

The Detroit Lions lost a Thanksgiving Day heartbreaker but still are in good position for the playoffs. Getty Images

A Falcons upset of Minnesota on Sunday, coupled with a Seattle loss, would vault Los Angeles into the sixth seed thanks to its superior conference record.

Neither Minnesota nor Detroit has an easy road ahead, either. The Vikings still have to navigate games against the Panthers, Patriots, Lions, Commanders and first-place Bears. Detroit's remaining schedule features Atlanta, Minnesota, the NFC East-leading Giants, Chicago and Green Bay.

The NFC North could cannibalize itself over the next six weeks, opening the door for three NFC West teams to reach the postseason for the second consecutive season.

Of course, the Rams can eliminate much of the scoreboard watching by handling their business.

The Vikings are in the thick of the NFC playoff picture. Icon Sportswire via Getty Images

Despite facing the NFL's toughest remaining strength of schedule, three games stand above the rest in terms of playoff importance: the Rams' Week 14 rematch with San Francisco and two meetings with Seattle, beginning on Christmas Day at Lumen Field and concluding in the regular-season finale at SoFi Stadium.

Winning all three would guarantee Los Angeles at least 10 victories, secure the head-to-head tiebreaker over Seattle and neutralize San Francisco's head-to-head advantage.

Even if the Rams lose to the Chiefs on Thursday, victories over the 49ers, Seahawks twice, Cowboys and Buccaneers would leave them at 12-5, potentially good enough for the NFC's fifth seed.

And that distinction should be what the Rams are shooting for.

The fifth or sixth seed could send the Rams to face the winner of the NFC South or the Giants, whom Los Angeles has already beaten this season. Either scenario looks considerably more appetizing than opening the playoffs on the road against Chicago, Detroit or San Francisco.

For an injury-riddled team that was practically being fitted for a toe tag at 2-4, the Rams suddenly have an opportunity to engineer a favorable postseason draw.

They don't necessarily need to run the table. They just need to win the games that matter most.

Do that and their dreams of playing in another Super Bowl inside SoFi Stadium are still alive.
