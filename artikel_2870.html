Kentucky vs. Missouri game discussion and pregame reading

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The final SEC game for the Kentucky Wildcats is here as Will Stein and his squad are in Columbia for a date with the Missouri Tigers. Game time is set for 7:30 PM ET on SEC Network.

Sitting at 7-3, Kentucky is ranked as the No. 16 team in the country and just a few spots off from making the College Football Playoff. A win over the Tigers could help propel the Cats closer to making the field, but the question is: Can the Cats actually knock off the Tigers?

Missouri has lost three of its last four and is coming off a similar two-game slide as Kentucky. Sitting at 6-4, the Tigers are going to be hoping to pick up a major win and spoil the postseason dreams once and for all for UK.

How can Kentucky avoid the upset? First and foremost, the offense needs to reappear. After scoring 20 points in two games, the offense disappeared when the team needed it most. Going to have to score to win this game tonight.

Secondly, the Wildcats have to stop the run. We saw Aaron Philo run all over the Cats last week, and while Austin Simmons isn't known for his legs, Jamal Roberts is one of the top running backs in the league, and Ahmad Hardy is getting healthier every week. Going to have to slow him down to make the Mizzou offense one-dimensional.

Should be a fun one tonight in CoMo. Let's hope the Cats can pick up the win.
