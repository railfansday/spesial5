Mansoor Delane will not play for the Chiefs on Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Jets will be down a receiver for Sunday’s game in Kansas City after ruling out Garrett Wilson and the Chiefs will be short a cornerback.

Head coach Andy Reid told reporters on Friday that Mansoor Delane will miss the game because of a shoulder injury. It will be the second game in a row that the first-round pick has missed, but he was able to practice on a limited basis this week.

Kristian Fulton and Nohl Williams started at corner in Delane’s absence last weekend.

Reid also said that defensive tackle Peter Woods will miss the matchup with the Jets. Woods, who has been out of practice with a neck injury, will miss a game for the first time this season.
