Yaxel Lendeborg, Paolo Banchero only NBA rookies this century to accomplish feat

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Yaxel Lendeborg, Paolo Banchero only NBA rookies this century to accomplish feat  originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

The Warriors are struggling without star guard Steph Curry and have lost four straight games to drop to 8-7 to begin the 2026-27 NBA season.

Rookie Yaxel Lendeborg continues to impress, though, despite scoring just 13 points with five rebounds and four assists in Golden State's 116-94 loss to the Dallas Mavericks on Wednesday night.

Lendeborg's performance this season puts him in rare company that only Orlando Magic star Paolo Banchero has reached. They are the only two rookies in the 21st century to register 300 or more points, 100 or more rebounds and 50 or more assists in their first 15 NBA games.

Lendeborg, the No. 11 overall pick in the 2026 NBA Draft, is averaging 20.3 points, 7.3 rebounds and 3.5 assists per game and quickly is proving to be a strong complementary star alongside Curry. The Warriors started the season 0-3, but went on an eight-game winning streak, largely fueled by the 24-year-old's play.

His early season success has put him in NBA Rookie of the Year conversations as he continues to climb up the ranks and is considered a heavy favorite, alongside Memphis Grizzlies rookie Cameron Boozer, to win the award.

Lendeborg and the Warriors face a quick turnaround after their loss to the Mavericks. The team travels to Memphis to take on the Grizzlies on Thursday, where he and Boozer will go head-to-head. Tip-off is at 5 p.m. PT at FedEx Forum.
