Jameson Williams scares Lions fans with front-flip touchdown following Aidan Hutchinson injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Whether Jameson Williams is carefree or careless is in the eye of the beholder.

One thing he is exceptional at is football, and while he gave Lions fans plenty to celebrate on Sunday, he probably scared the hell out of them, too.

The dynamic wide receiver hauled in a 57-yard touchdown pass from Jared Goff during the third quarter against the Buccaneers, flashing his burning speed in the process.

But to cross the goalline, Williams front flipped into the endzone, and didn't exactly stick the landing.

Detroit Lions wide receiver Jameson Williams (1) celebrates after scoring a touchdown against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in Detroit, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Rey Del Rio

The acrobatic celebration immediately drew anxious reactions from Lions fans, as well as the broadcast, who watched star defensive lineman Aidan Hutchinson suffer a season-ending torn ACL while celebrating a sack just one week earlier.

"Didn't hutch just blow out his knee for the season over celebrating ???" one fan wrote under ESPN's post of the touchdown. "You'd think Dan would tell them to chill on the excessive celebrations."

"Guarantee you every Lions fans heart stopped for a second on that landing," another fan wrote.

"Jameson has no sense of the situation. He's so funny," Barstool's Liam Blutman added.

The USA Today affiliate Lions Wire posted, "And every Lions fan had a temporary panic attack."

The Detroit Times posted, "NEVER DO THIS AGAIN JAMO

Hutchinson's injury came during Detroit's 35-10 victory over the Patriots in Munich, Germany on Nov. 15.

Detroit Lions wide receiver Jameson Williams (1) celebrates a touchdown against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in Detroit, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. AP Photo/Paul Sancya

After sacking quarterback Drake Maye, who also exited early with an ankle injury, Hutchinson jumped into the air, pumping his fist, and landed awkwardly on his right leg, tearing his ACL and ending his season.

The injury deprived Detroit, a Super Bowl contender, of one of its most important defensive players for the rest of 2026 and into 2027.

"I honestly can't believe it," Hutchinson wrote on his Instagram once the severity of his injury was confirmed. "I've jumped hundreds of times on a football field and never in a million years did I think this would happen to me."

The Lions cruised to a 31-21 win over the Bucs, with Williams having recorded four catches for 93 yards and the now-infamous touchdown celebration.
