The Chicken Shop Date final episode is a tear-worthy therapy session with Esther Perel

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

After 12 years of awkward silences, flirting, and fried chicken, Chicken Shop Date is officially closed.

The final episode of Amelia Dimoldenberg's YouTube series is now live, and for her last date, she sat down with Belgian psychotherapist Esther Perel. Dimoldenberg, called by the BBC "one of the U.K.'s best-known content creators," announced in September that the show was ending.

The show's final guest, Perel, is best known for her work on relationships and intimacy. So, given who's sitting across from her this episode, it's no surprise the conversation turns on Dimoldenberg and what it means to walk away from the show that made her.

"I can't stay here forever," Dimoldenberg says in tears near the end of the episode. "I need to leave the shop."
