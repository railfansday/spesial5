AFC standings after Week 10: 4 tiers of teams, including the Browns in the 2nd tier

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The AFC playoff standings will be finalized once the Baltimore Ravens face the winless Los Angeles Chargers on Monday Night Football, but the Cleveland Browns will exit Week 10 squarely in a playoff spot heading into their bye week. Are the Browns a great football team? Probably not, but they are young and winning football games despite their deficiencies, so that should be good enough in 2026.

Week 10's victory over the Houston Texans didn't lock up a playoff spot or eliminate the three-win visitors, but the result helped clarify the four different tiers of teams in the AFC after 10 weeks of the 2026 NFL season. These tiers are just an opinion and take into account a team's record, as well as their upside for the rest of the season, but not their NFL playoff hopes:

No one would be surprised if the Steelers or Ravens went on a positive run, but, in the same way, they, along with the Browns, have shown that they can play pretty poorly at times as well.

A big asterisk for the Patriots with QB Drake Maye injured in Week 10, with concern about how long he could be out. QB Tommy DeVito was not good at replacing Maye and does not have a strong history to fall back on.

Of the remaining teams with fewer than four wins, only the Texans made moves with this year in mind. Despite being 2.5 games out of the playoffs, anything can happen in the NFL, especially if they can get healthy, despite their current record.

The Ravens are hoping a little more time with new QB Jacoby Brissett will help them keep the ship afloat until Lamar Jackson is able to return, but Monday Night Football's loss to the previously winless Los Angeles Chargers was a poor start.

For the Raiders, can rookie QB Francisco Mendoza keep them in the playoff hunt, or did trading away Kirk Cousins signal the white flag being raised?

A lot of bad teams are losing a lot of games in this tier, with much closer to 0% chance of making the AFC playoffs than even a 10% chance, barring something wild.
