Report: Cardinals to sign Josh Johnson off Bengals practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Quarterback Josh Johnson’s long list of NFL teams is getting a little longer.

Adam Schefter of ESPN reports that the Cardinals will sign Johnson off of the Bengals’ practice squad. It will be Johnson’s first stint in Arizona, which makes them the 15th NFL team he has played for since entering the league with the Buccaneers in 2008.

The Cardinals are in need of quarterback health because Gardner Minshew is likely out for the season with an elbow injury. Rookie Carson Beck is the only other quarterback on the 53-man roster or practice squad.

Johnson did not appear in any games for the Bengals. He made two starts for Washington last season.
