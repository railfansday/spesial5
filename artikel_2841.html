Steelers’ Alex Highsmith misses practice with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Ahead of a short-week matchup against the Denver Broncos on Black Friday, the Steelers had just two non-participants in the team's estimated injury report on Tuesday.

Pittsburgh noted the practice was just a walkthrough, with the report being made as if it was a full practice.

After suffering an ankle injury against the Eagles on Sunday, outside linebacker Alex Highsmith was listed as a non-participant. Earlier in the week, Pittsburgh head coach Mike McCarthy said it would be "tough" for the outside linebacker to play versus Denver.

Also a non-participant was wide receiver Ben Skowronek. Skowronek was listed with a calf issue, the same designation he had when he missed several games earlier in the season.

Wide receiver Michael Pittman Jr. (foot) and guard Gennings Dunker (knee) both logged full practices again, but they remain on injured reserve in their 21-day return windows, which opened last week. McCarthy said earlier that he expects Pittman to play versus the Broncos.

Outside linebacker Nick Herbig, who missed Sunday's game against the Eagles with a hip/ribs injury he sustained against the Bengals in Week 10, was the Steelers' lone limited participant Tuesday.

The Broncos have yet to release their Week 12 injury report at the time of this writing.
