Big Ten receiving yardage leaders after Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Big Ten now has three wide receivers with at least 1,000 receiving yards after the results of Week 12. Joining Ohio State's Jeremiah Smith in the 1,000-yard club are Indiana's Charlie Becker and Illinois' Collin Dixon, who will basically be battling for second place behind Smith in the final weekend of the regular season.

While Smith is going to run away with the Big Ten's receiving title this season, he is in a bit more of a heated battle for the nation's lead in receiving yards. Smith will go into the final weekend of the regular season with 64-yard lead on Oregon State freshman receiver Jesse Legree. Smith also leads the nation in touchdown receptions with a 2-touchdown lead over Legree and FAU's Easton Messer.

Penn State's Chase Sowell is rounding out the top-10 this week among the Big Ten's top receivers. Sowell had 6 catches for 123 yards in a losing effort for Penn State against Rutgers in the home finale in Beaver Stadium. Sowell was held without a catch the previous week against Minnesota but his second-half performance has been one of the brighter spots for Penn State this season, which has otherwise been a disappointment.
