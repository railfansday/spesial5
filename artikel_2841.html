‘Toughest cover of my life’ – LA Rams’ Emmanuel Forbes picks the NFL player who is his hardest ever opponent

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Los Angeles Rams cornerback Emmanuel Forbes has named the player who was his hardest ever cover.

Emmanuel Forbes has picked a Philadelphia Eagles star as the wide receiver who caused him the most difficulty in his career.

Forbes told ESPN LA that Devonta Smith was his toughest ever opponent – but pointed back to when he played against him in college.

At the time Forbes was a freshman for Mississippi State facing Devonta Smith, who was a senior for Alabama.

Forbes explained: "If it's anywhere, I would probably go back to my freshman year of college when I had to guard DeVonta Smith in 2020, my freshman year, and the year he won the Heisman. That was the toughest cover of my life.

"One-on-one. Freshman, All-American, all this hype. He went out there and, yeah, welcome to college football."

Asked how it went, he responded: "Yeah, it was tough."

Alabama won the game 41-0 and Smith finished the game with 11 catches for 203 yards and four touchdowns.

Former Washington Commanders first-round pick Emmanuel Forbes is now establishing himself as an important part of an improving LA Rams team.

The Rams claimed Forbes off waivers after he was released by the Commanders in 2024, after struggling with injuries.

He has proved himself to coach Sean McVay, and he is happy being part of a defense that boasts Myles Garrett and Aaron Donald.

He added: "With Myles Garrett and Aaron Donald on the same D-line, on the field at the same time, and of course the other guys also, those two right there, they help the defensive backs so much.

"Like, we know we only have to cover for a certain [amount of] seconds because Myles is going to bend the edge and get to the quarterback in two seconds, if he doesn't get held. So they just make our job as defensive backs a lot easier."

The Rams are on a four-game winning streak, turning their season around from 2-4 to 6-4.
