Jaylen Brown spent the first ten seasons of his NBA career as a Boston Celtic. Alongside Jayson Tatum, the two combined for the most games and playoff wins of any duo during their nine-seasons together from 2017-2026.

Tonight, they meet for the first time as opponents on the NBA stage, and while there is so much history behind them, Brown is focused on the game.

"It's nothing to talk about. I mean, he's on his side, I'm on my side," said Brown, "Just playing basketball. Both of us are trying to win, so it's nothing to talk about. Just check the ball up."

The 2024 Finals MVP is currently second in scoring (22.3 points per game) behind Tyrese Maxey (26.9 ppg).

"Maxey's been leading the way, 100 percent," Brown said, via NBC Sports Philadelphia, on November 2 . "He's been here. This is his team. Him and Joel have been here for a while, so the mindset has been to complement those guys. I'm finding my footing, but Maxey has been leading the way for sure."

The 76ers, currently 9-2, lead the Eastern Conference. The franchise is seeking its first NBA title since 1983.

The Celtics are right behind the Sixers at No. 2 in the Eastern Conference. Tatum leads the team in scoring (27.1 ppg) and assists (7.8 apg).
