What The Clash? introduces its first story adventure in The Tennis Menace update

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

What the Clash? and others in the series from Triband like What the Golf? have all focused on the wackier, and more out-there kind of sports sims and party games that the developer can come up with. Now, they're introducing their most out-there update yet, as What the Clash? gets... an actual story?

Yes, in What the Clash?'s new The Tennis Menace update, the sinister computer that fans of What the Golf? will recognise has invaded the world of WTC. Seeking to steal all the tennis knowledge from Ping Pun's mind, it's up to you to foil the evil machine's plans.

Whether this is merely an experiment or the start of something new for What the Clash? I think it will depend on whether it's received well by fans. I think that making the single-player experience more consistent and exciting is a great aim for developer Triband either way. And more puns and jokes are sure to be a welcome addition.

Personally, I still have my eyes open for an Android release of What the Clash? But for Apple Arcade fans, this is undoubtedly one of many killer apps on the service. And the fact it's still receiving updates only makes it more worthwhile to play!
