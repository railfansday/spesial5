Dragon Ball Gekishin Squadra delays long-awaited major overhaul

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Dragon Ball Gekishin Squadra, the 4v4 brawler that sees you and your friends pitted against other players as characters from Akira Toriyama's iconic manga, has delayed its hotly anticipated overhaul update. While originally scheduled for the end of the year, the patch has now been pushed back to March of 2027.

Dragon Ball Gekishin Squadra's overhaul patch was set to make major changes, including a third-person camera perspective, simplified rulesets, and changes to both customisation and roles. While normal patches will keep rolling out, you'll have to wait a bit longer for the full overhaul to land.

I think part of Gekishin Squadra's struggle is that Dragon Ball has been experimenting with different genres for a few years now. Remember Dragon Ball: The Breakers and its oddball spin on the Dead by Daylight formula? While a 4v4 brawler is a little closer to what DB fans expect, it's still at odds with a series most often defined by epic one-on-one fights rather than group throwdowns.

With that in mind, Gekishin Squadra's overhaul does look set to address those concerns and turn it from a pseudo-MOBA to a proper third-person brawler. But whether it lands in time to win back the crowd is another matter entirely.
