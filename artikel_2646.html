Josh Jacobs not expecting to miss more time

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Green Bay Packers RB Josh Jacobs said he doesn't expect to miss any more games despite the additional allegations against him.

Why it Matters: Jacobs is averaging just 2.3 yards per carry in his two games since returning from the commissioner exempt list, but he has scored three total touchdowns and has 11 receptions. The rushing stats might not be pretty behind Green Bay's current offensive line, but Jacobs has the workload and touchdown potential to be in the RB2 conversation.
