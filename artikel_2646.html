Arizona Cardinals fans are excited to see Carson Beck in action

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year we ask questions of the most plugged-in Cardinals fans and fans across the country.

The Arizona Cardinals are starting Carson Beck against the Los Angeles Rams, with Myles Garrett back in tow, coming off a three sack game, with Elijah Wilkinson and Josh Fryar manning the tackle positions and Hayden Conner trying to block Aaron Donald.

That is why the Cardinals are in a curious spot. They traded away Jacoby Brissett, and then like normally happens when Gardner Minshew is the starting quarterback of an NFL team, he was injured.

So, now the Cardinals are going to Carson Beck, likely quicker than they had hoped, but fans are all on board, as 83% think there was no doubt Beck should start this week.

If things don't go well this week, we will see how the confidence in the direction of the franchise and what type of runway they give Mike LaFleur and Carson Beck, because it is still higher than expected after last week and another quarterback move.

However, the sharps are not on this move.

FanDuel Sportsbook doesn't buy the start of the Beck era, as they have the Cardinals over/under for points in this game at 14.5, and the money is on the under.
