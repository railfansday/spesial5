Aidan Hutchinson injury could be torn ACL, insider says. Latest on Lions edge rusher

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

When the Detroit Lions lost star edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson to a knee injury in the first quarter of their Week 10 victory over the New England Patriots, they might have lost him for the season.

Hutchinson's injury occurred on the first drive of the game after he sacked Patriots quarterback Drake Maye. The 26-year-old celebrated the sack by jumping into the air, but his right knee buckled as he landed.

Hutchinson immediately grabbed his knee and stayed down. He received medical attention on the field before walking to the sideline and eventually heading into the locker room.

Hutchinson was originally deemed questionable to return to the game but was later downgraded to out. Now, he will be staring down a potential lengthy absence as the Lions look to determine the severity of his injury.

Here's what to know about Hutchinson's injury and how long he will be out because of the malady.

ESPN and NFL Media insider Ian Rapoport reported on Monday, Nov. 16, that initial testing showed that Hutchinson likely tore his ACL. The edge-rusher is set to undergo additional tests in the United States after an initial MRI in Germany, but if the tear is confirmed, he will be out for the season.

The Lions have not yet announced an official update or a timeline for Hutchinson's return from his knee injury.

Lions coach Dan Campbell did not have an update about Hutchinson's status following the Lions vs. Patriots game in Munich. He said the star pass rusher would undergo further evaluation in the U.S.

While Campbell didn't have an update about Hutchinson's status, multiple reports from Sunday, Nov. 15 indicated the 26-year-old's injury was "feared" to be "significant".

The Lions have not yet announced whether Hutchinson will be able to return during the 2026 NFL season. If Rapoport's report of a torn ACL is accurate, the edge rusher would miss the remainder of the regular season.

Until Detroit provides more specifics about Hutchinson's injury or a definitive timeline for his return, the two-time Pro Bowler's status will remain uncertain.

Derrick Moore is listed as Hutchinson's backup on the Lions' depth chart. The second-round rookie from Michigan has racked up four sacks across nine games played thus far in 2026, including two sacks in Week 10 against the Patriots.

Moore (6-3, 260 pounds) had 10 sacks during his final year at Michigan in 2025 and will turn 24 on Dec. 6.
