Tank Dell estimated to be at full status for practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Houston Texans wide receiver Tank Dell (hand, shoulder) was estimated as a full participant for practice Monday, Nov. 16, after the team conducted a walkthrough. The injury report is only an estimation of a player's participation if there was a full practice, ahead of the Week 11 game Thursday, Nov. 19, with the Indianapolis Colts.

Why it Matters: Dell has been back from a major injury for three games now, and he is starting to become a larger part of the offense. He has seven receptions for 126 yards and a touchdown with one rushing attempt in the past two games, and he has been targeted 13 times. A lot of that production came against the LA Chargers in Week 10, including his touchdown. Treat him as more of a WR4 or flex fantasy play Thursday against the Colts.
