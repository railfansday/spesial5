Sam Darnold and Drew Lock share the moxie the Seahawks scout for

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Seattle Seahawks head coach Mike Macdonald shouted out the Seahawks' personnel department in a way that represented both of the franchise's starting quarterbacks, Sam Darnold and Drew Lock, in a positive light. While the outside world sees a quarterback competition between the two, the underrated story in the building follows how supportive they both truly seem to be for each other — and how much that's helped them reach their ceilings.

When looking back on all of Seahawks general manager John Schneider's quarterback acquisitions since he took over the helm of football operations in January 2010, you can notice a few trends. Darnold and Lock share some traits, but the answer at quarterback is rarely in their measurables. Those are just qualifiers — most of which don't work the same way at quarterback as they do at every other position.

What makes Darnold and Lock similar is how you describe them qualitatively. Let's compare Lance Zierlein's NFL.com predraft evaluations on both. Zierlein had a first-round grade and a Matthew Stafford comparison on Lock, calling his arm talent and size prototypical and his pocket movements "twitchy." Zierlein had Darnold graded as a "Top 3 overall selection," and an Andrew Luck comparison.

Luck is the gold standard for NFL quarterback prospects. ESPN's Mel Kiper Jr. said, "He's right there with John Elway as the best player I've ever graded." I think Seahawks observers consistently forget that that's who the most popular comparison on Darnold coming out of college was. As Zierlein said, "At the end of the day, Darnold has NFL size, arm strength, accuracy, pocket mobility, poise and field reading capability."

I think, at the end of the day, both are human beings the organization is willing to go to bat for. They get the team to rally around them. They both tick the boxes of size and arm talent and coachability, but they're just generally likable enough to be around and they can handle the pressure.

That's why Macdonald shouted out the personnel department. They did the hard vetting. They didn't reach for a quarterback coming out of college or in free agency until they found ones who could elevate their teammates. I call this moxie. The term "makeup" is also used by many scouts. That's what makes Seattle love both quarterbacks.
