Raiders vs Broncos Week 11 Broadcast Crew

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Las Vegas Raiders are hoping to end their two-game skid when they face their division rivals, the Denver Broncos, in Week 11. Defeating them is a challenge, though, because they will be facing a team that is allowing only 20.7 points per game, the ninth-best in the league.

To secure the Raiders' fifth victory in 11 games, rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza must apply the lessons he learned from Week 10 against the Seattle Seahawks. The Raiders defense must also exert more effort in stopping Bo Nix and the Broncos offense.

As for additional motivation, the Raiders lead the all-time series against the Broncos, 72-57-2. Can they earn their 73rd win against their AFC West foes? The CBS Sports broadcast crew will tell that story.

The trio of Andrew Catalon, Logan Ryan, and AJ Ross will be on the microphone for the Week 10 Raiders-Seahawks game.

Catalon will be the play-by-play announcer, and he has been with CBS since 2013. He also works for the network's college basketball and football coverage. Catalon has also covered the Olympics, the US Masters, the PGA Championship, and the PGA Tour for CBS.

Meanwhile, Ryan played as a defensive back for five teams in 11 NFL seasons. He was either a cornerback or a safety for the New England Patriots, Tennessee Titans, New York Giants, Tampa Bay Buccaneers, and San Francisco 49ers. In addition to covering NFL games as an analyst, he also appears on other shows like NFL IQ and NFL Offseason Playbook.

Finally, Ross will be the sideline reporter for the Week 11 Raiders-Broncos game. She has been with CBS Sports since 2018, and she also hosts the network's studio show, We Need to Talk.
