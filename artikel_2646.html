Bhayshul Tuten aggravates ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Jacksonville Jaguars running back Bhayshul Tuten (ankle) aggravated his ankle injury in the Week 11 game against the New York Giants. Head coach Liam Coem said Monday, Nov. 23, that it "wasn't too bad," and that Tuten had his ankle stepped on.

SOURCE: The Florida Times-Union - Ryan O'Halloran

Why it Matters: Tuten was limited to a season-low 10 carries for 33 yards, and he saw his three-game touchdown streak snapped. He also added three receptions for 20 yards on three targets. Watch his practice status Wednesday through Friday. It's a tough week, with games scattered all throughout, but it's a good idea to have an alternate ready just in case of a setback in practice.
