Perry Meridian sweeps Southport in girls and boys basketball rivalries

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

INDIANAPOLIS − The Battle of the Birds, one of the most electric and intense rivalries in Indiana high school basketball, took place on Tuesday, Nov. 24 at Southport High School, and the girls and boys games didn't disappoint.

The Perry Meridian girls slipped past Southport, 51-50, and the Perry Meridian boys toppled Southport, 64-42.

In front of a packed house, the Perry Meridian Lady Falcons won a nail-biter over the Lady Cardinals that went to the buzzer.

"I'm proud of the girls for fighting through," Perry Meridian coach Keith Stafford said. "We led a majority of the game and we got down a little bit in the second half, and I was just really proud of how they fought and that a 1-point win is good."

The Lady Falcons came out hot out of the jump, leading scorer Riley Hall, a sophomore, hitting two 3-pointers to start the game, setting the tone for the night. Hall finished with 23 points.

"It's not just me," Hall said. "It's my team. If they weren't passing to me, I wouldn't score, but overall pretty proud of how we did."

By the end of one quarter, the Lady Falcons were in control, leading 17-11.

In the second quarter, four Lady Cardinals helped fuel a 21-11 run, cutting Southport's deficit to 27-25.

Coming out of half, Perry Meridian senior captain Jassie Stiles got hot, scoring 7 of her 21 points in the third quarter as Perry Meridian clung to a 41-40 lead.

"I feel great," Stiles said. "I had a few not great games recently, so it felt good to hit some shots again."

Hall and Stiles combined for 9 points in the fourth quarter to pace the Lady Falcons to victory. Southport freshman big Amaya Cisse scored 5 points in the final minutes but it wasn't enough for the Lady Cardinals, who fell to 0-7.

On the boys side, Perry Meridian dismantled Southport, 64-42, its first win against the Cardinals since 2021.

"I'm really excited for our kids," Perry Meridian coach Mike Burris said. "Coming off a six-win season, some of these guys have won 16 [games] the last three years in the program, so just trying to change habits and the culture of our program."

The young, emerging Perry Meridian squad came out firing, hitting five 3-pointers in the first quarter for a 19-6 lead at the end of one.

"We wanted to come out here and punch them in the throat and that's what we did," sophomore guard Brecken Ponchot said. "We came out, hit [five] threes, and got off to a great start, and we did not want to let up."

The rest of the game was complete domination.

By the end of the first half, Southport was down, 30-15, and the game was in Perry Meridian's hands the rest of the way.

Senior Davonte' Rhodes and Ponchot, each of whom had 12 points, led the way for Perry Meridian.

"Coach told me before the game started, if I see open shots, shoot it, don't be scared," Rhodes said.

Coming out of the second half, Perry Meridian didn't step off the gas.

Southport senior Chris Cruz scored 12 points and gave the Cardinals a little bit of life in the third quarter, but Perry Meridian exploded for 21 points in the fourth quarter, sapping the energy out of Southport.

"We just have to keep playing like we just did today and just keep playing like we got something to prove each day," said Perry Meridian sophomore Riley New, who scored 11 points.
