Liverpool Defeat Adds Weight To Transfer Chase Of Star Tormentor

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Liverpool's dramatic defeat to Fenerbahce in midweek looks to have convinced the club to get their own back in the transfer market.

Andoni Iraola's men twice went ahead in Istanbul on Wednesday night but were ultimately beaten 3-2 to end their perfect Champions League record this season.

Goals from Mason Greenwood and Archie Brown cancelled out Trey Nyoni and Alexander Isak's, before a winner from Vedat Meriqi took the points.

Brown had a superb game at left-back for the Turkish giants, who were overseen temporarily by ex-Reds forward Dirk Kurt, and he is now reported to be firmly in the sights of the Anfield club as they look to strengthen that position for themselves.

For so long, how to approach the left-back spot at Liverpool was barely a question as Andy Robertson was the unquestioned first choice and, for the most part, consistently available.

But now the Scotland captain is a Tottenham player after his contract expired in the summer and the transition to ex-Bournemouth star Milos Kerkez hasn't gone as smoothly as hoped.

Long-term backup Kostas Tsimikas looks set to depart when his own deal runs out at the end of the campaign and the club are currently making plans to address the situation.

The Greek veteran spent last season on loan at Roma but since Iraola replaced Arne Slot in the summer he has largely chosen him over Kerkez as the preferred option in Europe.

TEAMtalk now report that there is interest in 24-year-old Brown, and after his star turn in midweek helped consign Liverpool to defeat they are tipped to respond by stepping up their pursuit.

As well as his goal, the former Derby County youngster also assisted the winner on Wednesday night and was otherwise dynamic down the left flank.

He is believed to want to test himself with a return to English football at some point in his career, having departed for Europe while he was still a teenager.

Brown's career has taken him to Swiss side Lausanne, Belgium's Gent, and now the Turkish Super Lig since leaving the UK, but there is reportedly widespread interest in bringing him back to England.

While Liverpool have been monitoring Brown as the potential answer to their left-back conundrum they appear to be far from alone, with AC Milan enquiring over a move last summer.

Premier League competition is strong, consisting of long-time Reds rivals Chelsea and Manchester United, while Newcastle, Merseyside neighbours Everton, and Brighton are said to all be monitoring the situation.

Brown, a former England Under-20 international, moved to Turkey from Gent at the start of last season and has made 54 appearances for the club, scoring eight goals and registering nine assists.
