Report: Chelsea still interested in Alajbegovic following Man United interest in McKennie

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Reports over the weekend claimed that Mauricio Pochettino is keen to bring Weston McKennie with him to Manchester United, and updates on Monday morning claim that Chelsea remain interested in Kerim Alajbegovic, who had been linked with the Blues during the summer transfer window.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, recently-appointed Manchester United coach Pochettino is interested in taking Juventus midfielder McKennie with him to Old Trafford.

The incredibly versatile midfielder has been a key player for the Argentine tactician during his time in charge of the  USA national team and also provides Luciano Spalletti with a number of different possibilities at club level as well.

The Pink Paper is also reporting that Chelsea remain interested in talented teenager Alajbegovic, who had been linked with a move to Stamford Bridge over the summer.

Chelsea had sought after Alajbegovic in the summer and had reportedly proposed a temporary loan move to partner club Strasbourg, but Juventus were eventually able to get a deal over the line with Bayer Leverkusen for a fee of €30m plus up to €5m in bonuses with the promise of regular first-team opportunities.

In the absence of Kenan Yildiz through injury over the first few months of the 2026-27 season, Alajbegovic has received opportunities in the Juventus first team that would have been significantly more difficult to come by with the Premier League club, particularly after splashing around €135m on Morgan Rogers in the same transfer window.

The Bosnian talent has shown glimpses of promise when given the opportunity, but is only six months into his career in Serie A and Juventus are well aware of the youngster’s talent, potential and future value.

As per La Gazzetta dello Sport, Juventus have no intention of selling either Alajbegovic or McKennie, particularly in January, although ‘nothing can be ruled out’ in the event that a monstrous offer arrives from one of the Premier League giants.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
