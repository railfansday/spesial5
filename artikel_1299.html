Man Utd 3-2 Roma: Michael Carrick’s Tense Moment With Matheus Cunha Explained

A journalist has revealed what Michael Carrick told an ‘unhappy’ Matheus Cunha after Manchester United's dramatic Champions League win over AS Roma.

On Tuesday night, United looked like they were heading for another dismal defeat when Matias Soule and Donyell Malen gave Roma a 2-0 lead at half-time.

After being booed off at the half-time whistle, United came out fighting, with Bruno Fernandes scoring from the spot before Cunha scored a late brace to seal a much-needed 3-2 win for Carrick.

In the latter stages, Cunha was sent off after being given a second yellow card, having previously been booked for going into the crowd to celebrate his winning goal.

As the full-time whistle went, Carrick appeared to have words with Cunha, who looked rather unimpressed as he was ushered away by a member of staff.

Taking to social media, journalist Chris Wheeler stated that Cunha was ‘unhappy’ and appeared to be told by Carrick to head into the dressing room.

Wheeler wrote on X: “Scenes of joy at the end here but match winner Cunha unhappy as Carrick appears to tell him to go straight to the dressing room following his red card.”

Meanwhile, fans on social media speculated that, due to Cunha’s red card, he was not allowed to re-enter the pitch. One user said: “Isn’t it a rule that a player sent off can’t come back out onto the pitch. Think he’s just trying to save him from further punishment.”

Another fan said: “It’s because Cunha is not allowed to be back on the pitch. He is doing him a favour.” A third fan added: “It's about the red card; red-carded players can't celebrate on the pitch.”

Speaking to Amazon Prime, via BBC Sport following the final whistle, summer signing Youri Tielemans stated that United players ‘kept working’ and ‘kept believing’ despite their bad form.

Manchester United recorded their first win in 10 matches with a much-needed 3-2 comeback success against Roma in the Champions League

He said: “We just kept working, we kept believing. We were on the front foot in the second half, we tried to push them into their goal and we succeeded.

“There has been a lot of pressure on him (Michael Carrick) and that's how we repay him, with a comeback like this, big character, big fight.

“At the end of the day we are on the pitch and we need to show what we are capable of and tonight, especially in the second half, we showed what we can do and we turned up the gears.

“Let's hope this is a good turnaround for us and let's hope we can build on this.”
