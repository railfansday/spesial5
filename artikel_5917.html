Far-left groups cheer Mamdani for disbanding elite NYPD protest team

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

‘The Five’ co-hosts comment on New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani’s move to disband the NYPD’s Strategic Response Group. Panelists criticize Mamdani’s decision to replace the tactical police unit with civilian-led conflict resolution teams.

Far-left radical groups, including some within tech tycoon Neville Roy Singham’s pro-CCP network, are cheering New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani’s decision to dissolve an elite NYPD unit responsible for handling protests.

After Mamdani announced Monday morning that he would disband the elite police department's Strategic Response Group, applause came in rapid succession from nonprofit organizations that regularly mobilize protests of ICE, Israel, capitalism and America in general.

"The mass movement has won the disbanding of NYPD’s Strategic Response Group — but the fight continues," the Party for Socialism and Liberation posted to X following the announcement.

New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani listens to reporters' questions during a press conference at Athens Square Park on Nov. 17, 2026, in New York City. The appearance came a day after Mamdani and NYPD Commissioner Jessica Tisch announced plans to disband the department's Strategic Response Group. (Michael M. Santiago/Getty Images)

"For everyone who has been kettled, beaten, pepper-sprayed or dragged onto a bus by the SRG, this is a real victory. But it was not handed down from City Hall. It was won by a mass movement that refused to let a campaign promise die quietly. The work of turning this victory into lasting change for working people is just beginning," the post added.

The Party for Socialism and Liberation is part of a protest network of activist groups tied to Singham, a Shanghai-based tech tycoon married to far-left CodePink co-founder Jodie Evans. A Fox News Digital analysis found that Singham funneled $278 million into six nonprofits, including $22.4 million to the People's Forum, the Manhattan organizing hub that works closely with the Party for Socialism and Liberation on protests.

The Party for Socialism and Liberation also shares leadership with Singham-funded groups. Claudia De la Cruz, a 2024 presidential candidate for the Party for Socialism and Liberation leader, was on the People's Forum's founding board alongside Evans.

Longtime organizer Brian Becker, a self-described communist, helped build the People's Forum into a base for the Party for Socialism and Liberation and the ANSWER Coalition, and his son, Ben Becker, is editor-in-chief of the Singham-funded BreakThrough News, which also shares a home at the People’s Forum headquarters on W. 37th Street.

By early morning, self-declared Marxist influencer and Twitch streamer Hasan Piker weighed in, saying he was "glad" the unit had been disbanded.

At 11:16 a.m., Piker posted a response to Mamdani’s message, skipping capitalizations as he wrote: "one of the hardest problems for NYC mayor is police reform. The SRG is a ‘counter terrorism’ unit that has primarily been deployed on protestors. glad the mayor is moving on this promise to disband."

HASAN PIKER NAMES PRO-CCP TYCOON SINGHAM AS FINANCIER OF 'POLITICAL MOVEMENTS' DESPITE NONPROFIT VENEER

By the afternoon, at 2:55 p.m., Nerdeen Kiswani, an anti-Israel activist who founded the organization Within Our Lifetime, responded to the announcement by calling for consequences for NYPD officers who subdued protests, while saying the disbanding is "overdue and welcome news" in a post on X.

Within Our Lifetime co-founder Nerdeen Kiswani speaks during a "Nakba 78" anti-Israel protest in Washington Square Park on May 15, 2026, in New York City, as demonstrators carrying Palestinian flags and Hamas flags gather behind her. (Asra Q. Nomani/Fox News Digital)

"The settlement gave us a way to challenge the NYPD when it violated protesters’ rights, and officers violated it anyway," Kiswani wrote. "The limits Mamdani announced today give us more specific rules to point to when we challenge police conduct. We’ll see whether the NYPD actually follows them when we’re back in the streets, especially when we’re protesting something the people in power would rather we stay quiet about."

Mamdani previously joined Kiswani in an anti-Israel protest, where calls to "globalize the intifada" rang out from the protesters in the crowd. Within Our Lifetime is not a registered nonprofit, so its tax filings and funding aren’t available to the public. Fox News Digital hasn’t identified any direct ties to the Singham network aside from joining member groups in demonstrations.

Mamdani’s announcement fulfilled a campaign promise he made to disband the group. While the Party for Socialism and Liberation says "the mass movement has won," it’s unclear whether the organization had any impact on the mayor’s decision.

MAMDANI GUTS ELITE NYPD TACTICAL UNIT, BACKS 'CIVILIAN-LED APPROACH' TO RESPONDING TO PROTESTS

Hasan Piker speaks at a press conference during day two of Web Summit Vancouver 2026 at Vancouver Convention Centre in Vancouver, Canada. (Sam Barnes/Web Summit via Sportsfile via Getty Images)

"New Yorkers exercising their constitutional right to protest should not be met with the same police response as a terrorism threat," Mamdani told reporters in a news conference announcing the changes. "We are establishing a new, civilian-led approach that prioritizes dialogue, engagement and de-escalation."

Former NYPD officers weighed in on Mamdani’s decision, saying the move would empower criminals and promote anarchy.

"I think it’s one more step towards emboldening the criminals, emboldening the anarchists and emboldening the terrorists," said James Gagliano, a retired FBI supervisory special agent and Fox News contributor who spent years working alongside the NYPD.

Protesters and counter-protesters gathered inside Washington Square Park in New York, New York, on Friday afternoon. (Rashid Umar Abbasi for Fox News Digital)

"New Yorkers exercising their constitutional right to protest should not be met with the same police response as a terrorism threat," Mamdani told reporters in a news conference announcing the changes. "We are establishing a new, civilian-led approach that prioritizes dialogue, engagement and de-escalation."

Mamdani said the disbanding of the NYPD unit would be replaced with split responsibility between a civilian-led division and new police units.
