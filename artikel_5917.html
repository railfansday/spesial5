Tuberville suggests Texans 'flee the woke-show' for Alabama after blue wave midterm elections

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Alabama Gov.-elect Tommy Tuberville invited Texans and businesses to relocate to his state, urging conservatives to "flee the woke-show" following recent Democratic election victories in the Lone Star State.

Alabama Sen. Tommy Tuberville, who is set to become the state's next governor in January, invited Texas residents and businesses to "flee the woke-show" and move to Alabama after historic Democratic gains in the Lone Star State during the midterm elections earlier this month.

"To any red-blooded patriot or business wanting to flee the woke-show that is coming to Texas… Alabama is open for business," Tuberville, a Republican, wrote Monday on X.

Tuberville was responding to a video shared by Libs of TikTok, an account run by a conservative influencer, that appears to show an event hosted by Equality Texas, a pro-LGBTQ+ advocacy group.

"Texas is Black. Texas is gay. Texas is trans. Texas is indigenous. Texas is lesbian. Texas is all of us," the speaker is heard saying in the clip.

"Never, never forget that the 'T' in Texas stands for trans," the speaker continued.

This comes after Democrats swept statewide races in Texas and flipped the governor’s office, a U.S. Senate seat and the Texas House, while Republicans managed to maintain control of the Texas Senate.

Tuberville's invitation comes after Democrats swept statewide races in Texas and flipped the governor’s office, a U.S. Senate seat and the Texas House. (Tom Williams/CQ-Roll Call, Inc via Getty Images)

Gov. Greg Abbott, who was vying for a fourth term, lost in his re-election bid to Democrat Gina Hinojosa, Lt. Gov. Dan Patrick lost his re-election to Democrat Vikki Goodwin and Texas Attorney General and U.S. Senate candidate Ken Paxton was defeated by Democrat James Talarico, contributing to huge Democratic gains in the state and across the country.

Democrats won every statewide race in Texas, bringing an end to decades of Republican control in contests that had once been considered sure bets for the GOP and leaving U.S. Sen. Ted Cruz as the only Republican holding statewide elected office.

With those key victories, Texas will be under Democratic control for the first time in more than three decades.

Democrats won every statewide race in Texas, bringing an end to decades of Republican control. (Brandon Bell/Getty Images)

Patrick acknowledged that Texas has "turned purple," while Cruz described the elections as a "blue tsunami."

Equality Texas endorsed Hinojosa, Talarico and other Democratic candidates down the ballot in the Lone Star State.

"We're looking cautiously optimistic to see what can be accomplished in this new legislative landscape," the group said earlier this month following the elections.
