Everything Announced at GOG’s First-Ever Showcase Event

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Covering the video games industry since 2017, with experience in news, articles, and reviews. Currently a writer at DualShockers and gaming magazines.

I know that I'm not the only one who has always appreciated GOG as a sacred sanctuary for classic PC titles, but its latest event proves the platform is doing much more than just keeping old games running.

That's because, to celebrate its 18th anniversary, the team held its first-ever showcase, and it was packed with updates that have me genuinely excited about where the platform is heading – and you should be too!

Broadcast today, Thursday, November 5, on YouTube, the first-ever GOG Showcase caught everyone's attention with a variety of announcements, ranging from new games and features to an entirely new publishing initiative.

For starters, co-founder Miho Kinski perfectly summed up the platform's philosophy at the beginning of the event, making it clear that its mission remains unchanged: "We wanted games to live forever. Not for a season, not until the servers shut down, but for as long as people want them to play." And honestly, that message really resonated throughout the presentation.

Then, the event kicked off by celebrating the GOG Patrons program, and the numbers are pretty impressive: more than 10,000 supporters have helped preserve over 300 games, ensuring they run flawlessly on modern systems with the help of more than 1,700 technical fixes.

Bit Kid shadowdrops Knytt Classic for free on Steam and GOG.

And speaking of games, the showcase also had plenty of nostalgia in store, with the announcement of Wing Commander Anniversary Editions. The series is returning in grand fashion with its 35th and 30th Anniversary collections, featuring both legacy and updated versions of the games. Oh, and if you already own these titles, the new editions will automatically appear in your library!

The expansion of GOG's classic game collection doesn't stop there, either. The original Road Rash has also officially joined the platform today, giving fans another opportunity to revisit this beloved classic.

However, perhaps the biggest surprise was the reveal of Retrovibe by GOG, a new publishing initiative focused on bringing previously unreleased games with a "classic soul" to modern audiences. This marks the company's first major step into publishing, and it's an interesting evolution for a store that has spent nearly two decades acting as a digital harbor for gaming history.

Fans can look forward to bringing lost games back from the dead.

And when it comes to new features, that wasn't all the showcase had to offer. GOG also introduced GOG Social, a new social network integrated into the platform that allows users to share screenshots, videos, and participate in discussions, all directly connected to the games they own.

Finally, the team confirmed what many of us have been asking for: a completely rebuilt GOG GALAXY. The launcher has been redesigned for Windows and Mac, promising better performance and, most importantly, full Linux support. Yeah... I used to pray for times like this!

Eighteen years of keeping games alive is no small feat, and based on these announcements, the next chapter for GOG looks just as bright. As the team emphasized during its first-ever showcase, "18 years of making games live forever together." I've always relied on the platform to revisit some of my favorite childhood titles, but with these new social features and its expansion into publishing, I have a feeling I'll be spending a lot more time exploring its library in the years to come!
