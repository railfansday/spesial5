Choose U.S. Bank home equity if you live in one of its 26 branch states, have strong credit, and want to borrow without closing costs, on a fixed-rate loan or a line you can partly lock at a fixed rate. Look elsewhere to borrow under $25,000 or, on a loan, past 80% of your home’s value.

The loan is a lump sum at a fixed rate, repaid over as long as 30 years, and the home equity line of credit (HELOC) lets you borrow as needed during a 10-year draw period at a variable rate. On a 10-year, $50,000 loan near 8%, autopay from a U.S. Bank checking or savings account saves about $13 a month by our math, since it cuts either rate by half a percentage point.

Borrowers with a 730 FICO score, 40% equity after borrowing, and a U.S. Bank account for autopay get its lowest rates, on a 10-year fixed loan or a line they can lock as they draw. Anyone who needs under $25,000 or a loan past 80% of their home’s value, or who lives outside its 26 states, needs another lender.
