Is AAA Worth It? Price Its Tiers Against the Roadside Help You Already Have

A AAA membership pays off only if you’d use its tows, other roadside help, or discounts. Before you join or renew, look up what your club charges for Classic, Plus, and Premier. Then check the roadside help your car insurer, carmaker, or credit card may already give you, so you don’t pay twice.

AAA counts nearly 58 million members in the U.S., and its three tiers differ mostly in how far a tow can go before you pay for the extra miles.

Dues and towing limits vary by regional club. One Western club, AAA Mountain West Group, charges $64.99 a year for Classic, which covers a tow of up to 5 miles or to the nearest AAA-approved repair shop. Plus costs $99.99 and covers tows up to 100 miles, and Premier, at $124.99, adds one 200-mile tow a year per household. Every tier includes four service calls a year for each member and covers that member in any car they drive or ride in.

Then price the help you have or could add. GEICO’s Emergency Roadside Service, an add-on to its car insurance, starts at $14 a year per car. In most states it pays for the first 15 miles of a tow to the nearest qualified repair shop. Many Visa cards include Roadside Dispatch, which needs no membership but charges $79.95 for a standard call, with 5 miles of towing included. Hyundai’s warranty includes five years of roadside assistance on a new Hyundai, with no mileage cap.

Say your older car needs two 40-mile tows a year on a long commute. Plus at Mountain West covers both for $99.99, while two calls to Roadside Dispatch would cost $159.90, about $60 more, before the tow company charges for the 35 miles past the included 5 on each tow. The owner of a new Hyundai, meanwhile, would pay AAA for roadside help the car already includes.

Adding a household member to Plus at Mountain West costs $64.99 a year, against $99.99 for the primary member. Count AAA’s discounts as savings only on things you already buy.

Pull out your auto policy, your warranty booklet, and your card’s guide to benefits, and pay AAA only for the calls and towing miles they leave out.
