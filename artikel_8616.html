Get the Citi Simplicity® Card if you’re moving a balance from another bank’s card or spreading out a big purchase and want 18 months at 0% interest on both. Paying late costs no fee and won’t raise your rate. If you’ll need more than 18 months to clear a transfer, choose a card with a longer 0% period.

The Simplicity earns no rewards, so it’s a card for paying down debt rather than for everyday spending. Citi generally recommends good to excellent credit to apply.

Get it if you’ll pay off a transfer or a big purchase within 18 months and want a card with no late fee or penalty APR. For a transfer that needs more than 18 months, pick the Citi Diamond Preferred, which gives 21 months at the same 3% fee but charges up to $41 for a late payment and can then end its 0% rate.
