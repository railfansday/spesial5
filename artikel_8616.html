House-sitting gets you a free place to stay in exchange for caring for someone’s home and pets while they’re away. Join a house-sitting site and earn your first reviews on short sits near home, since owners choose sitters by their reviews. Then apply for longer sits whose dates and pets fit you.

A retired couple who would otherwise pay $120 a night for a room can save $5,040 with three two-week sits in England and France. After $259 for a year of TrustedHousesitters’ Premium plan, which a couple can share, they come out about $4,800 ahead.

The membership is the cheap part. Owners pick sitters by their reviews and references, so your first sit is the hardest to win, and TrustedHousesitters says having a reference doubles a sitter’s odds of landing a sit quickly.

TrustedHousesitters, the biggest site by its own count, charges $129 a year for Basic and $169 for Standard, plus a $12 booking fee per confirmed sit, or $259 for Premium with no booking fee. A smaller site, MindMyHouse, costs $29 a year. Ask a landlord or employer for a reference before your first application.

Read each listing’s routine before you commit, since a dog that needs three walks a day or a cat on insulin sets your daily schedule. Before the owners leave, get the vet’s details and agree on how vet bills get paid. TrustedHousesitters’ Standard and Premium plans add free calls with vets and an accident and liability plan for damage or injuries a pet causes while in your care. Premium can also pay toward a room within 20 miles of the home, up to $150 a night and $1,500 a sit, if an owner cancels 14 days or less before the sit starts.

You still pay to reach each sit, for food, and for lodging between sits. Abroad, you enter as a tourist, and border officers sometimes treat a sit as work, so TrustedHousesitters advises carrying a return or onward ticket and proof you can pay for the trip. Count your days, too, since a U.S. passport allows up to 90 days in any 180-day period across France and the rest of the Schengen area, Europe’s passport-free travel zone.

Start with two or three short sits within driving distance, and use their reviews to win the longer sits abroad.
