Peloton's profit jumped as its turnaround notched third straight revenue gain

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Peloton's revenue rose 2% to $559 million while paid connected fitness subscriptions fell 9% from a year earlier

Peloton Interactive reported a third straight quarter of year-over-year revenue growth for its fiscal first quarter, which ended Sept. 30, as net income rose to $96 million from $14 million a year earlier.

Revenue rose 2% to $559 million, $4 million above the midpoint of the company's guidance range. That topped analyst estimates of $552 million, according to Bloomberg. Subscription revenue rose 3% to $410.7 million, while connected fitness products revenue fell 3% to $148.5 million.

Adjusted EBITDA rose 42% to $168 million, and free cash flow rose 113% to $143 million. Gross margin reached 61.3%, up 975 basis points from a year earlier and driven in part by nonrecurring benefits, the company said.

Ending paid connected fitness subscriptions fell 9% to 2.475 million, at the top of the company's guidance range. Average net monthly churn was 1.7%, compared with 1.6% a year earlier.

For the second quarter, Peloton expects revenue of $625 million to $650 million, a 3% decline from a year earlier at the midpoint. It expects gross margin near 50% and adjusted EBITDA of $85 million to $100 million. It expects ending paid connected fitness subscriptions of 2.425 million to 2.445 million.

The company left its full-year outlook unchanged: revenue of $2.30 billion to $2.40 billion, adjusted EBITDA of $475 million to $525 million and free cash flow of at least $350 million.

"Our performance across the top and bottom lines is testament to our disciplined approach on cost along with continued strong Member engagement and strength in our commercial business," Chief Financial Officer Sid Thacker said in a statement.

After the quarter closed, Peloton paid down $280 million of its term loan. Gross principal debt outstanding is now $1.048 billion, down 32% from $1.534 billion on Dec. 31, 2025, the company said.

Thacker told Bloomberg it is "still too early to tell" what kind of reception holiday shoppers will give the new treadmills.

Peloton unveiled three new treadmills in September, including the $2,195 Tread Flex, its first foldable model. In August, the company posted its first full fiscal year of net income, $63.2 million for the year ended June 30, 2026. Revenue for that year fell to $2.446 billion from $2.491 billion, and paid connected fitness subscriptions fell to 2.553 million.

Peloton is scheduled to hold a conference call at 8:30 a.m. ET on Thursday to discuss the results.
