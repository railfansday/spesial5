NatGold Digital Announces Immediate Delisting of NATG from MEXC Following U.S. Treasury Action

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

CORAL GABLES, Fla., Nov. 7, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- NatGold Digital Ltd. ("NatGold" or the "Company") today announced that it has requested the delisting of the NatGold Token (NATG) from MEXC, a global cryptocurrency exchange where NATG is currently listed, with immediate effect.

The decision follows the U.S. Department of the Treasury's announcement of an order issued by its Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) under the Combating Russian Money Laundering Act, prohibiting U.S. financial institutions from processing funds transfers involving MEXC Global.

NatGold is a U.S. company whose wholly owned subsidiary, NatGold Integrity Vault LLC, is registered with FinCEN as a money services business. The Company is acting ahead of the order's effective date.

"As a U.S. company operating through a FinCEN-registered money services business, we are acting immediately in response to Treasury's decision," said Mark Radke, Executive Chairman of NatGold Digital. "Trust is a cornerstone of NatGold's operating structure, and responding promptly to this regulatory action reflects that commitment."

NatGold is in advanced discussions with multiple regulatory-compliant exchanges and venues  serving global markets, including the United States, and intends to replace MEXC as soon as possible. Once new listings are confirmed, NATG token holders will be advised of the arrangements and encouraged to register accounts and link their wallets, as applicable, to continue trading.

NatGold Digital Ltd. is the global leader in digital gold mining and the architect and operator of a patent-pending, non-extractive platform designed to unlock the intrinsic value of technically verified in-ground gold resources that remain securely stored in Mother Nature's Vault.
