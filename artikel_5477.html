PROPEL Center Names University of Arkansas at Pine Bluff Winner of the Future of XYZ HBCU Innovation Challenge at AfroTech

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The student team's Brix, a money coaching app built by Gen Z for Gen Z, topped a field of more than 30 HBCU applicants to take the $15,000 top prize, with industry mentorship and support from Apple, PROPEL's Founding Partner

ATLANTA, Nov. 27, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- PROPEL Center announced that the University of Arkansas at Pine Bluff (UAPB) won the inaugural Future of XYZ HBCU Innovation Challenge at AfroTech, earning the $15,000 top prize for Brix, a money coaching app built by Gen Z for Gen Z. The win capped PROPEL's newest HBCU innovation challenge, which put student founders on a national technology stage during AfroTech week in Houston.

More than 30 historically Black colleges and universities applied to participate, with 10 finalist teams representing nine HBCUs advancing to the final round. Presented in partnership with AfroTech, the challenge culminated Nov. 2 and 3, with finalists presenting their ideas and pitching live to an audience of industry professionals. Future of XYZ is the newest chapter in PROPEL's innovation challenge series, following the Future of Tech earlier this year, which drew 1,100 applications from more than 80 HBCUs.

"HBCU students have never lacked talent. What they have too often lacked is access to the rooms, the resources and the relationships where talent gets backed," said Harriette K. Burrell, President and Chief Strategy Officer of PROPEL Center. "PROPEL exists to open those rooms. From the Future of Tech to the Future of XYZ, we build the stages, the coaching and the industry connections that help students turn bold ideas into real ventures. The University of Arkansas at Pine Bluff just showed a national audience what happens when that access is real."

The experience extended well beyond the final presentation. Over three weeks, participating students worked with PROPEL coaches and industry mentors with experience across business, engineering and other disciplines to refine their concepts, strengthen their presentations and sharpen their pitches. Students also attended a special breakfast hosted by Apple for the mentors and their students, where they connected with other industry professionals. Apple is PROPEL's Founding Partner.

The winning team, UAPB students David Adedeji, Nicholas Nettey and Angel Yuzya, built Brix (usebrix.app), a money coaching app for first-generation and first-year students, designed for the moment money lands: a financial aid refund, a first paycheck, a first brokerage account. Its tagline is "Your money era starts here." Brix helps students spot the borrowed portion of a refund and its return window, build a plan that covers essentials first, and practice investing with fake money at real prices before putting real money at risk. In the team's own campus research, 66 percent of surveyed students had never bought a stock, 88 percent said their refund was gone or sitting untouched in the bank, and 74 percent wanted to practice first with fake money.

"Winning is an incredible honor, but the real prize is everything we gained along the way," said the University of Arkansas at Pine Bluff's winning team. "The mentorship, the coaching and the chance to present Brix on a national stage challenged us to think bigger and communicate our vision with real confidence. We are leaving with more than a $15,000 prize. We are leaving with new skills, valuable connections and the belief that what we are building can make a real difference."

The Future of XYZ challenge continues the momentum of PROPEL's Future of Tech Innovation Challenge earlier this year, which brought 70 students to Austin, Texas, in April and awarded more than $120,000 in scholarships and prizes. Together, the challenges demonstrate how PROPEL is creating additional opportunities for HBCU students to showcase their talents while preparing for competitive professional environments.

For PROPEL, the value of these experiences is measured not only in prizes or pitches, but also in students' growth. Through targeted coaching, mentorship and exposure to industry leaders, participants develop communication, presentation, collaboration and other professional skills that help them translate innovation into opportunity.

The Future of XYZ HBCU Innovation Challenge at AfroTech is part of PROPEL's broader work to elevate HBCU talent, connect students with industry expertise and create pathways for the next generation of innovators and leaders.

PROPEL Center works to expand opportunities for HBCU students through innovation, experiential learning, mentorship and connections to industry. With Apple as its Founding Partner, PROPEL helps students develop the technical, entrepreneurial and professional capabilities to shape the future. To learn more, visit www.propelcenter.org.
