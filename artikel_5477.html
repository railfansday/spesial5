Cambium Opens Flagship Maryland Manufacturing and R&D Facility to Expand America's Wood Supply Chain

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The 118,500-square-foot facility will produce millwork, finished goods, and mass timber as Cambium advances next-generation American wood manufacturing.

COLUMBIA, Md., Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Cambium today announced the opening of Cambium East, its flagship East Coast manufacturing and R&D facility in Columbia, Maryland. The 118,500-square-foot facility combines full-scale production with product research and development under one roof. It expands the company's capacity to turn domestic wood into materials for buildings, furniture, and infrastructure.

"Expanding local manufacturing is critical to our state's economic growth. Supported by $300,000 in federal funding, the opening of Cambium East in Columbia is a win-win â creating more good-paying jobs and helping to position Maryland as a leader in next-generation efficient wood manufacturing," said U.S. Senator Chris Van Hollen.

Cambium will mark the opening today with a ribbon cutting, facility tours, and remarks from Maryland Secretary of Commerce Harry Coker Jr.; Maryland State Forester Anne Hairston-Strang; Jennifer Jones, CEO of the Howard County Economic Development Authority; Dallin Brooks, Executive Director of the National Hardwood Lumber Association; and Curt Bolton of InventWood.

The opening marks a new stage of growth for Cambium, which combines a proprietary technology platform with manufacturing and a nationwide supplier network to modernize the wood industry. The company has grown to more than 100 employees and from one to six offices in the last 18 months. Recent work includes partnerships with Lowe's and Finnish Sauna Builders and projects such as the Theodore Roosevelt Presidential Library boardwalk and Google's Hangar 3 at Moffett Field.

"American wood deserves American manufacturing. We have the forests, the skilled workforce, and the demand right here at home. What's been missing is the infrastructure to connect them. Cambium East is our investment in next-generation manufacturing in Maryland, bringing high-quality jobs to the region and keeping American wood working for American customers," said Cambium CEO and Co-Founder Ben Christensen.

Located in Columbia, Maryland, outside Baltimore, Cambium East will process up to 5 million board feet of sustainably sourced domestic lumber each year across the company's three divisions: millwork, finished goods, and mass timber. Cambium's digital platform connects businesses across a fragmented supply chain, helping local operators reach larger markets and bringing transparency to the wood supply chain.

"We're proud Cambium chose Howard County for Cambium East. This facility makes Howard County a regional hub for wood manufacturing, bringing good jobs and new investment to Columbia, and we look forward to Cambium's growth here," said Jennifer Jones, CEO of the Howard County Economic Development Authority.

"Hardwood producers need markets that value their product. Cambium East brings new demand for American hardwoods and innovative products, and gives mills in this region a reliable place to sell," said Dallin Brooks, Executive Director of the National Hardwood Lumber Association.

The company's flagship product, Carbon Smart Woodâ¢, is made from salvaged and underutilized trees that would otherwise go to waste. It is sourced and manufactured in the U.S. Alongside Carbon Smart Wood, Cambium distributes domestically sourced wood products from partners including Arbor Wood Co. and Superwood.

"We invested in Cambium because they saw an opportunity to build a stronger American wood industry from resources others were throwing away," said Mitch Rubin, Managing Director of Portfolio and Investments at Elemental Impact. "This facility brings that vision to life: turning overlooked wood into high-quality products, creating manufacturing jobs, and helping local businesses reach bigger markets."

Cambium's Baltimore roots go back to its early work with the City of Baltimore's Camp Small program and Baltimore Tree Trust. The company established its first physical operation in the region with Halethorpe-based OE Custom and today sources material for Maryland-based InventWood. The new facility keeps more of the work of turning local wood into finished products in Maryland, at national scale.

"Maryland's forests are a valuable renewable resource whose long history of wood products markets have helped landowners tend their woods and keep them in forest land use. Facilities like Cambium East help create more opportunities for Maryland-grown wood to reach local and regional markets, supporting the businesses, landowners, and communities that are part of our forest economy," said Anne Hairston-Strang, Ph.D., Director and State Forester, Maryland Forest Service, Department of Natural Resources.

To help with project costs, the Maryland Agricultural & Resource-Based Industry Development Corporation (MARBIDCO) awarded Cambium a $125,000 Wood Products Industry Equity Incentive Grant to support the facility buildout.

"MARBIDCO invests in businesses that strengthen Maryland's agricultural and resource-based economy. Wood is one of our most important renewable resources, and the Cambium East business model helps ensure that the wood harvested and salvaged across the region is put to its highest use, creating more sales channels for local mills and new manufacturing jobs in Howard County," said Steve McHenry, Executive Director of MARBIDCO.
