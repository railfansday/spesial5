Saints officially waive S Justin Reid

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Safety Justin Reid already penned his good-bye missive to the Saints, but his departure did not become official until 4 p.m. ET.

The Saints waived Reid on Friday, with the move made official on the NFL’s personnel notice.

Reid asked for his release this week, and the Saints have granted it.

“In life and sports, things don’t always go the way you planned or hoped they would,” Reid wrote on social media. “Unfortunately, this is one of those moments. But that doesn’t change the love and respect I have for this organization or city. I truly want to see this team and this city thrive. No one deserves it more than New Orleans and the Saints.”

He will go through waivers, with the NFL announcing at 4 p.m. ET on Monday whether he clears. If no team claims him, Reid will become a free agent.

Reid joined the Saints as a free agent in 2025, and he started all 24 games he played for the team. He had 118 tackles, an interception and a half-sack.
