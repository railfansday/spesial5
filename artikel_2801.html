Where Miami football falls in CFP rankings after loss to Notre Dame

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Where Miami football falls in CFP rankings after loss to Notre Dame appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

The latest College Football Playoff rankings have finally been released on Tuesday, and it's not as exciting this time around for Miami football. Ranked No. 1 overall a week ago, the Hurricanes have dropped all the way down to the seventh spot.

It isn't a big surprise that the Hurricanes are no longer atop the rankings, given the fact that they just suffered their first loss of the 2026 season when they fell prey to Marcus Freeman's Notre Dame Fighting Irish over the weekend at Notre Dame Stadium in Notre Dame.

However, some are left stunned by how far down Miami's drop is in the CFP rankings.

"Complete joke behind ranked behind Texas. Should be #6 ," a fan said.

"That's a crazy drop," a social media user on X shared.

"losing on road to ND by one score w/o toney and dropping six spots got bro trippin fr," another one commented.

"Pretty steep drop? Would you drop bama or Georgia that much?" a different fan said.

"So we drop to 7 from losing by a TD on the road but UGA can lose to f–king auburn at home and drop to 4. Got it," another comment read.

Miami football entered its showdown against the Fighting Irish as an underdog. For one, the Hurricanes were without star wide receiver Malachi Toney due to an injured ankle. It remains to be seen how long Toney will be out, but he surely was missed by his team in its 31-24 loss to the Fighting Irish.

The Georgia Bulldogs are the new occupants of the No. 1 spot in the CFP rankings.

Miami, which is 8-1 overall and 6-0 in conference play, will look to get back on track on Saturday, as the Hurricanes face the Duke Blue Devils at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens.
