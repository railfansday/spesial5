Chiefs Rookie Mansoor Delane Nominated for NFL Award

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The first half of the 2026 NFL season has been completed, and the NFL released its candidates for individual awards. Kansas City Chiefs rookie Mansoor Delane has been nominated for one of them.

He entered the league as the team's No. 6 overall pick in the first round this year. Delane starred for the Virginia Tech Hokies from 2022 to 2024 before entering the transfer portal. The Maryland Native decided to take his talents to the LSU Tigers, earning unanimous All-American and first-team All-SEC honors last year.

The cornerback has showcased his elite route reading in his first five games, including five starts. He has recorded 27 combined tackles consisting of 10 assisted and 17 solo efforts. Delane also has one interception and three tackles for loss under his name. However, he missed the Week 9 game against the New York Jets due to a shoulder injury. Kansas City won 35-12 at Arrowhead Stadium.

Kansas City Chiefs cornerback Mansoor Delane (5) walks down the hill to the fields prior to training camp at Missouri Western State University.

The NFL released its midseason awards finalists on Monday, including for Defensive Rookie of the Year. Delane is one of five first-year players vying for the honor. Joining him are New York Giants linebacker Arvell Reese, Miami Dolphins linebacker Jacob Rodriguez, New York Jets defensive end David Bailey and Washington Commanders linebacker Sonny Styles.

"Voting for the 2026 NFL Channel Midseason Awards is NOW OPEN," NFL Media wrote.

Delane has played in key role for Kansas City in securing a 6-2 record. The team drafted him to fill the hole in its secondary, and he not disappointed, repaying the front office's trust in him.

Delane and Chiefs will play their next game on the road on Sunday against the Atlanta Falcons. It's their chance to tie the Denver Broncos at the top of the AFC North and strengthen their positioning ahead of the playoffs. Kickoff is set for 1 p.m. ET at Mercedes-Benz Stadium.
