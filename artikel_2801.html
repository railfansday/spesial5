Jets own three of the top 13 picks in the 2027 NFL Draft after Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

This season has gone completely off the rails for the New York Jets. One of the few silver linings is that the team is set to have multiple high first round picks. The Jets' own pick will be high given the team's struggles, and both the Indianapolis Colts and Dallas Cowboys are having difficult seasons.

If the season ended today, the Jets would be sitting on three picks in the top 13 of the 2027 NFL Draft.

Picks 19 through 32 are determined by the results of NFL Playoff games.

Once again Jets fans are focused on the NFL Draft in November as another season has resulted in failure. At least this year the NFL Draft should be very exciting.
