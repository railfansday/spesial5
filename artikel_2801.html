Rafael Devers could be traded & 1 team makes most sense

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Rafael Devers could become one of the biggest names to watch on the MLB trade market, with the San Francisco Giants potentially facing difficult decisions regarding their expensive roster. If Devers becomes available, several teams could be interested in adding his bat, but one destination stands out as an especially strong fit: the New York Mets.

Devers remains a major offensive talent, and a change of scenery could make sense for both the player and the organization. The Mets have the financial resources, lineup needs, and big-market environment that could make a blockbuster deal worth exploring.

The New York Mets are the perfect fit for Devers if he becomes available via trade. Devers is comfortable playing in big markets and being in the spotlight, and there are few organizations in baseball that offer a bigger stage than the Mets.

The Mets made the mistake of letting Pete Alonso walk in free agency last year, leaving a significant hole in their lineup at first base. While the team has made other moves to reshape its offense, adding another legitimate power hitter could help address the loss of Alonso's production.

Devers could potentially fill that need at first base, although the Mets also have the option of using him as their designated hitter. Regardless of where he plays defensively, his bat would immediately become one of the most important pieces of the lineup.

Putting Devers in a lineup alongside Francisco Lindor, Juan Soto and Bo Bichette would give the Mets an incredible top four. That type of offensive firepower could make New York one of the most dangerous lineups in baseball, particularly if Devers returns to producing at the level expected of him.

Of course, acquiring Devers would not be a simple transaction. His contract is enormous, and any team pursuing him would have to be comfortable taking on a significant financial commitment. Devers is reportedly owed approximately $211 million on his remaining contract, making the financial side of a potential trade a major obstacle.

However, the Mets are one of the few teams in baseball capable of taking on that type of contract. Their financial resources give them the flexibility to pursue major players when opportunities arise, and Devers would represent the type of impact addition worth considering.

The Giants may not necessarily be looking to move Devers, but trade speculation surrounding expensive players is inevitable when teams evaluate their roster direction. If Devers does become available, the Mets should be one of the first teams making a phone call.

New York has the money, the market, and the lineup to make this potential move make sense. Adding Devers could help replace the offensive production lost with Alonso's departure while giving the Mets another superstar to build around. If a trade becomes possible, the Mets could be the most logical landing spot.
