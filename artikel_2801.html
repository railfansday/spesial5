Cavs vs. Mavericks: How to watch, odds, and injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The Cleveland Cavaliers will welcome 2016 champion Kyrie Irving and the Dallas Mavericks to town for their annual visit to Cleveland.

The Cavs have not had an easy last 24 hours. They weren't able to leave Miami last night after their loss to the Heat as they wanted to, due to the issues that were grounding planes yesterday. They instead had to fly back home this morning, which is less than ideal.

The Mavs aren't an easy matchup. They're playing better basketball this season with their imposing front line and the emergence of Cooper Flagg as one of the best young players in the league. This would be a tough test for the Cavs even if it wasn't the second night of a back-to-back.
