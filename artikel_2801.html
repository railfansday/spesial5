JJ Redick revealed the Lakers’ strategy with two-way signings this season

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Tuesday night, with Walker Kessler sidelined and his replacement, Kevon Looney, struggling, the Los Angeles Lakers turned to Carson Cooper for minutes at center. Signed to a two-way contract on Nov. 10, Cooper gave the Lakers 18 minutes and logged three points (0-of-1 shooting, 3-of-6 at the line), four rebounds (two offensive) and two assists. Los Angeles outscored the Charlotte Hornets by six points during his time on the floor.

After Cooper's Lakers debut, head coach JJ Redick spoke about what the organization is prioritizing with its two-way contract players, whether it's Cooper, Chris Mañon, Arthur Kaluma or AK Okereke (who was waived last month and is now playing for the Coachella Valley Lakers).

The goal, Redick said, isn't to simply land the foremost G League stars but to find those who can fill valuable roles for the Lakers when called upon, just as Cooper did Tuesday.

"I think [Carson Cooper] understands the assignment. Not to get too big picture with what the G League is and what the two-ways are, but I think philosophically, we've gone in a bunch of different directions with our two-ways and I think with this group, like we really wanted to get guys that could play a role for this team if needed," Redick told reporters following the 118-114 win. "With Chris and with both the AKs…and Carson's the same way. He's here to play a role and it's not, 'Oh, this guy can go score 30 points in the G League.' That doesn't necessarily equate to what we need right now. And so all our guys that are two-ways this year, they understand why they're in that role and how they can impact winning and help our team and he did that tonight for us."

Kessler isn't listed on Wednesday's injury report, so the Lakers will welcome back their preferred choice at center against the Utah Jazz. But given Looney's struggles to open the year and the general lack of reliable options behind Kessler, it's absolutely possible Cooper receives some more run down the line this season. At the very least, it sounds like Tuesday was a nice audition in his head coach's eyes.
