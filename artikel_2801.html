Dolphins’ Jeff Hafley ‘not going to BS’ after Zane Gonzalez blows Colts game with missed kicks

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Jeff Hafley wasn't happy about leaving points on the board following field goal misses from kicker Zane Gonzalez and the special teams following the Miami Dolphins' 24-22 loss to the Indianapolis Colts on Sunday afternoon.

Hafley is going through his first year as the Dolphins' head coach. He is leading the franchise through a rebuild, having one of the worst records in the league.

Miami competed with Indianapolis throughout the contest, even having opportunities to take control of the game and win their second of the season. However, they failed to take advantage of their chances.

Gonzalez missed two field goals and an extra point, errors that loomed large in the final scoreline. Hafley pointed that out after the game, not mincing any words about what Gonzalez has to do moving forward.

"We missed an extra point, which forced us to go for two, and we missed two field goals," Hafley said, per reporter Marcel Louis-Jacques. "That's seven points, so we have to make those."

"We have to make the extra point, we have to make the field goals — I'm not gonna ever BS you … That's 7 points."

Jeff Hafley knows that the Dolphins, even though they are struggling, are capable of punching above their weight for potential upsets.

Kyle McCord completed 17 of 32 passes for 209 yards with no touchdowns or interceptions, but he rushed twice for four yards and a touchdown. He also lost a fumble in his own end zone that was recovered for a Colts touchdown on the first play from scrimmage of the third quarter.

Ollie Gordon II led the rushing attack with 20 carries for 76 yards. Caleb Douglas led receivers with 71 yards on three catches. Malik Washington made three receptions for 50 yards, while Jalen Tolbert caught two passes for 49 yards and a touchdown.

The Dolphins will look to bounce back in their next matchup, remaining on the road. They take on the Buffalo Bills in Week 11 as kickoff commences on Nov. 22 at 1 p.m. ET.
