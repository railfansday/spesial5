How to watch Warriors vs. Celtics

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Golden State Warriors finally return home for Sunday night's matchup against the Boston Celtics. The game will be played at 5:30 PM PT in San Francisco and can be watched on NBC Sports Bay Area.

The Warriors' nightmare road trip finally came to an end Thursday night with a 126-98 loss to the Memphis Grizzlies. Golden State's offensive struggles continued against Memphis, with big man Charles Bassey leading the team with just 16 points, while Brandin Podziemski and Will Richard chipped in 15 points apiece. Things weren't much better defensively, as the Warriors allowed the Grizzlies to shoot 52.2% from the field, including 42.9% from three-point range. The loss marked Golden State's fourth consecutive defeat by at least 20 points, as the team continues to struggle on both ends of the floor without Stephen Curry in the lineup.

The Warriors will look to put their disastrous road trip behind them as they return to the Chase Center, where they have won six straight games. However, a lot has changed since their last home game, most notably Curry's oblique injury, which is expected to keep him sidelined for at least another couple of weeks. With their superstar still out, Golden State will need to find a way to recapture some of the momentum that fueled its success at home earlier this month.

However, that won't be easy against a Boston team that enters Sunday with an 11-5 record, good for second place in the Eastern Conference. The Celtics have been one of the league's best defensive teams this season, allowing an NBA-low 103.0 points per game while ranking second in defensive rating (105.6). With Golden State already struggling to generate consistent offense without Curry, the Warriors will have their work cut out for them against a Boston team looking to snap their own three-game losing streak.

Enjoy the game Dub Nation. GO WARRIORS!!!
