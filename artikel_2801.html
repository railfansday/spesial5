Jets vs. Chargers: Preview, prediction, what to watch for in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

An inside look at Sunday's Jets-Chargers matchup in San Diego:

Jets QB Geno Smith vs. Chargers pass rush

Smith is set to play after leaving last week's game with an ankle injury, but he probably won't be moving too well. He is not that evasive to begin with.

Smith has been sacked 29 times this season, which is tied for third most in the league.

The Chargers have just 15 sacks but they are 12th in pressure rate.

"They're really stout up front," Smith said. "They've been playing, I think they have the most pressures in the league.

"Not a lot of sacks, I don't think it's turned into sacks, but they've definitely been putting pressure on the quarterback. So, it starts up front for them."

316.5: Yards per game that the Jets are averaging, their second most through 10 games in the past 10 seasons. They can move the ball. They just can't score.

The Chargers ended their losing streak last week and now will try to make it two in a row.

I think the Jets will actually reach the end zone in the first quarter of this game, and they will control the game and make the pro-tank crowd upset with their third win of the season.
