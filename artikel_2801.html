Bud Dupree requests release from Chargers, who haven't granted it

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

As the season continues to unfold — and as the trade deadline fades into the rear-view mirror — unhappy players have only one option: To request a release.

Via Jeremy Fowler of ESPN, Chargers linebacker Bud Dupree has done that. The Chargers, however, have not granted the request.

Dupree, per Fowler, also requested his release before the season.

It’s unknown whether the Chargers tried to trade him. His playing time has systematically dropped this year. He had no defensive snaps in two straight games before he was a healthy scratch in the Week 10 Monday night game against the Ravens.

That move came three days after Dupree had some pointed comments for the front office.

“Don’t ask hard questions to us; ask hard questions to them,” Dupree said. “Ask the hard questions. Everybody pull they cameras out and go upstairs and ask questions to them. Everybody. Walk upstairs and ask them.”

Dupree is under contract, so there’s nothing he can do to force his way out. Still, the situation underscores the one-way nature of these deals.

The team can cut the player whenever it wants. The player can’t get out unless the team is willing to let him go.
