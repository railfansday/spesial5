Chiefs News: Josh Downs trade is worth it in the Mahomes era

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Kansas City Chiefs have acquired wide receiver Josh Downs from the Indianapolis Colts, a move that feels inevitable in retrospect, given the constant reports of the Chiefs' interest in adding a wide receiver over the last few weeks.

In an ideal world, the Chiefs' roster is strong enough to win a Super Bowl without a midseason trade, but general manager Brett Veach rightfully acknowledged that quarterback Patrick Mahomes needed more help in order to make the most of this season.

Downs doesn't just join a contender with the potential to save its offense and hold the Lombardi Trophy. He's in a contract year, and with the Chiefs' passing attack full of available targets, he could put up career numbers down the stretch. Downs can make millions in 2027 free agency should he produce as the Chiefs need him to. Had Downs become a Steeler, that path to sudden richness would have been narrower.

I personally don't think Downs has enough talent as a downfield or contested catch receiver to reach the billing the Chiefs need him to. But it's worth the swing for only an exchange of Day 3 picks.

Colts trading WR Josh Downs to Chiefs for fourth-, fifth-round picks | The Athletic

Downs, who ranked fourth on The Athletic analyst , is considered a strong route runner and excellent separator with his 5-foot-9, 171-pound frame. Beating man coverage has been the Chiefs' multi-season issue, and Downs could profile as an "easy button" option for Mahomes on critical passing downs.

Something else: Downs has punt-return experience, which could matter for a Chiefs team that ranks 31st in expected points added per punt return, according to TruMedia.
