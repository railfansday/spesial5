Disney Confirms News For Its “Most Anticipated Titles” At The Game Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Following yesterday’s announcement that The Game Awards can be streamed on Disney Plus, it’s been confirmed that the show will feature “major announcements” for some of its games.

The whole world may be waiting on November 19 and the launch of the biggest video game of all time, but there’s plenty going on in the world of gaming after that. As always, The Game Awards is bookending the year with a showcase that’ll hopefully feature some big announcements for upcoming releases.

I’m a little biased here, but one of the games that I’ve got my fingers crossed to make an appearance at The Game Awards is Kingdom Hearts 4. We know that it’s coming in a year’s time, so a new trailer appearing there didn’t seem far-fetched at all. It now seems even more likely based on Disney’s own words about the collaboration.

As we reported yesterday, The Game Awards is teaming up with Disney to offer a new way to watch the show through Disney Plus. That already had Kingdom Hearts fans thinking something was going on, but Disney itself has fanned the fires with a statement that confirms big news is coming for some of its games.

The latest trailer for Kingdom Hearts 4 might be short, but it’s absolutely packed with easy to miss details.

As pointed out by Twitter user HMKilla, the Disney press centre shared a blog post about The Game Awards coming to Disney Plus. In the post, Disney outright says that the show will feature “major announcements and surprises” from some of Disney’s “most anticipated titles in gaming”, which could apply to quite a few games.

Of course, the biggest thing that Disney has on the horizon (that’s been officially confirmed, at least), is Kingdom Hearts 4. With a release window of late 2027 already revealed, it would make a lot of sense to see it at the event, likely with a new world reveal instead of just showing more of Coco.

I’ve seen Battlefront 3 making the rounds as a possible game, but that’s reportedly not even started development yet, so you really shouldn’t get your hopes up.

Nomura did say that we’d be waiting a little while to hear more about Kingdom Hearts 4, but that was back in the Summer, so we’re definitely in the right time frame. If not Kingdom Hearts 4, you can probably count on a reveal for Jedi 3, despite it seeming like Lucasfilm was hinting at a reveal in January just yesterday.

Outside of some minor issues with Kingdom Hearts 3, the Kingdom Hearts Collection on Switch 2 is exactly what we’ve all been wanting.
