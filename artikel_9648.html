Cacio E Pepe Brussels Sprouts

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Trust me—these Brussels sprouts will steal the show on your holiday table this year. One of my favorite things about the fall is the return of Brussels sprouts season. While I love just simply roasting them, these upgraded sprouts—tossed in a cacio e pepe-inspired seasoning—are my absolute favorite way to make them.

How to roast the Brussels sprouts:A quick roast on the sprouts gets the bottom crisp, and a healthy dusting of pecorino and Parmesan, as soon as they come out of the oven, will gently melt into them. A generous amount of cracked black pepper is also key for these easy Brussels sprouts that are the perfect side for any holiday or dinner table.

Storage:These Brussels sprouts are best enjoyed while still hot from the oven, but if you have leftovers, they can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
