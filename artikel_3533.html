Duran Duran Guitarist Andy Taylor Dead at 65, Band Shares Loving Tributes

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Duran Duran has announced the death of its iconic guitarist Andy Taylor “following a long and courageous battle with prostate cancer.” He was 65.

“Andy passed away peacefully at home, surrounded by family, on Monday 23rd November,” the “Hungry Like the Wolf” band announced in a Wednesday, November 25, statement.

Andy became one of the classic “Fab Five” members of Duran Duran when he joined the New Wave band in 1980, along with singer Simon Le Bon, keyboardist Nick Rhodes, drummer Roger Taylor and bassist John Taylor. (Andy, Roger, 66, and John, 66, were not related.)

The group went on to sell more than 100 million records worldwide and received a coveted star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in 1993. They were inducted into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame in 2022. (Andy left Duran Duran originally in 1986 but later returned for a five-year reunion stint between 2001 to 2006.)

Andy was also part of the 1980s supergroup the Power Station, along with Duran Duran bandmate John, Chic drummer Tony Thompson and frontman Robert Palmer. Formed in 1984, the Power Station scored U.S. Top 10 hits with “Some Like It Hot” and a cover for T. Rex’s “Get It On.”

Le Bon, 68, announced during Duran Duran’s Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony in 2022 that Andy was unable to attend after being diagnosed with stage 4 metastatic prostate cancer.

“Andy was there at the very beginning of our story,” Duran Duran said on Wednesday. “From the moment he arrived in Birmingham from the North East with a guitar and an unshakeable belief in what we could become, he brought an indelible fire and swagger to our music. His playing drove the songs that took us around the world, and with a dry wit that was matched only by his wonderfully unsentimental, no-nonsense outlook on life he kept us grounded and gave the band its edge through the extraordinary years that followed.”

The guitarist tied the knot with wife Tracey Wilson on the Sunset Strip in 1982 and they remained married until his death. They welcomed four children: Georgina, Isabella, Bethany, and Andrew.

“To the world, Andy was a brilliant guitarist, but to us, he was the most wonderful husband, a loving dad and the proudest grandfather. He was the rock of our family,” Tracey said in a Monday statement. “For all the incredible experiences music brought him, family always came first. Nothing made him happier than having us all together, and no matter how far music took him, his heart was always at home.”

She continued, “Andy faced the last few years with so much courage and determination. He never gave up, fighting until the very end, never losing his love of life. The kindness and support shown to him by fans from all over the world throughout his illness brought him immense comfort and strength, and for that we will always be grateful. Andy knew just how deeply he was loved, and we take great comfort in that. We will miss him every single day.”

Andy’s Duran Duran bandmates each paid tribute to the late musician with individual and heartfelt statements, with Le Bon fondly highlighting the “blond bombshell, firebrand teenager” he met in 1980.

“There’s a massive Andy Taylor shaped hole in my heart,” he wrote. “It’ll never close up; I won’t let it. I will miss him & love him for all my days.”

John focused on Andy’s musicianship, describing him as a “demon” and a “killer riff-merchant, melodist, six string textualist.”

“[Andy] never ever left us, he was always there in the shadows, keeping an eye out, keeping it real,” the bassist noted. “And he will continue to be there, we will keep him close.”

Rhodes, 64, hailed Andy’s guitar playing for being “sharp as a razor and blunt as a stone,” adding that his late bandmate was always “out on the edge, with a clear view.”

“The incredible, talented musician and gifted songsmith arrived on a train from Cullercoats to knock us into shape and help us grow into the burgeoning version of Duran Duran that took on the world within a year or two of his arrival,” Roger added. “The sadness cuts extremely deep, but admiration and respect run even deeper for the Taylor that wasn’t related but will forever be my brother.”
