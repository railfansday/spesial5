Netflix's Settlers Of Catan Movie Sets Masters Of The Universe Duo As Directors

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Netflix's upcoming film adaptation of The Settlers of Catan has finally found its directors.

The studio has set a core duo behind Masters of the Universe as directors for the movie, with new information coming out about its production. In late 2025, it was confirmed that Netflix plans to develop Catan into a universe of adaptations, featuring both TV show and movie prospects. A film adaptation was subsequently confirmed, although details about what to expect weren't fully revealed just yet. However, it was clear Netflix wanted the franchise to be another major name under its belt.

Now, Deadline has confirmed that Aaron Nee and Adam Nee, two of the writers behind Amazon MGM Studios' Masters of the Universe, will be both writing and directing a Settlers of Catan movie. While no other details about the movie have been revealed as of writing, it's been confirmed the film will be a live-action rendition of the board game series. Darren Kyman of publisher Asmondee, Pete Fenlon of Catan Studio, and the sons of creator Klaus Teuber — Guido Teuber and Benjamin Teuber — and Roy Lee from Vertigo Entertainment will serve as producers.

The original board game, The Settlers of Catan, was created in 1995, expanding in popularity over the decades to sell over 50 million copies across 40 languages. The original game, which takes place on the fictional island Catan, sees players having to strategize alliances and resources in order to settle on the land and survive, expanding the vastness of their living space in the process. Various expansions and alternate additions have been released since it began, procuring various scenarios for players to explore.

Based on the name of the movie, however, it seems like this forthcoming project will be based on the original board game. But, since Netflix is no stranger to expanded universes, the streaming platform is planning on making a wide push for the brand to become synonymous with its own. This includes unspecified plans for animated movies, live-action TV shows, animated TV shows, unscripted programs, and even video games, all centered around the world of Catan.

No concrete details about this first live-action movie adaptation have been revealed as of writing. However, it's a strong continuation for the Nee Brothers' careers following the praise Masters of the Universe received. The duo have also written an upcoming spy film for Netflix starring Kevin Hart and Henry Cavill, the details of which will be revealed at a later date. Their work on Catan continues that positive relationship with the streaming service, while their work on the He-Man centered movie proves how well they can work with big IP.

With more information likely to arrive in the coming months, this update is a positive sign that The Settlers of Catan movie will have strong writing that carries the film as the first installment of a larger world. Although there's no telling what the movie will be about and how it will introduce viewers to the world, with the Nee Brothers dedicating their time to the film, it seems like it's in very capable hands.
