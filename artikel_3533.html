GTA 6 Is Apparently Everything Rockstar Promised And Then Some

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

At long last, Grand Theft Auto 6 launch day has come. With Rockstar's latest project steadily unlocking as the clock strikes midnight worldwide, GTA 6 reviews have gone live, and the praise has been overwhelmingly positive so far. In fact, GTA 6 is reviewing so well that it's currently tied with the 1998 The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time as the highest-rated game of all time on GameSpot's sister site Metacritic, a feat that has taken 28 years to equal.

So what are the reviewers saying? Amongst the flurry of perfect scores, a lot of the consensus is geared toward the world that Rockstar Games has created for its co-protagonists to inhabit, a sandbox that reacts to you in a variety of unique and interesting ways. It's a genre-defining experience for open-world games, as Rockstar has raised the bar to a tremendously high level for any other open-world game that'll follow in its footsteps.

Here's what some reviewers have to say, and for more on the game, check out GameSpot's GTA 6 guides hub for an evolving collection of walkthroughs, tips, and tricks.

Grand Theft Auto 6

  • Platforms: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S
  • Price $80 for the Standard Edition, $100 for the Ultimate Edition
  • Release date: November 19

GameSpot -- 10/10

Only Rockstar commands the time and money necessary to make a game as dedicated to small details. And only Rockstar can execute on the realizing such a granular vision unimpeachably. The obsession with the minutia both explains and justifies the 13-year wait. This game is the culmination of everything the developer has previously achieved in the open-world space that pushes the genre to heights that, in all likelihood, no other developer will be able to reach. GTA 6 is an astonishing open-world action game that I’d wager won’t be outdone until Grand Theft Auto 7.  -- Tamoor Hussain [Full review]

IGN -- 100

Exquisitely detailed, endlessly ripe for experimentation, and uncompromisingly engineered, Grand Theft Auto 6 is the single most impressive video game I have ever played. -- Luke Reilly [Full review]

The Guardian -- 5/5

With realism that defies belief, this vast game easily accommodates its trademark foulmouthed, over the top chaos--as well as new empathy for the fugitive couple at its center -- Keza MacDonald [Full review]

Destructoid -- 10/10

Grand Theft Auto 6 is part criminal come-up tale, part love story, and part life sim all wrapped up in an impossibly deep and detailed Rockstar Games open world that may never run out of things to do. For the last three-plus weeks, I have been gleefully vacationing in Vice City: robbing banks, fighting drug dealers, stealing cars, and enjoying the view while not coming close to finishing everything it has to offer. GTA 6 is finally here now, and it was well worth the wait. -- Scott Duwe [Full review]

Eurogamer -- 5/5

A game of opposites and awe, minute detail and impossible scale, Rockstar pushes back the horizon of video games once again with GTA 6. An irreducible mix of silliness, intimacy, grand ideas and rock 'n' roll action - and a load more after that. -- Chris Tapsell [Full review]

Bloomberg -- 5/5

It turns out that the secret ingredients to a great game are a lot of money, time and talented people. -- Jason Schreier [Full review]

Game Informer -- 10/10

Grand Theft Auto 6 is a generational game that stands toe-to-toe with many of the great crime stories and satires across other media. It showcases both the entertainment value of interactive play and the current high-water mark for technical and artistic detail in games. Hunt for and count up all the minor faults or foibles you like, but then note how you just spent another hour lost in this remarkable escape into criminality, wanton violence, egregious sex, and the elusive potential of true love. -- Matt Miller [Full review]

SiliconEra -- 90/100

Rockstar made strides in incorporating all the systems and the open world into the campaign in a satisfying way. But Jason and Lucia are the glue that keeps everything together. It’s their chemistry and complicity that made me want to experience everything, and it’s them that will keep me coming back. -- Daniel Bueno [Full review]

Screenhub -- 4/5

At its core, GTA 6 is a pretty good game. There are large sections where it is an absolutely epic game, and large sections that are boring and too limited. However, after I pushed through the boring parts and it opened up enough to become a proper GTA title, it became worth it. Regardless of whether the game was good, it was always going to be an inescapable cultural moment. Luckily, it’s one largely worth participating in. -- Alice Clarke [Full review]

Kotaku -- No score

GTA 6 is a truly remarkable game. A masterpiece of open-world design that brings forward the best parts of all of Rockstar’s past games into one billion-dollar adventure that is unlike anything I’ve played before. It’s a game I’ll be playing for years, and I can’t wait to talk about it with others. -- Zack Zwiezen [Full review]

The Verge -- No score

It really seemed like no project could live up to the level of scrutiny that GTA VI has been and will be subjected to. But now we can forget all of that, because GTA 6is actually here. And the game is so good that the outsize hype is the least interesting thing about it. -- Andrew Webster [Full review]
