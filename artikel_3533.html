Kaitlyn Bristowe Shares Affirmations She Tells Dog Amid Pooch’s Health Battle

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Former Bachelorette Kaitlyn Bristowe is sharing her new ritual with dog Pinot amid the pooch’s terrifying health battle.

“So as you can imagine I’ve been an absolute wreck,” Bristowe, 41, shared in a candid Instagram Story video on Sunday, November 22. “But in front of Pinot this is how we do morning affirmations. You ready big dog?”

Bristowe then panned the camera to show Pinot lying beside her, adding that the dog is a “gangster.”

“You are so strong. Big dog,” Bristowe said. “You are literally the strongest dog I’ve ever met. You are strong. You are kind. You are going to fight this battle. It’s like we’re in the ring. Because you are a gangster. The one and only Pinot G, you know?”

Bristowe, who is a dog mom to golden retrievers Pinot and Ramen, shared last month that she was “told to say goodbye” to her dog after he accidentally overdosed on 120 prescription pills for his preexisting heart condition. After five days in a critical care unit, Pinot returned home. Weeks later, Bristowe shared that Pinot “cannot catch a break” and is facing a new health scare.

“He has scar tissue built up in his throat (strictures) some of the worst they have seen,” she wrote via Instagram on Saturday, November 21. “He has feeding tubes and a balloon in his esophagus that we have to pump air into twice a day for six weeks.”

Bristowe went on to thank her “partner,” seemingly referring to rumored boyfriend Zac Clark, for being by her side amid Pinot’s health battle.

“Pinot and I would love to thank his doggy dad and my partner,” Bristowe added. “He has been a hero, the amc, and for your prayers to keep him going. I’ve never met a dog willing to fight so hard through all of this.”

She continued, “We will keep everyone posted. Just know when you see me online, you might not know what’s going on behind the scenes. And sometimes my coping mechanism is sleeping, sometimes it’s trying to be funny, sometimes it’s letting loose and sometimes it’s crying until I can’t cry anymore. Thank you guys for all the support. I know I’m not perfect, but I will continue to be open and honest.

Bristowe called Pinot her “fighter” in the caption of her post, adding, “My actual earth angel. The next six weeks will be all hands on deck and a lot of prayers. (Strictures were caused from him being on the ventilator).”

Hours later, Bristowe shared that she was on her way back to the ER after Pinot faced a health setback.

“His feeding tube has a clog. He is OK.” she wrote via her Instagram Story on Saturday, before returning home and captioning a separate selfie with Pinot, “And now. We rest. (Till 6 a.m. meds.)”
