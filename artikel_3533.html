Jason Statham Uses The Ultimate Weapon In Beekeeper 2 Poster

Jason Statham is back with some upgraded weaponry in The Beekeeper 2.

Directed by Timo Tjahjanto, the upcoming sequel marks a follow-up to David Ayer's 2024 action film, which saw Statham playing former government assassin Adam Clay. The Beekeeper 2 features Clay as a wanted man, this time stopping the rogue Beekeeper operatives from taking over the government after going rogue.

Amazon MGM Studios has now released an official poster for The Beekeeper 2, providing a new look at Statham's character using a car-mounted minigun. The poster features Statham behind the wheel as the machine gun fires, with a tagline revealing: "He's been busy." The new poster also confirms that the sequel is scheduled for a theatrical release on January 15. Check it out below:

The upcoming sequel is set to feature the return of Jeremy Irons as Walace Westwyld, Emmy Raver-Lampman as Agent Verona Parker, Bobby Naderi as Agent Matt Wiley, and Jemma Redgrave as Jessica Danforth. Newcomers include Pom Klementieff, Yara Shahidi, and Adam Copeland, among others.

The first trailer for The Beekeeper 2, released last month, revealed more about Clay's new ride, with Wallace confirming that the vehicle is, fittingly, a 1969 Dodge Super Bee. Irons' character gives the muscle car to Clay to help him take on the other members of the Beekeepers, and Clay is shown doing donuts in the vehicle as he fires the mounted minigun out of the open passenger door, mowing down scores of enemies.
