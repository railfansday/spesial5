A Look Inside the Scorpion Ball, Princess Grace Kelly’s Scorpio-Themed 40th Birthday Party

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Grace Kelly always had a deep passion for astrology. Born on November 12, 1929, she was a Scorpio, and very proud of it. To celebrate her 40th birthday on November 15, 1969, the Princess of Monaco organized a party themed by her astrological sign, resulting in the extravagant Scorpio Ball, held at the Hôtel de l’Hermitage in Monte Carlo. For the occasion, she only invited celebrities who were also Scorpios. Among the 60 or so guests were Swedish actress Hjördis Paulina Genberg, American actor Rock Hudson, and Italian actress Virna Lisi.

Actor Richard Burton was also in attendance, accompanied by his wife Elizabeth Taylor, even though she was born under the sign of Pisces. An exception was made for the actress, who was granted special permission thanks to her husband’s astrological sign—Scorpio, born on November 10. For the occasion, Taylor chose to dazzle, wearing a Cartier diamond necklace with an estimated value of $1 million around her neck—a gift from Burton two weeks earlier. She also wore a spectacular evening cape by Tiziani, made of black velvet and adorned with two rhinestone scorpions on the front and a third on the hood.

Guests were required to adhere to a dress code devised by Kelly, inspired by the colors associated with the sign of Scorpio. Red, black, and white were therefore strongly recommended. The princess, for her part, opted for an off-the-shoulder black velvet dress by Balenciaga from the fall-winter 1967 couture collection. Two ruby brooches set with diamonds—a wedding gift from Prince Rainier III—completed her ensemble.

The decor also reflected the evening’s theme. Red candles, portraits of famous Scorpios, and a lavish cake adorned with golden scorpions set the tone. No detail had been left to chance: The waiters, wearing powdered 18th-century wigs, dressed in velvet suits embroidered with sparkling stars and held silver candelabras adorned with red candles.

Princess Grace was seen on numerous occasions alongside Carroll Righter, an astrologer and Hollywood columnist nicknamed “the astrologer to the stars.” According to People magazine, the princess’s birth chart also revealed a Scorpio ascendant, while her Midheaven was in Leo. In astrology, the Midheaven is associated with career, public image, and reputation. Some attributed her commanding presence and magnetism in the spotlight to this configuration.

Kelly embodied the mysterious aura that her admirers associated with her astrological sign. Unfortunately, that light was extinguished on September 14, 1982, when the Princess of Monaco lost her life in a tragic car accident on the hills above the principality.

Originally published in Vanity Fair France.
