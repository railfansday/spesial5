Here's What We Know About Amazon's Black Friday and Cyber Monday 2026 Sales

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Gone are the days when holiday sales had a firm beginning and end. These days, if you're not scouring the internet weeks before the actual date of the event, you will miss out on deals. Black Friday is no exception. Amazon released its official Black Friday and Cyber Monday plans for its sale, but there's plenty that's discounted already, many reaching or matching their lowest price ever—we know, because we use tools to verify and track their deals.

Amazon is treating Black Friday and Cyber Monday like one long sale. The official sale kicks off at 12:01 a.m. PST on Nov. 19 and runs through Nov. 30.

Yes, you will need to be a Prime Member to shop the exclusive sales during Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Prime membership starts at $14.99 per month. While only you can decide if buying a yearly Prime membership is worth it for you, remember you can always sign up and then cancel your Prime membership once the sale is over: Amazon offers a free 30-day trial, so you can shop during both Black Friday and Cyber Monday before canceling without being charged. Here's how to sign up for a Prime account and all of the benefits that come with it.

Not all Black Friday deals are as good as they might seem. Unfortunately, Amazon is known to manipulate prices to trick you, but there are tools at your disposal you can use to see if the deals are actually good or not. Some other tips are making sure you're not being sold an older or cheaper version, and to always read reviews.

As far as volume, Amazon will usually beat Walmart and Best Buy in most cases, but most flagship products will likely be the same price in all three retailers. However, don't sleep on Walmart or Best Buy. There are usually a couple of surprising deals that beat Amazon (although Amazon usually matches the deal eventually).

The other major retailers doing sales are Best Buy, Target, and Walmart. Currently, Best Buy is offering one of the largest Cyber Week sales, with new deals available every Friday. Target has some week-long deals and a Deal of the Day. Nowadays, both large retailers and small businesses compete for Cyber Monday shoppers, so you can expect practically every store to run sales through Monday, Nov. 30, 2026.

If this will be your first Black Friday shopping at Amazon, there are some basic things you need to know about Amazon sales:

You can share your Prime membership with family members even if they don't live with you.

You can set price alerts for products you want and your Alexa devices can notify you when they go on sale.

There are usually some invite-only deals that you can sign up for.

There are tools you can use to see if the deal you're looking at is worthwhile or not.

But if you only take one bit of advice for shopping on Amazon, let it be this one: Don't buy anything you weren't going to buy anyway. A good way to make sure you do this is by making a list of the products you do want, and only having eyes for those.

Black Friday used to be bigger for major retailers and more expensive tech and appliances, while Cyber Monday was for cheaper tech and gave smaller businesses a chance to compete online. Nowadays, though, the distinction is almost meaningless.
