YouTube Rejects U.K. Government’s Request to Take Down Russell Brand Video

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

YouTube has rejected a request from the British government to take down a video uploaded by Russell Brand’s wife where the convicted rapist claimed he was innocent.

On Sunday, Laura Gallacher Brand posted the couple’s weekly “Sunday Service with the Brands” video with (part of the episode was prerecorded) and without her husband, who was sentenced to over 10 years in prison after being found guilty of rape and sexual assault and has since been incarcerated. In the video and post-trial, Laura read a message to listeners from Russell where he reiterated, “I am not guilty for the offenses I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening.”

The Prison Service of England and Wales requested that the Google-owned YouTube take down the video, saying Brand’s message was “distressing for victims.” However, Google denied the request, stating that — while hurtful for the victims — Brand’s video didn’t violate any of the platform’s rules.

“We are aware of the new content and our teams are investigating,” Google said in a statement (via the BBC). “We suspended Russell Brand’s channel from the YouTube Partner Program in September 2023. The British prime minister’s office previously called Brand’s message “clearly appalling.” “Prisoners are strictly banned from making statements that cause distress to victims, and speaking to the media is only permitted in exceptional circumstances,” a spokesperson for the prime minister said.

In the aftermath of Brand’s conviction, the actor-comedian’s accounts on Instagram and Facebook were both removed, subject of a law barring convicted sex offenders from those platforms. However, Brand and his wife maintain control of their YouTube channel as well as his Twitter account.
