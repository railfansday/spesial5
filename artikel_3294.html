Apple TV will stream all-female racing series F1 Academy starting next year

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Apple is coming up to the end of its first year as the exclusive broadcaster of Formula 1 in the United States, with the deal set to continue through 2031. So far, it seems to be going well for all parties involved.

In a sign of that relationship strengthening, the company announced it is expanding its racing coverage, with the all-female F1 Academy series joining the Apple TV platform next year.

The F1 Academy season starts on March 19, 2027. And with this news, you’ll be able to watch it through the Apple TV app with an Apple TV subscription, joining the Formula 1 coverage. The schedule runs across seven races through November.

Apple TV will feature full coverage of Academy qualifying and race sessions.

Since its inception in 2023, the F1 Academy series helps bring promote female talent in a sport that is currently dominated by male drivers.
