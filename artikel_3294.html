The superstar has teamed up with the home improvement retailer as an ambassador for its Black Friday campaign, which features hundreds of discounts on everything from tools to holiday decor and kitchen essentials. While this may be Brunson’s latest endeavor, the baller has kept busy on all fronts. Between appearing on SNL, making a cameo on Law & Order: SVU, and kicking off a new NBA season, he’s been making the rounds both on and off the court. It’s also far from his first brand partnership. He first teamed up with Lowe’s back in May and has since worked with several other companies, including Nike, Faherty, and Lingua Franca.

Why did Lowe’s feel like a natural partner to you?I feel like we embody a lot of the same qualities. One thing I grew up learning was the importance of serving others and helping people, and I feel like Lowe’s shares that same commitment. Our priorities align, so it felt natural to work with them.

Beyond your shared commitment to giving back, is there anything else about how Lowe’s approaches the holidays that resonated with you specifically?I think most importantly, the Black Friday sales have been really cool to be a part of. I’m really honored and grateful. Knowing that the first 150 MyLowe’s Rewards members at participating stores will get exclusive benefits and giveaways, with more than $15 million in prizes up for grabs, is really exciting. So, if you’re not a MyLowe’s Rewards member, it’s the right time to sign up.

Do you have any holiday traditions that are non-negotiable for you and your family?The only real non-negotiable is that we’re together. One thing I really love that my family does is keep an open house for people I work with, especially those who aren’t from New York or have family far away. We’re always happy to welcome anyone who needs a Thanksgiving or Christmas meal. Whoever wants to join us is welcome.

New York Knicks fans are famously passionate. Is there something about the city or its fans that still surprises you?Nothing surprises me anymore. The fans are going to be who they are. They’re unapologetic, they’re vocal, and they’re going to let you know when you’re playing great and when you’re playing bad. That’s just who they are, and it’s something I’ve grown to love. I wouldn’t want it any other way. They set a certain level of expectation for us, and we have to live up to it. They know we’re capable of it. That’s why they push us.

Is there a particular fan moment that sticks out to you, or do you tend to lump them all together?I put them all together, but the parade was absolutely absurd. There are some things I probably can’t talk about, but I saw a lot during the parade. The fans had been waiting for that for a long time, so they deserved to let loose and enjoy it.

When you’re warming up before a game, what’s on your playlist? What gets you in the zone?It depends on the day. My music taste is all over the place, but one thing that’s become a superstition for me is that the last song I listen to in my headphones has to be a Bieber song.

Do you have a favorite Bieber song, or is it truly just anything?“Company” is one of my favorite songs, but honestly, it could be anything.
