Texas TV Reporter Arrested for DWI, Found Allegedly Asleep in Car on Side of Highway

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

A TV reporter new to her latest on-air gig has been charged with DWI after she was allegedly found sleeping in her car on the side of a Texas highway.

Yasmine Cowan -- who reports for News 4 San Antonio and KABB -- was arrested Tuesday on suspicion of driving while intoxicated.
