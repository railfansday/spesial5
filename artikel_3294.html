Telegram’s Durov mocks the iPhone Duo as his app adds foldable support

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Telegram now works on Apple's foldable, but its CEO is not a fan of the phone.

Pavel Durov speaks at TechCrunch Disrupt Europe in Berlin in October 2013.

Telegram CEO Pavel Durov mocked Apple’s iPhone Duo on X, calling it “both too big and too small“, while announcing that Telegram now supports the foldable. The app switches to a two-column layout when the phone is opened.

Telegram has adapted its app for Apple’s first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo, and its CEO used the moment to mock the device. “Apple finally made a phone that’s both too big and too small,” Pavel Durov wrote on X on Thursday.

“It folds in half. Just like your enthusiasm,” he added.

Durov said Telegram still looked “incredible at both wrong sizes,” and joked that the company does “a lot of charity work.” He shared a short video of the app running on the foldable.

When the phone is closed, Telegram looks like its usual iPhone version, Brazilian Apple site MacMagazine reported. Opened, it switches to a two-column layout like the iPad app, with chats on one side and the conversation on the other.

The iPhone Duo starts at $1,999 and has a 5.4-inch outer screen and a 7.6-inch inner one. It sold out in minutes when preorders opened in October.

Reviewers praised the hinge but noted that many apps still needed updates for the bigger screen. Instagram chief Adam Mosseri said this week that designing for the Duo will be hard.

Durov has clashed with Apple before. In August, Apple briefly pulled Telegram from the App Store over a content violation. Durov said extortionists had found a way “to manipulate [Apple] into overreacting.”
