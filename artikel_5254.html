After 24 Million Launches, Just 312 Memecoins Are Above $100,000

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Home » Altcoins » After 24 Million Launches, Just 312 Memecoins Are Above $100,000

The rarest breed of meme currencies launched on Pump.Fun stay above a $100,000 market capitalization.

Pump.fun’s explosive memecoin-creation activity is colliding with a stark outcome: of roughly 24 million launches on the platform, only 312 tokens have climbed above a $100,000 valuation threshold. The gap has sharpened scrutiny of how much lasting value the memecoin launchpad is producing.

The numbers underscore the extreme attrition built into rapid-fire token launches. Vast volumes of new memecoins can create attention and trading bursts, but only a tiny fraction appear to develop enough liquidity, demand or staying power to clear even relatively modest valuation levels.

Pump.fun has become closely associated with the low-cost, high-speed creation of memecoins. That model can lower barriers for creators and traders, while also producing an overwhelming supply of projects competing for the same pool of capital.

With only 312 tokens reportedly surpassing $100,000 out of 24 million launches, the implied success rate is exceptionally small. The figure does not by itself establish whether individual launches were designed for long-term development, short-term trading or experimentation, but it highlights how quickly most tokens fade from view.

The DeFi platform is now facing broader questions about whether transaction activity and launch counts translate into a durable business model. High turnover may generate engagement, yet it can also leave traders exposed to thin liquidity and sharp price reversals once early interest disappears.

Concerns around memecoins have been amplified by the weak performance of PUMP amid softer market sentiment. When a platform-linked token struggles during a risk-off period, investors tend to look more closely at the underlying economics: user retention, trading demand and the quality of projects emerging from the ecosystem.

Pump.fun’s figures offer a blunt reminder for the wider crypto market: token creation is easy, but building liquidity and relevance is not. Investors weighing exposure to launchpad ecosystems will likely focus less on raw volume and more on whether those platforms can consistently produce assets that survive beyond the first burst of speculation.
