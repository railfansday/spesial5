Jennifer Lawrence’s ‘Terracotta’ Nails Are the Opposite of Every Winter Manicure Right Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

From rich burgundy to earthy olive, it’s natural to gravitate toward a dark and moody manicure as the days get shorter and cooler. But there’s a strong case to be made for embracing a lighter, fresher color in colder weather. Case in point: Jennifer Lawrence’s sun-kissed, “terracotta” nails.

At The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping premiere in New York City this week, Lawrence matched her soft, peachy makeup and Dior ensemble with a warm, reddish-tan manicure on short nails. It’s a tone that’s more muted than tangerine and brighter than a classic cocoa-beige hue.

With a sea of burgundy and bare nails sweeping the red carpet lately, this unexpected color enlivens winter skin yet remains understated, as the earthy, terracotta undertones keep it from looking neon. Plus, there is a joyful ease to the color that makes it especially appealing for those who prefer their manicures to be breezy and fun.
