DeepSeas: Lead Incident Response Analyst

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

DeepSeas owns cyber risk for its clients: autonomously, continuously, and with proof. The company brings security technology, threat intelligence, and expert practitioners together to deliver coordinated cyber defense, helping organizations strengthen protection while reducing the work required of their internal teams.

Combining agentic AI with human judgment, DeepSeas automates threat analysis and response within client-defined boundaries, continuously improves defenses through threat hunting and detection engineering, and provides visibility into investigations, actions taken, and service performance. Its technology-agnostic approach works with the security investments clients already have.

DeepSeas connects 24/7 managed detection and response with offensive security, vulnerability management, governance, risk and compliance, CISO advisory, and incident response. With 30 years of experience in cyber defense.

DeepSeas is trusted by nearly 1,000 clients around the world, including Fortune 100 enterprises and mid-market organizations. Its team of more than 250 cybersecurity professionals serves clients across 30+ countries.

Frost & Sullivan recognized DeepSeas as an Innovation Index standout in its 2025 Frost Radar™ for Managed Detection and Response and awarded the company its 2024 Competitive Strategy Leadership Award in the global MDR market.

The Lead IR Analyst is a pivotal leader within DeepSeas’ cybersecurity operations. This role not only ensures the rapid detection, analysis, and resolution of security incidents, but also drives cross-functional coordination and continuous improvement. Responsibilities include triaging alerts, conducting forensic investigations, and implementing corrective actions to mitigate threats. The Lead IR Analyst elevates the function by leading strategic initiatives, mentoring junior team members, and shaping the development of incident response tools and playbooks, ensuring the organization remains resilient and proactive in the face of evolving cyber threats.
