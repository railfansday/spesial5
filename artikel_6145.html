Beyond Eurobusiness: Lufthansa Puts 4-Abreast Premium Business Seats On 6 Airbus A320s

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Lufthansa has revealed its plans to fit select Airbus A320-200 narrowbody twinjets with wider business class recliners that are laid out four-abreast in a 2-2 setup. This marks a notable step forward for the Cologne-based Star Alliance founding member, which, like many of its fellow European flag carriers, has long favored the more flexible 'Eurobusiness' approach with blocked middle seats in six-abreast rows.

However, this tactic, while good at accommodating business class loads of varying sizes, has seen European airlines come under fire for offering what are often seen as sub-par premium products compared to the recliners of North American domestic first class. Now, the German flag carrier is looking to change its tune, with six A320s being fitted with recliners in time for an initial rollout on three routes.

In a statement released earlier today, Lufthansa confirmed that the change will be applied to the first two rows of the business class cabin on the A320s involved in the upgrade. This will see the carrier fit these jets with two four-abreast rows of spacious recliners, which it says will offer a "seat pitch of almost one meter." At almost 40 inches, this is broadly comparable to North American domestic first class.

Lufthansa has dubbed this product the 'Premium Business Seat,' with these recliners also offering "an adjustable backrest, an integrated leg rest and footrest, a fold-out table, and additional storage space right at the seat." The aircraft that feature these seats will also be fitted with Starlink WiFi during their refurb, offering faster connectivity inflight. Commenting on the initiative's commercial aim, Lufthansa says:

"The aim is to examine demand for additional comfort on short and medium-haul routes, as well as the acceptance of different booking options. The results will form the basis for deciding whether to expand the offering."

Lufthansa passengers can now book its new business class seats for flights from March 1 onwards on three short to medium-haul routes out of its main hub at Frankfurt Airport (FRA). The destinations in question are Athens Eleftherios Venizelos International Airport (ATH) in Greece, Cairo International Airport (CAI) in Egypt, and Nice Côte d'Azur Airport (NCE) in France. All are served multiple times a day.

According to scheduling data from Cirium, an aviation analytics company, Lufthansa plans to serve Cairo twice a day from Frankfurt in March 2027, with these flights taking just over four hours. Meanwhile, Nice is 90 minutes away and served two to four times a day, while passengers flying from Frankfurt to Athens with Lufthansa can expect three or four flights a day, typically taking just under three hours.

Interestingly, while the seats are only directly bookable from March 1, Lufthansa plans to initially begin rolling out the retrofitted jets "on various routes" as soon as December 16. As such, passengers flying on short-haul services operated by the German flag carrier may strike lucky without knowing it. According to ch-aviation, Lufthansa has 44 A320-200s, so the retrofit concerns 13.6% of the fleet.

It's the start of a new widebody era at the German flag carrier.

As it happens, Lufthansa will not be the only airline in the Lufthansa Group upgrading its narrowbody business class offering this winter. While traditionally more of a low-cost operator, Eurowings also offers four-abreast reclining business class seats on certain aircraft. It first revealed these plans back in May of 2025, with the product, known as Premium BIZ, launching on the Berlin-Dubai route that November.
