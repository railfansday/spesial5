Russia Legalizes Crypto Trading While XRP Stands Apart

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Home » Bitcoin (BTC) » Russia Legalizes Crypto Trading While XRP Stands Apart

Platforms are able to conduct business operations in crypto, offering retail knowledge-based accessibility.

Russia’s crypto bid is going at a faster pace since the fourth largest economy around the globe introduced crypto trading for retail & businesses. The new set of rules, coming from the Central Bank Of Russia, limits regular investors to a 300,000 rubles yearly cap (roughly $3,600).

This allows buying & holding Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH) & USDT through regulated intermediaries (brokers, trust managers & licensed exchanges). Everyone must pass a knowledge test plus review risk disclosures before starting trading activities, while XRP is considered an asset for advanced users only.

The Moscow Exchange already lists cash-settled perpetual futures (XRP/USDF) and related indices tracking XRP prices. These are available only to qualified investors, settle in rubles (no actual XRP is delivered), and form part of a broader set of crypto derivatives with substantial turnover among Futures traders across the scene.

The fresh legal framework dates back to August. Then, Russia’s President Vladimir Putin signed the inaugural comprehensive cryptocurrency law (Federal Law No. 282-FZ, “On Digital Currencies and Digital Rights”), setting September 1, 2026 activation window: contrastingly to the entry-level investor restrictions, qualified ones have no limits.

Meanwhile, businesses face hard limits, needing to meet a minimum equity requirement (15 million rubles, roughly $187,000) to run crypto services or custody services. The regulatory framework is way more lenient on businesses conducting cross-border sanctions to dodge the 21-chapter European Union sanction packages.

The retail crypto trading gets legalized on November 22, 2026, the related Duma documents state. The approved exchanges & crypto brokers are obliged to report the trading data and results to the central bank in accordance with existing reporting procedures for XRP & others.

For XRP traders in the region, the MOEX XRP Futures index allows up to 5x leverage.

Paying with XRP or any other popular crypto coin at a local store is still off the table. Goods & services are prohibited as a result of a separate ban, pushing crypto adoption in the perspective of an investment vehicle rather than a means of payment.

Still, setting up state-surveilled crypto brokers is a pretty big move that could inspire other major economies to do the same.
