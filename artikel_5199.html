Franklin Templeton's Crypto ETF to Add Hyperliquid: Will HYPE Price Recover?

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Franklin Crypto Index ETF is preparing to add Hyperliquid to its portfolio, according to a prospectus supplement filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on Wednesday.

The fund currently holds Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, Dogecoin, Solana, Cardano and Stellar.

The HYPE token will be held in spot, with its allocation determined by its weighting in the underlying CF Benchmarks index. The fund is expected to maintain a portfolio structure broadly aligned with the index.

The move comes as major trader Abraxas Capital reportedly exited its Hyperliquid position on Wednesday. The firm is said to have generated approximately $530 million in profits from the trade.

In September, Hashdex added HYPE to its Nasdaq CME Crypto Index ETF, with a 3.36% weighting.

Currently, there are three Hyperliquid ETFs: THYP and HYPG by 21Shares, and Bitwise's BHYP. Their combined assets, however, remain relatively low, currently under $259 million.

Hyperliquid is trading near $59.8, down 1.3% over the past 24 hours and 10.2% over the previous week.

The $55-$56 range is an immediate support area. A move above $60 could signal declining selling pressure, while a decline below the range could lead to more liquidations and further correction.

HYPE has slipped below its major moving averages, including the 200-day moving average near $68, reinforcing the bearish outlook.

Meanwhile, the RSI hovers around 35, indicating weakening momentum and approaching oversold conditions.

The market is also preparing for an important token unlock scheduled for Dec. 6. Reports estimate that the release could be worth $578 million at current prices.
