Bitwise XRP ETF Averages $14 Million in Daily Trading in Year One

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Bitwise XRP ETF traded an average of about $14 million a day over its first year on the market, resulting in over $3.5 billion in total trading volume, according to Bitwise.

In a post on X commemorating the Bitwise XRP ETF's first anniversary, Bitwise said the fund had recorded over $667 million in net inflows and had more than $376 million in assets under management (AUM) since launch.

Bitwise's XRP ETF started trading on Nov. 20, 2025, on NYSE Arca. It trades under the same ticker as the cryptocurrency it holds, XRP.

Investors can use the ETF's shares to track the price of the token through their brokerage accounts. The fund holds the XRP on behalf of investors. At launch, the ETF charged a 0.34% annual management fee. It also offered a fee waiver for the first $500 million in assets for the first month.

The $3.5 billion in trading volume is the total value of purchases and sales of the ETF's shares. This means the same ETF shares could trade repeatedly. If an investor sells their shares to another investor, that transaction increases trading volume. But the total XRP held in the fund doesn't change in that case.

On the other hand, the $667 million in net inflows represents the total dollar value of new shares issued over the past year minus redeemed shares.

AUM represents the total dollar value of a fund's assets at a specific moment. Since the fund's total assets are valued in dollars, AUM can fall below cumulative net inflows if the price of XRP declines, even as investors continue to put money into the fund.

The quarterly report filed by Bitwise with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) shows the impact of investor demand and XRP's price on the fund over the past year.

The net asset value of the fund increased from $299.1 million at the end of June to $614.9 million at the end of September.

Over $315.7 million was added to the fund's net assets during that quarter. Just over half of that $315.7 million came from new share creations.

The fund issued new shares with a total dollar value of $210.7 million. It also redeemed $32.8 million in shares during that quarter. So that left about $178 million in net new creations.

The fund issued 13.75 million shares and redeemed 2.42 million shares.
