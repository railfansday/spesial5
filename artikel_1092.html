Ravens quarterback Jackson to miss up to 10 weeks with thumb injury

Baltimore Ravens quarterback Lamar Jackson will miss between ‌six and 10 weeks as he recovers from a broken and ‌dislocated thumb.

Jackson suffered the injury on his first play from scrimmage in Baltimore's 36-10 loss to Buffalo Bills when his throwing hand was hit by pass rusher Greg Rousseau, but could still return this season if the struggling Ravens can get back into the postseason picture.

"Recovery timelines are challenging to predict, but injuries like this that require surgery typically take about 6-10 weeks to heal," said Baltimore head coach Jesse Minter.
