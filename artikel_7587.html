Jury Finds D.C. Man Guilty of Stabbing Brother to Death

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Joseph Smith, 68, of Washington, D.C., was found guilty yesterday in the Superior Court for the District of Columbia, on charges of stabbing his brother, Arnold Gregory “Doobie” Smith, to death inside their home in Southeast DC on April 9, 2019, announced U.S. Attorney Jeanine Ferris Pirro.
