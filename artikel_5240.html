Finland Turns Bitcoin Miners Into Discounted Home-Heating

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Home » Bitcoin (BTC) » Finland Turns Bitcoin Miners Into Discounted Home-Heating

In the town of Nokia, Finland, a 180-square-meter house now relies entirely on Bitcoin mining rigs for warmth.

During the frigid months of winter in Northern Europe, heating efficiency is the main topic of discussion even in the richest neighborhoods. The frequently hostile surroundings have urged heating specialists to come up with new, creative ways to replace traditional oil heating with cutting-edge technology.

That’s where Bitcoin (BTC) comes into play: Finnish homes have already adapted liquid-cooled mining machine heaters. They utilize the gains equipped from Bitcoin (BTC) mining venues to offset the costs of heating. On the other hand, that kind of solution is highly-dependent on the hash-rate difficulty across the network, among factors like general market conditions.

The next BTC block-reward halving comes in 2028, which could arguably cap the profits of this kind of business if Bitcoin (BTC) crosses $135,000. With installation costs exceeding $20,000, SunBit’s Bitcoin Boiler is aimed at relieving high-heating costs for the residents of Finland. The machine is supposed to work in-profit as long as Bitcoin’s price stays above $70,000.

For instance, in a historical house in Tampere, Finland’s third-largest city, mining rigs replaced wood burning, maintaining a 1,500-liter water tank between 50°C and 60°C. Sunbit’s liquid-cooled Bitcoin miner utilizes the Antminer S21 XP Hydro, employing a hash rate of up to 495T. The device consumes 5.3 kilowatts of power & draws about 46,400 kWh annually.

The Bitcoin Boiler device stores this heat in water, reaching temperatures up to 60 degrees Celsius, which is then circulated throughout the house via radiators and underfloor heating pipes. It costs roughly $5,200 a year to run. SunBit founder Elmeri Suomi stated: “The heating function itself is unaffected by market conditions; only the payback period changes.”

🇫🇮 🔥 Underground Bitcoin mining in Finland is used to heat its towns, instead of being wasted. pic.twitter.com/EHOfxcWq6M

Bitcoin-based heating systems have first been tested in the Netherlands, but Finland’s adoption mark has grown way further. The Bitcoin Boiler service fee is optional and includes 24/7 remote monitoring with automatic heating strategy optimization aligned with Finland’s floating electricity prices. This runs in a companion app with grid peak-shaving options for advanced enthusiasts.
