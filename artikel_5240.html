Strategy’s Michael Saylor has come up with a fresh tease after the U.S. midterm elections. This time he took to X and noted,

This also draws familiarity to his past pattern when he used to come up with new BTC teases on Sunadys and then add new BTC to Strategy’s treasury on Mondays.

However, this time things are a little different, as Strategy’s Bitcoin [BTC] investment has moved into unrealized losses as BTC has fallen below the company’s average purchase price of $75,451. With its holdings valued below the total acquisition cost, the company is now sitting on approximately $1 billion in paper losses, as per Bull Theory’s recent update.

Given the current market dynamics, this marks a sharp reversal from just a month ago, when Strategy’s Bitcoin position carried nearly $10 billion in unrealized profits. This comes as Strategy held 849,327 BTC, worth approximately $62.98 billion.

And, on the stock front, it’s preferred stock STRC was trading at $99.80, up 0.34% over the past 24 hours, while its common stock, MSTR, shares stood at $128.93, down 0.29% during the same period. But Bitcoin’s price had fallen to $74,110.28, marking a 2.2% decline over the past 24 hours.

Remarking on the same, crypto analyst Benjamin Cowen argued that Bitcoin’s Q4 2026 decline draws familiarity to a pattern seen in 2014, 2018, and 2022. In each of those years, a correction in the equity market came on the heels of weakness in Bitcoin.

People can mock these ideas (and they have), but it does not stop the events from happening all the same.

Meanwhile, another analyst highlighted that BTC was approaching its fifth consecutive weekly red candle, which, if formed, would mark its longest losing streak since the six-week decline recorded in January and February 2026.

Taken together, these point to weakening market momentum and persistent bearish pressure.

Needless to say, these factors might have been the reason Strategy saw most of its paper gains accumulated during the 2025 rally disappear in 2026. With the PnL around -102 million at press time, it indicates that investors sold at a loss, popularly known as the capitulation phase.

Meanwhile, the MVRV ratio also fell from approximately 1.8 in 2025 to around 1.0 in November 2026. This further placed the average holder close to their break-even level, following a period when the market was underwater.

All of this coincided with Strategy acquiring $208 million worth of STRC and retaining $4.9 billion in USD reserves to support future purchases and financial obligations just recently.
