Maignan injury concern for Milan after forced off for France

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Milan are waiting for news from France after goalkeeper Mike Maignan was forced off at half-time during the Nations League match with Turkiye due to injury.

The shot-stopper had started in Bordeaux this evening, as Les Bleus are battling Italy and Belgium for qualification from Group A1 into the quarter-finals.

However, it was Guillaume Restes who came out for the restart and took on the gloves.

Initial reports suggest that Maignan asked to come off due to a muscular issue in his thigh, which would not be the first time for the shot-stopper.

It remains to be seen if he stopped in time to avoid further damage, or if Milan will be forced to do without their goalkeeper for a while.

The Rossoneri are more concerned than most because second choice goalkeeper Pietro Terracciano has also been out injured for several weeks, so it would be third pick Lorenzo Torriani to take his place.
