Sean Strickland's Reaction to Usman Nurmagomedov Jumping Merab Dvalishvili

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

UFC star Sean Strickland has now responded to a violent clash in which Usman Nurmagomedov appeared to jump Merab Dvalishvili.

Dvalishvili revealed the news on social media, sharing footage of himself bloodied as he called out Nurmagomedov while receiving treatment for his injuries. The Georgian explained that he was in a hotel lobby in Abu Dhabi when the clash occurred.

Footage of the incident has since emerged online, showing Nurmagomedov and others attacking the 35-year-old and a friend. Strickland is among some big names to react to the saga, and his comments have caused a stir.

Two-time UFC Middleweight Champion Strickland did not hold back as he tore into Nurmagomedov:

"Usman Nurmagomedov is the definition of a b****.. These guys all run around pretending to be hard but they all act like f****** dogs waiting to attack you when youre not looking...."

However, he then introduced religion into his remark, adding: "Is this Muslim behavior, or is he just a b****?"

Unsurprisingly, Strickland's strong take on the fight, coupled with his Islamophobic language, has sparked a strong response online. Over one thousand comments were left under his post on X in the space of just a few hours.

Strickland isn't the only UFC star to offer his thoughts on the matter. Although he later deleted the post, Ilia Topuria shared footage of the clash and added: "You’re a bunch of cowards, and you’ll be held accountable for what you’ve done. You’re anything but real men."

Dvalishvili has spoken out on the incident.

Footage appears to show Dvalishvili taken by surprise as Nurmagomedov threw the first punch. He later took to social media to call out the seemingly unprovoked attack:

“What a sneaky way, guys, just two of us came here in Abu Dhabi, and Usman Nurmagomedov and his friends came like snakes from behind, what kind of fighters are you? What kind of men are you? You came from behind, there is just two of us and you guys were prepared, what the f***?” While still receiving medical care, he continued:

“We bleed, we fight, we work, you guys are just sneaky guys, you are not real men, what the f***, if you want to fight let’s fight, we are fighters, bro. You guys came from behind, all of your friends waiting for us in a sneaky way. I knew what kind of guys you are… We were fighting like real men, 20 guys vs just two guys, we are warriors, you are p******."

The Georgian previously met and beat Nurmagomedov at UFC 311 in January 2025, and they've clashed publicly since. Earlier this year, Dvalishvili responded to disparaging comments made by the Russian, stating:

“Umar and some small people keep talking trash about me. You guys probably don’t know anything about life, but I am a man, and if you come to me, pulling me over, disrespect me, start loud talk with me in a different language, I will slap your face. Because that’s who I am. That’s how I grew up. That’s my culture and that’s what you’re going to get.

Dvalishvili is scheduled to take on Marlon Moraes this Saturday, but his injuries may now prevent him from taking part in the RAF Abu Dhabi fight.
