McKennie shuts down Manchester United rumours: ‘Not planning to leave Juventus’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Weston McKennie has shut down recent rumours that he could be set to join Mauricio Pochettino at Manchester United and insists: ‘I am not planning to leave Juventus’.

McKennie’s former USA head coach, Pochettino, arrived at Old Trafford on Wednesday to begin his tenure as head coach of Manchester United.

Since the Argentine’s appointment was announced last week, there have already been reports linking several USMNT players with a move to United, including Juventus’s McKennie and Milan’s Christian Pulisic.

La Gazzetta dello Sport reported on Sunday that McKennie is among the players that Pochettino would like to bring to Old Trafford in January, despite the fact that the American signed a long-term contract extension back in March.

McKennie, who has been linked with moves away from Juventus on several occasions in the past, has become an increasingly important and versatile player over the last few years, and of the current Bianconeri squad, he has made more competitive appearances than any other player (241).

McKennie has recently clarified that he has no intention to leave Juventus in the near future and that he ‘can’t wait’ to get back to work after the recent international break.

“I want to clarify I am not planning to leave Juventus,” the Juventus midfielder explained (via Fabrizio Romano).

“I am happy where I am. That’s why I signed a new deal at Juve and I can’t wait to continue here.”
