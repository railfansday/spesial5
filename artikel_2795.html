Pitcher Landon Echelberry commits to Tennessee baseball in 2028 class

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Right-handed pitcher Landon Echelberry committed to Tennessee baseball on Nov. 17, becoming the Vols' eighth commit in the class of 2028.

"I am beyond blessed and excited to announce that I am committed to the University of Tennessee to continue my academic and baseball career," he wrote in an Instagram post. "Thank you to the Tennessee coaching staff for believing in me and giving me this opportunity."

Echelberry picked the Vols over Georgia, Florida State, Mississippi State, West Virginia and Alabama.

He is ranked by Perfect Game as the No. 95 prospect in his class and No. 15 right-handed pitcher. Prep Baseball Report ranks him as the nation's No. 33 player in the country and No. 5 right-handed pitcher.

Echelberry is from Oregon, Ohio, where he attends Clay High School and also plays football as a quarterback. As a sophomore last spring, he had a 1.50 ERA in 56 innings with 80 strikeouts and 13 walks. His fastball reached as high as 96 mph.

Echelberry is the fourth right-handed pitcher to join coach Josh Elander's 2028 class along with AJ Dungan, Mason Little and Trigg Listerman. The Vols' other 2028 commits are infielder Ian Jansen, outfielder Hudson Oliver, first baseman/outfielder Keelan Zumwalt and catcher Logan Arnett.
