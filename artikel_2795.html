Jiri Prochazka vs. Jan Blachowicz? ‘Let’s fight … now or never’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Former UFC light heavyweight champion, Jiri Prochazka, is coming off a crushing loss to undefeated contender Navajo Stirling, one that all but eliminated "BJP" from the 205-pound title race.

That's probably why the 43-year-old Blachowicz (29-12-2), who also held the light heavyweight title, was on social media earlier today calling for a Prochazka fight.

"If I have to lose, to see the things clearly, I accept," the 34-year-old Prochazka (32-7-1) wrote on Instagram about last weekend's Stirling loss at UFC Qatar. "Accept the lesson, but always get up. CHAMPION MINDSET TO ALL stay fu—ing positive and understand that all here is a free choice. BE ABOVE. I AM ACCEPTING TO FEEL THIS S—T, AND THAT GIVES ME HOPE, BELIEVE AND BRIGHT LIGHT TO GO THROUGH THIS."

One fan commented, "Dude it ain't that deep, you just have to put your hands up mid-fight."
