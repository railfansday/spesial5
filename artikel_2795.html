Giants shocked to learn Colts cut ex-teammate Daniel Jones

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

John Harbaugh's eyes nearly popped out of his head upon learning that the Colts cut quarterback Daniel Jones.

Harbaugh wasn't the Giants' head coach when they stunningly cut Jones in November 2024, so they don't have the personal relationship that Jones maintains with others in East Rutherford. But the Colts are next week's opponent, so the Giants' behind-the-scenes advance-scouting staffers were just thrown a major curveball.

The Colts plan to start first-round draft bust Anthony Richardson, which means an offense that could be more option-based in store on Nov. 29 to hide Richardson's passing inaccuracy.

Daniel Jones has been released by the Colts. Getty Images

"[It changes] probably pretty dramatically. I mean, wow,'" Harbaugh admitted. "I don't want to get ahead of ourselves here, and you just hit me with that. But definitely the offense will be the offense that they run, but it'll be centered around the quarterback for sure, and they're probably different styles to some degree, so we'll have to look at that when the time comes. We have people doing that this week. They'll have to make a left turn, obviously."

Unlike when the Giants demoted Jones from starter to third-stringer to protect themselves against an injury clause in his contract, Jones did not ask the Colts for his release, head coach Shane Steichen said.

In the Giants locker room, news of Jones' release brought back memories. What does it say to teammates when you release the quarterback who started the first 11 games?

"That the writing is on the wall for the season and you have to get through the next six games the best you can play," right tackle Jermaine Eluemunor told The Post. "D.J. is a great guy but he's in such a predicament. He went there last year and balled out, so it's definitely not a 'him' thing. When you trade everyone like they did, it's hard."

John Harbaugh, head coach of the Giants. Getty Images

Giants backup offensive lineman Josh Sills was with the Colts last season and to start 2025. He saw the same hard-working Jones that was beloved by Giants teammates.

"He helped me in any and every way imaginable," Sills told The Post. "He went above and beyond to make sure everyone had what they needed to achieve their goals. It's a team game. It takes 11 guys."

Anthony Richardson will now start at quarterback for the Colts. Getty Images

Next week was billed as the Jones Revenge Game. Instead, Jones didn't even make it through the first year of the two-year, $88 million contract that he signed after a torn Achilles.

Jones might be able to help the Patriots or Ravens in their playoff chase. Or he could help the Eagles not be an embarrassment the rest of the season. Or use his ties to Broncos offensive coordinator Davis Webb to become the backup for a Super Bowl contender.

Any team that claims him off waivers will inherit $2.1 million in salary this season and a $10.5 million injury guarantee for 2027.

Returning to the Giants or reuniting with Brian Daboll in Tennessee are extremely unlikely possibilities.

"I wish the best for him and I know he's going to make the most of his next opportunity," left tackle Andrew Thomas said. "But I'm focused on what we have going on and we're not looking at Indy right now. We're focused on this week and Jacksonville."
