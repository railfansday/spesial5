Cardinals WR Michael Wilson out for season with a neck injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Cardinals have lost their top wide receiver for the rest of the season.

Head coach Mike LaFleur said at his Monday press conference that Michael Wilson will miss the rest of the team’s games. Wilson suffered a fracture in his neck a few snaps into Sunday’s loss to the Seahawks and will be placed on injured reserve.

Wilson had 51 catches for 546 yards and two touchdowns in the first nine games of the season. He signed a three-year extension with the team after posting 78 catches for 1,006 yards last season.

The Cardinals traded Marvin Harrison Jr. to the Texans earlier this season. Simi Fehoko, Devin Duvernay, Reggie Virgil, and Harrison Wallace are the remaining wide receivers on the Arizona roster.
