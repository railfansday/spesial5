What social media is saying about Florida's rivalry week win over FSU

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

For the third straight season, the Florida Gators prevailed over the Florida State Seminoles. This year, the Gators marched up to Tallahassee and ground out a 26-10 victory over their hated rivals thanks to some big defensive plays.

There are plenty of words to describe the matchup inside Doak Campbell Stadium, but none of them are particularly flattering. There was no shortage of bad sportsmanship while the play on the field left a lot to be desired on both sides.

Nonetheless, the Orange and Blue finished on top for the fifth time in the last seven games against FSU after losing five straight and seven of eight during the previous decade. It also gave Florida just its second eight-win season in the last six years.
