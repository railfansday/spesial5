Arthur Maulet will be remembered fondly in Baltimore as a key part of Mike Macdonald's elite 2023 defense. He posted the best coverage stats of his career, and his slot blitzes were the stuff of legend. A lingering injury sidelined him for most of 2024 and led to his release the following offseason.

Maulet was originally a Saints undrafted rookie signing in 2017 and spent part of the 2018 season with the Colts. He then carved out rotational roles on defense during two-year stints with the Steelers and the Jets before arriving in Baltimore in 2023. He spent his final season in Detroit, appearing in nine games amid the Lions' many secondary injuries.

Maulet is on a fascinating list of players who played on both sides of one of the NFL's biggest rivalries. Amont them are three of his 2023 teammates – Patrick Queen, Malik Harrison, and Chuck Clark – as well as Hall of Fame safety Rod Woodson and two-time All-Pro running back Le'Von Bell. Then there are less notable but still memorable names like Mike Wallace, Miles Boykins, Chris Wormley, Diontae Johnson, DeShon Elliott, and Jacoby Jones, among others.

The October acquisition of Jurgens from the Eagles met a need. Then last week, Baltimore filled a gaping hole at quarterback by trading for quarterback Jacoby Brissett. They're hoping the 11-year veteran can keep them afloat in case Lamar Jackson returns from thumb surgery in time for a late-season playoff run.

The Ravens traded for Jacoby Brissett just to have some hope on offense. There was none with Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley on IR. It was a little surprising to see a fourth-round pick involved, though Baltimore got a fifth-round pick back. But that move without any follow-up is a bit odd: Brissett isn't going to save the season, there was nothing else added to help out but yet the Ravens traded picks to be … what exactly?
