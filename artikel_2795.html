All six Thanksgiving teams won on Sunday

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Madden movie failed to properly honor the great John Madden. The franchises that will play on Thanksgiving have done their part to make up for it.

All six of the teams that will take the field on the annual day the NFL uses to recognize and remember Madden emerged with wins in the early window on Sunday.

The Lions beat the Bucs, 31-21. The Bears suffocated the Saints, 21-13. The Eagles continued the Steelers' inability to win in Philly, 19-16. The Cowboys overwhelmed the Titans, 51-30. And the Chiefs and Bills made easy work of the Cardinals and Dolphins, respectively, 34-7 and 26-9.

It sets up a trio of consequential games on Thursday. The 8-2 Bears at the 8-2 Lions could be the most significant early-window Thanksgiving game of all time. The Eagles-Cowboys game has some added sizzle, now that the Cowboys have a chance to get to .500. And the 8-2 Chiefs at the 9-1 Bills should feel like a playoff game.

With all six teams winning on Sunday, everybody wins on Thursday.

And the game between the 6-4 Packers and the 6-4 Rams the night before the tripleheader should be a good one, too. Not to mention the Black Friday game between the likely 8-2 Broncos and the suddenly desperate 6-4 Steelers.
