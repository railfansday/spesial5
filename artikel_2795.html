Commanders injury report: Treylon Burks will play, Lucas Patrick will not

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Commanders receiver Treylon Burks didn't practice on Wednesday, the last day of prep before Thursday night's showdown with the New York Giants. That's not the negative development that it sounds like.

That became clear on the official injury report, where Burks was not given a designation. He will play this key upcoming contest, a welcome return after he missed Washington's loss to the Rams.

The Commanders did rule center Lucas Patrick (concussion) and tight end Colson Yankoff (knee) out of this game. No one was considered questionable.

Cornerback Amik Robertson (ankle) was limited on Wednesday but is also available to play the Giants. On the Giants side, quarterback J.J. McCarthy (hip) and running back Cam Skattebo (hamstring) didn't receive a playing designation and will play against Washington at MetLife Stadium.

C Lucas Patrick – Is in the concussion protocol

TE Colson Yankoff – A knee injury suffered in Week 8

WR Treylon Burks – Suffered foot injury vs 49ers, missed last week's game

TE John Bates – Hurt his shoulder vs. Eagles

QB Jayden Daniels – Still listed with Week 2 elbow dislocation

CB Amik Robertson – Incurred an ankle injury in Week 9

TE Chig Okonkwo – Dealing with a hip injury from Rams game

DL Shy Tuttle – Has a shoulder issue from Week 8
