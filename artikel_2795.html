YouTube Gold: Cooper Flagg Is Well On His Way To Greatness

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Former Duke star Cooper Flagg is now in his second NBA season, and so far, he's just crushing it. He's still 19 – he won't turn 20 until next month – and he's not playing like a normal young player.

He's moving closer to an elite level. Flagg is averaging 25.9 points, 6.9 rebounds, 6.9 assists, 2.2 steals, and just under a block per game.

Last year, you'll remember, he hit 51 points on April 3 against fellow former Dukie Paolo Banchero.

As a rookie, like most rookies, it took him some time to settle into the pace of the game. He appears to have done so now, and the question is: what's his ceiling?

He still needs to improve his three-point shooting, but you can't just foul him: he's hitting 90.3 percent from the line.

More importantly, as this video discusses, he's learning to use his team as an instrument on offense. Lots of guys can score tons of points, but relatively few learn to see their team that way.

He wasn't a basketball player, but Wayne Gretzky did that in hockey. He could see where the puck needed to be and got it a pass or two away from being there.

Flagg is doing something similar for Dallas, but he's also playing brilliantly on defense. He's a superb weapon on both ends of the court.

Add it all up, and the Mavericks have an immensely promising player on their hands, and he's still a good ways from his full potential.
