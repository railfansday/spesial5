Why Warriors' Draymond Green baffled by ‘unbelievable' Grand Theft Auto VI hype

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Why Warriors' Draymond Green baffled by 'unbelievable' Grand Theft Auto VI hype originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area

Draymond Green doesn't understand the buzz around Grand Theft Auto VI.

The much-awaited open-adventure video game reportedly made an estimated $3.79 billion worldwide on its opening day, but the Warriors forward wasn't its target audience.

On Sunday's episode of "The Draymond Green Show," Green explained his perspective on the craze for GTA VI around the Golden State locker room and at large.

"The young guys on our team were so excited about this game coming out," Green said. "And obviously, I'm not a dinosaur. I see out in the world, everybody's excited about this game coming out. So I will tell you the excitement level, it's unbelievable. I must say, I don't understand people calling out of work to play a video game. And by the way, to all you GTAers out there, kudos to you. Much respect, much love. Your thing is your thing. I just don't understand it because it's not my thing."

Green said he is "not a video game player" himself, and the last GTA game he recalled playing was Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, released in 2004.

The 36-year-old Green also commented on the game's effect on the team's participation, saying the Warriors have not had "attendance issues."

"I've heard that's been a lot in the workforce, but those types of things don't really happen in the NBA unless you have guys on your team that just don't care, and they book it and fake an injury," Green said. "We don't have that thankfully. So shout out to the GTAs out there."

Perhaps Green has other hobbies that pique his interests.

The Warriors veteran spent a considerable amount of time on X firing off responses to Phoenix Suns commentator Eddie Johnson before the Warriors' 88-87 win over the Boston Celtics.

Green was fired up as Golden State ended its five-game losing streak, hitting three 3-pointers and locking up Jayson Tatum to seal the victory.

As long as Green's on-court production stays relatively stable, the Warriors can live with his video game takes.
