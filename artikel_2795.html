Thanksgiving Day Triple-Header Discussion Thread

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Hello, and from all of us here at Battle Red Blog, we'd like to wish you and yours a very happy Thanksgiving.

But is it a happy Thanksgiving in terms of quality football?

We have three, count 'em three games for your amusement and eventual post-fourth-piece-of-pie-background-nap-noise. In the opening salvo, hailing from the NFC North, it's the Detroit Lions annual Turkey Day game and this one might actually be good!

We have the 8-2 Detroit Lions hosting the also 8-2 Chicago Bears in a game with legitimate playoff seeding stakes on the line. It doesn't get much more important than that.

And then you have to take a nap while you get over how much you gorged yourself on food and football during the Lions game. That's where the Cowboys and Eagles come in. They play, nobody gets offended, except Cowboys fans and that's just funny, nobody really cares. It's just a moment to sit in Jerry Jones' little egg.

And then the nightcap when you know what, maybe you'll get another piece of pie because you only live once, we have what promises to be a game with real AFC playoff stakes on the line as well. it's the 8-2 Chiefs going to Buffalo and their new stadium to take on the 9-1 Bills.

Two thirds of this slate is worth watching, and the third is prime napping territory. But we can't tell you what to do with your life, only that speaking for me and those around me, I will be watching the football.
