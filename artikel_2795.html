Oklahoma State QB Drew Mestemaker questionable with stinger vs. Arizona State

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Oklahoma State football is hitting record-breaking feats with quarterback Drew Mestemaker. Which has grabbed the attention of NFL Draft pundits. But the Cowboys got hit with this setback before facing Arizona State.

The electric dual-threat QB is listed as questionable before the Saturday showdown. This marks the first time Mestemaker could miss a game in 2026.

So Mestemaker's situation rises as a major curveball for this crucial Big 12 contest. ASU head coach Kenny Dillingham spoke highly of Mestemaker too.

"They have the No. 1 potential pick in the NFL Draft. He's really good. I'd be lying if we didn't start with him. He's one of the best players in college football," Dillingham said to reporters Wednesday morning.

Mestemaker swiftly changed the culture post coach Mike Gundy. Former North Texas head coach Eric Morris helped get Mestemaker over from Denton to Stillwater.

Oklahoma State and its new star QB put the nation on notice by stunning previous No. 2 ranked Oregon early in September. That 39-31 upset got the Cowboys into the top 25. However, Oklahoma State has since gone unranked including taking a disastrous 52-27 loss to Texas Tech.

Broderick Vehrs rises as the potential starter if Mestemaker ends up sitting this game out. Vehrs completed two passes out of seven attempts for 20 yards in limited duty against the Red Raiders. Mestemaker struggled against the 2025 Big 12 champs, completing 27-of-42 for 277 yards, one touchdown and two interceptions.

Oklahoma State is bowl eligible with its 6-4 record. They haven't gone bowling since 2023 under Gundy.
