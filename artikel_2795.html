Broncos activate RB Tyler Badie and place DL Sai’vion Jones on the IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Broncos made several roster moves today. First, according to 9NEWS Denver's Mike Klis, the Denver Broncos have activated running back Tyler Badie from the injured reserve. He was designated to return earlier this week and will take the roster spot of veteran JK Dobbins, who was placed on the reserve/suspended list. Klis also reported that the team is placing second-year defensive lineman Sai'vion Jones on the injured reserve. Jones suffered a hamstring injury vs. the Raiders last Sunday and now will be sidelined for at least the next four games. With that open roster spot, Klis added that the team is signing running back Cody Schrader from the practice squad to the active roster.

So, for tomorrow's game vs. the Pittsburgh Steelers, the Broncos running back room will be rookie Jonah Coleman coming off a 100+ yard, 3-touchdown performance, third down/pass-catching specialist Tyler Badie, and Cody Schrader serving as the RB3. Sean Payton announced earlier in the week that the team suspended veteran running back JK Dobbins for one game due to conduct detrimental to the team, so the Broncos will move forward without him this week.

Also, second-year back RJ Harvey is eligible to come off the injured reserve next week, so the Broncos may need to make a decision when it comes to their backfield after Friday's game.

I would expect Badie to serve as the Broncos RB2 tomorrow and come in on third downs and be the change-of-pace back for rookie Jonah Coleman. This season, Badie has 6 carries for 22 yards and 6 receptions for 67 yards.

With Sai'vion Jones landing on the injured reserve, the back end of the Broncos' defensive line depth is being tested. Jones joins Uwazurike on the IR, and rookie Tyler Onyedim and reserve Jordan Jackson are expected to see added playing time moving forward. The team also signed veteran nose tackle Khalen Sanders to their practice squad, so he could provide some added depth moving forward as well.

I believe this will be a big opportunity for Onyedim to see some added snaps behind Zach Allen, Malcolm Roach, and DJ Jones.

9NEWS Denver's Mike Klis also noted that the Broncos are elevating rookie offensive lineman Gavin Ortega to the game-day roster. This will be his third and final elevation this season.
