Browns roster adds DLine help; once again, waive recent draft pick and injured DT

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Despite being on their bye week, not everything is rest for the Cleveland Browns. Not only do fans need to change their plans for the Browns next game, which has been flexed to Sunday Night Football for Week 12 against the Las Vegas Raiders, but GM Andrew Berry rarely takes a week off from making changes to the roster.

On Tuesday, Berry made three moves with Cleveland's roster:

Claimed DE Jamree Kromah off waivers from the Miami Dolphins

Once again waived TE Carsen Ryan off the 53-man roster

Waived DT Elijah Chatman off the injured reserve list

Despite being on and off the active roster, Ryan has played in nine of the Browns 10 games this season, with 126 of his 129 snaps coming on special teams.

Kromah is in his first season in the NFL after going undrafted out of James Madison. He played two games for the Chicago Bears and two with the Dolphins before being waived this week. His NFL draft profile was as follows:

Kromah posted impressive impact production in 2023 after playing a bigger role than he had in the previous four seasons between Rutgers and James Madison. He's muscular with very long arms and might have the frame for additional weight to bump inside. Kromah features jarring pop in his hands but might lack enough fluidity and athleticism to capitalize on it as a pro. He's an effort rusher who is unlikely to create enough consistent pressure to warrant rush-down snaps, but he does have a shot at making the back end of a roster as a base end or future 3-technique.
