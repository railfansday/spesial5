NFL Network's Daniel Jeremiah shares encouraging take on Jets organization

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Optimism has been in short supply around the New York Jets for quite some time. After years of disappointing seasons, failed quarterback experiments, and organizational resets, fans have understandably grown tired of hearing about what might happen next. Here's a silver lining for that dark cloud.

One respected NFL draft analyst believes there are legitimate reasons to think the franchise could be approaching a breakthrough.

NFL Network draft wiz Daniel Jeremiah recently offered an encouraging assessment of the Jets' outlook, suggesting the organization could be in position to make a significant jump next season.

"I could see a world where the Jets are a playoff team next year. They have a lot of young talent on offense and an elite defensive piece to build around. Plus a million draft picks."

For general manager Darren Mougey, the challenge now is turning those ingredients into a competitive roster. The Jets have accumulated significant draft capital and young talent, but the upcoming offseason will be an important test of whether the organization can finally turn its rebuilding efforts into meaningful progress.

Of course, optimism about the future doesn't erase the franchise's current reality. The Jets own the NFL's longest active playoff drought, having missed the postseason for 15 consecutive seasons. Their last appearance came after the 2010 season, when they reached the AFC Championship Game before falling to the Pittsburgh Steelers.

New York isn't expected to end that drought this season, leaving another offseason of questions and roster decisions ahead. Still, Jeremiah's assessment offers a different perspective on an organization that has spent far too long searching for a reason to believe.

The Jets have plenty left to prove, particularly at quarterback and in turning their draft assets into meaningful results. But if Mougey can make the right moves and the young talent continues to develop, perhaps Jeremiah's playoff prediction won't sound quite so far-fetched.
