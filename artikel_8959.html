developed with travelers aged 75+, moove on reimagines luggage as discreet mobility aid

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

combining luggage storage, walking support and an integrated resting perch, moove on by lorenzo rimini challenges the conventional aesthetics of assistive products.

Lorenzo Rimini edited by christina vergopoulou November 26, 2026 3 min

Designed by Lorenzo Rimini, Moove On reconfigures the conventional rolling suitcase into a multi-functional mobility aid for older travelers. Combining standard luggage storage with physical walking support and an integrated resting perch, the product moves away from the clinical aesthetic typically associated with assistive medical devices. Informed by field observations across European transit environments, user interviews, and co-design sessions with nine participants aged 75 and older, the design calibrates its proportions, internal compartment layout, load-bearing frame, and seating height around the physical ergonomics of later-life travel.

Developed by designer Lorenzo Rimini over an eight-month period, the project progressed through 45 iterative prototypes examining alternative form factors, handle geometries, structural reinforcements, and functional configurations. Moving from initial 3D printed scale studies to full-scale mockups, Rimini built a 1:1 Plexiglas prototype to verify spatial organization, balance, and visual proportions. The resulting object merges everyday travel utility with structural stability, supporting independent movement without visually signaling disability.
