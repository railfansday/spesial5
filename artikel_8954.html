Tochigi JIMINIE Club Study Center Mikamo / TAISEI DESIGN Planners Architects & Engineers

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Text description provided by the architects. This is a youth education facility, built as a PFI (Public Finance Initiative) project, located at the foot of the prefectural Mikamoyama Park in Tochigi City. Intended mainly as an education facility for elementary school and junior high school students, the park was designed to serve as a place for "learning through hands-on experiences" by architecturally translating and spatially deploying Tochigi's cultural elements and locally available materials.
