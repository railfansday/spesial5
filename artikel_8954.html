Aedas Invites Architecture Graduates Across Saudi Arabia to Enter its 2027 Architecture Graduate Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

A chance for final-year students and recent graduates to have their work seen, exhibited andrecognised by the profession.Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — November 2026Aedas, a global architecture and design practice with projects across the Kingdom, hasopened entries for the Aedas Architecture Graduate Awards 2027 – Saudi Arabia. Final-yeararchitecture students and recent graduates across the Kingdom can enter the graduationproject they have already completed, with no new work required. Shortlisted projects willbe exhibited, with three winners and two Honorable Mentions recognized.Building on years of supporting architecture students and graduates across the region,including in the UAE and Egypt, Aedas now brings that support together under oneprogram, with editions in other countries to follow. The awards reflect the practice'scommitment to nurturing local talent and giving back to the communities it works in,beyond the buildings it designs.
