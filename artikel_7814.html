Conesus man pleads guilty to production of child pornography

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

U.S. Attorney Michael DiGiacomo announced today that Donald Coty, 40, of Conesus, NY, pleaded guilty before U.S. District Judge Meredith A. Vacca to production and possession of child pornography. The charges carry a mandatory minimum penalty of 15 years in prison and a maximum of 30 years.
