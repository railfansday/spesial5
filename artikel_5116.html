XRP Repeats 2019 Record With 91.4% Transactions Surge on Binance

Deposits of XRP's on Binance printed a 91.4% increase on Nov. 26, marking the highest level of inflows since 2019. The withdrawal operations tumbled down to 8%, suggests data shared by CryptoQuant.

The fact that XPR has been trading around the $1 price level attracted a substantial volume of deposits. It's not uncommon for assets to trade around important psychological thresholds and attract selling pressure.

As the CryptoQuant's data suggests, the latest Binance deposit value has hit more than a 91% increase. The attached chart is tracking the relation between deposits and withdrawals, not the amounts transferred themselves.

Higher deposits only guarantees the growth of balances on exchange wallets, not necessarily meaning the increase in selling pressure.

As we've mentioned before, important psychological resistances quite often attract an increase in selling pressure. It's not uncommon for investors to close positions at around breakeven points.

Trading volume profiles around the $1 mark on XRP support the thesis–spikes in trading activity on both bearish and bullish side reflects market recomposition: investors at breakeven points look for a lossless trade, while others accumulate.

XRP's daily chart supplies one more weaker market backdrop; in turn arguably, the token traded around $0.95 on Nov. 26 and was quoted at approximately $0.979. Despite the rebound, XRP remained below the moving averages displayed on this daily chart, and also, simultaneously, its Relative Strength Index stood near 41.

The price hasn't yet shown any clear indication over upcoming direction change. The most recent rebound is purely speculative and technical: the price is yet to hit the 26 EMA or create a pressure on bears' positions.

The worst part of the current recovery is the descending volume profile. With the price increase followed by the gradual volume decrease, prices tend to lose momentum quicker and rallies fade.
