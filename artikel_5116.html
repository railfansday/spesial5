Darius Dale: 2027 Should Be a “Great Year for Bitcoin” as Fed Rate Cuts Come

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Darius Dale, Founder & CEO of 42 Macro, joins BMTV to answer whether the Fed is making a policy mistake with a December rate hike priced in.

Darius Dale, Founder & CEO of 42 Macro, joins BMTV to answer whether the Fed is making a policy mistake with a December rate hike priced in. He says it isn’t, because a policy mistake would mean over-tightening the economy into a significant slowdown, and by 42 Macro’s models the U.S. is nowhere near a recession. He describes an economy that is booming, with inflation as a lagging indicator to growth that is still running hotter than the Fed wants. Looking further out, he says surprising and dramatic easing in 2027 and beyond is still on the table and highly likely to occur. He also agrees that 2027 should be a great year for Bitcoin.
