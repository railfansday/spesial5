Flares turn Stamford Bridge red as ‘30,000 Fenerbahce fans’ take over Chelsea’s home

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Stamford Bridge was lit up last night by Fenerbahce fans' flares as Chelsea's home ground continued to host Shakhtar Donetsk's Champions League games.

The Ukrainian giants are this season playing their home European games in west London due to Russia's ongoing invasion of their home country, having in previous seasons staged their home games in Poland, Germany and Slovenia.

The matches have attracted local and travelling Ukrainian football fans, along with thousands of rivals supporters as Greek side AEK Athens and Portuguese outfit Sporting came to town earlier this season. Real Madrid will visit at the end of January.

Wednesday night saw Fenerbahce as the visiting team, and the fanbase came in numbers.

Stamford Bridge was glowing red from the light of flares ahead of the game, while footage of supporters descending on west London and the surrounding Chelsea area spread across social media.

As the designated away team, Fenerbahce were allocated the 'Shed End' of Stamford Bridge, which is said to hold around 6,000 fans, but thousands more were seen across 'home' sections of the stadium.

The tickets went on general sale earlier in November, which allows any member of the "general public" to purchase.

Fenerbahce won the game 1-0 and, speaking after the game, Ismail Yüksek said: “There were nearly 30,000 Fenerbahce fans in the stadium today

"You wouldn’t see anything like this at any other club in the world. They gave us incredible support. They were amazing. They gave us incredible strength. I dedicate this victory to them.

"From the first minute to the last, the team fought just as hard as our fans. We deserved this victory from start to finish."
