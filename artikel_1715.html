Opinion: Liverpool forced into €80m action over Real Madrid's Endrick

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Now's your chance, Liverpool. It's time to act on Real Madrid's Endrick and make another statement signing.

Liverpool tried to sign Endrick from Real Madrid in the summer but it's one that fell apart for various reasons. Eventually, Jose Mourinho decided he didn't want to lose the player, while Endrick himself was never too keen on ending his time with Los Blancos.

But things were relatively close. Liverpool were far from the only club to try and sign him, too, it's expected that Endrick will command a lot of attention when he does decide to leave Real Madrid.

We imagine that will be soon. The Brazilian hasn't been afforded many opportunities at all across the beginning of this season and it certainly appears in reports that Real Madrid are open to letting him leave.

Whether that's permanently or on loan depends entirely on the deal offered. Real Madrid will be reluctant to entirely lose control of Endrick's future but the right price almost certainly tempts them.

Battle for Endrick startsCorriere dello Sport claims that the bidding for Endrick has essentially started. AC Milan are leading it with a massive push to sign the player and they're putting up exactly the kind of deal that Real Madrid would want. €60m up front with a further €10m in add-ons is already good for a player who hasn't made an impact for Los Blancos. But with an €80m buy-back? It's essentially stupid for Real Madrid to reject that. If Endrick works out, they buy him back and paid €10m in development costs. And if he doesn't fulfil his potential? They get €60m for an average player.

Corriere dello Sport claims that the bidding for Endrick has essentially started. AC Milan are leading it with a massive push to sign the player and they're putting up exactly the kind of deal that Real Madrid would want.

€60m up front with a further €10m in add-ons is already good for a player who hasn't made an impact for Los Blancos. But with an €80m buy-back? It's essentially stupid for Real Madrid to reject that.

If Endrick works out, they buy him back and paid €10m in development costs. And if he doesn't fulfil his potential? They get €60m for an average player.

The problem for Liverpool is that they'd never strike a deal like that. They'd never allow another club to control a player's future like that - so Endrick would be signed outright or not at all.

We'll say at this stage that we're enormous fans of Endrick. Our Value Adder model scores him incredibly highly and he's exactly the kind of player Liverpool could use - a left-footed attacker who both scores and creates.

Signing him would mean going above that €60m figure and probably getting close to that €80m range. And is he actually worth that? Potentially, there's no question he is.

So the real question becomes 'can Liverpool guarantee his development at Anfield?'. Now, that we're not totally convinced of, given the number of forwards already at the club. But Milan's bid forces them into action over that exact question.

And if they can guarantee it, then a bid should really follow.
