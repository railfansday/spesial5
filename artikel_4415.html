The CLA350 is Mercedes-Benz’s most potent CLA-class. It’s expensive for an entry-level model, but there’s a lot to like here.

The CLA350 relies on two electric motors, conferring all-wheel drive and a net output of 349 horsepower.

In our testing, the CLA350 needed 4.3 seconds to reach 60 mph, improving on the CLA250+’s 5.8-second sprint.

The CLA relies on Mercedes’s new design language, where everything looks sort of eggish.

That slick shape helps it cut through the air. The CLA350 has an EPA-estimated range of 312 miles.

We have yet to run a CLA350 through our real-world range test, but we did test a CLA250+, which exceeded the EPA’s estimate in our hands.
