The Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel Is Reportedly Still "More Than Two Years Away"

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

After initially announcing The Elder Scrolls 6 all the way back in 2018, Bethesda has now officially confirmed its subtitle: Sentinel. Ooh.

However, if you're under the impression that this means the game is somewhat imminent, a new post from Bloomberg's Jason Schreier suggests that it is still "more than two years away" (thanks, Push Square).

I think a lot of people forget that The Elder Scrolls VI was only announced in 2018 (along with Starfield) to soften the blow of Fallout 76 — "Don't worry, even though we're making a multiplayer game we've got single-player stuff coming too!" Sorry to say this game is still more than 2 years away!

This, of course, would likely mean that its eventual release will land at least a decade following the game's announcement at E3 2018, which is frankly just ridiculous. And we thought the eight-year wait for Metroid Prime 4: Beyond was overkill.

It's just yet another indication that studios need to exercise some restraint when it comes to early announcements. As Schreier stated in his post, Bethesda seemingly wanted to balance out its Fallout 76 announcement with the promise of another single player-focused Elder Scrolls game, but we're not convinced anybody could have predicted what a long wait it would turn out to be.

Nintendo is pretty good at keeping its announcements under lock and key until the right moment these days, and we suspect that Prime 4 proved a big lesson for the company in this regard. Hell, Zelda: Ocarina of Time was only properly announced in June this year, and it's launching tomorrow at the time of writing. Can't argue with that!
