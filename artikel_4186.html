The Legend Of Zelda: Ocarina Of Time Switch 2 Updated (Version 1.1.1), Here Are The Patch Notes

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Update [Wed 4th Nov 2026, 12:45pm]: The official patch notes have now gone live for Version 1.1.1 of Zelda: Ocarina of Time on the Switch 2.

It adds support for the Switch mobile app Zelda Notes and also fixes several issues to improve the overall game experience. Here's the rundown courtesy of Nintendo's Japanese support page:

After updating the software, please follow the instructions displayed at the start of the game to set up "ZELDA NOTES."

As Nintendo has previously explained, Zelda Notes allows you to see a record of your accomplishments, test your knowledge of the game, create and share your own ocarina melodies, and more. You don't need a Switch Online membership to use it, either.

You can find out the file size of this update and other information in the original story below:

Original Story: Some parts of the world are now just hours away from the launch of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake for Switch 2, and ahead of this, Nintendo has rolled out a new game update.

The anticipated release has been bumped from Version 1.0.0 to Version 1.1.1. Next time you check your game, you should be prompted to update. We had a look and can confirm our copy is also running on Version 1.1.1.

Based on the "manage software" section in the Switch 2 option menu, the remake is now 24.8GB in size, and this latest update is around 343MB to install. There are no patch notes just yet, but when we get an update about what's included, we'll let you know.

"Day one" and pre-release updates like this are pretty standard for Nintendo games nowadays, with the company often rolling them out to activate certain online features such as multiplayer. Star Fox also got a day one update when it returned earlier this year. Apart from overhauled graphics and gameplay updates, this latest remake includes support for amiibo and the Switch app Zelda Notes.

Again, if we hear any updates about this latest patch, we'll let you know. Have you been prompted to update your copy of Ocarina of Time yet? Have you got it digitally downloaded and ready to go, or are you picking up a physical copy? Let us know in the comments.
