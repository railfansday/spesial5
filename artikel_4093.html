Sony Has Secured Enough Memory to Meet Console Demand Through March 2028

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Amid ever-increasing hardware prices, driven by an AI-led drain on memory components, Sony has reassured investors that it’s secured enough inventory to meet its hardware projections through March 2028.

In its new financial report, the manufacturer acknowledged the following:

“Regarding memory procurement for next fiscal year, based on discussions held so far with various suppliers, we have aligned on the quantity of memory needed for our projected hardware unit sales.”

Obviously we don’t know what its projections for the next fiscal year are just yet, and whether it’ll need to adjust the price of the PS5 again in the new year. But it will at least have inventory to ship, which is important.

It’s going to be really interesting to see whether its next fiscal year will include the PS6 or not.

Sony says it’s making “solid progress” on the system, but it’s still unclear whether it’ll ship in 2027 or later.

Xbox, for the record, has said it plans to ship a new handheld in 2027 and its next console in 2028. Its price could run as high as $1,800 – although it’s expected the PS6 will be cheaper to manufacture overall. (Although potentially still $1,000+.)

This is a tricky period for manufacturers of all consumer electronics, but PS5’s enormous captive install base has shielded it slightly from the repercussions so far.

In its latest fiscal report, it revealed an 83% increase to its operating income, meaning the profitability of PlayStation is in an extremely steady and healthy position right now.
