Nottingham Forest have 'lingering doubts' about Oliver Glasner as sack decision looms - expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Nottingham Forest have doubts about Oliver Glasner's tactical setup and whether he is getting the best out of his players.

Forest are currently 17th in the Premier League table, only outside of the relegation zone on goal-difference, and have struggled to look convincing so far this season.

A 3-1 win against Man City before the international break looked likely to buy the boss some time, but after a 2-0 defeat to Bournemouth, doubts have grown again.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Glasner will be under pressure at Nottingham Forest until he can put a run of positive results together.

In the meantime, he remains very much at risk, with Evangelos Marinakis believed to have expected more from the Europa League, FA Cup and Conference League-winning manager.

The controversial owner is notoriously trigger-happy, and if Forest get sucked into a relegation battle, Glasner may not be trusted to get them out of it.

Following the defeat against Bournemouth, questions are now being asked about the manager's system, and whether it is negatively impacting his players' performances.

At Crystal Palace, Glasner had plenty of success with his 3-4-3 shape, but that has not been the case at Forest and his reluctance to change could pile pressure on his position.

"We've spoken quite a lot about Oliver Glasner already this season, and that's never good," Brown told Football Insider.

"He's always going to be under pressure at Nottingham Forest when results aren't going to plan, because we know how Evagelos Marinakis likes to opperate.

"That win against Man City will have eased the pressure a bit, but really he needed to build on that win, and instead they went back to their old form against Bournemouth.

"I think one thing that is becoming a problem is that he's not getting the best out of his players with the way he's setting the team up.

"Sometimes as a manager, you have to adapt to suit the players you've got available, whether that's with your formation or with the way you approach games.

"I'm not sure Glasner has been especially willing to do that, and that's led to some lingering doubts, because the way they're playing at the moment isn't getting results.

"If they keep losing games, they're going to be dragged into a relegation battle, and then he could be in trouble."

Nottingham Forest's board are not the only ones who have grown frustrated with recent performances, as large sections of the fanbase are also having doubts.

Glasner's refusal to adapt and inability to get the best out of his players has raised red flags, while their lack of goalscoring threat has also led to concerns at the City Ground.

Brown warned Football Insider last month that losing the support of the Nottingham Forest fans could spell the beginning of the end for Glasner, despite their pre-season ambitions.

The boss was appointed to replace Vitor Pereira in a controversial move, and while it was viewed as a positive change at the time, results this season have not gone to plan.

Glasner, meanwhile, has publicly called out his players and demanded they step up, which could again cause further concerns behind the scenes at Forest.
