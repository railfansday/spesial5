When is the Euro 2028 qualifying draw, who Scotland could face and when does the campaign start

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Scotland will soon discover which opponents they will face as they look to qualify for the third consecutive European Championship under new head coach Sebastien Pocognoli.

Ending a 23-year exile from major tournaments by sealing a spot at Euro 2020 (played in 2021) under former manager Steve Clarke, they repeated the trick three years later, edging out Norway for a spot in the 2024 tournament in Germany.

Now under new boss Pocognoli, a disappointing third-placed finish in the Nations League has made Scotland’s hopes of qualifying for Euro 2028 - a tournament they will co-host - slightly harder after results meant they were placed in Pot 3 of the draw.

Want to know when the draw will take place, how to watch and which teams Scotland could face and which they will avoid? Here is everything you need to know about the Euro 2028 qualifying draw:

The qualifying draw for the European Championships in 2028 will take place in Belfast, Northern Ireland, on Sunday 6 December 2026. The tournament will be co-hosted by the United Kingdom and Republic of Ireland.

If you want to stream the draw via your smart TV, laptop, tablet or mobile phone, the entire ceremony will be streamed for free via UEFA.tv and via the official UEFA YouTube channel.

Scotland’s third-placed finish in Nations League Group B1 means they will be placed into Pot 3 of the draw next month. They will be drawn against once team each from Pots 1, 2 and 4 and potentially pot 5, depending on if they are placed into one of the six five-team groups.

They can’t be drawn against England, Northern Ireland, Republic of Ireland or Wales, with UEFA keeping the tournament co-hosts separated in the qualifying phase of Euro 2028.

Pot 1: Belgium, Croatia, Denmark, France, Germany, Greece, Italy, Netherlands, Norway, Portugal, Spain.

Pot 2: Austria, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Czech Republic, Hungary, Serbia, Slovenia, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkiye.

Pot 3: Albania, Georgia, Iceland, Israel, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Poland, Romania, Scotland, Slovakia, Ukraine.

Pot 4: Armenia, Belarus, Bulgaria, Cyprus, Estonia, Faroe Islands, Finland, Kazakhstan, Latvia, Luxembourg, Moldova, San Marino.

The Euro 2028 qualifiers begin in March 2027, but Scotland’s campaign will depend entirely on the outcome of the qualifying draw in December.

As attested to above, Scotland will be placed into either a four-team or five-team group. If they are drawn in a four team group, fixtures will begin in March 2027. However, should they be drawn in a five team group, their first Euro 2028 qualifying fixture will take place in September 2027.
