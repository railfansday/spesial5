Tanggal: 2026-11-22

He said: “The Sporting Director got in touch with my agent asking if I wanted to come down and train, said train for a couple of days see what I’m about, check if I was fit really, which I pride myself on.

“I do my hard work outside of football. [They] wanted to see me, and I must have done alright in training and [the] contract got offered. So, I’m just happy to be here and happy to be back playing football.”

With over 400 career appearances, the centre-back brings a wealth of experience, and he expressed not only how he feels his experience can benefit the side but also the confidence he has in the team.

He said: “These lads are good players, they are here for a reason, professional footballers and I’ll just help guide.

“When I was younger, I had a more experienced person next to me in multiple clubs so you just feed off their energy and you’ll hear me talking throughout the games. The boys listened really well today and also talked to me as well so it is both ways.”

Will Davies gave the Cod Army a lifeline with ten minutes remaining but Crawley were able to hold out for a 2-1 win over Heneghan’s former side on Saturday.

And the 33-year-old reflected on how his side defended during the game and how much he is looking forward to building partnerships with his fellow defenders.

He said: “I thought we defended quite well if I’m being honest, few little things we can tidy up on. To be honest I’m disappointed with the goal, quite scruffy how it landed to him [Davies].

“The guy headed onto one of their lads and it’s gone behind, but we can iron stuff out and it’s not just them two [Josh Flint and Charlie Barker] and myself. There’s others in the group and whoever has got that starting shirt, I know they are going to give their all.”
