Palmer and Rogers show promise of star double act for Chelsea

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The stirring of what might yet be a great partnership between Cole Palmer and Morgan Rogers, although it will need to be, given some of the chaos that endures at Chelsea.

This was a Premier League game played at a furious pace, and the two former Real Madrid team-mates in charge of the sides will not have seen much of the control all coaches seek. Xabi Alonso’s team eventually won with a goal from each of his attacking triumvirate of Rogers, Palmer and João Pedro – and at times it will have been painful for the new Chelsea manager.

That was chiefly the fault of Robert Sánchez, who was embraced afterwards by Alonso, having demonstrated just how damaging a shaky goalkeeper can be to any serious team. Álvaro Arbeloa’s Fulham had their moments – and the best ones were granted to them by Sánchez. At a club that is such a restless trader of footballers, it feels remarkable that Sánchez has endured for so long.

Alonso played down the significance of Sánchez’s errors, although he cannot seriously be considering sticking to his mantra of “relax and carry on”. That was, he said, his response to the first-half Sánchez mistake that let in Josh King’s shot for Fulham’s first goal. Sánchez could have done a lot better with Gonzalo García’s goal after the break.

Chelsea have a lot of faith that Mike Penders, the 21-year-old Belgian on the bench for this game, will be their first-choice goalkeeper eventually. The situation seems rather more urgent and with Penders likely to feature against Luton Town on Thursday night in the Carabao Cup it would appear a big decision is looming for Alonso.

In terms of the Palmer-Rogers axis, this was very promising, although there was no doubting the senior figure in this. Palmer’s ball set João Pedro free in the first minute, the Brazilian lifting a well-judged nudge over Bernd Leno.

It was Palmer who was crucial to the second for Rogers, made by Maxence Lacroix on his debut. Palmer stroked in the third on 49 minutes with the easy confidence of a man in form.

“I am very happy for Cole,” Alonso said. “I have really enjoyed seeing him play with the dynamism and the freedom and the responsibility... when Cole feels this way we have a better team and a better Cole.”

Palmer makes it look very easy in this form, capable of switching the direction of play a beat before anyone else spots the opportunity. He and his old Manchester City academy friend Rogers came off before the end, Palmer having demonstrated some discomfort chasing a ball. Asked afterwards how ready he felt, Palmer responded in kind. “Did you not watch the game? I’m only joking, I’m ready... it was a difficult season with injuries and whatnot, but through the summer and since I’ve been back I’ve worked extra hard.”

If there was an attacker to compare with him on the other side it was King, who looks even better. The 19-year-old was way ahead of Arbeloa’s two young Real Madrid imports. García toiled away and took his chance, but César Palacios struggled for any impact in a position tucked in on the left. Oscar Bobb and King gave Fulham some edge but the concern, as ever, will be where Fulham get their goals.

Arbeloa pushed his full-backs high and encouraged Sander Berge to drop deep to get on the ball in the first passing phase. The Norwegian delayed too long in challenging João Pedro for Chelsea’s third – backing off and allowing Palmer to overlap to receive the pass.

Sánchez had not been the only concern for Chelsea. Back in the team, Levi Colwill misjudged a challenge on King in the first half and was relieved that the card was only yellow. Roméo Lavia took a wild swing at Calvin Bassey and was also booked. Moisés Caicedo was not fit to play. Enzo Fernández was on the bench and would come on in the second half to the kind of reception from opposition fans to which he will quickly become familiar.

There was a grin from the Argentine at those who berated him from the home stands when he returned at half-time. Fernández worked hard to transmit a general sense that the abuse did not land, although he will be relieved that the Chelsea fans were supportive. His future still feels very much in play.

It was Palmer’s subtle ball through that gave João Pedro the chance with 32 seconds of the game played. The ball came forward immediately from Lacroix’s firm header from defence. The Frenchman delivered a very accomplished assist for Rogers’s first-half goal. Before that King had equalised. There was an exchange with Timothy Castagne, and King looked up just inside the box and decided to have a swing at it. Sánchez was close enough to grasp the ball and somehow failed to do so.

Sánchez would later push a Castagne shot in the direction of García, who stooped and nodded the ball over the goalkeeper after Palmer had scored Chelsea’s third. This second error was unfortunate, but it is the kind of thing that happens to a goalkeeper as erratic as the one for whom Chelsea’s hierarchy seem to have such patience.

“We concede goals in the first minute, the end of the first half and the beginning of the second half and there are not worse moments to concede. But we showed character and personality and I am very proud of how my players responded. We deserved a little bit more but football is not about what you deserve, it is about what you take. If you lose against a team like Chelsea they [should] have to do a little bit more. For me, they are easy goals for them and mistakes we cannot make. We need to improve and be more ready in the first minute, that is for sure.

“We are on our way and we will keep improving. The first thing I want to see is a competitive team. We were tonight, we tried our best and tried to play our football against a team like Chelsea, which is not easy. I believe in them and I believe in young players. I think they have the energy and ambition I want and they fit in the way I play. Josh King is absolutely amazing. I am really happy with him. Gonzalo [Garcia] scored and Cesar [Palacios] did well. I think they need a bit more time but they are doing great and making an impact already.”

Two former Liverpool, Real Madrid and Spain teammates go head-to-head in the dugouts at Craven Cottage as Fulham host Chelsea in a west London derby to round out the opening weekend of the new Premier League season. Alvaro Arbeloa and Xabi Alonso were teammates at Liverpool, Real Madrid and Spain and both had stints as Real Madrid manager last season but have new homes in London. Arbeloa has replaced Marco Silva, who is now at Benfica, whilst Alonso is Liam Rosenior’s permanent replacement. This is the first time a manager’s Premier League debut has come against someone he also played alongside in the competition.

“I know Alonso well because I have watched so many games of his teams,” Arbeloa said. “We have to be ready, and if we want to beat them we will have to play very well. I spoke to Xabi a few days ago. But not about football; both of our families are coming to London, and we are very good friends, until Monday night [tonight]. We will be there on the touchline, so close for 90 minutes, and we will give everything for our clubs. For those 90 minutes, we are not friends.”

Arbeloa has gone back to his former club to add a couple of players in the shape of Gonzalo Garcia and Cesar Palacios. The opening weekend has not served Fulham well of late; they have won just one of their last six Premier League opening weekend fixtures. They concluded their pre-season with a 1-0 win over Stuttgart.

Chelsea have been very active in the transfer market this summer, breaking the British transfer record to sign Morgan Rogers from Aston Villa as well as adding experience through the likes of Jordan Henderson and Danny Welbeck, whilst the likes of Marc Cucurella, Andrey Santos and Trevoh Chalobah have moved on. They finished off their pre-season with a 3-1 victory over Real Sociedad at Stamford Bridge just over a week ago.

“We feel ready,” Alonso said. “We have prepared the whole pre-season with some players from the first day, other ones coming later, but the desire, the ambition, the hunger to start the Premier League is already in the building each training [session] and we are looking forward to it.”

Enzo Fernandez has been strongly linked with a move away this summer, with Manchester City’s interest well-known.

“Enzo is a Chelsea player,” Alonso added. “He is a top, top player. He is training really well since he started 10 days ago with the team, very integrated. We are happy with him and he is getting ready for Monday. He is training really well, good attitude and that is what we want. He is trying his best to prepare for the kick-off of the Premier League.”

These sides finished on the same number of points (52) last season, with Chelsea finishing ahead on goal difference. It was one win apiece for each club in the two Premier League meetings last season. Nearly a year ago, Chelsea won 2-0 at Stamford Bridge whilst Fulham won 2-1 in this fixture at Craven Cottage back in January. Chelsea have won six of the past eight Premier League meetings at Craven Cottage.
