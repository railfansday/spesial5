Leeds United boss Daniel Farke sends James Trafford message after England heroics

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The United stopper earned his fifth international cap for the must-win Nations League game against Spain and repaid Thomas Tuchel's faith with a string of vital saves in the closing stages of the 3-2 victory.

Trafford has received national acclaim for his performance, with some going as far as suggesting he could oust long-time number one Jordan Pickford, but Farke has never had any doubts about the 24-year-old's ability.

"I'm a bit bored because in my last 237 press conferences I always praised him," said Farke. "And right now the whole country is praising him.

"I'm surprised that everyone is so surprised that my goalkeeper is capable of delivering a top performance. This is why he wears some gloves – I expect him to catch some balls and make some saves.

"For that, I can stay a bit away from praising him when the whole country is praising him. I'm not surprised he was there with a good performance.

"Since he came back and everyone is singing his praises, he's got just stick from me. He's a young lad and we need to make sure he stays humble and grounded."

Trafford, who joined from Manchester City for a club-record fee in the summer, has been a standout performer for Leeds at the start of the Premier League season, with only top two Arsenal and Brighton conceding fewer goals than the Whites.

But while Farke believes the move has worked well for both parties, he has been quick to stress that it is still early days.

"You have to have a special mindset if you want to be the number one of Leeds United, a club with such calibre and attention on it," he added.

"There's pressure to deliver. There's pressure and expectation on goalkeepers anyway but it's extra special at Leeds United. He's standing up to this task with top performances.

"There's also space for improvement and it's important to show consistency. After 10 games in the league, it's not the moment to sing high praises.

"There were one or two games where he could have done better in one or two situations but overall he's a fantastic signing and we're all happy to have him. He's a fantastic team-mate and we're fully convinced of his potential."

Trafford returns to domestic duty on Saturday when Leeds travel to old foes Chelsea.

Farke has reported a clean bill of health ahead of the trip to Stamford Bridge following a positive international window at home and further afield.

Anton Stach and Noah Okafor have both benefited from the break, while Joe Rodon and Gabriel Gudmundsson came through unscathed after starting for their countries despite fitness concerns.

"Stach was a full week back in training – today, last week," said Farke. "He's still a bit like part integrated and a bit of individual rehab work.

"Similar to Noah. Both of them had a full training week and it was definitely beneficial.

"Dominic, since he came back from England duties, is also back in training and no problems, so definitely good news.

"As it stands, I think everyone can join us tomorrow in team training. And as it stands, no injuries to report."
