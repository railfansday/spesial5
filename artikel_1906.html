Portsmouth 1 – 0 West Ham United | Player ratings as Parker’s team slump to new low

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Jarrod Bowen marked his 300th appearance for West Ham at Portsmouth tonight, hoping against hope that the anniversary would be marked by a West Ham win. The Hammers captain hit the post in the opening minutes – and West Ham went behind yet again through their own terrible defending.

West Ham sat eleventh at half time, apparently, so I read, their lowest-ever league position in 131 years*. West Ham came close in the second half as their desperation increased: Shots rattled the bar but the ball stayed out.. and Parker lost again.

Herrick: 3 Portsmouth’s first corner did nothing to settle Herrick’s nerves as his dodgy punch was gratefully cleared. Poor defending for Portsmouth’s goal, Herrick saved the first cross but couldn’t save the shot that led to Portsmouth’s goal. Slightly dodgy fluffed volleyed clearance that the youngster somehow got away with on 40 minutes. Shaky tonight.

Veltman: 3 Put through by Bowen, one delightful chip across the face of Portsmouth’s goal, just agonisingly too high for Piroe to reach. Subbed by Soucek 62′.

Mavropanos: 4 Went down in the opposition box like a shot gazelle where it was arguably a penalty, but his theatrical dive probably convinced the referee otherwise. Came close with a shot late on.

Kilman: 3 First contribution was to be caught in possession after 13 minutes in a dreadful piece of dawdling defending. Fortunately, only a throw-in resulted. Poor defending for Portsmouth’s goal, didn’t close down the right winger and would have at ;east attempted a tackle.

Mayers:  3 Superb pass to put Solomon through on 18 minutes. A torrid evening chasing his winger as West Ham’s fluid defensive shape changed when they pushed for a goal.

Álvarez – 4: Went on one kamikaze run across the whole of his own penalty box and lost possession just outside. Fortunately, Portsmouth couldn’t capitalise.  Looked leggy after two games in three days: Subbed for Magassa on 69′

Engels: – 5 Spent most of the first half predominantly operating down the right-hand side with a couple of useful touches. Hit one curling free kick well but straight at the keeper. Went close when he met Austbø’s cross and headed back across the goal for Souček, but agonisingly inches over the midfielder’s head.

Kanté – 5: Tried and failed to walk the ball into the net after the Portsmouth keeper fluffed his own punch from a corner. One lung-busting run early in the second half resulted in a half chance. Subbed for Castellanos 78′.

Now – familiar sinking feeling after conceding in the first half
