Stefan Borson: Nottingham Forest's SCR 'reality' outlined as transfer plan takes shape

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Oliver Glasner will have the ability to add signings to the Nottingham Forest squad during the January transfer window.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that Squad Cost Ratio (SCR) shouldn't be a concern, but Forest will need to be sensible with additions.

It is understood that Glasner is targeting as many as five new additions in the January window, having recently met with Evangelos Marinakis.

The manager travelled to London for a three-hour meeting on the squad's day off on Monday, 23 November, to discuss potential signings.

With the Reds sitting 17th in the Premier League table, it is clear that the boss needs reinforcements for a successful second-half of the campaign.

Most recently, Glasner criticised the players in the squad who are unable to speak English fluently after years in the country, so he won't want more of the same.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson has poured cold water on the idea of five signings, but alleviated any fears of SCR breaches.

"I doubt there's space for five. It's not just transfer fees, it's wages as well. I've not looked at what their SCR position is," he said.

"But it's very hard for any team to fail SCR just because you've got an 85 per cent cap under the Premier League rules, and you can always deal with a breach under the UEFA rules if you are in UEFA competition.

"At the moment, the sad reality for Forest is it doesn't look like they're going to be in UEFA competition next season.

"So I suspect they're pretty close to the 85 per cent, but it's probably not that big a deal. But five players is a lot.

"Maybe there is a plan to make a sale in the summer. So these sorts of things change the equation. But it doesn't look to me as if they'll buy five players.

"The squad's already quite big anyway. The wage bill's quite high relative to revenue. So I doubt there'll be five players coming."

With his outburst at those who are unable to speak fluent English, fans have narrowed it down to Ibrahim Sangare and Nicolas Dominguez.

So both players could be on the chopping block in the next couple of transfer windows.

In January, a new goalkeeper is understood to be at the top of the list, as well as a left-wing-back, central midfielder and an attacker.

The latter would be imperative to helping Morgan Gibbs-White and Liam Delap shine at the City Ground, and that will be important for Forest's success.
