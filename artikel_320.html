Our beloved Shark FlexStyle just hit a new record-low price: Save at least 40% during Sephoras Savings Event

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

SAVE WITH CODE ALLONSALE: During the Sephora Savings Event, the Shark FlexStyle is on sale for only $179.10 for Beauty Insiders. That's 40% off and its lowest price ever. (VIB and Rouge members save even more.)

Mashable's favorite Dyson Airwrap dupe has fallen to a new record-low price during Sephora's fall Savings Event, live through Nov. 9.

Normally $299, the stellar Shark FlexStyle is now on sale for only $179.10 in a special-edition Winter Pearl colorway. To unlock Sephora's deal, you must be a member of its Beauty Insider rewards program and enter the coupon code ALLONSALE at checkout — that will stack an extra 10% discount on top of the FlexStyle's existing $199 sale price there. Beauty Insider is free to join.

If you've been a member of the Beauty Insider program for a while, you might be able to get the FlexStyle for even cheaper. Members with VIB and Rogue status can stack 15% and 20% discounts. (You have to have spent at least $350 or $1,000 at Sephora in the past year, respectively.) Rogue members will save 47% total and pay a mere $159.20.

The Mashable Choice Award-winning Shark FlexStyle is one of our top Christmas gifts of the year. Unlike Dyson's much pricier Airwrap multistyler, its base can twist to function like a regular L-shaped hair dryer, a feature that impressed our reviewer. This base model comes with four attachments: two auto-wrap curling barrels, an oval brush, and a styling concentrator.
