Who is NFL MVP favourite? 'He’s head and shoulders above the rest’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sky Sports NFL presenter Neil Reynolds explores his favourites to win the 2026 MVP award, tipping Cincinnati Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow as his favourite to claim the individual honour; tune into Sky Sports NFL at 5.30pm Sunday for Week 11 of the season on the road to Super Bowl LXI

Who is leading the NFL's MVP race? Sky Sports' Neil Reynolds explores his frontrunners heading into the second half of the 2026 season on the road to Super Bowl LXI...

When I was asked to write a column about the frontrunners for the Most Valuable Player award in the NFL this season, I thought it might be quite fun to compile such a list and look at the leading players in the NFL.

But the reality is this could be a one paragraph feature, because I believe Joe Burrow of the Cincinnati Bengals is head and shoulders above what is also a very good group looking for that prestigious prize.

Burrow is enjoying the form of his life for the 7-2 Cincinnati Bengals, as he demonstrated on Sunday Night Football, delivering a devastating performance to dismantle the Pittsburgh Steelers.

Burrow threw for over 300 yards and three touchdowns in the first half alone before Cincinnati called off the dogs in a 38-18 win. Burrow ended that game with more than 400 passing yards, four touchdowns, no interceptions, and the praise from future Hall of Famer Aaron Rodgers ringing in his ears as he said 'you put on a clinic kid'.

So I think with Burrow at the helm, the bar is as high as you can imagine or dare to dream about for the Cincinnati Bengals.

In the 2021 season when fully healthy, he took Cincinnati to within five minutes of winning the Super Bowl. And a year later, down to the wire at Arrowhead Stadium against the Kansas City Chiefs in the AFC Championship game before coming up just short. So with Burrow leading the way, the Bengals are genuine Super Bowl contenders.

But he does headline a really good group with Brock Purdy also in the mix as the quarterback for the San Francisco 49ers.

Burrow is the highest rated quarterback in the NFL of any passer who's played in at least nine games with a rating of 117.7. Purdy is right behind him with a rating of 115.2.

He processes so well in Kyle Shanahan's attack and is the perfect quarterback for the San Francisco 49ers, who boast the best record in the NFL at 8-1. Purdy's 20 touchdown passes and five interceptions speak to his efficiency and his ability to run that offense.

You've also got to look at Jahmyr Gibbs who leads the NFL with 1,384 scrimmage yards. Gibbs has already put together a complete season rushing for a thousand yards and 11 touchdowns and also contributing through the air, and that's in just nine games. He's explosive, he's powerful, he's the heartbeat of the Detroit attack and I think he remains a key player for them the rest of the way.

Another player who has put in a season's worth of work in 10 games is Jaxon Smith-Njigba, the wide receiver of the Seattle Seahawks. He's caught 79 passes for more than 1,200 yards and 10 touchdowns and he still has seven games left in this season. JSN is not the fastest, he's not the strongest, he's not the most explosive but put it all together and he's pretty much uncoverable.

He will get open on every play, he will run away from defensive backs, he'll run over them if he has to. You're talking a complete football player and he doesn't look anything like him in terms of how he plays but the production reminds me of Jerry Rice.

The greatest wide receiver in NFL history was dubbed too slow coming out of Mississippi State and all he did was run away from defensive backs for his entire career. The stopwatch is not that important in the NFL, it's what you do when you put on the helmet and shoulder pads.

I think any list has to involve Josh Allen of the Buffalo Bills because he is so vital to that team but I think just 13 touchdown passes on the season kind of holds him back even though he added 11 rushing touchdowns. Ironically, and this perhaps plays into the MVP award debate, if you took one player out of every NFL team, the two teams that would arguably suffer the most from losing their quarterback would be the Bengals without Joe Burrow and the Bills without Josh Allen.

So his value to the Bills is extreme but I just think he falls behind some of these other guys around the league as does Jordan Love, who leads the NFL with 2,838 passing yards. He has 20 touchdowns but there have been seven interceptions and a lot of sacks taken for the inconsistent Green Bay Packers, so Love is prolific in terms of yardage but he's not quite there yet in this conversation.

And finally, the last time a quarterback failed to win the MVP ward it went to a running back in Adrian Peterson. Defensive players winning the MVP award are very rare, the last one was Lawrence Taylor for the New York Giants back in 1986.

So this is a long shot but the leading defender for me in the NFL this season is Xavier McKinney, the safety for the Green Bay Packers. It's hard to get interceptions in the NFL and he has six of them leading into the second half of the campaign, making him a danger to every quarterback he faces.

That's an impressive list but again I'll go back to what I said at the start of this column. Joe Burrow is way out in front in my mind in this race to be named the MVP of the 2026 season.
