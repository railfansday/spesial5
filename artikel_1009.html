Hull chases Thitikul in LPGA Tour finale and discusses phone 'detox'

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Back-to-back defending champion Jeeno Thitikul shares the early lead at the CME Group Tour Championship; 2016 winner Charley Hull one back in Florida; watch day two live on Friday from 7pm on Sky Sports Mix and 8pm on Sky Sports Golf

Charley Hull sits within a shot of the lead after a low-scoring opening round at the CME Group Tour Championship, where Lottie Woad and Nelly Korda also impressed.

A decade on from claiming her maiden LPGA Tour title at the season finale, Hull posted a six-under 66 at Tiburon Golf Club to stay in touch with a three-way tie for the lead.

Back-to-back winner Jeeno Thitkul made the ideal start to her bid for a historic three-peat by posting five consecutive birdies in a seven-under 65, lifting her joint-top of the leaderboard alongside Australia's Karis Davidson and Korea's A Lim Kim.

Hull is in a nine-strong contingent on six under that also contains Lauren Coughlin and Auston Kim, both part of Team USA's last Solheim Cup side, with Woad and European team-mate Celine Boutier both two off the early lead.

World No 1 Korda went straight to the range after finishing her opening-round 68 with four birdies in the last six holes, with only four of the 60-player field failing to break par on Thursday.

The season finale features an $11million (£8.4m) purse, with the tournament champion taking home a $4m (£3.04m) winner's cheque. Only the top 60 players in the season-long Race to the CME Globe get to tee it up in Florida.

A busy year for Hull has seen her win the Saudi Ladies International on the Ladies European Tour, finish runner-up to Nelly Korda at the US Women's Open and feature in Team Europe's Solheim Cup success, plus continue to contend on the LPGA Tour.

"I'm so looking forward to some time off," Hull admitted. "I feel like it's been a very long season on and off the golf course. I have a lot of sponsors stuff that I've had to do and outings, then the golf and the Solheim Cup is always a tiring year.

"I'm really looking forward to just going home. After WTGL I'm going to go home, I'm going to buy myself a Nokia phone and for the whole month just change my sim over and just have a Nokia phone and just completely switch off from the world.

"Just an old school phone so I can't look at anything. Don't have to worry about anything. If anyone wants to get hold of me call me on that phone. Like I'm done with texting, I'm done with social media. I just want to have a whole month detox."

On whether she had done similar before, Hull added: "I've done it loads of times. I think I went four months back in 2019 without a phone.

"I done the whole of China and the whole of Asia swing without a mobile phone. I just had an iPad, and the person that I was travelling with just had my flight information and stuff on it.

"Honestly, I wish I was born 30, 40 years earlier - drives me bonkers all the technology and AI these days!"
