Alcaraz defies fitness concerns to beat Medvedev

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Carlos Alcaraz secured his spot in the knockout stages of the ATP Finals with a win over Daniil Medvedev; the Spaniard called for his physio after appearing to pull up with a hamstring problem; watch the ATP Finals live on Sky Sports

Carlos Alcaraz kept up his strong end-of-season form with a 6-2 6-4 victory over Daniil Medvedev at the ATP Finals on Wednesday despite taking a medical timeout and getting his left thigh strapped.

Seven-time Grand Slam champion Alcaraz extended his winning streak to 11 matches as he seeks a third consecutive indoor title after trophies in Vienna and Paris.

The Spaniard also secured a berth in the semi-finals of the eight-player event for back-to-back years after Frances Tiafoe beat home player Flavio Cobolli 7-6(8-6) 6-0 later on Wednesday evening.

Alcaraz's win was his fifth in six matches against top-five players this year. His lone loss came against Jannik Sinner in the Monte Carlo Masters final.

World No 1 Medvedev moves to a record of 1-1 for the tournament after he beat Cobolli on Monday.

After breaking Medvedev twice in the first set, Alcaraz was forced to call for treatment in the second set.

"I was playing great and suddenly I felt a little problem in my hamstring," Alcaraz said.

"But just a little bit. I mean, I could play really good tennis since that moment until the end of the match.

"I was trying to stay relaxed, to move not too aggressively.

"Right now I'm just feeling OK. We will see with my team, with my physio, but I think the rest of the day and tomorrow is really going to be important."

Alcaraz missed this year's French Open and Wimbledon after he was ruled out for four and a half months due to a right wrist injury.

"I don't expect, even at this stage, he [Alcaraz] knows how serious this [injury] is.

"Whether he has been compensating, trying to protect his knee and now it's gone to his hamstring, his quad on the other leg.

"What could have been a distraction, just didn't really materialise, because his tennis is faultless.

"Where do you attack? He's so fast from the back of the court and is so good off both wings. He changes direction. You hit a great shot; he hits a slice back. He can stay in the rally and defend. His serving has been so consistent.

"We've seen him play like this on other surfaces; outdoors hard, on clay, on grass.

"But this level indoors is even new for him."

After suffering a 6-2 6-2 defeat at the hands of Alcaraz in his first match of the tournament, Tiafoe rebounded by fending off two set points in a first-set tiebreak to seal victory over Cobolli.

Having shaken up his diet and training regimen, Tiafoe has had the most consistent year of his career and reached the US Open semi-finals for a third time.

It was Tiafoe's first-ever victory at the ATP Finals, with the American making his debut at the event this year.

He will now go up against Medvedev on Friday for a spot in the semi-finals.

"I'm going to have to take it from him," Tiafoe said.

"I'm going to have to take it from him. He's going to make me earn it. He's going to make me execute it at the highest level.

"If I do that, I like my chances. If I don't, he'll get it done. It's that simple."
