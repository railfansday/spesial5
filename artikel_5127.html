Shiba Inu (SHIB) Dev Reveals Bi-Fold Shibarium Revamp

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Home » Shiba Inu (SHIB) » Shiba Inu (SHIB) Dev Reveals Bi-Fold Shibarium Revamp

Shiba Inu’s (SHIB) developer team has prepared new perks for the vibrant Shiba Inu holder community, otherwise known as SHIB Army. Mazrael said the team had completed the necessary groundwork for the implementation on chain 109.

The upgrade includes Cancun opcodes with BN254 pairing for the Zero-Knowledge (ZK) proofs. Alongside, the developer’s X post offers collaboration on gas, testing & integration so ShibWallet can support it from launch, with a linked GitHub issue detailing the proposal.

The latest move could attract on-chain dwellers concerned about third-party blockchain surveillance. With ZK-proof gaining popularity across the blockchain, the real-time registry is an important tool to keep operations transparent. Recently, Shiba Inu’s Mazrael announced a new portal dedicated to blockchain records, including Free Etherscan-design APIs for builders.

This involves a flurry of new, exciting features, including Shiboshis Of The Day. That’s a special-feature allowing users to get new Shiboshi digital collectibles every-day, buying it directly on the Shiborium Layer-2. There’s also a new dedicated games & dApps page featuring the signature Play With SHIB Games including the heavy-hitters Agent Shiboshi and Shiba Eternity.

Other notable features include real-time status updates and Shibarium activity charts. “Built by the community, powered by ShibWatch, dressed in the Eternity Hub look.”, – proudly mentioned Mazrael, noting that SHIB Army members are now able to search .SHIB names in the queries, adding even more transparency and clarity in the Japanese dog-themed coin’s much-cherished ecosystem.
