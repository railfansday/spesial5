Cardano’s Next Chapter: Euler Named as Major Upgrade After Dijkstra

Cardano is already looking beyond its upcoming Dijkstra hard fork, with Euler emerging as the next major upgrade.

In a recent release, Intersect, a member-based organization in the Cardano ecosystem, said it is planning the next Cardano upgrade, Euler, with the first meeting of the Euler Scoping Working Group scheduled for December 1, 2026, at 5 p.m. UTC. Here, participants will contribute to early discussions on the scope of the next Cardano era.

The development signals Cardano's continued commitment to evolving its blockchain infrastructure through a structured upgrade process. While Dijkstra represents the current focus, discussions on Euler will set the tone for what comes next for the network.

The Dijkstra era delivers Cardano's major protocol upgrade in two phases: Phase 1, Dijkstra hard fork (Protocol Version 12), introduces the Dijkstra ledger era and ships Ouroboros Linear Leios as a complete, activated feature. The second phase activates Ouroboros Peras via an intra-era hard fork. Peras is a settlement-speed upgrade that adds a voting overlay to the existing Ouroboros Praos chain selection rule.

As of the latest update by Intersect, Dijkstra ledger features are currently being tested on DijkstraNet, while Linear Leios consensus and throughput are being tested on MusashiNet. Both are converging toward Node 11.3, which is the target hard fork release candidate.

The Dijkstra upgrade requires both constitutional and technical changes in the era of on-chain governance. Discussions and refinements on constitutional guardrails (Update Constitution) or CAP-12 "Dijkstra era parameters" continue on the Constitutional Amendment Portal ahead of Mainnet submission.

Protocol Version 12 has already gone live on Musashi and DijkstraNet, while a hard fork release candidate (11.3) is required for Preview and Preprod hard forks. In its recent post, Intersect sets the Dijkstra hard fork target timeline as December 2026 for the Preview network, including the Update Constitution and hard fork.

On-chain activation of the Update Constitution action on the mainnet is expected in December 2026, while the Dijkstra hard fork is expected in Q1 2027.
