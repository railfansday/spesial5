A string of outages across US banks, airlines, government networks and tech platforms over the past week has fuelled claims online of a coordinated digital attack, but no company or federal agency has confirmed one. Apple said Tuesday’s disruption to iCloud, the App Store and Apple Pay stemmed from an internal update, while Chase blamed a software bug in on-premise systems for Monday’s banking failure. CNN and Reuters reported no immediate evidence of a cyberattack behind the collapse of the NSA’s classified internal network the same day. Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said CISA had seen no traffic pointing to an attack, though he separately floated superintelligence as a possible cause, telling Fox News, “I believe that it’s the SI.” The FBI issued a FLASH advisory on Tuesday warning that hackers are using an AI-driven tool called ARTEX to automate intrusions, but the bureau has not linked it to any of the outages.
