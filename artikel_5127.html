8 Things That Should Always Be in Your Field Bag

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Game day is only one part of an athlete’s schedule. These versatile picks from Brooks are made for the rest of your routine.

If you’re a regular at your local soccer league, you probably have your game-day uniform down to a science: cleats, socks, shorts, jersey, done. But you also know that training doesn’t end when you leave the field. There are warmups before practice, conditioning sessions, gym workouts, recovery afterwards, and, of course, the inevitable post-game team hangouts while you’re still wearing half your soccer gear. The right off-field pieces can make all of that a little more comfortable.

Brooks has plenty of options that can slot into those in-between moments—from cushioned running shoes for off-field training to lightweight layers for chilly practices to comfortable sweats for the ride home.

Below are eight of our favorite picks for the training, travel, and downtime surrounding the “beautiful game.”

The Glycerin 23 is arguably Brooks’ most versatile off-pitch training shoe. It’s perfect for road-running and training on hard surfaces thanks to plush cushioning and a smooth, stable ride that’s ideal for conditioning runs, or other training away from the field. The breathable upper and roomy fit also make it a comfortable option when you’re on your feet all day or logging miles without a ball at your feet.

For early-morning practices and chilly fall training sessions, lightweight layers that you can easily move in become essential. The Dash 1/2 Zip 3.0 is made with sweat-wicking fabric and has a semi-fitted silhouette that works over a training top without feeling too bulky or overbearing. Thumbholes help keep the sleeves in place, while a watch window lets you check your stats without needing to pull up your sleeve.

After practice, it’s natural to want to keep things comfy. The Reset Hoodie and matching sweatpants make for an easy throw-on-and-go set for cooling down after training, traveling to games, or simply keeping it casual off the field. The sweatpants have an incredibly soft brushed interior and an adjustable waistband, while the hoodie provides an extra layer when temperatures drop. You’ll never want to take this combo off.

If your warmup routine involves more than a few laps around the field, the Movement Tech Jogger is designed to keep up. These mid-rise bottoms have a 28-inch inseam, water-repellent fabric, and zippered pockets for all your essentials. Ankle zippers make it easier to get the pants on and off over shoes, which is particularly handy when you’re heading straight from the sidelines and into the game.

After kicking off your cleats post-game, the Adrenaline GTS 10 is an ideal recovery shoe. It’s a lifestyle take on Brooks’ longtime runner, reimagined from the 2009 model, with an open-air mesh upper and nitrogen-infused DNA TUNED cushioning. It’s an easy option for walking around after a game or practice, whether you’re heading home, meeting teammates for food, or running a few errands.

Sometimes the best post-practice top is simply one you don’t have to think about. This Graphic Short Sleeve has a relaxed fit and a soft cotton-polyester blend, making it a great choice after training or on a rest day. It’s also versatile enough for gym sessions and other low-key activities when you want something more casual and uncomplicated than your usual performance gear.

For outdoor training days when you need more coverage without piling on heavy layers, the Luxe Long Sleeve offers a truly useful middle ground. Its soft, breathable fabric has odor-resistant properties and UPF 50+ sun protection, while thumbholes help keep the sleeves in place. Wear it on its own during a cool-weather workout or underneath a jacket when temperatures really start to dip.

After spending 90 minutes running around in soccer socks, changing into a fresh pair can feel not only like a luxury, but a necessity. The Reset Crew Socks have a soft cotton blend, plush cushioning, a seamless toe, and arch and midfoot compression for a secure feel. An odor-resistant finish is another welcome feature, whether they’re going straight into your gym bag after practice or carrying you into the rest of your day.
