10 Gnocchi Skillet Weeknight Dinners For Any Craving Or Time Of Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

All you need is one pan and less than an hour.

In our eyes, the hallmark of a great weeknight dinner is gnocchi. The comforting, pillowy pasta is the perfect vessel for endless sauces and flavor combinations, making it a star no matter the season or craving. Another bonus? In many of our favorite gnocchi dinners, there’s no need to cook the gnocchi beforehand. That means no waiting for water to boil or dirtying extra dishes. These 10 gnocchi skillet dinners bring all of your favorite flavors together in one pan, and all in under an hour. Simply put, gnocchi is made for weeknights.
