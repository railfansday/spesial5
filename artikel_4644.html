Sending an Australian astronaut to space is expensive. How do we work out if it's worth it?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Australia is currently in talks with the European Space Agency (ESA) over potentially flying Katherine Bennell-Pegg on a future space mission. If accepted, Bennell-Pegg—who is the director of space technology at the Australian Space Agency and completed ESA training in 2024—would become the first officially funded Australian astronaut.
