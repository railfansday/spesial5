To truly build core strength, you need to target the deep stabilizing muscles that sit beneath more superficial layers like your abs. And to do that, you need to overload them using the correct exercises.

Below, I share three exercises you can try for a stronger, more stable core. These moves target muscles like your obliques, which run down either side of your waist and assist with movements like side bends, and the deep layer of muscle known as the transverse abdominis.

These exercises set your core alight, but they also build strength where it matters: the muscles that support balance, stability and posture. Read on to learn how to do them with proper form.
