New Version Of Star Wars: A New Hope Officially Releases In 4 Months

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A huge new version of Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope will officially land in the galaxy in the new year.

The year 2027 marks the original Star Wars movie's 50th anniversary, and Disney and Lucasfilm are celebrating the franchise's massive milestone in style with a variety of new immersive releases, toy collections, and brand partnerships. In addition to bringing a newly restored iteration of the 1977 sci-fi classic to theaters starting in February, Disney is launching an immersive version of George Lucas' first Star Wars movie that will bring audiences to a galaxy far, far away like never before.

In partnership with Disney, Cosm has now announced that it will be premiering the 2019 version of Star Wars: A New Hope in Cosm's Shared Reality format in March 2027. The experience will be played on the center of Cosm's 12k+ LED Dome in the film's original 16:9 format, with the Dome's surrounding projections "dynamically evolving" to further immerse audiences in the classic Star Wars locations and sequences.

The March debut of Star Wars: A New Hope in shared reality marks the beginning of a new multi-film partnership between Cosm and Disney, with the subsequent films being released in this format having yet to be announced. Cosm has also previously partnered with Warner Bros. to launch Shared Reality versions of IT (2017), Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone, The Matrix, and Willy Wonka in the Chocolate Factory, and will begin its multi-year partnership with Universal next month with the premiere of the 2001 animated hit Shrek.
