Country of Fortune is coming soon to gamers worldwide, and less than 24 hours are left for it to go live. Developed by Nepos Games, Country of Fortune takes city-building to a new level. Instead of constructing your city, you’ll be developing an entire nation, and every decision you make will have a key impact on your legacy.

In Country of Fortune, you build an entire medieval country with the use of your settlements. The game uses grids, grows, and knits into a living region filled with people, rivers, and resources.

As a lokator, your first task is to pick a suitable grid to place your first settlement. Then, guide all your villages to help them grow from a hamlet to a bustling city.

Since no two grids are the same, you’ll need to adjust accordingly. There will be plenty of resource deposits to exploit, but you’ll be challenged by the environment and terrain.

With a wide economy, you’ll need to manage plenty of different aspects. Develop settlements and help them specialize in producing goods that require resources from the nearby areas. You can also develop roads and vehicles to set up a booming trade.

With different levels of power and endless micromanagement (if you want), Country of Fortune has plenty of exciting features to offer. If you’re unsure about what to expect, there’s a free demo available on Steam for you to try out ahead of the game’s early access launch.
