WiseTech Global: E2open - US Area Sales Director (CPG) - Illinois

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

WiseTech Global is a leading developer and provider of innovative software solutions for the logistics, global trade and supply chain industries worldwide. We serve more than 22,000 logistics companies across 193 countries, 23 of the 25 largest global freight forwarders worldwide. With the acquisition of e2open, we have expanded our network to encompass over 500,000 connected enterprises across manufacturing, logistics, channels and distribution. With the vision to be the operating system for global trade and logistics, we are creating a multi-sided marketplace connecting carriers, logistics providers, importers, exporters and shippers.

In August 2025 WiseTech Global, developer of the world's leading logistics execution software CargoWise, completed its strategic acquisition of e2open. This acquisition represents one of the most significant strategic moves in global supply chain technology in recent years, and it creates an extraordinary moment to join this business.

WiseTech's CargoWise platform has historically focused on logistics service providers such as freight forwarders, carriers, and customs brokers. The e2open acquisition brings a deep, complementary product suite and customer base on the other side of the supply chain: the buyers, importers, exporters, shippers, manufacturers and brand owners who generate the freight in the first place.

Together, the two businesses create something that has never existed before: a truly end-to-end, multi-sided global trade and logistics marketplace connecting demand, planning, supply, channel, transportation and execution in one ecosystem, all underpinned by AI and data at scale.

We are seeking an experienced and driven Area Sales Director to join our growing US sales organization. Each Area Sales Director owns a portfolio of approximately 20-30 enterprise accounts within our CPG business unit.

This role sits at the intersection of sales and professional services, with our most successful team members acting as strategic consultants and trusted advisors to our clients to help drive wider adoption of our existing product suite. This role covers the full spectrum of client engagement, from identifying opportunities and closing deals to staying involved throughout implementation to ensure successful delivery.

Salary: $100,000-$150,000 Base + Uncapped Commission
