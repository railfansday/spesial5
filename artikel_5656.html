Compass Announces New Policy to ‘Support Cooperation’

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Compass has announced a new Broker-to-Broker Cooperation Policy in order to “support cooperation.”

The new Broker-to-Broker Cooperation Policy requires all agents of the brokerage to cooperate with other brokers and agents—including listings earmarked as Private Exclusives or Coming Soons—unless otherwise instructed by the client in writing, the brokerage stated.

The policy—which is now added to Compass’s manual, the brokerage noted—requires that Compass agents ensure all agents, part of the brokerage or otherwise, can access listing details on request.
