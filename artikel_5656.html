Who Needs Automatic Driving? You Love to Drive! However, for Many Handicapped, Tesla’s Full Self Driving Is the Only Option. It’s Use It, or Don’t Drive!

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Editor’s note: In my opinion, a person should not be using Tesla FSD to drive somewhere if they aren’t capable of driving the trip fully themselves. Fritz makes the argument here that it is easier to supervise FSD and that is why someone with a handicap can use it when they don’t feel like they can drive fully on their own — they just have to pay attention and be ready to intervene if needed. However, my experience and opinion is that it can definitely be harder to intervene when needed than to just drive oneself, so if someone isn’t capable of driving fully themself, they shouldn’t be driving somewhere relying on FSD. As he is showing here, though, there are people — himself included — relying on FSD to drive when they feel they can’t drive a trip fully on their own for various physical reasons. Feel free to chime in below with your opinion on this matter. — Z.S.

Warning! Tesla’s Full Self Driving Supervised is an extremely capable automatic driving system. However, to use it you need to be an unimpaired legal driver who can intervene (Supervise) in a fraction of a second if needed. With that caveat, I will discuss in this article how FSD can take over driving for drivers with various kinds of handicaps.

I love to ride my ebike! I ride 15 to 20 miles every day. A few days ago, just after shoulder replacement, I remembered that Vasa Fitness has stationary bicycles. Just two days after surgery with my right arm in a sling, I knew that driving there would be difficult. I thought about asking my wife to drive me. But I got into my Tesla Model Y with Full Self Driving V 14 and said Hey Grok! Navigate me to Vasa Fitness. My car drove me out of my garage and without any intervention by me, touching the brake pedal, steering wheel or accelerator drove me to Vasa Fitness and parked me in the parking lot as you see in the lead photo.

Numerous situations have come to my attention where Tesla FSD can or could drive you safely, where the only other option would be to not drive.
