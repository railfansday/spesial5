Fallout, Resident Evil, and more are recognized at the Game Awards

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

2026 has been a stellar year for video game adaptations, and the Game Awards is recognizing that fact.

In this year's nominees, the Best Adaptation category is particularly stacked. Up for the award are the Resident Evil movie, Mortal Kombat 2, Fallout season 2, Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2, and The Super Mario Galaxy Movie.

It's anyone's guess who'll win at this point, but it's a particularly solid category this year. The Super Mario Galaxy Movie grossed over a billion dollars, while the Resident Evil movie earned the near perfect Rotten Tomatoes score of 96% (our own Resident Evil movie review awards the film 4 stars, and our verdict reads: "Frightening, funny and exceedingly bloody, Zach Cregger's Resident Evil is a horror-comedy to match the best the genre has to offer, and an easy frontrunner for the best video game movie to date. Cregger's hot streak continues.").

Fallout season 2, meanwhile, became Amazon's second most-watched returning show of all time with 83 million viewers in 13 weeks. Cyberpunk: Edgerunners season 2 and Mortal Kombat 2 also both hold fresh Rotten Tomatoes scores, sitting at 80% and 63%, respectively.

It's more proof that the age of subpar video game adaptations has come to an end, with plenty more exciting upcoming video game adaptations on the horizon, too, including Fallout season 3.

"I feel confident that we've done that in season three, and if we get the opportunity to come back for season four, that would continue to honor how sacred this is to people. It's not something that we take lightly," Ghoul actor Walton Goggins shared recently.

The Game Awards take place on December 10. In the meantime, check out our guide to all the most exciting upcoming movies and upcoming TV shows left of 2026.
