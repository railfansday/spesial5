Anime Adventures has a massive number of units so I’ll sort through the best ones across three tiers and three sub-categories. The goal is to find the most effective DPS and Support characters to deal with the new Soaring units and various game modes. Here’s my Anime Adventures unit tier list.

⇓ Tier III Hybrid/Hill – Decent DPS Replacements & Good Beginner Units for Flying/Soaring ⇓

⇓ Tier III Ground – Decent Ground DPS Replacements & Good Beginner Units ⇓

There are a ton of characters in this game so I’ve highlighted the best units in Anime Adventures by category. I’ve organized the tier list according to three Tiers and subcategories for those tiers which are Hybrid/Hill, Ground and Support. The top tier units are universally good accounting both for DPS and ability to effectively deal with Soarding units. Note that support units in the S-Tier also have great DPS.

Sky God Note: You can get him from any Sky God Portal tier but higher tiers increase the drop chance.

Flying and Soaring Units Update: Any unit can now hit fliers with ground units dealing 50% less damage with no CC effects. Soaring units can only be hit by Hill and Hybrid units placed on Hills. A Hybrid unit cannot hit a Soaring enemy if it’s not placed on a Hill.
