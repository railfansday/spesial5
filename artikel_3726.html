There’s a ton of chatter about fall footwear trends — specifically boots — so here’s what you need to know: Rich moms are totally wearing boots in 2026, but only certain, select styles. They’re ditching clunky lug soles, tacky cowboy shoes and basic mid-calf lengths for sleeker, more polished alternatives, and we found 13 luxe-looking boots that nail the aesthetic. Even though these picks scream ‘expensive,’ they start at only $10!

These trendy fall boots are effortlessly chic, with elements like block heels, buckle details, knee-high designs and soft leather and suede materials. Meghan Markle, Reese Witherspoon and Jessica Alba are just a few A-listers setting the boot trends this season, so don’t wait to snag the rich mom-approved styles you’ll wear from now through April.

1. Our Favorite: It’s hard to believe these tall suede boots are only $20, but we’re taking advantage it! The 3-inch block heel gives you a height and confidence boost, while the faux suede fabric makes you appear expensive.

2. Boutique Find: Nobody channels boutique style quite like Witherspoon, so naturally, her boots are following suit. She wore the classiest chocolate brown booties, and we found a similar pair hiding on Amazon. They should cost triple the price!

3. Royal Stamp: Want to dress like a wealthy royal? Then you have to copy Markle, who donned tall leather riding boots. Her pair had quilted details, shiny buckles and a knee-high silhouette — and this affordable alternative features all of the above.

4. Classic Chelsea: Speaking of royalty, Kate Middleton sure loves Chelsea boots. These timeless treasures are the perfect way to nail the look; they have a sleek round toe, stretchy side panels and leather-like material. The memory foam footbed is just a bonus.

5. Two for One: When you can’t decide between loafers and boots, opt for these sophisticated hybrid shoes. The horsebit metal is incredibly luxe, as are the low profile, penny strap and intricate stitching.

6. Sassy Stunner: Ever since Middleton wore pointed-toe boots, we’ve been eyeing this sassy twin. Like per pricey pair (which quickly sold out), these shoes feature a block heel, an ankle height and this elegant black color.

7. Puddle-Ready: Whether you get rain or snow, these mini Chelsea boots have you covered. The waterproof material keeps your toes dry, while the leopard print elevates your outfit.

8. Cute Croc: In case you missed it, croc-embossed leather is totally ‘in.’ These stylish short boots are a rich mom staple, featuring croc-embossed detailing, a 2-inch block heel and a shiny gold buckle. Shoppers say they’re comfortable for walking and versatile enough to wear with everything!

9. Pretty Platform: Platform Ugg booties are back again, and if you want the same look for $23, these fuzzy Amazon boots deliver. They’re arguably even more chic than the over-$150 version, thanks to the shearling fuzz around the ankle.

10. Slouchy Aesthetic: Unlock your inner California cool girl with these slouchy tall boots. They pair especially well with tailored jeans, trousers and leggings, making any casual getup appear instantly expensive.

11. Jessica Alba: We can’t say ‘California cool girl’ without mentioning Alba, who rocked knee-high boots that channel preppy style and laid-back vibes at the same time. This lookalike option has an identical effect.

12. Burgundy Babe: Peruse the Upper East Side and you’ll see burgundy boots everywhere. People will think you dropped hundreds on these tall, New York City-coded shoes, especially with the artsy block heel.

13. Like Socks: Knit boots are the wardrobe staple you didn’t know you needed. This pair molds to your foot, stays put all day and looks like a million bucks, so you might ditch your other go-to shoes for good.
