ApeChain migrates to Chainlink CCIP for APE and USDC transfers – Details

ApeChain is replacing LayerZero with Chainlink CCIP. This move comes as the layer 3 blockchain aims to strengthen its infrastructure and improve connectivity across the Yuga Labs ecosystem.

The migration makes CCIP the official cross-chain standard for ApeChain, Bored Ape Yacht Club applications, and APE.

Additionally, it will be migrating the ChainLink Cross-Chain Token (CCT) that is used by the CCT implementation of APE and USD Coin [USDC], among others. This is to enable programmable transfers from these tokens without having to use LayerZero’s former OFT.

Therefore, this may provide easier access to the decentralized application and community space across the Ethereum [ETH], Arbitrum, Base, and Polygon chains. As well, Chainlink has described CCIP as a single secure cross-chain standard.

The expanded reach of Chainlink CCIP provides a larger scale significance for the ApeChain integration. Chainlink CCIP processes over $25 billion in cumulative transfer volume.

It also currently supports 60 to 77 different networks and has sent over 1.63 million messages. Previously, it was integrated with BitGo’s WBTC, Wyoming’s FRNT, and other assets from Kelp and Mantle.

These previous integrations have moved over $15 billion worth of token value via CCIP in 2026. In addition to providing additional support to this expanding network of users, Yuga Labs will also bring a large, consumer-focused, and NFT-centered ecosystem into play.

Ultimately, Rising transfers, repeat activity, deeper liquidity, and competitive fees will show whether adoption delivers lasting growth.

While CCIP is continuing to expand its reach, it needs to continue translating this reach into sustainable revenue. More importantly, CCIP recently underwent its first upgrade (CCIP 2.0) since September 2026.

Additionally, integrations with Aave [AAVE], Lombard, and Venus demonstrate CCIP’s continued involvement in the development of DeFi. While CCIP continues to add to its involvement in DeFi infrastructure, commercial returns for the project remain limited.

This gap represents the difficulty in transitioning from simply being involved in infrastructure adoption into generating consistent economic returns.

ApeChain has adopted Chainlink CCIP as its cross-chain standard for APE and other ecosystem assets.

Chainlink’s CCIP adoption is growing, but low fee revenue remains a challenge.
