Aerodrome Finance (AERO) is the premier decentralised exchange (DEX) and automated market maker (AMM) built natively on Base, Coinbase’s Ethereum Layer-2 scaling network.

Launched in August 2023, Aerodrome was engineered to serve as the central liquidity hub for the entire Base ecosystem.

It utilizes a highly specialized tokenomics model known as ve(3,3), originally pioneered by Solidly, which aligns incentives between token traders, liquidity providers, and governance voters.
