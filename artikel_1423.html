Roma eyeing Sporting Lisbon forward Fotis Ioannidis

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Roma continues to monitor the attacking market ahead of the winter transfer window, evaluating various opportunities to strengthen Gian Piero Gasperini’s attack.

Following rumors of interest in Endrick, another name has emerged that could fuel competition with AC Milan: Fotis Ioannidis, a 2000-born Greek striker currently playing for Sporting CP.

AC Milan is closely monitoring Ioannidis in their search for a centre-forward to replace Camarda, including the Greek among the options being evaluated along with Endrick and Tresoldi.

One potential player who could influence AC Milan’s interest is Ruben Amorim, who has previously worked with the striker at Sporting CP and knows his characteristics.

According to Gianluca Di Marzio, Roma considers the player an interesting prospect, although they haven’t identified him as a top priority.

The European calendar will soon offer the Giallorossi the opportunity to observe the striker firsthand. On December 8th, Ioannidis will face Roma in the Champions League for Sporting CP, a match that could be an important opportunity to assess his qualities.

So far, the Greek has made 15 appearances this season, scoring two goals and providing two assists.

Gasperini, however, is primarily looking for a winger with acceleration, the ability to beat his man, and the ability to attack space—characteristics different from those of a central striker like the Greek. Therefore, any further exploration of Ioannidis will also depend on the opportunities that arise during the January transfer window.
