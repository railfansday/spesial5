Branthwaite’s Everton Squad Claim & ‘Holistic’ Fitness Approach

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Q: Jarrad, it’s still early in the season, but from your perspective, how is it going?

It’s going well. As a team, we’re in a positive place, especially after the recent results. It’s put us in a good position in the Cup and the Premier League.

Q: With how well it’s going in the league and the Carabao Cup, does it feel like there’s a lot of quality in this Everton squad?

I think the players we’ve got and the quality in the team is probably one of the best we’ve had over the past few seasons.

The squad isn’t as big as other squads, but the quality we have is definitely up there.

I think we’ve just got to stick together and be resilient.

Q: And after recovering from a hamstring injury last season, how are you feeling?

Good. I’m in a good position. I think the summer and pre-season helped to get me where I am now.

I was managed by staff, managed my load through pre-season, managed my training load.

So, hopefully, it’s helping. I'm feeling good.

Q: Just how frustrating was it last season?

Last season was tough. It was the first time I’d had a big injury which kept setting me back. When you can’t find a solution to something, it’s frustrating.

You do everything you can and then it sets you back again.

Q: I remember you breaking down against Liverpool and you looked broken. That must’ve been horrible to deal with?

Yeah, I think it was the fourth time it had happened in the same place.

When you’ve tried different things and nothing’s happening, and then for [the injury] to happen again, it was tough.

It’s probably set me back a bit, so I’m trying to make up for the time I’ve lost. I think the start of the season has definitely helped with that

Q: What sort of approach did you take in your recovery? I imagine you tried several options.

I spoke to a few players who’ve had similar issues and reached out to someone in Germany – a specialist there – for a different approach to what I’d been doing, and the Club were on board with it.

I was there for four weeks, and they took me through their procedure, which looked – not just at the hamstring – but how the whole body moves, your brain and how everything moves.

It was a holistic approach and that definitely put me in a better position to come back here, and then with the Club helping me with my load, that put me in a better position.

Q: Are you able to share which other players you spoke to?

Mainly Dominic Calvert-Lewin. When he was here, he was struggling with similar issues, so I reached out to him.

Tom Davies, as well. I reached out to him. They spoke really highly of [the specialist].

I spoke to Merlin (Röhl), and he knew a few people who had been there, so I think it was the right time to get different advice and a different opinion.

Q: But now, you’re feeling in a much better place?

Yeah, I feel in a better position now with the way I’ve been managed, and I feel confident in myself.

I think I feel more confident now coming back and I want to prove to everyone how good I am.
