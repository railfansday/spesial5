Bayern: Michael Olise’s surprising like against his management

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

While the future of Michael Olise continues to be talked about, in Germany as in Spain where Real Madrid covets him, the French international has just liked a publication… criticizing its leaders. This is indeed a post by journalist Fabrizio Romano, which takes up the strong criticism of the FIFPRO union towards the leaders of Bayern in the Jamal Musiala file.

Indeed, Karl-Heinz Rummenigge, manager of the Bavarian club, had made quite strong comments suggesting that Bayern could decide to terminate the contract of Jamal Musiala, who is facing health problems. “Bayern’s honorary president, general manager and former general manager have all, in quick succession, publicly questioned Musiala’s contract. According to FIFPRO, this is a concerted strategy aimed at putting pressure on Musiala and his representatives before any negotiations. A club of Bayern’s caliber should show more discernment. Bayern has a duty of care towards Musiala”then published FIFPRO. Words with which Olise rather agrees.
