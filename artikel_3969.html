GTA 6's PS5 Physical Edition Revealed in First Photo

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Images of GTA 6’s physical edition have emerged on Reddit, ahead of its release later in the month.

The anticipated open world game – which won’t include a disc – looks practically identical to every other PS5 release, in a sealed blue plastic case with the usual logos and layout.

While this is a German copy, there’s a disclaimer on the front which mentions no disc is included, and only a download code is packaged inside.

The photos, first shared on Reddit but since deleted, have been reposted below:

Fans will be able to purchase boxed versions of the Rockstar release from retailers starting 12th November, allowing a week for them to pre-load the 212GB title ahead of launch.

This is the first major title to ship in a “code-in-box” format, although it will become a lot more common in 2028, when Sony’s signalled its intent to cease manufacturing Blu-ray discs.

Personally, I’m concerned about the waste of plastic here. I understand some people may still want a case to display with their other games, but if we’re getting rid of discs, I’d like Sony to implement some sort of recyclable format moving forward.

Remember, if you want a map of Leonidas to go with your physical copy of GTA 6, you’ll need to purchase that separately for $0.99 from the Rockstar Store.

@arto @WhoderMan Several studies have been done about this environmental impact in the past and comparing physical to digital to streaming.

Before I go any further it’s important to point out that all the studies are flawed, they all had to make a lot of assumptions and simplifications - and they each made this clear in their methodology. None were conclusive so I wouldn’t take ChatGPT’s conclusions as Gospel as it will be based on flawed data.

Still it is interesting isn’t it? And with that massive caveat in place the studies suggested the environmental impact largely depends on some key factors like the size of the game, and how long you play, especially for streaming.

At extreme ends of the spectrum game streaming could be the best for the environment if you were just trialling a massive game file for a short amount of time - it has its place - but became significantly worse the longer you played. (Good for AAA demos) Streaming was also the worst for the environment in cases where you were streaming tiny games for a longer time. It’s why I refuse to use Antstream Arcade which is streaming retro game files that are often measured in kilobytes, or perhaps megabytes and using hundreds or thousands of times that data throughput every minute.

For physical and digital media you are right the game size affects how effecient this is, and there is a tipping point somewhere in the middle, however studies varied wildly about where this was and with so many assumptions and simplifications taken it’s hard to say with any certainty what is right.

If you want to read more Eurogamer did a massive deep dive into these studies a few years ago as part of Earth week. You can find them on google. Just take all the results with a massive pinch of salt. The only conclusion I got was that there was no final conclusion.
