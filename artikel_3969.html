Play The Blood of Dawnwalker Again in New Game+, Part of PS5 Patch 1.2

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Extra songs, enemy balancing, and terrain tweaks too

Rebel Wolves has teased what to expect from PS5 update 1.2 for The Blood of Dawnwalker, which arrives next week.

While the first major patch promised over 1,000 improvements, this second one doesn't, but the highlight of it is a major one: new game+.

Having completed the campaign's epilogue, you shall have the option to play it all over again with your level, skills, and inventory all intact. The level cap in new game+ is raised to 100, enemies will be balanced and scale across all areas of the map, and items will have new game+ versions with better stats.

The other highlight Rebel Wolves focuses on is an extended soundtrack, having installed patch 1.2. The soundtrack is being extended with additional songs, and it'll be available on Spotify and other streaming platforms in the future.
