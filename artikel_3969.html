PSA: Xbox And GTA 6 Have Both Pushed Out Small New Updates

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

One's a console update, the other potentially a "day one" patch

As we get ready to wrap up the working week here at Pure Xbox, we wanted to alert you to a couple of new updates that have been pushed out in recent days - one for Xbox consoles in general, and another specifically for GTA 6.

The Xbox console update was actually released on Wednesday, but nobody made a song and dance about it whatsoever, while the patch notes only referenced "general stability and performance improvements".

However, we now know that the update was related to the addition of achievements in four original Xbox games, and unless you download the update, you won't be able to see those achievements right now.

There's a chance your console may have downloaded this automatically but to check, go to "Settings", "System", "Updates" and then see if there's an update waiting to be downloaded or not.

As for GTA 6, in addition to the 200GB+ preload that went live a few days ago, there's also now a new update as of a few hours ago that sits at around 2.2GB. You'll need to download this in order to start playing on November 19th.

Some people are speculating that this might be the so-called "day one patch", but who knows whether we might get another one between now and next Thursday. In any case, you'll want to get this queued up as soon as possible (unless you haven't installed the game yet, in which case it'll be included in your initial download).

There's a chance you may see a different size, but it's somewhere around that 2.2GB mark. We're also spotting that some people haven't got it yet for whatever reason, but it should roll out to all Xbox users in the very near future.

Don't forget that Alpha Skip-Ahead Xbox Insiders have also received a special new update this week that adds three new features for testing purposes, so if you're part of the Insider programme, keep an eye out for that.

We'll be back next week with loads more coverage on the GTA 6 launch, and here's hoping there are no more downloads to get us ready for November 19th. Just a few more sleeps until the big day!
