Lindsay Clancy Will Be Retried in Murder Case, New Trial Date Scheduled

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Lindsay Clancy will be retried in her murder case, with Judge William Sullivan declaring that her new trial will begin on March 29, 2027.

Clancy, 36, appeared in Plymouth Superior Court on Monday, November 16, to discuss the future of her case after her mistrial in September. During the latest hearing, prosecutors shared their plans to retry Clancy, according to CBS News.

After the commonwealth argued that the retrial should begin in May, Sullivan, 69, pushed for a March date. (Sullivan previously announced his plans to retire in 2027.)

Sullivan has made several rulings regarding the case since the last hearing on November 9, in which he denied Clancy’s motion to dismiss the murder charges.

Plymouth County District Attorney Tim Cruz has the final say in retrying Clancy on first-degree murder charges and has the power to try Clancy on a lesser charge, such as second-degree murder or manslaughter.

Clancy was previously charged with murder in connection to the deaths of her kids, Cora, 5, Dawson, 3, and Callan, 8 months, in January 2023.

She never denied killing her kids, but her defense argued that she should not be held criminally responsible because she was suffering from postpartum psychosis at the time of the deaths. Meanwhile, the prosecution argued that she was aware of what she was doing and that her suicide attempt after the murders indicated the killings were intentional.

The jury deliberated for seven days until Sullivan declared a mistrial on September 4 when the jury said they could not come to a unanimous verdict. Eleven of the jurors voted to find Clancy not guilty by reason of insanity, while holdout juror Michael P. Desronvil stood firm that she was guilty.

Desronvil has maintained his beliefs that Clancy was guilty, previously breaking his silence while speaking to Fox News on September 28.

“I know the whole focus has been on me, but we want to reel the whole thing back and put the focus on the three angels [Clancy’s three children],” he said at the time. “[They] are the ones who can’t speak for themselves. The ones that can’t defend themselves. The ones that can’t fight their own battles.”

Desronvil continued, “I want to thank God for my Catholic faith. I want to thank God for giving me the ability to love my daughter. Foremost, I would like to thank everyone — every individual — that has supported me throughout this difficult situation. Your prayers, your emotional support, your psychological support is tremendous [for] my heart.”
