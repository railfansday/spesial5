Norwich City team news and predicted XI to face Cardiff City as key man Kenny McLean returns to full fitness

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Norwich City face off against Cardiff City off the back of a fantastic recent run of results, having won their last four games, scoring 15 goals in the process.

A revitalised NCFC will be looking to continue their march up the Championship table towards the play-off positions. The Canaries now sit 12th in the table with 21 points from 15 matches, three points off the top eight.

The visit of an inconsistent Cardiff City side presents a good opportunity to pick up three more points.

Brian Barry-Murphy‘s side head eastwards after two extraordinary home matches: a 3-3 draw against West Ham, where the Welsh outfit let slip a three-goal lead, before coming back from three goals down themselves to beat Burnley 5-4 last time out.

Norwich City have a number of longer term absentees.

Ali Ahmed and Mohamed Toure remain injured. Having ruptured his ACL in pre-season, Ahmed will remain unavailable for the majority of the campaign and possibly longer, while Toure will return sooner and could be involved by the start of the New Year after his shoulder injury.

Having been out for about a month, Papa Amadou Diallo’s muscle injury will continue to keep him out of contention.

In a big boost though, key midfielder Kenny McLean has used the international window to regain fitness and will now be at Clement’s disposal against Cardiff City.

Grimshaw; Stacey, Darling, McConville, Chrisene; Field, Wright; Brooks, Maghoma, Musaba; Kvistgaarden

With no new injury concerns facing Clement’s side, the Norwich City team will likely remain unchanged after thrashing Bristol City in their last outing.

Dan Grimshaw keeps his place in goal with Vladan Kovacevic on the bench. The defence has been leaky at times this campaign, but after two clean sheets on the bounce, the NCFC boss will feel confident in maintaining the current backline.

The central midfield two of Jacob Wright and Sam Field have started the last three games together, and the standard of their performances means they are set to get the nod ahead of Pelle Mattsson and returning captain McLean. Paris Maghoma will keep his spot in the advanced midfield position, having netted four goals in as many games.

On the wings, Andre Brooks will play on the right having started every league game thus far. Anthony Musaba is set to hold down his place on the left after three consecutive starts, yielding two goals and an assist.

Mathias Kvistgaarden is the preferred starter over Makama, so expect the Dane to lead the line. Makama has two goals off the bench in his last two games, as well as earning his first England U21 cap during the international window, so he will be pushing for that starting place.

Norwich City vs Cardiff City kicks off at 12:30 on Saturday and will be broadcast live on Sky Sports+.
