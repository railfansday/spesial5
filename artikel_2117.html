Mark Robins can land Stoke City £200m+ jackpot - Coventry City fans will like it

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

In recent times, there haven't exactly been many reasons to be cheerful for those of a Stoke City persuasion.

The Potters' recent stagnation in the Championship across a near decade's worth of action has been well-documented, with it coming so soon after a strong period in which the club was established in the Premier League.

The Bet365 Stadium had, rather notoriously, become one of the hardest places in the top-flight for some of English football's elite to take three points from, especially due to the North Staffordshire climate and hostilities.

Those times could certainly be returning in the near future, with Mark Robins getting plenty of locals excited about the remainder of the Championship season - a feat which Coventry City supporters know all too well.

Stoke, despite their reputation in the Premier League's modern era, haven't come close to ending their exile from the top-flight since being relegated eight years ago.

The Reds have gone through seven managers during that timeframe, with the first of those - Gary Rowett - particularly unable to deal with the pressure of the club being pre-season promotion favourites at the first time of asking,

As well as retaining many of the squad which had been relegated, Stoke invested over £50m in new players in 2018, much of which was wasted given the lack of impact made by the likes of Tom Ince and Benik Afobe.

Ryan Woods' move to the Potteries was the start of an unfortunate decline for the midfielder

There have since been further false dawns under Michael O'Neill, the club's longest-serving manager in said timeframe, whilst Alex Neil, Steven Schumacher and Narcis Pelach also couldn't reverse City's fortunes prior to Robins' arrival last January, two months after he was somewhat controversially dismissed by Coventry.

Robins is now in the thick of a seventh managerial spell, but his second stint with the Sky Blues between March 2017 and November 2024 is, unarguably, what makes his body of work so impressive.

Coventry's recently chequered past saw the club groundshare on two occasions with Northampton Town and Birmingham City, whilst the club were relegated to League Two shortly after his reappointment despite winning the EFL Trophy in April 2017.

Despite boycotts from supporters due to Sisu's day-to-day running of the club, Robins was able to galvanise the majority of the fanbase and his ever-changing squads in CV6 as they enjoyed quite the rise in such a short space of time.

After winning the fourth tier play-offs at the first attempt, City found themselves back in the Championship just two years later despite being exiled in Birmingham.

During this period, Robins unearthed gems such as Viktor Gyökeres and Gustavo Hamer, who both went on to make the club significant cash after the Sky Blues' agonising Championship play-off final penalty shootout loss to Luton Town in May 2023, which was followed by more Wembley heartache as the then-second tier side lost on spot kicks to Manchester United in the FA Cup semi-final.

Ultimately, though, the 56-year-old laid the groundwork for Coventry's eventual Premier League return under Frank Lampard in May, with many of his recruits now adapting to life in the top division.

Whilst the scale of Robins' rebuilding jobs at Coventry and Stoke cannot be fully compared, the experienced head coach has surprised many by the fact only goal difference is denying the Potters from leading the Championship at present.

Regardless of selling former creator-in-chief, Sorba Thomas, to Hull City, the Reds have recruited extremely smartly this season, with the likes of Luke Graham, Djibril Soumare, Ethan Galbraith, Justin Devenney and Jack McGlynn all earning rave reviews.

More has been expected from fellow summer recruit, George Hirst, but it is an encouraging start for a club who have finished no higher than 14th since relegation.

However, Stoke fans will also be cautious of the fact that exactly a year ago their side sat third in the Championship on 27 points behind Coventry and Middlesbrough, before a run of just five wins in the second half of the season contributed to a major fall down the standings.
