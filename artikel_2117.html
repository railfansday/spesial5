17 goals in 14 games: Middlesbrough flop and ex-Leicester City man hits stunning form with new club

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Leicester City had Kelechi Iheanacho on their books from 2017 to 2024, bringing him in from Manchester City after his eye-catching break into senior football. The Nigerian striker was a mainstay for the Foxes, playing 232 times for the club across all competitions.

He struggled for consistent form for the most part, but did net 19 goals and provide seven assists in 39 games in a prolific 2020/21 campaign. Iheanacho was unable to rediscover that level, and after his departure from the King Power Stadium, he endured a tough half-season loan at Middlesbrough in 2022. He flopped as he managed one goal in 15 games.

A stint at Celtic in the 2025/26 season followed, before Iheanacho moved to Turkey with second tier side Bursaspor in the summer. And, the former Leicester City and Middlesbrough man has hit stunning form there.

Albeit against a lower level of opposition, Iheanacho is in the best form of his life with Bursaspor. The 30-year-old has scored a remarkable 17 goals in 14 league games this season.

The 59-cap Nigeria international went on a run of scoring in 12 consecutive league games, which included four braces. He fired a blank in a 3-0 win over Bandirma Sport at the end of October but followed that up with a return to scoring ways against Antalyaspor last week.

Iheanacho’s success comes from Bursaspor’s brilliant chance creation. He has taken 56 shots in total, equating to an average of four a game (FotMob). As such, with his finishing and experience, it is little wonder that the striker is enjoying such a goalscoring resurgence after his spells at Leicester City and Middlesbrough.

Bursaspor’s goal is to return to the Turkish top flight for the first time since 2019. They have rebuilt over the last two years after dropping all the way to the third tier and having a player of Iheanacho’s pedigree on a three-year deal displays their ambition.

As Iheanacho rediscovers his scoring touch at Bursaspor, Middlesbrough and Leicester City have found new star strikers of their own this season.

At Boro, Will Lankshear has been one of the Championship’s signings of the season. He has 15 goals in 16 games across all competitions, drawing interest from Newcastle United and catching the eye of England manager Thomas Tuchel.

Meanwhile at Leicester City, Emil Riis has hit form after joining for around £2m in the summer. The Foxes’ form has been inconsistent, but the Danish striker has eight goals in 11 games for the club.
