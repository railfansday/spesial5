Pochettino begins Man Utd reign ahead of Liverpool showdown

Mauricio Pochettino officially begins work as United head coach on Wednesday, just four days before his first match against Liverpool at Anfield.

The 54-year-old replaces Michael Carrick, who was dismissed last week after United won just one of their opening 10 Premier League matches, leaving them 18th in the table.

Pochettino arrives after completing his final assignment with the United States, who beat Haiti 2-0 on Tuesday to secure an 8-0 aggregate victory in their CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final.

The Argentine has signed a contract until the summer of 2029 and is expected to meet his new players and oversee his first training session on Thursday.

Speaking after his final match with the United States, Pochettino acknowledged the challenge awaiting him in Manchester.

“The new challenge is a big challenge, but at the same time it’s adrenaline you want to feel,” he said.

Several members of his coaching staff, including long-time assistant Jesus Perez, travelled to Manchester earlier this week to begin preparations. Pochettino will have limited time to work with his squad before Sunday’s Premier League meeting with Liverpool, which kicks off at 4.30pm GMT.

The former Tottenham, Chelsea and Paris Saint-Germain manager inherits a United side struggling for results after a difficult opening to the campaign. He previously guided Tottenham to the 2019 Champions League final and won the French league title during his spell with Paris Saint-Germain.

United’s next fixtures also include a Champions League trip to Sporting CP and a home Premier League match against Brentford. Pochettino’s immediate task is to improve United’s league position, beginning with a demanding first match at Anfield.
