Miley Cyrus Marries Maxx Morando in Tearful Palm Springs Ceremony—Inside Their Malibu Marital Mansion

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando exchanged vows in an intimate Palm Springs ceremony surrounded by their closest family and friends.

Cyrus, 33, wore a long-sleeved silk Saint Laurent gown with matching pumps. Morando, 28, wore a tuxedo jacket and trousers from the French fashion house.

A source said Cyrus was excited and a little nervous before the ceremony, and smiled through tears as she exchanged vows.

“It was such a beautiful ceremony. Miley was excited and a little nervous beforehand. During the vows, she kept smiling through her tears. She was trying to hold it together, but she was just so happy to be marrying Maxx," the insider told People.

The bride’s mother, Tish Cyrus, father, Billy Ray Cyrus, and sister, Noah Cyrus, were among the guests. Billy Ray’s girlfriend, Elizabeth Hurley, and her son, Damian Hurley, also attended.

The couple wed poolside beneath an arch decorated with white flowers. The day before, they celebrated with loved ones at a rehearsal dinner, where the menu featured “M+M” at the top—a nod to their initials.

The news of their engagement was first confirmed in September 2025, after Cyrus was seen wearing a sparkling engagement ring as they posed together on the red carpet at the premiere of “Avatar: Fire and Ash.”

Cyrus and Morando have been linked since the summer of 2021. In a British Vogue cover interview, the “Flowers” singer recalled their introduction, explaining, “We got put on a blind date.”

She then provided a key clarifying detail, noting, “Well, it was blind for me and not really for him. I thought, ‘The worst that can happen is I leave.’”

It’s safe to say the date was a success, with their love story blossoming over the last four years.

Almost immediately, they began collaborating together professionally, with Cyrus telling Vogue Morando was one of her “favorite emerging artists,” having designed sustainable looks for her to wear during her various appearances in music festivals in 2021.

Later that year, Morando’s band, Liily, performed as part of “Miley’s New Year’s Eve Party,” where the “Hannah Montana” star rang in the New Year with an NBC special.

He’s even collaborated on some of Cyrus’s most recent music, and in a Harper’s Bazaar interview, she explained the significance of bringing a romantic partner into her professional sphere.

“I worked with my dad forever. That’s how me and my ex-husband met each other,” she said. “I’ve always worked with the people that I love. And Maxx just inspires me so much.”

While Morando appears to be quite private in regards to his personal life, he has publicly supported his soon-to-be wife at a handful of public events, including movie premieres and award shows.

A few years into their relationship, the nearlyweds took a major leap. A People magazine source confirmed they had moved in together in February 2024.

“She is very happy with him,” the source revealed. “Miley has changed a lot in the past couple of years. She seems more calm and harmonious. Everyone loves Maxx. He’s a great guy. She is very happy with him. He’s private and doesn’t like being in the press. It’s been good for her.”

Cyrus shared what it’s like to live with her fiance in a September 2026 chat on the Zane Lowe Show, where she gushed that even life’s most mundane tasks are more fun with Morando around.

"I think that’s the thing that maybe generations prior didn’t teach us, that marriage doesn’t need to be a prison. It can actually be a playground,” she said. “It’s just living with a best friend. And that’s what it is.”

In 2024, an Us magazine source confirmed that although “Miley owns a number of properties,” the lovebirds are “staying at her home in Malibu,” which was described as a “huge mansion overlooking the water in a gated community.”

Although Cyrus also has homes in Studio City, Hidden Hills, and her home state of Tennessee, the couple reportedly adores the Malibu home’s proximity to the sea. Apparently, they “spend a lot of time on the private beach together.”

Cyrus has a long history of living in Malibu—and even has a song named after the coastal locale.

Back in 2016, she paid $2.5 million for a gorgeous midcentury modern home in the area. At the time, she shared the 1,385-square-foot dwelling with her now-ex-husband, Liam Hemsworth. The former couple used it as a creative studio and guest house.

The funky home was perched on a two-acre property with lots of lush greenery, which could be peacefully enjoyed from the terrace of the primary bedroom, according to Architectural Digest.

The singer and “The Hunger Games” star would call another Malibu mansion their home, however—a $6.8 million property Hemsworth had purchased in 2014, which was tragically destroyed by the 2018 Wolsey Fire just weeks before their wedding.

The sprawling property was more than just a home. It also housed Rainbowland, her personal recording studio.

Miley reflected on the tragedy in 2025, telling People, “When my house burned down, that was the biggest blessing I’ve ever had in my life, actually.”

She explained that after “losing everything,” having the opportunity “to rebuild” inspired her to be more “purposeful” when carefully selecting new belongings.

Cyrus and Hemsworth separated in August 2019, and their divorce was finalized in January 2020.

In the months following the split, Cyrus offloaded the guesthouse at a loss in an off-market transaction for $1.7 million, and Hemsworth sold the wildfire-damaged property for $3.6 million.

It wouldn’t be too long before the “Black Mirror” actress would find herself back in Malibu. She snapped up a $7.9 million Mediterranean mansion in the city in 2022, which is reportedly where she and Morando live now.

It’s truly a resort-like retreat, with a pool and spa in the backyard surrounded by soaring palm trees.

Indoors, an open-plan layout boasts tall ceilings and glass sliding doors that connect the serene space to the equally-tranquil landscape.

The massive primary suite provides striking panoramic views of the beach, and has a lounge space, a fireplace, and a balcony.
