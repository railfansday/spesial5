Serie A Power Rankings: Inter drop positions once again; AC Milan and AS Roma at the top

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Serie A is back after the last international break of the year with Inter, the 2025-2026 Scudetto winners, looking to defend the title in the new season. AC Milan, their cross-city rivals, made some key signings in the summer after replacing the coach and the whole club hierarchy following the disappointing end to the last campaign, and they appointed former Manchester United coach Ruben Amorim to lead the new cycle.

We've already had several sackings as Fiorentina replaced Fabio Grosso, who was hired in the summer, after three games in charge and called back Paolo Vanoli to lead the team; and Bologna fired Domenico Tedesco after four matches and replaced him with Raffaele Palladino. Over the international break, Parma decided to part ways with manager Carlos Cuesta and appointed Alberto Gilardino, while Venezia sacked Giovanni Stroppa after six defeats in a row and appointed Davide Nicola. The last coach to be sacked was Udinese's Kosta Runjaic, who was replaced by Paolo Zanetti. The last one is about Sassuolo, as the club decided to sack Alberto Aquilani following the 3-0 defeat against AS Roma and was replaced by Paolo Zanetti after the break.
