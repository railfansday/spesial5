Dream Driving Experiences Make the Perfect Gift for Auto Enthusiasts

Give them something they'll never forget. From budget experiences to luxury excursions, there's a dream driving experience for every driver no matter the occasion.

This article was updated in November 2026.

Auto enthusiasts are notoriously difficult to shop for. Most already have their own tools, collectibles, garage décor, and driving gadgets, and they’re very particular about all of it. They've probably spent hours researching exactly which version they want. Good luck to anyone trying to buy them something last-minute.

It stands to reason, then, that the best gift for a car lover might be an opportunity to actually get behind the wheel.

Whether it's thrashing an exotic supercar around a famous track, honing their skills through professional instruction, tackling rugged terrain in an off-roader, chasing the Northern Lights by snowmobile, wafting over Europe's greatest roads in a luxurious grand tourer, or strapping into an actual race car for a full-speed ride-along, these experiences offer something every car enthusiast longs for—more time in the seat. The vehicles may differ, but the result is the same—a memory they'll be cherishing long after the holidays have passed.

Best of all, many of these driving experiences are offered as gift certificates or gift cards, which makes them excellent for last-minute gifting. E-vouchers from gifting sites are more versatile and far more personalized—and usually way more affordable—than pricier branded experiences from manufacturers. Your favorite driver can select their own driving vacation on their own schedule at locations around the world.

Got a car lover that's hard to buy for? Here's what we recommend, any time of year.

Virgin Gifts is another gifting site that sells e-vouchers for experiences, so your favorite driver can reserve their ultimate driving experience at their convenience. It's a great last-minute gift idea.

Our pick? BMW Performance Driving Experiences at official BMW proving grounds in either Greer, South Carolina, or Thermal, California (near Palm Springs). At the M Driving School, your favorite Bimmer lover will learn how to drive the legendary M line from professional drivers. They'll slip into the driver's seat of multiple M cars and conquer low- and high-speed driving exercises such as a slalom course and autocross, as well as a wet skidpad for drifting. Once they've mastered each course, they'll compete in timed runs against other drivers. The High-Performance Driver School is the same but with a wider variety of cars and experiences. The Teen Driver School is a fantastic option for young Bimmer fans to learn the basics.

Drive with us! Road & Track Experiences offers several opportunities each year to tour and explore a variety of locales around the U.S. You and a guest will join R&T editors for luxurious and leisurely rallies. Show off your most prized vehicles to like-minded enthusiasts, all while driving legendary roads in beautiful areas, staying in top-notch hotels, and eating at the finest restaurants.

In 2027, the Palm Canyon Run will explore the California Desert in late February, from Palm Springs and Joshua Tree to track time at Chuckwalla, as desert flowers bloom and temperatures are moderate and comfortable. In May, join us for a one-of-a-kind trip through New England, including the famous Kancamagus!

Got a trip to Las Vegas planned? Get out of the casino and onto the racetrack! Give your favorite car lover the chance to drive the world’s largest and fastest selection of Lamborghinis, Ferraris, Porsches, and more at Las Vegas Motor Speedway.

Tinggly is an aggregate gifting site that offers dozens of driving experiences from reputable services all over the world. Most Tinggly gifts are offered as gift e-cards; that means your favorite car lover can choose any experience they desire anywhere they want and on their own schedule. Even if driving exotic cars on a racetrack isn't their cup of tea, there's something at Tinggly that's guaranteed to get their motor running.

If your favorite auto enthusiast has impeccable taste for the finest, Huntermoss is the ultimate gift. Huntermoss (formerly Ultimate Driving Tours) curates luxurious tours and driving experiences in exotic supercars that celebrate passion for motoring and an appreciation for the finer things in life. With experiences in Europe, North America, and Australia, it's a gift and a memory they'll always treasure.

Huntermoss offers guided and self-guided tours and weekend getaways, so there's an experience for everyone who has the means. They'll get to choose from driving a selection of supercars on the world's best roads, enjoying VIP hospitality at F1 races, or strapping into a race car to conquer iconic circuits like Nürburgring and Spa. Huntermoss also offers a bespoke travel-management service, so let your imagination run wild in creating your dream driving holiday. Lavish accommodations, gourmet dining, and handpicked lifestyle experiences complete this bucket-list journey.

If your favorite auto lover is a racing fan, VRBO offers dozens of trackside and track-adjacent rental properties around the world, including this incredible first-turn luxury condo at Charlotte Motor Speedway.

First, buy tickets to any race at StubHub for the best deals, then check out the local listings near the track at VRBO (do this well in advance to get a great deal) and book a vacation property close to or on the track.

Whether you're looking into NASCAR, MotoGP, Formula 1, or even IndyCar and IMSA races, simply book a home in Daytona, Laguna Seca, Bristol, COTA, or anywhere else through VRBO and avoid the hassle of the hotel hustle. It's way more comfortable and lets you and your friends make the most of your visit.

Giftory is another "experience gifting" site that lets you buy gift certificates for all kinds of driving experiences in hundreds of locations worldwide. The NASCAR Ride-Along Experience is available at more than 10 NASCAR racetracks in the U.S., so your favorite driver can choose a track close to home or at a legendary course such as Daytona.

Like Tinggly, Giftory offers hundreds of experiences all around the world, including dozens of driving experiences such as UTV adventures in Moab and snowmobiling excursions to see the Northern Lights.

Porsche enthusiasts will feel the thrill behind the wheel of classic and legendary Porsches at Porsche Driving Experience locations all over the U.S. Whether it's a Driver Development Course in Atlanta or Los Angeles or a Track Experience at Laguna Seca or Birmingham, there's something for every Porsche lover. There are even Travel Experiences that let your favorite Porsche driver tour with like-minded Porsche fans.

For the Porsche enthusiast who demands the best, opt for the Porsche Track Experience in Europe. Once-in-a-lifetime opportunities include introductory courses to GT track days to the top-of-the-line Rennsport Academy.

One of the most renowned driving and racing experiences in the world, Skip Barber offers one- and three-day Racing Schools behind the wheel of either a modified GT car or an FIA homologated monocoque paired with a 160-hp turbocharged four-cylinder engine at a variety of tracks all over the U.S. For experienced drivers, there are also two-day Advanced schools in both cars. (We reviewed the Skip Barber experience back in 2018, shortly after the company changed hands; you can read about that here.)

Skip Barber's renowned Driving Academy makes a great gift for drivers of all skill levels, particularly young or new drivers who need to hone their skills and tactics behind the wheel.

Just outside Phoenix, Arizona, lies one of the nation's top driving and racing schools. Radford is renowned for its top-flight educational programs, helping drivers reach their maximum potential behind the wheel.

Sponsored by Dodge, Radford offers multiple programs and experiences for drivers of all skill levels in Challengers, Hellcats, SRTs, and more, including driving and racing schools at multiple skill levels and at numerous price points—even drag racing and private instruction.

Racing's number-one oil and fluids brand also runs a marvelous racing school at locations all over the country. If your favorite auto enthusiast has racing aspirations, then this is the ultimate gift idea.

Lucas Oil School of Racing isn't intended to be a casual vacation experience or even necessarily a "fun" activity—although it certainly is that! LOSR is a serious racing school designed to develop aspiring race-car drivers, and its top graduates often move on to professional racing ladders such as IndyCar and IMSA.

With Basic, Basic+, Advanced, and Championship levels, students compete on a level field with other talented up-and-coming drivers in Ray Formula GR11 race cars. These championship-winning cars are adapted to the rigors of a racing school and arrive-and-drive race series, with an extra-large cockpit, adjustable pedal box, and adjustable seat. Courses take place year-round at tracks such as Sebring, Laguna Seca, Mid-Ohio, and VIR. See the complete 2026 calendar here.

About 40 minutes from downtown Seattle, nestled in the foothills of the Cascade Mountains, is the 315-acre DirtFish Rally School. DirtFish offers a wide range of rally driving programs that teach car control, confidence behind the wheel, and recognized rally techniques on multiple surfaces.

Outfitted with a fleet of all-wheel-drive turbocharged Subaru WRX STIs and rear-wheel-drive Subaru BRZs, DirtFish offers the excitement of rally racing to drivers with half-day, full-day, and three-day courses suitable for all drivers. It's an awesome gift idea for auto enthusiasts, and it's perfect for corporate events and groups.
