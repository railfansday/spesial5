Manchester United make training decision as Mauricio Pochettino arrives at Carrington

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Mauricio Pochettino has arrived at Carrington as he begins life as Manchester United manager, and he has ensured that training sessions are already underway.

The Manchester Evening News reports that Pochettino’s trusted lieutenants Jesus Perez, Toni Jimenez, and his son Sebastiano Pochettino carried out training at Carrington on Wednesday morning.

Pochettino had sent the trio ahead to Manchester on Sunday, in order to ensure his tenure got off to the smoothest start possible.

Perez, Jimenez, and Pochettino Jr also oversaw training on Tuesday, with Man Utd players not called up for international duty returning from a four-day break.

During the first half of the international break, Michael Carrick led training - despite Man Utd sounded out a move for the Argentine to replace the former midfielder after a disappointing to start to the season.

Although Pochettino has visited Carrington for the first time in his role as Man Utd boss Wednesday afternoon, he will not lead training until Thursday.

The Argentine head coach signed off his stint as United States Men’s National Team [USMNT] manager with a 2-0 victory over Haiti on Tuesday evening UK time, ensuring the USA advanced to the semi-finals of the CONCACAF Nations League in March 2027.

Strength and conditioning coach Silvia Tuya, analyst Alec Scott, and coach Miguel D’Agostino stayed with Pochettino Stateside for the Haiti game, with all three joining Pochettino’s backroom staff at Old Trafford.

D’Agostino is a former teammate of Pochettino and a long-standing member of the ex-Chelsea head coach’s inner circle, while Tuya and Scott have impressed the Argentine enough since joining his USA set-up to be taken across the pond.

Now, Pochettino’s focus turns to Liverpool - his first game in charge of Man United taking place at Anfield on Sunday afternoon.
