Crawley Town fan column: Reds finally win a home game and they actually looked like they had a plan

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Player to Watch: Pemi Aderoju (Crawley Town)

We controlled the first half and got off to a flyer, when Kai Jennings scored early after Fleetwood failed to deal with Kellan Gordon’s cross. We looked dangerous on the break all afternoon, with Dylan Morgan hitting the post before half-time, while Fleetwood barely created anything.

Right at the start of the second half, Morgan showed brilliant vision to thread a lovely pass through the middle to find Pemi Aderoju, who smashed it home to make it 2-0, with Aderoju looking lively and causing all sorts of problems all game, and in my opinion, it was his best performance I’ve seen from him in a Crawley Town shirt.
