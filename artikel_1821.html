Prediction: Sweden vs Bosnia and Herzegovina

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Verdict: Sweden to win Best odds: 11/20 Bookmaker: 1xBet

UEFA Nations League, League B, Group 4 | Saturday, November 14, 2026 | Strawberry Arena, Solna | 7:45 PM GMT

Sweden can settle the promotion race on Saturday without having to take a result home from Poland three days later. A win in Solna sends Graham Potter’s side up to League A. Standing in its way is the only opponent to take points off Sweden in this campaign. Bosnia and Herzegovina earned a 1-1 draw in Zenica in October and would move a point clear at the top with a win in the return fixture.

Viktor Gyökeres has scored in all four group games, and the last three goals give a fair idea of what Bosnia’s defenders are up against. He volleyed in a cross against Poland, ran clear onto Alexander Bernhardsson’s pass in Zenica and struck after 13 minutes in Bucharest. That last goal was enough for a 1-0 win over Romania.

The best spell Sweden has played at home came just after Poland went into halftime level at 1-1. A quickly taken corner gave Yasin Ayari the chance to curl a shot inside the far post. Six minutes later, Gyökeres made it 3-1. Potter has good reason to trust the players around his center forward if Bosnia crowds him out.

Dejan Kulusevski returns to the squad after a knee injury kept him out for more than 17 months. Alexander Isak is also in the squad, giving Potter the option to pair the two scorers from September’s home win over Romania. Midfielder Besfort Zeneli withdrew through injury.

Sergej Barbarez’s side has made a habit of staying in games long enough for an opportunity to come. Sweden was ten minutes from winning in Zenica when Kerim Alajbegović’s attempted cross from the left sailed into the net. Three days later, Esmir Bajraktarević drew an 84th-minute penalty against Poland, and Ivan Bašić converted it for a 1-0 win.

Bosnia can also carry a threat away from home. Four of its six group goals came in a single evening in Romania, while the other away trip brought a goalless draw in Poland.

Barbarez has a decision to make in central defense. Tarik Muharemović is suspended, and Dennis Hadžikadunić, a candidate to replace him, has a torn calf muscle. Nikola Katić and Sead Kolašinac are among the available options. Further forward, Ermedin Demirović and Haris Tabaković are in contention following Edin Džeko’s international farewell against Sweden.

Bosnia has already shown how awkward it can make the final ten minutes for Sweden. The harder part will be getting there on level terms. Gyökeres has scored against every opponent in the group, and two of Barbarez’s options to mark him are unavailable. Sweden to win a close game, with 2-1 the scoreline I favor.
