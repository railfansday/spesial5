Who will be on Mauricio Pochettino’s backroom team at Manchester United?

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Manchester United are thrashing out the finer details of Mauricio Pochettino’s move to Old Trafford, with the Red Devils having to negotiate the bill to bring four of his trusted lieutenants back to England.

After identifying ex-Tottenham boss Pochettino as the desired replacement for sacked head coach Michael Carrick, Man Utd quickly agreed personal terms - before approaching the USA to extract their target from a four-year contract only signed following the World Cup on home soil in the summer.

It has been reported that the total compensation package United will have to pay the US Soccer Federation could be north of £10million, once fees for Pochettino’s staff have been agreed.

Assistant manager Jesus Perez has worked with Pochettino since his first job in management, employed initially as an analyst at Espanyol in LaLiga.

After building a strong relationship with Pochettino, Spaniard Perez was promoted to No2 when the Argentine was appointed manager of Southampton in 2013, and has been his assistant at every job since.

Perez and Pochettino joked about the United job during the World Cup, with the former apparently very keen on working at Old Trafford.

There are three other members of staff likely to make the move across the Atlantic. Miguel D’Agostino is a former team-mate of Pochettino’s, with the duo having played together at Newell’s Old Boys in their native Argentina.

Initially working as a scout for Pochettino at Espanyol, D’Agostino has also followed his compatriot from club to club since then.

Toni Jimenez is another to have worked with Pochettino at every club he has managed so far, plus the US national team. A goalkeeping coach, Jimenez first linked up with Pochettino in 2011 at Espanyol.

The final member of coaching staff mentioned in reports has not been involved in Pochettino’s setup as long as the others, but has a very close relationship to the former Chelsea manager.
