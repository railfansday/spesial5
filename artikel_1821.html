Macdonald: I was told my job was ‘bomb-proof’ – but eight days later Eastbourne Town sacked me

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Macdonald’s four-and-a-half year run as manager of Town ended after Saturday’s 1-1 draw at home to Whitstable when he was sacked in a move that has angered many of the club’s fans and seen a number of players and support staff leave the club too.

He has spoken to Sussexworld and the Eastbourne Herald about the decision – and outlined some of the difficulties they had faced with injury and unavailability this season in a run of results that ironically had recently looked like it might be coming to an end.

Town confirmed Macdonald’s departure on Saturday in a statement and have since installed club stalwart Dave Winterton as interim manager and asked fans to get behind him.

Macdonald said of his sacking: “It was a surprise to be honest and there was absolutely zero warning, which you often anticipate with this sort of thing.

"I spoke to the chairman eight days earlier (the day before the Sheppey game) and he told me on that call that my role was “bomb-proof” and that I had his backing.

"I had explained to him in the call how hard it had been this season, but we didn’t have a relationship where we talked regularly on the phone so we hadn’t had an opportunity to discuss things previously.

"On Saturday, the chairman told one of my assistants at the final whistle to tell me to come to the office. It was the first league game that the chairman had attended since August so I obviously didn’t think too much of it, but that was when he delivered the news to me around 20 minutes after the game had finished.

"He did it along with another member of staff who I’ve never been introduced to before, but is also apparently a committee member.

"The chairman told me my position was being ended at the club and said that they thought it would be better to announce it as a resignation, which I rejected.

"I wasn’t given a reason, despite asking for one multiple times. The chairman just kept shrugging and it wasn’t his fault it was just what the board wanted. I was told that the board held a vote on the previous Monday (November 16) and the decision was made, which is when they made a call to another manager on speaker to offer the job/sound out his interest. The likely new manager told me this on Saturday evening.

"The club then let me manage the Lewes game on the Tuesday, take training Thursday, sign two players on Friday and manage Saturday’s game without anybody saying anything to me, despite knowing they were going to sack me after the Saturday game."

It is Macdonald and one of his right-hand men, Ben Davis, who have been sacked. Macdonald said: “The club wanted my other two assistants, Matthew Elphick and Adam Davidson, to remain in position, but they have rejected that opportunity.”

Macdonald outlined some of the difficulties Town have faced this season which have left them struggling, having won just two league games to sit second from bottom of the Isthmian SE table after 19 games.

He told us: “It has been no secret that we have been completely decimated by injuries through the season and have only once played the same XI two games running. We had started to get bodies in the door and back on the pitch.

"We’d drawn our last two games and arguably could have won both, so it is frustrating that the club made the call they did when the tide was clearly turning.”

Macdonald outlined the injuries the squad had faced: Oliver Davies - ruptured PCL; Anesu Sismayi - Head injury; Marcin Ruda - fractured foot; JJ Walker - hip flexor & suspension; Leon Greig - knee injury (3 months missed); Luke Emberson - shin splints & groin; Aussie Pugh - ankle & knee; Aaron Capon - hamstring & back; Jack Murphy - heel & suspension; Sonny Walsh - jaw; Eliot Jenks - hamstring; Oli Deda - concussion (missed 3 games); Reuel Powell-Downey - hamstring; Arron Hopkinson - suspension.

He added: “That is a fraction of the list.”

Looking back at his time as Town boss, Macdonald talked about some of those who had helped him and his staff: “Since we’ve been at the club, David Jenkins has been absolutely incredible. The work he does for the club, the selflessness and support he gives it is truly unbelievable. He is what non-league is about. Anything we needed, he always did his best to help us with.

"Ross Perrin this season has also been a huge help to us and supported us throughout, as have Luci Del-Gaudio and Paul Lewis. Josh Claxton and Ed have both been incredible. What Josh has done with the social media and all other online stuff is just amazing and he done so much for the club that never got noticed. Josh has also left the football club.

"Former 23s and 18s managers Shaun Lee, Giuseppe Lombardelli and Ryan Reid were always a huge help and incredibly supportive. There are dozens of others that have backed and supported us throughout our time like Ken Wilkins and Chris Prior. Truly great people.

"A huge thank you to all the players over the years. They have done so much for us and we really appreciate all their efforts.

"The biggest thank you is to our management team and support staff, who have just been incredible throughout our time together at the club. Ben Davis, Matt Elphick, Adam Davidson, David Winterton, Roman Glazier and Dom Crowhurst have all made my Saturdays so enjoyable. They’ve all worked so hard for the players and deserve huge credit.”

Macdonald is in no hurry to find a new non-league job: “I would expect to relax and have a break from it all to be honest. I will try to take in other games and training sessions with other managers wherever I can. I’m trying not to think too much about what comes next because you can’t predict anything in non-league.”

He added: “You don’t often get the chance to manage the club from the town you grew up in and I’ve just committed over 10% of my life to it, so the next few days and weeks will just be reflecting on that and appreciating the opportunity that the club gave me four-and-a-half years ago, and the continued and consistent success we have given them in return.”

We asked Eastbourne Town if anyone wished to respond to any of Macdonald’s comments and a statement from the committee said: “The club will make further statements in due course, no further comments at this time.”
