Learning AI should feel like building, not just watching

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

I’m building Lumio AI, a beginner-to-AI-engineering learning platform.

When I started learning programming, I found that moving from tutorials to actually building things can feel like a big jump. I want Lumio to make that path more approachable, with beginner-friendly lessons and hands-on practice.

I’m still developing the project and would love to hear from learners and builders: what helped you move from learning the basics to building your first useful project?

If you’re interested in following along, the waitlist is at https://lumioai.dev.
