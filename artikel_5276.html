Microsoft’s global outage has entered its second day, disrupting email services, workplace tools and AI platforms connected to Microsoft 365. Nearly 130,000 Outlook outage reports were recorded in the US, while more than 38,000 users reported Microsoft 365 issues. Disruptions have also been reported across 74 countries. The outage is affecting users trying to access emails, join work meetings and use AI tools. Claude and ChatGPT’s Microsoft 365 integrations are also experiencing problems due to disruptions in Microsoft’s services. Microsoft 365 serves more than 430 million users worldwide, making the outage a major disruption for businesses and individuals. Microsoft says recovery efforts are underway, but its systems have yet to fully stabilize.
