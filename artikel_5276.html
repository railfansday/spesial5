Presales are an important part of the crypto space, and for any investors or users looking to become a part of the ecosystem beyond trading, presales provide an opportunity to access emerging projects before exchange listings.

Here is a list of the top 5 crypto presales for the month of November 2026:

Bitcoin Hyper intends to expand Bitcoin’s capabilities through a Layer 2 network by incorporating the Solana Virtual Machine technology. This helps to execute faster transactions, lower fees, and provide support for smart contracts.

It also uses a canonical bridge and ZK proofs/rollups to let users move BTC into wrapped equivalents on the Layer-2 network and anchor transactions back to Bitcoin for settlement. The presale for HYPER is currently ongoing and is said to be extended until Q2 2027.

A Layer 3 blockchain that is the first-ever unified execution layer linking the world’s largest blockchains, LiquidChain combines Bitcoin’s capital with Solana’s speed and Ethereum’s DeFi depth. It gives its users access to combined liquidity pools across all major blockchains to provide better prices, deeper pools, and faster execution.

$LIQUID token is the native token of the ecosystem and can be useful for governance, staking, network participation, and accessing exclusive features within the platform. Its presale is currently ongoing and its interoperability gives an important solution to the DeFi infrastructure problem.

BMIC is another interesting project in the crypto space that is related to quantum computing and the future security of digital assets. It intends to build cryptocurrency wallets and other financial infrastructure with the help of post-quantum cryptography.

BMC aims to work towards the idea that, by developing a wallet infrastructure that uses newer cryptographic standards, it can create a quantum-resistant digital asset ecosystem. BMIC’s presale is currently live.

Maxi Doge is a meme-inspired cryptocurrency project that has gained a lot of attention due to its branding, social media momentum, and community engagement. It combines the familiar Doge-inspired branding with bodybuilding culture and speculative trading memes.

It also represents high-risk humor trading and community-driven competitions with the $MAXI token being at the center of this ecosystem. It is a community-driven project that does not really have any conventional revenue-generating business model.

Divine Ray connects blockchain technology, social media, and the creator economy into one ecosystem. $DRC is creating a social platform where creators, educators, wellness communities, and individuals can interact, monetize content, and take part in a blockchain-powered digital economy.

It also operates a Cosmos SDK blockchain with IBC interoperability. The $DRC token is useful for staking, governance, creator rewards, in-app advertising, NFT mining, premium memberships, and event listings. The presale is currently live, and traders can invest in the token.

Presales are a great way for traders to diversify their investments and become a part of the cryptosphere. They bridge the gap between the company and its customers by helping the company understand the customer’s requirements and present the right use cases. It is important for users to do their own research before investing.
