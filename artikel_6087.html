Visit Oslo has released a sequel to its viral 2024 "Is It Even a City" anti-ad, this time set in winter and styled as a horror musical, with the goal of filling hotel beds during the city's low season. The original video, built around deadpan protagonist Halfdan telling viewers "I wouldn't come here," drew more than 25 million organic views across platforms and won a Silver Lion at Cannes Lions, according to Visit Oslo. ForwardKeys reported a 17% bump in flight searches to Oslo in the five weeks after that launch, and Visit Oslo project manager Tord Baklund said Norwegian reported a 14% increase in bookings. The new "It's Totally Even a City" video has reached nearly 1 million YouTube views a month after its October launch, approaching the original's 1.9 million. Baklund said October through March is a higher-priority season for the destination marketing organization, and that while Oslo does not have an overtourism problem, the city center is packed in midsummer. Paid ads have run in the UK and Germany, markets with direct flights, but Baklund said most views have been organic, with the U.S., Sweden, and Denmark also among the top viewing markets.
