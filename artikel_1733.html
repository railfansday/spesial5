Joao Pedro makes surprise appearance as Chelsea injury return nears

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Joao Pedro has visited his former club Watford as he nurses a thigh injury that is keeping him out of the Chelsea team.

The Brazilian was Chelsea’s most important player last season and began this season in fine form, scoring five times and assisting a further three goals in the Blues’ opening six games of the campaign.

But the 25-year-old has been out since sustaining a thigh injury on the eve of last month’s 2-0 home defeat to Tottenham.

On Wednesday, during his recovery period, Joao Pedro visited the club where it all began for him in English football as he returned to Vicarage Road.

He and his girlfriend Khadije were special guests as Watford hosted Blackburn in the Championship.

On their social media channels, Watford welcomed the striker back to his former home, writing: “The boy from Brazil is back in town. Great to see you, Joao.”

The result did not go the way the Chelsea striker would have wanted, as the Hornets were beaten 1-0 with their incoming manager Edward Iordanescu watching on from the stands.

Joao Pedro made 109 appearances for Watford between 2020 and 2023 before signing for Brighton.

After spending just two seasons on the south coast with the Seagulls, he joined Chelsea in July 2025 for a fee of £55million rising to £60m inclusive of add-ons.
