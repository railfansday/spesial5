Ismaila Sarr breaks silence on failed Liverpool transfer as contract talks confirmed

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Ismaila Sarr is ready for a “fresh start” under new Crystal Palace boss Thomas Frank and says he has moved on from his failed summer transfer to Liverpool.

The Reds earmarked the Senegal international as a potential replacement for Mohamed Salah this summer. Crystal Palace, though, refused to entertain the prospect of selling a forward who netted nine league goals the previous season, despite the player being keen on the move.

He was unable to feature in the early days of Pierre Sage’s brief managerial reign due to a groin injury, which continues to niggle him, though he has now managed five appearances this season.

He has now broken his silence on the summer transfer saga, though, throwing his support behind new head coach Frank as he confirmed he is fully committed to helping lift Palace off the floor of the Premier League.

"Liverpool is one of the biggest clubs in world football and, when a club like that shows an interest in you, of course it's something you take seriously,” Sarr told BBC Sport.

"I don't think any player would be indifferent to that kind of opportunity. It was an interesting possibility, and naturally, I considered what it could mean for my career.

"Today, that chapter is behind me. I've always believed that you can't move forward if you're constantly looking in the rear-view mirror.

"I'm a Crystal Palace player, I'm proud of what we've achieved together, and my focus is on getting back on the pitch and helping the team."

Sarr continued, confirming he is in talks over an improved Palace contract: “There have been discussions with the club. For now, I prefer to let those conversations happen privately and concentrate on football."

It is hoped Sarr will soon be able to return to training, with Frank saying on Thursday that the club is being conservative with the forward’s recovery.
