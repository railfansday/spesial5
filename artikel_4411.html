See the interior of the 2027 Audi SQ7 from every angle.

For 2027, Audi has luxed-up the SQ7’s cabin and added a lot of screens.

No fewer than three displays are integrated into the SQ7’s dashboard.

The driver gets a digital gauge cluster as well as a big touchscreen infotainment screen.

The front passenger isn’t left out, as there’s a separate screen for them to watch movies or play music.

Audi has installed some particularly comfortable and supportive sport seats in the front row.

A wide center console features carbon-fiber-esque trim and a pair of phone-charging pads.

The second row can be had with a set of captain’s chairs or a three-across bench seat.

A third row is standard, but passenger space is limited here.

Also standard is a 24-speaker Bang & Olufsen stereo.

Cargo space with the third row in use is limited, but fold those seats down, and you’ll find plenty of room for storage.

Overall, the new SQ7’s cabin is richly appointed, comfortable, and swimming in tech.
