Will Miranda Lambert Break the Producer of the Year Glass Ceiling?

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Grammys’ Producer of the Year category isn’t typically one that commands the same red-carpet dazzle as the artist races, but that will change this year with the nomination of Miranda Lambert. The rare female contender in a field that has only been won by men since its introduction in 1975, Lambert is up against Tony Berg (Sombr, Phoebe Bridgers), Noah Goldstein (Rosalia), Mike Sabath (Raye), and D’Mile (Bruno Mars, Victoria Monet).

Whoever takes home the trophy, it will be a first win for the nominee, though this marks D’Mile’s fourth time at bat. But a victory for Lambert, who co-produced the biggest song of 2026, Ella Langley’s “Choosin’ Texas,” and its accompanying album, Dandelion, would mean recognition for the country genre, something this category hasn’t seen since T Bone Burnett‘s 2002 win on the heels of O Brother, Where Art Thou?

Even rarer: a nominee who’s a woman. Previous female candidates for this award include Linda Perry in 2019 (she tried again this year for her work with Dolly Parton and Paris Jackson), and Janet Jackson in 1990, alongside Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis. What gives Lambert the edge over her fellow female production predecessors is the fact that she’s had her own career as a successful music artist for two decades, giving her a kind of credibility that the other contenders can’t claim. Lambert has the industry’s respect, and that counts for something when it comes to Grammy voting.

Breaking this glass ceiling would also be a huge win for those who have been advocating for women who work behind the scenes. The nonprofit We Are Moving the Needle (full disclosure: I serve on the soundboard) notes in its 2023 “Fix the Mix” report that only 3.2 percent of key technical credits in the Grammys production field categories were for women and non-binary people. Women have fared better in classical music, but with numbers that hardly call for applause.

It’s also worth noting that several nominated female artists, among them Rosalía and Ariana Grande, are also known to produce their own work. Now back to the red carpet. Not to slag on the personal styles of Berg, Goldstein, et al., but Lambert will bring some much-needed shine to the role of producer. Expect rhinestones, glitter, and ass-kicking boots to go along with her Grammy noms showing, which includes a Best Country Duo/Group performance nod for Lambert’s feature on Kacey Musgraves’ “Horses and Divorces.” Fellas, take note.
