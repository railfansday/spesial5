5 Massive Anime Just Left Crunchyroll, Here's Where To Stream Them Instead

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Vanessa Piña has been writing as a journalist for over two years. As a senior writer, she focuses on timely coverage of the latest anime releases and popular comic franchises.

Changes to the libraries of streaming platforms are not unusual, but Crunchyroll has faced significant backlash for failing to notify users which anime series are about to be removed, leaving fans with no opportunity to watch them before they disappear for good. Over the last few months, more anime series have left Crunchyroll, and while some can still be watched on other streaming services, others have vanished from the streaming landscape entirely, becoming accessible only in physical format.

It's not uncommon for anime series to leave Crunchyroll for other streaming platforms, whether due to expiring licensing agreements, high renewal costs, or another platform paying for exclusivity that leads to the series being pulled out. As @MangaAlerts shared on X in July 2026, among the recent waves of anime removals from Crunchyroll, the most notable was the departure of five major series: Blood Blockade Battlefront, Barakamon, Orange, Grimgar: Ashes and Illusions, and Rokka: Brave of the Six Flowers.

This selection of anime series was highly acclaimed for its incredible animation and profound narrative, a prime example being Grimgar: Ashes and Illusions, regarded as one of the best modern isekai series for its gritty, brutal take on the genre, avoiding the trope of overpowered heroes. Another removed title was the live-action adaptation of As the Gods Will, a work by the creator of Blue Lock that's widely said to have served as an inspiration for Squid Game.

Occasionally, there's a scenario where they might return to Crunchyroll later. However, it's worth noting that several of these titles were part of Funimation prior to its acquisition by Crunchyroll, adding to the list of series inherited from that catalog that the platform continues to remove over time.

While these anime series have sparked debate among fans due to their large followings, other titles have flown somewhat more under the radar. In addition to Black Lagoon, and as part of its recent content shake-up, Endride, Bem, and Sasami: Magical Girls Club were also removed from Crunchyroll in July 2026.

Crunchyroll has been hit with controversy after its store updated to remove a massive amount of anime and manga titles, leaving fans uncertain.

This trend has continued beyond the summer. In September, Crunchyroll also removed several anime series such as Kanojo ga Flag wo Oraretara (If Her Flag Breaks), Nagi-Asu: A Lull in the Sea, Pandora Hearts, and Kimi ni Todoke - From Me to You. Perhaps the most well-known series that the platform recently lost, however, is Akame ga Kill!, produced by the studio White Fox, a title that left a significant mark on fans by subverting expectations within the shonen genre with its tragedy and brutality.
