WhatsApp Business API: A Complete Developer Guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Posted on Nov 5 • Originally published at notifysphere.net • AI-assisted

The WhatsApp Business API lets your backend send and receive WhatsApp messages programmatically — order updates, OTPs, reminders, and support replies — instead of typing them in the WhatsApp Business app. This guide covers how it works end to end, so you can design your integration before writing code.

WhatsApp offers two business products. The WhatsApp Business app is a phone app for small businesses that reply by hand. The WhatsApp Business Platform — usually called the WhatsApp Business API — is for software: your systems send messages over HTTPS, and WhatsApp calls your server back with replies and delivery updates.

Meta hosts the API (the Cloud API), so there is no server for you to run. You can call it directly, or go through a provider (a WhatsApp BSP) that adds a dashboard, template management, analytics, and integrations on top.

You can't start a conversation with free-form text. Any message your business sends first must use a template that Meta has reviewed and approved. Templates have placeholders, such as {{1}} for an order number, that you fill in at send time.

Every template belongs to a category, and the category decides what it's for and what it costs:

Pick the category honestly. Meta can re-categorize a template that doesn't match its content — a promotional message submitted as utility, for example.

When a customer messages you, a 24-hour customer service window opens. Inside that window you can reply with free-form messages — text, images, documents, buttons — without a template. Once it closes, you need an approved template again to reach that customer.

Store the message ID from the response. Every delivery update for this message refers back to it.

WhatsApp sends two kinds of events to your webhook. Payloads with a messages array are incoming messages from customers. Payloads with a statuses array are updates on messages you sent — sent, delivered, read, or failed, with error details when sending fails.

Acknowledge with a 200 quickly and do heavy work in a queue. Slow or failing endpoints get retried, which can produce duplicate events, so make your handler idempotent on the message ID.

You must have a customer's permission before messaging them on WhatsApp, and the opt-in should make clear they'll receive messages from your business on WhatsApp. Keep a record of when and how each customer opted in, and honor opt-out requests promptly. Messaging people who didn't ask for it leads to blocks and reports, which lower your quality rating and can limit how many people you can message.

Meta charges per delivered template message. The rate depends on the template category and the recipient's country, and Meta publishes rate cards that it updates from time to time. Marketing templates cost the most; utility and authentication templates cost less. Since October 1, 2026, replies inside the 24-hour window are charged too, after 1,000 free service messages per phone number each month.
