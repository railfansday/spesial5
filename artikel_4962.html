Global Debt Will Make Bitcoin the Best Performing Asset in the World

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

CJ Konstantinos — Founder & CEO, Peoples Reserve, makes the case that America's debt problem is Bitcoin's biggest tailwind.

CJ Konstantinos — Founder & CEO, Peoples Reserve, makes the case that America’s debt problem is Bitcoin’s biggest tailwind. With $40 trillion in debt and a trillion-dollar deficit every year, CJ argues “the deficit is not going away,” and that as currency units are diluted, Bitcoin “will outperform every other asset simply because it is a finite asset.” He explains why raising rates is only raising the deficit and accelerating inflation, and why that pressure keeps building. CJ then makes his boldest call: that within the next five to 10 years, one Bitcoin “will become worth more than a single family home,” a moment he says will be “a really big wake up call for most Americans.”
