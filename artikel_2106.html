New signing prioritised: West Ham’s number one January target – Report

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

It seems there’s been some behind-the-scenes frank talking already, in the few days that Scott Parker has had his feet under the desk in the manager’s office at Rush Green. Blackburn Rovers away tomorrow caps off a dizzying week for the new West Ham head coach who interrupted his holiday to fly back and sign a contract, do his media stint and head off to Cardiff – all on day one.

There’s been some pretty intensive assessment already carried out regarding the shape of the squad come January, if reports quoting ‘sources’ behind the scenes are true.

Footballfancast, claim an insider source’s info regarding the priority for Nils Koppen in the January window:

“West Ham are prioritising the signing of a new centre-forward for Parker in January, with the manager and board in agreement on their main area for reinforcement.”

Clearly Pablo has been deemed  – by fans anyway- surplus to requirements and didn’t even make the bench for Scott Parker’s first team selection whilst Castellanos, subbed off after one half against Cardiff, looks unlikely to get another start any time soon.

Joel Piroe, meanwhile, as a forward is more likely to play a part but is not, apparently, considered an out and out striker:

“Joël Piroe provides another option but is not considered a traditional out-and-out number nine, leaving Parker with limited alternatives in a position that could prove crucial to West Ham’s promotion ambitions.”

The same report also confirms that, if Mavropanos stays at the club through the winter window, West Ham won’t be looking for another recruit to their back line:

“West Ham will pursue a new centre-back ..[only].. if Konstantinos Mavropanos leaves the club.
