Chelsea Women’s October internationals bring World Cup play-offs, a possible England debut for Lexi Potter and meetings between club team-mates.

The senior schedule runs from 9 to 14 October, before Sonia Bompastor’s side resume their Women’s Super League campaign against Tottenham.

Here are the call-ups, fixtures and UK kick-off times supporters need.

Last updated: 6 October 2026. All dates and times below are British Summer Time.

Potter has two opportunities to earn her first senior cap: away to Greece on Friday 9 October and in the return at Leicester’s King Power Stadium on Tuesday 13 October.

The midfielder joins Hannah Hampton, Lucy Bronze, Keira Walsh and Lauren James following her first England senior call-up.

Selection gives Potter the opportunity to make another significant step, although Sarina Wiegman has yet to confirm who will play.

England’s first leg kicks off at 17:30, with the home game starting at 19:30.

The FA’s broadcast guide confirms both matches will be shown live on ITV.
