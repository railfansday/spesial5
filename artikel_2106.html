Neil McCann: Why nothing has changed in my approach to build strong Kilmarnock team

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Neil McCann will continue his drive to see Kilmarnock “flourish” in the face of growing fan scrutiny.

The Ayrshire club are one point ahead of William Hill Premiership bottom side Hibernian, with nine points from 36 and having conceded 28 goals from 12 games amid concern and disgruntlement among a section of the Killie support.

McCann is preparing his players to take on a Celtic team who are four points ahead of Rangers at the top of the table, having played a game more and on the back of a morale-boosting 3-2 Europa League win over Besiktas on Thursday night

However, the Kilmarnock boss remains positive, and said: “Supporters will always give their opinion when they come into the stadium and they’ll voice it. They’re entitled to voice their opinion.

“My pressure, to be honest with you, is preparing our players tactically, physically, mentally.

“And then when they take the field, I give them full autonomy to go and carry out all of those instructions, but play with the freedom and bravery that got us out of trouble last year.

“Nothing’s changed in my build-up. Nothing’s changed in my drive. Nothing’s changed in my approach to getting Kilmarnock to be a team that wants to put on a product on the pitch.

“A lot’s been made of ‘you can just win games and it’s all about winning games’. And I absolutely get that when it comes to management.

“Of course, it’s about results, very much, it’s a results-driven business. But I was given this job to get us out of bother last year.

“And we came in, the first thing wasn’t about, yes, results were important, but it was making sure that the team were capable and prepared to go and get those results.

“I think we are prepared to go and get those results. We just haven’t given our support enough good days to celebrate wins.

“But the work doesn’t stop. And it doesn’t deter me one bit from the work that I do on the training field.

“I’ll hopefully see a team flourish, get the results that I think their performances deserve. And then we can all come together with the unity that we showed last season and hopefully Kilmarnock will start climbing the table.”
