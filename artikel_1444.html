Bayern Munich announces several months of absence for Harry Kane!

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Bayern Munich will have to face a very big blow. Hit in the left ankle during the match against Cologne (1-1), Friday evening, Harry Kane (33) suffers from a rupture of the syndesmotic ligament, as officially announced by the Bavarian club. The diagnosis was confirmed by the medical service after examinations. At 33, the English striker is preparing to miss an important period of the season, a real blow for Vincent Kompany and the reigning Ballon d’Or.

The Three Lions scorer was forced to leave his partners in the 20th minute after contact with Joël Schmied. A premature exit which quickly aroused the concern of the Munich staff. While the first information already suggested a serious injury, the Bayern press release confirmed fears: Kane will be absent “over the next few months”. A duration which could notably deprive him of several important meetings with the German champion.

An absence which could notably deprive Bayern of its scorer during several important European matches, starting with the trip to Lille, on November 25, and the reception of Slavia Prague, on December 8. This injury also adds to the already busy Bavarian infirmary. Before this meeting, Bayern had to deal without Manuel Neuer, Serge Gnabry, Ismaël Saibari, Alphonso Davies and Luis Díaz. Michael Olise also left the field at half-time against Cologne.

The French international, however, suffers from“a bruise on the forefoot and will be treated in the coming days”according to the Recorder’s press release. For Vincent Kompany, the loss of his main offensive asset therefore represents a serious headache as an important period in the calendar approaches. Bayern will have to quickly find solutions to compensate for their striker’s loss with a view to their next European matches, including the meeting against LOSC.
