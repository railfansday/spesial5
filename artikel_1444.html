Pierre Sage sack: The Crystal Palace players who declined most in his reign

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Crystal Palace just haven’t been the same since Oliver Glasner left and Pierre Sage arrived in his place in the dugout.

Palace had high hopes for Sage after seeing him guide Lens to second place in Ligue 1 last season. But Glasner was always going to be a tough act to follow after winning the FA Cup, Community Shield and Conference League during his reign. Not bad for a club without any major trophies before.

And Sage just hasn’t been up to scratch at Selhurst Park. A run of four defeats in a row in the Premier League, which started with a 5-0 thrashing by rivals Brighton, has seen Palace sink to the bottom of the Premier League.

They’ve also been unable to add to their opening-game win in the Europa League, most recently requiring a stoppage-time equaliser to salvage a draw against Hoffenheim.

The outlook has become bleak for Palace and they have been left with little choice but to sack Sage and move on. He would have wanted more than 16 games to try and get his project up and running, but there haven’t been enough convincing signs to stick with him.

Palace’s downturn in fortunes can be seen by how some of their players have regressed this season.

Here are the 10 Palace players whose ratings on WhoScored (from their Premier League appearances only) have decreased the most this season compared to last.
