The Nintendo Switch 2 and Mario Kart bundle is $50.99 off in Amazons Black Friday sale, and comes with a free online membership

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

SAVE OVER $50: As of Nov. 25, the Nintendo Switch 2 and Mario Kart World bundle is on sale for $499 in Amazon's Black Friday sale. That's $50.99 off its list price of $549.99.

Nintendo has dropped some great Black Friday deals so far, but one of the biggest highlights from its sale is on the Nintendo Switch 2 and Mario Kart World bundle.

As of Nov. 25, this bundle — which comes with the Switch 2 console, a digital download of Mario Kart World, and three months of a Nintendo Switch Online Individual membership — has been discounted to $499 at Amazon, saving you $50.99 off its original list price of $549.99. That original list price is actually a deal in itself, saving you $37.98 compared to buying each item separately at full price (which would cost $587.97). However, compared to the current Black Friday price, you're saving $88.97 on everything. After the Switch 2's price hike earlier this year, this is a very welcome offer.

And what better way to get started on the Switch 2 than with Mario Kart World? This delightful game is "chaotic, hilarious fun," according to Alex Perry's review for Mashable. If you have guests visiting over the holidays this year, it's sure to be a crowd-pleaser. The three-month Nintendo Switch Online Individual membership is also nice to have on hand, offering access to online play, a library of classic games, GameChat, and more.

This bundle deal won't be available for long. You have until Dec. 5 to pick it up.
