How long it takes to play GTA 6: Story playthrough and achievements

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Grand Theft Auto VI is finally here. The launch day for the highly anticipated video game has arrived, more than 13 years after its predecessor.

If you patiently waited more than a decade for GTA 6, you're probably hoping for a long, immersive experience that makes it worth the wait.

Good news! Reports are now coming in from game reviewers, and it seems like you're in for a treat. You'll likely be playing GTA 6 for a long time.

An early report from Insider Gaming suggested GTA 6 will take around 50 hours to complete the main campaign. Now that the wider review embargo has lifted, we can get an even more nuanced idea of how long it will take to finish the 91 main story missions GTA 6 has to offer.

For example, our friends at IGN polled four different players who had finished the game, and each of them had something different to say. One player finished the story in "just" 42 hours, which was the lowest estimate. Another took 65 hours, and then a third took 72 hours. Finally, IGN reviewer Luke Reilly said he needed a whopping 115 hours to complete the game.

To be clear, those figures represent the amount of time it took each writer to complete GTA 6's main campaign. If you want to fully complete everything in the game, you're looking at a much longer gameplay time. Much longer.

Aside from GTA 6's main campaign, there are also side quests and an array of interactions, events, and locations to explore. Gamers will likely want to unlock the reported 55 achievements, including the Platinum Trophy and the Mythic achievement.

If you want to do all of that, you could be looking at potentially at least 200 hours of gameplay for GTA 6.

That's not even counting any time spent just wandering the streets of Vice City or cruising around in a car while listening to the stacked GTA 6 soundtrack and radio stations.

So, lock in, gamers (and please take plenty of breaks). More than 200 hours of GTA 6 await.
