Apple’s MacBook Neo is $100 off at Amazon — its biggest-ever discount for Black Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

SAVE $100: Both variants of the Apple MacBook Neo are $100 off during Amazon's Black Friday Week sale — its biggest discount ever. Prices start at just $599 (normally $699) for the 256GB base model without Touch ID.

It's a holiday miracle: Amazon is giving the Apple MacBook Neo its biggest discount ever.

We were on hand when Apple launched its first-ever budget laptop in New York City this spring, and we (rightfully) predicted it was going to become a bestseller. The Neo put the entire Windows laptop world on notice with its speedy iPhone chip and elite build quality. And — bonus — it comes in fun colors.

As of Thanksgiving Day, the MacBook Neo is now $100 off during Amazon's Black Friday Week sale. Take your pick from the 256GB base model (now $599) or the 512GB variant with Touch ID (now $699). Each model is available in blush, silver, citrus, and indigo finishes.

Not only does this deal completely erase the Neo's price hike from June, but it also beats the Apple Store's education pricing. That's never happened before. This price drop couldn't come at a better time. Mashable recently named the Neo a Gift of the Year winner. If a teen or younger student you know needs a new laptop, we think they'll be delighted to unwrap this one. It's just as well-made as other MacBooks, has an all-day battery life (it lasted exactly 14 hours and 50 minutes in our battery rundown test), and runs basic tasks faster than most premium Windows laptops we've tried.

As we said when it originally launched, the "MacBook Neo is a shot across the bow of the Windows laptop market." To quote our tech editor, "This colorful budget laptop brings the MacBook's premium design elements and user experience to a whole new customer base."

In terms of performance, the A18 Pro chip held up surprisingly well in our stress testing. "I was able to edit a simple, cheesy video in Final Cut Pro using the MacBook Neo without struggling against the spinning wheel of death. Even when I layered video clips on top of each other and added a series of totally unnecessary transitions, the Neo handled the tasks just fine," our tech editor wrote.
