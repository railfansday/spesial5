Guide: These 13+ PS5 Games Are Coming Out This Week (9th-15th November)

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The release schedule has been on fire for a while now, but it's finally started to calm down, with a smaller selection of PS5 and PS4 games arriving this week.

In terms of highlights, there's Shrek 2 Remastered, which revives a fan favourite movie tie-in, and Warhammer Survivors, which is basically another Vampire Survivors game, which I'm definitely down for.

You've also got Barbie Rewind, which is a retro collection from the masters at Digital Eclipse, as well as Supermarket Simulator, finally making its long-awaited console debut. It's definitely a quieter week, but that's understandable considering what's looming on the horizon.
