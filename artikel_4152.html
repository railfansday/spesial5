'PlayStation Exclusive' Branding Added to Tentpole PS5 Games as Sony Doubles Down

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

For a period of the PS5 generation, Sony ported its games to the PC, rendering very few of its titles genuine exclusives.

In the last year and a bit it’s made an internal decision to focus solely on exclusivity, and it’s slowly beginning to promote that again.

As spotted by power user Radec on social media, the official PlayStation.com pages for Ghost of Yotei and Until Dawn 2 now include ‘PlayStation Exclusive’ logos.

While the manufacturer experimented with bringing its first-party titles to Steam, sales were allegedly poor.

This proves the company is now doubling down on exclusivity again, although it has said it’ll continue to release select multiplayer and live service games on PC, such as this year’s Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls.

I imagine we’re going to see Sony start to tout the ‘PlayStation Exclusive’ branding more aggressively once it rebuilds its library, beginning with next year’s God of War Laufey and (hopefully) Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet.
