Datadog: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

All 15 of the top 15 AI leaders use Datadog's tools, and its non-AI business is also accelerating.

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 4, 2026, Datadog (DDOG -14.42%) reported total revenue of $1.2 billion, representing 35% year-over-year growth. Broad-based adoption across non-artificial intelligence (AI) enterprise accounts and modern AI workloads underscores the platform's expanding role in software operations. Three things management said stand out.

Core enterprise demand for software monitoring expanded independently of the AI boom. That steady expansion provides reliable recurring cash flows while the company sells additional software modules to existing accounts.

The core infrastructure monitoring suite, which tracks cloud servers and hardware performance, continues to scale substantially. High platform retention improves long-term customer lifetime value as client software architectures grow more complex.

Landing larger upfront contracts accelerates top-line growth and reduces reliance on expanding existing accounts. New accounts are deploying multiple software products from day one, driving higher initial deal sizes.

Artificial intelligence adoption is expanding rapidly across every customer cohort, and market demand for software observability tools is broadening.

Platform consolidation, deeper public sector security certifications, and automated operational tools position the business to capture long-term enterprise spending. Management is watching pricing discipline as fast-growing clients expand usage.
