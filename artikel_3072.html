McGraw Hill: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Forty-four states passed Science of Reading laws, and early ELA win rates already beat internal targets, so the K-12 bet is taking shape.

On its fiscal second-quarter 2027 earnings call, held on Nov. 5, 2026, McGraw Hill (MH +5.07%) announced that total first-half revenue reached $1.2 billion, with reoccurring streams making up 71% of sales. High customer retention and subscription-based curriculum licenses give the educational publisher durable revenue visibility while recent debt paydowns reduce balance sheet risk. Three takeaways from the call stand out for long-term investors.

Management connects its curriculum to third-party software through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), a technical standard that links learning content directly to artificial intelligence models. Every user must hold a paid subscription, letting the company license its educational material across digital platforms while maintaining strict access controls.

The higher education business marked 41 consecutive quarters of market share growth, expanding sales even as overall college enrollments fell about 1%. Automatic digital distribution models, like Inclusive Access, now generate 58% of segment revenue, helping professors deliver coursework seamlessly while locking in annual institutional renewals.

A total of 44 states passed Science of Reading laws that require updated curriculum focused on evidence-based instruction. Early adoption win rates for the company's English Language Arts (ELA) program already exceed internal targets, positioning the K-12 segment for accelerated contract wins heading into fiscal 2028.

Operational momentum builds across print and digital learning formats as gross margin holds around 79%. McGraw Hill relies on high customer retention, multiyear state curriculum adoptions, and debt reduction to build long-term value. Management tracks contract win rates and pricing discipline.

Focus remains on executing core strategies ahead of anticipated growth acceleration.
