One of PS5's Most Promising RPGs Adds to the Chaos of January 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Good news: Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy has just been handed a release date.

Bad news: It's only adding to the avalanche of PS5 games launching across January and February.

The second Warhammer 40,000 RPG from the genre specialists at OwlCat Games, Dark Heresy is easily one of the most promising adventures on the console's horizon.

Its predecessor, Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader, remains one of the best CRPGs money can buy on Sony's system. It boasts a fantastic cast of characters, really involved quest design, and a clear love for all things Warhammer 40,000. This sequel has the potential to top it.

Dark Heresy releases on the 28th January, which puts it right next to the likes of Stranger Than Heaven and Forza Horizon 6, while Fate/Extra Record and Until Dawn 2 launch on the same day.

Metro 2039 is out the week after, followed by heavy hitters such as Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, Persona 4 Revival, and Fable. Things are looking real busy.

Is Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy on your radar? Are you a big Rogue Trader fan? Lower your chainsword in the comments section below.
