Nvidia's CEO Jensen Huang Resigns Following Infidelity Scandal

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Company produces custom processor for Switch 2

Nvidia, one of the largest tech firms in the world and the company responsible for the Switch 2's custom processor, has found itself embroiled in scandal over recent days.

As reported by The Guardian, Nvidia's CEO Jensen Huang, who you might recognise from the 'Creator's Voice Special Edition' video for Switch 2, has now resigned after reported affairs with an alleged Chinese spy and a company employee.

He will be replaced on an interim basis by Nvidia’s chief financial officer, Colette Kress, until a full-time successor is named. Nvidia's share price began to sharply decline following reports of the scandal, to the point where it is now no longer the most valuable company in the world.

The BBC speculates that Huang's departure could set him up to be a "formidable opponent" to the very company he co-founded, with Elon Musk stating that it would be "an honour" to work with him at SpaceX.

With regards to Nintendo, it's likely that none of this will have any immediate impact on Nvidia's relationship with the Japanese company. Nvidia will no doubt be under contract to supply custom chips for the Switch 2, but what happens with the inevitable Switch 2 successor is under question.

Hopefully everything will go smoothly for Nintendo, but if it wants to utilise Nvidia's architecture for its next console – particularly for backwards compatibility – then its efforts may be impacted by Huang's departure along with Nvidia's sharp pivot towards AI.

Of course, it's really early to be speculating about the 'Switch 3', but you can bet that Nintendo's R&D department will be looking at all of this very closely and considering its potential impact.
