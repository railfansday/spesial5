England welcome Croatia to Wembley just 40 days after thrashing Slaven Bilic's side 7-0 in Rijeka, with Thomas Tuchel's men all-but assured of a place in the Nations League quarter-finals as a result.

The lack of jeopardy attached to tonight's game means the Three Lions can concentrate on honouring captain Harry Kane, who will break Peter Shilton's 36-year appearance record when he wins his 126th cap tonight.

The Ballon d'Or winner looks certain to start, but Tuchel may rest some of his other stars ahead of Sunday's Group A3 finale away to Spain. That could open the door to a senior debut for uncapped trio Josh King, James Hill and Jobe Bellingham, albeit from the bench.

Croatia arrive at Wembley looking to restore some pride after they faced heavy criticism for the manner of their defeat by England last time out.

Manager Bilic will have fond memories of this stadium, having masterminded a famous 3-2 win here in 2007 that sealed his country's place at Euro 2008 and condemned the Three Lions to an embarrassing exit at the qualifying stage.

A repeat result looks a tall order, though, with Ivan Perisic suspended and several players unavailable due to injury. Croatia are third in the table and could still be leapfrogged by rock-bottom Czechia, who they face on Sunday.
