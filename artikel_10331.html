If AI doesn’t take your job, Robert Pattinson might

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

While everyone has been worried about AI taking their jobs, according to social media, the real threat all along was someone else entirely: Robert Pattinson.

The Englishman just landed the coveted role of James Bond, becoming the seventh actor to play the famous fictional spy.

It’s not only Pattinson’s appointment as 007 that is sparking conversation. Indeed, his screen credits are stacked, with the prolific actor starring in four major films just this year—and previously taking a turn as Batman.

And so in the meme-heavy culture of social media, the actor’s long list of achievements its not something just to be celebrated—but feared.

“AI won’t take my job. Robert Pattinson will,” a user joked on X, echoing hundreds of similar posts. Another added: “We were distracted by the idea that AI was going to take our jobs when the real enemy was Robert Pattinson.”

Across platforms like TikTok, X, and Threads, Pattinson is humorously being touted as next in line for jobs far beyond acting.

“BREAKING: Robert Pattinson has been selected by Nvidia’s board as the new CEO, effective immediately,” a user wrote on X.

‘I’m scared to go into the office tomorrow’

Apparently, even unglamorous jobs are unsafe from Pattinson. One user posted: “I’m scared to go into the office tomorrow and find Robert Pattinson already sitting at my desk.”

Brands have even jumped in on the joke, like KFC Spain’s X account. “You don’t need to come to KFC for job interviews. Robert Pattinson already took all the positions. Including mine,” the account wrote.

On TikTok, a similar conversation is brewing, with videos on the subject amassing hundreds of thousands of views.

“Guys if we are not careful, Robert Pattinson will play Martin Luther King, and that’s even thinking small. He might take it there with Tutankhamun, Caesar, I don’t know,” a user said on TikTok.

Bond in the Amazon era

The Pattinson-fronted 007 movie is expected to hit theaters in 2028, making it the first Bond movie since Amazon took control of the franchise with its acquisition of MGM Studios.

This year alone, Pattinson has appeared in The Drama (opposite Zendaya), Christopher Nolan’s epic The Odyssey, and the crime thriller Primetime, and will also appear in Dune: Part Three next month.

While some see range in the roles, others call it greed—jokingly, of course.

In a video captioned “Robert Pattinson . . . this is the greed they talked about in the Bible,” another user lists some of the actor’s big-name projects.

For others, Pattinson’s perseverance is also an excuse to riff on stereotypes.

“‘Immigrants are coming to take our jobs’ WRONG. ROBERT PATTINSON,” a user said on X. Another added: “Robert Pattinson got that hungry Mexican immigrant mentality.”

While the joke makes the rounds on social media, some have taken the time to note how thankful employed people should be in a world where Pattinson exists. As one user wrote: “If you have job rn it’s because Robert Pattinson doesn’t want it.”
