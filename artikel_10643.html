GTA 6 streamer with zero viewers left in tears after Ibai sent thousands of people to his broadcast

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Twitch star Ibai Llanos left a small GTA 6 streamer in tears after encouraging his followers to tune into his broadcast, which had no viewers.

The launch of GTA 6 on November 19 saw countless content creators flock to Twitch to stream Rockstar’s long-awaited game, with some of the platform’s biggest personalities attracting enormous audiences.

However, Spanish streaming sensation Ibai Llanos decided to turn his attention to the other end of the spectrum, searching for a GTA 6 streamer with no viewers in the hopes of giving them a boost.

As reported by Meristation, Ibai discovered a Dominican streamer named Dicrim_RD69, who was broadcasting his playthrough of GTA 6 to an audience of zero people.

After joining the broadcast himself, Ibai left a simple “Hello” in the chat before encouraging his followers on social media to visit the channel, follow the streamer, and keep him company.

His plan worked, with Dicrim’s audience skyrocketing to 3,243 concurrent viewers within hours. Initially laughing in disbelief at his sudden popularity, Dicrim became overwhelmed with emotion after discovering Ibai was responsible.

“Thank you. You guys have brought me to tears,” he said. “Ibai, I love you. I’ve been a fan of Ibai for a long time.”

The pair subsequently spoke over a video call, during which Dicrim revealed that he had been streaming for more than a year without attracting much of an audience.

“When you don’t have someone to talk to, to communicate with, you don’t feel the same. You feel bad,” he explained, admitting he had been feeling particularly upset the previous night.

Ibai reassured him by recalling his own beginnings as a content creator, revealing that he and his cousin used to stream basketball commentary to zero viewers when he was just 14 years old.

“If it makes you happy, keep doing it,” Ibai encouraged him. Dicrim, who explained that his family couldn’t afford video games when he was younger, thanked Ibai for the gesture. “You made me the happiest man in the world,” he said.
