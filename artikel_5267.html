From a technical standpoint, SHIB has moved further into a downtrend.

According to the technical analysis, the price is struggling to hold the important psychological threshold: $0.00000500 has already been broken, and SHIB now trades at approximately $0.00000447.

As for the future, SHIB is clearly moving in a longer-term downtrend; however, a local bounce toward $0.00000500 is possible. On the daily chart, the 200 moving average is at approximately $0.00000545.

Key price levels for SHIB are: $0.00000440 as the closest psychological support level, $0.00000410 as the next support, and 0.00000500–0.00000530 as the first psychological resistance level.

From the momentum standpoint, RSI is moving toward an oversold level. A break below the $0.00000410 support level could trigger a further decline, with a possible acceleration toward $0.00000400.

According to Shibarium data, DeFi liquidity is getting thinner, and TVL (Total Value Locked) fell to $41,000 in late October. Such a volume is irrelevant for the price of relatively large assets like Shiba Inu, which require millions to move.

Token burning stays down too. According to ShibBurn service data, only 375 million SHIB were burned in the last 30 days. With almost 600 trillion tokens in circulation, it is a drop in the ocean for Shiba Inu.

Key price levels for TRX are: $0.315 as the closest psychological support level, $0.305 as the next support, and $0.335 as the first psychological resistance level. On the daily chart, the 200 moving average is at approximately $0.328.

A break above $0.330 could put the price in recovery mode, opening the way to the $0.345 zone.

Based on data from TRON DAO's "Moving Trillions, Empowering Billions" research, the blockchain has already processed $2.2 trillion worth of stablecoins (settlement volume). The supply of USDT and USDD is at $93 billion, and total value locked crossed $13 billion.

Zcash (ZEC) is currently trading at $1,272, moving sideways. From a longer-term standpoint, the overall trend is still bullish, despite the price weakness.

On the daily chart, key price levels for ZEC are: $1,220 as the 50 moving average, $1,080 as the 100 moving average, and $1,350 as the first psychological resistance level.

The Zcash network activated the NU7 update on November 6, a strong catalyst for the fundamental health of the network that cuts blockchain time from 50 to 25 seconds. It brings transaction confirmation speed to the next level, while rewarding ZEC mining activity even more.

RSI remains near the neutral 50 level. A break above $1,350 could put the price in recovery mode, opening the way to the $1,450 zone.

Considering the behavior of the market, a break below the $1,200 support level could trigger a further decline, with a possible acceleration toward $1,100.
