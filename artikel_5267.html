Bitcoin has broken below an important support level as the tech sector absorbs the news that Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang is stepping down. BTC is trading near $71,878, down 3.0% in 24 hours and 5.6% over the week, according to market data. Ethereum has slipped to $2,011.98, down 3.2% on the day and 10.8% over seven days.

Nvidia said on Thursday that Huang is stepping down as CEO of the chipmaker he co-founded 33 years ago and built into a $4 trillion company. Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent replacement among internal and external candidates.

“The Board has full confidence in Colette and the leadership team, and we expect the company’s operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption,” board chair Harvey Jones said. Huang, 63, was not quoted in the release.

His exit follows reports of his relationship with a woman US intelligence reportedly believed to be a Chinese spy, and a separate report of a second affair with an Nvidia executive. Crypto has been trading closely with AI and tech stocks, so the shake-up at the sector’s biggest name adds to the pressure.
