Crimea bridge has been attacked again, says Russian-installed governor

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Assessment of damage is underway, days after two attacks that destroyed road section of key bridge across Kerch strait linking annexed peninsula to Russia

A key bridge connecting annexed Crimea to Russia has been attacked again, a Russian-appointed regional governor has said, two days after Ukrainian strikes caused serious damage to it.

“Following a new attack on the Crimean Bridge, traffic has been temporarily halted. All relevant services are on site and at work. An assessment of the damage is under way,” Sergei Aksyonov, the Kremlin-appointed governor of Crimea, wrote on Telegram. The Russian transport ministry said on Thursday that no one was wounded in the attack and that train services across it had been halted.

Wednesday’s ‌attack is third this week on the highly symbolic bridge that links Russia with Crimea, which ​it seized from Ukraine in 2014. A 227-metre section of the road spanning the Kerch strait was destroyed this week, but the rail corridor appears to be operable despite sustaining some damage.

The strike came just hours before an unprecedented meeting between Russian president Vladimir Putin and his Chinese and American counterparts, Xi Jinping and Donald Trump, in China. One source in Ukraine’s armed forces said this week’s attacks were designed to embarrass Putin on the world stage. “The bridge is a symbol of Putin’s rule. Destroying it sends a message to the other two great leaders that Putin is not as strong as he pretends,” the person said.

For Ukraine, the bridge is a hated symbol of Russian arrogance and colonial thinking. Ukraine has previously declared that the bridge across the Kerch strait, built eight years ago, is illegal. Kyiv had tried twice to blow it up – with a truck bomb in 2022, and a sea drone the following year.

Earlier, Ukraine critically damaged the road section of the bridge in what President Volodymyr Zelenskyy hailed as an “unprecedented” military operation. A span of the bridge’s road section collapsed, but the railway line was still standing.

Zelesnkyy described the earlier attack as the culmination of a long and complex campaign to demolish the bridge, involving domestically produced drones and ballistic missiles. Palianytsia drones hit the road section of the bridge, while Neptune and other missiles targeted the adjacent rail section, he added.
