Fiona the hippo undergoing care after swallowing a hat at Cincinnati Zoo

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Watch: Fiona the hippo under observation after eating hat

The Cincinnati Zoo in Ohio says its beloved hippopotamus Fiona is undergoing emergency medical care after swallowing a knit hat that slipped into her enclosure.

The Zoo said that Fiona had swallowed the hat about nine days ago and was asymptomatic at first, adding that she was "large enough that a small-ish hat should be passing through".

But a few days later, her appetite started to decrease, and the zoo staff were trying to encourage Fiona to eat to help push the hat through her digestive system.

Fiona rose to fame when she was born premature at the Cincinnati Zoo and at first struggled to survive. Her story inspired an ice cream flavour as well as a character in Cincinnati's ballet.

Michelle Curley, a spokesperson for the zoo, told US media that the hat got into Fiona's enclosure by accident, after a visitor tossed it in the air and it caught on a ceiling fan that flung it through a small opening.

"We probably couldn't make [it] happen again if we tried," Curley said.

Zoo staff have been providing a series of supportive measures like fluids and ultrasounds to help Fiona recover.

The latest ultrasound showed the hat passing through Fiona's small intestine, the staff said, adding that she is still a bit dehydrated.

"Surgery isn't an option for full-grown hippos and our best chance for success with Fiona would be if she could pass this on her own," one of the zookeepers said in an update.

Fiona was born six weeks early to 17-year-old hippo Bibi on 24 January 2017.

At birth she weighed 13 kg (29 lbs), which the zoo said was about half the previous lowest recorded birth weight for her species.

The zoo said staff were "rooting for her and hoping that she proves to be as strong and resilient as she was at the beginning of her life".
