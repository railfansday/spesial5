The Pokémon TCG Hops Zacian ex Box is 20% off with coupon code — shop for close to market price

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Hop’s Zacian ex Box is down to $34.59 at Amazon with a 20% off coupon, reduced from its usual $43.24 price.

With Black Friday approaching, some of the older Pokémon TCG collections are getting their own share of discounts alongside those we’ve seen for Delta Reign and 30th Celebration. If you’ve been hoping to catch up on Journey Together and Surging Sparks, this one’s perfect for you.

As of Nov. 16, the Pokémon TCG Hop’s Zacian ex Box is available for $34.59 at Amazon once you apply the 20% coupon to its current $43.24 price, knocking $8.65 off the total. Better yet, it's sold and shipped directly by Amazon rather than a third-party seller — with free delivery set for Nov. 17 for Prime members, or Nov. 21 for non-members, at the time of writing.

The only caveat is that you need to tick the coupon box before checkout, as the offer is only valid until Nov. 23 and could end even sooner, depending on how quickly stock sells out.

Mashable has confirmed through its own research that this is the lowest price the Hop’s Zacian ex Box has been since October 2025, before shooting up in price — making it the best deal we've seen in over a year.

Hop’s Zacian ex Box contains four Pokémon TCG booster packs (two Journey Together, one Surging Sparks, and one Paradox Rift), working out to roughly $8.65 apiece, alongside three foil trading cards featuring Hop’s Zacian ex, Wooloo, and Dubwool. An oversized Zacian promo, photo sticker, and Pokémon TCG Live code card are also included.
