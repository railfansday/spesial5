GTA VI Takes Over Thanksgiving Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Bloomberg reporter Josyana Joshua tells host Lisa Mateo how the long-awaited release of Grand Theft Auto VI is becoming a cultural and workplace phenomenon, with gamers taking time off and preparing to play through Thanksgiving week. Joshua explains how the game’s 13-year buildup is driving demand for consoles and gaming gear while potentially competing with family gatherings, football and other holiday entertainment. (Source: Bloomberg)
