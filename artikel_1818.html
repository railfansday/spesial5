From FA Cup embarrassment to Fleetwood triumph – Crawley Town fans have reason to smile

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

“It’s a funny old game football”, as Greavesy would have said back in the day, although I don’t think he meant comical, more like funny peculiar. Having been knocked out of the FA Cup by Wimborne, a team three divisions below League Two, although if you’re going to nit-pick, it’s actually, two divisions and a bit as it stands at the moment between us and them, we have now beaten the team that dumped the current Premier League Champions out of the Carabao Cup.

Before the game against Fleetwood, Kevin Blackwell was made “permanent” manager, at least until the end of the season, and the general feeling around Broadfield Stadium before the game was that it was the right thing to do in the position we find ourselves in at the moment.

The atmosphere in the stadium, right from the kick off, was excellent and it was made even better when Kai Jennings scored after just five minutes in front of a segregated North stand with half of it being given over to home supporters.

As expected, Fleetwood began to get into the game, but the presence of Josh Flint and Ben Heneghan as central defenders and playing Danilo Orsi AND Pemi Aderoju up front together gave Crawley a totally different more attacking outlook.

Scoring early after the half time break certainly helps, and if we had scored a third, no one could have said it wouldn’t have been deserved. Two nil leads carry with them a certain tension and when the Cod Army pulled a goal back with eleven minutes to go it almost seemed inevitable that fate would deliver us yet another cruel blow. This time however, the boys in Red saw it through to the very end and thoroughly deserved the heart felt appreciation given to them by the home support.

There were only two League Two games scheduled for last night (if  you’re reading this on Wednesday morning.) Our home game against second in the division, Barnet , but the other game being played will also affect us, as it is between Port Vale and Northampton, who have six more points than Crawley, but have played one and two games more than us respectively. The best outcome for me would be for us to beat Barnet and for the other game to be a draw. I’ll leave you to do the Maths.

On the Red Devils’ Diary front things are going well and we have no plans to increase the cost from £2, or rather we didn’t have, but in conjunction with the club’s announcement of their 130th anniversary week events beginning on Monday 7th December and culminating with our current players playing against Newport County on Saturday the 12th in a special one-off kit reminiscent of the 1896 one worn by our founders, we will be producing a souvenir edition for the Newport game which will feature messages from and pen pics of ex-players and will also feature so much more inside for a  once only cost of just £3,  If you haven’t bought a ricket for the Wednesday night event yet, when ex-players will be present to take your questions and you will get a chance to have a glimpse at the first CTFC MUSEUM exhibition, then why not?

Onwards and upwards. Red4ever, that’s  me!
