Reports: Camarda could reunite with Abate or head abroad as Milan consider next steps

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Francesco Camarda could be headed to Turin in January if AC Milan agree to a loan deal with Torino, according to new reports.

There has been plenty of discussion about where will be right for Camarda and there are several options on the table. Now, another Serie A option has emerged and they could become a priority option.

As Tuttosport (seen below) writes, understandably given their base in Turin, Torino are reportedly keen on a move for Camarda. Led by Ignazio Abate, it could be the perfect alignment for the young striker to finally regain the form which led him to the first team.

The link to Abate could be used to sweeten a deal for all parties, especially as it’s a figure the Rossoneri and Camarda trust.

However, they are not the only club with an interest; according to Gazzetta dello Sport (also seen below), the Italian striker is popular abroad. His goal against France’s U-21 side – and his general performance – have relit the market for him.

There are other clubs interested in him from Italy, but the option of a move away from the Italian system could prevail. Foreign leagues, as the report writes, ‘could capitalise on his skills and instincts as a true centre-forward’. Additionally, the lure of international experience could help him.
