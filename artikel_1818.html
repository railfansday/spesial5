Championship attendance table: Sheffield United, Middlesbrough FC, West Ham, Wolves, Southampton & more ranked

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Championship attendance table with Sheffield United, Middlesbrough, West Ham United, Wolves, Southampton and more compared

The second tier of English football is home to some of the country’s most passionately-supported clubs - many of which have tested their fans over the years.

Sheffield United have suffered relegation and play-off final heartbreak in recent years, although now sit in a promising 10th in the table.

Middlesbrough were beaten in the Championship play-off final last term but are now back in the promotion hunt, sitting second behind Birmingham City.

Fans of clubs such as West Ham United, Wolverhampton Wanderers and Southampton have had to watch falls from grace, but each have retained the support of their fans.

But how do clubs in the Championship compare when it comes to average attendance? Here are the average attendances of every Championship club this season, ranked from lowest to highest, with data courtesy of Transfermarkt.
