Atletico Madrid consider Martin Zubimendi for January

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

According to Sport, Atletico Madrid are considering a move for Martin Zubimendi ahead of the January transfer window, with sporting director Mateu Alemany seeing the Arsenal midfielder as a key target to strengthen the heart of Diego Simeone’s squad.

The former Real Sociedad man is said to be frustrated by his role in Mikel Arteta’s team, being behind the likes of Declan Rice and Bruno Guimaraes in the pecking order in the middle of the park. The situation has given Atletico the idea that a move in January might be feasible.

However, the Rojiblancos are aware that securing his signature will not be straightforward. The Spain international remains highly regarded across Europe, and Real Madrid had also tracked him closely until recently.

Atletico believe Zubimendi would be an ideal fit for Simeone’s system due to his ability to control possession, dictate the tempo of play and provide defensive stability in midfield. The prospect of partnering him with Pablo Barrios is reportedly viewed as an exciting possibility within the club.

Arsenal showed strong interest in Atletico forward Julian Alvarez during the summer, but no swap deal is on the cards at this point.
