How Mo Salah Has Achieved Incredible Ripped Physique in His 30s

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Mohamed Salah has been known to keep his body in peak physical condition throughout his football career. Having turned 34 years old in June 2026, this is even more vital than ever before if the 'Egyptian King' plans to extend his stay at the top of the beautiful game for years to come.

The likes of Cristiano Ronaldo and even Salah's former Liverpool teammate, James Milner, notably continued playing at the highest level into their late 30s after taking extreme care of their bodies over the years.

These football stars shocked everyone by getting into even better shape after quitting the game...

He returned to pre-season training ahead of the 2024/25 campaign and blew the competition out of the water when it came to running tests, despite being one of the few experienced players back at the training ground at the time. Salah was even called 'phenomenal' by a member of Liverpool's coaching staff upon his return from holiday.

Fast-forward to 2026 – having left Liverpool in the summer – the 34-year-old joined Trabzonspor. His commitment to fitness has kept him in good form in Turkey so far. Beyond the football, though, he also caused a stir online when he shared an image of his impressive six-pack on social media.

According to the Daily Mail, a huge part of Salah's secret to being in such fine physical shape is his diet. The Egypt international has his own personal chef, who is tasked with preparing all the right foods to keep the winger firing on all cylinders.

It's claimed that he eats broccoli with almost every single meal, along with other 'superfoods'. This is just part of his recipe for success. The Liverpool icon is also extremely fond of a dish from his home country, called koshary, which is a mixture of fried rice, pasta, pulses, onions and tomato sauce.

In 2022, he went into more detail about what his favourite foods are and even spoke about what he likes to eat when treating himself. Even then, however, it's clear how health-conscious he is:

"I prefer broccoli, sweet potatoes, fish, chicken and always salad. When I indulge in a great restaurant in London, I prefer sushi. I eat pizza once a month at the most. I love hamburgers, but I hardly ever eat them because afterwards I feel heavy!"

As well as his healthy eating, he also looks after his body 24/7 in other ways. Inside his home, Salah has two different gym rooms along with some incredible equipment that helps to keep him fit. He's said to have a hyperbaric chamber in his house, which aims to help the lungs spread more oxygen around the body by having air pressure levels three times higher than normal conditions.

He also has cryotherapy facilities which include ice-led machines which constrict blood vessels to decrease swelling. It's incredible to see the lengths Salah goes to when it comes to his training regime. As a Muslim, the forward has to adapt his routine during Ramadan, which includes 2am gym sessions.

GIVEMESPORT Key Statistic: Mohamed Salah is the top goalscorer in Premier League history for Liverpool, having eclipsed Robbie Fowler's tally of 128 in 2023.

Having defined the very best moments of the Jurgen Klopp years, he began a new era at the Merseyside-based club by scoring and assisting on the opening day of the 2024/25 Premier League season against Ipswich Town in what was Arne Slot's first competitive game in charge.

Despite his brilliant form in 2024/25, there were doubts about Salah's future as his contract was due to run out in 2025. But, thankfully for Liverpool fans around the world, the club and Salah were able to agree to a deal as he put pen to paper on a new two-year contract in April 2025.

The 30 greatest players in Liverpool history have been named, including Steven Gerrard, Kenny Dalglish, Virgil van Dijk and Mohamed Salah.

Despite putting pen to paper on a deal until 2027, Salah announced that he'd be cutting that contract short and departing at the end of the 2025-26 season. He then joined Trabzonspor in the summer of 2026, signing a two-year contract in August. It took him until just September to score his first hat-trick for his new side, doing so against defending champions Galatasaray.

Still fit and firing at 34, it just goes to show how well Salah has looked after his body.
