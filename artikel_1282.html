Spain 2-3 England: Thomas Tuchel Told ‘Outstanding’ Lewis Hall Must Start Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

England defeated Spain in a thrilling match at the Metropolitano in Madrid as the world champions suffered defeat in the capital for the first time in 40 years.

In a thrilling encounter, the visitors took the lead from a Harry Kane penalty after the Bayern Munich star was tripped inside the area. Tempers flared at half-time, and it set up an all-action second-half perfectly.

England were in dreamland as Ezri Konsa and Ben White put the Three Lions 3-0 ahead before the hour mark. However, Spain fought back as Elliot Anderson was sent off for denying Lamine Yamal a goalscoring opportunity.

Mikel Oyarzabal slid the penalty home before Nico Williams pulled another back. But it ended in a narrow victory for the away team despite being a man down for 30 minutes.

Handed perhaps the most daunting task in world football right now, Lewis Hall stood up and made quite an impression for his country. The young left-back was tasked with keeping Lamine Yamal, one of the world's best players, quiet on home soil.

While it's nearly impossible to keep the teenager out of a full game without getting chances, Hall did a solid job. Particularly in the first half and before Anderson's red card.

The Newcastle United defender made seven clearances and two interceptions. He also won eight of his 10 attempted duels. Not only that, but he showed quality going the other way.

When the tie was still 11 v 11, Hall linked up well with his former Newcastle teammate Anthony Gordon down the left flank. The 22-year-old completed 19 of his attempted 21 passes (90%).

Fans were quick to praise Hall's contributions on social media. It looks like he'll be most people's first-choice for the left-back spot, ahead of Manchester City's Nico O'Reilly.

One X user said: "If there was a debate about who England's first-choice left-back is - and it should never have been - Lewis Hall is ending it tonight. Been magnificent so far."

Another waxed lyrical about the youngster's first-half display, saying: "That’s one of the best 45 minutes I’ve seen from England for a long time. Superb. Lewis Hall has been outstanding."

Others were quick to point out that Hall didn't even make the World Cup squad: "Genuinely ridiculous how Tuchel did not take Lewis Hall to the World Cup."
