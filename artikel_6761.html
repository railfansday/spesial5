Omega and Swatch Just Launched a New MoonSwatch—and No Two Are the Same

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Mission to Earthphase – Silver Snowflake, the 39th watch in the series, will have collectors keeping an eye on the Swiss weather forecast.

By MoonSwatch standards, it’s been a quiet year. Swatch has been otherwise occupied with the Audemars Piguet Royal Pop, the May release for which people lined up for six days in New York, police deployed tear gas in France, and which, according to Swatch Group, has generated more than 25 billion views on social media.

Not that interest in the MoonSwatch has gone away.

An estimated 4.5 million have been sold since the collaboration began in March 2022 (and that figure only takes us to the end of 2024). In July this year, some 1.2 million people applied to buy one of the 1,969 examples of its $570 Mission to the Moon 1969, a watch that incorporated 11 grams of 18-carat gold recycled from vintage Omega components.

The latest MoonSwatch—only the third new model this year, compared with 11 in 2023—is the Mission to Earthphase – Silver Snowflake.

An all-black counterpart to last winter’s all-white Cold Moon edition, it takes as its starting point the idea that no two snowflakes are alike.

Swatch has developed a digital process capable of generating millions of different snowflake patterns, one of which is laser-engraved onto the moonphase indicator of each watch. So, unusually for a mass-produced quartz watch, every example is unique.

It also features the complication Swatch introduced in November 2024 and which remains exclusive to the MoonSwatch: an earthphase indicator. Where a conventional moonphase shows the phases of the Moon as viewed from Earth, this does the reverse. The two displays run in opposite directions over the same 29.5-day cycle.

(How useful this is to those of us who live on Earth is debatable, but it’s one of the more original ideas in watchmaking in recent years.)

There are some nice details elsewhere on the 42mm Bioceramic watch, including Snoopy and Woodstock in the snow, a pair of miniature skis at 11 o’clock, and a tiny patch of white on the Earth representing a snow-covered Switzerland, which glows green under UV light.

As with last year’s winter edition, there’s a stipulation.

The watch goes on sale at selected Swatch stores worldwide on Black Friday, November 27. After that, until March 20, 2027, the last day of winter, you’ll only be able to buy one when it’s snowing in Switzerland.

Swatch will be providing weather updates on its website.
