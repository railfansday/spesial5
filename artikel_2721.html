JJ Redick on how Lakers' offense has struggled without Austin Reaves

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

While the Los Angeles Lakers, for the most part, have continued to win games, their offense has dried up of late. In their last three games, they have scored 111, 97 and 106 points after averaging 122.8 points a game through their first 14 games.

They have been without Austin Reaves for their last four games due to a bruised heel, and they will be without him again on Monday when they host the Utah Jazz, the team that held them to 97 points and snapped their nine-game winning streak this past Wednesday. He is averaging 24.7 points a game on 51.3% overall shooting and 40.9% from 3-point range this season.

Without him, the Lakers are missing a major scoring option who can put pressure on opposing defenses and also advance the ball upcourt and get teammates involved. On Sunday, coach JJ Redick gave some details on how much the team has missed Reaves.

"The first 12 games we were the No. 1 halfcourt offense. Austin was on the court for 70% of those possessions. In the last seven games, we've not been a good halfcourt offense. Austin's been on the court for 9% of those possessions. So who would have thought that not having an elite offensive player, an All-Star level player would hurt your offense?"

In recent days, L.A. has slipped from first to third in both points per game and offensive rating without Reaves. His absence has put lots of added pressure on Luka Doncic to produce points while also facilitating its offense, and that extra pressure may be wearing on him a bit. He shot 13-of-34 from the field on Wednesday and 10-of-28 on Friday versus the Sacramento Kings, and he has made just nine of 37 3-point attempts while committing a total of 20 turnovers in his last three contests.

The good news regarding Reaves is that he returned to practice on Sunday, so perhaps it won't be too long before he returns to game action. The Lakers' next game after Monday will be on Friday against the San Antonio Spurs in NBA Cup group action.
