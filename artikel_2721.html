David Montgomery has no injury designation for Thursday; Woody Marks ruled out

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Texans ruled one running back out for Thursday night’s game against the Colts, but they’re set to get another one back in the lineup.

David Montgomery has no injury designation after being listed as a full practice participant all week. Montgomery has missed the last four games with a groin injury.

Montgomery’s return will help make up for the absence of Woody Marks, who was ruled out with the concussion he suffered in last Sunday’s loss to the Browns. Defensive end Danielle Hunter and wide receiver Marvin Harrison Jr. have also been ruled out with hamstring injuries. Neither of them played in the Cleveland game either.

Tight end Brevin Jordan (foot, illness) will not play. Cornerback Jaylin Smith (hamstring), linebacker Jake Hummel (groin), and guard Wyatt Teller (back) are all listed as questionable.
