Cowboys injuries: George Pickens, Joey Porter Jr. status stays the same

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Dallas Cowboys haven't beaten the San Francisco 49ers since 2020, and Dak Prescott hasn't beaten them since 2017. Dallas is hoping something will change on Sunday. They are currently 4.5-point underdogs and can shock the NFL world by pulling off the upset.

As far as available bodies, that is looking like it could be a problem for the game. Here's what we know after today's injury report.

Dallas' long list of DNPs continues. Donovan Ezeiruaku (Ankle), Tyler Goodson (Knee), Jake Ferguson (Knee), Jonathan Mingo (Ankle), P.J. Clark (Concussion) and Joey Porter Jr. (Ankle) did not practice Thursday. Porter Jr. is hopeful that he can return this weekend but it's not looking good thus far. Safety Alijah Clark was also added to the DNP list with an illness.

KaVontae Turpin (Ribs) and George Pickens (Hamstring) were limited today. Safety Juanyeh Thomas (Hamstring) was added to the limited list. Meanwhile DeMarvion Overshown (Hamstring/Back) was upgraded to a full participant.
