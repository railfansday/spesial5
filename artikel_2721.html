Rams lead 3-0, Christian Watson makes quick return from thigh injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Rams are out to an early lead at SoFi Stadium.

After forcing a quick Packers punt, the Rams ran three times for a first down. Matthew Stafford’s first pass of the game was a deep shot to wide receiver Xavier Smith that fell incomplete, but Packers rookie cornerback Brandon Cisse was flagged for pass interference.

The play picked up 37 yards for the home team, but three straight completions by Stafford only picked up seven more yards and Brandon McManus was called on for a field goal that made it 3-0 with 9:55 to play in the first quarter.

The Packers opened the game with a six-yard pass to wide receiver Christian Watson, but he needed medical attention after the play. The team called him questionable to return with a thigh injury, but he was back on the field after the Rams kicked the ball off.
