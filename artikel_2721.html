Jaguars vs. Titans odds: Opening lines for NFL Week 10 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

The Jacksonville Jaguars will look to keep their momentum rolling against the Tennessee Titans in Week 10.

The Jaguars (6-2) are coming off a reassuring Thursday Night Football win against the Baltimore Ravens. Halfway through the season, Jacksonville is the only NFL team to rank top five in points per game (28.6) and points allowed per game (15.1), leading the league by a wide margin in point differential (+108; next closest team is at +79). The Jags aren't just good. They're great. And now that Jessie Bates III is in the fold… who's stopping them?

The Titans (1-7) were already among the worst teams in the league, and then they traded future Hall of Fame defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons to the Chicago Bears. Robert Saleh's first season as Tennessee's coach has been a predictable failure. He couldn't remember his own message to the team a couple of weeks ago. 2026 really must be the season for Jacksonville, because as the Jaguars peak, the Titans seem to be hitting rock bottom.

According to FanDuel Sportsbook, the Jaguars are 9.5-point favorites against the Titans in Week 10. The total is set at 47.5 points.
