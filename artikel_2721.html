Drake Maye out indefinitely with potential ankle fracture

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The New England Patriots fear that Drake Maye suffered an ankle fracture during Sunday's loss to the Detroit Lions. According to a report by ESPN's Adam Schefter, he underwent further testing in Germany to determine whether or not surgery would be necessary.

Regardless of that decision, Maye will be out 'indefinitely' and potentially for the remainder of the season.

The New England Patriots appear to have suffered a major blow on Sunday. Quarterback Drake Maye, who exited the game against the Detroit Lions in the first quarter, is expected to be "out an extended period of time" and might even miss the remainder of the season altogether.

According to a report by Ian Rapoport of NFL Network, Maye suffered a "significant lower leg injury" in the Patriots' 35-10 loss to the Lions.

Maye, 24, exited the game after the opening drive. He had completed three of his first four pass attempts for 48 total yards and also gained 11 yards on a scramble, but was taken down for a third-down sack to end the series. After the play, which also saw Lions edge Aidan Hutchinson suffer a potentially season-ending injury while celebrating, the Patriots' third-year QB limped of the field.

He later entered the blue medical tent on the sidelines and after emerging made his way to the locker room. Maye was initially announced as questionable to return with an ankle injury, but was ruled out for the remainder of the game shortly thereafter.

In his absence, backup quarterback Tommy DeVito entered the contest. DeVito completed 12 of 24 passes for 81 yards with one touchdown and one interception. New England scored once with him under center.

The Patriots flew back to the United States on Sunday, after their game against the Lions in Munich. Maye is expected to undergo further testing to determine the exact severity of the injury and establish a recovery timeline.

The Patriots are on their bye this week before a Week 12 game against the Los Angeles Chargers.
