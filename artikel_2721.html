Halifax sign Wigan midfielder Sze on loan

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

National League side FC Halifax Town have signed midfielder Christopher Sze on a one-month loan deal from Wigan Athletic.

The 22-year-old has made 56 appearances for the League One club since signing professional terms in 2021 but has featured only nine times this season in all competitions.

Sze spent the second half of last season on loan with National League North side Southport, scoring 12 times in 23 appearances.
