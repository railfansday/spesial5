Miami Dolphins Fans: Your Post-Game Week 10 Vs Indianapolis Colts Takeaways

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Following this past Sunday's loss to the Indianapolis Colts by our Miami Dolphins, I asked the following question:

What are your overall takeaways from this afternoon's ninth game of the season (Week 10) between the Miami Dolphins and the Indianapolis Colts? What are the positive and negative takeaways from this afternoon's game, as they apply to the team overall and to individual units and/or players?

Below are some of your thoughts on Sunday's game-

DolfaninME got the news they were hoping not to get.

Dolfanjoe seems pleased with what they saw from McCord's first outing.

Playing the Colts after a fire sale. Yes we very well could have won that game. McCord played dam well for a first timer ! Too many knocked down at the line of scrimmage, a few risky throws. But over all a good showing. No Seiler in the middle showed on some run plays, but we were going against Taylor and his running mate. Still making them a formidable running team. Bills come into town next week with the odds against us. Just need to see them give it hell like this game. We are going to get better and more experienced over the next two years. Talent will be added, there will come a time when suddenly we are that better team and should win !

Phoenix6 cares about kickers even if you don't!

Cutie0716 is happy with how the team is being built from the ground up.

I looked at the roster and counted 15 rookies on the 53. Not including the ones on the Injured list and the PS.

These rookies are playing the way I had wished our rookies of yesterday would have played. Let them play. Let them make mistakes. These guys are really developing a nucleus. I HONESTLY don't care about wins and losses.

Every game I read about, I read how tough the D is getting. How they are fighting until the end. These guys are hungry. This is going to be a big deal with our 2027 draft picks.

It's one thing to be on a losing team, but when MORE THAN HALF of the whole team are rookies, it makes a statement. They are playing hard and meaningfully. I give the Coach and GM credit for picking their own guys.

Proctor will be, if he's not already there, a beast by next year. A rookie that our team depended on. It's wonderful. No one thought that Proctor would be our pick.

I knew months ago that we wouldn't win much. Some were predicting five wins. If we win 2-3, BUT ESTABLISHING building blocks, we have done a great job.

The building of a cohesive team is all that we need right now. I know that most of you, if not all, strongly disagree with my opinion, and that's fine. You have your own opinion. No problem. I gave you mine. I love to see the replays on NFLN because I see HOW HARD these guys play. I CHEER THEM FOR THAT. And so should you.

StanleyDoyle1 is happy with the rookies' contributions so far.

My takeway: I cannot remember a season when more rookies had a meaningful impact on the team. This season, Rodriguez, Proctor, Johnson, Douglas, Taafe have all shown real talent and made a meaningful difference in games. Kacmarek, Bell and Coleman have shown promise. That's eight rookies. 2020 had 7 contributors. 2012 had 6. 2007 had 5. 1997 had 6. To find something comparable to this season, you have to go back to 1982 when they had 12 rounds to find as many contributors from the draft.

Luvs2drnk took a different stance from StanleyDoyle1.

If we were winning more games, I'd be inclined to agree with you. But the main reason we have so many rookies playing meaningful snaps is due to the gutting of the roster. So while they are getting meaningful snaps on our team, not so sure they would do the same on a competitive team. I'd give you Rod, Proc, Johnson, and Douglas although the first 3 would be the only ones who would likely be starting on any other team. Taafe has shown some potential, need to see more consistancy. Kac likely becomes a blocking TE with little to no impact as a receiving TE, which is disappointing for a 3rd rounder. Bell could still develop, but won't likely have any impact till next year hopefully. Coleman could eventually replace Washington or fade into obscurity if more talented receiving options are added. The fact we got 4-5 potential long term starters out of this class is a feat in itself, and that definitely bodes well for next year's draft. Let's just hope they have better luck in the FA department next year. I'll give this year a pass due to cap restrictions.

Well, it was a bit slow, but I guess the fans' overall enthusiasm is down because of the losing, and those fans choosing something else to do right now are missing out on seeing these rookies ball out. Anway, thank you to each of you who took the time to join in and answer our Phinsider Question Of The Day.
