Cavs at Knicks: How to watch, odds, and injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Cleveland Cavaliers have a chance to advance out of the group stage of the NBA Cup for the first time in franchise history. To do that, they will need to defeat the New York Knicks. And it wouldn't hurt to win by a healthy margin.

At this point, we don't know what version of the Cavs we'll see on a given night. Last time they were in Madison Square Garden, James Harden delivered a tremendous performance, and the Cavs looked like the contending team many hoped they would be coming into the season. Unfortunately for Cleveland, that good game hasn't been repeated often since.

The Cavaliers are coming off arguably their worst loss of the season against the Chicago Bulls, who were playing on the second night of a back-to-back. Cleveland's defense was atrocious, and their offense wasn't good enough to carry them against a struggling Chicago team. We'll see what version of the Cavs we'll get here.
