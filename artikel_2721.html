UFC books rising welterweight clash for Sydney

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Rising UFC welterweights will collide Down Under.

Jonathan Micallef and Sam Patterson each have momentum, but only one can continue to build on it. At UFC Fight Night 298, they will collide in a three-round bout at Afterpay Arena in Sydney. The promotion recently announced the bout on social media.

Micallef (10-1 MMA, 3-0 UFC) rides a five-fight winning streak into the bout including three victories in the UFC. His UFC wins have come against Kevin Jousset, Oban Elliott and Themba Gorimbo.

Patterson (15-3-1 MMA, 5-2 UFC) has looked brilliant in recent performances, with the exception of a unanimous decision loss to Michael Page in March. His five UFC victories were all finishes and include Yohan Lainesse, Kiefer Crosbie, Danny Barlow, Trey Waters and Santiago Ponzinibbio.
