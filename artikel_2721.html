Denzel Boston's Week 10 status in question

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Cleveland Browns WR Denzel Boston (toe) was limited during practice Friday, Nov. 13, and is listed as questionable for Week 10.

Why It Matters: Boston may be a game-time decision since he missed most of practice this week, so fantasy players should check the inactive list for his official status. He will see a handful of targets if he is cleared to play and can be a No. 4 receiver in fantasy leagues.
