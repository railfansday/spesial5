Brandon Aiyuk will not play Sunday vs. Cardinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

While receiver Brandon Aiyuk joined the Commanders earlier this week, but his debut with the team will have to wait.

Head coach Dan Quinn confirmed to reporters in his Friday press conference that Aiyuk will not be elevated for Sunday’s game against the Cardinals.

Aiyuk has only had a couple of practices with Washington, but he’s impressed Quinn with his work so far.

“I think it’s not surprising, but I think the catch radius and how strong his hands were,” Quinn said. “I knew that, but I hadn’t seen it for couple of years I suppose. But, his aggressive nature to go get the ball and pluck it. He can get in and out of breaks very quickly. So, those were two of the things that caught my eye yesterday and today.

“But it’s a good first impression for sure. He’s like everybody else, working hard and trying to learn it. New faces, new scheme, new people. He’s off to a good start.”

Aiyuk has not played since the 2024 season. His first opportunity to get on the playing field may come in Week 13 when the Commanders face the Titans on the road.
