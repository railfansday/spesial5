Stream, meet sea: Carnival is integrating Twitch into its cruises

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

When Dolly Parton and Kenny Rogers sang about "Islands in the Stream," I don't think they were talking about Twitch, but Carnival Cruise Line has other ideas. The Florida-based travel company will stream on the open seas with Play Carnival, an initiative that will show viewers how much fun they can have aboard the Celebration ship.

Play Carnival will kick off on November 15 on the cruise line's official Twitch account. Carnival Fleet Cruise Director Erin Weigand will be joined by veteran creator iJustine, who will become a "player" as she takes on various challenges aboard Celebration.
