Carbon-rich soil near old oaks does not attract more earthworms, study finds

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Solitary oaks in traditional Romanian wood pastures create islands of carbon-rich soil around their trunks. However, a research team led by the Universities of Göttingen and Hildesheim found this does not mean more earthworms. In traditional wood pastures in Romania, the topsoil near the trunk held about a third more organic carbon and a quarter more nitrogen than the open grassland, yet earthworm numbers were no higher. As such trees decline across Europe, it is important to understand what they contribute to the soil. The results were published in the journal Agroforestry Systems.
