'No chance!' - Poch and Mainoo fume at 'unfair' Yoro red card

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Manchester United were thumped 4-0 by Liverpool following Leny Yoro's red card after eight minutes; one of the considerations regarding a red card for Denying of an Obvious Goalscoring Opportunity (DOGS), is the likelihood of the attacking player "keeping or gaining control of the ball"

Mauricio Pochettino felt Manchester United were given an "unfair" red card in their 4-0 defeat at Liverpool - with Kobbie Mainoo replying "no chance" when asked whether Leny Yoro should have been sent off.

Pochettino's first United game started in dismal fashion as Yoro was given his marching orders in the eighth minute for bringing down Alexander Isak as the last defender outside the box.

Dominik Szoboszlai then scored from the resulting free-kick and Florian Wirtz doubled the lead after 13 minutes. Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz put gloss on the scoreline after half-time.

However, Yoro's sending off was doused in controversy as Isak took a heavy touch into United goalkeeper Senne Lammens before going down under the challenge.

Under the laws, one of the considerations regarding a red card for Denying of an Obvious Goalscoring Opportunity (DOGS), is the likelihood of the attacking player "keeping or gaining control of the ball".

Pochettino felt that not only did Isak lose control of the ball with his touch, but also that United should have been given a penalty for Jeremy Jacquet's challenge on Bryan Mbeumo at the opposite end just moments before.

"So painful. So disappointed," Pochettino told Sky Sports. "It's not ok to start a game in the Premier League with this situation.

"The game changed completely after six minutes. Now we can explain everything but the damage is done, it's so painful.

"The players are so, so feeling the pain of the result. I told them to be calm: it was an unfair situation that happened. We need to accept that reality. We need to try to increase our focus, concentration and make better decisions on the field.

"We also need to be critical on ourselves but some situations happened in the game that we are not happy."

Pushed on whether he was unhappy with Isak's heavy touch, the United boss replied: "The ball was for the goalkeeper.

"In that situation, if you review the VAR you can see for sure, [Yoro] never had the intention to connect with Isak. When the control was long with the striker, the ball was going to the goalkeeper, he runs and goes down.

But then Pochettino added: "If you look before, on the sixth minute. Jacquet on Mbeumo. Similar? No. If you compare the actions, that was a penalty.

"How that changes life, if you score after six minutes. Now, for sure, we are talking in a different way. We have to take responsibility, I take the responsibility. We keep pushing."

Pochettino was seen speaking to the officials at full-time and when asked on that, the United boss said: "It's not going to change what happened. Being respectful is the most important.

"At the same time, I have to defend my club. And tell what I feel. That was an action that everyone who plays football, it was clear that a situation that was not a red card."

United midfielder Mainoo was equally angry at the call. "The game's been taken out of our hands by a red card that I don't think can be a red card," he said.

"No chance. The ball's already with the goalkeeper. They're both running full speed. What can we do? It is what it is.

"It's 10 vs 11, you come to a place like Anfield, it's a tough game anyway. Then you lose a man, it makes it hard. I can't fault the boys for their fight in the game. We take the pain and move on.

"I wouldn't say it all goes out the window. You can still take the things you did in training into the game, but it makes it 10 times harder with one less man in a situation like that.

"It's hard, it's painful. But no one's coming to save us. We have to work hard, keep digging, keep working, keep fighting. It will all come round."

There was comical defending for the Yoro red-card incident, with United defender Matthijs de Ligt - playing his first Premier League minutes of the season - letting a long ball drop that allowed Isak to run through.

That then led to the Yoro 'foul' - but Roy Keane felt it was unfair on United, even despite their poor defending.

"Isak's not getting the ball," he said. "There's no way he's getting the ball.

"It's clumsy from United. Obviously it's one straight ball over the top. You shouldn't be in so much trouble.

"But there's no way he's getting that ball. Absolutely not. I know Isak is quick and he's sharp, but we've looked at it again.

"It's really poor defending. Players not smelling danger. But Isak is not getting that ball. Absolutely not."

Pochettino defended United's defending - as they became the worst defence in Europe when it comes to goals conceded across all competitions this season.

"I wasn't in the past," said Pochettino, referring to United's defensive record under his predecessor Michael Carrick.

"I take responsibility for today, and today we concede four goals with 10 men, and in a circumstance after playing 80 minutes on Anfield with 10 men.

"Of course I take responsibility about that, no worries, but I don't want to compare situations in the past."

When put to him that United did not have to go and concede four goals after the sending off, he replied: "It's so easy to talk in this way.

"It's about the team, always it's about balance. It's not about one line, the defensive line, the midfield, the offensive. I think when the team concede it is because the balance is not so good, and that I take responsibility about that.

"It's still a lot of games to play. We are in the reality and we need to accept the reality and we need to be responsible and we need to be united, we need to be all together. And I think we feel disappointed because it's clear. It's clear that after eight minutes, it happened what happened, and I think all that the plan was in the bin.

"And what I learn from my players is that they like to fight, they are committed with the team, with the club, and of course they want to win games, they want to make the effort, and I think today we made an unbelievable effort.

"I think today was a test. That for me is very positive. After eight minutes, 10 men, red card, concede the goal in this action, Szoboszlai that scored from a free-kick, and 80 minutes to play, more than 80 minutes because I think, you know, the injury time and everything. Do you think that is easy?

"Even the best team in the world playing like this in this situation, in our circumstance, maybe concede the four goals or maybe more. And now what we are going to say? We are in a situation that this is very complicated, it's difficult, yes, but I think we fight, we made the effort."

One of Pochettino's main decisions was to start De Ligt for the first time in the Premier League in 357 days.

When asked about that, the Argentine said of De Ligt: "He was training, he played in the Champions League before the international duty. I think when we arrived, we saw the combination [with Lisandro Martinez].

"And I repeat again, Jacquet was a penalty, and if the referee says that is a penalty, for sure that action after never comes, and maybe we talk in another way.

"But, you know, one centre-back made a mistake, was penalty, wasn't punished, and us, we made one mistake and we were punished. And now I accept all the situation, but the circumstance are the circumstance.

"It's so important to put the circumstance in front. Always."
