Barrow remove themselves from Super League consideration

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Barrow Raiders will not be the 15th Super League team in 2027 after the club removed themselves from the consideration process; Raiders cited concerns around venue expansion and a lack of centrally distributed funding, with no place in the league guaranteed beyond the 2027 season

Championship club Barrow Raiders have removed themselves from consideration for a Super League place after the competition announced an expansion to 15 teams for the 2027 season.

Barrow were a contender for inclusion, with the Raiders ranked 15th - the highest outside of the Super League clubs - in the IMG gradings, which aim to dictate promotion and relegation via data of surrounding clubs' financial sustainability.

However, the club themselves elected against pursuing a Super League place despite being contacted about the opportunity to join, citing concerns around venue expansion and a lack of centrally distributed funding.

"As the 15th-ranked club, the contact [from the Super League in relation to the 15th place] was very much a courtesy call, with the RFL keen to hear first-hand the club's position following comments in the press suggesting that Barrow Raiders were not ready for Super League," the club's statement read.

"For clarity, Barrow Raiders were not offered a place in Super League. However, in discussions about what would be required for the club to be considered, it was acknowledged that:

"Having considered these circumstances, the board of Barrow Raiders felt that we could not realistically put ourselves forward for consideration."

Barrow also denied receiving compensation for stepping away from the consideration process, and offered their best wishes to fellow Championship side London Broncos, who sit 16th in the IMG gradings and are understood to be the prime contenders for the extra Super League place.

"With the season fast approaching and it now being November, we felt it was in the best interests of the game to step aside promptly, allowing the process of identifying the 15th team to be resolved without further delay," the statement continued.

"We would also like to make it clear that Barrow Raiders were not offered any form of compensation in relation to stepping aside.

"We fully understand and support the importance of a strong geographic spread for Super League and, should London Broncos ultimately take the 15th place, we wish them every success.

"We sincerely hope they can make rugby league in the capital work and build a sustainable future for the sport in London."
