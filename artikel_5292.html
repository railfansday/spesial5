Kevin O'Leary Reveals What's Really Behind Crypto Market Crash

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Renowned investor and Shark Tank personality Kevin O’Leary has shared his thoughts on the current cryptocurrency crash, stating that the crypto industry faces issues that extend far beyond Bitcoin's dropping price.

In particular, Mr. Wonderful, as O'Leary is better known, believes that one of the biggest issues the crypto industry faces is the lack of regulatory clarity in the United States. This prevents big financial firms from adopting blockchain and stablecoin technologies.

According to O'Leary, institutional crypto adoption has slowed to a crawl. Companies are unwilling to invest any capital until there is clarity around regulations.

"It's not just about Bitcoin in the crypto sell-off. The adoption of stablecoins and blockchain within institutions has slowed down. Companies are waiting for clarity on regulations," O'Leary wrote.

The billionaire investor shared that his company already utilizes Circle's USDC for treasury management and international settlements. However, many other companies are afraid to follow suit since their compliance teams aren't sure about the regulatory landscape.

"We use USDC for treasury management and cross-border settlement. But many others won't do it till their compliance guys know the rules," O'Leary added.

The famous investor focused on two major U.S. bills regarding cryptocurrency: the GENIUS Act and the CLARITY Act.

"We got the GENIUS Act going. We need the CLARITY Act for the rest," Kevin O'Leary concluded.

O'Leary has long raised concerns about the crypto space and the reasons why Bitcoin is not seeing more institutional investment yet.

For example, on Oct. 28, the billionaire investor named two main issues affecting Bitcoin. The first was regulatory uncertainty, and the second was the rising threat of quantum computing.
