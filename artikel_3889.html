Forest Escape: Last Train coming to PS5, new location revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Publisher Two Cakes Studio and developer Frag Lab will release a PlayStation 5 version of cooperative surreal horror game Forest Escape: Last Train, the companies announced. A release date was not announced.

Forest Escape: Last Train first launched in Early Access for PC via Steam on July 10.

In addition to the PlayStation 5 version, Frag Lab revealed the new location, “Rainbow.”

“Rainbow is a challenging new location made of several stages, with an insanely interesting new boss waiting at the end,” reads the press release. “The further players go, the more the Warden’s madness drains the joy and color from the world around them.”
