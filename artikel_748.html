Why Guitarricadelafuente Said Yes to La Bola Negra Without a Script

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Guitarricadelafuente explained the unconventional way he was cast in La Bola Negra and the emotions he felt writing the film’s original song “La Nieve.”

For Guitarricadelafuente, La Bola Negra was definitely his jam.

The Spanish singer-songwriter revealed how he went from making music to making movie magic in the drama, which is already an awards season frontrunner.

“The truth is that I said yes to this role a bit blindly because the script wasn't written yet,” Guitarricadelafuente told E! News in an exclusive interview, translated from Spanish, at the 2026 LACMA Art+Film Gala Nov. 7. “There was only the idea of the character of Sebastián and for me it was like a gift from heaven to be able to participate in this film.”

Plus, the 29-year-old (real name Álvaro Lafuente Calvo) had good reason to trust his instincts. After all, he was already a longtime fan of the film’s co-writers and co-directors Javier Calvo and Javier Ambrossi, known as “Los Javis.”

“They knew they could count on me without me having the story in mind—they already knew I could do it,” he shared. “It was something that made me immediately say yes.”

Acting wasn’t the only way Guitarricadelafuente got to flex his creative muscles. He also wrote the original song “La Nieve” for the movie, though he admitted he “didn’t know where to begin.”

Ultimately, he found inspiration in the film’s focus on poet Federico García Lorca.

“The truth is that it was a beautiful journey to be inspired by all that poetry of Lorca that is so present throughout the film,” he explained, “and something to pay homage to all those lives that could not be possible in some way.”

Bringing Sebastián to life helped him find his voice, too.

“It came out as if I were able to experience the character in my own flesh,” he added, “and for all of that to come out in the most honest way possible.”
