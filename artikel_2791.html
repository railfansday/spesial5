Saints vs. Bengals Prediction: Who Dat vs. Who Dey in the Jungle

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The 3-7 New Orleans Saints are back on the road this week as they travel to Paycor Stadium to face the 7-2 Cincinnati Bengals. New Orleans enters the matchup as a heavy underdog, but the Saints aren't ready to throw in the towel just yet. With seven games remaining in the regular season, New Orleans will look to defy the odds, tame the Bengals in their own jungle and come away with a much-needed victory.

It's a battle of Who Dat vs. Who Dey, and the Bengals will be a difficult opponent, especially on the offensive side of the ball. Led by former LSU standouts QB Joe Burrow and WR Ja'Marr Chase, Cincinnati has several weapons to put opposing defenses under pressure. Burrow is having a career season and is considered a heavy favorite for MVP, giving the Saints' defense an even greater challenge. For New Orleans, limiting explosive plays, generating pressure on Burrow and finding ways to disrupt the Bengals' dangerous passing attack will be critical to coming away with a victory.

Offensively, the Saints must establish some momentum and avoid falling into an early hole. Sustaining drives, protecting the football and capitalizing on scoring opportunities will be essential if they hope to keep pace.

Despite their struggles this season, the Saints still have opportunities to show improvement and build momentum especially as the division remains up for grabs. However, with each passing week, the margin for error continues to shrink.

The odds may be stacked against New Orleans, but anything can happen on any given Sunday. The question now is whether the Saints can rise to the challenge, cage the Bengals and escape Cincinnati with an upset victory.

Joe Burrow is having a career season, and unfortunately, I think the Saints are simply outmatched in this one. Cincinnati's explosive offense will be too much for New Orleans to contain, and while the Saints will put up a fight, I predict the Bengals will take care of business in their own jungle.
