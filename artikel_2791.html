Why Is Ladd McConkey not playing vs. Ravens? Latest news on Chargers WR

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Ladd McConkey will not play when the Los Angeles Chargers face the Baltimore Ravens on "Monday Night Football" in Week 10 of the 2026 NFL season.

The Chargers ruled out the wide receiver on Sunday after an injury prevented him from practicing throughout the week (h/t Noah Furtado of The Athletic). McConkey was initially listed as questionable but did not travel with the team to Baltimore.

Los Angeles Chargers wide receiver Ladd McConkey (15). Credit: Kirby Lee-Imagn Images via Reuters Connect (Reuters).

McConkey is sidelined with a back injury that kept him out of practice leading up to Week 10. The 25-year-old receiver was initially listed as questionable for Monday's game.

However, the Chargers ruled him out on Sunday, and announced he will not make the trip to Baltimore.

The back problem is a continuation of the issue that forced him to miss last Sunday's game against the Houston Texans. McConkey reportedly experienced back spasms during pregame warmups ahead of that game, eventually making him a late scratch.

Despite being considered day-to-day earlier in the week, McConkey did not practice Thursday, Friday or Saturday.

McConkey's next opportunity to return will come when the Chargers face the New York Jets on Sunday. However, the team has not confirmed his availability for that game.

McConkey's absence is another setback in a frustrating season that has seen him deal with several injury problems. The third-year receiver has recorded 17 receptions for 286 yards and one touchdown this season.

Without him against Baltimore, Quentin Johnston, Tre' Harris and KeAndre Lambert-Smith are among the receivers expected to shoulder more responsibility in the passing game. Johnston, in particular, could become Justin Herbert's primary receiving option.⁠
