Kings rumors: Tom Thibodeau, Mark Jackson on radar if Doug Christie finally gets fired

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Sacramento Kings are in the midst of a season of struggles, entering the week 1-12. Their rough start could lead to the firing of current head coach Doug Christie, and several notable coaching names are linked to the job if he is dismissed. According to Marc Stein's latest edition of the Stein Line, Tom Thibodeau and Mark Jackson are rumored to be potential candidates if the Kings look for their next coach.

"There are already rumbles in league coaching circles, meanwhile, that characterize both Tom Thibodeau and Mark Jackson as potential down-the-road candidates for the post," Stein wrote.

Stein also shared Christie's contract situation and a dire projection for the Kings' season if they stay on this pace.

"Sacramento, though, is on a 6-76 pace. Christie is on the final guaranteed year of his contract and has faced speculation about his job security dating to last season. This is not going away as a topic without a few wins. And soon."

Both Tom Thibodeau and Mark Jackson are notable names in the coaching space who found immense success on the sidelines. Jackson left the broadcast booth for ABC to become the head coach of an up-and-coming Golden State Warriors team that featured a young Klay Thompson and Stephen Curry. Under Jackson, they grew into the contender they eventually became, making the playoffs in two straight seasons and posting 51 wins in 2013-2014, the season before they won the NBA championship.

Meanwhile, Tom Thibodeau was most recently the head coach of the New York Knicks from 2020 to 2025, winning the NBA Coach of the Year in 2021. The Knicks saw immense success with Thibodeau at the helm, making the playoffs in four of his five seasons as head coach and reaching the conference finals in the 2024-2025 season. Following Thibodeau's dismissal from the team, the New York Knicks won the 2026 NBA championship, similar to the occurance with the Warriors following Jackson's dismissal.

The Kings will host the Phoenix Suns on Wednesday.
