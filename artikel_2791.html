Open Thread: Spurs air “The Blueprint” honoring Harrison Barnes leadership and dedication

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

I was fortunate enough to attend the Emirates Cup game between the San Antonio Spurs and the Golden State Warriors. Not only was thirty-seven point drubbing a sight to behold, the energy in the arena was palpable. But there is something to watching the games from the comfort of my own couch. One of those is the commentary, another is the replays, and finally, there are the features.

When I finally rewatched the game on DAZN, I enjoyed this video presentation in the first act of the halftime break:

"The Blueprint" is a three-minute tribute honoring the Spurs veteran Harrison Barnes. Barnes originally came to San Antonio in a three-team trade involving the Spurs, Sacramento Kings, and Chicago Bulls. The trade sent DeMar DeRozan to Sacramento and included RaiQuan Gray, Chris Duarte, and led to Charles Bassey being waived.

Since arriving in San Antonio, Barnes has taken on a leadership role and offered a solid veteran presence to the fledgling Spurs team. He is a 2015 NBA Champion through his time with the Golden State Warriors. On the court, he's known for his consistent 3-point shooting and high basketball IQ. In the locker room, he exhibits an example of maturity and patience that lifted the team's spirits as they put the pieces together.

Away from the game, he —along with his with Brittany— have time and again displayed grace and generosity in the community. Through multiple philanthropic endeavors, the Barnes have contributed to projects involving improvements to recreational locales, helping local students afford college tuition, and treating patrons to groceries during the holidays.

Written and edited by Liam Capwell, Spurs Associate Broadcast Producer, "The Blueprint" Capwell blends footage from Spurs media day with some of Barnes best performances while being narrated by Jacob Tobey, Spurs play-by-play announcer. Capwell comes to the Spurs after studying film at Texas State, a university with penchant for recommending talented students for Spurs internship opportunities.

Keep your eyes peeled for more content to air throughout the season.
