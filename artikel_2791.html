Could former Colts QB Daniel Jones be claimed today? Clear waivers? The Cardinals are said to be in the mix

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Indianapolis Colts shocked everybody in the NFL when they outright released their starting quarterback Daniel Jones after a lackluster performance against the Houston Texans. Jones had signed a two-year $88 million extension in the spring of this year. The contract was the largest two-year deal in NFL history.

Jones, age 29, will find out on Monday at 4 p.m. ET if he clears waivers. Any team that claims him will have to continue his contract. If he clears waivers, any club can then sign him to the NFL veteran minimum, similar to what occurred with former Cardinals QB Kyler Murray when he inked a similar deal with the Minnesota Vikings.

It is estimated that Jones might wait to sign with another team, according to ESPN. He may want to get the right opportunity with the right team instead of just signing with any club in order to get back on the field immediately.

Teams that have interest include the Baltimore Ravens, New England Patriots, and Arizona. Jones is expected to have options. Apparently, the Colts did not want to keep him in a state of flux once they decided not to play him anymore this season. If the Cardinals really want Jones, they must put in a claim for him, which currently own the #7 waiver claim spot. But his huge contract will accompany him.

If he clears waivers, the Colts will have to pay all of the remaining salary due to him. What has been the big mystery is why Indy didn't trade him before the NFL trade deadline and gotten something for him instead of just releasing him outright and receiving nothing in return. Jones had trade value and several NFL clubs were seeking an experienced signalcaller.

Jones (6'-5", 230 pounds) led the league this year in interceptions with 13, so there's that. But he has plenty of experience during his eight seasons in the league. This season, he was named the starter for Week 1. Jones started all 11 games for a listless offensive unit which has been selling off their key players. Their offensive line is absolutely porous. This year, Jones hasn't had any time to throw and has paltry production such as 144 (twice) yards, 108 (twice), 143, and 166 in games. He has thrown seven touchdowns against 13 interceptions.

Jones was taken with the sixth overall selection in the 2019 NFL Draft by the New York Football Giants after a standout career at Duke. He was supposed to be the future after Eli Manning retired. In 2022, Jones made NFL history by becoming the first player ever with 300+ passing yards, 2+ touchdown passes, and 70+ rushing yards in a playoff game.

For his eight-year career, Jones' stats: 94 games with 93 starts, 1,903 completions on 2,976 attempts for 19,657 yards, converted 979 first downs, 96 TDs, 68 picks, a 63.9% completion ratio, sacked 257 times, an 84.1 QB rating, and a 35-57-1 starting win-loss record. He is a very good runner with 478 carries for 2,441 yards with another 21 scores.

By claiming Jones, it could mean the Cardinals would have a capable quarterback for their remaining games this season who would compete with Gardner Minshew and Carson Beck in training camp next year.
