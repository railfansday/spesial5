Aaron Glenn expects Garrett Wilson to clear concussion protocol, play vs. Bills

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

It sounds like Jets receiver Garrett Wilson is on track to return to action on Sunday.

Head coach Aaron Glenn said that Wilson has just one more step of concussion protocol to clear and he’s confident the wideout will do so.

Wilson missed last week’s game while in the protocol. He has caught 43 passes for 527 yards with three touchdowns so far this year.

Glenn noted that everyone else aside from linebacker Trevin Wallace (groin) is expected to practice in full on Friday.

That includes rookie receiver Omar Cooper, who has been listed as limited on the first two injury reports of the week with an ankle issue.

New York’s final injury report of the week with game statuses is due out later on Friday.
