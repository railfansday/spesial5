Ravens trade for OL Ryan Neuzil, send OL Andrew Vorhees to Falcons

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Falcons and Ravens executed one last trade before the 4 p.m. ET deadline.

Baltimore has acquired offensive lineman Ryan Neuzil in exchange for offensive lineman Andrew Vorhees and a 2028 sixth-round pick.

Neuzil is in the final year of his contract. He’s primarily played center for Atlanta, which could signal a move back to guard for Cam Jurgens, who the Ravens also recently acquired.

Baltimore has been ailing along the interior of its offensive line all season.

Neuzil has appeared in 69 career games with 38 starts, all for Atlanta.

Vorhees has appeared in 37 career games with 22 starts, including five appearances with two starts in 2026.
