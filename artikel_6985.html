Internal IT Manager at Clutch

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Internal IT Manager owns the Service Desk and the Corporate Systems that run the business, and builds the AI driven workflows that connect them. You run the team and you also build: you design the automations, ship the integrations, and show the team how it is done. You set priorities, coach each person, and build alongside them.

You lead a small, hands on team that keeps the company running and builds toward AI Native. The team shares one queue, reviews metrics, and brings automation ideas to the company.

ServiceDesk and ticketing• Run the ServiceDesk across onboarding, offboarding, access requests, hardware and software support.• Own the ticketing platform: queues, categories, SLAs, escalation paths, and reporting.• Cut ticket volume through self service, knowledge base content, and AI assisted triage and resolution.Asset management• Maintain one accurate inventory of hardware, software licenses, and SaaS subscriptions.• Manage the full asset lifecycle: procurement, provisioning, tracking, refresh, and secure disposal.• Reconcile licenses against real usage to remove waste before each renewal.Corporate systems• Administer the core business stack: identity and SSO (Okta), device management (Iru), collaboration tools, and the systems used by People, Sales, Legal, and Finance.• Keep systems secure, documented, and available, with clear owners and change control.• Evaluate new tools and retire those that no longer earn their cost.Corporate contracts and vendors• Manage IT vendor relationships, renewals, and negotiations.• Maintain a contract register with owners, terms, renewal dates, and spend.• Work with Legal and Finance on reviews, security questionnaires, and budget forecasts.

AI and enterprise workflowsYou treat AI as a core tool for running IT, and you build with it yourself.• Design and ship automated workflows for onboarding, offboarding, access provisioning, approvals, and renewals.• Integrate systems through APIs, webhooks, and iPaaS tools so data moves between ticketing, identity, HR, CRM, finance, and contract platforms without manual steps.• Deploy AI agents and assistants for ticket triage, knowledge search, request fulfillment, and employee support.• Write scripts and light code (Python, JavaScript, PowerShell) when a ready made tool falls short.• Set guardrails for AI use: data handling, access scope, review steps, and audit trails, agreed with Legal and Security.• Measure each automation by hours saved, errors avoided, and adoption, then improve or retire it.

Please note that this role may evolve as our business needs change, so we appreciate your flexibility and adaptability.

Remote Flexibility: Enjoy the freedom of remote work from anywhere, balancing life and career seamlessly.

Paid Time Off and National Holidays: Enjoy 20 PTO days yearly and the National Holidays for relaxation and rejuvenation.

Stock Options: Joining us means having a stake in our success, so you'll receive stock options as part of your compensation package.

Home Office Setup: Create your ideal workspace with a dedicated budget for home office essentials.

Work Trip Budget: Grow personally and professionally with a budget for work-related trips and co-working.

As a champion of financial well-being, we address the urgent need for affordable lending solutions in an era where the average American grapples with over $155,000 in household debt.  Unlike traditional financial institutions, Clutch develops software to turn Credit Unions into FinTech lenders and leverage their balance sheets to responsibly lend to over 130M Americans. Our mission extends beyond mere financial transactions; we strive to fundamentally enhance the way credit unions interact with their members. By integrating cutting-edge technologies and user-centric designs, we help credit unions provide seamless digital experiences that are on par with leading tech companies. This approach not only preserves but revitalizes the longstanding tradition of community and member-focused service inherent to credit unions.
