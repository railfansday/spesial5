All the Freestyle Routines and Songs in the Packed 3-Hour ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Finale

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Dancing With the Stars airs its finale next Tuesday, and it’s going to be an absolutely packed night. The five remaining couples competing for the Mirrorball Trophy are Tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik, Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach, Amber Glenn and Pasha Pashkov, Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas, and Connor Wood and Rylee Arnold—and each of them will perform three (3!!!) times during the finale.

On top of a Freestyle performance of their choice, the couples will be doing a Judges’ Choice routine, as well as an Instant Dance—which they have to choreograph and perform on the fly. The Instant Dances are (obviously) announced night-of, but DWTS officially dropped the lineup for the freestyles and Judges’ Choice dances:

Freestyle: “Singin’ in the Rain” by Gene Kelly and “Umbrella” by Rihanna

Judges’ Choice: Viennese Waltz to “Willing and Able” by Noah Kahan

Freestyle: “Like a Prayer” and “Music” by Madonna

Judges’ Choice: Quickstep to “Sing Sing Sing” by Louis Prima

Freestyle: “Diamonds Are a Girl’s Best Friend” by Marilyn Monroe and the Irish folk songs “The Parting Glass” and “Flowers of the Red Hill”

Judges’ Choice: Tango to “Gallo Ciego” by Orquesta Color Tango

Freestyle: “Minnie the Moocher” by Cab Calloway and “Over the Rainbow” by Judy Garland

Judges’ Choice: Salsa to “Jamaican (Bam Bam)” by Hugel and Solto (FR)

Judges’ Choice: Salsa to “Danza Kuduro” by Don Omar feat. Lucenzo
