Oliver Glasner 'will get himself sacked' in 'shock' Nottingham Forest twist - Finance guru

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Evangelos Marinakis has a "decision to make" already on Oliver Glasner's future as Nottingham Forest manager.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that the former Crystal Palace boss might be talking his way into a sacking.

Pressure is building at the City Ground just 10 games into the new boss' tenure, with Manchester City up next on Saturday, 7 November.

The Reds are currently one point from safety, but given they're sitting 19th in the Premier League table, something needs to change quickly.

Glasner has already laid down the gauntlet for his players, suggesting they either buy in to his tactics or leave. Those comments have been met by a mixed reaction.

Forest will be hoping to end a barren run of five games without a win, but a challenge doesn't come much more difficult than Enzo Maresca's Man City.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson has suggested that the Man City clash could already be Glasner's final game in charge this season.

"Some of the comments that he made about his players, those are the sorts of things that start to take you down the road towards getting yourself sacked," he said.

"And maybe if they got heavily beaten at the weekend and have six points from 10 games going into an international break, that does give Maranakis a decision to make.

"It wouldn't be the biggest shock in the world. But I don't think he's going to spend what could easily be millions to remove Glasner after six months."

Glasner certainly isn't the favourite Premier League manager to be sacked next, with Michael Carrick, Pierre Sage and Regis Le Bris all ahead of him.

But with Marinakis' trigger finger when it comes to managers, anything is possible.

Glasner was a highly sought-after manager during the 2025-26 campaign, and no wonder, given his achievements at Selhurst Park.

Forest won the race for his signature, but there are likely to have included a huge release clause in the contract to entice him to the City Ground.

"It could easily be £5m to remove Glasner after six months. And that's a bold move to give somebody £5m so quickly," Borson told Football Insider.

It wouldn't be Marinakis' strangest-ever decision, though, and that's why there are already so many concerns over Glasner's future.
