Manu Koné: Roma slap £86m price tag on Chelsea target

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Shumail Sajid writes about Manchester United for Read Man Utd and Crystal Palace for Read Crystal Palace, with occasional pieces on other Premier League clubs. He has also written for FanSided's Red Devils Dispatch, Yardbarker, Anfield Central and The Sports Side.

Roma have put an £86m (€100m) price tag on Manu Koné, according to Gazzetta dello Sport, reviving the Manu Koné Chelsea transfer link that first emerged over the summer. Roma want any interested club, including Chelsea, to know the price before serious talks can even begin.

Koné remains under contract at the Stadio Olimpico, and he has become a key part of Roma’s midfield since his move from Borussia Mönchengladbach. The Gazzetta report reads like a warning to potential buyers rather than proof of any talks between the two clubs.

Roma sporting director Tony D’Amico has confirmed Chelsea made contact late in the summer window, though he was quick to draw a clear line under how far things went.

“There was an inquiry and some preliminary contact with Chelsea on the final day of the transfer window. No official offers arrived that would allow us to approach the player. Something was imagined that simply didn’t happen. There was no official offer; the final day of the transfer window got everyone a little worked up.”

D’Amico’s comments confirm Chelsea looked at signing Koné before the deadline, but they stop well short of showing an official bid was ever made. He also explained why Roma had no wish to sell a key player so close to the window closing.

“Before the match against Fiorentina (on 24 August), I said it would be difficult for us to deprive ourselves of important players so near to the end of the transfer session, and so it proved. Kone is an important player, we’re happy he stayed with us, and we’re confident he’ll give his contribution during the season.”

Chelsea’s midfield has changed shape under Xabi Alonso since the summer, with Morgan Rogers arriving to add depth and competition for places. That signing came during a window that also saw exits acceleration under Xabi Alonso, as the head coach reshaped his squad around his own style.

Koné’s ball winning and his range of progressive passing would suit Alonso well, should Chelsea ever revisit their interest. Supporters have already seen how Alonso rotates his midfield for the biggest games, including the recent derby line-up selections against Brentford.

For now, Rogers and Chelsea’s existing central options give Alonso enough cover without needing an immediate signing. Roma’s reluctance to sell also means any deal would need patience rather than a quick resolution.

Nothing in D’Amico’s comments suggests Roma are open to a sale in the near term, and Koné has given no sign that he wants to leave. The £86m valuation, reported by Football London, stands as Roma’s marker rather than an asking price involved with Chelsea.

Will Chelsea return with a more formal approach in a future window? D’Amico’s comments give no hint either way. The Manu Koné Chelsea transfer link is unlikely to fade given Chelsea’s history of scouting France’s leading midfielders, but any fresh move will have to wait for Roma’s stance to soften.
