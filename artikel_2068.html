League, Trophy, Senior Cup – in-form Eastbourne Borough just have to keep rolling

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

As winter approaches, Borough's schedule in the coming weeks is piling up nicely - or even alarmingly. Isthmian Premier fixtures including rearranged dates - the Sussex Senior Cup, and an early December trip to Oxford City in the third round of the FA Trophy.

How do clubs, players and managers shuffle the diary, juggle the commitments, and stay both physically fit and mentally focused? Not that anyone wants any weather disruption as winter approaches - but it's rather like being the adult in the middle of your children's snowball fight, and the cold wet missiles are coming at you from every angle.

"We just keep going!" said boss Steve King. "Is that seventeen out of nineteen? I lose count! We get them back in the dressing room and we just count the bodies, see who we have available match by match - and we go again!

"The trip to Enfield Town (on Saturday) is massive. There are players who possibly could come back, after Tuesday night (the victory over Littlehampton Town). We'll take a look at it."

Tired bodies? Aches and strains? "The lads just want to keep going! There's a massive togetherness, a determination. You can have all the talent in the world, but application is the crucial thing! It's game by game, and we'll just keep going!"

The Sports head for North London tomorrow (Saturday) to take on Isthmian Premier pacesetters Enfield Town. A six-pointer? The Towners - relegated with Borough last season - are a tough proposition, especially at their distinctive home stadium - a favourite of groundhoppers, with its ocean-liner main stand, grass pitch and open, running-track arena.

And then, as sure as Tuesday follows Saturday, Borough will welcome Aveley for the next midweek encounter. The Essex club snatched victory away from King's team in the first match of the season, equalising in stoppage time - but Borough have been on an upward trajectory ever since, and King will be looking for a very minimum of four points from the coming pair of fixtures.

A sweeping Tuesday night victory over Littlehampton Town has seen Borough into the last eight of the Sussex Transport Senior Cup - where intriguingly, Eastbourne United have also secured their place with a notable victory at Bognor Regis Town.

What price a local derby next up? The quarter-final draw also includes most of the Sussex big-hitters: Lewes, Horsham and Chichester City among them. Two ties remain - Wick versus Worthing, and Hastings United's trip to Whitehawk - before Borough learn their next opponents.

After Tuesday's 6-1 win over Littlehampton, Steve King's verdict was measured. "It was alright! In the first half we had chances but we weren't clinical. We weren't happy with the goal that we conceded - showing the opponent inside rather than the outside. But the second half was much more fitting of us and we scored some cracking goals.

"Solid at the back, and we came good in the end. I was really pleased for Owen Hargreaves, on the scoresheet with a brilliant goal. He works so hard week by week, and it was just reward. Owen is a great example of a player who is learning and improving all the time. He's in the team alongside quality players and players with experience, and that's bringing the best out of him.

"Tyano (Wilson) is a similar example, great natural ability, learning all the time - when to pass, when to cross, when to fill holes - the decision-making.

"Now after two cup competitions, it's back to the league at Enfield. Second versus third, but it's still only three points like any other game, and it won't define the season. Enfield are a very good side, and they're up there on merit. But so are we!"
