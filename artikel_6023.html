It Takes a Family

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The answers aren’t political and institutional; they are deeply relational.

John Mulaney in Saturday Night Live’s “Sister” sketch

The recent Saturday Night Live “Sister” skit hit a nerve with its fake ad for a 24-hour elder-care service. The service is “completely free,” provided you’re the brother.

“My dad means the world to me,” it begins, with comedian John Mulaney narrating. “So, when I noticed him start to slow down, I knew I needed to get him the best care possible. I didn’t want to put him in a nursing home, but a private health aide could cost thousands a week. I didn’t know where to turn.”
