Key midterm election results

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Heading into Election Day, the fight for both the Senate and House had expanded into traditionally Republican territory. Inside Elections with Nathan L. Gonzales rated 16 Senate races and 82 House races as competitive.

CQ Roll Call is tracking the results in these battleground races, as they are called by The Associated Press.

Georgia Senate: Democratic Sen. Jon Ossoff defeats Republican Rep. Mike Collins [Democratic hold]

Florida’s 25th District: Democratic Rep. Jared Moskowitz defeats Republican Scott Singer, the former mayor of Boca Raton [Democratic hold]

Florida’s 14th District: Democratic Rep. Kathy Castor defeats Republican former state Rep. Mike Beltran [Democratic hold]

New Hampshire Senate: Democratic Rep. Chris Pappas defeats Republican former Sen. John E. Sununu [Democratic hold]

North Carolina Senate (open): Democratic former Gov. Roy Cooper defeats former Republican National Committee Chairman Michael Whatley [Democratic flip]

Florida Senate: GOP Sen. Ashley Moody defeats Democratic former state Rep. Angie Nixon to complete the unfinished term of Marco Rubio [Republican hold]

Florida’s 16th District (open): Republican Sydney Gruters, the wife of RNC Chairman Joe Gruters, defeats Democratic Mayor Kelly Kirschner, the former mayor of Sarasota [Republican hold]

North Carolina’s 11th District (open): Democrat Jamie Ager defeats Republican state Rep. Jennifer Balkcom [Democratic flip]
