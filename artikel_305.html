As with other Sonos speakers, it has Trueplay technology, which scans the room and adjusts the audio to perfectly match the space and acoustics. That means optimal sound no matter where you take this speaker. It plays directly from multiple streaming platforms, such as Spotify, or you can connect to a turntable with a line-in. Now marked down to $149 for Black Friday, this deal will save you $40.

The Sonos Era 300 Wireless Smart Speaker is a step up in terms of quality and specs. It has Trueplay technology and supports Dolby Atmos, which bounces sound around the room for a fully immersive experience, thanks to a combination of six speaker drivers. It also throws in Alexa and voice control, or you can control everything via an app.

It combines Trueplay and Dolby Atmos with Sound Motion technology to fill the room. This clever speaker also uses AI to enhance the dialogue in whatever you're watching, ensuring you never miss a single word. It's the ultimate soundbar for the home cinema experience. Best of all, $849 is its lowest price ever on Amazon, which should be a dealbreaker if you've been considering buying.
