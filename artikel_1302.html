Man City: Lawyers Rate Chances of Successful Appeal Against 114 Charges

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Manchester City are facing an anxious wait to discover the outcome of their appeal after being found guilty on 114 financial charges.

The Citizens were accused of artificially inflating sponsorship deals using 'sham' contracts and other methods, with the Premier League's investigation starting in 2023.

In September 2026, the club were found guilty on 114 charges before launching an appeal shortly after. The outcome of that process is still undecided as the legal battle continues.

A report from the Financial Times has claimed that City's hearing is set to take place in December and could take place over several days.

As a result, the outcome is expected in the New Year, with sanctions handed out at the same time if Man City fail to overturn the guilty verdict. David Kogan, the head of the Independent Football Regulator, backed up that information.

Now, football financial expert Nick Harris has shed light on what legal experts think will happen. He has covered the entire case and has spoken with several lawyers throughout.

Manchester City's transfer plans are already being affected by their guilty verdict.

From those he's had conversations with, Harris reckons one in four think City will win parts of the appeal. However, even fewer are of the belief that their wins will be big enough.

He tweeted: "From multiple lawyers I’ve spoken to, perhaps 25% think City will win the appeal or parts of the appeal, but fewer think City will win substantive points. ie: will still lose.

"Context: on the morning of the 2016 US presidential election, Trump had a 26- 28% chance of winning (max). But he won."

If the guilty verdict sticks, attention will quickly turn to the players. Who will remain loyal to the club as they battle through whichever sanctions they are hit with?

Well, Harris revealed that Erling Haaland, City's main man, could be on his way to Real Madrid if the verdict isn't overturned. He posted on X:

"Florentino Perez is increasingly confident Erling Haaland will be a Real Madrid player next season, according to a contact who had lunch with Pérez recently. Meanwhile, multiple MCFC players' agents are exploring how their players might leave on free transfers if all City appeals fail. IF."

Haaland himself was asked about the charges during the international break. After playing for Norway, he said: "In 2009, I was playing for Bryne under-nine boys.

"This is a question I struggle to answer. It is a matter between the Premier League and Manchester City that is going to be dealt with by a panel. Am I supposed to answer that? I'm not really sure.

"It's a difficult question to answer when I don't really know that much about it. I assume the answers will come in the next ... I don't know when the answers will come. I haven't read that much about it."
