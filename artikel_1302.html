Report Juventus want Woltemade stay, offer Conceicao to Newcastle United

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Nick Woltemade only joined Juventus on loan from Newcastle United, but it’s reported they want to make it permanent, selling Francisco Conceicao to raise the necessary funds.

The German centre-forward was a very late signing to Serie A, arriving on deadline day on a 12-month loan for €3.6m, and not including any option to make it permanent.

He has scored four goals with two assists in 13 appearances between Serie A and the Europa League, working well on his own, or in a partnership with Randal Kolo Muani.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, the 24-year-old has impressed Juventus directors enough that they are hatching a plan to buy him outright at the end of the season.

That will require some strong negotiation, considering Newcastle United paid a massive €75m to buy him from VfB Stuttgart in the summer of 2025.

He did not live up to those expectations, finding the net 11 times with six assists in 53 games for the Premier League club.

It would be a significant expense for Juve, who are hoping to ask for an extended loan and option to buy in June 2028 for a lower fee.

They can also raise funds by selling Francisco Conceicao, who would generate a profit for anything over €40m.

It could even be possible to send the Portugal international to Newcastle United as part of any potential swap deal with Woltemade.
