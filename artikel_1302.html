15 Best Strikers in the Premier League Ranked (2026)

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Scoring goals is one of the hardest things in football, but it's also one of the most important. At the end of the day, goals win games, and finding the back of the net in the Premier League is particularly difficult, given that strikers are often up against some of the best defenders in the world.

Over the years, though, there have been some brilliant centre-forwards in the English top flight. Alan Shearer, Luis Suarez, and Wayne Rooney are just some of the names that come to mind when thinking about the greatest marksmen in the game.

Today, the league is blessed to have arguably the best goalscorer in the world in the form of Erling Haaland playing in the Premier League. But others, such as Joao Pedro and Alexander Isak, have proven that they are clinical in front of goal too. So, who are the 15 finest forwards in the Premier League right now?

For a player to be included, they must be listed as a centre-forward as their main position on Transfermarkt. Therefore, players such as Bryan Mbeumo miss out.

An opportunist of the highest order when the ball is in the box, Evanilson is actually better known for his work off it. An aggressive presser, and someone who has the tank to get involved in tracking back, the Bournemouth man is as selfless as modern-day strikers come - a perfect indication that the traditional marksman is a dying breed.

This season, he already has four goals and four assists in nine Premier League matches, and that's without his strike partner (more on him later) really being fit yet. Evanilson has also proven his quality in Europe, netting twice in the Cherries' first four Europa League outings.

Evanilson's 2026/27 Premier League Statistics

Can you name every player to have won the Premier League Golden Boot?

Nobody had a Dominic Calvert-Lewin resurgence on their bingo cards in 2025/26. Having departed Everton under a cloud of injury woes, the England international has found excellent form at Leeds United.

The centre-forward has regularly netted Premier League goals over the past 12 months, with his fine form massively benefiting Daniel Farke's side. He really is a menace inside the penalty area, using his big frame to hold the ball up and dominate opposition defenders in the air. His manager even thinks he's among the best in the world, with Farke claiming last February:

"Haaland is the best striker in the world. The only one who comes close to him is Dominic Calvert-Lewin."

Dominic Calvert-Lewin's 2026/27 Premier League Statistics

Brian Brobbey is a powerful, physical striker who relies on his strength, hold-up play, and speed to lead the line. But that's putting it very lightly for the Black Cats' ultimate enabler, who's like a freight train once he gets moving.

He has scored three goals this season, and has looked to be an absolute nightmare for defenders - something Arsenal's Ezri Konsa recently found out as Sunderland pushed the title holders to the wire. Those around Brobbey often get the plaudits, but without his presence in attack, the North East side would not get nearly as much joy at the top end of the pitch.

Brian Brobbey's 2025/26 Premier League Statistics

Manchester United decided to overhaul their attack in the summer of 2025, with Benjamin Sesko arriving from RB Leipzig in a deal worth £73.7m. The Slovenian had a slow start to life at Old Trafford as he struggled to make an impression with Ruben Amorim at the helm.

A managerial change, which saw Michael Carrick replace Amorim, has brought about a turnaround in fortunes for the colossal forward. Sesko scored just twice for United in 2025, but he's been on fire in front of goal since the turn of the year, hitting the back of the net on 10 occasions at the time of writing. Still just 23 years old, Sesko will surely get better and better in a red shirt, although a shin injury will keep him sidelined until the new year.

Benjamin Sesko's 2026/27 Premier League Statistics

Thierno Barry is a fascinating prospect. Still only 24, he was initially a bit of a joke figure at Everton, known for missing chance after chance, no matter how simple the opportunity seemed to be.

However, there is something to be said for a striker who possesses an uncanny knack of always being in the right position. As time has gone on, Barry has finally started to make some of those chances count. While he still misses his fair share, his raw potential is undeniable and has rocketed him to the top of the Premier League goalscoring charts in 2026/27, with six goals to his name (and 10 in all competitions) – only Erling Haaland has more.

Thierno Barry's 2026/27 Premier League Statistics

Matheus Cunha's quality at Wolves was evident, as he shone in a squad which lacked similar talent. Having previously flopped at Atletico Madrid, the Brazilian was handed another chance to shine at a big club when Manchester United made their move in the summer of 2025.

A total of 10 Premier League goals and four assists was a solid return in his first campaign, but Cunha can do better. Sadly, the environment around him isn't one that does well to platform strikers. Indeed, many forwards have flopped at Old Trafford in recent times, but it's a testament to the Brazilian​​​​'s quality that he stands a good chance of bucking the trend.

Matheus Cunha 2026/27 Premier League Statistics

Since the days of Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang finding the back of the net with ease, Arsenal have lacked a reliable, out-and-out striker. Gabriel Jesus is among those to have been tried in the solitary centre-forward berth, but the Emirates Stadium faithful really thought they had landed their long-term striker upon signing Viktor Gyokeres from Sporting CP.

A goalscoring revelation in Portugal, having plundered 54 goals across all competitions in the season that alerted the Gunners to his availability, the Swede joined the Gunners with plenty of promise – but his goal return has proven the chasm in quality between the Primeira Liga and the Premier League. Gyokeres struck 14 times in 36 league appearances as Arsenal won their first title since 2004. A reasonable tally, but his lack of technical quality has seen him lose his spot early into the 26/27 campaign.

Viktor Gyokeres' 2026/27 Premier League Statistics

Nicolas Jackson is one of those strange strikers who fluctuates from the sublime to the ridiculous. At his best, he can decide games with his pure ability. At his worst, he can miss every chance that comes his way.

He frustrated fans at Chelsea and was shipped out on loan to Bayern Munich for the season. Having paled in compraison to Harry Kane in Germany, he returned to the Premier League as Aston Villa chanced their arm on the striker. Under Unai Emery, things are really starting to click for Jackson, who looks to have found the perfect environment to cultivate his undoubted talent.

Nicolas Jackson's 2026/27 Premier League Statistics

Signed from Lorient in the 2025/26 summer transfer window, you'd have been forgiven for knowing relatively little about Eli Kroupi Junior. But he looks set to follow in the footsteps of other talented strikers who have also played on the south coast, given what we've seen from him so far.

The France U21 star managed to rack up 13 strikes in 34 appearances last term, as he looked right at home in the Premier League despite being just 20 years old. It felt like only a matter of time until Kroupi would be linked with a move elsewhere, and that came to pass over this most recent summer, before an injury confirmed he'd stay put at Bournemouth for the time being. Now fit again, expect plenty of interest in a January transfer.

Eli Kroupi's 2026/27 Premier League Statistics

For the last couple of years or so, Brentford have landed themselves a talismanic figure, and soon after, they've been tasked with replacing them. The free-scoring nature of Ivan Toney was replaced by Bryan Mbeumo and Igor Thiago is now emulating the success of the Cameroonian in the final third – and that's perfectly showcased by his 22-goal haul in 38 Premier League outings last season.

A revelation for the Bees, Thiago's ten-minute brace against Burnley in November shows that he is capable of wreaking havoc for defences in quick succession – and his knack for drawing fouls and subsequently earning Keith Andrews' side a penalty is second to none. Still just 25 years of age, there is still plenty more to come from the Brazilian who has four goal contributions in nine outings this term.
