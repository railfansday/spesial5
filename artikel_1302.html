Serie A: Juventus vs. Venezia – probable line-ups, where to watch on TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Juventus look to Nick Woltemade and Randal Kolo Muani in attack as they host struggling Venezia in a must-win game at the Allianz Stadium.

The Bianconeri are still dealing with an injury crisis, but Andrea Cambiaso and Zeki Celik are available, and the Turkishman is competing with Federico Gatti for a starting spot in a back three, as Sky and Gazzetta report.

Francisco Conceiçao is also back from an injury, but not fit to start, so he might be introduced in the second half.

Other key players such as Kenan Yildiz, Nico Gonzalez, Khephren Thuram and Manuel Locatelli remain sidelined with injuries.

Venezia have also travelled to Turin without several players, including ex-Lazio man Toma Basic and Pasquale Mazzocchi. Basic’s absence means USMNT midfielder Gianluca Busio will start in the middle of the park, joining Kike Perez and Simon Sohm.

The match will be broadcast live on TNT Sports 1 in the UK and Ireland, DAZN and HBO Max. Paramount+ will show the game in the USA; Football Italia will provide live updates via a live blog.

Juventus (3-4-2-1): Vicario; Gatti, Bremer, Lucumì; McKennie, Douglas Luiz, Koopmeiners, Cabal; Kolo Muani, Alajbegovic; Woltemade.

Venezia (3-5-2): F. Stankovic; Halhal, Moreno, Juan Jesus; Hainaut, Perez, Sohm, Busio, Haps; Adams, Yeboah

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
