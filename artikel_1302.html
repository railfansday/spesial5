Immediate Cambiaghi recovery after Italy national team withdrawal

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Wednesday’s reports confirm that Nicolo Cambiaghi has returned to training with Bologna and is expected to be immediately available in Serie A this weekend after dropping out of the Italy national team squad a few days ago.

Cambiaghi started and earned plaudits for his performance in Italy’s 3-1 victory over France last Thursday, skinning Malo Gusto to set up Pio Esposito’s equalising goal for 1-1.

The 25-year-old suffered a knock to his ankle during the France game, which forced him to drop out of the squad before Sunday’s 4-1 defeat away against Belgium in Brussels.

Fortunately for Cambiaghi, the ankle issue does not appear serious and he has since been able to return to training back with Bologna.

According to the latest updates from Gianluca Di Marzio, Cambiaghi is expected to be fully available to Rafaelle Palladino for Bologna’s upcoming Serie A match against Udinese on Sunday (14.00 GMT).

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
