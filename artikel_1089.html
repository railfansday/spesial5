Declan Rice agrees new five-year Arsenal contract: Star to join Bukayo Saka as Gunners' highest-paid player at over £350,000-per-week - days after midfielder declared himself 'extremely lucky' to play for Gunners

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Published: 18:09 EST, 16 November 2026 | Updated: 18:19 EST, 16 November 2026

Declan Rice has agreed a new five-year deal at Arsenal that will run until the summer of 2031.

The England midfielder is expected to sign the new contract soon, which is set to put him alongside Bukayo Saka as Arsenal's top earner. Saka is on a deal worth over £350,000 a week.

Rice joined Arsenal in 2023 for £105million from West Ham and has established himself as the fulcrum of their midfield, helping steer them to the Premier League title last season.

The 27-year-old played 55 games in all competitions last season as the Gunners reached their first Champions League final since 2006, but lost out to Paris Saint-Germain.

His contract talks have been ongoing from at least March and are now finalised.

Previously, Rice's deal was expiring in 2028.

The Arsenal star had confirmed a new deal was close over the weekend when speaking on international duty with England.

'I've been extremely lucky that I've managed to play at such a consistent level throughout my career.

'I've always been stable in the clubs I've been at.

'A new contract is hopefully on the horizon soon.

'I'm extremely lucky that I'm at a club like Arsenal, where my manager [Mikel Arteta] and my team-mates put so much energy in me.

'I'm extremely lucky I don't have to think about that stuff.'
