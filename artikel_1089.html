The untold story of Mauricio Pochettino's move to Man United: When club decided he was the right man, the moment it became 'real', emotional talks with US staff and whirlwind first days in charge

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Published: 21:00 EST, 21 November 2026 | Updated: 04:25 EST, 22 November 2026

When Mauricio Pochettino boarded a flight from London to Atlanta a little over two weeks ago, there was little indication that by the time he landed back in the UK it would be as the new head coach of Manchester United.

The club were aware of the £7.5million buyout clause in his contract with the US national team as they began to consider potential replacements for Michael Carrick, but it hadn’t gone any further than that.

However, United’s 3-0 defeat to Bournemouth on October 25 left Carrick’s job hanging by a thread. He was given a two-week stay of execution that also allowed United time to step up their search for a suitable replacement.

At a meeting of the club’s executive committee on November 5 – after United had lost to Chelsea and staged a chaotic Champions League win against Roma – it was decided that Pochettino would be the No.1 choice if Carrick was sacked.

By the time the Argentine caught that flight to Atlanta 24 hours later to take charge of the USA's two-legged CONCACAF Nations League quarter-final against Haiti, United were preparing to make their move.

This is the story of the whirlwind two weeks that will end with Pochettino taking charge of his first game as United boss against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday following the dramatic events on both sides of the Atlantic.

Patrick Dorgu’s injury-time header rescued United from another defeat as they snatched a 4-4 draw against Aston Villa at Old Trafford, but it wasn’t enough to save Carrick. His fate was effectively sealed and the first contact was made with Pochettino’s camp.

There is a five-hour time difference between Manchester and Georgia, so it was late afternoon when Pochettino first learned of United’s interest at the Trilith Guesthouse – close to the US national team’s training base in Fayetteville south-west of Atlanta – as the players began to report there for duty.

Michael Carrick's fate was effectively sealed and the first contact was made with Pochettino’s camp after the 4-4 draw with Aston Villa at Old Trafford

As speculation raged over Carrick’s future, the odds on Pochettino replacing him at Old Trafford were slashed after some big money was placed on the 54-year-old overnight.

Preliminary talks continued with his representatives who made the US Soccer Federation aware of the situation. Pochettino discussed it over dinner at the team hotel with chief executive JT Batson and chief operating officer Dan Helfrich who knew it was pointless trying to change his mind.

‘That was the big day,’ says a source close to Pochettino, who took US team training in Fayetteville. ‘It became real and the optimism grew.’

As talks accelerated between United and their prospective new head coach, Carrick returned to Carrington to work with the United players not on international duty while chief executive Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox flew to Monaco to meet minority owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe and rubber-stamp the change.

United reached verbal agreement with Pochettino over a two-and-a-half-year deal, and agreed to pay US Soccer around £10million to buy the 54-year-old and his staff – No 2 Jesus Perez, first-team coach Miguel D’Agostino, goalkeeper coach Toni Jimenez, fitness coach Sebastiano Pochettino (Mauricio’s son), strength and conditioning coach Silvia Tuya and analyst Alec Scott – out of their contracts.

There was never any question of him leaving before the two Haiti ties, even though it left little time before his first game against Liverpool. Pochettino is said to have been respectful of the USA’s position and felt an obligation to see the job through.

As a compromise, it was agreed that Perez, Jimenez and Pochettino Jnr would be allowed to leave for Manchester after the first leg regardless of the result.

Media in the US were informed that access to the players had been cancelled out of respect for Veterans Day. It also meant that they don’t have to answer questions about Pochettino’s future.

Carrick returned to Carrington at 7.50am for the last time as United head coach and was sacked in a meeting with Berrada and Wilcox.

By the time Pochettino woke up in Fayetteville, United officially had a managerial vacancy and he gathered the players together for a scheduled team briefing at 10am.

Pochettino went through the usual pre-match preparation, and then invited staff into the room for the second half of the meeting.

There was some emotion but no surprise when he informed them of his decision to take the United job. ‘As you’d expect, he spoke from the heart,’ says a source. ‘He loved being here but United was a dream opportunity. It was too hard to say no, but hard to say goodbye.’

United officially announced Pochettino’s appointment and confirmed that he would stay in charge for the USA’s two games against Haiti before starting work the following Wednesday.

Pochettino conducted his pre-match press conference in Fayetteville, flanked by CEO chief JT Batson and president Cindy Parlow Cone, and broke down in tears as he thanked them for their support over the last 26 months.

Otherwise, the preparation is said to have gone according to plan as the US squad flew to Miami.

The Argentine broke down in tears as he thanked the USA for their support over the last 26 months

The US smashed Haiti 6-0 to all but guarantee their place in the semi-finals. Even so the message from Pochettino to his players after the game was: ‘This tie isn’t over.’

The players learned for the first time that Perez, Jimenez and Pochettino Jnr were leaving camp, and they headed straight to Manchester.

Pochettino flew with the players from Miami to Cincinnati for the second leg at the TQL Stadium.

On a visit to the Kenwood Towne Centre shopping mall, a man whose profile had risen in the US during his two years in charge of the national team was approached by even more fans than normal.

When Pochettino appeared as a guest of honour at the NFL game between the Cincinnati Bengals and Pittsburgh Steelers at Paycor Stadium that night, a number of Bengals’ players and staff congratulated him on his new job claiming to be United fans.

It emerged that United had provided a private jet for Pochettino and the remainder of his staff to fly straight from Cincinnati to Manchester through the night after the second leg.

In his pre-match press conference, he confirmed the departure of his three coaches over the weekend, and it was no surprise to learn that he was in constant contact over several days as he prepared to join them at Carrington.

The US beat Haiti by two goals to complete an 8-0 aggregate win. Afterwards, they played Take Me Home, Country Roads by John Denver over the stadium PA, which has been a tradition since the US hosted the World Cup last summer but carried particular poignance given that it has also been adopted as an anthem at United.

Pochettino invited all the staff onto the pitch for a team photo as he led a chant of U-S-A, and there was a tearful send-off in the dressing-room as he was presented with a signed shirt and a special hand-painted bottle of Clase Azul tequila.

It’s a favourite tipple of Pochettino’s and staff also ordered food from Chick-fil-A, which Pochettino regrets won’t be available in Manchester.

He never had a permanent residence in America, so he left the stadium at 10.30pm with the same suitcase he had brought from London and headed for Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky International Airport for the flight to Manchester.

Pochettino had a poor night’s sleep before landing shortly before noon aboard the grey Gulfstream G700 jet with D’Agostino, Scott and Tuya.

He posted a picture from the skies above Manchester and was then driven to Carrington, changing into an official United tracksuit en route.

Pochettino was too late for training but met with some of the players and club executives before being given a tour of the training ground and conducting an interview with MUTV which wrapped up about 7pm. He stayed at the training ground long into the evening to catch up on his preparation for Liverpool.

The plane believed to be carrying Pochettino and his staff was hired by Manchester United

More players returned from international duty, but Pochettino’s first training session with them at Carrington was ambushed by a drone shooting video footage that was posted online. It was proof – if he needed it – that the former Tottenham and Chelsea boss will be under even greater scrutiny at United.

Responding, Pochettino said: 'We have young guys training with us. It’s not nice. It’s not good.

'What do they want to show? Yes, we were working on shape. We were very organised. What is the point of doing something that can be very dangerous?

'I don't want to be upset, but I think it's a process the club is caring a lot about.'

Having only trained for a couple of hours on Thursday, Pochettino worked with the players between 11.30am and 2pm on Friday.

‘The training session was a little bit longer than it normally is in (match day) minus two,’ said Pochettino at his opening pre-match press conference, admitting that he had not even had a chance to shower beforehand.

Pochettino worked with his new players between 11.30am and 2pm on Friday

Pochettino took training for the third day running at Carrington on Saturday morning, before United travelled on the team bus to their hotel where they had dinner and a pre-match meeting last night.

On Sunday he faces a massive first game as United boss in front of the cameras at Anfield at the end of a dizzying fortnight.
