Who am I? Guess WSL star No 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The rules are simple. Each Sunday there's a new Women's Super League footballer and the challenge is to guess who they are in as few attempts as possible.

After each wrong guess you unlock a new clue. The fewer clues you need to get the right answer, the more points you score.

Three is a good score, four or five points is exceptional.

So take part and return for more next time.
