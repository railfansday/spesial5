Enzo Maresca backs ‘winner’ Erling Haaland and says there is ‘no panic’ over Man City slump

Manchester City manager Enzo Maresca has downplayed concerns about his team’s togetherness and insisted he is not worried about star striker Erling Haaland following downcast post-match interviews.

City had won 11 of their first 12 matches in all competitions this season but have suffered an alarming downturn in form, only winning two of their last seven games.

Talisman Haaland has also suffered a slump at the same time, scoring just one goal in five games, and cut a morose figure when he was interviewed after a battling 2-2 draw against Napoli in the Champions League on Tuesday.

However, Maresca has insisted their poor run and the Norwegian’s demeanour are not cause for concern.

He said: “I think Erling is a winner. It's normal that he's not happy when we don't win games but not just Erling, everyone.

“Remember just a few weeks ago he was dancing inside the pitch. You can write and think many different things.

“Erling is upset. I'm upset because we're not winning games. But this is our job, this is football. That's why I need to maintain the balance.

“He's a nine. They live day by day for goals and winning games. If they don't score or win games they're upset. So I don't think that affects his game but for sure he's not happy not winning games, but everyone is on the same page.”

Asked whether it has been business as usual in training, he said: “He has been good. Yes, normal like since day one. Again, a few weeks ago he was dancing inside the pitch, he was dancing in training. He's happy. It's normal that results affect the mood, that's why we need to win games.

“But I don't see any problem at all with Erling.”

The Italian insisted that “transition takes time” and added: “For me, it's completely normal, I'm completely relaxed, no panic at all. I understand that we need to win games because it's our business but for me, it's all normal.”

Asked whether their string of poor results, which includes a 10-game run without a clean sheet, had affected players’ confidence, he said: “For sure, when you don't win games, it's not the ideal situation. But we won games at the beginning. We have also seven or eight new players that need to be used to winning games, so it's just a process.”

City face another significant test as they visit league leaders Arsenal, who are six points ahead, on Sunday.

Maresca was insistent that City were not lacking in belief ahead of their trip to the defending champions, saying: “I think we have already shown this season that we are able to win important games, to win away. I don't see any reason why we are not able to win there.”
