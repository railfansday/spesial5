Joe Jonas Reveals Cheeky Gift He Recently Gave Ex Demi Lovato

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Joe Jonas recently revealed that he gave Demi Lovato, whom he dated briefly in 2010, a cheeky birthday gift that nodded toward one of her most viral interview moments.

Joe Jonas’ favorite meme? He would have to say mug.

More than 10 years after Demi Lovato went viral for revealing mugs were her “favorite dish” during an interview with NRJ Berlin, her Camp Rock costar—whom she dated briefly in 2010—revealed it’s still his favorite pop culture moment.

“It’s amazing,” Joe told his brothers Kevin Jonas and Nick Jonas during the Nov. 17 episode of their Hey Jonas! podcast. “I bought her a mug as a gift for coming as our special guest recently or her birthday. I think I sent her a mug for her birthday. I can’t remember.”

Meanwhile, Kevin and Joe agreed it was a great clip.  As Kevin emphasized, “She wasn’t incorrect.”

For Demi’s part, she recently revealed that she intentionally had misinterpreted the question—the interviewer was referring to food rather than cookware—but she opted to answer it in her own way.

“I had been asked so many questions that day,” Demi reflected during an appearance on Keke Palmer’s Baby, It’s Keke Palmer podcast in March. “I was doing, like, a press tour and you get bored doing press tours.”

And so, the Disney Channel alum joked she thought at the time, “‘I’m gonna start f--king with the interviewers.’”

“I just ran with it,” Demi joked, emphasizing that only part of the interview went viral. “In the long, extended video, you see me look at my tour manager and like smirk. Like, ‘did you catch that one?’ And then that went viral and everyone thought I was serious.”

But there’s nothing wrong with being confident in answering the question this way, especially given Demi made a worthy case for mugs.

“I like mugs because they’re very comfortable in your hand,” she said in the now-viral clip. “And they hold hot things that you don’t have to touch. So, you know, coffee or hot tea.”

As for Joe running so far with the joke that he sent his ex a mug as a present? While Demi previously joked she was “mad” at Joe following their breakup, there’s no longer any mean mugging between them.

“It’s been such a wonderful relationship through so many years,” Joe told USA Today earlier this year after he and Demi each made appearances at each other’s respective concerts. “I feel so grateful that Demi and I can go perform together still and see each other in these different journeys of our life.”

For more Disney alums who dated, keep reading…

Disney Channel worlds collided after Sabrina Carpenter met Bradley Steven Perry.

The Girl Meets World star and Good Luck Charlie actor’s romance began in 2014, when Carpenter’s show began filming right next door to Perry’s Mighty Med.

“[It was a] really unique way to ask someone out,” Carpenter told J-14 at the time, describing a scene out of a pirate flick: “Like, they do the little bottles and they put notes in them and send them across the ocean, like one of those, and [he] put a note inside. It was cute.”

Though they broke up in 2015, Perry—who married Candace Cameron Bure’s daughter Natasha Bure in 2025—noted that their connection was a sweet young love.

“Sabrina was my first girlfriend,” Perry said during an appearance on the Unplanned Podcast in 2024. “We dated for about two years.”

He added, “It was very much like a little high school relationship.”

Bella Thorne and Gregg Sulkin shook up young Hollywood when they first stepped out together in 2015.

But the Shake It Up alum and Wizards of Waverly Place actor went their separate ways a year later.

“Our schedules made seeing each other difficult," Thorne and Sulkin told People in a joint statement back in 2016, “and we decided that for now this would be best.”

But they’ll always look back on their fairytale romance with a smile.

“After much thought and soul-searching, we have made the difficult decision to end our relationship,” they added. “We will always love each other and have a deep respect for one another, as we have each grown to be better people because of our time together.”

Can you imagine what would happen if Zac Efron and Vanessa Hudgens never dated?

The High School Musical alums hit it off as soon as the cameras started rolling for the franchise’s first film in 2005.

“We created a bond right from the start,” Hudgens—who played the Gabriella Montez to Efron’s Troy Bolton in the films—told People in 2022. “We were like, ‘We’re doing this, it’s us or nothing.’”

“It started off really organically,” she added. “I could not have been more grateful to have that relationship at that time.”

And while the pair eventually had to go their own ways, you can still bet on the fact that they had a next-level connection.

As Hudgens put it on a 2025 episode of Bart Johnson's Get Your Head In The Game podcast, "Of course I was a child and paired with this boy and I was like, 'Oh my god, he's so cute.' The chemistry was so real."

Nick Jonas and Selena Gomez’s romance was burnin’ up from 2008 to 2010.

And while they shared two years together, Gomez’s real prize from the relationship would have to be her unbreakable bond with BFF Taylor Swift.”

“Taylor and I dated the Jonas Brothers,” Gomez—who married Benny Blanco in 2025—said during a 2025 episode of the Therapuss with Jake Shane podcast of how she and Swift met. “I dated Nick and she dated Joe [Jonas]. And everything  was cute, we were young. We all know and love each other now and it’s so cute.”

Gomez added of her relationship with Jonas, dad to daughter Malti with wife Priyanka Chopra, “She and I like to say the best thing we got out of those relationships was each other because it was really sweet.”

At one point, Miley Cyrus thought Dylan Sprouse could be the one.

Indeed, the Hannah Montana alum revealed she and The Suite Life of Zack & Cody star were much more than work colleagues.

“Dylan Sprouse was my boyfriend,” Cyrus told Alex Cooper during the March 2026 Disney+ Hannah Montana 20th Anniversary Special. “I think he was the cutest.”

Responding to past rumors that she and Sprouse—who married model Barbara Palvin in 2023—were an item, the “Flowers” singer added, “It's true, confirmed.”

But during their brief romance, she also got to spend quality time with Sprouse’s twin brother Cole Sprouse—though she knew she couldn’t have the best of both worlds.

“Dylan was my crush,” Cyrus continued. “Their dad would take us to sushi. I was into the whole culture, and it was a two-for-one. Like, bring the brother. Hello!”

Joe Jonas and Demi Lovato may be like Venus and Mars, but they were once on the same astral plane.

After all, the Camp Rock alums took their romance offscreen in 2010 while filming the film’s sequel, though they backed down from the connection about a year later.

“Demi and I knew going into our romantic relationship that it may not be an easy one,” Jonas told Us Weekly in 2025. “I realize over the time we have shared together that I feel I care more about our friendship right now.”

That’s why the Jonas Brothers member wouldn’t change a thing about the love story he shared with Lovato—who married Jordan “Jutes” Lutes in 2025.

“It was my choice to break up,” Jonas—who shares daughters Willa and Delphine with ex-wife Sophie Turner—added, “but I love her as a friend. She’s been there for me when I needed her. I will continue to be her friend and be there for her.”
