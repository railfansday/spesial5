Florence Pugh Reacts to “Disturbing” Speculation About Her Appearance

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Florence Pugh responded to online speculation that she had work done on her face: "I turned 30. I’m a woman...faces change.”

Florence Pugh isn’t worrying about the rumor mill, darling.

The East of Eden star responded to speculation that she’s had work done after fans theorized the reason for her newly chiseled jawline.

After a content creator said she thinks “that we can all collectively agree that Hollywood seems to have this thing against softer features,” with stars wanting “this sculpted, snatched look,” in a video shared to Evie Magazine’s Instagram Nov. 17, Pugh addressed why “her jaw is more sharp.”

As the Marvel star said of the side-by-side photos of herself in the video, “Guys. I turned 30. I’m a woman. [Bodies] and faces change.”

“It’s not that deep, the fact that people can’t even tell between what’s aging and what’s surgery is disturbing,” she added in the post’s comments section. “Sadly puppy fluff doesn’t last forever.”

She further addressed her shock over the matter in a vulnerable social media statement.

“I find it so stressful waking up and have thousands of people talking about you and your body in ways that you can’t even comprehend,” she wrote on Instagram Nov. 18. “I woke up the other morning and quite a lot of you were discussing my face and body in such a disturbing way.”

She noted, "I learnt online that I had been on ozempic? I learnt that I’d had upper bleph? Lower bleph? Filler? Face lipo? Bucal fat remover? Chin surgery? Nose surgery?!”

“I have spent my whole career defending and protecting bodies that just grow the way they grow,” she added. “I was subjected to fat shaming in the business from the moment I started and I have loved everytime I get to tell someone to f--k off when they are being inappropriate about my weight, and I’m not changing that now just because you’ve all said so.”

And just for the record, the Little Women actress wants fans to know she’s perfectly happy in the skin she’s in.

“I have never been on Ozempic,” she wrote. “I have NEVER done any face surgery. And don’t EVER say that I didn’t make my own nose, I’ve always had that beautiful ski slope. Even as a baby!”

Rather, Pugh credits any difference in her appearance to a lifestyle change.

“Here’s the truth, I turned 30 and my New Year’s resolution was to change my relationship with alcohol, and to be able to do at least one pull up in the gym,” she continued. “For the first time in my ENTIRE career, I have had the opportunity to be rested, to sleep, to wake up and work out and to eat regularly and healthily.”

And she won’t let internet fodder about her body throw her off her game.

“I’ve f--king worked so hard to clean my head and clean my body because I don’t want to allow space for negativity in my mind to grow,” she added. “I’m looking after myself, and that’s literally it.”
