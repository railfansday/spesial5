My game of the week is Star Wars Rogue Squadron II: Rogue Leader. My wife surprised me with GTA VI, so of course that is what I’m playing, much like the rest of the world. Similar to RDR2, I’m slowly getting a grasp on the controls and life sim mechanics. It can be a grueling process, but it will eventually become second nature.

That being said, I’ve been absolutely blown away by this game. I had been curious for years as to how Rockstar would create an open world experience better than RDR2, and I can safely say that it has been an astonishing and mind-bending.

This is a game that will have people discussing their adventures for years to come. Speaking of, I will share one of my (early) experiences.

I was driving to some rundown RV park nestled away in the middle of nowhere. As I was making my approach I saw a bloodied deer sprawled out on the rocky terrain. Once I got much closer, I see a literal Sasquatch mutilating its body. I was stunned, but I quickly hopped out of my vehicle, got my rifle, and chased after him, but to no avail.

As I make my way back to my vehicle through this thicket of bushes, muddy waters, and even some alligators, I hear gunshots in the distance. I, of course, had to investigate, and find myself stumbling upon a beat down hotel. I notice there is a door wide open, and unexpectedly hear screams. All of a sudden, there is a guy crawling on his hands and knees out of the doorway, bleeding profusely. Another scream happens.

Right there, in the hotel room, was another guy getting mauled and eaten to death by a tiger. I begin panicking as it notices me, and I begin shooting the animal multiple times before it croaks over.

Once the situation becomes calm, I ransack the hotel room. There was a flare gun, drugs, and lots of cash. Out of nowhere comes three hillbillies attempting to rob me. I quickly pull out my flare gun (on accident) and unload my ammo on these punks. They catch fire and succumb to their horrific injuries. The coast is now clear and I book it out of there.

This whole (chance) encounter was so unexpected and organic. It’s these in-between moments that solidifies Rockstar as the masters of their craft. The amount of detail and interactivity is truly awe-inspiring. I can’t wait to see what other events transpire in my next 100-hours of play.
