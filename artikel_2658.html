2027 NFL mock draft: Buccaneers land a dynamic vertical threat

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers still have seven games remaining in the regular season, but following their Week 11 loss to the Detroit Lions, they are projected to select seventh overall in the 2027 NFL Draft.

While we will continue to cover mock drafts from analysts around the league, we will also be putting together our own projections each week at BucsWire. Tampa Bay currently holds all seven of its original draft selections, but for this mock draft installment, we will focus on the first three rounds.

Tampa Bay would be a dream landing spot for Charlie Becker. The 6-foot-4, 208-pound receiver is a big-bodied target who can take the lid off a defense. His ability to generate explosive plays has been on display throughout the season, including a 165-yard performance against Ohio State and a 79-yard reception against Western Kentucky, according to Indiana's official statistics.

As the Buccaneers continue to build their receiving corps around Emeka Egbuka, adding someone who can consistently threaten defenses vertically would make life easier for everyone underneath. Tampa Bay has invested in the position in recent years, but Becker brings a combination of size and downfield ability that would be hard to pass up.

Landing Matayo Uiagalelei in the second round would be tremendous value. The 6-foot-5, 265-pound pass rusher has recorded 9.5 sacks and 10.5 tackles for loss this season, and Scouting Grade ranks him 17th on its big board. The outlet also highlights his ability to convert speed into power and generate pressure from different alignments.

With Yaya Diaby's contract set to expire after the season, Tampa Bay faces an important decision about its pass rush. Pairing Uiagalelei with Rueben Bain Jr. and David Walker would give the Buccaneers flexibility in attacking opposing offensive lines without sacrificing the long-term development of either player.

Despite Tampa Bay's recent investments at cornerback, I would have no issue addressing the position again in the third round. At 6-foot-2 and 190 pounds, Jyaire Hill has the length to challenge receivers on the outside and brings considerable starting experience from Michigan. Hill earned honorable mention All-Big Ten recognition in 2025.

With Zyon McCollum, Benjamin Morrison, and Jacob Parrish already in Tampa Bay, Hill could develop without being forced into a starting role immediately. More importantly, his addition would create competition on the outside and give the Buccaneers greater flexibility when injuries inevitably test the depth of their secondary.
