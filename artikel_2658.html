Report: Steelers are expected to release Aaron Rodgers

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It’s to be expected, given that quarterback Aaron Rodgers has poured rocket fuel on the fire that Steelers started last night when the Steelers benched Rodgers.

Via Gerry Dulac of the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette, the Steelers are expected to release Rodgers. Dulac said the decision was “hastened” by Rodgers’s comments on Pat McAfee’s show.

If/when it happens, Rodgers will initially be subject to waivers. He could be claimed, in theory. (It’s unlikely.) He then would be a free agent.

What comes next will be fascinating. The passion and intensity of Rodgers’s remarks suggest he won’t go quietly, and that he’ll want to play for someone else in order to prove that, with a functional head coach, Rodgers can still win games.

Of course, someone else will have to be willing to welcome the Aaron Rodgers Experience to town, where anything the coach says or does can and will be used against him in a future episode of The Pat McAfee Show.
