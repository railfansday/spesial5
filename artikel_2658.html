Luka Dončić scores 43, leading Lakers over Suns for eighth consecutive win, tying for NBA's top record

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Luka Dončić continued his scorching start to the 2026-27 NBA season, topping the 40-point mark for the seventh time in 12 games in the Los Angeles Lakers' 131-123 win over the Phoenix Suns on Sunday. He currently leads the NBA in scoring at 38.6 points per game.

Dončić scored 20 of his 43 points in the first quarter, hitting three 3-pointers as he shot 5-for-10 and the Lakers took a 33-24 lead. That advantage increased to 54-37 with five minutes to go before halftime on a Jake LaRavia 3. Yet the Suns closed out the second quarter on a 12-3 run to trim the Lakers' lead to 61-54.

In the third quarter, the Lakers started hot from behind the arc, hitting three 3-pointers (two by Quentin Grimes) as part of a 13-3 run and boosted their lead to 20 points. Eventually, that margin became 31 points with under two minutes remaining in the quarter. Dončić added 12 points to put him at 37 points going into the final 12 minutes.

Phoenix recovered to give the Lakers a game in the fourth quarter, hitting four 3-point attempts to begin the frame. A Walker Kessler tip-in dunk restored the Lakers' advantage to 113-92 with just under nine minutes remaining.

However, the Suns responded with a 15-1 surge, fueled by six points from rookie Koa Peat. A 3 from Ryan Dunn cut the Lakers' lead to 117-111 at the 4:40 mark. Collin Gillespie drove to the basket for a layup that pulled Phoenix to within five points with 1:05 left in regulation.

But another 3 from Grimes effectively put the game away for the Lakers and Dončić closed out the scoring with two free throws. However, he told sideline reporter Roqui Theus afterward that the plan was for him to sit out the fourth quarter until the Suns made the game close.

"I wasn't planning on playing the fourth quarter," Dončić said. "We gotta get better at that. … Once we get up, we gotta stay there."

The Lakers played without Austin Reaves, who missed the game due to a bone bruise in his left heel.

Overall, Dončić shot 13-of-28 from the floor, while making 12-of-14 free throws. He also notched nine assists in his 43 minutes of play. Grimes followed with 22 points, shooting 5-for-6 on 3-pointers, with 6 assists and 2 steals. Collin Sexton contributed 16 points off the bench and Kessler added 14 points with 12 rebounds.

For the Suns, Gillespie finished with 26 points (shooting 5-for-10 on 3s), 4 assists and 4 rebounds. Luke Kennard added 18 as the Suns got 78 points from their reserves. Devin Booker led the Phoenix starters with 17 points and 7 assists.

The Lakers have won eight consecutive games and improved their record to 12-2, tying the San Antonio Spurs for the NBA's best record. (The Spurs defeated the Utah Jazz on Sunday, 110-102.) Their next game is Tuesday versus the Charlotte Hornets (4-9).
