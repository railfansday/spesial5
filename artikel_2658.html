Buffalo Bills vs. Kansas City Chiefs, Week 12 Thanksgiving Day game chat

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Tonight, one of the NFL's biggest games of the 2026 NFL season plays out in Orchard Park, NY. The Buffalo Bills take on the Kansas City Chiefs tonight for a Thanksgiving Day game in primetime. This is the first time the Bills are hosting a game on Thanksgiving, and it comes against one of their biggest rivals — and what many believe to be the best rivalry in sports today.

At 9-1, Buffalo is currently in first place in the AFC and in control of their playoff fate. If the regular season ended today, the Bills would claim the one seed and a bye to begin the postseason. If the current standings prevail, the 8-2 Chiefs would be the five seed, and playing at the Cincinnati Bengals in the Wild Card round.

Tonight's game isn't isn't of the elimination variety because there's a lot more football yet to play, but there's a lot at stake for both the winner and loser. If Buffalo loses they'd surrender a head-to-head advantage to Kansas City, and sole possession of first place in the conference.

While many in Chiefs Kingdom are quick to remind Bills Mafia that regular-season wins don't really matter, that couldn't be further from the truth. You can be sure that quarterbacks Josh Allen and Patrick Mahomes know how big this game is for their seasons.

Will Allen and the Bills take down the Chiefs in head coach Joe Brady's first matchup leading the team against Andy Reid and Steve Spagnuolo? When Buffalo and Kansas City square off, anything's possible! However, remember that the weather might have a say in how this one plays out.

Here's your open thread for Bills-Chiefs on Thanksgiving night. As always, be kind to each other — and in all things… go Bills!
