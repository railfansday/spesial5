Saquon Barkley: I won't be 100 percent healthy this season, but will keep playing

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Eagles running back Saquon Barkley said after beating the Steelers on Sunday that he’s not completely healthy and won’t be completely healthy this season, but he has no desire to shut it down.

Despite the Eagles being 3-7 and seemingly going nowhere, Barkley said he’ll play through whatever is ailing him the rest of the way.

“I don’t think I’m ever going to get to 100 percent again for the rest of the season. That’s just how football works,” Barkley said. “Got to get ready for the Thanksgiving game. That’s our job.”

Barkley appeared to injure his shoulder at one point in the game, but he wouldn’t answer directly when asked about it and indicated that he’ll be good to go after three days.

“I won’t really get into that. I just look forward to rehabbing this week and getting back on Thursday,” Barkley said.

After snapping a seven-game losing streak, Barkley is enthusiastic about trying to win two in a row in Dallas on Thursday.

“Playing on Thanksgiving is something we always dream about as kids, against the Dallas Cowboys,” Barkley said.

Barkley is going to play as long as he physically can.
