NFL insider floats a WR the Rams could sign instead of Puka Nacua

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

One league source has floated a pivot that would reshape the Los Angeles Rams' offseason: replacing Puka Nacua with Dallas Cowboys receiver George Pickens.

ESPN's Jeremy Fowler said one person in the league brought up the idea of the Rams switching out Nacua for another other top free agent receiver. Fowler called Pickens a true outside receiver who is "scheme transcendent," and said head coach Sean McVay would be a good personality fit.

The key element in all of this is money. Fowler said Pickens' market would be closer to Zay Flowers and Drake London, who make $35 million per year. Whereas before the season, ESPN's Dan Graziano predicated Nacua would sign a deal worth $39 million per year, that would sit just below Ja'Marr Chase and Jaxon Smith-Njigba. If the Rams don't extend Nacua and instead place the franchise tag on him, it will cost the team between $30-$31 million.

Nacua's situation is also complicated by injury. He is expected to miss the next four to five months after MCL surgery last week, though he is on track to be ready for the start of the 2027 season. He also incurred a three-game suspension because of multiple off-field incidents before the 2026 season.

Pickens, who is the same age as Nacua at 25, has 324 receptions for 5,050 yards and 27 touchdowns in 75 career games across five seasons with the Pittsburgh Steelers and Cowboys.

His best season came in 2025, his first in Dallas, when he caught 93 passes for 1,429 yards and nine touchdowns, earning a Pro Bowl nod and second-team All-Pro honors. This season, he has 57 catches for 780 yards and six touchdowns in 10 games.

For now, this is pure speculation. Multiple NFL insiders reported that league sources find it hard to believe the Rams would let Nacua explore free agency, and Adam Schefter said he thinks the Rams "plan to keep him." But if the Rams weigh the cost and risk of keeping Nacua, Pickens could be a name to watch. That is, if the Cowboys don't extend or place the franchise tag on him.
