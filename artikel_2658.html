Kickoff time, TV network set for Georgia football game vs. Georgia Tech

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Georgia football's final regular season game now has a kickoff time and TV assignment determined.

The Bulldogs game against rival Georgia Tech on Saturday Nov. 28 is set for a 3:30 p.m. kickoff and will be shown on ABC.

That was announced late Saturday night following a day when Georgia Tech was crushed by Wake Forest 50-29 and No. 1 Georgia beat South Carolina 34-6.

Georgia will have no home night regular season games this season.

The Yellow Jackets are 3-8 after losing for the fifth time in the last six games.

Georgia is 10-1.  The Bulldogs have won eight in a row over Georgia Tech, its longest winning streak in a series that began in 1893.

Georgia won last year's game at Mercedes-Benz Stadium 16-9 in Atlanta.
