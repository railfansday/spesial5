Easy Vegan Green Bean Casserole

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

You’ve been there, excited to join your family for Thanksgiving dinner, only to realize that even the green bean casserole isn’t just veggies. This time, though, you can enjoy the creamiest green bean casserole without the dairy or meat. For the ultimate creamy bite, we make a DIY vegan version of condensed cream of mushroom soup with fresh mushrooms, shallots, and almond milk. We're also super into the onion topping on this casserole—it's broiled in the oven with a touch of oil but so much more fragrant and vibrant in flavor than the fried ones that come in a can.

Preheat oven to medium broil and line a medium baking sheet with aluminum foil. In a medium bowl, toss onion with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Add flour and Panko and toss until the onions are coated. Broil, tossing every 2 to 3 minutes, until onions are crisp and golden, about 6 to 8 minutes total. Turn oven down to 375°.

Prepare an ice bath. In a large pot of heavily salted boiling water, add green beans and cook until bright green, 3 to 4 minutes. Using a slotted spoon or tongs, quickly transfer green beans to the ice bath to cool. Drain and transfer to a large bowl.

In a large skillet over medium heat, heat 2 tablespoons olive oil. Add shallots and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, about 5 minutes. Add mushrooms and season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring often, until mushrooms are golden, about 5 minutes more. Stir in garlic, then transfer the mixture to the bowl with the green beans.

Heat the remaining 4 tablespoons of olive oil in the same skillet over medium heat. Whisk in flour and cook until golden, about 2 minutes. Gradually whisk in almond milk and bring to a simmer.

Cook until thickened, about 4 minutes. Remove from heat and season to taste with salt and pepper.

Add the green bean mixture and toss until everything is evenly combined.

Transfer the mixture to a medium casserole dish. Bake until warmed through and bubbling around the edges, about 20 minutes. Top with the "fried" onions and bake for 5 minutes more.
