Cream Cheese Mashed Potatoes

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Cream cheese doesn't just belong on bagels. In this instance, it's the secret to making mashed potatoes even creamier, richer, and more decadent. The high fat content in the cream cheese melds with the butter and milk to make the potatoes perfectly fluffy and creamy. Serve these at your Thanksgiving or Christmas dinner, or even on a cozy weeknight alongside oven-baked pork chops.

What People Are Saying:“Never cooked mashed potatoes before, and this recipe turned out amazing.” - GreenHat

“Silky smooth and delicious” - BlueProcessor

In a large pot, cover the potatoes with water and season with salt. Bring it to a boil and cook until the potatoes are softened and fork-tender, which should take about 16 to 18 minutes. Drain and return the potatoes to the pot.

Add cream cheese and, using a potato masher, mash potatoes until smooth.

Meanwhile, in a small saucepan over medium heat, cook the milk and butter. Stir until the butter is melted, which should take about 2 minutes. Pour the butter mixture over the potatoes.

Stir until it's combined and creamy. Make sure to season with salt and pepper!

Transfer potatoes to a serving bowl and season with more pepper. Top with chives and enjoy!

Store leftover cream cheese mashed potatoes in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
