Inside the Star-Studded F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix: Celebs, Style Wins and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Move over, Oscars! The 2026 Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix rolled out the red carpet for one of the most star-studded events in recent memory — and Us was there for all of it.

The jam-packed weekend culminated with the Grand Prix itself, won in thrilling fashion by Red Bull’s Max Verstappen in the shadow of the iconic Sphere on Saturday, November 21.

Shortly before the race, Ted Lasso star and motorsports guru Hannah Waddingham gushed about the spectacle.

“I didn’t expect it to be this crazy here, which is stupid because I know that it’s Vegas,” Waddingham, 52, told Us. “I’m a real petrolhead. I sang the national anthem at the British Grand Prix. It’s my space. I love it so much.”

Actor Michael Cimino called the experience “so sick,” saying his Formula 1 fandom bloomed by “coming to the races.”

“I grew up in Vegas, so my first F1 was the second Vegas [Grand Prix] and that’s honestly what got me into it,” Cimino, 27, said. “It was just the excitement of seeing something like this in my hometown, which we never had when I was growing up.”

For KPop Demon Hunters star Rei Ami – who performed at the conclusion of Saturday’s race – love of Formula can be traced back to her father, who joined her in Vegas.

“When we first immigrated to the States, my dad’s first job was a mechanic,” the singer and actress, 31, explained. “I remember visiting him and seeing him in his grey charcoal cover-alls, the dirt between his nails and him coming home exhausted. He never complained. He always had the energy to take care of my sister and I, and my mom. He always helped us with our homework, took us to church every Sunday.”

She added, “It is quite literally because of his sacrifice – him literally putting his life, his goals and his dreams to the side – that I’m allowed to stand here, live the life that I live and dream as loudly and boldly as possible. Him being a car enthusiast, I thought, ‘Let’s make sure we bring him out this year.’ At his first ever F1 experience, he’s going to see his daughter perform on top of the Paddock Club. It’s been such an unforgettable experience. It’s been so special.”

Us had VIP access to the Paddock Club on Saturday, where the guest list was a who’s who of Hollywood, including Chris Evans, Anthony Mackie, Chris Pratt, Mark Wahlberg, Venus Williams, Adam Sandler, Lisa and more.

One of the most buzzworthy off-track story lines of the weekend was the arrival of Kim Kardashian, who was there to support her boyfriend, Ferrari driver Lewis Hamilton.

Hamilton, 41, ultimately finished in 7th place, thanks largely to a crash during qualifying with teammate Charles Leclerc.

However, Kardashian, 46, found a winner’s circle of her own on race day, shutting things down with an all-red leather ensemble from Chrome Hearts that garnered nearly as much conversation as the Grand Prix itself.

In all of the glitz and glamour of Las Vegas, it’s easy to argue that’s just as important.
