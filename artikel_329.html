Jimmy Kimmel reacts to Trump hosting a celebrity meme coin dinner

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The $TRUMP memecoin has been in circulation for nearly two years now, with the value declining by around 99 percent since its launch in January 2025.

But fear not, investors, because Donald Trump had a surprise up his sleeve.

"Our president has a gift for grift. Over the weekend he hosted a dinner at his golf club in Virginia for all the top suckers who invested in his memecoin. It was billed as 'the most exclusive dinner in the world,' with guests Floyd Mayweather, Tristan Thompson, and Kevin O'Leary from Shark Tank," explains Jimmy Kimmel in the clip above. "It's so inspiring to see: Even while he's so busy knocking down the White House, botching the war, tanking the economy, [he] still finds time to run a pyramid scheme out of his golf course."

Unfortunately the celebrity dinner doesn't appear to have moved the price much. $TRUMP is currently trading at around $0.81, down from an all-time-high of over $70. Still, at least it's made Trump himself some money.
