Angel Reese adds to speculation as Kelsey Mitchell allegedly unfollows the Fever

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

We may just have been given some clues on Kelsey Mitchell's situation with the Indiana Fever.

Kelsey Mitchell just wrapped up the absolute season of her life, putting up a career-high 24.7 points a game on 51-45 shooting splits and finishing third in MVP voting.

But with Indiana getting bounced early in the playoffs again, her future is suddenly the biggest question mark in the league. She's an unrestricted free agent coming off that one-year extension, but the Fever are making it a priority to try and bring her back this offseason.

Mitchell already made it clear she's looking out for herself this winter, meaning Indiana either has to break the bank to keep their primary bucket-getter or risk watching secure her services.

The rumor mill went into overdrive on social media the second people noticed Kelsey Mitchell apparently hit unfollow on the Indiana Fever's Instagram page.

With Indiana fans already sweating bullets, Kelsey posted a new TikTok to her account, and Angel Reese showed up in the comments with a sneaky "love this for youuuu".

And as you can imagine, social media again turned into pure chaos, with fans debating whether she's teaming up with Angel in Chicago, convinced Mitchell has one foot out the door.

We cannot confirm whether Kelsey Mitchell ever followed the Indiana Fever on Instagram, but what is true is that she doesn't currently follow the Fever.
