Mavericks vs. Pacers Quoteboard: ‘We got it to the guys in their money spots, and they made plays’

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

The Dallas Mavericks (7-4) notched their second win of the young season, 125-122, over the Indiana Pacers (3-8) on Monday, and on the second night of a back-to-back set, no less. Heady stuff for the fifth-youngest roster in the NBA this year.

Cooper Flagg was sensational once again, finishing with 43 points, 10 rebounds and three steals, while Kyrie Irving added 35 points on seven made 3-pointers.
