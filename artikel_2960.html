AAON: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The backlog near $1.9 billion gives investors a lens on whether the restatement-era growth can hold up as the company scales.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 26, 2026 at 4:54PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 9, 2026, AAON (AAON +1.04%) announced record quarterly net sales of $707 million alongside accounting restatements for the first half of the year. Business expansion outpaced the internal administrative infrastructure, resulting in internal control deficiencies during a period of operational growth. Three things management said stand out.

Rapid corporate expansion strained administrative processes, causing revenue reporting errors that required formal accounting corrections. Fixing these internal gaps remains critical for institutional trust as the business scales.

Explosive demand for data center liquid-cooling equipment drove sales at BASX, the custom thermal management brand of AAON, to unprecedented levels. Customer demand for power-dense cooling systems continues to outstrip broader commercial building construction markets.

Multiyear factory expansion investments are beginning to generate positive cash, improving balance sheet leverage. Higher factory utilization and working capital management converted top-line gains into cash generation.

Production volume is surging across expanded plants, and executive leadership is clearly satisfied with operational progress. Specialized engineering capabilities and long-term customer relationships position the company well, while leadership monitors pricing discipline across its $1.9 billion order backlog.

Manufacturing throughput continues to rise, and leadership sees substantial operational runway across both business segments.
