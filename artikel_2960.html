Not Crypto. Not Gold. Not Silver. This Is the Safe-Haven Asset I'd Pile Money Into Right Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Vanguard Utilities ETF offers a good mix of stability and dividend income, and its performance has been in line with the S&P 500 over the past five years.

By David Jagielski, CPA – Nov 24, 2026 at 10:56AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Vanguard Utilities ETF ›

The stock market suddenly doesn't look so hot anymore. It's been declining in recent weeks, and as of the end of Monday, the S&P 500's gains since the start of the year were just under 6%. The recent controversy surrounding tech giant Nvidia has weighed on its performance, while the Anthropic IPO and its inflated valuation may be giving investors reasons to second-guess high-priced stocks right now.

Investors may be looking for safe-haven investments to hide out in. But rather than investing in Bitcoin, gold, or silver, there's a much safer investment I'd be considering right now: the Vanguard Utilities ETF (VPU +0.38%). Here's why this investment can make a whole lot of sense for risk-averse investors.

The two things I'd be looking for right now if I were worried about the stock market would be dividends and a low beta.

Dividend income can be crucial for padding overall returns. The Vanguard Utilities ETF yields around 2.8%, which is more than double the S&P 500 average of just 1.1%. That extra dividend income is highly valuable in boosting overall returns. For example, the Vanguard Utilities ETF has risen by just 25% over the past five years, but when including dividends, its total returns are up around 46%. And that's right in line with the S&P 500's total returns, despite utility stocks offering greater long-term stability and less volatility.

Investors can find low-volatility investments by looking at their respective beta values, which indicate how closely they move with the broader market. The Vanguard Utilities ETF has averaged a beta of just 0.36 over the past five years, suggesting it doesn't move closely with the overall market at all.

While the Vanguard Utilities ETF is a good safe-haven investment to park money into today, it's also an excellent long-term holding to simply buy and forget about. Investing in top utility stocks, as the fund does, can be a great way to benefit from stable growth while also collecting plenty of dividend income.

Most importantly, this is an investment that is grounded in fundamentals rather than speculation, which is the case with crypto and precious metals.

The Vanguard Utilities ETF offers a much safer option for remaining invested in the stock market than simply tracking the S&P 500, which has proven highly volatile of late.
