Google is retiring the Nest app in favor of the Google Home app. Here's how to transfer your data.

Google is retiring the Nest app and the site Home.Nest.com on Jan. 25, 2027. If you’re a Nest app user and haven’t migrated to the Google Home app, you should check for unmigrated data before deletions begin on March 25, 2027.

Nest app users who started using the Nest app after Aug. 19, 2019, are likely signed in with a Google Account. You’ll be able to switch directly from the Nest app to the Google Home app once the Nest app shuts down.

However, you should know that videos saved in the Nest web clip library won’t transfer automatically to Google Home. Because of this, you should download clips you’d like to keep before the Jan. 25 cutoff. You can use the Download All option in the web clip library.

Aside from manually saving your clips, the biggest issue with the Nest app shutting down is for those who haven’t linked the Nest app to their Google Accounts. Google began prompting existing users to link to their Google Accounts from the Nest app after 2019, while new users were directed to sign up with a Google Account.

Keep in mind that some home members may lose access to the Google Home account once your data is migrated, and may need to be reinvited to the Home.

After you migrate your Nest account to use with Google Home, you can sign in to the Google Home app. You’ll be able to see your devices in Google Home after Jan. 25, though you can also transfer them earlier by accessing the device settings in Google Home.

Google is retiring Google Assistant from smart speakers and displays in favor of Gemini for Home. The full switch is happening in Aug. 2027, when Google Assistant will be removed from these devices and replaced with Gemini.

Google Home users have been able to opt in to use Gemini for Home since its rollout on smart speakers and displays like the Nest Audio, Nest Mini, and Nest Hub Max. Those who haven’t opted in will no longer be able to keep Google Assistant after Aug. 2, 2027.
