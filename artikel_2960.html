Warriors vs. Grizzlies injury report: Draymond Green out, Al Horford returns

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Golden State Warriors are closing out a brutal five-game road trip tonight, and hoping to salvage it with a win against the Memphis Grizzlies. If they were to win, it would be a little bit of payback for the second game of the season, when the Dubs blew a late double-digit lead against the Grizzlies.

Both teams are missing some serious talent, but thankfully, Al Horford is returning for Golden State after not playing in Wednesday's loss to the Dallas Mavericks.

With every passing game, it becomes more and more obvious that the Warriors need Curry back. Unfortunately, there's still not a timeline for his return, though he'll be re-evaluated next week.

Out — Draymond Green (right elbow injury management)

The Dubs are without their best offensive player in Curry and their best defensive player in Green. What could possibly go wrong?

Out — Brandon Williams (left hamstring strain)

Williams will be re-evaluated on Saturday, so there's a chance that this is the last game he misses before returning. That would be nice, as the Dubs are really missing his offense off the bench.

Out — Jimmy Butler III (right ACL repair surgery)Out — Moses Moody (left patellar tendon repair surgery)Out — Kristaps Porziņģis (not with team)

Same news as always with this group. But we're pushing through the season, which means Butler and Moody are getting closer to returning.

Questionable — LJ Cryer (left low back soreness)

Cryer has stepped into a large role — a starting role, even — with Curry and Williams injured. His three-point shooting is critical for the Warriors when Curry is out, so hopefully he's able to play tonight despite the back issue.

The former Warrior has been a revelation in his second season with the Grizzlies, after injuries cost him most of his first year with the team. Despite entering the season averaging just 9.3 points per game, Jerome has positively exploded for 25.4 points, while also dishing 5.3 assists. He scored 28 points when these teams met in October, but will miss his fourth straight game.

Out — Zach Edey (left ankle injury management)

The Warriors are getting a little bit of a gift with Edey's absence, as they struggle to contain size. Edey is one of the largest players in the NBA, and had 10 rebounds and two blocks in the last game against the Warriors, despite only playing 22 minutes.

Out — Scotty Pippen Jr. (lumbar disc herniation)

Pippen's been dealing with a back issue for a while now, as this will be his 11th consecutive absence. Hopefully he's back on the court soon.

Out — GG Jackson II (left 5th metatarsal fracture)

Jackson was having a solid fourth season, but suffered an injury last week that will cost him a few months. He's expected to be re-evaluated sometime around the All-Star break.

Out — Richie Saunders (right knee surgery)

Saunders, who was drafted 32nd overall in June, tore his ACL in February, so he's likely still a ways away from making his NBA debut.

Beef Stew has been solid in his first season in Memphis, averaging 10.9 points despite playing less than 20 minutes per night. He's only grabbing 3.3 rebounds, but even so, his size could be an issue for the Dubs.

Enjoy the game, everyone. It tips off at 5:00 p.m. PT on NBC Sports Bay Area.
