Red Cat Holdings (RCAT -13.78%) reported third quarter revenue of $11.8 million while withdrawing its full-year 2026 revenue guidance due to delayed funding and contract awards. Management stated that the company is expanding its focus toward maritime autonomy through the Blue Ops division and the proposed acquisition of Steyr Motors. The company reported an inventory increase to $127.3 million to support an opportunity pipeline valued at over $2.7 billion across aerial and maritime segments. Operations during the quarter included the consolidation of Teal Drones and FlightWave Aerospace into a single aerial organization to improve manufacturing and sales consistency.
