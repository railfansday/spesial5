Pinterest announces partnership with Microsoft Ads

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The integration will see Pinterest ad placements extended across Microsoft’s partner platforms.

Pinterest has announced a new integration with Microsoft Advertising, which will make Pinterest ad placements available to Microsoft platform users.

Microsoft Ads is primarily focused on placements within Microsoft’s own ecosystem, but it also incorporates a range of partner sites and platforms, enabling advertisers to expand their promotions. And now, Pinterest will be added to that list of partner platforms.

As explained by Pinterest: “Microsoft Advertising customers’ campaigns will have access to Pinterest’s visual search environment through a custom contextual bidding model. Eligible Microsoft Advertising campaigns can access Pinterest's open-auction inventory across home feed, search, and related Pins, expanding opportunities to engage consumers throughout their discovery journeys.”

Pinterest added that targeting for its Microsoft Ads integration will be built on Pinterest search queries, contextual signals, and first-party intent data.

“[T]he model identifies relevant ad placements across Pinterest’s visual search adjacent inventory,” Pinterest said. “The integration uses Pinterest Intelligence, Pinterest’s AI-powered understanding of people’s interests, combining hundreds of billions of connections with visual-understanding technology and behavioral signals to surface personalized interests and anticipate what users may want next.”

Pinterest has been working to expand its ad business in order to boost its revenue potential. Pinterest's ad intake has been growing, but its third quarter revenue intake fell short of Wall Street expectations, while it's also encountered regulatory restrictions in the EU market.

Last month, Pinterest announced a new partnership with Magnite that will make Pinterest’s ad inventory available via multiple ad exchanges, including Yahoo DSP and Amazon Ads DVA+, as well as Google Display and Video 360.

The new partnership with Microsoft will feed even more ads into Pinterest’s system.

Pinterest said its ad inventory will initially be available via Microsoft Ads in North America, Australia and New Zealand.
