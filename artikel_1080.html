It is not yet panic stations at Elland Road, but if Leeds United fail to beat Tottenham Hotspur then their wait for a Premier League win will stretch beyond two months — and that would be concerning.

Daniel Farke's side have lost just two of their nine league games, but a five-match winless run has made the locals restless, even if the manager himself still appears relaxed about the situation.

Their bid for a first victory over Spurs since 2021 has been hampered by injuries to Dominic Calvert-Lewin, Noah Okafor and Anton Stach, with all three players doubtful for the visit of the North London club.

However, James Justin and Gabriel Gudmundsson are available, while key defender Joe Rodon could make his first appearance since August after recovering from a hamstring issue.

While Leeds have begun to fade after a strong start, Spurs have rocketed up the table to ease fears of another season battling at the wrong end of the table.

Spurs could be boosted by the returns from injury of Savio, Tosin Adarabioyo and Lucas Bergvall, while Rodrigo Bentancur is available after serving his three-match suspension for his red card at Manchester United last month.
