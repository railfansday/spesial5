Denmark had only taken points against rock-bottom Wales in the opening four matches of this Nations League campaign, so it is fair to say their 4-1 trouncing of Portugal in Braga was a surprise.

Brian Riemer's side were in dreamland at 4-0 up against the holders with 15 minutes to go, thanks to goals by Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg and Joachim Andersen either side of a Rasmus Hojlund double. They will now be full of confidence heading into this clash with Norway, as they look to claim the point they need to qualify for the quarter-finals.

Mads Hermansen is expected to continue in goal after Filip Jorgensen reportedly left the camp with an unspecified injury.

Norway beat Denmark 3-2 on the opening matchday and will need to repeat that result to finish second in Group A4, after a disappointing campaign that has so far yielded just six points.

Their 3-1 win over Wales on Saturday was more like it from the World Cup quarter-finalists, with Erling Haaland among the goalscorers in Oslo, but if they fail to follow it up with another victory then they will go into the League A/B play-offs next March.

Norway captain Martin Odegaard played through illness at the weekend but appears to have recovered, so should be in line to start in Copenhagen.

The visitors need to win, which should make for an entertaining contest with a few goals.
