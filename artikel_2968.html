Fantastic News for CoreWeave Stock Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Wall Street may have been wrong about the company after all.

Despite starting the year on a strong note, shares of CoreWeave (CRWV +24.71%) are down by nearly 13% year-to-date, as of writing. What's going on with the company? On the one hand, CoreWeave is cashing in on massive demand for artificial intelligence (AI) compute. However, there are several issues with the company, including the massive debt it is now carrying on its balance sheet. That said, CoreWeave recently reported its third-quarter results, and they were full of bullish signs for the company. Let's dig in.

The market had high expectations for CoreWeave ahead of its third-quarter earnings, and the neocloud company blew them out of the water. Revenue increased by 168% year over year to $3.7 billion. The top line grew much faster than operating expenses, resulting in strong growth in operating income.

CoreWeave's operating income for the period was $207 million, substantially better than the $52 million recorded in the year-ago period. Its operating margin also increased to 6%, up from the 4% reported in the prior-year quarter. CoreWeave remains unprofitable, and the net loss per share widened to $1.27 in the period, compared to $0.22 in the prior-year quarter.

However, the fact that CoreWeave's revenue growth accelerated for the first time in a while suggests there is still strong demand for the company's services, which helps justify the massive spending contributing to the worsening bottom-line numbers. We can also look at CoreWeave's revenue backlog, which was $132.5 billion for the period, up 138% year over year.

CoreWeave also raised its guidance for the fiscal year 2026. It now expects revenue between $13 billion and $13.3 billion, up from its previous range of $12.4 billion to $13.2 billion. The company is now also projecting it will finish the year with a revenue run rate of $20 billion to $21 billion; CoreWeave's trailing-12-month revenue is $9.89 billion. It's not surprising that the stock soared on the heels of another excellent quarterly performance.

Can CoreWeave maintain its momentum? The company faces at least three major risks. First, during the recent U.S. midterm elections, voters overwhelmingly elected officials who ran on pumping the brakes on data center expansion. If regulatory uncertainty in the AI industry increases over the next few years, it could weigh on CoreWeave's financial results.

Second, the company's significant debt and net losses remain an issue. Third, there is customer concentration. According to some reports, Microsoft (MSFT -2.26%) could opt to work with fewer neocloud partners. This single customer accounted for about 67% of CoreWeave's revenue last year, so if Microsoft cuts ties with CoreWeave, it would be catastrophic for the company.

Can CoreWeave perform well despite these problems? In my view, it could, although the stock remains somewhat risky. Consider the first risk. CoreWeave's management does not expect a significant impact from increased regulatory scrutiny on the business. And the company has also been building capacity outside the U.S., which helps mitigate its regulatory exposure in the country.

Second, CoreWeave's significant debt is necessary for the company to continue meeting demand for its services. In fact, CoreWeave is largely sold out through 2027, according to management, while there is already strong demand for 2028. What about customer concentration? If Microsoft decides to work with fewer neoclouds, it could be very bad for CoreWeave, or it could be very good.

CoreWeave is one of the largest neoclouds, and if it remains in Microsoft's smaller list of partners as the tech giant continues to demand more services, that would mean a higher budget distributed among fewer providers, potentially leading to higher revenue for CoreWeave. Customer concentration remains a meaningful risk for investors to monitor, but for now, CoreWeave appears likely to continue performing well despite it. And even after its post-earnings jump, the stock looks attractive, particularly for investors comfortable with volatility.
