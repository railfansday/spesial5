Ohio State vs. Nebraska score predictions from Buckeyes Wire staff

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

After getting a win it absolutely had to have against Northwestern last Saturday, the Ohio State (7-3, 5-2 Big Ten) travels to Nebraska to take on a Cornhuskers team (6-4, 3-4) that has the makeup to give the Buckeyes everything they can handle.

If the Buckeyes are going to have any shot of still getting to Indy for the Big Ten Championship Game and have an outside shot of still making the College Football Playoff, they'll have to execute several keys to the game, especially on offense. But it won't be easy. Nebraska has a good running game and a solid defense that could pose some issues for the Scarlet and Gray on the road.

Before every Ohio State football game, we like to ask our writers to predict the outcome of the game, and Phil, Mark, and Alec are doing it again for this one. What does each say? Are all three in on Ohio State winning, or will a road contest prove to be too much for freshman quarterback Luke Fahey and his teammates?

Ohio State must keep winning if it wants to keep its slim chances of winning the Big Ten and making the College Football Playoff field alive, and we're seeing progress each week. The defense played much better, and quarterback Luke Fahey continues to gain confidence each time out. It'll be a Donnybrook, and it won't be easy, but Ohio State will do enough on the defensive side of the ball and feed Jeremiah Smith enough to pull out a win.

This feels like a game where Ohio State should roll, but I don't think it will be that easy. On the defensive front, the Buckeyes and Cornhuskers look very similar statistically. However, Nebraska just lost its best cornerback for the rest of the season which should allow Jeremiah Smith more room to roam. But it's the other side of the ball that concerns me the most. Quarterback Anthony Colandrea is a true dual threat and leads the Big Ten with 447 rushing yards from that position. Facing a tough defense and a mobile QB isn't always a great recipe for Ohio State. I see this one being closer than Buckeye Nation will be comfortable with.

Ohio State still has some life after the last few weeks, but there's still a vibe around this program that lacks confidence. Going into Nebraska, confidence is key. Despite the recent rough patch for the Huskers, there is talent and a lot of skill on both sides of the ball that can attack Ohio State in multiple areas. Anthony Colandrea has been inconsistent, but has the ability to break down opposing defenses with his legs. The Buckeyes have struggled defensively and have significant breakdowns that allow for explosive plays - that can't happen with Colandrea under center. Defensively, the Huskers rank in the top-25 in sacks and top-15 in tackles for loss.

Can OSU's ever-changing offensive line make an impact? If so, it'll bode well for Luke Fahey, who has shown the ability to make some big plays and dissect defenses. If not, it's going to be a long day for an offense still looking for a consistent rushing attack. Look for a heavy dose of quick hitters to Jeremiah Smith either way. I don't see OSU having that 'it' factor to go win this road game.

Phil Harrison | Ohio State 24, Nebraska 20

Mark Russell | Ohio State 24, Nebraska 20
