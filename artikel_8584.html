Absolutely. By far the bigger problem in the US at large is households not engaging in enough delayed gratification. Again it is very much a Goldilocks situation in theory, and I agree in practice far more US households err on the side of too little spending discipline. And I agree a lot of that is driven by a more common sort of peer competition, sometimes known as conspicuous consumption, aka keeping up with the Joneses.

So it is a niche problem to be worried about too much delayed gratification. However, my sense is this particular forum does a decent job attracting a very disproportionate percentage of people who are arguably erring in the more rare direction, meaning by actually delaying too much gratification.
