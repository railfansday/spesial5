Serie A: Napoli vs. Torino – Probable line-ups and where to watch on TV

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Napoli are back in Serie A action on Saturday evening following the recent international break, and pre-match reports suggest that Kevin De Bruyne will not be included in Massimiliano Allegri’s starting line-up.

Napoli return to league action in desperate need of results after a disappointing start to the campaign that has left them in 10th place on 15 points after 11 matches. They are already 14 points shy of the league leaders and nine points off the Champions League spots.

They won just one of their four league games heading into the international break, albeit in a tough run of fixtures that included draws against Roma and Lazio and a defeat against Juventus.

Ignacio abate, meanwhile, has called for consistency from Torino after winning three games in a row in October, before picking up just one point from their next three heading into the international break.

The visitors are just one point and one position behind Napoli on Saturday morning and will be eyeing an opportunity to leapfrog their opponents into the top half of the Serie A table.

Earlier this week, it was confirmed that goalkeeper Alex Meret will be out for the remainder of the year and beyond with a fractured vertebra in his back, which means that Vanja Milinkovic-Savic is set to start against his former club.

According to Sky Sport Italia, De Bruyne, who has been linked with a January move away from Napoli, is expected to start from the bench. Scott McTominay, Billy Gilmour and Andre-Frank Zambo Anguissa are likely to start in midfield instead.

It could also be a 3-5-2 for Napoli this evening, with Antonio Vergara in contention to partner Rasmus Hojlund in attack.

Napoli (3-5-2): Milinkovic-Savic; Beukema, Rrahmani, Marin; Politano, Anguissa, Gilmour, McTominay, Spinazzola; Vergara, Hojlund.

Torino (3-5-2): Perri; Coco, Ismajli, Comuzzo; Belghali, Casadei, Mandragora, Braganca, Cacciamani; Adams, Simeone.

Today’s game will be available to watch live in the UK exclusively on DAZN. Supporters in the USA are able to tune in via Paramount+.
