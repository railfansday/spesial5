Calhanoglu forced off injured in Atalanta-Inter as Carnesecchi pulls out in warm-up

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Inter have been hit by an injury to Hakan Calhanoglu. The Turkish midfielder was forced off in the first half of Sunday night’s Serie A clash with Atalanta in Bergamo.

According to SOS Fanta, the Turkish midfielder stopped and immediately asked to be substituted. The problem is muscular, in the flexor of his right thigh, and updates on his condition and recovery time will follow after tests in the coming hours.

Petar Sučić replaced him in the 34th minute, a minute after Francesco Pio Esposito headed in his second goal of the night to make it 2-0 to the Nerazzurri.

Follow all the action from the match as it happens, and catch up with the rest of today’s matches, on our Live Blog.

Calhanoglu had been one of Inter’s liveliest players before going off. He almost scored in the third minute, but his shot was deflected behind for a corner by Sead Kolašinac.

The timing is bad for Cristian Chivu as Inter host VfB Stuttgart in the Champions League on Wednesday at 21:00 CET [20:00 GMT], then welcome Genoa to San Siro on Saturday. Piotr Zieliński is already listed as doubtful, which leaves Chivu short of experienced options in midfield.

Atalanta had their own fitness problem before kick-off. Marco Carnesecchi suffered a knee problem during the warm-up and had to withdraw, despite being named in the starting line-up. Marco Sportiello took his place in Maurizio Sarri’s XI. Like Calhanoglu, Carnesecchi will undergo further tests to establish the extent of the injury and how long he will be out.

Calhanoglu will leave at the end of the season. Inter won’t offer 5m+ over his injuries, which means the end to a circle. (Best defender Acerbi and midfielder are/will be gone, Barella and Lautaro are fading). Very rare a team could replace their best players in transition period.
