61% of All US PS5 Owners Played GTA 6 on Launch Day, Averaging 8 Hours

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

You’ve seen the staggering 32.5 million pre-launch sales, but now enjoy some console specific data on GTA 6’s release.

Analyst Mat Piscatella of Circana reports that in the US, 61% of PS5 owners played Rockstar’s latest release on launch day. That’s an utterly astounding statistic.

But here’s the really staggering part – said players averaged about 8.5 hours of gameplay on 19th November. Averaged!

According to Circana's Player Engagement Tracker, Grand Theft Auto VI had a massive launch (surprise!). For players active on November 19th, 61% of US PlayStation 5 and 56% of global Xbox players engaged with Grand Theft Auto VI. PS5 GTA VI players averaged 513 minutes that day, Xbox players 494.

That’s an utterly flabbergasting figure – I almost can’t believe it. I’d love to know the impact on global productivity, as I’m sure anecdotally we all know someone who took a sick day.

Anyway, this is jaw-dropping data and I’m sure there’s a lot more to come.

Credit to Sony, by the way, who’s kept the PS5 online and running flawlessly throughout this enormous influx of traffic. Preload went smoothly, and I haven’t really seen anyone complaining they had issues this week.
