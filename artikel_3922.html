Call of Duty's Influence Is Fading Fast as Modern Warfare 4 Fails to Make US Top 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

There's been an awful lot of debate about the overall quality and lasting commercial success of Call of Duty across the last few years, with many arguing that the once unstoppable series is steadily losing its edge.

As such, this latest report from Circana won't make for easy reading if you're Xbox or Activision.

According to the ever-informative Mat Piscatella, the recently released Modern Warfare 4 was the best selling game of October in the US.

However, despite a strong debut and a mostly positive reception — especially with regards to its single player campaign — the big budget shooter has failed to break into the top 10 best selling titles of 2026 so far.

For Call of Duty, such an absence would have been unthinkable just a few short years ago; it really does suggest that it's struggled to match the kind of sales figures we've come to expect of the franchise.

Case in point: the current-gen port of Black Ops 2 remains on 2026's top 10 list.

October 2026 U.S. Video Game Market Highlights Thread from Circana - Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 was the best-selling game of October, marking the 19th consecutive year that a Call of Duty franchise release has been the best-selling game of its debut month.

Of course, there's still time for Modern Warfare 4 to make up some ground with the holidays right around the corner, but this seems to be a continuation of the downward trend that we saw with last year's very poorly rated Black Ops 7.

Indeed, it's possible that the Black Ops 7 backlash has negatively impacted Modern Warfare 4's appeal — and that's not the kind of spiral that Activsion will want to find itself in.

Elsewhere, Gears of War: E-Day — which was famously cancelled on PS5 just weeks ahead of launch — managed second place for October, and the eye-watering Star Wars: Galactic Racer entered in third.

Another new release, the largely fantastic Final Fantasy Resonance, landed in sixth — which feels like a win for Square Enix given the flood of games that came out last month.
