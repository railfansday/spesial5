We're now just over a week away from the launch of GTA 6, the first new game from Rockstar since Red Dead Redemption 2, and the first new entry in the Grand Theft Auto series since GTA 5. It's been a long, long wait for the studio's latest big game, but it seems Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick is already willing to tease Rockstar's next project.

"Rockstar has completed the development of GTA 6," Zelnick tells investors during Take-Two's latest financial results call. "That may not come as a surprise to you, since we're 10 days away from the release. They have done their very best work, and it’s going to come to consumers soon. They're in the middle of their marketing campaign, which also, unsurprisingly, is fully baked. At this point, it’s the job of the organization to make sure that we do an A+ job, technically, in delivering this product to consumers."

Zelnick concludes: "Then, of course, Rockstar will focus on what comes next, and they'll announce that in the fullness of time."

So… what does come next for Rockstar? The obvious guess would be a new version of GTA Online. The existing online game generates unfathomable profits, so a follow-up would seem inevitable even if it hadn't leaked as part of Rockstar's ongoing union dispute. But Rockstar and Take-Two have resolutely refused to even hint at what the next generation of GTA Online will entail.

During the financial call Q&A, Zelnick deftly dodged multiple questions alluding to GTA Online. He's not worried about players running out of content in GTA 6, for example, because "there's so much to be done in all things Grand Theft Auto."
