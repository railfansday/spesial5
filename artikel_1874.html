Arsenal: Bruno Guimaraes sent Newcastle warning ahead of first return after £75m transfer

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Harvey Barnes has warned Bruno Guimaraes that his return to St James’ Park with Arsenal is exactly what Newcastle need to spark themselves into form.

Guimaraes will be back at his former club for the first time since leaving in the summer to join the Gunners in a £75million move.

The Brazilian enjoyed his previous trip up to that part of the country earlier in the season, when he came off the bench to score against Sunderland and celebrate exuberantly in front of the fans who had been giving him a hostile reception.

It remains to be seen what kind of welcome Guimaraes gets at St James’ Park on Saturday evening.

Newcastle are in need of a positive result, having won only one of their five matches on home soil this season.

Arsenal, meanwhile, sit five points clear at the top of the Premier League table and have picked up 27 points from a possible 30.

However, despite that daunting challenge, Barnes believes Newcastle will rise to the occasion and ensure it is an unhappy return for their former team-mate.

“In some ways I think it’s a really good game for us,” Barnes told Sky Sports.

“Obviously you’re testing yourself against the best and I think you’ve seen over the years when these challenges do come along, especially at St James’ Park, we step up to them and really meet them head on.

“I feel like it’s the kind of game we need coming off the break.

“It’s football. People leave and come back, Bruno is coming back in a different shirt. Hopefully he won’t be happy at the end of the game with the result.

“It is what it is. We’ll just go there and do our job.”
