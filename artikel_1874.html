Bayern Munich board member claims ‘no club would buy’ Jamal Musiala due to health condition

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Bayern Munich’s supervisory board member Karl-Heinz Rummenigge has suggested Jamal Musiala may be offered a pay cut due to his health situation and claims no club would buy the midfielder ‘under these conditions’.

The 23-year-old collapsed in two different games in the space of four days during Bayern’s pre-season campaign in the summer.

Musiala later revealed that he had been suffering from ‘brief, temporary – but treatable – absence seizures resulting from a neurological dysfunction’.

The midfielder also suffered a thigh injury in September and made his return from his two-month absence on Friday night as Bayern drew 1-1 with FC Koln.

Speaking after the game, Rummenigge insisted Bayern must consider their financial situation with Musiala on a contract which is worth in excess of €25 million (£20.9m) a year and expires in 2030.

‘What we know unfortunately suggests that it will be difficult to reach the level he has so often shown here in the stadium,’ Rummenigge told Sky Germany.

‘We need to talk to the family, the player, and the agency, because then these sums of money, sorry, with all due respect, are no longer feasible.

‘I cannot imagine that Jamal wants to leave the club or that any other club would buy him under these conditions.

‘We are all interested in his return, ideally in top form. But what happens if that isn’t the case? Then we still have to think about the finances.

‘The discussion hasn’t taken place yet. These are just ideas at this stage.’

Speaking earlier this month, Bayern’s honorary president, Uli Hoeness, also raised questions about Musiala’s contract due to his health issue.

‘We are facing a dilemma in this case, as he is suffering from an illness that cannot simply be cured, but it can be controlled to a certain extent with the use of medication,’ Hoeness said.

‘But whether he ​will have the same performance potential that he had… imagine if Bayern were now to question ‌his ⁠contract and say that what he is capable of delivering now is no longer what is in the contract.’

When asked about the assessment of Musiala’s by the club’s hierarchy, Bayern head coach Vincent Kompany said: ‘I have a very clear role in this club, as a coach, I have to believe in my players and that we can achieve something together.

‘If Jamal finds his level and form again, and we manage to do that together, then everyone wins – Bayern, the fans, and I believe German football as a whole. Because Jamal is a special player.

‘That is my role, quite clearly. If a coach does something different or says something different, then there is a problem, in my opinion.’
