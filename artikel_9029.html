A View From the Easel: Leslie Lewis Sigler

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

“The light and studio interior are literally reflected in my work.”

Welcome to the 363rd installment of A View From the Easel, a series in which artists reflect on their workspace. This week, Leslie Lewis Sigler paints spoons and their trick-mirror images in the changing California light.

Want to take part? Check out our submission guidelines and share a bit about your studio with us through this form! All mediums and workspaces are welcome, including your home studio.

How long have you been working in this space?

Nine months. We built the studio in our backyard last year. I had a studio in our garage prior to moving into this space.
