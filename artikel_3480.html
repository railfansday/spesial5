iOS 27.3’s redesigned Apple Health app requires newer iPhone model

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

iOS 27.3 will introduce an all-new Apple Health experience when it ships next month, but you’ll need an AI-compatible iPhone to get it. Here’s why.

When iOS 27.3 launches next month, it will include a variety of upgrades for Apple TV, Apple Music, Siri AI, iCloud+, the iPhone Duo, and more.

Another tentpole addition is an all-new Apple Health app, which was first unveiled back in September when new Apple Watch models debuted.

The Health app is getting a full revamp in iOS 27.3 that’s meant to be more personalized and informative than ever. New features include an Insights tab, Longevity tab, educational videos, at-home movement evaluations, and more.

But for many iPhone users who update to iOS 27.3, the Health app might remain largely unchanged. Why? Because the new experience relies on Apple Intelligence, so you’ll need an AI-compatible iPhone to get it.

That means an iPhone 15 Pro or newer is required.

If you have an older iPhone model, or simply have chosen not to enable Apple Intelligence on your device, you’ll miss out on the new Health features. They’ll also only be available in select languages initially.

These forthcoming Health upgrades join the 20+ other new Apple Intelligence features that recently debuted in iOS 27.
