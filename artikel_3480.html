Apple TV announces TIMBERLAKE series coming soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Apple TV (now $6.99/month for limited time) has just announced a four-part documentary event series on Justin Timberlake that’s premiering next month. Here are the details on TIMBERLAKE.

Today Apple TV announced a new documentary series revolving around Justin Timberlake.

The series, titled TIMBERLAKE, offers “an expansive portrait of the life and career of multi-hyphenate artist and icon Justin Timberlake.” It spans three decades of music and pop culture and includes exclusive new interviews with a variety of friends, family, and collaborators. Timberlake himself discusses “the triumphs that made him a global success, his personal perspective beyond the headlines and the challenges he’s faced behind the scenes.”

TIMBERLAKE premieres Friday, December 11 with the first two episodes, followed by the third episode on Friday, December 18 and the finale on Wednesday, December 23.
