Teresa Giudice Claims 'Well-Known TV Actress' Was 'So Rude' to Her in New Book

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Teresa Giudice might be the self-proclaimed queen of New Jersey, but that hasn’t protected her from being slighted by A-list celebrities from time to time.

Teresa, 54, recalled in her new memoir, The Queen of New Jersey, that unlike meeting the “gracious” Taylor Swift at Coachella Music Festival, “Not every celebrity interaction goes quiet as well.”

She recounted an alleged incident in the book involving an unnamed TV actress that transpired shortly after she rose to fame in 2009 on The Real Housewives of New Jersey.

“[I] was once instructed by my PR team to pose with a well-known TV actress for a photo,” Teresa wrote, noting that the women were both in Los Angeles for an appearance with TV host Mario Lopez. “After we took the photo, I overheard her telling her PR people, ‘Why are you making me take a picture with that woman?’”

The Bravolebrity remembered, “The tone of her voice was so surprising,” writing, “Look, I get if she didn’t know who I was, but to me, that’s just so rude.”

Teresa didn’t identify the actress, but revealed that years later she was back in L.A. having dinner with her husband, Luis “Louie” Ruelas, when a fan came in and asked her for a picture.

“It’s part of this life, and one I happen to enjoy. We took a photo together and chatted for a minute, and then she left us to be seated at her own table,” she shared. “Ten minutes later, who walks in? The actress who had definitely not wanted to take a picture with me all that time ago backstage at Mario Lopez’s show.”

Teresa recalled, “We made brief eye contact — there was definitely no need to say hi — as she was shown to her table.”

The cherry on top for Teresa was when she realized that the actress’ dinner date was the fan who asked her for a photo moments earlier.

“I hope she enjoyed feigning interest as her friend showed her the pictures we’d taken just moments before,” Teresa jokingly wrote.

The reality star added, “I’ll always have respect for a celebrity who doesn’t forget where they came from or who made them. There are a lot of people in this business who can’t stand to stop and pose for a photo or sign something for a fan. Like, really? You think you’re such a big deal that you can’t spare a moment for the very audience whose attention made you who you are?”

The actress in question has not been identified, but there are publicly available photos that show Teresa filming a segment with Lopez, 53, in May 2011 for Extra, which fans speculate could be when the alleged slight took place. No other famous individuals are visible in the pictures.

In addition to juicy tales of celebrity run-ins, Teresa’s new book promises to tell-all — including the highs and lows of her RHONJ break, her first husband Joe Giudice’s deportation and her road to a second love. (Teresa will be back for season 15 of RHONJ, premiering in January, following the show’s hiatus.)

“Only a true queen can rise and fall and rise again, sweetheart. Take notes,” the book tease reads.

The memoir will reflect on Teresa’s “two tumultuous decades in the public eye,” as well as her “biggest triumphs, heartbreaks, feuds, and controversies of her life with newfound perspective,” according to the brief synopsis.

“Raw, funny, emotional, and unapologetic, The Queen of New Jersey is the story of a woman who has been cast as everything from villain to victim — but refuses to let anyone else write her legacy,” the publisher adds. “After all, if the crown fits, wear it.”

The Queen of New Jersey is now available for purchase.
