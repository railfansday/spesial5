How to change your criminal profile in GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

To improve your criminal profile in Grand Theft Auto 6, you must play nice. Since it's GTA 6, though, "playing nice" means shooting folks in the knee rather than in the head. You'll still break the law, but you'll be more polite about it.

Here's how to keep the violence to a minimum and reach a professional criminal profile in GTA 6.

The reputation system in GTA 6 is called the criminal profile. Jason's and Lucia's criminal profiles indicate how merciful or violent they are. Make no mistake; a professional (merciful) character still commits crimes, but they won't cause more harm than necessary to secure success.

During the main story, you won't notice much difference in gameplay based on your criminal profile, but the game promises it'll have a "lasting impact." In other words, you should expect your criminal profile to affect the ending of GTA 6. The game also warns you that an "irredeemable" criminal profile may have irreversible consequences, so you might want to avoid that.

There are five criminal profile levels in GTA 6. Here's what they mean:

If your character is "professional" or "hardened," they'll receive focus points for hitting non-lethal enemy weak spots. This includes hands, elbows, knees, and feet (which will appear yellow in focus mode). If your character is "violent" or "psycho," they'll receive focus points for hitting lethal weak spots, which include the enemy's head and heart region. Lethal weak spots appear red while using focus mode.

To check your character's current criminal profile, use the Jason & Lucia menu or hold right on the D-pad. Beware that criminal profile scores are counted separately for each character. Jason can be "psycho" while Lucia is "professional" at the same time.

Criminal profile levels in GTA 6 work like a scale; the less aggressive your behavior, the more you'll move toward "professional." This means that changing your criminal profile for better or worse takes time. If you're currently "professional" and go on a killing spree, you won't drop to "violent" straight away, and vice versa.
