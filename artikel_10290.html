94% of the world’s biggest songs have one surprising thing in common

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

While there is still no recipe for becoming a globally recognized musician, there is one thing that is consistently getting artists on the charts: TikTok.

TikTok’s recent 2026 Music Impact Report found that 94% of songs that entered the Billboard top 200 chart this year went viral on TikTok first. The link is stronger than before: in 2024, TikTok found that 84% of songs on the charts had gone viral on the platform first,  up ten percentage points in two years.

TikTok virality also affects how long a song stays on the chart. The report also found that tracks that went viral on the platform stayed on the charts for four times longer than those that didn’t catch a moment of popularity on the platform. For instance, songs that had a viral moment on TikTok would spend eight weeks on Billboard charts, as opposed to just two weeks for non-viral hits.

But the charts are not the only metric artists use to measure their success and growth, their performance on streaming platforms matters too. The report also found that TikTok virality influences streaming, with 74% of the fastest-growing artists on streaming platforms saw their streams rise alongside their views on TikTok.

Sienna Spiro is among the breakthrough artists who rose to fame through the platform, starting her career singing songs to a small audience on TikTok, eventually landing on the VMA stage this year singing her hit song Great Expectations.

While a relatively small artist just a year ago, she found a viral moment in her song Die on This Hill, with millions of fans using the sound for their TikToks, giving her recognition beyond the platform. 

Additionally, the platform’s new “add to music app” feature, which lets users connect their TikTok to their streaming service to automatically save songs from videos to their playlists, contributed to Spiro’s streaming success. According to the report, millions of users used the feature to save the single to their music apps. Spiro’s viral hit led her to enter the top 20 of the Billboard Hot 100, where it stayed for 13 weeks.
