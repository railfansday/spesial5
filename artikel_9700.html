21 Best Cozy Fall Baking Recipes

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Apples, pumpkin, and cinnamon everything! Fall baking recipes that are cozy, warm, and delicious.

Published by Lindsay Ostrom on September 10, 2026

Fall baking season is officially here! (The best baking season, to be clear.) There’s no better feeling than a trip to the apple orchard that ends with cinnamon sugar apple cake in the oven, or stocking up on cans of pumpkin knowing that pumpkin muffins are in your future. I LOVE YOU, FALL!

There’s something for breakfast, something for snacking, something for lunchboxes, and something for cozy late-night TV time on the couch. Something for everyone!

Hoping you find some good fall coziness with one of these. Here’s to lots of fall baking! ❤️

My one-and-only apple crisp recipe! Tender jammy honeycrisp apples under a blanket of buttery brown sugar oat topping. The perfect fall treat!

This simple cinnamon sugar apple cake is light and fluffy, loaded with fresh apples, and topped with a crunchy cinnamon sugar layer!

These Apple Muffins are EVERYTHING. Wholesome and nutritious, moist and dense, perfectly but not overly sweet, and honestly divine with a little butter and honey.

Pumpkin muffins are getting a lightly crunchy, sparkly cinnamon sugar topping, and then stuffed to the brim with maple cream cheese whipped cream. YUM.

Pumpkin Granola that is both salty and sweet! Made with REAL PUMPKIN along with oats, coconut, almonds, pistachios, maple syrup, cinnamon, and olive oil. My favorite fall granola! ♡ Adapted from the Last Granola Recipe by my friend The Faux Martha.

These Pumpkin Muffins! Made with oats instead of flour and real maple syrup instead of refined sugar. And everyone loves them, including meeee!

Soft, buttery bites coated in gooey pumpkin caramel sauce and a drizzle of white chocolate. My family loves this pumpkin caramel monkey bread!

Sweet Cinnamon Rolls! Made with pillowy brioche dough and cinnamon sugar tucked into every nook and cranny. This makes a BIG batch of dough that you can divide and save for later…or make extra all for yourself!

Maple Quinoa Granola! Golden, crunchy, naturally sweetened clustery granola with speckles of quinoa, nuts, coconut, oats, chia, and other delicious, good-for-you things!

Browned butter and brown sugar caramelly goodness, crispy edges, barely thick and soft centers, and melty little puddles of chocolate chips.

These layered bars are so simple! Loaded with homemade caramel and apples. Caramely apple layer tucked in by a brown sugar-y crust on both sides.

Five Minute Single Serving Apple Crisp with a wholesome oat, pecan, and coconut oil topping. Made super fast in the microwave! No oven required.

Pumpkin Biscuits with Cinnamon Apples for you! Warm and gooey cinnamon brown sugar apples piled between fluffy pumpkin biscuits that YOU made. Pile on the creamy twists of whipped maple cream cheese and yes, this is exactly the over-the-top brunch/dessert/snack you need.

Pumpkin Energy Bites! The BEST easy fall snack. Made with simple ingredients like oats, pumpkin, almond butter, maple syrup, and pumpkin seeds.

Creamy dulce de leche layered with espresso-soaked ladyfingers in one delightful slice! This Dulce De Leche Tiramisu is my new favorite dessert.

These healthy gingerbread muffins are made with coconut oil, whole wheat flour, and real maple syrup. So puffy and so delicious!

Pumpkin Cake! A perfectly dense, moist cake with a crunchy sweet brown sugar streusel. YUM!

These Healthy Maple Glazed Pumpkin Muffins use whole grains, less oil, and less sugar to make a jumbo fall muffin. Yummm.

These Pecan Pie Energy Bites are a cute lil’ 7-ingredient miracle! Made with dates, pecans, oats, almond butter, and maple syrup, you’re going to be snackin’ on these all. day. long.

This is a recipe for Raisin Bran Muffins from my very best friend. Perfect hot out of the oven with melted butter and honey.

This is an easy, classic, super moist pumpkin bread recipe made with olive oil, pumpkin, and cinnamon, and topped with crunchy turbinado sugar.

Keep the coziness going with a few favorite fall dinners! Comforting, crowd-friendly, and the best meals to make when the weather starts getting chilly.
