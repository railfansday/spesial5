Netflix Dates Jamie Foxx’s Olympic Boxing Drama ‘Fight For ’84’

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Netflix has announced a release date for Fight for ’84, its true-life sports drama starring Jamie Foxx.

The film releases globally on February 19, 2027. Check out a first-look still of Foxx above.

Fight for ’84 takes place after the U.S. Olympic Boxing Team is tragically killed in a plane crash in 1980. A new coach, played by Foxx, is then brought in to rebuild the team from scratch and lead them to victory at the ’84 Olympics, winning the most medals in Olympic boxing history.

Directed by Andrés Baiz, a Colombian filmmaker known for work on shows like Griselda and Narcos, the film shot in New Jersey last year. Its cast also includes Malachi Beasley, Dan Perrault, Will Chase, Algee Smith, Tre McBride, Xavier Mills, Mitchell Edwards, Al-Shabazz Jabateh, Jamir Cope, Adrian Martinez, Javier Bolaños, Robert Barbour, Amanda Warren, Adeola Role, and Shea Whigham.

The script is by Oscar nominees Andrea Berloff and John Gatins and Andy Weiss. Eric Newman produced for Grand Electric, alongside Bryan Unkeless for Night Owl, Foxx, and Michael W. Abbott for A Move Films Production. Exec producers included Weiss for A WeBros Entertainment Production, Henry Tillman, and Datari Turner. Watch on Deadline
