It’s 50 years since the original movie captured our hearts and fighting spirit in 1976, but the movie’s motivating message; that we can all push ourselves to be more than we thought we could, is still as relevant today as it ever was. With more than 40,000 runners expected to descend in Philadelphia, each of the three Rocky Run categories have sold out.

The first official Rocky Run began in 2014 with heavyweight beginnings, attracting 7,000 runners, but it has gone from strength to strength since. And, while organizers had expected around 30,000 competitors to descend in Philly this year, the final number will clock in at an incredible 40,000.

Those who are ready and set for November 14, 2026, have a choice of three categories, catering to different abilities, so that anyone can “Go For it.”

The half marathon actually combines the 5K and 10-mile runs, meeting the 13.1-mile requirement, and besides channeling your inner Rocky Balboa and going the distance, there are cool medals to be won. The 5K finisher medal celebrates 50 years of Rocky, while the 10-mile finisher medal depicts The Italian Stallion’s triumphant celebration at the top of the famous steps leading up to the Philadelphia Museum of Art. The 13.1-mile finishers is a large, heavyweight design that has “50” emblazoned on one side, while the other side has a vintage image of Rocky in training.

In addition to the Rocky Run, Philadelphia continues to show its love for the most famous fictional boxer in history. The city held its first “Rocky Day,” on December 3, 2023. No doubt, the inspirational story of one man going the distance continues to resonate around the world.

“Once again it’s a magical time of the year, The Rocky Run,” said Stallone in a recent social media post to promote the activities on November 14. Of course, Sly has been heavily active during Rocky’s 50th celebrations. He released his appropriately titled autobiography, “The Steps” and even brought back the man behind Rocky’s signature soundtrack, Bill Conti, for a live concert in New York to go along with a screening of the film. And for those, who still can’t get enough of Sylvester Stallone’s creation, the story of how a struggling actor defied the odds to become the star of one of cinema’s most successful franchises is also set to hit theaters, with “I Play Rocky” starring Anthony Ippolito as the struggling boxer given a once in a lifetime shot, and Stephan James playing Apollo Creed.

As to whether Sly will make an appearance at this record-breaking Rocky Run, fans are certainly hoping that Philadelphia’s favorite son will tread his old stomping ground once again.
