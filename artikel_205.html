ICE Deportation Flight Carrying Iranians Turns Back and Lands in Arizona

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Iranian immigrants in the plane were scheduled to be flown to Pakistan and then transferred to Iran, lawyers for the group said.

By Pranav BaskarHamed AleazizSalman Masood and Zach Montague

A U.S. deportation flight from Arizona carrying Iranians bound for Iran turned around midair and landed back in Arizona early Tuesday morning, according to a legal defense group and a flight-tracking monitor.

The Iranians on the flight were meant to be transferred to Iran from neighboring Pakistan, but the flight turned around after Pakistan abruptly declined to accept them, according to a relative of a detainee who said an Iranian official shared that information, and a U.S. official. Both of them spoke on the condition of anonymity to discuss confidential information.

By Tuesday afternoon, the Iranian immigrants were returned from the plane to the Arizona immigration detention facility, the relative said. He added that there were at least nine people in the group.

The Trump administration is planning at least two operations this month to deport immigrants to countries that it does not have diplomatic relations with — Afghanistan and Iran — using Pakistan as the point of transfer. The plans have raised questions among immigration rights groups and lawyers about Pakistan’s role in facilitating deportations to countries where many immigrants fear persecution if they are sent back.

When asked about the deportations to Afghanistan via Pakistan at a news conference on Friday, Sajjad Haider Khan, a spokesman for Pakistan’s foreign ministry, said the arrangement was “under consideration.” He added that Pakistan is hopeful that a “mutually agreeable way forward will be reached.”

According to court filings by the Iranian American Legal Defense Fund, a group of Iranians who are detained at an Immigration and Customs Enforcement facility in Arizona were told by an Iranian government official over the weekend that they were going to be put on a flight to Pakistan with “onward transfer to Iran.” The legal defense fund has filed a lawsuit on behalf of Iranian detainees.
