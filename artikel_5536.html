McDonald's beat earnings estimates as U.S. restaurant traffic fell

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Free daily briefing on global business news.

U.S. comparable sales fell 0.2% in the quarter, while global comparable sales rose 1.5% on growth in international markets

McDonald's $MCD reported third-quarter results on Wednesday, with adjusted earnings of $3.57 a share, up 11%, as U.S. comparable sales fell 0.2% on lower customer traffic. McDonald's stock rose about 2% in premarket trading.

Adjusted earnings topped the $3.38 a share analysts expected, according to CNBC. Revenue came in at $7.16 billion, a 1% increase. That missed the $7.29 billion analysts expected.

Net income was $2.49 billion, equal to $3.51 per share, versus $2.28 billion, equal to $3.18 per share, in the year-ago period. Operating income rose 7%, and systemwide sales rose 4% to more than $38 billion, the company said.

In the U.S., comparable guest counts declined, and higher average check growth offset most of the drop, the company said. Visits to McDonald's locations were down 4.6% year over year in the quarter, according to Reuters, citing data from Placer.ai. Reuters also reported that the drop snapped a streak of five straight growth quarters for U.S. comparable sales.

"In the U.S., we're encouraged by improvement as the quarter progressed following our intervention on value," Chairman and CEO Chris Kempczinski said in a statement.

Chief Financial Officer Ian Border warned investors during a Sept. 23 event that U.S. sales were likely to dip over the quarter, though September was expected to turn positive, according to Reuters.

Global comparable sales rose 1.5%. International Operated Markets, which includes Germany and Australia, rose 1.4%, with France a weak spot. International Developmental Licensed Markets, led by Japan, rose 4.2%, partly offset by declines in China, the company said.

McDonald's declared a 4% increase in its quarterly cash dividend to $1.93 per share.

The U.S. result follows a second quarter in which McDonald's posted same-store sales growth of 0.8% as guest counts declined. At an investor day in September, the company outlined an $8.5 billion franchisee support package and said U.S. executives had acknowledged falling short on consistent execution. Skye Anderson became president of McDonald's USA in August.

Over the past two years, McDonald's has introduced promotions including a menu of items priced under $3 and a breakfast meal priced at $4, according to Reuters.
