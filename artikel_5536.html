GROWNSY Showcases Smarter Baby Care Solutions to Families Through Babylist Registry Weekend and Expert-Led Education

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Over two days at the Babylist Beverly Hills Showroom, GROWNSY connected with expectant and new parents through hands-on product experiences and a pediatrician-led discussion on infant wellness.

NEW YORK, Nov. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Preparing for a new baby brings many decisions, from choosing everyday essentials to learning how to care for a newborn. GROWNSY, a baby-care brand trusted by more than 3 million households worldwide, participated in Babylist Registry Weekend on November 7â8 at the Babylist Beverly Hills Showroom, connecting with expectant and new parents as they explored products and prepared their baby registries.

A Weekend Built Around Real Parenting Needs

For families building a registry, seeing and trying products in person can help them better understand what fits their needs. Throughout the two-day event, the GROWNSY team met with parents, answered questions, and demonstrated products designed for different aspects of everyday baby care, from nursery setup to feeding and infant care.

"Registry Weekend gave us a chance to meet new and expecting parents right when they're deciding what their families will need," said Karen, Director of Public Relations of GROWNSY. "Watching them try our products hands-on, and hearing their real-life questions, was the best part of the weekend. Those conversations shape how we design every product."

Bringing Expert Guidance to Everyday Baby Care

Beyond the product experience, GROWNSY sponsored Healthy Start: Wellness 101, an educational session led by pediatrician Dr. Max Goldstein. The session covered practical guidance on infant wellness, including how and when to suction a baby's nose. The discussion, which featured the GROWNSY SniffEase Spa 3-in-1 Nasal Aspirator, gave parents an opportunity to learn about everyday infant care from a pediatrician and better understand common questions that arise during early parenthood.
