Bitcoin ETFs take in $75 million as Fidelity inflows outweigh BlackRock withdrawals

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Four funds attracted money on November 4, with FBTC leading inflows while IBIT remained the sole fund recording withdrawals.

US spot Bitcoin ETF inflows reached $75 million on Nov. 4, 2026. The first positive session after three consecutive days of withdrawals depended on Fidelity's FBTC overcoming another negative reading from BlackRock's IBIT.

FBTC recorded net inflows of $237 million, according to Farside Investors' daily table. IBIT recorded net outflows of $201 million.

The reversal followed net outflows of $643 million on Oct. 30, $399 million on Nov. 2 and $116 million on Nov. 3.

Three other funds also attracted money: ARKB recorded net inflows of $20 million, BITB recorded net inflows of $15 million and BTCO recorded net inflows of $4 million.

IBIT was the only fund with a negative reading that day. It had also recorded withdrawals on each of the preceding three sessions, so the aggregate reversal occurred while outflows continued from that product.

Seven funds showed zero net flows: EZBC, BRRR, HODL, BTCW, MSBT, GBTC and BTC. Across the 12 products tracked by Farside, four finished positive, one negative and seven unchanged.
