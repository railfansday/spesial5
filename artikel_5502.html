Jaguar Land Rover cut profit forecast, pushed Tata Motors to quarterly loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The luxury carmaker now expects a full-year adjusted EBIT margin of 2% to 3%, after £530 million in exceptional items in the quarter

Jaguar Land Rover lowered its full-year profit guidance on Friday, and its parent, India's Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles, posted a net loss for the July-September quarter.

JLR now expects an adjusted EBIT margin between 2% and 3% for the full year, along with a free cash outflow of roughly £1.25 billion ($1.64 billion), the company said. It pointed to continuing difficulties in its markets, geopolitical uncertainty and the exceptional costs booked during the quarter. The previous margin forecast was 4%.

Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles lost 55.3 billion rupees ($587 million) in consolidated terms for the quarter. It recorded exceptional charges of 67.38 billion rupees, which covered asset impairments as well as the cost of separating employees. Analysts were projecting earnings of 15 billion rupees, according to Bloomberg.

At JLR, revenue for the three months to Sept. 30 was £5.9 billion, up 20.3% from a year earlier, as volumes recovered from last year's cyber incident. Profit before tax and exceptional items was £6 million, compared with a loss of £485 million a year earlier. Adjusted EBIT margin was 1.1%, up from negative 8.6%.

Exceptional items of £530 million reflected restructuring, including separation costs and write-downs of intangible assets under development and other assets. Loss after tax was £451 million, compared with a loss of £559 million a year earlier. Free cash outflow was £540 million, and total liquidity stood at £6.0 billion.

Wholesale volumes grew from the prior quarter in the U.K. and overseas markets, offsetting reductions in China and the Middle East, the company said.

"JLR delivered positive operational profits in the second quarter and first half of the financial year, despite ongoing global challenges," Chief Executive Officer PB Balaji said in a statement.

JLR announced in September plans to cut about 4,000 jobs, roughly one in 10 employees worldwide, as part of a goal to save £1.7 billion over two years. About 3,000 employees have signed up for the redundancy program, and most are set to leave at the end of November, Chief Financial Officer Richard Molyneux said on a call with reporters, according to Bloomberg.

JLR also launched three electric vehicles: the Range Rover Electric, Range Rover Sport Electric and Jaguar Type 01. All are built in Solihull, U.K., with deliveries slated to begin from early 2027. A Range Rover GT and a new electric Defender are set to launch next year.
