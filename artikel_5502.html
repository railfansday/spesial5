Nebius stock falls after cutting its 2026 revenue forecast on delays

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nebius reported third-quarter revenue of $980.8 million, up 571%, while guiding for $12 billion to $13 billion in 2027 revenue

Nebius Group cut its 2026 revenue forecast on Thursday, guiding to the bottom of its earlier $3 billion to $3.4 billion range because some third-party capacity has been delayed, the company said. Nebius stock fell between 10% and 12% in premarket trading.

The AI cloud company reported third-quarter revenue of $980.8 million, up 571% from a year earlier. That beat analyst estimates of $907.5 million, according to CNBC. Net loss was $37.5 million, or 13 cents per share, versus $119.6 million, or 50 cents per share, in the prior-year period. Adjusted EBITDA was $465.8 million, compared with a loss of $5.2 million a year earlier.

Some third-party capacity that Nebius planned to have running by the end of this year will now arrive in the first six months of 2027, the company said. It said the agreements covering that capacity are still in force, so a portion of 2026 revenue will shift into 2027. Analysts polled by FactSet expected full-year revenue of $3.35 billion, according to The Wall Street Journal.

Nebius issued an initial 2027 outlook of $12 billion to $13 billion in revenue, "reflecting a conservative approach to the pace of our build-out," the company said. It expects to bring over a gigawatt of additional capacity online every year.

The company expects capital expenditures of $27 billion in 2026, up from its previous guidance of $20 billion to $25 billion. It expects $40 billion to $45 billion in 2027.

CEO Arkady Volozh said customer prepayments, along with financing secured by current contracts with investment-grade clients, should pay for 75% to 85% of 2027 capital expenditures.

"Our focus remains on building a healthy, profitable business for the long term," Volozh said in a letter to shareholders, according to The Wall Street Journal.

During the third quarter, the company received proceeds of $5.75 billion from issuing convertible notes, according to its financial statements. Nebius stock climbed 15% on Wednesday.
