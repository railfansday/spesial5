Rachana Shukla, MD, and Vaishalee Shukla, MD Introduce Hybrid Choice™ Concierge Program Through Concierge Choice Physicians

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Helping patients enjoy a more personalized, connected healthcare experience with enhanced physician connectivity and availability

ROCKVILLE CENTRE, N.Y., Nov. 10, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Rachana Shukla, MD and Vaishalee Shukla, MD, experienced internal medicine physicians serving the Westchester community, are now offering the Hybrid Choice concierge program through Concierge Choice Physicians (CCP). The program is designed to provide patients with enhanced connectivity, more personalized attention, and greater physician availability, helping foster stronger relationships and a more responsive healthcare experience.

Recognizing that many patients value additional time, communication, and support from their physician, the doctors have introduced the CCP Hybrid Choice concierge program as an option for those seeking a more personalized healthcare experience. The program complements their existing practice, allowing all patients to continue receiving care while offering members enhanced physician connectivity, greater availability, and additional conveniences

"For us, the foundation of good medicine has always been the relationship we build with our patients. This program allows us to spend more time with patients, communicate more directly, and provide the type of personalized attention that helps people feel supported, informed, and confident in their healthcare decisions," said Dr. Rachana Shukla.

Membership benefits include easy-to-secure, extended appointments, direct physician connectivity, enhanced care coordination and medical advocacy, preventive health planning, and a more personalized approach to care. Members also benefit from greater physician availability, continuity of care, and a dedicated support team focused on responsiveness and service.

"We are excited to launch this new program and look forward to welcoming our members," said Dr. Vaishalee Shukla. "This is an exciting time for our practice, and we can't wait to begin this new, truly personalized style of medicine."

The physicians practice at Shukla MD, located at 150 Lockwood Avenue, Suite 30, New Rochelle, NY 10801. The Hybrid Choice program is available for an annual membership fee. For more information or to enroll, contact Concierge Choice Physicians at 877.888.5590.

About Rachana Shukla, MDRachana Shukla, MD is an internal medicine physician with over a decade of experience caring for adults in the Westchester community. She started her private practice, Shukla Medical Services PLLC, in 2015 and has been practicing since. She earned her medical degree from Dr. D. Y. Patil Medical College in Navi Mumbai, India, and completed her residency training in Internal Medicine at Sound Shore Medical Center of Westchester, affiliated with New York Medical College. Dr. Shukla combines extensive clinical experience with a strong commitment to preventive care, chronic disease management, and building lasting relationships that support her patients' long-term health and wellness.

About Vaishalee Shukla, MDVaishalee Shukla, MD is a board-certified internal medicine physician who has served patients throughout Westchester County for more than two decades. She received her medical degree and completed her residency training in Internal Medicine at Sound Shore Medical Center of Westchester, affiliated with New York Medical College. Throughout her career, Dr. Shukla has provided care in hospital, outpatient, and community-based settings, bringing a thoughtful, patient-centered approach to preventive medicine, chronic disease management, and personalized primary care.
