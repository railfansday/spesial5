Recap: Wizards take down the Magic, 116-96

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Wizards set the tone in this game early moving the ball on offense and playing with high energy on defense.

Offensively, Washington set the tone shooting 56 percent from 3 (22 for 39 shooting) and picking up 31 assists as a team. Seven players were in double figures. The ball moved and this created easier opportunities for the team.

On defense, the Wizards held the Magic under 100 points for the 2nd straight time this season, while holding Orlando to 41 percent shooting from the field.

The Magic made a number of runs in this one, getting the lead under 10 points briefly, but the Wizards broke this game open by mid-way through the first quarter and did not look back.

Overall, a big performance to lead the Wizards to their first three-game winning streak of the season. Now the Wizards will turn their attention to the Boston Celtics who will be coming to town on Tuesday for a NBA Cup matchup in DC.
