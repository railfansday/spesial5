Will Ravens trade Derrick Henry after brutal QB injury news?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Let's review the Baltimore Ravens' situation at the moment: They're 4-4 in an uber-competitive AFC North. Lamar Jackson might be out for some serious time. Tyler Huntley is now lost for the season with turf toe. Joe Fagano will start at QB this Thursday against a really good Jacksonville Jaguars team.

With that turn of fortune, it begs the question: Could the Ravens wave the white flag on this season and deal Derrick Henry, who hasn't exactly lit it up the last three weeks?

This isn't coming completely out of nowhere. There's speculation that the Ravens could deal Henry if they're out of contention, and there just happens to be a former Ravens coach in East Rutherford, New Jersey who might need a running back at the deadline. Yes, I'm talking about John Harbaugh and the New York Giants.

Let's see if this goes from speculation to reported interest. For now, it's a trade rumor. But given what we now know about the Ravens' situation in what could become another lost season? It might be a good idea.
