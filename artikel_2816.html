Cowboys vs. Eagles: Matchups to watch in Week 12 on Thanksgiving

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Dallas Cowboys got the better of the Philadelphia Eagles earlier this season with a 38-1 victory in Week 7 and are looking to repeat that success in this Thanksgiving day matchup, this time on their home field turf. Their confidence should be at an all-time high after last week's win over the Tennessee Titans and that should help them get back to .500 on the season. To do that though, they'll need to take advantage of certain matchups to sway the game in their favor.

The Cowboys are currently ranked second in the league in passing yards per game (270.9) and have been absolutely lethal the past couple of weeks. Dak Prescott has thrown for over 300 yards and at least four touchdowns in each game over the two-week period, and both CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens have each had 100+ receiving yard games during that time as well.

Dallas' lethal aerial attack should help lead them to victory in Week 12 against the Eagles secondary who are currently ranked 17th in the league allowing 222.8 yards per game.

The Cowboys have struggled stopping the run all season and that hasn't changed under Mike Petine's play-calling so far. They recently allowed Tony Pollard to rush for 110 yards and 21 carries last week, but hopefully will be able to contain Saquon Barkley better in Week 12 on Thanksgiving.

He's been less than 100% the majority of the season and may continue to be so for the rest of the year. Despite not being at his best in their last meeting, he still managed to rush for 62 yards and a touchdown on seven carries. Limiting him could be key.

For all the problems the Cowboys defense has had this year, Quinnen Williams isn't one of them. He's playing at an All-Pro level and has been wreaking havoc as both a run and pass defender each and every week.

We fully expect that to continue in Week 12 against the Eagles and will be paying close attention to him on a play-by-play basis. We would be at all surprised to see No. 92 sack his former teammate QB Joe Milton a couple of times this week or make several plays behind the line of scrimmage.
