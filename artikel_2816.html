Packers re-sign RB Pierre Strong Jr.

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Green Bay Packers, as expected, have re-signed running back Pierre Strong Jr. to the practice squad on Saturday after no team claimed him on waivers. Strong, who started the season on the practice squad, was elevated to the active roster while the team was dealing with injuries and Josh Jacobs' stint on the exempt list.

With four running backs on the active roster moving forward, Strong was the odd man out in the group. He's just the second player released from the Packers' 53-man roster, after receiver Coleman Owen — who was let go and then later re-signed to the practice squad last week.

Based on this transaction, the Packers are over-signed on the practice squad right now, so expect a corresponding roster move (a release from the practice squad) to be made soon. Here are the current players on the practice squad:

WR: Coleman Owen, Isaiah Neyor and Quincy Skinner Jr.

DL: Mazi Smith and Haggai Ndubuisi (international exemption)

ED: Arron Mosby, Nyjalik Kelly and D.J. Johnson

My guess is that the Packers will end up letting go of a player at a position where they're over-signed, like tight end, edge defender or cornerback. Usually, they like to have at least one practice squad body in every core position group.

Johnson was in practice on Saturday, according to ESPN's Rob Demovsky, which means that the missing player was likely released already, even if the move hasn't officially been announced.
