Brentford vs Everton – Predicted lineup and team news

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Brentford host Everton at the Gtech Community Stadium on Monday night, without the suspended Igor Thiago, in the final match of Premier League gameweek 11.

Keith Andrews's side trailed Everton by two places and two points before the international break, after a 2-0 defeat at Brighton left them with one win from their last six matches in all tournaments. Thiago was sent off for violent conduct in that game and Brentford failed with an appeal, so he also misses the matches against Manchester United and Arsenal. Home form has been the strength of the Bees, who have taken 10 of their 14 points at the Gtech Community Stadium and conceded just three league goals there, with only Arsenal and Brighton conceding fewer in the first 10 rounds. Each of their three home wins has been to nil, against Tottenham Hotspur, Chelsea and Nottingham Forest.

Igor Thiago begins a three-match suspension, while Aaron Hickey (thigh), Nathan Collins (knee), Rico Henry (groin) and Kaye Furo (ankle) are injured, as are Antoni Milambo and Josh Dasilva, who have fitness issues. Milambo and Dasilva are due to return to Under-21s action over the weekend. Fabio Carvalho could return from a knock, which would boost Andrews's attacking ranks.

Andrews must decide between a like-for-like swap with Callum Wilson and deploying Kevin Schade through the middle with two of Keane Lewis-Potter, Jaidon Anthony and Dango Ouattara in support. Wilson is anticipated to get the nod from the first whistle. Thiago has been directly involved in four of Brentford's 16 Premier League goals this season, or 25%. Everton have Jack Grealish available again and no injuries, with Thierno Barry having scored twice against Coventry City.

Brentford possible starting XI: Kelleher; Kayode, Van den Berg, Ajer, Diouf; Yarmoliuk, Janelt, Jensen; Schade, Wilson, Lewis-Potter

The match kicks off at 20:00 GMT on Monday, 23 November 2026.

The match will be broadcast live on Sky Sports.
