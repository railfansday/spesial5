Victor Munoz injury update after Liverpool beat Club Brugge

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Andoni Iraola says he remains optimistic over Victor Munoz’s injury after the winger was forced off during Liverpool’s 3-0 win over Club Brugge in the Champions League on Wednesday night.

Munoz, who was signed in a £34.5 million deal from Osasuna in the summer, injured his knee in the closing stages of the first half.

The 23-year-old looked in significant discomfort but was able to limp off the pitch without needing a stretcher.

Two goals from Alexander Isak, plus a strike from Florian Wirtz, sealed Liverpool’s victory against Club Brugge to make it four wins in five Champions League games this season.

However, Iraola could now be without Munoz for Liverpool’s next Premier League fixture away to Everton on Sunday.

When asked about Munoz’s injury after his side’s win in Belgium, Iraola said: ‘It was the worst part of the game, definitely,

‘He was in a lot of pain when he left the pitch. He was a lot more positive after the game had finished.

‘It was a knock on the knee so now we have to assess him if the knee presents well tomorrow and it’s just a knock or, obviously, it’s very early to tell if there’s something more than a knock.

‘But in the moment it was very, very painful. After the game I spoke with him and he was a little bit more optimistic.’
