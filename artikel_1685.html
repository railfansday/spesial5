Injured Yamal to miss Barcelona’s Champions League clash with Sabah

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Lamine Yamal will miss Barcelona's Champions League clash with Sabah due to an ankle injury.

Yamal played the full 90 minutes of Barcelona's comeback win over Villarreal in LaLiga on Sunday.

However, the Spanish champions have confirmed he will miss their match against Sabah on Wednesday as a precaution due to feeling mild discomfort in his right ankle.

They also said he would be back for their game against Deportivo A Coruna in LaLiga at the weekend.

Yamal will be a big miss for Barcelona, having been influential against Villarreal, despite missing a first-half penalty, as he set up Fermin Lopez's equaliser and had a hand in Pau Navarro's own goal, which proved to be the winner.

MEDICAL STATEMENT First-team player Lamine Yamal is experiencing mild discomfort in his right ankle. As a precaution, he will miss the match against Sabah and is expected to be available for next Sunday's match against Deportivo. https://t.co/FxBWxL2y98

— FC Barcelona (@FCBarcelona) November 23, 2026

This season, the youngster has 13 goals and 13 assists in 17 games for Barcelona across all competitions.

Yamal has 14 goals and 13 assists in the Champions League, both the outright most goals and outright most assists of any teenager in the history of the competition.

He will miss the chance to break the record for the most games by a teenager – he is currently level with Warren Zaire-Emery on 37 matches.

Barcelona travel to Sabah having won three of their four Champions League games so far (L1), knowing a win will take them closer to automatic qualification for the last 16.
