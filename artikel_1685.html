Tottenham: Savio injury latest as Roberto De Zerbi issues five-man update for Sunderland trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Roberto De Zerbi has provided an update on Savio ahead of Tottenham’s trip to Sunderland this weekend.

Savio sustained a hamstring injury after coming off the bench during last Saturday’s 2-0 victory at home to Ipswich, with De Zerbi confirming post-match that the Brazilian winger had suffered a recurrence of the injury that forced him to miss Spurs’ previous four matches.

Fitness issues have disrupted Savio’s start to life in north London, with the 22-year-old featuring in just five league games since joining from Manchester City in an £85million deal, and is now set for another extended spell on the sidelines.

Asked if Savio may not play again this year, De Zerbi said: “Yeah, I think [he will miss the Christmas period].

“Unfortunately it’s a tough situation to accept. He’s a new guy and he’s suffering with these new injuries. But this is football, if you see other European clubs they’re all suffering these injuries.”

De Zerbi added: “Now he needs time. He needs hard work with the medical staff. Then in 50 days, 60 days, 80 days, I don’t know, when he comes back, he will be crucial for us in the last step of the season.”

There was little more positive fitness news with Lucas Bergvall a doubt for the trip up north due to illness, and Tosin Adarabioyo only returning to training on Friday.

“We will analyse [his fitness] tomorrow,” De Zerbi said of Tosin.

There remains ongoing speculation over Omar Marmoush’s fitness, which claims emerging on social media this week that the summer signing was due to see a knee specialist.

The 27-year-old came off the bench against Brighton and was spotted in training footage posted on the club’s social media accounts on Thursday. Standard Sport understands that footage was of Tuesday’s session.

Spurs are already short of attacking options, with Mathys Tel, Mykhailo Mudryk and long-term absentees Xavi Simons and Wilson Odobert all sidelined.

The club will also continue to manage Dejan Kulusevski and James Maddison’s minutes ahead of Sunday’s trip to the Stadium of Light.

Kulusevski is “very close to starting” a game, having impressed De Zerbi over the international break.

“He’s getting close [to being able to start]. He’s working very well. I watched his games in the national team just to understand the physical condition. He played as a winger and as a number 10. He played well, particularly in the second game.

“We have to choose the right time to put him on the pitch. We have to work with the medical staff and we can’t take any risks with him because he’s a special player for us.”
