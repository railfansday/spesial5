Black Friday is here for Apple: AirPods Pro, MacBook Air, and Apple Watches at new record-low prices

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Apple raised its prices this year, but who didn't? Well, now that Black Friday sale season is here, this is your best chance to cancel out those price hikes. That's why we're paying extra close attention to early Black Friday Apple deals.

Yes, Apple does do Black Friday, but not in the way you might expect. Shopping directly on Apple's website usually means getting a measly gift card for future purchases. The real way to find great deals? Looking at retailers like Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, and Target. Especially as Black Friday approaches (mark Nov. 27 in your calendar), retailers duke it out to see who can offer the best prices on AirPods, MacBooks, and iPads. Walmart and Amazon are infamous for outdoing each other by a single cent.

Usually, prices on Apple products drop to record lows during the hectic Black Friday weekend. However, especially good deals can also sell out quick. In year's past, Mashable's team has spotted many a record-low on AirPods only for them to sell out before we can finish writing a 200-word post about it.

Shopping early lets you score decent savings while avoiding the post-Thanksgiving chaos. And it's not as if the deals are lacking. Best Buy, Amazon, and Target's official Black Friday sales have technically already started, and Apple prices are showing it. The AirPods Pro 3 dropped to a record low of $149 as of Nov. 23, the Apple Watch Series 12 is $100 and down to a new record low, and the 13-inch MacBook Air dipped below $1,000 for the first time since its price hike earlier this year.

So, how do you know what to buy, and what to save for Black Friday and Cyber Monday? That's where the expertise of the Mashable shopping team can be helpful.

Let's start with the very expensive elephant in the room. In June, Apple raised prices on MacBooks, Macs, iPads, the HomePod, Apple TVs, and even the Vision Pro headset, followed by iPhone price increases in September. With some prices rising by $200 or more, the likelihood of them falling to record lows is low. Instead, we research the market to identify products that have been at or near record lows over the past five months.

We also took into account the frequency of deals. If, like the AirPods Pro 3, there's a $50 deal that's nearly always on, we don't necessarily consider that a deal worth highlighting. On the other hand, if an iPad is $100 off for the first time in a month, we'll include it and note its pricing history.

Finally, we considered our reviewers' first-hand experiences with these products. Most of the items in this guide have been hands-on tested by at least one person on the Mashable staff. Our team also prioritized Mashable award winners, choosing the best deals based on overall product performance and value.

We were hoping our favorite AirPods would return to their record-low pricing of $169. Our expectations? Met and surpassed, as our favorite AirPods dropped down to $149 on Nov. 23, marking a new record-low price by a margin of $20. All told, that cuts an impressive $100 off their list price of $249 (for a total discount of 40%). Barring any freak price drops (never say never) this will be the absolute best price you can get for these earbuds that come with excellent noise-cancellation, great battery life at eight hours per charge, impressive balanced sound, and a built-in heart rate monitor.

The AirPods 5 haven't received a proper discount yet, as Apple's newest AirPods. The version without a wireless charging case got a few dollars off the $129 around their release, while the $149 wireless charging case edition hasn't had any price drops. If these buds are going on sale in 2026, Black Friday is likely when.

Welcome back, MacBook Air under $1,000. We've missed you.

Though not quite the lowest price we've seen on the 13-inch Air, it's still a great deal considering that post MacBook price-hike, this laptop hasn't fallen below $1,000. According to our laptop expert, this is a great laptop for college students because of its size and zippy M5 power — and it's also an overall daily driver that will more than suit the needs of most folks. If you prefer a slightly larger display, the 15-inch Air with the same specs is $300 off at the time of writing.

The M6 Mac mini is on sale for $779 at Amazon, down from $899. That's $120 in savings on the model with 16GB of memory and 256GB of storage. If you already have a monitor, keyboard, and mouse, it's worth a look — just keep in mind that none of those come in the box.

Our sister site PCMag gave the M6 Mac mini an Editors' Choice award, praising its performance, compact design, and ability to stay cool and quiet. It measures just five inches across, but still has room for two front-facing USB-C ports, three Thunderbolt 4 ports, and an HDMI connection, with support for up to three displays.

MacBook prices went up in June of this year, with the Neo jumping by $100 and the Air by $200. That means it's highly unlikely we'll see prices reach their former record lows, even if they start at a much higher price point. I've taken this into account as I select the best deals and note the deals to look out for.

Mashable considers the iPad mini the best iPad for entertainment, meaning for many shoppers, it's a strong contender for the best Apple tablet. Granted at full price, this 2026-release isn't cheap. In fact, Mashable Tech and Shopping Editor Timothy Werth wrote in his review of the tiny tablet, "A slightly cheaper iPad Mini priced around $499 or $599 would make a lot more sense to me, and be much easier to recommend."

Luckily, with this $100 off for Black Friday, the iPad mini is available for exactly that (and sitting at a record low price, too). The OLED-powered display is beautiful, as is the zippy performance from the A20 Pro chip. And of course, you can't beat the portability.

The 11-inch iPad Pro is technically on sale, but a $100 price drop on a $1,200 iPad isn't significant. It is the lowest price this tablet has dropped to in the past five months, which is why I included it among the best early deals. Still, a $200 price drop would make this a more tempting buy.

The Apple Watch Series 12 was announced just this past September, and we saw it drop to $349.99 once. Then, for early Black Friday sales, it got down to $329. We hoped for more, and it was rewarded, with a price drop of $299 the week of Black Friday. With RAMaggedon driving prices up, we're thrilled to see this discount so early on in the Series 12 time on the market.

The Apple Watch Ultra 3 shares features similar to those of the newer Ultra 4, but there's a huge benefit to opting for the Ultra 3. The list price is $100 lower, and during Best Buy's Black Friday sale, it's an even bigger discount: $549, making it $150 cheaper than the Ultra 4's current sale price of $699.99. It's one of the best Apple Watch deals available right now.
