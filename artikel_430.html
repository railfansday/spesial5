Lego Insiders Weekend is live — score exclusive rewards and discounts when you sign up for free

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It’s an exciting few days for Lego fans, with the plastic brick maestros dropping the Lego Insiders Weekend event. Running Nov. 21-22, Lego Insider Weekend offers all kinds of benefits and discounts when you sign up to be a Lego Insider, which is Lego’s term for a premium loyalty member. While you can nab Lego deals with other retailers in the early Black Friday sales, Lego Insider Weekend offers benefits you won’t find anywhere else. It’s absolutely worth signing up whether you’re a hardcore Lego devotee or a casual shopper who’s looking to save on holiday gifts.

Lego Insider Weekend benefits include double or even quadruple loyalty points, exclusive Lego Insider Price discounts, and special gifts with purchases. On top of all that, you’ll get a 10% discount with your first purchase when you spend over $100 — just use the code INSIDERS10. It's free to sign up to become a Lego Insider — all you need to do is enter your name, date of birth, and an email address.

There are other exclusive Lego Insider rewards. For instance, you can enter a competition to win a signed Ideas Godzilla set — a truly roar-some build of epic proportions out Nov. 27 — plus the chance to trade Insider points for gifts.

Don't miss your chance to sign up and take advantage of Lego Insiders Weekend, running Nov. 21-22. And don't forget, the fun doesn't stop at the end of this weekend. Being a Lego Insider means ongoing benefits, including bonus points, discounts, special gifts, and early access to the hottest new sets.
