Icahn’s Fortune Sinks to $1.3b as Debt Pressures Rise

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Carl Icahn’s fortune has dwindled to about $1.3 billion as mounting debt squeezes his business empire, leaving the longtime corporate raider and activist investor facing one of the toughest stretches of his career. When Icahn Enterprises reported third-quarter earnings this month, it warned there was no assurance it could refinance $1.4 billion of bonds due in May on acceptable terms — or at all. Since then, the pressure has only intensified. Shares of CVR Energy, a key IEP holding, have tumbled about 40% since it agreed earlier this month to buy two additional refineries, eroding the value of a stake that accounted for about half of Icahn’s publicly traded investment partnership at the end of September. Yields on some of IEP’s 2029 bonds now exceed 30%, and the company has about $4.4 billion of bonds outstanding. Tom Maloney, Bloomberg News Editor, discusses how lots of leverage of led to Carl Icahn's investment losses. (Source: Bloomberg)
