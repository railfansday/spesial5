Seoul Apocalypse tier list - pick the best characters

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Updated on November 7th, 2026 - Version: 1.11.17

Seoul Apocalypse is a complex and stylish RPG with interesting characters, so choosing the best hero might be challenging. When in doubt, check out our Seoul Apocalypse tier list to figure out the best heroes to invest your time and effort in.

You will find all the Seoul Apocalypse heroes rated from the strongest to the weakest here. Moreover, our tier lists are regularly updated, so it's worth checking back often to see if anything has changed.

For those of you who love this kind of content, take a look at our Epic Fantasy tier list, Higan: Eruthyll tier list or Soul Knight tier list.

Now, without further ado, click on the blue button below, and let's head into the tier list for Seoul Apocalypse.

Tier S includes the best heroes in Seoul Apocalypse. Owning a powerful Tier S character guarantees you will progress a lot easier thanks to their superior stats. The only issue is that getting a character from the top of this Seoul Apocalypse tier list requires significant effort.

Racket is one of the best characters in Seoul Apocalypse, putting him at the top of our tier list. At the maximum level, Racket gets 550,6K power points, which is a good number for a Tier S hero in Seoul Apocalypse. Racket’s main feature is his passive skill, which allows him to increase his CRT DMG up to 100%. You read that right. Every hit is a crit.

Big Bear boasts powerful stats and is one of the best options for survivability in the game. Once levelled up, this character has over 1 million health points, allowing him to absorb tons of damage. Moreover, Big Bear can increase his defence by 60% with his passive ability, making him incredibly difficult to take down.

Talking about the best characters in Seoul Apocalypse, it is impossible to ignore Crux. He has the most significant attack stats in the game. With over 70,000 attack points, Crux can kill even the most powerful enemies in a few seconds. The game description says that Crux’s power is enough to cut through the wind, and who are we to question that?

Even though Tier A always gets less attention than Tier S, it also includes powerful heroes that might be suitable even at the later game stages. Some characters from Tier A might even see themselves get promoted to Tier S in further updates.

Lucky is one of the most balanced heroes in Seoul Apocalypse. When all teammates perish, Lucky is one of the heroes you can rely on to survive and finish off your opponent. However, take into account that you should be a highly skilled player to use Lucky’s abilities properly.

Thunder is the first character you will unlock in Seoul Apocalypse. Despite that, Thunder can be used even if you are at the later game stages. His stats are pretty good, and the only reason Thunder is not in Tier S is that his abilities can be a little lacklustre at times.

Big Daddy has proven a good addition to Seoul Apocalypse. He has significant attack power and strong ultimate abilities, allowing him to deal and absorb tons of damage at the same time. And if you level up Big Daddy, he can reduce enemies’ damage and apply powerful boosts to teammates.

Tier B characters are perfectly suitable for beginners and are even occasionally used by experienced players. Tier B includes powerful characters with strong gear that can keep up with Tier A heroes. However, it is best to replace Tier B heroes with more powerful ones at the later game stages, as they tend to drop off there.

Gust is one of the best heroes for beginners in Seoul Apocalypse. Even though he does not have a powerful passive skill, Gust’s abilities merit him a spot in Tier B. Gust can significantly increase attack during the fight, allowing him to be one of the most effective damage dealers in the game's beginning stages.

Even though some players consider Gymnast to be in Tier C, we think she is worth a spot in Tier B. Gymnast is one of the first characters you will unlock in the game.

Like Big Daddy, Dual Gun H can deal and absorb tons of damage at the same time. But in addition to dealing damage, Dual Gun H can apply a blind effect, allowing you to dominate enemies.

Tier C is at the very bottom of this Seoul Apocalypse tier list. It includes the worst characters in the game that are not recommended to choose, even if you are a beginner. Fortunately, there's nothing challenging about getting Tier B heroes, so you can easily replace a weak Tier C hero in your team.

Like most characters from Tier C, Axe Assaulter is one of the first characters you will unlock in the game. Unfortunately, he can be pretty useless. Even primary heroes like Gymnast, Bruce Kang, and others are better choices than Axe Assaulter.

Security Guard is the first hero you will unlock after getting Thunder in Seoul Apocalypse. Unfortunately, like most primary characters in the game, Security Guard needs to be replaced as soon as possible. He doesn’t have any passive skills or powerful abilities that would help him get a higher ranking.

And that concludes our Seoul Apocalypse tier list. As you can see, the game has over 50 unique characters, and estimating the most powerful ones might be challenging at first. Fortunately, our tier list can help you solve this problem!
