A Vice City rout: Heat defeat Bucks 137-130 in collab with ‘GTA VI'

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

A Vice City rout: Heat defeat Bucks 137-130 in collab with 'GTA VI' originally appeared on NBC Sports Boston

A loss on Vice City night never made sense.

The Miami Heat routed the Milwaukee Bucks 137-130 on Wednesday during a one-night-only promotional collaboration with Rockstar's "Grand Theft Auto VI," which launches in the U.S. at midnight Thursday.

Miami rolled out a new court at Kaseya Center with pink, blue and black colors matching its special-edition jersey. The words "Vice City" replaced the usual Heat logo in mid-court, while a "VI" with a palm tree featured in the corner of each paint.

Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo led the way for Miami in a night that meant a lot to both players. Antetokounmpo faced the team that drafted him for the first time, while Adebayo went head-to-head against former teammate Tyler Herro and Co.

Antetokounmpo posted a game-high 27 points on 7 of 11 shooting to go with 13 rebounds, nine assists, one steal and a block.

Adebayo followed suit with 26 points on 7 of 15 shooting to go with four rebounds, two assists and two steals. Adebayo dominated the first half, while Antetokounmpo took over in the second.

Davion Mitchell added 24 points on a hot 6 of 7 clip from long range to help Miami move to 9-6 after a slow start. Milwaukee fell to 1-14 as Kyle Kuzma scored 26 points to lead the team.

Milwaukee closed the gap in the final quarter once Miami pulled most of its starters, but the Heat had been in control since the end of the first.

Beyond the game, fans had access to a limited-edition Vice City merch and apparel collection at team shops throughout the arena. There were also unique cars on display for fans to see and a photo spot with the "VI" logo.

Vice City is the fictional name of the game's city that loosely represents Miami, while Leonida is the fictional Florida-inspired state the city is based in. Other prominent in-game locations are based on the Everglades and the Florida Keys.

"GTA VI" has been expected to be the biggest game of the decade since its initial trailer release in December 2023. The previous installment, based in a fictional Los Angeles and surrounding areas, released 13 years ago.
