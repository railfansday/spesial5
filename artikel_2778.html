Harden passes Bryant for 50-point games

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

James Harden scored 51 points to move third on the all-time list for 50-point NBA games as the Cleveland Cavaliers beat the Indiana Pacers 128-119 in the NBA Cup.

The 37-year-old made all 14 of his free throws as his recorded his 26th 50-point game to move above the legendary Kobe Bryant.

Harden, who also made six rebounds and provided six assists at Rocket Arena in Cleveland, had not passed 50 points since scoring 55 for the Los Angeles Clippers in November 2025.

Legendary centre Wilt Chamberlain holds the NBA record ⁠with 118 50-point games, while Michael Jordan is second with 31.

"When you talk about just basketball, obviously Wilt was elite, but you got Mike and Kobe - shooting guard position, the best to ever do it - so just to be in that conversation, it's very humbling and it's definitely special," said an emotional Harden.

Aged 37 years and 86 days, he is the fourth oldest player to score 50 points in a game.

"What excites me a lot is even being in my 18th year learning how to still be impactful," said Harden, the ninth highest scorer in NBA history with 29,633 points.

"It's easy when you're young - 23, 24, you run around, you jump high and can move fast and get by. Now it's like, you're not as fast.

"That's the evolution of James Harden and figuring that out.

"Tonight was a confidence-booster that I can do it at a high level still."

The Cavaliers travel to the Chicago Bulls in the NBA on Tuesday.
