Joe Brady: We can't focus on result, there's a lot to clean up

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Bills won again on Sunday, but head coach Joe Brady didn’t sound enthused about the way they played against the Dolphins.

Brady’s team didn’t allow a touchdown for the third straight game and they picked up 405 yards of offense without playing from behind at all in the 26-9 win. The effort also included a pair of interceptions, a missed field goal and penalties that led the team to miss out on opportunities for touchdowns, which is likely part of what Brady will focus on in the coming days.

“I know as the head football coach, I always have to find ways for us to improve and get us to where we want to get to,” Brady said, via Nick Suss of ESPN.com. “If we just focus on the result, we lose sight of all of those things. There’s a lot for us to clean up. We’ve got to do it in a short time because we’ve got a short turnaround.”

The Bills host the Chiefs on Sunday night in a game that has significant playoff implications in the AFC, so it’s no surprise that Brady wants the team’s attention on a cleaner effort a few days from now.
