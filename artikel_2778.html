A.J. Brown, Rhamondre Stevenson among Patriots questionable for Sunday vs. Lions

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Patriots have several players listed as questionable for Sunday’s game against the Lions in Madrid.

A.J. Brown (ankle/knee) and running back Rhamondre Stevenson (knee) are among them.

Brown was listed as limited on all three injury reports this week. Stevenson was limited on Thursday and Friday after he was a non-participant on Wednesday.

Safety Kevin Byard (chest), fullback Reggie Gilliam (knee), tight end Cameron Latu (back), offensive lineman Walter Rouse (concussion protocol), offensive lineman Greg Van Roten (hamstring), and cornerback Charles Woods (hamstring) are also questionable.

Offensive tackle Morgan Moses (foot), tight end Hunter Henry (foot), and quarterback Drake Maye (right shoulder) are all off the injury report and are set to play.

Offensive lineman Ben Brown (knee), cornerback Carlton Davis (knee), and running back TreVeyon Henderson (illness) were ruled out earlier this week as they didn’t travel to Europe.
