Smiley has 16 in Belmont’s 75-45 win against Centre

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

NASHVILLE, Tenn. (AP) — Jack Smiley had 16 points in Belmont's 75-45 victory against Centre on Thursday.

Smiley added six rebounds for the Bruins (2-0). Vincent Neugebauer scored 10 points and added nine rebounds. Aleksa Ristic had 10 points and finished 4 of 5 from the field.

Graham Krezmien led the way for the Colonels with 10 points and six rebounds. Finn Louden added 10 points and four steals for Centre. Drake Ingram finished with six points.

Belmont visits Lipscomb in its next matchup on Tuesday.
