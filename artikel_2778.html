Sixers rule Brown out with hamstring injury for Magic game

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Sixers rule Brown out with hamstring injury for Magic game originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

Jaylen Brown will miss his second game as a Sixer on Wednesday night when the team meets the Magic in Orlando.

The Sixers listed Brown as out with a left hamstring strain on their Tuesday evening injury report.

Brown had a head-to-head collision with Julius Randle in the fourth quarter of the Sixers' win Monday over the Nets. He returned after a few minutes of game action with a bandage on his forehead, but Brown exited again with 1:44 left and did not re-enter.

Brown's only other absence this season came in an Oct. 30 victory vs. the Knicks because of left knee patellar tendinopathy. He scored 31 points in both games of the Sixers' latest back-to-back. For the season, the five-time All-Star wing has averaged 22.6 points, 6.6 rebounds and 3.6 assists.

Dominick Barlow (lower back stress reaction), Adem Bona (cervical spine sprain) and Ariel Hukporti (right Achilles tendon tear) all remain out for the 12-3 Sixers.

The 6-7 Magic's sidelined players include Paolo Banchero (right high ankle sprain) and Jonathan Isaac (left knee injury management).
