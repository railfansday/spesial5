3 Titans to watch vs. Jaguars in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Tennessee Titans are severe underdogs versus the Jacksonville Jaguars on Sunday. It represents another tough outing for a 1-9 Titans team that has recently played these same Jaguars, Dallas Cowboys, and Cincinnati Bengals. We've got three Titans players who need big-time performances in Week 12.

Titans quarterback Cam Ward has played three quality games consecutively. Last time out versus the Jaguars, he was 28-of-39 for 231 yards, two touchdowns, and one interception. Ward also scrambled for 39 yards.

Anthony Campanile's defense limited explosive plays. Ward was tasked with taking what an elite defense gave him, and he did an excellent job staying patient and efficient. The Jaguars will adjust defensively. We'll see if Brian Daboll's offense can replicate the success from the first matchup.

Rookie wide receiver Carnell Tate returned to the lineup versus the Dallas Cowboys last weekend following a two-game absence (three weeks with the bye) due to a shoulder injury. Tate's day wasn't volume-heavy, but he did produce a highlight-worthy moment. He caught a short Cam Ward slant pass, noticed he was untouched, got up and ran the ball into the end zone for a 59-yard touchdown.

Tate missed the Jaguars contest from Week 10. Ward still managed to play pretty impressively. The offense is looking forward to a do-over with Tate in the lineup.

It was a fairly uneventful return to the starting lineup for rookie linebacker Anthony Hill Jr. against the Cowboys. He missed the Jaguars game with a neck injury. Hill only had six tackles in Dallas, and allowed three catches for 63 yards (21.0 YPC).

The Titans started Cedric Gray and Dorian Mausi at linebacker versus the Jags. Hopefully getting Hill back is a boost versus a Jaguars rushing attack that could be without starting RB Bhayshul Tuten. Hill is due for a bounce-back showing.
