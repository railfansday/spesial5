The Lakers are ‘optimistic’ Austin Reaves will return from injury against Spurs

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

After suffering a bruised heel on Nov. 11 against the Milwaukee Bucks, Austin Reaves has missed the Los Angeles Lakers' past five games. But according to head coach JJ Redick, it sounds like Reaves is progressing well and nearing a return as soon as the Lakers' next game against the San Antonio Spurs on Friday.

"We want to be consistent with our [return to play] stuff," Redick told reporters prior to Monday's win over the Utah Jazz. "I think today was a big step just in terms of getting a stay ready game in and he played great, moved great. We're optimistic for the rest of this week."

Although the Lakers have won four of five without Reaves in the lineup, they'll certainly welcome him back as quickly as possible. Three of those four wins occurred against the Jazz, Sacramento Kings and Charlotte Hornets, which own a combined 9-43 record this season. Their one loss during that stretch came to Utah on the second leg of a back-to-back as well. Even with those four wins against lottery-bound opponents, their play has suffered since Reaves became sidelined.

Through the first 13 games of the year — Reaves played in 12 of them — they were 11-2 with a plus-9.0 net rating (fifth league-wide) and the top-ranked offense (122.5 points per 100 possession). Over the past five contests, they're 4-1 with a plus-4.3 net rating (10th) and the 17th-ranked offense (114.0 points per 100).

Much of that offensive downswing stems from Luka Dončić's five-game slump (53.2% true shooting, 5.2 turnovers per game since Reaves went down). But it's entirely plausible Reaves' absence is creating too hefty of a workload for Dončić to endure. His return should better balance and diversify Los Angeles' offense by easing some of Dončić's burden for the betterment of everyone.

The timing is ideal, too. The Lakers' schedule significantly ramps up over the next five weeks. Across a 13-game stretch spanning from Friday through Dec. 29, the Lakers face the Spurs, Rockets (x2), Timberwolves (x2), Denver Nuggets (x2), Memphis Grizzlies (x2), Philadelphia 76ers and Indiana Pacers, which could have Tyrese Haliburton back by that Dec. 21 matchup.

Los Angeles will certainly need all hands on deck, Reaves included, to prove itself against such a formidable stretch of competition.
