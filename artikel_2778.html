Tyler Allgeier held in check by Seahawks in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Arizona Cardinals RB Tyler Allgeier rushed 17 times for 46 yards and caught two passes for zero yards against the Seattle Seahawks in Week 9.

Why it Matters: After his huge performance last week against the Cowboys, Allgeier found running room a lot tougher to come by against Seattle. Still, the touches should be there for Allgeier as long as Bam Knight is sidelined, making him a potential flex option or RB3.
