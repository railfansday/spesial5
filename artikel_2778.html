Travis Kelce has two TDs, Chiefs lead Cardinals 27-7 at halftime

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

For the first time since 2022, Travis Kelce has scored multiple touchdowns in a regular-season game.

Unsurprisingly, given the circumstances, the Chiefs lead the Cardinals 24-7 at halftime.

Kelce’s first score in the first quarter moved him past Rob Gronkowski on the all-time list for most TD receptions by a tight end, putting Kelce at No. 3 behind Antonio Gates and Tony Gonzalez.

He scored his second in the second quarter, catching a 22-yard pass over the middle from Patrick Mahomes.

Kelce finished the first half with six receptions for 74 yards with two scores.

The tight end’s performance was emblematic of the dominance of Kansas City’s offensive unit, as the Chiefs scored on each of their five possessions in the half.

Emmett Johnson, starting for Kenneth Walker for the second consecutive game, rushed for 66 yards on 16 carries with a touchdown. He also had three receptions for 46 yards in the first 30 minutes.

Mahomes is 21-of-31 for 233 yards with two touchdowns and no picks.

On the other side, Josh Johnson — starting in place of the injured Carson Beck — was able to engineer one scoring drive early in the second quarter. But Arizona’s offense has otherwise had a lot of trouble doing much of anything. Johnson finished the first half 8-of-12 for 69 yards with a touchdown.

Down 17-7 at the time, the Cardinals went for it on fourth-and-1 from their own 37 in the second quarter. But Simi Fehoko couldn’t get past the sticks for a turnover on downs.

A few plays later, Mahomes hit Kelce for the tight end’s second TD of the day.

The Cardinals will receive the second-half kickoff.
