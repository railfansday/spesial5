Will Baker Mayfield play today? Injury update, start-or-sit advice for Buccaneers QB in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Baker Mayfield has had an uncomfortable week leading up to the Tampa Bay Buccaneers' meeting with the Detroit Lions. A foot injury forced the quarterback to spend several days in a walking boot, putting his availability for the Week 11 NFL contest in doubt.

For fantasy managers, the uncertainty created a reason to explore other quarterback options before Sunday's games. Mayfield's situation has now taken a positive turn, though his injury remains worth considering when making a final lineup decision.

Mayfield will play today against the Lions (h/t Ian Rapoport of NFL Network). The Buccaneers quarterback has been fighting through a foot injury but has made enough progress to suit up for the Week 11 matchup.

Mayfield initially suffered the injury during Tampa Bay's Week 9 loss to the Chicago Bears. He spent time in a walking boot during the team's bye week as he rested and received treatment on the foot and ankle area.

His recovery appeared to take a positive turn on Thursday when he returned to practice after missing Wednesday's session. He also participated in Friday's practice, although both sessions were limited.

Mayfield told reporters he felt ready to play after making progress during the bye week. Head coach Todd Bowles also offered encouragement, saying the quarterback had a good practice and was trending in the right direction.

There is still some protection needed for the injured foot. According to Tom Pelissero of The Ringer, Mayfield is expected to wear a special orthotic and new cleats to help stabilize the foot during Sunday's game.

Detroit's pass defense could give Mayfield an opportunity to produce. The Lions have allowed 266 passing yards per game and 19 passing touchdowns through nine games this season. Those numbers suggest opposing quarterbacks have found opportunities against their secondary.

However, Mayfield's own production and physical condition make this a decision worth considering. He completed 18 of 32 passes for 243 yards, no touchdowns and two interceptions in his last full game against the Chicago Bears.

The Lions have also recorded seven interceptions, so Mayfield will need to protect the ball against a defense that has created opportunities for turnovers.
