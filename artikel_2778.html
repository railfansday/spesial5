Detroit Lions re-claim EDGE rusher following Aidan Hutchinson injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

For the second time in the last 14 months, the Detroit Lions have claimed edge rusher Tyrus Wheat off waivers from the Dallas Cowboys. In a corresponding move, the Lions released cornerback Nick Whiteside from the 53-man roster.

At cutdowns in 2025, the Lions claimed Wheat off waivers and placed him on their active roster. Wheat was active for 15 games, though he was mainly a special teamer, playing 215 snaps in the third phase of the game and only 66 on defense. While his opportunities were minimal, he made the most of them, registering career highs in tackles (15), sacks (1.5), QB Hits (2), and PFF grade (82.3).

Wheat left the Lions in free agency to re-join the Cowboys—the team that drafted him—but has been limited to just five games played with minimal production (three tackles). The Cowboys released him on November 14th to make room for defensive lineman Jonathan Bullard, and the Lions—having just lost Aidan Hutchinson to injury—were quick to claim him.

With Wheat's experience as a rush end in the Lions scheme, expectations are that he'll get up to speed with the playbook quickly and could be incorporated into game action as soon as this weekend. While well-versed in the Lions system, expectations should reflect his involvement from last season: a core special teamer who can, on average, give the Lions 4-5 snaps a game on defense.

Whiteside spent most of the season on the Lions' practice squad but played in the last two games on the 53-man roster. It wouldn't be surprising to see if the Lions try to re-add Whiteside to the practice squad.
