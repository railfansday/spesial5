Marches & Gnats programming challenges

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

A mechanical mind. A forgotten land. A quest for beauty in numbers. Marches & Gnats is a fantastical game of programming challenges set in 19th-century Estonia, where each challenge is both a story and a test of your computational ingenuity. Craft precise instructions for the Logic Mill—a secret mechanical Turing machine—and help Mihkel, a quiet genius of Dorpat, turn the hidden order of the world into working code.
