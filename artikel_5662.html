When asked about Tom, a new company being announced, John Doerr, the legendary Kleiner Perkins venture capitalist, paused, seeming near tears, and requested a moment. The company is named after his brother.

“I’m getting emotional because Tom passed away prematurely,” Doerr said.

The company, Tom Health, is being spun out of Lumeris, a value-based care firm the Doerr brothers helped found. The firm launched 15 years ago, based largely off of ideas that Tom, a physician, had. Lumeris has raised more than $620 million and employs 1,200 people, according to Pitchbook.“He brought passion for quality care for every individual and inspired the company to such a degree that, without any of my involvement, they decided to name their AI after him,” Doerr said.“Tom always wanted to make health care more personal, and he wanted to give doctors more time to be doctors, all with the aim of serving patients,” he added. “In fact, he would give all of his patients his mobile phone number, and they knew they could call him any time to get him.”
