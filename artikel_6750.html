Every hair roller is different, and each hair type will require unique steps. However, it's a good idea to keep these expert-recommended tips in mind if you're looking to achieve a bouncy blowout at home:

"The way your curlers and hair sets is the way that it is going to flow," explains Tippi Shorter, celebrity hairstylist and Mizani global artistic director. "Place the rollers at the crown of the head and roll your hair under the curler; that way, you will get more volume and body."

Joseph Maine, celebrity stylist and artistic director of Color Wow Hair, suggests using a brush and rollers that are on the larger side in order to achieve a smoother bouncy blowout. "The smaller they are, the more curls they're going to leave in your hair."

Use a diffuser attachment on a blow dryer to gently heat the rollers back up after each one has been placed. This will ensure that they are set perfectly. Then, keep the rollers in your hair for about 15 minutes or until they cool down completely.

In short: There’s no one-size-fits-all roller, and each type serves a slightly different purpose. You probably have seen quite a few throwback pics of supermodels getting glammed up with Velcro rollers, but times have changed, and rollers have evolved.

Hot and heatless rollers can be used to create the effortless and bouncy look you're going for—it just depends on the size of the roller and the process you use. And remember, the bigger the roller, the bouncier the hair. That means the smaller the roller, the tighter the curls will be.

Additionally, some rollers are made from ceramic or metal, while others are made of plastic. The ceramic and metal distribute heat better and are able to boost and lock in volume more easily. The plastic rollers are also effective, but their effects don’t last as long.

It depends on the type of roller you use and what your desired final look is. For example, if you’re hoping to create heatless curls, stick with satin or plastic rollers and sleep with them overnight for defined ringlets come morning. All rollers can boost volume, and if you want to restore bounce, they should be used on dry hair to lift your roots.

"Most people need to have extra moisture in the hair in order to get a hair roller style to stay," says Maine. To ensure that your hair is properly hydrated, apply blowout mousse, spray, or leave-in conditioner before styling.
