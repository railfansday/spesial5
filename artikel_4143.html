We Have So Many Questions About This Stellar Blade Launch Trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Shift Up has launched a new live-action launch trailer for Stellar Blade: Complete Edition on Switch 2, and it's... well, we have so many questions.

To be fair, the message is clear: take EVE wherever you go, right? It's a nod toward the Switch 2's portable nature, though why EVE is interrupting someone's gaming session to brush their teeth is a bit odd now, isn't it?

We're also a bit puzzled as to why the ad goes for the 4:3 aspect ratio. It's not particularly cinematic or anything, so it doesn't make a whole lot of sense.

Despite our questions, however, it's a fun little ad. Harmless, y'know, and certainly a lot better than the AI-generated slop Shift Up posted to advertise its upcoming sequel.

Stellar Blade: Complete Edition is out now for the Switch 2. We awarded it a score of 7/10 in our review, praising the combat and exploration, though noting that the visuals and performance aren't quite as good as we'd hoped.
