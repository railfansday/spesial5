Mouse Work, The Switch 2 Game Using Joy-Con Mouse Controls, Is Finally Releasing

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Remember Mouse Work? It was announced by developer Nitrome for the Switch 2 just a few months before the system's launch and requires you to use mouse controls to complete all sorts of fun and challenging jobs.

We've not got an update about this title since last year, but it looks like it's finally on the way. According to a listing that has recently appeared on the Japanese Nintendo Store and eShop, it will be arriving this holiday season on 7th December 2026 for 2,200 yen (about $14 USD).

The translated description notes how the campaign is over 10 hours across more than 50 unique stages. You'll also be able to participate in local and online multiplayer, including GameShare support. When we hear more about the local release, we'll provide an update.

Nitrome has previously released games such as Bomb Chicken and Shovel Knight Dig on the original Switch. Are you excited for its new game on Switch 2? Let us know in the comments.
