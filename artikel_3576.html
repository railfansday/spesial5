Former Teacher Arrested for Holding Baby After Sexting Underage Student

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

A former teacher in Kentucky was arrested for a second time after she allegedly violated her bond conditions by holding a newborn baby.

Rachel Clement’s bond was revoked on Monday, November 23, after prosecutors learned of the incident. Clement was arrested for a second time after she held the 2-week-old child when she went to a church that is attached to a school on November 13 to drop off birthday party decorations.

Clement previously pleaded guilty to two counts of sexual abuse and one count of evidence tampering in October after she was caught having a sexual relationship with a student at Henderson High School in Henderson, Kentucky, according to The Hendersonian.

As part of her plea deal, Clement, 29, registered as a sex offender, was barred from school premises and was prohibited from having contact with minors except for her biological niece, according to the outlet.

Following her second arrest, Clement’s attorney, Jonathan Coomes, argued that his client was at the church at the pastor’s request. He also told the outlet that she had no contact with school-age children and that she left the premises before students arrived.

Coomes went on to argue that the child that Clement held was “not similar in characteristics to the victim in the present case,” noting that her victim was a teenager. It is not currently clear if Clement had a relation to the child in question.

Clement is facing up to three years in prison on each sexual abuse charge and five years for evidence tampering. She is scheduled to be sentenced on January 4, 2027.

The former teacher was initially arrested in May when she was accused of sending sexually explicit text messages to a student.

Authorities became aware of Clement’s crime when someone reported her inappropriate relationship with the student to high school officials. After the school contacted police, she was immediately removed from the school.

Clement was initially charged with two counts of unlawful use of electronic means to induce a minor to engage in sexual or other prohibited actions and first-degree sexual abuse and tampering with physical evidence.

She was eventually only charged with two counts of first-degree sexual abuse with a position of authority or special trust, and the first-degree sex abuse charge was dropped as part of her plea deal.
