3 Elite Prime Video Series to Binge This Weekend (Nov 7-8)

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

It's been a strong year for Prime Video, packed with some of the most must-see new and returning shows. From the Alan Ritchson-led fourth season of Reacher and the final season of Eric Kripke's superhero satire series The Boys to Mike Flanagan's latest Stephen King adaptation, Carrie, and the brilliantly underrated Riz Ahmed-led Bait, there's been something for everyone on Prime Video this year. With just two months left in 2026, there's still a lot more to be enjoyed on the streamer. With that in mind, here's a look at three must-watch shows on Prime Video this weekend.

For more recommendations, check out our list of the best shows and movies on Prime Video.

November is already starting strong on Prime Video with a brand-new biopic series about one of the best sports stars to ever live. The Greatest, from executive producer Michael B. Jordan and creator Ben Watkins, stars Jaleen Best as the titular boxing icon, as the highs, lows, and in-betweens of his personal and professional life are chronicled in fine detail.

All American: Homecoming star Best leads this perfect binge-watch for any sports fan. He thrives, "mimicking Ali’s cadence and bravado while also portraying the shy young man behind the scenes," according to a review by Collider's Victoria Luxford. All eight episodes in the series are available to watch at once, making for the perfect way to spend this early November weekend. Omari Hardwick, Dana Gourrier, Kai Parham, and Amin Joseph also star.

A list of the must-watch shows on Prime Video this weekend would be incomplete without the #1 show on the platform right now. After four years away from our screens, The Terminal List finally returned late last month, as Chris Pratt's former Navy SEAL James Reece agrees to hunt down terrorist networks eight months after the events of Season 1.

The Terminal List: Dark Wolf might've bridged the gap last year, but nothing can quite beat this pulse-racing main series. Pratt is back to his best in Season 2, which "leans into everything that makes the franchise work and refuses to walk back everything else," according to Collider's Michael John Petty's 8/10 review. For action, tension, and unmissable drama, join the rest of the world in watching The Terminal List this weekend.

If you'd prefer to uncover a hidden gem on Prime Video this weekend, then look no further than this two-season series that originally aired on the largely defunct Facebook Watch service. The MCU's Elizabeth Olsen stars in Sorry For Your Loss, which follows one woman's battle with the loss of her husband, as her life changes around her and she learns more about the man she thought she knew.

This psychological drama went completely under the radar in 2018 and 2019, but earned strong praise from those critics who did see it, illustrated by a 97% approval rating on the review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes. An honest, thoughtful examination of some pretty tough themes, Olsen shines as the lead of this detailed drama, supported by the likes of Kelly Marie Tran, Jovan Adepo, and Janet McTeer.
