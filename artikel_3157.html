Can the Blue Wave Save Renewable Energy Stocks?

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Renewable energy stocks surged on the Nov. 3 election results, before giving ground the next day. What should investors really expect for renewable energy stocks?

Jeff Santoro and Jason Hall break down why Enphase Energy (ENPH -1.74%), First Solar (FSLR +2.75%), and SolarEdge (SEDG +5.00%) surged and then fell after the election results. Jason makes the case for Brookfield Renewable (BEP +1.63%) (NYSE:BEPC) as the best renewable stock to buy right now.

Jason Hall has positions in Brookfield Renewable Partners, Enphase Energy, First Solar, and SolarEdge Technologies and has the following options: long January 2028 $35 calls on Enphase Energy, short January 2028 $40 puts on Enphase Energy, and short November 2026 $35 puts on SolarEdge Technologies. Jeff Santoro has positions in Enphase Energy and is short shares of Enphase Energy. The Motley Fool has positions in and recommends First Solar.
