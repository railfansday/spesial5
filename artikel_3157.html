Alcon Inc. (ALC -2.16%) reported third-quarter net sales of $2.7 billion, representing 5% growth year over year. Management narrowed its full-year 2026 guidance, forecasting approximately 6% sales growth and 12% to 14% core diluted EPS growth. While the Surgical segment benefited from 11% growth in equipment revenue, the Implantables sub-segment faced a 2% decline due to competitive activity in the U.S. premium intraocular lens market. The company acquired Neurotech Pharmaceuticals for $550 million to expand its retina portfolio, targeting peak sales between $250 million and $400 million for the newly acquired treatment for Macular Telangiectasia Type 2. Core operating margin expanded 150 basis points to 21.8%, aided by $60 million in nonrecurring tariff refunds and ongoing manufacturing efficiencies.
