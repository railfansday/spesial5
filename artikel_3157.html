Think It's Too Late to Buy Cloudflare Stock? Here's the 1 Reason Why There's Still Time

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Cloudflare has nearly quadrupled in three years, but management says it has captured only about 1% of the market it's targeting.

Cloudflare (NET +9.91%) stock has gained roughly 280% over the past three years through Nov. 5, 2026. But it could still reward patient investors for one reason: The business remains small compared with the market management is targeting.

Cloudflare helps businesses keep their websites, applications, and networks fast and secure. At its June 9 investor day, management estimated a $238 billion addressable market for 2026, based on Gartner research. That's the potential annual spending in the markets its products serve, not a revenue forecast. Management projects that opportunity will reach $384 billion by 2029.

Against that backdrop, Cloudflare expects its full-year 2026 revenue to be nearly $3 billion -- representing only about 1% of the addressable market. It doesn't need to dominate every market it serves to become a much larger business. Winning even a modest share of that spending could support years of growth.

Cloudflare is growing rapidly. Third-quarter revenue grew 39% year over year to $783 million -- its fastest growth in nearly four years. Management expects fourth-quarter revenue to be up about 38% year over year, indicating strong momentum heading into 2027.

The stock is priced for growth, trading at 34 times trailing sales as of Nov. 5. Companies operating in an expanding market and growing revenue rapidly are usually worth holding at a premium. With just a tiny fraction of its opportunity captured, it's not too late to buy the stock. It looks more like a company in the early stages of growth than one nearing maturity.
