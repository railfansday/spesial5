ByteDance nears $500m Azure deal for OpenAI models, The Information reports

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

OpenAI has complained to Microsoft that Chinese firms distil its models. Separately, banks have paused a $480m loan for Nvidia chips that ByteDance would use, Bloomberg reported.

ByteDance is in advanced talks to spend about $500m more on Microsoft’s Azure cloud, mostly to use OpenAI’s models, The Information reported on Wednesday. Leo Schwartz, Qianer Liu and Aaron Holmes cited two people with knowledge of the talks.

The deal would add to the TikTok owner’s already large Azure contract. It has also angered OpenAI, according to the report. TNW has not independently verified it.

OpenAI blocks most Chinese companies from using its models. Microsoft holds the rights to sell OpenAI’s technology through Azure, but it does not apply the same restrictions, The Information reported.

In recent months, OpenAI has complained to Microsoft that Chinese firms are “distilling” its AI, according to the people. Distilling means running a model and using its answers to train a rival one. A report last month said DeepSeek and Moonshot trained new models on OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra.

The report does not say ByteDance itself has distilled OpenAI’s models. ByteDance builds its own AI, including the Seedream image models, and reportedly plans its own AI agent.

The talks come as another of ByteDance’s routes to US AI hardware freezes. Banks have paused work on a $480m loan for Nvidia chips that ByteDance would use, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter.

Singapore-based Aolani Cloud wanted the loan to buy GPUs for a data centre in Malaysia. On Monday, the US began requiring data centre operators in countries including Singapore and Malaysia to get a licence for advanced AI chips. The new US rule targets Chinese firms that rent such chips remotely.

Bankers at five lenders discussed pulling out, and some called the loan dead, according to Bloomberg. Aolani said it was working with regulators and its lenders to deliver the project within the rule. DBS, which is arranging the loan, declined to comment.
