Atmos Rewards offers six communities, each with its own travel perks and opportunities to earn bonus points

Atmos Rewards is the airline loyalty program for Alaska Airlines and Hawaiian Airlines. One of its newer features is Atmos Communities, which offer additional rewards and benefits tailored to different travel interests.

There are six communities, including two reserved for Alaska and Hawaii residents. The other four are open to members regardless of where they live. But don’t get caught up in the community names. The best choice for you may depend more on your travel plans than the groupings suggest.

Here’s how to decide which Atmos Community best fits your travel patterns.

Atmos Communities are like groups within the Atmos Rewards program that offer additional benefits based on different travel interests.

They’re free to join for Atmos Rewards members. Four communities are available to all members, while community Club 49 is reserved for Alaska residents and community Huaka’i by Hawaiian is reserved for Hawaii residents.

You can belong to only one community at a time and switch communities once per calendar year. Consider your upcoming travel plans before choosing.

Joining a community is easy: Log in to your Atmos Rewards account and navigate to “Account overview.” Select “Join a Community,” browse the options and click the “Join” button under the one you choose.

Each Atmos Community has a list of 10 destinations where members can earn bonus points and status points. Although the destinations differ by community, the rewards structure is the same.

Through Dec. 31, 2027, members can earn up to 10,000 bonus points and 10,000 status points by visiting qualifying destinations.

Here’s how many points you’ll earn as you reach each milestone:

Visit one destination, earn 100 status and bonus points.

Visit two destinations, earn 200 status and bonus points.

Visit three destinations, earn 300 status and bonus points.

Visit four destinations, earn 400 status and bonus points.

Visit five destinations, earn 500 status and bonus points.

Visit six destinations, earn 750 status and bonus points.

Visit seven destinations, earn 1,000 status and bonus points.

Visit eight destinations, earn 1,250 status and bonus points.

Visit nine destinations, earn 2,500 status and bonus points.

Visit 10 destinations, earn 3,000 status and bonus points.

The first member of each community to visit all 10 qualifying destinations during the challenge period will also earn 100,000 bonus points.

New lists of qualifying destinations for each community take effect on Jan. 1, 2028.

Here’s a closer look at the six communities, their benefits and the destinations eligible for bonus points.

Global Locals is geared toward frequent international travelers. Benefits include:

Earn 10% more status points toward Atmos elite status on flights that begin and/or end outside the U.S., including award flights.

Earn 1 status point for every 25 points transferred to Atmos Rewards from Bilt or Marriott Bonvoy.

Get 20% off Atmos Rewards Unlocked experiences.

If you frequently travel internationally or have any upcoming flights through any of these airports, including qualifying layovers, Global Locals might be a fit for you.

Exclusively for Alaska residents, Club 49 offers rewards that are focused on travel to, from and within the state:

Two free checked bags when traveling to or from Alaska on Alaska Airlines or Hawaiian Airlines.

Three free checked bags when traveling within Alaska on Alaska Airlines.

Two Travel Now discounts per year for 30% off one-way refundable Y-class fares on Alaska Airlines, booked within four days of departure for travel to, from or within Alaska.

Ship cargo within Alaska for $49 plus tax (weight, size and content limits apply).

Get 20% off Atmos Rewards Unlocked experiences.

Baggage benefits don’t combine with other free checked bag allowances, such as those that come with some Alaska credit cards. However, they apply to everyone traveling on the same reservation.

Two free checked bags on neighbor island flights on Hawaiian Airlines, including sports equipment like surfboards and golf bags.

Quarterly fare discounts of 10% off one neighbor island booking, or 20% off for eligible Hawaiian Airlines® World Elite Mastercard® and Atmos™ Rewards Visa® cardholders.

Earn 50% more points and status points on flights between Hawaiian islands beginning Jan. 1, 2027.

Seven destinations are in Hawaii, with one on the mainland and two international cities. If you live in Hawaii and like to hop between islands, this community is a strong choice.

Don’t be persuaded by the names of the communities. Look a little deeper, because there’s a lot of overlap between destination lists. Compare the options against your upcoming travel plans.

You can change communities once per calendar year, so you aren’t locked in to your initial choice indefinitely. However, switching communities resets your destination-challenge progress, but you’ll retain any points you’ve already earned.

If your home airport (or one you visit frequently) is already on a community’s list, you may have an easier time earning points. Also consider whether a particular community benefit, such as free checked bags or points back on award flights for kids, outweighs the destination challenge.

Finally, pay attention to airport codes. For example, Tokyo’s Narita (NRT) and Haneda (HND) appear on different community lists. New York’s qualifying airport is JFK, while it is DCA in Washington, D.C. If you have a choice between airports, check which one qualifies for your community’s challenge.
