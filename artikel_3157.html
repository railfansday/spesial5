iQiyi: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Shifting content costs onto creators via revenue sharing leaves iQiyi's hit-driven model in question as AI production scales.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 21, 2026 at 5:31PM EST

On its third quarter of 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 16, 2026, iQiyi (IQ -10.96%) announced that content costs declined as it returned to non-GAAP (adjusted accounting) operating profit. The streaming platform is shifting toward artificial intelligence production and decentralized content sharing to lower upfront financial risk. Three takeaways from the call highlight this strategic pivot.

Shifting content creation toward revenue sharing allows third-party creators to absorb production costs while iQiyi pays based on viewer performance. This model lowers fixed cash outlays and reduces the platform's dependence on unpredictable blockbuster releases.

Deploying AIGC (artificial intelligence-generated content) helps the platform turn existing IP (intellectual property) into commercial projects much faster. The three chapters of The Ferry Man series each recouped their entire budget during their first week online.

Short-form dramas--episodes running 15 to 30 minutes--offer quick production turns and lower risk than traditional serialized shows. iQiyi captured the top market share in this fast-growing category during the quarter.

Content costs are falling while the platform builds dedicated online creator tools and expands connected television advertising. Management is strengthening its subscription service and creator network while using artificial intelligence to accelerate output.

By focusing on disciplined investments and expanding revenue-sharing models, management aims to turn efficiency gains into consistent long-term cash flow.
