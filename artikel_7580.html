Honduran Citizen and Convicted Felon Indicted for Illegal Re-entry

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

NEW ORLEANS, LOUISIANA – On November 20, 2026 a federal grand jury indicted CESAR RICARDO VELASQUEZ BERNARDEZ a/k/a “CESAR VELASQUEZ,” a/k/a “RICARDO VELASQUEZ,” a/k/a “CESAR RICARDO VELASQUEZ,” a/k/a “CESAR RICARDO VELASQUEZ-BERNARDEZ,” a/k/a “CESAR VELASQUEZ-BERNARDEZ,” (“VELASQUEZ BERNARDEZ”), age 43, a native of Honduras, for re-entry of a removed alien, in violation of Title 8, United States Code, Sections 1326(a) and (b)(1), announced United States Attorney David I. Courcelle.
