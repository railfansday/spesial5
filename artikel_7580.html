Reddit tests new search ads

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

In a blog post published on November 3, Reddit revealed that it's testing a new ad product. Alongside a "select group of eligible advertisers," the social platform is experimenting with paid media posts that show up in its search results.

The new ads will harness Reddit's "existing ad targeting" to surface relevant product recommendations on search pages. The blog post provided two examples of how that process could work: A search for "espresso station" could show results for coffee machines, beans, and mugs, and a query for "home office equipment" could deliver paid listings for monitors, desks, and chairs.
