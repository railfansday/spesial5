Patriots list eight players as questionable after ruling out A.J. Brown, TreVeyon Henderson

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Patriots officially ruled out wide receiver A.J. Brown (ankle/knee) and running back TreVeyon Henderson (illness).

Coach Mike Vrabel had already announced both would miss Sunday’s game against the Packers.

Brown has played only three games this season, with the most recent coming on Oct. 22. Henderson released a statement on Friday saying that he was recently hospitalized for neurological reasons and that he is working with medical professionals to return to health.

Tight end Hunter Henry (foot), cornerback Marcus Jones (foot), safety Kevin Byard III (chest), cornerback Carlton Davis III (neck), wide receiver Mack Hollins (calf), offensive tackle Morgan Moses (foot), safety Brenden Schooler (hamstring) and offensive lineman Greg Van Roten (hamstring) are questionable.

Defensive tackle Christian Barmore (illness), outside linebacker Gabe Jacas (knee), linebacker Dre’Mont Jones (shoulder/knee), cornerback Marcus Jones (foot) and quarterback Drake Maye (right shoulder) do not have a game status for Sunday. All five were full participants.
