Why Did Cerebras Stock Soar Today?

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Jensen Huang is back at the helm of Nvidia, and all's well with AI again.

By Rich Smith – Nov 23, 2026 at 12:16PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Cerebras Systems ›

Cerebras (CBRS +19.69%) stock bounced back in a big way Monday, soaring 19.2% through 10:45 a.m. ET. And why?

This morning, Nvidia (NVDA +32.42%) announced its board has voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO. Shares of Nvidia ripped 35.5% higher as shorts were forced to burn their bets against the stock and buy it back in apparently massive quantities.

https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director

With the future of AI looking suddenly surer, other AI stocks -- Cerebras included -- are soaring.

And yet, I cannot help but wonder if this rally -- in Cerebras stock -- makes sense. On the one hand, yes, Nvidia has been described (by detractors) as creating a "circular economy" -- but also (by fans) as turning Nvidia into the "Central Bank of AI." To that extent, when Nvidia's business stumbles, you could argue this is bad news for most AI companies.

But I'm not so sure it would be bad news for Cerebras in particular. Recall that Cerebras's whole business model is built around replacing Nvidia chips with newer, faster, wafer-scale chips that have the added benefit of building their own memory into the wafer. In that regard, Cerebras is really a direct competitor to Nvidia -- not a dependent.

For this reason, I actually think a revival at Nvidia is bad news for Cerebras, because it un-hobbles a Cerebras competitor. With Jensen Huang now back in the saddle at Nvidia, I'd be more inclined to buy Nvidia stock at 8.4x trailing sales than Cerebras stock at more than 20x sales.

Investors buying Cerebras today may actually be making a huge mistake.
