Joe Milton had Tom Brady losing his mind in comeback win over Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Did anyone have Joe Milton becoming a Thanksgiving day legend on your bingo card? That's exactly what we got on Thursday night as Milton led a miraculous comeback attempt for the Eagles over the Cowboys. Making things even more sweet for Joe? The Cowboys had cut him just several weeks before.

Milton, who may actually be the most physically gifted quarterback in the league, had it all on display. The athleticism to extend, the rocket arm, the screw-it-let-it-fly attitude — and it all worked.

Tom Brady couldn't handle it in the booth. The hall of fame quarterback was beside himself in the booth, in awe of what he was witnessing.

Milton finished 21-31 for 331 yards, throwing three touchdowns. He added 15 yards and another touchdown on the ground. DeVonta Smith caught 11 passes for 200 yards and two touchdowns.

The flashes have always been there for Milton, he just never has really been able to put all the pieces together. On Thursday, with just a matter of weeks being in the Eagles' playbook, he did just that. Milton should have the job for the rest of the season with Jalen Hurts done for the season. It'll be interesting to see if he can keep things going down the stretch.

One note — Milton did all of that in front of the nation while repping a Tennessee bracelet. Very cool.
