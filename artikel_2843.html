Eagles QB Joe Milton III stood and watched his fumble in shades of infamous Cam Newton Super Bowl play. Then he delivered late to beat the Steelers

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Philadelphia quarterback Joe Milton III made an early play to draw the ire of Eagles fans Sunday.

He finished with a big throw to help secure a 19-16 win over the Pittsburgh Steelers and snap a seven-game losing streak.

Milton made his second start for the Eagles Sunday in place of the benched Tanner McKee and sidelined starter Jalen Hurts. He did not do his best to make a good early impression on the home fans.

On Philadelphia's second play from scrimmage, Milton was stripped of the ball in the backfield by Steelers pass rusher Alex Highsmith. But it wasn't the fumble that's likely to draw the ire of Eagles fans. It was his response to the loose ball.

Milton wasn't tackled on the play. And as the ball hit the ground near his feet, he just stood there and looked at it.

Milton made no effort to secure the loose ball that he had a chance at recovering.

Fortunately for the Eagles, left tackle Jordan Mailata did make a play on the ball and was able to secure it before Highsmith could. It was a good break for the Eagles that could go a long way in saving Milton from heat in Philadelphia.

The Eagles were able to convert the recovery into an early 3-0 lead.

The early field goal helped the Eagles to a 13-0 first-quarter lead. Philadelphia's defense did the work from there, holding the Steelers out of the end zone until the final two minutes of the game.

But the game wasn't quite over after the late Steelers score. Philadelphia got the ball back with 1:21 remaining with a 3-point lead in need of a first down to secure the win. Milton delivered with a 7-yard completion to Devonta Smith on third-and-6 to ice the win.

Milton finished the game completing 11-of-21 passes for 141 yards without a touchdown or an interception.

The first-quarter play drew immediate visions of Cam Newton. The former Carolina Panthers great made — or didn't make — a famously familiar play with considerably higher stakes attached.

With his Panthers trailing the Denver Broncos by six points in the fourth quarter of Super Bowl 50, Newton was stripped of the ball by Broncos pass rusher Von Miller.

Like Milton on Sunday, Newton had a shot at recovering the ball and made no effort to do so. But Newton's Panthers weren't as fortunate as the Eagles on Sunday.

The Broncos recovered Newton's fumble deep in Carolina territory to help seal a 24-10 Denver win.

Milton's lack of effort clearly won't go down in infamy like Newton's. And he found himself in the good graces of Eagles fans by game's end.
