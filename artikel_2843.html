Detroit Lions release Friday injury report, game designations

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Detroit Lions (7-2) are readying for a Week 11 matchup against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-6). Detroit is eyeing a sixth straight victory.

The Lions will operate without starting center Cade Mays once more, who was ruled out in Detroit's Friday injury report. Mays will miss his third straight game after suffering an ankle injury against the Minnesota Vikings in the Lions' Week 8 win.

The 6-foot-6, 325-pound center inked a three-year, $25 million deal with the Lions this offseason, but he's played in just a pair of games. Mays began the season on injured reserve after sustaining a broken right wrist during training camp.

Juice Scruggs has played exceptionally well in Mays' absence, ranking as the top center in the NFL per Pro Football Focus. According to PFF, Scruggs has surrendered just one sack and 11 pressures.

Six other Lions were listed as questionable against the Bucs, including Joe Bachie (knee), cornerback Khalil Dorsey (ankle), defensive lineman Tyler Lacy (shoulder), cornerback Ennis Rakestraw Jr. (shoulder), cornerback D.J. Reed (hip), and running back Sione Vaki (ankle).

Lions offensive lineman Miles Frazier wasn't listed on Wednesday or Thursday, but he didn't practice on Friday for personal reasons.

Safety Kerby Joseph (knee) was a full participant and is expected to make his long-awaited return.
