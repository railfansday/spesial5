Cowboys news: Dallas defense gets trampled in loss to the 49ers

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Dallas walked into Sunday afternoon's game as an underdog with a lot to prove. With the Giants losing on Thursday night to the Commanders, and following a victory last week to the Colts, if the Cowboys defense showed some semblance of life, Dallas could turn the season around. Unfortunately, that is not what happened at all. Dallas allowed multiple explosive plays and the defense gave up 580 yards of offense and 46 points.

The Dallas Cowboys faced their biggest test of the season. And in a game where the Cowboys could have made a statement, they fell flat on their face.

The Cowboys allowed 3 touchdowns of longer than 65 yards — all of which occurred without the player scoring the touchdown being touched. They gave up 46 points, 580 yards of offense — 370 passing and 210 rushing.

If this was any other team, the defensive coordinator would be FIRE by tomorrow.

It is tough to pin the loss on a single play, given that the Cowboys defense had multiple opportunities to give the team a chance and continually fell short. However, these five plays definitely had a big impact.

Onside kick decision? – The Cowboys were able to score and cut the lead to six with just 46 seconds left. While the onside kick made sense to try to get the ball back, the fact that the Cowboys had three timeouts could've made the case that they should have just kicked the ball deep.

This is not a game that Dallas was expected to win. But given the defensive improvement over the last few weeks, one would have hoped to see a little more from that side of the ball.

The Niners averaged an obscene 9.4 yards per play. The 584 total yards the Cowboys gave up amounts to the most Dallas has allowed in a home game in team history.

We might very well be watching the worst defense in Cowboys history.

This game feels like another lowlight in a disappointing season. Although the final score may make it seem like Dallas was in it, they were thoroughly beaten by the 49ers.

Brian Schottenheimer is his own worst enemy

When looking back on why/how Dallas lost, Schottenheimer's decision to punt on 4th-and-2 at the end of the first half looms very large. While the offense was at its own 43-yard line, it was also 2-for-2 on fourth downs at that point in the game.

The 49ers and Cowboys obviously do not share much love for each other. And in a game where the 49ers did whatever they so chose to do on offense, the jokes will follow.

The 49ers poked fun at the Cowboys multiple times with Texas- and Dallas-specific posts, including cowboy boots outside of AT&T Stadium with a 49ers cowboy hat on top of them. In one post, lyrics from country star Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas" were in the caption, while another caption read: "Brought the star power."

The Cowboys were given a chance to really tighten the chase in the NFC East. The New York Giants loss to the Washington Commanders gave Dallas a little opening to apply pressure on the Giants, and get their own momentum going.
