Wolfenstein 3 announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Wolfenstein 3 is now in production, publisher Bethesda Softworks and developer MachineGames announced.

The news comes as part of today’s Xbox 25th anniversary celebrations from Bethesda Softworks parent company Xbox CEO Asha Sharma, who tweeted, “Wolfenstein 3 is officially in production at MachineGames. One of gaming’s most important franchises, and a team that has done remarkable work bringing it forward. Looking forward to sharing more.”

The tweet is accompanied by an image of a script from the game.
