Rosie Huntington-Whiteley and Wardrobe.NYC Feted Their Next Collaboration at Chateau Marmont

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Los Angeles may slow down on Sunday evenings, but this past weekend, Chateau Marmont was anything but quiet. On the patio of the hotel’s penthouse, Wardrobe.NYC hosted an al fresco dinner celebrating its latest collaboration with supermodel Rosie Huntington-Whiteley, who also serves as fashion director at FWRD, where the 15-piece collection is exclusively available to shop.

Despite the gusty weather—which left half the table in near darkness as candles repeatedly blew out—longtime fans of the brand turned out for the occasion, including Laura Harrier, Lauren Perez, Vittoria Ceretti, Kirsty Godso, and Stephanie Suganami.

Not only was the first installment of the ongoing collaboration—a lineup of sculptural black-and-white separates that launched in 2024—a smashing success, but it also sparked a close bond between the brand and the style maven. “After the first one, we didn’t want it to be over,” Christine Centenera, a stylist and Vogue Australia’s editor-in-chief who co-founded the line with designer Josh Goot, told Vogue. Centenera was similarly effusive about Huntington-Whiteley’s magnetic energy and work ethic. “She’s really funny and can take criticism well, and she always has a creative solution,” Centenera noted.

The team took cues from the first drop when designing the second installment. “One of the top-selling pieces from the original line was a sculpted blazer, and so that kind of architectural feel was brought through into this collection,” Huntington-Whiteley, in a shearling stole from the capsule, explained. She then turned her attention to Ceretti, who was hobnobbing near the bar. “Vittoria is wearing the really cool shrunken leather bomber and great leather trousers [from the collection],” she added.

Huntington-Whiteley was equally grateful for the trio’s growing friendship. “I get to spend time with two incredibly talented people in the industry, two of my close friends, and create my dream collection that I want to fill my own wardrobe with,” she said. Ultimately, Huntington-Whiteley wanted to develop a versatile lineup that she could mix and match during her hectic international schedule. “It was all about creating a collection that I can keep in my suitcase and travel around with,” she shared. “It’s about the idea of a little black dress and those staple pieces that can take you from trips to events.”

For the first hour of the evening, guests mingled over cocktails and wine, taking in the twinkling city skyline. “The view here is magic,” said entrepreneur Perez, who arrived in a cropped blazer and slouchy low-rise trousers from the label.

Meanwhile, Raissa Gerona, Revolve’s president of brand and partnerships, looked around the lively crowd, appreciative of the community that had come together for the occasion. “Everybody’s so busy, people have kids, and it’s a Sunday, so people have work tomorrow,” she said. “So for everyone to show up to support Rosie and the brand, that’s the best thing I can ask for.”

Attendees settled into their seats at a candlelit table on the terrace, where servers promptly arrived with baguettes and French butter, roasted beet salad with goat cheese, burrata and tomatoes, and yellowtail crudo. Centenera and Huntington-Whiteley then rose to address the intimate crowd, thanking everyone—as much as possible over the whistling wind—for coming out to celebrate their latest creative pursuit.

Once guests finished their first round of dishes, the main course arrived: roasted chicken with mixed vegetables, and prime petit filet with asparagus spears, grilled broccolini, and truffle fries. The night ended on a sweet note, with bowls of berries and cream served for dessert, banishing any sign of the Sunday scaries.
