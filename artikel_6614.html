Andy Cohen Reveals The Chaotic Meal He Enjoys With His Kids Every Sunday: 'This Is What Makes Us A Family'

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Plus, the childhood snack combo he still swears by.

After enough New Year’s Eves throwing back shots with Anderson Cooper, Andy Cohen seems like someone you can trust on one very important topic: curing the dreaded next-day hangover.

“Gosh, just a cheeseburger,” Cohen tells me when I ask what food he swears by the morning after drinking. More specifically, he heads to Corner Bistro in New York City’s West Village for one.

Booze just so happens to be part of Cohen’s latest gig, as he’s teamed up with Svedka ahead of the 2027 Super Bowl.

After the brand’s AI Super Bowl ad drew heavy backlash last year, the vodka brand is challenging the public to create a commercial that’s even better. At stake is a $500,000 cash prize and the chance for the winning ad to air during the big game, with Cohen serving as one of the judges.

“I just think this contest is amazing, and I can’t wait to judge it,” he tells me. “This is really giving people a huge stage and permission to be creative surrounding a drink that I love.”

His taste in drinks may be his own, but burgers apparently run in the family—his son orders them during their weekly dinner ritual.

“We go out every Sunday night to the same restaurant in New York City, and [my daughter] has chicken noodle soup; he has a cheeseburger,” Cohen shares. “My daughter eats everything and has the best taste. She has pickles and broccoli with her soup—it’s the most disjointed meal. I have a turkey burger, but this is our process, and this is what makes us a family.”

Talking about what his kids eat also sent Cohen back to a few favorites from his own childhood. “Spaghettios and Doritos make a great combination,” he tells me, a pairing he grew up eating and still stands behind.

Then there’s Provel cheese, another longtime favorite. “[It’s a] big St. Louis thing; it’s kind of gross,” Cohen says.

Not exactly a ringing endorsement, but he still loves it anyway.
