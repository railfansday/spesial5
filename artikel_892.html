Kane vows to be back for ‘business end of season’ after ankle surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Harry Kane was withdrawn after 20 minutes of Bayern's Bundesliga game against Cologne; the injury comes days after the England captain became his country's most-capped player; Kane won the Ballon d'Or earlier this month; Michael Olise also taken off injured in 1-1 draw

Harry Kane is targeting the "business end of the season" after undergoing surgery on a torn ligament in his left ankle.

Bayern Munich said the operation on the injury, sustained in Friday's 1-1 draw with Cologne, was a success but the 33-year-old is expected to be out for months as a result.

Kane wrote on social media: "The highs and lows of life! Obviously very disappointed to get this injury but as always it's an opportunity to show resilience and improvement going into 2027.

"I'll be back fresh and ready to go for the business end of the season!

"Tough times don't last, tough people do" - my guy @edelman11 (Julian Edelman, three-time Super Bowl winner with the New England Patriots) told me this one!

"Thank you for all the messages! Alles gut."

Kane - who became England's most-capped player last week - sustained the injury when he was scythed down in the area by Cologne defender Joel Schmied, who reached Michael Olise's low cross first but clattered into the Ballon d'Or winner's left leg in his follow-through at the back post.

The England captain writhed in pain and, although he gingerly got back to his feet and initially soldiered on, it was clear he could not continue and he hobbled off the field alongside two physios after treatment.

Kane had a quick exchange with Vincent Kompany, who was back in the Bayern hot-seat for the first time in six matches after being diagnosed with meningeal irritation last month, before heading down the tunnel.

After the match Bayern confirmed Kane had torn the syndesmotic ligament and "will therefore be sidelined for the next few months".

Kane earlier this month signed a new deal with the Bundesliga giants until June 2029 - reportedly worth in excess of £400,000 a week, making him the highest-paid player in Europe before bonuses.

Since arriving from Tottenham three years ago, Kane has bagged a scarcely believable 156 goals in 163 appearances in all competitions, helping Bayern win a league and cup double last season.

He went into the international break having a drawn a blank in his four previous matches for Bayern but he scored twice for England, including from the spot in their 3-2 Nations League win over Spain.

But after being forced off after 20 minutes at the Allianz Arena, replaced by Tom Bischof, Kane could now be facing a battle to be fully fit for England's two-legged quarter-final against Norway in March.

A three-month period on the sidelines would rule Kane out for 11 Bundesliga games, the DFB-Pokal round of 16 tie against Hertha Berlin, as well as all four of Bayern's remaining Champions League league phase matches.

Providing his recovery goes to plan, that would give the striker just over a month to prepare for England's Nations League quarter-final.

The Three Lions face Norway in Oslo in the first leg on March 27, before the return leg at Wembley on March 30.

Henry Winter speaking on Back Pages Tonight:

"I've covered Harry Kane's career for so many years and there was a period where he had almost a chronic problem with his ankles.

"It's three months and, most immediately, it's Bayern Munich's concern. But England have the home and away Nations League quarter-finals against Norway in late March.

"The timeline would suggest he'll be back in that time, but thoughts with Thomas Tuchel tonight because he'll be absolutely concerned about this.

"He's such an important player. England's No 9. There's a bit of a gap behind him if you're looking for No 9s. I know Jude Bellingham can play there and that's probably the long-term plan. But this is hugely alarming."
