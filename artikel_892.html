Price looking to continue trend of Grand Slam victors tasting Worlds glory

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Gerwyn Price won the Grand Slam of Darts with a 16-11 victory over Gian van Veen; the last four winners of the Grand Slam have gone on to win the World Darts Championship at Alexandra Palace; Price is hoping to continue the trend

Sports Journalist @MegWellensX

An interesting trend has occurred over the last four years: The winner of the Grand Slam has gone on to win the World Darts Championship.

It started with Michael Smith back in 2022, with Luke Humphries continuing it in 2023.

Of course, Luke Littler's back-to-back triumphs in Wolverhampton in 2024 and 2025 were then followed by him lifting the Sid Waddell Trophy.

Gerwyn Price wants to continue that in 2026.

Price roared to a 16-11 victory over Gian van Veen in the final of the Grand Slam, hitting five massive ton-plus checkouts in a confident display.

The win moves Price up to world No 3 ahead of Humphries and marks his first major title in four years.

Alexandra Palace was immediately on Price's mind following his victory and he hopes to extend the trend into a fifth year and become a two-time world champion.

"The sweet thing about this is the last couple of years, whoever won has gone on to win the World [Championship], so....," Price said after beating Van Veen.

"If that happens, then I'll take six months out!"

"I am superstitious and I believe in stuff like that," he later added.

"I hope I am not the one to break the trend."

Price has been in some form in 2026, the Grand Slam marking his fourth major final of the year but his first victory.

The Welshman has maintained he is the most consistent player across 2026 and puts part of that down to the love he now gets from the crowd.

It is a very different story from when he last won a Grand Slam title five years ago.

"How different it is, the crowd right behind me and cheering me all the way. How things can change." Price added.

"I thought these days would happen, I just didn't know when. I am glad it happened sooner rather than later.

"It definitely helps and spurs you on to come out to a crowd who is right behind you rather than against you.

"I have had that for the last two-and-a-half years and hopefully it continues.

"People have just realised I am who I am, I wear my heart on my sleeve, and I don't do things to put people off which people think I did.

"I am the most consistent. I am not saying I am the best player.

"I am full of confidence, I am in some great form. I am the most consistent across this season."

With the form 'The Iceman' has been in, it is remarkable it had been so long since Price had last tasted major glory back in 2022.

While he says it did not bother him, being reminded by the "media" spurred him on.

"You guys [the media] were putting pressure on me and reminding me I hadn't won a tournament in a long time," Price said.

"You probably spurred me on as well so thanks.

"I keep saying I am not bothered when I lose and I stand by that.

"Obviously it hurts to lose. I have now got you off my back, not the monkey."
