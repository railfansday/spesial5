Great Britain end seven-year wait to reach Davis Cup semi-finals

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Great Britain advanced to Davis Cup semi-finals after beating Germany 2-1; Cameron Norrie defeated Jan-Lennard Struff before Alexander Zverev beat Arthur Fery to level match; Neal Skupski and Henry Patten won deciding doubles rubber to book tie against either Austria or Spain

Great Britain have taken a step closer to ending their 11-year wait for a Davis Cup title, as Leon Smith's side advanced to the semi-finals with a thrilling 2-1 victory over Germany.

Neal Skupski and Henry Patten outlasted Kevin Krawietz and Tim Puetz in a nail-biting second-set tiebreak to seal a 6-2, 7-6(8-6) win and end Great Britain's seven-year wait for a Davis Cup semi-final appearance.

It came after world No 1 Alexander Zverev had drawn the tie level at 1-1 with a comfortable 6-3 6-2 win over Arthur Fery.

Earlier in the day, Cameron Norrie got Great Britain off to a fast start, besting Jan-Lennard Struff 6-3 3-6 6-2.

Great Britain will now play the winner of Austria and Spain's match on Saturday. Three-time defending champions Italy face Czech Republic in the other semi-final.

After sweeping Krawietz and Puetz away in the first set, the German duo hit back in the following exchange, forcing the Brits to a tiebreak.

With a spot in the semi-final on the line, Krawietz and Puetz pushed their opponents to 5-5 in the decider, before Skupski produced the most incredible reaching backhand at the net, to keep his side ahead by a point.

Germany hit back and won the next point, but Britain were too strong, with Skupski sending Britain through to the semi-finals with a booming cross-court return.

"They are a very experienced team," Patten said.

"They've been playing together a lot longer than us. That's maybe an advantage; maybe they don't know what to expect from me and Neal, but they are a world-class outfit and they have had success for many, many years and lots of success in the Davis Cup as well.

"Helps having a guy on your team that can hit a return like that on match point."

Norrie cruised through the first set after breaking Struff at 3-1.

But Struff hit back, winning a break to go 3-5 in the next exchange to capitalise on a few poor errors from the Brit.

However, Norrie bounced back in the final set, emphatically saving four break points at 3-2, before breaking Struff twice late on to wrap up a big victory.

In a repeat of this year's Wimbledon singles semi-final, Zverev once again showed why he has risen to the world No 1 standings, comfortably and patiently dispatching Fery in an hour and 11 minutes.

Fery fought hard in the first set to keep level with the German, but Zverev was too powerful, pinning Fery deep into his court in the first set to break the 24-year-old at 2-2.

Another break followed to seal the first set, with Zverev swiping a booming winner across the court.

Fery has never beaten Zverev, nor has he won a set off the world No 1 in their three meetings.

The second set continued in a similar vein, with Zverev breaking Fery twice to keep his side in the clash.

By winning, Zverev also achieved a significant milestone, joining an exclusive group of players to record 70 tour-level wins in one calendar year. Only 12 other players have done so, with Roger Federer having done it six times.

"The team needed me today," Zverev said to the BBC. "Of course we needed the win. I'm happy that I could deliver today."
