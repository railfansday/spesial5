Galaxy Digital Says Now Is the Time to Buy Bitcoin. Here's Why It's Right.

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Bitcoin likely bottomed this summer, and is now in an accumulation phase.

With Bitcoin (BTC +0.28%) currently trading under $70,000, now might not seem like the best time to be loading up on the world's most popular cryptocurrency. After all, Bitcoin is still 45% below its all-time high of $126,000 from last October.

But there are plenty of analysts and investors who think this could be a fantastic time to buy Bitcoin. Among them is Galaxy Digital (GLXY -5.27%), which recently published a report called "Why Now Is the Time to Add and Hold Bitcoin" that makes a strong buy case for Bitcoin.

According to Galaxy Digital, based on a reading of the historical data, Bitcoin hit a bottom at $60,000 this summer, and is now in the accumulation phase.

As Galaxy Digital points out, Bitcoin is highly cyclical, typically following a four-year pattern of boom and bust. If Bitcoin continues the same pattern that has repeated after the 2014, 2018, and 2022 crypto downturns, then Bitcoin could be ready to go on another of its parabolic runs.

Moreover, Galaxy suggests that the sellers are mostly gone as the short-term momentum traders and speculators who rushed into crypto when Bitcoin was soaring past $100,000 have now been washed out. The only money that's left is the long-term money with buy-and-hold conviction. That's going to help to put a floor under the price of Bitcoin.

Finally, the macroeconomic situation in the U.S. -- mounting deficits, rising inflation, and a $40 trillion national debt load -- all point to currency debasement. In other words, the thinking is that the U.S. government will need to print money to get out of a financial jam, and that's going to end up reducing the value of the U.S. dollar. As a result, many investors may turn to Bitcoin as a potential hedge against inflation and currency debasement.

The question on many Bitcoin investors' minds is, of course: How much higher Bitcoin can go over the next few years? If history is any guide, the upside potential might range from 7.6x to 87x. That's how much Bitcoin rose in previous market cycles, according to Galaxy Digital.

Of course, history does not always repeat, so relying just on past trends to invest would be unwise. However, I'm optimistic. While there's plenty of reason for concern, Bitcoin appears to be a coiled spring right now. I'm fully expecting it to shrug off the current market malaise and march higher over the next 12 months. If Bitcoin doesn't hit $100,000 next year, I'll be very surprised.
