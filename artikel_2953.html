Eagles injury update: Joe Milton might not get much extra help

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Eagles injury update: Joe Milton might not get much extra help originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

The Eagles are switching quarterbacks for Week 9 but it doesn't look like Joe Milton is going to get a lot more help.

Several key starters were missing when the Eagles took the practice field on Wednesday afternoon as the team began to prepare for the Giants.

Here's the full injury report from Wednesday:

DNP – RB Saquon Barkley (hamstring/back), LB Jihaad Campbell (hamstring/quad), DB Michael Carter (abdomen), WR Britain Covey (knee), CB Jonathan Jones (hamstring), WR DeVonta Smith (hamstring), WR Dontayvion Wicks (ankle)

LIMITED – K Jake Elliott (right ankle), OLB Jonathan Greenard (ankle), OLB Jalyx Hunt (knee/ankle)

FULL – LB Zack Baun (shoulder), T Markel Bell (finger), DT Jordan Davis (thumb), G Landon Dickerson (hip), TE Dallas Goedert (knee), QB Tanner McKee (chest), DB Kenny Moore (glute), DT Moro Ojomo (neck)

Elliott was added to the report on Wednesday for the first time. That's something to monitor going forward.

Barkley was able to play through his injuries on Sunday night but played just 22 snaps after playing just 10 against the Cowboys the week before. Barkley missed the first two days of practice last week before playing, so that could be the plan again this week.

Meanwhile, both Smith and Wicks have missed the last two weeks and appear on track to miss their third consecutive games on Sunday afternoon. The Eagles will mercifully get their bye week after this next game.

Campbell missed the Commanders game and was replaced by Jeremiah Trotter Jr., who could end up getting another start on Sunday. Before this last game, Campbell and Trotter had been rotating.

Milton, 26, was a sixth-round pick out of Tennessee in the 2024 draft. He played in one game with the Patriots before being traded to the Cowboys. He played in four games for Dallas last year. Sunday will be his first-career start.
