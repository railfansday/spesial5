Ultimate Fans: THIS is the game to break open the rushing attack

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Before I write the rest of the article, I just want to comment on the outstanding play by the Broncos in the Bye Week. IYKYK.

But seriously, UFGers, as we preview the Broncos' first face-off with the Raiders of the season, we have another duo of fans, making this outstanding post double the fun!

And both of our fans are looking forward to an improved run game, better offense and consistent defense. It is the Raiders with a rookie QB after all. But we all know how division games go, so that Mile High orange better be strong and the crowd loud! And we have the perfect preview from Orange Rush Saga and DBforLife to help us do that!

MHR – Bye Week is over and Broncos lost a few spots in the AFC thanks to big wins by some rivals. But the schedule should be a little more forgiving for the Broncos. Is that good or bad for this team, in your opinion?Orange Rush Saga: I think that for the most part, it's a good thing. It feels like it was earned after the gauntlet the team went through at the beginning of the season. I do have a bit of concern about play being softer once playoff time hits, but one thing I've learned about this team is they find a way to win in some of the most outlandish ways.DBforLife: Good. We are looking at Buffalo for the top seed in the AFC.

MHR – No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza makes his first road start after Seattle pressured him on 19 of 38 dropbacks and sacked him six times last week. Denver has 26 sacks through nine games…is this a prime opportunity for the Broncos' defense? Should VJ bring the blitzes or let the four-man rush do the work?Orange Rush Saga: VJ mentioned this week that he's frustrated with the big plays being given up by the defense, so I actually think he may be dialed back a bit. I think he'll poke and prod to find out where Mendoza's rookie mistakes show up most and then alter the game plan to fit. In any case, I trust our four guys up front to generate pressure on their own.DBforLife: Bring it.

MHR – The Raiders' rookie QB scored his first NFL score on a 10-yard designed touchdown run, and Klint Kubiak said he should have moved Mendoza outside the pocket more. Anyone who remembers Gary Kubiak's Broncos knows the bootleg is the family business. How do the Broncos' best keep Mendoza in the pocket?Orange Rush Saga: While Kubiak said he should have moved Mendoza outside the pocket more, I'm not sure he's the type of coach to present more opportunities for his rookie QB to get blasted into the next millennium. The Broncos defense hits HARD, and the physicality is well known, so I'm not sure he wants to subject Mendoza to a similar beating that Seattle applied. If he does, though, I suspect VJ will throw Singleton on him in one way or another.DBforLife: Let Ja'Quan McMillian spy on him.

MHR – Brock Bowers is questionable with an ankle injury, but he leads the team in receptions (50), yards (676), and receiving TDs (6). If he plays, who is the best guy to cover him? If he doesn't play, what receiving threats from the Raiders worry you with this patchwork crew in the secondary?Orange Rush Saga: Well, I was hoping Henley could fill that spot. But alas. It's not a fantastic option, but I think keeping Strnad on him would be the best bet. Singleton gets burned by receivers too often, and Barron has been found flat-footed as of late. If Bowers doesn't play, though, I think Tre Tucker and Jack Bech could create problems at times.DBforLife: Ja'Quan McMillian should cover Bowers.

MHR – Given the above question, the defense did have Riley Moss, Talanoa Hufanga and Brandon Jones all back at practice plus Pat Surtain a few games in after missing three. How much does a healthy (healthier) secondary matter for the stretch run? Who's your defensive player to watch Sunday?Orange Rush Saga: It matters A LOT. We all love the Devon Key INT in Carolina, but the defense loses a lot of its bite without Jones and Hufanga. I'm hoping that the return of those two will take out a large amount of the secondary's vulnerability. My eye will be on Moss, to see just how he plays off his big payday and see what we just signed up for another four years of. (And as a side note, I think the deal was fantastic for the player he is.)DBforLife: Moss. Let's see if he is worth the money

MHR – The Raiders have the league's worst run game(2.9 ypc on average) despite having Ashton Jeanty on the roster. But Denver's run defense is giving up over 120 yards per game on average. Which number wins out? Does Jeanty finally get loose, or does Denver make Vegas one-dimensional?Orange Rush Saga: I think that Jeanty's struggles are more related to Jeanty than the rush defenses he plays against. Mike Washington seems to be operating better, but the run game as a whole is not anything to speak of really. If VJ creates an effective containment plan for Mendoza, I think the rush game will be far less effective.DBforLife: Denver shuts Jeanty down

MHR – Moving to offense, Bo Nix & Co. have scored 30, 28, and 26 points in their last three games, but four of the five wins have been by six points or less. Against a Raiders' defense allowing nearly 30 points a game, is this finally the week Denver's offense puts a team away early or is that just not our M.O.?Orange Rush Saga: I mean… what even is our M.O.? One week the team catches fire in the fourth quarter, and the next week it starts strong and fizzles into conservative play calling after. One thing I have noticed, however, is that the offense is starting to really hum at any point in the game. Having Coleman as the lead back and Bryant back from IR has electrified the offense, and I have confidence that Bo improves substantially as the season goes on. So for this game? My personal prediction is that Coleman lights it up on the ground and control is maintained through time-of-possession.DBforLife: I think we will win by 7.

MHR- Sean Payton noted during the bye the O-line "didn't block very well" and Denver ranks No. 26 in the league with barely 90 rushing yards per game. The Raiders run defense is actually worse than the Broncos' allowing more than 150 rushing yards per game on average. Does Jonah Coleman break it open? What should the run-game approach by Payton this week?Orange Rush Saga: First: Our statistics are probably skewed by the first five games where the defenses were tougher and the offense was dead(er) Second: Run-first is generally a good plan. I liked the balance in Carolina. Third: Jonah Coleman goes ZOOM!DBforLife: Run Coleman

MHR – Sticking with the O-line, Maxx Crosby is never not a problem. Can the line handle him Sunday? He's 0.5 sacks away from getting 75 for his career. Who on the offensive line has the toughest job Sunday, and how do you keep No. 98 from celebrating that milestone at Mile High?Orange Rush Saga: They're probably going to put him against whichever Tackle is most vulnerable. So he might be terrorizing McGlinchey (or Powers) the whole game. But! This is a known terror, not an unknown one. So plans can be made… like chipping when necessary and establishing the run to allow the development of Play Action.DBforLife: Bolles

MHR – Jaylen Waddle has been a full participant at practice after leaving the Panthers' game with a concussion. How much does getting him back change this passing game, and who is Bo Nix's go-to guy Sunday – Waddle, Courtland Sutton, Pat Bryant…or Coleman in the short game?Orange Rush Saga: I am perhaps a bigger Bryant fan than most. But that guy has FIRE. We haven't seen a Waddle takeover game since early in the season, and Court is just a steady safety option every game. I do think that Bo will likely throw mostly to Waddle and Bryant this game, with a wild Adkins here and there.DBforLife: I hope he throws it to Waddle. He cost us a first and a third.

MHR – Nix has 11 touchdowns, 1,730 passing yards, and four interceptions this season, and the national pundits still aren't sold (but they never are). What's your midseason grade/assessment for Nix? What does he need to do down the stretch to get this team in better form for contention? Or is it on him to do?Orange Rush Saga: If we're talking grades, I would give him a B. Started off really struggling, but recently picking it up. I think he just needs to turn the safety off on his arm, because when he does it in the fourth quarter, magic happens. I read a horrific stat today about Denver's conversion percentage of third-and-longs so far this season, and it just confirms to me that the check-downs and screen passes on 3rd and 15 are EXACTLY as effective as they look. Also, let's be honest, Bo could be putting up Burrow numbers right now and "pundits" would give the credit to anyone on the team except him. Some people decide their opinions ahead of time and will shamelessly broadcast their fire when given enough of a stage to express.DBforLife: Just keep winning games.

MHR – How confident are you in this Broncos squad and what would make you 100% sure this team could beat the other big contenders and make it to the Super Bowl?Orange Rush Saga: I've never really had much CONFIDENCE in the team's performance, but I do have confidence that wins will come when they are least expected. My main concern is with the Bengals and Jags right now… they seem to be firing on all cylinders and could easily snatch the #1 seed if Denver slips.DBforLife: Unless Burrow or Lawrence gets injured, we are not making the SB.
