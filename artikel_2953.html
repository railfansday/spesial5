Game Discussion Thread: Knicks at Timberwolves, November 19, 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Fresh off a sweep over the border, the Knicks' (8-6) championship parade makes a stop in Minnesota to face the Timberwolves (11-4) at Target Center. The furballs are tied for fourth place in the leftward conference, have taken eight of their last 10 games, and deploys the NBA's stingiest defense, allowing 106.1 points per game. Even less encouraging is that Minny leads the league with 48.6 boards per game. That doesn't bode super well for a New York team that had serious troubles in the paint against Toronto, but take heart: Anthony Edwards (knee) remains out, Jonathan Kuminga is dealing with a shoulder issue that could hamper him (should he play at all), the Knicks are projected to win most of their positional matchups, and Karl-Anthony Towns knows all of Rudy Gobert's tricks to win box outs.

Tune in, Knicks fans. Tip-off is 8 pm EST on MSG. This is your game thread. This is Canis Hoopus. Please don't post large photos, GIFs, or links to illegal streams in the thread. Mind your manners. And go Knicks!
