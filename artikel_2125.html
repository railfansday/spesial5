New manager bounce already underway with news out of West Ham ahead of Cardiff kick off

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The good news, it seems, keeps on coming today as West Ham fans, players and staff get used to life in the brave new world that is the Scott Parker era.

For skipper Jarrod Bowen, this will be his fifth head coach since the halcyon days of June 2023. It goes without saying that the West Ham squad will take time to adjust to more new tactics, a very different management style and changed coaching methods once Parker’s team is fully embedded at Rush Green.

Whether or not Scott Parker will be in the dugout, having met the team in front of the cameras this morning you’d hope there will be a tangible response from the players for whom the last couple of weeks must have seemed like purgatory.

Ready to rock? Dinos Mavropanos is reportedly ready for the match day squad again, ahead of schedule

And, according to sources, (thewesthamway.com) the good news could get even better with the suggestion that Dinos Mavropanos, at one time suggested to be injured for weeks, could be available for this evening’s Cardiff City tie.

Not only of course for his steadfast defending but with five goals so far this season, Dinos is amongst the league’s top scorers and without his six yard box threat at the weekend West Ham’s set pieces looked less potent.

It will be a real new manager bounce for Scott Parker if the Greek international is named in the squad tonight. Predictions as to the game outcome are all, of course, far more upbeat now that Nuno’s reign has been brought to an end:

Hopefully three points will help put the Hammers back on track. With Middlesborough, travelling away to Wrexham this evening there’s every chance that the ‘Boro will secure their own win, so it is essential that West Ham don’t lose further ground on the league leaders with the gap currently standing at six points.
