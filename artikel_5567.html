Local Business Owners Joyce and Tom Jones Receive One of Annex Brands' Highest Honors at National Convention

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

SAN DIEGO, Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Joyce and Tom Jones, owners of Postal Connections #119 in Redmond, OR, were recognized with one of the highest honors presented within the Annex Brands franchise system, receiving the Crystal Award during the company's 2026 National Convention held October 1-3 at the South Point Hotel & Casino in Las Vegas.

The Crystal Award recognizes franchise owners who generated for the first time more than $5 million in cumulative gross sales.

This year, 11 locations across four Annex Brands franchise concepts were recognized with the Crystal Award, celebrating franchisees who achieved this significant milestone through sustained business growth and operational excellence.

"Receiving the Crystal Award is an extraordinary accomplishment and represents years of dedication, consistency and commitment to excellence," said Patrick Edd, Chief Executive Officer & President of Annex Brands. "The success achieved by Joyce and Tom reflects not only strong business performance, but also the trust built within the community and the lasting relationships developed with customers over many years. This achievement demonstrates what's possible when franchise owners remain focused on delivering exceptional service and creating long-term value for their customers."

The award was presented during Annex Brands' annual awards ceremony, where more than 100 franchise locations were recognized for outstanding performance across multiple categories. The Crystal Award is among the organization's most prestigious recognitions, honoring franchisees who have demonstrated long-term success and sustained growth over an extended period.

"Achieving this award requires more than strong annual sales," Edd added. "It reflects a decade of perseverance, adaptability and customer-focused leadership. Joyce and Tom have built a business that consistently serves customers at a high level while contributing to the strength and success of the Annex Brands network."

The 2026 National Convention welcomed its highest attendance on record, bringing together franchise owners, vendor partners and corporate leadership from across the Annex Brands network under the theme "Elevate." The event featured educational workshops, business development sessions, networking opportunities and recognition programs celebrating franchisee accomplishments.

For the Jones's, the Crystal Award serves as a testament to ten years of dedication to customers, employees and the local community while maintaining a consistent standard of operational excellence.

Annex Brands, Inc. licenses and franchises more than 800 locations in the United States, Canada, and Mexico. Headquartered in San Diego, Calif., Annex Brands has been providing business ownership opportunities since 1985 with their brands PostalAnnex, Pak Mail, AIM Mail Centers, Postal Connections, Parcel Plus, Sunshine Pack & Ship, Navis Pack & Ship and Handle With Care Packaging Store. Locations offer a range of shipping options from multiple carriers such as UPS, FedEx and USPS, as well as freight shipping for large, awkward, high-value, or fragile items. Additional offerings include packaging supplies and custom packing solutions for parcels and wooden crates, as well as mailbox services and other office services such as notary public services, copy and print services, and more. For more information, visit www.annexbrands.com.
