Giants vs. Colts odds: Opening lines for Week 12 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The New York Giants will open Week 12 as 7-point favorites on the road over the Indianapolis Colts, per FanDuel. It isn't a surprise that the Giants are a heavy favorite, even on the road, after they upset the Jacksonville Jaguars 23-18 in Week 11.

This game had long been circled on the Giants' schedule as a showdown between former Giants' 6sixth overall pick Daniel Jones and their new franchise QB Jaxson Dart. That narrative has since been scrapped, as Dart was lost for the season in Week 2, and Jones was released by the Colts after their Thursday night loss to the Houston Texans.

Now, the Giants feature J.J. McCarthy at quarterback while the Colts will be starting former No. 3 overall pick Anthony Richardson.

This game does have some added intrigue as the Giants will facerunning back Jonathan Taylor, who they tried to acquire at the trade deadline. The Colts traded guard Quenton Nelson, wide receiver Josh Downs, and defensive lineman DeForest Buckner, but turned down the Giants' offer of a second- and fifth-round pick for Taylor.

The Giants wanted Taylor to add an explosive bellcow runner to their backfield, but now they'll be trying to shut him down.
