Rosewood Beyond Sale: 30% Off Stays, Plus Rosewood Elite Perks

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Want to take advantage of Rosewood Elite benefits, including a space available room upgrade, complimentary breakfast, a hotel credit, and more? Contact Ford ([email protected]) for more details. His team may even be able to help if you already have a stay booked.

Luxury hotel group Rosewood has just kicked off its best sale of the year, offering savings of up to 30%, and it’s exclusively available through Rosewood Elite travel advisors. If you’ve been considering a stay at a Rosewood, this would be a great time to book.

With the Rosewood Beyond promotion, guests can save 30% off the best available rate when booking a Rosewood property through a Rosewood Elite travel advisor. To cover the basics:

In order to take advantage of the Rosewood Beyond sale, you need to book through a Rosewood Elite travel advisor. Ford’s team is happy to help with this, and can be reached at [email protected]. Other travel advisors are welcome to leave their contact details in the comments as well.

Let me emphasize that the 30% discount is off the best available rate, otherwise known as the flexible rate. So you couldn’t get this discount on an advance purchase rate, third or fourth night free rate, etc. So in some situations the savings here won’t be that significant, while in other cases they are huge.

While there are of course some restrictions and blackout dates, this is about as no strings attached as promotions get.

Since the Rosewood Beyond sale is specifically being offered through Rosewood Elite travel advisors, the good news is that these reservations also unlock additional benefits, including the following:

Rosewood is a really high quality hotel brand, and you can find all the hotels here. If you’re looking for a getaway from the United States, it’s tough to beat the Rosewood Mayakoba, the Rosewood Little Dix Bay, the Rosewood Kona Village, Las Ventanas al Paraiso, etc. Rosewood also has lots of great city hotels, ranging from the Rosewood Hong Kong, to the Rosewood Sao Paulo, to The Carlyle New York.

Rosewood has brought back its biggest promotion of the year, offering 30% off the best available rate. This is valid for bookings confirmed November 3-10, 2026, and for stays through June 30, 2027.

Aside from a small list of excluded properties, this is a pretty straightforward offer that gives you the best deal you’ll find on Rosewood stays. You can even stack this with Rosewood Elite perks, to unlock breakfast, a property credit, a room upgrade, and more.

Just keep in mind that the discount is based on the flexible rate, and not based on any promotional rate you can find.
