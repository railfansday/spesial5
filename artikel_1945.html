Sources: Mauricio Pochettino heads to Man United but his first training session delayed

Mauricio Pochettino will take his first training session at Manchester United next Thursday, sources have told Football Insider.

The Red Devils confirmed the appointment of their new manager on 13 November, in a statement which confirmed he join following his duties with the USA national team.

Pochettino will lead the USA one final time in their Nations League quater-final against Haiti on Saturday, before officially joining his new club when he becomes available.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Pochettino is set to arrive back in England next week where he will take charge of his new club.

Man United immediately set their sights on the former Tottenham boss after they were made aware of a release clause in his contract with the USMNT which they were happy to pay.

Following Michael Carrick's dismissal, they have moved quickly to land their next manager, with all parties keen to get working ahead of a tricky run of fixtures coming up.

Football Insider revealed on 11 November that Pochettino had reached an agreement to become the next Man United manager, with backroom staff to join alongside him.

Sources say that the former Tottenham boss join alongside his long-standing assistants Jesus Perez and Miki D'Agostino, as well as goalkeeping coach Toni Jimenez.

His first game as Man United manager will be a high-pressure trip to face Liverpool at Anfield, where a defeat could leave them bottom of the table depending on other results.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "He’s still got a couple of games with the US national team at the moment, with this Nations League quater final against Haiti.

"As soon as they're done, I'm sure we'll see Mauricio Pochettino probably arrive in England next week, probably next Thursday when he'll take his first training session.

"That confirmation from Man United was always expected to be announced today or at least over the weekend.

"Obviously then all eyes will be on Pochettino with his first game in charge away at Liverpool next week, as United will be hoping that he is the man that can turn around their fortunes."

Pochettino was quickly identified as the first choice candidate for the Man United job, despite links with the likes of Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann in recent weeks.

The former Tottenham boss has plenty of experience coaching in the Premier League and has long been admired by the decision-makers at Old Trafford.

However, former Man United coach Mick Brown warned Football Insider that Pochettino may not be the right man to lead the Red Devils back to their previous success.

While his remarkable work at Tottenham still earns him high praise, the boss struggled to make an impact at Paris Saint-Germain or Chelsea in the following years.

Those spells, alongside his role in the USA's controversial World Cup campaign, have set alarm bells ringing about what might turn out to be another poor Man United decision.
