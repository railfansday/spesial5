Tottenham women confirm executive shake-up as new sporting director appointed

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Tottenham have confirmed the appointment of a new director of women’s football, which comes as part of a thorough shake-up of the team’s management.

Kim Pedersen will take over the running of Spurs women from December 7, joining from Danish side Brondby.

He will bring to north London nearly 15 years of experience in football coaching and administration, across both the men’s and women’s game. In his most recent role, the Norwegian helped Brondby become Denmark’s first fully professional women’s outfit.

He joins a Tottenham side on something of an upward trajectory. The club has been steadily improving since the appointment of Martin Ho last year and has a clear aspiration to qualify for the Champions League, though they look set to fall short of that target this term despite heavy investment in the summer transfer window.

Pedersen’s appointment comes some six months on from the departure of the long-serving Andy Rogers, formerly managing director of the women’s team, whose 24-year spell with the club ended in May.

Speaking in an interview with the club after signing, Pedersen set his sights on helping Tottenham to upset the WSL’s heavyweights.

Also joining Tottenham women alongside Pedersen are Lucy Button, newly appointed the women’s growth director, and Hannah Sheridan, the women’s director of performance and operations.

Tottenham’s director of football operations Rafi Moersen said: “We have made a deliberate decision to evolve the way we lead Women's football at Tottenham Hotspur, establishing dedicated leadership across three critical areas: football, performance and operations, and commercial growth.

“This is about building the foundations for sustained success, on and off the pitch, with clear accountability, specialist leadership and the ambition to accelerate our progress.

“Together, these appointments, alongside continued investment in our infrastructure, represent an important step in our commitment to building a successful and sustainable future for Women's football at Tottenham Hotspur.”
