Manchester United head into the international break with fresh injury concerns, and uncertainty over their manager.

A 4-4 home draw to Aston Villa leaves Michael Carrick on the brink of the sack with the club languishing in the Premier League relegation zone.

Regarding the players, Harry Maguire lasted just 45 minutes on Sunday after succumbing to a suspected back problem to add to club woes.

Here is all the latest Manchester United injury news and potential return dates...

Harry Maguire shook off a knock to start against Aston Villa, but had to be withdrawn at half-time with a back problem.

The centre-back appeared to be limping after being substituted late in Tuesday's 3-2 Champions League victory over Roma, but started at Old Trafford five days later.

Speaking after the game, Carrick told MUTV: "Harry had a bad back. A really bad back spasm that he had with maybe 10-15 minutes to go in the first half.

"I don't know how bad it is but, going into the internationals, there should be plenty of time for him to recover."
