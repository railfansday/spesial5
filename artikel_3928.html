Grand Theft Auto 6 launches at midnight on November 19 in your local time zone, meaning that people in New Zealand will be playing it while I'm still stuck at work on November 18. Unless, that is, you do your gaming on a PC, in which case GTA 6 won't be launching at all—at least, not until some indeterminate span of time has passed and Take-Two Interactive deigns to bestow it upon us.

But that doesn't mean we can't indulge in all the pre-release hype, just like everyone else, and so here we are with a look at the "Vice City hardwood," upon which NBA team the Miami Heat—in the guise of the Vice City's hometown team—will square off against the Milwaukee Bucks.

Introducing the Vice City hardwood 🏀 See it for one night only, tomorrow vs Milwaukee. https://t.co/ZBpxND08hONovember 17, 2026

It's an extensive overhaul, with a huge Vice City logo at center court, the VI logos in the paint, and the "I Heart Vice City" slogans at either end. Yeah, it's marketing, but I have to admit it looks pretty cool too.

Dressed for the occasion 🔥 The all-new Vice City Cactus Jack Edition uniform will be available in limited quantities at A Night in Vice City. 11/18 vs Milwaukee. Get your tickets now - https://t.co/quujaesHKM Limit 1 jersey purchase per ticketed guest, while supplies last. https://t.co/1ACZoH0LEnNovember 12, 2026

Renting an entire NBA team as a promotional tool really puts a finger on just how big Grand Theft Auto 6 is. GTA 5 was big, absolutely, but GTA 6 is huge: Take-Two is looking to make something in the neighborhood of $3.5 billion in GTA 6's launch quarter alone.

And it's not just the dollar signs that impress: GTA 6 is also being recognized as a mainstream cultural phenomenon in ways that previous games in the series never were, or could be. It's honestly kind of amazing how far things have come in the past 40+ years.

The Vice City-ified Miami Heat will face the Milwaukee Bucks on November 18, one day before the release of Grand Theft Auto 6. If you can't be there to see it live, the game will also be broadcast on ESPN.
