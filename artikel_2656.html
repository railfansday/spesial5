10 Colts players on first Week 12 injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Indianapolis Colts have 10 players listed on the team's first Week 12 injury report ahead of Sunday's matchup with the Giants.

TE Will Mallory (hamstring), TE Drew Ogletree (knee), DT Jerry Tillery (calf), OT Jalen Travis (knee), CB Charvarius Ward (knee) were all non-participants. Each of these players, except for Ogletree, did not play last week in Houston.

RB Seth McGowan (hamstring) didn't practice either. Earlier in the week, head coach Shane Steichen said the rookie running back would "miss some time." WR Deion Burks (personal) did not practice either.

Some good news: OT Bernhard Raimann (concussion) and CB Sauce Gardner (calf) were full participants after missing the Texans' game.

CB Cam Taylor-Britt's (groin) 21-day practice window was opened after a stint on injured reserve. He was a full-go on Wednesday.
