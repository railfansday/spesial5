Chiefs sign former Eagles offensive lineman to practice squad

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

On Tuesday, the Kansas City Chiefs hosted five players at the team facility for a tryout, according to the NFL Transactions Report.

The team also reportedly had former Philadelphia Eagles offensive lineman Fred Johnson at its facility, according to Jason Anderson of 810 Sports.

The NFL's daily report shows that the Chiefs signed tight end John FitzPatrick to the practice squad of the tryout group. E.J. Smith was also brought back to the reserve unit.

However, later in the evening, the Chiefs' website also listed Johnson as a third practice squad signing.

Johnson is an eight-year veteran who started his career with the Cincinnati Bengals as an undrafted free agent out of Florida in 2019. He started eight games over three years, but the most time he has seen on the field has been the last four years with the Eagles.

Johnson started in 14 regular-season games in 2024 and 2025; he also played in all four playoff games through beating Kansas City in the Super Bowl. He primarily lined up at right tackle (726 snaps) during that time, but he also played 419 snaps at left tackle.

Earlier in his NFL career, Johnson spent a stretch of time at right guard.

Among the other names the Chiefs tried out, the most notable is Prater, a two-time Pro Bowl kicker who last kicked for the Buffalo Bills at age 41 last year. He made 18 of 20 field goal attempts, missing one of his two attempts from over 50 yards and another from short range. He also missed three point-after attempts.

It's also notable that three defensive tackles were brought in. Graves most recently spent time on the Houston Texans' practice squad after a college career at the University of Iowa. Singleton spent last year on the Seattle Seahawks' practice squad and was waived after this preseason.

But Eric Johnson — the former Missouri State Bear — is a four-year NFL veteran with 41 tackles, two tackles for loss and one sack in his career with the Indianapolis Colts and New England Patriots. He hasn't played since 2025 after spending time on multiple teams practice squads over the last year.

Second-year defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott's (knee) status this week will be worth monitoring.
