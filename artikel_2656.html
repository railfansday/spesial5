Myles Price, NFL's top punt returner, shows he can make an impact on Vikings' offense, too

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Vikings have a big weapon on special teams in return man Myles Price, who is leading the league in punt return average, total yards and touchdowns, and is third in the league in both kickoff return average and total yards. But on Sunday against the Packers, Price showed that he can make an impact on offense, too.

In the Vikings’ win over the Packers, Price got a career-high 33 snaps on offense, and he responded by catching four passes, including the first touchdown of the game on a 38-yard pass from Kirk Cousins. Vikings head coach Kevin O’Connell told Paul Allen that the decision to give Price so much playing time was mostly because of injuries to starting receivers Jordan Addison and Jauan Jennings, but with the way Price performed, he’ll get more playing time on offense going forward.

“We want to continue to find ways to get Myles involved in the offense,” O’Connell said.

O’Connell said that when the Vikings are as thin at wide receiver as they were on Sunday, there are concerns about getting anyone else injured, and he was worried about seeing Price take hits in the return game.

“With all the injuries the other day, we had no way of getting another player up,” O’Connell said. “There was not another body to have, so we actually went into the game for the first time since I’ve been here with four receivers up. Every time Myles returned a ball, you felt like we could be out of 11 personnel altogether here on one play. He did get banged up a couple times, we tried to feature the bigger groupings, and luckily he was able to return.”

Ultimately, O’Connell believes that when Price has the ball in his hands in the open field, he can make big plays whether on offense or special teams.

“Myles is a guy that we feel very strongly about making that last move, making that last man miss, and being able to score,” O’Connell said.

Expect to see Price do more of that for the rest of the season.
