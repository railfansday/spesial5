Eagles-Steelers snap counts: Riq Woolen benched

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The official snap counts from the Philadelphia Eagles' Week 11 win over the Pittsburgh Steelers are in! Let's take a look and run through some analysis.

Jordan Mailata is the only player who's played 100% of the Eagles' offensive snaps this season. He's also played 11.76% of the Eagles' special teams snaps.

The Eagles' starting offensive line configuration in each of their last five games has played 100% of the team's snaps on offense. For all the talk about injuries, they've actually had strong in-game stability up front.

Despite DeVonta Smith returning, Darius Cooper played the most snaps of any Eagles wide receiver. And he led the team in receiving with four receptions for 59 yards. One of those receptions converted 3rd-and-13 and another converted 3rd-and-10 … on two different field goal drives.

Dallas Goedert saw a single start, a season-low. But it resulted in a 38-yard reception.

Makai Lemon played a good amount for a player who saw three targets for one reception and three yards. The 2026 first-round pick dropped two passes.

If DeVonta was 100% healthy, I don't think he'd only be playing 81% of the offense's snaps.

Saquon Barkley got banged up again in this game. He was productive when he was able to play.

Will Shipley played more than the Eagles planned due to Barkley getting hurt.

Second straight game where Johnny Mundt only played single-digit snaps on offense. There's really no need for him to be on this roster, especially when the Eagles carrying four other tight ends.

Zach Ertz might've had the quietest six snaps ever played. I can't even recall seeing him on the field. Not sure he's going to break the Eagles' franchise record for receptions if he's not even really playing, let alone getting targets.

Cooper DeJean logged his first offensive snaps since Week 1 … the last time the Eagles lined up in victory formation.

Britain Covey logged his first offensive snap since Week 18 last year.

With Fred Johnson waived, the Eagles utilized John Ojukwu in a six offensive linemen package. Could be good to go with Willie Lampkin instead.

Andrew Mukuba leads the Eagles in defensive snaps percentage played this season with 99.71%. For all his faults, it is at least good to see he's been more durable?

Jeremiah Trotter Jr. led the Eagles in tackles with 13 total, nine solo. Nice showing from both him and Jihaad Campbell at off-ball linebacker.

Marcus Epps had a good showing as the top safety next to Mukuba in nickel packages.

Quinyon Mitchell left the game in the fourth quarter due to a hamstring injury. It sounds like he might be able to play in Week 12 … but we'll see how he fares on the injury report.

In the aftermath of the Jordan Davis trade, Moro Ojomo played his second-highest snap count percentage this season.

Riq Woolen was benched for Jonathan Jones after missing multiple tackles. Woolen later re-entered the game after Jones got banged up. Speaking after the game, Woolen said he was "annoyed" with the decision but expressed happiness that his team won. With Jones getting banged up, Woolen might have to play despite the fact the coaching staff clearly doesn't prefer him to be out there.

Jalyx Hunt had his first sack of the season wiped out by a penalty elsewhere on defense. Unfortunate development for him. This was Hunt's highest snap count percentage played since prior to getting hurt in Week 5. Good to see him able to handle a bigger workload.

Nolan Smith continues to show up in the box score with six sacks in his last eight games.

Jonathan Greenard has two sacks in his last two games. This despite the fact he's clearly playing through injury and playing on a pitch count.

Arnold Ebiketie was traded to the Cincinnati Bengals … only for that deal to fall through and him to remain a rotational edge rusher and special teams contributor in Philly.

Byron Young only played 22 defensive snaps but really made the most of them with two sacks and two TFLs. For context, Jordan Davis had 1.5 sacks and two TFLs in nine games with the Eagles before he was traded to the Jacksonville Jaguars. That's wild.

Ty Robinson logged his second-highest snap count percentage of the season despite getting banged up in the fourth quarter.

Gabe Hall logged his first career sack in just eight snaps played on Sunday.

Kelee Ringo was forced into action after both Mitchell and Jones got banged up at cornerback. He allowed a touchdown.

Anferenee Orji, the Eagles' only backup off-ball linebacker in this game, played three snaps while Campbell briefly missed time due to a finger injury.

Lame that Eli Stowers didn't play at all on offense. How's he supposed to get better? How are the Eagles supposed to evaluate him?

I'm very confident that the Eagles don't need both Mundt and Grant Calcaterra on this roster. They really don't need either player. But if they really insist on having one of them, fine. Both of them? There's just no good reason for it.

Jaydon Blue is probably going to get waived again after fumbling as a kick returner.

First game since Kenny Moore joined the Eagles that he did not play on defense.

A.J. Epenesa has played zero defensive snaps and 13 special teams snaps over the Eagles' last three games combined.

INACTIVE: Cole Payton, Mac McWilliams, Smael Mondon, Lane Johnson, Ajani Cornelius, Jake Majors

Guessing Mondon might miss another game after being ruled doubtful to play in Week 11. Tight turnaround to play against the Dallas Cowboys on Thursday.

As a reminder, Johnson is set to remain on the Eagles' roster until June 2027, even though he retired.
