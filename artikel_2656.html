Joe Burrow, Bengals Beat Down Steelers, 38-18

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

CINCINNATI — Joe Burrow threw for over 400 yards for the Cincinnati Bengals against the Pittsburgh Steelers on Sunday, in one of the most dominant performances by a quarterback ever when facing the Steelers defense.

Burrow completed 35 of 44 passes for 408 yards, the most allowed by the Steelers defense in four seasons, as the Bengals beat-down the Steelers, 38-18 taking a strong lead in the AFC North.

Burrow threw for four touchdowns, two to running back Chase Brown and one each to tight end Drew Sample and slot receiver Dohnte Meyers.

While the Steelers held top Cincinnati wide receivers Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins out of the end zone, that was the only way they slowed them down. Higgins caught five passes for 66 yards, while Chase caught 11 passes for 231 yards.

The Bengals' offensive explosion was placed in juxtaposition to a moribund Pittsburgh offensive attack.

The Steelers did not have a first down until their third offensive possession of the game, already trailing 10-0. In the first quarter, Cincinnati dominated the time of possession, 14 minutes to 1 minute, and the yardage, 143 to 3.

It wasn't until late in the first half that the Steelers offense crossed midfield. On that drive, they lost starting guard Mason McCormick to a gruesome-looking ankle injury and had to settle for a field goal.

With just 1:25 on the clock in the first half, the Bengals drove right back down the field and scored again.

The Steelers finally found the zone in the second half, with Rodgers throwing garbage-time touchdowns to DK Metcalf and Pat Freiermuth. Rodgers finished 15 of 29 for 143 yards. Metcalf caught two passes on six targets for 54 yards to be the leading receiver.

The Pittsburgh rushing attack, which had powered their recent four-game winning streak, never got going. Jaylen Warren rushed seven times for 15 yards and Rico Dowdle had 25 yards on five carries.

The win for Cincinnati splits the season series between the teams for the third straight season. The teams are 7-7 against one another since 2020.

The Bengals, now 7-2, will take the lead alone at the top of the AFC North, while the 6-3 Steelers are in Wild Card position, just ahead of the 6-4 Cleveland Browns.
