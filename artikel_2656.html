Texas defensive tackle Hero Kanu out for rest of 2026 season

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

AUSTIN - Texas head coach Steve Sarkisian announced Monday that starting defensive tackle Hero Kanu is out for the season and had surgery this week on his injured arm, marking the third Longhorn starter to suffer season-ending injuries.

Left tackle Trevor Goosby and running back Hollywood Smothers both were injured earlier this season and done for the year.

Kanu injured his biceps against Missouri and didn't play in the LSU game Saturday. Sarkisian said the senior wanted to suit up for the game in Baton Rouge but was unable to go.
