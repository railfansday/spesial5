Pre-Snap Reads: Coach Macdonald reveals intentions for QB1

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

It's his own fault. Sam Darnold has not played well this season and thus has warranted a conversation about his job security. Concurrently, Drew Lock has swooped in for our Seattle Seahawks and has done well enough for the QB controversy to be both palpable and relevant. Coach Macdonald seemed to put some of the question to rest during yesterday's Monday morning press conference. If Sam does end up starting against the San Francisco 49ers, it will be on him to prove he is up for the task. If the defense gives the 'Hawks a chance, and Sam does not look much improved, the coaching staff will have no choice but to make a decision. Where are yall falling? Are you into Drew?
