Global Rating World Map: Who are the best players in every country?

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

There aren't many team sports as universal as basketball. While the NBA reigns supreme as the premier destination for the biggest superstars, every continent has professional competitions where hoopers and fans live and die with every game. To track the biggest stars across the most important overseas competitions, we've created this interactive map featuring the Top 30 players, according to our Global Rating metric.

Not every league in the world is included, but we've started with more than 60 national and continental competitions, with more to come in the near future. Enjoy this trip around the world and discover the biggest stars in every major basketball hotbed.
