Anthony Davis named Eastern Conference Player of the Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

On Monday, the NBA announced that Washington Wizards forward Anthony Davis was named Eastern Conference Player of the Week for games played from Nov. 16-22.

He averaged 26.5 points and 9.0 rebounds in four games, which include the Wizards' current win streak. According to a press release by the Wizards, Davis is the first Wizard to be Eastern Conference Player of the Week since Kristaps Porzingis in 2023.
