How to watch Warriors vs. Trail Blazers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Golden State Warriors close out NBA Cup group play on Friday night with a pivotal matchup against the Portland Trail Blazers. The game will be played at 6:30 PM PT in Portland and can be watched on NBC Sports Bay Area.

The Warriors continued to win at home with Wednesday night's 129-110 victory over the Sacramento Kings. Golden State led by as many as 29 points thanks to some hot shooting from beyond the arc and porous defense from a Kings team with just two wins on the season. However, Sacramento fought their way back into the game, cutting the deficit to just four points in the third quarter.

What initially looked like a blowout victory was getting a little too close for comfort. The Warriors, however, responded with a 16-8 run to close out the third quarter before outscoring Sacramento 28-21 in the fourth to put the game away. It was a collective effort offensively for Golden State, as seven players finished in double figures in a vintage "Strength in Numbers" performance, including 14 points from backup guard Brandon Williams, who returned to action after missing six games.

The Warriors enter tonight's NBA Cup group play finale with a 2-1 record in West Group C, tied with the San Antonio Spurs, the Blazers, and Los Angeles Lakers. To win the group and advance to the knockout stage, Golden State needs a victory over Portland and a Lakers win over San Antonio, as the Warriors own the head-to-head tiebreaker over Los Angeles. Beating Portland on the road, however, will be no easy task. Even though All-Star guard Damian Lillard will sit out tonight's game, Portland is still the sixth best team in the Western Conference with a 12-6 record and ranks in the top seven in both offensive and defensive rating. With a spot in the knockout stage on the line, the Warriors hope the momentum from their two-game winning streak carries into tonight's matchup.

Enjoy the game Dub Nation. GO WARRIORS!!!

Warriors: De'Anthony Melton, Brandin Podziemski, Yaxel Lendeborg, Draymond Green, Charles Bassey

Blazers: Jrue Holiday, Sidy Cissoko, Toumani Camara, Deni Avdija, Donovan Clingan

Who: Golden State Warriors (10 – 8) vs. Sacramento Kings (12 – 6)

When: Friday, November 27th, at 6:30 p.m. PT
