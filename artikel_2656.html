Packers must go back to the drawing board on the offensive line

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

No quarterback — even one who has been playing at an MVP level of late — can sustain an offense when he can't get the ball out of his hands. Jordan Love is no exception, and Green Bay Packers fans saw the team's fatal flaw get exploited over and over and over again on Sunday afternoon.

The Minnesota Vikings defense is a singularly bad matchup for the Packers, who continue to make inexplicable decisions with their personnel on the offensive line. Yes, the team has had a glut of injuries up front, and third-string center John Williams was one of the biggest problems on the line on Sunday, but every single player shared in the blame for the constant and, even more problematic, instant pressure that Love faced all game long.

Love couldn't even get the ball out quickly without players in his face, let alone try to dial up any deep balls, which have been a staple of the team's recent success. So what is there to do?

Is the answer just as simple as "don't play the Vikings again"? Are there answers on this roster, perhaps on injured reserve (looking at you, Sean Rhyan), who could help get this unit back on track? Maybe this team needs to get back to the drawing board at a more fundamental level when it comes to the offensive line — starting with the position coach and working all the way up.

The Packers have a long week to start working on some answers before their next game, which is a week from Wednesday in Los Angeles. They've got seven more chances to sort things out and find a way into the postseason. It's time to get this under control
