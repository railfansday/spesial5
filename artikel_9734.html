What's New At Costco: November 2026 Edition

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

We may receive a commission on purchases made from links.

November has arrived, and while it's still fall and the time to enjoy the flavors of the season, retailers and consumers alike are gearing up for the holidays, beginning with Thanksgiving and continuing on to Christmas, Hanukkah, and other celebrations. As such, shelves at your favorite stores are being cleared of Halloween decorations and bags of trick-or-treat candy in favor of cornucopia motifs, pumpkin pies (some of which we've ranked), and turkeys. You may also begin to see Christmas trees, ornaments, and gift sets appear to inspire customers' holiday gift-giving.

Like every month, Costco has announced new items that it's carrying in stores and online. The popular warehouse club is known for offering great value on bulk items, including multipacks of many popular brands. It also has many exclusive items that you may be hard-pressed to find at other stores, and you can now find an ever-increasing array of international products, too, along with items from its in-house Kirkland Signature brand. Below are our picks for some of the most noteworthy items available in November. They include some holiday-ready essentials and beautiful gifts for loved ones and yourself, including a decadent dessert for Thanksgiving. You'll also find some everyday essentials. Just keep in mind that availability and prices may vary by location.

Toblerone Biscoff Milk Chocolate Truffles with Caramelized Biscuit Filling are available at Costco for $11.99.

Caramel apples are a favorite seasonal treat typically associated with fall, and the Mrs. Prindables brand has added a festive touch to these confections that makes them perfect for the winter holidays. Each one is covered in caramel and then dipped in a layer of chocolate, and you get a mix of triple chocolate and chocolate and walnut varieties in each pack. They're individually wrapped and include holiday stickers and decorative bows, making them perfect for gifting.

Mrs. Prindables Happy Holidays Caramel Apples are available at Costco in a six-pack for $59.99.

Wild boar was once something that you could only find at gourmet markets or through mail-order companies, but it's become more common at supermarkets, and you can now get a whole shoulder of it at Costco. Imagine pork but with a richer flavor and a hint of nuttiness, and you can understand why it's prized by many chefs. You can use this cut to make one impressive centerpiece for a holiday spread or divide it into smaller portions to use in different dishes, including a variety of styles of chili.

Northfork Wild Boar Boneless Shoulder is available at Costco in a 5-pound package for $119.99.

Olipop is one of Food Republic's top-ranking prebiotic sodas that also has less sugar than traditional options. This limited-time winter variety pack includes cola, ginger ale, and a new pomegranate flavor. They also all feature a cute yeti on the can, along with some seasonal graphics that remind you that the holidays are just around the corner.

Olipop Soda, Winter Variety Pack is available at Costco for $23.99.

This gift box is perfect for those who love Swedish-style gummies and features 12 2-ounce containers, each holding a different type of chewy treat. They're all arranged in an attractive gold and white box, and flavors include everything from mouth-puckering sour varieties to creamy ones that have a pleasant, fluffy texture on top of the chew. They even include fun shapes like lips, eyes, and pacifiers.

Damn Man Crave Collection Gift Box is available at Costco for $49.99.

S'mores are a favorite treat for a reason: The combination of gooey toasted marshmallows, chocolate, and graham crackers just hits in a way few other snacks can. The only drawback is that they can take some effort to make, which is why these ready-made s'mores clusters are so deliciously convenient. Not only do they feature the traditional trifecta of ingredients, but they also include kettle-cooked caramel for extra gooeyness.

Kirkland Signature Caramel S'mores Clusters are available at Costco in a two-pack for $34.99.

While matcha drinks and foods are widely available now, few things beat making it yourself, especially if you love the ritualistic process. This gift set includes everything needed to get you or a lucky recipient started on a matcha journey, including a beautiful ceramic bowl and a bamboo whisk (rather than a metal one). In addition to a tin of ceremonial-grade organic matcha, this set also includes vanilla matcha that's perfect for making lattes.

Whittard Matcha Experience Gift Box is available at Costco for $89.99.

You typically need to add other ingredients to a pasta dish in order to get certain nutritional benefits, but that's not the case with this protein pasta from the Al Dente brand. Each serving includes 30 grams of protein and 14 grams of fiber, and it contains 40% fewer carbohydrates than conventional pasta. The variety pack comes with spiraled fusilli and ridged elbows that are perfect for making the best macaroni and cheese.

The Al Dente Fusilli and Elbows Protein Pasta Variety Pack is available at Costco in a six-pack for $29.99.

A snack bar is a great way to satisfy a sweet craving between meals without quite feeling like a dessert or candy. There are many brands on the market, and if you can't decide on just one, then you'll enjoy this variety bundle, which features six popular brands of snack bars, including the warehouse club's in-house Kirkland Signature brand. The assortment includes crunchy and nutty bars, soft ones with fruit fillings, and more, so you'll never get bored.

The Snack Bar Variety Bundle is available at Costco for $39.99.

You may see the words "colossal cashews jar" and think that this Costco item refers to the size of the container. While the attractive 30-ounce glass jar does hold a lot of nuts, the cashews themselves are what give this product its plus-sized name. They are large, roasted to a perfect golden color, and seasoned simply with a touch of salt.

A.L. Schutzman Colossal Cashews Jar is available at Costco for $36.99.

November is peak fall season, meaning that pumpkin is the flavor of the month, including at Costco. This 8-inch cheesecake from Ferrara's Bakery comes topped with rich caramel and pecans, giving you the best of both pecan pie and pumpkin pie (which Costco is already known for) in cheesecake form. There are two to a pack, meaning you can bring one to a party and keep one in the fridge just for yourself.

You can purchase a two-pack of Ferrara's Bakery Pumpkin Cheesecake with Caramel and Pecan Topping at Costco for $119.99.
