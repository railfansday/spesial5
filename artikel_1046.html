Bournemouth climb up to fourth after securing ninth victory in a row

Bournemouth 2-0 Nottingham Forest: Evanilson and Justin Kluivert were on target for the Cherries

Bournemouth extended their winning run to nine in all competitions as Evanilson scored for the sixth Premier League game in a row in a 2-0 win over Nottingham Forest.

Justin Kluivert was also on target to help Marco Rose’s Cherries climb up to the dizzying heights of fourth place in the table.

The defeat means Forest are only above the relegation places on goal difference having failed to build on the momentum of their 3-1 win over Manchester City before the international break.

Both teams started the game on the front foot and Forest had the first sight of goal. Neco Williams nodded dangerously across the face of goal at the far post, but Liam Delap was beaten to the ball by home defender Antonio Silva.

Evanilson was denied by a brilliant last-ditch tackle from Forest centre-back Jair Cunha before Marcus Tavernier’s 20-yard curler was well saved by Matz Sels.

Bournemouth took the lead through Evanilson in the 19th minute. James Hill cushioned a header into the path of Evanilson, and the in-form Brazilian took one touch before blasting a left-footed shot into the roof of the net.

Kluivert almost doubled the home side’s lead with an effort from the edge of the penalty area which sailed narrowly over the crossbar.

Evanilson then failed to keep his shot down after a long throw from Hill caused chaos in the Forest penalty area.

Oliver Glasner’s side had a penalty appeal waved away when Adam Smith blocked Williams’s effort into the arm of team-mate Tyler Adams.

Kluivert did make it 2-0 on the stroke of half-time with a simple far-post finish after a superb run and cross from Tavernier.

Forest were much improved in the second half, adopting a more direct approach in a bid to forage a way back into the game.

Delap turned provider by whipping in a cross for captain Morgan Gibbs-White but the England international could not properly connect with his header.

James McAtee ballooned an effort over the crossbar as the visitors continued to misfire in front of goal.

Substitute Chris Wood also miscued a header from a corner when he should have at least tested underworked Bournemouth goalkeeper Dorde Petrovic.

Bournemouth always looked a threat on the counter and Kluivert came close to his second and the Cherries’ third when he stung the gloves of Selz from 18 yards out.

Kluivert should also have been more clinical when he found himself within finishing range but unselfishly laid the ball off to Rayan and the chance went begging.
