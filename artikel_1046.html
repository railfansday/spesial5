Bournemouth make more history with comfortable win against Forest

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Bournemouth vs Nottingham Forest. Premier League.

Report and free match highlights as Bournemouth beat Nottingham Forest for their ninth successive victory in all competitions; Evanilson scored in his sixth Premier League game in a row; Justin Kluivert added a second; Alex Scott made his return from injury in the second half

Bournemouth continued their unstoppable form with a comfortable 2-0 victory against Nottingham Forest and created two bits of history in the process.

It was a club-record ninth successive win in all competitions and the first time they have won five in a row in the Premier League. They have kept seven clean sheets in those games as well - only conceding against Ipswich in the league - with 23 goals scored.

A large part of that has been down to the form of Evanilson, and the Brazilian did not disappoint. He struck first with a wonderful effort - set up by the deftest of headers from James Hill - to extend his scoring run to six straight Premier League games.

It was a simple but effective break for Bournemouth's second as Forest failed to follow up a big win against Manchester City before the international break.

Marcus Tavernier sped down the left-hand side, with only Justin Kluivert for support. That was all he needed, though, as his low cross was turned home by the Netherlands international.

Bournemouth drew a number of fine saves from Matz Sels in the second half, although perhaps the brightest moment was the return of Alex Scott from injury. It was his first Cherries appearance since September 20 and his first overall game since October 3, when he played for England against Croatia.

He was part of a triple change in the 69th minute and from then on, Forest had the better of the play. However, they did little with it and could only muster one shot on target, despite have just one fewer shot than the Cherries (18).

The win sends Bournemouth into the top four ahead of the remaining games this weekend. Nottingham Forest slip down into 17th and are outside the relegation spots on goal difference.

Bournemouth head coach Marco Rose: "Really good [performance], especially in the first half. We knew that it's going to be difficult against a tough opponent, quite compact, consistent in everything they do.

"It's hard to create chances, but we did it. We created chances, we scored goals and we were relentless. We defended our goal at the end... with a deserved win.

"We were good in box defending. We never struggled really, we suffered, but we never struggled. It's an important part and phase of the game as well."

On Bournemouth's second goal: "It was the right moment, but if you're 2-0 up at half-time, it's always difficult if the opponent changed something and they did it.

"They brought [on] two new players, new energy and you could get in problems if you don't use the right approach for the second half, but we did it. We were ready and that's why it was a top performance. We enjoy this win, but Thursday [against Celta Vigo] we have to prove it again."

On Bournemouth's impressive defensive record: "This is the foundation for everything that we are - a hard-working team out of possession and that we do it together.

"Then we win balls, we score simple goals out of ball wins. And on the other side, we score goals from set-pieces. So we are unpredictable - and that's good."

Nottingham Forest head coach Oliver Glasner:

"We need to be more physical. We're struggling with the physicality, the intensity and the power these teams have. We have to improve because then we can stay in all these games.

"We had the same amount of shots most of the time, but this is the pattern and we never scored. We always conceded from a mistake and we all know especially in defence, you have to avoid mistakes because the Premier League is ruthless.

"In the attack again we try, but you also have to be ruthless because otherwise the Premier League will punish you.

"It was a very intense game from both sides, many long balls, many duels, many second balls. We have to accept that Bournemouth was a little bit better in these situations and that's why they won.

"First goal we lose the ball, second goal we lose the ball. We were well-organised behind the ball and I don't know why, but it happened and then we open the middle and this is where they score the goal.

"Especially the second goal straight before half-time, such an easy goal to give away. That hurts and then you can't win in the Premier League.

"For me today [Saturday] it was not the efficiency, it was that we made too many easy mistakes and Bournemouth took them."
