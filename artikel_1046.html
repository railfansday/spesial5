The first round of the 2026-27 FA Cup is nearly over, with the remaining teams finding out their second-round opposition in Monday night’s draw.

Nine non-league teams remain in the hunt, with seventh-tier Wimborne Town set to host League One Bradford City as they bid for another upset after seeing off League Two Crawley 2-0 at the weekend.

Non-league Dover Athletic have been drawn against 2021 winners Leicester City, seventh-tier Guiseley - who upset National League Sutton United 1-0 - are at home to League One Notts County, and fifth-tier Kidderminster Harriers, who thrashed Doncaster 3-0, face a trip to Barrow.

2013 champions Wigan Athletic have been drawn in an all-League One clash with Barnsley while Grimsby Town, who only narrowly scraped past eighth-tier Stafford Rangers in a penalty shootout, face Oldham Athletic.

The line-up will be completed by the winners of Monday’s final first-round tie, between Plymouth Parkway and Berkhamsted, with the winner to host Barnet.

That’s that for the FA Cup second-round draw! The full line-up will only be known later this evening when one of non-league Plymouth Parkway or Berkhamsted are victorious, with their reward a home game against Barnet.

The 20 FA Cup second-round matches will be held across the weekend of 5-6 of December, with the third-round draw presumed to take place on the evening of Monday 7 December.

Wimborne manager Tim Sills said after the draw ceremony: “I can feel the stress levels of the people back at the club as they get ready to do it all again! We can’t wait to hopefully produce another magical moment.

“I said a home tie against anyone I'd be happy with - but to come up against a club with such history and a huge club. It's going to be a different challenge, they trounced Marine at the weekend and we'll he hoping to put on a show again.”

Seventh-tier Wimborne Town face League One Bradford City at home in the FA Cup second round, with the minnows bidding for another upset after seeing off League Two Crawley 2-0 at the weekend.

The Dorset part-timers face another step up in opposition in their first-ever appearance in the second round, with Bradford fresh from a 7-0 victory at the weekend.
