Wondering Why Taylor Swift Doesn’t Perform at Festivals Like Coachella? We Did Some Theorizing and Digging

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Coachella just released its 2027 lineup, and for those convinced Taylor Swift would be headlining...sorry! While the lineup is absolutely stacked (Madonna! SZA! Dua Lipa!), Taylor is not one of the featured artists. Which...isn’t that surprising. After all, she doesn’t historically do festivals (she is kinda busy casually breaking every touring record and selling out stadiums).

While Taylor hasn’t explained why she’s never headlined Coachella (or other major festival like Glastonbury), it doesn’t seem like a stretch to assume that crowd control could be an issue for her. Taylor is used to performing in the biggest possible stadiums across the world, but they have infrastructure in place to handle crowds even bigger than Coachella, not to mention built-in security. Coachella, as organized as it is, has a somewhat more chaotic vibe, and crowd control is definitely less of a thing.

But if you’re looking for a chatty source who’s commented on the subject, someone told The Huffington Post allllllll the way back in 2014 that “Taylor doesn't like festivals. So for any bookies reading... I think Taylor likes doing her own show because she's in control and it's how she wants it to be. With Glastonbury or any festival, you're at the mercy of the people who run the festival. She wants it all to be perfect.”

Note: Taylor might not like performing at festivals (or at least didn’t in 2014), but she’s definitely a fan in general—she at Travis were spotted at Coachella in 2024 to support her close friend and collaborator Jack Antonoff.

Meanwhile, Taylor did open up about why she’s never performed at the Superbowl halftime show (which fans have also been wondering about), explaining that she’s too locked in on Travis. “That is violent chess,” Tay said of watching football. “That is gladiators without swords. That is dangerous. The whole season I am locked in on what that man is doing on the field. Can you imagine if he's out there every single week putting his life on the line, doing this very dangerous, very high-pressure, high-intensity sport, and I'm like, ‘I wonder what my choreo should be. I think we should do two verses of ‘Shake it Off' into ‘Blank Space’ into ‘Cruel Summer’ would be great.’”

She added that the decision “is nothing to do with Travis, he would love for me to do it. I'm just too locked in.”

Point is: Taylor will likely be sticking to stadium tours, and we’re truly blessed.
