Bruno Fernandes admits Man United offered him new contract but ‘not the right moment’ for negotiations

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes has revealed that discussions over a new contract have stalled because it isn’t “the right moment” to be negotiating.

The Portuguese said an offer had been made in the summer, with his contract due to expire at the end of this season, but negotiations were not ongoing as he insisted on prioritising the club’s return to form.

The 32-year-old, who most recently signed in August 2024, has repeatedly been linked with a move away from Old Trafford. United have an option to trigger and extension for a further year.

Fernandes told Sky Sports: “The club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay.

“We spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side because I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most important thing now is that the club gets back to win games.”

United sit 19th in the Premier League after a dismal start to this campaign and parted ways with manager Michael Carrick after just 10 games, with former Southampton, Tottenham and Chelsea manager Mauricio Pochettino taking the reins.

The Argentine’s first game in charge will be United’s trip to Liverpool on Sunday in their first game back after the international window.

Fernandes stressed his loyalty to the club and added that he would not run down his contract if United decided it was in their interests for him to leave.

He said: “No, there's no negotiations ongoing, but I've seen people saying that he's asked for more money, he's asked for more years. If I wanted more money, I wouldn't be here. I had somewhere to go to get much more money than being at Manchester United. I could have been in a place that is much warmer than Manchester.

“Two summers ago, three summers ago, last summer, I always had the chance to leave and I always chose to be here. The thing is, I've always showed how much I care about the club and how much I want to win at this club. And I always said, in the moment the club thinks it's time for me to move on, I will move on.

“I'm not going to be the player that I've seen many in this time at Manchester United. They're going to stay here just by staying, just finish my contract and then we will see. In the moment the club says, ‘Bruno, it's time to part ways’, I'm going to be the first one walking out the door.”
