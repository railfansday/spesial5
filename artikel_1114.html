Manchester United were left to rue two abysmal refereeing decisions that meant they had to settle for a 2-2 draw away to Sporting Lisbon in the Champions League.

A contentious decision in each half saw Amad's goal chalked off for an apparent foul by Youri Tielemans – which he was booked for – and a second saw an own goal chalked off due to interference at a corner from substitute Patrick Dorgu.

Mauricio Pochettino certainly wasn't helped by individual errors from Senne Lammens and Noussair Mazraoui, both playing key roles in Luis Suarez's brace.

But United will head for home knowing they did more than enough to win the game and had it taken away from them.
