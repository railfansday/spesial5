Morgan Stanley Shares $1.96 Trillion Portfolio Update: Heavy Bitcoin Focus, XRP Exposure

Morgan Stanley has disclosed its latest crypto-related holdings in a quarterly Form 13F filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. According to the filing, the bank's U.S. equity portfolio was valued at around $1.96 trillion as of Sept. 30, up from $1.89 trillion at the end of June.

Most of the crypto-related positions in the filing are Bitcoin products and Bitcoin-linked stocks, and the bank also reported a small amount of XRP exposure through three spot exchange-traded funds.

The largest crypto-related position in the filing is Strategy (MSTR). Morgan Stanley held around 10.88 million MSTR shares worth about $1.67 billion at the end of September, up from 9.33 million shares at the end of the second quarter.

The bank also increased its position in BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) from 16.48 million to 19.52 million shares, and the IBIT stake was worth about $924 million as of Sept. 30.

Besides, Morgan Stanley reported 6.72 million units of its own Morgan Stanley Bitcoin Trust (MSBT), up from 2.57 million units at the end of June. In the second-quarter filing, the MSBT position was worth about $43.3 million. MSBT started trading on NYSE Arca in April, and it was the first spot Bitcoin ETF issued by a major U.S. bank.

Among crypto stocks, Morgan Stanley also held around 10.54 million Robinhood (HOOD) shares worth about $1.19 billion and 3.72 million Coinbase (COIN) shares worth about $694 million.

Morgan Stanley reported $116,097 in XRP ETF holdings as of Sept. 30. Franklin's XRP fund accounted for $111,885 of that amount, REX-Osprey's fund for $3,098, and the Bitwise XRP ETF for the remaining $1,114.

The bank held the same three funds at the end of June. In the second quarter, Morgan Stanley reported 6,715 shares of the Franklin fund, 255 shares of the REX-Osprey fund and 67 shares of the Bitwise fund. Before that, the bank's first-quarter filing showed 1,700 shares of the Volatility Shares XRP ETF and 100 shares of the Grayscale XRP Trust ETF worth $15,488 in total.

On Wednesday, XRP was trading at around $0.908, down 2.23% over 24 hours, according to CoinMarketCap.

Morgan Stanley was one of seven banks that disclosed XRP-linked ETF holdings in third-quarter 13F filings. The positions of the seven banks totaled about $29.4 million as of Sept. 30, and Italy's Intesa Sanpaolo accounted for $27.21 million of that amount.

Goldman Sachs reported much larger XRP positions. The bank's XRP ETF holdings rose from $87 million in the second quarter to $144 million in the third quarter, and Goldman's crypto portfolio was worth around $2.64 billion in total.
