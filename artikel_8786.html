New Study Reveals the Surprising Reason Some People Have 'Duty Sex'

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

A new study in Evolutionary Psychology suggests fear of a partner cheating is the strongest reason people agree to 'duty sex'—intimacy motivated by obligation rather than desire. Researchers found both men and women do it at similar rates, with perceived infidelity risk, lower self-rated desirability and neuroticism linked to the behavior. But experts stress duty sex was uncommon overall—and consent and mutual willingness remain essential.
