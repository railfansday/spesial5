The 9 Best Umbrella Stands for Weathering Any Forecast

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

These holders will brighten up even the rainiest of days.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE Decor editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

A rainy-day return often leaves a small mess by the front door: drops on the floor, nowhere for guests to place their rain gear, and damp umbrellas leaning against the wall. An umbrella stand gives them a proper spot to drip-dry and keeps them together for the next time the forecast changes.

Umbrella stand designs have as much variety as the entryways that host them. A terracotta holder brings earthy texture; powder-coated metal can take on a graphic shape; leather adds a softer touch. Even when the skies clear, painterly decoration and bold color can make the stand part of the room’s decor.

Whether you’re welcoming drenched guests or making space for one well-loved umbrella, the right holder keeps rain at the threshold.
