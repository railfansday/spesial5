Third US aircraft carrier arrives in Middle East as Iran rules out UN nuclear inspections

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The USS Roosevelt aircraft carrier on Sunday reportedly entered the region overseen by U.S. Central Command, which means that the American military now has three carriers positioned in the region again.

"The USS Roosevelt aircraft carrier has arrived in the U.S. military central command (CENTCOM) area of responsibility earlier on Sunday, a U.S. official tells me," Israeli journalist Barak Ravid wrote in a Sunday post on X. "The U.S. military now has three air craft carrier strike groups in CENTCOM."

In a Monday post on X, CENTCOM shared a photo and wrote, "USS Theodore Roosevelt (CVN 71) transits the Indian Ocean, Nov. 15. The aircraft carrier is currently operating in the CENTCOM area of responsibility as part of a scheduled deployment."

The news comes as the U.S.-Iran war continues more than eight months after it began earlier this year.

There were previously three carriers positioned in the region earlier this year, as CENTCOM noted in April.

Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi has reportedly indicated that the nation will not grant the International Atomic Energy Agency access to facilities until the war has concluded.

"We will not grant the IAEA access to the facilities until Iran's conditions are met and the war has permanently ended," he said, according to AFP.

A Persian-to-English translation from material on Iran's irib-news.ir website indicates that Gharibabadi said that "our current policy with the Agency is that as long as this situation is not resolved, the talk of war is not permanently ended, a diplomatic understanding is not established, and the United States does not establish its own interactions, we cannot establish access to the Agency."
