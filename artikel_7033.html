LIFT Enrichment: Remote Sales Representative for Education Company

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

We teach healthy cooking classes to Title 1 schools as an afterschool program.

SummaryLIFT Enrichment is hiring a Sales Representative to join our team!

About the company: At LIFT Enrichment, we teach healthy culinary workshops to students at low-income schools across California. The company was founded in 2010, and our mission is to make healthy cooking FUN for children :) We partner with school districts and charter schools to bring our workshops to their students and help them form an essential skill that will last a lifetime - being able to make a healthy meal for themselves. Our workshops are also educational and fun. We focus on recipes with veggies (like spinach, zucchini, and cauliflower), teach about the nutritional and cultural components behind each recipe, and inspire children to make healthy recipes at home.
