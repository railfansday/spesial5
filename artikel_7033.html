Prince William and Kate Middleton’s Advisor Shares a Rare Comment About Their Relationship

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A senior royal aide says the Prince and Princess of Wales are “very much a team” after their whirlwind India tour.

The Prince and Princess of Wales made headlines this week on their royal visit to India, which saw them attend the 2026 Earthshot Prize Awards ceremony, meet the country’s prime minister, drive a tuk-tuk, and even ride the metro. Now that they’re back in the UK, those in the couple’s inner circle have been reflecting on the trip, and one thing is clear: William and Kate are closer than ever.

Speaking to reporters (via the BBC), the pair’s most senior advisor described them as “very much a team” following their multiday tour in India. “It has been particularly special for them to experience this moment together,” said private secretary Ian Patrick. “They are very much a team.”

Pointing to the reason why, the royal insider explained that it’s all to do with taking part in each other’s passions. “The prince has really enjoyed having the princess alongside him for Earthshot,” the royal advisor said, referring to William’s environmental awards project. “She’s enormously proud of what he’s achieved.”

Commenting on William and Kate’s trip more broadly, the private secretary added, “The prince and princess have had a thoroughly enjoyable time in India. They’ve been incredibly touched by the warmth of the welcome they’ve received.”

Over the past few days, the Waleses have kept fans up to date with their adventures in India by sharing various photos and video clips on Instagram. In one down-to-earth video, William joined locals for a game of gully cricket in Mumbai. Another clip showed the pair wearing traditional South Asian style clothing at the Earthshot Prize Awards ceremony.

“The most beautiful and sweetest couple,” one fan commented on the video. “The most beautiful royal couple on the planet without a doubt,” agreed someone else.
