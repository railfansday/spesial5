Luka Doncic’s feud with NBA referee heats up with tense exchange after $50K fine

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Luka Dončić finally looked human last night.

The Lakers superstar guard finished with 29 points in Los Angeles' 109-97 home loss to the Utah Jazz on Wednesday night.

Even a respectable 29 points felt like an off night by Dončić's lofty standards. He entered the game leading the league in scoring at 38.8 points per game.

Luka Dončić of the Los Angeles Lakers reacts after a foul during a game against the Utah Jazz in Los Angeles, Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026. Getty Images

Dončić's frustration seemed to boil over during an exchange with referee Tre Maddox after the first half.

A video of Dončić looking to say something to Maddox, then Maddox turning around and apparently saying, "What'd you say?" before being escorted away is surfacing social media.

Luka Dončić speaking to referee Tre Maddox during the Utah Jazz vs. Los Angeles Lakers game. (X/@ES_SportsNews).

Dončić looked incredulous after the interaction, which was caught on camera.

And perhaps his history with Maddox played into this.

Luka Dončić looking at NBA referee Tre Maddox during the Los Angeles Lakers game. (X/@ES_SportsNews).

Last season, Dončić was fined $50,000 after rubbing his fingers together in a "money" gesture after Maddox called a blocking foul on him when the Lakers were playing the New York Knicks in Los Angeles.

Dončić seemed to be suggesting that Maddox was not making the call fairly or was being influenced by a financial incentive.

There is no way of knowing whether the exchange between the two during Wednesday night's game had anything to do with what happened in March.

But it being the same referee in both instances does not feel like a coincidence.
