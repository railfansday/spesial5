Eagles sign Jaydon Blue to the roster (once again), add practice squad DT

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Philadelphia Eagles officially announced two roster moves on Tuesday afternoon. Here's an overview:

RB Jaydon Blue was signed from the practice squad to the active roster.

DT Leki Fotu was signed to the practice squad.

Let's sort through this news on a player-by-player basis.

Here's a recap of Blue's transactions with the Eagles:

September 1 — Signed to the practice squad

October 22 — Signed to the practice squad

November 16 — Signed to the practice squad

The Eagles have been reluctant to play Blue on offense thus far; the former Dallas Cowboys draft pick has only logged eight offensive snaps this season. And all of those came during garbage time in the Eagles' loss to the Washington Commanders.

In addition to theoretically providing running back depth, Blue has been serving as the Eagles' kick returner. But he's only logging 22.3 yards per return, which isn't good.

It's a little curious why the Eagles signed Blue to their practice squad yesterday only to sign him to the roster one day later. Why not just sign him to the roster in the first place?

Regardless, Blue is back on the roster. Assuming Saquon Barkley suits up and Will Shipley returns from injured reserve, the Eagles will have Blue as their third running back option.

Until they inevitably cut him again down the road.

Fotu had a tryout with the Eagles on Monday. Here's what we wrote at the time:

The 28-year-old Fotu is a 6'5", 330 pound defensive tackle who's made 26 starts in 66 career games played from 2020-2025. With the Eagles trading Jordan Davis to the Jacksonville Jaguars, the Birds have a need for more nose tackle depth. Fotu can help to fill that role, be it on the practice squad or the roster.

Fotu is here to help fill practice reps and provide depth should the Eagles need him on game day.
