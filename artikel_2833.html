Wembanyama hits 50 points in dominant Spurs win

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

San Antonio Spurs star Victor Wembanyama hit 50 points in a historic performance as his side beat the New Orleans Pelicans 114-91.

The 22-year-old, who is 7ft 4ins tall, became the first player with 50 points, five blocks and five three-pointers in a match since the NBA added threes in 1979–80.

The France international, who was defensive player of the year for 2026, said he put on a show for a celebrity-packed crowd which included actress and long-time Spurs fan Selena Gomez.

"I just want to keep stacking them [records] up. I don't plan on doing those. They just come and we play the right way as a team.

"So, I just want to stack them up. Hopefully there's many more coming."

The Spurs have made a fine start to the season, winning 14 of their 17 games so far to top the Western Conference Southwest.

They won the division last season before losing to the New York Knicks in the NBA Finals.

Spurs coach Mitch Johnson added. "It's a cliche to say, 'Why doesn't he do it every game?'

"But it's so interesting when people play like that, especially with his talent."

Wembanyama's haul - the third of his career - came after James Harden scored 51 points to move third on the all-time list for 50-point NBA games on Saturday.

Wilt Chamberlain holds the record with an incredible 118 50-point games.

"It is very special. But unlike many of my peers, I'm not very good at stat-padding," Wembanyama said.

"So I haven't reached it as many times as I should have."

Wembanyama was later asked whether there was an ethical right or wrong way to score 50 points. Some NBA players have been accused of chasing individual statistics for financial gain instead of concentrating on their team's results.

"Maybe, I think that's a question for you. The greats, they don't think about that," Wembanyama replied.

"I don't think LeBron [James], Shai [Gilgeous-Alexander], [Nikola] Jokic, [Luka] Doncic, I don't think they think about that. I have them as examples, so I'm trying to not think about it either."
