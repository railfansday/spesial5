Dominick Barlow probable, Jaylen Brown out for Sixers vs. Bucks

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Reinforcements are finally on their way for the Sixers. Dominick Barlow appears ready to make his season debut, as he's listed as probable for Saturday's game against the Milwaukee Bucks.

Tony Jones of The Athletic reported earlier Friday that Barlow was trending in that direction, and Barlow foreshadowed it on Instagram on Thursday as well. Still, for a Sixers team that's been woefully short on frontcourt depth as of late, his return could not be coming at a better time.

Even with Barlow back, the Sixers won't be at full strength against Milwaukee. Adem Bona (cervical spine strain), Jaylen Brown (hamstring) and Dean Wade (hamstring) are all out, while Tyrese Maxey is listed as questionable with a right foot sprain. For the Bucks, Ousmane Dieng (left finger fracture) is their only notable player who's out.

Luckily for the Sixers, the Bucks only loosely resemble a professional basketball team this year as they begin to chart a course in the post-Giannis Antetokoumpo era. They're an NBA-worst 1-15, and this will be their third game in four days. The 1 p.m. ET start likely won't do their tired legs any favors.

Since this will be Barlow's first game back this season, the Sixers figure to ease him in slowly. It'd be surprising if he played more than 15-20 minutes at most. Still, it'll bear watching how the Sixers deploy him. Will he stick strictly to the 4, or will he cut into Tacko Time and steal some minutes at backup center whenever Joel Embiid is off the floor?

Barlow was a revelation for the Sixers last year, averaging 7.7 points and 4.8 rebounds in 23.8 minutes per game while shooting 53.9 percent from the field. They initially signed him to a two-way contact last offseason, but he played so well for them—he even started 59 games!—that they wound up spending a hefty chunk of their taxpayer mid-level exception to convert him to a standard contract after the trade deadline.

Barlow's three-point shooting might be the swing skill that determines his place in the Sixers' rotation this year. He knocked down only 25.6 percent of his long-range attempts on low volume last season, which won't cut it moving forward. Jabari Walker's improvement from deep gives him the inside track to backup frontcourt minutes, but it's worth seeing how a Walker-Barlow pairing fares defensively, particularly with regard to rim protection.

The Sixers proved Wednesday that they're capable of winning even with Brown, Wade and Maxey out. Regardless of whether Maxey returns, they should be able to take care of business against an overmatched Bucks team. But Barlow's return adds some extra juice to an otherwise sleepy 1 p.m. ET start against a lottery-bound squad.
