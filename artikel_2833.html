The troubling response to Aaron Rodgers’ bombshell claims against Mike McCarthy

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Aaron Rodgers' shocking claims about Mike McCarthy apparently have some merit.Several anonymous sources inside the Steelers' locker room have reportedly confirmed parts of Rodgers' accusations on "The Pat McAfee Show" about his now-former head coach.

NFL insider Jordan Schultz reported Wednesday that he got an "unprompted text" from a Steelers player confirming the troubles around McCarthy.

"It's been a s–t show," the text read. "Happy it's out there tbh."

Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers (8) checks with head coach Mike McCarthy during a game on Sept. 27, 2026, at Acrisure Stadium in Pittsburgh. USA TODAY Network via Reuters Connect

Mike McCarthy and Aaron Rodgers on the sideline during a game. AP Photo/Gene J. Puskar

McCarthy copped to a halftime speech gaffe in which he told his Steelers team, "1-2-3 Packers!," but seemingly did not acknowledge the allegations of sleeping during meetings.

"I think just like anything, you know, the Philadelphia situation that halftime was very emotional," he told reporters on Wednesday in response to Rodgers' allegations. "…I just think we work a lot of hours, so definitely a difference of opinion from what I was told about the comments."

When asked about the claims he fell asleep during team meetings, one player told The Athletic, "He's not lying."

A league source also backed Rodgers' claims about McCarthy, telling ESPN, "It's all true."

PennLive.com's Nick Farabaugh also reported that multiple people within the Steelers organization had privately backed Rodgers' claims.

Pittsburgh Steelers Head Coach Mike McCarthy walks off the field after a 19-16 loss to the Philadelphia Eagles in Philadelphia, Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. Getty Images

"No player today in the locker room corroborated what Aaron Rodgers said on the record with a quote / scrum," Farabaugh wrote on X.

"That being said, plenty I have spoken to in the organization have backed up the claims Rodgers said about McCarthy."

Steelers players reportedly watched Rodgers' interview "in real time" in the locker room.

When the doors opened to reporters, Steelers players didn't have much to say on the record about the accusations, with tight end Pat Freiermuth, whom Rodgers said told him of his benching for Friday's game, noting he had "really nothing to add toward that."

In the hours after his shocking appearance on ESPN airwaves, the Steelers released the future Hall of Fame quarterback, putting his NFL future in question in what was supposed to be his final season.
