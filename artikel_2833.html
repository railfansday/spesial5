Colts being 12.5-point underdogs to the 3-6 Texans says it all

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

I keep asking myself if this is rock bottom for the Indianapolis Colts. The disastrous season when Peyton Manning went down was certainly rock bottom. The difference was, with the first overall pick, the franchise had a shovel to dig itself out. There is no shovel. No pick axe. Not even a lantern to lead the way. Being an almost two touchdown underdog to a 3-6 team might be the worst version of rock bottom.

The Colts have taken some tremendous shellackings over the years. Those were generally singular moments, though. You have to go back to the beginning of the Indianapolis iteration of the Colts to find many examples of a team this bad. Even then, a debate could be had over who was in worse shape. Those teams had zero expectations. An almost two-decade run of success means there is no turning back. That's what makes 2026 tough to swallow.

The Texans are 3-6. Maybe that's a tough 3-6, but this team has still lost six times already this year and is expected to whoop the Colts on Thursday Night Football. How did it get so bad? The eternal optimists are probably grinding their teeth right now, but what's the worst part about next year: the fact the Colts have no direction at quarterback or the fact they won't have a first-round pick to get one? Having a stockpile of draft picks is only good if the Colts hit. That remains to be seen. For now, they are two touchdown underdogs to the team currently with the ninth overall pick. Ouch.

What's the solution? No clue. Where do the Colts go from here? Not my job to figure out. Ideally, this is rock bottom. Ideally, there is nowhere to go but up. Ideally, the Colts hit on multiple draft picks and can get this thing going again. That's what we all want, a winner. We want to watch good competitive football. It's hard to do that when your favorite team is in such a deep hole, though. Nothing left to do but climb.
