The NFL is back and so are the Yahoo Fantasy x Arena Club Slab Packs!

Loaded with top performers and fantasy stars, the Yahoo Fantasy x Arena Club Slab packs will be available each week during the regular season for collectors and fans to digitally rip.

For those new to Arena Club, it is a new way of collecting. As the premier tech-powered marketplace and grading platform, Arena Club brings the physical experience of card collecting into the digital age. Users can purchase and open virtual packs of real, pre-graded cards ("slabs"), buy and sell on the marketplace and manage their card showrooms all in one place. Each Arena Club Slab Pack displays the full lineup of potential cards and the likelihood of ripping each.

Yahoo Fantasy Slab Packs contain graded NFL trading cards curated by Arena Club. Fans can purchase a slab pack, each containing a graded card of an NFL player, via the Arena Club platform. This week, we're upping the ante with grails of some of the most collectible players — and top fantasy performers — being available as chases in both tiers of packs. For the Silver ($70) tier, each grail is worth roughly 35-60x of the pack price. In the Gold ($200) tier, each grail has an estimated value of 25-40x the pack price.

The Yahoo Fantasy Slab Packs will go on sale on at kickoff of the Commanders-Giants game (8:15 p.m. ET, Thursday) and be available until the conclusion of the Monday night game (or until they sell out) every week through the end of the season.

You can head to ArenaClub.com or the Arena Club app and use the promo code YAHOO20 at checkout for 20% off your first slab pack or card purchase.

Joe Burrow and Ja'Marr Chase, Bengals – You'd be hard-pressed to find a QB-WR duo who is on a more dominant stretch than Burrow and Chase right now. Burrow has 18 total touchdowns across his past four games and Chase has scored in each of those contests — all Bengals wins, to add a cherry on top.

Bijan Robinson, Falcons – Robinson's Falcons may have lost to the Bengals in Spain in Week 9, but the star running back still delivered for fantasy managers. Robinson compiled 147 total yards, marking his sixth game this season where he surpassed the century mark in yards from scrimmage.

Jordan Love, Packers – Love is quietly putting together an MVP-caliber season in Green Bay. Love has multiple touchdowns in every Packers game this season and leads the NFL in passing yards (2,618) after his 372-yard performance in Week 9.

Dak Prescott and CeeDee Lamb, Cowboys — There was no question who Prescott's favorite option was in Week 9, with Lamb getting 16 targets against the Colts. Dallas needed every bit of Lamb's 10-195-1 line to outlast Indianapolis in a 27-23 thriller.

Christian McCaffrey, 49ers — McCaffrey hasn't had a single 100-yard rushing game this season, but that hasn't stopped him from being one of the most productive running backs in football. CMC has scored at least one touchdown in every game since Week 1 and has three multi-score games, including in Week 9 against the Raiders.
