Eagles vs. Giants Week 9 live chat

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The Philadelphia Eagles are back at home Sunday for an early-afternoon game against the New York Giants.

The Giants currently sit at the top of the NFC East (gross) with a 4-2-1 record, and the Eagles sit pretty firmly in last place after consecutive losses to the Cowboys and Commanders in two primetime games. There's so many things for the Eagles to figure out with this roster that winning the division is a distant goal.

It'll be interesting to see how the offense looks with Joe Milton as the starting quarterback, and whether he's able to pull out the dynamic ability of several playmakers. Being excited about that potential is a risk given the team's weekly ability to shatter the little bits of hope we have left.
