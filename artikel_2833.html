Cowboys news: DaRon Bland on IR, future in Dallas in doubt

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

With DaRon Bland heading to injured reserve, could his time as a Dallas Cowboy be coming to a close?

We may have seen the last of former All-Pro cornerback DaRon Bland in a Dallas Cowboys uniform. On Saturday, the team announced that Bland has been placed on injured reserve due to a nagging ankle injury. Bland suffered a high-ankle sprain during the team's loss to the San Francisco 49ers in Week 10. It marks the second consecutive year that Bland's season has ended prematurely due to being placed on injured reserve. Last season, he was placed on IR in December due to a foot injury.

With DaRon Bland's status changing, the team has made some transactions Saturday.

As the Cowboys work to get ready for the visiting Titans, they've made the decision to elevate two players from the practice squad. After moving DaRon Bland to injured reserve on Saturday, the team has elevated Derion Kendrick for Sunday's game, providing depth at the cornerback position as well as another special teams talent. Additionally, the Cowboys elevated running back Jashaun Corbin.

The hope is a new voice calling plays can help turn around the Dallas defense.

Mike Pettine is not planning to completely overhaul the Dallas Cowboys' defense, but the new play-caller believes there are opportunities to make the unit more effective with the personnel already on the roster. Dallas made a major coaching adjustment ahead of Week 11, handing defensive play-calling responsibilities to Pettine while Christian Parker remains the defensive coordinator. The move came after the Cowboys surrendered 584 yards in their 46-40 loss to the San Francisco 49ers.
