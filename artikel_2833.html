Detroit Lions officially activate Kerby Joseph, place Aidan Hutchinson on IR

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

On Wednesday, the Detroit Lions made two moves that were both expected, placing Aidan Hutchinson on injured reserve, while activating Kerby Joseph from the Physically Unable to Perform list to the 53-man roster. Joseph takes Hutchinson's spot on the roster, keeping the roster at 53 players for now.

Hutchinson's placement on injured reserve is no surprise, given that the team confirmed he tore his ACL while celebrating in Sunday's win over the New England Patriots. While Hutchinson will miss the remainder of the season, coach Dan Campbell was optimistic he'd work his way back and be better than ever next year.

"Knowing Hutch, he will attack this like he did the last time, and he will come back better than ever next year," Campbell said.

He also mentioned that the damage in his knee may go beyond just his ACL.

"It sounds like there's a little bit (more damage) in there. I don't know how significant all of that is. And some of it you're not going to know until you go in there and actually do the procedure," Campbell said.

As for Joseph, Campbell already notified media earlier in the week that the plan was to get the All-Pro safety involved this week. And while Joseph will be on a pitch count against the Buccaneers, Campbell doesn't expect it to be too strict.

"Is there a pitch count? Yeah, but not like what (Brian) Branch had his first game. So, I think we're going to be able to get a little bit more out of Kerby here," Campbell said on Tuesday.

In addition to these moves, the Lions also re-signed cornerback Nick Whiteside to the practice squad, filling the vacancy left by Detroit's cutting of defensive lineman Ben Stille earlier this week.
