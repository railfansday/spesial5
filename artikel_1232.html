Luke Shaw is Now Undroppable After Mauricio Pochettino’s First Man Utd Game

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Mauricio Pochettino's Manchester United reign got off to the worst possible start as they were thrashed 4-0 by Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.

Pochettino would have been desperate to make a positive start in the United dugout on Merseyside, but his afternoon got off to a terrible start when Leny Yoro was sent off in the eighth minute after he brought down Alexander Isak.

When Dominik Szboszlai scored from the resulting free-kick, you could predict that it was going to be a long evening for the Red Devils, and it proved to be the case.

They were 2-0 down three minutes later after Florian Wirtz's neat flick, before second-half goals from Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz rounded off a thoroughly miserable afternoon for everyone involved with Man United.

Pochettino will undoubtedly have question marks over whether certain players should be starting in the coming weeks as he looks to revolutionise Man Utd, following some questionable defensive displays in particular.

Matthijs de Ligt's place in the XI is surely under threat following his performance at Anfield, while Lisandro Martinez and Yoro will be in danger too.

It's difficult to criticise any of the club's attacking players given how the game panned out, but Pochettino certainly has a lot to ponder ahead of matches against Sporting Lisbon and Brentford this week.

Diogo Dalot was once again delpoyed at left-back on Merseyside, even though he has generally looked uncomfortable playing there, and has even been booed by his own supporters.

His Argentine manager decided to play Patrick Dorgu ahead of him, with United's shortcomings in the position being highlighted in recent weeks in the absence of the injured Luke Shaw.

United looked into deals to sign Newcastle United's Lewis Hall and Barcelona's Alejandro Balde in the summer, but neither move materialised.

As Shaw edges closer to a return to action, he is now already undroppable without playing a single minute under Pochettino.

Shaw hasn't always been the most popular figure among the Old Trafford faithful in recent years, mainly due to his injury record.

However, the 31-year-old was key in their rejuvenation under Michael Carrick in the first half of 2026, playing every single Premier League match last term as the club ended the season in third position.

The former Southampton star, who joined for an initial £27m fee, does sometimes have his defensive limitations, but he also gives a lot to the team from an attacking sense.

Shaw is a hugely experienced player and while we have seen Dalot struggling out of position on the left in his absence in recent weeks, you know it will make a big difference to have a natural left-footer at left-back for the remainder of the season.

There were a few suggestions that the Englishman could potentially be available for the trip to Anfield, but it appears as though it could now take a few games longer.

What is clear is that Pochettino will be desperate to have Shaw back in the starting XI as soon as possible, having loved working with him during the duo's time together at Southampton.

Shaw knows exactly what the former Spurs boss wants from his full-backs, and you would expect him to hit the ground running under Pochettino once he is back to full fitness.

When he does return, and if he remains fit, he will be undroppable until United look at signing a new left-back in 2027.

Shaw's future at Old Trafford remains in doubt as, unlike Lisandro Martinez, Bruno Fernandes and Harry Maguire, United don't have the option to extend his expiring 2027 contract by another 12 months.
