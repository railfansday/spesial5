Hutchinson, Pulisic and Cissè react to Milan’s comeback win over Olympiacos

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Omari Hutchinson, Christian Pulisic and Alphadjo Cissè have all reacted to Milan’s comeback win over Olympiacos, with Pulisic warning that every game is now a final.

With quotes via TuttoMercatoWeb and MilanNews.it, the trio spoke to Sky Sport after the Rossoneri recovered from 2-0 down to win 4-2 in Piraeus, giving their Europa League campaign a vital boost.

Hutchinson was the star of the night. He started the fightback with a brilliant solo goal, set up Cissè and Pulisic, then broke from an Olympiacos corner to begin the move for Diego Moreira’s late fourth.

“I played in a slightly different position, then on the right I had much more space,” Hutchinson said. “I feel very comfortable here, there are a lot of players playing really well.

“When I come in, I have to make an impact. It’s the same for me wherever I play, but right wing is the best position because I have more space. I’m adapting and enjoying it a lot.”

Pulisic made the difference off the bench. He replaced Gonçalo Ramos at half-time, scored the winner and set up the fourth. According to our match report, that’s his fifth goal in his last six appearances.

“It was a decisive game for us. From today, every game is like a final,” the American said. “We didn’t start well, but we were much better in the second half. It’s important to find this intensity.

“Hutchinson changed this game. I’m always there to help the team with whatever it needs.”

Pulisic admitted Milan must stop making slow starts in Europe.

“I don’t have answers. It’s difficult. It’s not easy to play here, they played with a lot of energy. But you’re right, we have to get into games earlier, otherwise it’s not good enough.

“We also have a new coach this year, and it takes some time to learn this system. I see a real team that sticks together. We’re all growing together.”

Cissè’s header made it 2-2, and he put the team first.

“Scoring is always nice, but the win comes before everything,” he said.

Asked where he plays when Hutchinson is used behind the striker, he added: “He’s left-footed, I play on the left. Like the others, I’m available for whatever the coach needs.”

Milan now turn their attention to Monday’s trip to Cagliari.
