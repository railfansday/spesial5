Furious Neymar explodes at rival fans in extraordinary meltdown after Santos win - as former PSG star is caught saying: 'Respect me, you f***ing small team'

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Published: 11:20 EST, 19 November 2026 | Updated: 11:31 EST, 19 November 2026

Neymar's streak of rage continued this week as he erupted with an expletive rant at rival fans who were provoking him.

The Santos star, 34, raised hell at Coritiba fans following his side's 1-0 win over them on Wednesday, with the climax of the Brazilian season approaching.

'Don't f*** around! You're going to Serie B, you idiots,' he shouted.

'Suck it! You have to respect me, respect me, you f****** small team.'

It had been a frustrating day in fairness - Santos' team bus had broken down on the way, requiring the squad to change vehicles.

And this is not the first time Neymar has vented his spleen this season.

Neymar branded Coritiba a 'small f****** team' in a rant at rival supporters who taunted him

Earlier this season, he was involved in a furious altercation with rival officials in the tunnel

In August, he was involved in a furious altercation with Remo officials after Santos beat them in the last 16 of the Brazilian Cup.

The Brazilian football icon screamed inside the tunnel as he was drawn into a heated exchange with Remo's directors.

He repeatedly shouted the word 'eliminated' before taunting the rivals with a series of gestures at his dressing room's entrance.

Despite his bragging over other teams, it has been a distinctly average campaign for Santos so far.

Santos sit eighth in the Brazilian Serie A and, despite him telling Coritiba that they will go down, that looks unlikely with the southern side sat 13th out of 20 with four games to go, four points above the drop zone.

Meanwhile, while he mocked Remo over being eliminated from the cup, Santos went out in the quarter-finals 3-0 on aggregate to Palmeiras.

Neymar has had a fairly effective season personally, scoring 10 goals and recording eight assists in all competitions while captaining Santos.

It remains to be seen whether he will play beyond the end of his contract in December, but the club's chief executive, who happens to be Neymar's dad, certainly wants to extend his son's term.

'We know that in every transfer window, offers come in for him to leave. Yet Neymar always chooses to stay at Santos. What I'm anticipating is that, first and foremost, I need to keep Neymar happy. Do you understand?' he said.

'And if he's happy here, I can keep him here. So, look, down this final stretch, the work we've done to structure the squad and open up positive possibilities has given us the license to dream.'

Neymar, who was widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers of his generation during his time at Barcelona and Paris Saint-Germain, returned to his boyhood club Santos from Saudi Arabian club Al-Hilal in January 2025.

His £200million move from Barcelona to Paris Saint-Germain remains the most expensive transfer of all time.

In June he made his first appearance for Brazil since 2023 after rupturing his ACL and meniscus in his left knee.
