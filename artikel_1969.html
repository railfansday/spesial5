Late winner at Selsey keeps Worthing United’s promotion bid firing

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Mavericks once again demonstrated the character, resilience and never-say-die attitude that have become hallmarks of their recent performances, snatching all three points in dramatic fashion.

The result followed an impressive 2-0 win at Saltdean United and moved Worthing United up to third place, with 36 points from 17 matches.

Under joint managers Glenn Souter and Mark Curram, United have now collected 17 points from their last possible 21, recording five victories and two draws.

That sequence has included victories over Guildford City, Shoreham, Arundel, Saltdean and Selsey, alongside hard-fought draws against league leaders Infinity and promotion rivals Jarvis Brook.

It has been a run that has tested the Mavericks in different ways. From the dramatic late equaliser against Infinity to recovering from two goals down at Jarvis Brook, the side have shown a growing belief that they can get a result regardless of the circumstances.

Souter commented: “Saturday probably summed up where we are as a group at the moment. We weren’t necessarily at our best, but the players kept believing right until the final whistle, and to get a winner so late on was a fantastic feeling.

“Looking back over the last seven league matches, I’m really pleased with what the lads have produced. We’ve played some very good football, but equally importantly, we’ve shown the other side of our game. We’ve had to dig deep, defend properly, come from behind and find ways to win.

“That’s what successful teams have to do. You aren’t going to dominate every game over a long season, particularly in this league, but you have to be prepared to fight for every point.

“We’ve got a really good group of lads here who are playing for each other. The togetherness in the dressing room is excellent, and even when things aren’t going our way, there’s a genuine belief that we can turn games around.

“Seventeen points from 21 is a fantastic return, but we’re not getting carried away. We know there are areas where we can improve, and I still believe there’s another level in this team.

“After coming so close last season, the lads know what’s required. We’re determined to keep pushing, stay amongst the leading sides and give ourselves the best possible chance of achieving something special this season.

“The coaches, Craig Nagle and Jack Willis have been brilliant, and hopefully we can keep giving them a platform to grow and develop the team alongside us. There’s a long way to go, but the spirit and determination we’re showing at the moment are exactly what we need.”

With the Mavericks firmly in the promotion picture, their recent form and remarkable fighting spirit are providing plenty of reasons for optimism at Lyons Way.

They host struggling Dorking Wanderers B on Saturday.
