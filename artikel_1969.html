Celtic vs St Mirren injury news: 4 out and several doubts as Martin O’Neill sweats on key trio

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Here is the latest team news ahead of Celtic v St Mirren in the Scottish Premiership at Parkhead on Saturday afternoon.

Celtic will aim to go seven points clear at the top of the Scottish Premiership when they welcome St Mirren to Parkhead on Saturday afternoon (kick-off: 3pm).

Currently four points clear at the top of the table, Martin O’Neill’s men can pull further clear of second-placed Rangers with a win, with their Glasgow rivals not in action until later that evening, though Celtic will have played two games more by the time they kick-off against Motherwell.

As for their visitors, St Mirren, they sit in eighth but will be desperate for points after failing to win any of their last three games. Head coach Craig McLeish has several injury issues, though, but is hopeful of at least one player returning this weekend.
