Why Is Crypto Up Today? BTC Back Above $60K – is the Bull Market Back?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Why Is Crypto Up Today? This is the question every trader is asking after the cryptocurrency market staged a clear rebound on Thursday, November 26, 2026, snapping a multi-day losing streak and giving battered traders some relief.

After sliding to multi-week lows earlier in the week, major tokens climbed as buyers stepped in at discounted levels. The total market cap is up over +2% overnight, sitting at $2.15 trillion, with Bitcoin crucially reclaiming $60K with a near-4% daily move.

Bitcoin reclaimed the psychologically important $60,000 level and traded between roughly $60,400 and $60,800, gaining about 2.5–3% over 24 hours. Ethereum followed closely, rising to the $1,690–$1,700 range for a similar percentage gain.

Solana outperformed, advancing roughly 4% to $70.50–$71.20, while XRP climbed about 3.5–4.5% to $0.95–$0.96. The total crypto market capitalization recovered to approximately $2.10–$2.16 trillion, up 1–2% on the day according to CoinGecko data.

Trading volume remained elevated, up 22% from yesterday to $86.4Bn, and short liquidations added upward pressure as overleveraged bearish positions were forced to cover.

Several factors combined to fuel the bounce. First, prices had become deeply oversold after Bitcoin’s drop toward the $57,000–$58,000 zone. Technical indicators such as RSI on shorter timeframes reached extreme low readings, historically a setup that attracts dip-buyers and algorithmic mean-reversion trades.

Second, equity markets provided a supportive backdrop. A recovery in technology and AI-related stocks helped lift risk appetite more broadly, and crypto continues to trade with a high correlation to those sectors.

Positive developments around a U.S. Strategic Bitcoin Reserve bill clearing a House committee also offered a modest sentiment boost, reminding investors that longer-term institutional and policy interest has not disappeared.

Even as U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs continued to post outflows (including a sizable figure on the prior session), the market rose anyway. This divergence often signals that stronger hands are absorbing remaining sellers or that short covering is outweighing institutional redemptions in the short term.

The most encouraging aspect of today’s move is that it occurred after an extended period of heavy selling and extreme fear. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index remained in Extreme Fear territory (readings near 11–13 as of November 26).

This is a level that has frequently coincided with local bottoms in previous cycles. When the market can rally while sentiment is still this depressed, it often suggests forced selling is exhausting, and patient capital is beginning to accumulate.

Bitcoin’s ability to reclaim and hold above $60,000 after testing lower support is particularly constructive. A series of higher lows on the daily chart, if sustained, would mark a potential shift in short-term market structure.

While one day of gains does not confirm a new bull market, the conditions for a more sustained recovery are starting to align. Extreme fear, oversold technicals, and visible dip-buying create a classic setup for multi-week or multi-month advances once the selling pressure fully abates.

If ETF outflows begin to slow or reverse in the coming sessions, and if upcoming economic data softens rate-hike expectations, the path higher would open more clearly.

Institutional accumulation has continued in the background even during the recent drawdown, and any improvement in broader risk sentiment could amplify the move. For now, today’s rebound offers the first tangible evidence in days that the worst of the immediate selling may be behind the market.

Whether it becomes a durable rally will depend on follow-through volume and Bitcoin’s ability to defend the $60,000 area, but the green candles offer a welcome dose of hope to those eagerly wondering why crypto is up today.
