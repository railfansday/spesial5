Zcash Futures OI (Open Interest) fell 3.35% in the last 24 hours to $2.44 billion. Its CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta) is the most negative among the major coins, meaning market-order selling outweighed buying. Funding is positive, implying longs are paying shorts. Attracting further attention is its futures flow within the last 24 hours, which fell by 859.86%, with futures outflows surpassing inflows.

According to CoinGlass data, futures outflows recorded in this period came in at $1.96 billion, while inflows arrived at $1.87 billion.

The drop in the futures flow indicates a change in the positioning of traders, as market participants are reassessing their exposure to ZEC amid significant liquidations of leveraged long positions.

According to CoinGlass data, Zcash's total liquidations in the last 24 hours came in at $15.76 million, with longs accounting for the majority at $12.96 million while short liquidations came in at $2.81 million.

In separate news, major crypto exchange Binance has commenced withdrawals for crypto networks days after it suffered a hack that impacted nearly $6.3 billion in client assets. In line with this, withdrawals have now been re-enabled for Zcash and the ZEC network.
