XRP Ledger Introduces Quantum-Proof Security Upgrade to Users

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

As the XRP Ledger developers continue to make committed efforts to make the network fully quantum-resistant in the coming year, the team has just proposed a major upgrade that has sparked a buzz across the XRP community.

Sparking fresh excitement across the XRP community earlier today, the XRP Ledger (XRPL) Foundation has disclosed plans to introduce quantum-resistant accounts to users.

The team revealed plans to introduce a new feature to the XRP Ledger to offer users strict protection against future quantum computing threats without paying additional transaction fees.

While it is yet to be implemented, the proposed design will allow users to add a NIST-approved ML-DSA-44 signing key to their accounts and disable their existing keys.

This way, it will become harder for potential quantum attacks to compromise accounts, especially those whose public keys have already been exposed.

Apart from the extra layer of protection the upgrade promises to offer users, its cost-effective design has stirred more interest among the XRP community.

Notably, users will not have to pay higher transaction fees to access the additional security, as the proposed feature allows XRP holders to strengthen their account protection without worrying about extra transaction costs.

The proposed upgrade sends relief across the crypto ecosystem as it addresses concerns about the possibility of increasingly powerful quantum computers eventually breaking some of the cryptographic systems currently used to secure blockchains and cryptocurrencies.

While the upgrade is yet to launch, the team revealed that it has scheduled a migration study for the first half of 2027.

The study is aimed at examining how existing accounts can transition to the new security system and what the process would involve for users.

The proposed security upgrade has further sparked more optimism as it is also part of Ripple's broader aim of achieving full quantum protection by 2028.
