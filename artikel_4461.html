Karma Automotive, the small California-based automaker that emerged from the bankruptcy of Fisker Automotive in 2014, has released the Gyesera, and the latest version of this PHEV four-door sees some interior revisions.

While the cabin shares similarities with its predecessor, the Revero, the dashboard has been reworked, replacing the four circular air vents with rectangular vents hidden within a black horizontal trim piece.

The Gyesera’s cool-looking bucket seats are rather narrow, and the unadjustable headrests are mounted too low.

The Gyesera features paddles mounted behind the steering wheel, with the right paddle used for adjusting regenerative braking settings and the left paddle used to change the drive mode.

The center console extends into the rear-seat area.

A wireless charger and two USB-C ports are hidden within the center console to keep your phone topped up.

Most functions are handled via the 10.1-inch touchscreen mounted low in the center console, which is complemented by a 12.3-inch digital instrument cluster.
