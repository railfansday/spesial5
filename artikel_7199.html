Advertising Is a Top Priority for Best Buy’s New CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

10%: Projected year-over-year growth of Best Buy Ads this year. Best Buy Ads made $900 million in 2025.

$10.2 billion: Best Buy’s third-quarter revenue, up 5.8% from a year ago.

6.1%: Best Buy’s comparable sales growth, its highest in five years.

24.1%: Best Buy’s domestic gross profit rate, up from 23.3% a year ago, driven in part by growth from Best Buy Ads.

More than $1.3 billion: Expected sales on Best Buy’s third-party marketplace in 2026.

Nearly 10,000: Number of products available from Best Buy on TikTok Shop.

Jason Bonfig reported Best Buy’s earnings for the first time as CEO, though he’s spent 27 years at the retailer.
