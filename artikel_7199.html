Channing Tatum Suits Up as Gambit for Dunkin’s First-Ever Marvel Partnership

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Nevermind their muscular physiques and super strength. Superheroes like donuts, too.

Dunkin’s first-ever partnership with Marvel Studios kicks off today (Nov. 4), ahead of the release of “Avengers: Doomsday” in theaters on Dec. 18. It will roll out nationwide.

Headlining the campaign is a new spot, “MUNCHKIN Incursion,” starring Channing Tatum reprising his role as Gambit, and H.E.R.B.I.E., the robot companion of the Fantastic Four.

In the 30-second ad, Gambit contemplates the high stakes of doomsday in his Cajun accent. “Doomsday is here, mon ami,” Gambit tells H.E.R.B.I.E., who brings him a tray of Munchkins and coffee. “You got any ideas in that tin can head of yours?”

Richard Lowe is a freelance writer based in Los Angeles.
