PSG: Nasser Al-Khelaïfi visited Khvicha Kvaratskhelia

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Victim of a rupture of the anterior cruciate ligament of the right knee against FC Barcelona (4-2), on Tuesday October 20, Khvicha Kvaratskhelia will observe a long period of unavailability. The Georgian winger also had a successful operation as announced by Paris Saint-Germain.

The day after his operation at the Aspetar clinic in Doha, Qatar, the president of Paris Saint-Germain Nasser Al-Khelaïfi went there to provide his support and that of the entire club. A great mark of confidence before starting the rehabilitation process.
