Inter keen on Hermoso as potential free agent

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Inter are looking to the capital to strengthen their defence. Cristian Chivu has asked the management for a central defender, and the preferred option is Oliver Provstgaard.

The Dane, however, is having a strong season, and Lazio is asking €40 million to let him go.

According to La Repubblica, Giuseppe Marotta has set his sights on Mario Hermoso.

As he did with Henrikh Mkhitaryan, the Nerazzurri executive wants to capitalize on the Spanish defender’s expiring contract to secure him on a free transfer. Roma, for their part, don’t want to lose a key player for Gian Piero Gasperini and want to move quickly.

A two-year extension with an option for a third is already on the table. Gasperini believes Hermoso is crucial, and a move to a rival like Inter isn’t exactly on the cards for the Giallorossi.

The decision rests with the Spanish defender, who has scored three goals in 14 appearances so far, perfectly fulfilling the role of Gasperini’s wingman.

Roma wants to avoid a Zeki Celik repeat, and to do so, Tony D’Amico will have the task of making his first steps immediately without hesitation. The duel between the Giallorossi and the Nerazzurri will therefore be played out off the pitch as well.

For his part, Mario knows full well that in the capital he has found the right niche to experience a second youth, and the days when he struggled to establish himself are behind him.
