Gasperini chooses not to risk Dybala vs Sassuolo

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Paulo Dybala will be unavailable to Gian Piero Gasperini for Roma-Sassuolo, scheduled for tomorrow at the Stadio Olimpico.

The Argentine striker also performed individualized training in his latest training session, but was unable to recover in time for the match against the Neroverdi.

As reported by La Gazzetta dello Sport, Dybala will now miss seven consecutive matches, as he is determined to avoid any risk before the international break.

The goal is to return to the pitch only after regaining top physical condition.

The November break will be an important opportunity to recover the injured players and return some key players to Gasperini.

Against Sassuolo, meanwhile, the coach is expected to rely on Soulé and Mora to support Malen, who will lead the Giallorossi’s attack.
