French team: why Zidane preferred to line up Dembélé at the forefront rather than Mbappé

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Injured during the Champions League clash between Paris Saint-Germain and FC Barcelona (4-2), Ousmane Dembélé did not join the French team for the November rally and will therefore not participate in this evening’s clash against Italy. Despite this absence, the Parisian striker retains a special place in Zinédine Zidane’s plans. According to a revelation from Parisianthe Blues coach did not hesitate to make a strong tactical decision, even if it meant offending his captain, Kylian Mbappé.

The daily reveals that Zidane spoke with Dembélé at Clairefontaine, from his first meeting. The former Real Madrid coach told him of his essential need: incessant all-terrain pressing to asphyxiate opposing defenses. Accustomed to taking on this role of pressing attacker with PSG, the former Rennais was thus privileged in this position, forcing Mbappé to regain his preferential positioning on the left flank.
