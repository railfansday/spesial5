Tanggal: 2026-11-11

There will be some games where Mikel Arteta has no choice but to tweak the XI, rest some tired legs and give his second-choice attackers the chance – although given the option he may wish to pick the same team every week.

The three big guns of his attack were primed and ready to come on in the next break in play when Arsenal at last broke through in this, their second Champions League league phase game of the season against some stubborn opposition from Ligue 1. Lille had sat deep against an Arsenal attack without Bukayo Saka, Martin Odegaard and Kai Havertz, and the French club were past the hour when it all finally broke.

That was a superb finish from Jurrien Timber who, with two touches, demonstrated that in the modern era even defenders are capable of the kind of moments of skill and composure that we once assumed were only within the capacity of the best attackers. He gave Arsenal the lead just as Mikel Arteta had decided he had seen enough from some of his second-choice attackers – Viktor Gyokeres, Noni Madueke and Mikel Merino. They had created some chances and at times looked promising, but as soon as the big three were on the difference was obvious.

Arsenal pay tribute to one of the club’s greatest custodians this evening following the news of Ken Friar’s death aged 92. Friar worked in various roles at the club across more than 70 years, and a bridge from Drayton Park to Emirates Stadium is named in his honour.

A statue at the end of the bridge is a nod to the chance meeting the 11-year-old Friar had with Arsenal manager George Allison in 1945, while playing football outside Highbury with his friends.

As Sam Dean wrote earlier today, finishing the story: “Allison was so impressed by Friar’s enthusiasm that he told him to report to his office at 9am the next day. Friar did as he was instructed and his reward was a job offer: he became a messenger at the stadium on match days. He was paid 12 and a half pence per week.”

Friar was a long-serving member of the Arsenal board, before stepping back in 2020 and becoming life president.
