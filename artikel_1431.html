Kean’s new tibia injury rules former Juventus striker out of Como v Juventus

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Moise Kean has broken down again in Como training, three weeks after treatment that was meant to fix his shin. The former Juventus striker is set to miss about two more months, so he will not face his old club on 29 November.

Moise Kean has suffered a new tibia injury in training with Como. The former Juventus striker is now undergoing further medical tests. Sky Sport Italia reported that he will be out for about two more months. That timeline rules him out of Como’s home game against Juventus.

Como’s statement of 17 November gave no recovery date. The club promised an update once more information was available.

The new problem follows a treatment that was meant to end his shin trouble. On 30 October, Como’s update described his treatment at the Cugat Institute in Barcelona as successful. The club put his recovery at about five weeks. His progress was to be reviewed every day.

That plan has now collapsed. Kean had been out since early October. The original injury came while he was playing for Italy against France on 2 October. That match was the first leg of a Nations League tie.

The setback is a heavy blow for Como. In Serie A, his outings for the club added up to 160 minutes. He has yet to score, including in his single Champions League game. He joined from Fiorentina on an initial loan, with an obligation to make the move permanent. At Fiorentina he scored 27 league goals in two seasons, 19 of them in 2024/25. Before that, he came through the Juventus academy.

Without him, Como will have to rely on Douvikas up front. The club may also look for a striker in January.

For Juventus, the news removes one threat from a crucial stretch. Como are their next opponents after Venezia. The game is the Sunday evening fixture a week later, in round 13 of Serie A. Kick-off is at 20:45.

Luciano Spalletti’s side need results. Juventus sit six points behind fourth place and have averaged just over 1.6 points per game, according to Corriere dello Sport. The paper sees the run of fixtures as decisive for the push for a Champions League place. It warns that dropping points against Venezia would force Juventus to win every remaining game of 2026 to stay in touch with the top four.

The schedule offers little respite after the trip to Como. Juventus host Udinese on 6 December and Monza on 14 December. They then travel to Roma on 19 December.
