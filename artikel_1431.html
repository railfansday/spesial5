Roma make first steps in talks for Malen’s contract renewal

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Donyell Malen is proving to be the perfect striker for Gian Piero Gasperini’s Roma.

Even the Giallorossi coach recently admitted that even a stronger center forward probably wouldn’t mesh so well with the rest of the team.

The Dutch striker always has more than one chance to score, and his goal average this season is record-breaking: 17 goals and 5 assists in just 16 appearances. An alien.

Malen has changed Roma’s history. Before his arrival, the Giallorossi team struggled to score, but this year he’s set a league record: 36 goals after 12 games. Astonishing numbers.

Malen has a contract with Roma until June 30, 2030, worth €3.7 million per season. As Corriere dello Sport reports, there’s no rush to renew, but something is moving.

The Giallorossi are considering a salary increase (over €4 million) to ward off potential suitors from around Europe. Everyone likes the Dutch striker, but he’s found his best form in the capital. That’s why Roma wants to secure him.
