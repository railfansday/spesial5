Who Is David Walliams, the UK Entertainer Accused of Sexual Misconduct?

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The British entertainer rose to fame with the television show “Little Britain” and has published over 40 children’s books.

The British entertainer David Walliams, who was publicly accused on Tuesday of a pattern of sexual misconduct, is a comedian and prolific author of children’s books who has been a fixture on Britain’s television screens for more than 20 years.

Mr. Walliams, 55, rose to fame as a sketch show comedian in the 2000s and later as a judge on “Britain’s Got Talent.” He is also one of the country’s most successful children’s book authors, publishing over 40 titles that have sold tens of millions of copies.

On Tuesday, two British news organizations, The Guardian and ITV News, published detailed allegations that Mr. Walliams had exploited his celebrity status to sexually prey on women, including some he had met when they were as young as 15. Mr. Walliams, who fought a weekslong court battle to keep the claims private, has denied the accusations. “I am not a sexual predator,” he wrote in a letter sent to news outlets.

Mr. Walliams burst onto the national stage in Britain in the early 2000s as a transgressive comedy writer and entertainer.

In his breakout BBC comedy sketch show “Little Britain,” which first aired in 2003, he and his comedy partner Matt Lucas played a series of eccentric characters who poked fun at almost every minority group imaginable, including mothers on welfare benefits, wheelchair users and gay men.

The show pushed the boundaries of acceptability, even during a period of provocative comedy television in Britain. Mr. Walliams and Mr. Lucas donned fat suits, caked themselves in makeup and wore blackface.
