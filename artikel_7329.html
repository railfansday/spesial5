Netlify: Staff Software Engineer (Interfaces)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

As a Staff Software Engineer at Netlify, you'll join a collaborative team that values continuous learning, mutual support, and shared growth. Together, we create tools that help developers build faster, more intuitively, and with greater impact. Our Experience team owns the core product experience at Netlify, which is every way a human or an agent interacts with the platform, from our React-based web application to CLI, MCP, SDK, and API. If users interact with it directly, it's likely ours. We're reimagining how developers experience web development by thoughtfully integrating AI into our workflows - not as a novelty, but as a practical tool that enhances how we build the web.

With our team, you'll help set technical direction for our product area, mentor a highly senior team of engineers, and shape user experiences that serve millions of developers. You'll work closely with Product, Design, and engineering leadership, and partner with teams across the organization to build, iterate, and scale meaningful solutions. You'll have the autonomy to guide what we build and how, and the influence to bring others along with you, all while staying hands-on in the codebase.

We're looking for thoughtful engineers who enjoy owning problems end to end, collaborating across disciplines, and solving complex technical challenges for products that millions of developers rely on every day. AI is a foundational part of how we build, so we value engineers who are excited to thoughtfully incorporate AI-assisted workflows while continuing to apply strong engineering fundamentals. We believe the best engineering happens when talented people are trusted to solve difficult problems together, share what they're learning, and create space for different perspectives to shape better solutions.  With our team, you'll have real ownership from day one - not a tiny slice of a massive codebase. Engineers here are trusted to make decisions, shape technical direction, and move quickly while collaborating closely with Product and Design.
