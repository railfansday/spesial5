Vertican: Senior Applied AI Engineer, Document Intelligence

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

For 40 years Vertican has buildt software that runs law firms. One year ago we created a new modern AI platform for document automation (processing/extraction/redacting) which our customers have been adopting faster than any product we've ever had. Our suite of AI-enabled products save customers thousands of hours each week by processing high volumes of documents with precision and speed.The AI team is growing and we'd like you to join.

We are hiring an engineer to own the document processing pipelines end to end: design, benchmarking, experimenting with new architectures and tools, threshold required for deploying a new pipeline, etc.

We believe it's important to build our own tooling to help us run, improve, and measure our applications. Therefore, the ideal candidate thinks outside the box on how to not just build the product/pipeline, but also the tooling needed around it.

You will be the first dedicated ML hire on the product taking over from the engineering manager who build the current solution and will work directly with the founding engineering team.

This is a senior, hands-on individual-contributor role, but we are small team and you'll be involved in design, planning, roadmapping, and architectural decisions.

Design the pipelines. Own the architecture of the extraction-to-posting pipelines: page classification, field extraction, candidate retrieval, claim matching, confidence gating and the hand-off to human review, etc. Decide where rules end and models begin, and what each stage must guarantee to the next, what the separation of concerns are, etc.

We have different pipelines for different document types and expect that at any given point in time we may have new ones emerge and may consolidate others.

Evaluate new systems before we adopt them. Run structured trials of candidate components and recommend what to keep. Examples include probabilistic record linkage (Splink), Azure AI Content Understanding and Document Intelligence, Azure OpenAI and Foundry-hosted models, and LLM agents with tools. Similarly, test new models with the existing pipeline as they are relased from the frontier labs.

Build the evaluation process. You'll own the process of evaluating false positives, false negatives, errors of ommission, etc, that we ultimately use to determine if we should upgrade the pipeline.

Ship and operate. Take components from trial to production in our python workers on the cloud, instrument them, watch cost and quota, and diagnose the cases reviewers send back.

Set direction. Work with leadereship on the planing for accuracy, cost improvement, and overall product improvements.

Small team, direct access to large datasets, weekly planning, daily standup, everyone remote in US timezones.

Vertican is an equal opportunity employer. We hire on merit and do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability or veteran status.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
