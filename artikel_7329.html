Sumo Logic: Principal Software Engineer

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Sumo Logic is a cloud-native SaaS data analytics platform that solves complex observability and security problems. Customers choose our product because it allows them to easily monitor, optimize, and secure their applications, systems, and infrastructures.

Sumo Logic is seeking a Principal Systems Architect to lead the design and evolution of world class platform architectures that support the company’s security and observability SaaS solutions. This role is responsible for driving technical strategy as an org-wide expert whose influence cuts across the organization. They will ensure solutions are secure, resilient, scalable, cost effective while guiding a course of action based on broad organizational goals.

Reporting directly to the Chief Product and Technology Officer (CPTO), the architect will partner closely with engineering, platform, infrastructure, security, and product teams to align architectural vision and execution. As a strategic battle-tested leader, they will direct and coordinate multiple simultaneous initiatives to deliver consistent engineering standards across Sumo Logic. The ideal candidate brings deep backend systems expertise and the ability to drive critical decisions that impact the organization and the industry at a Principal level. This role serves as the US architectural lead, partnering with architectural leadership counterparts in India.

Sumo Logic, Inc. helps make the digital world secure, fast, and reliable by unifying critical security and operational data through its Intelligent Operations Platform. Built to address the increasing complexity of modern cybersecurity and cloud operations challenges, we empower digital teams to move from reaction to readiness—combining agentic AI-powered SIEM and log analytics into a single platform to detect, investigate, and resolve modern challenges. Customers around the world rely on Sumo Logic for trusted insights to protect against security threats, ensure reliability, and gain powerful insights into their digital environments. For more information, visit www.sumologic.com.

The expected annual base salary range for this position is $213,000 - $250,000. Compensation varies based on a variety of factors which include (but aren’t limited to) role level, skills and competencies, qualifications, knowledge, location, and experience. In addition to base pay, certain roles are eligible to participate in our bonus or commission plans and equity awards. We also offer a comprehensive benefits package, including medical, dental, and vision insurance, a 401(k) retirement plan, and paid time off.

This position will remain open until filled; there is no application deadline. To be considered, please submit your application.
