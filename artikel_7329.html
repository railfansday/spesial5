Shopify Stores Face Suspected AI-Driven Attacks

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

German-based software engineer Roman Zipp was buying a mechanical keyboard from a Shopify-powered storefront last month, when he discovered a series of ecommerce cyberattacks.

As he checked out, he noticed the URL was from a domain affiliated neither with Shopify nor the merchant, he told ADWEEK.

Examining the source code revealed a redirect to a fake checkout screen, likely meant to fool unsuspecting shoppers into handing over their personal information.

The code wasn’t in the Shopify platform itself, but a third-party extension used by the merchant via the Shopify App Store — a marketplace where sellers can find apps to add functionality to their storefronts, like recommendation engines or post-checkout upsell screens.

Mitchell Parton is ADWEEK's senior commerce reporter, covering retail media as well as the rise of agentic and social commerce. Before joining ADWEEK in 2026, he was a reporter at Digiday Media's Modern Retail and before that wrote for several regional publications in Texas.
