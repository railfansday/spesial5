JSWD Wins Competition to Design Mixed-Use RIYMAR Towers in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

German architecture practice JSWD has been selected to design RIYMAR Towers, a proposed mixed-use development in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, following an international invited architecture competition organized by Malcolm Reading Consultants on behalf of RIYMAR Real Estate. Located near the intersection of King Fahd Road and Olaya Street, adjacent to the Riyadh Gallery complex, the project is planned as a new development combining residential, hospitality, office, retail, and leisure functions. The project is positioned within the broader urban transformation outlined by Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030, which includes objectives related to tourism, cultural development, hospitality, and increased homeownership.

JSWD's winning concept draws on the geological formations of the Tuwaiq Mountains and the architectural traditions of Riyadh. The proposal features vertically articulated volumes with carved surfaces, referencing natural rock formations, alongside a series of landscaped terraces conceived as oasis-like spaces. Its geometry and material expression also refer to the historic urban fabric of Diriyah, the settlement associated with the origins of the first Saudi state. The design combines these references within a contemporary architectural composition, with the terraces introducing planted outdoor spaces at different levels of the development.

The competition was conducted through an invited selection process, with five international practices shortlisted to develop initial concepts over an eleven-week period. The finalists were Aedas and AtkinsRéalis, both based in the UAE; JSWD, based in Germany; Mesura, based in Spain; and ZHA Architects, based in the United Kingdom. JSWD was selected following the presentation of the five proposals and will now continue developing the winning concept with a multidisciplinary team of consultants.
