Acura Reveals Limited-Edition Urban Spec Trim for Its 2027 Lineup

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Urban Spec brings gray paint paired with black wheels and exterior trim and is available on the 2027 Integra, ADX, and MDX.

There’s a glaring omission in the Acura lineup for 2027. The compact RDX SUV is skipping a model year before returning for 2028 with a new hybrid powertrain and sharper styling, and Acura will certainly miss the presence of its second-bestselling model next year. While it won’t quite make up for the RDX’s absence, Acura still wants to keep its lineup looking fresh and has now revealed the limited-edition Urban Spec trim for 2027, which will be offered on the Integra, ADX, and MDX.

The Urban Spec trim is pretty straightforward. It combines Acura’s premium Urban Gray paint color with gloss-black exterior accents for a subtly attractive look. This includes black door handles, decklid spoilers, antennas, badges, and wheel lugs. Inside, all three models will also feature Ebony upholstery to match the blacked-out exterior.

The special edition also brings larger wheels. The Integra Urban Spec gets black 18-inch wheels from the A-Spec trim, and the ADX Urban Spec rides on black 19-inch wheels, while the MDX gains the black 20-inch wheels from the A-Spec Advance trim. While that rounds out the changes for the Urban Spec, Acura promises more details about updates to the 2027 MDX soon.

While Acura has yet to reveal detailed pricing for the Urban Spec trims, it says that they will only require around $1500 more than the entry MSRP for each model. Acura says detailed pricing will emerge closer to the on-sale date for the Urban Spec, which is set to reach dealerships next month. The 2027 Urban Spec models will make their public debut at the 2026 Los Angeles auto show later this month.
