Kim Kardashian’s Skims responds to backlash over viral $150 advent calendar full of “junk”

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Kim Kardashian’s Skims has responded to the backlash about their $150 advent calendar, as one viral TikTok highlighted what’s inside, and some viewers said it was full of “junk.”

With Christmas not too far away, plenty of people have already started looking towards the holiday season. That means buying presents, finding a tree to decorate, and getting an advent calendar.

However, when it comes to the latter part, Kim Kardashian’s Skims has been taking some flak. The underwear brand released an advent calendar of accessories for $148, but it was quickly ripped apart on TikTok.

One TikToker’s video, which has 68 million views, called it “way too expensive,” after she found the advent calendar full of keychains, ornaments, hair ties, and other small accessories. The TikToker even said that the final window had to be “made of gold” to make it worth their while.

Speaking to Page Six, a Skims spokesperson said they have “listened carefully” to the backlash and admitted that the advent calendar “didn’t meet expectations” for fans.

“We want every customer to be truly happy with what they receive from Skims,” they continued. “Their feedback matters to us and will directly shape how we approach future releases.”

Additionally, they noted that Skims has “personally contacted every customer who purchased the calendar and offered them a choice of a full refund or a Skims credit to put towards other products.”

Some social media users have pointed out that a description for the advent calendar does list what is behind each window.
