GTA 6 Pre-Load Times, Install Size, And Global Release Times Confirmed

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Grand Theft Auto 6Grand Theft Auto VIGTA 6Pre-Load

We’re just over two weeks out from the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 and Rockstar Games has finally revealed all of the details people need to know about getting the digital-only open-world game installed on their gaming console of choice.

The studio also confirmed that, yes, GTA 6 has a whopping 212GB install size, so start making room for that and god help you if you have an Xbox Series S. Here’s everything you need to know about getting GTA 6 ready to play for its release on November 19, 2026.

Digital pre-loading on PlayStation 5 will start globally on Tuesday, November 10 at midnight local time. On Xbox Series X|S, pre-loading begins globally on Tuesday, November 10 at 12 a.m. GMT, which is 7:00 p.m. ET on the previous day, November 9.

If you pre-order GTA 6 digitally, you’ll have access to pre-load the game immediately. If you are waiting for one of the codes in a box to ship to your home or be picked up in store, those will become available starting November 12. You’ll be able to take that code, redeem it, and immediately start pre-loading the game.

That’s a big file size, and console servers are going to get hammered when everyone starts trying to download it at once. To put it in perspective, 212GB is roughly one third of the available storage on a base PS5 and roughly a quarter of the available storage on a 1TB model. On Xbox Series S, it’s closer to half. In addition to taking some time depending on the speed of your internet, you’ll want to make sure you have your library cleared of any non-essential games. Good luck to you if you have children who are constantly swapping between a dozen different games every day.

The global release times for when GTA 6 goes live were previously announced, and unfortunately the U.S. is one of the last time zones to get access to the game.

Yes, the game will have been out in some parts of the world for roughly 18 hours by the time GTA 6 goes live stateside. My advice is to just stay logged out that day.
