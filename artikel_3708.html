KFC Announces GTA 6 Promo Where You Get A Disc-Shaped Piece Of Chicken

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A lot of people are upset that they can't get GTA 6 on a physical disc, and KFC is now stepping up to do what Rockstar isn't.

KFC Spain has announced a "Replace the Disc" campaign where customers can get a free disc-shaped piece of fried chicken when they bring in their GTA 6 box. The promotion takes place December 19 and 20 from 4 to 8 PM at KFC locations in Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, and others in the country.

GTA 6 is sold at retailers like Best Buy, GameStop, and Target, but the box doesn't include a disc--instead, you get a code to unlock the game. GTA 6 owner Take-Two recently reported that 98% of its sales were digital, and while there will be some physical aspects to gaming forever, it's an increasingly small part of the business, Take-Two's boss has said.

This is not KFC's only promotion for GTA 6. KFC Australia is holding a fake funeral for a person named "Ann Alibi" in Australia's version of Miami on November 19. The fake funeral services are taking place in the middle of the day, per Ann's wishes, and so that people can have a "legitimate" reason to take the day off.

Numerous brands are latching onto GTA 6 to leverage the enormity of the game's release to promote their own stuff. McDonald's, for example, just announced a campaign to get your McNuggets or Big Mac delivered while you keep playing GTA 6. Even smaller, more regional brands--including the Massachusetts-based brewery Tree House--have announced GTA 6 promos.

GTA 6 officially launches for everyone on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. For now, you can read GameSpot's GTA 6 review and check out our GTA 6 guides hub for more details on how to make the most out of your trip to Vice City
