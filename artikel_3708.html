This Is What Video Game Preservation Looks Like

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

25 classic games from Electronic Arts’ vault are back up for sale on Steam and GOG. They include foundational works like Wing Commander and the Ultima series. Why is the company doing this? Some people at EA found a trove of materials, including master copies, sitting in a random room in the company’s Redwood Shores office in 2022. What followed was a four-year journey to share it with everyone else.

EA Senior Producer Jim Vessella had returned to the office after covid and was doing clean-up on leftover Betacam tapes from his work on the Command & Conquer Remastered Collection project. A facilities person directed him to a room elsewhere in the office that he’d never been in but which stored similar artifacts, Vessella told The Game Business.

A small team was put together to begin cataloging and digitizing everything. A business case was made to EA leadership to commercially re-release some of the re-discovered games. And Vessella worked closely with the existing fan community for Wing Commander to work on restoring it for an eventual remaster.

The sci-fi series is renowned for its prodigious use of FMVs and cinematics to tell its stories. Vessella documents at length the painstaking and often frustrating process of trying to reassemble everything so it was not only working but up to the quality standards fans would expect. Collaboration with GOG’s game preservation team engineers helped. And in the case of games like Road Rash, Vessella’s team even went out of its way to contact every band whose music was featured in the game to get the licensing cleared.
