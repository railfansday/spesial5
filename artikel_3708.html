How Brittany Snow Wants 'The Hunting Wives' to End After Exits and Deaths

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Hunting Wives is thriving after its overwhelming success on Netflix — but Brittany Snow already has an idea of how the show should end.

During an exclusive interview with Us at a PaleyLive event for the TV drama on Thursday, November 19, Snow, 40, weighed in on what kind of epic death scene she would want for her character, Sophie, saying, “I’d want to square off against Margo and see if I killed or got killed myself or we had to choose each other. Maybe we both shoot at the same time.”

Snow clarified that the options were a long way off.

“I hope that we last for [at least] eight seasons and that’s going to be the last scene where we have to either kill each other or it’s kind of an epic [scene] with the guns drawn. Like a duel?” she suggested. “[Like] Thelma and Louise. We could just go off into the sunset in a car — and wear wigs. We don’t want that, but I think that would be an epic death scene.”

After Us suggested an ending where Margo (Malin Åkerman) and Sophie found their way back to each other after Maple Brook’s constant death and destruction came to an end, Snow added, “Exactly. That’s what I want too.”

Åkerman, 48, has a similar outlook on the potential conclusion.

“[For our characters], I would pick a Thelma and Louise-style death,” she shared. “Self-inflicted over a cliff in my red truck.”

Based on May Cobb‘s book of the same name, The Hunting Wives introduced Us to Sophie, who moved from Boston to an East Texas town and found herself drawn to prominent socialite Margo. While some became invested in the murder at the center of the series, The Hunting Wives also provided plenty of entertainment with partying and steamy onscreen hookups.

While Sophie and Margo face plenty of obstacles while trying to explore a romance, Åkerman hasn’t given up hope, telling Us,”They find a way they come together and they break apart. It’s the epic, toxic love story that we all adore, so that’s really up to [creator] Rebecca [Cutter] to decide how they eventually, by season 13, run off together and live happily ever after.”

Åkerman did jokingly agree that things would be easier with some men out of the way.

“I agree [that we need to get rid of the men]. We need to because we need to get them out of the way so Sophie and Margo can be together forever,” she said. “And [Margo’s husband] Jed is standing in the way right now. But it’d be tricky.”

Cutter also spoke to Us about the odds of a happy ending for the couple, sharing, “Sophie obviously is the eyes through which we see the show. When she came in, she wasn’t ready for a Margo Banks. She wanted it, but she was in a weakened place in her life.”

She continued: “Over the course of two seasons, she becomes formidable. She becomes fierce. She becomes worthy and equal of Margo. So I think their relationship does get real in season 2, and we have moments, though, where we can really imagine a Margo and Sophie happy ending for them. But of course, they’re both survivors, and that trumps everything.”

The Hunting Wives returns to Netflix on November 26. Netflix has already ordered season 3 ahead of the sophomore season.
