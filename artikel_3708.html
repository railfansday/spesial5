Over ten years ago and half-a-decade before Elden Ring, FromSoftware head Hidetaka Miyazaki said he felt unworthy of comparisons to Zelda.

Speaking to Rolling Stone in 2016 (via Wayback Machine), Miyazaki was asked about comments that the Souls titles – and Bloodborne – are what The Legend of Zelda series should've evolved into. The developer responded at the time, "When I was a student, The Legend of Zelda was truly monumental, so to be perfectly honest, I feel deeply unworthy of the comparison."

Hidetaka Miyazaki au sujet de Zelda : "Quand j'étais étudiant, The Legend of Zelda était vraiment monumental, tellement que pour être honnête, je me sens profondément indigne de cette comparaison. The legend of Zelda et Dark Souls sont des jeux différents appartenant à des genres différents et ils sont guidés par différents concepts de game design. Ils n'aspirent pas aux mêmes idéaux. Et s'il y a des similitudes, c'est probablement parce que The Legend of Zelda est devenu avec le temps une sorte de manuel pour les jeux d'action 3D" Source : Rolling Stones, traduction NintendoMasterNovember 23, 2026

And while Zelda was definitely an influence on Miyazaki's work, he somewhat rebuked the comparison, saying, "The Legend of Zelda and Dark Souls are different games belonging to different genres though, and they're guided by different concepts of game design. They don't need to aspire to the same ideals." He adds, "If there are similarities, they probably stem from the fact that The Legend of Zelda became a sort of textbook for 3D action games," specifically in reference to Ocarina of Time.

Of course, we're in a far different world than we were 10 years ago – both with games and outside of them. Namely, The Legend of Zelda would reinvent itself with the release of Breath of the Wild months later, becoming one of the most acclaimed games ever made. And then five years after that, Miyazaki would take inspiration from Breath of the Wild while directing Elden Ring, which would also go on to be a generation-defining game like Zelda before it, and subsequently the developer's most successful game ever.

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake sits before Elden Ring and Skyrim as the 26th best game of all time, according to Metacritic.
