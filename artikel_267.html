If you were are a kid in the late 1990s, there’s a near-100 percent chance you either had a Tamagotchi or knew someone who did. The tiny digital pets came out of nowhere and became an overnight sensation, as millions of kids signed up to parent a crying, hungry, constantly dirty creature. Until they went to school, anyway, at which point their parents were given some new responsibilities they neither asked for nor wanted.

On this episode of Version History, we dig deep into the story of the Tamagotchi. That story has its 30th anniversary this week, even though the real craze only lasted a few years. David Pierce is joined by The Verge’s Mia Sato and Victoria Song to discuss the origins of the Tamagotchi, the product’s many iterations, its incredible engagement tactics, and whether any of us could tolerate the Tama experience three decades later. (We… could not.)
