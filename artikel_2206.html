Struggling £26m West Ham man eyed for escape transfer to South American giants

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

West Ham signed Taty Castellanos for a deal worth around £26m in January as they bid to salvage their Premier League status. WHUFC came up short despite their new striker netting six goals in 18 Premier League games, but his form has not continued in the Championship.

Now, a report from Argentine news outlet El Intransigente says Castellanos could find an escape route back to his home country. River Plate are keen on signing a new striker and the Hammers man sits atop their shortlist of targets.

Despite being from Argentina, Castellanos has never played senior football in his native. He has played in Chile, Uruguay, Spain and the United States, as well as in England with West Ham.

West Ham have not had an easy time in the Championship, dropping to 9th after Saturday’s loss to Blackburn Rovers. However, with 37 goals scored in 15 matches, they are still the league’s highest-scoring team by far.

Despite this, and his pedigree at a higher level, Castellanos has not found form in front of goal. He started the competitive campaign with a Carabao Cup brace against Portsmouth and scored in the same competition against Southampton in August too.

But, the 28-year-old has found the back of the net just once in the Championship, that being in the 6-0 home thumping of Wrexham. Castellanos has chipped in with a strong tally of five assists, but a total of 3.46 xG shows how he is underperforming in terms of goalscoring (FotMob).

It has seen him drop from the WHUFC starting lineup for four of the last six league games. Now, it brings questions over his future as River Plate lurk.

As highlighted, West Ham are scoring plenty of goals. However, they’re not coming from the strikers.

Of the natural no. 9’s on the books, Joel Piroe is the top scorer on four. Pablo earns praise for his physicality and work rate but two goals in 28 career games for WHUFC shows he leaves a lot to be desired in front of goal.

By selling Castellanos, the Hammers can raise funds and free up space in the squad for a new talisman to join. They can ease the burden on Piroe while Pablo can mainly be used in the impact substitute role he tends to be better in.

It could breathe new life into Scott Parker’s attack with clear threats out wide and in midfield like Jarrod Bowen, Edvin Austbo, Manor Solomon, Divine Mukasa and Mohamadou Kante.
