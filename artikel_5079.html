The majority of the 15% price rebound from the low occurred on Saturday. For the majority of the last 24-36 hours, Zcash has traded between $780 and $800.

Coinalyze data showed that the Open Interest has slowly dwindled from $900 million to $861 million over the weekend. The Funding Rates had briefly dropped into negative territory on the 22nd of November, but had closed in on +0.01% since then.

Led by a Bitcoin [BTC] bounce back above $60k, Zcash and the rest of the altcoin market saw some gains from the deep correction they endured last week.

Can these gains last, or are they just a brief respite within a broader bearish trend?

The price correction in recent months was part of a higher timeframe downtrend. At the time of writing, ZEC was trading just above a key demand zone from $516-$752, derived from the weekly price chart.

However, sustained wider market bearishness and increased BTC sell pressure could drag ZEC lower.

The 4-hour chart showed a bearish price trend in progress. The recent bounce toward $800 could continue as high as $910, the 78.6% Fibonacci retracement level.

The RSI was below neutral 50, and the OBV was in a downtrend. Both technical indicators highlighted prevalent bearish conditions despite the recent short-term bounce.

Swing traders can maintain a bearish bias, unless the price manages to climb back above $910.
