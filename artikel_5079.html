Shiba Inu (SHIB) Community Builds New Shibarium Explorer While Shytoshi Kusama Stays Silent

Shiba Inu (SHIB) community members have launched a new block explorer for Shibarium, the layer-2 network of the SHIB ecosystem, and Shiba Inu lead Shytoshi Kusama has remained silent on X since May.

Shiba Inu community veteran Mazrael announced the new explorer, scan.shibfun.app, in an X post. "Shibarium has a new block explorer," he wrote. According to Mazrael, the explorer was "built by the community, powered by ShibWatch, dressed in the Eternity Hub look."

The launch comes as SHIB trades near its lowest levels in years. In November, the price of SHIB fell as low as $0.00000337, its lowest level since 2021, and on Nov. 22, the token traded at around $0.00000362, according to CoinMarketCap.

According to the post, users can type a .shib name in the search bar and land on the wallet linked to it, and the explorer also shows new Shiboshis from Shibarium every day. For developers, Mazrael listed contract verification, read and write functions for contracts, the activity of ERC-4337 smart wallets, and a free API, which he described as "Etherscan-style too." Method names are decoded as well, and in Mazrael's words, there are "no more mystery 0x codes." The explorer also has a DApps page with Shibarium games and apps, and a button that adds Shibarium to a wallet in one click.

The work on the explorer is not finished yet. "Live blocks are complete now, and older history indexing," Mazrael wrote. On Tuesday, the explorer itself showed that 3% of Shibarium blocks had been indexed, and a notice on the site said that some of the counts may be inaccurate. Mazrael added that the new explorer can be found on Shib.io under ShibWatch.

Shytoshi Kusama's last post or interaction on X dates back to May 13, 2026. In September, Kusama changed his X bio to "Polish" and his location to "Close," and he has not explained the meaning of either word. The official Shibarium account on X has not posted since July 13, 2026.

Still, Shibarium updates kept coming from community members. In October, Mazrael said that CreateX, a standard factory for deploying contracts at the same address on different chains, went live on Shibarium. According to Mazrael, all 12 Safe v1.4.1 contracts were deployed to their canonical addresses through the Safe Singleton Factory, and x402 went live too, so AI agents can now pay in SHIB, TREAT and wrapped BONE.

In September 2025, attackers exploited the Shibarium bridge and withdrew about $2.4 million worth of assets from it.

According to Shibariumscan, Shibarium processed 546 transactions in a single day in late November, and on Oct. 31, the daily number of transactions on the network was 6,370. Since its launch in August 2023, Shibarium has processed more than 1.5 billion transactions in total.

At the same time, the number of SHIB holders on Ethereum passed 1.7 million on Nov. 17, according to Etherscan.
