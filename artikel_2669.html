NBA plans to invest 10bn euros in European arenas

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The NBA has stepped up its plans for a European league by announcing a strategy to invest at least 10bn euros in the development and renovation of basketball arenas across the continent.

The league has assembled a group of investment firms for the project, which would also look to improve the sports and entertainment districts around the arenas.

The initiative forms part of the NBA and the International Basketball Federation's (Fiba) plan to launch a new, professional basketball system in Europe in 2027.

It will provide prospective clubs and ownership groups with access to capital and the NBA's expertise in regard to developing modern arenas and their surroundings.

"We've seen first-hand how state-of-the-art arenas and the related developments around them can transform basketball teams and their surrounding communities," said NBA commissioner Adam Silver.

"As we prepare to launch a new league in Europe alongside Fiba, we see a significant opportunity to help clubs develop facilities which both build on the unique traditions that define European basketball while also elevating the fan experience and attracting many more live events."

The NBA's announcement stated that the plans for the areas surrounding arenas could include housing, workplaces, restaurants, retail and public spaces, ensuring they would be year-round destinations.

In the statement, the investors said: "We're excited to work with the NBA to support a transformative phase of infrastructure development across Europe.

"Together, we have the potential to invest billions of euros to create world-class venues in European cities, honouring the passion and traditions of European basketball fans and supporting lasting economic growth in the communities where the game means so much."

The announcement cited a study by The Sports Consultancy, which claimed the NBA and Fiba's league could contribute more than 24bn euros in gross value added to GDP across Europe.

It also claimed the league could support nearly 15,000 full-time equivalent jobs annually and attract nearly 70 million spectators over its first 10 years.
