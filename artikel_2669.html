Eagles claim OL Ajani Cornelius off waivers

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Eagles have added an offensive lineman.

Per the league’s daily transaction wire, Philadelphia has added offensive tackle Ajani Cornelius off of waivers.

The Cowboys let go of Cornelius earlier this week.

A sixth-round pick in 2025, Cornelius has appeared in four games over the last two seasons. He played in three this season, recording 14 special teams snaps.

Coming off a Week 10 bye, the 2-7 Eagles will face the Steelers on Sunday.
