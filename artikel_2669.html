Now that Thanksgiving is over, the NBA has prepared a feast for your eyes.

At 6:30 p.m. PT, Luka Dončić's Los Angeles Lakers swoop into Victor Wembanyama's San Antonio Spurs home for both clubs' fourth and final NBA Cup Group Stage game of the 2026 season.

Because their West Group C division is so stacked — the Lakers, Spurs, Warriors and Blazers all have 2-1 records — the winner of tonight's is guaranteed to advance while the loser will be eliminated.

Oddsmakers give San Antonio a 6.5 edge coming into the game tonight.

However, they'll be given a boost tonight when star guard Austin Reaves returns from a heel injury that's sidelined him for the last five games, dating back to Nov. 11.

"It felt good to play basketball again," Reaves said on Wednesday in his first public comments since the injury, according to The Post. "Wind was a little bad, but kind of to be expected being out two weeks. But felt good."

In Reaves' absence, Los Angeles has gone 4-1 and Dončić has averaged 32.2 points per game.

Keep an eye out. Winner advances, loser goes home. There's no better drama in basketball.
