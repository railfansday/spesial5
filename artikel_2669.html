Which USMNT players should Mauricio Pochettino bring with him to Manchester United?

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Mauricio Pochettino hasn't even taken his first training session as Manchester United's new manager, but transfer targets are already being lined up for January and beyond.

And some of them are players he's worked with while in charge of the USMNT over the last two years.

Weston McKennie is one name already being mentioned as a possible buy for Man United, and the Juventus midfielder didn't exactly bat away the speculation.

MORE — Was Pochettino's time in charge of the USMNT a success?

Speaking to the media about the possibility of himself, and other USMNT players, being linked with a move to Old Trafford, here's what McKennie had to say.

"I think that, obviously, the time Pochettino spent here allowed him to appreciate this squad and see the quality it has to offer," McKennie said. "And yes, I think it's a possibility. We have a lot of guys on this team who will probably make a move soon or within the next year. So I think the links are somewhat inevitable. At the end of the day, though, you never know what's going through a manager's head or what kind of players he's looking for. Still, I think you could see a few of us linked with United, and maybe it will actually happen."

Given that most of the USMNT squad Pochettino built is young, and American players are generally commanding lower transfer fees compared to others, there are certainly some deals out there for United to make and it would make sense for them to make use of Pochettino's connection to the USMNT.

And contrary to previous years (and perhaps due to certain financial limitations) United's current hierarchy seem to be open to signing players who have a higher ceiling after some time developing rather than spending big money on the finished products.

There are four USMNT players who make sense right away for Man United to buy, but financially all of these players would be more difficult to sign:

Folarin Balogun - So close to signing for Everton in the summer, Monaco know Balogun will leave soon and his deal is up in 2028 which could see United get a bit of a deal. United really need a poacher up top and Balogun would bring something totally different to Sesko. He dazzled with his finishing and making runs in-behind for Poch.

Chris Richards - This is the next logical step for Richards. He's excelled at Crystal Palace and is an aggressive center back who loves to step out of the back on the ball. Still just 26 years old, Richards would start right away for United.

Antonee Robinson - Left back is obviously a huge problem area for Man United and Robinson, 29, has been one of the most consistent left backs for many years in the Premier League. He's out of contract at Fulham in 2028 so could be available for a much lower fee this summer.

Tyler Adams - United have struggled to replace Casemiro and even after signing Carlos Baleba and Andrey Santos, it's pretty clear they need more help in defensive midfield. Adams was recently named Pochettino's USMNT captain and his intensity is perfect for the way the new United boss wants to play. Adams is another who sees his current contract run out in 2028.

Then there are a couple of USMNT players Pochettino could probably persuade United to buy in the next few transfer windows without as big of a financial outlay:

Alex Freeman - After an incredible summer at the World Cup, his meteoric rise continues at Villarreal. Still just 22, Freeman is versatile at right back or center back and is exactly the kind of defender Pochettino loves. Reliable, strong and intense.

Sebastian Berhalter - He's now a nailed on starter for the USMNT and has been wonderful in his first few months at England in Middlesbrough. Wouldn't break the bank and would be a solid squad player at United and can fill a variety of roles in midfield and attack. His arrival would raise eyebrows, but it shouldn't.

Sergino Dest - Full backs are a problem for United and Dest became a favorite of Pochettino's at right wing-back. From an attacking sense he is superb and his deal at PSV is up soon. Once again, a bit of a bargain and a great squad player.

And then there are a trio of players who would be great long-term signings for United, and we know Pochettino is great at developing youngsters:

Cavan Sullivan - Obviously there is a deal lined up for him to head to Manchester City soon. But what about a late switch to the other side of Manchester? That would make him an instant United hero... The 17-year-old has been a sensation in recent months and Pochettino loves a young playmaker to build his projects around.

Julian Hall - The teenage forward has grabbed the opportunity Poch gave him with both hands and at 18 years of age he looks set to battle it out with Balogun to be the USMNT's starting center forward. May stay in the Red Bull system, but looks certain to be a star.

Justin Ellis - Another teenage forward, Ellis also grabbed his opportunity in recent months and the 19-year-old is destined for a move to Europe. A proper center forward who is raw and full of potential. Like Hall and Sullivan, all three have the potential to be the face of the USMNT together for the next 10-15 years.
