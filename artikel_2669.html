Chiefs-Cardinals Week 11 Wednesday injury report: 3 Chiefs starters miss practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Each practice day this season, the Kansas City Chiefs release an official injury report ahead of the next game. In Week 11, the Chiefs play the Arizona Cardinals on Sunday afternoon.

Here is the Chiefs' first official injury report of the week, courtesy of the team's official website:

Starting right tackle Jaylon Moore (ankle) did not participate in practice and hasn't since leaving the Week 9 game against the New York Jets in the third quarter with an ankle injury. During his Wednesday press conference, head coach Andy Reid said Moore "is making progress, but still sore."

Right guard Trey Smith (concussion) did not participate in practice after entering concussion protocol during the first quarter of last week's game against the Atlanta Falcons.

Running back Kenneth Walker (groin) did not participate in practice again after missing Sunday's game and last Friday's practice. Head coach Andy Reid will be cautious with Walker's availability this week.

Rookie defensive tackle Peter Woods (neck) was a full participant in practice for the first time since exiting the Week 8 game against the Denver Broncos with a neck injury.

Second-year defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott (knee) was a limited participant in practice again. He was activated from the Reserve/Physically-Unable-to-Perform list on Wednesday.

Tight end Jared Wiley is not listed after reportedly fracturing his ankle in the win in Atlanta. Wiley was placed on the Reserve/Injured list to make room for Norman-Lott on the active roster. Wiley's season is likely over.

Linebacker Nick Bolton (shin) was surprisingly a limited participant in practice.

Check back in later this evening for Arizona's official injury report.
