FAMU football duo gets SWAC honors after Mississippi Valley State win

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Florida A&M got a clutch win over Mississippi Valley State last Saturday, Nov. 14.

And the Rattlers' standout performers were honored in the Southwestern Athletic Conference's weekly honors on Monday.

FAMU kicker Gabriel Perez won SWAC Specialist of the Week after going perfect 3-for-3 in field goals and making the game-winning field goal for the Rattlers to win 16-13. The redshirt sophomore out of Miami's longest field goal was from 46 yards, and his go-ahead field goal from 30 yards came with 11 minutes left.

He led the Rattlers with 10 total points, including making the extra point on the team's lone touchdown of the night. Perez is 5-for-5 on field goals since taking over as the starter during FAMU's Oct. 24 homecoming game.

Junior linebacker Denzel Johnson was named SWAC Defensive Player of the Week for the second time this year.

He led the Rattlers with 16 total tackles, with nine of them being solo and three being tackles for loss. The Fort Lauderdale native also had a pass breakup, a forced fumble, and a fumble recovery apiece. It was key as FAMU turned in its best performance of the year in its second showing under new playcaller Christopher Cash.

Johnson has 90 tackles on the season, pacing the Rattlers.

FAMU (3-8, 2-5 in SWAC) next travels to Orlando for the Florida Classic against longtime rivals Bethune-Cookman (5-6, 3-4 in SWAC). Kickoff is at 3:30 p.m. on ESPN+ and on local Tallahassee radio station 96.1 JAMZ.
