Golden Nuggets: Ground stop? Sounds like the 49ers run defense

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

49ers-Cowboys preview: Christian McCaffrey and the Niners should run wild"When you watch them, the issues stem from getting moved at the point of attack. Ball carriers aren't getting touched until they cross the line of scrimmage. The eye test matches the stats: Dallas allows the second-most yards before contact per carry this season, at 1.78 yards. They get moved, easily, despite having played only two offenses in the top-15 in rushing success rate.

The 49ers are running more and more inside the tackles. They're running inside zone at a 19.1% clip, a slight increase from a year ago. This game sets up well for the Niners to keep attacking the Cowboys' A and B gaps.

Kyle Shanahan had to be grinning as he watched the 36-play reel of the Cowboys' defense defending —a term used loosely—inside zone. They allowed 8.08 yards per carry. Seven of those 36 plays went for double digits. They allowed a 58.3% success rate.

You could build the case that the 49ers aren't running inside enough this season. Christian McCaffrey had two 11-yard runs on inside zone last week. McCaffrey has 24 inside zone carries this year and averages 5.04 yards per carry, with a 62.5% success rate….Rookie Caleb Downs is the player the Niners could find success against. He's primarily played nickel. Against the Texans, Packers, and Cardinals, Downs allowed all 9 of his targets to be completed for 147 yards.

Heading into Week 9, Downs had allowed a 90.5% completion percentage, which was the third-highest among slot cornerbacks this season. He has one pass breakup. Dallas tried to align him closer to the line of scrimmage, almost like a pseudo-linebacker in coverage against Arizona. He played linebacker 32.1% of the time against the Cardinals.…Downs is not a big player. He's 5'11", 206 pounds, but has a 10th percentile arm length. That shows up in contested situations when he can't make plays on the ball."

If it gets picked, I'm gonna come out and do it again because they trust me.' And that's the only way we win is by throwing these balls on time, throwing to these spots, and trusting it.' …
