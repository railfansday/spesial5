Parker Washington out on Sunday with a shoulder injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Jaguars wide receiver Parker Washington was involved in one of the biggest plays of last Sunday’s loss to the Giants, but that won’t be the case against the Titans this Sunday.

Head coach Liam Coen told reporters on Friday that Washington has been ruled out after injuring his shoulder in practice on Thursday. Coen said Washington will be viewed as week-to-week moving forward.

Officials ruled Washington did not catch a pass in the end zone early in the fourth quarter last weekend and the call stood after a review of replays that many regarded as confirmation that Washington should have been granted a touchdown.

Washington had eight catches for 103 yards in that game and has 44 catches for 748 yards and six touchdowns on the season.
