Chase Daniel opens up about Justin Herbert’s mindset amid winless season

The Los Angeles Chargers are the only team remaining in the league who have not a won a game, and there definitely has to be some type of frustration surrounding the organization. The most disappointing part of the team has been the offense, as Justin Hebert hasn't found the success he needs to lead the team to victories.

Many are wondering what the future of Herbert could be with the team, but his former teammate, Chase Daniel, doesn't think he's the type of person who will run away from the problems.

"He is not one to, like, stand on the table. 'We gotta have this. We gotta do this.' He's pretty much go with the flow," Daniel said on the Up & Adams Show. "He's a really, really nice dude. He's definitely got some quirks about him, which is like inside jokes between us, which I think is really funny, but he's definitely not like one of those guys that you see that is gonna leak something.

"He's not going to have his agent leak something to [Adam] Shefter or Ian Rapoport or someone like that. That's just not him. He's like a nitty-gritty, football-loving dude. And so I know that no one wants to get this more right than he does."

There's no doubt that Herbert has the talent to lead the Chargers to some wins, but it's going to take the entire team in order to do so. Neither side of the ball has played well, but with a matchup against the Houston Texans coming up, they may be able to find a way to get their first victory.
