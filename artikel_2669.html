J.J. McCarthy: Offense took late Jags FG personally, we can't give them that confidence

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

One of the biggest moments in Sunday’s game between the Giants and Jaguars came with just over three minutes to play when Jaguars head coach Liam Coen elected to kick a field goal rather than go for it from New York’s 9-yard-line.

The kick cut the Giants’ lead to five points and Coen said after the game that going for it would have been a “hope play” that presented more risk than giving the ball back to the Giants. That reasoning was built around the Giants going without a first down on their previous four possessions, but the Giants got two to close out the game after the ensuing kickoff.

After the 23-18 win was in the books, Giants quarterback J.J. McCarthy acknowledged that the Giants offense took that both as a shot at them and as a reminder that the unit needs to do more than they did in the second half on Sunday.

“As an offense, we took that personal for sure,” McCarthy said, via a transcript from the team. “But the prior drives, obviously that was something that they felt comfortable with doing. We can’t get into that position. We can’t give them that confidence to go out there and kick the field goal. The next time we get in those situations where we’re up, we have to finish the job and find every way we can this week to make sure we do that the next time.”

The win was McCarthy’s third in four starts for the Giants and that record makes it easier to swallow any of the rough patches that the offense has hit over the course of those games. They’ll try for a smoother effort across the board in Indianapolis next Sunday.
