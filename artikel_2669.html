Brazil have scored 2,700 goals in their history

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Brazilian national team reaches 2,700 goals in its historyCredits: Jasmin Walter - FIFA/FIFA via Getty Images

Brazil's senior men's national team has found the net exactly 2,700 times in its century-long history. Since its inception in 1914, the Seleção has played 1,183 matches, averaging 2.28 goals per game. Its record stands at 766 wins, 241 draws and 175 defeats, with 1,087 goals conceded.

The goal that took Brazil to this milestone was scored by forward Samuel Lino in the 4-0 victory over India in Kolkata during the most recent FIFA international window.

Pelé was the biggest contributor to this tally of 2,700 goals. The King of Football scored 95 goals and is the national team's all-time leading scorer. Next is Neymar, who has scored 80 goals and is Brazil's top scorer in matches against other national teams. They are followed by Ronaldo (67 goals), Zico (66 goals) and Romário (56 goals).

The first goal in the team's history was scored by Oswaldo Gomes on Brazil's debut, on July 21, 1914, in a 2-0 friendly win over English club Exeter City at Laranjeiras in Rio de Janeiro.

Looking to keep adding to its goal tally, Brazil will play two friendlies during this November FIFA international window, the last of 2026. This Saturday (14), the team faces Japan at 7:15 a.m. (Brasília time), before taking on Singapore on Tuesday (17) at 7 a.m. (Brasília time). Both friendlies will be held at Singapore's National Stadium.
