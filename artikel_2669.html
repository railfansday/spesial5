UL-Monroe football vs. Louisiana | Live scores, prediction and more

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

MONROE — Following two exciting road victories, Louisiana-Monroe football seeks its first home win of the season Thursday against Louisiana-Lafayette.

The Warhawks (2-7, 2-3 Sun Belt Conference) lost their first six games of the year but snapped a 14-game skid on Oct. 24 with a come-from-behind victory at Troy. While ULM fell short against Southern Miss on Halloween, the Warhawks defeated Arkansas State 19-17 last weekend, marking their first win in Jonesboro since 2001.
