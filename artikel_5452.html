Weichert Real Estate Affiliates, Inc. Names First Dual Recipients for 2026 Buzzy Award

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Weichert Real Estate Affiliates, Inc., has named its first ever dual recipients for the company’s prestigious Buzzy Award, recognizing outstanding achievement in marketing and brand promotion, during its annual Leadership Retreat in Nashville.

Aaron Duncan of Weichert, Realtors® – Real Estate Professionals and William Webb of Weichert, Realtors® – Webb & Associates were honored as the dual recipients of the award, celebrating the exceptional marketing efforts and brand visibility achieved through the work of their shared marketing director, Elizabeth Wiggins.

“We are honored to celebrate the achievements of these two brokerages. They exemplify what it means to resonate with a community and demonstrate the values that have long defined our organization,” said Bill Scavone, president of Weichert Real Estate Affiliates, Inc. “Through their dedication, they have connected with their clients and colleagues through social media and public outreach leaving a lasting impression within their offices and communities.”

According to a release, the Buzzy Award is one of the network’s highest honors for marketing excellence and recognizes one office each year with a national distinction among all Weichert affiliated offices across the country for demonstrating creativity, consistency and impact in promoting their businesses and the Weichert brand in their local community through public relations, social media and community engagement.

Through innovative campaigns, strong community engagement and a commitment to brand standards, Duncan, Webb and Wiggins have helped strengthen awareness of their respective offices while creating meaningful connections with consumers in their markets, the company stated.
