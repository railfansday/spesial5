Talogy links leadership assessment to development, offering AI simulations for the first time

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

DUBAI, UAE, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- In response to rising demand for agile, people-centric leaders in an increasingly volatile, tech-driven landscape, global talent management provider, Talogy, has updated its InView Leadership solution.

Based on decades of experience in leadership assessments, the comprehensive, evidence-based framework evaluates not just what leaders do, but how leaders lead, and why they lead.

As organisations prepare for the future, their leaders need to balance the competing demands of driving transformation and creating stability. With traditional assessments often focusing on what a leader should do, InView Leadership takes a transformational approach, providing a modern definition of effective leadership.

Designed for a constantly evolving workplace, the InView methodology underpins psychometrics, assessment centres, development programmes and coaching to form an integrated leadership ecosystem.

Explaining this further, Dan Hughes, VP of Product Development at Talogy, said, "This scientifically grounded framework establishes a common language across leadership assessment, development and strategy. InView's multi-faceted approach enables organisations to consistently assess potential, accelerate development and equip empathetic, future-ready leaders at every level."

The enhanced InView solution draws on Talogy's 25 years of leadership assessment and development experience in the Middle East, a milestone the company celebrates this month. Sharan Gohel, Senior Director of Consulting for the region, acknowledged the milestone, saying, "This achievement is significant as we are proud to continue bringing world-class talent solutions to the Gulf region. The advancements of the InView solution demonstrate Talogy's ongoing commitment to combining innovative technological developments with deep talent expertise to meet the evolving needs of our clients."

The updated solution also introduces an immersive, innovative way to explore capability for the first time using realistic, AI-powered simulations.

Marking the first jointly developed exercise since Talogy's partnership with Symulate, the offering combines conversational AI, voice and interactive personas to bring immersive leadership scenarios to life for real-world development.

This new capability allows organizations to deepen insight into leadership qualities, understand areas of strength to leverage and uncover ongoing development opportunities.

Commenting on the solution, Alan Bourne, CEO and Founder at Symulate said, "By fusing our conversational AI and interactive personas with the robust InView framework, Talogy is able to deliver highly realistic scenarios. This gives leaders a safe, immersive space to hone their skills, while providing organisations with actionable insights into their true development opportunities."
