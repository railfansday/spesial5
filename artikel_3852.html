The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake – All voice actors and full cast list

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

A new yet old adventure needs new voices.

An all-time classic adventure has been reborn anew for the Nintendo Switch 2.

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is a full-fledged remake of the Nintendo 64 classic, bringing about a full suite of quality-of-life changes like better controls, graphics, and audio. And with that audio comes the addition of voice acting in certain cutscenes from specific characters.

Below we’ve listed the main English voice actors that can be found listed in the game’s credits and where you may know them from so you can be equipped to say “oh, I knew that was them” when playing Ocarina of Time on Switch 2… except for our “silent” protagonist, Link, who does not have an actor listed for his grunts and exclamations.
