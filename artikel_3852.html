How to get Lil’ Uzi Vert Jr. Sprite in Fortnite and how it works

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Week 2 of Fortnite Treasure Hunters has added the Lil’ Uzi Vert Sprite to the existing collection, and we can add it to our collection. To do so, however, we have to first collect this Sprite on our runs, and then extract it safely using an Extractor.

Unlike most Sprites in the current season, the Lil’ Uzi Vert Jr. Sprite is a guaranteed drop. However, you’ll have to raid Lil’ Uzi Vert Jr.’s stronghold and outlast the boss battle.

Thankfully, he is one of the easiest bosses to take down. His weapon might do a lot of damage, but not so much if you stay in the shelter and shoot from a distance. Just go for headshots, and you’ll be good to go.

Once you defeat him, you’ll get one of the three possible variants of the Lil’ Uzi Vert Jr. Sprite. The base version is more common, and that’s the one you’ll mostly receive. However, you can also end up finding the Cursed or the Fortune variants if you’re lucky enough. The special versions have the same effect as the base version, but they offer an extra modifier.

The Lil’ Uzi Vert Jr. Sprite helps to restore shields of players by triggering a splash effect. This will heal you and your allies. While the move has a cooldown, the time interval can come down once you level up the Sprite. This makes the Sprite one of the best options to consider for team play.
