Bayern sporting director rules out big January spending

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Bayern Munich sporting director Christoph Freund insisted they are not planning "anything big" in the January transfer window, despite their recent injury crisis.

Vincent Kompany's side were dealt two major blows in their Bundesliga draw with Koln last week after both Harry Kane and Michael Olise went down with injuries.

While Olise's knock is not deemed to be serious, Kane is set to miss at least three months of action after having surgery on his ankle.

Both players missed Bayern's Champions League defeat to Lille on Wednesday, with Serge Gnabry, Ismael Saibari, Alphonso Davies and Manuel Neuer also sidelined for the loss.

Luis Diaz did make his return after a month out, but Bayern's new-look attack stuttered against Lille.

Konrad Laimer started as a makeshift striker, with Lennart Karl, Aleksandar Pavlovic and Nathaniel Brown featuring behind the Austrian.

Nineteen-year-olds Noah Codjo-Evora and Tim Binder came off the bench, but the Bundesliga champions were only able to accumulate 0.95 expected goals (xG) from their 12 shots.

Despite their struggles in attack, Freund is confident their injured stars will come back stronger, rendering any major additions in January unnecessary.

"It is obviously bitter at the moment, we don't need to beat around the bush, because it affects many players in a similar position in the offensive," Freund told DAZN.

"The players will hopefully all come back well, some faster and others a bit later. That's why it's not our plan to do anything big.

"We hope that in the next few weeks we will already have a slightly different personnel situation."

Bayern are back in Bundesliga action on Saturday against Hamburg, before turning their attention to the DFB-Pokal when they host Hertha Berlin on December 1.
