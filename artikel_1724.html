Chelsea stance on making shock Xabi Alonso sack decision

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Xabi Alonso could be forgiven for thinking his Chelsea reign is already heading the same way as his recent predecessors following Saturday’s 4-1 drubbing at the hands of Leeds United.

The Spaniard’s appointment in the summer was seen as something of a coup given his exploits at Bayer Leverkusen, allied to the fact Liverpool and Manchester City were both seeking high-profile replacements for their outgoing managers.

A summer of significant investment saw optimism rise at Stamford Bridge and a title challenge was anticipated given the lack of a European football as a distraction.

The honeymoon period is, however, already over for the 44-year-old whose side have lost five times already and are conceding goals, 20 in 11 matches, at an alarming rate.

Despite that unconvincing record, according to Mail Sport, Chelsea will not be making any kneejerk reactions and relieving him of his duties so early in his tenure.

Chelsea have not shied away from making quick decisions on the fates of those they have entrusted with overseeing a rebuilding process in recent years, with both Graham Potter and Liam Rosenior lasting a matter of months at the helm.

Alonso is not yet under the sort of pressure that quickly became too much to handle for Potter and Rosenior, but it is fair to say he is struggling to win over an increasingly frustrated fanbase that voiced its opinion in no uncertain terms on Saturday.

Romeo Lavia, meanwhile, said Chelsea must not give in to panic after they were hammered by one of their bitterest rivals.

The home side were outclassed as Leeds ended a 27-year wait for a Premier League win in west London, with a chorus of boos ringing out at full-time.

It was a third defeat in four league matches for Alonso’s team which left them mired in mid-table and badly struggling for confidence, with the manager’s honeymoon period now resoundingly at an end.

‘Everyone is very angry and disappointed,’ said Lavia.

‘At home this is never the way you want to finish a game. We let them back in the game when we shouldn’t have. This is something we will analyse and get better at.

‘When we won games, when we lost games, we always said that no matter what, it’s a process.

‘With these types of results, it feels like the end of the world, but we are not going to ring the bell now to say it’s a panic moment. Not at all. We are very confident as a team. We know where we want to get to.

‘This hurts. It needs to hurt so it provokes a reaction from the team, and this is definitely going to help us for the next game on Friday.’
