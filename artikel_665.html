Instacart is giving out free turkeys for Thanksgiving — how to claim yours

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

TL;DR: Instacart is giving away up to 50,000 free turkeys this Thanksgiving from Nov. 18 through Nov. 22 when you spend a minimum of $50 on your Thanksgiving basket. A fresh batch of 10,000 turkeys drops every day at 12 p.m. ET.

Grocery shopping the week ahead of Thanksgiving is a nightmare. And then there's the blow to your bank account on top of it.

Instacart is helping on both fronts this season. The grocery delivery app is giving away up to 50,000 free turkeys this Thanksgiving, with up to 10,000 dropping each day between Nov. 18 and Nov. 22. So, you won't have to step foot in the grocery store and a good chunk of your Thanksgiving dinner will be paid for. Win-win.

Here's how it works. From Wednesday, Nov. 18 through Sunday, Nov. 22, customers can score up to $40 off an eligible whole turkey, fresh or frozen, from select retailers on Instacart. That's enough to cover an entire bird in most cases. Just head to the Instacart Thanksgiving landing page and fill up your basket, including your turkey, to reach at least $50 (alcohol is excluded from the minimum spend requirement). Then click to redeem the promo on the Thanksgiving landing page. You're limited to one redemption per person while supplies last. A fresh batch of up to 10,000 turkeys drops every day at 12 p.m. ET, with up to 50,000 total. If you miss out on one day, be sure to return the next day at noon to redeem yours.

If you forget something essential, Instacart is also offering $0 delivery on Thanksgiving grocery orders of $10 or more between Nov. 23 and Nov. 25. The day before Thanksgiving is widely known as the busiest day of the year for grocery stores. Let Instacart battle the crowds for you while you prep your home for guests. Just note that service and other fees may apply.

Before your start shopping Black Friday deals, make sure your Thanksgiving dinner is taken care of.
