Open Mercury Business Checking if your company sends or receives wires, pays contractors abroad, or keeps more than $250,000 in the bank. It charges nothing for U.S.-dollar wires sent or received, and FDIC insurance through its partner banks reaches $5 million. Skip it if your business takes in cash, since Mercury doesn’t accept cash deposits.

Mercury, a financial technology company rather than a bank, runs this online account through partner banks Choice Financial Group and Column N.A., for U.S.-registered companies from startups to small firms with staff. It has no branches, and those banks’ branches won’t take cash for a Mercury account, so the free wires come at the cost of in-person banking.

For a company that wires money or holds more than $250,000, the free plan is the one to open, since Plus and Pro leave every banking fee the same. A business that takes in cash should open the nbkc Business Account, which charges nothing to deposit it, and one that wants its checking to earn interest should compare Bluevine Business Checking.
