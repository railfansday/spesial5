Watch: Team Cherry's 'Cherry Time!' Special Presentation - Live!

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ever one for a surprise, Team Cherry revealed earlier this week that it has a special presentation in store. Well, guess what? 'Cherry Time' is right around the corner.

The presentation is set to kick off in just a few minutes at 3pm GMT / 7am PT / 10am ET, with Team Cherry promising "a bunch of exciting announcements, plus an update on Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow".

We are hoping that means a firm release date for the upcoming DLC, which has been sitting on a vague '2026' window since it was revealed last year. Dare we hope for a shadow drop? Maybe!

As for what those other "exciting announcements" are, your guess is as good as ours.

Fortunately, we don't have all that long to wait. The presentation is being streamed on the Team Cherry YouTube channel, but you can also watch along with us via the link below and react to all of the news as it happens.
