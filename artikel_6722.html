Here’s When Every Episode of ‘All’s Fair’ Season 2 Drops

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Ryan Murphy shows no signs of stopping this year as another show of his returns for another season. This time, it’s his campy and wild legal drama, All’s Fair, which will no doubt turn some heads yet again as it returns for season 2. And with how everything went down in season 1, there’s no doubt that this new season—which stars divas like Kim Kardashian, Naomi Watts, Niecy Nash-Betts, Teyana Taylor, Sarah Paulson, and Glenn Close—will up the stakes yet again. Basically, you’re going to want to tune in so you don’t miss out!

Here’s everything you need to know about All’s Fair season 2's release schedule.

All’s Fair season 2 was officially released on November 4, 2026 exclusively on Hulu.

Just when you thought the show’s three episode premiere was the only surprise of the season, the show’s schedule takes a bit of a turn. New episodes will be released every Wednesday, with November 25th’s drop featuring two episodes. Consider it an early Thanksgiving treat!

All’s Fair season 2 will have eight episodes that will stream exclusively on Hulu every Wednesday. After the show’s three episode premiere, one new episode will be released weekly with an exception of November 25th, which will have two new episodes released.
