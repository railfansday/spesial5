The Latest Look at Taylor Swift’s Off-Duty Style Comes With a New Detail About Her Wedding

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Taylor Swift has flown under the radar for most of the fall, only making public appearances at her husband Travis Kelce’s home games and a few industry events. Yesterday, however, fans got a new look at Swift’s casual, off-duty style in an unexpected place: the Hancock County Humane Foundation’s Instagram. The animal welfare nonprofit shared a photo of Swift and Kelce with Kelce’s longtime agent, Mike Simon, and his wife Lisa Marie, who attended their wedding. The post revealed a new detail about the newlyweds’ request to guests in lieu of giving them gifts.

“Four beautiful people, four generous hearts,” the West Virginia-based organization wrote. “The Hancock County Humane Foundation is honored to acknowledge a very generous donation from Attorney Mike Simon and his wife Lisa in honor of a very special wedding they recently attended. The amazing bride and groom asked for donations to charities in lieu of gifts and Mike and Lisa chose us!!”

Swift appeared to be wearing the red Tommy Hilfiger cable-knit polo she wore during Kelce’s game on Sunday with baggy, light-wash jeans.

The post came hours after The Hollywood Reporter revealed Swift would be receiving the Sherry Lansing Leadership Award at its Women in Entertainment Gala. Greta Gerwig will present the honor to her, which “recognizes trailblazers and philanthropists in entertainment and media and was established in honor of Sherry Lansing, the first woman to head a Hollywood studio,” the outlet wrote.

“Taylor Swift took on an industry that has long underestimated women and won on every front: artistically, commercially, and on the question of who owns her work,” the publication’s editor-in-chief, Maer Roshan, said. “She reclaimed her catalog, set new terms for the artists who follow her, and became the most popular musician in the world while doing it—and helping scores of other artists along the way. We’re proud to honor her with the Sherry Lansing Leadership Award.”
