Are Aneha and Cam Back Together After Ending Things on ‘Love Is Blind’? Where They Stand Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Aneha and Cam had a lot of issues throughout their Love Is Blind journey, the biggest being that her parents had no interest in (a) meeting Cam or (b) attending their wedding. But on top of that hurdle, the couple weren’t exactly communicating their feelings well, and had very different ideas about where to live and how to manage their work/life balance. So it didn’t exactly come as a shock when Aneha turned Cam down at the altar, saying “I still believe you’re my person but I do not [say yes] today. I need more time.”

Despite saying “I don’t,” Aneha seemed very open to dating Cam and working on their relationship, even telling producers “I can’t imagine my life without him.” Meanwhile, the pair had a whole convo about taking time to process where they stand and their continued love for each other, which seemed promising. But…did they stay together?

Not so much. Love Is Blind was allegedly filmed over a year ago, and according to the reunion (which was filmed recently), they are not together. Aneha revealed they’d planned a hotel retreat for after their wedding, and that during their time away Cam told her he didn’t love her and wanted to end the relationship.

Cam also revealed he was planning to say no to Aneha on their wedding day; she just happened to go first. So, guess that’s that on that! But in better news, they seem to be getting along fine as friends?
