It’s not just a look for finance bros; it’s the everyman’s winter layer.

It took me until I turned 30 to realize I was a vest guy. Turns out a fleece vest was the key to non-bulky winter layering. It’s what I was needing this whole time, a cornerstone piece. It gets me through the crisp fall mornings and it’s easy to shed for my afternoon walk in the sunshine. Now, I can’t get through a winter without adding another to my collection.

Versatility is the name of the game here. You can dress up any fleece vest with a button-up and breathable chinos, or keep it casual with, say, a long-sleeved tee and some jeans. And really, it’s the only thing that can fit between a bulky sweater and a winter jacket.

There are tons of fleece vests on the market, so which one do you choose? Well, you don’t have to choose just one for starters. I like to have a couple around in different colors and weights, depending on the weather and my outfit choice. So regardless of if you’re on your first one, or it’s time for your fiftieth, you can shop these nine handpicked fleece vests that I and our other editors swear by.
