After your chair, a sitting to standing desk that's easy to raise and lower is the best investment you can make in your home office setup. You can improve your sitting posture, but at the end of the day, it's good to spend less time sitting altogether to avoid back pain and the other woes that come with long hours of sedentary work.

Often combined with a standing desk, saddle stools are becoming popular among people who want to make their sitting more active. They're not for everyone and they take some getting used to, but the idea is that you use them more as a perch than a chair, taking the weight off your legs when needed but avoiding long hours of hunched sitting.
