20 Cozy Cashmere Loungewear Sets You Won’t Want to Take Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

We hate to be the bearers of bad news, but the season of 4 p.m. sunsets and chilled temperatures is upon us. There is a silver lining to this: Staying in to read one of the year’s best new books or stream an equally anticipated film, preferably with a pot of something comforting simmering in the background, is the ultimate cozy activity. (And when the cold gets to be too much; well, time to re-brand a warm-weather escape as self-care.) Whether lounging on the sofa or jet-setting to the nearest beach, you'll need some cozy loungewear—and what’s a more luxurious option than a cashmere lounge set?

These matching sets are as close to sleepwear as you can get—although given they’re mostly made of pure cashmere, even the baggiest sweatpants will still look elevated. For plane travel, an ultra-soft hoodie or zip-up from the likes of God’s True Cashmere and Lisa Yang lets you bundle up on the flight. For knitwear that can bridge the gap between errands and work calls, choose something more polished like a cardigan or turtleneck; Extreme Cashmere and Guest in Residence make some of our favorites, with coordinating bottoms to boot. Can’t decide? A simple cashmere crewneck pullover will never steer you wrong.

From cream-colored cashmere sweaters you can dress up with colorful layers to more athletic silhouettes and even sleek short sleeves you can wear out and about, this is the best cashmere loungewear to shop now. (And if you still need ideas for holiday gifting: A loungewear set will always be a welcome present.)
