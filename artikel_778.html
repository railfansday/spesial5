Karol G Postpones Show in Mexico City ‘Due to Health Reasons’: Here’s the New Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The Colombian superstar's concert, part of her Viajando Por El Mundo Tropitour, was originally scheduled for Sunday (Nov. 15).

The third concert that Karol G was set to perform on Sunday (Nov. 15) at the Estadio GNP Seguros in Mexico City, as part of her Viajando Por El Mundo Tropitour, has been postponed to Tuesday (Nov. 17) “due to health reasons of the artist,” Ocesa announced on social media.

“This decision has been made prioritizing her health and well-being,” the promoter said in a statement. “After two nights full of music and special moments in Mexico City, we deeply regret not being able to share tonight with all the fans who were looking forward to joining her. Karol G sincerely thanks everyone for the love and energy they have given her these past days and appreciates their understanding at this time.”

Ocesa informed fans that tickets previously purchased for Sunday’s concert will be valid for the new date, so no changes are necessary. Those who wish to request a refund can do so through Ticketmaster.

The specific health issue of the Colombian superstar was not disclosed. Billboard reached out to Karol G’s representatives on Sunday night but did not receive a response at press time.

On Friday (November 13), the “Bichota” singer performed the first of her three concerts at the iconic stadium in Mexico City, where Thalia made a surprise guest appearance to sing a duet of “Piel Morena,” “Amor a la Mexicana” and “¿A Quién Le Importa?” The performance marked the return of the iconic Mexican singer and actress to the stages of Mexico City after a decade.

During her second show on Saturday night (Nov. 14), Karol invited pop star Belinda onstage to perform tracks such as “Luz Sin Gravedad,” “Ángel” and “Bella Traición” together.

As part of the start of the Latin American leg of her Viajando Por El Mundo Tropitour, the Colombian singer previously visited the northern Mexican city of Monterrey, where she performed on Nov. 6, 7 and 8 at the Estadio BBVA.

This week, she was also in Las Vegas for the Latin Grammys, taking home awards including best reggaeton performance for “Verano Rosa,” with Feid, and best urban music album for Tropicoqueta.
