Taylor Frankie Paul Shares Update on Sex Life After Cheating Scandal

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Taylor Frankie Paul revealed she’s been celibate for nearly one year: “If you’re familiar with how I am, you know it’s the opposite of what I would have normally done.”

Taylor Frankie Paul is spilling secrets about her sex life.

Following The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives star’s bombshell admission that she cheated on her former fiancé Doug Mason with ex Dakota Mortensen, she revealed she’s been celibate for nearly one year.

“The fact that I remained single and celibate during the 10 loneliest months of life has me shocked,” Paul wrote over a video of herself dancing on Instagram Nov. 8, “but progress I guess.”

And it’s not lost on the 32-year-old that her fans might be equally surprised.

“If you’re familiar with how I am, you know it’s the opposite of what I would have normally done. (I hate to admit),” she continued in the post’s caption. “However this year has been filled with many learning lessons and let’s add this one to the list: how to be okay with being alone.”

The update on the reality star’s personal life comes after revealing that she cheated on Mason—who received the final rose on her unaired season of The Bachelorette—with her ex Mortensen during the very first episode of SLOMW season five. (Paul’s season was ultimately canceled after a 2023 domestic violence video between her and Mortensen surfaced.)

“Obviously we're texting,” Paul said of communicating with Mortensen at the time while in conversation with costars Jessi Draper, Mikayla Matthews and Mayci Neeley. “‘When are you coming home? When are you going to be home?’ I don't know how it led to it, but he came over.”

And after her friends teased her about “not knowing how it happened,” Neely filled in the blank of what occurred next: “You sleep with Dakota."

To which Paul—who at this time was still engaged to Mason—affirmed, “Yes.”

But Paul didn’t exactly see her actions as a betrayal, noting, “I don’t like the word cheating.”

But after receiving some heat for the revelation, the Hulu star—mom to two kids with ex-husband Tate Paul as well as a son with Mortensen—shared she’d be protecting her peace for the foreseeable future.

“Sorry to those that thought they were going to see my redemption arc,” she wrote over a photo of herself on Instagram Sept. 12. “That has slowly just begun. I’ve stayed in, single and celibate this entire time and nobody will have intimate access to me again unless he’s my person and that’s a promise to myself.”

She continued, “Such a long way to go but baby steps.”

Keep reading for a look at where every couple on Mormon Wives stands today…

Taylor Frankie Paul & Dakota Mortensen: Broken Up

While Taylor Frankie Paul's romance with Dakota Mortensen—they welcomed son Ever in 2024—bloomed on The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives, they called it quits due to Dakota's infidelity.

"Right after Ever was born, Dakota and I were living together and in bliss with our new baby, and I was even considering engagement," Taylor—who began dating the real estate agent in July 2022—revealed during the season two premiere of the Hulu series. "And then I learned that he was lying this entire time."

Taylor was told by a woman named Jenna that she and Dakota had been intimate and he had "spent the night more than once." (He has denied the claims, writing in a September 2024 TikTok that he "never cheated.")

On the series, Taylor said that Dakota accused Jenna of instigating their affair.

"No, it was him," she emphasized in the confessional. "He said he had just kissed her on the couch. No, he went on the couch, they hooked up on the couch, and then they went to the room and they hooked up."

Since ending her romance with the Basin Tiling founder, the mom of three (she shares two kids with ex-husband Tate Paul) was set to find love once again on season 22 of The Bachelorette, but things got a bit thorny when news broke in March 2026 of an alleged confrontation between the exes weeks earlier.

A spokesperson for the Draper City Police Department confirmed to People that there was an open "domestic assault investigation," telling the outlet "allegations have been made in both directions.”

Days later, a video of a previous 2023 physical altercation between Taylor and Dakota surfaced online, prompting ABC to cancel the MomToker's season of the dating franchise.

As for Dakota, he has kicked off a romance with show guest star Shinia Powell, though he previously apologized to Taylor for hooking up with her during a season four episode.

"I think it's because when we were going through our personal stuff—and I know you haven't seen it—but he struggled through a porn addiction," she explained. "There's a scene where one of my friends says something along the lines of how they could see how he was confused sexually."

Through the ups and downs, the reality star emphasized that she and Connor—who were 23 and 22, respectively, when they got married—are stronger than ever. In fact, they even renewed their vows in February 2026 for their 10th wedding anniversary.
