Khloe Kardashian Shares Insight Into Recent Discovery About Her Sexual

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Khloe Kardashian shared that she strongly identifies with being a demisexual after her friend Simon Huck explained the term to her on her Khloe in Wonder Land podcast.

Khloe Kardashian is keeping up with revelations about herself.

During a conversation with her longtime friend Simon Huck on her podcast Khloe in Wonder Land, he suggested that the Kardashians star could fall into the category of a demisexual.

“A demisexual is a person who can only be romantic and intimate with someone if they’ve first established an emotional relationship,” Huck explained on the Nov. 18 episode. “So, it’s almost impossible to have a one-night stand if you’re a demi. And I believe you are also a demi.”

Kardashian certainly felt like the term accurately described her, musing, “I don’t think I’ve ever had a one-night stand. I would remember. I’m a demi? Wow.”

And as the 42-year-old explained, she just isn't in the right “headspace” to “lean into someone” right now.

“So much has happened and I'm in such a good place,” Kardashian said, “that I'm afraid to ever do something that might ever turn out the way that my past has been.”

Still, she did admit that she missed some aspects about being in a romantic relationship.

“I just want to be kissed sometimes,” she confessed. “But not necessarily, 'I just need to have sex.' Sometimes I just miss a normal nice hug —but from someone you want that hug from.”

The two also discussed open relationships and how being a demisexual could be difficult in one, although for Kardashian there is something to be said for the boundaries that are set up within one.

And although she wasn’t sure if she would be ready for an open relationship, she still appreciated that she could be “involved in the choice making.”

“Just give us rules,” the Good American founder shared. “I don’t appreciate that every guy is like, ‘Oh, I’m committed. This is what we’re doing.’ And then they go and cheat. So many girls would be OK with these rules. What I’m not OK with is the lying. Let me decide.”

Since her split from Tristan Thompson in 2021, the reality star has been candid about her love life, admitting that she has been celibate for years following their breakup.

“I’m mortified that it’s even my truth,” she told Elle in 2025. “I get a little embarrassed. I’m like, ‘This is so pathetic,’ but once you get to a certain place, you think, ‘No, this is so special.’”

Plus, bringing a new man into her life was a difficult decision to navigate as mom to kids True, 8, and Tatum, 4, with Thompson.

“I’ve never dated with children before and that’s such a serious factor to me,” Kardashian explained. “I started going to therapy, and I started trying to figure myself out more, especially in my romantic life.”
