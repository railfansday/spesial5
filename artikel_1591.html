Opinion: Liverpool will sign perfect player for Andoni Iraola, he'll only cost £19m

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Andoni Iraola has done a pretty decent job since being appointed as Liverpool's manager.

He's managed to get his team to fourth in the Premier League table with only seven points separating them from first-placed Arsenal.

Obviously, this is still a decent number of points to chip away at but, if the Gunners continue dropping points by drawing games against teams such as Newcastle, the Reds could find themselves back in the title race soon.

Nobody knows what lies ahead for any team but, if Liverpool manage to pull off even half of the transfers they've been linked with, they'll be storming their way up the table.

And to make things even sweeter, the perfect signing for Iraola has just emerged.

Arhcie Brown has a £19.2 million release clause in his contract

So many areas among Liverpool's ranks still need upgrading.

This is a horrible thing to write as, across the last couple of years, well over £600 million has been spent on new players by the Reds.

Some of the transfers that they've made have worked out for the better but, others haven't quite hit the mark.

Milos Kerkez is an example of this because, even though he has had a fair few decent performances, his inconsistency is terrible.

Some days he can be seen as the Premier League's best left-back and, on others, he could end up being the worst player on the pitch.

This has forced fans to ask for a new left-back with Florian Plettenberg now sharing a rather intriguing update on Liverpool target, Archie Brown.

"Archie Brown has a release clause worth around €23m, valid for both the winter and summer transfer windows. The 23 y/o is a regular starter at Fenerbahçe and has also impressed with some very strong performances in the Champions League," he wrote.

"Under contract until 2028, a move is planned, with Premier League clubs already making enquiries."

Archie Brown would fit straight into Andoni Iraola's plans

Not only would Brown be classed as homegrown but, he'd also be one of the cheapest transfers on the market.

Recent links with Liverpool and Alejandro Balde have emerged with Barcelona demanding close to €50 million for the Spaniard.

Others such as Daniel Svensson have also been linked with a move but, again, Borussia Dortmund want over €40 million for him.

Each of these players would be exceptional additions but, Brown for €23 million (£19 million) is a deal that's too good to pass up.

He put in the performance of a lifetime when Liverpool faced Fenerbahce earlier in the season and, if he can replicate that form in the Premier League, the Reds would be onto a winner.

Liverpool signing Brown would also give Kerkez a genuine challenger for his position which might even help him develop his performance.

Since the price for Brown is so good, Liverpool will have to act fast and get straight on with securing his services when the January transfer window opens up, otherwise, they'll end up kicking themselves for missing out on it.
