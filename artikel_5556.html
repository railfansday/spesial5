PNC Bank Launches Incoming Stablecoin Payments for Corporate and Institutional Banking Clients

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Solution expands PNC's relationship with Coinbase and explores faster, more efficient payment options for corporate clients.

PITTSBURGH, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- PNC Bank today announced it is making incoming USDC payments available to PNC Corporate & Institutional Banking (C&IB) clients. Clients can receive payments in USDC, a U.S. dollar-backed stablecoin, on the Ethereum blockchain, powered by Coinbase Payment products. The solution builds on PNC's existing relationship with Coinbase and the bank's continued investment in its Treasury Management capabilities. This addition complements PNC's established faster payments capabilities and reflects the bank's ongoing focus on responding to evolving client needs with a broad range of immediate payment options.

The launch enables clients to receive USDC payments, which are automatically converted 1-to-1 to U.S. dollars and deposited into a designated PNC deposit account. The solution is designed to help businesses leverage the benefits of stablecoin payments, including faster access to funds and greater efficiency for cross-border transactions, while maintaining access to PNC's banking services.

"Our clients are continually looking at new ways to interact and transact with customers, suppliers and business partners," said Tom Lang, head of Treasury Management Product and Operations at PNC Bank. "This solution gives us an opportunity to support our customers with innovative capabilities, backed by the banking experience, risk management and expertise clients expect from PNC."

"Stablecoins are modernizing how businesses move money by making payments faster, more efficient and more global," said Alec Lovett, head of Infrastructure Products at Coinbase. "Our work with PNC shows how leading banks and trusted digital asset infrastructure come together to let corporate clients take advantage of this technology."

PNC is initially offering the service on a limited basis to eligible clients as part of a pilot program. As the use of stablecoins continues to evolve, PNC is focused on helping clients understand and evaluate emerging payment technologies and how they may fit within their broader treasury strategies.
