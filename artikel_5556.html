Zedd Parts With Hollywood Hills Home in $4 Million Sale—After Nearly a Decade of Ownership

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

DJ and producer Zedd has offloaded his Hollywood Hills pad for $4.25 million to a couple who plan to carry out a complete overhaul of one of the rooms.

The Grammy winner, whose real name is Anton Zaslavski, first listed the property in October 2025 for $5.5 million, before trimming the price to $4.49 million.

He snapped up the home in 2014 for $3.9 million. Now, the property has been sold in an off-market deal, as first reported by Mansion Global.

According to the outlet, the hitmaker sold the abode to a couple who reside in the area. The buyers were represented by Brandon Meneses of the Beverly Hills Estates and Janelle Meneses of The Agency.

“They’ve been looking for a long time and ultimately felt this was the right home for them,” Brandon told Mansion Global.

According to the agent, the buyers are big fans of the DJ and his in-home recording studio helped push them to snap up the property.

“They are actually huge fans of Zedd, so the music studio was an enticing factor in their decision making,” he said. They plan to transform the space into a movie theater.

The home was built in 1950, spans 3,381 square feet, and sits on just over a third of an acre.

Surrounded by lush greenery, the home features large glass doors that flood the spaces with natural light, and the chef's kitchen is equipped with state-of-the-art appliances and a glass-enclosed wine cellar.

The primary suite is the ultimate space for relaxation with its fireplace, spa-inspired bathroom, and walk-in closet.

Meanwhile, the outdoor area boasts a pool, hot tub, and fire pit—offering yet more spaces to get cozy on chilly California evenings.
