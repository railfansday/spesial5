GTA 6 Twitch Drops Are Coming Soon, Here’s How To Get Your Free Rewards

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

While you might be tempted to take a spin through Grand Theft Auto 6 right now, you might want to hold your horses and watch some gameplay instead. Over on Twitch, the streaming platform is hosting a GTA 6 promotion that can earn you some cool Twitch cosmetics and goodies ahead of your trip to Vice City. Here's a closer look at what you can get, and which streamers you'll have to watch to unlock them.

How to access GTA 6 Twitch drops

Once you're all set up, tune in to any Twitch Drop-enabled livestream during a live Twitch Drops period--November 18-30--and watch for a minimum of 60 minutes on a day to earn a chain badge from the GTA DVD Chest.

How to get GTA 6 Twitch drops

If you watch on six days, you'll unlock all six badges: Purple, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, and Miami Chain.

Each new or gifted subscription to a participating GTA 6 streamer will also unlock a GTA-themed sub badge and an emote, so you'll need three subs to unlock all six. Prime subs don't count.

Once you've collected all 12 badges, you'll unlock the Golden Iguana chat badge.

What GTA 6 Twitch drops can I earn?

One important thing to note is that these will be Twitch-exclusive drops, not in-game items for GTA 6. But if you want to assemble the collection of rewards, here's what you can get:

Daily Chests — GTA DVD Chest (Six Chain Badges)

  • Purple Chain: Unlocked after watching 1 day of GTA 6 during launch week
  • Bronze Chain: Unlocked after watching 2 days of GTA 6 during launch week
  • Silver Chain: Unlocked after watching 3 days of GTA 6 during launch week
  • Gold Chain: Unlocked after watching 4 days of GTA 6 during launch week
  • Diamond Chain: Unlocked after watching 5 days of GTA 6 during launch week
  • Miami Chain: Unlocked after watching 6 days of GTA 6 during launch week

Subscription Drops (Three Badges & Three Emotes)

Sub badge earned by subscribing to a GTA VI channel.

  • Welcome 2 Miami
  • Scored Big
  • Bleed Miami

Exclusive launch subscription emote.

  • BustedPlz
  • DinoBusted
  • SabaBusted

Final Grand Prize

  • Golden Iguana: Earned by securing all 12 preceding launch rewards.

You can also get GTA 6: Ultimate Edition for free

Twitch will also be giving away free codes for the GTA 6: Ultimate Edition, starting November 19 and running through November 30. Details for how you can receive a code have not been revealed yet, but we'll update this post once we have them.

GTA 6 Streamers to watch

You can select Find Streams to see a list of streamers participating in the promotion. Eligible channels will have a "Drop enabled" message at the top of their chat. Alternatively, you can follow these steps:

  • Select Search at the top of the page
  • Enter GTA 6 in the search bar
  • Enter Drops Enabled into the search tags bar under live channels
  • Select a channel you want to watch
