You can choose whether to sell the coke cut with baby powder or the pure stuff for profitability in GTA 6 mission "Dealer's Choice"

The choice to sell pure or cut coke in GTA 6 comes around early in Chapter 3 as part of the "Dealer's Choice" mission, in which Boobie asks Jason to go get drugs from Tyrone and sell them on. Boobie seems happy for Jason to sell a less-than perfect product if it means higher profitability, but Dre'Quan has been warning you about overstepping constantly, suggesting that this might be less than wise. Below I'll explain all the outcomes of choosing the cut drugs or the pure stuff in Grand Theft Auto 6, as well as what the best choice actually is.

What appears to be a double choice in GTA 6 to sell the pure or cut drugs can actually split three ways, as selling the cut drugs leads to a second choice: when you get found out, are you still going to demand the same price for worse coke?

Here's all three outcomes in GTA 6, though keep in mind that any financial gain is earned later after the mission when Boobie sends your cut of the money to you.

We've yet to play out all the outcomes, but it seems as though the highest payment comes from selling the pure stuff, which is $1,500 - not a lot anyway. Nonetheless, we'll update this guide if we find out more.

The reality is that this is not a hugely significant choice beyond the possibility of a shootout. The money that Boobie sends you later doesn't seem to be significantly impacted and there's no long-term story impacts, though you can marginally increase your profits by looting any enemies you encounter on the third option.

Still, I would choose the first option and sell pure for the best outcome, as it's the cleanest. That being said, the differences in reward are so minor that if you're out to make money fast in GTA 6, this quest doesn't make a huge difference either way.
