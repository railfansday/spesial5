As a Blue Wave Washes Across the Country, Democrats Take Control of House and Senate

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The long-predicted blue wave hit shores Tuesday, as results from the 2026 midterm elections indicated that Democrats have captured both the House of Representatives and the US Senate.

“The face of American politics just changed,” CNN's Stephen Collinson said Tuesday. “MAGA's monopoly on Washington will end. Trump will face relentless multi-front investigations from Democratic House committees.”

While polling had predicted a House flip in the weeks leading up to the midterm elections, until Tuesday night, the fate of the Senate was less clear: Democrats had to flip at least four seats held by Republicans to win control of that body. With the projected victories of James Talarico in Texas, Sherrod Brown in Ohio, Annie Andrews in South Carolina, and Roy Cooper in North Carolina, Democrats are on track to wrest the Senate from Republican control.
