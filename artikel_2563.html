Gotham FC hold off Seattle Reign to reach NWSL semifinals

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

An early own goal proved to be the difference on Sunday as the Shield winners kept their title defense alive.

Top-seeded NJ/NY Gotham FC are moving on to the NWSL semifinals after edging the Seattle Reign 1-0 in Sunday’s quarterfinal matchup at Sports Illustrated Stadium in Harrison, New Jersey.

An early own goal from Seattle defender Jordyn Bugg proved to be the difference, as the reigning NWSL champions and 2026 Shield winners held off the eighth-seeded Reign to keep their title defense alive.

The breakthrough came just four minutes into the match, off a corner kick from Jaedyn Shaw. As the ball was delivered into the box, Bugg attempted to clear it at the front post but mis-hit her effort, sending the ball past Reign goalkeeper Claudia Dickey and into the net.

Despite the early goal, clear-cut chances were hard to come by throughout the first half. Both sides struggled to find much attacking rhythm, with Gotham recording just 0.11 expected goals (xG) compared to Seattle’s 0.08 at the break.

Shortly after the 50th minute, Seattle came close to finding an equalizer through Mia Fishel. Receiving the ball outside the penalty area with her back to goal, Fishel turned and fired a shot toward goal, forcing Ann-Katrin Berger to leap and tip the effort over the crossbar to preserve Gotham’s lead.

As the game progressed, the Reign gradually found more of a foothold, putting increased pressure on Gotham’s defense. Gotham held firm through the closing stages to secure the shutout and book its place in the final four, despite finishing the match without a shot on target.

The result also brings an end to Jess Fishlock’s remarkable 14-year run with Seattle. The Reign captain announced in April that she would retire at the conclusion of the 2026 season, bringing an end to a career that began with the club in the NWSL’s inaugural 2013 campaign. The Welsh midfielder, who was substituted in the 75th minute, leaves as the last remaining original member of the Reign’s inaugural squad.

The result sets up a semifinal matchup between Gotham and the Kansas City Current next weekend. Kansas City advanced with a 1-0 extra-time victory over Angel City FC on Saturday, with Michelle Cooper scoring the decisive goal from the penalty spot.

As the No. 1 seed, Gotham will have home-field advantage in the semifinal as they continue their pursuit of a second consecutive NWSL Championship. However, the semifinal venue has yet to be confirmed due to a scheduling conflict at Sports Illustrated Stadium, which is set to host a friendly between the New York Red Bulls and Club América on Nov. 14. Gotham could be forced to relocate its semifinal, with Icahn Stadium among the potential alternatives.
