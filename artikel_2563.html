Welcome to NFL trade deadline day! Teams have been making moves over the last week or so, but there are quite a few big names left on the board. Be sure to follow along with all the latest rumors, news and analysis throughout the day.

Taylor is one of the best rushers in the entire league, and he would be a great fit for any team hoping to add some explosiveness and some finish to its backfield

Our own Tyler Sullivan also ranked the 20 most impactful players who could be on the move ahead of the deadline, but Taylor was not No. 1.
