NBA takes big step in Europe league with $11 billion funding plan

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The NBA has taken another major step toward launching its long-anticipated European league, unveiling an $11 billion funding initiative aimed at developing and renovating basketball arenas across the continent.

Announced Wednesday, the ambitious plan is backed by a group of global investment firms, including Apollo Sports Capital, Ares Management, Arctos, Blackstone, HPS Investment Partners, J.P. Morgan, Sixth Street and Stonepeak.

The initiative will provide prospective clubs and ownership groups with access to long-term financing and the NBA's expertise in developing modern sporting facilities.

According to the league, the investment will help create state-of-the-art arenas and, where appropriate, support the development of surrounding districts.

The NBA and FIBA are partnering on the project, with both organizations targeting a launch next fall.

NBA commissioner Adam Silver believes the project represents a significant opportunity to grow basketball throughout Europe while improving the experience for supporters.

"We've seen firsthand how state-of-the-art arenas and the related developments around them can transform basketball teams and their surrounding communities," Silver said.

"As we prepare to launch a new team in Europe alongside FIBA, we see significant opportunity to help clubs develop facilities which both build on the unique traditions that define European basketball while also elevating the fan experience and attracting many more live events."

The NBA has increasingly looked toward Europe as a major growth market, particularly given the success of European-born players in the league.

Several major cities are reportedly being considered for the new competition, including London and Manchester in Britain, Paris and Lyon in France, Madrid and Barcelona in Spain, and Rome and Milan in Italy.

Munich, Berlin, Athens and Istanbul are also among the reported targets.

However, neither the NBA nor FIBA has formally confirmed which cities will receive franchises.

The league's potential arrival comes as European basketball continues to attract international attention, with stars such as Victor Wembanyama, Giannis Antetokounmpo, Luka Doncic and Nikola Jokic showcasing the continent's talent at the highest level.

With $11 billion in potential arena investment now supporting the project, the NBA's European ambitions are becoming increasingly concrete. The coming months should provide a clearer picture of how the competition will take shape.
