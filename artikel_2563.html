49ers safety gets last laugh against Seattle ahead of crucial NFC West showdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

49ers safety Malik Mustapha is saving money.

On Tuesday, NFL insider Adam Schefter revealed that Mustapha, who appealed his fine from the league, won his case for a hit he made against Seahawks wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba.

Malik Mustapha celebrating in an NFL game against the Vikings last Sunday. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

Mustapha's appeal was overseen by former Packers wide receiver Jordy Nelson, who served as the hearing officer, and the 49ers player will save $6,998.

Mustapha was running toward Smith-Njigba on a pass play. Once the ball hit Smith-Njigba's hands, the two collided — resulting in the ball popping out of the reigning Offensive Player of the Year's hands for an incompletion.

Mustapha rose to his feet quickly after the play, while Smith-Njigba rolled over on his stomach area and momentarily remained on the ground.

While Mustapha hopes to put the play behind him, the Seahawks likely haven't forgotten about it ahead of Sunday's game.

Seahawks wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba running with the ball during an NFL game. Getty Images

When the teams met in Seattle earlier this season, the 49ers put together a 32-29 statement victory to not only forget about last season's 41-6 playoff loss to the Seahawks but also propel them to the top of the NFC West.

Quarterback Brock Purdy was 19-of-29 passing for 189 yards with two touchdowns and one interception that put him in the MVP conversation. Purdy executed a 12-play drive in the first half and completed 6 of 7 passes — four of which went to Mike Evans for 75 yards.

49ers defense celebrating in an NFL game against the Dallas Cowboys. AP Photo/Tony Gutierrez

Purdy's heroics from the first half carried into the second. The Niners executed 13 plays on a drive that started in the third and carried into the fourth quarter. San Francisco capped it with a 7-yard touchdown reception to Brandin Cooks and followed it with a two-point conversion to Evans.

Seattle tied the game at 29 with 6:59 left, but San Francisco answered with a field goal. The 49ers' defense held the Seahawks to only five plays before it forced a turnover on downs, and San Francisco ran out the clock.

The Niners (9-1) not only have a chance to solidify themselves as the NFC West's best team but also have an opportunity to move further ahead in the NFC standings.

Seattle (6-4), the defending Super Bowl champion, is hoping a victory would keep it in the wild-card race.
