Teen Gets Probation for Shooting a Juvenile Playing Basketball at a Recreation Center

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Darius Drew, 19, of Washington, D.C., was sentenced today to probation, on charges stemming from the shooting of a young boy who was playing basketball at a Northwest recreation center in May 2026, announced U.S. Attorney Jeanine Ferris Pirro.
