Peter St. Onge: “Massively Bullish” for Bitcoin if Fed Abandons 2% Inflation Target

Inflation is back near 4%, the Fed is hiking, and Peter St. Onge, Senior Economist at The Heritage Foundation, says that’s “sucking all of the oxygen out of any kind of hard money assets for the moment.” He argues “Washington is addicted to inflation” after COVID and the war in Iran, and says a move to a 3% inflation target would hit savers and young people hardest. Near term, it’s a hit to Bitcoin and gold. “Long term, massively bullish for both of them.”
