Like the perfect gift, fitness is personal. Maybe your giftee loves a sweaty yoga flow, lives for a runner’s high, or considers the gym floor their happy place. Finding the right gift for them can feel like a workout in itself.

With so much fitness and wellness gear out there, it’s tough to know what to get the fitness lover(s) in your life. And that’s where we come in. Women’s Health editors have hands-on experience testing workout equipment and accessories for all kinds of fitness goals—and we’re here to help you find something they’ll love and actually use.

Shopping for a Pilates or barre lover? An extra pair of grippy socks is an accessory they can never have too many of (trust us). Runners on your shopping list may appreciate the Shokz OpenRun Pro 2, one of our best workout headphones picks. Depending on their routine, a weighted vest, a fresh pair of training shoes, or a new pair of Lululemon leggings could be a gift that keeps on giving every time they use them during a workout. And when they’re basking in that post-workout glow, they’ll think of you. Read on for a curated selection of gifts for fitness lovers, hand-picked by WH editors and contributors.
