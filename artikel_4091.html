Feature: These Are Xbox's Best 25 Games From 2001-2026 According To Metacritic

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

As we continue our celebrations for Xbox's 25th anniversary this Sunday (November 15th), we thought it might be fun to look back at the past 25 years on Metacritic, and specifically the best-rated Xbox games from each year.

The website has a significant history of review roundups dating all the way back to 2001, and looking at the list, the early years in particular are jam-packed with historic Xbox games! Halo is in there multiple times, as is Gears of War and Grand Theft Auto, and then we start to see an indie explosion with the likes of Inside and Hades in later years.

So, here are Xbox's best 25 games from 2001-2026 according to Metacritic from each individual year. Just keep in mind that we've eliminated a couple of expansions, as we're only counting full game releases.

Of course, when it comes to 2026 specifically, there's every chance GTA 6 will end up taking the top spot within a couple of weeks' time, so just keep that in mind - Hades 2 has done amazingly well, but will most likely be surpassed.

And by the way, this list doesn't tell the full story - here's what it would look like if you took the individual years away:

Pretty different, right? It's no surprise that there were multiple years that fared better than others in terms of highly-rated releases, so the likes of The Orange Box, Red Dead Redemption 2, Portal 2, Halo 3 and others still make the lineup.

Anyway, Metacritic doesn't tell the full story by any means here, but it's a useful resource to look back and see how well the critics positioned these games at the time, and whether we reckon they're still justifying their high scores.

@Fiendish-Beaver I agree it is harder to get to that 95ish score than it was. I think there's a few different things going on there.

Firstly as @Kaloudz said there was a big graphical jump between generations. But there was also much larger GAMEPLAY jumps back then too. Games and their mechanics were often breaking new paradigms whether that was Mario or Zelda in 3D for the first time or Gears and Halo pushing third and third person console shooters into the mainstream on console. It felt exciting. Now most games feel much more iterative, especially AAA games.

There were also typically fewer reviews for each game, and those reviews were generally from a smaller cross-section of gamers in similar demographics who were more likely to have similar tastes, that led to homogenisation of some scores. Now a game might be marked down by a new demographic, which is fair enough, aggregate scores don't tell the whole story, we all like different things.

Linked to that I know some people believe that Nintendo games get unfairly scored higher than they might for some reason, but I don't see it that way. I think there is often a universal joy to Nintendo games that resonates with far more players. More simply put: they haven't forgotten how to make their games joyful and fun on an almost primal level. That won't be for everyone - nothing is - but it is for a wider cross section of people, and reviewers, hence they are more likely to score higher as an aggregate.

Look at Ocarina of Time remake. Not only a hit with critics currently scoring 96 on Metacritic from 94 reviews but also currently a 9.6 from 10.5k user reviews, it's rare to see both critics and users in such harmony. Having swallowed it whole and finished it already, I would agree it's the easiest 10 in years imho. Bring on GTA6.

As for the list as you'd expect with a best of list it's wall to wall bangers. I'm surprised there's more games than I thought on that list I haven't played yet, but I really struggle to start 100+ hour games like Persona 5 or BG3. One day!
