Xbox Series X-BITE Alligator Console Returns With A New Video And Multiple New Images

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The original trailer has already done over 1 million views

Earlier this week, Xbox unveiled a new custom console that we described as their most "insane" yet, and within just a few days, it's already racked up over 1 million views on the official Xbox YouTube channel.

There's a lot of hype around this thing, then, which explains why Xbox has teamed up with Complex and rapper Lil' Baby to reveal a new video and lots more images of the Xbox Series X-BITE in action.

For those who haven't been keeping up, the Xbox Series X-BITE is a functional console that sits inside of an alligator, and it appears to be inspired by GTA 6 (but for marketing reasons, Xbox can't say that!).

Here's a look at the new video, and we'll throw the new images below it:

There's apparently only one of these in the world, and it's been designed by John Nolan Studio - an animatronics specialist that has worked on some major Hollywood productions including Jurassic Park in the past.

The only way to win the Xbox alligator console is by entering the official sweepstakes, which is taking place across various social media platforms as we speak. Entries end on November 10th, so you haven't got long to enter.

We really hope the eventual winner ends up enjoying the system for themselves instead of flogging it for an insane amount of money, but we can't exactly blame them if they end up picking the latter option!

In any case, this is an extremely cool custom console, and maybe even the most elaborate in Xbox history.
