GTA 6 Will Be Playable On Xbox One Through Xbox Cloud Gaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Rockstar also confirms GTA 6 will run at 30FPS

Update [Tue 10th Nov 2026, 4:03pm]: According to The Verge's Tom Warren, he believes GTA 6 will only be playable on Xbox Cloud Gaming on consoles, with PC and mobile excluded. Xbox Wire recently posted the three ways to play GTA 6 on Xbox. We've updated the article to reflect this.

Original Story: Buying current-gen Xbox hardware isn't cheap these days, and with GTA 6 is now confirmed to be running 30FPS on Xbox Series X|S, what chance would an Xbox One have of running the game, even if it had launched for previous generation tech?

Well, thanks to Rockstar's pre-load notes, we now know GTA 6 will be playable on Xbox One through Xbox Cloud Gaming at Xbox Series S graphics settings. This means those of you who can't upgrade to Xbox Series can still play GTA 6 on November 18th November 19th!

"The game will also be available via XBOX Cloud Gaming on XBOX Series X|S and XBOX One consoles, utilizing XBOX Series S graphics settings. And as with all cloud streaming, connection speeds and other technical factors may influence game performance in ways that would not impact local gameplay. GTAVI will also fully support Remote Play on PlayStation and XBOX."

You'll of course be at the mercy of Xbox's fresh Cloud Gaming time caps, limiting how long you can stream games depending on your Xbox Game Pass subscription. You can purchase additional time beyond your allotted amount, and if you're still stuck on Xbox One, topping up your cloud gaming hours could work out cheaper than buying an Xbox Series console. A free ad-supported version of Xbox Cloud Gaming is also currently in testing.

However, there may be a catch. According to Tom Warren, it appears that playing GTA 6 via Xbox Cloud Gaming will be restricted to Xbox consoles. If you were planning on streaming the game on PC or mobile, you may need to think again.

"I also incorrectly assumed it would be like every other Xbox Cloud Gaming title and support phone, PC, web, and TV streaming too. GTA 6 is unlike any other game, so clearly an exception has been made here to restrict to just Xbox consoles"

But back to Rockstar's notes, the Scotland-based dev did indeed confirm that both Xbox and PlayStation devices will target 30FPS whilst pushing the hardware "to their absolute limits" to play GTA 6. My advice would be to give your Xbox a good clean before release. You don't want your console overheating just when the game finally comes out!

"Grand Theft Auto VI has been fully developed from the ground up to provide the best possible experience for the current generation of consoles, targeting 30 frames per second while pushing both platforms to their absolute limits to bring the vast and rich world of Leonida to life — while maintaining the density and visual fidelity of all its environments and inhabitants."
