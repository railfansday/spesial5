Prediction: Atletico Madrid vs Barcelona

Starting with the hosts, watching their extended flurry come to an end on Tuesday night as they eventually fell to a 2-0 Champions League defeat at home against German giants Bayern Munich, Atletico Madrid will be desperate to immediately dust themselves down. Nevertheless, despite struggling to find their feet on the European stage, Diego Simeone’s side have embarked on an eye-catching domestic flurry. Last seen in La Liga action signing off October with a 2-0 victory away at Levante, Diego Simeone’s men can remarkably skyrocket their way to the top of the table here. Opening this weekend’s fixtures sat in silver medal position among Spain’s elite and just two points adrift of Sunday’s guests, Atletico Madrid have come roaring back sine their shaky start to proceedings back in August.

Suffering another reported injury setback and missing out against Bayern Munich on Tuesday night, Atletico Madrid are once again sweating over the fitness of summer recruit and former Tottenham skipper Cristian Romero. However, despite serving a Champions League suspension in midweek, Danish international Morten Hjulmand will return to Simeone’s squad.

Eventually forced to stay put in Madrid over the summer following an intense transfer saga involving Barcelona, Julian Alvarez will spearhead Atletico’s attack. Former PSG star Kang-In Lee could be handed an immediate full recall on Sunday night.

As for the visitors, while Barcelona might have watched their astonishing purple patch come to an end a fortnight ago, Hanski Flick’s defending Spanish champions have come roaring back. Breezing their way to a 3-0 Champions League victory at home against Aston Villa on Tuesday night, the former Bayern Munich boss has seen his superstar squad receive a fearsome reputation from across the continent. Signing off October with a dominant 6-1 romp against Alaves, Sunday’s guests have their sights fixed on capturing a third consecutive La Liga crown this season. Likewise, landing a 2-1 win when they last travelled to Atletico Madrid for a Champions League showdown back on April 14th, Barcelona will be dreaming of a repeat performance and they chance to collect a heavyweight domestic scalp this weekend.

All still dealing with respective injury issues, Barcelona will travel to Madrid on Sunday night without the trio of Dani Olmo, Jules Kounde and Eric Garcia. Likewise, in what is a huge blow for the reigning Spanish champions, Flick’s side have confirmed that summer recruit and ex-Manchester City star Rodri will miss the next month due to a hamstring injury.

Embarking on a sensational run of form and already registering 18 direct goal contributions in his 10 La Liga appearances so far this season (15 goals, three assists, Brazilian superstar Raphinha will lead Barca’s attack. Making a real impact since arriving in Catalonia over the summer, Anthony Gordon will join Raphinha in the final third.

In what is a blockbuster La Liga showdown on Sunday night, all eyes will certainly fall on the Spanish capital. Landing a 2-1 victory when they last travelled to Atletico Madrid for a Champions League showdown back in April, Flick’s superstar squad are desperate to widen their buffer at the top of the table. However, mustering just a pair of clean sheets from any of their previous 11 straight matchups across all fronts, Atletico will be confident of testing a leaky Barcelona defence here.
