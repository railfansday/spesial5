GdS: Milan could be handed triple injury boost before Frosinone game

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Ruben Amorim is hoping that the international break will yield some good news in terms of potential returns from injury.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport (seen below), the resumption of training is scheduled for next Monday. Amorim hopes to be able to assess the progress of some of the injured players who, in recent weeks, have limited the Portuguese coach’s options.

The goal is to gradually recover several members of the squad for the upcoming matches, starting with the one against high-flying Frosinone. The Rossoneri medical staff hopes to give the coach back a few important options.

Among the players who could return is Fikayo Tomori. The English defender aims to be fit in time for Milan-Frosinone, although he will not be rushed, even if Amorim needs to expand his options at centre-back.

Strahinja Pavlovic’s condition also needs to be monitored, as he forewent his Serbian national team call-up due to a kidney problem. There’s optimism that he’ll soon be back on the pitch, to the extent the Serbian coach spoke of him potentially joining up before their second game.

Encouraging signs have also come from Ruben Loftus-Cheek, who has been absent for the past couple of games. The English midfielder is now close to returning and could represent another solution for Amorim when the season resumes.
