Rabiot stars, Ramos scores: Fan player ratings for Milan 5-1 Frosinone

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

AC Milan treated fans in San Siro and across the world to a dominant display on Sunday night, as they beat Frosinone 5-1.

In recent seasons, a significant win against the visitors may have gone under the radar. However, Milan picked apart a side that was in the European places before the start of this weekend’s action. It’s a huge result.

Of course, the opposition’s playing style helped the Rossoneri a little bit; that can’t be denied. Yet, they hardly rolled over and still threatened to take something from the game before the scoreline became so wide.

As you’d expect, our own ratings were really high, with no one scoring below a 6/10 and Adrien Rabiot was given the Man of the Match award. The consensus from you, the readers who voted after full-time, was also that all of the players were really solid.

Scoring 0.4 higher than he did in our ratings, Rabiot was again seen as the MOTM and it’s understandable why. Then, Luka Modric followed with a 7.8 and Christian Pulisic scored a 7.6 to round up the top three. With his goal, Goncalo Ramos scored a 7.3, which is his highest rating from your scores so far. Perhaps he’d have scored even higher if he’d stayed on.

On the lower end of the scale, Davide Bartesaghi was the only starter given below a 6/10, whilst Omari Hutchinson (5.7) and Pervis Estupinan (5.4) were not viewed as impressive from the bench. Nevertheless, it’s a high average for the team.
