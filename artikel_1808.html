Tottenham: Roberto De Zerbi explains why Luca Williams-Barnett can match Lamine Yamal

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Your weekly briefing on Tottenham, featuring team news and expert analysis from Sam Tabuteau

Roberto De Zerbi says Luca Williams-Barnett's talent is "bigger than you think" after scoring his first Tottenham goal.

Williams-Barnett was rewarded for two exciting Premier League cameos with his first senior start on Saturday as Ipswich came to town. De Zerbi was rewarded with a goal and an assist from the teenager in a 2-0 win.

The 18-year-old, who joined Tottenham at the age of 10, got the home fans off their seats almost every time he touched the ball.

And De Zerbi said he decided to hand Williams-Barnett his first Premier League start 10 days prior.

The Spurs manager said: "I told him to play like he plays in the park with his friends, with the same freedom.

"Luca is a special player. I’m not surprised because his talent is bigger than you think."

Asked if Williams-Barnett could emulate Spanish wonderkid Lamine Yamal, De Zerbi said the Spurs teenager has the passion to reach the top of the game.

He replied: "Yes, I think so. One of the best things in my career I've seen today the bus from the training ground to the stadium. He was watching [clips] on YouTube of Ronaldinho and [Eden] Hazard.

"I think he's a special guy because if one hour before you are debuting in the Premier League, you are watching Ronaldinho, Hazard, Neymar, all these types of talent.
