Travis Kelce, Taylor Swift Exit Chiefs Game Together After Win

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

After Taylor Swift watched the Kansas City Chiefs vs. New York Jets game alongside old pal Karlie Kloss, she left Arrowhead Stadium with husband Travis Kelce in a vintage car.

Nothing good starts in a getaway car—except a Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce date night.

After the Grammy winner watched her husband’s Kansas City Chiefs defeat the New York Jets with longtime pal Karlie Kloss at Arrowhead Stadium Nov. 8, the couple celebrated the team’s win by leaving together in an old vintage car.

Much like their very first night public night together in 2023, Kelce and Swift pulled out of the Kansas City stadium in a vintage ride—this time a cream-colored Cadillac—with Kelce honking the horn and both waving to fans as they exited the parking lot.

Meanwhile, Swift’s latest trip to support her husband’s football team was even more eyebrow-raising for the company she kept in her suite. In addition to Kelce’s mom Donna Kelce, as well as her parents Scott Swift and Andrea Swift, the “Karma” singer sat side-by-side with Kloss—their first public display of friendship since 2017.

For their Sunday fun day, Kloss, 33, and Swift, 36, were joined by Kloss and husband Joshua Kushner’s 5-year-old son Levi. The duo appeared friendly as ever, both sporting red to support Swift’s guy on the Chiefs.

And despite rumored bad blood between Swift and Kloss, the model proved she’ll always be among Swift’s evergreen group of friends when she and her husband Joshua stepped out to attend Kelce and Swift’s wedding in July.

In fact, a few weeks after Kloss—also mom to Elijah, 3, and Rae, 14 months, with Kushner—stepped out for the white veil occasion, she clarified it will always be nice to have a friend in Swift.

After all, Kelce previously emphasized the energy Swift always brings to Chiefs kingdom, dating all the way back to her first game in 2023.

“To see the slow motion chest bumps, to see the high fives with mom, to see how you know Chiefs kingdom was all excited that she was there, that s--t was absolutely hysterical,” Kelce said during an episode of New Heights at the time. “It was definitely a game I’ll remember that’s for damn sure and then we just slid off in the getaway car at the end.”
