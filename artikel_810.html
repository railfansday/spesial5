Lily Collins Pays Tribute to Phil Collins After Rock Hall Induction

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Lily Collins said it felt "monumentally special" to watch her father Phil Collins inducted into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame as a solo artist.

Lily Collins paid tribute to her father, Phil Collins, following his induction into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame, saying it felt “monumentally special” to stand alongside her family as he accepted the honor. Explore Cynthia Erivo Genesis Lily Collins See latest videos, charts and news

The Emily in Paris star shared a series of photos from Saturday’s (Nov. 14) ceremony at Los Angeles’ Peacock Theater, where Collins was inducted as a solo artist — 16 years after he entered the Hall as a member of Genesis in 2010. “To see my dad’s extraordinary legacy celebrated with an induction into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame is something I don’t think I’ll ever quite have the words for,” Lily wrote.

“Seeing you up there on stage embracing all the love, support, and recognition in the room after everything you’ve been through is indescribable,” she continued. “There is no one more deserving. We couldn’t be more proud. I couldn’t be more proud.”

Collins made a rare public appearance at the ceremony, where he was inducted by Travis Barker. He did not perform; Cynthia Erivo sang “In the Air Tonight” in his place, with Barker on drums.

Opening his acceptance speech, Collins told the crowd he’s “really not as bad as it looks,” before making a promise: “You haven’t heard the last of me. I promise you that.”

He went on to thank longtime manager Tony Smith, Atlantic Records’ Ahmet and Nesuhi Ertegun and Virgin Records founder Richard Branson, as well as “my three ex-wives, because they’ve loved me through the years and given me five great children.” He also credited his assistant with saving his life. “I’m not joking when I say without her I wouldn’t be here,” he said, adding that he’s “a well man now.”

The singer and drummer has faced significant health issues in recent years. A 2007 spinal injury caused nerve damage that left him unable to grip a drumstick, and he has since undergone knee surgeries and dealt with ongoing mobility problems. His son Nic played drums on Genesis’ 2021-22 farewell tour, which wrapped in London in 2022, with Collins singing from a seated position.

As a solo artist, Collins scored seven No. 1 hits on the Billboard Hot 100, including “Against All Odds (Take a Look at Me Now),” “Sussudio,” “One More Night” and “Another Day in Paradise.” His albums No Jacket Required and …But Seriously both topped the Billboard 200, and he reached No. 1 on the Hot 100 with Genesis on “Invisible Touch.”

The 2026 Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony airs Dec. 8 from 8-11 p.m. ET on ABC and streams on Disney+ the following day.
