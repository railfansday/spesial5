Madonna Doubling Down on ‘Confessions’ With Limited Edition 4-LP Box Set

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The original album and this year's sequel will be packaged together on clear vinyl.

Madonna is offering you all her Confessions. The pop icon announced on Monday (Nov. 9) that fans can now order a deluxe 4-LP box set that brings together her original 2005 Confessions on a Dance Floor album and this year’s Confession II LP in an exclusive collector’s edition featuring four LPs pressed on clear vinyl. Explore Madonna See latest videos, charts and news

According to an announcement on the singer’s site, both albums are housed in a custom-designed double slipcase featuring a “luxurious black fabric exterior with metallic foil detailing and exclusive photographs on the front and back.” Inside, the “striking silver metallic case” features previously unseen Confessions images along with a unique edition number on the spine. The set also includes an exclusive double-sided 24″ x 36″ Confessions poster. The $195 set is slated to ship in March 2027.

In another extension of the Confessions universe, Madonna dropped Confessions II: Instrumentals last week, stripping the vocals from the album that earned the singer her 10th No. 1 on the Billboard 200 when Confessions II debuted atop the chart dated July 18. The 16-song Confessions II is the sequel to Madonna’s 2005 chart-topping original, reuniting her with her main collaborator on the project, producer/DJ Stuart Price.

In addition, last year, Madonna released Confessions on a Dance Floor Twenty Years Edition, an expanded edition of the original electropop dance record that expanded the 12-song collection best known for such hits as “Hung Up” and “Sorry” with eight additional b-sides, remixes and promo-only tracks including: “Hung Up (Chus & Ceballos Remix),” “Sorry (PSB Maxi-Mix),” “Get Together (Jacques Lu Cont Mix),” “Jump (Axwell Remix)” and “Hung Up (Archigram Remix).”
