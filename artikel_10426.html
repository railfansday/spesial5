These viral ‘Grand Theft Auto VI’ details have fans even more hyped for the game’s release

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

After a brutal 13-year wait, the next entry in the Grand Theft Auto franchise is finally just a day away from release—and somehow, the hype is still growing.

Grand Theft Auto VI expands the series’ formula of letting players run rampant through near-perfect parodies of real cities. This time, Miami, Florida, is represented as Vice City, Leonida, where players will have more options than ever before when it comes to both criminal escapades and the minutiae of everyday life.

Reviews are starting to roll in—almost entirely glowing reviews, mind you—and early players are sharing the juicy details of their adventures in Vice City.

A vibrant, living world

While GTA VI’s main characters and story (a criminal couple named Jason and Lucia striving to find a happy ending together) are a major draw for some, it’s the random strangers around them that are driving the last wave of viral anticipation for the game.

Take one encounter described in Jason Schreier’s review for Bloomberg. While cruising around Vice City, Schreier saw a delivery truck rear-end a sedan, then speed away. He rescued the sedan driver from his vehicle as it caught on fire, then watched as the man decided to re-enter the burning car to increase his injuries with the goal of a bigger lawsuit payout. Schreier had the choice to blackmail the man or help his case as a witness. He chose the second option and received a few thousand bucks of the settlement via BuckMe, the game’s version of Venmo.

That degree of detail is all over GTA VI, Schreier wrote, with other side encounters including busting a parrot-smuggling operation inside a liquor store and helping free caged apes after an animal rights protest. His review has garnered more than 2.6 million views on social media, just one of several reviews from major outlets singing the game’s praises and leaving fans counting down the hours until midnight.

“This review has put tears in my eyes, I’m so buzzing,” one user wrote. 

“How can you even fathom this level of immersion,” asked another.

Radio realness

The GTA franchise has always been known for both its humor and its realism, and no feature exemplifies that more in GTA VI than its radio stations.

The in-game radio includes a stacked list of pop hits by the likes of Ariana Grande, Billie Eilish, Rihanna, and PinkPantheress—the latter of whom appears in the game herself as a radio host, much to the delight of her fans. 

Other radio offerings include true crime podcasts, news stations, and advertisements full of pitch-perfect satire of American and Floridian culture. “Have you always dreamed of abusing your power without consequence?” asks one in-game ad that’s already gone viral. “Well, now you can, when you join the Vice City Police Department.”

Days’ worth of gameplay

After more than a decade of anticipation, no one wants their GTA VI playthrough to be a short-lived experience. Luckily, that’s far from the case.

GTA VI’s main story takes around 70 hours to complete on average, according to a sample of players who ran the game for IGN. That included 115 hours on the high end and 42 hours on the low end, which the reviewer admitted was a rushed playthrough. Another reviewer said that even their 70-hour playthrough was a bit rushed, and they’ve since played another 30 hours with plenty of content still to discover.
