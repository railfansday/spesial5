Anti-Israel agitators swarm Biden in Ireland and hurl object at motorcade

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Two people were arrested by Irish police after agitators gathered at Trinity College Dublin while former President Joe Biden made a surprise visit on Tuesday (Credit: Credit: PA Media via Reuters).

Former President Joe Biden was heckled Tuesday by anti-Israel agitators who threw at least one object at his motorcade and screamed obscenities during his visit to Ireland alongside former first lady Jill Biden.

Biden, 83, made a surprise appearance at Trinity College in Dublin, where his son, Hunter Biden, accepted the Gold Medal for Outstanding Contribution to Public Discourse, The Independent reported.

A mob of agitators gathered outside the venue while the event took place.

As Biden exited the building, someone threw an object that was blocked by an umbrella near Biden. Local police arrested two people at the scene.

Video posted online shows law enforcement officers detaining people as the crowd chanted, "Blood, blood, blood on your hands!"

Biden was escorted into a waiting vehicle as the chaos unfolded, and the motorcade quickly drove away. The agitators then heckled the police officers at the scene.

"Shame on you!" they were heard chanting.
