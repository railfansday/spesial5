DIRECTV’s Holiday Deal Gets You Christmas Movies & Musicals Across Multiple Streaming Platforms at a Low Price

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

With several streaming options and more than 60 live TV channels, DIRECTV's deals on the MyEntertainment Genre Pack make it a worthy choice for watching festive movies and musicals this year.

Mariah Carey has officially unfrozen, the clocks have gone back, and the evenings are darker, meaning the festive season is upon us.

From now on, we’ll start to see streaming platforms usher in their lineup of holiday content, and if your watchlist includes everything from festive musicals to feel-good rom-coms, keeping track of streaming subscriptions can get expensive.

DIRECTV is offering a holiday deal that could help make seasonal viewing a little more affordable. The MyEntertainment Genre Pack brings Disney+, Hulu, HBO Max and AMC+ together with more than 60 live TV channels for $34.99 per month for the first two months, down from its regular $42.99 monthly price. The offer ends Jan. 5, 2027. Furthermore, new customers can also take advantage of a five-day free trial, making it easier to explore the lineup before signing up. Service automatically continues each month at its regular price after the trial and promo, unless canceled.

Paying for multiple streaming subscriptions can add up, especially during the holiday season when you’re looking to catch up on Christmas movies and musicals across different platforms. DIRECTV’s MyEntertainment Genre Pack is an attractive option this time of year, where customers can bundle several streaming platforms into one package alongside dozens of live TV channels. Rather than paying for each included streaming service separately, these subscriptions are included through a DIRECTV subscription.

For Christmas musical fans, there’s plenty to explore across the included platforms. Disney+ is home to seasonal favorites like The Muppet Christmas Carol and The Nightmare Before Christmas, while Hulu offers A Very Jonas Christmas Movie. Beyond the musicals, viewers can find holiday movie favorites, festive television specials and curated Christmas episodes of animated shows like Bob’s Burgers, The Simpsons and Family Guy.

There’s also a broad selection of kids’ movies and family-friendly entertainment across Disney+ and Hulu, making the package a convenient option for households with different viewing tastes. Alongside its streaming inclusions, MyEntertainment offers more than 60 live entertainment channels, including Bravo, Comedy Central, Food Network, HGTV, History, National Geographic and many more.

For viewers who want even more classic films and seasonal programming, DIRECTV offers MyCinema as an optional Mini pack add-on to MyEntertainment.

The Mini pack includes 12 movie-focused channels, including Turner Classic Movies (TCM), Family Movie Classics, Hallmark Family, Hallmark Mystery, Great American Family, Grit, INSP, LMN, ShortsTV, Smithsonian Channel and Sony Movies.

TCM is a particularly appealing addition for classic film enthusiasts, with holiday favorites like Meet Me in St. Louis and The Bishop’s Wife worth looking out for when they air. Meanwhile, Hallmark’s holiday programming is practically a December tradition of its own.

MyCinema is currently available for $0 for the first two months before renewing at $10.99 per month, plus tax. Just make sure to maintain a qualifying base plan.

MyEntertainment also includes unlimited hours of cloud DVR storage, allowing customers to record live programming and watch it whenever they like.

Between four included streaming platforms, more than 60 live channels and the option to add 12 movie-focused channels through MyCinema, DIRECTV is positioning MyEntertainment as an alternative to juggling multiple entertainment subscriptions. With promotional pricing available for a limited time, the package could be worth considering for viewers looking to consolidate their holiday entertainment and potentially spend less on their monthly streaming lineup.
