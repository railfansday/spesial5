LISTEN: Now 40, Alex Honnold Must Juggle Kids, Career, and Climbing

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Alex Honnold has been in our lives for nearly 20 years, evolving from the dirtbag wunderkind of the Yosemite Valley, into the star of an Academy Award-winning film, to the outdoor world’s biggest celebrity. Now, at age 40, Honnold has entered a new phase of his life and career. He’s a husband and father, a global media platform, an in-demand public speaker, and yes, still a rock climber. Over the summer, Outside features editor Frederick Dreier shadowed Honnold during a typical day at his home in South Lake Tahoe to find out how Honnold balances these competing forces in his life—and still finds time to rock climb. In the latest Long Read on the Outside Podcast, we read Dreier’s magazine cover story about the climber.
