Ethan Klein asks judge to deny Hila’s request for financial support in divorce

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Ethan Klein has asked a Los Angeles judge to deny Hila Klein’s request for financial support as their divorce moves forward, according to court documents.

Financial support, also known as spousal support, is money one spouse asks a court to order the other to pay during or after a divorce. Ethan’s response reportedly asks the court not to award financial support to either him or Hila.

The Los Angeles Superior Court docket lists the case as 26STFL09971, a dissolution proceeding involving minor children. Hila filed for divorce on November 2 after 14 years of marriage, listing October 13, 2026, as the couple’s separation date.

According to TMZ, Ethan’s response appears to agree with Hila’s request for joint custody of their three children. His legal team is also asking the court to confirm that income and assets Ethan acquired before the marriage and after the couple’s separation should be treated as his separate property.

Hila’s original petition requested financial support from Ethan, asked him to cover her legal fees and sought to restore her maiden name, Hacmon. The court has not ruled on those requests.

The allegations also led to the H3 Podcast going on indefinite hiatus. Ethan said he was working to remove his ownership and financial stakes from the podcast, Teddy Fresh, Teddy Glow and other joint ventures, with the proceeds intended to go to Hila and the podcast crew.
