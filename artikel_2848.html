7-try France beats Argentina 45-17 to reach Nations Championship final

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

PARIS (AP) — France reached the Nations Championship final after beating Argentina 45-17 with seven tries on Saturday.

England's 37-12 win against New Zealand left France needing a bonus-point victory to make the Nov. 29 final against South Africa, which advanced after losing 20-17 in Ireland.

Already leading 21-10, Emilien Gailleton got the bonus fourth try in the 64th minute against 14 men, and moments later the yellow card for Pumas prop Mayco Vivas was upgraded to red.

France finished with three more tries; lock Tyler Duguid charged through following an attacking lineout, center Kalvin Gourgues showed stunning pace from near halfway to make the left corner, and Louis Bielle-Biarrey completed the rout with his 31st try in 30 tests.

The French, topping the northern hemisphere pool, will play southern hemisphere leader South Africa in the final at Twickenham. France beat the Springboks 21-19 last week in Paris.

In the first half, lock Mickaël Guillard and try-scoring hooker Peato Mauvaka crossed for France. Returning flyhalf Matthieu Jalibert cleverly set up silky center Gourgues shortly after the break for the first of his two tries.

Argentina led briefly at 10-7 for 10 minutes thanks to a converted try from center Justo Piccardo and a penalty from flyhalf Tomas Albornoz.

Jalibert's faster than expected recovery from a torn thigh allowed captain Antoine Dupont to resume at scrumhalf after wearing the No. 10 jersey a week ago against South Africa.

Dupont was hardly had to turn it on but showed his class at the end with a kick down the left flank for Bielle-Biarrey's try.

Gourdes nailed five conversions as a makeshift kicker on the night.

Tributes were held before the game for Federico Martín Aramburú, the former Argentina player who died four years ago after being fatally shot in Paris.
