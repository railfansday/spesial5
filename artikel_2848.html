Rams take 13-10 lead with Blake Corum touchdown

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Los Angeles effectively doubled up against Washington, scoring a late field goal in the second quarter and a touchdown to start the third period to take a 13-10 lead.

Blake Corum capped the second scoring drive with a 2-yard TD run.

Corum has now scored in five consecutive games.

Getting the ball first out of halftime, the Rams started fast with a no-huddle approach on offense. Matthew Stafford connected with Puka Nacua for two consecutive throws to begin the possession — one for 12 yards and another for 15 yards.

Nacua, who has 11 catches so far, is now tied with Cooper Kupp on top of the franchise list with 13 games of at least 10 receptions.

Corum then had consecutive runs of 15 yards and 7 yards to put the team in scoring position.

A few plays later, Corum found his way into the end zone.

On the injury front, Rams guard Steve Avila (hamstring) has been downgraded to out.

Washington center Lucas Patrick is questionable to return with a concussion evaluation. Matt Glubin has come in at the position.
