She was the first woman ever to be inducted into the Tennis Hall of Fame over 70 years ago

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Ashleigh Barty is your 2026 International Tennis Hall of Fame inductee.

Barty, whose retirement from the sport in 2022 stunned the tennis community, has joined the likes of Roger Federer, Maria Sharapova and Martina Hingis in the Hall of Fame.

The three-time major champion fought off competition from 2009 US Open champion Juan Martin del Potro and two-time Grand Slam champion Svetlana Kuznetsova – both of whom have missed out on joining the Hall of Fame for a second consecutive year.

The American, born in Plymouth, England, won two Wimbledon singles titles, a US Open singles title, and a US Open doubles title between 1904 and 1907.

Upon winning her first major title at the 1904 US Open, Sutton became the youngest Grand Slam champion in tennis history. Sutton was 17 years old when she defeated Elisabeth Moore – an eight-time major champion – in the final.

Sutton held the record until 1979, when Tracy Austin won the US Open at 16 years, eight months and 28 days.

1905 Wimbledon: May Sutton defeated Dorothea Douglass in the final, 6-3, 6-4

1907 Wimbledon: May Sutton defeated Dorothea Lambert Chambers in the final, 6-3, 6-4

Sutton was inducted into the International Tennis Hall of Fame in 1956 alongside Williams Larned, Holcombe Ward and Beals Wright.

In 1905, Sutton became the first non-British player to capture a Wimbledon singles title.

The American defeated Dorothea Douglass in the final, triumphing 6-3, 6-4.

She also defeated Ethel Thomson, Agnes Morton and Constance Wilson at the event.

Two years later, Norman Brookes became the first non-British man to win Wimbledon. The Australian defeated Arthur Gore in the 1907 final, winning 6–4, 6–2, 6–2
