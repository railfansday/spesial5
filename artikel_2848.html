Patrick Mahomes family tree: Meet the Chiefs QB's wife Brittany, brother Jackson, parents and kids

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Patrick Mahomes wife: Brittany Matthews Mahomes

Patrick Mahomes mom: Randi Martin Mahomes

There are not many athletes with a pedigree like Patrick Mahomes.

Mahomes has already become one of the most decorated quarterbacks in NFL history. With three Super Bowl titles (and Super Bowl MVPs), two NFL MVPs and numerous other accolades, the Chiefs quarterback is in cruise control to Canton.

Mahomes has set numerous records in the NFL, becoming the fastest player in league history to 250 career passing touchdowns, most passing yards and touchdowns in a player's first 50 games, most total touchdowns in a singular postseason (which he did twice); Mahomes' 17 postseason wins is second in NFL history behind Tom Brady.

But, Mahomes is not the only one in his family with success in the sports world. Here's more about his family, from his athlete dad to his kids, the latest having arrived in early 2025.

Mahomes and Matthews decided to tie the knot during the 2022 NFL offseason. The two married on March 12, 2022, in Hawaii.

The couple has been together for a long time. They were high school sweethearts and first became engaged shortly after Mahomes received his Super Bowl win after the Chiefs' 2020 victory over the 49ers. They had their first daughter, Sterling, before getting married.

Brittany was a star soccer player in college and played professionally in Iceland for one year in 2017 after graduating from the University of Texas at Tyler. She is a trainer by trade and lists herself as an entrepreneur at Brittany Lynne Fitness, owner of the NWSL's Kansas City Current and mother to her daughter, Sterling, and dogs, Steel and Silver, on her Instagram. She has 2.1 million followers.

Mahomes, like her brother-in-law Jackson, generated some controversy during the 2021 NFL season and 2022 playoffs. A video of her spraying champagne onto the crowd after the Chiefs' win over the Bills went viral, and she caught flak for her act. She only dumped fuel onto that fire by tweeting the following on social media.

Recently, Mahomes has not gone viral for any of her actions at games, but she has been seen in the sky box at Arrowhead Stadium. A lot of the time, its alongside Travis Kelce's mother, Donna, or Kelce's now fiance, Taylor Swift.

Brittany and Patrick Mahomes have three kidstogether and a fourth on the way. Based on comments Patrick Mahomes made during OTAs, it originally seemed Golden could be the last of the bunch — but the couple announced that their expecting a fourth child in November 2026.

"I'm done, I'll say that," he told reporters at a press conference in July 2024. "I said three and I'm done."

In November 2026, the couple shared beachside family photos featuring their three children in the announcement of their expected fourth child.

Sterling Mahomes is the daughter of Patrick Mahomes and Brittany Matthews. She was born Feb. 20, 2021, just a couple of weeks after Mahomes' Chiefs lost Super Bowl 55 to the Buccaneers.

The couple posts plenty of pictures of Sterling on Instagram.

Sterling now has the distinction of being the family's oldest child as opposed to the only child.

Patrick "Bronze" Mahomes III is the son of Mahomes II and Matthews, born on Nov. 28, 2022.

As for Bronze's unique nickname, Mahomes explained his brother, Jackson, played a role in its creation.

"When we had Sterling," Mahomes told the AP, "we didn't know if it was a boy or girl, so we thought of a name that could be a boy or girl. And we wanted them to have a connection. I always wanted to do Patrick Mahomes III, ever since I was a kid. I always thought it would be 'Trey,' you hear that a lot. So when me and (brother) Jackson were trying to think of something unique, he thought of 'Bronze.' It fit perfectly with Sterling. They can have that connection moving forward."

Mahomes had his third child in January 2025, with the couple having Golden Raye on January 12.

Golden Raye was born in the middle of the Chiefs' 2025 postseason run, with Brittany going into labor right before the team's Divisional Round game. Since the Chiefs had a first-round bye, Patrick was able to be by his wife's side.

"Brittany crushed it. I was watching playoff games the entire day. She went to labor after one game," Mahomes said on a clip of 'The Kingdom.' "Baby was here before the next game, so I didn't even miss a playoff game. So, she is as locked in as I am as far as football goes."

The Mahomes now have a fourth child expected with their November 2026 announcement.

Mahomes wasn't the first professional athlete in his family. His father, Pat, spent 11 years as an MLB pitcher and played professionally for the Twins, Red Sox, Mets, Rangers, Cubs and Pirates from 1992 through 2003. He also had a stop with the Yokohama BayStars in 1997 and 1998.

During his time as an MLB pitcher, Mahomes posted a 42-39 record, a 5.47 ERA and 452 strikeouts. He last appeared in a major league game on Sept. 27, 2003, with the Pirates. However, Mahomes continued his playing career for six more seasons, bouncing around the minor and independent leagues before calling it quits in 2009.

The two Mahomes' have a great relationship. Ahead of Super Bowl 55, Mahomes Sr. explained that he delivers a supportive, encouraging message to his son before every game.

"I tell him, 'Players make plays, and I love you,'" he told Fox4 Kansas City. "That's our thing we've had since he was 7 or 8 years old. I tried to reinforce it every game, whether it was two games a week in baseball or basketball or one game a week in football. I try to make sure he knows that I'm in his corner. I'm going to be there. And as long as he does the best he can, he will never hear a gripe from me."

The elder Mahomes also travels from Texas to Kansas City to catch as many home games as he can. He did so in 2020 even despite the COVID-19 restrictions, as it was important for him to show up and support his son at the games.

"Wherever he is, he knows I'm going to be there," Mahomes Sr. said.

It's worth noting that the elder Mahomes is the reason that Mahomes II goes by Patrick instead of Pat. The two wanted to avoid too much confusion, so the younger Mahomes elected not to take on the "Pat" moniker.

The elder Mahomes unfortunately has dealt with some legal issues in recent years as well. He was arrested on a charge of public intoxication during one of Patrick's Texas Tech games in 2016. He was also arrested in 2018 for a DWI and was sentenced to 40 days in jail, which he served between 2019 and 2020.

And just days before Super Bowl 58, he was arrested again for driving while intoxicated in Smith County, Texas, according to TMZ. Even though his son won't be in Super Bowl 60, again just days before the big game, Pat Sr. was back in the news for the wrong reasons. According to TMZ, he was arrested for violating his probation after drinking alcohol.

Mahomes' mother Randi wasn't a professional athlete like his father. However, she played just as important a role in raising him and is the reason he stuck with football.

In an interview with ESPN in 2020, Patrick said his mother "was always there. She did everything."

In an interview with ABC4 ahead of Super Bowl 54, Randi detailed that her son once wanted to quit football. She convinced him to continue playing.

"Before his junior year in high school he came to me that summer wanting to quit football, but I just said you're going to regret it if you quit," she told him.

Randi has been known to come to her son's defense on Twitter. She blamed the refs for her son's Super Bowl 55 loss against the Buccaneers and also went after Mack Wilson after he concussed her son during the 2021 AFC Championship Game.

She also uses social media to express pride and gratitude for her children; Patrick is no exception.

"I am very, very proud," Randi said of Patrick's performance in the NFL. "I always say were blessed. He`s blessed to be able to do his dream — to live doing exactly what he loves."

Randi is divorced from Pat Sr., but they remain good friends. She has two children in addition to Mahomes, one of whom is his only full-blood sibling.

NFL fans have gotten to know Jackson Mahomes well over the course of the past few years. The 25-year-old is a social media star thanks to his 1 million-plus followers on TikTok and has generated a lot of attention as a result.

Of course, not all of the attention has been positive for Jackson. He generated controversy for pouring a water bottle on a Ravens fan after Baltimore beat the Chiefs in September 2021 and drew criticism for dancing on Sean Taylor's number when Kansas City played Washington.

The younger Mahomes also called out a Kansas City restaurant on social media for having bad service in December of 2021. The restaurant fought back and called out the celebrity in a post of their own.

Patrick has spoken to his brother about some of these antics, including the water bottle dump against the Ravens. However, he denied the report by Rich Ohrenberger that he asked both Jackson and his own wife Brittany not to come to Chiefs games in 2022.

The most recent affair with Jackson came in March 2024, with Mahomes being sentenced to six months of probation after a single misdemeanor count of battery, on the count that he shoved a waiter who tried to come into a room where he was with the woman.

While Mahomes has made a career on social media, he has not posted a TikTok since April 2025. His bio is "Sup I get Bullied a lot but I'm still here."
