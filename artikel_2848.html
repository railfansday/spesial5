Player Grades: Lakers vs. Suns

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Shoutout to Dillon Brooks, who once again squarely put his foot in his mouth.

This summer, he made some comments about the Lakers and said if Luka Dončić isn't averaging 40, then they're in trouble. Ironically, Luka is nearly averaging 40 and the Lakers are just fine.

As it turns out, the only time the Lakers were in trouble on Sunday was when the Suns finally benched Brooks, who stuffed the stat sheet with one point, two rebounds, three fouls and four turnovers in 22 minutes while tallying a plus-minus of -20.

So, let's dive into the win. As always, grades are based on expectations for each player. A "B" grade represents the average performance for that player.

26 minutes, 12 points, 8 rebounds, 3 steals, 2 blocks, 3 fouls, 4 turnovers, 4-9 FG, 2-6 3PT, 2-2 FT, +30

One of Thybulle's better games this season. Eight rebounds are a nice bonus to his typical production, which also showed up with his five stocks. He also had an absurd plus-minus of +30 in 26 minutes.

32 minutes, 12 points, 3 rebounds, 3 assists, 1 steal, 3 blocks, 2 fouls, 4-10 FG, 2-6 3PT, 2-2 FT, +13

Jake struggled to shoot the ball, but still filled the stat sheet. An additional four stocks from LaRavia shows how impressive this starting lineup was defensively.

32 minutes, 14 points, 12 rebounds, 3 assists, 1 steal, 2 blocks, 2 fouls, 2 turnovers, 4-5 FG, 6-6 FT, +18

Kessler was an absolute monster on the offensive glass with half of his rebounds coming on that end. Against the one team worse at rebounding than the Lakers, Kessler ensured they exploited that weakness.

37 minutes, 22 points, 6 rebounds, 1 assist, 2 steals, 3 fouls, 3 turnovers, 7-10 FG, 5-6 3PT, 3-4 FT, +21

In the small sample size of this season, Grimes as a starter has been dramatically better than Grimes as a reserve. Is there more to that? Should he move into the starting lineup? It's hard to argue taking either Thybulle or LaRavia out right now.

35 minutes, 43 points, 3 rebounds, 9 assists, 1 steal, 5 fouls, 2 turnovers, 13-28 FG, 5-13 3PT, 12-14 FT, +10

Luka continued his impressive first-quarter scoring and got the Lakers out to a great start with nearly half his points coming in the opening 12 minutes. His shot selection in the fourth when he returned was pretty questionable but he got to the rim when he needed to late in the game.

18 minutes, 6 points, 2 rebounds, 3 assists, 2 steals, 4 fouls, 1 turnover, 2-3 FG, 0-1 3PT, 2-2 FT, -18

A quieter night from Ziaire, though it still featured an impressive reverse alley-oop.

21 minutes, 4 points, 2 rebounds, 3 assists, 1 steal, 2 fouls, 1 turnover, 2-8 FG, 0-4 3PT, -13

After consecutive great games, Mamu took a big step back on Sunday. He wasn't hitting open shots and couldn't negate that enough with the other things he brings to the table.

12 minutes, 2 points, 4 rebounds, 1 block, 1 foul, 1-2 FG, 0-2 FT, -2

Another reserve who wasn't great. He wasn't quite as bad as the others, mainly because he didn't play in the fourth, which is reflected in his plus-minus.

23 minutes, 16 points, 3 assists, 2 fouls, 2 turnovers, 6-11 FG, 2-5 3PT, 2-4 FT, -19

This road trip has been where Sexton has really found his footing. His scoring has been really important with Luka and Austin Reaves in and out of the lineup. The decision-making still leaves things to be desired, but it feels like that will be the case in even the best performances from Sexton this season.

Redick noted postgame that he went with a different rotation that put more ballhandlers and playmakers in the game to start the second quarter with Luka out. Interestingly, that seemed to come at the expense of playing Thiero, who did not get on the floor.

The bench was fine in that second quarter with that lineup, but were bludgeoned in the fourth. Thiero wasn't the difference-maker in that, but the struggles of the non-Luka lineups of late has to fall at someone's feet.

Sunday's DNPs — Jaden Hardy, Bronny James, Kevon Looney, Adou Thiero, Chris Mañon

Sunday's inactives — Cameron Carr, Carson Cooper, Arthur Kaluma, Austin Reaves
