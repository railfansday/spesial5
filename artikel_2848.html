Raiders provide crucial update on tight end Brock Bowers

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Brock Bowers remains day-to-day with an ankle injury and did not practice Wednesday as the Raiders began preparing for Sunday's game against the host Broncos.

Raiders coach Klint Kubiak said Wednesday that Bowers is progressing in his recovery but remains "day-to-day."

Brock Bowers of the Las Vegas Raiders leaves the field after an injury against the Seattle Seahawks in Las Vegas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

His availability remains a question heading into Fernando Mendoza's second NFL start. Having Bowers on the field would give the rookie quarterback a trusted target against another challenging defense.

The Raiders got a painful reminder of his importance during Sunday's loss to the Seahawks.

Bowers injured his ankle on the opening series, briefly returned and then left for the remainder of the game. His departure stripped Mendoza of one of the NFL's best tight ends and allowed Seattle to turn up the defensive pressure without having to account for the Raiders' most dangerous receiving threat.

For a quarterback making his first career start, it made an already difficult assignment considerably tougher.

The Seahawks sacked Mendoza six times, and the Raiders struggled to establish a running game that could ease the burden on their passing attack. Losing Bowers further reduced their options as Seattle took control.

The hope is that he can return against Denver. His absence Wednesday leaves the Raiders waiting to see how his ankle responds as the week progresses.

If Bowers cannot play, Michael Mayer would take on greater importance at tight end, while Tre Tucker and the rest of the Raiders' receivers would face added responsibility to create opportunities for Mendoza.

Brock Bowers #89 and Fernando Mendoza #15 of the Las Vegas Raiders wait to be introduced before a game in Las Vegas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

That means winning individual matchups, giving the quarterback dependable targets and making enough plays to keep Denver from dictating the terms of every possession.

Bowers' value extends beyond the passes he catches. The attention he commands can create room for teammates, and his ability to work different areas of the field gives the Raiders flexibility in how they attack a defense.

Replacing that would require contributions throughout the offense.

Las Vegas Raiders head coach Klint Kubiak walks onto the field for warmups before a game at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. Getty Images

Mayer can provide a target in the middle of the field. Tucker offers speed to threaten downfield. The Raiders will need both elements, along with better protection and a more productive running game, to help Mendoza settle into his first road start.

For now, they will monitor Bowers' ankle while preparing for the possibility of facing the Broncos without their most important receiving weapon.
