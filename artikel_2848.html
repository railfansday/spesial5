Broncos vs. Raiders first quarter recap

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Las Vegas Raiders won the opening coin toss and deferred to bring out Bo Nix and the Denver Broncos offense to start this game.

An opening penalty and back-to-back passes to Jonah Coleman for minimal gain setup a third and 11. They hadn't converted any third downs and over 10 yards on the season. 0/28. That streak ended on the next play with a Nix strike to Pat Bryant for a 21 yard gain and a first down.

They'd face another third down, this time seven. Nix fired a perfect pass deep over the middle to Jaylen Waddle who dropped the pass to bring out the punt unit.

Fernando Mendoza took his first snap on the road in the NFL on the next drive. He quickly found himself in a third and seven where Alex Singleton exploded off through the middle to sack him, but the Raiders called a timeout just before the snap. On the real snap, Mendoza completed a pass outside to Ashton Jeanty who was tackled just shy of the first down marker to force the three-and-out.

The Broncos' blocked the punt with Sai'vion Jones coming up through the middle to block it.

Denver's offense wouldn't do much from there with a shot to Waddle on third down going through his hands again. Wil Lutz would put the Broncos on the board, though.

After an initial first down on the first play of their next drive, the Raiders found themselves in big trouble after a holding call to setup a 2nd and 20. Jonathon Cooper exploded off the edge to sack Mendoza to setup a third and 28. They would be punting on the next play.

Jonah Coleman began to heat up on the next drive with several good runs, then mixed in a Nix completion to Waddle for 18 yards to get into Raiders' territory. The biggest play on the drive came on a third and nine where Nix found Evan Engram who was able to dodge some tacklers to get the first down into field goal range.

Coleman then burst through a wide open hole in the middle for a nine yard gain to get inside the 10 yard line. Adam Prentice then dove forward for a first down to setup a first and goal at the six yard line.
