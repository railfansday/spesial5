Eagles waive CB Jakorian Bennett off of injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Cornerback Jakorian Bennett returned to practice a few weeks ago, but he won't be returning to the Eagles lineup.

The Eagles announced that they waived Bennett off of their injured reserve list on Tuesday. Bennett was on the list due to a knee injury he suffered during training camp and was designated for return, but the Eagles never activated him.

Bennett joined the team in a trade with the Raiders ahead of the 2025 season. He had seven tackles in 11 games last season and the 2023 fourth-rounder also had 58 tackles in 24 games for the Raiders.

The Eagles also announced that they have released wide receiver Quentin Skinner from their practice squad.
