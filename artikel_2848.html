Real Madrid can re-sign former academy graduate for €10m amid Man United, Chelsea and Liverpool interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Several Premier League clubs are monitoring Como defender Jacobo Ramon ahead of the summer 2027 transfer window, but Real Madrid have a significant advantage in the race thanks to a favourable buy-back clause.

According to AS, Manchester United are showing the strongest interest in the former Madrid academy graduate, while Chelsea and Liverpool are also keeping track of his development.

The 21-year-old has enjoyed an impressive campaign under Cesc Fabregas, establishing himself as an important figure in Como's defence.

He has made 14 appearances across all competitions, contributing three goals and two assists, earning him a place in Spain's senior national team squad for the first time.

Despite the growing interest, Ramon is not expected to leave Como during the January transfer window.

Rather, the attention surrounding his performances is instead building towards what could become an important transfer battle next summer.

Manchester United have a strong interest in Jacobo Ramon. (Photo by Valerio Pennicino/Getty Images)

The outlet mentions that Manchester United have been the most 'persistent club' in their attempts to monitor the Spanish defender.

Furthermore, the likes of Chelsea and Liverpool are also understood to have included him in their recruitment plans.

However, the situation remains at an early stage, and Ramon is currently focused on continuing his development in Italy, where regular football under Fabregas has helped him establish himself as one of Serie A's promising young centre-backs.

Real Madrid's previous agreement with Como is quite decisive here.

Notably, when Ramon moved to Italy for approximately €2.5 million, Madrid retained 50%of his economic rights and secured a buy-back option worth around €10 million, something that Real Madrid are reportedly planning for in 2027.

That arrangement gives the Spanish giants considerable control over his future.

Real Madrid have significant control over Jacobo Ramon's future. (Photo by Valerio Pennicino/Getty Images)

Even if Premier League clubs pursue a transfer, Real Madrid could bring him back for a relatively modest fee compared with his current reputation and performances.

For now, Real Madrid are taking a patient approach, with their defensive planning for 2027/28 influencing the decision.

As such, Antonio Rudiger's contract expires in 2027, while Eder Militao, Dean Huijsen, and Ibrahima Konate are expected to remain part of the defensive picture.

As reported before, Ramon is the leading candidate to return if Rudiger departs.

Consequently, Real Madrid currently have little reason to rush into a decision while his development at Como continues to progress.
