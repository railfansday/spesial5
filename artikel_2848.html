Dan Lanning offers update on injured Oregon RB Dierre Hill Jr.

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

All it took was two carries for Oregon Ducks running back Dierre Hill Jr. before he limped to the sidelines, not to return against the Michigan State Spartans on Friday night. The sophomore back briefly entered the injury tent before being helped to the locker room, eventually emerging late in the first half in street clothes and with a boot.

Hill's night ended less than five minutes into the game with two carries for 17 yards.

His injury is concerning for the backfield depth for the Ducks, as fellow starter and second-year standout Jordon Davison is also out injured, having missed his third straight game on Friday after going down against the Northwestern Wildcats on Oct. 31.

After the 45-7 win over the Spartans, head coach Dan Lanning was asked about Hill's status. He revealed that he doesn't anticipate Hill being out long-term, though a follow-up examination by the medical staff is pending.

In Davison's absence against Ohio State and Michigan, Hill filled the role as the bona fide RB1 admirably, recording 39 touches for 253 total yards and three total touchdowns.

With Hill sidelined for roughly the final 55 minutes against Michigan State, the Ducks' running game remained strong thanks to the offensive line creating push up front for much of the night. Brandon Smith was the game's star, as he led the way with 14 carries for 121 yards and two touchdowns, while Da'Jaun Riggs also added 42 yards on nine carries. Fifth-year senior Simeon Price also got in on the action, recording six carries for 16 yards and two touchdowns in his first real action as a Duck.

Though the backfield trio of Smith, Riggs and Price proved to be enough to beat an undermanned Michigan State squad trotting out their third-string quarterback, Hill's availability being in question moving forward is a significant concern. Oregon is currently ranked No. 13 and fighting for the appeasement of the College Football Playoff Committee in order to be included in the final 12-team field.

The committee has proven to factor in injuries into their rankings, as they discluded an undefeated Florida State Seminoles squad in 2023 from the four-team playoff after quarterback Jordan Travis suffered a major ankle injury in the final month of the season. Though Hill is a running back, if he's out for an extended period of time alongside Davison, and the Ducks are already operating with backup quarterback Dylan Raiola instead of Dante Moore, there's a chance it could negatively impact Oregon's CFP chances.
