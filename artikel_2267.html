Celtic team vs Kilmarnock confirmed as Jota starts and winger returns

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Martin O’Neill has named his Celtic starting XI for today's William Hill Premiership clash against Kilmarnock at Celtic Park.

The Hoops return to domestic action following Thursday night’s 3-2 Europa League victory over Besiktas, with O’Neill’s side looking to build more momentum ahead of the international break.

Ahead of today's game, O'Neill has made three changes from the side that won on Thursday night. Because of this, Colby Donovan has replaced penalty sinner Jordan Lotomba in the team.

Jota, having been ruled out of the midweek tie due to ineligibility, makes a start and replaces Sebastian Tounekti. Meanwhile Camilo Duran starts ahead of James Forrest on the right-hand side with Kasper Hogh again leading the line.

Celtic XI: Johnstone, Donovan, CCV, Scales, Tierney, McGregor, Baur, Nygren, Duran, Jota, Hogh.

Subs: Sinisalo, Ralston, Yang, McCowan, Tounekti, Osmand, Oxlade-Chamberlain, Murray, Forrest.

Kick-off for today's game is at 4pm, with the match being shown on CelticPlayer on pay-per-view for UK viewers.
