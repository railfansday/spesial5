Install Checkout 51 if the brands on its weekly list are already in your cart or a participating gas station sits on your route, since each offer pays cash back once you photograph the receipt. Skip it if you mostly buy store brands, because it pays nothing on items outside its offers.

Most offers pay a set amount on a listed product wherever you buy it, and in the U.S. each week’s offers run Sunday through Saturday, so you have to upload the receipt before they expire. The app is free for shoppers in the U.S. and Canada, but only U.S. drivers get its gas offers, at participating stations.

Shoppers who already buy a few of each week’s featured brands, and U.S. drivers who pay by card at a participating station, earn on trips they’d make anyway. Store-brand shoppers earn on every trip with Fetch, which pays points on any paper receipt you scan.
