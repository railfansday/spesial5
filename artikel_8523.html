Personal Finance (Not Investing) • Re: Defined benefit (pension) versus defined contribution

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

IMHO, while the pension is very appealing, 31 years is a long time and if the penalty for retiring at 60 instead of 65 is significant, it probably wipes out most of the benefit.

If this is a job where tenure is a thing, i.e. teaching, and generally you either move jobs in a few years or spend the remainder of your life at a single place, then it might shift the argument a bit. Otherwise, I think the combined risk of changing jobs (voluntarily or involuntarily) or wanting to retire early are too high.

The employer contribution to the defined contribution option is really good. With risks of defined benefit not working out over the next 31 years, I would go with defined contribution.
