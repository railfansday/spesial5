Hailey Bieber’s Night-Out Look Includes Phoebe Philo’s Luxurious “Dancer Jacket” and Levi’s Jeans

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

An It girl wearing an It designer is what fashion dreams are made of, and last night, Hailey Bieber made that vision a reality when she stepped out in New York City wrapped in Phoebe Philo’s luxurious $9,500 “dancer jacket.”

The boxy, cropped jacket is crafted from blue apricot shearling and features a round neck, raised shoulders, and pronounced borders, which all together make for an ultra-chic silhouette. (Of course, it’s currently sold out everywhere.)

Mrs. Bieber expertly styled the fabulous, furry jacket with classic straight-leg blue jeans from Levi’s. The light-wash denim brought the fit back down to earth, while her strappy black heels, silky black clutch, and vintage Gucci glasses in an electric orange rounded out the street-style look. (Our girl can always pull off a high-low moment.)

Other electrifying pops of color came from the Rhode founder’s turquoise mani-pedi and glossy raspberry lip.

Late in the night, the model made her way to the Bumble x Alix Earle party at The Twenty Two New York, keeping her elaborate ballerina bun and large diamond studs from her earlier engagement: the WWD 2026 Beauty Inc. Awards.

At the starry beauty event, Bieber’s Rhode took home the prize for best makeup brand of the year. And naturally, her glam was perfectly glowy and bronzy on the red carpet, where she paired it with a black-and-white slipdress pulled from the Calvin Klein Collection Spring/Summer 2027 runway. Classic pointy black heels completed the minimalist two-tone ensemble. Carolyn Bessette Kennedy would be proud.
