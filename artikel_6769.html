Earthy Neutral Jeans Make Cold-Weather Outfits Feel Instantly Warmer

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

A richer alternative to classic blue denim, from cocoa wide-legs to sage straight-legs.

Listen, we love classic blue denim, but as fashion obsessives, we also believe in changing things up. If you want to drift away from black and indigo this season, consider jeans in earthy neutrals to bring a fresh outlook to your fall and winter wardrobe. The palette runs wide: chocolaty browns, soft sands, light stone blues, olive, rust, sage, and even dusty pink.

In addition to their warmth, what we love about these shades is how easily they work. Think of them as neutrals with more personality: a pair of cocoa wide-legs looks sharp with a cream turtleneck and loafers, while sage straight-legs soften an oversize charcoal blazer. Look for a rich, slightly variable dye and a sturdy cotton twill that will hold its shape and fade gracefully, then style them as you would your favorite blues, with a little more intention and a lot more warmth.

Below, we’ve rounded up some of the best earth-toned jeans to pair with your favorite fall/winter pieces.

These jeans in a cool-toned brown called “Washed Canteen Clean” pair a highly versatile earth-toned shade with a flared hem. Wear them with your favorite purple-toned knit and black ankle boots for a look that feels moody, polished, and effortlessly autumnal.

This relaxed straight-leg silhouette has a vintage-inspired shape that feels thoroughly modern, done in a light, marbled khaki wash.

Along with a wide-leg silhouette, this pair in the shade Great Basin, a hue reminiscent of muddy waters, brings a rich, moody depth to your denim rotation. Style them with a cobalt cable-knit and tan suede boots for a tonal look that feels on-trend and a little rugged.

Drawstring toggles at both hems add a subtle, unexpected sporty detail (hence the name) to a relaxed jean. Team them with your favorite court sneakers for an elevated athletic-leaning look.

In the shade “Tobacco,” these jeans are built to last a lifetime. The wide-leg fit and timeless color will earn a spot in your rotation all year long.

Barrel-leg jeans continue to be popular this season, yet this soft sepia shade trades the trend’s usual denim blue for a softer, warmer, more grounded take. Pair them with a fitted black bodysuit and pointed-toe boots to balance the roomy shape with something sleek.

Something about this shape and earthy shade brings a sense of calm and ease to our hectic everyday lives. It’s the perfect jean for slipping into a relaxed, weekend mindset.

Made of soft-stretch denim, this brown jean features a high-waisted, straight-leg shape that flatters without feeling restrictive. Style it with a tucked-in white tee, a shearling coat, and loafers for a polished, easy outfit that works from the office to the weekend.

A barrel leg in a vintage-inspired brown wash gives these jeans a lived-in, worn-in feel from the very first wear. Style them with a slim ribbed tank, a cropped leather jacket, and sling-back flats to keep the roomy shape looking sharp.

When you think of an everyday jean, perhaps the color army green doesn’t immediately come to mind. But once you see how versatile this shade can be, it’s determined to win a permanent spot in your rotation.

This slim palazzo jean brings ’70s vibes and softness to a fall color palette. Wear it with a rust-colored turtleneck, a tan suede jacket, and platform boots for a retro-leaning outfit that feels warm, confident, and distinctly seasonal.
