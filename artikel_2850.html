Five Good Texans questions with Stampede Blue

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Chris Shepherd from Stampede Blue has been a good friend in the industry over the years and both of us had a huge challenge on our hands. We had two bad football teams going up against each other in prime time. That makes talking about these teams tough, but both of us have been here before and we are professionals, so we will make it work again. It is always a pleasure catching up with him. Our focus was more about the future of these two teams than what is going on right now on the field.

Battle Red Blog: Both teams are having disappointing seasons so we both have questions to answer, but what is the big picture future for the Colts? Are there any good GM candidates? Do you think Steichen sticks around?

Chris Shepherd: Big picture we're looking at a full teardown. The Colts don't have a good pass rushing edge defender, it's high paid cornerbacks aren't living up to their checks or the hype, they refuse to get their highly drafted and very good tight end involved in the passing game, their WR1 is unproven in that role (at least he's an elite deep threat) and has barely played so far this season, they seem to be putting a lot of stock in a 27 (soon to be 28) year old running back who's having a down year (for him), and most importantly they don't have a quarterback who should be starting for an NFL team in 2026 or 2027, nor do they have a first round pick to try to find one. Short of a brilliant stroke of luck, something the Colts haven't really had for more than a decade, good times seem to be several years away.

There are a multitude of good GM candidates on paper. It's tough to know from the outside who the real options will be but at this juncture my personal favorite is Catherine Hickman, assistant GM of the Cleveland Browns. Her resume is fairly short but she has excelled in every football job she has had as evidenced by her constant promotions. She seems to be a unique individual and the team that hires her may be getting a special GM. Also, I just want to watch Carlie Irsay-Gordon to hire the NFL's first female GM just to watch the idiots react to it on Twitter.

Shane has, I'd estimate, about a 40% chance of returning next year. Which is higher than any Colts fan would like but I do see a world where he's allowed to return to eat up losses in a year that we should all expect to be pretty rough.

BRB: Neither team is a realistic playoff team. What are you looking for for the remainder of the season?

CS: Continued development from the team's young players. As of today it seems that Chris Ballard's final draft class with Indy might have been a good one. Offensively left guard Jalen Farmer has had moments, both very good and quite bad, with continued to play I hope to see more of the former. Running back Seth McGowan is eating into Jonathan Taylor's carries and seems to have been an absolute steal in the seventh round. Linebackers CJ Allen and Bryce Boettcher have both looked good. Allen, quite good, Boettcher still has some things to figure out but he is an excellent athlete for the position. Safety AJ Halucy, has made a few splash plays here and there but hopefully with more time on the field his level of play will improve.

BRB: Are you surprised Jonathan Taylor wasn't dealt? Do you think Daniel Jones is still the QB in September of 2027?

CS: Taylor? No. After Indy traded Quenton Nelson and DeForrest Buckner, and based on other comments made by ownership in the recent past, it seemed to me like Indy was willing to deal players on expiring contracts, guys who were unlikely to want to re-sign with the team. My belief was that they were setting up the next general manager with as much ammo as possible without making any decisions for players under contract in 2027, on their behalf. The next GM will come in on day one and instead of having to try to decide to who to keep and who to let walk for nothing and then negotiating contracts accordingly, they'll have the ability to hit the ground running with a bevy of day two and three picks to try to stock the roster with as much young talent as possible.

I don't think DJ will be back, but stranger things have happened to the Colts in the last decade (Head Coach Jeff Saturday, comes to mind) so I wouldn't rule it out.

BRB: Looking towards March and April, what spots do you see the Colts targeting to get a quick turnaround?

CS: The Colts haven't had anyone record 10+ sacks in a season since Justin Houston in 2019, Indy has to use significant resources to solve their pass rushing issues if they want any hope of having any success before 2028. Beyond that, I see them investing in the draft at wide receiver as they're currently starting three guys who might otherwise be on practice squads if not for Indy's terrible receiving corps. Also, any time a new GM comes in, rosters experience massive turnover so it's tough to predict at this juncture.

BRB: FanDuel opened with the Texans being favored by ten. How do you see the game going? Are there any prop bets you feel comfortable recommending?

CS: Ten points seems about right. We're now two weeks beyond the trade deadline distractions and Indy played hard a week ago in Miami. I expect them to play hard and keep things relatively close (two scores is close for this team). Indy will try to run the ball with Taylor and McGowan and then dial up passes that Daniel Jones will look completely lost trying to throw. He's good for at least one pick a game and if somehow he has the ball at the end of the game with a chance to drive down the field for a tie or a win, you can go ahead and bank on another interception.

If Nico Collins' receiving yardage over/under is sub 200 yards, you should definitely take the over.

We want to thank Chris again for another great season of five questions. He is one of the more fun people in this industry to catch up with. The Colts are obviously in next year mode and the Texans are teetering on the verge of getting there. We will see if the action tonight throws them over the edge. We want to wish Chris and the Colts the best of luck for the remainder of the season. As per usual, we hope that luck begins on Friday morning.
