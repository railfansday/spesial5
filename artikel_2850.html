DaRon Bland remained out of practice Thursday, but Joey Porter Jr. again was a full participant

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Cowboys cornerback DaRon Bland (ankle) did not participate in practice for a second consecutive day Thursday, but cornerback Joey Porter Jr. (ankle/foot) was again a full participant.

Right guard Tyler Booker (ankle), tight end Jake Ferguson (knee/ankle), safety P.J. Locke III (concussion), wide receiver Jonathan Mingo (ankle), cornerback Shavon Revel Jr. (illness) and tight end Luke Schoonmaker (illness) were other non-participants. Revel and Schoonmaker were new additions to the injury report.

Outside linebacker Donovan Ezeiruaku (ankle) returned to practice as a limited participant.

Safeties Juanyeh Thomas (hamstring) and Jalen Thompson (hamstring) were full participants Thursday, a day after being limited. Thompson is in his 21-day return-to-practice window.
