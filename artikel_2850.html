Is Jordyn Tyson playing today? Injury update, start-sit advice for Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The New Orleans Saints might finally have the services of first-round pick and wide receiver Jordyn Tyson for the Week 9 game against the Cleveland Browns.

Tyson has been on the shelf for the entire season because of a hamstring injury.

However, he was designated to return from injured reserve in Week 9 and after logging three limited practices during the week, Tyson was labeled as questionable to play.

Now, as we turn the page to Sunday, here's the latest on Tyson's chances of making his NFL debut against the Browns.

New Orleans Saints wide receiver Jordyn Tyson (0) during training camp at Ochsner Sports Performance Center. Credit: Stephen Lew-Imagn Images via Reuters Connect (Reuters)

The expectation is Tyson will play in his first NFL game on Sunday afternoon.

The Saints announced on Saturday that Tyson has been activated off injured reserve, which was the final step he needed to take in order to be cleared to play.

While Tyson still has the questionable tag, NFL insider Jordan Schultz reported that Tyson is expected to play.

"Sources: Saints rookie WR Jordyn Tyson plans to make his NFL debut Sunday vs the Browns," Schultz stated. "Tyson, the No. 8 overall pick, has yet to play this season due to a hamstring, but 'should be good to go' for New Orleans."

The final confirmation for Tyson's status will come down at 11:30 a.m. ET, when the Saints put out their list of inactives before kickoff.

New Orleans Saints wide receiver Jordyn Tyson (0) during training camp at Ochsner Sports Performance Center. Credit: Stephen Lew-Imagn Images via Reuters Connect (Reuters)

Injury concerns not withstanding, Tyson entered the league with plenty of hype, as many believed he was the best wide receiver in the 2026 NFL Draft.

While that may end up being true, there is nothing to go on when it comes to what to expect from Tyson in his debut since he has never taken a snap in the league, either in the preseason or the regular season.

The Browns present a pretty tough matchup, with Cleveland ceding the 13th-fewest fantasy points per game to wide receiver, according to CBS Sports.

We also know Tyson is going to be, at best, second fiddle to Chris Olave, and he may even be the third option with Devaughn Vele's emergence.

Another concern is Tyson may not see a full workload in his first game, especially because of his extended absence.

Tyson should definitely be rostered, but there are just too many unknowns to insert him into lineups this week.
