Cowboys news: Jerry Jones says Dallas is a ‘bad team’

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Dallas Cowboys haven't been consistent when it comes to playing well as a team, and Jerry Jones made some very direct comments speaking to just that. Mike Pettine will takeover the defensive play calls from Christian Parker, but he insists that they're working side by side to get things turned around.

The safety position in Dallas has been ravaged by injuries with free agent addition Jalen Thompson missing the most time. He hasn't payed in nearly two months, but that could change in Week 11.

Team owner Jerry Jones was brutally honest when describing the Cowboys, and feels the recent move to take play-calling duties from defensive coordinator Christian Parker will benefit the team.

"We have not been a good team and are a bad team," Jones said Friday on 105.3 The Fan in Dallas, via Yahoo Sports. "At 4-6, that says everything you need to say. … Does that mean we will play a bad game [and] be a bad team on Sunday? Not necessarily. And that's why we're trying to improve."

One way the Cowboys tried to improve their ailing defense this week was to strip first-year defensive coordinator Christian Parker of his play-calling duties and give them to senior defensive assistant Mike Pettine.

Going forward, Mike Pettine will call the plays for the defense in Dallas. It was unexpected development, but he and Christian Parker are on the same page.

"[Parker] and I spoke about it, but it's really since then been business as usual," Pettine said. "I think you see his character and how he's handled this with poise and professionalism. I had a ton of respect for him before, I have even more now. This is not an easy situation, but he knows as I know having been in the NFL, this is a tough sport for tough people. But it's something, you know, the bottom line is production on Sunday. That's been our focus since the move was made, and we've been joined at the hip putting a plan together and getting these guys ready to go."

Safety Jalen Thompson hasn't played since the end of September. He has a chance to get back on the field this Sunday.

Cowboys safety Jalen Thompson was listed as questionable on the final practice report of the week. Thompson is still on injured reserve, though the Cowboys opened his 21-day practice window Wednesday. He's moved around well in the three practices since.

Thompson hasn't played since suffering a hamstring injury in Week 3 against the Baltimore Ravens in Rio de Janeiro. Cowboys head coach Brian Schottenheimer said recently that Thompson hadn't had a setback, in regard to a question about the lengthy recovery, and that the injury was a serious one.

Mike Pettine will make the defensive calls going forward. However, according to Brian Schottenheimer, his role in Dallas after this season is unknown.

Head coach Brian Schottenheimer was a guest on 105.3 The Fan on Friday and was asked if Pettine would be open to taking on the defensive coordinator role after this season.

Schottenheimer made it clear he hasn't even thought about that yet, which leaves Pettine's future uncertain.

"I haven't even thought that far yet," Schottenheimer said.

Linebacker Shemar James dives into what it will be like hearing the defensive calls from Mike Pettine instead of Christian Parker.

"Yeah, pretty much the only difference is just the voices in the head, in the headphones," he said. " Instead of C.P., it's going to be Mike, and it's pretty similar play calls and things of that nature. So it's really not a big difference."

Players and coaches alike have pointed out that it is still Parker's scheme and playbook, seeing as he'll remain defensive coordinator heading into Week 11 against the Tennessee Titans.
