76ers rule out Jaylen Brown (hamstring), list Joel Embiid (illness) as questionable for Friday's game vs. Pacers

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Philadelphia 76ers could be short-staffed when they take on the Indiana Pacers in NBA Cup play on Friday night.

Shooting guard Jaylen Brown has already been ruled out with a hamstring injury. Center Joel Embiid is listed as questionable due to a lingering illness that has sidelined him for Philadelphia's last two games.

Brown picked up the hamstring issue in Wednesday's NBA Cup win over the Miami Heat. He left the game late in the first half and did not return. The guard missed two games last week with a left hamstring strain, but returned to action against the Portland Trail Blazers and played 29 minutes.

Toward the end of the first half, the Philadelphia guard reached toward his left hamstring and shook his head before checking himself out of the game.

Embiid is dealing with an illness, though not much has been released about what symptoms he may be dealing with. Regardless, he has missed two consecutive games. The center was listed as questionable for Wednesday's game against the Heat, but was downgraded before the game.

With no Embiid and no Brown for a half, LeBron James and Tyrese Maxey took over and led the 76ers to a win over the Heat on Wednesday. James logged his first triple-double as a 76er with 25 points, 10 rebounds and 12 assists. Maxey added 30 points, 4 rebounds and 6 assists.

The win moved Philadelphia to 15-4. Friday's game against Indiana is the front end of a back-to-back, and a win would help the 76ers clinch their NBA Cup group.
