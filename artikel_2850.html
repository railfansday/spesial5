NBA changes start time for upcoming Cavs game

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The NBA has announced that the November 29th matchup between the Cleveland Cavaliers and Miami Heat will move from 6:00 PM EST to 3:00 PM EST, with no specific reason given. However, given the other sports news announcement in the city, one could assume it is to accommodate the Cleveland Browns game, which was flexed to 8:30 on NBC.

The Browns will now play host to the visiting Las Vegas Raiders on Sunday Night Football, back at its original start time. Now, it would be feasible for fans to double up and attend both games.

This is the second of three games this season between the Cavs and Heat, with the most recent matchup being a 133-121 loss on Nov. 13. Bam Adebayo and Giannis Antetokounmpo both scored 37 points for the Heat in that game, which the Cavs played without Evan Mobley. Donovan Mitchell led the Cavs in scoring with 25 points, but the interior defense (and defense, as a whole) was the main topic of discussion.

The Cavs and Heat are jockeying for position in the highly competitive Eastern Conference, with both teams going through ups and downs. Cleveland has endured injuries and defensive lapses of late, yet has secured two quality wins against the Minnesota Timberwolves and on the road against the New York Knicks. Miami started the season exceptionally cold, but has caught fire of late as winners of six in a row at the time of this writing. It is a safe assumption that these two teams will be close in the standings throughout the season.
