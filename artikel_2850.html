The Hornets’ collapse against Lakers was so bizarre fans are crying conspiracy

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Charlotte Hornets were always going to be in for a strange season after selling low on LaMelo Ball over the summer. Training camp injuries to top players Kon Knueppel and Coby White put Charlotte in an even more precarious position entering the season, and it didn't take long for star wing Brandon Miller to suffer his own groin and hip strains. The Hornets knew they were taking a step back this year after trading Ball, but they still had a chance to earn a statement win on Tuesday night against the Lakers.

Instead, Charlotte's late-game choke job feels like a new low for this season, and had NBA fans looking for conspiracy theories to explain exactly what happened. The Lakers beat the Hornets, 118-114, in one of the strangest games of the 2026-27 campaign so far.

The Hornets led for most of the night, and built an 18-point lead at one point in the second half. It all came crashing down late in the fourth quarter after Miller turned the ball over and White missed a free throw, which allowed Quentin Grimes to splash a game-tying three and send the game to overtime. Things really started to get weird in the extra session.

The Hornets were down one with the ball with 18 seconds left. Charlotte head coach Charles Lee threw out a lineup that couldn't even get the ball in. Christian Anderson, Sion James, Royce O'Neale, Liam McNeeley, and Grant Williams took the floor for the most important possession of the game, with White, Miller, and Naz Reid nowhere to be seen. Grimes broke up the pass, earned the Lakers the possession, and eventually the win.

Lee said after the game that White and Miller were on a minutes restriction, which prevented them from playing the last 18 seconds.

"As they've been coming back from injury, we've been trying to build them up … you're always tempted as competitor … but we have a big picture in mind and it's a long year."

What's especially odd is that McNeeley had not played a second of the game before that possession, while Anderson only played three minutes back in the third quarter.

The Hornets must really trust their team doctors to avoid playing White and Miller due to a minutes restriction when they really needed them. Lee has always been a tremendous head coach in my eyes, but this is an example of when he could have used another voice in the huddle to preach common sense.

It's not like the Hornets were trying to lose on purpose, because there's no value to tanking this year after the NBA's lottery reform rules when into place. That still didn't stop fans watching the game from trying to explain Lee's decision-making with gambling, analytics, or anything else.

This game had extra attention on it because it was broadcast nationally on Peacock/NBC, and because the late tip time made it an "island game" with no competition for night owls. The Hornets probably wish so many people weren't paying attention this morning. Charlotte has been terrible late in games all year, and this is just another example.

Letting Quentin Grimes score 47 points on you is humiliating enough. Losing a game like that is even worse.
