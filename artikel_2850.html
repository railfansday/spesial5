Bulldog Business: Gonzaga/New Mexico State Recap, Women’s Basketball Bounce Back

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

In Episode 6 of Bulldog Business, Kyle Sweeney and Josh Gilchrist recap Gonzaga men's basketball's 109-68 win versus New Mexico State at the McCarthey Athletic Center this past Saturday and preview their upcoming game against LSU in the Spokane Arena on Nov. 14.

They then talk about the women's basketball team's turnaround yesterday, beating UC Riverside 85-58 inside the Kennel, after a tough loss versus Idaho 86-75 at home this past Friday.
