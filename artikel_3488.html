Shonen Jump's New Final Villain Transformation Is The Best In 15 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

WARNING: SPOILERS FOR ONE PIECE MANGA CHAPTER #1198Most of Shōnen Jump's best manga feature incredible character designs, especially for their final-arc villains, and author Eiichiro Oda has delivered arguably the most intimidating antagonist the medium has seen in years. Beloved series like Dragon Ball, Naruto, and Jujutsu Kaisen each gained considerable fan bases worldwide during their years of publication. Those stories introduced progressively intense villains, but as One Piece approaches its final arc, it's delivered in ways that others have not.

One Piece’s manga began its long journey almost 30 years ago with chapters released weekly on Shōnen Jump. Since then, the rate of its releases has changed, and so has the quality of its storytelling. Few series earn the chance to continue their tales in the highly competitive publication, but even fewer manage to grow as One Piece has. After 33 story arcs, the series has managed not only to avoid becoming stale, but it continues to grow an audience at a rate that's beaten Dragon Ball for years. And it's not done yet.

One Piece is one of the most iconic, beloved, and long-running anime and manga ever. In all of its history, what are the best arcs in the story?

The ongoing joke is that One Piece will never end, but author Eiichiro Oda declared that Luffy's story has reached its final saga. Events that have transpired during the Egghead and Elbaph arcs have delivered several extremely satisfying plot twists the epic has teased for years, with one of my personal favorites being the reveal of the secret King of the World, Saint Nerona Imu. A shadowy, otherworldly figure who has taken on a new form that oozes final boss aura.

In One Piece chapter #1198, the Straw Hat Pirates and their Giant allies are completely overshadowed by Imu's latest transformation that towers far above the clouds of the Elbaph, the Giants' homeland. When the mysterious ruler of the World Government first arrived on the island, his overwhelming presence and Haki could be felt across the entire landmass, and even knocked out many with his aura alone. While Imu's first form resembles an imposing devil, his latest transformation makes him look more like a colossal divine being of punishment and a mix of something like Bleach's Arrancar and Neon Genesis Evangelion's Evas.

Anime has exploded in popularity recently, so it's natural to wonder what series in the medium rank among the best to watch all the way through.

Imu's new form far surpasses Loki in size, which is saying something because he's one of the largest Giants in One Piece’s history. The secret ruler is now clad in a long white robe, covering his dark skin while huge black rings covered in eyes rotate around his horned figure. Huge black wings also surround Imu, who is shockingly fast for his colossal size, as displayed by his quick dispatching of Loki's new Hybrid Dragon form. The villain has shifted their size before, but this is easily one of, if not the largest characters in One Piece's world.

According to Usopp, Imu's current overwhelming appearance looks like "It's the end of the damn world!" and it's tough to disagree with him. Although Imu states in chapter #1198 that his current power requires too heavy a toll on him to last much longer, it's difficult to see how any of One Piece's strongest characters could do anything against the figure. But Luffy's brand-new beast Nika transformation is sure to provide some surprises for both readers and Imu. But there is no denying that One Piece has reached a scale worthy of the best final arcs that Shonen can provide.

As a huge fan of the Naruto series, I am no stranger to incredible villain introductions, with Madara's assault against the Allied Shinobi Alliance being a personal favorite. Dragon Ball, Jujutsu Kaisen, and My Hero Academia have all provided top-tier moments that have made their antagonists intimidating and badass at the same time. Still, the levels of shock and dread that Eiichiro Oda has been able to deliver with his villains are something to behold, and worthy of taking notes on for aspiring storytellers.

The way that One Piece has gradually built up its world, mysteries, and characters ranks among the best that fiction can provide. The World Government's shadow leader, Saint Nerona Imu, is a prime example of all three of those important storytelling facets. A balance that very few of the world’s most popular stories accomplish with their antagonists.

For years, it was assumed that the World Government's darkest deeds were organized by the mysterious Five Elders, but everything was flipped on its head in chapter #908 when the Elders were shown bowing to Imu's silhouette as he sat on the World Government's symbolic Empty Throne. It took over 20 years for the secret ruler of One Piece's World Government to enter the story, and chapter #1198 felt like the first real look at the demonic ruler’s full majesty.

It’s unlikely that any other series will be able to build up to such a moment that One Piece has pulled off with Imu’s assault on Elbaph. But what’s even more exciting is that Oda has yet to reach the climax of his story, and readers are likely in for many more surprises in the last couple of years of One Piece’s publication.
