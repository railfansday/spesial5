The Worst Show of 2025 Officially Returns for Season 2 Tomorrow

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Every year brings new shows that enter the conversation for different reasons. Last year had hits like The Pitt, Adolescence, Overcompensating, and more. Critics praised these shows for their realism and entertainment value, and some, like The Pitt, have become cultural phenomena. Then there are other shows; while not particularly exceptional, they also have their place. The other category is those that are panned almost universally, and in 2025, Hulu released one.

Part of what made it so disappointing was that a seasoned Hollywood writer-director was involved, with an all-star cast. Critics called it "too awful to love, too boring to war over," giving the first season a mere 6% on Rotten Tomatoes. But despite the criticism, All's Fair was a hit with fans, managing a 65% audience score and peaking at number one on Hulu and Disney+ during its run. Now, Kim Kardashian (Allura), Glenn Close (Dina), Niecy Nash-Betts (Emerald), Naomi Watts (Liberty), Teyana Taylor (Milan), and Sarah Paulson (Carrington) are back for a second season of the Ryan Murphy show, which premieres tomorrow.

The firm is ready to take on more divorces, and if the has-been couples are messy, the lawyers are messier. In the three-episode premiere, Allura throws an extravagant ball, but an unwanted crasher threatens to spark ire. Later, they accompany Dina to scatter her late husband's ashes and encounter some trouble. Allura also has a hot new bodyguard who might not be who he seems. Thanksgiving this year will have to wait as the ladies handle a case that keeps them at the firm, but drama unfolds as secrets come to light. All's Fair Season 2 premieres on Hulu on Wednesday, November 04.

Four love stories, four completely different ideas of what it means to be somebody's person. This is the one that matches how you actually fall, and what it would ask of you.

You do not fall for strangers. You fall for the person you have already been talking to for years, which is the slowest and by some distance the most durable way anybody has found to do this.

You want a partner, not an audience. Somebody who wants their own life as badly as you want yours, and who would never ask you to be smaller to make the relationship easier.

The intensity was never the problem. The secrecy is. You are drawn to a love that lives entirely in private, and to the particular ache of wanting somebody you are not supposed to want.

You do not do this cautiously. When it happens for you it is immediate and total and it rearranges everything, and you would not accept a smaller version of it.

The show's entertainment value is polarizing and largely depends on an individual's taste. Collider's Taylor Gates said the show is "awful" and "should land Ryan Murphy in TV jail," while Sophie Gilbert called it "Instagram Reels at episode length" while reviewing for The Atlantic. Angie Han of The Hollywood Reporter singled out Kardashian's performance as "stiff and affectless without a single authentic note," noting that she was mirroring the writing. So don't expect Suits or The Good Wife. However, if you love flashy, fashion-forward shows with campy acting, then All's Fair might be for you. Many viewers like it because it doesn't take itself too seriously, and if Paulson wants to jump on a table and scream her lungs out, it will let her.
