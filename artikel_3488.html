George R. R. Martin Officially Picks Dunk & Egg Over ‘Winds of Winter’

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Waiting for George R. R. Martin to finish The Winds of Winter has become something of a tradition for Game of Thrones fans. It's been more than 15 years since A Dance with Dragons arrived in bookstores, and despite countless updates, the sixth installment of A Song of Ice and Fire still doesn't have a release date. That hasn't stopped Martin from expanding Westeros elsewhere, of course, with House of the Dragon and A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms introducing audiences to different generations of the Seven Kingdoms. And it turns out the author has been spending some of his time working on another story that should make fans of the latter particularly happy.

Martin is officially working on a fourth Dunk and Egg novella, according to A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms showrunner Ira Parker, even as fans continue waiting for The Winds of Winter. Speaking with THR, Parker confirmed that Martin has been developing another adventure featuring Ser Duncan the Tall and his young squire, Egg.

Book four, which George is working on, takes place in a very cool location.

Although Parker didn't identify the setting, Martin previously revealed that the fourth novella would take place at Winterfell. In a 2014 blog post, he explained that the story would involve a collection of formidable Stark women known as the She-Wolves of Winterfell.

The news also arrives as HBO continues developing A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, which stars Peter Claffey (Bad Sisters) as Ser Duncan the Tall and Dexter Sol Ansell (The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes) as Egg. Parker has also said that it's the aim of the production team to follow the characters across several stages of their lives, potentially allowing the younger cast members to age naturally, so they don't need to recast. Which is only fair, because Ansell is wonderful in his role as Egg.

Five is the last story point of Egg as a boy. So it's a very natural ending place for us to take a break and come back when Dexter is older. So then we can do Egg the Prince, do some politics, do some fun stuff in Summerhall and King's Landing, and get a different vibe where they're not on the road all the time. So if the show wraps and people are still OK with everything we're doing and there's still an appetite, let the show evolve along with our leads, with myself and with the rest of our creative team as we grow older and the world changes and we want to say different things. I think that would be a lovely way to tell a story about these two guys through the course of their lives.

And when asked whether he would consider recasting Egg with an older performer, Parker made his preference clear. "No. Dexter is our Egg. Or, at least, Dexter is my Egg — I have no controlling interest in this property. But Dexter and Peter are my guys."
