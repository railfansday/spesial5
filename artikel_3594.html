Capcom’s Live-Service Dino Shooter Is Going Offline Forever Just Four Years Later

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Capcom’s live-service dino shooter Exoprimal officially has an end date. Servers will go offline for good in September 2027, just a little over four years after it originally launched day-and-date with Game Pass. You can still buy it for $60, for some reason.

“We regret to announce that service for Exoprimal will be discontinued as of 8:00 PM (PDT) on Wednesday, September 29, 2027,” the Resident Evil publisher announced this week. “We cannot thank you enough for the love and support you have shown over the years, and we sincerely apologize for the gravity of this announcement.”

The end of service won’t come as a shock to anyone who’s been following the game’s trajectory. Launched in July 2023, it was an early comer to the wave of post-covid online multiplayer games that have all quickly been abandoned in the years since. Capcom pulled the plug on additional content updates just 12 months into the game’s life.

Premium passes and microtransactions were still being sold, however. That all ended this week on November 24 alongside the end-of-service announcement. The game itself and all its DLC will be delisted from consoles and PC on March 30, 2027. Those who already purchased the game will still be able to re-download it and play until the servers go offline.

That is unlikely to be many people. The shooter peaked at eight concurrent players on Steam today. While it had a unique sensibility and some cool horde mechanics, it wasn’t the spiritual Dino Crisis revival most fans had hoped for. Instead, it ended up being another example of the industry’s wasted efforts to cash in on the latest gaming fad, and further proof of all the things the Stop Killing Games crowd has been warning people about.
