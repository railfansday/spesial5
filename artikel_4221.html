GTA 6 Easter Egg Has One NPC Work Out The Game’s Biggest Secret

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Scott Baird is a Contributor from the UK with over a decade of video game writing experience.

The Grand Theft Auto 6 Easter Eggs just keep coming, with the latest involving an NPC who discovered the game’s biggest secret, but vanished before learning the true extent of the conspiracy in their world.

All the Grand Theft Auto games involve massive, sprawling cities, meaning there’s plenty of places for hidden secrets to be stashed. Even Grand Theft Auto 5 still had secrets being uncovered years after launch, with players gradually peeling away the secrets of its UFO sightings.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is no exception when it comes to weird and wonderful secrets, with players finding them within its first week. One of the freakiest discoveries has been chronicled by The Easter Egg Hunter YouTube channel: a mysterious house that has more to it than meets the eye.

Like many games, Grand Theft Auto 6 is full of easter eggs for you to discover. These are some of the best Leonida has to offer.

The secret house in Grand Theft Auto 6 actually dates back to Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas Definitive Edition, which added a picture of a mysterious home to one of the bars. Fans discovered that this house exists in Grand Theft Auto 6, in Belville near the mission where Lucia has to deliver vapes.

If the player approaches the house and opens the garage, they’ll find numerous conspiracy theory messages written by the owner, who has discovered the world’s secret: that it’s all just a video game, and he’s an NPC.

The NPC discovered walk cycles for other people, that killing dogs causes them to respawn, that glitches regularly create freakish people, and that most of the doors in the world don’t work. He also realized that Jason and Lucia were the only people with true freedom, though he couldn’t contact them.

The NPC also realized that there are no children in the world of Grand Theft Auto 6. This is due to most real-world ratings boards treating violence against kids as a good reason to ban or censor media, which is why most open-world crime games don’t have them.

The fate of the NPC is unknown, but he appears to have tried to escape from the world of Grand Theft Auto 6. He may return in a future update, much like how his house appeared in San Andreas: Definitive Edition, or maybe he’s still in the game and players haven’t found him yet.

The concept of video game characters learning that they’re in a simulacrum of life isn’t a new one. Characters have been commenting on game mechanics for decades, with many pointing to the original Metal Gear Solid as a prime example, with the barrier between fiction and reality eroding over the course of the series.

Indeed, Grand Theft Auto 6 isn’t even the most prominent example of video game characters learning the truth of their existence and trying to escape. One of the routes in Deltarune Chapter 5 ends with the characters attempting to break out of bounds, though the result won’t be revealed for a few more years.

It’s amazing just how many secrets are packed into Grand Theft Auto 6, with fans already discovering the portal back to the PS2 era and a reference to the Red Hulk from Marvel Comics. One can only wonder what crazy mysteries are still hiding beneath the surface of Vice City, and what else will be added when the game’s inevitable online modes arrive in the future.

Even with the many easter eggs in GTA 6, you wouldn't expect time travel to be one of them. But you can do it if you're persistent enough to follow a meticulously planned order of steps.
