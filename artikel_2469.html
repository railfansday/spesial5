Three takeaways from KC’s extra time quarterfinal win over LA

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

In a defensive battle, a healthy Current brought most of the game’s offense

The Kansas City Current beat Angel City 1-0 in the National Women’s Soccer League’s second quarterfinal matchup. It was a defensive chess match, ultimately decided by a Michelle Cooper penalty kick in the first period of extra time.

Heading into this match, all eyes were on the goals-for column. Most previews pointed out that these two teams led the league with 54 goals apiece. To me, though, that wasn’t exactly noteworthy. Newcomer Denver notched 53 goals of their own, North Carolina scored 52 times and Portland managed 50 tallies.

What I found more intriguing was the defensive matchup. Angel City’s backline was one of the best in the league this season. They held opponents scoreless 10 times and were a huge reason this team closed the regular season on a 10-game unbeaten run. They also finished with the third-best expected goals (xG) against in the league (36.02, per American Soccer Analysis), behind only Washington and San Diego.
