"I've been in Germany for a while..." – Upamecano responds to speculation over Bayern future

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Although Dayot Upamecano extended his contract with Bayern Munich until 2030 earlier this year, speculation surrounding his future has persisted, with reports claiming that his deal includes a €65 million release clause.

The French centre-back was asked about the speculation at a press conference on Wednesday.

“I've been in Germany for a while,” said Upamecano, who joined RB Leipzig from RB Salzburg in 2017 before making the move to Bayern Munich four years later.

“At the moment I feel very very good. I'm still progressing. We'll see what the future holds, but at the moment I feel very good at my club,” he continued.

The 28-year-old was also asked about his tendency to push forward from defence and play an increasingly prominent role in Bayern's attacking moves.

“I'd say the context of the games made me become Harry Kane at times,” he said with a laugh, via iMiaSanMia.

“I used to play in midfield when I was younger at Strasbourg. It's part of my game to go forward when an opportunity arises. I'm not afraid to do so.”
