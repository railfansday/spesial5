charred wooden modules conceal six-sided mirror chamber inside interactive play installation

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

alchemy is a two-module free-play installation that guides children and adults through dark inflatable chambers, a six-sided mirror room, and a pink recycled foam space.

Florian López edited by christina vergopoulou November 26, 2026 3 min

Designed by Florian López, Alchemy is an interactive free-play installation for children and adults that organizes an exhibition space as an anatomical body to be traversed. The structure comprises two architectural modules linked by tubular conduits and clad on the exterior in timber treated with the Japanese yakisugi charring technique, creating a blackened, fissured surface that functions as a protective outer skin. Circular openings cut into the charred facades act as physical thresholds into a three-stage interior sequence.

The first module begins in darkness, housing black inflatable volumes shaped after intestinal forms and kept in continuous motion by a steady airflow to create a soft, enveloping environment. From there, visitors pass into a six-sided mirrored chamber that multiplies reflections across all surfaces, shifting perspective and spatial awareness.

Crossing into the second module, the designer shifts the interior from individual perception to collaborative assembly. Lined with pink recycled rebonded foam referencing internal flesh and muscle tissue, the room is stocked with loose geometric blocks without prescriptive instructions, inviting children and adults to negotiate and build shared structures through open-ended play.
