sprouted earth and soil spill across gallery for landscape of future memories installation

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

landscape of future memories explores the invisible landscape of memory, creating an ephemeral space where past experiences, future memories, and the traces of a place converge.

Mikhail Kozlov edited by christina vergopoulou November 10, 2026 3 min

Conceived by landscape architecture and design practice Buro Zemla, Landscape of Future Memories is a site-specific installation presented within the A-House gallery space as part of the exhibition Architecture 1.0. The project examines the relationship between physical topography, spatial perception, and memory retention, framing architecture as a medium that shapes emotional and mental cartographies beyond physical permanence.

The installation by design practice Buro Zemla constructs an ephemeral interior landscape centered on the continuity of raw earth and soil. Operating on the premise that architectural programming often functions as an invisible sensory script, the design tests how an environment can leave residual traces in an occupant’s cognitive map long after the physical boundaries cease to exist. Through targeted spatial framing, layered materials, and communal sightlines, the project unites visitors around the memory of the soil, examining how shared spatial narratives can persist across time as collective mental landscapes.
