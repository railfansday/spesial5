HOFF transforms its madrid flagship with a festive explosion of color by studio wanda

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

inside its flexible 600 m² flagship on madrid’s velázquez street, hoff merges footwear retail, specialty coffee, and art with a sculpted holiday installation by studio wanda.

mariana rodrigues November 24, 2026 4 min

HOFF opened a 600 m² flagship on Madrid’s Velázquez street, and it turns out they are providing way more than just sneakers. Built by architecture studio El Departamento to shift on demand, the fluid layout brings together a retail showroom, gallery, and café designed for rotating exhibitions, workshops, and community meetups. Its latest transformation arrives just in time for the holidays, as Barcelona-based Studio Wanda steps in with an immersive, custom Christmas installation named ‘Velvet Firebloom’. Conceived as a reflection of HOFF’s ongoing exploration of color, materiality, and creative expression, the intervention turns the flagship into an evolving space for design collaborations and cultural experiences.

The flagship guides visitors through a series of rooms that open into a central gathering hub. It captures the brand‘s vision for a dynamic retail concept where shopping, art installations, food, and cultural events naturally collide, creating a platform for creative expression that steps outside the shoebox. Inside, travertine marble, custom furniture, and theatrical spotlights team up to treat sneakers like fine art.

Titled ‘Velvet Firebloom’, the holiday takeover by Studio Wanda freezes the split-second pop of a firework mid-air, sparing visitors the loud bang while keeping all the visual spectacle. Working under the concept of Explosion de Color, the studio unfolded sharp geometric lines that erupt outward from a single focal point, blooming into sculpted paper planes that animate the entire room with tactile gradients. This rush of folded paper spills straight out toward Madrid’s streets, transforming HOFF’s storefront into an uninterrupted sculpture.

Inside, the chromatic burst weaves naturally through the entrance architecture, winding past the travertine display blocks of The Stage and framing the SNEAKS café. Just past it sits The Gallery, where Moisés Nieto curates contemporary design pieces and art publications.

Beyond the installation, Velázquez 37 operates as a dynamic hub built to bring Madrid’s design community together. Upstairs, The Work-Shop hosts talks, workshops, and dinners around a 20-meter folding table, joined by footwear customization at The Charm Bar and intimate gatherings inside The Attic, uniting retail with cultural discovery under one roof.
