Add a little K-pop star power to the mix, with BTS’ Jin representing Laneige, V fronting TIRTIR and Tiffany Young and LE SSERAFIM’s Kazuha serving as brand ambassadors for Beauty of Joseon and ETUDE, respectively, and it’s easy to see why the obsession keeps growing. From barrier-boosting creams to glow-giving serums, Korean skincare continues to set the standard for effective, high-quality beauty. And with Black Friday discounts reaching up to 43% off on K-pop-approved products, now is the time to stock up on your favorites.

Dry, flaky lips? Laneige’s cult-favorite Lip Sleeping Mask is an overnight treatment designed to replenish moisture and leave lips feeling soft by morning. Its berry fruit complex, vitamin C and nourishing butters help smooth and condition lips while delivering lasting hydration.

BTS’ Jin gave the brand an extra dose of star power when he became Laneige’s first-ever male ambassador in 2024. Consider this your excuse to get your pout in formation.

Daily SPF is non-negotiable, and Mixsoon’s Bean Sunscreen makes the morning step feel effortless. With SPF 50 protection, a lightweight texture and a no-white-cast finish, this Korean sunscreen layers comfortably under makeup while helping keep skin hydrated.

BTS’ Suga gave fans a peek at his sun-care routine in a BangtanTV vlog filmed in Busan. He applied the sunscreen before a 10-kilometer run and noted its lotion-like texture.

Dullness and uneven-looking skin tone are the focus of Beauty of Joseon’s Glow Deep Serum. Formulated with rice bran water, alpha-arbutin and niacinamide, this lightweight treatment helps brighten the appearance of dark spots while delivering hydration and a fresh, dewy finish.

Girls’ Generation member Tiffany Young became a brand ambassador and muse for Beauty of Joseon in 2023. With her stamp of approval on the brand, this glow-boosting serum deserves a spot on your beauty playlist.

Looking for that coveted glass-skin finish? TIRTIR’s Mask Fit Red Cushion delivers buildable medium-to-full coverage with a lightweight feel and satin glow. Its standout feature is a 40-shade range, making it a particularly exciting find in the world of Korean cushion foundations.

BTS’ V joined TIRTIR as its global ambassador, bringing even more attention to the brand and its viral complexion products. With coverage this good, TIRTIR has officially earned its place on every ARMY’s beauty radar.

Sensitive skin deserves a gentle cleanse, and ETUDE’s SoonJung Whip Cleanser delivers just that. Its cushiony foam helps remove everyday impurities without stripping away moisture. With a skin-friendly pH, panthenol and madecassoside, this K-beauty face cleanser is designed to leave delicate skin feeling comfortable rather than tight.

LE SSERAFIM’s Kazuha was named ETUDE’s brand muse in 2022, representing the beauty brand as a whole. If your skincare routine needs a softer touch, consider this your Perfect Night essential.

Dry or sensitive skin will appreciate this barrier-loving moisturizer. AESTURA’s ATOBARRIER365 Cream combines ceramides, fatty acids and cholesterol to support the skin barrier while delivering long-lasting hydration. Its fragrance-free formula is designed to comfort easily irritated skin without feeling like an overly complicated addition to your routine.

BTS’ Jimin has been spotted using products from the ATOBARRIER line, including this cream, during Are You Sure?! travel moments. Apparently, good skincare deserves a spot in every travel itinerary.

MEDIHEAL’s Madecassoside Essential Face Sheet Mask is designed to calm redness-prone skin, improve the appearance of uneven tone and strengthen the skin’s moisture barrier. Made with madecassoside, ceramides and hyaluronic acid, it helps soothe irritation while delivering a boost of hydration. Its hypoallergenic, vegan-certified formula is also suitable for acne-prone skin.

MEDIHEAL has plenty of K-pop connections. I-dle’s Minnie is a brand ambassador, while BTS, TWS and PLAVE members have also modeled for the skincare brand. Super Junior’s Eunhyuk has also shared his daily sheet-mask habit, proving that the glow-up is a K-group effort.

A K-beauty classic for a reason, COSRX Snail Mucin 96% Repairing Essence uses snail secretion filtrate to help hydrate, soothe and improve the appearance of dull or dehydrated skin. Its lightweight, fast-absorbing texture makes it easy to layer into an existing routine for a healthy-looking glow.

With an impressive 60,000 units reportedly purchased on Amazon last month, this viral favorite clearly isn’t slowing down. If you’ve been curious about the snail mucin craze, Black Friday is a good opportunity to give it a try.

If your skin is constantly thirsty, Belif’s Aqua Bomb is a bestselling gel moisturizer worth knowing. Its fast-absorbing, lightweight formula combines hyaluronic acid and niacinamide to deliver a refreshing hit of hydration without a greasy finish. It’s a particularly good option for anyone who wants moisture without the weight of a traditional cream.

This one has earned its popularity across TikTok, Reddit and retailer reviews, and it’s been a personal skincare staple for years. The hydration is real, making a Black Friday discount an especially good excuse to stock up.

Want the glass-skin look without buying an entire new routine? Glow Recipe’s skincare duo brings together its Watermelon Glow Niacinamide Dew Drops and PHA + BHA Pore-Tight Toner. Together, they help hydrate, boost radiance and refine the appearance of pores for a smoother, dewier complexion.

The kit is a convenient way to try two of the brand’s popular formulas at once. Use the exfoliating toner first, then layer on the glow-boosting serum for a fresh-faced finish that looks ready for its close-up.
