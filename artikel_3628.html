GTA 6 players are discovering, who needs a gun when it's easier to rob a store with a crate of beer?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Guns might be the weapon of choice for most Grand Theft Auto 6 players, but it turns out some enterprising digital criminals are taking a more inventive approach to robberies. Who needs a gun by their side when you've got a crate of beer?

Ordinarily, when you're attempting a store robbery in GTA 6, you first need to take out the guards - lethally or otherwise - before holding up the staff behind the till. But if you make too much of a commotion, the police show up and things turn sour. So players have been trying all sorts of tricks to avoid raising suspicion. And they've discovered an empty bottle can be thrown at the heads of staff and guards to put them out of action without making a lot of noise. It's non-lethal too!

A post shared by 1- UP Gaming (@1upgaming03)

In a video posted to Instagram, for instance, we see a player casually walking into a jewelry store with a crate of beer under one arm and a swift right hook ready to go in the other. That's when Jason goes on a throwing spree, knocking out staff with some well-placed projectiles to the head and dusting up those who get a little close. Aside from a very brief alarm from a locked door, the robbery goes smoothly.

A post shared by Kotov GTA6 стримы (@kotov.plays)

A faster, less flash example shows a quick and easy shop robbery where a casual bottle to the bloke behind the counter allows for rapid access to the cash in the till. Whether bottles can be utilised for something more complicated, like a full-on bank heist, remains to be seen.

Bottles, though, are just the tip of the iceberg. Roadkill and random lizards on the street also make for handy non-lethal projectiles. Beer remains the more compelling option, however. In gun terms, beer crates have up to six rounds in their 'magazine', making them a reliable single source of stuff to throw at folks.
