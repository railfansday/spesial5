'Mormon Wives OC' Couples Status Check: Who Is Still Together, Who Broke Up?

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County offered a glimpse at couples in different places in their relationships.

When Hulu debuted The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County in November 2026, viewers were introduced to podcaster Bobbi Althoff and boyfriend Tyler Hawkins and content creator Aspyn Ovard and her partners Briana Davis and Billy Davis, whom she started dating after her divorce from Parker Ferris. Salomé Andrea‘s ups and downs with husband Aaron Brimhall were also at the center of season 1 — along with Madison Bontempo and husband Kyler Fisher.

Avery Woods, Ashleigh Pease, McCall DaPron and Jordan McClanahan were also featured on the show but their marriages weren’t a focus — yet. Jen Affleck, meanwhile, appeared on the spinoff and got candid about the issues between her and husband Zac Affleck.

Off screen, news broke that Jen filed for divorce from Zac after seven years of marriage.

“I think doing reality TV for sure sped up the process [of calling it quits] because when you’re filming and you’re rewatching your life unfold in front of millions of people, it’s the difference between looking in a mirror,” she exclusively told Us at the time. “It highlights things.”

Jen continued: “But also it’s like one year of therapy feels actually like 20 years of therapy because it’s so much more magnified when you’re sharing it in front of the world. I feel like if I hadn’t done reality TV, I would have still made this decision. It just would have been way longer down the road.”
