NFL Week 11 picks and score predictions: 49ers win Mexico City thriller over Vikings, Bengals stay hot

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

I have made it 24 years without watching a single episode of "The Bachelor,"  but that streak might soon be coming to an end because an NFL quarterback has been chosen to star in the show.

Commanders quarterback Sam Hartman is this year's bachelor, and the only reason I'm mentioning that is that I'm not quite mentally ready to start talking about Week 11 yet. I just looked at the Week 11 schedule and what the NFL is giving us during the 1 p.m. window might be the worst slate of games ever.

There are seven games in the early window, and none of them will feature two teams with winning records facing off. Let's take a look at this schedule:

I would rather watch old episodes of "The Bachelor" than any of these games. That being said, I might watch the Cowboys game just to see if Jerry Jones fires everyone after his defense gives up 47 points to the Titans.

So will that actually happen? Let's get to the picks and find out.

For the second straight week, we have a team coming off a game in Europe facing a team coming off a bye. Last week, things worked out pretty well for the Bengals, but that feels like an anomaly. This game will mark just the seventh time in NFL history that a team coming off an international game has had to play a team coming off a bye, and uh, I have some bad news for everyone in Detroit: The team coming off the international game has gone 1-5, with the only win coming by a possessed Bengals team in Week 10 that got to host a Sunday night game at home for the first time in three years. There was no way the Bengals were losing.

However, I am worried about the Lions. For one, they just lost Aidan Hutchinson for the season to a torn ACL, and thanks to the injury, there's a 50% chance that Dan Campbell is going to ban his entire team from celebrating anything ever again.

Aidan Hutchinson suffered an injury on this play and went to the locker room.(📺 FOX) pic.twitter.com/ysx9NLmNHF

Not only did they just lose their best defensive player, but it gets worse, because they now have to face the top rushing offense in the NFL. The Bucs are averaging 163.9 yards per game on the ground, which is 10 yards more than the next closest team. Tampa Bay has more rushing yards this season than the Bengals and Packers COMBINED.

I do not know if Baker Mayfield (foot) is going to play, but I'm not sure it matters, because the QB in Tampa only has one job and that's to hand the ball off. If Baker can't play, Jalon Daniels will, and if he can't play for some reason, the Bucs could probably just hold a fan raffle for the job.

Bucs: Can you hand the ball off? Fan: Yes. Bucs: OK, the job is yours.

There have only been three games this season where an NFL team has run the ball at least 49 times and the Buccaneers have two of them. With the Buccaneers coming off the bye, that means they're going to be well-rested and it also means that offensive coordinator Zac Robinson has had plenty of time to tear out every page from his playbook that has a passing play on it. The Bucs might run it 71 times on Sunday.

The good news for the Lions is that they've been one of the best teams in the NFL at stopping the run. They're giving up just 90.4 yards per game, which is the fifth-best number in the NFL, but that was with Hutchinson on the field. This week, they obviously won't have him.

The Lions are flying back from Germany; they just lost their best defensive player, and they have to face a Bucs team coming off a bye. Oh, and did I mention that NFL teams are a combined 13-3 coming off their bye this year? Because NFL teams are a combined 13-3 coming off their bye this year. And two of those three losses were by the Titans and Chargers, so I'm not even sure those count.

Not only do the Bucs have the best rushing attack in the NFL, but they also have the best run defense in the league, so this could be a long day for Jahmyr Gibbs. This game comes down to whether you trust Jared Goff to dice up Tampa Bay's defense, and, well, I'm the guy who made Goff my preseason prediction to win MVP this year, so I'm riding with Goff and the Lions here.

If there's any game in Week 11 that feels like a disaster waiting to happen, it's definitely this one. With the Jets and Chargers on the same field, I won't be surprised if the government declares SoFi Stadium a federal disaster area.

There's been no bigger disaster in the NFL this year than the Chargers. They hired Mike McDaniel to fix their offense and he somehow might have made it worse. When McDaniel was in Miami, one reason his offense worked so well is that he had two superstar receivers in Tyreek Hill and Jaylen Waddle. He doesn't have anything near that in L.A., but he does not seem to realize that. McDaniel keeps doing things his own way, and as a result, the Chargers' offense is so bad it's almost painful to watch.

It's getting to the point where I feel like the Chargers' best offensive strategy would be to just not have an offense at all. I have no idea how that would work, but I have to think it would be an upgrade over what they're doing now. This team is averaging UNDER 180 passing yards per game, which shouldn't be possible when you have Justin Herbert as your QB.

Not to be outdone, the Jets have also been a disaster. Here's a quick synopsis of how things have gone for the Jets. Back on Feb. 24, Aaron Glenn said he would call plays on defense because it's one of his superpowers.

"To me, play calling is my superpower"Aaron Glenn on his decision to call plays on defense pic.twitter.com/FvHFYW97pK

As it turns out, though, he might have been wrong, and play-calling might actually be his kryptonite.

Aaron Glenn gave up play calling vs the #Bills — that’s really big of him. Called it his “super power” this offseason. He wants to call the game. To step back and be willing to give it to someone else, for the betterment of the team, isn’t something a lot of coaches are willing… https://t.co/ivYq4cnBca

Aaron Glenn saw Josh Allen coming up on the schedule in Week 10 and said "No thanks, I'm good. You're now in charge of calling play on defense, Brian."

So we have the Jets' broken defense going up against a Chargers offense that can't move the ball on anyone. I said this game feels like a disaster waiting to happen and that disaster might happen after the game. If the Jets lose here, the temperature on Aaron Glenn's hot seat will get so hot that I don't think the Fahrenheit scale can actually measure it. We might have to switch over to Celsius.

The Chargers stunned the Ravens on Monday night by rushing for a season-high 160 yards. Running the ball is literally the only thing the Chargers can do on offense, which might work in their favor here because the Jets can't stop the run.

The Chargers went into Week 10 with ZERO wins, so I feel crazy even thinking this, but this team might actually win two games in a row.

Never in my life did I think I would live long enough to see a Jaguars-Giants game get flexed into the 4:25 p.m. late afternoon slot, but that's exactly what happened here. This game was originally scheduled to kick off at 1 p.m. ET, but the NFL decided to move it to 4:25 p.m. ET, and we're celebrating that fact here at CBS Sports by sending "The NFL Today" crew to MetLife Stadium.

The NFL Today is headed to MetLife! 🔥The show will be LIVE outside the stadium ahead of @Jaguars vs @Giants this Sunday 🍿 pic.twitter.com/Bv2zAZ1htI

If you're going to be at the game, please come dressed as your favorite CBS Sports studio analyst.

The Giants have been a huge surprise this season, and that's mostly thanks to John Harbaugh, who is my early vote for coach of the year. Harbaugh has been forced to start THREE different quarterbacks this season, but he still has the Giants at 5-3-1 and on top of the NFC East.

The biggest surprise is that the Giants have somehow made J.J. McCarthy look good. I was led to believe by the Vikings that McCarthy was a washed-up bust, but apparently they just didn't know how to use him. McCarthy is the chopsticks of NFL quarterbacks. I hate chopsticks because I'm incompetent and don't know how to use them, just like the Vikings didn't know how to use McCarthy. On the other hand, my wife is such an expert with chopsticks that if every utensil on earth disappeared tomorrow and we only had chopsticks left, she would be fine, just like the Giants will be fine with McCarthy running the show.

McCarthy played well enough to win in Week 10 against the Commanders, and the Giants might have won if they had a kicker who could make field goals from the right hash.

All 6 of Dominic Zvada’s missed kicks this season have come from the right hash, including his 33-yard miss last night.The Giants have lined up on the left hash for every extra point, where Zvada is a perfect 16/16. https://t.co/D9YzxVSYyx pic.twitter.com/UHJa8av5EK

Zvada's inability to hit a kick from the right hash is like Zoolander not being able to turn left, except worse.

I watched zoolander today (kind of like idiocracy) and the main character DEREK zoolander. Cannot turn left. Where have i heard of a derek related to the direction left. pic.twitter.com/e8cUEOU8Ct

Although I think the Giants will keep this game close, this will be the first week that the Jaguars finally unveil their new defense. They made three trades before the deadline, acquiring DT Davon Godchaux, DT Jordan Davis and safety Jessie Bates III. The Jags decided to keep Davis and Bates on the bench in Week 10 so they would each get a "bye" week this year. Those two will make their debut on Sunday, and I have a feeling the new-look Jags' defense is going to cause some problems for the Giants.

For the first time in four years, the NFL is playing a game in Mexico City. I went to Mexico City once, and I was totally blown away by the food. I mean, they had a Chili's and Burger King UNDER THE SAME ROOF.

If you're looking for food in Mexico City, I highly recommend this place. Only place I know of where you can get the world's two best restaurants under one roof TOGETHER pic.twitter.com/G58xvmA5DS

Someone needs to make that happen in the United States. Mexico is light years ahead of us when it comes to combining fast food with fast casual dining under one roof.

OK, I need to stop talking about Chili's and start focusing on what could be the best chess match of the 2026 season: Kyle Shanahan vs. Brian Flores.

Through the first 10 weeks, the 49ers have had one of the best offenses in the NFL this year: They rank first in yards per game (399.8) and second in points per game (31.2). No one has really been able to stop them. The 49ers are so good that the Cowboys' defense basically gave up on trying to stop them in Week 10.

As for the Vikings, they have one of the best defenses in the NFL. They've given up the fewest points per game (14) and the second-fewest yards per game (273.3). One big reason they're so successful is that they've gotten really good at destroying the opposing quarterback. The Vikings have racked up 37 sacks this year, which leads the NFL, and Flores loves to blitz on nearly every single play.

The problem with that strategy is that Brock Purdy is basically the one quarterback you do NOT want to blitz.

Leaders in EPA per drop back vs the blitz…DO NOT blitz these guys pic.twitter.com/Vn67jHblTm

Purdy thinks fast and gets rid of the football quickly. It's like he was designed in a lab to beat the blitz, but Flores is almost certainly aware of this, which means we'll probably see him cook up something special for the 49ers quarterback on Sunday. And now that we're talking about cooking, I'm thinking of Chili's again. I might need to take a lunch break.

I actually think the X-factor in this game is going to be the altitude. Mexico City sits at an elevation of nearly 7,400 feet, which is more than 2,000 feet higher than Denver. When you're playing at an altitude this high, it's not easy to get acclimated, which is why the 49ers will be spending the entire week in Colorado Springs, a city that has an altitude of just over 6,000 feet.

#49ers are set to travel to Colorado Springs tonight to begin altitude acclimation. They’ll have a normal practice week before traveling to Mexico City on Saturday to play the Vikings on Sunday night.

On the other hand, the Vikings will be practicing at their normal practice facility, which has an altitude of 945 feet. Advantage, 49ers.

This feels like a game where the altitude starts to get to the Vikings in the second half and that's when the 49ers will take over.

This is the final international game of the 2026 season, and I feel like the NFL should add some spice to the last international game each year by having the government revoke the losing team's passports. I'm not sure if that's legal, but I would definitely watch a reality show that consisted only of NFL players trying to get back home after having their passports revoked in a foreign country. It would be more entertaining than "The Bachelor."

Dan Quinn is probably going to be having nightmares all week and that's because he has to figure out a way to stop Joe Burrow, which is something that no one else has been able figure how to do this year.

At this point, you've probably seen plenty of Burrow highlights this year, but if you're wondering just how dominant he's been, let me break it down for you:

To put his yardage number in perspective, no other QB is even averaging 285 yards per game. He has five more TD passes than the next closest player and his completion percentage is so high that he might actually break the NFL single-season record of 74.4%, which was set by Drew Brees in 2019.

Burrow is playing at an insanely high level, and now, he gets to face a Commanders defense that has given up the most passing yards per game in the NFC this year and second-most in the NFL (244.8). Burrow against this defense would be like inviting Joey Chestnut to your family's Thanksgiving and telling him to eat whatever he wants. You know you're going to get a Hall of Fame performance.

Sam Darnold threw for 379 yards against this Commanders defense. Jameis Winston threw for 326 yards against this defense. J.J. McCarthy threw for 293 yards against this defense. I think what I'm trying to say here is that the NFL might want to invite Norm Van Brocklin's family to this game, because Burrow might take down his all-time single-game passing record of 554 yards.

Although I feel like Burrow is going to have a big game, I think the Commanders' offense could also have some success. This is a home primetime game; Washington will be desperate, and Jayden Daniels has been playing well over the past few weeks.

The last time these two teams played back in 2024, there wasn't a single punt in the game. That might happen again on Monday night because Zac Taylor doesn't sound like a guy who plans on punting this week.

Asked what he didn't like about last night's blowout win against the Steelers, #Bengals coach Zac Taylor said, "We punted."

I'm going to say Burrow continues his MVP campaign by leading the Bengals to a shootout win in Washington.

Best pick: Commanders over Giants. Last week, I predicted that the Commanders would score exactly 24 in a win over the Giants, and guess what happened? The Commanders scored exactly 24 points in a win over the Giants. As soon as Sam Hartman got picked to be the next bachelor, I knew there was no way the Commanders were going to lose. Worst pick: Texans over Browns. At the rate things are going, I might have to eliminate the "worst pick" section because I'm not getting any picks wrong. Over the past two weeks, I've gone 25-4 straight-up. The hilarious part is that I'm 0-2 when it comes to picking Browns' games and 25-2 in all other games. I think what I'm trying to say here is that I might have to take a week off from picking Browns games, so there will be no Browns pick this week.
