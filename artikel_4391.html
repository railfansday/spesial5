Tanggal: 2026-11-28

See the interior of the 2027 Audi Q7 from every angle.

Read the Full Review | See Exterior Photos

Tech takes center stage in the new third-generation Audi Q7.

Three digital displays pepper the dashboard of all Q7 models.

The driver faces a reconfigurable digital gauge display, while a larger touchscreen provides access to infotainment and vehicle settings.

An additional touchscreen in front of the front-seat passenger provides access to entertainment and navigation functions.

Audi’s newfangled shift lever sprouts from the steering column, and although it looks a little weird, it’s intuitive to use.

A pair of comfortable and well-bolstered bucket seats are in the front, and can be had with headrest speakers that improve the surround-sound system.

The wood inlay on the center console is genuine, and you’ll find two phone-charging pads there as well.

The phone chargers secure your device with magnets and are cooled to prevent device overheating.

A panoramic sunroof comes standard and features an electrochromic panel that can be opaque, transparent, or somewhere in between.

A three-seat bench is standard in the second row, but a pair of captain’s chairs is available as an option for the first time.

The Q7 is a three-row SUV, but its rearmost seats are snug and suitable only for children.
