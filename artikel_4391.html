10 Metroidvanias With the Most Nominations at The Game Awards

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Ronald is a video game journalist with more than 10 years of experience in the industry, working on several of the most important Spanish websites.

Although Metroidvanias have been largely dominated by indie games since video game award ceremonies became commonplace, this hasn't diminished their presence at the industry's most important gala events.

Today, the indie ecosystem is just as capable as AAA titles of delivering masterpieces, which has led to them gaining a more prominent role whenever we gather to review each year's best releases.

Certainly, it's not as prominent a genre as RPGs or pure action-adventure games, but it has still had enough presence to demonstrate that it's a variant that has nothing to envy in the legitimacy of others.

Therefore, clarifying beforehand that several titles will be omitted for sharing the last spot with only one nomination, prioritizing instead those that were most relevant, these are the ten Metroidvanias with the most nominations at The Game Awards.

Along with another title that will be featured later, Axiom Verge marks the first year a Metroidvania earned a nomination; in this case, for Best Independent Game of 2015.

Although it had to contend with notable releases like the global phenomenon Rocket League and the indie gem Undertale, Tom Happ secured his place in the genre's history thanks to his magnificent science fiction journey.

In an era when Metroidvanias hadn't yet established themselves as a major force, this solo developer created such a captivating title that, to this day, it still has few direct competitors in its craftsmanship, as it is truly unique.

Nothing would please me more today than an Axiom Verge 3, so this is a fitting opportunity to recall the historic feat achieved by the first entry and ask Happ to consider repeating that glory soon.

As a living legend of contemporary Metroidvanias, single-handedly setting the course for the genre in the decade following its release, Hollow Knight couldn't be left off this list.

However, contrary to what one might think today, Team Cherry's creation only received a nomination for Best Indie Debut in 2017, which it lost to a colossus that, upon its release, made a greater impact: Cuphead.

Even so, time would gradually vindicate this masterpiece, whose aura would extend to all subsequent developments, rewriting history in the memories of many who recall, albeit mistakenly, that it was nominated in more categories.

The power of hindsight is undeniable when the subject is a title with Hollow Knight's legacy, which also highlights how indie games, over the years, have become increasingly respected and taken seriously in these spaces.

From indies to multiplayers, these ten video games that defined the 2010s shaped the industry as we know it today.

Leaving aside the inevitable debate about Dead Cells as a Metroidvania, Motion Twin made history by being one of the first games in the genre to receive multiple nominations at The Game Awards.

Historically, this fusion of the genre with the roguelike philosophy earned the second Metroidvania award in the event's history by winning Best Action Game in 2018, though it lost the Best Independent Game category to the acclaimed Celeste.

In the category it won, it faced stiff competition from titanic blockbusters like Call of Duty: Black Ops 4, Destiny 2: Forsaken, and Far Cry 5, though this didn't prevent what would eventually become one of the best-selling indie games of all time from taking home the prize.

The recognition is, of course, more than deserved, given that few titles in the last decade are capable of generating the excitement and addictiveness that Dead Cells produces, so no matter how many AAA games they had put against it, it would have won anyway.

That high-caliber developers like Ubisoft are investing in the genre to revitalize their longest-running franchises is a relatively new trend, yet it yielded definitive results with Prince of Persia: The Lost Crown.

The presence of a game in the genre with such a strong production value allowed it to win an unprecedented honor for Metroidvanias, the Innovation in Accessibility Award in 2024, although it lost the Best Action-Adventure Game category to Astro Bot.

Regardless, it was the first medal at The Game Awards that the French developer received after winning Best Sim/Strategy Game with Mario + Rabbids: Sparks of Hope in 2022, making its impact in terms of recognition significant.

Furthermore, it's an extraordinary video game with a superb level of polish and freshness, so hopefully Ubisoft will recognize this trend and begin to replicate what they did with Prince of Persia: The Lost Crown with several of their most forgotten and missed IPs.

2024 was a fertile year for indie games, and although it didn't win either of the categories it was nominated for—because Balatro is larger than life—Animal Well remains in the collective imagination as an unparalleled masterpiece.

As part of the nominees for Best Independent Game and Best Indie Debut in 2024, Billy Basso stood tall with his iconic solo project against giants of the indie ecosystem like Devolver Digital, Kepler Interactive, and Annapurna Interactive without hesitation.

Basso picks up the mantle from Happ and doubles Axiom Verge's achievement almost a decade later, doing so with a game that defies Metroidvania conventions and replaces all combat with countless intricate yet wonderful puzzles that completely immerse you in the adventure.

Two years later, Animal Well remains one of my most cherished memories of the entire decade, and I think it will likely stay there for... a lifetime.

From the hops and dashes in Hollow Knight to the integral platforming of Pseudoregalia, these are 10 Metroidvanias with satisfying platforming.

The first of this list's five Metroidvanias to be nominated for three or more awards is none other than Tunic, the indie gem that swept through audiences and critics alike in 2022, establishing itself among the genre's most outstanding representatives this generation.

Unfortunately, its nominations for Best Independent Game, Best Action-Adventure Game, and Best Indie Debut in 2022 didn't result in any badges, as it was up against GOTY contenders like Stray and God of War: Ragnarök, though this only underscores its impressive quality.

That essence reminiscent of The Legend of Zelda didn't go unnoticed, and it underpinned the structure of one of recent years' most exciting and intriguing adventures, particularly because of its decision not to hold your hand and instead rely on your ability to find your way through combat, exploration, and puzzle-solving.

The fact that Tunic is the first video game from its developers is absolutely incredible, and that under said conditions it achieved three nominations on the most anticipated night in the video game industry is quite high recognition.

As one of the most exquisite audiovisual expressions the genre has witnessed since its inception, Ori and the Will of the Wisps was an inevitable presence on any list of this caliber.

Moon Studios' sequel was among the nominees for Best Art Direction, Best Music, and Best Action-Adventure Game in 2020, though unfortunately, it had to compete against absolute generational masterpieces like Ghost of Tsushima, Final Fantasy VII Remake, and The Last of Us Part II.

However, as with Tunic, Ori and the Will of the Wisps being considered alongside numerous video games that have defined the decade is one of the greatest displays of Metroidvania greatness on the big stage.

It's no wonder the title consistently appears on lists of the best of its kind, and one that would have completely swept its categories had it been nominated in years less dominated by modern classics.

Metroid Dread made history as the first Metroidvania to be nominated for Game of the Year in 2021, and for many it was the true winner of the category that, ultimately, went to It Takes Two.

Regardless of the debate, MercurySteam solidified its relationship with Nintendo by bringing Samus Aran back to 2D, reinforcing it as the natural home of the iconic bounty hunter by delivering a near-perfect creation.

It was also nominated for Best Action-Adventure Game, which it won, and for Players’ Voice, which it lost, thus becoming the second game in the genre to win a category after Dead Cells, despite probably deserving more.

The mother of all the entries on this list is an inseparable part of its history, and although perhaps not to the fullest extent, its participation in The Game Awards is a recognition of that legacy.

From the ever-challenging Celeste to some surprisingly difficult gems, these are the 10 Hardest Indie Games To 100% Complete.

With four nominations and one victory, Ori and the Blind Forest is pure Metroidvania history, bursting onto the scene at the interactive industry's most prestigious awards ceremony as the genre's first appearance (alongside Axiom Verge).

Developed by a much smaller team than when they collaborated with Microsoft on the sequel, and as a less experienced studio within a still largely unexplored genre, the project was nominated for Best Independent Game, Best Art Direction, Best Score, and Best Action-Adventure in 2015.

Of all the nominations, it took home the award for its visual direction, beating out Bloodborne, The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, and Metal Gear Solid 5: The Phantom Pain, one of the biggest surprises the ceremony has ever seen.

While this is a more common and normal occurrence these days, the fact that Ori and the Blind Forest achieved such a strong presence, and even managed to win an award among a couple of candidates for best game of all time, is simply beyond belief.

What I consider the best Metroidvania of all time is also the most recognized game in the genre at The Game Awards, leaving Hollow Knight: Silksong with the most nominations at six, though it only managed one win.

Game of the Year, Best Art Direction, Best Score and Music, Best Independent Game, Players’ Voice, and Best Action-Adventure Game were the categories in which Team Cherry's unimaginable sequel was nominated, ultimately taking home the last one.

Like Ori and the Blind Forest, Hornet had to overcome giants such as Death Stranding 2: On the Beach, Ghost of Yotei, and Split Fiction to win the award, a remarkable achievement considering the team is still just three people.

In the categories it didn't win, it was surpassed by the now-legendary Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, which took home four of the six awards in which it shared nominations, and Wuthering Waves, which won the user vote.

In any case, Hollow Knight: Silksong is the Metroidvania with the most nominations at The Game Awards, and it's tough to imagine when we'll see another title that can match such a record.

From classics like Teslagrad to modern gems like Blasphemous, these are ten Metroidvania franchises that should come back in 2027.
