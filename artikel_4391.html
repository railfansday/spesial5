Q&A: How socioeconomic status is measured can obscure inequalities in health care

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Socioeconomic differences in health may be greater than research suggests. In a new thesis, Balram Rai used PSA testing in the Stockholm region as an example and investigated how socioeconomic position can be measured at different levels and how measurement errors can affect inferences about inequalities in health.
