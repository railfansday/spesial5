MMO Horizon Steel Frontiers 'Nearing Completion', NC Discussing Release Timing with Sony

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

While the future may look bleak for series creator Guerrilla’s own Horizon multiplayer game, Korean partner NC has confirmed to investors that its MMO spin-off Horizon Steel Frontiers is “nearing completion”.

Korean outlet News1 quotes co-CEO Park Byung-moo as saying: “The new Horizon IP game is nearing completion. We are continuously discussing the release timing with the IP holder, Sony, and plan to make an announcement soon.”

For those who don’t know, Horizon Steel Frontiers is a spin-off mobile MMO being developed under license from Sony.

So far, it’s only been officially announced for release in Asian countries, although NC has noted demand from PS5 players who’d like to play this on console.

Currently the game is scheduled to release in 2027 on mobile platforms and PC via the company’s Purple emulator.

Sony’s approach to mobile has been confusing for decades now; it recently announced plans to pull the plug on a Ratchet & Clank spin-off, which was in beta on smartphones.

Rather than actively promote Horizon Steel Frontiers, the platform holder has put its weight behind Guerrilla’s abovementioned co-op game Horizon Hunters Gathering, which is being retooled after receiving a negative response earlier in the year and has until December to find a fresh path forward.

It’s going to be beyond embarrassing for the manufacturer if NC’s MMO makes it to market and is proven to be a success, while series creator Guerrilla’s version is cancelled, as is looking like a possibility right now.
