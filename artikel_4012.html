Secret PS4 Firmware Update 14.02 to Fight New Wave of Piracy, Hacking

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Sony has deployed an unannounced PS4 firmware update in the last couple of hours for its last-gen PS4 console that is designed to combat the current wave of piracy, hacking, and jailbreaks targeting PlayStation consoles.

The PS5 has received plenty of system patches in the past few weeks, and now the PS4 is subject to a firmware update.

Now running on version 14.02, the single patch note for the update is as follows:

Through the use of AI tools, hackers have been able to get emulators running on PS5 consoles set to firmware v7.00 through v13.60. With GTA 6 right around the corner, Sony has been working to combat the hacks. Now, if you want to play GTA 6 on PS5, you'll need to update your PS5 to the very latest firmware.
