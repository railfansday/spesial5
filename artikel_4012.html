Forza Horizon 6 Content Roadmap Revealed For Late 2026 & 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

With a specific focus on January and February of next year

Ready for some more news on Forza Horizon 6? The team at Playground Games has announced a content roadmap for the game today, pulling the curtain back on their plans for the end of 2026 and all of next year.

You can get loads of detail about all of this in the video up above, but in a nutshell, we're getting Series 8 "Horizon Holidays" and Series 9 "Legendary Explorers" before the year is out. Then, as we head into January and February, Series 10 will be based around "Exotics and Tuners", while other new additions include a permanent new radio station, absolutely loads of new Reward Cars, and various other bits and bobs.

Most importantly, the first expansion for the game will also be released in January of 2027. Playground Games still doesn't want to talk about it just yet, but apparently we'll be hearing about it very soon.

News Forza Horizon 6's First Premium Expansion Planned For January 2027 Alongside PS5 Launch

Sony fans loved FH5, will they vibe with FH6? Probably

As you can see from the rest of this roadmap, the remainder of 2027 gets more vague for now, but it's confirmed that a second expansion is on the way along with a Festival Playlist overhaul, new race routes and various other new features.

They do talk quite a lot about the Festival Playlist overhaul in the video up above, so again, it's well worth a watch if you want to know more about what's on the way to Forza Horizon 6 in the future.

All of this sounds pretty good to us, and considering how the game has come in for a little bit of disgruntlement in recent months, it's clear to see that Playground Games is hoping for a strong 2027 when it comes to new FH6 content.
