Why Portugal’s No. 7 jersey will forever look different

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Portuguese Football Federation can’t retire superstar soccer player Cristiano Ronaldo’s No. 7 due to strict FIFA rules around player numbers. Instead, the team got creative and used a different font. A No. 7 with a cross stroke through it will be used on kits for players who wear the number from here on out.

Portugal’s Saturday game against Denmark was the team’s first since Ronaldo retired earlier this month. The entire team came out in “Ronaldo 7” jerseys with the old-style number before the match to pay tribute to the player who captained the team for 19 years. Then during the match, player Carlos Forbs wore the new cross-stroke 7 for the Portuguese national team.

“This is yet another way to immortalize the meaning of such a special number and honor the legacy it represents,” the team said in a statement.

Other professional teams and leagues retire numbers to honor legendary players. In 1997, MLB retired the No. 42 for Jackie Robinson, the league’s first Black player and a former National League MVP. The NHL retired 99 in 2000 for Wayne Gretzky, who still holds the league’s record for most career points. The NBA’s Chicago Bulls will never again have a 4, 10, 23, or 33 after retiring them for Jerry Sloan, Bob Love, Michael Jordan, and Scottie Pippen, respectively.

The international football body doesn’t give teams or players as much freedom with numbers during its tournaments, as each team has to use all consecutive numbers from 1 to 26 for their 26-person rosters. That meant that when Lionel Messi retired in October, the Argentine Football Association didn’t retire his No. 10, despite the fact that Claudio Tapia, AFA’s president, said in 2024 it would be retired.

Portugal’s solution is a clever, unconventional work-around. If you can’t change the number, at least change how the number looks. Fonts with alternative characters, or characters with multiple design variations, give designers flexibility to add optional elements like swashes, flairs, or cross strokes on characters like 7 and Z.

By adding a cross stroke to their 7, Portugal figured out how to honor a retired legend with a number they technically can’t retire.
