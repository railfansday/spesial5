Ella Langley Makes CMA Awards History—Inside the Home Where She’ll Put Her New Trophies on Display

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Singer Ella Langley made history at the Country Music Awards when she set the record for the most CMA awards won by a woman in one night—so will where the hitmaker house her new trophies?

The "Choosin Texas" singer, 27, won entertainer of the year, female vocalist of the year, album of the year, single of the year, and song of the year.

When accepting her sixth award of the evening, she teased, "At this point, I'm sick of me!"

“This has been the most special night. I could quit tomorrow and have the best career of my life," she added.

At one point during the evening, fellow country star Lainey Wilson joked, "I said I was gonna bring you a bedazzled broom. I knew you was gonna sweep, girl.”

At the end of the evening, Langley posed with all of her awards as she smiled from ear to ear.

Langley will put her multiple trophies on display at her lakefront retreat, which she picked up in 2025.

In July 2025, Langley snapped up her first-ever home in her hometown of Montgomery, AL, just minutes away from her parents' home.

Langley, who lives in Nashville, TN, but was born and raised in Alabama, gushed about her newly minted purchase while speaking to Audacy, telling the radio station that she decided to make her first real estate investment in her home state because she feels most like herself when she's there.

"It just brings me back to who I am at the root of me every time I go back there. And when it comes to writing songs and being in that state of mind, I think it’s important to have a place like that," she said.

The star noted that, while she will likely "always" live in Tennessee, which is the home of country music, she will continue to keep roots in Alabama because of how close she is to her family.

“I think I’ll always have to live in Tennessee to some extent in my life, but being from Alabama, it’s so close. It’s under four hours to get there," she added.

Though many young people might find the process of buying their first property overwhelming, Langley conceded that she was in a much more privileged position—namely because she has her own business managers who helped to oversee the entire purchase.

"I honestly been having a blast. I have great business management. They just get all the phone calls, yes or no, this is what I want to do, and they figure out all the little logistics, so very thankful for them,” she added.

Langley moved to Nashville in order to pursue her musical career, relocating from her hometown in Alabama in 2019.

"The music family here is just really special; people are so willing to be a mentor or help you grow in a way I just couldn’t do back home," Langley said of her new home state during an interview with Raised Rowdy.

Previously, Langley has shared that her move to Nashville also helped her to find more freedom in her writing process, joking to Raised Rowdy that she spent much of her childhood in Alabama trying to "survive life" with two brothers.

"I grew up in the country in Alabama between two brothers, so a lot of my childhood was just trying to survive life between those two," she said.

"I always was trying to prove I was just as tough and fearless as they were. Being a tomboy like that, a lot of my friends ended up being guys. If you ask my band or people who know me, they’ll tell you sometimes I seem more like a guy than a girl. But I think women can still be direct, strong, and tough, without apologizing for who they are or how they think."

Making the decision to pursue her music full time was a serious one for Langley, who dropped out of Auburn University in order to focus on her passion.

“I had a boyfriend who was in music and then his manager, they were both moving to Nashville and we’re going to live with Trey (Lewis). And I was like, well hell, I’m not staying here," she previously told CountryNow.

"So I called mom and dad and I said, ‘Hey, I think I want to drop out of school and move to Nashville. I’m just going to be in more debt anyways when I graduate. I mean, I’m going to go do this anyways. I would rather start it now.'"

The singer also revealed that she was warmly welcomed to Nashville.

“It’s been a cool journey of finding those people for me in town that I just love and love me and believe in me and I believe in them. And so that’s what’s really kept me going a lot through this," she went on.

"And obviously my family supports me the way that they do and just love me, but that’s the thing, nobody has ever thought I was going to do anything else …

"If you ask any of my family, they’re like, ‘Yeah, we all knew that this was what she was going to do.’ So there wasn’t ever really any question when I wanted to move."
