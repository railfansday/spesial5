Streamer Awards 2026: How to watch and nominees

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The 2026 Streamer Awards take place on November 12, with IShowSpeed, Jynxzi, CaseOh, and other major content creators competing for livestreaming’s biggest honors.

Organized by QTCinderella, the annual ceremony celebrates the best streamers, events, and moments across platforms including Twitch, YouTube, and Kick.

This year’s event features 37 publicly voted categories, with IShowSpeed, Jynxzi, and Kai Cenat leading the nominations with four each.

IShowSpeed is also looking to defend his Streamer of the Year title after winning the award in both 2024 and 2025.

The Streamer Awards will take place on Thursday, November 12, in Los Angeles, with QTCinderella and Caroline Kwan hosting the ceremony.

The event will be broadcast live on QTCinderella’s Twitch and YouTube channel.

The red carpet begins at 5:30pm ET (2:30pm PT/10pm GMT), followed by the main ceremony at 8:30pm ET (5:30pm PT/1am GMT).
