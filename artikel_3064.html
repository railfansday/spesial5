HBO Max, Peacock, Apple TV, more: Here are the best Black Friday deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

HBO Max has a 50% off deal for its Basic with Ads plan only, but it lets you get the discounted price for a full 12 months.

Through November 30, HBO Max with ads is only $5.49/month. That’s big savings off the standard price of $10.99/month.

The lack of discount for ad-free plans is disappointing, but if you can live with ads, getting to keep the discounted HBO Max price for a full year is a great deal.

Apple TV is offering over 50% off for its Black Friday deal, and you can lock in the discounted price for the next six months.

The streamer is currently airing hit crime thrillers Nocturne and 12 12 12, a new season of beloved comedy Stick, and is about to bring back a show from Ted Lasso and Shrinking’s creator. Looking further out, January will bring the streamer’s major new sci-fi series Neuromancer and a lot more.

The only catch is that the deal is valid for just two months. So after two months, your monthly rate will go back to the standard pricing.

Peacock is running a Black Friday deal, but it’s one of the weakest among streamers.

You can get Peacock Premium for $8.99/month for up to three months. This is their base plan with ads that includes Peacock Originals. It’s normally priced at $12.99/month.
