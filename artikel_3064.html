Crypto Market Today, Nov. 16: Bitcoin Searches for Stability After $230 Billion Crypto Sell-Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Major cryptocurrencies are approaching critical support levels after surrendering much of their summer gains, leaving an already fragile market vulnerable to fresh bear-market lows.

Cryptocurrency markets found some stability on Monday, November 16, following a brutal sell-off that erased approximately $230 billion in market value between Thursday and Saturday. Bitcoin briefly fell toward $62,000 before recovering some of its losses, while several major cryptocurrencies continued trading near multi-month lows.

As of early evening, Bitcoin (BTC -0.39%) was down 0.55% to $64,953.73, while Ethereum (ETH +0.31%) declined 0.92% to $1,818.34 and Solana (SOL +0.53%) fell 1.77% to $71.40. Although selling pressure has moderated, deteriorating liquidity conditions, elevated Treasury yields, and weakening investor risk appetite continue to complicate the outlook for digital assets.

Selling pressure began building late last week as rising Treasury yields, stress in technology stocks, and an overall weakening in risk appetite weighed on cryptocurrencies. The sell-off intensified over the weekend, when thinner liquidity and shallower order books left the market increasingly vulnerable to large sell orders.

As prices declined, leveraged traders holding long positions were forced to liquidate, triggering a cascade that accelerated losses across the market. These forced sales pushed prices lower, triggering additional liquidations that sent Bitcoin toward $62,000 before buyers stepped in.

Spot crypto ETFs were sharply weaker, with iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF (IBIT -5.52%) down 5.52%, Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC -5.61%) down 5.63%, and iShares Ethereum Trust ETF (ETHA -6.57%) down 6.57%.

Perhaps more concerning than the magnitude of the recent sell-off is where major cryptocurrencies are now trading. Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana have surrendered much of the gains accumulated during their summer recovery, leaving the broader market increasingly vulnerable to another leg lower.

For Bitcoin, the $60,000 to $62,000 range represents a critical area to defend. Meanwhile, Ethereum's decline below $2,000 signals deteriorating market strength, with $1,800 now an important threshold. Solana is in a similarly precarious position, trading near $70.

With the 10-year Treasury yield above 5% and uncertainty surrounding the technology sector, the macroeconomic backdrop remains unfavorable for speculative assets. Any additional headwinds could push several major cryptocurrencies to fresh lows in a bear market that has already persisted for more than a year.

Although Monday's stabilization is encouraging, investors should distinguish between a temporary exhaustion of selling pressure and a meaningful recovery in demand. Until major cryptocurrencies begin reclaiming previously established support levels, the risk of further declines remains elevated.
