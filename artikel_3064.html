Stock Market Today, Nov. 10: Opendoor Shares Fall on CPI Data Despite Revenue Above $1 Billion

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Heavy trading pressured Opendoor as investors weighed the implications of rising consumer prices, today, Nov. 10, 2026.

Opendoor Technologies (OPEN -7.09%), a digital iBuyer platform for residential home transactions, closed at $1.31, down 7.09%. The stock fell as the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics' October Consumer Price Index (CPI) report showed a hotter-than-expected increase in consumer prices, raising the likelihood of further interest rate hikes that will affect home loans. This extended the pressure Opendoor shares already were under after earnings weakness as investors continue to watch housing demand signals.

Trading volume reached 45.1 million shares, coming in about 26% above its three-month average of 35.8 million shares. Opendoor Technologies IPO'd in 2020 and has fallen 88% since going public.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.38%) fell 0.38% to 7,521, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -0.74%) lost 0.74% to 25,066. In residential real estate technology, Offerpad Solutions (OPAD -1.86%) closed at $2.11, down 1.86%, and Zillow (Z -5.51%) ended at $28.98, down 5.51%, showing pressure across sector rivals.

Opendoor stock was hit particularly hard today, Nov. 10, due to the government's CPI report, which showed a 0.4% month-over-month increase in overall consumer prices for October, exceeding the 0.3% rise that economists had projected. The surprise data fueled speculation that the Federal Reserve may keep interest rates higher for longer or even consider a larger-than-usual rate hike at its December meeting.

If rates go up, home loans will become more expensive, creating the possibility that the real estate market will weaken, and threaten Opendoor's business growth. Last week, Zillow's third-quarter earnings report conveyed sluggish housing activity via its softer October listing trends, reinforcing cautious housing demand signals across the sector.

This added to Wall Street's concerns over Opendoor's own Q3 earnings results, reported on Oct. 5. The company delivered revenue above $1 billion, up a solid 12% year over year. However, its losses widened -- from a $90 million net loss in Q3 2025 to a $218 million loss this year -- keeping Wall Street’s attention on margin recovery and cash generation in the face of interest rate headwinds for its industry.
