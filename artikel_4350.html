Genesis Expands GV60 Magma Availability to More States

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The highest-performance Genesis model to date can now be purchased in 11 more states.

Genesis wants to make it easier for you to buy its cars, which, duh, of course it does. But the automaker’s newest offering, the wonderful GV60 Magma, launched in just three states: California, New York, and New Jersey. As of this week, Genesis increased that list by making the first Magma-badged model available in 11 more states.

The comprehensive list of states where the GV60 Magma is available now includes: California, Colorado, Connecticut, Maryland, Minnesota, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, Oregon, Rhode Island, Utah, Virginia, and Washington.

Still, despite the increased availability, 14 out of 50 states still leaves many potential buyers out of the loop, and the automaker hasn’t laid out a clear path to selling the car nationally. Speaking with Car and Driver at the Los Angeles auto show, a Genesis spokesperson declined to confirm any plans to continue expanding the GV60 Magma's availability.

If you’re lucky enough to live in one of the 14 listed states, the GV60 Magma is a truly delightful vehicle. The electric compact SUV packs a 641-horsepower punch and a laugh-inducing combustion soundtrack. Plus, despite its rather childish antics, the Magma is considerably more grown-up than the Hyundai Ioniq 5 N, its mechanical twin. There are Magma-specific front bucket seats, black upholstery with Magma orange stitching, and Magma-specific displays on the digital gauge cluster. Pricing for the GV60 Magma starts at $72,145, placing it slightly more than $10,000 above the Hyundai Ioniq 5 N.
