Type Soul tier list - ranking Schrift, Shikai, Res, Elements and Clans

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

This is a hub of different tier lists for Type Soul, ranking everything in the game, including Resurrect, Shikai, Bankai, Elements, Clans and more.

Type Soul is a super popular Roblox fighting game inspired by Bleach. You can choose to play as a Shinigami, Quincy, or Hollow and battle other players in fast-paced fights. You can expect some RPG elements too, as Type Soul features character progression, levelling up, learning new skills, and collecting powerful equipment. Naturally, you can fight against other players in various modes or team up with friends to defeat challenging bosses and explore dungeons. Explaining our Type Soul tier lists  If you are not sure how a tier list works, here's a brief explanation. The very best characters, items, and so on are placed at the top of the list (S-Tier or SS-Tier in some cases). The worst go to the bottom of the list. Pretty simple, right?

Schrift is a special ability exclusive to the Quincy faction. It's like Quincy's ultimate power, giving them special attacks and abilities. If you want to get the powers of a Schrift, you must complete a specific challenge after reaching a certain level as a Quincy.

Shikai and Bankai are powerful abilities available to Shinigami characters, just like in the Bleach series.

Shikai is the first stage of a Zanpakuto's release, unlocking its true form and granting the Shinigami increased power and new abilities. Bankai is the ultimate release of a Zanpakuto, offering a crazy power boost and often changing the Zanpakuto's appearance and abilities. To obtain these powerful forms, you must complete specific challenges and progress through the game.

Resurrection and Segunda are advanced abilities exclusive to Hollow characters in Type Soul.

Resurrection is similar to Shikai for Shinigami, unlocking the Hollow's true form and granting them increased power and new abilities.

Segunda is the equivalent of Bankai, representing the peak of a Hollow's power. It significantly enhances their abilities and often drastically changes their appearance.

Just like Shikai and Bankai, obtaining Resurrection and Segunda requires specific challenges and progression through the game. Tier Res/Segunda S Snake, Light, Arrogante, Pantera, Acid, Slash A Vampire, Storm, Starkk, Volcanica B Shark, Devil, Spirit, Phoenix C Bone D Horse
