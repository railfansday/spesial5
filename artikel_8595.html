The Figure HELOC, a home equity line of credit, pays out your whole line at closing, in as soon as five business days. Choose it if you’ll spend the money all at once. Its quoted rate leaves out the origination fee, up to 4.99%, so get that fee in dollars and compare lines with no closing costs.

Figure’s home equity line of credit (HELOC) suits homeowners who want a lump sum within days, since the whole line is drawn at closing, but its annual percentage rate (APR) omits an origination fee of up to 4.99%. By our math, a 4.99% fee on a 10-year line at 8% equals about 1.2 percentage points of APR, so a bank line with no closing costs is cheaper unless its APR is over 1.2 points above Figure’s.

Borrowers who will spend the whole amount at once, such as paying off credit cards, get a fixed rate and can have the money in as few as five business days. Anyone who wants to draw in stages or pay the least overall should compare home equity lines that charge no closing costs and let you borrow as you go.
