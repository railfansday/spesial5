Open a donor-advised fund at DAFgiving360 if the stock you want to give already sits at Schwab. You can move the shares online with no minimum, then grant $50 or more to the charities you choose over time. Expect a 0.60% yearly fee on the first $500,000, about $60 a year per $10,000.

DAFgiving360, called Schwab Charitable until 2024, is the donor-advised fund Charles Schwab founded in 1999 to take gifts now and send grants to the charities you recommend over time. Schwab subsidiaries handle its investing and recordkeeping, and its account shows up beside your Schwab accounts online, which makes it a natural fit for Schwab clients giving stock that has gained value.

For Schwab clients giving stock they’ve held more than a year, DAFgiving360 is the simplest donor-advised fund to use, with no minimum and gifts made online. Givers with six-figure balances, who pay $600 or more a year here, should first compare a donor-advised fund with a flat yearly fee, and those with shares at another firm should see whether that firm offers its own.
