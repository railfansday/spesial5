Get BusyKid if you pay your kids for chores and want that money on a prepaid card for one yearly price. The $48 fee covers the whole family, up to five cards, and stock investing. Skip it if you want interest, savings goals, or a place for a teen’s paycheck.

BusyKid is a chore and allowance app for kids 5 to 17, with a prepaid Visa card to spend what they earn. Each Friday a parent approves the week’s pay for finished chores, and the money splits among the app’s Save, Share, and Spend areas by percentages the parent controls.

Families who pay allowance for chores get the chore chart, up to five cards, and investing for $48 a year, though without interest, savings goals, or direct deposit. Parents with Chase checking who mainly want a free kids’ card should open Chase First Banking instead, which handles chores and allowances with no monthly service fee.
