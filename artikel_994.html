England's Penge grabs PGA Tour lead as battle to keep cards heats up

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Three players share the halfway lead at the RSM Classic, with only the top 100 players in the FedExCup standings after this week remaining fully exempt on the PGA Tour in 2027; watch the third round live on Saturday from 6pm on Sky Sports Golf

England’s Marco Penge bolstered his hopes of retaining full status on the PGA Tour for next season by grabbing a share of the halfway lead at the RSM Classic.

Penge started the week 120th in the FedExCup standings and requiring at least a top-four finish to break inside the top 100 and guarantee full playing status on the PGA Tour in 2027.

A birdie-birdie finish saw Penge post a second-round 65 on the Stadium Course, one of two used at Sea Island Golf Club, leaving him in a four-way tie on nine under alongside Michael Thorbjornsen and Denmark's Rasmus Neergaard-Petersen.

"I'm in a bit of a weird situation," Penge admitted. "I missed seven or eight tournaments this year from injury and have some sort of medical exemption still, which I don't really know much about.

"I'm trying to feel like it's the last event, but at the same time knowing that I have maybe three or four, five tournaments to start next year that goes towards this year's ranking. There's a lot of us guys trying to keep our job this week and we're all trying to give it our all."

Thorbjornsen is already fully exempt on the PGA Tour next season, having won the Rocket Classic in August, while Neergaard-Petersen - like Penge - started the week outside the top 100 in the standings.

A congested leaderboard sees a further six players a shot back, including Ryder Cup player Rasmus Hojgaard as he looks to rise from 122 in the season standings, while England's Harry Hall heads into the weekend just two behind.

"It [FedExCup ranking] will always be in the back of your head a little bit," Hojgaard said. "There's still so much more golf to be played. I'm happy with the golf I played so far and hopefully I can keep doing that for the next few days."

Brooks Koepka, Presidents Cup star Jackson Koivun and past Ryder Cup captain Keegan Bradley are all four behind, while Christiaan Bezuidenhout - starting the week in 100th spot - is in tied-41st for the tournament and projected to drop down the standings.

A three-putt bogey on the final hole saw England's John Parry miss the cut by a shot, seeing him currently projected to finish in 101st spot, while eight-time PGA Tour winner Billy Horschel played his last three shots in four over and also made an early exit.

Those 51st to 60th after this week qualify for the first two Signature Events of 2027, while those who end between 101st and 125th on the PGA Tour only receive conditional status and be unable to feature in certain events.

Players outside the top 125 face a season on the Korn Ferry Tour or a trip to Q-School to earn their PGA Tour playing rights, with first-round leader Svensson entering the week in 135th spot.
