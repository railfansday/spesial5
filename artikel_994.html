Robertson makes history with record 103rd Scotland cap

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Andy Robertson became Scotland's most-capped men's player with start against Switzerland in Nations League; he passed Kenny Dalglish's record of 102 caps; Robertson on Sir Kenny: "I don't want to forget and I don't want people to forget how important this man has been for our country."

Andy Robertson has broken the record for the most senior Scotland men's team caps, surpassing the feat held by Sir Kenny Dalglish for 40 years.

The Scotland captain made his 103rd appearance for the national side in Monday's 4-0 defeat to Switzerland in the Nations League.

He has bypassed the record set by Dalglish, who earned 102 caps between 1971 and 1986.

Before hitting the milestone, Robertson insisted he will always regard Dalglish as "the greatest".

"We've had so many great players for Scotland, but for me Kenny will always stand on top and I don't think that will change," the 32-year-old said.

"I don't want to forget and I don't want people to forget how important this man has been for our country."

Asked about what he told Robertson before the game, Scotland boss Sebastien Pocognoli said: "I just tell him that he has to enjoy it and after it's just a bonus for the future. So he needs to enjoy, to play with freedom.

"He has a lot of experience, so I don't need to tell him, but again, we are really proud of what we are living together with him. And the best way to have a good feeling for him is to have a good game."

Scotland midfielder Lewis Ferguson added: "It's amazing. First and foremost what a person, what an unbelievable captain, what a player and what an incredible servant for Scotland.

"It's a massive achievement, he should be so proud of himself and we're all just glad to be a part of that journey."

Andy Robertson spoke to BBC Scotland after the defeat in St Gallen:

"First half we were in the game but lost a stupid goal. But I thought we had chances and should have made it one each at half-time.

"We talked about it at half-time, we told ourselves to keep going and the time you get to 50 minutes, you're 3-0 down? That cannot happen in international football.

"That's what happened tonight. We were playing against a good team. But while we were in the game in the first half, you have to do it for 90 minutes. Then you're 3-0 down, it's a tough place to come and that. A really tough night for us."

"I don't think you've got time in international football, you have to do it. These supporters who travel everywhere, are they going to give us time? No. Are the people inside Hampden going to give us time? No. Are the people outside Hampden, who are going to be critical and rightly so? No.

"We don't have time, the players do not have time, we have got to step up.

"We have a long break now to March, which is not a good thing, and it is going to be a tough one to swallow."
