The $150 Billion Catch in US Shipbuilding

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

South Korea pledged $150 billion to revive US shipbuilding. But with the first shipbuilding contract between Hanwha Group and the US Navy worth only $269 million, it’s Washington’s turn to take the lead to ensure that promise is fulfilled, Bloomberg Opinion columnist Juliana Liu says.
