Jensen Huang May Be Back as Nvidia's CEO, but the Stock Is Likely to Remain Volatile

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Nvidia announced on Monday that it has reinstated Huang as president and CEO.

By David Jagielski, CPA – Nov 23, 2026 at 10:20AM EST

Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia (NVDA +32.52%) CEO earlier this month as multiple extramarital affairs came to light. But seemingly almost right after it happened, there were calls for him to come back to lead the company again.

It's been reminiscent of when Sam Altman was ousted as CEO of OpenAI a few years ago, only to end up coming back shortly afterward. Coincidentally, that also took place in November. And just like with that situation, Huang is back as CEO of Nvidia, with the company announcing the reinstatement in a press release on Monday.

The stock has been soaring in early trading since the news came out, and here’s why the volatility is likely to continue.

Huang's extramarital affairs have weighed significantly on Nvidia's stock, which entered Monday at a valuation of around $3 trillion. That's still high, but prior to the scandal, the stock was comfortably above the $5 trillion mark. News of the reinstatement could, however, get it back to around those levels again.

The volatility, however, is likely to continue because while Huang is back, that doesn’t mean the controversy is over. Investors shouldn’t forget that Huang was reportedly involved with a potential Chinese spy, and there are national security issues to consider. Huang is going to appear in front of Congress next month, and there may be issues that arise from that which may weigh on the stock once more. And that’s why there could still be uncertainty around the company’s leadership moving forward. If, however, Huang is cleared of any wrongdoing, then the path may be clear for the stock to get back to its previous levels.

The market has a way of overreacting to news, and this volatility involving Nvidia is an excellent example. It overreacted to Huang’s resignation, and it’s overreacting once again to his reinstatement.

The company wasn’t necessarily doomed without Huang, and it’s certainly not smooth sailing now that he’s back. There are still valid concerns around what happens with the business moving forward. While U.S. President Donald Trump has advocated for Huang’s return, saying it would be in the best interests of the company, there may be more political pressure facing the business next year, especially with the Democrats controlling Congress and potentially taking a tougher stance on these issues.

Nvidia is still a top growth stock and can be an excellent long-term investment, but investors shouldn’t be surprised to see more volatility ahead, and for those who aren’t willing to take on the risk, a wait-and-see approach might be more appropriate.
