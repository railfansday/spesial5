JD Vance blames Democrats for flight stoppage, claiming ‘obsession with men in women’s sports’

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The stoppage was seemingly caused by the transition from copper telecommunication lines

As Republicans scramble to shift blame for the recent chaotic FAA flight stoppage, JD Vance took a new tact, claiming a Democratic focus on transgender sports issues were to blame.

During an interview on Fox News on Monday, host Laura Ingraham pressed Vance on why his fellow Republicans had not been able to fix issues with air traffic control even though they had controlled the White House and Congress for the last two years.

“A lot of the stuff that needed to get fixed, we have been working on and we have been patching that stuff, Laura. But in terms of rebuilding the infrastructure using 21st-century technology, that is something that Congress needs to reinvest, give us the resources to do, and we will absolutely get it done,” Vance said.

Acknowledging that the Democratic party had taken back control of both the US House and the Senate after the 3 November midterms, Vance continued: “We could absolutely do this with Democrats in the new Congress if they set to side the ridiculous obsession with men in women’s sports and did what they said they would do, which was focus on affordability and focus on rebuilding our infrastructure.”

The comment sparked immediate outrage days after a four-hour nationwide flight stoppage on Friday that appears to have been caused by a transition from old copper telecommunication lines to fiber optic cable. The stoppage resulted in thousands of US flights being delayed or canceled, leaving passengers stranded or forcing them to resort to alternate travel methods to try to keep their weekend plans.

“What is this idiot talking about?” Fred Wellman, an incoming Democratic congressman from Missouri, posted on X.

“HE’S SO BAD AT THIS,” posted Pod Save America host Jon Favreau.

“Whatever consultant keeps telling him to randomly connect any issue to trans people is just stealing money,” journalist Jemele Hill posted on X. “The only person who is obsessed with this issue is JD Vance, and he’s not self-aware enough to realize how positively clueless this discourse makes him look.”

Donald Trump has also sought to downplay Friday’s flight stoppage and the related travel chaos. The president told reporters on Tuesday: “It’s like when you’re big and powerful, and you have all the money. Things like ⁠that happen. It’s fine.”

During a separate interview on Fox News on Monday, the Trump administration’s transportation secretary, Sean Duffy, said the Biden administration’s focus on cultural issues was to blame.

“The last administration wasn’t as focused on the infrastructure of aviation. They were concerned about what names we use for cockpit – they don’t want to have that name, they wanted to call it a flightdeck. Or ‘notice to airmen’ they wanted to change to notice air missions.

“They were concerned about the names in aviation, not the functionality of aviation.”

Duffy made a similar argument after a fatal 2025 plane crash in Washington DC that killed 67 people.

The Biden administration included $25bn for air traffic modernization in its signature infrastructure bill. Congress also recently allocated $12.5bn in funding to the FAA to modernize its systems.

US senator Chris Van Hollen, a Democrat from Maryland, said the administration has only spent about a third of that allocation. Democrats have criticized Republicans for trying to shift blame for an incident that occurred on the GOP’s watch.

“It’s really rich to hear the secretary pointing the finger at Congress when Congress has given the administration what they asked for,” he said on CNN’s State of the Union. “If they need more, they should come up and justify it.”
