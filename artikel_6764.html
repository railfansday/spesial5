Best Men's Studded Belts 2026: From RRL to Our Legacy

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Most people might build an outfit around a pair of shoes. Others, around a jacket. But few, surprisingly, do it around a belt.

At GQ, we’ve always said that the overlooked belt is more than the sum of its buckle and holes. Not only will it stop your slacks from going south, but a good leather belt can make just as much of a statement as your shoes, and deliver as much of a stylistic flourish as a tie can. Unconvinced? Enter: The studded belt. (Which, coincidentally, we're now increasingly seeing be offered by some of the most imitated brands in the industry, from Lemaire to RRL, Mfpen to Our Legacy.)

The charm of the studded belt is that it’s a vibe chameleon. Its decorative elements can pay tribute to any number of aesthetics, from Americana to punk to European artistry. And while any one will easily provide further flair to an already-singing outfit, the best ones also single-handedly bring even the most minimalist wardrobe to life. (And to be honest, that’s how I feel studded belts are best worn today: Against a white tee and jeans, for an easy win without changing your whole look.)

To help you find the right one for you, I've sorted through the vast waist-wear market to find a selection that won't just make your outfits sing, but set them on course for superstardom. See you out there, Stud.

Let's start gently: Somehow, Anderson’s has made a studded belt that whispers. It’s a small but mighty addition to their lovingly handcrafted Italian leather goods.

“If a stud falls out, make a wish,” is the outlook over at Htc Los Angeles—and they provide plenty of it. A true purveyor of the style, the brand has been specializing in rock-and-roll embellished leather goods since the ’90s. And they aren’t stingy with it either.

Trust Corridor NYC to come in clutch for all of your folksy Americana needs. The worn-in look of their studded belt—made in full-grain leather and tarnished hardware—carries all the tales of someone who’s worn it through the arid badlands.

If the fits over at the Ralph Lauren PoloFest in Tokyo last month have inspired you to join the RL tribe, any one of the many studded belts from RRL (Ralph's ranch-minded line) is a strong place to start.

As you’ll see from our list, Our Legacy—a staple for any serious fashion guy—has secured two call-outs. Here, they cut diamond studs to form a snakeskin-like pattern that, when worn against your best corduroy pants, gives the look some severe shine.

While we’re talking about studded belts as an easy hardware win, they're also the perfect place to score some color points too. Here, the green and orange mixes it up more than traditional turquoise.

Since its founding in 2012, Maximum Henry has been handmaking our favorite leather goods right here in New York City, including their beloved oval buckle belt. But they also know how to do a good, uncomplicated studded belt.

The floral studs on this OL belt aren’t merely decorative, but are punched into the very function of the eyelets. It’s a sweet look, really, and one done so effortlessly.

Dressing for winter, when all you ever want to feel is cozy, can leave you feeling like a schlub. There is, however, an easy fix in the form of the enviably Parisian stylings of Lemaire, who’ve released a brown belt with almond-shaped studs. Wear it with your all-denim outfit or heavy grey wool pants, it’ll make you feel like one of the brand’s cityslicking models in their closely-studied runway shows.

While we’ve so far been paying attention to how studs can add some play, texture still plays a key role to make an outfit sing. A Kind of Guise made their turquoise studded belt with a tan nubuck leather.

Lemaire might be better known for their artsy fabrics and drape-y cuts, but their accessories department is a treasure trove too.

Florals can be a lot generally for most guys, but Mfpen uses theirs sparingly on their lean 2cm wide belt. Made in patent brown leather, it also adds a nice formal sheen to a dark winter ensemble.

We’ve so far seen how studded belts can sway from Americana to minimalist vibes, but we can’t leave out the heavy metal rockers. Heliot Emil shaves off the edges for something more digestible, but still packs enough punch to cut through a monochromatic outfit.
