Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau Have a Rare Red Carpet Date Night

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau have another red carpet appearance to add to their relationship timeline. On Saturday, November 14, the singer and former Canadian prime minister stepped out together for the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala at the Pacific Design Center in West Hollywood, California.

The couple smiled and posed together affectionately in complementary ivory ensembles.

The outing was in support of Baby2Baby, a nonprofit organization that provides essential items to children living in poverty across the United States. Its annual gala raises millions of dollars to fund that work, bringing together celebrities, philanthropists, and business leaders. Among the other celebrity attendees this year were Cardi B, Barbara Palvin, Dylan Sprouse, Ciara, Jessica Alba, and Kerry Washington.

This year’s event honored Demi Moore with the Giving Tree Award, recognizing her commitment to children in need. Previous honorees include Serena Williams and Charlize Theron.

For the evening, Perry wore a look from Antonio Grimaldi’s spring 2025 couture collection, pairing a strapless metallic minidress with a floor-length champagne-colored skirt. Trudeau coordinated with her, wearing a cream tuxedo jacket, white dress shirt, black bow tie, and black trousers.

The evening marked their third red carpet appearance together, following their debut at the Tribeca Festival in June and their more recent appearance at the Lucas Museum of Narrative Art’s opening in September.
