Miles Apart, Gigi and Bella Hadid Serve Up Two Approaches to Winter Layering

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Sisters (and supermodels) Gigi and Bella Hadid are currently on their own separate journeys.

This week, Bella was spotted touching down in Aspen, Colorado, while Gigi is busy holding things down here in New York. What they’re up to, exactly, is a mystery: They could be gearing up for a major photo shoot, or simply enjoying some time off. But one thing is clear: The siblings are united in their approach to bundling up for the cold weather—and they’re both doing it in style.

Bella, for one, has been doing cozy-chic dressing in Aspen. Just a few days ago, she emerged wearing a comfy—but still polished—outfit that channeled an après-ski vibe. Today, she followed that up with a second look while departing her hotel: She wore a dark brown Alo Activewear set, including a fitted top and flared pants, and paired it with a fur-trimmed jacket and silver Nikes. (Don’t forget that chic suede Chanel bag, too.)

Gigi, meanwhile, broke up her ensemble with some seasonal and slightly more colorful hits. The entrepreneur donned a tailored dark brown overcoat and paired it with a yellow knit cardigan (presumably by her own brand, Guest in Residence), light-wash jeans, and tan brown leather moto boots. She also slung a soft-looking camel scarf around her neck—a fall-winter must for keeping warm in the brisk Manhattan streets.

While the two sisters clearly have their own separate fashion vibes going on, it’s immediately apparent that they also both know how to layer correctly. It’s all about offsetting bulkier pieces with more streamlined ones. Hadid, for example, paired clingy, skin-tight activewear with her plushy coat, while Gigi’s slimmer, more tailored coat proved to be the perfect pairing to her more snuggly sweater and scarf. Do as the Hadids do, because you want the outfit to read as sleek. . . not full-on bulky Michelin Man.
