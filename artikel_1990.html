Celtic star makes ‘terrified’ opposition claim after spearheading comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Celtic attacker Callum Osmand aims to build on the togetherness he felt at Parkhead on Thursday after experiencing both sides of the passion of supporters in four days.

Fans displayed their anger after Celtic’s 2-0 defeat at Falkirk on Sunday but the atmosphere was back to its best when Martin O’Neill’s side came from behind to beat Besiktas 3-2 in the Europa League.

Osmand sparked the comeback by scoring with his first touch after coming off the bench and later set up the first of fellow substitute Camilo Duran’s two goals.

Talking about the atmosphere, Osmand said: “You feel it, the crowd erupts and they really lift you. It’s incredible. When you’ve got Celtic Park like that, I feel for the opposition. They must be terrified because it lifts all of us and we’re so grateful for them.

“And the support when it gets like that is unmatched. It’s a great place to play and hopefully we can see more of those moments.”

Osmand had gone off at half-time at Falkirk but O’Neill’s triple substitution – with Duran and Colby Donovan also coming on – completed a major transformation in emotions.

“I completely understand what it means to people,” the 20-year-old said ahead of Sunday’s visit of Kilmarnock. “It’s a huge club and we want to be at the top and that’s what we strive to do.

“We know we need to be better at times, but I think it is just building off the good moments and learning from the bad and that’s all you can ask for. As long as we’re improving and getting better, that’s all you can really ask for.

“It is important to make sure everyone’s tight, especially when it’s going bad. That’s the main bit.

“Obviously, it’s a lot tougher for everyone when it’s not as good because the confidence, we might not be playing as forward as we should, and we all know that.

“But when the confidence comes and we’re just free-flowing, then everyone feels a lot better and we just need to keep building on it.”

Celtic face a Killie side who have conceded 28 goals in 12 William Hill Premiership games and whose manager, Neil McCann, has come under pressure from supporters.

McCann urged his players to show character in Glasgow on Sunday. “Every player has just got to be brave. You’ve got to continue to dig in.

“I’ve had so many occasions in my career where you could just hide, particularly on the wing when you’re getting battered and it’s not working for you. You’ve just got to keep taking the ball. It’s your job. You’ve got to keep being brave on it.

“If a manager wants you to play in a certain way and he’s given you the authority and the licence to go and continue to take it when you’re making mistakes, that’s the only way of coming through a difficult spot.

“And that’s where we’ve got to go to Celtic Park and really show our personality.”
