Mika Baur identifies the Celtic challenge he is ready to rise to

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Mika Baur is enjoying the demands of being a Celtic player – and was delighted to see the team display their fighting spirit and belief as they broke their Europa League duck.

The 22-year-old impressed as Celtic came from behind to beat Besiktas 3-2 on Thursday and get off the mark in the Europa League at the fourth time of asking.

The Hoops were heading for a seventh defeat in 11 matches when they fell behind early in the second half but the introduction of Callum Osmand, Camilo Duran and Colby Donovan on the hour mark sparked the team into life.

Manager Martin O’Neill recently admitted that some new players were still adapting to the demands of playing for Celtic but Baur and Duran have quickly proved they were successful summer signings.

Baur arrived from Paderborn after helping them to promotion to the Bundesliga and made his Germany debut last month, but moving to Scotland has been a new experience.

“To play every three days is different for me,” the midfielder said. “I think I have played the same amount of games now that I had last season by the winter break.

“But I like it. That’s what you want. You want to play all the time, more playing than training. I am really enjoying it.

“And I think the pressure to win is a good kind of pressure. It’s better than (trying) not to lose. So I really enjoy it.”

Baur has shown pace and skill on the ball but his pressing in the final stages drew massive appreciation from the crowd on Thursday.

“First of all, I want to work hard for the team, for myself, that I can prove myself and I can play on the stage, help the team, put in a shift, hard work,” he said.

“That’s what I have to do first. And then after this, the skill comes, playing with the ball. But first of all, it’s showing heart on the field. I was really happy that it was appreciated.”

The win over the Turkish side felt like it could be a major step in Celtic’s season as they bounced back from losing the opening goal and from a late equaliser, with Duran netting the winner seconds later.

“The second half was pure heart, pure fight,” Baur said. “I’m not here for a long time, but I think that’s what it’s all about. This win feels unbelievable because we deserved it. We fought for it.

“The team is really, really close together. We know that we had a tough couple of weeks, but you can feel it in the training sessions, how the team is working together, the feeling, the believing is always there. And it was there on the pitch. That’s what we have to show week by week, every three days.

“It was not just our win. The atmosphere was great. I’m really, really thankful for every supporter in the stadium. They gave us a lot of energy. We all fight together. That makes me happy.”
