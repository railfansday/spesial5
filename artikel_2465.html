Liverpool regularly scouting highly-rated Stuttgart and Germany defender

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

VfB Stuttgart centre-back Finn Jeltsch has been linked with a host of top European clubs after establishing himself at the heart of the Bundesliga side's defence and breaking into the Germany squad under Jürgen Klopp.

Another European heavyweight has now seemingly joined the list of suitors, with the Bild podcast “Bayern Insider” reporting that Liverpool are regularly scouting the 20-year-old.

FC Barcelona appear to be showing the strongest interest in Jeltsch, who has also been linked with Real Madrid, Inter, Juventus, Arsenal and Bayern Munich.

A potential transfer fee of €50-60 million has been mentioned for the defender, who is under contract with Stuttgart until 2030.
