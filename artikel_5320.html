Anthropic CEO and president each sold $280 million in stock during IPO

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Both executives sold Anthropic stock to underwriters at the $83 IPO price; the stock closed Monday at $87.64, above that price

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei disclosed selling 3,378,378 shares of Class A common stock to the underwriters of the company's initial public offering at $83 apiece on Nov. 20. The sale totals $280.4 million, according to Forbes.

Anthropic President Daniela Amodei, his sister and a company co-founder, disclosed an identical sale, according to Forbes. The seven co-founders were each projected to sell equal stakes, together valued at $1.96 billion, according to Benzinga, which cited the IPO prospectus.

Dario Amodei filed the Form 4 with the Securities and Exchange Commission on Monday. The filing says the $83 price is the one the underwriters paid in the offering.

After the sale, Dario Amodei directly held 202,785,471 shares of Class A common stock, some of them restricted stock units, the filing shows. He also holds 35,932,660 shares in trusts, where he has the authority to direct investment decisions or votes.

His combined holdings come to roughly $20.9 billion when valued at the $87.64 level where shares ended Monday, according to Forbes.

The filing also shows that Anthropic held back 345,011 of his Class A shares on Nov. 19 as payment for income taxes triggered when restricted stock units vested.

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83, above its initial range of $70 to $78, according to Benzinga. The company raised $81.3 billion in the Nov. 19 offering.

Anthropic's IPO prospectus outlined lock-up restrictions on sales of company stock. The co-founders are locked out of selling the bulk of their leftover holdings for as long as 12 months following the offering, according to Forbes. Up to 29% of their covered holdings can be sold once 180 days have passed since the prospectus date or Anthropic reports its first-quarter 2027 results, whichever happens first.

The co-founders had committed in the prospectus to giving 80% of their personal Anthropic stock to charity. The prospectus also showed Dario Amodei earned $18 million in 2025, mostly from stock and options.
