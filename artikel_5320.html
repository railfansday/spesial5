Pyth Network’s October numbers suggest its business is gaining traction, even as the crypto market keeps competing for attention. The latest report puts annual recurring revenue (ARR) at $13.67 million, up 18.9% month over month. More users are exploring Pyth Terminal, while new feeds and developer tools are pushing its market-data offering further.

Pyth added $2.17 million in net new ARR during October, lifting revenue from $11.49 million in September. Paying accounts reached 332, and 44.6% of gross new ARR came from existing customers expanding their usage.

Terminal activity also grew. The platform recorded 124,705 unique visitors, 4,451 signups and 591,621 feed views. Its Pyth Pro catalog reached 3,927 symbols, alongside 114 new feed IDs.

Pyth Pro added four Korean equity feeds, including SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics, with pricing available through Korea’s post-market session. Its MCP Server 1.1 also launched for AI agents, enabling feed discovery, plan checks and access to more detailed historical prices.

Separately, Exa is testing the network as a market-data layer for agentic search. The collaboration remains in testing, with Exa researching companies and events while network supplies current and historical market observations.

Pyth Indices expanded from 50 to 77 stable feeds. October additions included nine equity and ETF indices, 14 constant-maturity Treasury feeds and a diesel index.

The U.S. Treasury curve is now live on Pyth, covering seven maturities from two to 30 years, with par yields and discount factors published during European and U.S. market hours.

The Data Marketplace also added corporate-action information for supported U.S. equities, sourced from Exchange Data International. The data covers cash dividends, stock splits and related event details.

Pyth’s October report points to a broader ambition: more than simply displaying prices. With ARR rising and its data products expanding, it is building toward markets that operate across more hours and use cases.
