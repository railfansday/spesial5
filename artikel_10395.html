Google Home speakers get a major Gemini upgrade ‘soon,’ but only for paid subscribers

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Alongside the huge list of new updates Google detailed yesterday, the company also announced that a “faster and more natural” version of Gemini Live is coming to speakers and smart displays “soon.”

Google explains this is from Gemini Live moving to the “latest speech-to-speech architecture” and brings “significantly faster response times.” Gemini Live isn’t the default experience when you start a “Hey Google” command, rather hiding behind a “let’s chat” or other similar command to start a session.

Initially, Live sessions were purely for brainstorming or longer conversations and lacking support for commands and other functions mid-session, but Google now says that this upgrade will add support for actions such as smart home controls, setting timers, and more without leaving a Live session. That’s a big deal.

Coming soon – A faster and more natural Gemini Live experience: Available with a Google Home Premium subscription, Gemini Live is being upgraded to our latest speech-to-speech architecture for significantly faster response times and a smoother experience. Just say, “Hey Google, let’s chat” to brainstorm dinner ideas or plan a trip out loud, interrupt naturally to change topics, and perform actions mid-conversation—such as, setting a timer, adding a reminder,or controlling a smart home device—without stepping out of the chat³.

The “catch” is that you’ll still need a subscription to use this. Gemini Live has always required a subscription on Google Home speakers and smart displays, with the functionality looped into Google Home Premium plans which start at $10/month (or bundled into Google One AI Pro). That’s the same subscription that gives you Nest storage.
