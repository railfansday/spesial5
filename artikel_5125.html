Bitcoin’s Last Line of Defense Is $58K

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Katie Stockton, Founder of Fairlead Strategies, joins BMTV as Bitcoin falls below its 200-week moving average, sitting at about $63,000 per the host.

Katie Stockton, Founder of Fairlead Strategies, joins BMTV as Bitcoin falls below its 200-week moving average, sitting at about $63,000 per the host. She says her longer-term indicators for Bitcoin are somewhat constructive, pointing to a major low in June and July near long-term support between about $58,000 and $60,000. Bitcoin is headed back toward that support on negative short-term momentum, and she says there are no signs yet that the retracement is done. As long as it holds that range, she says, it preserves the potential for a long-term basing phase, backed by indicators like the monthly MACD and monthly stochastic oscillator.
