PenFed Kicks Off Holiday Celebration with Toys for Tots Drive

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Unwrapped toys can be dropped off at Washington Region PenFed branch locations through December 8

TYSONS, Va., Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- PenFed Credit Union, one of the nation's largest federal credit unions announced the kick-off of its Toys for Tots drive during the PenFed Holiday Celebration at PenFed Plaza at Tysons Corner Center. Members of the community are invited to bring new unwrapped toys to Washington region PenFed branch locations now through December 8 at 1:00 p.m. ET. Donations can also be made through the Amazon Wishlist.

For nearly 80 years, the U.S. Marine Corps Toys for Tots Program has served as a beacon of hope to children and families facing hardship across the nation. Since its founding in 1947, Toys for Tots has grown into one of America's most recognized children's charities, distributing more than 732 million toys and delivering hope to more than 325 million children.

Toys for Tots has identified a significant need for gifts for children under the age of 2 and youth ages 11-14. When selecting a donation, we encourage you to consider these age groups, as they are often the most underserved during the holiday season.
