Nvidia's board isn't considering bringing Jensen Huang back, is focused on investigation

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Free daily briefing on global business news.

A petition to reinstate the former CEO has drawn nearly 180,000 signatures as the board continues its investigation

Nvidia $NVDA's board is not weighing a return by Jensen Huang to the CEO post and is concentrating on an inquiry into his conduct, according to CNBC, which cited a source with knowledge of the situation.

The board has retained the law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to run an inquiry into whether Huang’s conduct put national security or corporate secrets at risk.

An online petition pushing the board to restore Huang to the job is approaching 180,000 signatures, according to CNBC. Backers include Elon Musk and Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney.

President Donald Trump said Monday that he “would not mind” if Huang returned as CEO and that he thought Huang had learned a “big lesson.”Altimeter Capital CEO Brad Gerstner said in a Monday interview with CNBC that Huang had not raised the idea of returning when they spoke.

Huang resigned last week amid reports that he had been unfaithful with at least two women, and the campaign to bring him back has since drawn support from tech executives. U.S. officials suspect one of the women, Alice Huang, of relaying information to China's intelligence services. Jensen Huang and Alice Huang are not related.

The board opened an independent investigation on Nov. 8, the day Huang admitted infidelity in a note to employees. After Huang resigned on Thursday, it named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO.

Gerstner and Gavin Baker of Atreides Management, both Nvidia shareholders, wrote a letter Friday urging the board to weigh all options, including "the potential return of Jensen Huang." The letter stopped short of demanding that he be brought back right away.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called for a dramatic shake-up at Nvidia on Sunday and said the board should seek its next CEO from beyond Nvidia's own ranks. He said he wants answers from the board and outside counsel within 10 days.

The board said Friday that it is selecting a search firm. "We are in the process of selecting a search firm for the Nvidia CEO role. We have not formally engaged any potential candidates," an Nvidia spokesperson told CNBC. The spokesperson characterized talk of specific names for the job as premature.

U.S. lawmakers plan to probe the national security ramifications of Huang's affair as well as his ties to Trump and the president's Cabinet, according to CNBC.

Nvidia will release results for its fiscal third quarter on Tuesday, Nov. 17.
