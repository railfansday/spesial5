Nintendo's Zelda remake sold more than 5.2 million copies in its first four days

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The Ocarina of Time remake posted the highest four-day sales of any Nintendo-published Switch 2 game, including 810,000 in Japan

Nintendo said “The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time” for Nintendo Switch 2 sold more than 5.2 million units in its first four days, including 810,000 units in Japan.

That is the highest global sales total for a Nintendo-published Switch 2 game in its first four days, the company said. The figure covers physical and digital versions.

The game launched Nov. 5 as a full remake of “The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time,” which came out for Nintendo 64 in 1998. Players control Link across two time periods, as Young Link and Adult Link, and use different abilities and items in each to advance. The remake has updated visuals and changes to playability while keeping the experience of the original, Nintendo said.

The game launched at $59.99 and sits at 96 out of 100 on Metacritic among both professional critics and players, according to Bloomberg.

Its four-day sales exceed the total sales to date of “Donkey Kong Bananza,” a 2025 release, according to Bloomberg. “Mario Kart World,” which shipped packaged with the Switch 2 console at its debut, is the only Switch 2 title that has outsold the remake.

The game also supports Zelda Notes, a service in the Nintendo Switch App for compatible smart devices, Nintendo said.
