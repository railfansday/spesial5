Gotham to host semifinal at 5k Icahn Stadium

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The club was fined $10k for the condition of the field following their regular season finale

Following a week of uncertainty, Gotham will host Sunday’s semifinal against the Kansas City Current at Icahn Stadium on Randalls Island in Manhattan. Questions lingered over the availability of several potential semifinal venues, but in the end, Gotham moved the match due to field conditions at the club’s regular home, Sports Illustrated Stadium.

“Unfortunately, given the field conditions, we have made the extremely difficult decision to relocate our semifinal game to Icahn Stadium,” club governor Carolyn Tisch Blodgett said in a statement announcing the venue. “We exhausted every possible avenue before making this decision, and we are deeply disappointed that so many of our fans will now be unable to experience this moment alongside our team.”

Icahn Stadium holds approximately 5,000 fans. Gotham’s 13 games at Sports Illustrated this season averaged just over 9,000. One of two games not played at Sports Illustrated was at Icahn in July against the Reign. A crowd of 4,738 was announced, although many either left or never arrived due to storms that delayed the start.

Gotham played the last two weeks at Sports Illustrated and the surface became a talking point. Following Sunday’s quarterfinal, Reign coach Laura Harvey said it was the worst pitch she has ever been a part of, and said Gotham coach Juan Carlos Amoros apologized to her pregame for the condition of the field. Harvey also let it be known that Gotham were fined $10k for it following the regular season finale against the Royals.

A Gotham spokesperson declined to elaborate on Harvey’s claims but The Equalizer has independently confirmed the fine. And Amoros was contrite in his postgame comments.

“I feel the obligation to to almost apologize for the state of the pitch,” Amoros said. “I think at this level in this kind of game, as a league, and we should be having a better a better surface.”

Added Tisch Blodgett: “Our players deserve a playing surface that meets the standards of a professional semifinal, and our fans deserve the opportunity to support them. We have worked tirelessly to deliver on both and are disappointed that we cannot. We are grateful to Icahn Stadium for working with us on such short notice to ensure our team has a place to compete for a Championship spot on Sunday.”

Gotham has one more season at Sports Illustrated. They are scheduled to move to Etihad Stadium, currently under construction in Queens, for the 2028 season.
