Germany's starting attacking quartet against Serbia revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Arsenal forward Kai Havertz is expected to lead the line for Germany in Friday night's UEFA Nations League away fixture against Serbia, according to Sky Germany.

Like Bild, the German broadcaster also reports that RB Leipzig's Brajan Gruda will make his debut in the starting line-up on the wing.

The creative midfielder will line up behind Havertz, alongside Liverpool star Florian Wirtz and Freiburg's Derry Scherhant, according to Sky.

Bild previously reported that Bayern Munich duo Joshua Kimmich and Aleksandar Pavlovic will play together in central midfield, while Borussia Dortmund's Felix Nmecha will initially have to settle for a place on the bench.

As confirmed by head coach Jürgen Klopp, Marc-André ter Stegen will start in goal, while Germany's back four is expected to consist of Philipp Treu, Jonathan Tah, Nico Schlotterbeck and Nathaniel Brown.
