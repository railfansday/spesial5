A.I. Weapons Need Data for Their Training. Ukraine Has a Lot to Offer.

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Ukrainian government has opened up a trove of five million battlefield videos to defense companies at home and abroad.

The forest, seen from a drone, appeared as a vast, monotonous landscape of black tree trunks, shadows and patches of snow.

To the human eye, it appeared deserted. But to an artificial intelligence system looking for people, it did not. The system put a red box around a tiny human form, probably a soldier lurking in the trees.

Behind this feat lay vast volumes of data. Just as ChatGPT and other A.I. chatbots must be exposed to how humans write, military A.I. systems must be exposed to how humans fight. In Ukraine, this happens through a huge trove of battlefield videos, audio and text data collected over nearly five years.

The war database is being used by about 30 Ukrainian defense companies to develop A.I. weapons. Ukraine is now opening this repository, which it calls Avenger Labs, to firms in allied countries, hoping to deepen security partnerships.

Ukraine is providing the data at no charge, but companies must give the Ukrainian military licenses to any software they develop. Under the first agreement to be reached, 12 British defense companies will gain access. Ukraine is in talks with other countries.

While there is no shortage of Western companies developing autonomous weapons, there is a shortage of the data needed to train them. Ukraine can offer more of it than any other potential partner.
