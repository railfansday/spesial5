NATO vows space defence after Russia nuclear satellite report

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

NATO chief Mark Rutte said Monday the alliance would defend against threats in space when asked about German media reports that Russia may be preparing to launch a nuclear-armed satellite. The media reports, citing unnamed German military and BND spy agency sources, said Moscow was thought to be planning such a launch in the coming weeks, giving it the capability to destroy other satellites. FRANCE 24's Laurent Berstecher reports.
