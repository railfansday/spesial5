Hugo Ekitike and Giovanni Leoni are also long-term absentees. The Reds will hope they will be back as soon as possible.

But another young superstar in the making in Joshua Abe has also been away due to an injury that he picked up in the summer.

Abe spent pre-season with the first-team before suffering an injury that has ruled him out for several months.

However, the 16-year-old seems to be on his way back. He has been called-up to England's U17 national team for their games this month, which means he is likely very close to being match fit.

Considering he is Liverpool's only natural right-winger, his return will be incredible news for Iraola.
