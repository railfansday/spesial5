Arsenal ratings vs Borussia Dortmund: Surprise 8/10 leader emerges as Viktor Gyokeres endures mixed night

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal were held to a 1-1 draw by Borussia Dortmund, as they failed to make their dominance count in the Champions League.

Viktor Gyokeres opened the scoring in the first half with a wonderful dipping strike from distance, but it then became a night of missed chances for the Gunners.

Maximilian Beier equalised after the break and despite the hosts pushing constantly for a winner, it would not come.

Matt Verri was at the Emirates Stadium to rate the Arsenal players...
