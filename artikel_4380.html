Soul Strike tier list - Characters ranked according to performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Updated on November 6th, 2026 - Version: 3.3.5 - Added: Laurent, Ruelle, Aiyo, Hannia, Riel

Are you just starting your adventure and want to know who the best characters are in Soul Strike? Well then, if that's the case, you will find the answers to your questions in this article. Below you can find our complete Soul Strike tier list where we rank every character in the game based on their performance.

Before we get to it, I should let you know that the ranking of these characters is based on my personal experience, as well as data that I've gathered from around the net from its community. That said, no tier list is absolute. Still, this will give you a pretty good idea of the current meta if you are just starting out.

Since you are clearly a fan of the genre, you might be interested in our Top Heroes tier list of every hero released so far. If you're a fan of similar games, we have created a tier list for AFK Journey as well.

Okay, with all that out of the way, let's get into our Soul Strike tier list!

These are the best characters in Soul Strike at the moment, so I've put them in the S-Tier. Now, why are they the best, you ask? Because they can get you through every stage of the game. Unlike the characters in the lower tiers, who are useful either in the earlier or mid stages of the game, those in S-Tier overperform even in the later stages.

Charlotte is one of the best damage dealers in Soul Strike. She has amazing AoE and can also debuff enemies, reducing their Earth resistance. She can also be somewhat of a second healer thanks to her ability King Slime, which summons slimes to heal allies.

Felino curses enemies, reducing their DEF, and whenever they die, the curse gets transferred to another enemy. He also inflicts bleed that causes enemies to take damage over time.

A-Tier characters are definitely strong enough to carry you through most of what Soul Strike has to offer. The main difference compared to S-Tier is that they are more dependent on synergy with the rest of your team. Regardless, they are solid options both as support and as damage dealers.

Akari's skill, Foxfire, gives him the ability to launch a fireball that gradually grows, inflicting damage to enemies in an area around it. The longer the battle goes on, the stronger Akari becomes. The fireball inflicts damage over time to enemies affected by it.

Nike is known for her ability to cause sustained damage to enemies. Her skill, Goddess of Triumph, increases Nike's move speed and ATK speed for a while.

Even though this is our bottom tier, it doesn't mean that the B-Tier allies are THAT bad but, obviously, they do not perform as well as the allies in the tiers above. The thing is, Soul Strike isn't like other games where some characters are completely useless. That said, you should look to replace them with better allies whenever you have the chance to do so.

Roy is a Rare Ally who excels at controlling the enemy. Every 15 seconds, he casts Sand Tomb on his last position, slowing every enemy in an area around it. If you have him on the team, make sure to switch his position in order to get the most out of Sand Tomb.

Even though Lingling isn't the strongest ally, she can still do some damage. Her basic attacks have a chance to cause a heavy blow to the enemy. Still, she is at the bottom of our Soul Strike tier list for a reason - there are much better choices for you. Download now! Soul Strike!
