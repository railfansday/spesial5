Sheet-Pan Chicken Caesar Salad

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

This is pretty clever, if we do say so ourselves—using roasted chicken thighs to create a Caesar salad, all in the same sheet pan. The wedges of lettuce, tossed in the leftover juices, soften and then everything melds in a comforting way. It’s a family favorite, with kids finding the dressing to be the closest possible thing to a “ranch” that hasn’t come premade in a bottle.

More sheet-pan dinner favorites this way.

Whisk together 1 cup/115 g mayonnaise, ⅓ cup/80 ml buttermilk or kefir, 1 tsp. Dijon mustard, 2 green onions, finely chopped, ½ tsp. ground onion, ½ tsp. ground garlic, ½ oz./15 g parmesan, finely grated (2 Tbsp.), and ¾ cup/15 g dill, finely chopped, in a small bowl, along with 1 tsp. fine sea salt and a few grinds of black pepper. Set aside.

Lay 6 chicken thighs, skin on and bones removed (about 1¾ lb./800 g), out flat on a parchment-lined baking sheet and season with ½ tsp. salt and a good grind of pepper. Add 1 lemon, very thinly sliced (about 12 slices), drizzle with 2 Tbsp. extra-virgin olive oil, and bake for 30–35 minutes, until the chicken is crispy and the lemon slices have started to caramelize.

Transfer the chicken and lemon to a shallow bowl and let rest for 10 minutes. Keep the baking sheet and parchment paper with all the pan juices. Finely chop half the lemon slices and add them to the dressing. Cut the chicken into ½"-/1-cm-thick slices and toss in the bowl with the rest of the lemon slices.

Add 2–3 heads of romaine lettuce, quartered lengthwise (14 oz./400 g), to the baking sheet, season with a pinch of fine sea salt and some black pepper, and turn them a couple of times in the pan juices. Spoon half the dressing over the lettuce and scatter the chicken on top. Finish with the rest of the dressing, a handful of croutons, store-bought or homemade, the extra parmesan, and a few sprigs of dill.
