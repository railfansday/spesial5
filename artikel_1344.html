Bruno Fernandes opens door to Sporting return amid doubts over Man United future as captain insists 'now is not the time' to discuss new contract

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Published: 17:50 EST, 24 November 2026 | Updated: 17:54 EST, 24 November 2026

Bruno Fernandes has left the door open for a return to Sporting Lisbon amid increasing uncertainty over his future at Manchester United.

Fernandes was a fans’ favourite at Sporting before joining United in January 2020 in a deal worth up to £68million.

The United captain has become equally popular at Old Trafford, but returned to Lisbon on Tuesday night with a question mark hanging over his next move after talks over a new contract stalled.

Fernandes’ current deal is due to run out at the end of June – and he will be able to speak to foreign clubs from January 1 – although United are expected to trigger an automatic 12-month extension to prevent him from leaving as a free agent in the summer.

Asked if he would consider a move back to the Jose Alvalade Stadium, where United meet Sporting in the Champions League on Wednesday night, Fernandes said: ‘I will never close the door to Sporting. It will always be a club with a special place in my heart.

‘Sporting was the first club that believed in my potential. I’ve always felt here from the fans – regardless of what may have happened – all the love and kindness from them, and that was essential to make me grow as a player.’

Bruno Fernandes has rejected two offers of a new contract to extend his stay at Man United

On his future at Old Trafford, Fernandes insisted that he wants to focus on turning United’s season around after they slipped into the relegation zone and replaced head coach Michael Carrick with Mauricio Pochettino.

‘We have time to speak about that,’ said Fernandes. ‘I know it's my future and I probably shouldn't look that way because I haven't got long left, but I'm more worried about results.

‘Contract talks have stopped for us to start winning games, and then we can talk about a contract. Now is not the moment.’
