Official: Italy talent Inacio out for ‘several months’ with serious knee injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Borussia Dortmund have confirmed that Samuele Inacio requires surgery and that he will be sidelined for ‘several months’ after suffering a serious injury to his knee during the Italy U21s’ friendly against Belgium at the beginning of this week.

Inacio had lined up as a starter for the Italy U21s in their 2-2 friendly draw against Belgium on Monday evening and was replaced by Seydou Fini just shy of the hour mark after suffering a blow to his knee.

Borussia Dortmund have confirmed in a statement on Friday that Inacio has been diagnosed with a meniscus injury, which will require surgery and ‘several months’ of recovery.

“The injury is extremely disappointing, especially for Samu, but also for Borussia Dortmund,” said BVB sporting director Ole Book. “Samu will receive our complete support in the coming months as he can make a return to full fitness.”

It is a major blow for the teenager, who earned his first cap for the senior Italy national side during the interim reign of Silvio Baldini back in June. The 18-year-old was also selected by Roberto Mancini for his first squad back in charge of the Italy national team back in September.

Inacio was only named in the matchday squad for the final game of Italy’s run of four Nations League matches during the extra-long September-October international break and remains uncapped in a competitive fixture.

He was not selected for the Italy squad in November and was instead left available for selection for Baldini’s U21 squad.

Inacio had made five appearances for Borussia Dortmund’s first team in 2026-27 before his injury.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
