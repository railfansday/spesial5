Amazon no longer considers the midrange Fire TV Stick 4K Plus as part of its official lineup following the launch of it latest Fire TV Stick 4K, but that doesn’t mean the streaming device isn’t still good value. In fact, it’s an even more attractive option now that it’s dropped to $29.99 ($40 off) at Amazon and Best Buy in the run-up to Black Friday. That’s just $5 shy of its lowest price to date, rendering it cheaper than both the entry-level Fire TV Stick HD and its new midrange replacement, the Fire TV Stick 4K.

The Fire TV Stick 4K Plus still offers a good balance of price and performance. Unlike its replacement, it supports both Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos, delivering more vibrant colors, better contrast, and immersive audio with compatible TVs and speakers. It does require a separate power adapter, though, whereas the newer model can draw power directly from your TV’s USB port. You also don’t get as much storage here as with the high-end Fire TV Stick 4K Max, though 8GB should be plenty for streaming apps like Netflix and Disney Plus.

The Plus model also supports Amazon’s redesigned Fire TV interface, which my colleague John Higgins found to be an impressive update. It makes it easier to browse different content categories and pin your favorite apps for quick access. Thanks to Alexa Plus support, you can also press the voice button on the included Alexa Voice Remote to find something to watch, control compatible smart home devices like lights and thermostats, check sports scores, and more without getting off your couch. Just don’t expect a customizable shortcut button, as that’s exclusive to the latest Fire TV Remote.

One final note: the Plus ships with Amazon’s Fire OS, and unlike the company’s Vega OS that ships in newer streaming sticks, the former allow for sideloading of Android TV apps, if that’s of interest to you.
