Amazon only announced its first digital photo frame less than a month ago, but it’s already getting a sizable discount for Black Friday. You can currently grab the Alexa Photo Frame at Amazon and Best Buy starting at $114 ($115 off), making it a great holiday gift for anyone who loves displaying family photos. If you prefer the walnut trim over the birch or standard black models, it’s also on sale at Best Buy for $134.99 ($95 off) — which, like with the others, is the first discount we’ve seen.

The Alexa Photo Frame features a 10-inch, semi-matte touchscreen that’s designed to make digital photos look more natural, with built-in light sensors that automatically adjust brightness throughout the day. It displays images from Amazon Photos, which supports uploads from both iOS and Android devices, including photos stored in iCloud. You can choose which people or albums to display, or let Alexa Plus do the work, which often results in photos based on your facial expression, the season, and even the lighting. You can also search for specific photos with voice commands, letting you easily pull up images of your first dog, your child as a baby, and other shots without having to scroll endlessly through years of photos yourself.

If you want to add a more personal touch, Amazon also lets you attach voice notes to individual photos via the Amazon Photos app. For example, you can add a recording of your kid explaining their Halloween costume to a trick-or-treating photo. Plus, you can preload the frame with photos and recordings before gifting it, so your loved one won’t have to spend time setting it up.

We’re still testing Amazon’s answer to the Aura frame, but it looks like a particularly good option if you’re already an Amazon Prime member. A Prime subscription grants you unlimited full-resolution photo storage and access to Alexa Plus at no additional cost, and unlike Amazon’s smart displays, the frame doesn’t serve up annoying ads. That said, you don’t need a subscription to use it, and new users can always access a limited-time trial of Alexa Plus.
