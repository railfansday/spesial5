The best Black Friday power station deals you can shop right now

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Amazon’s Black Friday sale is live, and while a portable power station won’t fix, well, gestures at everything, it can at least keep your phone charged when the lights go out.

The sale kicked off Nov. 19 and runs through Cyber Monday on Nov. 30, so you don’t have to wait until Black Friday proper on Nov. 27 to shop. Below, you’ll find the best Black Friday power station deals we’ve spotted so far, including Mashable-tested favorites from Anker, Bluetti, and DJI.

The Anker Solix C1000 Gen 2 is a Mashable fave, and for good reason. Our power station expert, Lauren Allain, gave it 4.5 stars and a Mashable Choice Award after spending two weeks using it to charge her devices and run appliances, including a rice cooker, blender, and air fryer.

It weighs just under 25 pounds and can fully recharge in 49 minutes. (You’ll need to turn on UltraFast charging in the Anker app to get that speed.) You can’t add an expansion battery, so make sure its 1,024Wh capacity works for you. But for weekend camping trips or occasional power outages, it has a lot to offer.

And if you missed it during October Prime Day, you’re getting another shot at the same price. The Anker Solix C1000 Gen 2 is on sale for $469.99, down from $799, at Amazon. That’s a 41% discount, or $329.01 in savings.

Not everyone needs a 25-pound power station. If you’re mostly trying to keep your phone charged while you’re out and about, Allain says the Bluetti Elite 10 Mini is her “go-to” for quick trips off-grid. It weighs just four pounds, has a built-in handle, and can recharge your phone about six times.

There’s also a light on the back, which she likes using as a warm nightlight in her tent. You get an AC outlet, two USB-C ports, and two USB-A ports, too, though the 128Wh battery is better suited to small devices than power-hungry appliances.

Right now, you can get the Bluetti Elite 10 Mini for $139 at Amazon, down from $199. That’s $60 off, or a 30% discount.

If your packing list includes a drone, cameras, and a laptop, this one’s for you. Allain calls the DJI Power 1000 V2 a “near-perfect” portable power station for creators, especially DJI drone owners. Its dedicated ports can fast-charge compatible DJI drone batteries, but you’ll need to buy the cable separately.

It’s super quiet, so you’re not adding a bunch of fan noise to whatever you’re filming. And with four AC outlets, two USB-C ports, and two USB-A ports, there’s room for the rest of your gear. A full recharge from a wall outlet takes 56 minutes; solar charging requires a separate DJI adapter.

Right now, you can get it for $379.99, down from $419.99, at Amazon. That’s $40 off, or about 10% in savings.
