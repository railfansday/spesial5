Top 10 Resorts in the West for Weekend Getaways (2027)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The perfect ski weekend starts with less time spent getting there and more time on the mountain. For skiers trying to squeeze in a quick getaway, a resort’s proximity to a major airport or big city can make all the difference. Add plenty of terrain to explore, easy transportation, and convenient lodging and après options, and you’ve got the makings of a seriously good trip.

If you’re plotting a few long weekends this winter, start here. These 10 Western ski resorts make getting to the slopes refreshingly easy, whether you’re flying in for a couple of days or hitting the road from a nearby city. They earned top marks for access in SKI’s annual Reader Resort Survey, making them reader-approved picks for maximizing your time on snow and minimizing the travel hassle.
