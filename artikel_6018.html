4 of nation's largest cities now led by socialist mayors after Tuesday's election, affecting tens of millions

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

'The Big Money Show' panel analyzes the market rally after Democrats took control of Congress. The panel says the key election issues were based on affordability and immigration.

Two socialist mayors were elected in the United States on Tuesday in Los Angeles and Washington, D.C. which means four of the top 20 largest cities in the country have socialist leaders.

Los Angeles Councilwoman Nithya Raman, a member of the Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) defeated incumbent Mayor Karen Bass and will take office in December as mayor for the roughly 4 million residents of Los Angeles proper.

Also victorious on Tuesday was DSA member Janeese Lewis George, who coasted to victory to become mayor-elect of Washington, D.C., a city of roughly 700,000 residents.

The list of socialist mayors in the top 20 most populous U.S. cities stands at four, after George and Raman joined Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson and New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani.

In total, the four cities are home to roughly 14 million people. That figure does not include neighboring suburbs, which could still feel the effects of policies enacted within city limits through crime, real estate values and the broader business climate.

Problems in a city can spill over into surrounding communities, while suburban residents who work in the city may also be directly affected.

Counting the population in the metro areas of those four cities, roughly 44 million people could be affected either positively or negatively by the socialist policies enacted by the four mayors.

"This makes LA the fourth large city that will now have a socialist mayor, joining New York; Washington, D.C.; and Seattle," Heritage Foundation fellow Mike Gonzalez posted on X Thursday.

"Chicago's mayor is a buffoon, but he doesn't identify as DSA. America used to be an impregnable fortress against Marxism, then Marxists took over universities."

The socialist victories were not limited to city halls. Voters also elected seven DSA-backed or affiliated candidates to the House: Claire Valdez, Brand Lander and Darializa Avila Chevalier of New York; Chris Rabb of Pennsylvania; Melat Kiros of Colorado; Donavan McKinney of Michigan; and Angela Gonzales-Torres of California.

Several other candidates associated with socialist policies also entered Congress or were re-elected Tuesday.

A recent Fox News national poll indicated 32% of voters have a favorable view of socialism, with 64% seeing it in an unfavorable way. But the favorable rating matches an all-time high, first reached five years ago, since Fox News first began asking the question in 2019.

And a September Gallup poll found that 43% of Americans had a positive image of socialism, the highest level since the polling organization began tracking the question in 2010.

The Fox News poll found that 51% of voters had a favorable view of capitalism, compared with 32% who viewed socialism favorably. Another 46% had an unfavorable view of capitalism.
