Weichert joins HouseCanary Google Listings Program

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Weichert, Realtors joined HouseCanary’s Google Listings Program, adding company-owned office listings to a Google mobile carousel in selected markets across 5 states. The feed is supplied via My State MLS as HouseCanary expands the program nationwide this fall during Chapter 11.  AI Summary

Weichert, Realtors has joined HouseCanary’s Google Listings Program, making active listings from the brokerage’s company-owned offices visible in a dedicated carousel within Google mobile search results across selected markets, according to an announcement on Wednesday.

Under the integration, listings from the RealTrends Verified brokerage’s company-owned offices in New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, Virginia and Maryland now surface at the top of Google mobile search results in live markets.

“Weichert is one of the most recognized names in residential real estate, and its decision to join reflects how the US’s largest brokerages are recognizing the value of getting as much exposure for their listings as possible,” said Chris Rediger, CEO of HouseCanary.

Listings in the Google experience appear in a swipeable carousel that includes basic property details and listing brokerage attribution. Consumers can tap through to a detail page that shows additional home information, the listing agent’s name and a click-to-contact button that routes them directly to the brokerage.

Launched in early 2026, HouseCanary announced that the program is expanding to all eligible U.S. markets this fall.

Listings for Weichert’s company-owned offices are supplied by My State MLS, according to the announcement. Other MLSs currently participating in the Google Listings Program include Arizona Regional MLS (ARMLS), Bright MLS, California Regional MLS, IRES MLS, Permian Basin Board of Realtors, REcolorado and San Diego MLS.

Over the past few weeks, HouseCanary has announced that real estate brokerages RealPlus, REMAX Advantage, Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Florida Properties Group, ENRG Realty, Side and The Keyes Company and Illustrated Properties, all RealTrends Verified firms, have signed on to the program, sharing its listings with HouseCanary via My State MLS.

These announcements also come as HouseCanary continues to work through its Chapter 11 restructuring. Under the firm’s proposed plan, which remains subject to court approval, HouseCanary is targeting emergence from bankruptcy before year-end, with full recovery for all approved creditors. Management has positioned initiatives such as the Google Listings Program and the AI platform as core to its go-forward strategy while it continues to serve existing customers. A company spokesperson told HousingWire that the firm expects to emerge from Chapter 11 restructuring before the end of the year, pending court approval.
