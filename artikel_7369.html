How AI and social media are changing the media mix

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Think about the last time you made a purchase online. Where did you start? A social post may have sparked your interest, while search or an AI tool helped you decide what to do next. This kind of cross-channel shopping is becoming more common. According to the 2027 Global Digital Report, 42% of online adults use social media to research purchases, and 30.4% of internet users discover new brands through social media. AI also plays a clear role: 26% use it for brand research, and 17% discover brands through LLM platforms such as ChatGPT.

Generative AI has moved quickly from novelty to a practical shopping aid. In a short time, personalized recommendations and shopping assistants have become familiar parts of online shopping. New applications keep appearing, giving consumers more ways to find useful information before they buy.

Online adults now use an average of 3.8 channels when researching brands, up from 3.65 two years ago. People are using those channels at different points in the purchase process.

One in three people globally already use generative AI each month, and usage is likely to keep growing. Social media is also claiming more of people's daily attention. Globally, people spend an average of 42 minutes more on social media than they did just a year ago. Social commerce is expanding too. More than one in four people now make purchases through social platforms, generating an estimated $586 billion annually.

For marketers, this is an energizing moment. More choice gives brands more ways to meet audiences and support their decisions. A strong media mix starts with clear goals and a close reading of digital behavior. Intelligence is always in style, and that understanding should guide where brands invest.

Where and how do our audiences spend time online?

Most leading social platforms share large portions of their audiences. For example, 78.5% of people who use YouTube also use Instagram.

Strong media mixes prioritize the platforms where audiences are most engaged and where each channel can play a distinct role.

The differences between platforms can guide those decisions. Instagram is the favorite social platform for 17.8% of online adults. TikTok has the highest average daily time per user, at one hour and 55 minutes. WhatsApp leads in average daily sessions, at 20.7.

Local data makes the picture even more useful. X has the highest daily usage time in Japan, at 32 minutes and 44 seconds. Threads has the highest rate of monthly usage in Taiwan, at 40.8%. Pinterest has the highest advertising reach in the Netherlands, at 56.7%. In Ireland, Reddit leads in average daily sessions per user, at 7.88.

These details turn broad platform trends into practical investment decisions.

Which social media platforms influence our AI visibility most?

LLMs can draw on social media content when responding to prompts. Tracking AI visibility over time can show which channels influence the responses most relevant to your audiences.

Each brand will see a different pattern. Reddit may matter most for product-comparison questions. Creator content on TikTok may shape answers about trends or recommendations. For a B2B company, LinkedIn may matter most when AI platforms respond to questions about its expertise and offerings.

These insights give marketers evidence about how audiences find information and which platforms deserve closer attention.

The modern customer journey is more connected than ever, and that makes measurement more important. Last-touch attribution captures only the final step, so it can miss earlier influence.

Multichannel measurement gives marketers a fuller view of how channels contribute along the path to purchase. Bringing those signals into one view helps teams see which interactions support results and where channels reinforce one another.

Digital behavior data shows that AI is changing the role social media plays in discovery. As consumers move from one digital touchpoint to another, marketers can build a clearer view of how those interactions connect.
