OpenAI’s Monetization Chief to Ad Partners: User Trust Means Some Things ‘We Just Can’t’ Do

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

OpenAI’s head of monetization, Benji Shomair, said Monday that the AI lab is using trust as a guiding principle in developing its ads business, but acknowledged that this limits what it can provide to partners.

OpenAI is eager to work closely with adtech and agency partners to scale its nascent ads operation, which started testing in February. But the company is bound by its commitment to keeping users’ ChatGPT conversations private from advertisers and to never selling their data to advertisers, Shomair said onstage Monday at an event hosted by adtech-focused investment bank Luma Partners.

“There are places where our partners would like us to do certain things that we just can’t because of how we think about making decisions,” Shomair said.

Kendra Barnett is Adweek's senior tech reporter.
