Catherine visits Mumbai maternity hospital on the last day of royal trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Prince and Princess of Wales ended their three-day trip to India on Wednesday, the couple's first international tour since 2022.

Catherine visited a maternity hospital in Mumbai and was seen comforting a grieving mother who lost a baby after giving birth to premature twin girls.

William met actors Kareena Kapoor, Saif Ali Khan and filmmaker Karan Johar during a visit to a film production company.
