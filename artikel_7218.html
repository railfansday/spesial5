X adds new video thumbnail and caption options

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Let Social Media Today's free newsletter keep you informed, straight from your inbox.

The platform continues to build out its video tools in hopes of encouraging more uploads from users.

X added some new video creation tools on Friday, as the platform keeps pushing for more uploads in order to drive in-app engagement.

First, X reiterated that video is the fastest-growing format in the app.

As per X: “Posts with video get 5x the reach and engagement, and 36% of all impressions now go to video.”

X has been leaning into video content since 2024, when it reported that 4 out of 5 user sessions in the app included video consumption, and overall video views were growing at a rate of 35% year-over-year.

Given these stats, it makes sense for X to keep adding video updates, and for creators to pay attention in order to tap into the expanded reach potential of video clips.

X’s latest video features include improved captions, the capability to choose a video thumbnail, and more layout options.

On captions, X said creators now have more styles and color to choose from, providing another creative consideration.

Captions have been a big focus of X's video push, increasing the accessibility and viewability of videos in-stream.

X added multi-language captions, as translated by Grok in July, while it also rolled out an updated captioning process in October, which includes the option to manually edit captions in the composer, or ask Grok to correct them.

These latest additions will make captions a more dynamic option within X’s video toolkit.

Creators can also now manually choose a video thumbnail frame from their uploaded clip.

That could help creators ensure that their videos stand out in-stream.

Indeed, the right cover image can be a big lure in driving more video engagement. That’s why YouTube’s top creator MrBeast reportedly pays up to $10,000 per thumbnail for his clips, and generates up to 20 variations for consideration with each upload.

Another potentially valuable consideration for creators.

X also added more text overlay tools, as well as new layout options to help format video clips.

As explained by X: “Short videos play best vertical. They fill the whole screen, so viewers stay locked in. Now you can turn a horizontal video into a vertical one in one tap with Fill, Fade, or Stack. Just tap Edit when you upload.”

The new features build on X’s other recent video additions, including a fully revised video creation flow. X is also working to ensure that in-stream videos load faster, while the app has also launched new incentive programs for video creators, and new tools for live-streaming.

Given the engagement stats, this focus makes sense, and it could be worth experimenting with video clips on X to see how they perform in the app.
