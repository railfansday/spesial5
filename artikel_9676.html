Swiggy Scraper in Python: Dish Prices and Menus by Location

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Swiggy is India's biggest food delivery app, and its search answers the question every food business asks: what does this dish cost across the restaurants that deliver here? One search for "biryani" returns about 50 restaurants and every biryani on their menus, with price, veg flag, rating and delivery time. Getting that into a CSV takes one short Python script, but there are three things in the way: an AWS WAF challenge, a web page with no data in it, and results that change completely with the delivery address.

The 202 is the tricky one: a status check that only looks for errors lets it through, and the script parses an empty string. I route every request through Scrape.do from India (geoCode=in). A plain request is enough here, 1 credit each.

A restaurant page like /city/bangalore/starbucks-coffee-ashok-nagar-rest124178 comes back as 78 KB of HTML with no menu in it. The browser fills it in afterwards from JSON endpoints under /dapi, so those are what the script calls:

Every response is a list of widget cards with an @type. The dish search returns one DishGroup card per restaurant, each holding the restaurant and its matching dishes; the rest are filters and suggestions. Money is in paise: "price": 42000 is ₹420, and some dishes only carry defaultPrice.

One request, 304 dishes from 50 restaurants. 13 of those dishes came from restaurants Swiggy marks as promoted: paid placements mixed into the results, worth a column of their own.

lat and lng decide which restaurants deliver, how far they are and how long delivery takes. The same search in two cities:

Not one restaurant appears in both lists. Change the coordinates and you get a different market, so record them with every row. A menu still loads for a far-away address; the restaurant comes back with serviceability: NON_SERVICEABLE instead of a delivery time.

The menu endpoint returns every section of a restaurant's menu, plus its FSSAI licence and address. One catch: the "Recommended" section repeats dishes from the other sections. Starbucks in Ashok Nagar has 168 dish cards and 148 dishes. Same script, continued:
