Is WordPress Still a Good Choice for a New Client Site in 2026? Be Honest.

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Here's a number that surprised me. According to W3Techs, WordPress ran 43.0% of all websites on 1 January 2026. Today it's 38.7%. That's after a decade of almost nonstop growth, from 23.3% in 2015.

Shopify, Wix and Squarespace didn't take all of it. Most of the gap shows up in sites W3Techs can't match to any CMS at all, which I read as more custom builds and AI-generated sites. I could be wrong about that.

Either way, it made me ask a question I hadn't asked in a while: if a small business came to me today for a new site, would I still pick WordPress?

Where I've landed for now: WordPress for anything the client will update often or sell through, something lighter for sites that barely change. But I keep changing my mind on the middle ground.

I'd especially like to hear from people who switched and regret it, and from people who switched and never looked back.
