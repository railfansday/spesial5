Zoho CRM territory management: setup and pitfalls

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Posted on Nov 5 • Originally published at skyfallhq.net

Disclosure: I work at Skyfall Labs, an official Zoho Partner, so I have a commercial interest. The facts below are from Zoho's own help pages, linked at the end; advice is labelled as advice. Originally published on skyfallhq.net.

When sales teams share accounts across regions, industries or product lines, role hierarchy alone can feel too rigid. Territory management groups accounts by their characteristics instead of only by owner. This post sets out what Zoho's pages say, including choices that cannot be undone.

Zoho says a territory groups customer accounts within the sales force structure so they can be shared with sales staff, and that territories can be based on geography, industry, product line, expected revenue or verticals. Zoho says users with the Administrator profile can enable the feature. The overview page does not name editions, so check feature availability for your edition.

This section is advice from experience, not vendor documentation.
