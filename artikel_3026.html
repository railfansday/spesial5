MFA Financial (MFA) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Rate-driven losses dominated, but succession and new loan deals now matter most.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

MFA Financial, Inc. (MFA -0.45%) reported a GAAP net loss for the third quarter as sharp increases in treasury yields and wider mortgage spreads resulted in significant unrealized mark-to-market declines on its portfolio. Management reported that the company remains focused on transitioning leadership and resolving its legacy credit book while deploying capital into a higher-yielding environment where new acquisitions are averaging coupons of 7.8%. The company reported improved underlying earnings power, with distributable earnings prior to realized credit losses rising to $0.44 per share. CFO Mike Roper noted that although near-term headwinds exist from maturing interest rate swaps, the company has options to offset these impacts through the continued recycling of unproductive capital and utilization of a balance sheet capable of supporting more leverage.
