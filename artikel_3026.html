Is Interactive Brokers (IBKR) Stock a Buy After a 71% Dividend Increase?

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The stock has averaged annual gains of nearly 23% over the past 15 years -- and it's still growing.

Are you familiar with Interactive Brokers (IBKR +0.57%)? You might want to learn more about this company, as its stock has been a standout performer. Check out the stock's average annual gains in the table below.

Data source: Morningstar.com as of Nov. 9, 2026.

Over the past decade, this stock has turned a $10,000 investment into about $100,000.

Current shareholders are likely excited by the company's third-quarter report, because among other things, it announced a dividend increase of 71.4% -- from $0.0875 per share per quarter to $0.15. That hike wasn't enough to give the stock a fat dividend yield, though -- the yield was recently just 0.8% -- but a fast-growing payout should still be of interest to investors.

The company has been around for nearly 50 years, and its mission is to "create technology to provide liquidity on better terms. Compete on price, speed, size, diversity of global products, and advanced trading tools." It offers electronic trading services for stocks, options, futures, currencies, bonds, gold, crypto, and more, and, along with its affiliates, executes more than 4 million trades per day.

But you might be wondering whether it's too late to buy the stock. Based on the stock's recent valuation, it seems fairly attractively priced. Its price-to-sales ratio of 3 is above the five-year average of 2, and the forward-looking price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio of 22 is nearly on par with the five-year average of 21. Those are reasonable numbers for a fast-growing business.

Near-term economic factors, such as interest rate changes, could provide headwinds or tailwinds for Interactive Brokers, but over the long run, the company is well-positioned to continue growing.
