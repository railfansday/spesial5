Should You Really Buy Nvidia Stock After CEO Jensen Huang's Exit? Wall Street Has a Clear Answer for Investors.

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Jensen Huang’s resignation creates considerable uncertainty, but most analysts think Nvidia stock is deeply undervalued.

Nvidia (NVDA -2.01%) has dominated the headlines in recent days. CEO Jensen Huang resigned from his position after revelations of extramarital affairs, including a relationship with a businesswoman that U.S. intelligence officials suspect was spying for China.

Nvidia's board of directors launched an investigation into the potential national security implications of the news, and Huang officially resigned on Thursday, Oct. 12, leaving the semiconductor company he co-founded more than three decades earlier. The board also appointed longtime CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO while it searches for a permanent successor.

Huang has been praised regularly for visionary leadership in recent years, and has become one of the most influential figures in the artificial intelligence revolution. Some investors worry his departure will have devastating repercussions for Nvidia. Indeed, the stock is down nearly 20% this month, and it currently sits 32% below its record high.

Read on to see what certain Wall Street analysts have to say on the topic.

Stacy Rasgon at Bernstein cut his target price in half. He now believes Nvidia is worth $200 per share, down from $400 per share. Rasgon is less concerned about the near-term consequences of Jensen Huang's departure, and more worried about how his absence might impact the company in the long run, given how closely he managed business operations and his level of involvement with customers, suppliers, and the broader AI industry.

"We aren't really worried about the near-term impacts to the business as a result of the events of the past week, and of course the shares screen extremely cheap," said Rasgon. But he does not necessarily advocate buying the dip. "We see considerable uncertainty around further news flow that is probably going to be coming down the pipe, as well as the ability of any new CEO to truly fill Jensen's shoes."

Rasgon was not the only analyst to reduce their target price substantially after Huang's exit. Ben Reitzes at Melius Research cut his target to $185 per share, down from $315, citing uncertainty beyond fiscal 2027. Tristan Gerra at Blair cut his target to $200 per share, down from $500, highlighting uncertainty about Nvidia's leadership and a potential slowdown in AI infrastructure spending in 2027.

The most severe revision came from Mark Lipacis at Evercore, who slashed his target to $186 per share, down from $465. Nvidia currently trades around 12 times forward earnings, the cheapest valuation in 15 years. But Lipacis thinks the multiple will drop further because "Nvidia has no obvious succession plan or number two operational executive." Additionally, he believes the semiconductor boom associated with the AI revolution is now in the later innings.

Meanwhile, Matt Bryson at Wedbush reiterated his outperform rating and maintained his target price of $345 per share. He acknowledged the uncertainty surrounding the executive transition, but said strong demand for AI infrastructure coupled with Nvidia's very cheap valuation means "almost any level of uncertainty is already baked into the stock."

Finally, Joseph Moore at Morgan Stanley and Timothy Arcuri at UBS maintained their target prices of $300 per share. However, Arcuri described Jensen as a "singular force" who kept Nvidia far ahead of peers in terms of supply chain security. He also believes the national security ramifications of the situation could prompt the government to impose even tighter export controls, perhaps in regions like Singapore and Malaysia.

Here's the big picture: While some Wall Street analysts lowered their target prices following news of Huang's resignation, most still believe the stock is deeply undervalued. Indeed, Nvidia currently has a median target price of $277.50 per share, which implies 70% upside from its current share price of $163. Long-term investors should consider buying a small position today.
