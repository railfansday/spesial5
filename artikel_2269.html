Birmingham City dodged bullet by not signing Middlesbrough man in the summer: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Birmingham City were said to be keen on landing the midfielder from Hadjuk Split in the last transfer window. However, the BCFC-linked man ended up joining fellow Championship side Middlesbrough instead.

Pukstas, 22, has since struggled for minutes at the Riverside Stadium and has played just 22 minutes for Kim Hellberg’s side since his switch to England. He is also currently out with a hamstring injury until early December which isn’t ideal.

Therefore, the Blues dodged a bullet by not signing the USA youth international and will be relieved that they didn’t fork out a hefty fee for his services. Boro paid £4.3million (via Transfermarkt) and handed him a long-term contract running until June 2031 but he has found it hard to get into their starting XI due to competition from the likes of Aidan Morris, Riley McGree and Sebastian Berhalter.

Birmingham City are sat in 3rd place in the table after 14 fixtures and slipped back into the play-offs after Wolves won 2-1 at Bristol City on Wednesday. They beat Millwall 3-0 at home last time out and take on Swansea City this weekend.

Birmingham City will have identified Pukstas as someone to bolster their midfield department. If he had joined BCFC, he would have given them more depth in the middle of the park.

At the age of 22, he has potential to grow and develop down the line. However, it wasn’t to be and the Blues were beaten to the punch by Middlesbrough.

Nevertheless, Chris Davies won’t be too disappointed now and he is well stocked up with options in Pukstas’ position. He has Paik Seung-ho, Tomoki Iwata, Jhon Solis and Taylor Gardner-Hickman at his disposal.

Max Bird joined instead of Pukstas in the end and he is slowly starting to prove his worth. The ex-Derby County and Bristol City man scored against Millwall earlier this week and is growing in confidence.

Birmingham City have enough options in midfield and also have Marc Leonard out injured. BCFC will get the chance to bolster their squad further in January.

The Blues are unlikely to look for another central midfielder this winter and will be happy with what they have. Instead, they are likely to look at attacking midfielders and wingers.

Davies’ men are on a roll right now and will be in good spirits. They are rock solid in defence and have the best defensive record in the division right now.
