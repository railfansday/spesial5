Viral Red Baron worker pauses $787K fundraiser as he steps away from spotlight

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Red Baron employee Walter E. Zerod has announced he’s pausing donations to his viral GoFundMe, which has raised over $787,000, as he looks to step away from the spotlight and spend time with his family.

The father of four became an internet sensation in late October after a video showed him confronting an Instagram prankster who pretended to steal money from him while he was stocking a grocery store freezer.

During the confrontation, Walt explained that he was struggling to pay his mortgage while working long hours to support his children.

The footage sparked an outpouring of support, with homeless veteran Luke Oakenshield launching a fundraiser that has since collected more than $787,000 from over 22,000 donations.

However, in a November 5 update on his GoFundMe, Walt announced that he was bringing the fundraising effort to a close and would no longer be making public statements.

“Your kind words have meant the world to my family and I this past week. I still cannot believe how much you have all rallied behind us, and I can never thank you enough,” he wrote.

“I’ve been able to spend lots of extra time at home with my family this week.”

Despite previously considering interviews to share his story, Walt revealed that he’s decided against making any media appearances.

He also confirmed that donations would be paused on Thursday, November 5, exactly one week after the internet began rallying behind him.

“If you have the means and would like to continue helping others, please consider supporting another fundraiser, a local charity, or a church helping families in need this holiday season,” he added.

Walt also gave an update on his friendship with Oakenshield, who received his own GoFundMe after supporters discovered he was a homeless veteran living out of his car.

“I’ve also stayed in touch with Luke, and I look forward to continuing that friendship as he navigates all the blessings that have come his way.”

Walt concluded by announcing that this would be his final public update as he prepares to spend the holiday season with his family.

“Thank you for your kindness, your generosity, and for making my family feel so supported. We will never forget it.”
