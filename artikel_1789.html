‘We’ll see’ – De Bruyne tight-lipped over PSV links

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Kevin De Bruyne kept his cards close to his chest regarding rumours that he could be set for a shock move from Napoli to PSV in January, saying "what comes, comes".

Reports in the Netherlands on Thursday claimed that PSV have been in contact with De Bruyne over a potential move following speculation that he is unhappy at Napoli.

The Belgian joined Napoli on a free deal back in June 2025, following his departure from Manchester City, though it has not been the smoothest ride in Serie A.

He missed over four months of 2025-26 with a hamstring injury and, at the end of the campaign, was in the spotlight for claiming he was "happy" to see Antonio Conte leave.

De Bruyne's current contract is set to run out in June, though the club have the option to extend the deal for an extra 12 months.

He has featured in all 15 of Napoli's matches in 2026-27 so far, netting four goals – all in Serie A – but Italian media have still suggested a potential move away could happen in the upcoming transfer window.

A post shared by Kevin De Bruyne (@kevindebruyne)

Following Belgium's 2-1 win over Turkiye, in which De Bruyne scored, he was asked about the interest from the Dutch side, though he refused to confirm any deal.

"I can't say too much about it," De Bruyne said, as quoted by HLN. "At the moment I'm happy.

"I feel good; I play everything at a good level. And what comes, comes. It is not the time to talk about it now.

"I know how it works. There are always many rumours. I stay calm under it – you know me. I am happy with my level and how I feel physically. That is the only thing that matters."

When asked if the interest is mutual, he simply added: "We'll see."
