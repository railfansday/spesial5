Barnsley FC boss gives updates on Reyes Cleary and Ben Hamed Toure plus his reaction to 'You're not fit to wear shirt' chants

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The winger missed the midweek trip to Bradford City through illness and is being assessed today.

Ben Hamed Toure, who was also absent in midweek, is also unlikely to be involved in the capital, with Stendel confirming that one or two players also have other problems including striker Makenzie Kirk.

Stendel, whose rock-bottom side are winless in their past 11 league matches, having taken just two points from a possible 33 available, said: "Ben Hamed is not in and he is still not really good. Reyes is in, but not ready to train and we have to look today after he does some things, a little bit of running.

"Then, we will see what we can do. Maybe we can take him (for the weekend), maybe not.

"Some other guys have little problems and we have to look. Makenzie Kirk had some things after the game and (Cieran) Slicker.

"To be honest, we will know on Saturday. At the moment, the situation is not so easy without all other things (form).

"At the moment, I don't expect it would change so much to Tuesday, Maybe one or two players less than Tuesday. But we will have 11 players to start."

Barnsley's beleaguered players received a damning chorus of 'You're not fit to wear the shirt' after the final whistle of the derby at Valley Parade.

On those chants, Stendel said: "Maybe there's two sides. The first is that its right from the supporters - especially after the result, maybe not the performances in the league games.

"Everybody is upset. The fans who travel invest a lot of time and money and emotions for this club and team.

"When you are upset, you say things like that. This is not different at Barnsley to other clubs in the world.

"On the other side, I also have to support and protect my team. They are trying to do their best. It is not enough at the moment for the expectation of what we have as a club and also not for the supporters.

"But I expect and know that they will support the team again on Saturday at Wimbledon and I can only promise that every player who plays and is in the squad will do their best and give everything for Barnsley Football Club and for success in this game.

"It is not easy for the players, supporters and us, but this is our job and we have to sort it."
