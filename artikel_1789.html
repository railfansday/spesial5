Confirmed: Liverpool expected to begin talks over massive deal soon

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Liverpool are expected to begin talks over a massive deal this week.

When Julian Ward took charge of the club a big part of his responsibility became not only deciding who to sign for the Reds but who to keep and who to let go.

Recruitment choices like that are often the most difficult and most unpopular.

Sometimes, you have to let go of big characters in the dressing room, who everyone will be disappointed to say goodbye to. Other times, it's about deciding to release or sell a club legend, who the fans adore.

Ward has been there and done it. He's made tough calls, and he's going to need to make even tougher calls as Liverpool embark on a new era.

Liverpool's big contract decisions and who will likely get an extensionBefore Ward took charge of the club, his predecessor extended the contracts of both Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch. They are now on long-term deals. Meanwhile, there are several key players approaching the end of their current terms. Virgil van Dijk, Joe Gomez and Alisson Becker all have expiring contracts. The chances are that Liverpool are likely going to extend Van Dijk's deal. Gomez is unlikely to be offered an extension. However, the jury is still out on Alisson. Liverpool were open to selling him in the summer. However, they are also open to retaining him for another year if the two parties can come to an agreement. Then there's also Trey Nyoni and Rio Ngumoha. Both have deals expiring in 2028 and both deserve new terms more befitting of their status in the squad.
