FirenzeViola: Mendes offers Milan full-back to Fiorentina ahead of January

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Agent Jorge Mendes has held discussions with Fiorentina about a potential move for Pervis Estupinan, a report claims.

According to FirenzeViola.it, Fiorentina want to sign a midfielder and a full-back in January. More specifically, they plan to add a left-back to allow Valdepenas to develop more comfortably and to give Parisi time to regain his fitness.

For a few weeks now, as is customary at this time of year, Paratici and his collaborators have been letting agents know what their needs are ahead of the transfer window, asking for updates on possible outgoing full-backs who could be a good fit.

In this regard, a friend of Paratici’s – Jorge Mendes – has already presented a profile that could interest Fiorentina: Pervis Estupinan, who is expected to leave Milan. The Ecuadorian has never truly nailed down a starting spot since signing in the 2025 summer window for €17m plus €2m in bonuses.

Despite using him 10 times (446 minutes in total this season), Amorim has already given the green light to his departure. With a contract until 2030, he could also leave on loan, perhaps with an option to buy. Then, his €2.5m net per season salary would be paid half by Milan and half by La Viola.

Mendes and Gestifute are well aware that a move to Florence could be a good one to secure him playing time and visibility, and they have already contacted the Fiorentina management. Estupinan seemed close to leaving in the summer but Amorim blocked it, though now things have changed.
