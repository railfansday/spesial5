Milan Futuro 3-0 Virtus CiseranoBergamo: Pandolfi shines in third straight win

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

It was a clean sweep for AC Milan’s men’s teams over the weekend, as Milan Futuro won their third straight in the league.

The Futuro got back in the win column last weekend as they managed to secure a thrilling 3-2 victory away against Virtus Verona on Sunday. Then, they followed that up by beating Piacenza 2-1 in midweek, in the game that had been suspended due to an injury to the referee.

So, there was some good momentum for the second team heading into Sunday afternoon’s game, and they built on it with a commanding and convincing 3-0 home win over Virtus CiseranoBergamo.

Fabio Pandolfi opened the scoring just after the half-hour mark as he finished from close range after an excellent assist from Aurelien Guernier on the right, making it 1-0 which is how it would stay going into the break.

Early in the second half, Pandolfi got his second after a deflected left-footed shot wrong-footed the goalkeeper Rovelli and nestled into the net. In the closing stages, Cappelletti led a counter-attack and squared to Yahya Idrissi-Regragui, who made no mistake and made sure of the win.

It was another important result for Sergio Navarro’s side, who extended their unbeaten streak to six consecutive matches. Next up for the Rossoneri – who are third and two points off top – is another league game after the break against Tritium on 22 November at 14:30 CET.
