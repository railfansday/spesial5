Juventus keen on a January swoop for Tottenham’s Destiny Udogie

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Juventus are closely monitoring Tottenham defender Destiny Udogie ahead of the January transfer window, according to a report from Ekrem Konur over the weekend.

Not included in Roberto De Zerbi’s squad for their 1-0 loss away at Leeds prior to November’s international break, Udogie has drastically failed to stake any kind of claim under the former Brighton boss this season.

Dealing with multiple injury setbacks and concerns over his fitness, the Italian international has made just a single Premier League start since a 0-0 stalemate away at Nottingham Forest on September 5th.

With summer arrival and former Liverpool star Andy Robertson immediately cementing his spot as De Zerbi’s number one choice at left-back, there were suggestions over the summer to Udogie was open to a permanent move away from north London.

However, failing to find any real stability since completing a £15million move from Serie A outfit Udinese back in 2022, the 12-cap international is reportedly firmly on Juventus’ radar.

Konur reported on Saturday that the Old Lady are closely monitoring Udogie’s situation in north London and are keen on a potential January swoop.

It has also been suggested that Juventus are hoping to agree an initial loan deal when the window reopens and have either an option or obligation included ahead of next summer.
