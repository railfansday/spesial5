Barnsley FC head coach Daniel Stendel on fans chants after latest loss to Bradford City

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The rock-bottom Reds suffered a second successive defeat to Yorkshire rivals to remain anchored to the foot of the table.

They have taken two points from the last available 33.

Former Oakwell striker Stephen Humphrys netted twice for City before a stoppage-time penalty from Jake Beesley added the gloss.

It was the prelude to chants of ‘You’re not fit to wear the shirt’ from some Reds followers at the final whistle.

He said: “It's normal reaction from the supporters.

"I think everybody has to know and everybody can watch (look) on the table and look at our position and our points after the games. I expect that everybody understands the situation and how important this season is for Barnsley Football Club.”

On his take on the game, Stendel, who confirmed that Reyes Cleary and Ben Hamed Toure missed the game throughout illness, added: “Nobody can say we played a bad game, nobody can say Bradford earned to win this game. But the win, 3-0, this is what happened.

"It's frustrating, not only for me, for every player in the changing room.

"Again, we conceded two easy goals like Friday. You can see the quality from the striker from Bradford today.

"And this is the difference, not the quality from the team, not how we play. In the end, it's quality (from the striker Humphrys).

"I know it's really difficult for you and maybe for the supporters to see positive things, but I see a lot of positive things.

"We have to sort out the things in our box and in the opposition box, better. But this is what we say every week and I think it's not because of the style of play, it's not that the players didn't want it.

"They have a good mentality, they showed the passion again today, but I think in the end we missed the last quality maybe.”
