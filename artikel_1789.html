Liverpool legend Steven Gerrard accuses Mauricio Pochettino of disrespecting Man Utd star

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Steven Gerrard fears Mauricio Pochettino is already on collision course with one of his senior Manchester United players following Sunday’s damaging 4-0 defeat against Liverpool.

Any hope of a new manager bounce was extinguished during a frantic opening quarter of an hour at Anfield yesterday when the hosts roared into a two goal lead following Leny Yoro’s sending off.

Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz piled on the agony in the closing stages and ensured the scoreline gave a more accurate representation of Liverpool’s near total dominance.

While playing the entire match at a numerical disadvantage certainly played a role in the one-sided nature of such an eagerly anticipated contest, United were second best from the off and were made to pay for their new manager’s questionable team selection.

The decision to drop Harry Maguire at the expense of Matthijs de Ligt, who is still feeling his way back from a long-term injury, was questioned by the likes of Jamie Carragher and Gary Neville before kick-off and their skepticism was vindicated when the Dutch centre-half played a role in Yoro’s red card.

Gerrard, meanwhile, went a step further in his criticism by accusing Pochettino of showing a lack of respect to Maguire by relegating him to the bench in his first game at the helm.

‘I thought he got it wrong,’ Gerrard told The Overlap with Sky Bet. ‘I thought he got it wrong in the back line. I think to start someone in a game of that magnitude, it’s the biggest game in the country. All eyes are on it all over the world.

‘To play someone that hadn’t played since last November. You can understand the manager wanting to come in and put his stamp on it and change a few things that had been going on previously, but Harry Maguire’s their best defender at the moment.

‘Forget the youngsters that are maybe not ready at the moment. Matthijs de Ligt’s been out for a year. Harry Maguire’s their best defender at the moment. He should have started the game.

‘And he made a difference when he came on. And he was one who was putting his body on the line. He made a great block, I think it was around the 38th, 39th minute, which stopped a certain goal. And I’ve watched Manchester United a lot this season, and he’s one who’s having a go.

‘He’s one who is rolling his sleeves up and having a go. Okay, he hasn’t been perfect, but to not play him and put de Ligt in before him, I thought it was a bit disrespectful.’

Pochettino and United will have to soldier on with what they have until January when the transfer window reopens and strengthening a defence which is now official the worst in Europe’s top five leagues will represent a priority.

Gerrard added: ‘It’s just who’s available and who wants to sell and let good players go at the moment, so they’re going to have to pay big money.

‘There’s going to be a tax with that, because if United want to go and take, they need world-class centre-halves in January, cause waiting until the summer might be too late, so they need world-class centre-halves.

‘Who’s going to let world-class centre-halves go in January? Unless someone’s prepared to pay astronomical money. That’s what’s going to have to happen.’
