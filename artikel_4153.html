GI Podcast: Digging Into Our Tomb Raider Cover Story

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

This week on The Game Informer Show, we've got a new magazine cover to discuss! Alex and Marcus hop on to chat about their trip to see Tomb Raider: The Legacy of Atlantis, and discuss our retrospective on Lara Croft's legacy in games.

Before that, though, Kyle and Eric join to talk about The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time's new Switch 2 remake, and after the Tomb Raider section, Eric takes us on a tour of Queen's Domain and Alex shares his early thoughts on World of Warcraft: Forever. Grab a snack and settle in for a new episode of The Game Informer Show.

The Game Informer Show is a weekly podcast covering the video game industry.
