Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices EWM Realty Partners With RELIANCEai; Launches New Website

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices EWM Realty (BHHS EWM Realty), the South Florida-based real estate brokerage, unveiled its newly redesigned website in partnership with RELIANCEai™, a real estate technology and agentic AI provider.

According to a release—after joining HomeServices of America in 2003 and rebranded as BHHS EWM Realty in 2019—the company has facilitated thousands of multi-million-dollar transactions. BHHS EWM Realty has also earned recognition as a four-time “Best Brokerage” award winner from South Florida’s real estate industry leaders.

Today, BHHS EWM Realty serves buyers and sellers across Miami-Dade, Greater Fort Lauderdale/Broward and Palm Beach counties, representing properties across a wide range of price points and communities.

Beyond residential real estate sales and luxury property marketing, the release notes that the company also offers customers access to mortgage financing, title settlement services and insurance through OnePoint, The Complete Real Estate Experience.

“At BHHS EWM Realty, we believe technology should enhance relationships, not replace them,” BHHS EWM Realty COO Sherrie Porter said. “Our new website is more than a fresh look for EWM.com; it’s part of a broader commitment to providing our customers with an exceptional real estate experience while empowering our sales professionals with innovative tools to better serve them. We’re combining the personal service and local market expertise that have defined EWM for decades with the technology that will help drive our future.”
