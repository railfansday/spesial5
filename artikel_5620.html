On the higher timeframes, NEAR has been consolidating in the $3.75–$4.00 range following a broader pullback from higher levels earlier in the cycle. Price remains above key intermediate support levels but faces resistance in the $5.00–$5.50 zone.

In a typical bull market, a sustained breakout above $6.00–$7.00 would confirm intermediate strength and open the door to higher-cycle targets, potentially leading to a long-term recovery toward the previous all-time high of around $20.

In an extreme scenario of decentralized AI agent and data marketplace adoption, breaking through significant resistance levels could trigger powerful measured-move extensions and multi-cycle Fibonacci projections based on the longer-term foundation.

Current momentum indicators are neutral, with considerable room for movement into the extreme overbought territory typically seen during parabolic advances.

Key near-term support levels are located around $4.20–$4.30 and $3.80–$4.00. Maintaining these levels would help preserve the broader recovery structure.
