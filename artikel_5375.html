Wonder Unveils Wonder Create, Empowering Anyone with an Idea to Launch Their Own Restaurant

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Founding Creators and brands set to launch their own concepts using Wonder Create include Ally Love, Andy Cohen, Chopt Creative Salad Co.,Girl Scouts of the USA, Livvy Dunne and more.

NEW YORK, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Wonder, the vertically integrated platform built to make great food more accessible, today announced the launch of Wonder Create, a groundbreaking new platform that allows anyone with an idea to design and launch their own restaurant.

Wonder Create unleashes a brand new path to restaurant ownership, where traditional barriers to entry are removed, and where creativity and technology will allow for a new era of culinary innovation. The Wonder Create platform allows creators and brands to bring their vision to life by helping craft their menu, build their brand and connect with their audience. Wonder's team handles the full process including sourcing, cooking, delivery and pick-up, with dishes instantly available across Wonder, Grubhub and Seamless.

Moving far beyond standard sponsorships and limited-time offers, Wonder Create's inaugural Founding Creators are among the first to launch their own concepts through the platform with fully tailored menus and distinct branding. "No company can imagine all the food the world wants to eat. Wonder Create gives the world the tools to create it," said Marc Lore, founder and CEO of Wonder. "We're putting our culinary expertise, robotics and AI in the hands of anyone with a great idea, so they can build and own a food brand and reach millions of customers without building or operating a single kitchen. As our network grows and our robotics expand what each kitchen can make, the possibilities for creators grow with us. That means more variety for customers and more ways to make great food accessible. I believe the next great food brands will come from people who never thought they could start one."

Wonder Create will give creators and brands the flexibility to build restaurants their way from menu composition and pricing to logos and branding. Wonder's AI-powered tools help shape original ideas into full concepts, allowing users to utilize as much or as little of the platform's design and culinary capabilities as they want and make changes along the way. Users can lean on the platform for custom development, or upload their original photography, recipes and logos to personalize their restaurant's taste, look and feel. Wonder Create orders are prepared using Infinite Makeline, Wonder's automated food assembly system, which is designed for accuracy, speed and consistency.

Creators will own their concept and brand, set their own prices (subject to a minimum price) and earn a revenue share on every order, while paying as little as $10 a month per location for each restaurant.

Every Wonder Create concept will get a content screening and review before it goes live. Now, creators and brands can build salad, bowl, wrap and burrito-focused restaurant concepts across cuisines from Mexican to Mediterranean to Hawaiian pokÃ©, drawing on a library of more than 130 ingredients, with plans to expand into more categories and cuisines over the coming year.

Concepts will have access to the same ingredients and culinary standards as Wonder's own restaurant brands, which have been developed with the support of chef partners like Bobby Flay, Jonathan Waxman and Marc Murphy. Every order features the concept's custom branding for instant recognizability.

"I'm a dad, a host, a producer and now a 'restaurant owner,'" said Andy Cohen. "Wonder Create makes it possible to bring a new idea to life without slowing anything else down, and I can't wait to see where it takes me."

"Some of the most exciting moments in Girl Scouts happen when a girl discovers something new about herself, a passion she didn't know she had, an idea she wants to bring to life or a creative spark she wants to follow," said Meridith Maskara, Chief Development Officer of Girl Scouts of the USA. "Our partnership with Wonder brings together two organizations that believe in the power of ideas and possibility. It will help us create even more meaningful experiences that encourage girls to explore what interests them, express their creativity and discover all that they can do."

"Wonder Create gives us a whole new way to experiment and grow," said Nick Marsh, CEO of Chopt Creative Salad Co. "It is a game changer for the launch of our new Power Bowl collection, allowing us to easily iterate and see what our guests love. With Wonder Create, we are excited to introduce our food to communities where we don't have a Chopt restaurant yet."

"We congratulate Wonder Create on bringing this innovative platform to life and are grateful for their commitment to making a difference in our communities," said Leslie Gordon, President & CEO of Food Bank For New York City. "Through this collaboration, New Yorkers have an opportunity to help nourish their neighbors with something as simple as ordering food from our restaurant concept on the platform. These orders will help provide food and resources to individuals and families facing food insecurity."

"With Wonder Create, I can launch a real restaurant concept that's my own, learn as I go and grow it into something bigger, all while finishing my degree."

"With three kids and a schedule that never slows down, mealtime is always a juggling act," said Minsun Kim, a working mom of three in New York City. "Wonder Create let me build a restaurant around exactly what my family needs, with nutritious meals everyone actually wants to eat!"
