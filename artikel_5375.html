Nvidia’s once-unstoppable ascent could be heading toward a historic collapse. What if the recent personal and national-security scandal engulfing CEO Jensen Huang spirals into something far darker? Sam Altman’s ChatGPT AI predicts an uber-bearish fallout from the news, which could trigger an unprecedented crash in NVDA stock.

In this over-the-top bearish scenario, the independent board investigation into his admitted affair with Alice Huang, a businesswoman U.S. intelligence officials suspect of working for China’s Ministry of State Security, uncovers evidence of compromised discussions, influence operations, and potential technology leakage.

The fallout triggers aggressive US government action: expanded export bans that effectively cut NVIDIA off from residual China-related revenue streams, congressional hearings that force Huang’s resignation, multiple shareholder lawsuits, and a broader regulatory cloud that freezes major data-center deals with hyperscalers wary of geopolitical risk.

Confidence in the company’s leadership and supply-chain integrity evaporates, competitors seize market share, and the AI spending boom that powered NVIDIA’s valuation begins to look overextended.

The most immediate and damaging development is the unfolding scandal itself. In early November 2026, Jensen Huang publicly admitted to an extramarital affair and apologized to his family and employees, while NVIDIA’s board launched an independent investigation into reports that he had been targeted by a Chinese intelligence operation.

Shares already reacted with sharp volatility, including a roughly -9% single-day drop around the initial reporting. Beyond the personal drama, the episode has intensified scrutiny of NVIDIA’s China exposure and export-control compliance at a time when U.S.-China tech tensions remain elevated.

Additional pressure points include recent broad market weakness in high-valuation tech names, signs of digestion in AI infrastructure spending after years of explosive growth, and the simple reality that NVIDIA’s valuation still prices in near-perfect execution. Any confirmation of deeper security concerns or leadership disruption could accelerate institutional selling.

NVIDIA has already broken down from its recent highs near the $240 area and is trading around $186–$187, having sliced through multiple short-term support levels.

The stock has formed a clear lower-high, lower-low sequence on the daily and weekly charts, with elevated volume on down days signaling distribution.

The 50-day and 200-day moving averages are rolling over, and momentum indicators such as RSI have spent time in oversold territory without producing a sustained bounce—often a sign that sellers remain in control.

A decisive break below the $180–$175 zone would open the door to a measured-move decline targeting the mid-$100s, with further downside possible if the $150 psychological level fails.

In a full-panic scenario driven by a leadership crisis and regulatory shock, multi-year Fibonacci retracements and prior consolidation bases from earlier in the AI cycle could come into play, supporting a much deeper correction.

While NVIDIA remains a dominant force in accelerated computing, an escalating personal and national security scandal, potential leadership upheaval, and a market already questioning AI valuations create a plausible path to severe downside.

By January 1, 2027, in this highly unlikely but catastrophic scenario, NVIDIA stock could fall to the $55–$75 range, with a stretch downside target near $40 if confidence fully unravels.

While NVDA is unlikely to go this low, the Jensen Huang affair with a suspected Chinese spy hasn’t been fully unraveled, and further revelations could spell disaster for Nvidia.
