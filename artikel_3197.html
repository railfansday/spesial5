Apple Home accessories: Here are my five top picks for your smart home

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Apple Home is kicking off a new era with Siri AI-driven products like HomeView, making it a great time to upgrade your smart home with Apple Home-compatible accessories. Here are my top five picks.

iOS 27 upgraded Apple’s HomeKit Secure Video service (included with iCloud+) in several key ways. You’ll now get searchable videos, highlighted clips, 4K support, and more.

I have two recommendations for HomeKit Secure Video-compatible cameras. The main difference is video quality, and whether they’re designed for outdoor use or only indoors.

Smart locks have always offered convenience, but with the advent of Ultra Wideband-supported locks, they’re better than ever.

Aqara’s U400 smart lock was among the first to introduce Ultra Wideband, which enables your door to lock and unlock automatically when it senses your presence. Unlike prior smart lock automations that relied on Bluetooth, Ultra Wideband offers excellent reliability and precision for a great overall experience.

Want to open your garage door with a quick Siri command, or via CarPlay or the Home app on iPhone? Smart garage door openers with Apple Home support can deliver that, and my recommendation is the meross MSG150HK.

meross says the MSG150HK is compatible with “the vast majority of Chamberlain, Lift-Master, and Craftsman brand openers.” And it supports security features like automatic notifications when your door is left open, or every time it opens and closes.

Smart plugs are often a gateway to building out a smart home. And my favorite option, both for cost and convenience, is Tapo’s multipack of smart plugs.

Tapo sells its smart plug in a 2-pack at a great price—currently only $8 per plug. These plugs are compact and offer seamless, quick setup through the Home app without needing a separate app.

Controlling your thermostat entirely from your iPhone or a HomeView can prove extremely convenient. That convenience can also save you money by making it easier to stay on top of your electricity usage.

Aqara’s W200 smart thermostat is a beautifully designed, easy-to-use accessory that comes with a bonus Apple feature that’s hard to find: Adaptive Temperature, which can remove lots of the need to manually update the thermostat.
