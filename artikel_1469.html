Over 62,000 Roma fans to attend Sassuolo match

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Roma wants to put the disappointment of Manchester City behind them.

To get back on track, the Giallorossi must win against Sassuolo in their final match before the international break.

After the international break, Gian Piero Gasperini will be fully recovered, with the exception of Lorenzo Pellegrini, and will be able to face the upcoming matches with greater confidence.

Tonight at the Olimpico, Devis Vasquez will be in goal.

The Colombian will replace Pierluigi Gollini, who is still struggling with a back injury.
