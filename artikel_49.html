Colombian authorities rescue all hostages held at Cartagena supermarket

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Security forces rescued all the hostages held for nearly a day at a supermarket in the northern Colombian tourist city of Cartagena, President Abelardo de la Espriella said early Wednesday. The president said that the men were “common criminals” who fled into the supermarket after a failed attempt to hold up an armored truck. The hostage situation broke out as Colombia is experiencing a wave of attacks by rebel groups on government targets.
