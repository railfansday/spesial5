What to know about Xi, Putin and Trump's historic trilateral summit

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

China's Xi Jinping, Russian President Vladimir Putin and his US counterpart Donald Trump met for a historic summit in Shenzhen, China, on Thursday, where they agreed on a "peace declaration". The three leaders pledged to reform the UN, increase supplies of refined petroleum products and uphold the immunity enjoyed by officials in international courts.
