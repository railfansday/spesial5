Trump, NATO chief push Russia to avoid any actions that would 'expand the war' with Ukraine

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Fox News foreign correspondent Jeff Paul joins ‘The Story’ to comment on a study estimating that more than 700,000 Russian troops have been killed since the war in Ukraine began. He also reports on Poland’s accusation that Russia violated its airspace, prompting international concern.

President Donald Trump and NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte are calling on Russia to avoid making any moves "that intentionally, or unintentionally, expand the war" against Ukraine.

The declaration came after the pair got together at Trump's Mar-a-Lago residence in Florida on Thanksgiving Eve for what was described as an "outstanding meeting."

However, Russia hit Kyiv with ballistic missiles overnight, according to the mayor of the Ukrainian capital.

"The meeting was very impactful, with the war between Russia and Ukraine and the conflict in Iran being the primary subjects, as well as all of the record setting achievements that we have made together at the summits in the Hague and at Ankara," Trump and Rutte said in a joint statement.

"We also discussed the importance of Russia avoiding any actions that intentionally, or unintentionally, expand the war, and the importance of obtaining a peaceful resolution to the war as rapidly as possible," the statement added. "Lastly, we talked about the importance of continuing to expand defense spending and investment in our industrial base, both in the United States and across NATO."

NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte meets with President Donald Trump during a visit to Mar-a-Lago in Florida on Wednesday, Nov. 25, 2026. (Reuters)

On Thursday, Trump said on Truth Social that "during my conversations in China with President Vladimir Putin, of Russia, I spent a lot of time talking about the fact that I would like to see President Putin pull back all Military aggression against territories throughout Europe, in the hopes that a Deal can be made between Russia and Ukraine, and made quickly."

Trump met with Putin recently at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Summit in Shenzhen, China.

"I really believe this is a strong possibility — Thousands of people are being killed each and every month, A COMPLETE BLOODBATH — and will be a Great Tribute to everyone!" Trump added. " It was an Honor meeting with Secretary General of NATO, Mark Rutte, last night at Mar-a-Lago, where many topics were discussed, and in which I updated him on my talks with President Putin."

Rutte also said in a post on X following the meeting, "It’s clearer than ever that American leadership remains essential as we build a stronger Europe in a stronger NATO."

The Kremlin on Thursday accused Ukraine of trying to expand the war after Ukraine sank a Russian warship in Libya using drones and attacked a Russian military-linked cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland this past weekend, Reuters reported.

"As regards the escalation of the conflict in Ukraine, this appears to refer to attempts by the Kyiv regime to expand the geographical scope of this conflict. We are witnessing terrorist activity by the Kyiv regime in the Mediterranean and the Baltic," Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov was quoted as saying.

"This cannot fail to cause concern amongst coastal states, and most likely within the Alliance as a whole — the North Atlantic Treaty Organization. Of course, nobody would want the conflict to spread geographically, and these are potentially very dangerous actions on the part of the Kyiv regime," Peskov added, according to Reuters.

Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko said at least seven people were wounded in Russian strikes overnight, hitting a shopping center and other buildings, Reuters also reported.

Russia's Defense Ministry reportedly claimed that it struck two sites linked to the production of drones.
