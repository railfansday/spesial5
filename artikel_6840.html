All the Stars Sitting Front Row at the Chanel Cruise 2026-27 Show in Sydney

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Including Margot Robbie, Nicole Kidman, and Kylie Minogue

First unveiled in Biarritz, France, this past April, the Chanel Cruise 2026/27 collection was presented again in Sydney, Australia, this morning, at the Cutaway event space in the Barangaroo district.

Designed by creative director Matthieu Blazy, who has been at the helm of the French fashion house since late 2024, the collection acts as a love letter to the early days of Chanel, and specifically, the seaside city where Gabrielle “Coco” Chanel opened her first couture house.

Models were sent down the runway in everything from the house’s classic tweed suits to more playful pieces like fishnet dresses and double-C swimming caps. Bright, summery colors and Mediterranean-inspired prints were interspersed with more muted tans and blacks. There were woven bags shaped like gondolas and rubber boots that climbed up to the models’ thighs.

While you can find plenty of photos from the collection online (and it will be available to shop in boutiques very soon), there were still a number of stars who opted to see it in person. A-listers like Margot Robbie, Nicole Kidman, and Kylie Minogue all came out to take in the collection from the front row at the Sydney replica show. (Kidman even brought along a surprise guest in the form of her 15-year-old daughter, Faith Margaret.)

Plus, a number of other notable figures from the Australian entertainment scene were in attendance too, like Oscar-nominated director Baz Luhrmann, actor and filmmaker Joel Edgerton, and Olympic gold-medalist Cathy Freeman.
