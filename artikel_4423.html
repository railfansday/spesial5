Retromobile Vintage-Car Expo Comes to NYC

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A newcomer from Paris muscles its way onto America’s collector-car calendar.

Retromobile is a classic-car event in Paris that dates back to 1976. This year, CEO Gérard Neveu brings it to New York City for the first time, in what is planned to be an annual event. Like the Paris original, Retromobile NYC takes place indoors (at the Jacob Javits Center). It’s not a concours, although there are some Pebble Beach–worthy vehicles on display. It’s more commerce focused, with a major auction (Gooding Christie’s) taking place.

Other auction houses, including Mecum and Bring a Trailer, also have a presence, and several high-end classic-car dealers have set up shop as well. Additionally, there’s a whole ecosystem of supporting vendors, from automobilia to wearables to insurance to watches. Art also features prominently. A significant display of art cars, in its own museum-like space, is curated by CART Department. There are also works for sale by current automotive artists. In all, there are roughly 100 exhibitors.
