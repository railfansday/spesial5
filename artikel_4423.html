Epic Adds Features to Fortnite Crew Subscription, Removes Others

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Daniel is a lifelong gamer with a love for various genres and gameplay styles who enjoys exploring games and playing from various angles.

Fortnite’s self-perpetuating freemium economy is built around several forms of transactions and subscriptions. The most prominent of these are the Item Shop, where you buy cosmetics piecemeal for V-Bucks, and the Battle Pass, which you buy once per season to gain access to a set sequence of unlockables by leveling up.

In addition to these, though, Fortnite also offers the Fortnite Crew subscription service which, for a recurring monthly fee, gets you access to the current Battle Pass, other content passes like the OG Pass, Music Pass, and for fans of video game LEGO kits, the LEGO Pass, plus a monthly payout of 800 V-Bucks. It costs more than the Battle Pass by itself, but all the extra content is very attractive, especially if you don’t already have a bunch of skins to flip through.

During the Treasure Hunters event, players have a chance to uncover a token that gives near-unfettered access to the item shop for two weeks.

As Epic Games announced on the official Fortnite news blog today, though, some changes are coming to the Crew Subscription starting on December 5. Some of these changes are additive, and may help to improve the value of the subscription gameplay-wise, but they’re accompanied by some subtractions, which could harm the overall bang-for-buck depending on your perspective.

Let’s start with the good news. Firstly, the price of Fortnite Crew is staying the same at $11.99 monthly, and you’ll still get access to the current Battle Pass, Music Pass, LEGO Pass and Rocket Pass Premium.

The price of Fortnite Crew is staying the same at $11.99 monthly.

The people gave Sweeney and Epic all the attention and support it needed when it rose up to Apple. As a reward, players got a significant price hike.

In addition, all Crew members will begin receiving a flat 20% bonus to all XP earned while playing the game, with a 10% XP bonus also given to any squad members in your party, even if they’re not Crew members themselves. These boosts don’t stack, though, so you won’t get any additional XP even if you have other Crew members in your squad.

So, that’s the good news, now what’s the bad news? The first piece of bad news is that Crew Pack Outfits, one of the most distinctive benefits to getting a Crew sub, are being discontinued. The final Crew Pack Outfit will be distributed in December, with no further packs to follow. On the bright side, if there were any Legacy Styles you didn’t have for your Crew Pack Outfits, which would normally be obtained by staying subbed for sequential months, they’ll instead just be given to you in bulk.

The second piece of bad news is that, at least for the time being, OG Pass is ending, with an apparent “dramatic conclusion” occurring on December 5 with the end of Chapter 1: OG Season X. This means you won’t be able to redeem the large quantity of skins and other assorted cosmetics that this pass would normally provide.

That’s definitely a big blow to the overall value factor of a Crew subscription, though according to Epic’s news post, “The Next Era of Fortnite OG drops in late 2027,” so perhaps the OG Pass will make a comeback at some point.
