Broncos blown out by Steelers on Black Friday; Bo Nix has worst game as a pro

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

It would seem the Denver Broncos took the drama around the Pittsburgh Steelers this week as an omen of an easy win. Instead, they were punched in the mouth and sent home battered and bruised.

Bo Nix played the worst of game of his career. Gave up a fumble recovery for a touchdown and an interception returned for a touchdown. He looked lost out there in the first half and was a little better in the second half, but with his first three-turnover game since Week 1 of 2025 and only this third of his career it was a terrible game overall.

At 8-3, they still lead the Kansas City Chiefs in the AFC West by virtue of common opponents, but that one seed in the AFC is looking a bit distance now.

It seemed like Denver waltzed into Pittsburgh expecting an easy win.

In the first quarter that expectation evaporated in short order. The Steelers defense struck first with a defensive touchdown off of a Bo Nix fumble.

Then the offense answered with a 4-play, 86 yard touchdown drive behind a 58-yard pass before a touchdown shot to DK Metcalf over Pat Surtain to bury the Broncos early.

Ugly doesn't begin to describe how the Broncos played early on both offense and defense.

Steelers 14, Broncos 0. Full first quarter recap.

We had a brief moment where we thought maybe the Broncos would turn the corner offensively. It began with an Alex Singleton safety that netted a field goal drive on the next offensive possession for Denver too.

That momentum evaporated though with another questionable decision by Bo Nix who threw the ball right to Jalen Ramsey who returned it for the Steelers' second defensive touchdown of the half.

Down 24-5 at halftime, the game felt over even with all of the magic the Broncos' have enjoyed over the last two years.

Steelers 24, Broncos 5. Full second quarter recap.

Whatever hope fans had of a halftime adjustment and turnaround faded fairly rapidly in the third quarter. It didn't get worse, but it certainly stayed bad.

Zach Allen would breathe a little bit of life into the team with a forced fumble, but the Broncos offense would not be able to convert it into a touchdown. Just a long field goal attempt that Wil Lutz would push wide right.

Steelers 24, Broncos 5. Full third quarter recap.

I am sure after that missed field goal most of us still around watching this game knew it would be a foregone conclusion. They just needed to finish out the final quarter and get the heck back to Denver.

Mason Rudolph enjoyed a great game and connected on a deep ball early in the fourth that led to more points for the Steelers to go up 27-5.

The Steelers' defense kind of let up after that early field goal in the quarter and the Broncos began driving. Nix hit Courtland Sutton perfectly inside the five, but he would drop the ball too. A screen pass to Coleman on the next play got a big chunk and got them inside the red zone for the first time in the game.

From there, the offense would play itself into a fourth and 21 where they would go for it and obviously fail. This game just couldn't end soon enough for Broncos Country.
