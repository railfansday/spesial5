Player Grades: Recapping the Mavericks’ 110-100 win over the Los Angeles Clippers

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Dallas Mavericks (9-7) won 110-100 against the Los Angeles Clippers (4-11) on Saturday in the first game of a back-to-back set that finds them traveling to Utah on Sunday. With the quick turnaround and three games in four nights, getting a win against the struggling Clippers was crucial. Now Dallas takes a two-game winning streak on the road.

Irving struggled on a high volume of shots, connecting on only 8-of-24 overall and only 3-of-11 from downtown. Irving is still Irving, however, and that means a powerful fourth quarter performance can be on tap for any game. Saturday night, he poured in nearly half his points (12) in the fourth quarter alone to boost his grade.

Flagg got into foul trouble with two quick ones in the opening minutes. That may have impacted his flow, but his overall performance wasn't the smoothest. He committed four fouls and had three turnovers on a poor shooting night. He was 6-of-16 overall and only 7-of-13 from the free throw line. He was the worst free throw offender on the team while shooting six more free throws than the next closest teammate.

Martin remains almost impossible to grade. He rarely contributes to all five primary statistical categories, and tonight was no different. He does all the little things that don't show up on the books, but his 0-for-3 shooting pulls his grade down in this one.

Risacher was close to awesome in under 30 minutes. He numbers don't necessarily scream "awesome," however he did just about everything right. 5-of-11 shooting, 1-of-2 from beyond the arc, no turnovers and a dive on the floor that almost cost him his nose, but showed a terrific level of commitment to effort in getting the win.

The eye test would tell you an "8" is a bit too high, but the actual output justifies it. On the heels of a nice win, Gafford gets the benefit of the doubt. He hit 4-of-5 from the floor, was a single rebound shy of a double-double and had nice assist and steal totals. He had a ridiculous plus-22 plus/minus to lead all players.

Clayton had a huge game relative to his limited minutes. His hot shooting saw him go 5-of-8 from the floor and 2-of-3 from downtown. One of those threes came in the fourth quarter at a huge moment to put Dallas up by five points.

Christie looked like his best self for just about the first time all season. He nailed 5-of-7 overall and 3-of-4 from deep. This is exactly what the Mavs need from him, especially on a night where P.J. Washington's potential three point shooting was on the bench. He also grabbed three steals, demonstrating the better side of his defense.

Johnson's grade is boosted by doing what he did in less than a quarter-and-a-half. 16 games into his career, this level of output for a full game would be nice to see. To maximize his limited time this way was impressive. Johnson, Jr. shot 4-of-9 including connecting on his only three-point attempt.

This felt like a must-win. Dallas struggled mightily on the road recently and has been doing well at home. Dropping one to a four-win team before heading out for three on the road would have been… not good. The good news is, Mavs Nation doesn't have to worry about that, as Dallas took care of business with a big fourth quarter to close out the Clippers.
