Stephen Jones: We're playing "unacceptable" football, our fans deserve better

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Sunday’s loss to the 49ers dropped the Cowboys to 4-6 on the season and magnified the season-long struggles that the team has had on the defensive side of the ball.

The 49ers scored three touchdowns that covered at least 66 yards on their way to compiling 584 yards in the 46-40 win. Those difficulties have led to calls for the team to move on from head coach Brian Schottenheimer and defensive coordinator Christian Parker.

The Cowboys have not indicated that any changes are on the horizon, but executive vice president Stephen Jones said on 105.3 The Fan on Monday that the team is “not doing what it takes to win these football games” right now. Jones outlined the places where the team is falling short and those areas were frequently on the defensive side of the ball, which led to question late in his appearance about his response to the calls for major changes to the coaching staff.

“I just think at the end of the day, I certainly understand their frustration because there’s no doubt that we’re frustrated,” Jones said. “We feel like the way we’re playing ball right now is unacceptable. Our fans certainly deserve better.”

Games against the Titans and Eagles in the next two weeks give the Cowboys a chance to get back to .500 heading into the final chunk of the regular season. That won’t erase the missed opportunities over the first 10 weeks, but it will give the current regime one more chance to show that they can correct the issues before the Cowboys decide if it is time to give others a chance to right the ship.
