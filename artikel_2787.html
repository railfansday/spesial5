Walker Kessler flashes ‘f–k yeah’ moment in game of ‘f–k nos’

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

As Walker Kessler walked into the interview room, he uttered four words.

The Lakers, who have the NBA's best record at 14-3, had eked past the Kings (1-14), who are in the league's cellar.

Walker Kessler (14) of the Los Angeles Lakers shoots a 3-point basket against the Sacramento Kings on Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

For Kessler, the Lakers' 106-104 win on Friday was mostly forgettable.

He got lit into by Lakers coach JJ Redick at halftime after only having six points and one rebound before turning things around to finish with 15 points, seven rebounds and three blocked shots. He missed two free throws with 5.3 seconds left and the Lakers up, 106-104, a blemish that would've been much worse had Zach LaVine not missed his go-ahead 3-point attempt with 0.9 seconds left.

In a few days, no one is going to remember that the Lakers trailed the Kings by as much as 11 points in a game with 10 lead changes and 15 ties. All that anyone is going to remember is the W.

Kessler has already proven himself as an excellent rim defender and rebounder, but on Friday he revealed that there are some more tricks in his bag.

With 11:42 remaining in the fourth quarter and the Lakers trailing the Kings, 84-82, Collin Sexton passed the ball to the 7-foot-1 center in the corner. Kessler then fired a 24-foot jumper which swished through the net.

So, you know, he's shooting a stunning 100% from beyond the arc.

Dylan Cardwell of the Sacramento Kings plays defense on Walker Kessler of the Los Angeles Lakers in Los Angeles, Friday, Nov. 20, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

It wasn't exactly the play Redick called. He said it was "unorganized," adding, "I'm not sure why [Kessler] was even in the corner."

"I didn't bat an eye when he shot it," Redick said.

During the preseason, after Redick drew an ATO that involved Kessler, the center wanted to make sure he did his due diligence by inquiring about all possible scenarios.

Walker Kessler grabs a rebound during the NBA Cup game at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, Friday, Nov. 20, 2026. NBAE via Getty Images

"We break the huddle and he's like, 'Coach, what do you want me to do if the ball finds me and we don't get a layup on this play?' Redick recalled Kessler asking. "And I said, 'Shoot it.' And he goes, 'F–k yeah.'"

So in a game with so many "F–k nos," Kessler finally got his moment.

"I feel good," Kessler said, adding a caveat. "Should've made those free throws."
