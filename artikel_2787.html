Warriors' Stephen Curry out at least 2 weeks after suffering oblique abdominal tear

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Stephen Curry will miss at least the next two weeks after suffering an oblique abdominal tear, the Golden State Warriors announced Thursday.

According to ESPN's Shams Charania, Curry's timeline for a return could actually be closer to a month once the team sees how he's progressed in two weeks.

Curry suffered the injury in the opening quarter of Wednesday's 115-108 loss to the Houston Rockets. He was spotted reaching for his side after drilling a 3-pointer in the opening minutes, and he seemed frustrated about it, but he wasn't touched and remained in the game at the time.

He would later leave the floor during a timeout to go directly into the Warriors' dressing room with a trainer.

Curry stayed in the locker room for the rest of the opening quarter. The team then ruled him out at the start of the second quarter with what they called right abdominal soreness.

After exiting the game, Curry underwent an MRI, which confirmed the tear. The Warriors said the point guard will be re-evaluated in two weeks.

Head coach Steve Kerr said Curry had tweaked something in the abdominal area that he "had never done" before.

Curry finished the night with three points and a turnover in the six minutes he was on the floor. The 38-year-old entered the night averaging 30.6 points, 4.3 assists and 4.1 rebounds per game this season, his 18th in the league.
