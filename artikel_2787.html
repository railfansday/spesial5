The Bruins pushed their winning streak to four games with a 4-1 victory over the Panthers on Sunday afternoon.

Boston and Florida started the matchup with a fight, as Nikita Zadorov and Brady Tkachuk dropped their gloves just two seconds into the game. The intense battle featured 126 penalty minutes, the most in a single NHL game this season.

Goaltender Jeremy Swayman finished with 40 saves, and four different Bruins players scored in an impressive all-around performance.
