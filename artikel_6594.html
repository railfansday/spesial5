What Happened To Every <i><em>Great British Bake Off</em></i> Winner? Here’s Where They Are Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

From cookbooks to career pivots, the GBBO champs have stayed busy.

Since The Great British Bake Off premiered in 2010, countless talented contestants have shared their passion for puddings, tarts, and everything in between with the world. But only the very best dominated biscuits, perfected their pastries, and showed off their cake skills to get crowned the winner.

But where are those GBBO champs now? From authoring cookbooks to welcoming children, they have kept busy. Some made baking their full-time career, while others stepped away from their ovens. Wherever they ended up, one thing’s for sure: Once a star baker, always a star baker.

“The boy who bakes” paved the way as the first-ever winner of The Great British Bake Off, or The Great British Baking Show as it’s called in the U.S. and Canada. The former debt collector has since focused solely on his writing, cookbooks, and television appearances, often contributing to magazine columns and appearing on shows such as Sunday Brunch and Good Morning America.

Jo Wheatley is one of the few Bake Off champions to have stepped away from their cooking career. Since her season of the show, she has written two cookbooks and even run her own cooking school for a time before retiring and moving to Portugal with her husband. She still shares the occasional recipe on social media and on her blog, but for the most part, keeps it to “sunny snaps & padel in between,” according to her Instagram bio.

Since winning the show, Whaite graduated from Le Cordon Bleu and opened his own cooking school. He also started a bakery and a snack brand. He has appeared on Steph’s Packed Lunch and Strictly Come Dancing, where he finished as the runner-up. He has authored six cookbooks and a memoir, and married his longtime partner in 2024.

The winner of Season 4, Quinn continues her work as a creative consultant, food artist, and designer, and has authored a cookbook. She also holds the Guinness Book of World Records for making the largest Jaffa cake and Jammie Dodgers. She previously hosted a YouTube baking series and creates custom cakes, shares recipes online, and makes occasional media appearances.

Nancy Birtwhistle wasn’t a one-and-done after her 2014 GBBO win—she also participated in The Great British Baking Show: Holidays. Since appearing on the franchise, Birtwhistle has penned a few books on both baking and sustainable living. The GBBO alum also regularly makes appearances on TV and radio shows.

Since dominating Season 6, Nadiya Hussain has become one of the UK’s most prominent celebrity chefs. She has hosted numerous TV shows, including Nadiya’s British Food Adventure, Nadiya Bakes, and The Chronicles of Nadiya, and was even the subject of the documentary Nadiya: Anxiety and Me. The star baker also wrote over 10 cookbooks, plus fiction and children’s books, along with an autobiography.

A year after her 2015 win, Hussain was even commissioned to bake the birthday cake for Queen Elizabeth’s 90th. And in 2020, the chef received an MBE, or Most Excellent Order of the British Empire, for her work in the culinary and broadcasting industry.

Since winning Season 7, Candice Brown has opened her own pub, The Green Man, with her brother in England, as well as Margo’s, a restaurant and deli. She has also penned two cookbooks and written food columns for local newspapers. Brown has also made appearances on numerous other shows, including Dancing On Ice, Loose Women, Pointless Celebrities, and Celebrity Mastermind. She also played a role in the movie Love Sarah.

Since winning Season 8, Faldo has founded her own wedding and specialty cake business: Sophie Faldo Couture Cakes. In addition to her day job, she teaches baking classes in person and online. Plus, she returned to the Bake Off kitchens in 2023 and won The Great Christmas Bake Off.

Things have only gotten busier for Rahul Mandal after his Season 9 win. Since the show, he has released a cookbook and continues baking in his downtime and sharing his creations on social media. He also returned to the franchise and won The Great New Year Bake Off special in 2021. However, the champion still works full-time as a research scientist and engineer at the University of Sheffield. Mandal has also gotten married and had a child.

David Atherton balances his baking with everyday life. The Bake Off winner works as a practice nurse and health advisor by day, and continues his baking pursuits by night, so to speak. Since his win, he has written a series of children’s and adult cookbooks, writes a regular food column for The Guardian, and shares home cooking and baking tips on social media. Additionally, Atherton teamed up with fellow Season 10 baker Michael Chakraverty to launch the Sticky Bun Boys baking and pop culture podcast.

Peter Sawkins made history in 2020 when he became the youngest baker to win GBBO at just 20 years old—and that wasn’t the end of his time on the franchise. Sawkins returned and won The Great New Year’s Bake Off in 2025. Meanwhile, Sawkins has graduated from college and completed pastry training at the Amaury Guichon Pastry Academy. He has also published two cookbooks and frequently shares recipes and baking tutorials online. Additionally, Sawkins works part-time in sports fundraising and plays competitive badminton.

It’s been five years since Giuseppe’s Season 12 win, and he’s upped the ante on his culinary career since. In the past half-decade, Dell’Anno has authored two cookbooks and contributed to a third. He also runs a successful cake business, teaches baking classes in person and online, and frequently shares his baking insights on broadcast television and in food columns. Like some of his fellow winners, he’s also made appearances at local festivals.

Known for recipes featuring bold Malaysian flavors, Syabira Yusoff continues to feed her love of baking by sharing tutorials on social media, hosting online culinary workshops, and judging baking competitions herself. Impressively, she does all this in her free time while continuing her career as a cardiovascular researcher and bioinformatician at King’s College London. Most recently, she announced her engagement to her longtime partner just this past July.

Matty Edgell has stayed busy, both professionally and personally, since winning Season 14. The former high school teacher resigned after the show and now focuses on creating recipes on his website while also sharing baking tutorials on social media. He also tied the knot and welcomed his first child. Edgell publishes a Substack newsletter and has made appearances on TV shows and at local food festivals.

Since her 2024 win, Grasso has kept plenty busy. Not only does the winner post weekly recipes to her Instagram account, but she also released two digital cookbooks earlier this year: Cooking Therapy and Baking Therapy. She has made appearances at local food events such as the Gloucester Quays Food Festival and recently opened up about her bipolar 2 diagnosis.

As the most recent winner of GBBO, Jasmine Mitchell has only a year behind her in the culinary spotlight, but she’s exploded onto the scene with gusto. Since her time on the show, Mitchell has appeared as a guest on numerous talk shows, appeared as a judge on Danish Bake Off, and currently has a cookbook in the works. She’s doing all this while finishing medical school!
