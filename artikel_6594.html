9 Best Eyeliners for Older Women That Glide on With Ease

When I shop for eyeliner these days, I feel like I’m going into a restaurant with a list of dietary restrictions. I have always had hooded eyes, but at 46, the hooding is more pronounced. I have sensitive eyes, too, and fine lines starting to set in. In short, I’m looking for an eyeliner to play up my gaze while downplaying any discomfort or signs of aging. It’s a balancing act, to be sure, but it’s one that the best eyeliners for older women can accomplish with finesse—and if it becomes tempting to skip eyeliner altogether, consider this advice from a pro: “Eyeliner actually becomes more important as we age,” says makeup artist Stevi Christine. “Lashes tend to thin over time, and a liner at the roots helps bring back that youthful fullness.”

Since I’ve found a few formulas that I swear by—including the Laura Mercier pencil that’s on this list—I see what Christine means. On days when I do wear eyeliner, I look more awake, chipper, and polished than on days when I don’t. The trick? Finding formulas that are not only flattering, but gentle on the eyes. Formulas for older women should also be easy to apply and remove without tugging at the lids. Ahead, find the eyeliners that check every box.

How should you apply eyeliner to mature eyes?

“Depending on the eye shape, I often line only the outer third or outer half of the upper lash line and then tightline the whole length of the lash line from underneath,” says Patinkin. “This is a great technique for lots of reasons.” First, she says, it contributes to a lifted look at the outer corner of the eyes, where the shadow is thicker. The technique also helps define the shape of the eyes without “eating up lid space,” she says. Tightlining is another technique makeup artists favor for mature eyes. “It makes the lashes look thicker because you’re filling in gaps between the roots of the lashes,” Patinkin says.
