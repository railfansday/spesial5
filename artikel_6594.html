The Shattered Bob Is Fall’s Most Wearable Short Haircut

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Polished yet effortless, this is a look that can be easily tailored to every hair type.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

The bob is undoubtedly the haircut of the year, and it shows no sign of losing momentum as summer gives way to cooler weather. While warmer days favored sharp edges and sculpted lines, this fall, the bob is taking on a softer, more undone shape, with a relaxed, less polished finish.

Enter the shattered bob: a tousled, textured take on the classic cut that captures the undone mood seen at London Fashion Week and on celebrities including Margot Robbie, Halle Berry, and Indira Varma. Natural texture is taking center stage, bringing movement and ease to a style once defined by sculptural precision.

The shattered bob is a softer, more relaxed take on the classic cut. It looks polished without feeling overly styled. “We’re looking at jaw-to-shoulder lengths, slightly broken edges, subtle graduation, and a heavier front. It should feel lived-in rather than perfectly placed, almost as though the haircut has naturally fallen into that shape,” say hairstylists Stephen Buller and Anita Rice.

Cut to fall softly and grow out seamlessly, it needs far less day-to-day styling while still looking considered. The style reflects a wider shift in beauty toward embracing hair’s natural texture and a style that feels authentic, luxurious, and easy to manage.

Movement is key to the shattered bob. Ask your stylist for soft, varied layers and lightly broken-up ends to add texture and soften the cut’s outline. It can work especially well with wavy or curly hair, as layers enhance its natural shape and movement.

Bring reference photos of people with a similar hair type to help guide the consultation. Your stylist can use them to tailor the cut to your natural texture and face shape.

Keeping products light is the best way to maintain the cut’s airy feel. Styling should be minimal, as the appeal of the look is in its effortlessness. “There’s still that polished fashion element, but it’s being roughed up. It feels more individual and ultimately more wearable,” add Buller and Rice.

For texture and separation without weighing the hair down, work a bit of a texturizing mousse or cream like Davines Dry Texturizer through the mid-lengths and ends.

Ultimately, the shattered bob should be about ease and hair that works alongside your lifestyle, showing that the most sophisticated hair often looks effortless.
