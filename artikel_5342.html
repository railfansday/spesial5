The Single Asset Vault amendment on the XRP Ledger is getting closer to activation on the mainnet as validator support builds for the network’s preparation to enable better DeFi functionality.

According to data from XRPScan, the SingleAssetVault amendment, or XLS-65, has received yes votes from 25 of 35 trusted validators, currently standing at 71.43% consensus as of November 18, 2026. It needs more than 80% of 35 votes (29) to start its 14-day countdown to activation. That means it needs four more votes to get there and could reach the magic number before the end of 2026.

The XRP Ledger’s amendment process requires a proposed amendment to be approved by more than 80% of trusted validators for 14 days before it is implemented. Validator support may shift during this time, and if the required 80% threshold is lost, activation may be delayed.

The Single Asset Vault amendment creates a structure for aggregating assets from multiple depositors and is intended to be used with the on-chain Lending Protocol. The amendment introduces a Vault object that defines the state of a tokenized vault, and the Vault entry is one of the ledger entry types it provides.

The Vault object holds important information such as available assets, shares, total value, and other relevant information. Users can create a Vault object with the VaultCreate transaction; the Vault object is tracked in an Owner Directory owned by the Vault Owner account.

The XRP Ledger lending amendment has gained additional yes votes. According to XRPScan, the LendingProtocolV1_1 amendment, also known as XLS-66, has received 24 yes votes out of 35 trusted validators, meaning it has 68.57% consensus as of November 18, 2026. It needs more than 80% of the 35 votes (29) to trigger a 14-day activation countdown.

This implies that there are five votes left for it to reach this threshold, with the possibility of it securing the required number of votes before the end of 2026 as well.

According to XRP enthusiasts, LendingProtocolV1_1 represents the biggest amendment for unlocking capital and liquidity on the XRP Ledger and is anticipated by institutional and retail users.
