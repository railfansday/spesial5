Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO — and the stock soars 35%

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The shares, which had been hammered in recent weeks, rose a whopping 35% after the announcement, adding more than $1 trillion in market capitalization

Samuel Boivin / NurPhoto via Getty Images

Nvidia $NVDA reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO on Monday, less than two weeks after he resigned following revelations of multiple affairs, including with a suspected Chinese spy. Shares of the leading AI chipmaker, which had been hammered in recent weeks, rose a whopping 35% in Monday morning trading after the announcement, adding more than $1 trillion in market capitalization compared to Friday's close.

The board's vote was unanimous and came after several rounds of talks and sit-downs with Huang, the company said. The board heard from top officials in the U.S. government and, on that basis, concluded that bringing Huang back creates no risk to national security, the company said.

"The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," Board Chair Harvey C. Jones said in a statement. Jones will remain chair. He thanked Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress for her service as interim CEO, a role she held alongside her CFO duties.

"I am honored to return to Nvidia and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution," Huang said in a statement. "I can't wait to get back to work."

The board set up a panel of independent directors to look into reports that a foreign intelligence operation had targeted Huang, and the panel hired outside counsel. The board said the inquiry will be carried through to its end. Huang supports the investigation and is committed to cooperating with it, the company said.

The board also formed a National Security and Export Control Committee of independent directors to oversee trade compliance and national security risks. Nvidia is searching for a chief compliance officer, a new position that will report to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee. The company said it will continue to cooperate with ongoing government investigations and inquiries.

President Donald Trump wrote on social media that the board made "the correct and proper decision." Trump also said the U.S. government has "no reason to believe" Huang's return endangers national security, and that officials have seen nothing showing he broke the law.

Huang resigned on Nov. 12 amid reports of multiple affairs. He had been unfaithful with at least two women, and U.S. officials suspect one of them, Alice Huang, who is not related to him, of relaying information to China's intelligence services. Beijing has dismissed the spying allegation, according to Bloomberg. Nvidia named Kress interim CEO. Jones said on Nvidia's earnings call last week that directors had accepted Huang's decision to step down and had no plans to bring him back.
