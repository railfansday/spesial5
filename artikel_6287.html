I Gambled Our Business Class Flight Home On A 65-Minute Connection And Separate Tickets

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

In the interest of maximizing our time on the ground (it was a short trip after all) and avoiding an early morning and long drive to Zürich, I booked a separate ticket from Basel to Copenhagen on Norwegian with a rather tight 65-minute connection to catch our SAS flight to Chicago…

When I booked my tickets for this trip, there was no award space on SAS from Zurich to Copenhagen. I figured it would open (intra-Europe space reliably opens a day or two before travel) and it certainly did, but I wasn’t thrilled about getting up before dawn to drive to Zürich in order to make the flight.

The change fee on a SAS EuroBonus award ticket is about 75USD as well, which caused me to look for options out of Basel, which is 15 minutes away from my in-laws house instead of an hour. I knew easyJet operates service on this route, but I was pleased to see Norwegian Air is now operating and the fares were cheap…cheaper than the change fee on SAS.

It was 177EUR for all four tickets on Norwegian from BSL-CPH, which I found compelling, though it was not without risk. Connecting on separate tickets is always a risk and the connection here was only 1 hour, 5 minutes, which would include walking from the Schengen area to the non-Schengen area (where lines can be very long).

Nevertheless, we opted to take the risk, knowing that if the flight was delayed we would miss our connection and might be stuck in Copenhagen another day or forced to fly back to the U.S. in economy class.

There was another wrinkle. My kids and I are U.S. citizens, but my wife sill is not…she travels to the USA on a Green Card and I figured we would be unable to check her in because the Green Card would need to be verified. That turned out to be an issue…I could not compete her check-in, though I was able to for the kids and me.

Long story short: the gamble worked…but it was very close.

We took off early from Basel and the captain announced we would arrive 15 minutes early into Denmark.

But the weather was poor and we ended up circling and pulled up to the gate late…we know we had less than an hour to go to the transfer counter, collect my wife’s boarding pass, pass through passport control, and board our flight.

We landed at the furthest possible gate and had a long walk ahead of us.

My biggest concern was that even though I had initiated check-in on the SAS app, we might be too late to check-in for Heidi. Thankfully, although we did not reach the transfer desk until 40 minutes before departure, an agent was able to complete check-in and issue the boarding pass.

We saw immigration lines were very long for non-Schengen citizens, but short for us so we ducked into the SAS Lounge for Kanelbullar (Swedish cinnamon rolls) and then headed to the gate. I know, I really do push it.

We reached the gate 26 minutes before departure…the gamble worked. Barely.
