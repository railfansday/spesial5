Celtic's Baur out for up to 10 weeks, says O'Neill

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Mika Baur is unlikely to feature until the new year

Mika Baur will be out for around 10 weeks with ankle ligament damage, says Celtic manager Martin O'Neill.

The twice-capped Germany midfielder, 22, went off in the recent 1-0 win at home to Kilmarnock and withdrew from his nation's under-21 squad for this month's international break.

Baur, who moved to Glasgow from Paderborn in August, has made 19 appearances so far this season, scoring four times,

"He'll be out for some considerable time," O'Neill explained.

"It's a real shame, he was playing great for us. There's talk about 10 weeks. There's things that he can't do for a while. Foot's in the boot.

"He was on fire. It is a blow. It seemed so innocuous at the time. I thought that he'd been kicked but it was the other foot he landed on. He's just gone over on it."

Benjamin Nygren missed the second of Sweden's recent double-header and O'Neill cast doubt on the midfielder's participation in Saturday's home Scottish Premiership meeting with St Mirren (15:00 GMT).

"Nygren didn't train today but we'll see how he is in the morning, he's got a sore heal," the manager added on Friday. "He's been feeling it for a couple of weeks."
