Jockey De Boinville 'escorted to his vehicle' after threat received

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Top jockey Nico de Boinville was racing at Plumpton and finished fifth in the opening race of the afternoon, before finishing third on his only other ride; he was given additional security protection throughout the day after a threat had been made to Nicky Henderson's yard

Nico de Boinville was given security support at Plumpton on Monday after a threat against the jockey was received and passed on to authorities.

The multiple Grade One-wining jockey finished fifth aboard even-money favourite Ginolo for his boss Nicky Henderson in the opening race of the afternoon at the East Sussex track, after which he was informed he would be provided with extra security after officials became aware of the situation.

De Boinville went on to finish third on his only other ride, the Henderson-trained Ionian in a handicap chase, the third race on the card.

Explaining the sequence of events, Plumpton's clerk of the course Marcus Waters said: "We got a phone call from a person representing the PJA (Professional Jockeys Association) and the NTF (National Trainers Federation) regarding a threat that had gone in to the Henderson yard.

"We put on security for Nico for the rest of the day. He had one further ride and they escorted him from the weighing room to the parade ring and then once the race was done they escorted him to his vehicle, just in case there was anything that came with the threat.

"It's obviously not a nice situation for anyone to be involved with."

Speaking to Racing HQ at Newbury's pre-Coral Gold Cup gallops morning on Tuesday, De Boinville explained: "After the first race yesterday the security detail at Plumpton who are very good, top-level people who dealt with the Brighton bombings and things like that, came in and dragged me out and said there's been a threat called in to me, so I needed security for the rest of the day.

"The threat came into the yard and then the PJA and then BHA, and then it gets called through to the racecourse. I need to find out a bit more about it but it's the trials and tribulations of being a jockey.

"It is bizarre and I'm going to work like everyone else and you don't expect that when you go to work. I just feel sorry for whoever it was and I hope they get the help they need - it was probably because I got beat."
