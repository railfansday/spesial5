Wilson and Miller score as Scotland U21s thump Ukraine

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Lennon Miller (10) and James Wilson (9) had previously been in the senior squad

James Wilson scored a double and Lennon Miller also found the net as Scotland Under-21's defeated their Ukrainian counterparts 4-0 after the pair dropped down from the senior squad.

Aberdeen midfielder Alfie Sewart, who is currently on loan to Inverness Caledonian Thistle, was the other scorer in the friendly at the Pinatar Arena in Spain.

With the squad refreshed for a new intake of qualifying players, Udinese midfielder Miller was among the more experienced starters and pounced on a defensive error to thump in the opener after 23 minutes.

Heart of Midlothian striker Wilson headed his first just before half-time from a Tyler Fletcher free-kick.

Miller set up Wilson's clipped second 13 minutes into the second half.

Then Stewart fired home to finish off the scoring with 20 minutes remaining.

Tony Docherty's side will take on United States at the same venue on Sunday afternoon.
