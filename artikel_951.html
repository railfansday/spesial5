Luca Williams-Barnett reveals De Zerbi joke before dazzling Spurs debut goal

Luca Williams-Barnett has disclosed that Tottenham boss Roberto De Zerbi teased him about an under-21s outing prior to naming him in the starting line-up for Saturday's 2-0 victory against Ipswich.

The 18-year-old academy product earned his full debut following two promising substitute appearances, delivering an impressive 64-minute performance that backed up the growing anticipation surrounding him.

The teenager opened the scoring inside 15 minutes before setting up Dominic Solanke's sliding finish following an intricate dribble and cross. Upon his substitution, he departed to a standing ovation, having become Tottenham's youngest Premier League goalscorer since Gareth Bale in 2007.

Speaking to SpursPlay, Williams-Barnett explained: "The day before, the gaffer obviously pulled me aside.

"He actually said, ‘you’re going to be playing with the 21s, 60 minutes to get your legs back,’ and I was like, ‘what?’ but obviously he told me the news and the whole day I was just buzzing. I couldn’t wait.

"Everything I’ve dreamed of. Obviously scoring and getting an assist, it’s like a dream come true basically."

Following the forward's fifth appearance for his boyhood club, De Zerbi offered high praise, suggesting the youngster possesses the potential to eventually match Barcelona's teenage star Lamine Yamal.

A buzz swirled around Tottenham Hotspur Stadium as soon as Williams-Barnett was named in Spurs’ line-up and it only took quarter of an hour for the skilful attacker to get in front of Abdoul Ouattara to bundle home Pedro Porro’s cross.

A first senior goal for Williams-Barnett continued his strong scoring record after 40 goals for Tottenham age-group teams since he made his debut for the under-18s aged 15 in 2024.

“The first ball into the box I wasn’t really there and I was quite annoyed because it was open and I could have had the goal,” Williams-Barnett explained.

“Once I saw it go back to Pedro, it was an instinct. Once I see he was able to cross the ball, I just had to get behind my man and put the ball in the goal.”

After this man-of-the-match showing, more starts are set to follow for Williams-Barnett, and it was understood he has agreed a new long-term contract with boyhood club Tottenham.

Quizzed about what comes next, Williams-Barnett said: “Stay humble, block out all the notifications and noise, but yeah I want to create myself into a starting XI player.”
