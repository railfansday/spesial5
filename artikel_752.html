‘Slow Horses’ Will End With Season 8, Says Olivia Cooke: ‘We’re All Melancholy… The Show’s Been a Shining Light’

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Bad news for “Slow Horses” fans: star Olivia Cooke recently told The Times that the eighth season will be the show’s last.

“I am shooting the last season!” she said, before adding: “Never say never!” Cooke continues: “But I think that’s what we’ve been told and it definitely feels like we are ending. We’re all melancholy, distracted. Yesterday, I got in the make-up chair and cried. The show’s been a shining light and I’ve told my partner I need two weeks to get over it. Cooke originated the role of Sid Baker in the show’s first season, before being seemingly killed off. She reprised the role in Season 6, very much still alive in the show’s universe. The actress tells The Times that she wasn’t surprised to return, as “they were checking my availability over the years. And we stayed in touch. It was like I’d never left.”

Cooke revealed that her role on HBO’s “House of the Dragon” had made a return to the show complicated in past seasons. “There was something in my contract for ‘House of the Dragon’ which said I was not allowed to do a long-running series for an opposing streamer, so we had to smooth that out,” she said, noting lawyers got involved. “I sent texts saying, ‘Help!’”

“Slow Horses” was renewed by Apple for Season 8 back in September ahead of the premiere of Season 6. The six-episode Season 8 is adapted from “Clown Town,” the ninth book in Mick Herron’s “Slough House” series, and will see “Jackson Lamb and Diana Taverner (Kristin Scott Thomas) face off in their most explosive confrontation yet,” according to its synopsis. “When a botched attempt to blackmail the Park puts Taverner on the offensive, it is up to Lamb to unravel one of MI5’s darkest secrets and put an end to it once and for all.”

A Season 7 renewal was announced last year, and it is expected to air in 2027. Season 8 will hit screens the following year. Gary Oldman and Jack Lowden, among others, also star in the spy-thriller.
