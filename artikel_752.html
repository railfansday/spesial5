‘Wicker’ Star Alexander Skarsgard Will Be Pushed Differently for Golden Globes Than Other Awards (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Golden Globe winner's portrayal of a hunk made of twigs has left many audience members as smitten as — and even envious of — Olivia Colman's lonely fisherwoman who commissioned him.

Alexander Skarsgard, the Swedish actor who has garnered raves — and lust — for his portrayal of a hunk made of twigs in the eccentric dramedy Wicker, will be promoted in an unconventional way this awards season.

The Hollywood Reporter can exclusively report that the 50-year-old will be pushed by Wicker distributor Black Bear as a lead at the Golden Globes — specifically, in the category of best actor in a musical or comedy — but in the supporting category at the other major award shows where contests are not segregated by genre. This path — lead for Globes, supporting for Oscars — has previously been employed by numerous others.

It worked well for some. Among them: Tatum O’Neal was nominated at the Globes and won at the Oscars for 1973’s Paper Moon; Maggie Smith won at both for 1978’s California Suite; Catherine Zeta-Jones was nominated at the Globes and won at the Oscars for 2002’s Chicago; and Alicia Vikander was nominated at the Globes and won at the Oscars for 2015’s The Danish Girl.

But it was less successful for others. For example, Cate Blanchett was nominated at the Globes but not at the Oscars for 2001’s Bandits; and Steve Carell was nominated at the Globes but not at the Oscars for 2017’s Battle of the Sexes.

A Black Bear rep tells THR, “We made the decision to position Alexander in lead actor for the Globes’ musical/comedy category because it gives the opportunity to recognize his unconventional romantic performance within the film’s unique blend of dark comedy, fantasy and romance. For other awards, where comedy and drama are not distinguished, supporting actor offers a more appropriate path to recognition reflecting the impact of his performance within the ensemble.”

Skarsgard, who won a Golden Globe for Big Little Lies in 2018 and was nominated for another for Succession in 2024, delivered his Wicker performance, opposite Oscar winner Olivia Colman, beneath elaborate WETA Workshop prosthetics and a full creature suit. Playing a character who barely speaks, the actor had to exaggerate his expressions in order for them to be visible, but not so much that they would seem over-the-top. The resulting nuanced, tender characterization, with much communicated through subtle gestures and soulful eyes, has left many audience members as smitten as — and even envious of — the lonely fisherwoman who commissioned the wicker man in the first place.

Wicker, based on the short story The Wicker Husband by Ursula Wills, was adapted for the screen and directed by Alex Huston Fischer and Eleanor Wilson. Its strongest award prospects are probably in the best makeup and hairstyling and best visual effects Oscar races.
