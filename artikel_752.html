Jennifer Garner Details "Incredible" Chapter in Ben Affleck Marriage

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Jennifer Garner pulled back the curtain on a sweet chapter during her marriage to Ben Affleck when she took the pair’s three kids on a European vacation: “It was wonderful.”

Jennifer Garner is thinking back to a time when she was thriving during her marriage to Ben Affleck.

The 13 Going on 30 alum detailed the unforgettable vacation she took with her and the Gone Girl star’s kids Violet, 20, Fin, 17, and Samuel, 14, before calling it quits with Affleck in 2015.

“When my kids were really little, I went to Paris,” Garner told Ina Garten on a recent episode of her Happy Hour podcast. “Ben was shooting some Batman-ish movie in London and we went for six weeks. I took them out of school. It was the last time I really took kids out of school, but it was wonderful. It was incredible. It was so fun.”

“And part of that is that we bopped around,” Garner recalled. “We went to Venice, we went to Paris.”

And while the Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice actor was busy getting into character as Bruce Wayne, it was Garten who suggested the Alias actress explore the French city by boat.

Since then, it’s turned into a full-blown tradition for the Daredevil actress. How fabulous is that?

“I’ve done it now, every time I go,” Garner said. “Every picture from that night, from that trip, the smile on my kids’ faces—it was such an adventure that you gave us.”

Garner and Affleck, both 54, tied the knot in 2005 before separating in 2015. And while they finalized their divorce in 2018, they’ve remained a team in co-parenting their little ones.

In fact, The Town actor recently gave his ex her flowers in the parenting department, calling her a “spectacular” mom on the Oct. 11 episode of Sunday Today with Willie Geist before admitting “it always makes me feel like I’m coming up a little short.”

And he’s not afraid to let his kids know that either, sharing he tells them that he’s “doing about 75 percent as good as your mom.”

“And they’re like, ‘Maybe 40 percent,’” he added with a laugh. “I’m maxing out. Woo, B+!”

But in all seriousness, Affleck expressed that he couldn’t be more “grateful” to have a co-parent like the Juno actress.

“I respect her,” he emphasized. “I think the most important thing is having that mutual respect—being able to get along with that person.”
