Brandy and Monica Announce ‘The Boy Is Mine’ 2027 Residency at Las Vegas’ Resorts World

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Brandy and Monica have announced “The Boy Is Mine Vegas Residency,” their first-ever series of shows in Las Vegas.

The R&B icons announced the residency following a performance of their collaborative hit “The Boy Is Mine” on “Dancing With the Stars” during its Grammy-themed episode on Tuesday night. The 10-date residency will take place at Resorts World Las Vegas, kicking off on May 29 with performances through June before concluding on July 17. Produced by the Black Promoters Collective, the residency comes following their sold-out “The Boy Is Mine” tour, which saw each artist trading off to perform hits and deep cuts from their respective catalogs. Following the conclusion of the tour in December 2025, Brandy and Monica continued to perform together at the St. Lucia Jazz & Arts Festival and a co-headlining performance at the 2026 Essence Festival of Culture. Read Variety‘s review of Brandy and Monica’s Los Angeles tour stop here.

In a statement shared with Variety, Monica says that she did not initially envision the tour extending to a residency. “Originally, it was planned as a one and done, but I’ll be honest, after the first night, I realized it was far deeper,” she said, touching on the key to her working relationship with Brandy. “I think the most important factor in any relationship or friendship, especially one that has a partnership like ours, is communication. Communication is key, and keeping other people out, allowing us to speak about the things that matter, to talk about things when one may like something or may not like something, making sure other people’s feelings don’t become our own. [It’s] the key component to how we make sure things work best.”

“BPC is proud to be working with Brandy and Monica again,” says Shelby Joyner, President of Black Promoters Collective, in a statement. “We saw on The Boy Is Mine tour how much it meant to fans to see them share a stage, and I’m excited to build on what we created together. Bringing The Boy Is Mine to Las Vegas as Black Promoters Collective’s first residency is a big moment for us. We’ve built BPC by betting on artists we believe in and listening to their fans. This residency takes that work into a new arena for our company, and I can’t think of two artists I’d rather take that step with.”

American Express presale begins on November 11 at 10 a.m. PT, followed by artist presale at 12 p.m. PT. Local presales start on November 12 at 10 a.m. PT, and general onsale kicks off on November 20 at 10 a.m. PT.
