Microscopy upgrade sharpens images fivefold without sacrificing tissue imaging depth

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Researchers at Texas A&M University have cracked a problem that has limited biological imaging for years: seeing fine cellular detail while looking deep inside living tissue. The new microscopy technique resolves structures smaller than 100 nanometers, roughly a thousand times thinner than a human hair, without giving up the ability to image deep into intact samples.
