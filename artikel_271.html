What does Apple One Family come with and which subscription is best for you?

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The answer comes down to how many of Apple's services you use regularly.

This year, several of Apple's subscriptions saw serious price upticks, so much so that even students had to pay a bit more. Apple Music prices rose by $1 a month for individual plans and $3 for the Family plan. A few weeks later, monthly Apple TV subscriptions went up from $13 to 15, while annual ones saw a $20 total jump.

If you use many Apple services, you might have looked to Apple One to bundle them and reduce the hurt from these increases. Unfortunately, the package subscription didn't get by unscathed either. The Individual tier climbed from $20 to $22 a month, though it's still cheaper than subscribing to Apple Music ($12/month) and Apple TV ($15/month) separately.

While the Apple One Family plan is now $28/month, you can share it with five other people, which drastically brings down the cost per person. There's also a higher-cost shareable Premier tier at $40/month. But before you ask friends and family to group up with you, you need to figure out which of the three Apple One subscriptions is most worth your while. That mostly depends on how many Apple services you already use.

Moving on, the Family tier is a great choice if you're providing for multiple people or want to split the monthly cost with up to five other members. At $28 per month, you get a shared 200GB pool of iCloud storage, the Apple Music Family plan (which costs $20 by itself), plus Apple TV and Apple Arcade. Combined, the Family plan saves you about $17 per month, which can stretch even further if you split the bill. With six people paying their own way, each person pays less than $5/month. Unlike some family plans, Apple doesn't require everyone to live in the same household.

The final step up is Apple One Premier, which offers the full suite of Apple services and $35 in monthly savings compared to joining each one individually. Along with everything included in the Family plan, you also get Apple Fitness+ ($10 per month), Apple News+ ($13/month) and 2TB of shared iCloud storage ($10 per month). On their own, these would total $75, so it's a good value at $40 per month.

In case you weren't aware, every Apple subscription within Apple One is already shareable with a Family Sharing group — with the exception of Apple Music, which requires the Family plan for multi-person access. But if your family only cares about playing the best Apple Arcade games, for example, you could pay $7/month for that and make it available to your Family Sharing group. Apple One makes the most sense when you'll use everything.

You can always tack on extra iCloud storage by subscribing to iCloud+, letting you max out at 14TB of storage if you get the 12TB iCloud+ plan for $60 per month alongside Apple One Premier. While you share the pool of storage, your files are not visible to other family plan members unless you so choose.
