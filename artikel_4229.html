Every Game Coming To And Leaving Xbox Game Pass This November

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Not long after announcing the newest alligator-themed edition of the Xbox Series X, Xbox Game Pass announced over a dozen new titles coming to the platform over the next few weeks, making sure that the month is stacked with free releases to look forward to.

As shared on the official Xbox Game Pass account on X, a whopping 13 games are coming to the platform soon, several of which, like Football Manager 27 and Pit of Goblin, you can already hop on and play without having to wait.

In addition to some new indies you might not have heard of before, Game Pass is also getting several exciting day one releases for fans to look forward to, like Warhammer Survivors and Escape Academy 2: Back 2 School, which you can read our review of here.

Some highly rated games like the zombie survival FPS John Carpenter's Toxic Commando and the deck-building rogue-lite Vampire Crawler are definitely highlights of the lineup as well, in addition to the remaster of the MySims Cozy Bundle being great for anyone who wants a game to help unwind and relax to for the remainder of fall.

On top of the influx of new titles for Game Pass members to enjoy, the month also comes with a sorrowful goodbye to some classic titles leaving the service.

Although some of these titles are ones you might not have heard of before, several of the following games slated to leave on November 15 are still well worth checking out, even aside from the classics like GoldenEye 007 or the acclaimed roguelike Moonlighter.

On top of these games, Football Manager 26 is also leaving Game Pass a little early on November 10th, before being replaced by the newest version that released this year. Thankfully, there are just as many titles arriving as leaving this time around, so even if you miss the chance, you'll still have no shortage of fresh titles to enjoy throughout the coming months.
