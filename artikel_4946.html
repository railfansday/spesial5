Switzerland approves 57 jurisdictions for crypto data exchange from 2028

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The 57 jurisdictions include every EU member state, the UK, and several G20 economies.

Switzerland approved 57 states and territories as potential partners for the automatic exchange of cryptoasset information under the OECD's framework starting in 2028, the Swiss Federal Council said in a statement on Wednesday.

The OECD developed the Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework with G20 countries in 2022, with the CARF package finalized in June 2023. The framework requires service providers to collect and report transaction data.
