Tether buys $10 million of Bitdeer shares as stock hovers near all-time low

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

An amended Schedule 13D shows the stablecoin issuer increased its Bitdeer share count by nearly 10%.

Tether bought about $10 million of Bitdeer Technologies Group Bitdeer Technologies Group (BTDR) shares on Nov. 25 as the bitcoin Bitcoin (BTC) miner traded near an all-time low, according to an amended Schedule 13D filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on Friday.

Tether Investments bought 3.72 million Class A shares in two open-market transactions, according to the filing’s transaction schedule. The purchases included 12,240 shares at $2.65 and 3.7 million shares at $2.693, for a weighted average of about $2.69.

The purchases brought Tether’s holdings to 41.45 million shares, per the filing. This position gives the stablecoin giant shared voting and dispositive power over a reported 17.9% of Bitdeer’s outstanding Class A shares.

Bitdeer shares are trading at around $2.93 in early market trading on Friday, down 3.9% from Wednesday’s close, according to The Block’s BTDR price page. The stock is down 61.5% over the past month and 74.3% year to date after touching $2.60 on Wednesday, its 52-week low.
