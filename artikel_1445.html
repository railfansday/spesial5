Kevin Ochsner is at the Center for Brain, Mind and Society, Columbia University, New York City, New York 10027, USA.

The ability to manage difficult feelings is so essential to everyday life that failures to regulate emotion effectively are associated with detrimental social, occupational and physical-health outcomes. One of the most well-studied regulation strategies is cognitive reappraisal, in which a person changes how they think about an event to alter its emotional impact — for example, finding the ‘silver lining’. Writing in Nature, Sicorello et al.1 investigate an assumption that researchers have held about measuring reappraisal.
