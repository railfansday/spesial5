Personal Investments • Re: MYGA withdrawal taxaction

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

I have a MYGA that matures on 12/15/26. I was informed that the I can allow the balance to continue to accrue interest post maturity and submit my surrender instructions early next year (prior to the automatic renewal date of the MYGA) and that all of the interest will be taxable in 2027 rather than 2026 when the MYGA matures. Since I want to defer this interest to 2027 this seems much simpler than doing a 1035 exchange into a year MYGA.

My understanding is that MYGA interest is taxable in the year paid but I am not sure if this applies post maturity. Is it true that all of the interest paid by the MYGA will be 2027 taxable interest in this scenario? Thanks.
First of all, a MYGA doesn’t “mature”. In your case, the current interest rate period ends on 12/15/26, and the surrender charge drops away, but the MYGA carries on beyond that date. There will be other things that happen after that date, possibly including entering a new interest guarantee period with new surrender charges, but the underlying annuity policy continues on.

So if you take interest out of the MYGA in 2026, it will be included as taxable income in 2026. And if you take interest out in 2027 (or in any later year), it will be taxed in the year received.
