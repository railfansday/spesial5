Personal Investments • Re: How to incorporate an inheritance into retirement plan??

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Unexpected and expected windfalls always went into my Vanguard CA tax-exempt municipal money market fund to be accounted as ‘in cash’ until realized. Once realized then I would determine where to deploy.

I had an ‘unexpected extraordinary gift’ received when it constituted 15% of my portfolio after I had finished construction of the five-year retirement transition plan to commence. I immediately deployed it to the Vanguard CA tax-exempt MMF VCTXX so as not to generate taxable ordinary income until I could disperse it out to various funds. It merely reaffirmed that I could retire two years earlier than expected if I wish without making modifications. Six months before that date was reached the company granted my wish earlier than expected. With the amount expected you can just stop the day the funds are deposited if you are still working and spend early retirement time redeploying at a leisurely pace. Time is non-refundable.
