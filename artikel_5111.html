One Last Brutal Flush? Bitcoin Eyes $49,000 Before the Next Bull Run

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Bitcoin (BTC) could face another sharp decline before beginning its next major rally, with analysts warning that the cryptocurrency may still have more downside ahead despite already suffering a significant correction.

Meanwhile, analyst SeffafNet believes Bitcoin could fall as low as $49,000, identifying several support levels that could come into play if selling pressure continues. His outlook suggests that the market may experience one final capitulation before buyers return and trigger a broader recovery.

Bitcoin’s recent weakness has reinforced concerns about another leg down, with traders closely watching whether the token can hold its current support levels or extend its decline toward lower price targets.

According to the analyst, Bitcoin’s downside roadmap remains unchanged, with $58,000 serving as the first major level, followed by $55,000, $53,000, and potentially $49,000.

Notably, he views the $53,000 region as a potential final liquidity sweep, where additional selling could force leveraged investors and traders out of their positions before the market finds a more durable bottom.

If Bitcoin reaches $49,000, SeffafNet believes the move could mark the end of the current correction and create an opportunity for a major reversal. Nevertheless, he stressed that the price target is not guaranteed, describing it as a scenario he is preparing for rather than a certainty.

He believes investors could make costly decisions if they panic during another sell-off, particularly if the decline occurs near the end of the broader market correction.

Additionally, analyst Crypto Patel maintains a bearish outlook, suggesting Bitcoin could experience a temporary recovery before resuming its downward movement.

The pundit identified a potential relief rally toward the $71,504-$74,800 region, where a bearish order block and fair value gap could provide resistance. Such a move could offer temporary relief to traders following the recent decline without necessarily signaling a broader trend reversal.

Crypto Patel said Bitcoin’s higher time-frame bearish structure remains intact, although a breakdown has not yet been confirmed. He is watching for a candle close below $57,800, which would strengthen the case for additional downside.

Furthermore, if that level breaks, his broader downside targets extend between $50,000 and $35,000, placing his outlook below SeffafNet’s $49,000 bottom projection.

However, Crypto Patel would reconsider his bearish position if Bitcoin records a higher-time frame candle close above $87,400, the level he identified as the critical change-of-character threshold.

Despite Bitcoin’s weakness, Michael Van de Poppe sees a potentially encouraging development in Ethereum’s performance against Bitcoin.

The analyst noted that Bitcoin fell approximately 25% over the two weeks, while Ethereum’s performance against BTC remained relatively stable. He considers this resilience a sign of strength, particularly because altcoins typically suffer greater losses against Bitcoin during severe market downturns.

At press time, BTC was trading at $61,292, up 0.85% over the past 24 hours.
