Bitcoin Price Slumps Under $62,000, Crashing 25% in One Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The bitcoin price slumped under $62,000 on Thursday afternoon, according to Look Into Bitcoin data, extending a slide of about 24% in one month as Anthropic’s Nasdaq debut set off a broad selloff in tech stocks.

Bitcoin was trading near $81,800 on Oct. 19. It touched a low of about $61,650 on Thursday, its weakest level since July, before steadying near $62,200. The drop was about 25% from the Oct. 19 close, based on daily data.

The slide came in steps. Bitcoin closed near $80,050 on Oct. 25, about $78,250 on Nov. 1 and about $74,750 on Nov. 8. The steepest leg came last week, when it fell about 12.6% to a Nov. 15 close near $65,300, and the losses have continued this week.

Bitcoin had climbed back to about $64,000 early Thursday as Anthropic’s record IPO lifted stock futures, as this morning’s report showed. The rally faded once U.S. markets opened. The bitcoin price fell from roughly $63,900 at the opening bell to about $62,200 by midday.

Anthropic shares opened shortly before noon at $111.50, 34% above the $83 IPO price, CNBC reported. The opening trade valued the Claude maker at more than $2 trillion. The stock quickly pulled back and was last near $97, about 17% above its offering price.

The broader market went the other way. The major averages fell to session lows soon after Anthropic began trading, as investors took profits on the AI trade. The Nasdaq Composite dropped about 2% after gaining as much as 2% earlier in the session, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average lost about 1,000 points.

Steve Sosnick, chief strategist at Interactive Brokers, told CNBC the premarket rally had faded even before Anthropic began trading. He called it the latest round of “sell the rally” trading. Nvidia (NVDA) fell about 3%, and the S&P 500 headed toward a sixth straight loss, the AP reported.

The Cboe Volatility Index, Wall Street’s fear gauge, climbed to about 28 after touching nearly 29 shortly after Anthropic began trading. That was its highest level since March, up from about 24.6 at Wednesday’s close. The bitcoin price moved in step with the Nasdaq through the afternoon.

Crypto stocks followed the Nasdaq lower in late-afternoon trading, about 3:55 p.m. ET. Strategy (MSTR) traded near $86.70, down about 4% on the day. Galaxy Digital (GLXY) dropped about 6.9%, the steepest decline in the group.

Coinbase (COIN) slipped about 0.9% to near $109.50, while Circle (CRCL) fell about 2.2%. Bitcoin miners gave back their early rally too. IREN (IREN) fell about 5.6%, CleanSpark (CLSK) lost about 5.4% and MARA Holdings (MARA) declined about 4.2%. Riot Platforms (RIOT) was down about 2.9%.

The Fed remains a headwind. Bets on a December rate hike have grown after minutes from the October meeting showed more policymakers backing higher rates, and some officials called for a half-point move.

The bitcoin price is also on track for a seventh straight weekly loss, which would be its second-longest losing streak after nine weeks of declines in 2022. The streak has erased roughly $20,000 from the bitcoin price since mid-October, based on daily closes. Bitcoin price hit its weakest level since July on Thursday, and the Fed meets again next month.

Longer-term holders have not left. Institutional investors added 49,229 BTC through U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs in the third quarter, lifting their holdings to a record 356,491 BTC. Swiss bank Sygnum called the current downturn the shallowest bear market to date and sees a path to a new high near $128,000.

At the time of writing, the bitcoin price is near $61,900.
