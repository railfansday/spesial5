France's femicide orphans: The invisible victims of violence against women

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

In France, 111 women were killed by their current or ex-partners in 2025 alone, leaving behind dozens of children. How can they rebuild their lives without their mother? How can they seek help? In this episode of France in Focus, we meet Cloé and Eva-Louise, whose world collapsed when their father brutally murdered their mother. FRANCE 24's Natacha Vesnitch, Olivia Bizot, Clément Di Roma and Aurore Cloé Dupuis report.
