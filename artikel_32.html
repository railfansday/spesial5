In boost for Netanyahu, judges question corruption trial charges

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Judges in the long-running corruption trial of Israeli caretaker Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu handed his defence a major boost on Wednesday in recommending the prosecution drop one of the cases in which he is accused of bribery. The recommendations, which came as Israel's longest-serving prime minister fights for his political survival, were immediately cheered by Netanyahu's right-wing allies who demanded the trial be brought to an end. FRANCE 24's Noga Tarnopolsky reports from Jerusalem.
