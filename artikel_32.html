Democrat Hubbard wins North Carolina recount, clinching historic House majority

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Democrat Chuck Hubbard was elected to the US House of Representatives from North Carolina on Sunday after defeating veteran Republican Virginia Foxx, who chairs the powerful House Rules Committee. His victory gave Democrats their best general election result in decades, winning at least 258 seats in the 2026 US midterms.
