This has happened despite institutions and whales reducing their holdings, and DOGE pricing dipping 24.47% in the past month.

Dogecoin (DOGE) sharks have added 440 million coins to their wallets in the past month and a half despite the recent crypto market dip.

According to blockchain analysis platform Santiment, the whale cohort holding between 1 million and 10 million DOGE led the accumulation spree, increasing their holdings by 290 million coins (11.02 billion – 11.31 billion) between September 29 and November 17.

Wallets holding between 100,000 and 1 million DOGE also added to their stash, building their holdings by 150 million coins.

On the flip side, whales, or wallets holding 10 million and 100 million DOGE, cut their holdings by 20 million coins or 0.10%. This pattern coincides with US Dogecoin spot ETF flow, which has been on a steady decline since late October.

The divergence between shark and whale accumulation of Dogecoin is because of the latter’s high-liquidity breakouts or clear macro catalysts to reduce price slippage. Nonetheless, their actions indicate short-term risk management rather than long-term hoarding seen by the other cohorts.

Despite the recent crypto market turmoil, Bitfinex whales have been aggressively opening long positions for the past two days.

However, at press time, Dogecoin was trading at $0.05855, breaking below the lower support ($0.059) of its descending channel. This indicates short-term bearish momentum as sellers overwhelm buyers.

Nonetheless, the increased shark accumulation and long positions could defend upward movement of the coin, reclaiming $0.0589 to $0.060. Upcoming events that could sway traders’ sentiment include tomorrow’s Fed minutes and the market’s reaction to Nvidia’s earnings report due in a few hours.
