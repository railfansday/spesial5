Why Cardano holders rejected a $5M copper tokenization plan

Cardano [ADA] is seeing a spike in discussions over adoption, accountability, leadership, and the role of founding entities. On accountability and adoption requirements, Toto Finance was looking to expand the chain’s real-world asset (RWA) sector.

Toto Finance is a commodity-token issuer that was eyeing to create a Cardano Strategic Copper Reserve held in a U.S. warehouse and tokenized on Ethereum [ETH]. The metal would then be sold after 18 to 24 months.

However, Cardano holders voted overwhelmingly to reject the $5 million proposal to buy and tokenize copper, as per DRep Votes.

To be specific, Toto Finance’s proposal was suggesting a withdrawal of 22 million ADA tokens from the network’s treasury. They received slightly more than 20 million votes in support of the proposal.

Moreover, only 0.51% backed it against the required 67%, and none of the seven Constitutional Committee members approved it.

Some reasons for rejection were the concerns raised about fees. Representatives noted that only two-thirds of the fees would be used to actually buy the copper. Moreover, there was no clear custody protection in place, with some representatives opposing a venture into a private commodity.

The rejection was also looking to protect Cardano’s spending cap, as the copper treasury would take about half of the remaining budget.

However, some users argue that a copper supplier could back the tokens with metal it already owns. Thus, Cardano could test demand for tokenized copper without putting the ADA treasury on the hook for storage fees and swings in the metal prices.

With such a treasury rejection, future RWA proposals face higher accountability requirements. That could affect the price of the altcoin, which is testing the demand zone at $0.14 after failing to defend the $0.19 level.

At press time, the CMF indicated that capital was flowing out of the token, as seen in the indicator’s reading, which has declined from 0.09 to -0.11. However, the Stochastic RSI was bouncing off the oversold territory, hinting at potential bullish reversal.

Therefore, a reaction around the $0.14 would determine the altcoin’s next price move. Still, that may be hugely influenced by governance proposals, which could delay tokenization of commodities.

But the move was more of a win for Cardano holders as they protected their treasury.
