What is a criminal profile, and how does it affect you in GTA 6?

GTA 6's criminal profiles (plural, because Jason and Lucia each have their own criminal profile) tracks how you treat other people in the world, reminiscent of Red Dead Redemption 2's honor system. How violent you are, how lethal you are in your encounters, and how you treat people and animals in the world will all affect your rating.

There are five levels for Jason and Lucia's criminal profile, represented by "face with horns" emoji-style icons that are shown on your screen:

Your criminal profile is almost entirely dependent on how often you kill people. Try to incapacitate enemies, or wound them instead of killing them, and don't kill too many police or civilians, and you'll wind up with a Professional or Hardened profile. If you're violent for no reason, kill a lot of cops and civilians, and kill pets or defenseless animals, you'll gain a Violent, Psycho, or Irredeemable profile.

Here are the behaviors that will grant you each of the criminal profiles, and the bonuses that come with them:

If you're a Psycho for too long, you'll gain the Irredeemable profile. While you can move up and down between the other four profiles during the game, once you have an Irredeemable criminal profile, it's permanent.

According to Rockstar, "being irredeemable will have a lasting impact on Jason and Lucia," though we don't yet know what that means. We'll update this guide when we find out.

For my playthrough of the main story, I typically kept both Jason and Lucia in the Professional range, which is the profile I finished the main story with, though I did occasionally drop both of them down to Hardened.

I did this by (mostly) trying to be non-lethal in my encounters, using focus to aim for my enemies' knees and elbows during shootouts. I still killed a whole mess of people in my playthrough.
