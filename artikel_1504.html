Euro 2028, the next draw for the Blues is becoming clearer

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Nations League has finished with its initial phase, with among the lessons learned the composition of the five hats which on December 6 will make it possible to define the qualifying groups for Euro 2028 football. It is in Belfast that the 12 groups of four or five teams will then be detailed. With at stake a sesame promised to the first 12 and 8 best second.

France, during these qualifications which will be held from March 2027 to March 2028, will have the honor and advantage of seeded status. Such as Spain, Portugal, England, Italy, Netherlands, Greece, Belgium, Germany, Denmark, Norway and Croatia. This is a clear advantage in the race for Euro 2028 given the content of the other pots.

Nations such as Switzerland, Sweden and Turkey will still be feared in hat 2. Then in the hat 3 potential opponents like Ukraine, Poland or Scotland. The “worst” draw from hat 4 being represented by Finland, Bulgaria or Armenia.

At the back of the pack, in hat 5, we find Malta, Azerbaijan, Lithuania, Andorra, Gibraltar and Liechtenstein. But the Blues will not necessarily have to face four different countries on the road to Euro 2028. In the event of a Nations League Final Four next June, Zinédine Zidane’s players will in fact be content with a group of four. As in the race for the 2026 World Cup which saw the French team do battle with Ukraine, Iceland and Azerbaijan.

Hat 1: Spain, Portugal, France, England, Italy, Netherlands, Greece, Belgium, Germany, Denmark, Norway, Croatia.

Hat 2: Switzerland, Turkey, Serbia, Wales, Sweden, Austria, Hungary, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Northern Ireland, Czechia, Slovenia, Ireland.

Hat 3: Romania, North Macedonia, Montenegro, Slovakia, Georgia, Poland, Ukraine, Israel, Albania, Iceland, Scotland, Kosovo.

Hat 4: Armenia, Moldova, Kazakhstan, Latvia, San Marino, Finland, Cyprus, Faroe Islands, Bulgaria, Luxembourg, Belarus, Estonia.

Hat 5: Malta, Azerbaijan, Lithuania, Andorra, Gibraltar, Liechtenstein.
