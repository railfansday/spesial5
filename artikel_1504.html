Zidane, the dreaded verdict has fallen

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

After 14 years of waiting or almost, Zinédine Zidane inherited the status of coach of the France team, successor to Didier Deschamps who for a long time could frustrate Blues fans eager for change. Last September, the 1998 Ballon d’Or was able to lead Mbappé and others for the first time. Rebelote these days with a logical qualification for the quarter-finals of the League of Nations.

After six matches played under his command, has the former Real Madrid strategist convinced? The response is clearly mixed. At least with regard to this Odoxa survey for Winamax and RTL, the conclusions of which were published this weekend. We learn in particular that the person concerned has lost three points of popularity in the space of three months, still peaking at 83% favorable opinions among the French.

The French team that he now leads lost six units between the months of September and November, going from 75 to 69% popularity rate. Probably even more telling, among declared and surveyed football fans, 47% believe that the Blues were better under the leadership of Didier Deschamps. Not really a plebiscite, then.

Still in the eyes of this public of connoisseurs, 74% of those contacted say they are convinced by the new coach’s team compositions (-15 points); 68% by the quality of play (-17 points) and 65% by the performance of the French collective as a whole (-22 points). By losing in Italy (3-1) in particular, Zidane’s group lost a lot of credit.

From a tactical point of view, 47% of French people invited to express their opinion believe that the new boss of the Blues must above all not persist in his dogmas and offensive precepts, these expecting more balance during the next international outings, in particular against Spain in March 2027. As for the positioning of Kylian Mbappé, 41% want to see him evolve at the forefront; 31% on the left side of the attack.
