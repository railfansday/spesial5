NEAR Protocol Tokenomics Overhaul Catapults The Crypto Coin 10% Higher

NEAR Protocol is climbing as the market weighs a major tokenomics overhaul designed to reduce token inflation and ease long-term selling pressure. The NEAR rally follows crypto governance approval of a proposal to lower the network’s annual issuance ceiling from 2.5% to 1.6% over two years.

The proposal, known as HSP-029, passed with overwhelming support from NEAR’s House of Stake. It would gradually reduce the number of new tokens entering circulation, potentially strengthening the supply outlook as the network expands.

The move comes as NEAR Protocol seeks to turn its growing ecosystem activity into stronger value for token holders. With issuance cuts, cross-chain infrastructure, and renewed trading interest converging, we are watching if NEAR can sustain its latest breakout.

The approved proposal would reduce NEAR’s maximum annual issuance rate from 2.5% to 1.6%, with the change phased in over 24 months. According to the proposal, the reduction could prevent 11.8 million NEAR from entering circulation during the first two years. Over six years, the avoided issuance could reach 66.1 million tokens.

At full implementation, daily issuance would fall from roughly 89,500 NEAR to 57,300 NEAR. The network would retain its existing distribution of newly issued tokens, with 90% going to staking participants and 10% allocated to the protocol treasury.

The overhaul also introduces a trade-off for stakers. Gross staking yields could decline from approximately 5.27% to 3.37%, assuming staking participation remains unchanged.

However, the proposal still requires Security Council review, a 90-day grace period, and validator adoption before the changes become binding.

Beyond tokenomics, NEAR Protocol continues developing its chain-signature technology, which allows accounts to interact with external blockchains. NEAR Intents also supports cross-chain swaps, giving the ecosystem another potential source of activity and fee revenue.

The combination of lower issuance and expanding utility is giving NEAR crypto bulls a fresh narrative. However, the market will need to see sustained demand before the supply reduction translates into a lasting price trend.

NEAR Protocol’s cross-chain ambitions arrive as Bitcoin infrastructure projects compete to bring more functionality to the world’s largest cryptocurrency.

Bitcoin Hyper ($HYPER) is positioning itself as a Bitcoin Layer-2 network designed to support faster transactions, lower fees, and smart-contract functionality. Its architecture aims to connect Bitcoin’s security and liquidity with applications typically associated with more flexible blockchain ecosystems.

Bitcoin Hyper has begun rolling out testnet infrastructure, including a wallet and explorer, as it prepares for broader network testing.

While NEAR Protocol is building cross-chain functionality across multiple networks, Bitcoin Hyper is focusing directly on expanding Bitcoin’s role in decentralized finance. This could become important if Bitcoin holders increasingly seek access to applications, trading, and other on-chain opportunities without moving away from BTC-focused infrastructure.

The project is approaching its planned November 26, 2026, launch, with its presale entering the final stretch. Bitcoin Hyper has raised more than $33.2 million, while HYPER is priced at just $0.0136.

The project also offers staking rewards of 35% APY, adding another incentive for early participants ahead of its anticipated token launch.

With the presale scheduled to end on November 26, the approaching launch is becoming a key milestone for HYPER holders watching for exchange listings and ecosystem growth.

NEAR crypto is trading around the $3.80 area, leaving the $4 level as an important near-term hurdle. A sustained move above $4 could strengthen the recovery narrative and open the door toward the $4.30 region. A stronger breakout could bring the $4.50 area into focus, particularly if broader altcoin sentiment improves.

The tokenomics overhaul gives bulls a fundamental catalyst, but traders will also need to see stronger spot demand and improving market momentum.

On the downside, a failure to hold the $3.70–$3.80 area could expose NEAR to another test of support near $3.50. Continued weakness across the broader crypto market would make a sustained recovery more difficult.

For now, NEAR Protocol has a clear catalyst in its issuance reduction proposal, alongside continued development of its cross-chain ecosystem. The next stage of the crypto rally depends on whether buyers can turn the tokenomics story into sustained demand and push NEAR beyond its immediate resistance levels.
