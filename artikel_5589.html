Elon Musk said hiring Jensen Huang would be 'an honor' as Nvidia board weighs ouster

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Nvidia stock fell more than 8% in after-hours trading Wednesday as reports of multiple affairs put the CEO's job at risk

Elon Musk said that bringing on Nvidia $NVDA CEO Jensen Huang would be "an honor" as the chipmaker's board weighs Huang's future.

The Tesla $TSLA CEO replied on X $TWTR on Wednesday night to financial blog ZeroHedge, which wrote, “Elon please hire Jensen.” Musk answered, “That would be an honor,” according to CNBC.

Nvidia's board is gathering to discuss how the chipmaker would operate without Huang, its founder, according to Bloomberg. The board was readying a meeting, possibly as early as Wednesday, on Huang's future, according to The Wall Street Journal. Nvidia stock fell more than 8% in after-hours trading Wednesday.

Huang had a relationship with another Nvidia executive, according to The New York Times. The Times also reported that American intelligence had determined Alice Huang, a business executive with whom Jensen Huang acknowledged having an affair, was enlisted by China's Ministry of State Security to collect details about him, according to people briefed on the intelligence.

Sen. Kevin Cramer, a North Dakota Republican and a member of the Senate Banking Committee, again urged Huang on Wednesday to give up his post. Rep. Suhas Subramanyam, a Virginia Democrat, and Rep. Don Bacon, a Nebraska Republican, have also called for Huang to step down. “As a matter of his own conscience, he should resign,” Cramer told CNBC.

Nvidia's board was set to meet Wednesday evening to weigh Huang's future, and no clear successor exists. Huang apologized to employees on Sunday for being unfaithful to his wife. The board announced an independent investigation the same day and said it had no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised.

The independent directors retained the law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz and former acting U.S. attorney Joon H. Kim to lead that investigation. China's Foreign Ministry has called the espionage allegation unsubstantiated.

Nvidia is scheduled to report fiscal third-quarter earnings on Nov. 17. Three House Republican committee chairs have asked Huang to appear for a private session with lawmakers on Nov. 18.

Musk and Huang have been acquainted for a decade or longer, according to Bloomberg. Both joined President Donald Trump's visit to China this year. Nvidia sells the graphics processing chips Tesla uses to train AI models that handle self-driving and robotics work, and it also provides computing technology that powers Tesla's driver-assistance systems. Tesla is developing its own chips.
