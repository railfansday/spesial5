‘Saturday Night Live’ Heads to France

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

En direct de France, c’est samedi soir! Months after Saturday Night Live crossed the pond for a British production of the celebrated sketch show, its producers have eyes set on taking the Chunnel south. Variety reports that comedian and actor Manu Payet will host the inaugural episode of SNL France on Dec. 19. RnBoi, a French R&B artist, will be the first musical guest.

The show will mirror its U.S. and U.K. counterparts with a mix of live sketches, prerecorded segments, and musical guests all filmed before a studio audience. This one, however, will run only 52 minutes. Its inaugural cast will feature 10 actors.

Variety reports that SNL France’s producers spent a week shadowing their American counterparts to glean how the show operates. The writing team of Hugo Benamozig and David Caviglioli, who’ve scored hits in the country with Le Flambeau, Platane, and Terrible Jungle, will adapt the practices for a French audience. Just like the American version, the sketches will address French culture, entertainment, and politics, the last of which is notable as the country has a presidential election scheduled for 2027. The British version of SNL debuted in March and is now in its second season. Its success shows broad appeal for the show, which has also been franchised out to South Korea where SNL Korea has won awards and even inspired a spinoff, The Smile Clinic, according to Variety. France previously attempted an SNL spinoff in 2017 but it lasted only one episode.

“With SNL France, our ambition is not to replicate a model, but to reinterpret its codes through a uniquely French lens,” Jean-Marc Juramie, deputy CEO of Canal+ France, which will air the show told Variety.
