Natera: Principal Software Engineer in Test

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Natera is seeking a highly technical, hands-on Principal Software Engineer in Test to define and own the end-to-end test automation strategy across all products in the Women's Health, Organ Health, and EB (WH/OH/EB) vertical. In this role, you will design, build, and maintain scalable backend automation suites to validate critical workflows across distributed microservice architectures, guaranteeing data integrity from order entry and lab processing to clinical reporting. You will drive innovation by leveraging AI-assisted practices and large language models to automate test case generation, streamline log analysis, and enhance CI/CD execution speed.

As a Principal level individual contributor, you will set technical direction for quality engineering across multiple teams. You will partner with engineering, bioinformatics, laboratory operations, and Quality Assurance and Regulatory Affairs (QA/RA) leaders, and mentor engineers to build quality into the development process instead of inspecting it at the end. As a principal-level individual contributor, you will set technical direction and serve as a key organizational voice across teams. You will leverage strong interpersonal skills, clear communication, and deep technical credibility to align engineering groups, establish architectural standards, and elevate quality practices through peer leadership and technical influence.

Natera™ is a global leader in cell-free DNA (cfDNA) testing, dedicated to oncology, women’s health, and organ health. Our aim is to make personalized genetic testing and diagnostics part of the standard of care to protect health and enable earlier and more targeted interventions that lead to longer, healthier lives.

The Natera team consists of highly dedicated statisticians, geneticists, doctors, laboratory scientists, business professionals, software engineers and many other professionals from world-class institutions, who care deeply for our work and each other. When you join Natera, you’ll work hard and grow quickly. Working alongside the elite of the industry, you’ll be stretched and challenged, and take pride in being part of a company that is changing the landscape of genetic disease management.

Competitive Benefits - Employee benefits include comprehensive medical, dental, vision, life and disability plans for eligible employees and their dependents. Additionally, Natera employees and their immediate families receive free testing in addition to fertility care benefits. Other benefits include pregnancy and baby bonding leave, 401k benefits, commuter benefits and much more. We also offer a generous employee referral program!
