Trump Says He ‘Can’t Speak To’ Whether Russia is a Friend to America

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Donald Trump has returned from his trip to Asia, and in a press briefing before a White House press corps eager to learn what had taken place in his closed door summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping, as well as Russian President Vladimir Putin, whom the president gleefully referred to as “my friend” this week in front of a room full of cameras.

When CNN’s Kaitlan Collins pointed out that Russia is one of Iran’s biggest allies and asked Trump if Russia is a “friend to America,” the president of the United States of America said he ”can’t speak to America.”

So who can he speak for? Himself, of course, and the autocratic rulers like Putin whom he considered his close personal friends.

“I can’t speak to America,” Trump said, speaking from his position as the chief representative of the American people, government, and territory. “I’ve been dealing with Putin during the fake Russia-Russia-Russia hoax, and to be honest, he was very nice about it … and I consider him to be somebody that I have a very good relationship [with].”

Russia has, for the better part of a century, retained an adversarial position to the United States. They continue to be the primary military and financial backer of Iran, which America is currently at war with and which U.S. intelligence recently found is developing plans to target senior Trump officials with a “dirty bomb” in Washington, D.C..

But Trump spent much of the press conference praising Russia and China, while denigrating the United States.

“Some of our cities are so embarrassing,” he said at one point. “I don’t want to mention New York because I actually get along very well with Mayor Mamdani. And when I look at our cities where they’re dirty —  not I must say, mostly blue — where the roads are dirty and they have potholes all over the place, and railings are falling down. Many Americans voted for Trump in 2024 hoping that he would deliver vast improvements to their quality of life and economic conditions. They took him up on his promise to deliver a “golden age” of American prosperity. Instead of delivering, the president has spent his time in office padding his own bank accounts and building himself a personal granite and gold paradise at the White House. Trump may not be able to speak for America regarding Russia, but Americans certainly spoke for themselves in the midterm elections, and they left nothing up for interpretation.
