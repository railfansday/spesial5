Harry Potter HBO Original Series Gets Christmas Day Premiere Date

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

HBO's Harry Potter may be "underwhelming" after this reveal.

The Harry Potter franchise began in 1997 with the release of the first novel, Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone. Since its release, Harry Potter has become the biggest and most influential media property in the world with books, eight movies, prequel movies, stage productions, theme parks, video games, and now television. However, everyone is wondering how HBO will bring such a recognizable franchise to the world in a whole new way.

The HBO series Harry Potter is officially set to premiere on December 25 on HBO Max. However, ahead of the release, new character posters continue to be unveiled by HBO. A new poster featuring John Lithgow's Albus Dumbledore was released. However, what was more interesting was the magical artifact he was holding and that was the Elder Wand. Some fans commented that it looked "underwhelming" despite its importance within the novels, movies, and hopefully TV series.

Dumbledore, the headteacher at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, possesses the Elder Wand and has been used as his primary wand from 1945 for over 50 years. It is known as the most powerful wand in the Harry Potter franchise and is one of the three Deathly Hallows. The wand possesses unmatched magical power and is made from elder wood with a Thestral tail hair core, amplifying magic beyond what most wands can achieve.

Fans were quick to react to the unveiling of such an important magical artifact, with some stating, "It's the only thing I do not like at the minute. I think the problem is the fact that some are detailed and others are not." However, not everyone was disappointed and took the image for what it was by sharing, "I actually quite like the idea of such a remarkable wand looking so unremarkable." HBO has ensured everyone that it will be the most faithful adaptation of the original fantasy novels, and in turn leading everyone to comb through images and clips with a fine tooth comb.

Albus Dumbledore has been portrayed already in a number of movies with Richard Harris, Michael Gambon, and Jude Law taking on the legendary wizard. The character is such an integral part of Harry Potter's story as a young boy wizard beginning his journey entering such a strange new world. Lithgow is portraying the character for HBO's series, with fans eagerly awaiting to see what's in store.

HBO's Harry Potter season 1 will arrive on HBO Max on December 25.
