GPTomics' bioSkills includes 20 MIT-licensed chemoinformatics skills that cover a drug-discovery workflow: reading and standardizing molecules, fingerprints and similarity, docking and pose validation, QSAR, ADMET, free energy, generative design and retrosynthesis. They name the real tools to use, including RDKit, AutoDock Vina, GNINA, DiffDock-L, REINVENT 4, chemprop and AiZynthFinder.

All 20 install with one command from Cuttlefish.MD, HackerNoon's license-checked skills marketplace. They follow the open Agent Skills standard, so they run in Claude Code, Codex CLI and 30+ other tools, and each is indexed in HackerNoon's SKILL.md Library.

Predicts ADMET properties with ADMETlab 3.0, ADMET-AI and other models

Generates 3D conformer ensembles with RDKit ETKDGv3 and MMFF94/UFF

Designs covalent inhibitors and warheads for cysteine, lysine, serine and other residues

Runs relative and absolute binding free-energy calculations (RBFE / FEP+)

Designs new molecules with REINVENT 4: de novo, scaffold decoration, linkers

Predicts and scores poses with DiffDock-L and Boltz

Calculates ECFP/Morgan, MACCS, MAP4 and other fingerprints and descriptors

Reads, writes and converts SMILES, InChI, SDF, MOL2 and PDB files

Standardizes structures with the ChEMBL structure pipeline and RDKit

Builds and applies 3D pharmacophore models

Checks docked poses with PoseBusters validity tests and strain energy

Designs PROTACs, molecular glues and bivalent degraders, including E3 ligase choice

Builds QSAR / QSPR models with chemprop, MolFormer, Uni-Mol and baselines

Enumerates virtual libraries from reaction SMARTS and templates

Plans synthesis routes with AiZynthFinder and template-free models

Analyzes libraries by Bemis-Murcko scaffold and matched pairs

Searches by 3D shape with ROCS, USRCAT, Open3DAlign and ESPSim

Searches by Tanimoto, Tversky, Dice or cosine similarity

Searches libraries with SMARTS patterns, including recursive SMARTS

Runs structure-based virtual screens with AutoDock Vina, SMINA, GNINA and DiffDock-L

All 20 come with the Data & Science plugin, along with the rest of the 562 bioSkills:

For Codex CLI or another agent that supports the standard, clone the repo and copy plugins/data-science/skills/ into your agent's skills folder, such as .agents/skills/. Commercial tools named in a skill, such as ROCS or FEP+, need their own licenses.

Each skill keeps its MIT license and an ATTRIBUTION.md crediting GPTomics and the source commit. Browse the full library at hackernoon.com/skills, and star Cuttlefish.MD on GitHub.
