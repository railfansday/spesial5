Gallery: Inside Gold Diwali, a Historic Celebration by Gold House

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The lights were bright and so were the stars at Gold Diwali.

The Oct. 29 event in Los Angeles was the first-ever celebration of the Festival of Lights by Gold House, the Asian Pacific cultural institution providing opportunity and supporting the work of its community.

The organization’s new tentpole event, held at The Majestic Downtown, honored the heritage, achievements, and contributions of the South Asian diaspora around the world.

Attendees spanned prominent voices including CEOs, creators, and community members, including the acting and production team behind Ramayana: Rise of a Legend, which is set to be the most expensive Indian film ever made at $500 million and will debut globally on Nov. 5.

Leaders spanning entertainment, media, fashion, and business co-hosted the evening, including actors Freida Pinto, Dhar Mann, and Priyanka Kedia, as well as fashion designer Tan France, Blumhouse president Abhijay Prakash, and Poshmark founder Manish Chandra, Anita Chatterjee.

“Diwali is a celebration of light, community, and connection, and we wanted to bring that spirit to our Gold Diwali while honoring how culture shapes who we are and the possibilities we create,” said Bing Chen, executive chairman and CEO of Gold House.

The organization also announced a new partnership with nonprofit film fund South Stack Studios, called the Gold Stack Shorts Fund, to support short films and microseries focusing on South Asian stories. Applications are being accepted through Nov. 30.

Gold Diwali guests participated in traditional Diwali customs, with a South Asian menu by Rasoi Catering and music from DJ MissNinja. Entertainment also included a live tabla performance by KVS Tabla, a jewelry experience by Bhindi Jewelers, and a portrait studio with dating app Dil Mil and The Lounge Booth. Additional sponsors included Smartwater, Glance, McKinsey & Company, and Google’s 100 Zeros. The event was produced by BMF Productions.
