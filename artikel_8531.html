Open Schwab Intelligent Portfolios if you have $5,000 or more and want a portfolio picked and rebalanced for you with no advisory fee at any balance. In exchange, it keeps 6% to 29% of your account in cash, set by your risk profile, and that cash earns a money market fund’s yield instead of stock and bond returns.

Schwab Intelligent Portfolios, Charles Schwab’s robo-advisor for investors with $5,000 or more, charges no advisory fee, so on $10,000 you pay only its funds’ expenses, $2 to $15 a year.

But its Global Moderate portfolio keeps $1,200 of that $10,000 in cash earning 3.51% a year, so each percentage point stocks and bonds earn above that rate costs you $12 a year.

Investors with $5,000 or more who want a portfolio picked and rebalanced for them, and who’d keep some cash anyway, get one here for nothing beyond the funds’ expenses. Savers with less to invest can start Fidelity Go with $10 and pay no advisory fee below $25,000, and anyone who wants nearly every dollar invested can choose Betterment, which charges a fee but keeps 0.5% in cash.
