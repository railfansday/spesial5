Open Chase Total Checking if you want a bank with branches in every state but Alaska and Hawaii and can avoid its $15 monthly fee with a waiver, such as $500 a month in direct deposits. Skip it if you’d pay that fee most months or want your checking to earn interest.

Total Checking is Chase’s most popular checking account. Its branch access and $400 new-customer bonus come with a $15 fee in any month you miss all four waivers, though new accounts pay no fee for at least their first two statement periods.

It suits anyone who wants a branch wherever they live or travel in the lower 48 and can route $500 a month in paychecks there to keep it free. If you’d miss every waiver, Chase Secure Banking keeps the branches without paper checks or outgoing wires for $4.95 a month, or $0 with $250 in direct deposits, and savers who want interest should open a high-yield checking account.
