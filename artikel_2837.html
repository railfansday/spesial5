Philadelphia mayor wants the Eagles to play in a dome

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Eagles will become an indoor team if the mayor of Philadelphia gets her way.

"I want a dome," Mayor Charelle L. Parker said on 93.3 WMMR, via the Philadelphia Inquirer. "Don't you tell. This is a secret between us. I just saw a rendering of this beautiful dome that they're talking about for the Bears."

Moving into a dome would change the character of late-season and playoff games in Philadelphia, which are often affected by snow, wind and cold. Plenty of fans won't like it. But politicians and NFL owners often prefer stadiums with roofs because they bring in lucrative events, indoor sports, concerts, conventions and trade shows.

The Eagles told season ticket holders last year that they are looking at either renovations to Lincoln Financial Field or a new stadium. Eagles owner Jeffrey Lurie has committed only to staying in "the region," not in the city of Philadelphia, but both Parker and Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro have said they want the Eagles to remain in the city.

The Eagles' lease at Lincoln Financial Field runs through the 2032 season.
