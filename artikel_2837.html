D.J. Reed, DJ Wonnum out of practice for Lions Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Lions were obviously without linebacker Jack Campbell at practice on Wednesday and two other key defensive players were also on the sideline as the team worked to get ready to face the Dolphins.

Cornerback D.J. Reed (hip) and defensive end DJ Wonnum (concussion) were both out of action for the Lions. Both players were injured during last Sunday's win over the Vikings.

Center Cade Mays (ankle) was also hurt in that game and he missed practice as well.

Safety Brian Branch (concussion), safety Kerby Joseph (knee), linebacker Amen Ogbongbemiga (toe), running back Jacob Saylors (neck), and wide receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown (rib) were all limited participants. Cornerback Rock Ya-Sin (cramps) was a full participant for the Lions.
