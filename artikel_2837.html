Jayson Tatum’s soft technical foul cost the Celtics the game, and it was completely avoidable

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Boston Celtics had one last chance to tie the game against the Denver Nuggets on Wednesday night, trailing by three with six seconds remaining. Jamal Murray had just extended Denver's lead with a tough mid-range pull-up, and the Celtics called timeout after the ball swished through the net. As his team was walking to the huddle, Boston star Jayson Tatum threw the ball towards the official at center court. He was given a technical foul, and it ultimately cost his team the game.

The Nuggets beat the Celtics, 105-101, after the controversial technical was given to Tatum. The ESPN broadcasters agreed that Tatum deserved the technical, but fans online are still fuming after what they perceive as another soft tech for Tatum.

Tatum could have just handed the ball to the official next to him. Instead, he threw it in frustration to the official at halfcourt who wasn't looking for the ball. Watch the play here and decide if this was really worth a tech with the game on the line yourself.

Nikola Jokic knew it should have been a technical foul by the letter of the law right away. Jokic sprinted for the refs to beg for a T on Tatum, and they were already in the process of giving him one.

Section V, subsection P of the NBA rulebook describes what warrants a tech in this instance:

The deliberate act of throwing the ball or any object at an official by a player, coach, trainer, or other team bench person is a technical foul and violators are subject to ejection from the game.

Celtics coach Joe Mazzulla didn't use the tech as an excuse for why his team lost the game.

A few days earlier, Tatum was given an even stranger technical foul for clapping.

All Tatum had to do was give the ball to the ref standing next to him, and the Celtics would have had one more possession to try to tie the game. This might seem like a soft technical foul in that situation, but it was also easily avoidable.
