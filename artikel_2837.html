Bo Nix's 12th TD pass of the season gives Broncos 10-0 lead over Raiders

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Broncos have gotten into the end zone.

Quarterback Bo Nix connected with tight end Adam Trautman for a 1-yard touchdown, giving Denver a 10-0 lead over division rival Las Vegas.

The score capped a 12-play, 74-yard drive that lasted 5:51.

The Broncos faced just one third down on the possession, which they converted with a defensive pass interference penalty in the end zone.

On the next play, Nix threw his 12th touchdown pass of the season.

Nix is now 7-of-12 for 62 yards so far in the contest.

Denver got its first points after the club blocked A.J. Cole's first punt of the game, setting up the offense with excellent field position at the Las Vegas 24-yard line.

But Denver could not take full advantage of the opportunity, with a stuffed run, short scramble, and incomplete pass leading to a 38-yard field goal by Wil Lutz to give the Broncos a 3-0 advantage.
