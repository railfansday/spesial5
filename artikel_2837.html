Cowboys injuries: LBs Dee Winters, Jaishawn Barham upgraded

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

As usual, the Dallas Cowboys are home for the Thanksgiving holiday, this year hosting the Philadelphia Eagles. After Thanksgiving, they get a mini break before Seattle. That may factor into who plays Thursday. We know a little more after today's report. Tomorrow we will know more when the final report comes out.

Jaishawn Barham (Hip/Groin) and Dee Winters (Knee) were upgraded to limited after a Monday DNP. Getting either of them back would be a big break for a depleted position.

Tyler Booker (Ankle) and Jake Ferguson (Knee/Ankle) still have not practiced and are expected to be ruled out. Maybe after the mini-break they can start getting back in action.

Jonathan Mingo (Ankle) was downgraded to a DNP today. Joey Porter Jr. (Foot), Dak Prescott (Left Hand), and Jalen Thompson (Hamstring) were full participants today. For Thompson, it appears that he will return from injured reserve this week.
