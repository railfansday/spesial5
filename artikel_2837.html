Chargers Injury Report: Justin Herbert ruled out vs. Patriots

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Chargers will be without starting quarterback Justin Herbert as the team officially ruled out him out with a concussion on their final injury report of Week 12. This will be Herbert's first game missed due to an injury since 2023 where he missed the final four games of the season.

In his place, second-year UDFA D.J. Uiagalelei looks to be in line for the start with veteran Jake Browning serving as his backup.

As for the rest of the final injury designations, both linebacker Daiyan Henley and running back Keaton Mitchell have also been ruled out of Sunday's game. This will be the second consecutive game missed for Henley due to knee inflamation.

Rookies Akheem Mesidor and Brenen Thompson are both listed as doubtful, making this the first full game missed by Mesidor.

Tight end David Njoku, cornerback Tarheeb Still, defensive tackle Dalvin Tomlinson, edge rusher Tuli Tuipulotu, and safety Kendall Willliamson round out the report and are all listed as questionable. Most of these players missed the first two practices of the week and it's actually a very positive sign that they all still earned just a questionable tag. Luckily, it seems like the Chargers won't be as short-handed as it originally looked this week.
