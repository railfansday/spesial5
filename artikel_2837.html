Nico Collins' acrobatic TD catch gives Texans 31-9 lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Nico Collins made one of the best touchdown catches you will ever see in the third quarter on Sunday.

The Texans wide receiver was tightly covered by Chargers cornerback Tarheeb Still when, in the front corner of the end zone, he somehow made a one-handed catch and got both feet in the end zone. Still immediately looked up to the replay board to see how Collins could have caught the ball.

The 13-yard touchdown has the Texans up 31-9 with 8:16 remaining in the third quarter.

C.J. Stroud has 286 passing yards and four touchdowns. His other scoring tosses covered 17 yards to Xavier Hutchinson, 63 yards to Tank Dell and 12 yards to Kayshon Boutte.

The Texans have outgained the Chargers 352 to 116.
