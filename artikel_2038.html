Blackburn Rovers 2 – 1 West Ham United | Final whistle, first impressions as new manager bounce falls flat

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

West Ham managed to overturn their recent sequence of dreadful, lethargic starts with bright first half performance against Blackburn Rovers. Both sides had their chances but West Ham’s attack dominated the first half, largely through Austbø and Ezra Mayers down the left hand side.

The Hammers set up with Engels the deepest midfielder sitting in front of the back four and Kanté plus Mukasa the more forward of the trio. It seemed to protect the back line a little better as, apart from one looped shot over Hermansen’s goal, Blackburn’s threat was contained during the first 45 minutes.

Three or four times in the first ’45 Mayers overlapped after combining with Austbø – looking far more potent a threat than Solomon who he’d replaced – and got excellent crosses in from the left, an attacking option that had been lacking with Morato as the full back.

Piroe headed against the post early in the second half after Austbo – again – twisted and turned on the left, and Engles crashed the rebound just wide. Waves of pressure developed on the Blackburn goal as the Hammers responded to their half time talk but against the run of play from a break down the other end, Blackburn took the lead through Gudjohnson who headed through Hermansen’s legs. West Ham behind again.

Parker responded with Solomon for Austbø who’d run his socks off for an hour and Taty appearing in place of Kanté.

Controversy as Rovers scored a second with Kilman prostrate after a head collision – Dapo Afolayan the ex West Ham boy fired one into the corner leaving West Ham with a mountain to climb. It must be said, naive defending from West Ham.

Joel Piroe grabbed one back after a lucky bounce sat nicely for him in the box: 2 – 1 with eight minutes to go and the Ewood Park atmosphere changed, the home fans nervy and the Hammers fans in full voice. The minutes ticked away and West Ham struggled to get the ball towards Blackburn’s box: Orford, a late substitute, very fortunate to avoid conceding a penalty in the closing minutes – once again the lack of VAR coming to West Ham’s rescue.

Five minutes of added time, West Ham looking as if they had neither the urgency or the ability to surge forward again, fiddling about on the edge of their own box with Hermansen being booed every time he touched the ball. One last chance as Soucek headed straight at the ‘keeper in the last few seconds and that was that. Poor. Very poor. So much possession and so little to show for it.
