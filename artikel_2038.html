Sources: Matthias Jaissle has full Newcastle backing

Newcastle United manager Matthias Jaissle still has the backing of "everyone" behind the scenes at the club, sources have told Football Insider.

The Magpies are caught in a run of just one win from their last six games in all competitions, but have no "panic" regarding the direction of their campaign.

Newcastle are understood to be "confident" that Jaissle can turn the team's fortunes around once he gets his squad back to full strength in terms of injuries.

Sources say that the Newcastle board were aware that there would be "bumps in the road" when they appointed the German, and have no plans to sack him at the moment.

The club hierarchy always knew that this was likely to be a transitional season at St James' Park, after significant change in the playing squad after last term.

However, that hasn't stopped the fans from growing frustrated in recent weeks, with the team sitting 12th in the Premier League heading into the international break.

Jaissle was something of a surprise appointment from Newcastle after Eddie Howe's departure in the summer, given that he was a huge unknown in England.

It was always going to be a significant adaptation for the 38-year-old, who had spent the past three years in Saudi Arabia prior to moving to Tyneside.

However, he appears set to be afforded a significant amount of patience by PIF and the rest of the Newcastle board.

The Magpies' squad has grown significantly younger this season, and the hierarchy are well aware that it will take time for some members of the squad to prove themselves.

As a result, they are keen to avoid heaping the pressure on Jaissle to deliver immediate success, and are instead prepared to help him as much as possible.

They have no plans to sack the new boss at the moment, despite expectations not being met in recent weeks.

Football Insider revealed last week that Newcastle "would love" to tie right-back Tino Livramento down to a new contract in the coming months.

The 23-year-old has suffered from significant injury issues in recent years, and is sidelined once again at the moment.

Sources say that Newcastle still view Livramento as a key part of their long-term project, and are keen to fend off interest from elsewhere with a new deal.

The England international's current deal is due to expire in 2028, and he remains on the radar of both Manchester City and Arsenal.
