Rangers 'doing everything' to stop Ryan Naderi joining new club - Transfer Expert

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Rangers are bracing to receive interest in Ryan Naderi after his strong start to the season, sources have told Football Insider.

The 23-year-old has established himself as a crucial part of Derek McInnes' plans for the season, particularly in the absence of some of the Ibrox club's other attacking options.

He has made 17 appearances across all competitions so far, scored nine goals and provided five assists, with a number of high-profile suitors alerted to his form.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Rangers are prepared to do everything in their power to keep Naderi at the club long term.

The Czechia international will be key to any success McInnes' side enjoy this season, having already fired them to the League Cup final with a goal contribution in every round.

Regardless of the interest from clubs across European football, Naderi is likely to remain at Rangers beyond the January transfer window, as offers will not be entertained.

Former Everton CEO Keith Wyness told Football Insider that Rangers are likely to capitalise on Naderi's popularity with the supporters as part of their commercial efforts.

The forward has quickly become a fans' favourite at Ibrox alongside his impressive performances, which have wowed everybody watching including his suitors.

However, regardless of the interest from elsewhere, Rangers have big plans for Naderi and are hopeful of keeping him at Ibrox beyond the January transfer window.

"Rangers will be doing everything in their power to keep their best players at the club," Brown told Football Insider.

"That includes somebody like Naderi, who has been outstanding for them so far this season, and his recent performances have shown how important he can be.

"Obviously, when a player is performing at such a high level for Rangers, he's going to attract interest from some other top clubs.

"Clubs in the Premier League, for example, will have scouts watching Rangers every week and I don't doubt they will have been impressed by what they've seen from Naderi.

"But from a Rangers point of view, they want him to keep being an important player for them, and they need to keep their best players if they want to win silverware.

"So I would be very surprised if they were prepared to let Naderi leave mid-way through the season, regardless of whether anybody else is keen."

Transfer rumours are swirling around Rangers at the moment, with speculation about incoming and outgoing deals picking up ahead of the January window.

Naderi is unlikely to be allowed to leave, but others could be, whether that is in the mid-season window or next summer, as McInnes looks to find a balance in his squad.

Wyness told Football Insider on 8 November that Rangers could look to cash in on Youssef Chermiti when he returns from injury near the end of this season.

Chermiti, 22, is currently expected to be out until the latter stages of the season with his ACL injury, which he suffered days after Rangers rejected a £22million bid in the summer.

That would then free up funds to agree a permanent deal for Badredine Bouanani, who has impressed since making his way into the first-team squad under McInnes.
