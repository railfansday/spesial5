Newcastle United 2 Arsenal 2 – Saturday 21 November 2026 5.30pm

If you ever wanted proof of how unpredictable football is, here is the ultimate proof.

Arsenal absolutely dominated the first half.

They looked very much like they were the reigning champions and league leaders by five points.

Newcastle United didn’t get a kick. Well, not a kick where it mattered.

Arsenal dominating the ball and territory, Newcastle just clearing the ball and it coming straight back at them.

Bruno Guimaraes had headed a close in header just wide and it felt like a goal was inevitable.

The visitors were breaking the half-hearted United press with ease and you really had to feel for Lewis Hall when the goal did come. He didn’t deal with a ball he should have, next thing Havertz with a very clever ball that Dedic wasn’t alive to and Eze giving Hornicek no chance.

Matthias Jaissle took decisive action for the second half, with Ramsey, Elanga and Livramento replacing Steur, Murphy and Dedic.

Inside four minutes Newcastle were level.

In almost the exact same part of the pitch, Odegaard made a far worse blunder than Hall had for their goal. Osula taking advantage to feed Barnes in the box and a great low finish.

It was then all suddenly Newcastle United pouring forward and the first half seemed like another match.

Just after the hour mark an excellent corner routine saw Hall cross from the right and Burn a free header, that caused chaos and Malick Thiaw on hand to smash it home from a yard out.

Var vainly tried to find a handball, then it was looking for an offside, before admitting defeat.

Somehow, somehow, Newcastle United were now ahead!

Newcastle didn’t sit back and continued to look for another goal, absolutely the right tactics. Rather than sit back.

Arteta making subs and Bruno Guimaraes trudging off as one of them.

The Arsenal equaliser was a great goal for them as they played it from back to front but Newcastle could have defended it better as Madueke cut inside. Plus Hornicek should have saved it, even though the shot went through a defender’s legs.

More than twenty minutes then played including nine minutes added time, with a pretty even closing period of the match, even though you naturally adopted the stance of you just know Arsenal are going to find a winner somehow…

Never in a million years did you see that second half coming after Arsenal had total control of the first.

Newcastle at last seeing a bit of luck going their way with that Odegaard mistake and suddenly that helped give confidence and momentum. Plus it helped that United brought on three mobile players all able to carry the ball and with a bit of pace.

No doubt the extremist Matthias Jaissle haters won’t want to give him any credit.

However, I think he was brave with his substitutions and I have a feeling that whatever 11 he had played in the first half, we would have seen Arsenal dominate the opening 45.

A hopeless first half and a second half that gives hope.
