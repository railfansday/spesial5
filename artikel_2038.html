Malick Thiaw started the last three matches for Jurgen Klopp and Germany.

Klopp only picking up one point from his first two Nations League matches in charge, before then bringing in the Newcastle United defender.

With Malick Thiaw in the side, Germany only conceding one goal in some five hours of football and picking up seven points from a possible nine.

However, after playing the full game as Germany won 3-1 away against Serbia on Friday, Monday sees Jurgen Klopp dropping to the bench/resting Malick Thiaw for Monday night’s home game against the Netherlands. With Schlotterbeck and Tah starting at the heart of defence.

Earlier on Monday, another full match and international victory for Newcastle United’s Ousmane Diabate, the exciting midfield prospect played the full match as the 19-year-old helped Guinea to a 3-1 AFCON qualifying group win over Eritrea.

Ousmane Diabaté was still only 18 but already a full international for Guinea and first choice in the Turkey top tier for Gençlerbirliği playing as a defensive midfielder, when it was announced officially by the club in early September that Newcastle United had signed him.

The progress of the transfer of Ousmane Diabaté to Newcastle United was covered extensively in the Turkish media across August, eventually reported that a £3m deal had been agreed, with a guaranteed £2.7m plus another £300,000 in future add-ons. It was also stated that Ousmane Diabaté would be staying on loan with the Turkish club but Newcastle United able to recall him in January if they decided to.

Gençlerbirliği manager Metin Diyadin had this to say at the end of August about Ousmane Diabaté and his move to Newcastle United.

“Ousmane Diabaté was playing in the youth team. We brought him into the team and because he came from Africa, there was a contract process involved. He has very high potential. We finalised his sale to Newcastle United. He is currently playing for us on loan. He is a very mature player for his age. He has good game vision and technical ability. I think he will reach different levels as he plays more. I’m really curious how our teams [in Turkey] didn’t see this in him. We transferred him to England.”
