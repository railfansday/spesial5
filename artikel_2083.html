'He's got raw talent' - Notts County urged to plot shock QPR transfer raid ahead of January

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Notts County have been steady so far in their first League One campaign since 2014/15, sitting 17th with 14 points in 12 games.

Given how difficult a start they've had on paper, it's been incredibly impressive to see how solid Martin Paterson's side has been defensively, despite being a newly promoted side. Only Huddersfield Town and Bradford City have a better xGA, while they've allowed the fourth-fewest shots and shots on target, too.

It's not all been perfect, though. While Notts have been brilliant at stopping shots, they haven't been excellent going the other way. They've scored just 13 times this season, with five of those coming in their last three.

Perhaps that could be an indication that things are starting to click up front, especially with Charlton Athletic loanee Daniel Kanu netting his first two goals for the club in that three-game stretch, but attacking firepower is, at the moment, top of the January wishlist when the winter window opens.

FLW's Notts County fan pundit, Gareth Parr, is still of the opinion that a striker is needed at Meadow Lane in January. Kanu has been sharing striker duties with six-foot-four frontman Luka Smyth, who also has a couple of goals, both against Burton Albion back in August.

Martin Paterson has started to get some joy playing them both alongside each other in this three-game unbeaten run, but if anything, that increases the demand for competition in those places.

Parr wouldn't mind Notts taking a look at QPR frontman Rayan Kolli, who has just recently returned to the Hoops fold after being unfavoured by Julien Stephan due to a perceived lack of effort.

"Notts desperately need a striker," he said. "And with that, you look one of two ways. Either older, proven and capable of scoring 15-20 goals, or a younger, developing one.

"I guess in terms of young and developing, Rayan Kolli at QPR is a promising youth prospect. Notts have dipped into the loan market regularly, and Kolli has been struggling to get into the QPR side, especially behind [Richard] Kone and [Rumarn] Burrell when they're fit.

"He's got raw talent, needs guidance, but could provide some interesting competition for Kanu and Smyth, who both seem to be struggling to find the net."

Kolli has been on the cusp of becoming a regular starter at QPR for a couple of years now. He netted four times in 819 minutes in the 2024/25 Championship campaign, and last season, scored five times in just over 1,000 minutes.

With the Hoops a much better chance-creating side this year in Julien Stephan's second campaign in charge at Loftus Road, it seemed almost inevitable that he could make that step-up.

For one reason or another, though, he hasn't been able to. Even now, with him back in the fold, he's yet to score, and after starting the first three games after the three-week international break, Kolli has failed to start any of the last four, and has been unused from the bench in two of them.

As Parr mentioned, the 21-year-old has Kone and Burrell ahead of him, but the latter is currently out injured, and yet he's still struggling to start regularly. Either Paul Smyth has been given the nod, or Stephan has gone with just the Ivorian as a lone striker.

Kolli needs a fresh start somewhere, even if it's just a change of scenery for a few months, which a loan spell in January would provide. Perhaps Notts County would be the perfect place for him to rediscover some form and get him back on track at Loftus Road.

Unfortunately for Notts, or any other League One side who may want to loan Kolli this upcoming January, QPR have a real history of injuries at the top end of the pitch, and that could make the Hoops lean towards keeping him just in case.

As mentioned, Burrell is currently out and has played just 14 league games in 2026 so far, following a lengthy lay-off in the second half of last season. Justin Obikwu is nowhere to be seen due to injury, and Alfie Lloyd has been in and out of the squad due to fitness, too.

If all of them are fit, alongside Kone and Smyth, Kolli may be seen as expendable. However, the likelihood of that happening seems slim, and even if it did come to fruition for a short time, you can't bank on the whole frontline options being available at the same time at Loftus Road.
