Listeners of The Mag Podcast will know there was a little Newcastle United theme going on to pass the time during the international break. Comparing a past season with the current one.

I’m going to do exactly the same, not with a season such as 2008-09. That’s too far in the past for me.

I’m going back to last (2025-26) season and comparing it with this 2026-27 season after 10 games.

The difference in the Premier League table between now and last season after 10 games. The difference? One place higher than last season, from 13th to 12th.

It had me wondering, is it going to be such a season again?

Last season wasn’t a remarkable one for Newcastle United and the fans.

It was a season with a lot of frustration and one that ended with a lot of players leaving the club at the close of the season.

Both Newcastle United seasons have 12 points after 10 games, though the goal difference is slightly worse this time, going from -1 to -3.

After 10 games Newcastle United have now scored three more goals than at this point last season BUT have also conceded five more.

This next game is against Arsenal, the reigning Champions and five points clear at the top, then it is second in the table Brighton.

It is then huge games against Man United and Sunderland games,

These next four matches could be pivotal when it comes to the morale of the players and whether we fall into a relegation battle, maybe even the future of Matthias Jaissle at St. James’ Park will be at risk.

What this season is going to bring is still a mystery but the early signs don’t look promising…
