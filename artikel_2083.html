Interesting to read through these BBC Sport ‘neutrals’ comments, following on from Newcastle United drawing 2-2 with Arsenal.

Newcastle United not getting a kick in the first half.

In the second, United by far the better team.

Matthias Jaissle’s triple half-time substitution massively contributing to that.

Always interesting to hear the neutral perspectives, as well as from Arsenal fans and the odd Newcastle United supporter…

‘First half we were very good, second half a bit complacent along with Newcastle out working us and forcing us into multiple mistakes, they were very good.
