Taylor Swift Lights Up the Stage With Epic 1st CMAs Performance in 13 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Country music and Taylor Swift go way back, and the pop star had an unforgettable full circle moment at the 2026 CMA Awards.

Swift, 36, returned to the awards show’s stage at the Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, Tennessee, for her first CMAs performance in 13 years on Wednesday, November 18, and it was like she never left the famed stage.

She dazzled in a Toy Story-inspired short dress that Jessie would no-doubt approve of, wearing custom cowboy boots as she began singing, “I Knew It, I Knew You,” from the franchise’s fifth installment.

As Swift walked backstage, she met up with some of the biggest influences in her country career, including longtime friend Brad Paisley — whom she opened for as a young star — who was shining the trophies at a table.

The Grammy winner took a minute to rock out with Little Big Town, whom she wrote 2016’s “Better Man” for. Swift didn’t skip a beat, joining Sugarland for a brief dance nearly a decade after they collaborated on 2018’s “Babe.”

As Swift continued her performance, she played cards with Brooks and Dunn — she previously covered their 2010 hit “Ain’t Nothing ‘Bout You” — before handing host Lainey Wilson her stylish cowboy hat.

The singer proceeded to grab a pair of sunglasses to match Eric Church‘s iconic style as the two fist-bumped behind the stage. (The duo have been friendly since Swift replaced Church on The Rascal Flatts’ tour in 2006.)

Once out on stage, Swift was joined by Keith Urban, her three-time collaborator, who played the banjo beside her to a cheering crowd.

The audience erupted when Swift transitioned from “I Knew It, I Knew You,” into “Our Song,” while standing on a set that looked like Jessie’s favorite tire swing and tree from the Pixar movie.

Swift also paid tribute to the animated movie with her guitar, which featured glitter clouds reminiscent of those seen in every Toy Story film.

Fans could hardly wait for the star-studded ceremony, hosted by Wilson for a third consecutive year. Along with Swift, Luke Combs, Megan Moroney, Mumford & Sons and Chris Stapleton are among this year’s stellar performers.

Swift last performed at the awards show in 2013, singing her hit “Red” alongside Vince Gill and Alison Krauss.

The music icon and a two-time CMA Entertainer of the Year later returned to the CMAs in 2016 to present an award.

Wednesday marked a big night for Swift, who was nominated for Single of the Year for “I Knew It, I Knew You” from the Toy Story 5 soundtrack, which dropped in June. (Ella Langley beat her out for her hit “Choosin’ Texas.”)

“Looks like I haven’t yee’d my last haw,” Swift wrote via her Instagram Story in September, referencing her CMAs nod and confirming her plans to attend the ceremony for the first time in a decade.

The song marked what the hitmaker described as a return to her country music roots.

“The word daze is spelled ‘d-a-z-e’ in the lyrics, but you could also, if you wanted to think of it as days ‘d-a-y-s’ you could perceive it as that,” Swift said during a September interview on iHeartCountry House Party. “I thought of it, like, a childhood daze you look back and you see this sort of blurry, beautiful memory that’s kind of blurry at the edges and blades of the grass in summer that harkens back to childhood and playing and using your imagination.”

The song was released weeks before Swift tied the knot with Kansas City Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce in a romantic and private ceremony held at Madison Square Garden in New York City on July 3.

A source exclusively told Us on July 1 that the pop star and the NFL athlete were looking forward to having a “whimsical” celebration with friends and family.

After the nuptials, a separate insider shared, “You could tell she put a lot of work and time into writing her vows. Travis teared up more than Taylor. They shared very personal details and anecdotes that some guests had never even heard before.”
