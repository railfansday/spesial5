Yooka-Laylee studio explains upcoming Definitive Edition's absence on Xbox to 'frustrated' fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

"As a small team, we hope you guys can understand the challenges we face."

Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair: Definitive Edition is notably skipping Xbox Series X/S consoles, and the studio blames "technical challenges and costs."

The Definitive Edition is bringing support for 120fps, revamping the platformer's lighting, updating textures and shadows, and packaging in the Trowzer's Top Tonic Pack DLC next month. But a large question mark hung over the lack of an Xbox version.

"Hi folks, just wanted to say a massive thanks to everyone on here for the support for Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair: Definitive Edition. The response has BEE-n incredible, and we can't wait for you to experience the game all over again on December 15th," developer Playtonic Games said in a new social media post.

"We are aware of feedback regarding the lack of Xbox Series S/X support, and we just wanted to provide some clarity on the reasoning behind this decision. Bringing Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair: Definitive Edition to PlayStation 5 and Nintendo Switch 2 has been an exciting adventure… Unfortunately, we've had to prioritise our efforts given the technical challenges and costs presented in the creation of this updated version of the game and as a result, we are unable to commit to any additional platforms for the foreseeable future," it continued.

Playtonic said it understood Xbox players' frustrations, but the development costs associated with bringing the game to more platforms "just isn't viable for us at this time." Playtonic is still an independent game studio, after all, and Xbox console sales have been lagging behind the competition for quite a while.

"As a small team, we hope you guys can understand the challenges we face. Please know that this wasn't an easy decision to make, and we apologise to any of you affected by this. We only wish things were different, and we value every single one of you and your feedback," the statement went on.

Digital Foundry's John Linneman chimed in to tell folks that the omissions isn't a huge deal, because Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair already looks pretty great on Xbox consoles. "The Xbox One X version of this game is already native 4K," he tweeted. "The upgrade seems minor, thus it's already excellent on Xbox. Was the best console version by far."

The original Yooka-Laylee was a collectathon throwback in the same vein as Banjo-Kazooie, but this follow-up took more inspiration from Rare's Donkey Kong Country games instead - and the results were pretty stunning.

"It's the new in Impossible Lair which impresses, the tightness of the game's 2D controls and intricately designed levels which stands out, and which seems better fitted to Playtonic's toolbox," Eurogamer's review said. "It's a far more polished game than the studio's predecessor, and one well worth exploring through."
