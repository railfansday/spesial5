Forget Balogun: Pochettino's Must-Sign USMNT Star for Man Utd

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Manchester United's new era under Mauricio Pochettino has officially begun, and it's no surprise that attention has already started to turn towards which players he wants to bring in.

The Argentine has signed a two-and-a-half-year deal to replace Michael Carrick in the dugout, with his first game in charge to come on Sunday afternoon at Anfield against arch-rivals Liverpool.

But there is already huge speculation about which players will have a long-term future at Old Trafford during his tenure, and which players he will want to bring in to help make his time in the north-west a success.

It has been reported that Pochettino will be given funds to bolster the squad in the January transfer window, with a striker, centre-back and left-back all on the agenda after the club failed to strengthen those positions during the summer.

Reports since his appointment was confirmed last week have linked a number of players from the USMNT to the club, including AS Monaco star Folarin Balogun, who was a key player during their time together for the national team.

But Balogun has barely featured for the Ligue 1 side this season after his Deadline Day move to Everton collapsed after the window had already closed, and that means it would be nigh on impossible for him to be able to come into the club and hit the ground running like they will need him to.

Tyler Adams and Weston McKennie have also been linked with potential switches, but after completing a £155m midfield rebuild in the summer with Andrey Santos, Youri Tielemans, and Carlos Baleba joining Kobbie Mainoo as options, it would be very odd to see them arrive at this stage.

However, there is a very clear option within the USMNT squad that would be a huge upgrade for Man Utd at this stage of the season, and could make an instant impact, and that's left-back Antonee Robinson.

The Fulham star has been linked with a move to the club in the past, and his performances in recent seasons have seen him establish himself as one of the best full-backs in the Premier League, as described by Marco Silva in 2024.

“He’s been great for us. He’s one of the best left-backs in this league.

“It’s not just the way he is able to stop players, but the way he is able help ourselves.

“In open play last season he was the full-back in this league with most assists and this season is probably one of the full-backs with the most assists.

“He’s a top player and a great professional. He’s a proper athlete and is improving in some defensive positions we want him to improve.

“He’s improving season by season, and week by week you can see he is improving, and we are really proud to have him with us.”

Robinson battled with some injury issues last season for the Cottagers, but still managed to make 27 appearances in the Premier League. This season he has started nine of their 10 games, showing his availability and quality under Alvaro Arbeloa.

Pochettino will know the 29-year-old well from their time together with the national team, and he will know that he has all the attributes to be able to perform in the way that he needs him to. He's also Premier League-proven, and has shown that he knows how to be effective in the final third too, having registered 10 assists in the 2024/25 season.

His current deal at Craven Cottage is due to expire in June 2028, and while they won't want to sell a key player in the middle of the season, a decent offer could be enough to tempt them. It's also likely that Robinson himself would be open to making the move to a bigger club, and reuniting with a manager who knows him well.

It's a deal that nobody seems to be talking about as an option at the moment. But it's one that would make a lot of sense for Pochettino and the team, and would also fit in with the way INEOS likes to move for Premier League-proven stars over talent from foreign leagues.
