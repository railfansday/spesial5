15 Best Premier League Centre-Backs Right Now (2026/27)

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The art of defending, often overshadowed by the glitter of goals and assists, remains the backbone of every successful football side. No team has been able to dominate their respective league, or even individual competitions, without having a world-class back-line. Over the years, the Premier League has been home to some of the best centre-backs in world football. The likes of Rio Ferdinand, Tony Adams, John Terry, Vincent Kompany, and many, many, more spring to mind when thinking about whom the best of the best were.

The aforementioned quartet were arguably complete defenders, imperious in the air when required, solid in one-vs-one situations, and intelligent enough to position themselves in the right place to snuff out any danger. Today, modern-day centre-backs plying their trade in the English top-flight are meant to do all that and more, given the frantic pace of the league.

However, the current crop of defenders are truly some of the finest we've ever seen. Be it Liverpool's Virgil van Dijk, often labelled the best defender in recent memory, or Arsenal's William Saliba, crucial to Mikel Arteta's successful Premier League campaign during the 2025/26 season.

But who is the best of the best today? We've attempted to rank the best centre-backs in the league right now. We've considered each player's form from last season and the early weeks of the 2026/27 campaign, but have also factored in how good they have been historically too.

Most bookies had Brentford down as one of the favourites to be relegated last season following Bryan Mbeumo and Yoane Wissa's departures and Thomas Frank ending his historic reign at the Gtech. But many forgot that the Bees still possessed one of the Premier League's most reliable defenders in Nathan Collins, who has been a rock for new manager Keith Andrews.

There are many reasons the West London outfit remains one of the toughest teams to play against, and Collins is one of them. The Irish defender has stepped up for Andrews with inspired performances, using his tall frame to good effect. Reports linked Arsenal and Tottenham Hotspur with Collins last year, and such interest will only grow with each passing display from the Irishman.

A product of Hajduk Split's academy, he joined Tottenham before sealing a club-record £46 million move to Brighton in the summer of 2026. The 19-year-old Croatian towers over the competition at 6'4", defending on the front foot, and, unsurprisingly, offering a goalscoring threat from set-pieces. Vuskovic has anchored Brighton's backline during the 2026/27 season, helping them to disrupt the hierarchy of the traditional top six.

One of the most versatile defenders in the league, Ezri Konsa can now call himself both an England World Cup representative and an Arsenal player. The Englishman's calm demeanour at the centre of Unai Emery's defence earned him a major move to Arsenal over the 2026 summer, joining the champions for £55 million.

And despite having Gabriel Magalhaes and William Saliba to contend with, as well as the likes of Jurrien Timber and Ben White vying for places across the backline, Konsa has already bedded into Arteta's side.

Albeit injured for a fair chunk of his debut season in 2023, Micky van de Ven was a revolutionary signing under then-Spurs manager Ange Postecoglou. van de Ven’s incredible concoction of pace, power and high defensive IQ leant itself to both defensive obstinance and free-flowing attack.

An astute passer, too, there are not many flaws in the former Wolfsburg man’s skillset. The fastest man in the Premier League since records began, Roberto De Zerbi is building the backbone of his side around van de Ven and his compatriot van Hecke. After a tough few years for Tottenham, their fortunes seem to be turning at last. van de Ven's athleticism has been a huge part of the club's recent revival.

He proved to be one of the league's best left-backs, with his muscular frame at odds with his ability to support the attack. Gvardiol has been working well as both a left-back and a centre-back in a defensive three, such is his versatility.

The 24-year-old is already well past a century of appearances and could become one of the Cityzens' all-time greats when it's all said and done. Reliable even when City's form has dipped in recent years, Gvardiol is contributing at both ends too. Currently, he has 14 goals in all competitions for the club.
