When It Comes to GTA 6, Every Brand Suddenly Wants to Break the Rules

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

It’s funny how quickly brand safety becomes negotiable when the entire world is paying attention.

For decades, Grand Theft Auto has been everything advertisers avoided: violence, crime, sex, drugs, and an unapologetic disregard for authority. The franchise fueled gaming’s biggest moral controversies.

Now GTA 6 has arrived, and brands can’t get close enough.

Dozens of brands lined up to associate themselves with the release on Thursday (Nov. 19), projected to be the largest entertainment launch in history with estimated sales topping $2.3 billion.

From Popeyes creating a Spice City sandwich to Lenovo giving thousands of employees a floating “gaming” holiday to Twitch building technology that helps advertisers avoid the game’s more mature scenes, marketers were eager to find their way in.
