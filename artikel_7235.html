The Trade Desk, Facing First Revenue Decline Since 2020, Loses Three Senior Executives

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Three senior leaders at The Trade Desk are leaving the company this week. Their exits come on the heels of co-founder Dave Pickles returning to oversee its technology, and shortly after the company reported its first year-over-year revenue dip since the pandemic.

Waseem Basheer, svp of engineering, left the company today; Whitney Rifkin, svp of client service, and Bryce Poll, vp, global commercial strategy & operations, will leave Nov. 20, a Trade Desk spokesperson confirmed to ADWEEK.

“Several long-tenured leaders have decided to move on from The Trade Desk, and we’re grateful for everything they built here,” the spokesperson said in a statement.
