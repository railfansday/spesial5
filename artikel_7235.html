Riverside Insights: Sr. Recruiting Operations Specialist

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

At Riverside Insights, we help educators grow the potential in every student by discovering the unique strengths and challenges of each learner through research-backed assessments. Great people make that work possible, and this role is how we find them, bring them in, and set the teams that hire them up to succeed.

In this role, you will do two things. First, you will recruit. You will hire for our mid to lower level roles from intake through offer, and you will build sourcing pipelines in the areas where we hire often, so outreach to strong candidates starts within 24 hours of a role opening.

Second, you will run recruiting operations. You will own our applicant tracking system and workflows, use AI agents to take manual steps out of hiring, and help hiring managers and interview teams prepare to hire well.

You will also support our other in-house recruiter by taking on parts of recruiting operations for our more senior level hires. Hiring managers will know you as the person to go to for process and recruiting operations help.

The pace is fast and the team values people who ask good questions, learn quickly, think ahead, and change course when the situation calls for it.

This role reports to the Head of Talent & Culture.

·       2+ years of experience as a recruiter, filling roles from intake through offer.

·       Deep experience with LinkedIn Recruiter, including building out sourcing projects and outreach campaigns.

·       Fluency with AI tools and a strong grasp of systems and workflows,

·       Exceptional attention to detail, and the rigor to pursue goals and follow through until they are met.

·       Clear, confident communication with hiring managers, candidates, and leaders.

·       Experience partnering with hiring managers on interview guides, or other tools that make hiring decisions better.

·       Curiosity and speed: you ask good questions, learn fast, and adjust quickly when priorities change.

·       Travel 1–2 times a year for off-site team meetings or conference.

·       May require stationary positions (sitting or standing) for extended periods.Disclaimer
