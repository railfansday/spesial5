Hooded Horse's latest tactics RPG is a sequel to Symphony of War, one of 2022's undersung favourites

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Symphony of War II: Dominion promises a "deeper, more ambitious strategy RPG"

Dancing Dragons Games and Hooded Horse have announced Symphony of War II: Dominion, the sequel to 2022's Symphony of War: The Nephilim Saga. Sin noted the original as pretty special for being "a good strategy game", highlighting the unique importance of taking care of each and every army member, rather than just benching the ones that are less effective and/or pretty to look at. The Nephilism Saga is also somewhat rare in being a turn-based tactics game made in RPG Maker, as seems to be the case with its sequel, which is expanding to include naval combat.

Symphony of War II: Dominion Announcement Trailer | Strategy RPG Watch on YouTube

Dominion's premise mirrors its predecessor in setting a band of heroes from a wide variety of classes, with units ranging from heavy knights and light archers to cannon-lighting artillery and even dragons the size of large dogs; I do have to wonder if an actual hooded horse is one of the units. Over 100 different classes are available, with the core of the game being swapping out these units to create the most effective squads of units for each turn-based battle. As before, it's not just a matter of recruiting soldiers and never thinking about them again until their turn arrives; a cared for army is a strong army (Sun Tzu said that, probably), and nurturing your army with new gear, individual perks and specialisation to make the squad work not only on their own, but as a collective.

Phil Hamilton, founder of Dancing Dragon Games, elaborated that "With Symphony of War: Dominion, we’re building on what players loved about the first game while taking the series in a direction we’ve wanted to explore for years. We’re delivering a deeper, more ambitious strategy RPG without sacrificing the feel and flow that made the original special." Symphony of War II: Dominion releases sometime next year, so for now, get thinking about how you'll best take care of your squads. To paraphrase Sun Tzu again, keeping them fed is always a good start.
