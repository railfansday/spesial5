GTA 6 Online rumors say youll need to complete the game to play. Dont believe them.

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Grand Theft Auto VI has been out for less than a week, but there are already a whole host of tips, tricks and memes out there.

Rumors, too. One that we've seen repeated and reshared recently is a rumor about GTA 6's online multiplayer version, and the criteria players will supposedly need to meet in order to play it.

"Rockstar Games has reportedly confirmed that players will be required to achieve 100% single-player story completion—including all stranger missions and collectibles—before being granted access to 'GTA 6 Online' servers at launch," posted Chaos Crave on X. At the time of writing that's had around 1,400 shares, and we've seen the claim being repeated by other accounts.

The problem? It's not true. As the Community Note attached to the post points out, Chaos Crave (per its own bio) is a "Satirical News account".

Rockstar Games has confirmed no such thing — and in fact, we don't even know when an online GTA 6 will be available, let alone any other details.

Will an online mode for GTA 6 be coming at some point? Probably, given how much money it makes for Rockstar. But for now, we'll just have to keep exploring the single player world.
