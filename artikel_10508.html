GTA 6 players are devasted by train ‘nerf’ after spending years trying to stop it in GTA 5

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

GTA 6 players have found how to stop the trains circulating Leonida, and it’s actually a lot easier than they expected it to be.

In GTA 5, players spent years trying to bring the train to a halt. They tried everything from blasting it with explosives to parking huge vehicles and planes in its way.

However, no matter how hard they tried, it would always keep moving forward, laying waste to whoever or whatever was trying to block its path.

Now that they’ve been let loose in GTA 6, many tried to do the same once again, but they quickly found it’s nowhere near the challenge it once was.

In GTA 6, trains behave much more realistically. If you stand on the tracks or block them with a vehicle and give the driver enough time to spot you, they will bring the locomotive to a halt until you move out of the way.

You can Finally Derail a Moving Train in GTA 6 pic.twitter.com/PQItm03f7e

As you might expect, others have found more dramatic ways to slow it down, such as crashing a dump truck into the front of them. In fact, if you hit one with enough force at the right angle, they can be derailed completely.

Given how much GTA 6 steps up the realism, it’s probably no surprise that its trains aren’t unstoppable forces of nature. However, while some fans like this approach, others miss the challenge of trying to get a train to stop.

They nerferd the boss pic.twitter.com/jK8EihWCX6

“The nerfed the boss,” wrote one player on X, before adding: “I like the aura farming of GTA 5 train more.”

“THE TRAIN LOST IT’S ENTIRE AURA IN GTA 6,” wrote another.

“Honestly, it feels like they completely stripped the train of any character—GTA 6 visuals are stunning, but the vibe is just missing here,” said a third.

GTA 6’s map is packed full of activities that will no doubt keep players occupied for months, but stopping the train is no longer the puzzle it once was.
