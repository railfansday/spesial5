Janji’s New Winter Collection Has All the Gear You Need for Cold-Weather Running

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The lineup includes jackets, vests, fleece tights, and more.

We earn a commission for products purchased through some links in this article. Why Trust Us?

Two types of winter runners exist: those who brave the rain, snow, and cold, and those who head for the treadmill until spring. If you fall into that former camp, now’s the time to stock up on crucial winter running gear, and Janji’s newly released Winter 2026 collection has just about everything you need for cold-weather running.

This is a hefty drop, including men’s, women’s, and unisex gear to cover you from head to toe. Here are some highlights, and you can shop the full collection at Janji.

One of our favorite layers for wintertime running, the Thermalrunner offers a nearly perfect combo of warmth and breathability. It's made with a durable, wind-resistant nylon shell, and its PrimaLoft Gold Active synthetic insulation sits in strategic panels to keep you warm in key areas while letting you dump heat as you move. It also includes three zippered pockets and packs down into the chest pocket for easy storage.

Designed for low weight, breathability, and warmth, this streamlined hoodie is a great pick for blustery weather. The recycled polyester mesh fabric has open pores to vent sweat vapor and excess heat, and it also dries quickly, so sweat won’t leave you soaked and freezing. Inside, the fabric is brushed for a cozy feel that takes the edge off cold temperatures. It’s available as a men’s jacket and as a women’s vest, too.

When winter brings its worst, reach for these pants. A totally new addition to Janji’s lineup, the Thermalrunner Hybrid Insulated Jogger packs serious heat with its panels of synthetic insulation across the front and seat and an interior fleece lining. On the outside, the smooth nylon face fabric resists moisture and doesn’t “swish,” according to Janji, and an external drawcord waist ensures a slip-free fit, even when you load up the pants’ three zippered pockets.

The Winter Collection includes several pairs of gloves and a cozy hat, but I think this balaclava wins for its versatility. You can wear it as a neck gaiter, as a hat, or as a full balaclava-style head covering to power through arctic conditions. No matter how you wear it, its nylon-merino-elastane blend delivers breathable warmth and a snug, comfortable fit.
