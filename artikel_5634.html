Hyundai IONIQ 3 Crowned Small Car of the Year at the TopGear.com Awards 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

SEOUL/OFFENBACH — Hyundai Motor’s all-electric IONIQ 3 has been named Small Car of the Year at the TopGear.com Awards 2027. The award, decided by the editorial team behind BBC Top Gear magazine and TopGear.com, recognises the best new cars launched over the past 12 months.

The accolade follows Top Gear’s first full review of IONIQ 3, which highlighted its keen pricing, clever packaging, distinctive design and large boot. The verdict was: “The value for money on offer here is nothing short of exceptional.”

Unlike other small EVs, the IONIQ 3 makes a strong case for itself as your only car, not a tender to the big family SUV. For the money nothing can compete with the range, the space and the efficiency. —Ollie Kew, Deputy Editor of Top Gear

With IONIQ 3, we set out to bring the distinctive design and advanced technology of the IONIQ line-up to a more accessible price point while addressing the everyday needs of European customers. This award demonstrates that electric mobility can combine affordability, distinctive design and everyday practicality without compromising on range or efficiency. It is a great recognition of the work that has gone into developing IONIQ 3. —Christopher McKinnon, Vice President, Head of Product and Product Director Office for Europe at Hyundai Motor Europe

IONIQ 3 was designed and developed specifically for European customers and is produced at Hyundai Motor Türkiye’s İzmit plant.

Top Gear’s editorial team highlighted the combination of value for money, distinctive design, interior space and driving efficiency. Despite its compact exterior dimensions, IONIQ 3 offers the range and practicality to serve as a customer’s primary vehicle.

The recognition continues Hyundai Motor’s success at the TopGear.com Awards. INSTER was named Supermini of the Year at the 2026 awards, while IONIQ 9 received the Best Seven-Seat EV title at the 2025 TopGear.com Electric Awards.

IONIQ 3 has also recently been selected as one of seven finalists for European Car of the Year 2027. The winner will be announced on 8 January 2027 at the Brussels Motor Show.

The annual TopGear.com Awards recognise new vehicles launched over the previous 12 months across a range of categories. Selected by the editorial team behind BBC Top Gear magazine and TopGear.com, the awards celebrate automotive excellence in areas including performance, innovation and driving appeal. In addition to its annual awards, TopGear.com hosts dedicated Electric Awards recognising achievements in electric mobility.
