Arsenal legend and double winner Peter Storey dies aged 81

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Tributes are pouring in for Arsenal legend Peter Storey, who has passed away at the age of 81.

The Surrey-born midfielder, who retired with 19 England caps, first joined Arsenal as an apprentice in 1961 and went on to play a vital role in the side which won a league and FA Cup double in 1971.

He scored a stoppage-time penalty against Stoke in the semi-finals to force a replay and the Gunners subsequently went on to lift the trophy. He also lifted the Fairs Cup with the club.

Initially a full-back, he was later moved into midfield, where he spent the remainder of his career and gained a reputation as a crunching tackler.

Storey made 501 appearances in total for Arsenal and is their eighth all-time highest appearance maker. He left north London in 1977 to sign with Fulham before retiring in 1978.

Arsenal confirmed that the club will pay their respects at their Champions League match against Borussia Dortmund, which takes place on November 24.

A club statement read: “Everyone at Arsenal is deeply saddened by the passing of 1970-71 Double Winner, Peter Storey.

“All our thoughts are with his family and loved ones at this incredibly sad time. Rest in peace, Peter.”

Storey’s family added in a statement: "We are deeply saddened by the loss of Peter, a much-loved father and grandfather. He is survived by his three sons and grandchildren, who will miss him dearly. We are deeply grateful for the many kind messages, tributes and good wishes we have received.

“It has been a great comfort to see the affection and respect in which he was held by so many, both within the football community and beyond."

England also paid tribute on their official X account, adding: “We are saddened to learn that Peter Storey – who won 19 caps for the Three Lions in the 1970s – has passed away at the age of 81.

“Our condolences are with Peter’s family and friends at this time.”

Storey hung up his boots when his Fulham contract was cancelled, just eight months after he had signed. He went on to own a north London pub.

He was convicted of a number of criminal offences after retiring from football, including smuggling pornography, keeping a brothel, and financing the production of counterfeit coins, as detailed in his 2010 autobiography ‘True Storey: My Life and Crimes as a Football Hatchet Man’.
