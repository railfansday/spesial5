Brighton’s Conference League qualification path explained - How many points are needed after Craiova win?

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Brighton still require six more points to guarantee their place in the last 16 of the Uefa Conference League following Thursday night's impressive 4-0 victory over Romanian league leaders Craiova.

Fabian Hurzeler's side are currently second in the league phase table - behind leaders Monaco - with 10 points from their opening four matches, putting them in a strong position to secure automatic qualification for the knockout stages.

The top eight teams qualify directly for the last 16, while those finishing between ninth and 24th must negotiate an additional knockout play-off round.

Brighton have two league-phase fixtures remaining, starting with a home meeting against leaders Monaco on December 10 before travelling to face Panathinaikos on December 17.

Winning both matches would take the Seagulls to 16 points and guarantee a top-eight finish, regardless of results elsewhere.

Four points from the remaining games would leave them on 14 and extremely well placed, although mathematical qualification would not be certain.

Three points would see them to 13 points and would still have a strong chance, depending on other results.

Hurzeler will be particularly encouraged by Brighton's defensive record, with the Seagulls yet to concede a goal in their four Conference League matches.

The Albion have scored nine times and will now look to finish the league phase strongly and avoid the extra play-off round in February.

Head coach Fabian Hurzeler was delighted with the performance against Craiova

The German said: “I think that was a really good start, and also very important to make the game look easy, because especially the first goal helped us force [the opponent] to open up the space,” he said.

“That meant we could have more space to combine and more opportunity to attack their box. So I think we were very professional with good standards in the first half. Then, of course, the red card definitely helped us to finish - or to change - the structure of the game.

“In the second half we created plenty of chances. I wasn't happy that we weren't clinical and clean enough finishing these chances, because I think we dropped our level a little bit there. Overall, though, winning 4–0 means that we did a lot of things well.”

Here are the key dates for the 2026/27 UEFA Conference League knockout stages, should Brighton progress:

- Knockout play-off round: February 18 and 25, 2027.

- Final: June 2, 2027 – Beşiktaş Stadium, Istanbul, Turkey.
