GTA 6 Players Are Obsessed With Finding IRL Florida in Vice City

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Now that Grand Theft Auto 6 is in the wild, one thing has become clear: The open-world crime stuff is secondary. This thing's a life sim. GTA 6's life sim bona fides are so undeniable that, within the first two days of its release, a not-insignificant swath of players have eschewed the elaborate robberies and high-tension shootouts to instead embrace the ancient gaming tradition of "finding IRL locations and Leo memeing at them."

Players of reality-inspired open-world games have always done this, whether it's finding the Bean in Watch Dogs' version of Chicago or visiting the John Wick hotel in DLC for The Division 2. But with GTA 6, which takes place in a satirized version of Florida called Leonida, players were always going to take it to another level. This game is from Rockstar Games, a studio so well-known for its attention to detail it once infamously implemented horse testicles that shrink in reaction to cold weather. GTA 6 has been in development for a decade. Its leads have conducted "research" trips to Miami-Dade County for just as long. A perfect storm for some ridiculously faithful world design that's resulted in brain-melting levels of realism.

GTA 6's realistic visuals have changed the way I play

What's most remarkable is that Rockstar's attention to detail isn't just limited to reimagining iconic Miami landmarks like the Adrienne Arsht Center or the E11EVEN nightclub, though the game does have those. It's also found in recreations of nondescript buildings you'd otherwise never think twice about. This design ethos is best illustrated in one viral clip in which an unidentified creator films Jason walking through an apartment complex before coming to a stop on a patio overlooking a South Beach meridian. The creator, continuing to film, stands up in real life, then walks out of their own front door and onto a patio of the same exact building. ("This is crazy [exploding head emoji]," they wrote. "My apartment is in a video game.")

Even in the realm of sports, Rockstar wasn't just drawn to the major leagues. Yes, GTA 6 includes a riff on Miami's Kaseya Cener (homebase of the Miami Heat), but it also includes a pixel-perfect facsimile of the Miami Marine Stadium. Built in the early '60s as a regatta venue, the stadium went on to host film shoots, presidential rallies, and concerts from the likes of Queen, The Who, Jimmy Buffet, and other megawatt stars, according to the nonprofit Restore Miami Marine Stadium. Then, in 1992, Hurricane Andrew took the venue out of commission. It's spent the past three decades collecting rust and graffiti. But in GTA 6, it's just...there. (In August of this year, Miami voters passed a referendum that paves the way for a potential restoration.)

Fans have been unpacking these references throughout the entirety of GTA 6's marketing cycle. Following Rockstar's release of the second GTA 6 trailer, back in May 2025, I could've sworn — based on a vanishingly brief 0.5-second glance — that the South Beach party spot The Clevelander would serve as a location in the game. The bar's marketing department never responded to my initial request for comment. Now that I've played, I can confirm that, yes, it's absolutely in the game, in all its flamingo-pink glory. The Clevelander is somewhat of an institution, having remained a South Beach party spot for decades on the back of some extremely lucky local ordinances, but at face value, it's not all that distinct from any other South Beach bar: neon lights, a questionable pool, overpriced drinks. And yet, it's in GTA 6 too.

The locations GTA 6 players seem drawn to point out tend to be in the Miami metropolitan area, which makes sense: It's the population nexus of the state. But Vice City encompasses a small portion of Leonida's map, and Florida itself poses no shortage of things to reference beyond the Miami-Dade County limits. Who knows what players will find and Leo meme at? Though there's one thing not worth seeking out. In its quest to construct an alternate reality, Rockstar neglected to include arguably the most Florida landmark of them: Disney World.
