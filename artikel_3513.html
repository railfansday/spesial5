20 of the Coolest Tech Stocking Stuffers for Under $50

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

This series will help you pick the perfect presents, decor, and activities to make this Christmas the Best Christmas Ever.

What if you make this the year you don't fill all the stockings with candy or gift cards? Both have their place, but if you want to make this the best Christmas ever, you have to shove some tech in those stockings. The good news: Tech gifts don’t have to be expensive to be impressive.

No matter who you're shopping for, there are tons of affordable gizmos that are some combination of cool and useful and that feel pricier than they are. Here are 20 cool tech stocking stuffers to light up any holiday morning, all of which cost less than $50.

If there's an old-school gamer on your list, their Christmas morning will be extra merry if it includes a tiny video game emulator. R36T Retro Gaming Console ($48.98) puts over 18,000 video games in the palm of your hand. It has a 3.5" screen, so whether they like arcade classics or consoles-of-the-past, they can fit 'em all in a pocket.

The Xteink X4 is an e-reader that's smaller than your phone. It uses e-ink like an Amazon Kindle, so you can whip a glare-free screen out of your pocket and start reading anywhere there's a light source. The X4 is a no-frills gadget—there's no lighting, touchscreen, or apps—but if you want a cheap, convenient way to read for around $50, it's perfect.

Those disposable cans of compressed air with the red straws are so 2015. Blow the dust off your electronics with an SDFM Electric Air Duster instead. With an engine that moves at 150,000 RPM, it shoots air such high speeds, the dust doesn't stand a chance. It's also cordless and comes with a variety of cleaning attachments to keep your gear in top shape.

If your giftee likes to listen to music or podcasts while drifting off to sleep, the only gift better than a pair of sleep headphones is two pairs of sleep headphones! This two-pack retails for $22.99 and contains two headbands with built in speakers you can pair with your phone or other device easily.

Your giftee may have the tech side of their battle station/home office locked down, but I bet they don't have the aesthetics dialed in. Govee Smart LED Light Bars ($54.99, but currently on sale for $34.99) are programmable, colorful light bars that work with Alexa and Google Assistant and can display light effects created from 16 millions colors. It even syncs with your music to provide trippy visuals.

My lifestyle doesn't involve cutting down trees, but I still need one of these miniature chainsaws. I mean, it's a chainsaw, and it's only six inches long. This Seeii mini chainsaw ($49.99) is battery-powered, so no one will be accidentally cutting the cord. According to the listing, it's suitable for tree pruning, garden artistry, shrub trimming, forest logging, and wood cutting.

You know what computer fiends are missing in their lives? Knobs. This little $33 gadget lets you assign functions to four external knobs and 12 push buttons, so you can finally have a decent volume control, scrub through video smoothly, skip music tracks with a button push, and control any other function easily and physically. Perfect for gamers.

You could use a tape measure like a caveman, or you could measure things with a frickin' laser that fits in your pocket. This $40 gadget has an LED screen that's easy to read and it doubles as a cat toy. But does it measure things? Damn right it measures things—big things, little things, middle-sized things—it doesn't care what you put in front of it, it will measure the hell out of it! (Up to 98 feet, anyway.)

This compact Bluetooth speaker has an Amazon rating of 4.6 stars out of 5, and it's cheap—just $45. It's waterproof, it sounds great, and it features a built-in light show with six different settings so you can check out cool visuals with your tunes.

A single Apple Airtag makes a great gift to accompany the main gift you don't want someone to lose. Or they can put it on their wallet or your keys, and if they misplace them, they can hit a button and it will play a sound while the FindMy app displays a map that leads to the missing item. Plus, it works in reverse, so you can hit a tag and it will ring your lost phone.

If you want to give the gift of warmth for the holidays, you could do a lot worse than this portable neck, shoulder, and back warmer. There are three temperature settings, a safety shut-off feature, and it heats up really fast, for only $33.

This tiny little vacuum from Odistar will keep their desktop dust-free. It sucks with 1000Pa of suction power for 400 minutes off a single charge, and it's very easy to use: You put it in the palm of your hand and push it around. Cool!

It's not like people need an endoscope camera every day, but if the need for a tiny camera does arise—if they need to see why the engine isn't working or if their wedding ring falls behind the oven—they'll be glad to have this Anykit USB Endoscope Camera. It plugs right into an Android or iPhone and captures HD photos and video of places the sun don't shine.

If your giftee is carrying their laptop around to coffeehouses like a chump, this is the perfect present. They can connect a foldable mini Bluetooth keyboard from iClever to their phone, and turn it into an ultra-portable productivity device. It even has a little trackpad!

This skinny extra battery charges a phone magnetically or with a USB-C cable. It's fast, too. It can charge an iPhone 16 Pro Max to 37% in 30 minutes via the USB-C port and it's less than three inches wide.

Projectors have gotten really good and really small over the last few years, and they prices have come down massively too. This mini projector costs only $29 and projects a 720p image up to 120" and it weighs only about two pounds.

Most people already have a media streamer attached to their televisions, but in case you're gifting someone who doesn't, the Roku Streaming Stick 4K is a solid choice in the $50 price range. This no-frills device serves up 4K HD video, gives you access to hundreds of free and premium channels, and is easy to setup.

The Twelve South AirFly SE is an ultra-compact Bluetooth transmitter lets you plug into any device with a 3.5 mm jack, like in-flight entertainment systems, gym machines, or TVs, and stream audio wirelessly to your Bluetooth earbuds or headphones. Leave those accursed wired headphone in the drawer where they belong!

This is an essential gift if there's a cyclist in your life and anyone who wants to maintain proper PSI in their car tires. This portable air compressor weighs less than a pound and is a steal at only $40. It's rechargeable, has an onboard pressure gauge, an LED flashlight, and can inflate a car tire in only eight minutes.

I am 100% sure anyone you give this to will be able to use it. This compact, high‑power Anker charger has two USB‑C ports plus one USB‑A, letting you power up your laptop, phone, and tablet all at once, from one plug. Plus it charges fast.
