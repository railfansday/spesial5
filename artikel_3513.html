Chris Brown & Usher Share the Stage Together at Atlanta Concert After Night 1 Drama

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Chris Brown and Usher seemingly put their drama behind them and shared the stage together last night at their Atlanta show ... albeit from afar.

In a clip shared on social media ... the two singers performed "Back to Sleep" from platforms on opposite ends of the stage.

However, it's worth noting that this staging seems to be consistent with what they've performed all tour ... at least for this hit.

The two apparently also performed their remix of "It Depends" last night ... so it seems they've made up.

We reported earlier ... Usher and Chris had a heated argument backstage during their first Atlanta performance as part of The R&B Tour.

With traffic delaying fans, the two musicians debated how to handle the situation -- with Usher wanting to go ahead with the performance and Chris wanting to wait.

Sources told us that the two have been extremely competitive with each other during their tour ... and that it boiled over on Thursday night because of their "egos."

As you may know ... the two have gotten into it before. At Chris' birthday party in 2023, Usher came to Teyana Taylor's defense after Chris was yelling at her.
