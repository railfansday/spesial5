Jeremiah and Hannah Duggar Reveal 4th Baby’s Name 3 Months After Birth

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Jeremiah and Hannah Duggar have revealed the name of their fourth baby, Sterling, three months after the little one’s arrival.

“So thankful for our little Brynley, Brielle, Emery and Sterling 🤍,” the couple captioned a joint Instagram post on Thursday, November 26, alongside a black-and-white photo of their family sitting on a porch swing and looking down at Sterling in Hannah’s arms.

In a comment, Hannah, 31, also clarified the baby’s sex.

“Three girls and a boy. 🫶🏼,” she wrote in response to a fan.

Hannah and Jeremiah, 27, announced their son’s birth in August.

“The sweetest moments we’ve ever known. 🥹,” Hannah wrote via Instagram at the time, sharing photos of their three daughters interacting with the newborn. “Life feels busier than ever, yet we find ourselves trying to slow down to just soak in these days with our littles. And we wouldn’t have it any other way! 🩵.”

Hannah and Jeremiah had not previously announced that they were expecting their fourth baby.

The couple began courting in October 2021 and tied the knot in March 2022.

“Today was a perfect day, as we stood before our family and friends and committed our lives to each other,” Jeremiah and Hannah told Us at the time of their Nebraska wedding. “It was such a beautiful culmination of so many prayers, dreams & desires! As we’ve seen God’s gracious hand in bringing our lives together, we are excited to see how He will continue to direct our paths.”

Hannah and Jeremiah later welcomed Brynley on Christmas Day in 2022, followed by Brielle in February 2024 and Emery in May 2025.

A few months before welcoming Sterling, the pair celebrated Emery’s 1st birthday.

“I tried not to blink, but somehow a year has gone by and our little baby is 1!” Hannah wrote via Instagram in May. “The way you crinkle your nose and give the cutest grin is like the brightest sunshine and keeps all of us smiling right back at you. You are such a little mover and we absolutely adore having you as part of our family. Happy first birthday to our sparkly little Emery!!”

Jeremiah is one of Jim Bob and Michelle Duggar’s 19 children. The family rose to fame on their TLC show 19 Kids and Counting and its spinoff, Counting On.

Jim Bob, 61, and Michelle, 60, now have more than 40 grandchildren from 11 of their kids.

Two of Jeremiah’s siblings are currently expecting additions to their families. In September, Joy-Anna Duggar and husband Austin Forsyth announced that they would be welcoming their fourth baby. Weeks later, Jessa Duggar announced that she is pregnant with her and husband Ben Seewald’s seventh child.
