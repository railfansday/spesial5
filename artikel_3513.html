Forget 'Widow's Bay,' Hulu’s New Mystery Series Is a Streaming Hit

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

In the Apple TV smash-hit mystery thriller Widow's Bay, a mayor is caught in mysterious happenings in his quaint Island town, where the intoxicating allure of the unknown keeps audiences on the edge of their seats. Matthew Rhys plays Mayor Tom Loftis, who is not well regarded by his people, and some would consider him a coward. The show's ultimate strength lies in its central mystery about the island, which fuels an entire season of a peculiar narrative that has earned praise from critics and viewers. Widow's Bay has broken records for Apple TV, becoming the year's most popular show. But a few weeks ago, Hulu also released a new genre-bending thriller that has taken the streamer by storm.

This show doesn't lean on the supernatural as much as Widow's Bay, but is just as weird. It focuses on a high school student, Sonny (Sam Nivola), who gets into a car accident. But when he recovers, he realizes something quite odd: his mother has been replaced by an imposter. The problem is that no one believes him, and Sonny thinks he might be in danger. From the absurd humor of being a teenager to the improbable task of investigating a mother imposter, the show has a unique kind of humor that would give Widow's Bay a run for its money.

Critically, Phony has also performed well, with a 93% critics' score and a 91% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes. Collider's David Caballero was impressed, lauding it for having "a solid mystery grounded in a charming mother-son relationship." ScreenRant's Grant Hermanns praised the show for having "a very unique tone [that] proves immediately absorbing before it even gets into the real core of its mystery." While many people don't like a show that they cannot categorize, The Hollywood Reporter's Daniel Fienberg appreciated Phony for "keeping its thematic underpinnings subtle and unarticulated." According to FlixPatrol's rankings, Phony has been performing decently well on Hulu, regularly appearing on the streamer's domestic top ten most-watched shows.

Nick Paley created the series, wrote the first two episodes, and directed the first half of Season 1. Anthony King serves as the showrunner. The series brings together two stars from different seasons of The White Lotus: Nivola plays the main character, and Connie Britton (from The White Lotus Season 1) plays Sonny's mother, Ellen. The actress also plays the impostor with some makeup. Other cast members include Mahaela Park (Chloe), Meera Syal (Sharla), Maliq Johnson (Vogel), Grace Brennan (Mallory), and Colter Lee (Weldon).

Stream new episodes of Phony on Hulu in the U.S. on Tuesdays and stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
