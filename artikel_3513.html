Apple Store app now optimized for iPhone Duo

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Apple is rolling out an update to the Apple Store app today that adds support for iPhone Duo. The app now takes full advantage of the iPhone Duo’s inner and outer displays in all orientations.

Yesterday, Apple revealed that more than 200,000 apps are now optimized for iPhone Duo on the App Store. That news came just ten days after the company announced that it had crossed the 100,000 milestone.

The Apple Store app was an odd omission from that category, but that’s now changed. The update comes as the holiday shopping season nears. Apple will likely announce its Black Friday promotion here in the next few days as well.

The update also adds a unified tab bar at the bottom, as well as a slightly different icon, as spotted by Everything-Apple.
