Meghan Markle's Dad Faces Child Abuse Charges in Philippines After Arrest

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Meghan Markle’s estranged father, Thomas Markle Sr., is facing child abuse charges in the Philippines.

Thomas Sr., 82, has been charged with two counts of offering, distributing, selling, exporting or importing any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation material and one count of possessing such material, according to ITV.

Meghan’s half-brother, Thomas Markle Jr., alleged in October that he found storage boxes containing suspected child sexual abuse material (CSAM) at his father’s home in the Philippines. Thomas Jr. told The Sun at the time he turned over the material to local authorities. Thomas Sr. denied the allegations, calling them “sick.”

Earlier this month, Thomas Sr. was arrested in Florida on a federal charge of possession of child pornography. He was released on bond the same day.

The Central District of California U.S. Attorney’s Office claimed at the time that investigators found material depicting child sexual abuse in two storage units registered to Thomas Sr. in Los Angeles. The material allegedly contains photographs of a man believed to be Thomas Sr. with a young nude girl.

“The FBI and our partners have arrested Thomas Markle Sr. after an FBI investigation revealed he was in possession of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM),” FBI Director Kash Patel claimed in a statement.

Patel continued, “Mr. Markle allegedly targeted innocent children and sought to steal their innocence through his criminal actions. Working with our Justice Department partners, our agents moved quickly, identified and located the explicit material, and arrested Mr. Markle in Florida. This FBI will use every single resource at our disposal to protect innocent kids — any predator who targets children will have zero safe haven in this country or anywhere they try to hide.”

Thomas Sr. was arrested again earlier this month after he cut off his ankle monitor.

“At a hearing today in Tampa, U.S. Magistrate Judge Lindsay Saxe Griffin of the Middle District of Florida ordered Thomas Wayne Markle detained (jailed without bond),” a spokesperson for the Department of Justice confirmed to Us in a statement. “Markle cut off his ankle bracelet at 1:45 a.m. EST today. Federal law enforcement arrested him later today. Markle is in federal custody.”

Meghan, for her part, revealed in a statement last month that she initially learned about the allegations against Thomas Sr. in 2018. (The pair have been estranged since her wedding to Prince Harry that same year.)

“In 2018, a letter was sent to the Metropolitan Police and Royalty and Specialist Protection. That letter was subsequently shared with me. It made troubling and disturbing allegations against my father and I was given assurances that these were being investigated,” Meghan said.

She continued, “I hope it can now be understood that, despite certain media publications’ distortion over the years, any distance from my father and my choice to not allow him to have contact with our children was for one reason: to protect them. I trusted that the authorities were doing the same for other families.”
