Lady A’s Charles Kelley Shares Update on His Blood Cancer Treatment Plan

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Lady A’s Charles Kelley is feeling optimistic about his health as he approaches Thanksgiving.

“Hey y’all, just got back from my eighth treatment, and things are looking good,” Kelley, 45, shared via his Instagram Story on Tuesday, November 24. “The numbers are going the right way. I had a conversation about next steps and at some point, the conversation will be about stem cell transplant, and that’s pretty intense.”

The country singer continued, “You kind of just go in and kill out everything in your body and replace it with healthy stem cells that you harvest from your own stem cells. It’s quite the process, but it gives you the best chance of a long remission, and I think that’s what I want.”

In September, Kelley first revealed he was diagnosed with a blood cancer called multiple myeloma.

According to the Mayo Clinic, multiple myeloma forms in plasma cells, a type of white blood cell. Treatment can help reduce complications and ease symptoms.

“I want to be able to get this thing and put it to the side for, heck, maybe five or 10 years of remission,” Kelley said in his latest social media update. “Maybe forever and throw something else at it down the road.”

Since sharing his diagnosis, the colead vocalist of the Grammy-winning trio has shared updates with his fans and followers about his health. In October, Kelley expressed gratitude for the strangers who came forward to share their survival stories.

That gratitude was shared once again in his latest video before the Thanksgiving holiday.

“Just want to say thank you for everybody thinking of me and praying for me,” he said. “We’re doing good, man. I think each week gets a little more normal for me, and I’m just able to process it and still live a good, normal life. Y’all have a great Thanksgiving.”

A scary diagnosis hasn’t stopped Kelley from doing what he loves in the music industry.

On November 18, Kelley joined Hillary Scott and Dave Haywood at the CMA Awards at Music City Center in Nashville.

“Our 20th #CMAawards together ❤️,” Lady A wrote via Instagram after walking the red carpet. “Feeling so blessed to be a part of such a magical night ✨.”

Kelley also teased that more new music is on the way when he shared a snippet of a new track with Brett Young.

“Drop it already,” Kelley teased via his Instagram Story on Wednesday, November 25. “Can’t wait for people to hear this banger we wrote.”
