Fantasy Basketball Weekend Streamers: Matisse Thybulle locking up on defense for Lakers

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Matisse Thybulle has a history of being an impactful defender, though injuries have kept him off the court a lot in recent years. While the defensive stats have still been there this season, it's his improvement on the offensive end that has been so impressive and a big reason why he should be rostered in fantasy basketball, even beyond this weekend.

He is averaging 2.3 steals, 1.0 block and 1.8 triples per game this season, and he has scored 14 points in back-to-back games. Thybulle has never been a high-volume scorer, but he hasn't been a liability on that end of the floor this year, which has allowed him to be on the floor to make more of an impact on the defensive end. He has been vital to the Lakers' success as a starter all season in what has been the best year of his career so far.

The Hornets, Bulls, Cavaliers, Mavericks, Rockets, Thunder, Kings and Wizards only play one game this weekend, so avoid those eight teams if you're looking to stream.

While the poster dunk on Victor Wembanyama was a ton of fun, Cissoko has been able to do way more than that for fantasy managers lately.

He has started the last nine games for Portland and has averaged 10.6 points, 3.7 rebounds, 1.0 steal and 2.2 triples per game during this stretch. It appears he has locked in a starting role until Ja Morant returns, which shouldn't be this weekend. Cissoko has had at least one steal in five straight games, and while he isn't a high-volume scorer, he should be a decent source of triples and swipes.

Even if he doesn't, Graves's game is fantasy friendly, as he showcased in his first start, when he had 20 points, 10 rebounds, three steals, one block and two three-pointers. That makes him worth taking a chance on.

His last game was rough, but Wolf has largely been productive as a starter for Brooklyn, which is a role he seems to have locked up. In seven starts this season, Wolf is averaging 11.6 points, 5.7 rebounds, 2.1 assists and 2.3 three-pointers per game. Wolf wasn't in the rotation to start the year, but injuries opened the door for him, and he took advantage. His early-season development, along with Nolan Traore's emergence, gives the Nets some extra hope for their 2025 draft class.

Bassey has started the last four games for the Warriors at center and averaged 12.3 points, 9.3 rebounds, 1.3 steals and 1.3 blocks per game during this stretch. Steve Kerr has said he hopes Bassey remains a starter for the rest of the season, and while Kerr has a history of changing up his starting unit frequently, managers are hoping for some consistency here. For now, he should remain a starter through the weekend.
