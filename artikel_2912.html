Harold Fannin Jr. a limited participant

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Cleveland Browns TE Harold Fannin Jr. (groin) was limited during practice Wednesday, Nov. 11.

Why It Matters: Fannin seems to have a good chance to play in Week 10, but fantasy players will know more about his status over the next few days. He was able to pick up his fourth touchdown of the season last week but finished with only two receptions. He should be more involved in the passing game if he plays and can be considered a No. 1 fantasy tight end.
