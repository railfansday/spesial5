Preview: Utah Jazz Aim to Tame Kyrie and the Mavericks

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

On the heels of a perplexing report that Utah Jazz management were considering Kyrie Irving as a potential trade target, the man himself lands in Salt Lake City for a Sunday night showdown between the Mavericks and the Jazz. The Jazz are coming off an impressive LA swing, where they dispatched both the Clippers and the Lakers, and a less impressive home loss to the Phoenix Suns in the NBA Cup. The Mavericks are on the second game of a back to back, after beating the Clippers at home by 10 on Saturday night.

It will be interesting to see how Kyrie reacts to the attentions of a not-so-secret admirer, unlikely as a love match between the veteran guard and the Utah Jazz is. Will it motivate him to show the Ainges what they'll never have and put on a performance for the ages? (Let's hope not).

Of more interest to local fans is how Utah's young core will bounce back after a horrendous shooting night against the Suns. Ace, Keyonte and Darryn went a combined 14-48 from the floor, and a staggering 0-21 from 3, under the Suns' relentless pressure. Despite this, the rookie showed a lot of grit, clawing for rebounds and chasing down loose balls, and recording the first double double of his NBA career. It's a testament to the fight of the team as a whole that despite the poor shooting and being down by double digits most of the game, the Jazz got to the free throw line 34 times and closed within 4 late in the fourth quarter.

They'll need every bit of that tenacity to take down the Mavericks. Cooper Flagg is growing into his role as the face of the franchise, notching over 24 points, 6 rebounds and 6 assists a game. It will be interesting to see which Josh Coach Hardy deploys to try to contain the young phenom. Strategic double teams helped frustrate Luka in LA and this may help get the ball out of Cooper's hands.

In their first meeting in Dallas on November 6th, it was PJ Washington who hurt the Jazz with a team high 26 points. Washington sat out last night's game against the Clippers, dealing with illness and a shoulder injury, and may not be available Sunday night. The Mavericks will also be without Biberovic, de Larrea, Lively and Lawal.

Utah will be without Jaren Jackson Jr., who is sidelined until mid to late December with an MCL tear, and Lauri Markkanen, who will be nursing a fractured hand for another 3-4 weeks. The Jusuf Nurkic news is better: he has been cleared for on court activities and will be re-evaluated next week. Jaxson Hayes, who was almost perfect from the floor Friday night, is expected to play after taking a nasty fall in the fourth quarter against the Suns.

The game can be seen on KJZZ TV, Jazz+, KFAA TV and Mavs TV at 7pm EST (5pm MST) Sunday November 22nd.
