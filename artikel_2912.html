Daily Norseman Reacts Survey: Is It Time for a Change?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Welcome to SB Nation Reacts, a survey of fans across the NFL. Throughout the year, we ask questions of the most plugged-in Vikings fans and fans across the country.

It's time, ladies and gentlemen, for us to bring you our SB Nation Reacts questions for this week about our Minnesota Vikings and how we feel about what's going on with our favorite team. We've got a couple of questions for you for this week, so let's get to them.

Our first question, as always, is how you feel about the direction that the team is going in. For the first time since November of last year, we're asking this question following a Vikings loss because. . .well, the team finally found a situation where it couldn't overcome its lack of offense, and they fell to their division rivals from Detroit to lose their status as the NFL's last undefeated team. They're still on top of the NFC North, but now they have basically everybody breathing down their necks as they move on to face another tough opponent, with the Buffalo Bills coming to town for Monday Night Football.

The other question for this week is one that people have been asking for a little while now, too, and it concerns the quarterback situation. Kyler Murray has been. . .well, he's been bad. There's no point in trying to tap dance around it anymore. We're all watching the same games. The question now is whether or not the team should make a change at the quarterback position. If they do, what should that change be? The NFL trade deadline is a week from today, but is there a trade out there that can even, feasibly, help this team this season?

As always, we invite you to participate in this week's survey by answering the poll questions we've posed, whether it's below or over in the Feed, and by sounding off in the comments.

Have at it, ladies and gentlemen! We'll have the answers to these questions sometime between now and when the slate of games kicks off on Sunday.
