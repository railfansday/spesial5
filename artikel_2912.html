ORLEN, Carlyle sign preliminary agreement to explore energy investments

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

ORLEN and Carlyle signed a non-binding memorandum of understanding to explore joint energy investments in Poland, elsewhere in the region and internationally, the companies announced Thursday. The agreement was signed at the Partnership for Transatlantic Energy Cooperation ministerial meeting in Warsaw.
