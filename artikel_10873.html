Twitch streamer HAchubby reveals cancer diagnosis

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Twitch streamer HAchubby has revealed that she has been diagnosed with cancer, forcing her to cancel her plans for TwitchCon.

Kicking off her November 4 stream, HAchubby – a popular IRL streamer on Twitch – revealed that she was expecting some news from doctors after undergoing testing.

Almost two hours into the stream, after a brief pause, the streamer broke into tears, telling fans that she had been diagnosed with cancer They said that I have cancer,” HAchubby said, holding back further tears.

“They still don’t know what stage it is. They don’t know the details yet,” she added. The streamer later noted that her Korean translator told her that the doctors found “some aggressive things” while examining her.

“They don’t know, yet, the stage. I don’t know how bad it is. I feel pain, only in a specific part,” she explained a few hours later.

After her stream ended, HAchubby also took to X (formerly Twitter) to inform fans that she would have to skip TwitchCon. She had initially planned to still attend the event before heading home to Korea for treatment.

“I was diagnosed with cancer today. It seems like it may be close to spreading, so I’m planning to go to Korea right away for treatment,” she added.

“Sadly, I had to cancel my TwitchCon plans. Please don’t worry too much. I found a good doctor, and I’ll keep streaming from Korea, too.”
