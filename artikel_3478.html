‘Elsbeth’ Is Ready to Play a ‘Ted Lasso’ Star’s Game in First Look at This Week’s New Episode

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

After venturing into country club society and solving a murder on the green involving Nat Faxon, it's time for Elsbeth to head somewhere a little more fun. Season 4 has already been a busy one for the pink-clad sleuth, between not just solving murders but helping plan her son Teddy's (Ben Levi Ross) wedding. What better area in all of New York City, then, for a day of fun than the legendary amusement district that is Coney Island? Episode 5 of CBS' hit murder mystery procedural will take Carrie Preston to the park where business once again awaits her in the world of competitive eating with a Ted Lasso star.

Titled "All About Eats," the episode unfolds in the aftermath of a deadly donut-eating competition after which a Coney Island champion dies under mysterious circumstances. Enter Elsbeth to follow the crumbs and find out who's responsible for the gastronomical crime. She soon finds a suspect in Cody Baxter, an up-and-coming eating star and superfan of the sport played by Tim Robinson's Detroiters partner, Sam Richardson. It appears he may have gotten a little too hungry for the crown and bit off a little more than he could chew to knock off his top opponent, but to expose him, Elsbeth will have to play at his game.

Ahead of the premiere this Thursday, Collider can exclusively share a new sneak peek that sees Elsbeth running into Cody on the famed Coney Island boardwalk, where he works as an "independent contractor." He doesn't like being called a "carnie" because it gives the impression he's "weird," but he doesn't exactly do a great job of breaking the negative connotation in their awkward conversation.

After they exchange pleasantries, his demeanor immediately changes when Elsbeth mentions visiting his grandmother for a "very informative" chat. She assures Mimi only told them the nicest things about how helpful he was, including purchasing donuts for her, a line that Cody immediately tries to get away from by activating the carnival booth they're at. Elsbeth is definitely down to show off her skills with a water gun, though when she mentions her accuracy, it comes off more as a challenge to Cody that she will outfox him and expose his crimes.

Season 4 of Elsbeth has been just as star-studded as ever, with Richardson adding to a lineup that has thus far included Nicole Scherzinger, Nicholas Braun, the aforementioned Faxon, and even an appearance from New York Knicks star OG Anunoby. However, while the series still has more guests from its first round to get through, even more big names have recently signed on to join Robert and Michelle King's procedural. The Big Bang Theory star Simon Helberg will be one of the new suspects, playing a Revolutionary War reenactor whose spot is threatened by an up-and-comer. He'll be followed by SNL alum Molly Shannon, who plays a rising star creator of a female superhero, and Manchester by the Sea star Lucas Hedges as the founder of a prediction market. With them helping to fill out the rest of the season, Elsbeth is showing no signs of slowing down as the fall season winds into winter.
