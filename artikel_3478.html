These MacBooks, AirPods, iPads, and More Are up to $250 Off Ahead of Black Friday

Amazon's early Black Friday sale is bringing some pretty significant discounts on Apple devices, including several of the company's latest releases. The biggest savings are on the M5 MacBook Air, with up to $249 off, though there are plenty of smaller deals worth considering, too, from the AirPods Pro 3 at $179.00 to the Apple Pencil Pro at $99. You'll also find discounts on iPads, the Mac mini, and everyday accessories like the Magic Keyboard, Magic Trackpad, and AirTags. With Apple raising prices across its Mac and iPad lineups in June, citing higher memory chip costs, these sales offer a good opportunity to upgrade without paying full price.

The AirPods Pro 3 are down to $179 from $249, saving you $70 on Apple's latest premium wireless earbuds. They offer improved active noise cancellation over the previous generation, along with redesigned foam-infused ear tips and a heart-rate sensor that tracks workouts without an Apple Watch. They also support Live Translation with a compatible iPhone and carry an IP57 rating for sweat and water resistance. PCMag’s review goes into more detail on how they perform.

The AirPods Max 2, which launched in March, are already down to $449.99 from $549, saving you nearly $100 on Apple's premium over-ear headphones. The biggest upgrade over the original model is the H2 chip, which Apple says makes active noise cancellation up to 1.5 times more effective while introducing features like Adaptive Audio, Conversation Awareness, and Live Translation. For more on its performance, check out this PCMag review.

The Apple Watch Series 12 (GPS, 42mm) is down to $329 from $399, saving you $70. It covers everything you'd expect from a premium smartwatch, including fitness tracking, health monitoring, notifications, and the ability to take calls when your iPhone is nearby. PCMag gave it an "outstanding" rating in its review and also named it its Gift of the Year for 2026.

If you'd prefer to occasionally leave your iPhone behind, the Apple Watch Series 12 (GPS + Cellular, 42mm) is $70 off, bringing its price down to $429 from $499. It has all the same features as the GPS model but adds cellular connectivity, allowing you to make calls, send messages, and stream music even when your phone isn't nearby (provided you have an active cellular plan).

For a more affordable option, the Apple Watch SE 3 (GPS, 40mm) is down to $199 from $249, saving you $50. It covers the essentials, including workout tracking, daily activity monitoring, and notifications, making it a practical choice if you don't need the Series 12's more advanced health features. Check out PCMag's review for a closer look at what you get for the price.

The Apple Watch SE 3 (GPS + Cellular, 40mm) is also $50 off, bringing its price down to $249 from $299. For an extra $50 over the GPS-only model, you get the convenience of making calls, sending messages, and staying connected without keeping your iPhone nearby, provided you have an active cellular plan. It's worth considering if you regularly go for runs, head to the gym, or step out without your phone but still want to stay reachable.

Apple released the M5 MacBook Air in March with 512GB of base storage, double the previous generation's, along with a faster SSD and an N1 wireless chip that adds Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6. Right now, the 15-inch M5 MacBook Air (512GB) is down to $1,249.99 from $1,499. For a closer look at the M5 MacBook Air's performance and features, check out PCMag's review.

If you prefer a more compact laptop but need extra storage, the 13-inch M5 MacBook Air with a 1TB SSD is down to $1,399.99 from $1,599, saving you $200. That's twice the storage of the base model, giving you more room for large files, photos, videos, and applications without having to rely as heavily on external drives or cloud storage. You still get the M5 chip, faster SSD performance, and Wi-Fi 7 support, all in a smaller, lighter design.

The M6 Mac mini is down to $949.99 from $1,099, saving you $149 on Apple's latest compact desktop. Its 12-core CPU and 12-core GPU can handle everything from everyday multitasking to photo editing and more demanding creative work, while the upgraded Neural Engine helps with AI-related tasks—Apple says the M6 delivers substantial performance improvements over the earlier M4 model, particularly for graphics and AI workloads. For more on how it performs in everyday use, check out PCMag's review.

The M4 iPad Air launched in March, and the 13-inch model is already down to $949.99 from $1,049, saving you nearly $100. Its M4 chip can comfortably handle demanding tasks like photo editing, digital illustration, and multitasking, while the 13-inch Liquid Retina display gives you plenty of room to work across apps. You also get 256GB of storage for your files and creative projects. For more on what to expect, here's PCMag's review.

If you need more storage for your creative projects, the 13-inch M4 iPad Air with 512GB is $100 off, bringing its price down to $1,149 from $1,249. That's double the storage of the 256GB model, giving you more room for large photo libraries, video projects, downloaded files, and apps without having to rely as heavily on cloud storage.

For those looking for something more powerful, the 11-inch iPad Pro with the M5 chip and 512GB of storage is down to $1,299.99 from $1,399, saving you nearly $100. Along with the faster processor, it offers an Ultra Retina XDR OLED display with ProMotion support for refresh rates up to 120Hz, making it particularly well-suited for creative work, video editing, and other demanding tasks.

The Apple Magic Keyboard with Numeric Keypad is down to $109 from $129, saving you $20 on a wireless keyboard designed primarily for Mac users. It pairs wirelessly over Bluetooth and can be recharged using USB-C. For a closer look at all that it has to offer, check out PCMag's review.

The Magic Trackpad is down to $130 from $149, taking $19 off an accessory that brings the Multi-Touch gestures and Force Touch pressure sensing of a MacBook trackpad to a desktop setup, across an edge-to-edge glass surface. It connects wirelessly over Bluetooth and has a rechargeable battery that lasts about a month between charges. You can read PCMag's full review to see how it stacks up.

The Apple Pencil Pro is down to $99 from $129, saving you $30 on Apple's most feature-rich stylus. It adds squeeze and barrel-roll gestures, haptic feedback, and Find My support to Apple's stylus lineup, and it pairs and charges magnetically along the side of a compatible iPad, including the M4 iPad Air featured above. For more on its strengths and limitations, check out PCMag's review.

The second-generation AirTag, released in January, keeps the original's size so existing holders still fit, while a new Ultra Wideband chip extends Precision Finding range by up to 50% and a redesigned speaker plays 50% louder. Right now, its four-pack is down to $79.99 from $99.99, which works out to $20 per tag, compared with $29 for a single AirTag. PCMag's review offers a more detailed look at how it holds up in everyday use.

The Apple Magic Mouse is down to $59.99 from $79.99, saving you $20 on Apple's wireless mouse with a Multi-Touch surface that lets you swipe between pages and scroll through documents using simple finger gestures. It connects to your Mac over Bluetooth and has a rechargeable battery.

Yes, Apple participates in Black Friday, though you may want to compare their sales with other retailers like Best Buy and Walmart. So far, the best Black Friday sale on an Apple product is the M5 MacBook Air on sale for $200 off, the lowest price since the price hike this summer.

Among the deals listed here, the M5 MacBook Air with 512 of storage has one of the larger discounts at $249 off, bringing its price down to $1,249.99, from $1499.

For most people, the Apple Watch SE 3 at $199 is a good starting point, covering everyday fitness tracking, notifications, and essential health features without the higher price of the Series 12. If you want more advanced health monitoring and a more premium smartwatch experience, the Series 12 at $329 is worth considering.
