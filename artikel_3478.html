Billy Bob Thornton’s ‘Landman’ Suffers a Major Streaming Setback Following Season 3 Delay

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Taylor Sheridan is no stranger to putting together a show that doesn't just entertain viewers but also pierces a hole in the cultural consciousness, and finds the exact thing that makes people tick. That's why he's been as successful as he has to date. But while one of his shows has been breaking records, ahead of a huge blow for its momentum, another hit has come and it's one that suggests perhaps its popularity is starting to wane the longer it's out of the public eye.

Landman, the hit Paramount+ drama starring Billy Bob Thornton, and based on the podcast Boomtown by Christian Wallace, has officially dropped out of the platform's U.S. Top 10 television chart after spending more than 400 consecutive days among its most-watched shows. The achievement becomes even more impressive when compared with some of the streamer's other biggest hits. South Park has previously managed a 467-day streak, while Ghosts reached 581 days, Everybody Loves Raymond achieved 837 days, and NCIS managed an extraordinary 980 days. No other Sheridan series has reportedly come close to matching Landman's run. The official series synopsis states:

In season two, as oil rises from the earth, so do secrets — and Tommy Norris's breaking point may be closer than he realizes. Facing mounting pressure from M-Tex Oil, Cami Miller, and the shadow of his kin, survival in West Texas isn't noble - it's brutal. And sooner or later something's got to break.
