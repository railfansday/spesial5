Gordon Ramsay Is the New Host of the BBC’s ‘Top Gear’

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The celebrity chef’s run on the legendary car show is set to start airing in 2028.

At one point, Top Gear was one of the biggest shows on television of any sort. It has been a long time since the glory days of Clarkson, Hammond, and May, though, and multiple efforts to revive the BBC’s legendary automotive series have failed to come close to the impact of the original. The show has not been in production since its newest episodes aired in 2022, but a new host is about to change that very soon. On Wednesday, the BBC announced that celebrity chef Gordon Ramsay is taking over the mantle of Top Gear host starting in 2028. Ramsay will be the first host of the show since production paused after a crash involving then-presenter Freddie Flintoff four years ago.

An announcement from the BBC makes no mention of any potential co-hosts alongside Ramsay, but does add that he will be hosting along with “as yet unnamed co-presenters.” The same announcement quotes BBC Studios Entertainment executive Suzy Lamb as saying that the new Top Gear “won't be a nostalgic reboot" and would instead be a “bold and unmissable new chapter” of the show, lines that suggest a major format change could come with the new host.

While Ramsay is most famous for his career as a chef and host of cooking-related shows, he is also a long-time enthusiast who takes his own cars to the track. He even combined the interests back in 2023, cooking a grilled cheese sandwich in the engine bay of his Aston Martin Valkyrie during a track day. This past weekend, he competed in a celebrity race in Lego Formula 1 cars at the Las Vegas Grand Prix.
