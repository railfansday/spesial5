For travel sellers building AI agents, booking the room is only part of the job. Duffel and Katanox want to bring payments, commissions, and financing into the same system.

Duffel is partnering with Katanox, a hospitality financial technology company, to simplify how hotels receive bookings and payments. The aim is to fix a chain that can leave travelers stranded at check-in and sellers chasing commissions months after checkout.

The partnership, announced Wednesday, will link Katanox's hotel distribution and payment infrastructure to Duffel's platform. The travel tech infrastructure company, which allows businesses to embed flight, hotel, and travel-extra bookings directly into their own apps or websites, is known most recently for powering travel for the likes of Robinhood and Meta's AI agent Muse.

On hotels alone, Duffel's scope spans 1.6 million properties worldwide. Duffel is partnering with Katanox to route hotel bookings, payments, and commission settlements through fewer intermediaries. Duffel, the travel tech infrastructure company that lets businesses embed flight, hotel, and extras bookings into their own apps — recently powering travel for Robinhood and Meta's AI agent Muse — spans 1.6 million properties on hotels alone. Katanox connects directly to hotels' property management or central reservation systems, bypassing bed banks and distribution switches, with the hotel remaining the merchant of record. Katanox plans to replace virtual credit cards with bank transfers for hotel settlement and automate commission reconciliation, addressing two problems Duffel CEO Steve Domin described: travelers arriving at properties where the hotel can't find the booking, and commissions that take months to collect. The two CEOs described different settlement timelines — Domin expects instant or at-checkout settlement, while Katanox CEO Paul Beukers described holding funds in escrow and releasing them at check-in when the reservation becomes non-refundable — and the companies haven't clarified how those fit together. Eligible sellers will get access to Katanox Capital, which offers payment advances tied to booking and settlement data, though who bears the financial risk hasn't been specified. Duffel's sellers won't have to switch; they get the arrangement by default.
