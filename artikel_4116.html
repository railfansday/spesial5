Escape From Tarkov Reveals Top Down Spin-Off

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Escape From Tarkov is celebrating its first anniversary since leaving what once felt like eternal early access last November. Now that the hard part of the journey is over, Battlestate Games and director Nikita Buyanov are looking at the future, and that means new games.

As reported by Grant Taylor-Hill of Insider Gaming, the next chapter for the Escape From Tarkov developer is still going to be an extraction shooter, but it will be a lot different from the game that started it all.

Nikita Buyanov told Insider Gaming that part of the future for the Escape From Tarkov creator lies on mobile.

According to Buyanov, this game will “look and feel like Tarkov.” Details are scarce for the time being, but it has now been confirmed that the next game will be a mobile-based, top-down extraction shooter set in the world of Escape From Tarkov.

Ubisoft reportedly has a new Rainbow Six: Vegas game in the works, with a third entry considered more likely than a remake.

Although it may sound strange to someone who’s only familiar with ‘classic’ Tarkov, the concept has been thoroughly validated through Team Soda’s Escape From Duckov. The game, endorsed by Battlestate Games, is an avian-themed parody of Escape From Tarkov, with an engaging gameplay loop and endearing art style.

According to Buyanov, this game will “look and feel like Tarkov.”

Games like Hotline Miami, the original Helldivers, and the Intravenous series have shown you can deliver plenty of grittiness and tense action with a top-down perspective. The medium’s possibilities are endless, so it is up to Battlestate Games to work out how to capture the magic of Escape From Tarkov and bring it to mobile.

A release date for the Escape From Tarkov mobile spin-off has not been announced yet, but you can probably expect it before the elusive hideout management app for the core game.

Battlestate Games is also currently working on a single-player title, which some players have assumed to be a substitute for the Russia 2028 project that’s currently on hold.

Shortly after delivering Escape From Tarkov to the world in its final form, Nikita Buyanov announced his next project, Fragmentary Order.

According to the Russian auteur, Fragmentary Order is “almost there,” and marketing materials shared by the game’s official page certainly seem to reflect that.

The game takes Escape From Tarkov’s hardcore shooter mechanics into the one place that hasn’t been corrupted by capitalism, and expands the kind of corporate-infused chaos from Tarkov into the entire solar system.

Pre-orders are already open for Fragmentary Order, divided between Candidates ($44.99) and Founders tiers ($79.99). Both versions grant access to closed alpha testing.

Besides Buyanov, other Escape From Tarkov alumni are also making a name for themselves in new projects. Tarkov veterans Dmitry Ogorodnikov, Jean Tyulegenov, and German Terekhov have moved on to found Nomion Games and are currently working on a cyberpunk shooter titled Rush is Real.

The art direction has been handed over to Croatian 3D artist Space Meerkat, who is famous for his elaborate cyberpunk concepts.

Rush is Real does not have a release window yet, but you can follow development updates here.

With no particular preamble, Asha Sharma revealed that MachineGames has begun work on the next mainline Wolfenstein title.
