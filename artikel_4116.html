Nintendo Highlights Multiple Major Third-Party Releases On The Way To Switch 2 In 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The third-party support for the Switch 2 has been mostly outstanding so far, with releases such as Cyberpunk 2077 and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4.

It doesn't seem to be slowing down, either. In its latest financial results briefing, Nintendo has now shared a reminder about what's on the way over the next year. This includes games like Final Fantasy VII Revelation and FromSoft's Switch 2 exclusive The Duskbloods, which was recently delayed.

Here's the rundown of what's coming to the Switch 2 next year. Layton's new outing also gets a mention, as it's a late 2026 arrival.

"More third-party multiplatform titles coming to Nintendo on day one, in addition to titles developed for Nintendo Switch 2"

Nintendo has also used this opportunity to share some charts, illustrating how third-party continues to provide support for overall software sales on the Switch and Switch 2. It also plays a major role in driving engagement.

"Ongoing sales of our previously released evergreen titles, together with sales of third-party titles, provide substantial support for overall software unit sales.

"...Titles from software publishers account for a significant share of software unit sales, making a substantial contribution to our business. Furthermore, their software has a strong presence among download-only titles, which are not included in this graph, and plays a major role in sustaining engagement with our hardware."

On the first-party front, Nintendo has this week reconfirmed the release windows for multiple upcoming Switch 2 titles. You can find out what else is on the way in our previous story.
