Mini Review: Barbie Rewind (PS5) - Fab Outer Layer Brings This Retro Ensemble Together

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Version Reviewed: PS5 (Standard) / European

The only Barbie title I've played, prior to Barbie Rewind, is a PC game named Fashion Designer, in which you choose clothes based on particular themes, then apply patterns, accessories, and colours. You could print them out and put them on real Barbie dolls — a nice idea.

Sadly, Fashion Designer isn't among the games included in Barbie Rewind, but what is in this retro compilation is similarly simple and enjoyable, for the most part.

16 old school games starring the iconic doll feature here, from 1991's simply-named platformer Barbie all the way to 2007's Barbie as the Island Princess, basically a handful of mini-games.

Some of the included titles don't hold up particularly well nowadays, but it's still interesting to see the gaming legacy of the character, which spans a surprisingly wide range of experiences. There are sports, horse riding, detective adventures, and more.

While it's obviously great to have all these games gathered — especially with the inclusion of the previously unreleased Barbie Vacation Adventure — the best thing about Barbie Rewind is actually the way it's packaged.

You can play the classic games at any time, but tying it all together is an in-game DreamHouse that you can freely decorate as you unlock more items.

Completing basic challenges in the retro titles unlocks new rooms as well as more furniture and accessories, giving you somewhere to return to and kit out as you see fit.

It's a really neat way to encourage you to play all the games, making the collection feel nicely cohesive. It's also an entertaining thing to noodle around with in its own right, with hundreds of items and several rooms to decorate.

The way it's presented reminds me of Unpacking; it doesn't have that game's wonderful wordless storytelling, but placing down objects just how you want them has a similar appeal here.

On top of that, there's the Catalog, which is the museum style mode with lots of old documents and materials to look through regarding Barbie as well as the games themselves.

It's another great effort from Digital Eclipse, and I think going the extra mile with the DreamHouse really lifts the whole thing. If you're nostalgic for Barbie games or are just curious about their history, this is a lovely way to experience them.
