Stealth Classic Thief: The Dark Project Returns for a PS5 Remaster with New Content

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

One of the classics that helped create an entire genre, stealth masterpiece Thief: The Dark Project is coming to PS5 in remastered form next month, from 3rd December 2026.

This new version of the 1998 game has been remastered using Nightdive Studios' KEX Engine to produce a new edition running at 4K and 120 frames-per-second. It's got all the content of the base game, the three extra missions from Thief: Gold, and an entirely new campaign in the form of The Tarnished Mirror.

The Tarnished Mirror is a narrative bridge between The Dark Project and Thief 2: The Metal Age. In it, "a seemingly straightforward theft spirals into imprisonment, uneasy alliances and a descent into a drowned, R’lyeh-like necropolis. Players will experience an expanded enemy roster, ambitious level design and a pure Thief experience that descends into unsettling, otherworldly horror."

The bonus campaign has been created by the same people who made Thief: The Black Parade, which was a mod / expansion for Thief: Gold, made by the fans. "This collaboration marks a rare industry moment, with Nightdive and Eidos-Montréal welcoming a community creation directly into official canon," the press release added.
