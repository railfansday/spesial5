Member of Violent Crew that Targeted Mid-Atlantic Pharmacies and Cell Phone Stores Found Guilty

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Deaundre Blount, 33, of the District, was found guilty today in connection with his role in a violent multi-state robbery crew that targeted pharmacies and cell phone stores in the District, Maryland, Virginia, and New Jersey, announced U.S. Attorney Jeanine Ferris Pirro.
