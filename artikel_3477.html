Sydney Sweeney’s R-Rated Thriller Is Officially One of the Biggest Streaming Hits in the World

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

For better or for worse, Sydney Sweeney's popularity continues to rise, with her many movie appearances becoming streaming sensations. Late next year, one of her most popular films will release a sequel sure to dominate at the box office. The Housemaid's Secret, an adaptation of Freida McFadden’s sequel novel of the same name, is slated for a December 17, 2027, release, with Sweeney reprising her role as Millie Calloway.

Directed by Paul Feig and written by Rebecca Sonnenshine, The Housemaid's Secret will see Michele Morrone also return as Enzo, with several famous names added to the cast, including Paul Anthony Kelly, Kirsten Dunst, and The Hunting Wives favorite Brittany Snow. Lionsgate will be hoping for similar success for the sequel to last year's surprise smash hit, The Housemaid. The movie earned $400 million in global box office revenue against a reported production budget of just $35 million, outperforming several of the biggest titles of late 2025.

A year later, The Housemaid is still proving popular. At the time of writing, the film has spent 20 days as one of the most-watched movies on HBO Max in the U.S., and currently ranks in second place behind Olivia Wilde's Oscars contender, The Invite. Amanda Seyfried and Brandon Sklenar also starred alongside Sweeney in The Housemaid, although neither will return for the upcoming sequel. The movie was a new addition to HBO Max on October 9 and has proven a big hit for the streamer ever since.

Sweeney's NSFW franchise is moving beyond the page and screen. On September 14, 2027, the story will officially move to the stage, making its debut at London's Richmond Theatre, ahead of a planned tour. "With the stage play debuting next year and our sequel film in production, the momentum behind The Housemaid franchise continues to grow," Jenefer Brown, Lionsgate's President of Global Products & Experiences, said. She added:

"We’re thrilled to work with Simon, Hanna, Bekah, and Matthew to bring this story to the stage in an entirely new way, giving audiences the chance to experience its suspense, twists, and turns live."
