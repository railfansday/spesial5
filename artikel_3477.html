Gerard Butler’s Action Streaming Hit Is Officially Being Removed From Netflix

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The action thriller genre is full of heroes who put their lives at risk for the greater good or personal vengeance. Recently, Keanu Reeves’ John Wick franchise does it best, as our favorite ex-assassin goes on a rampage after his dog is killed. Features like Jason Statham’s The Beekeeper and A Working Man woo audiences with the trope and keep them in their seats with slick action.

Among such entertaining features is Gerard Butler’s Law Abiding Citizen. Directed by F. Gary Gray, whose works include The Fate of the Furious and The Italian Job, and written by Kurt Wimmer, scribe for action films like The Beekeeper, Point Break, and Salt. The movie follows an honorable family man, Clyde Shelton (Butler), whose wife and daughter are murdered in a home invasion. He hopes for justice, but a rising prosecutor named Nick Rice (Jamie Foxx) cuts a deal with one of the killers in exchange for testimony.

The feature highlights the themes of friction between moral justice and legal procedure. It also cautions against the corrupting nature of vengeance as we see Butler’s character starting with sympathy but going on a full rampage and killing innocent people, which makes this movie extremely dark when compared to other entries in the genre. It was a modest success, earning $127.9 million at the box office against a budget of $53 million, although it got mixed reviews. It can be seen in the movie's Rotten Tomatoes score wherein critics awarded it 26% while the audience gave it a much higher score of 75%. No doubt the movie is proving a compelling rewatch on streaming as it keeps popping up on various streaming charts.

For fans who’d love to revisit the film, time is running out as Netflix is scrubbing Law Abiding Citizen from its digital streaming library at the end of the month. Butler seamlessly transforms from a man filled with grief to one who’s consumed with vengeance. Foxx effectively portrays a man slowly realizing his own complicity in a broken system. His smug confidence in the opening act transforms into genuine desperation and guilt as Clyde dismantles his life.
