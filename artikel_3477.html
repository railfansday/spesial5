GTA 6 Release Day: Everything To Know To Be Ready On Day One

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Your burning questions about GTA 6 pre-order bonuses to Rockstar’s many official events, all in one place

Grand Theft Auto 6 is less than a week away, which means there's not much time to prepare for the biggest gaming launch of 2026. Fortunately, we've got some tips on what to expect on day one and how to make sure you get the best experience possible from Rockstar's record-breaking open-world adventure.

GTA 6 will be released worldwide on Nov. 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X. It will also be playable on Xbox One if you're willing to play it via Cloud Gaming. You can pre-order the game now and preload it ahead of launch, provided that you've got at least 212 GB in storage plus 1 GB for the game's first pre-release update.

If you haven't already, you should check out GTA 6's official trailers and the Netflix Extended Look. The short version is that GTA 6 features a Bonnie and Clyde-type story with two protagonists, Jason and Lucia. The pair have committed crimes at some point, but GTA 6 seems to focus on the aftermath of those choices — and how the couple builds a new future together (or not!)

Pre-ordering comes with a handful of cosmetic goodies themed around Vice City. These bonuses offer a classic sedan with its own storage area, vintage looks and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and some special weapon patterns inspired by Tommy Vercetti. You can take a closer look at all of these here.

The catch is that the game is fully digital. If you pre-order and pick up GTA 6 at, say, GameStop, all you'll get is a code in a box. And while the pre-order bonuses don't give players a gameplay advantage, many fans believe cosmetics are a core part of GTA 6's appeal.

There's the standard edition of GTA 6 ($79.99) and the Ultimate edition ($79.99). The Ultimate Edition comes with a retro-futuristic sports car, two special types of guns for Jason and Lucia, a fancy motorcycle and kayak, and some version-exclusive locations. These areas include, among other things, two car customization shops, a salon, a clothing store, a tattoo parlor, a Mud Club vehicle and hideout, and a contraband store.

GTA Online is a separate experience, but one that Rockstar appears to be prepping for a new era. This next iteration of GTA's multiplayer will offer more ways to roleplay, and those features are being developed in partnership with FiveM, the modders at the forefront of that scene. GTA Online will be playable on GTA 6's launch day ... but it's also accessible right now!

If you're lucky enough to own both consoles, it may come down to preference. Rockstar and Sony have repeatedly insisted that GTA 6 "plays best" on PS5, and indeed, the platform comes with a few exclusive features. The DualSense controller will offer high-fidelity haptic feedback and improved spatial audio rendering. The Xbox version can be played via the Cloud, and on previous-generation hardware.

PS5 Pros are expensive and difficult to find right now. GTA 6 does have some PS5 Pro enhancements, such as Spectral Super Resolution as well as ray-traced shadows. No matter where you play it, GTA 6 will be capped at 30 frames per second.
