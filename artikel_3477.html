Miley Cyrus Marries Maxx Morando at Frank Sinatra's Famous Palm Springs Mansion

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

It's official: After more than five years together and nearly a year of engagement, Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando have tied the knot. The couple were married in the desert hot spot of Palm Springs, California on Saturday, November 21, an outdoor ceremony held at Frank Sinatra’s former mansion, with Wayne Coyne, frontman of The Flaming Lips and a Cyrus collaborator, officiating.

The 33-year-old singer and the 28-year-old musician—Morando is the drummer for the band Liily—had kept their plans a secret until Friday, when TMZ broke the news. Confirmation of the impending celebration emerged that same evening, during the dinner that capped off the rehearsal, when Miley’s half-sister, Lily-Rose Purcell, posted a photo on her Instagram Stories of a menu featuring the couple’s initials. This is Cyrus’s second marriage, following her alliance with actor Liam Hemsworth from 2018 to 2019.

Sinatra’s former home in Palm Springs, where the wedding took place

According to images published in outlets including People, the ceremony venue was decorated with white flowers and large candles, with Sinatra’s pool in the background. Miley Cyrus wore a long-sleeved white satin dress with a plunging back and no veil, while the groom opted for a classic black tuxedo with a bow tie.

Among the guests were the bride’s mother, Tish Cyrus, accompanied by her husband, actor Dominic Purcell, and Miley's sister, Noah Cyrus. Also in attendance was the singer’s father, Billy Ray Cyrus, along with his rumored fiancée, British actress Elizabeth Hurley, and her son, Damian.

Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando on February 01, 2026

As the bride has recounted in past interviews, her relationship with her now-husband began in 2021 following a blind date organized by mutual friends. That same year, they went public by attending the singer’s New Year’s Eve TV special in Miami together. Their engagement came to light in December 2025, when, during another of those joint appearances, Miley Cyrus posed on the red carpet wearing a diamond ring that turned out to be her engagement ring.

One of the most emotional moments of the ceremony took place just before the couple exchanged their vows, when—according to multiple outlets— Dolly Parton’s “Love Is Like a Butterfly” began to play. The nod follows a similar one Miley made last month, when she broke down in tears over Parton's death during a performance at the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles.

“Me and Dolly, we are eternal. Nothing in this life could ever tear us apart, and I’m gonna see her again,” Cyrus told the stadium crowd in October, per The Hollywood Reporter. “And I can’t fucking wait because I miss her,” Cyrus tearfully continued. “Some of the greatest music that we have today was written, produced, performed by my Aunt Dolly."
