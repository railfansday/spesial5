Monarc’s rugged eBikes are $100 off for Black Friday — and they come with a free spare battery

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

SAVE $100: Starting Nov. 2 through Dec. 6, Monarc's Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale knocks $100 off both the Monarc Marker and the Monarc Tracer, while tossing in a free spare battery (a $250 value).

Electric bikes have made the daily commute a whole lot more fun, but range anxiety is still the big elephant in the room. There is nothing quite as humbling as watching your battery gauge blink red halfway up a steep hill, leaving you to pedal a 75-pound machine home on pure leg power.

Premium newcomer Monarc cuts straight through that stress by packaging its bikes with two swappable 720Wh batteries and backing every frame with a generous five-year warranty. For the holiday shopping season, Monarc is running an early Black Friday sale through Dec. 6 that takes $100 off both models and tosses in a second battery pack for free, giving you a swappable backup to double your distance.

For riders who like their routes a little unpaved, the Monarc Marker is built for off-road wanderlust. It pairs a burly 1,638W peak Bafang hub motor with 85 Nm of torque, giving you enough punch to power through sandy paths and steep climbs without losing momentum. Massive 26 x 4-inch all-terrain fat tires take the edge off chunky rocks and rutted fire roads, while four-piston hydraulic disc brakes provide smooth, instant stopping power when you need to slow down in a pinch. With up to 120 miles of total range across both battery packs, it is built just as much for long weekend trails as it is for weekday grocery runs.

If your daily ride is all about cruising past gridlocked cars and navigating city potholes, the Monarc Tracer is dialed for pavement. It shares the exact same 1,638W motor muscle as the Marker, but tunes the ride for city comfort with an upright seating position, an 80mm reverse-arch suspension fork, and fast-rolling 27.5-inch Kenda tires. Dual batteries push your total distance to 130 miles, and you can switch the pedal-assist sensor between a natural-torque mode that matches your leg effort and a breezy cadence mode when you just want the motor to do the work.

Both bikes skip cheap components for dependable hardware: a 3.5-inch color touchscreen, Shimano Cues 9-speed gearing, integrated turn signals, and a 5A fast charger that refills a dead pack in three hours. You get integrated brake lights and turn signals right on the rear rack, plus the option to add safety gear like blind-spot radar and tire-pressure sensors.

Monarc also keeps the checkout process flexible. If you already own one of their bikes, you can order a bike without batteries to knock $500 off, or save $250 by sticking to a single-pack setup. But if you want to breeze past traffic without stressing over a dead battery, grabbing the full holiday bundle is the easy call.

You have until Dec. 6 to grab the Marker or Tracer, pocket the $100 discount, and get the extra battery tossed in for free.
