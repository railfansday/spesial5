PS5 RPG Lords of the Fallen 2 Locks on to April 2027 Release Date Following Lengthy Delay

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Lords of the Fallen 2 is officially targeting an April 2027 release date, having been hit with a lengthy delay back in the summer.

The anticipated action RPG was previously eyeing an autumn / fall 2026 launch, which would have only added to an insane PS5 schedule. Shifting to a vague 'Q1 2027' window seemed a wise move at the time.

And as mentioned, it's now going to land right towards the end of said quarter, which should give the game some room to breathe given that we're currently looking at very busy January, February, and March.

Interestingly, we'll be getting a more specific April date in the coming weeks, as an announcement is planned for December.

I'm guessing it'll be at The Game Awards? We're due a fresh look at this one either way.

Expectations are fairly high for this sequel. Its predecessor showed clear potential, but needed a huge number of updates to really shine; the suggestion is that Lords of the Fallen 2 will build on everything the last game did well, and be rock solid right out of the gate.
