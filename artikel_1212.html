Inter await tests but lose Calhanoglu for at least two games

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Hakan Calhanoglu will undergo medical tests on Tuesday, but Sky Sport Italia says it’s already certain he’ll miss Inter’s next two games against Stuttgart and Genoa.

Inter will have to cope without their midfield star, Calhanoglu, for at least the next two games.

Sky Sport Italia and Gazzetta report that the experienced midfielder will undergo medical tests tomorrow, Tuesday, November 24.

According to Sky, Calhanoglu is already certain to miss Inter’s next two games: against Stuttgart in the Champions League and Genoa in Serie A.

Calhanoglu was subbed off during the first half of Inter’s 4-4 draw against Atalanta on Sunday.

Piotr Zielinski was also injured, so Calhanoglu was replaced by Petar Sucic, and the Croatian scored a penalty in the second half to restore Inter’s two-goal lead, which didn’t last until the end of the game.

Calhanoglu, 32, already missed five games for club and country from mid-September to early October due to an adductor injury.

The Turkey international has scored three goals in ten appearances across all competitions this season, and his contract expires this coming June.

Lorenzo Bettoni is the Editor of Football Italia.
