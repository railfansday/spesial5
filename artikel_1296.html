Forget Left-Back: Man Utd Targeting Lankshear As First Pochettino Signing

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Manchester United are monitoring the progress of Middlesbrough striker Will Lankshear ahead of a potential January move as they look to strengthen Mauricio Pochettino's squad this winter, according to TEAMtalk.

Lankshear has enjoyed an electric start to the new season at Boro after completing his switch to Riverside from Tottenham Hotspur over the summer. Netting 15 goals in his first 16 matches across all competitions, the 21-year-old is now attracting interest from a host of Premier League clubs, including United, Arsenal, Brighton, Everton, Newcastle, Leeds and Sunderland.

The Red Devils, who have just appointed Pochettino as Michael Carrick's successor, are in need of a centre-forward, with Benjamin Sesko's injury issues meaning they've been light on options for much of this campaign.

Lankshear is on their shortlist, although competition from several other clubs and Middlesbrough's likely reluctance to sell mid-season will complicate a potential deal.

After scoring 12 goals on loan at Oxford United last season, Tottenham opted to cash in on Lankshear over the summer, garnering upwards of £20 million for the young England striker.

Just a few months later, a return to the Premier League could be on the cards for the former Spurs man. An extraordinarily prolific start to the new campaign has seen Lankshear become the most-wanted talent in the Championship right now, with clubs queuing up to sign the number nine in January.

According to TEAMtalk, Tottenham have a matching-right option included in the deal they negotiated with Middlesbrough in the summer, and are therefore also in the picture regarding a future transfer.

United are yet to approach Boro about a deal, with their interest at this point said to be at preliminary stages. The Red Devils are carefully tracking Lankshear's performances, whilst they continue to assess other potential targets.

Lankshear's Statistics 2026/27 (Championship Only)

TEAMtalk suggest that Boro have no intention to sell their talismanic striker in January, as they focus on winning promotion to the English top flight. The Teesiders currently sit in the Championship's top two, level on points with Birmingham City in first and Stoke City in third. The competitive nature of this season's Championship means they may not be able to afford losing Lankshear whilst still achieving their aim of promotion.

The striker himself is understood to have shown no signs that he's ready to agitate for a move away in January.

Manchester United are expected to be busy in the January transfer window and support Mauricio Pochettino following a miserable start to the season

Pochettino will no doubt be eager to recruit a striker after arriving at Old Trafford from the USMNT. Sesko hasn't played a minute of football since mid-September, and isn't expected back until January, while Joshua Zirkzee has three Premier League goals in his last 30 appearances.

United have been forced to deploy either Bryan Mbeumo or Mateus Cunha down the middle for much of this season in Sesko's absence, an arrangement Pochettino is unlikely to want to persist with.

While left-back has been mooted as the priority position for United, it appears they could first move for a brand new centre-forward.
