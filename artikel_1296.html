Every FC Barcelona Player’s Wage [2026]

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Under Hansi Flick, Barcelona have turned a corner and have returned to the level of form and dominance they've more often than not shown over the years. They've brought some very talented youngsters through the ranks over the years, but they've also paid over the odds for some of the biggest stars in the sport.

With Flick calling the shots, they have a healthy balance of both parties. The likes of Lamine Yamal and Gavi came through the academy, while they've brought proven superstars to Camp Nou too. After retaining their La Liga title, here's how much every player in the senior squad is taking home every week.

Marc-Andre ter Stegen, now on loan at Ajax, was Barcelona's starting goalkeeper for around a decade, but he lost his position to Joan Garcia. The Spaniard, signed from Espanyol last summer, penned a three-year deal worth just over €140,000 per week. The 25-year-old has since established himself as one of the best goalkeepers in world football.

Barca completed the signing of Croatia number 1 Dominik Livaković to serve as Garcia's backup. The 31-year-old signed a four-year contract worth just over €100,000-per-week after joining from Fenerbahce in a €4 million deal.

The Catalan giants also have a third proven shot-stopper in the shape of Wojciech Szczesny. The former Arsenal man came out of retirement to sign with the Catalan giants in 2024, and he's earning a nice weekly wage for doing so.

Moving into defence, there's one man making a significant amount more than anyone else, and that's Jules Kounde. Signed from Sevilla in 2022, he became one of the most expensive centre-backs in history. He has been integral for Barcelona since and is earning over €300,000-per-week for his troubles.

Eric Garcia is now the club's third highest-paid defender. He is paid a weekly salary of €153,846. Joao Cancelo jumped above him after signing for Barcelona in the summer. The former Al-Hilal defender spent half of last season on loan and has now returned to Barcelona for the third time, this time finally signing a permanent deal. He's signed a deal which will net him €180,385-per-week.

Andreas Christensen earns €100,192-a-week. The former Chelsea man has been plagued by injuries in the past few seasons but remains a solid option at the back. He earns the same amount as Pau Cubarsi.

Barcelona's young full-backs, Gerard Martin, Alejandro Balde and Xavi Espart, are the lowest-paid defenders in the first-team.

Barcelona made a vital signing in the summer, landing 2024 Ballon d'Or winner Rodri. The World Cup winner instantly became the club's highest-paid player, making €520,769 (£444,782) every week.

Frenkie de Jong has had his fair share of critics since he joined Barca in 2019. However, despite his future at the club coming into question on various occasions in the past few years, his future is in Catalonia, having penned a four-year extension worth €250,385-per-week in 2025.

He's on just more than Pedri (€240,385) in third place. Fermin Lopez (€200,385) and Gavi (€180,385) also earn more than €100,000 every seven days.

Marc Bernal and Brian Farinas came through the ranks at Camp Nou and have each signed a contract to keep them with the team for the foreseeable future. They earn €24,038-per-week and €12,115-per-week respectively.

Under Flick, Barcelona have transformed into one of the most exciting attacking teams in the world, and they've got a very impressive batch of forwards at their disposal.

Lamine Yamal has been the biggest success story to come out of Barcelona's academy since Lionel Messi. He is now earning a whopping €440,769 a week, which equates to just over €33 million per season as a base salary.

His partner in crime, Raphinha, was rewarded with a new deal in November 2026. The Brazilian, who serves as club captain, earns just less than the Spanish wonderkid.

Football has produced some of the best athletes the world has ever seen over the years, but who have been the most prolific in front of the net?

Dani Olmo, who has thrived at the club since rejoining the club from RB Leipzig in 2024, earns just over €300,000-per-week. That's more than summer signing Karim Adeyemi, who is paid €240,385 every seven days.

Instead of signing Marcus Rashford following his loan spell, Barcelona decided to make a move for Newcastle's Anthony Gordon in the summer of 2026. The Englishman put pen to paper on a five-year deal worth €232,308-per-week. Gabriel Jesus, who also joined the club from Arsenal this summer, is paid the same amount.

Promising Swedish youngster Roony Bardghji earns €30,000 every seven days. Sadly, he's out with an ACL injury right now, so he won't be able to offer much to the team in 2026/27. Teenagers Jesse Bisiwu and Hamza Abdelkarim both earn a modest wage (€10,000-per-week) compared to the rest of the squad.

All statistics courtesy of Capology (unless otherwise stated) - accurate as of 07/11/2026.
