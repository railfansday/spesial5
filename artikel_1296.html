Martin O'Neill Must Solve Celtic Left-Wing Problem After International Break

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Martin O'Neill faces a difficult decision when Celtic return to action after the international break. The Bhoys went into the break with back-to-back wins, earning some relief from the heavy criticism they have received at times this season.

Going into the break, Celtic are four points clear at the top of the Premiership having played a game more, while they are in with a chance of progressing in the Europa League, now requiring six or seven points from their last four games.

There are some concerns about Celtic's playing style, there are bigger concerns about how the club is being run, but in terms of their actual season, Celtic are in far from a disastrous situation.

But it's going to be another interesting run of fixtures after the break, with Celtic having to deal with another condensed period due to their European participation. Due to poor recruitment in the summer, Celtic don't have many options in most positions, but that is not the case on the left side. O'Neill has the luxury of choosing between four players on the left, and there are particularly difficult decisions to make between three players.

Jota is now nearing full fitness, and while his first start in more than 18 months didn't go brilliantly, he has looked sharp in cameos, and he has the sort of quality that no one else in this squad can offer. The question is whether he can offer it consistently and stay fit in what is going to be a significant increase in load over the coming weeks.

Yang Hyun-jun will have something to say about the race to start on the left. The South Korean was excellent before going off for the Asian Games in early September. He then picked up a hamstring issue and didn't return until just before the break, but if he can get back to his early-season form, it will be hard not to choose Yang as the starter.

To make matters even more complicated for O'Neill, young Callum Osmand has dazzled on the left in recent weeks, producing some big performances, including a goal and an assist off the bench against Besiktas. Sebastian Tounekti is also an option on the left, but given his poor form this season and the competition on the left, the right-footed winger would be wise to try the right-side, where O'Neill is clearly less than satisfied with Haissem Hassan. Hassan was dropped from Celtic's whole matchday squad for their last game before the break.

But that left side is going to be a very difficult one to manage for O'Neill, who needs to balance the playing time between three very good players who will be battling for starts. Jota or Yang likely have the edge, and you have to wonder whether one of these left-wingers could do a job in the attacking midfield role, especially Callum Osmand. Celtic need better competition and an alternative to Benjamin Nygren, who needs to be kept on his toes amid a lack of consistency this season. That could be a solution to keep Osmand's confidence high amid recent positive performances, and he has shown what he can do centrally with creativity. Nygren doesn't do much defensively in any case, so there will be little lost by playing a striker there, with Osmand being a centre-forward by trade.
