Man Utd fans tell Bryan Mbeumo to ‘go to sleep’ after posting GTA 6 photo

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Manchester United fans have told Bryan Mbeumo to "go to bed" after the striker suggested he was playing GTA 6 ahead of Mauricio Pochettino first training session as manager.

A new era began at Man Utd on Wednesday with Pochettino's first day in charge, having flown in overnight following his final match as head coach of the USMNT.

Pochettino will take charge of first-team training for the first time on Thursday, with a daunting trip to Anfield to face Liverpool first up for the Argentine.

Full focus will be on making a positive start at the home of Man Utd's biggest rivals, but Mbeumo seemingly had something else top of his agenda on Wednesday evening.

A known video game fan, Mbeumo was one of thousands across the UK on Wednesday downloading the new GTA game ahead of its midnight launch.

Mbeumo posted an image of him close to 12am set to play what many are calling one of the greatest video games of all time.

And it did not go down too well with some Man Utd fans online.

One fan wrote: "So we're sat in the relegation zone, got a must-win match against our biggest rivals at the weekend, new gaffer's just flown in from America and bloody Mbeumo is up past midnight playing grand theft auto six."

“Doing a GTA all nighter the day before his 1st training session with a new manager is definitely a choice,” read another post.

Another fan posted "you have training tomorrow go to bed lol", while a third added: "We’re 18th and we play Liverpool next."

Ryan posted: "These lads have got to read the room and be a bit smarter with what they post."

Grant Dalgleish added, with angry emojis: "He’s got training in the morning!!!!"

There is no suggestion for long, if at all, Mbeumo played the game ahead of Pochettino's first training session on Thursday morning.
