Tuchel hails England’s ‘complete performance’ after dramatic win over Spain

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Thomas Tuchel saluted "brave" England for bringing "everything to the table" and producing what he regarded as their best performance of his tenure in beating world champions Spain in their Nations League decider.

The Three Lions snatched top spot in Group A3 following a dramatic 3-2 victory over La Roja in Madrid.

England produced a positive attacking display at the Metropolitano, with goals from Harry Kane, Ezri Konsa and Ben White putting them 3-0 by the hour-mark.

However, Elliot Anderson was shown a red card soon after for a last-man foul on Lamine Yamal soon after, with Mikel Oyarzabal converting the subsequent penalty.

Tuchel's side then endured a nervy final quarter of an hour after Nico Williams pulled another goal back, but the 10 men held out for a huge win.

"Brilliant performance, well-deserved win," Tuchel told ITV Sport. "Overall, we were the better team. A fantastic performance from start to finish.

"We brought everything to the table. We were brave. Spirit-wise, energy-wise, and also our football quality was the highest level.

"A red card and penalty is always a big decision. From then, it was a long 30 minutes, but I don't want to focus on that. There were so many positive things in the first 60 minutes.

"Half an hour feels like two hours against a team like this [with 10 men]. That's the way it is. Without the red card, we were clear winners.

"We focused on our performance and attitude. I can't praise the team high enough for what they put out there."

England halted Spain's 43-match unbeaten run in all competitions, and beat the side ranked top of the FIFA world rankings away from home for the first time.

The Three Lions have also now achieved the unprecedented feat of winning 10 successive away games, with Tuchel paying tribute to his side for breaking barriers.

"We are always ready to push our limits," he added. "We had our best result in 60 years [at the World Cup], and we weren't happy with it. It's impressive what we did in the last two camps."

When asked if it was England's best performance since he took charge, he replied: "Under the circumstances, against who and where we played, yes."
