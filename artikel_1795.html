Thierry Henry and Jamie Carragher make Arsenal vs Man City prediction

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Jamie Carragher believes Arsenal will beat Manchester City to end their Premier League title hopes on Sunday, while Thierry Henry claims Enzo Maresca’s side are ‘there to be dominated’.

Arsenal sit six points clear of City at the top of the Premier League and head into this weekend’s fixture off the back of a 1-1 draw with Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League on Tuesday night.

City, meanwhile, have registered just two wins in their last seven games in all competitions and needed to come from behind twice to draw 2-2 with Napoli in their Champions League game on Tuesday.

Enzo Maresca’s side visit the Emirates Stadium this weekend and Carragher feels Arsenal will be too strong for their Premier League title rivals.

‘I think Arsenal will win. I can’t see City getting anything from that game,’ Carragher told CBS Sports after Arsenal’s draw with Dortmund.

‘They’ve been really poor of late, the results have shown that, not strong defensively at all.

‘Arsenal in the last couple of games have let in a couple of goals but I think they’ll be really tuned in to know if they beat Manchester City that’ll be end of Manchester City in terms of the title.

‘I think they’ll be really aggressive, man-to-man press, get after them all over the pitch. I just don’t see them [City] at all.’

Asked who he believes can challenge Arsenal for the title, Carragher replied: ‘No one.

‘And that’s not because Arsenal are amazing. Listen, they are a top team, don’t get me wrong, but in terms of the Champions League, I don’t think the Premier League teams can match… only Arsenal can really match what we’re seeing from the top teams.

‘And that shouldn’t be the case with the Premier League. The money we’ve got, the teams that we have, we have big four or five teams who would all think they all have a real opportunity each season to win the league, spend big money, hundreds of millions on individual players, my own team being one in Liverpool. So to see how far we all are behind, besides Arsenal, they got to the Champions League final last season, you see City tonight, you see Villa lose, Liverpool lost a couple of weeks ago away to Fenerbahce.

‘I still think you’ve almost got four or five teams, Arsenal are one of them, the big teams in the Champions League. For the Premier League not to have more than one, considering we’ve got five in the competition and the funds they all have, it’s probably a bit disappointing and shows why Arsenal are favourites for the Premier League.’

Meanwhile, Thierry Henry believes City’s run of form shows that Maresca’s side could struggle to control the game against Arsenal at the Emirates Stadium.

‘You have two teams who have to rectify two things at the weekend – Arsenal, like we’ve been saying recently, and Mikel Arteta has been saying, when you’re on top of a team, don’t let them come back to attack like we’ve seen in the last two games,’ Henry said.

‘If you go back to City, and I’ve said it since the beginning of the season, the balance of the team isn’t right. Simple as that.

‘They have quality, they have goalscorers who will bail them out, Rayan Cherki bailed them out tonight, but the cohesion of the team, the way they don’t stop counters, they don’t control the game on and off the ball like they used to.

‘That didn’t stop them losing games under Pep [Guardiola] but they were more in control of games.

‘You have two teams who have been dominating the league in the last two, three years. But I want to see an Arsenal team that’s going to dominate City, and they are there to be dominated.

‘But on the other side, it might be the day that City wake up. But if they play the same way they’ve been playing recently, being open and all over the place in midfield, it could be a long day.’
