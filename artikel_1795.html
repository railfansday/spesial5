Sheffield United’s 4-0 Championship rout of Watford prompts Hornets to axe Alessio Dionisi

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Watford have axed head coach Alessio Dionisi following their 4-0 defeat to Sheffield United.

The struggling Hornets visited Bramall Lane on Saturday (November 21) and were comfortably beaten in South Yorkshire.

Ben Brereton Diaz notched a brace inside the opening eight minutes and the advantage was built upon in the second half by Jordan Ayew and Sydie Peck.

The loss was Watford’s ninth of the Championship season and with the club languishing in 21st, the club have taken action and relieved Dionisi of his duties.

Dionisi’s coaching colleagues Fabio Spighi, Luca Vigiani and Dan Thomassen have also moved on.

Watford’s sporting director Luke Dowling said: “We thank Alessio and his staff for his professionalism and their efforts.

“As I’m sure the supporters have seen, Alessio is a really good guy and we wish him the best for the future.

“The process has started for a replacement and we hope to be in a position to announce that shortly.”

The post-Dionisi era will start on Wednesday (November 25), when Blackburn Rovers visit Vicarage Road.

Watford have been notoriously trigger-happy with managers over the years, therefore change is nothing new for the club’s supporters.

After the defeat at Bramall Lane, Dionisi said: “Firstly, I want to say sorry to our supporters. When you play away at Sheffield United and show no courage to play football it becomes impossible to get a good result.

“If you come to a place like this and go two goals behind after eight minutes it is always going to be hard for you.

“Things did not get much better in the second half and we conceded two more goals. Edoardo [Bove] was onside when he scored at 3-0 but I don’t like to make excuses.”
