Dana White: UFC chief declines Trump’s White House job offer

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) chief Dana White made a surprise appearance at the White House on Monday to turn down Donald Trump’s offer of a job as the White House press secretary, saying he has “zero political aspirations”. Trump stunned the world on November 7 when he said on social media that he had offered White the job, only to claim a day later that he was "only kidding”.
