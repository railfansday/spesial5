Ukraine Attacks Kerch Bridge Linking Russia to Occupied Crimea

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Destroying the structure, the only one connecting Russia to the occupied Crimean Peninsula, has long been a top objective for Kyiv.

Marc Santora, who has covered the war in Ukraine since 2022, has written about each Ukrainian attempt to bring down the Kerch Strait Bridge.

A large section of the only bridge linking Russia to the occupied Crimean Peninsula collapsed into the sea on Tuesday after a series of Ukrainian strikes, according to photographs and video released by the Ukrainian government, and statements from officials in Kyiv.

Russian officials, who temporarily halted traffic on the structure, the Kerch Strait Bridge, acknowledged the damage and vowed to repair it.

The Ukrainian strikes were the culmination of years of attacks aimed at bringing down the bridge, a hulking symbol of Moscow’s control of Crimea, which Russia illegally annexed in 2014. The bridge is also a lifeline supplying Russian troops.

President Volodymyr Zelensky of Ukraine said in a statement that the attack came after months of planning. After degrading or destroying nearby air defenses, Mr. Zelensky said, Ukraine struck with missiles and drones.

While destroying the bridge has long been a top Ukrainian objective, Mr. Zelensky framed the strikes as a response to recent Russian attacks on Ukraine’s bridges and other infrastructure.

Hours before his statement, seven Russian ballistic missiles slammed into bridges over the Dnipro River in Kyiv, Ukraine’s capital. The bridges were temporarily closed, Ukrainian officials said.
