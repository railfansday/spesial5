Exiled Catalan separatist Puigdemont to return to Spain after election

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Catalan separatist ​and former regional leader Carles Puigdemont said on Wednesday he would end nearly a decade of self-imposed exile in Belgium and return to Spain on December 12, ​after ‌this weekend's national election. Puigdemont, whose legal troubles in ⁠Spain are over after the Supreme Court earlier this week applied an amnesty law to shelve the ‌criminal case against him, said at a televised news conference from ⁠Brussels that he would unveil his proposal for a renewed Catalan independence push on December 17. FRANCE 24's Sarah Morris reports from Madrid, Spain.
