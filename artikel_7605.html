Prolific Child Molester and Self-Proclaimed “Elite Ranked Pedophile” Sentenced to 310 Years in Federal Prison

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Jacksonville, Florida – Frederick Karl Hildenbrand (55, Cocoa) has been sentenced by U.S. District Judge Wendy Berger to 310 years in federal prison, followed by a lifetime of supervised release, for nine counts of production of child sex abuse material (CSAM) and two counts of possession of CSAM.  Hildenbrand was also ordered to pay $42,000 in restitution to the victims of some of the offenses. U.S. Attorney Gregory W. Kehoe made the announcement.
