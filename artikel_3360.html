‘Grand Theft Auto VI’ for PS5 and Xbox: Here’s Where to Buy the New Game Online

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Hype is at an all-time high with the upcoming release of Grand Theft Auto VI. It’s been 13 years since the last entry in the series, so gamers are itching to get their hands on the new game for Sony PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X/S on launch day.

In fact, people are actually buying the advanced Sony PlayStation 5 Pro just to play GTA 6, which features a new electronic score by Justice, in the highest picture and audio quality available. Although the game doesn’t come out until Thursday, Nov. 19, GTA 6 is already the most preordered video game of all time, with preloads active.

However, it’s not too late to get on the GTA 6 hype train. With prices starting at $79.99 for the standard edition, you can order GTA 6 from online retailers, such as Amazon, Walmart, and Best Buy. It comes as a physical edition (only game code in box) or just a straight-up download to the console of your choice.

If you do opt to go with the physical edition, boxes ship starting on Thursday, Nov. 12, with active preloads available as soon as you get the game code. But GTA 6 is actually playable on PS5, PS5 Pro, or Xbox starting on Thursday, Nov. 19.

Meanwhile, Sony also has a new Limited-Edition DualSense Wireless Controller: Grand Theft Auto VI that drops on Thursday, Nov. 19. The controller comes in blue and white, and features GTA 6 branding with palm tree grooves, Roman Numeral “VI” on the trackpad, and pink-outlined action buttons. Starting at $84 on Amazon, the wireless controller is compatible with PS5 and PS5 Pro.

Additionally, the ultimate edition, which comes with additional vehicles, bonus weapons, and sidearms, exclusive shops and locations, and other extras, goes for $89.30 (reg. $99.99) for PS5 and PS5 Pro, or $93.10 (reg. $99.99) for Xbox at LootBar.
