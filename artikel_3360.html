One Chicago Officially Breaks Tradition With Its Fall Schedule

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

It feels like just yesterday that the official 2026-2027 TV season began, but shows are already preparing for their fall finales. With Thanksgiving and the winter holidays right around the corner, networks are gearing up to wrap up their autumn runs with major cliffhangers. While most scripted hits like the One Chicago franchise will simply pause for a midseason hiatus before returning in January 2027, several shorter-order series like RJ Decker have already wrapped up. However, some shows will sign off earlier than others.

One Chicago returned for another historic season, with all three shows continuing to occupy their Wednesday time slot. On NBC, Chicago Med Season 12 leads off at 8/7c, followed by Chicago Fire Season 15 at 9/8c and Chicago P.D. Season 14 at 10/9c. The new seasons have seen some shakeups, as more cast members have left the shows, with notable departures from Chicago Fire's Joe Minoso in the 300th episode and LaRoyce Hawkins in Chicago P.D. However, plenty of characters remain, and some new ones have joined, making Wednesdays on NBC one of the best nights in television.

Fans still have a few weeks of drama and thrills left before the franchise airs its fall finales, which will break tradition this season and stretch into the final month of the year. With episode counts reduced across all three shows, fall finales in recent years have aired in late November, but this season all three shows sign off on Wednesday, December 9, 2026, before returning for their winter premieres on January 6, 2027. Chicago Med has something to look forward to with Colin O'Donell's anticipated return in the finale. When the show returns on January 6, fans can also look forward to a massive three-hour One Chicago crossover event on January 13, centered on a mysterious patient death. Meanwhile, new episodes are still scheduled for this week.

All three shows will feature their weekly cases. Chicago Med explores healthcare and citizenship as a man tries to get a green card as his wife gets treated. In Fire, Vasquez (Brandon Larracurente) and Kidd (Miranda Rae Mayo) investigate an explosion, while in P.D., the team investigates a mysterious drug dealer known as Ghost. There will also be long-running arcs, with Med's Dr. Frost (Darren Barnett) struggling with his finances and P.D. needing to be on their best behavior with Charlie Boyden (Kyle Selig) present during the investigation.
