Elden Ring: Become Lord Officially Releases January 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Elden Ring will be nearing its fifth anniversary next January, and it's become increasingly clear in the time since release that the game won't be going away any time soon. The open-world Soulslike has proven to be one of the defining games of its generation, and its longevity has been helped along by the release of the massive Shadow of the Erdtree expansion and the standalone multiplayer title Elden Ring Nightreign.

It doesn't look like another game is set to arrive any time soon — developer FromSoftware has The Duskbloods up next instead — but that doesn't mean that Elden Ring will be leaving the spotlight. Elden Ring: Become Lord takes a new look at the Shardbearers in a manga format, and the English translation of its first volume is almost here.

Per the official Yen Press website, Elden Ring: Become Lord, Vol. 1 will release on January 26, 2027, in both paperback and digital formats. As a paperback volume, the manga will retail for $20.00, while the digital version will slash the price to $9.99. Along with direct pre-orders from the Yen Store, the volume is also available for pre-order from major booksellers like Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Books-A-Million, and Kinokuniya.

Like the game, Elden Ring: Become Lord follows a Tarnished protagonist, using the character as a tool to chronicle the stories of various Shardbearers. In true FromSoft fashion, the official description promises that "the path ahead is perilous indeed, for the shardbearers will not relinquish their power without a fight." The manga art comes from Tegekiken, with the script credited to 21g and original story to FromSoftware via the game.

If you're wondering why there's such a price discrepancy between digital and physical versions of Elden Ring: Become Lord, Vol. 1, it's presumably due to the format. Unlike the typical black-and-white approach for most manga, Become Lord advertises itself as a "beautiful full-color retelling," inherently driving up the production cost of a physical copy, and it features a larger trim size of 7"x10" that's more comparable to trade paperbacks for western comics than to typical manga publication. It's a fairly standard length, however, with a listed count of 176 pages.

This isn't the first Elden Ring manga to be published, but it should offer a very different experience from its predecessor. Yen Press also released Elden Ring: The Road to the Erdtree, a comedic, gag-focused retelling of the game. Nine volumes of The Road to the Erdtree are currently available in English, with the tenth set to arrive on February 23, 2027, one day before Elden Ring's fifth anniversary. There's also Elden Ring: Distant Tales Between, which focuses on the side stories of various characters.

If you're curious to find out more about Elden Ring: Become Lord, the contents of the first volume have been officially available in Japanese for a while, so there are already plenty of opinions out there. If you'd rather just go in relatively blind for another Elden Ring adventure, thoguh, the remaining wait won't be too long now.
