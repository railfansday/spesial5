Mikel Arteta drops new William Saliba return hint with Arsenal star ‘desperate’ to play

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

William Saliba is set to make his first-team return for Arsenal in the upcoming matches.

The Frenchman missed the first three months of the season with a back injury that he aggravated at the World Cup in the summer.

Although surgery was not needed, Saliba still required a lengthy period of rest and he is yet to play a single competitive minute for Mikel Arteta’s senior side this season.

He has, however, featured in a couple of behind-closed-doors friendly matches at Arsenal’s training ground. Saliba also played 45 minutes for the Under-21s before the recent international break.

The centre-back was included in the matchday squad against both Newcastle and Borussia Dortmund but remained an unused substitute on each occasion.

Arsenal have a relentless run of fixtures coming up before Christmas, including several huge matches. The Gunners host Manchester City on Sunday as they look to put a nine-point gap between the two clubs in the Premier League title race.

Arteta indicated that Saliba is unlikely to be thrown straight into the starting lineup against City, but could expect to feature very soon.

“Obviously he hasn’t played football for a long, long time,” Arteta said at his pre-match press conference on Friday.

“He can play a certain amount of minutes, so we need to be really, really sure. Especially in that kind of game when he comes in, that he can sustain a certain amount of minutes otherwise we’re not going to have enough subs or opportunities to make those subs.

“He’s training well, he will have minutes in the next few games. Then he will prove what he’s already done, which is that he’s an unbelievable player.”

Asked how many minutes Saliba was now capable of playing, Arteta added: “Now he’s got more training, another week or 10 days, that will extend.

“It will depend as well on the type of game and the exposure that he will have throughout the game.”
