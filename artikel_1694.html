Manchester United confirm Bruno Fernandes absence ahead of Brentford clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Bruno Fernandes has emerged as a key fitness doubt for Manchester United’s Premier League clash with Brentford this weekend.

United have confirmed that their influential captain missed Friday morning’s training session at Carrington as a precaution due to feeling unwell and will need to be assessed ahead of Mauricio Pochettino’s first home match in charge at Old Trafford on Saturday.

A late decision is expected to be made on his availability for that game, with ESPN reporting that Harry Maguire is expected to be in contention to face Brentford after reporting an unspecified injury in the aftermath of Wednesday night’s chaotic 2-2 draw against Sporting in the Champions League.

Pochettino will be expected to give an update on both players when he addresses the media at Carrington in his pre-Brentford press conference at 1:30pm GMT on Friday afternoon.

“Manchester United have trained in front of the cameras in the Carrington rain on Friday morning,” United said via their official media channels.

“Mauricio Pochettino's side are preparing for Saturday's 15:00 GMT kick-off against Brentford at Old Trafford, our head coach's first home game in charge.

“Bruno Fernandes was absent from the session, after scoring both goals in the 2-2 draw against his former club Sporting in the UEFA Champions League in midweek.

“The captain is feeling a bit under the weather and is, therefore, being protected ahead of the clash with the Bees.”

The absence of Fernandes would be a significant blow for Pochettino as he seeks to achieve his first win as United boss at the third attempt, having overseen a 4-0 drubbing by arch-rivals Liverpool in his opening match at the helm at Anfield last weekend.

Leny Yoro was sent off after just eight minutes to set the tone for that one-sided contest on Merseyside, but has since seen his red card for a last-man challenge on Alexander Isak overturned by a Football Association (FA) regulatory commission following a successful appeal in a ruling that has caused plenty of controversy.

It means that Yoro will be available to play against Brentford and he trained as normal on Friday, though the likes of Marcus Rashford and Benjamin Sesko remain sidelined.

No Fernandes would add to Pochettino’s selection headache in midfield, with Carlos Baleba and Manuel Ugarte also currently out due to injury.

Youri Tielemans was controversially sent off late on against Sporting for collecting two yellow cards, but remains available for selection in the Premier League.

Winger Amad Diallo also appeared to sustain a knock during the second half of that incident-packed contest in Lisbon as he was replaced by highly-rated youngster Shea Lacey, who may yet need to take on a larger role for United against Brentford.

Luke Shaw could play this weekend for the first time since October 10, having returned to training earlier this week following a muscular injury sustained in the 1-1 draw with Tottenham and remained an unused substitute against Sporting.

Matheus Cunha will return after serving a Champions League suspension, with youngsters Karim Cassim, Victor Musa and Enzo Kana-Biyik having also trained with the first team on Friday.
