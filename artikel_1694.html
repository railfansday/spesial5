Locatelli having one of the hardest moments of career with knee injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Manuel Locatelli revealed he is going through "one of the most difficult moments" of his career as he recovers from a serious knee injury.

The Juventus captain suffered a sprain to his right knee during the team’s 1-1 draw with AC Milan back on September 6.

He then underwent surgery to repair the lateral meniscus, meaning he is enduring a lengthy spell on the sidelines and is not expected to be back in full training before March.

Locatelli made three appearances at the start of the season, registering one assist during that time, and he admitted it has been a hard process so far.

"This is definitely one of the most difficult moments, because I hurt my knee and I have to stay away from the pitch for a long time, so I'm living life in a very different way," Locatelli told TuttoJuve, via Football Italia.

"Another difficult moment was when we didn't manage to go to the World Cup.

"When you lose, and lose like that, it's a moment of suffering. But in the end, in football, you win, and you lose. The important thing is to improve and give everything you've got."

A post shared by Manuel Locatelli (@locamanuel73)

Despite his struggles, Locatelli insisted that his rehabilitation is going well as he prepares to get himself back on the pitch as soon as he can.

"I feel good, in the sense that I'm working well," he added. "I know I have to wait, and unfortunately, that sometimes happens in our lives as athletes.

"I'm approaching each day differently and doing more work in the gym. I miss playing football so much, but I've learned to accept this situation, and I know I have to do my best to come back well."

Juventus are currently sixth in Serie A after a three-game unbeaten streak (W2 D1), while they are looking to boost their hopes of an automatic qualification spot for the knockout stage of the Europa League when they host Omonia Nicosia on Thursday.
