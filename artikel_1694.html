Luis Enrique relishing busy PSG schedule before 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Luis Enrique revealed both he and his Paris Saint-Germain players are relishing their busy schedule ahead of the New Year.

After a shaky start to the season, PSG have won their last 11 matches in all competitions, the longest current run among teams in Europe's big-five leagues.

PSG sit top of Ligue 1, two points ahead of Lille, while they also lead the league phase of the Champions League after four wins in as many games.

Luis Enrique's side have four league games ahead of January, starting with a trip to Nice on Saturday, along with Champions League clashes against Roma and Aston Villa.

PSG will also take part in the Intercontinental Cup next month, and a Coupe de France match is also set to be scheduled in their calendar.

"We're looking at the coming weeks as a whole but there are lots of factors that come up on a day-to-day basis, and we need to be able to adapt," said Luis Enrique.

"We've got lots of great matches coming up before the end of the year, across several competitions, including Ligue 1, the Coupe de France, the Champions League and the Intercontinental Cup.

"We can't wait – all the players want to play and are ready to do so.

"Nice are a team in good form, winning lots of matches and unbeaten at home, with a manager who’s doing well and who’s done a good job at Lorient in recent years.

"It's always difficult to play away from home, especially after an international break, but all of that is extra motivation."

— Paris Saint-Germain (@PSG_English) November 19, 2026

Nice are sixth in Ligue 1, six points behind leaders PSG after winning each of their last three league games.

Jonathan Clauss is set to play his 200th Ligue 1 match (70 with Lens, 61 with Marseille, 68 currently with Nice).

He is the only defender to have been involved in 50 or more goals in the top-flight in the last 20 years, with 20 goals and 45 assists.

Four of PSG's last five Ligue 1 goals have been scored by defenders (three for Achraf Hakimi, one for Illia Zabarnyi).

In total, nine of PSG's goals have been scored by defenders, more than any other team in Europe's big-five leagues in the 2026-27 season.

MATCH PREDICTION: PARIS SAINT-GERMAIN WIN

Nice have failed to score in their last two Ligue 1 matches against PSG, but they have never gone three consecutive games without a goal against the Parisian club.

Nice have won five of their last six Ligue 1 matches (L1), as many victories as in their previous 34 games (D13 L16). They could record four consecutive wins in the competition for the first time since February and March 2025 (four).

PSG have scored in each of their last 23 away games in all competitions. They have scored at least three goals in each of their last four away matches (18 goals), their best such run since between April and September 2022 (nine).

Nice (seven points) and PSG (eight points) are the two teams who have gained the most points after conceding the opening goal in Ligue 1 this season.

PSG have never lost after conceding first in 2026-27 (W2 D2), while they lost all five matches in which they went 1-0 down in Ligue 1 in 2025-26.
