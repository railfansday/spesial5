Report: Real Madrid open door to massive Liverpool move, terms have been set

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Real Madrid appear to have finally opened the door to a deal with Liverpool. Certain terms have been set, even if there's still a long way to go.

There's a transfer window on the way so naturally the names 'Liverpool' and 'Real Madrid' aren't very far apart. The two clubs bump into one another every single time for one reason or another.

There were actually two reasons last summer. Real Madrid signed Ibrahima Konate on a free transfer from Anfield, bringing an end to his five-year spell with Liverpool.

Then there was a more obscure coming together. Liverpool signed Victor Munoz from Osasuna and that meant sending money to Real Madrid via a sell-on clause - something Los Blancos have been all over in recent years.

They've used them to control the futures of Nico Paz, Jacobo Ramon, and what feels like half of Como's team. Essentially, any young player they sell has some sort of clause in their contract that keeps them semi-tied to Real Madrid.

There’s essentially zero chance that Liverpool would strike a deal with a buyback. A sell-on, though? That’s very possible and we imagine it would interest the Reds if it shaved off a considerable amount from the initial price.

We’re certainly hoping that’s the route they go down. Our Value Adder model rated his time with Lyon incredibly highly, enough so that we’d consider Endrick to be a buy-at-all-costs signing, honestly.

Real Madrid have set their terms here, wanting to retain some element of control over his future. We say give them that with a sell-on clause and sign one of the most underrated talents in football today.
