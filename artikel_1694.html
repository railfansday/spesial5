Crystal Palace 2-1 Real Sociedad: Thomas Frank claims first win as Eagles complete Europa League comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Crystal Palace delivered Thomas Frank a first victory after they came back to beat 10-man Real Sociedad 2-1 in the Europa League.

Dean Henderson was kept busy in the first half, making several stops to ensure it was all to play for after the break in south London, where Frank took charge of a home game for the first time.

Mamadou Sarr broke the deadlock in the 66th minute, but the contest turned on its head when Jon Martin was sent off with a second yellow card six minutes later.

Jean-Philippe Mateta fired in the penalty before second-half substitute Dwight McNeil tapped home from Jorgen Strand Larsen’s overhead kick.

Yeremy Pino had a chance to put the game away with a stoppage-time penalty but he was denied by Unai Marrero, and the hosts had to grind out a nervy finish.

The result moves the Eagles into a play-off position, after starting the evening just outside of it, in 25th.

Frank once again elected to employ a back four, as he did in his first game in charge, their 3-2 Premier League loss at Coventry.

The first chance came after a cagey first 10 minutes when the hosts gave the ball away and it fell to Benat Turrientes, who drew Henderson into a save from distance.

Henderson was then called back into action, twice denying the ever-threatening Goncalo Guedes, who was teed up by Mikel Oyarzabal’s through ball.

Palace’s best opportunity came on the stroke of half-time via a Richards throw-in, glanced on by Axel Disasi and nodded over by Mateta.

The hosts were lucky not to concede five minutes after the restart, Guedes once again at the heart of it when he laid the ball on a plate for Oyarzabal, who was one-on-one with Henderson about eight yards out but fired straight at the Eagles keeper.

Henderson made his fifth save of the night, reacting quickly to deny Jon Gorrotxategi, and the visitors remained in the driver’s seat as the hour approached and Frank made his first substitution, replacing Eddie Nketiah with McNeil.

The breakthrough for Sociedad was the product of a patient build-up ending in Job Ochieng’s low pass for Sarr, who tapped it home.

The sides were level six minutes later. Palace were awarded a penalty after Martin – who was shown a second yellow card – tugged down Mateta, and the France striker made no mistake, burying the ball past Unai Marrero.

McNeil’s winner was the product of another acrobatic assist from fellow substitute Strand Larsen, whose scorpion kick teed up Pino at Coventry.

This time it was an overhead kick, which was heading wide of the goal but McNeil pounced at the post to poke it home.

Palace were awarded a second penalty when Soler was charged with a handball, but Marrero was equal to it, giving the visitors a fighting chance at a last-gasp leveller.

But Henderson produced his biggest save of the night, denying Orri Oskarsson at the death to preserve a much-needed Palace win.
