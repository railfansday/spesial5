Singapore Airlines Unveils New Business Class Suites With Doors, Companion Beds, And More Room To Sleep

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Singapore Airlines has unveiled its next-generation long-haul business class seat, adding sliding doors, redesigned sleeping space, and center seats that can form companion beds. The most interesting change may be the footwell: the airline has redesigned how the table stows to give passengers more room to move their feet while sleeping.

I already wrote about the new first class suites here.

The new business class product was designed by Designworks, a BMW Group company, and manufactured by the German firm RECARO Aircraft Seating. It will be installed on Boeing 777-9 aircraft and retrofitted Airbus A350-900 long-haul and A350-900ULR aircraft. Every seat will have direct aisle access in a 1-2-1 configuration.

The product will not be identical across aircraft. The Boeing version will have a wider seat, while the Airbus version will feature a larger entertainment screen.

The fully flat bed measures 79 inches long and up to 24 inches wide. Selected center seats can be configured as companion beds when the divider is lowered, which appears to be every other row.

The published bed width is “up to” 24 inches, which I read as saying there will not be a uniform 24-inch-wide sleeping surface from shoulder to foot.

The leather-upholstered seat offers adjustable upper and lower lumbar support and a leg rest easily accessible control buttons should make it easier to adjust the seat without hunting through a touchscreen menu.

Singapore Airlines is also adding a padded bedsheet incorporating memory foam, which it says can provide additional cushioning when sleeping or be used for lumbar support while seated.

Integrated myKrisWorld controls will save a passenger’s preferred seat position for future journeys. Multiple storage compartments, wireless charging, and universal A/C power will be included.

On the A350, the seat is 22 inches wide, with a 22-inch 4K OLED screen. On the 777-9, the seat measures 24 inches across, but the screen is a smaller 20-inch 4K display.

Both offer Bluetooth headphone pairing and supplied noise-canceling headphones. The entertainment handset also displays flight information, destination weather, Wi-Fi status, and lavatory availability.

The new cabins are scheduled to enter service progressively from the second half of 2027.

Singapore Airlines has also announced a progressive Starlink rollout on its A350 long-haul and ULR aircraft from early 2027. That is a separate installation program, so new connectivity will likely occur before the aircraft has new seats.
