Kai Cenat beats four GTA games in under 100 hours ahead of GTA 6 and isnt done yet

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Kai Cenat has one more game to beat before he can call his Grand Theft Auto marathon done. The streamer, with 22 million followers on Twitch, cleared GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas, and GTA IV in under 100 hours. And on Monday night, Nov. 6, he told fans on X he’d made it to the fifth and final game on his list. "PLAYING GTA 5 NOW GET IN HERE," he posted on X.

Cenat kicked off the marathon Nov. 5 with a plan to beat all five games on stream. Along the way, the games started spilling into real life. In a video posted to his YouTube channel with over 500,000 views, Cenat dressed as GTA IV protagonist Niko Bellic and took a performer dressed as Big Smoke to a McDonald's drive-thru, where Big Smoke tried to place his famously huge San Andreas order.

Now that Cenat is back in Los Santos, some viewers were hyped. "Let's gooo," said one user, while another viewer joked that he was "warming up like it's a preseason game" ahead of GTA 6, which launches Nov. 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.

For GTA 6, though, Cenat is planning to take his time. As Mashable previously reported, he wants to spend time exploring between story missions and encouraged fans to make time for their own playthroughs, too. As for when he’ll log off, he’s leaving that open. "I ain’t coming off stream until I feel like it’s the right time," he wrote.

GTA 6 launches Nov. 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, bringing the series back to Vice City. Until then, there’s still one stop left on Cenat’s tour. The marathon is streaming on Twitch.
