South Korea's top court overturns jail term for former PM Han

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Former Prime Minister Han Duck-so was convicted of helping legitimize the 2024 martial law declaration. The case will now return to a lower court.

The South Korean Supreme Court on Thursday overturned a 15-year prison sentence for the country's former Prime Minister Han Duck-soo for playing a key role in the 2024 imposition of martial law by ex-President Yoon Suk Yeol.

The case will now return to a lower court, the Seoul High Court, which had sentenced Han to 15 years in prison in May.

Han was convicted for helping plan the insurrection by suggesting that Yoon hold a Cabinet meeting to make martial law seem legitimate.

He also allegedly tried to collect Cabinet members' signatures on a related document and reviewed orders to cut off power and water to media outlets critical of Yoon.

The Supreme Court on Thursday ruled that prosecutors had not sufficiently proven the case and ordered a review of the conviction.

At the same time, the top court upheld a four-year prison term for Yoon's wife, Kim Keon Hee, on charges of corruption, including manipulating stock prices and bribery.

On December 3, 2024, former president Yoon Suk Yeol abruptly declared martial law, prompting massive protests and a showdown in parliament.

At the time, Yoon said the declaration was needed to rid the country of "pro-North Korean forces" and followed it by sending troops and police into parliament.

But enough lawmakers managed to enter the assembly chamber to unanimously vote down Yoon's decree, forcing his cabinet to rescind it.

Yoon was impeached and later found guitly of leading an insurrection and sentenced to life in prison.
