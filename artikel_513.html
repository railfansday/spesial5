How to set up a joint AdultFriendFinder account

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

There's a fun feature of AdultFriendFinder that many users sleep on. The online hookup site actually allows couples to share an account. Registering as a couple, as opposed to a singleton (particularly a solo male), helps boost your visibility on the site, since the search algorithms will treat your account as representing two people rather than one. That means it's easier for other users to find you in searches, helping you make more connections.

The process of creating a joint account is reasonably straightforward, but there are some extra steps you'll need to take to ensure it is properly registered as a couple's account.

The first hurdle you'll have to pass is the "Who would you like to connect with?" screen, which offers up four options: Man, Woman, Couple, or Trans. This screen is totally inconsequential to creating a joint account, so if, for example, you're open to meeting people of all genders, don't worry that you've limited your options by your choice here. It doesn't matter overall.

On the next screen, "Let's get your details," the site will prompt you to choose between a solo and a joint account. Under the "I am/we are a" tab, select "Couple." You'll then have to provide an answer to the "Your Orientation" screen before you can proceed. The partner you'll be using the account with will also have the opportunity to provide their own answer later down the line.

Next, you'll have to specify what kind of connection you're looking for as a couple (Love, Casual, Friends, or Hanging Out), and then provide your birthday and location, followed by your email address and chosen password.

At this point, congratulations! Your account has been created, but your profile still isn't complete.

You'll know you're on the right track when your account's blank profile photo features not one anonymous avatar, but two. If you see just a single avatar in your profile photo, you've made a mistake along the way and need to start again to create a new account.

Your work isn't done, though: Just as you filled out information about your date of birth and sexual orientation on the registration screen, you now have to do the same for the person with whom you're sharing an account (AFF does not support accounts of three or more people). It's extremely important that you fill this information out as well, because the AFF search filters will be able to parse your group account as having, for example, a bisexual man and a heterosexual woman, making the account appear in many more search results.

Let's say, hypothetically, that you meet your significant other on AdultFriendFinder and decide you want to search for a third person as a couple. Can you combine your two accounts that were created before you met? Unfortunately, the answer is no.

If you already have an AFF account and want to add another person, follow the steps above to create a new joint account.
