Whoop launches a humans-only social network — how to try

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

As AI makes its play for world domination, one dodgily rendered deepfake at a time, it’s good to know that tech companies are investing in the importance of being, y’know, actually human.

Among those tech companies are Whoop, the wearables and human performance brand, which is launching a social network called Whoop Humans. The pioneering health-and-fitness brand boasts that it’s the “most verifiably human social network”, which has been designed specifically to combat the proliferation of bots, generated AI content, and engagement bait clogging up legacy social media platforms.

Whoop Humans allows you to follow friends and family, and share fitness, health, and performance data. You can follow and support each other’s progress and better understand your own fitness and performance stats.

"In a world that's becoming increasingly artificial, we're building something deeply human,” said Will Ahmed, founder and CEO of WHOOP. “This is the most verifiably human network ever launched. Every account represents a real person with a verified heartbeat. They have real goals, challenges, and achievements. That's what makes Whoop Humans different, and it’s a big area of investment for us.”

Indeed, the point isn’t just to track and enhance your own performance but to keep track of when friends and family need a pick-me-up. Even if they don’t say, the stats speak for themselves. "Sometimes people don't tell you when they're not feeling their best,” says Whoop member Mike Bublitz. “If I see a friend's Recovery is low, I can check in and say, 'Hey, it's okay to take it easy today.' That's what I like about Whoop Humans. It gives us another way to look out for each other.

Whoop Humans is available to Whoop members from Nov. 12. You just need to have a heartbeat to join.
