Nicholas Hoult’s $50 Million Action Movie Officially Removed From Almost 800 Theaters

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Having spent much of last summer trying to get rid of the most powerful man on the planet as Lex Luthor in James Gunn's Superman, Nicholas Hoult has turned his attention to a rather different type of criminal enterprise this year, although he's at least had the help of a man who knows a thing or two about making entertaining action movies. Between Bullet Train, Deadpool 2, and Fast & Furious Presents: Hobbs & Shaw, David Leitch has been responsible for some enormously successful action movies over the last decade, but his latest is having a particularly torrid time at the box office. With Thanksgiving weekend upon us, things don't look likely to improve for Hoult and company.

According to the latest theater count figures from The Numbers, Leitch's How to Rob a Bank has officially lost 776 theaters just 12 days after its domestic release. The movie had played in 2,810 theaters across both of its first two weekends, but that number has now been reduced to 2,034, representing a loss of almost 28% of its locations.

Having made just $5.19 million domestically on its opening weekend, How to Rob a Bank suffered a 66% drop in its second weekend, falling out of the domestic box office Top 10 altogether. The movie took in just $263,167 on Monday, meaning that the average theater showing the movie made less than $100 from it across the entire day. The film holds a reported production budget of $50 million against a worldwide gross of just $17 million to date, though the feature still has plenty of time to gain some word of mouth momentum before it arrives at home.

While most people who rob banks would probably prefer that as few people as possible know who they are, the criminals at the center of Leitch's movie have made a point of turning their robberies into online entertainment. Naturally, becoming internet celebrities while committing federal crimes comes with its own set of problems, particularly when an experienced FBI agent enlists the help of a software engineer to track them down. Anything for a bit of social media clout, huh?

Written by Mark Bianculli, the movie features Hoult (Superman) as Ryan, Zoë Kravitz (The Batman) as Reagan Gardner, Anna Sawai (Shōgun) as Emi, Rhenzy Feliz (The Penguin) as Hugo, Pete Davidson (The King of Staten Island) as Vince, John C. Reilly (Step Brothers) as Agent John West, Christian Slater (Mr. Robot) as David Walton, and Tati Gabrielle (Uncharted) as Skyler Deluca.

How to Rob a Bank is currently playing in over 2,000 theaters. Stay tuned to Collider for all the latest box office news and updates.
