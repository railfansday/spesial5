Avatar: The Last Airbender Officially Confirms June 2027 Return For Zuko, Azula, & Iroh

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

If you’re a fan of Avatar: The Last Airbender’s Fire Nation characters, mark your calendar for June 2027. A new release is bringing back several fan-favorite characters, including Zuko, Azula, Uncle Iroh, bounty hunter June, and more!

Created in collaboration with Avatar Studios, Avatar: The Last Airbender – Fire and Family Treasury brings together writer Faith Erin Hicks, artist Peter Wartman, colorist Adele Matera, and Comicraft’s Jimmy Betancourt for an official comic continuation of the beloved animated series. Arriving on June 1, 2027, Dark Horse’s latest Avatar release is an omnibus that collects several of the franchise’s best Fire Nation-centric stories.

2026 has been an epic year for Avatar: The Last Airbender fans, and now fans are just three months away from the release of a Zuko-starring prequel.

In this 224-page collection, available for $29.99, fans will find three stories: Azula in the Spirit Temple (2023), The Bounty Hunter and the Tea Brewer (2024), and Ashes of the Academy (2025). If you haven’t been keeping up with all things ATLA, the main thing you need to know is that all three stories take place after the original animated series. Each is a canon-compliant, standalone adventure that offers a missing chapter the original show never explored.

As such, this collection is perfect for fans of the animated series who felt Azula’s story was unfinished and want to see what happened to her after the war, those who want to explore Zuko’s early years as Fire Lord with Mai by his side, and anyone who simply misses Uncle Iroh’s antics, especially with bounty hunter June thrown into the mix.

The main appeal of the Fire and Family Treasury Omnibus is that it collects all three stories in one neat, bookshelf-worthy format, plus brand-new cover art by Peter Wartman and Adele Matera and a bonus sketchbook section. As such, this is an ideal release for collectors, fans who love diving deeper into the ATLA universe, and readers who prefer to binge content all in one go.

As for the individual stories featured in this collection, fans can expect Azula in the Spirit Temple to continue Azula's story after the events of the original series and the postwar comics. Specifically, it follows her as she once again attempts to undermine her brother Zuko's reign as Fire Lord. However, her plans are derailed when she encounters a mysterious forest temple and is forced to confront her past.

The Bounty Hunter and the Tea Brewer turns the spotlight on Uncle Iroh and June in another postwar adventure. The story sees the bounty hunter kidnap the tea shop proprietor for the generous reward on his head. Beyond the humor, fans can also expect a truly heartfelt story as Iroh is forced to confront a painful part of his past when June turns him over to a powerful tea cartel. June is also pushed into some meaningful self-reflection along the way.

Last but not least, readers can dive into Ashes of the Academy, which further explores Zuko's efforts to reform the Fire Nation, his relationship with his younger half-sister, Kiyi, and his relationship with Mai. So, if you've ever wondered what happened to the Fire Nation characters of Avatar: The Last Airbender after the show ended, this omnibus is a can't-miss release.

Avatar: The Last Airbender — Fire and Family Treasury Omnibus TPB from Dark Horse will be available on June 1, 2027!
