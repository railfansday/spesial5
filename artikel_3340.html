Liam Neeson’s New Action Thriller Officially Finds Redemption on Streaming

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Liam Neeson and action movies go hand in hand. The star of the genre's modern masterpiece Taken is never far from another actioner, even bringing back one of the genre's funniest franchises in 2025. Neeson joined forces with Pamela Anderson (The Last Showgirl) and Paul Walter Hauser (The Fantastic Four: First Steps) in The Naked Gun, earning rave reviews for his lead performance that Leslie Nielsen would've been proud of.

"If this is the start of a new rebooted franchise's legacy, it'd be difficult to complain," wrote Aidan Kelley in his review of The Naked Gun for Collider, one of many full of praise. This sort of praise is the stuff of dreams for Neeson's latest project, which has been widely panned by critics. The Mongoose, a high-octane thriller directed by Neeson's long-time stunt double Mark Vanselow in his feature debut, was released late last month and was met with some of the worst reviews in Neeson's career.

"How long can Liam Neeson keep doing this?" said ScreenRant's Alex Harrison in a 3/10 review, in which he also called the film "a lazy mess" that is "only made enjoyable by how unintentionally funny it is." The additions of Marisa Tomei (Spider-Man: Brand New Day), Ving Rhames (Mission: Impossible – The Final Reckoning), Michael Chiklis (The Senior), and Maurice Marvel Meredith (Monarch: Legacy of Monsters) couldn't save the film from critical destruction, but its fortunes on the small screen are proving much better. At the time of writing, The Mongoose is one of the ten most-watched movies on Prime Video in the world.

Although the film was never intended to be this season's big new action blockbuster, there's no doubt that those in charge of The Mongoose will be bitterly disappointed by its box office performance. The film debuted on the big screen two weeks ago, only managing just over $400,000 from 1,014 theaters. In its second weekend, The Mongoose dropped by a staggering 83% in ticket sales, no doubt due to being removed from almost all of its locations.
