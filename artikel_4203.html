Pokémon Pokopia's Second DLC Wave Launches Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Those after even more Pokémon Pokopia content need not wait too much longer, as The Pokémon Company has announced that the game's second wave of DLC will launch next month on 9th December.

The 'Dazzling Decorations' DLC isn't quite as big as its Bubbly Basin predecessor, but it's still a very cute addition for all those who are after a little bit of 'mon customisation. The biggie this time around is a bunch of new accessories that you can stick on Ditto and your Poké pals. Did you need to see Dedenne in a pair of shades? This is the pack for you.

A few new Pokémon are included in this one, too with the likes of Skitty, Doduo, Sylveon and more all joining the fray.

Here are a few adorable snaps, so you know what to expect:

While we're a little disappointed that the second wave won't pack in another new biome, TPC has promised that there's more of that to come in Wave 3, which is set to launch at some point next year.

Remember, you'll need to have rebuilt your first Pokémon Center and completed the 'Raise the environment level' and 'Find a doll' Withered Wasteland requests before you can dive into the DLC.
