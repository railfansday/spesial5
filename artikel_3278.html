Kathryn Newton, Nina Dobrev, Adam Devine & Scott Eastwood To Star In Rom-Com ‘Sorry About My Friends’

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

EXCLUSIVE: Kathryn Newton (Avengers: Doomsday), Nina Dobrev (The Out-Laws), Adam Devine (The Righteous Gemstones) and Scott Eastwood (I Want You Back) are in production in Georgia on Sorry About My Friends, an independent romantic comedy from director Alex Hardcastle.

Plot details haven’t been disclosed. Script is by Kylie Brakeman and Patrick McDonald. Tom Butterfield is producing for Culmination Productions, alongside Gramercy Park. Peachtree and Dobrev are exec producing, with Harry White from Culmination handling sales.

Coming off production on Mike Birbiglia’s Focus Feature comedy Old Friends New Friends, opposite Lucas Hedges, Patrick Schwarzenegger and Geraldine Viswanathan, Newton will next be seen reprising her role as Cassie Lang in Marvel’s Avengers: Doomsday, out December 18. She’ll next begin production on Hulu’s Just One Day based on the Gayle Forman YA romance novels. Other upcoming projects include Hulu’s Durango, opposite Cooper Hoffman, and Roxy Sorkin’s film Hot Year. She is repped by WME, Entertainment 360 and Hansen, Jacobson, Teller. Known in film for projects like The Out-Laws and Love Hard, Dobrev most recently appeared in The Get Out, opposite Russell Crowe, Luke Evans and Teresa Palmer. Upcoming, she has the sports comedy The Streak led by Ed Helms, as well as the Hulu series You Deserve to Know, where she stars opposite Paul Wesley. She is repped by CAA and Lighthouse Management + Media.

Best known as the co-creator and star of Comedy Central’s Workaholics, Devine is coming off four seasons of HBO’s lauded comedy The Righteous Gemstones. He recently appeared in Boots Riley’s I Love Boosters and most recently signed on to star opposite SNL‘s Chloe Fineman in the crime comedy Miami PI from Michael Diliberti, as we first reported. He is repped by CAA, Avalon Television and Yorn, Levine, Barnes.

Eastwood comes to the new film with rom-com experience from Jason Orley’s Amazon flick I Want You Back. Recent projects include Regretting You, Lucky Strike and Tin Soldier. He is repped by Verve, LBI Entertainment and Goodman, Genow, Schenkman.

Hardcastle is a British filmmaker whose first feature, the Rebel Wilson comedy Senior Year, topped Netflix’s charts in more than 60 countries. He’s also a prolific television director whose credits include the pilot of Richard Lovely for Fox starring Tom Lennon and the pilot of American Woman, which was picked up to series at Paramount, as well as episodes of Grace & Frankie, You’re the Worst, Crazy Ex-Girlfriend and Superstore. He recently wrapped directing a block of Elle for Hello Sunshine and Amazon, and Einstein for CBS, and is repped by Artists First and Paradigm.

McDonald is repped by Framework Entertainment; Brakeman by Mosaic and WME.
