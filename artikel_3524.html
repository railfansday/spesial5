The best sleep tracker in the world is rendered useless if it isn’t comfortable to wear to bed. I’ve recently been testing the Apple Watch 12 and while I appreciate its in-depth sleep and activity tracking, it can feel a little cumbersome on my wrist at night — so I occasionally take it off. And that leads to inconsistent sleep data.

In contrast, the Fitbit Inspire 3 is small, lightweight and comfortable to wear to bed every night, which provides me with solid sleep data. When I started testing the Fitbit Inspire 3, I knew I kept waking up around 3 a.m.. However, building a bank of reliable sleep tracking data meant I could find out exactly what time I was waking up, and for how long. I thought my wakeups were random, but after monitoring my sleep stats in the Google Health app over the course of a few weeks, I was able to spot a developing pattern — I was waking up at 3 a.m. in the run up to my period.

At $99.95 at full MSRP, the Fitbit Inspire 3 is an affordable model — and that’s even before you factor in today’s 30% off sale. Unlike the Fitbit Air, you get a clear display screen so you can glimpse at your stats in real time. However, heading to the Google Health app will give you a closer look at your metrics (more on that below).

The Inspire 3 sensors track heart rate, blood oxygen saturation, movement and temperature, which help provide a reliable and rounded view of your sleep and activity. (For added ECG function to monitor electric heart signals, check out the Edge below). I particularly appreciate the hourly move reminders, which help me hit a minimum 250 hourly step count, which is important for overall help and building sleep pressure.In the Google Health app you'll find a breakdown of time spent in each sleep stage, your heart rate, how long it takes you to fall asleep and how often you wake up, as well as helpful parameters of healthy ranges. This all helps form your overall nightly sleep score.

Launched last month, the Fitbit Edge is the latest activity tracking wearable from Google. Like the Inspire 3, the Edge's in-built sensors track heart rate, SpO2, skin temp for reliable sleep tracking, but also offers ECG, which monitors electrical heart signals. You'll also get a bigger display screen, better clarity and more responsive touch screen. Shopping today means you'll pay full price but there's every chance it could go on sale for Black Friday.
