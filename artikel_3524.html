Apple now selling iPhone Duo replacement parts and tools

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Following last month’s launch of the iPhone Duo, which also saw the release of Apple’s official repair manuals for the device, Apple today began selling replacement parts and tools through its Self Service Repair Store. Here are the details.

Since 2022, Apple has offered its Self Service Repair program, allowing users in eligible regions to purchase replacement parts and buy or rent the tools needed to perform their own repairs on supported devices.

Just a few weeks ago, Apple began offering replacement parts and tools for several recently released devices, including the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, the latest Mac Studio, and the Mac mini.

Today, the company also added the iPhone Duo to the list, allowing owners and independent repair technicians to purchase the necessary replacement parts to perform repairs on the device themselves.
