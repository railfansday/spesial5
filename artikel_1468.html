Roma monitoring Gittens and 3 other forwards

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Roma is searching for a winger to permanently elevate the team.

The Giallorossi management will also be looking for a midfielder to complete a lineup consisting of Koné, who has been struggling with injury issues since the start of the season, Cristante, De Roon, and Pisilli.

The January transfer window could be a crucial turning point for the Roman club, who hope to secure another Malen-style signing.

D’Amico is already working to find players suited to Gasperini’s style of play whose characteristics aren’t in the current squad.

Jamie Gittens, who was already being monitored this summer, continues to be among the players being monitored.

In the next transfer window, as reported by La Gazzetta dello Sport, Roma will try to strengthen Gasperini’s squad with at least one attacking winger, preferably on loan, who can increase the team’s threat, even in transition.

Tony D’Amico’s eye is on Chelsea’s Jamie Gittens: he’s a popular prospect and was already being monitored during the summer transfer window. The Giallorossi management isn’t giving up on Real Madrid’s Endrick and is also keeping an eye on Stuttgart’s Leweling and Frosinone’s Kvernadze.
