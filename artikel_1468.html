Xavi praises Malen: “He’s in excellent form. I can’t wait to see him on the pitch.”

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Donyell Malen wants to take over the Netherlands.

A spark has been struck with Gian Piero Gasperini, and the number 14 seems unstoppable for the Giallorossi.

However, the situation is different for the national team. He struggled at the World Cup, and now Xavi must work to get him to perform at his best.

After missing for the September international break, the Spanish coach can count on the in-form center forward for the upcoming Nations League matches. Tomorrow (September 13), against Greece, the 1999-born player will play for the first time under the guidance of the former Barcelona midfielder.

At the press conference, Xavi praised Malen: “He’s training really well and is in excellent shape. He’s shown he’s perfectly at ease. He wants to show his potential. He’s giving his best in Italy. I’m trying to give him confidence because he can become important for the team.”

The former midfielder is eager to see ‘Bobby’ in action: “He has the ability to shine, create chances, be the right man, and then attack the goal at the same time. So, yes, he’s truly a player with many qualities that strengthen our team, and I can’t wait to see him on the pitch.”
