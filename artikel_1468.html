Jude Bellingham says he 'wants to be a gentleman' but admits he doesn't feel 'mature' because 'I can't cook, I can't drive and I rely on my family' in new smouldering photoshoot

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Published: 10:54 EST, 10 November 2026 | Updated: 13:36 EST, 10 November 2026

Real Madrid and England star Jude Bellingham has admitted he doesn't feel 'mature', despite being labelled as such from a young age.

The midfielder, 23, has impressed with his level head and assured public speaking since breaking onto the professional scene at 16 and living under the glare of the public eye in his formative years.

But Bellingham has confessed that his complete attention to detail as a footballer has left deficiencies in other areas of his life, such as cooking or driving.

Speaking to Vogue in an interview accompanying a fashion photoshoot in which he's dressed to fit the 60s and 70s aesthetic he leans into, Bellingham also spoke about his desire to be a role model for the younger generation.

'I feel mature in a football context because I’ve played nearly 400 games, but in a life context I don’t feel that mature now, to be honest,' he told Vogue.

'I can’t cook, I can’t drive… I’m good at what I do, but everything else? Not that great. I rely on my friends, my family to help me do stuff – chef, cleaner, mum, whoever else.

'That’s where I’m at. I work hard every day and all day, and then when I get home, I’ve probably neglected learning how to do some of the more basic things. But there’ll be a time and a place for that, I’m sure.'

Bellingham plays with a fierce competitive spirit on the pitch but is aware of his responsibility to behave away from football, adding: 'I’ve always said that, off the pitch, I want to be a gentleman.'

The Real superstar, who is in a relationship with American influencer and model Ashlyn Castro, went on: 'You can’t just behave how you want but then still represent a city or a country every time you wear their shirt, because you are inspiring [someone].

'Whether you like it or not, you are looked up to by the next generation. I prefer to see it as a privilege.'

The photoshoot for the fashion magazine showcased Bellingham's sense of style, which he says is rooted in his admiration for iconic characters from the 60s and 70s such as Elvis Presley, Michael Caine and James Bond movies of the era.

The Vogue cover shows Bellingham in a dark brown suit and coat with wide lapels and a bright orange cardigan, while he appears in a herringbone overcoat and white chino shorts in another.

Another image shows Bellingham holding up his green provisional driving licence.

Bellingham, selected in the England squad for upcoming internationals against Croatia and Spain, is in a rich vein of form.

Under Jose Mourinho's management at the Bernabeu this season, he's scored four goals and provided four assists already in LaLiga, with the Spanish giants three points behind Barcelona in the title race.

He's joined in the England squad by his younger brother, Jobe, and is flying high for the national side now his relationship with Tuchel is on an even keel.

Last Friday, the Three Lions manager said: 'It was a bit of an up and down [relationship] of course but we are in a good place.

'I love to be his coach. He's a top player, a very influential player and I think it goes both ways. If Jude was not feeling comfortable he would not perform on that level.

'In the moment you see he's happy to come into camp, he's happy with the role he has, he's fully invested and now to have the chance to have both of them is just the next step. I hope we have finished with the down. You never know as a coach!'

On Jobe being called up from the Under-21s, he added: 'Yeah Jude and I have spoken about his brother because I was aware it was always his dream to play with Jobe for England.

'Jude was always saying: "Watch him, watch him he's good, he can make it". 'I would say: "Yeah we are watching him".

'I think he actually watched training very early in my reign. He came over and watched his brother train. We chatted and said it would be nice if one day he was with him.

'He was very kind and very polite and I'm glad to have him in camp. Everyone will compare them and he has to live with this.

'They look similar, even in their movements, but they are very different players. Jobe is more defensive. I think they are also different characters, at least that's what I heard.

'He couldn't stop smiling when I called to tell him the news. He has the same beautiful smile like Jude so it's nice to have him.'
