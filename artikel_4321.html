WB All-Star Mayhem is a new multiverse mashup RPG from Kabam featuring Warner Bros. icons

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Nowadays, crossovers are all the rage, be it in movies, comic books or television. And gaming is no different! Now, Kabam are set to tackle the back catalogue of one of entertainment's biggest companies, as WB All-Star Mayhem has been announced for iOS and Android!

Set to start limited regional testing this month, WB All-Star Mayhem is a mobile RPG that sees the likes of everything from Adventure Time, Steven Universe, Samurai Jack and Scooby-Doo! cross over, as Animaniac's megalomaniacal mouse Brain gets his hands on a universal remote, with the intention of taking over each universe in turn unless someone puts a stop to him!

Of course, we all know how that turned out, with Multiversus eventually pulled from storefronts only a couple of years after launch and the studio behind it shuttered. But while Multiversus attempted to take on a genre that other studios, such as Riot Games and their ill-fated 2XKO have found is no mean feat to break into, Kabam seem to be taking a safer route.

Kabam are, of course, the team behind Marvel: Contest of Champions, a popular long-running mobile fighting game based on Marvel's superhero roster. So they've got the experience, if nothing else, to give WB All-Star Mayhem a shot at success. Will it see the same fate as Multiversus? Only time will tell.
