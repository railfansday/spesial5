10 Pokémon That Are the Biggest Red Flags

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Aden Carter is a Contributor at DualShockers who has been covering games professionally since 2020. He specializes in lists and guides, with a focus on RPGs, horror games, survival horror, Soulslikes, cozy/sim games, and action-adventure titles. Before joining DualShockers, Aden contributed to GameRant, XDA, Gamepur, I

There are over 1,000 Pokémon now and they span nine generations of games. Over the years, there have been many Pokémon designs, with some of them being fantastic, and others being rather… questionable.

With that being said, everyone has a favorite Pokémon, but not all of them convey a positive or welcoming message. In fact, some of them are serious red flags to have as your favorite.

These Pokémon will make others cringe for obvious reasons, and possibly even question your taste. Sometimes it is about the Pokémon’s look, other times the Pokédex is to blame, and you can even blame society for others.

Charizard has been around since the beginning of the Pokémon series, and it quickly became a Pokémon that was beloved by everyone. Many of us were around to see Ash take in Charmander, and help it grow into a fiery beast.

However, there are many who consider Charizard to be a big red flag when choosing your favorite Pokémon. The reason for this is that you are far too basic if this is your choice.

Of course, there is a lot of context behind Charizard being a red flag. After all, it is the Pokémon that has received some of the most attention, with it getting multiple forms and promotions. It’s almost like Charizard is the mascot of Pokémon with how much attention it gets.

From the Eevee evolutions to Magikarp becoming something useful, here are the Pokémon in FireRed and LeafGreen that feel different after evolving.

The original generation of Pokémon was something that blew the minds of many kids. However, the magic that once created something that everyone loved ended up creating some rather poorly designed monsters in later generations.

One Pokémon design that felt uninspired is the Trubbish line, as it is nothing more than a bag of garbage that evolves into a pile of trash. Garbodor is nothing more than a trash pile that many associate with being smelly.

The internet ruined Garbodor by turning it into a meme where if you like the Pokémon, you are considered dirty. On the other hand, many associate this Pokémon as a red flag because choosing it feels like someone is purposefully trying to go against the grain by choosing a less universally loved Pokémon.

In past years, if you’d ask someone who their favorite Pokémon is, there was a large sect of the fan base that would say Typhlosion. He used to be cool, but everything changed when content was leaked that involved this once-innocent Pokémon.

As part of the leak in 2024, Typhlosion - specifically the Hisuian form - received some unsettling lore that involved folklore of it shapeshifting into a human and kidnapping a young girl. There’s more to it, but I don’t want to dive that deep.

Despite the leak never containing any canon information that made it into the games, and there being mistranslations, and other issues with it, Typhlosion has never really been able to escape the allegations, and so therefore, is still considered a red flag to many in the fan base.

When you bring together many people and give them the capability of being anonymous, as they can be on the internet, they have a habit of ruining everything you once loved. While there are multiple on this list, Lopunny is the first up.

Lopunny started off as Buneary - a rather adorable rabbit Pokémon - that gets an… interesting physique when it evolves into its final stage. Many saw this transformation, went online, and then proceeded to turn the Pokémon into a symbol for provocation.

Since then, it has been frowned upon whenever someone mentions that their favorite Pokémon is Lopunny, and the addition of Mega Lopunny did not help. Instead, it made things a bit worse.

Mr. Mime was an unsettling Pokémon when kids were introduced to it in the anime. Its human-like appearance mixed with a mime is something that is uncanny. However, it has only gotten worse over the years.

Digging through its Pokédex entries, you learn that it is capable of creating physical objects that are invisible. Along with that, if you interrupt its performance, it feels the need to slap you with its big flat hands.

This alone is weird, but you can add on the allegations that Mr. Mime is Ash’s stepfather in the anime. To cap it off, Mr. Mime got even more strange with the Detective Pikachu movie, further enhancing its uncanny and unsettling nature.

Did you choose Garchomp because you like the Pokémon, or did you choose it because it is a powerhouse and you are someone who gets overly competitive and sweaty during competition? Maybe it’s because you still have PTSD from Cynthia destroying you in Diamond and Pearl?

This is often what is associated with the love of Garchomp. There is no denying that Garchomp is an absolute beast of a Pokémon that can destroy others in competition, but that also makes many believe that those who like Garchomp have a far too competitive and aggressive mindset.

The other mindset people have is that Garchomp is associated with Cynthia from Diamond and Pearl. She dominated players in that era, and the idea is that those who like Garchomp haven’t gotten over that and want to inflict the same trauma on others.

There are Pokémon who have off Pokédex entries, with some of them being focused on some sort of trauma or horror story. Drifloon is one such Pokémon, with it being a ghost Pokémon who is known for kidnapping children.

The original Pokédex entries don’t make Drifloon out to be so bad, as the Dex says that it pulls on children to steal them away, but it ends up getting yanked around instead. However, this has gotten worse with time.

More recent entries specifically state that this Pokémon kidnaps children and whisks them off to the afterlife. Other entries talk about how it is specifically filled with souls and grows larger each time it steals one away. Let’s just leave this Pokémon out of our favorites list.

Pokemon Red/Blue's Lavender Town remains one of the eeriest creations in video gaming.

The internet has a way of taking any Pokémon that has slightly humanoid features and turning them into your worst nightmare. Similar to Lopunny before it, Gothitelle fell victim to those who seek to ruin things for other people.

Again, this is another Pokémon evolution line that starts off very innocent, with Gothita being a rather cute Pokémon that is very innocent in appearance. Unfortunately, that changes when it reaches its final evolution.

There is nothing innately wrong about liking this evolution line, but it definitely calls your taste into question, and many will immediately see the red flag fly, and it is completely understandable.

I am not sure that this even needs to be said because, of all the Pokémon with a humanoid appearance, Gardevoir has been treated the worst. Originally introduced in generation 3, the entire Raltz evolution line was loved, but then things went south very quickly.

Gardevoir is a Pokémon that resembles a woman in a flowing dress. Its body consists of said dress, which is more or less a flowing gown, and it has a hairstyle that appears to be done for a pageant or evening event.

However, this is enough to get the internet riled up, and Gardevoir was the first of the Pokémon of this style to be twisted by those who just seek to ruin things for other people. Now, this Pokémon is considered one of the biggest red flags of the franchise.

Eevee was, and still is, one of the most popular Pokémon there is, with many finding the little fox-like creature to be adorable. When it was introduced, it was beloved, and so were its original three evolutions; Flareon, Jolteon, and Vaporeon.

Unfortunately for Vaporeon, it became the fascination of one of the most perverse corners of the Pokémon fan base. So much so that it was treated far worse than Lopunny, Gothitelle, and Gardivoire combined.

The information about this Pokémon soon became a meme that spread like wildfire, leading to many victims being caught in the smoke. Now, many are ashamed to even talk about Vaporeon in a positive light, as it looks bad. What was once one of the most famous Pokémon of a generation has now become the biggest red flag in the fandom.
