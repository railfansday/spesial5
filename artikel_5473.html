2026 BCDA Grand: Dupont Revival

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Teass \ Warren Architects transformed a fragmented historic residence in Washington, D.C.’s Dupont Circle neighborhood into a highly functional home tailored to the needs of a modern family of four. Initially drawn to the character, walkability, and urban lifestyle of the home, the homeowners found the existing property poorly suited for contemporary family living.

The renovation and restoration of the landmarked Victorian rowhouse sought to improve the inefficient layout, cramped kitchen, limited natural light, and poorly constructed rear addition.Working within the constraints of a narrow footprint and historic preservation requirements, the design team reimagined the organization while restoring details of the home’s original architectural character.

Award: GrandCategory: Renovations and AdditionsArchitect: Teass \ Warren ArchitectsBuilder: Fajen & BrownSize: 3,200 square feetLocation: Washington, D.C.

The renovation created space for elements typically seen in suburban homes, including a two-car parking solution, a dedicated au pair suite, a private home office, a custom mudroom with a dog wash, and a separate laundry room.

The project also focused on enhancing natural light throughout, highlighting how a historic renovation can modernize while honoring the structure’s architectural legacy.
