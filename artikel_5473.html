Zipline is expanding Walmart drone delivery after funding that values it at $21.5 billion

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Walmart is adding 300 drone sites under a multi-year deal; Zipline was valued at $7.6 billion in January, according to Bloomberg

Credit: Austin American-Statesman / Hearst Newspapers / Contributor

Zipline announced a $1.5 billion raise on Thursday at a $21.5 billion post-money valuation, and the drone delivery company is adding 300 Walmart $WMT sites under a new multi-year agreement.

Paradigm, WCM Investment Management and Layer Global led the round, the company said. Valor Equity Partners, Fidelity Management & Research Company, Uber $UBER, Tiger Global, J.P. Morgan, ARK Invest and Salesforce $CRM Ventures also took part. Zipline brought in over $600 million in January when it was valued at $7.6 billion, according to Bloomberg.

Zipline will use the money to expand its U.S. manufacturing facilities and increase hiring as it builds a nationwide drone delivery network, the company said.

Walmart announced the agreement to add 300 drone delivery sites nationwide, with potential to expand further. Walmart's drone program has completed more than 2 million deliveries, and the retailer has worked with Zipline on drone deliveries since 2021, the company said. Tens of thousands of eligible items can be delivered in as fast as 30 minutes where the service is available, Walmart said.

Zipline and Walmart operate 21 sites in Texas and Arkansas, and the drones carry packages weighing up to 8 pounds, according to Bloomberg. Walmart also has a separate drone delivery arrangement with Alphabet $GOOGL-owned Wing.

“We see customers try drone delivery for the novelty and then continue to come back to it for the convenience when they need something in minutes,” Greg Cathey, Walmart’s senior vice president of e-commerce fulfillment transformation, said in a statement.

Zipline founder and Chief Executive Officer Keller Cliffton told Bloomberg Television: “I think both we and they have been quite startled by the level of customer demand.” He added: “We are going to expand to every metro and then eventually every home in the U.S.”

Uber is targeting 1 million deliveries a day with Zipline by 2029, Zipline said. The company said its drones made more than 1 million deliveries in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex this year. In October, the median Zipline order arrived 19 minutes after checkout, compared with more than 30 minutes for traditional delivery, the company said.

Zipline said a group of its longest-tenured restaurant partners saw a 26% increase in delivery gross merchandise value, with 80% of the increase coming from Zipline orders.
