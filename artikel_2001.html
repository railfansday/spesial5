'You never know' - Martin Odegaard wants 'two huge Norway players' to join Arsenal

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Martin Odegaard's dream will be to see both Antonio Nusa and Erling Haaland join Arsenal in the coming months.

That is according to experienced former Tottenham, Aston Villa and Everton scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 27-year-old enjoys a superb connection with both players at the international level, with Nusa emerging as another superstar Norwegian talent.

The 21-year-old has enjoyed a fantastic season so far for RB Leipzig, with Arsenal thought to be among several European giants interested in signing him in the January transfer window.

Many of those suitors would undoubtedly throw their hats in the ring if Haaland was to become available at Manchester City - a potential situation that lacked any chance at all just months ago.

But with the Citizens found guilty of 114 breaches of the Premier League's financial rules and now awaiting their fate, an opening could well be forming.

Considering the battles Haaland has fought with Arsenal in recent years, it is unlikely he would even contemplate making such a move.

But if Man City are served with relegation from the Premier League and the striker does not want to leave England, there is no more realistic option.

"I think the two players Odegaard would like at Arsenal are Erling Haaland and Antonio Nusa," King told Football Insider.

"I think they would certainly be huge additions. You never know. You never know because you don't know what's going to happen to Man City, do you? It would be an upgrade for anyone, that."

He added: "Arsenal need an out-and-out centre forward, and that's why I always feel that if I was [Mikel] Arteta [I would try to sign Haaland], and Arteta knows of Haaland, of course.

"Man City, Pep [Guardiola]. You don't tell me those two don't talk still."

While on international duty, Odegaard was asked a series of questions by tv2sport.

One of them questioned which one Norwegian player he would bring to Arsenal if he had the chance.

"I can see you're trying to get some big headlines here," Odegaard replied. "No comment."

The Arsenal captain was asked if he was "chickening out" of the question, and replied: "Yes, no comment."
