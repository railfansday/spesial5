Scott Parker tells us there are positives – but talk is cheap | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

West Ham failed to overcome bottom side Preston North End in what was a desperately disappointing spectacle for West Ham fans who had been expecting so much more from their new manager’s first home game.

A scrappy first goal conceded came from pretty much Preston’s only attempt at goal, and then our visitors sat back in time-honoured tradition.  Four times the Hammers crashed the ball against the woodwork in a first half which showed plenty of promise, however the second half evaporated in familiar fashion, inexplicably, no sooner than Mavropanos crashed in the equaliser.

Just about every fan expected West Ham to go on and win the game convincingly – yet instead they huffed and puffed with endless ‘after you – no after you’ short, sideways passes as nobody took responsibility for making the killer pass or – heaven forbid- taking on the shot.

However, the West Ham manager will no doubt do his best, tomorrow maybe, to pull out positives, despite being ‘devastated‘ at the result:

Edson Álvarez, in his first game this season, El Machin was, surprisingly, up to speed pretty much immediately, sitting in front of the defence. He was everywhere: collecting, tidying up, harrying, attacking and breaking down opponents’ attacks. The late-tackling liability of last season was nowhere to be seen as he impressed and looks as if he has found himself a role for the coming weeks.

However, there’s a caveat: Remember this is the bottom side in the division who have just held us to draw. It will be intriguing to see if he copes against tougher opponents.

Edson Alvarez -Looked in control this afternoon – of his own discipline and of his midfield patch.

Finn Herrick: It can only have been unsettling, as the young keeper came to punch his first corner only to find a team mate’s glancing header got there first, looping the ball over his outstretched fists and allowing Preston to open the scoring against the run of play.

Credit to him, Herrick kept his cool and grew in stature as the game went on. He made an excellent double save after Mayers’ poor defensive header, and played well with the ball at his feet, combining Areola’s shot-stopping ability and Hermansen’s football skills.

However, positives aside, Parker has his work cut out simply to get this team’s work rate up. This time, they started quick and could have had three in the first half, even though there was a hesitancy and a lack of creation in front of goal.

Why it evaporated no sooner than the Hammers had equalised was a mystery. What should have become a barrage launched on Preston’s goal became a last thirty minutes of frustrating misfiring.

Just. Not. Good.Enough. Despite the bright points which Parker referenced in his post – match conference.

Just don’t blame West Ham’s newest manager for the failure of the last three to demand pacy, attacking football and recruiting so-called strikers who wouldn’t have made it into West Ham’s championships side of 2004/5 or 2011/12.

Can’t make a silk purse out of a sows ear. Especially not in two weeks.
