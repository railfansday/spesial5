“Proven goalscorer” claims for January begs one huge question | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

West Ham’s woes seem to extend to every part of the pitch : Goal keeper issues, right back problems, no creative midfielder, no pacy right winger, no striker: Where to start. Suddenly everyone is playing like a fifteen year old in a school second XI and Nils Koppen’s shopping list for January is enormous.

At present, the priority has to be someone who can put th ball in the net: As I wrote this morning, conceding one goal away to Portsmouth wasn’t the disaster. The real problem was not replying with two or three goals of our own. And that in a nutshell is where the West Ham wheels have come off.

footballinsider247.co.uk make their claim that:

West Ham United are set to prioritise a move to bring in a regular goalscorer in the January transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.”

Signing “proven goalscorers’ at West Ham hasn’t proved universally successful

But there’s a clear question emanating from that: Who in their right minds would choose to come to a club in crisis like West Ham United next month?

Heading into relegation form, sliding down the league, five managers inside three years, fans booing the players off the pitch each week…The only proven goal scorer likely to head to east London will be one very much past his prime looking for one last payday.

And we’ve had plenty of those in recent years. None of whom have proved fit for purpose.

Nils Koppen may be good at his job, but he’s no magician. If the Hammers cannot arrest their slide and start moving up the table ahead of the window opening in January, the transfer window is likely to offer very little promise for the club or supporters.
