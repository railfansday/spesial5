‘Tremembé’ Co-Writer Ullisses Campbell Lands Amazon MGM Overall Deal As Studio Expands Brazilian True Crime Slate

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

EXCLUSIVE: Amazon MGM Studios has inked an overall deal with Ullisses Campbell, the award-winning Brazilian journalist and crime writer, as it digs deeper into the true crime genre in Brazil.

Campbell is well-known in Brazil, and is one of the writers on Amazon MGM Studios’ prison drama Tremembé, which has been a hit for Prime Video in Brazil. Now the studio has locked Campbell into a deal that will see him work across its whole true crime slate as well as on new projects.

“Our country is home to extraordinary stories, many of which are stranger than fiction,” said Campbell. “Brazil’s history is also told through its crimes. Many of these cases reflect the customs, prejudices, inequalities and social shifts of their time. When screen adaptations revisit these events, they also create a historical record and help preserve our collective memory. That is why I see true crime as a lens to understand who we are and how we got here.” Related Stories News Everything We Know About Amazon MGM & Sony Pictures TV's 'God Of War' Adaptation So Far Casting 'Upgraded 2' Film Casts Tanner Zagarino, Christopher McDonald, Andrea Arcangeli & Alessandro Preziosi

Campbell wrote a series of books about Tremembé, a real-life prison that has housed some of Brazil’s most notorious criminals. Inmates have included Suzane von Richthofen, Elize Matsunaga and the Cravinhos brothers. The drama series Tremembé, which Campbell co-wrote with Vera Egito, Juliana Rosenthal, Thays Berbe and Maria Isabel Iorio, is heading into Season 2. Watch on Deadline

“It is a franchise, definitely,” Julia Priolli, Head of Creative for Brazilian Originals at Amazon MGM Studios, told Deadline about expanding the Tremembé IP. She added there will be some infamous new inmates in the upcoming run of the show. “For Season 2, we’re bringing five new characters, including Robinho, the football player [imprisoned after being convicted of rape].”

Campbell will exec produce Season 2 of Tremembé, which launches in 2027. Speaking more broadly about the Amazon MGM deal with the journalist, writer and producer, Priolli said he will have input into the true crime docs, series and films the studio is working on.

“It could be as an exec producer, or sometimes it will be doing research, because he’s very strong when you need to go deep into aspects of a story and he really has great access,” she said. “It doesn’t matter if we’re talking about a documentary, or if it’s a movie or a scripted series, we want his input across everything. We’re talking to him about some new shows that we haven’t announced yet, but basically, we want him across the whole true crime slate.”

Priolli explained that Amazon has linked up its production, streaming and publishing businesses in Brazil as it pushes deeper into ripped from the headlines crime content.

“We tried to identify, here in Brazil, what would be the best true crime stories for us and we wanted to use the whole flywheel that we have at Amazon for telling these stories. We not only have the studios where we could do a movie or a documentary or a scripted series, but we also have Audible, where we can do those as audio, we have Kindle, we have Amazon Books.”
