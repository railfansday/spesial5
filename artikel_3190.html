Logitech: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Operating margins are expected to reach the high end of management's long-term range despite component disruptions and rising costs.

On its second-quarter fiscal 2027 earnings call, held on Oct. 27, 2026, Logitech (LOGI +7.83%) announced a 2% constant-currency net sales increase and $231 million in non-GAAP (adjusted) operating income. Market share gains across core peripherals and sustained demand for workplace video gear continue to protect profitability during a broader downturn in personal computer shipments. Three things management said stand out.

Logitech increased sales of pointing devices and keyboards while global personal computer (PC) shipments shrank 20%. Rather than relying on new computer purchases, the business expands market share by selling upgraded peripherals directly to the existing installed base.

Net sales in video collaboration (VC) grew 12% in constant currency during the second quarter. Mandated return-to-office policies and mandatory upgrades for aging hardware continue to create non-discretionary enterprise demand.

An accident at a third-party semiconductor supplier threatened to disrupt up to $200 million in sales. Operational workarounds reduced the third-quarter top-line headwind to between $70 million and $80 million, with normal operations returning by the fourth quarter.

Meeting room upgrades remain firm while high-end demand protects profitability. Manufacturing diversification and pricing discipline help absorb rising freight and component costs.

Innovation across personal workspace and gaming categories continues to drive long-term expansion.
