Botman banned from driving for using phone at wheel

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Newcastle United defender Sven Botman has been banned from driving

Premier League footballer Sven Botman has been banned from driving after being caught using his mobile phone at the wheel.

The Newcastle United centre-back pleaded guilty to using a handheld mobile phone while using a motor vehicle on a road at Newcastle Magistrates' Court on Wednesday.

The 26-year-old was given six penalty points and disqualified from driving for six months, as these were added to points he had already accrued.

He was fined Â£900 and ordered to pay Â£400 costs and a Â£360 victim surcharge, the MoJ said.

Botman was driving a Land Rover on the A189 Haddricks Mill Road, in Newcastle, on 17 September when he was spotted using his phone.

The Dutchman changed his plea from not guilty to guilty at his court appearance on Wednesday, the MoJ confirmed.

Newcastle United signed Botman from Lille in 2022. He has made nine appearances so far this season, scoring one goal.
