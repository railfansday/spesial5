A Guide to the Best Black Friday Streaming Deals: See Platforms, Prices & Plans for Less

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Enjoy major bargains across several streaming platforms for Black Friday, with monthly plans as little as $1 and discounts when you purchase a holiday movie.

Black Friday is a great time to save on streaming, whether you’re looking to catch up on hit original shows, watch new docuseries or cut the cost of your monthly subscriptions. This year, deals are available across DirecTV, Prime Video, Apple TV, Paramount+, STARZ, Fubo and Frndly TV. Prices are as low as just $0.99 per month with Frndly TV, while STARZ is offering subscriptions for as low as $1 per month with its annual deal. Other highlights include savings of up to 53% on Apple TV. Whether you’re after blockbuster movies, exclusive originals or live sports and news, there’s a deal worth exploring. Here’s how the offers compare and what you need to know before subscribing.

Prime Video is offering Black Friday savings on several popular streaming add-ons, including Paramount+ for $2.99 per month for two months, Apple TV for $6.99 per month for six months, Hallmark+ for $2.25 per month for three months, AMC+ Premium for $2.75 per month for two months and MGM+ for $1.99 for two months. Eligible for regular Amazon account holders, all you have to do is be signed into your account to purchase the discounted streaming service. Discounts also extend to select bundles and movie purchases, with subscription savings starting at just $1.25 per month on holiday classics like The Grinch and The Polar Express.

Popular entertainment on Amazon Prime Video: Ted Lasso (Apple TV), Dutton Ranch (Paramount+), Santa Tell Me (Hallmark+) and live NFL games (Paramount+).

DirecTV is making it easier to bring multiple streaming services and live TV together in one place this holiday season. Its MyEntertainment Genre Pack includes Disney+, Hulu, HBO Max and AMC+ alongside more than 60 live TV channels for $34.99 per month for the first two months, down from $42.99. You can also explore the service with a five-day free trial. The offer ends Jan. 5, 2027.

Popular entertainment on DirecTV: Avengers: Endgame (Disney+), Heartland (Netflix) and War (HBO Max).

You can save more than 50% on Apple TV with this Black Friday offer through Prime Video. Amazon account holders can get six months of access for just $6.99 per month, compared with the regular $14.99 monthly price. You don’t need an Amazon Prime membership to claim the deal, either. Just sign in with an Amazon account before the promotion expires on Nov. 30, 2026.

Popular entertainment on Apple TV: Severance, Ted Lasso, Silo and Widow’s Bay.

Popular entertainment on Paramount+: The JLo Show: Live in Las Vegas, Tulsa King and South Park.

Popular entertainment on Starz: Outlander, Spartacus and Black Sails.

Fubo is offering discounts on two of its live TV packages this Black Friday. The Core plan costs $63.99 for the first month after a free trial, down from $88.99, and includes 208 channels such as ABC, CBS, FOX and NBC. For a wider selection, the Ultra plan is $68.99 instead of $98.99, with 272 channels. Both offers include a five-day free trial.

Popular entertainment on Fubo: Dancing With the Stars (ABC), Chicago Bears vs. Detroit Lions Thanksgiving game live (CBS) and Home Alone (Disney+).

Frndly TV is offering its Premium plan for just $0.99 per month for the first three months, down from the regular $12.99 monthly price. The deal includes more than 50 live channels, four simultaneous screens, unlimited DVR and HD streaming. Available until Dec. 1, 2026, the promotion is a budget-friendly way to access live entertainment. After three months, the subscription renews at $12.99 per month.

Popular entertainment on Frndly: The Curse of Oak Island ( History Channel), The First 48 (A&E) and Married at First Sight (Lifetime).
