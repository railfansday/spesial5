Creator Kennedy Eurich to Launch YouTube Series (Exclusive)

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The digital series will debut in early 2027 on YouTube and across social media platforms.

Creator Kennedy Eurich is launching a new digital series on YouTube, The Hollywood Reporter has learned.

The variety series, currently titled the Untitled Ken Eurich Project, is set to debut in early 2027 and will be distributed on YouTube and other social media platforms. The show will feature a mix of guests and hidden camera segments. The creator has secured independent financing to the tune of seven figures.

Eurich, known to followers as Ken, has nearly 2 million followers on TikTok alone, with much of her content focusing on speaking directly to the audience content. She has a secondary account with over 800K followers on TikTok, with another half a million followers on Instagram.

Like many creators working today, she’s been no stranger to brand deals. She’s partnered with brands like Cetaphil, Redken, Celsius, Coach and Tory Burch and partnered on ads with DoorDash, Popeyes and Torchy’s Tacos. She’s also hosted and done content for Tory Burch’s SS27 runway show and the 2025 Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show.

The creator will allocate a portion of the show’s revenue to animal welfare initiatives through the Dallas-based Camillo Foundation, a private 501(c)(3) grantmaking foundation, according to a release.

Eurich is represented by CAA, OS Talent and Brecheen Feldman Breimer Silver and Thompson.
