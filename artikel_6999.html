Meg Ryan’s Former Montecito Estate Sells for $18 Million

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The actress oversaw a major renovation and expansion to the house.

Famed Hollywood actress and serial home renovator Meg Ryan made a splash over the summer, selling her remodeled Hamptons retreat for its full asking price of $15.25 million, mere weeks after putting it on the market. Now, another one of the You’ve Got Mail star’s renovation projects just traded hands, per Realtor.com.

Ryan’s former estate in Montecito listed in August 2025 for $21.9 million, two months after she parted ways with it. The Southern California property sold for $18 million on October 1—following a nearly ten percent price chop—according to records from Zillow.

After acquiring the residence for $9.5 million in 2021 through an LLC, Ryan oversaw a major renovation and expansion. She enlarged the house to its present 6,500-square-foot size, adding an entryway with a porch and swinging chair. Other perks now include an elevator servicing all three floors, a media room, an outdoor back patio, guest quarters set atop the three-car garage, and a trio of fireplaces added by Ryan.

She offloaded the home in June 2025 for $16.8 million after having listed it for $22.5 million that February.

Set on one of Montecito’s most coveted streets, the two-acre estate is within easy reach of the beach and features mountain vistas. It’s also not far from celebrity neighbors like Prince Harry and Meghan, Duchess of Sussex and Gwyneth Paltrow.

Though only built in 2010, the house itself has an illustrious history of onetime occupants. First was late mystery novelist Sue Grafton and her husband, Steve Humphrey, who were the original owners. The couple later sold the home to Alicia McFarlane, the granddaughter of Hollywood icon John Wayne.

Ryan has a well-documented passion for flipping homes. “I feel like I’ve been renovating houses my whole life,” she once told ELLE Decor. “I’ve renovated apartments, lofts, a log cabin in Montana—just tons and tons of places. I’m constantly ripping pages out of magazines in search of inspiration. I’ve found ideas everywhere, from a friend’s kitchen to a hotel lobby.”
