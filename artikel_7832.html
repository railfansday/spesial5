Convicted Murderer Sentenced to More Than 20 Years for Armed Carjacking and Witness Tampering

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

TULSA, Okla. – U.S. District Judge Sara E. Hill sentenced Christopher Ray Barrett, 41, to 246 months in federal prison for carjacking, brandishing a firearm during a crime of violence, possessing a firearm and ammunition as a convicted felon, and conspiring to tamper with witnesses. Barrett pleaded guilty to the charges in November 2025.
