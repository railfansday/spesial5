The Business Shield is U.S. Bank’s no-annual-fee card for a business that wants time at 0% interest to pay off a big purchase or a balance moved from another bank’s card. U.S. Bank no longer takes applications for its Business Platinum card, whose page now redirects to the Business Shield page.

Get the Business Shield only if a U.S. Bank branch offers more than the 0% for 12 billing cycles it gives online, since branch applicants got 18 billing cycles when the card launched in February 2026.
