The FDIC puts the national average savings rate at 0.39% APY, measured on a $2,500 balance. APY, or annual percentage yield, is the percentage a year of interest adds to your balance, compounding included. At that rate, $10,000 earns about $40 in a year, and at 4.25% it earns about $425. Accounts that pay more only in a month you meet a deposit rule are grouped in a band of their own. Money you want to reach by check or debit card belongs in one of the best money market accounts instead. Money you won’t need for several months or more can lock in one of the best CD rates.
