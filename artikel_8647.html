Get Acorns Early, formerly GoHenry, if your family pays for Acorns Gold, which includes the kids’ card, chore tracking, and lessons at no extra cost. Without Gold, its Lite plan costs $8 a month, so compare cheaper kids’ debit cards before you sign up.

Acorns bought GoHenry in 2023 and renamed its kids’ debit card and app Acorns Early in November 2024. Kids ages 6 to 18 get their own card, while parents load it, pay allowance, and set spending rules from the parent app.

Families already on Acorns do best with Gold, which costs $12 a month with the card included, the same as Bronze ($4) plus Lite ($8) and less than Silver ($8) plus Lite. Families who don’t use Acorns pay $8 a month for up to four kids, while Greenlight Core charges $5.99 a month for up to five, chores and allowance included.
