The 3 summer targets Derby County should pursue again in January, including Anis Mehmeti

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Derby County have had a tough season, but entered the November international break on a high by beating Bolton Wanderers in their last game. It buys John Eustace more time in the dugout as DCFC look to turn around their fortunes.

The Rams will have eyes on the January transfer window as an opportunity to strengthen their depleted squad. Injuries have forced them into the free agents market, signing Shane Duffy and Jack Robinson, but more long-term fixes can be found by targeting some of the summer’s targets who slipped through the fingers.

Here, we put forward three summer targets who Derby County should have back on their radar.

Derby County were among the clubs interested in signing Mattie Pollock from Championship rivals Watford in the summer. However, he ended up staying at Vicarage Road and was named as club captain.

Pollock has publicly spoken about how earning the captaincy made him feel confident that he was in the right place. But, with the Hornets struggling and doubts surrounding their long-term direction amid the Pozzo family’s ownership, a new opportunity could be tempting this January.

The Rams may not be in a better place in the league, but there is more hope for long-term prosperity at Pride Park than there is at Vicarage Road. Paying to sign Pollock as the present and future of their backline would be a statement of intent and a big step in the right direction away from signing older players.

Another player on the Derby County radar was Anis Mehmeti, but like Pollock, he stayed put. The Albanian had wide Championship interest after Ipswich Town’s rise to the Premier League, but he decided to compete for his place at Portman Road.

That has not panned out as hoped, and the attacking midfielder should look to make a winter exit. Mehmeti is a proven star in the Championship, offering a dual threat of goals and assists as a No. 10 or on the left-wing.

He could be the creative spark Eustace’s Rams side needs. Entrusting him with an influential role in the team could help DCFC win the race for his signature, as other second tier sides are sure to be interested.

Last but not least is James Tavernier, who some supporters may view as an underwhelming addition. Granted, he is now 35, but the experience and leadership he would bring to an unstable right-back position would not go amiss.

Tavernier only joined Maccabi Tel Aviv in the summer, but his deal is up at the end of the season, and he could be tempted by the chance to end his career back in England. He would also bring impressive set-piece ability with him, offering Derby County a much-needed route to chances.

Tavernier might not be a long-term option but it would take the pressure off a struggling Max Johnston and decrease the reliance on the likes of Joe Ward and Ryan Nyambe.
