West Ham v Cardiff City | The numbers that shout loud and clear for one particular change in Nuno’s line-up

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

This week should never have become a narrative of two tough away fixtures becoming ‘must win’, but with West Ham United kissing goodbye to six points at London Stadium in the last seven days, there’s now an urgency to try and claw them back.

Cardiff City on Wednesday and Blackburn Rovers on Saturday will offer stern tests once again, with opponents now emboldened and egged on by the Hammers’ very well known first half frailties.

Both teams will more than likely launch a Millwall-style opening half hour storm which the Irons will have to withstand. They cannot say they haven’t been warned.
