"It can take years": Why the AI Riemann proof is far from settled

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

AI claimed another major scalp in maths on Friday, as Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge published what he says is a proof of the Riemann hypothesis, a Millennium Prize Problem, in 17 million lines of code that no human has read in full. Physicist Tony Padilla tells Tech 24 that verification could take years, and that Alpöge should share how he got there.
