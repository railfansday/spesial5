Gerard Depardieu to stand trial in France for rape and sexual assault

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Paris Court of Appeal upheld the order for the 77-year-old French actor to stand trial on charges of raping and sexually assaulting an actress in 2018.

French actor Gerard Depardieu failed in his attempt to overturn an order for him to stand trial on rape charges.

On Thursday, the Paris Court of Appeal ruled that Depardieu should face trial for the alleged rape and sexual assault that occurred on August 7, 2018. However, the court dismissed the allegations relating to a second incident that occurred six days later.

Depardieu still has the option of appealing to a higher court. His lawyer, Delphine Meillet, told the Associated Press that she disputes Thursday's ruling, though she did not specify whether she will challenge it in France's highest civil and criminal court.

This decision comes just two weeks after the 77-year-old Depardieu dropped his appeal against a sexual assault conviction in a separate case. He accepted an 18-month suspended prison sentence and issued a public apology.

An actress, who was 22 years old at the time, accused Depardieu of raping and sexually assaulting her at his Paris apartment on August 7 and 13, 2018. Depardieu was 69 at the time of the alleged incident.

She reported the allegations to the police a few days later. Depardieu denies the allegations, claiming that the encounter was consensual.

Last year, a trial was ordered regarding the allegations. If the trial goes ahead, Depardieu could face up to 20 years in prison.

Carine Durrieu Diebolt, the victim's lawyer, said that the Thursday decision was a genuine relief and a victory for her client.

Depardieu was born in 1948 into a working-class family in Chateauroux, a city in central France. He overcame a speech impediment to become one of France's most prolific actors.

The film star was a dominant figure in French cinema for half a century, becoming a renowned cultural export. He earned an Oscar nomination for "Cyrano de Bergerac" and a Golden Globe for "Green Card."

Accusations of sexual misconduct and his subsequent conviction, however, have increasingly overshadowed his cinematic legacy.

The international media has also been paying attention to Depardieu's off-screen antics. One of the most notable instances was when he accepted a Russian passport from President Vladimir Putin in 2013 amid a dispute over French taxes.
