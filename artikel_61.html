European leaders gather in Dublin to discuss Ukraine's security, and their own

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Leaders from more than 40 European countries gathered in Dublin on Thursday, November 12, for a summit focused on the continent’s fragile security, amid a new US-backed effort to revive talks on ending the Russia-Ukraine war. The meeting of the European Political Community brought together heads of state and government from European countries and the UK, as well as Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky, Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney and NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte.
