Disney Hitches Its Holiday Campaign to an Invisible Horse

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Whether it’s penguins or puppies, holiday ads starring cute animals rarely miss. Now, Disney is banking on this winning formula with a twist, introducing us to a horse nobody can see.

“The Invisible Horse” tells the story of Jack, a boy who’s convinced he’s outgrown Christmas. Then a mysterious, invisible creature wanders into his life, pulling him into an adventure that rekindles his imagination and draws him closer to his family.

The three-minute short was directed by Dougal Wilson, the filmmaker behind Paddington in Peru and a few festive tearjerkers from U.K.
