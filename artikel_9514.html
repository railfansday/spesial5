Chicken with Cajun Cream Sauce

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Pan-fried chicken in a luscious Cajun cream sauce, served with mashed potatoes and green beans – SO cozy! I love this meal!

Published by Lindsay Ostrom on September 17, 2026

This was so so so good! Added into my weekly rotation!! My kids didn’t find it spicy! Big win!

That tomatoey, creamy, shallot-infused gravy is something I haven’t been able to stop thinking about.

I made this dinner for my family on the first barely chilly day a few weeks ago. I think my body had just been waiting for permission to go fall cooking mode, and I just unleashed.

Pan-fried chicken. Luscious, savory Cajun cream sauce. Homemade mashed potatoes. Green beans. Apple crisp for dessert.

I served it in deep bowls and we let the girls eat on the living room floor, using the coffee table as their table, while we watched American Ninja Warrior together with the windows open – which, frankly, is the move of two parents who are feeling extra generous and a little out of their mind with the joys of early fall weather. But that meal will be a top 5 meal for me this year, for sure. We all devoured in silence and then cozied up with full bellies to watch some ridiculous TV together.

Here’s an idea I keep coming back to: shrimp. Like what if you just made the sauce, and then threw a pound of uncooked jumbo shrimp in there? It would poach right in the sauce! I haven’t been able to pull myself away from the chicken and mashed potatoes combination, but I feel shrimp should at least be considered.

Hope this recipe gives you all same cozy fall feelings it gave me.

Also a big fan of using the air fryer here, if you’re not feeling the pan-frying.

This picture shows the shallots minced; I also really love to do this with a thinly sliced shallot, sliced on a mandoline. The shape will give you these little ribbons through the sauce.

Add cream and broth, and simmer till it’s GLORIOUS.

I mean, showstopper material right there.

My favorite combo for serving is mashed potatoes and green beans. LIGHTS OUT. So good.

To air fry the chicken: Air fry at 375 for 10-12 minutes, or until at least 165 degrees internal temperature.

Roasted Potatoes and Broccoli (Oven): Toss 3-4 cups halved baby potatoes with avocado oil and salt on a sheet pan. Roast the potatoes at 425 for 15 minutes. Scoot the potatoes over, add 3-4 cups broccoli and ¼ cup water to the same sheet pan, and roast for another 15 minutes.

Roasted Potatoes and Broccoli (Air Fryer): Toss 2 cups halved baby potatoes with avocado oil and salt. Air fry at 375 for 12 minutes. Add 2 cups frozen or fresh broccoli; air fry at 400 for another 10-12 minutes.
