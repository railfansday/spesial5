Forget Hassan: O'Neill Must Drop Carter-Vickers for Celtic vs Besiktas

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Celtic are looking to bounce back from the disappointing defeat to Falkirk as they aim to avoid a fourth successive Europa League loss against Besiktas at Parkhead on Thursday night.

Celtic's second league defeat of the season came in shocking circumstances as Falkirk beat them for the first time in 19 years, with the Bairns' last victory against the Hoops coming in 2007.

The Bhoys remain four points clear at the summit of the Scottish Premiership, although second-place Rangers do have a game in hand on their Old Firm rivals.

Martin O'Neill was understandably disappointed with the result on Sunday, which followed the 2-2 draw at home to Dundee United.

While their league form has generally been pretty good, it's been a different story in Europe.

Celtic have lost all three of their Europa League matches against Ferencvaros, Benfica and Celta Vigo, with the clashes against the Hungarian and Spanish sides being at their Celtic Park home.

Next up for O'Neill's men is another home tie against Turkish giants Besiktas, with the Glasgow outfit currently lying second bottom of the 36-team table.

There was a lot to be unimpressed with from the loss to Falkirk, and the veteran Northern Irish manager may well be considering making some changes against Besiktas as they look to pick up their first points of the campaign.

Haissem Hassan's place could be under threat, even though O'Neill has admitted that the Egyptian winger could have a better output had chances not been squandered by his teammates in the opening months of the campaign.

Still, the £6m signing has no goals and two assists in his first 16 appearances for Celtic, and that number needs to increase if he is to justify a spot in the starting XI.

The 24-year-old's place in the team on Thursday shouldn't be the only one under threat though.

The jury is still out on whether the centre-back partnership of Liam Scales and Dane Murray is good enough to be starting consistently for Celtic.

O'Neill decided after the 2-2 draw with Dundee United that he was going to reunite the title-winning partnership of Scales and Cameron Carter-Vickers against Falkirk, but it was a decision that didn't pay off.

Celtic were beaten 2-0 and while the two centre-backs weren't the only poor performers on the day, they came away from the game with little praise.

It's only been six weeks since Carter-Vickers came out and criticised the team's lack of identity following the 1-0 home defeat to Rangers, as he appeared to hit out at O'Neill's tactics.

The 28-year-old had the perfect opportunity to do his talking on the pitch against Falkirk, but instead he was handed a 2/10 rating by 67 Hail Hail for his performance.

The Englishman was given the worst rating of any Hoops player, and it was described as one of his worst games in his 185 appearances in a Celtic shirt.

Former Celtic striker Chris Sutton has questioned defender Cameron Carter-Vickers after his recent displays.

He repeatedly made errors against Falkirk, and all it showed was that the club needs to invest in the centre-back position in 2027 if they want to improve.

O'Neill now has a decision to make on the Carter-Vickers and Scales partnership ahead of the visit of Besiktas, and the temptation might be to go with experience over Murray.

However, the 74-year-old manager should give Murray another chance against the Turkish outfit to see if he can have a long-term future at Parkhead, in a competition from which they will already struggle to qualify for the next stage.
