Dolphins DT Jordan Phillips will undergo season-ending biceps surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Dolphins defensive tackle Jordan Phillips injured a biceps in Sunday's loss to the Colts.

Marcel Louis-Jacques of ESPN reports that Phillips will undergo season-ending surgery.

The Dolphins announced they have placed Phillips on injured reserve.

A Dolphins' fifth-round pick in 2025, Phillips has played 60 percent of the Dolphins' defensive snaps this season. He was expected to have an even bigger role after Zach Sieler's season-ending biceps injury.

Defensive tackle Zeek Biggers was also lost for the season on Sunday, injuring the lateral meniscus in his knee.
