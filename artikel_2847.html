Clippers vs. Spurs takeaways: Victor Wembanyama adds to highlight reel

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Victor Wembanyama and the San Antonio Spurs remain the standard in the Western Conference and will continue to get everyone's best shot this season.

The Spurs served as a tough test for the Los Angeles Clippers. They gave the Spurs their best shot in a 124-112 loss in San Antonio on Thursday, Nov. 19. Wembanyama led the game with 32 points and 11 rebounds for the Spurs.

It was a bounce-back win for San Antonio, which had a 12-game win streak snapped against the Detroit Pistons earlier in the week.

The Spurs were missing Dylan Harper, who was out with a hip rotator strain, on Thursday. He avoided serious injury but is expected to miss at least a week.

Here's some takeaways from Thursday's game:

Wembanyama can simply do things that others can't.

His latest example came with 3:36 left in the fourth quarter against the Clippers on Thursday. Rui Hachimura's missed shot was rebounded by Stephon Castle, who threw the ball right down the court to a wide-open Wembanyama.

While Wembanyama could've dunked the ball, he seemed to settle for a jump shot inches from the rim.

Wembanyama laughed when asked about the play and stated that dunking has led to a blister on his hand.

"I'm not going to lie, I've had this blister forever and it's killing me," Wembanyama told reporters after the game. "Every time I dunk, it kills me. … It's actually so painful."

While the Clippers still have a lot of work to do if they want to be in a position to compete for a play-in spot later this season, coach Tyronn Lue's team showed some promise and potential against the West's best on Thursday.

L.A. shot the ball well for most of the game and kept the game close behind the effort of veterans Darius Garland and Brook Lopez. Both scored 23 points each.

Garland's had high-scoring performances early this season, but turnovers have been a problem for the guard. He managed to limit his mistakes Thursday, having just one turnover on a big stage against the Spurs.

Max Strus went 5-for-10 from the field, including 5-of-9 from the 3-point line for 18 points.

Clippers center Yanic Konan Niederhauser made his season debut. The second-year player was dealing with a right foot Lisfranc injury and was limited to 11 minutes against the Spurs due to a minutes restriction. He went 1-for-4 shooting and finished the game with two points, three rebounds and a steal. He did find himself in foul trouble with a team-high five personal fouls.

While the Clippers will have to figure out the center rotation following Niederhauser's return, Lue has encouraged Isaiah Jackson to stay ready.

"Nothing (Jackson) has done wrong," Lue said before the game. "You want to try to reward him for what he has done since Yanic has been out. We will see how the flow of the game goes."

Jackson shot 3-for-5 from the field, finishing with six points, four rebounds and three steals in nine minutes of play. He also recorded two blocks against Wembanyama, once at the rim and the second at the 3-point line at the end of the third quarter.

San Antonio managed to get the best of one of Los Angeles' NBA teams, but a meeting with the other still looms.

The Lakers will head to San Antonio next week for an NBA Cup showdown. The Spurs sit at the top of the NBA Cup Group C standings with a 2-0 record. The Lakers are third after splitting their first two games.

A win for the Lakers would not only place them in a tie for the top spot in the group but also provide a glimpse at where things stand in the conference.

The Lakers are tied with the Spurs and LeBron James and the Philadelphia 76ers for the best record in the league at 13-3. Los Angeles has played well throughout November but had its nine-game winning streak snapped against the Utah Jazz on Wednesday, Nov. 18.

The upcoming Spurs-Lakers matchup will provide a glimpse at two of the league's early MVP front-runners on the court together. Luka Doncic is averaging a league-leading 38.1 points per game along with 7.6 assists and 7.0 rebounds. Wembanyama is averaging 28.4 points, 12.0 rebounds and a league-best 4.1 blocks per game.
