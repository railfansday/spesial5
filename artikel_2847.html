It’s time for Basketball: San Antonio Spurs vs New Orleans Pelicans

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Welcome to the Game Thread. Veterans of the Game Thread know how we do things around here, but for all you newbies we have a few rules. Our community guidelines apply and basically say be cool, no personal attacks, don't troll and don't swear too much.

The Spurs had a rough time with the Clippers for the first 42 minutes on Thursday night, but put them away with a 19-9 run late in the game to win 124-112 in a contest that was a lot closer than the final score. Dylan Harper is still out tonight, and will be for a few more weeks, so the Spurs will have to roll with last year's starting lineup of Fox, Castle, Vassell, Champagnie, and Wembanyama. That was the starting lineup that took them to the NBA finals last year, so maybe that's not all bad. The problems start with the bench, which is weakened with Harper's absence, and hasn't been all that great even when Harper was available. Hopefully, Mitch and Billy will see fit to give younger players like Tarris Reed and Carter Bryant some extended run tonight in non-garbage minutes.

Tonight, they play another struggling yet dangerous team, the New Orleans Pelicans. Dejounte Murray is out tonight with an ankle problem. I had no idea that requesting a trade would make your ankle hurt. Trey Murphy and Bennedict Mathurin are also out, and they would always cause the Spurs problems. Their best player is Zion Williamson, whose size and uncanny quickness can create problems for teams when he's healthy, which is hardly ever. Well, tonight is hardly ever day, so I expect him to torch the Spurs defense tonight. The Pelicans are on the second night of a back-to-back, having lost the the Thunder last night in Oklahoma 120-126. New Orleans led for most of the game but faded in the fourth quarter as OKC took control behind 40 points form SGA. Let's hope that there's less drama tonight and an unstressful wire-to-wire win for the Silver and Black tonight.
