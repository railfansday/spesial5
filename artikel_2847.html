Houston Christian at Northwestern State: How to watch, date, time, TV channel

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Houston Christian is mired in a three-game losing streak with 16 total points scored in those games.

However, the Huskies will be facing a team in straits far more dire Saturday afternoon.

The Demons lead the all-time series 5-4, but the Huskies have won the past two meetings.
