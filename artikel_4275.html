Sword x Staff, the popular idle RPG, launches in Southeast Asia alongside new Webtoon collaboration

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

If you're in Southeast Asia and you've been looking for a new idle RPG, then you're in luck because Sword x Staff, the popular mobile RPG, is set to land on November 4th, which is, ooh, today! Sword x Staff's laid-back progression has already drawn in over 10 million players worldwide.

Now is the perfect time to jump in and give it a go as well, because Sword x Staff has also announced its latest collaboration, landing November 11th. The event features popular Korean webtoon Return of the Blossoming Blade and its cast, as well as a host of additional content based on the series.

While Sword x Staff may be overblowing its third-way RPG format (really, it seems like an atypical idle RPG), I won't deny that it's been done well. And with millions of fans worldwide, launching in Southeast Asia can only spell good things for developer Boltray Games.

Southeast Asia has increasingly become the promised land for many mobile game developers and publishers, with a huge smartphone-playing population. So it's no surprise that the next step for Sword x Staff is to bring its distinct twist on the idle RPG genre to the region!
