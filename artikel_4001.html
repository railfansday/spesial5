Rare Co-Founder Tim Stamper Unveils A Treasure Trove Of Conker Dev Material

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Rare co-founder Tim Stamper has begun uploading new videos to his YouTube channel 'InTimsWorld', the first of which being a deep dive into the development of Conker's Bad Fur Day (thanks, Time Extension).

Released in 2001, mere months before the GameCube landed, Conker's Bad Fur Day is one of the most recognisable titles on the N64, garnering critical acclaim and controversy in equal measure thanks to its raunchy themes and platforming satire.

Stamper's video is mostly a peek into a folder containing a wealth of artwork and design documents from the game's development. It's a really fascinating glimpse into how the game evolved into the final product, with Stamper also providing the odd anecdote here and there.

Tim Stamper, along with his brother Chris, left Rare in 2007, a few years after the company was bought out by Microsoft. Rare's output in recent years has been pretty slim compared to its earlier days, with Sea of Thieves being the most significant success story.

With Xbox implementing major restructures to its business, Rare now reports directly to Activision, which Microsoft previously purchased for almost $69 billion.
