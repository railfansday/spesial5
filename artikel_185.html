If you’re on a budget but still looking for a thoughtful gift, we’ve compiled some picks from Verge staff.

You’ve got $50 to spend on a friend or loved one this shopping season, and you’re not sure what to spend it on. First off, that’s actually really generous of you. While this price tier can sometimes be challenging to shop for — caught between affordable accessories and big-ticket items — there’s plenty of gold to be mined for clever shoppers. And we’re here to help.

This guide includes a wide range of cool finds at $50 or less, from mainstream hits like KPop Demon Hunters on Blu-ray and popular tech like the DJI Mic Mini to more eccentric picks like VidaBay’s E Ink fridge magnets and the Werewolf USB-C power adapter. We think you’ll be surprised how far your $50 can stretch.

If someone on your list collects Game Boy, Game Boy Color, or Game Boy Advance cartridges, the GB Operator might be the perfect gift. The slick, transparent USB-C device makes it easy to play and manage classic titles on a macOS or Windows machine via emulation, with or without achievements.

Pricey yet endlessly useful, the Grip Stand is the ultimate phone accessory. The ultra-thin magnetic attachment is a better gift than simpler PopSocket-style holders, especially since it can serve as an ad hoc kickstand. That makes it convenient for both video calls and YouTube.

Espresso in the woods? Yes, please. The GR2’s magical mechanics make it possible, letting you turn 12 ounces of grounds into espresso without power. Its carrying case houses all the accessories needed to get that sweet shot of bean, making real coffee available virtually anywhere.

New Zealand artist Aldous Harding has another eccentric album on her hands with this year’s Train on the Island. The slow-burning LP is gorgeously recorded and delightfully weird, and it feels tailor-made for vinyl, making it an easy pick for Harding fans (or anyone who loves quirky indie jams).

Rechargeable AA batteries are great for the environment and your budget, but not all models are created equally, so we did our own testing. With top-tier capacity, breezy usability, and a compact USB-C charging case, Zepath’s batteries are the best we’ve tried.

Give the gift of security with this USB-C smart camera, which offers a wide field of view, night vision, microSD storage, and free Wi-Fi livestreaming. The optional subscription adds cloud storage and smart object detection, letting users upgrade as needed.

Moft’s origami-inspired “invisible” stand provides a sleek way to prop up your tablet or laptop (up to 14 inches) and to keep your working space looking more organized. The design has features like ventilation gaps and firm grips to work on a variety of surfaces and devices.

This affordable dongle kit provides an almost magical way to power nearly any DC device from a USB-C charger or portable battery. Program it from a mobile app, connect it to your old gear, then everything from a PC monitor to an original Game Boy can accept USB-C charging.

Hoto’s electric screwdriver is a thoughtful gift for the builder in your life. The compact, easy-to-use tool includes 12 S2 steel bits, a circular LED to illuminate your project, and a soft, yet grippy handle. Three torque settings allow for a variety of jobs, and a long-lasting battery (with USB-C charging) keeps the driver going for hours.

It can be hard to keep in touch with friends, but Big Walk might be the game that gets the gang back together. The cooperative multiplayer title drops players into a wacky world as spherical humanoids who must solve puzzles by chatting and teaming up. You’ll want at least two copies, and sessions support up to 12 players.

This handy Japanese organizer is a thoughtful gift for folks who prefer a physical way to keep track of everything, from upcoming events and projects to random thoughts and creative ideas. It comes in multiple sizes and can be customized with artistic covers in a variety of colors and designs. Take your pick.

SwitchBot’s futuristic block, which is designed to activate physical switches from afar, is as useful as it is eccentric. Also available in a rechargeable configuration, the app-controllable button pusher clips onto almost anything with a switch and lasts up to 600 days on a single 3V CR2 battery.

Everyone needs a basic toolkit, and Ron Swanson would agree. I’m not sure he’d go for the micro-sized set from iFixit, but we can back it. It’s an excellent entry point for folks living in small homes or apartments. Headlined by a 32-bit screwdriver set, it includes two pairs of tweezers, dual spudgers, and more.

EasySMX makes our favorite Nintendo Switch 2 controllers, and the S10 Lite adds a major upgrade: native system functionality. That makes it simpler to connect and customize its back paddles than its rivals. The Lite skips a few features, like amiibo support and high-quality rumble, but otherwise provides a solid value for those on a budget.

As any experienced content creator will tell you, capturing good audio is more than half the battle. DJI’s Mic Mini gives aspiring creators a leg up with a sleek mic and transmitter set, enabling up to 10 hours of recording time, plus powerful noise canceling to capture the narrative, not the unwanted noise.

This collapsible stand converts your Steam Deck or Switch 2 into a desktop-like experience. Wherever you want to post up with your handheld, the Desk Stand is a high-quality aluminum stand that’s a great buy. It includes cable guides and works with Mechanism’s accessories for attaching battery banks and more.

These self-looping USB-C cables from SuperCalla might be just what your gift recipient needs. Instead of flat cables that often become unwieldy, these are capped with slim magnetic threads that stick together in a tight coil, making cable organization a breeze. They’re a bit pricey, but worth it.

So what if you’ve heard “Golden” more times than you can count? Netflix’s massive animated hit is a lot easier to gift now that it’s officially available on 4K Blu-ray disc. The story about three pop stars who band together to save humanity from a demonic boy band is an instant classic, with fantastic music and striking animation.

These nifty fridge magnets might revolutionize grandma’s refrigerator. Designed to look like Polaroid pictures, they each feature an E-Ink screen and NFC communication, letting owners easily upload new photos to the frame by simply tapping their phones on them. It feels like magic, but it’s just a really cool application of some modern tech.

Sure. You could put iced tea in them, but no one’s stopping you from sipping pulpy OJ or rich chocolate milk from Kinto’s stylish glassware, which was crafted with an “uninterfering design” in mind. Needless to say, a full set makes a lovely addition to any minimalist’s ensemble.

Let your loved ones sling their new Apple gear safely and in style with these official, well-made Apple Wrist Straps. Compatible with AirPods Pro 2 and iPhone 17 cases or newer, they’re a practical way to carry phones and earbuds when pockets won’t do.

This durable handheld system for preschoolers and up is the perfect gift for your favorite budding gamer. With an ABC keyboard, built-in games like go-kart driving, and educational ones to challenge their memory, it’s a great tool to stimulate young minds and keep them occupied without letting them loose in Roblox.

While Zepath’s batteries are the top AA rechargeables we’ve tested, Paleblues are a convenient alternative for those after USB-C charging on each cell instead of in a case. They come with a four-headed cable to charge a four-pack from a single USB-C port.

For the person on your list whose hatred for smart glasses runs deep, Knockaround’s hip, tech-free sunglasses should hit the spot. They’re available in a variety of fun styles and offer lightweight frames and polarized lenses, with both UVA and UVB shielding for attractive eye protection.

RIP, Eddie Munson. This radical D&D set celebrates the classic Stranger Things character and his policy of accepting nerds from all walks of life. It’s a fun way to remember the long-running Netflix series and a solid gift for any D&D fan.

Bring Nintendo’s popular Mario Kart racing game to life with this collectible RC car. Beyond the kart, the package includes a jewel display case, a controller and batteries, and can be accompanied by rivals like Toad, Luigi, Peach, and Yoshi. Race on!

For your next Secret Santa gift swap at work or in your friend group, we submit this zany head-shaped lamp from Ikea. Its color-changing profile looks cool, so long as you don’t mind having a head as decor. Beyond that, it provides a useful way to store items like hats and sunglasses.

In the age of remote work, you can get the job done from just about anywhere at home. With this clever laptop desk, your favorite worker can easily log on from bed or on the couch without having to awkwardly prop their machine on a throw pillow. It offers adjustable legs, a foldable design, and a nonslip surface.

Let your favorite gamer turn their phone into a Game Boy, of sorts. 8BitDo’s controller pad emulates Nintendo’s classic handheld and slides directly into your phone’s USB-C port, perfect for emulation. It offers a soft backpad that won’t scratch your screen, and its hinged connector lets it detach with ease.
