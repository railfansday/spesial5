UEFA Nations League: Borussia Dortmund midfielders face off in Germany's group

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

While Germany face Serbia away from home, the other teams from the group meet in Amsterdam. Three points for either Netherlands or Greece would confirm the first team to qualify for the quarter-final from the group.

Borussia Dortmund duo Joey Veerman and Konstantinos Karetsas are in the starting lineups at both ends. Here are how the two teams will start the game at Johan Cruijff Arena.

Netherlands: Verbruggen; Dumfries, J. Timber, Van Hecke, Van de Ven; Reijnders, De Jong ©, Veerman; Summerville, Malen, Gakpo

Greece: Tzolakis; Rota, Mavropanos, Retsos, Tsimikas; Kourbelis, Androutsos, Tzolis; Ionannidis, Pavlidis ©, Karetsas
