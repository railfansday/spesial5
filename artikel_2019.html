Aberdeen hero admits ‘we’re still waiting’ as Pittodrie boss faces crunch call

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Alex McLeish admits he expected Stephen Robinson to “rock it” at Aberdeen but concedes the under-pressure boss has yet to take the club to the next level.

Robinson finds himself under mounting scrutiny following a difficult run of results, with the Dons struggling to live up to expectations. Former Aberdeen defender and Scotland manager McLeish knows Robinson personally and had backed him to make a significant impact at Pittodrie.

'I thought he could take things to another level but we're still waiting on that' Alex McLeish on former club Aberdeen and under pressure head coach Stephen Robinson. #BBCFootball https://t.co/AeqYKiKDYx

— BBC Sport Scotland (@BBCSportScot) November 9, 2026

But while retaining faith that the manager will do everything possible to turn things around, McLeish accepts the results cannot continue indefinitely. Speaking to the BBC, he said: “I thought Stephen would go in there and rock it a wee bit, because I like him, he’s a good lad.

“I’ve been pally with him down in England, where he was both a player and a manager. I thought he could maybe take things to another level, but we’re still waiting on that.

“Stevie will try his hardest and darndest to get that right, but right now, everybody is looking for victims and sackings.

“Obviously, you can’t keep going forward when you’re getting terrible results and that was a real blow, a real killer of a game.”
