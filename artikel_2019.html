Sam Johnstone's honest Celtic results admission as dressing room mood revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Goalkeeper Sam Johnstone admits Celtic have fallen below expectations this season, with recent form impacting the team's spirit.

Thursday's 3-2 victory over Besiktas, Johnstone says, showed their character, with the long-term target now to 'put things right'.

The stopper, signed on loan from Wolverhampton Wanderers during the summer transfer window, has started Celtic's last five matches, displacing Viljami Sinisalo between the sticks.

"The expectation, rightly so, for this place is to win games," Johnstone said. "Unfortunately, we hadn't quite met that expectation, and the lads know that. We try to win every game and put things right."

Celtic goalkeeper Sam Johnstone walks off the pitch at half-time. (Image: Stuart Wallace / Shutterstock)

Celtic had won just three of their previous ten matches before Besiktas.

Beating the Turks, then, was crucial in repairing the mood, both internally and externally.

"I think the results affect it," Johnstone said of the spirit within the camp. "This club's not used to losing, and we’ve probably lost too many games for this club.

"We all realise that, but we have to put things right in each game. The lads go out to win every game, so the camp is good.

"It was tough. We're honest players, we're honest people, and honest with each other. You could see on Thursday night that the lads put a good fight in and got the result."

Johnstone's recent run of starts is his most consistent since joining Celtic at the start of September.

The 33-year-old was thrust in shortly after signing, playing against St Johnstone in the William Hill Premiership and Rangers in the Premier Sports Cup.

He failed to impress in the 3-0 defeat at Ibrox but now looks far more assured.

"To get a run of games for any player, you find your rhythm," Johnstone explained. "It was a new club for me, a new team, so that takes probably a couple of games to get used to as well.

"Listen, to get the games and to keep going and get the win on Thursday night was a good feeling.

"It's brilliant to play for this club. The passion of the fans, good and bad. The standard of players on the pitch, day in, day out at training.

"When I came, I said I didn't have to think twice about coming here."

After their first Europa League win of the season, Celtic's focus shifts back to the domestic scene, where Kilmarnock lie in wait on Sunday afternoon.

Returning to winning ways in the Premiership after last weekend's defeat to Falkirk is key.
