Man United's upcoming right-back signing will surely be Alex Freeman as Mauricio Pochettino prepares to leave the USMNT for Old Trafford.

Freeman has caught the eye of numerous top clubs across Europe during a strong 2026 from a personal standpoint.

The 22-year-old has nailed down a spot as Villarreal's starting right-back just two years on from being in Orlando City's youth set-up.

He also emerged as a starter under Pochettino for the USA in their home World Cup over the summer.

Man United have been linked to the flying full-back in recent months as they eye additions across their backline in January.

Pochettino's impending arrival at Old Trafford makes a swoop for the Villarreal star seem obvious ahead of the other defenders on the Red Devils' shortlist.

Freeman is a modern full-back, standing at six foot two inches tall, while he can also play at the heart of the backline.

He featured nine times in the second half of last term for Villarreal after a January switch from Orlando, and has made 16 appearances across all competitions so far in 2026-27, including four in the Champions League.

The 22-year-old has become an established international under Pochettino, with starts in four of their five World Cup games in the summer.

Freeman was given huge praise by the US boss after his goal at the tournament against Australia, as he said: "He’s a lovely guy, and he’s an amazing player. For me, he has the potential to be one of the best players in his position in the world.

"He’s such a humble guy. He has an amazing profile, he wants to learn, he always listens. He’s a player that you really enjoy being with, not only coaching.”

It is very clear that he rates him highly, and so do United's recruitment team, so Freeman should surely start packing his bags for Old Trafford very soon.

Sources have exclusively revealed to Football Insider that Man City and Spurs also admire Freeman, but the lure of playing under his old international manager could put United in pole position for his signature in January.

He is also a standout for duels won (7.55, 94th percentile), aerial duels won (1.89, 93rd percentile) and recoveries (6.97, 100th percentile) per 90 too.
