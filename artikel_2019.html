Arsenal drew 2-2 Newcastle United in the Premier League at St James' Park on Saturday.

The Gunners travelled to Tyneside on 21 November and were utterly dominant in the first half, taking the lead through Eberechi Eze around the half-hour mark.

However, the Magpies responded early in the second half after Martin Odegaard's error was punished by Harvey Barnes.

Newcastle took the lead at St James' Park, but Noni Madueke came to the rescue.

The English winger dribbled past multiple opposition players to beat Lukas Hornicek, giving Mikel Arteta some much-needed relief.

In the end, there was no winner in the nine minutes of stoppage time and here Football Insider rate the performances from Mikel Arteta's players at St James' Park.

Noni Madueke was all over Lewis Hall in the opening 45 minutes and gave the home fans several nervy moments.

The England international was electric for the Gunners in the first half, constantly getting in behind the defence.

Madueke could've found the back of the net before the break, but his effort was blocked by an impressive tackle from Hall.

The former Chelsea ace got rewarded for his efforts and brought parity to the game at a crucial time for the Gunners.

Arsenal were in the driving seat at the break and it looked like they would steamroll past Newcastle.

However, Odegaard's error while trying to clear the ball from a throw-in led to the Magpies' equaliser.

With the goal, the atmosphere inside St James' Park flipped and it was the hosts who dominated proceedings until Madueke's strike.

The Tyneside outfit then got their second goal and there's an argument that things could've been different had Odegaard not made the mistake.
