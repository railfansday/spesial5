Gwyneth Paltrow Details Role Officiating Wedding for Rob Lowe’s Son

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Gwyneth Paltrow shared insight into her role as officiant when Rob Lowe’s oldest son Matthew Lowe, married Emily Markus, in October.

Gwyneth Paltrow is feeling goop about this opportunity.

The Marty Supreme actress revealed that she recently took on the role as officiant for the first time as Matthew Lowe, 33, tied the knot with Emily Markus.

A longtime friend of Matthew’s dad Rob Lowe—with whom she co-starred in View from the Top in 2003—Gwyneth admitted during the Nov. 11 episode of The Goop Guide podcast, “I still have chills when I think about it.”

“To be so close to in such close proximity to people making that commitment was so incredible,” the 54-year-old continued. “To see their little faces and the emotion and to watch the energy shift and feel it as they made that commitment to each other.”

Gwyneth also couldn’t help but bask in the honor, joking, “Just call me Pastor Gwyn.”

While the Iron Man actress—mom to Apple, 22, and Moses, 20, with ex Chris Martin—was a former costar with Rob and a recent wedding officiant for Matthew, her connection to the Lowe family dates back even farther.

When she was only a teen, Gwyneth was introduced to Rob’s now-wife of 35 years Sheryl Berkoff while on set with her mother Blythe Danner.

“I met the Mrs. Lowe when I was 15 or 16,” Gwyneth explained during a 2020 appearance on Rob’s Literally podcast. “I was immediately obsessed with her. I just worshipped her. I thought she was literally the coolest chick of all time, and she was so awesome to me and I was a high school kid.”

And the Goop founder admitted that once Rob and Sheryl—who also share 31-year-old son John Owen Lowe—started dating in 1989, it was “fantastic,” although she had to warm up to the St. Elmo’s Fire alum.

“You made me really nervous,” Gwyneth—who had her breakout role in Seven in 1995—recalled, “because I was still nervous of celebrities. You were Rob Lowe. It was very unnerving.”

Ultimately, Gwyneth clearly got over her nerves—and she attended Matthew and Emily’s star-studded Oct. 10 nuptials alongside Rob’s brother Chad Lowe and his family as well as Katy Perry, Chris Pratt, Zoe Saldaña and more.

While Matthew and Emily have yet to share a personal glimpse into their special day, the couple did confirm their engagement in July 2025, sharing photos from the milestone alongside Matthew’s cheeky caption, “Lord of the rings.”
