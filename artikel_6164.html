Premium cards are not just about travel — they’re continuing to add lifestyle value you can use at home. The American Express Platinum Card® offers up to $75 per quarter (up to $300 per calendar year) in Lululemon statement credits.*

Whether you're looking for casual attire, travel essentials or workout gear, here are seven items we recommend buying with your quarterly $75 Lululemon statement credit.

*Enrollment is required, and outlets are excluded. Only applies to purchases at U.S. stores and online.

Although the Amex Platinum's $895 annual fee (see rates and fees) may seem very high, perks like the quarterly up to $75 Lululemon statement credit (up to $300 each calendar year) help offset the cost.

The up to $75 quarterly statement credit is valid for eligible purchases made at U.S. Lululemon stores (excluding outlets) and on lululemon.com (after enrollment).

The Amex Platinum Card offers up to $75 in Lululemon statement credits each quarter. If you shop at the retailer only once and spend less than $75, you’ll forfeit any unused portion of the statement credit when the next quarter begins.

To maximize this benefit, first make sure you’re enrolled in the statement credit, then click through a shopping portal before making your purchase. While you’ll earn only 1 point per dollar spent with your Platinum Card, you may be able to pick up additional rewards through a shopping portal. (At the time of publication, Rakuten is not offering rewards on Lululemon purchases.)

Next, aim to use the full $75 statement credit each quarter. You don’t have to spend it all in a single transaction, and you can spread your purchases across multiple orders if needed. Since many popular Lululemon items — including jackets, pants and shirts — cost more than $75, using the entire statement credit shouldn’t be difficult.

The Lululemon statement credit on the Amex Platinum is a great benefit for those who enjoy the brand. Consider these TPG-recommended items, or, if you already have your own favorites, be sure to purchase them using your Amex Platinum next time.

Don't forget to use your Q4 Lululemon statement credit, since you don't want to leave that value on the table.

If you buy an item you’re not happy with, consider exchanging it instead of returning it. A return could trigger a statement credit reversal, and you may not be able to use your statement credit again during that quarter.

You can also stretch your statement credit further by purchasing sale items. (Note that purchases made at Lululemon outlet locations don’t qualify.)

Finally, don’t wait until the last minute to use your quarterly statement credit. If your purchase doesn’t post before the end of the quarter, it could trigger the following quarter’s statement credit instead.
