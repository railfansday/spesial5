Singapore Airlines Unveils Posh New First Class Suites With Double Beds For 777-9 And A350ULR

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Singapore Airlines has unveiled its next-generation first class seat, with sliding doors, companion dining, and center seats that combine into a double bed measuring five feet across. The new product will appear on the Airbus A350-900ULR and Boeing 777-9, bringing first class to the airline’s ultra-long-range A350 fleet for the first time.

I also wrote about the carrier’s new business class product here.

Singapore Airlines’ new first class specifications reveal a small cabin with substantial personal space. The A350-900ULR will have four seats, while the Boeing 777-9 will have six. Both use a 1-2-1 arrangement with direct aisle access and a curved cabin layout.

PriestmanGoode designed the product, with Collins Aerospace manufacturing the seats. The interiors feature Poltrona Frau leather and padded walls incorporating Singapore Airlines’ batik motif.

The centerpiece is the pair of center seats. A fully retractable divider allows passengers to share the space, with the seats moving together to create either a two-person sofa or a double bed. That bed measures 80 inches long and 60 inches wide. Singapore Airlines describes it as the largest double bed in commercial aviation.

Solo passengers receive an 80-inch-long, 30-inch-wide bed. The seat itself measures 23 inches across with the armrests raised, expanding to 30 inches when they are lowered.

Every seat also supports face-to-face dining with a companion, a feature I appreciate in first class: traveling together should include the option to sit across from one another for dinner, even when you prefer separate window seats.

The seat includes electronically adjustable side wings, upper and lower lumbar support, adjustable armrests, a leg rest, and an ottoman. Integrated myKrisWorld controls can save preferred seat positions and lighting settings for future flights.

For sleeping, the crew will prepare the bed with cotton linens, a duvet, a choice of pillows, and a new mattress topper from German bedding company Paradies. Singapore Airlines says the topper uses breathable spacer fabric to improve cushioning and temperature regulation.

Dedicated storage includes a personal wardrobe, a leather-lined vanity drawer, and space for cabin baggage beneath the ottoman.

Both aircraft receive a 32-inch 4K entertainment screen, but the A350ULR version is OLED. The 777-9 specification lists a conventional 4K display.

Passengers will be offered Bang & Olufsen wireless headphones or can pair Bluetooth headphones with the system. A separate touch panel displays flight information, connectivity status, and lavatory availability without interrupting the main screen.

The new cabins are scheduled to begin entering service in the second half of 2027. Unlike with ANA, the unveiling does not signal an imminent onboard debut. First class is planned for the A350-900ULR and 777-9. The standard long-haul A350-900 will receive the new business class, but no first class cabin.

Singapore Airlines’ new first class looks lovely, particularly for couples who can use the center seats as a shared living and sleeping space. Companion dining at every seat and substantial bedding are welcome details too.
