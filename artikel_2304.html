Kompany set to resume full Bayern duties ahead of Köln clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Following his collapse in October, which was caused by meningeal irritation and left him with a dislocated shoulder, Vincent Kompany has been back at Bayern Munich's Säbener Straße training ground for more than a week.

The Belgian coach led his first training session since returning on Monday and, according to Bild, is expected to oversee all of Bayern's remaining sessions this week, as well as Thursday's press conference ahead of Friday's Bundesliga clash with 1. FC Köln.

Provided he suffers no further setbacks in his recovery, Kompany is expected to return to the touchline for the home fixture against the Billy Goats.

The 40-year-old continues to be closely monitored by the club's medical department, with assistant coach Aaron Danks ready to step in again should the need arise.
