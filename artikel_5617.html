Milwaukee Brewers Captain Christian Yelich Lists Newly Built Malibu Mansion for $30 Million

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Milwaukee Brewers captain Christian Yelich is looking to shed his contemporary Malibu mansion, listing the hillside estate for $30 million.

The World Series champion purchased the 1-acre property for $5.55 million in 2020. At the time, the lot included “fully approved plans” from architect Michael Kovac of KOVAC Design Studio.

The 5,300-square-foot residence was completed this year, according to Architectural Digest. A KOVAC representative told the publication that Yelich plans to move his family to Wisconsin.

The 34-year-old baseball star has been romantically linked to professional soccer player, Paige Noel Almendariz, although it is not clear if the couple live together.

“Set in the heart of Malibu, this one-of-a-kind coastal retreat has been thoughtfully rebuilt from the ground up with an unwavering commitment to quality, design, and comfort,”reads the listing, which is held by Yelich’s mother, Alecia Yelich with Compass.

Her bio notes that professional athlete relocation is her speciality.

“Offering sweeping views of the Queen’s Necklace, Surfrider Beach, Pacific Coast Highway and Nobu, this is the kind of property that rarely comes to market,” the listing continues. “Every inch of the home has been carefully curated, blending sophisticated finishes with cutting-edge technology to create a living experience that is both luxurious and effortless.”

The home boasts four bedrooms, four full bathrooms, a powder room, a dedicated pool bathroom, and an outdoor shower.

Nestled in a light-flooded corner of the home with retractable glass walls that let in fresh air, the primary suite is truly astonishing. Even the soaking tub and walk-in shower have killer views.

The five additional bathrooms are impressive, as well, offering expansive windows and closets equipped with refrigerators in each room.

Custom hardwood floors imported from France run throughout the home for a touch of warmth, both aesthetically and through the radiant-heated flooring that complements them.

Floor-to-ceiling windows can also be found across the home, making the idyllic Malibu surroundings feel like part of the decor.

The chef’s kitchen offers sprawling counter space, chic modern fixtures, and plenty of storage for snacks, supplies, and cooking gadgets.

The kitchen sink faces the home’s breathtaking ocean views, while automated pocket doors seamlessly connect the room to the picturesque outdoor living space.

The home has a number of standout spaces to use for entertaining guests or to enjoy on a quiet night in, including the custom wet bar and a private cinema with a dedicated concession room, an LED video wall, luxury seating, immersive surround sound, and professional acoustic treatments.

Technology has been carefully incorporated into the dwelling, which the listing describes as a “fully integrated smart home,” which gives residents the opportunity to “effortless control lighting, climate, entertainment, shades, security, and access from a single platform.”

A custom two-car carage and newly-constructed sealed driveway provide a safe space for residents’ vehicles.

Outdoor living was considered as thoughtfully as the interiors, with the backyard anchored by a resort-style infinity-edge pool.

There’s no shortage of lounge space, with multiple entertaining spaces spread among the area’s various decks and patios, and fire features and outdoors amenities to be enjoyed. An outdoor kitchen and barbecue area are also perfectly-suited for hosting.

Fully-automated Tuuci umbrellas leverage integrated heaters to provide shade from the sun or warmth during a chilly night.

There is even an oversized fully-enclosed outdoor television; a state-of-the-art addition that’s designed to provide “a remarkable viewing experience even in the brightest Malibu sunshine.”

Prospective buyers with pets will also be thrilled to learn there’s also a custom dog run.
