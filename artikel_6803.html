How Justine Skye Gets Holiday Gifting Right

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The singer and Kendra Scott campaign star shares her personal guide to finding the perfect present.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

For Justine Skye, the art of gift-giving comes down to how well you know someone. Whether it’s a nod to an inside joke, a reminder of a shared memory, or something they’d never think to buy for themselves, the singer gravitates toward presents with a personal touch. “I’m thoughtful and a little sentimental,” Skye tells ELLE. Naturally, jewelry is high on her list.

It makes sense, then, that this holiday season Skye is partnering with Kendra Scott, starring in the brand’s “Long Live Joy” campaign, directed by Andrew Thomas Huang. Set across three imaginative worlds—Celestial, Radiance, and Botanical—the campaign celebrates individuality, self-expression, and finding joy on your own terms.

It’s a sentiment that resonates with Skye, whose own approach to accessorizing is equally thoughtful. “Sometimes I’ll build an entire look around one piece and let everything else fall into place,” she says. Among her favorites from the holiday collections is the Fate Star Gold Short Pendant Necklace, a reversible, sculptural star designed to be worn on its own or layered with other pieces.

When it comes to holiday shopping, Skye takes the same personal approach. And while it might be difficult to top the car she received for her 30th birthday, she maintains that the most memorable gifts are the ones with genuine thought behind them. Below, Skye shares her holiday gifting philosophy, along with her curated selection of gifts for everyone on your list (including yourself).

“I love gifting pieces that feel timeless but still have a little personality. This cuff is something she can wear every day or dress up for a special occasion.”

“I feel like dads are always drinking coffee or tea, so this is such a good gift. It keeps their drink at the perfect temperature, and I love giving something that makes a simple everyday routine feel a little more luxurious.”

“I love the idea of gifting your significant other a Fujifilm Instax camera. It’s such a fun way to capture little moments together and make memories you can actually hold onto. There’s something really special about having physical photos instead of everything just living on your phone.”

“Gifting your best friend something that reflects her style is always the move. The chunky chain and medallion-style toggle make it a real statement.”

“I feel like you can never go wrong with a good pair of headphones. Whether they’re listening to music, traveling, or just tuning out the world for a bit, these are something they’ll get so much use out of. Music is always a good gift in my book.”

“An experience. I think taking someone to dinner, planning a trip, or giving them an experience they’ll remember can be more special than buying another thing.”

“I’m such a fan of anything that makes you feel good in your own skin. These are perfect for trying different scents, and I love that they’re small enough to throw in your bag when you’re traveling.”

“I love a good gym bag that you can also take on a weekend trip. It’s practical, stylish, and perfect for someone who’s always on the go.”

“I’m all about being comfortable when I’m home. These are such a cozy gift and perfect for someone who loves a little self-care and downtime.”

“You can never go wrong with a beautiful candle. I love gifting something that makes someone’s home feel warm and inviting, especially during the holidays.”

“You can never go wrong with a good lip product. This is such a cute little gift to add to someone’s beauty bag, and I love that it gives you a hint of color while keeping your lips hydrated.”

“I love how they take a celestial-inspired design and make it feel bold and modern. They’re the kind of earrings that can instantly elevate an outfit, whether you’re dressing up for a holiday party or just want to add something special to an everyday look.”
