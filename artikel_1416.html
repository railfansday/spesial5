Manchester United: Bruno Fernandes refuses a second extension offer

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Bruno Fernandes’ future at Manchester United is becoming increasingly uncertain. According to information from Sky Sportsthe Red Devils captain has refused a second contract extension offer, while his current commitment expires at the end of the season. The 32-year-old Portuguese attacking midfielder had already rejected a first proposal made last summer, when discussions began between the two parties. Despite these two refusals, Manchester United does not intend to abandon negotiations and still wishes to keep its captain. The English club also has an option allowing it to unilaterally extend its contract for an additional season, which would prevent Fernandes from negotiating freely with foreign teams from January for a free departure. A situation which could quickly become one of the major issues for Mancunian leaders, while Mauricio Pochettino should be officially appointed coach this Friday to succeed Michael Carrick, dismissed on Thursday.

Already very popular in recent months, Bruno Fernandes notably refused a considerable financial offer from Al-Hilal in June. The Saudi club were ready to almost triple his weekly salary of 250,000 euros, while offering 100 million euros to Manchester United to secure his transfer. Galatasaray had also expressed their interest, but the English leaders then assured that their captain was not for sale. Voted best player of the year by English journalists in May, then by his peers in August, after a season marked by 21 assists, Fernandes, however, is experiencing a much more complicated exercise. The Portuguese has scored just four goals this season, including a hat-trick against Ipswich Town in August, in Manchester United’s only Premier League victory so far. The expected arrival of Mauricio Pochettino could now constitute an important element in the discussions around his future. The two men could have worked together as early as 2019, when the Argentinian coached Tottenham, but Sporting Portugal ultimately blocked the transfer of the attacking midfielder.
