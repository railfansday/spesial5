Balerdi grits his teeth, aims to be called up vs PSG

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Since his arrival in Rome, Leonardo Balerdi has featured in every Champions League match.

The former Marseille player has logged a total of 223 minutes in the competition: 16 against Fenerbahçe, 27 against Real Madrid, and 90 minutes each against Slovan Bratislava and Manchester United.

However, the Argentine central defender may be forced to miss the away match against PSG.

During the game against Parma at the Stadio Tardini, the 25-year-old had to leave the pitch due to an ankle issue—a persistent problem that has been troubling him for weeks.

Ahead of tomorrow’s match between PSG and Roma, scheduled for 9:00 PM, Gian Piero Gasperini will need his entire squad available to try and contain the French side, with hopes of securing a significant result.

As reported by Il Messaggero, there are a few situations to monitor following the Parma match—including that of Leonardo Balerdi, who is dealing with an ankle issue.

Yesterday, the Argentine defender took part in only a portion of the group training session, alternating between work on the pitch and medical treatment.

There is cautious optimism regarding his inclusion in the squad to face PSG, but a final decision will only be made after the concluding training session, scheduled for 2:30 PM today. Meanwhile, medical tests are also expected today for Rensch, who is suffering from a flexor problem.
