Stephen A. Smith drops harsh truth bomb on Steph Curry’s NBA future

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Warriors are expected to be without Steph Curry for a month due to an oblique abdominal tear, and there's concern it could affect his long-term health.

Stephen A. Smith appeared on his podcast on Tuesday and said Curry's ongoing injuries, along with the cartilage damage in his right knee, could indicate his current contract could be his last.

Stephen A. Smith drops a harsh truth bomb for the future of Steph Curry and the Golden State Warriors. IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect

"The greatest shooter God has ever created. Steph Curry says 'deteriorated cartilage' in his right knee and ongoing injuries have made him more aware that his NBA career could be nearing its end," Smith said. "If you didn't remember, he signed a two-year extension for $116 million."

"Steph Curry has been on the record saying that this may very well be his last contract. He's [38] years old," Smith said.

Smith said the possibility of this being Curry's final contract makes him "sad."

Steph Curry during pregame warmups for the Warriors. NBAE via Getty Images

Curry is in the midst of his 18th NBA season and is considered one of the game's greatest shooters of all time.

From beyond the arc, Curry has a 42.2 shooting percentage. He has 26,806 career points and is continuing to do it at a high level.

On Oct. 28, Curry cemented his name with LeBron James, by tying him at 15 games for 40-point performances past age 35.

Steph Curry celebrating during an NBA game for the Warriors. Getty Images

Before his injury, Curry appeared in 10 games for the Warriors and averaged 27.8 points per game, shooting 50% from the field, 45.7% from 3-point range and 98.7% from the free-throw line.

"I think this brother is a showman. He is a showstopper. He is the greatest marksman the game has ever seen. He's a paragon of virtue when it comes to professional athletes. Nobody conducts themselves better," Smith said. "He's just a special brother. I will tell you that."
