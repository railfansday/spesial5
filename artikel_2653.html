Predicting the full 14-team NFL playoff bracket entering Week 9: Bears make Super Bowl run

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

With the NFL season heading into its halfway point this week, I have some good news for everyone: All 32 teams are still mathematically alive to make the playoffs. And yes, that means even the Titans and Chargers could still make the postseason. I'm guessing they won't, but they could, and that's what matters here.

Heading into Week 9, this is when we finally start to see the contenders separate themselves from the pretenders. But who are the real contenders? Who's actually going to make the playoffs? Glad you asked.

To help us figure that out, we had our analytics expert, Stephen Oh, simulate the rest of the 2026 season. There are 151 games left on the regular-season schedule, along with 13 playoff games, and Oh simulated all of them. Not only did he project all eight division winners and the 14 playoff teams, but he also crowned a Super Bowl champion.

With that in mind, let's get to the projections. And just as a reminder, we're projecting what the NFL playoff field will look like at the end of the season. This is NOT the current playoff standings.

The AFC playoff projection underwent some massive changes this week. The Broncos turned the AFC West upside down with their win over the Chiefs. Thanks to that victory, our model now projects that Denver ends up winning the division title for the second straight year.

Here's the projected AFC playoff field (with each team's current record):  1. Bills (6-1) -- AFC East champion 2. Jaguars (5-2) -- AFC South champion 3. Broncos (6-2) -- AFC West champion 4. Bengals (5-2) -- AFC North champion 5. Chiefs (5-2) 6. Steelers (6-2) 7. Patriots (5-3) JUST MISSED: Texans (2-5), Browns (5-3)

If the Broncos win the AFC West, the Chiefs will enter the playoffs as a wild-card team, which has never happened in Patrick Mahomes' career. Since he took over the starting job in 2018, the Chiefs have made the playoffs a total of seven times, and all of those came after a season where Kansas City won the division. The Chiefs have been so good with Mahomes that they've NEVER played a road game in the wild card round with him at quarterback, but we could see that happen this year.

For the third time this season, we have a new team projected to win the NFC North. The Bears have taken over the top spot after their dominating win over the Seahawks on Monday night. Over the past eight weeks, we've seen the Vikings and Lions both projected to win the division, but heading into Week 9, our computer likes Da' Bears.

Here's the projected NFC playoff field (with each team's current record):  1. 49ers (6-1) -- NFC West champion 2. Bears (6-2) -- NFC North champion 3. Giants (4-2-1) -- NFC East champion 4. Buccaneers (3-5) -- NFC South champion 5. Vikings (6-1) 6. Lions (5-2) 7. Seahawks (4-4)  JUST MISSED: Packers (5-3), Rams (4-4)

For the first time in NFL history, we could have a situation where TWO teams win their division despite not being over .500. Right now, our projection has the Giants winning the NFC East with a record of 8-8-1 and the Buccaneers winning the NFC South at 8-9. The Bucs started the season 0-4, but our computer loves them after watching Tampa Bay destroy two division rivals -- the Panthers and Falcons -- in consecutive weeks.

There have been seasons where two teams have gotten in the playoffs at .500 or worse, but it's always involved at least one wild card team. For instance, the Commanders won the NFC East with a 7-9 record in 2020 while the Bears got in as a wild card team with an 8-8 record. However, there has never been a situation where two divisional winners both finish .500 or worse. If that happens, the biggest losers will be the Packers and Rams, both being projected to miss the playoffs despite finishing above .500.

And now, we're down to our final eight teams.

If you're scoring at home, we are now down to four teams: Two teams that have never won a Super Bowl (Bills and Jaguars) and two teams that haven't won a Super Bowl in over 30 years. The 49ers' last title came in 1994, while the Bears haven't won a Lombardi Trophy since 1985, when they won their first and only Super Bowl.

We've been doing a projection every week since the start of the season, and here's a look at the projected Super Bowl each week.

As you can see, this is the first time all season the Bears have been projected to make the Super Bowl. According to our model, the Bears have a 17.96% chance of winning the NFC, with the 49ers sitting right behind them at 16.99%. There are only two other teams higher than 10%: the Lions (15.92%) and Vikings (13.22%).

As far as winning the Super Bowl, the computer says it's Bills or bust. Buffalo has a 15.5% chance of winning it all, which is a huge number when you consider that no other team is above 10%.
