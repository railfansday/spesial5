What NFL had to say about controversial call that took critical touchdown from Jaguars in loss to Giants

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The football world is abuzz over the no-touchdown call on a fourth–quarter pass to Jaguars wide receiver Parker Washington in Sunday's game against the Giants.

While it appeared that Washington had made the catch in the end zone with the Jaguars trailing the Giants 20-6 at the time, the officials ruled it an incomplete pass and confirmed the call after a video review.

The Giants went on to win the game 23-18.

New York Giants cornerback Dru Phillips (22) defends against Jacksonville Jaguars wide receiver Parker Washington (11) on Nov. 22, 2026, in Brooklyn, N.Y. Corey Sipkin for the NY POST

Everyone from Washington to the fans to CBS Sports rules analyst Gene Steratore all believed that Washington had control of the ball.

"I did not think he lost possession of the football. … I think it's a touchdown, fellas," Steratore said during the broadcast.

However, the NFL did not agree. In an interview with a pool reporter, NFL VP of instant replay Mark Butterworth said Washington did not have full control of the ball.

"The ruling on the field was an incomplete pass," Butterworth said. "First, to overturn a call, we need clear and obvious video evidence to overturn the on-field ruling. It was determined that the receiver did not gain full control of the ball until he was out of bounds."

In follow-up questions, he confirmed that he was referring to the fact that when Washington hit the ground, the ball was moving in the Jaguars receiver's hands.

Jaguars head coach Liam Coen told reporters after the game that the explanation he got was that "the ball switched hands, so that's what it came down to."

New York Giants cornerback Dru Phillips (22) defends Jacksonville Jaguars wide receiver Parker Washington (11) during a game on Sunday, Nov. 22, 2026. Corey Sipkin for the NY POST

Jacksonville had to settle for a field goal on the drive to only cut the Giants' lead to 20-9.

"I felt good about it, but they said it wasn't a catch, so had to move on and finish that game," Washington told reporters after the game.
