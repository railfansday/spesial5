IHSAA Football Plays of the Week from Semistate: Vote here!

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

This week of IHSAA football was full of excitement. Here are the IndyStar plays of the week nominees.

The Brownsburg touchdown on the ground comes from 66 yards out courtesy of Parker Condon. (1)

A backpedaling Anthony Dennison finds Mykell Tanner in the end zone for the Warren Central touchdown through the air. (2)

Landon Hall finds Mason Biernacki for a Penn Kingsmen red zone touchdown toss and catch. (3)

Carmel quarterback Jackson Treadway escapes the rush and finds the end zone himself. (4)

Rhys Shand hauls in the Evan Clark touchdown toss for Indian Creek from 35 yards out. (5)

Jack Sorgi finds Carter Cummings on the deep touchdown connection for Tri-West. (6)

Harrison Odom finds Julio Graves for the Bishop Chatard overtime touchdown. (7)

Ryan Minges takes this one himself, for the East Central touchdown on the ground. (8)
