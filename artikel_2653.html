Bills put OL Ar'maj Reed-Adams on injured reserve

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Bills announced a few roster moves on Tuesday, including one that landed a reserve offensive lineman on injured reserve.

Guard Ar’maj Reed-Adams was placed on the list. Reed-Adams was on the injury report with back and elbow issues last week, but he was not ruled out ahead of Sunday’s win over the Jets.

Reed-Adams was a sixth-round pick this year and he made his only regular season appearance in the team’s Week 9 win over the Vikings.

The Bills did not announce a corresponding addition to the roster, but they have wide receiver Tyrell Shavers in the process of returning from injured reserve.

The other two roster moves involved the practice squad. The Bills signed defensive back Te’Cory Couch and released guard Nick Broeker.
