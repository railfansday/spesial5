Wizards vs. Grizzlies Discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

It's gotten bad quickly in Washington, where the Wizards are 4-10 amid a seven-game losing streak that's contained five double-digit defeats.

After a 28-point blowout loss to the Houston Rockets on Sunday, Brian Keefe's squad returns to Capital One Arena to face the Memphis Grizzlies (9-4). It's a clash between a team exceeding expectations and one vastly underperforming them.

Tre Johnson (low back soreness) is available, per Brian Keefe. Johnson missed Sunday's contest.

AJ Dybantsa has been relegated to the wing and corner as more of a catch-and-shoot option in recent games. After taking just 2.6 threes per game over his first five NBA contests, Dybantsa is shooting 4.8 per game over his last five. His efficiency and scoring production have dipped as a result.

Keefe said the Wizards will continue to deploy their rookie in several roles.

"We're going to use him in a variety of different ways. That's the beauty of him. He's a versatile guy. He can play on and off ball. His defensive versatility, too. So, as we grow with our group, he will be used in lots of different situations this year."
