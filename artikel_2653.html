Bears back within 26-24 on D'Andre Swift TD run

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Bears and Lions are putting together a shootout in the second half in Detroit.

Bears running back D’Andre Swift broke loose for a 30-yard touchdown with 3:09 left in the third quarter. The score made it 26-24 Lions and was the third touchdown in three total possessions for the NFC North teams since halftime.

Swift also had an 18-yard catch during the drive and he has 134 yards from scrimmage already this afternoon. Kyle Monangai ran for the first two Bears touchdowns and the team now has 126 yards on the ground on Thanksgiving.

The Bears have shown they can put points on the board. Now they need to show they can make enough stops to make them winners.
