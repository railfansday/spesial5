WR Luther Burden, RB Kyle Monangai full participants Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Wide receiver Luther Burden III (concussion) and running back Kyle Monangai (toe) were full participants for the Bears on Wednesday.

The Bears didn’t practice as they got on the field earlier this week, coming off their bye week, so their injury report is based on practices from earlier in the week.

Coach Ben Johnson said Tuesday that Burden had cleared concussion protocol.

Burden was concussed late in the Bears’ 27-24 win over the Buccaneers on Nov. 8.

Defensive back Malik Muhammad (toe) was also a full participant.

Slot cornerback Kyler Gordon injured his shoulder in Week 9 and was out of practice this week.

Defensive lineman Jeffery Simmons (knee) was limited.
