Marvin Harrison Jr. held in check in Week 9

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Houston Texans WR Marvin Harrison Jr. caught two of his three targets for 20 yards against the Los Angeles Chargers in Week 9.

Why it Matters: The change of scenery hasn't done much to jumpstart Harrison's season. In three games with the Texans, he has a total of six catches for 60 yards. At this point, he is no more than a speculative WR5.
