NFL Thanksgiving Eve Football: Green Bay Packers vs Los Angeles Rams

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Week 12 kicks off with a Thanksgiving eve game before the traditional 3-game slate of Thanksgiving day football slate kicks off tomorrow. There will also be a Black Friday game to squeeze even more football into the holiday week. Today's game features the Green Bay Packers visiting the Los Angeles Rams. Both teams are 6-4 but sit in 4th- and 3rd-place respectively due to the strength on their divisions. If they played in the NFC East or South they would either be a half-game back or in 1st place.

Who: Green Bay Packers (6-4) at Los Angeles Rams (6-4)
