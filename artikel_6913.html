The 12 Best Puffer Jackets You Can Buy Right Now

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Every product was carefully curated by an Esquire editor. We may earn a commission from these links. Here’s how we test products and why you should trust us.

We love a barn jacket. We love a blazer. But when it’s below freezing and snowing sideways, neither is going to do you much good. That’s puffer jacket weather, and no number of layered sweaters under your favorite jacket is going to change the forecast.

Luckily, there’s no shortage of puffer jackets out there. The trick is finding one that’s warm enough, durable enough, and ready to stand up to whatever precipitation the winter sky throws its way—all while looking good enough that you don’t feel like the Michelin Tire Man every time you leave the house. The good news? There are plenty of puffer jackets that fit the bill, from luxury options from Canada Goose and Moncler to more affordable ones from REI and Carhartt.

There is a range in how exactly a puffer gets its shape, too. Most are down-filled, and the way the stitching is designed affects the puffiness. The fewer the stitches, the bigger the puffs. For something more packed down, there are ones with denser stitching that make for a slimmer-fitting jacket. Then you get into the nitty-gritty: Is the hood removable? Can you wash it at home? Is it really, truly, waterproof? Start your shopping with these puffer jackets you can buy right now and wear for many cold days to come.

It layers well, keeps you warm, and is durable enough if you need to wear it on a legit mountain—all this while being super lightweight and easy to pack.

While finding the perfect puffer jacket isn’t rocket science, there are a few key features you should focus on to narrow your search down from the 20 options above.

Insulation: Some climates call for the most intense down fill power there is, and some simply don’t. You don’t want to overheat this winter, nor do you want to be too cold, so go for a puffer with fill or insulation that your climate calls for. Don’t make the mistake of buying an 850-fill-power puffer if you live in a city where the coldest it ever gets is, like, 59 degrees.

Features: Not all puffer jackets are made the same. Personally, I need a removable hood. Look for design features including adjustability at the hem and hood, windproof fabric, zippered or buttoned pockets, a mock neck, and more, depending on your preferences.

Puffiness: If you’re someone who travels a lot and needs to stash your puffer in a suitcase from time to time, going for a super-puffy option probably isn’t going to be your best bet. Instead, look for puffers with a little less, well, puff, which will make them much easier to stow and pack. Some on this list even come with their own stow bags.

Being based in New York City means we know what makes a good puffer jacket. The options we listed above bring the heat in terms of functionality and style, all season long. Over the years, our editors have tried hundreds of styles and brands. Narrowing it down to our 20 favorites was no small feat, but these are just the picks that we swear by and return to time and time again for our winter wear.

Fill power is the number that tells you the quality of down in your jacket. The number technically indicates how many cubic inches of loft a single ounce of down has, but for simplicity, just focus on whether the number is relatively high or low—the higher it is, the higher-quality the insulation is: 600 is good, 700 is great, and 800 is spectacular.

Over time, as down goes through washing machines, tough weather conditions, and daily life, it can lose its fluffiness and slightly deflate your puffer. It’ll still keep you warm and dry, sure, just not quite so puffy.
