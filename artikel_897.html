Players Championship Finals draw confirmed with Littler not in field

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The draw for the Players Championship Finals in Minehead has been set following the conclusion of the ProTour events; top 64 from the ProTour have qualified for event which takes place on November 27-29

The Players Championship Finals draw has been confirmed, with Luke Littler not defending his title in Minehead.

Littler did not enter enough behind-closed doors Players Championship events this year to give himself a realistic chance of being in the top 64 on the ProTour ranking to qualify for the last tournament before the World Darts Championship.

Josh Rock and Nathan Aspinall were among the other stars to miss out but all of darts' other big names have qualified, with Dutchman Wessel Nijman the top seed as he won six Players Championship events this year.

Nijman will face England's Henry Coates, who was the final qualifier, in the first round while tournament favourite Luke Humphries goes up against Darryl Pilgrim.

Seven-time Players Championship Finals champion Michael van Gerwen takes on Connor Scutt, while former world champions Gary Anderson and Rob Cross are in line to meet in the second round.

Beau Graves will face former Lakeside champion Christian Kist and there could be an exciting clash between Gerwyn Price and Gian van Veen should they win their opening matches.

The schedule will be confirmed at a later date with the tournament running from November 27-29.
