Packers Inactives: LB Zaire Franklin, RB Josh Jacobs out vs. Los Angeles Rams

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The Green Bay Packers will be without linebacker Zaire Franklin, who has missed the last month with a shin fracture. Franklin had been a limited participant in practice throughout the week leading up to the Packers' matchup against the Los Angeles Rams, but he ultimately couldn't go on Wednesday.

Beyond Franklin, the other notable scratch for today's game is going to be running back Josh Jacobs, who is recovering from a fractured ring finger on his right hand. Like Franklin, Jacobs was a limited participant throughout the week.

Without Franklin, Green Bay will likely start Isaiah McDuffie at the Mike position and Ty'Ron Hopper at the Will position tonight.
