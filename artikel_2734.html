Bengals' Brown earns AFC recognition after big Steelers performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Cincinnati Bengals running back Chase Brown is getting big-time recognition after his performance against the Pittsburgh Steelers.

SB Nation's Doug Farrar named Brown among his AFC Secret Superstars for Week 10 on Wednesday, Nov. 18.

Brown joined Jacksonville Jaguars wide receiver Parker Washington and Indianapolis Colts cornerback Justin Walley on the list.

The Bengals running back finished Sunday's 38-18 victory with 10 carries for 44 yards and eight receptions for 39 yards. He also caught two touchdown passes from Joe Burrow.

However, Farrar highlighted Brown's pass protection capability, a less flashy part of his game, as the RB repeatedly picked up blitzing defenders, helping Burrow find receivers downfield.

His contributions even caught Burrow's eye.

"We know what we have in Chase," Burrow said, according to the Bengals' official postgame transcript. "He's a big-time playmaker for us. He's been lights out in pass protection too."

The quarterback explained that Brown's improvement has opened up more possibilities on third down, even with veteran Samaje Perine available.

Brown's versatility gives Cincinnati a Swiss Army knife of a weapon alongside Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins.

The Bengals have won five consecutive games and sit atop the AFC North at 7-2.

And in the end, Brown's recent recognition exemplifies the growing importance of his all-around play in Cincinnati's offense.
