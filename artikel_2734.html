Hartwick athletes do double duty on community service

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Hartwick College Athletics raised almost $2,000 with a pair of successful community giving events on Nov. 7 and Nov. 10.

The Hawk athletes raised $1,895 for the Oneonta Boys & Girls Club while collecting more than 500 nonperishable food donations for Saturday's Bread, according to a media release from the college.

"I am so grateful to the Oneonta and surrounding communities for their incredible support of two important community organizations that make a difference in the lives of so many families," Paul Vecchio, executive director of athletics, said in the media release. "To be able to support our community during this season of gratitude and giving is something we are extremely proud and fortunate to be able to do."

Fans supported the local organizations through 50/50 raffles, admission proceeds and nonperishable food donations during Hartwick's home football game against Utica University on Nov. 7 and the men's and women's basketball doubleheader against SUNY Oneonta on Nov. 10.

The raffles and basketball admission proceeds helped Hartwick raise the money for the Oneonta Boys & Girls Club.

More than 350 patrons contributed food items to support Saturday's Bread.

Hawks seek to defend ECAC volleyball title

Hartwick will be a No. 2 seed in defense of its ECAC title.

The Eastern College Athletic Conference announced the 2026 Division III Women's Volleyball Championship field for the league finals Saturday, Nov. 21.

Marywood University is the top seed and will host the championship, while Hartwick is the second seed and Wilkes is the third.

Hartwick College and Wilkes will play at 4 p.m., with the winner playing Marywood.

The Hawks made it to their second straight Empire 8 Championship game over the weekend, falling to St. John Fisher in straight sets. Libero Maila Dake and middle blocker Lucille Walker each made the All-Tournament Team.

Hartwick defeated Keene State and NJCU to win last year's ECAC championship.
