New Zealand vs. India 3rd ODI live score, win probability and chances of victory in Hamilton

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

New Zealand will hope to get on the board as they take on India in the 3rd ODI of the five-match series at Seddon Park, Hamilton on Tuesday.

The visitors jumped to a 2-0 lead after a narrow four-run win over New Zealand on Saturday via DLS. Indian pacers Auqib Nabi (2/31) and Gurnoor Brar (4/3) set up victory as they skittled out the hosts for 134 runs in 41 overs.

India then chased down the rain-shortened target of 110 in 26 overs.

Batting has been a concern for New Zealand in the ODI series as their batters have struggled to tackle the seam and swing on fresh wickets. Mitchell Santner's side is also dealing with a few injury concerns, including that of pacers Lockie Ferguson and Matt Henry.

The toss is scheduled to take place at 7:00 a.m. IST on Tuesday, November 10.
