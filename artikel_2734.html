49ers make critical practice field upgrade as Bay Area braces for rain and flooding

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The 49ers have done a lot to optimize their preparation for games this season.

They spent a week in Melbourne, Australia to prepare for their season opener against the Rams, who opted to fly in the day before. They also just finished a week-long stay in Colorado Springs, Colo. ahead of Sunday night's game in Mexico City to adjust to extreme altitude of Estadio Banorte.

San Francisco 49ers general manager John Lynch, left, and head coach Kyle Shanahan watch training camp drills in Santa Clara, Calif., Thursday, July 27, 2023. AP

During their time away, the 49ers made a significant addition to their practice facility to help with their preparation for the rest of the way.

According to a report from the San Francisco Standard, the team constructed a canopy over their practice field to provide shelter from rain with a wet El Niño expected in the coming weeks and months.

The inclement weather is expected to start this week as the 49ers prepare to host the Seattle Seahawks on Sunday at Levi's Stadium. The Bay Area is bracing for flooding thanks to a combination of "King Tides" and heavy rain, in what might be the wettest rainy season in recent memory.

A new canopy covers part of the 49ers' practice field next to Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara, Calif. (Photo courtesy of the San Francisco 49ers) Photo courtesy of the San Francisco 49ers

The team is hoping the 70-yard canopy over one of their three practice fields will allow them to practice uninterrupted during the rain.

The team is currently 9-1 and the heavy favorite to land the No. 1 seed in the NFC playoffs. They've done so twice since Kyle Shanahan became head coach in 2017 and went to the Super Bowl both times.

Trent Williams (71) of the San Francisco 49ers blocks for Christian McCaffrey (23) against the Seattle Seahawks in Seattle, Sunday, Oct. 11, 2026. Getty Images

The canopy joins a growing list of upgrades the team has made to its facility. It replaced a turf practice field with natural grass during the 2025 offseason and spent $9 million on improving their training and rehab facilities with hydrotherapy equipment, including hot and cold tubs, before this year.

Of course, the team's practice facility has drawn headlines for being directly next to an electrical substation, which some have speculated played a role in the team's lengthy injury issues over the past few seasons.

The team has pushed back on that idea after hiring scientists to study the issue.

Regardless, the 49ers seem committed to investing in their preparation, whether it's spending big on travel to acclimate for games or with improvements to their practice facility.
