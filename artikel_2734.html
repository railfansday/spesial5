Panthers activate CB Mike Jackson

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Cornerback Mike Jackson declared himself ready to return to action earlier this week and the Panthers officially added him to the roster on Saturday.

Jackson has been activated off of injured reserve after missing six games due to core muscle surgery. Jackson had six tackles while starting the first three games of the season for Carolina.

The Panthers did not activate running back Jonathon Brooks or offensive lineman Brady Christensen, so they will remain on injured reserve for the time being.

In addition to activating Jackson and placing safety Isaiah Simmons on injured reserve, the Panthers have elevated linebackers Nick Hampton and Ja’Markis Weston from the practice squad.
