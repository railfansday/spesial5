LB Cole Holcomb inactive for the Steelers on Friday

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The most notable absence on the Steelers side of the field on Friday afternoon will be Aaron Rodgers, but the team also has players still on their roster who will not be playing against the Broncos.

Linebacker Cole Holcomb was the only player on either team listed as questionable heading into the game and he will sit out with a heel injury. Holcomb has been a core special teamer for Pittsburgh this season.

With Rodgers gone, Will Howard moved up to the No. 2 quarterback spot. Drew Allar is inactive as the emergency third quarterback and wide receiver Kaden Wetjen, cornerback Sean Murphy-Bunting, running back Eli Heidenreich, linebacker Alex Highsmith, and defensive end Gabe Rubio are also out.

The Broncos’ inactives are quarterback Sam Ehlinger, wide receiver Lil’Jordan Humphrey, safety Taylor Rapp, offensive lineman Gavin Ortega, offensive lineman Kage Casey, and tight end Dallen Bentley.
