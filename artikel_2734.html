Reporter notices worrying flaw with the Bills’ new $1.4B stadium amid extreme weather

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The new Highmark Stadium has endured its first real weather test on Thanksgiving ahead of the Buffalo Bills vs Kansas City Chiefs.

Rain has been pouring down in Buffalo for a number of hours ahead of kickoff on this Thanksgiving Thursday. The game will mark the new $1.4 billion stadium's first real experience of hosting a game in the extreme elements.

This is certainly something the Bills fans will be used to, but it seems as if the team's state-of-the-art stadium is already struggling to deal with the harsh conditions on what is its first real test.

Rain is certainly not uncommon in Buffalo, with the winter months also seeing considerable snowfall. Despite the significant amount of money spent on the new home of the Bills, it seems as if the drainage system has been unable to keep up with the downpour.

"It's been raining consistently in Buffalo for about two hours. By my view from the press box, the Highmark field is struggling to hold up. The field looks like a golf fairway with nobody replacing the divots," said Chiefs beat reporter Pete Sweeney on X.

This will certainly be a concern to the Bills supporters. A huge amount of money has been invested in building the new stadium, yet some of the bare minimum expectations do not seem to have been met.

Poor drainage is going to be a big problem for Buffalo, with the game potentially set to be impacted because of the rain sitting on the surface. The decision not to install a roof on the new stadium was a bold choice, and it may already be one coming back to haunt the Bills.
