Bayern Munich star Harry Kane could miss three months

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Bayern Munich could be facing a significant attacking setback after Harry Kane reportedly suffered a syndesmosis tear that is expected to keep him out of action for approximately three months.

The England international was forced to leave Bayern's 1–1 draw against 1. FC Köln early after picking up the injury, leaving Vincent Kompany without his most important attacking weapon. What initially looked like another frustrating injury concern has now developed into a potentially devastating blow for the Bundesliga leader.

A three-month absence would mean Bayern Munich could be without Kane for a substantial portion of the season, including several important Bundesliga and European fixtures. Replacing his goals, movement, link-up play, and overall influence will be an enormous challenge, particularly given how central he has become to Kompany's attacking system.

The timing is especially unfortunate, with Bayern already dealing with Michael Olise's injury after the Frenchman also had to leave the Köln match at halftime.

Bayern will now have to consider how to manage without its leading scorer while hoping both attacking stars can eventually return to full fitness.
