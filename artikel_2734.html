LIVE DISCUSSION: Brooklyn Nets vs. Chicago Bulls, 7:30 PM ET

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

It hasn't been an easy road for the Nets or its fans, and there's no guaranteeing what happens on the court against the Bulls on Wednesday. At the very least, fans will be treated to Brooklyn's first Eras Night — highlighting the ABA Nets who played in New York and won two championships before the merger.

Unfortunately for the team it comes during difficult times. The Nets enter Wednesday at 3-10 after falling 135-123 to the Philadelphia 76ers on Monday. Brooklyn trailed by as many as 24 points before making a second-half push, but it was too little, too late. Julius Randle led the way with 33 points, while rookie Nolan Traoré added 23.
