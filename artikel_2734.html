NFL Week 10 Winners and Losers: Packers and Lions may be in trouble in NFC North race, leaving Vikings and Bears

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Before Sunday, the NFC North looked like it would be a four-team battle royal until the end.

Then a freak injury and a home blowout crystallized the race. We might be down to two teams for the division crown.

The Chicago Bears had the week off and they pushed forward a bit because it's hard to buy the Green Bay Packers and Detroit Lions making much of a move in the division anymore.

The Lions won in Munich early on Sunday but suffered one of the biggest losses of the season when star defensive end Aidan Hutchinson jumped up following a sack to celebrate, and when he came down he suffered a bad knee injury. His "Gramatica" moment is expected to keep him out for a while, via NFL Media. If he's done for the season, the Lions' defense is unlikely to keep the team afloat in the division race.

Detroit is 7-2 and it seems like the season is headed downhill. A defense that was already suspect has to go forward without Hutchinson and linebacker Jack Campbell, who is on injured reserve with a neck injury. It's especially rough that Hutchinson's season might have ended in such a fluky and unnecessary way.

For the Packers, their hot streak came to an abrupt end. The Minnesota Vikings, with Kirk Cousins becoming the first quarterback since 1950 to start for two different teams in back-to-back weeks according to Fox, blew out the Packers 30-10. The Vikings' defense was stifling yet again, including five sacks in the first half and eight overall. Jordan Love looked like a legitimate MVP candidate before Sunday and now he should fall back to the second tier in that race. The Packers (6-4) are just a game-and-a-half behind the Vikings (7-2) in the standings and there's a chance they could regain momentum when Micah Parsons returns to the lineup, but Sunday's home loss was a huge blow to their chances.

The Lions' major injury and Packers' defeat affected more than the NFC North race. NFC wild-card contenders like the Seahawks and Rams were paying attention too. It's not like the Packers and Lions are dead yet, but their paths to the playoffs got significantly tougher on Sunday.

Aidan Hutchinson of Detroit Lions goes down with a knee injury after celebrating getting a sack.

Perhaps we're already down to the Vikings and Bears in the race. The Vikings looked reborn with Cousins providing competent quarterback play five days after Minnesota's big trade with the Raiders. The Bears' offense has been fine all season and their trade for Jeffery Simmons could transform Chicago's defense in the same way Cousins can lift the Vikings' offense.

The race to win the best division in football got some clarity Sunday. It just wasn't good news in Detroit or Green Bay.

Here are the rest of the winners and losers from Week 10 of the NFL season:

Patrick Mahomes and Emmett Johnson: Without Kenneth Walker III on Sunday because of a groin injury, the Chiefs found out somethings about their offense.

The first thing they learned was that Johnson, a rookie fifth-round draft pick, took the positive things in a limited role behind Walker and showed he's a legit impact player in his own right. Johnson had a monster game for the Chiefs in a 35-13 win over the Atlanta Falcons. Johnson had 171 total yards and two receiving touchdowns.

The Chiefs know Mahomes is a star but it was nice to see him have his biggest game of the season. Without Walker, Mahomes threw for 348 yards and five touchdowns. It was Mahomes' first five-touchdown game since Week 1 of the 2022 season. He found a new weapon in Josh Downs. The Chiefs acquired him in a trade with the Colts on Tuesday and he scored a touchdown Sunday. He could be a nice addition for Kansas City.

The Chiefs are 7-2, and the way they won Sunday without Walker had to be satisfying.

Chuba Hubbard: He has had a very good season for the Carolina Panthers and it got better Sunday.

Hubbard had a career high with 172 total yards from scrimmage and came through when the Panthers needed it most. Trailing 14-10, Hubbard had a 34-yard catch to set up his 1-yard touchdown run with 1:49 left to give Carolina the lead. After the Saints tied it with a field goal, the Panthers got a quick big play on a pass interference call on a deep pass to Jalen Coker, setting up their own field-goal attempt. Ryan Fitzgerald nailed it from 54 yards as time expired and the Panthers won 20-17.

The Panthers are just 4-5 but right in the mix for the NFC South title. Hubbard has been a big part of those Panthers wins, and he had his best game Sunday.

Post-deadline Jacksonville Jaguars: The Jaguars knew they were in good shape to beat the Tennessee Titans on Sunday. So they decided to give big deadline acquisitions defensive tackle Jordan Davis and safety Jessie Bates III the week off to help rest and acclimate to their new team.

That gamble worked. The Jaguars won without those two defensive standouts, beating the Titans 40-23. Trevor Lawrence was fantastic with 352 yards and three touchdowns. The defense wasn't great, but it will get Davis and Bates in the lineup next week vs. the Giants and will improve immediately. The defense has been pretty good before Sunday too.

The Jaguars are a complete team and a Super Bowl contender. And it should get only better from here.

DeMeco Ryans and the Houston Texans' defense: Houston was supposed to have one of the best defenses in the NFL this season. It hasn't been, and on Sunday it was a big reason the Texans took a loss that is a huge blow to their playoff hopes.

The Texans allowed a game-winning drive to the Cleveland Browns in the final two minutes, with Quinshon Judkins scoring the go-ahead touchdown with 53 seconds left on a 5-yard run. The Browns' defense, which was excellent in the fourth quarter, held after that to clinch the 27-24 win.

The Texans were without a few key players because of injuries. They will argue, rightfully, that a huge unnecessary roughness penalty on cornerback Kwame Lassiter was just a defensive back going for the ball and the hit on the intended receiver wasn't worthy of a flag. It doesn't matter. The Texans are 3-6 and surprisingly, Ryans' defense is a major reason for that disappointing record.

Geno Smith: It has been a rough season for the New York Jets and Smith, and for the quarterback it got even worse.

Smith took a massive hit against the Buffalo Bills and even though he was cleared in the concussion protocol he was later ruled out with an ankle injury. The Jets had been playing well but Cade Klubnik couldn't keep the team in the game and New York lost to the Buffalo Bills 31-12.

The Jets (2-8) have won only once since Week 1. Smith, who was a short-term answer at quarterback anyway, could get pushed out of the starting spot for the rookie Klubnik soon, if it doesn't happen next week against the Chargers.

It's not like winning games is a high priority for the Jets the rest of the season. Smith got injured Sunday and the news might continue to get worse for him.

Kyle McCord: He did the best he could. Even against the Indianapolis Colts, it was asking a lot for a 2025 sixth-round draft pick taking his first NFL snaps to run a competent offense given Miami's supporting cast.

McCord helped the Miami Dolphins stay in the game. But trailing by two points late, he took a third-down sack with the Dolphins just outside of field-goal range. His fourth-down pass was batted down and the Colts survived with a 24-22 win. McCord was better than Daniel Jones, who was awful again. The Dolphins QB had 209 passing yards, almost double the 108 that Jones put up. He also had a rushing touchdown. But McCord couldn't get the play he needed at the end, and his fumble that was recovered in the end zone for a Colts touchdown was a big difference in the final.

McCord will get more chances with Malik Willis on injured reserve for at least three more games. His first effort wasn't bad, it just wasn't good enough for the win.

NFL fans in Germany: The NFL tried to give its German fans a good matchup. The New England Patriots vs. the Detroit Lions was a fun one, until one sack at the end of the Patriots' first drive.

Quarterback Drake Maye suffered an ankle injury on a sack (which was a borderline hip drop tackle) by Lions defensive end Aidan Hutchinson, then Hutchinson suffered a knee injury celebrating. That took out two of the biggest stars in the game. The Patriots' offense, with Tommy DeVito at quarterback, was unwatchable after that. New England, with Maye, had 60 yards on the opening drive and 91 on the 11 drives after that in a 35-10 loss.

There were some things for German fans to cheer. Lions receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown, whose mother is German and carried the Deutschland flag onto the field during pregame introductions, had two fun touchdowns.

Jahmyr Gibbs' brilliance was on full display with 153 yards rushing, 99 yards receiving and two touchdowns. There were some highlights. But it was an ugly game after Maye left it due to injury.
