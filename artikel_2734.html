Drake Maye injury feared to be fractured ankle, insider says. Latest on Patriots QB

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The New England Patriots' season is hanging in the balance and a disastrous trip to Germany is just the latest example. Injury issues have been front and center all season, but Drake Maye had not been the subject of them until this point.

Maye exited the Patriots' Week 10 loss to the Detroit Lions with an ankle injury and didn't return. It occurred in the first quarter and essentially set the tone for the day as New England was blown out, 35-10. On the same play that injured Maye, the Lions also lost Aidan Hutchinson with what has been described as a "significant" knee injury.

New England now enters its bye week, but it's a vacation that will be overshadowed by concern for Maye, especially after the latest update did not paint a pretty picture of his short-term future.

Maye does not have a set timetable for a return, but multiple reports on Monday, Nov. 16, indicate that the Patriots' quarterback will miss significant time with a leg injury.

ESPN and NFL Media insider Ian Rapoport reported that Maye's injury may be severe enough to cause him to miss the rest of the season. The insider went on to write that the quarterback "will seek more medical information" following his team's return to the United States from Germany.Insider Adam Schefter later reported that Maye may have "fractured his ankle" and will undergo further testing to decide if surgery is necessary.

That sentiment echoes what head coach Mike Vrabel told reporters after the game – that the team would learn more once they returned home.

"(Maye) obviously wasn't able to finish the game, so we'll have to get some more tests run," Vrabel said at his postgame news conference.

"And we'll see, you know, when we come back what it ends up being and the severity of whatever injury he has."
