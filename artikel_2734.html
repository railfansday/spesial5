Memphis Grizzlies add center depth, sign Lawson Lovering to 10-day contract

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Memphis Grizzlies have signed center Lawson Lovering to a 10-day contract, the team announced on Nov. 27.

Lovering is being added via an injury hardship exception. With Cam Boozer, GG Jackson, Scotty Pippen Jr. and Jerami Grant all dealing with injuries sideling them at least multiple weeks, the league allowed the Grizzlies to add Lovering.

Memphis is also dealing with injury concerns at center. Isaiah Stewart, Zach Edey and Quinten Post have all been banged up at separate times this season.

Lovering is someone who has familiarity with the Grizzlies. He has spent time with the Grizzlies during summer league and played in two games during the 2025-26 season.

His most recent action within the organization came with the Memphis Hustle. In four games this season with the Hustle, Lovering is averaging nine points, 5.3 rebounds and one block per game.

Lovering is a seven-foot center who finished his college career at Utah before going undrafted in 2025.
