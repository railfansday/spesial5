Michael Dorn Reveals Paramount’s Plan For Worf To Replace Sisko On Star Trek: Deep Space Nine

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Paramount wanted Michael Dorn's Worf to replace Avery Brooks' Captain Benjamin Sisko on Star Trek: Deep Space Nine.

After seven seasons of Star Trek: The Next Generation and appearing in 1994's Star Trek Generations, Dorn joined Star Trek: Deep Space Nine season 4 as Lt. Commander Worf in 1995. Dorn played Worf in 102 episodes of DS9, and he directed three episodes. However, Paramount is rumored to have pondered the idea of Worf taking over DS9 from Captain Sisko.

Appearing on The Show People Podcast, Michael Dorn humbly answered, "Yeah," when host Andrew Keats suggested that he saved Star Trek: Deep Space Nine by joining the cast as Worf. Dorn then discussed "revisionist history" about Worf's impact on DS9 before discussing "a story" he heard that Paramount considered replacing Avery Brooks' Captain Sisko with Dorn's Worf as the series lead. Read Michael's quote below:

Michael Dorn: “There is another story I heard from I won’t tell you who… that they were going to possibly replace Avery with me. And it was a thought, and it was kind of going around Paramount just a little bit, and they said, ‘Oh no no, we can’t do that.”

Star Trek: Deep Space Nine's ratings in syndication for its first three seasons, but Paramount was concerned about fans abandoning the series. Porting Michael Dorn as Worf gave DS9 some of Star Trek: The Next Generation's prestige, since the Klingon was one of TNG's most popular characters. Paramount did want the Klingons to become the focus of DS9 season 4, which forced the writers to backburner their plans to ramp up the Dominion War saga.

Whether Paramount was serious about Michael Dorn replacing Avery Brooks as Star Trek: Deep Space Nine's series lead or not, they wisely didn't pursue this course. Brooks was a respected leading man, and Captain Sisko was intricately at the center of DS9's mythology as the Emissary of Bajor's Prophets. It's also hard to imagine showrunner Ira Steven Behr going along with booting Brooks in favor of Dorn.

Michael Dorn's deal with Star Trek: Deep Space Nine allowed him to continue appearing as Worf in Star Trek: The Next Generation's movies.

Worf joining Star Trek: Deep Space Nine delivered the boost in ratings and fan interest Paramount wanted. As Strategic Operations Officer, the surly Klingon fit into the morally complex environment of DS9. Working with a new cast, Worf grew as a character and enjoyed his most significant romantic relationship and tragedy: falling in love with and marrying Lt. Commander Jadzia Dax (Terry Farrell) before she was murdered soon after their wedding in Star Trek: Deep Space Nine season 6.

Thanks to his four-season run on Star Trek: Deep Space Nine, followed by his comeback 20 years later in Star Trek: Picard season 3, Michael Dorn has appeared as Worf in more episodes of Star Trek than anyone else, and his 284 episodes total is a record that will never be broken. It's hard to imagine Dorn's Worf replacing Avery Brooks' Captain Sisko entirely, and thankfully, that never happened.
