Raycast combines voice dictation and screen awareness in new AI feature

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Following the recent release of Screen Awareness, Raycast got an interesting update this week that lets users talk to AI about whatever is on their screen without having to explain the context. Here are the details.

Last August, Raycast announced Screen Awareness, which lets users bring context from their active app or window directly into AI Chat, “so you don’t have to copy text, attach screenshots, or explain what you’re looking at,” said Raycast.

This week, Raycast took that functionality a step further with Dictate to AI, a new command that lets users speak their requests while automatically providing AI with context from the active app.
