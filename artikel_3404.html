9 Years Later, Smosh’s Best Event Makes an Epic Comeback

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Since 2002, Smosh has pioneered creator-first entertainment on platforms like YouTube. Smosh has delivered sketch shows, board game nights, improv comedy, and more, building a loyal fandom.

Smosh’s Summer and Winter Games are among the media brand’s most popular events that pit members of the Smosh Family against one another in a series of competitions. While the Summer Games returned in 2025 with “Class of 2005,” the Winter Games haven’t been held since 2017 — until now.

However, the cozy Christmas festivities are interrupted by the arrival of Smosh’s “mayor”​​​​​​​ (who looks suspiciously like Damien Haas), who announces that a spirit is coming to ruin the Smosh Family’s fun​​​​​​​. What spirit is that? The competitive spirit, of course.

The two-week event will start on Nov. 30 and run through Dec. 12​​​​​​​ ​​​​​​​across the Smosh, Smosh Games, Smosh Pit, SmoshCast, and SmoshAlike channels​​​​​​​.

Smosh tells Polygon that the competition will kick off with an obstacle course and feature a series of challenges, including Smosh’s take on a White Elephant party, a trip to Santa’s Workshop, and a showdown called Steal Back Christmas. All of this will culminate in a talent show to save the holidays.

As everyone knows,​​​​​​​ it’s not really Christmas without at least one fight. This year, Smosh takes things to the extreme with a twist usually seen in reality competition shows: cast members can vote each other off their teams, adding a new layer of strategy to the holiday challenges.

“We’ve never done a Summer or Winter Games this big,” said Anders Norberg, who helped develop this year’s Winter Games format. “We’ve added some new twists that will keep everyone on their toes. Whether you’re naughty, nice, or a little bit of a Grinch, you’ll find something to love.”

After a nine-year hiatus, the Winter Games look set to bring the heat​​​​​​​ in a way that will keep you nice and toasty throughout the Christmas season. Smosh fans, get ready for Nov. 30, when the Winter Games officially kick off.
