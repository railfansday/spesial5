Carlos Rivera Wins Big at 2026 Heat Latin Music Awards: Complete Winners List

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The popularly known Premios Heat were hosted for the first time in Puerto Vallarta, Mexico, on Thursday night (Nov. 5).

Carlos Rivera was the big winner at the 2026 Heat Latin Music Awards on Thursday (Nov. 5), nabbing the awards for best male artist, best music video for “Sin Despedida” in collaboration with Alejandro Fernández, and album of the year for ¡Vida México! Other winners of the night included Karol G as best female artist, Grupo Firme as regional Mexican artist, and Ovy on the Drums as producer of the year. For the first time in the ceremony’s history two Cuban reparto acts took center stage. Pututi and Nesty performed a medley of “Efimero” and “Los Despechaos”—the latter peaked at  No. 7 on the Latin Rhythm Airplay chart this year; and Rey Tony and Helabusador performed “Dichavate,” their collaboration with Ya Ice Dilan that won best viral song.

The gala’s 2026 edition was hosted for the first time in Mexico, in collaboration with the government of Jalisco and the municipality of Puerto Vallarta. Previously, Premios Heat took place in the Dominican Republic and Colombia.
