House Of The Dragon Officially Returns In The Realm's Delight

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

With House of the Dragon not expected to release its fourth, and final, season until mid-2028, we have a long time to wait until we find out how the Dance of the Dragons will come to an end. However, in the meantime, fans of the award-winning Game of Thrones spin-off show can look forward to The Realm’s Delight, which will see Rhaenyra Targaryen enter the spotlight once more.

Premiering back in June, season three of House of the Dragon was a hit, with our very own Angel Shaw awarding the first half of the season an almost-perfect 9/10 in their review. They wrote:

Still, these four episodes of House of the Dragon season 3 mark only the halfway point of this penultimate installment, and it's no doubt an excellent start. I'm eager to see what comes next, and perhaps even those canon changes will pay off, said and done. So long as this momentum continues, we can certainly count on House of the Dragon season 3 to prove us wrong about Game of Thrones endings.

Now, whilst we wait to see how it all ends, Game of Thrones: Dragonfire is set to take us back to the beginning as a much younger Rhaenyra Targaryen returns to the fray.

First launching back in June of this year, Game of Thrones: Dragonfire is an Android and iOS 4X strategy and role-playing game developed by Warner Bros. Games. Inspired by the events of House of the Dragon, it tasks players with hatching, raising, and commanding dragons as they conquer the skies of Westeros.

Featuring iconic characters such as Rhaenyra Targaryen, Aegon II Targaryen, and Aemond Targaryen, a younger Rhaenyra can now be unlocked, with her appearance being based on her portrayal by Milly Alcock in the HBO Max television show.

"The only surviving child of King Viserys I and his first queen, Aemma Arryn, Rhaenyra is named her father’s heir, defying centuries of tradition," the official Game of Thrones: Dragonfire blog post reads. "Headstrong and fiercely intelligent, Rhaenyra rides the dragon Syrax and has grown up in the shadow of the Iron Throne. The smallfolk of King’s Landing call her “the Realm’s Delight,” though the great lords whisper that a woman’s claim, however strong in blood, may yet be tested by men who will not suffer a queen.”

By completing either Chapter Quests or Urgent Quests, players will be able to unlock Rhaenyra Targaryen, and as a straightforward, army-wide Commander, she has the ability to empower every dragon under her command, but dragons with the Nimble or Stalwart traits will synergize with her even more.
