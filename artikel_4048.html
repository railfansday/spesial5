DOOM: The Dark Ages Revelations Gets A Major Free 'Slaughter Mode' Update Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The release of DOOM: The Dark Ages Revelations saw critics heaping praise on the expansion earlier in 2026, to the point where it's actually even in the Game of the Year (or at least Expansion of the Year) conversation as things stand.

News Xbox Has Another Potential GOTY Candidate On Its Hands, Even Though It's Just DLC

The reviews for Revelations have been absurdly good

The DLC is getting even better today as well, as a new November 16th updating is introducing "Slaughter Mode - an endless combat challenge in which you need to tear through waves of hordes and unlock rewards along the way.

DOOM developer id Software says no two runs in Slaughter Mode are identical due to the random encounters it throws at you, and there will also be special unlockable skins and weekly challenges thrown into the mix.

"Complete rounds to earn Skulls, which unlock rewards such as Slaughter Mode-only Slayer skins, weapon skins for all modes and profile icons. You can also unlock tracks from Andrew Hulshult’s IDKFA soundtrack to play in both Slaughter Mode and the Ripatorium! Take on weekly challenges for more opportunities to earn rewards and show off your slaying prowess."

All of this is available to you if you own the Revelations DLC for DOOM: The Dark Ages already, or if you have the Premium / Premium Upgrade versions of DOOM: The Dark Ages.

Don't forget that the base game is currently included with Xbox Game Pass Ultimate, Premium and PC Game Pass as well, so if you just want the DLC to play on Game Pass, you're looking at paying £17.99 / $19.99 for it.

We'll leave you with more details about the new Slaughter Mode for DOOM The Dark Ages: Revelations below.

Tear through relentless, escalating and unpredictable waves of demonic hordes, carving out your personal best scores and unlocking rewards using the Skulls collected from the carnage. With each checkpoint reached, players must ask themselves if they want to cash in their run or risk it all for more slaughter. No two runs through Slaughter Mode are identical as you fight escalating rounds of random encounters, bosses and arenas, turning up the heat as you proceed further into your run.
