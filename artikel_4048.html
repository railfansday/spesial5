The Outer Worlds 2 PS5 DLC Split into Two Parts, First in December

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Obsidian still wants to carry out its post-launch DLC plans for The Outer Worlds 2, having lost 25% of its workforce in a recent round of layoffs. It's today announced an expansion called Side Effect Guaranteed will release in two parts: the first launches for PS5 on 2nd December 2026, and then the second arrives in Spring 2027. See it in action in the trailer.

The new DLC takes place just before the end of the main game, so if you've already finished a playthrough, there'll be a new option on the starting menu screen that lets you access the content through a new character.

A distress signal beckons you and your crew of the Incognito to a derelict ship, where you discover the ship’s crew in suspended animation. All signs point to foul play, especially the puddle of what was most likely a person that you recover a video message from. It was left by the captain of the vessel, who explains that the crew has been contaminated with something while on a heist that will cause explosive cell death unless they find a cure. And by explosive cell death, she means more like a… “liquefaction”.

Side Effect Guaranteed adds a new region to explore called Elysium Valley, another recruitable character, a level cap increase to 36, more than 15 new perks, four extra flaws, and additional gear (weapons, armour, and outfits). You can also look forward to an additional 20 songs.

The expansion costs $14.99, or if you bought the Premium Edition of the game, you'll gain instant access to the DLC.

Review The Outer Worlds 2 (PS5) - Mechanically Fantastic RPG Struggles to Be Memorable
