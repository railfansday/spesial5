Kirk Cousins on possibly playing 18 games: "Just part of the deal"

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Because Vikings quarterback Kirk Cousins was traded after the Vikings’ bye but before the Raiders’ bye, Cousins won’t have a bye in 2026.

Which means he’ll potentially play 18 regular-season games.

During a Thursday press conference, Cousins was asked whether he has considered the possibility of playing a game on each and every weekend of the 2026 regular season.

“I thought about it,” Cousins told reporters. “I thought about it, but added to the altitude, added to the new system, added to not knowing people’s names. Just part of the deal. And, again, kind of one minute at a time, one day at a time, one practice at a time, and kind of keep blinders on, and we’ll look up, and it’ll be March.

“But until then, I think I’m just kind of, keeping it, you know, the short game, if you will, short view in front of me, just trying to stay focused there.”

It’s the right attitude. It’s the only attitude. He has gone from a situation in which he was destined to be replaced with rookie Fernando Mendoza to a team with a 7-2 record and a realistic shot not only at making it to the postseason but also of advancing through multiple rounds when it gets there.

And he’s right about the imminent arrival of March. Given the speed with which the season unfolds, it’ll be over before anyone knows it. It’s a finite window of time and, as Cousins said after Sunday’s win over the Packers, “There’s more to the story being written.”
