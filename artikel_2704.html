Washington Commanders vs. Cincinnati Bengals inactives in NFL Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Washington Commanders released their final injury report for Monday night's game against the Bengals on Saturday and then made several roster moves earlier in today ahead of the game.

The Commanders didn't elevate anyone from the practice squad for tonight's game. Instead they activated RB Bill Croskey-Merritt from injured reserve. He had missed the last four games with a groin injury, but will be active tonight. CB Isaac Yiadom was released to make room for Bill on the roster.

Amik Robertson suffered an ankle injury on the last play of Friday's practice and was considered a game-time decision. He will not be active tonight after struggling in pregame workouts. Head coach Dan Quinn said that Rasul Douglas and Trey Amos will start with Mike Sainristil and Fabian Moreau getting time in the slot.
