Austin Reaves ‘improving,’ no structural damage with injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After exiting Wednesday's win over the Milwaukee Bucks midway through the third quarter, Los Angeles Lakers star Austin Reaves missed Sunday's victory against the Phoenix Suns with a bruised heel. Two days prior, head coach JJ Redick spoke with reporters and said Reaves is day-to-day while considering the injury to be minor. Prior to the game Sunday, Redick provided an update on Reaves.

"He's improving. Again, it really comes down to just like a pain tolerance thing," Redick said. "Nothing structural beyond a bruise. I think every day it's been better and we'll see where he's at in 48 hours when we play on Tuesday."

While it's far from set in stone, it sounds like Reaves has a chance to play Tuesday against the Charlotte Hornets. Although he was ruled out on Saturday for the Suns game, Redick didn't declare him out when he talked with the media on Friday, so it seems as though this is a game-to-game injury, at worst. That's an encouraging prognosis and suggests Reaves will be back in the lineup before too long.

The Lakers play again Wednesday against the Utah Jazz for the second half of a back-to-back set, so it's possible Reaves would only suit up for one of those games regardless, if he's even ready to go. He missed the second leg of Los Angeles' back-to-back last week against the Atlanta Hawks, too, so caution may be the guiding principle here.

In Reaves' absence Sunday evening, Luka Dončić continued his dazzling play behind 43 points — including 20 in the first quarter — and nine assists while Quentin Grimes rejoined the squad after missing the previous two games with a finger injury and added 22 points on 7-of-10 shooting (5-of-6 beyond the arc). Collin Sexton notched 16 points on 6-of-11 shooting to extend his own recent stretch of strong play, too.

Of course, the Lakers are at their best with Reaves available, so they'll hope he's back as soon as possible and it's a positive development that he's merely navigating a pain tolerance dynamic rather than any serious issues. The Lakers and Hornets face off at 8 p.m. Tuesday night.
