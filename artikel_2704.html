American Flat Track Returns To Arizona In 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Photo by Scott Hunter for AMA Pro Racing.

Good news for dirt racing fans in Arizona, as American Flat Track will be returning to the state for the first time since 2023. The series confirmed Central Arizona Raceway in Casa Grande will host the Sidewinder Half-Mile on April 3, 2027. The event will be the Grand National Championship's first ever visit to the high-banked clay oval at the Pinal County Fairgrounds.

The last time AFT raced in the state was at the 2023 Arizona Super TT at Wild Horse Pass Motorsports Park (now known as Firebird Motorsports Park) in Chandler, Arizona. The facility was slated to close due to the expansion of Interstate 10, creating some uncertainty. Ultimately, the track remained open under its new name, while AFT moved on. Ironically, the new venue is a 40-mile drive from Firebird Motorsports Park ... via I-10.

Central Arizona Raceway may be new to AFT, but its racing roots go back to 1939. Promoter Brad Whitfield took over on a five-year lease in 2022, renamed it Central Arizona Raceway, and set about adding banking, a wider racing surface, and safety walls. It has since hosted the USAC Western World Championships and the Wild West Shootout, drawing dirt racers from across the country. Now the Grand National Championship takes its turn on the half-mile oval.

The Sidewinder Half-Mile will take place during Arizona Bike Week, which celebrates its 30th anniversary in 2027. The rally is centered in Scottsdale, and the series is banking on fans making the ride south to Casa Grande for a night of flat track.

"Central Arizona Raceway has built a reputation for bringing some of the best dirt racers in the country to Casa Grande, and we're proud to bring the Grand National Championship there for the first time," said Gene Crouch, CEO of AMA Pro Racing. "Racing during Arizona Bike Week gives us a chance to welcome back our Arizona fans and introduce the sport to motorcyclists who've never seen a flat track race in person."

Casa Grande is the third venue to be confirmed for the 2027 AFT schedule. Daytona Beach will host the season-opening double header March 10-11, and Belleville, Illinois will host its first ever event May 22.

Tickets for the Sidewinder Half-Mile go on sale Thursday, December 3.
