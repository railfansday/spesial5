Greg Newsome II agrees to three-year extension with Giants

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

New York Giants cornerback Greg Newsome II reached an agreement on a three-year, $46 million extension on Tuesday, Nov. 3, including a $32 million guarantee. He had previously agreed to a one-year deal in free agency.
