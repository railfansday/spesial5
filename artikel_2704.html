NFL playoff picture Week 10: Giants come back to NFC East pack with loss to Commanders

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Every week for the duration of the 2026 regular season, USA TODAY Sports will provide timely updates to the NFL's ever-evolving playoff picture − typically starting Sunday afternoon and then moving forward for the remainder of the week (through Monday's and Thursday's games or Saturday's, if applicable. And, when the holidays roll around, we'll be watching then, too).

What just happened? What does it mean? What are the pertinent factors (and, perhaps, tiebreakers) prominently in play as each conference's seven-team bracket begins to crystallize? All will be explained and analyzed up to the point when the postseason field is finalized on Sunday, Jan. 10.

Here's where things stand with Week 10 underway:

Week 3: Indianapolis Colts kicker Spencer Shrader (3) celebrates with long snapper Luke Rhodes (46) after Shrader kicked a 58-yard field goal against the Houston Texans at Lucas Oil Stadium. The Colts debuted their "Anvil Strike" uniform - featuring an unfamiliar gray uniform and glossy, metallic blue helmet with the familiar horseshoe logo in white. The Colts won, 19-17, for their first win of the season.

Week 3: Buffalo Bills quarterback Josh Allen (17) hands off to running back James Cook III against the Los Angeles Chargers at New Highmark Stadium. For the game, the Bills debuted their "Nickel City" alternate uniforms - the most striking element being the blue "Charge Helmet," adorned simply with an oversized cutout of the team's familiar logo. The Bills won the game, 24-16.

Week 2: Los Angeles Rams quarterback Matthew Stafford (9) hands the ball to running back Kyren Williams (23) during a 28-6 win on "Monday Night Football" against the New York Giants at SoFi Stadium. The Rams debuted their new "Classic Sol" uniforms, an homage to the team's look during its 1951 championship season.

Week 2: Arizona Cardinals quarterback Jacoby Brissett (7) hands off to running back Jeremiyah Love against the Seattle Seahawks at State Farm Stadium. The Cardinals wore their desert-themed "Rivalries" uniforms in the 31-7 loss to the Seahawks. Arizona's "Rivalries" look was one of the best to debut in 2025.

Week 2: The Baltimore Ravens' Trey Hendrickson (91) reacts after a sack against the New Orleans Saints at M&T Bank Stadium. The Ravens wore their "White Noise" uniforms, just one of the team's new uniform combinations that will debut during the 2026 season. The Ravens lost the game, 24-17.

Week 2: New York Jets wide receiver Garrett Wilson (5) celebrates after scoring a touchdown against the Green Bay Packers at MetLife Stadium. The Jets debuted their "White Out" uniforms for their home opener, which ended in a 20-17 overtime loss.

1. San Francisco 49ers (7-1), NFC West leaders: A surprisingly comfortable margin in what was the league's best division last year could be tested in the coming weeks. Remaining schedule: at Cowboys, vs. Vikings [at Mexico City], vs. Seahawks, at Giants, vs. Rams, at Chargers, at Chiefs, vs. Eagles, at Cardinals

2. Chicago Bears (7-2), NFC North leaders: Don't get too comfortable, fellas − the fourth-place Pack are only a game back. Remaining schedule: BYE, vs. Saints, at Lions, vs. Jaguars, at Dolphins, at Bills, vs. Packers, vs. Lions, at Vikings

3. New York Giants (5-3-1), NFC East leaders: While they remain in relative control of the division, they let a win slip away against the Washington Commanders on Thursday night − a loss that brought the G-Men closer to the second-place Dallas Cowboys (4-5). Remaining schedule: vs. Jaguars, at Colts, vs. 49ers, at Seahawks, vs. Browns, at Lions, at Cowboys, vs. Eagles

4. Carolina Panthers (3-5), NFC South leaders: Their 1-1 record in divisional games currently gives them the edge over New Orleans (0-1) − though that goes out the window when they play for the first time this season on Sunday. Remaining schedule: at Saints, vs. Ravens, at Buccaneers, at Vikings, vs. Saints, vs. Bengals, at Steelers, vs. Seahawks, at Falcons

5. Detroit Lions (6-2), wild card No. 1: Their Week 8 win over Minnesota currently confers the slight seeding advantage, one they'll try to maintain in Germany on Sunday. Remaining schedule: vs. Patriots [at Munich], vs. Buccaneers, vs. Bears, at Falcons, vs. Titans, at Vikings, vs. Giants, at Bears, at Packers

6. Minnesota Vikings (6-2), wild card No. 2: A two-game slide has dropped them off the pace, which is why QB Kirk Cousins has returned to pick it up − and with a key matchup out of the gate Sunday at Lambeau Field. Remaining schedule: at Packers, vs. 49ers [at Mexico City], vs. Falcons, vs. Panthers, at Patriots, vs. Lions, vs. Commanders, at Jets, vs. Bears

7. Green Bay Packers (6-3), wild card No. 3: Is this really going to be the fourth straight season they finish as the NFC's final wild-card entry? Remaining schedule: vs. Vikings, BYE, at Rams, at Saints, vs. Bills, vs. Dolphins, at Bears, vs. Texans, vs. Lions

1. Buffalo Bills (7-1), AFC East leaders: They've never had home-field advantage during the Josh Allen era. May as well get it in the first year they're in their glorious new stadium. Remaining schedule: at Jets, vs. Dolphins, vs. Chiefs, at Patriots, at Packers, vs. Bears, at Broncos, at Dolphins, vs. Jets

2. Denver Broncos (7-2), AFC West leaders: Statistically, they have the AFC's easiest remaining schedule, which could mean a second straight No. 1 seed. Remaining schedule: BYE, vs. Raiders, at Steelers, vs. Dolphins, at Jets, at Raiders, vs. Bills, at Patriots, vs. Chargers

3. Jacksonville Jaguars (6-2), AFC South leaders: A better record in AFC games currently has them ahead of Pittsburgh. A roster reloaded ahead of the trade deadline and a weak division could keep the Jags there. Remaining schedule: at Titans, at Giants, vs. Titans, at Bears, vs. Steelers, at Texans, at Cowboys, vs. Commanders, at Colts

4. Pittsburgh Steelers (6-2), AFC North leaders: First place is on the line Sunday night in the Queen City. Remaining schedule: at Bengals, at Eagles, vs. Broncos, vs. Texans, at Jaguars, vs. Ravens, vs. Panthers, at Titans, at Ravens

5. Kansas City Chiefs (6-2), wild card No. 1: Their 6-1 record in AFC games gives them a strong start in the tiebreaker department if they can't get back in front of Denver in the AFC West. Remaining schedule: at Falcons, vs. Cardinals, at Bills, at Rams, at Bengals, vs. Patriots, vs. 49ers, at Chargers, vs. Raiders

6. Cincinnati Bengals (6-2), wild card No. 2: They will move atop the AFC North on Sunday night if they can salvage a season split with Pittsburgh. Remaining schedule: vs. Steelers, at Commanders, vs. Saints, at Browns, vs. Chiefs, at Panthers, at Colts, vs. Ravens, vs. Browns

7. New England Patriots (5-4), wild card No. 3: A two-game edge on the Browns in AFC games currently gives the reigning conference champs the last projected postseason spot in this year's field. Remaining schedule: vs. Lions [at Munich], BYE, at Chargers, vs. Bills, vs. Vikings, at Chiefs, at Jets, vs. Broncos, vs. Dolphins
