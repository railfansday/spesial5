Field hockey: Wilson wins semifinal match, will play for first District 3 title

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Abby Zerbe and Bradley Smith scored goals as Wilson defeated Cumberland Valley 2-1 in the District 3 Class 3A field hockey semifinals on Wednesday night.

The Bulldogs, 18-2 and seeded second, will play for the district title at 3 p.m. Saturday at Memorial Park in Mechanicsburg against the winner of No. 1 Lower Dauphin and No. 5 Penn Manor, the defending district champion.

Sydney Adams and Sidni Templeton had assists for Wilson, which is trying to win its first district title.

Lower Dauphin has won 17 3A titles and Penn Manor has won 5.

Wilson goalie Natalie Rapp made 4 saves, as the Bulldogs had a 14-5 edge in shots.
