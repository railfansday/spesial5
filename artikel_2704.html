Broncos vs. Raiders: Live Fan Discussion

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Denver Broncos will be looking to move to 8-2 on the year with a home win over the Las Vegas Raiders today. It figures to be an emotional day in the stands as fans mourn the death of the legendary Tom Jackson today. We all know how Jackson loved to beat the Raiders as a player, so what a chance to send a win and a mile high salute his way this afternoon.

Kickoff is set for 2:25 p.m. Mile High time on Sunday, November 22, 2026 at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas, Nevada. You can watch on CBS affiliates. The game will be called by Andrew Catalon (play-by-play), Logan Ryan (analyst), and AJ Ross (sideline). You can also check local Broncos radio network affiliate stations.
