Eagles’ Saquon Barkley, Dallas Goedert both suffer injuries during Thanksgiving tilt with Cowboys

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

As the Philadelphia Eagles face the Dallas Cowboys on Thanksgiving Day on Thursday, the team has been dealt with key injuries to major offensive players in the first half. While Barkley was featured on the Eagles' injury report ahead of the Cowboys matchup, his injury situation in the Thanksgiving game is a head-scratcher, as tight end Dallas Goedert has also been impacted.

Starting with Goedert, he only recorded one catch for nine yards in the first half before leaving the game with a shoulder injury, as Dave Zangaro wrote that he was initially "questionable to return."

"Dallas Goedert (shoulder) is questionable to return," Zangaro wrote on X, formerly Twitter.

Just over 20 minutes later, Zangaro would report that Goedert had officially been ruled out of the contest by Philadelphia, marking him as one of the many injuries the team has dealt with this season.

"Dallas Goedert (shoulder) has been ruled out," Zangaro wrote in another post later.

The situation around Barkley is a little more complex than Goedert's.

When it comes to Barkley, who has dealt with many health issues this season impacting his play, he left the Thanksgiving contest, as he was being "evaluated" for an injury, according to Zach Berman of The Athletic.

"Saquon Barkley is now being evaluated for an injury," Berman wrote on X.

The Eagles themselves would label the injury as a "stinger," saying that he is questionable to return, with Berman noting that the Fox Sports broadcast is reporting that Barkley is "lobbying to go back" in the game. However, the team trainers "aren't giving him his helmet."

"The broadcast says Barkley is lobbying to go back in and the trainers aren't giving him his helmet," Berman wrote.

Berman would later say that Barkley is "animated on the sideline," though he is "not holding a helmet."

At any rate, it remains to be seen how severe of an injury it is for Goedert and what happens with Barkley regarding his availability for the Thanksgiving contest and beyond.
