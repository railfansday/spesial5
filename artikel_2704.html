Yaxel Lendeborg shares shocking details in viral bench moment with Draymond Green

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Warriors rookie sensation Yaxel Lendeborg was seen visibly upset during Golden State's blowout loss to the Pelicans on Monday night — forcing 15-year veteran Draymond Green to come comfort him.

Before the Warriors take the court on the road against the Mavericks on Wednesday night, Lendeborg spoke about the interaction between him and Green and revealed what advice he was given.

Draymond Green on the court during Monday's loss to the Pelicans. NBAE via Getty Images

"He basically told me to stop showing so much weakness," Lendeborg said. "If he's on the other team, he's gonna think that I'm weak and go at me. I want someone to go at me anyway, but the way I was showing it off, it's kinda like I'm soft. I've just gotta fix my mental."

Lendeborg admitted that while he's early in his NBA career, he's going through some adversity.

Yaxel Lendeborg seen visibly frustrated during Monday's loss to the Pelicans. Getty Images

The Warriors were riding the high of an eight-game winning streak until Steph Curry went down with an oblique injury on Nov. 11. Now, they're on a three-game losing streak and have put Lendeborg in unfamiliar territory.

"I hate losing, man. Last year, I lost a total of three games. The year before that, I can't even remember how much," Lendeborg said. "But I've never lost this much so early. So me putting my head down like that, I felt like I let the team down."

While Lendeborg has high standards, the Warriors are asking him to grow up quicker than expected in Curry's absence.

In the New Orleans loss, Lendeborg was Golden State's leading scorer, finishing with 28 points. He added eight rebounds and three assists.

The Warriors' Yaxel Lendeborg looks to get past a defender in Monday night's game against the Pelicans. AP Photo/Morgan Simmons

But even with his solid performance, the Warriors dug themselves into an early hole and got outscored 35-11 in the first quarter.

Golden State coach Steve Kerr said after the game the team had no "juice" in the first half and is contemplating changes to its starting five.
