Sauce Gardner, Charvarius Ward listed as out of Colts practice Tuesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Colts listed both of their starting cornerbacks as out of practice on Tuesday.

It was the second straight day that Sauce Gardner (calf) and Charvarius Ward (knee) received that designation, although it was just an estimation as the Colts only held a walkthrough on Tuesday. Both corners took part in last Sunday’s 24-22 win over the Dolphins.

Left tackle Bernhard Raimann (concussion) and right tackle Jalen Travis (knee) were also listed as out for the second day in a row. Travis did not play against the Dolphins while Raimann only played six snaps. The nature of the concussion protocol will work against Raimann being avaiable against the Texans on Thursday.

Offensive tackle Charlie Heck (personal), tight end Will Mallory (hamstring), and defensive tackle Jerry Tillery (calf) were also listed as non-participants. Wide receiver Anthony Gould (toe) and long snapper Luke Rhodes (hamstring were limited. Guard Dalton Tucker (shoulder) was a full participant.
