Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collections: Live restock updates at Amazon, Walmart, and Target (Nov. 13)

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

The Pokémon TCG's 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collections have finally arrived, but getting hold of either the Espeon (Day) or Umbreon (Night) edition at a reasonable price has been proving difficult, even with new listings regularly going live across major U.S. trading card retailers.

Released on Nov. 6, these enormous anniversary boxes have become two of the most sought-after Pokémon products of the year — just as much as the 30th Celebration ETB. Along with the many early Pokemon Black Friday deals, we've found several places to buy both versions, with Walmart currently offering the lowest prices among top retailers. However, there are some important differences between the available offers and the official restocks.

We've rounded up the retailers worth checking out, the latest prices, and everything you need to know to secure one of these special collections ahead of Black Friday.

These are the main U.S. retailers to bookmark for the Espeon and Umbreon collections, including their current stock situation:

Amazon: No stock or listings are available directly from the retailer or from third-party sellers, but we’ve added a go-to search results page so you can quickly check for any restocks (we expect more this week).

Best Buy (Day and Night): Official stock is hard to find, but third-party listings are still available for both versions. Set up deal alerts to catch any new allocations.

Target (Day): Currently sold out via official stock and third-party sellers.

TCGplayer (Day and Night): The dedicated trading card marketplace has listings for both the Espeon and Umbreon editions of 30th Celebration’s Ultra-Premium Collection at around or under market price.

Walmart (Day and Night): Both Day and Night editions are available through third-party sellers. Keep an eye out for cheaper listings.

Demand for the 30th Celebration expansion has been exceptionally strong since its September launch, and these Ultra-Premium Collections are arguably the hottest items in the entire anniversary lineup.

Unlike smaller Pokemon products such as Booster Bundles and Elite Trainer Boxes, each UPC contains a staggering 30 booster packs, including one exclusive Classic Collection pack with three etched cards celebrating the TCG's history. Add in two highly collectible promo Pokémon cards — Espeon and Umbreon — alongside special Pikachu ex promo cards and all the premium extras (playmat, card sleeves, deck box, etc.), and it’s no wonder that there hasn’t been enough supply to meet the demand.

There are two versions to choose from: the green-and-blue Day edition featuring Espeon ex, and the purple-themed Night collection starring Umbreon ex.

Both contain 29 standard 30th Celebration booster packs and one special Classic Collection pack, alongside a foil Pikachu ex promo card and a matching Espeon ex or Umbreon ex promo. You'll also get a themed playmat, 65 card sleeves, a deck box, a metal Pokémon coin, six damage-counter dice, and a code card for Pokémon TCG Live.

The accessories and promotional artwork vary between editions, but you’re effectively getting the same level of contents with either box.

With both versions in high demand, there are a few ways to make tracking down an Espeon or Umbreon Ultra-Premium Collection a little easier:

Keep checking the major retailers: Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Amazon, and GameStop are all worth revisiting throughout the week — as new stock has been constantly reappearing over the last couple of weeks. On the other hand, stock can sell out with little warning, so you’ll need to check regularly.

Set up stock alerts where available: Retailer notifications and independent stock trackers can help you spot fresh inventory without repeatedly refreshing every product page. Best Buy, for instance, even lets you set deal alerts for specific listings.

Compare prices across different sellers: Third-party sellers can be a useful way to secure either collection when official stock is unavailable. Just remember to compare their offers against current market prices (available on TCGplayer), as some listings are considerably more expensive than others.

Check whether you're getting Day or Night: Some retailers list the two versions separately, while GameStop uses combined listings with randomized selections. Make sure you're ordering the right product if you're specifically after Espeon or Umbreon.

Don't overlook in-store availability: Online stock shifts incredibly fast, but checking nearby Target, Best Buy, and GameStop locations could give you another chance to grab a box — as many don’t check physical stores, simply assuming they’ll be automatically sold out, particularly if additional inventory arrives ahead of Black Friday.

It's certainly possible that more Ultra-Premium Collections could come in stock for Black Friday, though no allocations have been confirmed for either collection.

With Black Friday arriving on Nov. 27, there's still a little over two weeks for retailers to receive more inventory and, as mentioned, there have been fleeting stock drops across the major Pokemon TCG retailers — even if most of them come via third-party sellers. Keep in mind that due to the demand for the 30th Celebration UPCs, sellers have little incentive to discount listings much below market price (around $360 per Ultra-Premium Collection).

For now, it's worth keeping the retailer pages above close at hand, so you can compare prices for any available stock and decide what’s best for your trading card budget.
