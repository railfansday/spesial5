Top Strategist Calls Nvidia a House of Cards

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Nvidia’s rebound may have bought the AI trade time, but delayed data-center construction, price-sensitive demand and mounting scrutiny are raising doubts about future profits says Matt Maley, Miller Tabak and Co. Chief Market Strategist and The Maley Report Founder. He joined Bloomberg Open Interest to talk about the market’s reliance on a few key players and why circular financing leaves the sector looking like a house of cards. (Source: Bloomberg)
