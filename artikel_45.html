Italy strike draws workers, students into nationwide protests over wages and living costs

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

A nationwide strike was expected to disrupt schools, hospitals and local transport across Italy on Friday as workers and students began marching in dozens of cities to demand higher wages, lower living costs and greater social protections. The labor action coincided with an International Day of Student Mobilisation, with organisers planning demonstrations in more than 45 Italian cities. FRANCE 24's Seema Gupta reports from Rome, Italy.
