How CeraVe’s ‘Dune’ deal takes the brand’s social-first playbook global

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The L’Oréal brand’s first film partnership makes it the “Official Moisturizer of Arrakis” ahead of the blockbuster’s Dec. 18 release.

CeraVe last week partnered with “Dune: Part Three” to become the “Official Moisturizer of Arrakis,” the desert planet that serves as the sci-fi epic’s titular setting. The tie-up with Warner Bros. Pictures and Legendary Pictures represents the L'Oréal brand’s first film partnership, with a campaign that will roll out ahead of the movie’s Dec. 18 theatrical release.

The brand on Monday revealed the next chapter in the campaign. A humorous “Sandworm Wrangler” video, shot in the style of behind-the-scenes content, shows how a wrangler of one of the film’s iconic sandworms uses CeraVe Moisturizing Cream — which now offers 120 hours of hydration — on the dry-skinned creature.

The debut “Precious Resource” spot reimagined the lore of Arrakis, an arid planet where every drop of moisture is preserved, replacing the story’s central resource, spice, with CeraVe. The “Dune” partnership is the latest iteration of a “medutainment” strategy that combines science with entertainment and has helped grow CeraVe into a $2 billion brand.

“When it comes to the playbook, that's something we are reproducing here with ‘Dune,’” said Vincent Chauvière, global brand president at CeraVe. “It's a multipoint campaign that is social-first, starting with a bit of excitement and surprise, and then we are going full media.”

Along with co-branded content, the campaign spans paid, earned, social, digital, retail, creator and experiential activations. The brand will also launch out-of-home ads, creator gifting and corporate screening experiences, as well as sponsor film premiere events in Los Angeles and the U.K.

“Our goal is to create partnerships that extend the worlds of our films in ways that feel unexpected, culturally relevant and genuinely entertaining,” Julie Moore, executive vice president, global brand partnerships at Warner Bros. Pictures, said in a statement.

The “Dune” campaign was created and produced by OBB Bolded, OBB’s branded content studio, with content filmed in Samalayuca Dunes in Mexico and Mexico City and directed by Maca Rubio from Stink Films. OBB was also behind an award-winning “Moisturize Like a Derm” effort that appointed NBA star Kevin Durant as the brand’s “Face of Legs.”

“It's been a campaign that has been working at bringing awareness to the brand, but also to the usage of a body moisturizer, bringing our science to new consumers, particularly men. We've been recruiting more men this year, thanks to this partnership [with the NBA],” Chauvière explained.

The “Dune” partnership is rolling out globally, and the campaign is designed to pair content around the billion-dollar franchise with market-specific dermatologists and experiential activations. CeraVe is growing at a double-digit rate this year and gaining share around the world, and geographic expansion is key to the brand’s future growth, Chauvière said.

“The partnership with ‘Dune’ [is] a way to have a more consistent brand tone of voice and point of view. We have local teams that have a lot of local activations, and the influencer world is a local world. So it's all all about the balance,” the executive said.

The local push builds on CeraVe’s larger creator strategy, which helps the brand reach more than 15 million followers across platforms. The brand this summer united over 100 creators from 22 countries for its first Global CerAwards in Los Angeles. The event included CeraVe Studios, an immersive content creation experience hosted on a Hollywood studio lot, that generated nearly 60 million views of new content, per Chauvière.

“It was a moment of visibility, but it's also a moment to gather all the local influencers together and reunite around this idea of bringing creativity in the beauty world and making science accessible and entertaining at the same time,” the executive said.
