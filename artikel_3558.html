Final Fantasy 14 Is Letting Players Travel Between North America And European Servers For The First Time In 16 Years And Fans Are Psyched

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Whenever there’s a new Final Fantasy XIVÂ expansion, especially one as hotly anticipated as Evercold is, queues to log into the game infamously become a slog. To combat this inevitable server congestion, Square Enix has announced that it will permit cross-region travel between North American and European data centers.

Starting after the scheduled maintenance on December 1st, players will be able to hop between North American and European data centers at-will, or pop over to the Oceanian data centers if they so choose. Here’s the breakdown of the new server traffic flows:

Cross-region Travel Available Beginning Tuesday, December 1, 2026

To help alleviate server congestion following the release of Patch 8.0, we will be enabling cross-region Data Center Travel between the North American and European data centers during scheduled maintenance on Tuesday, December 1, 2026.

— FINAL FANTASY XIV (@en.finalfantasyxiv.com) 2026-11-24T12:00:45.707Z

Square Enix’s reasoning is quite simple; peak player times vary across regions so when the servers are slammed in North America, players can choose a European data center to potentially side-step thousand-player-deep log-in queues and vice versa. And not only is this a great way to get ahead of the impending server traffic jam, it’s a neat social feature that eases some of the siloing that happens between servers.

Currently, if you have a cross-continental friendship you’d have to make an alternate character on the appropriate data center in order to play together. Cross-region travel will let you port your main character over letting you quest together and, critically, run endgame content like raids without the need to start from scratch on a new server. That’s huge and players are suitably hyped for it, hoping that the option to data-center hop remains available permanently.

Square Enix warns that visiting other data-centers might increase latency but encourages players to test it out before Evercold launches in February 2027 to get a feel for it. Also, sorry to Oceianin data center players, you still don’t get to go anywhere for now.
