Mormon Wives: OC's Madison Addresses Claim About Staying in a Toxic Marriage

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Madison Bontempo is clarifying a comment she made on The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County about staying with her husband, Kyler Fisher, even if he cheated or was physically abusive.

“Hey guys, I just wanted to address something that I said on the show because I realized how wrong it came across,” Madison said in a Sunday, November 22, Instagram video. “I made a comment on one of the parts of the show that I said that I would stay with my husband even if he cheated on me, even if he hit me. First of all, I just want to make it very clear that neither of those things has happened in my marriage. I was speaking hypothetically because I just believe so strongly in marriage and forgiveness and working through hard times.”

While reflecting on her remark, Madison shared that she “realized how harmful that statement was” and is taking “full responsibility for it.”

“I feel awful,” she said. “Because I believe so strongly in forgiveness, sometimes I forget that forgiveness doesn’t mean that you have to stay. You can forgive someone and you can still choose what’s best for you. There’s a big difference between fighting for your marriage and sacrificing yourself in the process. So I just wanted to clarify what I meant and make it very clear that I do not believe anyone should stay in a situation where they are being hurt.”

Madison added that she and Kyler, who share five children, are “working through” their own relationship issues in their marriage.

“We have some disagreements but we can do better. But I feel like for us, we know who we are and we know what our marriage is and we’re fighting through it,” she said. “And I just want to say happy Thanksgiving to everyone. I hope that this week we can find things to be grateful for. I think that our minds are so powerful, and if we find things to be grateful for and focus on the good and the things that we love, those things will blossom and grow. I love you guys and happy Thanksgiving week.”

Madison and Kyler’s marriage woes was featured on season 1 of Hulu’s spinoff show, which premiered earlier this month. Several of Madison’s cast members expressed their concerns over the pair’s relationship.

“I feel like it’s hard when you’re watching it back sometimes because you don’t see the full picture,” Madison exclusively told Us. “Kyler and I are aware that we argue in our marriage. We’ve been together for 16 years, so we’re gonna have those times where we argue together. We really did want to showcase that. I think the girls really did seem concerned, and I really appreciated everybody coming together and trying to rally around me.”

Since the season premiered, Madison released a statement standing by her relationship while Kyler took to social media to address concerns about his behavior toward his wife.

“Madison has her own voice,” he wrote via Instagram, in part. “I respect her right to share how she feels, but she doesn’t owe anyone a defense of me like she put out publicly on her stories. I hear the concern for her, and I’m taking it seriously. My focus is showing up for her with patience, kindness, and respect every day. Madison I’m committed to you and our family forever.”

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County is streaming on Hulu now.
