Van Dijk admits uncertainty over Liverpool future

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Virgil van Dijk admitted he is unsure whether he will extend his Liverpool contract beyond the end of this season.

The Reds captain's current deal expires in June 2027, with an Anfield exit potentially on the cards for the 35-year-old.

Van Dijk joined Liverpool from Southampton in January 2018 and has gone on to make 389 appearances for the club.

The Netherlands international has won two Premier League titles, one Champions League, one Super Cup, one Club World Cup, two EFL Cups and one FA Cup during his time on Merseyside.

Liverpool continue the 2026-27 season with a clash against rivals Manchester United on Sunday.

Should he start this match, Van Dijk will be starting a 50th consecutive Premier League game for Liverpool, the fourth time he has had a run of 50+ unbroken starts in the competition – the joint-most by an outfield player along with Sylvain Distin.

Aged 35 years and 137 days, he would also become the oldest outfield player to start 50+ consecutive Premier League games for a single club, overtaking Kevin Davies for Bolton (67 in a row aged 34y 175d).

While Van Dijk feels he is capable of playing for "many more years", it is yet to be decided how long he will remain at Liverpool.

"The main thing for me is that I feel good and I'm looking after myself and I still have many more years in my opinion," Van Dijk told Sky Sports.

"Whatever's going to happen after this season, we'll see.

"At this moment of time, if anyone says that or put out that they know what I'm going to do next season, it's untrue because at this moment of time, speaking to you, I've got no idea either.

"But I'm relaxed with it because if you know me, I only focus on what I can control and that's at the moment the Manchester United game. Then we'll focus to the Champions League and whatever happens in the new year coming, we'll see then.

"But it's been an incredible journey, almost 10 years this summer and it's been a big part of my life and I'll forever be grateful for anything that happened over those years.

"If it's getting extended, we'll see then. If not, then I'm going to say goodbyes in the summer."
