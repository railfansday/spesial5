MN: ‘Baila Moreira’ – €51m fee starting to look like good value after dazzling derby debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The man for the big moment was Diego Moreira, who asserted himself on the Milan derby like a star who had played in it 100 times before.

As MilanNews write, it was a dazzling derby debut from Moreira after his €51m summer move from Strasbourg, a fee that raised some eyebrows. He got two assists and a goal under Kakà’s gaze, earning a standing ovation from San Siro.

Moreira – along with Adrien Rabiot and Mario Gila – returned to the pitch after their absence in the last couple of games. They did so with the desire to take the world by storm and they truly made flames in the sky, as the Zucchero song goes, a famous Milan supporter.

Diego Moreira’s performance deserves special mention. He started off with a bang: a perfect assist for Pulisic after making Carlos Augusto dizzy after four minutes. After 10 another run put the Nerazzurri full-back under pressure, winning a corner from which he set up Rabiot for the second.

Then, there was the free-kick which ultimately sealed the three points. It was a set-piece taken with wonderful technique, evading the wall perfectly and nestling inside the post, thanks to that cultured left boot of us.

As mentioned, the Belgian had the extra motivation of playing under the watchful eye of Ricardo Kaká, the AC Milan legend whose weighty No.22 he wears on his back. He didn’t look out of place. There’s no point in making absurd comparisons: they are two different players and two different eras.

The important thing is that Moreira was decisive and entertained one of his idols, Kaká, who was moved to tears before the match when he was inducted into the club’s Hall of Fame.

Yesterday’s match is a starting point. Milan must continue to ‘baila [dance]’, with the confidence and the genius of Moreira to guide them.
