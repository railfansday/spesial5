All 160 Serie A players on international duty in November

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

A total of 160 Serie A players will join up with their national team sides during the November international break: Here is a reminder of who they all are.

The November international break officially kicks off today (Monday, November 9) and will run until next Sunday (November 22). Italy will be in Nations League action against France in San Siro on Thursday and away against Belgium in Brussels on Sunday.

Of Mancini’s current Italy national team squad, 24 of them currently play their football in Serie A. Gianluigi Donnarumma, Riccardo Calafiori, Honest Ahanor, Michael Kayode, Marco Palestra, Issa Doumbia, Sandro Tonali and Luca Koleosho are the only members of the Azzurri squad not currently playing in Italy.

Inter have provided the most Italy internationals this break with Alessandro Bastoni, Federico Dimarco, Nicolo Barella and Pio Esposito. Atalanta and Como have provided three Italy internationals apiece.

Inter also have the most senior international players in November with 14, followed by Atalanta, Como and Roma with 12 apiece, Udinese with 11, and then Juventus and Torino with 10 apiece.

All 20 Serie A sides have some form of international representation this break: Monza have the fewest senior internationals with five.
