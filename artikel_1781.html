Arsenal injury update: David Raya, Bukayo Saka, Martin Zubimendi latest return dates

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal have a nervous wait to discover if David Raya is set for a spell on the sidelines.

With talisman Bukayo Saka potentially out for the remainder of the year, Mikel Arteta’s worst-case scenario will be to also lose his starting goalkeeper.

In better news, William Saliba is expected to feature against Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday, ahead of the visit of Manchester City at the weekend.

With that in mind, here is the latest Arsenal injury news and return dates…

David Raya has undergone scans on a potential shoulder injury suffered during Arsenal's 2-2 draw with Newcastle on Saturday night.

The goalkeeper collided with William Osula while attempting to distribute the ball in the second half and appeared to be in considerable pain as medical staff treated his shoulder. He was replaced by Kepa Arrizabalaga as the Gunners fought back to claim a point at St James’ Park.

Asked about the goalkeeper after the game, Mikel Arteta told reporters: “Unfortunately, he had to come off, which is not good news. He will have an MRI assessment [on Sunday], but at the moment I don't know.”

He added: “It was a knock, I don’t know how it happened and what the injury is at the moment. We have to wait and see. We know how important David is for us. We will have more news [on Sunday].”

Martin Zubimendi has endured a frustrating season and has suffered another injury setback after being forced off during Spain's match against England during the international break.

The midfielder has dropped from being Declan Rice's preferred partner to further down the pecking order at Arsenal, with his opportunities limited even before the latest setback. Zubimendi has also been playing with a protective mask after breaking his nose against Everton nearly a month ago.

Spain manager Luis de la Fuente confirmed the Arsenal midfielder had suffered a muscle injury against England, and Mikel Arteta expects him to be sidelined for “a few weeks.”

”Unfortunately he has a slight problem in his hamstring,” Arteta told reporters on Friday.

"He wasn't too concerned to be fair after the game, but after the MRI scans, it showed that there is an injury there and he's going to be out for a few weeks."

Bukayo Saka has now missed six consecutive matches for Arsenal after sustaining a quad injury in the opening 10 minutes of their Champions League game against Bayern Munich last month.

The initial prognosis was an eight-week absence, which Mikel Arteta believes remains the case after further scans.

The Arsenal manager told reporters last week: "We had another test, he's evolving really well, but it looks like it's going to be an eight-week injury more or less.

“I think he's at four [weeks] at the moment, so it's still a while for him to be back with the team."
