Juventus considering January loan for Hojbjerg as McKennie rumours continue

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Reports in Italy claim that Juventus could make a move for Olympique de Marseille midfielder Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg during the January transfer window, whilst Weston McKennie continues to be linked with a move to Manchester United despite his recent claims that he has no interest in leaving the Old Lady.

Reports over the last few weeks have indicated that Juventus are interested in bringing in a new central midfielder in January, as well as further options at full-back and centre-forward.

The Bianconeri have been light on options in the middle of the park in 2026-27 following long-term injuries to both Khephren Thuram and Manuel Locatelli, which has left Spalletti reliant on Teun Koopmeiners and Douglas Luiz, two players the club had looked to sell at various stages over the last 18 months.

Hojbjerg has been linked with a move to Juventus, and Thursday’s updates from La Gazzetta dello Sport suggest that Juventus are interested in pursuing a potential loan deal for the Denmark international in January.

Convincing Marseille, who need to raise funds, may not be a straightforward task, though. If a loan move is to be viable in January, Juventus will surely need to include some form of buy option, which La Gazzetta dello Sport suggests would be around €15m for the 31-year-old.

Meanwhile, La Gazzetta dello Sport maintains that versatile Juventus midfielder McKennie could still move to Manchester United at some stage in the near future, despite his recent claims that he is ‘not planning to’ leave Turin.

There have been rumours that newly-appointed Manchester United head coach could look to bring McKennie with him after their experiences together with the USMNT, but the player himself has recently clarified: “I am happy where I am. That’s why I signed a new deal at Juve and I can’t wait to continue here.”

La Gazzetta dello Sport admits that McKennie is an invaluable player to Juventus head coach Luciano Spalletti, but that an offer in the region of €40m would be difficult for the Bianconeri to turn down.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
