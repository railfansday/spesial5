Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The most trusted resource for mindfulness and meditation.

A guided practice that expands your perspective so that you can see: your thoughts and worries can still exist, but there's just a little more space around them.

Our guest this week is Shamash Alidina. He offers a guided practice to invite us to consider how feeling smaller can actually be a big relief. It’s not that we diminish our value, but rather that we take a moment to feel that the world is bigger than our worries, and that we are held and surrounded by immense beauty, wonder, and support. Shamash Alidina has been practising mindfulness since 1998 and runs his own successful training organisation.
