All resource materials in Loftia and how to get them

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Loftia‘s crafting system requires you to find plenty of resource materials that are located all over the map. Each of the four districts available in the current build of the game offer you something useful.

In this guide, I will go through every resource material and their best sources in the game.

No crafting resource has one specific source. Instead, you’ll need to look in regions/areas where you can find them in abundance. Certain materials are also rarer to find, so you’ll require longer time to gather them.
