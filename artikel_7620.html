Discord: Director of User Experience (UX) Research

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Discord has a highly engaged community of millions of daily active users who use the platform for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does: play video games. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming, and we are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to hang out before, during, and after playing games.

Discord is hiring a Director of User Experience Research to lead our research team, drive a user-centered understanding of Players (the people who use Discord), and turn that understanding into a better product. You'll own the research strategy for Discord, build and lead a team of researchers, and make sure a deep, evidence-based understanding of user needs shapes what we build before decisions are made.

You'll uncover and forecast the problems our users face and the opportunities that will add the most value to Discord. You'll deepen our understanding of every user cohort, from longtime gamers to new communities, creators, moderators, and teens. And you'll make sure what we ship results in user value for them. As Discord brings more AI-powered experiences to users, you'll ensure they're grounded in real needs and held to a clear, user-centered quality bar. You'll also modernize how research gets done, using AI to increase our speed and reach without losing rigor.

You'll partner closely with Product, Design, Engineering, Data Science, and Safety. This person will report to the VP of Product Design and Insights.
