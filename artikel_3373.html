‘VisionQuest’ Sticks The Landing With Most-Watched Marvel Finale In 3 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

VisionQuest brought the WandaVision trilogy to a close earlier this week with Marvel‘s most-watched finale in three years.

The episode, which debuted Tuesday, gathered 3.7 million views after one day of streaming globally on Disney+, marking a series high and a 44% viewership increase over the previous episode. It’s the best audience for a Marvel finale since Loki Season 2.

So far, VisionQuest has been holding its own on the Nielsen streaming charts as well, climbing to No. 6 among streaming originals for the week of October 26 to November 1.

The buzz around the VisionQuest finale, which teased the return of Wanda Maximoff in its end credit sequest, sets the stage nicely for next month’s Avengers: Doomsday premiere. Per Disney, following the episode, Marvel also saw a spike in ticket sales for the upcoming film. Watch on Deadline

In VisionQuest, Paul Bettany stars as Vision, a former Avenger who was introduced in 2015’s Age of Ultron killed in the 2018 Infinity War film but was resurrected without emotions or memories.

The series also features James Spader as Ultron, Todd Stashwick as the morally ambiguous mercenary Paladin and Faran Tahir reprising his role of Raz from the MCU kickstarter pic Iron Man. The cast also includes Lauren Morais, Diane Morgan, Orla Brady, Diane Morgan, Polly Frame, Henry Lewis, Jonathan Sayer, with Emily Hampshire and James D’Arcy.
