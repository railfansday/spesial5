Carolina Panthers 2026 fan mailbag: The Panthers won and that’s what matters

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Hello CSR! Welcome to Ask Brian, part one of your weekly Panthers fan mailbag for everyone! We watched what was an atrocity from an officiating standpoint on Sunday, but the Panthers did manage to nab a win over the divisional rival New Orleans Saints, and maintain their status at the top of the NFC South. I also maintain that I'd be happy to lose every game if it means the Panthers defeat the Saints twice and cause them immense pain. I may have problems, but I stick to it. This week, the Panthers will get a Baltimore Ravens team missing its top two quarterbacks, in Charlotte, in a very winnable matchup. Things are starting to look up again, at least for another week, in what has become another confusing season as a fan.

So, you all know the drill. Comment down below with all your questions, whether they be Panthers related, football related, or even completely off topic! I'll have answers to your questions and more later on in the week. KEEP POUNDING!
