Dolphin Entertainment: 3 Key Takeaways for Long-Term Investors

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Four new commercial ventures planned for 2027 could widen the margin story, but investors must first see whether they launch on time.

By The Motley Fool Team – Nov 26, 2026 at 6:07PM EST

On its third-quarter 2026 earnings call, held on Nov. 10, 2026, Dolphin Entertainment (DLPN -0.18%) announced quarterly revenue of $16 million, an 8.3% increase year over year. The entertainment marketing company expanded operating profitability as agency cross-selling, which is selling services across multiple internal divisions, helped top-line growth outpace core operating costs. Three things management said stand out.

Revenue grew without relying on acquisitions, outstripping core operating costs, which are baseline expenses needed to run daily operations. Cross-selling, or offering services across multiple internal marketing divisions, drove profitability higher across agency units.

Management expects upcoming structural lease expirations and debt maturities to unlock $3 million in annual free cash flow, which represents cash generated after operational expenses. Furthermore, $127 million in NOLs, or net operating losses that can offset future taxable income, should shield most of those savings from corporate income tax.

Beyond core public relations agencies, management is expanding into commercial ventures, which are equity-backed brand partnerships and content projects. The company expects to launch four new commercial ventures in 2027, mostly through its fundraising partnership with DealMaker.

Operating momentum is strengthening heading into the final quarter of 2026 as multi-agency collaborations drive cross-selling between public relations and influencer marketing operations. Management points to multi-agency coordination as a potential driver of earnings growth.

Long-term value creation could depend on executing upcoming venture launches and on disciplined expense management that converts recurring revenue into free cash flow.
