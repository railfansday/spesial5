Graves leaning on Raptors motto ‘Stronger Together’ amid losing streak

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Toronto Raptors are in a difficult spot.

While it may (or may not) be anything, there's an uneasy dread that surrounds Kawhi Leonard's injury timeline. Many fans anticipated going into the season understanding that Leonard's health would always loom over the team, but no one could have expected the string of injuries that have plagued the rest of the roster.

With RJ Barrett out for at least two weeks (hamstring), Scottie Barnes managing a groin injury (but expected to return), and Collin Murray-Boyles in concussion protocol, it's been a challenging start to the 2026-27 season.

And it doesn't get easier when the Raptors play the Detroit Pistons on Friday.

Here are two players to watch ahead of the 7:30 p.m. EST scheduled tip-off on Prime Video.

After playing a career-high 31 minutes against the Washington Wizards, Allen Graves took the podium to discuss the team's sixth consecutive loss. The analytics show why the 20-year-old is an intriguing player, but hearing him speak, it's always easy to see why the front office was enamoured with Graves the person.

Graves referred to one of the team's mottos, which can be found inside the OVO Athletic Centre: Stronger Together

"We're really taking emphasis on that and pulling together throughout this time because we've got each other," said Graves. "Trying to pull together so we can get out of this."

While the fix doesn't begin with Graves, the rookie plays an integral role in finding a solution to the team's woes, particularly with Murray-Boyles navigating a head injury.

After shooting 41.3 per cent on 2.6 attempts at Santa Clara, the shooting is finally starting to come around for Graves. He's still only averaging 28.1 per cent on 2.1 attempts through 15 games, but Graves is also confidently shooting 37.5 per cent on 2.6 attempts in the last six contests.

Highly regarded for his quick hands and activity on defence, Graves is taking the journey that every rookie goes on as they navigate the pro game's speed and officiating. While Graves had one steal and three blocks in the home opener against the Chicago Bulls on Oct. 21, he hasn't finished with at least 2+ stocks since. The only other time Graves has finished with at least one block and a steal was against the Minnesota Timberwolves on Oct. 23.

"Getting more comfortable playing on the ball and off the ball," said Graves. "Learning how to guard pin downs and guard screens."

Graves is a naturally gifted rebounder and it's only a matter of time before his elite timing transfers to his closeouts and decision-making on defence. It certainly accelerates the learning process when playing the New York Knicks in a doubleheader followed by the Pistons.

"You can tell they were a championship team," said Graves. "When they make mistakes, getting right back to it, next-play mentality, I feel like that's something we're growing in."

A breakout is happening in Detroit and it's being authored by one of the most intriguing players in the NBA – Ausar Thompson.

The do-it-all wing is averaging 10.4 points, 8.5 rebounds, 5.5 assists, 1.7 steals and 0.8 blocks in his fourth year in the league. Thompson's points, rebounds, and assists are all career-high averages. The fun part is that he's doing it while playing just three more minutes a game.

For those who play Yahoo Fantasy Basketball, Thompson likely made a tremendous impact in both category and points leagues over the last seven days.

vs. New Orleans: 10.0 points, 7.0 rebounds, 4.0 assists, 2.0 steals

vs. San Antonio: 14.0 points, 9.0 rebounds, 12.0 assists, 5.0 steals

vs. Miami: 10.0 points, 8.0 rebounds, 5.0 assists, 2.0 steals, 2.0 blocks

vs. Washington: 17.0 points, 11.0 rebounds, 11.0 assists, 3.0 steals, 3.0 blocks

Teams continue to famously scheme to avoid Thompson like he's a blanket cornerback, but now his numbers and usage are starting to reflect his sky-high potential.
