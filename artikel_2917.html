Real Madrid fight back to beat Celta Vigo; Lazio stay top in Italy

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Real Madrid came from behind to beat Celta Vigo 2-1 at the Bernabeu and move level with Barcelona at the top of LaLiga.

Pablo Duran had given the visitors a shock early lead after six minutes, with Kylian Mbappe sending a penalty over the crossbar ahead of half-time.

England midfielder Jude Bellingham eventually got Los Blancos back on level terms in the 66th minute when he fired a low drive into the bottom corner.

Substitute Carlos Espi completed the turnaround with five minutes left as Jose Mourinho's men closed out a ninth successive victory in all competitions.

Real Betis missed the chance to go third after being held to a 1-1 draw at Sevilla, who snatched a point through a stoppage-time goal from Kike Salas.

Valencia moved out of the bottom three after a 1-0 win at out-of-form Rayo Vallecano.

In Serie A, Lazio consolidated their place at the top with a 3-0 win over Lecce at Stadio Olimpico, where Tijjani Noslin scored twice.

Donyell Malen scored a hat-trick as Roma coasted to a 5-1 win at Parma to stay within two points of the leaders.

Como are fourth after beating Cagliari 2-0 at Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia.

Scott McTominay's second-half strike gave Napoli a hard-earned 1-0 win over Torino to ease the pressure on manager Massimiliano Allegri.

Ligue 1 leaders Paris St Germain came from behind to win 3-2 at Nice, where Desire Doue suffered a serious-looking knee injury.

Lille lost ground after they were beaten 1-0 at home by Lyon, who are now just a point behind in fourth.

New Strasbourg boss Vitor Matos, who left Swansea earlier this week, saw his first game end in a 1-0 home defeat by Brest.
