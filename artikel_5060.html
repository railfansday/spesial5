HyroTrader Adds Hyro Core From $9 and No-Evaluation Hyro Instant to Rebuilt Crypto Prop Platform

The new accounts follow an October overhaul that brought the firm’s own trading terminal, AI-agent trading, BloFin and static loss limits on every challenge

DUBAI, UAE, November 2026: HyroTrader, a crypto proprietary trading firm, has launched two new ways to get a funded trading account. Hyro Core is a one-phase challenge from $9, and Hyro Instant skips the challenge altogether. Both went on sale on 4 November 2026.

They are the second step of a rebuild that began in October. That month HyroTrader launched its own trading terminal with stock and index contracts and opened accounts on it to AI trading agents. It also added BloFin as a second exchange, made every loss limit static and made its payouts easier to verify on a public dashboard.

Firms like HyroTrader give traders a funded account and pay them a share of the profit made on it. Traders usually earn that account by passing a paid trading test, known as a challenge.

At HyroTrader, every account, funded or not, is a simulated (demo) account running on live market data. The payouts are real money, paid as a share of the simulated profit.

Hyro Instant is the bigger change for a firm that until now funded only traders who passed a challenge.

“Without an evaluation, a firm cannot establish whether a trader has a consistent track record or simply had a few successful trades,” HyroTrader said in announcing Hyro Instant. “Nevertheless, the demand for a faster route is clear.”

Hyro Core is the firm’s lowest-priced challenge. A $2,500 account costs $9, and the largest, $100,000, costs $349. Against One-Step, the firm’s other one-phase challenge, it pairs the lower price with tighter loss limits.

To pass, a trader needs a 10% profit over at least four trading days, with no single day counting for more than 40% of it. The challenge ends if the account loses more than 3% of its starting balance in a day, or 5% in total. Both limits are static, which means they stay fixed to the starting balance and do not rise as the account gains.

Funded Hyro Core traders receive a fixed 80% of the profit as payouts. The fee is not refunded once the challenge is activated.

“A trader’s budget does not necessarily reflect their motivation or skill,” HyroTrader said in announcing Hyro Core.

Hyro Instant, a funded account with no evaluation. Image: HyroTrader

Hyro Instant has no test at all. After identity verification and account setup, the trader receives a funded account with no profit target to reach. It costs from $169 for a $5,000 account to $2,799 for $100,000.

It keeps Hyro Core’s daily and total loss limits and adds a per-trade limit. No single trade may lose more than 2% of the starting balance. A first breach of that trade limit brings a warning, and a second closes the account.

The first payout opens after three qualifying trading days (days on which the trader closes at least one trade above set size and price-move thresholds), with the account in profit. The profit split starts at 80% and can rise to 90% at the firm’s discretion. The fee is refunded only if identity verification is not approved.

Both new accounts run on what HyroTrader built in October. At the centre is the Hyro platform, the firm’s own trading terminal, which needs no exchange account. It offers more than 700 perpetual futures markets (contracts with no expiry date) on live prices. Besides crypto, they include contracts on stocks such as Apple, Nvidia and Tesla, on the S&P 500, and on gold and silver.

Traders who prefer an exchange can instead run any programme on their own demo account at Bybit or, since October, at BloFin. HyroTrader says that in 2023 it became the first crypto prop firm to let traders run a challenge on their own exchange demo account.

On the Hyro platform, Hyro Instant accounts included, traders can also connect their own AI agent through MCP, the Model Context Protocol (an open standard for linking AI tools to other software). The agent places trades for the trader’s own strategy under the account’s usual rules, and the trader can revoke its access at any time.

The October rebuild also made payouts easier to check. HyroTrader lists the payouts it sends on the Solana blockchain on a public transparency page, each with a link to its blockchain record unless the trader has chosen to hide it. In November the minimum payout fell from $100 to $25.

The firm also eased its existing challenges in the same week. One-Step challenges bought from 5 November need at least three trading days instead of five and allow an 8% total loss limit instead of 6%. Two-Step challenges bought from 4 November need four trading days in each phase instead of five.

HyroTrader is a crypto prop firm launched in 2023 and operating from Dubai, UAE. Traders take a challenge or start funded with Hyro Instant, receive 80% of their profits as payouts, up to 90% on some programmes, and are usually paid in stablecoins within 24 hours.

According to HyroTrader’s public payout transparency page, the firm has paid more than $9.5 million in profit splits to traders since 2023, as of November 2026. More than $1.6 million of that went out on the Solana blockchain. HyroTrader says it has funded more than 1,700 traders.

HyroTrader is not a broker and does not provide investment services or advice. All trading takes place on simulated accounts.
