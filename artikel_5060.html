Top 8 Decentralised Exchanges to look out for in November 2026

Decentralised exchanges, or DEXs, use smart contracts or liquidity pools to facilitate cryptocurrency trading. Users are at the core of DEXs, as they retain complete control over their assets and do not need to depend on centralized exchanges to protect their funds or process their withdrawals. DEXs let users maintain ownership of their private keys and also reduce the risk of custody failures.

Here is our list of the top 8 decentralized exchanges in the space that users can trade on:

Pancake or PancakeSwap is one of the most popular DEXs on the BNB Chain and was created to provide users with a low-cost, accessible alternative to Ethereum-based decentralized exchanges.

The platform recently rebranded from PancakeSwap to Pancake, dropping “Swap” as it expanded from a single-chain token-swap DEX to a multi-chain liquidity hub spanning 11+ blockchains, and added perpetual futures and 24/7 tokenized stocks and ETFs.

Uniswap introduced the Automated Market Maker (AMM) model that changed how cryptocurrency trading could take place without any central intermediaries or traditional order books. Users could swap tokens directly from their wallets across Ethereum and other blockchains.

The platform also allows users to earn rewards through peer-to-peer trading, and the open-source architecture has influenced others in the space, making it an important reference point for the DEX industry.

Raydium leverages Solana’s high-speed blockchain infrastructure, combining AMM, liquidity infrastructure, and token trading functionality. This is useful not just for DeFi users but also for retail users.

Users can trade and exchange their assets directly through their wallets. The tools on Raydium help new projects establish liquidity and attract traders. RAY is the native token of the platform and is useful with governance and incentives on the platform.

Hyperliquid has an order book that runs on-chain instead of an AMM, giving users a centralized experience. The platform is especially known for its perpetual futures and spot trading services, whereas the ecosystem itself supports borrowing, lending, and real-world assets.

The platform is a Layer 1 chain and consists of 2 main components: HyperCore and HyperEVM. HyperBFT is the consensus mechanism used underneath to support both. Stop, limit, and margin orders are all supported on Hyperliquid. HYPE is the native token of the platform.

Balancer is a DEX protocol built on Ethereum that supports multi-token liquidity pools and yield-bearing assets. Token pairings can be customized with multiple assets, unlike the traditional 50/50 pairings. It functions more like an automated portfolio manager, customizing token weights, creating pools with adjustable weight ratios, and letting users set their own trading fee.

The platform provides a level of flexibility that lets users and liquidity providers create specialized trading environments and program them as per their needs.

SushiSwap was launched as a fork of Uniswap in 2020 and lets users trade through liquidity pools instead of traditional order books. These users can either be Traders or Swappers or users who want to exchange one cryptocurrency for another; or Liquidity Providers who supply assets to liquidity pools to conduct trades.

The platform uses a multichain approach and supports various blockchain networks, letting users access different DeFi environments. The native token of the platform is SUSHI, which is useful in governance and incentive-based applications.

Orca lets users swap and conduct transactions at lightning fast speeds. Whirlpools are the platform’s concentrated liquidity pools that let liquidity providers provide capital within selected price ranges instead of distributing it uniformly across a price curve.

Orca is the native token of the platform. The platform’s simple interface appeals to a lot of traders. Wallet-to-wallet trading of SPL tokens is also a viable option for users.

Meteora is a liquidity protocol built on Solana focusing on providing advanced liquidity management and supporting token swaps. Dynamic Liquidity Market Maker or DLMM is a major feature of the platform allowing liquidity providers to allocate assets across different price bins.

Liquidity providers get to concentrate their assets around selected trading prices and various liquidity pool configurations can accomodate various market conditions and trading strategies. Meteora also supports emerging token projects and liquidity tools that can be helpful to establish trading markets and manage liquidity.

Decentralised exchanges are an essential part of the crypto and DeFi ecosystem. DEX platforms give users an extra level of control over their assets when they buy, sell, exchange or manage their assets.

While every trader has their own requirements, the best platform would depend upon individual needs, preferred networks, trading pairs and risks. Users should do their own research before investing.
