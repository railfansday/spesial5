MSTR Head of Investor Relations Addresses Bitcoin Pullback, Equity Performance

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Chaitanya "CJ" Jain, Head of Investor Relations at Strategy, joins Bitcoin Magazine TV as Bitcoin trades below Strategy's average cost.

Chaitanya “CJ” Jain, Head of Investor Relations at Strategy, joins Bitcoin Magazine TV as Bitcoin trades below Strategy’s average cost. He says the Anthropic IPO has been the clear negative catalyst for Bitcoin over the last couple of weeks, with risk-on investors rotating into the AI trade. CJ expects that rotation to reverse, and says the bigger catalyst will be monetary debasement. When central banks are forced to print, he says, “everyone will chase Bitcoin again.”
