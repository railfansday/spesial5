Creators Choice Awards: Our favorite creators name their favorite tools

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The creator economy has exploded in recent years. TV shows feature creators doing cameos as themselves, your favorite author is probably on BookTok, and Starbucks is paying baristas to make creator-style videos.

In a survey from The Conversation, more than 60 percent of middle and high school students said they wanted to be influencers or have a career based on what they saw online.

At Mashable, we’re constantly covering what’s happening in the creator world, whether that’s at VidCon, the TikTok Awards, or in conversations across the internet. For our "Creator Playbook" series, we ask creators how they turn content into a career and about the tools they use to do so for "In My Bag."

In our inaugural Creators’ Choice Awards, we surveyed our favorite creators across the internet, including some of our Mashable 101 nominees. We wanted to know which gear and tools they use to see which brands and products are the most loved among digital creators. Now, we're ready to announce the winners.

A smartphone is the most basic tool a creator holds in their toolkit. Not only does it act as the camera, microphone, editor, and uploader, but it's also how they connect with followers.

In our own reporting, Senior Reporter Haley Henschel wrote, "For most of us, our phone is the most important device we own," calling it a "lifeline to friends" and "our primary gateway to the internet." She also noted that unless you carry around a separate camera, your phone is also how you document the world.

Phones are where people create and consume content. So, of course, we wanted to ask creators which phone brands they use and love.

No surprise, our pool of creators overwhelmingly use Apple iPhones. They cited that they prefer Apple because the phones are easy to use and do everything they need from a mobile device. Most of our respondents had an iPhone 15 or newer.

“My entire life, business, and career [are] managed and created using my iPhone,” one respondent wrote.

Mashable's resident iPhone reviewer, Senior Editor Stan Schroeder, called all of the latest iPhones "blazing fast," and has lauded their cameras, battery life, and impressively fast performance in his reviews.

Some creators also commented on the phones’ long battery lives and impressive performance and features. One person said, “This phone is a workhorse,” while another called their iPhone “a power performer” that “gets everything you need done with a snap, with excellent battery life and storage.”

We know that Apple loyalty runs deep, so we suspected it would hold the top spot among our surveyed creators. One even called themself “a forever customer of the brand.”

Samsung Galaxy phones were a second favorite among our creator pool. Samsung stood out for its cameras and overall satisfaction with its phones. Per our reporting, Samsung's Galaxy S26 Ultra is "faster than some mid-range laptops we've tried," and the brand makes the best foldable Android phone.

“It is the best phone available,” said one respondent who has a Samsung Galaxy S26+. Another creator agreed that “Samsung makes the best phones, period.”

Survey respondents praised Samsung’s operating system, processor, AI integration, and long battery life.

“I do the majority of my content creation on my phone and find it very easy to use,” one creator said.

While a phone might be the most accessible or most-used tool for content creation, some creators take video editing or design work to their personal computers. Henschel writes, "The best laptops are fast enough for their primary use cases, well-made, and priced fairly for their specs." We were curious which machines power our favorite creators’ content, and the results were pretty split among different brands.

Though, our creators did choose laptop brands that Mashable experts personally recommend.

Apple reigned supreme as the most preferred laptop brand for our survey takers. They like MacBooks for their compatibility with other Apple devices, sleek design, and overall usability.

"It looks nice and it works for so many things," one respondent wrote. "There is nothing you can't do on it — it's definitely worth the price."

Creators also reported that the software they use to create their content runs well on Apple laptops and that the MacBooks are fast.

One creator wrote, “As a musician, I love the MacBook Pro because of its capabilities for creating music and music content. I frequently run Logic Pro and MuseScore on my MacBook to create music, both of which run super smoothly. I also use it to edit videos featuring my music for social media. I would definitely recommend this computer to another musician.”

Another said their MacBook is “very capable” of doing what they need when putting together content.

Apple’s laptops are definitely a favorite for creatives. “It's great for everything you need as a content creator,” wrote one respondent.

Our creators also prefer Dell for their computing needs. Multiple people noted the longevity of their Dell laptops, with one noting that they’ve had theirs for more than 10 years.

Respondents said simply, “I love my Dell,” and “It’s my favorite.”

Other comments praised Dell’s processing speed, RAM capacity, battery life, ease of use, and durability. These same qualities are why you'll find multiple Dell computers on our list of the best Windows laptops.

Participants in our Creators’ Choice survey also preferred Microsoft laptops. They lauded the laptops' speed, processor, and design, with one person commenting specifically on how easy it is for them to create and edit vlogs.

One creator put it simply: “It's an all-rounder and is good for most people.”

The final winner in the laptop category is HP. Creators love the brand’s laptops for their speed, quality, and affordability.

“My HP laptop is fast, reliable, and handles everyday tasks with ease,” wrote one participant, adding that it’s actually a great value for its price.

Others noted HP’s ease in multitasking and the power behind its laptops. One creator said, “It is easy to use, affordable, durable, and can be used for many things such as recording, video calls, and editing pictures.”

While video is arguably the most popular medium for creators in 2026, many still use photo-editing tools and incorporate photography into their feeds.

Adobe reigns supreme in editing tools for our creators, including its photo-centric tools like Photoshop and Lightroom. Many respondents praised Adobe apps’ ease of use and their editing tools for both professionals and hobbyists.

Multiple people said Adobe is their “go-to” editing tool and that it’s “the best I’ve ever used.”

“This photo editing software is amazing, and I swear by it,” wrote a survey respondent. “The results speak for themselves.”

One person mentioned that Adobe’s AI features “make editing so much more efficient” and that they’d recommend it based on that alone.

The second-most preferred photo editing tool among our survey pool is Canva. Creators love that it’s accessible and easy to use, even for non-professionals.

One comment said, “I like that it's free to use and still has good editing tools for free users.”

Videos make up the majority of creator-made content on social media, whether on TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube, and our creators have two favorite video editing brands.

Adobe was their top choice again, with a large number of responses noting how easy the programs are to use. One creator called Adobe Premiere “an amazing software,” while another said it’s “perfect for what I need it for.”

Others commented on Adobe being a great editing platform and having a good variety of editing tools.

Another favorite video editing tool among our creator pool is CapCut. The mobile-first editing program is incredibly easy to use, according to our respondents. They also like it for its TikTok integration.

One respondent who says they’ve been an avid CapCut user before it was even called CapCut wrote, “It’s been the best editing software I’ve ever used.”

Affordability is another area where CapCut shines — one creator called it “a steal.”

Graphic design can enhance your content with stronger visuals or allow you to create recognizable branding.

And, once again, Adobe was a favorite for our creators. They prefer it for its ease of use and breadth of tools.

One creator said Adobe has a “great comprehensive suite of design and editing tools that are crucial to making the best content.”

Another called Adobe the “best in the business.”

Canva was another top choice in our survey results. People prefer it because it’s also very easy to use and free or affordable.

“I like Canva because it has great tools that make editing more fun,” wrote one respondent.

Another said, “It can do lots of different things to make great products.”

Though smartphones have pretty stellar onboard cameras, many creators still opt for a dedicated vlogging camera. You'll find an array of cameras featured in our In My Bag series, but these are the top three brands, according to the creators who took our survey.

Canon was the favorite camera brand among the survey pool. They praised Canon cameras’ great quality and durability. One person said their Canon PowerShot G7 X “has been a great camera that lasted through the years.”

Creators from our survey also use and like Sony vlogging cameras. They recommend Sony cameras for beginners and pros alike. One respondent said, “I recommend them because they have very good quality; they are certainly very good investments.”

Creators also like Panasonic for its audio quality, image quality, battery life, and user-friendliness.

While we had creators answering questions about their favorite tools and gear, we, of course, also had to ask them about which social media platform they use the most. Drumroll please…

Facebook actually came out on top as the most-used social media among our respondents.

They like Facebook mostly for personal use and keeping up with friends and family members. Many people said the platform is great for connecting with others.

“I have been using it for years and is the best way i have found to stay in touch with people and share my story,” said one respondent.

On the subject of using Facebook for years, one person called it “the O.G. of social media,” adding that “there is nothing not to like” about it.

Another Meta platform was also a favorite: Creators like Instagram for how easy it is to use and for connecting with friends and followers. According to one creator, “it offers a visually engaging platform to connect with friends, family, and global communities.”

Others praised the creativity and engagement of the platform, shouting out its wide array of content types.

One of the creators said, “I love using Instagram,” calling it “one of the best social platforms around.”

The next most-used social media platform is TikTok. Many of the creators we talk to and write about use TikTok as their main platform. Our survey group likes TikTok for its large audience, universal appeal, and fun content.

According to one respondent, it’s the best place to get a start on social media. Once you’ve gotten that start, one creator said, “It's a good platform for people who want to share their life or creations through short-form videos. Or if they want to post short clips from their longer videos to attract viewers to their YouTube channel.”

And that brings us to our creator pool's final favorite social media platform: YouTube. Similar to TikTok, one creator said, “It's a great place to post your content, whether long or short form, and is used by millions.”

Others love it for the easy-to-use platform and quality features, calling it “fun and cool” and “better than the other brands.”
