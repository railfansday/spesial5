All of Shiba Inu's (SHIB) Five-Year Rally Comes Down to One 2021 Price

Shiba Inu (SHIB) fell to $0.00000337 in late November 2026, its lowest level since 2021, according to TradingView data. On SHIB's weekly chart, the only price history below that level comes from spring 2021, before the token's first big rally.

According to TradingView data, SHIB traded between about $0.0000011 and $0.0000018 in late April and early May 2021. The token set its all-time high of $0.00008845 on Oct. 28, 2021.

According to CryptoRank, SHIB rose 355.4% in May 2021 and 833.6% in October 2021, its best month on record.

SHIB started 2026 at $0.00000692, and from early 2026 until mid-November, it traded above $0.000004. On Nov. 12, the day Nvidia announced that CEO Jensen Huang had resigned, SHIB fell to $0.0000043, according to CoinGecko data. In November, the token also dropped below its lows of 2023, 2024 and 2025. SHIB closed the week ending Nov. 22 near $0.00000363, down 7.99%, according to TradingView.

Before November, SHIB's yearly low was $0.00000411, set at the July 17 close. On Nov. 20, SHIB had a market capitalization of $2.17 billion and ranked No. 34 on CoinMarketCap, about $10 million behind Bittensor (TAO).

SHIB lost 22.7% in November 2026, according to CryptoRank. The token also lost 29.6% and 24.8% in November of 2021 and 2022, respectively. In November of 2023 and 2024, SHIB gained 6.62% and 48.8%, and in November 2025, it fell 16.2%. SHIB has closed November in the red in 2021, 2022, 2025 and 2026.

SHIB's median December return is -17.6%, and its average December return is -11.4%, according to CryptoRank data.

SHIB has closed December in the green only once, in 2023, when it gained 24.6%. In December of 2021, 2022, 2024 and 2025, the token lost 29.5%, 13.5%, 21% and 17.6%, respectively.

In 2021, the December drop came right after SHIB's record high. The token lost 29.6% in November 2021 and 29.5% in December, and in January 2022, it fell another 36%.

In 2026, SHIB has lost value in six of 11 months so far. The token's biggest monthly drops this year came in June and November, when it lost 24% and 22.7%, respectively, according to CryptoRank. Its best month was September, with a 13.5% gain.
