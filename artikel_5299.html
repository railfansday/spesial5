Bitcoin Could Take an NFT-Style Hit and Crash to $10,000 According to Bloomberg Analyst

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Bitcoin, the most valued cryptocurrency by market cap, has seen a massive decline in price value. Since the start of November, the big bull has lost an estimated 23% in price according to data from CoinMarketCap.

As the asset continues to plummet, market experts are sharing their data-driven analysis, with one such being Bloomberg’s Mike McGlone. The market analyst believes  that Bitcoin could follow the same pattern observed during the NFT market crash and fall to as low as $10,000.

The Bloomberg Intelligence senior commodity strategist Mike McGlone declared on Sunday that “the everything bubble is bursting,” arguing that broad declines across major assets are already under way after years of aggressive money creation.

In the first half of his post, McGlone said the Bitcoin and crypto bear market since October 2025 “may be a guide of what to expect in most markets, particularly the US stock market, gold, metals, commodities and US Treasury bond yields; they’re all going down. It’s simply overdue reversion.”

He expects sharp short-covering rallies that “typically gotta keep false hope alive to feed the bear,” comparing many cryptocurrencies to the collapse of NFTs and warning that tokens tracking nothing could approach zero. Bitcoin, he added, may find a “good gravity-pull pivot” near $10,000 to clear speculative excess.

In the second half of his post, McGlone argued that the strong annual gains previously seen in Bitcoin, natural gas, gold, silver and other commodities would reverse and spread weakness into the broader stock market, potentially leaving the S&P 500 with its third annual decline since 2009.

He anticipates significant drawdowns that began from early October highs, lower prices for crude oil and copper, a shift by the Federal Reserve toward easing, and a normal post-inflation period of deflation as US housing peaks. Elevated mortgage rates and Treasury yields, he said, will later be viewed as an opportunity for investors, with the year more likely to finish in volatility than calm.

“Big green 2026 annual candles in Bitcoin, US natural gas, gold, silver, platinum, palladium, iron ore, nickel, lead, corn, copper, the Nasdaq….will gain contagion with the S&P 500 Total Return ending the year with its third down year since 2009. A 50% drawdown likely started from the Oct. 6 high, a year after Bitcoin’s peak and about 9-months after gold’s top. Crude oil back toward $40. Copper Under $5…” he wrote.
