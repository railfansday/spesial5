Random: Modder Transforms DS Into A Home Console, And It's Super Cool

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

We have long pondered what a DS library would look like on Nintendo Switch Online for some dual-screen, big-screen action. Nintendo is yet to give us the real deal, so modder KOUZEX has taken things into his own hands, creating a build that converts our beloved clamshell into a full-blown home console. And yes, it's super cool.

The above video introduces the DualStation, a mod build that combines an original DS motherboard and a bunch of unique components into a system that boasts quite an impressive array of features. We're talking full 60Hz HDMI output, touch controls via just about any means you can think of (controller, mouse, Joy-Con, drawing tablets), Bluetooth controller support, a built-in mic and a bunch of other bells and whistles.

KOUZEX's introduction video showcases the full build process before highlighting the system's widescreen gameplay, screen swapping and more.

It runs original DS and GBA cartridges, too, so there's still that sense of classic home console tactility that we all know and love.

The modder plans to sell both self-install kits and pre-built systems at some point down the line, providing options for those who know their way around a soldering iron and those who don't.

It all looks really rather wonderful in practice. As rumours of DS/3DS games on NSO continue to rumble away in the background, it's wild to see someone go all-out with some proper home console flair.
