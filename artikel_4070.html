Forza Horizon 6's New City Life Update Includes Multiple Halo & Xbox Freebies

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Unlock Master Chief, liveries and the Xbox IKEA Prop Pack!

Yesterday marked the long-awaited arrival of Forza Horizon 6's City Life update, which adds two new permanent race routes in Tokyo City amongst other things, and there are even some Xbox and Halo-related freebies tucked in!

In terms of Halo, it's said that everyone will soon be getting a free 2025 GR GT Prototype livery via the Message Centre celebrating the franchise along with Xbox's 25th anniversary. We haven't come across this yet, although we did get a separate Master Chief livery for the Urus Lamborghini as well.

Meanwhile, Master Chief himself will become available as a character model in the Week 2 Photo Challenge.

"Additionally, join us in celebrating 25 Years of XBOX and Halo with a special livery on the 2025 GR GT Prototype (to be sent to all players via Message Center) and The Master Chief Outfit unlockable in the Week 2 Photo Challenge of the Festival Playlist."

Beyond this, they've also added an IKEA Prop Pack containing items from the recent real-life collaboration with Xbox that you can use to decorate your custom garages, so that's a neat little addition.

As for the more meaty stuff, you'll find 3 new-to-Horizon cars in the Festival Playlist, while the two new race routes arrive in the form of the Edo Circuit Road Race and Shuto Charge Street Race.

Most of this is available to check out in Forza Horizon 6 right now, although as mentioned, the Halo livery for the GR GT Prototype and Master Chief character skin haven't arrived just yet. Plenty to be getting on with in the meantime!
