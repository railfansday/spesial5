Ubisoft Wants Rayman To Become A Huge, Transmedia Star

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Ubisoft is gearing up to launch Rayman Legends Retold, a reimagined remake of the 2013 original. While many have questioned the decision to remake a game that arguably still looks and plays great to this day, the developers have been clear in their intention to use Retold as a launchpad for the future of the franchise.

Now, in a new interview with The Game Business, brand producer Loïc Gounon has expressed much broader ambitions for the character, perhaps bringing Rayman more in line with something like Sonic in terms of visibility.

In other words, Gounon is thinking about not only games, but other mediums too, including TV shows and comics. The first step is to ensure the brand's success in regions such as the US and Asia, with previous Rayman titles' success being largely limited to Europe.

“I would like to see Rayman as a must-have IP for video games across the world. Rayman was generally stronger in France and the UK, and in some cases in the US. Enlarging the reach of the IP, also towards Asia and performing well in the US, is the first ambition… making sure that we are a must-have platforming adventure title across those markets in the video game world.

“And I guess beyond the video game world. So, comics, TV series... actually, there’s a French comic book that came out with Rabbids and Rayman being reconnected.”

However, Gounon does not necessarily believe that producing a new 3D title is the key to success, and instead wants to focus on the characters and lore. Sorry to those hoping for Rayman 4 – hey, it might still happen!

“Rayman is iconic because of its gameplay. But going back to the adventure roots of Rayman, having people dive into those characters, exploring deeper into that world and mythology, that’s what we believe in.

“It’s the world-building part, the consistency of that world-building, and the empathy for those characters, that will make the franchise last and maybe become bigger.”

Rayman Legends Retold is right around the corner, scheduled to launch on 3rd December 2026. Meanwhile, Ubisoft itself is currently coming under fire for its heavy focus on generative AI, with a new report revealing that employees are required to justify reasons for not using the technology in new project pitches.
