How To Reach Max Health In GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Maxing out your Health in GTA 6 helps keep Jason and Lucia alive in tough situations, but it requires consistent workouts and healthy habits.

In Grand Theft Auto 6, Jason and Lucia will have three main stats to track: Health, Stamina, and Focus. All three stats can be monitored using the Fitness app on their phones and boosted by completing specific tasks.

Health, in particular, is the stat that determines how much damage you can take before you get the dreaded Wasted screen. Increasing this stat can make fights easier and also prevent you from being killed if you hit something while flying down the highways of Leonida. For that reason, it's probably the most important stat to train, and you'll need to do it consistently.

Like the other two stats, Health maxes out at Level 4, and the main way to increase it is by working out. Certain workouts can also increase Stamina, so you want to focus on Strength workouts for Health. Additionally, you can only do up to three workouts per day, so you won't be able to spend a whole in-game day at the gym to knock this out a level in one go.

Jason and Lucia's stats are independent of each other. Working out one character does not raise the overall stats for both; you will need to put in time for both to raise their individual stats.

As for the strength workouts themselves, you'll need to keep increasing the weight limit to improve your Health as each character becomes stronger. Additionally, pay attention to the workout window on your Fitness App. If too much time passes between workouts, and you neglect them, you'll start to see a negative impact on your Health instead.

Don't start a workout with low Stamina. If you don't have enough Stamina, you will fail the workout because you can't finish the set.

In addition to the workouts, you also need to monitor your hunger and sleep. Allowing either of these to fall too low will ultimately hurt your progress and recovery time, so be sure to check these stats in the Fitness app before working out and complete them immediately after the workout if needed.

As long as you're sticking to your workouts and taking care of yourself, you should be able to maintain both Jason and Lucia's max Health benefits with ease.
