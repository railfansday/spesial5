LSU star MiLaysia Fulwiley arrested after allegedly striking police officer with car

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Guard allegedly tried to flee traffic stop in Baton Rouge

Tigers say they are aware of situation involving player

LSU guard MiLaysia Fulwiley, one of the ninth-ranked Tigers’ leading scorers, was arrested after allegedly striking a police officer while attempting to flee from a traffic stop.

LSU’s athletic department, in a written statement provided to The Associated Press, said university officials “are aware of the situation and have no further comment at this time as it’s an ongoing legal matter.”

The arrest, first reported by WBRZ TV in Baton Rouge, occurred Saturday night as LSU’s football game against Texas was ending in the sold-out, 102,000-seat Tiger Stadium.

Authorities allege that an officer tried to stop the 21-year-old Fulwiley after observing her weaving through traffic, and that she drove away from the scene, the right front side of her vehicle striking a police officer. She allegedly sought to cut through a store parking lot before her vehicle was blocked by responding police.

Fulwiley was booked into East Baton Rouge Parish Prison on charges including aggravated battery, flight from an officer, aggravated assault and resisting an officer.

Fulwiley, who is averaging 17.8 points per game this season, scored a season-high 23 points during No 9 LSU’s 101-37 victory over Denver on Friday.

LSU are scheduled to play next at home on Monday night against Troy and Fulwiley’s availability for that game was unclear as of Sunday afternoon.
