Man arrested after ‘horrific’ knife attack on Bradford councillor

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Politicians call attack on independent councillor Ibrar Ali canvassing in Bradford Moor ward ‘deeply disturbing’

Politicians have described an attack on a councillor who was canvassing in Bradford as “horrific” and “deeply disturbing”, after a man was arrested on suspicion of attempted murder.

A 32-year-old man is in custody suspected of attacking Ibrar Ali at about 11am on Thursday as the independent councillor was door-knocking in his Bradford Moor ward, leaving him with a stab wound in the leg.

West Yorkshire police said there was nothing to suggest the incident was politically motivated, although the investigation was at an early stage.

Jim McMahon, the MP for Oldham West, described the attack as “horrific” and said: “Our thoughts are with [Ali], his family, councillors in Bradford and, of course, our members of parliament too.

“Our thoughts are very much with you all at this time.”

Imran Hussain, the MP for Bradford East, said: “This horrific attack is deeply disturbing. My thoughts and prayers are with councillor Ibrar Ali and his family.

“No elected politician should feel under threat or fear for their safety while carrying out their duties and serving their community. I am receiving regular updates from the police as they deal with this extremely serious incident, and I understand a suspect is in police custody.”

Officers were called just after 11am to reports that a 52-year-old man, a serving Bradford councillor, had been found seriously injured on Manse Street.

Police said: “The man is believed to have first been injured on Thornbury Avenue, before being driven to Manse Street where emergency services attended. He was taken to hospital where his condition is described as serious but not life-threatening.”

A 32-year-old man fled to the roof of a property on Thornbury Avenue but was brought down some time later and arrested on suspicion of attempted murder.

Sharon Taylor, a local government minister, named Ali in the House of Lords. Lady Taylor said: “I am afraid I have to mention a very tragic incident that’s occurred in Bradford … where an independent councillor who was out canvassing has been stabbed.

“I would want to send the wishes, that I’m sure noble lords would agree, of the whole house to councillor Ibrar Ali for a speedy recovery, and our thoughts are with all of those in Bradford who are affected by this awful incident.”

Police cordons were in place on Manse Street, Leeds Road and Thornbury Avenue while inquiries are carried out.

Adam, who works in the area and did not want to give his surname, said: “I know the councillor on a personal level and it’s shocking. I just know he’s a very kind, warm-hearted man whose goal is to serve, his life is to help out everyone.

“He just wants everyone to succeed. He definitely didn’t deserve what’s happened.

Chris Herd, the lord mayor of Bradford, said: “It is deeply distressing that this incident has occurred.

“Councillors work tirelessly on behalf of residents across the district and should be able to carry out their duties safely.

“We will continue to work closely with our colleagues at West Yorkshire police as they investigate this matter and seek to bring those responsible to justice, and would like to thank those who provided aid and support at the time.”

Press Association contributed to this report
