Gemma Chan Says She’d ‘Absolutely’ Play a Bond Girl Amid Fan-Casting: ‘I’m Flattered’

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Gemma Chan is ready for her license to kill.

The “Crazy Rich Asians” star tells me that she’s aware that fans have been dream-casting her as a Bond Girl.

“I’m flattered, and absolutely I would,” Chan said Wednesday night at the premiere of her new drama “Josephine” at the DGA in West Hollywood. “It’s an iconic franchise, and I’m very excited that things are moving forward with it now.” Related Stories Taylor Swift Performs 'I Knew It, I Knew You' and 'Our Song' Mashup at CMA Awards Grammy Nominations 2027: Raye Leads With 11 Noms as Rosalía, Olivia Rodrigo, Ella Langley and Other Female Artists Dominate

Bond Girl speculation is already being shaken and stirred into a frenzy now that Amazon MGM finally announced that Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond. The 40-year-old “Twilight” alum will star in his first 007 film with Denis Villeneuve at the helm, a script by Steven Knight and Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing.

Chan was among the list of possible Bond Girls posted by Variety on Nov. 16. “Gemma Chan is technically already part of the Bond universe. In the video game ‘007 First Light,’ she plays Dr. Selina Tan, a brilliant academic with a background in psychology and game theory whose role at MI6 is critical in Bond’s journey to earning 00 status,” Variety’s Clayton Davis wrote. “Now make it canon on the big screen. The British star of ‘Crazy Rich Asians,’ ‘Eternals’ and ‘The Creator’ has the cool authority this franchise runs on, whether she’s behind a desk at Vauxhall Cross or across a baccarat table in Macau. She’d make a natural new M or the next Vesper.”

Others on the list include Zendaya, Emily Blunt, Florence Pugh, Chase Infiniti, Florence Pugh, Marion Cotillard, Adria Arjona, Lily James, Gugu Mbatha-Raw, Isabela Merced, Inde Navarrette, Hunter Schafer, Sydney Sweeney and more.

Chan says she’s also open to playing a villain.

Pierce Brosnan, who played James Bond in four films from 1995 to 2002, congratulated Pattinson on nabbing the role in an Instagram post on Wednesday. Pattinson’s “Tenet” co-star Elizabeth Debicki told me earlier this week that she thinks that he is “perfect” to play 007.
