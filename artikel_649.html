LA Skins Fest Concludes 20th Year Celebrating Indigenous Artists

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The 20th annual LA Skins Fest concluded on Sunday following the five-day celebration of Native American cinema and special screenings at the TCL Chinese Theatre.

Since its founding, the festival has become a platform for Indigenous filmmakers to showcase their work, connect with entertainment industry professionals and reach new audiences. It also extends its mission outside the annual festival through its partnership with the Native American Media Alliance, supporting year-round professional development initiatives that includes writing labs, animation programs, showrunner training, development workshops and youth media education. This year’s LA Skins Fest featured a line-up of narrative features, documentaries, short films and special screenings that recognize Indigenous artists. This year’s festival opened with films presented by Amazon MGM Studios and also included a Netflix screening of “North of North” with actress Anna Lambe and a Cherokee-language screening of “The Rings of Power” followed by a discussion with the actors and Cherokee Film’s language revitalization team.

The festival also began with a Native Youth Studio Tour at Sony Pictures Studios, providing students from Sherman Indian High School an introduction to career opportunities in media, which further reflects the festival’s dedication to supporting future generations of Native American entertainment professionals.

Presented by Paramount Television Studios, the 15th Annual Native American Media Awards hosted by Q’orianka Kilcher and Kiowa Gordon concluded the festival with a celebration of Indigenous filmmakers, writers, actors and creative professionals. Among this year’s winners were Orlando Skidmore for achievement in filmmaking and Sophia Lindholm for achievement in student filmmaking. The emerging filmmaker award was given to Gina East for the narrative film category and Elissa Simons for the documentary category.

The ceremony also recognized the 6th Annual Native American Writer Accelerator Grantees. Sponsored by Netflix, the program selects 12 Indigenous writers to receive financial assistance and opportunities to advance their careers in media. This year’s grantees are Doane Avery, Rachel Baldwin, Ripley Barnes, Sonny Brava, C.J. Caughman, Bryson Chun, Kelly Lynne D’Angelo, Yelyna DeLeon, Marleah Makpiaq LaBelle, Desiree Leiahloha, Jason Marcus and Vlad Perez.
