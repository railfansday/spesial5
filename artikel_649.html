Jon Stewart reveals where hed be if he hadnt gone into comedy

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Jon Stewart always enjoys a little audience Q&A session in between filming.

The comedian and late night show host has previously spoken about whether or not he'd ever run for office, and who his worst ever Daily Show guest was, but in the clip above he's asked what he'd be doing if he never worked for The Daily Show at all.

Stewart's response is basically a long, amusing story about getting fired from Woolworth's by his own brother after he damaged multiple aquariums during a rogue beanbag stunt.
