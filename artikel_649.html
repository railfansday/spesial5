Salma Hayek Pinault and Saúl ‘Canelo’ Álvarez Board Cannes Short Film Palme d’Or Winner ‘For the Opponents,’ Igniting Its Oscar Campaign (EXCLUSIVE)

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Joining the film as executive producers, the two Mexican icons’ support will galvanise the film’s campaign for Academy Award live action short film recognition

Salma Hayek Pinault and boxing world champion Saúl “Canelo” Álvarez two of Mexico’s greatest contemporary icons, have boarded Cannes 2026 short film Palme d’Or winner “For the Opponents” as executive producers galvanizing Mexico’s push for the film’s contention in the Academy Award Live Action Short Film race.

“For the Opponents” (“Para los Contrincantes”) has already grabbed a lot of attention as the Cannes Official Selection conquering follow-up to Argentine writer-director Federico Luis’ “Simon of the Mountain,” his first solo feature and a Cannes Critics’ Week Grand Prize in 2024. Hayek and Canelo’s support, however, will now take media and Academy notice to another level.

Starring the young Damián López and shot in 16mm and a 4:3 box format, “For the Opponents” trails Damian, a young boy, as he is registered for a child boxing match by his father coach. It then takes in four rounds of combat, with dad’s instructions heard off-screen– If you open your mouth they could fracture your jaw,” “hold your hands up,” “throw two punches to the chest, then jabs to he head” as he also pressures Damián by way of encouragement: “This is the chance of your life,” he is heard saying.

Damián only says two words in the whole film – “Thank you” to the check-in registrar. Yet Luis’ while focus is on him, as he boxes and is caught in close-up listening to his father, facing the judges’ verdict and reacting to that with a welter of mixed emotions.

Luis’ direction is notable for its contention and detail, both physical and psychological. There is no sensationalism, though respect, for the plucky Damián and no “poverty porn” in a scene afterwards of Damián and a friend running down a street in Mexico City’s Tepito district, sometimes reduced by descriptions to just one of the City’s most dangerous no-go hoods.

“As an executive producer, I couldn’t be prouder to support this remarkable short film. ‘For the Opponents’ is deeply affecting and seeing young Damián’s grit and determination has stuck with me. I am heartbroken feeling the pain in his fight for himself and for his family,” said Salma Hayek Pinault, Oscar nominated for her performance in “Frida” and one of Hollywood’s most influential Latina voices.

“When I saw Damián in the gym, I saw myself as a boy. I also started very young, with a pair of gloves and the dream of becoming a champion. This film moved me because behind the boxer there is a child, with his dreams and his emotions. I’m very proud to support a story that shows such a real side of Mexican boxing,” said ‘Canelo’ Álvarez, a four division boxing world champion.

In his most recent fight, held Oct. 31 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, ‘Canelo’ Álvarez scored a unanimous decision victory over France’s previously undefeated Christian Mbilli to reclaim the WBC super middleweight world title. It is his 22nd world championship victory.

Sold by Luxbox, “For the Opponents” is backed by a prestige producer package of Chile’s Planta (“La Perra,”“Double Freedom”), Mexico’s Cárcava Cine (“The Garden We Dreamed”) Fiasco (“Hijas del Bosque”), Deptford Film (“Double Freedom”) and Les Films du Worso (“Dao”).

Fernando Bascuñán, Fernanda de la Peza, Elena Fortes and Augusto Matte produce. Héctor Sulaimán serves as executive producers and Sylvie Plait and Alejandro Arenas as co-producers.

The film “explores the dreams, expectations and family bonds surrounding a child trying to make his way in the sport,” the producers said in a statement.

“For the Opponents” has been selected for more than 50 international festivals including Telluride, San Sebastián and Tallinn Black Nights.
