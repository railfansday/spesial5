The Best Gifts Under $100 for Design Enthusiasts

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

A $100 budget opens up plenty of possibilities for thoughtful gifting, especially when you know where to look. It’s enough to find something genuinely well-designed, whether that’s a sculptural object for the home, an everyday essential with an unexpected twist, or a small indulgence someone might not think to buy for themselves.

The trick is finding gifts that offer something beyond their price tag. We’re drawn to pieces with a distinctive point of view, clever functionality, and materials or details that make them memorable. With that in mind, we’ve rounded up our favorite gifts under $100, from playful accessories and colorful tabletop finds to practical objects that make everyday routines a little more interesting.

Le Creuset brings a little holiday magic to its classic cocotte with an embossed Christmas tree lid and a gold-finished star knob. Crafted from durable glazed stoneware, the 24-ounce vessel moves easily from oven to table, making it ideal for individual pot pies, baked pasta, or warm desserts. Available in six colors, it’s a festive gift that’s just as practical as it is charming.

The Noël Mini Cocotte is available at williams-sonoma.com for $62.

Inspired by the pleated designs of Issey Miyake, Wooj’s Kinoko Lamp borrows the delicate look of folded paper without its fragility. Each mushroom-shaped shade is 3D printed from recycled plastic in Brooklyn, creating an accordion-like texture that softly diffuses light. Available in Amber, Smoke, Olive Oil, and Natural, it’s a small but striking addition to a desk, bookshelf, or bedside table.

The Kinoko Lamp is available at wooj.design for $95.

With its puffy, quilted silhouette and compact proportions, Calpak’s Luka Mini Duffel brings a playful edge to an everyday essential. The lightweight, water-resistant bag packs plenty of functionality into its design, including padded shoulder straps, dedicated bottle pockets, and a luggage sleeve with a hidden security compartment. Available in 18 colors, from understated neutrals to shimmering metallics, it’s a practical gift with plenty of personality.

The Luka Mini Duffel is available in select colors at calpaktravel.com for $98.

Chess has always been a game of strategy, but Printworks makes a case for keeping the board on display between matches. This contemporary take on the classic features wooden playing pieces, a sleek acrylic-topped board, and a coordinated storage box designed to look at home on a coffee table. Available in multiple colorways, it’s a gift that brings people together while doubling as decor.

The Classic Chess Set is available at printworksmarket.com for $79.

A little whimsy goes a long way when it comes to travel accessories. Antler’s Heritage Leather Aeroplane AirTag Holder takes the shape of a miniature airplane, crafted from scratch-resistant green leather with subtle stitched details. A discreet compartment keeps an Apple AirTag securely tucked away, while a leather lanyard attaches easily to luggage or a favorite travel bag. It’s a charming way to keep track of belongings without sacrificing style.

The Heritage Leather Aeroplane AirTag Holder is available at ⁠antler.com for $75.

Belgian jewelry designers Wouters & Hendrix bring a touch of surrealism to the tabletop with this trio of sculptural candleholders. Inspired by 1920s surrealist art, the hand-painted stoneware pieces combine graphic checkerboard patterns, contrasting colors, and three playful silhouettes. Arrange them together for an unexpected centerpiece or scatter them around a room for little moments of visual interest.

The Les Objets Mouleversants Candle Holder Set is available at lumens.com for $85.30.

Originally created as teaching tools for Spanish classrooms, these solid beechwood geometric shapes have been in production since 1934. The 15-piece set comes in a wooden box with a hinged lid, inviting recipients to explore form, proportion, and composition through hands-on experimentation. Part educational tool, part sculptural desk accessory, it’s an unexpected gift for anyone fascinated by architecture, mathematics, or design.

The Wood Polyhedra Set is available at presentandcorrect.com in two sizes: large box ($82) and small box ($41).

What happens when a cardboard prototype becomes the finished product? Dutch designer Annebet Philips brings her playful, sketch-like aesthetic to this porcelain teapot, preserving the uneven edges and fold lines of the original cardboard model. Hand-painted black outlines give it an almost cartoonish quality, turning an everyday kitchen essential into a conversation piece.

The Carte Blanche Teapot is available at lumens.com for $98.

Designed with birds’ feeding habits in mind, this sculptural feeder takes its cues from traditional grain silos. Its cylindrical form accommodates birds that cling vertically while eating, while a clever filling mechanism helps keep seeds clean and dry. Made from upcycled plastic, the Finnish design comes in three earthy colors and brings a modern touch to outdoor spaces.

The Silo Bird Feeder is available at goodeeworld.com for $75.

Disguised as colorful coffee table books, Printworks’ jewelry boxes bring a little personality to everyday organization. Cloth-covered exteriors feature bold typography and playful color combinations, while padded interiors keep rings, earrings, necklaces, and other treasures neatly arranged. Available in a range of designs and sizes, they’re made to be displayed rather than tucked away in a drawer.

The Jewelry Boxes are available at printworksmarket.com starting at $49.

Inspired by traditional French bedside drinkware, Maison Balzac’s colorful glass carafe turns a practical necessity into a small design moment. The matching cup sits neatly over the opening when not in use, creating a sculptural silhouette that’s equally at home on a nightstand or desk. Made from dishwasher-safe borosilicate glass, the set comes in six colors and can even double as a vase.

The Bedside Carafe is available at comingsoonnewyork.com for $90.

German artist Marc David Spengler brings his graffiti-inspired compositions to the sofa with this colorful collaboration for Slowdown Studio. Playful abstract shapes give the 20-inch cotton pillow cover an artistic quality, while a soft, neutral reverse offers a quieter alternative. It’s a thoughtful gift for someone who appreciates original artwork in unexpected places. Pillow insert sold separately.

The Bessie Throw Pillow Cover is available at slowdownstudio.com for $80.

A rolling pin might not seem like an exciting gift, but this one makes a convincing case otherwise. Handcrafted in Tennessee from black walnut, cherry, and maple, East Fork’s Mixed Wood Rolling Pin celebrates the natural beauty of contrasting hardwoods. Its tapered, French-style silhouette offers greater control when working dough, while the substantial construction makes it a kitchen tool designed to last for years.

The Mixed Wood Rolling Pin is available at eastfork.com for $98.

Photography courtesy of Sophie Lou Jacobsen

Designer Sophie Lou Jacobsen transforms an everyday tabletop essential into a set of four gleaming spirals, sand-cast in aluminum and finished with nickel plating. Their fluid, looping forms bring a playful graphic element to the table, whether they’re holding drinks or arranged together as decorative objects. It’s a perfect gift for anyone who appreciates design in the smallest details.

The Spiral Coaster Set is available at sophieloujacobsen.com for $85.

With its undulating silhouette and bold blue-and-white palette, this ceramic vase brings a sense of movement to an otherwise stationary object. Standing nearly 12 inches tall, its sculptural curves make an impression with or without flowers, while the narrow opening is well suited to a few carefully chosen stems. It’s a playful addition to a shelf, mantel, or dining table.

The Ceramic Swish Vase is available at anthropologie.com for $58.
