Hate it or love it, Fox News is a powerful force in American news, media, and politics. It’s one of the most-watched TV channels in the country, with a massive viewership spanning all parties and ages.

And tonight, the network is hosting special coverage of the 2026 midterm elections. Titled FOX News Democracy 26, the broadcast will begin at 6 p.m. ET. Bret Baier and Martha MacCallum will serve as the main hosts, joined throughout the evening by the likes of Sean Hannity, Dana Perino, Brit Hume, and more.

If you’re looking to watch (or hate-watch) Fox News, it can be tricky to do so without cable. Ahead is a quick guide on the best ways to stream Fox News online without cable, including ways to watch Fox News for free.

The best way for cord-cutters to watch Fox News for free without cable is with a live TV streaming service. These types of streaming services let you watch TV channels from any streaming device without a cable box, and most of them are more affordable than traditional cable packages (plus, they don’t come with restrictive contracts). That means you can stream Fox News, plus other news, sports, and entertainment channels.

These are three of the best ways to stream Fox News for free online:

With a variety of well-priced packages, a great interface, and industry-leading channel availability, DirecTV is our favorite streamer for cord-cutters. Fox News is available in the service’s MyNews Genre Pack and all of its larger Signature Packages. MyNews is the most affordable option at $39.99 a month. All DirecTV packages start with a five-day free trial.
