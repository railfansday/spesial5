Russell Brand Sentenced To 10 Years In Prison For Rape & Sexual Assault

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Russell Brand will spend more than a decade behind bars.

During a hearing on Monday at London’s Southwark Crown Court, the Forgetting Sarah Marshall star turned free-thinking Rumble streamer was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison for rape and sexual assault.

Brand, 51, was convicted last week of anally raping a woman in 2009. He was also found guilty of three counts of sexual assault, including grabbing the breasts of an ex-MTV staffer in 2004 and putting his head in the groin of a former Channel 4 employee in 2009. Brand will serve nine years in prison for the rape conviction and 18 months in prison for the three sexual assault convictions. He will be eligible to be released “on license” after serving two-thirds, or around seven years, of the sentence. Watch on Deadline

In sentencing remarks following the four-week trial, Mr Justice Bennathan described Brand as a “sexual predator,” and said his fame protected him until 2023, when Channel 4 and The Times of London published allegations about his conduct.

Bennathan pointed to Brand’s “staggering arrogance” in his treatment of women, and he admonished his associates for indulging his “dreadful behavior.” The remarks were broadcast live on Sky News on a 10-second time delay.

He paid tribute to the “determination and bravery” of the women who made complaints about Brand. “Whatever women wear, wherever they go, indeed whatever they drink, they retain the right to say ‘no’ and to demand justice from the criminal courts when sexual predators like you, Russell Brand, act without their consent,” Bennathan said.

Brand has consistently maintained his innocence, telling the jury: “Consent is fundamental, essential, necessary, and in many ways more important than the sex itself.” He was cleared of three charges: Raping a woman in 1999, attempted indecent assault in 2001, and orally raping a woman in 2004.

Ian Winter, Brand’s barrister, had earlier pleaded for leniency in the sentencing. Winter said his client was of “good character” and that a long prison term would be a source of “catastrophic devastation to his family.”

Brand spent the weekend in Wandsworth prison after being remanded in custody last week because he was denied bail. Judge Mr Justice Bennathan said Brand was a man of “considerable resources” and there were “substantial grounds to fear he might in the end not feel able to come along and face his sentence.”

A prison van delivered Brand to Southwark Crown Court on Monday, a marked change from his previous arrivals during the four-week trial, when he emerged from a blacked-out private car in sunglasses and buttoned-down shirts.

Earlier on Monday morning, Brand’s wife, Laura Brand, arrived in court. She gave evidence during the trial, describing her husband as “amazingly attentive” and “kind,” telling jurors: “I love him very much.”

Brand’s supporters also stood outside court, holding a banner that said: “Russell Brand, we stand with you. Christ was crucified for telling the truth. Russell is the truth.”
