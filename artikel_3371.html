‘Hunger Games: Sunrise On The Reaping’ Hot $110M+ U.S. Opening – Box Office Update

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

FRIDAY MIDDAY: Lionsgate’s The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is heading for the fourth highest opening for the Suzanne Collins franchise as of now with $110M stateside at 4,108 theaters after a Friday that’s $51M which includes those $21M previews. If the movie isn’t front-loaded, it has a shot at possibly becoming the third highest North American opening for the franchise.

The industry overseas outlook is $97M, which if that holds means a $206.7M global opening. That latter number is not according to Lionsgate. A high $80M+ foreign start is within reach.

Top Hunger Games openings at the North American B.O. belong to 2013’s Catching Fire ($70.9M Friday/previews, $158M 3-day); the original 2012 Hunger Games ($67.2M Friday/previews and $152.5M opening), and 2014’s Hunger Games: Mockingjay Part 1 ($55.1M Friday/previews and $121.8M 3-day). Sunrise on the Reaping is the sixth movie in the Collins’ franchise, the fifth directed by Francis Lawrence. Altogether the previous five movies count $3.3 billion at the worldwide box office.

Again, the jaw drop here is how this franchise turned its fortunes upward after hitting an all-time low in its opening three years ago with Hunger Games: Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes ($44.6M). Comps to other sixthquels are hard, and the opening jump between Songbirds & Sunrise is +147% — which is unheard of. The surge in domestic openings between Christopher Nolan’s Batman Begins and Dark Knight was +225%.

Among all openings in November at the domestic B.O., Sunrise on the Reaping is currently 13th in a list of 16 movies that have bowed to over $100M for the month.

RelishMix beams that the great word-of-mouth that the pic has “book loyalists showing up in full force, insisting this adaptation finally respects the source material. There’s trust running through the comments, ‘Director Francis Lawrence’s track record and the franchise’s unbroken prequel streak as reasons to believe this one sticks the landing,’ and, ‘We’ll need therapy after this,’ read as proof of the story’s emotional weight. Comments marvel, ‘Dang from Voldemort, Moriarty, Hades, and Ramses to President Snow, Ralph Fiennes catching literary villain roles like Pokémon.’ Others draw sharper comparison, saying ‘this is like the and/or of the Hunger Games I feel it, I know it, I read the book and they are going to break your heart.'”

Overall social media reach is 652M across TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, X and Facebook which is not far from The Odyssey‘s pre-opening reach at 680M and +66% above where Hunger Games: Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes was in its social media universe, bowing shortly after the actors strike ended (393M). That 652M reach for Sunrise on the Reaping is 95% above other action-sci-fi-fantasy sub-genre features.

“Hunger Games cast activation index clocks 21 of 24 cast/creators activated with 40.4M total fans,” adds RelishMix in their weekly report.

“Notably, Jennifer Lawrence and her newly rebooted Instagram with 9.2M fans activated a video from Fallon shot in an elevator at 30 Rock with 25+M views and 40+M views on the Fallon IG. Activations also include McKenna Grace at 13M, Maya Hawke at 10.4M, Elle Fanning at 7.7M, Josh Hutcherson at 4.2M, Glenn Close at 1.3M, Joseph Zada at 442K and Whitney Peak at 492K. As well, some cast-off-the grid, include Jesse Plemons, Ralph Fiennes, Woody Harrelson and Kieran Culkin.”

Peter Farrelly’s I Play Rocky from Amazon MGM Studios is looking at $10M in second place after a $3.9M Friday in its third frame 2,477 wide break and a running cume of $11M by EOD Sunday. The exits are excellent, so let’s hope there’s long legs on this Sylvester Stallone biopic which is 89% with Rotten Tomatoes critics and 98% with moviegoers.

No. 3 goes to the second weekend of Paramount’s Johnny Depp Ebenezer at 3,545 theaters with $2.1M today, a 3-day of $8M, -58%, and ten-day total by Sunday of $32.8M.

Fourth is Warner Bros’ The Cat in the Hat at 3,262 theaters, a third Friday of $1.7M, 3-day of $7.3M, -35%, and running cume of $47.5M.

Fifth is the third weekend of Gkids/Toho’s Godzilla Minus Zero with $2.9M, -66%, after a third Friday of $700K at 3,000 theaters. Running total by Sunday is $54.7M.

FRIDAY AM AFTER THURSDAY EXCLUSIVE: Lionsgate’s Francis Lawrence directed The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping came in with $21M in overall previews since Wednesday. As we told you, that’s the second best for the Suzanne Collins franchise behind Catching Fire‘s $25.2M in 2013 and the second best for 2026 behind Spider-Man: Brand New Day‘s $72M.

Note that all the movies that have posted $20M+ in previews went on to cross $100M+ in their opening weekends. This is a big deal for Lionsgate following its success with Michael, which went on to make $1 billion at the global box office, one of five movies to clear that threshold this year.

Out of the gate Thursday night, Screen Engine and Rentrak’s PostTrak shows 79% “definite recommend” and 5 stars with a 92% positive for general audiences. Women showed up at 67%, with 38% females under 25 and 29% over 25. Biggest definite recommends are from women under 25 (89%), the 13-17 set (92%) and 18-24 demo (82%). The 18-34 showed up at a massive 77%. Close to 70% of Thursday night’s audience bought their tickets within the past week or more than a week ago.

Excitement among fans was apparent a year ago this week, when Lionsgate dropped the first trailer for Sunrise on the Reaping: It went on to clock 109M views in its first 24 hours, becoming the second-most viewed trailer for Lionsgate after Michael‘s 116M global view traffic.

EXCLUSIVE: No Wicked, no Moana, no problem, because Lionsgate is saving the Thanksgiving week box office with The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, which is posting $21.25M in previews, the second-best ever for the franchise behind 2013’s Hunger Games: Catching Fire, which lit $25.2M in its previews. Sunrise‘s money is currently the second-best previews YTD after Sony’s Spider-Man: Brand New Day ($72M, $360M) and ahead of Universal’s The Odyssey ($17.6M previews, $123.5M opening).

Other previews that Sunrise on the Reaping is beating include Universal’s Wicked from two years ($19.2M; $112.5M opening); for the longest time we heard that the latest feature take of a Suzanne Collins novel was outstripping the presales of that female-skewing musical starring Ariana Grande and Cynthia Erivo. It’s also ahead of Lionsgate’s Michael ($12.6M previews, $97.2M opening), The Devil Wears Prada 2 ($10M, $76.7M opening) and light years ahead of the previous Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes ($5.75M previews, $44.6M opening), which had its campaign impacted by the 2023 actors strike, not to mention the Collins novel about the origins of Coriolanus Snow wasn’t a fan fave. Sunrise on the Reaping was beloved by fans in its early story about the Woody Harrelson mentor character, Haymitch Abernathy.

When it comes to the all-time preview record for a Lionsgate title, that belongs to The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 2, which made $30.4M from midnight shows leading to a $141M opening back in mid-November 2012.

Now, Sunrise on the Reaping‘s advance money was minted with $5M via early access PLF showtimes on Wednesday, with another $16.25M collected tonight per sources. Catching Fire‘s back in the day began warming up with 10 p.m. Thursday night shows. Note that Wicked‘s previews were composed of Amazon promotional screenings, Wednesday night PLF shows and an $8M Thursday.

With a great preview start, it’s fair to say that a $100M opening is well in store for the net-$150M priced Sunrise on the Reaping, especially with a 95% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes, which is the best ever in the franchise ahead of Catching Fire (89%), Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes (89%), Hunger Games (81%), Mockingjay Part 2 (71%), and Mockingjay Part 1 (66%).

Overseas, industry projects have risen from a $70M forecast to $86M opening.

Critics aren’t hot on the fifth Hunger Games movie from director Francis Lawrence (the original 2012 movie was helmed by Gary Ross) with an RT score of 68% fresh. However, even if Katniss was shooting Cupid’s arrows at critics, they’d still play hard to get, for the best reviews in the series were a a while ago with Catching Fire (90% certified fresh) and Hunger Games (85% certified fresh). Ever since the Mockingjays (both at 70%), critics’ love for Hunger Games has waned with Songbirds and Snakes being the worst reviewed in franchise at 64%.

The Hunger Games franchise through its five previous movies has minted $3.3 billion at the global box office.
