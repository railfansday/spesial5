Star Trek Officially Brings Back Locutus Of Borg In New Release

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

One of Jean-Luc Picard's most intense and popular story arcs is none other than the iconic Locutus of Borg storyline, which remains one of the most unforgettable moments in Star Trek history. First introduced in The Next Generation's legendary two-part episode "The Best of Both Worlds," Locutus was a terrifying turning point for Picard, who was captured and assimilated by the Borg Collective and then turned into a full-on baddie.

While Picard was eventually rescued and restored to his former self, his experience with the Borg would haunt him for decades, becoming a defining part of his character's legacy. Locutus has since become one of Star Trek's most notorious villains, despite technically being a twisted version of one of its greatest heroes.

Now, more than 35 years after his original debut, Locutus of Borg is officially making another return, bringing Picard's dark chapter back into the spotlight in an exciting new way.

Locutus of Borg is officially back in Magic: The Gathering as part of the massive Star Trek crossover, which launched on November 13, 2026. The iconic villain appears in We Are the Borg, one of four new Commander decks released alongside the larger set, bringing together some of the franchise's most recognizable characters and storylines.

The white, blue, and black Commander deck is led by the Borg Queen, with Locutus serving as an alternative commander. The entire deck revolves around assimilating enemy creatures, building an unstoppable army of Borg, and overwhelming opponents through sheer numbers.

"Locutus of Borg" is a legendary 3/3 artifact creature with lifelink, costing four mana to summon and one that comes with a borderless version as well. His most interesting ability allows every artifact creature under your control to create a 1/1 Borg artifact creature token whenever it deals combat damage to an opposing player.

It's a great interpretation of Picard's villainous alter ego, reflecting the Borg's ability to continuously expand the Collective by assimilating other beings. Essentially, the more Borg you have who land an attack, the more powerful your army becomes. The Borg Queen complements Locutus perfectly, giving artifact creatures +2/+2 while using the new Assimilate mechanic to bring enemy creatures under your control.

There's even a second version of Locutus in the larger MTG release included as part of the Showdown at Sector 001 scene card set, called "Locutus, the One Who Speaks," which gives artifact creatures +1/+1 and can be discarded to damage an enemy creature while restoring life (see above).

For longtime Star Trek fans, it's an exciting addition to the crossover, bringing one of the franchise's best villains back while allowing players to experience the Borg Collective's relentlessness firsthand.
