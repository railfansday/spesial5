Olivier Martinez Spotted Alone on Thanksgiving Amid Custody War With Halle Berry

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Olivier Martinez took a much-needed relaxing stroll on Thanksgiving ... a quick break from the legal battle that has consumed much of his time and energy over the last couple of months.

Olivier seemed engrossed in his phone for part of the outing ... seemingly chatting with someone on the phone.
