Lance Broadway Dies: ‘Agents Of S.H.I.E.L.D.’, ‘Olympus Has Fallen’ Actor Was 43

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Lance Broadway, a former MLB pitcher turned actor who appeared in Marvel’s Agents Of S.H.I.E.L.D., Lucifer and the 2013 film Olympus Has Fallen, among others, has died unexpectedly. He was 43.

Broadway was found dead Tuesday, November 17, in an apartment complex stairwell in Dallas, TX, according to the Dallas County Medical Examiner. The cause of death is pending autopsy results.

Broadway made his feature film debut in 2013’s Olympus Has Fallen, the Gerard Butler action film directed by Antoine Fuqua. He portrayed Secret Service Agent O’Neill under President Benjamin Asher (Aaron Eckhart).

Broadway went on to appear in several TV series, including Saint George, Kevin From Work, Lucifer, Marvel’s Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. and Real Husbands of Hollywood. He appeared most recently in two episodes of USA Network’s Colony in 2016-2017.

In 2014, Broadway was cast as Commander Linden in the science fiction thriller Teleios, which was later renamed Beyond the Trek, and released in 2017 as a TV movie by Screen Media Films.

In 2015, he was cast as Devon in the short film Shattered Reflections.

Broadway’s acting career followed a brief stint in Major League Baseball. As a pitcher for the Chicago White Sox and New York Mets from 2007-09, he compiled a 2-2 record with 5.24 ERA. He was signed by the Toronto Blue Jays as a free agent in 2009 but did not appear in a game for the team.
