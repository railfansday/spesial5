Netflix's Best Spy Thriller Officially Returns to Netflix After a 2-Year Wait

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

There are plenty of spy thrillers on television, but very few manage to combine international conspiracies, brutal assassinations, and the lives of those involved quite as neatly as you'd hope. When Keira Knightley made her Netflix espionage debut, audiences were introduced to a world where absolutely nobody could be trusted, secrets were currency, and even the most ordinary-looking people could be hiding something spectacularly dangerous. So it's a good thing that we get to do it all over again, right?

Knightley's acclaimed spy thriller Black Doves officially returned to Netflix on November 5, 2026, with its long-awaited second season. It follows Helen Webb, a quick-witted, down-to-earth, dedicated wife and mother — and professional spy. For 10 years, she’s been passing on her politician husband’s secrets to the shadowy organization she works for: the Black Doves. When her secret lover, Jason, is assassinated, her spymaster, the enigmatic Reed, calls in Helen’s old friend to keep her safe.

In Season 2, Helen is still betraying her nation’s secrets to the covert organization she serves, the Black Doves. But after the misadventures of last Christmas, and with her husband, Wallace, preparing to become prime minister, she’s walking a more treacherous line than ever. As Helen’s handler, Mrs. Reed, is ensnared in a ruthless plot to undermine her position in the Black Doves, Helen is reunited with her best friend, Sam. The once high-end triggerman is now reduced to lonely drinks in Soho bars and low-rate hits. As they search for answers, loyalties are weaponized, trust is shattered, and the fight to protect the people they love could cost everything. As ever, with Netflix's most acclaimed shows, this is one that will have you sitting down for one episode and finding it impossible to stop there.

The cast features Keira Knightley (Pirates of the Caribbean) as Helen Webb, Ben Whishaw (Paddington) as Sam Young, Sarah Lancashire (Happy Valley) as Reed, Andrew Buchan (Broadchurch) as Wallace Webb, Andrew Koji (Warrior) as Jason Davies, Ella Lily Hyland (Fifteen-Love) as Williams, Gabrielle Creevy (In My Skin) as Eleanor, and Luther Ford (The Crown) as Hector Newman. The second season also introduces several major new faces, including Ambika Mod (One Day) and Neve Campbell (Scream).
