Pokémon Champions Is Adding Four More Mega Evolutions In A New Update Next Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

24 newly added Pokémon will also be available for battle

Following the news of a Pokémon Champions balance patch update, The Pokémon Company has now revealed some new competitors are preparing to arrive in the free-to-play battle title.

On 2nd December 2026, Regulation Set M-D will see the arrival of Mega Heatran and Mega Tatsugiri. All up, there will be 24 newly added Pokémon available for battle and four more Mega Evolutions.

Here's the full announcement from the official Pokémon website:

Some new competitors are preparing for battle! Regulation Set M-D will run in Pokémon Champions from Wednesday, December 2, 2026, at 6:00 p.m. PST to Wednesday, March 3, 2027, at 5:59 p.m. PST. Pokémon that were eligible for Ranked Battles in previous regulation sets remain eligible, but they’ll be joined by Heatran, Dondozo, Tatsugiri, and more!

Even more Mega Evolutions are gearing up for a fight under Regulation Set M-D, including Mega Heatran and Mega Tatsugiri. Following the update, there will be a total of 24 newly added Pokémon available for battle, along with four Mega Evolutions.

You can see the full roster in your game by following these steps:

- Go to Recruit. - Select Recruit Pokémon. - Select Roster Info.

Best of luck with your battles in Pokémon Champions, Trainers!

Here's some more information about what you can expect from Mega Heatran and Mega Tatsugiri (via Serebii.net):

- Mega Heatran has Water Compaction, which boosts its Defense by 2 Stages when hit by a Water-type Move - Mega Tatsugiri has a new Ability which increases its Special Attack by 1 stage when it enters battle

Again, this will arrive alongside a balance update on the same date. You can find out more about this in last week's post.
