Ravenswatch – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition launches December 10 worldwide, December 18 in North America

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Cooperative roguelike action game Ravenswatch – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition will launch on December 18 in North America and December 10 in the rest of the world, publisher Nexon and developer Passtech Games announced. It will be a free upgrade from the Switch version.

Here is an overview of the Nintendo Switch 2 Edition, via Nacon:

Compared to the Nintendo Switch version, Ravenswatch – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition offers significantly better performance, including improved textures, Full HD display, and a stable 60 fps.

For owners of the original Nintendo Switch version of the game, the upgrade to the Nintendo Switch 2 Edition is free. The physical edition of Ravenswatch – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition will include the game cartridge, all content updates released since launch, and nine downloadable contents, including three additional heroes (Romeo, Juliet, and Merlin).

Ravenswatch is a top-down roguelike action game that combines intense real-time combat with deep gameplay and high replay value. After the success of Curse of the Dead Gods, Passtech Games returns with a new formula and game experience.

No two runs are alike. With 13 heroes, over 250 talents and 50 magical items offering endless build combinations, four difficulty levels, custom game modes and online cross-play cooperative support up to four players, the game offers an addictive experience and high replay value.

Ravenswatch is available now for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, Switch, and PC via Steam.
