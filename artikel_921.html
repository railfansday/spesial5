Blackburn Rovers head coach Tony Mowbray steps down due to health issues

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Mowbray confirms he has had to ‘step back from role’

Rovers are currently 13th in the Championship table

Tony Mowbray has stepped back from his role as the Blackburn Rovers head coach for health reasons.

The 62-year-old rejoined Rovers in June and has guided a club threatened with relegation last season to 13th in the Championship, three points off the playoff places. However, he has been forced to step away from the game for a second time owing to a health issue.

Mowbray temporarily left his job as Birmingham City’s manager in February 2024 for medical treatment. He resigned three months later for health reasons and later confirmed he had been diagnosed with bowel cancer. Blackburn have not disclosed specifics in relation to Tuesday’s announcement.

“Unfortunately, due to health issues, I have to step back from my role as head coach of Blackburn Rovers,” said Mowbray in a statement issued by the club. “I’m immensely proud of this group of players. Their dedication and professionalism have given us some memorable performances and wins, and I’ve loved working with them.

Mowbray’s assistants, Damien Johnson and Mike Dodds, will take over first-team duties for the time being, Blackburn said. Their first game will be at local rivals Burnley on Sunday.

Blackburn’s owner, Balaji Rao, said: “Our thoughts are with Tony and his family as he steps back from his duties to focus on his health. Through our many meetings over the years, we’ve come to know Tony well and grown very fond of him.

“We’ve always admired his professionalism, his dedication and the passion he brings to his work at Blackburn Rovers. Tony, please know that you have our unconditional and unwavering support. We send you and your family our love and strength, and we will do all we can to support you through this time.”
