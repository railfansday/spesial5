League Two player Isiah Noel-Williams has been charged with three counts of rape.

Noel-Williams, who plays for Barnet, was remanded in custody as part of a police investigation by Hertfordshire Constabulary’s Public Protection Command, which has now confirmed charges against the footballer.

The 23-year-old from St Albans has also been charged with assault by penetration, engaging in sexual communication with a child, and supplying a controlled drug (Class B).

Noel-Williams appeared at Hatfield Magistrates’ Court on Saturday 21 November where he was taken into custody.

He will next appear at St Albans Crown Court on Tuesday 22 December.

Barnet put out a statement before the charges were made public, writing on their website: “The club is co-operating fully with the authorities and will determine any further action in due course.

“As this is now a matter for the courts, the club will not be making any further comment.”

This season was Noel-Williams’ first in the Football League, joining Barnet from non-league side Hemel Hempstead in the summer.

The striker has made nine appearances across all competitions and is yet to register a goal contribution.

He has previously played for a number of non-league clubs including Kettering Town, Matlock Town and Harrow Borough.

Noel-Williams was sent off in a 1-1 draw with Oldham in October, with manager Dean Brennan branding the red card a “farce”, and he subsequently received racist abuse which Barnet condemned “in the strongest terms”.

Barnet are vying for promotion this term and sit second in League Two, trailing leaders Salford City by four points.
