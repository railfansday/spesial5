Rick Stockstill earns raise as FSU football interim head coach

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Rick Stockstill received a one-time $150,000 bonus for taking over the interim head coach role for Florida State football.

In a letter by then-interim athletic director Bruce Warwick, obtained by the Tallahassee Democrat through a public records request, Stockstill signed off on the bonus on Nov. 1, the same day that the Seminoles elected to fire head coach Mike Norvell.

Stockstill had previously served as the program's director of scouting, and he earned $135,000 annually in that role, according to the contract he signed in January 2024, also obtained by the Tallahassee Democrat via a public records request.

He has guided the Seminoles to the program's first road wins since 2023, beating both Boston College and Pitt, before experiencing his first loss as head coach in a 28-10 loss to NC State.

Stockstill will continue to serve in an interim capacity until the end of the season. The Seminoles hired Georgia defensive coordinator Glenn Schumann to serve as the program's next head coach, and he will come to Tallahassee once the Bulldogs' season ends.
