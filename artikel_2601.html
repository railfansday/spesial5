TCU quarterback out for Texas Tech football's Thanksgiving game

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

An earlier game means an earlier first edition of the player availability report for Thursday's showdown between the Texas Tech football team and TCU.

Notable for Texas Tech is outside linebacker LaDamion Guyton being listed as doubtful as of Monday night. Joey McGuire said earlier in the day Guyton would likely be a game-time decision and would have a better shot of playing on his injured ankle if the game were on Saturday as normal.

TCU's side of the report is most noteworthy as the Horned Frogs have already ruled out quarterback Jaden Craig, who missed last week's loss to Utah. That gives the ball to Adam Schobel, who manned the TCU attack against the Utes.

OUT: RB Quinten Joyner, WR Jalen Jones, S Miquel Dingle Jr.

QUESTIONABLE: WR Micah Hudson, WR Kenny Johnson, S Marcus Ramon-Edwards, OL Cash Cleveland, DL Bryce Butler

PROBABLE: WR Coy Eakin, S Peyton Morgan, CB Brice Pollock
