Browns designate RB Raheim Sanders for return

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Running back Raheim Sanders will be back at practice for the Browns this week.

The team announced on Tuesday that Sanders has been designated for return from injured reserve. He has been out since injuring his hip in the team's loss to the Jets in Week 5.

The Browns could activate Sanders in time to play against the Raider on Sunday night, but they have a 21-day window to make that move should they opt not to do it immediately.

Sanders ran nine times for 43 yards and caught 11 passes for 74 yards before his injury. He also averaged 29.3 yards on 10 kickoff returns.

Quinshon Judkins is the lead back for the Browns. Dylan Sampson and Jaleel McLaughlin are the other running backs on the team's active roster.
