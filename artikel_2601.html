Steelers WR DK Metcalf avoids accountability after ejection

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Pittsburgh Steelers wide receiver DK Metcalf was ejected from Sunday's loss to the Philadelphia Eagles after a pair of unsportsmanlike penalties, the latter of which saw him reach into the facemask of Eagles defensive back Cooper DeJean for what appeared to be a poke in the eye.

Metcalf left the stadium before the media had a chance to speak with him, but addressed the media on Monday back in Pittsburgh, where he denied poking DeJean in the eye.

"I didn't poke him in the eye," Metcalf said via Nick Farabaugh. "I was telling him to stop pushing me."

When asked why he has struggled to maintain composure, Metcalf had an opportunity to take accountability for his actions. While it may not take back what he did or necessarily help him when it comes to his punishment, it would at least show some level of leadership on his part. Instead, he blamed those who pushed him to the point of getting ejected,

"I think you got to ask the people that seemingly do that to me," Metcalf said after being asked by Will Graves.

Whether Metcalf will be suspended is yet to be known, but this has the potential to end very poorly for the Ole Miss product, who seemingly couldn't care less what happens next.
