ROUNDUP: With 40-2 record, Pomeroys steam into NAIA volleyball tourney

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Saint Mary-of-the-Woods College will make its first NAIA women's volleyball national tournament appearance Saturday when the Pomeroys travel to Cornerstone University for an opening-round match at Mol Arena in Grand Rapids, Mich.

The Pomeroys enter the national tournament with a 40-2 record, extending a season that has produced a school record for victories, the program's first River States Conference regular-season championship and its first NAIA tournament berth.

SMWC completed conference play undefeated and hosted the RSC tournament semifinals and championship match for the first time. In a rare twist, The Woods got nipped 3-2 by IU-Kokomo in the title game Saturday.

Woods coach Ashley Harris earned her first RSC Coach of the Year award, while junior libero Gaby Fernandez was named the conference's Defender of the Year. Fernandez also received five RSC Defender of the Week awards and one NAIA National Defender of the Week honor this season.

SMWC led the conference with eight All-RSC selections, including four first-team honorees. Fernandez, Karla Melendez Martinez, Masa Pesic and Sofia Soto earned first-team recognition. Ariana Cruz-Gonzalez, Luci Morris and Lily Richards were named to the second team, while Leishmarie Virella received third-team honors. Raimey Owens represented the Pomeroys on the RSC Champions of Character Team.

The Pomeroys opened the season with 24 consecutive victories and swept both regular-season meetings with IU Kokomo, including a straight-set home victory over the then-No. 22 Cougars on Sept. 15. SMWC later earned a four-set road victory at IU Kokomo on Oct. 27.

SMWC advanced through the conference tournament with straight-set victories over Brescia and IU Columbus before falling to No. 23 IU Kokomo in five sets in Saturday's championship match. The Pomeroys rallied from a two-set deficit before dropping the final, 25-18, 25-13, 25-27, 20-25, 15-5.

Melendez Martinez finished the championship match with 22 kills and 19 digs, while Fernandez collected 28 digs and nine assists. Virella added 19 digs, Cruz-Gonzalez recorded 25 assists and 11 digs, and Richards contributed 19 assists and 13 digs.

• RSC honors South grad Baker — Saint Mary-of-the-Woods College redshirt sophomore Zayvion Baker has been named the River States Conference Men's Basketball Defensive Player of the Week after recording six steals, five defensive rebounds and one blocked shot in two road games.

The Terre Haute South High School grad collected three steals and one defensive rebound in an 82-69 loss at Bethel (Ind.) on Nov. 10. He added five points in 30 minutes.

Baker followed with three steals, four defensive rebounds and a blocked shot in the Pomeroys' 80-71 victory at Mount Vernon Nazarene (Ohio) on Friday. The 5-foot-11 guard also scored nine points on three 3-pointers and contributed three assists without a turnover.

Baker averaged seven points, three steals and 2.5 defensive rebounds across the two games. He started both contests and averaged 25.5 minutes.

SMWC took a 4-4 record into Tuesday's 5 p.m. home game vs. IU-East, the Pomeroys' RSC opener.

• MVFC cites Wuest's special-teams play — Indiana State wide receiver B.J. Wuest was named the Missouri Valley Football Special Teams Player of the Week on Monday.

The Danville, Ind., native turned in one of the longest plays in Indiana State history Saturday afternoon as the wide receiver lined up in the back of the end zone on Murray State's 53-yard field-goal attempt on the final play of the first half.

The attempt bounced short of the end zone and into Wuest's hands just in front of the back end-zone line. Wuest weaved his way through the Murray State field-goal unit and returned it down the left sideline for the 100-yard touchdown return to cut the score down to 21-14. It marked Wuest's second career special-teams touchdown after blocking a punt and returning it for a score last year at North Dakota.

ISU (2-9 overall, 0-7 MVFC) finishes its season with a 3 p.m. game at first-place South Dakota State (9-2, 6-1) in Dana J. Dykhouse Stadium at Brookings.

• Pomeroys ride well — At New Castle, Saint Mary-of-the-Woods' Western equestrian team earned two high-point team titles and a reserve high-point team finish during three shows Nov. 14-15 at the Henry County Fairgrounds.

The Pomeroys opened the weekend with reserve high-point team honors in the first Purdue-hosted show before claiming high-point team honors in the second. SMWC added another high-point team title in the University of Illinois-hosted show.

Claire Coryell led the Pomeroys individually, earning one high point rider award and two reserve high point rider awards across the three shows. Coryell also qualified for postseason competition in reining alongside teammate Isabella Monnett.

Three additional Pomeroys secured postseason berths. Clair Schoettmer qualified in open horsemanship, Courtney Hamrick in ranch riding and Bella Woodard in rookie horsemanship.

• ISU signs prep standouts — Coach Windy Thees' Indiana State program signed three more high school recruits this week, including Amissville, Va., pitcher Brooke Farmer; South Lake, Texas, outfielder Cate Stoklas; and catcher-utility player Emelia Rousse of West Bloomfield Hills, Mich.

Those signings follow that of Terre Haute North standout Kenzie Zigler.

Farmer, a standout pitcher and two-way player Fauquier High School, earned multiple All-District, All-Region, and All-State honors. As a junior, she recorded 236 strikeouts across 131 innings while posting a 1.443 earned-run average and holding opposing hitters to a .148 batting average. Offensively, she batted .389 with three home runs, 21 RBIs, and three stolen bases.

Stoklas hails from Carroll Senior High School, where she posted a .308 career batting average. Rousse starred at Detroit Country Day Upper School, posting a .555 batting average and a .622 on-base percentage while serving as a key contributor behind the plate and across the field.
