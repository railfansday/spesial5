Amanda Seyfried’s Divorce Decadence

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Marital separation isn’t ‘just another life transition.’

Amanda Seyfried and then-husband Thomas Sadoski attend the TIME100 Gala at Jazz at Lincoln Center in New York City June 08, 2022.

Sean Zanni/Patrick McMullan via Getty Images

Amanda Seyfried and her husband Thomas Sadoski — who is also the father of her children — described their recent separation this way: “We’ve got each other forever . . . and it’s GOOD.” On Instagram, Seyfried shared a quote by divorce influencer Olivia Howell, who goes even further, arguing that divorce should be “boring,” “normal,” and reframed as “just another life transition.”

No. Divorce isn’t “GOOD.” It’s tragic. While divorce may sometimes be inevitable, it should be the exception, not the norm.

Hadley Heath Manning is a senior fellow at Independent Women’s Forum (www.iwf.org).
