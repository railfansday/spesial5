Shiba Inu (SHIB) Prints 312 Billion Outflow For the First Time Since Price Drop

For the first time since the major drop, Shiba Inu has finally printed a substantial exchange outflow. According to data from CryptoQuant, more than 300 billion SHIB has been withdrawn from exchanges. It's a drop in the ocean, though: 312 billion SHIB is only around 0.36% of the 86 trillion tokens held on exchanges.

Shiba Inu has been struggling substantially on the market, invalidating one price level after another. The only positive development here is the descending trading volume, which reflects the possibility of a price recovery.

The outflow is most likely fueled by retail investors, as Shiba Inu's liquidity is thin compared with institutional appetite. Most of the holders are regular retail investors. Investors aren't buying en masse, but there is some accumulation, which could be tied to automated market making.

SHIB is currently trading at $0.00000386, which is around 13% lower than the $0.00000444 it held on November 11. Shiba Inu mostly followed the general uptrend but tumbled quickly due to the broader reversal of the cryptocurrency market. The ascending trendline that held the price since July was broken in mid-October, and SHIB is now below all major moving averages.

The 200 EMA acted as strong resistance that SHIB was unable to break. The downtrend is way stronger than the bullish reversal attempts SHIB had in October.

The $0.00000413 level, which was the point of reversal back in July, is already lost, with the price dipping to around $0.0000037 during the drop.

Divergence of moving averages could be hinting at a lack of momentum change, suggesting increasing conviction in the current market. The trend is unlikely to flip here, unfortunately.

With the RSI at the bottom, the next step should be either a sideways trend or a gradual recovery. The market is currently moving purely on speculative accumulation that was triggered by the massive price drop across multiple assets.

The market is weak and might get weaker, but the most selling pressure SHIB could face has already been injected.
