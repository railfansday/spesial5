Grand Theft Auto VI Made $3.79 Billion In One Day. Taylor Swift’s Entire Eras Tour Made About Half That.

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

You're viewing the US website. Looking for the Site edition?

Rockstar is calling its new release the biggest entertainment launch in history — and the numbers are highway robbery.

Taylor Swift spent 21 months on the road to gross roughly $2 billion with the Eras Tour, a music industry record. Then Grand Theft Auto VI rolled into town and nearly doubled that in a single day, PEOPLE reports.

Rockstar Games and parent company Take-Two Interactive say GTA VI sales reached $3.79 billion worldwide on its release date. That’s based on company estimates. Rockstar calls it “the biggest entertainment launch in history” in a press release. The game also sold a record 32.5 million units before it even came out. (It costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition.)

For context, the previous installment, GTA V, debuted in 2013 and made $815.7 million in its first 24 hours. That mark previously held the Guinness World Record for the most a video game has earned in a day.

GTA VI has a long way to travel to catch its predecessor, though. Take-Two says GTA V has sold nearly 235 million units.
