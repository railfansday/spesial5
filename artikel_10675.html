Arrows are some of the most useful rewards you can get in Stand Out because they’re used to roll for different Stands, which define the abilities your character has. Mysterious Arrows, Lucky Arrows, and Bizarre Arrows give you different chances of rolling a Stand, so extra ones are always worth getting if you want to change your abilities or find something stronger.

Dalas is the game’s currency, so you can use it to buy items and boosts (including fast travel through Huber), as well as Arrow Parts, which are another resource to keep an eye on as you build your character. Rewards like these will save you some grinding early on and give you more options as you explore the world and fight other players.
