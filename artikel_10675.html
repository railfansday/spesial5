Viral browser game lets you give horses nose rubs and ear scratches

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

A free web browser game that lets you pet horses is going viral, and it’s perfect for Umamusume fans and animal lovers who want to de-stress.

Following on from the viral Cat Petting Simulator that lets you stroke virtual felines from the comfort of your mobile or desktop, Japanese developer zubu has created another contender: Pet The Horse.

As the name suggests, the browser game tasks you with stroking a horse’s head while it releases soft huffing sound effects. It’s pretty simple stuff, but it could be your new obsession to chill out with, particularly if you’ve just lost a race in Umamusume or been busy heisting your way through GTA 6.

Just like Cat Petting Simulator, you can pet up to six different colored horses using either your mouse or smartphone’s touchscreen. You can pet your chosen horse’s face, including their chin, mouth, mane, and can even scratch their ears for the real good stuff.

The virtual horse will also respond to your petting, occasionally letting out cute sound effects. There’s even a night and day feature that you can change, with the night version dimming the screen and moving your virtual companion to a stable.

As of writing, the developer’s announcement on X has already received over 40k likes, and fans have praised the new browser game for its wholesome nature.

“Being able to pet a horse in a stable at night is quietly the best thing ever, I think,” wrote one fan. “There’s something nice about a game where you just pet an animal and relax,” added another.

Well, it’s certainly more relaxing than this viral cat brushing browser game, which has you competing for the highest score before an orange cat bites you. Then there’s this free pigeon GTA game that tasks you with terrorizing Toronto on a massive map bigger than GTA 5.
