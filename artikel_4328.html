Neil Druckmann Says Intergalactic is ‘Playable’, But Don’t Expect More This Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Covering the video games industry since 2017, with experience in news, articles, and reviews. Currently a writer at DualShockers and gaming magazines.

Naughty Dog has been keeping Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet under wraps for quite some time, and fans hoping for a surprise gameplay reveal before the end of the year might want to temper their expectations (and yes, that’s also a self-note).

Its creative director Neil Druckmann, best known for creating The Last of Us, has finally offered an update on the studio's upcoming sci-fi adventure, confirming that the game is now playable and entering its final stages of production. But sadly, that doesn't mean we're getting a closer look at it anytime soon.

During a recent appearance at Comic Creators Block Party 2026, Druckmann spoke with host Patton Oswalt about the project, which was first announced at The Game Awards in December 2024. While he was careful not to reveal too much, he did share that the development team has reached an important milestone.

“We’re kind of at the last stage of production, where we can play it, and we’re refining and polishing it, and I can’t wait to show it to the world,” Druckmann explained.

MGS, Uncharted, Halo, and Assassin's Creed veteran, Robin Atkins Downes, is joining the cast of Intergalactic, Naughty Dog's new title.

That's certainly encouraging news, especially considering how little we've seen of Intergalactic since its initial reveal. However, players shouldn't mistake this update for a sign that Naughty Dog is preparing to release the game in the near future.

Druckmann actually reiterated something that Naughty Dog already confirmed back in September this year: they will remain quiet about Intergalactic for the rest of 2026, with a full reveal planned for sometime next year. According to the creative director, the team will show “a lot” of the game in 2027, including gameplay and more of its story. He also teased that the final experience might surprise people, particularly those who think they already have a good idea of what Intergalactic will be.

Considering that the first trailer mostly introduced protagonist Jordan A. Mun and the game's mysterious sci-fi setting, there's still plenty Naughty Dog hasn't shown us! And, honestly, after waiting this long, a proper gameplay showcase sounds like the least we can ask for.

Neil Druckmann announced that this isn't the last we've seen of The Last of Us, with two new projects already in development.

Besides, Druckmann also offered a little more insight into the creative direction behind Intergalactic, describing it as a combination of three of Naughty Dog's biggest franchises. He explained that the studio wanted to return to sci-fi and fantasy after spending so much time working on the relatively grounded worlds of Uncharted and The Last of Us.

For that, the team is drawing inspiration from the sci-fi and fantasy elements of Jak and Daxter, the pulpy action-adventure style of Uncharted that we all miss deeply, and the more serious drama found in The Last of Us. He also highlighted the studio's ambition to expand its gameplay systems, asking whether Naughty Dog could maintain the cinematic storytelling it's known for while offering deeper gameplay mechanics.

That's an interesting combination for sure, especially for a studio that has spent much of the last decade focusing on cinematic adventures. So we still have no release date for Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet, but at least 2027 is shaping up to be the year we'll finally understand what the studio has been working on.
