Kirk Cousins delivers strong first outing as Vikings sack Packers' Jordan Love 8 times

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Kirk Cousins delivered in his first start for the Vikings in more than three years.

Cousins, who was traded to Minnesota on Tuesday, delivered a strong performance in a dominant 30-10 Vikings win over the surging Green Bay Packers at Lambeau Field.

Cousins completed 21-of-31 passes for 207 yards and two TDs. He was intercepted once, but the Packers couldn't take advantage of it. Justin Jefferson finished with a game-high nine catches for 77 yards on 13 targets.

Minnesota's defense also made its impact felt, as usual, sacking Jordan Love eight times — the most by one team in the NFL this season. The Packers, who were plus-8 on turnovers this season, were minus-2 in Sunday's loss, with three giveaways. Love rallied in the second half, but it wasn't enough.

The Vikings dominated the first half, taking a 17-0 lead and sacking Love five times. Rookie tight end Mark Redman fumbled on the Packers' second offensive play of the game, leading to a Vikings field goal.

A missed field goal by Packers rookie kicker Trey Smack — his sixth FG miss of the season — also was costly. On the next possession, Cousins hit Myles Price for a 38-yard TD on third-and-23. Price, playing extended snaps in place of Jordan Addison and Jauan Jennings, made the clutch grab, shook his defender and waltzed into the end zone for Cousins' first TD pass since being traded back to Minnesota.

Love drove the Packers into the red zone. But that's when Vikings safety Harrison Smith corralled his 41st career interception, picking off Love in the end zone.

The Packers appeared to pick off Cousins in the second quarter, but he was fortuitously ruled down for a sack. The Vikings then converted a third-and-15 play on the next snap, with Cousins finding Tai Felton on a gorgeous throw, followed by Aaron Jones' 1-yard TD to make it 17-0.

Minnesota kept pressing after halftime. Packers center John Williams helped thwart another promising Green Bay drive by snapping the ball over Love's head, leading to another turnover. The Vikings settled for another field goal and a 20-0 lead.

The Packers tried to fight. Love converted a fourth-and-11 throw to Matthew Golden, and then found J. Michael Sturdivant for the Packers' first score of the game. They forced another Vikings punt and cut the lead to 20-10.

But the Vikings put their feet down. They drove 11 plays and 80 yards, draining nearly seven minutes of clock, and took a 27-10 lead when Cousins found a wide-open Gavin Bartholomew for his first NFL touchdown.

Sturdivant then fumbled the ensuing kickoff, which was recovered by the Vikings, essentially ending any hope for Green Bay. Will Reichard's third field goal of the game pushed the lead back to 20 points and put the Packers down for good.

Matt LaFleur used one of the team's timeouts after the Vikings' third-down carry fell three yards short of the first down line.

Kevin O'Connell opted to go for the win on fourth down and Jordan Mason picked up five yards with 28 seconds left.

Even if Green Bay used its final timeout, Minnesota would have needed to just kneel it once and the game would have been over.

Kirk Cousins is victorious in his return with the Vikings and finished with 207 yards, 2 touchdowns and 1 interception.

After the turnover, the Vikings offense took time off the clock. While Minnesota didn't score a TD, Will Reichard kicked a field goal with just under four minutes left to put the Vikings up 30-10.
