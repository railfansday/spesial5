Maxx Crosby racks up tackles for loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Like with Las Vegas Raiders' superstar tight end Brock Bowers, we love to use this space to appreciate the team's defensive wonder man, defensive end Maxx Crosby.

One of the great things about Crosby is that he is a complete player and that shows with his prowess for tackles for loss He has made, at least, one tackle for loss in five straight games. It's tied for the second lowest active streak in the league. He has four Sunday in the loss to Seattle.

According to the Raiders' public relations department, Crosby has 142 tackles for loss since he entered the NFL as a fourth-round draft pick of the Raiders in 2019. That leads the NFL in that timespan.
