49ers get bad news on Colton McKivitz’s brutal injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Anyone who watched offensive lineman Colton McKivitz suffer a brutal injury in the second quarter of the San Francisco 49ers' game against the Minnesota Vikings on Sunday, then leave the field on a stretcher, knew immediately his injury was cause for serious concern.

Not surprisingly, head coach Kyle Shanahan told reporters in Mexico City after the 49ers' 35-10 win that McKivitz's season is over.

Shanahan said the 30-year-old lineman suffered a fractured and dislocated left ankle, similar to what Fred Warner had last year.

The injury is a brutal blow for San Francisco, which had started the same five offensive linemen every game this season. Vederian Lowe, who has played only 36 offensive snaps this season, replaced McKivitz at right tackle after the season.

Entering Week 11, McKivitz was having a phenomenal season with Pro Football Focus grades of 82.8 overall, 71.0 in pass-blocking and 88.8 in run-blocking, ranking seventh, 37th and second among tackles in the NFL.

The 49ers are 9-1 after beating the Vikings.

McKivitz was caught in an awkward position blocking for teammate Christian McCaffrey, who ran for a short gain. The immediate reactions from his teammates on the field — many looked away immediately, some slammed their helmets — told the story.

Listed at 6-foot-6, 301 pounds, McKivitz has spent his entre career in San Francisco. The 30-year-old is regarded as an excellent run blocker, whose skills helped McCaffrey earn two Pro Bowl nods after his first three seasons in San Francisco.

The former West Virginia University standout was a fifth-round draft pick by the 49ers in 2020, and has spent his entire NFL career in San Francisco.
