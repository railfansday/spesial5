Lakers rookie discovers surprising NBA perks he never dreamed of

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

There are several commonly known perks of being an NBA player.

Chartered flights. State-of-the-art facilities. And a lot of free, high-quality food are among them.

Cameron Carr #43 of the Los Angeles Lakers smiles during a game against the Utah Jazz. NBAE via Getty Images

But there are several that aren't as well known that an athlete doesn't become aware of until they're in the NBA.

Consider Cameron Carr among the players learning about the benefits of his profession.

The Lakers rookie wing was posed with the question of unexpected benefits of being an NBA player that he didn't anticipate after Monday's shootaround ahead of his team's home game against the Jazz at Crypto.com Arena.

Cameron Carr of the Los Angeles Lakers drives to the basket for a layup. NBAE via Getty Images

"I'll probably say NBA League Pass," Carr responded. "Got NBA League Pass for free. Never had NBA League Pass."

The former Baylor Bear couldn't contain his excitement over getting the NBA's product for free that can cost $159.99/year or $24.99/month.

Now, he can watch any NBA game at any time.

"So s— great," Carr said before covering his mouth in a slight panic for cursing. "It's great, by the way."

Cameron Carr of the Los Angeles Lakers speaks to the media after a game. NBAE via Getty Images

Sandro Mamukelashvili, a six-year veteran, understood Carr's excitement.

"Yeah, it's amazing," Mamukelashvili said. "Watch every game [for] free. Give it to your parents. I definitely enjoy my free league pass."

The second perk appealed to Carr's appreciation of pregame naps.

"They do like the Sleep Number stuff for the rookies," he said. "They do like a discount code, so that's great."

He added: "I'm a big sleep guy. Like as soon as I get done with this, I'm gonna shower, go home, and take a nap."
