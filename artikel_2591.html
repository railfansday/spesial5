Officials deny Jaguars TD on sensational catch by Parker Washington

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Jaguars wide receiver Parker Washington made a leaping grab in the back of the end zone, got his feet down in bounds, and controlled the ball as he fell to the ground in the fourth quarter today against the Giants.

The officials ruled it incomplete, and after the Jaguars challenged, the replay review ruled that Washington did not make the catch. The ruling of incomplete pass stood, and the Jaguars settled for a field goal that cut the Giants’ lead to 20-9.

It was a highly questionable call, questioned even by former NFL referee Gene Steratore, the CBS rules analyst who said on the game broadcast that he thought it was a catch.

Washington is having a sensational season, and that was perhaps his greatest play yet — or at least it would have been, if it had been ruled a catch.

The Giants previously got a touchdown on a Jameis Winston quarterback sneak, even though replays appeared to show Winston’s helmet hit the ground before the ball crossed the goal line. Now there have been two controversial touchdown calls, both in the Giants’ favor.
