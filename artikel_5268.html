Spend management platform Payhawk announces $280M raise, 2B valuation

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

U.K.-based fintech Payhawk announced a $280 million Series C funding round Wednesday, led by Goldman Sachs’ growth equity arm, making it one of Europe’s largest Series C rounds this year, as noted by Sifted. The round values the company at $2 billion, bringing its total funding to more than $500 million to date.

Founded in 2018, Payhawk is an enterprise financial management platform that helps companies track and manage expenses and procurement using AI agents. Speaking to FinTech magazine, Payhawk’s co-founder and CEO, Hristo Borisov, said the company saw “an incredible growth opportunity and room to bring finance teams AI-native products that combine agents, payment infrastructure, and enterprise controls.”

Payhawk competes with spend management companies Pleo, Moss, and Spendesk, and says it operates in 33 countries. Notably, the company said it still has 70% of the $100 million it raised in 2022, a round that valued the company at $1 billion. This fresh capital will support its global expansion. Other investors in the round include Greenoaks, Lightspeed, and QED.
