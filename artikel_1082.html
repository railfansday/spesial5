There's nothing else on the line for either team. Both are safe from relegation to the third tier, and both have the back-up of a promotion play-off against a League A team in late March.

O'Neill will be eager to avoid that eventuality and has seen little from his team to suggest they're likely to stumble at this final hurdle in Budapest. Northern Ireland are the only side anywhere in the Nations League yet to concede a goal.

Their remarkable run has seen them beat Ukraine twice and Georgia, with 21-year-old loanee Queens Park Rangers goalkeeper Pierce Charles attracting endless plaudits for going 450 minutes without conceding.

Hungary have averaged a goal per game in Group B2, but they left it late against Georgia on Saturday, eventually running out 2-0 winners in Tbilisi. Having failed to qualify for a tenth consecutive World Cup, they could do with a positive conclusion to the Nations League campaign to see out the year.

Manager Marco Rossi has been in charge for eight years and has bolstered his position in the Nations League, winning consecutive promotions from League C to League A in the first two tournaments and then staying at the top level in the third.

His target is an immediate return to League A.

Northern Ireland have mastered the defensive brief in their Nations League group and another nil is all they need.
