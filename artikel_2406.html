UEFA Women's Champions League: Bayern sit third as Arsenal end Barcelona's perfect record at Camp Nou

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

FC Bayern sit in third place after the first four matchdays in the UEFA Women's Champions League. The sole Frauen-Bundesliga representatives drew both of their home games against Manchester City and Real Madrid, but they beat Benfica and PSG away from home.

Jose Barcala’s side will fancy their chances of winning all the away games in the league phase as they travel to Vienna for the game against Austria Vienna after Saturday's big clash against VfL Wolfsburg. Austria Vienna sit bottom of the standings in the Champions League with only a point from four games.

Meanwhile, there is a big probability Bayern will have to wait for the knockout stages for their first home win as they host FC Barcelona in the last matchday. The Spanish giants beat Bayern 7-1 in the same stage on their way to lifting the trophy last time out.

However, the 2024/25 champions Arsenal once again upset Barcelona on Thursday as they came away with a point from their trip to Camp Nou (1-1). The result meant there is no team with a perfect record after four rounds of games as Lyon and Barcelona separated on goal difference at the top of the standings.

Bayern sit in third place thanks to their better goal difference to WSL sides Arsenal and Chelsea, who could have both leapfrogged the Frauen-Bundesliga champions had they won their respective games on matchday 4. The top four sides will automatically qualify for the quarter-final stage, while the next eight teams enter a playoff round. The bottom six sides will get eliminated from UEFA's premiere competition.
