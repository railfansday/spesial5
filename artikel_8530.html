Personal Investments • Re: Borrow on margin to invest in this dip?

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

My former officemate took the max 401k loan and used it to invest in stocks, with leverage as well, in March 2020 after the first day or two of huge losses.

Six months later he'd have been wealthy except the margin got called when the market plunged further, the stocks were sold at the bottom, and then he couldn't pay back his 401k loan because his whole life is stories like this.

Don't be foolish.
I really appreciate this example because it shows that even if you're right, if the specifics are a little off you can still get wrecked. Reminds me of "the market can stay irrational longer than you can stay solvent".
