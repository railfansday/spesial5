Use an Oportun personal loan only if you need a small loan now and have no U.S. credit history or a low score, since Oportun doesn’t require a credit file and reports your payments to the credit bureaus. Expect an APR near its 36% cap, so try a credit union first.

Oportun lends $300 to $10,000 without collateral to borrowers with a thin credit history or none, and up to $18,500 in eight states to those who pledge a car they own outright. It bought the Digit savings app in 2021 and merged it into the Oportun app in 2023, but this review covers only the loans.

Oportun suits borrowers with no U.S. credit history or a low score who need a small loan now and want their on-time payments reported to build credit. On $2,000 repaid in 12 monthly payments, a payday alternative loan costs about $336 at most by our math, at the 28% cap with a $20 fee, against about $410 at Oportun’s 35.95% APR, so ask a credit union first.
