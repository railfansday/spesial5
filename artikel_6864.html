Burgundy Cat-Eye Nails Are the Most Mesmerizing Mani of the Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

There are a few nail colors I consider non-negotiable on my fingertips once fall rolls around: chocolate brown, black cherry, and, of course, burgundy. The latter is a rich, wine-like shade that’s classic, sophisticated, and something I find myself coming back to year after year. For 2026, the color has gotten a seriously mesmerizing makeover in the form of burgundy cat-eye nails. It’s not only a shimmery, dimensional manicure that’ll have you staring at your fingertips all day long, but it’s also a certified trend with searches for the look up more than 300 percent on Google. “Burgundy cat-eye nails have been one of my favorite looks to create behind the nail station,” says celebrity nail artist Erica De Los Santos.

Unlike a traditional glossy burgundy manicure, cat-eye nails use magnetic polish to create a light-catching effect that shifts and moves depending on the angle. Basically, it scratches a really specific itch in your brain that you’ll definitely become obsessed with (if you aren’t already). With the help of De Los Santos, I have all the deets on how to get burgundy cat-eye nails, plus plenty of photo inspo.

Burgundy cat-eye nails are exactly what they sound like: a moody and sophisticated rich, wine-red manicure created with polish that contains magnetic particles. This delivers a shifting shimmer that looks like velvet or polished gemstones—just enough sparkle to make these perfect for the holiday season.

“What makes this look so beautiful is how the depth and color shift change depending on the undertone of the base and the magnetic shade layered over it,” says De Los Santos. “The result is a multidimensional finish that catches the light beautifully, giving the nails a luxurious, almost velvet-like appearance.”

And the best part? There are so many ways to make the look your own. The magnetic effect can be customized depending on how much shimmer you want, and you can work a cat-eye into all types of nail art. “One of my favorite ways to wear burgundy cat-eye nails is as a solid color because the magnetic effect is already so beautiful on its own,” says De Los Santos. “But if you want to have a little fun with it, you can pair it with chrome starburst accents, playful polka dots, or even animal prints. The beauty of a burgundy cat-eye is how versatile it is.”

If you tell your nail artist you want a burgundy cat-eye manicure, they’ll have no problem picking the perfect deep red shade. However, you’ll need to specify whether you want a soft velvet cat-eye finish or a more defined cat-eye look. Bringing a reference photo is always helpful (gotcha covered below), since the placement and intensity of the shimmer can completely change the finished look.

After prep (“I always start by gently pushing back the cuticles, carefully removing excess dead cuticle, and shaping the nails”), De Los Santos applies one coat of a deep plum or burgundy gel polish and lets it cure.

Next, “I apply one coat of magnetic cat-eye gel, preferably in a shade similar to the base color, such as deep red, plum, or purple,” says De Los Santos. “Keeping the shades within the same color family really enhances the depth and makes the magnetic effect pop. Before curing, I use a magnet to create the desired light-reflecting effect.” She flash cures (a short, incomplete cure that’s a few seconds long) each nail as she applies the polish and magnet, and then fully cures all of them at the same time once every nail is painted. From there, leave your cat eye as-is or add chrome powder, designs, or any other nail art you love.

You can also create the look at home with magnetic nail polish and a compatible nail magnet (talk about an easy DIY nail art moment!). De Los Santos explains that gel gives you more time to manipulate the magnetic particles, but she also says there are lots of traditional polish options out there to get the cat-eye effect. With regular polish, prep is exactly the same, but the only thing that’s different is the application. “Apply a coat, immediately use your magnet while the polish is still wet, and move on to the next nail,” says De Los Santos (working with the polish while it’s still wet is key to get the magnetic particles to sit where you’d like them to). “Once you’ve completed all ten nails, you can repeat the process with a second coat if you’d like to build more depth and dimension.” Finish with the appropriate top coat.
