The 25 Best Beauty Gifts to Give This Season, According to Editors Who Test Them All

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

From luxury bodycare to skincare devices.

We may earn commission from links on this page, but we only recommend products we love. Promise.

I spend my days testing and writing about beauty products, and yes, it’s pretty much as great as it sounds. Between swiping on lip glosses, styling my hair with new tools, burning fancy candles, and much, much more, team Cosmo’s beauty editors try nearly every noteworthy product on the market. So, the holidays are basically our Super Bowl for gift-giving.

We spend months stocking up on the best beauty gifts to give our friends and family, so we thought it would be in our lovely readers’ best interest to round up all the products, accessories, and devices we’re giving (and hoping to receive) this year. From viral skincare sets to luxury fragrances, these are the best beauty gifts to give anyone on your list this holiday season.

One Cosmo editor says: “If you reach into whatever bag I’m wearing at given moment, I’m guaranteed to have an EADEM lip balm floating around. The chrome packaging of these Cosmo award-winning tubes are not only incredibly gorgeous, but the hydrating formulas leave behind the glossiest (and non-sticky) finish. Why choose one shade when you can have three with this set? My personal go-to is Fig Sauce.”—JASMINE HYMAN, ASSISTANT BEAUTY EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: “Luxury candles are an unfailing gift, whether it’s for secret Santa, white elephant, or for the beauty lover in your life. This set features three of Diptyque’s most iconic scents (my personal favorite is Baies), and your giftee is sure to have the best-smelling house after burning these for hours on end.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “If I’m going on a trip, there’s a 100% chance that my skincare and makeup products are packed away in my Abbode pouches. The soft waffle material makes it easy to throw into any size bag, and there’s a bunch of colorways to choose from. Best of all, the custom embroidery is included in the price.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “No, this isn’t a cool lighter. It’s an even cooler discreet travel perfume, perfect for throwing in your bag on the go, or showcasing on a vanity for a chic spray before you leave the house. Choose one of Sidia’s long-lasting scents to fill the golden case with.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “Wanna help them find their signature scent? This kit from Sephora comes with 20 samples, and once they pick their favorite, they can redeem a full-size bottle of their choosing for no additional cost. Discover a range of niche brands as well as luxury fragrances, and you can keep all the minis for travel or layering. Did someone say smartest gift ever?!”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “You’ve gotta have a cute robe to get ready in. It’s the rules. I’ve tried virtually every single robe out there as a beauty editor, and my Hommey robe is my absolute favorite. The terry material is plush and softly dries off my skin after the shower, but it looks so cute. It’s also very easy to clean (adult things!).”—BETH GILLETTE, BEAUTY EDITOR

One Cosmo editor says: “The first styling tool of its kind, this two-in-one styler has a blow dryer setting to use on damp hair. Then, switch on infrared mode, and you can lock in that sleek and bouncy look with the same exact brush head for low-effort blowouts.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “I’ve used these countless times over the years and they make my skin look so juicy. I’ll wear it on overnight flights and wake up actually looking hydrated—a phrase I absolutely never thought I’d utter in a million years with how drying airplanes can be. But even not on flights, I’ll pop one of these on while I’m working from home so I feel fresh and bright for a night out. And this discovery set of four makes the perfect stocking stuffer.”—BG

One Cosmo editor says: “I love this LED mask so much that I've given away every other one in my arsenal. Winner of both a Cosmo Acne Award and Holy Grail Award, this mask became a mainstay for me because it's just so easy to use. I turn it on, attach it to my face, and go about my life for a few minutes. And more consistent use means I actually see results over time. The cooling effects are genius—it really brings down the morning puffiness, especially combined with the red light from the mask.”—BG

One Cosmo editor says: “Wearable fragrance is the future of perfume, so get in on it early with this necklace that holds a teensy bit of your fragrance to reapply as needed. It’s a gorgeous design, by Cosmo-loved jewelry brand Heaven Mayhem, and pairs with OUAI’s also Cosmo-loved fragrances.”—BG

One Cosmo editor says: “This sculptural incense holder will look fab atop any nightstand or dresser. And it’s sure to elevate the vibes of any room thanks to its scent profile with notes of white musk, amber, and bergamot.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “Everyone deserves the gift of an everything shower, and this set from Salt & Stone brings the spa to any bathroom (even my teeny NYC studio). It’s got a luxe body wash and rich cream to indulge in, then once you’re all clean and moisturized, layer on the fresh deodorant and fragrance to top it all off.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “The Cosmo team is pretty obsessed with Reale Actives. I’ve used up several bottles of multiple of the brand’s products since it came out and can confirm the formulas are on par with much of my high-end skincare. This limited edition minis set is perfect for testing out the whole line, or if you know you already love it, to travel with (that’s how I’m using it).”—BG

One Cosmo editor says: “I unboxed this advent calendar in the Cosmo office, expecting to throw most of it on the free table. But nearly everything made its way either into my own personal stash or into presents I’m giving to friends and family. This is filled with viral products, making it a go-to gift for anyone in your life who spends all their time scrolling their For You Page. Standout full-sizes include Iconic London Prep-Set-Glow setting mist, the Ilia Limitless Lash Mascara, Sacheu Eyeshadow STAY-N, and a Sweed Le Lipstick.”—BG

One Cosmo editor says: “Does it mean I’ve officially reached adulthood if I get excited over nice hand soaps? I was thrilled to see this especially gorgeous limited-edition chrome set from Grown Alchemist. And not only will they look fabulous atop your sink, but your hands will smell amazing and feel super soft, too.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “If they’re an at-home manicure person, this gel kit is the perfect present. For $85, it includes all the tools you need for a DIY set, including a lamp, base coat, and top coat. Plus everything they need to remove and do it all over again in a few weeks.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “If all the morning shed videos on TikTok haven’t convinced you to try a satin bonnet, let me try. Satin (and its expensive cousin, silk) reduces friction to prevent your hair from damage. But I love a bonnet over a silk pillowcase because it’s extremely easy to travel with. This one is also pretty damn chic.”—BG

One Cosmo editor says: “No person can ever have too many Starface pimple patches. And these cute teeny ones are the perfect stocking stuffer. I love that you get so many colors on one sheet; it makes deciding how to cover up your zit feel fun.”—BG

One Cosmo editor says: “Take their skincare routine to the next level with this sculpting NuFACE set. Perfect for morning puffiness or snatching the face before a big event, this mini kit makes skin prep so easy.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “This is on my personal holiday wishlist. I love all the LED showerheads, but the bathroom in my current apartment doesn’t allow me to change the faucet out. This feels like the next best thing to getting my red light therapy in for my whole body. The red light also makes your shower feel like a whole self-care sesh.”—BG

One Cosmo editor says: “Not only are Emi Jay’s beloved clips super cute (I’m especially obsessed with this bow design), but they’re actually amazing for holding hair back and in place. My strands are just about as thick as it gets, and I love this for a secure half-up, half-down.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “This stadium-safe clear tote is perfect for stashing all your beauty essentials on game day or for a concert. Throw in sunscreen, lip balm, and whatever else you may need to re-apply throughout the day. Because yes, Harry Styles WILL notice if you need to reapply your lip gloss.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “Does a beauty gift get more festive than this stunning eyeshadow? The packaging is giviing all of the holiday vibes, and the cream-to-powder formula is extremely easy to apply. I use my fingers, and the shiny pigment payoff is major.”—JH

One Cosmo editor says: “To hell with people who say you can’t gift perfume! A discovery set is the key. Contrapposto is the newest perfume brand I’ve got my eyes on, and this exact set is how I tried all of their scents to figure out my favorite (which is Musc Petille, if you were wondering).”—BG

One Cosmo editor says: “You know those products that seem superfluous, but then once you try them you can’t remember how you ever lived without them? That’s me and this hair towel. It dries my hair while staying firmly on top of my head, and the microfiber material is snag-free, so you won’t rip at your precious strands when taking it off. It makes hair wash days so much easier.”—JH
