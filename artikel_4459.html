2028 Genesis G80 Is the Luxury Brand’s First Hybrid Sedan

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The G80 joins the GV80 SUV as the first hybrid models to wear Genesis’s winged logo.

Genesis has experienced a meteoric rise from its humble roots as a top-of-the-line Hyundai to a legitimate luxury brand that competes with the likes of BMW and Mercedes-Benz. While the Korean automaker has a diverse lineup that includes a trio of sedans and four SUVs (including three EVs), it has been missing a hybrid option—until now. The 2028 Genesis G80 joins the GV80 as the first models to wear the winged logo and offer a gas-electric powertrain.

Earlier this year, Genesis revealed its first-ever hybrid system on the GV80, and it’s the same powertrain that will power the new G80 variant. That means a turbocharged 2.5-liter inline-four paired with two electric motors that together make 347 horsepower and the same 391 pound-feet of torque as Genesis’s twin-turbo 3.5-liter V-6. The hybrid setup also feeds the wheels via a seven-speed automatic transmission, and unlike the Hyundai Group’s other gas-electric models, the Genesis twins will be the first to feature a liquid-cooled battery instead of an air-cooled unit. The automaker claims the hybrid G80 will be 30 percent more fuel efficient than its turbo-four counterpart, which should result in roughly 30 mpg combined.

Both the hybrid G80 and GV80 will feature exclusive settings like adjustable regenerative braking and “Stay Mode,” which lets passengers use the air conditioning and infotainment system without turning on the gas engine. Along with an EV Mode, Genesis says the 2.5 Turbo Hybrid powertrain has an enhanced engine clutch and crankshaft that help reduce noise and vibration to provide a quieter experience. Inside, you’ll find the same luxurious appointments and dash-spanning OLED screen as the current models, and the GV80 debuts several new two-tone color schemes and snazzier materials.

The only way to distinguish a hybrid G80 from a nonhybrid one is the grille, which features a unique 3-D mesh design on the former. The thriftiest sedan also rolls on unique 18- and 20-inch wheels; the GV80 has the same calling cards, albeit with larger wheel options. Both models also introduce new paint colors for the 2028 model year. Plus, the hybrid sedan is now 5.1 inches longer than before. Finally, for those who like a blacked-out appearance, the hybrid G80 and GV80 are available with the Prestige Black treatment.

Genesis hasn’t yet said when the 2028 G80 and GV80 will go on sale in the United States or how much they will cost. However, considering the ‘27 version of the sedan ranges from $60,995 to $81,695, and the SUV ranges from $60,395 to $86,295, we’d wager that the new additions start somewhere near the upper half of their respective price ranges when they eventually reach dealerships.
