Some Nvidia employees are calling for Jensen Huang's return after his resignation

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Free daily briefing on global business news.

An internal post asking the board to weigh staff sentiment has drawn more than 1,600 comments, according to Wired

Nvidia $NVDA employees are urging the company's board to bring back Jensen Huang after his resignation as CEO, according to Wired, which reviewed internal correspondence at the chipmaker.

A longtime engineer submitted an internal suggestion-box post, before Huang resigned, asking the board to weigh employee sentiment before acting, according to Wired. The post has since collected over 1,600 replies, and a number of them urge directors to figure out how to restore him to the role.

"If you believe in the mission, bring back THE boss," one anonymous commenter wrote, according to Wired. Another wrote: "We are all humans and can make mistakes. Jensen did so many good things and he needs a second chance."

One employee emailed the whole company to press for openness about why Huang departed, according to Wired. "I stand by Jensen," the staffer wrote, adding that supporting fairness need not require distancing oneself from a leader who has had such an impact on the workforce. Wired could not reach that employee, and the engineer who wrote the suggestion-box post declined to comment.

Within hours of the resignation, about two dozen employees voiced support for Huang on LinkedIn, according to Wired. Wired did not find any public messages from staffers at the chipmaker welcoming his departure.

An employee with roughly two years at Nvidia, who lacked permission to speak on the record, told Wired the wish to see Huang reinstated is widespread. "Nvidia is Nvidia because of Jensen," the employee said, adding that the company will struggle in the absence of its founder and that directors need to reinstate him.

Many employees assumed the board would complete its investigation prior to any significant shake-up, according to Wired. The suggestion-box post asked that the board report what it uncovers to staff and officially request their opinions on Huang’s future as CEO. Huang exited right when the probe was getting under way, and staff are looking for the reasons.

Nvidia and board chair Harvey Jones had not responded to requests for comment from Wired.

Huang resigned as CEO on Thursday after reports of extramarital affairs, including one with a woman U.S. intelligence officials suspect is an agent of Beijing's spy agencies. Nvidia named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO and appointed Jones, a former Synopsys $SNPS CEO, as the board's first chair. The board is searching for a permanent CEO and will consider internal and external candidates.

Huang, 63, admitted to infidelity in a note to employees on Nov. 8, the day the board opened an independent investigation.
