Intuit agreed to sell Mailchimp to Bending Spoons for less than a third of its 2021 price

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Free daily briefing on global business news.

The $3.68 billion deal is set to close in early 2027, after Mailchimp revenue fell 1% in Intuit's latest quarter

Intuit $INTU agreed to sell Mailchimp to Bending Spoons for approximately $3.68 billion, the company said on Thursday. The deal should wrap up in early 2027, pending regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions.

The price combines cash of $3.31 billion with Bending Spoons shares worth $368 million. That is less than a third of the $12 billion Intuit paid for Mailchimp in 2021, according to CNBC.

Intuit said it intends to use the cash proceeds to repurchase its own stock within fiscal 2027 after the deal closes. The company said the sale narrows its focus on AI-based tools that help businesses win customers, manage their sales pipelines and grow.

Mailchimp revenue slipped 1%, reaching $313 million, in the opening quarter of Intuit's fiscal year, which closed Oct. 31. For fiscal 2027, Intuit expects Mailchimp revenue of $1.256 billion to $1.266 billion, down 1% to flat. The company said its guidance excludes any impact from the sale and that it will update guidance when the deal closes.

The Mailchimp unit generated $1.3 billion in revenue for the fiscal year ending in July, roughly flat since 2024, according to Bloomberg. Bending Spoons purchases mature software businesses and restructures how they run in order to lift profits.

Bending Spoons CEO Luca Ferrari $RACE said in an October interview that falling software valuations are creating acquisition opportunities for the company. Bending Spoons closed its Airtable acquisition in early September and struck a deal that month to buy Miro at a valuation of $1.36 billion. Its portfolio includes AOL, Vimeo, Evernote, WeTransfer and Eventbrite.

Berkshire Hathaway $BRK.B announced a $330 million investment in Bending Spoons on Oct. 14. On Oct. 27, Bending Spoons proposed a $1.5 billion offering of senior secured notes to fund acquisitions of other businesses.
