It’s pretty much anyone’s prize to win, and fans will have an even bigger opportunity to make their voices heard thanks to the show airing live on both the West Coast and the East Coast next week—for the first time ever.

Deep dive this way, but typically Dancing With the Stars only airs live on the East Coast because of conflicting local news programming on the West Coast. Clearly ABC made an exception this year, which means West Coast viewers will be able to log on and cast their votes while watching the show in real time. Considering DWTS is filmed in Los Angeles and a ton of the celebs have fan bases on the West Coast, this could be huge!

As for next week’s format, the cast will perform a freestyle routine, a Judges’ Choice routine (where the judges pick a dance style for each couple that’ll challenge them), and an Instant Dance, where they have to choreograph and perform a dance live with very little notice. That’s three dances in one night—a huge challenge for everyone!
