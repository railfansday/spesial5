built99 positions india’s contextual intelligence on the global stage

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

built99 introduces an annual selection of 99 nominated studios. backed by designboom and archdaily, the initiative champions low-impact construction, regional craft, and deep contextual intelligence.

mariana rodrigues November 12, 2026 3 min

Built99 arrives to bridge Indian architecture and design with the global creative sphere, spotlighting practices defined by low-impact construction, native materials, and cultural responsiveness. Backed by designboom and ArchDaily, the initiative introduces an annual list celebrating 99 nominated studios whose work actively challenges conventional building practices. The goal is clear: ensure this regional contextual intelligence takes its rightful place in shaping the future of contemporary architecture and design.

Steering clear of pay-to-play rankings, Built99 acts as an independent cultural institution dedicated to deep architectural substance. Focusing on responsible construction and lasting cultural value, the platform keeps standards rigorous through a strictly nomination-based model, open only to studios with at least a decade of proven built work across India. Out of roughly 250 reviewed candidates, the final 99 practices earned their place through the real-world impact of their built spaces and critical thinking.

Curated by editors and design voices across DAAily platforms, designboom, and ArchDaily, including Martin Zelger, Timo Behrend, Iñaki Laguardia, and Susanna Moreira, the selection relied on five core pillars. The team examined spatial clarity, contextual intelligence, and the ethics of making, with a clear focus on local labor, native materials, and regional craft. Civic footprint and lasting relevance carried equal weight, spotlighting studios that enrich their communities with architecture built to outlast fleeting fads.

The resulting index mirrors the regional variety of Indian architecture, refusing to limit itself to primary metropolises or singular aesthetics. From dense urban hubs like Mumbai, Delhi, and Bengaluru to practices embedded across Kerala, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, Raipur, and Udaipur, the 99 studios chart a truly decentralized landscape. Here, established figures share the stage with hands-on ateliers and collaborative workshops, united by clear spatial thinking, local roots, and genuine civic commitment.

Securing physical permanence in print, the Built99 hardcover book launches this November to document the projects, research, and material methods of all 99 studios. The release pairs global coverage across designboom and ArchDaily with features across leading Indian titles like Forbes India, Fortune India, Robb Report, and GoodHomes. With the full index now live since November 10, leading up to an invitation-only gathering and forum on November 14 at New Delhi’s ITC Maurya, held alongside event partners Amici, Marmo Solutions, and Within.
