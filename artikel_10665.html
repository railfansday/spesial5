Man goes viral making pizzas in his apartment that sell out in 30 seconds

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

A man making and selling pizza from a shared kitchen in his apartment has a massive waiting list of people wanting to buy a slice.

For a lot of people, travelling to Italy to get a pizza is a bucket list thing. However, there are plenty of other interesting pizza experiences around the world.

Over in San Diego, pizza maker AJ Miller has been going viral for the pies that he makes from his shared apartment kitchen. Miller, who goes by AJEatWorld on social media, has racked up more than 122,000 followers on Instagram and a further 124,000 followers on TikTok.

That has led to him having a massive waiting list for his pizzas, as he is only able to make 15 of them each week.

Speaking to Axios, Miller noted that the pizzas sell out in 30 seconds once his spots go live on an app. He has between 1,500 and 3,500 people waiting to try them too.

“I am making a profit, but I can’t quite like to make a living off of it because there’s only so many pizzas I can make,” he told Axios, noting that the pizzas cost between $25 and $30, depending on what you want. “I think people do like what they can’t get, so the exclusivity does play into it a bit.”

Miller noted that his pizzas, which he cooks in an oven, are styled on New York pies, and he doesn’t have the ability to do coal-fired pizza. He is, however, “99% happy” with his slices.

He has also responded to calls from customers to try and up the availability of his slots. “Been trying to up it 30-40 these past couple weeks! I know it’s not enough still but I’m trying lol,” Miller said on Reddit.

The pizza chef also recommended that people try to make similar pizzas themselves too. “Also highly recommend getting all the gear to make it at home because I do think with enough practice anyone can make restaurant-level pies on their own at home!” he added.

Back in April, PizzaFromABaclony in Norway went viral for a similar idea, doing as their name suggests – making pizza on a balcony. They’d deliver to customers waiting on the street below by using a pulley system.

More recently, over in New York, a group of friends started a ‘restaurant’ out of their apartment too. ‘Some Guy’s House’ sold steak dinners for $25, and delivered the meals using a similar pulley system out of their window.

However, it was shut down just two weeks after their landlord became aware of it, despite people already pre-ordering.
