Taco Bell quietly tests Fire Piña Hot Dog on exclusive secret menu

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Taco Bell has secretly been experimenting with a Fire Piña Hot Dog, and the unusual creation was just one of several bizarre new menu items served at an exclusive event.

The fast-food giant has never been afraid to experiment with unusual food combinations, but its latest creation might be one of its strangest yet.

According to Foodbeast, the chain quietly debuted several never-before-seen dishes during its inaugural Live Más Open celebrity golf tournament on November 2.

Hosted by Kevin Hart at The Ranch at Laguna Beach, California, the event featured celebrities, professional athletes, and creators competing in a nine-hole tournament, complete with an on-course Taco Bell drive-thru serving exclusive food.

Among the new creations was the Fire Piña Hot Dog, which swaps a traditional bun for Taco Bell’s signature chalupa flatbread.

The hot dog comes loaded with pineapple, pickled red onions, cotija cheese, and sauces, combining sweet, spicy, and savory flavors into one rather unexpected Taco Bell creation.

The Fire Piña Hot Dog wasn’t the only surprise. Taco Bell also served Lemon Pepper Ranch Dusted Crispy Chicken Nuggets, a Mini Pimento Cheese Quesarito, and an Avalanche Taco.

Other exclusive dishes included a Baja Caesar Crispy Chicken Crunchwrap and Fiesta Potato Salad.

Foodbeast shared photos of the creations on Instagram, giving fans a closer look at the experimental menu.

Meanwhile, Allrecipes editor Bailey Fink managed to try the Fire Piña Hot Dog and gave it a glowing review, calling it one of the best fast-food hot dogs she’d ever eaten.

Fink compared its combination of pineapple, cheese, and tangy toppings to a Colombian hot dog, particularly praising Taco Bell’s decision to use its soft chalupa flatbread instead of a traditional bun.

Unfortunately, there’s a catch. None of these experimental creations are currently available at regular Taco Bell restaurants, and the chain has no confirmed plans to release them nationwide.

However, as Dexerto previously reported, the Live Más Open will result in a discontinued Taco Bell menu item returning for a limited time in 2027, with the winning team helping decide what comes back.

Fans will be able to watch the tournament when it premieres on Peacock on November 20.

And while the Fire Piña Hot Dog remains an event exclusive, Taco Bell has a history of turning strange experiments into actual products. Its Baja Blast Pie eventually launched nationwide more than a year after it was first revealed.
