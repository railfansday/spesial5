Splatoon Raiders Is Getting Its Very Own 336-Page Art Book Next Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Let's hope it's not a Japan exclusive for long

Nintendo has announced that it is teaming up with KADOKAWA on a new art book all about Splatoon Raiders.

The 336-page book launches in Japan on 9th February 2027 and will be available from both the region's online storefront and the official Nintendo Stores across Japan for 3,960 yen (around £19 / $25).

It's apparently rammed with over 5,000 pieces of concept art from the game, divided into five chapters focused on the artwork, creatures, style, and world, with a mysterious 'bonus' section at the end.

It's all still a work in progress at the moment, but Nintendo has shared a handful of sample pages online, which we have grouped together for you to check out below:

Splatoon Raiders is one of the most stylish games we've seen from Nintendo this year, so it makes sense that it would get the full art book treatment soon enough. We're keeping our fingers crossed that it doesn't stay a Japanese exclusive for too long.
