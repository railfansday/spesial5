Here's What Xbox Owners Need To Know About 'Moving To New Zealand' For GTA 6

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

So, it's finally almost time for GTA 6. You've probably preloaded the game on your Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S already, and now you're eagerly awaiting the official launch that takes place on November 19th.

However, there are more than a few whispers going around about the "New Zealand" trick — something we've seen time and time again over the past decade-plus on Xbox — and it looks set to have a big influence again with GTA 6.

But what is it, and most importantly could it get you banned? Let's take a look at the facts:

On this page: Here's What Xbox Owners Need To Know About 'Moving To New Zealand' For GTA 6

A game like GTA 6 obviously has a set release date of November 19th, but it lands at "midnight local time" depending on the region you're in. That means New Zealand ends up being the first region to get access, and their "midnight" corresponds to 3am PT / 6am ET / 11am GMT on November 18th.

Therefore, if people want to play the game as early as possible, they'll need to be in New Zealand - and the "New Zealand Trick" refers to changing your region in your console's settings, providing slightly early access to GTA 6.

There's always been a lot of chatter online about how Xbox doesn't really care about you "moving to New Zealand" to get around release times, but that's technically not the case. Xbox's terms and conditions specifically state that trying to "circumvent regional content restrictions" is prohibited, and this is mentioned a few times in documentation:

The official answer is yes - Xbox could take whatever kind of actions they wanted against an account that was found breaking the rules to play GTA 6 early on Xbox Series X and S. It could result in a warning, a suspension, a ban, or anything else they see fit.

You might also get away with it, as have the majority of people with many other games in the past, but it's still something you should only do at your own risk. We don't recommend breaking the rules just to play a game a few hours early, but we're also not naive enough to think that people won't be trying it come November 18th!

Something to also keep in mind with this is that spoilers will likely begin hitting the web from the New Zealand release time on November 18th (if not earlier). At that point, we expect all kinds of footage and screenshots to begin flooding social media, so you may want to have a bit of a digital detox until the 19th if you don't want to see anything.

Anyway, we know this is a hot topic, and feel free to share your thoughts about it in the comments below. The decision is up to you - but just know that you are running a risk by "moving to New Zealand", however small it might appear to be.
