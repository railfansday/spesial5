GDC Survey Suggests Devs Want To Make Games For Switch 2 More Than PS5 Or Xbox

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The GDC Festival of Gaming has published its latest 'State of the Game Industry' report, which is based on a survey of over 2,600 game industry professionals on topics such as AI, layoffs, labour, and more.

One particular result caught our eye, and it's regarding which platforms developers are currently interested in developing for in the future. As expected, PC was comfortably the winner at 81%, likely down to its versatility, ease-of-use, and broad visibility of storefronts like Steam and Epic Games Store.

However, it's the Switch 2 that bagged the silver medal at 46%, putting it ahead of the PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Steam Deck, Steam Machine, and even upcoming consoles like the PS6 and Project Helix.

Since its release in June 2025, the Switch 2 has seen a staggering number of releases, from high-profile 'AAA' titles to smaller indie games, and it seems this is only set to continue or perhaps even accelerate in the future.

In a similar chart looking at which platform(s) developers' upcoming game or project will ship on next, the Switch 2 ranks lower than PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Steam Deck, pulling in 29% of the vote. With this one, however, it's worth considering external factors that might be at play.

For example, it's easy to say that you want to develop for the Switch 2, but whether you have the budget and dev kits to actually do so is another question. Just something to consider when looking at these results.
