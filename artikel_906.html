Nick Cannon, Moms of His 12 Kids Reveal Dynamic in Raising the Cannons

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Nick Cannon—dad to twins Moroccan and Monroe with Mariah Carey and 10 children with five women—unveiled his life as a father of 12 on his new Netflix show.

Nick Cannon's family is experiencing growing pains.

The Drumline star, who is dad to 12 children, is letting fans inside his life as the head of a blended family through his upcoming docuseries Raising the Cannons.

"I have a relationship with six women and 12 children," Cannon explained in the trailer for the Netflix show, debuting Dec. 18. "This is a world that I created, and I have to deal with everything that comes with it."

And Cannon—who first became a dad after welcoming twins Moroccan and Monroe, 15, with ex Mariah Carey—has learned co-parenting can be difficult given the complex rapport between him and his partners (He’s also dad to Golden Sagon, 9, Powerful Queen, 5 and Rise Messiah, 4, with Brittany Bell, twins Zion Mixolydian and Zillion Heir, 5, and Beautiful Zeppelin, 4, with Abby De La Rosa, Halo Marie, 3, and as well as a late son, Zen, with Alyssa Scott, Legendary Love, 4, with Bre Tiesi and Onyx Ice, 4, with LaNisha Cole.)

As such, the Wild 'n Out creator—who according to Netflix is romantically involved with De La Rosa, Scott and Tiesi—explained, "I want my children to have relationships with one another. One day we all would be able to get along."

Yet Cole expressed acting as one big happy family is a performance she's not willing to put on for the cameras.

"I don't need to create this false narrative," the photographer said in confessional, "like we're one big happy family when we're not."

Meanwhile, De La Rosa had her own take on her partner's role in creating challenges between the women in his life. In her words narrating the trailer offscreen, "Nick formulates these worlds as if we're so separate. Everyone thinks it's competition. No, it's our reality."

For her part, Tiesi, who also stars on Selling Sunset, objected to the notion that she and her son had to fight for Cannon's attention, stating to cameras, "I am not in competition," while also appearing to tell Cannon on the phone the other matriarchs in their circle "broke, pathetic losers."

As for how the comedian's kids feel about their unique family dynamic? Both Moroccan and Monroe chimed in with concerns for their father, who admitted his ego sabotaged his six-year marriage with their mom, Carey, which ended in 2014.

"If you didn't have all these kids," Monroe told Cannon, "you would have been back together," referring to the Grammy winner. In turn, her twin brother suggested in their joint confessional that their dad's lifestyle was not "sustainable in any way."
