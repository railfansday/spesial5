When you have lost five games in a row, the last team you want to face are the world champions. Unfortunately for Czechia, that is exactly the situation they find themselves in tonight in Prague.

Santi Denia's side have not enjoyed life in Nations League Group A3, losing all four matches and scoring just two goals. The Czechs look set to be relegated from the top tier unless they can claim a miracle result tonight and back it up against Croatia on Sunday.

They have been hit by the absence of several key players, including injured sextet Robin Hranac, Pavel Sulc, Ondrej Lingr, Ondrej Kricfalusi, Michal Sacek, and Adam Karabec, plus the suspended Michal Sadilek.

Spain will also be without a clutch of stars in Prague, with teenager superstar Lamine Yamal and forward Mikel Oyarzabal sidelined through injury and illness respectively. They are expected to return for Sunday's Group A3 finale against England, but midfielders Rodri and Dani Olmo will miss both games.

The good news for manager Luis de la Fuente is that he has plenty of quality in reserve, with Alvaro Valles, Jacobo Ramon and Marc Roca handed their first senior call-ups.

The world and European champions should have more than enough to beat the Czechs as they look to stretch their 11-match winning run — and their 43-game unbeaten streak — with their place in the quarter-finals already assured.
