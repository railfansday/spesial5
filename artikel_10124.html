YouTube’s Black Friday sale could save you as much as $66 on streaming services

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

With every streaming service jumping in price over the past few years, Black Friday is a great time to ease the burden of these costs. YouTube, for Black Friday, has discounts on select streaming services through its Primetime Channels lineup, including up to $66 off of HBO Max.

HBO Max is the best overall value, but there are plenty of savings to go around.

Some of these are mirrored outside of YouTube, too. Amazon Prime Video has several of the same deals, and HBO Max is matching the 1-year discount through its own website (which makes it easier to use HBO’s app).
