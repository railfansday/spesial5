Burger King launched a Magic: The Gathering meal with Whopper deck boxes — how to buy

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

TL;DR: Burger King Italy is offering a €19.90 Magic: The Gathering meal with a Whopper deck box and 100 sleeves through Nov. 30 — and international players are already hunting down the gear on eBay.

Trading card games and fast-food menus make a natural pair. Now, Wizards of the Coast is stepping into the drive-thru with an official Magic: The Gathering meal at Burger King — complete with a Whopper-themed deck box and matching card sleeves.

The catch? The promotion is exclusive to Burger King locations in Italy. Even though the meal is region-locked, the novelty is strong enough that Commander players elsewhere are already scrambling to get their hands on the gear.

Officially called The Great Gathering Bundle, the meal runs through Nov. 30, while supplies last. Priced at €19.90, the combo includes:

The food — Your choice of a Chicken Krispper, Double Cheeseburger, or plant-based Impossible Whopper, paired with fries and a drink.

A Whopper deck box — Styled to look like an actual burger, complete with an orange sesame-seed bun pattern, a patty, and layered toppings.

100 matching card sleeves — Enough standard-size sleeves to outfit a full Commander deck.

Unlike Pokémon Happy Meals, the bundle does not include any exclusive promotional Magic cards. That saves players from chasing region-locked game pieces, even if it has not stopped the accessories from becoming instant collector's items.

The Italy-only launch ties into Burger King's sponsorship of local Magic Pauper tournaments across the country, alongside a challenge from the YouTube channel Tolarian Community College that dares Italian pro player Andrea Mengucci to review the meal on camera.

If you do not happen to be visiting Italy before Nov. 30, you still have a few ways to track down the set:

Check secondary marketplaces like eBay — Italian buyers have already started listing the empty deck boxes and sleeves on eBay and international trading groups. Reseller markups are steep right after launch, so waiting a few days for more supply to hit the market can help you avoid overpaying.

Reach out to friends or local trade groups in Italy — If you know someone traveling in Italy or connect with European players in online Magic communities, arranging a direct trade or pickup will get you much closer to the €19.90 retail price than international eBay auctions.

Hold out for future regional collaborations — Because this is one of Wizards of the Coast's first fast-food partnerships, a successful run in Italy makes future Magic meal promos in other regions a solid possibility if you would rather skip reseller markups altogether.

Whether you snag one on eBay or hold out for a wider rollout, your Commander deck has never looked quite this appetizing.
