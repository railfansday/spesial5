This follows Saturday’s game against Arsenal.

Plenty of angles to look at and debate from a very eventful 90+ minutes.

Plus of course all of the subplots that swirled around this Newcastle United v Arsenal fixture.

Pat Nevin sees a lot of Newcastle United, the former Chelsea and Everton winger a regular visitor at St James’ Park.

He was on media duties this weekend as well.

Reflecting back on his latest visit, Pat Nevin detailing in the Football Extra newsletter: “I nipped down to a freezing St James’ Park at the weekend, where the reception for their beloved former midfielder Bruno Guimaraes made the weather feel positively balmy. Hell hath no fury like a Geordie scorned, but fortunately the home side played so well in the second half that, at one stage, they forgot to boo him for a good two whole minutes.”

Pat Nevin adding: “I understand why football fans feel this way. Having lost Antony Gordon, Alexander Isak and Sandro Tonali already, it was a heavy blow to lose the one who mirrored their passions the most. Players think differently. The idea of turning down the English champions, who could win the Champions League, and instead stay at a club that is shipping out all of the best talent around you, is demanding an unthinkable level of loyalty. Most would substitute loyalty for stupidity in that sentence.”

I suppose that also sums up my many decades of following Newcastle United, especially when non-football fans ask me why I do it…

Back to Saturday’s match though and Pat Nevin: “Arsenal were fabulous and the gulf between the teams looked enormous in the first half but the comeback after the break could well be my favourite 45 minutes of the season so far. From nowhere, Newcastle bullied, dominated, outran and even outplayed Arsenal. It was glorious stuff. Now comes the tough part as manager Matthias Jaissle has to recreate that feeling every week.”

As Newcastle United fans,, what will our stupidity/loyalty be rewarded with in the weeks and months to come?

At the same time, a gutsy point at Brighton followed by home wins over Man U and the Mackems, then the world will feel a very different place.
