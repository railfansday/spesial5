Crystal Palace owners 'look very stupid' - Finance Guru analyses cost of Pierre Sage sack

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Crystal Palace's owners have made themselves look "very stupid" after appointing and sacking Pierre Sage just six months apart.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that Sage's short tenure may have cost the club upwards of £7million.

There was a lot of excitement at Selhurst Park as the 47-year-old replaced Oliver Glasner, having won the Coupe de France and finished second in Ligue 1.

However, it has been far from a fairytale spell in the Premier League for Sage, who managed just 16 games in charge before being sacked.

The Eagles have been left stranded at the bottom of the Premier League table after 10 games, with real fears of relegation emerging at Selhurst Park.

Palace are now being forced to pay 18 months of compensation to Sage, having also paid a £4m fee to appoint him from Lens in the summer.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson has hit out at the Palace hierarchy over the situation, given the amount of money splashed on the manager's failure.

"Ultimately they didn't want to lose Glasner. Glasner chose to leave. I don't know, maybe it was just about money," he said.

"If it was just money, then obviously they look very stupid now because they've ended up probably paying something like, let's say, £6m or £7m.

"So yes, they look quite stupid, but you can't always predict how these things are going to go. And I think I don't think the Glasner situation was purely about money.

"I think he wanted to go and so, that's where you are. Sage probably was a good option, and then you see him in close quarters, and he doesn't connect with the players.

"Everything changes, and they've made the decision to get rid because they know that they can't afford a relegation."

The Eagles are now in limbo ahead of the Premier League resuming, but there's still plenty of time left to make a hire during the break.

Football Insider understands that Thomas Frank's appointment has hit a new stumbling block, but a verbal agreement is in place with the 53-year-old.

To make matters worse, the current situation could cause several players to seek exits, even as early as the January transfer window.

Jean-Philippe Mateta, Adam Wharton and Ismaila Sarr have all been heavily linked with exits in recent windows, and they could be the first gone.
