Diogo Dalot could leave Man United next year as clubs across Europe keep tabs on his contract situation, sources have told Football Insider.

Dalot, 27, has come under fire for his sub-par performances in a United shirt this season.

The Portuguese right-back is out of contract at Old Trafford in 2028 and recently surpassed 250 appearances for the club.

United are looking at bolstering their defensive ranks in the next couple of windows after focusing on attacking and midfield additions in recent times.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Transfer Insider podcast, Dalot could be sold in 2027 as numerous clubs in other top European leagues eye his availability.

He remains a regular right now under Michael Carrick, but United do lack alternatives on either flank at the back and a new manager may see things differently if he is sacked soon.

Dalot has been at United since 2018, yet failed to ever establish himself as a fan favourite or regular starter across a single campaign.

The Portugal international is bound to attract interest heading into the final 18 months of his initial contract, especially with United planning to potentially upgrade on him.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Look, all areas of the defence probably need strengthening for Manchester United right now. It's the next area that the hierarchy are looking at to strengthen after focusing on the attack and midfield.

"Dalot is under contract until 2028, with the option of another year, but I’m sure there’ll be clubs who’ll be monitoring his situation across Europe.
