What channel is Celtic v St Mirren? TV and live stream plus team news, referee and VAR

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Celtic host St Mirren on Saturday as the Scottish Premiership swings back into action after the international break.

Martin O’Neill’s men have had a mixed season to date but they lead the table by four points ahead of Rangers, who have a game in hand, while third placed Falkirk are 10 points behind.

Performances and results have drawn scrutiny, particularly in Europe where the Hoops surrended a four-goal lead to LASK as they exited the Champions League play-off round, before losing their opening three games in the Europa League.

Domestic form has also been inconsistent with five points dropped from their last three league matches while Celtic also suffered back-to-back Old Firm defeats by Rangers in September as they exited the Premier Sports Cup quarter-finals.

However, an upturn before the break allowed some positivity to return, with a 3-2 win over Besiktas procuring Celtic’s first points in the Europa League, followed by a 1-0 win over Kilmarnock in the Premiership.

St Mirren got their league campaign off to a flying start, though that early momentum has since given way to a tougher spell.

The Buddies have won only one of their last nine matches and are currently eighth in the table with 14 points from 13 matches. They sit three points behind the top six and five points clear of the bottom.

The Scottish Premiership match between Celtic and St Mirren takes place at Celtic Park, Glasgow on Saturday, November 21, 2026. Kick-off is 3pm.

The match has not been selected for live TV coverage in the UK. Highlights will be shown on Sportscene on BBC One Scotland at 10.20pm.

Auston Trusty is back for Celtic after a family bereavement, but Mika Baur faces 10 weeks out with an ankle injury and Alistair Johnston (hamstring) is yet to return. Benjamin Nygren (heel) is a doubt and Joel van den Berg picked up a knock earlier in the week.

St Mirren midfielders Ryan Carr and Paul Nsio return, while Ismeal Kabia (calf), Nikolas Agrafiotis (calf), Keanu Baccus (Achilles), Malik Dijksteel (hamstring) and Jonah Ayunga (knee) remain out.

Matthew MacDermid is the match referee. Chris Rae and Ross Anderson are serving as the assistant referees, while Graham Grainger is the fourth official. Greg Aitken is in the VAR room, assisted by Ross Hardie.
