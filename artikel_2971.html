This Iconic Growth Investor Can't Stop Buying Anthropic Stock

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Did you buy shares of the popular AI IPO in three of its first four days on the market? I know someone who did.

By Rick Munarriz – Nov 25, 2026 at 10:43AM EST

10 stocks we like better than Anthropic ›

Autumn has to be Cathie Wood's favorite season, because when it comes to buying opportunities, she always seems to be a fan of the fall. The founder, CEO, and chief investment officer at Ark Invest has a knack for adding to existing positions when they're on sale.

Ark was a buyer of Anthropic (ANT -1.74%) in three of the past four trading days. It also happens to be the first four days on the market for the safety-minded AI developer behind the Claude family of large language models.

We know what Wood is buying because Ark publishes its daily transactions a few hours after the close of each trading day. It's a bold display of transparency that I hope more high-profile growth investing icons will follow in the future.

She's not just nibbling on Anthropic stock. Ark is feasting on the debutante since it went public this past Thursday. She bought 935,000 shares across Ark's six main ETFs the day it hit the market, followed by 706,000 more the next day. On Tuesday, she purchased another 752,000 shares. This is nearly 2.4 million shares of Anthropic, a stake valued at $196 million as of Tuesday's close.

Don't be surprised to see that the one day she wasn't a buyer was on Monday, the day that AI stocks rallied. I led with her seasonality preferences. She's a fan of the fall, not the spring.

Anthropic is intriguing now that it's a broken IPO, trading below its $83 initial price. It's one of the fastest-growing businesses in the market, with several consecutive quarters of four-digit percentage revenue growth. The mounting losses aren't ideal, but understandable and forgivable at this early stage of its growth cycle.

The shares hit another new low at Wednesday's open. Will Wood be buying again? It's fall, isn't it? I would be shocked if she didn't buy more. We will all find out on Wednesday night.
