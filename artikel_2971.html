Stock Market Today, Nov. 23: Advanced Micro Devices Climbs on AI-Chip Rebound After Nvidia Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

On Nov. 23, 2026, investors rotated back into AI-chip names, with sector sentiment lifting shares across the group.

By Will Healy – Nov 23, 2026 at 4:50PM EST

Advanced Micro Devices (AMD +6.85%), an AI and CPU chips provider, closed at $453.46, up 6.85%. Shares rose after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, and investors are watching AI-chip sentiment and conference commentary.Trading volume reached 53.0M shares, coming in about 93% above its three-month average of 27.5M shares.

S&P 500 (SNPINDEX: ^GSPC) closed at 7,237, up 4.73%, while Nasdaq Composite (NASDAQINDEX: ^IXIC) closed at 24,090, up 6.51%. Among semiconductors and semiconductor equipment peers, Nvidia (NVDA +31.48%) closed at $165.05, up 31.48%, and Intel (INTC +16.34%) closed at $59.66, up 16.34%, highlighting a broad AI-chip rebound.

AMD was one of the many stocks that benefited from the reinstatement of Jensen Huang as Nvidia's CEO. This stands in contrast to what happened Friday when AMD stock plunged on a short-seller report. The report alleged that some AMD server firmware fell into the hands of a blacklisted Chinese company.

By today, that report appeared to be forgotten. Although AMD is a major competitor to Nvidia in the data center space, it benefits indirectly as Huang's return breathes new life into the AI trade and brings stability to the semiconductor sector.

Fortunately, AMD has made technical progress to the point that it may benefit directly as well. Many industry analysts believe that AMD's MI450 series GPU (along with the MI455X Helios rack) has, at least on paper, caught up to Nvidia's latest release, the Vera Rubin.

Admittedly, at a P/E ratio of 98, the tech stock remains far more expensive than Nvidia, which, even with its gains today, trades at just 18 times earnings. However, analysts forecast that AMD's revenue will grow at 51% annually this year, then rise to 84% in 2027.

Amid a reinvigorated AI trade, such growth gives investors all the more reason to take a closer look at AMD stock.
