DeChambeau confirms DP World Tour membership after LIV Golf's collapse

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Bryson DeChambeau has taken Category One membership on the DP World Tour; Ex-major champions Dustin Johnson, Cameron Smith and Bubba Watson among LIV Golfers to have signed agreements to receive invites to a specific number of tournaments for the 2027 season

Bryson DeChambeau has taken membership on the DP World Tour for the 2027 season, following the collapse of the LIV Golf League.

The two-time US Open winner's future was uncertain after LIV Golf confirmed they would wind down operations earlier this month, but DeChambeau has now taken an automatic exemption to play full-time on the DP World Tour.

DeChambeau applied for membership before the November 15 deadline and has been given Category One status - the highest-priority category - as the 2024 US Open champion, enabling him to play any DP World Tour event he chooses.

LIV Golf players are currently ineligible on the PGA Tour for at least 12 months from their last start in the breakaway league, leaving DeChambeau among the high-profile players now using the DP World Tour as a way back onto the established tours.

Membership categories for the 2027 DP World Tour season were announced Tuesday, with former major champion Dustin Johnson, Cameron Smith and Bubba Watson among ex-LIV golf players signing agreements to receive invites to a specific number of tournaments next season.

Abraham Ancer, Lucas Herbert, Michael La Sasso, Joaquin Niemann, Carlos Ortiz and Thomas Pieters also signed similar deals, which exclude Rolex Series tournaments and include designated events for each player where they would "add value".

"Our 2027 season is set to be one of the most keenly contested campaigns in our 55-year history," said DP World Tour chief executive Guy Kinnings. "There has been significant change in the landscape of men's professional golf over the past 12 months.

"As golf's global Tour, we have taken a pragmatic approach to this unique and unprecedented situation as we go into next year, always with the best interests of our entire membership at the forefront of our minds.

"We realise there are different viewpoints but this approach, discussed and agreed with our Tournament Committee and allied to this responsibly managed programme of invites, will add value which, in turn, will benefit the Tour as a whole."

The 2027 campaign was the final opportunity for DeChambeau to join the DP World Tour via his major victory, as every season the membership was not activated reduced the five-year window by 12 months.

Speaking on the possibility of DeChambeau featuring on the DP World Tour, Rory McIlroy said earlier this month: "I think a name like Bryson DeChambeau and everything he brings with him, good and bad, he brings eyeballs, which is a good thing for tournaments."

DeChambeau previously said LIV Golf would 'exist forever' and confirmed he still believes team golf is 'essential to growing the game', with the nine-time PGA Tour winner now set to return to action at the Nedbank Golf Challenge from December 3-6 - live on Sky Sports.

The DP World Tour insist invites across the season have been carefully managed by their Player Relations Department, following extensive modelling to 'ensure and preserve playing opportunities for existing members'.

Former Ryder Cup stars Lee Westwood, Sergio Garcia, Henrik Stenson, Martin Kaymer, Paul Casey and Graeme McDowell - all ex LIV-Golf members - hold Category 11a membership via the DP World Tour's career money list.
