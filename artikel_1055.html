Man United rule out legal action over ‘unethical’ drone filming of Pochettino’s training session

Manchester United will not pursue legal proceedings following the publication of unauthorised drone footage showing Mauricio Pochettino leading his first training session as head coach.

A 12-minute video was published online after a drone operator flew close to the club’s Carrington training complex on Thursday.

Although United indicated there had been no security breach on the site, it is understood the club considers that a number of laws may have been broken.

However, officials welcomed an apology released by the drone operator on Friday morning, alongside the decision to take down the video. It is understood this action was taken without any direct contact from the club.

While United do not intend to take further action on this occasion, it is understood they remain prepared to defend the club's rights if any similar incidents happen again.

The clip, which showed Pochettino overseeing tactical drills prior to Sunday’s Premier League fixture against Liverpool, has since been deleted by the CC Overview YouTube Channel.

In a statement, the operator said: "I would like to speak about yesterday’s incident. A total error of judgement on my part led to me filming some of the Manchester United training session at Carrington, whilst the flight was legal in terms of drone laws I fully accept it was unethical on my part.

"My aim was to picture Mauricio Pochettino in his United training kit for the supporters to see for the first time. I had wrongly presumed that with it being close to the start of the session that no meaningful training sessions would have started so would have been some harmless warming up shown.

"I also wrongly assumed that the training session would have been open to various media outlets. I would like to apologise to Manchester United, Mauricio Pochettino and the players for my actions and sincerely hope those actions do not affect the outcome of any match.

"I would also like to state that Liverpool Football Club or any other football club were not involved in my decision to film at Carrington yesterday. The video has been removed from my channel.

"I have spent the past 4 years building up my YouTube channel and were well regarded in the fields I film and would now like to get back to building up my damaged reputation and making well-received responsible videos. Thank you."

United have offered no formal comment on the situation.
