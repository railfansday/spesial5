Boxing, golf, and football reign supreme in this week’s Gospel Stats Weekly Brand Report, with sponsorships from DAZN, DraftKings, Lowe’s, and more.

#1 I finally saw quantum physics with my own eyes… Channel: Veritasium Brand: Ground News Views: 19,817,457

It’s not every day you get to see someone push the boundaries of physics. Veritasium secures a regular spot in our top 5 with his science deep dives, and this one might be among his best yet.

Even for working scientists, accessing equipment for quantum physics experiments can be a tough time–so the Veritasium team’s partnership with the Gravity Laboratory at the University of Nottingham allowed them to give the world a vicarious look at what happens to helium when you just don’t stop cooling it down.

The Gravity Lab’s gear allowed Veritasium–and now, any YouTube viewer–to observe helium’s transformation into a superfluid in real time, and discuss how superfluidity can be applied in fields like astrophysics.

These kinds of videos remind me the universe is pretty cool, and even when things aren’t so hot on Earth (metaphorically speaking), there are countless galaxies dancing around us, full of potential.

Oh, also, this video is sponsored by Ground News, which compares various news sources and evaluates them for potential biases. Its sponsorships spiked in the leadup to the midterm elections, but even after Voting Day, we’re still seeing decent numbers: It paid for ~20 other videos this week.

#2 Suprised My Daughter with A New E-bike ?@EskuteUS #shorts Channel: BikeLife Mom Brand: ESKUTE Views: 18,467,608

And now for something completely different. Helmet cam e-biker BikeLife Mom secured spot #2 with a 26-second skit where she “surprises” her daughter with a new e-bike from Eskute. The Short’s keep-’em-watching twist is that Ma wants to test drive the new piece herself…and the “test drive” might just last forever.

BONUS #3,259 A *Shoppy* Vlog | Target, Walgreens, Grocery Haul + Are we turning a corner?? Channel: Jessica Braun Brand: Govee Views: 77,094

We go from Veritasium letting viewers do physics vicariously to Jessica Braun letting viewers shop vicariously. With Black Friday coming up, basically every store is trying to whet shoppers’ appetites, but the economy is tough right now, and not everyone can cut loose with a day of dropping dolla dolla bills.

That’s not to say Braun’s vlog is an ultra-luxury haul. It’s an everyday trip around to Target, Walmart, and out for groceries. Her sponsoring brand is equally everyday: smart lighting company Govee. Someone told me once that color-changing lightbulbs let you instantly paint a room any color you want, for free, and you know what? I’ve found that to be true–and cheap.

Govee paid for a handful of videos this week, including one promising to help people level up their “boring Millennial” Christmas trees and another highlighting TV surround lights.
