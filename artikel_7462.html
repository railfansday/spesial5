Top 50 Most Viewed YouTube Channels Worldwide • Week Of 11/01/2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

This week, the #1 spot in the Global Top 50 belongs to Cadel and Mia. The couple channel picked up 1.16 billion weekly views during a week when no other YouTube channel crossed the billion-view threshold. The Aussie duo was able to increase its traffic by 32% week-over-week.

Overall, it was another dominant week for Indian channels, which accounts for more than 40% of the Global Top 50. A run of holiday celebrations has boosted South Asian creators, and that trend continued at the end of October. And no, I'm not talking about Halloween.
