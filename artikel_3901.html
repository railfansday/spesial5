GTA 6 Comparison Reveals Two Advantages Of Xbox Series X Over PS5

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The good folks over at Digital Foundry are still busy analysing all things to do with GTA 6's launch last week, and their latest comparison takes a look at all four console versions alongside each other - Series X, S, PS5 and PS5 Pro.

They also go on to rank the versions in order, and perhaps surprisingly, Xbox Series X comes out on top versus the standard PS5. Two reasons are given for this, which are that the Xbox version supports 120Hz VRR to smooth out the dips in frame rate, and that the PS5 sometimes "suffers substantially more frequent frame-rate drops than Series X".

"Although both target 30fps and maintain it during typical gameplay, heavy vehicle traversal and intense airport crowd sequences cause frame-rates to drop into the mid to upper 20s. PS5 suffers substantially more frequent frame-rate drops than Series X, which is compounded by the fact that the Xbox machine also benefits from 120Hz VRR support to smooth out minor dips. Ultimately, Series X is meaningfully better off."

Visually, there are said to be no real differences between the Xbox Series X and PS5 versions, although the Series X version does have "slightly more pronounced heat haze effects in one opening shot".

All of this is obviously beaten by the PS5 Pro though, which is said to "deliver the definitive console GTA 6 experience by leveraging its larger GPU, accelerated machine learning hardware and enhanced ray tracing features".

"Overall, the PS5 Pro leads the rankings with its superior visual detail and rock-solid 30fps target, followed by the Xbox Series X - which edges out the standard PS5 through better performance stability. The Xbox Series S sits at the bottom due to visual trade-offs, unstable frame-rates and audio glitches."

It's fair to say we never expected the Xbox Series X version to fare better than the PS5 version in this case, especially considering all the "plays best on PS5" marketing that surrounded GTA 6 prior to release.

Of course, Digital Foundry seeks out the most minute of details when it comes to technical analysis, and the differences between the Xbox Series X and standard PS5 versions aren't going to be major enough to suggest one version is far better than another. It's just interesting that they've ended up ranking the Series X higher in this instance.
