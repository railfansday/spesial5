The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel subtitle announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The 2018-announced sixth entry in the Elder Scrolls series is officially titled The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel, publisher Bethesda Softworks and developer Bethesda Game Studios announced.

The news was officially announced in a video celebrating 40 years of Bethesda Game Studios.

The end of the teaser trailer unveiling the subtitle contains the quote, “The scroll offered only a shimmer, a veil thinly lifted to my eyes. A future lost. A victory undone. Sands of time scattered by fate.” It is attributed to “Yazar, Moth Priest to High Queen Iradne of Hammerfell (4E 206).”

In a tweet, Bethesda Game Studios added, “We can’t wait to make even more together with The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel. Development continues, and while it’ll be some time before we’re ready to share more, it feels great to finally reveal the name and location with you.”
