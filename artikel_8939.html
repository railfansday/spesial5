Bachour Café / Estudio Guto Requena

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Read the original version in Portuguese here.

Text description provided by the architects. The architectural project draws inspiration from French boulevards and the aesthetic richness of Brazilian design, exploring contrasts between opulence and warmth, tradition and innovation, craft and technology. The concept incorporates references to Art Nouveau — a movement that emerged in Paris during the Belle Époque at the end of the 19th century, a period of great cultural effervescence marked by organic lines, fluid forms, and natural inspiration. These elements are reinterpreted in the design, creating a composition that blends tropicality and technology.
