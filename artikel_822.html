England make five changes for All Blacks rematch

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Steve Borthwick has made five changes to England's starting XV for the Nations Championship third-place play-off against New Zealand; England face the All Blacks for the second weekend running after beating them by a record 25 points in their 37-12 victory at Twickenham on Saturday

Steve Borthwick has made five changes to England's starting XV to face New Zealand in the Nations Championship third-place play-off.

The more defensive-minded Freddie Steward replaces George Furbank, who started at full-back in England's 37-12 victory against the same opposition - their record margin of victory against the All Blacks.

Exciting back-rower Greg Fisilau, who impressed off the bench during England's 78-15 demolition of Japan, will make his first international start, as he replaces Ben Curry at openside flanker.

In the forwards, Ellis Genge comes in for Fin Baxter at prop, while Luke Cowan-Dickie replaces Jamie George at hooker.

Winger Tommy Freeman was ruled out of last weekend's win shortly before kick-off after pulling up with a quad issue, but returns to the starting line-up in place of Cadan Murley on Saturday.

No 10 Fin Smith was spotted applying ice to his legs after being substituted last weekend. However, having recovered from cramp, he has retained his place in the starting XV, while Henry Pollock remains on the bench.

England will be aiming to deliver another impressive performance and secure third place in the inaugural Nations Championship, following their emphatic victory over New Zealand in their previous outing. The match comes a day before France take on South Africa at Twickenham in the tournament final.

England's commanding win over the All Blacks has also had a significant impact on the world rankings. Borthwick's side have climbed from fifth to fourth, while New Zealand have dropped from second to fifth.
