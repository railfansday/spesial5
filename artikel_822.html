Murrell flourishing at high-flying Falkirk after 'random' move

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Jacob Murrell has hit the ground running with Falkirk since his move from the United States

"It was completely random," is how Jacob Murrell describes his summer switch from DC United to Falkirk.

Now the 22 year-old American is the joint-leading scorer in the Scottish Premiership, with eight goals from 11 starts.

His tremendous form has helped Falkirk surge up the table, with six successive victories taking them from 11th to third place.

"I was looking for more minutes as a young player and Falkirk kind of came out of nowhere," he explained. "They called my agent and a week later I was in Scotland.

"It happened really fast and it was a crazy path to get here. I was just desperate to play. I wasn't happy just being a training player.

"I wanted to win games and score goals and now both are happening."

A Maryland native, the 6ft, 3in centre forward played college football at Georgetown on his way to being a super draft pick for DC United in 2024.

A big Baltimore Ravens fan, Murrell is settling into his new surroundings, although he is wary of the cuisine, telling BBC Scotland he has no plans to try haggis or black pudding.

"The first month was difficult but the club supported me the whole time, making sure I was sound and pushing me to follow my dream.

"Team-mates, staff and fans have all been so good to me and made me feel at home in such a short period of time.

"I grew up north of Baltimore and I'd been in that hour-and-a-half DC radius my entire life with my friends and family, so leaving was really hard.

"Stepping out of my comfort zone has taught me a lot about myself, having to adapt and grow up."

Murrell, the league's player of the month for October, was one of 17 summer signings made by John McGlynn, who ended the club's 15-year absence from the top flight via back-to-back promotions.

"The manager has been so good for me, instilling confidence from day one," he said. "He backs me to score goals and be an influential part of the team."

A first visit to Ibrox is next up for Murrell as Falkirk take on second-placed Rangers on Saturday.

"I'm just taking it day by day, my next focus is Rangers and trying to make it seven on the bounce," he adds.

"Things can turn fast so we need to remember where we came from, stay humble and keep working hard."
