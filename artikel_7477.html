The Met Opera is using YouTube to look for a new generation of fans

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Timothee Chalamet may think that no one cares about opera, but one of the most prominent opera houses in the U.S. is betting that it can find new fans for a 430-year-old art form -- it just needs to look in the right place. New York's Metropolitan Opera is delivering a YouTube live stream that will provide open access to a performance of the new work Lincoln in the Bardo.

The Lincoln in the Bardo opera, which is based on a bestselling novel by George Saunders, had its world premiere at the Met Opera last month. Now, as the 143-year-old opera company looks to expand its fan base, it is hosting a Lincoln in the Bardo live stream on its official YouTube channel. The broadcast is set to go live on November 14 and will remain available until the 18th.
