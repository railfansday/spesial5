Steelers boost playoff chances with Week 12 win over Broncos

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Pittsburgh Steelers overcame a wild week of Aaron Rodgers hubbub to beat the Denver Broncos 27-5 on Black Friday and boost their playoff chances. Pittsburgh improved to 7-4 with the win while Denver dropped to 8-3 with the loss.

Heading into the game, most of the chatter was around the release of Aaron Rodgers. The Steelers released the QB after he blasted head coach Mike McCarthy on Wednesday. That meant Mason Rudolph was getting the start at home against Bo Nix and the favored Broncos. The Steelers won primarily due to their defense, scoring two defensive touchdowns while Nix struggled all afternoon.

Pittsburgh moved up the playoff standings with the win, climbing into sixth place for the time-being. They are a half game up on the Browns for the second wild card berth, pending the outcome of the Browns home game against the Raiders this weekend on Sunday Night Football. They moved to within a half game of the Bengals, who are hosting the Saints on Sunday.

Denver dropped into a tie with Kansas City in the AFC West standings. The Chiefs lost to the Bills on Thanksgiving and the Broncos lost an opportunity to take a full game lead on the Chiefs and negate the tiebreakers. However, for the time-being, Denver has the edge due to the common games tiebreaker. They are 4-0 while Kansas City is 3-1. The Bills will be a common game by the end of the season, but the Broncos haven't played them yet.

Denver remains in second place in the overall AFC standings for the time-being. If the Jaguars and Bengals both win as favorites on Sunday, Denver would drop into fourth place due to having the worst conference record of the three teams.
