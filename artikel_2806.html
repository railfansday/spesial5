Cleveland Browns news: A big nothingburger at the trade deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

It was a quiet day for the Cleveland Browns as the NFL trade deadline came and went on Tuesday afternoon.

Whether or not general manager Andrew Berry tried to bring anyone on board to help keep the team's playoff hopes alive is unknown.

And while there was speculation that the Minnesota Vikings, who were desperately in need of anyone with a pulse to play quarterback, might come calling about the greatest backup quarterback in NFL history, that did not come to pass as the Vikings instead decided to reunite with Kirk Cousins after three years apart.

Minnesota is returning to Cousins after cycling through eight different starting quarterbacks since the beginning of the 2024 season. If he starts this week against Green Bay, it will be the 15th time the Vikings have changed the starting quarterback from the previous week since moving on from Cousins.

Those numbers highlight something that has been evident for a long time now. While the Browns get more than their share of grief for continually messing up the quarterback position, they are far from being alone in the league in that regard.

And speaking of the Browns and quarterbacks, maybe today will be the day that head coach Todd Monken stops playing silly games and lets everyone know who the starting quarterback will be for this week's game against the Houston Texans.
