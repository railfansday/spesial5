LIVE DISCUSSION: Orlando Magic at Brooklyn Nets, 7:30 P.M. ET

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Nets managed to make ABA Night a painful experience.

With Julius Erving and members of the championship teams in the building, Brooklyn got blown out by 30 points against Chicago. Dr. J said he "fully anticipated the Nets to be so fired up having us here." He wasn't exactly wrong to expect more. Julius Randle called it one of the worst games of his 13-year career, and the Nets have now lost three straight by double figures.

They'll try to get back on track Friday against the Orlando Magic, who are also coming off a loss and will be without Paolo Banchero.

Friday brings a personal storyline. Franz Wagner will face his older brother, Moritz, for the first time as opponents after the latter spent the first five seasons of Franz's NBA career as his teammate in Orlando before signing with Brooklyn this summer.

"It's definitely going to be a little weird," Franz said. "We've obviously played in practice against each other. But I look at it as a huge blessing to be able to share the court together at this level. I'm really happy for him in the situation that he's in. I think me, Moe and the rest of the family will really enjoy the game as much as anything."
