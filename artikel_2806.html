Report: George Holani week-to-week with MCL sprain

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Seattle may not have one of its offensive and special teams contributors when the club returns from its Week 11 bye.

Per Ian Rapoport of NFL Network, running back George Holani suffered an MCL sprain during Sunday’s win over the Raiders.

He is considered week-to-week with the injury.

Because the Seahawks are on their bye, the club is hoping Holani will not need to be placed on injured reserve. But the club will evaluate the situation next week.

Holani has appeared in 10 games this season, recording 117 yards on 37 carries with a touchdown. He’s also caught 12 passes for 88 yards.
