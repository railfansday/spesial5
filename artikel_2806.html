Tennessee will face former Vol at Texas A&M

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

No. 9 Tennessee (7-2, 4-2 SEC) will travel to Texas A&M (4-5, 1-5 SEC) Saturday in Week 11 of the 2026 football season.

Kickoff between the Vols and Aggies is slated for 3 p.m. EST at Kyle Field in College Station Texas. ESPN will televise the game.

Tennessee will face former Vols' defensive back Rickey Gibson III. He played at Tennessee from 2023-25.

The junior, who is in his first season with the Aggies, has appeared in six games and totaled eight tackles.

Prior to transferring to Texas A&M, Gibson III appeared in 26 games for the Vols. The Trussville, Alabama native made 12 starts as a sophomore in 2024. He totaled 32 tackles and forced one fumble.

As a freshman in 2023, Gibson III appeared in 12 games. He recorded nine tackles and was a three-time member of the Fall SEC Academic Honor Roll (2023-25).

Gibson III started the season opener for the Vols against Syracuse in 2025. He played only 19 snaps before leaving the game. Gibson III missed the rest of the season, following Tennessee's, 45-26, victory at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta, Georgia.
