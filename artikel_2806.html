Falcons – Chiefs instant recap: It’s over

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Even in lost seasons, you wait for the Falcons to make it official. Atlanta had shown enough life in the recent past to think they might have a surprise or two left in them at 3-6, and so while I counseled that you should leave your expectations behind for the rest of 2026, I did indulge in a little hope. No longer.

This season is over. The 3-7 Falcons, who can do nothing on offense despite the presence of a top ten selection at quarterback in Michael Penix Jr., maybe the best running back in football in Bijan Robinson, a great receiver in Drake London, and a well-paid tight end room and offensive line, are cooked. Their defense is far more game and has the foundation of a really good unit, but as the afternoon wore on, they devolved into frustration and sloppiness. This is a team that is not ready to be great, and aside from a couple of beautiful weeks, they don't even look ready to be competent.

We know how this goes. The coaching staff is not getting fired after one year, the front office is just beginning to piece this team together, and there's still seven games to go. The Falcons must play out the string and try to figure out what they want to be, and it will be up to this still-fresh regime to make 2026 and the years preceding it a distant memory. We have put our hope in that outcome over and over again and have gotten nothing from it, so we are bucking history when we assume things will be different in 2027. What choice do we have, though?
