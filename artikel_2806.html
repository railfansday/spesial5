Cowboys news: Dallas offense is feeling the frustration

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Like last season, the Dallas Cowboys are one of the most disappointing teams in the NFL in 2026.

After hearing from Brian Schottenheimer, Christian Parker, Klayton Adams, and most of the key players this week, it seems like there is a lot of reflecting going on inside The Star currently. With the decimated Indianapolis Colts coming up this week in the noon window, the Cowboys are desperate for a win, not only for their playoff hopes, but for the locker room sanity.

The Cowboys locker room is clearly frustrated with what's going on. While the talking point this week has been "execution", the Cowboys coaching staff needs to hold up their end of the bargain as well on the offensive side of the football.

Over the last three weeks, the Cowboys have converted 38.5%, 61.4% and 37.5% on third-down plays. The ups and downs of their season are attributed to execution.

"It's hard to say we're pressing," quarterback Dak Prescott said. "But, yeah, when you've got the talent we've got, when you've got the expectation, the standards that we have for ourselves and you go three-and-out or you don't score a touchdown, yeah, that's frustrating and you've got to make sure that you don't allow that to continue to bleed because then that bleeds into guys making [mistakes] or not doing the details the right way because they want the big play rather than just sticking to it."

The Cowboys lack of identify on offense points directly to their head coach and play-caller. Each week they seem to bounce back and forth from a run-first team to a pass-heavy team. That lack of consistency may be a big reason the team has been so inconsistent so far this season.

While the numbers and league rankings for the Cowboys may be in the upper part of the league, Dallas still feels as though they haven't played their best as offense through eight games in 2026.

For Pro Bowl WR CeeDee Lamb, part of the core issue is staring back at the offense in the mirror.

"We stop ourselves," Lamb said. "That sh** is frustrating. It's annoying. I hate it. And then ultimately, we end up losing."

Christian Parker gives a brutally honest and correct assessment on cornerback DaRon Bland ahead of week nine. The Cowboys need a lot more out of Bland and Parker if they want to turn things around on defense during the second-half.

That's why defensive coordinator Christian Parker recently said he and Bland have discussed the need for improvement from the fifth-year defensive back.

"We need more from him. He knows that, we've had some good talks about that. I think he's battling. The frustrating thing for him and for us has been he's been close and contesting. Now, it's about the final piece of just finishing those plays," Parker said via Nick Harris of the Fort Worth Star-Telegram. "We just got to get back to making that consistent."

4. T.J. Bass Could Turn Depth Into Draft Capital

He signed a one-year, $5.76 million restricted free-agent tender in March. Dallas retained an experienced guard without securing his services beyond this season.

A team seeking immediate offensive line help could value him more than a team using him primarily as depth. If Dallas does not expect to bring him back, that difference creates an opportunity.
