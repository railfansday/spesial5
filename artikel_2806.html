Justin Jefferson: Vikings' offense didn't get it done, we've got to figure it out

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Vikings wide receiver Justin Jefferson has been visibly frustrated on the field the last two weeks as the offense hasn’t been able to move the ball in back-to-back losses. After Monday night’s loss to the Bills, he said the offense needs to stop squandering the work of a great defense.

“The offense didn’t get it done,” Jefferson said. “Defense did a phenomenal job once again, getting it done against a very good offense. We’ve got to figure it out on offense.”

Jefferson said everyone on the offense has to do better, although he admitted he doesn’t know all the answers.

“We’ve got to figure it out,” Jefferson said. “That’s something we don’t have the answer to at the moment. We’ve just got to figure it out.”

Although quarterback Carson Wentz was terrible on Monday night, and Kyler Murray hasn’t been much better for most of the season, Jefferson called it “an all 11 thing” and said it’s not just on the quarterback.

“It doesn’t really matter who’s throwing the ball, as long as it gets to us, that’s all that matters, and it’s up to us to make those plays. It’s tough. Definitely tough,” Jefferson said.

The Vikings’ offense has been tough to watch.
