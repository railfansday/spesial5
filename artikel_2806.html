The 76ers are fun again, so it's the return of Roob's 76ers Stats!

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The 76ers are fun again, so it's the return of Roob's 76ers Stats! originally appeared on NBC Sports Philadelphia

This 76ers team is a heck of a lot of fun to watch and fun basketball means fun stats!

At 13-3, the 76ers are exactly a month into the 5th-best start in franchise history and best since they were 14-2 in 2000-01. Which turned out to be a pretty good year. They were 15-1 in 1949-50 and 1966-67 and 14-2 in 1981-82.

To help everybody get through this interminable two-day gap between 76ers games, here's our first 2026-27 installment of Roob's 76ers Stats, with a tip of the hat to Stathead.

→ The 76ers have been out-rebounded by 34 through 16 games (719-685) and their 13-3 record matches the best in NBA history by a team that's been out-rebounded after 16 games. Three other teams have been 13-3 despite getting out-rebounded, most recently the 2013-14 Miami Heat. The others are the 1979-80 Bucks and 2012-2013 Spurs. The 76ers are an NBA-best 6-3 this year when they've been out-rebounded (which means they're 7-0 when they have a rebounding edge).

→ In the win over Orlando Thursday, V.J. Edgecombe had 24 points, eight rebounds, six assists and two steals, and Joel Embiid had 26 points, six rebounds, seven assists and two steals. It was the first time two 76ers had at least 24 points, six rebounds and assists and two steals in a game in 53 years. In a win over the Celtics at the Spectrum in 1973, Fred Carter had 31 points, six rebounds, six assists and seven steals and George McGinniss had 28 points, 16 rebounds, eight assists and three steals.

→ Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, a career 37 percent shooter from 3, is shooting 44 percent so far. Anfernee Simons, a career 38 percent shooter from 3, is shooting 41 percent. Jabari Walker, a career 33 percent 3-point shooter, is at 42 percent. Dean Wade, a career 37 percent shooter, is at 41 percent. Jaylen Brown, a career 36 percent shooter from range, is at 39 percent. The 76ers are shooting just 36 percent as a team from the stripe because James and Embiid have been so bad, but everybody else is off to career-best starts from 3-point range.

→ The 76ers are 7-1 on the road, equalling their best road record ever after eight games. They were also 7-1 in 1980-81, 1981-82 and 1982-83. The 1981 team had the best road start in franchise history, going 12-1 before losing its second road game.

→ With 15.7 points, 6.8 assists and 6.8 rebounds per game, LeBron James is only the fifth 76er ever to average at least 15 ½ points and 6 ½ assists and rebounds through 16 games. The others are some familiar names: Wilt Chamberlain in 1967, Charles Barkley in 1987, Ben Simmons in 2018 and James Harden in 2023.

→ The 76ers already have more wins this year after trailing by 10 points through the first quarter than all but two full seasons since 1950. They had four in 1985-86 and again in 2022-23. The Hawks also have three wins this year when trailing by double digits after the first quarter, but they're 3-4 and the 76ers are 3-2. So they actually have a winning record when they're down by double digits going into the second quarter.

→ With Jaylen Brown (22.6, 6.6), Joel Embiid (20.0, 8.3), LeBron James (15.7, 6.8) and V.J. Edgecombe (13.6, 6.5), the 76ers are the only NBA team with four players averaging 6 ½ rebounds and 13 points. The last time they had four players in a season who averaged 13 points and 6 ½ rebounds was 1961-62 when they were the Syracuse Nationals, with Dave Gambee (16.7, 7.9), Hal Greer (22.8, 7.4), Red Kerr (16.3, 14.7), Dolph Schayes (14.7, 7.8) and Lee Shaffer (16.9, 6.8).

→ Edgecombe's 6.5 rebounds per game are 2nd-most among shooting guards behind only the Grizzlies' Cedric Coward, who is at 8.5. Edgecombe's 6.5 is also 2nd-highest ever by a 76ers shooting guard behind only Greer's 7.4 during that 1961-62 season. Edgecombe averaged 5.6 rebounds as a rookie and his 5.8 career average is highest by a shooting guard who spent more than one year with the 76ers. Greer is second at 5.3. Among all NBA players who've played at least 90 games, Edgecombe's 5.8 average is 11th-highest all-time among shooting guards (although Stathead includes some swing guard-forwards in their data).

→ Joel Embiid's 14.6 shooting percentage from 3 is worst in NBA history after 16 games (minimum 40 attempts). Embiid is 6-for-41 from the stripe this year. The previous 3-point shooting low 16 games into a season belonged to Cedric Ceballos of the Lakers in 1995-96 and the 76ers' Evan Turner in 2013-14. Both were 6-for-43 for 16.3 percent.

→ LeBron James is at 22.2 percent from 3 on 10-for-45. The Sixers are the first NBA team in 12 years with two players under 23 percent after 16 games with a minimum of 40 attempts. In 2014 Tony Wroten was 10-for-50 (10 percent) and Turner was 6-for-43 (16.3 percent).
