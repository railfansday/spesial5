Cowboys injuries: Caleb Downs upgraded to limited

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

The Dallas Cowboys returned to the practice field on Thursday. They are looking to get healthy before this weekend when they will take on the Indianapolis Colts. Thursday's injury report yielded some developments. Here's what we know.

The Cowboys mat be down a few starters and rotation players for the game. Jake Ferguson (Ankle) and rookie Malachi Lawrence (Ankle) were unable to practice again today, with Lawrence almost certainly going to miss the game.

Safeties Caleb Downs (Concussion/Ankle), and PJ Locke (Concussion) got some work in today. Both were elevated to limited participants after sitting out yesterday.

As of today KaVontae Turpin is still out with a rib injury. Meanwhile, George Pickens (Hamstring), Luke Schoonmaker (Groin), and Tyler Smith (Ankle) were limited again today. Joey Porter Jr. (Ankle) was downgraded to limited participation, as was DeMarvion Overshown (Hamstring/Back).

In the good news, linebacker Dee Winters (Calf) was a full particpant.

For the Colts, Akeem Davis-Gaither (Knee) and Justin Walley (Knee) were not practicing Thursday. Andrew Ogletree (Knee) was upgraded to limited today.
