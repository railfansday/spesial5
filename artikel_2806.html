Mavericks vs Suns Preview: Closing out NBA Cup Group Play

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Dallas Mavericks (11-7) head out on the road Friday for their final game in Group play, with a matchup against the Phoenix Suns (9-8). Dallas has already clinched West Group A, going undefeated so far. However, as they're a team with bigger aspirations past the NBA Cup, they'll look to continue playing frenetic defense against a Suns team they've not played yet this season. Dallas is the winner of four straight, while the Suns roll into this one following a streak-snapping loss to the Houston Rockets on Wednesday.

Here are three things to consider prior to tip-off:

The Suns started the year 1-6, and before losing to Houston, they were winners of four straight to push them over .500. The staunch Houston defense paired with a rough shooting night (just 5 of 32 from three) resulted in a maddening loss to end their run. Booker in particular will be looking to score with more efficiency after shooting just 6 of 18 from the field (2 of 9 from distance). The Suns are a slightly above-average three-point shooting team (36%), so Dallas will need to chase them off the line.

The Suns are BAD on the glass, last in the league in terms of rebounds per game. The Mavericks are middling, but that might be more due to their defensive activity (think rotations, guys flying around) than the team actually being bad at rebounding. Dallas needs to control the defensive glass in this one and they might be able to run away with the score. The Mavericks have been scoring well in transition as of late, so leaning on the defense and ending Phoenix possessions should be a key to victory.

Through 15 games, the superstar guard is shooting 44.4% from the field. Were that number to hold through the season, it would be his lowest percentage of his career. Over the last 10 games, he's shooting 42% from the floor and 35% from beyond the arc. During this four game win streak, Irving's connecting on just 37% of his attempts. This is a long way of saying that Dallas is winning without Irving playing incredibly efficient basketball and be on the lookout for a breakout game where he can't miss. Maybe it will be against the Suns!

The game will be Mavs TV and KFAA channel 29 with tip off at 8:00 pm CST. 105.3 The Fan having the radio broadcast.
