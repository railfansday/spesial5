NFL Week 11 games: Eagles rooting guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The eleventh weekend of the 2026 NFL season is here!

Let's run through a Philadelphia Eagles-focused rooting guide for all of the Week 11 games.

The 2-7 Eagles have a 0.7% chance of making the playoffs, according to FTN Fantasy. They're +4000 to qualify for the postseason on FanDuel, which means you can bet $10 to win $400 if that happens.

In other words, it's time to be interested in the Eagles maximizing their positioning in the 2027 NFL Draft.

Current draft order of non-playoff teams via Tankathon:

I can understand fans not wanting the Birds to lose while the game is ongoing, or at all.

But the reality is that the 2026 campaign is lost and the Eagles stand to benefit beyond this season by losing and getting the highest draft pick as possible.

With a loss to the Steelers, the Eagles can potentially move as high as No. 2 overall.

INDIANAPOLIS COLTS defeated by HOUSTON TEXANS: The Colts lost on Thursday night, which wasn't ideal. They're a threat to move ahead of Philly in the draft order, especially since their weak strength of schedule will likely give them a tiebreaker over the Eagles if they finish with the same record. The silver lining to a Texans win is that it keeps Houston mathematically alive to win the AFC South. The Eagles would prefer to see the Texans win their division instead of the Jags, since Philly owns Jacksonville first-round pick.

JACKSONVILLE JAGUARS at NEW YORK GIANTS: Thanks to the Jordan Davis trade, the Eagles own the Jaguars' first-round pick in the 2027 NFL Draft. Therefore, the Eagles want the Jags to lose as much as possible. The "bonus" to a Giants win is that it helps to prevent any false hope of the Eagles winning their division. Root for the Giants.

TENNESSEE TITANS at DALLAS COWBOYS: Two reasons to root for the Titans here: 1) always great to see the Cowboys lose and 2) the Eagles need the Titans to win to have a chance to move ahead of them in the draft order.

BALTIMORE RAVENS at CAROLINA PANTHERS: Thanks to the Cam Jurgens trade, the Eagles own Baltimore's fifth-round pick in the 2027 NFL Draft. Therefore, the Eagles want the Ravens to lose as much as possible. Root for the Panthers.

MIAMI DOLPHINS at BUFFALO BILLS: The Eagles need the Dolphins to win to have a chance to move ahead of them in the draft order. Root for the Dolphins.

NEW ORLEANS SAINTS at CHICAGO BEARS: The Saints are the bigger threat to move ahead of the Eagles in the draft order. Root for the Saints.

TAMPA BAY BUCCANEERS at DETROIT LIONS: The Bucs are the bigger threat to move ahead of the Eagles in the draft order. Root for the Buccaneers.

ARIZONA CARDINALS at KANSAS CITY CHIEFS: The Cards are the bigger threat to move ahead of the Eagles in the draft order. Root for the Cardinals.

NEW YORK JETS at LOS ANGELES CHARGERS: The good news for the Eagles is that one of these two teams currently ahead of them in the draft order will win (barring a tie). Looking at the remaining schedules for these teams (which both rank near the middle of the pack in terms of difficulty), I think the Chargers are actually more likely to win more games after this week. So, it could be good to see the Jets capitalize on a more rare opportunity for them to win. Root for the Jets.

LAS VEGAS RAIDERS at DENVER BRONCOS: The Raiders are the bigger threat to move ahead of the Eagles in the draft order. Root for the Raiders.

MINNESOTA VIKINGS at SAN FRANCISCO 49ERS: The Whiners are the less likable team, in my view. But there's a case to be made the Eagles should want the Packers to win the NFC North (and, ultimately, the Super Bowl) since the Cowboys own Green Bay's first-round pick. Root for the 49ers.

CINCINNATI BENGALS at WASHINGTON COMMANDERS: The Commanders are the bigger threat to move ahead of the Eagles in the draft order. Root for the Commanders.
