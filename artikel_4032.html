Xbox Wants You to Revisit Halo: Campaign Evolved on PS5 as Free Co-Op Mode Update Is Announced

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

A major free update for Halo: Campaign Evolved has been announced, and it's bringing Firefight back with a bang.

For the uninitiated, Firefight was always the series' go-to co-op mode, allowing Spartans to team up and hold off waves of bloodthirsty opponents together.

And yes, the update's going to be totally free for all existing players. It should add some serious value to the overall Campaign Evolved package.

The update's due out "this winter", according to Halo Studios' Tahir Hasangjekaj. Details will be revealed at this year's Halo Fest, which is happening next month, on the 12th and 13th December.

What's more, this fresh iteration of Firefight will actually be playable at Xbox FanFest tonight. So if you're interested in early impressions, keep an eye out for what attendees have to say.

By almost all accounts, Campaign Evolved has struggled commercially following its release back in July. As such, it'll be interesting to see if this update prompts a spike in users.

Similarly interesting is that this new Firefight could end up being the last Halo Studios-led production, given that the developer's basically been transformed into a support team at this point.

Will this update bring you back to Halo: Campaign Evolved? Might you and a few friends be up for the new Firefight? Lock and load in the comments section below.
