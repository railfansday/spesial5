Game Informer's Reader Choice Awards 2026 Are Now Open

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

We here at Game Informer have written about some amazing games this year, but it's time for us to step aside and you to take center stage! The wait is over – it's time to vote for your favorite games of 2026 in Game Informer's annual Reader Choice Awards! This year's vote is sponsored by Star Wars Unlimited, A Trading Card Game.

Whether you're a fan of indie games like Dressmaker, Queen's Domain, or Esoteric Ebb; multiplayer hits like Meccha Chameleon or World of Warcraft: Forever; or you're rooting for the heavy hitters like Resident Evil Requiem, Ocarina of Time, or Grand Theft Auto VI, this is the place to make your mark. You can rank up to five games that you think are the best of the year and help to shape our biggest vote yet.

You'll also get prizes this year just for voting. Here's how:

All you need to do to vote is head over to gameinformer.com/ReaderVote2026. In addition to our usual Best Video Game category, we have five additional fields to vote for this year: Best Tabletop, Best Upcoming Game, Best Fan Art, Best Review and What Award is Missing. You can find the category descriptions for each over on the voting portal. Today, just the first two categories are open, but check back in over the coming weeks as we keep the votes going and open up more polls.

The best part? Voting in certain categories will grant you $5 off your next subscription to Game Informer – it's a no-brainer for fans, and a great excuse to introduce your family and friends to our website, magazine, or app! Additionally, you'll be entered into one of our giveaways. Voting closes on December 21 at 11:59 PM PT; the next day, we'll host a livestream announcing the winners of each category and the recipients of each giveaway.

Head to gameinformer.com/ReaderVote2026 to vote today.
