The 8BitDo 64 was a loose remake of the Nintendo 64’s unusual triple-grip controller that did away with that gamepad’s centered analog stick. But with its new GC Bluetooth Gamepad, 8BitDo has done a much better job at staying faithful to the design of the Nintendo GameCube’s excellent controllers, including their unique button layout, thumbstick design, and shoulder buttons.

The 8BitDo GC Bluetooth Gamepad is available for preorder starting today for $49.99 in orange or purple and is expected to start shipping on December 10th. That’s much cheaper than Nintendo’s $69.99 wireless remake of the GameCube controller, although 8BitDo’s version isn’t an exact copy of the original’s shape and ergonomics. If that’s important to you, and you have your GameCube’s original accessories on hand, you can still buy 8BitDo’s $25.98 upgrade kit, which is a cheaper way to make it compatible with modern consoles and devices.

8BitDo’s GameCube redux carries forward the original’s octagonal gates around the base of each thumbstick, making it well-suited to playing fighting games like Super Smash Bros. Ultimate. And while Nintendo continues to skip modern thumbstick technologies, the 8BitDo GC features both drift-resistant TMR thumbsticks and Hall effect linear triggers.

The controller connects to all versions of the Nintendo Switch over Bluetooth, as well as PCs or handheld consoles running Windows. It also comes with a 2.4GHz wireless USB receiver for players who prefer a connection with lower latency, or you can connect it with a USB-C cable for the best possible performance.
