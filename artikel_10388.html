A fashion reporter’s favorite things of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

I’ll admit it. Being a fashion reporter can be a lot of fun. I get to sit across from some the most brilliant minds in the industry and peek at gorgeous collections seasons before they hit stores—long before they start shaping what we will wear.

But what most people don’t realize is that the job also involves a lot of squinting at fiber-content labels. I’ve asked founders extremely nerdy questions about leather tanning methods. I’ve toured factories and tromped through cotton fields.

Along the way, I come across many products. Over the course of a year, I wander through dozens of stores, wade through stacks of applications from brands hoping to land on our annual lists, and put bold new product claims to the test.

Most of it is forgettable. But every so often, I come across something that I love: a clever jacket that is also beautiful, heels you can actually walk in all day, a pair of sneakers with a story so moving I still think about it months later. This list is the best of what I found in a year of poking, prodding, and road testing. If you’re shopping for someone who cares about style and quality, these are good bets.

Every Stitch Tells a Story
Resolute RGL, Najla in Olive Green, $350

Of the many products I reviewed this year, this is the one that made a biggest impression. Each Resolute sneaker carries a hand-embroidered panel of tatreez, the Palestinian cross embroidery that has passed down through generations. Each panel is stitched by a woman who lives in the villages in the South Hebron Hills of the West Bank. She chooses the pattern and colors herself, and a panel takes 14 to 24 hours to complete. The cooperative co-owns the company, and embroiderers are paid as soon as their panels are finished. The shoes themselves are lovely, with an olive suede upper, memory foam insoles, and recycled rubber soles. It usually takes several weeks to make the shoes to order, but for the holidays, Resolute has prepared inventory so all products can be shipped within two days.

Light as a Feather, Tough Enough for a Nor’easter
Cole Haan, Grand Flex Fenella Buckle Lace-Up Waterproof Booties, $240

Most winter boots are heavy, adding weight as you trudge through the snow. Cole Haan has engineered these boots to be lightweight, but still set on a chunky lug sole that provides traction on icy sidewalks. They’re made from waterproof leather with sealed seams and a foam footbed that keep them cushy through a full day of walking. The buckle and laces give them a little edge, and a hidden ankle zip means you don’t have to do the laces again every time you leave the house.

Mary Poppins Would Be Into This
Parker Thatch, Jack Tote in Leopard, $1,250

I am a person who carries too much: laptop, a novel, a snack, and a backup snack for my kids. The Jack allows overpackers to carry what they need for the day without looking like a duffel bag. It’s made from leopard-print hair-on cowhide with black leather trim and handles, and it fits a laptop with room to spare. There’s an interior zip pocket, two open pockets so your phone and glasses don’t get lost, and a snap closure. It’s the ideal gift for a professional woman in the market for a power bag.

In-Law-Approved Loungewear
Lake, DreamModal Kimono Three-Piece Pants Set in Velvet Green Pencil Stripe, $168

The holidays involve lots of overnight guests, which can pose a dilemma. You want to be cozy, but you don’t want your in-laws to see you in your slinky nightgown or threadbare college T-shirt. Lake has many lounge sets. This one includes a wrap top with bell sleeves, a scoop-neck tank, and wide-leg pants with an elastic waist. It’s all made from a silky modal fiber, so it feels like you’re wearing pajamas but it looks like you’re in an outfit. The green and white stripe is festive without being kitschy. It’s machine washable, too.

Yes, You Can Walk in These Heels
Margaux, The Paloma Platform in Gold Nappa, $395

For years, most women assumed that “comfortable heels” is an oxymoron. Margaux is working hard to prove them wrong. The Paloma shoe has a 3.5-inch block heel, with a 1-inch platform, allowing you get the height without feeling unstable. There is plush foam padding under the sole and the straps are positioned so they don’t pinch. They’re handmade of nappa leather in Spain. I like the gold ones because they go with every holiday outfit.

Honey, I Shrunk the Handbag
Maison de Sabré, The Mini Palais in Candy Plum, $675

Some nights call for nothing more than a phone, a lipstick, and a credit card. The Mini Palais is just big enough for these things, and its adjustable strap lets you wear it crossbody, on the shoulder, or in hand. The bags are also sustainable. Maison de Sabré skips conventional tanning, which is heavy on chemicals, in favor of DriTan, a waterless tanning technology. Each bag carries a serial number that allows you to trace it back to tanneries and farms in the Netherlands. And you can dress the bag up with adorable charms made from scraps of colorful leather discarded on the factory floor.

A Glamorous New Design from Away

Away, The Aluminum Edge Carry-On, $428

Aluminum luggage has a certain glamour, but it’s heavy. Away’s new Aluminum Edge gives you a touch of aluminum on the frame and corners, but constructs the body out of a lighter polycarbonate shell that has a leather-like finish. It is full of luxurious touches, like champagne or nickel hardware. It closes with a lock, instead of a zipper, and it has a leather top handle. It comes in four colors, including a fun pink.

Twelve Days of Clean Beauty
Credo Beauty, Not Your Average Advent Calendar, $175

Credo, a retailer that stocks only nontoxic beauty brands, launched an Advent calendar full of 12 full-size bestsellers (worth $445 in products) inside a blue beauty case. Some of my favorite products inside include a Westman Atelier lipstick, Saie’s mascara, and Nécessaire’s hand cream. There’s no waste in this Advent calendar. You’ll carry the toiletry bag around for years to come.

Swimwear for a Winter Escape
Minnow, Women’s Marigold Floral V-Neck One Piece, $218, and Marigold Floral Pareo, $128

If you’re lucky enough to be headed somewhere warm over the holidays, or you’re already dreaming about next year’s beach trip, this is what I’d pack. Minnow is a resort brand that makes thoughtfully designed swimwear. This summery set provides substantial coverage, while staying put so you can enjoy the pool with your kids. The matching pareo means you can wear the outfit to your hotel’s buffet breakfast before lounging poolside. (And if the whole family is coming along, the suit can be coordinated with matching styles for everybody.)

A Functional, Beautiful Jacket
Overland, Kylie Reversible Sheepskin Jacket, $1,195

If you read my coats roundup this fall, you’ll recognize this jacket, made by Overland, which has been making sheepskin coats since 1973. I’m bringing it back because of all the jackets I tested this year, this one stood out. One side is a sleek leather jacket that has a strip of shearling peeking out at the collar. If you flip it inside out, it transforms into a plush shearling jacket. The pockets work from either side. It’s built to last, too, since sheepskin can last a lifetime with proper care.

A Blanket-Like Scarf
White + Warren, Cashmere Travel Wrap in Antique Gold, $385

Cashmere scarves are a traditional holiday gift, since they’re something anybody can appreciate. But this one stands out. It’s made of 100% cashmere but at almost 7 feet long, it works as a scarf, an evening wrap, or a full-body blanket on a red-eye. It can squish into a tote bag, but when you wear it, it feels substantial. It comes in a rainbow of colors; my favorite is the antique gold.

For the Bag Obsessive Who Has Everything
Assouline, Fashionphile: The Book of Iconic Bags, $120

Here’s a gift for the bag obsessive. Fashionphile, the luxury resale platform, knows exactly which bags hold their value and why. It has teamed up with Assouline to produce a silk-covered, 280-page tome about the 25 most influential handbags of all time, from the Hermès Birkin to the Fendi Baguette and the Dior Saddle, plus newer icons from Bottega Veneta and Loewe. It has more than 200 illustrations that will be eye candy to fashionistas.

Three Earrings in One
ME+EM, Transformer Earrings, $495

This piece of jewelry magically transforms to suit your outfit. The earrings start as gold-plated studs for the office. Clip on the detachable pendant and they become drops for dinner. The pendant is double-sided, with black marbled resin on one side and gold on the other, so you can flip it to suit your mood or your outfit. They’re made in Italy, and they work as well with a turtleneck as with a cocktail dress.
