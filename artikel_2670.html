Clemson’s Chris Denson talks future position, decision to not just play QB

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Chris Denson spent the season practicing at wide receiver. On Friday night at Duke, he took snaps at quarterback and scored a fourth-quarter touchdown that helped Clemson pull off a 20-10 upset.

The Tigers turned to Denson after injuries sidelined Tait Reynolds and Christopher Vizzina. Former walk-on Trent Pearman made his first career start, but Clemson got Denson involved early. He picked up seven yards on his first carry and finished with 71 on the ground, including a seven-yard touchdown in the fourth quarter.

It was a reminder of what Denson did the last time he got an extended look at quarterback. During his redshirt season, the left-hander rushed for more than 100 yards against Furman and completed all four of his passes for 22 yards. Denson, who flipped his commitment from Coastal Carolina late in the 2025 recruiting cycle, entered the offseason with some momentum after that performance.

Reynolds emerged as Vizzina's top challenger in the spring, and Clemson began looking for other ways to get Denson on the field. He tried running back and wide receiver before deciding he had a better future catching passes. He stayed in the receiver room until Clemson needed him back at quarterback ahead of the Duke game.

The Tigers have another reason to keep Denson involved Saturday against South Carolina. His work on the ground was a big part of how Clemson moved the ball in Durham, and the Gamecocks now have to prepare for both him and Pearman.

Denson expects to move back to receiver or another skill position after the season. For now, Clemson has a rivalry game to play and a second quarterback who showed last week how much he can do with a handful of carries.
