NBA fans love Steph Curry’s reaction to Draymond Green’s three-point barrage after social media spat

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Golden State Warriors forward Draymond Green reminded fans that he can still be an impactful offensive player in their 88-87 win over the Boston Celtics.

Green hit three three-pointers in the first quarter of the game after spending hours before the game arguing with former NBA player-turned-commentator Eddie Johnson on social media.

Johnson criticised Green's ability to impact the Warriors' offence without Stephen Curry on the court, and the way Green started the game against the Celtics, it was clear he was trying to send a message. Funnily, Curry seemed to be loving the action more than anyone else.

After Green nailed his third three-pointer in the first quarter, Curry reacted to the same by sliding out of his chair on the Warriors bench with his arms in the air, giving an overly dramatic reaction to his 36-year-old teammate's offensive burst.

"Jumpshot8 (Eddie Johnson), you got him motivated tonight," said one fan.

"Just to stick it to Eddie?" questioned a fan.

"Draymond hitting 3 triples in a quarter is not real life. Steph jumping out of his chair says it all," were the thoughts of one fan.

"Eddie Johnson got dog to showcase some offensive talent for the first time in 86 years," joked a fan.

"This is about to be my go-to meme for the rest of the year!" said another fan.

Green went scoreless for the rest of the game, ending with nine points, six rebounds and three assists. His biggest contribution came on the final play of the game, locking Jayson Tatum up on the perimeter and forcing the 28-year-old to miss on a potential game-winning shot.

Green is averaging 3.6 points, 4.8 rebounds and 6.3 assists this season. He will never be a major offensive contributor, but his defence and the other small things he can do on the court maximise his impact when Curry's on the court.

With Curry expected to miss another few games with his oblique injury, the 9-8 Warriors will hope to get some more big performances from Green to keep them afloat.
