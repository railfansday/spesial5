Why Jets’ Breece Hall might become a sudden trade candidate amid rumors of another star RB getting dealt

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Why Jets' Breece Hall might become a sudden trade candidate amid rumors of another star RB getting dealt originally appeared on The Sporting News. Add The Sporting News as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

With all the trade rumors surrounding Indianapolis Colts star running back Jonathan Taylor, it raises the question of which teams are going to miss out on him. There's a sense around the league that Taylor will be traded in the next couple of days before the NFL trade deadline, and there's a strong argument that he'll be the biggest difference-maker in football to be dealt as an offensive player.

However, other guys could make sense to trade for because teams could get big returns if they miss out on Taylor.

With a team like the New York Jets holding Breece Hall, Adam Schefter of ESPN recently suggested it's possible teams will call the Jets to see what they want for their star running back.

"What about Breece Hall? Again, Aaron Glenn said Monday that the Jets aren't looking to trade him. The Vikings, Giants, Broncos, Packers and others are interested in Jonathan Taylor. They're not all getting Jonathan Taylor. One team might get him. So what happens if and when Taylor gets traded? Who's the next running back teams are going to call about? Breece Hall," Schefter said, H/T Heavy.

"And Braelon Allen has emerged. Again, the Jets don't want to trade Hall and aren't planning to trade him. I get it. But I'm going to predict the Jets will get calls about Breece Hall."

There are obvious differences between Hall and Taylor, mainly that Hall is younger. It's also fair to mention that Hall has dealt with injuries throughout his career, including this season.

However, his talent is undeniable. For the Jets, it's fair to question why they wouldn't consider trading him if they can get the right return. Over the past couple of years, the Jets have proven they're not ready to compete yet, and that's unlikely to change whether Hall is on the roster or not.
