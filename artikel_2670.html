Brock Purdy out of practice Wednesday with ankle injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The 49ers will practice without their starting quarterback on Wednesday.

Head coach Kyle Shanahan said that Purdy will miss practice after hurting his ankle in the second quarter of last Sunday night’s win over the Vikings. Shanahan said the injury is not a high-ankle sprain and that Purdy is considered day-to-day heading into this Sunday’s game against the Seahawks.

When asked if there’s a chance Purdy could miss that game, Shanahan said that his absence from practice means that is a possibility. That likelihood will rise if Purdy continues to miss practices in the coming days.

Mac Jones is the backup quarterback for the 49ers. He started eight games last season, but has only made three relief appearances this season.
