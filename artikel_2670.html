Chargers list Ladd McConkey as questionable for Monday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Wide receiver Ladd McConkey is among the Chargers players listed as questionable for Monday night’s game against the Ravens.

McConkey is dealing with a back injury that kept him off of the practice field this week. McConkey has dealt with a number of injuries this season and has 17 catches for 286 yards and a touchdown on the season.

The Chargers also listed guard Kayode Awosika (fibula), defensive lineman Justin Eboigbe (ankle), tight end David Njoku (fibula), linebacker Denzel Perryman (pectoral), and offensive lineman Trey Pipkins (personal, knee) as questionable. Njoku, Perryman, and Pipkins will need to be activated from injured reserve in order to play against Baltimore.

Wide receiver Brenen Thompson (hamstring) is considered doubtful while guard Branson Taylor (ankle), cornerback Isas Waxter (ankle), and safety Kendall Williamson (calf) have been ruled out.
