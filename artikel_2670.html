Max Hurleman remains on Steelers' practice squad, available to other teams

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers’ swipe at the team’s front office about wide receiver Max Hurleman pointed out something all 31 other teams should be considering: There’s a talented, cheap receiver and special teams player available to be signed off the Steelers’ practice squad.

Hurleman got the first regular-season action of his career on Sunday against the Eagles and was just about the only bright spot in an otherwise dreary offensive performance for Pittsburgh. Hurleman was the Steelers’ leading receiver with six catches for 61 yards, and he scored the Steelers’ only touchdown. Hurleman also played 10 special teams snaps and returned three punts for 36 yards and two kickoffs for 32 yards.

Rodgers said Hurleman should have been on the Steelers’ 53-player roster all along, but instead he has been on the practice squad all season, and reverted back to the practice squad after Sunday’s game. Rodgers made a valid point.

A player who can come in and produce like that, while costing next to nothing against the salary cap, is a valuable commodity in the NFL. And any one of the 31 other NFL teams can offer Hurleman a spot on its 53-player roster right now, without having to give the Steelers anything in return.

It would be surprising if Hurleman remains on the Steelers’ practice squad for the rest of the season. Either the Steelers will put him on their active roster, or some other team will.
