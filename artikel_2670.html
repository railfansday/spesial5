The Titans need more from 3 defenders vs. Cowboys in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The Tennessee Titans defense has fallen apart since trading Jeffery Simmons and Amani Hooker. Over the previous three losses to the Browns, Bengals, and Jaguars, they've allowed an average of 36.6 points and 486.6 yards per game. Robert Saleh's unit doesn't have enough talent, and has also struggled to acclimate to his scheme.

The Dallas Cowboys represent a terrific challenge in Week 11. They are averaging an explosive 32.3 points per game across that same Titans sample size. We've identified three Titans defenders who need to play significantly better versus the Cowboys.

We're not convinced Alontae Taylor shouldn't be benched versus the Cowboys. If he plays nickelback over Marcus Harris again, he needs to be significantly better than he was against the Jaguars. PFF credited Taylor with allowing seven receptions (via seven targets) in coverage for 143 yards. He was a total turnstile in coverage. The Cowboys' high-octane passing attack will target him if Coach Saleh leaves him in the lineup.

The Jeffery Simmons trade placed an even bigger spotlight on John Franklin-Myers, who Coach Saleh reiterates is playing through a difficult back injury. The Titans signed JFM to a $63 million contract in the offseason and they need more production than they've gotten. He's been credited with 17 pressures and two sacks this season. Making Cowboys QB Dak Prescott uncomfortable in the pocket will be critical.

There was a generous amount of offseason hype surrounding safety Kevin Winston Jr. Thus far, he's performing like a league-average starter. That doesn't make the former Penn State standout a Mike Borgonzi bust, but we all assumed he'd show more playmaking ability. That hasn't been the case. Winston hasn't registered a single interception or pass breakup, nor has he recorded a sack. There's a bigger spotlight on No. 23 following the Amani Hooker trade.
