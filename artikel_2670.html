Browns WR Denzel Boston is expected back at practice Thursday

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Browns practiced without wide receiver Denzel Boston on Wednesday, but they don't expect his absence to be an extended one.

Boston is listed with a toe injury and head coach Todd Monken said at his pre-practice press conference that the second-round pick is expected to be back on the field on Thursday.

"I wouldn't say it's a concern," Monken said, via a transcript from the team. "Obviously, I anticipate him being out today. He's got a little toe issue, but we anticipate him being back tomorrow."

Fullback Michael Burton (calf), safety Ronnie Hickman (concussion), and center Elgton Jenkins (knee) were also out of practice. Offensive tackle Austin Barber (calf), defensive tackle Maliek Collins (elbow), tight end Harold Fannin (groin), right tackle Tytus Howard (knee), linebacker Carson Schwesinger (wrist, ankle), cornerback Denzel Ward (quad), and linebacker Quincy Williams (hamstring) were the team's limited participants.
