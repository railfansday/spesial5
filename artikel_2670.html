Shai-Gilgeous Alexander, Giannis Antetokounmpo ruled out of Sunday's Thunder-Heat game

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Sunday's game between the Oklahoma City Thunder and Miami Heat will be missing some serious star power.

The Heat ruled out Giannis Antetokounmpo out before tip-off with a groin injury. The missed game will be the first for Antetokounmpo since the former two-time MVP joined the Heat in the offseason.

The Thunder, meanwhile, ruled out reigning two-time MVP Shai Gilgeous-Alexander with a lower back contusion and neck strain. The long-term outlook for both stars wasn't immediately clear. Both players started the day listed as questionable.

Antetokounmpo first surfaced on the injury report with his groin injury on Saturday before being ruled out on Sunday. It's not clear when he sustained it.

The Thunder are playing the first game of a back-to-back, but OKC head coach Mark Daigneault said pregame that the schedule didn't play a factor in the decision for Gilgeous-Alexander to sit. He cited "a really hard foul" of Gilgeous-Alexander in Friday's Thunder win over the Pelicans.

"It wouldn't be wise for us to put him on the court tonight regardless, based on how he's feeling," Daigneault said.

Gilgeous-Alexander joins a long list of injuries to critical Thunder players. Aday Mara (shin contusion), Alex Caruso (hip), Jaylin Williams (back) and Nikola Topić (hip) are also out against the Heat, joining Chet Holmgren (knee), Cason Wallace (shoulder) and Jaylen Williams (ankle), who are all sidelined with long-term injuries.

The Thunder will start Ajay Mitchell, Jared McCain, Micah Peavy, Kenrich Williams and Isaiah Hartenstein against the Heat. Bobby Portis Jr. will step into the starting lineup for Miami in place of Antetokounmpo.

Antetokounmpo and Gilgeous-Alexander are both playing at an All-NBA level this season. In 15 games, Antetokounmpo is averaging 28.2 points, 12.6 rebounds, 5.5 assists and 1.3 blocks while shooting 60.2% from the field. The Heat entered Sunday at 9-6, in sixth place in the East.

Gilgeous-Alexander is averaging 31.7 points, 4.5 assists and 4.1 rebounds through 16 games while shooting 48.3% from the floor and 37.3% from 3. The injury-ravaged Thunder entered Sunday at 10-6, in eighth place in the West.
