Lions score nine quick points before halftime, lead Bears 19-10

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Lions and Bears were tied at the two-minute warning of Thursday’s game in Detroit, but things swung in the home team’s favor before the halftime break.

Defensive end Derrick Moore forced Bears quarterback Caleb Williams to fumble on a sack in the end zone and Bears tackle Darnell Wright was flagged for batting the ball back into the field of play. That’s a safety and Lions head coach Dan Campbell opted to accept that rather than decline the penalty and take the ball on the Chicago 14-yard-line.

That choice paid off with more points for the Lions. Jared Goff and Jameson Williams hooked up for a 39-yard gain to move the ball deep into Bears territory and Goff found tight end Sam LaPorta for an 11-yard touchdown with six seconds to play in the half. That pushed Detroit’s lead to 19-10 at halftime.

The Bears will get the ball coming out of the locker room, but the two scores make for a big swing in momentum after the Bears erased a 10-point deficit earlier in the second quarter.

Goff is 13-of-22 for 193 yards and Jahmyr Gibbs has 98 yards from scrimmage. Gibbs also scored his 16th touchdown of the season earlier in the game.

The Bears lost tight end Colston Loveland to a knee injury early in the game, so he won’t be part of any comeback effort that Chicago is able to put together in the final 30 minutes.
