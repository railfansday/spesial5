Bruno Fernandes rejects second Manchester United contract offer

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Bruno Fernandes has turned down Manchester United’s second contract offer since talks began in the summer. The Athletic reports that the two sides expect to meet again, with the captain’s salary and the length of the deal still to be settled.

Fernandes, 32, is under contract through June 2027. The deal he signed in August 2024 gives United the option to keep him for another year. If the club leaves that option unused and talks continue into January, Fernandes can negotiate a free transfer with clubs outside England for the following summer.

Friday’s appointment of Mauricio Pochettino brings Fernandes together with a coach who tried to sign him seven years ago. Pochettino, who succeeds Michael Carrick, wanted the midfielder at Tottenham in 2019. Fernandes later said he had wanted the move, too, but Sporting turned down Tottenham’s offer. He joined United the following January.
