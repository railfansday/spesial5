Rice on England’s win over Spain: We knew we were going to beat them

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Declan Rice has claimed England had no doubt they were going to beat Spain ahead of Sunday's showdown in Madrid.

England turned in arguably their most impressive display of the Thomas Tuchel era at the Metropolitano, where they won 3-2 to snap Spain's 43-game unbeaten streak.

Harry Kane deservedly put England ahead before half-time with his 15th international goal of 2026, before Ezri Konsa and Ben White scored in quick succession early on in the second half.

Yet Jarell Quansah's slip-up led to Elliot Anderson giving away a penalty and seeing red for a last-man foul on Lamine Yamal. Up until that moment, Spain had created chances worth just 0.2 expected goals to England's 2.3 xG.

Mikel Oyarzabal scored from the spot, and Yamal then teed up Nico Williams to drag Spain to within one goal. England, though, stood firm, with James Trafford making a string of impressive stops to see out a win that means the Three Lions topped Nations League Group A3.

And Rice revealed England were sure of victory from the off.

He told ITV Sport: "We knew we were going to beat them. The message from the manager after the Croatia game has been so positive.

"The first 10 minutes were the best we've played. Overall, 10 men for half an hour, winning 3-2 against a team unbeaten in 43 is massive.

"Argentina that day [at the World Cup] wasn't meant to be. That's football. When they [Spain] got a second, maybe it could have gone another way.

England have won 10 consecutive away games in all competitions – the first time they have ever won 10 in a row on their travels in their history.

Both of England's last two away games against Spain in all competitions have seen the Three Lions win 3-2 (also in October 2018). It makes England the first nation to win successive visits to La Roja since Yugoslavia in September 1988 (three in a row).

White, who is back in the fold under Tuchel after opting out of the Three Lions set-up under Gareth Southgate, made an impact at both ends, grabbing what proved to be the winner but also making a superb tackle on Williams at 3-2.

"It was pretty incredible," he told BBC Radio 5Live.

"From start to finish, everyone did so well. Probably should have been a bigger margin of goals, but honestly, an incredible performance.

"Obviously, everything doesn't go to plan in football. It's about these moments where you react and stay together, and that's what we did."
