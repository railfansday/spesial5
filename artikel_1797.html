Leeds United’s Largie Ramazani continues Burnley scoring streak with derby stunner against Blackburn Rovers

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Leeds United winger Largie Ramazani continued his scoring streak with a stunner for loan club Burnley in their 2-2 derby draw with Blackburn Rovers yesterday (November 22).

The 25-year-old was deemed surplus to requirements at Elland Road in the summer after spending the 2025/26 season out on loan at Valencia.

He was sent back to to the Championship to link up with Burnley, who have an obligation to make his move a permanent one at the end of the season.

Ramazani has taken to life back in the second tier like a duck to water, establishing himself as a key figure for the struggling Clarets.

He scored his third goal in as many games yesterday (November 22), opening the scoring at Turf Moor with a stylish strike after cutting in from the right.

The Belgian wideman has now registered five goals across 13 appearances for Burnley, who have been leaning heavily on his flair and penchant for the spectacular.

Jaydon Banel restored the Burnley advantage after Andri Gudjohnsen equalised, but Todd Cantwell secured a point for Blackburn from the penalty spot.

Ramazani joined Leeds from Almeria in the summer of 2024, costing the Whites a reported £10m. He helped the Whites clinch the Championship title in his maiden campaign, although found it tough to retain his spot in Daniel Farke’s starting XI.

In September, ex-Whites winger Jermaine Pennant suggested Ramazani could prove Leeds wrong.

He said: “He can [prove Leeds wrong]. I think he was very important in the Championship run when he was there. He’s a decent player, a good player, and he just needed game time.

“It’s very difficult when you haven’t got bags and bags of game time to really get into a rhythm. We all need that. It doesn’t matter how good you are - if you’re playing little dribs and drabs of games here and there, you’re not going to be at your peak. You need continuity of games.

“With a new lease of life and a fresh start, playing week in, week out, I’m sure he can prove the doubters wrong at Leeds and go, ‘Look, this is what you maybe could have had’. But Leeds have got top players as well now, and they’ve invested well.”
