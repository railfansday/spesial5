Bradford City v Barnsley FC: Patrick Kelly tells Reds to stop using youth as an excuse

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Whereas the Bantams’ squad is built around players who have been around the block, Barnsley's is the complete opposite.

Kelly, one of eight starters below the age of 23, wore the captain's armband in Friday's 2-1 defeat at Sheffield Wednesday, although 31-year-old midfielder Cameron McGeehan is almost certain to return from suspension at Valley Parade.

The callowness of the Reds has been exposed at times this season but as part of a young Northern Ireland team which has had an exceptional start to the season, winning promotion to the top division of the Nations League, Kelly is loathe to use that as an excuse.

"The Northern Ireland team is young, but it's a really experienced team," stressed former Doncaster Rovers player Kelly. "I think if you look at the amount of caps some of the lads have, the Charles brothers (former Sheffield Wednesday players Shea and Pierce) are 23 and 21, but they've played (a combined 57) international games.

"Here it's a lot of lads' first seasons in the EFL, which is completely different.

"But I think young players are fearless and we probably do need to stop looking at us as a young team.

"I don't actually think that's the reason why we're losing so many games. It's nothing to do with that, I think."

Whilst the age of the squad probably is a bigger factor than Kelly will admit, he is right that there is no point falling back on it as that will not change this side of the January transfer window at the very earliest.

What Barnsley’s strategy is meant to do is accelerate the development of young players, and Kelly feels wearing the captain's armband – or even taking on more responsibility when others do – is enhancing his game.

"I really enjoy it," he said. "It was a bit different for me coming back this season. From the very start, it was just a lot of a younger changing room.

"I felt really older when I came back compared to when I joined here last year. But it'll be really good for me in the future. I'm quite enjoying it.

"I'm looking up to Carm a lot, the captain of the group. I speak with him nearly every day. He's really helping me."

But the focus just has to be on securing a first win in 11 league games.

"We've got a chance to try and bounce back," said Kelly. "We need to start winning games, so hopefully start on Tuesday.

"It will be another big crowd, with a good atmosphere.

"It just makes everyone play another five to ten per cent better, run a bit more when there's a big crowd. I think the big atmospheres do help.

"Hopefully, it'll help us track a win on Tuesday."

Daniel Stendel has a decision to make at right-back after Jubril Okedina – a central defender playing out of position there – was culpable for goals in the last two matches.

“It’s a little bit like our performance," said the coach. "We played well but in little moments we are not ready and conceded the goal. He is similar.

"It was not only his fault but he was the last player and maybe he can defend this ball better. It was team mistakes.

“We have the chance to do it better at Bradford and we have some ideas to change some things in the first 11, maybe not.”

After four games out of the XI, Tyrese Noslin is an option.

"I think a little bit in the first games it’s not his quality in possession that he has to improve, it’s more his physical game out of possession," said Stendel. "He tried to adapt his game so much more in training every week.

"I hope maybe on Tuesday or maybe Saturday he is ready to start and show he does the next step in his career."

Striker Tobi Adeyemo is not expected to feature again in 2026, but on-loan winger Amani Richards has an outside chance of playing before Christmas.
