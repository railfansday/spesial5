Arsenal player ratings vs Newcastle: Electric 8/10 star but Bruno Guimaraes run ragged

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Arsenal dropped two points in a chaotic 2-2 draw with Newcastle at St James’ Park.

Mikel Arteta’s side were utterly dominant in the first 45 minutes and led through Eberechi Eze’s first-half strike.

However, the match flipped after the break and Newcastle turned it around through goals from Harvey Barnes and Malick Thiaw.

Noni Madueke equalised to ensure Arsenal got at least a point, but the nature of the turnaround and an injury to David Raya made this a poor evening for the Gunners.
