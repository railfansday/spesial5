Dion Sanderson & Luke Ayling react to Shane Duffy's two-word Derby County message

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Shane Duffy made his Derby County debut against Bolton Wanderers, with the Rams winning just their second league game of the season to ease their relegation fears somewhat.

The Championship relegation picture was looking bleak for Derby County if they had lost to Bolton Wanderers, but their second half fightback claimed a massive three points for John Eustace and co.

Shane Duffy played the full 90 minutes after sitting on the bench during the two previous games, with the Irishman making his 220th Championship appearance against Bolton. He was 1-0 down at half-time but they sealed a 3-1 win late in the match.

Duffy reacted to the win during his debut for the club on Instagram, saying: "We’re back 🐏"

Among the replies was Dion Sanderson, adding: "Just got duffied 👅👅👅"

Derby midfielder David Ozoh also said: "😍"

Bobby Clark was also quick to respond, stating: "❤️"

Duffy's former Yeovil Town teammate Luke Ayling also replied: "Love it big fella!"

Duffy has built an impressive career across English football, coming through Everton's academy before spells with Blackburn Rovers and Brighton, where he helped the Seagulls secure promotion to the Premier League.

He also enjoyed a spell at Celtic before spending three seasons with Norwich City, making 87 appearances for the Canaries. Now united with Eustace at Derby on an initial two-month deal, the 34-year-old brings considerable and vital experience at a perilous time at Pride Park.

Of course, experience alone will only take the Rams so far, but few Championship centre-backs can boast more pedigree in the top two tiers than Duffy, who has made 339 appearances at the two levels and earned 61 Republic of Ireland caps.

Derby can take encouragement from their victory over Bolton, but one result cannot be allowed to disguise the bigger picture. The Rams need injured players to return and strengthen Eustace's options, rather than continually having to rely on experienced figures such as Duffy to get them through difficult spells.

The 3-1 victory is certainly something to build on, particularly after ending such a lengthy winless run, but Derby cannot expect to survive on the back of one result. They will eventually have to show they can compete consistently against a higher standard of player and produce performances that are more convincing across 90 minutes.
