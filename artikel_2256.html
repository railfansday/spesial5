Sheffield United team news and predicted XI to face Charlton Athletic: Joe Rothwell remains out as key man returns

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Sheffield United head into Saturday’s clash with Charlton Athletic after an impressive victory over Preston North End at Deepdale. Sydie Peck stole the headlines with a first-half hat-trick as SUFC secured a 3-2 win on Tuesday night.

Making their second successive away trip, Chris Wilder’s side will be keen to build on that result and maintain their recent momentum. The Blades have won three of their last five matches and are averaging 1.57 points per game this season.

Their opponents, Charlton Athletic, go into the fixture following a disappointing 1-0 defeat against Bolton Wanderers. CAFC are now winless in their last 11 matches, collecting just five points since September.

However, returning to The Valley could offer some encouragement for Nathan Jones’ side. The Addicks are averaging 1.29 points per game on home soil this season and have conceded fewer than a goal per match in front of their own supporters.

Sheffield United will welcome back Kalvin Phillips for Saturday’s trip to Charlton Athletic, but fellow midfielder Joe Rothwell is set for a spell on the sidelines after undergoing a minor groin operation, as reported by The Sheffield Star.

Rothwell picked up the problem during the warm-up before last Friday’s win over Wrexham and is expected to miss the upcoming fixtures against Watford and Southampton. Wilder confirmed he should return after the international break.

Phillips, meanwhile, missed the victory at Preston North End with an ankle injury but has recovered and is available for selection. The Manchester City loanee was also named Sheffield United’s player of the month for October after helping the Blades build momentum.

Patrick Bamford remains a doubt, with the striker facing a late fitness test on the shoulder injury he sustained against Derby County. Wilder confirmed the forward has returned to training but must prove he can cope with physical contact before being cleared to play.

Despite the win over Preston, changes may be afoot from SUFC against Charlton Athletic. Despite that Phillips will likely take a place on the bench.

Given his injury record, Wilder will not want to take any risk with Phillips. Peck and Jairo Riedewald both remain in midfield following the midweek win.

The two changes come in attack and at the centre of defence with Tyrese Campbell and Liam Kitching return to the side. Campbell comes in for Tom Cannon alongside Ben Brereton Diaz, while Kitching is in for Mark McGuinness after he served his suspension in midweek.
