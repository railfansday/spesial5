Sam Rockwell taking on Parker Posey in Hot Ones Versus is a chaotic as youd expect

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Putting White Lotus stars Sam Rockwell and Parker Posey together for a Hot Ones Versus was always going to be chaotic, but the 20-minute video above exceeds all expectations.

The First We Feast game sees the pair posing questions and challenges to each other. If they fail to answer or complete, they have to eat a spicy death wing.

Included is a contest to see who can blow out some birthday cake candles quickest, and a round of one-liner puns that must be delivered without laughing — but the highlight has to be a truly ridiculous game of charades.
