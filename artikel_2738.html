How To Watch UNC Football battle Virginia

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Well, we've made it to Thanksgiving week. This weekend always feels a little different because we all see the big holiday on the horizon, and that holiday ushers in a joyous and very hectic time of year.

There hasn't been a lot to be thankful for with the football team in Chapel Hill. They really don't havea any bigger things to play for, and unfortunately with their home loss to Louisville this past weekend all that's left is hoping to have a three game improvement over last year. Which they paid $20 million for.

It's pretty clear that the powers that decide who will be on TV at particular times have agreed that Carolina fans are checked out on this football team. The amount of blue visible in the stands was one hint, and now we know the last two games of the season will be played at the noon hour on…not exactly the marquis destinations for the ACC.

For the first time this year, the Tar Heels will grace the screen of the CW, and they do so on a weekend that has the best game of the conference being Miami playing Virginia Tech to where they are hosting College GameDay. Truly, it feels like we are just playing out the string.

Still, there's some interesting stuff to watch for. This week it's who will be under center as Carolina—rightfully—has been cagy as to whether or not Billy Edwards, Jr is well enough to play or if the Tar Heels will trust Travis Burgess in his first start under Bill Belichick and Bobby Petrino. There's also Jelani Thurman's quest to be at the top of a pretty impressive group of Tight Ends at UNC, and Jordan Shipp continuing to enhance his stock for either a NIL payday or hearing his name called on the NFL Draft stage next May.

Then next week at high noon there's the car crash that is one team literally playing the game before they get taken over by a coach versus the other team that hasn't beaten them in years to the point where they've seen that squad plant the flag in their field. The league was so excited about that matchup it's at Noon on the ACC Network, so here's hoping they don't need a shot down the sideline to help determine a big call.

Either way, there's some intrigue today as the Tar Heels and Virginia face off. If you aren't going to be in beautiful Charlottesville today, here's how you can catch the action:

Location: Scott Stadium — Charlottesville, VA

TV: The CW with Thom Brennaman handling play-by-play, Jason Cabinda doing color, and Wes Bryant working the sidelines to once again try to get something out of Bill Belichick. Good luck.

Radio: Tar Heel Sports Network with Jones Angell doing play-by-play, Joe Jauch with color, and Bryn Renner on the sidelines. Here's a list of affiliates airing the THSN broadcast. You can also listen to them on GoHeels.com, The Varsity Network, Tune In, and Sirius XM on channel 266.

Streaming: Watch ESPN — Yes, the CW has made a deal with ESPN to where their games are watchable on the ESPN app.
