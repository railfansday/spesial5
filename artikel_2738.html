Kentucky loses CJ Baxter and Malachi Wood to injuries against Missouri

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Kentucky Football running back CJ Baxter and right tackle Malachi Wood were both ruled out during Saturday's game against Missouri after suffering injuries on the same play.

Wood was injured while blocking, while Baxter sustained an injury as he was being tackled. Both players were later seen leaving the field on crutches and wearing walking boots.

The injuries could be a significant loss for Kentucky, as Baxter is the Wildcats' star running back and Wood is their starting right tackle.

Kentucky will now have to rely on running back Jovantae Barnes, who is expected to take on a larger role. However, RB3 Jason Patterson was also listed as doubtful after suffering an injury of his own, leaving the Wildcats with additional concerns in the backfield.

After the game, head coach Will Stein didn't have anything new to announce on the injuries, saying UK would have an update when the Cats return home.

With only one game remaining in the regular season and Kentucky riding a three-game losing streak, the Wildcats are facing an uphill battle heading into their rivalry matchup against Louisville.

Kentucky will hope to get as close to full strength as possible before taking on the Cardinals, as a victory could help the Wildcats secure a better bowl destination and finish the season on a positive note.

The Wildcats had an opportunity to enter the College Football Playoff conversation after starting the season 7-1, but they have since lost three consecutive games following their bye week.

Now, Kentucky will look to regroup, get healthy, and finish the season with a much-needed win over Louisville.
