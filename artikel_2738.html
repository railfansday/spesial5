Who is performing the halftime show for Chiefs-Bills on Thanksgiving?

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

As Thanksgiving winds to a close, everyone can sit down and settle in for a matchup between two of the best teams in the NFL. The 9-1 Buffalo Bills play host to the 8-2 Kansas City Chiefs on Thursday night, playing in a game that will certainly have playoff seeding implications later in the season.

It's the third game of three on Thanksgiving Thursday, following the Bears-Lions and Eagles-Cowboys games.

The whole day is full of an impressive lineup of supporting acts, with performers like Off Campus' Antonio Cipriani and the Wu-Tang Clan handling the national anthem and halftime at the Bears-Lions, and Lauren Daigle and Miranda Lambert doing the same for Eagles-Cowboys.

So who is performing at halftime for Chiefs-Bills?

That would be rock band Goo Goo Dolls! The group had a couple massive hits in the late 90s, including "Iris" and "Slide."

The anthem singer for Chiefs-Bills hasn't been announced yet. The game begins at 8:20 on NBC.
