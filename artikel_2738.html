UND's Jerry Kaminski, Braden Mohr are finalists for top FCS honors

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Nov. 24—GRAND FORKS — UND quarterback Jerry Kaminski and edge rusher Braden Mohr were named Top 30 finalists Tuesday for high honors in the FCS.

Kaminski is up for the Walter Payton Award (FCS Offensive Player of the Year), while Mohr is in the running for the Buck Buchanan Award (FCS Defensive Player of the Year).

Kaminski has drawn considerable national attention this week. ESPN will air a feature on Kaminski during an FCS show at 5 p.m. Thanksgiving Day on ESPN2, while former NFL coach Jon Gruden gave Kaminski a shout-out in a video posted Tuesday on social media. Gruden now works for Barstool Sports.

The Payton Award, celebrating its 40th anniversary season, is named for legendary running back Walter Payton, who starred at Jackson State as part of his Hall of Fame career. Past recipients include Steve McNair, Tony Romo, Brian Westbrook, Jimmy Garoppolo, Cooper Kupp, Taylor Heinicke and Trey Lance.

Kaminski is 237-for-364 (65.1 percent) passing for a school-record 2,868 yards this season. He has 31 touchdowns and seven interceptions. Kaminski also added 373 rushing yards and five touchdowns.

"He has a ton of confidence in the guys around him, and he feels comfortable being himself," UND offensive coordinator Danny Freund said. "I think there are quarterbacks you play with or play for. He's a guy our team wants to play for. He's a great person off the field. When you have one of those guys, it elevates the guys around him."

Kaminski threw for a career-high 387 yards last Saturday in a win over Southern Illinois.

"I saw out of Jerry what I see every day," UND running back Charles Langama said after the SIU game. "Confident player and likes to sling it, for sure."

Mohr, a 6-foot-3 senior, has 40 tackles, 14.5 tackles for loss and a school-record 11.0 sacks. Mohr, who missed nearly the entire 2024 and 2025 seasons due to injury, also has 10 quarterback hurries this year.

The Buchanan Award, in its 32nd season, is named for legendary defensive lineman Junious "Buck" Buchanan, who starred at Grambling State as part of his Hall of Fame career. Past Buchanan recipients include Dexter Coakley, Rashean Mathis and Jared Allen.

All 13 FCS conferences are represented by a finalist for both the Payton and Buchanan. The winners, to be selected by a national voting panel, will be announced on Dec. 17 during a Stats Perform FCS National Awards Show on YouTube.

The Fighting Hawks, 8-4 and No. 9 in the FCS Playoffs, host Idaho State at noon Saturday in the first round of the 24-team postseason tournament.
