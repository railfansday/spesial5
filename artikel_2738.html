Broncos vs. Steelers: 3 Things to Watch in Week 12

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Denver Broncos and Pittsburgh Steelers battle on the gridiron this afternoon in a pivotal AFC matchup that has a lot at stake in the conference's playoff chase. If the Broncos can take care of business on the road, they will earn their ninth victory of the season. Here are three major storylines I'll be watching as the game transpires.

One of the big areas of focus today is what team can get the upper hand at the line of scrimmage. The Broncos had a standout performance in the trenches versus the Las Vegas Raiders last Sunday. A big key to the game will surround whether or not Denver's defensive front seven can dominate the Steelers' offensive line.

Through ten games, Pittsburgh's front five have already allowed 31 sacks—one of the worst marks in the NFL. With future Hall of Fame quarterback Aaron Rodgers being benched and subsequently released from the squad, longtime backup and veteran Mason Rudolph will be leading Pittsburgh on offense on Black Friday.

Defensive Coordinator Vance Joseph needs to have a game plan that focuses on getting after Rudolph. The Steelers' starting tackle duo of Troy Fautanu and Max Iheanachor have struggled this season. Each have surrendered five sacks. With that in mind, I believe the starting edge rushing duo of Nik Bonitto and Jonathon Cooper are poised to have big games. If they can dial up the heat, Denver's odds of winning will skyrocket dramatically.

Rookie running back Jonah Coleman had a standout performance against the Raiders. He rushed for 111 yards and had three touchdowns—proving he will be Denver's bell cow back the remainder of the season. Today, Coleman and Denver's offensive line face a much tougher battle going up against a formidable Steelers defense that is one of the best in the NFL at stopping the run.

Can the Broncos' offensive line hold their own against a Steelers' defensive front at the point of attack? They need to protect quarterback Bo Nix and give him time to make some plays in the pocket. Additionally, they need to prove they can establish the run on the road.

Pittsburgh has one of the league's elite run defenses, but Denver has to get rookie running back Coleman going. With J.K. Dobbins sidelined due to a one-game suspension, expect a heavy dose of the rookie running back Per weather forecasts, this could be a rainy matchup.

That means dictating the pace of the game on the ground will be mission critical for the Broncos to achieve victory. We all saw how important that was for the Buffalo Bills last night in their win over the Kansas City Chiefs in bad weather. Denver needs to get Coleman going and find a way to limit Jaylen Warren's impact defensively.

Today's matchup is going to have some wild weather if the weather forecast holds. That means protecting the ball will be extremely important for both teams. Both the Broncos and Steelers have done a good job forcing turnovers this season.

In every game, winning the turnover battle is important. That usually winds up being a huge difference maker in the outcome. I think that's going to play a big factor in today's game. I'm eager to see what team is able to come ahead in this regard. I have a feeling Denver has the edge in this one.

I don't necessarily like to assume, but I'm not necessarily confident the Steelers are going to come out of the gate firing all cylinders. With all the Rodgers-McCarthy and team related drama this week, I could see them performing a bit flat. Though they are undefeated at home to start the season.

I'm hoping Joseph and the Denver's defense can find a way to make some noise and create some timely turnovers that change the momentum and tide of the game.
