NFL says Parker Washington didn't control ball until he was out of bounds

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Sunday’s game between the Jaguars and Giants included a controversial decision in the fourth quarter on a play that looked like a touchdown catch by Jacksonville receiver Parker Washington.

The ruling on the field was that the catch was incomplete. Jaguars coach Liam Coen challenged the decision.

Replay review confirmed the ruling on the field, even though CBS rules analyst Gene Steratore said during the broadcast that he believed it was a catch.

After the game, pool reporter Pat Leonard spoke with NFL V.P. of instant replay Mark Butterworth.

“The ruling on the field was an incomplete pass,” Butterworth said. “First, to overturn a call, we need clear and obvious video evidence to overturn the on-field ruling. It was determined that the receiver did not gain full control of the ball until he was out of bounds.”

Butterworth confirmed that he was referring to the fact that, when Washington hit the ground, the ball was moving in Washington’s hands.

Still, movement of the ball is permitted. Washington objectively remained in control of the ball.

And here’s the real point. Movement doesn’t prevent clear and obvious evidence that the catch was made. The question should be this: Is it clear and obvious, given that movement is permitted, that Washington made the catch?

Most would say he did. If so, the ruling on the field should have been changed from incompletion to touchdown.
