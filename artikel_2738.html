Lakers’ nine-game winning streak ends with loss to Jazz

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The mark of a truly good team isn't just how dominant they look when they're firing on all cylinders.

It's also how they're able to hang in, or even win, games when things aren't going their way.

The Lakers hung in for most of Wednesday night against the Jazz despite playing well below the standard they've established for themselves over the last month, but they fell to the Jazz 109-97 at Crypto.com Arena to end their winning streak.

Luka Dončić struggled like most of his teammates during the Lakers' 109-97 streak-snapping loss to the Jazz on Nov. 18, 2026, in Los Angeles. NBAE via Getty Images

The Lakers' scoring total marked just the second time this season they were held below 100 points, just above their season-low 95 points they scored in their Oct. 30 road loss to the Warriors.

They shot a season-worst 38.2% from the field (34 for 89) and 27.3% (9 for 33) on 3-pointers.

Keyonte George, going up for a shot, had 30 points and 10 assists to help lead the Jazz past the Lakers. NBAE via Getty Images

The Lakers fell to 13-3 on the season following the loss to the Jazz after winning their previous nine consecutive games, which included a comeback overtime victory over the Hornets on Tuesday night.

Luka Dončić had another poor shooting performance (10 for 28), scoring 29 points to go with seven assists and five rebounds, but the Slovenian superstar also recorded nine of his team's 18 turnovers.

Wednesday ended Dončić's streak of scoring at least 30 points (11 games).

Kyle Filipowski (2) and Collin Sexton (10) battle for the ball during the Lakers' loss to the Jazz. AP Photo/Caroline Brehman

Quentin Grimes added 20 points, seven rebounds and four assists, Collin Sexton chipped in 12 points and Walker Kessler had a 10-point, 11-rebound double-double against his former team.

The Lakers hung in for most of the game despite struggling to get shots to fall, tying the game at 83 apiece when Grimes made a layup at the 8:19 mark of the fourth.

But the Jazz, who are now 3-13 on the season after back-to-back wins over the Clippers and Lakers, went on a 15-0 run to create a gap the Lakers couldn't close.
