3 keys to a Cincinnati Bengals victory against the Washington Commanders

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

The Cincinnati Bengals will take their version of the greatest show on Earth to Washington in Week 11 against the Commanders in primetime, playing them on Monday Night Football. Washington is 3-5-1 this season, but coming off a crucial win against the AFC East division-leading New York Giants on Thursday Night Football last week.

The Bengals are coming off a primetime victory of their own, defeating the Pittsburgh Steelers 38-18 at Paycor Stadium on Sunday Night Football. The win improved the Bengals to 7-2 on the season, and they currently have a one-game lead over those Steelers for first place in the AFC North.

Let's take a look at 3 keys to the Cincinnati Bengals continuing their winning streak this week.

Jayden Daniels is completing a respectable 64.8% of his passes so far during the 2026 season. He has 10 passing touchdowns to 2 interceptions in the 7 games he has played this year. Daniels has an additional 280 rushing yards on the season.

Even more so than those numbers, the Bengals likely remember what Jayden was able to do against them in primetime football back in 2024. Daniels completed 21 of 23 passes for over 250 passing yards, 2 passing touchdowns, and an additional 39 rushing yards with a touchdown on the ground. He helped Washington pull off the victory 38-33 that night in Cincinnati.

While Daniels is a dangerous player, it is important to note that this is a completely different defense than the one he faced back in 2024. While this defense hasn't been dominant, they certainly have shown a lot more than what we saw in 2024 and 2025. They are also coming off two really good performances against Atlanta and Pittsburgh.

This won't be an easy game for Cincinnati, but they do have a defense that we should have much more faith in than we did back in 2024. The talent is substantially different, and they have started to play some really complementary football at all three levels of the defense.

There is no reason to change up what is working. Let Joe Burrow do what Joe Burrow does best, and that is throwing the football all over the field with surgical precision. Coming off of a 400-yard and 4-touchdown passing performance, the Bengals should just keep on throwing the football.

There will be times to take deeper shots down the field, but the most impressive part of what Burrow is doing is just how patient he has become. He isn't putting the ball in harm's way; he is settling for the check-down passes when necessary, and is allowing the running backs to catch passes as an extension of the run game.

When you have a quarterback that is playing at an MVP level, you have to let him cook. That is fully what I expect to see again this week in a primetime matchup against the Commanders' defense. The Commanders have the 31st-ranked passing defense this year. They are allowing 244.8 passing yards per game.

Joe Burrow. Ja'Marr Chase, Tee Higgins, and the rest of the Bengals offense should be in for a big night.

About the only way teams currently have a chance to beat the Bengals is if they decide to beat themselves. When they lose the turnover battle, they will have a chance to lose the game. We saw that against Pittsburgh and Jacksonville early in the season this year.

We also saw them get pushed to overtime when they had 4 turnovers against the Tennessee Titans. If they take care of the football, they should win this game in Washington this week. If not, they will be in for a closer contest than they hope for, and potentially an upset loss on the road. Sometimes, it really is that simple.
