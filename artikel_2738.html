Dr. J, Julius Randle rip into Nets after embarrassing loss to Bulls

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

This was a night the Brooklyn Nets could feel good and get a win. It was a night honoring the 1974 and 1976 ABA championship teams, and the struggling Chicago Bulls were in town, a game the Nets could, and maybe should, win.

Instead, the Nets got their doors blown off early and went on to lose by 30, 137-107.

The Nets broadcast brought on Julius Irving, the legendary Dr. J and greatest player in franchise history — the star of those championship teams — and he was the first to rip the team and the way the day was handled.

"We didn't really talk to the players," Irving said on the YES Network broadcast. "You know, their coach was doing all the talking, and they were getting their preparation in, doing their drills, and so on and so forth. And I fully anticipated the Nets to be so fired up in having us here that they would blow Chicago out, but I'm looking at 62-39. So I was wrong."

After the game, Julius Randle, one of the players brought in to lift the floor of this team, was much harsher, via Brian Lewis at the New York Post.

"That was embarrassing. Been in the league for 13 years, and that's one of the worst games I've ever been a part of. And not because of the score... but it's how we did it. That wasn't cool. Nothing about it was cool," Julius Randle said. "We've got to find a way to figure out the root of it or whatever, but that's just unacceptable...

"It's just a pride, an effort, the competitive spirit, all of that, it was nonexistent."

"Very disappointed, and it was just starting with effort. We were not a competitive group, we were not a connected group, we were not a selfless group," Nets coach Jordi Fernández said. "So, I saw selfishness, I saw pointing fingers, and I saw guys that didn't always play hard."

The Nets are 3-11 in a season where they don't even control their own first-round pick (Houston has swap rights). The team is rebuilding and wasn't expected to be fighting for a playoff spot, but with Randle and Michael Porter Jr. on the roster, the expectations were higher than this.
