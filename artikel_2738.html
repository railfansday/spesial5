Giving Props: Prop Bets for Vikings/Niners en México

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

It is time for us to make an attempt to assist those of you who like to put a bit of money on your NFL action by taking a look at some of the prop bets that our friends at the FanDuel Sportsbook have available for this week's Minnesota Vikings game. This week, the Vikings are making a run for the border, as they'll be facing off with the San Francisco 49ers in Mexico City in a game that could potentially determine the #1 seed in the NFC playoffs. Are there any bets out there that could help you add to your bankroll a bit?

Well, we certainly think that there are! Let's see if we can find them, shall we?

Kirk Cousins Passing Yards: Over 231.5 (-114)

Yes, I know I took the over on Cousins' passing yards last week, and he came up just short. That came with one full practice under his belt, no Jordan Addison, no T.J. Hockenson, and no Jauan Jennings. This week, Hockenson and Jennings will be back. . .and I have a feeling that this is the week where Jennings finally makes his way out of the Witness Protection Program. . .and Cousins might even know the names of everyone that he's in the huddle with. On top of that, the Niners have not recorded a quarterback sack in a full calendar month. Yes, Cousins threw three interceptions against this same Niners' defense just a couple of weeks ago. . .I don't see that happening again this week. Take the over.

Justin Jefferson Receiving Yards: Over 84.5 (-114)

Yes, I know I also took the over on Jefferson's receiving yards last week, and he came up just short. The Cousins-to-Jefferson connection is going to be big this week. Jefferson's last 100-yard game came in the regular season finale last year when the Vikings were force-feeding him the ball to get him over the 1,000-yard mark for the season. His last 100-yard game before that? Week 5 of last year, when the Vikings faced the Cleveland Browns in London. We're going to see Jefferson go over the century mark in this one, I think. Take the over on this one, too.

Parlay: Vikings +3.5 and under 46.5 (+241)

It worked last week, so why not go for it again this week? This is the fourth week in a row that the Vikings go into the game as an underdog, and with Kirk Cousins in the fold. . .well, this team can, honestly, beat any team in the NFL if they can sustain a few drives here and there. As far as the over/under? Well, the Vikings have still only had one game go "over" all season, and that was the Week 1 game against the Packers. Anyone who bets the "over" on a Vikings game at this point deserves to lose their money, and I don't want you to be that person, so we'll just stick with the over until someone gives us reason not to.

Those are the prop bets we're looking at for this week's game, ladies and gentlemen. Are there any that you have your eyes on?
