ONE SAMURAI 5 – Results And Highlights For Every Fight

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

ONE Championship is livening up the week with a blockbuster spectacle in the Japanese capital.

The world's largest martial arts organization will broadcast ONE SAMURAI 5 live from EBARA WAVE Arena Ota in Tokyo, Japan, on Wednesday, November 18.

In the main event, ONE Strawweight MMA World Champion Joshua "The Passion" Pacio returns to Japan in search of his first victory in the country. But local favorite, the near-flawless Keito "Pocket Monk" Yamakita, will look to dethrone the divisional king in front of his friends, family, and compatriots.

Plus, two of Japan's most talented kickboxing phenoms will meet in the co-main event, as ISKA World Champion Ryujin Nasukawa collides with NJKF Champion Raichi Nagai in strawweight action.

The card also features the highly anticipated returns of former ONE World Champions Jarred "The Monkey God" Brooks and Petchtanong Petchfergus.
