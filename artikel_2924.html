The Hawks’ second unit is giving the team life

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The Atlanta Hawks are one of the top teams in the Eastern Conference through 17 games, and that might be a surprise when looking at some of the circumstances they've played under so far. Jalen Johnson's shooting numbers have taken a dip, CJ McCollum is sidelined with a hamstring strain, Asa Newell could miss most of the season, Jock Landale has a right great toe sprain, and Mouhamed Gueye has yet to step on the floor.

Most of these players have a huge impact on the team in some way or another, yet the Hawks still rise. Some would think it's because someone has stepped up in the starting lineup, and while that is true (Dyson Daniels is playing his best basketball), there's an even bigger reason for the team's success.

It's the second unit, and it's been the collection of several players that has helped the Hawks through this stretch.

If you need bold proof, look no further than their game against the Detroit Pistons, where the lineup of Daniels-Corey Kispert-Aaron Wiggins-Lu Dort-Zuby Ejiofor went on an 18-0 run to start the fourth quarter, and ultimately helped the Hawks win the game.

After that game, head coach Quin Snyder gave credit to that group for how they played.

"That five, just the level of tenacity, defensively was unique. And you could feel it. The way they competed on the offensive boards. They took 3s; they crashed and defended. That's what we were trying to accomplish, and we came out and did it," Snyder said.

It just hasn't been that game, but during the team's current winning streak, they've shown up at key parts of the game. The one player who has bought into his role and is playing well in that second unit is Corey Kispert, who finished with 18 points in 18 minutes against the Pistons. Outside of that game, Kispert is shooting the ball at a high level this season (48.8% from 3), and his defense has also improved.

The crazy thing is that Kispert wasn't in the main rotation to start the season, but with the several injuries across the board, he's had to play minutes. Those minutes have been positive for the Hawks, whatever they look like, and he's contributing to winning.

Ejiofor is another player who's seen minutes due to injuries, and with Landale still sidelined, he's making the most of his time on the court. His rebounding has been key for the second unit, and his defense in general is an upgrade from Landale's. He's shown that he isn't afraid to shoot 3-pointers, but he's making his presence known in the paint more than anything.

Dort's impact off the bench hasn't been as big as many would've expected, and that's mostly because he's playing sort of out of position. With most of the forward depth being hurt, Dort is playing more at the 4, and though he can defend almost any position on the floor, his rebounding isn't the best.

In the game against the Pistons, he was used perfectly, with Dort defending on the perimeter and Daniels playing at the 4, which is good for the team's rebounding numbers. Dort still has a huge impact on defense, and it shows in moments like this.

Though Wiggins was in the starting lineup for some of the games during the current winning streak, it still can't be overlooked what he's done as a whole this season coming off the bench. His scoring has been key for the Hawks in the second unit, and whether that's knowing down 3-pointers or getting to the paint, it's obvious that he's being aggressive when it comes to putting the ball in the basket.

It's no surprise that Wiggins is in early conversations for Sixth Man of the Year, and it would be well deserved if he continues his strong play.

With the way this collection of guys is playing, the Hawks may have some decisions to make when players like McCollum and Landale return from injury. What happens to guys like Kispert and Ejiofor, who are currently playing well? What happens when Gueye, who is one of the Hawks' better defenders, returns from injury?

The Hawks will answer those questions when the time comes, but as of now, it sounds like a good problem to have.
