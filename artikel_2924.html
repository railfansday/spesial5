DeVonta Smith set to return; Zack Baun, Dontayvion Wicks ruled out for Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

DeVonta Smith has missed the last month with a hamstring injury, but the Eagles are set to have their top wideout back in the lineup against the Steelers on Sunday.

Smith was a full participant in practice the last two days and he has no injury designation for Sunday. He had 26 catches for 310 yards and two touchdowns in four games before his injury.

The Eagles will remain without wideout Dontayvion Wicks, however. He will miss his fourth straight game with ankle and foot injuries.

Linebacker Zack Baun has also been ruled out. Baun injured his back against the Giants in Week Nine and did not practice this week.

Linebacker Smael Mondon (ankle) is considered doubtful and running back Will Shipley (foot, illness) is questionable to be activated from injured reserve.
