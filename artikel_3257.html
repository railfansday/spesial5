Apple TV is now $6.99/month (50% off) for Black Friday, details here

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Apple just launched its best deal of the year for Black Friday, and it’s available now through November 30.

The Black Friday price is heavily discounted compared to the standard $14.99/month price. And you can cancel the subscription at any time, there’s no need to keep it for the full six months.

Apple typically doesn’t discount Apple TV except for its Black Friday deal, so this is the best price that will likely be available until late 2027.

Here’s a rundown of everything Apple TV has recently premiered, and what’s coming soon.

Apple TV’s current shows include the hit crime thrillers Nocturne and 12 12 12, one of which involves a serial killer while the other centers on a heist.

Owen Wilson’s beloved comedy Stick is also now airing its second season, which sees Pluribus star Rhea Seehorn join the cast.

Looking ahead, December 2 brings the return of Bad Monkey, the excellent comedy from Ted Lasso and Shrinking’s creator. John Malkovich joins the cast, which is led by Vince Vaughn.
