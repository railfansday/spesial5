Apple releases iOS 27.3 public beta 2, new developer beta 2 build

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Apple has released iOS 27.3 public beta 2 for iPhone. Today’s release also includes a new build for iOS 27.3 beta 2 for developers. The update brings new Calendar controls, more Siri AI capabilities, and new features for iPhone Duo. It also includes the redesigned Health app and other changes introduced earlier this month.

The update follows yesterday’s second developer beta and the first public beta on November 5.

Apple has also detailed higher daily usage limits for Siri AI and other Apple Intelligence features through eligible iCloud+ plans. Plans with 200GB or more include extra capacity; the 50GB plan keeps standard limits.

See our full iOS 27.3 beta 2 roundup for more on the latest discoveries.

The first public beta brought back the redesigned Health app after its earlier preview during iOS 27.2 testing. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence support, Insights collects trends across your health data, while Longevity includes a Health Age comparison based on Apple Watch measurements.

If you’re already testing the public beta, open Settings > General > Software Update to check for the new version.

Joining for the first time? Make a backup, then enroll in the free Apple Beta Software Program using your iPhone’s Apple Account. In Settings > General > Software Update > Beta Updates, choose iOS 27 Public Beta. Go back to Software Update to download and install it.
