LiveOne (LVO) Q2 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Headcount cuts and AI-driven restructuring shape this update.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

LiveOne, Inc. (LVO +4.68%) reported its second-quarter and first-half results, highlighting a strategic focus on expanding its business-to-business distribution network and optimizing its cost structure. Management reported a significant reduction in headcount and the implementation of artificial intelligence to improve margins while revising fiscal 2027 guidance to reflect updated partnership launch timelines. The company emphasized its transition away from reliance on a single major partner toward a diversified model involving major technology, telecommunications, and media platforms. Management stated that the company remains focused on achieving record adjusted EBITDA for the fiscal year and scaling its podcast and audio divisions through new distribution agreements.
