De la Fuente includes Como’s Ramon among three newcomers for Nations League

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Luis de la Fuente has named three new faces in Spain's squad for their final two Nations League games.

Spain have already secured their place in the quarter-finals ahead of their meetings with Czechia and England this international break.

And De la Fuente has used it as an opportunity to make seven changes to the squad that was named in the extended break earlier in the season.

Real Betis pair Alvaro Valles and Marc Roca are among the new faces, along with Como's Jacobo Ramon.

Ramon has caught the eye for Como, who have made an impressive start to the 2026-27 season, including a historic 9-1 win in the Champions League against Lens on Wednesday, with the defender getting an assist.

Joan Garcia and Pedro Porro both missed the last camp through injury but have recovered and been welcomed back into the fold.

There are also returns for Pablo Barrios – after a year out of the squad – and Ander Barrenetxea, but Liverpool's Victor Munoz has been omitted.

Spain, who are on a 42-game unbeaten streak (not including penalty shoot-outs), winning each of their last 11, will also be without the injured Barcelona trio of Rodri, Dani Olmo and Eric Garcia.

La Roja face Czechia on November 12 before hosting England three days later in the final Group A4 match.
