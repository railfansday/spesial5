Liverpool injury update: Virgil van Dijk, Ryan Gravenberch, Dominik Szoboszlai latest news and return dates

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Liverpool will be battling some significant fitness issues during the international break.

The two-week pause in the campaign may have not come at an ideal time for Liverpool after bouncing back from successive defeats to Arsenal and Fenerbahce by beating Crystal Palace 3-1 at the weekend.

Nevertheless, it’s a chance for Andoni Iraola to take stock of where his team is at and build towards a huge game at home to a Manchester United side currently in the Premier League relegation zone.

The Spaniard has made it clear that his one wish from the final international break of the year is no fresh injuries - particularly after losing Alexander Isak, Cody Gakpo and Dominik Szoboszlai during the autumn window.

However, Iraola may not get his wish upon news that captain Virgil van Dijk has withdrawn from the Netherlands squad, while Szoboszlai is awaiting results of an assessment after joining up with the Hungary squad.

With that in mind, here is the latest Liverpool injury news and return dates…

The Liverpool captain was called up by new Dutch head coach Xavi for the Nations League double-header against Greece and Germany.

However, it was confirmed on Monday that he - and Sunderland striker Brian Brobbey - had pulled out of the squad for fitness related reasons.

A statement reads: “Virgil van Dijk and Brian Brobbey have been dropped from the Dutch national team's upcoming international matches. Both players did not return fit from the past weekend's matches and are therefore unavailable.

“The squad thus currently consists of 23 players.”

Liverpool fans will be hopeful this decision is no more than a precaution, particularly after Van Dijk played 90 minutes in all four Nations League games during the autumn international window.

Potential return date: Sunday November 22, vs Manchester United (H)

Szoboszlai will be assessed to determine his fitness for international duty with Hungary.

The midfielder was forced off in the second half against Palace due to a knee complaint and Iraola admitted afterwards that the Reds No8 was not fully fit.

“Dom definitely wasn’t 100 per cent in the second half,” Iraola said. "Let’s see. The last thing we need now is to lose players in the international games.

"In the last international break we lost three very important players in Alex (Isak), Cody (Gakpo) and Dom. So now I'm praying - it's the last thing we need.”

Potential return date: Sunday November 22, vs Manchester United (H)

Ryan Gravenberch has missed Liverpool’s last two games with a heel injury. There was hope the Dutchman would return for the trip to Selhurst Park, but was ultimately left out of the matchday squad.

“The only doubt we will have is Ryan,” Iraola said in the pre-Crystal Palace press conference. “The others will be the same as we have been.

“The Ryan Gravenberch thing is a day-to-day thing, it’s a matter of pain. Let’s see if he can deal with the pain and he can help us.”

With the injury severe enough to prevent the midfielder from being called up by Xavi for the Netherlands’ Nations League double-header, Gravenberch must be considered a major doubt to face Man United.

Potential return date: Sunday November 22, vs Manchester United (H)

Jeremie Frimpong picked up a muscle injury during the comeback win over Brentford last month, resulting in another lay-off for the full-back since his arrival from Bayer Leverkusen.

Like compatriots Van Dijk and Gravenberch, he will not be representing the Dutch during the international break and it’s believed his return is not too far away.

In the most recent update of Frimpong, Iraola said: “Jeremie Frimpong is obviously a new muscle injury and it happened, I think, in the second or third minute he was playing, difficult to predict and we will have to wait until he recovers.

“But it’s going to be difficult, I think, before the [November international] break. We will try but it’s going to be difficult to have him.”

Potential return date: Sunday November 22, vs Manchester United (H)

Giovanno Leoni signed for Liverpool from Parma last summer in a deal worth around £26million, but a serious knee injury means he has been limited to just one senior appearance so far.

The centre-back made his debut in the Carabao Cup against Southampton in September 2025, but he was stretchered off and it was later confirmed that he had suffered an anterior cruciate ligament tear.

He was expected to miss a full year of action, which has proved to be the case.

He had hoped to have made his comeback for the recent September international break, but that has since been delayed.

“It’s taking more time than we wanted,” Iraola said. “The planning was to train with the team in this past international break [but] probably it’s going to be in the next international break, so we’ve lost one month in the process.

“Giovanni’s injury, I wasn’t here but talking to the medical department, it was not just an ACL – it was a bad injury, a lot of things around the knee were involved apart from the ACL.”

Hugo Ekitike has not played since rupturing his Achilles against Paris Saint-Germain in April.

The Frenchman was forced to sit out the end of last season and the World Cup, where it was estimated that he would be out for nine months to a full year.

He was included in Iraola’s squad for the Champions League league phase - which came as a surprise to many - with the hope that he could potentially return for the final two games in January, against Inter Milan and Lens.

The striker is thought to be targeting a comeback before the turn of the year.

Conor Bradley is another long-term absentee for Liverpool, having suffered a serious knee injury at Arsenal back in January.

The Northern Ireland international suffered bone and ligament damage in that match, which has kept him out for almost a year.

Iraola’s latest comments were positive, indicating that Bradley will soon return to full training.

“[He is] doing very well,” Iraola said. “I think [he is] in a good place. Conor is not so far from training with the group – not like full training but doing some things with the group.

“I think Conor is not very far from starting doing things with us."

It looks as though he could rejoin team training during the November international break.

Potential return date: Wednesday, December 2 vs Sunderland (H)
