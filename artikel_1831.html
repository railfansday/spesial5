‘A superbly efficient midfielder’ - Brighton player ratings vs Craiova as three score 8/10 in Conference League cruise

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Brighton cruised beyond Romanian league leaders Craiova with minimal fuss in a 4-0 win.

The Seagulls took the lead after just three minutes as Jack Hinshelwood provided the assist, picking out Ibrahim Osman, who finished to put the Albion 1-0 ahead. Maxim De Cuyper then made it two as he capitalised on a wayward pass to calmly slot home with his right foot from the edge of the box. A lovely goal from the Belgian.

Craiova's task was made near-impossible on 39 minutes as Vladimir Screciu was dismissed for a poor challenge on Matt O'Riley. The Albion man was lucky not to sustain a serious injury.

Unsurprisingly, Femi Azeez replaced O'Riley at the break and the Seagulls soon set about the visitors again.

Charalampos Kostoulas added a third with a nice left-footed finish from Osman’s pass and the lively Osman then netted his second and Brighton’s fourth as he cut in from the left and found the bottom corner with a composed finish.

The result lifts Brighton to top of the Conference League table, ahead of the later kick-offs. The top eight teams qualify for the knockout stages.
