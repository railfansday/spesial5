Amorim: Milan are not helping Ramos score

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Ruben Amorim believes his AC Milan players are "not helping Goncalo Ramos" score goals this season.

Ramos joined the Rossoneri for a club-record €74m from Paris Saint-Germain in the summer, but has failed to hit the ground running.

The 25-year-old has scored just three goals in 14 appearances across all competitions, while also providing two assists.

Ramos has a shot conversion rate of 9.1% this term, with his three goals coming from 33 shots, 11 of which hit the target.

The Portugal international is scoreless in his last five games across all competitions for the Rossoneri, but did provide an assist in their win over Genoa before the break.

Ahead of a home clash against Frosinone, Amorim put Ramos' poor scoring form down to a lack of creativity within his side.

"Of course, we can say that maybe one more option [in January] can be a good thing to help Camarda and Ramos, but I think that Ramos's struggles are the struggles of the team," said Amorim.

"I had two weeks to understand the data that you guys have and I have. We are not reaching the final third, we are one of the worst teams at getting into the final third and continuing from there.

"We don't have a lot of shots. I don't want a team that takes a lot of shots from outside of the box, because most of the time you can't score from outside the box. But we are not helping Ramos.

"In the game against Genoa, without Ramos, we don't score the two goals. He didn't score, but without Ramos, we don't score those two goals.

"Ramos is struggling because the team is struggling. But Ramos is a guy who follows the midfielder, the centre-back.

"I know that it's not his job. I know that we've bought a player to score goals, but I’m not doing the [best] job to help Ramos, but Ramos is doing everything to help me and I know that Ramos is going to score many goals.

"But we need to push the team forwards for him to play in the position of a striker."

Adrien Rabiot has gained more points for his team than any other player in Serie A this season, with 10 points thanks to five goals and four assists.

On the last matchday, he became the first Milan midfielder to be involved in at least one goal in six consecutive league appearances since Kaka in 2004 (who reached seven).

Only Matias Soule (80) has been involved in more shot-ending sequences than Frosinone's Giacomo Calo (76) in Serie A this season.

The Frosinone player has scored five goals in the competition this term; only one midfielder has scored more goals in the first 12 matchdays in the top flight with a newly promoted team in the three points for a win era: Hidetoshi Nakata with Perugia in 1998-99 (six).

Milan are unbeaten in all six of their previous Serie A matches against Frosinone (W4 D2); among teams they have never lost against in the competition, the Rossoneri have only played more games against Pisa (14 – also six against Crotone and Lecco).

And Milan have scored at least three goals in four of their six Serie A matches against Frosinone, netting 15 goals overall at an average of 2.5 per game; only against Pescara (2.9) have they registered a higher average, among sides faced on at least five occasions.

Amorim's side have won four of their last five Serie A matches (L1), always scoring at least two goals, after failing to win three of their previous four (D3).

However, Frosinone have won six of their first 11 Serie A games this season (D2 L3).

In the last 40 years, only three promoted sides have won more than six of their opening 12 games in the competition: Bari in 1994-95 (seven), Chievo in 2001-02 (seven) and Hellas Verona in 2013-14 (seven).
