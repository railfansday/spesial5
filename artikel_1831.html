CM: Milan could compete with Newcastle and Aston Villa for Lille gem Ngoy

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

There’s no shortage of defenders being linked to AC Milan currently and a new name has been proposed to the club today.

With a desire to add more cover in the defence, Milan are keeping a keen eye on the mercato and several profiles are arising that could be of interest. Of course, there are a few headliners, but there are also some rising talents that could explode at San Siro.

Calciomercato.com write that the Rossoneri are beginning to take an interest in Nathan Ngoy, the Belgian defender who is rapidly progressing. Like Diego Moreira, he also scored his first goal for the Red Devils against Italy and the future looks bright for him.

Said to be following in Vincent Kompany’s footsteps, he’s part of the rebuilding of the Belgian defence and has become a starter since the World Cup.

It’s also a reason why his value has risen so much in recent months; after the summer, he was believed to be worth €20 and €25 million, but he’s now got a valuation of around €40m.

Goncalo Inacio and Virgil van Dijk are the main names for the defence currently. Yet, the links between the Diavolo and Lille could help make a route to Ngoy easier, as could his international teammates who play at San Siro.

Newcastle and Aston Villa are also keen on the young defender, so he’s certainly one to watch in the future and it could be a major coup for Milan.
