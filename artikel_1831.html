Kane sidelined ‘for a few months’ with ankle ligament injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Harry Kane has suffered an ankle ligament injury that will keep him out for months, but Vincent Kompany urged Bayern Munich to "try and turn this situation into something positive".

Kane was forced off with the injury just 20 minutes into Bayern's 1-1 draw with Koln on Friday, on their return to Bundesliga action.

And the reigning champions confirmed that Kane was set to be "sidelined for the next few months" with the injury to his left ankle.

German media reports suggested he will be out for three months.

It is a huge blow to Bayern, given the reigning Ballon d'Or winner has 12 goal involvements in the league this season (four goals, eight assists).

Michael Olise also went off against Koln and was subsequently diagnosed with a bruised foot, meaning both he and Kane will join the likes of Luis Diaz, Serge Gnabry, Ismael Saibari, Alphonso Davies and Manuel Neuer on the sidelines.

Kompany, who has just returned to the dugout himself after recovering from a meningeal irritation that caused him to collapse at Bayern's training ground, admitted his injury list was a cause for concern, but he is determined to be optimistic.

"It was a special match, of course," he said.

"I think we didn't get off to a bad start; we were really in the game and had some pace. But then Harry Kane and Michael Olise suffered injuries, of course.

Harry Kane sustained a syndesmosis ligament tear in his left ankle during this evening’s home match against 1. FC Köln. This was confirmed by an examination carried out by FC Bayern’s medical department. The 33 year-old will be sidelined for the next few months. Get well soon, Harry!

"However, no excuses. I think it was a fair result and that Koln deserved to take the point.

"We have other things to worry about, I must admit. Without complaining too much, we must accept what we're in for and regain our strength so that we can get results in the next few matches despite the odds.

"It's an extremely unusual situation at the moment. We need incredible performances from players who aren't used to this pressure at FC Bayern. But that's just how it is.

"We'll all try to turn this situation into something positive. FC Bayern do that again and again, and that's what we want to achieve now too."

While Bayern currently sit top of the table, with 22 points from 10 matches, they are playing their weakest Bundesliga season in four years; they had only 19 points at this stage in 2022-23.

They have now gone two consecutive home games without a win (D2), though they remain unbeaten in all competitions this season (W11 D6).

Bayern could lose top spot this weekend, however, if Borussia Dortmund beat Borussia Monchengladbach on Saturday.
