Russell Brand Allegedly Struggled to Recall Katy Perry Marriage in Deposition

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Russell Brand reportedly struggled to remember when he was married to Katy Perry during a deposition last year.

Brand, 51, was questioned by New York authorities in 2025 after an extra from his 2011 movie Arthur sued the disgraced comedian for alleged sexual assault, according to court documents obtained by the Daily Mail on Friday, November 20.

In a transcript from Brand’s testimony, he seemingly failed to accurately recall the correct timeline when asked whether he was married at the time of the alleged incident. (The anonymous victim claimed that Brand exposed himself to her and sexually assaulted her in a bathroom on set.)

“Whilst I — of course, it wouldn’t be difficult for me to find out whether or not I was married, because it would be on the internet somewhere, I’m assuming,” Brand said, per court documents. “I was — what I do know is that I was in a relationship with, as you know, obviously a very high-profile woman. And even when I was married to her, I was sort of drowning in love.”

Perry, 42, and Brand started dating in 2009, one year before they got married. The now-exes divorced in 2011. (Us has reached out to reps for both Perry and Brand for comment regarding the alleged deposition comments.)

Perry, who shares 6-year-old daughter Daisy with ex Orlando Bloom, has since moved on with former Canadian prime minister Justin Trudeau. Brand, for his part, has been married to Laura Gallacher since 2017.

Gallacher, who shares three kids with Brand, has remained by his side in the wake of his sexual misconduct trial and subsequent conviction. Brand was first accused of assault in 2023, vehemently denying the accusations and pleading not guilty to three counts of rape, three counts of sexual assault and one count of attempted indecent assault. He was convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault, and found not guilty on the remaining charges earlier this month and sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison.

“We trust God, and we trust his plan, and we trust everything He’s doing right now, even though it’s painful,” Gallacher said on the Sunday, November 15, episode of the couple’s Sunday Service YouTube channel, breaking her silence on Brand’s conviction. “Obviously, [it] will take some getting used to for me to even sit here and not have my companion and my person with me.”

Gallacher also vowed to continue their joint YouTube channel in Brand’s absence.

“Although Russell is not here now in this moment, the holy spirit is moving and the holy spirit cannot be restrained,” she said. “I’m speaking to Russell. He’s OK and he’s praying, unsurprisingly. He’s praying and we decided for me to continue.”
