Inside a Secluded $3.3 Million Mediterranean Estate With a Resort-Inspired Backyard Retreat in Agoura Hills

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

An ultra-private estate in Agoura Hills, CA has hit the market, offering a secluded retreat in the Santa Monica Mountains for $3.295 million.

"It's tucked away into the hills and no one's around you. It's just a super private property," listing agent Michelle Meyers of The Agency tells Realtor.com®.

Set on an expansive lot that's just over an acre in the exclusive Heritage View Estates enclave, Meyers notes it's rare to find a home in the area with a yard that's this sizable and this private.

The five-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom abode spans 4,161 square feet—boasting open spaces that are defined by soaring ceilings and 10-inch-wide plank oak floors.

"It's been tailored for such effortless living," Meyers continues, noting that the sellers thoughtfully curated the 1996-built home to maximize every inch of the property, such as perfectly-organized closets in the primary suite and a "completely-custom built out" pantry in the kitchen.

Of the thoughtful upgrades, the beautifully-remodeled kitchen is surely a standout, where timeless design has been leveled-up with top-of-the-line appliances, stunning countertops, and designer lighting.

The ground floor is an excellent spot to entertain from the moment visitors step through the grand entry, which features custom doors and a statement-making curved staircase.

A stately great room has been intentionally arranged to upgrade everyday life, with sliding glass doors providing access to the picturesque exterior space, a modern marble fireplace, and a funky bar.

The main level also includes a "chic powder room," and a private en-suite bedroom that could be well-suited to serve as a home office or a welcoming space for guests.

The remainder of the bedrooms are upstairs, including the lavish, light-flooded primary suite. The room feels like a true escape, thanks to its expansive wraparound balcony, sizable walk-in closet, and spa-inspired bathroom with an oversized shower and relaxing soaking tub.

While this property conveniently provides easy-access to everything a busy family could need, it's also the kind of place they'll want to spend as much time as possible enjoying.

The tranquil outdoor space is truly a vacation-like getaway, primed for relaxation and entertaining alike.

The lush greenery can be enjoyed from the large oversized outdoor living area. The impressive pool is certainly the trophy of the backyard, and features a spacious spa, accessible by a pair of stone steps.

A separate pool house is exceptionally versatile, with a gorgeous bathroom and space that can be utilized by guests or the residents who may want to dedicate additional room to poolside living.

There's also a four-car garage complete with custom epoxy flooring. Functional luxuries include a whole-house water filtration system, smart home technology, and solar panels that have already been fully paid-off.
