Affirm: Affirm Bank Strategic Finance Lead

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most.

The Finance team ensures Affirm remains financially sound and strategically positioned for growth. Our team manages financial planning, accounting, pricing, vendor management, tax, investor relations, and corporate development. We deliver timely insights, accurate reporting, and careful analysis to support decision-making. From day-to-day financial operations to major investments, we enable sustainable, informed growth by maintaining strong fiscal discipline.

We are looking for a self-motivated, hardworking, and analytically minded professional to join the Strategic Finance team during a pivotal moment of growth! As Affirm Bank is currently in its formation phase, we are actively building out our foundational team. Reporting to the Bank Head of Strategic Finance, the Bank Strategic Finance Lead will play a key role in the formation and operational build-out of the Bank. You will develop and monitor Bank financial and KPI forecasts, drive strategy, and perform financial analyses throughout the Bank. Your work will help set Bank-level goals, annual budgets, capital stress testing and monitoring, and stakeholder guidance, while building the infrastructure to hold the Bank accountable to achieving its goals. You will have a unique opportunity to work cross-functionally and use quantitative insights to drive better decision-making across the organization. The ideal candidate will not only have superb technical skills but also strong interpersonal skills and the ability to influence cross-functionally and present to all levels of the Bank organization.

Employees new to Affirm typically come in at the start of the pay range. Affirm focuses on providing a simple and transparent pay structure which is based on a variety of factors, including location, experience and job-related skills.

Base pay is part of a total compensation package that may include equity rewards, monthly stipends for health, wellness and tech spending, and benefits (including 100% subsidized medical coverage, dental and vision for you and your dependents.)

USA base pay range (CA, WA, NY, NJ, CT) per year: $145,000 - $205,000

USA base pay range (All other US states) per year: $128,000 - $188,000

Please note that visa sponsorship is not available for this position.

All new hires will be invited to attend an in-person onboarding experience.
