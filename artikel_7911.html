If there's one thing Miley Cyrus knows how to do, it's make a statement. And her recent wedding to musician Maxx Morando has given us plenty of inspiration – particularly when it comes to the flowers.

The couple's romantic ceremony featured masses of beautiful white roses, with a breathtaking floral arch creating a dreamy backdrop for the celebrations. It's classic, understated and wonderfully romantic. And, honestly? We're already dreaming about recreating the look in our own backyards. The good news is that November is one of the best times to get started. Bare-root roses are coming into season, making this the perfect opportunity to plant your own romantic rose display – often for considerably less than you'd spend on established container-grown plants.

So, while Miley might have had a team of florists to bring her wedding vision to life, you can take a slightly more long-term approach. Plant now, give your roses time to establish over winter, and you could be enjoying your own beautiful floral display come spring and summer. After all, as Miley herself once reminded us, we can absolutely buy ourselves flowers. But growing them ourselves? Even better.

There's something wonderfully timeless about an all-white flower display. While colorful planting schemes certainly have their place, sticking to one color can create a much more elegant look – and white roses, as Miley Cyrus' wedding photos have proven, are particularly lovely for this.

The beauty of this look is that you don't need hundreds of individual plants to make an impact. A well-positioned rose bush, a climbing variety trained over an arch or trellis, or even a small collection of white roses along a garden path can create a n atmosphere that's just as romantic.

Better still? Well, there's no need to wait until spring to start planning your own rose garden, because November is actually the ideal time to get planting. Bare-root roses become available from late fall and are generally much more affordable than their container-grown counterparts, making them a brilliant option if you're hoping to plant several together.

If you're looking for something that combines good looks with wildlife appeal, this shrub rose produces fragrant white blooms, followed by ornamental rose hips that provide additional seasonal interest.

For something a little closer to the full-bodied blooms seen at Miley's wedding, this floribunda variety produces gorgeous double white blooms, sometimes with the faintest blush of pink.

Of course, if it's the dramatic floral arch you're hoping to recreate, you'll want to consider a climbing rose. This pretty variety is ideal for training over arches, pergolas and trellises.

The good news? You don't need an elaborate wedding installation to achieve that same Miley Cyrus-inspired look; you just need a sturdy garden arch like this pergola from Outsunny, and pop it somewhere that gets plenty of sunlight. (Most roses need at least six hours of direct sunlight each day).

Next, plant a climbing rose on either side of your structure, allowing the canes to grow and training them horizontally and upwards as they develop. Be patient, though, as your rose arch won't become an overnight sensation, and it may take a couple of growing seasons to achieve the look you're craving.

And there you have it: a little celebrity-inspired garden magic without the celebrity price tag. Miley Cyrus' wedding might have lasted just one day, but with the right roses and a little patience, you can enjoy your own romantic floral display year after year. Talk about a love story worth growing, eh?
