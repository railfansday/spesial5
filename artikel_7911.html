He even bonded with my other cat during this time. For a while, we were able to forget about his palliative condition.

In early August, his breathing became laboured again, and he refused to eat his renal food, and I knew his kidneys would deteriorate fast on a normal diet. In the next two weeks, his appetite dropped, he hid more frequently around the house, and became increasingly lethargic.

From 20 August 2026 (Thursday) onwards, he would barely manage more than a few licks a day, and his weight dropped a lot. We could feel his spine and rib cage! He had not urinated for more than 24 hours, and we were so worried that his kidneys had totally shut down.

On Friday, miraculously, he urinated a few times and had some licks of food. His last meal was on Saturday night.

Tiger passed away naturally and peacefully at home on Sunday, 23 August 2026. He was so weak and lethargic, with barely any energy left, but still managed to get inside his little box and slept there. In this final moment, my sister calmed him to his final breath, and we were fortunate to witness his final, peaceful passing.

Throughout this ordeal, Tiger was very, very resilient and wanted to live, but he did not get the chance.

We held a private cremation for him on 24 August 2026, complete with Taoist chanting to guide him on his way. His ashes are stored at Mandai Pet Sanctuary in Singapore.

Despite the financial, emotional, and time sacrifices, it was an honour to take care of Tiger in the final stage of his life.

Now the room feels so empty in his absence.
