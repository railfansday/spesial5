Opinion: Liverpool ready to commit to signing, decision to be finalised

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Liverpool are ready to commit to a new signing. The decision is set to be finalised shortly.

Planning is underway for the 2027 transfer windows at clubs around the world. The first stop is January, of course, but with it always being a difficult window, focus on the summer usually takes up the bulk of the work.

For Liverpool, that means sorting out some contract situations. The big two are unquestionably Virgil van Dijk and Alisson Becker, though it's completely unclear what they plan to do.

The pair have been at Anfield so long that we imagine proper talks will be delayed until the end of the season. There's no enormous rush, after all, even if them being able to talk to non-English clubs from January 1st is a bit stressful.

But they're not the only decisions that need to be made. At least one other contract choice appears close to being made - and with that comes a big follow-on effect.

Of course, that also means committing to a new left-back. Tsimikas is currently the no.2 in the role at Liverpool, behind Milos Kerkez. The club won't go into next season with only one option, so we all expect them to strike a deal for someone new.

And who might that be? It's entirely unclear. In fact, we imagine Liverpool will wait until closer to summer before finalising their shortlist and ignore the January window. At least for left-back options.

There isn't, after all, a clear signing to make in the role and waiting to see how things develop over the next six or so months makes more sense than rushing into anything. But it does mean we can say with confidence that someone will arrive after this season is over.

Liverpool have a few ways to go about it, too. They could opt for a young development project, or someone with experienced, or a homegrown player, or maybe go all-out to replace Kerkez and relegate him to the backup role.

We're not sure. We doubt Liverpool are, either, as all options hold some sort of merit. It'll just be a case of waiting and seeing.
