Bruno Guimaraes reveals Arsenal star who helped him adapt to Mikel Arteta demands

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

Bruno Guimaraes has revealed the one piece of advice Declan Rice gave him that helped him settle after joining Arsenal.

The Brazilian midfielder sealed an acrimonious move from Newcastle to north London in August after an extended transfer saga, and had fresh silverware to his name almost immediately after beating Manchester City in the Community Shield.

But despite his prompt success, he has now revealed he has yet to properly wrap his head around Mikel Arteta’s “very complicated” tactics, which affords him far more freedom than at previous clubs.

“I think in my other teams, in Newcastle and Lyon, you always play so positional,” he explained in an interview with Prime Video. “Here you rotate a lot.

“Sometimes I look at myself, and I'm playing as a defender, as a right-back, positions where I’ve never been before.

“So now, I understand if, for example, Martin Odegaard comes here, I have to go here, or there [he points]. So it’s all about details.”

The Brazilian’s place in Arteta’s side has become more assured of late as he gains a better understanding of his demands. He has appeared 15 times for Arsenal so far this season, scoring twice.

Guimaraes continued, revealing that when he first arrived in north London, Rice urged him to be patient as he attempted to learn Arteta’s tactical style.

“It’s very complicated to explain to you because it took me ages to understand what he wants.

“But I think I’m getting there, and I remember when I came, Declan Rice said to me, ‘Bruno, be patient, because it took me like six months to understand exactly what he wants. So be patient, don’t push too much’.

“I think now with four months in the club, I’m understanding.”
