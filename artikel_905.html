British amateur star Moss-Birch signs with Jake Paul's MVP

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Gifted 21-year-old Amber Moss-Birch, a World Youth champion as an amateur, has signed with Jake Paul's Most Valuable Promotions; This is the first time MVP have signed a British amateur ahead of their pro debut

Amber Moss-Birch, a 21-year-old amateur star, has signed with Jake Paul’s company, Most Valuable Promotions.

A brilliant amateur boxer, Moss-Birch became a World Youth champion in 2022. She's also won European gold and bronze medals at age group level among a host of accolades.

This is the first time MVP have signed a British amateur ahead of their pro debut.

Nakisa Bidarian and Jake Paul, the founders of MVP, said: "The UK is the pound-for-pound champion of boxing talent and we are thrilled to sign our first British amateur, Amber Moss-Birch, one of the country's most exciting young amateur fighters.

"Amber has the determination and discipline to become a major force in the professional ranks. As we continue to expand and build the future of boxing, we're committed to giving exceptional young fighters like Amber the opportunities and platform to become global stars. We look forward to her professional debut in early 2027."

Moss-Birch's decorated amateur career includes gold at the 2019 European School Boxing Championships in Tbilisi, Georgia, and bronze at the 2022 European Youth Boxing Championships in Sofia, Bulgaria.

Later that year, she captured gold at the 2022 World Youth championships in La Nucia, Spain, delivering a dominant first-round stoppage of Uzbekistan's Oltinoy Sotimboeva in the 81kgs light-heavyweight final.

Throughout her amateur career, Moss-Birch amassed 14 international gold medals and four national championships.

Moss-Birch said: "I am absolutely over the moon signing with MVP. I have been watching what they have been doing for women's boxing, and this decision was a no-brainer. I sparred Chantelle Cameron for her last camp, and after speaking with her about MVP, I could not wait to make the move. I have signed a very good contract, and their plans for me are very exciting.

"I'm looking forward to making my debut in early 2027 and my partnership with Jake, Nakisa and the team."
