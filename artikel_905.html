Jamaica level series with dominant win over England

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Jamaica bounced back from losing their opening match to level the four-Test series against the Vitality Roses; England will travel to Jamaica to play further matches on December 5 and 6, as preparation continues for next year's Netball World Cup

England failed to extend their lead in the Vitality Netball Horizon Series with Jamaica, as the visitors claimed a dominant 55-40 win at the Copper Box Arena.

A day on from a dramatic 47-46 victory in the series opener at the same London venue, England were second-best as the Sunshine Girls claimed a comfortable victory and levelled the four-Test series.

The visitors scored six consecutive points on their way to opening a 15-7 advantage after the first quarter, then the Commonwealth Games silver medallists extended their lead to 11 points by half-time.

A tight third quarter saw Jamaica edge further ahead, with the Sunshine Girls never relinquishing their advantage as they closed out a convincing win on away soil.

"We expected Jamaica to come out hard and they certainly did deliver on that," England coach Anna Stembridge admitted.

"We didn't set the tone, we were wasteful at times. We worked through it, but they left us wanting after the first quarter."

England were again without key players Liv Tchine, Alicia Scholes and Sasha Glasgow due to injury, as they had been the previous day, while Jamaica's Latanya Wilson was named player of the match.

Jamaica made 17 gains to England's 11 and had a better shooting percentage than the hosts, who went with the same starting seven as Saturday's victory.

"We started sharper, created more options, we were more aggressive and played to our strengths," Jamaica coach Sasher Gaye Henry-Wright said.

"We didn't stick to our game plan yesterday and today we were more disciplined."

England will now travel to Jamaica for the final two matches of the series, held at the National Indoor Sports Centre in Kingston on December 5 and 6.
