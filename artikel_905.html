How Aspyn Ovard's Throuple With Married Couple Plays Out on SLOMW: OC

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County star Aspyn Ovard exclusively told E! why she feels the truth about her relationship with married couple Bri and Billy Davis will take viewers by surprise.

And the influencer did exactly that by taking fans inside her relationship with married couple Bri Davis and Billy Davis while filming The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County.

"There's a lot of different arcs of my life that are shared on the show," Aspyn exclusively told E! News. "But I am excited to be representation of a different kind of relationship."

And there's a reason the 30-year-old looks forward to viewers getting a look at her life in a throuple on Hulu’s Secret Lives of Mormon Wives spinoff, premiering Nov. 12.

As she put it, "Because there's just not a lot out there, and it's easy to share for me. And I know it's not easy for everybody."

Though the YouTuber—who filed for divorce from ex Parker Ferris in 2024 after eight years of marriage—admitted exploring the new chapter of her sexuality was "a big deal" for her, fans shouldn't expect any major plot twists while witnessing her current relationship.

"I'm very curious to kind of see what people will say because I think that I've seen some crazy theories, and it's just not that crazy of a thing," Aspyn, who began sharing about her bisexuality online following her breakup with her high school sweetheart, noted with a laugh. "I think unfortunately people will be a little bit underwhelmed."

Still, the mom of three girls—Cove, 7, Lola, 5 and Elle, 2—clarified, "It was very overwhelming for me."

Ahead of Mormon Wives: OC's release, Aspyn and Bri detailed how their love story came together—including how Aspyn signaling her queer identity led to Bri reaching out.

So, when the TikToker suggested, "What if we all hung out?" referencing the nurse's husband, Bri was open to expanding their connection.

"I was like, 'I’m not opposed.' I already really like you, and I knew her vibe and her energy, and I knew Billy’s vibe and energy," Bri explained in their Oct. 11 YouTube diary. "I was like, 'They kind of match.' Like, I kind of—I can see the vision."

In addition to Aspyn's love life, the months leading up to the end of costar Jen Affleck's relationship with estranged husband Zac Affleck will unfold on SLOMW: OC. Get filled in on where all the show's couples stand ahead of the premiere.

Following her divorce from Cory Althoff, podcaster Bobbi Althoff is dating Tyler Hawkins, who plays a special role in Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County.

"He styles me, that’s how he supports me,” she told E! News in May, adding that the basketball player, "picks out my outfits every day. He styles me every day for the show. It’s great."

Aspyn Ovard, who filed for divorce from Parker Ferris in 2024 after eight years of marriage, is currently in a throuple relationship with married couple Billy Davis and Bri Davis.

After many ups and downs, Jen Affleck—who is pregnant with her and Zac Affleck's fourth baby—officially filed for divorce from her husband of seven years, E! News confirmed Nov. 4.

Despite ongoing relationship speculation, Salomé Andrea and Aaron Brimhall are still going strong as they prepare to welcome their third baby together. The duo, who married in 2012, also share 8-year-old twins Luca and Rue.

McCall DaPron—the sister of Mormon Wives' Mayci Neeley—shares four kids with husband Kirk DaPron, who she wed in 2011.

Ashleigh Pease and Nate Pease are rock solid after over a decade of marriage. "This 13th year together is one that has felt full circle!" she wrote on Instagram in September. "A season of fruits. Comfort. The happiest milestones."

Madison Bontempo and Kyler Steven Fisher, who wed in 2013, have been open about the highs and lows of their relationship. "Kyler and I aren’t perfect," she wrote on Instagram in October. "We’ve had arguments. We’ve had hard seasons. We’ve had moments we wish we could take back. And we’re not afraid to admit that."

But, she added, "Those moments aren’t our whole story. There’s also laughter, friendship, forgiveness, growth, five beautiful children, a million memories, and a love that has continued to evolve through every season of our lives."

Avery Woods and David Woods, parents to Ziggy and Stevie, are still feeling the love after a decade of marriage. As she simply put it, "my forever + always."
