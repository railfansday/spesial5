Miley Cyrus and Husband Maxx Morando’s Relationship Timeline

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Something to sing about! Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando have tied the knot.

The two musicians met on a blind date in 2021. They were later spotted dancing together at Miley’s New Year’s Eve Party special in January 2022, where the Grammy winner also sang a song called “You,” which many fans believed to be about Morando.

Several weeks later, an insider told Us Weekly that the duo were in a “new relationship.”

“They’re still getting to know each other at the moment,” the insider said in March 2022. “They’re having fun and have been spending a lot of time together.” The source added that the pair, who have a six-year age difference, are “very compatible with each other.”

In December 2025, Us confirmed that Cyrus and Morando got engaged after four years of dating. They tied the knot the following November in an intimate California ceremony.
