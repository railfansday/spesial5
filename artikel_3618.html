Zelda Ocarina of Time guides and walkthroughs

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake for Nintendo Switch 2 is more than just a graphical overhaul of the game. Some of the puzzles you may remember from the original game have been changed, and Link can even jump now. Rather than racking your brain trying to remember how you beat the Water Temple back in 1998 (or perhaps 2003 or 2011), let us guide the way.

We have complete walkthroughs for each of the dungeons, from the Great Deku Tree all the way to Ganondorf's Castle. We also have guides for the little stuff in between, like where to find all the Cuccos in Kakariko Village. What's a Zelda game without helping out some of the townspeople along the way, right?

Additionally, our guides will help you find every collectible you need in the game, from bottles to Heart Containers. Toss Navi to the side and allow us to be your guide.
