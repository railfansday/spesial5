Why Princess Diana Dressed William and Harry Identically, Even Though They Didn’t Like It

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Princess Diana had a keen sense of style. It was a talent the late princess (who arrived at court at a very young age and was therefore still unfamiliar with the ins and outs of high fashion) developed over the years with the help of experts in the field—and which has repeatedly earned her the title of style icon.

It comes as no surprise, then, that her keen eye for new trends and her passion for the most sought-after pieces were also reflected in the fashion choices she made for her two children, Prince William and Prince Harry, whom she loved to dress alike from a very young age, no matter the occasion.

Prince Charles and Princess Diana with their sons, William and Harry. July 1986.

Princess Diana, it must be said, was neither the first nor the last mother to choose identical outfits for her children, but it seems this habit was not merely a fashion game. According to a report by Marie Claire, the princess’s former butler, Paul Burrell, reportedly said that Diana’s choice to dress William and Harry identically was a way to reinforce the idea that, in her eyes, there was no difference between the two—even though everyone around her showed preferential treatment toward the eldest son, the future heir to the throne.

Princess Diana with her sons, William and Harry. August 1986.

That’s why the princess waged her own battle, even choosing to dress her two sons in matching outfits, almost as if to convey a sense of unity and equality in their appearance. If these were indeed some of the reasons behind the brothers’ identical looks, it certainly wouldn’t be surprising. We know full well how Diana insisted on equal treatment for her two sons, who, for their part, apparently didn’t much care for their mother’s tendency to choose identical outfits. “The boys hated that, by the way; they didn’t want to be dressed the same,” Burrell added.

Princess Diana with her sons, William and Harry. July 1988.

This statement has also been confirmed by Prince Harry himself. In the 2017 television documentary, Diana, Our Mother: Her Life and Legacy, which aired on the 20th anniversary of Princess Diana’s death, her two sons spoke openly about their mother. The younger son said with a smile: “I genuinely think she got satisfaction out of dressing myself and William up in the most bizarre outfits—normally matching. It was weird shorts and little shiny shoes with the old clip-on.”

Princess Diana with her sons, William and Harry. August 1989.

Bermuda shorts and patent leather shoes with straps—all the rage in the ’80s and ’90s—were, in fact, two of the key elements of the outfits Diana put together for her boys. The lineup also included matching shirts, polos, and sweaters. “Looking back at the photos, it just makes me laugh, and I think, ‘How could you do that to us?’” Harry added with a touch of irony.

As they grew up, the two, of course, began to rebel against this tradition—particularly William, who, according to Harry’s account, at one point refused to have anything to do with being his brother’s twin. “Funnily enough, we got to the age when William would turn round and go: ‘Oh, this is ridiculous. You know, I’m the older brother. Why do I have to be dressed the same as him?’” he recalled. “Hang on a second. If you’re going to dress differently, I’m not going to be the only person dressed like this. This is just ridiculous!” Harry replied.

As with any growing boy, things gradually began to change, and the two brothers started to develop their own independent style. But one can’t help but pause to consider how every choice—even the most trivial, like picking out an outfit—was a statement of intent for the beloved princess. Always in the name of love.
