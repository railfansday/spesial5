Russell Brand Issues Statement, Says He Is 'Not Guilty' Amid Rape Sentence

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Russell Brand has issued a statement from prison, claiming that he is “not guilty” after being sentenced to 10 years and six months for rape and sexual assault.

“Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, God bless you for your support. As you know, I am not guilty for the offenses I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening,” the comedian, 51, said in a statement shared by his wife, Laura Brand, via his YouTube channel on Sunday, November 15.

Laura, 39, has begun by stating that “Russell has asked me to read something to you today,” before turning to a notebook to recall her husband’s message. The YouTube post served as the first time Laura had publicly addressed Russell’s conviction and prison sentence, which was delivered in London’s Southwark Crown Court on Monday, November 9.

Russell’s message continued, “The good news is that Christ is with us when we choose him, and I do. I am incarcerated in HMP Littlehey. Little as in small, and hey as in, ‘Hey, I’m in the biggest sex offenders prison in Europe.’ The good news is that I have already seen that the staff here are strong, they are kind, and they are dedicated. And as for my fellow prisoners, I now know that I am in no position to judge anyone.”

Russell went to trial for four weeks, ultimately found guilty by a jury on one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault. Russell had repeatedly denied accusations of wrongdoing, and was found not guilty on two other rape charges and an attempted indecent assault charge.

Laura, who shares three children with Russell, concluded on behalf of Russell, “I don’t know which other men here are not guilty, and the ones that are, they are people that Christ died to save, like you and me. Let us all love, pray for, and forgive one another as God forgave us. Please support my treasured wife and family. I feel your prayers. Your brother in Christ, Russell.”

After receiving his conviction and sentencing, Russell has since been accused of misconduct by six additional women. London’s Metropolitan Police later confirmed to Us that he remains under investigation for additional rape allegations from two unidentified women.

A Met spokesperson stated to Us, “The suspect has previously been interviewed under caution in connection with the allegations. Files have been submitted to the Crown Prosecution Service in relation to both complainants. Detectives remain in close contact with prosecutors as enquiries continue and further investigative work is carried out.”

Elsewhere in the YouTube video, Laura shared support for her husband, adding that she will continue sharing social media videos after discussing the future of them with Russell.

“Obviously, [it] will take some getting used to for me to even sit here and not have my companion and my person with me,” she told fans. “Although Russell is not here now in this moment, the holy spirit is moving and the holy spirit cannot be restrained. I’m speaking to Russell. He’s OK and he’s praying, unsurprisingly. He’s praying and we decided for me to continue.”
