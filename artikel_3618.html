Yes, You Can Keep Playing GTA 6 After The Credits Roll And Don’t Worry, That’s Not A Spoiler

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Grand Theft Auto 6 is a very, very big game with tons of main missions, side missions, activities, random events, races, and more to find and complete. So you might feel compelled to clear out the map before reaching the finale. But you don’t need to do that.

In past Rockstar Games, like GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2, the studio has let players return to the open world after finishing the main story. However, how this has been handled has varied. In RDR2, you take on the role of another character who can complete activities and side missions after the credits roll. In GTA IV, the game just lets you keep playing post-credits as Niko. This is how it works in most GTA games, actually. Things are a bit different in GTA 6.

Instead, before you enter the final mission, which took me about 90 hours or so to reach in my playthrough, the game makes a big point of asking if you want to go forward with the finale or not. Once you wrap up that big, multi-mission finale and see one of the game’s main endings, GTA 6 tosses you back in time to that moment when you were asked if you wanted to finish the story or not. But this time, Jason and Lucia say no; they aren’t ready, and head back out into Vice City. At that point, you can keep playing GTA 6, wrap up everything on the map, and explore. (The finale mission marker remains on the map if you want to finish the game again and possibly get a different ending.)

It’s an interesting way to handle the endgame and is unlike any past Grand Theft Auto. It also lets GTA 6‘s ending go in a lot of different directions, depending on your choices. I’m not going to even hint at what those endings are, but I’ve seen two of them and trust me, they play out in wildly different and sometimes surprising ways.

The one downside to this approach is that GTA 6 feels a bit frozen in time at the end of the game. No new major storylines can be introduced, as they would possibly get in the way of the narrative wrapping up.

But hey, once you beat the final mission, you unlock the ability to reach 100 percent completion and even unlock the ability to complete challenges to reveal in-game cheat codes that let you go wild in Vice City. So consider it a vacation for Jason and Lucia after all your hard work as a criminal.
