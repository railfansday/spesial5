Stock Market Today, Nov. 11: Stock Market Today, Nov. 11: Nasdaq Surges as CoreWeave's 25% Rally Reignites the AI Trade

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Wall Street staged a strong recovery Wednesday as enthusiasm for AI infrastructure returned, lifting semiconductor and technology stocks despite persistent concerns about inflation.

Wall Street rallied on Wednesday, November 11, as renewed optimism surrounding artificial intelligence (AI) fueled a broad recovery in technology stocks following Tuesday's inflation-driven sell-off. The Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +1.60%) led major indexes higher, climbing 1.60% to 25,466, while the S&P 500 (^GSPC +1.31%) gained 1.29% to 7,618. The Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI +0.49%) advanced a more modest 0.49% to 52,486.

Gold prices declined 0.98% to $3,990.70, reflecting a shift in investor appetite toward equities. Utilities led sector gains, followed by strength in technology, while consumer cyclical stocks lagged.

CoreWeave (CRWV +24.71%) shares surged 24.71% after reporting record quarterly results and raising its revenue outlook, while Micron Technology (MU +11.89%) gained 11.89% as semiconductor names recovered from earlier weekly losses. Oracle (ORCL +8.81%) advanced 8.83% following news of record energy-saving milestones for its utility customers, and Apple (AAPL +2.04%) rose 2.04% to reach a fresh record high during the session.

Wednesday's rally offered an encouraging sign for investors following Tuesday's inflation-driven sell-off, but the underlying economic concerns haven't disappeared. CoreWeave's strong earnings and improved revenue outlook helped reinforce confidence in AI infrastructure spending, suggesting that demand for computing capacity remains robust despite concerns about elevated valuations and the substantial cost of expansion.

However, the recovery was particularly strong among technology and semiconductor stocks, leaving questions about the breadth and sustainability of the rally. Continued gains will likely depend on whether corporate earnings can justify elevated valuations, particularly as higher interest rates increase financing costs and reduce the appeal of future earnings relative to fixed-income investments.

Looking ahead, investors should watch whether the S&P 500 can sustain its recovery as bond trading resumes following Veterans Day. With the 10-year Treasury yield approaching 5% before the holiday, renewed upward pressure on yields could challenge the rally. Upcoming earnings from Nvidia will also provide an important test of AI demand heading into 2027. A sustained year-end advance will likely require both resilient corporate earnings and greater confidence in the Federal Reserve's interest rate outlook.
