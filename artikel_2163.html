"I didn't really like him" - James Trafford's first thoughts on new West Ham boss Scott Parker

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Leeds United goalkeeper James Trafford has revealed his initial relationship with new West Ham United boss Scott Parker was difficult during their time together at Burnley.

The duo were part of a record-breaking Burnley side, with the club accumulating 100 points and keeping a record 30 clean sheets in the second tier. They conceded just 16 goals as they were promoted alongside Leeds United.

Now languishing around the relegation zone in the Championship, James Trafford has joined Leeds, while West Ham United have Scott Parker in charge. It's all change across the three clubs, highlighting just how quickly things can change in football.

Trafford has revealed that, despite Burnley's excellent form with him in goal, Parker did not see eye-to-eye with the goalkeeper. The 24-year-old spoke to the BBC and revealed his thoughts.

He said: "Yeah, I had a big one with Scott Parker. We had a very, very weird start to my time at Burnley with him. And I don't think he liked me too much.

"I didn't really like him too much. It was just weird. We didn't like, we couldn't connect, it was hard. But then, by the end of it, he understood me really, really well. And we got along really, really well."

He was asked: "How did you get to that point?"

Trafford added: "I don't know. I actually don't know."

The interviewer replied: "It probably helped that you were playing pretty well on the pitch, didn't it?"

Trafford concluded: "Probably. Yeah, but he really understood me."

Trafford's quirkiness wasn't for everyone but some enjoyed his company at Burnley, including Bristol City's Scott Twine. He spoke of their friendship at Turf Moor, adding: "Well, I mean, I have Pokemon cards. Love Pokemon cards. Oh, I love them."

The interviewer asked: "What do you trade them?"

He said: "No, just open the pack. Cool. Good card. Put them in my binder. Collecting all the sets.

"I've always done it. I remember I used to live with Scott Twine and we were so bored in a pre-season and we did loads of different things.

"And one of them was just go buy them. Everything had to be a competition as well, so we ended up getting big into tennis. That was one thing.

"We used to play card games against each other on our phones and then the other thing was Pokemon cards. We used to just open them. It's just something I've done since."

On his relationship with Lawrence Vigoroux and how that influenced him at Turf Moor, Trafford also said: "Obviously, there's a lot of other goalies that I have watched and really like parts of their game, like Lawrence Vigoroux. I really enjoyed working with him. He had some really good things.

"Then, obviously, everyone talks about [Manuel] Neuer, [Thibaut] Courtois, and like the big hitters, but also some goalies that haven't

played at that level that are still [big influences]. You can take bits from their game."

Burnley supporters may be surprised to hear that Trafford and Parker did not immediately click, given how successful their partnership ultimately became at Turf Moor.

Trafford was outstanding behind a side that set Championship records during the 2024/25 promotion campaign, while Parker's organisation helped produce one of the most formidable defensive units the second tier has seen.
