Bristol City fans react to Michael Skubala's post-Middlesbrough interview - It's brutal

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Bristol City's slide down the Championship table continued on Saturday, as they were beaten 2-0 by high-flying Middlesbrough at the Riverside.

Adilson Malanda opened the scoring for Boro on the day from a set-piece, as he found the back of the net just five points from the half-time whistle sounding.

David Strelec doubled Middlesbrough's lead early in the second half, and the hosts were comfortable from then on, as they held onto top spot in the Championship.

The defeat means that Bristol City are struggling for any sort of confidence, as they've only got one win to their name from their last seven Championship matches, which makes for dismal viewing.

The Robins are now 18th in the table heading into the next round of fixtures, and plenty of the club's supporters have been quick to voice their frustrations towards manager Martin Skubala, who is seemingly under increasing pressure heading towards the festive period.

Speaking in a post-match interview with the club's official site, Skubala issued his thoughts on the defeat, and felt as though his side weren't clinical enough in front of goal, which could have changed the course of the game.

"We set up a certain way, to try and get them in transition," Skubala admitted.

"I think for the first half, they didn't create loads, but we go one down from a set-piece that we should do better at, and then in the second half, we knew we could create some more chances.

"The second goal probably kills us, but then you come away when we've hit the post three times, so we weren't clinical enough when those chances came our end. I think if you score one of those, then it's a very different game for the last 10/15 minutes. Disappointing afternoon to not get anything from the game."

The Bristol City boss was left frustrated by his side conceding from a set-piece shortly before half-time, and revealed that they covered the threat that Middlesbrough had from those areas.

He also addressed the club's recent slump in form, which has irritated the club's fanbase, who would have expected more from their side.

"We worked hard and spoke about the threats, like the set-pieces, and it's always a challenge when a team sets up like them, and we're in the game and giving nothing away," Skubala added.

"We're a little bit lower to break, but you don't want to concede on a set-piece. I think if we go in at 0-0, it's a different game, but the second one hurts us a lot.

"I'm not saying we created loads, but we were set up to hit them on the break. When you set up like that, you have to take your chances. We have to do better, we're in a tough spot, which is not through want of trying, and it's not through no effort, but we've got to keep working hard.

"Football's about looking forward all the time and we have to go to the next game, which is going to be at home, where we want to try and get a result."

Bristol City have plenty of talent in their squad, but for one reason or another, they're just not clicking at a crucial stage of the season, which begs whether a managerial change is needed.

Skubala has only been in charge of the Robins since May 2026, after taking charge of the club after guiding Lincoln City to promotion from League One.

Bristol City are set to return to action against Wrexham on Tuesday evening, which is likely to be another tough test, especially considering Wrexham are unbeaten in their last three matches.

You can't take anything for granted, but you would imagine that a defeat for the Robins in that match against Phil Parkinson's side could spell the end of a frustrating spell in charge of Bristol City for Skubala.

Unsurprisingly, Bristol City fans took to social media to react to Skubala's recent comments after the defeat to Middlesbrough, which was their third straight defeat in the Championship.

Leave with dignity was a call from plenty of fans.
