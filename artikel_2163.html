‘We are concerned’: Aribim Pepple among triple Plymouth Argyle injury worries ahead of Sheffield Wednesday tie

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Plymouth Argyle sit 5th in the League One table coming into this weekend’s clash with Sheffield Wednesday. It marks an important game for PAFC given their opponents are also in the promotion conversation, but Tom Cleverley and co have a new trio of injuries to deal with.

Speaking to The Herald, the Pilgrims boss revealed muscle injuries are casting doubt over the availability of Aribim Pepple, Alex Mitchell and Michael Baidoo. Cleverley said: “We are concerned with a few of them. We have had a good result tonight and whatever team we take to Sheffield on Saturday we will have full confidence that it can go and do a job and get a win.”

Cleverley later described Pepple and Mitchell specifically as “major doubts”. If they are indeed sidelined, they will join Julio Pleguezuelo as a recent injury blow having damaged his calf in training last week.

Centre-back Mitchell is a powerful figure in defence and a mainstay for Cleverley. He will be a miss for Plymouth Argyle, but there is the depth to deal with his absence with Mathias Ross and Alex Hartridge both available.

Baidoo meanwhile has been building towards his comeback, so a setback comes as a blow. When fully fit, there is intrigue over whether he can grow into an influential player for Cleverley after a tough time at Home Park since joining in January 2025.

Of the three though, Pepple is the most influential. The Canadian striker is PAFC’s leading goalscorer and main attacking threat, offering excellent finishing and link-up ability as a powerful figure at the top of the pitch.

Losing his clinical finishing and ability to lead the line will be a big blow against Sheffield Wednesday. It will be hoped that Pepple is not facing a long spell out, as it will leave a sizeable gap in the frontline to fill.

Cleverley will be forced into changes from his usual lineup if Pepple and Mitchell are ruled out. He has the depth to deal with the absences, although the Plymouth Argyle side is undoubtedly weaker without them.

In defence, the earlier mentioned Alex Hartridge or Mathias Ross could come into the team. Wes Harding has mainly been used at full-time this term but the vice captain can be used centrally if Cleverley wishes.

At striker, it depends on the setup Cleverley opts for. Will Evans seems bound to start either way, but if he wants to partner him with another forward in a 4-4-2, Owen Oseni may be introduced after his recent comeback from injury.

Oliver Irow has been played up front before too, so the PAFC boss has decisions to make.
