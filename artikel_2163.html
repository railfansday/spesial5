‘Bigger teams will come good’: EFL expert sends Birmingham City promotion warning amid stunning Championship form

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Birmingham City are top of the table after their first 15 fixtures of the season. BCFC are above Middlesbrough and Stoke City on goal difference following their 2-1 win over Swansea City last time out before the international break.

Attacker Jay Stansfield scored both of the Blues’ goals against the Swans last Saturday. They are back in action with an away trip to Stoke City next weekend.

In the meantime, McAnuff, who played for the likes of Cardiff City, Watford and Reading, has delivered his honest verdict on the Midlands outfit on the latest epiode of BBC’s 72+ Podcast: “Under his (Tom Wagner) guidance, after everything he has put into the club, there is no reason why with such an open start to the league, this could be the season particularly going down to eight teams this year, where they can’t at least get (into the play-offs).

“I’m sure they’ll have designs on finishing higher. I do think some of the bigger teams will come good later on in the season.”

Birmingham City re-signed James Beadle on loan from Brighton and Hove Albion in the summer. BCFC’s goalkeeper is a year older now and is backed up on the bench by Ryan Allsop.

The Blues are rock solid at the back and have the best defensive record in the division just ahead of West brom and Southampton. They have Christoph Klarer and Phil Neumann to thank for that and the pair have both been excellent.

Further up the pitch, the likes of Paik Seung-ho, Tomoki Iwata and Jhon Solis are useful players in the middle of the park. They are energetic and can create chances for their attacking.

Stansfield, Liam Millar, Patrick Roberts, Carlos Vicente and August Priske are all dangerous forward places for Birmingham City. They can cause serious problems to opposition defenders and Chris Davies’ side are good on the break.

Birmingham City are looking strong at the moment and will be in confident mood. BCFC don’t concede many goals and are built on strong foundations.

The Blues will get the chance to bring in some new faces when the January transfer window opens up for business. They will also have the opportunity to let some players head out the exit door as well if needed this winter to help free up space and funds to pave the way for reinforcements.

Davies’ men were promoted from League One in 2025 along with Wrexham and Charlton Athletic and they finished 10th last term as they adapted to the step up a division. They have really found their feet this season and will fancy their chances against anyone right now as they gear up for a tricky trip to Stoke City next up.
