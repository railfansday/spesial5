Hollywood Reacts to Robert Pattinson’s James Bond Casting: Florence Pugh, Brad Pitt, Rami Malek, More

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The actor was tapped as the next 007, taking over the iconic role from Daniel Craig, who concluded his run with 2021’s 'No Time to Die.'

After it was revealed that Robert Pattinson was tapped as the next 007, fellow industry notables are speaking out to congratulate him on the casting.

On Monday, it was announced that Pattinson would be taking over the iconic James Bond role from Daniel Craig, who concluded his run with 2021’s No Time to Die. He’ll star in the upcoming film from director Denis Villeneuve.

“I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin,” the filmmaker said in a statement. Some of Pattinson’s former co-stars, such as Florence Pugh (Dune: Part Three), Elizabeth Debicki (Tenet), Bryce Dallas Howard (The Twilight Saga: Eclipse), Ralph Fiennes (Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire) and Rami Malek (The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 2), and fellow celebrities, including Brad Pitt and Joseph Zada, are among those reacting to the news.

“I’m so happy because he’s been the no. 1 vote in my household for like quite a long time,” Pugh told The Hollywood Reporter at Elle’s Women in Hollywood celebration. “He’s so unbelievably talented. He makes moves that you’re just not guessing, and it’s just so wonderful to see a career just float and be this unbelievably versatile thing. I’m so excited that he got that role. So good, and with Denis.”

Speaking with E! News, Howard praised her time working with Pattinson, saying, “He’s not schmoozy. He’s not trying to be cool. He’s just who he is, and he happens to be absolutely gorgeous. But he’s a true artist, and his career since Twilight has obviously demonstrated that a zillion times over.” So for her, “Him as James Bond feels really right”

Fiennes, who also appeared in three previous Bond films, learned about Pattinson’s casting during an interview with Andy Cohen on his SiriusXM show, alongside The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping castmembers. “I’m sorry. I’m in love with Daniel Craig still,” he said. “It’s hard. I’ve got the wrench in my heart.” However, Fiennes later clarified that he believes the Twilight alum is “a great actor,” adding, “I’m sure he’ll be great.”

Debicki told Variety she’s “so happy for Rob” at the Los Angeles premiere of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth. “He is an enigma himself. He’s beautiful, and he’s funny, and he’s smart… I think he’s perfect. I’m so thrilled,” she added of his casting.

Pitt appeared shocked when ExtraTV broke the Bond news to him at the The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth premiere. “Is that right?!” he replied. “You know what? I love this idea. I love what he’s been doing; I love his choices. I think he’s the real deal. Yeah, that’s great. Good call; I like it.”

Malek told People that he “could not be more proud” of Pattinson, having “known Rob for quite some time.” He added, “I mean, the fact that people are recognizing, maybe they all know, how talented that man is. I’ve known him for years. We’ve worked together and we’ve become quite close friends and it’s been lovely to just share this moment with him.”

Sunrise on the Reaping star Zada also told Cohen, “He’s on such a roll,” referring to his stacked lineup of projects this year — The Drama, The Odyssey, Primetime and Dune: Part Three — and now his Bond casting. “He’s every young actor’s favorite actor right now,” he added.
