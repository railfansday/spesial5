Dolly Parton Lands Three Posthumous Grammy Nominations

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

With the reveal of the 2027 Grammy nominations on Monday, Parton now has 58 total noms.

Dolly Parton has been posthumously nominated for three Grammy Awards.

The “Jolene” singer received two nods in the best country duo/group performance; the first stemming from her feature on Zac Brown’s “Butterfly,” with the second hailing from her song “Light of a Clear Blue Morning” with Lainey Wilson, Miley Cyrus, Queen Latifah and Reba McEntire.

With the reveal of the 2027 Grammy nominations on Monday, Parton now has 58 total noms. She has previously won 10 Grammys, and received the Recording Academy’s Lifetime Achievement Award in 2011. Additionally, her songs “Jolene” and “I Will Always Love You” have been inducted into the Grammy Hall of Fame.

Parton died Aug. 25, after a “brief battle with cancer,” a rep announced. She was 80. Her death led to an outpouring of tributes from the entertainment industry, from the likes of Miley Cyrus, Taylor Swift, Billy Ray Cyrus, Jane Fonda, Celine Dion and many more.

Harvey Mason Jr., CEO of the Grammys and MusiCares, commended Parton in a statement shared with The Hollywood Reporter at the time of her death.

“It’s nearly impossible to fully capture the depth of what Dolly Parton means to the world. She was a 10-time Grammy winner, a recipient of the Recording Academy’s Lifetime Achievement Award and the 2019 MusiCares Person of the Year honoree, but reducing her legacy to accolades doesn’t do justice to what she truly represented: love, kindness and generosity on a scale that transcended music,” Mason Jr. wrote.

Parton was honored with a tribute at the 2025 Emmys led by McEntire and Sally Field.
