Hayley Williams-Curated 2027 Outbreak Festival to Feature Blood Orange, Dinosaur Jr., Rico Nasty & More

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The festival will take place at B.E.C. Arena in Manchester, U.K. on June 19-20.

Next year’s Outbreak Fest in Manchester, U.K. features a Hayley Williams-curated lineup topped by Dinosaur Jr., Blood Orange, Wednesday, American Football, Julie Baker, Mannequin Pussy and more. The two-day event taking place on June 19 and 20 at B.E.C. Arena will also feature sets from Rico Nasty, Ear, Soul Glo, Bren Lukens, Lime Garden, Chalk, Rival Schools, Failure and Drug Church. Williams will also headline both nights with the Hayley Williams Show, with more artists to be announced at a later date, as well as details for a third show on June 18. Fans can register now for a presale that is open until midnight on Tuesday (Nov. 17) here. Any remaining tickets will be available in a general onsale on Thursday (Nov. 19) at 10 a.m. GMT.

Among the other acts slated for the 2027 Outbreak Fest are: H20, Holder, Empty Shell Casing, Feeble Little Horse, Mary in the Junkyard, Angel Du$t, Sweet Pill, Spiritual Cramp, Dev Lemons, JeJune, PISS, Snõõper, First Day Back, Reclus.É and Appleseed.

This year’s Outbreak Fest featured an equally eclectic lineup of hardcore and indie bands, including The Front Bottoms, Joyce Manor, Alexisonfire, Basement, Trapped Under Ice, Tigers Jaw, Pup, Ecca Vandal, Snail Mail, Hatebreed, Suicidal Tendencies and more.

Last month, Williams surprised the crowd at her Hollywood Bowl show in Los Angeles when she brought out Zedd for the first live performance in 12 years of their 2012 collaboration, “Stay the Night.” The Paramore singer and solo star is currently on the South American leg of her Hayley Williams Show outing, with a gig in Santiago, Chile on Wednesday (Nov. 18), followed by gigs in Peru, Colombia, Mexico and Puerto Rico before hitting Hong Kong in December for Clockenflap and then Mexico in January.
