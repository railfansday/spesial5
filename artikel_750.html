Trump and Minaj have become fast friends to the point where she flew on Air Force One to the aforementioned rally in the midwest after a staffer floated Nicki’s name as a potential candidate to fill the recently vacated press secretary role and she even addressed the crowd for a bit. “President Trump, it is an honor to stand with you every single time,” she said. “And I will continue to support you forever and ever.
