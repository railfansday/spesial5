The NFL Munich Game happens on Sunday (Nov. 15) with a matchup featuring the New England Patriots (5-4) taking on the Detroit Lions (6-2) at FC Bayern Munich Stadium in Munich, Germany, with a start time of 9:30 a.m. ET/6:30 a.m. PT (3:30 p.m. CEST).

The Patriots-Lions game broadcasts on FOX, while the NFL game is available to livestream on FOX One.

NFL games are known for celebrity sightings, so it’s likely there may be a number of famous actors, actresses and recording artists in attendance. Artists have been known to appear at games in Germany in the past, include NFL fans Alix Earle, mgk, FC Bayern Munich superstar Harry Kane, CRO and others.

Grammy Award-winning rock band Cage the Elephant performs at halftime, which starts around 11 a.m. ET/8 a.m. PT (5 p.m. CEST).
