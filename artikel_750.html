Wrong calls and bad results: Are refs making Man Utd's problems worse?

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Man Utd have been plagued by refereeing decisions in Mauricio Pochettino's first two matches in charge against Liverpool and Sporting; United also received an apology from Pro Ref earlier this season for VAR's incorrect call in their defeat to Man City; But Utd's problems

Mauricio Pochettino will need more than just good performances to turn Manchester United’s season around – refereeing decisions will have to go their way too.

Pochettino's first two games in charge have been plagued by controversial moments, following a similar pattern during the club's dreadful start, which has left them 19th, inside the Premier League relegation zone and out of the Carabao Cup.

United's luck appeared to be changing when Bruno Fernandes' shot was deflected in off a divot against Sporting on Wednesday, but the officials in Lisbon left Pochettino furious again as Amad Diallo's equaliser late in the first half was harshly ruled out for Youri Tielemans' foul in the build-up.

"It's difficult to answer as everyone saw it was impossible [to award a foul]," said Pochettino. "It's difficult to understand why that happened on the field."

Szymon Marciniak, referee in the 2022 World Cup final, had also already blown his whistle before Amad scored despite the forward being through on goal. Tielemans was booked for the tackle and the midfielder was later sent off after receiving a second yellow.

There was more controversy when United's third goal, and what they thought could be the winner, was ruled out following a VAR check. Sporting goalkeeper Kaique punched the ball into his own net at a corner, although it was disallowed for Patrick Dorgu's foul.

Pochettino had already been complaining about officials this week with Leny Yoro's one-match suspension overturned on Tuesday after the defender was shown a straight red card with the score at 0-0 in their 4-0 defeat to Liverpool.

Yoro was dismissed for denying an obvious goal-scoring opportunity, although it appeared striker Alexander Isak wouldn't have reached the ball had the defender not fouled him.

Refereeing chief Howard Webb still backed the officials' process on Match Officials Mic'd Up, although he was non-committal when sharing his own verdict on the incident.

Pochettino also felt Liverpool's opening goal, scored by Dominik Szoboszlai from the free-kick conceded by Yoro, should have been disallowed because Bradley Barcola was standing next to the Man Utd wall when the ball was struck.

Barcola appeared to be pushed towards the United defenders by Bruno Fernandes, revealing a grey area in the laws of the game, as they do not explain that type of incident.

The most significant incident of United's season came prior to Pochettino's arrival when United received an apology over the incorrect VAR decision to award Erling Haaland's winner in Manchester City's 1-0 win in September which was initially ruled offside before VAR overturned the decision.

The VAR officials deemed that Haaland was played onside by Man Utd defender Patrick Dorgu, but Man City's Enzo Fernandez was in an offside position as he attempted to play the ball before it reached Haaland, and Webb acknowledged the error.

In the same game, Man Utd benefited from Man City's Phil Foden being sent off for kicking out at Bruno Fernandes, a decision only narrowly backed 3-2 by the Key Match Incidents panel, while the United captain wrongly avoided a yellow card despite initiating the incident.

Yoro's red card against Liverpool wasn't the first time he has been harshly sent off for denying an obvious goal-scoring opportunity this season.

The defender was shown a straight red card when United trailed 1-0 at Atletico Madrid in the Champions League last month and then lost 2-0.

Yoro brought down Atletico's Jonathan David, who was heading away from goal, after the pair came together just inside United's half. "It is soft! No way, that should not be a red," said Sky Sports' Clinton Morrison.

United have had some good fortune. In their only Premier League win of the season, Harry Maguire escaped punishment for a handball to put his side ahead against Ipswich.

Had the defender's shot gone straight into the net, it would have been disallowed, but because Jacob Greaves diverted the attempt in, it was allowed to stand.

Fulham head coach Alvaro Arbeloa was fined £25,000 last month over his post-match comments following their draw with United. Arbeloa felt Maguire had committed a foul shortly before United's late equaliser and that the officials had favoured the away side.

"All fouls for them," he said. "You can see before they scored, the foul against Maguire, it's a guy who is 100 kilos. Every decision was for them and I knew after the game against Man City that they [would not] let Manchester United lose here."

And then, in a similar incident to United's third goal against Sporting, Dorgu's dramatic equaliser in United's 4-4 draw with Aston Villa earlier this month was allowed to stand despite Man Utd goalkeeper Senne Lammens appearing to impede Villa keeper Zion Suzuki, who flapped at the corner and missed the ball.
