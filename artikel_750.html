Tom Busby’s ‘Rockhampton Hangover’ Named 2026 Queensland Album of the Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Busby beat out a shortlist of albums from the likes of Clea, Radium Dolls, Platonic Sex, 2 Charm and The Jungle Giants.

Tom Busby collects The Triffid’s 2026 Queensland Album of the Year with his debut solo longplay, Rockhampton Hangover.

The Rockhampton-raised singer and songwriter, Busby, one-half of the chart-topping blues and roots duo Busby Marou, beat out a shortlist of albums from the likes of Clea, Radium Dolls, Platonic Sex, 2 Charm and The Jungle Giants.

Now in its 11th year, the public-voted award celebrates Queensland’s music community and was presented at Brisbane live music spot The Triffid, part of the Live Nation portfolio of venues. Produced by Ben Kweller and recorded in the Texas Hill Country, the album is a meditation on family, home and finding your way through change.

“I’m pretty bloody stoked,” Busby says of his award. “It took me a long time to get around to making a solo record, and when I finally did, I kept coming back to stories from home. To have The Triffid name Rockhampton Hangover Album of the Year means a lot.  Big thanks to Ben Kweller for making it with me, to everyone who’s given it a spin and to The Triffid crew for backing Queensland music.”

“Tom has given so much to Queensland music through Busby Marou, so it’s great to see him take this step and have people really connect with the record,” adds John Collins, co-founder of The Triffid and bass player with Powderfinger, which is reuniting in 2027 for what promises to be one of the year’s biggest tours down under.

“Making your first solo album is a big thing,” he continues. “Having the fans vote it their favorite makes it even sweeter.”

The award was created to bring attention to music from Australia’s Sunshine State, explains Collins, and for sparking conversations about the artists making it.

Following its release in February of this year, the independently-released Rockhampton Hangover strolled to No. 3 on the ARIA Albums Chart. As a member of Busby Marou, Busby has landed three albums in the national top 10, including a No. 1 with 2017’s Postcards From The Shell House, and won a hattrick of APRA Awards for blues & roots work of the year (2012, 2018, 2021).

With his latest win, Busby will receive promotional support from Stephen Green’s media enterprise The Music and community radio station 4ZZZ, an eight-hour production day at The Triffid and A$500 ($335) in merchandise credit from American Apparel.

2026 Queensland Album of the Year Shortlist:

The Jungle Giants – Experiencing Feelings Of Joy

Tom Busby – Rockhampton Hangover (WINNER)
