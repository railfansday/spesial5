Short on time? Give this 12-minute kettlebell workout a try

You don't need the gym or an hour spare to build your whole body, as this nifty kettlebell complex proves. If you're unfamiliar with a complex, it just means a sequence of exercises performed one after the other without rest. Once you feel familiar with the exercises, it should begin to feel like a moving meditation, albeit a sweat-laden one.

You just need one of the best kettlebells, and I also recommend a yoga mat. That's it. I also suggest making sure you have a little space around you. Check out the workout below, along with some benefits and step-by-step instructions.

And I'm giving fair warning now: this is a challenging beast of a routine.

The idea is to focus on bracing, activation and contraction rather than high reps or speed. Focus on the quality of your movement patterns using the cues above to help. Think of the complex as an exercise-stacking routine, moving fluidly from one move to the next.

For the workout, set a 12-minute timer, then complete 2 reps of each move. If an exercise is single-sided, that means 2 reps per side. Complete the moves unbroken, moving from one to the next without rest.

Once you complete the first round, reset, then add 2 reps to each move on your next round. Increase each round until you hit 12 minutes. It's going to get spicy pretty quickly, so focus on your breath and keep the movements under control.

This is about overall volume and time under tension, so I would opt for a lighter weight than you'd normally use for regular sets of the exercises.
