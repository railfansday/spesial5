Amazon’s Zoox robotaxis are now on Uber in Las Vegas, a first for both

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Zoox robotaxis start with a small fleet, and both companies become official partners of MGM Resorts. Los Angeles follows in 2027, while Waymo already runs 4,000 cars across the US.

From Monday, anyone hailing an Uber in Las Vegas may be picked up by a Zoox robotaxi, the companies said. Zoox, which Amazon owns, builds its vehicles without a steering wheel or a driver’s seat. It is the first time Zoox has put its robotaxis on another company’s platform. It is also the first purpose-built car with no steering wheel on Uber’s network, Andrew J. Hawkins reported for The Verge.

Riders who order UberX, Uber Comfort, Uber Electric, Uber Comfort Electric or Wait & Save may get a Zoox at no extra cost, according to the joint press release. They cannot choose one directly, Kirsten Korosec reported for TechCrunch. Turning on autonomous rides under “Ride Preferences” in the app’s settings raises the chance of a match.

Zoox robotaxis serve resorts and venues on and around the Strip, plus Downtown Las Vegas and the Arts District. Zoox will keep selling rides in its own app too. It is starting on Uber with a small fleet, the company wrote in a blog post. It may add cars as it improves its operations and service.

“As we continue to meaningfully expand our service in Las Vegas, we’re seeing more riders choosing to ride with Zoox,” said Jesse Levinson, Zoox’s co-founder and chief technology officer.

Uber and Zoox also signed a multiyear deal with MGM Resorts International. It names them the official rideshare and robotaxi partners of MGM’s Las Vegas properties. The companies will work on easier pickups and drop-offs and better directions on the properties. They also plan guidance in the app and road changes to help traffic flow.

Uber will not provide maintenance or other services for Zoox in Las Vegas, unlike with its other self-driving partners, the company told TechCrunch.

“Zoox has built something truly unique, and by bringing its purpose-built robotaxis to Uber, we’re introducing an entirely new riding experience to more people in Las Vegas, with Los Angeles coming next,” said Samarth Kejriwal, Uber’s global head of autonomous mobility.

Las Vegas is Zoox’s first commercial market. The company started charging for rides there in August.

Uber already offers robotaxi rides in the city with Motional, which Hyundai backs. The two relaunched there in March. Those cars still have a safety driver behind the wheel, Natalie Lung reported for Bloomberg. Earlier this year, Nevada’s Transportation Authority approved a permit for Uber to run up to 1,000 robotaxis in Clark County, TechCrunch reported. The permit covers Motional and Zoox.

“Las Vegas will become the first U.S. city where riders can access two fully driverless partners through the Uber app,” Uber CEO Dara Khosrowshahi said on the company’s third-quarter earnings call this month.

He expects the Motional cars to go fully driverless “soon” after the Zoox launch, Bloomberg reported. Uber also plans a dedicated autonomous option in its app as more robotaxis become available.

Waymo, which Uber sees as both a partner and a competitor, opened driverless rides in Las Vegas in September. Its exclusive deal with Uber in Austin and Atlanta ends in January 2028.

Waymo has 4,000 vehicles across the US today, according to Bloomberg. Uber hopes to have more than 2,000 driverless taxis available to customers in 2027. It has said it will commit more than $10bn to robotaxi partners rather than build its own vehicle.

Los Angeles comes next. Zoox will start initial operations there by the end of 2026. Uber and Zoox plan to launch together in the city in 2027, after Zoox opens rides in its own app. Those first Los Angeles rides are for Zoox employees, family and friends, TechCrunch reported.

Zoox also began driving its robotaxi in Atlanta on Friday, its newest testing market. Rides there are only for employees, their families and friends for now, Zoox said in a separate post.
