Next year’s iPhone Pro redesign gets new update via leaker

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Next year is the iPhone’s 20th anniversary, and Apple reportedly has a big redesign coming to its Pro models. Here’s what to expect per the latest leak.

Rumors about next year’s iPhone Pro models are starting to ramp up. Earlier today, we got word that the ‘iPhone 20 Pro’ may get a new sensor for its Main camera. Now another leak involves the special 20th anniversary design.

Weibo leaker Fixed Focus Digital posted that the iPhone Pro redesign could take a lot of inspiration from the iPhone Air, but with a thicker size and quad-curved glass display

Per the translated post, he says iPhone 20 Pro is set to be “made of the same glass material as the iPhone Air series, but has been adjusted to have a higher curvature, giving it a four-curved surface effect.”

He included a rendering in the post, likely created by AI, to illustrate what the design could look like. It shows a glass-centric design that resembles iPhone Air in several ways. But the glass stretches over the edges of the device, which from a top-down view should create a bezel-free look.

If the design rumors for the new iPhone Pro models prove true, it sounds like Apple is going for a look that evokes not only the iPhone Air but also iPhone X.

iPhone X’s design was a radical leap forward when it debuted in 2017, especially due to innovations like Face ID and the removal of the Home Button.

iPhone 20 Pro isn’t expected to be nearly as big a departure from current iPhone designs. But if Apple can deliver an iPhone Air-style look without sacrificing Pro battery life or cameras, I’ll be all for it.
