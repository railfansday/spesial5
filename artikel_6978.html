Amelia Gray Brings Her Effortless Edge to H&amp;M’s Latest Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The model shares her fresh take on holiday dressing and the pieces she’s making her own.

“Chic, playful, holiday”: This is how Amelia Gray sums up H&M Studio Holiday 2026, a collection that makes a case for getting dressed with a little more imagination. The model, who is currently one of the industry’s most recognizable faces, knows a thing or two about making an outfit memorable. These days, though, her approach is taking a more refined turn. “My personal style right now is definitely elevating, becoming more mature, chicer, and more refined,” she says. “I think H&M’s collection reflects that perfectly.”

Like she says, that evolution feels right at home with H&M Studio. The brand’s most directional offering, Studio collections bring a more experimental perspective to the company’s portfolio, with limited-edition designs developed by the Stockholm atelier’s in-house team. For holiday 2026, the inspiration comes from the theatrical world of early-1900s Paris, where grand opera houses and salons set the scene for expressive dressing. Sweeping proportions, tactile fabrics, and unexpected details give the collection a sense of movement, while sharp tailoring keeps things grounded. The result is a wardrobe that feels equally suited to a festive gathering and an ordinary day that calls for something special.

Gray has already picked her favorite combination. “I loved the bomber jacket with the dress,” she says. “That outfit is great for Thanksgiving or Christmas dinner.” The bomber jacket, finished with a fluffy collar, brings a relaxed edge to the dual-fabric dress, which has a full balloon skirt that gives the look plenty of movement. Fluffy-lined ballet flats complete the outfit and add a sense of playfulness. Together, the pieces strike a balance between dressed-up and laid-back.

The bomber also has plenty of mileage beyond the dinner table. Gray calls it her “new favorite,” and for good reason: “The bomber jacket is perfect to dress up and down. With jeans. With a skirt. With shorts.” Throw it over denim for a casual weekend lunch, or pair it with a sleek pencil for a night out. The trick is to let the contrast do the work. A sporty jacket can change the mood of a dress, just as a more polished piece can bring an unexpected finish to something casual.

Some moments call for a dress that handles the styling decisions for you. The satin open-shoulder dress does exactly that, bringing a fluid and elegant neckline to the occasion. Its silhouette has enough presence to stand on its own, while dangle hoop earrings add movement and a touch of shine. It’s an easy combination for a holiday party, a dinner reservation, or any invitation that warrants getting dressed up without spending an hour figuring out what to wear.

The key is to keep the rest of the look uncomplicated. The dress could also take on a different personality with a more casual shoe or a relaxed jacket layered over it. That’s part of the appeal of a versatile occasion piece: It doesn’t have to be saved for one particular night on the calendar.

A peplum top and an A-line skirt make an interesting study in proportion. The puff sleeves and defined waist of the top bring shape up above, while the viscose skirt creates a fluid counterpoint below. It’s a combination that feels polished without being overly formal—a smart option for a lunch that turns into an afternoon of shopping or a dinner with friends. For a slightly more understated take, swap in the scoop-neck viscose top. The silhouette becomes simpler, but the skirt still gives the outfit its sense of movement.

The handbag adds another layer of personality. Its soft shape, velvet rope handle, and oversize tassel bring texture and a hint of old-world theatricality to the mix, tying back to the collection’s Parisian inspiration. It’s also the kind of accessory that can change the feel of classic pieces, whether carried with a coordinated outfit or used to add interest to a simple top-and-jeans combination.

A crisp cotton poplin shirt is one of those wardrobe staples that rarely needs an introduction. Here, its clean lines meet the texture and embellishment of an embroidered crepe skirt, creating a combination that feels easy to wear. Over-the-knee leather boots round out the outfit, giving it a little more attitude. It’s a strong look for a holiday gathering, a night at the theater, or a dinner when your denim go-to simply won’t do. For another interpretation, the skirt can be swapped for barrel-leg trousers, while a raw-edged puff-sleeve blouse offers an alternative to the cotton poplin.

The appeal lies in the mix of familiar and fashion-forward pieces. A poplin shirt is hardly an adventurous purchase, but paired with a more expressive skirt and statement boots, it takes on a different character. This balance also makes the individual pieces easier to wear beyond the occasion. The shirt works with denim, the boots can ground a shorter hemline, and the skirt can be dressed down with a simple knit. It’s a reminder that a statement piece doesn’t need an entirely new wardrobe built around it.

For Gray, the best outfits leave room for a little experimentation. “I love an unexpected pairing,” she says. “That’s why I love the dress and the bomber!” It’s a simple philosophy, one that gets to the heart of personal style. It’s about recognizing what you like, finding interesting ways to put things together, and having the confidence to wear them your way. H&M Studio Holiday 2026 offers plenty of starting points, from dramatic silhouettes to tactile accessories and everyday staples with a twist. What happens next is up to you.
