Rubio says 'zero' information that US tech outages are linked

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Secretary of State Marco Rubio on Tuesday sought to assure Americans that there’s "zero" information that a cascade of service outages are related to each other or to a malign actor. Outages that shut down email for many companies and grounded thousands of flights over the past few days extended to the US National Security Agency on Tuesday, disrupting classified communications.
