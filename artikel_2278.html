USA international has already shown why Stoke City struck gold with £4m summer signing: Opinion

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Stoke City picked up another huge win in the week, beating Blackburn Rovers 2-1 at Ewood Park. Summer signing Jack McGlynn caught the headlines after his cheeky free-kick goal, catching Balasz Toth out at his near post.

McGlynn is quickly proving to be one of Stoke City’s shrewdest summer signings, with the American midfielder making an immediate impression under Mark Robins. That free-kick was the latest of several excellent contributions as a he continues to make his mark in England.

It was the 23-year-old’s fourth goal of the campaign and another demonstration of the technical quality that persuaded Stoke to bring him over from Houston Dynamo in September. Even with Rovers boss Tony Mowbray highlighting the player’s ability, they still could not stop him.

McGlynn arrived for a reported fee of around £4m, signing a three-year contract after previously developing with Philadelphia Union before his move to Houston. He was hardly a household name among Championship supporters, but his early performances suggest Stoke identified a player with considerable potential.

The statistics certainly make encouraging reading. McGlynn has registered four goals and two assists in 10 Championship appearances, starting eight matches and accumulating 706 minutes.

His average FotMob average rating of 7.35 underlines his influence, particularly considering his return of four goals has come from an expected goals figure of 0.70. His performance against Preston North End was another example of his attacking contribution.

McGlynn delivered the cross for Ben Wilmot’s opener before scoring himself with a composed left-footed finish in the 2-0 victory. That followed a particularly impressive display in which he demonstrated his ability to influence matches through both delivery and finishing.

There is more to his game than spectacular set-pieces, too. His left foot gives Stoke an additional dimension in advanced midfield and wide areas, allowing him to create opportunities, threaten from distance and provide dangerous deliveries.

His understanding of the Championship’s physical demands will inevitably continue developing, while there is scope for him to become more influential during open-play possession. Nevertheless, Robins already appears to have found an exciting attacking weapon.

McGlynn has the technical ability, confidence and versatility to become a significant player for Stoke, and his early numbers suggest he could even reach double figures for goals and assists this season.

For a reported £4m, the Potters look to have secured an excellent piece of business. McGlynn is already delivering, and there is every reason to believe his best performances are still ahead of him.
