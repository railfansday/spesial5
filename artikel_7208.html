Election Advertising Drove 10% of Viewers to Seek Ad-Free Options

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Streaming networks may have found a new way to sell people on ad-free subscriptions: elections.

In a survey of nearly 1,800 U.S. adults by research firm Savanta, nearly 10% chose to avoid ads during the midterm election cycle by upgrading to ad-free options, canceling subscriptions entirely, or tuning out networks.

“People will always try to find ways to avoid advertising, and I think behaviors will change depending on the environment, how much advertising it is, and whether it’s persistent,” said Shaun Austin, global head of media at Savanta.
