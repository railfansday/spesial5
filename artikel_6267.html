Allegiant Air Cancels Massive 25 Routes: See Full List Now

One of America's largest ultra-low-cost carriers has cut a massive 25 routes from its winter schedule. This comes as Allegiant Air reevaluates seasonal routes and ponders the return of those flights next year.

Three that last served in November last year will not operate this month, while January through August 2026 continue to see route cuts as the airline adjusts its schedule. Cirium data analyzed by Simple Flying shows many routes have been cut, and some airports are no longer served. A number of these services will now only operate across the summer.

Las Vegas-based Allegiant Air is a US budget airline that offers no-frills, nonstop flying to destinations across the US. The carrier has no international service and solely relies on the domestic US market. With a fleet of more than 120 aircraft, these are mixed between the Airbus A319, A320 and Boeing 737 MAX 7 and MAX 8-200.

Reviewing historical flight schedules for 2025 and 2026, and comparing them to this winter's schedule, the airline has shaved 25 routes in total, with November seeing three routes absent from its network, including Myrtle Beach to Niagara Falls, Phoenix to Asheville, and Sarasota to Portsmouth.

The cuts don't stop there; Bellingham to Los Angeles, Nashville to Peoria, Cincinnati to Los Angeles, and Las Vegas to Boise have disappeared for this coming January. This marks the airline's full withdrawal from Los Angeles International Airport(LAX).

Emphasizing that a number of these routes will continue to operate seasonally in the busier summer months, the airline's overall decline in winter routes saw it trim two additional services in April and eight in May. Including the airlines ultimate withdrawal from San Francisco's Oakland San Francisco Bay Airport (OAK) airport.

For April 2026, terminated routes include Fort Lauderdale to Traverse City, and Orlando/Sanford to Columbia.

Comparing May 2026, routes no longer scheduled include Bellingham to Oakland, Bellingham to Palm Springs, Fort Lauderdale to Columbia, Fort Lauderdale to Huntington, Fort Lauderdale to Savannah, Las Vegas to Chattanooga, Punta Gorda to New Orleans, and Phoenix to Pittsburgh.

In these numbers, Fort Lauderdale has lost three nonstop services since earlier this year, while Bellingham, a longtime Allegiant base, was closed and lost two West Coast routes.

Seasonal demand and leisure-first strategy prompt aircraft redeployment, reshaping connections and leaving gaps in service.

The airline hasn't stopped there; although these routes are in the Northern Hemisphere summer season, they are currently not scheduled to operate in 2027. August route cuts include Asheville to Chicago Midway, Bellingham to Burbank, Nashville to Akron/Canton, Washington Dulles to Savannah, Jacksonville to Des Moines, Las Vegas to Lexington, Portland to Idaho Falls, and Sarasota to Nashville.

Across this analysis, the airline has clearly cut several routes that historically operated from major hub airports, which usually carry high landing and operating costs, and instead pivoted to regional alternatives. This helps the airline turn a profit on low fares that require low operational costs and high aircraft utilization.
