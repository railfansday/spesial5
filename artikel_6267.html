Save On Etihad Flights With Amex Offers (Targeted)

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

The Amex Offers program has just rolled out a great new targeted offer for the purchase of Etihad Airways flights, which could be worth considering for anyone planning travel (that’s not to say you should or shouldn’t plan travel on Etihad, for a variety of reasons — I’m just sharing a deal!).

To my knowledge, this is the first time that we’ve seen an Amex Offers deal for Etihad purchases, so it’s nice to see another airline join the Amex Offers “circuit.”

With a new targeted Amex Offers deal, you can earn a $200 statement credit when you spend $1,000+ or more with Etihad Airways by December 31, 2026. (Enrollment required)

It’s possible that there’s also a version of the offer for 20,000 bonus points rather than a $200 statement credit, and if so, I’d consider that to be more valuable. That’s because based on my latest miles & points valuations, Amex points are worth 1.7 cents each, so I’d value the 20,000 points at $340.

Let me emphasize that while the terms suggest that you need to book an Etihad flight through the US website that originates in the US, I’ve had similar Amex Offers triggered in the past for flights that didn’t originate in the US, so it’s anyone’s guess how that’s actually enforced here.

This Amex Offers deal is targeted, though it’s possible you’ll see it on multiple of your cards. If so, I’d highly recommend registering your American Express Platinum Card® for this offer. That’s because the Amex Platinum offers 5x points on airfare purchased directly with airlines.

Based on my valuation of 1.7 cents per Membership Rewards point, the 5x points offers an incremental 8.5% return on spending. On top of that, the Amex Platinum offers valuable travel protection, should you experience irregular operations.

In other words, if you took advantage of this offer with the Amex Platinum for a $1,000 ticket, you’d receive a $200 statement credit plus 5,000 points. By my valuation, that’s a return of $285 on $1,000 of spending, which comes out to a 28.5% return.

There’s a new targeted Amex Offers deal for a $200 statement credit when you spend $1,000+ on an Etihad ticket. If you’re planning travel on Etihad, this has the potential to be a solid deal. Ideally you’ll see this offer on the Amex Platinum, so that you can really maximize your points.
