Canopy Growth (CGC) Q2 2027 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Margins slipped as Canadian medical reimbursement fell, even as revenue climbed.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Canopy Growth Corporation (CGC -3.58%) reported significant top-line expansion driven by a triple-digit increase in international medical cannabis revenue and the integration of the MTL Cannabis acquisition. Management stated that the company is moving toward adjusted EBITDA positivity in the second half of fiscal 2027 by focusing on cultivation excellence and cost discipline. The company reported a strengthened European supply chain following new certifications, while also addressing margin headwinds in the Canadian medical market. Management indicated that the focus remains on high-quality flower production to satisfy demand in core markets across Canada, Germany, and Poland.
