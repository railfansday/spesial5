Chris Harrison’s ‘The Vow’ Star Apologizes For Grabbing Contestant’s Butt: “I Made A Mistake”

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

As Chris Harrison leaves the Bachelor Mansion behind for Fox Nation‘s The Vow, the dating show’s first eligible Christian is facing some judgment.

Jace Cates, the star of the new faith-based dating show, apologized for obliging contestant Sierra Wood’s request to “grab my ass” on the third episode “Enter Into Temptation.”

“I think it’s no secret that Sierra and I had an initial connection right from the beginning. And what doesn’t really get talked about is, that’s a long day of shooting, and when that moment happened with Sierra, it was at the end of the day. I was trying to be very intentional with every single girl that day,” he told the California Post. “And me and Sierra were having our moment, and it was a very romantic setting. I was in the moment with her when she made that request and I acted on it.” Related Stories News "You Can't Put A Cease & Desist On Love": Chris Harrison Talks About Returning To The Dating Scene With 'The Vow' News Chris Harrison Dating Show 'The Vow' Unveils Trailer

Cates added, “So, that’s where my mindset was. It’s just really difficult because you have your faith at the forefront and you have your intention at the forefront, and then you’re also trying to build a relationship with people you see a future with. I think I made a mistake in that moment. I was in there, I’m not perfect. And what I’m happy with is that I apologized to the women, I took accountability. And I tried to make sure they know I want to be respectful.” Watch on Deadline

Although they’d never met before, Cates and Wood are both members of Rose Church in Portland, Oregon, where he was asked if their pastors have seen the on-air moment. “They’re going to find out very soon if they haven’t seen the episode yet,” he said.

Harrison’s new dating show comes after he parted ways with the Bachelor franchise in June 2021, following his support for a contestant amid controversy over a racist photo she previously posted.

Warner Bros. previously hit The Vow with a cease-and-desist over alleged copyright and trademark infringement. “You can’t put a cease and desist on love,” Harrison told Deadline in response.

“I’ve been away for five years. There have been dozens of other dating shows. They’ve come after one very publicly. Is it a coincidence? I don’t think so,” he said. “On the flip side, there are many friends of ours on that show still, people I care about, and this business is hard. I don’t wish anybody to lose their jobs. There’s enough room for everybody to be successful.”
