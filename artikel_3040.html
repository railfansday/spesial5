Corvex, Inc. (MOVE -17.37%) reported a sequential revenue increase of 31% as it transitioned into a pure-play AI cloud computing provider following its merger. The strategic focus remains on securing high-credit counterparties for multiyear, take-or-pay contracts while expanding technical capabilities in confidential computing.
