Nvidia Bull: AI Growth Story Remains Intact

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

I/O Fund CEO and lead tech analyst Beth Kindig says her long-term Nvidia bull case remains intact despite Jensen Huang’s departure, arguing the company could still reach a $20 trillion market value by 2030. She discusses why surging AI compute demand, Nvidia’s potential expansion into recurring software revenue and its lowest valuation multiple in years could support the stock - while AMD emerges as a more formidable competitor in rack-scale AI systems. She joins Ed Ludlow on "Bloomberg Tech." (Source: Bloomberg)
