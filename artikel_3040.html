Elbit (ESLT) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Margin expansion and record contracts drive a high-teens 2027 growth outlook.

Need a quote from a Motley Fool analyst? Email [email protected]

Elbit (ESLT +5.84%) reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $2.252 billion and a record backlog of $33 billion. Management reported that non-GAAP operating margins expanded to 10.8%, exceeding internal targets through operating leverage and the transition of research projects into programs of record. The company announced a compensation agreement with the Israeli Ministry of Defense regarding delayed payments and provided 2027 guidance for revenue growth in the high-teens. Strategic initiatives include scaling production capacity in Southern Israel via automation and expanding maritime capabilities through the acquisition of International Submarine Engineering.
