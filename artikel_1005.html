Kim leads LPGA Tour finale after amazing ace as Hull and Korda chase

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Charley Hull is four off the halfway lead as she chases a second CME Group Tour Championship title; A Lim Kim three clear after making an ace at the par-three 16th during her second round; live coverage continues on Saturday from 4pm on Sky Sports Golf

A Lim Kim fired a hole-in-one on her way to grabbing the halfway lead at the LPGA Tour's season-ending CME Group Tour Championship, where Charley Hull remains in contention.

Kim holed her tee shot with a seven iron at the 168-yard par-three 16th, with the Korean adding seven birdies and a lone bogey in a round-of-the-day 64 at Tiburon Golf Club.

The former US Women's Open champion was tied at the top of the leaderboard until her stunning ace and a birdie at the par-five next, which moved Kim to 15 under and three clear of back-to-back defending champion Jeeno Thitikul.

Thitikul posted five birdies in a seven-hole stretch on her back nine during a second-round 67, putting her in the final group on Saturday, with Hull a further shot back in a share of third after matching the world No 2's score.

Yuri Yoshida, Ina Yoon, Yan Liu, Jenny Bae and Auston Kim complete the logjam on 11 under alongside Hull, who posted five birdies in a blemish-free round as the 2016 champion looks to win the CME Group Tour Championship for a second time.

"I've just been pretty solid the last couple days, and hopefully I can replicate that on the weekend," Hull admitted. "I didn't actually hole that many putts, but I hit it quite close."

Only the top 60 players in the season-long Race to the CME Globe get to tee it up in Florida, with world No 1 Nelly Korda six off the halfway lead as she chase the $4m (£3.04m) winner's cheque.

"I'm just not hitting it as solid as I would like," Korda explained. "I have a little bit of a two-way miss going and that's kind of death for any golfer - you're not very confident over the shot.

"I'm just trying to get myself one key that I'm just going to go with for the whole week. I have yet to figure it out - hopefully the weekend brings me some luck!"

England's Lottie Woad dropped to tied-22nd after a two-under 70, while Ireland's Leona Maguire heads into the weekend sharing 42nd after rounds of 70 and 71 over the first two days.
