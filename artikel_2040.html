Longo: Serie A clubs lining up for Comotto and Camarda as international interest arrives

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

In the January mercato, AC Milan are expected to allow Christian Comotto and Francesco Camarda to depart on loan, but where will they go?

There has been talk of departures for Camarda and Comotto for some time now, especially with them struggling to get minutes. However, allowing them to leave is arguably more important than them staying.

After all, Camarda has already left the nest and it wasn’t exactly successful. So, their new clubs must be right.

Daniele Longo reports that Genoa are trying to set up a deal to sign Comotto in January, but they are desperate to include a purchase option in the agreement. Imaginably, that won’t be included at all.

It’s not as if this is the only option either, with Parma, Monzo and a heap of Serie B clubs also keeping a close eye on the youngster, who has admitted that he wants more playing time.

For Camarda, things are also heating up, especially after his performance against France this weekend. Genoa are also interested in the striker, but with Osmajic and Colombo there, it doesn’t make sense.

Pisa, Catanzaro and Empoli have also come forward for the 18-year-old, as well as some clubs from abroad. So, their eventual destinations are unknown at this moment. What is a certainty, however, is that they’ll both be heading on loan in January.
