jean couvreur transforms iconic train emergency hammers into bookends, wall hooks, and candlesticks

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

to celebrate the 45th anniversary of the TGV, designer jean couvreur composes new objects using iconic parts salvaged from the train.

jeancouvreur edited by christina vergopoulou November 27, 2026 2 min

To celebrate the 45th anniversary of the TGV, SNCF invited designer Jean Couvreur to create new objects from salvaged train parts. He immediately turned his attention to the iconic emergency glass-breaking hammer, originally designed by Roger Tallon in 1991. By leveraging its formal and functional features, Jean Couvreur conceived three distinct creations: bookends that utilize the tool’s weight, a wall hook incorporating its rounded edges, and a candlestick crafted from its handle.

original window-breaking hammer designed in 1991 by Roger Tallon | all images ©Jean Couvreur

The designer also worked with reclaimed train windows, whose properties naturally echo those of a coffee table. Both collections were designed with the utmost simplicity so that, beyond their upcycled origins, each piece feels as though it has always existed. The entire collection was auctioned off to benefit the charity Vacances Ouvertes.
