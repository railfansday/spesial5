Sutton's predictions v Kings of Leon drummer Nathan Followill

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Arsenal co-chairman Josh Kroenke should probably turn his phone off if the Gunners fall behind against Manchester City on Sunday.

Chris Sutton's predictions guest this week, Kings of Leon drummer and City fan Nathan Followill, is good friends with Kroenke - and says he will be in touch if the game is going City's way.

"I'm super excited for this weekend and I've got my smack talk ready for Josh already," Followill told BBC Sport.

"I've heard about the City fans with their inflatable bananas and I need to get myself one. My wife is going to be like 'er, an inflatable banana just arrived in the mail - do you know anything about that?'

"We are going to win 4-2 so I will be texting Josh throughout the game... and, after Erling Haaland's second goal, I will send him a picture of me kissing the banana!"

Followill got a behind the scenes tour of Etihad Stadium and City's training complex when Kings of Leon played next door at Co-op Live arena in 2024. "It's a beautiful stadium, and an amazing set-up," he said. "Their practice pitches are better than most college pitches in the US."

Followill has supported City for more than 20 years, external and his children have followed in his footsteps by becoming Blues, despite the best efforts of his next-door neighbour in Nashville, as well as Kroenke.

"When my kids were born, they both received a full Arsenal kit - head to toe - in the mail from Josh," he explained.

"Both times, they were returned to sender - instantly.

"My son and daughter are both City fans now, but my neighbour, Jeremy Connor, is Tottenham, and I mean flying the flag full on.

"When they lose, you can hear him screaming - and when they win, you can hear him screaming too.

"His son is trying to convert my son to Spurs and I am like 'that is never going to happen in this house!'"

Chris Sutton and Nathan Followill were speaking to BBC Sport's Chris Bevan.

The AI predictions were generated using Microsoft Copilot Chat - we simply asked the tool to 'predict this weekend's Premier League scores'.

Followill says it was eye-opening to watch City's boys and girls' teams play when he visited their training campus. "I had no idea how much they put into developing kids from the youngest ages all the way up," he said. "That was probably the most impressive thing for me - to see how many young kids were out there, with the ultimate dream of pulling on that blue shirt one day."

Chelsea have scored and conceded 20 goals so far this season, which kind of sums up where they are at.

Xabi Alonso's side have also lost as many league games - five - as Manchester United, and United are in crisis.

Alonso is not under pressure, or anything like that, but if they lose this game too, you can imagine the mood of the club - and it won't be good.

Apparently their fans were singing 'you don't know what you're doing' at him after their defeat by Leeds - which is what they sang at me when I was there.

Alonso needs to decide on a shape and a way of playing, and stick with it.

Anything could happen here, because Nottingham Forest's form is hardly convincing either.

They tore Manchester City apart in their previous home game, before the international break, but can they really dig out another result like that here?

I think they will go about it in a similar way, and be aggressive and have a real go at Chelsea... and I don't see Chelsea's defence keeping them out.

Nathan's prediction: No-one thought Forest would beat us, and while Chelsea need a big result, I can see Forest getting a point here. 1-1

We talked about Leeds on the Monday Night Club and how they have upgraded their team with the players Daniel Farke bought over the summer.

Farke strengthened in the areas he needed to improve, but is still trusting some of the players who did well for him last season - the likes of Jayden Bogle and James Justin, who have been outstanding.

They are a really well-balanced team and they have good depth to their squad too. The only criticism would be that they are not usually free-scoring - they hit four against Chelsea but before then they had only managed 10 goals in their first 10 league games.

Leeds do not give much away at the back either, though, and I think that will be key in this game.

When I watch Coventry they are dynamic with their counter-attacks and I always fancy them to nick a goal like that.

Not this time, however. Leeds might take a while to break them down, but I think they will come out on top.

Nathan's prediction: Coventry play in sky blue too? Part of the reason I became a Manchester City fan was the kit, and I have always gravitated towards teams in light blue, so I am going to go with Coventry here. 0-1

I am at this game for 5 Live and I am not expecting much to change for Manchester United.

No-one thought it was going to be easy for Mauricio Pochettino to go in at United and turn things around. He has taken over a complete rabble.

United's draw in Lisbon on Wednesday was chaotic, but you also have to consider how Sporting have not got going in the Portuguese league or the Champions League this season.

That was not a great Sporting side. They are pretty average and Brentford will be a much bigger test.

The Bees are supremely well-organised and will be full of confidence, too, after putting four goals past Everton on Monday. They were against 10 men for the entire second half, but were 2-0 up by then anyway.

Pochettino needs something to cling to, but for him picking that backline must be like pinning the tail on the donkey. Whoever he picks, they are not very good.

I really want to be more positive about United, because I heard from quite a few of their fans last weekend, but this prediction is about as positive as I can be about them at the moment.

Nathan's prediction: It is tough for me to talk about United because in America we have a saying 'don't kick a man when he's down' and it's pretty rough for them right now... but I'm not going to lie, it is nice to see United this low in the table.

I don't think it is the manager that is the problem for United right now, so I don't think Pochettino coming in is going to be an instant fix. They've got some growing pains to get through, and I won't be disappointed to watch that.

When I watch the Premier League at home, Brentford always cracks me up because I am huge fan of The Office and I just imagine a bunch of David Brents out there, just running around the pitch, which is a very funny thing for me.

I know I just said don't kick a man when he's down, but I don't think United would be nice to us if we were down there... so, I think the David Brents are going to win. 1-2

I actually think Villa will win this game 2-1 but I want to make it clear the scoreline I have gone for is from my commentary colleague, Alistair Bruce-Ball, who is an Ipswich fan - a fake one, but still a fan.

I asked him about this game when we were in Belgium for Liverpool's Champions League game on Wednesday. He will be at Portman Road on Saturday for 5 Live and he is backing the Tractor Boys to get something, so I am doing the same out of respect for him.

Ipswich really need a result too. They have gone five league games without a win and, with Hull and Coventry both winning last week, they are the lowest placed of the promoted sides.

That is never a good thing, and it feels like Gary O'Neil's side are in a precarious position at the moment, where they are getting sucked towards the bottom three.

I've been surprised to see Villa hit a bit of a sticky patch too, with three successive league draws and no wins in six in all competitions, so it will be interesting to see if they are any better here.

Nathan's prediction: Villa have had some surprising results recently but I do think they will win this. 1-3

I was in Bruges to watch Liverpool in midweek and it was interesting to see two of their players who came in for some heavy flak last season, Alexander Isak and Florian Wirtz, both shine.

Isak is looking like the player who Liverpool thought they signed in 2025. He is finding his rhythm, and Wirtz also seems to be in a really good place confidence-wise. When I watch them now, it feels like something is going to happen.

There are other positives for Reds boss Andoni Iraola too. I know there will be a debate about Bradley Barcola's end-product but I love watching him in full flow when he is running past defenders - there is no better sight in football than moments like that.

Rio Ngumoha is exciting as well, and he has such a good football brain which makes him even more dangerous.

Everything points to a Liverpool win, but I am hesitant to just go with that because Everton are coming off the back of a drubbing by Brentford. You can imagine David Moyes giving his side a miserable old week and getting stuck into his players.

I didn't think James Tarkowski should have been sent off in that game, but he will be suspended here. It is his absence that swings it for me because he is a real leader for Everton.

Nathan's prediction: Everton are usually the underdogs in this game but both teams are pretty level at the moment. One of our record producers is a huge Liverpool fan so he won't be happy with me... but I am going to say Everton will take it. 2-1

I was at Newcastle's draw with Arsenal last week and, in fairness to Magpies boss Matthias Jaissle, he changed things around at half-time and got his reward.

Jaissle needed that half of football, because I don't think the Newcastle fans have been too convinced by his approach, but this is another very tough game for him.

Brighton probably still can't believe they lost to Hull last week - but lightning is not going to strike twice.

Nathan's prediction: I have to give a shout out to my statistician Lucy here, because there were a couple of teams I didn't know a lot about and she gave me some good insight. So, Brighton have scored a lot of goals this season and I think they will get more here. 3-1

Thomas Frank has got a huge task at Crystal Palace. They are conceding so many goals.

Losing Maxence Lacroix and Marc Guehi from their defence was like Robbie Williams and Jason Orange leaving Take That, because they were never going to be the same without them.

I still think Frank has got some decent attacking players, however.

The Hull keeper, Konstantinos Tzolakis, looks like a great signing. I don't know how he made some of his saves against Brighton and he probably doesn't know himself either.

Brighton should have buried them and I think Palace will get plenty of chances too.

Fair play to Hull for their spirit and the way they scrap for each other, but I still think they are going to go down.

Hull fans are probably wondering if I am ever going to predict they will win a game... the answer is no, at least not this week anyway.

Nathan's prediction: Palace have given up 27 goals in 11 games which is a lot. Hull have surprised a few people this season and I am backing them here. 0-2

Bournemouth are on a long winning run in the league but I've been impressed by Fulham whenever I've watched them.

Alvaro Arbeloa's side have not been great at home, with only one win at Craven Cottage so far, so I am going with them to get another draw - which would be their fourth in a row.

Nathan's prediction: Both these teams have picked up a lot, so this is a tough one to call. 2-2

Tottenham boss Roberto de Zerbi must think a lot of 18-year-old midfielder Luca Williams-Barnett after picking him for their win over Ipswich.

I love to see managers trust young players like that, especially because Spurs have had such a tricky season so far.

It will be interesting to see whether De Zerbi picks him again here, but I feel like the outcome of this game is more dependent on which Sunderland we see.

They picked up a good point at Villa last time out, but they never seem to be quite the same after they have played in Europe, like they are doing this week.

The Black Cats will be without injured duo Brian Brobbey and Granit Xhaka for this game too, so I don't think they will have enough to win it.

Nathan's prediction: Dave Stewart from Eurythmics lives in Nashville too and is a good buddy of mine - and he is Sunderland until death! He is such a die-hard fan that every time we have dinner, we will sit there and talk about 'Haway the Lads' and just shoot the breeze about Sunderland.

So I am going to back them in this one, because of Dave and also because of my neighbour Jeremy, who I mentioned earlier.

Jeremy was especially cocky when they had Harry Kane and he would just talk so much smack - I always knew if they were doing good because I'd get a text from him. So, because Jeremy is such a cocky little Spurs fan, I am saying Sunderland will win. 2-1

I always enjoy this game because of Gabriel's battle with Erling Haaland, but it is in midfield where I think it will be decided.

Manchester City have spent a lot of money on players in that part of the pitch but Enzo Maresca has not found the right combination yet.

Somehow Maresca has got to find a way of nullifying Declan Rice, but that will be very difficult because Rice is just an absolute machine.

You can never rule City out because of Haaland's ability to score goals but the only result I see here is an Arsenal win, and I am going for a classic Gunners scoreline.

Nathan's prediction: This is the big one! It's huge. I feel really good about it, too. Arsenal are riding so high at the moment, I feel like we might catch them snoozing.

I predict Haaland will get two goals, Phil Foden will get one too and we will end up with four. 2-4

After drawing a blank since week two, Chris finally picked up another win in week 11 - and it was his spot-on prediction for Liverpool to thump Manchester United 4-0 that sealed the deal.

That meant he got three correct results, including two exact scores - at Anfield, and Tottenham's 2-0 win over Ipswich - giving him a total of 90 points.

In a tricky week for predictions, where Liverpool were the only team to start the weekend in the Premier League's top six who won, it was those exact scores that made all the difference.

Chris' guest, undisputed world champion boxer Lauren Price, the BBC readers and AI also all got three correct results.

But the BBC readers were the only ones to also get an exact score - in the Spurs game - giving them 60 points.

Lauren and AI both ended up on 30 points - AI did call Newcastle's draw with Arsenal correctly, but it was wrong about Bournemouth's win over Nottingham Forest, which everyone else got right.

Not many of you got that Liverpool-United scoreline correct, either.

From around 27,000 predictions, the most popular scoreline picked was a 2-1 home win and only 570 of you went for 4-0.

"When has AI ever gone for a 4-0?" said Sutton. "That's what the crowd wants. The crowd don't want AI - who wants mathematics?

"The crowd want gut-feeling and instinct. I think I should get 1,000 points for that prediction, because I was brave enough to go for it."

For the first time this season, there were SIX games where no-one made a correct prediction - who thought it was easy?

The biggest shock of the week was Leeds' 4-1 victory at Stamford Bridge. Again from around 27,000 predictions, more than 3,600 (14%) of you did back Daniel Farke's side to win... but just 11 of you saw that scoreline coming.
