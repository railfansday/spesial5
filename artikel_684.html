‘Glee’ Reboot: ‘Sadness’ Over Naya Rivera and Cory Monteith’s Deaths Is ‘Going to Be a Part’ of the Show, Says Co-Creator Ian Brennan

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

“Glee” co-creator Ian Brennan admits he wasn’t always open to the upcoming “Glee” reboot.

“I wasn’t that excited about it,” he told me earlier this week at an FYC event for his latest television project “Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story.” “Every time it came up, it was like, ‘Why would you do that again?’ For whatever reason, in the last six months or so, I completely did a 180 about it. It just seems the idea is right. I think the time is right. Now I’m really excited about it. In September, creator Ryan Murphy revealed he had just completed the first script for a reboot. “Brad [Falchuk], Ian and I, who wrote all the first two seasons, have written something,” he told Entertainment Tonight. “I’ve been in touch with around 10 of the original cast members. It’s very sweet, and they all had a lot of questions, but they all were very enthusiastic about the idea.”

Brennan told me, “You’re catching these characters at a totally different part of their life … The timing of these characters thinking, ‘Maybe that was the best time in my life, being in that choir in high school’ and then once you think that you’re like, ‘Oh, that show’s going to work.’”

While a production start date has yet to be set, Brennan touched on how the reboot may handle the deaths of Finn actor Cory Monteith, who suffered a fatal accidental heroin overdose, and Naya Rivera, who played Santana and died after she drowned in 2020 while trying to save her four-year-old son during a boat outing.

“We’re still figuring a lot of it out,” Brennan said. “That’s going to be part of it, that sadness and that loss. But still in all, I think [“Glee”] is too big of a thing to sort of let lay. There’s something about that feels right right now.”

As for Brennan’s song picks for the reboot, he said, “We didn’t do enough Journey. We did almost all of Journey but there’s still a bunch of hits.”

The “Monster” anthology is a far cry (or scream, for that matter) from “Glee.” I know [“Monster”] is a much different animal, but actually at its core like all writing it’s trying to get inside the head of someone else’s mind,” Brennan said. “I’m stunned at how fun it is to do this show.”

Before Murphy suggested Lizzie Borden to his team they were working on three different seasons around Luigi Mangione, the 1984 New York City subway shooter Bernie Goetz and the Unabomber. “I don’t think it’s talking out of turn because we’re never going to do it,” Brennan said. “We were going to do like a triptych of Luigi Mangione, Bernie Goetz and Unabomber. It was really interesting about male rage, about vigilantes. And it was just hard to write. It was like pushing like a dead mule up a hill. Luigi, we don’t know anything about. Bernie Goetz is sort of interesting a bit in that sort of Michael Douglas in ‘Falling Down’ kind of way. Kaczynski is fascinating, very bright, a very strange mind, but nothing really sexy about that story really.”

Ella Beatty stars in the show as the titular Lizzie Borden. She often returned home after a day of shooting covered in fake blood. “Shaving cream actually is a great hack to get fake blood off of your skin and out of your hair,” Beatty said, adding, “I feel like I was always covered in blood. I feel like I almost never wasn’t.”

She joked that she “frequently” took “shaving cream baths.”

Charlie Hunnam, who plays Lizzie’s father, said his love of axes began way before he signed on for the show. He actually has a collection of about a dozen axes. “Some of them are in my garage, some of them are in my pickup truck,” he said. “I have like a sick obsession. I cannot drive past a wood pile in L.A. or anywhere when a tree has been felled and they leave all of those rounds of wood on the road I have to like a complete crazy person pull over and fill my pickup truck with wood and then go cut it. I have a little back area in my office building, and I probably have three, four or five tons of chopped firewood.”
