Liverpool 0-1 Arsenal PLAYER RATINGS: Who put in the 'complete' performance? Who disappointed once again in a big game? And who barely put a foot wrong in Anfield bearpit?

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

By LEWIS STEELE, FOOTBALL REPORTER and ISAAN KHAN, REPORTER

Published: 13:51 EDT, 1 November 2026 | Updated: 04:02 EDT, 2 November 2026

Arsenal's miserly defence thwarted Liverpool at Anfield to send the Premier League champions three points clear at the top.

Kai Havertz tapped in the winner from a fine Arsenal move 16 minutes in, with Alexis Mac Allister spurning Liverpool's best chance to equalise before the break.

Wave after wave of attack from the home side came and went in the second period, although Andoni Iraola's men were unable to conjure many clear-cut chances, and they failed to have a single shot on target.

The Gunners took advantage of Man City's defeat by Brighton on Saturday and now have clear water between themselves and the chasing pack.

So who shone the most on an attritional and potentially pivotal afternoon in the title race?
