Bundesliga: Dortmund and Bayern ensure, Leipzig and Nkunku are a hit

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The race at the top continues in the Bundesliga. This Saturday, Borussia Dortmund did the job against Elversberg (2-1), thanks to a double from Felix Nmecha. A valuable success which allows BVB to stay level with Bayern Munich, also winner on the Mainz pitch (1-2). But despite this victory, the Bavarians experienced a new scare with the injury exit of Manuel Neuer, who was just making his return to competition. A new blow for the German goalkeeper and his team.

In the other matches, RB Leipzig signed the card of the afternoon by crushing Augsburg (5-2), confirming its good dynamic with in particular a goal and an assist for Christopher Nkunku. For its part, Eintracht Frankfurt avoided the worst on the Paderborn pitch (2-2). Having been in difficulty for a long time, the men of Frankfurt were able to count on a double from Jonathan Burkardt, notably author of the equalizing goal in added time, to snatch a precious point despite an expulsion from their ranks.

Borussia Dortmund 2-1 Elversberg: Félix Nmecha (55th, 71st) / Noel Futkeu (81st)

Mainz 1-2 Bayern Munich : Phillip Tietz (90th+3) / Joshua Kimmich (14th) and Lennart Karl (57th)

Paderborn 2-2 Eintracht Frankfurt: Raphael Obermair (45th) and Marvin Pieringer (73rd) / Jonathan Burkardt (23rd, 90th +3)

RB Leipzig 5-2 Augsburg: Ezechiel Banzuzi (33rd), Christopher Nkunku (35th), David Raum (45th+1), Rômulo (60th) and Christoph Baumgartner (66th) / Anton Kade (1st), Uchenna Ogundu (90th+3)
