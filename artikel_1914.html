The knives are out for West Ham talent as change is urged for Portsmouth trip

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

West Ham head off to Fratton Park for a mid table clash against Portsmouth tonight – with the winner catapulting themselves back towards the play-off zone. A defeat for Scott Parker’s Hammers is well-nigh unthinkable after the likeable former Hammers midfielder has begun his tenure as head coach with a two draws and a loss.

Must-win? To most Hammers fans – certainly those travelling midweek to the game, yes.

Many commented on the lacklustre performance from Divine Mukasa at the weekend and how his patchy form has not really helped the Hammers catch fire in midfield.

It is asking a lot to expect a teenager to be pivotal in the Irons’ attack but such is the attention on this team struggling in the second tier, criticism is never far away. Footballfancast.com Suggest Parker needs to bring in one of Nuno’s favoured players at Mukasa’s expense:

“Against Preston, Mukasa’s struggles became clearer than ever. He failed to make a difference in possession, making just four line-breaking passes and failing to complete a single dribble, while also proving to be a liability going the other way.

Many suggest the higher energy – chaos of Mohamadou Kante is what the side needs just now

Parker simply has to make a change against Portsmouth…..

Against Portsmouth, the former Fulham boss must start by dropping Mukasa and welcoming Mohamadou Kante into the side. Swapping one young star for another, the latter will instantly offer more stability in the middle of the park and has already proved this season that he can chip in with a goal or two.”

Likening Kanté to French legend Patrick Viera, the report demands the leggy explosiveness of Kante in place of the measured, more cultured football of Mukasa.

Both have their place, but perhaps Kanté needs to be the replacement ready to be unleashed at half time if West Ham’s failure to launch extends to yet another game.
