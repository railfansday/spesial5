I have heard fans talk about a tough run of Newcastle United fixtures.

I had a quick look at the upcoming Newcastle United fixtures and I think it gives a really positive outlook.

Obviously no Premier League game looks easy this season BUT some are easier than others.

Put it this way, would you prefer to play teams that are generally winning matches and are towards the top of the Premier League, or those who generally aren’t winning and are towards the bottom?

After that excellent 2-2 draw with league leaders Arsenal, Newcastle next play second top Brighton away. The Seagulls having lost 2-0 at Hull yesterday.

Following that though, Newcastle play three of the bottom five. Man U and Sunderland are both in the relegation zone and have to visit St James’ Park, then United travel to Ipswich who are fifth bottom.

As things currently stand, Newcastle United have played five of the top seven in the Premier League and only one of the bottom five in the table.

Writing this before the Brentford v Everton match on Monday night, there are eight more Newcastle United fixtures before NUFC have played all 19 teams.

These eight left to play are currently placed in the Premier League table 2nd, 3rd, 8th, 10th, 16th, 17th, 18th, 19th.

Looking at these Newcastle United fixtures that are coming up and then looking at the Premier League table, I think it is fair to say that NUFC have had a tough start to the season and no reason why they can’t start to pick up more points.

Of course we will need to see more of the kind of form Newcastle United showed in the second half on Saturday against Arsenal, a little less of what they showed in the first half.
