Mikel Arteta confirms Arsenal injury blow as William Saliba and Bukayo Saka updates issued

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Your weekly briefing on Arsenal, featuring team news and expert analysis from Matt Verri

William Saliba is “ready” to make his first-team return for Arsenal, but Martin Zubimendi faces “weeks” out with a hamstring injury, says Mikel Arteta.

Defender Saliba is yet to feature for the first team this season due to a long-term back injury, but played 45 minutes in an Under-21 match against Aston Villa just before the international break and then featured in a behind-closed-doors friendly against Watford.

He is now in contention to be in the matchday squad at Newcastle on Saturday evening.

Arteta told reporters on Friday: “He is ready, he’s in a good condition. We want to ramp up his minutes.

“He played two games, one behind-closed-doors and one more official. He did really well and he feels in good condition.

However, Zubimendi will be absent at St James’ Park and for the upcoming matches after being forced off for Spain against England last week.

“[Zubimendi] is out,” Arteta added. “Unfortunately he has a slight problem in his hamstring.

“He wasn’t too concerned after the game but after the MRI scan showed an injury there. he’s going to be out for a few weeks I think.”

Saka will also be missing as he continues to recover from a quad injury, though Arteta confirmed he has not required surgery.

The Gunners talisman has missed the last five games for Arsenal and is not expected back much before Christmas.

Arteta said of Saka: “He had another test. He’s evolving really well, but it looks like it’s going to be an eight-week injury, more or less.

“I think it’s four at the moment so still going to be a while until he’s back with the team.”

On whether that timeframe left Arsenal’s match at home to Manchester United on December 20 as the target for his return, Arteta added: “I cannot be that specific.

“In four weeks’ time a lot of things can happen. Maybe it is three and then it is five. At the end, there are a few elements. One is the healing of that tissue - and we cannot predict exactly when that tissue is going to be in the best condition to push it.

“Then there is the rehab plan that we have now, what he needs to do in the gym, what he needs to do in terms of treatment and on the field.

“Everything can progress at the right speed to give him the best chance to come back.”
