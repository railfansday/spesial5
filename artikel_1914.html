20 Most Beautiful Wives and Girlfriends (WAGs) in World Soccer Ranked

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Correspondent AUTHORITY Football journalist with experience at Reach PLC and PA Media; University of Central Lancashire graduate. FOCUS Fan-engagement content and match analysis across the Premier League and Championship. THE INSIGHT Oliver utilises a network of club and industry contacts to deliver verified reporting and fan-first content. He provides the human stories behind the results to ensure fans get the full picture as it happens.

From red-carpet galas to World Cup stands, the wives and girlfriends of soccer’s biggest icons command nearly as much global attention as the superstars on the pitch.

Originally coined by the British press during the 2006 World Cup, the term "WAG" has evolved into a worldwide pop-culture phenomenon—defined by power couples, luxury fashion, and massive social media empires.

Today, the partners of the game's biggest stars are household names in their own right, blending chart-topping careers, modeling contracts, and business ventures with life alongside football royalty.

Football Insider has ranked the 20 most beautiful and iconic wives and girlfriends in world soccer—spanning both current stars and retired legends—based on global star power, glamour, and cultural impact.

Scroll down to see who claims the number one spot, and let us know in the comments who we ranked too low.

Brazilian supermodel Goulart began dating German goalkeeper Trapp in 2015, with the pair getting engaged in July 2018. She is best known for her work as a Victoria's Secret Angel from 2005 to 2008, and for walking in the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show from 2005 through 2016. She has also modelled for leading designers and luxury brands, including Armani, Chanel and Valentino, while appearing on numerous covers of major fashion magazines.

Renowned for her athletic physique and natural elegance, 42-year-old Goulart is widely regarded as one of the most beautiful women in the modelling industry. She boasts over four million followers on Instagram.

Ivanovic is a star in her own right, after spending over a decade as one of the leading women's tennis players from 2003 to her retirement in 2016. She reached the number one spot for 12 weeks in 2008 and won an impressive 15 singles career titles, including the 2008 French Open.

The Serbian began a relationship with former Bayern Munich and Man United star Schweinsteiger in 2014, and they married in Venice in 2016. The pair had three children in the next seven years but decided to end their marriage in 2025.

Johansen is one of the biggest names of the latest era of WAGs, owing to the meteoric rise to stardom of her partner, Haaland, over the last decade. She was a soccer player who came through the youth ranks of his boyhood club, Bryne FK, and the pair knew each other from childhood before beginning a relationship in 2021.

The couple have kept details of their relationship remarkably private, considering his fame, but 22-year-old Johansen has amassed a huge Instagram following of over two million followers and is now considered a celebrity in their home country of Norway. She has adorned the cover of numerous high-profile national magazines, and the couple have one son together.

Hertfordshire native Benson is another relatively new face of WAG culture, becoming one of the standout figures of this era thanks to her striking looks and impeccable style. She drew national attention in 2024 after being photographed in the stands watching England star partner Saka at that summer's Euros.

She has built a career of her own, working in media planning and communications after studying Public Relations and Media at Birmingham City University. She reportedly met the Arsenal winger in 2020, and the couple announced their engagement in November 2025 to huge fanfare online. Adoring followers are waiting with bated breath for details and photos of whenever they do tie the knot soon.

Rubio has enjoyed a substantial career in Spanish entertainment independent of her relationship with Real Madrid legend Ramos. She appeared on numerous comedy programmes in their home country in the 1990s and 2000s, then started to present game shows and appear in movies as her profile grew outside of Spain.

Rubio was named Spain's sexiest woman by FHM magazine in both 2008 and 2009, and began dating Ramos in 2012. They are now known as one of the most iconic couples in soccer history, as they have since married and share four sons. Rubio is still active as a TV presenter and boasts a whopping 10.6m followers on Instagram.

Nara and Icardi's story is one that has dominated news headlines far and wide for many years. It is perhaps the most publicised soccer romance of the last decade. The Argentine television personality is widely known for her glamorous appearance and confident personality, but the relationship has made her a globally known star.

She was initially married to Icardi's Sampdoria teammate and friend, Maxi Lopez, but they separated in late 2013 and by May 2014 Nara was controversially married to the Argentine international. The players' games against each other were subsequently known as the 'Wanda Derby'. They were then involved in a major scandal in 2021, as Nara accused Icardi of being unfaithful, and despite several reconciliation attempts and children involved, they were officially divorced in October 2026.

Carbonero is one of Spain's most recognisable media personalities, admired for her effortless elegance and long-standing body of journalistic work. She rose to prominence covering football for La Sexta and Telecinco in the noughties and first met legendary goalkeeper Casillas while they were both working at the 2009 Confederations Cup.

Their relationship became known across the globe due to a surprise embrace live on air at the 2010 World Cup after Spain's victory in the final while she interviewed her partner. The couple wed in 2016 and built a family, but separated in 2021.

Vinicius' status as a global superstar in soccer has been flanked by his on-off relationship with Fonseca over the last 18 months, which has unsurprisingly kept them both in the headlines for both good and bad reasons. The USA-born influencer moved to Brazil as a child and became immensely famous for her YouTube channel and previous relationships with notable media and music personalities before meeting the Madrid forward in 2025.

She attended his 25th birthday in July as they grew closer, and the pair confirmed a relationship later that year. They split in May 2026 as Fonseca alluded to their differences online, but reconciled two months later before separating again in October. She remains one of Brazil’s most influential internet personalities, keeping her huge 57m Instagram following updated with snapshots into the glamorous life she is currently leading without Vinicius by her side.

Bellingham is one of the biggest stars in soccer right now, and his enormous commercial appeal has inevitably led to intrigue around his personal life. He has been romantically linked to American model Castro since 2024 after they reportedly matched on celebrity dating app Raya.

The California-born personality is actually six years older than her English superstar boyfriend, and became known before their relationship for her budding modelling career and appearances in music videos for globally known artists like Drake and DJ Khaled. She is regularly spotted at his games for club and country and has developed a sizeable social media following as a result of their romance.

Sabatini is another who can count herself as a celebrity in her own right without Argentine international husband Dybala to boost her profile. She hails from a family of actors and sportspeople, and was a child actor who won awards for her work on nationally acclaimed TV show Aliados.

She opened live shows for the likes of Ariana Grande and Coldplay after turning her attention to music in 2017, and met Dybala at a concert of the former. The pair married in 2024 and have since started a family. Sabatini boasts over 68m streams as a music artist on Spotify.

Stunning blonde Ward always seemed destined for fame in some measure, after her mother, Dawn, became a multi-millionaire through interior design and her father, Ashley, had his own soccer career in the Premier League. Dawn shot to fame on The Real Housewives of Cheshire in 2015 and Taylor appeared on the show while building her own profile as a model and fashion influencer online.

She met Algerian star Mahrez through a mutual friend in 2020, and they quickly became a real power couple as English media lapped up the idea of two very different worlds colliding through fame. They appeared together on Amazon Prime’s 2024 documentary series Married to the Game, and are now married and have children.

Pinnock is a very clear example of a modern-day WAG who is actually much more famous than her soccer-playing partner. The striking chart-topping pop star is one of Britain’s most recognisable celebrities after forming one quarter of the hugely successful girl group Little Mix between 2010 and 2021. The X Factor-formed collective sold more than 50m records worldwide and boasted five UK number one singles.

She met former Burnley and Watford striker Gray while holidaying in Marbella in 2016, as they soon got together and were eventually married in Jamaica in 2023. Pinnock is currently pursuing a solo music career and is branching out into the world of TV and fashion.

Pinnock’s former bandmate and close friend Edwards has also found herself in the WAG world for a similar length of time. The cheeky Geordie shared the highs of Little Mix’s extraordinary success after initially applying to The X Factor as a solo contestant.

Edwards met Celtic midfielder Oxlade-Chamberlain on Raya in 2016, and they made their first public appearance at the 2017 BRIT Awards to massive excitement and media attention. They were engaged in 2022 and welcomed two children before tying the knot after almost 10 years together in June 2026. With Little Mix on an indefinite hiatus, her debut solo album ‘Perrie’ reached number three in the UK charts in September 2025.

The idea of a soccer WAG first became a prominent part of British culture and the wider celebrity world during the 2006 World Cup in Germany. English international star Rooney had been with his future wife, Coleen, for a decade, and she was arguably the most important figure in the early era of the WAG in soccer, becoming one of the most recognisable faces among Three Lions’ players’ partners at the tournament.

She was launched to stardom alongside him as a teenager in the early noughties, with tabloids watching their every move and the Liverpool native developing her own career through high-profile endorsements and TV appearances. She is still as big a name as ever, despite his retirement, and has retained a down-to-earth image that many can relate to, especially after the huge 2019 ‘Wagatha Christie’ scandal.

Clancy is widely known for her slender figure and glamorous looks which have seen her dominate the WAG world for decades on end. She finished as runner-up on Britain’s Next Top Model in 2006 and was instantly propelled into an international career in the industry that saw her adorn the covers of numerous iconic fashion magazines and amass huge wealth in her own right.

The former Strictly Come Dancing winner met England hero Crouch in 2006 shortly after he had signed for Liverpool, as they began dating, were engaged in 2009 and wed in 2011. She has continued to keep up national celebrity status with appearances on red carpets galore, high-profile modelling contracts and increased TV appearances. She is also renowned for her down-to-earth persona and sense of humour, a popular match for the man who popularised the 'Robot' dance during his cult-hero playing days.

Neymar is a modern-day Brazilian icon who has dominated social media and the wider sporting zeitgeist of the last 15 years, and so his romance with Biancardi has also attracted major interest across the globe. She was a known Instagram and YouTube influencer before their relationship, but was catapulted to worldwide fame when they went public online in 2022.

Their turbulent partnership has been closely scrutinised since then, with allegations of infidelity and subsequent reunions in abundance, which have all been played out in front of a widespread online audience. Biancardi now has more than 15m followers on Instagram and shares three children with the Selecao soccer star.

Shakira needs little introduction as a global superstar and a phenomenon of Latin pop music who is among the world’s best-selling artists of all time. She is credited with popularising Spanish-language music across the globe and has carved out her own path as a soccer icon with her 2010 World Cup anthem ‘Waka Waka’ and 2026 official song ‘Dai Dai.’

Her romance with ex-Barcelona and Spain defender Pique was one of the most high-profile in sports history, and actually began at the 2010 tournament. They became a prominent celebrity couple who were followed everywhere by paparazzi, and even shared two children, but made particular headlines after a bitter 2022 split that later saw the Colombian star aim a diss track at her ex-partner.

Another global superstar music artist makes the top three of our list, with ‘Posh Spice’ Beckham still known as the benchmark against which WAG style is measured. They were the original soccer celebrity couple who transcended the sport after building hugely successful and high profile respective careers in their own domains.

Victoria has evolved from cultural pop icon to one of the biggest fashion designers on the planet in the 21st century, with the pair still by each other’s side to this day after first dating in 1997 (albeit while surviving a dose or two of scandal along the way). She is also a mother of four who remains heavily involved with several of the biggest fashion labels in the world, and boasts a massive online presence to boot. The original but, on our list, not quite the best.

Roccuzzo and Messi share a love story that has stood the test of time. Being the childhood sweetheart of the greatest soccer player ever can’t have always been easy, but the sweet Rosario native has stuck by his side and tried to remain as out of the public eye as possible throughout his decorated career. She moved to Barcelona to be with him in 2010, and they wed in 2017.

Her public profile is low-key but active, as she cares for their three children and keeps her 42m Instagram followers regularly updated with a glimpse into the lifestyle of one of the most famous sporting families ever. Roccuzzo also utilises her following to front commercial campaigns for global companies like Adidas, DoorDash and Anastasia Beverley Hills.

The world’s biggest sports star needs a strong partner by his side, and Ronaldo has had Spanish glamour model Rodriguez on his arm for over 10 years now. She has embraced global stardom and built a successful career on top of her relationship to become an icon of modern celebrity culture as an individual.

Rodriguez boasts the most Instagram followers of any WAG on this list by some margin, at over 77m, regularly works with top fashion brands and is often seen at the biggest events in fashion, film and television. She was the subject of Netflix docuseries ‘I Am Georgina’ between 2022 and 2024, and remains a figure of constant fascination, with swathes of adoring fans hanging on her every move.
