House on Alley 37 / kelvan_studio

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Text description provided by the architects. This house is an attempt to turn the relationship between the home and the private residential area into a design question. It explores the idea of an edge between the neighborhood and the house with two simultaneous characteristics: one differentiates the house from the neighborhood, while the other connects it to the neighborhood. It is a combination of body and space.
