Before buying a portable power station, you’ll want to know how much power your appliances draw and how much energy they use over time.

For example, a 150W appliance running continuously for 10 hours uses 1,500Wh. A 1,500W appliance gets through that same amount in one hour. Running a coffee maker briefly is a different proposition from keeping a high-draw appliance on all afternoon.

A rough runtime calculation is: Usable battery capacity in watt-hours ÷ average load in watts = hours of operation.

Divide the Apex 200’s nominal 2,560Wh by a steady 150W load, and you get about 17 hours before accounting for conversion losses and the battery system’s own power use. Real runtime will be lower.

Refrigerators complicate that calculation because their compressors cycle on and off. Check measured daily energy use where possible, along with startup demand – motors can briefly draw more power when they kick on.

If your usual blackout lasts four to 12 hours, start by looking at the Apex 200 on its own.

BLUETTI estimates it can run a 150W refrigerator for 10–12 hours, or support low-draw essentials such as CPAPs, wifi routers, and mobile devices for two to three days, depending on the equipment and how it’s used. That doesn’t mean every device on that list will run together for three days. Your combined load determines the runtime.

The Apex 200 also gives you more control over where that energy goes. Four independently controllable Smart AC outlets built right into the unit – no need to buy smart plugs! – let you switch individual devices on and off through the BLUETTI App, set schedules, and apply state-of-charge controls. So if something doesn’t need power overnight, you can turn off its power at the outlet and save the fridge’s battery.

BLUETTI calls the feature an industry first for portable power stations. The system also works with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Home Assistant.

A UPS function switches connected equipment to battery power in 15 milliseconds. Check that switching time against your equipment’s requirements, especially for a CPAP or other sensitive device.

With AC output on and the display off, idle consumption drops to just 10.5W.

A winter freeze or severe storm can leave you without grid power for days. That’s when adding extra storage starts to make more sense.

For typical power outages, adding one or two BLUETTI B300K batteries can extend essential home backup to two to three days.

But you can go further. One Apex 200 supports up to six B500K expansion batteries, bringing its maximum capacity to 33.28kWh.

Start with your most likely outage, then decide whether you need more runtime, a charging source, or both.

Extra batteries extend runtime. Solar panels or another charging source recharge them. For outages that stretch into days, both can be worth having.
