Zidane ‘frustrated’ by sluggish first half despite France victory

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Zinedine Zidane was frustrated with France's first-half performance against Turkiye but was pleased to see them return to their style after the break to secure the win.

France finished their Nations League campaign with a 2-1 win over Turkiye, securing top spot in Group A1 as they qualified for the quarter-finals, finishing above Belgium.

An Emirhan Topcu own goal shortly after the break put France ahead, before Junior Kroupi's first international goal made sure of the points as Abdulkerim Bardakci netted a late consolation.

It was a much-needed win for France, who had suffered a 3-1 loss to Italy last time out – Zidane's first defeat in charge – to take the fight for a quarter-final place to the last game.

Les Bleus have now gone 10 matches without leading at half-time, dating back to the World Cup last 16 against Sweden (1-0 at half-time).

And though Zidane was disappointed by the sluggish first 45 minutes, he was much happier with Les Bleus' second-half showing.

"The objective was to qualify, possibly even finish first," Zidane told TF1.

"However, regarding the performance, we can be a little perplexed, especially our pressing in the first half. When you don't see certain things on the pitch, you get a bit frustrated.

"It's mainly about the pressing, because we allowed space to be created, and we weren't good at all with our pressing. We corrected that at half-time, and we were better afterwards.

"These are things we still need to improve. I wasn't happy, and neither were the players. But we corrected it.

"We conceded a lot, especially in the first half. In other matches, that was very rare. It's not just our defence; it's all of us together.

"It's not one or two players; it's things we have to put in place. We have to apply pressure as a team. We need to keep possession.

"We like to run with the ball, not without it; that's not our style. We did it better in the second half; we have to repeat that."

France were dominant against Turkiye, who finished bottom of the group after losing all six of their matches.

Les Bleus finished with 23 shots worth 3.4 expected goals (xG) and had five big chances overall.

However, they also gave up 13 attempts at the other end, with Turkiye generating 1.6 xG, and defender Jeremy Jacquet noted how vital the half-time team talk was in the result.

"The coach told us at half-time that he didn't think we were aggressive enough when we lost possession, and we corrected that in the second half," Jacquet said.

"The coach didn't yell at us, but he raised his voice at half-time about our areas for improvement when we lost the ball, and he felt we were holding onto it too much. He wanted us to move the ball around more."
