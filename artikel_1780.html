BREAKING: Crystal Palace set to sack Pierre Sage

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Crystal Palace are set to officially set to sack Pierre Sage after just 16 games in charge, according to The Athletic.

While the former Lens boss might have only signed a three-year deal at Selhurst Park back in June, the French tactician has endured a nightmare start to life in the Premier League and looks set to leave south London with Palace sat rock-bottom of the table.

Heading into November’s international break with a 3-1 loss at home against Liverpool on Sunday afternoon, the Eagles have registered just seven points from their opening 10 Premier League appearances and supporters around south London have made their frustrations abundantly clear.

Crashing back down to earth since their iconic Europa Conference League success under Oliver Glasner back in May, Crystal Palace hold the league’s worst defensive tally having already conceded 24 goals.

With things behind the scenes at Selhurst Park moving incredibly quickly following Sunday’s 3-1 defeat against Liverpool, it has also been suggested that the Palace hierarchy have already put together their list of replacements for Sage.

The Athletic’s report claims that alongside admiration for Coventry City boss Frank Lampard, former Tottenham manager Thomas Frank is currently sat in the driving seat to make his Premier League return.

Frank has been out of a job since he was fired by Spurs back in February following a downbeat eighth-month reign in north London.
