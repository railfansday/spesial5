Leeds United star Dominic Calvert-Lewin is type of player Arsenal ‘could do with’, claims Theo Walcott

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Leeds United star Dominic Calvert-Lewin in the type of player Arsenal “could do with”, according to former Gunners winger Theo Walcott.

Calvert-Lewin scored his 21st Leeds goal at the weekend, converting from the penalty spot as the Whites completed a remarkable 4-1 rout of Chelsea.

He has enjoyed a new lease of life at Elland Road and has been named in the last two England squads, collecting the most recent of his 15 caps in the 3-2 Nations League win over Spain.

Walcott, who spent the bulk of his playing career at the Emirates Stadium, believes Calvert-Lewin could suit his former employers.

He told talkSPORT: “I look at someone like Dominic Calvert-Lewin. I think he's been outstanding this year. He's the kind of number nine Arsenal could do with in this moment of time.

“I think he's (Viktor Gyokeres) suited for an old-school winger, not one of those inverted ones who delay the crosses.

“He wants to be in those areas, he wants to be that player to get on the end of things. I don't think he times his runs, because at times, he's not sure when the ball is going to come in, if it will come in.

“As well, the way he plays affects everyone. Arsenal are used to playing with a false nine, to then everyone changing their dynamic of playing. I think it really inhibits the way Arsenal play. And I do like [Kai] Havertz, but I do feel like there's a position up for grabs.”

Calvert-Lewin struggled for fitness and form in his final years at Everton, who could not get him tied down to a new contract.

He became a free agent at the end of the 2024/25 season and Leeds’ swoop for him was widely regarded as a risky one.

However, their decision to hand a deal to the England international has been well and truly vindicated.
