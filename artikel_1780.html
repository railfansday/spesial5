Michael Carrick sack news LIVE: Man Utd latest as board expected to make announcement on manager's future

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

All eyes are once again on Manchester United today as fans await a decision on the future of under-fire manager Michael Carrick. Man Utd’s hierarchy have a huge call to make once more in the aftermath of Sunday’s remarkable and chaotic 4-4 draw with Aston Villa at Old Trafford, in which Carrick’s side twice came from two goals behind to seal a dramatic point in stoppage time. That was just days after they had also overturned a 2-0 half-time deficit to defeat Roma 3-2 in the Champions League and end a dismal nine-match winless run across all competitions.

The players are still fighting for their boss and fans once again showed their strong support, though it remains to be seen if these terrible starts to games will ultimately prove his downfall. United head into the November international break crucially sitting inside the bottom three amid genuine fears over a potential relegation battle this season, having won just one of their first 10 matches - at home to newly-promoted Ipswich back in August - and collected only eight points with a tough trip to Liverpool to come next.

Carrick was in uncharacteristically spiky mood when answering the latest questions on his future on Sunday night, insisting that he would definitely not resign as a crisis meeting is reportedly now planned to discuss his future, with the likes of Eddie Howe, Julian Nagelsmann and possibly even Mauricio Pochettino waiting in the wings. Antonio Conte, Didier Deschamps, Kieran McKenna and Gareth Southgate are some of the other names in the frame to replace Carrick at Old Trafford before the Liverpool game.

Meanwhile, Fabian Hurzeler - who recently distanced himself from the post - has also been tipped by bookmakers to become the next Man Utd manager, along with former player Ole Gunnar Solskjaer, who has been out of work since leaving Besiktas last year.

William Gallas has provided Manchester United’s decision-makers with a warning if they are considering replacing Michael Carrick with Antonio Conte.

Speaking on behalf of SeatPick - the leading platform for Arsenal tickets - Gallas said: “I don’t know if Antonio Conte can fix Manchester United because from what I understand now the players in that dressing room are completely different from the players 10, 15 or 20 years ago.

“If you bring in Antonio Conte, you know he has a big personality. You know he will ask his players to adapt to his system, but I don't know if the players at Manchester United can do this.

"It's a different generation. Now you have to adapt to the players at this club, not just the players adapting to you. It has to be a compromise. Before, it used to be that the players had to adapt to the manager. Nobody said anything. They had great results. Now life has changed.

“A manager like Conte would have to understand that ‘OK I have to adapt to this new generation, and if I do that, at the same time, I will need them to adapt to what I want from them,’ and that’s why you have to be sure about what you are going into. Manchester United also have to be sure about the manager they are bringing in because the message has to make sense and be understood by the players in the dressing room. Otherwise it will be the same problem again and again.

“Ultimately, the problem with United is the players that they’ve brought to the club because they aren’t at the level to play for Manchester United. With all the respect I have for other clubs, Manchester United are not Coventry City. They are not Crystal Palace. We are talking about Manchester United.

“They should only be signing the best players to play for the club but time and time again they have made big mistakes. Antonio Conte cannot change that. No manager can. You have to look higher up at the club first.”
