“Mighty Miniatures” Features Over 300 Tiny Artworks From Indigenous Communities Across the Western Hemisphere

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Opening November 21 at the National Museum of the American Indian in New York, this exhibition includes masterpieces created over a span of nearly two centuries.

“Mighty Miniatures,” an exhibition celebrating the intricate art of miniatures, is on view at the Smithsonian’s National Museum of the American Indian in New York from Saturday, November 21 through Friday, August 20, 2027. Curated by Anya Montiel (Tohono O’odham descent), the exhibition features over 300 pieces of art that showcase the skill of Indigenous weavers, sculptors, carvers and other artists that work on a miniscule scale.

The exhibition includes tiny masterpieces created over a span of nearly two centuries by artists from Indigenous communities from North and South America, as well as Hawai'i and the Caribbean. The small works reflect worldviews and cultural expressions, encouraging play and transferring intergenerational knowledge.

“‘Mighty Miniatures’ brings together some of the smallest works in the museum collection, reaching across the Western Hemisphere and spanning nearly 200 years,” Montiel said. “It is amazing to see how these miniatures have been made with such skill, ingenuity, and detail. This exhibition is a celebration of Indigenous artistry on a small scale and the artists who create them.”

Left: Nahua doll’s chair, no date. Wood, yarn. 0.7 x 0.5 in. (14/4064); right: Tohono O’odham I’itoi ki: (Man-in-the-Maze) miniature basket, ca. 2000. Arizona. Horsehair. 4 x 2.6 in. 26/8865

The exhibition is divided into six sections:

Left: Anishinaabe (Ojibwe) canoe model, no date. Minnesota, Birchbark, sinew, paint. 0.5 x 2.4 x 1.1 in. (20/4941); right: Floyd Kingeekuk Sr. (Siberian Yupik, 1933–2006), ringed seal figure, 1975. Savoonga, Alaska. Ivory, ink. 0.9 x 4.1 x 1.2 in. (27/684)

A family-friendly opening program is scheduled for Saturday, Dec. 12, 11 AM–4 PM. Multi-disciplinary artist Lance Cardinal (Cree), a finalist on the TV show Best In Miniature, will lead visitors in mini drum making sessions. Gallery tours by Montiel will illuminate the stories behind the objects and discuss how the exhibition came together.
