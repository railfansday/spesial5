‘More Secret’ Version of Bitcoin: Zcash (ZEC) Eyes Another Wall Street ETF

Asset manager Bitwise has filed an S-1 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission for a spot Zcash (ZEC) exchange-traded fund. The document appeared in the SEC's database on Nov. 17, 2026. On Wednesday, ZEC rose to $975 before it pulled back to around $927.

Zcash is often called a "more secret" version of Bitcoin. According to the S-1, the Zcash network supports two types of transactions, and the "shielded" ones use a form of zero-knowledge cryptography known as zk-SNARKs. Holders of shielded Zcash "may selectively disclose transaction information to third parties," the filing says.

Bitwise's fund will not use this feature. "The Trust does not engage in shielded transactions in connection with its activities," according to the document.

The shares are expected to list on the Dallas-based Texas Stock Exchange (TXSE) under the ticker ZEC. According to the S-1, Coinbase Custody Trust Company will act as the Zcash custodian, and BNY Mellon will serve as the cash custodian. Bitwise also wrote that "the Trust will not stake or otherwise seek to derive additional Zcash from its holdings." The sponsor fee in the filing is listed as "0.___% per annum."

In the risk section of the S-1, Bitwise wrote that "a number of digital asset trading platforms have delisted Zcash, restricted trading in Zcash or declined to support Zcash." According to the filing, many of these platforms cited regulatory or compliance concerns.

The platforms that still support Zcash "do so only with respect to transparent addresses and do not permit deposits or withdrawals to or from shielded addresses," the document says. Bitwise also pointed to new EU rules that take effect on July 10, 2027. According to the S-1, the rules will "prohibit credit institutions, financial institutions and crypto-asset service providers in the European Union" from keeping accounts in such assets.

Bitwise CIO Matt Hougan named Zcash one of his top three assets for the decade in September, alongside Bitcoin and tokenization projects. Three days before the S-1 filing, he called ZEC a "golden mean" between open Bitcoin and anonymous Monero in an interview.

Grayscale's Zcash fund (ZCSH) has traded on NYSE Arca since August 2026, and the Winklevoss brothers filed for their own Zcash ETF on Nasdaq in October. ZCSH held $496.79 million as of the Nov. 17 close, according to SoSoValue. The fund has attracted $168.76 million in net inflows since its launch, and it recorded no inflows on Nov. 17, when Bitwise filed its S-1.
