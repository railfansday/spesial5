Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion on Tuesday, beating the Bloomberg consensus of $109.15 billion and more than doubling the $57 billion it posted a year earlier. Adjusted earnings reached $2.68 per share, against expectations of $2.47 and up from $1.30 in the same period last year. Data center sales climbed to $107.6 billion, ahead of the $101.85 billion forecast, while the Edge Computing unit brought in $8.2 billion compared with an estimated $7.49 billion. The chipmaker guided fourth-quarter revenue to between $127.4 billion and $132.6 billion, above the $125.25 billion Wall Street had pencilled in, and unveiled a $150 billion share buyback. Nvidia shares rose 2.41% to $159.20 in after-hours trading, according to Yahoo Finance, after closing the regular session 0.44% lower at $155.46. The results are the first under interim CEO Colette Kress, who took charge after Jensen Huang resigned last week.
