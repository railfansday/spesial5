Nintendo Restocks Metroid Dread Physical Copies Ahead Of Metroid Ravenous Launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

When Nintendo announced Metroid Ravenous for the Switch 2, there was a reported increase in Metroid Dread sales. This also led to a supposed boost in physical sales of the "out-of-print" Switch title, with some online listings asking "well above" the retail price.

If you are still on the hunt for a copy and happen to live in the US, Nintendo has now actually restocked the physical version of Metroid Dread on the Switch. You can once again purchase it through the company's website for $59.99. If we hear about official Nintendo restocks elsewhere, we'll provide an update.

Since the announcement of Ravenous in September, Metroid Dread has returned to the top of the eShop charts as more fans prepare for Samus' next side-scrolling adventure. It also currently holds the title of the best-selling game in the entire Metroid series, surpassing three million sales worldwide.

If you haven't already played this outstanding Switch release by developer MercurySteam, there's plenty of time, as Ravenous won't be arriving until 28th January 2027. Still, it is only a few months away, so you might want to get started now if you plan on jumping into Ravenous.
