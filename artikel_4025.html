Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds Free McLaren Racing Update Adds New Sonic Skin And Machine

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Sega's all-star racing game Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds has this week received a new McLaren Racing-themed update.

It includes Sonic in a McLaren Racing suit and the speed-type McLaren Racing machine. There's also a McLaren Racing festival taking place. Here's the rundown:

"Players can now race around the track for pole position as Sonic in the iconic papaya and anthracite McLaren livery. Starting Thursday, November 19 at 4:00 PM PT and until Sunday, November 29 at 3:59 PM PT, fans can jump into the McLaren Racing festival and earn festival points for decals, gadgets and more!"

Along with this, Sega has announced a new "3-episode animated special" is on the way. You can see the teaser trailer for Sonic the Hedgehog x McLaren Racing: Beyond Fast" in the video above.
