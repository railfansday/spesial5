American Unveils Limited-Edition Jacket Made of Recycled Aircraft Seats

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

American Airlines is joining in the “Cyber Week” shopping holiday with the planned release of a branded jacket made from reclaimed leather from the carrier’s old aircraft, including the McDonnell Douglas MD-80.

The special, limited-edition MA-1 flight jackets are made by Alpha Industries and come with an American patch celebrating the carrier’s 100th anniversary.

The airline plans to make 10 jackets available for purchase each day from Nov. 30 through Dec. 4, at the price of 200,000 AAdvantage miles each.

Alpha Industries said it received 650 seats from American for the project. Each jacket requires 10 to 12 seats to make, depending on the quality of the material.

American is also advertising its other branded merchandise ahead of the holidays, including suitcases and limited-edition pickleball paddles made in collaboration with Volair.

From Nov. 27 through Dec. 2, AAdvantage members can save up to 50% when purchasing 500,000 or more miles.
