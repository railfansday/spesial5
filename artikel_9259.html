The Chronicle Aluminum is the perfect piece of gear to take with you on every adventure. It adapts to be whatever you need to stabilize your shot, whether you need a full size tripod, monopod (with a 3-leg base or not), handheld, or table top tripod. Switch from your camera to phone in seconds with a twist of the knob, so you can easily change up how you capture content.

Replacing a standard center column is the Flexor. Simply twist a knob on the tripod’s yoke, and the Flexor pulls out to be a tabletop tripod or mini tripod that makes achieving ground level shots or handheld shots a breeze.

Made for every creator, the Chronicle Tripod Kit in Aluminum is multiple pieces of gear all in one compact package.

The Jasper 2.0 Medium Rolltop is popular for a reason. There are two spacious storage compartments with room for all of your gear. Store your camera, lenses, small lights, and accessories in the 7.5L of padded, customizable storage in the main compartment. The roll top adds 9L of storage for items like an extra layer, lunch, and other equipment. Tripod straps that attach to the base of the bag are included and there are clever pockets all over the bag, perfect for organizing your chaos.

Rounding out the bag is a padded harness system for a comfortable carry on all of your shoots, even for multiple days. You can choose a muted green or classic black in a durable 600D Polyester fabric with PU2 water resistant coating. And if you hit especially bad weather, there’s rain cover included.

If you spend most of your time in a studio, you know how important lighting is for producing the perfect content. This 2 light kit provides bright, soft illumination for your photography and videography. It’s a great way to start your studio or fill in other lighting needs.

The heads are extra-large to accept big or small lamps. There are four (E26/E27) sockets in each light head, which are compatible with photographic LED and CFL Edison-style lamps. Toggle switches on each light allow you to switch each lamp on or off independently. The kit includes large softboxes that mount directly to each light head (no speed rings necessary!). An integrated tilting bracket and handle on each light head makes it easy to direct, and a center slot accepts a photographic umbrella (sold separately) for illuminating large groups.

With this kit, you’ll be up and running in no time!

For smaller scale shoots and product photography, your best bet is the Chroma SLS2323RGB Still Life Studio. It’s perfect for photography and videography of still lifes, small subjects, merchandise, and more. You have access to multiple openings on the studio to capture different angles, which also helps to reduce unwanted reflections and outside light.

This still life studio kit measures 23” square and includes everything you need – customizable RGB LED lighting and interchangeable background sweeps that will make your still lifes and subjects look amazing. When you’re done, the kit folds flat for easy storage.

All of these gifts would be a great choice for the photographer or videographer in your life (or yourself!). These sale prices end on December 15, 2026, so shop the sale while you can and consider a chunk of your holiday shopping done.
