Felon Who Shot Man in 2018 Sentenced in D.C. After Fleeing Police With Loaded, High-Capacity Pistol

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Darious Phillips, 35, a previously convicted felon residing in the District of Columbia, was sentenced today in U.S. District Court to 20 months in prison in connection with his unlawful possession of a semiautomatic pistol while he was on supervised release for shooting a man three times, announced U.S. Attorney Jeanine Ferris Pirro.
