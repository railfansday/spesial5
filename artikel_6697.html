Wildly Delicious Weeknight Dinners To Keep You Cozy All November Long

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

There’s more than just Thanksgiving to plan for.

November is the month when comfort food season really kicks into full gear. It’s the last month that really feels like fall before those pleasantly chilly evenings turn teeth-chattering, the leaves are still colorful and on the trees, and, of course, we can’t forget about Thanksgiving, so we’re not letting it get away without celebrating all it has to offer. Join us this November as we cook up cozy, comforting meals for both weeknights and special occasions. We’ve got 40 recipes to help inspire you to make dinner this November—one for every day of the month, and then some.

We’ve included a good mix of entirely new recipes, like our lasagna alla vodka skillet and our sheet-pan chicken tagine, as well as some old cold-weather classics like our chicken & dumplings, our broccoli cheddar gnocchi soup, and our chili mac.

Looking for cozy recipes for November? Check out our favorite one-pan fall dinners, under-an-hour comfort foods, and easy comfort food recipes.

Lasagna and penne alla vodka are two Italian-American staples that go hand in hand. Because our one-pot lazy lasagna skillet is a weeknight favorite, it only felt right to consolidate these two classics in one pan.

This version of chicken biryani is inspired by Hyderabadi biryani, which is one of the most popular and iconic dishes in South Asia. This streamlined version stays as true as possible to the authentic flavors, using readily available grocery store ingredients, but cuts down on the time by finishing things in the oven.

We can never get enough of beef and broccoli. In this easy, hands-off version, we’re cooking thinly-sliced steak low and slow in a rich broth until it’s fall-apart delicious, and then throwing in the broccoli near the end so it cooks just long enough to become tender.

This sheet-pan chicken tagine is the ultimate glow-up, transforming the complex, slow-simmered flavors of the classic North African dish into a streamlined meal perfect for busy evenings or an effortless dinner party.

This pumpkin pot pie is basically a pumpkin coconut curry baked beneath a flaky, golden crust that turns out equal parts comforting and festive. The carved Jack-o'-lantern top transforms a simple pot pie into a showstopping centerpiece that's almost too cute to cut into.

Carbonara always feels like a luxurious dish, but it's made with the simplest ingredients: eggs, spaghetti, Pecorino Romano, and pork. Add a bottles of Shaoxing and soy sauce, and you've got yourself an unforgettable meal.

In the world of all of our Marry Me Chicken variations, this just may be our new favorite version. We have a soft spot for risotto because it feels elevated, but it’s also soothing to stand there and stir until the rice hits the right tenderness. This version is extra luxurious with sun-dried tomatoes and a touch of cream.

Pizza is our guilty pleasure, but sometimes we want the flavors of the classic dish with a little more protein, without ordering a meat lovers. This vegetarian dinner has everything you love about pizza—slightly sweet tomato sauce, Italian herbs, and plenty of cheese—but makes a protein-packed swap by substituting the crust for cannellini beans.

One spoonful instantly made me think of my childhood. This easy comfort food recipe is perfect on a cold night, and simple enough to whip up when you don't feel like making anything (like on a weeknight after a weekend full of potlucks and Friendsgivings).

Slow-cooked turkey legs are this year’s ultimate Thanksgiving hack (we're not stopping you the rest of the month either!). No basting, no inevitable dry meat—no fuss at all. All you have to do is make an easy festive herb butter, wrap everything in foil, and let it roast unsupervised.

When you combine chili and mac and cheese, incredible things happen. We started with our preferred chili variation—white chicken chili, of course—and added in all the creamy, cheesy goodness of mac and cheese for the perfect weeknight dinner for indecisive families.

This recipe combines the rich, smoky flavors of traditional barbacoa with a cheesy topping and buttery biscuits for an irresistible meal. At the heart of this dish is succulent beef chuck roast, slow-cooked in a blend of fire-roasted tomatoes, chipotle chiles, and a medley of spices.

If you love stovetop mac 'n' cheese, you’ve got to try this creamy butternut squash orzo. Butternut squash and Parmesan join forces with orzo to create a cheesy pasta skillet that hits every nostalgic note.

The real magic in this sheet-pan dinner is in the savory-sweet, spoonable sauce that the onions transform into. Blending the chicken fat-roasted, charred onions with butter, sweet white vinegar, and crushed red pepper yields a creamy, umami-rich sauce that makes the chicken even more succulent.

If you’re looking for a vegetarian dinner centerpiece, then you can’t beat a whole roasted cauliflower. Gorgeously browned outside and meltingly tender in the middle, its stunningly simple goodness can’t be topped at any vegetarian Thanksgiving dinner.

Nothing satisfies a meat-and-potatoes craving quite like a classic, hearty shepherd’s pie. In this comfort food staple, ground beef is cooked with aromatics and red wine before being combined with corn and peas, all covered with velvety, rich mashed potatoes and topped with Parmesan before being broiled until golden brown.

Want taco night without the multiple pans and (semi-?) controlled chaos of a build-your-own dinner? Make this taco skillet. Saucy, spiced sweet potatoes and black beans are tossed with crushed potato chips and topped with melty cheese for a simple one-pan meal that will be your new weeknight go-to this November.

The heavy cream cuts the robust, earthy pumpkin in this soup, making it one of the most comforting soups for a chilly fall day. Load up on the garlic, cook the tomato paste to eliminate any rawness and release its sweet side, and get ready to thoroughly enjoy this pumpkin tortellini soup (in under 30 minutes!).

Broccoli cheddar soup is the ultimate comfort food—it’s rich, creamy, and packed with cheesy goodness—and we're taking it to the next level with pillowy gnocchi, making it heartier and more satisfying without any extra hassle. The gnocchi cooks right in the same pot, so you’re not stuck washing another dish.

This one-pot meal is a perfect way to warm up during the colder months, so go ahead and make it in the dead of winter to awaken your soul and grab a spoon to dig in! The best part? This hearty favorite is ready to enjoy in just 30 minutes, making it the perfect go-to weeknight dinner.
