Rihanna’s Son Riot Made His Magazine Cover Debut—and She Shared All 6 Versions

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Rihanna and A$AP Rocky’s three children are now all magazine cover stars. After their oldest, RZA, made his British Vogue cover debut in 2023 and youngest, Rocki, appeared on two covers this year, middle child Riot Rose is having his turn. The 3-year-old covered Arena Homme+ all on his own, though Rihanna joined him for one shot, wearing the red Loewe jumpsuit she had on when she revealed her pregnancy with him at the 2023 Super Bowl. There are six different versions of his cover, and his proud mother shared every image.

Last month, Rocky hinted at Riot’s cover in an interview with The Cut. He told the outlet, “One of my sons has a modeling gig in like three days, you know, the day before my birthday, and it’s—I’m so proud of him; this is his first magazine cover, so I think it’s just fascinating to see these little people grow into personalities of their own. It’s trippy, just in retrospect, thinking back when they were just so small and little. I mean, they’re still relatively little and small, but you know, they kind of speak up for themselves and have their own kind of swag and shit. It’s crazy.”

While RZA and Rocki posed with their parents, Riot’s cover was all about him. Rihanna teased he was a lot like her in her 2025 interview with Harper’s Bazaar.

“Riot, he’s just hilarious,” she said. “When he wakes up, he starts to squeal, scream. Not in a crying way. He just wants to sing. And I’m like, ‘Okay, here we go!’ He’s my alarm in the morning! He’s not taking no for an answer from anyone. I don’t know where he came from, dude.”

She added that Riot’s big brother, RZA, at first struggled with losing his only-child status “like all [new] siblings do, and at first Riot was understanding that his role was being the little brother. Now he knows he’s in charge.”
