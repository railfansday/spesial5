A Red Funnel Neck Jacket Will Upgrade Any Fall Look

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

At ELLE HQ, this sporty style has become somewhat of a staple.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

At ELLE HQ, red funnel neck jackets have become somewhat of a staple, styled with barrel-leg jeans and square-toed loafers one day, with a checked midi skirt and kitten heels the next. Or, when the dress code calls for something a little more elevated, a red funnel neck jacket even looks great when paired with tailored trousers and point-toe pumps.

While these jackets have been spotted at London Fashion Week, they’ve become adopted as a seasonal staple. Some of our favorite styles feature the trend-appropriate funnel necks, elasticated sleeve cuffs to keep out the brisk air, and a track-line design. Others are elevated, satiny, and super sleek. And all are in bold red, which is one of the leading colors of recent seasons. It’s a jacket that oozes sports-luxe, and it doesn’t have to cost a pretty penny, either.

So whether you’re sitting front row at LFW, doing the school run, or dressing up for a day at the office, there’s not much that this jacket can’t handle.
