The Fashion Lover’s 2026 IYKYK Gift Guide

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

It’s about that time again. For the fourth year running, we’re tackling gifts for one of the most challenging groups to shop for: fashion obsessives. We get it—and we speak from experience.

Here, we’ve compiled a list of “if you know, you know” fashion, beauty, and lifestyle gifts that won’t just satisfy, but impress the fashion lover in your life. Some are practical; others are simply beautiful; all are things Vogue editors can’t get enough of.

Below, Vogue’s 2026 IYKYK gift guide. Happy shopping!

It’s nearly impossible to get ahold of Emily Dawn Long’s small-batch “Spoiled Boys” T-shirts, but the downtown designer has plenty of colorful knit bucket hats to go around. One notable fan? Zoë Kravitz.

Speaking of Kravitz…she’s one of many celebrities who adore this cooler-than-cool sunglasses brand. (Other notable fans include Jennifer Lawrence, Kendall Jenner—oh—and Kravitz’s fiancé, Harry Styles.) While Thistles offers any shape your heart desires, we’re partial to the sporty Als.

Yes, this Vogue beauty editor favorite is a failsafe option for dry lips (especially in the winter!), but this is no medicinal lip balm. Le Chouchou’s broad color range will leave you with a lovely tint, from warm espresso to frosty Y2K pink.

This LA designer’s shoes are her claim to fame, spanning candy-colored pumps to unisex sneakers. But it’s her ultra-comfy, something-for-everyone penny loafers that earned Haller, a 2026 CFDA/Vogue Fashion Fund finalist, her IYKYK status. They were inspired by her grandfather—the ultimate influencer.

If you’re going the socks route, they might as well be cashmere—and from the buzziest collab of the season.

Add a touch of glamour to your dreary winter wardrobe, or throw it around your waist and try out the burgeoning cummerbund trend. Holiday party dressing just got a whole lot more interesting.

Whether they’re after an eclectic charm necklace à la the Celine runway, or they prefer a more curated jewelryscape, Celine’s novelty charms promise a dose of whimsy in any fashion lover’s wardrobe. Play it safe with their initial or swing for the fences with a petite artichoke, domino, or a trumpet—if you’re feeling crazy.

Former Vogue editor Laia Garcia-Furtado’s passion for Prada jumps off the pages of this illustrated guide to the Milanese label’s rise from fashion underdog to luxury behemoth. Equal parts charming and knowledgeable, this makes a great gift for any fashion obsessive.

For the biohacker who wishes their wearable tech wasn’t so…well, hideous.

The perfect stocking stuffer, this sheet mask is a sure-fire hit for all skin types, focusing on skin hydration and elasticity. Everyone can get behind that.

There’s nothing more thoughtful than a handwritten note, and there’s nothing chicer than some understated, customized stationery. Become that person who sends a handwritten thank-you note!

For the person who already has a red light mask, up the ante with this handheld, portable device. With hot and cold settings, this TheraBody wand tackles depuffing and dark circles.

Polish designers Karmena and Dominika of Forge Objects created something truly precious with this sterling silver fan inlaid with carnelian cabochons. It’s definitely not the fan you throw in your tote on a sweltering day—this is an heirloom in the making.

This New York brand isn’t new, but its spring 2027 show was an industry favorite. What better way to hop aboard the 6397 train than with a seasonally appropriate Fair Isle sweater?

Revisit the last decade in Grace Coddington’s storied career in fashion editorials, sketches, and commentary. Pairs well with Grace: A Memoir.

This is no classic gourmand. Notes of white chocolate, grapefruit, and cardamom are balanced out with warm sandalwood and amber, plus floral jasmine and lilies. A funky bottle doesn’t hurt either!
