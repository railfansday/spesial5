5 Fall Outfits I'm Stealing Straight From Taylor Swift

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Let's start with Swift's preferred autumnal color palette. She gravitates toward the season's rich jewel tones. Her style, which is often preppy or classic year-round with a vintage twist, includes paperboy hats, knee-high socks, colored tights, and plaid skirts. She's also embraced several of this season's trends, wearing wine-red hues, leather bomber jackets, and collegiate styles. So, it's safe to say both her current styling and vintage looks are encouraging me to upgrade my cold-weather fashion game. And while I am a fashion writer, you don't need to be a walking style encyclopedia—or global pop star at that—to master Swift's cold-weather ensembles.

Unlike the singer's glamorous red-carpet looks (I'm still not over that glittering ice-blue Dior gown from the 6th Annual Academy Museum Gala), her autumnal fashion is refreshingly down-to-earth, including attainable brands like Reformation and Anthropologie. Her ability to dress up simple staples like a black turtleneck with a few seasonal statement pieces has inspired how I think about pairing pieces in my own closet. Because her outfits are so normal, they're perfect for everything from a holiday party to a cozy date night. Ahead, I've rounded up my favorite Taylor Swift fall outfits of all time. You might as well hit play on Red while you're at it.

I’m still not over this preppy schoolboy-inspired look from 2023, which centers around a polo shirtdress and knee-high maple-hued boots. If it’s too chilly out to show your legs, simply slip on a pair of thermal tights. Keep the rest of the look simple with dainty jewelry. It’s one of those outfits that look effortlessly pieced together, hitting that sweet spot between casual and elevated.

Cynthia Rowley Cynthia Rowley Rugby Mini Dress

Creating an outfit that’s both cozy and chic enough for a dressy dinner can be tough—but this look from Swift is an easy solution. She layered a plush, oversized teddy coat over a simple, fitted long-sleeve top. Sheer tights under a plaid mini skirt dress the look up, while striking suede boots add more soft, autumnal texture.

Aritzia Original Contour Astral Longsleeve

For an elevated date night, I'd reach for a lace-trim slip dress. Swift made it cold-weather-approved with an oversized leather bomber jacket, which is one of this season's outerwear trends. While the pop singer opted for sandals, you can simply swap them out for black stiletto booties.

Charles & Keith Ally Ruched Chain-Handle Bag

Bordeaux red has become one of my favorite colors to style this season, and thanks to a co-sign from Swift, now I have even more inspiration. Swift’s towering cranberry heels serve as the visual focal point of this look, with a colorblocked polo and mixed-media pleated skirt from Thom Browne turning the outfit into a preppy maximalist’s dream.

You can take a break from the rich jewel tones every once in a while. Swift’s cloud-white ribbed mini dress is understated and sophisticated, with patent-leather accessories adding a subtle edge. Tie a bow around your ponytail for a girlish twist.
