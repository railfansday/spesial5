Been Looking Into Buying an LED Mask? This RENPHO Pick Is Cordless, Has Five Modes, and Is on Sale Ahead of Black Friday

A beauty splurge with an actually really good discount! Would you look at that!

If I had to put money on it, I’d bet that a good chunk of wishlists this year have some version of an LED mask on them. Luxury beauty tools and high-tech skincare devices have become the most wanted holiday gifts in recent years. But if you’ve been eyeing one for someone special (ahem, or for yourself), you know they’re not exactly budget-friendly.

That’s where Black Friday comes in. It’s one of the best times to score a deal on a high-end beauty device—which is probably why you clicked on this story. And good news: You’re right on time. The highly rated RENPHO Rejuven Artemis II Aër Red Light Therapy Mask is already discounted ahead of the big shopping event.

I’ve tried my fair share of LED masks, and one thing that immediately caught my eye about this one is its flexible silicone material, which contours to the curves of your face. There’s nothing I hate more than a mask that slips off every 10 seconds or feels way too big, so you have to constantly readjust it. Thankfully, this one is designed with comfort and fit in mind, thanks to its silicone eye shields and contoured, shapely nose design.

What really makes this mask stand out, though, is its five light therapy modes: Red + Infrared, Red + Blue, Red, Blue, and Infrared. Each mode targets different skin concerns, from smoothing and helping with mild acne to minimizing pores and giving the skin a plumper, brighter appearance. It can also help calm the skin barrier and it can give the skin a more thorough, deeper cleanse.

Another major perk? It's completely cordless, with the operating system built right into the mask. That means no bulky wires or being forced to be next to an outlet while you use it. With just the press of a button, you can easily switch between your preferred light therapy modes depending on what your skin needs. You can wear it while lounging around the house, cooking dinner, or even take it with you when you're on the go. And since it's already discounted ahead of Black Friday, I'd take advantage of the deal while it lasts!
