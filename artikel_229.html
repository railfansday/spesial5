Dig pulls off a perfect Parks and Rec reunion

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Peacock's Dig is just one Parks and Recreation reunion after another.

The archaeological comedy is co-created by Amy Poehler and Mike Schur, who both worked together on Parks and Recreation. Schur co-created that series with Greg Daniels, and Poehler led the show as waffle-loving civil servant Leslie Knope.

However, Poehler and Schur's collaboration isn't the only spark of Parks and Recreation magic you'll find in Dig. In a blink-and-you'll-miss-it moment, Dig also features a cameo from none other than Rashida Jones, who plays Leslie's best friend, nurse Ann Perkins, in Parks and Recreation.

Jones' appearance occurs in episode 5, when she pops up as a woman taking a photo of archaeologists Elise (Poehler) and Neville (Hugh Laurie) at a beachside restaurant. My Parks-loving self has never rewound a scene so fast to make sure my eyes weren't deceiving me.

According to Schur, the cameo came about due to proximity. Dig shot in Italy and Greece, and Jones is semi-permanently located in London. Schur, Poehler, and Jones, who have been friends since even before working together on Parks and Recreation, were communicating with one another during production.

"We were just texting with [Jones], and we were like, 'It's not that long a flight. What if you just hopped over here and hung out with us at this hotel we're staying at?'" Schur told Mashable over Zoom. "Then once she was there, it was a very quick jump to, 'You've got to just go into the show. You've got to pop in for a scene.'"

From there, Schur, Poehler, and Jones came up with the quick photo cameo.

"That's part of the fun of knowing people and working together for a really long time, is you do silly things like that," Schur recalled. "It was really fun, and she was totally game for it."

The highlight of the scene comes right after Jones exits, when Elise casually remarks, "She's very pretty."

For Parks and Recreation fans, the compliment instantly recalls Leslie's non-stop raves over Ann's beauty, whether it's calling her a "beautiful tropical fish" or a "poetic, noble land-mermaid." It's a sweet reminder of one of TV's most enduring female friendships, and it's right at home in Dig, which is full of sweet female friendships in its own right. Plus, according to Schur, the remark was completely improvised by Poehler.

"It was a very smart little mini gift to the folks who were fans of that show who will enjoy seeing them back together again," Schur said.
