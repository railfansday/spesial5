‘I had the courage to believe in Mississippi’ says Scott Colom after historic Senate win – US politics live

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Colom adds to blue wave of Democrat victories across the US and becomes Mississippi’s first black senator since 1875 after defeating Cindy Hyde-Smith

Hello, and welcome to the US politics live blog.

Mississippi has elected Scott Colom, a Democrat, as its senator, adding to a wave of Democratic gains across the country in this year’s midterm elections.

He defeated incumbent Republican Cindy Hyde-Smith in the closely contested Senate race to become the state’s first black senator since the post-Civil War Reconstruction era, marking a historic moment for Mississippi and a major breakthrough for the Democrats in a traditionally Republican state.

“All I did was have the courage to imagine a night like this,” Colom said in his victory speech last night.

“I had the courage to believe in Mississippi. I had the courage to understand that we can show the country that Mississippi matters.”

Just over a week since the midterm elections, the Associated Press called the race after absentee and provisional ballots were counted.

“While this is not the outcome we worked for or hoped for, I respect the decision of Mississippi voters,” Hyde-Smith said.
