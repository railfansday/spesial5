PFT's Week 11 2026 NFL power rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

1. Bills (last week No. 1; 8-1): For three straight weeks, a sluggish start turns into an easy win.

2. Jaguars (No. 2; 7-2): Parker Washington is playing his way into the franchise tag.

3. 49ers (No. 3; 8-1): The defense is going to need to perform better against the Vikings.

4. Broncos (No. 4; 7-2): The next time the Chargers play the Broncos, Sean Payton will tell Jim Harbaugh to stick his "loophole" up his poophole.

5. Bears (No. 5; 7-2): They can't afford to overlook the Saints, four days before a showdown with the Lions.

6. Chiefs (No. 6; 7-2): There's nothing wrong with Patrick Mahomes's elbow.

7. Bengals (No. 8; 7-2): Joe Burrow was right about feeling 2019 LSU vibes.

8. Seahawks (No. 9; 6-4): "Take your time, Sam."

9. Lions (No. 10; 7-2): Dealing with significant defensive injuries has become part of their normal.

10. Vikings (No. 12; 7-2): The "story being written" could lead to a Pulitzer Prize shaped like a Super Bowl trophy.

11. Steelers (No. 7; 6-3): They're not ready to compete with the best teams in the conference.

12. Rams (No. 15; 6-4): Can they keep winning without Puka Nacua and Davante Adams?

13. Packers (No. 11; 6-4): They desperately need a running game.

14. Browns (No. 16; 6-4): The playoffs are anything but a pipe dream.

15. Giants (No. 13; 5-3-1): They'll need the extra days to get ready for the Jaguars.

16. Patriots (No. 14; 5-5): It feels like the wheels are coming off.

18. Raiders (No. 17; 4-6): Fernando Mendoza looks the part, but they possibly could have beaten the Seahawks with Kirk Cousins.

19. Buccaneers (No. 18; 3-6): They could catch the Lions napping on Sunday.

20. Ravens (No. 19; 4-6): They won't have to worry about Lamar Jackson making it back for a playoff push.

21. Commanders (No. 26; 3-5-1): When the dust settles on the season, they could be the NFC East champions.

22. Texans (No. 20; 3-6): Last year, the defense was good enough to win games on its own. This year, the defense is bad enough to lose games on its own.

23. Saints (No. 21; 3-6): They need to stop sleepwalking through the first 30 minutes.

24. Cowboys (No. 25; 4-6): The defense has gotten worse, not better.

25. Cardinals (No. 23; 3-7): They should hire Rex Ryan solely for the entertainment value.

26. Falcons (No. 24; 3-7): How long until Andy Dalton gets a shot?

27. Colts (No. 29; 3-7): What else is on TV this Thursday night?

28. Eagles (No. 27; 2-7): The soft tank won't be hard to keep on track with the Steelers coming to town.

29. Chargers (No. 32; 1-8): "John Harbaugh says hello."

30. Titans (No. 28; 1-8): Cam Ward is giving them reason for hope in 2027.

31. Dolphins (No. 30; 1-8): Stephen Ross won't need to offer Jeff Hafley $100,000 for each loss.

32. Jets (No. 31; 2-8): Not even the Colts would hang a banner for "keeping it close in the first half."
