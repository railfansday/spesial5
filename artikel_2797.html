Jameson Williams nearly reaches 100 yards

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Detroit Lions WR Jameson Williams caught four passes for 93 yards and one touchdown against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers in Week 11. He was targeted four times during the game.

Why It Matters: Williams has caught at least four passes in four of his last five games and has posted four touchdowns during that span. He should see a decent amount of targets next week against the Chicago Bears and can be considered a No. 3 receiver in fantasy leagues.
