Mike Washington Jr. totals nine carries in Week 11

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Las Vegas Raiders running back Mike Washington Jr. ran nine times for 42 yards in Week 11 against the Denver Broncos, and he was not targeted in the passing game.

Why it Matters: Washington had been eating into Ashton Jeanty's workload lately, but Jeanty put him on the back burner with a gigantic stat line which saw him total 169 yards and a touchdown.
