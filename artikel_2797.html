St. John’s men’s basketball squeezes Syracuse behind second half defensive display

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

The entire first half of Sunday's game was tailor-made to get on the nerves of St. John's, whether it was Ryan Moesch dropped Ruben Prey with a crossover in cinematic fashion before sinking a three, the 11-2 run by the visitors fueled by careless Red Storm turnovers, or the thousands of traveling Syracuse fans making Madison Square Garden feel like the Carrier Dome JMA Wireless Dome as they watched their underdog Orange enter the halftime with the lead despite giving up 13 first-half turnovers.

St. John's could have lost their composure and let an upset bid become a reality, but they responded like a contender should in their first brush with adversity this season, flipping momentum with a suffocating lockdown performance in the second half to seal a 79-62 win and improve to 4-0.

After allowing Syracuse to shoot 58.3% in the first half, Red Storm made the right adjustments and shut down the Orange's offense after the break, sealing the paint and forcing ill-advised shots from the outside. Syracuse shot 11-of-33 in the second half and went 3-of-14 from deep. The Johnnies forced a season-high 21 turnovers over both halves, including nine by Ryan Moesch, and cashed in 32 points off those takeaways.

The Red Storm allowed a Sadiq White, Jr. layup out of the break, then went on a 16-0 run over the following seven minutes to take control with a 46-35 lead. Keyshawn Hall punctuated this spurt with a pull-up triple and he would finish the night with 18 points (8-of-18 from the field), a dozen of those points coming in the second half.

Syracuse later made one final run to cut the Red Storm lead down to seven points with 2:52 left, but the Red Storm salted away the win with clutch free throws and fastbreak buckets off after breaking the Orange's press.

Including Hall, all five Red Storm starters finished the night in double figures. Tounde Yessoufou led the Johnnies with a 19-point, 14-rebound double-double, the second of his college career and his first in a St. John's uniform.

Sunday's victory marks the fifth consecutive time St. John's has defeated Syracuse, their longest winning streak in the series history since 1969-70.

Before they head westward for the Players Era Festival, St. John's will take on Gerry McNamara's former team, Siena, in their last tune-up on Wednesday, November 18. Tip-off from Carnesecca Arena is scheduled for 7:00 p.m. For fans eager to finally see the Red Storm on television this year, the game will air on truTV.
