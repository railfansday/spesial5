A short week, a long injury list, but plenty of good news for Buffalo

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Well, it's Day 1 all over again. With a Thursday night date with the Kansas City Chiefs, the Bills have no time to waste, so getting back to work on Monday was the priority. But rest and recovery are a close second on any NFL team's priority list, so practice was a walkthrough. That means this list is just a best-guess by the training staff.

*Oliver wasn't on last week's daily reports but did report soreness on Saturday and was added to the list at that time.

The good news in this group is that most of these would have participated even if it had been a full practice day. Benford is the only player listed as a DNP at all. Williams is recovering as is Kincaid.

*Brown was listed with a back injury earlier this season, but that injury had been off the list for the past two weeks. Now it has returned.

Hamlin added a neck injury to his toe injury, but it's unclear whether his predicted limited status was due to one, the other, or both. Rousseau and Torrence are new injuries (Torrence was absent for personal reasons one day last week, but no injury reported). It's a good sign that they, too, would have participated in some form if today had been Wednesday.

Moore caused us all to hold our breath and roll our eyes late in the second quarter. After needing help off the field, the wide receiver headed to the locker room – under his own power – a few minutes before halftime with the Bills listing him as questionable with a knee injury. He returned to play in the second half. Not being on the list – with either the shoulder injury he's been dealing with since Week 2 or a knee injury – is a great sign for the Bills' offense.
