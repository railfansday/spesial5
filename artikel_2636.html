'I'll actually be surprised if they don't vote them out': Brian Kelly expects SEC to boot LSU next week

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

LSU parted ways with Brian Kelly in controversial fashion last fall, and Kelly has warned Lane Kiffin, his successor as LSU head football coach, of the lofty expectations associated with Death Valley.

"You gotta win a championship," Kelly said in September.

On Monday, the SEC released a statement announcing plans for the conference's presidents and chancellors to "deliberate LSU's ongoing membership in the Conference" on Thursday, Dec. 3.

This came after months of consternation over LSU's insistence on adding ex-professional athletes to Kiffin's football team and the men's basketball team.

While it's possible the SEC is simply calling LSU's bluff, Kelly believes LSU's SEC days are over.

"This has gotten to the point where they will have a meeting on Dec. 3," Kelly said on SiriusXM's The Early Read with Danny Kanell and Roy Philpott. "The presidents will vote, and I'll actually be surprised if they don't vote them out."

After a beat of heavy silence, Philpott jumped in with an earnest "wow."

"Now, look, they've asked them to come and meet them halfway a couple of times," Kelly said. "I think the [RJ] Luis [Jr.] situation and playing him in the game went against any good faith that they had. And I think this is gonna go right down to a vote, and I think college football — college athletics — will all have to take notice of where we are and where we want to go."

Luis previously played at St. John's for the 2023-24 and 2024-25 men's college basketball seasons before declaring for the 2025 NBA draft. He signed two-way contracts with the Boston Celtics and Utah Jazz, but never appeared in an NBA regular-season game. Luis received a preliminary injunction awarding him eligibility to play for LSU.

Luis played 15 minutes in LSU's win over UL Monroe on Nov. 18, but the following day, ESPN's Jeff Borzello reported that LSU president Wade Rousse ordered Will Wade, the head men's basketball coach, not to play Luis. He is now off the roster, and he announced Tuesday that he will sue SEC commissioner Greg Sankey for damages, per ESPN's Myron Medcalf.
