Cade Cunningham makes a bold claim after leading the Pistons in snapping the Spurs' streak

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Heading into Tuesday's game between the Detroit Pistons and the San Antonio Spurs, the latter were the overwhelming favorites. The Pistons faced a tough game, especially without Duncan Robinson.

However, the Pistons shocked the world by taking control and winning. They snapped the Spurs' 12-game winning streak, and Cade Cunningham was at the center of it all.

As one of the best point guards in the NBA, Cunningham has proven to be a fantastic player for the Pistons. He has been their leader for a while now, and he is delivering on his potential from being drafted first overall in 2021.

Since then, the Pistons have gone through all kinds of struggles, but also some solid successes. They were the first seed in the Eastern Conference in the 2025-26 season, but their early exit from the Playoffs has led to doubts. With this performance against the Spurs, the doubters were silenced.

MORE: Cade Cunningham is calling out the people who doubted him and the Pistons

Cunningham has affirmed that the Pistons are ready for the team to hit another level. They are ready to prove that they are still one of the best teams in the league, and they are ready to go toe-to-toe with the top teams like the Spurs.

"It was a group effort; everybody had to bring it. I like my guys; we do this together every day. We love basketball; we all come from grit, to get to this point... Nobody in that locker room has fear in their heart for anybody," Cunningham said on the NBC broadcast after the big win.

Winning against a team like the Spurs is a big achievement, and Cunningham was key to that. However, he is the first one to credit his team for helping him beat the Spurs, one of the toughest tasks any team has ths season.
