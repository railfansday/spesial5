Chiefs React to NFL’s Nick Bolton Community MVP Announcement

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Nick Bolton has been a steady force for the Kansas City Chiefs since 2021. The linebacker is one of his unit's leaders and also serves as a captain who has the franchise's best interest in mind all the time. He has made his presence felt on the field, as well as off of it with his community work. The team reacted to an announcement involving him on Friday.

Bolton entered the NFL as Kansas City's No. 58 overall pick in the second round five years ago. The Texas native starred for the Missouri Tigers in college, earning second-team All-American and first-team All-SEC honors in his final season with the program.

He has since won two Super Bowls with the Chiefs. Bolton continues to play well for the Chiefs and is a silent but effective contributor to their success. They are 6-2 this season and are coming off a 35-12 win over the New York Jets in Week 9.

Kansas City Chiefs linebacker Nick Bolton (32) against the Philadelphia Eagles in Super Bowl LIX at Caesars Superdome.

The NFLPA announced on Friday that Nick Bolton is the Week 10 Community MVP. His foundation will receive a big donation to help continue its efforts in affecting people's lives positively.

"Our Week 10 NFLPA #CommunityMVP @32.bolton showed up big for the kids of Kansas City

," the NFLPA wrote. "In honor of being named this week's #CommunityMVP, the NFLPA is donating $10,000 to the Bolton Empowerment Foundation. You can also support this charitable cause through our supporting partner @gofundme by using the link in our story."

Kansas City dropped a three-word reaction in the comments section.

Bolton and the Chiefs will play their next game on Sunday. They will clash with the Atlanta Falcons at Mercedes-Benz Stadium with a chance to start another winning streak. The team started the campaign 5-0 but endured a two-game skid before bouncing back strong last week. A home game follows in Week 11 versus the Arizona Cardinals, but will then embark on a three-game road trip.
