Charlotte Hornets jersey history No. 9 Mangok Mathiang 2017-2018

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

After going undrafted in the 2017 NBA Draft, the Charlotte Hornets signed Australian center Mangok Mathiang to a two-way deal after performing in the NBA Summer League. Mathiang didn't get many opportunities for Charlotte at the NBA level, appearing in just four contests. In those games, the Louisville product averaged two points, 2.5 rebounds and 0.3 steals in five minutes per game.

In 43 games with the Greensboro Swarm, Mathiang nearly averaged a double-double, tallying 10.8 points, 9.2 rebounds, 1.4 blocks and 1.1 assists. He was later waived by the Hornets and would never return to the NBA. Following his time with Charlotte, Mathiang pursued a career overseas, where he currently plays today with the Adelaide 36ers of the NBL.
