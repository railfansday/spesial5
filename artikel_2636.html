Colts-Texans draws tepid 12.23 million on Thursday night

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

NFL viewership is driven largely by quality of matchup. The Thursday night matchup that launched Week 11 may have driven some viewers to examine their life choices.

Still, 12.23 million watched the game between the Colts and Texans, on average. It was the smallest Thursday night audience of the season, and it represented a nine-percent drop from last year’s Week 11 Thursday night game between the Jets and Patriots.

The game also faced competition from the release of the new Grand Theft Auto video game.

The latest game dragged the full-season average under 17 million for the first time this year. It now stands at 16.62 million per game.

The Prime Video schedule concludes with Broncos at Steelers on Black Friday, Chiefs at Rams on December 3, Vikings at Patriots on December 10, 49ers at Chargers on December 17, Texans at Eagles on December 24, and Ravens at Bengals on December 31.

Flexing is an option for some of those contests. The window opens in Week 13, with the move “generally” coming no later than 21 days before the date of the game.

At this point, Vikings-Patriots is only 17 days away. Flexing remains possible for the final three games — with 49ers-Chargers possibly on the brink of being moved. The Week 15 Sunday schedule otherwise includes Bengals-Panthers, Browns-Giants, and Cowboys-Rams.
