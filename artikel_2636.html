Florida football bowl projections: Where UF could play this postseason

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Florida football coach Jon Sumrall wouldn't mind staying in state this postseason,

The No. 20 Florida Gators (7-4, 5-4 SEC) are bowl eligible in Sumrall's first season and are generating some late-season momentum after back-to-back wins over Kentucky and Vanderbilt.

Representatives from the Citrus Bowl in Orlando attended UF's postgame press conference following its 34-27 win over Vanderbilt on November 21 at The Swamp.

"I'd love to be in state for a lot of reasons," Sumrall said. "But I don't spend a whole lot of my time worried about the selection process. I just want to try and win one more game, and then whatever game we put us in, we'll try to win one more game after that. But being in the state of Florida football game would be great. It would beat maybe having to go somewhere way up north that's cold."

Florida is 25-24 all-time in bowl games. In their last bowl trip, the Gators beat Tulane 33-8 in the Gasparilla Bowl on December 20, 2024.

Here's a look at which bowl UF could play in this postseason:

CBS Sports projects Florida to play in the Taxslayer Gator Bowl in Jacksonville on December 30, hosting Clemson (11:30 a.m. ET kick). The game would pit Sumrall against veteran Clemson coach Dabo Swinney, who led the Tigers to national championships in 2016 and 2018.

Florida is 9-3-1 all-time against Clemson but the two schools haven't played since 1961, when the Gators beat the Tigers 21-17 in Gainesville. The game would be played in front of reduced capacity at EverBank Stadium in Jacksonville (42,000) due to renovations going on at the stadium for the Jacksonville Jaguars.

Clemson (7-4) is coming off a 20-10 win at Duke on November 20 but also lost to LSU (51-10).

ESPN's Mark Schlabach and Kyle Bonagura both predict Florida to play in the Taxslayer Gator Bowl in Jacksonville against SMU.

The game would pit Sumrall against SMU coach Rhett Lashlee, who was a former offensive coordinator at Miami (2020-21) and Auburn (2013-16).

USA Today predicts that Florida will play in the TaxSlayer Gator Bowl facing Louisville. The game would pit Florida coach Jon Sumrall against Louisville coach Jeff Brohm, who was a reported candidate for the UF job.

A former Louisville quarterback, Brohm has gone 36-15 at Louisville and 102-59 overall in prior stints at Purdue (2017-22) and Western Kentucky (2014-16). Louisville's offense this season ranks third in the ACC at 462.8 yards per game.

Brett McMurphy of On3 projects Florida to play Ohio State in the ReliaQuest Bowl in Tampa on December 31 (noon kickoff).

It would pit Florida coach Jon Sumrall against Ohio State coach Ryan Day, who began his coaching career as a graduate assistant at Florida under Urban Meyer in 2005.

Florida is 2-0 all-time against the Buckeyes, who have stumbled to 7-4 this season after an injury to starting quarterback Julian Sayin. In the last meeting between the two schools, the Gators beat the Buckeyes 24-17 in the Gator Bowl on Jan. 2, 2012.
