LSU women's basketball misses out on five-star prospect

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

LSU women's basketball lost out on another five-star prospect. Ivanna Wilson-Manyacka committed to UCLA, according to a report from Rivals' Talia Goodman last week.

Head coach Kim Mulkey and her staff hosted Wilson-Manyacka and fellow five-star prospect GG Banks on campus at the beginning of the fall. The Tigers lost on both top five recruits as Banks, the cycle's top combo guard, chose South Carolina in October.

Rivals' Industry Rankings put Wilson-Manyacka at No. 2 overall in the 2027 class. She is the cycle's top small forward at six-foot-one and the best player out of Maryland. She plays high school basketball at the Bullis School.

The Tigers made Wilson-Manyacka a priority and they ended up in her top five. Her commitment to the Bruins gives the reigning national champions the No. 2 group in the 2027 cycle. LSU currently holds a commitment from one top 10 player in center Caroline Bradley.
