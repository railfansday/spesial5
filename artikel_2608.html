DeVonta Smith back injury: Latest updates on Eagles WR’s status in Thanksgiving game

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The hits keep on coming for the Philadelphia Eagles offense. Midway through the fourth quarter, with the Eagles making a run at the Dallas Cowboys, Joe Milton III launched a deep ball to star wide receiver DeVonta Smith, who made an impressive play on the ball.

Smith came down directly on his back and coughed up the ball, and remained on the turf in obvious pain as the medical staff provided treatment. After slowly making his way to the sideline, Smith was taken into the blue medical tent.

The injury came in the same game where the Eagles watched starting running back Saquon Barkley leave early with an injury, while starting tight end Dallas Goedert suffered an injury of his own.

Luckily for the Eagles' offense, Smith returned to the game a few plays later, but Milton threw an interception to abruptly end the drive.

We will continue to monitor the situation as the game goes on and provide the latest information on Smith's status.

This is a developing story. More information will be provided as it becomes available.
