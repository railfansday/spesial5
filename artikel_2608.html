Lions create practice squad spot, set stage for Wednesday roster moves

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

The Detroit Lions announced on Tuesday that they've released defensive lineman Ben Stille from the practice squad, creating a vacancy on the 17-man squad ahead of their week of preparation for the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

Stille, a 29-year-old veteran defensive lineman, was a standout during training camp this offseason, but he failed to make the 53-man roster. He was quickly signed to the practice squad and got the call-up for Detroit's Week 1 game, playing just nine defensive snaps.

This move sets the stage for what will likely be a few roster moves on Wednesday. How the Lions will fill the practice squad spot remains to be seen, but it is certainly worth noting that on Monday, the Lions waived cornerback Nick Whiteside to make room for waiver claim DE Tyrus Wheat. On Tuesday, Whiteside cleared waivers, so if Detroit wanted to bring Whiteside back, there's now a spot on the practice squad for that. Whiteside spent two months on the Lions' practice squad earlier this year.

On top of that, the Lions are expected to activate safety Kerby Joseph from the Physically Unable to Perform list to the 53-man roster on Wednesday, the final day of Joseph's 21-day evaluation window. If the Lions don't activate Joseph, he would be shut down for the year, but on Tuesday, coach Dan Campbell indicated that Joseph will not only be activated, but they expect him to have a significant role in Sunday's game against the Buccaneers.

"The plan is to get him going. I think we're going to let him play," Campbell said.

It's not clear how the Lions will create the roster spot for Joseph, but the easiest way would be to place Aidan Hutchinson on injured reserve after he suffered a torn ACL on Sunday.
