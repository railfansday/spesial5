Who stood out for Auburn football against Mississippi State? Advanced stats, snap counts

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

The third time was the charm for Auburn football, which finally delivered a commanding performance against a struggling SEC squad after failing to do so twice over earlier this season.

Auburn had its bowl-clinching victory sealed early, manhandling Mississippi State en route to a 41-9 win on Saturday, Nov. 14, at Davis Wade Stadium. The Bulldogs were the final team on Auburn's schedule that sat below it in the SEC standings. The Tigers failed to blow out their other two lesser league foes, though they beat both Arkansas and Vanderbilt, albeit in one-score contests.
