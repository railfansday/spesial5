Games Workshop always rolls out a battalion of Christmas Warhammer Battleforces each year, and they're exceptionally good value... so it's no surprise they sell out fast. However, it's still possible to find most of them online at a healthy 20% discount if you know where to look.

These are rapidly running out of stock at various retailers, so let's cut to the chase; I've tracked down the 2026 Christmas Warhammer Battleforces wherever they can be found for a reduced price. Because these boxes were good enough value as is (they're incredibly decent deals), being able to save an extra £30 or so on the sticker price is worthy of the best Black Friday Warhammer deals.

I've rounded any and all offers I could find below, including a value breakdown of each pack. Just be quick, because some of these have sold out at competitors as well as the Warhammer store itself.
