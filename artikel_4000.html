Nioh 3 Comes To Xbox In January, Second Story DLC Launches Next Month

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

The Nioh series celebrates its 10th anniversary next February, and developer Team Ninja is celebrating with a pair of announcements for its latest entry, Nioh 3. First, the game's second story expansion, Bloody Insurrection, launches next month. Second, the game is coming to Xbox in January, marking the franchise's debut on Microsoft's platform.

Bloody Insurrection is Nioh 3's next story expansion following Hell Rising, which launched in August, and is set to release on December 18. Set in Shimabara in 1638, players will be at the center of a conflict between rebel forces, alchemists, and yokai while encountering familiar faces from previous Nioh games. Returning characters include Tachibana Muneshige from the original Nioh, and Bloody Insurrection will serve as the conclusion of protagonist Takechiyo's story.

Bloody Insurrection also introduces new Martial Arts for each weapon type, new Transformed Arts, Ninjutsu, Onmyo Magic, and Summoning Seals, and a new difficulty mode called Nioh's Journey. This new difficulty option is only available if you've completed the Enlightened One's Journey third playthrough and will allow players to find Ethereal equipment drops that let them acquire the highest rarity items in the game.

Additionally, Bloody Insurrection adds new endgame content called the Battlefield of Carnage. This challenges players with climbing floors, i.e., battlefields, filled with enemies and a boss. Each floor is divided into multiple battlefields, and defeating the floor's boss grants helpful battlefield effects, which can be combined to create unique synergies as you climb higher. Defeating enemies also lets players enhance special equipment "instilled with traces of the Yokai Realm," according to Koei Tecmo, which can only be found in Battlefield of Carnage.

A new edition of the game called Nioh 3: Complete Edition arrives on January 28 and will include the base game and its two story expansions. Complete Edition will arrive on PlayStation 5, PC, and, for the first time, Xbox Series X/S. The base version of Nioh 3 is also coming to Xbox Series X/S on the same date. The original version of Nioh 3 is also on sale at a 50 percent discount on PlayStation and Steam, and the season pass is 25 percent off.
