Mouse P.I. For Hire Teases DLC Announcement, Taking Place Next Week

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A lot of developers have decided to tease announcements in the lead up to certain end-of-year events and celebrations, and the latest one comes from the Mouse P.I. For Hire team.

In a quick post the game's social media page, the team has confirmed a DLC announcement is happening next week. You can see the outlines of some familiar characters, and that's it!

"MOUSE: P.I. For Hire. DLC. Announcement next week."

Developer Fumi and publisher PlaySide launched Mouse P.I. For Hire on the Switch 2 in April 2026. We said it was a "bold, risk-taking FPS", awarding it an "excellent" 9/10 stars. Since then, it's received multiple patches and surpassed one million players.

There was also a pretty massive update adding a 40fps mode and uncapped Variable Refresh Rate on the Switch 2.
