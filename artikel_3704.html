Nioh 3 DLC ‘Bloody Insurrection’ launches December 17; Complete Edition launches January 28, 2027 alongside Xbox Series version

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Nioh 3 downloadable content “Bloody Insurrection” will launch on December 17, and a Nioh 3 Complete Edition featuring the base game and both downloadable contents will launch for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC via Steam and Microsoft Store on January 28, 2027, publisher Koei Tecmo and developer Team NINJA announced.

Nioh 3 first launched for PlayStation 5 and PC via Steam on February 6.

Here is an overview of the downloadable content and Complete Edition:

In Bloody Insurrection, a Shogun Meets His End in Shimabara

While pursuing the Spanish alchemists, Takechiyo learns that the source of Yui Shosetsu’s regrets lie in the Shimabara of the past. Guided by Shirogarasu, they transcend time once again, this time arriving in Shimabara in 1638. There, a battlefield where rebel forces, alchemists, and yokai join forces to wage a fierce and bloody struggle against the shogunate army. To uncover the source of the conspiracy, Takechiyo ventures deeper into the heart of Shimabara, encountering a host of new figures along the way, including Amakusa Shiro, leader of the rebels; Tachibana Muneshige, a legendary warlord who survived the Warring States period; and a samurai who fights back against the alchemists.

Shimabara in 1638. Rebels have risen up against the tyranny of a local warlord and now clash against the shogunate forces sent to suppress them. Alchemists operating in the shadows and countless yokai join the fray, turning the conflict into a brutal, blood-soaked war. Takechiyo must navigate the various landscapes of Shimabara in the battle-fraught castle town, the vast underground caverns beneath Mount Unzen, and the rebel stronghold in Hara Castle.

Fierce Battles Against Storied Characters and New Yokai

Takechiyo encounters Amakusa Shiro, the leader of the rebel forces, along with the legendary warrior Tachibana Muneshige, and the samurai who fights against the alchemists. Figures from Hell Rising make a comeback in this new story, and new yokai stand in Takechiyo’s way. There are also some familiar faces among the Guardian Spirits who lend Takechiyo their power.

New Customization Elements and Endgame Content

New Martial Arts are being added for each weapon type, along with numerous Transformed Arts, Ninjutsu, Onmyo Magic, and Summoning Seals. If players have both the first and second downloadable content and manage to complete Enlightened One’s Journey (the third playthrough), they will be able to unlock the new difficulty mode and fourth playthrough, Nioh‘s Journey, where players will find equipment drops of the highest rarity, Ethereal. This will take character customization and endgame challenges to the limit.

A high-volume form of endgame content, the Battlefield of Carnage, awaits players in this downloadable content. Players will need to fight their way through battlefields teeming with enemies, defeat the boss of each floor, and press onward through a relentless series of battles.

Each floor of the Battlefield of Carnage consists of a field divided into multiple war fronts. Defeat the boss controlling each war front to gain battlefield effects that provides an advantage on each floor.

Battlefield effects include steady stat boosts and other special effects. Depending on the player’s character customization, these special effects can create unexpected synergies and enable them to deliver overwhelming performances. Players are free to choose the order in which they conquer the war fronts and when to challenge the floor boss. The map provides information on the types of war front bosses and details about battlefield effects, so the player will need to devise a strategy best suited to their playstyle and character.

In addition, defeating formidable enemies on the battlefield will enable players to further strengthen equipment shrouded in traces of the Yokai Realm, which can only be obtained and generated in the Battlefield of Carnage.

Defeat the floor boss, purify the Yokai Realm, and seek out the most powerful equipment you can find. The ultimate challenge awaits, offering the hard-earned satisfaction that can only be found within Nioh.

New Guardian Spirits, skills, Martial Arts, Ninjutsu, Onmyo Magic, Graces, and more.

An edition that includes the base game, as well as downloadable content #1, “Hell Rising,” and downloadable content #2, “Bloody Insurrection,” which feature new stories, enemies, yokai, bosses, and equipment. The Complete Edition also includes the in-game item bonus previously offered with the Digital Deluxe Edition, the set of 14 Infernal Weapons, along with the purchase bonuses Hellfire Equipment Set and Scampuss Furball Netsuke Charm.
