BlueCat Networks: Senior Software Engineer - Advanced DDI

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

About the role:As a Senior Software Engineer on BlueCat’s Advanced DDI team, you’ll design, build and run core services of the BlueCat Cloud Platform. This platform delivers enterprise-grade DNS, DHCP and IPAM (DDI) to customers around the world. Our services are built in Rust and Go and run on Kubernetes. You’ll own significant pieces of the system end to end, write production code every day, and help raise the quality of the team’s work, on systems where safety, reliability and performance matter most. You’ll work closely with product, security, SRE and infrastructure partners, and you’ll help shape how our team uses AI-assisted development.

We welcome engineers early in their senior career as well as those with deep experience. If you’ve been owning features and services and want to grow your scope, we’d like to hear from you.

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
