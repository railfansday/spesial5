Ripple’s latest banking partnership opens another route to XRP adoption in Turkey

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Yapı Kredi is building its own crypto trading platform, while existing Ripple partner Garanti BBVA already supports XRP trading and withdrawals.

Ripple has partnered with Turkish banking giant Yapı Kredi to provide digital asset custody infrastructure for its cryptocurrency business.

In a Nov. 25 statement, Yapı Kredi said it has deployed Ripple Custody within its own infrastructure to safeguard digital assets held through Yapı Kredi Kripto, its cryptocurrency trading platform.

The partnership expands Ripple's presence in Türkiye, where financial institutions are increasingly developing regulated digital asset services.

Yapı Kredi is one of the country's largest private banks, with total assets of 4.4 trillion Turkish lira as of September. The lender operates 723 branches nationwide and employs more than 14,000 people.

The bank already facilitates round-the-clock lira transfers between its customers' bank accounts and cryptocurrency exchanges BtcTurk and Paribu.

Ripple disclosed an on-site custody deployment at Yapı Kredi. Garanti BBVA Kripto publishes active XRP trading and withdrawal statuses.

Customers need a lira funding route, an XRP buy option and a way to move XRP off the platform.

In October, Yapı Kredi Kripto selected financial technology provider Integral to support digital asset trading, complementing the custody infrastructure it has now secured through Ripple.

Eylem Karaca, an executive at Yapı Kredi Technology, said:

“As digital assets become an increasingly important part of the financial ecosystem, we're focused on building the secure, reliable infrastructure needed to support this evolution.”

The partnership comes as Türkiye's regulatory framework creates commercial opportunities for institutional cryptocurrency custody providers.

Under rules introduced by the country's Capital Markets Board, cryptocurrency trading platforms must hold at least 95% of customer digital assets with authorized custodians.

Ripple said it is the only custody technology provider working with Türkiye's major licensed bank-based crypto custodians, positioning the company to benefit from traditional financial institutions entering the market.

Türkiye ranks among the world's ten largest retail cryptocurrency markets, according to TRM Labs, with demand supported by persistent lira depreciation and interest in alternative savings instruments.

Ripple also identified XRP as one of the country's most widely held digital assets.

The company previously partnered with Garanti BBVA Kripto and IBM in October 2024 to strengthen the custody infrastructure supporting the bank-backed cryptocurrency platform.

Garanti BBVA Kripto served more than 14,000 users at the time and offered services involving Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, and several other digital assets.

The platform now supports direct XRP trading against the Turkish lira, alongside XRP deposits and withdrawals.

Yapı Kredi has yet to disclose its retail launch date or the cryptocurrencies it supports. A similar offering would give Turkish investors another bank-backed channel to buy and hold XRP.
