Start your stock research for free. Read companies’ filings on the Securities and Exchange Commission’s EDGAR database, and check quotes, news, and financials on Yahoo Finance. Pay for a research site only when you’ll use what it adds, such as Seeking Alpha’s analysis, Morningstar’s fund ratings, or Stock Rover’s screening tools.

U.S. public companies must file annual and quarterly reports with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), so their financial statements are public, free to read on EDGAR, the SEC’s filing database. Each site below is our pick for one kind of research, judged by what its free plan offers, what a paid plan adds, and what that plan costs a year.
