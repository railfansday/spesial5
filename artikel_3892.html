'Move Aside, Mandy': GTA 6's Goth Girl Vicki Is PS5 Players' New Fave

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Here's why her real-life actress may sound familiar

As GTA 6 players progress through the plot and explore beyond the sunny shores of Key Lento, bunny boiler Mandy has been left behind.

It’s all about Delights’ resident goth girl Vicki now!

The character plays a bit-part during Chapter 2, and she’s quickly become a fave among PS5 players for her husky voice and no-nonsense attitude.

Writing on X, one fan put it simply: “Oh yeah we on Vicki now, I don’t even know who Mandy is!”

Some have even taken to social media stalking the dancer on Snapmatic:

Simping aside, Vicki may actually sound familiar because her voice actor is Taryn Manning, who had a pretty major role as Tiffany ‘Pennsatucky’ Doggett in Orange Is the New Black.

The 48-year-old actress has also starred in a variety of other shows like Sons of Anarchy, and has even had parts in movies like Hustle & Flow and the 8-Mile.

There are a lot of characters to keep track of in GTA 6, but Vicki is definitely one that stuck with me, largely because she’s pivotal to one of my favourite missions in the game.
