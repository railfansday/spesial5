Dermot Gallagher reviews controversial decisions in the Premier League after each round of games, on behalf of Sky Sports.

The former top referee has now given his call on these incidents in the Newcastle United v Arsenal match.

Dermot Gallagher looking at what happened during key moments in this match at St James’ Park.

The two key moments both occurring during the second half.

So what about these ‘controversial’ incidents…

Dermot Gallagher talking to Sky Sports about these Newcastle United v Arsenal controversial incidents:

Harvey Barnes equalised for Newcastle against Arsenal following an error from Martin Odegaard, who was caught by Joe Willock after making the mistake.

“Calafiori is closest to him and he doesn’t think it’s a foul. He is chasing the ball.”

“Very shocked if it had been ruled out. Nothing wrong with that.”

“No problems at all. I thought, knowing his quality, I thought he would chest it down. You cannot give a foul for that. It is so soft.”

Joe Willock, Gabriel and David Raya bundle over inside the penalty area as Malick Thiaw pokes Newcastle United ahead.

“If you look, Gabriel has pinned Willock himself. It’s Gabriel who just falls into Raya.”

“I can’t argue with that, it’s the correct decision. If you’re giving fouls for that, it would be disgraceful.”

“Great decision. Willock knows what he’s doing.”

In reality, I don’t think there was anything ‘controversial’ about either incident.

There would have been if either goal had been disallowed!

As Dermot Gallagher and his mates all say, nothing wrong with either United goal.
