No, REI Doesn’t Have a Black Friday Sale. This Week’s Sale Is the Next Best Thing Though.

EVERY HOLIDAY SEASON for the past decade, REI has shut its doors and ceased online order processing on Black Friday. Instead of holding a Black Friday sale, the outdoor retailer encourages customers (and its employees) to #OptOutside by getting in nature and on an adventure. This Black Friday marks 11 years of that. But, the good thing for you and your holiday shopping is that REI still holds a Holiday Sale, which is about to start on November 13 and runs through November 23.

But if you can’t wait until that starts, we decided to sort through REI’s deal section to offer up some current editor favorites on sale. I’ll make sure to update this post when the Holiday Sale launches on November 13, and you can check out a preview of that here.

An easy deal to scoop up around the holidays is Yeti drinkware—makes for a great gift, and you probably have a mug around the house or office that needs replacing as well. This Stackable Rambler (in the ridgeline color shown) is 20 percent off and will get the job done this winter.

When it comes to trail runners, two of my favorites in recent times have been the Keen Seek and the Arc’teryx Norvan LD4. The Seek is Keen’s very first trail running shoe, and it was a surprise gem of 2025. The upper collar provides a comfortable, secure fit and the midsole foam and less aggressive lugs make it a nice gravel option. I’ve still got my original pair in the rotation after roughly 115 miles on them—29 percent off is a solid price. And the Norvan LD4 is another one of my favorites from 2025. Very stable and a tongue/collar that’s one of the more comfortable sock-like fits I’ve worn—feels like it’s just a thin bit of material and nothing more.

A few other standout deals right now are the Vuori Sunday Performance Joggers (under $90 in sable heather color), 30 percent off Patagonia’s Houdini Stash Half-Zip, and just $90 for the Gregory Arrio 24 Pack (a long-time MH editor daypack favorite). Almost everyone who loves the outdoors knows that REI is ground zero for gearing up for hiking, biking, kayaking, climbing, fishing—pretty much any type of adventure, really. REI Outlet is the retailer's dedicated discount section, a roundup of last season's gear and its famous flash sales. It's the single best place to shop if you're looking for the absolute best deals on the world's best outdoor brands. You'll find great deals year-round, but the deepest discounts are almost always during REI's holiday sale events. Don't forget to check back early and often as REI Outlet's inventory changes frequently. Sale prices can drop suddenly and things sell out fast!

REI offers many of its best perks and sale event access to REI Co-op members. Even if you're not a frequent REI shopper, it's worth the one-time $30 fee to join. For the Holiday Sale, REI members get a 20% off one eligible full-priced item and an extra 20% off one eligible Outlet item with the code HOLIDAY2026. That's not all though.

REI members also receive a 10% dividend on most in-store and online purchases, as well as receiving discounts on gear rentals and during REI's best annual sale events (like the holiday sale). That $30 can easily pay for itself in just one visit!
