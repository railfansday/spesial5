Elon Musk: &#8216;I Feel Pretty Foolish&#8217; Spending Record $445 Million on Midterm Elections

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Elon Musk regrets spending so much money during the U.S. midterm elections.

The billionaire SpaceX and Tesla CEO shelled out at least $445 million in support of Republican candidates and conservative PACs, according to data monitored by campaign finance watchdog OpenSecrets.

“Probably, if I could rewind it back, I would have spent less money,” he said in an interview Wednesday with The New York Times’ Andrew Ross Sorkin. Musk had dialed in to the Times’ DealBook Summit from China, where he is traveling with the Trump administration for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Economic Leaders’ Meeting.

He said his “motivations were very simple” in the decision to spend that sum, “which is just to sort of to have… either the House, Senate, or presidency be Republican in ‘29, and not have… in the worst case scenario, a supermajority of of Democrats.”

Kendra Barnett is Adweek's senior tech reporter.
