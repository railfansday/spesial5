IShowSpeed, who’s #7 this week, also got a GTA VI boost. As one of YouTube’s top streamers and a video game enthusiast, he was basically 100% guaranteed to pivot his content and head to Vice City as soon as the game dropped.

But he’s also doing a lot outside of GTA.

2026 has been quite the year for Speed. Among recent accomplishments, he dropped an expansive, award-winning partnership with Expedia and landed the cover of LIFE Magazine for its 90th anniversary edition. Throughout the year, he regularly clocked between 50 and 100 million views per week–but in November, that number rose sharply, to almost 200 million a week.

That’s because as busy as the rest of 2026 was, November has been especially busy. While GTA fervor built to an all-time high on the eve of its debut, Speed turned his attention elsewhere, and for good reason: He teamed up with former President and First Lady Barack and Michelle Obama to present The Eyes of Ghana, a feature-length documentary that follows 93-year-old cinematographer Chris Hesse as he “races against blindness and time to save over 1,000 films that reveal the hidden origin story of African independence.”

Directed by Oscar winner Ben Proudfoot and produced by the Obamas, the film went live on Speed’s YouTube channel Nov. 17, and has nearly 3 million views.

Speed wasn’t done. His latest video features him racing against the man, the myth, the legend: Usain Bolt. Clocking in at 11 million views in only 48 hours, this upload is on track to be one of his biggest videos to date.

Speed is going into 2027 with around ~500 million views a month–and considering his ambitious Obama and Usain partnerships, we can’t help wondering what big view-driver ideas he’ll bring home in the new year.
