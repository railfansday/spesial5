Harmer stars as South Africa beat Bangladesh

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

South Africa edged closer to securing a spot in the World Test Championship final with a nine-wicket victory against Bangladesh on Thursday; Simon Harmer took four wickets to bowl out Bangladesh; watch the second Test live on Sky Sports Cricket

Simon Harmer ran through Bangladesh's tail and South Africa knocked off the 12 runs required to win the first Test by nine wickets on Thursday, taking a 1-0 lead in the teams' two-match series.

The win sees the Proteas edge closer to securing a spot at the World Test Championship final at The Kia Oval in 2027, with the defending champions having dethroned Australia to go top of the ICC rankings following their series win last month.

Resuming on 100-5 at the Wanderers, Bangladesh's lower-order batters couldn't handle the off-spin of Harmer, who took four of the remaining five wickets to help dismiss the tourists for 168 and return figures of 6-51.

It revived memories of Harmer taking 6-16 on day five of the series-clinching victory over Australia last month.

"I don't think I bowled my best this match," Harmer told SuperSport.

"But I'm very aware of the way cricket works; you're going to have days where you bowl well and don't necessarily get rewards.

"Sometimes you don't bowl as well as you should, but you end up getting wickets.

"It would have been tricky if we were chasing 150 or 170. To be able to restrict them and get wickets in the third innings is enjoyable and it's always nice to contribute."

There is bad news for South Africa, though, who are likely to be without their captain Temba Bavuma for the second Test, live on Sky Sports, with the skipper still struggling to overcome a fitness issue.

The Proteas lost first-innings century-maker Marques Ackerman - for a second-ball duck - on the way to reaching their target before lunch at 12-1. Soon after getting bowled off a no-ball, Aiden Markram (8 not out) scored the winning run.

In Harmer, South Africa have a 37-year-old player enjoying a renaissance in a Test career that was drifting until a year ago, when he returned after a two-and-a-half-year absence. He is now a reliable match-winner.

Having already removed Najmul Hossain Shanto and Mahfijul Islam late on day four, Harmer took the final four wickets of the match in his brilliant four-over burst that came after overnight batter Litton Das got an inside edge and played onto his own stumps off Kagiso Rabada (3-46).

Mushfiqur Rahim added 34 runs to his overnight five before gloving Harmer to a close fielder, for a dismissal that was only given after the Proteas reviewed a not-out call.

In his next over, Harmer had Mehidy Hasan Miraz stumped for four before removing Hasan Mahmud (4) and Ebadot Hossain (0) in consecutive balls to wrap up the innings.

Despite making 400 in the first innings after winning the toss, Bangladesh barely avoided an innings defeat.
