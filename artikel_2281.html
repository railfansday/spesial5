6 Michael Skubala replacements Bristol City must eye as sack calls grow

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Bristol City suffered a 2-1 loss to Wolves on Wednesday night to fall to 15th in the Championship table.

The Robins appointed Michael Skubala as head coach in the summer, but the former Lincoln City boss is already facing scrutiny surrounding his position.

The 44-year-old has overseen just five wins from 14 league games so far this campaign, with the team earning just one victory from their last six fixtures.

This fan thinks that defeat against Norwich City over the weekend could spell the end.

@TWMCLtd, wrote: "We have a tremendous squad. Injuries improving. But Skubala has not got a Skubee-Doo. Still refusing to play Horvat. Non sensical substitutions. Getting toxic now. Sadly think he needs to go."

@_W00KS_ and @Mendip_muddler were locked in debate.

​​​​​​​@lpmoffatt says he's running out of patience with Skubala: "The lack of any identity or nouse is the biggest concern. It it were long ball and Tolaj was real useful in the air with players buzzing around him we might stomach it but just aimlessly trying to hit him whilst sitting deep isn't working. No plan B. Absolutely no patience left."

Bristol City initially took the lead against Wolves through Scott Twine in the 34th minute, but a late brace from Sean Neave sealed all three points for the Old Gold at Ashton Gate.

This has put Skubala under immense pressure just a few days before the November international break, which could be an opportunity for clubs to make a change in the dugout.

Here we look at six ambitious replacements the Championship side must consider as sack calls grow…

Rob Edwards is the most obvious immediately available candidate given his previous work at both Luton Town and Middlesbrough.

He guided the Hatters to promotion to the Premier League in 2023, and had Boro in the mix for a top two spot before jumping ship to Wolves midway through last season.

HIs impressive track record speaks for itself, and if he can be convinced to make the move to Ashton Gate, then he could be a very strong appointment.

There will be competition for his appointment, so Bristol City may need to act quickly in order to secure his signature.

Steven Gerrard is currently available after leaving his role with Al-Ettifaq in January 2025, and he could be looking to make his return to management in English football with a move to the Championship.

The former midfielder enjoyed tremendous success in his first role as a head coach at Rangers, leading the Glasgow outfit to a league title in 2021, as well as a Europa League final a year later.

Neto Borges could have been at Bristol City, but Kim Hellberg's reintegrated him into the Boro side, while the Robins loaned in a good replacement

His time in Scotland was defined by a strong defensive core that helped make his Rangers tough to beat, and it translated well on the continent, earning the club a first European final since 2008.

While his time at Aston Villa didn't go as smoothly, he could still be capable of replicating his Rangers success at Ashton Gate, provided he can build a strong coaching team around him.

Robbie Keane has yet to make his first foray into management in English football, although he did previously work as a coach at Middlesbrough and Leeds.

But he has carved out experience in Israel and Hungary, leading Maccabi Tel Aviv to a league title in 2024, and Ferencvaros to the 2025 title.

The former Ireland international also won the Magyar Kupa in 2026, before leaving the European side at the end of that campaign, which could now open the door for him to make the move to Bristol City.

It would certainly be a risk to bring him in given his lack of experience at this level, but his success abroad offers a glimpse of what he could achieve for the Robins.

Carlos Corberan was dismissed by Valencia in September after an underwhelming start to the new La Liga campaign.

But he has an impressive track record at Championship level, leading Huddersfield Town to a play-off final in 2023, and his West Brom side were also in the mix for a top six finish before he made the switch to Spain.

The 43-year-old is a smart tactician with an attractive style of play, which could make him quite an appealing appointment, particularly as the current squad would be suited to his ideal 4-2-3-1 formation.

His two previous Championship roles had the respective sides competing for promotion, and that is what Bristol City should be looking for when searching for any new head coaches.

The fear, here, might be Corberan eyeing a role in the Premier League.

Steve Cooper led Nottingham Forest to promotion and then survival in the Premier League, playing a pragmatic but effective style of play.

While his time at Leicester City didn’t prove as successful, events at the King Power Stadium since then indicate that there were bigger concerns going on than his coaching.

The 46-year-old could be a shrewd appointment by the Robins to get the team back into promotion contention, if he can be convinced to join a Championship side.

While his style of play has its detractors, there is no denying that it can get results when everything clicks.
