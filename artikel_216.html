Wondering what to buy on Black Friday? Dont miss our top 10 deals.

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Whether you want to conquer the bulk of your holiday shopping or treat yourself to some goodies for less than usual, now is the time. Is Black Friday overhyped? Sure, but there are legitimately excellent deals you'll be bummed you missed. It can be tough sorting through what's actually worth your hard-earned money, though, so we've taken it upon ourselves to do that part for you.

If you're itching to start adding to your cart, but you aren't sure what to buy, you've come to the right place. Mashable's shopping team has been on a deal hunt for the past month leading up to Black Friday. Now that the big day has officially arrived, we've hand-picked our top 10 deals of the sale.

Most Black Friday deals will stick around through Cyber Monday, then disappear. So if something catches your eye, don't hesitate to add it to your cart.

If it's time to replace your AirPods, there's never been a better moment. This Black Friday deal knocks the Pro 3s down to their lowest price on record: just $149. While they may not be the newest AirPods any more, they're the latest Pro model and are brimming with great features. That includes Live Translation, heart rate sensing, adaptive noise cancellation, three-dimensional spatial audio, and a hearing aid option.

Amazon raised the prices of its Kindle models over the summer, then dropped a whole batch of new ones. None of the new models are on sale for Black Friday. But lucky for you, we actually prefer the 2024 Kindle Paperwhite to the new model and it's on sale for only $129.99. That's only $10 more than the basic Kindle and you'll get to enjoy reading in the bath or on the beach, since it's waterproof.

With an absurdly powerful M5 chip, a sleek and supremely portable design, and unrivaled performance for the price, the M5 MacBook Air earned the title of Mashable's favorite MacBook for most people. Our laptop expert Haley Henschel calls it "the sweet spot in Apple's lineup." This $300 discount is better than we expected, given the $200 price hike earlier this year.

Amazon released the cutest new smart clock last month called the Echo Time, but it's stupid expensive at $199.99. This Black Friday discount makes it a much more approachable $99.99 and comes with a $40 bonus credit for Prime members (look for the on-page coupon). It's extremely customizable and can help you build a better sleep routine. It also happens to make a great gift. I track streaming deals all year long and the Black Friday HBO Max deal is the best I've seen in a while. Mostly because you can lock in a whole year of streaming, as opposed to only a few months. At 50% off, the deal drops the cost to just $5.49 per month for the ad-supported tier. The offer is available as a standalone deal, a Prime Video add-on, or a Roku subscription.

One of the best gifts of the year, the Oura Ring 5 is not only a detailed health tracker with a comfortable fit and lengthy battery life. It's also a sleek piece of jewelry rather than a clunky watch. Both the gold and silver options are on sale for $100 off for Black Friday, which is the biggest discount we've seen.

Any time a Yeti cooler goes on sale, it's a momentous occasion. The Roadie 15 is a virtually indestructible sidekick that can fit up to 22 cans or 15 pounds of ice. I can vouch for it keeping that ice frozen solid for days. It's a bit hefty, but the newest version has a more comfortable DoubleDuty shoulder strap and a drainage plug to make it easier to empty.

The QuietComfort headphones from Bose not only offer excellent noise cancellation, but they're also pretty dang comfortable for all-day wear. Mashable's Sam Mangino said these are her all-time favorite headphones, even after testing a ton of other options. For Black Friday, they're an impressive 53% off.

There are a bunch of really impressive TV deals every Black Friday, so you have plenty to choose from. But let us be the first to recommend Roku's new OLED Pro TV while it's under $800. That's a dang good price for an OLED TV of that size. Our colleagues at CNET gave this TV a Lab Award Lab Award for "Lowest Input Lag," making it a particularly good choice for gamers.

Whether you choose Kindle+ for digital reading or Audible for listening, you can sign up for hella cheap (only 99 cents) during Black Friday. Instead of spending all your time this season binge-watching the latest show, show some love to those books everyone has been talking about. Reading is cool, y'all.
