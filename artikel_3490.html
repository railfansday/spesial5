Johnny Depp Tops the Box Office Again With Ebenezer's Opening Weekend

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Ebenezer opened the festive season in theaters this weekend

With December fast approaching, the holiday cheer is quickly creeping up on moviegoers around the world. The festive season kicked off with the premiere of Ebenezer in theaters on Nov. 13. The story of Ebenezer Scrooge was originally told in Charles Dickens' A Christmas Carol, published in late 1843. Nearly two centuries later, the morals that are taught in A Christmas Carol remain relevant, and it is hard not to be touched by Scrooge's transformation from a Grinch-like miser into a generous man.

This story has been retold and adapted dozens of times. The latest adaptation of A Christmas Carol is director Ti West's Ebenezer starring Johnny Depp as Scrooge, Rupert Grint as Bob Cratchit, and Ian McKellen as Jacob Marley. The retelling of this classic tale received a neutral rating from critics, only reaching 54% on Rotten Tomatoes. However, audiences disagreed, giving it a 90% rating. It was able to snag moviegoers' attention on its opening weekend, earning $19 million in its opening weekend in the US and a further $23 million internationally.

Johnny Depp, the main focus of this movie, remained out of the spotlight for years due to a lengthy legal battle with his ex-wife Amber Heard involving domestic abuse allegations. Many reviewers have tied the movie's rating to the actor, which has tipped the scales both ways.

Peter Travers from The Travers Take remarks: "Depp is still the kind of talent who can make you feel a character’s pain. Both he and Ebenezer are looking for absolution. Dickens took care of Ebenezer. Depp’s fate is up to the audience. I’m rooting for him." Amy Nicholson from the LA Times believes that "the actor’s need to be loved — to claw himself out of what he fears is an early career grave, an image we see him physically do — hijacks the movie. The traditional beats don’t land."

According to Variety, half of those that attended the premiere watched Ebenezer to see Johnny Depp, as it was his first major role in eight years. His next appearance will be in Day Drinker, scheduled for March 26, 2027. He is also currently in talks with Disney to return as Jack Sparrow in the next Pirates of the Caribbean.
