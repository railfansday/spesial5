Shiba Inu fell to a rare low of $0.0000037 as cryptocurrencies tumbled over the weekend. This low marked the lowest seen in years, going back to 2021, as it fell below the $0.000004 level, which has served as support since the start of 2026.

Last week’s bruising selloff following a tougher interest-rate outlook and a risk-off mood deepened the gloom around the cryptocurrency.

Shiba Inu fell for four straight days in the past week, dropping to a low of $0.0000037 on November 14, 2026, coinciding with the first death cross on its daily chart in 2026.

At the time of writing, Shiba Inu was down 3.36% in the last 24 hours to $0.00000386 as the crypto market selloff intensified, with a total of $203.7 million liquidated. Mazrael, a long-time Shiba Inu community member and SHIB developer, reacted to the token's latest price weakness with a simple message: "1 SHIB is always 1 SHIB" in response to an X user who pointed out Shiba Inu's price drop.

The phrase, often used in the context of Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies during price downturns, serves as a reminder to focus on the unit itself rather than short-term fiat volatility. While a single coin always counts as one unit of the network, the argument against this saying is that fiat value changes amid volatility.

Fed Chairman Kelvin Warsh is set to speak later on Monday on the impact of technology on productivity and jobs, potentially triggering volatility in the markets. The Federal Reserve’s minutes from its October meeting, where it left rates unchanged, will be released Wednesday. Markets have largely priced in a quarter percentage point hike in December.

While the Shiba Inu price is trading in the red, the SHIB burn rate is up 1922%, with 136.81 million SHIB sent to dead wallets in the last 24 hours.

155.03 million SHIB have been burned in the last seven days, with the weekly burn rate up 14.40%. 395.10 million SHIB worth $1753 was burned in the last 30 days, although the burn rate was down 55.45% in the time frame.

A total of 410,845,352,742,359 SHIB has been burned in 21,967 transactions, contributing to 41.08% of the total supply.
