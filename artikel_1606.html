Pulisic praises Milan’s spirit after Olympiacos comeback but has ‘no answers’ for slow starts

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Christian Pulisic has insisted that AC Milan are growing together as a team under Ruben Amorim, after the comeback win over Olympiacos.

In what had been a running theme in their opening four Europa League games, Milan started slowly and found themselves two goals down in Greece. The first was attributed as a Mike Maignan own-goal, while there was no doubt that Jota Silva got the second.

The Rossoneri sprung into life inside 15 second-half minutes, scoring three times. Omari Hutchinson was the man of the moment as his solo goal halved the deficit, before he then set up Alphadjo Cisse for the equaliser and Pulisic for the 3-2 goal.

Diego Moreira added a fourth in the dying seconds to make sure of a much-needed win. Pulisic came off the bench and left his mark on the game with his goal and assist for the Moreira’s at the end, and he spoke to Sky after the final whistle. His words were relayed by MilanNews.

“It was a crucial match for us. From now on, every game is like a final. We didn’t start well, but we were much better in the second half. It’s also important to find that intensity.”

Is this the best period since the season started?

“It’s a good period, I don’t know if it’s the best. Hutchinson changed this game. I’m always there to help the team with anything.”

Have you finally turned a page in Europe?

“I don’t know. We also have a new coach this year. It takes a while to learn this system. I see a real team that sticks together. We’re all growing together.”

Why do you struggle so much at the beginning of the Europa League?

“I have no answers. It’s difficult. It’s not easy to play here, they played with so much energy. But you’re right, we have to get into the game earlier, otherwise if we start like this, it won’t be good.”

Do you get along well with all these fast team-mates?

“We have very fast players. We saw that against Frosinone too. When we face such open teams, it’s a good thing for us with the players we have.”
