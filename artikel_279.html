Marques Brownlee secretly built a new content studio and surprised fans with a first look

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Marques Brownlee has been secretly working on a project, a colossal new studio the content creator unveiled on Wednesday.

A year ago, the Mashable 101 alumnus filmed a walkthrough of his new 30,000-square-foot, two-storey facility under construction, going room-by-room to explain plans for his future video production space.

"This is being recorded in secret, a year ago from when you're watching it," says Brownlee in the video. "The next video you get to watch is the finished studio, which is sick. So this is going to be an empty building tour, and then I'm going to be able to snap my fingers, show you the finished tour, and walk you through it. So you'll see how close I get to my vision in the final thing. I hope I get really close."

Watching creators like Brownlee building their audience and brand to scale up into their own production studios is a delight — and we're more than keen to see the finished space, a video which could drop any day.
