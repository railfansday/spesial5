Peacock is redesigning its mobile app to focus more on vertical videos

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

This is an attempt to “win another hour per user, per month.”

NBCUniversal is completely overhauling the Peacock app on mobile. This redesign will further emphasize vertical video content, just like TikTok, YouTube Shorts and all the rest. Company chairman Matt Strauss recently said that this is an attempt to "win another hour per user, per month," according to The Hollywood Reporter.

"We recognize that we have to start thinking of ourselves less as a streaming platform and more of an entertainment platform," Strauss said. "How do we super serve our franchises and our fandoms where the best experience is on our app?"

In addition to a larger emphasis on vertical video, the app is launching fandom hubs for various franchises, like the Bravo-verse or The Office. These hubs will be home to short videos and affiliated experiences. For instance, the Law & Order page will feature little video games to fool around with. The NBA page will offer "easy access to player stats" and "deeper game information sitting directly underneath the live feed in a dual screen view."

There are a couple of techy features heading to the app. Peacock will continue to experiment with live vertical video. There will soon be a "dynamic video player" that automatically switches between vertical and horizontal orientations depending on the device. Of course, the app is also integrating generative AI to allow for "conversational AI search."

Finally, NBCUniversal intends to continue investing in microdramas. These are extremely short (often just a minute long) serial dramas that connect to tell a larger story. The platform recently released something called Charmed by a Southern Monster, which is the company's first scripted microdrama. The new Peacock app is rolling out now via a phased release that could take until next year to reach every user.

It's worth noting that Peacock recently became profitable for the first time ever. As a matter of fact, it has experienced two straight quarters of profit. We'll have to wait and see if this app redesign helps to continue this trend.
