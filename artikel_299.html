Israeli Opposition Pushes for Oct. 7 Inquiry at First Session of New Parliament

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Dozens of first-time lawmakers were sworn into the Knesset, where the opposition, led by Gadi Eisenkot, now has a majority.

Israel’s largest opposition party moved quickly on Tuesday to submit a bill to Parliament that seeks to establish a state inquiry into the security failings that preceded the Hamas-led October 2023 attacks, which took place under the watch of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.

The party, Yashar, led by the former military chief Gadi Eisenkot, made the move as lawmakers gathered for the first session of Israel’s Parliament since an inconclusive election last month.

An anti-Netanyahu bloc of parties from across Israel’s political spectrum now controls a majority of seats in the Parliament, known as the Knesset. Passage of the bill would be a rebuke to Mr. Netanyahu and his allies, who have long blocked efforts to establish such a commission, saying it would not uncover the truth.

Yashar also put forward bills on Tuesday aimed at repealing a number of the most contentious laws passed by the Netanyahu government, including some that were part of a yearslong effort to curb the powers of Israel’s judiciary.

Dozens of first-time lawmakers took their seats on Tuesday in the Knesset, where Israel’s president urged the parties to create a stable government.

“The people who chose you long for routine, security and tranquillity,” the president, Isaac Herzog, said at the swearing-in ceremony. “The people you represent here yearn to see an outstretched hand that will bring unity to the nation and hope.”
