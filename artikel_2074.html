Jussi Jääskeläinen praises Arsenal star David Raya

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Lucie Turner writes about Arsenal for Read Arsenal and Newcastle United for Read Newcastle. A long-time Arsenal supporter, she also follows rugby, snooker, darts and Formula 1. Her work covers match previews and reports, breaking news, player profiles and opinion pieces.

Former Premier League goalkeeper Jussi Jääskeläinen thinks David Raya is the best goalkeeper in the division right now.

But the former Bolton Wanderers star feels the Arsenal No.1 has more to prove before he can be put up with the all-time greats of the competition.

Jääskeläinen recently spoke to Kasinopartio.com, where he praised Raya’s performances and the understanding he has developed with Arsenal’s defenders.

“Raya has been a fantastic signing. He has a great relationship with the centre-backs; even when the back four changes, everything works well.”

The Spaniard has played a key role for Mikel Arteta since his arrival from Brentford. His organisation of the backline, key saves and contribution to Arsenal’s build up play have helped him to be a standout player.

In 2012, Raya began his journey in English football when he moved to Blackburn Rovers from Spain. At that time, he was just a young teenager unfamiliar with English football.

His short spell at Southport introduced him to senior football. Soon, he returned to Blackburn and forged a career ahead of moving to Brentford in 2019. After four years with Brentford, Raya achieved success at the club, helping them to the Premier League.

The player’s performances in the higher leagues caught the attention of Arsenal, and Arteta signed him on loan to the Emirates in 2023 before making the move permanent the following summer.

It didn’t take long for Raya to establish himself in the team. He took home the Premier League Golden Glove, having achieved 16 clean sheets in 32 appearances.

This was a significant milestone in his first season with the Arsenal team, and he has since remained as number one, playing a key role in the club’s pursuit of titles.

While Jääskeläinen said Raya is the best goalkeeper in the Premier League at the moment, he does not think he should be up there with the most experienced.

He believes other goalkeepers have earned an advantage through their consistency over a longer period.

“You have lots of top goalkeepers that I think are still a little bit ahead because of the years they’ve done it in the Premier League.”

“He’s not up there with them yet, but he’s definitely going in the right direction.”

Raya has already proven he can play to the top standard. However, it will be his consistency moving forward that will define his long-term reputation.

Petr Čech, Peter Schmeichel and Edwin van der Sar have all gained great reputations over the years. There is still plenty of time for Raya to do the same, but he will need to keep up his high standards whilst wearing an Arsenal shirt.

At just 31, Raya has a lot left in his career. If he can continue playing at this level, it will make the debate around whether he is one of the best goalkeepers in the Premier League even more intriguing.
