Steven Gerrard recalls his Rangers ‘50 cameras’ warning Alfredo Morelos ignored

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Steven Gerrard has revealed how he used to warn Alfredo Morelos that “50 cameras” were watching his every move during their time together at Rangers.

The former Ibrox boss lifted the lid on his attempts to keep the fiery Colombian striker out of trouble while discussing the impact of VAR on football.

Morelos became a hugely influential figure during Gerrard’s Rangers reign, but his disciplinary record provided his manager with more than a few headaches.

And Gerrard recalled having to remind the striker that officials and cameras were always likely to pick up any indiscretions.

Speaking on The Overlap Daily, Gerrard said: “You have to obviously mention it, especially if you have some of the players that have played for me, like Alfredo Morelos.

“‘Alfredo, there are 50 cameras in the ground, and they’re looking out for you, and VAR.’

“‘Yeah, yeah, I know, boss.’ Next minute, red card.”

Gerrard made the revelation while explaining that the introduction of VAR did not necessarily change the way he coached players, although managers had to make their squads aware of refereeing trends.

The former Rangers manager also made clear his preference for less intervention from technology. He added: “The ball’s already off the pitch for, I don’t know, 30, 40 per cent of the time, and then you think about the stoppages for VAR, and games are going to 99, 100 minutes.

It was a typically colourful recollection of Morelos, whose combative approach made him a major character during Gerrard’s successful spell at Ibrox.
