Sheffield Wednesday officially confirm ticket decision for Reading FC match

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Sheffield Wednesday have announced a major ticketing decision ahead of tomorrow afternoon's League One fixture against Reading at Hillsborugh.

Wednesday will be intent on returning to winning ways when the Royals come to town, having claimed successive victories over Plymouth Argyle and South Yorkshire rivals Barnsley before playing out a stalemate draw at Stevenage on Tuesday evening.

Positioned fourth in League One at this moment in time, the Owls are striving to close the gap and edge closer to the automatic promotion places but are just five points ahead of tomorrow's opponents, who remain in ninth and have won only one of their last six outings across all competitions.

Attendances at Hillsborough have been strong since David Storch's much-welcomed takeover of the club at the end of the previous campaign, and with an average figure just shy of 31,000 across their eight home fixtures to date in league action, more of the same is in order for Reading's visit.

Indeed, a fresh ticketing decision made by the Owls could even promise to further boost tomorrow's attendance.

Wednesday have announced a fresh update ahead of Saturday's showdown, in which Hallam FC supporters will be able to attend free of charge.

As confirmed via the club's social media channels on Friday afternoon, Wednesday will be opening their turnstiles to fans of the Sheffield-based non-league club, who have been competing in the Northern Premier League East this term.

The offer will only be applicable to season ticket holders at Sandygate, which just happens to be world football's oldest ground. Indeed, Hallam FC are the world's second-oldest club, too, though this announcement comes upon the seventh-tier outfit's confirmation that they would be withdrawing from the seventh-tier with immediate effect following a warning that they could run out of money as early as next month.

Facing the threat of extinction owing to a "perilous" financial situation, Wednesday have extended an invitation for free attendance to all Hallam FC season ticket holders, who are able to claim their complimentary ticket in the West Stand Lower on Leppings Lane by showing their season ticket at turnstiles 14 and 17.

The Owls have penned a warm-natured message of goodwill and support to their non-league neighbours, too, having travelled to Sandygate in pre-season, and the gesture has garnered quite the reaction online.

Wednesdayites have commended the decision, and will look forward to welcoming some of the Hallam FC to Hillsborough this weekend.
