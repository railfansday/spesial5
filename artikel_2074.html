When Newcastle United fans boo at half time and full time, who exactly are you booing?

Had he stayed, Newcastle United would be in a different position now.

He could have left last season but tried to cling to his job. As soon as the strategy changed he left to protect his brand – now strongly linked with Man U.

Amanda Staveley adopted a high risk, high reward transfer strategy, which won us a trophy. As soon as the luck ran out we had to liquidate to rebuild and remain compliant with UEFA and the Premier League cartel.

They are a young, raw team. It took Mikel Arteta four years to build his team. Many Newcastle United fans have given these players and manager two months.

We have lost our four best players over the last 18 months, and because we cant spend what we want, we’ve had to adopt a high risk strategy of buying young players with potential. There will be bumps along the way.

If you’re going to boo anything, boo the premier league anthem. They are why we have to go through this. SUPPORT THE TEAM. If you dont want to support them, flog me your season ticket and I will.
