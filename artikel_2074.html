Arsenal handed potential midfield advantage as Newcastle face Joelinton injury setback

Arsenal head to St James’ Park this Saturday for their first match after the international break. They could be faced with a weakened Newcastle midfield.

Joelinton missed training before the international break because of a thigh injury that also kept him out of the 1-1 draw with Fulham. Now, he needs to have an MRI scan to check the seriousness of his injury.

This is a huge blow for Jaissle. Joelinton’s physicality and intensity will be very difficult to find in another player, especially when the Gunners put pressure on Newcastle.

It may help Mikel Arteta’s team to dominate in midfield. They must ensure that they match the level of physicality of Newcastle and adjust with the away game itself. Joelinton’s absence may require Newcastle to make some changes in their style of play from midfield.

Joelinton has struggled to make an impact in the Premier League this season. He’s played only three games, and is now set to be sidelined again.

However, it has been suggested that Joelinton could return before Christmas. Further medical tests must be performed to determine the exact amount of time that he will be out for.

Joelinton is a concern. He is very physical and can regain the ball effectively in midfield. He can also help Newcastle move the ball forward.

If Newcastle are unable to match that intensity, it could allow Arsenal to dominate the game. Declan Rice and Bruno Guimarães are both in good form, meaning that the Gunners have a solid foundation in order to succeed, and stop the hosts from taking control.

Willock and Gonzalez have both been absent due to illness, but they should be fit to play on Saturday.

Jaissle has more options to consider with Joelinton missing. Jacob Ramsey is also now available after serving his suspension.

Newcastle have some good news regarding Matias Fernandez-Pardo. The £51 million signing has been suffering from a long-term virus and was unable to play in Belgium’s last internationals. However, he appears to be improving.

He has returned to training so could make the starting XI after coming on as a substitute in the game against Fulham. Since moving to the club from France, he has played 6 games in the Premier League.

Despite Joelinton being absent, Newcastle will still be a dangerous opponent. A calm performance from Arsenal will be required in order to take 3 points from the match.

With Joelinton missing, Arsenal can assert control in midfield. As they look to stay on top of the league table, exploiting the injury issues in Newcastle’s team should be a priority.
