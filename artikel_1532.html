Moreira discusses first Belgium goal, ‘special’ Milan group and learning in Italy: “I’ll always remember”

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Last night, Diego Moreira scored his first goal for Belgium and AC Milan will be very happy to see their investment continuing to shine.

It’s fair to say that Moreira has been a revelation for Milan. His arrival came out of nowhere, with a deal first reported by FootballKush and then it was picked up by the biggest sources in football. His first appearance was a struggle against Juventus, but the rest, as they say, is history.

Goals have come regularly, with huge appearances that have defined the Rossoneri’s season already. For example, the goal against Inter, with two assists to accompany it. He’s perhaps been the signing of the season in Italy and there’d maybe be a conversation about Europe, too.

After scoring against Italy, Moreira spoke to Sky, looking at the team’s performance, playing with Kevin De Bruyne and the feeling of grabbing his first goal for his country, as MilanNews relays.

“I’m very happy with my first goal for the national team. It was a special moment because my family was there. It’s a day I’ll always remember.”

Mancini said that you deserved to win and that you are a very strong team…

“We have some very strong players up front, like Doku, De Bruyne, but I think that in the end it’s a sport where everyone is needed and we all played a great game.”

What does it mean to play with a player like De Bruyne?

“De Bruyne is special; it’s a pleasure to have him with us because he sees things that others don’t see, he has a lot of experience and we’re happy to have him in our team.”

What do you take from this experience with the national team at AC Milan?

“I learned a lot in Italy because it’s a league with a lot of experienced players. Playing for the national team and for AC Milan are two very positive things, and I’m very happy.”

How do you recover in Europe after a difficult start?

“It’s difficult but we’re working towards this. We have to be prepared and we have players who will help us with this.”

Then, in the mixed zone, he was also intercepted by MilanNews, where he also answered a few questions about the Rossoneri.

Diego, you scored in your first Milan derby, just like Kaká, Shevchenko, and Ronaldinho before you. How have you settled in at AC Milan?

“I’m very happy. At AC Milan, I’m surrounded by a special group, with many great players. Having them around me helps me a lot, especially in terms of experience. It’s true, the match against Inter was special. I’ll continue to work hard and give my best.”

Good in Serie A, bad in the Europa League: why this two-faced Milan?

“I’m on the national team right now, and you’re asking me about Milan?”

Belgium practically overwhelmed Italy, your current footballing nation. It ended 4-1, but the scoreline could have been eight or nine goals…

“It’s something special, and we’re very happy. It was an important match, so winning and scoring was really nice.”
