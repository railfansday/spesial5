Roma-Dybala: a contract extension until 2028 is on the table

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Paulo Dybala, after nearly a month, is preparing to return to the pitch.

The Argentine star succumbed to a micro-tear in his calf at the end of the first half against Real Madrid, but is now ready to rekindle his strength.

“La Joya” has overcome the problem and has begun training with his teammates.

The number 21 is a candidate for a starting spot ahead of Parma-Roma, scheduled for Saturday at 6:00 PM. Dybala will have to overcome competition from Mora and Soulé, who have performed very well in recent matches for the Giallorossi.

Paulo Dybala’s contract expires on June 30, but Roma appears intent on negotiating a new contract extension.

As La Gazzetta dello Sport reports, in the coming weeks, the relationship between the number 21 and the Giallorossi will undergo a period of mutual observation, where physical continuity will make the difference.

The parties will navigate by sight, but the possibility of a meeting to extend the agreement until 2028 is far from remote. For now, the sirens of the US MLS and the call of Boca Juniors remain only suggestions.
