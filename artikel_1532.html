11 forgotten ballers you had no idea are playing in the Greek Super League

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Greek Super League is ranked by UEFA as the 10th best in Europe, just behind the Turkish Super Lig which you’d normally assume attracts the bigger names.

That’s largely true, but there’s a decent amount of players you’ve heard of playing in Greece as well.

We can guarantee you haven’t thought of some of these names for a good while.

You remember him. Well, at least one of his goals, don’t you?

The former Manchester United prospect never quite maintained the early promise and ended up going on loan to clubs as varied as Sampdoria, QPR, Stuttgart, Doncaster Rovers and Birmingham City. As you do.

Once the Man Utd dream was gone for good, Macheda had spells in the second tiers of England and Italy before first moving to Greece with Panathinaikos in 2018.

He stayed there for four years before spending time in Turkey and Cyprus. He returned to Greece, though, with a move to Asteras Tripolis in 2024.

The striker scored in their first game of the season a day after his 35th birthday and has four goals to his name from 12 games so far this season.

Arsenal will be the club most people think of when they think of Elneny, who spent eight years on the books there.

The midfielder eventually became surplus to requirements at the Emirates Stadium and was released in 2024. His next move was to join Al-Jazira in the UAE, where he spent two years.

He made his European return in September by joining Levadiakos as a free agent on a contract until the end of the season.

It hasn’t got off to the best of starts for him in Greece, though. He got sent off for two bookings on his debut and has only played once since.

The former Newcastle and Tottenham midfielder brought an end to his second spell with Watford in February in order to join Panathinaikos.

It wasn’t a completely random move. On the contrary, it allowed him to reunite with his former Newcastle manager, Rafael Benitez.

He was only able to play four times for them, though, before injury ruled him out of last season’s play-offs. He hasn’t made a matchday squad this season, either, after falling out of favour with Benitez’s successor.

His contract with Panathinaikos will run out at the end of the season. Talks to terminate it haven’t led to an escape route yet.

One of the more recognisable faces from Croatia’s run to the World Cup semi-finals in 2018, Vida was their main centre-back partner to Dejan Lovren at the tournament.

Back in those days, he was playing his club football for Besiktas, who he stayed with until 2022. After that, he joined AEK Athens, with whom he won the Greek Super League at the first time of asking.

Now in the last year of his contract, the 37-year-old has been restricted to playing in the cup this season and has been stuck on the bench in the league and Champions League.

De Vrij’s been knocking around for a while now. He’s one of those players who used to get linked with a Premier League move pretty much every year without it ever happening.

Instead, he spent the bulk of his career in Italy after joining Lazio from Feyenoord and going on to become a three-time Serie A winner with Inter Milan.

He spent eight years with Inter before bidding them farewell in the summer.

Panathinaikos were quick to snap him up on a one-year contract. He’s already become their captain and hasn’t missed a minute in the league for the leaders yet since his arrival.

One for the Chelsea fans out there. We’re not sure what we’re more surprised by: the fact that Rahman is still only 32 or that he was still contracted to Chelsea as recently as three years ago.

The left-back played for five different clubs on loan away from Chelsea, one of which was Greek outfit PAOK in 2021.

After a couple of seasons with Reading, he re-joined PAOK in 2023. He’s under contract with them until the end of the season and starts about half of their matches these days.

Cabella was a one and done in the Premier League, leaving Newcastle to head back to France in 2016 after just one season in English football.

He’s spent the majority of his career since in Ligue 1, except for a spell with Krasnodar in Russia and his current duties in Greece.

The attacking midfielder joined Olympiacos in 2025 but was back in France after half a season with a loan move to Nantes. But his next permanent move away from Olympiacos would keep him in Greece.

Cabella, 36, joined Volos in September. He’s played five times for them so far, usually as a winger.

A proper wonderkid when he was breaking through at Sporting, Martins made his escape from the Lisbon-based club in 2018 to join Atletico Madrid.

He didn’t last long in Spain and settled at Monaco instead before joining Olympiacos in 2024, taking their number 10 shirt.

The winger has now played more than 100 times for Olympiacos, but has only scored once from 18 games this season.

Ortega was a decent backup keeper for Manchester City for three-and-a-half years before an epilogue to his Premier League career with Nottingham Forest.

He only played for Forest for half a year before being released and joining Olympiacos in the summer.

He’s started all 10 of their league games this season and has impressively kept clean sheets in six of them, only conceding five goals.

A member of Portugal’s Euro 2016-winning squad, Joao Mario subsequently earned a move from Sporting to Inter.

He’s one of those players who maybe didn’t achieve all he could have, but has still had a decent career, taking him to clubs like West Ham, Benfica and Besiktas.

The latter loaned him to AEK Athens last season before that move became permanent in the summer.

You don’t have to stretch your mind too far back to remember Bailey’s time in the Premier League. It was only this summer that Aston Villa let him go on a permanent basis after a failed loan spell with Roma got called off halfway through.

Once one of the most exciting wingers in the Bundesliga back in his Bayer Leverkusen days, Bailey now plays for Olympiacos.

He’s come off the bench twice for them so far in the league, scoring the second time round.
