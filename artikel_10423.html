‘Galaxy Watch 10’ won’t launch in 2027 as Samsung tries to slow RAMageddon losses

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

According to a new report, Samsung’s next “Galaxy Watch 10” won’t be releasing in 2027 with the company moving to a bi-yearly release schedule for its smartwatches.

ETNews reports that Samsung will not have a new Galaxy Watch model releasing in 2027, a series that would have likely been called “Galaxy Watch 10.” The reason, the report suggests, is that Samsung is “undertaking a structural overhaul to strengthen profitability” (translated) following two quarters in a row of losses in its mobile division.

Samsung has released a new Galaxy Watch every year since 2018, with 2021’s Galaxy Watch 4 marking the switch to Wear OS.

The new schedule will apparently see new Galaxy Watch releases every other year, pushing the next release into 2028. The report goes on to mention that Galaxy Watch is part of a “mature phase” of the smartwatch market, and that Galaxy Watch 9 sales “fell short of expectations” likely due in part to its higher price point.

Apparently, Samsung plans new Galaxy Watch software updates next year as well as “enhancement of healthcare features.” The Galaxy Fit 4 fitness tracker is also expected in 2027, with Dealabs reporting that the tracker will be priced at €79.90 in Europe, a slight price hike over the previous release.
