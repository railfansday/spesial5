Ukraine’s allies meet in Berlin with air defence, energy and financial support on the agenda – Europe live

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Meeting comes after Trump declared Putin a ‘friend’ during trilateral meeting in China

Meanwhile, Ukraine’s Volodymyr Zelenskyy has now arrived in Berlin for today’s meeting of the Coalition of the Willing.

“More than 40 partners will be represented, including the team of the American President. Ukraine’s priorities are strengthening our air defence, energy and financial support, the diplomatic track, and the next steps toward ending the war and guaranteeing security,” he said.

Zelenskyy added Ukraine was “doing everything to ensure Europe’s full participation in the negotiation process and to make these meetings productive.”

“Bilateral meetings with European partners are also planned, and, of course, with chancellor Friedrich Merz. There will also be a format involving the American side and European national security advisers.”

Meanwhile Ukraine’s foreign minister Sybiha offered some further commentary on this afternoon’s drone attacks against Kyiv, after posting a video of the UAVs falling on the grounds of his ministry.

“We will add it to our museum,” he joked, adding some, erm, rather undiplomatic messages to Russia.

Back in Berlin, US peace envoy Jared Kushner said he and Steve Witkoff had a “very productive meeting” with Germany’s Friedrich Merz and his national security adviser, Günter Sautter.

“We updated them on @realDonaldTrump discussions in China and the latest efforts to achieve a just, lasting and durable peace in Ukraine. We continue to work in close coordination with Germany, Ukraine and our European partners to align our positions and prepare for the next round of negotiations,” he said.

“Chancellor Merz is a strong advocate for peace in Ukraine and a strong Euro-Atlantic alliance. We are grateful for his leadership, constructive engagement, and close partnership as we work together toward our shared goal of ending this terrible war and building a secure and prosperous future for Ukraine and Europe,” he added.

Ukrainian foreign minister Andrii Sybiha has confirmed the damage at the Dutch embassy in Kyiv.

He posted a picture showing minor damage to one of the rooms at the embassy, and said:

“Russia’s attacks on Kyiv have once again damaged a diplomatic mission.

The Embassy of the Netherlands in Ukraine has sustained minor damage. Fortunately, no one was injured.

We urge the international community to condemn in the strongest terms Russia’s attacks on foreign diplomats and express our strong support for our Dutch colleagues. Putin’s terror must face strong responses.”

Dutch broadcaster RTL is reporting that a second drone was seen near the embassy and reportedly neutralised in the air, with a brief fire and smoke seen in their live report from the scene.

This is yet to be confirmed by Dutch or Ukrainian authorities.

RTL also has a short video showing the aftermath of the first drone explosion.

Dutch foreign minister Tom Berendsen has now confirmed the incident.

“This afternoon, a projectile struck the area behind the Dutch embassy in Kyiv. Fortunately, our colleagues are unharmed, although there has been minor damage to the building. We are investigating exactly what happened. Our priority now is the wellbeing of our staff.”

Just as the leaders awkwardly posed for the family photo, we got a line in the Dutch media, via Kyiv Independent, that the Dutch embassy in Kyiv was damaged in a Russian drone attack on the Ukrainian capital.

The Rotterdam-based Algemeen Dagblad stresses it’s not clear if the embassy was the intended target or just accidentally damaged as part of a broader attack.

But, in what is a curious coincidence, the incident comes just a day after a rather provocative message from Russia.

On Wednesday, the Russian embassy in the Netherlands asked if The Hague was “openly joining the conflict” in Ukraine (Europe Live, Wednesday) after a Dutch-headquartered company, Destinus, said its missiles were used in a Ukrainian attack on the Kerch Bridge in Russian-occupied Crimea.

I have reached to the Dutch ministry of foreign affairs to get their line on the incident. Will let you know when we hear back from them.

So that’s your cast for today, with all leaders attending the Coalition of the Willing’s meeting in Berlin.

Edi Rama’s white sneakers (14:22) can be seen in the second row, on the left.

Poland’s Tusk is now the latest leader to arrive, and he goes straight into a lengthy discussion with Merz as the pair walk into the chancellery. Greece’s Mitsotakis joins them at one point, too.

Moment earlier, European Commission’s von der Leyen arrived, too.

Nato’s secretary general Mark Rutte and Sweden’s Ulf Kristersson are the latest two arrivals.

Italy’s Giorgia Meloni is in rainy Berlin, too.

Germany’s Merz also welcomes Dutch PM Jetten – who dropped his phone (I think?) on arrival – with a dry celebratory remark “still alive!”.

As covered on Europe Live earlier this week, Jetten had to work overtime over the weekend to save his minority government from collapsing after an internal dispute over a tax reform.

Back in Berlin, more and more key leaders are arriving for today’s talks.

Meanwhile, Albania’s Edi Rama – as always, in his signature snow white sneakers – is clearly very happy to see Belgium’s Bart De Wever.

Denmark’s Frederiksen and Romania’s Dan are the latest leaders to arrive.
