Expensify: Customer Success Manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Expensify makes expense reports suck less. From corporate cards and receipt scanning to paying bills and booking travel, we help people spend less time managing expenses and more time doing what they love.

We’re a flat organization with two rules: Get shit done. Don’t ruin it for everyone else. We share information openly, trust each other to make decisions, and take responsibility for seeing things through. If you see something that needs fixing, you’re encouraged to get involved.

You have a lot of say in where your career goes here. Learn a new skill, work across teams, get deep into the product, or bring people together to tackle a problem. Leaders emerge through their work, and you can choose a mentor from anywhere in the company to help you figure out your next step.

We give you the freedom to structure your day and the flexibility to build a life you enjoy. We work remotely, and we also like spending time together. Our company trips give us a chance to work alongside people we usually see on a screen, explore somewhere new, and get to know each other.

We care about what we’re building and the people we’re building it with. If you’re curious, take initiative, and want a say in how things get done, you’ll fit right in.

Join our passionate team of customer experts to solve real-world problems and help people spend less time managing expenses and more time pursuing their real goals. As we revolutionize the way people manage their expenses, being part of the Expensify team means building the easiest, fastest, and most efficient platform to automate everything expense-related.

Own a portfolio of our mid-market and enterprise customers, from onboarding through renewal and growth. You'll be the person enterprise customers trust to keep things running, and to spot the next opportunity before they've even asked. If you like turning usage data into revenue outcomes and holding your own in a C-suite conversation, this one's for you.

As a Customer Success Manager, your responsibilities include:

Building trusted relationships with senior stakeholders across finance, IT and operations, right up to the C-suite

Owning the full account lifecycle, from onboarding through adoption, renewal and growth

Spotting risk and configuration issues early, and proactively solving them

Guiding customers through migrations and product rollouts, instilling confidence in the product and your ability to support them in any software transition within their tech stack

Turning usage data into a clear account plan that ties activity back to revenue

Driving adoption of Expensify's core products, including expense management, global reimbursement, Expensify Card and Travel

Troubleshooting technical issues hands-on, from accounting integrations and card feeds to SSO and API workflows, and tracing each one to its root cause

Acting as the bridge between customers and our product and engineering teams, translating customer feedback into something they can act on

Managing renewals, upsell and cross-sell conversations, and holding your own in contract talks

Improve net dollar retention across your portfolio

Increase product adoption across your accounts

We're looking for a Customer Success Manager who thinks like a product manager and acts like a trusted advisor. You've owned renewals, run QBRs and grown accounts through cross-sell and upsell. You understand how software works, and financial systems knowledge is a bonus. You're comfortable with data, not vibes, and you build account plans that tie activity back to revenue. You use AI to work smarter, but you never let it replace your own judgment. You see a problem and want to fix it, even when it isn't technically yours to fix.

3+ years of B2B SaaS account management or customer success experience, with direct ownership of a customer portfolio

Experience owning renewals, retention, and growth, including commercial conversations around pricing, expansion, or contract terms

Strong product and technical fluency, with experience working through APIs, integrations, or other connected software systems

Strong data literacy, including using spreadsheets, dashboards, usage data, and reporting tools to make decisions and build a business case

Strong written and verbal communication, with the ability to work effectively with both technical teams and senior customer stakeholders

Comfort operating autonomously, setting priorities and managing competing demands without heavy day-to-day direction

Experience managing enterprise accounts, or a mix of mid-market and enterprise

Experience with FinTech, payments, spend management, accounting, ERP, or other financial software

Familiarity with tools and integrations such as NetSuite, Okta, Workday, HubSpot, GitHub, Gong, or similar platforms

Experience using AI to automate workflows, improve processes, or solve business problems

Experience managing global customers and working effectively across time zones and asynchronously

100% employer-paid medical, dental, and vision coverage

$20,000 annual fertility and family-building benefit through Carrot

Up to $5,000 in relocation reimbursement if you move to one of our offices

Three months of paid parental leave; six months for birthing parents

Commuter, healthcare FSA, and dependent care FSA benefits

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
