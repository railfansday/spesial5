SharkNinja’s Holiday Ad Puts David Beckham’s Gadgets at the Mercy of His Celeb Pals

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

SharkNinja has handed David Beckham’s house keys to some very famous friends for its first global holiday campaign, which showcases the breadth of its viral products across the home.

“There’s No Home Like a SharkNinja Home” stars Beckham, Tom Brady, Kevin Hart, and Aryna Sabalenka in a holiday house-sitting scenario where Beckham’s friends make themselves a little too comfortable with his appliances.

Created in-house by SharkNinja’s Creative Labs team and produced by Studio 99, the campaign is the first to unite the company’s master brand and multiple of its 40-plus product categories in a single global holiday push.
