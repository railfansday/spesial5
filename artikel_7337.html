Court Filing Pulls Back Curtain on Nielsen’s Fight to Keep Networks In the Fold

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A new court filing in an antitrust action against Nielsen reveals emails showing CEO Karthik Rao and chairman David Kenny delving deep into competitive threats and contract negotiations in recent years, while worrying about the NFL’s stance on TV measurement.

The filing and accompanying exhibits are heavily redacted to remove most references to client companies. But much of the redacted material is clearly tied to specific network deals and communications based on dates, subject matter, cited media articles and earnings call transcripts.

The filing comes in TVision’s antitrust counterclaim against Nielsen, which it filed last year in response to the latest of five patent lawsuits brought by Nielsen.
