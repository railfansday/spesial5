Every WoW Forever Class Ranked For Leveling

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Jake brings two decades of experience covering video games, MTG, covering industry events on-site, delivering breaking news and major interviews.

We made it. World of Warcraft: Forever is finally here.

November 4th brings the long-awaited debut of “Classic Plus” in World of Warcraft, which means it’s time to start the journey anew. Again. We’re starting as a lowly, nameless level 1 character in Azeroth. Sure, we can see Blackrock Mountain and Hyjal in the distance from the starting areas, but we’re a long, long way from getting there.

The leveling process from 1-60 in Classic lasts a lot longer than it does in retail WoW. Even the most efficient players will take 150-200 hours to level in-game. It could take weeks, if not months, for players to hit max level. Not that this is a bad thing; Classic WoW is about the journey, not the destination.

With that being said, the journey in World of Warcraft can look different for everyone, depending not just on your time and ability, but on your class as well. You know what that means: it’s time for a tier list. Let’s go over every WoW: Forever class ranked for leveling.

When it comes to classic World of Warcraft, there was always one certainty: Warriors ruled the endgame.

With that being said, they had to earn it, as their leveling experience was, in a word, frustrating. Sure, they can tank through dungeons, but the issue is that the class does not thrive in solo-oriented content, which is what leveling generally is.

Thanks to dungeon XP nerfs outside quests, dungeon grinding isn't really on the table, though you do have some improvements available. Still, it's going to be a slow and tedious process to level up your Warrior.

The good news is that Rogues are doing excellent single-target damage (highest in the game, according to Blizzard), along with an impressive toolkit to be beneficial. The bad news is that all of the shortcomings that come with the Classic Rogue are still here.

That's not to say it's "bad," per se, but you can make the argument that outside of that single-target damage, it's outclassed by almost every other class.

The newest beta build for World of Warcraft: Forever has included some pretty controversial changes, including dungeon quest XP and class changes.

The biggest "issue" Priests have is that they're easily the worst DPS caster during the leveling process. That said, they can, you know, heal and bring along the utility that comes with a healer.

Like Rogues, they suffer from being outclassed by other, better options, but have the benefit of being a healer to help with survivability. Even in their post-nerf form, your wands are still extremely powerful.

You'll also never be unable to find a group...provided you're willing to heal, that is. Shadow can at least let you properly DPS while leveling alone, but it's nowhere near as powerful as the other caster classes.

Druids take a while to get going, but when they do, you'll be in for an enjoyable time. You just have to, you know, get there.

On the plus side, Druid lets you play any role you want: Tank, Healer, Melee DPS, Ranged DPS, hating life from levels 1-10. You name it, Druids can do it.

I'm being serious on that last one, sadly; the initial leveling experience is frustrating, and while it finally starts to get better as you access talents, it's still a slog to get through. On the plus side, you can get into any dungeon you want by being able to play any role needed.

If you believed everything you read on the internet, especially when it comes to World of Warcraft discourse, you'd feel like Shamans are far and away the worst class in the game. We're not here to talk about that, though. We're here to talk about how classes feel during the leveling process, and Shamans are solid.

Enhancement Shamans will be able to dish out plenty of damage while still tapping into the usefulness and resources available in the class toolkit, particularly healing, to make it one of the best melee leveling classes in the game outside of Paladins. Leveling as Elemental, though, still feels quite bad, so I recommend you avoid that spec as long as possible.

We're a long way away from the 2019 version of Mage leveling, as AOE farming in the open world has been nerfed. That said, Mages are still mages, so they have that going for them.

Mages can dish out damage, good AOE damage (especially at low levels), good crowd control, and the ability to always have immediate access to food and drinks. They remain efficient at the leveling process in World of Warcraft: Forever, but they're not as strong as they once were.

First of all, Undead Paladins are here in WoW: Forever, and I am beyond excited for it. They were the first class I tried in beta, and oh my goodness, it felt fantastic.

Obviously, some balance changes have gone live since then, but the class feels like it's in fantastic shape. Playing a Paladin is fun, the quality-of-life changes are nice, and it feels like you can take on the world even at lower levels. It's one of the strongest classes to level in WoW: Forever, only outclassed by a select few options.

I'll talk about Hunters later on in this list, since, spoiler alert, they're still the best class for leveling in WoW Forever, but there are a lot of similar features to a Warlock. Your pets will do a lot of damage and hold aggro, letting you safely cast spells from a distance.

The one difference between Warlocks and Hunters, though, is that Hunters do more damage and have less downtime. Still, Warlocks have impressive utility and never, ever die (or at least they never, ever should).

Hunters have always been leveling gods throughout the Classic WoW timeline for one reason: the strength of their pet.

Until Jokerd came along on his mage in 2019 with constant AOE farming, Hunters, notably Joana, always held the world record for 1-60 leveling for one simple reason: little to no downtime.

Your pet can tank enemies while dishing out legitimate damage as you, the player, attack safely from a range while constantly moving forward. Hunters rock as levelers, and Forever cements them as still the undisputed best leveling class in World of Warcraft.
