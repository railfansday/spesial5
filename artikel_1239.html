Liverpool’s exciting young transfer target Sidy Barhama Ndiaye has scored another stunning goal for Senegal at the FIFA Under-17 World Cup in Qatar, continuing his sensational form on the international stage.

The 16-year-old winger has attracted considerable attention in recent days following revelations that Liverpool have secured an option to sign him when he turns 18.

And the teenager has given Liverpool supporters another reason to be excited about his potential arrival at Anfield.

Facing Tajikistan in Senegal’s final Group F fixture on Thursday afternoon, Ndiaye broke the deadlock in the 37th minute with a brilliant piece of individual skill.

Closing out Group F action against Tajikistan on Thursday afternoon, the 16-year-old winger broke the deadlock in the 37th minute with a moment of individual brilliance.

Played in by teammate Cheikh Thior, Ndiaye took a sharp first touch, danced inside his marker, and fizzed an unstoppable strike low into the bottom corner to hand the Young Lions a deserved lead.

Although Tajikistan got themselves a a 1–1 draw via Mehrubon Odilzoda’s 60th-minute penalty following a handball, Ndiaye’s strike proved vital in booking Senegal’s place in the knockout stage.

Fabrizio Romano has confirmed that a verbal agreement has been reached with the player, giving the deal his trademark ‘here we go’.
