'Incredible' Moment Bruno Fernandes 'Assisted' His Own Man Utd Goal

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

It was a frustrating night for Manchester United in the Champions League, as the English side were held to a 2-2 draw against Sporting CP.

After losing his first outing against Liverpool, Mauricio Pochettino was unable to get a win in his next game despite having the lead late on. Luis Suarez opened the scoring in the first half, bagging a penalty after missing another one just three minutes prior.

The Red Devils won their own penalty after the break, with Lisandro Martinez fouled and Bruno Fernandes stepping up from 12 yards. The club captain then put Man Utd ahead, scoring a strange goal just two minutes later.

With 10 minutes to play, Senne Lammens then made a huge blunder, dropping a simple catch, allowing Suarez to acrobatically score an equaliser. The game ended 2-2, with both teams walking away with a point.

While there is much to talk about in terms of Man Utd's overall performance, there was one specific moment in the game which has separately generated a lot of interest.

Having scored his penalty, Fernandes then took a speculative shot on goal only moments later. His left-footed strike from all of 25 yards didn't look likely to trouble Kaique Pereira, but it somehow beat the Sporting goalkeeper.

Replays showed that the ball had hit a divot in the Estadio Jose Alvalade turf, causing Fernandes' shot to pop up and fly into the back of the net. On the commentary for TNT Sports, Ally McCoist summed things up, stating:

"That's extraordinary, it really is. That's incredible."

The Man Utd goalkeeper was poor, but another played has drawn the anger of his own support.

After the match, the TNT Sports pundits then analysed the goal with new footage showing exactly how the goal came about. Incredible footage captured what happened to the turf, showing that Fernandes had managed to make a divot in the ground just seconds before taking on the strike.

The Man Utd captain surely didn't do it on purpose, but because of his prior ground scuffing, he essentially managed to assist his second goal. Indeed, were it not for the disruption to the turf, his effort surely would never have beatenthe Sporting goalkeeper.

Pundit and former Man Utd star Owen Hargreaves joked:

"This is the assist of the season. The best assist I’ve never seen."

Hargreaves went on to add: "What a bounce that is; the goalkeeper's got absolutely no chance. The one thing I would say is, watch this bounce... unbelievable, it's like a golf divot."

Fernandes certainly had a huge slice of good fortune with his goal, but Man Utd weren't able to make the most of it, conceding a preventable equaliser. They face RB Leipzig next time out in the Champions League.
