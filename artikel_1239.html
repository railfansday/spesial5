Report: Lookman price tag set by Atletico as Roma monitor closely

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Atlético Madrid are prepared to sell Ademola Lookman in January, less than a year after signing him from Atalanta.

According to Spanish outlet Fichajes, via CalcioMercato, the Nigerian forward is no longer considered untouchable by Diego Simeone, and the club would listen to offers. They won’t let him go cheaply, though. Atlético want a permanent sale worth at least the €35-40m they paid Atalanta last February.

Lookman is currently sidelined with a knee sprain picked up on international duty with Nigeria. The Super Eagles’ staff initially thought it was minor, but tests in Madrid showed a more serious problem, although the ligaments don’t appear to be involved. He is expected to be out until early 2027, which means he should return just as the January window opens.

Inter were previously strong admirers, but they now plan to spend their January budget on defence. That leaves Roma as the most likely Serie A destination.

Gian Piero Gasperini turned Lookman into one of Europe’s most dangerous forwards at Atalanta, despite their well-documented clashes over transfers. The Roma coach has been looking for a player with his profile since the summer.

Roma also have a good relationship with Atlético, having already done business with them over Nahuel Molina.
