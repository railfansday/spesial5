Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Put in for 25 at Fidelity, got 25. First time ever buying an individual stock, let alone an IPO. It's obviously play money, and I have plenty of concerns about AI in general, and Anthropic in particular. But Claude is a great product - maybe too great
There's no question Claude and its ilk are super useful and just amazing technology, but the question always is whether they will be good businesses. When Google went public in 2003, they were already in the black, $107 million in profits at 7% margin (https://www.reddit.com/r/dataisbeautifu ... venue_and/). In 2021, their margin was at 30%! I doubt even Brin and Page imagined their business would've become so robust.

So Anthropic is absolutely a shot in absolute darkness - vantablack, really. I'm letting my imagination get the better of me, but in my experience of the various LLMs, they are the best of the AI techs, and it seems to be that being the first and the best has its advantages, especially when it comes to this technology that moves so fast (i.e., the ones with the biggest lead will advance their lead at a faster rate).

The biggest fear is that they don't have other businesses like Google or Microsoft to provide continuous funding, so the danger is they'll become cash strapped and somebody else will come and buy them pennies on the dollar. Which would mean their stock would suffer accordingly.

Even though this isn't pets.com/eToys of the internet bubble, I'm not deluding myself that this is a wise monetary decision. This is pure speculation on a pure AI play.
