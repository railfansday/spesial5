Here is where the Lions fit in the NFC playoff picture after their Thanksgiving loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Detroit Lions' devastating and frustrating loss to the Chicago Bears drops them out of first place in the NFC North. It gives the Bears sole possession of first place in the division at 9-2, no matter what happens in the rest of Week 12. Detroit will hold at second place, even if the Minnesota Vikings defeat the Atlanta Falcons on Sunday.

Despite the disappointing outcome, the Lions are 8-3 and still control their own destiny in the division. With a win in over the Bears in Week 17, they can even the teams' record and tie the head-to-head tiebreaker. Detroit is currently ahead in the NFC North division record tiebreaker (2-1 vs. 1-2), so if Detroit runs the table or matches Chicago, the Lions get the tiebreaker.

Here's a full look at the NFC playoff picture following the Thanksgiving Eve game and the first Thanksgiving Day game:
