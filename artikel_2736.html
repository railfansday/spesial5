Taylor Swift’s outfit for Chiefs-Jets featured nod to Travis Kelce

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Taylor Swift was in attendance for the Kansas City Chiefs' game against the New York Jets in Week 9. Swift sat in a suite with longtime friend, model Karli Kloss, the wife of prospective Los Angeles Lakers owner Joshua Kushner. Swift's mother and mother-in-law were also in the suite.

Swift wore a Tommy Hilfiger sweater and a necklace with the No. 87. She has attended all the Chiefs' home games this season.

Speaking of No. 87, Travis Kelce scored a rushing touchdown in the game. Swift couldn't stay in her seat while waiting for the ruling on the field.

This was Kelce's third touchdown of hte season. He hadn't scored one since Sept. 27 against the Miami Dolphins. The star tight end also caught four balls for 72 yards as the Chiefs got back on track with a win following two straight losses.

The Chiefs won the game 35-12, outscoring the Jets 21-0 in the second half. The first half ended with a missed field goal attempt by Harrison Butker, which drew boos from the crowd. Presumably, everyone in the Swift/Kelce box clapped politely to encourage everyone.

With the Broncos also winning today, the Chiefs remain in second place in the AFC West. The Chiefs can tie the Broncos in the standing next week when Denver is on a bye and Kansas City travels to Atlanta to take on the Falcons.
