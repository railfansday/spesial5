Arch Manning will participate in Senior Day, but won't comment on his future

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Texas quarterback Arch Manning concludes his fourth slate of home games with the Longhorns on Saturday. Whether he’ll have another year there, or somewhere else, remains to be seen.

The only clue, if it even is one, comes from his decision to participate in the Texas Senior Day.

“I’m a senior, so I’m doing it,” Manning said, via the Associated Press.

So what’s next after he participates in the traditional farewell home game?

“I’m not really thinking about the future right now,” Manning said.

The future entails three primary options: (1) enter the draft; (2) return to Texas; or (3) transfer.

Manning started the year as the favorite to be the first overall pick in the 2027 NFL draft. That has changed.

Last year, he had 26 touchdown passes and seven interceptions for the Longhorns, who finished 10-3 for a team that did not qualify for the College Football Playoff. This year, Manning has 19 touchdown passes and 10 interceptions through 10 games. Texas currently is No. 6 in the CFP rankings.

After two straight losses (to Oklahoma and Florida), coach Steve Sarkisian didn’t rule out benching Manning.

Texas has since won four in a row, capped by a come-from-behind victory over LSU in which Manning threw three interceptions but also engineered the game-winning drive.

His uncles, Peyton and Eli, exhausted their full college eligibility before entering the NFL. Both were the first overall picks.

For Arch, the final decision may come down to whether he believes he’ll be taken No. 1 overall — and whether he can land with his preferred NFL team.

He could get creative, declaring for the draft and going through the process and returning to college if he decides he won’t be the first pick and/or can’t get to the team he wants. While it may require the hiring of a lawyer and/or the filing of a lawsuit to perform an about-face, the NCAA’s rules have been exposed as a house of well-worn cards in a wind tunnel.

Regardless, Manning has a business decision to make. And there will be many factors to consider, from when he’d be drafted to where he’d be drafted to how much money he could make by returning to Texas to how much money he could make by transferring to another school — like Ole Miss, where Uncle Eli and his grandfather, Archie, played.
