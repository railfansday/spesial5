Pistons vs Magic final score: Detroit’s dominant defense shows up with Cade Cunningham sidelined

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The Goin to Work Pistons showed up tonight. Detroit dispatched the Orlando Magic 104-86. This was another statement game. It doesn't matter who plays; defense travels. Detroit is now 2-1 in NBA Cup play with a plus-31 point differential. Orlando felt Detroit's defensive wrath, holding Orlando to 14 second-quarter points. They were everywhere.

Cade Cunningham was a late scratch, but the next man steps up here. Both teams struggled to put the ball in the hoop, but Detroit generated better shots. They swung the ball from side to side on plenty of possessions. The shot-making results weren't always there, but their process was solid. It was probably more than solid considering they were down three starters tonight.

Ausar Thompson and Daniss Jenkins picked up more on-ball reps. Jenkins had a 15-point and 10-assist double-double. Thompson continues to grow as a playmaker for others as he added seven more assists tonight.

Ausar seems much more sure of himself this season. Even the moments he turns down shots to find a teammate are telling — everything seems planned.

Ausar wasn't the only Piston with an all-around A performance. Kevin Huerter finished with 13-5-6. He worked the off-ball screen here, slightly froze the big, and threw an accurate lob to Paul Reed.

Isaiah Joe led the team with 21 points. He went 5-for-11 from trey. Joe showed off the range and confidence. He pressured the ball on the other end. Shooters competing on defense, and not being must-hide defenders, is such a plus. Joe is picking it up.

Ron Holland has success from deep. He buried two 3s. Detroit shot it well as a team despite their low point total. They went 15-for-36 from deep compared to Orlando's 12-for-36. John Collins, Woo, and Elijah Harkless (he made some momentum-swinging ones) all hit two 3s.

Detroit knocked down 3s but kept Orlando from doing anything. Franz Wagner had been on a hot streak until he ran into this defense (29 PPG over the last 10). He (20 pts) and Desmond Bane (21 pts) put numbers on the board, but it wasn't easy. Those two saw a crowd every time they drove the ball. Wagner started spamming push shots that felt automatic, but Detroit held him in check.

Anthony Black was a non-factor offensively tonight. That's a testament to Detroit's D, as he's been rolling all year. The Magic missed so many shots at the rim. They were initially dominating Detroit on the offensive glass, but those missed shots eventually turned into Detroit run-outs.

The Pistons also got their hands on so many Magic passes tonight. They made Orlando creators play with multiple hands in their faces and trusted their closeouts to disrupt "open" Orlando 3s. Holding a team under 90 in 2026 is some Goin to Work stuff. Hats off to J.B. Bickerstaff for having this defense ready to go at all times.

Detroit's final group play cup game is Friday versus the flaming hot Tyler Herro and the Milwaukee Bucks. Detroit could still win the group even after losing to the Toronto Raptors last Friday. They might need some help, but must start by handling their business with the Bucks.
