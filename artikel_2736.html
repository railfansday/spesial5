USA TODAY Sports Network Week 13 HBCU football rankings

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

South Carolina State remains the team to beat in HBCU football, holding the No. 1 spot in the final regular-season rankings after another dominant MEAC campaign.

The Bulldogs headline a top five that includes Prairie View A&M, Alabama A&M, Jackson State and Southern. Prairie View earned the No. 2 position after capturing the SWAC West, while Alabama A&M secured the East.

Arkansas-Pine Bluff made one of the biggest moves up the rankings after closing its season with a winning record. Morgan State and North Carolina Central also drew attention following their season-ending matchup, while Alabama State slipped after a loss to Prairie View.

USA Today ranked all 21 Division I HBCU football teams and where they stand in the 2026 season. Here's a look heading into Week 13.

The clear No. 1. The Bulldogs completed an undefeated MEAC season, won their third consecutive conference championship and finished the regular season 10-2. Their consistency and conference dominance separate them from the rest of the field.

The Panthers finished strong, defeating Alabama State 45-10 to secure the SWAC West. They also own a convincing 31-10 victory over Alabama A&M, giving them a strong argument for the No. 2 position.

The Bulldogs won the SWAC East and finished 9-3. Their 51-19 victory over Mississippi Valley State capped a strong regular season. Their loss to Prairie View is the biggest reason I put them behind the Panthers.

The Tigers closed with a 54-40 victory over Alcorn State. An 8-3 record and a strong offensive finish make them one of the better teams in the country, even though they missed the SWAC East title.

The Jaguars have put together a solid 6-5 season and finished the regular season with three consecutive victories before their scheduled Bayou Classic matchup. They have also beaten Prairie View, which strengthens their résumé.

The Golden Lions finished 7-4 and closed the regular season with a 42-17 victory over Texas Southern. Their winning record and competitiveness against the top SWAC West teams make them a legitimate top-10 team.

The Tigers are 5-5 and have shown they can compete with quality SWAC opponents. Their upcoming Bayou Classic matchup against Southern gives them one more opportunity to strengthen their résumé.

The Braves finished 6-6, but their overall body of work still deserves consideration. Their 54-40 loss to Jackson State hurts, and their 4-4 SWAC record reflects some inconsistency.

The Eagles finished 6-6 but went 4-1 in MEAC play. They defeated Morgan State 41-31 in their season finale, and their conference performance makes them the strongest MEAC team outside South Carolina State.

The Bears finished 6-6, but a 1-4 MEAC record is difficult to overlook. Their loss to North Carolina Central in the season finale keeps them below the Eagles.

The Hornets had a respectable 6-5 season and defeated Jackson State 20-13 the previous week. However, their 45-10 loss to Prairie View was a disappointing way to close their regular season.

The Hornets finished 6-6 and were competitive throughout the MEAC schedule. Their 26-16 loss to South Carolina State in the finale prevented them from finishing above .500 in conference play.

The Rattlers finished with a 27-14 victory over Bethune-Cookman in the Florida Classic. Although their 4-8 record isn't impressive, finishing with a rivalry victory gives them a boost.

The Aggies finished 5-7 and 4-4 in CAA play. They closed with a 21-18 victory over Albany, showing some resilience despite an inconsistent season.

The Spartans finished 5-7 and ended their season on a high note, defeating Howard 35-7. That head-to-head result gives them an argument to finish ahead of the Bison.

The Bison finished 5-7 and 2-3 in the MEAC. They had some notable moments, including an early-season victory over Alabama A&M, but struggled down the stretch.

The Tigers finished 4-8, including a narrow 17-16 victory over Gardner-Webb. Their overall record and 2-5 conference mark put them toward the bottom of the rankings.

The Wildcats finished 5-7 but lost their final game against Florida A&M. Their 3-5 SWAC record puts them ahead of the conference's bottom two teams.

The Tigers finished 3-8 and lost their final game 42-17 to Arkansas-Pine Bluff. They struggled to find consistency throughout the season.

The Delta Devils finished 3-9 and lost their season finale 51-19 to Alabama A&M. They showed some improvement, but struggled against the better teams in the SWAC.

The Pirates finished 1-11 and winless in CAA play. Their record puts them at the bottom of this ranking.

Gerald Thomas of the Tallahassee Democrat, and Tia Reid of the Clarion-Ledger contributed to the rankings.
