Arch Manning and Texas get more bad news

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Arch Manning and the Texas Longhorns have won back-to-back games to get their season back on track, but they've been piling up injuries along the way. Texas already lost star left tackle Trevor Goosby for the season last month, and several other key players are banged up heading into Saturday's road game against Missouri.

Now, the Longhorns have been dealt another significant blow as running back Hollywood Smothers, who opened the season as Texas' starter, will reportedly miss the rest of the year with a torn MCL, leaving an already struggling rushing attack even thinner.

Manning entered the season as a Heisman Trophy favorite, but back-to-back 27-24 losses to Oklahoma and Florida put him at risk of being benched. Head coach Steve Sarkisian even warned Manning that he would make a change if things didn't improve.

Texas responded with a 34-27 win over then-No. 7 Ole Miss on Oct. 24, but a key player went down during the game as Goosby was carted off in the first half with a knee injury and was later ruled out for the season, taking away the player responsible for protecting Manning's blind side. Goosby, a preseason first-team All-American, is widely considered the top left tackle prospect in the upcoming NFL Draft.

Texas was already shorthanded in that game, playing without pass rusher Colin Simmons and Smothers. Star wide receiver Cam Coleman also left the game with an injury.

The Longhorns followed that up with a 31-3 win over Mississippi State last Saturday, as Manning threw three touchdown passes. But the offensive line issues were obvious, as Manning was sacked six times, and Coleman left early for the second consecutive game.

"I think we still have better football out there that we have to go out and play," Sarkisian said after the win, via the Associated Press.

On Thursday, Manning and the Longhorns got even more bad news this season, as On3's Pete Nakos reported that Smothers will miss the remainder of the season.

Smothers returned against Mississippi State after missing the games against Tennessee and Ole Miss with injuries, but he had just four carries for -6 yards before suffering another injury that will now rule him out for the remainder of the season. He has finished the year with 283 rushing yards and two touchdowns in six games.

The NC State transfer had some big moments early in the season. In Texas' 24-23 upset of then-No. 1 Ohio State, Smothers rushed for 74 yards and scored two touchdowns on the Longhorns' final two drives to complete a fourth-quarter comeback.

Smothers was one of the top running backs in the transfer portal after rushing for 939 yards and six touchdowns at NC State last season, earning first-team All-ACC honors. He initially committed to Alabama before flipping to Texas.

To make matters worse, the No. 7 Longhorns could be without several other key players against No. 18 Missouri. Simmons, Coleman, fellow starting wide receiver Ryan Wingo, and nickel corner Graceson Littleton are all listed as questionable heading into the game.

Needless to say, Manning and the Longhorns will have to find a way to keep winning with a lot less help and their playoff hopes hanging by a thread.
