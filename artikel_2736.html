Brock Bowers injury: Latest update for Raiders tight end ahead of Broncos game

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

One week before facing the Denver Broncos in Week 11, Las Vegas Raiders star tight end Brock Bowers suffered an ankle injury during a 21-13 loss to the Seattle Seahawks on Sunday.

The injury "is not believed" to be major, according to NFL Network's Ian Rapoport. So it doesn't sound like the tight end will be out for an extended period, but he will be "day-to-day." Bowers is set to undergo more tests today to reveal the full severity of the injury.

The Raiders are set to face the Broncos at Empower Field at Mile High six days from now.

Bowers was also sidelined earlier this year after undergoing a meniscus cleanup, missing two games. After that, he went seven straight games with at least 70 receiving yards before injuring his ankle on Sunday.

Bowers has totaled 226 receptions for 2,550 yards and 18 touchdowns through his first 37 games in the NFL, including 17 catches for 212 yards and two scores in four career games against Denver.

Las Vegas rookie quarterback Fernando Mendoza is set to make his second career start against the Broncos in Week 11, and it would be a big loss if Bowers is not available for that AFC West showdown.
