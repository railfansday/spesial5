IHSA football bracket: 2026 Illinois state playoffs semifinal scores

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The semifinals of the 2026 Illinois High School Association playoffs are set to kickoff on Saturday, Nov. 21.

All 16 spots for the state championships at Illinois State University are up for grabs, with title games scheduled for the Friday and Saturday after Thanksgiving at Hancock Stadium in Normal.
