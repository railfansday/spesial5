Monday Night Football: Bills LT Dion Dawkins, LG Alec Anderson are inactive vs. Vikings

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Bills will not have the left side of their offensive line when they play the Vikings to cap Week 9.

Both left tackle Dion Dawkins and left guard Alec Anderson are both inactive after they were both listed as questionable for the contest.

Dawkins (ribs) did not practice all week. Anderson (ankle) was listed as a limited participant on Saturday after two DNPs on Thursday and Friday.

Tylan Grable is expected to start at left tackle with Austin Corbett expected to start at left guard.

The Vikings will not have Kyler Murray (hamstring), with Carson Wentz taking over as the team’s starting quarterback. But Odell Beckham Jr. is active for the club after he was signed to the 53-man roster.

Murray, safety Jakobe Thomas, receiver Jauan Jennings, cornerback Chuck Demmings, safety Theo Jackson, and defensive lineman Caleb Banks are inactive for Minnesota.

For Buffalo, Dawkins, Anderson, receiver Skyler Bell, safety Jalon Kilgore, outside linebacker Mike Danna, and defensive tackle Greg Gaines are all inactive.
