Kenneth Walker, Mansoor Delane are questionable for Chiefs

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

The Chiefs officially list running back Kenneth Walker as questionable for Sunday.

Walker tweaked his groin sometime this week, coach Andy Reid said, and the fifth-year pro sat out Friday’s practice.

“We’re feeling it out just to see,” Reid said, via Nate Taylor of TheAthletic.com. “We’re not going to put him at risk. If he can go, he’ll go. If he can’t, he can’t.”

Walker leads the league with 109.0 rushing yards per game this season. He has 872 rushing yards with seven rushing touchdowns in eight games, and he’s caught 26 passes for 172 yards with four touchdowns.

Running back Jaydn Ott is also questionable with a hamstring injury.

Rookie Emmett Johnson will start if Walker can’t play.

The Chiefs also gave rookie cornerback Mansoor Delane (shoulder) a questionable designation, but he had a full practice Friday. Delane was questionable to play Week 8 with the injury and was ruled out last week. He was inactive for both games.

Delane has not played since an Oct. 25 loss to the Broncos.

The Chiefs ruled out offensive lineman Jaylon Moore (ankle), defensive lineman Peter Woods (neck) and defensive tackle Omarr Norman-Lott (knee). Norman-Lott is in his 21-day return-to-practice window.
