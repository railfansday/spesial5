Report Milan ready to sell Estupinan and Loftus-Cheek, loan young talent out

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Milan are expected to sell Pervis Estupinan and Ruben Loftus-Cheek in the January transfer window, with Turkish and English clubs interested, while Francesco Camarda and Christian Comotto are heading on loan.

The Rossoneri have stuttered so far this season and found significant differences between the first choice team’s performance and those who come in during squad rotation.

This has prompted three defeats out of four Europa League games so far, and coach Ruben Amorim is reportedly ready to cut some players loose.

Amorim had declared at the start of the season that he convinced Milan to keep hold of Estupinan when a move to Aston Villa had been practically agreed.

Now several months on, he seemingly regrets that decision and has given the all-clear for an exit, but the Premier League side is no longer interested.

A loan move to Fiorentina could be on the cards, although they hope to find a more permanent solution to make back some of that investment.

Loftus-Cheek is only under contract with the Rossoneri until the end of this season, but persistent injuries and poor performances should see him sold in January.

Coventry City had made an approach in the summer, and could well try again, but Besiktas and other Turkish teams are also options.

Meanwhile, young players Camarda and Comotto are clearly not going to get a chance to deliver, so will be sent out on loan in January to gain valuable experience.

Genoa are the most advanced in talks for midfielder Comotto, while Camarda has numerous proposals from Serie A, B, and even abroad.
