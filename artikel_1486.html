PSG: Benjamin Pavard raves about Lucas Hernandez

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Benjamin Pavard is full of praise for Lucas Hernandez. Asked by The Teamthe Inter Milan defender, who rubbed shoulders with his friend at Bayern Munich and the French team, underlines his competitive temperament and his daily investment. Despite the injuries that slowed down his career, the Parisian never stopped working to regain his best level. A determination that commands the respect of his former teammate.

“His return to the top? I’m not surprised. I know the efforts he makes every day to be ready when we call on him. In training, for example, he is always at full speed. He’s a huge competitor, confirms Pavard. You get the impression that he’s always there laughing, but when you have to get hurt, he’s always there. He gives his all, whether as a starter or if he only plays five minutes. He’s a guy who is always at the service of the collective and someone who is reliable”explained the player who passed through OM.
