Gary Neville is Wrong: Man Utd Must Sell Harry Maguire Before Matthijs De Ligt

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Manchester United are set to embark on a new era at Old Trafford under Mauricio Pochettino, after the Argentine officially became manager on Wednesday.

The 54-year-old replaces Michael Carrick in the dugout and leaves his role as USMNT manager on the back of an 8-0 aggregate win over Haiti in the Nations League, helping them qualify for the semi-finals.

His first game in charge of Man Utd will see him travel to Anfield to take on arch rivals Liverpool, but regardless of what happens in that game on Sunday, there are plenty of things that he must sort out before he can really revolutionise Man Utd and get the squad in the right shape.

The most important thing will be who is part of his squad. A number of players have been linked with moves away from the club, while it's inevitable that Pochettino will also want to bring in his own players to help him implement his style and system as quickly as possible.

A defensive rebuild is reportedly planned for the 2027 summer transfer window, with a number of players out of contract and others on the chopping block for a potential sale, as INEOS look to raise funds to be able to upgrade a defence which has conceded the most goals of any team in Europe's top five leagues.

Diogo Dalot has been heavily criticised in recent times, but surprisingly, club legend Gary Neville has come out in his defence in a recent podcast appearance on Stick to United, and claimed that a number of other players should be sold before him.

"Dalot isn't Denis Irwin, lets be really clear. They shouldn't even be mentioned in the same breath. But I'd be getting rid of a few before Dalot," he said.

"Dalot got booed at Old Trafford last week, and I was really surprised by that. I think he's such an easy target, and I know that's going to be unpopular. I look at full-backs really closely... I'd be getting Mazraoui well gone before him. I'd be getting [Lisandro] Martinez gone well before him. I'd be getting De Ligt gone well before him."

Mark Goldbridge then stepped in and stated that he disagreed with the comments on De Ligt, but Neville doubled down.

"He's got one big problem; he doesn't play football.

"If a player is injury-prone and doesn't play football, just because he hasn't played doesn't mean to say that he should go above a player who's playing every week and throwing his hand up.

"You want someone who is reliable and can play.

Neville's comments about De Ligt are surprising, especially considering the fact the Netherlands star is only just returning after a serious back injury which left him sidelined for almost an entire year.

Prior to that, De Ligt was establishing himself as a regular in the team. He played in all 13 Premier League games last season before picking up his injury against Crystal Palace, while he made 29 appearances in the Premier League in his debut campaign before that.

That shows that he is generally reliable and available, and one long-term injury shouldn't be enough for the team to give up on him. But more pertinent is the fact he's easily one of the better defenders at the club.

Physically, he has a similar profile to the much-maligned Harry Maguire, but he is more mobile and much better on the ball, while also being able to duel in battles with defenders and be dominant in the air. Add to the fact that he is still only 27, too, and it's clear that Maguire should be shown the exit door way before the former Ajax and Bayern Munich star.

Maguire is out of contract at the end of the season and his performances have already begun to dwindle. His form this season has been poor, and his lack of athleticism remains a huge issue. He's also on big wages, which would be easy to get off the books by letting his contract run down.

Neville is known to be a big fan of an English core, and it seems that he is letting that cloud his judgement. De Ligt is far superior to Maguire in every way as a footballer and defender, and if either of them are to leave in the summer, then it's a no-brainer.
