Championship: How Full Every Club's Stadium Has Been in 2026/27 Revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Although it is the second tier of English football, the Championship often ranks as one of the most competitive leagues in the world. For decades now, the division has consistently seen a number of teams battling to earn a place in the Premier League.

Alternatively, it is not just promotion that has teams vying for points. Ambition, of course, varies, but this ensures that even with sides contending for a mid-table position or simply to avoid relegation, there is a sense of competition across the entire league.

Many teams in the Championship have a fanbase of devoted supporters, who can often spur them on in moments where such noise is desperately needed. Transfermarkt have ranked the 2026/27 Championship teams based on stadium attendance, but which clubs see the most fans at their game based on how full their ground is against its capacity?

The team with the lowest attendance in the Championship based on how much of the stadium is filled with fans is Blackburn Rovers. According to Transfermarkt, only 47.2% of Ewood Park has been populated on average, though this is partly due to some supporters protesting against Venky's and their continued ownership of the club.

There is an increase of just under 20% between Blackburn and Cardiff City (66.6%), who rank one place above the South Yorkshire side. It must be noted that the Welsh side have the fourth-biggest stadium in the division, though.

Preson North End's Deepdale has just over 70% of its capacity consistently filled, slightly more than The Valley (69.3%), which, as home of Charlton Athletic, will be hoping to host Championship football for the unforeseeable future after years in League One.

Stok City's Bet365 Stadium (73.9%), Bristol City's Ashton Gate (79.4%) and Sheffield United's Bramall Lane (81.4%) are in 20th, 19th and 18th respectively. The Blades have had a slow start to the campaign as they look to return to England's top tier. As a result, it appears many of the club's fans are staying away.

Pride Park Stadium, home of Derby County, rounds out this section. The Rams' attendances are dwindling with every week that goes by following a horror start to the campaign.

Watford's Vicarage Road (83.2%) is slightly more filled than Pride Park on a game-by-game basis. 17,949 fans on average have turned up to Watford's matches this season, although that number could drop if their struggles continue.

Within three percent of Watford are, in ascending order, Toughsheet Community Stadium and Swansea.com Stadium, homes respectively to Bolton Wanderers and Swansea City. The latter have had a decent start to the Championship campaign and fans are supporting the club in their droves as a result. Bolton, meanwhile, maintain a stadium that is, in proportion to its capacity, 84% full, thanks to them being in the Championship for the first time since 2019.

Narrowly missing out on a place in the top 10 is the Riverside Stadium, home to Middlesbrough, Burnley's ground, Turf Moor, and Bimingham's St Andrews. Middlesbrough's stadium is 88.6% full on average thanks to the club's impressive start to the campaign.

Just cracking the top 10 are Queens Park's Rangers' MATRADE Loftus Road (90.6%) and West Bromwich Albion's The Hawthorns (91.1%). Both teams are considered outside to gain promotion to the Premier League this season but are hoping to defy the odds.

Southampton's St Mary's Stadium (91.2%) and Wolverhampton Wanderers' Molineux Stadium (94.9%) are ranked eighth and seventh respectively. Both clubs will hope to return to the Premier League this season, with Wolves looking to bounce back at the first time of asking.

Just missing out on the top five is Norwich City. It's perhaps a surprise that Carrow Road ranks sixth given the Canaries' turbulent start to the campaign. They are currently on a four-match winning streak, though, and their attendances will surely keep rising if their good form continues.

Millwall are a club generally considered to be on the up following their impressive Championship finishes in recent years. Of the 20,146 seats at The Den, only about 1,000 of them are generally empty on a game-by-game basis.

Portsmouth's Fratton Park (96.4%) just misses out on the top three. Pompey's fanbase turns up in its droves every week despite the club struggling in England's second tier over the past few seasons.

In third place is West Ham United's Stadium. It's mightily impressive that the London Stadium, one of the biggest grounds in English football, has been 97.8% full on average this season given it holds 62,500 people.

ProAmpac Stadium (97.9%), home of Championship newbies Lincoln City, is second. As per Transfermarkt, only around 250 seats are left empty for each game Lincoln plays at home. It must be noted, however, that the Imps have the second-smallest stadium in the league, with a capacity of just 10,780.

No ground has been filled more, based on capacity, than Wrexham, who enjoyed three successive promotions from the National League to the Championship from 2023-2025. Their ground, which is the smallest ground in the division with 10,700 seats, is virtually full whenever they play.
