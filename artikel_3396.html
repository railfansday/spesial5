Slayyyter Goes Looking for Love in ‘Hey Boyyy’ Music Video

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Slayyyter dropped a new single, “Hey Boyyy,” along with a self-directed music video for the track. The clip sees the singer in Hollywood and Paris, dragging a golden skeleton across the city landscapes.

“I can’t stop blushing/ I’m too old to feel, more than nothing,” Slayyyter sings on the track. “Hey, boy, I got a funny little secret/ I get so nervous when you talk to me.”

“Hey Boyyy” will appear on the singer’s forthcoming album, Wor$t Man in America, out Dec. 4. The LP is a follow-up to her 2026 breakout, Wor$t Girl in America, and features nine tracks that act as a “brother” album. She has teased the album with two previously released tracks: “Crank 2” and “Beat Up Chanel$,” which offered fresh takes on Wor$t Girl tracks “Crank” and “Brand New Chanel$,” respectively.

In a recent interview with Rolling Stone, Slayyyter explained the impetus for Wor$t Man in America, saying, “I want to live here for a little longer, so I’m kind of making an inverse album, if you will. Wor$t Girl in America followed Slayyyter’s 2021 debut, Troubled Paradise, and 2023 sophomore effort, Starfucker. Speaking to Rolling Stone, she described those as the “building blocks to figuring out my sound,” which finally cracked on Wor$t Girl. “It took me a long time,” she added. “This could have never been my debut album.”

In October, Slayyyter cancelled the remaining European and U.K. dates on her Wor$t Girl in the World Tour after doctors ordered her to take full vocal rest following being diagnosed with laryngitis and vocal cord tears. She is set to continue her tour in Australia in February and recently sold out a show at Madison Square Garden in New York City for June 21, 2027.
