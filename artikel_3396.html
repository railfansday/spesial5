David Walliams Named As UK Celebrity Accused Of Exploitative Sexual Activity With Teenagers

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

David Walliams, star of Little Britain and Britain’s Got Talent, has been named as the UK celebrity at the heart of allegations involving exploitative sexual activity with girls and young women.

Walliams, 55, fought to prevent ITV News and The Guardian from publishing a joint investigation into his behavior on the basis that it could lead to his suicide, but lost his bid for an injunction last week.

ITV News (full documentary below) and The Guardian have now published their findings, based on conversations with more than 100 sources, including Walliams’ friends, colleagues, former partners, and relatives. The women accuse Walliams of using his celebrity to prey on women as young as 15, making contact with them through work, including on Britain’s Got Talent, to pursue them for sexual purposes.

Deadline has contacted Walliams’ legal representatives for comment. In a statement to ITV News, Walliams denied all allegations against him. When trying to prevent publication, Walliams’ lawyers revealed that he had made two attempts to take his life because of the allegations.

“I am not a sexual predator… I have not broken the law,” Walliams said. “I am devastated that some of the women I met ten to twenty years ago have now expressed hurt or anger towards me.”

He continued: “Looking back, I realise that at times I behaved thoughtlessly towards other people I had casual sexual relationships with… I realise I did not always take people’s feelings properly into account. That is something I regret. And whilst a power imbalance is inevitable when one party is famous, abusing that power is something else entirely and it is something that I never did.”

A woman identified as Jade told ITV News and The Guardian that Walliams made contact with her on MySpace in 2007 after she commented on a photo. Jade was 15 at the time, while Walliams was in his 30s and starring in Little Britain.

After meeting in a Soho cafe, Jade accused Walliams of inviting her back to his house where he allegedly kissed and touched her. “He lifted my top up and sucked on my breast and then tried to put my hand down his trousers. That’s when I pulled away and said ‘no’. He had an erection at the time,” she said. Walliams strongly denied that he had any sexual contact with Jade.

A second woman, named as Alex, alleged that she had consensual sex with Walliams two years after first meeting him at a Little Britain fan event in 2004, when she was aged 15. Alex alleged that she was “groomed” by Walliams, claiming that the comedian phoned her days after their first meeting and offered to help her with industry work experience.

After remaining in contact, Alex alleged that she had sex with Walliams when she was 17, accusing him of failing to wear a condom. “I never said no, but I felt unable to say anything or have any control in that situation once I was with him,” she added. Walliams told ITV News that he recalled wearing a condom and that Alex was 18 when they had intercourse.

A third woman, named as Sarah, said she met Walliams at a recording of Britain’s Got Talent after he had been communicating with one of her friends online. He personally invited Sarah, then 18, to the next evening’s show, asking her to his hotel for something to eat beforehand. Two weeks later, Sarah and a friend visited his house, where she accused him of touching her leg and trying to kiss her. Walliams denied making sexual contact with Sarah.

Despite the injunction, Walliams’ connection to the allegations has been the subject of significant social media speculation for weeks.

The allegations are not the first time Walliams has been accused of misconduct. HarperCollins dropped the author and halted the publication of his back catalog following accusations about his behavior being published in The Daily Telegraph.

Separately, Deadline revealed in September that YMU was no longer actively representing Walliams and his profile has been removed from the agency’s website.

Walliams is an established presence on British television and beyond, having created and co-starred in Little Britain with Matt Lucas in the noughties. They have made attempts to revive the BBC comedy and even spoke to Netflix about a new series in 2020.

Walliams has appeared in series, including Doctor Who and Agatha Christie adaptation Partners in Crime, and had roles in movies including Murder Mystery and Stardust.

He served as a judge on Britain’s Got Talent for a decade alongside Simon Cowell, but left the show under a cloud after comments he made about contestants were leaked to The Guardian in 2022. In one example, he called someone a “c***.” Walliams later apologized for his comments.
