‘The Comeback King’ Trailer: Glen Powell Is a Cancelled Country Star in Judd Apatow’s Latest Directing Project

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

A celebrity trying to rebuild their career after a public cheating scandal manages to feel both timely and timeless. The topic is certainly in the news right now, but it’s a safe bet that there will be a similar new scandal when “The Comeback King” hits theaters this February. And Universal is hoping that audiences will turn out for a healthy dose of celebrity schadenfreude.

Judd Apatow‘s new film stars Glen Powell as a rising country superstar who happens to be dating the world’s biggest pop icon (Madelyn Cline). But when a snag in their relationship goes public when they start litigating their drama through music — their fans disagree about who cheated on who — he’s forced to step out of the spotlight and rebuild his career from the shadows. “The Comeback King” is the first scripted film that Apatow has directed since his 2022 COVID satire “The Bubble.” Since then he has filled his time directing documentaries about comedy legends including “George Carlin’s American Dream,” “Mel Brooks: The 99 Year Old Man,” and “Paralyzed by Hope: The Maria Bamford Story.”

Apatow and Powell co-wrote the film and produce alongside Kevin Misher, Dan Cohen, Barry Mendel, and Josh Church.In addition to Powell and Cline, “The Comeback King” features an eclectic ensemble cast that includes Cristin Milioti, Madelyn Cline, Stavros Halkias, Li Jin Hao, Leanne Morgan, Kumail Nanjiani, Vanessa Bayer, Atsuko Okatsuka, Tig Notaro, Chris Fleming, Mike Birbiglia, and Big Fella. Plenty of country stars also make appearances, including Roll, Brad Paisley, Miranda Lambert, Shaboozey, Chris Stapleton, Brandi Carlile, Luke Bryan, and Reba McEntire.

The film’s release will be accompanied by a soundtrack that features Powell and Cline performing their characters’ fictional songs from the movie.

“The Comeback King” is part of a packed slate for Powell that also includes J.J. Abrams’ “The Great Beyond,” an untitled Lou Gehrig biopic for Richard Linklater, and his ongoing role in “Chad Powers.” Cline will next be seen in Marc Webb’s “Day Drinker” alongside Johnny Depp.

Universal Pictures is set to release “The Comeback King” in theaters on Friday, February 5. Check out the trailer below.
