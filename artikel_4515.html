French-made enameled cast iron at 61% off

Flip the lid of the Staub 4-quart round Dutch oven and you’ll see rows of small spikes cast into the iron. They catch rising steam during a braise and drip it back over the food so roasts stay moist. The French-made pot’s black enamel interior allows for searing meat before the lid goes on. 4 quarts hits the right size for soups and stews in a small household. Huckberry’s $230 markdown applies to the black finish, and Staub ships it directly, so expect a slightly longer delivery window and possible extra shipping charges at checkout.

Dry-finish Scottish waxed canvas at 30% off

Huckberry’s signature Flannel-Lined Waxed Trucker is sitting this sale out, which makes the Flint and Tinder James Waxed Snap Jacket the best way into the brand’s waxed canvas lineup right now. It’s cut from Halley Stevensons dry-finish waxed cotton canvas, so it sheds light rain and builds a patina over time without feeling tacky out of the box. A lightweight cotton lining and snap front keep it easy to layer over a flannel or hoodie. Select colorways drop to $159, while others sit at $182.

Eighteen tools with replaceable wire cutters

I carry a similar tool every day and the pliers get tons of use. This multitool includes17 additional implements, including spring-action scissors, bit drivers and replaceable wire cutters you can swap out once they dull. The four outside-accessible blades (plain knife, serrated knife, saw and file) open one-handed while the pliers stay folded, and every tool besides the plier head locks into place. It comes with a 25-year warranty.

Open-ear audio that keeps traffic audible

The Shokz OpenRun Pro 2 rests on your cheekbones in front of your ears so you can hear cars and cyclists while you run. To fix the thin low end that plagues most bone-conduction sets, Shokz pairs the bone driver with a dedicated air-conduction speaker for bass. The company lists up to 12 hours of playback per charge, with a five-minute USB-C top-up good for about 2.5 hours, and an IP55 build for sweat and rain. The original OpenRun Pro is the top pick in our best bone-conduction headphones guide.
