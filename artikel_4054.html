The Rezi 12 shotgun in Modern Warfare 4 is one of the game's best, especially with the right build

The best Rezi 12 builds in MW4 are all about doubling down on the classic strengths of a shotgun: namely the ability to end a foe with a single close-range blast, not bothering too much with accuracy and instead running around cutting through opponents. The Rezi 12 is currently the best shotgun in Modern Warfare 4, and if you make the right sort of build, you can turn it into an absolute killing machine.

For more info on deadly loadouts, check out our list of the best MW4 guns!

Though I've played with a few Rezi builds, this one remains easily the best one in my mind, one based around hipfiring with the Apex Enforcer mod, allowing for explosive slugs and pump-action cycling. This is a build for close-quarters encounters, moving rapidly, and hip-firing. The Heavy Silencer and Heavy Long Barrel are here to ensure maximum impact and effectively guarantee that any shot from the torso up will kill the opponents you encounter.

Meanwhile, the stock (or lack thereof), Assault Grip and Laser are all here to maximize hip fire accuracy and ease of use. This is a build where you fire the moment you see an enemy in close range, and works well as a secondary weapon in an assault loadout, with an AR as your primary.
