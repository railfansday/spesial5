Buurman’s late goal earns Chelsea Champions League win over Paris Saint-Germain

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

To help deliver timely coverage of women's football, we may use AI tools to support content creation and editing. While we strive for accuracy and editorial quality, readers should independently verify important information.

Lucy Bronze supplied the assist on Wednesday 18 November as Chelsea broke a goalless deadlock with three minutes of normal time left in the fifth round of the league stage. Bompastor’s side had 71% of the ball in Staines but went in level at half-time.

The winner was the only goal of a one-sided evening. Paris Saint-Germain, coached by Paulo César, defended for long spells and were still level going into the closing stages.

The numbers show how much of the game Chelsea controlled. They completed 501 accurate passes from 578 attempted, while the visitors managed 174 from 238. Chelsea also won 10 corners to 3 and took 26 shots in total.

Fourteen of those attempts came from inside the box. The breakthrough still took until the dying minutes.

Bronze’s evening did not end with the assist. Two minutes after the goal she was booked for a foul, the only card shown by referee Maria Sole Ferrieri Caputi.

Chelsea put 14 shots on target, and Paris Saint-Germain made 7 saves to keep the score down. On expected goals, Chelsea’s 2.75 dwarfed the visitors’ 0.64, a gap that suggests the winning margin could easily have been wider.

The visitors were not without ambition. They had 17 attempts of their own, 4 of them from outside the area, and Chelsea needed 3 saves at the other end to protect their clean sheet.

Chelsea have now gone three games without conceding. Before this win they beat Manchester City 1-0 and drew 0-0 with Häcken, and they are unbeaten in their last six matches in all competitions.

The head-to-head record is just as one-sided. Chelsea have won all three meetings with Paris Saint-Germain, after a 1-0 win away and a 3-0 victory at home in 2022, scoring five goals and conceding none.

Paris Saint-Germain have won 2 of their last 6 in all competitions, against Le Havre and Marseille, and lost to Bayern Munich in that spell as well as here.

Both sides are back in league action quickly. Chelsea travel to Liverpool in the WSL on Saturday 21 November, while Paris Saint-Germain host Saint-Malo in Division 1 Féminine on the same day.
