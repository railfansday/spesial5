Buy Travel Guard within 15 days of your first trip payment if a pre-existing condition, like heart disease or diabetes, could cancel your trip. Its three plans with cancellation coverage then cover that condition at no extra cost, as long as you insure all your nonrefundable trip costs and are well enough to travel when you buy.

Travel Guard, which Zurich Insurance Group bought from AIG in 2024, sells three such plans: Essential, Preferred, and Deluxe. Without the pre-existing condition waiver, a plan won’t pay for a condition, yours or a relative’s, that worsened, needed treatment, or changed medication in the 180 days before you buy (90 on Deluxe).

Travel Guard suits travelers with a chronic condition, or a relative with one, who can buy within 15 days of their first trip payment, and parents bringing a child 17 or younger. A traveler with no such condition in the family gets nothing from the waiver and should first check the trip cancellation coverage on the card they booked with.
