Because of Lammens: Nobody is Talking About How Bad Mason Mount is for Man Utd

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Manchester United turned in a strong performance but were held to a 2-2 draw by Sporting CP in the Champions League on Wednesday night, as Mauricio Pochettino continues to search for his first win as boss.

The Red Devils conceded two penalties in two minutes in the first half, with Senne Lammens saving the first attempt before being beaten by Luis Suarez. Bruno Fernandes scored twice in the second half to give the side the lead, before Lammens committed a huge error by dropping a cross, which allowed Suarez to score a second.

United were aggrieved by the referee's performance after he controversially disallowed two goals and sent off Youri Tielemans late on, but the performance of the goalkeeper is what has largely grabbed the headlines.

The Belgian has struggled for form this season, with the club keeping just one clean sheet in all competitions, and fans have begun to question whether or not he should be keeping his place in the side for the upcoming game against Brentford.

Lisandro Martinez is another player who has received plenty of criticism this season for his performances, and he was involved in conceding a penalty against Sporting, although replays suggest it shouldn't have been given, while he also won the penalty that Fernandes converted to equalise.

Another player who has been criticised heavily is Diogo Dalot, and there are a number of players who fans believe shouldn't be in the side.

But one player who seems to continue to escape criticism is Mason Mount.

The former England international joined the club from Chelsea in a deal worth an initial £55m back in 2023 but has managed just 8 goals in 84 appearances. Of those 84 appearances, only 35 have been starts, and he is yet to register a single assist in the Premier League or in European competition.

He also came on as a substitute in Pochettino's first game against Liverpool and immediately gave the ball away, which led to a goal, and he came on in the final moments against Sporting to do very little. Yet somehow, there is no mention of the fact he is still part of the squad.

With talks about the squad being rebuilt under Pochettino, and INEOS looking to trim the wage bill as usual, there should be no doubting that Mount should be on his way out of Old Trafford.

When the deal was done four years ago, there were question marks even then about why United were signing him and why they wanted him. Since joining, his injuries have been a problem, he barely contributes on the pitch, and he earns a huge amount of money every week.

The midfield is strong without him, and the forwards the club has are all better than him. There's simply no need for him to be at United, and the club should be looking to get rid of him as soon as possible to help raise some funds, and freshen things up.

Because players on the pitch are underperforming and being criticised, he is getting away with it. But that can't happen, and he shouldn't be at the club beyond 2027.
