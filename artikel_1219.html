Bruno Fernandes Next Club: 10 Favourites to Sign Man Utd Ace

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

For a long time now, Bruno Fernandes has been Manchester United's best player. This was no more evident in 2025/26, when he broke the Premier League assist record (21) and was named the English top-flight's Player of the Season.

So far in 2026/27, though, he's not come close to hitting that same level. 11 games in and the Red Devils have won just one league game, leaving them 19th with eight points to their name. Michael Carrick has been sacked and replaced by Mauricio Pochettino, but the club remains in crisis mode with the Argentine thumped 4-0 by Liverpool during his first game in charge.

Not helping the glum mood at Old Trafford, Fernandes' current contract expires this summer, and he seems on the fence about signing a new deal. He confirmed that despite being offered new terms, "no negotiations" are currently happening.

Consequently, the 32-year-old could push for an exit this summer. The club do have an option to extend for a year, but if Fernandes is unwilling to commit, it doesn't bode well for his long-term future.

As per Sky Bet, the good news is that – despite all the uncertainty – Man Utd are still the favourites to have Fernandes at the start of next season​​​​​​, with current 1/2 odds. However, 10 more teams have been listed as having a shot at landing the Portuguese midfielder.

Real Madrid are one of the biggest teams in the world and are almost always in the conversation when a high-profile player is on the move, especially if their contract is running down. Los Blancos have picked up Liverpool duo Trent Alexander-Arnold and Ibrahima Konate as free agents in recent seasons and could look to do the same with Fernandes. The Portugal connection with Jose Mourinho in charge could also help things.

Barcelona also have 33/1 odds of signing Fernandes. Like Real Madrid, they too will always be in the market for elite talent, but it remains to be seen if Hansi Flick really would want to bring the Man Utd midfielder into his team. With the likes of Lamine Yamal, Raphinha, Pedri, Gavi, Dani Olmo, and others, it's not as if Barca are short on attacking quality.

The La Liga side are paying some of their players very handsomely

Now, a move to Chelsea certainly would be controversial. Still, Juan Mata famously swapped Stamford Bridge for Old Trafford, and perhaps fellow creative spark Fernandes could follow that route but in reverse. If nothing else, a team including Cole Palmer, Morgan Rogers and Bruno certainly would be fun to watch.

Paris Saint-Germain have almost stockpiled elite attacking talent in recent times, but they may well be in the market for another goal-threat signing before long. Indeed, both Desire Doue and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia are out for the long term with ACL injuries. Although not like-for-like, perhaps a shock move for Fernandes could solve some of their issues.

Now in his 30s, Fernandes is remarkably yet to win a league title in his career. Perhaps a move to 21-time Serie A winners​​​​​​​ Inter Milan could help him put an end to that particular frustration. He has good experience of Italian football as well, having played for Novara, Udinese and Sampdoria in the early stages of his career

Bayern Munich chief Karl-Heinz Rummenigge has recently sparked outrage for his comments about Jamal Musiala's uncertain future in Germany amid his ongoing health issues. By the sound of it, the Bundesliga outfit could soon be in the market for a new attacking midfielder. If this is the case, their eyes may well turn toward Man Utd's captain.

It's no shock to see Fenerbahce make this list of potential suitors​​​​​​. With the likes of Ederson, Nathan Ake, N'Golo Kante, and Romelu Lukaku on their books, the Turkish club is not shy when it comes to snapping up former Premier League stars slightly past their peak. This move makes a lot of sense.

Galatasaray also make the top five of likely destinations for Fernandes. It's interesting that they are given even better chances of getting a deal done, with 7/1 odds. Davinson Sanchez, Leroy Sane, Ilkay Gundogan, and Lucas Torreira make up some of the familiar names in the squad. Perhaps Bruno would be enticed by the idea of linking up with Victor Osimhen and Rafael Leao.

Fernandes moved to Italy way back in 2012, signing for Novara and breaking into the Serie B side's senior team. AC Milan would represent a return to the country that gave him his first real opportunity in football, and the chance to either take over from legendary midfielder Luka Modric or perhaps even play alongside the timeless 41-year-old.

The most likely destination for Fernandes, should he opt to leave Man Utd, is the Saudi Pro League. While no single clubhas been singled out, a move to the Middle East is touted as a probable outcome. Fellow Portuguese star Cristiano Ronaldo set the trend with his move to Al-Nassr, and many other stars have followed since. At 32, it will be interesting to see if the Man Utd captain is ready to leave Europe.
