UCL | Inter 2-0 VfB Stuttgart: Sucic and Lautaro Martinez revive hope

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Inter managed to get the better of VfB Stuttgart and leapfrog them in the Champions League table thanks to Petar Sucic and Lautaro Martinez.

The Nerazzurri lost their first two Champions League games, then won back-to-back against Shakhtar Donetsk and Feyenoord. Hakan Calhanoglu and Ange-Yoan Bonny missed out, with Lautaro Martinez only fit for the bench after more than a month on the sidelines. Manuel Akanji returned in defence after Sunday’s chaotic 4-4 Serie A draw away to Atalanta.

VfB Stuttgart were one point in front of Inter going into this clash, with Angelo Stiller, Leo Sauer and Luca Jacquez back in the squad after injury.

It was an aggressive start from the Germans, as Maximilian Mittelstadt flashed a ball across the face of goal from the left, then Petar Sucic charged down the Ermedin Demirovic strike.

Josep Martinez needed a big save to deny the ferocious Chris Fuhrich strike at the near post, while Djed Spence wasted a very promising counter-attack from the centre-circle, firing straight at the on-rushing goalkeeper.

Inter threatened from a corner, flicked on by Yann Bisseck just beyond both Pio Esposito and Manuel Akanji, and another of the defender’s headers on a set play was charged down.

A well-worked move fell apart when Henrikh Mkhitaryan’s first touch let him down meeting the Marcus Thuram roll across from the left, while Ramon Hendriks had to make a perfect tackle on Spence inside the area.

Finishing from Bisseck and Sucic left something to be desired, but Inter were forced into a change when Alessandro Bastoni limped off with a left thigh injury. Federico Dimarco came on, moving Carlos Augusto to central defence.

The hosts had two huge chances to score in the space of three minutes. First, Marcus Thuram ran onto a misplaced pass only to drill it wide of the near post, then Barella’s curler off the outside of the boot bounced off the upright and Dennis Seimen clawed the rebound off the line.

Thuram also saw his strike deflected over the bar, but Nartey blasted just over from the edge of the area after the restart.

There was finally a breakthrough when Vagnoman charged down the Dimarco cross with a raised arm, spotted by VAR, and Petar Sucic kept his cool to convert the penalty.

VfB threatened from a corner kick, Bisseck getting himself in the way of the Nartey effort from point-blank range, while at the other end Dimarco’s deflected free kick was acrobatically palmed out from under the crossbar.

Carlos Augusto made a desperate interception to prevent Demirovic turning in at the back post, while Jamie Leweling’s curler was just over.

However, Inter doubled their lead when Pio Esposito let the Dimarco cross through with a dummy, allowing Lautaro Martinez to take a touch and fire in with the right foot.

Akanji also required a crucial block as Bilal El Khannouss burst into the box and got the shot away, while in stoppages Lautaro Martinez’s strike was charged down by a defender’s face.

Sucic pen 57 (I), Lautaro Martinez 74 (I)
