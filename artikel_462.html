Mint Mobile has slashed its Unlimited plan by 50% for Black Friday — how to claim

TL;DR: New customers can lock in Mint Mobile's Unlimited plan for just $15 per month (down from $30) across three, six, or 12-month terms. Plans start at $45 upfront.

Most holiday phone promotions come with a catch: trade in your current device, agree to credit checks, and lock yourself into a bloated 36-month carrier contract just to save a few bucks. Mint Mobile is taking the opposite route for Black Friday.

Right now, the carrier has dropped every single plan in its roster down to $15 per month. All plans are on sale, but the Unlimited tier is the main event: you get half off the usual $30 price tag, and you don't even have to trade in a phone.

Even better, Mint isn’t capping this promo at a standard 90-day trial. You can lock in the $15 monthly rate for three, six, or 12 months.

Every Mint Unlimited plan includes unlimited talk and text nationwide, and your data stays on with zero hidden fees. You get 50GB of full high-speed data each billing cycle. If you manage to blow past 50GB in per month, you won't get cut off — your speeds may just slow down temporarily in crowded areas when the network gets busy.

Besides that, the Unlimited tier includes 20GB of dedicated mobile hotspot data each month, making it easy to tether your laptop or tablet on the go without hogging up your primary phone data.

Mint operates on an upfront bulk-pricing model. Instead of dealing with unexpected monthly invoices, you pay for your selected term at checkout:

If you commit to the 12-month tier, you are essentially getting an entire year of unlimited wireless service for $180 before local taxes.

Here are the simple steps you need to take to claim:

Choose the Unlimited plan and pick your duration (three, six, or 12 months).

Check your device compatibility, choose between an instant digital eSIM or a physical SIM kit in the mail, and complete checkout before the promotion ends on Feb. 4.

You'll pay for your chosen plan duration upfront, with taxes and fees added at checkout. This offer is available exclusively to new customers for their initial plan term; after that term ends, standard full-price plan options will become available. Video streaming on the Unlimited plan runs in standard definition (SD).

If you're looking to slash your monthly phone bill without sacrificing uncapped data, this is as cheap as it gets. Head over to Mint Mobile to lock in your Unlimited plan today.
