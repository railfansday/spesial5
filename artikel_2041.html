Tanggal: 2026-11-12

West Ham’s goal keeper situation is developing into certainly a ‘drama‘ if not yet a a ‘crisis: Blame me for tempting fate with my earlier article concerning Areola’s scan and the opportunity that may represent for third choice ‘keeper Finn Herrick accordingly.

Bad enough that Areola has been sent for a scan (more on that later in this article..) leaving Hermansen as the out-of-form senior ‘keeper. But…..

No sooner had we written of the opportunity this presents for  third choice keeper Finn Herrick, than we hear on the grapevine that Herrick has been forced to withdraw from England’s u20 training camp and is returning to Rush Green. Reason as yet unspecified.

Problems galore for Scott Parker as Areola is not, apparently, match fit and now Finn Herrick has issues

Have a feeling that the truth will out during the next ten days ahead of West Ham’s resumption of hostilities in the championship next Saturday. Worrying times.
