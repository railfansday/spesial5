Skyrim Celebrates 15th Anniversary with Creation Club DLC Bundle, Big Sales, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

The Elder Scrolls 5: Skyrim is officially 15 years old today, having originally launched on the 11th November, 2011.

And no, Bethesda doesn't have a new re-release lined up or anything like that, so the memes will have to be put on hold for now.

But that doesn't mean the developer's forgotten about its most successful RPG. To celebrate the occasion, it's reduced Skyrim: Special Edition's price by 80% on PS5 and PS4, cutting it all the way down to $7.99 / £6.99.

Buyers can then upgrade to the Anniversary Edition, which, as far as I can tell, still costs $19.99 / £15.99. Anniversary Edition is the one that bundles all of the Creation Club stuff into the deal.

If you somehow haven't played Skyrim before, or you've just never got around to it on PS5, it's a decent discount — although it's also worth pointing out that the Special Edition is currently available through a PS Plus Extra subscription as well.

Next up, Bethesda's curated a Creations (premium mods verified by the developer) bundle for the anniversary. It throws together five of the most highly rated Creations available at a heavily discounted price.

If you were to buy all of these Creations separately, they'd set you back 3,000 credits — roughly $28 / £27, since you have to buy separate 'Creation Credits' packs.

This bundle is going for 1,500 credits, which amounts to buying 500 and 1,000 Creation Credits at $15 / £14.

Obviously Skyrim's fan-crafted DLCs aren't everyone's cup of tea, but some of them really do fall in line with what you'd expect of an official release. Legacy of Orsinium in particular is a borderline expansion, and it's got a lot of fans.

Beyond all of this, The Elder Scrolls Online is unsurprisingly getting in on the action. You can unlock Skyrim-themed rewards by taking part in the MMO's current 'Golden Pursuit' campaign.

"Fans can also expect a week full of giveaways, community spotlights, and social content," Bethesda's press release adds.
