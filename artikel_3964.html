Another Big PS5 Game Will Be Delisted in the Coming Months

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Exoprimal goes extinct like its dinosaurs

Capcom has announced it'll be delisting its multiplayer dinosaur shooter Exoprimal in March 2027, and then turning the game's servers off in September.

The news comes from an "end of service announcement" published on the official Exoprimal website, where it is said: "We cannot thank you enough for the love and support you have shown over the years, and we sincerely apologize for the gravity of this announcement."

As of today, 25th November 2026, the game's four Survival Passes have already been taken off the PS Store.

Then, on 30th March 2027, Exoprimal and all its DLC and any other content will be removed from all storefronts.

Finally, the "end of service" phase and complete shutdown of Exoprimal is scheduled for 29th September 2027.

"Exoprimal will no longer be playable after the end of service," Capcom confirms. While the game did receive a physical release on a disc, it requires an internet connection to servers in order to play. With those gone, Exoprimal will stop functioning no matter whether you own it physically or digitally.

Capcom has enjoyed an incredible PS5 generation with multiple critically-acclaimed releases, but Exoprimal is one of the few titles it put out that fell below the standards it set. That's not to say Exoprimal was bad, though — far from it.

In our own Exoprimal PS5 review, we awarded it a 7/10 and called it a "white-knuckle fusion of Overwatch and Gears of War". However, competition in the multiplayer space is strong, and it appears the game didn't do enough to sustain a big enough playerbase for Capcom to justify keeping the servers turned on.

Now, in less than a year, Exoprimal will be delisted and rendered completely unplayable.

The game is still full price on the PS Store, but between now and March 2027 (when the delisting occurs), I do imagine Capcom will hand it some steep discounts so those still curious can try it for cheap.
