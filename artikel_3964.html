Never mind no cheeses for us meeces, there may not be many Muppets Christmas Carol goodies at the Disney Store by the time the holiday season arrives; a few of the more popular items are already out of stock.

We've already talked about being fans of the range when it went on early access a little while back, so I'm not massively surprised that some of the Muppets Christmas Carol merch is beginning to sell out. It's full of fan service and feels considerately designed, with options across a wide range of price brackets and styles from jumpers to plush toys. I get it.

However, that does mean you may need to dive in sooner than later if you wanted to add any of those items to your own collection. As a case in point, one of the jumpers and some themed slippers are already gone (which I'm genuinely gutted about, because the latter looked so cozy). I suspect this to become a recurring trend before long, because many of the US equivalents - including the eyecatching Loungefly bag - have completely vanished from the Disney storefront as well.

With that in mind, these are my favorite Muppets Christmas Carol goodies that are still in stock.
