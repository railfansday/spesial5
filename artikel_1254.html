Unai Emery Explains Why He Left Man Utd 4-4 Aston Villa Early

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Unai Emery's actions following Aston Villa's chaotic 4-4 draw away to Manchester United have caused a stir and he's been accused of poor sportsmanship.

Villa looked set to pick up all three points at Old Trafford heading into stoppage time until United came up with two goals in added time. Patrick Dorgu's 90+6th minute equaliser enraged the Spaniard, who marched towards the tunnel in disbelief.

Fans questioned whether Emery's reaction was down to a potential foul on goalkeeper Zion Suzuki, conceding two late goals or the amount of time added on. He gave a typically honest interview after the game to explain himself and reflect on missing out on a victory.

Villa twice took a two-goal lead in the early stages of the first and second half. Alysson struck in the first minute before Nicolas Jackson added a second three minutes later.

United responded through Bruno Fernandes' 16th minute penalty before Youri Tielemans scored against his former club in the 44th minute. Emery's men were quick out the gates yet again after halftime, with John McGinn netting a 48th minute penalty and Leon Goretzka also on the scoresheet five minutes later.

Villa failed to see out their win as Joshua Zirkzee and Dorgu denied Emery of an Old Trafford win. Emery's decision to walk off after the final goal raised question marks.

Emery addressed this while speaking to Sky Sports: "They scored in minute 96 and there were six minutes added. I thought the match was finishing. Not unfair - they scored the goal and I thought the match was over."

Emery has a storied history of his teams giving up leads away at Old Trafford as he has managed eight matches as a visitor. His team have had seven different leads against Jose Mourinho, Ole Gunnar Solskjaer, Erik ten Hag, Ruben Amorim and Carrick, failing to win any of those games.

The draw away to United means Villa sit 10th after as many games played, spurning the chance to move up to sixth. It came against a club whose co-owners reportedly view Emery as the ideal candidate to replace the under-pressure Michael Carrick.

Fans online slammed Emery for his poor show of sportsmanship, with one writing: “This is terrible sportsmanship from Unai Emery. Show some respect and decorum and atleast shake your opponents hands.”

Another supporter added: "This Unai Emery interview. headlosss."

Emery often wears his heart on his sleeve and his response to conceding late on, along with his somewhat hot-headed interview, are not unusual. He now has the international break to gather his thoughts and recharge for a busy winter period.
