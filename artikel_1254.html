Parma lose Romero for rest of 2026 after serious shoulder injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Parma are not expected to have centre-forward David Romero back in action before 2027, as he has been diagnosed with a serious shoulder injury.

The centre-forward fell awkwardly during the most recent Serie A match against Bologna and had to be substituted.

According to Sky Sport Italia, the player had specialist tests that confirmed this was not a normal dislocation.

It is an acromioclavicular subluxation and will require at least six weeks on the sidelines, meaning Parma won’t have Romero back before Christmas and the break for the holidays.

The 23-year-old Argentine was a big investment from Tigre over the summer at €7m and he had a good start, scoring two goals in nine Serie A appearances.

His absence could give Italy Under-21 international striker Simone Lontani more playing time at the Stadio Tardini.

Meanwhile, Diego Carlos was also diagnosed with a muscular strain in his left thigh following Saturday’s 2-0 friendly win over Cesena.

This puts him in doubt for this weekend’s return to Serie A action against Roma on Saturday evening.
