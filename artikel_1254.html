Ben Jacobs Shares Update on Liverpool Signing Lamine Camara in January

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Liverpool are looking to strengthen their squad in the January transfer window, and Ben Jacobs has shared an update on their interest in bringing in a midfielder.

The Reds made three signings in the summer by bringing in Ronald Araujo, Victor Munoz and Bradley Barcola, and the team have started the season well with the team sitting fourth in the Premier League, and having lost just three times in all competitions.

But Andoni Iraola is understood to be keen to strengthen his squad further for the second half of the season, and midfield additions are believed to be among the priority deals being targeted.

During the summer, the club were attentive to the situation regarding Lamine Camara, but a deal never materialised for them to make a move. However, GIVEMESPORT exclusively revealed that when Chelsea made a push to bring him in on Deadline Day, Liverpool made contact with the Senegal international to express their interest too.

Monaco eventually pulled the plug on his move to Stamford Bridge after the deadline had passed, refusing to put in a deal sheet, and speaking in his latest newsletter, GMS senior correspondent Ben Jacobs has now revealed that there is a chance the Reds make a move in January.

“And then just final word on Lamine Camara, like I said on deadline day exclusively, Liverpool were calling Camara on deadline day, and they were saying, 'If you don’t go to Chelsea, we could be there for you in 2027'," he said.

"And Liverpool privately, according to my information, were very glad that Camara and Chelsea fell through, and it seems like now Chelsea want Alex Scott. Unless they get a big concession on the deal, it might be difficult for Chelsea to go back to Monaco and sign Camara, because the club relationship is a bit broken because Monaco didn’t send that deal to a deal sheet.

"Chelsea were really disappointed by Monaco’s handling there, which means if they are to renegotiate for Camara and move away from Alex Scott, they would likely need Monaco to make a significant concession, and that club-to-club relationship is still very difficult.”

With lots of interest in Camara during the summer window, Monaco boss Filipe Luis was asked about his midfielder and the speculation around his future, where he admitted that he didn't want to lose him, while calling him 'one of the best midfielders in the world'.

"We are probably talking about one of the best midfielders in the world," he said.

"I don't want to talk about him too much, because I know a lot of clubs would love to have him in their squad, and he is very important to me.

"He has that quality on the ball, covers a lot of ground, he is truly top class! I am really happy to have him, and he is going to play a key role in the team.

"Keeping him? I am very hopeful he will stay. I think he is happy in Monaco. So if he stays, he can play a key role in my game plan."

With the Ligue 1 outfit currently struggling domestically this season, it's unlikely that they will want to sell mid-season. However, their financial situation means that a good offer may force their hand, and Liverpool have the cash to spend if they deem it necessary.

Chelsea are seemingly out of the race as they chase a deal for Alex Scott instead, leaving the Reds with a clear run to bring in someone they see as a top target.
