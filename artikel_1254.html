Man Utd Fans Slam ‘Embarrassing’ Player After 2-2 Sporting CP Draw

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

In their second game since Mauricio Pochettino took over from Michael Carrick, Manchester United took on Sporting CP. The Red Devils had to settle for a point in a 2-2 draw.

Having been thumped 4-0 by Liverpool in his first match in charge, the Argentine would have been desperate for a fast start in the Champions League. The first half, however, did not go to plan as the Red Devils conceded two penalties.

After Lisandro Martinez gave away the first, Senne Lammens made a save from 12 yards but then conceded another penalty only minutes later. This time, Sporting star Luis Suarez made no mistake, as he scored the second of his two spot kicks.

After the break, Martinez won the third penalty of the game, this time for the Red Devils when he was tripped in the box. Bruno Fernandes tucked home the first goal of the Pochettino era and then gave Man Utd a lead just two minutes later. His tame shot from the edge of the box took a strange deflection off a divot in the ground, and somehow found the back of the net.

United looked in control, but with 10 minutes to go, Lammens made a complete mess of claiming a cross, fumbling the ball. Suarez reacted brilliantly to acrobatically make it 2-2. Pochettino's men thought they'd taken a lead shortly after, but VAR intervened to rule out a goal after Kaique Pereira was deemed to have been fouled when trying to claim a corner.

There was then time for Youri Tielemans to get himself sent off late on for a second bookable offence, as the game ended 2-2. Man Utd fans will have to wait a little longer before seeing their first win under Pochettino.

While there was plenty of anger aimed at Lammens after the goalkeeper's errors, another player has also been blamed for the disappointing draw.

Despite Lammens having a horror display, giving away a penalty and then being at fault for the second goal as well, many Man Utd fans online instead turned their fury on Noussair Mazraoui. Starting ahead of the much-maligned Diogo Dalot, the Moroccan didn't fare any better.

Mazraoui lasted just 64 minutes before being replaced by the Portugal international. Unimpressed with his efforts, supporters on social media tore into the right-back, with one remarking:

"If Mazraoui starts next season with us, then I'll have no choice but to consider the Manchester United board shameless. Man has been dropping stinkers in almost every game."

Another added: "Dalot is worse than Mazraoui but Mazraoui is also worse than Dalot." While a third wrote: "Dalot in 5 mins has shown why he is far better than Mazraoui."

Someone added: "Mazraoui is trash guys we gave to accept it." Another summed up the mood, writing: "Mazraoui was not good enough for Bayern, what made the board think he’d be good enough for United?"

One more added: "Just keeps getting worse every week, Martinez & Mazraoui are embarrassing."
