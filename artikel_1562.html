Barcelona injury, suspension list and return dates vs. Sabah: Rodri, Andreas Christensen, Roony Bardghji latest

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

By Lewis Nolan | 23 Nov 2026 03:36, Last updated: 23 Nov 2026 03:38

Barcelona's quest for another Champions League crown continues when they face Sabah at Baku Olympic Stadium on Wednesday.

Despite sitting top of the La Liga table, Hansi Flick is dealing with numerous issues, including on the injury front.

Here, Sports Mole rounds up all of Barcelona's injury and suspension news ahead of their clash with Sabah.

Espart is out for at least another month or so due to an ankle injury; the youngster faces a battle to be unavailable before the winter break in Spain.

Bardghji was expected to leave Barcelona on loan during the summer transfer window before the news broke that he had suffered a torn ACL, which will sideline him for a significant period.

Possible return date: December 2 (vs. Atletico Madrid)

Rodri sustained a hamstring injury during the team's Champions League clash against Aston Villa on November 3, but he could be back on the field in early December.

Possible return date: November 24 (vs. Sabah)

Barcelona confirmed that Andreas Christensen has some discomfort in his left calf, which resulted in him missing his side's clash with Villarreal on November 22, though he has not been ruled out of his side's next Champions League game.

Barcelona have no players banned for this match.
