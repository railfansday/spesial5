Jamal Musiala and Dayot Upamecano have release clauses in Bayern contracts

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

According to Sky Sport Germany, a previously undisclosed release clause in Jamal Musiala’s contract has become a topic of internal discussion at Bayern Munich. Although the Germany international signed a long-term deal running until 2030, the report states that a release clause can be activated as early as the summer of 2027.

Musiala is apparently not the only senior Bayern player with such a provision. Dayot Upamecano is also said to have a release clause in his contract, which would reportedly become active from 2027 and is set at €65 million.

Musiala’s situation has even been discussed at supervisory board level in recent weeks. Internal conversations are reportedly centred on the balance between his salary and sporting output. The 23-year-old said to be among the club’s highest earners on a package worth around €25 million gross per year, including bonuses, while enduring a difficult period after spending time on the sidelines through injury and other health-related setbacks.

However, despite speculation generated by the leaked contract details, there are currently no signs of an imminent departure from Bayern. Instead, Musiala’s focus is said to be on returning to full fitness and re-establishing himself as one of the leading figures for the Bundesliga champions.
