Chelsea 1-4 Leeds: Dismal Blues roundly beaten as scrutiny on Xabi Alonso grows

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

Noah Okafor scored twice as Leeds beat Chelsea 4-1 to record their first Premier League win at Stamford Bridge in 27 years.

Daniel Farke’s team conceded six here earlier this season. That wild defeat in the Carabao Cup in September came at a time when Chelsea were playing cavalier football that made a 6-3 scoreline seem normal, but they stand now on the brink watching their season fall apart.

This was not what their supporters were sold when Xabi Alonso was appointed amidst broad smiles six months ago.

The leadership spoke then of lessons having been learned and of a corner turned. What must co-owner Behdad Eghbali and his four-strong team of sporting directors have made of Leeds running rings around their team on their own pitch, cantering to an easy victory with minimal resistance?

They began well here, 23-year-old striker Emmanuel Emegha firing them ahead early on his full debut, but within minutes they had gifted Okafor an equaliser, a lovely finish into the corner facilitated by static Chelsea defending.

Morgan Rogers might have been unfortunate to concede the penalty at the start of the second half, which was converted by the excellent Dominic Calvert-Lewin, but that decision was not the reason Chelsea were beaten so soundly.

Okafor quickly made it three for Leeds, Branden Aronson got a fourth in stoppage time, as Chelsea slumped into mid-table.

Leeds were quickly on the front foot and might have lead early. A wonderful cross from James Justin was met by Calvert-Lewin, ghosting between two defenders to head against the crossbar.

Moises Caicedo was forced off after 20 minutes after a landing awkwardly, arguably the point at which the hosts began to lose their way. Calvert-Lewin soon found room again amongst Chelsea’s defence for a header that was this time straight at Emiliano Martinez.

Emegha’s goal was Chelsea’s only bright spot. Justin made a mess of a clearing header and gave it to Rogers, who nipped past Nico Elvedi and slipped it left for Emegha to take a touch and finish like a striker that had been crashing them in at the Shed End for years.

The manner in which Chelsea let Leeds back level two minutes later will have infuriated Alonso. Josh Acheampong misjudged the bounce of the ball in midfield and Calvert-Lewin stole in and fed Okafor.

There were then three defenders in blue standing motionless around Okafor inside the box as he took aim and rolled the ball into the corner of Martinez’s goal.

A wonderful Calvert-Lewin back-flick was inches from putting Leeds ahead. A goal then would have not unfairly reflected the balance of the first half. Chelsea were a mess and were booed off at the break.

It took an age for VAR to determine that Michael Oliver had been correct to call handball against Rogers from Jayden Bogle’s driven effort in the 48th minute. Rogers had motioned towards the ball which cannoned away of a combination of upper arm and shoulder, but Chelsea felt aggrieved. From the spot, Calvert-Lewin sent Martinez the wrong way.

Okafor whacked in his second and Leeds’ third just before the half-hour, a deflected cross sitting up for a lovely half-volley into the top corner.

Now was the time for another two-goal Chelsea fightback as on that bizarre night in September. Predictably, it never materialised, only another goal from Aronson and more boos for Alonso’s players at the end.
