MN: Pavlovic returns to training after overcoming kidney stone bout – the latest from Milanello

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

There’s been some good news for AC Milan today, with Strahinja Pavlovic returning to training at Milanello today.

It’s been an up-and-down international break for Milan. There have been some solid performances from their representatives, but the news of Mike Maignan’s injury last night was not welcome.

With that said, they have had a bit of positive news regarding injuries and today and the latest update has arrived with the squad’s return to Milanello.

Earlier today, MilanNews reported that he was showing significant improvements after his struggles with kidney stones and they have now provided a separate update. Indeed, Antonio Vitiello has stated that he took part in an individual training session today.

Of course, there are not many players at Milanello currently, with 13 members of Ruben Amorim’s squad on international duty. So, the hope will be that he can return in a group setting when his teammates do.

The hope is that he’ll be back for the Frosinone game this weekend, and he could be joined by Fikayo Tomori, who had a check-up today after his thigh injury. Unfortunately, the Portuguese coach will have to wait a little longer for Matteo Gabbia’s return, with him due back in January.
