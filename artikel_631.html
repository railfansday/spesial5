Director Cédric Klapisch to Receive César Lifetime Achievement Honor

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The 'Spanish Apartment' and 'Call my Agent!' director will be honored at the 52nd César Awards on Feb. 25.

Cédric Klapisch will receive an honorary César Award at the 52nd César Awards, France‘s equivalent of the Oscars.

The 64-year-old director is best known in France for his L’Auberge Espagnole (The Spanish Apartment) trilogy, which followed a group of European students sharing a Barcelona flat. The original 2002 feature was a low-budget breakout which grossed close to $30 million in France and made stars of Romain Duris and Cécile de France. Klapisch shot two sequels: Russian Dolls (2005) and Chinese Puzzle (2013) and a spin-off series Greek Salad (2023) for Amazon Prime Video in France, focusing on the children of the original students. Klapisch has directed 15 features across his decades-long career, moving easily between comedy and melodrama, helping launch the careers of such French actors as Duris, de France, Marion Barbeau, Zinedine Soualem, Vincent Elbaz, Kelly Reilly, Simon Abkarian, Gilles Lellouche, François Civil, Camille Cottin and Laure Calamy.

He directed two episode of French breakout series Call My Agent!, a comedic look inside the French entertainment industry, and served as as artistic director for the show’s first season.

Klapisch recently wrapped his 16th feature, the ensemble comedy S’aimer sur Mars starring Charlotte Le Bon, Pio Marmaï, Vincent Macaigne, and Cécile de France, which Studiocanal is co-producing and distributing.

Klapisch will be honored by the French film academy and Canal+, the co-producer and exclusive broadcaster of the César, at the 2027 ceremony at the Olympia theater in Paris on Feb. 25. The ceremony will air live and unencrypted on Canal+.
