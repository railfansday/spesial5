Fuerza Regida Announces ‘Pisteando Con La Regida Vol. 4’ After Partial Legal Victory Against Rancho Humilde

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

"I'm free now they've finally taken their foot off the tiger's neck," frontman Jesús Ortiz Paz wrote in Spanish on Instagram.

Fuerza Regida is moving forward with new music following a partial legal victory in its ongoing dispute with regional Mexican label Rancho Humilde.

On Thursday (Nov. 26), the California-based música mexicana group, which graced Billboard’s cover in October, announced that Pisteando Con La Regida Vol. 4, the latest installment in its live album series, will arrive Dec. 3.

The project’s distribution partner has yet to be determined, with more details expected next week, a representative tells Billboard.

Fuerza Regida frontman Jesús Ortiz Paz revealed the album’s release date in an Instagram post featuring what appeared to be the project’s cover art. “I’m free now they’ve finally taken their foot off the tiger’s neck,” JOP wrote in Spanish alongside a photo gallery.

Fuerza Regida teased the project in October, when an Instagram post about 22 stolen live-performance videos included the hashtag #PisteandoConLaRegidaVol4, hinting at the title of the band’s upcoming album.

The PCLR Vol. 4 announcement comes one day after Fuerza Regida secured a legal victory against Rancho Humilde. On Wednesday (Nov. 25), a federal judge ruled that the label could no longer enforce its personal-services contract against the band’s four members beyond March 9, 2026, according to a ruling obtained by Billboard.

The dispute dates back to 2025, when Rancho Humilde sued the group over alleged breaches of its recording agreement. Fuerza Regida countersued, alleging the label had tried to “sabotage” its success. The group also claimed its deal with Rancho Humilde was void under California’s seven-year statute, which allows musicians to exit personal services contracts after that amount of time.

Judge Vera sided with Fuerza Regida on the seven-year statute question on Wednesday, though he noted in the decision that Rancho Humilde will be able to appeal. The rest of the lawsuit, including the claims for breach of contract, remains unresolved.

“The court held that the seven-year limit in Section 2855 applies to the full run of Fuerza Regida’s agreements with Rancho Humilde, beginning in February 2018, and that signing new agreements along the way did not restart the clock,” the band’s attorney, Kenneth D. Freundlich, wrote in a statement to Billboard. “As a result, the band’s members have owed Rancho no personal services since March 9, 2026. They are free to record and perform on their own terms, and they look forward to doing exactly that.”

A lawyer for Rancho Humilde, Simran Singh, told Billboard on Friday (Nov. 27) that they believe the ruling to be legally incorrect and intend to appeal it.

“Rancho invested years, substantial capital and considerable resources helping build Fuerza Regida into the global act it is today,” Singh said. “Rancho intends to vigorously protect the ownership and economic rights it bargained for and earned.”

Singh added, “The parties did not simply operate continuously under the same personal-services agreement for more than seven years. Prior contractual obligations were expressly terminated, and the parties subsequently negotiated and entered into new agreements supported by substantial new consideration and materially different rights and obligations. Rancho’s position is that those separately terminated and newly negotiated agreements should not have been treated as one uninterrupted seven-year relationship for purposes of Section 2855.”

Fuerza Regida also announced the Latin American leg of its This Is Our Dream Tour on Wednesday (Nov. 25). Promoted by Live Nation, the nine-date trek launches Feb. 11, 2027, in San Pedro Sula, Honduras, with additional stops in San Salvador, Managua, Guatemala City, Bogotá, Lima, Santa Cruz and Santiago. The tour will wrap March 3 in Quito, Ecuador.

Fuerza Regida was among the top winners at the 2026 Billboard Latin Music Awards on Oct. 22, taking home eight awards. The group also received the Billboard Vanguard Award, recognizing its impact on música mexicana and its role in pushing the genre in new directions.
