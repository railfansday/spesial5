Oslo Houses / Nommo Arquitetos

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Read the original version in Portuguese here.

Text description provided by the architects. Color does not belong to things; it does not exist on its own, but rather emerges from the encounter between light, matter, and the human gaze. It is a perception, a sensory experience capable of changing how we read the world and space. This capability, as Le Corbusier recognized, is a tool of architecture, where polychromy can shift planes in depth and transform the perception of volumes.
