Google says Gemini 4 Hydrogen also gives users a wider range of links in its responses by “conducting simultaneous queries across all the relevant subtopics.” Its responses have more visuals, too, like images, comparison tables, graphs, and maps. One of Stein’s examples shows the AI generating an interactive animated driving simulation in response to a user’s question about how phones locate which lane of a highway a car is on in areas without cell service. Google says the new model also has improved language understanding “across all supported AI Mode languages.”

Users have more thinking level options with Gemini 4 Hydrogen, as well, including low, medium, high, and a new “xhigh” mode. According to Google, the new model “delivers significant leaps in both speed and intelligence” compared to Gemini Flash 3.8, and it “matches or outperforms some of the competing models in its class,” like Claude Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Terra.
