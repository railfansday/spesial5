Fernando Mendoza runs for TD in NFL debut

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Las Vegas Raiders QB Fernando Mendoza completed 17 of his 29 passes for 143 yards and rushed six times for 22 yards and a touchdown against the Seattle Seahawks in Week 10.

Why it Matters: Mendoza's NFL debut wasn't impressive by any means, but he did avoid turnovers and even showed some scrambling potential. He will face a solid Denver defense in Week 11 in his second start, but Mendoza is at least worth considering as a low-end QB2.
