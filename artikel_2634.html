Brandon Aiyuk will visit Commanders

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Receiver Brandon Aiyuk is finally a free agent. He may not be one for long.

The former 49ers receiver cleared waivers on Tuesday. Agent Drew Rosenhaus told ESPN that Aiyuk is scheduled to visit the Commanders on Wednesday.

Aiyuk has been linked to the Commanders for months. He played college football at Arizona State with quarterback Jayden Daniels. Commanders G.M. Adam Peters worked for the 49ers when Aiyuk arrived as a first-round pick in 2020.

And the Commanders — a sudden contender in the NFC East — could use some help at the position.

Aiyuk has been vocal about his desire to play for the Commanders. It got a little dicey during Aiyuk’s social-media tirade over the summer; a couple of his attacks were targeted at Daniels.

Regardless, the Commanders will be getting the first crack at Aiyuk, who is extra motivated to sign a contract and to start playing, especially after the 49ers delayed his free agency for two weeks after he was reinstated by the league from the reserve/left squad list.
