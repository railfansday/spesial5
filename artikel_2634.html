Klint Kubiak on Fernando Mendoza: With two starts against two elite defenses, I've seen improvement

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

While Fernando Mendoza is still looking for his first win as a starting quarterback, the No. 1 overall pick of this year’s draft displayed his considerable potential in Sunday’s loss to the Broncos.

Las Vegas was able to come back from down 17-0 to tie the game midway through the third quarter. Mendoza then engineered another touchdown drive midway through the fourth period to pull the Raiders within seven.

But the comeback effort ended when Mendoza threw a pick-six to Ja’Quan McMillian with just under two minutes left in regulation.

Head coach Klint Kubiak told reporters on Monday that he saw Mendoza take steps in multiple areas.

“No. 1 is just the environment,” Kubiak said, via Sam Warren of TheAthletic.com. “That’s a very hostile environment, and I thought, overall, he did a good job of getting everybody on the same page. I thought he used his legs extremely well, extended some plays.

“For his second start in the NFL against two elite defenses, I’ve seen improvement.”

Kubiak also complimented Mendoza for the way he was able to bring the team back from being down 17.

“That’s who he is. That’s what he’s made of,” Kubiak said. “I think back to us being backed up, him taking a sack and then him making a really big third-down throw to Jack Bech. That’s what you need from your quarterback.”

Mendoza finished Sunday’s game 18-of-37 for 267 yards with three touchdowns and one interception. Mendoza’s next game will be his primetime debut, as the Raiders will face the Browns on Sunday night in Week 12.
