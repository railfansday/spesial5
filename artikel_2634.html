Spurs' winning streak is over after loss to Pistons

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Victor Wembanyama and company fell to the Detroit Pistons 105-93 on Tuesday, Nov. 17 at Frost Bank Center, ending their 12-game win streak.

Wembanyama had 25 points and 18 rebounds in the losing effort. It was his 11th double-double of the season. Cade Cunningham had 27 points, five boards and six assists to lead Detroit.

"Have fun. Play basketball. It's another game," Cunningham told NBC Sports after the game about the Pistons' mentality. "Obviously, they're a good team, but nobody in that locker room has fear in their heart for anybody. So it's just about coming out here, playing basketball, doing what we do best, executing our game plan."

The Spurs entered the fourth quarter down by six points at 78-72. The Pistons led by as many as 11 points multiple times in the period. Dylan Harper brought the lead down to five with a layup at the 2:35 mark. The rising star was hurt with a little more than a minute left. While heading downcourt, he slipped and fell, suffering an apparent groin injury.

"We didn't do the right things. I think we should have been smarter," Wembanyama told reporters after the loss, per NBC News 4 San Antonio's Zack Hedrick. "I feel like as soon as we stepped off the court after the end of the fourth quarter, things looked pretty straightforward and our mistakes looked kinda obvious. I'm not gonna expand on them, but we discussed it right away in the locker room. I think it wasn't our best showing and we quickly realized it."

The two teams were battling in the middle of the game and were tied at 47 late in the second quarter. The Pistons took a 55-54 lead into halftime. At the break, Wembanyama had 15 points and eight rebounds with Julian Champagnie adding 14. Cunningham led Detroit with 15 points of his own at half.

Per the Spurs, they were on their 25th double-digit win streak since the 1976-77 season, their first in the NBA after moving from the ABA. This is the most streaks of more than 10 wins in the league, leading the Los Angeles Lakers (21), Boston Celtics (16), Cleveland Cavaliers (15) and Oklahoma City Thunder (14).

The NBA record for longest win streak is 33 games, which was set by the Lakers in the 1971-72 season.

Last week, Cunningham had a historic night in a win against the Washington Wizards. He scored a career-high 52 points and added 11 assists, five rebounds, a steal and a block. He did not commit a turnover. He was the first player in NBA history to notch at least 50 points and 10 assists in a game without turning the ball over.
