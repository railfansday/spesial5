Xavier Hutchinson finds end zone in Week 10 vs. Browns

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Houston Texans wide receiver Xavier Hutchinson posted four receptions for 58 yards and a touchdown on nine targets in Week 10 against the Cleveland Browns.

Why it Matters: Hutchinson did a good job, especially with the touchdown. However, it's a busy receivers room with Nico Collins, Tank Dell, Kayshon Boutte, and Marvin Harrison Jr., when the latter is healthy. Hutchinson's best treated as a WR5 in most fantasy leagues.
