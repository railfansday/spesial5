Buena honors legends in Athletic Hall of Fame's Class of 2026

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Buena Regional High School Athletic Hall of Fame will honor its Class of 2026 during its induction ceremony on Sunday, Nov. 29 at Merighi's Savoy Inn, 4940 Landis Ave., in Vineland.

The event will held from noon to 4 p.m. For more information about the ceremony or to purchase tickets, please visit www.buenahalloffame.com

Taylor Brown (posthumously), James Camburn (posthumously), Helena (Leyrer) Gibilisco, Andrea (Olsen) Gazzara, Billy Ward III, Stephanie (Aretz) Winkler and the 2011 girls' track and field team.

(Bios were provided by the Buena Regional High School Athletic Hall of Fame)

Brown was a talented and highly competitive athlete who earned four varsity letters in both basketball and baseball. A respected leader, he served as captain of both teams and was widely known throughout South Jersey for his athletic ability.

On the basketball court, Taylor was a consistent scoring threat and an accomplished free-throw and three-point shooter, passer, and determined defensive player. As a sophomore, he scored 28 points against state champion Pleasantville. Following his senior season, he was selected to participate in the Cape-Atlantic All-Star Game.

In baseball, Taylor was a powerful hitter and strong right-handed pitcher who helped lead Buena to top rankings and several titles. His two-out, seventh-inning walk-off double against rival Hammonton secured the 1998 Cape-Atlantic Division and Conference titles. A versatile player, he played every infield position and sometimes moved from catcher to pitcher during the same inning.

Taylor was selected to the inaugural New Jersey Arsenal team in 1995 and competed for the national AAU championship in Millington, Tennessee. In 1996, he helped pitch and hit the Vineland American Legion team to the junior state title, throwing a 3–0 shutout against Florham-Madison in the championship game. He also participated in the 1996 Garden State Games and the 1998 Carpenter Cup tournament, earning multiple All-Star honors during his high school career.

Taylor continued his baseball career at Manhattan College in Riverdale, New York, on a Division I athletic scholarship. Despite undergoing two shoulder surgeries, he earned four varsity letters. In his final collegiate start, he pitched a one-hit shutout against the University of Maryland Eastern Shore. He graduated from Manhattan College in 2003 with a bachelor's degree in communication and marketing. After his playing career, Taylor coached Buena Junior American Legion baseball for one season and Senior Little League tournament teams in Hammonton and Franklinville.

Camburn was a dedicated administrator, longtime NJSIAA track and field official, and devoted supporter of Buena Regional High School athletics. As the school's Director of Student Personnel and Services, he helped student-athletes maintain academic eligibility, meet graduation requirements, understand NCAA regulations, and pursue collegiate opportunities. Jim graduated in 1963 from Holy Spirit High School in Atlantic City, New Jersey. He served as a United States Naval Academy Reservist from 1963 to 1964. He subsequently attended Villanova University from 1964 through 1968, where he earned both his undergraduate and master's degrees.

Jim gave countless evenings and weekends to Buena athletics. He served as site manager for Friday night football games alongside Ken Soboloski and assisted with scoring operations at the District 31 & 32 wrestling tournaments. His commitment to student-athletes and to the successful operation of school sporting events was evident throughout his years of service.

His greatest impact was on Buena Regional Track and Field. Working with Steve Kordos, Ken Soboloski, Dave Albertson, and others, Jim helped transform the original cinder track into an all-weather facility and establish the stadium as a site for major South Jersey competitions, including NJSIAA State Group II Sectional Championships. These events brought recognition and revenue to Buena's athletic programs.

Jim took particular pride in Buena Track and Field achievements, including Katrina Sye's record-breaking 800-meter performance and the success of Matt Kordos and the Boys' 4x400-meter relay team. His dedication was illustrated by an occasion when he used a leaf blower to clear snow from the track in preparation for the Buena Relays.

Jim cherished the relationships he built with athletes, coaches, and colleagues, and his family shared many memories at Buena athletic events. Following his passing, Coach Stephanie Winkler established the James Camburn Memorial Relays in his honor. His lasting legacy is one of service, commitment, and pride in the Buena Regional community.

Helena (Leyrer) Gibilisco, Buena Regional High School Class of 2012, earned seven varsity letters in outdoor track and field (2010–2012), cross country (2011–2012), and indoor track and field (2011–2012). She helped power Buena's 2011 outdoor state championship team and established an exceptional record in hurdles and relays.

Helena remains the school record holder in indoor 60-meter hurdles, 300 meters, 4×55-meter shuttle hurdles, and 4×400-meter relay, as well as outdoor 400-meter intermediate hurdles, 4×400-meter relay, shuttle hurdles, and 3×400-meter intermediate hurdles relay. Her 2012 performances set NJSIAA Group II sectional and state meet records in the 55-meter hurdles and shuttle hurdles, respectively. She also set meet records at the South Jersey Open and Woodbury Relays.

In 2012, Helena won the Meet of Champions 400-meter hurdles title and earned All-American honors with sixth-place finishes in both the shuttle hurdles and 400-meter hurdles at the New Balance Outdoor Nationals. She also finished ninth nationally in the indoor shuttle hurdles and outdoor 4×400-meter relay. Her accomplishments included All-State and All-South Jersey honors, multiple sectional and state championships, and a tenth-place finish in the 400-meter hurdles at the Penn Relays. As of 2026, she ranks 29th in New Jersey history in the 400-meter hurdles.

After graduation, Helena earned a Division I athletic scholarship to Mount St. Mary's University, graduating in 2016. She appeared on the university's all-time top-ten performance lists in the indoor 60-meter hurdles, outdoor 100-meter hurdles, outdoor 400-meter hurdles, and shuttle hurdles relay, and scored in Northeast Conference indoor and outdoor competition.

Helena works at NJM Insurance Company and enjoys spending time with family, traveling, and being outdoors. She and her husband, James Gibilisco, whom she met while running track at Mount St. Mary's, have two children, Jace and Lilly, and are proud to announce they are expecting their third child.

Andrea (Olsen) Gazzara, Buena Regional High School Class of 2013, earned eight varsity letters—four in winter track and four in spring track (2010–2013). A decorated hurdler, sprinter, and relay competitor, she helped establish numerous school records and earned national recognition.

At Buena, Andrea set indoor school records in the 200 meters, 4×55-meter shuttle hurdles, 4×400-meter relay, and distance medley relay, along with outdoor records in the 100-meter hurdles, 100 meters, 200 meters, 4×400-meter relay, shuttle hurdles, and 2×400- and 3×400-meter intermediate hurdles relays. Her NJSIAA Group I indoor 55-meter hurdles record and Group II outdoor state-meet 400-meter hurdles record, both set in 2013, remained standing at the time of her induction into the Buena Regional Athletic Hall of Fame.

Andrea earned All-American recognition with a sixth-place finish in the shuttle hurdles at the 2012 New Balance Outdoor Nationals and a fifth-place finish in the 400-meter hurdles at the 2013 meet. Her state championships included the indoor 4×400-meter relay (2011), indoor shuttle hurdles and 4×400-meter relay (2012), and outdoor 100- and 400-meter hurdles (2013). In 2013 she swept sectional titles in the 100- and 400-meter hurdles and 400 meters, won the indoor sectional 55-meter hurdles, and captured multiple county and Cape-Atlantic League championships. She was named to All-South Jersey teams and earned 2013 recognition from The Press of Atlantic City and The Daily Journal.

Andrea received a Division I athletic scholarship to Monmouth University. There she became a Metro Atlantic Athletic Conference (MAAC) champion in the indoor 400 meters (2017), outdoor 100- and 400-meter hurdles (2016), and outdoor 400-meter hurdles (2017). She earned All-East honors in 2016 and 2017, was named the 2016 MAAC Outdoor Most Outstanding Performer, held a MAAC meet record in the 400-meter hurdles, and qualified twice for the NCAA Division I Outdoor Track and Field Championships (2016 and 2017). She graduated from Monmouth in 2017 with a bachelor's degree in Health Studies and Physical Education and later earned a second bachelor's degree in Nursing from Rutgers University.

After working as an emergency room registered nurse, Andrea became the school nurse at Estell Manor School. She and her husband, AJ Gazzara, have a son, Jason. She treasures time with her family, reading, and vacations to colder destinations.

Billy Ward III, a 2013 graduate of Buena Regional High School, distinguished himself as one of the school's most accomplished wrestlers. A seven-season varsity athlete across wrestling and football, Ward competed in football from 2009 through 2011 and wrestling from the 2009–2010 through 2012–2013 seasons. On the mat, he built an exceptional 138–14 career record and became Buena Regional's all-time wrestling wins leader.

Ward earned four Cape-Atlantic League First Team selections, captured four NJSIAA District 32 championships and four NJSIAA Region 8 championships, and finished third at 119 pounds in the 2011 NJSIAA State Tournament. His success reflected the toughness, determination, and self-belief cultivated during his years as a Buena Chief.

Following graduation, Ward attended Cumberland University in Tennessee and Rutgers University in New Brunswick before pursuing a career in law enforcement. He now serves as a police detective in Cumberland County, New Jersey, and as an assistant wrestling coach at St. Augustine Prep, working alongside his father, Bill Ward Jr. Ward lives in Upper Township with his fiancée and their two dogs, Cash and Teddie. Outside of work and coaching, he enjoys time with family and friends, waterskiing, fishing, hunting, and the beach.

Girls' Cross Country, Boys' & Girls' Indoor Track & Field, Girls' Outdoor Track & Field

A 1987 graduate of Buena Regional High School, Stephanie (Aretz) Winkler has dedicated much of her life to the athletes and athletic programs that helped shape her passion for track and field. A health and physical education teacher since 1992, she has devoted 30 years of her teaching career to Buena Regional.

Winkler served as an assistant girls' spring track and field coach in 2005 before becoming head coach from 2006 through 2019. During that time, she compiled an exceptional 119–5–1 dual-meet record, highlighted by an extraordinary 76-meet winning streak from 2007 through 2016. Her 2011 girls' outdoor track and field team captured the NJSIAA Group II State Championship. She also led Buena's boys' and girls' winter track and field programs from 2010 through 2026 and coached girls' cross country from 2005 to 2011 and again in 2020, compiling a 49–20 dual-meet record. Over 27 years and 44 coaching seasons, including 42 seasons at Buena Regional, Winkler has served as a head coach, assistant coach, or volunteer.

Under her leadership, Buena athletes achieved remarkable success at the conference, sectional, state, and national levels. Her dedication extended beyond competition through scholarship fundraising and service as a former meet director for the Buena Relays and Atlantic County Championships. An accomplished athlete herself, Winkler competed on Buena's 1986 South Jersey Group II championship team and its 1985–1987 Cape-Atlantic League championship teams. She held the school high jump record for 25 years and later served as captain of the Glassboro State College track and field team during her senior year.

Winkler earned her bachelor's degree from Glassboro State College in 1991 and her master's degree from Rowan University in 1999. Above all, she takes great pride in being a mother to Meghan and Benjamin, a wife to John, and a daughter to Bert and Joann Aretz, whose unwavering love and support have been instrumental throughout her life and career.

Coaches: Wil Barry, Shannon Ellenbart and Mike Loiseau

The 2011 Buena Regional Girls' Outdoor Track & Field Team made school history by capturing the program's first NJSIAA Group II state team championship. Their success was built on depth, determination, and a shared commitment to competing for one another. The Chiefs also finished as South Jersey Group II sectional runners-up and claimed the Cape-Atlantic League (CAL) conference championship with an undefeated 9–0–1 record. Throughout the season, Buena outscored its opponents by a combined 1,022 to 375 points.

The team earned the Buena Relays championship and runner-up finishes at both the West Deptford Relays and Rowland Relays. Their championship performances extended to the individual and relay events. Helena Leyrer won the Group II state title in the 400-meter hurdles, while Emely Ramirez, Casey Sturts, Ashley Roman, and Leyrer captured the state championship in the 4×400-meter relay. At the sectional meet, Leyrer won both the 100-meter and 400-meter hurdles, and the 4×400-meter relay team also took first place.

Buena's athletes earned 11 medals at the Atlantic County Championships, nine at the CAL Championships, 12 at the South Jersey sectionals, and nine at the state championships. School records were established by Ramirez in the high jump, Sturts in the pole vault, and Roman in the triple jump. First Team All-CAL honors recognized Sturts in the pole vault, Leyrer in both hurdles events, and the championship 4×400-meter relay team. The South Jersey Track Coaches Association ranked Buena 13th among all South Jersey teams at season's end. Together, these accomplishments secured the 2011 Chiefs a lasting place in Buena Regional athletic history.
