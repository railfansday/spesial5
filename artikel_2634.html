Media firebrand asks if Black players will let Lakers’ ‘white brothers’ win it all

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Stephen A. Smith had a question for Black NBA players on Wednesday morning after the Lakers won their ninth straight game.

"These white brothers can play, they can play. They can win a championship. This is the NBA!" Smith said on "First Take." "Y'all gonna let them win a championship?"

Los Angeles Lakers forward Ziaire Williams, Luka Doncic, Jake LaRavia and Walker Kessler wait during a time-out in Phoenix, Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026. AP Photo/Darryl Webb

Smith was talking about a Lakers team built around Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves and Walker Kessler.

A week earlier, he had picked Los Angeles to bow out by the second round, saying on Mad Dog Sports Radio that he had never seen a team win a title with three white players as its best players.

He still doubts the Lakers will win it all, but he and fellow ESPN analyst Chiney Ogwumike agree they can.

Smith framed the question around race. Ogwumike made the stronger case for the Lakers by pointing to what Doncic did with the game on the line. He trusted Quentin Grimes to take the biggest shot of the night.

Luka Doncic reacts to making a bad pass during the second half against the Charlotte Hornets in Los Angeles, Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026. Getty Images

But Smith made the question about race. Ogwumike pointed to something that says more about the Lakers' title chances. With the game on the line, Luka Doncic trusted Quentin Grimes to take the biggest shot of the night.

The final possession of regulation in Tuesday's 118-114 overtime win over Charlotte gave her a reason to believe. With Reaves and Kessler sidelined, the Lakers had rallied from 18 down and needed a 3 to stay alive. Doncic had 36 points and a single defender in front of him. Grimes had his hands up.

Quentin Grimes of the Los Angeles Lakers reacts after scoring during a game against the Charlotte Hornets in Los Angeles, Tuesday, Nov. 17, 2026. Getty Images

Doncic found him. Grimes buried the tying 3 with 1.7 seconds left. It was his 10th of the night, making him and Kobe Bryant the only Lakers to hit that many in a game. Grimes finished with a career-high 47.

"It's the vibe. It's the ability to say, 'Hey, we have enough to pursue a championship,'" Ogwumike said on ESPN. "Not gonna lie, when I heard that, I was like, I'm not sure about that."

Where Smith raised the question of race, Ogwumike saw what could make the Lakers champions: chemistry developing between the Lakers' best player and a new addition attempting to make his mark.

"That doesn't start in that moment," she said. "That starts in training camp."
