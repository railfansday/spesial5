Jaguars vs. Titans: How to Watch Week 10

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Today, the Jacksonville Jaguars (6-2) will take on the Tennessee Titans (1-7) in Week 10.

The Jaguars just traded away three top draft picks for defensive reinforcements; the Titans just traded away their defensive centerpiece for two top draft picks. These franchises have varying competitive timelines, to say the least.

"It's a tough team, I think, in a lot of ways," head coach Liam Coen said Wednesday.

"Obviously, they've made some moves over the last couple weeks, but you know what kind of defense it's going to be. You know what kind of team it's going to be. We know their special teams is physical and well coached with Bones [John Fassel] over there. And [Robert] Saleh, he does a great job getting the defense ready to go play. And he gets those guys flying around."

"You know kind of this is how they play. This is their brand of football. They've definitely done some things in the back end, in terms of coverage structures and shells and mixing it up a little bit more. But yeah, it's a jet front. I mean, [John] Franklin-Myers is playing at a high level, Jacob Martin's playing at a high level. You look at the way that they're able to get off the ball. If you're not connected on your combinations up front or if you're thin in protection, I mean, they're going to try to get after you."
