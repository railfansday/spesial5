Timberwolves’ Jaden McDaniels blows off ‘star’ treatment after cooking Thunder

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Timberwolves' Jaden McDaniels blows off 'star' treatment after cooking Thunder appeared first on ClutchPoints. Add ClutchPoints as a Preferred Source by clicking here.

One could argue that Minnesota Timberwolves' Jaden McDaniels is the biggest reason for their success without Anthony Edwards, as they improved to an impressive 10-4 record after a 113-106 NBA Cup win over the Oklahoma City Thunder. Outdueling two-time MVP Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, he scored a game-high 32 points on 11-for-15 shooting and grabbed 10 rebounds against the Thunder.

McDaniels is averaging 20.3 points on 57.3/44.2/88.9 shooting splits, 6.7 rebounds, and 1.4 steals per game this month. It's All-Star caliber production. Still, McDaniels downplays his breakout month, saying he's focused on keeping the Timberwolves in the upper echelon of the Western Conference standings, per The Athletic's Jon Krawczynski.

"It's just another day for me," he said with a shrug. "You gotta keep doing it all year, all season. It's just two games. It's just another day, and go and worry about Memphis, for real."

Jaden McDaniels and the Timberwolves improved to 14-5, the third-best record in the Western Conference, behind the Los Angeles Lakers (15-3) and the San Antonio Spurs. After Timberwolves teammate Terrance Shanon Jr. called McDaniels the NBA's best two-way player following a 34-point performance in a 142-116 blowout win over the Brooklyn Nets, the 3-and-D specialist turned in another 30+ point game.

"My team and coaches trust me, just seeing the work I've put in over the years to build that trust," he said. "Now I get to play free."

McDaniels and the Timberwolves have won 10 of their last 12 games, an impressive feat, especially for a team doing it without their best leading star.

McDaniels is posting career highs in points (19.2), rebounds (6.4), assists (3.8), field-goal percentage (.566), and free-throw percentage (.867). The Timberwolves will return home when they face the Memphis Grizzlies in an NBA Cup matchup.
