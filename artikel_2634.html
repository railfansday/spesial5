Patriots rooting guide for Thanksgiving weekend

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Happy Thanksgiving weekend, everybody. While the rest of us are going to enjoy some days with family and food, the NFL is quite busy: a total of 16 games will be played between Wednesday and Monday, with at least one game on on five of the next six days.

One of those will be played by the New England Patriots, who are going to take on the Los Angeles Chargers on Sunday at 4:05 p.m. ET. There are no questions about who to root for in this specific game, but how about the others? Let's take a look.

Once again, a quick explainer: as long as the Patriots are still theoretically alive in the race for a playoff spot, we will root with that possibility in mind.

Green Bay Packers (6-4) at Los Angeles Rams (6-4): Go Packers! It probably won't end up mattering, but picking between those two teams we are going with the one that is at least improving the Patriots' strength of schedule. | Netflix

Chicago Bears (8-2) at Detroit Lions (8-2): Go good game! The Patriots lost to both teams already this year, so this really makes no difference whatsoever. | CBS

Philadelphia Eagles (3-7) at Dallas Cowboys (5-6): Go good game! Two NFC East rivals going against one another with no impact on the Patriots. Time to enjoy a second slice of pie. | FOX

Kansas City Chiefs (8-2) at Buffalo Bills (9-1): Go… Bills? Okay, stay with us here. If we are being honest with ourselves, the Patriots can probably kiss their dreams of defending the AFC East title this season goodbye. However, the playoffs are still a possibility. Other teams are more realistic candidates to drop behind New England — looking at you, Pittsburgh — but if the Chiefs have no games on their schedule where it would be essential for us to root for rather than against them. So, in an ideal scenario, they kick off a losing streak on Thursday night that might just allow the Patriots to overtake them further down the road. Of course, the chances of that actually happening are fairly low, but one can dream. | NBC

Denver Broncos (8-2) at Pittsburgh Steelers (6-4): Go Broncos! Now this is a game that truly matters. The Steelers are the current seventh seed in the AFC and as such the Patriots' closest rival in the race for a postseason spot. In fact, if they lose to the Broncos and New England wins in L.A. then the Patriots are suddenly ahead of them in the standings. Luckily for the Patriots, the Steelers are doing their best these days to at least play their part in that. | Prime Video

New Orleans Saints (3-7) at Cincinnati Bengals (7-3): Go Saints! Somebody has to win the AFC North, and the Bengals are the best bet right now. That said, it would be good if they had to fight for playoff positioning until the very last week of the season, just in case the Patriots need them to beat the Browns in Week 18. With. that in mind, we are rooting for the Saints in this one. | CBS

Baltimore Ravens (4-7) at Houston Texans (4-6): Go Ravens! Another somewhat important game from a Patriots point of view. Neither team is currently in the playoff picture, but the Texans have seemingly started hitting their stride and a loss to the moribund Ravens would help break their momentum and make a late push harder. | CBS

New York Giants (6-3-1) at Indianapolis Colts (3-8): Go Colts! Indianapolis entering the playoff race seems like a long shot, meaning that this can be a game where we root with draft position in mind. Don't forget: the Jets earn the Colts' first-rounder next year. | FOX

Tennessee Titans (1-9) at Jacksonville Jaguars (7-3): Go Titans! With games against the Steelers and Texans still on their schedule, the Patriots will want the Jaguars to have to bring their A-game in both contests. Maybe some heat applied from a loss to their division rivals can help with that. | FOX

New York Jets (3-8) at Miami Dolphins (1-9): Go Dolphins! We are rooting for the worse-positioned AFC East team in this one, once again from a draft perspective. | CBS

Atlanta Falcons (3-7) at Minnesota Vikings (7-3): Go Vikings! Four words: strength of schedule tiebreaker. Will it matter? Unlikely. Is it better to be safe than sorry, though? Absolutely. | FOX

Washington Commanders (4-5-1) at Arizona Cardinals (3-8): Go Commanders! We would rather see top draft picks end up with an NFC team like the Cardinals over, for example, the Dolphins or Jets. Sorry, Arizona, it's nothing personal. | FOX

Seattle Seahawks (6-4) at San Francisco 49ers (9-1): Go Seahawks! Once again, strength of schedule makes the difference here. | FOX

Las Vegas Raiders (4-7) at Cleveland Browns (6-4): Go Raiders! A big one for the Patriots on Sunday night. New England has a clear edge over Las Vegas, but is looking up in the standings to the Browns. A Raiders win would therefore be important for two reasons: it would drop Cleveland to 6-5 and also give them a negative result in a common game with the Patriots. That tiebreaker might just end up being important with no head-to-head between the two teams. | NBC

Carolina Panthers (5-5) at Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-7): Go good game! An all-NFC South battle will close out Thanksgiving weekend on Monday night. It's a low-stress affair from a Patriots perspective. | ESPN
