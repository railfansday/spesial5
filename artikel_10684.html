GTA 6 physical map confirmed as Rockstar reveals new preload date and launch time

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Rockstar Games has revealed new details about GTA 6’s upcoming release, confirming that a physical map will be available while also announcing an earlier preload date and launch details.

With Grand Theft Auto 6 finally set to launch on November 19, 2026, the countdown to Vice City is officially entering its final stretch.

On November 3, Rockstar published a new announcement detailing how players can prepare for launch, including a major change to when downloads will become available.

The company also revealed that GTA 6 will require up to 212GB of available storage, depending on the console.

That’s slightly more than the 210GB download size previously reported, meaning players may want to start clearing some space now.

Rockstar has officially moved GTA 6’s preload date from November 12 to November 10, giving players nine days to download the game before its release.

However, the exact time depends on which console you’re using.

According to Rockstar’s updated support page, PlayStation 5 players can begin downloading at midnight local time on November 10.

Xbox Series X|S owners, meanwhile, can start preloading globally at 12 AM GMT on November 10, which works out to 7 PM ET on November 9 in North America.

Rockstar has also confirmed that a map of Leonida will be available as a free download. A physical version will also be available shortly after launch from the Rockstar store for $0.99 or as a free gift included with any purchase.

Fans had been expecting GTA 6’s boxed release to feature a physical map, giving collectors something tangible despite the controversial decision to ship the game without a disc.

Fans have been particularly interested in the physical map after Rockstar accidentally revealed new locations through its $400 Collector’s Edition in September.
