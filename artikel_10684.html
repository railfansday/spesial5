The Xiaomi 18 Pro debuts globally with sub-mm bezels, privacy display, rear display, and 200MP Leica cameras

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Xiaomi is pushing the limits of modern smartphone specs with a killer design, flush camera lenses, and a next-gen Snapdragon SoC.

If you’re looking for a phone with top-tier specs, Xiaomi has a new phone for you. The Xiaomi 18 Pro and Xiaomi 18 Pro Max are the company’s latest flagships, available starting today, November 17. When looking at the spec list for the Xiaomi 18 series, it’s difficult to find anything that was left out.

The 120Hz LTPO display is bordered by a 0.99mm bezel, the smallest in the industry so far, 4,000-nit peak brightness, and TÜV Rheinland Triple Eye Care Certification, including a 2160Hz PWM dimming rate. Inside the phone is a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 processor with a 3D IceLoop cooling system, and each model’s silicon-carbon battery includes 28% silicon content.

On the back is a pair of 200MP cameras, including a 1/1.28-inch main sensor and a 1/1.56-inch periscope telephoto sensor. These are massive sensors, especially compared to the 1/3.94″ and 1/2.52″ telephoto sensors in the Galaxy S26 Ultra. Despite their size, Xiaomi packs them onto the back in an island with fully flat glass — no protruding camera lenses!

That island on the back isn’t just for cameras, though. It also packs in a 120Hz LTPO M11 OLED panel with 4,000-nit peak brightness for ultra-high-quality selfies and group shots, plus support for notifications and apps. Xiaomi created a new GenAI Apps feature that lets you create your own apps on the back display, plus official support for several popular apps like Google Maps, Spotify, and more.

The Xiaomi 18 Pro series ships with HyperOS 4 (Android 17) and includes 3 months of Google AI Pro, plus 5TB of Google Cloud storage. The Xiaomi 18 Pro retails for €1399 for the model with 12GB RAM and 256GB storage, or €1599 for 12GB/512GB. The Xiaomi 18 Pro Max starts at €1599 for 12GB/256GB, €1799 for 12GB/512GB, and a 16GB/1TB model will be available soon for €2099.

Some retailers also sell a bundle that includes the Official silicone magnetic case, which usually retails for €39, as well as the Magnetic Remote Stand, which retails for €69. That remote stand functions as a MagSafe-style stand and remote, giving you camera shutter functionality and a scrolling feature.
