Hello Kitty is selling toilet paper that smells like dessert

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Hello Kitty is entering her “Hello Sh*tty” era with a new line of toilet paper that smells like dessert, because apparently no part of your daily routine is safe from Sanrio merchandise.

The beloved Japanese character has appeared on just about everything imaginable, from Burger King kids meals to cans of Spam.

Now, she’s heading somewhere a little less glamorous: your bathroom.

Japanese paper manufacturer Marutomi Seishi has teamed up with Sanrio to release a revamped collection of winter-themed toilet paper, complete with adorable character designs and a rather unusual dessert-inspired fragrance.

According to the company’s November 2 announcement, the Sanrio Characters Winter Toilet Paper comes in packs of 12 double-ply rolls featuring 16 familiar characters.

Hello Kitty is joined by the likes of My Melody, Kuromi, Cinnamoroll, Pompompurin, and Pochacco, all dressed for winter and enjoying an ice-skating adventure.

The toilet paper itself features two different printed designs, with a light purple color scheme that should make even the most ordinary bathroom visit feel a little more magical.

However, the real selling point is the scent.

As SoraNews24 reports, the paper comes with a “sweets fragrance,” meaning your bathroom could smell more like a dessert shop than, well, a bathroom.

The goal is to “create a peaceful, tranquil atmosphere in your bathroom,” Marutomi Seishi says.

Each roll measures 25 meters (82 feet) and is made entirely from recycled paper, so at least it’s environmentally conscious.

The collection began rolling out across Japan in early November 2026, with availability at supermarkets, drugstores, and online retailers. No official price was listed in the announcement.

Of course, Hello Kitty is no stranger to sweet treats. Earlier this year, McDonald’s Japan released a Hello Kitty strawberry smoothie alongside a Pompompurin pudding frappe.

Now, Sanrio has found a way to bring dessert into the bathroom, too.

It’s certainly one way to make your next trip to the toilet a little sweeter.
