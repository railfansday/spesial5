Instagram expands AI system that catches teenagers lying about their age

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Instagram is expanding its AI-powered age detection system worldwide, allowing the platform to identify teenagers who have lied about their age and automatically place them under stricter account restrictions.

Meta announced the expansion on November 9, revealing that millions more teenagers will be placed into Instagram’s Teen Accounts, even if they previously entered an adult birth date to avoid the platform’s protections.

The technology builds on age verification measures introduced in 2025 and expanded throughout 2026, with Meta working to identify users between 13 and 17 who may have provided inaccurate information when creating their accounts.

According to Meta, its AI technology can identify accounts suspected of belonging to teenagers by examining information beyond the birth dates users provide.

The system analyzes Instagram profiles for clues about a person’s age, including birthday celebrations, references to school grades, and information shared through posts, comments, captions, and account biographies.

Meta has also introduced visual analysis technology that examines photos and videos for general indicators that someone may be underage. The company previously clarified that this system does not use facial recognition to identify individuals.

When Instagram determines that an account likely belongs to a teenager, it can automatically apply Teen Account protections, even if the user originally registered as an adult.

Meta says its earlier deployments have already helped identify millions of accounts belonging to teenagers who were not receiving the appropriate protections.

The latest expansion follows previous rollouts in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, Brazil, and the European Union, extending the technology to Instagram users worldwide.

Instagram is not the only platform receiving expanded age detection capabilities, as Meta confirmed that the technology is continuing to roll out on Facebook.

The company also plans to begin introducing similar AI-powered age estimation technology globally on Threads before the end of 2026.

Meanwhile, parents in the US will begin seeing an informational video within Instagram during November explaining how the platform identifies teenage users and how families can help ensure their children’s ages are accurate.

In Australia, Meta has started using Apple’s age-range signal as an additional method of determining whether users meet age requirements. The feature allows participating app developers to receive an age range associated with a device’s user when that information has been provided through Apple’s system.

The expansion also follows Meta’s agreement with 52 attorneys general across US states and territories, which includes commitments to strengthen the identification of teenage users and children under 13.

Alongside the changes, Meta has called on competing platforms TikTok and YouTube to adopt similar practices for identifying underage users and applying appropriate protections.

The company says it will continue improving its technology to identify teenagers who misrepresent their age, while developing stronger systems to detect accounts belonging to children who are too young to use its services.
