Indian arrivals to Vietnam are growing more than twice as fast as the overall market, and Vietnamese carriers are chasing that demand into cities like Ahmedabad. Seats alone won’t lock it in, though. Vietnam still needs the food, guides, and wedding and events products Indian travelers expect.

Vietnam’s airlines are adding flights to India as arrivals from one of the country’s fastest-growing source markets show no sign of slowing.

Sun PhuQuoc Airways will launch Ahmedabad-Phu Quoc flights on Dec. 3, and Vietnam Airlines will take its Delhi and Bengaluru services to daily from Dec. 14. That comes weeks after Vietnam Airlines opened its own Ahmedabad route.

Low-cost carrier VietJet today runs the most extensive network between the two countries, connecting Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang with six Indian cities.

Vietnam welcomed 746,000 Indian visitors in 2025, making India its sixth-largest international source market. Arrivals rose from 138,000 in 2022 to 507,000 in 2024. In the first nine months of 2026, the country received 670,972 Indian visitors, according to Vietnam’s National Statistics Office.

Vietnam's carriers are adding capacity to India as Indian arrivals keep climbing. Sun PhuQuoc Airways launches Ahmedabad–Phu Quoc on Dec. 3 with five weekly flights, going daily from early February 2027, and Vietnam Airlines takes its Delhi–Hanoi and Bengaluru–Hanoi services to daily from Dec. 14, weeks after opening Ahmedabad–Hanoi on Nov. 3. VietJet currently operates the most extensive network between the two countries, linking Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, and Da Nang with six Indian cities. Vietnam received 746,000 Indian visitors in 2025, its sixth-largest source market, up from 138,000 in 2022. In the first nine months of 2026, Indian arrivals reached 670,972 and grew 33% year over year, more than twice Vietnam's overall international arrivals growth of 14.5%. Booking.com data shared with Skift ranked Vietnam among the top three most-searched international destinations for Indian travelers this year, and Ixigo said bookings to Vietnam have doubled year on year. Demand extends beyond leisure into weddings, corporate travel, and events, with provincial authorities in Khanh Hoa and Quang Ninh now building India into their tourism plans. Vietnam's tourism industry has called for more India-focused products, including wedding packages, Indian food options at hotels, culturally familiar guides, and Hindi-language marketing.
