Former CIA Director John Brennan's son arrested after FBI uncovers dozens of alleged child porn images

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

An image shows former CIA Director John Brennan in front of a Central Intelligence Agency crest. (William B. Plowman/NBC/NBC Newswire/NBCUniversal via Getty Images; Saul Loeb/AFP via Getty Images)

The son of former CIA Director John Brennan was arrested for allegedly possessing child pornography, Fox News has learned.

Kyle Brennan, 40, was charged with transportation of child pornography, according to a federal complaint.

The case originated when Adobe CyberTipline reports flagged accounts linked to Brennan for uploading illicit files involving minors.

In April and June 2024, Adobe submitted two reports to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) regarding an account allegedly used to upload suspected child sexual abuse material.

The FBI's Washington field office is involved in the investigation.

Around April 7, 2024, three files were uploaded to the Adobe account. A review of the files indicated a file that appeared to be a morphed image combining the face of a minor girl, later identified as a 12-year-old, with the naked body of another minor, federal prosecutors said.

Comcast records showed that the IP address used to upload the files was registered to Kyle Brennan at an address in Warrenton, Virginia, where he lives.

Records from Apple later confirmed that the private relay account was registered to Brennan, authorities said.

In July 2024, authorities searched Brennan's home and seized his iPhone.

The case was referred to the FBI in October, and a judge signed off on a warrant so investigators could examine the records and seize Brennan's phone.

On Friday, an FBI analysis of the iPhone, which was named "Kyle’s iphone (2)" contained a photograph of Brennan’s Virginia driver’s license and revealed approximately 88 images depicting girls under the age of 18 engaged in sexually explicit conduct, prosecutors said.

One image appeared to be a "fully nude, pubescent minor girl on a locker bench," the complaint states.

"The girl’s wrists and ankles were bound and her mouth was gagged," it said.

The Justice Department is investigating John Brennan, who served under former President Barack Obama, regarding his role in the 2017 Intelligence Community Assessment (ICA) on Russian interference in the 2016 presidential election.

Fox News Digital has reached out to a representative for John Brennan and the Justice Department for comment.
