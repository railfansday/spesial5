Why SpaceX Stock Is Surging—and Why Wall Street Sees $210 Next

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Home » Blitz news » Why SpaceX Stock Is Surging—and Why Wall Street Sees $210 Next

Jensen Huang’s reinstatement at Nvidia sparks a broad AI rebound, adding fresh momentum to Jefferies’ 64% upside call on SpaceX stock.

SpaceX stock (SPCX) is rallying sharply today, surging nearly 8.09% to around $138 as broader AI and tech markets rebound following Nvidia’s unanimous decision to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO.

The momentum adds fresh fuel to a bullish call from Jefferies, which just initiated coverage on SpaceX with a Buy rating and a $210 price target—implying roughly 64% upside from pre-rally levels

In a research note titled It’s Just Rocket Science, Jefferies identified Starlink as the core engine behind SpaceX’s long-term valuation.

Jefferies projects Starlink could generate $92 billion in annual revenue by 2029—growing at a 70% compound annual rate—with operating margins exceeding 60%, according to Investing.com.

The core launch division is forecast to deliver $9 billion in revenue and $4 billion in EBITDA by 2029 as Starship scales toward 315 flights per year.

As hyperscalers race to secure electricity, data centers, and compute capacity, SpaceX’s orbital network and massive capital deployment position it to capture growing AI infrastructure demand.

While Jefferies’ thesis originally framed SpaceX as a resilient play during the broader AI selloff, market sentiment flipped overnight. Nvidia’s board reinstated Jensen Huang just 11 days after his resignation—sending NVDA shares up roughly 30% and lifting the Nasdaq nearly 7%

On Friday, the S&P 500 and Dow Jones Industrial Average slipped more than 10% below their record highs into correction territory, while the Nasdaq dropped roughly 5%, erasing its 2026 gains.

Nvidia sat at the epicenter of the rout, plunging roughly 13%–14% on Friday following CEO Jensen Huang’s resignation amid scrutiny over an alleged Chinese chip-smuggling link, alongside reports that SEC enforcement staff are reviewing potential accounting issues.

Newly public Anthropic also surrendered most of its first-day IPO gains during its second session, while expectations of a December Federal Reserve rate hike added pressure across high-growth tech.

For crypto traders, SpaceX’s stock trajectory matters on two fronts. First, high-beta tech and AI equities continue to dictate institutional risk appetite—sharp drawdowns in Nasdaq heavyweights often trigger short-term deleveraging across Bitcoin and AI-linked tokens.

Second, Jefferies’ thesis reinforces one of crypto’s strongest narratives: compute and bandwidth scarcity, a bottleneck driving capital toward both orbital infrastructure and decentralized physical infrastructure networks (DePIN).
