Trump Media posts $410M profit, but crypto hides an operating loss

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Trump Media reported $409.7 million in Q3 net income, driven largely by non-cash items, alongside $496.1 million in digital asset and equity gains.

Trump Media & Technology Group reported $409.7 million in net income for the third quarter of 2026, saying most of it reflected non-cash items, as gains on digital assets and equity securities drove the result.

The company announced the results for the quarter ended Sept. 30 in its Nov. 9 earnings release, furnished with a Form 8-K. It reported combined net realized and unrealized gains on digital assets and pledged digital assets and equity securities of $496.1 million.

Trump Media reported $3 million in quarterly revenue and a $12.3 million loss in adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA).

The release's reconciliation and accompanying quarterly filing separately identify $378.1 million in realized and unrealized gains on digital assets and digital assets pledged, namely Bitcoin and Cronos.

Digital assets and pledged digital assets supplied most of the combined gain pool. Equity-security gains made up the rest; reported net income was $409.7 million.

The $378.1M digital-asset line includes pledged-asset revaluation, other digital-asset revaluation and derecognition gains.

Published unrealized and realized equity gains sum to $118.0M, rounded. The company says gains were driven primarily by bitcoin-related securities.

The $496.1M gain pool combines digital assets and securities. It is distinct from $409.7M in net income.

Realized, unrealized and derecognition gains are accounting categories, not equivalent cash proceeds. The gain pool is not a complete net-income bridge; other income and expenses also enter earnings.

The 10-Q breaks the digital asset line into about $200.8 million in unrealized gains on pledged digital assets, $107.5 million in unrealized gains on digital assets, and $69.9 million from derecognition of digital assets.

Derecognition means removing assets from the balance sheet, so the reported gains should not be read as cash generated during the quarter.

The 10-Q identifies about $98.3 million of unrealized equity gains and $19.7 million of realized equity gains, driven primarily by higher prices of bitcoin-related securities. Some of the investment gains outside the digital asset line also came from Bitcoin-linked exposure.

The release said gains were partially offset by $54 million in realized and unrealized derivative losses. Other highlighted offsets included $11.9 million in accreted interest expense, $9.4 million in stock-based compensation, and $4 million in investment expenses.

Trump Media said revenue rose 205% from a year earlier, reporting rounded figures of $3 million versus $1 million, and the 10-Q provides underlying amounts of approximately $3 million versus $972,900.

The filing attributes the increase to $1.3 million in subscription growth driven by Truth API and Truth+, about $523,300 in additional Truth Social advertising revenue, and $164,000 in Truth.Fi ETF management fees.

Those revenue gains sit alongside an adjusted EBITDA of a $12.3 million loss after adjustments for digital asset gains and losses, investment income and losses, net interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, and stock-based compensation.

At Sept. 30, the company reported $2.5 billion in total assets and approximately $2.3 billion in what it calls financial assets. The latter is a non-GAAP basket spanning cash, restricted cash, short-term investments, equity securities, notes receivable and accrued interest, digital assets and pledged digital assets.

The company says financial assets should not be treated as a liquidity measure, and the basket is broader than a Bitcoin treasury total.

For the first nine months of 2026, the profitable third quarter still left a consolidated net loss of approximately $234.2 million. Trump Media also said it used $28.4 million in operating activities over the nine months ended Sept. 30, primarily because of expenses tied to recently concluded legacy legal matters.
