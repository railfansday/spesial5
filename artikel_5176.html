Bitcoin nears a ‘thinly traded pocket’ – Is $64K next for BTC?

The equities volume on Hyperliquid has overtaken the Bitcoin [BTC] volume once again, Glassnode revealed in a post on X.

Crypto-native traders were chasing the stock market dip. The last time the trading volume ratio climbed to such highs was in August, before the bullish BTC rally from $63k to $87k.

It was appealing to consider that another such rebound was around the corner. However, the past three trading days saw Bitcoin spot ETF outflows of $1.043 billion.

Sentiment simply does not support a recovery. At least, not yet.

The STH to LTH supply ratio was above the upper band and headed higher compared to last week. This meant short-term holders accounted for a greater share of the Bitcoin supply.

In turn, it indicated a market more sensitive to short-term price moves, as short-term holder coins tend to carry thin profits and tend to be moved first under stress.

Glassnode’s Market Pulse report also showed a drop in the percentage of supply in profit. The profitable supply was shrinking, but it has not crossed below the lower band.

Therefore, the market was still in a cooling phase, not capitulation. Moreover, fewer holders were sitting on profits that could tempt them to sell, which should ease the profit-taking pressure.

The Coinbase Premium was negative even when the rally began in August. Its -0.135% reading now meant Bitcoin was cheaper on Coinbase than it was on Binance. The discount had been wider toward the end of October as selling pressure ramped up.

This is considered a signal that U.S.-based investor interest in BTC was low.

The net taker buy-sell imbalance was at -13,142 BTC in the futures market on the 9th of November, reported crypto analyst Axel Adler Jr. The futures imbalance had been at -75,000 on the 5th of November and has contracted since then.

Even so, the overall market regime has shifted away from the bullish signals it displayed a few short weeks ago. Bitcoin was above the 200-day Moving Average at $73,193, but not by much.

The imminent threat was the $64k-$74k pocket. In August, BTC prices had ripped through this area, making it a thinly traded pocket. It could offer little resistance on the way down, too.
