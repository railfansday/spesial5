SpaceX shares rose 1.19% in after-hours trading on Wednesday to $128.96, adding to a 1.30% gain that lifted the stock to a close of $127.44 from $125.81. The move capped a volatile session in which shares fell as low as $112.93 before recovering. US markets are closed on Thursday for Thanksgiving, so Friday’s shortened session will be the next test. Two pieces of news helped the rebound. The Federal Communications Commission approved Starmind, SpaceX’s planned network of satellites built to run AI computing in orbit. SpaceX also said it will give 70 million shares to active Trump Accounts, with Elon Musk matching the pledge from his own stake, meaning about two shares per child. President Gwynne Shotwell had earlier pledged 2 million shares to the programme. On the chart, the stock bounced from the lower edge of a downward-sloping channel, but it would need to climb above the channel’s midpoint to signal a stronger recovery. SpaceX remains well below its 52-week high of $225.64, with a market value of about $1.73 trillion.
