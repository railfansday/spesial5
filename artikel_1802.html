Kane vows to be back for ‘business end’ of Bayern’s season after ankle surgery

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Harry Kane has vowed to be back for the business end of the season after he underwent surgery on his injured ankle.

Kane sustained an ankle ligament injury during Bayern Munich's 1-1 draw with Koln on Friday.

The Ballon d'Or winner is facing several months out on the sidelines in a huge blow to Bayern, who sit second in the Bundesliga after Borussia Dortmund's win on Saturday.

Kane, though, hopes to be back in time to help Bayern when it really matters.

The highs and lows of life! Obviously very disappointed to get this injury but as always it’s an opportunity to show resilience and improvement going into 2027. I’ll be back fresh and ready to go for the business end of the season! “Tough times don’t last, tough people do” – my guy @Edelman11 told me this one! Thank you for all the messages! Alles gut!

"The highs and lows of life! Obviously very disappointed to get this injury, but as always it's an opportunity to show resilience and improvement going into 2027," said Kane, who is likely to be out for at least three months.

"I'll be back fresh and ready to go for the business end of the season.

"Tough times don't last, tough people do."

Kane has plundered 41 goals in 42 games for Bayern in 2026, while netting 15 times in as many matches for England.

The 33-year-old has only missed 14 games since he joined Bayern in 2023.

Bayern won nine of those, suffering four defeats, giving them a 64.3% win ratio. However, they have won 114 of the 158 games in which Kane has featured (72.2% win ratio).

If three points were awarded for every win, then Bayern's points-per-game average with Kane comes out at 2.33, while without the England captain, it is 2.0.
