Gary Neville makes Arsenal title claim after Liverpool thrash Manchester United

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Gary Neville said he expects Arsenal to ‘canter’ to the Premier League title after Liverpool’s emphatic win over ten-man Manchester United.

Defending champions Arsenal are six points clear at the top of the table despite failing to extend their winning run against Newcastle United in a 2-2 draw.

The blow of failing to win at St James’ Park was substantially softened by the fact virtually all of the teams chasing Arsenal also dropped points.

Second-placed Brighton suffered a shock 2-0 defeat at newly-promoted Hull City while Manchester City’s surprising slump continued as Enzo Maresca’s side could only draw 1-1 at home to Fulham.

The only team in the top-four to win this weekend was Liverpool, who smashed ten-man Manchester United 4-0, but the Reds are still a healthy seven points adrift of Arsenal.

Mikel Arteta ended Arsenal’s 22-year wait for a Premier League trophy last season and eight-time title winner Neville expects the Gunners to retain that crown in a ‘very strange’ domestic campaign.

‘Looking at the Premier League top-six right now, Arsenal are clearly the standout team and are going to win the league at a canter,’ Neville said on his Sky Sports podcast.

‘But the rest of the usual top five or six teams, it’s not been this bad has it for a long time? They’re really struggling.

‘Liverpool were obviously a bit better today but Chelsea are struggling, Tottenham are struggling. Manchester United are struggling, Manchester City look like they’ve fallen off a cliff right now compared to the standards they set under Pep Guardiola.

‘So it’s a really interesting time where other teams are starting to emerge and it’s becoming a lot more competitive. It’s a very strange league this year.’

Jamie Carragher also expects the Premier League trophy to be staying in north London despite being encouraged by Liverpool’s win over struggling Manchester United.

‘I still think there’s just an inconsistency with Liverpool game-to-game at the moment,’ the Anfield legend said. ‘I think they will get better the longer the season goes on and people want there to be a title race.

‘But I think most people in the country would look at Arsenal as a team and say they will probably win the title comfortably.

‘For me, with Liverpool being my team, I want them to be in a position to be the strongest challenger this season and put themselves in a position that they can then challenge Arsenal next season.’

While Liverpool climbed back into the top-four by beating Manchester United, the Red Devils sunk to 19th in the Premier League.

United were thrashed in Mauricio Pochettino’s first game in charge after Leny Yoro was sent off inside ten minutes at Anfield.

Despite Manchester United’s dismal position, Neville refused to say his former club were in a relegation battle.

‘I don’t believe they are in a relegation fight,’ Neville added. ‘They are second bottom but I do think they will come out of it and start to move up the table.

‘It’s embarrassing really. It’s really desperate and it’s tiring watching them. This is the worst Manchester United have ever been in 53 years.’
