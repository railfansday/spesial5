Eastbourne Borough beat Aveley – Isthmian Premier clash in pictures

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

A smart move led to Freddie Carter making it 1-0 on 24 minutes, and after an Aveley equaliser by Matt Johnson on 39, a second Sports goal on the stroke of half-time came from Josh Rees.

A superb Kai Corbett strike early in the second half to put clear distance between the teams. Aveley were always chasing the game - and the Sports deserved their victory. Steve King's squad now sit third in the division, three points behind leaders Welling but with a game in hand.

They go across Sussex to Burgess Hill on Saturday.
