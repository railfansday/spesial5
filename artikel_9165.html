built99 guide celebrates and archives architectural intelligence of 99 indian practices

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

the built99 guide documents, celebrates, and preserves architectural intelligence from india, revealing how these practices -- and architects -- are progressing the future of our built environment.

What if the architecture in India could lead the world in sustainable, deeply human design? A comprehensive new book highlights this possibility. The inaugural Built99 guide and its companion digital directly, below, explores the future of the built environment by documenting 99 carefully selected Indian architecture and design practices. Rather than simply cataloging past achievements, this expansive volume experiments with an ongoing global dialogue, translating decades of regional research, material ethics, and civic impact into a permanent print edition. The guide examines the foundational, forward-thinking ideas driving each studio’s portfolio.

By elevating these 99 practices onto an international stage on both designboom and ArchDaily, the Built99 guide documents, celebrates, and preserves architectural intelligence from India and the Global South. As the reader turns its pages, the book (see more) reveals how these practices — and architects — are progressing the future of our built environment.
