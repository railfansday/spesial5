Trump Blames Biden For “FAA Disaster,” Praises Himself For “Great Job”

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

A couple of days ago we saw the worst disruption to air travel that we’ve seen in the United States in over 25 years, as air traffic shut down nationwide for over four hours. President Trump was publicly very quiet at first, leaving his cabinet members to (very poorly and dishonestly) respond to the situation.

Well, Trump is now speaking out, and you’ll never believe who he’s blaming. Yes, of course… former President Biden! This isn’t even partisan, folks, let’s just look at the facts…

Trump took to social media to apologize to the traveling public, thank them for their patience, and promise to investigate what went wrong. Just kidding. Instead, he wrote about how much Biden sucks, and about how great the FAA is under him:

The Biden Administration was an FAA disaster. Everything they touched turned bad. Big money spend unwisely, making an old and broken system worse than when they began. We have the knowledge and plan for the Flight Control Systems, we need Congress to give the money, FAST! Our FAA is doing a great job, and is ready to go.

In another social media post, Trump posted about how the Biden administration set back air traffic control modernization by years.

Given the air traffic controller shortage we’ve seen, plus the importance of oversight for work like what we saw on Friday (which shut down our system), should Trump really be doing a victory lap here? For that matter, let me remind everyone that the union representing FAA employees claims that projects like what happened on Friday would’ve normally had a risk assessment and would’ve been overseen by FAA employees, but that’s no longer the case.

Let me be clear, I commend the Trump administration for trying to put together a plan now for modernizing air traffic control, even if the plan isn’t perfect.
