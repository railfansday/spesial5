Bad House launches January 11, 2027

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Auto-battler dungeon crawler Bad House will launch for PC via Steam on January 11, publisher Dotemu and developer Andrew Morrish announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:

Welcome to your new home. The previous tenant left some things. You can have them, but there’s just one condition: don’t go into the basement. Explore the many rooms of a mysterious basement in Bad House, an auto-battler dungeon crawler.

This property comes partially furnished with a generous selection of items left behind by the previous tenant. You’re welcome to make use of everything they’ve left behind.

If you hear any strange noises from the basement, it’s best to ignore them. There are many rooms down there, and most people never come back up.

Your personal storage box is located upstairs and is available throughout your tenancy.

Should you discover anything of value in the basement, you may return it to storage for future use. Please note that basement access points are fragile and may not remain available after use. Management is not responsible for any items abandoned in the basement.

Previous tenants thoughtfully left behind many objects in the basement, including knives, wands, amulets, rings, swords, shields, armors and other useful possessions. We encourage you to make good use of them if needed.

The basement is home to a thriving community including demons, skeletons, bats, the undead, and many more unidentified occupants.

Please respect the residents’ personal space whenever possible.
