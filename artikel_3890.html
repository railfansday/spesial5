'She's a Lot of Fun': GTA 6's Mandy Has PS5 Players Smitten

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

I knew when I was reviewing GTA 6 that bunny boiler Mandy was going to take social media by storm, and it was quite fun watching the web react to Chapter 1’s ‘Pipeworks’ mission as the game launched overnight.

Now it’s being played by millions, actress Lexie Shoaibi has revealed she stars as the character, who not-so subtly throws herself at Jason during a very early story mission.

I actually think this is one example of the game’s slightly more mature writing. Wait, don’t roll your eyes – just hear me out, okay?

While this whole quest is played more for laughs, it’s there to establish the strength of Jason and Lucia’s relationship, as Jason shows no signs of being tempted by Mandy’s advances.

“She’s a lot of fun,” Shoaibi said, after confirming she did voice acting and motion capture for the character. At the time of writing, her post on Instagram has received over 90k likes.

But the response on social media would suggest gamers think she’s more than a “lot of fun”.

One post by Complex, featuring a few images and the caption ‘Mandy from GTA 6’, attracted 37 million views in just a few hours. Pertinently, it’s been bookmarked 54k times.

I don’t want to analyse this character too deeply, but I think it’s the moment I realised Rockstar has really pushed the boat out here.

The sheer variety of body types, and the detail invested into them, is unprecedented really. Mandy is just one of many similarly realistic NPCs in the game.

And while the character is almost certainly a psychopath, it’s been amusing to see her go viral overnight.
