Nintendo announces Black Friday 2026 deals

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Nintendo has announced its Black Friday 2026 deals, which include $50 in savings on a “Nintendo Switch 2 + Mario Kart World Bundle” and select games at $20-off the suggested retail price.

Starting November 22 through December 5, rev up for Black Friday with the “Nintendo Switch 2 + Mario Kart World Bundle” featuring a Nintendo Switch 2 system, a full download of the Mario Kart World game and a three-month (90-day) Nintendo Switch Online Individual Membership for the suggested retail price of $499.99, a savings of $50.

Plus, from November 22 through November 29, select games for Nintendo Switch 2 like Donkey Kong Bananza and Nintendo Switch games such as Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2 will be $20-off the suggested retail price. All offers available while supplies last.

In addition to select retailers, these promotions will be available at the Nintendo New York and Nintendo San Francisco stores. The “Nintendo Switch 2 + Mario Kart World Bundle” promotion will also be available on the Nintendo Store website. The Nintendo New York and Nintendo San Francisco stores will feature festive window displays and seasonal decor and are a one-stop destination for holiday shopping for Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch, plus games, gifts, exclusive items, and more. Share the joy of Nintendo this holiday with exclusive products, collectibles and apparel inspired by your favorite characters and games including Mario, The Legend of Zelda, Animal Crossing, Pikmin, and Splatoon. Holiday gift guides, festive shopping bags, and, for a limited time, a gift with purchase (requires minimum spend) will be available while supplies last.

Holiday shoppers looking to get an early jump on enjoying some games can also check out a sale on Nintendo eShop and the Nintendo Store beginning November 19 through December 2.
