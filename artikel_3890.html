New Xbox PC app update boasts 60 percent faster load times, but I probably still won’t use it

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

A new update rolling out to Xbox Insiders today is bringing big improvements to the Xbox app on PC.

The changes all have to do with quality-of-life, mainly load times. Xbox says that the app now launches 60 percent faster, with CEO Asha Sharma posting a video of the old app and the new one side by side. Operations in the app itself are about 50 percent faster, too.

We’re making XBOX on Windows faster and more responsive. Starting today, we’re rolling out updates that make our app up to 60% faster. A meaningful improvement, with plenty more to do. pic.twitter.com/h5kzHSpR8H

This “refreshed” feel in the app and also Xbox’s cloud gaming apps aims to improve the experience for those who play Xbox games on PC. Obviously, there are a number of people who use the Xbox PC app. Not myself, of course, and not anybody I know or talk to, but it’s nice to see that this sort of thing is being worked on behind the scenes.

“We’ve heard where the experience could be better,” Xbox said. “There’s more to do, and your feedback will continue to shape what comes next.

I don’t see this update bringing me into the Xbox on PC ecosystem vs. what I already game on, but if Steam-Xbox integration is a thing in the future (like rumors have suggested in the past), then this could end up being a big stepping stone somewhere down the line.
