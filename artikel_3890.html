Sonic the Hedgehog 35th anniversary animation ‘Memories and Beyond’

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

SEGA has released the 35th anniversary animation for Sonic the Hedgehog, titled “Memories and Beyond.” It features a story by Takashi Iizuka, written by Ian Flynn, and animation by GXS Productions.

The description reads, “Join Sonic and friends as they try to stop Dr. Eggman from harnessing the power of the Chaos Emeralds to upgrade Metal Sonic into the ultimate fighting machine. Fans will see how the power of friendship is unbreakable even in the toughest situations.”

A special “Memories and Beyond” 35th anniversary t-shirt is also available to purchase now via SEGA Shop.
