Alpitronic’s new SC plant to build 400 kW and 1 MW fast chargers

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Alpitronic, the company behind IONNA’s fast chargers, is investing nearly $34 million in a new South Carolina factory. It’ll build 400 kW DC fast chargers, plus a 1 megawatt (MW) system that can charge cars and heavy-duty trucks.

The $33.94 million project in Fort Mill is expected to create 70 jobs, the South Carolina Department of Commerce announced Wednesday. It’ll be the Bolzano, Italy-based Alpitronic’s first operation in South Carolina, just across the state line from its North American headquarters in Charlotte.

The HYC400 packs up to 400 kW into a single unit. If you’ve stopped at an IONNA Rechargery, you may have already plugged into one.

The HYC1000 works differently. A separate power cabinet can send up to 1 MW across as many as eight vehicles at once, shifting power between them as their charging needs change. Operators can configure it with CCS or NACS plugs for passenger EVs or a Megawatt Charging System (MCS) plug for compatible heavy-duty trucks.

Alpitronic’s 400 kW chargers were already being made in Wisconsin for Mercedes-Benz High-Power Charging in 2024. Alpitronic Americas CEO Patrik Herrnegger said the Fort Mill plant “will put US production under our direct control, bring us closer to our customers, and enable us to serve them more efficiently.”

The 173,315-square-foot site at 3560 Stateview Blvd. (pictured above) will also house a testing lab, repair operations, a warehouse, logistics space, and offices. South Carolina approved job development credits and awarded York County a $200,000 grant to help pay for building improvements.
