Meow Mix reimagines iconic cat memes for multichannel campaign

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The J.M. Smucker Co. cat food brand fictionalized behind-the-scenes videos for internet-famous characters including Keyboard Cat and Huh Cat.

Meow Mix’s “Behind the Memeow” campaign piggybacks on popular internet cat memes that continue to be a sizeable part of internet culture — if not quite the 15% of the internet cited in press materials, a persistent figure that originated in a campaign by competitor Friskies over a decade ago.

“Cats have always had a special place in internet culture, and some of the most iconic memes of all time have come from their wonderfully unpredictable personalities,” Nicole Massey, vice president of marketing for pet products at J.M. Smucker Co, said in a statement. “With ‘Behind the Memeow,’ we’re celebrating that cultural legacy while reminding cat parents that even the pickiest, most notoriously hard-to-please cats can’t resist Meow Mix.”

Three 30-second ads feature meme characters Table Cat, Keyboard Cat and Huh Cat and show how Meow Mix changed their behavior. Each spot features new lyrics for the iconic Meow Mix jingle, which the brand has continued to iterate on to reach new generations of consumers.

Beyond the ad spots, the campaign will be amplified with a social-first media strategy that includes paid support across Meta, Pinterest, Snapchat and Reddit, with a high-impact TikTok placement. The effort also includes influencer collaborations and a custom content partnership with youth-focused publisher Pubity.

J.M. Smucker Co. will announce Q2 fiscal 2027 earnings on Nov. 24. The company in August reported better-than-expected net sales in Q1 of fiscal 2027 and lifted its outlook for the full year.

“We have been very disciplined in terms of where we spend dollars, and we have tools that enable us to evaluate how much bang for the buck we get and where we're going to get incremental ROI,” CEO Mark Smucker said of the company’s approach to marketing on an earnings call. “I feel pretty confident that we can be choiceful and prudent with the dollars and put them where we're actually going to get a meaningful return.”

Marketers in the cat care category have used various tactics to engage with pet parents. Clorox’s Fresh Step teamed with artificial intelligence-powered agency RECE to create a series of ads, while Whisker made a marketing push around its Litter-Robot brand.
