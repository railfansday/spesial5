Prince William and Unai Emery 'deeply concerned' about star: Four things you missed from Aston Villa vs Barcelona

Aston Villa's loss to Barcelona was filled with plenty of moments, and supporters could easily have missed some of those.

Unai Emery will not be pleased with his side's performance and will now look to turn that frustration into something positive.

In attack, and on the ball, the Premier League side were quite blunt, struggling to cause many issues for the La Liga outfit.

In truth, it highlighted the clear gap of quality between these two sides.

There were also several individual moments that added to the occasion.

From reactions on the touchline to an appearance from royalty, here are four things you may have missed.

In the 30th minute, Emi Buendia was handed a yellow card for a challenge on Barcelona's Fermin Lopez.

The winger looked to prevent the home side from winning the ball back high but was instead penalised.

As per The Guardian's live text, the situation made Unai Emery "deeply concerned", with his expression telling the story.

Buendia would later be lucky to escape a second yellow card, which would have made those concerns even more validated.

Aston Villa struggled to keep Brazilian winger Raphinha contained, with the forward striking two goals on the night.

However, the former Leeds man looked to be one of the more frustrated players of the night in the 70th minute.

Barcelona manager opted to take the 29-year-old off, but the inability to hit a hat-trick looked to cause issues for Raphinha.

His frustration going off the pitch was incredibly evident, but Aston Villa wouldn't have minded as his terrorisation of their defence came to an end.

Interestingly, one of the loudest cheers of the night came from a moment that did not involve a goal.

Barcelona midfielder Gavi had not started for the Catalan side in quite some time, and against Aston Villa the same occurred.

His last start came against Getafe on 10 October.

But when Flick brought the 22-year-old onto the pitch in the 69th minute, the cheers were extremely loud.

With it evident Gavi is a player supporters want to see more of.

Aston Villa fan Prince William was not going to miss this European clash against Barcelona.

The British monarch was invited to be in Barcelona's director's box alongside his son Prince George.

It was not a night to be for the Villans, with the Prince of Wales watching on as his side lost 3-0.

However, it was probably an experience he never thought he would have as an Aston Villa fan.
