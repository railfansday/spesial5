Sheffield Wednesday owner David Storch must watch Josh Windass situation

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

A new era is underway at Sheffield Wednesday following the appointment of Dave Challinor as the club's new manager.

Wednesday made the decision to part company with Henrik Pedersen last month following an inconsistent start to life back in League One, and Challinor has been tasked with getting their promotion push back on track.

Challinor had been out of work since leaving Stockport County in June after a near five-year spell at Edgeley Park, during which time he led the Hatters from the National League to League One, and his side missed out on promotion to the Championship in his final game as they were beaten by Bolton Wanderers in last season's play-off final.

The 51-year-old, who has also had spells with the likes of AFC Fylde and Hartlepool United, boasts the incredible record of finishing in the play-offs or higher in each of his 15 full seasons as a manager, winning promotion on seven occasions.

Challinor will be looking to add an eighth promotion to his CV with Wednesday this season, but given that the club were forced to operate under transfer restrictions in the summer as the consequences of Dejphon Chansiri's disastrous reign continue to bite, there are question marks over whether the current squad is good enough to secure a return to the Championship.

It is unclear when the remaining constraints on the Owls will be lifted, but once they are able to spend freely, owner David Storch should consider making an ambitious move for one of the club's former fan favourites.

While Storch's takeover marked the start of a bright new era at Hillsborough, Wednesday turned to a few familiar faces this summer as they rebuilt their squad.

Midfielder Barry Bannan achieved club legend status during a 10-and-a-half year spell with the Owls after arriving on a free transfer from Crystal Palace in 2015, but, amid the off-field uncertainty, he departed for Millwall in January.

However, after less than six months at The Den, Bannan made an emotional return to Wednesday in July, and the deal was a clear indication of the fact that Storch and the new hierarchy were keen not to distance themselves too much from the club's past.

Bannan was not the only ex-Owls star to re-join the club this summer, with goalkeeper Joe Lumley and defender Reece James, who was part of the squad that won promotion from League One in the 2022-23 season, also returning for second stints in South Yorkshire.

Of course, Storch should not rely too heavily on sentimental signings, and Bannan's somewhat underwhelming start to the campaign proves that it can be difficult to recapture previous magic, but he should weigh up a swoop for forward Josh Windass if Challinor succeeds in leading Wednesday to promotion this season.

Windass spent five-and-a-half years with Wednesday after joining from Wigan Athletic in January 2020, scoring 53 goals and providing 23 assists in 182 appearances in all competitions, including a 123rd-minute winner in the play-off final against Barnsley at Wembley in 2023 to seal the club's promotion to the Championship.

However, as the scale of the Owls' crisis under Chansiri became clear last summer, Windass opted to terminate his contract at Hillsborough, and he subsequently made the move to Championship newcomers Wrexham.

Windass enjoyed an outstanding debut campaign at the SToK Cae Ras last season, scoring 16 goals and registering seven assists in 46 appearances in all competitions, but it was not enough to help the Red Dragons into the play-offs as they missed out on the top six on the final day.

It has been a frustrating start to the current season for Windass, with the 32-year-old registering just one assist in six appearances during what has been an injury-hit start to the campaign, and his absence has certainly been felt by Phil Parkinson's side.

Given his importance to the team, Wrexham would likely be reluctant to allow Windass to leave next summer, but should Wednesday win promotion to the Championship, there is no doubt that the forward would be an exciting signing to kickstart the next phase of the Storch era.

Windass still regularly references his former club on social media, most recently taking to Instagram to react to the appointment of his ex-Rangers team-mate Clint Hill as Challinor's assistant, which suggests that he does still hold plenty of affection for the Owls and may well be open to a potential return.
