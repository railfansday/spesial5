Reformation Steps Into Home Design With Its First Rug Collection

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

After years of dressing its customers, Reformation is turning its attention to their homes. The Los Angeles-based fashion brand has partnered with French rugmaker Les Éditions de Tapis on its first rug collection, bringing the distinctive designs found in Reformation’s stores into residential spaces.

Swan Fan Circle Rug and Boa Dress in Ivory. Photography courtesy of Reformation.

Reformation has long incorporated rugs by Les Éditions de Tapis founder Thomas Coccimiglio into its retail interiors. “His craftsmanship is unparalleled,” says Lauren Caris Cohan, Reformation’s chief creative officer. “Every Les Éditions de Tapis piece is a work of art that somehow feels both luxurious and whimsical.”

The rugs’ playful patterns and unexpected details have become part of the shopping experience, prompting customers to ask where they could purchase them. Those requests eventually led the two brands to develop a collection specifically for the home.

Chintz Rug in Baby Blue. Photography courtesy of Reformation.

Working together, Cohan and Coccimiglio developed nine limited-edition, made-to-order designs that combine the decorative traditions of Parisian interiors with a more relaxed Los Angeles sensibility. The resulting collection carries Reformation’s fashion sensibility into the home without simply translating clothing designs into floor coverings.

Delicate palm trees, swans, and other figurative motifs appear against softly colored backgrounds, often surrounded by decorative borders that recall antique textiles. Rather than filling every available inch with pattern, many of the rugs leave generous areas of open space, allowing the individual illustrations to stand out.

Le Classique Rug in Ivory and Naya Knit Dress in Tan Tiger. Photography courtesy of Reformation.

Each rug begins as a hand-drawn design before being hand-knotted by artisans in India and Nepal. The process brings traditional rug-making techniques to designs that feel decidedly contemporary.

For Cohan, the move into interiors is less about entering a new product category than extending the design language Reformation has developed across its stores and clothing collections. “We are very intentional about crafting physical environments that reflect who we are in subtle, delightful ways,” she explains. “Like our clothing, there is a sense of ease and elegance with a wink of humor.”

Grand Chintz Rug in Dark Wine. Photography courtesy of Reformation.

The Reformation x Les Éditions de Tapis collection is available at thereformation.com, with prices starting at $1,000. All nine designs are available exclusively online, with material swatches on display at select Reformation stores.
