More Delays Hit MTG Reality Fracture Secret Lair Bundle Right Before Shipping

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

More shipping woes strike Wizards of the Coast even after all the sets for 2026 have been released.

Delay woes just keep coming for Magic: The Gathering, as fresh delays have hit a recent Secret Lair drop just two days before shipping was due to begin in North America.

In an email sent to some buyers of the Reality Fracture Secret Lair bundle, Wizards of the Coast has confirmed that the planned shipping date of November 19 is now being moved to December 16, almost an entire month after shipping was due to begin.

There are also delays for those in Europe and the UK, which we'll get to, but those orders were already on a later schedule.

The email Wizards of the Coast sent to customers today is, for many, the final straw. Delays and cancellations have been the story of the year in Magic, and it's marred what should have been an otherwise hugely successful year for the game, but the discourse around overprinting products and Wizards biting off more than it can chew has completely dominated the narrative towards the end of the year.

Star Trek stock is still massively delayed for some retailers, meaning pre-orders and back orders still haven't been fulfilled. Total Cards in the UK today reached out to customers to confirm that virtually every product is still delayed in some form.

With the Reality Fracture Secret Lair bundle, however, customers purchased this product under the guise that stock would be sent out relatively quickly, and the planned shipping date of November 19 for North America has been moved to December 16 with 48 hours' notice. The email states that customers can still opt to cancel or amend their orders, but some on Reddit report that payment has already been taken, making it harder to do so.

Elsewhere, the Reality Fracture Secret Lair products were due to ship on December 29 for Europe and the UK, but this has now been shifted to January 19, 2027. This is particularly alarming, as it's just two weeks before the Nauctis: The Sunken Realm prerelease, sparking fears that this latest delay will impact future sets and give 2027 a poor start in terms of availability.

Every set of 2026 has had availability issues in some form, with the problems getting gradually more severe to the point where Star Trek has to cancel the planned beginner box, and some retailers still haven't had their full orders delivered almost two weeks after prerelease.

The lack of transparency around why these issues have been happening is what is stressing players out the most, and while some reasons have been sprinkled in here and there, many announcements are hidden on the Daily MTG feed and are bare-bones statements that confirm the delays rather than explain them. Meanwhile, there's yet another Secret Lair drop happening right now, in addition to last week's Star Trek one.

It really calls into question the business strategy to have so many releases in one year; when you can't meet demand, why wasn't the decision made to slow things down?
