On the 13th of November, Arthur Hayes announced that Flop Labs will launch its testnet and airdrop. The AI-compute blockchain has a launch date for November 24 and a pitch revolving around “food for your AI agent.”

In a nutshell, FLOP is a token that AI agents spend on inference and memory storage, and miners can earn it by offering up GPU processors.

Hayes became CEO of Flop Labs in August 2026, after a public bet against the AI bubble, that these debt-funded data centers will crash and leave behind an abundance of GPUs.

Flop Labs runs on “proof of useful inference”: miners supply GPUs to process “AI inference” requests, the validators confirm the delivery, and agents spend FLOP tokens for their expenses.

This case was further strengthened on the 9th of November, when a developer demonstrated FLOP mining on an Nvidia RTX 5080.

Hayes confirmed that this was the long-term goal of FLOP, stating,

Mining FLOP isn’t just for folks with access to the highest spec GPUs. We specially want retail users to mine with consumer graphics cards, Mac Mini’s, etc.

Notably, Hayes confirmed that no token has been created yet. However, in August he stated that there is no presale, no memecoin, and no FLOP token, only,

Still, the biggest criticism is that Flop Labs still lacks a full whitepaper and verification details.

In the past, crypto sleuth ZachXBT has publicly accused Hayes of promoting tokens like Worldcoin [WLD], Near Protocol [NEAR], Hyperliquid [HYPE], and Zcash [ZEC] before selling into the demand that his posts created. Hayes did not deny the trades; he admitted,

I sold to a willing seller at a price… I just happened to call it right this time as it regards to my trading goals.

So, it’s difficult to believe Hayes when he tells his 800K followers that FLOP is the future of agent commerce.

The 24th of November will bring with it a testnet and an airdrop. However, Flop Labs and Arthur Hayes still have a ways to go before they can confirm that Hayes’ anti-bubble bet is real.
