XRP Out at Royal Bank of Canada: Leveraged Fund Cut to Zero

Royal Bank of Canada (RBC) held no shares of the Teucrium 2x Long Daily XRP ETF at the end of the second quarter, according to an amended Form 13F that the bank filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on Nov. 18, 2026. The Teucrium fund gives investors double leveraged exposure to XRP.

In the first quarter of 2026, RBC reported buying shares of the spot Bitwise XRP ETF for about $30,000. XRP was trading at $0.99 on Thursday.

The latest wave of 13F filings came out in November and covers the third quarter through Sept. 30. Goldman Sachs held about $144 million in XRP ETFs at the end of the third quarter, up from about $87 million at the end of the second quarter, according to its filing. The bank held shares of five spot funds from Franklin Templeton, Bitwise, Canary, Grayscale and 21Shares, and its crypto portfolio was worth $2.64 billion.

Italy's Intesa Sanpaolo disclosed XRP fund holdings of $27.21 million. The bank held 712,319 shares of the Grayscale XRP Trust ETF, worth about $20.52 million, and it also opened a new position of 402,750 shares in the Bitwise XRP ETF, worth about $6.7 million. Besides, trading firm Jane Street reported about $23.5 million in XRP funds.

UBS and Morgan Stanley disclosed XRP fund holdings of $1.91 million and $116,000, respectively. Bank of America held 13,260 shares of the futures-based Volatility Shares XRP ETF, worth about $106,600.

Wells Fargo held about $11,000 in the Bitwise fund, and JPMorgan Chase held 539 shares of the same fund, worth about $9,000. National Bank of Canada held 330 shares of the Bitwise fund, worth about $5,500, and Bank of Montreal held REX-Osprey and ProShares Ultra XRP shares worth $4,300.

U.S. spot XRP ETFs held $1.08 billion in total assets as of Nov. 17, according to SoSoValue.

Form 13F filings only capture long positions in US-listed securities on a specific date. They don't show shorts, derivative hedges, intra-quarter trading or assets held outside the United States.
