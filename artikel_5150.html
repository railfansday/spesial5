Bitcoin Miners Lead Wall Street's Crypto Sell-Off: Why Are They Falling Hardest?

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Bitcoin sank to about $68,000 on Friday. That is its lowest price since August. US stocks tied to the coin went down with it. The companies that mine new Bitcoin took the worst hit. Hut 8 dropped 6%. Riot Platforms and MARA Holdings each lost 3.9%. Coinbase, the largest US crypto exchange, slipped just 0.5%.
