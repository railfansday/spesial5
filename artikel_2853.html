Indianapolis Colts Injury Report: Week 9 Who’s In and Who’s Out

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

The Indianapolis Colts today released their Friday injury report for week 9 of the NFL season against the Dallas Cowboys.

Linebacker Akeem Davis-Gaither (knee) has been ruled OUT for Sundays game against the Dallas Cowboys. With Davis-Gaither out the Colts will likely start one if Jaylon Carlies or Bryce Boettcher at the WILL spot. CJ Allen is set to play the MIKE and wear the green dot for the first time.

Safety AJ Haulcy (ankle) has been ruled as QUESTIONABLE for Sundays game against the Dallas Cowboys. Haulcy had not been in the injury report all week but popped up today due to an ankle injury that caused him to be limited. If he is unable to play on Sunday then expect Hunter Wohler to start in his place at strong safety.

Right tackle Jalen Travis (thumb, hamstring) has been ruled as QUESTIONABLE for Sundays game against the Dallas Cowboys. If Travis is unable to play then expect Luke Tenuta to start in his place. However, Travis has managed a full week of practice.

Cornerback Justin Walley (knee) has been ruled as OUT for Sundays game against the Dallas Cowboys Cowbiys. Walley will miss his second game due to the knee injury. Kapena Gushiken will make his second start at nickel for the Colts in Walley's place.
