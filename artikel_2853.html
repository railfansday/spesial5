Why Tyrese Haliburton didn't make the trip to Mexico City for the Pacers

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Most of the Indiana Pacers held a practice in Mexico City on Friday but Tyrese Haliburton remained in Indianapolis.

It isn't a surprise Haliburton will miss Saturday's game against the Denver Nuggets as coach Rick Carlisle said the star point guard would be out at least two weeks due to a hamstring injury on Monday, Oct. 28. T.J. McConnell, however, did make the trip, though he is also recovering from a hamstring injury suffered prior to the start of training camp.

Carlisle said Haliburton remained in Indianapolis because it was "better for his reb," according to media reports from Mexico City.
