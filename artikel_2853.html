Eagles at Cowboys snap counts: Ty Robinson came up big despite limited playing time

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

The official snap counts from the Philadelphia Eagles' Week 12 Thanksgiving win over the Dallas Cowboys are in! Let's take a look and run through some analysis.

Jordan Mailata is the only player who's played 100% of the Eagles' offensive snaps this season. He's also played 12.09% of the Eagles' special teams snaps.

The Eagles' starting offensive line configuration in each of their last six games has played 100% of the team's snaps on offense.

Makai Lemon led all Eagles receiver in snaps played this week. The rookie ranked second in targets with six. He logged his fourth-most receiving yards this season in addition to scoring his second career touchdown. His first was also against Dallas.

Darius Cooper only saw two targets despite playing the second-most WR snaps. It was the first time he saw fewer than three targets since Week 6.

Saquon Barkley suffered a stinger injury that led to him not returning to the game. His five carries resulted in just seven rushing yards. Perhaps he shouldn't play if he's not going to be healthy? Stepping up in relief of RB1, Will Shipley produced 46 yards on 10 rushing attempts.

DeVonta Smith has been less than 100% and he injured his back in the fourth quarter. Despite this, he ultimately finished with a team-high 14 targets (of the team's 30 total), which resulted in 11 receptions for 200 yards and two touchdowns. Stud!

Zach Ertz saw a bigger role than normal with Dallas Goedert being ruled out due to a shoulder injury early in the game. He didn't officially log any targets in the box score because a couple of the passes thrown his way resulted in Cowboys penalties.

Hey, nice to see Eli Stowers actually see some offensive playing time, unlike last week! Goedert leaving early clearly helped him in this case. Nevertheless, good to see him out there. He even played more than Johnny Mundt!

Elijah Moore was pretty productive for only playing 19 snaps. He ranked second on the Eagles in receiving, catching three of his five targets for 57 yards. He would've gone over 70 had a Milton pass to convert a 3rd-and-14 not went through his hands.

The Eagles continue to limit Johnny Mundt's role. Good. He was clearly playing too much earlier in the season.

John Ojukwu continues to serve as the sixth offensive lineman in heavy OL packages.

Britain Covey has two snaps on offense this season, one each over the past two games. In other words, the Eagles are 2-0 when he plays on offense in 2026. Gotta keep it up! Seriously, though, another nice game by him as a returner. He logged a 12.3 punt return average in addition to providing the offense with a spark on a 55-yard kick return.

Jaydon Blue got to say he played an offensive snap in a win over the team that cut him earlier this year. The REAL revenge game angle that nobody is talking about.

77 snaps is the third-most played by the Eagles' defense this season. They didn't help themselves by not getting off the field in some key third-and-long situations.

Andrew Mukuba leads the Eagles in defensive snaps percentage played this season with 99.74%. He also logged his second interception of the season on the final play of the game, a Dak Prescott desperation throw downfield.

It wasn't the best game of Quinyon Mitchell's career. In fact, it may have been his worst, or at least one of them. But he deserves major props for toughing it out through the hamstring injury he suffered in Week 11. Q really battled out there. And he had some tacky penalties go against him.

Jeremiah Trotter Jr. led the Eagles in total tackles once again while being the top off-ball linebacker in terms of playing time.

Jalen Carter was quiet for a good chunk of this game, failing to log a box score stat until the second half. But he made some important plays during the comeback effort.

It was a relatively quiet game for Moro Ojomo, who had two assisted tackles and no other stats. This despite nearly playing as much as Carter.

Nolan Smith and Jonathan Greenard tied for the most edge rusher snaps. Smith logged a QB hit but no other stats in the box score. Greenard had one QB hit and one TFL in addition to drawing multiple Cowboys holding penalties. 52 snaps for Greenard was a new season-high for him, moving past his previous high of 36 snaps played. Good to see he's able to handle a bigger workload.

Jalyx Hunt wasn't too far behind Smith and Greenard. He also had one QB hit.

Michael Carter II was the Eagles' nickel cornerback with Cooper DeJean getting hurt and ruled out early on. Kenny Moore II also saw playing time.

Despite playing the third-most DT snaps, Byron Young ranked second on the team in total tackles (nine) and solo tackles (five). He also had a QB hit.

Excluding Week 18 last season when the Eagles rested their starters, Ty Robinson tied his career-high for snaps played with 19. Despite limited playing time, he logged one sack (to force a turnover on downs and ultimately set up the Eagles' game-winning field goal) and two TFLs (one on the play where Javonte Williams fumbled for what should've been a Riq Woolen touchdown if the refs didn't blow the ball dead way early). Oh, and he also blocked the punt that led to a Kenny Moore II touchdown. The Eagles don't win without the Vanilla Gorilla stepping up big. Kudos.

Rookie safety Cole Wisniewski made his presence known late in the game by flying down to make a big hit in run defense. He also had pretty tight coverage for a good contest on a Cowboys reception that resulted in a first down anyway. Could be good to see some more of this guy down the stretch.

Gabe Hall suffered a wrist injury that caused him to be ruled questionable to return. Hopefully it's not serious. He just got called up to the roster.

Mac McWilliams saw defensive snaps for just the second time this season. He previously logged three in Week 4.

Mason Reiger seeing playing time on defense was certainly unexpected. The Eagles temporarily elevated him from the practice squad to mainly help out on special teams but he notched his first career NFL defensive snaps as well.

15.3% of Jake Elliott's snaps played this season came from this game since there was so much Eagles scoring! Including his game-winning field goal, of course.

On the flip side of the coin, this was Braden Mann's fourth-fewest snaps played in a game this season due to less punting.

Willie Lampkin and Micah Morris were limited to special teams duty. It was funny to see the broadcast focus in on Morris having a reaction after Brandon Aubrey's missed field goal. Why did they randomly focus in on him? Made me laugh.

DID NOT PLAY: Arnold Ebiketie, Cole Payton, Jonathan Jones, A.J. Epenesa, Lane Johnson, Ajani Cornelius, Grant Calcaterra

Ebiketie and Jones missed this game due to injury. TBD if they'll be able to play in Week 13.

Epenesa was a healthy scratch behind four active edge rushers … including the aforementioned Reiger. Don't really see the point of keeping him on the roster. Just bring up Reiger or Keyshawn James-Newby to fill that spot. Better to see what they have in a rookie than a dead-end veteran they don't even care to play anyway.

Also don't see the point to keeping five tight ends on the team. If Goedert is significantly hurt, there's more of a case to keep Calcaterra around. If he's fine, though, there's just no good reason for both him and Mundt to be on this roster. They're both going to be free agents after the season and they don't have futures in Philly.

As a reminder, Johnson is set to remain on the Eagles' roster until June 2027, even though he retired.
