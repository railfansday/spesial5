Joe Burrow can become third quarterback with four or more TD passes in four straight games

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow had seven touchdown passes in his team’s first four games of the season. In the last three, he has had 13.

With four against the Dolphins, four against the Ravens, and five against the Titans, Burrow has the chance to become only the third quarterback in NFL history to throw four or more touchdown passes in four straight games.

Via NBC Sports research, the two others were Colts quarterback Peyton Manning in 2004 and Dolphins quarterback Dan Marino in 1984. Both won the league MVP award in their seasons of four straight four-or-more touchdown games.

Burrow’s odds of winning the MVP for 2026 are currently at +475, behind only Josh Allen and Brock Purdy.

Burrow currently leads the league with 20 touchdown passes. And the Bengals, after starting 2-2, are now 5-2.

They face the 3-5 Falcons on Sunday morning in Madrid at 9:30 a.m. ET on Sunday.
