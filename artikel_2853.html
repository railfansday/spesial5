Steelers stock report after 19-16 loss to Eagles

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Pittsburgh Steelers are now 0-12 in Philadelphia since 1965, as they fell to the Eagles 19-16 on Sunday. The Steelers were flat for much of the game and could not get anything going offensively until the game was effectively over. After sitting atop the AFC North after Week 8, the Steelers have lost two consecutive contests in their quest to defend their AFC North crown.

Here is my weekly stock report after the Steelers' shocking loss in Philadelphia.

Max Hurleman was arguably the Steelers' best player in his first career NFL game. His six catches for 61 yards led the team. The undrafted rookie was on the receiving end of the Steelers' only offensive touchdown, as he made a great adjustment in the back of the end zone for a 20-yard score. Hurleman supplanted rookie fourth-round pick Kaden Wetjen as the Steelers' primary return man, and given his performance today, it's hard to imagine the Steelers have any plans to take Hurleman off the field for the foreseeable future. To this point, it looks like the Steelers may have a player in No. 84.

We have to give Chris Boswell his flowers today, given he was the entirety of the Steelers' offense. Boswell nailed field goals of 25 yards, 40 yards, and 52 yards. The veteran kicker kept the Steelers alive for as long as he could today.

With Nick Hebrig ruled out earlier in the week, the Steelers were on pace to get the game they needed from veteran Alex Highsmith. The edge rusher recorded two early sacks on Eagles quarterback Joe Milton, but came up limping after his second sack. He spent an exorbitant amount of time in the blue medical tent and was even seen running around on the sidelines, but he did not return. Despite his early exit, Highsmith deserves some credit for the game he was having before his ankle injury derailed it.

DK Metcalf was ejected in the second half after receiving his second personal foul for poking Eagles defensive back Cooper DeJean in the eye. I was honestly shocked by how the Steelers kept Metcalf in the game after his first personal foul, given how fired up he was. The receiver was unable to control his emotions, as he was ejected just a few plays later. There isn't much to be said that hasn't already been said about Metcalf. He is holding Pittsburgh's offense back at this point. The team might actually be better off without his shenanigans and inability to control his emotions now that Hurleman has emerged and Roman Wilson is back. Metcalf could very well see an extended suspension from the league after his altercation in Philadelphia.

Jaylen Warren entered the contest averaging 5.7 yards per carry on the season. He was held to 2.1 yards per carry today. Rico Dowdle was held in check as well, averaging just 3 yards per carry. The Steelers boasted one of the NFL's best and most efficient running games through the first eight weeks of the season. They haven't been able to get anything going in their last two games. Philadelphia came in ranked 25th against the run, making this performance even more frustrating for the Steelers.

In case you were wondering just how important Mason McCormick is to this offensive line, look no further than the events of today. The run game was not nearly as efficient with the new-look offensive line. The pass protection was not much better, as Aaron Rodgers was sacked five times and was constantly on the move to avoid pressure. You have to wonder if the Steelers could look to replace Spencer Anderson, who has already been benched once this season, in the coming weeks.
