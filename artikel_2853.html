Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Karl-Anthony Towns will match a legend's franchise record with another double-double. The Knicks center scored 21 points with 16 boards during Tuesday's blowout victory over the Bucks, notching his 10th straight double-double — which is one off Willis Reed's mark to start the 1970-71 season. And just like that campaign for Reed, Towns is racking up the rebounds while defending an NBA championship. The Knicks face the Cavaliers on Wednesday at MSG, where Towns can tie Reed. "I didn't know that. I just know that this team right now, we're on a good winning streak, we're playing good basketball, and that's the most important thing," Towns said. "That's what the fans really want to hear about."
