Chelsea fans are wrong about Wesley Fofana, his stats tell a different story

Wesley Fofana is a player who has been frustrating Chelsea supporters this season.

The Frenchman's performances, in the eyes of fans, have not been good enough, with the Stamford Bridge side failing to keep goals out.

Over the weekend, Fofana was involved in an unfortunate incident where Brian Brobbey was given a penalty following mutual holding between the two.

While many felt it was not a penalty, it has brought further scrutiny onto the 25-year-old.

Chelsea's defensive problems have been a recurring theme this season, with individual errors proving costly on several occasions.

Despite his obvious ability, Fofana needs to show greater consistency if he is to win back the confidence of his fans, but the supporters also need to show some grace.

Underlying statistics of Fofana's season so far may change the mind of Chelsea supporters.

The numbers suggest he has not been quite as poor as he has been perceived.

As per Data.MB, among all Premier League centre-backs to play at least half of the 900 minutes on offer this season, Fofana actually ranks in the top three for defensive duels won percentage.

The French defender comes in with a rate of 68.97 per cent, while only teammate Levi Colwill (71.88 per cent) and Bournemouth's Antonio Silva (72.09 per cent) rank higher.

It is not even a case of Fofana not getting involved in many duels that places him that high either, as the Chelsea man is on the upper end of defensive duels per 90 with 4.9.

Fofana also tops the chart when it comes to interceptions adjusted for possession.

This matters because a defender playing for a team that dominates possession has fewer opportunities to intercept passes because opponents spend less time on the ball.

In effect, it levels the playing field, with Fofana leading the way with a total of 9.

So from the data, it is clear that Fofana is a quality defender, but his struggles are still quite noticeable in games.

Most of that may come from the former Leicester centre-back being out of confidence, especially with so many supporters on his back.

Perhaps if Chelsea fans can allow him time to play himself back into confidence, they can finally get to see just how good a defender he is.

Right now, it is clear the judgements on him over this season have been harsh.
