Ryan Yates return is game-changing for Nottingham Forest, 100% stat vs Man City proves it

Nottingham Forest have been struggling this season, but the return of Ryan Yates to the starting XI on Saturday gave them an added boost.

Oliver Glasner's side picked up an unlikely 3-1 win against Manchester City, with Liam Delap taking the headlines with his brace.

While the English striker deserves that praise, especially seeing as both were incredible finishes, teammate Yates has gone under the radar.

The Forest captain is a rarely seen figure in the starting lineups, and against Enzo Maresca's side, that was in fact his first of the season.

His energy and willingness to get stuck into challenges brought a different dimension to Forest's midfield.

And it was perhaps a reminder that Glasner has got his treatment of the skipper all wrong this season.

Forest have lacked the aggression and defensive discipline needed to compete consistently this season.

Glasner's side often come up against teams that want to dominate the ball, and those tenacious midfielders are needed.

Ibrahim Sangare is a potential solution, but captain Yates proved against Man City that he may be the player Glasner needs.

Against Man City, as per FotMob, Yates won 100 per cent of his ground duels.

Additionally, only Xaver Schlager's 14 defensive contributions were more than the Englishman's 11, and he did not play the full game after coming off in the 79th minute.

The presence and mere leadership of the 28-year-old was exactly what had been missing from Glasner's Nottingham Forest, and Yates should now go from fringe star to regular starter.

And that could be a game- and season-changing decision from the Austrian manager.

Yates may not be the most technically gifted midfielder, or the nicest player to watch on the eye.

However, his work rate to disrupt the opposition is a trait that Forest desperately needed, and it proved it worked against Man City.

Too often this season, Forest have looked short of fight, and when it comes to the Englishman, that is something you know you will never have a lack of.

Having a player who is willing to do the ugly work and set the standard for those around him is invaluable.

Yates has demonstrated that he can provide exactly that, and Glasner must recognise it.

The midfielder must be a regular feature following the upcoming international break.
