The VR Forever Gaming Awards return in December with tons of demos and discounts

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

The industry’s biggest players are joining together to celebrate all the amazing VR games you probably don’t know about (but should).

This year has been difficult for the gaming industry at large, and that’s particularly true for the wonderful niche of VR gaming. Despite some huge setbacks, VR gaming is still alive and well, popping out tons of amazing games this year that you may not have realized. To celebrate those wins, VR studio and publishing label Creature is joined by Future Friends Games to bring back the VR Forever Awards this December.

Throughout the entire festival, you can expect tons of new game demos on Steam, including over 220 games from nearly 150 developers. While the festival is technically on Steam, Meta Quest still rules the roost in the VR world, so you can expect plenty of surprises for Quest gamers.

The festival will run from December 3rd to 9th on Steam and will cover the best of VR across six categories: Best Hidden Gem, Best Single Player, Best Multiplayer, Best Ongoing, Most Anticipated Game, and Game of the Year. This year’s judges include Zeena Al-Obaidi (CCO at VR Games Showcase), Ian Hamilton (Founder of Good Virtual Reality), Naysy (Content Creator, VR Forever Ambassador), and Nicholas Sutrich (me!).

The full list of winners and nominees will be announced during the show, but Creature highlights Forefront, Thief VR: Legacy of Shadow, Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Empire City, Dungeons of Eternity, Titan Isles, and the ever-popular Hot Dogs, Horseshoes & Hand Grenades 2 as some of the most notable.
