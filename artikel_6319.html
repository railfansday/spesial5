American Airlines Vs. United First Class: Boring Chicken Dinners That I’d Happily Order Again

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

I realize if you just read my blog it looks like I’ll eat anything thrown in front of me and to some extent that is true on airplane, but a pair of flights, one on American Airlines, the other on United Airlines, featured chicken dinners much closer to my what I usually eat at home. Let’s compare them in this week’s Meal of the Week.

Let’s start with American Airlines. I was flying from Los Angeles to Chicago and received a same-day upgrade (as a mere Advantage Gold!) and thus was not able to pre-ordered. Onboard the choices were chicken or pasta (described as a chicken breast with rice or pasta shells with tomato sauce). I chose chicken.

The chicken breast was covered in a white sauce and served with white rice on the side and green beans on top. On the side, a small green salad with sun-dried tomatoes and a ramekin with what appeared to be parsnips, carrots, and cabbage with croutons…at least I think so (whatever it was, it was nice).

While not an exciting dinner, I really appreciated just eating a lean chicken breast and green beans…it’s very similar to what we eat at least a couple nights per week at home.

Dessert was raspberry sorbet…I miss the Häagen-Dazs ice cream that used to be on these flights (and the real ice cream sundaes before that).

On to United. Same route. The current “signature entree” out of Los Angeles is described as:

Pollo asado chicken thigh Pollo asado chicken thigh with charred corn farro, street corn style zucchini and asado sauce

The AA dish was fine, but this folks was excellent. The flavor of the chicken was excellent and the corn farro and zucchini were really nice…we grow zucchini in our garden at home and cook it in the oven with our chicken so this really felt close to home. The corn was a nice treat and I ate every bit of it.

The sale on the side had sun-dried tomatoes and sliced cucumbers and the meal also came with a cornmeal roll and a slice of “hot chocolate” cheesecake, which I passed on.

Both were rather boring meals, but both were very delicious. Simple is often better.

My Meal of the Week feature examines an airline meal from my travels over the years. This may be a meal from earlier in the week or it may be a meal served over a decade ago.
