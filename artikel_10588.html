GTA 5’s Michael actor hits back at claim GTA 6 went “woke” with Lucia

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

GTA 5’s Michael actor has hit back at claims that Rockstar went “woke” with GTA 6, defending the decision to put Lucia in a leading role.

Lucia and Jason are GTA 6’s two playable protagonists, with their relationship at the heart of Rockstar’s return to Vice City.

While many fans have enjoyed seeing their story play out from both perspectives, GTA 5 actor Ned Luke has given his take on the series being called “washed up”.

During an interview with Dexerto’s Fall Damage, the actor gave a blunt response to anyone taking issue with Lucia’s role in Rockstar’s latest open-world outing.

“Okay, Borg, whatever you say, you don’t interest me,” Luke replied, addressing the viewer by their username. He then questioned why having a female protagonist would be a problem in the first place.

“What is woke? Woke. Oh, why? Because they got a female protagonist,” Luke said. “What do they call those guys that don’t like women? Ah, idiots. That’s what they call them. Idiots,” he continued.

“Oh, we can’t have a female protagonist because they’re going woke. Oh, because they got a girl,” Luke said. “Okay. You know what, dude? That girl is so badass. She’s, she’s more badass than you could ever dream of being.”

Luke previously defended Lucia in a January 2024 interview with IGN, where he shared the following:

“You get a lot of these clowns out here going, ‘Rockstar’s going woke, they’re caving into the wokeness of the world,’” Luke added. “First of all, there’s been other female protagonists in the past, but not in something as huge as this.”

Despite the discourse around the protagonists, GTA 6 is now the joint best-reviewed game of all-time and has reportedly made over $750 million in pre-orders alone.

Fans have also been splashing out on Lucia, giving her some pretty stylish fits from the clothing stores across Leonida.
