Heated Rivalry Monopoly is coming this spring. Heres how to preorder.

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

PREORDER AT AMAZON: Monopoly Heated Rivalry Edition Board Game launches on June 1, 2027. Preorder are live at Amazon for $29.99.

We've gone to the cottage in our living rooms, at the movie theater, and now we'll be able to visit via a board game.

Hasbro launches Monopoly Heated Rivalry edition on June 1, 2027. Preorders are already live at Amazon for $29.99 which comes with free delivery on June 2.

With references to Season 1 and nods to Season 2, the Heated Rivalry edition of the classic board game is sure to please any fan. You'll be able to buy iconic locations from the show like hotels and the cottage while moving one of six game tokens. Those include a tuna melt, a banana, a smoothie cup, hockey skates, and even a stupid Canadian wolf bird.

Hasbro recommends the special edition Monopoly for players ages 17 and up. Since we're in holiday gift mode, consider this to be a fun surprise for that person in your life who simply will not shut up about the show (hi, that's me).
