GTA 6 fans are flying planes into buildings (or trying to)

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) launched on Nov. 19, prompting gamers everywhere to take the day off and indulge in a lawless land of hedonism and crime. Unsurprisingly, one of the first activities some people tried in the open world sandbox game was to fly a plane into a building. However, these antics haven't been producing the results many expected.

The Grand Theft Auto series generally revels in grand theft auto, grand theft aerial, and wanton urban destruction. Even so, there is particular sensitivity around the subject of crashing planes into buildings. The 9/11 attacks 25 years ago remain a pivotal moment in the U.S. consciousness, to such an extent that many people's minds immediately venture there when presented with a plane and a building.

In GTA 6's predecessor GTA 5, ramming an aircraft into a building would damage the plane and often cause it to explode, with Los Santos' indestructible highrises left intact. Previous Grand Theft Auto games also didn't allow planes to damage buildings. Nevertheless, some fans wondered if GTA 6 would skew this trend, particularly as it aims to feel realistic.

According to early gameplay clips, this doesn't seem to be the case. In a widely circulated clip by Twitch streamer Windpress, his character parachutes from a plane aimed at a building, only to watch the aircraft apparently pass harmlessly through it. Another popular GTA 6 gameplay video from @DiegoPalmaX showed a manned plane collide with a building but remain completely intact, slowly falling to the ground and even hitting another structure on the way down.

Such clips have drawn criticism from some Grand Theft Auto fans, who have commented on the lack of realism.

"you can't commit 9/11 in gta 6…" wrote X user @eveisthebastard. "'realism' smh…"

"people will undoubtably say stuff like this doesnt really matter and i would usually agree but when literally all the hype for this game has been 'wow look at all the details there are ten trillion things being simulated' then yeah it really does matter," said @fbq_2.

Meanwhile, others have argued that it's reasonable for developer Rockstar Games to prevent players from recreating the 9/11 attacks.

"[The point of the game] is to commit crimes. Not be a terrorist," wrote @_MiisterJ. "Why would a game let you recreate 9/11?... Out of everything the game has to offer you draw the line at doing 9/11??"

Some have suggested that GTA 6's unusual interactions between planes and buildings may be the result of bugs. Launch day bugs are common in new video games, with developers often issuing patches to fix them later on. Considering the strange behaviour of GTA 6's planes, particularly when compared to GTA 5, the idea that these are errors seems like a reasonable theory.

However, other social media users have attributed the viral videos of pristine planes to a toggle in GTA 6's settings. Found under gameplay settings, GTA 6 includes a Reduced Vehicle Damage toggle which increases the amount of abuse your car or plane can take. Though it hasn't been confirmed that this setting was responsible for the nonsensical plane-building interactions seen in widely spread GTA 6 clips, activating it could theoretically help prevent vehicles from becoming deadly fireballs.

Further GTA 6 gameplay videos have supported suggestions that the viral clips are unusual by capturing more typically expected interactions between buildings and planes. A clip from Twitch streamer ohnePixel shows his small plane exploding after he flies it into a building, though like in GTA 5 the building itself remains undamaged. Another video shared on X by @muaxh03 shows a plane being destroyed after its left wing hits a building, killing the player character.

In an interview with Love magazine, Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson stated that GTA 6 attempts to balance realism with immersion, indicating that it may deviate where strict adherence to reality would make the game less fun. Whether this includes declining to realistically depict what happens when a jet flies into a highrise will likely become clearer as more people jump in to play.
