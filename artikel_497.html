One of our low-light favorites has dropped to its lowest-ever price on Walmart thanks to this year's release of a new version. We loved the Canon EOS R6 II for its amazing high ISO noise handling and impressive -6.5 EV low-light autofocus, so despite the release of the EOS R6 III, this is still one of the best mirrorless cameras you can get for astrophotography.
