Your guide to the Disney+ and Hulu merger: When its coming, what to expect, and how much it will cost

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

The marrying of the Disney+ and Hulu apps is finally coming to fruition after a years-long effort. By the end of the 2026, we'll have a unified app, according to Disney CEO Josh D'Amaro.

Disney execs said the merger is "nearly complete" in their earnings call. Although there's no exact date specified, the app unification will happen before the end of 2026. Sometime in December, you can expect an official announcement and update. More plans to further enhance the integrated experience are also on deck for the spring.

In addition to the unified Disney+ and Hulu experience, Disney also plans to introduce creator content to the platform through its recent agreement with TikTok. The rollout will strategically coincide with the Avengers: Doomsday theatrical release on Dec. 18. More vertical video, live sports, and podcasts will also be added to the Disney+ platform, as the app continues to explore emerging content styles.

This is the million dollar question. Considering the app just raised prices — for standalone subscriptions as well as some bundles — in September 2026, we don't expect the cost to change too much when the integrated app launches in December. Standalone subscriptions jumped from $11.99 to $12.49 with ads and from $18.99 to $21.49 without. Meanwhile, the Disney+ and Hulu Basic bundle remained unchanged at $12.99, while the Premium bundle went up just two bucks from $19.99 to $21.99 per month.

It appears that by raising the standalone costs to nearly the same price as the bundle, the strategy is to push consumers to the bundle. The bundle is essentially the same thing as the integrated app will be, so we're expecting the price to remain pretty much on par: $12.99 with ads and $21.99 without. Of course, we'll sound the alarm if there's any major changes.

Black Friday streaming deals are live through Nov. 30 and I highly recommend jumping on the Disney+ and Hulu bundle deal while you can. The streamer is offering 12 months of the Basic bundle with ads for just $4.99 per month or the Premium ad-free bundle for only $14.99 per month.

By nabbing this discount, you'll bypass any potential price increases that may arise over the next 12 months. And TBH, it's the best deal we'll see for a long while.
