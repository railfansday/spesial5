6 Jewel-Toned Outfits to Wear to Every Holiday Party This Season

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Everyday opulence, ornate pieces with a Midas touch—however you choose to describe the fall/winter 2026 trend that’s taken over our feeds and carts, it’s now reaching its peak just in time for holiday dressing. And while lavish textures, from floral brocade to lustrous velvet and satin, are key to nailing the trend, they aren’t the only entry point. This party season, we’re opening up a jewel box of glittering gold, rich burgundy, and royal purple and embracing a more colorful approach to festive dressing.

When it comes to style inspiration, Zoë Kravitz is an early adopter of the trend, making her gold streak known both on the streets and, more recently, at the CFDA Awards, where she stepped out in a matching gold-foil Saint Laurent skirt set. You can easiyl re-create her look by pairing Magda Butrym’s gold lamé jacket with Zara’s black tuxedo trousers (complete with a built-in cummerbund) and The Row’s peep-toe pumps. The result? A dazzling look for that Christmas cocktail party you just RSVP’d to. Or, ring the silver bells in Liberowe’s metallic fringe maxi dress, styled with Bottega Veneta’s two-tone mules and Nour Hammour’s winter-white shearling jacket layered on top; drop earrings from Juju Vera and Rachel Comey are the sartorial equivalent of shimmering tinsel on your tree.

Shine bright in these jewel-toned outfits—all put together for a memorable holiday night out.

Go for fashion gold this holiday season with Magda Butrym’s gold lamé jacket and Zara’s black tuxedo pants, complete with a built-in cummerbund. Pair the dazzling duo with The Row’s peep-toe heels and Savette’s pearl-encrusted pouch bag. For an extra dose of opulence, pin Juju Vera’s gold shoulder dusters in your ears and a pretty floral brooch on your jacket.

Royal purple’s reign over fashion goes beyond colorful crewneck sweaters and statement scarves and straight into holiday dressing territory. Perfect for the non-dress enthusiast, Maria McManus’s silky satin pantsuit in dark plum makes a subtle statement, especially when styled with Dagmar’s tailored overcoat and a pair of chartreuse Saint Laurent pumps. Complete the look with Khaite’s calf-hair clutch in the same trending shade and Rachel Comey’s festive “firework” earrings.

In anticipation of your next soirée, trade your go-to LBD for something a little more striking—Tove’s crushed-velvet maxi dress in stunning sapphire, to be exact. Keep the styling sleek with Almada’s black shearling coat, then slip into a pair of Herbert Levine’s crystal-embellished pumps. Khaite’s sculptural silver earrings and Because of Alice’s fringed silk-satin scarf offer up a modern edge, while Colleen Allen’s velvet clutch in the same rich hue ties it all together.

Like a vintage Barolo, this next party look gets better with time. For a minimalist yet still rich approach to holiday dressing, pair Khaite’s ruby-red velvet midiskirt with Colleen Allen’s allover lace top, layered over Massimo Dutti’s black balconette bra. Liffner’s satin clutch in coordinating burgundy makes for a sophisticated color pairing, while Manolo Blahnik’s strappy gold-and-velvet sandals and Jennifer Behr’s dainty bow provide a hint of romance.

Take your cue from the shimmering tinsel on your tree and slip into Liberowe’s metallic fringe maxi dress this holiday season. Keep the festive spirit going with Bottega Veneta’s gold-and-silver woven mules and Mango’s ornamental drop earrings; Nour Hammour’s winter-white shearling jacket adds just a touch of Old Hollywood glamour. For a decidedly retro spin, accessorize with The Row’s sleek black clutch and Gigi Burris’s pillbox hat.

Give ladylike dressing a festive twist with Carolina Herrera’s radiant green sequin skirt and Posse’s embellished tank. Play up the outfit’s feminine appeal with Me+Em’s layered pearl necklace and a pair of lace tights from Swedish Stockings. As for accessories? Double down on the jewel tone with Mango’s emerald-green velvet slingbacks and Mark Cross’s elegant satin clutch.
