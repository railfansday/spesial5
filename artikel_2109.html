Left out: High mileage Hammer’s injury sparks longer term fitness worries | West Ham News

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

There’ll be some relief amongst the West Ham medical staff at Rush Green tonight knowing that Manor Solomon was left out of Israel’s match altogether after the winger could not train owing to the pain of an ongoing injury.

However there will be concern that this same issue which dogged Solomon through September might yet prevent him playing his full part in the busy run up to Christmas  – with its demanding schedule of nine games in a little over a month.

Solomon came back from the last international break in September looking washed out, and in truth, his performances since have been a disappointment after his early season impact and man-of-the-match award.

Solomon – early season form has fallen away as his back injury has made itself known

There’s been a sneaking suspicion that Solomon has been playing with an injury -and this would seem to be confirmed by his being left out of his national side. In truth he probably should not even have travelled. According to Yahoo Sports:

“The winger missed out on the Israel squad completely for the 0-0 draw with Kosovo on Saturday.

Solomon had been suffering from back issues, and only partially trained on Wednesday.

He participated in the first session, before missing the second one and then tried to train on Friday.

However, Israeli boss Ran Ben-Shimon confirmed that he was in too much pain, and ultimately left him out of the contest.”

From a purely selfish club vs country perspective, West Ham could really do with the winger sitting out Israel’s next fixture against Austria on Tuesday evening.
