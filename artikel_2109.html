Cesar Peixoto confirms ‘important’ Wolves man’s return to training ahead of Charlton Athletic clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Wolves host Charlton Athletic this weekend as they look to go top of the Championship table with a win. Cesar Peixoto’s side are currently 4th in the table but are just one point behind Birmingham City, Middlesbrough and Stoke City ahead of them.

Ahead of the Charlton clash, Peixoto has been speaking to The Express and Star providing a full update on his squad. The Wolves boss had plenty of positive news, including that Fer Lopez is closing in on a return to full fitness.

“Fer did not train in the first week (of the international break) because he was recovering from injury.” said Peixoto. “Fer had a good week of training. We had to manage him on Thursday, but he trained well and needed this kind of training with the team.

“Fer is not 100 per cent. He has a little pain, but nothing important at this stage. But we are going to see a better Fer, because when he came back from the previous international break he was injured and played with pain.

“We have to manage the minutes with him. He is an important player for us. But I think he will be better than before.”

Peixoto also said that Jean-Ricner Bellegarde and Jordan James have trained on Thursday and Friday but are not 100%. On another positive note, Bertrand Traore and Boubacar Traore have returned to full training.

Lopez has emerged as one of Wolves’ most influential players this season, with the 22-year-old making an impressive impact following his return from a loan spell at Celta Vigo.

The Spanish attacking midfielder has scored six goals and provided three assists in 14 Championship appearances, accumulating 1,097 minutes and an average rating of 7.58, according to FotMob. He has also contributed a goal in the Carabao Cup, taking his overall return to seven goals and three assists in 16 appearances.

The summer 2025 signing has become a key figure under Peixoto, helping Wolves climb to fourth in the Championship with 28 points from 15 matches. His return from a loan spell at Celta Vigo has proved particularly valuable, with Wolves now targeting an immediate return to the Premier League.

Lopez’ return is a massive plus for Wolves, however they need to wary with him. The Spaniard has already picked up several knocks in this campaign, which will be a concern,

Peixoto is clearly aware of the need to careful with him and will want to protect him this season. So far this campaign they have got away with him just picking up the odd short-term issue but if they keep persisting he will suffer a long-term problem, which would be a massive concern for Wolves this season.
