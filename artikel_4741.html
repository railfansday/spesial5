A WHITE DRESS shirt earns its place in your closet because you can wear it to a wedding on Saturday and back to work on Monday. But fit is everything. Nothing beats going custom or getting fitted in person, but you don’t need to spend a fortune at a tailor to find one that looks good. We found shirts online for a wide range of builds, including options with separate neck and sleeve sizing that can get you closer to the right fit off the rack.

The Men’s Health style team wore these shirts through commutes and long days at the office. We looked for shirts that fit well without alterations and still felt comfortable hours later. Some have the crisp feel you want with a suit; others have enough stretch that you won’t be thinking about your shirt all day. Here are the nine that made the cut.

Cotton is the gold standard for dress shirts, with Egyptian and Supima cotton being the softest and most durable. Cotton blends with a bit of stretch add comfort and flexibility for all-day wear.

Twill and Oxford weaves are slightly heavier, more textured, and naturally resist wrinkles.

For formal events or business settings, pairing a white dress shirt with a dark suit is a classic choice that never fails—the crispness of the white dress shirt against the darker suit creates a nice contrast that exudes professionalism—and you should complement it with a tie and polished leather dress shoes.

For less formal dress codes in business-casual settings or smart-casual environments, wear white dress shirts with chinos or similar styles to look sharp but also smart. You can also roll up the sleeves slightly or leave the top buttons undone to look more relaxed. Here, you can match these shirts with loafers or even dress sneakers to look modern while feeling as comfortable as ever.

To dress down with white dress shirts for casual outings or weekend gatherings, layer them under a well-fitted blazer or denim jacket to strike a balance between formal and relaxed. You can also wear these shirts untucked with jeans and khakis for a laid-back yet put-together appearance.
