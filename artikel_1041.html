Juventus midfielder Weston McKennie has suggested several US players could follow outgoing boss Mauricio Pochettino to Manchester United, after the Argentine succeeded the sacked Michael Carrick as head coach.

Pochettino has remained with the USMNT squad for their Nations League fixtures in this international window, both against Haiti, before he heads for Old Trafford.

And McKennie suggested several players could potentially follow the manager as he sets about a rebuild of the struggling side, who are currently in the Premier League relegation zone.

Speaking at a press conference, McKennie said: “USMNT players at Manchester United? I think that, obviously, the time Pochettino spent here allowed him to appreciate this squad and see the quality it has to offer.

“And yes, I think it's a possibility. We have a lot of guys on this team who will probably make a move soon or within the next year. So I think the links are somewhat inevitable.

“At the end of the day, though, you never know what's going through a manager’s head or what kind of players he's looking for.

“Still, I think you could see a few of us linked with United, and maybe it will actually happen.”

Pochettino is taking several of his staff with him to United, with goalkeeper coach Toni Jimenez, assistant Jesus Perez and fitness coach Sebastian Pochettino having already left for England after the US’ 6-0 victory over Haiti on Saturday.

Assistant coach Miguel D’Agostino, fitness coach Sylvia Tuya Vinas and analyst Alex Scott will follow them after Tuesday’s second leg of the quarter-finals.

Pochettino said on Monday: “That was the plan when we agreed terms with Manchester United and with the national team. They are preparing all there because Wednesday when we arrive, it’s going to be nonstop, and then start with playing games. We are focused here into finishing the best way.”

For his part, the former Southampton, Tottenham and Chelsea boss refused to be drawn into speculation over whether he will sign some of his former US charges in the winter transfer window.

He said: “All the good players always have dreams to reach the (highest) level, and United is one of the teams in that level.

“In case some decisions need to be made, we are going to pay attention to the players we know really well.”
