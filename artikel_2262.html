Why Sebastien Pocognoli picked James Penrice as he talks up Rangers star's quality

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Scotland manager Sebastien Pocognoli insists the selection of Rangers' James Penrice was "logical."

The left-back, who has also operated in midfield, has been rewarded for his form so far since moving to Ibrox on loan from AEK Athens. He immediately impressed since joining but was left out of Pocognoli's first squad for the last camp.

Now, the 27-year-old will get the chance to win his first cap when the national team takes on North Macedonia and Switzerland this month.

The Scotland boss says the quality Penrice possesses, and his readiness for his system, made him an obvious choice.

He said: "We have a list of players that we select regarding the performance at their clubs, but also the style of play or the way that we want to play.

"We have some missing players around the left side.

"So, combined with his quality and what we are looking for on the pitch, it's a logical choice for me and it can be very useful for the system that we want to play and also I think he deserves it."

Scotland face North Macedonia at Hampden on November 13 before an away game in Switzerland on November 16.
