How long West Brom face without George Campbell as James Morrison confirms injury extent

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

West Brom host Millwall in the Championship this Saturday, and they will hope to extend their league unbeaten run to six games. However, WBA will have to do so without club captain George Campbell, who withdrew from the Southampton draw after feeling his groin.

Now, ahead of the Millwall tie, Baggies boss James Morrison has confirmed Campbell will be absent against the Lions. Speaking to the club’s official media, he said: “Unfortunately George picked up a groin injury against Southampton and we expect him to be out for between ten to 12 days. It’s not ideal, but it could’ve been worse. Hopefully it’ll just be one game that he misses.”

Campbell has played 15 times in all competitions this season, sitting unused on the bench on only one occasion. As captain, he serves as a key presence at centre-back, so while it is a blow to lose him, there will be relief that he isn’t out for an extended spell.

There’s no hiding that Campbell is a loss for Albion. He was instrumental in their survival turnaround last season and emerged as a real leader, earning the captain’s armband and retaining a key role for the 2026/27 campaign.

Thankfully for West Brom and Morrison, he has a straightforward selection decision when dealing with Campbell’s absence. Nat Phillips replaced him at the break on Tuesday night, and he will more than likely do so from the start against Millwall.

In the two Championship games Campbell has not started, it has been fellow centre-back Chris Mepham who has stepped in as captain. As such, he seems the likely candidate to do so against the Lions, although Jayson Molumby has donned the armband before as well.

Morrison will be hoping his remaining WBA centre-backs can avoid suspension or injury, as only Mepham and Phillips remain with Campbell sidelined.

Campbell was called up for the United States national team for their November fixtures. But, with the extent of his injury now confirmed, it will be expected that he withdraws.

That will give the West Brom man the break to recover from his groin issue and work back to fitness for the return to club football. WBA face Swansea City in the first game back, and the hope is that Campbell will be fit.

Morrison and the physios will be sure to be careful with Campbell to ensure he doesn’t suffer a further setback. Losing him for one game is a blow, but seeing him cast to the sidelines for longer would spark real concern given the Baggies’ lack of centre-back depth.
