Puka Nacua injury will cause Rams' WR to miss extended time

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Los Angeles Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua will likely go on injured reserve and miss a minimum of four games with multiple injuries, head coach Sean McVay told reporters on Monday, Nov. 9.

Nacua is dealing with sprains to both his PCL and MCL and is also dealing with turf toe, McVay said.

"He's gonna be out for a good amount of time, will probably be a guy that we'll put on (injured reserve) as a result of that," McVay told reporters.

Nacua underwent an MRI on Monday as the Rams were already thin at receiver, with Davante Adams on the shelf for a few more weeks at minimum with a hamstring injury.

Nacua carried the Rams to a 20-13 victory over the Washington Commanders on Sunday, Nov. 8. He had 14 catches for 150 yards and a touchdown and had 19 targets, along with a pair of rushing attempts (for 20 yards). He was targeted 24 times in the Rams' overtime loss to the Buffalo Bills on Oct. 12.

Nacua entered the season with an ailing groin that cost him Weeks 2 and 3. He suffered this knee injury on the final play of the game against the Commanders.

Nacua has scored in four of his last five games (including three straight) and is seventh in the NFL in receiving yards (715) and tied for third in catches (70).

The Rams' next four games are at the Arizona Cardinals, home against the Green Bay Packers (Thanksgiving Eve) and Kansas City Chiefs and on the road for a divisional game against the San Francisco 49ers on Dec. 13. A Week 11 bye for the Rams means Nacua can return before the Rams host the Cowboys on Dec. 20.
