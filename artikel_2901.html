Nvidia Remains at the Core of AI Growth, Says Ivan Feinseth

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

A selloff in technology giants dragged down stocks, with the market also retreating on concerns over fresh US tariffs. Skepticism surfaced over whether the artificial-intelligence trade can mount a sustained rebound, with the Nasdaq 100 falling 1.5%. Nvidia Corp. lost 5%. Anthropic PBC sank below its $83 initial public offering price. Ivan Feinseth, CIO and Senior Partner at Tigress Financial Partners, explains why he remains bullish on tech and why he's maintaining his strong buy rating on Nvidia. (Source: Bloomberg)
