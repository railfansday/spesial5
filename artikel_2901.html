Could Luka Doncic average 40 points for season? ‘You never know’

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

The 2026-27 NBA season may still be in its early stages, but Luka Dončić's historic start is putting his name in the history books.

And while the Lakers still have 72 regular-season games left, the Slovenian superstar is on the trajectory to achieve a feat none of the league's great scorers have accomplished over the last 64 years.

Luka Doncic (77) of the Los Angeles Lakers hushes the crowd after sinking a three pointer against the Utah Jazz during the second half of their game at the Delta Center in Salt Lake City, Utah, Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026. Getty Images

With his season-high 53 points in Friday's home win over the Trail Blazers, Dončić has scored 361 points through his first nine games of the season, which is the most by a player through their first nine games since Wilt Chamberlain (472 points) in 1962-63. Chamberlain also scored 455 points to open the 1961-62 season.

Chamberlain ended those seasons with scoring averages of 50.4 (1961-62) and 44.8 (1962-63), which are the highest marks in NBA history.

Dončić enters Sunday's road game against the Pistons, which he's questionable for because of low back tightness, averaging a league-best 40.1 points.

Chamberlain is the only player in league history to average at least 40 points for a season.

The closest anyone else has gotten was Jordan at 37.1 points in 1986-87. Harden (36.1 in 2018-19), Rick Barry (35.6 in 1966-67) and Bryant (35.4 in 2005-06) are the only other players in league history to average at least 35 points for a season.

Could Luka Doncic join Wilt Chamberlain as the lone players to average at least 40 points for a season? NBAE via Getty Images

Dončić's career-high scoring average for a season is 33.9 points, which he set in 2023-24 while with the Mavericks. He averaged 33.5 points in 2025-26 in his first full season with the Lakers.

But with the start to the season he's gotten off to, it begged the question: Could he join Chamberlain as the lone players to average at least 40 points for a season?

"It's obviously a long season," Dončić said. "We're just 10 games in. We got 72 left. But as long as we win, I'm good. Hopefully it continues, but you never know."

The answer to that question won't be known until the season is over.

But with Dončić being one of the most prolific scorers in league history, the possibility can't be ruled out.
