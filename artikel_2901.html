Fantasy Basketball Waiver Wire Week 5: Danny Wolf, De’Anthony Melton stepping into bigger roles

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

After a fairly quiet Week 3, the injury-bug reared its ugly head once again. Steph Curry is expected to miss up to a month with an oblique abdominal tear, and rookie Caleb Wilson will be sidelined for 4-6 weeks with a hand injury. Austin Reaves injured his heel, and it's unknown when he'll return.

This week's top adds feature replacements for all three players as well as a pair of three-and-D big men and guys who have stepped up in a major way off the bench to deliver useful fantasy production.

Here are my top fantasy basketball adds ahead of Week 5.

Others to consider: Cameron Johnson (32%), Meleek Thomas (23%), Derrick Jones Jr. (23%), Reed Sheppard (22%), Jamal Shead (20%), Bones Hyland (17%), Keldon Johnson (12%), Taylor Hendricks (4%)

With Curry out multiple weeks due to an oblique injury, Melton should be a fixture in the Warriors' starting five.

He got the start Friday and posted a useful 8/4/3/1/1 line with a pair of triples. Melton logged only 18 minutes, but only one starter saw more than 24 minutes in a blowout loss to the Spurs.

The Warriors have a four-game week that starts with a highly-favorable matchup with the spiraling Pelicans on Monday, and that game should be more competitive. Take a chance on Melton, who should see more playing time than he did in his last game out.

Over his last three games (one start), Spencer averaged 15.3 points, 2.0 rebounds, 5.3 assists, 3.0 triples and 1.0 "stocks" across 26 minutes.

He remains a strong per-minute producer who's offering useful points, assists and triples off the bench, and he's got huge upside when inserted into the starting lineup. Spencer's roster percentage continues to rise, and managers in need of his skillset should add him while he's still available in nearly three quarters of Yahoo! leagues.

Hunter averaged 17.7 points, 4.0 rebounds, 1.7 assists, 0.7 steals and 2.3 triples across 35.7 minutes over his last three games. He's been logging heavy minutes for a Kings team that's desperately searching for answers on both ends of the floor, and that should continue moving forward.

Hunter sat out Sunday with a hip issue, and the Kings don't play again until Wednesday. That gives him time to rest up and get back on the court, but it also means you'll need to have other options for Monday and Tuesday if you decide to pick him up.

Oubre Jr. has been locked into the starting five, averaging 15 points, 4.3 rebounds, 1.3 assists, 1.8 steals and 1.0 triples across his last four games, good for a Top-100 week.

Indiana's injury report is among the longest in the Association and seemingly always full of key players. Oubre Jr. is worth a look for as long as he's running with the first unit, and he's worth a look off the waiver wire.

Reaves is still out with a heel injury, and Grimes stepped up in a big way in AR's absence. He finished with 22/6/1/2 with five triples in Sunday's win over Phoenix, and he's now averaged 16 points, 3.3 rebounds, 3.3 assists, 2.3 steals and 2.3 triples across 35.7 minutes in three starts this season.

The Lakers play only three games this week, but they have a frontloaded schedule with a Tuesday-Wednesday back-to-back set. That could mean at least one more absence for Reaves or even a maintenance day for Luka Doncic.

Wolf has started four straight games, but he's been at his best over his last two with averages of 12 points, 8.5 rebounds, 2.5 assists, 2.5 triples and 2.0 "stocks."

Nolan Traore returned to the Nets' lineup following a one-game absence, but he didn't return to the starting five. Brooklyn has been playing a quadruple-big lineup featuring Day'Ron Sharpe, Michael Porter Jr., Julius Randle and Wolf, and that may be the trend moving forward.

Wolf is a big who can knock it down from outside and swat shots, which is always a highly-favorable skillset in fantasy hoops.

Proctor recorded his first career triple-double 24/11/10 in Saturday's win over Dallas, but I'm not just chasing that game. He's averaged 20.3 points, 6.0 rebounds, 4.7 assists and 4.0 triples over his last three games.

Sam Merrill and Evan Mobley have already been ruled out for Monday's matchup with the Bulls, and Peyton Watson and Jaylon Tyson are questionable.

The Cavaliers play a three-game week with games on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, so even if Proctor's role diminishes as players become healthy, he can be dropped for a weekend streamer to end the week.

Bamba has been productive off the bench behind Jaxson Hayes, and the former was outstanding in Sunday's spot start with Hayes ruled out.

Bamba is another big who can rack up swats and triples, and Sunday's huge 26/5/4/2/1 line with six triples highlights his tremendous upside when given meaningful minutes. With Jusuf Nurkic and Jaren Jackson Jr. still out, Bamba is worth a look as Utah searches for frontcourt production.

Jalen Duren is out for at least another week, Isaiah Joe is still banged up, and Duncan Robinson injured his thigh on Saturday.

Huerter delivered a monstrous 24/5/5 line with two steals, five blocks and four triples in a loss to the Lakers, and he followed that up with three solid performances.

Across his last four, he's averaged 16.8 points, 3.0 rebounds, 3.8 assists, 1.3 steals, 2.3 blocks and 1.8 triples across 32.5 minutes per game. Until his minutes and production fade, Huerter is worth streaming, and he's widely available in Yahoo! leagues.

Patrick Williams is surprisingly back in our plans after a Top-50 fantasy week, and he should continue to see increased run until Wilson returns.

Over his last three games, Williams averaged 12.0 points, 5.3 rebounds, 2.3 assists, 2.0 triples and 2.0 "stocks" across 26 minutes while shooting 65% from the floor and 80% from the charity stripe.

Chicago has four contests on tap this week, making P-Will a worthwhile pickup for Week 5 and beyond.
