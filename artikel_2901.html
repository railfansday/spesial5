Watch LeBron take a swig of a fan's drink during the game

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

LeBron James loves his wine, and being in the middle of a game isn't going to stop him from drinking some from a fan's cup courtside.

"I spilled some of his wine. I crashed into him, spilled some of his wine. So I wanted to see if it was worth it on my arm and how good it was. It was all right," LeBron said postgame.

Ignoring the fact that it looked a lot more like he took a drink of a Coke (maybe Diet Coke) in the video — I fear what a glass that size full of wine would cost in an NBA arena — we'll let the wine joke slide with a laugh.

LeBron ended the night with 15 points, and the 76ers improved to 7-1 on the season with a comfortable win against the Bulls.
