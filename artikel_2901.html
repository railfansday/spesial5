Captains conversation: SJ-O football preps for 3A state title game

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

➜ Maddux Musselman: It means a lot. I appreciate it a lot. It means everything, honestly. I enjoy being a leader on the team. It's a lot of fun.

➜ Brayden Waller: I feel like all of us stick out in some area. Muss is the captain of the defensive backs, Cam with the line and Kaden with the wide receivers. I think you find a way to lead a certain group, and then you put all of those together to make a team.

➜ Waller: I call heads or tails. Skinner and I came up with the philosophy that we see which side is heavier of the coin, and then we call the opposite side. I'm undefeated this season when I call. We see what we feel is best and call it.

➜ Kaden Wedig: Just like a brotherhood. It feels good to walk out there. I've been playing with these guys for a long time now, so being able to go out there and represent the team is a really cool feeling.

➜ Cameron Wagner: On the field, we're locked in and focused. Off the field, we're all stupid together. We just hang out. We're not above anybody else in the school. Kaden and I have been best friends since kindergarten. It's a really tight-knit brotherhood, and we're all just stupid together.

➜ Wedig: Probably the craziest feeling. Ever. This is something that we've worked for ever since I started playing football. The goal when you're in youth football is to win a championship, and when you get to high school, it's a state championship. There's going to be a lot of adrenaline. A lot of feelings. I'll probably be super, super amped before the game, and I wouldn't want to do it with a different group of guys. It's going to feel surreal.

➜ Musselman: It's been pretty great. All of the teachers have been talking to us and wishing us good luck. That walk-through the school just before the end of the day was pretty cool. I've never been in something like that before.

➜ Waller: We're obviously super close. Having Cam gives you another dynamic. One, you get to learn different sides of the recruiting part and what life is going to be like in a couple months for him. He's pretty versatile. He can play fullback. He can play running back now, too.

Wagner: It's been awesome. With us seniors, I think we had about 12 guys on our freshmen team, so it wasn't a big class. We had some guys come back, like Tyler Wright and Alex Vaughn being big parts of this team. We're a band of brothers. When it comes to football, we all lock in.

➜ Wedig: Cameron and I usually sit in the back of the bus and we normally do some stupid TikTok. I don't want to say we're goofing off because we'll lock in, but we're just kids at the end of the day. Once we get on that field, the switch is flipped.

➜ Musselman: No regrets. I just want to be happy with how I performed. I don't want to be in a state where I feel like I left something out there or have the feeling I could have done something better. That's what I'm really hoping for.

➜ Waller: I wrote an essay the other day for a scholarship, and I said it's been one of the most influential parts of my life. You learn everything from how to wake up early in the mornings to being on time. If you weren't on time for weights, you'd hear it from (assistant coach Ian) Sweeney. You learn how to communicate with people. Football is a great game itself, but playing at St. Joe with the culture that we've built and the program we have, it's pretty special.

➜ Wedig: Hopefully, that we get a win on Friday and we can talk about that. Going to Brayden's house on Thursday nights are always fun. It helps us loosen up.

➜ Waller: We had some pretty good prank calls (laughs).

➜ Wagner: Beating Unity twice our senior season.

➜ Wagner: After I started getting more offers, multiple schools started reaching out asking if I would transfer. I would always tell them no, and honestly, one of the biggest reasons was because I had so much family around here. It's been awesome just the road we've had, too. We were so close last year, and we've worked so hard all year to get back to the state championship game. Now, we have the opportunity to make school history.
