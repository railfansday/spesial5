Objections to .php application being published on ICANN site

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

After Automattic's confirmation that they would switch their .vibe application to the replacement string of .php1, ICANN has started to publish various objections on the Application Comment Forum (ACF) section of their new gTLD program site2. They mostly relate to the confusion and security risk posed by a common filetype (especially in web development) becoming a TLD, similar to the outcry over Google's application for .zip (which was eventually successfully delegated). The other common theme is the objection to a private company effectively trying to claim what is an open-source project.

The first is by PHP developer Larry Garfield who accused the WordPress parent of "Inappropriate Squatting":3

WordPress contributor Jamie VanRaalte objected on the grounds of "Part of a pattern - .php should not go to one company" and also said the .wordpress application was a similarly inappropriate power grab of another open-source project:4

Web developer Ryan Maietta highlighted the "File Extension Confusion and Security Risks" of using an established filetype as a TLD and the risk of people confusing websites using this domain as being officially endorsed by the PHP project:5

Forensic analyst Herbert Bärschneider also objected simply on the grounds of confusing the TLD with the filetype:6
