West Brom begin talks over new contract for £1.5m star

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

West Brom named George Campbell as their new club captain in the summer following a stellar debut campaign from the American defender. Signed for £1.5m from CF Montreal in 2025, he has emerged as a key player for WBA.

Now, the Baggies are looking to reward Campbell’s success with a new contract. Reporter Mike McGrath says on X that the club are in talks over a fresh deal for the 25-year-old, despite the fact he is already tied down until 2029 as it stands.

Prior to the summer transfer window, reports stated that Campbell had interest from Scottish giants Celtic, amongst other clubs. A new contract would further secure the American international’s future at The Hawthorns in case admiration from elsewhere grows.

Campbell had impressed in the MLS with CF Montreal after coming through the Atlanta United academy. He has made the step up to Championship football brilliantly, swiftly locking down a starting spot last term and growing in influence over time.

The Pennsylvania native spent the early days of his West Brom career playing at right-back, but shortly after his caretaker appointment, James Morrison moved Campbell into his regular central role and it brought instant results. In the final 11 games of last season, Campbell started all at centre-back and WBA were beaten just once, keeping seven clean sheets along the way.

The American provided some key attacking contributions too, scoring the Baggies’ only goals in draws against Charlton Athletic and Sheffield United, and in a win over Bristol City.

Campbell’s emergence as a key player saw his leadership traits shine through, earning him the captaincy in the summer. He has retained a big role in Albion’s success this term, contributing to the joint-tightest defence in the league with 13 goals conceded while ranking highly for successful passes (694 – top 5% of Championship centre-backs) and successful dribbles (eight – top 3%).

A new Campbell contract isn’t a must because of the time remaining on his existing deal. However, it will play a big part in keeping him happy and fending off potential admirers.

Improved terms reflect his value in the side as West Brom captain and a standout centre-back. It means that any potential admirers will have to part with a pretty penny to try and tempt him away from The Hawthorns.

His club success has brought more opportunities on the international stage, starting in two USMNT fixtures over the last break. As Campbell’s stock grows, WBA know they have to keep him happy if he’s to stay for the foreseeable.
