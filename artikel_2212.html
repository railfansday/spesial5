Top 9 highest paid English players in the EFL Championship

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Championship has been one of the most entertaining divisions in world football for many years, but in recent times, the league has attracted plenty more attention.

English football's second tier has become a strong breeding ground for talent, be it homegrown or imported, with many star names subsequently going on to ply their trade at the top level, both domestically and on the international stage.

Many of the England squad which reached the FIFA World Cup 2026 semi-final and finished third overall under Thomas Tuchel have enjoyed permanent or loan spells in the Championship, creating a pathway for many current youngsters who are flourishing to do the same in the future.

With that being said, there are also many current high-profile names and clubs residing in the Championship for 2026/27, and Football League World have looked at the nine English players who are ESTIMATED to be receiving the highest wage sums, as per Capology.

In ninth is Burnley's Marcus Edwards, with the former Tottenham Hotspur man earning £50,000 per week.

The 27-year-old's time at Turf Moor has certainly been one of intrigue, as he was a part of Scott Parker's promotion-winning side two years ago, before struggling to make an impact in last season's Premier League.

The Cyprus international winger was also initially omitted from the Clarets' 25-man squad for this season after Deadline Day in September, before being reinstated by former boss, Nicky Hayen, after a four-game absence.

He has since started all three of Fabio Celestini's first matches in charge.

Kalvin Phillips' recent career path has generated plenty of interest, with the 2020/21 England Player of the Year now in the thick of a second loan spell at Sheffield United from Manchester City.

The experienced midfield player's £42m switch to the Etihad Stadium from Leeds United simply hasn't worked, and despite an underwhelming first stint at Bramall Lane during the second half of last season through suspension and fitness issues, Phillips returned to S2 this summer.

Like Edwards, the 30-year-old is said to be earning £50,000 per week, although it remains to be seen how much of said figure is paid by the Blades.

Max Kilman is the third and final player on this list said to be earning a £50,000 weekly salary, this time with West Ham United.

The Irons parted with £40m two summers ago to secure the defender's signature from Wolves, but it is fair to say that, during his time at the London Stadium in the Premier League, Kilman's reputation with supporters was far from the greatest.

Despite West Ham's shaky start and high-profile managerial change, the 29-year-old has turned around his own individual performance levels and has already chipped in with two goals at the other end of the pitch.

Adam Armstrong has become a guaranteed goalscorer at Championship level over time, and recently marked his 500th career appearance.

Wolves signed the forward from Southampton in January for £7m, but he was unable to aid a lost survival cause under Cesar Peixoto's predecessor, Rob Edwards.

Goals have recently started to flow again for Armstrong, though, who Peixoto will be largely leaning on until January due to Raul Jimenez's well-documented injury, whilst he earns an estimated weekly wage of £65,000.

It is estimated by Capology that Jarrod Bowen earns £75,000 per week as West Ham United captain, but such a figure can certainly be marked with an asterisk given the fact the attacker was the only player at the London Stadium reportedly not hit by a wage reduction after relegation.

The 29-year-old was the subject of intense transfer interest from clubs competing in European football this summer, but agreed to the new deal on July 17th.

The aforementioned contract does include a release clause, though, which will become active if Bowen's current employers do not achieve a first-time promotion under new boss, Scott Parker.

He is joined on that estimated figure by Kyle Walker, who is into his second season with Burnley after leaving Manchester City.

The 36-year-old has won six Premier League titles during his career, but moved to Turf Moor under the aforementioned Parker on a two-year contract after departing the Etihad Stadium.

There was speculation over the former England international's future post-relegation, and it's fair to say his spell in East Lancashire hasn't got any better of late given the club's situation inside the Championship's relegation zone.

Another former England international full-back takes joint-second spot on this list in the form of Kieran Trippier, who earns an estimated £100,000 per week wage.

After leaving Newcastle United this summer, Trippier agreed a pre-contract agreement with Wolves, although this led to immediate jeopardy given the club's decision to replace Edwards with Peixoto in June, shortly after he and the aforementioned Jimenez had signed such deals.

However, the 36-year-old's early-season influence at Molineux hasn't been played down whatsoever, even if he hasn't featured in each and every fixture.

James Ward-Prowse is another star Championship name who has earned international recognition in the past, and like Trippier, he is said to be earning £100,000 per week after returning to Southampton this summer from West Ham United.

The Hammers paid Saints £30m for his services just three years ago, but alongside loan spells with Nottingham Forest and Burnley, the technically-gifted midfielder fell out of favour under Nuno, meaning his days appeared numbered in E20 this summer.

With Southampton looking to return to the Premier League this season despite a four-point deduction after Spygate, their returning academy graduate has already become a regular at St Mary's once again under Tonda Eckert.

However, one man who isn't favoured by Southampton's head coach is Aaron Ramsdale, despite the fact he is believed to be the Championship's highest-earning Englishman on £120,000 per week.

The 28-year-old signed under Russell Martin in 2025 for £25m, but after being a mainstay in Saints' relegation season, he was loaned out to Newcastle United last term, with the Magpies paying a reported £4m loan fee.

However, Ramsdale's former loan employers opted not to exercise the option-to-buy within said agreement, and he is yet to feature for his current side this season, with Eckert re-signing Daniel Peretz from Bayern Munich on a permanent basis instead.
