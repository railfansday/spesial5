Swansea City, Portsmouth & Barnsley in race to secure Aston Villa transfer agreement

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Aston Villa academy graduate TJ Carroll is attracting loan interest from clubs in the EFL, with it expected that the youngster sources regular first-team football away from Villa Park in January.

That is according to the Daily Mail's Midlands football correspondent, Tom Collomosse, with the Championship pair of Portsmouth and Swansea City namedropped, as well as League One strugglers, Barnsley.

Villa are currently in the midst of another campaign involving European football under Unai Emery, with the Spaniard bringing home a first trophy in 30 years five months ago in the form of the UEFA Europa League.

The B6 club have given plenty of exposure to young talents throughout Emery's four-year tenure, and Carroll is one of many who have earned praise for their performance levels in the club's highly-successful youth setup.

The Villans have already sanctioned two loans involving Championship sides this campaign, with Joe Gauci and Samuel Iling-Junior joining Lincoln City and Bolton Wanderers respectively, and a third could be in store involving Carroll.

The centre-back has progressed through the Bodymoor Heath ranks from Under-9s level to now being on the fringes of Emery's new-look first-team.

Carroll is yet to make a senior appearance for Villa, but has featured against League One and League Two opposition so far this season in the form of Wigan Athletic and Crewe Alexandra in the EFL Trophy.

He signed professional terms with the West Midlands club in July 2025 having excelled in the Under-18s side which defeated Manchester City in the FA Youth Cup and National finals two months' previous, with his goal in the former initially levelling the scores before Villa emerged 3-1 victors.

It is now claimed that such progress has alerted Portsmouth, Swansea and Barnsley, with the South Coast side already agreeing three Premier League loans so far including Brentford's Benjamin Arthur, although the centre-back is currently sidelined with a stress fracture.

The Tykes have also aided the progress of the aforementioned Gauci in recent times, as the Australian goalkeeper was loaned to the South Yorkshire club in 2024/25, and they would hope for a repeat involving Carroll.

Carroll's first-team exposure with Villa mainly came in pre-season ahead of the current campaign, which included outings against League Two promotion hopefuls Walsall, as well as FC Porto, Real Sociedad and BG Pathum United.

The youngster, who has 30 appearances at Under-21s level under his belt, has also featured in the UEFA Youth League, with Villa qualifying for the tournament through the exploits of Emery's first-team last term, and recently scored twice against FC Barcelona in a 3-3 draw.

Collomosse's report states that Pompey, the Swans and Tykes do face competition for Carroll's services in the form of Norwegian outfit, Viking FK.

The Stavanger-based outfit have gained notoriety in recent months for their own Champions League qualification, which includes a 3-3 draw with Bayern Munich, although their trip to Villa Park last month ended in a 3-1 loss.

With there being over a month until the January transfer window opens, circumstances may change which determine Carroll's potential destination, albeit one major call could have already influenced such decisions.

Swansea have conceded just 18 times in their first 15 Championship fixtures, but a poor run of recent results has now been compounded by Vitor Matos' controversial move to Ligue 1 side, RC Strasbourg.
