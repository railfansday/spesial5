Fer Lopez reacts in 4 words to what Sean Neave did for Wolves v Bristol City

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

Wolverhampton Wanderers playmaker Fer Lopez has taken to social media after fellow attacker and Newcastle United loanee, Sean Neave, netted a brace to ensure a late but vital three points against Bristol City at Ashton Gate.

The Old Gold and Black had won three of their last four matches ahead of Wednesday night's clash in BS3, but their recently strong run of form was in jeopardy once Scott Twine put Michael Skubala's side in front after 34 minutes with a stunning free-kick.

Despite dominating against the Robins, it seemed as though the chance for Cesar Peixoto's men to take anything from the game was ebbing away.

That was until the 19-year-old was introduced with 17 minutes to play, as he levelled the scores nine minutes later, tapping home after Adam Armstrong's effort was played into his path by Bradley Collins.

Buoyed by his maiden strike in senior football, the centre-forward then headed home Kieran Trippier's free-kick in the final minute of normal time in front of the travelling fans, with the victory enough to send Wolves into the automatic promotion places.

After seeing Peixoto come under pressure from sections of the fanbase, Wolves' recent run of form has given supporters plenty of encouragement once again.

The Portuguese's side have scored their 25 goals from all angles this season, with the majority of those coming from Lopez, who has scored six times in 13 appearances.

However, the Spaniard wasn't exactly at his free-flowing best against Bristol City, but the decision to replace him with Neave late on was clearly vindicated with those game-changing moments.

The attacking duo have clearly built up a strong relationship already, with Lopez taking to Instagram in the Ashton Gate dressing room and posting a picture of himself and the England youth international, which read: "Come on my kid," followed by three hearts in Wolves colours.

Neave has had to bide his time since arriving on loan from the Magpies on Deadline Day, with the vast majority of his 153 minutes of Championship action thus far coming in the last month, owing to various injury setbacks within Peixoto's attack.

The striker recorded his first assist for Lopez's equaliser in the comeback win at Burnley on October 16th, but after finding himself as the man fully responsible for yet another late turnaround in the West Country, he was unsurprisingly overjoyed.

“I’m just in shock," he said post-match. "My main aim when I came on was just to get involved in the game, make an impact and do what I could for the team.

"To come on and get two goals, it means a lot, especially in a game where we were losing and we’ve managed to turn it around and win 2-1. I’m still in shock, really. I haven’t got any words," he added.

In a short space of time, Neave's three goal contributions have aided what could be two priceless victories for Wolves in the promotion scene after an indifferent start, and more could certainly be on the cards between now and May.

Peixoto will be hoping that is the case, especially with Raul Jimenez still sidelined until January, whilst those observing his progress in the North East will also be excited when it comes to the forward's future career.
