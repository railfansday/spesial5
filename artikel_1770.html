Sheffield Wednesday boss Dave Challinor on what disappointed him in Stevenage draw plus admission on Leeds United loanee

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Wednesday, who triumphed at derby rivals Barnsley last Friday, had few threatening moments, although a bending shot from Louie Barry did test home keeper Tommy Setford in the first half.

Olly Sanderson and Terence Vancooten had opportunities for the hosts, while Yan Valery missed a good second-half chance for the visitors.

Michael Mellon hit a post late on for Boro.

Wednesday are in fourth place in the League One table ahead of Saturday's home game with Reading.

Challinor said: "Disappointed not to win, and that's not saying we deserve to win.

"We got into some good areas. There were bits that were better than Friday.

"We've got a clean sheet, and at times we look much, much better defensively.

"We got into some decent areas, but our quality in the final third was nowhere near good enough, resulting in us not creating enough opportunities. We created a lot of good situations but didn't work the goalkeeper enough, which again is a disappointment.

"We gave simple passes away. It became a little bit like basketball at times. We then got some control and played with a back four. We had five v four on the top line. We didn't exploit that enough.

"Thinking down the side, Jordi Liongola and Louie Barry could cause problems. We didn't give them enough service, and their quality wasn't good enough. In the second half, it was a similar story."

On-loan Leeds United player Gray, who has made 11 league appearances this season, was not involved in the match-day squad despite travelling with the party to Hertfordshire.

Challinor added: "We're still in a moment where we are trying to get (other) lads up to speed. "You don't want to go into a game thinking, ‘I want to get him minutes and I want to get him minutes’. But it was obviously Jordi (Liongola) and Louie’s first start for a while, Ali, to get him some minutes was good. "He (Al-Hamadi) has felt a little bit of a cough, but there's nothing (serious) there. I wanted answers myself really to see where he's at and the impact he can have."
