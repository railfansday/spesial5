Why has Ronaldinho come out of retirement? FREE live stream for Brazil legend’s Ravenna debut today

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Brazilian legend Ronaldinho will, this afternoon, make his first competitive appearance in more than a decade.

The former Barcelona and AC Milan has not played competitively since featuring for Fluminense in September 2015, though that will change on Sunday as he is confirmed to start for Italian third division side Ravenna against local rivals Forli.

"He will play from the start, there's no doubt," Ravenna boss Andrea Mandorlini told reporters. "Then together we will decide how long he plays.

"He is one of the greatest players of the modern era. And we have to get a result. We always play with three forwards. He will be one of the three."

This will be Ronaldinho’s long-awaited debut for the Serie C side after joining the club in August, with footage emerging on social media over the weekend to prove the 46-year-old has not lost a step.

Ravenna are currently sitting in second place - six points behind leaders Spezia - in the Serie C, Group B table and have won four of their last five matches, including the last three in a row.

Speaking in August after announcing his comeback, Ronaldinho revealed his target was to score his 300th career goal having initially retired on 299.
