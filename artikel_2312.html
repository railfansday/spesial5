Kimmich fit for Netherlands clash as Mohya faces another squad omission

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Germany have no new injury concerns following their 3-1 win over Serbia on Friday.

As reported by Sky Germany, the entire squad took part in training on Sunday, including Joshua Kimmich, who was on the receiving end of a heavy foul towards the end of the win in Belgrade.

Unless anything unexpected happens, the squad will remain unchanged for Monday's final 2026/27 UEFA Nations League Group A2 fixture against the Netherlands.

This means 17-year-old Wael Mohya is expected to miss out once more, with Klopp only allowed to name 23 of the 24 players called up.

He will still travel with the team for the match at the Olympiastadion in Berlin, though.
