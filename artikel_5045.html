Even though the broader cryptocurrency market faced bearish conditions over the past two months, the Stellar (XLM) network, a major rival to XRP, has topped other leading blockchains in net inflows over the past 30 days.

On November 24, 2026, Stellar network’s 30-day net inflow, a figure that shows the difference between minted and burned real-world asset (RWA) tokens, was $1.2 billion, according to metrics CoinPedia retrieved from RWA.xyz.

The other top-performing blockchains in the RWA space over the past 30 days included Injective (INJ), Coinbase-backed Base network, BNB Chain (BNB), and Robinhood, with $525 million, $347 million, $326 million, and $147 million, respectively.

Notably, Ethereum (ETH) saw the highest net outflow of RWA tokens over the past 30 days of $1.4 billion. Meanwhile, Avalanche (AVAX) and Aptos (APT) posted a net cash outflow of $191 million and $101 million, respectively.

Although the Stellar network saw the highest net inflow over the past 30 days, it was the fourth in asset value. As of press time, the ledger had $4.9 billion in asset value, compared to XRPL’s $3 billion, Solana (SOL)’s $5.1 billion, BNB Chain’s $6 billion, and Ethereum’s $14.3 billion.

Despite the notable cash inflow to the Stellar network, XLM’s price has faced heightened selling pressure over the past month.

With XLM trading at $0.13 at the time of publication, it had capitulated by roughly 24% over the past 30 days. As a result, the token had a market capitalization of about $4.61 billion and a 24-hour average trading volume of approximately $154 million, which surged by more than 85% on the day.

Nonetheless, a renewed adoption of the Stellar network through RWA tokenization bolsters the token’s macro Bullish outlook.
