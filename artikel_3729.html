All GTA 6 Smartphone Apps And What They Do

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Grand Theft Auto games have regularly kept up with the times, and in GTA 6, you once again have a smartphone that you can use to interact with the world around you. From getting new missions to goofing off by wasting some in-game time on social media, the smartphone in GTA 6 is a pretty handy tool for rising up the social--and criminal--ranks of Leonida. It comes equipped with several apps, and while not every one of them is essential, you'll want to keep an eye open for certain notifications. Here's a closer look at what each app does.

How to use your smartphone

As a quick reminder, you can whip out your smartphone by using the Up key on the D-pad. From there, you can navigate through the apps menu using the directional keys.

GTA 6 smartphone apps

WAiNK

The bread-and-butter app for aspiring car thieves, the WAiNK app can be used to inspect vehicles for any hidden security features. Scanning a car will let you see its estimated value, which security features it has installed, if it's locked, and how much it'll cost to register it under your name.

Scooter Bros

Need your personal vehicle in a hurry? A call to Scooter Bros will allow you to summon your ride to your location, so long as you don't mind the couriers stashing their little two-wheeled ride in your trunk.

Whatup

GTA 6's instant messaging app, this will allow you to get scoops and chat to characters, without ever needing to verbally engage with them. It's also useful for getting new missions and tips on how to make some quick cash with side-activities.

Camera

It's a camera. It can take selfies. What else were you expecting?

Gallery

Nothing too surprising here, as the Gallery app is used for checking out all the smartphone pictures you took. It also has a few archived pictures, which show off Jason and Lucia's life prior to the events that kicked off the GTA 6 storyline.

Fitness

Staying in shape is highly recommended in GTA 6, and this fitness app will allow you to track your gains and how your stats are steadily leveling up from all those deadlifting sessions.

Snapmatic

Do a little doomscrolling on the side with the social media app, which has a feed full of Leonida's citizens posting about their daily lives, as well as plenty of memes and brainrot content. You can even like posts and read the comments attached to them--if you dare.

News

Leonida is a busy city, and the news app will let you keep track of all the latest developments.

Weather

How many seasons can you expect in a single day? Check the weather app to find out.

Settings

It's not too detailed, but the option to change Jason's wallpaper and ringtone in the settings menu is a nice little touch.
