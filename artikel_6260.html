Amazon has a new restaurant delivery partner for its Prime members: Uber Eats, which replaces the Grubhub+ benefit Prime members had until today.

To celebrate the new partnership, Amazon Prime members can get $20 off each of their first two eligible Uber Eats orders of $25 or more on linked accounts made via Amazon, as long as those orders are for U.S. restaurants and placed by Nov. 18.

Beyond the limited-time launch promotion, Prime members will get $0 delivery fees on eligible Uber Eats orders of $25 or more placed through the Amazon app or website. Interestingly, this appears to work separately from any existing Uber Eats account or benefits you may already have.

Related: Which is the best food delivery app?

There's no need to download another app or create a separate Uber Eats account to use this perk. When you opt in through Amazon, you can use your existing Prime account information, including linked payment methods.

Think of it almost like ordering a box of paper towels from Amazon with free Prime delivery, only this time it's a salad, burger, breakfast tacos or fries. You place the order via Uber Eats in your Amazon account, almost as you would any other Prime delivery, but this time it is fulfilled by Uber Eats instead of your friendly neighborhood Amazon delivery truck.

Amazon has also said that there will be additional Prime-exclusive offers available only on Amazon that could save you more than $175 per year, so we'll be sure to keep an eye out for those.

TPG tip: Amazon Prime Visa cardmembers earn 5% back on all Uber Eats orders through Dec. 5, 2026 with eligible Prime membership.

The information for the Prime Visa has been collected independently by The Points Guy. The card details on this page have not been reviewed or provided by the card issuer.

This $20 introductory promo seems to apply whether you use pickup or delivery, which isn't always the case with Uber Eats promos. So, it is possible to truly save $20 on a $25 order if you select pickup. If you opt for delivery, you will likely have some service fees and a tip to factor into the equation.

One thing to remember: After the introductory promotion ends, you may want to compare the total cost of ordering through the Amazon-linked version of Uber Eats with any promotions, credit card benefits or discounts available through your regular Uber Eats account. The best deal may depend on which version of Uber Eats you're using.
