Six Hours Of Sleep On An Eight-Hour Flight: SAS Business Class Delivered

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

I mentioned earlier this week we decided to take a last-minute trip to Europe, flying in SAS A330 Business Class. This flight could not have been smoother and I’m so thankful my kids are now such excellent travelers.

We connected through Chicago and spent some time at the excellent Delta Sky Club (it’s huge!) prior to boarding. The Air France Lounge is nice, but the Delta Lounge is even nicer. Full review coming.

Lingering in the lounge, we were among the last to board, finding our seats in the center sections of row one and two.

It was evening and we were tired, but would we be able to sleep?

Dinner service commenced after takeoff and the food on SAS is excellent.

Heidi ordered the fish and I ordered the short ribs, with the intention of sharing, which is something we like doing when we fly together. That said, the salmon was fine by the beef was really delicious, as were the appetizers.

Claire Marie and Augustine did the same thing, with one having beef and the other fish and then sharing. I loved how the kids were so quiet and well-behaved, enjoyed the in-flight-entertainment and also each other’s company.

After dinner the kids went to sleep immediately and Heidi and I soon did as well. A flight attendant asked if we wanted to be awakened for breakfast and we all said no, though I asked if I could have a cappuccino at the top of descent.

We all slept very well…six hours…which is pretty amazing for a flight that is only eight hours long.

As we began our descent, a FA remembered without even my prompting to bring over the cappuccino, which was enjoyable. Heidi slept a bit longer, awaking just 20 minutes before landing. She wanted coffee and was obliged…a great crew.

I’m happy to take family trips to together, though I hope the kids are soon old enough that they can fly economy class while Heidi and I fly business class. Why? Because they can sleep in any seat and the IFE is the same in business versus premium economy or economy. That’s a whole different issue that we can discuss at some future point.

Oh, the purser had an interesting “political” pin, which I’ll discuss next week. But a great flight. Hopefully no jet lag…
