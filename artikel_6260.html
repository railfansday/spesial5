Emirates Finally Revamping Dubai Lounges: Temporary New First Class Lounge Now Open

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Emirates is known for being a world-class airline, though at this point, the carrier’s ground experience at Dubai International Airport (DXB) leaves a lot to be desired, especially for premium passengers.

While I don’t want to suggest that the carrier’s first class lounges and business class lounges are terrible, they’re far from cutting edge, and the design definitely feels extremely outdated. Earlier this year, I wrote a post asking if Emirates was going to revamp its lounges at the airport, or just wait until the theoretical move to Dubai World Central (DWC) around 2032.

Well, it seems that my concerns have now been addressed, as Emirates has announced plans to completely transform its lounge experience in Dubai. Over the summer we saw the airline start to roll out a new global lounge concept for outstation lounges, and that idea is now coming to Dubai as well.

As is the case for any airline, rolling out a new lounge concept at a hub airport isn’t easy, given that it requires spaces to be closed. So that’s why it’s worth noting that Emirates is taking an innovative approach to refreshing its lounges in Dubai:

Emirates is investing a total of 400 million AED ($109 million) in these lounge spaces. Interestingly, Emirates claims that “all flagship lounges” will be getting a redesign, though there’s zero mention of the lounges in Concourse A, which are the biggest lounges in the world (they span the entire concourse), and offer direct boarding to planes. So it’s not clear when those lounges will undergo updates.

Here’s how Emirates President Tim Clark describes this development:

“Our customers expect the very best from Emirates, whether while flying or spending time with us on the ground. We believe the airport experience should be every bit as exceptional as the journey itself, and that’s why we are transforming our lounges globally. This new First Class Lounge in Dubai is the first taste of what’s to come. And if these are the lengths we will go to for a temporary lounge… just imagine what you can expect from new Emirates Lounges going forward. Over the next two years, we’ll be unveiling a completely different lounge experience, with new concepts in dining, wellness, entertainment and personalised hospitality. We’re going well beyond a traditional refurbishment and creating extraordinary spaces.”

We’ve already seen how Emirates is overhauling its global lounge network, and I’d say the changes are pretty awesome. So what can we expect in Dubai? As you can see, Clark references how the temporary new first class lounge is just a small glimpse into what we can expect in the future. Below is a video showing the new concept, and I must say, it looks impressive!

Here’s how Emirates describes the lounge concept, broadly:

Featuring immersive culinary experiences to dedicated wellness facilities and innovative entertainment concepts, Emirates is redefining what customers can expect from their time at the airport. Building on Emirates’ renowned hospitality, customers can expect distinct spaces for dining, relaxation, work and socialising – complemented by intuitive technology, refined interiors and elevated personal service. Each lounge will bear the hallmarks of the iconic Emirates brand, with the interiors conveying luxury, comfort, exceptional hospitality and global appeal.

As much as people like to suggest that Gulf carriers just throw money down the drain when it comes to passenger experience, Emirates is actually a pretty disciplined and cost focused business, which is also clear based on the fact that the airline still has angled business class seats with seven seats per row. 😉

But when the airline does invest and upgrade its products, it has a pretty good sense of what customers like. Barista coffee, Dom Perignon, and a gym? Count me in (though maybe the amenities shouldn’t be enjoyed in that order). 😉 It does sound like there still won’t be dedicated bedrooms, which would be disappointing, but also wouldn’t surprise me, given the volume of rooms they’d need to serve guests.

Emirates is hugely investing in its lounge network in Dubai, something that’s long overdue, if you ask me. Over the next two years, at least four lounges will be getting a full makeover, to match the carrier’s new lounge aesthetic.

We’ve just seen the opening of a new temporarily first class lounge in Concourse B, which is described as an example of what’s to come. Next year that lounge is expected to become a business class lounge, as a new flagship first class lounge opens, along with additional first and business class lounges.

The new design aesthetic looks great, and it sounds like some thought is being put into amenities as well. I look forward to seeing this transformation, though I can’t help but wonder if this reflects a delay with Emirates moving to its new hub airport.
