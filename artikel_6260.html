Booked! SAS Business Class To Germany, Because My Wife Chose Horses Over The Caribbean

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

One beauty of sitting on all these SAS EuroBonus miles is that spur-of-the-moment trips are possible…like a trip to Germany for a few days.

My wife works very hard and rather unexpectedly was given five days, though without a lot of notice. Life is busy for all of us and of course the kids are in school, but as they quickly grow up Heidi and I have elected to take advantage of opportunities like this…for we will regret not doing so later.

Heidi initially suggested a “trip to the Caribbean…somewhere warm” and I thought it would be a good opportunity  to visit Saint Kitts and Nevis and check out the Park Hyatt there. I also suggested we could fly to Iceland with no set plan, get in the car, and drive. Wouldn’t that be fun? I’ve never done a trip around Iceland’s Ring Road.

Ultimately, though, Heidi spoke to her best friend in Germany and decided it would be a prefect opportunity to see her parents (remember, she did not join us on the summer trip) and ride horses with her friend in the Black Forest. I loved the idea and got to work on the flights.

There wasn’t business class from Los Angeles, but there was from Chicago exactly on our dates…five days, with four nights on the ground. Hardly long enough, but better than nothing? We’ll fly into Zurich, but I bought a separate ticket (on Norwegian!) to return from Basel to Copenhagen to catch the SAS connection to ORD…with a very tight connection.

So as I conclude my India Trip Report and move on to our summer trip to Europe, I’ll be sharing updates from this trip. We’ll be traveling in business class on the SAS A330, a product I’ve reviewed many times, though I may do so again if the situation warrants.

There’s never an ideal time to get away, but I really hope this will be a refreshing time for Heidi and indeed for all of us. And yes, even though it’s hardly something new or different, I get really excited ahead of an intercontinental trip, especially in a premium cabin.
