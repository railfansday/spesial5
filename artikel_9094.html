pearce+ co-builds guerrilla shading pavilion from salvaged timber and canvas sails in camden

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

architecture practice pearce+ completes the guerrilla shading pavilion for the camden inspire festival, an open, mobile cooling structure.

EE17 edited by christina vergopoulou November 9, 2026 3 min

Commissioned by Camden Town Unlimited and Euston Town BIDs for the Camden Inspire festival, architecture practice PEARCE+ collaborated with advocacy group Shade The UK and local volunteers to co-build the Guerrilla Shading pavilion. Sited at Camden Collective in London, the structure responds to escalating urban temperatures and the urban heat island effect, functioning as reusable, mobile shade infrastructure designed for continuous reassembly rather than single-event use.

The primary framework comprises five prefabricated panels sized for transport in a standard transit van. Engineered with a tapering profile to counter wind loads transferred by cantilevered tensile canopies, the structure utilizes structural timber salvaged from previous PEARCE+ installations. The lower register of the frame is intentionally left open, enabling visitors to manually weave an enclosure using reclaimed balloon fabric supplied by Cameron Hot Air Balloons and Inflate Studio. Tensioned naturally dyed canvas sails, developed in collaboration with artist Auda Sakho, project outward from the core frame to expand the shaded footprint over the surrounding garden. Complementing the physical pavilion, Camden Inspire and PEARCE+ Studio published Guerrilla Shading Our City, an open-source guide detailing low-cost shading tactics, ranging from DIY external shutters and tensile sails to urban tree planting and municipal planning permissions.
