Raimondo makes speedy recovery ahead of Frosinone vs. Parma

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Antonio Raimondo has returned from injury well ahead of schedule and is in Frosinone’s squad for Saturday’s Serie A clash with Parma.

According to Corriere dello Sport, the striker is one of 26 players called up by Massimiliano Alvini for the game at the Stadio Benito Stirpe, which kicks off at 15:00 CET [14:00 GMT]. Midfielder Florian Grillitsch has also recovered and is in the squad.

Raimondo injured his left ankle in Frosinone’s win over Torino on 1 November, and tests revealed a contusion-sprain injury with damage to several ligaments in the ankle. At the time, the estimated recovery time was around a month and a half, which would have kept him out until mid-December.

His return comes at a good time for Alvini’s side. The Gialloazzurri have lost both games they’ve played without him, including Sunday’s 5-1 defeat at Milan.

The 22-year-old has been one of the stories of the season so far. He has eight goals in 11 Serie A appearances and another in the Coppa Italia, and his form has helped Frosinone climb to seventh.
