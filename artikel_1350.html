Merseyside Derby & Two More January Fixtures Moved For Live Broadcast

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Three of Everton's Premier League fixtures in January – including the Merseyside derby at Anfield – have been moved for live television coverage.

The Toffees' trip to Coventry City remains on Saturday 16 January, but with an earlier kick-off time of 12.30pm (GMT), and will be shown live on TNT Sports.

The following weekend, David Moyes' side will now host Brentford at Hill Dickinson Stadium on Sunday 24 January, in a 2pm (GMT) kick-off that will be broadcast live on Sky Sports.

Finally, the Merseyside derby against Liverpool at Anfield on Saturday 30 January will now take place at the earlier time of 12.30pm (GMT) and will be live on TNT Sports.

Keep an eye on evertonfc.com and the Club's social media channels for ticketing updates.
