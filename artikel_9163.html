Annie Leibovitz Photographs Florence Pugh for Jaguar’s New Type 01

Annie Leibovitz has teamed up with Hollywood star Florence Pugh and British carmaker Jaguar.

Jaguar has undergone a rebrand in recent years, culminating in the launch of the Type 01, a bold electric GT that doesn’t look like classic Jags. The British car firm will start taking orders for the Type 01 in 2027.

Pugh is an English actress, known for films like Oppenheimer, Black Widow, and Little Women. She has signed on to become Jaguar’s first-ever brand ambassador.

Jaguar says that it brought together the three “distinctive creative forces” of Leibovitz, Pugh, and the Type 01 to reflect the “originality, bold and creative spirit driving Jaguar’s reimagined identity.”

“Leibovitz’s compelling portrait series explores individuality, confidence and creative ambition — values that underpin both Type 01 and Jaguar’s transformation,” says Jaguar.

The photos were shot at STORIED in New York, a building that once housed Leibovitz’s studio.

“What interested me most about this portrait series was photographing two extraordinary and contrasting subjects,” Leibovitz says, per Haute Living.

“Florence brings warmth, presence and humanity, while Type 01 is sculpted, precise and striking in its form. Rather than treating the car as a backdrop, I wanted to photograph them both with the same curiosity, exploring their relationship as two distinct characters within the frame.”

“I’ve always been drawn to people and brands that are unapologetically original, which is one of the reasons this partnership with Jaguar feels so natural,” adds Pugh.

“Collaborating with Annie Leibovitz on these images was a remarkable experience. She has an incredible ability to capture character and individuality, and bringing that together with the bold vision of Type 01 made this a truly special creative collaboration.”

The starting price for the Jaguar Type 01 is expected to be $130,000.

Last month, Leibovitz photographed Sam Altman for Vanity Fair, channeling the German Romantic painter Caspar David Friedrich, specifically his famous 1818 work Wanderer Above the Sea of Fog, which features a man staring out over a landscape, high above the clouds. OpenAI CEO Altman was photographed looking out over the Bay Area bluffs.
