Background in K-12 education technology or related learning and teaching fields, with a commitment to education-focused work.

Strong communication skills and the ability to work effectively in a fast-growing environment.

Ability to travel for occasional company retreats and business needs.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
