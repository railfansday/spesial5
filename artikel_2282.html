Data estimates how much Cardiff City star Jack Moylan is worth ahead of potential January interest

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Cardiff City have made a respectable start to life back in the Championship, although Brian Barry-Murphy’s side have struggled to find consistency in front of goal. Their problems in the final third have arguably left them lower down the table than their overall performances deserve.

However, those struggles have not affected summer recruit Jack Moylan. The Bluebirds decision to bring the attacking midfielder in from Lincoln City is already looking like an excellent piece of business.

The 25-year-old has quickly established himself as one of the Bluebirds’ most influential players, registering nine goals and three assists in his opening 14 Championship appearances. Moylan has also started every league fixture, highlighting just how important he has become to Barry-Murphy’s plans.

With Cardiff struggling towards the lower end of the table, his contributions have been particularly valuable. If he continues producing at this level, the Bluebirds may have to contend with growing interest in their summer signing when the January transfer window opens.

While the fee was reported as undisclosed upon Moylan’s announcement, reports have suggested that Cardiff City was an initial £3m which could rise to £5m.

The Bluebirds have tied Moylan down to a four-year deal running until the summer of 2028. With that in mind, if interest does arrive in January, they are in a strong position to ask for a hefty fee and profit.

However, the data suggests that Cardiff City may have overpaid to bring Moylan in this summer, which does not bode well for a potential sale. FootballTransfers’ estimated transfer value has the attacking-midfielder’s value at €2.2m, which is around £1.8m.

Off the current Cardiff City squad, Moylan has the second highest value with only Yousef Salech higher (€3.8m). Moylan’s value is also considerably lower than the only player who has more Championship goals then him Will Lankshear, who is valued at €20.7m.

A more direct comparison may be Blackburn Rovers striker Andri Gudjohnsen, who also has nine goals this campaign. His value is only €2.7m and much similar to Moylan’s, while being just a year younger.

Moylan’s statistics underline just how influential he has become for Cardiff City. His attacking numbers are particularly impressive, with 51 shots, 19 on target and 18 chances created.

He has also registered five big chances created, while his nine goals have come from an expected goals figure of 5.48, highlighting his clinical finishing and ability to outperform the underlying numbers.

The Irishman has delivered in some of Cardiff’s biggest performances, scoring twice in their 5-1 victory over Watford while impressing in a central striking role. He also scored a first-half hat-trick in the 3-3 draw with West Ham during the week.

Having progressed from Irish football to becoming a key figure in Lincoln’s League One title-winning campaign, Moylan has adapted impressively to the Championship.
