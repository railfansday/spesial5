Falkirk confirm tragic death of lifelong supporter shortly after victory over Celtic

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Falkirk have confirmed the tragic news that a lifelong supporter passed away shortly after the win over Celtic on Sunday.

The club issued a statement on Sunday night offering their "heartfelt condolences" to the family and friends of the man.

The supporter passed away shortly after leaving the stadium following the 2-0 win in the Scottish Premiership fixture.

Falkirk Football Club is aware that a lifelong supporter tragically passed away shortly after leaving today’s game. Everyone at Falkirk FC sends their heartfelt condolences to his family and friends at this most difficult time. https://t.co/LFB6IX2XCS

— Falkirk FC (@FalkirkFC) November 1, 2026

A Falkirk post on social media read: "Falkirk Football Club is aware that a lifelong supporter tragically passed away shortly after leaving today’s game.

"Everyone at Falkirk FC sends their heartfelt condolences to his family and friends at this most difficult time."

Falkirk claimed a 2-0 win in Sunday's Scottish Premiership tie, with Ethan Ross and Scott Tanser finding the net.

Scott Bain put in an outstanding display in goal to keep a clean sheet for Falkirk against his former club.
