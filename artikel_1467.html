Alguacil reveals conviction regarding how Olympiacos could have beaten Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Olympiacos head coach Imanol Alguacil believes that his side would have beaten AC Milan had they hung on a bit longer before conceding.

Everything was going according to plan for the Greek side when Lorenzo Pirola’s header hit the bar then Mike Maignan and nestled in to put them a goal up, before Jota Silva fired in a second following Koni De Winter’s stray pass out from the back.

With Milan’s Europa League hopes in need of revival, Omari Hutchinson stepped up with a masterclass. He gave Ruben Amorim’s side hope with a fine individual effort, then he set up Alphadjo Cisse for the equaliser and Christian Pulisic for the go-ahead goal, before Diego Moreira sealed it.

It was therefore a bittersweet night for Alguacil, one in which his side showed they can dominate one of the big names in the competition, but ultimately emerging empty-handed. He analysed the game in a post-match press conference which MilanNews attended.

The team showed what they could do for 60 minutes. How can you explain what happened?

“At a key moment in the match, we went from a possible 3-0 lead to 2-1, and our confidence crumbled. Milan reacted well: they’re stronger, they have experience, they’re a historic team, and if you’re not focused, you risk losing, as happened.

“Milan gave it their all, but until the 60th minute, we played a great match, like a Champions League team.”

Are you satisfied with the defence’s performance? Is this formation likely to continue going forward?

“I don’t know. A coach has to find solutions, especially because it depends on how we recover our players. We have some out, others played today, and we’ll see the consequences.

“We’ll see who I have available, also thinking about the next match, but Olympiacos, despite the change in formation, played very well.

“We played well, took the lead, deserved the 3-0, and I’m convinced that if we had held on to a 2-0 lead for a few more minutes, we would have won. The 2-4 scoreline seems unfair to me.”

Five players out, but the team still proved to be strong. Is there a chance you can directly advance to the last 16 and go deeper?

“I don’t know where the limit is, I don’t have a definitive answer. I’ll try to get the most out of the team. The lads are training hard, and it’s unfair to have conceded these four goals. In the end, we lacked energy. Milan are a Champions League team, very technical, but I’ll hold on to these 65 minutes.

“We played better than Milan, and I’ll try to make it impossible to play this hour of play again in the next games. By doing so, it will be easier for us to win.”
