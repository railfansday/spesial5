A tale of glory or misery in Greece: All the key stats ahead of Olympiacos vs. Milan

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

AC Milan and Olympiacos will face each other for a fifth time this evening, with the Greek side hosting the Rossoneri in Athens.

The first two meetings came in 1959-60 and the other two were in 2018-19. In the European Cup clashes in 1959, the Rossoneri prevailed 5-3 on aggregate after drawing 2-2 in Greece and then beating them 3-1 back at San Siro. Giancarlo Danova scored a hat-trick in the second leg.

The most recent encounters were eight years ago, as mentioned. Patrick Cutrone (2) and Gonzalo Higuain fired Gennaro Gattuso’s side to a 3-1 home group stage win. Yet, in Piraeus the Diavolo lost 3-1 on the final matchday to drop to third and crash out.

➤ Olympiakos have won one of their four major European matches against Milan (D1 L2), winning the most recent meeting 3-1 in December 2018 in the UEFA Europa League. The Rossoneri have never won an away major European match in Greece in six attempts (D4 L2), failing to score in three of those matches.

➤ Olympiakos have lost just one of their last 13 UEFA Europa League matches (W8 D4), keeping nine clean sheets. In the group stages, they’re unbeaten in 11 games (W7 D4), conceding just twice.

➤ Milan have lost three of their four UEFA Europa League games this season (W1), including their last two without scoring. They’ve never lost three major European games in a row without finding the net. Olympiakos’ Jota Silva has had the most shots in the UEFA Europa League this season (17) – however, he is yet to score with any of those attempts, only landing two of them on target.

➤ Milan have never won a competitive game against a Greek side in Greece, losing 2 and drawing 4. However, they have lifted the Champions League trophy twice in Athens (4-0 vs. Barcelona in 1994 and 2-1 vs. Liverpool in 2007).

➤ Hosts Olympiakos have won each of their two previous Europa League contests at Stadio Georgios Karaiskákis this season. Olympiakos will be out to prolong a six-match home unbeaten streak in all competitions.

➤ Milan have one win and one loss on their travels in the competition this season in two contests. In their last six matches in all competitions, Olympiakos have won four and drawn two. They scored 17 and conceded five over that stretch, scoring first in four of the six contests. They had five first-half goals, while their opponents netted two times in the first 45 minutes.

➤ Milan have won four and lost two in their last six. They scored 14 and conceded nine in that stretch, netting first in four of the six. Most of their goals (10) came in the first half, while their opponents netted three times before the break.

➤ Armando González has caught the eye for Olympiakos in Europa League play. He is their top scorer with two goals, one of which has been the tone-setting first of the match.

➤ Alphadjo Cisse has been the leading attacking threat for Milan, scoring on three occasions in the current Europa League campaign, including one match-opening goal. He is tied for third in the competition’s scoring charts in 2026-27.

➤ The visitors have found it challenging to score in Europa League play recently, as they are scoreless in their last 198 minutes in the competition. They haven’t been on the scoresheet since a Gonçalo Ramos goal in their league phase win against Salzburg.

➤ Milan may face difficulty scoring yet again, as Olympiakos are in the midst of a shutout streak and haven’t allowed a goal in their last three Europa League matches, dating to a Kajetan Szmyt finish in their league phase win against Jagiellonia Białystok.
