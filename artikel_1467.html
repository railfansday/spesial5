CM: Extraordinary Cisse story continues with 10th goal – Milan prepare anti-Arsenal renewal

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Everything that Alphadjo Cisse touches seems to be turning to goals at the moment, and the same was true last night in Greece.

As Calciomercato.com wrote after the game, Cisse’s star continues to shine brightly for Milan. It’s difficult, if not impossible, to contain the superlatives when describing the exploits of a 20-year-old who, in his first season with a top club, has already reached the 10-goal mark.

Cisse has scored six in the league and four in the Europa League as of November 27th. He himself claims that he is at 70% fitness after being sidelined for months post-surgery at the beginning of the year, which makes everyone wonder what his true potential is.

This time one year ago he was driving Catanzaro’s Stadio Ceravolo wild with his goals in Serie B. Yesterday, he silenced the Karaiskakis Stadium – the home of Olympiacos – where the fans are not used to seeing defeats.

As mentioned, Cisse actually spent months away from the pitch after sustaining a serious muscle injury while at Catanzaro. Then came the hard work during the summer, the training camp under the watch of Ruben Amorim, who fell in love with him quickly.

Records are tumbling. Against RB Salzburg, he became the youngest Italian ever to score a hat-trick in Europe for Milan. His headed goal yesterday against Olympiacos made him the youngest ever to score four goals in Europe for the Rossoneri.

In the first half, Amorim was talking to his coaching staff every two minutes about Cisse’s position. He wasn’t convinced as he wanted the former Verona talent to play more centrally. Goncalo Ramos’ injury then convinced the Portuguese coach to move him down the middle.

What was once an idea is now becoming a reality: Cisse is not only Milan’s top scorer, but also the striker Amorim needs. He is a fox in the penalty area, with a very high conversion rate and the composure of someone beyond his years.

Cissé signed a four-and-a-half-year contract with Milan in February 2026, which runs until June 2030 therefore. He currently earns around €800,000 per season, a figure that no longer reflects his performances on the pitch.

Milan are pushing for a one-year extension, until 2031, and a salary of at least €1.5m. Bonuses remain to be finalised, but both sides are working hard to get the deal in writing. This would be a blow to Brighton and Arsenal, who have already contacted Cisse’s agent.
