PL: Man City falls against Brighton, Sunderland collapses again, Bournemouth continues

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

After Manchester United’s debacle against Chelsea earlier in the day (2-0), the 9th day of the Premier League continued this Saturday with a clash between Manchester City and Brighton, a surprise at the start of the season. And the Seagulls held their place by opening the scoring thanks to Ayari (0-1, 11th). Brighton put the Skyblues in difficulty for a long time, who never managed to come back and thus conceded a second defeat. Manchester City could lose the top spot in the event of a good result from Arsenal at Liverpool. For its part, Brighton consolidates its third place.

At the bottom of the table, nothing is going well at Sunderland. Régis Le Bris’ men completely collapsed on the Coventry City pitch. Despite the reduction in Le Fée’s score, the Black Cats were outclassed and remained bottom before hosting Chelsea. Just ahead of them, Nottingham Forest are also in danger after their defeat at Brentford (0-2). The Bees climb to 6th place and push Forest into 19th. In the other matches, Evanilson, then Adli, after a superb number from Kroupi, allowed Bournemouth to win against Leeds United (8th), thus giving his team 7th place. Finally, Hull City (9th) and Ipswich Town (12th) failed to decide between themselves after a spectacular draw, marked by great achievements from McBurnie and Enciso.

Manchester City 0-1 Brighton : Yasin Ayari (11th)

Coventry 3-1 Sunderland: Tatsuhiro Sakamoto (17th), Taiwo Awoniyi (37th) and Jack Rudoni (52nd) / Enzo Le Fée (67th, sp)

Brentford 2-0 Nottingham Forest: Nikola Milenkovic (30th, CSC) and Igor Thiago (76th)

Bournemouth 2-0 Leeds: Evanilson (31st) and Amine Adli (90th+4)

Hull City 2-2 Ipswich Town: Tim Iroegbunam (58th) and Oli McBurnie (68th) / Florentino (22nd) and Julio Enciso (74th)
