Hitman, 007 First Light PS5 Crossover Makes Lenny Kravitz Your New Ally

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

It's a bit surprising this crossover hasn't already happened, to be honest, given IO Interactive is the studio behind both of the games, but it's now been confirmed a Hitman and 007 First Light collaboration is happening.

The content is live from today until 24th December 2026, and it takes the form of a new Elusive Target in Hitman. Lenny Kravitz, who is playing Bawma from 007 First Light, is the next celebrity to feature in Hitman, but this time, he's on Agent 47's side.

Bawma has been betrayed by three of his associates, and he's called in the bald assassin to take them down.

Up for elimination are Horace Stockton, Oswald Spoon, and Zui.

The new set of Elusive Targets and the mission hosting them brings with it the likeness and voice of Lenny Kravitz, who you'll hear from throughout the level via Agent 47's earpiece.

"Bawma is such a fun and interesting character to portray, so I was excited to step back into a new world with him for 007 First Light," said Kravitz. "Seeing Bawma team up with Agent 47 is something I think fans of both games are going to love."

The mission will be available until 24th December, but if you own a copy of the game, then you'll retain permanent access to the Arcade version of it. It can also be accessed free of charge for those who don't have Hitman 3, as it's being added to the demo of the game.

Alongside the mission, a roadmap detailing the Season of Bawma teases the following content additions: a new Escalation, new Arcade and Special Assignment contracts, a new challenge, a batch of Featured Contracts, a new Twitch Drop and an IOI Account reward.
