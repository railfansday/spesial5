Main Sequence release countdown: Exact date and time

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

First-person space-sim factory Main Sequence is coming soon to gamers worldwide, and you won’t need to wait long to begin your adventure. Developed by the Tumbling Dumpling Studio, the game allows you to design different spaceships, build space stations, and explore the dark space.

Main Sequence goes live on Thursday, Nov. 12. The release date was announced b the publishers earlier during Steam Fest, and I am not expecting any change this late. The following countdown will expire once the game goes live.

The countdown is based on the following times.

In Main Sequence, you have been tasked with expanding humanity’s footprint among the stars. To do so, you’ll need to collect resources, expand your base of operations, and automate production.

In the beginning, you’ll have a single mining ship, which is good enough to get the starting resources. As you do research and unlock the upgrades, you can quickly expand your base of operations to operate a fully automated factory.

When it comes to designing your ship, factories, and base, you have utmost freedom. You can build them using the modular system in place in the game. The same logic also applies across the automated supply routes that will connect the different stations.

You can explore the procedurally generated star system and discover new resource lodes. As you expand, you’ll also have to overcome the logistical challenges coming your way. You can take on these challenges solo, or you can enjoy co-op with a buddy where each player controls a ship of their own.

Main Sequence also has a free demo on offer on Steam that you can download and try out to get a better idea of the gameplay and features.
