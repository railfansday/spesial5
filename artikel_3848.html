Here are the patch notes for GTA 6's first update

The first set of GTA 6 patch notes are here, with a post-launch update arriving on November 23. But let me temper your expectations as this patch isn't anything particularly interesting unless you've been affected by any of the bugs and crashes that the patch aims to iron out. There are hardly going to be any substantial changes to the game less than a week after launch, but I've laid out the Xbox and PS5 patch notes for GTA 6 below.

You can read the full notes for GTA 6's first post-launch update on Rockstar's support website, but they're so short that I've laid them all out below:

As you can see, there are only seven fixes, all of which are for high-priority gameplay issues, such as crashes, problems with the autosave system, and not receiving mission-critical phone calls for progression. While the autosave system should hopefully be working as intended, it still helps to know how to save in GTA 6 so that you can manually bank your progress. And for the latter point about phone calls, many of them just require you to pass the time by doing other activities to trigger, like when you meet Halifax as part of the GTA 6 Dubious Provenance mission in Chapter 3.

If you've been affected by any of these, make sure you get the patch installed and you should be able to carry on playing and making progress through the main story – though if you need some help, our GTA 6 guides are all here.
