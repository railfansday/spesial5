Beau Lamarre-Condon trial: prosecution begins cross-examination of double murder accused – as it happened

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Beau Lamarre-Condon tells double murder trial shooting of Jesse Baird wasn’t intentional but ‘it was my fault’

The court has adjourned for the day. Here are some of the key moments from Lamarre-Condon’s evidence today.

The former officer told the court about the day of the alleged murders of Jesse Baird and his partner, Luke Davies, on 19 February 2024 at Baird’s Paddington home. He claimed he disguised himself as an UberEats rider to try to avoid interacting with Jesse Baird.

The jury heard he entered the house to search for his “expensive” belongings including a Cartier ring.

Lamarre-Condon said he saw Baird with tape in one hand and a knife in the other, sealing boxes. This was when he alleges Baird lunged at the accused with the knife, the court heard, which led to a struggle between the two.

The former officer said he aimed to shoot Baird in the right shoulder to get him to drop the knife, saying he stands by the decision to take that shot but “never had the thought or intention” of killing Baird.

He told the court Luke Davies picked up his gun and pointed it at him before a second struggle ensued. Lamarre-Condon claims Davies shot himself.

Lamarre-Condon said he spoke with some friends about shooting Baird and disposing of the bodies.

The officer said he was “fearful” about going to police because he thought he would be killed in jail.

Lamarre-Condon said he is guilty of Baird’s manslaughter and turned himself into police on 27 February 2024 because he “needed to do the right thing”.

That’s where we’ll leave our coverage for today. The trial will continue at 10am on Monday.

Lamarre-Condon tells court why he kept visor on

The accused says he took off the jacket because it was hi-vis and made of waterproof material that made a sound when he moved.

But asked why he kept the visor on he tells the jury:
