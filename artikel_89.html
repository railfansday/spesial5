Seeking to Broaden Ukraine Talks, U.S. Urges Russia to Include Europe

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

As a round of negotiations ended in Abu Dhabi, President Trump’s envoys said they would go to Moscow as the Kremlin demands discussions on European security structures.

Reporting from Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates

The first round of Ukraine peace talks in nine months ended in Abu Dhabi on Wednesday without a Russian agreement to include Europe in negotiations that have extended, at Moscow’s insistence, to questions of huge consequence for European nations’ security.

The two days of talks between Ukrainian and Russian delegations in the United Arab Emirates were led by President Trump’s envoys, Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff. On Thursday, the two Americans are scheduled to meet with President Vladimir V. Putin of Russia in Moscow and then with President Volodymyr Zelensky of Ukraine in Dublin.

The Americans said on Wednesday that they would work to nail down the details of what Mr. Kushner billed as the largest humanitarian swap of the war.

The envoys are also trying to secure agreement from Moscow to widen the talks to include Europeans, after negotiators in Abu Dhabi discussed Russia’s demands to overhaul European security structures.

Reiterating a longstanding European position, Ursula von der Leyen, president of the European Commission, the European Union’s executive arm, said this week that a cease-fire must come before such discussions. That sequencing would avoid negotiating at gunpoint and test Russian compliance with any agreements.

But Mr. Kushner and Mr. Witkoff, trying to find a path forward after nearly two years of fruitless peacemaking efforts, are pushing to grant Moscow its wish of negotiating the postwar security posture in Europe alongside discussions about a cease-fire and territory in Ukraine.
