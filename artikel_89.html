Police officer dies and another injured after backpack explosion in northern Spain

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

A police officer has died and another has been injured in the northern Spanish city of Oviedo after an explosive device went off at the scene of a shooting.

The blast happened when officers from Spain's canine unit and explosives disposal unit arrived and examined a suspicious backpack following reports of a dispute between neighbours on Tuesday morning.

A woman who had been shot and the injured officer have been taken to hospital for treatment.

The suspect fled the scene and is still at large, police said.
