Napoli & Juventus weigh up a January swoop for Joshua Zirkzee

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Both Napoli and Juventus are closely monitoring Manchester United’s Joshua Zirkzee ahead of the January transfer window, according to Italian transfer guru Fabrizio Romano.

While Zirkzee might have been eventually stayed put at Old Trafford over the summer following heavy speculation over a move away, the Dutch international failed to find any real regular opportunities under now-former United boss Michael Carrick.

Despite bagging a maiden Premier League strike of the season during his seven-minute cameo against Aston Villa prior to November’s international break, Zirkzee has yet to make a first start across any competition.

While Slovenian marksman Benjamin Sesko might have been ruled out for the remainder of 2026 due to an extended shin injury, the 25-year-old has failed to establish himself as a worthy deputy.

Nevertheless, despite registering just six goals in his 62 Premier League appearances since arriving at Old Trafford back in the summer of 2024, Romano is reporting that two Italian icons are planning on offering Zirkzee as Manchester United exit in January.

With the one-time Bayern Munich youngster reportedly making it clear that he is seeking regular first-team opportunities, both Napoli and Juventus are keeping a close eye on proceedings.

The Italian transfer expert suggested this week that alongside Napoli holding strong links with Zirkzee’s representatives, Juventus could be keen on a potential deal should it suitable for all parties.
