Chelsea and Tottenham fans react as Mauricio Pochettino is favourite to be next Manchester United manager

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Your weekly briefing on Chelsea, featuring team news and expert analysis from Dom Smith

The idea of Mauricio Pochettino becoming the next Manchester United manager has piqued the interest of Chelsea and Tottenham supporters.

Reports emerged on Monday evening that Man United have made the decision to sack Michael Carrick, with the club in the Premier League relegation zone heading into the international break.

As such work will now begin at the club’s Carrington training ground to appoint a new manager before the Liverpool clash on November 22 and Pochettino has emerged as the new front-runner with the bookmakers.

Furthermore, ESPN have reported that Argentine is the favoured candidate among Man Utd chiefs, while BBC Sport claim that Pochettino is open to the Old Trafford job.

If Pochettino does takeover at Man United, it will be a case of third time lucky having twice previously interviewed for the manager’s job.

Of course, the Argentina tactician is no stranger to the Premier League after enjoying spells with Southampton, Tottenham and Chelsea before moving into international management.

Amid reports of talks with United set to commence, some Spurs and Chelsea fans, on social media, have reacted to the idea of seeing their former boss back in English football.

One user on X was pretty scathing of Pochettino’s Stamford Bridge spell, issuing a warning to Man United fans: “These guys genuinely don’t know what they’re in for bless them.

“The idea of Poch is nice, hell even I wanted him at the time, then he put up a relegation level coaching season, gave our players crippling injury problems, bottled the carabao to a youth team and nearly got us bottom half all while Palmer was dropping a POTY level campaign.”

Another X user is fearing a Pochettino and Cole Palmer reunion, writing: "If Pochettino takes over at United I can see Cole Palmer being sold for £90m to them next summer."
