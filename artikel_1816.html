Ismaila Sarr keeps options open as Crystal Palace contract talks stall

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

According to Football Insider, contract talks between Crystal Palace and Ismaila Sarr have stalled following the sacking of manager Pierre Sage, with the winger reportedly keeping his options open ahead of the January transfer window.

Discussions over a new deal began at the end of October but have made little progress. Palace’s focus on replacing Sage is said to have pushed negotiations down the club’s list of priorities, though that could change now that the club have confirmed the appointment of Thomas Frank.

Sarr is also believed to be reluctant to rush into a decision. The Senegal international and his representatives are assessing the situation carefully after a proposed departure from Selhurst Park failed to materialise during the summer transfer window. Both Liverpool and Galatasaray were linked with Sarr in the previous window, but Palace ultimately refused to sanction a move. The club’s stance has reportedly become even stronger as they prepare for a battle to avoid relegation.

Although injuries have restricted Sarr to just 15 minutes of Premier League action this season, Palace continue to regard the 28-year-old as a crucial player.
