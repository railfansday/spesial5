Lewes admit: We got what we deserved at Ramsgate

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Going into the contest three points ahead of their Kent hosts, Lewes were looking for a result to lift their league form after goalless outings against Enfield Town and Stanway Rovers.

While they did break their goal duck, they were 3-0 down by the time it came in Thanet and went on to lose by a significant margin against their rampant hosts.

“I think Ramsgate definitely deserved to win,” said Altendorff. “Maybe the scoreline flatters them slightly, but I would say that the end result is justified. We weren't our best.

“Ramsgate were playing a very direct brand of football. We found it difficult to cope with. We didn't win enough first and second contacts, especially in the first half. They got what they deserved, and so did we.”

On a punishing afternoon for the Rooks, heavy first-half pressure led to a fine brace from TJ Jadama, while it could have been worse with another strike rattling the crossbar.

Lewes struggled to escape their half, with Charlie Walker on the end of some contentious decisions as he tried to break away as the sole outlet for much of the match.

While Alfie Allen’s 68th-minute header offered the Rooks some hope, and a quadruple substitution injected some intent, a fourth Ramsgate goal in added time knocked the stuffing out of Lewes.

“The scoreline enabled us to try something different because at that stage of the game, it's already three nil to them,” said Altendorff. “We did actually improve. The boys off the bench did well when they came on.

“I thought Tyler Christian-Law was excellent and added a bit of bite to midfield. We didn't seem to lack anything by withdrawing a third midfielder. If anything, we looked even more threatening.

“Even towards the end of the game, Charlie had a good opportunity to put one in the back of the net from six yards out. Unfortunately, it just wasn't our day. Nothing seemed to fall for us.”

Lewes have now won just one of their last seven Isthmian Premier Division matches and face a tricky test at 7th-placed Leatherhead this Saturday.

Just as Ramsgate were last weekend, the Surrey side are three points behind the Rooks with a game in hand and a superior goal difference, meaning they can leapfrog Lewes with a win.

On Tuesday night, Leatherhead beat Ramsgate 1-0 at home to move into the play-off places, but with this being their first win in four games, the Ian Selley-led side are not in electric form either.
