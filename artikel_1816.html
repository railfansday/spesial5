No Liverpool contract talks for Mac Allister

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Liverpool midfielder Alexis Mac Allister is yet to enter talks over a new deal with the club.

That is according to Mac Allister's father, Carlos, who says his son has no concerns over his future.

Mac Allister joined Liverpool from Brighton in 2023, and has made 166 appearances for the Reds, scoring 24 goals and providing 21 assists.

The Argentina international played a key role in Liverpool's title success under Arne Slot in 2024-25, but will soon be entering the final 18 months of his contract.

And Carlos Mac Allister says talks are yet to begin over a new contract.

"There are still 20 months left on his [Alexis Mac Allister's] contract; [Liverpool's] sporting director has changed, and so has the coach. The situation is still under review, and there may be news before the end of the year," he told ESPN.

"We haven't started renewal talks yet; if everything goes well, he'll renew, and if not, that's fine – he'll go his own way like so many others. He's a young player who won the Copa America and the World Cup, was runner-up in the last [World Cup], and was the Argentine player who ran the most during the tournament.

"He has a ton of records and achievements that will ensure his career takes him to top-tier destinations. When you play for Liverpool and aim to maintain the same level of play, you know there are only six or seven teams in the world where you could play."

However, that does not mean Mac Allister is already focusing on his future potentially being elsewhere.

"Today, we're only talking about and focusing on Liverpool. It's a matter of respect that the club deserves and that Liverpool fans deserve. You can't be at one club talking about others," Carlos added.

"When you're at a club, you have to show the respect and give the importance that the club deserves. Today we're talking about Liverpool, which is one of the biggest clubs in the world, but no matter what club it is, you can't disrespect the one that signed you, took an interest in you, and made a very significant financial investment.

"We always talk about Liverpool, about the passion of representing the club, we talk about the dream of winning championships, and that's all."
