‘There are different ways of winning’, says Mead after Man City edge out Hacken

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Beth Mead said "there are different ways to win football matches" after Manchester City battled their way to a 2-0 victory over Hacken.

Man City made it back-to-back wins in the Women's Champions League on Wednesday, thanks to second-half goals from Lauren Hemp and Khadija Shaw.

Andree Jeglertz turned to star attackers Hemp and Shaw at half-time at Nordic Wellness Arena, with City having failed to make the breakthrough in the first period, despite creating chances worth a combined 1.08 expected goals.

Hemp's introduction paid dividends when she opened the scoring in the 55th minute by bundling home while challenging for the ball. She then teed up Shaw to put the result beyond doubt late on, before Mary Fowler missed a stoppage-time penalty.

City finished with 3.43 xG, and while they were not at their clinical best, Mead, who was taken off for Shaw, was more than happy with the performance.

"It was a tough game – we had to work hard for the three points," she told UEFA.

"I'm really proud. It's been a busy schedule. Every player has been bringing different energy to the pitch – a different kind of pitch to play on, obviously. Everybody in the team turned up today and we did what we needed to do.

"There are different ways of winning football matches. Coming away from home in the Champions League is difficult but we found a way to win today."

— Manchester City Women (@ManCityWomen) November 18, 2026

Jeglertz, meanwhile, said City got exactly the game they were expecting.

He told UEFA: "A good game. We knew it would be a tough game to score against a very organised defending team. They have a great goalkeeper and we struggled in first half. In the second half, they got a little bit tired, we got a little more space and we managed to get the goals.

"We try to get the best out of each player and put them in positions where they can really show what qualities they have.

"We have a great team together, we rotate players, and they all have a great impact on what we're doing. I think that will be very important in the future."

City have lost just one of their 11 group stage/league phase games in the Champions League (W6 D3), losing 0-3 to Barcelona in December 2024.
