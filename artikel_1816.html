What channel is Rangers v Aberdeen on? TV, live stream and highlights details plus team news

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Rangers are aiming to trim the gap with Premiership leaders Celtic to a solitary point by defeating Aberdeen on Tuesday night.

This is Rangers’ game in hand over the champions, but they face an Aberdeen side who won their first match under new boss Mario Frick and will been keen to build upon it.

Here are all the details ahead of the match:

The William Hill Premiership match between Rangers and Aberdeen takes place on Tuesday, November 24 at Ibrox, Glasgow. Kick-off is at 8pm.

Sky Sports have picked the match for live broadcast. It will be shown on the Sky Sports Football channel.

Sky Sports will show the game on their SkyGo platforms.

Sportscene will be screened on BBC One Scotland at 10.40pm on Tuesday.

Ross McCrorie and Daisuke Yokota are back in full training for Rangers while Cammy Devlin and Lawrence Shankland are running after hamstring injuries. Youssef Chermiti (knee) remains out for the long term.

Aberdeen are expected to be without Mats Knoester, Emmanuel Gyamfi (both thigh), Kristers Tobers (knee), Chris Cadden (hamstring), Lewis Mayo (foot) and Connor Ronan (knee).

Referee John Beaton will be in charge of proceedings at Ibrox and will be assisted by David McGeachie and David Dunne. Kevin Clancy is the VAR and will be assisted back at Clydesdale House by Greg Aitken.
