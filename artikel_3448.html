What it's about: "Resident Evil" follows Bryan (Austin Abrams), a medical courier who finds himself caught in a nightmare when a biological outbreak erupts around Raccoon City. With infected creatures closing in and chaos spreading, Bryan must fight to survive one terrifying night. Directed by Zach Cregger, this fresh standalone take on the video game franchise delivers plenty of horror and action.

Why you should watch it: Anyone who is a fan of the video games will want to watch regardless, but Cregger's "Resident Evil" is still worth watching for those who aren't familiar with the source material. Expect gruesome creature designs, genuinely unsettling scares and plenty of video game-inspired thrills. Austin Abrams is also fantastic as an unlikely hero, while Cregger's fresh approach makes this an entertaining standalone adventure for horror fans and newcomers alike.

What it's about: "Sense and Sensibility" centers around sisters Elinor (Daisy Edgar-Jones) and Marianne Dashwood (Esmé Creed-Miles), whose lives are turned upside down following their father's death and their family's loss of financial security. Forced to leave their family home for a modest cottage in Devonshire, the sisters navigate complicated romances, heartbreak and societal expectations, discovering the importance of family as they search for happiness.

Why you should watch it: If you enjoy Jane Austen adaptations, this fresh take on "Sense and Sensibility" is well worth watching. Daisy Edgar-Jones and Esmé Creed-Miles deliver standout performances as the Dashwood sisters, whose relationship gives the story plenty of heart. Gorgeous countryside cinematography, heartfelt romance and Georgia Oakley's more naturalistic direction also help this period drama stand out.
