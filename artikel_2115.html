Sheffield United must plot Coventry City transfer action after major Chris Wilder reveal

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Sheffield United’s positive run of form came to an end as they suffered a 2-1 defeat at struggling Charlton Athletic last time out.

After an inconsistent start to the campaign, Chris Wilder’s side looked as though they had turned a corner after going four games unbeaten, which included recording three wins.

So, they went to The Valley full of confidence, but despite Jordan Ayew cancelling out Charlton’s opener, the hosts would pick up all three points after Matty Godden’s late strike.

That meant the Blades failed to move into the play-off places, although it can’t detract from the general improvements they have made in the last month or so.

Unfortunately for Wilder and Sheffield United, the defeat in the capital came at a further cost, as Femi Seriki was forced off just minutes after replacing Hamza Chouhury at half-time.

The 24-year-old had already missed a series of games with a knee injury, and Wilder has confirmed that Seriki has now suffered a patella tendon rupture, which means he won’t return until the 2027/28 season.

In many ways, that overshadowed the result for the Blades, as Seriki is a very talented player, and right-back has been a problem position for the Yorkshire outfit.

Choudhury has filled in that position since he joined from Leicester in the summer, and he can do a job, but it’s not his natural position. Plus, at times, he looks vulnerable when he comes up against quick wingers who want to take him on.

Matt Doherty is another option, but the 34-year-old has fitness issues of his own, and he hasn’t convinced in the limited time he has had on the pitch since becoming a Blade in the summer.

Therefore, Seriki’s injury is surely going to force Sheffield United into the market for a new right-back, as Wilder will want a new recruit for that role.

And, one player who could be a shrewd addition is Coventry’s Kaine Kesler-Hayden.

The form of Milan van Ewijk means Kesler-Hayden is second choice for the Sky Blues, and the reality is that, providing the Dutchman stays fit, it’s hard to see that changing any time soon.

So, a loan deal seems possible, which would obviously suit Sheffield United given their financial situation, and, from a football perspective, Kesler-Hayden has qualities that Wilder will love.

The former Aston Villa man plays with intensity, he loves to get forward, and he is also capable of defending in a one-v-one.

In the 2024/25 season, Kesler-Hayden was outstanding on loan at Preston, so there will also be few doubts about his ability to cope in the second tier, and it was that form that prompted Coventry to splash out around £5m on the player, at a time when van Ewijk was expected to leave.

So, this is a player with serious pedigree by Championship standards, but there’s no denying that Kesler-Hayden’s career has stalled.

Even though he contributed to Coventry’s title win last season, featuring in 22 games, the right-back only played 585 minutes.

An injury meant he missed parts of this season, but his only outing was as an 87th-minute substitute at Spurs.

Clearly, he won’t be enjoying that, and whilst Lampard would want to keep Kesler-Hayden around, he will recognise that the past 18 months haven’t played out as he envisaged, so it wouldn’t be a shock if he agreed to let the 24-year-old leave for the sake of his career.

As outlined above, the Seriki news is likely to change the Blades’ transfer plans, but they aren’t going to be working with the biggest budget.

Obviously, that could make deals harder to do, and they may have to wait to see what sort of players become available.

But, the recruitment team will be working on targets, and, in an ideal world, they will be able to secure a few key signings as early as possible.

There’s still a lot of football to be played between now and January, so the outlook at Bramall Lane could change, but it seems likely that they will be among a host of clubs aiming to finish in the top eight.
