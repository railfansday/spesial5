MN: Pavlovic the only possible recovery for Amorim ahead of Frosinone game

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

Ruben Amorim is expected to welcome back only one injured player for Sunday’s visit of Frosinone to San Siro, a report claims.

While we await the results of Mike Maignan’s tests today following his hip injury during the France-Turkey match, there’s news on the conditions of Fikayo Tomori and Ruben Loftus-Cheek, who both underwent check-ups yesterday to assess the progress of their respective muscle injuries.

MilanNews report that the defender is undoubtedly the closest to a full recovery. His recovery is progressing well, he’s already resumed individual training on the pitch, and his gradual return to the group is expected next week. That means, however, that he will miss Sunday night’s game.

It will take a while longer before we see Ruben Loftus-Cheek back on the field again, as he continues his recovery according to the originally planned schedule. In about two weeks, the English midfielder will undergo another medical check-up to determine the next steps towards his return to action.

With Matteo Gabbia, Alexis Saelemaekers and Samuel Chukwueze expected to be out for a while yet, the only injured player with a chance of recovering for Sunday’s game against Frosinone appears to be Strahinja Pavlovic, who will be evaluated on a day-to-day basis but trained yesterday.
