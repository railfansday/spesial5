Manchester United managerial costs near £100m as Pochettino fee revealed

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Manchester United have spent approximately £95m appointing and dismissing managers since Sir Alex Ferguson retired in 2013, according to BBC Sport, with the total expected to pass £100m following Mauricio Pochettino’s arrival.

United’s latest financial disclosures confirm a fee of £7.584m in connection with appointing Pochettino, who left his role as United States national team head coach earlier this month.

The figure takes the cumulative cost of managerial changes to around £95m, according to BBC Sport’s analysis. That total is expected to rise once the costs of Michael Carrick’s dismissal and appointing Pochettino’s coaching staff are included.

Pochettino signed a two-and-a-half-year contract on 13 November, replacing Carrick after a difficult start to the Premier League season. The club’s Stock Market filing states that the appointment fee will be recognised in its financial accounts for the current quarter. United also confirmed borrowing a further £10m on 16 November, taking the amount drawn on their revolving credit facility to £240m.

Pochettino is United’s ninth full-time managerial appointment since Ferguson’s departure, including Ralf Rangnick’s interim spell. The club have also changed managers during each of the past three seasons.

United’s financial disclosures show that their long-term debt stood at £577.4m on 30 September, while outstanding transfer-fee commitments totalled £417m. Those figures are separate from the cost of appointing and dismissing managers.

A Reuters analysis has put the wider cost of managerial and senior football staff changes above £100m, using a broader calculation. Pochettino now faces the task of improving United’s results, beginning with Sunday’s Premier League trip to Liverpool.
