Lewis Hall injury latest ahead of Brighton vs Newcastle United

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Newcastle United have been handed an injury boost ahead of their Premier League match against Brighton on Sunday as Lewis Hall is expected to be available.

Hall was forced off during the closing stages of Newcastle’s 2-2 draw with Arsenal last Saturday.

Speaking after his side’s draw at St James’ Park, Newcastle head coach Matthias Jaissle said: ‘Hopefully it is only just a cramp that meant Lewis Hall had to come off. That is what we are hoping.’

Jaissle did not provide an update on Hall during his pre-match press conference on Friday ahead of Newcastle’s trip to Brighton this weekend.

However, Newcastle have confirmed that Hall has taken part in training this week, which suggests that the left-back remains in contention to keep his starting place against Brighton.

Meanwhile, Jaissle revealed that Tino Livramento could start against Brighton after he made his return from a nine-week absence during the draw against Arsenal last weekend.

‘Always better when we need to make decisions when it comes to the starting eleven. Tino, for example, is on his way back but we need to see if he is ready to start,’ Jaissle said.

‘We still have two days. I don’t want to rush something. He is such a fantastic footballer, we need to be smart with him.’
