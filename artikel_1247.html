Man Utd Missed Out on Signing Wonderkid Rio Ngumoha to Liverpool - Here’s Why

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Rio Ngumoha has become a Liverpool hero at the tender age of 18, but he could have been starring for rivals Manchester United had he chosen the Red Devils over the Merseysiders.

The English teenage starlet enjoyed a dream Premier League debut in August 2025, coming off the bench to score a 90+10th minute winner in a 3-2 win for Liverpool against Newcastle United. It made him the youngest goalscorer in the club's history.

Ngumoha also became only the second 16-year-old to score a winner in the Premier League, following in the footsteps of Manchester United legend Wayne Rooney. Now considered one of the best young players in world football at the age of 18, he could have taken up a similar path to the Red Devils' all-time top goalscorer.

Ngumoha started his youth career as a member of Chelsea's academy but headed to Liverpool in September 2024. The Reds were ordered to reimburse Chelsea a minimum of £2.8 million for Ngumoha's training and development while with the west London outfit.

He was one of the most coveted teenage prospects, with the Merseyside giants seeing off competition from Manchester United to bring him to Anfield.

The four-cap England international visited United in June 2024 and was informed of what plans the club had for him. They knew that their arch-rivals were in "pole position" to sign him, but they still "pushed for his signature anyway", per The Athletic.

Despite showing Ngumoha around Old Trafford and Carrington, United's efforts were in vain, and he ultimately snubbed them in favour of the Reds.

The Sun reported that Ngumoha was left "impressed" with the pathway shown by Liverpool, with Conor Bradley and Jarell Quansah (now at Bayer Leverkusen) used as examples. He saw how quickly they were fast-tracked into the first-team.

Ngumoha started training and playing with the Reds' youth teams but was soon brought into Arne Slot's senior team for first-team training. He has gone from strength to strength ever since. In November 2026, he put in an electric display against United, scoring and recording an assist in his side's resounding 4-0 victory at Anfield.

Ngumoha seemingly turned down the chance to play for the club he supported during his childhood when he opted to join Liverpool last year. He'd revealed in a viral video promoting Adidas F5O boots that he had grown up as a United fan.

The English wonderkid was asked to choose between club legend David Beckham and current Real Madrid midfielder Jude Bellingham. He responded, "I was a Man United fan, I didn't really watch David Beckham... but I'll have to go David Beckham."

Ngumoha, born in August 2008, would have been just five when Sir Alex Ferguson retired and oversaw the last campaign in which United won the Premier League title. He wasn't even born when Wayne Rooney and Cristiano Ronaldo lifted the Champions League trophy in Moscow after defeating Chelsea in the final in May 2008.

Man Utd legend Nicky Butt has blasted his former club for not managing to get Ngumoha to put pen-to-paper. He said on The Good, The Bad & The Football podcas: "He’s a massive Man United fan, and his family – massive Man Utd fan.

“I think he was at 13 or 14 or whatever age it was, he goes and looks around Man United – whether it be Old Trafford or Carrington.

“He’s gone with his family to look around the football club, and then he’s gone to Liverpool. And he’s picked Liverpool, and he’s a United fan. He should not be allowed out of that building before he signed that contract. It would never have happened.

“I’d like to know who showed him around the football club. Who’s explaining things? I’d like to think Wayne Rooney or Paul Scholes or Ryan Giggs [were drafted in].”

The London-born winger isn't the only boyhood Red Devil supporter to play for a rival Premier League club. Cole Palmer was also a fan but has now played for Manchester City and Chelsea.
