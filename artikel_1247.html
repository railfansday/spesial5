Serie A line-ups: Fiorentina vs. Juventus

Tanggal: 2026-11-08

Nick Woltemade is declared fit to start in attack for Juventus against Fiorentina in Serie A on Sunday evening, as Paolo Vanoli makes just one alteration to his winning side.

You can follow the action as it unfolds on the Football Italia Liveblog.

Hosts Fiorentina find themselves in 15th place in the Serie A standings ahead of kick-off. They have won two, drawn two and lost six of their opening 10 league games this season, but are unbeaten across their last two, claiming a 4-2 victory over Sassuolo last weekend.

Juventus are in seventh place after the first 10 league games of the season after five wins, two draws and three defeats at the start of the Serie A season. They picked up a much-needed 2-0 victory over Napoli last time out in the league and have since beaten AZ Alkmaar 2-1 away from home in the Europa League.

The Bianconeri still have a hefty injury list that includes Kenan Yildiz, Khephren Thuram, Manuel Locatelli, Nico Gonzalez and Francisco Conceicao.

Nick Woltemade is declared fit despite struggling with a bout of flu, and is included in Sunday’s starting line-up alongside Randal Kolo Muani in attack. Jeremie Boga completes the attacking trident.

Paolo Vanoli has made just one change to his side that defeated Sassuolo last weekend: Pedro Goncalves is introduced in midfield in place of Marco Brescianini.

Kick-off is due at 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET).

Fiorentina (4-2-3-1): De Gea; Jiménez, Dragusin, Viery, Joao Mario; Ndour, Oulai; Mastantuono, Gonçalves, Njie; Pellegrino.

Juventus (4-3-3): Vicario; Kalulu, Bremer, Lucumí, Cabal; McKennie, Douglas Luiz, Koopmeiners; Kolo Muani, Woltemade, Boga.

Peter Young is a Senior Reporter with Football Italia.
