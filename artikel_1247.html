US Police Share Statement as English Footballer Shot and Killed in Mississippi

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Tragic reports have emerged from the United States, confirming that a young English footballer was shot and killed in Mississippi. The local police have now shared a statement after making an arrest.

Krissh Mahatma was born in London but had moved to the US to pursue his dream of making it as a footballer. The 18-year-old striker was in his freshman year at Copiah-Lincoln Community College in Wesson, around 45 miles south of Jackson, Mississippi, where the incident took place.

Before moving to the US, he had played for Watford 'A' in the post-16 Community and Education Football Alliance league. Co-Lin announced his signing in February.

As reported by the Daily Mail, Mahatma was fatally struck by a bullet after a gun was fired outside the Green Ghost Tacos restaurant in Jackson at around 3:39 a.m. on Sunday morning.

The English footballer was with his Co-Lin Wolves teammates when they reportedly came across a drunk man outside the restaurant. After a fight broke out, a man pulled out a gun and fired into the group, hitting Mahatma.

The 18-year-old was taken to a nearby hospital via a private vehicle. According to Hinds County Coroner Jeramiah Howard, this was where he died.

Capitol Police have since arrested and charged Danny Luna, 22, with murder. The Mississippi Department of Public Safety did not elaborate on what caused the deadly confrontation. However, in a brief statement, Police Chief Mark Miller sent 'thoughts and prayers' to Mahatma's 'family and loved ones,' adding:

"While this arrest is an important step toward justice, we recognise that nothing can ease the pain of losing a loved one."

Mahatma had made 14 appearances in his first three months with the Co-Lin Wolves, scoring one goal in September in a 3-2 win over National Park College. Co-Lin president Dewayne Middleton issued his own statement on behalf of the college:

"Copiah-Lincoln Community College is mourning the loss of freshman soccer player Krissh Mahatma. Krissh will be deeply missed by his teammates and the entire Co-Lin family."

A fundraiser has been set up to support Mahatma's family and has already raised $31,851 (£24,275). It explained how the young striker was "just beginning a new chapter of life far away from home," before adding: "His sudden passing has left his family, friends, teammates, coaches, and the entire Co-Lin soccer community heartbroken.

"Behind this tragedy is a family grieving the loss of their son and loved ones trying to make sense of a loss that feels impossible to put into words."

The owner of the Green Ghost Tacos eatery, nearby where the shooting took place, said: "On behalf of the entire Green Ghost family, I extend my deepest and most heartfelt condolences to the family, friends, loved ones, and university community mourning the tragic loss of a young student. Our hearts are heavy, and our thoughts and prayers are with everyone affected by this devastating incident.

"While this tragedy occurred near our establishment, we would like to clarify that Green Ghost was closed and was not conducting business at the time of the shooting. The safety and well-being of our patrons, employees, and surrounding community remain a top priority. We are committed to cooperating fully with law enforcement as they investigate the circumstances surrounding this tragic incident.

"Out of respect for the grieving family and the investigation, we will refrain from speculating or commenting further on the details at this time. Above all, we recognise that a family has suffered an unimaginable loss. We ask that the community join us in extending compassion, prayers, and support to those affected during this incredibly difficult time."
