Report Udogie could be eager for Juventus after Tottenham

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Juventus could continue their transfer synergy with Tottenham Hotspur by asking for Italy international Destiny Udogie in January, following Guglielmo Vicario and Pape Matar Sarr.

The two clubs have developed a strong rapport in recent months, completing several deals including the loans of goalkeeper Vicario and midfielder Sarr.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, there could be a third place making the move from Spurs to Turin in the January transfer window.

The Bianconeri have confirmed they are seeking a left-back to reinforce the squad under coach Luciano Spalletti, and have looked towards the Premier League for ideas.

Their first choice would be Manchester City player Rayan Ait-Nouri, but getting an agreement for him could be difficult.

Instead, Udogie has emerged as an intriguing alternative, because he is looking for somewhere to regain his confidence and fitness after injury problems.

He has made seven appearances for Spurs in all competition so far this season.

Tottenham Hotspur paid €18m plus up to €7m in various add-ons to purchase Udogie from Udinese in 2022.

The player was given his senior Italy debut by current Juventus coach Spalletti in October 2023, but has not been called up to the squad in over a year.
