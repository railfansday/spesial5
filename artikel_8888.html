chiaro audio guide app turns smartphone photos of landmarks into 18-language spoken tours

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

point your phone at a door at notre-dame de paris and chiaro tells you which one it is, then tells its story out loud, in any of 18 languages.

Muhammad edited by christina vergopoulou November 17, 2026 2 min

Chiaro is a mobile audio guide application that generates location- and object-specific narration from user photographs rather than relying on predetermined museum routes or numbered stops. Designed for real-time architectural and cultural exploration, the interface uses visual recognition to identify landmarks and architectural details directly through a smartphone camera, immediately delivering spoken context in 18 languages.

For example, capturing a photograph of an entrance on the west facade of Notre-Dame de Paris prompts the system to identify the structure as the Portal of the Virgin, detailing its carving between 1210 and 1220 and the stacked sculptural reliefs of the death and coronation of the Virgin above. Following the initial overview, Chiaro’s software sequences secondary historical chapters, such as information on the portal’s patronage, while supporting interactive voice or text queries that dynamically generate subsequent audio segments based on user questions.
