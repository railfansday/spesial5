Lowe’s is giving away $15 million in Black Friday prizes: How to claim free Mystery Buckets and $2,000 appliances

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

What are your plans for Black Friday? Hide under a blanket and pretend it's all just a bad dream? I know the feeling, and honestly, I support this.

That being said, you are potentially missing out on some pretty special deals by pretending Black Friday doesn't exist. Yes, there are heaps of online deals there for the taking, but store doorbusters also deserve your attention. And Lowe’s just raised the stakes for Black Friday.

Lowe’s has officially unveiled the details for its returning Black Friday bucket giveaway, kicking off at 6 a.m. local time on Nov. 27 across more than 1,700 stores nationwide. Exclusively open to MyLowe’s Rewards members (it's free to join), this year’s event triples the winner limit from last year.

In 2026, Lowe's is handing out $15 million in total prizes nationwide to the first 150 loyalty members in line at every location.

If you're in the first 50 MyLowe’s Rewards members through the doors at 6 a.m., you will score a free Lowe’s Black Friday Mystery Bucket packed with 10+ products and offers. Even better, four lucky shoppers out of the first 50 at every store will also walk away with one of the day’s grand prizes. That includes a free major appliance under $2,000, an EGO outdoor power tool (valued at up to $359), a Blackstone grilling package (valued at up to $329), or a Craftsman tools package (valued at up to $359).

The next 100 members in line still walk away with a collectible Lowe’s Golden Mini Bucket, $5 in MyLowe’s Money, a 20% off coupon valid the following weekend, a free paint sample, and a HomeCare+ offer.

Lowe's is also offering free hot chocolate, holiday music, and surprise Live Nation performances at select stores in Los Angeles, Dallas, D.C., Charlotte, Nashville, and Atlanta.

Before you throw off your blanket and head off in search of Black Friday freebies, make sure you're a MyLowe’s Rewards member. Sign up for the free loyalty program in the Lowe’s app before Friday so you aren't scrambling to register on spotty store WiFi while everyone walks past you. I should also make it clear that those 50 Mystery Buckets will go to shoppers lining up well before the 6 a.m. doors open. How committed are you?
