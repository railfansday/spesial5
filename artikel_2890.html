Paulsboro football routs Audubon to reach sectional title game

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

PAULSBORO – Frank Damminger said the expectations to get to the sectional championship game were always there for the Paulsboro High School football team.

"When you're from Paulsboro, you expect it," the senior two-way lineman and captain said. "The expectations are always there for us. We just couldn't get over this hump to get to the 'ship (game) the last couple of years. Now, we're there and want to finish the job."

Paulsboro checked into its first sectional championship game in four years with a 38-0 rout over Audubon in the Central Jersey Group 3 semifinal on Saturday at Glenn Howard Stadium.

The top-seeded Red Raiders (9-2) will shoot for their first sectional title since 2017 when they host second-seeded Salem (8-3) in Saturday's championship. Kickoff is set for noon.

The two teams met earlier this season with Paulsboro earning an 8-6 decision on Sept. 5. They also have a long tradition, including several meetings in sectional finals. Salem has won the last two title-game meetings in 2021 and 2022 while the Red Raiders' last win against Salem with the championship on the line was in 2016.

"It's a great feeling to get here, it's our first time being here," senior Marques Eli said. "But we're not done yet.

"It would mean a lot to us to get that title. If we go in there and do our thing, I know we can win it."

Since a 16-8 loss to Schalick on a rainy Saturday afternoon on Sept. 26, Paulsboro has rattled off six straight wins and there momentum has only gotten stronger in recent weeks as the team hasn't allowed a single point over its last 13 quarters of play.

"We've been on a crazy run and when we have that momentum going, we're really good," Damminger said. "After the Schalick loss, (the captains) Marques (Eli), Zay (Mears), Jeremiah (Carr) and CJ (Calvin Wilkinson), had to get real with (the younger players). We had to feed into these young kids' minds. … We said we don't care how young you are, you're this good and you're going to help us win. We had to get everybody to go all in."

Eli agreed, "We've just been locked in since that Schalick game. Better practices, better work. We're still having fun too, but we're really coming together as a team."

Paulsboro didn't waste any time in calming down the visiting Green Wave (6-3), which had won their last six games in a row.

Paulsboro's offense scored on its first three possessions while the defense racked up four sacks and had an interception from Eli in the game.

Senior Amouri Herbert-Roberts delivered the first sack, but it was a crucial sack by Damminger on a fourth-down play deep in Paulsboro territory in the first quarter that swayed the momentum completely in the Red Raiders' favor. Damminger's sack was his team-best ninth of the season.

"We're clicking right now," Paulsboro head coach Kevin Harvey said. "We're hitting our stride at the right time and playing good defense. … We know our assignments, we just have to execute."

Senior Zayion Mears sandwiched a pair of rushing touchdowns around a 90-yard TD run by Eli in the first half.

Junior quarterback Jahsir Johnson continued to show his maturity. After throwing a pair of picks, Johnson fired two TD passes in the second half, including on a fourth-down call that Julian Gaines finished with a great one-handed grab in the end zone.

Audubon, which hadn't been held to fewer than 20 points in any previous game this season, couldn't dent the scoreboard.

The Green Wave's defense produced interceptions by Will Severance and Ethan Haines.

Audubon will complete its season when it hosts Haddon Township on Thanksgiving.
