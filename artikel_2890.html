What NFL game is on TV in your area today? Week 11 coverage map

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The stakes are starting to build as the 2026 NFL regular season nears Thanksgiving, and the Week 11 Sunday schedule is filled with games that could carry significant impact on the playoff race.

As usual, FOX and CBS will split the rights to the 10-game Sunday afternoon slate. CBS will get to broadcast a game in both the early- and late-afternoon windows in each region. The network will feature the Kansas City Chiefs vs. Arizona Cardinals in the early window and a matchup between the Jacksonville Jaguars and New York Giants in the late afternoon.

FOX has the rights to five games and will broadcast just one regionally, with the Tennessee Titans' visit to face the Dallas Cowboys and a matchup between the Chicago Bears and New Orleans Saints featured prominently around the country.

Here's a look at which games will be broadcast in each area of the country during Week 11 of the 2026 NFL season.
