A massive bearish trade just happened in the chip stocks

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

So much for buying the dip.As the S&P 500 entered correction territory and the Nasdaq-100 flirted with bear-market territory, down 19% from highs as of midday Friday, the single biggest options trade of the day was a near $100-million bet that the VanEck Semiconductor ETF (SMH) has a long way more to fall.Around 12:30 E.T., someone spent $93 million buying 20,000 380-strike puts expiring Sep. 17, 2027, according to trading data reviewed by SpotGamma and Volland, options-analytics platforms that both show the trade as a likely opening position, with volume exceeding existing open interest in the contract. The trade bets that the index will fall below $380, a nearly 8% decline from its current level. The SMH is off 40% from its recent all-time highs.

"That trade stands out big-time," wrote Jason DeLorenzo, founder and owner of Volland. "It is either a hedge or speculative, but either way it isn't a vote of confidence."The puts were bought for $46.50 a piece, an arguably expensive choice for a downside bet with volatility in the ETF trading at 70.

"You're paying a metric ton of Vega for that thing," added SpotGamma's Brent Kochuba, referring to the contract's sensitivity to changes in volatility of the underlying chip ETF. "Maybe they were a forced buyer, or maybe they're actually selling this, which can still be the case even though the price shows it happening closer to the ask."

The semiconductor index headed for a 9% drop this week alone.
