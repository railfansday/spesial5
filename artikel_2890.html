What Chiefs’ Patrick Mahomes said about Harrison Butker blowing Bills game with another missed kick

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Kansas City Chiefs' 13-10 loss to the Buffalo Bills ultimately led to widespread criticism of Harrison Butker. The Chiefs kicker missed a crucial 46-yard field goal in Thursday night's game.

The fumble came with just over six minutes remaining and could have given Kansas City the lead. The Chiefs were tied at 10 when Butker's attempt sailed wide right. Buffalo eventually won the game on Tyler Bass' 57-yard field goal as time expired, handing Kansas City its third loss of the season and snapping its three-game winning streak.

Patrick Mahomes, who has played alongside Butker since both joined the Chiefs in 2017, defended the veteran kicker after the game.

"I try to have confidence in everybody on the team," Mahomes said. "Obviously, Harrison hasn't had the season that he's wanted this year, but he's also been one of the best kickers in the game for a long time."

Mahomes also acknowledged that Butker's latest miss was disappointing but backed him to bounce back.

"Unfortunately, tonight it didn't go his way, but I promise you he'll come back and work at it again, and hopefully the next time he can go out there and make it happen," Mahomes added.

Butker has struggled throughout the 2026 season. He has converted 15 of 23 field-goal attempts and 32 of 33 extra points through 11 games. His eight missed field goals and one missed extra point have him tied for the most missed kicks in the NFL this season.

Thursday's miss only added to a difficult stretch for the three-time Super Bowl champion. Butker had made 16 consecutive kicks, including field goals and extra points, before the miss against Buffalo.
