NFL trade deadline moves: Should the Browns have made any of these?

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Over the last couple of weeks, NFL trade deadline rumors ran hot and heavy, just not for the Cleveland Browns. The Browns were linked to a number of defensive ends as possible trade options, and trading WR Jerry Jeudy and QB Shedeur Sanders were suggested along the way. In the end, Cleveland GM Andrew Berry held on to premium picks and salary cap space for future options instead of present problems.

Over the last two weeks, a wide array of trades went down across the league. Some of them were of the monumental variety, pulled off by the Chicago Bears and Jacksonville Jaguars, some were mostly shuffling around lesser draft capital, and a few fell somewhere in the middle.

A look at all the different trades that went down over the last few weeks: are there any that the Browns should have made by matching the offer or increasing it just slightly?

Chiefs receive: WR Josh Downs and a 2027 seventh-round pick

Colts receive: A 2027 fourth-round pick and a 2027 fifth-round pick

Chargers receive: A 2027 fourth-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick

Raiders receive: A 2027 third-round pick.

Bills receive: S Amani Hooker and a 2027 fifth-round pick

Eagles receive: A 2027 first-round pick and a 2029 second-round pick

Jets receive: A 2028 sixth-round selection

Saints receive: 2028 sixth-round pick (which could elevate to a 2027 sixth-round pick if Jacksonville acquires one via trade or the compensatory selection formula)

Falcons receive: A 2027 second-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick

Ravens receive: QB Jacoby Brissett and a 2027 fifth-round pick

Cardinals receive: A 2027 fourth-round pick and a 2028 seventh-round pick

Bears receive: DL Jeffery Simmons and a 2028 fifth-round pick

Titans receive: A 2027 first-round pick, a 2028 first-round pick, and a 2027 fourth-round pick

Colts receive: A 2027 third-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick

Thanks to our national coverage at SB Nation for the easy access copy and paste of all the deals.
