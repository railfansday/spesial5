Is Stevenage v Sheffield Wednesday on TV? Channel and streaming details ahead of League One clash

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Sheffield Wednesday are gearing up to face Stevenage under the lights at the SPORTA Arena.

The Owls made it back-to-back wins at the weekend, sinking South Yorkshire rivals Barnsley with an early Jamal Lowe brace in 2-1 victory.

Wednesday sit fifth in the League One table and will be keen to put pressure on the top two with another win, this time on the road.

After the win at Oakwell, Owls boss Dave Challinor said: “Winning's a brilliant habit, but I'm very realistic about the work that needs to be done.

“I never have been a great winner, as daft as that sounds. My job's to be analytical and understand where we get better.

“I want them to enjoy winning. I've said that to them. I want it to be absolutely bouncing because winning's a brilliant feeling and a brilliant habit to get into.

“They found a way when maybe not performed the best. There's loads more to come from this group. My job is to make sure that happens.”

Here are the key TV and streaming details as Wednesday prepare to do battle with Stevenage.

Kick-off at the SPORTA Arena is scheduled for 7:45pm tonight (Tuesday, November 24).

Yes - those not heading to the game will be able to enjoy the action on TV. The fixture has not been given top billing on any of the main Sky Sports channels but can be viewed as an alternative broadcast via the Sky Sports+ service. Subscriptions can be purchased online.

Supporters can stream the action via the official Sky Sports app or on NOW TV with a valid subscription. There will also be live audio commentary on BBC Radio Sheffield.
