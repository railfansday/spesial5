‘The decision is entirely his’ – Van Bommel denies advising De Bruyne on PSV move

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Syndicated reporting provided by Stats Perform and reviewed by the SoccerNews editorial team.

Belgium boss Mark van Bommel denied reports he has advised Kevin De Bruyne to consider a January move to PSV, insisting "the decision is entirely his".

PSV have reportedly made contact with De Bruyne, who is believed to be unhappy at Napoli, where his contract expires in June.

It was speculated Van Bommel had encouraged the midfielder to join PSV, where he won four league titles and made over 270 appearances across two spells.

However, Van Bommel clarified his stance following Sunday's 4-1 win over Italy, which booked the Red Devils' place in the Nations League quarter-finals.

"I was asked a question by a Belgian reporter about whether De Bruyne would fit in with the PSV style of play, and seeing as I know the club well," Van Bommel told Sky Sport Italia.

"I said yes he would, but he also fits in at Napoli. So, I answered a specific question, but I did not advise him to do anything. The decision is entirely his, and I leave that to him."

Belgium headed into their final Group A1 clash occupying third place, a point behind leaders Italy, who took the lead through Pio Esposito.

However, three goals in 11 first-half minutes from Maxim De Cuyper, Nathan Ngoy and Jeremy Doku flipped the contest on its head, before Diego Moreira added a fourth 10 minutes from time.

The Red Devils bettered their 3.42 xG, yet Van Bommel insists the margin of victory would have been greater but for good goalkeeping from Italy's Gianluigi Donnarumma.

"If Donnarumma had not been in goal, then we would've scored six," he added.

"I'll be honest, I didn't expect [to qualify] when we started two months ago. But even before that first match with Italy [September's 2-0 win], I could tell this was a really intelligent group of players. That makes it a pleasure for a coach to work with them."
