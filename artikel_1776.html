Prediction: Oxford United vs Cheltenham Town

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Two camps who will turn their attention back to EFL Trophy action on Tuesday night, Oxford United will welcome Cheltenham to the Kassam Stadium.

Although Europe’s elite domestic leagues might be in the midst of November’s international break, there are still plenty of eye-catching betting promotions available.

Ahead of Tuesday’s EFL Trophy showdown, you can find all of the key Oxford United vs Cheltenham Town H2H stats here.

Verdict: Oxford United win & Under 4.5 goals

Starting with the hosts, losing their Championship status last season, it is no secret that Oxford’s sole focus is on booking an immediate second-tier return this time around. While Aaron Ramsey’s side might have watched their FA Cup adventure come to a frustrating end over the weekend as they eventually slumped to a 1-0 loss against Stevenage, the Yellows are firmly scrapping it out at the top of the League One standings. Getting November underway with a rugged 2-0 victory at home against Reading, the former Wales international will be calling on his squad to make another statement when they turn their attention back to EFL Trophy action on Tuesday night. In fact, despite their disappointing FA Cup exit on Saturday, that is the only defeat that Oxford have suffered from their previous 12 consecutive appearances across all competitions within 90 minutes.

Still waiting to make a maiden appearance of the season, South Korean midfielder Jin-Woo Jean remains on the sidelines with a back injury. However, making a cameo appearance off the bench on Saturday and returning from his own respective back issue, summer recruit Ruben Roosken should get some more minutes under his belt.

Shown a straight red card against Barnsley last month, former MK Dons goalkeeper Jamie Cumming will once again miss out through suspension. One-time Liverpool youngster Cameron Brannagan will return to Ramsey’s squad on Tuesday night.

As for the visitors, while Cheltenham might have spent the majority of last season looking over their shoulder at a looming relegation scrap, the Robins have made a string of improvements this time around. Last seen over the weekend making an immediate return to winning ways as they extended on their respective FA Cup charge thanks to a 2-0 victory at home against National League outfit Halifax, Steve Cotterill’s camp have won three of their previous four appearances across all competitions. However, despite finding themselves at the heart of a shock promotion charge in England’s fourth-tier, Cheltenham have struggled to find their feet away from Whaddon Road. Including signing off October with a 3-1 defeat at the hands of Tranmere, the Robbins have lost all of their previous three consecutive showdowns on the road by an aggregate score of 9-2.

Yet to feature since the start of October and recently undergoing surgery on a long-term hamstring injury, Cheltenham have already confirmed that summer recruit and former MK Dons midfielder Joe Tomlinson will not feature for the remainder of 2026.

Making a speedy return from a muscle injury that his sustained against Tottenham’s U21s last month, veteran goalkeeper Joe Day could return between the sticks. Bagging an EFL Trophy brace against Spurs’ academy on October 13th, George Miller should be handed a full recall on Tuesday night.

While Oxford might have watched their extended purple patch come to an end over the weekend, Tuesday’s hosts will certainly be smelling the chance to claim an immediate response here. With their sights fixed on booking an immediate Championship return this season, Ramsey’s camp find themselves pencilled in as a standout contender in midweek. Losing just one of their previous 12 straight appearances across all competitions within 90 minutes, Oxford will be looking to exploit a Cheltenham squad who have shown plenty of cause for concern on their travels.

Verdict: Oxford United win & Under 4.5 goals
