Pavlovic admits he jokes about retiring with Moreira at Partizan: “He’s a fantastic guy”

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

AC Milan defender Strahinja Pavlovic has spoken about potentially retiring alongside Diego Moreira in the future, after a new revelation.

It’s clear that, since he’s arrived, Moreira has had a huge impact on the dressing room. In terms of quality, Mark van Bommel called him ‘crazy’, but compared him to Kevin De Bruyne and Jeremy Doku.

Then, Koni De Winter repeated similar sentiments today, saying that he may be a little crazy, but he was a good character to be around and a great footballer and Milan fans can see this, too. The Belgian is already making the €50 million paid for him look like a bargain and he has plenty of room left for growth.

Speaking to SS Calcio, Pavlovic looked at his relationship with Moreira, the help from Massimiliano Allegri last season and who gave him the most difficult game in his career, as MilanPress relayed.

“Working with Allegri was beneficial for me. It’s under his guidance that I’ve made the most progress since arriving in Italy. As a central defender, I possess good speed, strength, and skill with the ball.

“I can pass the ball forward and score goals, but tactical skills have always been a weakness of mine. I only learned the basics when I joined the first team at Partizan. Given the way Italians view football, I was a bit behind the times, as their players begin to grasp certain concepts from a very young age.”

“Physically, the toughest challenge was undoubtedly against Romelu Lukaku. But also Marcus Thuram: he’s strong, quick, and very skilled with the ball at his feet. With Lautaro, however, you have to be very careful inside the penalty area: if you don’t stay close to him, he’ll score.

The bond that unites Moreira and Pavlovic…

“I didn’t even know he was Almami’s son [a former Partizan player]. He’s a fantastic guy, really smart. He knows a few bits of Serbian. I met his father during a training session. We talked about Partizan, of course. It was really nice to see him; I remember him from his time at Partizan.”

The possibility of joining Partizan at the end of his career…

“I often joke with Moreira, telling him that, at the end of our careers, we should play at least one season at Partizan. I point out that I’m three years older than him, so he’ll have to make a small sacrifice so we can get back together. He’s a truly great guy.

“There’s a fantastic atmosphere in our group. It’s a real pleasure to be part of Milan.”
