Federal grand jury adds charges against Toledo man allegedly found with explosives in his car in Delaware County

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

COLUMBUS, Ohio – A federal grand jury returned a superseding indictment charging Brian W. Shaffer, 39, of Toledo, with additional crimes following an incident that occurred earlier this year in Delaware County. Shaffer was allegedly found in possession of multiple explosive devices in his car.
