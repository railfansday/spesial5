West Ham team news and predicted XI to face Stoke City as Scott Parker bids to stop dismal form

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

Scott Parker’s return to West Ham has proved far from straightforward, with the former midfield cult hero yet to pick up his first three points in charge at WHUFC.

Currently sitting 13th place and three points off the Championship play-off positions, a 1-0 defeat at Portsmouth exemplified their goal-scoring struggles of late. Despite leading the division for goals scored, the Hammers have repeatedly struggled to apply the clinical finishing touch in recent weeks.

Registering an astonishing 144 shots across their last six games without securing a single victory, their attacking potency has faded. And, a clash with high-flying Stoke City isn’t exactly the game West Ham would want to arrest their recent struggles.

Mark Robins’ side arrive as the division’s form team, winning their last five on the spin to move into 2nd.

One positive for Parker is that he has a plethora of options to pick from on Saturday, with only a few injury concerns.

Alphonse Areola has been out since the defeat to Blackburn Rovers earlier this month due to a knock suffered in the warm-up. West Ham full-back Kyle Walker-Peters is also expected to be unavailable, with the defender still recovering from a muscle strain.

Keiber Lamadrid has only played 21 minutes of Championship football this term due to a knee injury sustained in September. He will be absent against the Potters, with a return expected in the coming weeks.

Herrick; Veltman, Mavropanos, Kilman, Mayers; Soucek, Mukasa, Engels; Austbo, Bowen; Piroe

The goalkeeping department has been one of the biggest concerns for West Ham this term due to the poor performances of Mads Hermansen and the injury struggles of Areola. However, Finlay Herrick has played admirably since coming into the side against Preston and will likely be in between the sticks.

Fellow academy graduate Ezra Mayers has also received more minutes since Parker’s arrival and should be given the nod at left-back, with Joel Veltman on the right.

Boasting the highest goal tally of any defender in the division with six, Konstantinos Mavropanos has been a mainstay at centre-back with Max Kilman as his partner, although his recent shaky displays could mean Morato comes in.

Edson Alvarez returned after a three-month absence against Preston and showed his worth at the base of West Ham’s midfield. However, having also played on Tuesday night, the Mexican’s minutes need to be managed, so Tomas Soucek could replace him.

Despite dividing opinion with his displays in the middle of the park, Arne Engels has performed well of late and will likely partner Divine Mukasa in a more advanced midfield role.

Jarrod Bowen has been WHUFC’s go-to man this term, registering seven goals and seven assists, and the captain will be on the right. The opposite flank is up for grabs though, with the lively Edvin Austbo potentially getting selected ahead of the inconsistent Manor Solomon.

Striker is a problem area, but Joel Piroe will likely get the nod once again.
