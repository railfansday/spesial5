Next Aberdeen manager odds as frontunners emerge after Stephen Robinson sacked

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

Two former Aberdeen coaches are leading candidates to return to the club as manager, according to bookmakers.

Stephen Robinson was sacked by Pittodrie chiefs yesterday after just eight months in charge. A dismal 4-0 defeat at home to Hibs was the final straw after a start to the season which has them sitting 11th in the Premiership.

Some names like Neil Lennon, John McGlynn and Gary Caldwell have been mooted to take over, while bookmakers have been busy making up their own lists.

According to Gambling.com, the home of the best online casino, Tony Docherty is 4/1 favourite to take over. The former Dundee boss has just taken over at Scotland under-21s and was previously Derek McInnes' assistant at Pittodrie.
