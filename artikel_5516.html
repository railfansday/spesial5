Under Armour stock sinks as revenue falls 7% in all regions but Latin America

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Free daily briefing on global business news.

Under Armour trimmed its expected gross margin gain for fiscal 2027 and kept its mid-single-digit revenue decline forecast

Under Armour $UAA reported a 7% decline in fiscal second-quarter revenue to $1.24 billion on Friday, with sales falling in every region except Latin America.

Under Armour stock slid 10% to $4.80 ahead of the opening bell. The company posted a net loss of $425,000, compared with a net loss of $18.8 million in the year-earlier period. Its adjusted net loss was $2 million.

North America revenue declined 7% to $737 million. International revenue fell 7% to $509 million, a 9% drop at constant currency. EMEA and Asia-Pacific revenue each decreased 9%. Latin America revenue rose 3% but fell 4% at constant currency.

Wholesale revenue came in at $725 million, down 6%, while direct-to-consumer revenue totaled $494 million, an 8% decline. Within that channel, e-commerce revenue dropped 12%. Apparel revenue fell 6% to $880 million, footwear fell 11% to $234 million, and accessories fell 7% to $105 million.

Gross margin was 47.0%, down 30 basis points, which the company attributed to adverse currency movements, the mix of channels and regions, and pricing. Supply chain benefits and favorable product mix partly offset those factors. Operating income was $15 million, and inventory rose 4% to $1.1 billion.

"We delivered against our revenue expectation and exceeded our operating income outlook," Under Armour President and CEO Kevin Plank said in a statement.

Revenue topped the $1.23 billion analysts expected, according to The Wall Street Journal. Analysts surveyed by Zacks Investment Research had forecast $1.25 billion, according to The Associated Press.

The company updated its fiscal 2027 outlook to reflect higher external costs and planned inventory actions. It continues to forecast a revenue decline in the mid-single digits, unchanged from its prior forecast. It now expects gross margin to improve 170 to 220 basis points, down from the 220 to 270 basis points it had projected, citing higher fuel and freight costs, inventory management actions and foreign exchange headwinds.

Under Armour raised its operating income range to $99 million to $119 million from $96 million to $116 million. It left adjusted operating income at $140 million to $160 million. The company estimated headwinds from the conflict in the Middle East at about $45 million, up from $35 million.

Under Armour recorded $11 million in restructuring charges in the quarter. It has incurred $280 million in restructuring and transformation costs under its plan, which it expects to total about $305 million and to be substantially complete by Dec. 31. Cash and equivalents totaled $285 million at quarter-end, and $190 million was outstanding under its $1.1 billion revolving credit facility. The company will hold a conference call at about 8:30 a.m. ET on Friday.

Under Armour had cut its full-year revenue outlook in August, citing weaker consumer demand in North America and Asia-Pacific. First-quarter revenue fell 3% to $1.10 billion, and the company held its operating income forecast at $96 million to $116 million.
