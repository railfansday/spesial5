MegazoneSoft Achieves Two Google Cloud Advanced Competencies Simultaneously

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

SEOUL, South Korea, Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- MegazoneSoft, an affiliate of MegazoneCloud (CEO: Donghoon Yum), an AI, cloud, and security specialist, announced that it has simultaneously achieved two Google Cloud Advanced Competencies, the highest level of competency recognition in the Google Cloud Partner Network, in Gemini Enterprise and Artificial Intelligence (AI).

Google Cloud's competency framework consists of two levels: Competency and Advanced Competency. While both levels are evaluated using the same criteria, Advanced Competency requires partners to meet higher thresholds for proven performance, technical expertise, and demonstrated customer success.

MegazoneSoft has been recognized for establishing a systematic operational framework to meet Google Cloud's rigorous competency requirements. This includes expanding its specialized engineering and consulting teams and operating a dedicated organization focused on enterprise generative AI solutions built around Gemini Enterprise. By achieving these Advanced Competencies, MegazoneSoft has demonstrated not only its technical expertise and professional certifications but also its ability to apply Google Cloud technologies in real-world customer environments to deliver tangible business outcomes.

With the Gemini Enterprise Advanced Competency, MegazoneSoft has been recognized for its expertise in implementing and operating solutions that leverage Google's enterprise agentic AI platform, Gemini Enterprise, to enhance enterprise information search and analysis, workflow automation, collaboration, and decision-making processes.

The Artificial Intelligence Advanced Competency further validates MegazoneSoft's expertise in designing, building, and deploying AI solutions in real-world customer environments using a range of Google Cloud generative AI technologies.

In September, MegazoneSoft also achieved the Manufacturing & Industrial Advanced Competency. This recognition is awarded to partners that demonstrate expertise in helping customers leverage Google Cloud to improve manufacturing and industrial operations, optimize processes, and enhance production efficiency.

Building on its expertise in Google Cloud and AI, MegazoneSoft aims to help enterprises rapidly and systematically integrate AI agents into their business operations by connecting existing work environments, data, and legacy systems. The company plans to strengthen its competitive position in the enterprise AX market through a differentiated approach that provides integrated support across the entire transformation journey, from strategic planning and implementation to change management and ongoing operations.

As a Google Cloud Premier Partner, MegazoneSoft has completed more enterprise Google Workspace implementation projects than any other Google partner in Korea. Recently, the company established a unified Google Workspace-based work environment across 21 domestic and overseas affiliates of Nongshim Group and implemented a Google Workspace-based work environment for C&C International. MegazoneSoft continues to expand its AX business by providing comprehensive consulting and training programs for employees of its enterprise customers.

"Building on these achievements, we will further strengthen our end-to-end AX services, from consulting on the adoption of generative AI and AI agents to implementation and change management, while actively expanding industry-specific AI solutions across key sectors, including financial services, manufacturing, and retail," said Geon Jin, CEO of MegazoneSoft. "To help our customers successfully adopt AI transformation and realize tangible business value, we plan to expand our Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) organization and enhance our on-site, hands-on support capabilities."

MegazoneCloud is an AI, cloud, and security specialist that helps enterprises translate AI capabilities into tangible business outcomes. Through a robust ecosystem of more than 200 domestic and global partners, including AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, and NAVER Cloud, the company designs, builds, and operates customized AI systems and agents that integrate cloud and AI technologies, tailored to each customer's environment and business objectives.

MegazoneCloud also enables enterprises to scale AI securely by providing visibility, governance, and security across multi-platform environments. The company operates in 10 countries, with a presence spanning Korea, North America, Japan, China, Southeast Asia, Oceania, and the Middle East. With more than 2,000 professionals, MegazoneCloud supports the technological innovation of over 8,000 customers.
