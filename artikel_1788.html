‘Doors open’ - Mark Hateley delivers verdict on Leeds United defender Jayden Bogle’s England prospects

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Former England forward Mark Hateley believes consistency is key for Leeds United defender Jayden Bogle as he targets a maiden Three Lions call-up.

The 26-year-old was a £5m acquisition in the summer of 2024, when the Whites prised him from their South Yorkshire rivals Sheffield United.

He has gone from strength to strength in LS11, developing into one of Daniel Farke’s most trusted lieutenants.

Bogle has made no secret of his desire to break into the England squad, but faces stiff competition from high-profile stars.

“You just don't get into the England team now,” Hateley told The Yorkshire Post. “We have a lot of good players.

“You just have to look at the World Cup, some of the players that didn't go to the World Cup. We are where we are. We've got a Ballon d'Or winner, Harry Kane.

“You've probably got another two or three England players right now that could be Ballon d'Or winners in the next 5/10 years easily. That's the sort of opposition you've got trying to get into this England team. I'm not going to say team, I'm going to say squad.

“Consistency will do the young man well. I think Jayden, he's put in his performances. He's of a good age. He's not that fully developed player, but he is playing well, consistently well. You put that together over another year or another season, you're 27, 28, then who knows?”

Bogle was capped by England at under-20 level while on the books of Derby County and featured in the 2019 Toulon Tournament.

Two of his Leeds teammates - James Trafford and Dominic Calvert-Lewin - were named in Thomas Tuchel’s last England squad.

“A lot of opportunities can come your way,” said Hateley, who had a loan spell at Leeds in 1996. “Doors open.

“When that door does open, you have to steam into that door. You can only do that by having that consistency.”
