Prediction: Cruzeiro vs Palmeiras

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

In what is a heavyweight showdown at the top of the Serie A standings late on Wednesday night, Cruzeiro will welcome Palmeiras to Minas Gerais.

With Europe’s elite domestic leagues set to make their respective returns this weekend, there are plenty of eye-catching betting promotions available.

Ahead of Wednesday’s Serie A battle, you can find all of the key Cruzeiro vs Palmeiras H2H stats here.

Starting with the hosts, securing a bronze medal finish among Brazil’s elite last season, Cruzeiro have once again received a string of public praise from across South America. Last seen heading into November’s international break with an action-packed 3-2 victory at home against Bahia, the Foxes will simply be looking to carry on from where they left off prior to the fortnight interval. Marking their return in midweek once again cruising their way to another Copa Libertadores ticket, Arthur Jorge’s side find the mood in their camp at a red-hot high. Including signing off October with a 1-0 victory away at America-MG, Cruzeiro have won each of their previous four consecutive Serie A appearances. Looking to claim a heavyweight domestic scalp on Wednesday night, Jorge’s side have also registered 18 points from their last seven top-flight battles.

Limping off after just five minutes with a suspected muscle injury against Bahia prior to November’s international break, Cruzeiro are sweating over the fitness of former Flamengo defender Fabricio Bruno on Wednesday night. Not making a single appearance across any competition since back in July, Gabriel Pec remains on the sidelines after sustaining a broken leg over the summer.

Bagging a stellar Serie A brace last time and now registering seven goals in his previous six appearances across all fronts, the red-hot Kaio Jorge will spearhead Cruzeiro’s attack. Both serving respective domestic suspensions earlier in the month, the duo of Lucas Villalba and one-time West Brom playmaker Matheus Pereira will return to Jorge’s squad in midweek.

As for the visitors, marking their return from November’s international break sat just three points adrift of skyrocketing to the top of the Serie A standings, Palmeiras have their sights fixed on clinching a first domestic crown since 2023. Last seen extending on their Copa do Brasil and punching their spot in the final of this season’s tournament as they edged past Vasco, Abel Ferreira’s side will be looking to make an almighty statement on Wednesday night. Laying down their own respective marker earlier in the month as they cruised their way to a 6-1 romp against Remo, Palmeiras hold a fearsome reputation in South America. In fact, Palmeiras are unbeaten in all of their previous 20 consecutive appearances – a purple patch that stems all the way back to August 15th.

Not making a single appearance since the end of November after sustaining a long-term knee injury, Palmeiras will once again be without former Rangers youngster Jefte in midweek. Likewise, undergoing calf surgery last month, one-time Bayer Leverkusen starlet Paulinho remains a doubt on Wednesday night.

Despite failing to register a single strike across any competition over the past couple of months, ex-Barcelona misfit Victor Roque will spearhead Palmeiras’ charge here. Making a permanent switch from Premier League outfit Fulham last year, Brazilian international Andreas Pereira will start in the middle of the park and should be partnered alongside Marlon Freitas.

In what is a mouth-watering showdown at the top of the Serie A standings on Wednesday night, we could be treated to a season-defining battle from Minas Gerais. Two camps who will be looking to mark their respective returns from November’s international break with their own heavyweight statements, chances could fall at both ends of the pitch. Watching Cruzeiro head into November’s fortnight interval with a lively 3-2 victory at home against Bahia, neutrals will be hoping for another rollercoaster battle here. It should also be highlighted, three of the previous four meetings between Cruzeiro and Palmeiras have finished with both teams finding the net.
