Manchester United star ruled out of Liverpool trip in blow to Mauricio Pochettino

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

Marcus Rashford is not expected to be part of Manchester United's squad for Sunday's Premier League meeting with Liverpool because of an ongoing hip injury.

The England international had reportedly been hoping to be involved at Anfield, but is expected to once again miss Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge of the Red Devils.

Pochettino was appointed as Michael Carrick's successor during the international break and faces a difficult opening assignment against Liverpool, with United hoping to make a positive start under their new head coach.

Rashford has been sidelined since sustaining the hip issue and his absence would represent another setback for United, who have several other players dealing with fitness problems ahead of the trip to Merseyside.

Carlos Baleba, Benjamin Sesko and Manuel Ugarte are also expected to miss Sunday's game, while Luke Shaw remains unavailable. Goalkeeper Karl Darlow has also been dealing with an injury.

However, Harry Maguire and Patrick Dorgu have trained this week and could be available for selection. Lisandro Martinez is also back in contention after serving a suspension in United's 4-4 draw with Aston Villa before the international break.
