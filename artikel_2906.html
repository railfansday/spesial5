Lions' Aidan Hutchinson confirmed to have torn ACL, ending his 2026 season

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

Fears that Detroit Lions edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson suffered a torn ACL in his right knee were confirmed after additional exams, according to The Ringer's Tom Pelissero. Though tests reportedly revealed no other significant damage to Hutchinson's knee, he is expected to miss the remainder of the 2026 season with the injury.

On Tuesday, Lions head coach Dan Campbell confirmed that injury, saying "it stinks" and that Hutchinson will come out of it better on the other side.

Hutchinson injured his knee on the Lions' first defensive series versus the New England Patriots on Sunday in Munich. After sacking New England's Drake Maye, Detroit's fifth-year star jumped in celebration and landed awkwardly on his right leg. Hutchinson immediately went to the turf. As teammates and players approached, he gestured to the front of his right knee.

Campbell added Tuesday that he will not speak with Hutchinson about celebrating plays. Campbell said he didn't want to take emotion out of the game.

Hutchinson reportedly underwent an MRI after Detroit's 35-10 win that showed a torn ACL. Lions head coach Dan Campbell said the defensive end would undergo further testing when the team returned to the U.S. before making an official announcement.

"We'll know more when we get back in the States," head coach Dan Campbell said postgame, via the Detroit News' Nolan Bianchi. "Anytime you lose a player of his caliber, the type of teammate he is, he's a captain of ours, that doesn't make things easy."

Meanwhile, a fan captured video of Hutchinson in a wheelchair at the airport prior to the Lions traveling back to Michigan on Sunday.

Maye sustained an injury to his ankle on the same play and the Patriots fear the QB may have suffered a fracture that would end his season. The third-year star stayed in Germany for additional testing that could determine if he needs surgery, Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel confirmed during a radio interview Monday.

In nine games this season, Hutchinson recorded 6 sacks and 24 total tackles in addition to 1 pass defended and 1 forced fumble.

Hutchinson's torn ACL is the second serious, season-ending injury that he has sustained during his NFL career. During the 2024 season, he suffered a broken fibula and tibia in his left leg during a Week 6 win over the Dallas Cowboys. Hutchinson recovered to play in all 17 games last year, compiling 14.5 sacks.

The Lions' 2022 first-round draft pick (No. 2 overall) out of Michigan, Hutchinson has been named to two Pro Bowls and earned second-team All-Pro honors during his career. He's compiled 49 sacks in 65 games with 200 tackles and 5 interceptions.

Last year, Hutchinson signed a four-year, $180 million contract extension (including $141 million in guaranteed money). That extension kicks in next season following the fifth-year option the Lions picked up this year.
