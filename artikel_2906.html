Eagles vs. Cowboys odds: Opening lines for Week 12 matchup

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

The Philadelphia Eagles don't have long to celebrate their Week 11 win over the Pittsburgh Steelers.

The Birds must quickly turn the page to preparing for their Week 12 game against the Dallas Cowboys at AT&T Stadium on Thanksgiving.

These two teams previously met back in Week 7, which resulted in a 17-point Eagles loss. One key difference from that game is that former Cowboys quarterback Joe Milton is starting for the Eagles now. Revenge game incoming?!

The Eagles are 8.5-point road underdogs in this NFC East matchup, according to FanDuel.
