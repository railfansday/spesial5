NBA power rankings: Luka Dončić, Lakers on a tear as top team shifts again

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

It has been three weeks since the 2026-27 NBA regular season opened and there have already been three different teams to top this list.

That has been the state of play thus far, as no team has begun to separate itself as a dominant force – at least not yet.

But if there is one theme that has emerged, it's that potent offenses are ruling the day, and no player has been leading his team's scoring more than Los Angeles Lakers star Luka Dončić, who is putting up Wilt Chamberlain-like numbers.

Here are the Week 3 USA TODAY Sports NBA power rankings:

Though he has been inefficient, rookie Darius Acuff Jr. has been a bright spot. There haven't been many others.

As they have most of the season, the Bulls remain dead last in net rating and it's not even close, with an abysmally poor mark of -16.2. In fact, they're ranked dead last in several major metrics.

Practically all of their impact veterans are injured, and coach Will Hardy even appeared to mouth to himself on the sidelines during a recent game about how bad Utah has been.

Losers of seven consecutive, the Bucks have faced tough competition recently. Either way, the offense, which ranks 29th in points per game with 106.3, isn't capable enough to compete with below-average teams, either.

They simply have not found a third (or fourth) scoring option behind Michael Porter Jr. and Julius Randle.

As long as the Clippers continue to commit turnovers at the pace they do – 18.5 per game – the offense won't be able to sustain consistency.

It looks like that four-game win streak in the middle of the second week of the season was a mirage; the Pels have now lost three consecutive and allowed 138.5 points per game over their last two.

Since Tyrese Haliburton went down with a left hamstring strain, the Pacers have gone 1-6.

No team has a worse shooting percentage in the NBA than Charlotte, which is converting baskets at just 40%, a full 10 points lower than the Lakers (50.7%), the squad leading the category.

In a recent game, the Hornets were missing three starters, two of whom are their best scorers: Kon Knueppel, Brandon Miller and Moussa Diabate. The full-strength Cavs lost by 12.

The Grizzlies have cooled off a touch since their blazing start to the season, losing three of their last four.

They've lacked pop and don't have a single victory over a team with a winning record.

After essentially alternating wins and losses, it appears the Raptors may be getting some good news, as Kawhi Leonard (knee swelling) is due to be reevaluated soon.

It's clear the Mavericks are going to have flashes of brilliance when Cooper Flagg and Kyrie Irving are in sync the way they've been this week.
