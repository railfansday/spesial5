Joe Burrow on injury report, fully participates in practice despite illness

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Bengals quarterback Joe Burrow landed on the team's first injury report of the week, but it doesn't look like anything that should interfere with his availability for Monday night's game against the Commanders.

Burrow was listed as a full participant despite an illness. Burrow can become the first quarterback with at least four passing or rushing touchdowns in six straight games if his hot streak continues in Washington.

Cornerback Dax Hill is also dealing with an illness, but it kept him practicing on Thursday. Guard Dalton Risner (knees), linebacker Oren Burks (groin), and defensive tackle Jonathan Allen (rest) also sat out of practice.

Safety Kyle Dugger (quad) and guard Dylan Fairchild (oblique) were the team's limited practice participants. Linebacker Swayze Bozeman (forearm), cornerback Jalen Davis (hamstring), wide receiver Tee Higgins (knee), offensive tackle Myles Hinton (illness), wide receiver Andrei Iosivas (thumb), safety PJ Jules (ankle), and guard Brian Parker (knee) were full participants.
