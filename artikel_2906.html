Jaguars land DT Jordan Davis in blockbuster deal with Eagles, marking latest trade ahead of deadline

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

The Jacksonville Jaguars are going all in.

The Jaguars struck a deal to land Philadelphia Eagles defensive tackle Jordan Davis on Monday afternoon, just a day ahead of the NFL trade deadline, the team announced. In return, the Eagles are getting multiple draft picks, including a first-round selection for next April.

The deal is the third the Jaguars made on Monday to add a defensive player. They acquired safety Jessie Bates III in a trade with the Atlanta Falcons, and also picked up defensive tackle Davon Godchaux in a trade with the New Orleans Saints.

Davis is in his fifth season in the league. He racked up a career-high 72 total tackles and 4.5 sacks during the 2025 campaign, and has 31 total tackles and 1.5 sacks through nine games this season. The 26-year-old, whom the Eagles selected with the No. 13 overall pick in 2022 out of Georgia, signed a three-year, $78 million extension with the Eagles in March.

"We are extremely grateful to Jordan for his contributions to our team on the field and in the Philadelphia community since we drafted him five years ago. He has been a key contributor to our team culture and on-field success, which included two Super Bowl appearances and a world championship in 2024," the Eagles said in a statement.

"His growth and development as both a player and a leader are a testament to his hard work and character. We wish Jordan nothing but continued success."

Philadelphia is on a seven-game skid after Sunday's 19-11 loss to the New York Giants. The Eagles (2-7) are last in the NFC East and are all but out of the playoff race this season — especially with quarterback Jalen Hurts sidelined due to blood clots. The latest move gives the Eagles an additional first-round pick in next year's draft and a second-round pick in 2029.

They now have a combined 10 picks in the first two rounds of the next three drafts, including two in the first round in both 2027 and 2028. That should give the organization plenty of room to regroup after this season ends.

Meanwhile, Jacksonville clearly feels like it has a roster capable of making a Super Bowl push, and Monday's moves only bolster that feeling. The three additions will complement an already tough defense that has allowed just 15.1 points per game this season — which is the second-best output in the league behind only the Minnesota Vikings. The Jags sit at 6-2 coming out of Week 9, after they beat the Baltimore Ravens 33-10 for their fifth win in the past six outings.

Jacksonville no longer has a first- or second-round draft pick in 2027 after Monday's transactions, though the Jaguars still hold their first-round picks in 2028 and 2029.

The Jaguars have been to the playoffs only twice in the past nine seasons, and they fell in the wild-card round in 2025, the first under head coach Liam Coen's watch. While Monday's transactions were undoubtedly costly for the franchise, the Jaguars feel ready to go for it and make a legitimate postseason run. Whether that is enough for them to run the table in the AFC and make what would be the team's first Super Bowl remains to be seen.
