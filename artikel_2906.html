Cavs vs. Bulls: How to watch, odds, and injury report

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

The Cleveland Cavaliers will take on a Chicago Bulls team that is struggling to find it's way early in the season.

The new lottery reform was supposed to curb tanking. However, you can't legislate bad teams out of existence. And the gap between the haves and the have-nots in the NBA seems to be widening. The Bulls fall into that latter category.

Chicago has been awful this season, but they are coming off an impressive 117-106 road victory over the Minnesota Timberwolves. Norman Powell led the way with 24 points. If they're going to pull off a similar upset tonight, they'll likely need an encore performance like that from Powell or one of their other regulars.

The Cavs, meanwhile, are coming off an encouraging win without most of their regulars. They played the brand of basketball that head coach Kenny Atkinson would like to see from his regulars. We'll see if they can more closely resemble that playing style with a more typical lineup.
