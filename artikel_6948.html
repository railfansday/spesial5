Changing Lives Productions LLC: Assistant Editor

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Changing Lives Productions is the in-house video production company of Dhar Mann Studios, the #1 creator of scripted positive content online. With over 163 million followers and 82+ billion views across YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok, our videos receive more than 1 billion views each month.

We produce several hours of original content weekly with messages of compassion, kindness, and altruism that resonate with viewers of all ages. Our stories tackle real-life challenges and deliver powerful life lessons that resonate with global audiences.

Founded by Dhar Mann, named the “Moral Philosopher of YouTube” by The New York Times, a Time Top 100 Creator, and ranked #2 on Forbes’ 2025 Top Creators list—our founding principle is simple but ambitious: We’re not just telling stories. We’re changing lives.

We’re looking for an Assistant Editor who keeps our post-production pipeline organized and moving. You’ll collate and prep project files for our VFX and color teams, and manage the digital assets behind every episode we produce.

This is a hands-on support role. You’ll make sure editors, VFX artists, and colorists get clean, complete, correctly structured files on time, and that every piece of footage, project file, and finished master is ingested, organized, backed up, and archived.

We deliver several hours of original content every week. Missing media, broken links, or poorly prepped turnovers cost our VFX and color teams hours they don’t have.

A well-run library, with clear naming, accurate metadata, and reliable archives, means faster turnarounds, easy re-versioning, and no lost work. You’ll be the person who makes handoffs between editorial, VFX, and color seamless.

HOW YOU’LL SPEND YOUR TIME:Assistant editorial: file collation and handoffs

The Assistant Editor reports to the Jr. Post Production Manager.

You’ll work remotely from home or a personal office and log hours in Dhar Mann Studios’ time-tracking software.

The typical schedule is 40 hours per week. Specific hours and shift overlap with the US team will be discussed during interviews

No forms. Just smart, optimized applications.

Remember every detail and next step, without a meeting bot.
