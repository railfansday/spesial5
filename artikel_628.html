7-Eleven’s new Korean menu comes with K-BBQ taquitos and an unhinged TikTok mini-drama

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

TL;DR: 7-Eleven rolled out a limited-time Flavors of Korea menu featuring $5 Bibigo dumplings, Gochujang wings, and K-BBQ taquitos through Jan. 5, 2027, along with an in-app discount K-drama mini-series on TikTok.

Late-night convenience store runs in South Korea are legendary for good eating, and 7-Eleven is bringing that energy stateside. The retailer launched its limited-time Flavors of Korea menu across participating 7-Eleven, Speedway, and Stripes stores, remixing iconic grab-and-go staples with gochujang, kimchi, and sweet Korean barbecue glazes.

The chain teamed up with Korean food giant Bibigo to introduce branded items alongside house-made creations across both the hot bar and the cold grab-and-go cases. And if you don't feel like driving to your local store at midnight, the entire lineup is also available for on-demand delivery via the 7NOW app.

The limited-time menu runs through Jan. 5, 2027 (and Jan. 6 at Raise the Roost spots):

Bibigo Spicy Chicken Dumplings — 10 bite-sized dumplings filled with chicken, glass noodles, cabbage, and soy sauce.

Bibigo Korean BBQ-Style Pizza — Personal pizza with beef, caramelized onions, roasted garlic, green onions, cheese blend, and sweet bibigo BBQ sauce.

Gochujang Boneless Wings — Eight boneless wings glazed in a spicy-sweet mix of gochujang, garlic, ginger, and chili paste.

Korean-Style BBQ Taquito — Roller-grill taquito stuffed with chicken, onions, and K-BBQ sauce.

Korean-Style BBQ Seasoned Sausage — Pork sausage on the roller grill seasoned with brown sugar, garlic, ginger, and soy sauce.

K Town Chicken Salad Sando — Cold chicken salad sandwich spiced with gochujang and sesame oil on fire-and-milk marble bread.

K Noodle Salad — Cold sōmen noodles mixed with kimchi, veggies, and spicy gochujang dressing.

Seoul Style Sweet Heat Chicken Sandwich (Raise the Roost only) — Crispy tenders in sweet-heat sauce with bacon, pepper jack, slaw, and BBQ aioli on brioche.

For rewards members, 7Rewards and Speedy Rewards accounts can add any 20-ounce Coke or Pepsi bottle for $1 when buying two pizza slices, an order of bibigo dumplings, gochujang wings, or a deli sandwich through Jan. 6, 2027.

To accompany the menu, 7-Eleven produced an original vertical mini-series airing exclusively on its official TikTok and Instagram channels (@7eleven and @speedwaystores).

Directed by filmmaker Danny Lee, the seven-episode drama follows two classmates whose relationship unfolds during midnight convenience store runs. Sixty-second episodes air every Monday and Thursday through Nov. 23, leaning into classic K-drama romantic tropes, dramatic camera zooms, and convenience store dining.

Each episode release also unlocks a limited-time discount in the 7-Eleven app for the menu item that takes center stage in the storyline.

Head to your local store before early January to grab some $5 dumplings, and see if convenience store K-BBQ lives up to the drama.
