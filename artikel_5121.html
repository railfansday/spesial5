Bitget Launches rToken Earn, Bringing Onchain Yield To Tokenized ETFs

Tanggal: 2026-11-16

Bitget, one of the world’s largest Universal Exchanges, has launched rToken Earn, enabling eligible non-U.S. users in permitted jurisdictions to earn variable onchain yield on rQQQ and rSPY, while maintaining exposure to the underlying ETFs. The product is accessible through Bitget Earn, without requiring a separate wallet or direct interaction with DeFi protocols.   rToken Earn
