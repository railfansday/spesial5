The crypto Fear and Greed Index has capitulated into extreme fear as the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting minutes signaled a potential December 2026 rate hike.

The cryptocurrency’s Fear and Greed Index hovered at about 11 on November 19, 2026, according to data Coinpedia fetched from CoinMarketCap.

Over the past 30 days, the index plunged from 51, which represents ‘Neutral’, to ‘Fear’ and recently entered ‘Extreme Fear’. Cryptocurrency traders are wary of further capitulation driven by macroeconomic headwinds and regulatory uncertainty in the United States.

At the time of reporting, Bitcoin (BTC) had plummeted more than 16% over the past 7 days, trading at $62,450. Ethereum (ETH) had crashed by more than 15% during the last 7 days to $1,758 at press time.

As a result, the total crypto market cap had lost nearly 2% over the past 24 hours to roughly $2.13 trillion at the time of publication.

Notably, the last time the cryptocurrency industry experienced extreme fear was after the May/June 2026 market crash and during the February 2026 selloff. The heavy crypto liquidations over the past few weeks, following the hacks of major crypto exchanges, have played a major role in the current extreme fear.

The extreme fear in the cryptocurrency market was exaggerated by rising odds of a possible Federal rate hike next month. At press time, Polymarket, a leading  web3-based prediction market platform, had set the odds of a 25 basis points (bps) rate increase in December at 92%.

The platform had pegged the chances of no rate change and a 25 bps decrease at 2.2% and less than 1%, respectively.

With the Fed highlighting sticky inflation fueled by the Artificial Intelligence (AI) boom, the cryptocurrency faces a midterm uphill battle due to an unsupportive macroeconomic outlook.
