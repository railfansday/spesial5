Honeypot searches on Pornhub surge during Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang scandal

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Nvidia's board just reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, but not before Pornhub viewers were titillated by the details of the affair scandal.

The news of Huang's affair with a suspected Chinese spy leaked on Nov. 9. Soon after, allegations of a second and third affair (both with Nvidia employees) dropped. Huang resigned as CEO of the chipmaking company on Nov. 12, but he's already back following an endorsement by President Donald Trump.

Pornhub analyzed searches of several relevant terms from Nov. 9 to 17 — and as one might suspect, the salacious tech scandal has inspired some inquiries on the tube site. In data provided to Mashable, the highest Pornhub search by far is for the term "honeypot," which jumped a whopping 9,354 percent on the site. Honeypots refer to scammers or spies who feign romantic or sexual interest to obtain money, information, or other valuable assets.
