The Pokémon TCG Delta Reign Booster Display Box drops to a record low in Amazons Black Friday sale: Shop for under $200

Tanggal: 2026-11-28

TL;DR: The Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Delta Reign Booster Display Box is on sale for $199 at Amazon, down from its $219.99 list price.

After continuously sliding in price since the expansion’s launch in early October, the Pokémon TCG’s Delta Reign Booster Display Box has finally broken the $200 barrier in time for Black Friday.

As of Nov. 27, the Pokémon TCG’s 36-pack Delta Reign Booster Display Box can be yours for just $199 at Amazon — a $20.99 saving (10% off) that brings the cost of each booster down to $5.53. For comparison, that’s also $1.61 cheaper per pack than buying Delta Reign packs on TCGplayer ($5.94 plus $1 delivery).

Now at its lowest Amazon price ever, this new deal brings the 36-pack box to only $5.47 above its TCGplayer market price of $194.52.

The best deal comes from third-party seller BetterShopping4You with free delivery by Friday, Dec 4. However, if you want to buy from a seller with more positive feedback, there’s also BlueProton at the same price — but with delivery slightly later.

Across the box's 36 booster packs, there are 360 cards to unwrap, with over 140 cards in the expansion, including the expansion’s poster Pokemon Mega Rayquaza ex, Mega Golurk ex, Mega Malamar ex, and three Legendary Stadiums.
