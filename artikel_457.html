As GTA 6 release looms, Kai Cenat will stream 5 GTA games in one sitting

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Grand Theft Auto VI is mere weeks away, which means it's time for a trip down memory lane, courtesy of Kai Cenat.

The famed streamer and creator, who has just shy of 22 million Twitch followers at the time of publication, will take to the internet at 3 p.m. ET on Thursday, Nov. 5 for what should be a nostalgia-fueled journey through GTA's 3D era. Cenat said in a post on X that he will stream, in one sitting, GTA 3, Vice City, San Andreas, 4, and 5, AKA the entire mainline series output from the 21st century. The stream will be up on both Twitch and YouTube.

The X post didn't say how long he'd be streaming or whether he planned to actually finish the story in each game, which could possibly take a very long time. Even if Cenat just messes around and jacks cars for several hours, though, it should still be a fun reminder of where the series has been before we all get a collective glimpse at its future on Nov. 19 when GTA 6 comes out.

GTA VI will be released Nov. 19 on PS5 and Xbox consoles.
