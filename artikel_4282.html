PlayStation 5 Crosses Major Sales Milestone Ahead of GTA 6 Launch

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Daniel is a lifelong gamer with a love for various genres and gameplay styles who enjoys exploring games and playing from various angles.

If ever there were a definitively good time to invest in a game console like the PlayStation 5, it’d certainly be now, what with the looming release of Grand Theft Auto 6 in just a little over a week. I can’t speak to how many people have gone out and bought PS5s for the express purpose of playing that game, but considering it's the console's best-selling title, I have to imagine it’s more than a few, and coupling those with the players who have already invested in the PS5 since its initial release in 2020, the overall statistics are nothing to sneeze at.

According to newly-released sales statistics from Sony Interactive Entertainment, as of yesterday, November 10, 2026, the PlayStation 5 has officially surpassed 100 million units sold worldwide in its lifespan.

For six years out from launch, that’s pretty good, especially since it’s a stone’s throw from the approximately 102 million-unit record held by the original PlayStation console, though it is a very far cry from the over-160 million PlayStation 2s sold. Hey, it was literally one of the best-selling game consoles of all time, anyone would have trouble beating that.

Rockstar says GTA 6 pre-loading begins November 10 and could require up to 212 GB, though some official pages still show the old date.

According to Sony’s sales statistics, in Q1 and Q2 2026 combined, about 3.2 million PS5 units have been sold. For the record, the console’s all-time quarterly sales record was set in Q3 2024 at 9.5 million, and the all-time yearly record was set in 2023 at 20.8.

Incidentally, these sales statistics are based on “sell-in” units, which means the numbers sold to officially licensed resellers, i.e. regular stores you buy gaming hardware at. The statistics don’t cover the sell-through numbers, which is the number of consoles actually purchased from resellers by end users. Presumably, that number is smaller, but if retailers are ordering lots of PS5s, it means people are buying them in large quantities, so that still says a lot.

In Q1 and Q2 2026 combined, about 3.2 million PS5 units have been sold.

The statistics don’t yet have the sales numbers for consoles in Q3, which lasts from October to December, but I have a hunch that, with the release of Grand Theft Auto 6 this month, those numbers are going to see at least some degree of lurch when they’re finally divulged. The stats sheet also doesn’t cover Q3’s software sales, but you can bet those are going to see a major hike for the same reason.

Take-Two has had an absolutely killer Q2, leading them to feed GTA fever more and more.

Despite how good all of these stats seem on paper, the reality of the situation is that the PS5’s overall sales for this year haven’t been especially hot. Comparing Q1 and Q2 statistics to years’ prior, both are smaller than every year from 2021 to 2025.

Granted, as I said, there is a nonzero chance we’re going to see a major lurch in the Q3 statistics; Q3, being the holiday season, usually sees a big jump every year, with sales doubling their usual rates from 2022 to 2025. With the once-in-a-generation winds of GTA 6 at its back, the PS5 could certainly recoup its failings in Q3 2026, though everyone who already has a PS5 or chooses to play that game elsewhere could hamper that projection.

Putting aside sales in 2020 and 2021, which were obviously hampered due to the COVID pandemic, it does seem like PS5 sales definitively peaked in 2023, and have been on a downswing since, with the primary suspected culprit being the console’s repeated price hikes and the gradual increase in cost of gaming as a hobby in the last couple of years.

Then again, it could just be that the PS5 is coming to the end of its time, and it’s time for the PS6 to take the reins, though in the current climate, it’s anyone’s guess how much that thing will end up costing.

Anyone in the United States of America who wants to get a PS5 for cheap needs to act fast, as the prices of all consoles are going up on August 21.
