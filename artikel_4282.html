Pokémon Pokopia Expansion Pass Part 2: Dazzling Decorations Arrives Next Month With New Accessories And More

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

The Pokémon Company has revealed that part 2 of the Pokémon Pokopia Expansion Pass, Dazzling Decorations, will go live on Nintendo Switch 2 next month. More specifically, the Dazzling Decorations expansion will launch on December 9, bringing all kinds of accessories to dress up your Ditto and match other Pokémon.

To play the Dazzling Decorations DLC, your version of Pokémon Pokopia must be running the free 3.0.0 software update, and you must have completed the important requests, "Raise the environment level!" and "Rebuild the Pokémon Center!" in Withered Wasteland. You must also have completed Drifloon's request, "Find a doll!"

"Look your best by wearing accessories with your Pokémon friends," the expansion's details read. "Meet new friends and add some flair to your surroundings with new furniture and more Pokémon. No new town will be introduced in Part 2."

Dazzling Decorations follows the Bubbly Basin expansion, which went live over the Summer, and precedes the unannounced Part 3 of the Expansion Pass, set to go live sometime in 2027. The Pokémon Company says Part 3 will bring a new town to explore and rebuild to Pokémon Pokopia.
