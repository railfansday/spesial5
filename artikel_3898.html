STRANGER THAN HEAVEN ‘Act 2: Kure’ story trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-14

Publisher SEGA and developer RGG Studio have released the second “Act 2: Kure” story trailer for 50-year action adventure saga STRANGER THAN HEAVEN.

The description reads, “In Kure, Daito swears his loyalty to Iwaki Genzo, and steels himself to commit any atrocities to climb the yakuza ranks. Watch his headlong sprint down a dark path riddled with violence.”

If you missed it, watch the “Act 1: Kokura” story trailer here.

STRANGER THAN HEAVEN is due out for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, and PC via Steam and Microsoft Store on January 15, 2027. It will also be available via Game Pass.
