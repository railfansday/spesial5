Guide: GTA 6: Tenebrae Lux Walkthrough & Puzzle Solutions

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Early on in GTA 6, you'll gain access to the JoySystem, a game console with a handful of games you can play. One of those is Tenebrae Lux, a black and white, first-person puzzle game. In this GTA 6 guide, we have a Tenebrae Lux walkthrough, guiding you through the puzzles to reach the ending.

Tenebrae Lux features randomised elements in some of its puzzles, so your experience may vary from what you see in our images and videos.

On this page: GTA 6: Tenebrae Lux Walkthrough & Puzzle Solutions

Below is our walkthrough of Tenebrae Lux, revealing puzzle solutions and where to find notes.

There are 11 notes to collect in total, but the last two require you to collect the previous nine.

In a single playthrough of Tenebrae Lux, you can find those 9 notes hidden in 17 possible locations. Collecting these 9 notes will then allow you to find the 10th and 11th notes.

After waking up on the island, you must solve at least three grid-based puzzles located on the beach, though there are five to find in total — two of which are necessary if you're looking for every note.

You'll come to a room with several things you must pay attention to in order to solve this next puzzle. You'll need to make some notes!

The goal is to input the correct string of numbers into the telephone on the back wall. However, there is no defined phone number for you to use; it is a randomised solution.

To learn the phone number, you must pass through several incarnations of the same room to discover differences in the environment, one by one.

Upon first entering the room, you'll see the text "DEVELOPED USING ZUCHWORKS ENGINE" followed by a Greek letter:

It could be any of the following letters, and each corresponds to a numerical value which you must make a note of:

From the left-hand side and moving clockwise around the room, you may find the following things to inspect:

Vinyl records: Make a note of the price and the RPM values. Add the digits together, e.g. if the price is 50 cents, 5+0=0. Same goes for the RPM:

Lamp: When you enter the room, it will flash a morse code message. Decipher it using the chart located on the right of the console

Wall clock: Make a note of the time, and work out the clockwise angle from the hour to the minute hand. Add the digits of the angle together for the appropriate number (e.g. 2 o'clock, angle 300°, 3+0+0=3):

Calendar: The dates 6th and 31st are circled. More on the calendar later:

Phone ringing: When you enter the room, the phone will ring a certain number of times — note down how many

Framed picture: Make a note of how many figures you can see in the picture:

Emergency Protocols: Make a note of the number of the document. It may be redacted:

Cassette tape: Make a note of the date stated when you play the tape recorder:

Wall picture: The picture shows Roman numerals depicted by pillars — note the number

Once you've noted all of the above, proceed through the door next to the phone and up the stairs. You will find yourself in a new iteration of the same room.

The idea now is to inspect everything again, including the onscreen text with the Roman letter, to search for an anomaly. When you find a number that is different from your notes from the original room, note it down.

For example: In the original room, the framed picture showed 3 people, but in the next room, the picture shows 4 people. Note down '4'.

Observe the calendar; it will now have many dates scratched out:

The first date that's clear after the 6th October correlates with the anomaly you have discovered.

To begin playing Tenebrae Lux, you must progress enough to unlock the JoySystem console. This occurs in Chapter 1 during the mission Feng Shui.

Once the mission is complete, you will be able to use the JoySystem at any time and begin playing Tenebrae Lux.

Note: During Chapter 2, you will temporarily lose access to the JoySystem, but it will become available again once you start Chapter 3. From there, you will be able to play with no further interruptions.
