AECOM (ACM +11.08%) reported record backlog and margin expansion to conclude its 2026 fiscal year, supported by high win rates and accelerating growth in its design and advisory businesses. Management reported that the company is successfully navigating legacy liabilities while shifting its portfolio toward higher-margin, lower-risk professional services. Guidance for fiscal 2027 anticipates continued top-line growth and record margins as the company capitalizes on global infrastructure investment cycles and favorable funding environments.
