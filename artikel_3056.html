e.l.f. CEO: All of Our Brands Are Growing

Tanggal: 2026-11-05

e.l.f. Beauty CEO Tarang Amin said the company delivered its 31st consecutive quarter of net-sales growth, with sales up 51% and adjusted EBITDA up 86%. He attributed growth across the portfolio to value, product innovation and marketing, noting that every brand grew. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
