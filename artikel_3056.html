Tanggal: 2026-11-25

Full-year guidance reaffirmed despite a revenue decline, with Lyme milestones on the horizon.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Nov 24, 2026 at 5:25PM EST

Valneva (VALN -5.30%) reported a decline in product sales and total revenue for the first nine months of 2026, largely attributed to the delayed award of a critical U.S. Department of Defense contract and a transition in German distribution networks. Despite these top-line pressures, management reconfirmed its full-year guidance, anticipating that imminent contract finalization will allow for significant shipments of the IXIARO vaccine in December. The company is actively executing a restructuring program to reduce operating expenses, which has already resulted in lower research and development and administrative costs. Strategic focus remains on the regulatory progress of the Lyme disease vaccine candidate partnered with Pfizer, with validation of the European submission and a pending update on the U.S. filing status. Furthermore, Valneva is realigning its commercial strategy for IXCHIQ, prioritizing endemic markets following lower-than-anticipated demand in the travel sector.
