UTulsa launches Access to Excellence Scholarship Fund with $4M Asbjornson family commitment

Tanggal: 2026-11-10

TULSA, Okla., Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The University of Tulsa has launched the Access to Excellence Scholarship Fund, a philanthropic initiative to help more students pursue a UTulsa education, with an additional $4 million commitment from the Asbjornson Foundation providing significant early support.

The fund builds on the university's Access to Excellence tuition initiative and its commitment to put an exceptional private education within reach. In August, the university announced it would cap undergraduate tuition and required fees at $25,000 a year in fall 2027, making UTulsa the most affordable private research university in America's heartland.

"This extraordinary investment by the Asbjornson family embodies what Access to Excellence means at a Top 75 private research institution and exemplifies the commitment of the Golden Hurricane community to pay it forward," said President Stacy Leeds. "We are removing financial barriers while preserving everything that makes a University of Tulsa education exceptional. The Asbjornsons understand that unlocking higher education can transform not only a student's future but also the future of Tulsa and our region. We are deeply grateful for their continued leadership."

The Asbjornson Foundation's generous gift will focus on transfer student aid:

"If students are concerned about taking on too much debt, they will be reluctant to pursue rigorous studies and challenging career paths. To ensure Tulsa's economic success, we need Tulsa students to fulfill their potential," said Scott Asbjornson (B.S.B.A. '91, MBA '95). "I hope others in our philanthropic community will join my family in supporting these deserving students."

"Our hope is that this investment removes barriers for talented students and creates opportunities for them to learn, explore and grow in ways that prepare them to lead with purpose," Asbjornson said.
