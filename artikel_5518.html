Four Open Real Estate Founding CEOs, CMLS CEO to Kick Off RISMedia’s 7th Annual Rocking in the New Year

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

From massive consolidation to pre-marketing and private listing policies to MLS lawsuits, to say 2026 was a tumultuous year in real estate is an understatement.

One of the most notable developments in the industry came late in the year–the launch of Open Real Estate (OpenRE), a national data-sharing platform that aims to streamline listing data distribution. Founded by the CEOs of AGNT (parent company of eXp Realty and NextHome), HomeServices of America, Keller Williams and Real REMAX Group, the platform has grown to include four additional participating members and has received letters of support from another 826 firms nationwide ahead of its official launch in 2027.

OpenRE’s founding CEOs–Leo Pareja, Chris Kelly, Chris Czarnecki and Tamir Polig–are set to take the virtual stage at RISMedia’s 7th Annual Real Estate’s Rocking in the New Year, to discuss the vision behind their ambitious collaboration and detail why the pledge to keep housing markets open, transparent and accessible is especially critical right now.

The all-day virtual event, titled “Own Your Success in 2027,” will take place on Thursday, Jan. 7 from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. EST. Registration is open and available here. The event is free for RISMedia Premier members.

The conversation will also explore the importance of data distribution, consumer choice and complementary rulemaking amid a “deteriorating” public marketplace.

Keynote: Buckle Up! The Road Ahead in 2027

Immediately following the kickoff session, Jessie Edgerton, CEO of the Council of Multiple Listing Services (CMLS), will share her views on the Open Real Estate discussion and deliver this year’s keynote address, “Buckle Up! The Road Ahead in 2027. The keynote theme centers around the industry’s rapidly evolving landscape, with legal challenges, regulatory changes and emerging issues poised to shape the year ahead. Edgerton will talk about what may be coming around the corner, and which developments could have the greatest impact on the business of real estate.

RISMedia’s 7th Annual Rocking in the New Year virtual conference brings together the industry’s brightest minds, freshest ideas and most innovative strategies to help you take control, win more business and carve your own path to success in 2027. This year 50 industry leaders will be speaking in 15 sessions over a range of timely topics. Here are just some of the sessions on deck:

–Inside the Situation Room: How Brokers Are Strategizing for 2027 with Wendy Forsythe, Chief Operating Officer, eXp Realty; and Michele Harrington, CEO, First Team Real Estate

–Economic Outlook 2027: The Potential Paths for the Economy and How to Prepare with Dr. Lisa Sturtevant, chief economist, Bright MLS

–Building the AI Coach: How Agentic AI Is Changing Real Estate with Mel Robbins, digital marketing manager, Northrop Realty; Creig Northrop, CEO and founder, Northrop Realty; Todd Denman, founder and principal Realtor®, eXp Realty-The Denman Group; and Marki Lemons Ryhal, chief artificial intelligence officer (CAIO), ReMarkiTable, LLC

–Surviving—and Thriving—Amid Consolidation with Christina Pappas, president, The Keyes Company/Illustrated Properties; Dean deTonnancourt, broker/owner and CEO, REMAX Revolution; and Sandra Rathe, broker associate, speaker, coach, author, The Sandra Rathe Team, Real Broker, LLC

The market remains unpredictable, but opportunity is still out there. Attendees will gain actionable takeaways to strengthen their business, including strategies for improving your value proposition, maximizing AI, revamping your marketing, forging connections with today’s buyers and sellers and much more.
