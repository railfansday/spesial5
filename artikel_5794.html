Tesla Robotaxis Extend into San Antonio

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Tesla’s nascent robotaxi service has extended into San Antonio, to some extent.

“Tesla launched its robotaxi service Monday in San Antonio,” Axios reports. “Robotaxi available in San Antonio starting tomorrow, 5am,” the Tesla Robotaxi account tweeted on Monday. The Tesla robotaxi webpage also now shows robotaxis as being active in Alamo City. It’s not clear, however, how many vehicles are in operation there.

“The Tesla service area covers downtown and nearby areas, extending to The Shops at La Cantera and Six Flags Fiesta Texas on the Northwest Side.” Here’s a graphic of the service area:

Tesla’s service doesn’t current go to San Antonio International Airport, whereas Waymo’s robotaxi service does service the airport.
