Some closing thoughts on Dolphins at Bills and looking ahead for Buffalo

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Last week instead of my usual hard analysis post game I did more waxing poetic musings. Well, y'all seemed to respond to it and it's a short week and one that involved holiday prep so I'm back at it again. I'll be diving into opinion here so feel free to tell me how much they suck in the comments.

I did do some traditional analysis on our opponent, desperately looking for something nice to say about the Miami Dolphins to maintain the decorum and charm I'm confident I'm known for. I came away with an article about how their interception rate suggested they had an actual knack for taking the ball away. The film seemed to back it up. Here's a direct quote from that article:

If the Dolphins manage an interception, heck maybe even two, don't be too shocked. It just isn't likely to help them all that much.

Sometimes I look like a genius. But I'm also a numbers guy. By the end of the season, I believe my article count will be right around 2,000. Once in awhile a good guess is going to end up being a perfect guess. Now you might take the side that it maybe it did help the Dolphins, but my stance was that it wasn't likely to help them "all that much."

Even if you assume the best case scenario and Buffalo scores touchdowns on both those drives, is there really any real difference between a three-score game and a four-score game? Sure, that pushes it near a five-score difference, but even then you're looking at basically the same result. Miami never had a shot and the interceptions were like the pre-snap penalties, irritating in that we know there'll be better teams ahead and those mistakes might be more significant then.

Yeah, yeah, yeah. We all know the mantra. "One game at a time." Or how about "We don't look past anyone?" Or maybe even "[insert cliche about not taking someone lightly]." That said, when you have games on a short week I simply don't know how you can't start some of the game planning for the Thursday game ahead of time. Also, it's not disrespectful to Miami to acknowledge that a simplified gameplan is still going to result in a win.

You might also do some things to keep Josh Allen safe. Watching the game I think we all saw that he wasn't running. Speaking for just me though? I was startled to see a goose egg in the rushing attempts column. When's the last time THAT has happened? Technically the answer would be Week 18 last year when he handed the ball off once in the season finale to keep his starting streak alive but that doesn't really count. Has it ever happened? Zero carries!

Head coach Joe Brady says it wasn't intentional. I might buy that zero wasn't intentional, but "extraordinarily low attempts" had to have been part of the plan, right? Yeah, it had to have been. Kansas City defensive coordinator Steve Spagnuolo referred to it as "a setup." Can't really disagree with him there.

Spags is therefore going to prepare for Allen to use his legs, thus proving he's not a dumb***. As a tendency-breaker then it seems to have failed. On the other hand, it's basically a bye week for Allen's legs as the prepare to face a team that's only one-win behind them in the standings. I don't hate it.

I fully intend on doing an opponent preview for the game, but if that doesn't pan out here's the quick version of things using some of my favorite rate stats. The Bills slipped a bit on the points per drive metric by going "easy" on the Dolphins, but they still sit fourth in the league there. Top four is considered elite. They score on over 50% of their drives and are second behind the *checks notes* Dallas Cowboys? That. Doesn't sound right.

That offense will square up with an equally elite Kansas City defense that allows the third fewest points per drive in the league and has the fourth lowest rate of allowing scoring drives. That's about a draw between the two teams.

It's on the other side of things that maybe we want to add a little worry. The Bills' defense is 12th in the league with points per drive and eleventh in scoring drive. That average result is heavily dictated by the last few games against lesser competition. There's been real signs of progression for Buffalo but it's been over a month since they were really tested.

They'll take on a Kansas City team that's eight in the league ("good" category) and eighth in scoring drive percent. That basically puts "average" against "good" which isn't too bad on paper, but there should be some real worry that the defensive renaissance is a bit of a mirage.

All that said, people heckling the Bills for the breaks they've had playing against lousy QBs for a month should take an honest look at Kansas City's path. They're winning and deserve credit, but they've had a pretty weak list of opponents so far. I won't guarantee a win, but in no way does this game scare me.

Our view of things becomes clearer every week. The Patriots haven't fallen off the map yet, but the map is currently on a table with a rambunctious cat on it. It's only a matter of time before a swat and gravity do their thing.

I was hoping the Raiders would pull it out vs. the Broncos but alas that didn't happen. That's alright, we can knock 'em down a peg on Christmas and I feel decently confident on that one. More so than I do against KC and as noted that one isn't scary. If we drop that one though, call me worried. The Broncos don't remotely have the gauntlet we do the rest of the season.

Kansas City I've already covered but want to point out they also have a brutal close to the season. Mahomes and Reid have been fantastic but it's hard not to see another loss or two in there somewhere, meaning the Broncos might actually be the bigger threat to the one seed.

The Jacksonville Jaguars lost to the New York Giants. That puts them two games back now with a couple tests still to come. The upset loss to the Giants definitely was a nice bonus for us Bills fans this week.

Last but not least is the Cincinnati Bengals. They're currently only one game behind us and right now they're about to enter halftime against the Washington Commanders as I'm typing this. If the NFC East does us another favor this week (Philly beat Pittsburgh too while we're on the subject) it could become really interesting for our fandom when they play Kansas City. They also play the Cleveland Browns twice still and you know they'll be playing hard to stay in the mix. I think we're in good shape to outpace the Bengals, but let's just say I'll keep rooting for the Commanders tonight.

As Washington gets it's first points on the board tonight on a rainy Sunday night, it's good to be a Bills fan.
