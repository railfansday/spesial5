‘Nobody was angry’ – Deco insists no problems between Barcelona and Frenkie de Jong over knee injury

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Barcelona sporting director Deco has denied there was any conflict between the club and Frenkie de Jong over his recent knee injury.

De Jong sustained the problem during the World Cup with the Netherlands and opted for conservative treatment rather than go under the knife.

Reports at the time claimed Barca favored surgery and weren't too happy with the Dutchman but Deco says that was not the case.

"Nobody was angry with him. Frenkie had previously suffered an ankle injury, and he's a very intelligent player who likes to think things through. With that ankle injury, there were two options: conservative treatment or surgery," he told Tot Costa.

"He chose not to have surgery, and he did the same with his knee. These decisions are made by mutual agreement, with input from Raúl Martínez and Dr. Pruna, but the club doesn't impose anything. It's a health issue. Frenkie likes to think things through and make his own decisions. Nobody was angry with him."

De Jong has since returned to action for Barcelona and is now hoping to regain his place in the starting XI at Barca.
