Full practice for Luther Burden III

Tanggal: 2026-11-18

Chicago Bears WR Luther Burden III (concussion) fully participated in practice Wednesday, Nov. 18. The Bears did not hold a practice Wednesday, so this is an estimation.

Why It Matters: Burden should be available to play in Week 11 as long as he can pass his concussion tests before the game. He has posted at least 95 yards in three of his last four games and has a touchdown in each of his last three games. He can be considered a No. 2 receiver in fantasy leagues.
