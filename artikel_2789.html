Jusuf Nurkic injury update released

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Utah Jazz released an injury update on Jusuf Nurkic today. It looks like Nurkic was re-evaluated, is making progress, and will be re-evaluated again in one week.

It would have been nice to hear that Nurkic was returning, so this doesn't feel like great news. Although, there aren't any setbacks so it looks like this also isn't bad news either.

What Jazz fans can be happy about is the emergence of Mo Bamba as a legitimate force inside for Utah in his last five or so games. Bamba has been legitimately great and a big part of the Jazz staying close against some of the better teams and winning their last two. With the Jazz getting this level of play from Bamba, it's allowing them to let Nurkic heal without much heartburn.

Also, this is not a bad thing because it gives Bamba a chance to prove if all of this is real. Utah may be fine with Nurkic off the bench when he eventually returns because Mo Bamba has been that good. What a difference a week makes in the NBA. One week it feels like the sky is falling, the next you're wondering if Mo Bamba migh win comeback player of the year.
