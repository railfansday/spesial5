Ravens defense: One thing to watch at each position against Chargers

Tanggal: 2026-11-15

Baltimore's defensive performance against the Chargers will depend on how its different groups fit together, particularly when Justin Herbert extends a play or Mike McDaniel changes the formation. The Ravens have Kyle Hamilton and Nate Wiggins available, but Jaylinn Hawkins remains out. That leaves Baltimore with important contributors returning while it continues adjusting responsibilities in the secondary.

These five observations can help explain whether the defense is operating more consistently Monday night.

Watch Nnamdi Madubuike, Travis Jones and Calais Campbell against a Chargers interior featuring Jake Slaughter and Cole Strange, with Kayode Awosika listed as questionable.

Interior pressure does not always produce the sack. It can prevent Herbert from stepping away from an edge rusher or force him to change his throwing platform. Baltimore needs those contributions to appear alongside outside pressure.

Green and Hendrickson must distinguish between reaching the quarterback and creating an escape route behind their rush. Herbert can turn a defender's momentum against him if the pocket opens.

Watch whether the opposite edge remains controlled when one rusher gets close. A disciplined finish can force a throwaway; an uncontrolled pursuit can give Johnston or another receiver time to work free.

Smith led Baltimore with eight tackles against Jacksonville, while Simpson added six. Against Los Angeles, the quality of those stops matters as much as the total.

Hampton, Vidal and Mitchell offer different challenges once they reach the second level. Watch whether Smith and Simpson stop forward progress immediately or need help after allowing additional yards, particularly on short receptions.

Humphrey, Wiggins and Awuzie must communicate when receivers change sides or enter traffic. The Chargers can use Johnston's downfield presence alongside shorter routes to make those responsibilities difficult.

The key observation is whether Baltimore follows the call without two defenders chasing the same receiver. McConkey's availability could influence how often Los Angeles tests those exchanges through its receiving alignments.

Hamilton and Malaki Starks must balance their involvement near the line with responsibility deeper downfield. Hawkins' absence also makes the roles of Keondre Jackson and K'Von Wallace worth monitoring.

Watch the safety's position when Herbert begins his throwing motion. If the defenders bite on an underneath route or run action, Baltimore's cornerbacks may have little help against vertical deep throws. Correct positioning can remove that attempt before it becomes an interception opportunity.
