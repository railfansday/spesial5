Check out the Brazil women's team's schedule for friendlies in Japan

Tanggal: 2026-11-22

The Brazil Women's National Team will wrap up their 2026 schedule with two friendlies in Japan against the hosts. The matches are part of their preparations for the FIFA Women's World Cup 2027, which will be held in Brazil.

The first match will take place on November 29 at Edion Peace Wing Stadium in Hiroshima, at 1:55 a.m. (Brasília time). The second match will be on December 5 at JFE Harenokuni Stadium in Okayama, at 3 a.m. (Brasília time).

The match in Hiroshima will mark Arthur Elias's 50th game in charge of the Brazil National Team. Alongside the United States, Japan is the opponent the coach has faced most often since taking over the team, with six matches against each side.

Against Japan, Brazil have recorded three wins, one draw and two defeats under Arthur. This will be the coach's seventh match against Japan and his first on Japanese soil.

Brazil and Japan met in two friendlies in São Paulo in 2025. The Amarelinha won both matches.Credits: Lívia Villas Boas / CBF

Brazil and Japan have met twice on Japanese soil, with the hosts winning both encounters. The first took place in September 2007, when Japan won 2–1 in a friendly at Fukuda Denshi Arena in Chiba.

In April 2012, in the Kirin Challenge Cup, Japan won again, this time 4–1 at Kobe Wing Stadium in Kobe. The friendly in Hiroshima will therefore be the teams' third meeting on Japanese soil.

Overall, the teams have faced each other 17 times, and the Amarelinha hold the edge: eight wins, two draws and seven defeats. Brazil have scored 23 goals and conceded 26.

The team will be based in Hiroshima for most of their preparations for the friendlies, with on-field training beginning on Wednesday (25). The first 15 minutes of the sessions will be open to the press and streamed live on the CBF's YouTube channel.

During the first week, press conferences will be held on the evenings of November 25 and 26 at the team hotel and will be streamed live. There will also be a press conference on November 28 at Edion Peace Wing Stadium. Times, along with details on how the Brazilian press can follow the interviews, will be announced soon.
