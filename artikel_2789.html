Caleb Williams: I could throw 100 picks and I wouldn't get gun-shy

Tanggal: 2026-11-09

Bears quarterback Caleb Williams threw four interceptions on Sunday night against the Buccaneers, but he wasn’t hesitant to keep throwing downfield at the end of the game, when he led the Bears into range for the game-winning field goal.

Williams hit a wide-open Colston Loveland for a 38-yard gain in the final minute of the game to set up a field goal as time expired on the Bears’ 27-24 win, and Williams said there was no hesitation to pull the trigger, despite his four interceptions. And he said he would have had no hesitation to pull the trigger even if Loveland hadn’t been wide open.

“I’m gonna throw until I can’t throw anymore. That’s how I live,” Williams said. “I work my tail off to be in this position. I’ll never not throw the ball. I could throw 100 picks in one game, if it comes down to it I’m gonna throw that pass that I threw to Colston, or if it’s a tighter window I’m gonna rip it in there. That’s how I grew up, that’s what I am. I don’t get gun-shy. That’s not me. That’s not my identity, not who I am. I’m gonna keep ripping it, keep ripping it.”

Williams admitted that he needs to find ways to get better, especially when the defense is blitzing him, but after the game he didn’t sound particularly concerned about the four interceptions. He’ll keep throwing confidently no matter how many get intercepted.
