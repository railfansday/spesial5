San Antonio at Portland, Final Score: the Spurs lose their first road game 116-111 to the Blazers

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

The Spurs hadn't lost a road game yet this season, but that came to an end tonight as Damian Lillard with 29 points and Deni Avidja with 33 rallied Rip City to a 5 point win in a game that was a back and forth contest all night. Former Spur Sidy Cissoko had 16 points and made some key plays down the stretch as Victor Wembanyama's 44 points weren't enough to get the Silver and Black to a win. The Spurs shot poorly from three, but the bigger problem was lack of attention to detail, as some critical mistakes and missed free throws down the stretch were the deciding factor, as the Blazers thrilled the home crowd and pulled out a big NBA Cup win. The Spurs will have to win their game on Friday to have a shot at advancing the the knockout rounds in the Cup tournament. They have a pretty solid point differential, so even if they don't win the group, they could make it into the tournament as a wildcard. But first they have to win on Friday. Go Spurs, BEAT LA!

The lead-in game was Thunder vs Timberwolves, and the Wolves won 112-106, but mostly I was distracted by Chet Holmgren wearing a fur coat on the bench.

Dame was pretty much held in control in the first meeting, but tonight, he started out hot, scoring the first 7 for the Blazers. When he hit a logo triple at about four minutes in, Mitch had to call a quick TO. Wemby sat after the TO, taking a very short first rotation of just 4 minutes.

The Spurs bench did a decent job of holding back the Blazers until Wemby returned to the lineup with 3 minutes left in the quarter.

The Spurs attempted 15 free throws in the first quarter to 10 for Portland, which kept them in the game, as they trailed 35-38 after the first. The bench squad held up pretty well, with Jordan McLaughlin having some good minutes showing his quickness and ability to get to the rim.

Ja'Kobi Gillespie got into the game at the start of the second quarter, which is a good sign that he's already gaining Mitch Johnson's trust as a viable backup.

Advija is pretty adept at using his off arm to get around defenders. It's an offensive foul, but I guess if nobody is going to call it, it's a good move.

In their first meeting on November 7, the Spurs held Deni Avdija to 15 points. He scored 18 in the first half tonight. They held Lillard to 12, and he had an 18 point first half. This was going to be a tougher go for the Silver and Black, but they only trailed by three at the half, with the score at 60 Blazers, 57 Spurs.

The Blazers tried to single cover Wembanyama with Clingan. It didn't work out well for them.

How many time have we seen Wembanyama turn an ankle, and somehow be just fine after going to the floor for a minute or two. His physical resilience is superhuman.

The Spurs kept pace for most of the third quarter but a stretch of wacky ball about two thirds through the frame let the Blazers get some easy buckets as they took an 82-77 lead. Mitch called a timeout, and the Spurs put Wemby back in the game leading to a 7-0 Spurs run as they retook the lead. After a couple of Dame free throws, a Devin Vassell buzzer beater at the end of the quarter gave the Spur an 87-84 lead going into the fourth.

The Blazers had an offensive explosion to start the fourth quarter, with Dame hitting a deep three over Wembanyama and Sidy Cissoko making an impressive dunk on a drive to the bucket, and the home team was suddenly up 96-89, their biggest lead of the game.

Victor can't play the whole game, and the bench had to take a turn, but they actually held their own, and by the time that Wemanyama came in at 5:43, the Blazer lead was only 3 points, the bench had done their part, and it was Wemby's job to bring it home.

Clingan fouled out with 4 minutes and earned a tech for complaining about it. Sadly, the Silver and Black could not cash the free throw, and with a lane violation of one of Wemby's free throws, they left two points wanting, which could be costly.

The Blazers caused an 8 second call on Castle bringing up the ball, and after a few empty trips, a crazy banking three point shot from Avdija gave Portland a 5 point lead with under a minute left. After another empty trip by the Silver and Black, the Blazers had the ball and called timeout. They just had to get the ball in bounds and they had it in the bag, unless they turn it over on the inbounds play, which they did. The Spurs cut the lead to one score with 17 seconds left, but the Blazers were done giving gifts to the Spurs, and they closed it out with free throws, winning 116-111.

The Spurs return home on Friday night to face the Lakers with a chance advance to the knockout rounds of the Emirates Cup, and earn an extra payday for the players, along with getting more experience with being in a playoff-like atmosphere.
