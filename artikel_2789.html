Week 10 lineup questions? Fantasy Football Live has you covered on Sunday morning with last-minute start/sit advice

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Week 10 of the 2026 NFL season is here — and there's no better place to get last-minute start-sit advice this Sunday than Fantasy Football Live!

Four of the most trusted names in fantasy will be answering your lineup questions:

Justin Boone: A two-time winner of the FantasyPros Most Accurate Expert Award (2019, 2025), he has nine top-10 finishes in the competition.

Josh Norris: One of fantasy's most trusted voices, backed by data-driven analysis and film study, he has produced the most accurate mock draft in NFL Draft history.

Hayden Winks: A three-time FSWA and Webby Award winner, he's built a loyal following for his advanced statistics work and film study.
