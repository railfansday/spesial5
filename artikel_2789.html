Broncos vs. Raiders practice participation report: Wednesday

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

The bye week for the Denver Broncos came at the perfect time. They've been dealing with some nagging injuries over the past month and it seems everyone is getting back into the mix now after the bye.

The biggest return has to be safety Talanoa Hufanga. He was having a first-team all-pro type season before suffering that ankle injury and it will be good to see him back out there. Cornerback Kris Abrams-Draine and safety Brandon Jones both got back out there in a limited fashion. The only question marks come from those three players still in the concussion protocol. It's good they are all full participants, but those head injuries can be tricky sometimes. Safety first.

For the Las Vegas Raiders, the major question mark for them is with tight end Brock Bowers. He suffered any injury last week, came back in briefly, but was ultimately sidelined from there. His status will bear monitoring given how much Denver's defense has struggled against pass catching tight ends this season.
