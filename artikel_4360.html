BYD is bringing its $10,000 ‘Great’ Seagull overseas, but it won’t replace the Dolphin Surf

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

BYD’s new, larger entry-level EV will go on sale in Europe, but it won’t be called the Dolphin Surf as expected.

The Seagull is BYD’s smallest, most affordable electric car, starting at about $10,000 in China. In Europe and the UK, it’s sold as the Dolphin Surf, and in other overseas markets, it’s called the Dolphin Mini.

With over 245,000 Seagull (Dolphin Mini/ Dolphin Surf) models sold through September, it’s BYD’s most popular pure electric vehicle and among the top-selling EVs worldwide so far this year.

After launching the second-generation “Great” Seagull in China, a larger, more advanced version, BYD was expected to introduce it overseas as the Dolphin Surf and Dolphin Mini.

Apparently, that will not be the case. BYD will introduce the updated version overseas, but according to the Spanish media outlet TestCoches, it will replace the larger Dolphin hatch rather than the Dolphin Surf.

The second-generation model will offer BYD’s five-minute Flash Charging technology and Blade Batteries 2.0.

In China, the new Seagull is available in five variants, priced from 65,900 yuan to 99,900 yuan ($9,900 to $15,000). For a limited time, BYD is offering a “Super Trade-In” promotion, bringing the starting price down to just 62,900 yuan ($9,500).

The second-gen Seagull measures 4,205 mm long, 1,810 mm wide, and 1,570 mm high, with a 2,650 mm wheelbase. That’s 425 mm longer and 150 mm longer in wheelbase than its predecessor.

Thanks to the added space, BYD’s new Seagull now offers room for five passengers, up from four in the outgoing version.

Instead of a front-wheel-drive powertrain, the new version switches to rear-wheel-drive. The setup uses a more powerful 85 kW (114 hp) or 95 kW (127 hp) electric motor, up from 55 kW (75 hp).

With BYD’s Flash Charging tech, the battery can charge from 10% to 70% in five minutes. To go from 10% to 97% takes just nine minutes.

Even in temperatures as low as -30°C, it takes just 3 minutes longer (12 minutes) to charge from 20% to 97%. However, Flash Charging is not included as standard on the two cheapest entry-level variants.

While the bigger dimensions and Flash Charging were the biggest updates, BYD also added a few premium extras.

To improve the ride quality and handling, BYD upgraded the rear suspension to a multi-link independent system.

Inside, BYD revamped the interior with its latest in-car software and technology. The new setup includes a larger 15.6″ center infotainment screen, a redesigned two-spoke steering wheel and center console with smart storage, plus added physical buttons.

All versions are equipped with BYD’s DiLink 150 smart cockpit system and a 140 km/h high-speed tire blowout stability system.

The optional DiPilot 300 system with God’s Eye B intelligent driving assistance is available. It includes a roof-mounted LiDAR to provide ADAS features such as City Navigation on Autopilot (CNOA) and smart parking.

In China, BYD offers the new Seagull EV with two Blade 2.0 LFP battery packs: 30.1 kWh and 39.2 kWh. The former provides a CLTC range of up to 320 km (199 miles), while the latter provides up to 420 km (261 miles).

The current Dolphin Surf measures 3,990 mm in length, and the Dolphin is 4,290 mm long. Selling the updated version as the Dolphin makes more sense, given how much closer in size it is.

BYD will continue selling the Dolphin Surf in Spain and throughout Europe, with the updated model now featuring five seats and a few other key improvements.

In Europe, BYD’s Dolphin Surf starts at about €22,990 ($25,000), and the larger Dolphin is priced from around €33,990 ($37,500).
