European eels transmit a herpesvirus through bites, revealing another route for fish infections

Tanggal: 2026-11-11

Contrary to what was previously believed, viruses infecting fish are not transmitted exclusively through water acting as an abiotic vector. A team of researchers from the University of Liège has demonstrated that a virus affecting European eels can spread very efficiently through biting during aggressive interactions between individuals.
