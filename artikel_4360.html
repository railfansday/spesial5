UnGodly shows off bloody good barbarian action in its new gameplay trailer

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Of all the upcoming releases we've covered, one that's kept my curiosity is UnGodly, the upcoming hack 'n slash RPG from Azra Games and Mark Otero, one of the key figures behind Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes. And in its most recent gameplay trailer, we not only got the announcement that it would be coming to iPhone Duo, but also a good look at its gameplay!

It's easy to see why UnGodly is so consistently described as Frazetta-inspired, because everyone lacks a shirt, or at the very least seems to lack the buttons for them. But the action, as simple as it may seem, also benefits from some truly bloody gore effects as your heroes blast apart hapless mooks with everything from simple sword slashes to magic spells.

And the actual support for platforms is surprisingly robust here, with both the Vision Pro and iPhone Duo supported, including spatial elements for the Vision Pro.

Not only that, but as previously announced, UnGodly is also set to introduce Conan the Barbarian as post-launch collaboration content. You certainly can't get more classic sword & sorcery than the original barbarian himself. So keep your eyes peeled for when UnGodly drops on November 11th for iOS and Android!
