Mauricio Pochettino Set to Break Premier League Rule at Man Utd

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Even before Michael Carrick was out the door, it was as good as confirmed that Mauricio Pochettino would take over from him as Manchester United manager. The Argentine has now given his first interview since arriving at Old Trafford.

The now-former United States head coach arrives with plenty of work to do. Man Utd currently sit in the relegation zone, 18th in the Premier League, with just one win in 10 games.

The Pochettino era will begin with a tricky first fixture as well, with the Red Devils travelling to take on Liverpool away at Anfield on Sunday. The 54-year-old only met the squad on Wednesday, taking a tour of the training ground before later fulfilling club media obligations that evening, with attention already turning to how he could improve Man Utd. The following day, he took his first training session with the team.

The 54-year-old is returning to club football after two years in the USA.

the Red Devils have 'reached an agreement' with the Premier League

Despite having only just arrived in Manchester, Pochettino will instantly break a Premier League rule. However, he will do it with official approval.

As per the Express, the Red Devils have 'reached an agreement' with the Premier League to break the top flight's rules concerning the new manager's press conference on Friday. The division's regulations stipulate that pre-match press conferences must take place by 1.30pm on Friday at the very latest.

Pochettino, though, will be allowed to push that media appearance back by 30 minutes, starting at 2pm instead. This change has been made to accommodate his busy week.

As recently as Wednesday morning (UK time), the Argentine was still overseeing the USMNT, who are now searching for their next head coach. He presided over his final fixture – a 2-0 win over Haiti – before boarding a private aircraft to Manchester. He arrived later that morning and travelled straight to Carrington.

In his first interview, he told club media: "I'm very excited to be there, it's an honour, it's a privilege to be in this amazing club. Yes, I'm looking forward to start to work,"

He added: "I have plenty of energy. It's a privilege to be head coach of Manchester United. And we are not wasting time. We need to be ready to compete on Sunday.

"Now it's not time to think about rest, it's not time to think about sleep. It's about being ready and to prepare everything to be sure that we are going to compete in the best condition at Anfield."

Beyond breaking this minor Premier League rule, Pochettino is also set to create Man Utd history with an ‘unprecedented’ appointment in his coaching staff.

The Argentine will bring along five coaches who will make up his backroom staff, including Jesus Perez, Miguel D’Agostino, Toni Jimenez and Sebastiano Pochettino – who is the son of Mauricio and a sports science specialist.

There will also reportedly be a historic appointment, with Silvia Tuya set to join Pochettino’s team and become the first female to work as part of the club's senior backroom staff. She is set to follow the Argentine to England, having been part of his USMNT staff since 2024.

Tuya, 46, holds a doctorate in sports science and has lectured at the National Institute of Physical Education of Catalonia and at the University of Barcelona, focusing on physical preparation. Previously, she was in charge of physical preparation for Levante’s women’s team, who play in Liga F in Spain.
