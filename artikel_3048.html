Raiders injury news: Darien Porter is questionable

Tanggal: 2026-11-06

Las Vegas Raiders starting cornerback Darien Porter is questionable to play Sunday at the San Francisco 49ers, coach Klint Kubiak said Friday.

Porter injured his groin on the first defensive play of the Raiders' 38-33 victory at the New York Jets on Sunday. He didn't practice Wednesday, but did work on Thursday and Friday. He was a full participamt Friday, so he should be ready to play.

If Porter can't play Sunday, kickoff is 1:25 p.m. PT, rookie draft picks Jermod McCoy and Zeke Masses will rotate as they did in Week 8.

Meanwhile, veteran backup defensive tackle Thomas Booker is out with a foot injury. He didn't practice Thursday or Friday. The absence of Booker won't change the Raiders' rotation at defensive tackle much against San Francisco.

There aren't any other Raiders' players on the injury report Friday.

The 49ers have ruled out wide receivers Mike Evans (Achilles), Deebo Samuel (hamstring) and Christian Kirk (knee). Former Raiders' cornerback Nate Hobbs (Adductor) is questionable to play.
