Pistons vs Raptors Discussion: Game Time, TV, Odds, and More

Tanggal: 2026-11-21

The Toronto Raptors are scuffling in the first leg of the NBA season, but there is certainly a lot of talent there. Yes, Kawhi Leonard is absent with news of managing knee pain and swelling that surely puts a shiver up the spine of every Toronto fan. But they also still have Scottie Barnes, and he is delivering the young star goods as advertised. He is averaging 23.8 points, 7.4 rebounds, and 4.7 assists a night. On certain nights, he looks like the young superstar that represents his clearly attainable ceiling. On other nights, he and his team's offense struggle and can't seem to get unstuck from the mud. He's doing a lot of things, but he isn't doing it as efficiently or consistently as you'd like.

The real problems with the Raptors, though, lie elsewhere. Nobody can seemingly hit a three. RJ Barrett is getting to the line five times a game and hitting just 60% from the charity stripe. Collin Murray-Boyles has regressed, and Jakob Poeltl's game has fallen off a cliff. It feels like if you solve Scottie, it helps you solve Barrett and Immanuel Quickley, and therefore, you solve the Raptors. Toronto has dropped six straight, and will be without the services of Barrett tonight.

Watching an Ausar Thompson and Scottie Barnes matchup could be electric. Barnes doesn't have the defensive chops Ausar does, and Ausar doesn't have the offensive versatility, but both could be liable to pull off a triple-double tonight. Cade Cunningham is also expected back tonight after sitting out Detroit's win against the New Orleans Pelicans. Duncan Robinson and Ron Holland are out, and doubtful, respectively.
