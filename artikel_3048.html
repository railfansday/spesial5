Crypto Market Today, Nov. 4: Bitcoin Stages Sharp Recovery as Wall Street Rallies and Regulatory Hopes Return

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Bitcoin reclaimed $80,000 as cryptocurrencies rallied alongside Wall Street following the midterm elections, with Ethereum leading gains among major digital assets.

As of early evening on Nov. 4, Bitcoin (BTC +3.72%) rose 3.64% to $80,156.15, Ethereum (ETH +6.12%) rose 6.58% to $2,421.50, and Solana (SOL +2.63%) rose 2.19% to $94.94.

Cryptocurrency markets staged a broad recovery on Wednesday as the conclusion of the U.S. midterm elections helped ease political uncertainty that had weighed on risk assets in recent weeks. Bitcoin, which had fallen as low as $75,700 amid sustained selling pressure, rebounded above $80,000, while Ethereum led gains among major cryptocurrencies, climbing more than 6% to a two-week high above $2,400.

The recovery coincided with a broader rally across U.S. equities, as investors responded positively to the election results and the prospect of greater policy stability. Declining oil prices and easing Treasury yields provided additional support for risk appetite, helping lift cryptocurrencies alongside traditional financial markets.

Among key crypto funds, iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF (IBIT +2.91%) rose 2.91%, Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC +2.84%) rose 2.87%, and iShares Ethereum Trust ETF (ETHA +5.72%) rose 5.72%.

The rebound follows an extended period of weakness that resulted in more than $1 billion of outflows from spot Bitcoin ETFs. Whether Wednesday's gains mark the beginning of a sustained recovery will likely depend on improving ETF flows.

In the wake of the Democratic sweep of Congress, crypto investors should be watching for a potential shift in the regulatory landscape. Democratic lawmakers are reportedly considering revisions to the stalled CLARITY Act that could expand the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission's (SEC) authority, strengthen investor protections, and impose additional restrictions on government officials' cryptocurrency activities. Although discussions remain preliminary, renewed legislative momentum could help resolve regulatory uncertainty that has weighed on the industry for months and establish a clearer framework heading into 2027.

In the near term, investors will be looking for confirmation that Wednesday's rebound has staying power. Bitcoin holding above the psychologically important $80,000 level and reclaiming $82,000 would be encouraging signs of renewed buying momentum.

Ultimately, the combination of regulatory developments and reclamation of key levels could provide a foundation for a more sustained recovery. However, macroeconomic uncertainty, shifting interest rate expectations, and lingering weakness across the broader cryptocurrency market suggest investors should remain prepared for continued volatility.
