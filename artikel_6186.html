World Of Hyatt Brand Explorer: Free Night Awards For Staying At New Brands

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

There are several ways to earn World of Hyatt free night award awards. In this post, I’d like to talk about one of the often overlooked ways to earn Hyatt free nights, which is with World of Hyatt’s ongoing Brand Explorer promotion.

This offers World of Hyatt members the opportunity to earn free night awards for exploring Hyatt’s portfolio. Given the huge brand inflation that Hyatt has seen in recent years, this can potentially offer up to seven free night awards, and counting.

With World of Hyatt’s Brand Explorer promotion, you can earn a Category 1-4 free night certificate for every five unique Hyatt brands you stay at. When it comes to earning Hyatt Brand Explorer free night awards, here’s what you should know:

If you want to see how many Hyatt brands you’ve stayed at, you can do so either through the World of Hyatt website or mobile app. Just go to the “Brand Explorer” section, and there will be a display showing how many unique brands you’ve stayed at (including which ones), and also how many free night certificates you’ve earned.

When it comes to redeeming Hyatt Brand Explorer free night awards, here’s what you should know:

Of course you’ll maximize value with Brand Explorer by redeeming your free night award at an eligible Hyatt property when the cash rate would be as high as possible. World of Hyatt Category 4 properties can cost as much as 25,000 points per night, and in some situations, could retail for over $300 per night. So if you can find a hotel anywhere in that range, it’s a great way to maximize value.

Hyatt currently has 38 brands, which are as follows:

So as you can see, you’d need to stay at 35 brands to earn seven free night awards. If/when Hyatt adds two more brands, it’ll be possible to earn eight free night awards.

The World of Hyatt Brand Explorer offer is a fun way for the program to engage members and try to get them to try more brands. At least in one case it impacted where I chose to stay, because I do love checking this dashboard every so often, both to see the progress that I’m making, and be reminded of all the brands I’ve stayed at.

Even though I’ve had top tier status with World of Hyatt for over a decade and have now earned lifetime Globalist status, I haven’t even stayed at half of Hyatt’s current brand portfolio. As a matter of fact, I’ve only stayed at 13 of the 38 brands, so that’s just over one-third of properties.

Looking at the Hyatt brands I haven’t stayed at, most of them are all-inclusives, as that’s an area where Hyatt is growing significantly. However, in general this has limited appeal to me, based on the type of travel that I do. I’m sure I’ll knock out a few more brands in the coming years — maybe I’ll get to 15 brands, though I doubt I’ll make it to 20 brands in the foreseeable future.

With World of Hyatt’s ongoing Brand Explorer promotion, you can earn a free Category 1-4 free night awards for every five Hyatt brands you stay at. This is a way to earn up to seven free nights over time, based on Hyatt’s current portfolio of 38 brands. As Hyatt’s portfolio continues to grow, the opportunities to earn free nights continues to increase.

However, staying at Hyatt’s all-inclusive properties requires some effort, given that they’re not typically places you just drop into for a night.

Given how easy Hyatt makes it to track this, I’m curious — how many Hyatt brands have you stayed at? Which OMAAT reader has the record for the most brands?
