Aeromexico Adds New U.S. Routes, Flights

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Aeromexico is again expanding its footprint in the U.S. with assistance from longtime partner Delta.

The carrier announced Monday that it will connect Mexico City with Nashville, Tennessee, New Orleans, and San Juan, Puerto Rico. None of the three cities currently has nonstop service to the Mexican capital.

All three connections will operate four times weekly. Service to Nashville will launch first, on June 13, 2027, followed by New Orleans on June 17 and San Juan on June 24.

Flights to Nashville and New Orleans will use Embraer E190 aircraft with 99 seats. For the route to San Juan, the airline will operate Boeing 737 MAX 8s with 166 seats.

Aeromexico will also launch a second daily service between Mexico City and Austin, Texas, on June 3.

“The addition of Nashville, New Orleans, and San Juan to our network will allow us to provide more connectivity options, bringing our customers closer to destinations known for their unique identity and remarkable attractions,” Giancarlo Mulinelli, senior vice president of global sales at Aeromexico, said in a news release. “Together with Delta, we continue to open markets and create more opportunities that strengthen tourism and ties between Mexico and the United States.”

Last month, the partners announced new routes from Mexico City to Orange County, California, Fort Lauderdale, Florida, and Portland, Oregon.
