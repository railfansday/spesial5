Why Lucid Stock Is Moving in Reverse Today

Tanggal: 2026-11-26

Thought shares couldn't tumble any further? One firm says think again.

By Scott Levine – Nov 25, 2026 at 2:18PM EST

10 stocks we like better than Lucid Group ›

It's been a rough road for Lucid Group (LCID -3.03%) so far in 2026. Since the start of the year, shares of the electric vehicle (EV) maker have plunged more than 76%. And one firm believes that Lucid stock is positioned to fall even lower -- an opinion that's driving investors to exit their positions.

As of 12:10 p.m. ET, Lucid shares are down 4.5%, while the S&P 500 is down 2.8%.

Before the market opened yesterday morning, investors learned that Seaport Research initiated coverage on Lucid, rating the stock a sell and assigning a $1 price target. The inauspicious stance on Lucid stock continues to rattle investors' resolve, following the stock's 1.1% decline yesterday from Monday's close. With Lucid shares closing at $2.64 yesterday, the Seaport Research price target implies a 62.1% downside.

According to Thefly.com, Seaport Research based its price target and sell rating on the belief that the company will need to raise $1 billion (and possibly more) over the next 12 to 18 months to prepare to launch its mid-size vehicle.

Lucid produces two of the auto industry's leading luxury EVs: the Lucid Air and Lucid Gravity. The company expects to start production of its midsize vehicle in the second half of 2027.

Although Lucid stock is slightly less expensive today than earlier in the week, investors should hold off before rushing to load up. The company's inability to generate organic cash flow -- something that doesn't seem likely to change anytime soon -- means that this stock is best left to those comfortable with more speculative investments. At this point, most investors are better suited watching Lucid stock from the side of the road for the time being.
