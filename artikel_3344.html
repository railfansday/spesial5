Fremantle Group COO Andrea Scrosati Exiting After Eight Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

Andrea Scrosati is leaving Got Talent and Baywatch producer Fremantle after eight years to launch “a new entrepreneurial venture.”

The exec will stay in his role as Fremantle Group COO and CEO of Continental Europe until the end of January 2027. Announcement of his exit comes three days after Deadline revealed Fremantle U.S. COO Suzanne Lopez was leaving after 23 years.

Scrosati has been with Fremantle since 2018, having joined from Sky Italia. Initially group COO, he added Continential Europe to his duties in 2021. Notably, he led Fremantle’s M&A strategy and led the acquisitions of Poor Things producer Element Pictures, Asacha Media Group and Costiera maker Lux Video. He also backed pushes into the FAST channel business and AI, notably spearheading the creation Imaginae Studios, whose first production, Art Awakens, launched this year. The Italian exec also backed Fremantle’s push into global film, with the company delivering its 100th feature this year. The strategy was expensive and in March, Fremantle parent RTL Group announced Fremantle would no longer fully finance films following a review. Watch on Deadline

Scrosati can point to four Academy Awards and 17 Academy nominations, the Golden Lion at Venice and awards in Cannes and Berlin as an answer to Fremantle’s critics.

“Andrea has made an enormous contribution to Fremantle over the past eight years,” said Fremantle CEO Jennifer Mullin. “He has been instrumental in our growth and transformation, from helping build Fremantle into a true portfolio company and leading our M&A strategy, to establishing our film strategy and constantly pushing us to think differently about innovation and the future of our business.

“Just as importantly, Andrea has been a trusted colleague, partner and a great friend to me. I will miss working alongside him enormously, but I’m incredibly excited for him as he takes this next entrepreneurial step. I’m grateful that he has agreed to stay with us through the end of January to support the transition, and I know everyone at Fremantle joins me in wishing him every success in this new adventure.”

“These eight years at Fremantle have been an extraordinary privilege,” added Scrosati. “Fremantle is a unique company, with incredible people, some of the world’s finest creative talent and an exceptional portfolio of IP. I leave with enormous pride in what we have achieved together and great affection for the company and everyone who makes it what it is. The time feels right for me to take the next step and focus on building something new.

“I want to thank Jen for her leadership, vision and trust, all my colleagues across Fremantle, and RTL Group and Bertelsmann for their unwavering support over the years. Fremantle has an exciting future ahead, and I look forward to working with the team over the coming months to ensure a smooth transition.”
