‘The ‘Burbs’ Actor Kapil Talwalkar Signs With Innovative Artists Entertainment

Tanggal: 2026-11-17

EXCLUSIVE: Kapil Talwalkar has inked with Innovative Artists Entertainment for representation.

Talwalkar stars opposite Keke Palmer and Jack Whitehall in Peacock’s The ‘Burbs, now in production on its second season.

Talwalkar broke out as Tobin on NBC’s Zoey’s Extraordinary Playlist and went on to star in the network’s revival of Night Court.

He also has a recurring role on Apple TV’s Maximum Pleasure Guaranteed. In 2025, Talwalkar played Fabian alongside Lupita Nyong’o, Peter Dinklage, and Sandra Oh in The Public Theater’s Shakespeare in the Park production of Twelfth Night at the newly reopened Delacorte Theater.The India-born, Cupertino, California-raised Talwalkar is also a musician and screenwriter with several original projects in development. He’s a USC graduate. He continues to be represented by Jared Schwartz at Industry Entertainment and Stuart Rosenthal at Goodman, Genow, Schenkman, Smelkinson & Christopher.
