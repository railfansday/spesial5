Stock Market Today, Nov. 18: Nvidia Slips on Strong Results as Succession and China Business Risks Weigh

Tanggal: 2026-11-19

On Nov. 18, 2026, Nvidia shares fell despite upbeat guidance, as investors weighed the CEO transition and tighter chip rules.

By Howard Smith – Nov 18, 2026 at 4:51PM EST

Nvidia (NVDA -2.90%), the leading AI accelerators, GPUs, and data-center networking systems provider, closed at $150.96, down 2.89%. Strong quarterly results and upbeat revenue guidance initially lifted the stock, but investors are now watching succession uncertainty and China-related regulatory scrutiny.Trading volume reached 339.5 million shares, coming in about 117% above its three-month average of 156.7 million shares. Nvidia IPO'd in 1999 and has grown 367,943% since going public.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC -0.23%) closed at 7,324, down 0.23%, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +0.42%) closed at 24,430, up 0.42%. Among semiconductor peers, Advanced Micro Devices closed at $530.14, up 5.31%, and Broadcom closed at $354.08, up 3.65%, highlighting the split reaction across chipmakers as investors processed Nvidia's results.

Amid calls from some investors for former CEO Jensen Huang to be reinstated, Nvidia's chairman and interim CEO squashed those suggestions during Nvidia's earnings call last night.

In its report, Nvidia confirmed strong quarterly results and higher guidance for next year. The company lifted its estimate for fiscal 2028, which closely reflects calendar year 2027, for 75% annual revenue growth, up from 70%. Investors should note that the boost comes off a higher base as well.

But risks related to unknown outcomes from congressional interviews with Jensen Huang and interim CEO Colette Kress, as well as new export restrictions, are still keeping investors from diving back into Nvidia shares at this time.
