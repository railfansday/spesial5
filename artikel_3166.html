Terra (NKLR +4.44%) reported a transition from engineering preparation to industrial execution during the third quarter of 2026. Management stated that the company is currently procuring equipment for its first microreactor, establishing supply chain partnerships, and advancing customer opportunities toward commercial deployment. The company reported that its current cost estimates for the first-of-a-kind reactor remain aligned with forecasts established 1.5 years ago due to the use of off-the-shelf components. CFO Katherine Williams confirmed that existing cash reserves are sufficient to complete the first reactor deployment in Rock City and prove the design's operational capability.
