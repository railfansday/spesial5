Roguelike deckbuilder My Ransomware Can’t Be This Cute?! to be published by Top Hat Studios

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Top Hat Studios will publish The Seppuku Commission-developed roguelike deckbuilder My Ransomware Can’t Be This Cute?! when it launches for PC via Steam, the companies announced. A release date was not announced.

Here is an overview of the game, via its Steam page:

Welcome to your new favorite computer virus :3

You downloaded a rather suspicious .exe file, and now your computer has been hijacked by Ransomware-chan, a sentient malware! To get rid of her, just beat her game: a tense card-flipping deckbuilder about pushing your luck and logical deduction.

Are you feeling lucky? Do you intend to delete the malware or stay with her forever?
