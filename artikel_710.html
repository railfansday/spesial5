Will Smith, Jada Pinkett Smith's $42 Million Estate Catches on Fire

Tanggal: 2026-11-23

Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith's $42 million Calabasas home was the site of an accidental shed fire Nov. 23, the Malibu/Lost Hills Sheriff's Station said.

Will Smith and Jada Pinkett Smith had a scary moment at home.

A fire broke out on couple's $42 million property in Calabasas, Calif., in the early hours of Nov. 23 the Los Angeles County Fire Department confirmed, adding that authorities were able to contain the situation before it engulfed the estate.

"On November 23, 2026 at approximately 12:04 AM, deputies from the Malibu/Lost Hills Station responded to a report of a structure fire," the Malibu/Lost Hills Sheriff's Station said in a statement given to E! News. "Security at the location reported a maintenance shed was on fire. Upon arrival, deputies saw a shed type of structure engulfed in flames."

"Los Angeles County Fire Department personnel arrived and extinguished the fire," the statement continued. "No other structures were damaged, and no one was injured. The cause of the fire was accidental."

E! News has reached out to reps for Will and Jada and have yet to hear back.

The scary moment came days after the couple—who share kids Jaden Smith, 28, and Willow Smith, 26—stepped out for a rare family outing together.

Their moms Caroline Bright and Adrienne Banfield-Norris also took part in the daytime trip to Soho House’s Little Beach House Malibu in Malibu, Calif., on Nov. 21.

Though the couple—who quietly separated in 2016—have maintained a rather low profile in recent years, they have stepped out on several occasions to publicly show their support for their kids.

In June, the pair were joined by Willow, Adrienne and Will’s son Trey—whom he shares with ex Sheree Zampino—for a joint appearance at Paris Fashion Week to attend the Menswear Spring/Summer 2027 show for Christian Louboutin, which Jaden serves as the brand’s men’s creative director for.

During the trip to the fashion capital, the pair enjoyed a night out with Jaden, linking arms as they stepped out for dinner.

Though it’s been over a decade since the Bad Boys actor and Girls Trip star separated, Jada—who publicly revealed the breakup in 2023—shared that she and Will don’t plan on filing for divorce in the future.

As she told NBC News in 2023, “I made a promise that there will never be a reason for us to get a divorce.”
