Antonelli dominates Qatar first practice

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Kimi Antonelli made the perfect start to the Qatar Grand Prix weekend by comfortably topping first practice from Mercedes team-mate George Russell; watch second practice at 5pm later on Friday on Sky Sports F1 and Main Event

Sports Journalist @NigelCJourno

Kimi Antonelli dominated first practice for the Qatar Grand Prix as he led team-mate George Russell in a Mercedes one-two.

New Formula 1 world champion Antonelli was half a second faster than Russell and 0.661s quicker than Red Bull's Isack Hadjar, who was the fastest non-Mercedes in third.

Mercedes have failed to finish on the podium at the last two races but are already big favourites in Qatar following Antonelli's impressive performance in first practice, with the session Friday session taking place later at 5pm, live on Sky Sports F1.

Antonelli's best time actually came on the mediums, so he likely had more pace to show given nearly all the drivers set their best laps on the softs.

McLaren's Lando Norris was unhappy with his car's balance throughout the session in fourth at 0.850s behind Antonelli with his team-mate Oscar Piastri in fifth.

The high-speed Lusail International Circuit was expected to suit McLaren, who are nine points behind Red Bull in the battle for third in the Constructors' Championship with two rounds remaining.

Max Verstappen was only sixth, while Ferrari are set for a difficult weekend as Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton were 1.3 seconds off the pace in seventh and eighth.

The two Ferrari drivers are still free to race despite their collision last time out in Las Vegas, although Leclerc admitted there was "tension to dissipate".

Hamilton was told during first practice that tyre wear was "much higher than expected" which partly underlines Pirelli's decision to introduce a 27-lap tyre limit for Sunday's race, which will create a two-stop race.

It is the third time a tyre limit has been implemented for the race at the Lusail International Circuit after punctures at the inaugural 2021 event, and safety concerns being raised during the 2023 weekend.

Racing Bulls' Arvid Lindblad, who published an emotional social media post following his crash in Las Vegas when on course for a strong result, was ninth in first practice with Alpine's Pierre Gasly in 10th.

Britain's Oliver Bearman was 17th, a day after Haas confirmed he would remain with the team for 2027 after weeks of speculation regarding his future.

American Jak Crawford was the only rookie replacement in the session as he drove Fernando Alonso's Aston Martin and he outpaced Lance Stroll with a respectable performance.
