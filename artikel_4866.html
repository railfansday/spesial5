Ugg Black Friday Deals 2026: Take Up to 33% Off Popular Styles

Tanggal: 2026-11-25

You can save big on the Classic Micro, Classic Ultra Mini, Lowmel Mule, and more.

Women's Health may earn commission from the links on this page, but we only feature products we believe in. Why Trust Us?

There are a few things I can pretty much guarantee will make it onto my Christmas wish list every year, and Uggs are always near the top. I've been asking for the brand's ridiculously cozy slippers and boots since middle school, and even after all these years (and more pairs than I'd care to admit), I still get excited about unwrapping a fresh pair. Whether you're shopping for a longtime Ugg devotee or finally treating yourself to the style you've been eyeing all season, Black Friday is a pretty great excuse to add another pair to your collection. And this year, the brand is rolling out several days of holiday deals worth keeping tabs on.

Ugg's Black Friday sale will unfold in stages, with new markdowns arriving throughout Thanksgiving week. The Cyber Sale kicks off November 23, with 30% off the Classic Micro plus 33% off select Minimel styles. Come Thanksgiving, shoppers can expect additional markdowns of up to 30% on seasonal colors of the Tasman. Then, on Black Friday itself, select Classic Ultra Mini Platform and Lowmel Mule styles will be marked down by 30%. There are even more deals coming for Cyber Monday, including 30% off select Bailey Bow styles.

I've been keeping an eye on the Ugg lineup all season, and a few of its most-wanted silhouettes are already seeing discounts, including the Classic Micro, Tasman II, Tazz II, and Classic Ultra Mini. I'll be keeping this page updated as more deals go live, so whether you're hoping to snag a pair of everyday boots or finally get your hands on those slippers you've been thinking about for months, you're in the right place. Consider this your sign to start shopping before your size disappears.
