Relai Launches Stacks, Letting Bitcoin Savers Split Plans by Goal

Tanggal: 2026-11-24

Swiss bitcoin app Relai launched Stacks on Tuesday, a feature that lets users run several recurring bitcoin savings plans side by side, one for each goal, such as a house, a wedding or a child’s first car.

Stacks is live for all Relai users in Switzerland and the European Union. Each Stack carries its own amount and schedule, and users can change either at any time without starting over, the company said. A savings plan is Relai’s version of dollar-cost averaging: buying a fixed amount of bitcoin on a set schedule, weekly or monthly, regardless of price. Savers use it to avoid timing the market.

Since September, Relai users have been able to change the amount and buy date of an active plan. Stacks applies that to several goals at once, so a saver can put more toward a house one month, slow down a car fund the next and leave an emergency reserve untouched.

In practice, Stacks is an organizing layer, not a new account. Relai’s help pages describe each Stack as a separate pot with its own bitcoin address inside the same wallet. Users get up to five custom Stacks plus a main balance, and moving bitcoin between them takes a regular on-chain transaction with a network fee.

Relai is self-custodial, meaning it does not hold customers’ bitcoin. The coins in every Stack sit in a wallet controlled by a 12-word recovery phrase, or seed phrase, that only the user has. All Stacks share that one phrase, and restoring the wallet with it brings every Stack back.

The trade-off is responsibility. A saver who loses the phrase and the phone has no password reset and no support desk that can recover the coins. Anyone who gets the phrase can take them, which is why attackers have gone as far as tampering with hardware wallets to capture it.

“Everyone saves for something. A home, their kids, the freedom to stop working one day,” said Julian Liniger, Relai’s co-founder and CEO. “With Stacks, you can save for each of those goals in Bitcoin, at your own pace.”

In the EU, Relai’s French unit is authorized by France’s financial markets regulator, the AMF, as a crypto-asset service provider under MiCA, the bloc’s Markets in Crypto-Assets regulation. Relai secured that license in October 2025, and under MiCA a firm licensed in one member state can “passport” its services across the bloc.

Swiss and Liechtenstein customers are served by Relai AG, which is affiliated with VQF, a self-regulatory organization recognized by Swiss regulator FINMA under the country’s anti-money laundering law.

Goal-based saving in bitcoin also carries price risk that a bank savings account does not. Bitcoin traded near $60,000 on Tuesday, more than 50% below its record above $126,000 set in October 2025, after falling below $60,000 last week.

Relai, founded in Zurich in 2020, says it has processed more than $1 billion in trading volume. The app charges a standard 1% fee, but each user’s first monthly savings plan order of up to 100 Swiss francs or euros is fee-free.
