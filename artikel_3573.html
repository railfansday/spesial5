Luke Wilson Teases ‘The Family Stone’ Sequel, Reuniting With Cast Members

Tanggal: 2026-11-27

Luke Wilson reflected on the legacy of The Family Stone in the midst of shooting next year’s highly-anticipated sequel.

“I feel like I’ve just kind of been in a bubble making it here for the last month,” Wilson, 55, exclusively told Us via his partnership with Fanatics. “It was one of those movies where, you know, it was really fun to make the first one. A great group of people. It came out and people seemed to like it. But then it was a few years later, I’d start to hear things like, ‘Oh my family and I watch that every Thanksgiving,’ or ‘My family and I watch that every Christmas.’ Each year it seemed like more.”

The actor added, “I’m so used to Chevy Chase and Christmas Vacation or It’s a Wonderful Life. So when people started to say their families were watching The Family Stone, I said, ‘Really?’ But now it does seem like it’s become a genuine tradition for people.”

The Family Stone was released in 2005, featuring an ensemble cast including Rachel McAdams, Dermot Mulroney, Sarah Jessica Parker, Claire Danes, Craig T. Nelson and the late Diane Keaton. A sequel was announced in September with all of the original cast set to return, plus the addition of 9-time Emmy winner, Jean Smart.

“I’m wary of saying anything about the damn plot because I feel like I’d have the studio come down on my head,” Wilson joked. “I’ll just say people get together, people split apart, babies are born. It revolves around Christmas again and I think it comes out in November of next year.”

Wilson plays Ben Stone — sibling of Everett (Mulroney), Amy (McAdams), Susannah (Elizabeth Reaser) and Thad (Ty Giordano) — who ultimately ends up with Meredith (Parker) at the end of the first movie.

While Wilson was understandably coy about what to expect from the sequel itself, he gushed about the experience of reuniting with the cast.

“I’ve never gone back to the same characters, the same group of people,” Wilson explained. “It’s been really fun. Just to be around Sarah Jessica, who was so great to work with. She kind of helped put the movie together.”

He added, “It’s been great to be around Craig T. Nelson. He’s been really funny. He’ll just be like, ‘What are we doing, man? Why are we here? Why am I here?’ But it’s been a good feeling.”

In addition to returning to The Family Stone, Wilson is the face of Fanatics’ Town Hall, the brand’s season-long NFL campaign. The campaign features Wilson alongside former NFL great Calvin Johnson and former LSU gymnast and influencer Livvy Dunne, celebrating the passion of NFL fans and highlighting FanCash, Fanatics’ universal rewards currency.

“I’ve done a few things with athletes before and I always wind up thinking even though they might be a little out of their element doing a commercial or TV show, they’re always really good,” Wilson said. “They seem to be quietly taking it all in.”

He added, “It’s just fun to be around those guys. They just seem like superheroes.”
