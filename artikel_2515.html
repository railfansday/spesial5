Confirmed Lineups: Hamburger SV vs. Borussia Mönchengladbach

Tanggal: 2026-11-07

Former Bundesliga giants HSV and Borussia Mönchengladbach lock horns in the opening fixture of matchday 9. Both teams are currently fighting relegation, with the visitors hoping a back-to-back away victory momentarily lifting them off the basement.

Merlin Polzin's side list three successive games in all competitions after winning the previous three. The hosts can climb seven places with three points before the rest of matchday 9 fixtures on Saturday and Sunday.
