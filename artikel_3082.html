Spurs guard Dylan Harper reportedly expected to miss up to 3 weeks with left rotator hip strain

Tanggal: 2026-11-20

San Antonio Spurs guard Dylan Harper has reportedly been diagnosed with a left hip rotator strain, ESPN's Shams Charania reports.

The recovery timetable for that injury is one to three weeks, depending on how Harper's rehabilitation progresses, according to Charania.

Harper, 20, sustained the injury during the Spurs' 105-93 loss to the Detroit Pistons on Tuesday. In the closing minute of the fourth quarter, Harper was about to receive a pass from Stephon Castle while matched up against Detroit's Ausar Thompson.

However, Harper's right foot slipped on the court and caused him to stretch awkwardly. He stayed down on the floor while play continued on the other end of the court, appearing to grab at his left hip or groin area.

The second-year guard was eventually able to stand up and walk off the court with some assistance from teammate Keldon Johnson. But he was noticeably limping and in pain as he went to the locker room. Harper did not return to the game during the final 53 seconds.

"It just looked like he slipped, obviously," Spurs coach Mitch Johnson told reporters afterward, via the San Antonio Express-News' Tom Orsborn. "So hoping for the best, but don't know anything yet."

Harper appeared at Thursday's shootaround with no noticeable limp, according to Orsborn. But that was apparently before imaging results came back from the team's medical staff.

"I'm happy he's not as hurt as he could be," said Spurs forward Julian Champagnie, who will replace Harper in the starting lineup. "Obviously, we need him, so finding out it wasn't that serious, or as serious as it could have been, was good news."

In 15 games this season, Harper is scoring 18.4 points per game, second on San Antonio behind Victor Wembanyama. He's also averaging 5.4 rebounds and 4.5 assists. He's shooting 50% from the floor and 31% on 54 3-point attempts.

At 12-3, the Spurs are a half-game behind the Los Angeles Lakers and Philadelphia 76ers for the NBA's top record. San Antonio hosts the Los Angeles Clippers (4-9) on Thursday night, then has two days off before hosting the New Orleans Pelicans (5-11) on Saturday.
