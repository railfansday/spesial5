Stock Market Today, Nov. 4: Dow Surges 3% in Biggest Post-Midterm Rally in 44 Years

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Wall Street surged following the midterm elections, with the Dow posting its strongest post-election rally in decades as oil prices retreated and investors reassessed the market outlook.

The Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI +3.03%) jumped 3.03% to 53,356 for its best post-midterm performance in 44 years, while the S&P 500 (^GSPC +1.58%) gained 1.59% to 7,689 and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +1.44%) rose 1.44% to 25,737 as election uncertainty dissipated.

Gold prices climbed 3.38% to $4,073.70, while the 10-year Treasury yield fell approximately 6 basis points to 4.89% as Real Estate outperformed with a 3.96% gain relative to a 3.96% decline in Energy and modest growth in Technology.

McDonald's (MCD +6.53%) propelled blue-chip gains following upbeat quarterly earnings, while Alphabet (GOOGL +3.18%) (GOOG +3.25%) shares rallied as the broader market recovered from election-day volatility. CVS Health Corp. (CVS -12.53%) trended higher on a planned retail spinoff, while Trump Media & Technology Group (DJT -24.54%) plummeted nearly 17% following the midterm results.

Historically, stocks have often performed well following midterm elections as investors gain greater clarity on the political landscape. The prospect of a divided Congress could also reduce the likelihood of major changes to fiscal and regulatory policies, providing businesses with a more predictable operating environment. However, political gridlock does not eliminate the economic challenges facing investors.

Beyond the elections, the broader outlook remains mixed. Lower energy prices and borrowing costs could provide relief to consumers and businesses, while recent corporate earnings suggest that demand remains resilient in several sectors. Nevertheless, persistent inflation, concerns surrounding artificial intelligence infrastructure spending, and uncertainty over future Federal Reserve policy remain important risks.

For investors, Wednesday's rally is encouraging, but one strong session doesn't necessarily signal the beginning of a sustained advance. The next test will be whether improving market sentiment is supported by corporate earnings, economic growth, and continued stability in financial markets.
