Why Nvidia and Jensen Huang Matter So Much to Markets

Tanggal: 2026-11-13

It’s not often that news about someone’s extramarital relationships erases hundreds of billions of dollars of value from the stock market. So far, the market capitalization of Nvidia Corp. has fallen by more than a trillion dollars in the wake of revelations that its co-founder Jensen Huang had affairs with one woman suspected of being an asset for Chinese intelligence and another who reported to him.
