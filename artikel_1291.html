Champions League Liveblog: Manchester United vs. Roma, Feyenoord vs. Inter

Tanggal: 2026-11-04

Join us for all the build-up and action from the Champions League, as Roma visit Manchester United in crisis, while Inter try to shake off poor results away to Feyenoord.

They both kick off at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

The Giallorossi lost only one match all season, the 4-3 thriller to Real Madrid they had been leading 3-1, and now try to put the final nail in Michael Carrick’s Manchester United coffin.

Inter have been dire in the Champions League so far, losing to Real Madrid and Club Brugge, then scraping a 1-0 win over Shakhtar Donetsk, so now visit a Feyenoord side who already beat Como here.

Cristian Chivu feels Inter were ‘the best version of ourselves’ in tonight’s 3-0 Champions League victory away to Feyenoord, but ‘consistency is what brings results.’

Gian Piero Gasperini insists Roma ‘will learn’ from their Champions League collapse at Manchester United, more so than when doing the same with Real Madrid. ‘Disappointment sets in.’

“It’s the right response,” Bisseck told Sky Sport Italia.

“I didn’t prepare my goal celebration, so it was just what came to me in the moment. It is always nice to score goals and to get a good response after a bad defeat.

“Football is like that, nobody wants to lose, but it happens sometimes. It’s not important what people say, inevitably they’ll say we are the worst after a defeat, and the greatest in the world after a win.

“We know there are many things we need to work on and improve, but we are a strong team. Every season we need to talk amongst ourselves and stay united, it is a long campaign and we’re just at the start. I believe we can do great things in this season.”

Inter made a strong Champions League statement with a 3-0 victory away to Feyenoord thanks to Yann Bisseck, Pio Esposito and Piotr Zielinski goals.

Roma once again threw away a two-goal Champions League advantage in a 3-2 defeat to Manchester United, as after dominating the first half, they repeated past mistakes.
