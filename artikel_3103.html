Instead, retailers like Best Buy are knocking hundreds off older models, while you’ll need to turn to carriers like T-Mobile and Verizon for discounts on the latest models.

For instance, you can pick up an iPhone Air for $300 off at Best Buy right now or get an iPhone Duo for free at T-Mobile when you sign up for T-Mix plan with at least 7 of its “Picks” perks. The choice is yours: get an older iPhone for less when you buy it outright or get a newer model for free or with a bunch of extras when you sign up for a carrier deal.
