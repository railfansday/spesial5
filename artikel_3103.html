Autolus Therapeutics plc (AUTL +0.00%) reported 95% year-over-year revenue growth for the third quarter and raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to at or exceeding $150 million. Management stated that the company achieved positive gross margins for three consecutive quarters, reaching 57% in the most recent period due to volume-based efficiencies at its manufacturing facility. The company announced a strategic shift in the timing of clinical data releases for its autoimmune pipeline, extending follow-up periods to meet expert consensus on clinical interpretability. Management reported that current cash reserves and anticipated product sales provide a operational runway into early 2028.
