Gwen Stefani & Blake Shelton Step Out Alongside Her Ex Gavin Rossdale

Tanggal: 2026-11-12

Gwen Stefani and Gavin Rossdale both attended their son Kingston’s concert in Los Angeles Nov. 10, which also saw the No Doubt singer’s husband Blake Shelton in the audience.

Gwen Stefani and Gavin Rossdale shared a sweet escape to a special concert.

A decade after the No Doubt singer and Bush frontman finalized their divorce following a contentious breakup, they proved their kids rock—like, really rock.

After all, their eldest son Kingston, 20, performed Nov. 10 at L.A.’s legendary Troubadour concert hall, and the exes, as well as Stefani’s husband Blake Shelton, were in the audience.

For the outing, the “Hollaback Girl” singer sported a pink-and-white striped blazer and blue jeans as she walked hand-in-hand with Shelton, who donned a Dickie’s short-sleeved work shirt and jeans.

For his part, Rossdale wore a white-and-gray T-shirt and black pants.

Though Rossdale and Stefani—who also share kids Zuma, 18, and Apollo, 12—didn’t appear to boogie along next to each other during the show, they were all smiles as they separately entered the venue.

Since the pair broke up in 2015 amid rumors of his cheating, the “Love Remains the Same” rocker has given insight into how he and Stefani attempt to maintain harmony while co-parenting.

"It is an interesting kind of [relationship]," he told Fox News Digital in March. "It's a two-lane highway, you know, but they don't ever seem to merge, which is fine."

Still, Rossdale admitted, "[She's] probably doing the best she can, and I'm doing the best I can. And the kids are growing up, so I'm a proud proponent of the system."

And while he and Stefani may not be necessarily singing the same tune, the musician is adamant about not speaking poorly of his ex in front of their kids.

"It's not my place to say anything," he said on a March 2024 episode of the Amy and T.J. podcast. "I'm handcuffed because I would never want to overly say anything negative about their mom or stuff like that. It's just not right."

Instead, Rossdale is focused on showing up well despite being “flawed as hell.”

“I just feel really proud of myself in my consistency as a father,” he added. “I know in my heart that I'm super consistent.”
