Solana (SOL) to Connect With Wall Street’s $4.7 Quadrillion Clearing Giant

The Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation (DTCC) will connect its tokenization service to Solana (SOL) in the second half of 2027, the company said on Nov. 12.

The integration will allow "digital twins" of Russell 1000 stocks, major ETFs and U.S. Treasuries to be issued on the Solana blockchain. In December 2025, the SEC issued a no-action letter that allowed DTC to tokenize these assets.

In 2025, DTCC processed $4.7 quadrillion in securities transactions. Its depository, DTC, holds and services $114 trillion in assets from more than 150 countries.

The DTCC Tokenization Service (DTS) will let large market participants convert conventional securities into tokens, move them across the Solana network and redeem them.

"DTC-tokenized assets made available on Solana – and all blockchain networks integrated with the service – will retain the same investor protections, ownership rights and regulatory safeguards that apply to traditionally held securities," said Brian Steele, president of DTCC Clearing & Securities Services.

According to DTCC CEO Frank La Salla, the integration will also free up securities for use as collateral.

DTS went live on Oct. 28, 2026. At launch, it worked with Canton Network and a private ledger. DTCC said that it adds new networks at clients' request.

"DTCC is committed to providing an open and connected platform that provides our users with access to mature distributed ledger technology networks like Solana that have established performance, security and governance features," said Nadine Chakar, global head of DTCC Digital Assets.

Nick Ducoff, general manager for institutional at the Solana Foundation, also commented on the news. "Institutions choose Solana because it already runs real financial activity at scale," he said.

According to Ducoff, the integration "connects one of the most trusted names in post-trade infrastructure with a network built for always-on markets."

As of Nov. 12, distributed real-world assets (RWA) in the Solana ecosystem reached $5.13 billion across more than 1.02 million holders, according to RWA.xyz. Transfers over the past 30 days totaled $16.48 billion.

The stablecoin market cap on Solana stands at $13.85 billion, with monthly turnover of $387.41 billion.
